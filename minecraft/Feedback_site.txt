The '''Minecraft Feedback Site''' is the official website location for players to share their feedback for ''[[Minecraft]]''. Similar to the [[bug tracker]], the site is moderated by the community team. The feedback site is located [https://feedback.minecraft.net here], and currently has 27 categories in which to post feedback in:

*[[Caves & Cliffs]]
*{{el|je}} [[Snapshot]]s
*[[Biome]]s and [[Dimension]]s
*Community and [[Minecon|Live Events]]
*Graphics
*{{el|earth}}
*[[Official pages/Parity issue list|Parity]]
*Scripting and [[Mod]]s
*User Interface, Input Methods and [[Menu]] Options
*[[Combat Tests|Java Combat Snapshot]]
*[[Add-on]]s
*[[Blocks]], [[Tools]], and [[Items]]
*[[Education Edition|Education]] Related
*[[Launcher]]
*Minecraft (Franchise)
*[[Realms]] and Dedicated [[Server]]s
*[[Sounds]]
*[[Villager]]s, NPCs, Humanoids
*[[Minecraft: Dungeons]]
*[[Bedrock Edition|Bedrock]] Betas
*[[Command]]s
*Gameplay
*[[Marketplace]]/Store
*[[Mobs]], Animals and non-Humanoids
*[[Redstone]]
*[[Structure]]s
*Website

There is also an official [https://discord.gg/MXxTqXnuD5 Minecraft Feedback discord server].

== Trivia ==
* There was a 27th category about what to name the Piglin Beast, announced at [[MINECON Live 2019]]. It was decided on the name [[hoglin]], and the category was removed, no longer having a use.
*As of recently, The site no longer shows commenter and suggestor names, but rather as “Registered User” and you can’t click on them to look at their profile. It is unknown when or if this will be fixed.
