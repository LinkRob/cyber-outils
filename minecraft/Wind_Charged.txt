'''Wind Charged''' is an effect that creates a [[wind charge]] when an entity with that effect gets killed. This potion effect is mainly used in [[Trial Chambers]], along with [[Weaving]], [[Oozing]], and [[Infested]]. Weaving effect occurs when the player is hit by a splash potion of weaving while the [[Trial Omen]] effect is activated. (Or by brewing it, then drinking it/using gunpowder to make your own splash potion/using [[Dragon's Breath]] to make it a [[Lingering Potion]].)
