'''Ice deserts''' were [[Unused features|unused]] snow-covered [[biomes]] which existed prior to the [[Adventure Update]], these biomes were large [[sand]] flats with a snow layer cover. 

== Description ==
Ice deserts are similar in appearance to [[tundra]]s, with fairly flat land and small hills. However, instead of [[grass]] the surface is primarily made up of sand. Flowers, trees and passive mobs are non-existent in this biome. Ice deserts usually generate surrounded by the tundra as well. The ice desert only existed as an unused biome; it was in the code but never implemented into the temperature/rainfall table and thus did not actually generate without the use of mods. It is the coldest biome to spawn as it generates if the temperature is below 10%.
