A '''{{EntityLink|zombie}}''' is a common undead hostile mob that comes in many variants.

'''Zombie'''  may also refer to:

== ''Minecraft'' ==

=== Mobs ===
*{{EntityLink|Drowned}}, zombie-like mobs that spawn underwater or if a zombie drowns
*{{EntityLink|Chicken jockey}}, a baby zombie riding a chicken
*{{EntityLink|Giant}}, unused passive giant zombie mob
*{{EntityLink|Husk}}, zombie-like mobs that spawn in the desert and inflict [[Hunger (effect)]] upon attacking the [[player]]
*{{EntityLink|Zombie villager}}, a zombified variant of the [[villager]] (zombie-like mobs that sometimes spawn instead of a zombie and also spawn when a zombie kills a villager)
*{{EntityLink|Zombified piglin}}, a zombified variant of the [[piglin]]s and the most common mobs that spawn in the Nether
*{{EntityLink|Zoglin}}, a zombified variant of the [[hoglin]]
*{{EntityLink|Zombie horse}}, a zombified variant of the [[horse]] (unused before [[Mounts of Mayhem]])
**[[Zombie Horse|Zombie Horseman]], a [[jockey|jockey variant]] of a zombie riding a zombie horse
*[[Zombie Nautilus|Zombie Nautilus]], a zombified variant of the [[nautilus]]
**[[Zombie Nautilus|Zombie Nautilus Jockey]], a jockey variant of a drowned riding a zombie nautilus

== ''Minecraft Dungeons'' ==

=== Mobs ===

*{{DuEntityLink|Zombie}}, a basic melee mob found in most places
*{{DuEntityLink|Baby Zombie}}, a small and faster variant of zombie
*{{DuEntityLink|Chicken Jockey}} baby zombie that spawn riding a chicken
*{{DuEntityLink|Chicken Jockey Tower}} A variant of Chicken Jockey that features multiple baby zombies stacked on top of each other
*{{DuEntityLink|Husk}}, a desert variant of zombie found only in desert locations
*{{DuEntityLink|Frozen Zombie}} a snowy zombie that attacks with snowballs in the Creeping Winter DLC
*{{DuEntityLink|Jungle Zombie}}, a mossy zombie that poisons players that can be found in the Jungle Awakens DLC
*{{DuEntityLink|Drowned}}, a mob that will appear in Hidden Depths DLC
*{{DuEntityLink|Zombified Piglin}}, a mob that attacks the player with a sword and has more piglin-like sounds than its base game variant
*{{DuEntityLink|Zombified Piglin Hunter}}, a ranged variant of the zombified piglin that shoots at players with a crossbow
*{{DuEntityLink|Zombified Fungus Thrower}}, a mob that throws poisonous fungus that produces blue poison clouds
*{{DuEntityLink|Zombified Baby Pig}}, a pet baby pig which is zombified.
