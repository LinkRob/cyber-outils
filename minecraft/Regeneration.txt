'''Regeneration''' is a [[status effect]] that restores a [[player]]'s (or [[mob]]'s) health over time without interference from [[hunger]].

== Effect ==
The amount of [[health]] healed over time is shown in the table below:
{| class="wikitable" style="text-align:right"
 ! Level !! Ticks per {{hp|1|notag=1}} !! {{hp|1|notag=1}} per second
 |-
 | 1 || 50 || {{hp|0.4}}
 |-
 | 2 || 25 || {{hp|0.8}}
 |-
 | 3 || 12 || {{hp|1.67}}
 |-
 | 4 || 6 || {{hp|3.33}}
 |-
 | 5 || 3 || {{hp|6.67}}
 |-
 | 6+ || 1 || {{hp|20}}
|}
Amplifiers outside the range 0–31 (corresponding to levels 1–32) are used [[Wikipedia:Modulo operation|modulo]] 32, making level 33 the same as level 1, etc.

==Immune mobs==
[[Undead]] mobs and [[ender dragon]]s are unaffected by Regeneration.
