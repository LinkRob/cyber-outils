'''Feather Falling''' is an [[enchantment]] unique to [[boots]] that reduces [[fall damage]] the player takes. It does not affect falling speed.

== Obtaining ==
At level 30, Feather Falling is one of the rarest enchantments to obtain through an enchanting table.<ref>{{bug|MC-130420||Feather Falling enchantment is very rare|WAI}}</ref> There is only a 2.7% chance for Feather Falling IV to appear on a pair of diamond boots when enchantments that require 30 levels are applied. However, Feather Falling appears much more commonly on an enchanting table at levels slightly lower than level 30. As seen from the table above, Feather Falling is most common at level 22 when enchanting diamond boots through an enchanting table. Levels 24 and 26 still provide chances of obtaining Feather Falling much higher than that at level 30.

Feather Falling IV can also be obtained by combining two Feather Falling III enchantments on an anvil, or trading with villagers.

In Bedrock Edition, Feather Falling enchantment can be rarely obtained by fishing. Otherwise, it can be earned through enchantment table, loot chests, and trading.

== Usage ==
Feather Falling reduces all forms of fall damage taken, even though fall damage normally ignores [[armor]].  This is not limited to falling from a great height either - Feather Falling will reduce the damage taken from thrown [[ender pearl]]s or from riding a [[mob]] that takes significant fall damage.

Feather Falling will reduce damage taken from crashing into the ground with an [[elytra]], but it will not reduce damage taken from crashing into a wall.

The damage formula that Feather Falling uses will reduce fall damage by {{Cd|(12 x level)%}}, up to a maximum of 48% with Feather Falling IV.  This can be further increased with the [[Protection]] enchantment.

Damage reduction from [[Protection]], [[Fire Protection]], '''Feather Falling''', [[Blast Protection]], and [[Projectile Protection]] stacks up to an upper limit cap of 80%.  Any further damage reduction that would normally occur is instead ignored.  Feather Falling IV cannot reach this cap alone, as it only reduces fall damage by 48%.  However, if combined with multiple Protection enchantments, the damage cap can be reached since Protection also reduces fall damage.  In order to reach this cap using only Feather Falling, level VII would be required which is only possible with the use of [[commands]].

== Trivia ==
* With [[boots]] enchanted with Feather Falling IV, and at least two other armor pieces enchanted with [[Protection]] IV, a [[player]] at full [[health]] can survive a maximum fall distance of 103 blocks.
** The fall distance can be extended to 503.3 blocks provided that the player lands on a [[hay bale]].
* Although Feather Falling should negate 12% of the damage per level, at level IV the height of guaranteed death is 55 blocks, not 57-58 (57.5) blocks.
