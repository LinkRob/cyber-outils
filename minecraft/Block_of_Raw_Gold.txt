A '''block of raw gold''' is a raw [[metal]] [[block]] equivalent to nine [[raw gold]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Blocks of raw gold only drop themselves as items when mined using an iron [[pickaxe]] or better.

{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Block of Raw Gold|Pickaxe|Iron}}

== Usage ==

Blocks of raw gold are a compact way to store [[raw gold]].

=== Crafting ingredient ===

{{crafting usage|Block of Raw Gold}}

=== Piglins ===
[[Piglin]]s become distracted upon a player dropping a block of raw gold as an item, and become enraged if a player breaks a block of raw gold near them.
