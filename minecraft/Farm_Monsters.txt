The '''farm monsters''' are hostile variants of [[cows]], [[chickens]], [[sheep]]s, [[pigs]], and [[wolves]] in ''[[Minecraft Mini-Series]]''.

== Appearance ==
The devil cow or the infernal cow is a red variant of the regular cow with bigger horns and a angry face. The mummy sheep is a [[undead]] variant of the sheep that is wrapped in bandages. The skeleton pig is a [[undead]] variant of the pig that has it's bones appearing. The hostile chicken has the same appearance as the regular passive chicken. The wolf monster or werewolf has blue fur, fangs, red eyes, and ripped brown clothing. It is never shown as a passive mob as it's passive variant resembles a human in the [[Mash-up pack#Halloween Edition|Halloween Edition mash-up pack]].

== Trivia ==
* A hostile variant of the wolves was later added in ''[[Minecraft Earth]]'', the [[Minecraft Earth:Skeleton Wolf|skeleton wolf]].
* A hostile variant of the chicken was later added in ''[[Minecraft Dungeons]]'', being the mount of the [[Minecraft Dungeons:Chicken Jockey|chicken jockey]].
** In the [[Java Edition 2.0#Mobs 2|April fools update 2.0]] there was a feature which made chickens neutral. This meant they would attack you if you hit them.
* All the farm monsters are based on minifigures made by Matel which in turn were based on the animals in the [[Mash-up pack#Halloween Edition|Halloween Edition mash-up pack]].
* Despite donkey robot and the mutant squid having a minifigure, they don't appear in the mini-series but are from the [[Mash-up pack#Halloween Edition|Halloween Edition mash-up pack]].
** This is the same for [[Mash-up pack#Minifigures based on Mash-Up Packs or Skin Packs|other minifigures]] that were Halloween themed except for the [[Enderall]], the [[Well Wisher]], [[Cropsy]] and the [[Black Widow Spider]].
* The hostile chicken does have a Halloween themed skin resembling a gray rat in the mash up pack, but it wasn't ever added to the series or made into a minifigure.
