'''[[Library]]''' is an ''Education Edition'' feature to simplify the process of finding and importing educational content directly in the game.

'''Library''' may also refer to:
* The buildings in [[village]]s that contain bookshelves and lecterns.
* The rooms in [[stronghold]]s that contain bookshelves and chests.
* The rooms in [[woodland mansion]]s that contain <!--perhaps not surprisingly--> bookshelves.
* The [[Tutorials/Enchantment mechanics#Bookshelf placement|enchanting library]], a player-built structure to increase enchanting table power.
