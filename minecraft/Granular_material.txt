Multiple types of '''[[wikipedia:Granular material|granular materials]]''' can be found within the game.

== Dust ==
'''Dust''' may refer to:
* {{ItemLink|Glowstone Dust}} - an [[item]] obtained from mining [[glowstone]] blocks, which can be used in [[brewing]] or to create glowstone blocks.
* {{ItemLink|Redstone Dust}} - an item obtained from mining [[redstone ore]], which can be placed as redstone wire, crafted into various [[Redstone (disambiguation)|redstone]]-related items or used in brewing.

== Powder ==
'''Powder''' may refer to:
* {{ItemLink|Blaze Powder}} - an item crafted from [[blaze rod]]s used mainly in [[brewing]] and crafting [[eyes of ender]].
* {{BlockLink|Concrete Powder}} - an item crafted from [[sand]], [[gravel]] and [[dye]] that turns into [[concrete]] when it touches [[water]].
* {{ItemLink|Gunpowder}} - an item dropped from [[creeper]]s, used to craft [[TNT]], [[Firework Rocket]] and in brewing.
* {{BlockLink|Powder Snow}}
* [[Dye]]s have an internal name "item.dyePowder.name", as shown by a glitch in early Pocket Edition and the statistics in early Java Edition
