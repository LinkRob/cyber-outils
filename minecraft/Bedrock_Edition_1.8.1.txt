'''1.8.1''' is a minor update to {{el|be}} released for Nintendo Switch on December 17, 2018, and for the other platforms on January 8, 2019, which fixes two bugs.<ref>{{cite|url=https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360020828092-Minecraft-1-8-1-Bedrock-|title=Minecraft - 1.8.1 (Bedrock)|date=January 8, 2019}}</ref>

== Fixes ==
* Fixed an issue with saving to disk (Nintendo Switch only).
* Fixed worlds not loading when downloaded from [[Realms]] (excluding Nintendo Switch).
