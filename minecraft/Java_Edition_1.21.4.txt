'''Java Edition 1.21.4''' is a minor update to 1.21 in [[Java Edition]], named [[The Garden Awakens]]. This update adds features from this game drop. The update is released on December 3, 2024.

== Additions ==
=== [[Block]]s ===
'''[[Creaking Heart]]'''
* Can be found activated in the [[Pale Garden]] biome.
* Can be mined and relocated with a [[Silk Touch]] tool.
* Can be crafted using two Pale Oak logs and a block of Resin.
* It only activates when placed between two Pale Oak logs in the same orientation and can't be deactivated unless the block is broken.
* Spawns [[creaking]]s at night or during [[thunderstorm]]s when activated and links itself to them.

=== [[Mob]]s ===
; [[File:Creaking Minecraft JE BE.png|36px|left|link=Creaking]] '''[[Creaking]]'''
* A new spooky hostile mob spawned by active creaking hearts during the night or thunderstorms.
** Can also be spawned using a spawn egg, by the {{cmd|summon}} command or from a [[Monster Spawner|monster spawner]], which is not connected to a creaking heart, persists through day and night, and deals {{hp|1}} damage, killing it instantly or in a single hit.
* It moves and attacks when a [[player]] is looking away from it.
* Unlike most mobs, it can only be killed by destroying its respective creaking heart.
** Although it can also be killed instantly when spawned using a spawn egg or by the {{cmd|summon}} command.
* It emits particles that lead to its creaking heart when hit or damaged.
*It cannot be put in [[boat]]s

=== [[Biome]]s ===
'''[[Pale Garden]]'''
* A rare new biome that generates with pale oak trees, pale moss blocks, pale moss carpets, pale hanging moss, and eyeblossoms.
* It is quiet in the daytime, but very eerie at night.
* It is also where creakings spawn during nighttime or during thunderstorms.
* Music soundtracks will go silent when moving from an existing biome into the Pale Garden. [[Music Disc|Music discs]] are exceptional.
