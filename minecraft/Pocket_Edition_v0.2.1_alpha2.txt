'''v0.2.1 alpha2''' was a minor update to '' [[Pocket Edition|Minecraft: Pocket Edition]]'' that was released on March 17, 2012. This version was released exclusively on Android devices to fix some of the bugs and crashes in [[Pocket Edition v0.2.1 alpha|v0.2.1 alpha]] affecting certain devices. This version was used as the demo version of ''Pocket Edition'' for many years.

== Fixes ==

'''2 bugs fixed'''

* A Major bug was fixed that prevented the game from opening on some devices.
* Acer Iconia Tab A500 fixed

== Internal Version ==
The internal version is 0.2.1(2016), and the size is 2.81 MB.

This was the final version of ''Pocket Edition Demo''/''Lite''. One of the ''Pocket Edition Demo''/''Lite''{{'}}s limited features was that your worlds would not be saved, and out of the total 36 different blocks, only 18 were available. The internal version is the same as the full version; however, it is 2.84 MB in size. </onlyinclude>
