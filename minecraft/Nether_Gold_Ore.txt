'''Nether gold ore''' is a variant of [[gold ore]] found only in [[the Nether]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Natural generation ===

Nether gold ore generates in [[the Nether]] in the form of [[ore (feature)|ore blob]]s. Nether gold ore attempts to replace [[netherrack]] 10 times per [[chunk]] in blobs of size 0–16, from levels 10 to 117, in all Nether biomes. {{IN|java}}, in [[Basalt Deltas|basalt deltas]], nether gold ore attempts to generate 20 times per chunk instead, as there are fewer valid generation areas.

=== Breaking ===
Nether gold ore requires a pickaxe to be mined.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Nether Gold Ore|pickaxe|wood}}
Nether gold ore drops 2-6 [[gold nugget]]s when mined with any [[pickaxe]]. [[Fortune]] has a 33.3% chance to multiply the drops by 2, Fortune II has a 25% chance to multiply the drops by 2 or 3 and Fortune III has a 20% chance each to multiply the [[drops]] by 2, 3, or 4 for a maximum possible drop of 24 golden nuggets. The average number of nuggets from Fortune III is 8.8, which is less than an ingot; therefore it is slightly more efficient to mine nether gold ore with [[Silk Touch]] and smelt it into [[gold ingots]].

Nether gold ore also drops {{xp|0|1}} when broken.

== Usage ==
=== Piglins ===
[[Piglin]]s become hostile toward [[player]]s that [[breaking|mine]] the ore, regardless if the player is wearing [[gold]] [[armor]] or is under the {{EffectLink|Invisibility}} effect. The same case applies if the player mines gold of any type, not just the ore. If any nether gold ore is dropped and in its [[Item (entity)|item]] form, piglins will run towards any nether gold ore and examine it for 6 to 8 seconds after picking it up.

== Trivia ==
[[File:Nether Gold Ore (original suggestion).png|thumb|96px|Nether gold ore as it appeared in the original suggestion.]]
*In 2017, [[Jeb]] showed interest in a suggestion regarding nether gold ore that was posted on [[wikipedia:Reddit|Reddit]].<ref>{{reddit|sub=minecraftsuggestions|5wzhty/rare_gold_ore_in_the_nether_concept_texture|deex5wk|I like this suggestion.  I would even go further and make the gold ore blocks appear in larger areas (as veins), and not speckled everywhere in [[the Nether]]. I agree on the aggro'ing of Pigman Zombies, it makes it more interesting.|jeb_|March 2, 2017}}</ref>
**This comment also suggested that nearby [[zombie pigmen]], the [[mob]] now currently known as [[zombified piglin]]s, should become angry at the [[player]] when they [[breaking|mine]] nether gold ore. Jeb also showed interest in this aspect of the suggestion, and this may have been the inspiration for [[piglin]]s becoming angry when gold of any type is mined near them.
**Nether Gold Ore requires a stone pickaxe or better to get gold, despite overworld [[Gold Ore]] needing an iron pickaxe or better.
