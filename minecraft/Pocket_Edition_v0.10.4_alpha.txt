'''v0.10.4 alpha''' was released to fix some bugs and crashes in [[Pocket Edition v0.10.3 alpha|v0.10.3]]. For iOS, this version includes changes from [[Pocket Edition v0.10.1 alpha|v0.10.1]], [[Pocket Edition v0.10.2 alpha|v0.10.2]] and v0.10.3, along with support for the iPhone 6 Plus's screen resolution. This is also the first version released for the Windows Phone.

== Changes ==

* App icon reverted to 0.8.0 look (shiny effect)
* Native support for iPhone 6 Plus
