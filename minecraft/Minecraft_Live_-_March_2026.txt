'''Minecraft Live - March 2026''' was an event that took place on March 21, 2026. It featured more information, including the release date about [[Tiny Takeover]], special guests, and some upcoming contents.

== Summary ==
The stream begins with an introduction, panning the animation, starting with the [[village]] flying past several characters including [[Agnes Larsson|Agnes]]' skin and skins resembling [[Jens Bergensten|Jeb]] and the [[villager]]s, before moving on to several other locations, such as bridge in [[the Nether]], a portal to [[cherry grove]] with a pagoda, an underground where [[Steve]] gets blown up by a [[creeper]], a shipwreck where [[Noor]] and [[Zuri]] travels to meet up with the [[nautilus]]es, and a [[mangrove swamp]] where the logo appears right after. The show then cuts to several areas of [[Mojang Studios]], with various new baby mobs superimposed over the footage. Jeb introduces himself and welcomes the viewers into the show, remarks the community's interest in the [[Tiny Takeover|first drop of 2026]]. He also tease for the [[Chaos Cubed|second drop of 2026]] that brings an [[Sulfur Cube|entirely new mechanic]], and thanking the players for their non-stop, continued dedication to the game. It then moves on to the first announcement, in-game social parties.

=== Parties ===
[[Laura Taranto]] explains how [[multiplayer]] works, as multiple players hang out with each other. They then introduce parties, a new feature where players can create and join parties.

=== Tiny Takeover ===
{{Main|Tiny Takeover}}
[[Agnes Larsson]] recaps the new features of Tiny Takeover: Craftable [[name tags]], baby mob and [[rabbit]] remodels, and [[golden dandelion]]s. She then announces that it will be released on March 24.

[[Jasper Boerstra]] makes an appearance, saying the name of a game drop. [[Felix Jones|Felix]] and [[Sarah Boeving|Sarah]] starts listing off the mobs they include on the update, ending with a [[villager|baby villager]] standing on the [[orange terracotta]]. It cuts to [[Sandra Karlsson]], who is on a field duty recording real-life animals. She recounts her experiences with a cow whisperer, and the time she got bitten by pigs. Jasper then explains the topic about golden dandelion, a new dandelion flower variant that can be crafted with a [[dandelion]] surrounded by 8 [[gold nuggets]] and keeps the baby mobs forever young. It then transitions to the [https://youtu.be/7A5YWn33eps cinematic trailer].

=== Chaos Cubed ===
{{Main|Chaos Cubed}}
Agnes returns to discuss the next game drop. She hints the viewers with a pan through [[desert]] to a [[cave]], then reveals the newest underground biome addition: the [[sulfur caves]].

Daniel Jansson talks about the unique color palette of [[sulfur]] and [[cinnabar]] block sets. Sulfur blocks is a group of sickly yellowish-colored blocks, while cinnabar blocks is a group of reddish-hue colored blocks. Both blocks can be crafted into [[slabs]], [[stairs]], [[walls]], [[polished]], [[Brick (disambiguation)|bricks]], and [[chiseled]] variants. The biome, later inspired by a hot spring, through a [[potent sulfur]] block boiling water. The potent sulfur block gives [[player]]s and [[mob]]s [[nausea]] when they get too close to or enter the sulfur pool, one of the features of the sulfur cave. They then discuss about [[sulfur cubes]], a new passive slime-like mob that absorbs [[block]]s, and can be treated as "bouncy cubes". Another feature of the sulfur cube is that when a large size dies, it splits into two, which then grow back to a larger size, unlike [[slime]]s and [[magma cube]]s.

The game drop was later revealed to be called "[[Chaos Cubed]]".

=== Minecraft Dungeons II ===
{{Main|Minecraft Dungeons II}}
Mojang announced that [[Minecraft Dungeons II]], a new RPG dungeon crawler serving a sequel to [[Minecraft Dungeons]], is scheduled for Fall 2026 release.

=== The Deep Dig ===
An aftershow where [[Jens Bergensten|Jens]], [[Vu Bui|Vu]], Agnes, and several content creators preview the [[Chaos Cubed]] game drop and the new features of [[Tiny Takeover]].
