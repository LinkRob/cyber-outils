A '''delta''' is a one-block-deep sheet of constrained [[lava]] found among the terrain of [[basalt deltas]] biomes. 

== Description ==

Deltas look like lava terraces which are smooth in elevation. The terraces can be small or large. [[Magma blocks]] sometimes generate in it. It tends to be adjacent to [[basalt column]]s.
