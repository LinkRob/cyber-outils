Sets the time before idle [[players]] are kicked from the [[server]].

==Syntax==

:<code>setidletimeout <minutes></code>

==Argument==

{{argument|minutes|integer}}
: {{arg desc|je=integer|min=0}}
: Specifies the idle kick timer.
:If 0, idletimeout is disabled.

==Result==

Always succeeds. Set the idle kick timer. Any players idle for <code><minutes></code> are kicked.
