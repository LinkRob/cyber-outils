'''1.5.25'''<ref>{{cite|url=https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360004593551|title=Minecraft: New Nintendo 3DS Edition - 1.5|date=June 5, 2018}}</ref> was an update for the {{el|ds}} released on June 5/6, 2018, which added [[coarse dirt]], [[glass|stained glass]], and a store for DLC packs.

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[Coarse Dirt]]
* Grass cannot grow on it.
* Can be crafted from 2 gravel and 2 sand.

; [[Stained Glass]]
* Comes in 16 different colors.

; [[Stained Glass Pane]]
* Comes in 16 different colors.

=== General ===
* Added Super Mario Mash-up pack.
* Cartoon and Fantasy [[texture pack]]s are available for purchase.
* Campfire Tales, City Folk and Town Folk Skin packs are available for purchase.

== Changes ==
=== General ===
* Improved stability and performance.

== Fixes ==
* Numerous bug fixes from previous builds.
