The term '''Aquatic''' refers to an in-game group of [[mob|mobs]] that live in [[ocean|oceans]] and [[river|rivers]] and are able to swim.

== Classification ==
They take extra damage from tridents enchanted with [[Impaling]],‌{{until|Java Edition Combat Tests}} and, except axolotl, do not cause pufferfish to inflate nor take damage from them. All aquatic mobs except dolphins are immune to drowning, and all except guardians, elder guardians, and turtles take suffocation damage if out of water for too long. Aquatic mobs have the ability to swim, whereas other mobs simply float on water or sink.

Although [[drowned]] are an underwater variant of zombies (and can swim {{in|bedrock}}), they are classified as undead, not as aquatic.

In Bedrock Edition, there is no in-game classification for these mobs.

== Trivia ==
* [[Coral]]s and [[sea pickle]]s instantly die if they are out of water for too long, similar to the aquatic mobs.
* [[Frog]]s are not considered an aquatic mob, while [[tadpole]]s are. Despite this, frogs will not drown in water.
* Being an undead mob, zombie nautiluses burn when moved out of the water during the day.
