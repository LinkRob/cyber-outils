'''Lava Chicken Add-on''' is a free [[Marketplace]] add-on that features "Steve's Lava Chicken" from the [[A Minecraft Movie|film]]. It is released by Spark Universe on December 20, 2024.

== Description ==
{{Quote|This chicken got cooked - will you? Feast on some lava chicken to turn up the heat and leave a... spicy trail for your foes. Can you win the battle before the heat overwhelms you? Just watch your step around your beloved builds.|Minecraft [[Marketplace]] description}}

== Additions ==
* Steve's Lava Chicken
** A new food item that leaves a trail of [[fire]] for seconds when eaten.
** Automatically obtained to a [[player]] upon activating the add-on.
*** Can also be crafted with a [[Lava Bucket|lava bucket]] and a [[Raw Chicken|raw chicken]].
**** Can produce 4 of them.
*** Also includes a warning chat message to not eat it beside or close to flammable blocks.
** Like any [[food]] items, it can be eaten.
*** Gives a "[[Resistance]] III" effect, forces to move forward in a fast speed, and leaves a trail of fire for seconds when eaten, dealing fire damage to targeting mobs.

== Trivia ==
* On December 31, 2025, this, along with other [[A Minecraft Movie]] DLCs had been delisted in Minecraft [[Marketplace]].
