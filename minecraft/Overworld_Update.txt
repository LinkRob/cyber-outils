The '''Overworld Update''' is the name for ''[[Pocket Edition|Minecraft: Pocket Edition]]'' version [[Pocket Edition v0.14.0 alpha|Alpha v0.14.0]], a major update released on February 18, 2016.

The official name for the update, the ''Overworld Update'', was revealed on February 10, 2016 in a blog post on the Xbox website.<ref>https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2016/02/10/minecraft-overworld-update-coming/</ref>

== Notable features ==

{{main|Pocket Edition v0.14.0 alpha}}

=== Additions ===

* Various additions to options.
* 10 new blocks:
** [[Comparator]]s, [[Dispenser]]s, [[Dropper]]s, [[Hopper]]s, [[Minecart with chest]], [[Minecart with TNT]], [[Minecart with hopper]], [[Cauldron]]s, [[Red sandstone]] (alongside its variants), and [[Slime block]]s.
* 3 new items:
** [[Item frame]]s, [[Map]]s, and [[Ender pearl]]s.
* [[Witch hut]]s.
* [[Witch]]es.

=== Changes ===

* Various changes to mobs, blocks, and items.

== Further revisions ==

* [[Pocket Edition v0.14.1 alpha|Alpha v0.14.1]] added the ''Minecraft: Story Mode'' skin pack.
* [[Pocket Edition v0.14.2 alpha|Alpha v0.14.2]] fixed some bugs and crashes from Alpha v0.14.1.
* [[Pocket Edition v0.14.3 alpha|Alpha v0.14.3]] added the Biome Settlers Pack 2 skin pack.
