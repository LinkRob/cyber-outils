'''1.16.42''' is a minor update to {{BE}} released only for the PlayStation 4 on September 22, 2020, which added support for the PlayStation VR.<ref>{{cite|url=https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049439612-Minecraft-1-16-42-PlayStation-VR-|title=Minecraft - 1.16.42 (PlayStation®VR)|date=September 22, 2020}}</ref> This version was re-released immediately after to fix further bugs.<ref>{{cite|url=https://games-guides.com/minecraft-update-version-2-14-patch-notes-on-september/|title=Minecraft Update 2.14 Patch Notes on September 22|website=Games-Guides.com|date=September 22, 2020}}</ref>

== Additions == 
=== General ===
* Added support for the PlayStation VR on the PlayStation 4.
** Wireless Controller are used to play in PS VR.<ref>{{cite|url=https://help.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049291652|title=Minecraft on PlayStation®VR FAQ|website=Minecraft Help Center}}</ref>

;[[Options]]
* Added "PlayStation VR" settings, as following:
** Center Cursor
*** Toggles to set the cursor to the center.
** Camera Movement
*** Chooses the toggle between "Snap", "Classic", "Wheel", and "Snap Wheel".
** Snap Angle
*** Toggles the size of each camera movement.
** Variable Snap Angle
*** Toggles the size of each camera movement should change based on how far the player push Right Stick.
** Snap Sound
*** Toggles sound to play each time the camera direction is changed.
** Movement
*** Chooses the toggle between "Linear" and "Classic" movement behavior.
** Jump
*** Chooses the toggle between "Linear" and "Classic" jump behavior.
** Immersive Mode Auto-Jump
*** Toggles the auto-jump feature, while on Immersive Mode.
** Head Steering
*** Chooses the toggle between "Follow Gaze" and "Follow Player Angle".
** Sticky Mining
*** Chooses the toggle between "Lock-On" and "Disabled".
** HUD Position
*** Chooses the toggle between "Drift" and "Fixed".
** HUD Distance
*** Controls how far from the center of the player's vision the HUD displays.

== Fixes ==
; 2.13 fixes
* Fixed trophy translations for several languages

; 2.14 fixes
* Minor bug and crash fixes.
