An '''azalea tree''' is a rare [[tree]] that can generate above a [[lush cave]] biome, marking the biome's presence above ground. They extend their roots underground into the cave beneath.

== Generation ==
[[File:Azalea Tree with Roots.png|thumb|right|upright|The rooted dirt and hanging roots that generate under an azalea tree.]]

Azalea trees generate on any empty space above a [[lush cave]], including in biomes that are not grassy or underground.<ref>{{bug|MC-238499||Azalea trees can generate in any (surface) biome|WAI}}</ref> Their roots consist of [[rooted dirt]] and [[hanging roots]] that reach all the way down to the lush cave at [[cavern]] systems such as [[ravine]]s and [[abandoned mineshaft]]s. The trees appear most commonly at the surface, but they can also generate inside caves given that there is enough room.

== Growth ==
Using [[bone meal]] on an [[azalea]] or [[flowering azalea]] eventually grows an azalea tree, replacing the block below where the azalea was grown with a single rooted dirt. It does not need to be over a lush caves biome, instead working in any biome like [[sapling]]s.

== Blocks and items ==
These items can be obtained from azalea trees:

* {{BlockLink|Azalea Leaves}} (harvested with [[shears]])
* {{BlockLink|Flowering Azalea Leaves}} (harvested with [[shears]])
* {{BlockLink|Azalea}} (from azalea leaves)
* {{BlockLink|Flowering Azalea}} (from flowering azalea leaves)
* {{BlockLink|Oak Log}} (harvested with any tool, including hands)
* {{BlockLink|Oak Planks}} (created from a log in the inventory crafting grid)
* {{BlockLink|Stripped Oak Log}} (created by {{control|using}} an [[axe]] on a log)
* {{BlockLink|Rooted Dirt}} (harvested with any tool, including hands)
* {{BlockLink|Hanging Roots}} (harvested with shears)
