'''v0.9.4 alpha''' was released to fix some of the bugs and crashes in [[Pocket Edition v0.9.3 alpha|v0.9.3 alpha]] on iOS and Android. For iOS devices, this version includes changes from v0.9.3.

== Changes ==

* When returning to the game after locking the device, going to the home screen or switching apps, the game opens up in the pause menu.
