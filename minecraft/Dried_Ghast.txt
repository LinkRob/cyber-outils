A '''dried ghast''' is a block added in [[Chase the Skies]]. It can be found next to [[Nether Fossil]]s in the [[Soul Sand Valley]], and turns into a [[Happy Ghast|ghastling]] when soaked in water over time. It was revealed in Minecraft Live - March 2025.<ref>[https://youtube.com/watch?v=jGNgh5zEFkY Minecraft LIVE - March 2025]</ref>

=== Bartering ===
Dried Ghasts are also obtained via [[Bartering|bartering]] with [[Piglin]]s.

== Usage ==
When dried ghasts are placed under [[Water]], they turn into Ghastlings. This process takes 20 minutes or so. There are four stages of the dried ghast when soaked in water.

Stages:-
* Dried stage (default stage)
* Partially hydrated
* Mostly hydrated
* Hydrated (After this stage, a [[Happy Ghast|ghastling]] spawns up.)

If the dried ghast is in between its hydration stages and the player breaks it, it will simply go back to its default stage. Removing water from the dried ghast during its hydration stages also reverts it back down to its default stage.
