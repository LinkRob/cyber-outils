{{EntitySprite|Skeleton}} A '''[[skeleton]]''' is a hostile undead mob that attacks with bows and arrows.

'''Skeleton''' may also refer to:

* {{EntityLink|Bogged}}, a skeleton variant that spawns in swamps and uses arrows of Poison
* {{EntityLink|Stray}}, a skeleton variant that spawns in snowy biomes and uses arrows of Slowness
* {{EntityLink|Wither skeleton}}, a tall skeleton variant that spawns in [[nether fortress]]es and inflicts the Wither effect
* {{EntityLink|Parched}}, a skeleton variant that spawns in deserts and uses arrows of Weakness 
* {{EntityLink|Spider jockey}}, a skeleton, stray, or wither skeleton riding a spider
* {{EntityLink|Skeleton horseman}}, a skeleton riding a skeleton horse
* {{EntityLink|Skeleton horse}}, an undead horse variant.
* {{EntityLink|Wither}}, an undead skeleton-like boss that fires wither skulls.
* {{BlockLink|Skeleton skull}}, a block that is obtained from a skeleton killed by a charged creeper, but can generate naturally in an [[Ancient City]].
* {{BlockLink|Wither skeleton skull}}, a block that is rarely obtained from defeating a wither skeleton.
* {{EnvLink|Fossil}}, an Overworld structure that consists of Bone Blocks and Coal Ores
* {{EnvLink|Nether Fossil}}, a Nether structure that consists of Bone Blocks
