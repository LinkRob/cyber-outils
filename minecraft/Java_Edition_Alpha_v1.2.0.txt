'''Alpha v1.2.0''', also known as the '''[[Halloween Update]]''', is a major update to Minecraft. It was released with [[Alpha server 0.2.2|server 0.2.2]] on October 30, 2010, adding [[the Nether]] along with new Nether-exclusive mobs and blocks. It also added [[fishing]], a new terrain generator, and other changes and fixes. This version is listed in the [[Minecraft Launcher|launcher]], but only [[Alpha v1.2.0_02]] is downloaded instead.

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[File:Cobblestone Stairs (N) JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Stairs]] and [[File:Furnace (S) JE1.png|32px]] [[furnace]]s
* Now face the same direction as the player when placed.

; [[File:Grass Block JE3.png|32px]] [[Grass Block]] and [[File:Oak Leaves JE3 BE4.png|32px]] [[Leaves]]
* Are now darker in shade, and their color is now dependent on temperature/humidity values.

=== Mobs ===
; [[Mob]]s
* Higher lighting values are required the deeper they are to keep mobs from spawning.

; [[File:Creeper JE2 BE1.png|28px]] [[Creeper]]s
* Now have new sounds.

; [[File:Skeleton JE2.png|32px]] [[Skeleton]]s
* Now hold [[bow]]s.

; [[File:Slime JE2 BE1.png|32px]] [[Slime]]s
* Completely removed from mob spawning code.

=== World generation ===
* Fake chunks spawn at 32,000,000 blocks instead of the void.
** They cannot be interacted with.
** They are not solid.
** Lighting stops working.
** Trees, flowers, grass, and dungeons will stop generating behind this point.

=== Gameplay ===
; [[Sunrise]]
* Sunrise now creates a red glow at the horizon.
; [[Heads-up display]]
* Pressing {{key|F1}} hides the HUD and the view-model from the person's view. In third person mode, pressing {{key|F1}} will create a view looking back at the player's own face.

=== General ===
; [[Menu screen]]
* Changed ''Minecraft'' logo from [[File:Java Edition logo 5.png|x20px]] to [[File:Java Edition logo 6.png|x20px]].

== Removals ==
=== General ===
* Removed [[Winter Mode]].
* The player "ough" sound no longer plays when the player takes damage.

; [[Java Edition distance effects/Historical effects|Distance effects]]
* Due to the removal of winter mode and therefore snow, it no longer becomes visually distorted at great heights.
