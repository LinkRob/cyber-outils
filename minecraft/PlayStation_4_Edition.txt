'''''Minecraft: PlayStation 4 Edition''''' is the {{el|lce}} version of ''Minecraft'' developed by [[4J Studios]] and [[Mojang Studios]] for the {{w|PlayStation 4}}.<ref>[[4J Studios]], [{{tweet|4JStudios|370111405216649216}} "Happy to confirm we're developing Minecraft PS3, PS4 & PS Vita Editions for Mojang, as well as Xbox 360 & Xbox One Editions!"], ''Twitter'', 2013-08-21.</ref> It was announced on August 20, 2013 at Sony Interactive Entertainment's exhibit at {{w|Gamescom}} 2013 and was available for purchase on the PlayStation Store for $19.99 between September 4, 2014 and December 9, 2019.<ref>{{tweet|Dinnerbone|369887393253699584|Minecraft was just announced for Playstation 3 & 4!…"|August 20, 2013}}</ref> Existing digital owners are still able to access, re-download, and play this version. A retail version was released on October 3, 2014 and can still be purchased. DLC for the game is still available on the PlayStation Store. Like the [[Xbox One Edition|''Xbox One'']] and [[Nintendo Switch Edition|''Nintendo Switch'']] editions players, the ''PlayStation 4 Edition'' owners have the option to download the [[Bedrock Edition|Bedrock]] version for free, however not all DLC will transfer. As with many games that are released on the PlayStation 4, ''Minecraft: PlayStation 4 Edition'' can be played on the PlayStation Vita and PlayStation TV through Remote Play.

Compared to the ''[[PlayStation 3 Edition]]'', ''PlayStation 4 Edition'' includes [[world]] sizes up to 5120×5120 blocks (36 times larger), 18 [[chunk]] [[render distance]], [[amplified]] worlds, and other enhancements offered by the PlayStation 4. The ''PlayStation 4 Edition'' has all [[Legacy Console Edition exclusive features]], the most notable being [[Mini games]].

As of [[PlayStation 4 Edition 1.95|1.95]], the ''PlayStation 4 Edition'' has not received new feature updates, such as [[Buzzy Bees]], the [[Nether Update]], [[Caves & Cliffs]], and so on. The ''PlayStation 4 Edition'' is playable on the {{w|PlayStation 5}} via backward compatibility.

The ''PlayStation 4 Edition'''s most recent feature update is 1.14, or [[Village & Pillage]].

==Controls==
{{main|Controls#Legacy Console Edition}}
In default settings, the [[controls]] are always displayed on the [[Heads-up display|HUD]]. The controls for ''PlayStation 4 Edition'' are similar to ''[[PlayStation 3 Edition]]'' and alongside Touch pad support.

==Gameplay==
The game is similar to ''[[PlayStation 3 Edition]]'', but supports the enhancements offered by the PlayStation 4. Players were able to transfer and expand upon worlds created on [[PlayStation 3 Edition]] and [[PlayStation Vita Edition]], however this feature was terminated in April 15, 2020.
