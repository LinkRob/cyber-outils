Breaking a fall is an essential of '''''[[Minecraft]]''''' because they are always one of the most common causes of death.

== Ways to Break a Fall ==
=== Block Clutch ===

===== Hay Bale Clutch =====
{{main|Hay Bale}}
This clutch uses {{BlockLink|hay bale}}s to at least lessen the damage.

=== Elytra ===
If the player is wearing an [[elytra]] or has one in their inventory, they can break their fall by gliding safely to the ground.

=== Slow Falling Potion ===
If the fall is great enough, there may be sufficient time to drink a [[potion]] of [[Slow Falling|slow falling]] before hitting the ground.

=== Boat Clutch ===
This involves placing a [[boat]] and entering it moments before the player hits the ground. If done correctly it can completely negate fall damage.
