A version of '''[[Minecraft Indev]]''' was released on '''February 11, 2010'''.

==Additions==
===Gameplay===
; [[Day/night cycle]]
* Each cycle lasts about 15 minutes each.<ref>[[wordofnotch:384494353|Day/night cycle in indev!]] – The Word of Notch, February 11, 2010</ref>
** 7.5 minutes of sun and 7.5 minutes of moon each cycle.
* Dictates when certain mobs spawn.

==Changes==
===Mobs===
* Added [[passive mob]] sounds.
** Prior to this update, they shared the [[player]]'s hurt sound.
* Sheep spawn with wool.<ref>{{ytl|9ZUQysClROM|Minecraft Indev|Chudooder|February 11, 2010}}</ref>
** They now drop 1-3 wool when punched, each piece being either white, light gray, or gray.

== Bugs ==
*The light level often wouldn't match the time of day.
*In some instances, performance would deteriorate and the game becomes near-unplayable as a result.
