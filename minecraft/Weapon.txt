'''Weapons''' are items that can be used to [[damage]] [[entities]]. They can take the form of a sword, bow, or various other items capable of dealing damage. They can also be [[enchanted]] to deal more damage or add other benefits.

There are various types of weapons:

== Melee weapons ==
Melee weapons can be held while punching to deal extra damage, but with a longer attack time than punching. (attack time is a cooldown time for a weapon)

* {{ItemLink|Sword}}s are generally the most effective melee weapon, due to their high damage per second{{only|je}} compared to other weapons. All swords have a cooldown time of 0.6 seconds, 60% faster than axes.{{only|java}} Swords are the only weapon type that can do [[Sword|sweep attacks]] to hit multiple targets. {{IN|be}}, they deal more damage than any other melee weapon except for {{ItemSprite|Trident}} [[trident]]s.

* {{ItemLink|Axe}}s {{In|je}} deal more damage per hit than their sword counterparts, but have a slower cooldown. Wooden and stone axes have a cooldown time of 1.2 seconds, 80% slower than swords. Iron axes have a cooldown time of 1.1 seconds, 70% slower than swords. Golden, diamond, and netherite axes have a cooldown time of 1 second, 60% slower than swords. Axes are the only weapon type that can disable a {{ItemLink|shield}}, which can be lengthened with [[Cleaving]]{{upcoming|Java Combat Tests}}. {{IN|be}}, axes deal one less damage than their sword counterparts.

* {{ItemLink|Trident}}s can be swung, dealing the same damage as an unenchanted stone, iron, or diamond axe{{only|je}} or the same damage as an unenchanted {{ItemLink|netherite sword}} {{only|be}}, and when thrown they always do the same damage as an unenchanted {{ItemLink|netherite sword}}. They have 10% faster attack speed than golden, diamond, and netherite axes{{only|je}}, being 0.9 seconds. Unlike most other [[projectile]]s, they are not slowed down in water, but a thrown trident must be retrieved, unless enchanted with [[Loyalty]].

* {{ItemLink|Mace}}s ... (info needed here)

* Other [[tool]]s, like {{ItemLink|hoe}}s, {{ItemLink|pickaxe}}s, and {{ItemLink|shovel}}s can be used as weapons, but they are not as effective as other melee weapons.

* [[Sword]]s and [[trident]]s use one [[durability]] point per hit, while [[axe]]s, [[pickaxe]]s, [[hoe]]s and [[shovel]]s use two.

== Ranged weapons ==
Ranged weapons are not as effective in close combat, but they are effective at attacking enemies from a distance.

* {{ItemLink|Bow}}s are the most common ranged weapon, shooting {{ItemLink|arrow}}s at enemies to deal damage. Bows have a charge up mechanic, similar to melee weapons, and arrows go the farthest and deal the most damage when shot from a fully charged bow.

* {{ItemLink|Crossbow}}s have a longer range than bows, and deal more damage, but take longer to charge when unenchanted. The charging time can be drastically reduced with the [[Quick Charge]] enchantment to charge far faster than a bow. They can also shoot {{ItemLink|firework rocket}}s to deal splash damage, but unlike the bow, crossbows cannot fire a partially charged attack; the player must fully charge a crossbow to load it and fire. Furthermore, a crossbow can be charged and put away to be fired later.

* {{EntityLink|Fireball}}s cannot be launched by a player, but can be fired by a {{BlockLink|dispenser}} or shot from a {{EntityLink|ghast}} or {{EntityLink|blaze}} to explode and set its surroundings on fire upon impact. Ghast fireballs can also be used to damage terrain, but can be deflected. The {{EntityLink|ender dragon}} uses {{EntityLink|dragon fireball}}s, a special type of fireball that is purple and releases a purple lingering effect cloud similar to that of a {{ItemLink|lingering potion of harming|link=Potion of Harming}} when it explodes. This particle cloud can be collected using a {{ItemLink|glass bottle}} to get {{ItemLink|dragon's breath}}.

*{{ItemLink|Splash potion}}s, {{ItemLink|lingering potion}}s, or {{ItemLink|tipped arrow}}s with the {{EffectLink|Instant Damage}}, {{EffectLink|Poison}}, or {{EffectLink|Wither|link=Wither (status effect)}}{{only|bedrock}} status effects can be effectively used to damage enemies. Other effects like {{EffectLink|Weakness}}, {{EffectLink|Slowness}}, and {{EffectLink|Slow Falling}} can be used to significantly hinder an opponent in a fight.

* {{ItemLink|Snowball}}s and {{ItemLink|egg}}s do not deal any damage, but can damage {{ItemLink|id=Chestplate|armor}} and deal [[Damage#Knockback|knockback]],{{only|be|short=1}}{{upcoming|Java Combat Tests}} and unlike a {{ItemSprite|bow}} bow, can be thrown quickly. {{ItemLink|Ender pearl}}s can be used, but they teleport the player to the entity.

== Other weapons ==
Other items listed below can be used as weapons but usually have other intended uses and should be used strategically.

* {{ItemLink|End crystal}}s and {{BlockLink|respawn anchor}}s can be used to deal excessive explosive damage to targets in quick succession.
*{{BlockLink|Cobweb}}s can be used to drastically slow the movement speed of targets.
*{{ItemLink|Flint and steel}}s and {{ItemLink|fire charge}}s can be used to set the ground on fire and damage enemies.
* A {{ItemLink|Lava bucket}} can deal serious damage to enemies and significantly slow them down, but it requires strategic placing. In certain situations, it can be used in conjunction with a {{ItemLink|water bucket}} to suffocate the entity in {{BlockLink|obsidian}}.
*{{BlockLink|TNT}} is an effective weapon against enemies, whether planted and ignited on the spot or buried beforehand and activated in a chase. The player can substitute this with a {{EntityLink|minecart with TNT}}, {{EntityLink|creeper}}, or a {{ItemLink|bed}} when used outside of the Overworld.
* [[Hostile mobs]] can be used as weapons when used strategically, but this can be hard to set up due to the fact that these mobs also attempt to attack the player.
* {{ItemLink|Redstone}} traps can be used to kill the entity with all sorts of environmental damage, ranging from fall damage to suffocation to anything in between, but these must be prepared beforehand.
*Anvils can be used to damage mobs and players, at the cost of damaging the anvil.
* Certain [[block]]s cause damage on contact, and can be used as barriers to mobs and players without the ability to break blocks. These include a {{BlockLink|cactus}}, a {{BlockLink|sweet berry bush}}, a {{BlockLink|wither rose}}, a {{BlockLink|campfire}} and a {{BlockLink|magma block}} (if the target isn't [[sneaking]] or wearing [[frost walker]] boots).
