'''v0.15.9''' is an update that added [[cape]]s and a new [[Skin/Skin pack|Skin pack]], as well as fixed bugs and crashes in [[Pocket Edition v0.15.8 alpha|v0.15.8]].<ref>[https://www.mojang.com/2016/09/minecon-2016-skins-incoming-get-em-while-theyre-hot "MINECON 2016 skin pack incoming! Get 'em while they're hot!"] - Mojang, September 22, 2016</ref>

== Additions ==
* [[Cape]]s support

===[[Skin/Skin pack|Skin packs]]===
* {{Version exclusive}}: [[Skin/Skin pack#MINECON 2016 Skin Pack|MINECON 2016 Skin Pack]], available for a limited time.
** The [[MINECON]] cape skins can be worn by anyone with the [[MINECON]] skin pack, even if they did not go to [[MINECON]].
** As of October 7th, 2016, the limited time offer for the [[skin pack]] is over, it has been removed from all app stores, and its redemption code is now expired. Players who obtained it while it was available are still able to use it.
