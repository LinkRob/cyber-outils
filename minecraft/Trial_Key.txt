A '''trial key''' is an item found in [[trial chambers]]. It is mainly used to unlock [[vault]]s.

==Obtaining==
The trial key can be obtained from [[trial spawner]]s (50%), or from [[decorated pot]]s and [[chest]]s (8%).

==Usage==
The trial key is used in the [[trial chambers]] to open [[vault|trial vault]]s, which give good loot.<ref>[https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/trial-key]</ref> Using one on any trial vault will result in the key being removed from the player's inventory. Only one trial key is required per vault. Once it is opened and loot dispensed, it cannot regain loot or take trial keys. 

==Appearance==
A trial key has a rocky, skull-like face, most likely made of tuff, at the head of the key, and a long copper shaft below it.

==Trivia==
* When trial keys were first introduced in snapshot 23w45a, their functionality remained a mystery for a pretty good time.
** However, in the Minecraft Monthly of January 2024, it was revealed that they open vaults, which, once unlocked, will drop valuable loot like [[armor]], [[emerald]]s, [[crossbow]]s, [[wind charge]]s, etc.
