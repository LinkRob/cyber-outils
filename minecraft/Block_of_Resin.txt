A '''Block of Resin''' is a decorative building [[block]] added in [[The Garden Awakens]] game drop.

== Obtaining ==
=== Crafting ===
Resin blocks are crafted with 9 [[Resin Clump]]s.

{{Crafting
|A1= Resin Clump
|B1= Resin Clump
|C1= Resin Clump
|A2= Resin Clump
|B2= Resin Clump
|C2= Resin Clump
|A3= Resin Clump
|B3= Resin Clump
|C3= Resin Clump
|Output=Block of Resin
|type= Building block
}}

== Usage ==
Resin blocks are mainly used to craft [[creaking heart]]s. This is done by combining them with 2 [[pale oak]] [[log]]s placed both above and below in a crafting table.

=== Crafting ===
{{Crafting
|A1= 
|B1= Pale Oak Log
|C1= 
|A2= 
|B2= Block of Resin
|C2= 
|A3= 
|B3= Pale Oak Log
|C3= 
|Output=Creaking Heart
|type= Spawn Eggs
}}

== Appearance ==
Resin blocks appear bright, contrasting orange, similar to [[lava]], but they are not animated. In fact, it appears slightly conglomerated with resin clumps tightly packed. [[Player]]s may confuse resin blocks with baked beans, due to their unique cobbled texture and colours.
