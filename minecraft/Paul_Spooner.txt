'''Paul Spooner''' is a 3D artist notable for his contributions to the global environmental elements of ''Minecraft'', specifically the iconic large trees.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/0/http://notch.tumblr.com/post/506311060/still-going-slow-but-heres-a-new-screenshot</ref> After being disappointed with his inability to build a tree fort in the small trees, he wrote a map modding tool which was then partially included in the base game.<ref>http://www.peripheralarbor.com/minecraft/minecraftscriptphotojournal.html</ref> Unlike [[Daniel Rosenfeld]], Spooner's in game character does not have a [[Skin#Capes|Mojang cape]]. Spooner has a [http://dudecon.deviantart.com/ deviantART account], and a [https://www.youtube.com/user/peripheralarbor YouTube channel].
