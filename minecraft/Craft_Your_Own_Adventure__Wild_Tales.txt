The '''''Craft Your Own Adventure: Wild Tales''''' is a free ''[[Minecraft]]'' choose your own adventure story promoting [[The Wild Update]]. It is authored by [[Wikipedia:Sofia Dankis|Sofia Dankis]], [[Wikipedia:Cristina Anderca|Cristina Anderca]], [[Wikipedia:Sophie Austin|Sophie Austin]], and [[Wikipedia:Per Landin|Per Landin]]. The story was published on June 4, 2022 through Twitter.<ref>{{Tweet|Minecraft|1533116603012874240|Where in the wild will you wander? The Wild Tales await... Craft your own adventure right now: http://redsto.ne/wild-tales|June 4, 2022}}</ref>
