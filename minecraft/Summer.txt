'''Summer''' is a character featured in ''Minecraft'' novels by [[wikipedia:Max_Brooks|Max Brooks]], first appearing in [[Minecraft: The Mountain|''Minecraft: The Mountain'']].

== Appearance ==
Summer depicts the Minecraft character Alex, as implied in Minecraft: The Mountain.

== Personality ==
Summer is Guy's companion beginning in Minecraft: The Mountain. When she had first spawned into the World of Minecraft, she, unlike Guy, immediately got the resources she needed to survive, and fought off mobs without any shelter for a while. She is much more practical than Guy, and cares more about making it then figuring out the rules for that world. She just pushes onward no matter the circumstances.
