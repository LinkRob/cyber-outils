'''Trial chambers''' are structures generated naturally underground and contain various mobs from the [[Trial Spawner|trial]] and [[ominous trial spawner]]s, including [[breeze]]s, which is an exclusive mob in the trial chamber. They are composed of various [[copper block]]s, [[copper bulb]]s, [[chiseled copper]], [[tuff bricks]], [[chiseled tuff]], [[chiseled tuff bricks]], [[copper door]]s, [[copper trapdoor]]s, [[copper grate]]s, and the blocks that correspond with the mobs that spawn from this structure. This structure contains [[vault]]s and [[ominous vault]]s that can only be opened by a [[trial key]] or an [[ominous trial key]] respectively.
[[File:Trial-Chambers-Minecraft-Snapshot-Slideshow-6.jpg|thumb|A view of the trial chambers.]]

== Locating ==
Trial chambers can be located using an explorer map (which can be bought from a cartographer [[villager]] when the villager reaches Journeyman aka level 3) or via the {{cmd|/locate structure}} commands.

== Mobs ==
[[File:Mobs from trial spawner in trial chambers.jpg|200px|thumb|The mobs that spawn in the trial chambers with their corresponding blocks. The "poison skeleton" is referring to the bogged.]]

[[Breeze]]s, [[silverfish]], [[skeleton]]s, [[stray]]s, [[bogged]], [[zombie]]s, [[husk]]s, [[slime]]s, [[spider]]s, and [[cave spider]]s can be spawned from the [[Trial Spawner|trial]] and [[ominous trial spawner]]s in the trial chambers. They are indicated with their blocks around their spawner.

== Loot ==
Trial chambers carry a large variety of loot and access to potions for low level players. Depending on the spawn biome the Chambers will vary in difficulty. For example, in colder regions stray might spawn from trial spawners providing little difficulty for the player adept at shield use but, Bogged found in swampy regions provide much more difficulty.
