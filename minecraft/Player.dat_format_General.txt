** {{nbt|int|Damage}}: The number of uses ''consumed'' (not remaining) of the item's [[Durability|durability]].
** {{nbt|byte|Unbreakable}}: 1 or 0 (true/false) - if true, the item doesn't lose durability when used.
** {{nbt|list|CanDestroy}}: The only blocks this item may break when used by a player in [[Adventure|adventure mode]].
*** {{nbt|string}}: A block ID. Validated the same way as the [[Argument_types#block_predicate|<code>block_predicate</code> command argument]], meaning block states and block tags are accepted, as well as '''client-side only''' block entity NBT tags.
** {{nbt|int|CustomModelData}}: A value used in the <code>"custom_model_data"</code> [[Model#Item tags|item tag]] in the overrides of item models.
