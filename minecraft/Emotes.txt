'''Emotes''' are a part of the [[character creator]] that allows players to perform animations.

== Usage==
The [[character creator]] includes emotes. Several emotes are available for free, purchase, and as [[achievement]] rewards. The player can "equip" up to 4 of their emotes at a time. When in a world, pressing {{key|B}} on a keyboard, {{Xbutton|onedpad-left}},  {{Nsbutton|left}}/{{Nsbutton|dpad-left}} or {{Psbutton|4dpad-left}} on a controller opens the main emote selection menu. A quick-emote selection menu also can be quickly accessed by holding aforementioned emote button and then selecting the corresponding input from the small pop-up window. When using touch controls, tapping [[File:Emote button.png|20px]] button on the top of the screen while in a world opens the emote menu. The player can then tap an emote to perform the animation. There is no quick-emote menu for touch controls.

== Trivia==
*It actually doesn't matter whether the player is submerged (partially or fully) in a [[liquid]] ([[water]] or [[lava]]), [[flying]], falling, [[levitation|levitating]], [[sneaking]], or on dry land, just as long as the player isn't riding anything, the player would still perform the emote.
*In the crossover fighting game [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]], the simple clap emote is used for Steve's clap animation when he loses a battle or if a player resigns from a battle, resulting in a "No Contest" screen.
*The "Celebrate" emote was shown at [[MINECON Live 2019]], but has not been made available.<ref>{{ytl|OZqNaEX8208|t=1h37m5s|MINECON Live 2019|September 28, 2019}}</ref>
