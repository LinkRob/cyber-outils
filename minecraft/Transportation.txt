===Effects===

Using status [[effect]]s from [[potions]] or [[beacons]], walking and sprinting speeds become competitive with other methods of transportation.

Each additional level of {{EffectLink|Speed}} allows the player to walk at nearly the sprinting speed of the previous level, and sprinting with Speed II is only slightly slower than a minecart at full speed or a boosted [[pig]]. With adequate food and potion supplies, sprinting can be more cost-effective than building high-speed transportation systems.

Falling speed is [[Talk:Health#Falling Speed|more complex]]: Every tick ({{frac|1|20}} second), non-flying players and mobs have their vertical speed decremented (less upward motion, more downward motion) by 0.08 blocks per tick (1.6 m/s), then multiplied by 0.98.{{Note|Horses have their vertical speed multiplied by 0.98 a second time in the same tick. This may or may not apply to some other mobs.}} This ''would'' produce a terminal velocity of 3.92 blocks per tick or 78.4 m/s. However, the sky isn't quite high enough for that: Falling from layer 256 to bedrock takes about 5.5 seconds, with impact at 3.5 blocks per tick (70 m/s). It is possible to fly higher in Creative mode, or by flying with elytra and then removing them, reaching terminal velocity falling from "above the sky".
{{notelist}}

An exact function to calculate the speed of a (free)falling player is the following:

<math>v(t) = \frac{392}{5} \times (\frac{98}{100}^{\left\lfloor 20t \right\rfloor} - 1)</math>

Where <math>\lfloor x \rfloor</math> is the [[wp:Floor and ceiling functions|floor function]], the velocity <math>v</math> is in blocks/second (or meters/second), the time <math>t</math> is in seconds, initial velocity is zero and downward motion is negative. <math>20t</math> is floored because Minecraft updates once each tick.

An exact function to calculate the displacement of the player is the following:

<math>d(t) = 392 \times (\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{5}t - \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{49}{50}^{\left\lfloor 20t \right\rfloor})</math>

Where the displacement <math>d</math> is in blocks (meters), the time <math>t</math> is in seconds, initial velocity and displacement is zero and downward displacement is negative. Or:

<math>d(N) = 196 - 196 \times 0.98^{N} - 3.92 \times N</math>

where <math>N</math> is the number of game ticks elapsed. Formula with initial conditions <math>d(N) = y_0 + 50 \times (vt_0 + 3.92) \times (1 - 0.98^N) - 3.92 \times N</math> where <math>y_0</math> is the starting height and <math>vt_0</math> is the upward speed in [m/tick]. Or <math>d(t) = y_0 + (2.5 \times v_0 + 196) \times (1 - 0.98^{20t}) - 78.4t</math> where <math>v_0</math> is given in m/s.

An approximate function to calculate the time it takes to fall from some height is the following:

<math>t(d) = 1000 - 5.102d + 989.960 \times W_{-1}(-0.36786e^{0.0051538d})</math>

Where the height <math>d</math> is in blocks (meters), the time <math>t</math> is in seconds, downward displacement is negative and <math>W</math> is the [[wp:Lambert W function|Lambert W function]].

===Other modes of transportation===

*Players are also able to use [[piston]]s for transport.
**It is possible to construct [[Tutorials/Flying machines|complex flying machines]] using pistons, [[blocks of redstone]], [[slime block]]s, and [[honey block]]s.
*[[Health#Death|Dying]] is an instant mode of transportation to the player's [[bed]] or [[spawn]] point, but at the cost of all items and XP (if the <code>keepInventory</code> gamerule is disabled) and the world (on Hardcore).
**If the player has already defeated the [[ender dragon]], and is near a [[stronghold]]/[[end portal]], they can enter and exit [[the End]] to instantly be transported to their bed or spawn point without dying.
*[[Explosion]] [[knockback]] can send an adjacent player flying for a short distance, depending on the strength of the explosion. This is not recommended in [[Survival mode]].
*It is possible to mount [[minecart]]s and [[boat]]s as long as they are within 4-5 blocks (inclusive) from the player. Placing these in a straight line allows for extremely fast transport. This also works with saddled [[pig]]s, provided they stay in position (placed on posts).
