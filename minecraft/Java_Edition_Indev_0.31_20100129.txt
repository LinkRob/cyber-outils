; [[Smelting]] 
* Done via throwing the item into [[fire]] or [[lava]].<ref name="0129 demo" />
* The player now spawns with a flint and steel.
* Currently the four recipes are:
** Coal ore to 1-3 coal.
** Iron ore to 1-3 iron ingot.
** Gold ore to 1-3 gold ingot.
** Diamond ore to 1-3 diamond.

== Changes ==
=== Items ===
; [[Control]]s
* Right-clicking deposits a single item from a hovering stack.<ref name="WoN 0129 release" />
; General
* Items now stack up to 64.<ref name="0129 demo" />
* Now get picked up quicker and don't die in fire.

; Textures
* Changed gold ingot texture from [[File:Gold Ingot JE1.png|32px]] to [[File:Gold Ingot JE2 BE1.png|32px]]
* Changed iron ingot texture from [[File:Iron Ingot JE1.png|32px]] to [[File:Iron Ingot JE2 BE1.png|32px]]

=== Mobs ===
; [[File:Rana.png|32px]] Rana
* Removed from the game.
** {{cd|Mob}} ID is now given to Black Steve and Steve.

=== World generation ===
; [[Indev house]]
* Removed the [[chests]] dedicated to [[TNT]] and [[wool]].<ref group="note" name="gameplay" />
* Removed all items from chests.<ref group="note" name="gameplay" /></onlyinclude>
