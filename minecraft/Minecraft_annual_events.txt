'''Annual Minecraft events''' (usually called '''Minecraft Live''', and formerly '''MINECON''') are events hosted by [[Mojang Studios]].

The official Minecraft Live web page is available at [https://minecraft.net/live minecraft.net/live].

== MINECON ==
=== 2010 ===
{{main|MINECON 2010}}

==== MinecraftCon ====
[[File:MinecraftCon 2010 logo1.png|thumb|MinecraftCon 2010 logo 1]]
[[File:MinecraftCon 2010 logo2.png|thumb|MinecraftCon 2010 logo 2]]

'''MinecraftCon 2010''' was a spontaneous gathering of ''Minecraft'' fans in Bellevue, Washington, USA on August 31.

It was conceived by [[Notch]] in a blog post.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20160118182136/http://notch.tumblr.com/post/1008498266/how-about-a-somewhat-spontaneous-minecraftcon-2010</ref> It was unofficial, and was held by his family and some fans.

==== MINECON 2010 ====
[[File:MINECON 2010 logo.png|200px|thumb|MINECON 2010 logo]]

'''MINECON 2010''' was a gathering of ''Minecraft'' fans on a Minecraft [[Classic]] server.<ref>http://www.minecraftforum.net/topic/11896-</ref> 

[[mcftopic:11896-|This thread]] on the [[Minecraft Forums]] was an unofficial organization point for the Con.
{{-}}

=== 2011 ===
{{main|MINECON 2011}}
[[File:MINECON 2011 logo.png|thumb|MINECON 2011 logo]]
'''MINECON 2011''' was held at {{w|Mandalay Bay}}, Las Vegas, Nevada, USA on November 18th, coinciding with the official release of the game.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20110515132912/http://notch.tumblr.com/post/5388689217/minecraftcon-2011-in-vegas</ref> Tickets went on sale on August 11, 2011.

MINECON featured many exciting events, including the official release of ''Minecraft'', keynote speeches from members of the ''Minecraft'' community (including one from [[Notch]]), building contests, breakout classes with different ''Minecraft'' topics, costume contests, exhibits, meeting worldwide ''Minecraft'' personalities and commemorative merchandise.

Attendees received an exclusive MINECON 2011 [[cape]], which depicts a [[creeper]].
{{-}}

=== 2012 ===
{{main|MINECON 2012}}
[[File:MINECON 2012 Logo.svg|thumb|MINECON 2012 logo]]

'''MINECON 2012''' was held in {{w|Disneyland Paris}} from November 24–25. The convention was attended by 4,500 people – approximately the same amount as the previous year.<ref>http://www.bbc.co.uk/news/technology-20492574</ref> Tickets went on sale on September 14, 2012.

Attendees received an exclusive MINECON 2012 cape, which depicts a [[pickaxe]].

At MINECON 2012, the [[Redstone Update]] was announced and some of its features were showcased.
{{-}}

=== 2013 ===
{{main|MINECON 2013}}
[[File:MINECON 2013 Logo.png|thumb|MINECON 2013 logo]]

'''MINECON 2013''' was held in Orlando, Florida, USA from November 2–3.

Mojang Studios revealed on June 27 that MINECON 2013 would be held in Orlando, Florida from November 2–3.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20130809010950/https://mojang.com/2013/07/where-in-the-world-is-minecon-2013/</ref> After a lot of mystery, it was officially confirmed by Mojang Studios that MINECON 2013 would be hosted in the {{W|Orange County Convention Center}} (OCCC).<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20130806003705/https://mojang.com/2013/07/minecon-tickets-are-here/</ref>

Early on July 31, Mojang Studios confirmed that tickets would start going on sale later that day. These tickets were said to be released in batches of 3,<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20130801043054/http://minecon.eventbrite.com/</ref> each of those batches selling 2,500 tickets and at $150 a piece (7,500 tickets in total).

On September 5, Mojang Studios posted that attendees of MINECON would receive an exclusive MINECON 2013 cape, which depicts a [[piston]].<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20130819125137/http://help.mojang.com/customer/portal/articles/1246918-minecon-2013 MINECON 2013 support page]</ref>
{{-}}

=== 2015 ===
{{main|MINECON 2015}}
[[File:MINECON 2015 Logo.png|thumb|MINECON 2015 logo]]

'''MINECON 2015''' was held at {{w|ExCeL London}}, London, UK from July 4–5.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20150328051446/https://www.excel-london.co.uk/whats-on/minecon-2015</ref>

On March 30, 2014, it had originally been announced that the next MINECON would take place somewhere in Europe. It was later confirmed that MINECON was unable to be held in 2014, instead it had to be moved to Spring 2015, and was held in London.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20140821162300/https://mojang.com/2014/08/minecon-2015/</ref>

On March 18, 2015, Mojang Studios announced that tickets would be on sale on Friday March 27th and Saturday March 28th, respectively.<ref name="2015Ticket">https://web.archive.org/web/20150324033550/https://mojang.com/2015/03/minecon-2015-tickets-announcement/</ref> The tickets were released in two batches<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20150330173150/http://www.eventbrite.co.uk:80/e/minecon-2015-tickets-16140200775</ref>.

Attendants of MINECON 2015 were given a MINECON 2015 cape, which depicts an [[iron golem]].

At MINECON 2015, some of the features of the [[Combat Update]] were announced and showcased.
{{-}}

=== 2016 ===
{{main|MINECON 2016}}
[[File:MINECON 2016 Logo.png|thumb|MINECON 2016 logo]]

'''MINECON 2016''' was held in Anaheim, California, USA from September 24–25.

On March 7, MINECON 2016 was announced.<ref>[https://youtu.be/1nP328vm5ik MINECON 2016 Announcement] - YouTube</ref>

On April 18, Mojang Studios announced that tickets would be on sale on Friday May 6th and Saturday May 7th, respectively.<ref name="2016Tix">https://web.archive.org/web/20160421093159/http://www.mojang.com/2016/04/minecon-2016-tickets</ref>

Attendees of MINECON 2016 got an exclusive MINECON cape, which depicts an [[enderman]].

At MINECON 2016, the [[Exploration Update]] was announced and some of its features were showcased.
{{-}}

== MINECON Earth ==
=== 2017 ===
{{main|MINECON Earth 2017}}
[[File:MINECON Earth logo.png|thumb|MINECON Earth 2017 logo]]

'''MINECON Earth 2017''' was an online livestream held at the official MINECON website on November 18.

On August 8, 2017, Mojang Studios announced that MINECON would be taking the form of an interactive 90-minute livestream on November 18, dubbed MINECON Earth.<ref>{{Mcnet|announcing-minecon-earth|Announcing... MINECON Earth|August 8, 2017}}</ref> Tickets were free to the event, and attendees were able to purchase MINECON-branded merchandise online to be delivered to their place of residence.

After this announcement came the introduction of "Official Community Events", which allow events such as Minefaire, Minevention and Multiplay's BlockFest to be considered "Official Minecraft Conventions".

At MINECON Earth 2017, [[Update Aquatic]] was announced and some of its features were showcased.

The [[Phantom]] won the community [[mob vote]] and was added in Update Aquatic.
{{-}}

=== 2018 ===
{{main|MINECON Earth 2018}}
[[File:MINECON Earth 2018 Logo.svg|thumb|MINECON Earth 2018 logo]]

'''MINECON Earth 2018''' was an online livestream held at the official MINECON website on September 29.

On April 10, 2018, it was announced that MINECON Earth 2018 would take place on September 29, 2018.<ref>{{Mcnet|minecon-earth-2018-save-date|MINECON Earth 2018: save the date!|April 10, 2018}}</ref>

At MINECON Earth 2018, [[Village & Pillage]] was announced and some of its features were showcased.

The [[Taiga]] biome won the community biome vote, and its features were added along with Village & Pillage.
{{-}}

== MINECON Live ==
=== 2019 ===
[[File:MINECON Live Logo.svg|thumb|MINECON Live logo]]
{{main|MINECON Live 2019}}

'''MINECON Live 2019''' was an online livestream held at the official MINECON website, YouTube, Twitch and Mixer on September 28, at 4 pm UTC.

On May 17, 2019, it was announced that MINECON Live 2019 would take place on September 28, 2019.<ref name=LiveAnnounce>{{Mcnet|announcing----minecon-live|Announcing… MINECON Live|May 17, 2019}}</ref>

The MINECON Live 2019 Founder's Cape (a yellow version of the 2011 cape) was available for free for a limited time on the [[Minecraft Marketplace]], as a promotion for the [[character creator]] on {{el|be}}.

At MINECON Live 2019, the [[Nether Update]] was announced and some of its features were showcased.

The [[Mountains]] biome won the community biome vote, and its features were added in the Caves and Cliffs update.<ref>{{Mcnet|everything-we-announced-minecon-live-2019|Everything We Announced at MINECON Live 2019|September 28, 2019}}</ref>
{{-}}

== Minecraft Live ==
=== 2020 ===
[[File:Minecraft Live 2020 Logo.png|thumb|Minecraft Live 2020 and 2021 logo]]
{{main|Minecraft Live 2020}}

'''Minecraft Live 2020''' was an online livestream held at the official Minecraft Live web page, YouTube, Twitch, and Facebook on October 3, 2020 at 4 pm UTC.

At Minecraft Live 2020, the [[Caves & Cliffs]] update was announced and some of its features were showcased.

The [[Glow Squid]] won the mob vote and was added into ''Minecraft'' in the Caves & Cliffs: Part I update.<ref>{{Mcnet|minecraft-live-the-recap|Minecraft Live: The Recap|October 3, 2020}}</ref>
{{-}}

=== 2021 ===
{{main|Minecraft Live 2021}}
'''Minecraft Live 2021''' was an online livestream that went live on October 16, 2021 at 4 pm UTC.

At Minecraft Live 2021, the [[The Wild Update|Wild Update]] was announced and some of its features were showcased.<ref>{{Mcnet|minecraft-live-2021-the-recap|Minecraft Live 2021 The Recap|October 16, 2021}}</ref>

The [[Allay]] won the mob vote and was added into ''Minecraft'' in the Wild Update.
{{-}}

=== 2022 ===
[[File:Minecraft Live 2022 Logo.png|thumb|Minecraft Live 2022 and present logo]]
{{main|Minecraft Live 2022}}
'''Minecraft Live 2022''' was an online livestream that went live on October 15, 2022.

At Minecraft Live 2022, the [[Trails & Tales]] update was announced and some of its features were showcased.

The [[Sniffer]] won the mob vote and was added as part of the [[Trails & Tales]] update.

{{-}}

=== 2023 ===
{{Main|Minecraft Live 2023}}
'''Minecraft Live 2023''' was an online livestream that went live on October 15, 2023.

At Minecraft Live 2023, the [[Tricky Trials|1.21 Update]] was announced and some of its features, including the [[Trial Chambers]], the [[Crafter]], and the [[Breeze]].

The [[Armadillo]] won the mob vote and was added as part of the [[Armored Paws]] game drop.

=== 2024 ===
{{Main|Minecraft Live 2024}}
'''Minecraft Live 2024''' was an online livestream that went live on September 28, 2024. This was the first Minecraft live stream without a community vote.

At Minecraft Live 2024, the [[Bundles of Bravery]] game drop was announced and some of its features like [[Bundle|Bundles]] and [[Hardcore]] mode for Bedrock Edition.

The [[Pale Garden]], along with the spooky, hostile mob called the [[Creaking]] and a unique block called the [[Creaking Heart]], are introduced as [[The Garden Awakens|a new Winter Game drop]].

=== 2025 ===
==== March 2025 ====
{{Main|Minecraft Wiki:Drafts/Minecraft Live 2025}}
'''[[Minecraft Wiki:Drafts/Minecraft Live 2025|Minecraft Live - March 2025]]''' was an online livestream that went live on March 22, 2025. This Livestream mentioned that there will be two Minecraft Livestreams that will take place in 2025.

At the Live, the [[Spring to Life]] game drop release date was announced and some of its features like [[Firefly Bush]]es, new warm and cold variants for [[cow]]s, [[chicken]]s and [[pig]]s, [[Minecraft Wiki:Drafts/Leaf Litter|Leaf Litter]], retextured [[spawn eggs]], etc.

The [[Happy Ghast]]s, [[Dried Ghast]]s, [[Harness]] and [[Happy Ghast|Ghastlings]] are introduced as [[Chase the Skies|a new Summer Game drop]]. Along with that, a new graphical shader upgrade called [[Vibrant Visuals]] is introduced for Bedrock Edition, and soon will be added to [[Java Edition]].
