A '''turtle''' ({{in|Java}}), also known as a '''sea turtle''' ({{in|Bedrock}}), is a common, semi aquatic [[passive mob]] found in [[beach]] biomes. They are the only source of [[scute]]s, which drop from baby turtles when they mature into adults.

== Spawning ==
Turtles spawn on the sand in the [[Overworld]] on [[beach]]es with daylight, but not in its [[Beach#Snowy Beach|snowy variant]] or [[Beach#Stony Shore|stony shores]], occasionally in small groups of up to 5 individuals.

10% of turtles spawned are babies.

{{IN|Java}}, turtles spawn at [[light level]]s greater than 8, on [[sand]] blocks less than 4 blocks above [[sea level]].

{{IN|bedrock}}, turtles spawn in groups of 2–6, at light level 7 or higher, at Y=60–67.

== Drops ==
When turtles [[die]], they drop:
* 0–2 {{BlockLink|seagrass}}. The maximum amount is increased by 1 per level of [[Looting]], for a maximum of 0–5 with Looting III.
* {{xp|1|3}}, if killed by a [[player]] or tamed [[wolf]].
* 1 {{ItemLink|bowl}}, if killed by [[Thunderstorm#Lightning|lightning]].<ref>{{bug|MC-125562}} resolved as "Works as Intended"</ref>

Baby turtles do not drop anything when they [[die]].

When a baby turtle grows up to an adult it drops:
* 1 {{ItemLink|scute}}

Upon successful [[breeding]], {{xp|1|7}} is dropped.

== Behavior ==
Turtles are semi aquatic, meaning they travel on land and swim in [[water]]. They move slowly on land, but they can swim at a fast speed in the water. Like other passive [[mob]]s, they move around aimlessly and flee when attacked. When on land, they generally attempt to move to the nearest water source with direct sky access.{{verify|Sky access does not seem to matter|edition=java}} When in an enclosed area, such as a player-made enclosure, they generally swim in circles if the water is in the area, and rarely go on land to explore, if there is any.

Turtles cannot be attached to [[lead]]s,<ref>{{bug|MC-125629}} resolved as "Works as Intended"</ref> but they can be led by a [[player]] holding [[seagrass]] within 10 blocks of the turtle.

Turtles can be picked up by [[boat]]s{{only|java}} and [[minecart]]s.

Turtles are killed instantly when struck by lightning.

As with other [[aquatic mobs]], turtles suffer extra damage when hit by a [[trident]] with the [[Impaling]] enchantment,{{only|je}}{{until|Java Edition Combat Tests}} and cannot drown, despite the fact that real-life turtles breathe air.

=== Home beach ===
Turtles remember the block on which they hatch as their home beach. No matter how far away the turtle is, it always attempts to return to its home beach to lay its eggs after [[breeding]].

If the player uses a tool enchanted with [[Silk Touch]] to obtain and move [[turtle egg]]s to a new location before hatching, the baby turtles remember the new hatching location as their home beach.

If a turtle is spawned from a [[spawn egg]], summoned with the {{cmd|summon}} [[command]], or spawned by [[mob spawner]]s, it remembers the spawn location as its home beach.
[[File:A Turtle Digging Sand.png|thumb|A turtle digs sand around the block on which it lays its eggs.]]

=== Breeding ===
[[File:A Turtle Lays Eggs.png|thumb|A turtle lays eggs after digging.]]
Feeding two turtles [[seagrass]] causes them to enter [[breeding|love mode]], causing one of the turtles to have [[turtle egg|eggs]] in its [[inventory]] and appear a bit bigger (about 0.2 of a block) than normal turtles. The pregnant turtle then travels back to its home beach, which is where it first spawned. Upon arrival, it seeks a nearby [[sand]] block on which to lay its eggs. A turtle always lays eggs within 9 blocks from where it was born. The turtle digs vigorously while turning 360 degrees for several seconds a few times, and then lays 1–4 [[turtle egg]]s. Turtles have a 90-second cooldown after breeding {{in|Bedrock}}, and a five-minute cooldown {{in|Java}}.

=== Eggs ===
[[Turtle egg]]s can be placed on any block in clusters of up to 4, however, they hatch only on [[sand]], [[red sand]], or [[suspicious sand]], above or below water. Eggs can be broken without tools and can also be trampled by living entities falling or walking on them. When a multi-egg block is trampled, eggs are broken one at a time. A turtle can walk on the eggs without breaking them. Harvesting eggs with [[Silk Touch]] drops an egg, which the player can collect. Mining an egg with [[Silk Touch]] resets the egg's growth.

Turtle eggs progress toward hatching by cracking. Cracking occurs during random ticks, and an egg hatches once it has cracked 3 times. When a multi-egg block hatches, all eggs within the block hatch simultaneously. Eggs grow fastest during the night and typically take 4 to 5 in-game days to hatch. See [[Turtle egg#Hatching]] for more details.

=== Babies ===
With a height of 0.12 blocks, baby turtles are among the smallest [[mob]]s in the game. Baby turtles are small enough that when they walk on [[soul sand]], they are entirely within the space at the top of the [[block]].

Upon hatching, baby turtles search for the nearest water block within a 47×1×47 region two blocks below the turtle and attempt to approach it.

Baby turtles take one Minecraft day to grow up (20 minutes). The growth can be accelerated using [[seagrass]]. Each use reduces the remaining growth duration by 10%. Upon becoming an adult, the turtle drops a [[scute]].
