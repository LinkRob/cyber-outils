'''Minecraft Dungeons II''' is an upcoming action RPG dungeon crawler by [[Mojang Studios]] and [[Double Eleven]]. It serves as a sequel to [[Minecraft Dungeons]]. It is set to release on September 29, 2026. It was revealed on [[Minecraft Live - March 2026]].

== Description ==
{{Quote|The next chapter of Minecraft Dungeons begins in the fall of 2026! A new threat is descending upon the land. Become a hero and clash with the forces of evil once more!|The game's description}}

== Development ==
The game refers to as <code>Spicewood</code> in Minecraft.net's media files.

== Content ==
The trailer suggests that the main opponents in Minecraft Dungeons II will likely include the [[deep dark]] biome and the [[warden]]. Pausing the trailer at exactly 13 seconds will reveal a seperate video that has music and a taller version of the warden. The new boss is the [[twisted warden]]; it is a slow, but very powerful boss that sucks up souls, creating stronger sonic waves. Some other new mobs are coming, such as the caretaker and the bulb.
