'''''Minecraft Pocket Edition J''''' was a subversion of the popular game for mobile devices, possibly available between 2012 and 2017 on Google Play Store. This version disappeared after the acquisition of Minecraft by Microsoft. Due to the transition, all versions of Minecraft Pocket Edition, including the “J” version, were removed from the store.

{{Program|title=Minecraft Pocket Edition J|image=Minecraft_Pocket_Edition_J.webp|date=}}

The first version of Minecraft for mobile devices was Pocket Edition, released on August 16, 2011. Contrary to popular belief, this edition consisted of four subversions, the most popular being Minecraft Pocket Edition Original and Minecraft Pocket Edition Lite. Each offered different features and limitations, allowing players to experience the game in different ways, adapting to the capabilities of mobile devices at the time.

Thus, Pocket Edition J was one of the subversions intended for low-end devices, with a reduced weight of only 1.84 megabytes. Due to its limitations, it had low performance and a very small inventory. Despite this, Pocket Edition J was paid, but a demo could be purchased.

Currently, it is not known for sure how many versions of Pocket Edition J were created, although the following are known: 0.3.0 J Alpha, 0.5.0 J Alpha, 0.5.0 J and 0.1.1 J. The last one is the only one available on the internet.
