}}A '''snowy taiga''' is a snow-covered [[biome]] that comprises a forest of spruce [[tree]]s, ferns and large ferns, usually generating adjacent to or within [[snowy plains]].

== Description==
Snowy taigas are similar to regular [[taiga]], featuring large numbers of [[spruce|spruce tree]]s, [[fern]]s, [[rabbit]]s, white-coated [[fox]]es and [[wolf|wolves]]. [[Sweet Berries|Sweet berry bush]]es are less common compared to regular taiga. [[Flower|Dandelion]]s and poppies{{only|java}} can also still be found. The main distinction are the [[snow|snow layer]]s that cover almost the entire landscape, save for the ground protected by the leaves of spruce trees. The gelid temperature of the snowy taiga means that any exposed [[water]] freezes into [[ice]], though lakes that generate within the taiga are often partially protected by the leaves of the trees, meaning lakes aren't usually completely frozen. 

Snowy taigas are most commonly bordered by [[snowy plains]], [[grove]]s, [[snowy beach]]es, and regular [[taiga]]s, and often separated from [[snowy plains]] by [[river]]s.

[[Igloo]]s and [[trail ruins]] may generate here, but villages and outposts cannot {{in|java}}<ref>{{bug|MC-154837|||WAI}}</ref>, although {{in|bedrock}} they can. Villages generated here use the same architecture as normal taiga villages, but all of the roofs are covered by stacked snow. The water in village farms is frozen, leading to the crops uprooting. [[Villager]]s wear the snowy plains outfit. Also, {{in|be}}, most of the snow layers are stacked, the water has an unique tone and the [[leaves]] of the trees become white as in snowy plains during [[snowfall]].
