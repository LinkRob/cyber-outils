'''1.9.19''' is the final update for the {{el|ds}}, released on January 15/16, 2019, which added features from the [[Discovery Update]], as well as the [[World of Color Update]].<ref>{{Mcnet|new-nintendo-3ds-update|New Nintendo 3DS Update|January 15, 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite|url=https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360022028351|title=Minecraft: New Nintendo 3DS Edition - 1.9}}</ref>

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[Concrete]]
* Comes in the 16 traditional [[dye]] colors.
* Created when [[Concrete Powder|concrete powder]] comes into contact with [[water]].

; [[Concrete powder]]
* Comes in the 16 traditional [[dye]] colors.
* [[Gravity]] affected (like [[sand]] and [[gravel]]).
* When it touches water, it turns into a [[concrete]] block.
* Can be crafted from 4 [[sand]] and 4 [[gravel]].

; [[Glazed terracotta]]
* Comes in the 16 traditional [[dye]] colors.
* Can be obtained by [[smelting]] [[terracotta]].

;  Dyed [[bed]]s
* Can be crafted with colored [[wool]] or by dyeing a white [[bed]].

; [[Shulker box]]
* Works similar to a [[chest]], but keeps its content when broken.
** If it has any content inside, it will drop itself in [[Creative]] mode as well.
** Cannot be placed inside another shulker box.
* Can be crafted by using a [[chest]] and two [[Shulker Shell|shulker shells]].
* Can be dyed in any of the available [[dye]] colors.

; [[Frosted ice]]
* Created when using "[[Frost Walker]]" enchantment.
* Cannot be obtained in inventory, even when using [[Silk Touch]].

; [[Magma block]]
* Can be crafted using four [[Magma Cream|magma cream]].
* Can generate in the [[nether]].
* [[Mob|Mobs]] and players take {{hp|1}} damage every second while touching it, similar to a [[cactus]].
* It emits as much [[light]] as it receives from other sources.

; [[Bone block]]
* Can be crafted from 9 [[bone meal]].
** Can be crafted back into 9 bone meal.

; [[Nether wart block]]
* Can be crafted from 9 [[nether wart]].
** It cannot be crafted back into nether wart.

; [[Red nether bricks]]
* Can be crafted from 2 nether wart and 2 nether bricks.

=== Items ===
; [[Explorer map]]
* Can only be obtained from cartographer [[Villager|villagers]].
* Can be used to locate some [[structure]]s.

; [[Totem of undying]]
* Can be obtained from killing [[evoker]]s.
* Holding it in a hotbar slot when the player dies will activate it and save their life.
** Gives player the [[Absorption]] and [[Regeneration]] effects when activated.

; [[Iron Nugget|Iron]] and [[Gold nugget]]s
* Can be smelted from [[iron]] and [[gold]] [[Tool|tools]] and [[armor]].
* Can by also obtained from [[Iron Ingot|iron ingot]] or [[Gold Ingot|gold ingot]].

; [[Spawn eggs]] for {{lc:Llamas, Vindicators, Evokers, and Vexes}}
* Used to spawn [[Llama]], [[Vindicator]], [[Evoker]] or [[Vex]].

=== Mobs ===
; [[Llamas]]
* Spawns in [[hills]] and [[savanna]] [[Biome|biomes]].
* Attacks by spitting.
* Neutral to mobs and players.

; [[Vindicators]]
* Hostile towards [[Player|players]] and [[villager]]s.
* Holds [[Iron axe]].
* Spawn in the [[Woodland Mansion|woodland mansions]] upon generation. They will not respawn.
* May drop [[Emerald|emeralds]] and an [[Axe|iron axe]] upon death.

; [[Evokers]]
* In battle, they will summon [[Vex|vexes]] and fangs to attack.
* Hostile towards [[Player|players]] and [[Villager|villagers]].
* Will always drop a [[Totem of Undying|totem of undying]] upon death. May drop [[Emerald|emeralds]] too.

; [[Vexes]]
* Spawn only when summoned by an [[evoker]].
* Hostile towards [[Player|players]] and [[Villager|villagers]].
* Hold an [[Sword|iron sword]] in their main hand.
** Can't drop their [[Sword|iron sword]].

; Cartographer [[Villagers]] {{more info}}

=== World generation ===
; [[Woodland mansion]]
* [[Illager]]s spawn here.
* Can be located with a woodland explorer maps.

; Underground [[fossil]]s
* Generates in the underground.

; Natural [[Magma Block|magma block]] generation in the [[Nether]]
