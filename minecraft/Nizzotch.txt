'''Nizzotch''' is a YouTube channel that was used by [[Markus Persson]] (Notch) to share videos of ''[[Minecraft]]'s'' development. Originally created on August 11, 2008, 39 videos (38 public and 1 unlisted) were uploaded to the channel between August 11, 2008, and August 30, 2011, most of them pertaining to ''Minecraft'', with a few others of some of Notch's other games.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20130925121445/http://www.youtube.com/user/Nizzotch/videos</ref><ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20200415202710/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9-fuwsWTL_8</ref>

Notch stopped using the channel for ''Minecraft'' content after the release of the [[Halloween Update]], but would upload more ''Minecraft'' progress videos to another channel named [https://www.youtube.com/@mojnotch mojnotch] and to [https://www.youtube.com/@minecraft the official ''Minecraft'' YouTube channel]. He also had [https://web.archive.org/web/20130217231320/http://www.youtube.com/channel/UCielLdGIGuEAMsbVuS-kZAg a now-deleted channel] for ''[[0x10c|0x10<sup>c</sup>]]'' videos and has [https://www.youtube.com/@_notch an additional channel] he still occasionally uses for gaming videos, both named Markus Persson.

All of the videos on the Nizzotch channel were blocked using a copyright claim filed by Notch after he left [[Mojang]], making them unviewable on the original channel.
