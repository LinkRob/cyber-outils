Causes a player in [[Spectator]] mode to spectate another [[entity]] or stop spectating.

==Syntax==
<code>spectate <target> [<player>]</code>
: Causes a player to spectate another entity.
<code>spectate</code>
: Stops spectating.

==Arguments==
{{argument|target|entity}}
: Specifies the target to be spectated.
: {{arg desc|je=entity|amount=single|type=entities}}

{{argument|player|entity}}
: Specifies the spectating player. If unspecified, defaults to the executor.
: {{arg desc|je=entity|amount=single|type=players}}

==Result==

{{Result table|onlyje=1}}
{{Result table|the arguments are not specified correctly|unparseable}}
{{Result table|<code><target></code> fails to resolve to an entity (named player must be online)|fail}}
{{Result table|<code><player></code> fails to resolve to a online player}}
{{Result table|<code><player></code> is not specified when the command's executor is not a player.}}
{{Result table|<code><player></code> and <code><target></code> are the same.}}
{{Result table|<code><player></code> isn't in spectator mode.}}
{{Result table|succeed}}
{{Result table}}
