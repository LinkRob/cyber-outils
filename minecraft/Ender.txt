'''Ender''' may refer to:

== Minecraft ==

=== Mobs ===
* {{EntityLink|Ender dragon}} - a [[Mob#Boss mobs|boss]] mob, also acknowledged as the final boss mob of [[Minecraft]].
** {{ItemLink|Ender Dragon Spawn Egg}}
* {{EntityLink|Enderman}} - a [[Mob#Neutral mobs|neutral]] mob that spawns in all three [[Dimension|dimensions]] and becomes hostile if looked at by a player or attacked, either by a mob or a player.
** {{ItemLink|Enderman Spawn Egg}}
* {{EntityLink|Endermite}} - a [[Mob#Hostile mobs|hostile]] mob, spawned from thrown ender pearls with a 5% chance.
** {{ItemLink|Endermite Spawn Egg}}
* {{EntityLink|Shulker}} - a hostile mob spawned within the end cities and shoots shulker bullets at players and gives the [[levitation]] effect if it hits them.
** {{ItemLink|Shulker Spawn Egg}}

=== Blocks ===
* {{BlockLink|Ender chest}} - an ender variation of the normal chests that are connected to other ender chests.
*{{BlockLink|End stone}} - a common block native to the end and makes up end islands of all types. 

=== Items ===
* {{ItemLink|End crystal}} - floating and destroyable crystals that are used to heal the Ender Dragon.
* {{ItemLink|Eye of ender}} - artificial eyes that are used to activate the End Portal and locate strongholds.
* {{ItemLink|Ender pearl}} - pearls dropped by endermen that are capable of teleporting players if thrown.
