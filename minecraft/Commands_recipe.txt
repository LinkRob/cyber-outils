Gives or takes (unlocks or locks) [[recipes]] for players.

== Syntax == 

*'''Java Edition'''
<code>recipe (give|take) <targets> *</code>
:Gives or takes all recipes from the player.
<code>recipe (give|take) <targets> <recipe></code>
:Gives or takes a specified recipe from the player.

*'''Bedrock Edition'''
<code>recipe give <player: target> *</code>
:Gives all recipes to the player.
<code>recipe take <player: target> *</code>
:Takes all recipes from the player.
<code>recipe give <player: target> <recipe: string></code>
:Gives a specified recipe to the player.
<code>recipe take <player: target> <recipe: string></code>
:Takes a specified recipe from the player.

== Arguments ==

<code>give|take</code>
:If <code>give</code>, the recipe will be given (unlocked) for the player. If <code>take</code>, the recipe will be taken (locked) for the player.

{{argument|target|entity|player: target|CommandSelector<Player>}}
:Specifies the player(s) to give or take the recipe from.
:{{arg desc|je=entity|amount=multiple|type=players|be=player}}

{{argument|recipe|resource_location|recipe: string|basic_string}} 
:Specifies a recipe to give or take.
