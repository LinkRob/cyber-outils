A '''pile''' is a [[feature]] that generates only inside [[village]]s.

== Generation ==
There are five variants of pile: hay, ice, melon, pumpkin, and snow. Hay piles generate with [[hay bale]]s, ice piles generate with [[packed ice]] and sometimes [[blue ice]], melon piles generate with [[melon]]s, pumpkin piles generates with [[pumpkin]]s and sometimes [[jack o'lantern]]s, and snow piles generate with [[snow]]. Piles can generate only in [[village]]s.
