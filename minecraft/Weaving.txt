'''Weaving''' is an [[effect]] that creates 2-3 [[cobweb]]s when a mob with that effect is killed. This potion effect is mainly used in [[Trial Chambers]], along with [[Oozing]], [[Infested]], and [[Wind Charged]]. Weaving effect occurs when the player is hit by a splash potion of weaving while the [[Trial Omen]] effect is activated. (Or by brewing it, then drinking it/using gunpowder to make your own splash potion/using [[Dragon's Breath]] to make it a [[Lingering Potion]].)
