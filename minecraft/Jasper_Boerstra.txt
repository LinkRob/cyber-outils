'''Jasper Boerstra''' is a full time employee who is currently working for [[Mojang Studios]] as the Lead Artist<ref>https://nl.linkedin.com/in/jasper-boerstra-927835a4</ref> for ''[[Minecraft]]''. He was hired to change the great majority of the old textures in the game. Most of his changes to the game's textures took effect in the [[Texture Update]], and he has since continued to create textures for new [[block]]s, [[item]]s, and [[entity|entities]].
