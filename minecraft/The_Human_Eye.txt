The Human Eye is a custom [[world]] released by [[Mojang]] for ''[[Minecraft: Bedrock Edition]]''. It is available to download from the [[Marketplace]] for free. It was released on May 8, 2017.<ref>https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/worlds/the-human-eye</ref>

==Description==
This world is intended to be played in [[Creative]] Mode. Players begin on a floating glass platform with a single copy of the [[Written Book]] "The Human Eye". There is a [[Chest]] on the platform, containing 27 additional copies of the same Written Book.

The world contains a model of the human eye, describing various parts of the human eye as well as their functions and how they work. Players are encouraged to fly around the model to read about each part. The human eye model has two interior areas, which players can enter via small holes in the model to see parts of the interior of the human eye.
