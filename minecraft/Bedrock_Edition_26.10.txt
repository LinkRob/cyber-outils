'''Bedrock Edition 26.10''', named [[Tiny Takeover]], is a game drop for [[Bedrock Edition]], released on March 24, 2026.

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Golden Dandelion]]
** A new decoration block which can be planted on [[flower pot]]s like other flowers does.
** Can be crafted with a [[dandelion]] surrounded by 8 [[Gold Nugget|gold nuggets]].
** Can be used to feed most baby mobs.
** Baby mobs will stop aging when fed one; will age again when fed again.
** Can be used to craft [[Yellow Dye|yellow dyes]] and [[Suspicious Stew|suspicious stews]].
** Unlike other flowers, they:
*** Are not compostable.
*** Do not attract [[bees]].
*** Are not pollinated by bees.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Menu screen]]
** Changed the [[panorama]] to reflect [[Tiny Takeover]] game drop.

=== Blocks ===
* [[Note Block]]
** Added a new trumpet sound.
*** Plays when placed on top of a [[copper]] block.
*** Sound differs based on oxidation stage.
* [[Stonecutter]]s
** [[Deepslate]] and [[stone]] blocks can now be crafted directly into their respective variants.
* [[Furnace]]s
** Added its own recipe book.
** Only available under "Furnace Recipe Book" experimental toggle.

=== Items ===
* Craftable [[Name Tag|name tags]]
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
