{{EntitySprite|rabbit}} A '''[[rabbit]]''' is an uncommon passive mob.

'''Rabbit''' may also refer to:

== ''[[Minecraft]]''==
*{{EntityLink|Killer Rabbit}}, a hostile rabbit.
*{{ItemLink|Raw Rabbit}}, a food item that restores {{hunger|3}}
*{{ItemLink|Cooked Rabbit}}, a food item that restores {{hunger|5}} 
* {{ItemLink|Rabbit Stew}}, a food item that restores {{hunger|10}}
*{{ItemLink|Rabbit Hide}}, an item used to craft leather.
*{{ItemLink|Rabbit's Foot}}, an item used in brewing.

===[[Achievement|Achievements]]===
*[[A Balanced Diet]], an [[advancement]] that requires eating cooked rabbit, raw rabbit, and rabbit stew.
*[[It Spreads]], an achievement and advancement that could be achieved by killing a rabbit near a [[Sculk Catalyst]].
*[[The Parrots and the Bats]], an advancement that could be achieved by breeding two rabbits.
*[[Light as a Rabbit]], an advancement requiring you to walk on [[Powder Snow|powder snow]] using [[Boots|leather boots]].
*[[Rabbit Season]], an achievement requiring you to cook and eat a rabbit.
*[[Two by Two]], an advancement that requires breeding two rabbits.
