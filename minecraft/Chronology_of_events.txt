=== April 2009 ===
*'''29''' – ''[[Infiniminer]]'' is released, a game by [http://www.zachtronicsindustries.com/ Zachtronics Industries] (creators of the critically acclaimed 2011 puzzle game ''[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceChem SpaceChem]''), whose concept mainly inspired Notch for ''Minecraft''.<ref>[http://thesiteformerlyknownas.zachtronicsindustries.com/infiniminer/ '''Infiniminer released:''' http://thesiteformerlyknownas.zachtronicsindustries.com/infiniminer/]</ref> It was regularly updated and became open-source less than a month later.<ref>[http://thesiteformerlyknownas.zachtronicsindustries.com/apparently-im-now-in-charge-of-an-open-source-project/ '''Infiniminer is now open-source:''' http://thesiteformerlyknownas.zachtronicsindustries.com/apparently-im-now-in-charge-of-an-open-source-project/]</ref>
