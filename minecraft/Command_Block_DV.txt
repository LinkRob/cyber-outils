| 0x1<br>0x2<br>0x4

A 3-bit field storing the command block's facing:
* 0: facing down
* 1: facing up
* 2: facing north
* 3: facing south
* 4: facing west
* 5: facing east

6 and 7 are unused but produce command blocks facing down and up respectively.
|-
| 0x8
| Whether the block is in conditional mode.
