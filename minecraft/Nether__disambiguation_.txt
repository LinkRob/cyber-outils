== Dimensions ==
* [[Nether]]— A dimension of infinite [[Lava]].

== Achievements and advancements ==
* [[Achievements#Into The Nether|Into The Nether]] — An achievement obtained by constructing a Nether portal.
* [[Advancements#We Need to Go Deeper|We Need to Go Deeper]] — An advancement obtained by building and entering a Nether portal.
* [[Advancements#Nether|Nether]] — An advancement obtained by entering the Nether.
