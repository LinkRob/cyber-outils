'''Beta 1.5''' is a version of [[Java Edition Beta]] released on April 19, 2011,<ref>{{Word of Notch|4746701552|Minecraft Beta 1.5, and some thoughts on this blog|April 19, 2011}}</ref> which added [[statistics]] and [[Achievements#Java Edition|achievements]], new types of [[rail]]s and [[sapling]]s, and [[cobweb]]s among other changes and fixes.

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[File:Birch Sapling JE1.png|32px]] [[Birch Sapling]]
* Grows into [[birch tree]]s.

; [[File:Cobweb JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Cobweb]]
* Unobtainable in the game without inventory editing until [[Beta 1.8]].

; [[File:Detector Rail (NS) JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Detector Rail]]
* Crafted with 1 [[stone pressure plate]], 6 [[iron ingot]]s and 1 [[Redstone|redstone dust]].
* Used to detect minecarts on top of it; when activated, they produce redstone power.

; [[File:Powered Rail (NS) JE1 BE1.png|32x32px]] [[Powered Rail]]
* Crafted with 1 [[stick]], 6 [[gold ingot]]s and 1 redstone dust.
* Needs to be powered by redstone.
* Transmits redstone signal to 8 powered rails far.

; [[File:Spruce Sapling JE1.png|32px]] [[Spruce Sapling]]
* Grows into [[spruce tree]]s.

===Mobs===
; [[File:Charged Creeper JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Charged Creeper]]s
* When struck by a lightning bolt, they now become a charged creeper, and have a much more powerful explosion.

; [[Pig]]s
* When struck by a lighting bolt they now become [[Zombified Piglin|zombie pigmen]].

===Non-mob entities===
; [[Lightning Bolt]]
* May strike during thunderstorms, creating fire and dealing damage around it.

===Gameplay===
; [[Achievements#Java Edition|Achievements]]
* Are now transferred between worlds.

; [[Weather]]
* Added natural occurring [[rain]], [[snowfall]]s, and [[thunderstorm]]s.

===General===
; [[Control]]s
* {{key|Shift}} + clicking on something in a [[chest]] will immediately move the selected item(s) into the player's inventory and vice-versa.

; [[Option]]s
* Ability to change GUI size (Small, Normal, Large, Auto).
* Advanced OpenGL (Occlusion Culling: doesn't render blocks that the player can't "see", helps performance in cards that support it).

; [[Statistics]]
* Are now transferred between worlds.

==Changes==
===Blocks===

; [[Bed]]

*Sleeping in thunderstorms is now possible, even in daytime.

; [[Crying obsidian]]

*Texture has now been removed from {{mono|[[terrain.png]]}}.

; [[Fire]]

*Now spreads much slower than before.

; [[File:Grass Block JE4.png|32px]] [[Grass Block]]s

*When using fancy graphics, the sides now use the same color as the top (example of a grass block in [[Java Edition Alpha 1.2.0|Alpha 1.2.0]]: [[File:Grass Block JE3 BE1.png|32px]]).

; [[Ladder]]s

*Its [[crafting]] recipe now produces 2 ladders to offset the fix of the staggered-ladder exploit.

; [[Sapling]]s

*Can now serve as fuel in [[furnace]]s.
**Have the same smelting time as [[stick]]s.

; [[Redstone Wire]]

*Fully off redstone wire is no longer black.
*Several changes in redstone wire rendering
**Due this change, it no longer appears stretched when travelling vertically up a block. However, it still appears offset.

===Items===

; General

*Dropped items in flowing [[water]] move faster than before.

; [[Redstone|Redstone Dust]]

*Can now be placed on [[snow]].

===Mobs===

; [[Pig]]s

*When killed by burning, now drop [[cooked porkchop]]s upon death.

; [[Wolf|Wolves]]

*Now more common, even in non-[[taiga]]/[[forest]] biomes.

===World generation===

; [[Birch]] and [[spruce|spruce trees]]

*Now drop [[birch sapling]]s and [[spruce sapling]]s, respectively.

===General===

; [[Control]]s

*Pressing {{key|Tab}} will switch between input boxes.
*{{key|Shift}} + {{key|F2}} no longer creates a 36450&times;17700 pixel screenshot.

; Performance

*Huge performance improvements for old and slow computers.

==Fixes==
;15 bugs fixed
*Players are only 'on' a ladder (can climb it/fall slowly) if the lower half is in the ladder block, rather than any part of the player.
**It is no longer possible to climb with a one-block gap in a ladder, or a ladder that starts at eye level.
*On fancy graphics, the sides of grass blocks are now biome-specific and match the shade on the top.
*Player movement through water has been fixed to previous speed.
*Wolves' pathfinding now has a better understanding of doors. They will go through doors most of the time, but will sometimes get stuck on the 3-pixel thickness of the door.
*The lighting on portals and ice has been fixed when using smooth lighting.
*[[Stairs]] are no longer transparent to light.
*On multiplayer, the health of [[wolves]] are now synchronized between server and client, causing wolves to only whimper when low on health, as opposed to all of the time.
*Redstone in off state is red again.
*Fixed saplings occasionally growing trees that stretch to world limit.
*Crops hit with water now drop seeds and wheat, as opposed to just wheat.
*Moving minecarts will continue to move if the world is closed.
*The Mojang logo is the correct size when the client is maximized.
*The exploit to make chests three or more side by side by placing a water source and placing a chest in that source has been fixed.
*{{key|Shift}} + {{key|F2}} doesn't attempt to create massive .tga screenshots, which are more than 1GB in size, anymore.
*Sugar cane can no longer be placed on any block that is not dirt.</onlyinclude>
