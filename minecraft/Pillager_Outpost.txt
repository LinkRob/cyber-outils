A '''pillager outpost''' is an assortment of [[structures]] inhabited by [[pillager]]s. 

== Generation ==
Pillager outposts are semi-rare structures, generating every several hundred to couple thousand blocks.<ref>https://chunkbase.com/apps/pillager-outpost-finder</ref> The closest it can be is 150 blocks. This makes them rarer than [[village]]s<ref>https://chunkbase.com/apps/village-finder</ref> but less rare than [[woodland mansion]]s.

Pillager outposts can generate naturally in any village-generating biome, including:
* {{BiomeLink|Plains}}
* {{BiomeLink|Desert}}
* {{BiomeLink|Savanna}}
* {{BiomeLink|Taiga}}
* {{BiomeLink|Snowy Plains}}
* {{BiomeLink|Snowy Taiga}}{{only|bedrock|short=1}}
* {{BiomeLink|Sunflower Plains}}{{only|bedrock|short=1}}
* {{BiomeLink|Meadow}}
Pillager outposts also generate in the following biomes, where villages do not spawn:
* {{BiomeLink|Grove}}
* {{BiomeLink|Snowy Slopes}}
* {{BiomeLink|Jagged Peaks}}
* {{BiomeLink|Frozen Peaks}}
* {{BiomeLink|Stony Peaks}}
* {{BiomeLink|Cherry Grove}}{{only|java|short=1}}

{{IN|java}}, pillager outposts never generate too close to, or within, villages. They can do so {{in|bedrock}} as well as generate far away from villages.

{{IN|java}}, if an outpost spawns in the water or in the air (e.g., spawning near a [[savanna plateau]]), then a platform of [[grass block|grass]] (or [[sand]]), [[dirt]], and [[stone]] spawns underneath it in a circular shape. This also applies to the smaller structures such as tents, targets, [[log]] piles, and cages. On occasion, the smaller structures still spawn in the air despite having a platform below them, depending on how high up they are.

{{IN|bedrock}}, pillager outposts generate with foundations similar to that of a woodland mansion: watchtowers use [[cobblestone]], birch [[planks]], and dark oak logs as their foundation; tents and cages generate dark oak planks as their foundation; log piles use horizontal dark oak logs as their foundation; and targets generate cobblestone as a foundation.

The outpost tower generates each time. Up to 4 other smaller structures may spawn around the periphery of the main outpost structure.

Eight extra [[air]] blocks also generate around the outpost.<ref>{{bug|MC-139711||Jigsaw block creates holes in the ground when generating pillager outposts}}</ref>

Iron golems and allays spawn only during the generation of the outpost structures. An iron golem has a 85% chance of spawning. Meanwhile, the allay has a 50% chance of spawning. These mobs spawn in cages. The iron golem can punch  pillagers who are one block away from the fences of the cage. 

Both regular pillagers and [[raid captain|pillager captain]]s can continuously spawn around the structure. Pillagers can still spawn if a player is inside the tower. {{IN|java}} they spawn in a 72×54×72 area centered on the top level of the watchtower, the same level as the chest. {{IN|bedrock}}, they spawn at or below a particular location in the outpost; specifically, they choose the northwest corner of the highest opaque block with a non-solid block on top.

{{IN|java}}, only pillagers may spawn within a pillager outpost spawn area. Pillagers may naturally spawn on any valid opaque block (except leaves) as long as the block light level is 8 or less. They cannot, however, spawn on blocks that emit a light level of 14 or greater. There is a 6% chance a pillager will spawn as a patrol leader.

{{IN|java}}, there are 15 jigsaw blocks that can generate the structures around the tower. Each structure has a 1 in 13 chance of being generated, including allay and iron golem cages. When generated, iron golem cages spawn one golem, and allay cages spawn two allays.

A total of eight pillagers, including the pillager captain, spawns in a pillager outpost.

=== Bad Omen ===
Upon killing an outpost captain, the [[player]] receives 1 level of the [[Bad Omen]] effect applied to them for 100 minutes (5 in-game days) ''(1.20 and below)''. In 1.21 and above, the player upon killing an outpost captain will receive 1 [[Ominous Bottle|ominous bottle]]. 

== Structure ==
{{main|/Structure}}
A pillager outpost consists of a 4 level watchtower with other smaller structures around it.

[[Structure block]]s can be used to manually load pieces of the pillager outpost from the {{cd|/data/minecraft/structures/pillager_outpost}} folder in [[minecraft.jar]]. To do so, set a structure block to Load mode, enter {{cd|pillager_outpost/''<structure_name>''}}, and press LOAD.

== Trivia ==

* Pillager outposts are the only natural source of [[Dark oak]] wood and its products in the biomes it spawns in( Pillager outposts don't spawn in [[Dark oak]] forests)
* A pillager outpost's base may be extended to help it stand without removing/obstructing terrain. This is common in [[Bedrock Edition]] due to the glitches in terrain generation that version faces.
* Pillager outposts are missing two blocks on one of the pillars for the roof, making the struture asymmetrical.
