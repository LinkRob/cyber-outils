'''Breath of the Nautilus''' is a [[status effect]] added in [[Mounts of Mayhem]]. It only activates when a [[player]] mounts a [[nautilus]] or [[Zombie Nautilus|zombie nautilus]], which freezes the oxygen bar, preventing a player from [[drowning]].

== Effect ==
When inflicted, it prevents an entity from drowning underwater.

== Immune mobs ==
The following mobs are immune to "Breath of the Nautilus":
* {{EntityLink|Wither}}
* {{EntityLink|Ender Dragon}}
