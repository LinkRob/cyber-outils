'''Gold''' is a material found in both the [[Overworld]] and [[the Nether]]. It spawns naturally as gold ore that can be smelted into gold ingots. The gold ingots can be crafted into blocks, items, tools, and armor.
== Items ==
* {{ItemLink|Raw Gold}} — an item that drops from gold ore when mined; it can be smelted into gold ingots.
* {{ItemLink|Gold Ingot}} — an item created by smelting gold ore, raw gold, or crafting using gold nuggets; it is used for crafting items such as [[netherite ingot]]s, dropped by [[zombified piglin]]s.
* {{ItemLink|Gold Nugget}} — an item that can be crafted into a gold ingot. Obtained by killing zombified piglins, smelting gold equipment in a furnace, or mining nether gold ore or gilded blackstone.
* {{ItemLink|Golden Apple}} — an item created by crafting gold ingots and an apple that gives the player various status effects.
* {{ItemLink|Enchanted Golden Apple}} — a variant of the golden apple, with better effects. Found only in natural generation.
* {{ItemLink|Golden Carrot}} — an item created by crafting gold nuggets and a carrot that is used for brewing.
* {{ItemLink|Glistering Melon Slice}} — an item created by crafting gold nuggets and a melon slice that is used for brewing.
* {{ItemLink|Clock}} — an item that displays the current time in-game.
* {{ItemLink|Golden Dandelion}} - an upcoming item created by crafting gold nuggets and a [[dandelion]] that is used for age-locking baby mobs.

== Blocks ==
* {{BlockLink|Gold Ore}} — an uncommon ore found deep underground.
*{{BlockLink|Deepslate Gold Ore}} — a variant of gold ore found in the [[deepslate]] layer of the world.
* {{BlockLink|Nether Gold Ore}} — a variant of gold ore that drops only nuggets when mined without [[Silk Touch]].
* {{BlockLink|Gilded Blackstone}} — a variant of gold ore that usually drops as itself, but occasionally drops as gold nuggets.
* {{BlockLink|Block of Raw Gold}} − a block used for compact raw gold storage.
* {{BlockLink|Block of Gold}} — a block used for compact gold storage.
* {{BlockLink|Bell}} — a block that produces sound when used.
* {{BlockLink|Powered Rail}} — a type of rail made of gold ingots.
* {{BlockLink|Light Weighted Pressure Plate}} — a pressure plate made of gold. Useful for measuring how many entities are in a given space.
* {{BlockLink|Gold|link=Gold (element)}} — an element included in {{el|education}}.
