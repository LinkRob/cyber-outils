An '''Ominous bottle''' is a unique [[potion]] bottle obtained from defeating [[raid captain]]s or unlocking [[vault]]s in the [[Trial Chambers]]. They inflict [[Bad Omen]] effect when drunk.

Unlike other potions, Ominous Bottles are stackable, with each stack holding over 64 bottles of the same level of [[Bad Omen]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Mob loot ===
Ominous Bottles are usually dropped by [[Raid captain|raid captains,]] which always spawn in [[Pillager Outpost|pillager outposts]] in groups of 1-3, as part of [[Patrol|patrols]], and as part of raids{{only|java}}. The bottle dropped by the captain gives the player [[Bad Omen]] I-V.

=== Trial Chambers ===
Ominous Bottles can be obtained in [[Trial Chambers]] as rewards from both [[Vault|normal vaults]] and [[ominous vault]]s. A normal vault has a 18.6% chance of containing an Ominous Bottle, and an ominous Vault a 13.9% chance.

=== Chest loot ===
Ominous bottles can be found in loot chests in [[Pillager Outpost|pillager outposts]] and [[Woodland Mansion|woodland mansions]].

== Usage ==
Unlike normal potions, ominous bottles disintegrate when drunk by a player. There are 2 main events that take place when the player drinks the bottle at certain locations.

=== Ominous Trials ===
When a player drinks the ominous bottle in the Trial Chambers, any nearby [[Trial Spawner]]s (especially the non-activated ones) will reactivate, turning from normal orange [[particles]] to soul blue trial omen particles. Trial Omen particles will look like miniature soul blue skulls. This event is called Ominous Trials, as the Bad Omen is changed to Trial Omen. This event is only possible in [[survival mode]] and when the [[difficulty]] is not set to [[peaceful]].


=== Village Raids ===
When a player drinks the ominous bottle in a [[Village]], the Bad Omen changes into [[Raid Omen]], thus summoning a Village Raid. This event is possible in both Survival and Creative Modes.

Raid Omen particles will look like miniature crimson red skulls floating.
