{{History||1.6|snap=Test Build 3|Unpowered redstone repeater items can be obtained from the newly added debug chests, mainly from the 8th slot of the 4th chest from the left, and the 22nd slot of the 7th chest from the left.
|Powered redstone repeater items can be obtained from the newly added debug chests, mainly from the 9th slot of the 4th chest from the left, and the 23rd slot of the 7th chest from the left.}}

{{History||1.7.2|snap=13w37a|The direct item forms of unpowered and powered repeaters have been removed from the game. They can no longer exist as an item in any way, only as a placed block.}}
