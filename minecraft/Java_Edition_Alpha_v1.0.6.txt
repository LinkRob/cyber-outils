'''Alpha v1.0.6''',<ref>{{ytl|wnMN_-X_bzE|Minecraft Gameplay|NeXuSJumpeR|July 16, 2010}}</ref> also known as '''Seecret Friday 5''', is the fifth [[seecret update]] which added [[boat]]s, [[cacti]] and large [[tree]]s.

== Additions ==

=== Blocks ===
; [[File:Cactus JE1.png|32px]] [[Cactus]]
* Player receives {{hp|1}} damage when ''punching them'', ''right clicking on them'', or ''making steps on them''.
* [[File:Cactus (item) JE1.png|32px]] They look like this in the inventory.
=== Non-mob entities ===
; [[File:Oak Boat JE1.png|32px]][[File:Oak Boat (item) JE1.png|32px]] [[Boat]]s
* Can be ridden by right-clicking on them.
* They will break if they are placed on the land, or if impact with the land is made.
* Can fit into a single water block.
=== World generator ===
* Re-added large [[trees]].
* Not as large as they were when they were introduced in [[Infdev 20100413]].

== Changes ==

=== Items ===
; [[File:Snowball JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Snowballs]]
* [[Snowballs]] now stack up to 16 instead of 8.
* Thrown snowballs won't disappear anymore.

'''General'''
* [[Item]]s can now be seen being held in [[third person]], but they are huge in terms of scale.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Animals]] only spawn on [[grass]].

=== Recipes ===
* [[Snow block]]s only take 4 snowballs instead of 9.

=== General ===
* [[Leaves]] don't disappear naturally.
* Nighttime is longer.

== Fixes ==
;1 bug fixed
* Fixed snow falling through snow blocks.</onlyinclude>
