'''Virtual reality''' ('''VR''') was a mode natively exclusive to {{el|be}} for {{w|Microsoft Windows|Windows}} (formerly ''[[Windows 10 Edition]]'') and {{w|PlayStation 4}}. It can be used with the {{w|Oculus Rift}}, {{w|Reality Labs#Quest|Meta Quest}} (via Link and Air Link), {{w|PlayStation VR}}, and all {{w|Windows Mixed Reality}} compatible headsets. It was previously available on {{w|Samsung Gear VR|Gear VR}} until October 2020, when it was discontinued along with some older devices.<ref name=vr>https://minecraft.net/vr</ref>

PlayStation VR support was unfortunately discontinued in March 2025, with its last supporting versions between 1.21.30 and 1.21.80.
