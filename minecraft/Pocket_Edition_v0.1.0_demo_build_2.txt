=== Pocket Edition v0.1.0 build 2 demo ===
Pocket Edition v0.1.0 build 2 demo is an unreleased version of Minecraft Pocket Edition, which was in its early testing phase. it's successor is [[Pocket Edition v0.1.0 alpha]] and it's major successor is Pocket Edition v0.1.0 alpha. It was released on August 15, 2011.
