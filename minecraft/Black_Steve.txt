'''Black Steve''' was a humanoid mob available during [[Java Edition Indev]], they were available from [[Java Edition Indev 0.31 20100129]] until [[Java Edition Indev 0.31 20100131|0.31 20100131]] (for 2 days).

== Spawning ==
[[File:In-game steve.png|thumb|Black Steve in the world along with other Steves.]]
Black Steve spawned randomly in the world in at any light level as long as there was enough space.

== Behavior ==
Black Steve was a passive mob. It had no animation, so it glided aimlessly around the world in the same pose and occasionally jumped, although no jumping animation existed either. As a result, it would simply be raised off of the ground and then fall back down. 
Upon [[death]], Black Steve would not fall over to the ground or flash red or white and appear to die, as most mobs do. It would just disappear shortly thereafter, and [[drops|drop]] the [[item]]s it was designated to drop.

== Drops ==
Upon death, Black Steve drops:

*{{ItemLink|Feather}} (0–2)
*{{ItemLink|Gunpowder}} (0–2)
*{{ItemLink|Flint and Steel}} (0–1) (extremely rare)
