'''''Minecraft: Trails & Tales (Original Game Soundtrack)''''' is the first soundtrack for ''[[Minecraft]]'' by [[Aaron Cherof]]. It was released on Spotify on April 26, 2023.

The album includes 5 songs for a total length of just under 22 minutes. It was released alongside [[Java Edition 23w17a]] to provide ambient music to many existing overworld biomes, along with the new [[cherry grove]] biome.

All five songs would later be remixed as part of ''[[Minecraft: Soothing Synths]]'', which was released on September 18, 2023.
