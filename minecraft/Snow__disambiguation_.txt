'''Snow''' may refer to:

== Blocks ==
* {{BlockLink|Snow}} - A layer of snow that accumulates during snowy weather.
* {{BlockLink|Snow Block}} - A full block of snow.
* {{BlockLink|Powder Snow}} - A type of snow that slows entities and inflicts freezing damage.
* {{BlockLink|Powder Snow Cauldron}} - A cauldron filled with powder snow.

== Items ==
* {{ItemLink|Snowball}} - An item harvested from snow and snow blocks, which can be thrown.
* {{ItemLink|Powder Snow Bucket}} - An item that holds powder snow.
* {{ItemLink|Snow Golem Spawn Egg}}

== Mobs ==
* {{EntityLink|Snow Golem}} - A mob created using snow blocks.
** {{EntityLink|link=Snow Golem#Behavior|Sheared Snow Golem}}
* {{EntityLink|Snow fox}} - Snowy variation of [[fox]].

== Biomes ==
* {{BiomeLink|Snowy Taiga}} - A forest-like [[biome]] covered in snow.
* {{BiomeLink|Snowy Plains}} - A largely barren biome covered in snow.
* {{BiomeLink|Grove}} - A forest-like [[biome]] covered in snow and powder snow.
* {{BiomeLink|Snowy Beach}} - A beach covered in snow.
