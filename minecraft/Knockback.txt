'''Knockback''' is an [[enchantment]] to a [[sword]] or [[spear]] that increases its [[Damage#Knockback|knockback]].

== Usage ==
The knockback multiplies distance of the base knockback. Knockback I will have a 105% increase in {{code|knockback.normal}} or {{code|knockback.sprinting}} values and knockback II will have a 190% increase in these values, it combines slightly with knockback caused by attacking while [[sprinting]]. [[Player]]s can cause a knockback of up to six [[block]]s without [[commands]].  

When using commands to increase the knockback value beyond two, the limit on the amount of knockback that a player can take is much less than the amount of knockback that any other entity can take. 

In ''[[Java Edition]]'', players can hit [[Armor Stand|armor stands]] back with a knockback enchantment, but not in [[Bedrock Edition]].  

There are two kinds of knockback in which the enchantment works, normal or sprinting. Both kinds have horizontal knockback, which affects only X and Z coordinate values, and vertical knockback, which affects only Y coordinate value. 

By default in Minecraft, if the attacking players are sprinting, they deal more knockback to the damaged ones. This is the reason of PvP movement-related actions like W-tapping (sprint reset). Damaged players start receiving less knockback while they are in the air. 

On a combat situation where two players are moving, the horizontal knockback received is multiplied by 0.6 (reduced by 40%) if the attacker send an attack packet and is moving forward. It does not stack. 

== Resistant mobs ==
* [[Iron golem]]s are unaffected by knockback from normal attacks, but are affected by knockback from enchantments.
* [[Squid]] are resistant to knockback when outside of water.
* [[Warden]]s are completely unaffected by knockback.
