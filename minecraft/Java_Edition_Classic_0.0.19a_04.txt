'''0.0.19a_04''' is a version of [[Classic]] released on June 20, 2009, at 00:53 UTC.<ref name="info">[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''P7:49:51 <Notch> if you're running too fast, it SHOULD kick you. But the client really should throttle it better''" [...] "''P7:52:15 <Notch> the client sends movement data while downloading the level''" [...] "''P7:52:25 <Notch> that could DEFINITELY cause the too much lag thing''" [...] "''P7:53:28 <Notch> _04 is up now, fixing that''", "''P7:53:36 <Notch> if you still get kicked in _04, let me know''" (June 20, 2009, 00:49 to 00:53 UTC)</ref>

== Fixes ==
* Tried again to fix a bug where the client incorrectly throttles players that get kicked for running too fast due to the client sending movement data while downloading a level.<ref name="info"/>
** This fix again did not work.<ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''P7:59:14 <Rroff> anyone else getting "rubber band lag" since the update?''" [...] "''P7:59:34 <Rroff> and getting kicked for "Cheat Detected: Too much lag"''" [...] "''P8:00:13 <Rroff> I get a feelig of taking 1 step forward, 2 steps back''" [...] "''P8:02:05 <Rroff> when jumping its very odd''", "''P8:02:10 <Rroff> I suddenly go fast then slow in spurts''" (June 20, 2009, 00:59 to 01:02 UTC)</ref>
** Notch said that a bug caused this in the sampling of his time adjustment code.<ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''P8:02:59 <Notch> ok, that's the sampling time of my time adjustment code''" (June 20, 2009, 02:59 UTC)</ref>

== Bugs ==
* [[Water]] tiles have light blue bits in their borders.
