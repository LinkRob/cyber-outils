The '''''Minecraft Official Novels'''''<ref>[https://news.xbox.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2021/04/Minecraft-Franchise-Fact-Sheet_April-2021.pdf ''Minecraft Franchise Fact Sheet - April 2021'']</ref> are a series of novels for ''[[Minecraft]]''. The novels are written by various authors, and the series has sold a combined five million copies as of June 2023.<ref>[https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/726225/minecraft-journey-to-the-ancient-city-by-danny-lore/ "The next blockbuster Minecraft novel from the bestselling publishing program–now with over five million books in print!" - ''Minecraft: Journey to the Ancient City'' by Danny Lore]</ref>
