'''Chase the Skies''' (also referred to as '''Drop 2 2025'''{{Only|BE}}) is a game drop that features [[Locator Bar]], [[Dried Ghast]]s, [[Happy Ghast]]s, [[Happy Ghast|Ghastlings]], [[Vibrant Visuals]]{{Only|BE|short=1}}{{Upcoming|JE}}, and improved functionality usage of [[Lead]]s. It was released on June 17, 2025.

Prior to its name reveal, it was referred to as "Summer Drop 2025".

== Additions ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Locator Bar]]
** A new feature for the [[Heads-up display|HUD]] that overrides in place of the XP bar.
** Has colored waypoints.
** Waypoints disappear if:
*** The player is under invisibility status effect.
*** The player is sneaking.
*** The player wears a mob head.
** Only works in multiplayer mode.
* [[Vibrant Visuals]]
** A new graphics mode that adds water shaders, specular highlights, emissive textures, and directional lighting and shadows.
** Affects the graphical appearance, and has no effect on gameplay.
** Only supports on high-end devices.
*** Mojang also promised to optimize Vibrant Visuals so it could run on low-end devices.{{Citation needed}}

=== [[Block]]s ===
* [[Dried Ghast]]
** Can be found at soul sand valleys in the [[Nether]].
** Can be crafted using a soul sand and 8 ghast tears.
** Turns into a ghastling when soaked in water over time.
** Has 4 stages:
*** Dried (default)
*** Partially hydrated (stage 1)
*** Mostly hydrated (stage 2)
*** Hydrated (stage 3)
** Consequence: Reverts back to its initial dried (default) stage if:
*** The [[player]] breaks the dried ghast (regardless of stage)
*** The water is removed during hydration

=== [[Mob]]s ===
* [[Happy Ghast]]
** Spawns after a ghastling grows up (either over time or feeding with Snowballs).
*** Can also be spawned using its spawn egg.
** Passive version of [[Ghast]].
** Can be ridable up to 4 players using a [[Harness]].
*** Can also stand on top of them (with or without harnesses), like normal blocks.
* [[Happy Ghast|Ghastling]]
** Spawns after a dried ghast gets hydrated.
*** Can also be spawned by using this command: {{cmd|/summon happy_ghast ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ minecraft:spawn_baby}}.
** Turns into a happy ghast over time.
*** Feeding it with [[Snowball]]s can speed up the process.

=== [[Item]]s ===
* [[Spawn Egg|Happy Ghast Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to spawn happy ghasts directly.
*** There is also a chance of spawning a ghastling directly when spawned using a spawn egg.
* [[Harness]]
** Can be crafted using 3 leathers, 2 glass blocks, and a matching wool color.
** Can be used to ride on happy ghasts.
** Comes with 16 colored variants.
* [[Music Disc|Music Disc Tears]]
** A new music disc consists of a song "Tears" composed by Amos Roddy.
** Like any music discs, it can also be played on [[Jukebox]].
** Can be obtained by killing a [[Ghast]] (by deflecting a [[Fireball]]).

{(This Section doesn't belong in [[Chase The Skies]] Game drop, and only appears in [[Java Edition 1.21.7]])
* [[Music Disc|Lava Chicken Music Disc]]
** A new music disc consists of the song "Lava Chicken" by Hyper Potions.
** Like any music discs, it can also be played on [[Jukebox|jukebox]].
** Can be obtained by killing a [[Chicken Jockey]].}

=== [[Advancement]]/[[Achievement]] ===
* Stay Hydrated
** Requires players to place a dried ghast into water

* Heart Transplanter
** Requires players to place a [[creaking heart]] in between two [[log|pale oak logs]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Item]]s ===
* [[Lead]]s
** Added more functionality usage:
*** Can now cause leashed mobs to unleash when using a [[Firework Rocket|firework rocket]] boost while flying with [[Elytra|elytra]].
*** Can now be leashed to a pre-existing Leash Knot by interacting with it.
**** Can now also retrieve entities tied to a Leash Knot by interacting with it.
*** Can now use [[Shears|shears]] to remove leash knots.
*** Can now be leashed to any other leashable entity.
**** To leash an entity to another entity, the player must:
***** Have the entity already leashed to themselves.
***** Sneak and interact with the target entity while holding the lead.
**** Can now be "stolen" by leashing them again, transferring ownership to the player.
**** Re-leashing an entity would result in immediate leash snapping due to distance.
**** To prevent erratic behavior when leashed to each other, llamas that are leashed no longer attempt to form caravans.
**** Cannot be leashed to itself.
**** Can only be leashed one way - to another entity; they cannot have other mobs leashed to them.
*** Can now be leashed to ridable mobs and entities (except [[Minecart|minecart]]s).
*** Can now be used on leashed entities to unleash them using shears.
*** Cannot cut other players' leashes.
*** Dispensers can now use shears to unleash leashed entities.
** Can now be crafted with 5 [[String|strings]] in the same pattern when crafting with 4 strings and a [[Slimeball|slimeball]] before this update.
* [[Saddle]]s
** Can now be crafted with 3 leathers and an iron ingot.
** Can now be removed on mobs equipped with it using shears.
* [[Shears]]
** Can now be used to unequip items from mobs.
*** Can only be removed when there are no players mounting on it.
