'''Wildflowers''' are a decorative block similar to [[pink petals]].

== Appearence ==
Unlike Pink Petals, Wildflowers consists of small yellow and white [[Flower]]s that look vaguely similar to dandelions and azure bluets.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Wildflowers can be broken instantly with any [[item]] or by hand.

Wildflowers also break if [[water]] or [[lava]] runs over its location, or if a [[piston]] extends or pushes a [[block]] into its location.

=== Natural generation ===
Wildflowers generate naturally in [[birch forest]]s, [[old growth birch forest]]s and [[meadow]]s.

=== Post-generation ===
Each time [[bone meal]] is applied to wildflowers, they drop a copy of themselves, similar to tall flowers.

Wildflowers also generate when bone meal is applied to a [[grass block]] in birch forests, old growth birch forests and meadows.‌

=== Trading ===
[[Wandering trader]]s sometimes sell wildflowers for 1 emerald.

== Usage ==
More than one wildflower can be placed on a block, similarly to other blocks such as [[pink petals]], [[sea pickle]]s and [[candle]]s. Up to four wildflowers can be placed in a group on one block.

=== Bees ===
[[Bees]] can pollinate wildflowers like other flowers. They can also be [[Breeding|bred]] using wildflowers. 

=== Bee nests ===
[[Oak]], [[birch]], and [[cherry]] trees grown from [[sapling]]s that are within 2 blocks of wildflowers have a 5% chance to grow with a [[bee nest]] containing 2-3 [[bee]]s.

=== Composting ===
Placing wildflowers into a [[composter]] has a 30% chance of raising the compost level by 1.
