'''''Minecraft: PlayStation 3 Edition''''' is the {{w|PlayStation 3}} edition of ''[[Minecraft]]'', developed by [[4J Studios]] for [[Mojang Studios]].<ref>{{tweet|4J|370111405216649216|Happy to confirm we're developing Minecraft PS3, PS4 & PS Vita Editions for Mojang, as well as Xbox 360 & Xbox One Editions!|August 21, 2013}}</ref> ''Minecraft: PlayStation 3 Edition'' is the first edition of ''Minecraft'' to be released on a console manufactured by {{w|Sony Interactive Entertainment|Sony}}.

''Minecraft: PlayStation 3 Edition'' was announced at Sony Interactive Entertainment's exhibit at [[wikipedia:Gamescom|Gamescom]] 2013, along with the [[PlayStation 4 Edition|PlayStation 4]], and [[PlayStation Vita Edition|PlayStation Vita]], and was officially released on the PlayStation Store on Tuesday, December 17, 2013 in North America and Wednesday, December 18, 2013 in Europe, for $19.99, £12.85, or €18.47n.<ref>{{tweet|Dinnerbone|369887393253699584|Minecraft was just announced for PlayStation 3 & 4!|August 20, 2013}}</ref><ref>{{tweet|jeb_|412596287222317056|The wait is over!|December 16, 2013}}</ref> It was originally announced that ''Minecraft: PlayStation 3 Edition'' would no longer be available for digital purchase due to the PlayStation Store closing on the platform on July 2, 2021, however this statement was later retracted.<ref>{{citation|url=https://blog.playstation.com/2021/04/19/playstation-store-on-ps3-and-ps-vita-will-continue-operations/|title=PlayStation Store on PS3 and PS Vita Will Continue Operations|author=Jim Ryan|website=PlayStation Blog|date=April 19, 2021}}</ref>

As of [[PlayStation 3 Edition 1.84|1.84]], the ''PlayStation 3 Edition'' has not received new feature updates, such as [[Village & Pillage]], [[Buzzy Bees]], the [[Nether Update]], [[Caves & Cliffs]], and so on.

Although the PlayStation 3 console has been discontinued with all the other [[Legacy Console Edition]]s, the ''PlayStation 3 Edition'' continued to receive feature updates until the release of the [[Update Aquatic]].<ref>{{Mcnet|update-aquatic-coming-legacy-console-editions|Update Aquatic Coming To Legacy Console Editions|May 3, 2018}}</ref>

==Retail Disc==

On April 16, 2014, Sony announced the retail disc version of ''Minecraft: PlayStation 3 Edition'', which was released on May 14, 2014 in Europe and on May 16, 2014 in North America. The game otherwise is identical to the downloadable version. Features such as buying or downloading content on the PlayStation Network are the same for both versions and they both updated at the same time.<ref>[http://blog.us.playstation.com/2014/04/16/minecraft-playstation-3-edition-coming-to-retail-may-16th/ "Minecraft: PlayStation 3 Edition Coming to Retail May 16th"] – PlayStation.Blog, April 16, 2014</ref><ref>[http://blog.eu.playstation.com/2014/04/16/minecraft-playstation-3-edition-gets-blu-ray-release-next-month "Minecraft: PlayStation 3 Edition gets a Blu-ray release next month"] – PlayStation.Blog.Europe, April 16, 2014</ref>

==Skin packs==

4J Studios planned to produce Skin Packs as downloadable content. Each Skin Pack was said to include 40+ skins to choose from. The first Skin Pack for PlayStation 3, [[Skin#Festive Skin Pack|Festive Skin Pack]] was released on the same day when ''Minecraft: PlayStation 3 Edition'' was released which was December 17, 2013.

[[Skin#Skin Pack 1|Skin Pack 1]] was released on March 27, 2014.

The [[Skin#Battle & Beasts Skin Pack|Battle & Beasts Skin Pack]] was also released on March 27, 2014.

[[Skin#Skin Pack 2|Skin Pack 2]] and [[Skin#Battle & Beasts Skin Pack 2|Battle & Beasts Skin Pack 2]] were released in May 2016.

== Cross compatibility with PS Vita==

Through Sony's "Cross Buy" initiative, owners of ''PlayStation 3 Edition'' are able to download ''[[PlayStation Vita Edition]]'' at no additional cost. Since the PS Vita Edition is based on the PS3 Edition, saves on PS3 Edition are compatible with PS Vita Edition and vice versa.

This does not apply to ''[[PlayStation 4 Edition]]''. It is possible to do a one-way save transfer from PS3 to PS4. However, since February 25, 2016, there is no longer a discount on PS4 Edition for owners of PS3 Edition. As of April 15, 2020, it is no longer possible to transfer saves between PS3s, between PS Vitas, between PS TVs, and from PS3/PS Vita/PS TV to PS4.<ref>"[https://minecraft.net/article/enjoy-saving-your-playstation-game- Enjoy saving your PlayStation game? | Minecraft]" Sofia Dankis, April 3, 2020</ref>
