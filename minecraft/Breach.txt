'''Breach''' is a [[mace]] [[enchantment]] that ignores 15% of [[armor]] damage reduction per level.

== Incompatibilities ==
Breach is mutually exclusive with [[Smite]], [[Bane of Arthropods]], and [[Density]]. However, if [[commands]] are used to have two or more of these enchantments on the same item, the effects stack.
