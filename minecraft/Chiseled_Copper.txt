'''Chiseled copper''' is a chiseled variant of [[copper]]. They mostly generate in the [[trial chambers]], especially surrounding a [[trial spawner]].

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Chiseled copper blocks can be mined only with a stone [[pickaxe]] or better. If a chiseled copper is mined without the use of a pickaxe, it drops nothing.

=== Waxing ===
Chiseled copper blocks can be turned into the respective waxed chiseled copper blocks by {{Control|using}} a [[honeycomb]] item on them. Waxed chiseled copper blocks do not oxidize and are identical to the non-waxed version.

=== Scraping ===
{{Control|Using}} an [[axe]] on a waxed chiseled copper turns it into the respective non-waxed chiseled copper. In addition, using an axe on an exposed, weathered, or oxidized chiseled copper reverts it one stage to a regular, exposed, or weathered chiseled copper, respectively.

=== Lightning strike ===
Non-waxed chiseled copper blocks are completely deoxidized when struck by lightning, and other non-waxed chiseled copper blocks nearby are deoxidized randomly.
