'''The Garden Awakens''' is a game drop that features [[pale garden]], [[flower|Eyeblossoms]], [[creaking]]s, and other related blocks. It is released on December 3, 2024.<ref>[https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/the-garden-awakens-drop-date The Garden Awakens drop date]</ref> It was also announced at [[Minecraft Live 2024]].

Prior to its name reveal on November 27, 2024, it was referred to as the "'''Winter Drop'''".

=== Items ===
* [[Resin Clump]]
** Can be obtained through creaking hearts.
** Can be used to craft resin blocks.
* [[Resin Brick]]
** Can be used to craft resin bricks.
** Can be used to trim armor.
* [[Creaking Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to spawn [[creaking]]s
*** Creakings spawned this way:
**** Does not link to the creaking heart
**** Persist through day and night
**** Vulnerable to all forms of damage
**** Dies in one hit

=== Non-mob entities ===
* [[Pale Oak Boat]]
** Can be crafted with 5 pale oak planks.
* [[Pale Oak Boat with Chest]]
** Can be crafted with a [[chest]] and pale oak boat.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Creaking]]
** A new hostile mob spawned by active creaking hearts during the night or thunderstorms.
*** Can also be spawned using a spawn egg, by the {{cmd|summon}} command or from a [[Monster Spawner|monster spawner]], which is not connected to a creaking heart, persist through day and night, and deals {{hp|1}} damage, killing it instantly or in a single hit.
** It moves and attacks when a [[player]] is looking away from it.
** Unlike most mobs, it can only be killed by destroying its respective creaking heart.
*** Although, it can also be killed instantly when spawned using a spawn egg or by {{cmd|summon}} command.
** It emits particles that lead to its creaking heart when hit or damaged.
** [[Illagers]] are afraid of the creaking and flee from it on sight.

=== Biomes ===
* [[Pale Garden]]
** A new biome that generates with pale oak trees, pale moss blocks, pale moss carpets, pale hanging moss, and eyeblossoms.
** It is quiet in daytime, but very eerie at nighttime.
** It is also where creakings spawn during nighttime or during thunderstorms.

=== Structures ===
* Pale Oak tree
** Generates as part of [[pale garden]].
** Made of pale oak logs and pale oak leaves.

== Trivia ==
* Unlike most Minecraft version updates, "The Garden Awakens Soundtrack" does not play in-game.
** The songs in this soundtrack album are mostly remixes of "Infinite Amethyst from [[Minecraft: Caves & Cliffs (Original Game Soundtrack)]], but named as "Infinite Spooky Amethyst".
