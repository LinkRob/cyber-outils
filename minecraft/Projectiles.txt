A projectile is a type of [[entity]]. They can be thrown like [[Ender Pearl|ender pearls]] or shot from tools like the [[bow]] and [[Arrow]].

They serve different purposes. An example is [[Splash Potion|Splash potions]] are used to heal allies or kill enemy [[Player|players]] and [[Mob|Mobs.]]
