'''Impaling''' is an [[enchantment]] for a [[trident]], causing the trident to deal extra damage on each hit against [[aquatic mobs]]{{Only|java}}, or extra damage to mobs in water{{Only|bedrock}}.

== Usage ==
Each level of Impaling adds 2.5 ({{hp|2|notag=1}} × {{frac|1|1|4}}) extra damage per hit.

{{IN|java}}, only [[aquatic mobs]] receive the extra damage. Aquatic mobs include {{EntityLink|axolotl}}s, {{EntityLink|dolphin}}s, {{EntityLink|guardian}}s, {{EntityLink|elder guardian}}s, {{EntityLink|squid}}, {{EntityLink|glow squid}}s, {{EntityLink|turtle}}s and {{EntityLink|id=cod|link=fish|{{EntitySprite|salmon}} {{EntitySprite|pufferfish}} {{EntitySprite|tropical-fish}} all variants of fish}}, but not [[drowned]],<ref>{{bug|MC-128249|||WAI}}</ref> as drowned are classified as undead mobs rather than underwater mobs.

{{IN|bedrock}}, as well as in [[Java Edition Combat Test 4]] and after, the damage applies to ''all'' mobs and players when in contact with water: underwater, in flowing water, and in rain.<ref>{{bug|MCPE-34025}} — resolved as "Works As Intended". </ref>
