* {{nbt|compound}} Entity data
** {{nbt inherit/entity}}
** {{nbt|int-array|Owner}}: The [[UUID]] of the entity that that fired the fangs, stored as four ints. If the entity is an [[Illager]], the fangs do not damage other Illagers.
** {{nbt|int|Warmup}}: Time in ticks until the fangs appear. The fangs appear and begin to close as soon as this value becomes zero or less; negative values simply result in no delay. The value continues ticking down while the closing animation is playing, reaching -20 on naturally spawned fangs.
