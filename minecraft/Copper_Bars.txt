A '''copper bars''' is a copper variant block that acts similarly to [[Iron Bars|iron bars]], and oxidizes like normal [[copper]] blocks.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Copper bars can be broken with any pickaxe. Otherwise, it drops nothing.

== Usage ==
Copper bars shares the same functionality as its [[Iron Bars|iron counterpart]].

=== Oxidation ===
Like most [[Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper bars undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
* Unoxidized - Normal stage
* Exposed
* Weathered
* Oxidized
Only lightning and [[axe]]s can reverse the oxidation process. Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper bars prevents the oxidation.
