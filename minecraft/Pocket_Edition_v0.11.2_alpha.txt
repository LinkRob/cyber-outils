'''v0.11.2 alpha''' is an update to ''[[Minecraft Pocket Edition]]'' that was released exclusively to Windows Phone. No fixes or modifications on this version were documented. This version includes all changes and fixes from [[Pocket Edition v0.11.0 alpha|v0.11.0]] and [[Pocket Edition v0.11.1 alpha|v0.11.1]] for the Windows Phone.
