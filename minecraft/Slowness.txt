'''Slowness''' is an [[effect]] that decreases [[walking]] speed.

== Effect ==
Slowness decreases walking speed by 15% × ''level'' and contracts the player's field of view accordingly. [[Status effect#negativelevels|Negative levels]] of Slowness increase walking speed. However, [[sprinting]]-[[jumping]] is barely affected.

== Unaffected mobs ==

[[Wither]]s and [[ender dragon]]s are immune to the Slowness effect. While not immune, flying mobs are unaffected by the Slowness effect.<ref>{{bug|MC-40949}} - Flying mobs do not obey generic.movementSpeed attribute</ref> [[Shulker]]s are also unaffected by, though not immune to, Slowness.

== Notes ==
* A weapon enchanted with [[Bane of Arthropods]] inflicts Slowness IV on [[spider]]s, [[cave spider]]s, [[silverfish]], [[endermite]]s and [[bee]]s; duration is dependent on the enchantment level.
* Levels 7–127 render the player unable to move unless jumping while throwing an [[ender pearl]].
* Alternative methods of reducing travel speed include: walking on [[soul sand]]s, [[slime block]]s, or [[honey block]]s, or through [[sweet berry bush|berry bushes]] or [[cobweb]]s; [[swimming]]; riding a [[boat]] on land; [[sneaking]]; eating; drinking [[honey bottle]], [[milk]] or a [[potion]] (travel speed is reduced while drinking ''any'' potion, regardless of the effects of the potion after drinking is finished); aiming a [[trident]] or [[bow]], charging [[crossbow]] and blocking with a [[shield]].
