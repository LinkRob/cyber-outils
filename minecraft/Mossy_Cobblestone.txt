'''Mossy cobblestone''' is a variant of [[cobblestone]] with green moss on it. It is mainly used for [[crafting]] or as building block.

== Obtaining ==
Mossy cobblestone can be mined using a [[pickaxe]], in which case it drops itself. If mined without a pickaxe, then the mining is slower and it drops nothing.

=== Natural generation ===
Mossy cobblestone occurs naturally in [[dungeon]]s, [[jungle temple]]s, [[pillager outpost]]s, [[underwater ruins]], and plains, taiga, snowy taiga, and zombie [[village]]s. It can also be found in the form of [[forest rock]]s in the [[old growth taiga]] biome.

== Usage ==
Mossy cobblestone can be used as a basic building block, as it is blast resistant and fairly common.
