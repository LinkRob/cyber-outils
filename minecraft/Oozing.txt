'''Oozing''' is an effect that spawns 2 medium sized [[slime]]s when any entity with the effect is killed. This potion effect is mainly used in Trial Chambers, along with [[Weaving]], [[Infested]], and [[Wind Charged]]. Oozing effect occurs when the player is hit by a splash potion of oozing while the [[Trial Omen]] effect is activated. (Or by brewing it, then drinking it/using gunpowder to make your own splash potion/using [[Dragon's Breath]] to make it a [[Lingering Potion]].)
