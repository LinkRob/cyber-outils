"'''Crops"''' are defined as "cultivated plants or agricultural produce, such as grain, vegetables, or fruit, considered as a group" (source: [https://ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=crop American Heritage Dictionary]). In ''Minecraft'', "crops" may refer to the following food-related items and blocks:
*{{ItemLink|Wheat Seeds}} – The growing wheat seeds block is called "Crops" in-game.
**{{ItemLink|Wheat}} – The crop harvested from fully-grown wheat planted from wheat seeds.
*{{ItemLink|Beetroot Seeds}} – A crop harvested from beetroots.
**{{ItemLink|Beetroot}} – A crop harvested from beetroots.
*{{ItemLink|Carrot}} – The crop harvested from carrots.
*{{ItemLink|Potato}} – A crop harvested from potatoes.
**{{ItemLink|Poisonous Potato}} – A crop harvested from potatoes.
*{{BlockLink|Melon}} – The fruit crop harvested from fully-grown melons planted from melon seeds.

*{{BlockLink|Farmland}} – The block where all the previous crops can and should be planted. It is created by using a hoe on dirt or grass.
*{{ItemLink|Bamboo}} – The crop harvested from a bamboo sapling.
*{{ItemLink|Cocoa Beans}} – The crop harvested from cocoa plants.
*{{ItemLink|Sugar Cane}} – The crop harvested from sugar canes.

*{{BlockLink|Cactus}} – The crop harvested from itself. Can only be planted on any sand block.
*{{BlockLink|Mushrooms}} – The crop harvested from itself. As long it is planted in dark areas or on podzol or mycelium it can be grown into a giant mushroom by adding bone meal. Also can spread under the same conditions.
*{{ItemLink|Kelp}} – The crop harvested from itself. It can be planted anywhere underwater.
*{{BlockLink|Sea Pickle}} – The crop harvested from itself. Should be planted on any alive coral block underwater. If all conditions are met can also spread over other coral blocks when planted.
*{{BlockLink|Nether Wart}} – A crop that can only be planted on soul sand. Primarily used for brewing.
*{{ItemLink|Chorus Fruit}} – A crop harvested from chorus plants.
*{{BlockSprite|All Fungi}}[[Fungus]] – A mushroom that grows in the Nether that is planted on nylium. With bone meal, these become huge fungi, with crimson and warped variants.
*{{ItemLink|Glow Berries}} – A crop harversted from cave vines.
