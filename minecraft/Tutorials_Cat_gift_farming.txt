Cat gift farming is a process of using a tamed cat to give you items in the morning if it sleeps on or is near enough to a bed which the player is sleeping in to skip the night. It allows you to renewably farm [[rabbit's foot|rabbit's feet]], [[rabbit hide]], [[string]], [[rotten flesh]], [[feather]]s, [[raw chicken]] and [[phantom membrane]]s without much effort, and is another option of obtaining phantom membranes if killing phantoms is not a viable option (e.g. a server with One Person Sleeps mod, or a half hearted hardcore world).
