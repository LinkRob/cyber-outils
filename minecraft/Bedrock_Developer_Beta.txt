== What edition is this? ==
The Developer beta is an edition of Minecraft that can only be used by developers and was originally created for testing purposes.

He received an email from [[Mojang Studios]] associates asking him to take down the video, threatening legal action against him, and the video was later taken down.<ref>{{tweet|elementx47|1528683227526877188|Ok, so I was contacted by mojang a few days ago requesting to remove this video. ==Notable Differences==
[[File:Minecraft Developer Give Debug.jpg|right|frameless|180x180px]]
[[File:Minecraft Developer Debug Stick.jpg|right|frameless|180x180px]]
This version has additional development features intended to aid developers, which include:{{cn}} 
*A [[debug screen]] similar to that in [[Java Edition]].
*The ability to enable on-screen touch screen controls to test them for mobile versions.
*Advanced settings for terrain generation and weather.
*A menu allowing for the modification of blocks, entities and particles in-game.
*Creation of custom [[biome]]s.
*A maximum framerate sliders
*
* Automation
*The ability to add free minecoins to the user's account.
*The ability to slow down time.
* The ability to view hitboxes, mob info and AI pathfinding.
*Various graphs.
*[[Structure Block|Structure block]] data mode available
*Offers on featured servers
*[[Debug Stick]]
