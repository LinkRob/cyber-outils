'''Wind Burst''' is an [[enchantment]] for the [[mace]] that launches the user into the air seven blocks per level after performing a smash attack.

==Obtaining==
Wind Burst is a rare drop and can only be obtained from [[ominous vault]]s found in [[trial chamber]]s.

==Usage==
A [[mace]] with Wind Burst will "rebound" a player into the air after performing a smash attack. This can allow the player to continue to "bounce" on a player or mob with a chain of smash attacks one after another.

Each level increases the "rebound" height by 7 blocks.

*Level I (1)  - 7 blocks
*Level II (2) - 14 blocks
*Level III (3) - 21 blocks

Wind burst will '''not''' save you from fall damage. Therefore, it is best to pair with [[boots]] enchanted with [[Feather Falling]], a [[Potion]] of [[Slow Falling]], or an [[elytra]]. Alternatively, have handy a way to catch your fall, like a [[water bucket]] or [[wind charge]].

Wind Burst III launches the player up an impressive 21 blocks, which, while it allows for tons of damage, is high enough to kill a player with no [[armor]] and seriously damage a player with armor upon falling. Thus, if you are not confident in your ability to break your fall on your way down, you might want to opt for a lower level.

To activate Wind Burst and start a chain of smash attacks on relatively flat terrain, one can launch themselves upwards with a [[wind charge]] or even jump from a one-block height before hitting their target.
