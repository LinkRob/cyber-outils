'''MINECON 2015''' was held in London, England at the ExCeL London Exhibition and Conference Centre from July 4 to 5.<ref name="el">{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150328051446/https://www.excel-london.co.uk/whats-on/minecon-2015/|title=MINECON 2015|website=ExCeL London}}</ref> The [[Combat Update]] was showcased during the event.

== Announcement ==
On March 30, 2014, it had originally been announced that the next MINECON would take place somewhere in Europe.

Since Mojang was busy developing the next Minecraft version, [[Java Edition 1.8]], news updates for the next MINECON were silently on hold.
<br>
Official announcements were never heard of until 5 months later, when the predicted date and location was announced on Mojang's site.
<br>
It was confirmed that MINECON would not be held in 2014, instead it was moved to Spring 2015, and was held in London.<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140821162300/https://mojang.com/2014/08/minecon-2015/|title=MINECON 2015|author=Vu Bui|website=Mojang.com|date=August 21, 2014}}</ref>

Mojang revealed on February 2 the details for '''MINECON 2015''', confirming that MINECON would be held in ExCel London Exhibition and Conference Centre on July 4–5, 2015.<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150202195025/https://www.mojang.com/2015/02/minecon-2015-announced/|title=MINECON 2015 announced!|author=Vu Bui|website=Mojang.com|date=February 2, 2015}}</ref> They also mentioned that tickets and discounted hotel bookings would be available for sale later in February.

On March 18, 2015 the [http://minecon.mojang.com MINECON Website] was updated to contain a countdown till tickets went on sale. The [https://web.archive.org/web/20150328051446/https://www.excel-london.co.uk/whats-on/minecon-2015/ ExCeL London page] was also published.

== Tickets ==
On March 18, 2015, Mojang announced that tickets would be on sale on Friday March 27 and Saturday March 28, respectively.<ref name="2015Ticket">{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150324033550/https://mojang.com/2015/03/minecon-2015-tickets-announcement/|title=MINECON 2015 tickets announcement!|author=Vu Bui|website=Mojang.com|date=March 18, 2015}}</ref> The tickets were released in two batches,<ref name="m2015"/> each selling 5,000 tickets at £129 ($190 USD) a piece (10,000 tickets in total).<ref name="2015Ticket"/> Attendees were also given discounted hotel bookings.<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150328185033/http://www.corporateteam.com/eventsv2/Evnt_Home.aspx?evnt=2110ME|title=Accommodation booking site for MINECON 2015}}</ref>

== Cape ==
[[File:MINECON 2015 Cape.png|thumb|Steve with the 2015 cape.]]

People who visited MINECON 2015 received an e-mail with a code they could use to add a [[cape]] to a Minecraft account [[Java Edition|(Java only)]].

[[Legacy Console Edition|Console Edition]] and [[Pocket Edition]] players received a free [[skin pack]].

It was available for a limited time, from July 1 until July 15, 2015. It had 8 skins to choose from.

* '''Steve [[MINECON 2011]] cape'''
* '''Steve [[MINECON 2012]] cape'''
* '''Steve [[MINECON 2013]] cape'''
* '''Steve MINECON 2015 cape'''
* '''Alex [[MINECON 2011]] cape'''
* '''Alex [[MINECON 2012]] cape'''
* '''Alex [[MINECON 2013]] cape'''
* '''Alex MINECON 2015 cape'''

The pack is now hidden and costs {{price|coin=1000000}} [[Minecoins]] in the [[Marketplace]].<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200609125002/https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/pdp?id=7dae6bfe-e92b-403e-842e-d8d75e329644|title=Minecon 2015 Skin Pack|website=Minecraft Marketplace}}</ref>
