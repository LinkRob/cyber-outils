A '''parrot''' is a [[Mob#Tamable mobs|tameable]] passive [[mob]] that spawns in jungle [[biome]]s. Parrots can imitate [[sound]]s of nearby monsters and can also perch on the [[player]]'s shoulders.

==Spawning==
Parrots naturally spawn in groups of 1–2 in [[jungle]]s, [[sparse jungle]]s{{only|bedrock|short=1}} and [[bamboo jungle]]s above [[log]]s, [[leaves]] or [[grass block]]s.

Unlike most passive mobs, parrots cannot be [[breeding|bred]].

==Drops==
A parrot drops from one to two [[feather]]s upon death. The amount is increased by one per level of [[Looting]], for a range of one to five with Looting III. From {{xp|1|3}} [[experience]] orbs are dropped when parrots are killed by a player or a tamed [[wolf]].

==Behavior==
Parrots are passive and [[swim]] in [[water]] by flapping their wings. A parrot flies and usually flies upward if it is struck; parrots flap their wings and fall slowly, preventing fall [[damage]]. Parrots fly under normal conditions, but they can get tired <ref>{{tweet|MiaLem_n|846995981208096768|Yes they fly, as high as they want to. :) But they do get tired and prefer to have somewhere to land.|March 29, 2017}}</ref> and return to the ground after a short time. Parrots crowd and settle around other nearby mobs, including neutral and hostile mobs.

Parrots can be tamed by feeding them [[wheat seeds]], [[melon seeds]], [[pumpkin seeds]], [[beetroot seeds]] or [[torchflower seeds]] with {{frac|1|10}} chance of success. Once tamed, {{control|interacting}} with a parrot makes it sit down and stand up.

A tamed parrot follows the [[player]] unless told to sit and teleports if there is a distance of 12 [[block]]s between it and the player. A [[death message]] is displayed to its owner upon death. The parrot teleportation is completely silent.<ref>{{bug|MC-166291|||Invalid}}</ref> {{IN|java}}, attempting to feed a parrot a [[cookie]] instantly kills it, emitting [[Poison]] particles as it dies. {{IN|bedrock}}, feeding a cookie to a parrot gives it [[fatal poison]] instead. This is a reference to the fact that chocolate is toxic to parrots.
[[File:Parrot Shoulder.png|right|thumb|[[Player|Kai]] with a parrot on their shoulder.]]
A tamed parrot on the ground can be made to perch on its [[player]]'s shoulder by moving through the parrot. On its own, a tamed parrot can also fly to and perch on the player's shoulder, unless it has been told to sit. A player can have one parrot on each shoulder. Parrots always prefer a player's left shoulder first, if it is empty.

A parrot dismounts its player when the player:
* does not land on a high-enough surface ({{frac|1|2}} block up or higher)
* drops off a ledge of higher than {{frac|3|4}} of a block
* takes damage
* submerges the player's feet into the water of any height
* starts drowning
* sleeps on a bed
* submerges the player's head in [[lava]] (the parrot dismounts and burns even if the player has [[Fire Resistance]])

Parrots on a shoulder always look in the same direction the player's head is looking.

A parrot on a shoulder cannot take any [[damage]] but may get hurt as soon as it dismounts, as when dismounting a player submerged in [[lava]].

A parrot sitting on the shoulder appears in the [[inventory]] interface.

In ''[[Bedrock Edition]]'', a parrot sitting on a shoulder prevents the player from entering a [[nether portal]].<ref>{{bug|MCPE-27950}}</ref>

===Imitating sounds===
Parrots imitate the idle [[sound]]s of nearby [[Mob#Hostile mobs|hostile]] and certain [[Mob#Neutral mobs|neutral mobs]] (including the hiss of [[creeper]]s but excluding [[guardian]]s and [[elder guardian]]s); they have a detection range of 20 cubical [[block]]s. The sound produced by the parrots is simply the same sound as the [[mob]] being mimicked at a higher pitch. Occasionally, a parrot may imitate sounds of mobs that are not in the area.<ref>{{bug|MC-115509|resolution=Works as Intended}}</ref>

Parrots dance near a [[jukebox]] if a [[music disc]] is inside it. Parrots even have the ability to dance while on a [[player]]'s shoulder.{{only|bedrock|short=y}} This is a reference to the Party Parrot meme.<ref>{{tweet|MiaLem_n|849665440733429761|It took you no time at all to find the party parrot easter egg! But there is one more you haven't found...|April 5, 2017}}</ref> The game does not seem to have any real way to determine when the music ends, though; as long as the disc remains in the jukebox, the parrot continues dancing even after the music stops.<ref>{{bug|MC-157412}}</ref> 

The dancing radius is 3 blocks from the jukebox. If they dance and then fly beyond this radius, they stop dancing. A parrot does not dance if the music disc was inserted prior to it spawning, dismounting or being within the 3 blocks range.<ref>{{bug|MC-115446}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* Red parrots are based on the real-life [[wikipedia:Scarlet macaw|scarlet macaw]] "with a little Minecraft twist".<ref>{{tweet|MiaLem_n|844956044170285057|Macaws with a little Minecraft twist.|March 23, 2017}}</ref> Similarly, blue parrots are based on [[wikipedia:Hyacinth macaw|hyacinth macaws]], cyan parrots are based on [[wikipedia:Blue-and-yellow macaw|blue-and-yellow macaws]], green parrots are based on the [[wikipedia:military macaw|military macaw]] or possibly the [https://www.nzbirdsonline.org.nz/species/antipodes-island-parakeet Antipodes Island Parakeet], and gray parrots are based on [[wikipedia:cockatiel|cockatiels]].
*Warnings texts against feeding [[wikipedia:Chocolate|chocolate]] or [[wikipedia:Avocado|avocados]] to parrots are referenced in two different [[splash]] texts.
*The [[Minecraft]] team uses various party parrot emojis when communicating with each other on discord.<ref>[[Special:Diff/1080413|"Really. We have over 20 different party parrot emojis. :D"]] – HelenAngel, April 5, 2017</ref>
*The [[advancement]] obtained for breeding two animals is called "The Parrots and the Bats", even though neither parrots nor bats can breed.
*The parrot's dancing animation is based on the [https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/party-parrot party parrot emojis], that are in turn, based on the [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9T1vfsHYiKY|Sirocco kākāpō].
*Interacting with a [[jukebox]] will drop a sticker, it will also play the "otherside" music disc. It can only be found in the 15 Year Map.
