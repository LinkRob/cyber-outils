A version of '''[[Indev 0.31]]''' was released on '''December 23, 2009''' at 14:59 UTC.<ref>[[wordofnotch:296862046|Tree shadows, grass growing in slightly lit tiles, and torch tiles]] – The Word of Notch, December 23, 2009</ref><ref name="torches">[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''A9:00:31 <Iguana> What's in the new update? [...] A9:01:10 <08Notch> torches''" (15:00 UTC)</ref>

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[File:Torch JE1.png|32px]] [[File:Wall Torch JE1.png|32px]] [[Torch]]es<ref name="torches"/>
* Emits [[light]].

* Their length is different from the next version (13 pixels instead of 10 on the next version ([[Java Edition Indev 0.31 20091231-1]]).

* If the [[player]] places a torch on the ground next to a wall, the [[torch]] will be attached to the wall. It is possible to create a flying [[torch]] by placing a torch on the ground, then by breaking the ground. It's also possible for [[flower]]s, [[mushroom]]s and [[sapling]]s (since the appearance of [[sapling]]s ([[Java Edition pre-Classic rd-161348]]) until the version [[Java Edition Indev 0.31 20100202]]) except that the torch will stay in the air forever.

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[File:Grass Block JE2.png|32px]] [[Grass block]]s
* Can now grow in slightly lit areas.
; [[File:Oak Sapling JE2.png|32px]] [[Sapling]]s
* Do not decay in slightly lit tiles.
; [[File:Water JE11.gif|32px]] Water
* Changed the side texture of water.
** Now has vertical lines flowing down it.{{verify|Must be the previous version.}}
; [[File:Oak Leaves JE2.png|32px]] [[Leaves]] 
* Now produce shadows.
** The grass under leaves is now dirt due to this.
