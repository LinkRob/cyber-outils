A '''bush''' is a natural decorative plant added in [[Spring to Life]]. Like [[grass|short grass]], It will adapt different shades of green depending on the biome.

== Obtaining ==
Bushes can only be dropped as an item using [[shears]] or any tool enchanted with [[Silk Touch]]. Otherwise, it drops nothing.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Bush|drop=0|shears=1}}

=== Natural generation ===
Bushes naturally generate in the following biomes (at small patches):
* {{BiomeLink|Birch Forest}}
* {{BiomeLink|Old Growth Birch Forest}}
* {{BiomeLink|Forest}}
* {{BiomeLink|Plains}}
* {{BiomeLink|Windswept Hills}}
* {{BiomeLink|Windswept Gravelly Hills}}
* {{BiomeLink|Windswept Forest}}
* Shores of {{BiomeLink|River}} and {{BiomeLink|Frozen River}}

=== Post-generation ===
Using a [[Bone Meal]] on a bush grows another bush on neighboring blocks.

== Usage ==
Like [[grass]], bushes adapt different shades of green, depending on the biome

=== Placement ===
Bushes can be placed on the following blocks:
* {{BlockLink|Grass Block}}
* {{BlockLink|Mycelium}}
* {{BlockLink|Podzol}}
* {{BlockLink|Dirt}}
* {{BlockLink|Coarse Dirt}}
* {{BlockLink|Rooted Dirt}}
* {{BlockLink|Farmland}}
* {{BlockLink|Mud}}
* {{BlockLink|Muddy Mangrove Roots}}
* {{BlockLink|Moss Block}}
* {{BlockLink|Pale Moss Block}}
In [[Bedrock Edition]], it is possible to snowlog bushes.

=== Composting ===
Using a bush on a [[composter]] has a 30% chance of raising a compost level by 1.
