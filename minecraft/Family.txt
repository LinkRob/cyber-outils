A '''family''' is a unique technical label for {{el|be}}, which specifies the family to which an [[mob]] or an [[entity]] belongs. The family of entity is defined in the entity components' '''[https://docs.microsoft.com/zh-cn/minecraft/creator/reference/content/entityreference/examples/entityproperties/minecraftproperty_type_family minecraft:type_family]'''. Families are used in commands such as [[Target_selectors#Selecting_targets_by_family|target selector]] and create [[Add-on]].
