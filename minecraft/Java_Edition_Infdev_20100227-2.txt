A version of '''[[Minecraft Infdev]]''' was released on '''February 27, 2010''' at 14:33 UTC.<ref group="note">The jar of this version is dated February 27, 2010 at 14:33 UTC.</ref>

== Changes ==
; [[Brick pyramid]]s
* Now generate with their tip at [[Altitude|height limit]], as intended.<ref>{{ytl|72Bc6zM8SO8|Putting Minecrafts /infdev/ To The Test}}</ref>
** During development of Infdev, and in the release of [[Java Edition Infdev 20100227-1|the previous version]], Notch reduced the build height from 256 to 128,<ref>[[wordofnotch:415398931|"Here, try some infinite maps"]] – [[The Word of Notch]], February 27, 2010</ref> without changing the generation of brick pyramids, so they still generated as if the height limit was 256, causing a huge part of the top to be cut off.<ref>{{ytl|bFigFw17hXg|Minecraft InfDev. Huge Temple Of Brick!}}</ref>
