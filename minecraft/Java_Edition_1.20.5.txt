'''1.20.5''', the first release of '''[[Armored Paws]]''', is a minor update of {{JE}} released on April 23, 2024, which adds the [[armadillo]], [[wolf armor]], and new variants of the [[wolf]].

== Additions ==
=== Mobs ===
;[[File:Armadillo.png|32px]] [[Armadillo]]
*Passive mob spawning in [[savanna]].
*Randomly drops its [[scute]].
*Can be bred using a [[spider eye]]. 

;[[Wolf]]
*New variants are added, including rusty, spotted, black, striped, snowy, ashen, woods, and chestnut.
**The wolf with classic look is now referred as pale wolf.<ref>{{article|new-minecraft-wolves|New Minecraft Wolves|March 5, 2024|Sophie Austin}}</ref>

=== Items ===
*[[Spawn Egg]]
**[[File:Armadillo Spawn Egg.png|32px]] [[Armadillo Spawn Egg]]
*[[File:Armadillo Scute.png|32px]] [[Armadillo Scute]]
*[[File:Wolf Armor (item).png|32px]] [[File:Wolf armor.png|32px]] [[Wolf Armor]]

=== Gameplay ===
;[[Advancement]]
* Added 4 new advancements:
** "Isn't It Scute?"
*** Get Armadillo Scutes from an Armadillo using a Brush
** "Shear Brilliance"
*** Remove Wolf Armor from a Wolf using Shears
** "Good as New"
*** Repair a damaged Wolf Armor using Armadillo Scutes
** "The Whole Pack"
*** Tame one of each Wolf variant
