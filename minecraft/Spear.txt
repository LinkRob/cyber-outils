A '''spear''' is a [[weapon]] that was added in the [[Mounts of Mayhem]]. It can be used to strike [[mob]]s and [[Entity|entities]] using its two attack types: charging and jabbing. It was revealed in [[Minecraft Wiki:Drafts/Minecraft Live 2025 2nd|Minecraft Live - September 2025]].<ref>[https://youtu.be/AEHc8jrcc6g Minecraft LIVE - September 2025]</ref>

=== Upgrading ===
Like other diamond tools, a diamond spear can be upgraded to a netherite spear via a [[netherite upgrade]], although increased durability and resistance to fire/lava in item form are the only benefits. The spear's performance as a melee weapon is unchanged by the upgrade.

=== Mob loot ===
[[Zombie]]s, [[husk]]s, [[Jockey|zombie horsemen]], and [[Camel Husk|camel husk jockey rider]]s naturally spawn wielding iron spears. [[Piglin]]s and [[zombified piglin]]s naturally spawn wielding golden spears.

===Generated loot===
Stone spears can be found in [[ocean ruins]], copper spears can be found in the chest of the [[Village|weaponsmith's house]]s, and iron spears can be found in both the weaponsmith's house chests and [[buried treasure]] chests.

Diamond spears can be exclusively found in [[bastion remnant]]s and [[End City|end cities]] chests, and can be obtained from [[vault]]s.

== Usage ==
=== Charging ===
To perform a charge attack, the [[player]] must hold down the {{control|use}} key. The spear will be lowered to the attack position, and when released, it will launch to a targeting entity. The amount of damage dealt to it depends on the velocity of the user. This means that the player can deal a very large amount of damage to an entity if they use the spear's charge attack while move very fast.

If the user holds the spear for too long, it begins to shake. If the player charges it even longer, it drops down lower, which no longer [[knockback|knocks back]] an entity, but deals full damage to it. It will then be drawn back to the holding position.

Some mobs, like [[zombie]]s when holding a spear, can use the spear's charge attack to damage [[player]]s and other entities.

=== Jabbing ===
To perform a jab attack, the player can use it while standing on the ground, dealing damage to an entity when hit, depending on the tier.

=== [[Piglin]]s ===
{{EntityLink|Piglin|Piglins}} are attracted to golden spears and run toward any golden spears on the ground. Then they inspect it for 6 to 8 seconds before putting it in their inventory.

=== Enchantments ===
Spears can be enchanted with [[Enchanting|Lunge]] (with the level ranging from 1 to 3), which allows the players to jab and cause more damage to an entity while swiftly moving past it. However, each time the player uses it, their [[Hunger|hunger bar]] drains out. And the higher the level of Lunge enchantment, the more hunger they lose.
