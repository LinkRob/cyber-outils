'''Alpha v1.1.2'''<ref name="pool">{{ytl|vurmjzf1XrY|Minecraft - Moon Pool|rocu57|September 18, 2010}}</ref><ref>{{ytl|zuckbin2NkQ|<nowiki>[Minecraft]</nowiki> Level Generator Fail|sunshine35anddaffy|September 18, 2010}}</ref><ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JWxvgXdq_ZM</ref> is a version of [[Alpha]] released on September 18, 2010 to fix sound and graphical bugs.  Shortly after this version's unannounced release,<ref group="note" name="jar" /> Notch made temporary, load-reducing changes to his web server.  Since one such change was disabling payments, he decided to declare Minecraft free-to-play for the weekend.<ref>[[wordofnotch:1145356099|Free Minecraft until this gets fixed]] – The Word of Notch, September 18, 2010</ref><ref>{{citation|url=https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/2010/09/18/mine-all-mine-minecraft-free-weekend/|title=Mine, All Mine: MineCraft Free Weekend|website=Rock Paper Shotgun|author=Quintin Smith|date=September 18, 2010}}</ref><ref>[[wordofnotch:1145456659|The state of the server]] – The Word of Notch, September 18, 2010</ref>
