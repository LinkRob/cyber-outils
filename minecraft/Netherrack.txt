'''Netherrack''' is a frail stone [[block]] found abundantly in [[the Nether]] and in blobs below [[ruined portals]]. Although it itself is not flammable, [[fire]] lit on top of it burns indefinitely.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Netherrack can be obtained only when mined with a [[pickaxe]]. It drops nothing if [[Breaking|mined]] without one.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Netherrack|Pickaxe|Wood}}
Netherrack can also be obtained by mining [[nylium]] with a pickaxe not enchanted with [[Silk Touch]].

=== Natural generation ===
Netherrack makes up most of [[the Nether]]'s terrain, and also generates as part of [[ruined portal]]s in the [[Overworld]].

Netherrack generates as part of the bridge and treasure room [[bastion remnant]]s.

=== Post-generation ===
{{IN|Bedrock}}, netherrack generates as part of [[nether portal]]s spawned floating. This is what makes netherrack renewable.

Crimson or warped [[nylium]] transforms into netherrack if a solid, opaque [[block]] is directly above it, or when it is covered by [[snow]].<ref>{{bug|MC-174639|||WAI}}</ref>

== Usage ==
Netherrack is primarily used as a cost-effective building material. Due to its abundance and softness, it is often used to construct temporary structures that serve as scaffolding, blocking, or for other purposes.

When netherrack's top is lit on [[fire]], the fire burns indefinitely. Unlike [[Fire|soul fire]], this fire can spread.

{{ctrl|Using}} [[bone meal]] on netherrack placed next to warped nylium or crimson nylium transforms the netherrack into more nylium.

Analysing the netherrack in a [[material reducer]], it turns out to be a volcanic silicate rock rich in mercury.{{only|be|ee}}
