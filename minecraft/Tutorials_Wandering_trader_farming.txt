[[Wandering Trader]] farming is the process of farming wandering traders for their trades, and/or disposing them and their [[Llama|Llamas]] to get [[Lead|Leads]] and [[Leather|Leathers]]. It's a farm to build to glitchlessly renewably farm items like [[Sand]], [[Sand|Red Sand]] and [[Small Dripleaf]]. However, in a normal [[Minecraft]] world, all those things are easy to get, so it isn't the most practical farm.  Additionally, it will require several [[Emerald|Emeralds]], so you might need to have a [[Villager]][[Tutorials/Villager trading hall|Trading Hall]] or [[Tutorials/Raid farming|Raid Farm]]. This farm is generally only useful for single [[Biome]] or other types of [[Survival]] Challenges, where some [[resource]] are limited (for example a [[Ice]] only world where you can't get [[Sand|Red Sand]]). 
== Concept ==
[[Wandering Trader|Wandering Traders]] spawn infrequently, so there wouldn't be an efficient way to get large amounts. Below is a general concept of what the farm can look like, keep in mind it hasn't been tested on a large scale:

There will need to be a platform over somewhere that can't spawn mobs, such as the [[Ocean]]. There will need to be a [[Bell]] in the middle, a hole around the [[Bell]] with [[Trapdoor|Trapdoors]] to trick [[Wandering Trader|Wandering Traders]] into walking over it and a [[Water]] stream in that hole which will transport the trader to the player. There will also need to be a system which will activate and deactivate a [[Dispenser]] which will dispense [[Lava]] to the trader after the player is done trading (using a [[Lever]] is best), and also [[Sign|Signs]] and a collection system so that the [[Lead|Leads]] and [[Leather|Leathers]] won't be burnt.
