{{BlockSprite|chest}} A '''[[chest]]''' is a block which stores items.

'''Chest''' may also refer to:

* {{BlockLink|Trapped Chest}}, a redstone component which sends redstone signals.
* {{BlockLink|Ender Chest}}, a chest whose contents are the same universally per player.
* {{EntityLink|Minecart with Chest}}, a chest in a minecart.
* {{EntityLink|Boat with Chest}}, a chest in a boat.
* {{BlockLink|Locked chest}}, an April Fools' joke block where items were (not actually) sold with real money.
* [[Buried Treasure]], an underground structure which consists of a chest buried underground.
* [[Bonus Chest]], a structure which consists of a chest surrounded with torches.
* [[Chest loot]], items found in naturally generated chests.
* [[Chestplate]], a type of armor that covers the upper body.
* {{BlockLink|Shulker Box}}, a portable chest.
* {{BlockLink|Barrel}}, a chest-like block.
* {{BlockLink|Copper Chest}}, a [[copper|copper variant]] of chests that are interacted by [[copper golem]]s
