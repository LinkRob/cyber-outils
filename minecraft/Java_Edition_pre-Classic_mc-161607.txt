A version of [[Java Edition Classic]] was released on May 16, 2009, at 16:07 UTC, referred to by Notch as "'''minecraft alpha'''".<ref name="-0">TigIRC logs: ''(12:07:06) notch: minecraft alpha is available. I need someone on windows, someone on mac and someone on linux!''</ref> This was the first version of ''Minecraft'' released for private testing.<ref name="-0"/><ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110301235605/http://notch.tumblr.com/post/108652448/early-private-singleplayer-alpha-coming-very-soon|title=Early private singleplayer alpha coming very soon|website=The Word of Notch|date=May 16, 2009}}</ref>

== Bugs ==

*Issues reported about this version were bad mouse movement<ref>TigIRC logs: "''(12:08:46) dock: the mouse movement is terrible''" [...] "''(12:09:16) dock: it barely moves, as though it is stuck, and then jerks''" [...] "''(12:11:55) dock: yeah the mouselook is pretty much unusable on mac''"</ref> and a lack of a Y-axis invert.<ref>TigIRC logs: "''(12:09:31) fartron: i don't have that problem but i need to invert the y axis''" [...] "''(12:09:39) fartron: or i will constantly walk into things''"</ref>

== Trivia ==
* The URL for ''Minecraft'' was given to users [[dock]] and fartron for testing.<ref>TigIRC logs: "''(12:08:29) notch: awesome. urls pm'd.''"</ref>
