'''Trains Add-on''' is a [[Marketplace]] add-on that features craftable trains and railroad signals. It is released by Lifeboat Network on November 13, 2024 as version 1.0.

The add-on can be purchased for {{Minecoin|990}} minecoins. In version 2.5, the add-on now supports with [[Vibrant Visuals]].

== Description ==
{{Quote|All aboard the Trains Add-On; build your dream railroad today!
: - 14 train engines including steam, diesel, and subway locomotives
: - 18 railroad cars for transporting players, mobs, and more
: - Custom stations and signals for autonomous trains
: - 500+ unique locomotive and wagon color variants
: - Easy-to-use train coupling
Now with 100% more [[Vibrant Visuals]]. Comparisons below!|Minecraft Marketplace description}}

=== Blocks ===
* Redstone Station Block
** Can be crafted with 4 [[Lightning Rod|lightning rods]], 3 iron bars, a piston, and a redstone dust.
** Stops a self-driving train when activated with redstone.
*** Departs when deactivated.
** Can be customized via its settings.
** Added in version 2.0.
* Timed Station Block
** Can be crafted with a [[clock]], a [[Block of Copper|block of copper]], and a redstone station block.
** Stops a self-driving train for the time set by a player.
** Can be customized via its settings.
** Added in version 2.0.
* Plus Switch Block
** Can be crafted with 3 iron bars, a [[piston]], and a redstone dust.
** [[Rail]] rotates in north-south direction when activated with redstone.
*** Rotates in east-west direction when deactivated.
** Added in version 2.5.
* Train Horn Block
** Can be crafted with 4 [[lapis lazuli]]s 3 iron bars, a [[note block]], and a redstone dust.
** Train blows the horn automatically when run over it.
** Added in version 2.5.
* Train Bell Block
** Can be crafted with 3 iron bars, a [[note block]], and a [[diamond]].
** Train blows the bell automatically when run over it.
** Added in version 2.5.
* Train Light Block
** Can be crafted with 4 [[gold ingot]]s, 3 iron bars, a [[redstone lamp]], and a redstone dust.
** Train toggles their light on or off automatically when run over it.
** Added in version 2.5.
* Train Buffer Blocks
** Comes in iron, wood, and copper variations.
** Iron variants can be dyed to 16 different colors similar to [[sheep]].
** Copper variants do not oxidize unlike most copper blocks.
** Wooden variants are not flammable and doesn't catch fire from [[lava]] unlike normal wooden blocks.
** Added in version 2.5.
