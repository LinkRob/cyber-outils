An '''Other Portal''' is a joke [[block]].

== Obtaining ==
The shape of the Other Portal is similar to a [[Nether portal|Nether Portal]], except that it uses [[Glowstone]] instead.

It also uses a [[Water Bucket]] instead of a [[Flint and Steel]] to light the portal.

The Other Portal must be enabled by passing a vote for it, or by using the [[Java Edition 23w13a or b|/vote command]] to approve it.

== Usage ==
The Other Portal was added as a reference to the [[ftb:The_Aether|Aether]], a popular mod for Minecraft which added a fourth [[dimension]] to the game.

However, when entering the Other Portal, the player is teleported 500 blocks upwards, and the message "'''''Error 404 Terrain Not Found!'''''" is displayed.

If the portal is created above Y=200, it teleports players to Y=650 instead, so they aren't teleported to the [[The Moon|Moon]] instead.
