'''{{EntityLink|Pig}}''', a passive mob that can be found within the [[Overworld]].

'''Pig''' may also refer to:
== ''Minecraft'' ==
* {{EntityLink|Piglin}}, a neutral mob that can be found within the [[Nether]].
** {{EntityLink|Pigman}}, a mob concept which was eventually worked into the Piglin.
* {{EntityLink|Piglin Brute}}, a fully hostile Piglin variant that attacks the [[Player]] on sight that can be found within a [[Bastion Remnant]].
* {{EntityLink|Hoglin}}, a warthog-like hostile mob found within a [[Crimson Forest]].
* {{EntityLink|Zoglin}}, an undead Hoglin variant that spawns only if a Hoglin enters the Overworld or the [[End]] for longer than fifteen seconds.
* {{EntityLink|Zombified Piglin}}, an undead Piglin variant that can be found within the [[Nether Wastes]], or if a Piglin enters the Overworld or the End for longer than fifteen seconds.
** {{EntityLink|Zombie Pigman}}, The name of the Zombified Piglin before the [[Nether Update]].
* {{ItemLink|id=music-disc-pigstep|Pigstep}}, a soundtrack by Lena Raine found within a Bastion Remnant.

===== Horde of the Bastion =====
{{Main|Horde of the Bastion}}
A horde of piglins that likes fortifying their outposts with many walls, thus having the most fortified outposts compared to the other two hordes.

* [[Minecraft Legends:Portal Guard|Portal Guard]],
*[[Minecraft Legends:Brute|Brute]],

===== Horde of the Spore =====
{{Main|Horde of the Spore}}
A horde of piglins that is less fortified than the Horde of the Bastion at their outposts as they don't use walls but they like building their outposts on the high ground.

* [[Minecraft Legends:Lava Launcher|Lava Launcher]],

===== Horde of the Hunt =====
{{Main|Horde of the Hunt}}
A horde of piglins that doesn't use any fortification in their outposts at all, but their outposts are larger than the others nonetheless. This horde has and uses a lot of bones for their outposts.

* [[Minecraft Legends:Pigmadillo|Pigmadillo]],
