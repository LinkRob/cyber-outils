A version of '''Minecraft Indev''' was released on '''February 14, 2010''',<ref>[[wordofnotch:389572897|"Visible player hand and new level themes"]] &ndash; The Word of Notch, February 14, 2010</ref> at 21:43 UTC.

== Additions ==
=== World generation ===
; [[Seed (level generation)|Seeds]]
* Added 2 new [[map theme]]s.
** Paradise.
** Woods.</onlyinclude>

==Changes==
=== General ===
; Blocks
* Any dirt at or below sea level, when exposed to enough light, is turned into grass, upon world generation.

; [[HUD]]
* The player's hand now displays again.
** However, the idle animation is shown when the player opens the inventory.

==Bugs==
* When there is no good place to spawn an [[Indev house]], it simply won't spawn.
** This is very common in Small, Deep maps and Small, Long maps.
** This bug lasts until the end of Indev.
*There is a chance that water particles will spawn outside of the water. This only happens in 3rd person.
