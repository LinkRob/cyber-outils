'''''Minecraft: Xbox 360 Edition''''' is the {{w|Xbox 360}} version of ''[[Minecraft]]'', developed by [[4J Studios]] in conjunction with [[Mojang Studios]] and {{w|Xbox Game Studios}}.<ref>[https://marketplace.xbox.com/Product/66acd000-77fe-1000-9115-d802584111f7 "Minecraft: Xbox 360 Edition"]</ref> It was announced at the 2011 E3 conference by [[Markus Persson|Notch]] and Microsoft, and was released alongside a free trial version on May 9, 2012 on Xbox Live Arcade. It is priced at US$19.99.

As of [[Xbox 360 Edition TU75|TU75]], the ''Xbox 360 Edition'' has not received new feature updates, such as [[Village & Pillage]], [[Buzzy Bees]], the [[Nether Update]], [[Caves & Cliffs]], and so on.

Although the Xbox 360 console has been discontinued with all the other [[Legacy Console Edition]]s, the ''Xbox 360 Edition'' continued to receive feature updates until the release of the [[Update Aquatic]].<ref>{{Mcnet|update-aquatic-coming-legacy-console-editions|Update Aquatic Coming To Legacy Console Editions|May 3, 2018}}</ref>

==Retail disc==
On June 4, 2013, Microsoft released the first retail disc of ''Xbox 360 Edition'' with a free gold membership. It cost US$19.99, the same price as the Xbox Arcade version. The game otherwise is identical to the downloadable version. Features such as buying or downloading content on Xbox Live are the same for both versions and they both updated at the same time. It was released in regions or countries supported by Xbox Live. The disc version is updated to TU9.

==Release==
The game was showcased as a playable demo at [[MINECON 2011]] and was deemed a 'work in progress'.<ref>{{tweet|newbreedofnerd|128581309370077185|You can expect some more details at Minecon and post Minecon with regards to features and differences between the PC and Xbox.|October 24, 2011}}</ref> 4J Studios confirmed that it had all the features of {{el|je}} [[Beta 1.6.6]] when released, and that it would later be updated. IGN also filmed a video at MINECON showing that the graphics look very similar to {{el|je}}.<ref>{{link|url=http://ign.com/videos/2011/11/18/minecraft-xbla-commentary-video|date=November 18, 2011|title=XBLA Commentary Video|website=IGN}}</ref>

Notch later announced the team developing the game would be [[4J Studios]].<ref>{{tweet|notch|81124564104515584|They ported Oblivion to PS3 and Perfect Dark to Xbox360! It's 4J Studios|June 15, 2011}}</ref><ref>{{tweet|notch|81124399985598464|Oh neat, I can announce the team implementing Minecraft for Xbox360! Part of their team made State of Emergency!|June 15, 2011}}</ref> [[Daniel Kaplan]] tweeted about receiving an early copy of ''Xbox 360 Edition'' on September 7, 2011.<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160604164040/https://twitter.com/Kappische/status/111533621449461761|title=Guess what I got from @4JStudios? That's right! First super early version of Minecraft Xbox :)}} – [https://twitter.com/Kappische @Kappische], September 7, 2011</ref>

In October 2011, Microsoft said the release was planned for Spring 2012.<ref>[http://gamespot.com/news/minecraft-for-xbox-360-pushed-to-spring-2012-6342148 "Minecraft for Xbox 360 pushed to spring 2012"] – GameSpot, October 25, 2011</ref> On March 22, the official release date was posted by {{w|Larry Hryb}}, the Director of Programming for Xbox Live, as May 9,<ref name="majornelson">[http://majornelson.com/2012/03/22/announcing-arcade-next "Announcing ‘Arcade Next'"] – Major Nelson, March 22, 2012</ref> and a few days before the game release, HatFilms released {{ytl|1LqZ4_g18xk|the official trailer}} for ''Xbox 360 Edition.''

The game was officially released on May 9, 2012, and set a new sales records on the Xbox Live Arcade, selling more copies in the first 24 hours of launch than previous titles.<ref>[https://polygon.com/gaming/2012/5/10/3012376/minecraft-xbox-360-edition-is-the-fastest-selling-game-on-xbox-live "Minecraft: Xbox 360 Edition is the fastest selling game on Xbox Live Arcade"] – Polygon, May 10, 2012</ref>

The retail disc version was released on June 4, 2013, being exactly the same as the arcade version, with the exception of internet connection not being a requirement to play.

=== Skin Packs===
{{Main|Skin pack}}

4J Studios has produced multiple Skin Packs as downloadable content. Each Skin Pack includes about 45 skins to choose from. The first Skin Pack, [[Skin pack#Main 3|Skin Pack 1]], was released July 16, 2012 at a cost of US$1.99. The Skin Pack contains characters from Xbox 360 games, such as the {{w|Master Chief (Halo)|Master Chief}} from {{w|Halo (franchise)|''Halo''}}. On ''Xbox 360 Edition'''s birthday, the player can download special skin packs for free. There are many more skin packs, including a Christmas Skin Pack, a Marvel Skin Pack, a Spider-Man Skin Pack and multiple Animal Skin Packs. The Skin Packs also include various original designs created by 4J Studios. In total, there are more than 10 skin packs.

==Marvel Skin Packs Bundle==
[[File:MarvelSkinPacksPromotionalImage.png|200px|thumb|right|Promotional image for the Marvel Skin Packs Bundle]]

The '''Minecraft Marvel Skin Packs Bundle''' was released on November 30, 2015, and contains all of the Marvel Skin Packs:
*[[Skin pack#Other|Marvel Avengers Skin Pack]]
*[[Skin pack#Other|Marvel Guardians of the Galaxy Skin Pack]]
*[[Skin pack#Other|Marvel Spider-Man Skin Pack]]

The bundle was meant to be a discount for the Marvel Skin Packs. On December 22, 2015, the bundle was removed from the Xbox Games Store, thus being no longer available for the Xbox One and the Xbox 360. This bundle costed US$6.99 and was available in the Xbox One and the Xbox 360.<ref name="store2">[https://microsoft.com/p/minecraft-marvel-skin-packs-bundle/bxp6gc8rj8p1 "Minecraft Marvel Skin Packs Bundle"]</ref><ref name="tweet">{{tweet|4j|678889627285979137|Today is the last day the #MinecraftXbox1 & #MinecraftXbox360 Marvel Skin Packs will be available in the Xbox Store.|December 21, 2015}}</ref> The Marvel Skin Packs from the bundle still transfer to Bedrock Edition, but they are not usable in online or LAN multiplayer.

==Trivia== 
*''Xbox 360 Edition'' was added to the Xbox Arcade download section in November 2011, although it was only possible to download the {{ytl|JZ37aH7s16U|"It's here!" trailer}}. Nonetheless, the entry held very high ratings.
*There are also ''Minecraft''-themed Xbox Live avatar items available. Among these are ''Minecraft'' shirts, pets, items, props, head wear, dashboard themes, and gamer pics, all of which range from US$1/£0.64 to US$2.99/£1.92 in price.
* The retail disk version of this edition includes [[Xbox 360 Edition TU9|Title Update 9]] and could be updated past [[Xbox 360 Edition TU9|TU9]] by connecting to Xbox Live.
*There are a few pre-release builds of Minecraft: Xbox 360 Edition.
