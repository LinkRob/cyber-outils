'''Resin Clumps''' are natural decorative [[block]]s generated from a [[Creaking Heart]], when a [[creaking]] is punched by a [[player]]. They are added in [[The Garden Awakens]] game drop.

== Obtaining ==
Resin clumps are obtained via punching a naturally spawned creaking during the night. When this happens, its creaking heart will generate resin clumps onto neighbouring [[Log|pale oak log]]s. Breaking a creaking heart also drops resin clumps.

Resin clumps are occasionally found in [[woodland mansion]]s in the form of loot.

== Usage ==
Resin clumps can be used for decoration, like placing on [[stone]] to mimic custom [[ores]], or underneath a [[bee nest]] to demonstrate [[honey]] drips.

== Appearance ==
Resin Blocks appear bright, contrasting orange, similar to [[lava]], but it is not animated. In fact, it appears slightly conglomerated with resin clumps tightly packed. [[Player]]s may confuse resin blocks with baked beans, due to their unique cobbled texture.
