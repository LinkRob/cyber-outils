An '''underwater magma''' is a [[feature]] found underwater in the Overworld.

== Generation ==
An underwater magma is a feature made entirely of [[magma block]]s. It is up to 3*3*3 in size. It can be found at the underwater floor in any [[Overworld]] biomes.
