'''mod_ore''' is a block of unknown use. It uses the same textures of the [[Block of Gold|block of gold]].

== Obtaining ==
The '''mod_ore''' block can only be obtained via [[commands]].

=== Breaking ===
{{Missing information|the block's breaking time.}}
When mined, the '''mod_ore''' block doesn't drop anything regardless of the tool that is used.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GXwLg-vHbdU&ab</ref>

== Usage ==
Its intended usage is unknown, however the name suggests it may be a block of a mod that isn't activated, and got replaced with this, similar to the [[Unknown]] Block
