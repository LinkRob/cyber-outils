'''Duncan Geere''' is a writer, editor and data journalist who wrote parts of the minecraft.net "Block of the Week" series, from the 21st episode to the last. This was continued in 2020 with [[scaffolding]], [[honey]], [[target]], and [[basalt]].<ref>{{Mcnet|block-week-note-block|Block of the Week: Note Block|June 1st, 2018|Duncan Geere}}</ref><ref>https://tinyletter.com/duncangeere/letters/s03e19-blocks s03e19: Blocks, by Duncan Geere</ref><ref>https://www.duncangeere.com/portfolio/blockoftheweek/ Block of the Week: A series for the official Minecraft website, covering every block in the game, by Duncan Geere</ref> He also currently writes the "Taking Inventory" and "Around The Block" series in minecraft.net. In 2010, Duncan wrote an article for Wired UK about his first experience playing ''Minecraft''.<ref>https://www.wired.com/2010/09/minecraft/</ref>
