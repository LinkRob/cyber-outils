'''{{BlockLink|Glass}}''' is a fully transparent solid block.

'''Glass''' may also refer to:

*{{BlockLink|Glass Pane}}, a thinner version of glass.
*{{ItemLink|Glass Bottle}}, a bottle version of glass, used for brewing [[Potion]]s
*{{BlockLink|Tinted Glass}}, a unique type of glass that blocks out [[light]].
*{{BlockLink|Hardened Glass}}, a harder version of glass (exclusive on Bedrock Edition and Education Edition).
*{{BlockLink|Hardened Glass Pane}}, a thinner version of hardened glass (exclusive on Bedrock Edition and Education Edition).
*{{ItemLink|Spyglass}}, an item that let you zoom.
*[[Java Edition 2.0#Blocks|Tinted Glass]], a type of glass block, but on Java Edition 2.0 (April Fools' joke). This joke feature includes coloured variants of tinted glass.
