'''Splash''' texts are the yellow lines of text on the [[Menu screen|title screen]]. They are randomly chosen from the list below and often feature references to popular culture (e.g. websites, quotes, and video games). The splash pulsates on the title screen twice per second.

Splash texts are not translated and appear the same regardless of the language setting chosen. In {{el|je}}, the splashes are defined in a plain-text file within the {{code|[[minecraft.jar]]}} file (specifically, in {{code|[[client.jar|''version''.jar]]/assets/minecraft/texts/splashes.txt}}). {{IN|bedrock}}, the splashes are defined in a {{code|.json}} file.
Splashes can be modified by editing the {{code|splashes.txt}} file via a [[resource pack]].

| Homeomorphic to a 3-sphere!
| A "{{w|Homeomorphism}}" is a mathematical property indicating that there is a continual topological mapping between two objects; in layman's terms, there exists a way to deform one into the other without cutting or gluing. A "{{w|N-sphere|3-sphere}}" is a sphere in 3 dimensions, or the set of points equidistant from a given center in 4-space, a higher-dimensional analog of the "2-sphere" (what is thought of as an ordinary {{w|Sphere}}.) Possibly a reference to the {{w|Poincaré conjecture}}.
Note this splash text could be wrong assuming a Minecraft world is R^3 because of the "Euclidian!" splash (splash 65), R^3 is not a compact subset but S^3 is compact therefore they cannot be homeomorphic. If the Minecraft world was a compact subset of R^3 then it would be a closed and bounded by the [[wikipedia:Heine–Borel_theorem|Heine-Borel theorem]] but this still doesn't work because S^3 has no boundary while the Minecraft world has a boundary. The {{w|Poincaré conjecture}} doesn't apply here since it requires the manifold to be closed (i,e compact and boundaryless). Attempting to continuously deform a sphere onto a euclidean space of the same dimensions will result in something called the [[wikipedia:Borsuk–Ulam_theorem|Borsuk-Ulam theorem]] which says two antipodal points will always meet making it not bijective.

== Removed splashes ==

When a splash is removed, the line it occupied in splashes.txt / splashes.json is deleted, meaning the line number of all subsequent splashes lowers by one.

== Trivia ==
* Both "Flint and Steel" and "Chicken Jockey" are movie quotes derived from [[A Minecraft Movie]] and names of an item and mob in the game.
* When [[Notch]] left Mojang, some splash texts were changed. However, the old splash texts can be seen in older versions before [[Java Edition 1.10]].
* In [[Minecraft Education]], "A creeper ate my homework" is a Minecraft alternate version of "A dog ate my homework".
* Two splash texts refer to the [[Far Lands]].
** The splash "Check out the far lands!" was ironically added after the Far Lands were fixed.
** However, the splash "One does not simply walk to the Far Lands" was added prior to when [[Far Lands|KurtJMac]] reached the Far Lands on 4th October, 2025.
