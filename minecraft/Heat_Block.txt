The '''Heat Block''' is a [[block]] that can melt [[snow]] and [[ice]] like a [[torch]], but does not give off [[light]].

== Usage ==

Heat blocks melt snow layers and ice within 2 blocks ([[taxicab distance]]). It is unique in that it is the only block that melts objects without giving off light.

== Trivia ==
* Iron corrodes in salt water way faster than it does in water, because [https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/General_Chemistry/Map%3A_Chemistry_-_The_Central_Science_(Brown_et_al.)/20%3A_Electrochemistry/20.8%3A_Corrosion electrochemistry is involved].
* The ingredients of the heat block [https://www.wired.com/2014/12/whats-inside-hot-hands/ corresponds to the contents of hand warmers]. A hand warmer generates heat due to the same electrochemistry that rusts iron in seawater, but with powdered iron the effect becomes more appreciable.
