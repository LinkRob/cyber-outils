'''''Minecraft: Tricky Trials Soundtrack''''' is the fourth soundtrack for ''[[Minecraft]]'' featuring [[Lena Raine]], as well as the second featuring [[Aaron Cherof]] and [[Kumi Tanioka]]. It was released on Spotify on April 26, 2024.

The album includes 12 songs for a total length of nearly an hour. It serves as the soundtrack for the Tricky Trials update and includes three new music disc tracks.<ref>https://youtu.be/ONs7kTlqGw4</ref>
