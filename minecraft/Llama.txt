A '''llama''' is a tamable neutral [[mob]] used to transport large shipments of items using caravans.

A '''trader llama''' is a special variant that follows [[wandering trader]]s.

== Spawning ==
A llama spawns at a light level 7 or above on [[grass block]]s in [[Savanna#Savanna Plateau|savanna plateau]], [[savanna]]{{only|be|short=1}}<ref>{{bug|MC-159092||Llamas do not spawn in regular savanna biome|WAI}}</ref> or [[Savanna#Windswept Savanna|windswept savanna]]{{only|be|short=1}} [[biome]]s in herds of 4 llamas and in [[windswept hills]], [[Windswept Hills#Windswept Forest|windswept forest]], and [[Windswept Hills#Windswept Gravelly Hills|windswept gravelly hills]] biomes in herds of 4 to 6 llamas, coming in four coat colors: brown, cream, white or gray. A [[wandering trader]]s spawns with two [[lead|leashed]] trader llamas. {{IN|bedrock}}, trader llamas always spawn with a wandering trader.

10% of llamas spawn as baby llamas.

Trader llamas often despawn one [[tick]] before their trader does, because a trader llama has its {{code|DespawnDelay}} set to 47999 ticks. This value is decremented each tick that the llama is untamed, unleashed, and not being ridden by a player, and is reset to one less than the trader's own {{code|DespawnDelay}} if leashed to a trader. The llama despawns if the value reaches zero.

== Drops ==
An adult llama drops when killed:
* 0 to 2 {{ItemLink|leather}}s or up to 0-5 with [[Looting]] 3.
* Any equipped {{blockLink|carpets|link=Carpet}} and {{BlockLink|chest}}.
*All items in their [[inventory]].
*{{xp|1|3}} [[experience]] orbs if killed by a tamed [[wolf]] or the [[player]]. Upon successful [[breeding]], {{xp|1|7}} is dropped.

Like other baby animals, killing a baby llama yields no [[item]] or [[experience]].

== Caravans ==
[[File:Llama GUI Strength 4.png|thumb|right|The GUI of a llama with strength 4 and with a chest.]]
Llamas do not accept [[saddle]]s, so the player cannot control its movement, even when tamed. [[Lead]]s are the best way to move llamas. Leashing a llama actually signals all other llamas not already in a caravan nearby (up to nine) to follow each other, forming a caravan of up to ten llamas. Leashing a second llama forms a caravan of ten more llamas and there is no limit on the number of llamas following a player.

A llama can be equipped with a [[chest]] by right clicking a chest on it once tamed, giving it 3, 6, 9, 12 or 15 slots of inventory space, scaling directly with its {{cd|Strength}} NBT (see {{slink||Data values}} below). Once equipped, the chest cannot be retrieved without killing the llama and can be accessed by shift + right clicking the llama. A llama's potential carry capacity is a number of item slots equal to 3*{{cd|Strength}}, with a maximum of 15 spaces.

Llamas can be equipped with [[carpet]]s in their carpet slot ({{SlotSprite|Carpet}}). Each carpet color shows as a different patterned rug on the llama's back. This can be useful for color-coding their storage, similar to dyed [[shulker box]]es.

For the purposes of the {{cmd|item}} command, a llama carries its carpet in the <code>horse.armor</code> slot. Trader llamas that do not have a carpet wear a unique blue rug design. It can be replaced with a carpet but not removed. When given carpets, both types of llama look the same. A llama's [[carpet]] decoration remains visible when the llama is under the effect of [[Invisibility]].

== Behavior ==
The llama floats when in water deeper than two blocks.

Llamas are neutral, but if a player or mob attacks one, it spits at the attacker once to deal {{hp|1}} damage. Sometimes their spit can miss their target and hit another llama, starting a fight within a group of llamas. Additionally, a [[wandering trader]]'s llamas spit at mobs or players who attack the wandering trader.

Llamas are hostile toward wolves and spit without provocation, but they don't attack tamed wolves unless provoked. Wolves are fearful of llamas of [[#Data values|strength]] 4 or 5 and always run away.<ref>{{Bug|MC-107643}}</ref> Wolves flee from weaker llamas less often.

Trader llamas are hostile toward [[illager]]s{{only|java}} and all zombie variants, defending both themselves and their [[wandering trader]].

Despite the spitting being a projectile, dying from llama spit still shows the death message "<player> was slain by llama/trader llama."

The spit entity must belong to an entity to do damage, meaning that a spit entity summoned by commands does no damage unless its <code>Owner</code> NBT attribute matches the UUID of an entity in loaded [[chunk]]s. 

The llama spit can be deflected if hit by the player.{{only|bedrock}}

Llama spit cannot pass through any blocks including [[:Category:Non-solid blocks|non-solid blocks]] (e.g. signs, open fence gates, and sugar cane) and water. The spitting animation will still show the trajectory, but the spit entity that does damage will terminate on the non-solid block.

Llama spit can be [[blocking|blocked]] by a [[shield]].

=== Breeding ===
Adult tamed llamas can be bred by being fed a [[hay bale]]. The baby llama takes on the coat color of one parent at random. Its strength is chosen as a random integer between 1 and the strength of the stronger parent, inclusive. 3% of the time the resulting strength is increased by 1, but it is capped at 5.

When two trader llamas are bred, the offspring wears a rug.

=== Taming ===
Llamas can be tamed by repetitively riding them until hearts are displayed, done by pressing {{control|use}} on the llama while holding nothing.

Taming success depends on the llama's {{cd|Temper}} NBT. {{cd|Temper}} is a positive trait, with higher values increasing the chance of successful taming. Llamas begin with a <code>Temper</code> value of 0 and a maximum of 100. When a player rides an untamed llama, a random number from 0 to 99 is chosen. The llama gets tamed successfully if this number is less than the <code>Temper</code> value, otherwise, the <code>Temper</code> is increased by 5 and the player is bucked off. <code>Temper</code> can also be increased by feeding the llama.

Tamed llamas can still retaliate at players should the player hit them. If a player kills a [[wandering trader]] and stays away from the trader llamas for a while, the llamas are tame when the player rides it and the player can place carpets on the llama.

Naturally spawned trader llamas are untamed and cannot be ridden while being led by their [[wandering trader]]. If unleashed, they become tamable{{only|java}} or tamed.{{only|bedrock}}

A death message is displayed to a tamed llama's owner if it is killed. Tamed trader llamas will not despawn.

Tamed llamas do not spit at mobs that attack its owner, although it spits at any mob that attacks the llama.

Feeding a llama food can alter its behavior, restoring lost health or making a baby grow faster (babies ordinarily take around 20 minutes to mature to adults). The table below lists the effects of the 2 food items llamas accept.

To feed a llama, hold a valid food item and press {{control|use|use}} while facing the llama. Llamas can be fed only when feeding would have an effect, similar to other animals. If the food is invalid, the player mounts the llama instead.

== Trivia ==
*The achievement title refers to the 1980 comedy movie ''{{Wikipedia|Caddyshack}}'', where Bill Murray tells a story of how he once caddied for the Dalai Lama. In return, on his deathbed he expects to receive total consciousness, thus uttering the line "So I got that going for me".
*Some carpets on a llama's back represent certain mobs; a green carpet uses the face of the green [[creeper]], while a purple carpet gains eyes like those of an [[enderman]]. A llama wearing a gray carpet has a mask, similar to that of a superhero or a bandit.
*A llama can kill another llama. When the player hits a llama and hides behind another, the spit will hurt the llama the player is hiding behind, leading to a battle. Both llamas will not stop spitting at one another until one dies.
*The llama was [[Agnes Larsson]]'s first mob she designed for Minecraft.
*Its [[wikipedia:Lego_BrickHeadz|LEGO BrickHeadz]] figure is notable for being the 200th produced,<ref>https://twitter.com/Minecraft/status/1646916167649353746?s=20</ref> and is included in several retail sets as the "Trader Lama".
