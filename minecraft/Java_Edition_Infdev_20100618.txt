A version of '''[[Minecraft Infdev]]''' was released on '''June 18, 2010''', marking the first Seecret Friday update.<ref>[[wordofnotch:712687607|Seecret Friday update! OooooOOOoo!]] – The Word of Notch, June 18, 2010</ref> This is the only version of Infdev available in the [[Minecraft Launcher|launcher]].

== Additions ==
===Blocks===
; [[File:Rail JE1.png|40px]] [[Rail]]s

=== Non-mob entities ===
; [[File:Minecart JE1.png|40px]] [[File:Minecart (item) JE1.png|32px]] [[Minecart]]s
* At the time, these were not rideable and were instead used as portable storage, similar to the [[minecart with chest]] from modern ''Minecraft'' versions.
* Right-clicking minecarts opens them like a chest (with the container called "Minecart"). Filling them up makes the "dirt" layer inside of them rise.
; {{animate|Sand JE2.png;Gravel JE2.png|32px}} [[Falling Block]]s
* Called "FallingBlock".
* Appears when sand or gravel lose their base of support.

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[File:Oak Door JE3 BE1.png|32px]] [[Door]]
* Texture changed from [[File:Oak Door JE2.png|32px]]

;[[Gravel]] and [[sand]] 
* Now fall realistically.

=== World generation ===
; [[Tree]]s
* Bug where [[log]]s would not generate inside of the tops of trees fixed.

=== Gameplay ===
; [[Void]]
* Falling into the void now kills the [[player]].

; [[Sky]]
* Appears as a slightly darker variant of light blue.
