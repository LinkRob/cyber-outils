'''Matthew Dryden''' is a QA Specialist for Java Edition at [[Mojang Studios|Minecraft]]. He was previously a Content Coordinator, and then Interim Content Lead.

== About ==

Moesh's job consisted of assisting with content management for the Minecraft franchise and adding new [[Realms]] maps. Moesh has a background of creating custom levels for games such as ''Quake II'' and ''Star Wars Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II'', and as such is also responsible behind the level design of structures like the [[bastion remnant]] and the [[ancient city]].<ref>{{tweet|HeartlessMoesh|1509608453274275841|My work is shown in this week's beta and snapshot for Minecraft! The work I've done was to tune the Ancient City structures so that the Warden had a better time moving around. I also added some evil bits for those who like to build pillars to avoid the Warden...|April 1, 2022}}</ref><ref>{{ytl|2PqhmjPg6FE|Podcast: Structure Design with Moesh|Minecraft Ideas Academy|February 28, 2022}}</ref>
