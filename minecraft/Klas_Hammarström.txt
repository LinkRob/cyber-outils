'''Klas Hammarström''' is a Production Manager at [[Mojang Studios]].

== About ==

As a production manager, Klas works with the overall portfolio plan and roadmaps for the development teams at Mojang Studios. Acts as a liaison partner between the leadership team and the board for development teams in mojang, structure and develop data-driven processes and recommendations across the studio. 

Hammarström joined Mojang in April 2019.<ref>https://se.linkedin.com/in/klibb</ref>
