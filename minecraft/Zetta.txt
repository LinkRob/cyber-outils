'''Zetta''' is a self-proclaimed "potioner" within ''[[Minecraft: The Dragon]]''. Born and raised in the [[desert]] village of Sienna Dunes, her dreams were constantly dashed by the conservative mindset of the community, including her father.

== Appearance ==
Zetta is a teenage girl, described as having dark skin, curly black hair, a blue tunic, and a leather pack.
