'''Air''' is a joke item from [[Java Edition 23w13a_or_b]].

== Obtaining ==
'''Air''' can only be obtained after voting for the rule <code>air_blocks</code>.

=== Crafting===
{{Crafting
|A1= Glass Bottle 
|B1= Glass Bottle
|A2= Glass Bottle
|B2= Glass Bottle
|Output= Air (April Fools joke)
|ignoreusage=1
}}

The 2×2 inventory [[crafting grid]] is sufficient to craft air.

==Usage==
Air can be eaten by pressing and holding {{control|use}} while it is selected in the hotbar to keep a player alive while on [[The Moon|the moon]].
