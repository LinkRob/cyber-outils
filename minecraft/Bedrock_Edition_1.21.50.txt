'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.50''', named [[The Garden Awakens]], is a game drop to 1.21, released on December 3, 2024.

== Additions ==
=== [[Block]]s ===
* [[Creaking Heart]]
** Can be found activated in the [[Pale Garden]] biome.
** Can be mined and relocated with a [[Silk Touch]] tool.
** Can be crafted using two Pale Oak logs and a block of Resin.
** It only activates when placed between two Pale Oak logs in the same orientation and can't be deactivated unless the block is broken.
** Spawns [[creaking]]s at night or during [[thunderstorm]]s when activated and links itself to them.
* Pale Oak Wood set
** Pale Oak Log and its stripped variant
** Pale Oak Wood and its stripped variant
** Pale Oak Planks
** Pale Oak Stairs
** Pale Oak Slab
** Pale Oak Sign and its hanging variant
** Pale Oak Button
** Pale Oak Pressure Plate
** Pale Oak Door
** Pale Oak Trapdoor
** Pale Oak Fence
** Pale Oak Fence Gate
* New foliage and vegetation blocks'''
** Pale Hanging Moss
** Pale Moss Block
** Pale Moss Carpet
** Pale Oak Leaves
** Pale Oak Sapling
* [[Eyeblossom]]
** Both open and closed variants
* Resin block set
** [[Resin Clump]]
*** Can be smelted into bricks
** [[Block of Resin]]
** [[Resin Bricks]]
** [[Chiseled Resin Bricks]]
** Resin Brick Slab
** Resin Brick Stairs
** Resin Brick Wall

=== [[Item]]s ===
* [[Resin Brick]]
** Can also be used to trim armor.
* [[Creaking Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to spawn [[creaking]]s.
*** The creaking spawns this way doesn't connect to the creaking heart, persists through day and night, and kills in one hit.

=== [[Mob]]s ===
* [[Creaking]]
** A new hostile mob spawned by active creaking hearts during the night or thunderstorms.
*** Can also be spawned using a spawn egg, by the {{cmd|summon}} command or from a [[Monster Spawner|monster spawner]], which is not connected to a creaking heart, persist through day and night, and deals {{hp|1}} damage, killing it instantly or in a single hit.
** It moves and attacks when a [[player]] is looking away from it.
** Unlike most mobs, it can only be killed by destroying its respective creaking heart.
*** Although, it can also be killed instantly when spawned using a spawn egg or by {{cmd|summon}} command.
** It emits particles that lead to its creaking heart when hit or damaged.

=== Non-mob [[Entity|entities]] ===
* Pale oak boat and with chest variant.

=== [[Biome]]s ===
* [[Pale Garden]]
** A new biome that generates with pale oak trees, pale moss blocks, pale moss carpets, pale hanging moss, and eyeblossoms.
** It is quiet in daytime, but very eerie at nighttime.
** It is also where creakings spawn during nighttime or during thunderstorms.
