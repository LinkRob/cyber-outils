'''Update suppression''' is the practice of forcing the game to skip block updates. <!--This works by causing enough block updates in a single game tick to cause a stack overflow in a way that doesn't crash the game. -->Update suppression has potential applications for portal slicing, [[Tutorials/Block and item duplication|block duplication]], controlled crashing, and placing blocks in otherwise impossible locations, such as a sign or torch floating in mid-air, or a flower on non-dirt-based blocks.

With the changing of update order in Java Edition 22w11a, stack overflow update suppression was "fixed". This was intended as {{bug|MC-249082}} was resolved as "Works As Intended". Nevertheless, the change was reverted in 22w12a and a separate but related bug report, {{bug|MC-249181}}, showed that the "bug" was fixed in this snapshot.
