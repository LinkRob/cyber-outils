A '''harness''' is an item used on [[Happy Ghast|happy ghasts]] to ride on and comes with 16 colored variants. It was revealed in Minecraft Live - March 2025<ref>[https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=jGNgh5zEFkY Minecraft LIVE - March 2025]</ref> and added in the [[Chase the Skies]] Game Drop.

A harness also drops as an item when unequipping it from a [[Happy Ghast|happy ghast]] using [[Shears|shears]].

== Usage ==
[[File:Happy Ghast Harnessed.gif|thumb|200px|A happy ghast equipped with a harness]]
Happy Ghasts can only be ridden up to 4 [[Player|players]] when equipped with a harness. Using a harness on happy ghast equips it, and harnesses can also be equipped using a [[Dispenser|dispenser]] block. Using shears on a happy ghast that is wearing a harness unequips it, and drops it as an item.

Happy Ghasts also follow players holding a harness if they don't have one equipped yet.

Harnesses feature large vintage-style aviator goggles, which lowers and covers the happy ghast's eyes when it is being ridden by a player.
