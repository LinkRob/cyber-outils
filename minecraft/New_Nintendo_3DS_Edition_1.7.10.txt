'''1.7.10'''<ref>{{cite|url=https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360015936372|title=Minecraft: New Nintendo 3DS Edition - 1.7|date=September 19, 2018}}</ref> was an update for the {{el|ds}} released on September 19/20, 2018, which added the features of the [[Ender Update]], which included [[the End]] along with all End-related content (excluding [[shulker boxes]]). This is the first version to be released outside of North America and Japan.
