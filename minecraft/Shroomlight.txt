'''Shroomlights''' are [[light]]-emitting [[block]]s that generate in [[huge fungi]].

== Obtaining ==

Shroomlight blocks can be [[breaking|broken]] using any [[tool]], but a [[hoe]] is the fastest. They always drop themselves as an [[item (entity)|item]]
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Shroomlight|hoe|sword=1}}

=== Post-generation ===
Both crimson and warped [[huge fungi]] can generate shroomlights near their tops. This includes both naturally generated huge fungi and huge fungi grown with [[bone meal]].

== Usage ==
=== Light source ===
Shroomlights provide a light level of 15, the highest level in the game. Unlike Glowstone, Shroomlights also allows redstone signals to pass through them, as they are treated like a solid block. 

=== Composting ===
Placing a shroomlight into a [[composter]] has a 65% chance of raising the compost level.
