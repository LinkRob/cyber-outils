This guide is a simplified overview of all changes in the [[Nether Update]] ({{edition|java}} 1.16.x releases), which assumes at least some familiarity with the [[Buzzy Bees]] update (Java Edition 1.15.x releases).

== Mobs ==
=== New ===
{|{{Simple Guide table|New Mobs}}
|-
| [[Hoglin]]
| {{Animate|Hoglin BE2.png;Baby Hoglin JE2 BE3.png|128px}} ||
* [[Crimson forest]]
* Hoglin stables in [[bastion remnants]]
||
* {{Drop|item|Raw Porkchop|2|4}}

* {{xp|5}} (when killed by a [[player]] or tamed [[wolf]])
||
* Hoglins will run if in contact with [[warped fungi]], respawn anchors, or nether portals.
* Hoglins will turn into [[zoglin]]s if in the [[Overworld]] or [[the End]] after 15 seconds.
* Hoglins can be bred with [[crimson fungi]].
* Hoglins are hostile towards the player.

|-
| [[Piglin]]
|| {{Animate|Piglin.png;Baby Piglin.png|128px}} ||
* [[Crimson forest]]
* [[Nether wastes]]
* [[Bastion remnant]]
|| 
* 8.5% of chance to drop their naturally-spawned equipment. 
** Which can be a [[golden sword]], a [[crossbow]], or random [[golden armor]].
*{{xp|5}} ({{xp|12}} if it's a baby), plus {{xp|1|3}} per armor piece (when killed by a [[player]] or tamed [[wolf]])

||
* Adult piglins are neutral and babies are passive.
* Adult piglins can be [[bartering|bartered]] with.
* Piglins will run from placed [[soul fire]] and soul fire items.
* Piglins will not attack the player if they're wearing a piece of golden [[armor]].
* Piglins will turn into [[zombified piglin]]s if in the [[Overworld]] or [[the End]] after 15 seconds.
* Piglins will occasionally hunt hoglins and [[wither skeleton]]s, with a 10% chance of doing a ”victory dance” after killing a hoglin by jumping and running around the area.
* Up to 3 baby Piglins can ride on a baby hoglin at one time. The top one(s) piggy-back.
* Piglins will also hold, but not barter for, gold items (golden carrots, golden armor, etc.)
* Piglins will attack any player that has opened a chest in the bastion, mined the nether gold ore, or mined a gold block in front of them.
|-

* Piglin brutes are always hostile unlike other [[piglin]]s.
* Piglin brutes attack the player even when they're wearing [[armor|golden armor]].
* Piglin brutes do not run away from placed [[soul fire]] and soul fire items.
* Piglin brutes will not hunt hoglins.
* Piglin brutes will turn into [[zombified piglin]]s if in the [[Overworld]] or [[the End]] after 15 seconds.

=== Changed ===
{|{{Simple Guide table|Changed Mobs}}
|-
| [[Bee]] 
|| [[File:Bee.gif|128px]] ||
* Reduced the maximum distance a bee can wander away from its home hive when randomly wandering to around 22 blocks.
* Grow up and count down their breeding cool down when inside a beehive.
|-
| [[Cow]] 
|| [[File:Cow.png|128px]] ||
* Cows can now be milked in [[Creative]] mode.
|-
| [[Enderman]] 
|| [[File:Enderman.png|128px]] ||
* Enderman now spawn in the Nether more commonly again, especially in [[warped forest]]s.
* Enderman now pick up [[nylium]], [[roots]], and [[fungi]] variants.
* Enderman no longer pick up [[netherrack]].
|-
| [[Ghast]]
|| [[File:Ghast.gif|128px]] ||
* Ghasts' sounds are now heard at a shorter range.
|-
| [[Mooshroom]] 
|| [[File:Mooshroom.png|128px]] ||
* Mooshrooms can now be milked in Creative mode.
* Mushroom stew and suspicious stew can now be obtained in Creative mode.
|-
| [[Parrot]] 
|| {{animate|Red Parrot.png;Blue Parrot.png;Green Parrot.png;Cyan Parrot.png;Gray Parrot.png|84px}} ||
* Parrots imitate hostile mobs less often.
* Parrots no longer randomly imitate hostile mobs when gamemode is on Peaceful.
|-
| [[Snow Golem]] 
|| [[File:Snow Golem.png|128px]] ||
* Snow golems are now damaged by splash and lingering water bottles.
* Snow golems now drop [[carved pumpkin]] when they are sheared.
|-
| [[Villager]] 
|| {{animate|Plains Villager Base.png;Snowy Villager Base.png;Jungle Villager Base.png;Savanna Villager Base.png;Swamp Villager Base.png;Taiga Villager Base.png|128px}} ||
* When any villager is struck by lightning, the witch it is converted to will no longer despawn, regardless of whether the villager had been traded with or not.
|-
| Farmer [[villager]]
|| {{animate|Plains Farmer.png;Snowy Farmer.png;Jungle Farmer.png;Savanna Farmer.png;Swamp Farmer.png;Taiga Farmer.png|128px}} ||
* Farmer villagers can now put seeds in the [[composter]] to create [[bone meal]].
* Farmers can now use bone meal to grow [[crops]].
|-
| [[Wither]]
|| [[File:Wither.png|128px]] ||
* Withers can now be summoned using [[soul soil]] in the base. 
|-
| [[Zombie Pigman]]
|| {{Animate|Zombified Piglin BE6.png;Baby Zombified Piglin BE5.png|128px}} ||
* Zombie pigmen were renamed to "zombified piglin" and given a new [[model]] and texture.
* Zombified piglins now also spawn rarely in [[crimson forest]]s.
* Zombified piglins no longer attack players who have not attacked a zombified piglin.
* Zombified piglins now stop being angry if the targeted player dies nearby. (But only if gamerule <code>forgiveDeadPlayers</code> is set to <code>true</code>)
