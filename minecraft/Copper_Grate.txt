A '''copper grate''' is a transparent, see-through variant of [[copper|copper block]]. Copper grates do not conduct redstone, and mobs cannot spawn on top of them. They can also be [[waterlogged]]. Copper grates adjacent to other copper grates are invisible when viewed through a copper grate that is identical in states of oxidation and wax. They naturally generate in [[trial chambers]].

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Copper grates can be mined only with a stone [[pickaxe]] or better. If a copper grate is mined without the use of a pickaxe, it drops nothing.

=== Waxing ===
Copper grates can be turned into the respective waxed copper grates by {{Control|using}} a [[honeycomb]] item on them. Waxed copper grates do not oxidize and are identical to the non-waxed version.

=== Scraping ===
{{Control|Using}} an [[axe]] on a waxed copper grate turns it into the respective non-waxed copper grate. In addition, using an axe on an exposed, weathered, or oxidized copper grate reverts it one stage to a regular, exposed, or weathered copper grate respectively.

=== Lightning strike ===
Non-waxed copper grates are completely deoxidized when struck by lightning, and other non-waxed copper grates nearby are deoxidized randomly.
