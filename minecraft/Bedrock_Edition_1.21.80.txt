'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.80''' is a minor update to 1.21, released on May 6, 2025. It contains experimental features for [[Chase the Skies]].

== Additions ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Locator Bar]]
** A new feature for the [[Heads-up display|HUD]] that overrides in place of the XP bar.
** Has colored waypoints.
** Waypoints disappear if:
*** The player is under invisibility status effect.
*** The player is sneaking.
*** The player wears a mob head.
** Only works in multiplayer mode.
** Only available under "Locator Bar" experimental toggle.
* [[Vibrant Visuals]]
** A new graphics mode that adds water shaders, specular highlights, emissive textures, and directional lighting and shadows.
** Affects the graphical appearance, and has no effect on gameplay.
** Only supports on high-end devices.
** Only available under "Vibrant Visuals" experimental toggle.
* '''[[Flat]] world presets'''
** Added an ability to choose different flat world presets when creating a flat world.
** Comes with 8 presets:
*** Classic Flat
*** Tunnelers' Dream
*** Water World
*** Overworld
*** Snowy Kingdom
*** Bottomless Pit
*** Desert
*** Redstone Ready
** Prior to this update, it was only exclusive to [[Java Edition]].

=== Blocks ===
* [[Dried Ghast]]
** Can be found at soul sand valleys in the [[Nether]].
** Can be crafted using a soul sand and 8 ghast tears.
** Turns into a ghastling when soaked in water over time.
** Has 4 stages:
*** Dried (default)
*** Partially hydrated (stage 1)
*** Mostly hydrated (stage 2)
*** Hydrated (stage 3)
** Consequence: Reverts back to its initial dried (default) stage if:
*** The [[player]] breaks the dried ghast (regardless of stage)
*** The water is removed during hydration
** Only available under "Drop 2 2025" experimental toggle.

=== Items ===
* [[Happy Ghast Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to spawn happy ghasts directly.
*** There is also a 5% chance of spawning a ghastling directly when spawned using a spawn egg.
** Only available under "Drop 2 2025" experimental toggle.
* [[Harness]]
** Can be crafted using 3 leathers, 2 glass blocks, and a matching wool color.
** Can be used to ride or stand on happy ghasts.
** Comes with 16 colored variants.
** Only available under "Drop 2 2025" experimental toggle.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Ghastling]]
** Spawns after a dried ghast gets hydrated.
*** Can also be spawned by using this command: {{cmd|/summon happy_ghast ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ minecraft:spawn_baby}}.
** Turns into a happy ghast over time.
*** Feeding it with [[Snowball]]s can speed up the process.
** Only available under "Drop 2 2025" experimental toggle.
* [[Happy Ghast]]
** Spawns after a ghastling grows up (either over time or feeding with Snowballs).
*** Can also be spawned using its spawn egg.
** Passive version of [[Ghast]].
** Can be ridable up to 4 players using a [[Harness]].
*** Can also stand on top of them (with or without harnesses), like normal blocks.
** Only available under "Drop 2 2025" experimental toggle.
