'''Pale Hanging Moss''' is a gray, pale moss variant block that naturally grows underneath [[Leaves|Pale Oak Leaves]]. They are mostly found in the [[Pale Garden]]. Unlike [[Vines]], [[Weeping Vines]], [[Twisting Vines]] and [[Glow Berries]], Pale hanging moss blocks are not climbable. However, when placed under pale oak leaves, they emit eerie ambient creaking and twisting root sounds, similar to the [[Creaking]].

[[Sapling|Pale oak saplings]] bonemealed by players generate [[Pale oak]] trees without pale hanging moss, making hanging moss quite rare to obtain. Pale Hanging Moss is not craftable.

Pale hanging moss can also be obtained via trading with a [[Wandering Trader]].
