A '''copper golem''' is a buildable passive mob that takes items from [[copper chest]]s and organizes them into [[Chest|normal chests]], and oxidizes like normal [[Copper|copper blocks]].

== Spawning ==
Copper golems cannot spawn naturally. Instead, the [[player]] has to place a [[carved pumpkin]] or a [[jack o'lantern]] block on top of, below, or next to a [[copper block]]. On construction, the copper block turns into a copper chest while the carved pumpkin or a jack o'lantern block turns into a copper golem, both with the same oxidation level as the copper block.

Copper golems can also be spawned by using their [[spawn egg]] or by {{cmd|summon}} command.

== Behavior ==
Copper golems visit up to 10 [[copper chest]]s (regardless of their oxidization or waxed status) in sequence to try to take out up to 16 of the first item if the copper golem is not holding anything. If the copper chest is empty, the copper golem will idle for 7 seconds before moving on to another copper chest. Copper golems visit up to 10 [[chest]]s and [[trapped chest]]s in sequence to try to place the item in if the copper golem is holding an item.

Like [[iron golem]]s, Copper Golems sink and do not drown in water; however, they can slowly walk on blocks underwater. Like many [[passive mob]]s, they panic and run around when attacked.

Players can [[Dispenser|dispense]] [[head]]s onto the copper golem.

When a copper golem gets fully oxidized, it will turn into a [[copper golem statue]]. In that state, it will freeze into a random pose and drop any items it holds. Using an [[axe]] to scrape the Copper Golem Statue’s oxidation reverts it back to its normal active state. 

Right-clicking a copper golem while it is holding an item will give the item back.

== Drops ==
Copper golems drop 2 to 3 [[copper ingot]]s upon death by a player or tamed [[wolf]].

=== Oxidation ===
Like all [[Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper golems undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
* Unoxidized - Normal stage
* Exposed
* Weathered
* Oxidized
After being fully oxidized, the copper golem becomes a copper golem statue

Only [[lightning]] and [[axe]]s can reverse the oxidation process. Using a [[honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper golem prevents the oxidation.

== Appearance ==
Unlike [[iron golem]]s, the copper golem has a small copper body, with tiny arms and legs. It also has a small head with a [[Villager|villager nose]], bright glowing yellow eyes similar to the [[copper bulb]] and a short [[lightning rod]].

When it is in a statue state, its eyes do not glow and look vaguely similar to its old appearance showcased in the [[Minecraft Live 2021]].
