'''Bush''' may refer to:

* {{BlockLink|Bush}} – a vegetation block; added in [[Java Edition 1.21.5]].
* {{BlockLink|Dead Bush}} – a transparent, brown, "dead-looking" block found in [[desert]], [[badlands]], and [[giant tree taiga]] biomes.
* {{BlockLink|Sweet Berry Bush}} – a transparent block found in [[taiga]]s and [[snowy taiga]]s which can be used to harvest {{ItemLink|sweet berries}} when grown.
* {{BlockLink|Rose Bush}} – a type of flower found in [[forest]]s, [[birch forest]], and [[dark forest]] biome variants.
* {{BlockLink|Firefly Bush}} – a bushy variant vegetation block that grows in swamps and near rivers; this block emits [[Java Edition mentioned features|Firefly]] particles during night.
* {{BlockLink|Leaves}} – blocks that grow as a part of trees, which have a "bush-like" look and are thus sometimes used to simulate bushes when building.
* {{BlockLink|Azalea}} – decorative blocks only obtainable from the creative inventory that have flowering and flower less versions.
* {{BlockLink|id=shrub-inv}}{{BlockLink|id=dead-bush}} [[Shrubs]] - two variants of grass, one of which has the same appearance as a dead bush and the other the same appearance as normal grass.{{only|console}}
* {{EnvLink|Jungle Bush}} – a bush like tree in jungles.
