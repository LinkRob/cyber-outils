'''Samuel Åberg''' is a [[Mojang Studios]] employee currently working as the lead sound designer of ''[[Minecraft]]''.

== Trivia ==
* To create the sounds that the [[dolphin]]s<ref name="D">{{Mcnet|talking-dolphins|Talking Dolphins|May 19, 2018}}</ref> and [[panda]]s<ref>{{ytl|7mi5au5qH8g|Recording Panda sounds for Minecraft!||October 9, 2018}}</ref> make in the game he went to [[Wikipedia:Kolmården Wildlife Park|Kolmården Wildlife Park]] and [[Wikipedia:zh:長隆野生動物世界|Chimelong Safari Park]].
* Samuel Åberg is credited under "Music and sound" as of [[1.9-pre3#Changes|1.9-pre3]].
* His in-game skin resembles American guitarist {{w|Buckethead}}.
* Åberg’s cat Odi was used to record the sounds of cats.
