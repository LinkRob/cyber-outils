A '''snow golem''' is a buildable [[mob|passive mob]] that throws [[Snowball|snowballs]] at monsters, which provokes them into attacking it. Depending on the [[Biome|biome temperature]] it will also either produce a trail of [[snow]], or take heat damage and die.

==Creation==
{{see also|Tutorials/Snow golems}}
{{FakeImage|align=none|style=display:block;float:left;margin:0 1em 1em 0;|{{BlockGrid|scale=2|p=Carved Pumpkin|s=Snow Block|p|s|s}}|Snow golem build configuration.<br>[[Jack o'lantern]]s can also be used.}}
[[File:Snow golem attacking a witch.png|thumb|Snow golems search for the nearest monster and attack it.]]
Snow golems are created by placing 2 [[snow block]]s in an I shape (as shown in the image), and then placing a [[carved pumpkin]] or [[jack o'lantern]] on top. The pumpkin may be placed by the player, a [[dispenser]], or an [[enderman]].<ref>{{reddit|korjk/endermen_can_make_snow_golems}}</ref> The building pattern also works when arranged sideways or upside-down, as long as the pumpkin is the last block placed. The player can place the snow blocks in any pattern and then place an uncarved pumpkin.{{Only|java}}

The snow golem spawns at the location of the snow block as its feet when created horizontally.
{{-}}

==Drops==
A snow golem drops between 0 to 15 {{ItemLink|snowball}}s upon death and a {{BlockLink|Carved Pumpkin|carved pumpkin}} when sheared.

==Behavior==
{{see also|Tutorials/Snow farming}}
A snow golem wanders aimlessly and tries to avoid [[water]], obstacles and environmental hazards, but is immune to damage from [[powder snow|powdered snow]].

[[File:Snow golem sheared.png|thumb|right|A snow golem after shearing the carved pumpkin from its head.]]
A snow golem wears its [[carved pumpkin]] like a helmet. Its actual head is hidden beneath the pumpkin. [[Shearing]] a snow golem removes the pumpkin, revealing its face and making it drop the pumpkin. The pumpkin is stored using the <code>Pumpkin</code> tag instead of <code>ArmorItems</code>; a snow golem does not display its <code>HandItems</code> or its <code>ArmorItems</code>. After shearing the pumpkin, the player can give a snow golem a new pumpkin only by using a command, such as {{cmd|data}}{{only|java}}.

As a snow golem moves, it leaves a trail of [[snow]] on the ground if the blocks can support it<ref name="SnowGolemTrail">{{Tweet|notch|116449906658516992|Also, they spread snow by walking around. Nice.|September 21, 2011}}</ref> in any biome regardless of the [[Biome#List_of_Overworld_climates|temperature]], unless you are in a mushroom field biome, in which it does not spread snow. If the <code>mobGriefing</code> [[game rule]] is {{cd|false}}, a snow trail does not appear.

Snow golems are damaged in contact with [[rain]] or water and in [[biome]]s with a temperature greater than 1.0. Examples include [[savanna]]s, [[badlands]], [[desert]]s or biomes in [[the Nether]]. The [[Fire Resistance]] [[effect]] allows them to survive in these biomes.{{only|java}}<ref>{{bug|MCPE-94448}}</ref>

[[File:SnowGolemBehavior.png|thumb|A group of snow golems throwing snowballs at a spider.]]
Snow golems move toward all monsters (except [[ghast]]s) and throw [[snowball]]s at them up to 10 [[block]]s away, provoking them, regardless of whether they attack the player.<ref>http://gaming.stackexchange.com/questions/54362/what-is-a-snow-golems-range</ref> When captured, they throw snowballs up to 16 blocks away. They throw one snowball per second. Thrown snowballs do not deal [[damage]] except to [[blaze]]s and [[wolf|wolves]],<ref>{{bug|MC-72151}}</ref> but the snowballs knock back any mobs that they hit.

Additionally, snow golems also attack [[creeper]]s and never intentionally attack the player. 

The snow golem's snowballs can set other mobs on fire in addition to knockback, if the shot flies through a lava block or a block that is on fire.{{only|bedrock}}

A snow golem is not provoked by another golem (snow or iron) attacking it, although an [[iron golem]] that is hit accidentally by a snow golem's snowball attacks the snow golem.

==Trivia==
[[File:Snow Golem Stats.png|thumb|Snow golem biome statistics.]]
*Snow golems cannot be made by [[piston]]s because the code for generating snow golems are found in the [[pumpkin]]’s <code>OnBlockPlace</code> ("placed by a player") event.
*Snow golems rarely spawn naturally in snow biomes; if two snow blocks happen to spawn naturally with a patch of pumpkins, a snow golem may spawn naturally.{{Only|bedrock}}
