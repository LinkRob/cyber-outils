'''Jonas Mårtensson''' is the chief executive officer of [[Mojang Studios]]. He joined the company as vice president on October 2, 2013, when his twin brother and the CEO of Mojang, [[Carl Manneh]], announced his recruitment.<ref name="CarlClone"/> With the acquisition of Mojang by Microsoft in November of 2014 and the subsequent retirement of his brother, he assumed the position of CEO.<ref>https://help.mojang.com/customer/portal/articles/331367-employees</ref>
