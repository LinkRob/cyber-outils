The '''Boss Update''' is the name for ''[[Pocket Edition|Minecraft: Pocket Edition]]'' version [[Pocket Edition v0.16.0 alpha|Alpha v0.16.0]], an update that was released on October 21, 2016.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20161022094949/https://www.mojang.com/2016/10/add-ons-realms-uploads-sponges-pocket-win-10-updated-to-016/</ref> The iOS version was released a few days later on October 25, 2016.<ref>{{tweet|tomcc|791060264917622784|MCPE 0.16 is out on iOS right now :)|25 Oct 2016}}</ref>

The official name for the update, the ''Boss Update'', was revealed on August 29, 2016 in a blog post on the Mojang website.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20160901030941/http://www.mojang.com/2016/08/sneak-peek-boss-update-is-coming-to-pocket-and-windows-10/</ref>

== Notable features ==

{{main|Pocket Edition v0.16.0 alpha|New Nintendo 3DS Edition 1.3.12}}

=== Additions ===

* Lots of settings changes.
* Add-ons.
* [[Commands]].
* [[Ocean monument]]s.
* 4 new blocks:
** [[Sea lantern]], [[Prismarine]], [[Wet sponge]], and [[Beacon]]s.
* 6 new items:
** [[Prismarine crystals]], [[Prismarine shard]]s, the [[Nether star]], and the Potion/Splash Potion/Arrow of Decay.
* 3 new mobs:
** [[Guardian]], [[Elder Guardian]], and [[Wither]].

=== Changes ===

* Added sounds for various items, blocks, and mobs.

== Further revisions ==

* [[Pocket Edition v0.16.1 alpha|Alpha v0.16.1]] fixed some bugs and crashes from Alpha v0.16.0.
* [[Pocket Edition v0.16.2 alpha|Alpha v0.16.2]] fixed some bugs from Alpha v0.16.1, and also added the Cartoon texture pack.
