'''Haste''' is a [[status effect]] that increases attack speed by 10% per level and increases mining speed by 20% per level. Negative levels decrease mining and attack speed, similar to [[Mining Fatigue]].

== Immune mobs ==
All mobs are immune to Haste. Most mobs are unaffected by Haste as they cannot mine blocks.

== Interaction with Mining Fatigue ==
[[Mining Fatigue]] III inflicted by [[elder guardian]]s prevents the player from [[instant mining]] most blocks regardless of the Haste effect, along with a huge decrease of mining speed. However, lower levels of Mining Fatigue can be overcome by Haste of level III or greater, with a resultant reduced Haste effect.

== Notes ==

* At levels 3 until 127 ([[Java Edition]] or [[Bedrock Edition]]) or 255 ([[Pocket Edition]]), the [[player]]'s arm does not move while mining.
* Haste decreases the charging time of [[sweep attack]].{{only|java}}
* An alternative method of increasing mining speed is the [[efficiency]] enchantment.
