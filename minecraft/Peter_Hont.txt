'''Peter Hont''' is the interim audio director<ref>https://se.linkedin.com/in/peterhont</ref> for [[Mojang Studios]] who helped compose the music for ''[[Minecraft Dungeons]]'', both for the [[MCD:Mainland|Mainland]] levels and all current DLC levels.<ref>https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/the-sound-scary</ref> He is most known for his [[Minecraft Dungeons:Original Game Soundtrack|''Minecraft Dungeons: Original Game Soundtrack'']] album.
