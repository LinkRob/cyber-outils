The '''GDC build''' was a [[developer version]] of {{Edition|xbox 360}} showcased at the Game Developers' Conference 2012.<ref>https://youtu.be/8lDVYWeUDJQ</ref><ref>https://youtu.be/3e7u9MnzrfU</ref> This version is based off [[Java Edition Beta 1.6.6]].

==Additions==
* New logo in the menu.
* New loading screen with hints.
* Panorama now loops endlessly.
* New world selection menu.
* Splitscreen improved.
* New music.
* Sound quality improved.
* Huge amount of bug fixes.
* Changed the crafting menu and the tooltips.
* Performance improvements.
* Numerous other changes.
