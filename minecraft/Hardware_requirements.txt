'''Hardware requirements''' are officially announced hardware configurations which are stated to run some software product at some level of quality. Hardware requirements are announced for some editions of ''Minecraft''.

* [[Java Edition hardware requirements]] lists hardware requirements for ''Java Edition''.
* [[Bedrock Edition#Compatible devices]] lists hardware requirements for Bedrock Edition. (Do note the hardware is old and may no longer be supported)
