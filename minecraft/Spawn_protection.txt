'''Spawn protection''' prevents players from building in spawn, undoing any attempts at placing or breaking [[block]]s. Players who have any [[permission level]] except for level 0 are able to bypass it and build anyway. There are several indirect <!--indirect, using commands and cheats--> ways to bypass spawn protection, namely activating pistons, dispensers, explosives and using mob griefing. If there are no ops on the server<!-- as in defined, not as in online-->, then spawn protection does not apply. Spawn protection only applies in the [[Overworld]]<!-- as a world, not as a dimension-->. Spawn protection does not apply in singleplayer.

The <code>spawn-protection</code> field in <samp>[[server.properties]]</samp> determines the side length of the square spawn protection area as 2''x''+1. Setting this to 0 or a negative value disables the spawn protection. A value of 1 protects a 3×3 square centered on the spawn point, 2 protects 5×5, 3 protects 7×7, etc. The area that it protects is (spawn-protection×2 + 1)×(spawn-protection×2 + 1).
