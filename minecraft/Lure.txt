'''Lure''' is a [[fishing rod]] [[enchantment]] that decreases the wait time for a bite on the hook.

== Usage ==
Lure increases the rate of something biting the [[player]]'s hook while [[fishing]]. Specifically, it decreases the wait time for something to appear by 5 seconds per level. See [[Fishing]] for more information.

If [[commands]] are used to increase the enchantment level beyond 5, the wait timer does not start and fish do not bite.
