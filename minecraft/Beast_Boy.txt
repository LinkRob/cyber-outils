'''Beast Boy''' was a humanoid [[mob]] that was used for testing purposes, available during [[Java Edition Indev 0.31 20100130]].

== Spawning ==
Beast Boy spawned randomly in the world.{{more info}}

== Behavior ==
Beast Boy was a [[passive]] [[mob]]. It had no animation, so it aimlessly glided and jumped around the [[world]] in the same, still pose, and never stopped moving around. Beast Boy, along with [[Rana]], [[Steve (mob)|Steve]], and [[Black Steve]] acted in this identical manner.
Upon [[death]], Beast Boy would not fall over to the ground or flash red or white and appear to die, as most mobs do. It would just disappear shortly thereafter, and [[drop]] the [[items]] it was designated to drop.

== Drops ==
Upon death, Beast Boy drops:
* {{ItemLink|String}} (0–2) (most common)
* {{ItemLink|Feather}} (0–2)
* {{ItemLink|Gunpowder}} (0–2)
* {{ItemLink|Flint and Steel}} (0–1) (extremely rare)

== Sounds ==
Beast Boy did not make any [[sound]]s.

== Trivia ==
*"Beast Boy" was named and modeled after [[Wikipedia:Beast Boy|the character of the same name]] from [[Wikipedia:DC Comics|DC Comics]], most well known as a member of the [[Wikipedia:Teen Titans|Teen Titans]].
*Beast Boy, as well as all the other mobs made by Dock, was meant to be animated, but the game appears to just pick a random frame and doesn't animate any further. They just glide around in the same pose. However, the MD3 model itself contains a 22 frame animation of it walking.
