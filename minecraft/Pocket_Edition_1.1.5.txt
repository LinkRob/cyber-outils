'''1.1.5''' was an update for {{el|pe}} that added new and updated existing Marketplace content as well as fixing several bugs.<ref>{{cite|url=https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360004168591|title=MCPE/WIN10/GEAR VR - 1.1.5}}</ref> This update is the last version for the [[Pocket Edition|''Pocket Edition'']] series except iOS ([[Pocket Edition 1.1.7|1.1.7]]), and is replaced by {{el|be}}. It made it possible for additional content to be added to the Marketplace without the need for a Pocket Edition version upgrade. It was the last update to be released for the [[Pocket Edition|''Pocket Edition'']] series, including [[Apple TV Edition|''Apple TV Edition'']] and was going to be replaced by {{el|be}}.</onlyinclude>

== Changes ==
=== General ===
; [[Marketplace]]
* Updates to ''PureBDCraft''
* All Marketplace-related modules were moved into a separate library.
** Future additional content to the Marketplace will not require new releases of ''Pocket Edition''.

== Fixes ==
* Fixed clock and compass textures becoming corrupted after reconnecting to the world with a resource pack
* Fixed the texture of the tripwire string
* Optimized the skin picker and all skins will now be displayed</onlyinclude>

== Trivia ==
* Despite being the final update to ''Apple TV Edition'', ''Apple TV Edition'' remained downloadable for another 413 days, until it was discontinued on September 24, 2018.
