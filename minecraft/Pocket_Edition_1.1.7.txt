'''1.1.7''' is an update for {{el|pe}}, released only for iOS on September 15, 2017, which added support for [[Wikipedia:IPv6|IPv6]].<ref>{{cite|url=https://itunes.apple.com/app/id479516143|title=Minecraft: Pocket Edition on the App Store}}</ref> It is the final update to be labeled under ''Pocket Edition'', before the release of [[Bedrock Edition 1.2.0|1.2.0]].

== Additions ==
=== General ===
; Networking
* Added support for IPv6.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
; [[Marketplace]]
* The ability to buy content in China is removed.

== Trivia ==
* This was the final update to the game subtitled ''[[Pocket Edition]]''.
