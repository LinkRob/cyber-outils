'''Fatal Poison''' inflicts [[damage]] to entities over time, and can kill both undead and non-undead mobs, similar to [[Wither (effect)|Wither]]. The effect is actually called "Poison," but its ID distinguishes it from the other [[Poison]] effect, which cannot kill.

The player's hearts turn yellow-green ({{hp|20|poisoned=1|notag=1}}).
Amplifiers outside the range 0–31 (corresponding to levels 1–32) are treated {{w|modulo operation|modulo}} 32 (as though reduced by 32 until they are within the range, or as the remainder from division by 32, i.e. 33 is treated as 1.)

Feeding a cookie to a parrot instantly applies the Fatal Poison effect to the parrot, causing it to die shortly after.

== Immune mobs ==
Boss mobs and witches are unaffected by this status effect.

== Death message ==
If the player is killed by Fatal Poison, the [[death message]] "<player> was killed by magic" appears, as when killed by a potion of Harming.
