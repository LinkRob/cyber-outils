The entries on this alphabetized '''list of minerals''' all are blocks and items in the game that share a name with real-world minerals. Minerals are inorganic, naturally occurring crystalline materials.

* {{BlockLink|id=Block of Amethyst|Amethyst}} – [[Overworld]] variety of quartz found in {{EnvLink|amethyst geode}}s
* {{BlockLink|Calcite}} – carbonate mineral found in {{EnvLink|amethyst geode}}s
* {{BlockLink|id=Ice|Ice (disambiguation)|Ice}} – frozen, crystalline [[water]]
* {{BlockLink|id=Snow Block|Snow (disambiguation)|Snow}} – sky-fallen snowflakes (microscopic water crystals)
; {{EnvLink|id=blob|Ore}} minerals
* {{BlockLink|id=Block of Copper|Copper}} – electrically conductive metal that readily oxidizes
* {{BlockLink|id=Block of Diamond|Diamond (disambiguation)|Diamond}} – very hard crystal of pure carbon
* {{BlockLink|id=Block of Emerald|Emerald}} – precious gemstone found in [[mountain]]s
* {{BlockLink|id=Block of Gold|Gold}} – soft, precious metal
* {{BlockLink|id=Block of Iron|Iron}} – hard, durable metal
* {{BlockLink|id=Block of Lapis Lazuli|Lapis Lazuli}} – used for enchanting

=== Fictitious minerals ===
While the materials that make up this list are unique to Minecraft, either their names directly imply they're minerals or they may be commonly perceived as such. Their actual nature is up to one's own imagination.
* {{ItemLink|End Crystal}} – craftable dragon-healing crystal from the [[End]]
* {{ItemLink|Glowstone Dust|Glowstone}} – brittle, glowing material from the [[Nether]]
* {{ItemLink|Netherite Scrap|Netherite}} – ultrarare, super-hard material that can't be destroyed in [[lava]]; found only in the [[Nether]]
* {{ItemLink|Nether Star}} – material dropped by the [[Wither]]
* {{ItemLink|Nether Quartz}} – [[Nether]] variety of quartz used as an ornamental block or for special redstone components
* {{ItemLink|Prismarine Crystals}} – rare building material found in [[Monument]]s
* {{ItemLink|Redstone}} – material that can carry signals and activate moving parts
* {{ItemLink|Resin Clump}} – natural material that is obtained from breaking [[Creaking Heart|creaking hearts]] or punching [[creaking|creakings]] to generate clumps

=== Rocks (non-minerals) ===
Minerals are not rocks. Minerals are crystals, and rocks are collections of minerals.
