'''Tricky Trials''' is a major update that features new [[copper]] and [[tuff]] blocks, [[mace]]s, [[trial chambers]], ominous events, and more. It is released on June 13, 2024. It is the last major update as Mojang switched to [[game drop]]s on September 2024.

== Additions ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Ominous Event]]
** A new in-game event that can be triggered with Bad Omen.
*** When in trial chambers, all trial spawners will become ominous.
*** When on a [[village]], it triggers a [[raid]] if [[raid omen]] doesn't remove in time.

=== Blocks ===
* [[Crafter]]
** A new redstone block that crafts an item when powered on.
** Can be crafted with 5 [[iron ingots]], 2 [[redstone dust]]s, a [[crafting table]], and a [[dropper]].
* [[Vault]]
** Can only be obtained in [[creative inventory]].
** Generates in [[trial chambers]].
** Requires a [[trial key]] to open it.
* [[Ominous Vault]]
** Can only be obtained by using this command:
*** [[Java Edition]]: <code>/give @s minecraft:vault[block_state={ominous:"true"},block_entity_data={id:"minecraft:vault",config:{key_item:{count:1,id:"minecraft:ominous_trial_key"},loot_table:"minecraft:chests/trial_chambers/reward_ominous"}}]</code> and <code>/setblock x y z minecraft:vault[ominous=true]{config:{key_item:{count:1,id:"minecraft:ominous_trial_key"},loot_table:"minecraft:chests/trial_chambers/reward_ominous"}}</code>
*** [[Bedrock Edition]]: <code>/setblock x y z vault ["ominous"=true]</code>
** Generates in trial chambers.
** Requires an [[ominous trial key]] to open it.
* [[Trial Spawner]]
** A new mob spawner block that generates in trial chambers.
** Spawns a limited number of mobs.
** Ejects a reward when a [[player]] successfully defeats all mobs.
** Has a 30-minute cooldown before resetting to a different one.
* [[Ominous Trial Spawner]]
** Activates when a [[Bad Omen]]-inflicted player gets too close to the normal trial spawner.
** Shoots projectiles and arrows in addition to mobs spawning.
* [[Heavy Core]]
** Can be obtained by unlocking an ominous vault at 7.5% chance.
** Can be used to craft [[mace]]s.

=== Items ===
* [[Mace]]
** A new melee weapon that deals greater damage to targeting mobs per block fallen when performing a smash attack.
** Can be crafted with a heavy core and a breeze rod.
* [[Trial Key]]
** Can be obtained from [[trial spawner]]s.
** Can be used to open vaults.
* [[Ominous Trial Key]]
** Can be obtained from [[ominous trial spawner]]s.
** Can be used to open ominous vaults.
* [[Breeze Rod]]
** Can be obtained by killing [[breeze]]s.
** Can be used to craft maces, wind charges, and flow [[armor trim]]s.
*** Can also be used to brew potion of wind charging.
* [[Wind Charge]]
** A new projectile that knocks mobs and players back when hit.
** Can be crafted with a [[breeze rod]].
*** Can produce 4 of them.
** Utilitized by [[breeze]]s.
* [[Ominous Bottle]]
** Can be obtained from unlocking vaults and by killing [[raid captain]]s.
** Has 5 levels.
** Gives a player [[Bad Omen]] effect that lasts for 60 minutes when used.
* Trial Explorer Map
** Are sold by [[villager|cartographer villagers]].
** Can be used to find and locate trial chambers.
* [[Breeze Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to spawn [[breeze]]s.
* [[Bogged Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to spawn [[bogged]]s.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Bogged]]
** A new undead [[skeleton]] variant that spawns in [[swamp]] and [[mangrove swamp]] biomes.
** It wields [[bow]] and shoot players with an [[arrow]], inflicting [[poison]] when hit.
** Like any undead mobs:
*** Are damaged by {{EffectLink|Instant Health}} and healed by {{EffectLink|Instant Damage}}.
*** Are not affected by {{EffectLink|Regeneration}} and {{EffectLink|Poison}}.
*** Take extra damage from [[Smite]] enchantment.
*** Are not targeted by the {{EntityLink|wither}}.
*** Burns in sunlight.
*** Causes an [[armadillo]] to hide in its shell.
* [[Breeze]]
** A new hostile mob that spawns in trial chambers.
** Shoots wind charges.
** Drops breeze rods when killed by player or tamed [[wolf]].

=== Effects ===
* [[Trial Omen]]
** A new status effect in which all trial spawners will become ominous when an inflicted player sees it.
* [[Raid Omen]]
** A new status effect in which it triggers a [[raid]] in a [[village]] if an inflicted player fails to remove it for 30 seconds.
* [[Wind Charged]]
** A new status effect that releases a burst of wind when an inflicted player or mob dies.
* [[Infested]]
** A new status effect that has a 10% chance of spawning a [[silverfish]] when an inflicted player or mob gets hit.
* [[Oozing]]
** A new status effect that spawns two [[slime]]s when an inflicted player or mob dies.
* [[Weaving]]
** A new status effect that leaves 2-3 [[cobwebs]] when an inflicted player or mob dies.

=== Enchantments ===
* Breach
** A new [[mace]]-exclusive enchantment that lessens the [[armor]]'s effectiveness.
** Has 4 levels.
* Density
** A new mace-exclusive enchantment that increases damage dealt per block fallen.
** Has 5 levels.
* Wind Burst
** A new mace-exclusive enchantment that creates a wind charge effect when executing a smash attack.
** Has 3 levels.

=== Advancements ===
* Wax On
** Now applies to copper grates, chiseled copper, copper doors and copper trapdoors.
* Wax Off
** Now applies to copper grates, chiseled copper, copper doors and copper trapdoors.
* Minecraft: Trial(s) Edition
** Requires entering a Trial Chamber.
* Lighten Up
** Requires deoxidizing the copper bulb.
** Requires the Wax Off achievement.
* Who Needs Rockets?
** Requires launching yourself 8 blocks up with a single wind charge.
* Blowback
** Requires killing a breeze via deflecting its wind charge.
* Crafters Crafting Crafters
** Requires crafting a crafter in a crafter.
* Under Lock and Key
** Requires unlocking a Vault with a Trial Key.
* Revaulting
** Requires unlocking an Ominous Vault with an Ominous Trial Key.
* Over-Overkill
** Requires smashing an entity one shot with a mace.

=== Structures ===
* [[Trial Chambers]]
** A new giant underground structure that provide players a mid-game combat challenges.
** Made up of [[tuff]] and [[copper]] blocks.
** It is where [[breeze]]s spawn via trial spawners.
** Has vaults and ominous vaults.
** Has several chambers connected altogether through corridors.
