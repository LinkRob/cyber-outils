A '''zombie nautilus''' is an undead neutral mob that was added in [[Mounts of Mayhem]]. It is an [[undead]] variant of the [[nautilus]] and is always ridden by [[drowned]], forming a [[Jockey|zombie nautilus jockey]]. It was revealed in [[Minecraft Wiki:Drafts/Minecraft Live 2025 2nd|Minecraft Live - September 2025]].<ref>[https://youtu.be/AEHc8jrcc6g Minecraft LIVE - September 2025]</ref>

== Spawning ==
Zombie nautili have two unique variants: the normal variant and the coral variant.

Normal zombie nautilus variants spawn in most ocean biomes, while coral zombie nautilus variants spawn in [[warm ocean]] biomes. Zombie nautili generally sometimes spawn with a [[drowned]] riding on it, forming a [[Jockey|Zombie Nautilus Jockey]]. Zombie nautilus jockies always appear hostile.

When the drowned rider is killed, the zombie nautilus becomes neutral.

Zombie nautili do not spawn in [[Difficulty|peaceful difficulty]], like all undead mobs.

== Drops ==
Zombie nautiluses drop 1 to 3 [[Experience|experience orbs]] and [[rotten flesh]] upon death by a player or tamed [[wolf]].

== Behavior ==
Like nautiluses, zombie nautiluses use their mouths to propel forward. When a player attacks an untamed nautilus, it uses its dash attack to propel forward and inflict damage on the player.

Unlike nautiluses, zombie nautiluses can survive on land instead of suffocating out of water.

=== Taming ===
Zombie Nautili can be tamed by feeding them [[Pufferfish (item)|pufferfish]] or with [[Bucket of aquatic mob|Buckets with Pufferfish]].

=== Riding ===
Tamed zombie nautili can be equipped with a [[Minecraft Wiki:Drafts/Nautilus Armor|Nautilus Armor]] and can be ridden on by using a [[saddle]]. When ridden, it gives a "[[Minecraft Wiki:Drafts/Breath of the Nautilus|Breath of the Nautilus]]" effect, which pauses the player's oxygen bar and prevents the player from [[drowning]].

=== Breeding ===
Unlike nautiluses, zombie nautiluses cannot be bred, as they do not have their baby variants.

A '''zombie nautilus jockey''' is an undead hostile jockey consisting of a [[drowned]] riding a zombie nautilus, making it hostile. It has a 50% chance of spawning.

=== As an [[Undead]] Mob ===
Like any undead mobs, zombie nautili:
* Are damaged by {{EffectLink|Instant Health}} and healed by {{EffectLink|Instant Damage}};
* Are not affected by {{EffectLink|Regeneration}} and {{EffectLink|Poison}};
* Take extra damage from [[Smite]] enchantment.
* Burns in sunlight (when on land)
* Are not targeted by the {{EntityLink|wither}}.
* Causes an [[armadillo]] to hide in its shell.

Unlike most undead mobs, zombie nautili are not targeted by [[iron golem]]s and [[snow golem]]s. However, zombie nautilus jockey riders are targeted.

== Appearance ==
Unlike nautili, zombie nautili appear in a dark, sea green colour and a greyish-tan colored cracked shell, with green plant ([[kelp]] or [[seagrass]]) stuck to it.

Coral zombie nautili variants share a similar appearance with their normal variants, except with [[corals]] stuck or grown on their shells.
