{{exclusive|java}}
[[Java Edition 1.13|1.13]] modified the IDs ​​of many blocks, items, [[biome]]s, [[particles]], [[painting]]s, [[entities]], [[statistics]] and sound events, removed numeric IDs, added and removed some [[Java Edition data values|block states]], and changed [[NBT format|NBT tags]] and display names.<ref>https://bugs.mojang.com/secure/attachment/151784/the_flattening.txt of {{bug|MC-121742}}</ref> This change is known as "'''The Flattening'''".
The contents of this page serve as a comparison between the identifiers of versions [[Java Edition 1.12.2|1.12.2]] and [[Java Edition 1.13|1.13]]. This was not done to Bedrock Edition, keeping most Bedrock item IDs the same.

== Block and item IDs ==

[[Block]]s and [[item]]s that change their ID in any way also affect their [[statistics]].

== NBT data ==

[[Chunk format]] was modified.

=== Items ===

Changes to [[Player.dat format#Item structure|item structure]]:
* {{code|Damage}} in the root of the item data removed.
* {{code|Damage}} added in the {{code|tag}} tag, only used for durability.
* {{code|map}} integer added in the {{code|tag}} tag, for [[map]]s.
* {{code|Base}} of [[Shield]]s has the colors in the opposite order for consistency.
* {{code|ench}} for [[enchantment]]s got renamed to {{code|Enchantments}}.
* [[Enchantment]] Ids in {{code|Enchantments}} and {{code|StoredEnchantments}} now use the string ids.

=== Entities ===

Changes to [[Chunk format#Entity format|entity format]]:
* {{code|CollarColor}} of [[Wolves]] has the colors in the opposite order for consistency.
* {{code|carried}} and {{code|carriedData}} of [[Endermen]] merged into {{code|carriedBlockState}}.<ref group="NBT note" name="tag">This tag will change to a compound format with {{code|{Name:"block_id",Properties:{state:"value"}}}}</ref>
* {{code|inTile}} and {{code|inData}} of [[Arrow]]s and [[Spectral Arrow]]s merged into {{code|inBlockState}}.<ref group="NBT note" name="tag" />
* {{code|DisplayTile}} and {{code|DisplayData}} of [[Minecart]]s merged into {{code|DisplayState}}.<ref group="NBT note" name="tag" />
* {{code|Block}} and {{code|Data}} of [[Tutorials/Falling blocks|Falling Block]]s merged into {{code|BlockState}}.<ref group="NBT note" name="tag" />
* {{code|ParticleParams1}} and {{code|ParticleParams2}}  from [[Area Effect Cloud]]s removed, merged with Particle (same formatting as in the command).

=== Blocks ===

Changes to [[Chunk format#Block entity format|block entity format]]:
* [[Flower pot]] block entity removed.
* [[Note block]] block entity removed.
* {{code|BlockId}} and {{code|BlockData}} of [[Piston|moving piston]]s merged into {{code|blockState}}.<ref group="NBT note" name="tag" />
* {{code|Base}} from [[banner]]s removed.

; Flower Pot
<div class="treeview">
* {{nbt|compound}} Block entity data
** {{nbt inherit/blockentity}}
** {{nbt|string|Item}}: The Block ID of the plant in the pot. Known valid blocks are ''minecraft:sapling'' (6), ''minecraft:tallgrass'' (31), ''minecraft:deadbush'' (32), ''minecraft:yellow_flower'' (37), ''minecraft:red_flower'' (38), ''minecraft:brown_mushroom'' (39), ''minecraft:red_mushroom'' (40), ''minecraft:cactus'' (81). Other block and item IDs may be used, but not all will render. Together with '''Data''', this determines the item dropped by the pot when destroyed.
** {{nbt|int|Data}}: The data value to use in conjunction with the above Block ID. For example if Item is 6 (a sapling block), the Data is used to indicate the type of sapling.
</div>
