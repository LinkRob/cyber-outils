A '''block of raw iron''' is a raw [[metal]] [[block]] equivalent to nine [[raw iron]]. 

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Blocks of raw iron only drop themselves as items when mined using a stone [[pickaxe]] or better.

{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Block of Raw Iron|Pickaxe|Stone}}

=== Natural generation ===
Blocks of raw iron can be found rarely inside of huge [[ore vein]]s below Y=0.
