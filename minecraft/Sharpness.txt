'''Sharpness''' is an [[enchantment]] applied to a [[sword]], [[axe]] or [[spear]] that increases melee damage.

== Usage ==
{{IN|je}}, Sharpness adds <code>0.5 * level + 0.5</code> extra damage.

{{IN|be}}, each level of Sharpness adds 1.25 extra damage.

An [[anvil]] is required for the player to apply Sharpness V to a wooden, stone, iron, diamond, or netherite weapon, though iron and diamond swords with it can generate in [[end city]] and [[bastion remnant]] chests. Golden weapons can get Sharpness V through enchanting.

==Incompatibilities==
Sharpness, [[Smite]], [[Cleaving]],{{upcoming|Java Combat Tests}} and [[Bane of Arthropods]] are mutually exclusive. However, if [[commands]] are used to have two or more of these enchantments on the same item, their effects stack.{{only|java}}
