'''v0.6.1 alpha''' was a minor update to '' [[Pocket Edition|Minecraft: Pocket Edition]]'' that was released on Android and Fire devices on January 31, 2013<ref>http://web.archive.org/web/20130306041212/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mojang.minecraftpe</ref>{{verify|prove for Fire as well}} and on iOS devices on February 2, 2013<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20130304210857/https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/minecraft-pocket-edition/id479516143?mt=8</ref> to fix some of the bugs and crashes in [[Pocket Edition v0.6.0 alpha|v0.6.0 alpha]]. This version was also used for [[Pi Edition]].
