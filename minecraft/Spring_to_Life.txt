'''Spring to Life''' (also referred to as '''Drop 1 2025'''{{Only|BE}}) is a game drop that features warm and cold variants on various [[Mob|mobs]], new test blocks{{Only|JE}}, new vegetation blocks, new ambience sounds and revamping the texture of all [[Spawn Egg]]s. It is released on March 25, 2025.

Prior to its name and release date reveal on Minecraft Live - March 2025, it was referred to as "Spring Drop 2025".

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Bush]]
** Can be obtained by using [[shears]] or any tool enchanted with [[Silk Touch]].
** Adapts different shades of green depending on the biome.
* [[Flower|Cactus Flower]]
** Naturally generates on top of [[Cactus|cacti]].
** Can be placed on any soild block
* [[Firefly Bush]]
** Naturally generates in [[swamp]] biomes and near [[water]].
** Emits firefly [[Ambience|particles]] when exposed to darkness.
** Emits ambient sounds
* [[Leaf Litter]]
** Can be obtained by smelting any [[Leaves|leaves block]].
** 4 of them can be placed in one block, similar to [[Pink Petals]].
** Can be placed on any solid block
* [[Wildflowers]]
* Short Dry Grass
** Naturally generates in dry arid biomes like [[desert|deserts]] and [[badlands]].
** Tall Dry Grass
* Test Block{{Only|JE}}
** Test Instance Block{{Only|JE}}

=== Items ===
* [[Egg|Blue Egg]]
** Dropped by cold chickens overtime.
** Has a chance to spawn baby cold chicken when thrown, regardless of biome they are in.
* [[Egg|Brown Egg]]
** Dropped by warm chickens overtime.
** Has a chance to spawn baby warm chicken when thrown, regardless of biome they are in.

=== [[Mob]]s ===
* [[Pig]]s, [[Cow]]s and [[Chicken]]s
** Added warm and cold variants.
*** The pre-existing variant before this update is now temperate.
* [[Wolf|Wolves]]
** Added 6 new wolf audio variants.

=== Structures ===
* Added [[Trees|fallen trees]] similar to Bedrock Edition, with birch trees having a long and short variant

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Lodestone]]
** Can now be crafted with an [[Iron Ingot]] surrounded by 8 chiseled stone bricks, making it renewable.
** Can now be found in ruined portal chests.
* [[Leaves]]
** All leaf blocks now have leaf particles dropping from them, similar to the cherry and pale oak trees.
* [[Beacon]]
** Beacon beams are now bigger from further away to make them more visible
* [[Block of Iron]]
** Changed the sounds of the Block of Iron to more low-pitched metal sounds.
* [[Dead Bush|Dry Bush]]
** Emits ambient sounds when placed on [[sand]] or [[Terracotta]].

=== Items ===
[[File:Creeper Spawn Egg New.png|thumb|100px|The newly redesigned [[Creeper]] Spawn Egg]]
* [[Spawn Egg]]s
** Changed the texture of all spawn eggs (except the [[NPC]] Spawn Egg).
** Each spawn egg now has their own unique visual which captures the personality and character of every mob it spawns.
* [[Bundle]]s
** Can now be found in [[village]]s.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Donkey]]
** Now spawns in savannas.
* [[Camel]]s
** Now spawns in deserts.
