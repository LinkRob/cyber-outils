'''Netherite''' is a material from [[the Nether]], used primarily to [[smithing table|upgrade]] [[diamond]] gear. Netherite items are more powerful and durable than diamond, and in dropped-item form are resistant to burning by [[fire]] or [[lava]]. Netherite blocks are also unbreakable by the strongest explosions from normal gameplay (such as [[TNT]]), with the exception of [[Wither#Wither Skull|blue wither skulls]]. It is also used as a blast shield in redstone contraptions due to its ability to block explosions while being able to be moved by [[piston]]s.

== Blocks and items ==
Many blocks and items make netherite, are made with netherite, are used to upgrade gear to netherite, or are directly 
stated to be netherite. They are:
* {{BlockLink|Ancient Debris}}: the blast-resistant ore form rarely found at low depths in the Nether or as loot in [[bastion remnant]]s, which may also contain netherite scraps and ingots.
* {{ItemLink|Netherite Scrap}}: an item obtained by [[smelting]] ancient debris, used to craft [[netherite ingot]]s.
* {{ItemLink|Netherite Ingot}}: an item obtained by crafting 4 netherite scraps with 4 [[gold ingot]]s, used for [[crafting]] and [[Smithing Table#Upgrading diamond gear to netherite gear|upgrading]] diamond gear to netherite.
* {{ItemLink|Netherite Upgrade}}: one of the 19 [[smithing template]]s, found as loot in [[bastion remnant]]s, that is used for upgrading diamond gear to netherite.
* {{BlockLink|Block of Netherite}}: the block form of netherite, crafted with 9 netherite ingots.

=== Joke feature ===
In snapshot [[20w14∞]], the [[swaggiest stairs ever]] were temporarily added as an April Fool's joke. It was the stair variant of the netherite block.
