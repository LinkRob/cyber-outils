'''v0.3.2 alpha''' was a minor update to '' [[Pocket Edition|Minecraft: Pocket Edition]] '' that was released on July 16, 2012.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20120717115310/http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/minecraft-pocket-edition/id479516143?mt=8</ref><ref>http://web.archive.org/web/20120803220009/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mojang.minecraftpe</ref> It added notable features such as [[furnace]]s, [[gold]], [[iron]], and [[diamond]] materials, as well as made other changes and fixes.<ref>{{link|url=https://www.mojang.com/2012/07/the-weekly-chunk-creepers-gonna-creep/|archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20190824070331/https://www.mojang.com/2012/07/the-weekly-chunk-creepers-gonna-creep/|title=The Weekly Chunk - Creepers Gonna Creep|date=July 18, 2012}} (Note: 1. and 2. are about MineCon, 3. is about MCPE)</ref>

v0.3.1 alpha does not seem to exist; the version skipped from v0.3.0 straight to v0.3.2. The second reupload of v0.3.0 may have been retro-conned as v0.3.1, due to it adding features, rather than fixing crashes like most other reuploads.{{verify|type=change}}

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[Furnace]]s
* Storage blocks used for smelting of [[blocks]] and [[items]].
* Can be crafted with [[cobblestone]].
; [[Sapling]]s
* Non-solid [[blocks]] that can be grown into their respective [[tree]] type.
* Are occasionally dropped by [[leaves]] when they break or decay.
; [[Glass pane]]s
* Transparent [[blocks]] that can be used as a more efficient alternative to [[glass]] blocks.
* Can be crafted with [[glass]] blocks.
; [[Chest]]s
* Currently have no functionality.
* Are not yet obtainable.
* Cannot make double chests yet.

=== Items ===
; [[Gold ingot]]s
* Obtained by [[smelting]] [[gold ore]].
* Can be crafted into [[blocks of gold]] or [[tools|golden tools]].
; [[Iron ingot]]s
* Obtained by [[smelting]] [[iron ore]].
* Can be crafted into [[blocks of iron]], [[shears]], or [[tools|iron tools]].
; [[Diamond]]s
* Obtained by mining [[diamond ore]] with an [[iron pickaxe]] or stronger.
* Can be crafted into [[blocks of diamond]] or [[tools|diamond tools]].
; Diamond, gold, and iron [[pickaxe]]s, [[shovel]]s, [[axe]]s, and [[sword]]s

=== Gameplay ===
; [[Smelting]]
* A method of producing refined goods.
* Done via a [[furnace]].

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[Leaves]]
* Now occasionally drop [[saplings]] when broken.
; [[Clay]]
* Now drops 4 [[clay balls]] when broken.
; [[Lapis lazuli block]]s
* Can now be crafted into [[lapis lazuli]].

=== Items ===
; [[Brick]]s
* Can now be crafted into [[brick block]]s.
; [[Clay ball]]s
* Can now be crafted into [[clay]].
; [[Lapis lazuli]]
* Can now be crafted into [[Lapis Lazuli Block|lapis lazuli blocks]].

=== Gameplay ===
; [[Survival]]
* Every available block and item can now be gathered, crafted, or [[smelting|smelted]] into.
* The player no longer starts out with [[shears]], [[bricks]], and [[glass]] in infinite stacks.
; [[Creative inventory]] 
* Additions:
** [[Cobblestone slab]]
** [[Glass pane]]
** [[Spruce leaves]]
** [[Birch leaves]]
** [[Lapis lazuli block]]
** [[Furnace]]
** [[Coal ore]]
** [[Iron ore]]
** [[Gold ore]]
** [[Diamond ore]]
** [[Lapis lazuli ore]]

== Bugs ==

* When equipping leaves in creative mode, the block will occasionally display a glitched crafting table texture

== Fixes ==

;5 bugs fixed
* Less flickering/Z-fighting on some devices.
* Having one of an item or block no longer displays the "1".
* Mobs have correct health.
* Fixed rendering of fence gate and half block in hand.
* Spruce and birch leaves no longer drop a glitched block when cut by shears.
