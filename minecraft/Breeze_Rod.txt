'''Breeze rods''' are items exclusively obtained from the [[breeze]], which is found in the [[trial chambers]]. They can be crafted into 4 [[wind charge]]s, crafted into the [[mace]] with the [[heavy core]] as well as repairing the [[mace]] in an [[anvil]], duplicating the [[flow armor trim]], and brewed into a [[potion of Wind Charging]].

==Drops==

Breezes will drop 1-3 breeze rods without looting, 2-4 rods with looting I, 3-6 rods with looting II, and 5-7 rods with looting III. Breeze rods also have a 11.5% chance to drop from regular [[vault]]s and a 5.5% chance to drop from [[ominous vault]]s.
                       
==Usage==

===Crafting===
Breeze rods are used to craft 4 [[wind charge]]s, the mace using the [[heavy core]], and the [[flow armor trim]].
