The '''lab table''' is a [[block]] used in [[wikipedia:chemistry|chemistry]] to design one's own experiments by combining substances and observing the results.

== Obtaining ==
Lab tables cannot be obtained in [[Survival]] without [[commands]]. {{IN|bedrock}}, the world must also have "Education Edition" enabled.

=== Breaking ===
A lab table can be mined using a [[pickaxe]]. Mining without a pickaxe is slow, but the lab table still drops as an item.

{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Lab Table|pickaxe|wooden|drop=∞}}

== Usage ==
{{control|Using}} the lab table opens a 1×9 grid, where [[element]]s and [[compound]]s can be inserted to perform experiments. The locations of the items in the grid do not affect the outcome. When ready to conduct an experiment, clicking the {{btn|Combine|be=1}} button initiates the process.

[[File:LabTableBeaker.gif|thumb|Beaker]]
[[File:LabTableFlask.gif|thumb|Flask]]
[[File:LabTableJar.gif|thumb|Jar]]
If the result is a viable product, the top images animate to indicate whether a liquid, gas, or solid has been produced. Shortly after, the menu closes, and the successful product appears on the table. If the combined materials did not create a viable product, the process results in a garbage item and one of few animations involving fire and/or explosions. {{IN|bedrock}}, the table sometimes catches fire when [[garbage]] is made.
