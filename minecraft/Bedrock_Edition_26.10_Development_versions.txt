= 26.10.20 =

*** {{EntityLink|Axolotl}}
*** {{EntityLink|Dolphin}}
*** {{EntityLink|Donkey}}
*** {{EntityLink|Glow Squid}}
*** {{EntityLink|Horse}}
*** {{EntityLink|Mule}}
*** {{EntityLink|Skeleton Horse}}
*** {{EntityLink|Squid}}
*** {{EntityLink|Turtle}}
*** {{EntityLink|Zombie Horse}}
** [[Chick]]s and baby [[horse]]s now has a new sound.
** [[Saddle]]s on baby equines will no longer render.
* Remodeled [[rabbit]]
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.

= 26.10.21 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.10.21''' is the second beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.10]], released on February 3, 2026.

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Golden Dandelion]]
** A new decoration block which can be planted on [[flower pot]]s like other flowers does.
** Can be used to feed most baby mobs.
** Baby mobs will stop aging when fed on.
*** It will age again when fed on again.
** Unlike other flowers:
*** Are not compostable.
*** Does not attract [[bees]].
*** Are not pollinated by bees.

== Changes ==
=== Mobs ===
* Baby mobs
** More baby mobs have remodeled the texture:
*** {{EntityLink|Armadillo}}
*** {{EntityLink|Bee}}
*** {{EntityLink|Camel}}
*** {{EntityLink|Fox}}
*** {{EntityLink|Goat}}
*** {{EntityLink|Llama}}
*** {{EntityLink|Polar Bear}}
*** {{EntityLink|Trader Llama}}
** [[Saddle]]s and [[carpet]]s on baby camels and [[llama]]s respectively will no longer render.

= 26.10.23 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.10.23''' is the third beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.10]], released on February 11, 2026.

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Note Block]]
** Added a new trumpet sound.
*** Plays when placed on top of a [[copper]] block.
*** Sound differs based on oxidation stage.
* [[Golden Dandelion]]
** Can now be crafted with a [[dandelion]] surrounded by 8 [[Gold Nugget|gold nuggets]].
** Can now be used to craft [[Yellow Dye|yellow dyes]] and [[Suspicious Stew|suspicious stews]].
** Can now be sold by [[Wandering Trader|wandering traders]] for 2 [[emeralds]] as part of their regular trade.

=== Mobs ===
* Baby mobs
** More baby mobs have remodeled the texture:
*** {{EntityLink|Drowned}}
*** {{EntityLink|Husk}}
*** {{EntityLink|Piglin}}
*** {{EntityLink|Villager}}
*** {{EntityLink|Zombie}}
*** {{EntityLink|Zombie Villager}}
*** {{EntityLink|Zombified Piglin}}
** Baby drowneds are now called gurgles.

= 26.10.24 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.10.24''' is the fourth beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.10]], released on February 17, 2026.

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Stonecutter]]s
** [[Deepslate]] and [[stone]] blocks can now be crafted directly into their respective variants.
* [[Furnace]]s
** Added its own recipe book.
** Only available under "Furnace Recipe Book" experimental toggle.
* [[Golden Dandelion]]s
** Now display green particles when fed on baby mobs.
*** Particles moving downwards indicate that aging is paused.
*** Particles moving upwards indicate that aging is resumed.

=== Mobs ===
* Baby mobs
** More baby mobs have remodeled the texture:
*** {{EntityLink|Hoglin}}
*** {{EntityLink|Panda}}
*** {{EntityLink|Sniffer}}
*** {{EntityLink|Strider}}
*** {{EntityLink|Zoglin}}
* Adult [[cat]]s, [[chicken]]s, [[cow]]s, and [[pig]]s
** Added new sound variants.
*** Cats now have a new "royal" sound.
*** Chickens now have a new "picky" sound.
*** Cows now have a new "moody" sound.
*** Pigs now have new "mini" and "big" sounds.
