A '''block sound type''' is a set of sounds that blocks use when they are broken, placed, hit, fallen on, stepped on, jumped from,{{only|bedrock}} or landed on.{{only|bedrock}}

If a player is stepping on a carpet, crimson or warped roots, moss carpet, nether sprouts, or snow (blocks in the {{cd|combination_step_sound_blocks}} [[block tag]]) then the step sound of the block underneath will play at {{frac|1|3}} of the volume and {{frac|4|5}} of the pitch.{{Only|java}}
