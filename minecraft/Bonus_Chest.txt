The '''bonus chest''' is a feature that appears near the player's spawn if the "Bonus Chest" option is toggled on the "[[Create New World]]" screen. It generates with a semi-random collection of basic items to help the player survive early on and gather necessary resources, including tools, blocks, and food.

== Generation ==
If the "Bonus Chest" option is set to "ON" in the "More World Options..." {{in|java}} or "Game Settings" {{in|bedrock}} part of the "[[Create New World]]" screen, a single bonus chest is generated in the world, no matter how many players there are. It appears somewhere near the player's initial spawn point, with up to four [[torch]]es generated around it on adjacent blocks.

The Bonus Chest option is not available in [[hardcore]] mode.

{{IN|java}}, on [[servers]], the bonus chest can be enabled by running the server with the {{code|--bonusChest}} argument.

== Construction ==
Bonus chests contain a single chest surrounded by four [[torches]]. The torches may be overridden if the torch locations are obstructed by other blocks such as uneven ground, trees, or leaves, or they may instead float.
