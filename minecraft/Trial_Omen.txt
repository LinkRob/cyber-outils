'''Trial Omen''' is an effect that starts when a player with [[Bad Omen]] enters in the [[trial chambers]]. It converts [[trial spawner]]s into [[ominous trial spawner]]s, which allows them to drop [[ominous trial key]]s after ominous trial events are completed.

The ominous trial spawners do not affect [[breeze]]s and [[cave spider]]s; but it guarantees [[husk]]s and baby [[zombie]]s an iron [[sword]] that can upgrade to [[diamond]]. [[Zombie (disambiguation)|Zombies variants]] (husks and normal zombies) and [[Skeleton (disambiguation)|skeletons]] ([[stray]]s, [[skeleton]]s and [[bogged]]s) may spawn wearing [[chainmail]], [[iron]] and [[diamond]] armor that has Bolt or Flow [[Smithing Template|armor trims]]s with [[copper]] material. They can sometimes be enchanted. Skeletons' [[bow]]s can also sometimes be enchanted as well.
