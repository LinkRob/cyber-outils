==Upscaling==
Boosts frame rates on GeForce RTX graphics cards with NVIDIA DLSS 2.0. It can be used for [[Ray Tracing]] to smoothen out gameplay.

=== Uses ===
This feature is used for smoothening out gameplay. It can be used if you are using [[Ray Tracing]] and you are lagging.
It improves performance by rendering the game at a lower native resolution and then results in a smoother gameplay.
