{{History|java beta}}
{{History||1.7|Piston heads have an unobtainable item form corresponding to its block ID. It can be obtained via inventory editors with numeric item ID 34.
|The moving piston has an unobtainable item form corresponding to its block ID. It can be obtained via inventory editors with numeric item ID 36.
|Both blocks do not appear to be able to be placed.
|If certain colors of [[cloth]] were obtained in a world prior to their mass removal in late Infdev, items of these could convert forward into piston head or moving piston items.}}
{{History||1.8|snap=Pre-release 2 ;)|Added pick block functionality. This changes the currently selected slot to any containing these otherwise-unobtainable items, but does not allow them to be obtained if not already in the hotbar.}}
{{History|java}}
{{History||1.2.5|snap=pre|Piston heads and moving pistons can now be obtained via Pick Block in creative.}}
{{History||1.3.1|snap=12w15a|Using pick block on a piston head or moving piston now causes a game crash.}}
{{History|||snap=12w16a|Piston head and moving piston items can now be obtained in singleplayer worlds via the {{cmd|give}} command using their respective numeric IDs.
|Piston heads and moving pistons can no longer be obtained with pick block in Creative.}}
{{History||1.7.2|snap=13w37a|The direct item forms of piston heads and moving pistons have been removed from the game. They can no longer exist as an item in any way, only as placed blocks.}}
{{History|pocket edition alpha}}
{{History||v0.15.0|Both the "moving piston" and "piston head" items have been added. The "piston head" item has an ID of 34 and the "moving piston" item has an ID of 250.}}
{{History|bedrock}}
{{History||1.18.30|snap=beta 1.18.30.26|The ids of sticky piston heads and piston heads are now changed. For backward compatibility, their original IDs ({{cd|stickypistonarmcollision}} and {{cd|pistonarmcollision}}) are registries as their alias IDs, which can be obtained with {{cmd|give}} command.
|Moving block's ID is also changed, but it still cannot be obtained by {{cmd|give}}.}}
{{History|foot}}
