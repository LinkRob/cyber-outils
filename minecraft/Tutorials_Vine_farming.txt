'''Vine farming''' is a way to harvest large quantities of [[vine]]s. Since vines can only be obtained when using a [[shear]], you should be [[Tutorials/Iron_golem_farming|farming some iron]] too if you really need a lot of vines.

==Designs==
===Niondil's design===
Pretty easy design, all you have to do is hold down the attack button and you'll get vines.

Uses water to move the player and the vines dropped.
