A '''cherry grove''' is a [[mountain]] biome, very similar to [[meadow]]s. The primary feature of cherry groves are [[Cherry|cherry trees]], which generate exclusively in this biome.

== Description ==
The cherry grove has the same shape as meadows, and the only difference is the vegetation and that cherry groves are rarer than meadows. Just like meadows, [[emerald ore]]s can be found here, especially at higher altitudes, and both [[Coal ore|coal]] and [[iron ore|iron]] ores are commonly found here. [[Infested block]] [[Ore (feature)|blobs]] also naturally generate in cherry groves. [[Village]]s cannot generate here, but {{in|java}}, [[pillager outposts]] can. {{IN|java}}, unlike most overworld biomes, [[sugar cane]]s and [[pumpkin]] patches cannot generate here. This biome usually generates in the lower sides of some mountains but can also generate standalone in plateaus, mainly next to [[plain]]s or [https://minecraft.fandom.com/wiki/Plains#Sunflower_Plains sunflower plains] and separated from [[meadow]]s by [[river]]s.[[File:Cherry Blossom Grove.jpg|thumb|A plains [[village]] near to a cherry grove.]]

Cherry groves are grasslands with [[grass]], [[tall grass]] and [[pink petals]]. The main environmental feature of the cherry grove are cherry [[tree]]s identified by their striking pink leaves. Their leaves drop petal [[particles]]. [[Bee nest]]s with [[bee]]s generate somewhat commonly on the side of the cherry trees. The cherry trees can generate densely enough to create a cover of leaves.

Like forests, cherry groves are one of the most preferred biomes to start a [[Survival]] mode world. The abundance of passive mobs and wood make this the easiest of the seven mountain biomes. The sparseness of trees can help the player not to get lost while collecting unique resources. 

While using [[bone meal]] on [[grass block]]s in this biome, pink petals generate on the ground in place of small flowers.
