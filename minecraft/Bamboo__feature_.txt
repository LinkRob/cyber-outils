A '''bamboo''' is a [[feature]] that consist of [[bamboo]] blocks, found throughout [[jungle]] biomes.

== Generation ==
A bamboo is a feature that represents a bamboo plant, made of [[bamboo]] blocks. They can be found in the Jungle, Sparse Jungle‌{{only|be}} and Bamboo Jungle [[biome]]s at the surface. They're less common in Jungle and Sparse Jungle‌{{only|be|short=1}} biomes.

In Jungle and Sparse Jungle‌{{only|be|short=1}}, there's no podzol under the bamboo, while in Bamboo Jungle, podzol sometimes generates.

Bamboos' height varies between 6 and 17 blocks, and if there's an obstacle preventing growth, it may be even shorter.
