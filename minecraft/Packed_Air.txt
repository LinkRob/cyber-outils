'''Packed Air''' is a joke block from [[Java Edition 23w13a_or_b]].

== Obtaining ==
'''Packed Air''' can only be obtained after voting for the rule <code>air_blocks</code>.

==Usage==
Packed air can permanently fills the player's breath bar when standing in it.

It can only be placed in closed rooms on [[the moon]] otherwise it disappears like water in the nether when placing it in an open space on the moon, anywhere in another dimension or when the closed room is opened.

There is a maximum size of room that can be filled with only one packed air, which is 108 blocks³
