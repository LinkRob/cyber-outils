'''Build 0016''' was a [[developer version]] of {{edition|xbox 360}} showcased at [[MineCon 2011]].<ref>[https://www.twitch.tv/videos/262369260 Minecraft: Marketplace Showcase] – Minecraft on Twitch</ref><ref>{{ytl|CFmXVjaFdCU|Extended Xbox 360 Minecraft playthrough|CurseNetwork|November 29, 2011}}</ref> This version is based off of [[Java Edition Beta 1.6.6]].

== Features ==
* All features from and before [[Java Edition Beta 1.6.6]].
* Console-exclusive controls and menus.
* Includes a [[tutorial world]].

== Trivia ==
* This is the first ever version of Minecraft to be shown on the Xbox 360.
