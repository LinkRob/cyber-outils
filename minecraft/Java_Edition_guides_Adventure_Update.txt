== Gameplay ==
=== New ===
{|{{Simple Guide table|New Gameplay}}
|-
| [[Creative Mode]] ||A new gamemode in which the player can fly, cannot take damage, can break any block instantly, and has an inventory containing an infinite supply of most blocks and items.
|-
| [[Flying]] ||A new ability available in creative mode, activated by double-tapping the jump key.
|-
| [[Sprinting]] ||Double-tapping the forward key allows you to move faster at the cost of losing [[hunger]].
|-
| [[Hunger]] ||Food no longer instantly heals, but instead fills up a hunger bar. Sprinting and regenerating health drains hunger.
|-
| [[Experience]] ||Killing mobs gives you experience points which can be used in enchanting.
|-
| [[Enchanting]] ||Using an enchanting table you can spend experience points to apply enchantments to items.
|-
| [[Brewing]] ||Using a brewing stand you can brew ingredients into water bottles to create potions.
|}
