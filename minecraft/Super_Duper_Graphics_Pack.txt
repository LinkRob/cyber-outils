The '''Super Duper Graphics Pack''' is a canceled graphics upgrade that was formerly in development for [[Bedrock Edition]]. It was announced at E3 2017,<ref>{{Mcnet|all-news-e3|All The News From E3!|June 11, 2017|Tom Stone}}</ref><ref>{{Mcnet|whats-super-duper-graphics-pack|What’s The Super Duper Graphics Pack?|June 15, 2017|Marsh Davies}}</ref><ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170615143116/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1WHYJH-JGk|title=Super Duper Graphics Pack DLC Sneak Peek|website=YouTube|author=TeamMojang|date=June 15, 2017}}</ref>  received further promotion at [[MINECON Earth 2017]],<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171119101442/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Od3lMFZIsE4|title=The Super Duper Minecraft Musical!|website=YouTube|author=TeamMojang|date=November 18, 2017}}</ref> was delayed due to problems with the graphics engine and performance issues,<ref name=sdgp>{{citation|url=https://www.windowscentral.com/minecraft-super-duper-graphics-pack|title=Minecraft Super Duper 4K graphics pack: Delay, pricing, and more (update)|website=Windows Central|author=Jez Corden|date=August 12, 2019}}</ref> and was ultimately canceled in 2019.<ref>{{Mcnet|super-duper-graphics-pack-ceasing-development|Super Duper Graphics Pack Ceasing Development|August 12, 2019|Staff}}</ref>

The pack was intended to add dynamic shadows, lighting that streams through fog, movement in leaves, grass and other foliage, new textures for blocks, items, mobs and villagers, directional lighting, edge highlighting, specular maps, additional 3D geometry, emissive maps, tone mapping, dynamic exposure, increased draw distance, parallax occlusion mapping, and other graphical improvements. It would have cost around $10.00 USD.<ref name=sdgp/>
