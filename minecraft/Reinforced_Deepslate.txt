'''Reinforced deepslate''' is a [[block]] that generates exclusively in [[Ancient City|ancient cities]]. It has high [[hardness]], is immune to [[explosion]]s, and cannot be obtained in [[Survival]] mode.

== Obtaining ==
Reinforced deepslate is not obtainable in Survival, even with [[Silk Touch]], and is only available through the Creative inventory or commands. It cannot be pushed by a [[piston]], is highly blast resistant, has higher hardness than [[obsidian]], and cannot be destroyed by the [[wither]] or the [[Ender Dragon|ender dragon]].

Reinforced deepslate has no tool associated with it, meaning it is mined at the exact same speed regardless of the tool and level of efficiency.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Reinforced Deepslate|drop=0}}

=== Natural generation ===
56 blocks of reinforced deepslate are found in the central part of each [[Ancient City|ancient city]], creating a portal-like frame structure.
