'''Piercing''' is an [[enchantment]] applied to a [[crossbow]] that causes arrows to pierce through entities.

== Usage ==
Arrows shot from a Piercing [[crossbow]] can pass through the number of entities equal to the enchantment level (for a total of {{math|level+1}} entities damaged). The shot arrows can still be retrieved after piercing a [[mob]], allowing for infinite use of all arrow types, including spectral arrows and [[tipped arrows]]. Piercing ignores shields even when they are blocking. Arrows do not change direction upon piercing a mob, meaning they hit only mobs in the line of flight.

Piercing has no effect on [[firework rocket]]s fired from a crossbow.

If [[commands]] are used to increase the enchantment level beyond 127, the piercing effect will stop working and will hit only one mob instead.

== Incompatibilities ==
Piercing and [[Multishot]] are mutually exclusive. However, if combined using [[commands]], both enchantments function as normal, with the two arrows added by the Multishot enchantment getting the effect as well.
