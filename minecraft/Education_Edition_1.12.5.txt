'''1.12.5''', dubbed the '''"Library Update"''', was a minor update to {{el|ee}} released on November 12, 2019.<ref name=release>{{cite|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191212034004/https://minecrafteducation.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035255232-What-s-New-The-Library-Release-Version-1-12-5-|title=What's New: The Library Release (Version 1.12.5)|website=Minecraft: Education Edition Support}}</ref><ref>https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/blog/the-library-update-is-now-available-for-minecraft-education-edition</ref>

== Additions ==
=== General ===
;Library
*The [[Library]] menu has been updated based on user feedback to bring lesson content in-game, providing lesson plans and categories to simplify navigating the library of lessons, worlds, build challenges, and tutorials.

;Demo Lesson
*Added a demo lesson for users without an Office 365 Education account. Users with an Office 365 Education account can access the world used in the demo lesson from the world library.
