'''Copper ore''' is a [[ore|mineral]] [[block]] found underground.

'''Deepslate copper ore''' is the [[deepslate]] variant of copper ore, usually found deeper underground.

== Obtaining ==

=== Natural generation ===
{{see also|Ore (feature)}}
Copper ore can generate anywhere on the [[Overworld]] in the form of [[ore (feature)|ore blob]]s.  Copper ore generates in two batches. The first batch generates anywhere on the Overworld biomes except [[dripstone caves]] 16 times per chunks in groups from 0 to 16, in layer -16 to 112, being most common in layers 47 and 48 and less common at the ends. The second batch generates only in dripstone caves 16 times per chunks in groups from 0 to 52, in layer -16 to 112, being most common in layers 47 and 48 and less common at the ends.

Copper ore can replace [[stone]], [[andesite]], [[diorite]], [[granite]], [[tuff]]{{only|je|short=1}}, and [[deepslate]]. Copper ore blocks that replace tuff or deepslate become deepslate copper ore. Copper ore can also spawn in rare [[ore vein]]s above Y=0, mixed with granite and the occasional [[Block of Raw Copper|blocks of raw copper]]. In [[dripstone caves]] biomes, copper ore veins are larger on average.

=== Breaking ===
Copper ore must be mined using a stone [[pickaxe]] or greater. If mined with any other tool or by hand, it drops nothing.

Breaking copper ore drops 2-5 [[raw copper]], unless mined using a [[Silk Touch]] pickaxe. It is affected by [[Fortune]] enchantment, dropping up to 20 raw copper, or 7.7 raw copper on average with Fortune III.

== Usage ==
Copper ore can be smelted to obtain [[copper ingot]]s, which are crafting ingredients for [[copper block]]s, [[spyglass]]es, and [[lightning rod]]s. Copper ore does not weather like its block counterpart, though the design of the ore itself shows some oxidization.
