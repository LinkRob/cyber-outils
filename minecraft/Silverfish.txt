'''Silverfish''' are small [[hostile mob]]s that [[Infested Block|infest stone and deepslate blocks]] in [[mountains]], [[stronghold]]s, [[igloo]] basements and [[woodland mansion]]s. They crawl at their targets to bite them, and attacking them will incite other nearby silverfish to emerge from their blocks and attack the aggressor.

==Spawning==
Silverfish spawn from broken [[infested block]]s, which generate in [[stronghold]]s, underground in [[mountains]] and [[windswept hills]] [[biome]]s, in [[igloo]] basements and in [[woodland mansion]] false portal rooms. Silverfish do not appear if the block is broken with the [[Silk Touch]] enchantment.<ref>{{bug|MC-48184|||WAI}}</ref>

Silverfish [[spawner]]s naturally generate in [[end portal]] rooms in [[stronghold]]s. Silverfish can spawn from spawners only at [[light level]] 11 or lower, or on stone types they can enter (stone, cobblestone, stone bricks, etc.) at any light level, and cannot spawn within a 5 block distance of any player{{verify}}. This is the only renewable source of silverfish and infested blocks.

1-3 silverfish have a 5% chance of spawning from an entity affected by the [[Infested]] potion effect when the affected entity is hurt.

==Drops==
Silverfish have no drops other than {{xp|5}} [[experience]] points when killed by a player or [[tamed wolf]].

==Behavior==
[[File:Silverfish infest anim.gif|alt=|thumb|right|A silverfish being freed from an infested block, which calls for help from other silverfish; causing them to emerge from more infested blocks.]]
Silverfish attack [[player]]s, [[iron golem]]s, [[snow golem]]s and call other silverfish in the area upon being hit. Silverfish pathfind toward the player, and can see the player through walls. 

When they suffer [[Poison]] damage or damage inflicted by the player and survive, they cause other silverfish within a 21×11×21 area to break out of their infested blocks. The player can prevent other silverfish from appearing by killing them in one shot.<ref>{{bug|MC-2432|||WAI}}</ref> If the game rule {{cd|doTileDrops}} is set to {{cd|false}}, the infested blocks are broken, but no silverfish spawn from them.

Silverfish, being [[arthropod]]s, take extra damage from the [[Bane of Arthropods]] enchantment, and receive [[Slowness]] IV upon being hit.

When idle, silverfish enter a nearby [[deepslate]], [[stone]], [[cobblestone]], [[stone bricks]], mossy stone bricks, cracked stone bricks or chiseled stone bricks block, transforming it into the respective [[infested block]] if the [[game rule]] {{cd|mobGriefing}} is set to {{cd|true}}, as per default. They cannot infest [[mossy cobblestone]],<ref>{{bug|MC-167357}}</ref> despite being capable of infesting both mossy blocks and cobblestone, and they also cannot infest [[slab]] and [[stairs]] of these blocks.<ref>{{bug|MC-167372}}</ref> They are also unable to infest [[andesite]], [[diorite]], [[granite]], or [[smooth stone]].<ref>{{bug|MC-189974}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*In real life, [[wikipedia:Silverfish|silverfish]] are harmless small and wingless insects that are often found in dark areas in buildings and the wooden pilings of seaside piers and trees. They are infamous for damaging paper-based products.
**This also means silverfish are the only hostile mob to be based on a real life animal.
*Silverfish are one of the only hostile mobs that are not imaginary.
*Silverfish appeared in the [[Java Edition 2.0|April Fools 2.0 update]] as the [[redstone bug]].
