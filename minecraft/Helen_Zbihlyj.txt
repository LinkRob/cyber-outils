'''Helen Zbihlyj''', also known as '''HelenAngel''', is a former community manager for [[Minecraft]] at [[Mojang Studios]], the first person in the company with that title. She is now the Director of Community for [[Minecraft]] at ReWrite Media & Jigarbov Productions.<ref name="talk comment">[[Special:Diff/1894112|Wiki edit #1894112]] by HelenAngel</ref>

== Personal life ==

Helen graduated from [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germantown_High_School_(Tennessee) Germantown High School]. There she was heavily involved in the [http://ppp.org award-winning theatre & television] program.<ref name="talk comment"/>

She graduated from [http://memphis.edu the University of Memphis] with a BA in psychology.<ref name="talk comment"/>

Her son, Nicholas, was born in November of 2000. She married Sneeza, the creator of TF2 Outpost, in June 2016. They live near Seattle, WA.<ref name="talk comment"/>

== Professional life ==

In the ''Minecraft'' community, Helen started as an active player and an occasional Minecraft Wiki contributor.

She joined Xbox Game Studios in November 2013 as Community Manager for the Windows 8 games portfolio, including titles such as ''Microsoft Solitaire Collection'', ''Microsoft Bingo'', ''Disney Fairies: Hidden Treasures'', and ''Hexic''. She assisted with community management of ''Minecraft: Xbox 360 Edition'' and later community management of the rest of the ''Minecraft: Console Edition'' platforms.<ref>{{citation|url=https://www.mobygames.com/developer/sheet/view/developerId,621974/|title=Developer Profile Helen Casper|website=MobyGames|date=March 27, 2021}}</ref>

She officially joined Mojang Studios full-time in July 2015 to provide community management for the Minecraft franchise.<ref name="talk comment"/>

She is no longer part of Mojang starting August 27, 2020.<ref>{{tweet|HelenAngel|1299100396170878976|I'm no longer with Mojang Studios. I have decided it's time I FINALLY pursue something that I have been passionate about for over 10 years: directly helping Minecraft communities!|August 27, 2020}}</ref> She continues to work with the Minecraft community through her role as Director of Community at ReWrite Media & Jigarbov Productions, two Minecraft Marketplace Partner studios.<ref>{{citation|url=https://www.linkedin.com/in/helenangel|title=Helen Zbihlyj|website=LinkedIn|date=March 27, 2021}}</ref>
