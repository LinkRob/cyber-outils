A '''potent sulfur''' is an upcoming [[Sulfur Block|sulfur]] variant that will soon be added in [[Chaos Cubed]]{{Upcoming}}. It creates noxious gas when placed in and on top of water, inflicting [[player]]s and [[mob]]s [[nausea]] for 3 seconds.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Like any [[stone]]-related blocks, potent sulfur blocks can only be mined with any [[pickaxe]], otherwise, it drops nothing.

=== Natural generation ===
Potent sulfur blocks naturally generate in [[Sulfur Cave|sulfur cave]]'s sulfur pools and in [[feature|sulfur spring]]s.

== Usage ==
=== Noxious gas ===
Potent sulfur blocks emit bubble column gas with yellow gas particles above the water. Players and mobs who get too close to or enter the water above the block will get inflicted with [[nausea]] for 3 seconds.

=== Geysers ===
{{In development|java|bedrock|26.2|26.30}}
[[File:Sulfur Geyser.png|thumb|400px|A sulfur geyser, created by placing magma block under potent sulfur.]]
Geysers are created when a potent sulfur block has a [[magma block]] underneath. it creates an eruption, with the eruption's frequency and duration, and the geyser's size depends on how many water-source blocks placed above the potent sulfur.

Geysers only naturally generate in sulfur springs with potent sulfur on it, with magma block underneath. Geysers does not naturally generate in [[sulfur caves]]'s sulfur pools, as sulfur pools only uses potent sulfur.
