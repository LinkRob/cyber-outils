'''Banner patterns''' are a set of 10 [[item]]s used to customize [[banner]]s inside [[loom]]s.

== Obtaining ==
[[File:Banner Patterns 20w15a.png|thumb|All six banner patterns {{in|java}} before 1.21 with their various sources. (To be Updated)]]

=== Crafting ===
{{see also|Banner/Patterns|title1=List of patterned banners}}
6 out of 10 banner patterns can be obtained by crafting. They are crafted by combining one [[paper]] along with a certain material.

=== Trading ===
'''Globe banner pattern''' is obtained by trading with a master-level [[Trading#Cartographer|cartographer villager]] for a price of 8 emeralds.

=== Chest loot ===
'''Snout banner pattern''' is obtained in the [[bastion remnant]] as loot.
{{LootChestItem|snout-banner-pattern}}

'''Flow''' and '''guster banner patterns''' are obtained in the [[trial chambers]] as loot from different [[vault]]s:

* Flow Banner pattern from an [[ominous vault]] (4.2%)
* Guster Banner pattern from a vault (15%)

== Usage ==
=== Loom ingredient ===
Banner patterns are used in [[loom]]s to add customization to [[banner]]s. The pattern must be combined with 1 banner and 1 [[dye]]. Upon usage in the loom, the banner pattern is not consumed.
