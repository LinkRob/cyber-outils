'''Minecraft Indev''' refers to 10 versions of '''[[Indev]]''' released from [[Indev (February 6, 2010)|February 6]] to [[Indev (February 23, 2010)|February 23, 2010]].

== Additions ==

=== General ===
;[[Daylight cycle]]
*The sun and moon move across the sky.
;[[Farming]]
*The player can plant [[seeds]] which can be grown into [[wheat]] and crafted into [[bread]].
;Third-person view
*Toggled by pressing {{key|F5}}.

=== Blocks ===
;[[Farmland]]
*Can have crops placed on it.
;[[Furnace]]
*Used to smelt items.

=== Items ===
;[[Hoe]]s
*Used to till [[dirt]] into farmland.
;[[Bread]]
*Can be eaten.
*Crafted from wheat.
;[[Seeds]]
*Can be placed on farmland.
*Grow into wheat.
;[[Wheat]]
*Can be crafted into bread.
;[[Armor]]
*Can be worn.
*Used for protection.

=== Non-mob entities ===
;[[Painting]]s

=== World generation ===
;[[Map theme]]s

[[File:Indev Hell Theme.png|alt=Hell map theme. No grass, the ocean is lava, always night.|thumb|251x251px|Hell map theme]]

* Normal - Generation from Indev

* Hell - Water from leftover land generation was replaced by lava.

*Paradise - Beaches expanded, flower was more common, and trees was less common with a bright sky.
*Woods - No flowers, trees generate commonly and the sky was dimmer.

== Changes ==

=== General ===
;[[Sound]]s
*Switched the sound system to use OpenAL.

=== Blocks ===
;[[Log]]
*Now drops itself instead of [[planks]].
;[[Cobblestone]]
*Smelts into stone when placed in a furnace.

=== Mobs ===
;[[Skeleton]]s
*Now burn in sunlight.
*Drop [[arrow]]s.
;[[Zombie]]s
*Now burn in sunlight.
*Drop [[feather]]s.
;[[Pig]]s
*Drop raw [[porkchop]]s.
;[[Creeper]]s
*Drop [[gunpowder]].
;[[Spider]]s
*Drop [[string]].
