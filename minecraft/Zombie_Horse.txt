A '''zombie horse''' is an [[undead]] variant of the regular [[horse]]. Zombie horses appear to be the horse equivalent of [[zombie]]s. Like zombies, zombie horses are green-skinned and their eyes are completely black.

== Spawning ==
Zombie horses spawn naturally in [[plains]], [[savanna]]s, [[snowy plains]], [[ice spikes]], etc. in the form of a [[zombie|zombie horseman]]  jockeys.

Zombie horses may be created by the {{cmd|summon}} command, with their spawn egg, or by naturally spawning as a zombie trap horse. When using a spawn egg, 20% spawn as foals.

Zombie horseman jockeys do not spawn in [[peaceful mode]], despite the zombie horse being passive. Zombie foals do not spawn naturally and can only be summoned via {{cmd|summon minecraft:zombie_horse ~ ~ ~ <nowiki>{IsBaby:1}</nowiki>}}.{{Only|java}}

== Drops ==
Upon death, zombie horses drop:
* 0-2 {{ItemLink|Rotten Flesh}}. The maximum amount is increased by 1 per level of [[Looting]], for a maximum of 0-5 with Looting III.
* {{xp|1|3}} when killed by a player or [[tamed wolf]].
If saddled, they drop their saddle. If worn armor, they drop their [[horse armor]].

Zombie foals yield neither items nor experience.

== Usage ==
{{see also|Transportation}}

Tamed and [[saddle]]d zombie horses can be used as one of the fastest means of [[transportation]] in the game. They can also be used to climb hills and jump fences.

{{IN|bedrock}}, they cannot be ridden.

=== Equipment ===
Zombie horses have two equipment slots.

* {{SlotSprite|Saddle}} Saddle slot: For equipping a [[saddle]].
* {{SlotSprite|Horse Armor}} Armor slot: For equipping [[horse armor]].

=== Riding ===
{{main|Riding}}

Once a zombie horse is tamed and saddled, the player can control it with directional controls, {{control|jump}}, and the mouse. The player dismounts using the {{control|dismount}} control. Like riding other entities, it is impossible for a player to use a [[nether portal]] or [[end portal]] while on a zombie horse.

A ridden zombie horse automatically runs up any one-block-high slope. The horse and rider can safely fit through a space as low as 2.75 blocks high. Lower clearance risks suffocating the rider if the rider's head enters a non-transparent block. The horse itself can enter gaps as low as 1.625 blocks high, but may itself take suffocation damage when clearance is less than 1.75 blocks. Horses cannot fit through a 1-block-wide gap.

Ridden saddled zombie horses have the ability of "jumping charging". When the player rides them, the experience bar on [[HUD]] is replaced by jumping charging bar. A ridden saddled zombie horses can be made to {{control|jump}} and holding the control charges for a higher leap. Zombie horses are not affected by jump boost beacons or potions.

Saddled zombie horses can float on water, without dismounting the player.

== Behavior ==
The zombie horse is an undead mob that is completely passive. Zombie horses behave like normal horses, roaming idly and occasionally stopping to rear, swish their tails, or lower their heads as though eating the grass. Unlike sheep, the eating animation does not actually cause any grass to be consumed.

Unlike other passive mobs, zombie horses slowly regenerate health. {{IN|bedrock}}, zombie horses will be killed by the [[Conduit]] like any regular hostile mob, despite being passive.

Zombie horses also burn during the daylight, despite being passive. However, [[horse armor]] can provide protection from the sunlight.

Zombie horses, in common with other undead mobs, take damage when affected by a [[Potion]] of Healing and are healed by a Potion of Harming. They are also affected by the [[Smite]] enchantment.

=== As an [[Undead]] Mob ===
Like any undead mobs:
* Are damaged by {{EffectLink|Instant Health}} and healed by {{EffectLink|Instant Damage}};
* Are not affected by {{EffectLink|Regeneration}} and {{EffectLink|Poison}};
* Burn in the sunlight.
* Take extra damage from [[Smite]] enchantment.
* Are not targeted by the {{EntityLink|wither}}.
* Causes an [[armadillo]] to hide in its shell.

Unlike most undead mobs, zombie horses are not targeted by [[iron golem]]s and [[snow golem]]s. However, zombie horseman riders are targeted.

=== Taming ===
Zombie horses can be tamed and ridden like many horse variants. They can be fed and healed with [[Mushroom|red mushrooms]]. 

==Appearance==
Zombie horses appear in a desaturated, mouldy-green color; however, they still share their previous distinctions. They also sometimes have bones protruding from beneath their flesh.

== Statistics ==
Zombie horses are spawned with constant health of 15, speed of 0.2, and have a jump strength ranging from 0.4–1.0, tending toward the average 0.7.

== Issues ==
Zombie Horses are an unsupported mob and as such issues relating to them may not be fixed.

== Trivia ==
* [[Mods/Mo' Creatures|Dr. Zhark]] appears in the credits after the [[End Poem]] as the creator of horses, including zombie horses.
* If a player pulling a zombie horse with a [[lead]] enters a [[boat]], the zombie horse follows by swimming at the same speed as the boat.
* A player riding a zombie horse is dragged along if the zombie horse is being pulled by a lead, and can even be lifted up in the air in the same way.
* The texture for zombie horses contains an unused saddlebag that has a different appearance from those of [[donkey]]s or [[mule]]s.
* Before [[Mounts of Mayhem]];
**Zombie horses were spawned via this {{cmd|summon}} command as they were only accessible in [[creative]] mode.
**Zombie horses weren't tameable. So a tamed zombie horse could only be summoned using {{cmd|summon minecraft:zombie_horse ~ ~ ~ <nowiki>{Tame:1b}</nowiki>}}.{{Only|java}}
* The old texture of the zombie horses shared a similar green color to zombies, with black eyes, mane, snout and hoofs. They sometimes have bones protruding from beneath their flesh.
