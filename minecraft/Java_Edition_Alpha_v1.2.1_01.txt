'''Alpha v1.2.1_01'''<ref name="hg"/><ref>{{ytl|94HcXdpcJlI|Minecraft Gameplay Episode 1 - The Basics|TheHomicidalTendency|November 5, 2010}}</ref> is a version of [[Alpha]] released on November 5, 2010, to fix a bug relating to armor.
This version is duplicated inside the [[Minecraft Launcher|launcher]], as attempting to play a version labeled "[[Alpha v1.2.1]]" will download and launch v1.2.1_01 instead.<ref>{{bug|MCL-9282}}</ref>

== Fixes ==
; 1 bug fixed
* Fixed armor not having an effect on the damage taken.</onlyinclude>
