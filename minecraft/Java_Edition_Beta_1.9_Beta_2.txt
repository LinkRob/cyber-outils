'''Java Edition Beta 1.9 Beta 2''' is the [[developer version]] of [[Java Edition Beta 1.9 Prerelease 2|Beta 1.9 Pre-release 2]], which was captured in one of the screenshots taken by Notch while testing [[Hardcore|Hardcore mode]].<ref>https://twitter.com/notch/status/119031614553587713</ref> Beta 1.9 was later renamed to 1.0.0 and released as a [[Java Edition 1.0.0|full release]].
