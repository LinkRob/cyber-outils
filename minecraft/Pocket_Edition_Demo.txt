'''Minecraft - Pocket Edition Demo'''<ref>{{ytl|jO-y5wzmK4E|Minecraft - Pocket Edition on Xperia Play|Minecraft|May 25, 2011}}</ref> was the first known developmental version of ''Minecraft: [[Pocket Edition]]''. This version was never publicly released, but was playable at the Electronic Entertainment Expo of 2011 and given to many individuals for private testing and showcasing.

=== General ===
* Inventory hotbar had a smaller look and could handle 9 items.
* Blocks in the item form use their own block texture (if there are several, then side texture).
* Block that was picked up by the player does not appear on the inventory hotbar.
* Only the items and blocks from the bottom is available on the full version.
