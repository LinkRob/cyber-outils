** {{nbt|int|AngerTime}}: Ticks until the mob becomes neutral. -2,147,483,648 to 0 for neutral mobs; 1 to 2,147,483,647 for angry mobs. Value depletes by one every tick if value is greater than 0. When the value turns from 1 to 0, the mob does not stop tracking the target until the target has exited the mob's detection radius. When hit by a player or when another mob of the same type within 32 blocks is hit by a player, the value is set to a random number between 400 and 800.
** {{nbt|int-array|AngryAt}}: The [[Universally Unique IDentifier]] of the entity that caused this mob to be angry, stored as four ints.
