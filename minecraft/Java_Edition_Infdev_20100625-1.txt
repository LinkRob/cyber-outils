A version of '''[[Minecraft Infdev]]''' was released on '''June 25, 2010'''.<ref>[[wordofnotch:734055276|I reverted back to the old save file format]] – The Word of Notch, June 25, 2010</ref>

== Removals ==
* Reverted the world-saving change from the previous build due to world size concerns.

== Bugs ==

*When the player breaks themselves out of a minecart, the speed of the player will be the speed of the broken minecart.
* While doing the first bug, the peaceful regeneration is equal to speeding up.
* Under certain conditions, the player can place minecarts infinitely.
* While doing the third bug the dropped minecart when picked up doesn't go in the player's inventory.
