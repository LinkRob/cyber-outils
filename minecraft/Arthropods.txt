[[File:Arthropod Mobs.png|thumb|Every arthropod in Minecraft.]]
The term '''arthropod''' refers to an in-game classification regrouping all [[Animal#Arthropods|bug]]s or [[Animal#Arthrodpods|bug]]-like [[mobs]].

== Classification ==
Arthropods take extra damage and receive [[Slowness|Slowness IV]] when hit with a weapon enchanted with [[Bane of Arthropods]] [[enchantment]].

All naturally hostile arthropods (that is, all except [[bee]]s) have a unique death animation in that they flip 180 degrees instead of the usual 90 degrees. This can prove useful when trying to find what mobs the Bane of Arthropods enchantment affects.
