'''Alpha v1.0.13_01'''<ref>{{ytl|0VWnQHS-ffs|Minecraft Tutorials - 01 - How to Survive your First Night|paulsoaresjr|July 29, 2010}}</ref> is a version released to fix a crash in [[Alpha v1.0.13]].

Alpha v1.0.13_01 was reuploaded to add new temporary logo "1K IN 24h" to celebrate 1000 sales in the last 24 hours.<ref>[[wordofnotch:875499109|I’m going all gaga now, 1000 sales in 24 hours!]] – The Word of Notch, July 29, 2010</ref><ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20230424051318/https://imgur.com/HVRgh 1K IN 24h title screenshot uploaded to Imgur on July 29, 2010]</ref><ref>{{ytl| TAZt4Mdpn74|Chronicles of Minecraft: Preview|ChazDragoon|July 29, 2010}}</ref>

== Fixes ==
; 1 bug fixed
* Fixed a crash that could occur near certain [[chunk]]s.

== ''1K IN 24h'' edition ==
; [[Menu screen]]
* Changed ''Minecraft'' logo from [[File:Java Edition logo 2.png|x20px]] to [[File:Java Edition logo 3.png|x20px]].
