'''1.90''' was an update to [[PlayStation 4 Edition]] released on April 15, 2019, which added more [[Village & Pillage]] features.<ref>{{tweet|4JStudios|1117727836268314624|An update for Minecraft: PlayStation 4 Edition is out now! Loom, Lectern, Lantern, and more! Full changes are here|April 15, 2019}}</ref> This update is equivalent to [[Bedrock Edition 1.10.0]], only missing experimental gameplay features like the new villages.<ref>{{Mcnet|r10-update-on-ps4|R10 update on PS4}} ("R10" means "Release 1.10")</ref>

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
;[[Loom]]
*Used to easily make patterns for [[banner]]s.
;[[Lectern]]
*Placing a [[book and quill]] or [[written book]] on the lectern allows multiple players to read it together.
**[[Redstone comparator]] functionality is not available yet.
;[[Lantern]]
*Acts as a new, hangable light source.
;[[Wood|Wood and Stripped Wood]]
*A [[log]] block with the bark texture on all 6 sides.

=== Items ===
;[[Crossbow]]
*New [[weapon]].
*Crossbows can be enchanted with three new [[enchantment]]s.
*Stronger than bow, but takes longer to recharge.
;[[Banner Pattern]]
*Made with the Loom.
*Can be added to Banners to apply designs.
;[[Shield]]
*[[Sneak]] while holding a shield in either hand to [[Blocking|block]].
*Banners can't be applied to shields yet.

=== Mobs ===
;[[Pillager]]
*They are hostile mobs, considered a subset of [[illager]]s.
*Have 24 health.
*They wield [[Crossbow|crossbows]].
*Drop arrows and their crossbows when killed (affected by Looting).
*They will raid, or take over, [[village]]s, killing [[villager]]s.
*They cannot equip [[armor]] of any type.
*Only available through [[creative mode]].
;[[Cat]]s
*Added a new skin: Jellie, the community cat contest winner.

=== Gameplay ===
; [[Enchanting|Enchantments]]
* [[Quick Charge]]
** Used on [[Crossbow|crossbows]] to decrease the reload time
* [[Multishot]] 
** Used on crossbows to shoot 3 arrows or [[Firework Rocket|fireworks]] but only consumes 1.
* [[Piercing]]
** Used on crossbows to shoot through [[Mob|mobs]].

=== General ===
; Texture packs
* Added Minecraft Classic texture pack (built-in), which contains pre–[[Texture Update]] textures.

; Trophies
* Added Expansion Pack 9, which contains five new trophies:
** (Silver) Atlantis? – Find an underwater ruin.
** (Bronze) Organizational Wizard – Rename a Shulker Box with an Anvil.
** (Bronze) Fruit on the Loom – Make a Banner with an Enchanted Apple stencil.
** (Silver) Plethora of Cats – Befriend twenty stray Cats.
** (Gold) Sail the 7 Seas – Visit all Ocean Biomes.

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
;[[Chorus Flower]] 
* Can now be broken when struck by an [[arrow]].
;[[Dead Bush]] 
* Can now be used as [[furnace]] fuel.
;[[Dispenser]] 
* Dispensers with shears in them can now shear [[sheep]] when activated.
;[[Leaves]] 
* May now drop [[Stick|sticks]] when decaying.
;[[Sponge]] 
* Placing wet sponge in [[The Nether|Nether]] and warm biomes will dry it out after some time.
;Undyed [[carpet]], [[glass]] and [[glass pane]]s
* Can now be dyed by surrounding them with dye in the [[Crafting Table|crafting table]].
* Regular glass and glass panes have changed textures.
;[[Log]]s and [[Stripped Log]]s
*Renamed from wood and stripped wood.

=== Items ===
;[[Rabbit Stew]] and [[Beetroot Soup]] 
* Now have shapeless crafting recipes. 

=== Mobs ===
;[[Cat]]s
* Can now be healed by feeding them [[fish]].
;[[Creeper]]s
*Can now drop a [[Music Disc|music disc]] if they are killed by a [[stray]], as well as a [[skeleton]].

=== General ===
;Textures
*The [[Texture Update]] textures have been added as the default pack.
