A '''sulfur spike''' is an upcoming pointy block that will be added in [[Chaos Cubed]]{{Upcoming}}. Like [[pointed dripstone]], it creates both stalactites and stalagmites and naturally generates in [[sulfur caves]].

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Sulfur spikes can be broken by hand, but a [[pickaxe]] is the fastest.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Sulfur Spike|pickaxe}}
Sulfur spikes also break instantly with a thrown [[trident]].

=== Natural generation ===
Sulfur spikes naturally generate in [[sulfur caves]].

=== Trading ===
[[Wandering trader]]s may sell 2 sulfur spikes for an [[emerald]].

== Usage ==
Like pointed dripstones, sulfur spikes can be found in 2 forms: stalactites and stalagmites. Stalactites are created when placed on above the ceiling, while stalagmites are created when placed on the ground.

When a [[player]] dies by a fallen stalactite, the [[death]] message will display as "'''<player> was skewered by a falling stalactite'''". When dying by falling to stalagmite, the standard fall damage is used instead of the death message "'''<player> was impaled on a stalagmite'''"
