{{History|java}}
{{History||1.5|snap=13w01a|Redstone comparators have extra, unobtainable item forms corresponding to their block IDs. They can be obtained via the {{cmd|give}} command or inventory editors with numeric item IDs 149 and 150.}}
{{History||1.7.2|snap=13w37a|The direct item forms of redstone comparators have been removed from the game. They can no longer exist as items in any way, only as placed blocks.}}
{{History|pocket alpha}}
{{History||?|Redstone comparators probably exist as an item.}}
{{History|foot}}
