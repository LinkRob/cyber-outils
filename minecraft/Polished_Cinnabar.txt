A '''polished cinnabar''' is an upcoming [[cinnabar]] variant that will be added in [[Chaos Cubed]]{{Upcoming}}. Like normal cinnabar blocks, it consists of [[slab]], [[stairs]], and [[wall]] variants.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Like normal [[cinnabar]] blocks, polished cinnabar blocks can only be mined using a [[pickaxe]], otherwise it drops nothing.

=== Sulfur cubes ===
[[Sulfur cubes]] can absorb full-sized polished cinnabar blocks, either picking it up or interacting with it, giving a regular/football (later slow bouncy{{Only|JE|short=1}}{{Upcoming|BE 26.30}}) effect{{Until|BE 26.30}} when hit.
