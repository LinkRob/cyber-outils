'''Speed''' is a [[status effect]] that increases an entity's [[walking]] speed by 20% multiplied by the effect level.  It expands a player's field of view (FOV) accordingly.  Negative levels decrease speed.

== Unaffected mobs ==

[[Wither]]s and [[ender dragon]]s are immune to the Speed effect. While not immune, flying mobs are unaffected by the Speed effect.<ref>{{bug|MC-40949}} - Flying mobs do not obey generic.movementSpeed attribute</ref> [[Shulker]]s are also unaffected by, though not immune to, Speed.

== Notes ==
* Alternative methods of traveling more quickly include: [[sprinting]]; walking on [[ice]], [[packed ice]] or [[blue ice]]; riding a [[horse]], [[pig]], or [[minecart]]; riding a [[boat]] on ice, packed ice, or blue ice; throwing [[ender pearl]]s; walking on [[soul soil]] or [[soul sand]] while wearing [[soul speed]] boots; or being near a dolphin to gain [[Dolphin's Grace]].

== Trivia ==

* When the player has Speed level 255, the player moves 500 blocks per tick.
* Speed allows [[slime]]s to jump farther. High levels of speed can make slimes bounce out of view with just one jump.
* Even With Speed Level 2 The Player Cannot Out Run a Wither
