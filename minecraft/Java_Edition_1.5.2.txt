'''1.5.2'''<ref>https://mcupdate.tumblr.com/post/49436600302/minecraft-152</ref> is a minor update for {{el|je}}, released on May 2, 2013,<ref>{{tweet|jeb|329957969053749248}}</ref> to fix some of the bugs and crashes in [[Java Edition 1.5|1.5]] and [[Java Edition 1.5.1|1.5.1]]. This is the last official ''Minecraft'' version that will run on PowerPC Mac computers and Java 5, and the last version to be playable with no download required.

A pre-release version of 1.5.2 was released on April 25, 2013.<ref>{{cite|url=http://www.mojang.com/2013/04/1-5-2-pre-release-and-13w17a-snapshot-oh-my/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/0/http://www.mojang.com/2013/04/1-5-2-pre-release-and-13w17a-snapshot-oh-my/|title=1.5.2 pre-release and 13w17a snapshot – oh my!|author=Dinnerbone|website=Mojang.com|date=April 25, 2013}}</ref> This pre-release was later reuploaded, and this reupload became the full release of 1.5.2.

=== Mobs ===
; [[Enderman]]
* Now won't become hostile if they take environmental damage.
