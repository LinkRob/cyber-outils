'''Cheese''' is a joke [[food]] and a [[block]] that can be eaten by the player. It was added in the [[Java Edition 23w13a or b|April Fools snapshot 23w13a_or_b]].

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Cheese drops itself if it has not been bitten. Once the cheese is bitten, it cannot be recollected even with the use of [[Silk Touch]].

{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Cheese|link=none|Any}}

=== Natural generation ===
Cheese generates naturally in the Moon dimension.

=== Mob loot ===
Upon death, [[Moon Cow]]s drop 0-2 cheese. The maximum amount is increased by 1 per level of Looting, for a maximum of 5 cheese blocks with Looting III.

=== Bucket ===
If the "Instacheese from Buckets" [[vote]] is active, using a [[milk bucket]] on the ground places cheese, similarly to [[powder snow]]. However, it cannot be picked up again via the bucket.

== Usage ==
Similar to [[cake]]s, cheese can be eaten when placed in the world. However, unlike cakes, the cheese blocks is divided into 8 smaller cubes. Clicking on one of them eats only the selected part, unlike cakes, where slices cannot be chosen.

Eating cheese not only restores hunger but also oxygen, allowing the player to survive on the Moon.

==Appearence==
Cheese shares a similar texture to [[End Stone]]; however, it is desaturated in colour.
