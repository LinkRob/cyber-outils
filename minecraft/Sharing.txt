'''Sharing''' is a joke [[effect]] from [[Java Edition 15w14a]] which replaces the data value of the weakness effect.

==Effect==
Sharing causes mobs to drop random items in a random amount of time. The items are:
*{{ItemLink|Bread}}
*{{ItemLink|Coal}}
*{{ItemLink|Diamond}}s
*{{ItemLink|Gold Ingot}}s
*{{ItemLink|Iron Ingot}}s
*{{ItemLink|Lapis Lazuli}}
*{{ItemLink|Melon Seeds}}
*{{ItemLink|Pumpkin Seeds}}
*{{ItemLink|Redstone}}
*{{ItemLink|Saddle}}s

The effect can be removed by drinking [[Milk Bucket|milk]].
