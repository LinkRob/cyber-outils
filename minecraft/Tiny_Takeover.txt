'''Tiny Takeover''' (also referred to as '''Drop 1 of 2026'''{{Only|BE}}) is a game drop that features craftable [[Name Tag|name tags]], [[Golden Dandelion|golden dandelions]], and revamping various baby mobs. It was released on March 24, 2026.

Prior to its name reveal, it was referred to as "First Drop 2026".

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Golden Dandelion]]
** A new decoration block which can be planted on [[flower pot]]s like other [[flower|flowers]] do.
** Can be crafted with a [[dandelion]] surrounded by 8 [[gold nugget]]s.
** Can be used to feed most baby mobs.
** Baby mobs will stop aging when fed on.
*** It will age again when fed on again.
** Can be used to craft [[Yellow Dye|yellow dye]]s and [[Suspicious Stew|saturation suspicious stews]].
** Unlike other flowers:
*** Are not compostable.
*** Does not attract [[bees]].
*** Are not pollinated by bees.
** Like all [[gold|golden items]], piglins can pick up the golden dandelion.
