'''Ray Tracing''' is a [[Player|humanoid]] [[Character|entity]] that spawns within the [[Overworld]] of ''[[Minecraft]]''.

==Spawning==
Ray Tracing can be allowed into the world by activating the vote <code>minecraft:ray_tracing</code> and waiting for the message "Ray Tracing has joined the game".

==Behavior==
Throwing a splash bottle of ray tracing at any mob can disguise it as Ray Tracing.

Ray Tracing wanders randomly in the world, sending random chat messages to the player. Upon death, Ray Tracing sends a random chat message.

When the vote <code>minecraft:ray_tracing</code> is repealed "Ray Tracing has left the game" will be sent on the chat and the entity will despawn.

==Trivia==

*It is impossible to summon Ray Tracing with the {{cmd|summon}} command.
*Ray Tracing is always in Survival Mode.
*If any mob is disguised as Ray Tracing, hostile mobs will attack the disguised target.
*If [[Java Edition 23w13a or b|French Mode]] is on, Ray Tracing will also have the clothing of French Mode.
