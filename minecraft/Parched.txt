A '''parched''' is a variant of a [[skeleton]] that spawns in [[desert]]s. They function similarly to skeletons, except they do not burn in [[sun]]light and shoot [[Weakness]] [[tipped arrows]].

== Spawning ==
Parcheds spawn at a light level of 0 in the [[desert]], mostly replacing skeletons in these biomes.

== Drops ==
Upon death, parched may drop:
* {{ItemLink|Bone|Bones}}
* {{ItemLink|Arrow|Arrows}}
* {{ItemLink|arrow of Weakness|Arrows of Weakness}}
* {{ItemLink|Bow|Damaged bows}}.

Unlike regular [[skeleton]]s, parched do not drop their head if killed by a charged [[creeper]] explosion.

== Behavior ==
Parched behave very similarly to [[skeleton]]s. They will attack nearby players by shooting [[arrow]]s tipped with [[weakness]] at them. However, like the [[bogged]], they are much slower to reload their bows, taking 3.5 seconds rather than 2.0, and also have {{Hp|4}} less health.

Parched seek out any nearby [[turtle egg]]s and trample them.

Parched can pick up [[armor|armor pieces]] if the game rule ''mobGriefing'' is set to true, which will prevent them from [[despawning]].

Like bogged, parched take damage from freezing in [[powder snow]].

=== As an [[Undead]] Mob ===
Like any undead mobs:
* Are damaged by {{EffectLink|Instant Health}} and healed by {{EffectLink|Instant Damage}};
* Are not affected by {{EffectLink|Regeneration}} and {{EffectLink|Poison}};
* Take extra damage from [[Smite]] enchantment.
* Are not targeted by the {{EntityLink|wither}}.
* Causes an [[armadillo]] to hide in its shell.
Since parched spawn in desert, they do not burn in sunlight.

==Appearance==
Like [[husk]]s, parched appear in a dark-muddy tan color, with pieces of parchment or sandy clothing wrapped around their skeletal body and head. Their eyes appear sandy yellow.

=== Experience ===
Parcheds drop {{xp|5}} and an additional {{xp|1|3}} per naturally-spawned equipment.
