A '''clay pot''' is an unfired, decorative pot. The pot can hold up to 8 [[ceramic shard]]s on its outer surface. If lit underneath, the pot can be transformed into a fired [[painted clay pot]]. It is not yet known if the pots have a purpose or are purely decorational.
