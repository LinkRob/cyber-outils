'''Buzzy Bees''' was a major<!-- major = change of second version number part; not related to content --> update, mainly focused on [[bee]]s and the items relating to them, as well as also fixing bugs from previous versions. The update was released on December 10, 2019, only for the {{editions|java|bedrock}} of ''Minecraft''.<ref>{{Tweet|slicedlime|1202583987698446336|Buzzy Bees should be fully available by December 11th. Java Edition in specific is releasing 10th.|5 Dec 2019}}</ref><ref>{{tweet|HelenAngel|1203079325106368513|10th, same as Java|December 6, 2019}}</ref>

This update was released on {{el|je}} as version [[Java Edition 1.15|1.15]]. This update's development started with snapshot 19w34a, which was released on August 22, 2019.<ref>{{Mcnet|minecraft-snapshot-19w34a|Minecraft Snapshot 19w34a|August 22, 2019}}</ref> The first pre-release for ''Java Edition'' 1.15 was released on November 21, 2019, followed by the second on November 25, the third on November 28, the fourth on December 3, the fifth on December 5, the sixth on December 6, and the seventh on December 9.<ref name="pre">{{Mcnet|minecraft-1-15-pre-release-1|Minecraft 1.15 Pre-Release 7|November 21, 2019}}</ref> 

It was released on {{el|be}} as version [[Bedrock Edition 1.14.0|1.14.0]].

Additionally, this update was released for {{el|ee}} in [[Education Edition 1.14.31|1.14.31]] on August 7, 2020.

For the {{el|ce}}, the full update was released on July 16, 2020.

The official name for the update, ''Buzzy Bees'' was revealed on November 21, 2019, in a blog post on [[Minecraft.net]].<ref name="pre"/>

== Notable features ==
{{main|Java Edition 1.15|Bedrock Edition 1.14.0|Education Edition 1.14.31}}

* New [[block]]s:
** [[Bee nest]]s
*** Are the homes of [[bee]]s.
*** Fill up with honey which you can collect through glass bottles.
** [[Beehive]]s
*** Can be [[crafting|crafted]] using 6 wood planks and 3 honey combs.
*** Are the homes of [[bee]]s.
*** Fill up with honey which you can collect through glass bottles.
** [[Honey block]]s
*** [[Entities]] touching their sides slide down slowly.
*** Take away some fall damage upon falling onto it.
*** A [[player]] or [[mob]] can not jump on honey blocks.
** [[Honeycomb Block]]s
*** Are decorative blocks.
*** [[Crafting|Crafted]] using four [[honeycomb]]s in a [[crafting table]]. 
* New [[item]]s:
** [[Honey bottle]]s
*** Remove the [[Poison]] effect when consumed like milk.
*** Collected by using a [[glass bottle]] on a [[beehive]] or [[bee nest]].
** [[Honeycomb]]s
*** Collected by shearing [[bee nest]]s and [[beehive]]s.
* New [[mob]]:
** [[Bee]]s
*** Inflict [[Poison]] if provoked (on normal and hard difficulty). After attacking once, they become passive and die shortly after.
*** Attack in groups if provoked, like [[zombified piglin]]s and [[Wolf|wolves]].
*** Can pollinate [[crops]], making them grow faster when passing over them in a pollinated state.
*** Avoid [[water]].
*** Homeless bees wander around searching for empty [[beehive]]s.
*** Can be bred with [[flower]]s.
