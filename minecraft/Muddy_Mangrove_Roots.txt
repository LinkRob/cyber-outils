'''Muddy mangrove roots''' are a decorative variant of [[mangrove roots]].

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Muddy mangrove roots can be broken by hand, but using a [[shovel]] speeds up the process. Muddy mangrove roots drop themselves when broken with any tool.

{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Muddy mangrove roots|Shovel|link=none}}

=== Crafting ===
{{Crafting
|showdescription=1
|A2=Mud
|B2=Mangrove Roots
|Output=Muddy Mangrove Roots
|type=Decoration Block
}}

=== Natural generation ===
Muddy mangrove roots naturally generate in [[mangrove swamp]]s where [[mud]] blocks intersect with normal [[mangrove roots]] at the bottom of [[mangrove tree]]s.

=== Post generation ===
When a [[mangrove propagule]] grows into a tree, if the tree roots intersect with nearby mud blocks, those mud blocks are converted into muddy mangrove roots.
