Official '''Minecraft demo''' releases available to try for free include:

== ''Java Edition'' ==
* A ''Minecraft'' [[demo mode]] of the current game version for users who have not purchased the game yet and would like to try it out first. They can play on a world for up to 100 minutes.
* The [[PC Gamer demo]] (outdated, but still available for free) - a limited version of [[Java Edition Beta 1.3|Beta 1.3]] that can be played on a world for up to 100 minutes.
* [[Java Edition Classic|Classic]] (outdated) - the first development version of ''Minecraft'' available in both Singleplayer and Multiplayer.
== Bedrock Edition ==
* A [[Demo mode#Minecraft Trial|Trial]] version for {{BE}} available on Windows, Android, and the PlayStation 4.
* [[Pocket Edition Demo]], the first [[Pocket Edition Alpha]] version, handed out to people for testing.

== Other ==
* ''[[Minecraft 4k]]'' (created separately from ''Minecraft'') - a very limited version of ''Minecraft'' developed for the 2010 {{w|Java 4K Game Programming Contest|Java 4K}} contest in less than four kibibytes.
