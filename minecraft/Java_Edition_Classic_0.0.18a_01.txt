'''0.0.18a_01'''<ref>{{ytl|robIDVtUC7I|Mine Craft, The Great Pyramid Server|mikeon|June 14, 2009}}</ref><ref>https://forum.mods.de/bb/thread.php?TID=195840&PID=1240505735#reply_1240505735 (June 14, 2009 01:24:12 UTC+2)</ref> was a version of [[Java Edition Classic]] released on June 13, 2009, at 22:43 UTC.<ref>{{link |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101210102932/https://notch.tumblr.com/post/123078678/custom-skins-are-live|title=Custom skins are live! |website=The Word of Notch |date=June 14, 2009}}</ref><ref>[[#0613 logs|June 13 IRC logs]]: "''P5:43:14 <C418> ah, yet another new client?''" == Bugs ==
* Reported problems with rendering corners of [[block]]s.<ref>[[#0614 logs|June 14 IRC logs]]: "''A4:05:45 <vdgmprgrmr> First, if you have a four-block cricumstance.''" [...] "''A4:05:51 <vdgmprgrmr> On two corners are solid blocks.''" [...] "''A4:06:17 <vdgmprgrmr> And in another corner you have liquid.''" [...] "''A4:06:28 <vdgmprgrmr> And in the corner on the other side of that one, you have nothing.''" (09:05 to 09:06 UTC)</ref>
* It was reported that a player swimming diagonally towards a corner where the right side is higher than the left side wouldn't be able to get onto the land until they look away from the block.<ref>[[#0614 logs|June 14 IRC logs]]: "''A4:08:14 <vdgmprgrmr> If you're in water, and you're in a situation like this:''" [...] "''A4:09:26 <vdgmprgrmr> And you're in the liquid, and you try to move diagonally toward the center while pressing space, you can't get on the block like you should be able to.''" [...] "''A4:10:01 <vdgmprgrmr> You have to be almost looking at the center of the block that would be to the left of the 1-height-block to get on it.''" (9:08 to 9:10 UTC)</ref>
** This bug existed at least as far back as [[Java Edition Classic 0.0.16a_02|0.0.16a_02]].
