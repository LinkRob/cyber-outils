'''Luck of the Sea''' is an [[enchantment]] to a [[fishing rod]] that increases luck while [[fishing]].

== Usage ==
{{see also|Fishing}}
With the default [[Fishing#Junk and treasure|loot tables]], this enchantment increases the chance of "treasure" catches (by about 2% per level), while greatly lowering the chance of "junk" catches (by about 2% per level) and slightly lowering the chance of fish catches (by about 0.15% per level).
