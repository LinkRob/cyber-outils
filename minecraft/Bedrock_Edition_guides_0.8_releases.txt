This guide is a simplified overview of all the changes in the [[Bedrock Edition]] 0.8.x releases, which assumes at least some familiarity with 0.7.x releases.

== Mobs ==
=== Changed ===
{|{{Simple Guide table|Changed Mobs}}
|-
|General
|
|
* Improved AI system (mobs can swim in deep [[water]], hostile mobs are smarter).
* Baby animals follow adults of the same species.
* Animals will follow players holding food.
* New visuals for burning mobs.
|-
|[[sheep]]
|{{Animate|White Sheep BE4.png; Orange Sheep BE3.png; Magenta Sheep BE3.png; Light Blue Sheep BE3.png; Yellow Sheep BE3.png; Lime Sheep BE3.png; Pink Sheep BE3.png; Gray Sheep BE3.png; Light Gray Sheep BE3.png; Cyan Sheep BE3.png; Purple Sheep BE3.png; Blue Sheep BE3.png; Green Sheep BE3.png; Brown Sheep BE3.png; Red Sheep BE3.png; Black Sheep BE3.png|64px}}
|
* Sheep have the color of the upper layer of wool on the face and after being sheared (iOS only).
|-
|[[Spider]]
|[[File:Spider JE3 BE2.png|128px]]
|
* Increased spider movement speed.
|-
|[[Zombie Pigman]]
|[[File:Zombified Piglin BE1.png|128px]]
|
* No longer takes damage from burning during the day.
|}
