'''Clay blobs''' were a terrain feature that existed as a way for [[clay]] blocks to generate naturally.

== Generation ==
Clay blobs would generate extremely similarly to conventional [[ore (feature)|ore blob]]s (potentially even to the extent of being impacted by [[Java Edition distance effects|the same precision loss bugs]]), replacing [[sand]] blocks in beaches. Whether they would generate in deserts as well is unknown.
