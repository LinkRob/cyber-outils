A '''tadpole''' is a [[Bucket of aquatic mob|bucketable]] aquatic baby passive [[mob]] hatched from [[frogspawn]]. They mature into one of the three [[frog]] variants depending on the biome in which they mature.

== Spawning ==
Tadpoles hatch from [[frogspawn]] in groups of 2-5.

== Drops ==
As with other baby animals, tadpoles do not drop any items or experience on death.

== Behavior ==
Tadpoles swim aimlessly in water. On land, they flop around like fish and seek out nearest water. They quickly die on land. 

Unlike frogs, tadpoles are hunted by [[axolotl]]s.

Tadpoles follow a player that is holding a [[slimeball]].

A player can pick up a tadpole with a [[water bucket]].

A tadpole grows up into one of the variants of a frog depending on the tadpole's location, as shown on the table below. Tadpoles take one Minecraft day (20 minutes) to grow up. Its growth may be accelerated by feeding it slimeballs. Each use reduces the remaining growth duration by 10%.
