'''v0.11.1 alpha''' is an update to ''[[Minecraft Pocket Edition]]'' that was released to fix bugs regarding world crashes and unavailable skin packs.<ref name="0.11.1">{{tweet|_tomcc|606495708372336641|0.11.1 is out and it should fix the world crash + unavailable Skin Packs, thanks to Apple for letting us jump the queue :P|4 Jun 2015}}</ref>

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
; [[Health]]
* Can no longer be filled by exiting and entering the game.
