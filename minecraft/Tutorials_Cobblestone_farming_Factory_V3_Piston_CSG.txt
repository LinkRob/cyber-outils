This quad-pillar generator fits in a 4&times;3 hole, 6 deep.  It produces 4 cobblestone on every fourth piston cycle.  ''Earliest Known Publication:'' Jul 6, 2012<ref>The build is demonstrated in [http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E0jeCUt8ko8 this video by ZirumsHeroTWR].</ref>

Build tips: 
# When planning, consider that the bottom-layer dust is directly below the on/off switch.  
# When building the clock (north row of layers 0 and 1), place the repeater and set it to max before covering the level 0 components.
# While you are mostly working from the bottom up, you'll want to finish the switch column early.  This includes a sticky piston facing down; to place this, it help to place at least the top block (level 5), then work down from there.
# Go up to the surface to flip the lever twice.  This will pick up the switch block, turning off the clock before you place the pistons.
# Then finish the north wall and continue building in 3&times;3.  The cobblestone need not be placed, any blocks placed there will go up with the blocks above them.
# Place water first. then lava.  
# Place any height limiters you want for the four columns, and fill the middle column with some non-pickaxe block—sand, gravel, or wood will do fine.  That's just so your loot doesn't get stranded between the columns.
# Then turn it on long enough to push up the spare blocks above the cobblestone, and mine those.  Your factory is now running!
