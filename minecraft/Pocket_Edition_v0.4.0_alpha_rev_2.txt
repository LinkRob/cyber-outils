'''v0.4.0 alpha rev 2''' was a minor update to '' [[Pocket Edition|Minecraft: Pocket Edition]]'' that was released on September 14, 2012.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20120917074331/http://itunes.apple.com:80/hu/app/minecraft-pocket-edition/id479516143?mt=8</ref> This version was exclusively released on iOS to add support for the iPhone 5 resolution and to fix some of the bugs and crashes in [[Pocket Edition v0.4.0 alpha|v0.4.0]].

== Changes ==

* Added support for iPhone 5 resolution
* Creative mode additions:
** [[TNT]]
***Cannot be activated due to absence of [[flint and steel]].
** [[Trapdoor]]s
** [[Stone Bricks]]

== Fixes ==
'''1 bug fixed'''
* [[Explosion]]s now destroy the ground.</onlyinclude>
