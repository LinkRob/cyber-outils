'''1.8.13'''<ref>{{cite|url=https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360020066051|title=Minecraft: New Nintendo 3DS Edition - 1.8|date=November 21, 2018}}</ref> was an update for the {{el|ds}} released on November 20/21, 2018, which added new downloadable content and fixed bugs.

== Additions ==
=== General ===
New DLC available for purchase:
* Steam Punk Texture Pack
* Strangers – Biome Settlers 3 Skin Pack
* Moana Character Pack
* Mighty Morphin Power Ranger Skin Pack
* Magic: The Gathering Skin Pack
* Halloween Costume Skin Pack
* Festive Mash-up 2016

== Changes ==
=== General ===
* Improved stability and performance.

== Fixes ==
* Numerous bug fixes from previous builds.
* Fixed a bug where the player could sprint while [[Bed#Sleeping|sleeping]] in a [[bed]].
