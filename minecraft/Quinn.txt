'''Quinn''' is a female character in the ''[[Minecraft Mini-Series]]'' and part of Team Stone Beach with [[Toby]]. She helps everyone to try and escape [[CommuniKate]]'s game show, winning a few minigames, and ultimately winning Season 1. She then teamed up with [[Durango]], [[CommuniKate]], and [[Zed]] in Season 2, ''The Greek Isles'' after [[Poseidon]] takes over.

== Appearance ==
Quinn has red hair in a ponytail, blue eyes, and tan skin. She wears a black jacket, a pink shirt with a white lighting bolt on it, light blue wristbands, dull tan pants, and brown shoes.

== Personality ==
Quinn is independent and determined. She likes solitude and dislikes bossy, authoritative, or cocky people but learns to work with everyone throughout the show. Notable friends are [[Toby]], [[Slugger]], and [[Mo]].
