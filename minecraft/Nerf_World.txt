'''Nerf World''' is a free [[Marketplace]] DLC that features Minecraft-themed Nerf blasters and various Nerf-themed Minecraft mobs. It is released on October 2, 2023.

== Description ==
{{Quote|Gear up for an epic Nerf x Minecraft adventure! Dive into a world of foam-flinging fun as you blast your way through hordes of Minecraft mobs, and unlock dimension-themed arenas. Jump into the blocky chaos and charge towards victory, it's a Nerf battle like never before!|Minecraft [[Marketplace]] description}}

== Additions ==
=== Collectables ===
* Nerf Bucks
** Can be obtained by killing enemies.
** Used as an in-game currency.
** Can be used to purchase items in the Blaster Shop.
* Darts
** Used as a projectile from blasters.
** Obtained as a starter every time the [[player]] enters the arena (can be increased by purchasing their starter packs at the Blaster Shop.)
*** Can also be obtained from:
**** Dropped by blasters on the ground
**** Dart packs
* Dart pack
** Drops over time in every arena.
** Grants additional 32 darts to the player per pack.
* Power-ups
** Drops any of the two over time in every arena.
** Has 2 power-ups:
*** Jump Boost
*** Speed Boost

=== Items ===
* Blaster
** Can be obtained by purchasing them in the Blaster Shop.
** Shoots projectiles by holding it.
** Can hold up to 3 blasters in the hotbar.
*** If owned more than 3 blasters, the selected owned blaster will be replaced with another owned blaster.
** Consists of 15 blasters:
*** Microshot blasters (can be purchased for 250 Nerf bucks)
**** Chicken Microshot
**** Cave Spider Microshot
**** Snow Golem Microshot
**** Elder Guardian Microshot
**** Guardian Microshot
**** Ghast Microshot
**** Ender Dragon Microshot
*** Sabrewing (can be purchased for 2000 Nerf bucks) - wielded by skeletons.
*** Stormlander (can be purchased for 3000 Nerf bucks) - wielded by vindicators.
*** Heartstealer (can be purchased for 1000 Nerf bucks) - wielded by zombified piglins and wither skeletons.
*** Pillager's Crossbow (can be purchased for 2000 Nerf bucks) - wielded by pillagers.
*** Ender Dragon Blaster (can be purchased for 6000 Nerf bucks)
* Leave the Arena button
** Placed on the player's 9th hotbar slot.
** Can be used to leave the arena and return to the hub by holding the item for 3 seconds.

=== Locations ===
* Hub
** Serves as the lobby area for players starting the game.
*** It also serves as the central area.
** Has 3 locations within the hub:
*** Practice Range
*** Blaster Shop
*** Portal to 4 different arenas.
* Arenas
** Can be selected by hitting the arrows with a blaster shot.
** Has 4 levels:
*** Overworld Arena
**** Unlocks by default.
**** Consists of the following mobs:
***** {{EntityLink|Zombie}}
***** {{EntityLink|Skeleton}}
***** {{EntityLink|Creeper}}
***** {{EntityLink|Slime}}
***** {{EntityLink|Spider}}

***** {{EntityLink|Pillager}}
***** {{EntityLink|Vex}}
***** {{EntityLink|Witch}} (mini boss mob)
***** {{EntityLink|Evoker}} (mini boss mob)
***** {{EntityLink|Ravager}} (mini boss mob)
***** {{EntityLink|Warden}} (boss mob)
**** Has 3 designated drop points for power-ups and dart packs.
*** Nether Arena
**** Unlocks when a player completes 20 rounds in the Overworld Arena.
**** Consists of the following mobs:
***** {{EntityLink|Zombie}}
***** {{EntityLink|Skeleton}}
***** {{EntityLink|Creeper}}
***** {{EntityLink|Zombified Piglin}}
***** {{EntityLink|Wither Skeleton}}
***** {{EntityLink|Blaze}}
***** {{EntityLink|Magma Cube}}
***** {{EntityLink|Ghast}} (mini boss mob)
***** {{EntityLink|Wither}} (boss mob)
**** Has 4 designated drop points for power-ups and dart packs.
*** End Arena
**** Unlocks when a player completes 15 rounds in the Nether Arena.
**** Consists of the following mobs:
***** {{EntityLink|Enderman}}
***** {{EntityLink|Ender Dragon}} (boss mob)
**** Has 5 designated drop points for power-ups and dart packs.
*** Nerf Arena
**** Unlocks when a player successfully defeats the [[Ender Dragon|Nerf Ender Dragon]] in the End Arena.
**** Consists of selected mobs from Overworld and Nether Arenas.
**** Has 3 designated drop points for power-ups and dart packs.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Heads-up display]]
** Changed to a Nerf-themed texture.
** Hearts are replaced with health bars.
** Hunger, armor, and XP bars are removed and are replaced with ammo and Nerf bucks counter.
* [[Death]]
** No longer display the "You died!" death screen since players' death take place in the arena.
*** Instead, it pans towards the full view of an arena for 10 seconds before respawning the player and resetting the current round.
** Added its own death sound.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Zombie]], [[Husk]], [[Drowned]], [[Spider]], [[Evoker]], and [[Pillager]]
** Changed to a Nerf-themed texture.
* [[Vindicator]]
** Changed to a Nerf-themed texture.
** Wields stormlander instead of axe.
* [[Skeleton]]s
** Wields sabrewing instead of bow.
* [[Zombified Piglin]]s and [[Wither Skeleton]]s
** Wields heartstealer instead of sword.
* [[Ender Dragon]]
** Changed to a Nerf-themed texture.
** Changed the name in the boss bar to "Nerf Ender Dragon".
** Becomes immune to damage and protected by crystals.
*** Can disable its invulnerability by blasting the crystals.
** Shoots long red tongue instead of dragon fireballs.
* [[Warden]]
** Now has its own boss bar.

== Trivia ==
* The [[creeper]]'s texture in the DLC resemble [[Minecraft Dungeons:Creeper|ones]] from [[Minecraft Dungeons]].
* The original price of the Ender Dragon Blaster was 5000 Nerf bucks.
