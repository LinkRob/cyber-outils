A '''moss carpet''' is a thin decorative variant of the [[moss block]]. There are two different variants of moss carpets: normal moss carpets and pale moss carpets.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Moss carpets can be mined using any [[tool]] or without a tool, but a [[hoe]] is the quickest.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Moss Carpet|Hoe}}

=== Natural generation ===

Moss carpets generate naturally in [[lush caves]] and [[mangrove swamp]]s.

Pale moss carpets generate naturally in [[pale garden]]s, usually on [[pale moss block]]s and near [[pale oak]] trees.

=== Crafting ===
Normal Moss Carpets require 2 [[Moss Block]]s to craft.
{{Crafting
 |A3= Moss Block
 |B3= Moss Block
 |Output= Moss Carpet,3
 |type= Decoration block
}}

Pale Moss Carpets require 2 [[Pale Moss Block]]s to craft.
{{Crafting
 |A3= Pale Moss Block
 |B3= Pale Moss Block
 |Output= Pale Moss Carpet,3
 |type= Decoration block
}}

=== Post-generation ===

{{Control|Use|text=Using}} [[bone meal]] on a [[moss block]] has a 25% chance to grow moss carpets.

[[Mangrove propagule]]s that grow into mangrove trees can generate normal moss carpets.

== Usage ==
Moss carpet has most of the properties of the standard [[carpet]], except that it is not flammable, cannot be pushed by [[piston]]s, cannot be equipped on [[llama]]s, and does not occlude [[vibration]]s. 

=== Composting ===
Placing a moss carpet into a [[composter]] has a 30% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

=== Piston interactivity ===
Moss carpets break when pushed by pistons. They cannot be pulled by sticky pistons.

* Unlike normal moss carpets, pale moss carpets generate a cluster of pale gray vines that grow vertically onto an opposing block.
** The vines can be bonemealed up to 2 blocks tall.
