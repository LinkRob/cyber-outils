Game mode 9 is one of the secret [[Game mode|game modes]] in [[Bedrock Edition|Minecraft: Bedrock Edition]]. The player has infinite resources.
[[File:Gamemode 9.png|thumb|423x423px|The player facing at a sunflower forest.]]
However, this game mode was patched on the latest versions, but can still be accessed by entering game mode "-1".

The player has the ability to fly.

[[Health]] and [[hunger]] bars are still visible, but you cannot take damage.

[[Tools]] have infinite durability, no matter what you try, you cannot break them.

== Interaction ==
[[Mob|Mobs]] do not attack the player, even if are hostile.

You can place infinite blocks, no matter what (unless you drop them).
