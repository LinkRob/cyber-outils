'''Carl Manneh''' is a co-founder and former Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of [[Mojang Studios]] until he left the company on November 6, 2014.<ref>{{tweet|carlmanneh|511536762137481216|Thank you @notch & @JahKob for inviting me on the ride. Thank you Mojangstas, it's been 4 amazing years. Can't wait to follow what's next!|September 15, 2014}}</ref>

Carl has passions for snowboarding and traveling. He likes mountains in Sweden, but his favorite is Whistler in Canada.{{cn}}
