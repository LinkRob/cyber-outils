A '''sulfur cube''' is an upcoming [[Bucket (disambiguation)|bucketable]] and passive [[Slime (disambiguation)|slime]]-like mob that will be added in [[Chaos Cubed]]{{Upcoming}}. It turns into a bouncy cube when it sucks up the block, creating various effects depending on the block absorbed. It was announced during [[Minecraft Live - March 2026]].

== Spawning ==
Large sulfur cubes naturally spawn in [[sulfur caves]]. When they are killed, they split into two smaller sulfur cubes, which can then grow up into large sulfur cubes.

{{IN|BE}}, sulfur cubes cannot spawn in peaceful difficulty, despite being a passive creature{{Until|BE 26.30}}.

== Behavior ==
Sulfur cubes jump around aimlessly, making it vulnerable to all sources of damage.

Sulfur cubes can climb through blocks that can be climbed such as [[ladder]]s and [[vine]]s. Launched sulfur cubes with absorbed blocks also has capable of climbing.

=== Splitting ===
Sulfur cubes has two sizes: small and large. When a large sulfur cube dies, it splits into two. However, unlike [[slime]]s and [[magma cube]]s, small sulfur cubes can grow into larger size.

=== Absorbing blocks ===
Large sulfur cubes have an ability to absorb blocks. In that state, it turns to a bouncy cube with various effects depending on the type of block absorbed.

Large sulfur cubes can absorb blocks that are dropped on the ground, and can also absorb blocks the [[player]] holds to. [[Dispenser]]s can also absorb blocks when launched.

=== Damage resistance ===
Sulfur cubes with a block absorbed in it becomes invulnerable to following types of damage:
* Melee attacks
* Projectiles
* Falling blocks (this includes [[anvil]]s and [[stalactite]]s)
* [[Explosion]]s
* Fall damage (this includes falling to [[stalagmite]]s)
* Freezing
* [[Cactus]] and sweet berry bushes
[[Fire]], [[lava]], suffocation, cramming{{Only|JE|short=1}}, and [[warden]]'s sonic boom still kills the sulfur cube, even it has an absorbed block in it.
