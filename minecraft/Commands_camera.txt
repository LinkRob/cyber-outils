== Arguments ==
{{argument|be=1|players: target|CommandSelector<Player>}}
: {{info needed}}
: {{arg desc|be=CommandSelector<Player>}}

{{argument|be=1|red: int|int}}, {{argument|be=1|green: int|int}}, and {{argument|be=1|blue: int|int}}
: {{info needed}}
: {{arg desc|be=int}} And must be between 0 and 255. 

{{argument|be=1|fadeInSeconds: float|float}}, {{argument|be=1|holdSeconds: float|float}}, and {{argument|be=1|fadeOutSeconds: float|float}}
: Specifies the camera fade in, hold, and fade out duration.
: {{arg desc|be=float}} And must be between 0.0 and 10.0 (seconds). 

{{argument|be=1|preset: string|basic_string}}
: A camera view/preset.
: Must be one of <code>minecraft:free</code>, <code>minecraft:first_person</code>, <code>minecraft:third_person</code>, <code>minecraft:third_person_front</code>, <code>example:example_free</code>, <code>example:example_player_effects</code>, and <code>example:example_player_listener</code>.

{{argument|be=1|easeTime: float|float}}
: Specifies the duration of the easing.
: {{arg desc|be=float}}

{{argument|be=1|easeType: Easing|enum}}
: Specifies an easing function/type to use for the camera.
: Must be one of <code>linear</code>, <code>spring</code>, <code>in_quad</code>, <code>out_quad</code>, <code>in_out_quad</code>, <code>in_cubic</code>, <code>out_cubic</code>, <code>in_out_cubic</code>, <code>in_quart</code>, <code>out_quart</code>, <code>in_out_quart</code>, <code>in_quint</code>, <code>out_quint</code>, <code>in_out_quint</code>, <code>in_sine</code>, <code>out_sine</code>, <code>in_out_sine</code>, <code>in_expo</code>, <code>out_expo</code>, <code>in_out_expo</code>, <code>in_circ</code>, <code>out_circ</code>, <code>in_out_circ</code>, <code>in_bounce</code>, <code>out_bounce</code>, <code>in_out_bounce</code>, <code>in_back</code>, <code>out_back</code>, <code>in_out_back</code>, <code>in_elastic</code>, <code>out_elastic</code>, and <code>in_out_elastic</code>.

{{argument|be=1|default: default|basic_string}}
: {{info needed}}
: {{arg desc|be=basic_string}}

{{argument|be=1|lookAtEntity: target|CommandSelector<Actor>}}
: Specifies the entity to make the camera facing to.
: {{arg desc|be=CommandSelector<Actor>}}

{{argument|be=1|lookAtPosition: x y z|CommandPositionFloat}}
: Specifies the coordinates to make the camera facing to.
: {{arg desc|be=CommandPositionFloat}}

{{argument|be=1|position: x y z|CommandPosition}}
: Specifies the camera position.
: {{arg desc|be=CommandPosition}}

{{argument|be=1|xRot: value|RelativeFloat}} and {{argument|be=1|yRot: value|RelativeFloat}}
: Specifies the camera rotation.
: {{arg desc|be=RelativeFloat}}
