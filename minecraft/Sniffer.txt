A '''sniffer''' is an ancient [[passive mob]] that does not spawn naturally, and can only be hatched from [[Sniffer Egg|sniffer eggs]]. It sniffs out and digs out seeds for various unique decorative plants out of [[Dirt (disambiguation)|dirt]], [[Grass Block|grass]], and [[Moss Block|moss blocks]].

== Spawning ==
Sniffers spawn by hatching from [[Sniffer Egg|sniffer eggs]] found in [[Suspicious Sand|suspicious sand]] from warm [[ocean ruins]]. Breeding sniffers with [[torchflower seeds]] would also result in a sniffer egg being laid as an [[item (entity)|item]].

== Drops ==
When killed, adult sniffers drop {{xp|1|3}} [[experience]] points if killed by a player or a tamed [[wolf]]; snifflets drop no experience just like other baby animals.

Upon successful breeding, {{xp|1|7}} is dropped.

== Appearance ==
Sniffers have 6 legs, a big feathery bushy body of red and dark green colours, and a big yellow beaked nose. They also have two long, feathery ears above their eyes.

== Behavior ==
Sniffers wander aimlessly, avoiding hazards and obstacles. They occasionally smell their surroundings and track ancient seeds by pressing their nose to the ground. When they find a seed, they sploot<ref>{{Ytl|GYO8KejSodg|Minecraft 1.20: Sniffer Secrets & Cherry Groves|Minecraft|May 14, 2023|t=155}}</ref> and use their noses to dig into the ground until they get [[torchflower seeds]] or a [[pitcher pod]]. For a sniffer to be able to dig, it needs to be doing nothing else, not in water, on the ground, not in a boat or minecart, and the block underneath the head needs to be diggable and reachable for the sniffer. The head location is defined as 2.25 blocks in front of the center of the body. The sniffer also stores the last 20 locations it has dug up so it can't dig there anymore. After sniffing out seeds, it shakes its head with joy, activating an eight-minute cooldown before it can dig in again.

The following blocks are diggable by the sniffer:
*{{BlockLink|Dirt}}
*{{BlockLink|Grass Block}}
*{{BlockLink|Podzol}}
*{{BlockLink|Coarse Dirt}}
*{{BlockLink|Rooted Dirt}}
*{{BlockLink|Moss Block}}
*{{BlockLink|Mud}}
*{{BlockLink|Muddy Mangrove Roots}}
[[Data pack]]s from ''Java Edition'' can change this list by modifying the <code>minecraft:sniffer_diggable_block</code> [[Tag#Blocks|block tag]].

Sniffers cannot dig on [[mycelium]].<ref>{{bug|MC-260259||Sniffers cannot dig nor find seeds on mycelium|WAI}}</ref>

=== Breeding ===
The player can [[breed]] the sniffers using [[torchflower seeds]]. When two adult sniffers are bred, they drop a sniffer egg, after which the parents have a 5-minute cooldown before they can breed again.

==== Healing ====
Sniffers are healed by {{hp|2}} health points each time they are fed a torchflower seed.

=== Snifflets ===
A baby sniffer is called a '''snifflet'''<ref>{{Mcnet|sniffer-coming-minecraft-120|Once you’ve unearthed your egg, it’s time to help it hatch! I implore you to pay close attention to this part of the process, because you have not lived until you have seen a '''snifflet''' burst out of its shell.}}</ref> and they have a bigger head than the adult. Snifflets take 48000 [[game tick]]s to grow up into adult sniffers, which is equal to 40 minutes or two in-game days, twice as long as most other baby mobs in the game.

Unlike most baby mobs, snifflets do not follow their adults.<ref>{{bug|MC-260272|||WAI}}</ref>

== Trivia ==
*The sniffer was originally a doodle Nekofresa made in their kitchen.<ref>{{Tweet|Nekofresa|1625173791318982667|Soon™ you will finally be able to meet the sniffer! Less than a year ago, this chonky baby was just a doodle made in my kitchen, they are growing so fast 🥺💖✨|February 13, 2023}}</ref>
**The sniffer was Nekofresa's first mob ever designed for Minecraft.<ref>{{Tweet|Nekofresa|1625399598670815232|We all brought it to the game! I only did the concept arts, but then the entire team brought it to life 💖 especially the anims|February 14, 2023}}</ref>
* The plants that sniffers find are based off ''[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kniphofia kniphofia]'' genus of perennial flowering plants, which can be similarly called "torch lilies" as a common name for torchflowers and pitcher pods are loosely based off existing pitcher plants but with an orchid or ornamental vibe.<ref>{{ytl|1pxa47yWydo|Ask Mojang|Minecraft|April 27, 2023}}</ref>
* The sniffer's walking animation was inspired by the leg movement that centipedes do.<ref>{{Ytl|GYO8KejSodg|Minecraft 1.20: Sniffer Secrets & Cherry Groves|Minecraft|May 14, 2023|t=81}}</ref>
* During the prototype phase for the sniffer's digging up of items the developers used beds as a placeholder.<ref>{{Ytl|GYO8KejSodg|Minecraft 1.20: Sniffer Secrets & Cherry Groves|Minecraft|May 14, 2023|t=105}}</ref>
* Due to the size of the sniffer it was a big challenge for the developers to perfect its pathfinding especially on 1 block edges.
* A new team made up of new developers were tasked with developing the sniffer, and most of them had never made a mob before.<ref>{{Ytl|GYO8KejSodg|Minecraft 1.20: Sniffer Secrets & Cherry Groves|Minecraft|May 14, 2023|t=155}}</ref>
* The sniffer's digging animation was inspired by the way {{w|corgi}}s spread their legs while lying down.<ref>{{Ytl|GYO8KejSodg|Minecraft 1.20: Sniffer Secrets & Cherry Groves|Minecraft|May 14, 2023|t=164}}</ref>
* To this day, the developers still don't know what the sniffer's front yellow part is. Whether it's a beak, a nose, or eyes, or whether it's used for breathing or finding seeds is unknown.<ref>{{Ytl|GYO8KejSodg|Minecraft 1.20: Sniffer Secrets & Cherry Groves|Minecraft|May 14, 2023|t=69}}</ref>
*  A baby sniffer is called a '''snifflet''' in an article on Minecraft.net<ref>{{Mcnet|sniffer-coming-minecraft-120|Once you’ve unearthed your egg, it’s time to help it hatch! I implore you to pay close attention to this part of the process, because you have not lived until you have seen a '''snifflet''' burst out of its shell.}}</ref> and in the achievement "Little Sniffs".
* Original ideas for what a baby sniffer would be called were Sniffling, Snort and Snifflet.<ref>{{Ytl|LQGF2EbuhNU|ASK MOJANG - How the Sniffer got it's name?|Minecraft|August 21, 2023|t=18}}</ref>
** Mojang developer [[Cory Scheviak]] personally calls sniffers "snuffleupaguses".
*The reason why sniffers don’t spawn naturally and are at first only hatched from their preserved eggs is due to the sniffer being extinct prior to the [[Player|player’s]] events in Minecraft.
