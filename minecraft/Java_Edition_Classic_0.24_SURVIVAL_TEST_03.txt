'''0.24_SURVIVAL_TEST_03'''<ref>{{ytl|iJJ9rc5fRpc|Minecraft SURVIVAL_MODE_TEST_03||Lucas Anderson - September 2, 2009}}</ref> is a version of [[Classic]] released on September 1, 2009, at 17:55 UTC.<ref><samp>.jar</samp> dates to 19:55 UTC+2</ref><ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''P1:22:12 <Marach> now it is _03 version!''" (18:22 UTC)</ref> This version is not available in the [[launcher]], but has been archived elsewhere.

== Fixes ==
* Fixed a bug where if a creeper blows up a liquid, the liquid will drop itself.{{check version}}
* Fixed a bug allowing players to copy blocks into their hotbar.{{check version}}
* Fixed most other bugs from [[0.24 SURVIVAL TEST]].{{info needed|too vague - should be replaced with examples}}

== Bugs ==
* Hitting a mob near a sign causes the text to turn white.<!-- (It may have something to do with the white flash that appears when attacking mobs and the sign also being an entity)-->
* Placing a sapling on sand, will make it disappear.{{Info needed|What is "it" mean, the sand or the sapling?}}

== Trivia ==
* This is the earliest Survival Test version to be archived.
