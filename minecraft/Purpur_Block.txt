'''Purpur blocks''' and '''purpur pillars''' are decorative [[block]]s that are naturally generated in [[End city|end cities]] and [[end ship]]s.

'''Chiseled purpur''' and '''smooth purpur''' are two further variants of purpur blocks exclusive to [[Bedrock Edition]] that can be accessed only by [[commands]], and appear identical to regular purpur blocks.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===

Purpur blocks and pillars can be [[breaking|mined]] using any [[pickaxe]]. If mined without a pickaxe, it [[drops]] nothing.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Purpur Block, Purpur Pillar|link=none, none|Pickaxe|Wood}}

=== Natural generation ===

Purpur blocks and pillars can be found naturally as part of the structure of [[End City|end cities]] and [[end ship]]s.

== Usage ==

Purpur blocks and pillars are primarily decorative [[block]]s, similar to other [[bricks|brick]]-type blocks. They can also be used to craft different variants of the block. Purpur pillar blocks can be placed sideways, similar to [[log]]s or [[quartz pillar]] blocks.

=== Crafting ingredient ===

Purpur pillars cannot be used to craft slabs {{in|bedrock}}. 

{{crafting usage|Purpur Block, Purpur Pillar}}

=== Note Blocks ===
Purpur block and purpur pillar can be placed under [[note block]]s to produce "bass drum" sound.

== Trivia ==
* Purpur is the word for a specific shade of purple in multiple languages, including Swedish, Polish, German, and Cornish (and French ''pourpre'' to a certain extent).
* Chiseled Purpur{{only|be}} is an unused purpur block that has the texture of the normal Purpur Block and is obtainable via {{cmd|give|@s purpur_block 1 1}} or inventory editing.
* Smooth Purpur{{only|be}} is an unused purpur block that has the texture of the normal Purpur Block and is obtainable via {{cmd|give|@s purpur_block 1 3}} or inventory editing. Its in-game name is tile.purpur_block.smooth.name.
