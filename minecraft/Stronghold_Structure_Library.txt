Stronghold Libraries are sometimes found in [[stronghold]]s. Stronghold libraries are composed of [[stone bricks]], mossy stone bricks, cracked stone bricks, oak [[wood planks]], [[fence]]s, [[torch]]es, [[bookshelves]], cobwebs, and may include [[ladder]]s if it is a duplex library. The variance of bricks, including [[Infested Block|infested stone bricks]], is random in all layers yet with a notable predominance of Stone Bricks. The chests can contain [[paper]], [[book]]s, and more rarely, a [[map]], [[compass]], or [[enchanted book]]. Note that in a single-story library, there are no ladders.
