A version of '''[[Indev 0.31]]''' was released on '''February 1, 2010'''.<ref>[[wordofnotch:365736105|Minor update, must sleep.]] – [[The Word of Notch]], February 1, 2010</ref>

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
; {{Animate|Coal Ore JE1 BE1.png;Iron Ore JE1 BE1.png;Gold Ore JE2 BE1.png;Diamond Ore JE1 BE1.png|32px}} [[Ore]]s and [[File:Stone JE2 BE1.png|32px]] [[stone]]
* Now will need a pickaxe to break the block.
** No longer possible to mine [[stone]]-related blocks without the correct tier pickaxe.
** Ores drop only 1 of their respective mineral when smelted or mined.
; [[File:Oak Log (UD) JE2 BE1.png|32px]] '''[[Log]]'''
* Now breaks faster than stone.
;Sponge
* Now soaks up [[infinite water]] and [[Lava Spawner|lava]] blocks.

=== Items ===
; [[Tools]] and [[Tiers]]
* Harder materials now require better tools.
* Slowed down the mining speed of pickaxes.

=== Mobs ===
; General
* Now also drop [[flint and steel]].</onlyinclude>

== Bugs ==
* When generating a new level any grass block below flower turns into dirt.
* When inside non-solid blocks the fog appears within two block radius.
