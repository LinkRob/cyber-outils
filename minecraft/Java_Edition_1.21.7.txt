'''Java Edition 1.21.7''' is a minor hotfix to 1.21 for [[Java Edition]], released on June 30, 2025.

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Painting]]s
** Added one new painting from [[Sarah Boeving]] depicting a [[wolf]].
** Its painting size is 3x3.
** The wolf on the painting refers to Dennis from [[A Minecraft Movie]].

=== Items ===
* [[Music Disc|Lava Chicken Music Disc]]
** A new music disc consist of a song "Lava Chicken" by Hyper Potions.
** Like any music discs, it can also be played on [[Jukebox|jukebox]].
** Can be obtained by killing a [[Chicken Jockey]].
*** The rider needs to be a baby zombie, if it is a drowned or zombified piglin then it won’t drop.
