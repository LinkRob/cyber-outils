'''Sulfur''' is an upcoming group of sickly yellowish-colored blocks that will be added in [[Chaos Cubed]]{{Upcoming}}. It consists of [[slab]], [[stairs]], and [[wall]] variants, and can be crafted into [[polished]], [[bricks]], and [[chiseled]] variants and [[Potent Sulfur|potent sulfur]].

=== Breaking ===
Like any [[stone]]-related blocks, sulfur blocks and their variants can only be mined using a [[pickaxe]], otherwise it drops nothing.

===  Natural generation ===
Sulfur and [[Potent Sulfur|potent sulfur]] generate naturally in [[sulfur caves]] alongside [[cinnabar]] blocks.

== Usage ==
Its slabs, stairs, walls, polished, bricks, and chiseled variants can only be obtained by crafting and [[stonecutter|stonecutting]].

=== Sulfur cubes ===
[[Sulfur cubes]] can only absorb full-sized sulfur blocks (including chiseled, polished, and brick variants), either picking it up or interacting with it, giving a regular/football (later slow bouncy{{Only|JE|short=1}}{{Upcoming|BE 26.30}}) effect{{Until|BE 26.30}} when hit. Sulfur cubes cannot absorb potent sulfur blocks.
