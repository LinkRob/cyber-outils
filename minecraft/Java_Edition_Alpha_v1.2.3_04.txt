'''Alpha v1.2.3_04''' is a version of [[Alpha]] that was released on November 26, 2010, which was the second part of a bug-fix update.<ref>[[wordofnotch:1690698977|Bug update #2. More coming early next week.]] – The Word of Notch, November 26, 2010</ref>

''Alpha v1.2.3_03 has no credible evidence that it exists.''
