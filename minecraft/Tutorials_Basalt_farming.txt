Basalt farming is the technique of using a basalt generator to produce [[basalt]] without needing to mine for it from [[Basalt Deltas|basalt deltas]] or from [[Basalt pillar|basalt pillars]]. Basalt generators work on the principle that when a [[lava]] stream encounters both [[Blue Ice|blue ice]] and [[Soul Soil|soul soil]], it will produce the block, which can be mined with a [[pickaxe]] and used for decoration. Please note that [[Soul Sand|soul sand]] will not work when making a basalt farm.
