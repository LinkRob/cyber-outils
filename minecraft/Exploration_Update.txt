The '''Exploration Update''' is the name for {{el|je}} version [[Java Edition 1.11|1.11]], a major update released on November 14, 2016.<ref name="1.11-pre1-blog">https://web.archive.org/web/20161114170041/http://www.mojang.com/2016/11/seek-out-the-exploration-update-111-on-pc-mac-now/</ref> The official name for the update, the ''Exploration Update'', was revealed on September 24, 2016, during the Opening Ceremony at [[MINECON 2016]].<ref>{{ytl|z27f6WkDWwk|MINECON 2016 Day 1 @ 2:32:27|Minecraft|September 24, 2016|t=2h32m27s}}</ref> During the "Casual Chat with Jens" panel at MINECON, he suggested that the name "Expedition Update" was more suitable to the theme of the update compared to the "Exploration Update".<ref>{{ytl|5b1kMBSb-tQ|MINECON 2016 Day 2 @ 4:31:22|Minecraft|September 25, 2016|t=4h31m22s}}</ref>

The main features of the update were announced on the second day of MINECON 2016, during "The Minecraft 1.11 Change Log" on September 25, 2016.<ref>{{ytl|Vm6oplvyyh0|MINECON 2016 The Minecraft 1.11 Change Log|Minecraft|September 25, 2016}}</ref> However, snapshots for the update initially began around two months prior to the reveal of the main features, with the features being added in a snapshot just a few days after MINECON. 

Mojang released the [[Java Edition 1.11-pre1|first (and only) pre-release]] for the Exploration Update on November 8, 2016.

== Notable features ==

{{main|Java Edition 1.11}}

=== Additions ===

* 3 new [[splash]]es.
* 2 new [[villager]] professions: 
** Cartographer
** Nitwit
* 2 new particles: <code>spit</code> and <code>totem</code>.
* 3 new NBT tags:
** <code>LocName</code>, <code>CustomPotionColor</code> and <code>ColorMap</code>.
* 2 new gamerules: <code>doWeatherCycle</code> and <code>maxEntityCramming</code>.
* 1 new command: {{cmd|locate}}.
* Curse [[enchantment]]s.
* 4 new mobs:
** [[Vindicator]]s, [[Evoker]]s, [[Vex]]es, and [[Llama]]s.
* 1 new generated structure: [[Woodland Mansion]].
* 2 new blocks: [[Observer]] and [[Shulker Box]].
* 8 new spawn eggs<!-- only counting the mobs that previously did not have spawn eggs, not the new mobs that were added -->.
* 4 new items:
** [[Shulker Shell]], [[Totem of Undying]], and the two [[Explorer Map]]s.

=== Changes ===

* Entity IDs, block IDs, block entity IDs, and item IDs are no longer case-sensitive. Plus, some names have had underscores added or have been completely rewritten.
* Lots of changes to NBT tags.
* Chat messages can now be 256 characters long.
* Various items, such as signs and wooden doors, can now be used as fuel for a furnace.
* Shields now block 100% of damage dealt in melee combat.

== Further revisions ==

*[[Java Edition 1.11.1|1.11.1]] added [[iron nugget]]s and the Sweeping Edge enchantment, among numerous changes and bug fixes.
*[[Java Edition 1.11.2|1.11.2]] was released soon thereafter to fix a crash involving [[chicken jockey]]s.

== Trailer ==
A trailer documenting some of the Exploration Update's features was released on Mojang's official YouTube channel on November 14, 2016. The trailer was made by Mojang themselves instead of Hat Films, who have made updated trailers and videos for Mojang in the past.
