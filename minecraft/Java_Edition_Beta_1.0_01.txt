'''Beta 1.0_01''' is a version of [[Java Edition Beta]] released on December 20, 2010,<ref>{{Word of Notch|2390438980|Minor patch 1.0_01. More bugfixes tomorrow.|December 10, 2010}}</ref> to fix some of the bugs and crashes in [[Java Edition Beta 1.0|Beta 1.0]].

== Fixes ==
; 3 bugs fixed
* Fixed double chest bug.
* Fixed rare crash on level load bug.
* Fixed the lighting bug from the chunks that are far away from the player during nighttime.
