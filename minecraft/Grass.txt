'''Grass''' (now called "Short Grass") is a [[solid]] [[plant]] [[block]] that has [[wheat seeds]] as a [[drop]]. Its [[color]] is [[biome]]-dependent, and matches the color of [[Grass Block|grass blocks]].

'''Tall grass''' ({{in|JE}}), also known as '''double tallgrass''' ({{in|BE}}), is a two-block high variety of grass.

'''Ferns''' are variants of grass found only in certain biomes and have the same characteristics as grass.

'''Large ferns''' are a two-block high variety of ferns. 

==Obtaining==
===Breaking===
When [[Breaking|broken]] normally, all variants have a 12.5% chance of dropping 1 unit of [[Wheat Seeds|wheat seeds]] when destroyed. This is affected by the [[Fortune]] enchantment, dropping 1-3, 1-5, or 2-7 wheat seeds respectively with Fortune I, II, and III. They can be obtained as an item by destroying them with [[shears]].

The double tall grass and large ferns drop two of the respective single blocks when broken using shears. By planting a single fern, growing it with bone meal and then using shears on the resulting tall fern, a single fern yields two ferns.

Placing any block on grass breaks the grass, and the grass does not drop seeds.

===Natural generation===
====Grass====
Grass generates in the form of [[random patch]]es, on [[grass block]]s in most [[biome]]s with grassy soil, like [[plains]] or [[forest]]s. Tall grass generates in plains, [[savanna]]s and [[lush caves]]. There is approximately 8.6 tall grass per chunk.

[[File:Grass farm at a woodland mansion.png|thumb|264px|Grass in a woodland mansion]]
A [[woodland mansion]] can have grass being farmed on [[farmland]] in the "wheat farm" room.

====Ferns====
Ferns occur naturally only in [[jungle]], [[taiga]], [[snowy taiga]] and [[old growth taiga]] biomes. Large ferns generate in the same biomes, excluding jungles. Large ferns can be found in some taiga and snowy taiga{{only|be|short=1}} village houses.

===Trading===
[[Wandering trader]]s can sell ferns for one [[emerald]].

===Post-generation===
[[Bone meal]] can be used on a grass block to grow grass, ferns{{only|bedrock}} and occasionally [[flower]]s on it and in its vicinity. It can also be used on grass or a fern to create a tall grass or large fern, respectively.

==Usage==
Double tall plants have a disproportionately large hitbox compared to other plants.<ref>{{bug|MC-33580|||WAI}}</ref> The only exception to this rule is [[seagrass|tall seagrass]].<ref>{{bug|MC-163896|||WAI}}</ref>

===Flower pots===
Ferns can be placed in [[flower pot]]s.

===Composting ===
Placing grass into a [[composter]] has a 30% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

Placing tall grass into a [[composter]] has a 50% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

Placing a fern into a [[composter]] has a 65% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

==Trivia==
* Grass cannot be directly set on [[fire]] from [[lightning]]. However, fire from nearby blocks can spread to it. Oddly enough, the top of grass can be lit but it does not burn this way. When any kind of grass '''is''' set on fire, the fire seems to hover just over the grass, as if it were a full block.
*Even though ferns do not produce seeds in real life, as they emit spores instead, they can drop seeds if broken.
*Grass is slightly offset from the block it is sitting on (as seen in the second screenshot). This causes fields of grass to appear more contiguous. 
** This offset is solely determined by x and z [[coordinates]], regardless of [[dimension]] or [[world seed]]{{verify}}. Based on this feature, it is possible to calculate the coordinates where a screenshot is taken, if the screenshot includes enough grass.
**This can sometimes cause grass to clip horizontally or vertically through block corners.<ref>{{bug|MC-63199}} – "Grass shows through block" resolved as "Works as Intended"<br>{{bug|MC-19005}} – "tall grass texture goes through blocks" resolved as "Won't Fix"</ref>
*Grass can appear in caves on dirt, even if there's no light source nearby.
*Using bone meal on grass in a spawn-protected area on a [[server]] still produces grass and flowers.
*The texture for grass is based on the grass texture from an early version of one of Notch's games, [https://s3.amazonaws.com/ld48/index.html Prelude Of The Chambered]
