'''0.25_01 SURVIVAL TEST''', an implicit retroactive renaming of the first reupload of [[0.25 SURVIVAL TEST]], so therefore labeled '''0.25 SURVIVAL TEST''' in-game, was a version of [[Survival Test]] released on September 3, 2009, at 16:15 UTC.<ref name="release">[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''A11:15:16 <Real> do arrows have increased damage now that they're limited, Notch?''" [...] "''A11:15:29 <08Notch> Real: Yes, in a few seconds. ;)''"</ref> This version is not available in the launcher.
