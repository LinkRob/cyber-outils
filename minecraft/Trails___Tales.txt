And, it’s the journey - your trails - that ties it all together. The Minecraft worlds belong to the players – and so do the stories, or tales, about those worlds.|[[Agnes Larsson]]|[{{article|the-trails---tales-update}} minecraft.net]}}

'''Trails & Tales''' is a major update released on June 7, 2023 as [[Java Edition 1.20]] and [[Bedrock Edition 1.20.0]].<ref>{{Mcnet|trails-tales-update-coming|The Trails & Tales update is coming|May 26, 2023|Sofia Dankis, Sophie Austi}}</ref> Its theme is "creativity and self-expression through representation and storytelling", focusing mostly on decorative blocks, customization options and resources for player-driven in-game storytelling and roleplay. It introduces [[Trail Ruins|trail ruins]], new passive mobs such as [[camel|camels]] and [[sniffer|sniffers]], an [[archaeology]] system, the [[cherry grove]] biome, and other features. The name of the update was revealed in ''[[Minecraft Monthly]]'' on March 2, 2023.<ref>{{Ytl|jQ58UEXrFzs|1.20 NAME REVEAL and more &vert; MINECRAFT MONTHLY|Minecraft|2 March 2023}}</ref>

== Further revisions ==
=== Java Edition ===
* [[Java Edition 1.20.1|1.20.1]] was released to fix critical bugs related to 1.20.
* [[Java Edition 1.20.2|1.20.2]] was released to add the Villager Trade Rebalance experimental toggle and the {{cmd|random}} command, and fix bugs.

=== Bedrock Edition ===
* [[Bedrock Edition 1.20.1|1.20.1]] was released to fix critical bugs related to 1.20.0.
* [[Bedrock Edition 1.20.10|1.20.10]] was released to bring more parity from {{JE}} and fix bugs.
* [[Bedrock Edition 1.20.12|1.20.12]] was released to fix critical bugs related to 1.20.10.
* [[Bedrock Edition 1.20.13|1.20.13]] was released to fix a crash in 1.20.12.
* [[Bedrock Edition 1.20.14|1.20.14]] was released exclusively on PlayStation to fix a crash in 1.20.13.
* [[Bedrock Edition 1.20.15|1.20.15]] was released to fix a crash in 1.20.13.
