'''Cave spiders''' are small variants of the [[spider]] that inflict [[Poison]] with their attacks. They are exclusively spawned by [[Monster Spawner|monster spawners]] found in [[mineshaft]]s.

== Spawning ==
[[File:SpiderSizeComparison.png|thumb|right|Size of a cave spider (left) compared to a regular spider (right).]]
Cave spiders spawn from monster spawners in mineshafts at a [[light]] level of 0. These monster spawners are surrounded by [[cobweb]]s in corridors of mineshafts.

== Drops ==
{{See also|Tutorials/Cave spider farming}}
When killed, a cave spider drops:
* 0–2 {{ItemLink|String}}. The maximum is increased by 1 per level of [[Looting]], for a maximum of 0-5 string with Looting III.
* {{xp|5}} when killed by a [[player]] or tamed [[wolf]].
*They also have a {{frac|1|3}} chance of dropping a {{ItemLink|Spider Eye}} when killed by a player or tamed wolf. The maximum amount of spider eyes is increased by 1 per level of [[Looting]]. The chance of a cave spider '''not''' dropping any spider eyes can be found using the formula <code>2 / (Looting Level + 3)</code>. For example, Looting III gives a {{frac|1|3}} chance of not dropping any spider eyes. All other amounts have an equal chance of occurring.

== Behavior ==

Cave spiders inherit their behavior from [[spider]]s:
* Neutral if under daylight or in [[light]] levels of 9 or above, otherwise they are hostile towards players and [[Iron Golem|iron golems]]. Once they become hostile, light does not affect them.
* Climb walls when needed and may sometimes reach a corner and become stuck in a climbing status, thus losing and becoming unable to be aggravated.
* Gravitate towards cobwebs.
* Attack by leaping.
* Are unaffected by [[cobweb]]s and [[Poison]].
* Can spawn [[spider jockey]]s.{{only|be|short=1}}

They have some distinctions from regular spiders:
* Can fit through a space that is one [[block]] wide and {{frac|1|2}} block tall.
* Can go through the spaces between two different types of (unconnected) [[fence]]s.
* Cannot spawn with [[status effect]]s.

Being [[arthropod]]s, they are weak against weapons with the [[Bane of Arthropods]] enchantment.

=== Poison ===

On Normal or Hard, cave spiders inflict {{EffectLink|Poison}} upon attacking.<ref>{{Tweet|jeb|109527533149630465|Your health hearts turn yellowish|September 2, 2011}}</ref> On Normal difficulty, the Poison lasts for 7 seconds and causes {{hp|6|poisoned=1}} damage. On Hard difficulty, it lasts for 15 seconds and causes {{hp|12|poisoned=1}}. Poison damage is not taken when the [[player]]'s health is at {{hp|1}}.

== Trivia ==

* While a cave spider's hitbox is only 13.9% the volume of a normal spider's, its model's volume is 34.3% as large. The shadow is even larger: compared to the normal spider's shadow it has an area of 49%.
