'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.120''' is a minor update to 1.21, released on October 28, 2025.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
[[File:New UI - Pause Menu (Bedrock Preview).png|thumb|300px|The newly redesigned [[pause menu]] (Preview).]]
* [[Pause menu]]
** Now redesigned with some buttons moved to the bottom with icons.
** Now has a large player skin.

=== Blocks ===
* [[Shelf]]
** Now emits vibrations when adding or removing redstone power to and from shelves.
* [[Copper Bars]]
** Changed the texture of weathered copper bars.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Copper Golem]]s
** Now emit scraping and waxing particles at its location.
* [[Wither Skeleton]]s
** Now picks up copper swords.
