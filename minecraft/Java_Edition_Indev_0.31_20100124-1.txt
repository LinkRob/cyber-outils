A version of '''[[Indev 0.31]]''' was released on '''January 24, 2010''', at 21:20 UTC.<ref>[[wordofnotch:351321921|Chests, and generic tile entity support]] – The Word of Notch, January 24, 2010</ref>

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[File:Chest JE1.png|32px]] [[Chest]]s
* Can be used to store blocks/items.
* Triple chests could be made by placing two chests with air in between, then placing a chest in the open space in between.

=== Items ===
; General
* The player can now drop whole stacks of items instead of one at a time.

=== Non-mob entities ===
; [[File:Arrow_JE2_BE1.png|32px]] [[Arrow]]s
* Now has a new sound effect.

=== World generation ===
; [[Indev house]]
* New generated structure.
* Player would spawn with chests full of every accessible block and tool in the game.
*Made out of [[Mossy Cobblestone]]

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[Lava]] 
* Once again sets flammable objects on fire

=== Items ===
; General
* Stacked blocks limit changed back to 99 items per stack.
* Now spin when dropped on the ground.{{verify|type=untestable|edition=java|could be the next version}} 

; [[File:Iron Axe JE3.png|32px]] [[Axe]]s
* Texture has been revised.
** The axe has been repositioned to the left instead of the right.</onlyinclude>
