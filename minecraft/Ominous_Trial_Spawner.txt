The '''ominous trial spawner''' is a stronger variant of the [[trial spawner]]. They will appear in the [[trial chambers]] if a player with [[Trial Omen]] enters the Chambers. This spawner will spawn stronger mobs, but when defeated, it will drop better loot.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Mining an ominous trial spawner with any items will drop nothing, even if mined with [[Silk Touch]], this makes ominous trial spawner unobtainable in Survival without glitches. Ominous trial spawner takes a long time to be mined and will take the same amount of time regardless of the items used to mine.
{{Breaking row|Ominous Trial Spawner|horizontal=1}}

==Ominous Trials==
{{Info needed|Trial Omen}}
First, a player with Bad Omen must enter a Trial Chamber. After that, the same player will be given Trial Omen. After this point, all [[Trial Spawner|Trial Spawners]] will become [[Ominous Trial Spawner|Ominous Trial Spawners]]. This is only for the time the Trial Omen effect is on a player in the Trial Chamber. Once Ominous Trial Spawners are activated, they will begin spawning harder enemies than normal Trial Spawners; however, when all monsters are defeated, the Ominous Trial Spawner may output an Ominous Trial Key, along with other goodies.

It should be noted that if a player desires to stop this Ominous Event, they may drink a [[Milk Bucket]], or if in multiplayer, the Trial Omen holder may leave the chamber. Alternatively, the player can [[Health#Death|die]] to remove the effect.
