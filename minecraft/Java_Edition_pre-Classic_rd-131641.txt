'''rd-131641''' is the sixth unreleased version of ''Minecraft'' [[pre-Classic]] (then known as ''Cave Game'') created on May 13, 2009 at 16:41-16:42 UTC. This version was never released to the public, and there isn't any footage either. Despite the fact that the development of Minecraft began some time before this version, older testimonies are not considered separate versions, as they were extremely unstable and, in fact, unplayable.

==Additions==
* Blue sky. (?)

==Imperfections== 
* Reduced performance
