== Differences from Beta 1.8 Prerelease ==
* [[Experience]]
** Has "skill points" over the experience bar
** For every level gained, the player gets three skill points
* [[Extreme Hills]]
** Does not generate with tall grass
* [[Village]]
** Can generate in any biome
* [[Rose]]
** Still generates in the world
