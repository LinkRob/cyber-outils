'''''A Minecraft Movie''''' is an American live-action animated film based on the video game ''[[Minecraft]]''. It was directed by {{w|Jared and Jerusha Hess|Jared Hess}} from a screenplay by Chris Bowman and {{w|Hubbel Palmer}}, and was released on April 4, 2025 by {{w|Warner Bros. Pictures}}.

A sequel titled ''A Minecraft Movie Squared'' has begun filming in New Zealand, and is set to premiere on July 23rd, 2027.

== Plot / Synopsis ==
{{Quote|Four misfits – Garrett “The Garbage Man” Garrison ({{w|Jason Momoa}}), Henry (Sebastian Hansen), Natalie ({{w|Emma Myers}}) and Dawn ({{w|Danielle Brooks}}) – find themselves struggling with ordinary problems when they are suddenly pulled through a mysterious portal into the Overworld: the bizarre, blocky wonderland that we know and mine extensively love.  To get back home, they’ll have to master this world (and protect it from evil things like piglins and zombies, too) while embarking on a magical quest with an unexpected, expert crafter, Steve ({{w|Jack Black}}).  Together, their adventure will challenge all five to be bold and to reconnect with the qualities that make each of them uniquely creative…the very skills they need to thrive back in the real world.|source=Warner Bros. Pictures and Mojang Studios}}

== Production ==

=== Development ===
On February 27, 2014, [[Notch]] posted on Twitter that [[Mojang Studios|Mojang Studios']] partnership with [[wikipedia:Warner Bros.|Warner Bros.]] about a ''Minecraft'' film was being "leaked", saying that he wanted to be the one to leak it out.<ref>{{tweet|notch|439164234099073024|Someone is trying leak the fact that we're working with Warner Brothers on a potential Minecraft Movie. I wanted to be the leak!|February 27, 2014}}</ref> That same day, it was reported that {{w|Roy Lee}} and {{w|Jill Messick}} were added as producers.<ref>[https://deadline.com/2014/02/minecraft-movie-warner-bros-lego-movie-producer-690664/ Video Game Sensation ‘Minecraft’ Coming To The Big Screen As Warner Bros Acquires Rights; ‘Lego’s’ Roy Lee And Jill Messick Producing] - Deadline Hollywood; February 27, 2014</ref>

On October 8, 2014, [[Vu Bui]] stated that the movie was in "its early days of development", saying that it was a "large-budget production" and that it was planned to release within three to four years.<ref name=guardian>[https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2014/oct/08/minecraft-movies-youtube-mojang-licensing Minecraft movie will be 'large-budget' but unlikely to arrive before 2017] – The Guardian; October 9, 2014</ref> Later that same month, it was reported that {{w|Shawn Levy}} would direct the film,<ref>[https://deadline.com/2014/10/minecraft-movie-shawn-levy-director-warner-bros-852503/ Shawn Levy Boards ‘Minecraft’ Movie] – Deadline Hollywood; October 16, 2014</ref> but soon left the project along with {{w|Kieran Mulroney|Kieran}} and Michele Mulroney, who were serving as writers, due to creative differences and scheduling conflicts. It was reported that Levy and the Mulroney's vision for the film didn't mesh well with what Mojang was looking for.<ref>[https://variety.com/2014/film/news/shawn-levy-leaves-warner-bros-minecraft-film-exclusive-1201374902/ Shawn Levy Leaves Warner Bros.’ ‘Minecraft’ Film (EXCLUSIVE)] – Variety; December 9, 2014</ref> Levy would later state in an interview that he envisioned a ''Minecraft'' film as an adventure movie with a ''Goonies'' flair.<ref>{{ytl|fp8D4MIkP70|'Minecraft' Movie: Why Director Shawn Levy Left|Wall Street Journal|December 12, 2014}}</ref>

On July 21, 2015, [[Vu Bui]] announced that {{w|Rob McElhenney}} would serve as the director of the film.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20150811145023/https://www.mojang.com/2015/07/weve-chosen-a-director-for-the-minecraft-movie/ We’ve chosen a director for the Minecraft movie!] – Mojang Studios, July 21, 2015 (archived)</ref>

On June 27, 2016, Mojang Studios announced the title and release date for ''Minecraft: The Movie'' by showing the front cover of the script and publicly announcing a release date of May 24, 2019.<ref name="2019 may release">[https://web.archive.org/web/20160629183903/http://www.mojang.com/2016/06/the-minecraft-movie-has-a-release-date/ The Minecraft movie has a release date!] – Mojang Studios, June 27, 2016 (archived)</ref>

On October 13, 2016, it was reported that {{w|Jason Fuchs}} would be writing the script alongside McElhenney.<ref>[http://variety.com/2016/film/news/jason-fuchs-minecraft-movie-writer-1201888253/ ‘Wonder Woman’ Scribe Jason Fuchs to Write Latest Draft of ‘Minecraft’ (EXCLUSIVE)] – Variety; October 13, 2016</ref>

On February 7, 2018, producer {{w|Jill Messick}} committed suicide at age 50, making ''Minecraft: The Movie'' the last movie she worked on.<ref name=MCMovieNewDirector>[https://variety.com/2019/film/news/peter-sollett-warner-bros-minecraft-1203105640/ Peter Sollett to Direct Warner Bros.’ ‘Minecraft’ Movie (EXCLUSIVE)] – Variety; January 11, 2019</ref>

On August 3, 2018, it was reported that Rob McElhenney and Jason Fuchs were replaced by writing duo {{w|Nee Brothers|Aaron and Adam Nee}}. The film was taken off the release schedule as a result of the replacement.<ref name="release date">[https://www.thewrap.com/minecraft-nee-brothers-rob-mcelhenny-release-date/ ‘Minecraft’ Movie Delayed: Rob McElhenney Out as Director, Nee Brothers Tapped to Write (Exclusive)] – The Wrap; August 3, 2018</ref> McElhenney would later state that his vision of the film was budgeted at $150 million, and that studios in Vancouver were booked for filming, but it "slowly died on the vine" when studio head Greg Silverman was replaced by {{w|Toby Emmerich}}, who had a different vision for the film.<ref>[https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-rob-mcelhenneys-150-million-minecraft-movie-fe/1100-6474007/ How Rob McElhenney's $150 Million Minecraft Movie Fell Apart After 2.5 Years Of Work] - GameSpot; February 23, 2020</ref>

On January 3, 2019, it was reported that {{w|Jim Berney}} has been appointed for the film's visual effects.<ref>[https://miamimorningstar.com/2019/01/05/minecraft-the-movie-after-significant-delays-finds-its-visual-effects-supervisor/ Minecraft: The Movie after Significant Delays Finds its Visual Effects Supervisor] – Miami Morning Star; January 5, 2019</ref> Later that same month, it was reported that {{w|Peter Sollett}} would write and direct the film, with Jon Berg being added as a producer, and {{w|Jon Spaihts}} being added as an executive producer, along with information about the plot revolving around the Ender Dragon destroying the Overworld.<ref name=MCMovieNewDirector/> This information would be confirmed the following April in a blog post made on the Minecraft website, announcing that the film would be released on March 4, 2022.

On June 24, 2019, it was reported that {{w|Allison Schroeder}} would be writing the latest script for the film.<ref>[https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/frozen-2-writer-allison-schroeder-tackling-minecraft-movie-1220710 'Frozen 2' Writer Allison Schroeder Tackling 'Minecraft' Movie (Exclusive)] - The Hollywood Reporter, June 24, 2019</ref>

On April 18, 2022, it was reported that {{w|Jared and Jerusha Hess|Jared Hess}} would direct the film, along with Chris Bowman and {{w|Hubbel Palmer}} writing the latest script. [[Lydia Winters]] and {{w|Mary Parent}} were also added as producers, while {{w|Cale Boyter}} was also added as an executive producer.<ref name=Direct>[https://thedirect.com/article/jason-momoa-minecraft-movie Jason Momoa Reportedly In Talks to Join Minecraft Movie] - The Direct; April 18, 2022</ref><ref name=Momoa>[https://deadline.com/2022/04/jason-momoa-in-talks-to-star-in-warner-bros-adaptation-of-minecraft-1235005112/ Jason Momoa In Early Talks To Star In ‘Minecraft’; Jared Hess Directing Video Game Adaptation For Warner Bros.] – Deadline Hollywood; April 18, 2022</ref>

On April 5, 2023, it was reported that the film is now slated to release on April 4, 2025.<ref name=Four425>[https://deadline.com/2023/04/aquaman-2-minecraft-wise-guys-color-purple-release-dates-1235318958/ ‘Aquaman 2’ Shifts Release Date Five Days Earlier, Warner Bros Dates ‘The Wise Guys’, ‘Minecraft’] – Deadline Hollywood; April 5, 2023</ref>

On May 23, 2023, it was reported that Jason Momoa and Torfi Frans Olafsson were added as producers.<ref name=Berry>[https://deadline.com/2023/05/minecraft-matt-berry-jason-momoa-warner-bros-1235370880/ Matt Berry In Talks To Join Jason Momoa In Warner Bros’ ‘Minecraft’] – Deadline Hollywood; May 23, 2023</ref>

=== Casting ===
In May 2023, {{w|Matt Berry}} entered negotiations to join the cast.<ref name="Berry"/> Later, in November, {{w|Danielle Brooks}} and Sebastian Eugene Hansen joined the cast.<ref name="BrooksHansen">[https://deadline.com/2023/11/minecraft-jason-momoa-danielle-brooks-sebastian-eugene-hansen-1235646560/ ‘Minecraft’: Warner Bros Movie Adds Danielle Brooks & Sebastian Eugene Hansen] – Deadline Hollywood; November 30, 2023</ref> In December, {{w|Emma Myers}} also joined the cast.<ref name="Myers">[https://deadline.com/2023/12/minecraft-emma-myers-wednesday-1235655987/ ‘Minecraft’: ‘Wednesday’ Star Emma Myers Digs Into Warner Bros Movie] – Deadline Hollywood; December 6, 2023</ref> In January 2024, {{w|Jack Black}} (who previously worked with Hess on ''{{w|Nacho Libre}}'') joined the cast, teasing his casting in the film via his official {{w|Instagram}} account.<ref>[https://variety.com/2024/film/news/jack-black-minecraft-movie-adaptation-1235859659/ Jack Black Joins Jason Momoa in ‘Minecraft’ Movie] - Variety; January 2, 2024</ref><ref name="Black">[https://deadline.com/2024/01/minecraft-jack-black-1235693816/ Jack Black Levels Up For ‘Minecraft’ At Warner Bros – The Dish] – Deadline Hollywood; January 2, 2024</ref> {{w|Jennifer Coolidge}}, {{w|Kate McKinnon}}, and {{w|Jemaine Clement}} were also cast in undisclosed roles.<ref name="Coolidge">[https://deadline.com/2024/01/minecraft-jennifer-coolidge-1235795092/ Warner Bros’ ‘Minecraft’ Movie With Jason Momoa Finds A Gem In Jennifer Coolidge] – Deadline Hollywood; January 17, 2024</ref><ref name="McKinnonClement">[https://deadline.com/2024/01/minecraft-kate-mckinnon-jemaine-clement-1235796568/ Kate McKinnon & Jemaine Clement Join Jason Momoa ‘Minecraft’ Gang At Warner Bros] – Deadline Hollywood; January 18, 2024</ref>

===Filming===
On June 19, 2023, it was reported that {{w|principal photography}} on the film would start on August 7 in {{w|New Zealand}},<ref>[https://movieweb.com/live-action-minecraft-film-to-start-filming-in-august/ Live-Action Minecraft Movie to Start Filming in August] – MovieWeb; June 20, 2023</ref> before filming was delayed in July due to the {{w|2023 SAG-AFTRA strike}}.<ref>[https://variety.com/2023/film/news/tv-shows-movies-affected-sag-actors-strike-2023-1235668990/ All the TV Shows and Movies Affected by the Actors Strike (Updating)] - Variety; July 14, 2024</ref> Following the conclusion of the strike in early November 2023, filming was reported to be aiming for an early 2024 start.<ref>[https://deadline.com/2023/11/actors-strike-deal-movies-restart-production-list-1235593653/ ‘Deadpool 3’, ‘Gladiator 2’, ‘Beetlejuice 2’, ‘Juror #2’ & ‘Venom 3’ Among Film Productions To Restart ASAP As Hollywood Gets Back To Work Post-Strike] – Deadline Hollywood; November 8, 2023</ref> With the announcement of Brooks and Hansen as part of the cast, filming was scheduled to begin in late December 2023.<ref name="BrooksHansen"/> Filming had begun by mid-January 2024 in Auckland and ended in mid-April, with Grant Major serving as the production designer.<ref>[https://www.sltrib.com/artsliving/2024/01/14/utah-filmmakers-talk-about-their/ What happened after ‘Napoleon Dynamite’ — and the big movie its Utah filmmakers are making next] – The Salt Lake Tribune; January 14, 2024</ref>
==Release==
The film is being distributed by Warner Bros. Pictures and is currently slated to release on April 4, 2025.<ref name=Four425/> It was originally scheduled to be released on May 24, 2019,<ref name="2019 may release"/> but was then taken off the release schedule in August 2018 due to the aforementioned replacement of McElhenney and Fuchs. In April 2019, it was announced that the film would be released on March 4, 2022, but it was again taken off the release schedule in October 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic, with ''{{w|The Batman (film)|The Batman}}'' taking its place.<ref>[https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/the-batman-delays-release-to-2022-as-warner-bros-shuffles-calendar 'The Batman' Delays Release to 2022 as Warner Bros. Shuffles Calendar] – The Hollywood Reporter; October 5, 2020</ref>

== TikTok/Online Trends and Memes ==
Following the release of the movie, multiple quotes below have been featured in TikTok trends, where if the quote is said, the audience starts cheering, clapping, yelling, and even throwing food around the theater. The most notable trend is “Chicken Jockey”, where people cheered and created havoc during the "CHICKEN JOCKEY" scene. In one of these situations, one person brought a live chicken into the theater. The "Chicken Jockey" trend has caused trouble for many theaters in the United States, United Kingdom, and Australia, promoting multiple measures to be taken to make sure that everyone has a safe and fun experience while watching the movie.<ref>https://abc7.com/post/minecraft-moviegoers-irvine-scream-throw-popcorn-during-viral-chicken-jockey-trend/16170997/</ref><ref>https://www.news18.com/movies/after-us-and-uk-minecraft-movies-chicken-jockey-scene-causes-mayhem-in-australian-cinemas-aa-9305446.html</ref>

The [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=41O_MydqxTU Steve's Lava Chicken] song has also been popular, with many cooking YouTubers like {{F|youtube|Nick_DiGiovanni|text=Nick DiGiovanni}}, {{F|youtube|albert_cancook}}, {{F|youtube|Bayashi_TV|text=Bayashi TV}}, and {{F|youtube|That_Little_Puff|text=That Little Puff}} trying to replicate the chicken Steve made in the movie with the song in the background.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/shorts/vASH8RAKLhQ</ref><ref>https://www.youtube.com/shorts/vkB7UQeKo2g</ref><ref>https://www.youtube.com/shorts/IAPhBcGI48o</ref><ref>https://www.youtube.com/shorts/RE5Lac2zCQc</ref> Later on, Mojang added a [[music disc]] named "Lava Chicken" to Minecraft, which is a remix of the song by HyperPotions.
