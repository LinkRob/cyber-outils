A '''bossbar''' is a bar that can track a [[Mob#Boss mobs|boss mob's]] [[health]] or the total health of [[raid]] mobs.

== Manual creation ==
{{Expand section}}
A bossbar can be shown using the {{Command|bossbar}} command. {{Only|Java}}

== Natural creation ==
=== Boss mobs ===
{{Missing information|section|the specific area near a wither where you get to see a bossbar}}
A bossbar appears in the top center of the screen when near a [[wither]] or [[ender dragon]].

For the ender dragon's bossbar to appear, the player must be within a [[Euclidean distance]] of 192 blocks from the [[coordinates]] (0.0, 192, 0.0) in [[the End]] dimension.{{Needs testing|Needs testing for Bedrock Edition}}

For the wither's bossbar to appear, the player must be loading the [[chunk]] the wither is in and be within 24 chunks and 3.5 blocks of a chunk the wither is in.

=== Raids ===
{{Missing information|section|the specific area near a raid center where you get to see a bossbar}}
When a player is within a 93 radius of the center of a [[raid]], a raid bossbar appears, showing the total health of all raid mobs present during the current wave.

== Parts ==
=== Color ===
The bossbar has colors depending on which [[Mob#Boss_mobs|boss mob]] or event is active.

The ender dragon has a color of pink. The wither has a dark purple{{Only|Java}} or pink{{Only|Bedrock}} bossbar. The raid bossbar has a color of red.

=== ID system ===
A bossbar has an ID, which can be used to do certain actions with one bossbar. An ID is not shown on the creation of the bossbar{{Verify}}, so you can't see it.

== Removing ==
{{Expand section}}
A bossbar can be disabled by using {{Command|bossbar remove ''<id>''}}.{{Info needed}}
