The '''Mob Vote''' was an annual event and community poll that allowed people to vote for a new mob to be added to the game in a future update. Each mob received its own video, introducing its behavior, biome(s), features, and impact on gameplay.

'''Mob Vote''' may refer to:

* The [[MINECON Earth 2017#Mob Vote|2017 Mob Vote]]
* The [[China Edition#History|2018 China Edition Mob Vote]]
* The [[Minecraft Live 2020#Mob Vote|2020 Mob Vote]]
* The [[Minecraft Live 2021#Mob Vote|2021 Mob Vote]]
* The [[Minecraft Live 2022#Mob Vote|2022 Mob Vote]]
* The [[Minecraft Live 2023#Mob Vote|2023 Mob Vote]]
The Minecraft [[Mob Vote]] has been officially discontinued as of September 9, 2024, by [[Mojang Studios|Mojang Studios.]] The mob vote was discontinued in 2024. On Social Media, [[Minecraft]] announced that they are "retiring the mob vote". In a [https://minecraft.net Minecraft] article on the Official Minecraft Website; 'The Future of Minecraft’s Development<ref>"[https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/the-future-of-minecrafts-development The future of Minecraft’s development]" by Ryan Cooper. Minecraft.net, September 9, 2024</ref>, Section 3; "A new format for Minecraft Live", or Paragraph 7, Ryan Cooper wrote "We’re changing the format for [[Minecraft annual events|Minecraft Live]], and '''retiring the mob vote''', to bring you something new.".
