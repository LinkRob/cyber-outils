'''Steve''' was a [[passive]] humanoid [[mob]], used for testing purposes, available from [[Java Edition Indev 0.31 20100129]] until [[Java Edition Indev 0.31 20100131|0.31 20100131]] (for 2 days).

== Spawning ==
[[File:In-game steve.png|thumb|Steve in the world, along with [[Black Steve]] to the left in red.]]
Steve spawned randomly in the world.{{more info}}

== Behavior ==
Steve was a passive [[mob]]. It had no animation, so it glided and jumped aimlessly around the world in the same pose, and would never pause or stop moving.

Upon [[death]], Steve would not fall over to the ground or flash red or white and appear to die, as most mobs do. It would just disappear shortly thereafter, and [[drops|drop]] the [[item]]s it was designated to drop.

== Drops ==
Upon death, Steve drops:
*{{ItemLink|String}} (0–2) (most common)
*{{ItemLink|Feather}} (0–2)
*{{ItemLink|Gunpowder}} (0–2)
*{{ItemLink|Flint and Steel}} (0–1) (extremely rare)
