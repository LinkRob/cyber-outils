The '''smiling creeper''' was a "hostile" [[mob]] present in the April Fool's [[Love and Hugs Update]].

== Spawn ==
Smiling creepers spawn under normal creeper spawning conditions.{{Verify}}

==Behavior==
[[File:Smiling Creeper Exploding.gif|thumb|A smiling creeper exploding.|left]]
A smiling creeper behaved like a normal [[creeper]], except that the explosion of a smiling creeper did not break blocks or cause damage. Instead, the smiling creeper dropped [[Flower|poppies]] that the player could not pick up.
