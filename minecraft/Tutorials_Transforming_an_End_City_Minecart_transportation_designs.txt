=== Below/above bridge ===

Feel free to replace the stairs on layer 2 with vertical slabs, or completely remove them (allowing for a viewing "window"). If you remove the stairs, you can also replace the purpur blocks on layer 2 with vertical End Rods.

For the above-bridge design, move the rails to layer 4 and remove the stairs on layer 2.

''All stairs are facing inwards, toward the bridge.''

''Stairs on layer 1 and 4 are upright, whereas stairs on layer 2 are upside-down.''
