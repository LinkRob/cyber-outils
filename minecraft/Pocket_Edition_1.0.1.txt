'''1.0.1''' was an update to {{el|pe}} released exclusively on iOS to fix game crashing issues specific to the platform.<ref>{{cite|url=https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360004168751|title=MCPE iOS ONLY - 1.0.1}}</ref>

== Fixes ==
* Fixed a crash that occurred when a player tapped the Mojang logo when launching the game.
* Fixed assorted other crashes due to back-end issues.
