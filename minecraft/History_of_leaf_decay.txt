This page documents ''[[Java Edition]]''&#8203;'s several historical [[Leaves|leaf]] decay algorithms.

== Indev 20100218 - Indev 20100223 ==
Leaves will NOT decay if:
* They are within a 5x5x2 box around a log with the log in the bottom center. (Note that the leaf does not have to touch the log)
* They are on top of a solid block.
* They are on top of a leaf that is unable to decay due to the reasons above.

If these conditions are not met, the leaf decays.

== Alpha v1.0.6_01 - Alpha v1.1.2_01 ==

In [[Java Edition Alpha|Alpha]], leaves would decay using an algorithm that checks whether it has a [[solid]] or leaf block underneath it or is adjacent to a solid [[block]]. If it doesn't, it must be connected to a leaf block that is also supported as long as it is no further than five blocks away from said support.
