'''{{BlockLink|Grass}}''' (now called "Short Grass") is a non-solid block that spawns abundantly in the Overworld.

'''Grass''' may also refer to:

* {{BlockLink|Grass|Short Grass}}, a natural vegetative block
** {{BlockLink|Tall Grass}}, a taller variant of grass
* {{BlockLink|Grass Block}}, a block with grass on top of it
* {{BlockLink|Dirt Path}}, a path block used for villages, formerly known as "Grass Path" 
* {{BlockLink|Seagrass}}, a sea variant
* {{BlockLink|Fern}}, a small natural vegetative block
** {{BlockLink|Large Fern}}, a taller variant of fern
* [[Spring to Life|Dry Grass]], a natural vegetative block found in arid [[biome]]s
** [[Spring to Life|Short Dry Grass]], a shorter variant of dry grass
* {{BlockLink|Nylium}}, commonly referred to as "nether grass"
* {{BlockLink|Mycelium}}, purple, alien-like grass that spawns in Mushroom Fields
* {{BlockLink|Podzol}}, a block mostly found in old-growth taiga and bamboo jungle biomes
