The '''Vibrant Visuals''' is a graphics mode added in [[Chase the Skies]]. It adds water shaders, specular highlights, emissive textures, and directional lighting and shadows. It was revealed on Minecraft Live - March 2025.<ref>[https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=jGNgh5zEFkY Minecraft LIVE - March 2025]</ref>

While Vibrant Visuals is currently exclusive to some [[Bedrock Edition]] devices, it is planned to be added in [[Java Edition]] in the coming months.

== Availability ==
Currently, Vibrant Visuals only supports on high-end devices. [[Mojang Studios]] also promised to optimize Vibrant Visuals so it could run on low-end devices.{{Citation needed}}
