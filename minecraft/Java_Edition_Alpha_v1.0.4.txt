'''Alpha v1.0.4''', also known as '''Seecret Friday 4''',<ref name="blogpost" /> is the fourth [[seecret update]] which added [[winter mode]], [[snowfall]], and two new [[block]]s. This is the earliest [[Java Edition Alpha|Alpha]] version to be available in the [[Minecraft Launcher|launcher]].

==Additions==
===Blocks===
; [[File:Ice JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Ice]]
* Is slippery.
* Generates when water is exposed to the sky on world generation.

; [[File:Snow (layers 1) JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Snow]]
* Spawns on top of most blocks when exposed to snowfall.
* Cannot be obtained yet.

===Body positions===
* [[Sitting]].

===World generation===
; Winter maps
* Added a new map type, featuring:
** Snowfall.
** Blocks exposed to sky generate with [[Snow|a thin layer of snow]] and those exposed to snowfall slowly get covered by it.
** [[Water]] exposed to snowfall generates as [[ice]].
*** However, water found on gravel beaches is not entirely frozen upon generation.
** Upon world creation there is a 25% chance for it to become a winter world.<ref>A winter world is defined by the boolean [[NBT format|NBT tag]] "SnowCovered=1" in level.dat.</ref>

==Changes==

===Non-mob entities===
; [[File:Minecart JE2 BE1.png|32px]] [[Minecart]]s
* Removed the [[chest]] model.

===General===
; [[Main menu]]
* Added an animated title screen.
* Logo is now made out of stone, rather than cobblestone, and has been changed from [[File:Java Edition logo 1.png|x20px]] to [[File:Java Edition logo 2.png|x20px]].

=== Gameplay ===
; [[Transportation|Movement]]
* The player's maximum jump length decreased from 3 to 2 blocks horizontally.

== Fixes ==
;3 bugs fixed
* Fixed a bug in the [[mob]] spawn code.
* The player no longer suffocates when they ride a minecart through a two block tall space.
* Breaking minecarts no longer crashes the game.</onlyinclude>

== Bugs ==
* Sometimes, when walking in the snow, the sound of a block underneath is played.
