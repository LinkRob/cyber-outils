'''{{BlockLink|Tuff}}''' is a natural block that spawns in [[Ore (feature)|ore blobs]] underground in the Overworld. [[Trial Chambers]] are mostly made of tuff and its variant blocks.

* {{BlockSprite|Polished Tuff Slab}}{{BlockSprite|Polished Tuff Stairs}}{{BlockSprite|Polished Tuff Wall}}, their slab, stairs, and wall variants.
* {{BlockLink|Tuff Bricks}}, the bricks version of tuff or polished tuff.
* {{BlockSprite|Tuff Brick Slab}}{{BlockSprite|Tuff Brick Stairs}}{{BlockSprite|Tuff Brick Wall}}, their slab, stairs, and wall variants.
* {{BlockLink|Chiseled Tuff}} and {{BlockLink|Chiseled Tuff Bricks}}, the chiseled version of tuff and tuff bricks respectively.
