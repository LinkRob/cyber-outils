'''v0.13.2 alpha''' is an update to Pocket Edition that fixed bugs in v0.13.1 alpha and added a new [[skin pack]].

A build of v0.13.2 alpha was accidentally uploaded to the Google Play Store on January 29, 2016. This build was known to cause numerous crashes.<ref>{{tweet|tomcc|693323345656438784|There’s been another mishap with the Google Store, somewhat 0.13.2 got released, but it crashes. Do *NOT* update. We're trying to fix it!|30 Jan 2016}}</ref>

==Additions==

===Skin packs===
* {{Version exclusive}}: [[Skin/Skin pack#Journey To The West Skin Pack|Journey To The West Skin Pack]].

==Fixes==
* [[Zombie]]'s and [[skeleton]]'s [[armor]] renders correctly again.
**[[Zombie pigmen]]'s armor still does not, however.
* [[Zombie pigmen]]'s hat layer now renders correctly.
* [[Ghast]]s now open their mouths when attacking.
* [[Wolves]]' collars now show their correct colors.
* Arm is now animated with items correctly.
* Fixed lag in [[the Nether]].
