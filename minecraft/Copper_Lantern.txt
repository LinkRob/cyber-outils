A '''copper lantern''' is a [[copper|copper variant]] block that was added in [[The Copper Age]]. Like [[Lantern|normal lanterns]], it gives off light when exposed to darkness and oxidizes like most [[copper]] blocks.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Copper lanterns can be easily broken by hand, but using any pickaxe can break it faster.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Copper Lantern|pickaxe}}
A copper lantern also drop as an item if:
* The attachment block is removed or destroyed.
* [[Water]] or [[lava]] flows into it.
* a [[piston]] tries to push it or moves a block into its space.

== Usage ==
Like [[lantern]]s, copper lanterns can be placed on top or bottom portion of any solid blocks.

=== Oxidation ===
Like most [[Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper lanterns undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
* Unoxidized - Normal stage
* Exposed
* Weathered
* Oxidized
Only lightning and [[axe]]s can reverse the oxidation process. Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper lantern prevents oxidation.
