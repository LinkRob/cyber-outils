'''Wither skeletons''' are tall black variants of [[skeleton]]s equipped with stone [[sword]]s that inflict the poison-like [[Wither (effect)|Wither]] effect. They are found exclusively in [[nether fortress]]es and are the only source of [[Head|wither skeleton skulls]], as well as the only [[renewable]] source of [[coal]].

== Spawning ==
Wither skeletons spawn in [[nether fortress]]es at a [[light#Light level|light level]] between 0 and 7, in [[Spawn#Pack spawning|groups]] of up to 4. They are the only mobs that can spawn inside [[flower|wither roses]] due to their immunity to the damaging effect.

====Halloween====
{{exclusive|java|section=1}}
On Halloween, wither skeletons have a 22.5% chance of spawning wearing a {{BlockLink|carved pumpkin|link=pumpkin}} and a 2.5% chance of spawning wearing a {{BlockLink|jack o'lantern}}. This is not dropped, except when killed with [[Looting]], which have a chance of 1-3% to drop depending on the [[Looting]] level.

====Withers====
{{exclusive|Bedrock
|section=1}}
The [[wither]] spawns 3-4 wither skeletons when below half health on normal and hard [[Difficulty|difficulties]].

====Jockeys====
{{exclusive|bedrock|section=1}}
[[Spider]]s and [[cave spider]]s spawned in [[the Nether]] have a 0.8% chance of spawning a wither skeleton on their back and form a [[spider jockey]]. Spiders do not naturally spawn in this dimension.

== Drops ==
* 0 to 2 {{ItemLink|bone}}s. The maximum drop is increased by 1 per level of [[Looting]], for a maximum of 0 to 5 bones with Looting III.
* There is a {{frac|1|3}} chance to drop a single {{ItemLink|coal}}. Each level of Looting increases the maximum drop by 1, so that Looting I drops 0-2 coal with an average of 0.75 coal per kill, Looting II drops 0-3 coal with an average of 1.2 coal per kill and Looting III drops 0-4 coal with an average of 1.67 coal per kill.
* 2.5% chance to drop a {{BlockLink|wither skeleton skull|link=head}} when killed by a player or tamed [[wolf]]. Looting increases the chance by 1% per level{{only|java}} or 2% per level{{only|bedrock}}, for a maximum of 5.5%{{only|java}} or 8.5%{{only|bedrock}} chance with Looting III. Their skull is always dropped if killed by a [[Creeper#Charged creeper|charged creeper]], though only one head{{only|java}} or all heads{{only|bedrock}} drop if many are killed by the same charged creeper explosion.
* 8.5% chance of dropping their unenchanted stone [[sword]] which has a random [[durability]]. This chance increases by 1% per level of Looting, for a maximum of 11.5% chance with Looting III.
* {{xp|5}} experience orbs when killed by a [[player]] or tamed [[wolf]], and an additional {{xp|1|3}} is dropped per piece of naturally-spawned equipment that ''does not'' drop upon death.
** Because all wither skeletons spawn with a stone sword, each of them effectively drop {{xp|6|8}} if the sword is not dropped.
** If the wither skeleton ''does'' drop its stone sword, it drops the default {{xp|5}} instead.
* If a wither skeleton wearing a [[carved pumpkin]] or [[jack o'lantern]] is killed using a tool enchanted with Looting, there is a chance equivalent to the level of Looting used to drop the carved pumpkin or jack o'lantern, a maximum of a 3% chance of a drop.

== Behavior ==
Wither skeletons wander aimlessly when idle, but [[sprint]] to attack [[player]]s, [[snow golem]]s, [[iron golem]]s, [[Turtle#Predators|baby turtles]], [[piglin]]s, and [[piglin brute]]s. They flee from [[wolves]] but retaliate if attacked. 

When an [[entity]] is attacked by a wither skeleton in any [[difficulty]], it is inflicted with the [[Wither (effect)|Wither effect]] for ten seconds, which turns the [[health]] bar black ({{hp|20|notag=1|withered=1}}) and decreases it by {{hp|1|withered=1}} every two seconds, similarly to [[Poison]], except that it can cause death.

Some wither skeletons may have the capacity to pick up dropped gear in Normal and Hard difficulties. The chance depends on [[Difficulty#Regional difficulty|regional difficulty]], with up to 55% of them capable of picking up [[sword]]s higher than stone [[tiers|tier]] plus any [[armor]]. Wither skeletons never take {{ItemLink|bow}}s; {{in|Java}}, if they hold a bow (which can be done with {{cmd|/summon wither_skeleton ~ ~ ~ {HandItems:[{id:bow,Count:1b}]} }}) they shoot flaming [[arrow]]s even if their bows are not enchanted. They cannot use [[crossbow]]s.

Wither skeletons are immune to [[fire]] and the Wither effect. They still seek shade or [[water]] during daylight if they are in the [[Overworld]] although they do not burn in [[sun]]light. Like most [[undead]] mobs, they sink in water but do not drown, are healed by [[Instant Damage]] and harmed by [[Instant Health]] effects, are immune to [[Regeneration]] and [[Poison]] effects and are not attacked by the [[wither]]. 

{{IN|bedrock}}, the player can burn them by throwing a [[trident]] enchanted with [[Channeling]] during a [[thunderstorm]].
