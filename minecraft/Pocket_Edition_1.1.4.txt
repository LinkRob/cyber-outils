'''1.1.4''' is an update for {{el|pe}} which added new Marketplace content.<ref>{{cite|url=https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360004127552|title=MCPE/WIN10/GEAR VR - 1.1.4}}</ref> While it was not the last update to 1.1, it was the last one before the beta builds of [[Bedrock Edition 1.2|1.2.0]] started.

== Additions ==
; [[Marketplace]]
* Battle & Beasts Skin Pack
* Pet Skin Pack
* Cyber Space Skin Pack

== Fixes ==
* Fixed lag on Android devices when accessing [[resource pack]]s.
* Fixed some crashes occurring when launching the game.
* Fixed a crash when loading into a world while holding a [[clock]] or a [[compass]].
