'''1.88''' was an update to [[PlayStation 4 Edition]] released on February 26, 2019, which added more features of [[Village and Pillage]].<ref>{{tweet|4JStudios|1100319558894792706|A new update for Minecraft: PlayStation 4 Edition is rolling out now. New flowers, walls, stairs, slabs, signs and more! Full changes are here|February 26, 2019}}</ref> This update is equivalent to [[Bedrock Edition 1.9.0]], only missing the experimental gameplay features such as [[pillager]]s.

== Additions ==

=== Blocks ===
; [[Flower]]s
* Added new flowers: [[Lily of the Valley]] and [[Cornflower]].

; [[Sign]]s
* Added new [[sign]] variants: spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, and dark oak.

;Smooth blocks
* Added [[smooth stone]], [[sandstone|smooth sandstone]], smooth red sandstone and [[block of quartz|smooth quartz block]].

;[[Shulker Box]]es
* Added an undyed variant of the shulker box. All previous purple shulker boxes are now undyed shulker boxes.
* The purple shulker box texture has been changed to the new color palette.

; [[Stairs]], [[slab]]s, and [[wall]]s
* Added new stair, slab, and wall variants:
** Stone stairs and slabs.
** Granite stairs, slabs, and walls.
** Polished granite stairs and slabs.
** Diorite stairs, slabs, and walls.
** Polished diorite stairs and slabs.
** Andesite stairs, slabs, and walls.
** Polished andesite stairs and slabs.
** Sandstone walls.
** Smooth red sandstone stairs and slabs.
** Smooth quartz stairs and slabs.
** Brick walls.
** Stone brick walls.
** Mossy stone brick stairs, slabs, and walls.
** Nether brick walls.
** End stone brick stairs, slabs, and walls.
** Prismarine walls.
** Red sandstone walls.
** Red nether brick stairs, slabs, and walls.
** Smooth sandstone stairs and slabs.
** Mossy cobblestone stairs and slabs.
** Cut sandstone slabs and cut red sandstone slabs.

=== General ===
; Mash-up packs
* Added Steven Universe Mash-Up Pack.

== Changes ==

=== Blocks ===
; [[Cauldron]]s
* Can now hold [[lava]].
; [[Tripwire]]
* Is now activated when broken, unless broken with [[shear]]s.
; [[Scaffolding]]
* Can now be placed on [[Glass|glass block]]s.
* More blocks can be placed on [[scaffolding]], including [[mob head]]s, and [[flower pot]]s.

=== Mobs ===
; [[Panda]]s
* Now panic when set on fire.

=== General ===
* A notification is now sent to chat when a tamed animal dies.
* Added "Immediate Respawn" host option.
* Added several new loading screen tips and trivia.
* Added several new splash texts to the main menu.
