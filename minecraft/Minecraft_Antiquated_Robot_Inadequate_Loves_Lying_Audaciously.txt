The '''Minecraft Antiquated Robot Inadequate Loves Lying Audaciously''', also known as '''MARILLA''' or '''M.A.R.I.L.L.A''', is a character in the official ''[https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1sELGmy5jp5fQUugmuYlXQ Minecraft]'' [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1sELGmy5jp5fQUugmuYlXQ YouTube channel] voiced by [https://marillawex.com/2019/06/10-things-you-didnt-know-about-minecraft/ Marilla Wex].

She hosted the series [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHdY_46KnYs3_DJBKiXhLg6vs7o9YFGy ''Ten Things You Probably Didn't Know About Minecraft''] and then co-hosted [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHdY_46KnYutPL4boezjUogDLKRbzXmE ''The Secrets of Minecraft''] with [[Narrator (YouTube)|Narrator]] from [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHdY_46KnYtODUzKsnX_KWI4fAdUVdGk ''How We Make Minecraft''].

== Appearance ==
MARILLA is an old box computer with a creamy tan colored base, dark brown casing, a green and white screen, a gray keyboard and a "Team Player Mode" switch on the back. A ROM cartridge with ''[[Minecraft]]'' on it can be seen sticking out and a joystick sitting at the side connected by a cable. She is able to run programs such as ''Minecraft'' on herself.

== Personality ==
MARILLA is lighthearted, sarcastic, confidant, and audacious. She is quick to give false trivia and can be a little superstitious but always does so with positive boldness. She likes [[endermen]].

== Trivia ==
* The introductory sentences has minor differences depending on the video, some being in the greetings: "Hello!", "Hello there!", or "Hello again!" along with the glitching. The modem sound is also occasionally "[MODEM SOUND VARIATION 3582]" instead of the usual "[OLD MODEM SOUND]".<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YUM7XiEX1DI</ref><ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OS5LMnCg5_g&list=TLGGlz9En6J79HcyNjA2MjAyMg</ref>
* MARILLA is the second computer character in the [[Minecraft (franchise)|''Minecraft'' franchise]] after [[Minecraft Story Mode:PAMA|PAMA]].
* The [[squid]] overlords taking over ''Minecraft'' that MARILLA occasionally mentions may be a reference to the [[wikipedia:Reptilian conspiracy theory|antisemitic conspiracy theory of reptilian overlords]] controlling Earth.
* MARILLA implied at one point that a witch offered her three magic beans in exchange for her body being trapped in a computer for all eternity.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2zrD0ijjbUk</ref>
* The description card on ''Minecraft''<nowiki/>'s YouTube channel has her as "Artificial Untelligence",<sup>{{Sic}}</sup> "Sentient computer/worryingly immortal", "Excellent liar", etc. This changes each episode.
* Her co-host, [[Narrator (YouTube)|Narrator]], believes that "MARILLA was created a few thousand years before rocks were invented".<ref>{{ytl|Z4haZTbU9ZA|The Secrets of Minecraft's Deep Dark @ 0:54|Minecraft|June 24, 2022|t=67s}}</ref>
* Her name is likely based on her actress Marilla Wex, as seen by the acronym having random words that spell out "MARILLA".
* The glitchy introductions feature MARILLA displaying a mockup of the boot loader 'Kickstart' from the Commodore Amiga line of computers, a slight nod to our underground demoscene roots.
