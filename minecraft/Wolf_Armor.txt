The '''wolf armor''' is an [[armor]] item for [[tamed wolves]]. It is a [[Dye|dyable]] armor that can be is crafted with [[armadillo scute]]s.

== Usage ==
[[File:Wolf armor colors.png|400px|thumb|Showcase of basic wolf armor colors.]]

{{Control|Using}} wolf armor on a tamed wolf will cause the wolf to equip it, in a similar manner to [[Horse Armor|horse armor]]. While a wolf is using wolf armor, it gains {{Armor|11}} armor points. In order to remove armor from the wolf, the player has to right click the wolf using shears, which grants the "Shear Brilliance" achievement.

=== Dyeing wolf armor ===
Wolf armor can be dyed using the [[cauldron]]. Similar to [[Dye#Dyeing armor|dying of leather armor]], several colors can be used at once to create various shades.
