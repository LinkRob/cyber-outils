** {{nbt|byte|Count}}: Number of [[item]]s stacked in this inventory slot. Any item can be stacked, including tools, armor, and vehicles. Range is -128 to 127. Values of 1 are not displayed in-game. Values below 1 are displayed in red.
** {{nbt|string|id<sup>[lowercase]</sup>}}: [[Data values|Item/Block ID]]. If not specified, Minecraft changes the item to [[stone]] when loading the chunk or summoning the item.
** {{nbt|compound|tag}}: Additional information about the item, discussed more in the subsections of [[Player.dat format#Item structure|the item structure section]]. This tag is optional for most items.
