A '''[[furnace]]''' is a block used to smelt items. It may also refer to:

== ''Minecraft'' ==
* {{BlockLink|Blast Furnace}} – a block that can only smelt metal and ore but twice as fast
* {{ItemLink|Minecart with Furnace}} – a ''Java Edition'' entity which can boost minecart speed
* {{BlockLink|Smoker}} – a block that can only smelt food but twice as fast
* {{BlockLink|Campfire}} – a block that can only smelt food
** {{BlockLink|Soul Campfire}} – soul variant of campfire

== ''Minecraft Earth'' ==
* {{EntityLink|Furnace Golem}} – a variant of an iron golem.
