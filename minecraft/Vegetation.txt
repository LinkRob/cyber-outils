'''Vegetations''' are [[feature]]s composed of plants and fungi including [[grass]], [[bush]]es, [[fern]]s, [[flower]]s, and [[mushroom]]s.

==Types==

====Grass and Fern====

Note that tall grass and grass can also generate in flower patches in meadow biomes. See [[#Flowers]].
