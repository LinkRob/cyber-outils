The '''swaggiest stairs ever''' is a joke [[block]] introduced in [[Java Edition 20w14∞]] which is the [[stair]] variant for the [[netherite block|Block of Netherite]].

== Obtaining ==
This block is available in the creative inventory, and naturally generate in the "missing" dimension. It is also seen in other dimensions, one of which is the "gallery" dimension, although it is difficult to find the stairs hidden among thousands of other blocks.

=== Breaking ===
This block requires a diamond or netherite pickaxe to mine. If it is mined with anything else, it drops nothing.

=== Natural generation ===
This block naturally generates in the "missing" dimension as the stairs leading up to the chest containing the [[footprint]]s. 7 stairs generate here.

== Issues ==
'''Swaggiest Stairs Ever''' blocks are an unsupported [[item]] due to being an [[Wikipedia:April Fools' Day|April Fools']] joke, and therefore such issues relating to them will not be fixed.

== Trivia ==
* Despite being an April Fools' joke block, it made an appearance in [[Minecraft Dungeons]] as part of the [[MCD:Nether portal|Nether Portal]] structure on [[MCD:Ancient Hunts|Ancient Hunts]].
* It is often mistakenly called as "Netherite stairs" or "Block of Netherite Stairs" due to its similar appearance to the Block of Netherite
