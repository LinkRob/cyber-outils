'''Desert wells''' are [[features]] made of [[sandstone]].

==Generation==
Desert wells generate in [[desert]] biomes. They have a {{frac|1|1000}}{{only|java|short=1}} or {{frac|1|500}}{{only|bedrock|short=1}} chance to be generated in the given chunk. It is possible for a well to generate around a [[cactus]]. The well is considered a [[feature]] rather than a [[Generated structures|structure]], and therefore it generates with the "Generate Structures" option disabled.<ref>{{bug|MC-143151}}</ref>

==Construction==
{{main|Desert Well/Structure}}
Desert wells are built of [[sandstone]] blocks and [[slab]]s with a [[water]] source inside. Underneath the water source, there are some [[suspicious sand]] blocks that can be excavated using a [[brush]] to get various items.
