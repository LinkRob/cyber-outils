'''Redstone Wire Ltd''' was the company formed by [[User:Citricsquid|citricsquid]], [[User:Andrewgodwin|aera]] and [[User:WedTM737@legacy41471684|WedTM]] to manage the [[Minecraft Forum]], [[Minecraft Wiki (website)|Minecraft Wiki]], Minecraft Museum and MinecraftServers.com<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20101013142940/http://redstonewire.com/</ref><ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20160407065746/https://rmbls.net/post/16577257153/history-of-minecraftforumnet-and</ref> After the acquisition of Minecraft Wiki and Minecraft Forums by Curse on November 15, 2010, the company ceased operations and was officially dissolved on September 6, 2011.<ref>http://data.companieshouse.gov.uk/doc/company/07393581</ref>
