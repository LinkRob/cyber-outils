The '''MinecraftEdu Launcher''' was the launcher for ''[[MinecraftEdu]]''. It served as the way to play ''MinecraftEdu'' until version [[MinecraftEdu 1.8.9 Build 3|1.8.9 Build 3]].

Since the release of ''MinecraftEDU'' 1.8.9 Build 3, the launcher has not received any updates, the launcher has since been replaced by ''[[Minecraft Education]]''.

On an unknown date, the launcher was removed from download, and was replaced by the ''Minecraft Education'' launcher.

== ''MinecraftEdu'' ==
''MinecraftEdu'' was the only available option in the launcher.
=== ''Minecraft Education'' ===
The now current version of ''MinecraftEdu'', ''Minecraft Education'', is the current launcher after surpassing the ''MinecraftEdu'' launcher, it is unknown if the original launcher converts to the ''Minecraft Education'' launcher.
