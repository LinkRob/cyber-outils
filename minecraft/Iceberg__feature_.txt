An '''iceberg''' is a [[feature]] composed of blue ice or packed ice. There is also a terrain feature known as an iceberg, see [[Terrain features#Iceberg]].

== Generation ==
Iceberg feature generates in [[frozen ocean]]s and [[deep frozen ocean]]s. They consist of [[packed ice]] or sometimes [[blue ice]], and there's a probability of 30% that an iceberg feature is topped with [[snow block]]s. {{IN|java}}, each chunk has an {{frac|1|16}} chance to try to generate a packed iceberg, and has an {{frac|1|200}} chance to try to generate a blue iceberg. {{IN|bedrock}}, each chunk has an {{frac|1|8}} chance to try to generate an iceberg, which has an {{frac|1|100}} chance of being a blue iceberg.

Unlike [[Terrain features#Iceberg|iceberg terrain feature]], iceberg feature is usually small. Iceberg terrain features generate in a wide variety of shapes and sizes, ranging from small islands to giant mountain-like icebergs.

Iceberg features have two types: circle-shaped and ellipse-shaped. There's a probability of 30%{{only|java|short=1}}/20%{{only|bedrock|short=1}} that an iceberg feature is ellipse shaped. For a circle-shaped icebergs, its radius is from 0 to 11; its max height above sea level has a 90% chance of being 3—17{{only|java|short=1}}/3—13{{only|bedrock|short=1}} and a 10% chance of being 10—42{{only|java|short=1}}/10—34{{only|bedrock|short=1}}; its max depth below sea level is from 3 to 18; it has a 30% chance of being carved. For an ellipse-shaped iceberg, the length of its semimajor axis is 7—11{{only|java|short=1}}/8—15{{only|bedrock|short=1}}; the length of its semiminor axis is from 3 to 5; its max height above sea level is 6—11{{only|java|short=1}}/6—9{{only|bedrock|short=1}}; its max depth below sea level is from 6 to 18; it has a 90% chance of being carved.

Packed ice iceberg generates sometimes with [[blue ice]] features attached to it. A blue ice feature is a pile of blue ices generating adjacent to a [[packed ice]] and at or below the surface of water, and may replace some existing packed ice within an iceberg.
