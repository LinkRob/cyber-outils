'''Polished tuff''' is polished variation of [[tuff]]. Matching other Polished Variations of Blocks

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Polished tuff drops as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, it drops nothing.
{{Breaking row|Polished Tuff|Pickaxe|Wood|horizontal=1}}

=== Natural generation ===
Polished tuff blocks generate naturally in [[trial chambers]], usually as ceilings and surrounding an [[ominous vault]].
