'''Infested''' is an effect that gives a 5% chance to spawn 1-3 [[silverfish]] when a mob with that effect takes damage. This potion effect is mainly used in [[Trial Chambers]], along with [[Weaving]], [[Oozing]], and [[Wind Charged]]. The infested effect occurs when the player is hit by a splash potion of weaving while the [[Trial Omen]] effect is activated. (Or by brewing it, then drinking it/using gunpowder to make your own splash potion/using [[Dragon's Breath]] to make it a [[Lingering Potion]].)
