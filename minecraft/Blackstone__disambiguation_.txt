'''Blackstone''' is a natural block that is found in [[The Nether]], especially in [[Blob|blobs]] and mainly in [[Basalt Deltas|basaltic delta biomes]]. Blackstone can be used to craft stone tools, weapons, furnaces, etc. [[Bastion Remnant|Bastion remnants]] are mostly made of blackstone and its variant blocks.
