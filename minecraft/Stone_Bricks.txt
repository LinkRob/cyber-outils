{{BlockSprite|stone-bricks}} '''Stone bricks''' and the variants {{BlockSprite|cracked-stone-bricks}} '''cracked stone bricks''', {{BlockSprite|mossy-stone-bricks}} '''mossy stone bricks''', and {{BlockSprite|chiseled-stone-bricks}} '''chiseled stone bricks''' are materials found in structures such as [[stronghold]]s, [[igloo]] basements, [[jungle temple]]s, [[ocean ruins]], [[ruined portal]]s, and [[trail ruins]].

{{BlockSprite|stone-bricks}} '''Smooth stone bricks''' are a fifth variant exclusive to ''Bedrock Edition'', visually identical to regular stone bricks, which cannot be found naturally and are only accessible via commands.

== Obtaining ==
Stone bricks can be mined using any [[pickaxe]]. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Stone Bricks|Pickaxe|Wood}}

=== Infested blocks ===
Stone bricks, mossy stone bricks, cracked stone bricks and chiseled stone bricks can also be obtained by mining their respective [[infested block]]s with [[Silk Touch]].

=== Trading ===
Apprentice-level stone mason [[villager]]s sell 4 chiseled stone bricks for one [[emerald]] as part of their trades.{{only|java}}

Apprentice-level stone mason [[villager]]s have a 25% ({{frac|1|4}}) chance to sell 4 chiseled stone bricks for one emerald as part of their trades.{{only|bedrock}}

=== Natural generation ===
Regular stone bricks, as well as occasional cracked and mossy stone bricks, generate as part of [[stronghold]]s, making up most of the walls, ceilings, and floors. They and chiseled stone bricks can also generate as part of [[ocean ruins]] and [[ruined portal]]s. [[Igloo]] basements are lined with stone bricks. In addition, three chiseled stone bricks generate as part of each [[jungle temple]]. Stone bricks and mossy stone bricks can also spawn in [[trail ruins]].

== Usage ==
As stone bricks offer no real advantage over [[stone]] or [[cobblestone]], their main use is for decoration.

=== Note Blocks ===
All types of stone bricks can be placed under [[note block]]s to produce "bass drum" sounds.

=== Silverfish ===
[[Silverfish]] have the ability to enter and hide in any variant of stone bricks, creating an [[infested block]] of the corresponding type.

== Trivia ==
* When [[smelting]] [[cobblestone]] blocks, the cracked form of [[stone]], they turn into stone without cracks. Yet, when smelting stone bricks without cracks, they turn into a cracked variant.
* On Bedrock Edition, there is an unused data value for stone bricks called smooth stone bricks, it can be obtained through commands but is unobtainable in survival mode or through the creative inventory.
*Chiseled stone bricks, like other chiseled bricks, have a symbol in its texture that references an in-game mob, item, or biome. In this case, it's an [[Eye of Ender]].
