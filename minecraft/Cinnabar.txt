'''Cinnabar''' is an upcoming group of reddish-hue colored blocks that will be added in [[Chaos Cubed]]{{Upcoming}}. It consists of [[slab]], [[stairs]], and [[wall]] variants, and can be crafted into [[polished]], [[bricks]], and [[chiseled]] variants.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Like any [[stone]]-related blocks, cinnabar blocks and its variants can only be mined using a [[pickaxe]], otherwise it drops nothing.

=== Natural generation ===
Only cinnabar blocks generate naturally in [[sulfur caves]] alongside [[sulfur]] blocks.

== Usage ==
Its slabs, stairs, walls, polished, bricks, and chiseled variants can only be obtained by crafting and stonecutting.

=== Sulfur cubes ===
[[Sulfur cubes]] can only absorb full-sized cinnabar blocks (including chiseled, polished, and brick variants), either picking it up or interacting with it, giving a regular/football (later slow bouncy{{Only|JE|short=1}}{{Upcoming|BE 26.30}}) effect{{Until|BE 26.30}} when hit.
