'''Minecraft Live 2024''' was a live event held on September 28, 2024, at 17:00 UTC that revealed new Minecraft features and other news. This was the first Minecraft Live event to have no community vote.

This Live event introduced the final release date for [[Bundles of Bravery]], introducing how [[Hardcore|Hardcore mode]] works in [[Bedrock Edition]], and spooky new features for [[The Garden Awakens|the Winter Game Drop]].

During the Minecraft Live, there are small segments from Villager News (animated by Element Animation), a sneak peek into [[A Minecraft Movie]] and an introduction to [[A Minecraft Movie Live Event]].

==Trivia==
* This was the first Minecraft livestream event to not have a Mob Vote, since it was [[Minecraft Live 2023|retired from the previous livestream]].
