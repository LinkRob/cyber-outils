'''Xbox Game Studios''', formerly '''Microsoft Studios''', is an American video game publisher and division of {{W|Microsoft}}. The company acquired [[Mojang Studios]], developers of [[Minecraft]], on November 6, 2014 for US$2.5 billion.<ref>{{tweet|XboxP3|530368089418784768|It's official, today we welcome Mojang to the Microsoft Studios family. We're excited for the possibilities ahead w/the Minecraft community.|November 6, 2014}}</ref> The studio helped with cross platform play across various editions of Minecraft, including mobile, PC and console versions. The studio also currently manages the [[Bedrock Edition]] of the game.{{Citation needed}}
