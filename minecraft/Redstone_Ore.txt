'''Redstone ore''' is the [[ore]] block from which [[redstone]] is obtained.

'''Deepslate redstone ore''' is a variant of redstone ore that can generate in [[deepslate]] and [[tuff]] blobs.

== Obtaining ==
=== Natural generation ===
{{see also|Ore (feature)}}
Redstone ore can generate in [[the Overworld]] in the form of [[ore (feature)|ore blob]]s. Redstone ore generates in two batches per chunk. The first batch attempts to generate 4 times per chunk, in blobs of 0&ndash;10, evenly from levels -64 to 15. The second batch attempts to generate 8 times per chunk, in blobs of 0&ndash;10, from levels -63 to -32, being more common the lower the elevation. Redstone ore can replace [[stone]], [[andesite]], [[diorite]], [[granite]], polished andesite‌{{only|be|short=1}}, [[tuff]]{{only|je|short=1}}, and [[deepslate]]. Any redstone ore that replaces tuff or deepslate becomes deepslate redstone ore.

=== Breaking ===
Redstone ore must be mined with an iron [[pickaxe]] or higher to drop [[redstone dust]]. When mined, it drops 4&ndash;5 redstone dust. It is affected by the [[Fortune]] enchantment, dropping 4-6, 4-7, or 4-8 redstone respectively with Fortune I, II, and III (averaging at 6 redstone dust with Fortune level 3<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20210311215700/https://imgur.com/a/UnIGPKn</ref><ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20230509054859/https://imgur.com/a/GirweJL</ref>). If mined with [[Silk Touch]], it drops itself instead of redstone dust.

== Usage ==
=== Light ===
Redstone ore produces the [[light level]] of 9 and emits bright red powered redstone particle when clicked, updated, or contacted by almost any entity (among some of the exceptions are a [[sneaking]] player, fired [[Arrow|arrows]], fired [[Fire Charge|fire charges]], [[Shulker|shulkers]], [[Shulker#Shulker_bullet|shulker bullets]], and the [[ender dragon]]). This effect continues until the redstone ore block receives a [[Tick#Random tick|random block tick]] (averaging 68.27 seconds). This effect does not actually produce redstone power, but the block changes can be detected with an [[observer]] or a [[Tutorials/Block update detector|block update detector]].

Subsequent clickings on a redstone ore block causes it to emit an extra burst of particles per click.
