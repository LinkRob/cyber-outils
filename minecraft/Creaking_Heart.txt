A '''Creaking heart''' is a "living" block that rarely generates in natural [[pale oak tree]]s of the [[pale garden]]. This block summons and puppeteers the [[creaking]], when it is active in the night.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
The Creaking heart can be broken using any tool, however the axe is the quickest. It can be obtained using a tool enchanted with [[Silk Touch]], otherwise it drops 1-3 [[resin clump]]s; [[Fortune]] can increase the maximum drops by 1 per level (up to 6 with Fortune 3).

Destroying a naturally-generated creaking heart drops 20-24 experience, only in Survival mode.

== Usage ==
The creaking heart can be activated by placing a creaking heart between 2 [[pale oak log]]s, which will spawn [[creaking]] at night as long as there is a valid spawn area in a 32 block radius of the creaking heart. The creaking heart also generates [[resin clump]]s on nearby pale oak logs upon a [[player]] hitting the creaking spawned by the creaking heart.
