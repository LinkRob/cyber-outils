The '''Better Together Update''' is an update to ''[[Pocket Edition]]'', ''[[Windows 10 Edition]]'', ''[[Gear VR Edition]]'', ''[[Fire TV Edition]]'', and a new version of the game on Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation 4. It was released for ''Pocket Edition'' as version [[Bedrock Edition 1.2.0|1.2.0]], and as a new, separate version of Minecraft for the Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation 4, known as the [[Bedrock Edition]]. Beta testing for the update for Android & Windows 10 began on July 31, 2017 and for Xbox One on August 24, 2017.<ref name="how-to-beta">{{Mcnet|beta-test-better-together|Beta Test Better Together!}}</ref> It was submitted to certification for 9 of the 10 platforms on September 15, 2017. It was released on September 20, 2017 for mobile devices, Windows 10, Gear VR, Fire TV, and on Xbox One in the form of [[Bedrock Edition]]. [[Bedrock Edition]] on Nintendo Switch released on June 21, 2018, both digitally and physically.<ref>https://twitter.com/Chupacaubrey/status/910213343528419328</ref><ref>{{Mcnet|everything-announced-minecon-earth|Everything Announced at MINECON Earth!|November 18, 2017}}</ref> [[Bedrock Edition]] on PlayStation 4 released on December 10, 2019. Eventually, [[Pocket Edition]] for Windows phones was discontinued after the official release of the Better Together Update on September 20, 2017.

The [[Bedrock Edition]] for Xbox One is free for players who own a digital copy of [[Xbox One Edition]] or have played the disc copy of [[Xbox One Edition]] for at least 5 hours in the past 12 months or have bought at least one DLC. The [[Bedrock Edition]] for Nintendo Switch is free for players who own a digital copy of [[Nintendo Switch Edition]]. The [[Bedrock Edition]] for PlayStation 4 is distributed as an update to the digital copy or disc copy of [[PlayStation 4 Edition]]. Because both versions are part of a single piece of software on PlayStation 4, a button is available on the main menu to toggle between editions.

The official name for the update, the ''Better Together Update'', aims to merge multiple platforms under a single version of Minecraft, reflecting the addition of cross-platform compatibility between mobile devices, Windows 10, Gear VR, Fire TV, and the port releases on Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation 4.

With the release of the ''Better Together Update'', the [[Java Edition|original PC version]] was renamed to ''Minecraft: Java Edition'' in [[Java Edition 1.12.2|1.12.2]] to distinguish it from the [[Bedrock Edition]].

=== New version-exclusive features ===
* How to Play tips in the Settings menu
* In-game shortcuts for certain commands
* [[Featured servers]] (accessible via Servers section in Play menu)
* New command {{cmd|tickingarea}}
* Custom characters.

=== Features Ported from Legacy Console Edition ===
* Blocks

** [[Coarse Dirt]]
** [[Jukebox]]
** [[Stained Glass]]
** [[Structure Block]]
* Items
** [[Music Disc]]
** [[Armor Stand]]
** [[Book and Quill]] and [[Written Book]]
** [[Firework Rocket]]
** [[Firework Star]]
* Mobs
** [[Parrot]]
* Generated structures
** [[Ravine]]s
* Other
** Ability to include a starting map and/or [[bonus chest]] when creating a world
** [[Splitscreen]]
** Choice of outline instead of highlight for block selection
** [[Brewing stand]] must be fueled with [[blaze powder]]
** Added [[recipe book]]
** Ability to change player skin while playing a world
** Player permissions
** No quasi-connectivity (old-BUD switches)
