An '''ore'''<ref>{{snap|20w45a}}</ref><ref>{{snap|21w13a}}</ref><ref>{{Mcnet|minecraft-changelog-1-17-0-50|Minecraft Changelog 1.17.0.50}}</ref> is a [[feature]] consisting of a natural deposit of [[ore]]s or other blocks in the ground.

== Generation ==
The ore feature has two types: '''ore blobs''' and '''scatter ores'''.

An '''ore blob''' is an ellipsoidal cluster of blocks. In the overworld, underground deposits of [[dirt]] and [[gravel]]<!--stone variants too--> are generated in this form, followed by the more precious ores, like [[coal]], [[copper]], [[iron]], [[gold]], [[redstone]], [[diamond]], [[emerald]] (in mountains biomes) and [[lapis lazuli]]. They can form only in stone, deepslate, [[andesite]], [[diorite]], [[granite]] and [[tuff]]{{only|java|short=1}}. Two or more ores can form next to each other and appear as an ore made of more than one material.

A '''scatter ore''' is a slightly dispersed cluster of blocks. Currently only ancient debris is generated in this form. Scatter ores also have a '''spawn size''' value. The maximum number of generated blocks in a scatter ore is just the spawn size.

There was also a deprecated ore type, which generates only one block in each generation attempt. It was used by emerald ore before 1.18.
