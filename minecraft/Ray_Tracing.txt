Ray Tracing (also known as '''''Path Tracing''''') is a feature of [[Bedrock Edition|''Bedrock Edition'']] that simulates real-life lights to make overall graphics realistic.<ref>https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/updates/ray-tracing</ref> It requires computer with graphics card that supports ray tracing.
== Requirements ==
*Windows 10 64-bit
*GPU: DirectX hardware ray tracing capable GPU like NVIDIA GeForce® RTX 2060 (RTX 20 series) and higher, and AMD Radeon™ RX 6000 Series and higher
*A PC; Virtual reality headsets and Mixed Reality (MR) headsets are not supported
*CPU: Intel Core i5 or higher 
*At least 8 GB of RAM 
*Minecraft [[Bedrock Edition 1.16.200]] or higher
