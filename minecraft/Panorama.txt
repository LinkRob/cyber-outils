The '''panorama''' (also known as the '''cubemap'''<ref>{{snap|21w41a|October 13, 2021}}</ref>) is a slowly panning wide-angle view of a ''Minecraft'' world or [[seed]] displayed on the [[main menu]].

== Panoramas ==
{{info needed section|Education Edition}}
=== Prototype (Java Edition) ===
[[File:Menu Panorama Revision 0.png|thumb|The unreleased menu screen panorama that came before the final version.]]

Dinnerbone had stated that the menu panorama was originally going to be an in-game render of a live world, with custom world generation with the seed 100. He also mentioned another panorama that came before the one seen in Beta 1.8, which was a plains biome near the beach.<ref>{{Reddit|url=hthrmk/big_news_we_have_found_the_seed_of_minecrafts|comment=fyherp0|title=Interesting stuff, nice work!<br><br>The first title screen panorama was actually originally in-game render of a live world, with custom worldgen and the seed <s>"splash"</s> 100. I would have figured this was a screenshot from that, but consider me surprised and impressed! :D<br><br>Edit: It turns out that was an even earlier panorama, and the one in this post came later. The first one was never released, though. Here's a screenshot of what it would have looked like: <nowiki>[link to screenshot on Discord]</nowiki>|author=Dinnerbone|date=July 18, 2020}}</ref>

This panorama had been introduced to Java Edition in Beta 1.8's early development and used for over six years before its replacement in the 1.13 prerelease stage.

This panorama used {{in|je|be}} are same. When this panorama was first introduced {{in|be}}, it had used the version which fixed the white line inside of it.

This panorama had been taken on the seed {{cd|2151901553968352745}} or {{cd|8091867987493326313}} (both are considered valid seeds) at coordinates X=61.48, Y=75, Z=-68.73. This seed 2151901553968352745 spawns you in a {{BiomeLink|Plains}} biome at -156, 244 from (Beta 1.9 Pre-release 4 to 1.12.2 and Pocket 1.0.0 to Pocket 1.1.0). The panorama distance direction is {{distdir|-156|244|61|-68}}. These seeds work in versions from [[Java Edition Alpha v1.2.0|Alpha v1.2.0]] to [[Java Edition Beta 1.7.3|Beta 1.7.3]] (except for population differences).<ref name=":0">{{reddit|url=hthrmk|title=BIG NEWS! We have found the SEED of Minecraft's title-screen background PANORAMA!|author=Tomlacko|date=July 18, 2020}}</ref> The actual version that was used to generate this panorama must have been a development version from [[Java Edition Beta 1.6.6|Beta 1.6.6]] to [[Java Edition Beta 1.7|Beta 1.7]], based on the file dates. [[Nathan Adams|Dinnerbone]] noted that the panorama was taken by [[Markus Persson|Notch]] while he was travelling, around June 1–15, 2011.<ref>https://discord.com/channels/720723932738486323/721776112014721096/738514461597630535</ref><ref>https://discord.com/channels/720723932738486323/721776112014721096/738517623637016706</ref>

{{IN|JE}}, this panorama is used for versions from Beta 1.8 Pre-release to 18w22c.

{{IN|BE}}, this panorama is used for versions from [[Pocket Edition v0.7.3 alpha|v0.7.3 alpha]] to [[Pocket Edition 1.1.7|1.1.7]].

{{IN|EE}}, it was used from its initial release to [[Education Edition 1.0.1|1.0.1]]. 

Usage in other editions is unknown.
