A '''redstone bug''' was a hostile [[mob]] present in [[Java Edition 2.0]], similar in form to a [[silverfish]].

== Spawn ==

Redstone bugs have a 1% chance to spawn when a redstone wire increases in strength. The chance increases by 5% for every redstone bug in a 7x7x7 area around the initial piece of redstone. The <code>Color</code> value of the bug can reach from <code>33292288</code> to <code>66846720</code>.

== Behavior ==
Redstone bugs, effectively having been reskinned [[silverfish]], shared all of their behavior.

=== Entity data ===
Redstone bugs used the ID of silverfish, different from them only by a newly-added <code>Color</code> NBT, which could have any color applied to it via editing. The first byte defines the alpha value and the other three the rgb color.
