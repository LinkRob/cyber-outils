'''v0.14.1''' was an update to ''[[Pocket Edition|Minecraft: Pocket Edition]]'' that added skins from ''[[Minecraft: Story Mode]]''. It was the last version to support iOS 5, 6, and 7.

== Additions ==
===[[Skin/Skin pack|Skin packs]]===
* {{Version exclusive}}: [[Skin/Skin pack#Minecraft Story Mode Skin Pack|Minecraft Story Mode Skin Pack]].

== Changes ==

* Loading in a world no longer displays the message "Generating World", and only the "Building Terrain" message.
