'''Sprinting''' is a method of [[transportation]] that allows the [[player]] to move faster, at the expense of depleting [[Hunger#Mechanics|saturation]]. While sprinting, the player's field of view expands slightly while increasing the player's reach by .5-1 block. Players also inflict more [[Damage#Knockback|knockback]] on the first hit when sprinting.

== Activating ==

To sprint, the player can press {{control|sprint}} while moving, or double tap {{control|forward}}. The player cannot sprint if their [[hunger|hunger bar]] is at {{hunger|6}} or below, or when they are under the [[Blindness]] effect.

Mobs that can sprint include:
*[[Ocelot]]s
*[[Cat]]s
*[[Iron Golem|Iron golem]]s
*[[Wither Skeleton|Wither skeleton]]s
*[[Witch]]es
*[[Wandering Trader|Wandering traders]]
*[[Enderman|Endermen]]
*[[Villager]]s
*[[Vindicator]]s
*[[Evoker]]s
*[[Pillager]]s
*[[Camel]]s

== Usage ==
[[File:sprinting_anim.gif|thumb|right|Particles coming out of the ground while sprinting.]]
Sprinting allows the player to move at around 5.612&nbsp;meters/second, which is 30 percent faster than the normal [[walking]] speed of around 4.317&nbsp;m/s, [[jumping]] while sprinting allows the player to move with an average speed of 7.127&nbsp;m/s. The player can turn while sprinting, resulting in slightly reduced speed. Sprinting also increases the speed of [[flying]] in [[Creative]] mode, and the speed of [[swimming]]. Although "sprinting" while mounted on a [[minecart]], [[boat]], [[horse]], [[pig]], or [[strider]] increases the field of vision like actual sprinting, it does not increase the entity's speed. 

While sprinting, it is possible to jump across up to four [[block]]s horizontally,<ref>{{ytl|cPWyGGA0mLk}}</ref> instead of the usual two blocks, although sprinting does not cause the player to jump higher.

Sprinting reduces the player's [[Hunger#Mechanics|saturation]].  When saturation reaches zero, the [[hunger]] bar depletes at a rate of {{frac|1|2}} every 40 meters, or 7 seconds.

Sprinting produces [[particles]] at the player's feet corresponding to the block type they are running on. When a player attacks any [[mob]] (excluding [[squid]], [[Iron Golem|iron golem]]s and [[shulker]]s) while sprinting, the mob suffers a larger knockback than usual. Sprinting and holding down jump at the same time is usually faster, but actually becomes slower when under the effect of [[Speed]] II or higher.

Sprinting ends when the player collides with a solid block at an angle of greater than 8 degrees<ref group="note">In the Bedrock version, there is a short window of time in which the player can move away from the solid block in order to continue sprinting. In the Java version, sprinting ends immediately upon colliding.</ref>, [[sneaking|sneaks]], [[Blocking|blocks]] or attacks a mob. If none of these happen, the player automatically stops sprinting after 30 seconds.<ref group="note">If {{control|sprint}} is held, sprinting lasts longer than 30 seconds.</ref> Sprinting also ends if the hunger bar is reduced to {{hunger|6}} or less.

Sprinting pauses when the player eats [[food]] or drinks [[potion]]s, but pressing {{control|sprint}} while eating food or potions does not start sprinting.

== Trivia ==

* Sprinting and jumping while under a 2-block high ceiling causes a player to move at a speed almost twice as fast as regular sprinting speed, but it consumes a hunger point every second. It is also possible to jump 5 blocks when jumping out of the 2-block high ceiling.
** This can be sped up even more by decreasing the ceiling height using [[trapdoor]]s or by sprinting on [[ice]].
** A similar effect occurs if the player sprints and rapidly hits {{control|jump}} at the same time on a set of stairs.
* Sprint-jumping actually costs less [[Hunger#Exhaustion level increase|exhaustion]] per block travelled compared to pure sprinting, despite being faster. This is because exhaustion increases while the player is on the ground. While sprint-jumping, most of of the distance is covered in mid-air.
