{{Italic title|Java Edition}}
{{about|all of the full Minecraft versions - 2009 to 2026|development versions|Java Edition version history/Development versions|future updates|Planned versions#Java Editions|version history of other editions|Version history|a timeline of all events related to ''[[Minecraft]]''|Timeline of events}}
{{/disclaimer}}
{{JE}} has been in constant development since mid-May 2009. What is now known as [[pre-Classic]] refers to the early unreleased versions of ''Minecraft'' during the week from May 10 to May 16, 2009. The next phase, what was later called [[Classic]], lasted from May 17 to November 10, 2009. [[Indev]], short for "in development", lasted from December 23, 2009, to February 23, 2010. [[Infdev]], short for "infinite development", lasted from February 27 to June 30, 2010. [[Alpha]] lasted from June 30 to December 3, 2010, and [[Java Edition Beta|Beta]] lasted from December 20, 2010, all the way through to November 11, 2011. The official release of ''Minecraft'' (''Java Edition'') came on November 18, 2011, at [[MINECON 2011]], and further updates have been released ever since, the most recent being {{v|java}}.

==Full release==
{{anchor|Full Release}}
[[File:Version History Java.png|left|thumb|All versions of ''Minecraft'' [[Java Edition]] up to 1.20.1.]]

The full release stage of [[Java Edition|''Minecraft: Java Edition'']]'s development started on November 18, 2011, and has continued until the present day. At this point of development, the game was considered "complete", having both an "[[The End|ending]]" and being ready for ratings and reviews. The game's price also increased from what it was in [[Java Edition Beta|Beta]], but users who previously bought the game were not charged extra.

The [[Java Edition Beta|Beta]] development phase started on December 20, 2010 and ended on November 18, 2011. The main goals of this development stage were a larger focus on polishing gameplay, additional content, and stability.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/0/http://notch.tumblr.com/post/2175441966/minecraft-beta-december-20-2010</ref> The game's price also increased from what it was in Alpha, but users who previously bought the game were not charged extra.

These were also the first versions which had to be purchased, though the price increased in later stages.

==Pre-Classic==

[[Java Edition pre-Classic|Pre-Classic]] was the initial development phase of ''Minecraft'', in which the very basic mechanics and elements of the game were developed, tested and improved over the period of less than a week—from May 10–16, 2009. It was not originally released to the public, but some versions are now playable via the [[launcher]].
