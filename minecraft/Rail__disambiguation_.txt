A '''{{BlockLink|rail}}''' is an item used to provide a path for minecarts.

'''Rail''' may also refer to:

* {{BlockLink|Powered Rail}}, an item used to speed or move minecarts
* {{BlockLink|Detector Rail}}, an item used as a redstone power source
* {{BlockLink|Activator Rail}}, an item used to "activate" certain blocks and entities such as minecart with TNT
* [[Achievement#List of achievements|On A Rail]], an achievement in Bedrock Edition
