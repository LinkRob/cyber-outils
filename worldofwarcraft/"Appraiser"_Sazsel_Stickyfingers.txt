'''"Appraiser" Sazsel Stickyfingers''' is a [[goblin]] [[vendor]] found in the [[Horde]] [[Garrison]] of [[Frostwall]]. She sells various crafting materials for [[Primal Spirit]]s. When the Garrison is in its final tier, she is seen standing at the entrance of the [[Tower Of War]].

She later appears in [[Valdrakken]], working with [[Fangli Hoot]] and the [[adventurer]] on appraising [[Preservationist Kathos]]'s [[Maruuk Burial Banner]].<ref>[[An Inside Job?]]</ref>
