==Trivia==
"Aurora Borealis", along with [[An Unforgettable Luncheon]] and [[Steamed Clams]], is a reference to the "{{wp|Steamed Hams}}" [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u1h8cHUnP9k skit] in the {{wp|22 Short Films About Springfield}} episode of ''{{wp|The Simpsons}}''. The scene has long been the subject of various online memes and video edits, particularly during a period in late 2017 and early 2018, a few months prior to the launch of ''Battle for Azeroth''.
