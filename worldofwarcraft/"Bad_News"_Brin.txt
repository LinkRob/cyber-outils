'''"Bad News" Brin''' is a [[dwarf|dwarven]] member of the [[Bloodsail Buccaneers]] who appears at the Buccaneers' beach party on the [[Cape of Stranglethorn]]'s [[Wild Shore]] during [[Pirates' Day]]. She offers to tell a number of pirate-themed jokes.

==Quotes==
;Gossip
:Take off yer shoes, grab a grog, and share a joke with me!

* {{gossip|Sure, tell me a joke.}}
* {{gossip|Your jokes are the best!}}
* {{gossip|Your jokes are terrible!}}

If the player selects the first dialogue option, Brin will tell one of the following jokes:

* {{text|say|Alright, batten down yer hatches, mateys. This one'll blow yer rudder off...}}
: {{text|say|I was drinkin' with a [[Dread Crew]] gunner in [[Booty Bay]] last week...}}
: {{text|say|After a few drinks, he looked me in the eyes and asked how the Bloodsail got to be so great...}}
: {{text|say|I raised my mug and told him we just ARRR!}}
: {{text|say|Ha, now someone get me a grog.}}

* {{text|say|Alright, batten down yer hatches, mateys. This one'll blow yer rudder off...}}
: {{text|say|So what's the deal with treasure chests nowadays? They're so expensive and shiny...}}
: {{text|say|It's like we want people to find 'em! All that shiny gold edging and gem-encrusted locks...}}
: {{text|say|It's like, are we storing treasure on the OUTside or the INside? Right?}}
: {{text|say|Ha, now someone get me a grog.}}

* {{text|say|Alright, batten down yer hatches, mateys. This one'll blow yer rudder off...}}
: {{text|say|This is one of 'em brain puzzles...}}
: {{text|say|What has six legs, six hands, and six eyes?}}
: {{text|say|Six pirates!}}
: {{text|say|Ha, now someone get me a grog.}}

* {{text|say|Alright, batten down yer hatches, mateys. This one'll blow yer rudder off...}}
: {{text|say|Ye all know crazy [[Rear Admiral Giggleplanks]], right?}}
: {{text|say|Did ye know he once used his [[gnome|gnomish]] gizmos to combine a [[parrot]] and [[shark]] into the ultimate pirate companion?}}
: {{text|say|It worked great... until it talked his ear off!}}
: {{text|say|Ha, now someone get me a grog.}}
*{{text|say|Alright, batten down yer hatches, mateys. This one'll blow yer rudder off...}}
: {{text|say|So Captain Salthoof got his first ship pretty quickly, right?}}
: {{text|say|I asked him how much he had to bribe the admiral with to make that happen, and he told me his secret...}}
: {{text|say|He got it on sail!}}
: {{text|say|Ha, now someone get me a grog.}}
If a player tells Brin that her jokes are the best, she will /bow or /cheer and say one of the following:

* {{text|say|I'll cut ye fer... Oh wait, ye liked em? Thank ye. Nobody ever says they like 'em.}}
* {{text|say|I've trained over countless oceans and pillages. This be authentic pirate humor!}}
* {{text|say|No hornswaggle? I thank ye kindly for that, friend.}}
* {{text|say|Spread the word. Tell 'em ol' Bad News ain't so bad afterall.}}
* {{text|say|Thank ye kindly, ye old sea dog.}}
* {{text|say|Yarrr! Splice the mainbrace!}}

If the player instead tells her that her jokes are terrible, she will perform a /rude gesture and say one of the following:

* {{text|say|A {{wp|Black Spot (Treasure Island)|black spot}} upon ye and all yer cockroach friends!}}
* {{text|say|Go dance with {{wp|Jack Ketch}}, and leave the jokes to the old salt.}}
* {{text|say|Ha! I'd like to see ye do better, scallywag!}}
* {{text|say|I'll cleave ye to the brisket!}}
* {{text|say|Shut yer squiffy grog hole and walk the plank, ye son of a biscuit eater!}}
* {{text|say|Shut yer trap or I'll run ye through, ye pathetic pirate wannabe!}}
* {{text|say|Yeah? Well yer face is terrible... and so is yer mother!}}
