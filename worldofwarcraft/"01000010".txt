'''"01000010"''' is a [[flavor text]] item looted from [[Blingtron 5000 Gift Package]].

==Trivia==
*[[Fluxfire Feline]]s are a kind of battle pet found in [[New Tinkertown]].
*'''01000010''' in binary translate to "B" in text and 42 in hexadecimal, possibly a reference to {{wplink|Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy}}.
