| Description             = '''[[Siegfried Horatio Hunch III (New Earth)|'Mazing Man]]''' is Siegfried Horatio Hunch III, a benignly deranged little man who becomes a bumbling super-hero in [[Queens]], [[New York City]]. Despite having become wealthy through a magazine sweepstakes, his costume is hand-made and his efforts are well-meaning but ineffectual. His best friend is dog-faced comic book writer [[Denton Fixx]]. He is unrelated to the legacy of [[Amazing-Man]]. ''<nowiki/>'Mazing Man'' was created by [[Bob Rozakis]] and [[Stephen DeStefano]], first appearing in {{c|'Mazing Man #1}}. ([[1986]])
