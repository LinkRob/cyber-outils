'''"Vis la Crac" Wine''' is a [[Blood and Wine quest items|quest item]] in the {{BaW}}.

It may be acquired off the body of a woman at the [[Seidhe Llygad Amphitheater]] alongside a [[Perfume-laced journal|journal]]. After the nearby candles are lit in the correct order, this must be drunk to finish the reenactment which will then summon the [[Phantom of the Amphitheater]], who must then be killed.

== Notes ==
* There are actually 2 versions of this item. The one initially looted isn't consumable (and has 0.36 {{weight}}), and it is replaced by a consumable version (0 {{weight}}) only after the candles are lit in the correct order.
