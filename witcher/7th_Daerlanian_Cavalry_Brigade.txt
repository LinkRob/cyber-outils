}}'''7th Brigade''' from [[Daerlan]] was the largest division of the [[4th Cavalry Army]], [[Nilfgaardian Empire|Nilfgaardian]] [[cavalry|mounted troops]] overseen by Major General [[Markus Braibant]]. The brigade itself was commanded by [[Elan Trahe]] and consisted of:
* [[Morteisen Battle Group]]
* [[Sievers Battle Group]]
* [[Vreemde Battle Group]]

7th Daerlan, together with the [[Nauzicaa Brigade]], were the last troops sent into battle during the [[battle of Brenna]]. Led by the Field Marshal [[Menno Coehoorn]] himself, these forces were supposed to rip through the [[Northern Kingdoms|Northerner]] armies, end up behind enemy lines and then attack them from the rear. This effort proved ultimately futile as the inexperienced [[Poor Fucking Infantry]], under the [[voivode]] [[Bronibor]], was surprisingly able to thwart the Nilfgaardian cavalry advance and stand their ground despite the heavy casualties suffered. From this point on, Nilfgaard's defeat was inevitable and swift.
