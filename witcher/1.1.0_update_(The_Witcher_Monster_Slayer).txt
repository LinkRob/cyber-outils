'''1.1.0 update''' is an update for {{TWMS}} released on November 24, 2021.<ref>[https://www.thewitcher.com/en/monster-slayer/news/39844/whats-new-1-1-0 What's new - 1.1.0]</ref> It introduced new gameplay features and minor bug fixes.

==Changes==
*We're introducing PAD, which divides the game into modules delivered in three ways: during installation, after installation, and on-demand. While in the store, you will download the base module. Please, be aware that extra data will need to be downloaded after downloading the game — connecting to a Wi-Fi network is recommended;
*We're introducing a new monster distribution system. Every two weeks, a new [[The Witcher Monster Slayer bestiary|monster]] will join the game;
*We've added new weather system information where you can now see when the time of day will change by tapping the weather icon;
* We've redesigned the What's New system so that all the latest news can be found directly in the game;
*We've added timed tasks. Each week you will be faced with new challenges;
*We've added the "[[The Witcher Monster Slayer contracts|Daily Hunt]]" where you can kill monsters and collect five stamps to claim a [[The Witcher Monster Slayer scrolls|Summoning Scroll]]. You can earn one stamp per day;
*We've updated Daily Contracts. You will now receive three contracts instead of one per day;
*We've added "[[The Witcher Monster Slayer ingredients|Fresh Herbs]]" to [[Thorstein's shop|Thorstein's offer]]. It gives you a bundle of fresh herbs;
* We've added a "[[The Witcher Monster Slayer scrolls|Lesser Summoning Scroll]]" to Thorstein's offer. It spawns three random common monsters around your character;
*We've fixed the bug in the [[Sword in the Stone]] quest where the quest failed to trigger the "Return to the sword at dawn" phase.
