}}'''"Starry Night Over the Pontar"''' is a painting by [[van Rogh]], and a [[Hearts of Stone quest items|quest item]] in the {{HoS}}.

It may be acquired at the auction during the main quest [[Open Sesame! (Hearts of Stone)|Open Sesame!]], either for 20 {{Crown}} or 60 {{Crown}} (only if you successfully got information from [[Yaromir Ivanovitz Zaytsev|Yaromir]] and then told [[Vimme Vivaldi|Vivaldi]]). If you gained the necessary information from Yaromir by correctly answering his question, buying the painting would later start the [[Avid Collector]] quest in which the painting can be sold on to [[Marcus T.K. Hodgson]], a famed [[Novigrad]] book dealer, who will pay quite a tidy sum for it.

Alternatively, [[Geralt of Rivia|Geralt]] can hold onto the painting and display it at [[Corvo Bianco]] with the {{BaW}} installed.

== Associated quests ==
* [[Open Sesame! (Hearts of Stone)|Open Sesame!]]
* [[Avid Collector]]

==Trivia==
*The painting is a reference to [[wikipedia:Vincent_van_Gogh|Vincent van Gogh]]'s ''[[wikipedia:Starry_Night_Over_the_Rh%C3%B4ne|Starry night over the Rhone]]''.

== Notes ==
* It's mentioned in the document [[list of objects in need of restoration]] found later on in the vault.
* With the {{BaW}}, it's possible to keep the painting while still completing the quest by first hanging it in Corvo Bianco, then selling it to Marcus. However, the painting will still be hanging in Corvo Bianco after selling it.
** While you can do this to keep it for your home, there's no way to put it in your stash as it'll always be marked as a quest item.
**Prior to {{Patch|game=3|patch=4.0}}, a bug existed where you couldn't hang this painting in your home in a [[New Game +]] without the use of mods.
