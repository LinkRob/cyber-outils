'''1.1.3 update''' is an update for {{TWMS}} released on December 18, 2021.<ref>[https://www.thewitcher.com/en/monster-slayer/news/40246/whats-new-1-1-3 What's New – 1.1.3]</ref> It provided various bug fixes and minor tweaks to the game.

==Changes==
*we've changed the [[katakan]]'s rarity from legendary to rare;
*we've fixed the bug where the marker for claiming a reward in timed tasks would keep displaying after the reward was claimed;
*we've raised the default chance for rare and legendary [[The Witcher Monster Slayer bestiary|monsters]] to appear in [[Nemeton|Nemeta]];
*we've fixed the bug regarding players not moving on the map (for devices with Android 12);
*we've fixed the bug related to the lack of an axe in the [[The Witcher Monster Slayer glossary|hermit]]'s hands in the AR view;
