General '''McDonnell''', also known as "'''Kingpin'''", is a major character who appears in ''[[Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare]]''. Appearing in multiple trailers for ''Advanced Warfare'', he and [[Jonathan Irons]] appear to be discussing a plan for [[Manhunt|military action on foreign soil]], with Irons asking for permission for the operation, to which McDonnell denies. After further arguments, Irons eventually gives up and leaves the General. He isn't heard again until "[[Sentinel (mission)|Sentinel]]", where it is revealed he is part of the [[Sentinel Task Force]]. His last physical appearance is in "[[Throttle]]", where he tells [[Gideon (Advanced Warfare)|Gideon]], [[Jack Mitchell|Mitchell]] and [[Cormack]] the plan to attack New Baghdad, Iraq.
