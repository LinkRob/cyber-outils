'''"Fats" Malloy''' is a character featured in ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops 6]]'' [[Zombies (Treyarch)|Zombies]] mode.

==Biography==
"Fats" Malloy was a bootlegger and one of the known owners of the [[Sam Colton Hall|Liberty Falls mansion]].

Malloy built a secret distillery beneath the mansion and began manufacturing his own moonshine. When the {{w|Prohibition in the United States|Prohibition}} began, Malloy became extremely rich, often giving out payoffs to the mayor and Sheriff of Liberty Falls. However, his empire soon fell, as Malloy was shot by {{w|Special agent#Federal government|Federal agents}} during a party at the mansion in 1923. Somehow, Malloy's spirit was transported into a liminal space inside the mansion where he was trapped in a loop, constantly searching for the hip flask.

The mansion had several owners afterwards until [[Edward "Eddie" Richtofen|Edward Richtofen]] and [[Project Janus]] used it as a research facility to house [[Synaptic Algorithm Module|S.A.M]] in the late 1980s. When the ex-Requiem arrived at the mansion on February 28th, 1991, they retrieved the metal hip flask to free Malloy's spirit and empower the [[Sentinel Artifacts|Sentinel Artifact]] with the [[Prima Materia]] contained in the liminal space.

==Trivia==
*The fate of Malloy is unclear as [[Synaptic Algorithm Module|S.A.M]] states he vanished and has been missing since 1923, while the "Thesis Statement" [[Shattered Veil/Intel|intel]] states that he was "shot up by the feds".
**The dialogue during the ritual in the Liminal Distillery also hints at Malloy being poisoned.
