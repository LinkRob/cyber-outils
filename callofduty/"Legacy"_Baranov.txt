'''Baranov''', also known as '''Legacy''', is a character featured in ''[[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III]]'' [[Zombies (Treyarch)|Zombies]]. Legacy is the second-in-command of [[Terminus Outcomes]]. He is stationed in his own fortress in [[Zaravan]], [[Urzikstan]].

==Trivia==
*Legacy uses a custom [[Origin 12 Shotgun|Haymaker]] blueprint, named "Tactical Negotiator". This blueprint can be acquired as a contraband weapon from a Reward Rift after defeating him.
