The '''"Five" Crew''' is the dedicated crew featured in the map [["Five"]] in the [[Zombies (Treyarch)|Zombies]] mode of ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops]]'', and a few select members were mentioned in the maps [[Revelations (map)|Revelations]] and [[Classified]] in ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops III]]'' and ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops 4]]'', respectively. 

The crew consists of [[John F. Kennedy/Zombies|John F. Kennedy]], [[Robert McNamara/Zombies|Robert McNamara]], [[Fidel Castro/Zombies|Fidel Castro]] and [[Richard Nixon]]. The four men were having a meeting inside the [[The Pentagon|Pentagon]] when a zombie outbreak ocurred, leaving them to fight together against the ensuing horde.

==Overview==
The "Five" crew is composed of prominent political figures during the early stages of the [[Cold War]]; John F. Kennedy was the President of the United States at the time, Robert McNamara was JFK's Secretary of Defense, Richard Nixon was the former Vice-President of the US, and Fidel Castro was the leader of Cuba, with whom the USA were having geopolitical tensions.

The events of "Five" occur at the same time as [[Ascension]]'s; it is due to the events that happen there that a zombie outbreak takes place within the Pentagon, and it is only after [[Ultimis]]' intervention that the outbreak is able to be contained.

==History==
===Pentagon Meeting===
On November 6th, 1963, a meeting was being held within the Pentagon between four high-profile political figures: John F. Kennedy, Rober McNamara, Fidel Castro and Richard Nixon. A few days earlier, McNamara was overheard by the Ultimis crew - [[Classified|when they were accidentally teleported into the Pentagon]] - to saying how he was preparing the meeting and how it was of "paramount importance" and that he "must ensure every detail is planned to perfection."<ref>https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Classified/Quotes#Robert_McNamara</ref> On the day of the meeting, at the Baikonur Cosmodre in the Soviet Union, [[Anton Gersh]] was tricked by [[Yuri Zavoyski (Aether)|Yuri Zaykov]] into activating a Gersh Device. The rift created allowed [[Samantha Maxis]] to travel through, and create a zombie outbreak there. It also absorbed Gersh, destroying his body and trapped him inside the Casimir Mechanism.

At the same time that this outbreak at the Cosmodrome was taking place, an outbreak had also taken place at the Pentagon - forcing the "Five" Crew to defend themselves and defeat the incoming zombies. Meanwhile, at the Cosmodrome, the Ultimis crew arrived there through a lunar lander that they had found near [[Kino der Toten|Deutsches sol Kino]] in West Berlin, and focused on freeing Anton Gersh from the Casimir Mechanism. Ultimis succeeded in the quest, and the outbreak was contained. Subsequently, the outbreak that had ocurred in the Pentagon was also contained, and the "Five" Crew had survived.

===Agonia Fracture===
In a radio found in [[Revelations (map)|Revelations]], McNamara can be heard stating that he was in a janitor's closet while also stating that he feared President Kennedy, Nixon, and Castro had all been killed by the zombies during the outbreak. These events took place in the Agonia Fracture, an alternate dimension in which [[Gorod Krovi]] also takes place.

==Legacy==
Following the Pentagon outbreak, the US Government created a new program with the intention of preventing another outbreak - [[Broken Arrow]]. According to radios found [[Die Rise|years later]], a fire has destroyed the main facility, which might have been lit by the organization's followers to destroy evidence implicating them in the zombie outbreak following the [[Moon|destruction of Earth]].
