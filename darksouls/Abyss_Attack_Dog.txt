'''Abyss Attack Dogs''' are [[:Category:Dark Souls: Enemies|enemies]] in ''[[Dark Souls]]''.  They can only be found in the [[Artorias of the Abyss]] DLC. 

==Location==
They are encountered in an isolated area of the [[Royal Wood]] within [[Oolacile]], along the river leading to [[Black Dragon Kalameet]].

==Description==
Abyss Attack Dogs are similar in appearance and behavior to [[Attack Dog]]s.
