The '''Abyss Seal''' is a [[Rings (Dark Souls II)|ring]] in ''[[Dark Souls II]]''.

{{Description
|First paragraph = Ring of the Pilgrims of Dark covenant.<br>Increases the attack strength of hexes,<br>but casting them reduces HP.
|Second paragraph = What is dark?<br>Perhaps we already know.<br>We fear dark, yet find solace in it.
|Third paragraph = Those who join this covenant can see the hidden Dark Chasm.}}

==Availability==
Given to the player by [[Darkdiver Grandahl]] upon joining the [[Pilgrims of Dark]] [[Covenant (Dark Souls II)|covenant]].

==Effect==
Wearing the Abyss Seal increases the [[Dark (Dark Souls II)|Dark]] damage of [[hexes]] by 7% at the cost of 30 [[HP (Dark Souls II)|HP]] per cast.

==Patch notes==
*Due to [[Patch (Dark Souls II)|calibration 1.06]], the damage boost for hexes has been changed from 20% to 7%.
