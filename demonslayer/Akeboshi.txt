{{Nihongo|'''Akeboshi'''|{{Ruby|明|あ}}け{{Ruby|星|ぼし}}|Akeboshi|lit. "Morning Star"}} is the first opening song for [[Kimetsu no Yaiba Season 2]]. It is performed by [[wikipedia:LiSA (Japanese musician, born 1987)|LiSA]].

== Description ==
"Akeboshi" is the opening song for the first cour of ''[[Kimetsu no Yaiba (Anime)|Kimetsu no Yaiba]]''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s second season. The song is performed by [[Wikipedia:LiSA (Japanese musician, born 1987)|LiSA]], who also sang the anime's first opening, [[Gurenge]], and the ''[[Kimetsu no Yaiba: The Movie - Mugen Train|Mugen Train]]'' movie theme song, [[homura]]. It, alongside [[Shirogane]], is her 20th single.{{Ref|text=[https://www.sonymusic.co.jp/artist/lisa/info/533561 Akeboshi / Shirogane] on [https://www.sonymusic.co.jp/ Sony Music]}}
