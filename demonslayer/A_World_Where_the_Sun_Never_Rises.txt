== Description ==
"A World Where the Sun Never Rises" is the first theme song for ''[[Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – Infinity Castle]]'', the movie sequel to the fourth season of ''[[Kimetsu no Yaiba (Anime)|Kimetsu no Yaiba]]''. The song is performed by [[Wikipedia:Aimer|Aimer]], who has worked with [[Wikipedia:ufotable|ufotable]] before, notably preforming the opening and ending of the anime's second cour of the second season, [[Zankyosanka]] and [[Asa ga Kuru]] respectively. The song was available for full download and streaming on July 19, 2025, and the single release was on July 23rd.
