{{Nihongo|'''Aoba Hashibira'''|{{Ruby|嘴|はし}}{{Ruby|平|びら}} {{Ruby|青|あお}}{{Ruby|葉|ば}}|Hashibira Aoba}} is the great-grandson of [[Inosuke Hashibira]] and [[Aoi Kanzaki]]. He is a renowned [[Wikipedia:Botanist|botanist]] involved in researching the [[Blue Spider Lily]].{{Ref|chap=205|page=8}}

== Appearance ==
Aoba shares a striking resemblance with his ancestor, having feminine features that cause people to mistake him for a woman. He has black, shoulder length hair with overlapping bangs and emerald green eyes. However, unlike Inosuke, Aoba has a physically smaller build and jet black hair rather than hair that fades from black to blue like Inosuke's, the same as Aoi.

As a researcher, he wears a standard uniform consisting of a white button up shirt and lab coat, as well as blue slacks and black shoes.

== Personality ==
Unlike his ancestor, Aoba has a timid personality and is incredibly intelligent. Being the leading researcher in charge of study the [[Blue Spider Lily]], he held a press conference to apologize to the public for a mistake in their research and took it upon himself, showing that he was earnest and sincere.{{Ref|chap=205|page=8}}

Despite their stark contrast in character, Aoba has shown a similarity to Inosuke. Following the mistake in his study, he had voiced his wish to "live alone deep in the mountains", following the backlash he endured. {{Ref|chap=205|page=25}}

== Synopsis ==
As the Agatsuma siblings make their way to school, Yoshiteru comes across an article detailing Aoba's research. In the article, it is revealed that Aoba had discovered that the Blue Spider Lily only bloomed during the day for only 2–3 days per year. However, following a mistake in his study, the flowers had withered and died. Aoba and his team then held a press conference to apologize to the public.{{Ref|chap=205|page=8}}

Later on, Aoba can be seen worrying over the possibility of being fired for his mistake, wishing that he could instead move deep in the mountains to live alone.{{Ref|chap=205|page=25}}

==Trivia==
* Aoba shares the rōmaji spelling of the first part of his name with Aoi (however, the "ao" in "Aoi" and the "ao" in "Aoba" are written in completely [[Wikipedia:Japanese_writing_system#Use_of_scripts|different scripts]]).
* Unlike the others, Aoba's name does not include characters from his incarnation/ancestor. Instead, the "ba" in Aoba's name and the "ha" in Kotoha's are the same, both written as {{Nihongo|"leaf"|葉|ha}}. The reason it's "ba" instead of "ha" in Aoba's name is due to [[Wikipedia:Rendaku|rendaku]].
* He became friends with [[Sumihiko Kamado]] after he lost his job and started crying whilst in the same park as him. They played badminton together afterwards.{{Ref|name=vol23}}
* He has one younger brother.{{Ref|name=vol23}}
