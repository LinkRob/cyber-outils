==History==
Amane first met the leader of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]] at the age of 17, when Kagaya was only 13 years old. She was specifically selected by a priest to become Kagaya's wife in order to care for his medical needs, but he only agreed to the marriage on the condition that she cared for him on her own free will.{{Ref|vol=16}} Amane gave birth to quintuplets; [[Hinaki Ubuyashiki|Hinaki]], [[Nichika Ubuyashiki|Nichika]], [[Kiriya Ubuyashiki|Kiriya]], [[Kanata Ubuyashiki|Kanata]], and [[Kuina Ubuyashiki|Kuina]].
[[File:Amane Ubuyashiki meets with Muichiro.png|left|thumb|200px|Amane meets Muichiro Tokito.]]
Amane once travelled to the mountains in order to recruit the descendants of the first Breathing Style, the young twins [[Yuichiro Tokito|Yuichiro]] and [[Muichiro Tokito]]. She explained their lineage and asked them to join the Demon Slayer Corps; despite Muichiro's excitement, Yuichiro bitterly turned her away, forcing her to leave. She would attempt several more times to recruit them, but was finally driven away when Yuichiro splashed her with water. Some time later, after the twins were attacked by a demon and laid in their house alone for several days, Amane and her daughters Hinaki and Nichika found them. Unfortunately, Yuichiro had already died.
[[File:Amane stops Nichika from interfering.png|thumb|200px|Amane stops Nichika from interfering with Muichiro's training.]]
Amane held the barely living Muichiro and prayed he would survive as her daughters tended to his wounds. When he recovered, Muichiro began to train intensely and relentlessly. Nichika attempted to stop him out of concern, but Amane prevented her, reassured that despite Muichiro's badly injured state, he was doing something he needed to do.

==Synopsis==
===[[Mugen Train Arc]]===
Amane is seen accompanying her husband as he visited the graves of fallen Demon Slayers. Later, they hear about the death of the Flame Hashira [[Kyojuro Rengoku]], with Kagaya expressing his proudness in the young man and believing he would soon join him.

===[[Swordsmith Village Arc]]===
[[File:Amane explains the Tokito family's ancestry.png|thumb|200px|Amane explaining the Tokito twins' ancestry.]]
During his [[Muichiro Tokito vs Gyokko|battle]] with Upper Rank Five [[Gyokko]], Muichiro was nearly killed as he was poisoned and nearly suffocated in one of the demon's Blood Demon Arts. His life flashed before him and he recalled his forgotten past, including the first time he met Amane and her attempts to recruit them as Demon Slayers. Later on, after awakening mysterious marks that dramatically enhanced his physical abilities, Muichiro confronts Gyokko with uncharacteristic anger, noting that it was the same boiling rage that allowed him to train even whilst severely injured. Amane had recognized his will to push on and even stopped one of her daughters from interfering with his training.

===[[Hashira Training Arc]]===
[[File:Amane leads the emergency meeting.png|thumb|200px|Amane leads the Hashira meeting.|left]]
Following Kagaya's further worsening state, Amane became the one to address and speak with the Hashira in his place. During an emergency meeting following the attack on the Swordsmith Village, she addressed the coming conflict that will arise when Muzan will attempt to take Nezuko Kamado, now immune to sunlight. She then speaks to the Hashira about [[Mitsuri Kanroji]] and Muichiro, being told that [[Demon Slayer Mark|marks]] appeared on their bodies in their battles. Amane explains that these marks have been known since the Sengoku period, but little information is known about them.
[[File:Kiriya, Amane, and Kanata confused by Mitsuri.png|thumb|200px|Amane confused by Mitsuri's explanation.]]
When Mitsuri, who had been admiring Amane's beauty privately, is first asked to explain how her marks manifested, her unusual slew of gestures and sound only confuse everyone, even leaving the usually collected and stoic Amane bewildered. Muichiro then speaks about specific conditions he felt in his battle, mainly his high body temperature and rapid heart rate. With information now available to help them, Amane adds one more crucial piece of information about the marks, which disconcerts the Hashira. She then left the meeting, leaving the Hashira to discuss their new plan for the future.

While treating Kagaya in their residence, Amane informed him that [[Gyomei Himejima]]'s proposal of [[Hashira Training]] is underway, bolstering the Corps' strength. She adds that their children have accepted his will and are willing to do anything to defeat Muzan. Kagaya then reached for her hand; Amane holds his and listens as he thanks her for everything he had done. Touched by his words, Amane tearfully tells him it had been her pleasure.
[[File:Muzan confronts Kagaya and Amane.png|thumb|200px|Amane sitting beside Kagaya as Muzan confronts him.|left]]
When [[Muzan Kibutsuji]] appeared in the Ubuyashiki Mansion, Amane sat beside Kagaya as he faced the Demon Progenitor. After mutual introductions, Kagaya then asked her to described the demon, to which she explains to him that Muzan appears as a man in his 20s, with pale skin and plum red irises. Amane continued to sit quietly beside Kagaya as he and the demon had a clash of ideals of the coming future, with the Oyakata boldly claiming that Muzan would fail entirely in his goal to become immortal.

When Muzan grew tired of his speech and prepared to kill them, an explosive trap in the mansion detonated, sacrificing Amane, Kagaya, and her two eldest daughter in an attempt to stun Muzan.
