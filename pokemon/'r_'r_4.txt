''''r'r 4''' is a [[glitch Pokemon]] found in ''[[Pokémon Yellow]]''. It is a {{Type|Water}}-type Pokémon. It can only be acquired via the [[Johto Guard Glitch]] with [[Politoed]]. It occupies hexadecimal slot E1 and will become a [['v]] if traded to ''[[Pokémon Red and Blue]]''. Although the summary says it's pure {{Type|Fighting}}-type, type effectiveness suggests pure Water-type. 

[[File:'r_'r_4_PokeDex_Entry.png|thumb|'r 'r 4's Pokédex entry.]]
It also features a glitch screen, particularly the purple-tint variant. It can evolve into a [[' B' ゥ]] or a [[Growlithe]] at level 4. It uses sprites of random Pokémon (depending on level, e.g.: [[Mew]]'s at level 6 or [[Electabuzz]]'s at level 9).
