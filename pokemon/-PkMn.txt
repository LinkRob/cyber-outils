TITLE : -PkMn

'''-PkMn''' is a [[glitch Pokémon]] that is found only in [[Pokémon Red and Blue]]. It occupies hexidecimal slot DE. It is a hybrid of [[Magnemite]]. When encountered, the game will start to play a glitched song. It can only be obtained by doing the [[Johto Guard Glitch]] with [[Marill]]. When its Pokédex entry is viewed, its cry never ends.
