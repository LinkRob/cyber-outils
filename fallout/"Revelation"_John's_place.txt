'''"Revelation" John's place''' was a building in [[New Canaan (Van Buren)|New Canaan]]'s [[New Canaan town|Union station]].

==Layout==
A super mutant named [[Revelation John|John]] (nicknamed "Revelation") lives here. The building is situated in the north section of the Union Station, left of the destroyed home gardens and with a road passing through the south of it.

==Appearances==
"Revelation" John's place was going to appear in ''[[Van Buren]]'', the canceled ''Fallout 3'' by [[Black Isle Studios]].
