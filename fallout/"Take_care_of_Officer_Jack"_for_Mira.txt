'''"Take care of Officer Jack" for Mira''' is a [[Fallout 2 quests|quest]] intended for combative characters in ''[[Fallout 2]]''. It is the only option left if talking [[Jack (Fallout 2)|Jack]] down doesn't work.

==Detailed walkthrough==
Talk to [[Mira]] with a stupid character and at least 7 [[Strength (Fallout)|Strength]]; [[Merk]] will point the [[Chosen One]] in her direction if spoken to. She will tell the player character to climb a [[Rope (Fallout 2)|rope]] over the city wall.

If it is nighttime when entering downtown, Jack will be at the police station. Wait until dawn and he will walk over to the power station and threaten to blow himself up, which is the best opportunity to waste him without annoying anyone; one can actually let him blow himself up with the same result. When he's dead, head over to the [[NCR Bazaar|bazaar]] and talk to Mira to claim the reward.

==Notes==
* This quest pops up when Jack dies even if it wasn't active before, so Mira's reward can be earned, despite not getting the extra [[Experience Points|XP]] for killing Jack.
** If Jack's suicide bombing was not prevented, she says she'll give the Chosen One a rad-a-waster, but gives no [[Booze (Fallout 2)|booze]] as usual, if one was bought.
** If Jack is killed, however, she gives a [[HK P90c]].
* For a normally non-stupid character, the best time to get this quest would be after talking Jack out of blowing himself up. Use [[Mentats (Fallout 2)|Mentats]] if [[Intelligence (Fallout)|Intelligence]] is below 7, [[Psycho (Fallout 2)|Psycho]] and/or [[Buffout (Fallout 2)|Buffout]] to get the quest, then go kill Jack silently in his cell for the 2000 XP. IN must go back below 4 to collect the reward.
* If one fought [[Lenny (NCR)|Lenny]] in the ring, Mira's dialogue will be different, thus the quest is unattainable.
** If one has already gotten the quest, Mira's reward can't be gained (whether 'stupid' or not).
