'''"Mr. Smith"''' is the pseudonym of a man living in the [[Capital Wasteland]] in [[Timeline (2250-2300)#2277|2277]].

==Background==
He hired [[Reilly's Rangers]] to map the terrain of the Capital Wasteland, and in particular, the “urban sections” of downtown [[Washington, D.C.]], which the [[Lone Wanderer]] can do on their behalf for a fixed sum.<ref>[[Ranger compound terminal entries#Personal Entry 177|Ranger compound terminal entries; Reilly's terminal, Personal Entry 177]]</ref> The contract for the project specifies certain tactical information to be acquired as part of the mapping, namely instances of enemy resistance, weapons caches, strategic positions, and retreat points, which will be submitted to Smith via holotape.<ref>[[Ranger compound terminal entries#Mapping Contract|Ranger compound terminal entries; Reilly's terminal, Mapping Contract]]</ref>

==Appearances==
"Mr. Smith" is mentioned only in ''[[Fallout 3]]''.
