ARTICLE : "I am not a synth" speech
==============================

[[File:FO4_McD_D_City_speech.png|240px|thumb]]
The '''"I am not a synth" speech''' is given by [[McDonough|Mayor McDonough]] in [[Diamond City]].

==Background==
The mayor gives the speech shortly after the [[Sole Survivor]] enters the city for the first time and approaches [[the Wall]], on the north side of the [[Diamond City market|market]]. Its reception by the city's populace was relatively poor; immediately after its conclusion, a resident can be heard mumbling to himself that the mayor is "so full of shit."<ref>[[Resident (Diamond City)|Diamond City resident]]: ''"{{Tooltip|He's so full of shit...|to yourself, but not mumbling, you don't care if people hear you}}"''<br />([[DiamondCityResident.txt|Diamond City residents' dialogue]])<!-- GenericNPC02 --></ref>

==Notes==
The transcript of a portion of the speech can also be found on McDonough's terminal.<ref>[[Diamond City terminal entries#The Wall|Diamond City terminal entries; Mayor McDonough's terminal, The Wall]]</ref>

==Appearances==
The "I am not a synth" speech occurs in ''Fallout 4''.

==Behind the scenes==
The writing of the speech, in particular the phrase "I am not a synth" itself, closely resembles the mannerisms of former U.S. President [[Richard Nixon]] and his reaction to the [[wikipedia:Watergate scandal|Watergate scandal]], during which Nixon emphatically told reporters "I am not a crook."
