'''"Little Jesus"''' is a unique [[Melee Weapons (Fallout 2)|melee weapon]] in ''[[Fallout 2]]''.

==Characteristics==
"Little Jesus" is a unique [[Combat knife (Fallout)|combat knife]]. Its damage rating surpasses those of all other non-powered single-handed [[Fallout 2 weapons#Melee Weapons|melee weapons]] available to the [[Chosen One]] and gains the Weapon Penetrate perk. Visually, Little Jesus' inventory graphic has a light red grip, compared to the standard combat knife's dark grey grip, but otherwise, they use the same [[animation frames]] as other knives.

==Location==
Found on [[Lil' Jesus Mordino]] in [[New Reno]].
