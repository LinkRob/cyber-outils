'''"Mission Critical" is a joke!''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76TP}}.

==Location==
The note can be found in [[the Trench]] in [[The Pitt (Fallout 76)|the Pitt]] during [[From Ashes to Fire]]. It is in a locker in the main laboratory area of the collapsed factory building.

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
We're being expected to pull insane hours to handle this "mission critical" task, but we aren't even given the equipment we need to do our research? We lost another vat to corrosion yesterday, and I had to use a [[Nuka-Cola (Fallout 76)|Nuka-Cola]] bottle in lieu of a graduated cylinder! If they really cared about this neutralizer so bad, maybe they'd bother to give us the most basic tools we need to do our job. This level of mismanagement is inexcusable. I bet the folks at [[HalluciGen, Inc. (company)|HalluciGen]] don't have to put up with this nonsense.
}}
