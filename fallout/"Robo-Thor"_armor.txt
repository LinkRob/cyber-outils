'''"Robo-Thor" armor''' and '''"Robo-Thor" helmet''' are a suit of [[Fallout 3 armor and clothing|armor]] cut from ''[[Fallout 3]]''.

==Characteristics==
While it looks the same as the [[Tesla armor (Fallout 3)|Tesla armor]], its stats more closely resemble the [[Enclave power armor (Fallout 3)|Enclave power armor]]. This armor also has 1000 HP more than the [[T-51b power armor (Fallout 3)|T-51b power armor]].

==Locations==
This armor set can only be obtained through [[Gamebryo console commands#Items & world manipulation|console commands]].
