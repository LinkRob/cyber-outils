'''"Excavator suit" - Final steps''' is a [[Fallout 76 notes|paper note]] {{In|FO76}}.

==Location==
The note can be found on a cabinet in the lower area in the sub-basement of the [[Hornwright Industrial headquarters]].

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
[[Daniel Hornwright|Mr. Hornwright]],

Our efforts at shuttering [[Garrahan Mining Company|Garrahan Mining]]'s "[[Excavator power armor|Excavator Suit]]" endeavor are nearly complete. Thanks to my team's "interventions" into the Man versus Machine competition, our Auto-Miners are now the talk of the region, while the Garrahan's stock price is in free fall.

Now - the final step.

From everything my advisors tell me, the tech behind the Excavator suits is solid. They say their mineral detection technology borders on revolutionary. There's an impressive amount of proprietary technology inside those walls; a hostile takeover of Garrahan Mining is the savvy move right now. A fleet of automated, nigh-indestructible Auto-Miners, armed with the capacity to independently seek out mineral wealth? 

That's the future, Daniel.

That's how we beat [[Atomic Mining Services|AMS]] at their own game. 

We just need to be bold enough to do it.

I would suggest we move quickly on this. Your "[[The Motherlode (robot)|Motherlode]]" project will still be there once we establish ourselves as the premier industrial concern east of the [[Rocky Mountains|Rockies]]. You say the word and I can have the board here within 24 hours to start talking strategy.

I would also highly suggest you burn this document as soon as you've read it.

[[Dutch Wharton]]
Senior Vice President - Corporate Engagement
[[Hornwright Industrial]]
}}
