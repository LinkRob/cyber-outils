'''"Missing" shipment''' is a [[Fallout: New Vegas notes#Lonesome Road|paper note]] in the ''[[Fallout: New Vegas]]'' [[Fallout: New Vegas add-ons|add-on]] ''[[Lonesome Road (add-on)|Lonesome Road]]''.

==Location==
It can be found in the [[Gun case (Fallout: New Vegas)|gun case]] with the [[Red Victory grenade rifle]] in a ruined tent on a cliff, west side of [[Dry Wells]]. 

==Transcript==
{{Transcript|text=
General,

We found a seized shipment of [[China|Chinese]] "Red Victory" model grenade launchers and thought you might like to have one for yourself. They're quicker to reload then{{sic}} our "[[Great Bear grenade rifle|Great Bears]]," but they don't quite pack the same punch. Still, it'll be nice to give those bastards a taste of their own medicine if it comes to that, right?
}}
