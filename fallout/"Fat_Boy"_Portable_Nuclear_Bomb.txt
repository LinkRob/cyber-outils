The '''Fat Boy''' is a portable nuclear bomb and [[Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel weapons|weapon]] that was to be featured in the canceled ''[[Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel 2]]''.

==Characteristics==
Presenting the Fat Boy, for all your daily needs. This handy little baby packs all the power of a nuclear bomb, with little of the clean up! Batteries required.<ref>[[Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel 2 design document#Page 18|Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel 2 design document - Page 18]]</ref>

==Components==
# Homemade plasma mine
# Mail order uranium kit
# Pip Boy neutron generator
# Conductive sphere
# Radiation dampener device

==Notes==
The Fat Boy was to be [[Jaffe]]'s "Uber Weapon", once all of the pieces had been retrieved he could repair it himself or pay an NPC.<ref>[[Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel 2 design document#Page 17|Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel 2 design document - Page 17]]</ref>
