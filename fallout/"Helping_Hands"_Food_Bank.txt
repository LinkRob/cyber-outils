'''"Helping Hands" Food Bank''' was a pre-[[Great War|War]] organization mentioned in ''[[Fallout 4]]''.

==Background==
Members of the [[US Coast Guard]] captured a smuggler named [[Connor (Fallout 4)|Connor]] attempting to carry 53 knock-off [[Jangles the Moon Monkey (Fallout 4)|Jangles the Moon Monkey dolls]], 108 toy soldiers, and 15 [[Fancy Lads Snack Cakes (Fallout 4)|Fancy Lads Snack Cakes]] into the country. The coast guardsmen disposed of the toys and donated the snack cakes to the food bank.<ref>[[Coast Guard pier terminal entries#CASE 266-Sm: Connor Smuggling Logs|Coast Guard pier terminal entries; CASE 266-Sm: Connor Smuggling Logs]]</ref>

==Appearances==
"Helping Hands" Food Bank is mentioned only in ''Fallout 4''.
