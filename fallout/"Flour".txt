'''"Flour"''' is a [[Fallout: New Vegas miscellaneous items|miscellaneous item]] in ''[[Fallout: New Vegas]]''.

==Characteristics==
Due to the suggestive quotation marks and the nature of [[Jack (Great Khans)|Jack]] and [[Diane (Fallout: New Vegas)|Diane]]'s work, these bags of "flour" are most likely satchels of a powdery drug such as [[cocaine]] or [[methamphetamine]], or perhaps a substance used to cut drugs, such as baking powder.

"Flour" has no use other than its novelty as a rare and somewhat humorous collectible.

==Locations==
Three bags can be stolen around the trailers at the [[Red Rock drug lab]].
