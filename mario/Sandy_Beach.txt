The '''Sandy Beach''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Sandy Beach is a regular beach located in the [[Seaside Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. A few palm trees are in the front, just before the beach itself, consisting of sand and the ocean. Sandy Beach consists of several seashells.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
There are no quests that begin at Sandy Beach, nor can anyone be found there. The Sandy Beach only has a role during the quest [[Accessorize!]], where the player's character has to come here to get a [[Lovely Seashell]] so that [[Goombob]] can present it as a gift to [[Goombetty]]. Otherwise, if the quest is not active, the game dialogue merely notes on how the beach looks nice, as well as its presence of Lovely Seashells.
