A '''Bellbell Plant''' is a species of plant that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Bellbell Plants are found throughout [[Lavalava Island]] mainly in [[Jade Jungle]]. Whenever [[Mario]] touches a Bellbell Plant, it will light up also making a ringing sound. These plants also indicate that there is a hidden block nearby that contains either items or coins. The items are often very rare that cannot be encountered normally in the game such as a [[Repel Gel]].
