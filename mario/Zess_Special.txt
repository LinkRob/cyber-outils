A '''Zess Special''' is a recipe made by [[Zess T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Description ==
Zess Special is among many of Zess T.'s dishes bearing her name. Zess T. can cook the Zess Special if she is given an [[Ultra Shroom]] and a [[Slow Shroom]] or a [[Fire Flower]]. Using the Zess Special restores 20 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 20 [[Flower Point|FP]].
