'''Cheepy''' is a character that appears in the episode "[[The Bird! The Bird!]]" from ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' He is the child of [[Cheepy's mother]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Cheepy is a little pink Birdo with a red scarf he wears.

== Biography ==
Cheepy lives at the [[Land of Ice]] on top of a tall mountain with [[Cheepy's mother|his mother]].

=== Cheepy's disappearance ===
At some point prior to the show, Cheepy was presumably buried in the snow frozen in a block of ice. His mother noticed Cheepy's disappearance, resulting his mother to call the [[Missing Bird Bureau]] to help her find Cheepy.

=== Rescuing Cheepy ===
{{Main|The Bird! The Bird!}}
After Cheepy's mother mistook [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] as his son, [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] and [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] ventured to go after Cheepy's mother and rescue Toad. However, the group discovered Cheepy frozen in a block of ice. Mario in his [[Fire Mario|fire form]], threw fire balls to defrost the ice block.

[[File:The Bird! The Bird (04).png|150px|thumb|left|Toad recognizing Cheepy.]]
After Cheepy's mother dropped Toad reuniting with his group, Toad assumed that Cheepy is his missing child. Upon the [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and his [[Koopa Troop]]'s arrival, Mario and Luigi let Toad and Toadstool take Cheepy to his mother while the brothers distract the King Koopa. After King Koopa and his troop retreated, Mario and his friends for finding her baby and invited them to her house for dinner.
