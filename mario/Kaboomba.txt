'''Kaboombas''' (also simply named '''Cannons''') are walking [[cannon]] enemies featured in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]] and ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. Kaboombas resemble navy-blue cannons with webbed feet. They typically attack by shooting [[Cannonball]]s which explode upon hitting the ground.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
A Kaboomba attacks by firing large [[cannonball]]s onto the ground, upon which the cannonball explodes after a few seconds. In the ''Yoshi's Island'' series, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can defeat a Kaboomba by either tossing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at it or by [[Ground Pound]]ing it.

==In {{series|Yoshi's Island}}==
===''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' / ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''===
[[Image:Kaboomba Cave.png|thumb|left|200px|A Kaboomba takes aim.]]
Kaboombas first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and later go on to appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. In these games, they can typically be found in caves and castles (in levels such as [[The Cave of the Mystery Maze]] and [[Raphael the Raven's Castle]]). In this game, they are often seen walking around and as soon as [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] gets close, they pause and fire a cannonball which explodes after a couple seconds. Kaboombas are immune to stomps due to their sturdy nature but can be beaten with [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s or [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]]s.

===''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''===
[[Image:Kaboomba Deluxe.png|thumb|Yoshi above a Kaboomba.]]
Kaboombas return as enemies, and they are typically encountered in [[castle]] levels such as [[Bowser's Castle Break-In]]. Just as before, they walk around and fire their [[cannonball]]s at [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] upon sight, once again they are tough enough to withstand a stomp but can still be beaten with [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] tosses or [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]]s.
{{br}}
== In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' ==
[[Image:Kaboomba Cannon Beach.png|thumb|left|200px|[[Peach]] sees a marching Kaboomba.]]
Kaboombas (under the name '''Cannon''' in this game) return as enemies. They retain their behaviors from before (once again walking naturally then launching [[cannonball]]s at [[Peach]]), [[Peach]] can easily beat a Kaboomba by whacking it with [[Perry]] or by using the [[Rage]] [[Vibe]]. If stomped on, the Kaboomba will flip over rendering it vulnerable allowing [[Peach]] to pick it up and toss it at other foes or objects. Kaboombas are first encountered in [[Shriek Mansion 3-8]], and go on to appear in various levels in subsequent worlds. Kaboombas also have a [[Calm]] variant named [[Calm Cannon]].
{{br}}

==Trivia==
*It is interesting to note that the name "Kaboomba" is a portmanteau of the words "Kaboom!" (onomatopoeia for an explosion) and [[Goomba]]. This portmanteau is ironic due to the Kaboombas' lack of similarities with [[Goomba]]s, though, the name "Kaboomba" may just be a form of "Kaboom!". Also of note is that the [[Kaboomka Fireworks Factory]] was named in a similar style.
*In the Nintendo Official Player's guide for ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', an image of a Kaboomba's cannonball is shown on the enemy chapter rather than an actual Kaboomba.
