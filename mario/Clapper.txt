'''Clapper''' is a seal and an [[Animal Friend]] from the ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' franchise. He makes his first appearance in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and also returns in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''. Aside from these two appearances, Clapper makes a cameo in the Freestyle Mode of ''[[Donkey Konga 2]]''.

In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'', there are seals with a similar appearance, simply named [[Seal]].

==In the games==
In both ''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' and ''Donkey Kong Land 2'', Clapper only appears in two levels, [[Lava Lagoon]] and his titular location [[Clapper's Cavern]]. In both appearances, Clapper's role is to cool the water so [[Diddy Kong]] and [[Dixie Kong]] can progress, although the temperature of the liquid that he cools differs between both levels. If either [[Kong]] jumps onto Clapper, he exhales cool air on to the water and then claps his paws. The effect of Clapper's cool air is temporary, and it wears off after several seconds.

In Lava Lagoon, Diddy and Dixie have to jump on a Clapper throughout so that he can cool the red-hot water into a moderate temperature. In Clapper's Cavern, while the water is at moderate temperature, there is a [[Lockjaw]] who follows Diddy and Dixie around, making it hazardous for them to swim in the water, although in ''Donkey Kong Land 2'', the water is frigid enough to damage Diddy and Dixie, so either Kong must jump onto a Clapper to have it cool the water.
