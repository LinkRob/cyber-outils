'''Smart Bombs''' are an item in the ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]'' issue "[[Duh Stoopid Bomb!]]." They were created to mitigate the mind-numbing effect that the [[Stupid Bomb|titular weapon]] may inflict.

== Description ==
Smart Bombs look like cartoon bombs, though their colour is red to distinguish the Smart Bomb from the [[Stupid Bomb]]. Detonating them causes a gas that raises the intelligence of those who breathe it to burst out of their insides. As are their stupidifying cousins, Smart Bombs are very fragile, as they can be easily detonated from blunt force trauma.

== History ==
Smart Bombs make their debut after the Snifit training the [[Shy Guy]] army accidentally stupifies his own troops with a Stupid Bomb. He reports to [[Bowser]], who shows him the Smart Bombs. The Snifit curiously grabs one, which spontaneously bursts in his hands, increasing his intelligence. He then usurps Bowser's position, but the Shy Guys are now spending their time creating as big an explosion as they can by slamming multiple Stupid Bombs together.

== Trivia ==

* The [https://starfox.fandom.com/wiki/Nova_Bomb Nova Bomb] from the ''Star Fox'' series has been referred to as a Smart Bomb in ''Super Smash Bros.'' games.
