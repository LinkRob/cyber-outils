'''Flutters''' (also called '''Flying Wigglers'''<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 126.</ref>) are the winged {{wp|imago}} of [[Wiggler]]s that first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. When hit, they act like the normal Wiggler, getting enraged. Instead of flying around aimlessly, though, they charge straight for the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] that hit them. In early games, they had light orange noses contrasting Wigglers' dark brown ones, though most recent appearances give both dark brown noses.

==History==
===''Yoshi'' franchise===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
{{multiframe|[[File:Flutter SMW2.png]] [[File:SMW2 Flying Wiggler mad.png]]}}
[[File:SMA3 GO! GO! MARIO!! Screenshot.png|thumb|left|A Flying Wiggler in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'']]
Flying Wigglers first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' are only seen in the level [[GO! GO! MARIO!!]]. Just before the goal, a swarm of Flying Wigglers appear over a flat turf. They fly babyfootforward in a wavy manner. If attacked with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] or a jump, they turn angry and charge towards [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s current position, disappearing off-screen after a time. Flying Wigglers can cause Yoshi to drop [[Baby Mario]] off his back if they come into contact with him. Yoshi can defeat them with a [[Ground Pound]] or by eating and turning them into an egg. If spat out instead, they fly away. They can also be defeated by touching them as [[Superstar Mario]].

In addition to reprising their role in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake, ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', a few Flying Wigglers also appear at the beginning of the secret level, [[Endless World of Yoshis]].
{{br|left}}

====''Tetris Attack''====
A Flying Wiggler appears in ''[[Tetris Attack]]'', which depicts it as a friend of [[Yoshi]] rather than an enemy. It replaces the jewel fairy, Ruby, from ''[[Tetris Attack#Panel de Pon|Panel de Pon]]''. Flying Wiggler is playable in Time Trial mode, and can be fought and rescued on the Flower Stage in the main Vs. Com mode. In the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] version, it is paired with the similarly-themed [[Eggo-Dil]].

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[File:Flutter 3ds.gif|thumb|100px|A Flutter in ''Yoshi's New Island'']]
Flutters appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' in the level [[Ride the Blarggwich]], where they act slightly different from their original appearance. They simply fly up and down on a predetermined line, like red [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, and cannot be eaten, though some fly the same as their debut appearance near the end of the level. If angered however, they charge in Yoshi's direction. Their angry appearance has their original nose color, along with some other changes.

====''Yoshi's Crafted World''====
[[File:Flutter in Yoshi's Crafted World.jpg|200px|thumb|A Flutter in ''Yoshi's Crafted World'']]
Flutters reappear in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'' during the level [[Weighing Acorns]] where they are seen flying above flower platforms and later on when Yoshi is on a bus ride. They are invincible and cannot be defeated by any means of attack. If Yoshi angers one, they swoop in his direction. Unlike previously, they will stop being angry after a few seconds. There is also a Flutter Sign [[List of crafts in Yoshi's Crafted World|craft]] that is found on the flip side of [[Spring Sprung Trail]] and serves as its local [[Blockafeller]]'s first souvenir request.

===''Super Mario-kun''===
[[File:Battra Flutter SuperMarioKun.jpg|thumb|left|A moth-like adult form of Wiggler]]
[[File:FlutterSuperMarioKun.png|thumb|An angry Flutter after Mario has plucked its flower]]
Prior to Flutters' official introduction in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', one Wiggler in volume 6 of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' transforms into a [[Battra Hanachan|moth-like form]] after [[Bowser]] and a [[Magikoopa]] replace the flower on its head with a [[Muncher]]. It otherwise looks very little like a Flutter, notably having its nose interpreted as a pair of pincers.

In volume 14 of ''Super Mario-kun'', a Flutter gets infuriated after Mario plucks a flower off it.

===''Mario Party'' series=== 
====''Mario Party 5''====
Flutters appear as the  [[Wiggler Capsule]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', which can take players to the [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]. They also appear in some [[minigame]]s.

====''Mario Party 6''====
[[File:Orb Flutter - MP6.png|thumb|left|120px|The Flutter Orb as seen in ''Mario Party 6'']]
Flutters reappear in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'', as well as the [[Flutter Orb]] which possesses the same effects as the Wiggle Capsule from the previous game.

====''Mario Party Advance''====
{{main|Flutter (character)}}
A Flutter appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'', where she resides on the third floor of the [[Mushroom Condo]]. She is in love with [[Mr. I]], but is also the crush of [[Petal Guy (character)|Petal Guy]] who seeks her affection.

====''Mario Party 7''====
[[File:Flutter Orb MP7.jpg|thumb|120px|A Flutter Orb in ''Mario Party 7'']]
Flutters once again appear in the Flutter Orb in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. It also appears in the minigame [[Spider Stomp]], where it is kidnapped and has to be rescued.

====''Mario Party 8''====
Flutter also makes several cameos in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It can be found in the minigames [[Specter Inspector]] as one of the hiding enemies (found in the piano), [[Moped Mayhem]] as a racer, and in the minigame contest [[Test for the Best]], holding the 4,000 points sign.

====''Mario Party: Island Tour''====
[[File:Star-Crossed Skyway.png|thumb|left|Flutter in ''Mario Party: Island Tour'']]
Flutter appears in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' for the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. On the [[Star-Crossed Skyway]] board, Flutter appears in the first, second, and last Star Stages. It gives out ten [[Mini Star]]s to the player who reaches it. It retains its updated nose from ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

====''Super Mario Party''====
[[File:SMP image3.png|thumb|Bowser buying an item from Flutter's item shop]]
Flutter appears in ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' as a non-playable character who owns the item shop. On [[Megafruit Paradise]] and its team-mode variant, [[Watermelon Walkabout]], after passing Flutter's shop, the thermometer next to it rises a small amount every time. When it reaches the top, a giant scoop of ice cream falls and destroys Flutter's shop, and Flutter tells the player that they need to clean it up, making the shop unavailable to all the players for the rest of the game.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tGdhz_Ggigo Showing Off The Flutter Ice Cream Event] "Youtube"</ref>{{Dead link}}
{{br}}

===''Mario Power Tennis'' / ''New Play Control! Mario Power Tennis''===
[[File:Flutter Return.png|thumb|Wiggler having turned into Flutter for his Defensive Power Shot]]
In ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', Wiggler transforms into a Flutter for [[Flutter Return|his Defensive Power Shot]]. If the player is to win a match as Wiggler, he will transform into Flutter during his winning animation.

===''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''===
[[File:Flutter PMSS.png|thumb|left|100px|Flutter as seen in ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''.]]
{{main|Wiggler (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)}}
Flutter makes its debut in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' as [[Wiggler (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|Wiggler]]'s fully grown and true form. Flutter explains he became his form after eating a plentiful amount of leaves from the forest. Flutter will offer to fly [[Mario]] and [[Kersti]] out to [[Bowser's Sky Castle]]. He also refers to himself as "grown up". In this game it appears with the brown nose of a Wiggler rather than the usual pink nose. Though Wiggler/Flutter can no longer take Mario to the shipwreck in Whitecap Beach, a pipe appears that transports Mario to the southwestern area of the island.

===''Mario Golf: World Tour''===
A large Flutter appears in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'' in the 9th hole of [[Wiggler Park]], where it hovers above the green. If a ball touches it, it does a loop.

===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
[[File:PDSMBE-FlutterFlameChomp-TeamImage.png|thumb|150px|A Flutter and Flame Chomp in ''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition'']]
Flutter appears in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]'' as an enemy and ally alongside a [[Flame Chomp]]. Its types are Fire and Light.

===''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''===
{{main|Wiggler (Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam)}}
A Flutter makes an appearance in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. After the Wiggler boss battle, a seemingly-exhausted Wiggler, along with some [[Paragoomba]]s, goes up into the sky, then returns as Flutter. Flutter takes this form for the rest of the game, and can take Mario, Luigi and Paper Mario between [[Gloomy Woods]] and [[Mount Brrr]].
{{br}}

===Other appearances and references===
====''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS''====
Although Flutters themselves do not appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', the player can attach wings to Wigglers, which references Flutters.

====''Super Mario Odyssey''====
[[File:SMO Lost Moon 12.png|thumb|Screenshot of the large butterfly in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.]]
In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', there is a location called the [[Forgotten Isle]] that is inhabited by [[Tropical Wiggler]]s. There is a mission on this isle titled "[[List of Power Moons in the Lost Kingdom|A Butterfly's Treasure]]" that features a large, colorful butterfly. ''[[The Art of Super Mario Odyssey]]'' directly suggests that this butterfly is an adult form of the Tropical Wigglers.<ref name=SMOArt>Kazuya Sakai (ed.). ''[[The Art of Super Mario Odyssey]]''. First English Edition. Translated by {{wp|TransPerfect}}. Milwaukie: {{wp|Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse}}, 2019. page 167. ISBN: 978-1-50671-375-5.</ref>

====''Super Mario Maker 2''====
Although no actual Flutters appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', Wigglers attached to wings, which references Flutters, make a return, retaining their behavior in its predecessor.
{{br}}

===Unused appearances===
====''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''====
Flutters [[List of Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3 pre-release and unused content|were originally going to appear]] in [[World-e]] of ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', where they were to have the same behavior as in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', but they were left unused likely due to the discontinuation of the [[e-Reader]].
