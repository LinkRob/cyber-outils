'''''Yoshi Demo''''' is a [[Game Boy Advance]] [[tech demo]] based on ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. A ROM of it was released as a part of the system's SDK, as the purpose of ''Yoshi Demo'' was to showcase the Game Boy Advance's technical features. The ''Yoshi Demo'' was showcased in images of the Game Boy Advance in 2000, prior to the system being released a year later.

==Gameplay==
[[Yoshi]] is the playable character, and he is controlled in a grassland [[level]] that endlessly loops as he progresses along in it.

==Trivia==
*Yoshi's shoes are blue instead of their usual orange.
