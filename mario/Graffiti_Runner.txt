'''Graffiti Runner''' is a minigame in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame is set in [[Bowser Jr.'s Playroom]]. The basic goal similar to [[Stamp Out!]] from the {{series|Mario Party}} in that players try to cover most of the area with their color, except this is done by creating [[goop|paint]] from their respective [[Magic Paintbrush]]es.
