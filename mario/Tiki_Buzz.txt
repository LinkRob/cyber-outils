'''Tiki Buzzes''' are an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tiki Buzzes are wooden tikis in the shape of a bongo drum. Their bodily features include carved black eyeballs, a yellow beak, red wings, brown tail feathers, and three beaded strings attached to the bottom. Tiki Buzzes are nearly the size of [[Diddy Kong]].

===Behavior===
Tiki Buzzes fly in various patterns, either from side to side vertically or horizontally.

==In ''Donkey Kong Country Returns'' / ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]''==
Tiki Buzzes commonly appear within the [[Mine Cart]] levels, such as [[Crazy Cart]], where the [[Kong]]s must often land their Mine Cart on them to reach higher rails. Tiki Buzzes are harmful if touched from the sides or the bottom, but they can be defeated with any attack. Even outside of Mine Cart levels, the Kongs can jump onto Tiki Buzzes to reach higher locations and cross wide gaps.

==In ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''==
Tiki Buzzes appear in the [[Donkey Kong Country Returns (microgame)|Donkey Kong Country Returns]] [[microgame]], in which they are one of the enemies that have to be defeated. Certain characters can defeat a Tiki Buzz by jumping onto it.

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''===
[[File:Tiki Buzz 3DS.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Three Tiki Buzzes in Smash Run mode in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'']]
Tiki Buzzes are enemies in [[Smash Run]], in which they can be defeated by being jumped onto, like in ''Donkey Kong Country Returns''. There is also a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] of a Tiki Buzz.
