==History==
The Flames are rather rare enemies that can be only found in [[Crystal Caves]] in one area. [[Lanky Kong]] will be tasked by the [[Rabbit (Donkey Kong 64)|racing rabbit]] from [[Fungi Forest]] to defend the [[TNT Barrel]] he is tied to from the Flames. Lanky must defeat the Flames before they have a chance to light up and blow up the TNT Barrel. Success will result in a [[Golden Banana]] for Lanky.
