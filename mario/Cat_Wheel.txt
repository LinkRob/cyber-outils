A '''Cat Wheel''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
A Cat Wheel is a type of wheel depicting a pink cat paw. It can only be spun by a character by scratching it while they are in their [[Cat Mario|Cat]] form. Spinning a Cat Wheel affects the environment in some way, such as a platform rising or a secret area opening.

Cat Wheels are likely a successor to the [[Tail Wheel]]s seen in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', but altered to match the cat theme previous game over a [[Tanooki Mario (power-up)|tanooki]] theme.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 3D World''===
These wheels are first found in [[Mount Beanpole]]. Using a Cat Wheel is often required to obtain a collectible item, such as a [[Green Power Star]].

===''[[Bowser's Fury]]''===
A Cat Wheel can extend [[rising pillar]]s in the Lakeside and Wasteland regions so that Cat Mario can collect their corresponding [[Cat Shine]], "Climb to the Shine" and "Spin, Scramble, Shine," respectively.
