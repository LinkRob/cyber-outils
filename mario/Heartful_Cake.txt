'''Heartful Cake''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
[[File:Heartful_Cake_TTYD.png|thumb|left]]
[[Mario]] can obtain the Heartful Cake by giving specific items to [[Zess T.]] in [[Rogueport]]. By using the Heartful Cake, Mario can restore twenty of his [[Flower Point|FP]].

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Heartful Cake is made by [[Dyllis]] in [[Flopside]]. Mario can use this item any time to gradually restore his [[Heart Point|HP]]. It is made from cooking a [[Cake Mix]] and a [[Lovely Chocolate]].
