'''Miles Tones''' is a minor supporting character in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
Miles Tones' role varies between the two versions, but he has a consistent purpose of managing the [[Information Booth]] in [[Beanbean Castle Town]].

In the original, his name is not specified, and his purpose is to sell information to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] for 100 [[coin]]s, normally indicating where to go or what to do next to complete the current objective of the main story. Near the end of the game, when Mario and Luigi are ready to travel to [[Bowser's Castle]], the Information Booth no longer has any information left to give.

In the remake, his name is revealed by another [[Beanish]] character at the [[Beanbean International Airport]], who plans to photograph [[Princess Peach]] upon her arrival from the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and sell it to Miles Tones, whom he refers to as the town story collector. In this game, Miles Tones can be located in the same place, though now rewards Mario and Luigi in exchange for information about their accomplishments thus far, viewing their Records to do so. Requirements include fighting a certain number of battles, defeating a certain number of enemies, performing a certain number of Excellent attacks, dodging a certain number of enemy attacks and uncovering a certain percentage of [[bean]]s and [[block]]s in the game, whereas rewards include items, recordings and gear. Once all of these achievements have been completed, Miles Tones reveals he is leaving his shop and thanks the duo for the information they have shared with him. He declines to reveal where he plans to go, and is not seen again for the remainder of the game.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
His name is a pun on "milestones," just with an added space.
