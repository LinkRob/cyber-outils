The '''Invincibility Mushroom''' is an item debuting in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

==Description==
If the player loses a specific number of lives in a level, a sprout with an Invincibility Mushroom will appear. Upon collecting the Mushroom, the player will be invincible throughout the entire level, much like the [[Invincibility Leaf]] from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. It does not protect against [[lava]], [[bottomless pit]]s, or being crushed.
