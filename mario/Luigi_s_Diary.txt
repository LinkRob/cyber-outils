|notes = Located in the basement that must be spin-jumped. Can be read at anytime afterwards.}}'''Luigi's Diary''' is an object found in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is written by [[Paper Luigi]] when he is staying at his and [[Mario]]'s [[Mario's Pad|house]].

==History==

During the course of the game, Luigi's Diary can be viewed after Mario obtains the Super Boots from [[Boo's Mansion]]. Mario can then [[Ground Pound|Spin Jump]] an area of the floor to access the basement. In his Diary, Luigi tends to reference other games outside the series.

==Entries==

===Part 1===
{{Q|Once again, my [[Paper Mario (character)|brother]] went on an exciting [[Paper Mario|journey]]. Once again, he went alone. It's so unfair! I remember the carefree days when we played [[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Golf]] and [[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Tennis]] and had [[Mario Party (series)|Parties]]. I remodeled the [[Mario Bros.' House|house]] and made a secret basement. My brother has no idea! It's the perfect place to write in you, my secret diary. I heard that a ghost appeared in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] today. It was big, really big. And it had red eyes, a giant, gaping mouth and a mustache.|Luigi}}

I'm sure it's there, but I can't look back! No! No! Get away! I think I'll be safe if I don't freeze with fear. I'll just shut my eyes and take five steps back, and then I'll jump and dash into bed. ==Trivia==
*Luigi's Diary is one of two diaries that can be viewed with the other being [[Bowser's Diary]].
*If [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] heads back to his house as soon he gets the Super Boots and heads down into the basement, he will actually catch [[Paper Luigi]] writing in his diary. Luigi will run off and hide his diary, making it unviewable for the remainder of the game.
*When Paper Luigi mentions in his first entry that he and Mario would play Golf, Tennis and Parties, he's referring to three different games of the Mario series (''[[Mario Tennis (series)|Mario Tennis]]'', ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'', and the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party games]]'').
**This could imply that these events canonically happened. 
*One of the diary's entries mentions that Luigi is afraid of ghosts and that he wants to go on [[Luigi's Mansion (series)|his own adventure]] soon. This directly foreshadows ''[[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|Luigi's Mansion]], a game that was in development at the time.''
