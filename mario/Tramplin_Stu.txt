'''Tramplin Stus '''are beta enemies that was originally planned to appear in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Appearance==
They were featured in the Spaceworld trailer, but never made it into the final release. However, its files still exist in the final game.

==Battle==
Their only attack was to run into the player or simply drop a [[Swoopin' Stu]] or a [[Strollin' Stu]]. However, no goop was found near a Tramplin Stu for a Swooping Stu to occur.
