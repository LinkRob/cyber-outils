The '''Thwomp Bros.''' are among the [[Thwomp]] inhabitants of [[Thwomp Volcano]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', specifically appearing within the volcano's interior, [[Thwomp Caverns]]. Each Thwomp Bro hosts a different [[minigame]] that [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] has to complete in order to continue progressing down Thwomp Caverns.
