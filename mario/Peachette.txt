{{Nihongo|'''Peachette'''|キノピーチ|Kinopīchi|, lit. Toadette Peach}} is a form [[Toadette]] can assume upon getting a [[Super Crown]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' for the [[Nintendo Switch]].

== Description ==
Peachette's appearance is similar to the original [[Princess Peach]], but she has some mushroom-themed elements. In this form, Toadette can recover if she falls into the [[bottomless pit]]. However, she cannot recover a second time unless she touches another platform beforehand, but she can recover from a falling platform. She can perform a midair jump by pressing {{button|ns|R}}.

Peachette bears a similar resemblance to Peach, only with several differences in her attire. Her hair is the same color as Peach's, although the rear is converted into pigtails similar to Toadette's appearance. Her eyes are pewter gray as opposed to Peach's eye color being vivid blue. Her earrings are scarlet red as opposed to Peach's earrings being blue. The top part of her dress contains puffy sleeves and a raspberry pink bow with a bright yellow brooch attached to the center. The collar décor at the top of the dress and strap bang in the middle of the dress are each colored rose pink. The lower portion of the dress has three layers; the first layer being standard pink with bright azalea pink circle spots, the second layer is bright azalea pink, and the third layer is vast white. Her footwear consists of old-fashioned Amish shoes colored chestnut brown, and her legs are cloaked in white tights.

== In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' ==
Peachette appears in ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe'' as a form of [[Toadette]]. Toadette will transform into Peachette after obtaining the Super Crown.

== In {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
=== ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
[[File:MKT Peachette Queen Bee.jpg|thumb|150px|Peachette in the [[Queen Bee (kart)|Queen Bee]] kart on {{classic course|GCN|Dino Dino Jungle}} in ''Mario Kart Tour'']]
Peachette appears as a playable character, making her debut in the ''Mario Kart'' series. She is one of the three characters you can be played from the start via the pipe, along with [[Toad (character)|Toad]] and Toadette. She is also classified as a High-End character.

===''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
Peachette was added into ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' in Wave 6 of the [[Booster Course Pass]], returning from ''Mario Kart Tour''. Peachette was added on November 9 2023 

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Peachette is a form of Toadette who also appears as a primary spirit in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''. The player must fight the possessed fighters of [[Princess Peach]], [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] in their default costumes along with a Mii Brawler in its Toad costume. This also concluded that Peach, Mario, and Luigi is one of the fighters to have [[w:c:supersmashbros:Puppet Fighter|Puppet Fighters]] in their default costumes. Peachette appeared in the "Super Smash Sisters" event that ran from May 30, 2019, to June 4, 2019.

==Behind the scenes==
=== Etymology ===
''Peachette'' is a portmanteau of ''Peach'' and ''Toadette''. ''Peach'' refers to appearance resembling [[Princess Peach]]. ''Toadette'' derives from ''Toad'', the type of species, and ''Jeanette''. ''Jeanette'' is a female name, comes from Hebrew meaning "God is gracious".
