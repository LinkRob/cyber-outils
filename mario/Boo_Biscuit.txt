'''Boo Biscuits''' are an item in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' before later returning in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]],'' ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

==Description==
Boo Biscuits are brown biscuits in the shape of a [[Boo]]'s face and arms. When using them in battle, it makes the Bros., [[Dreamy Luigi]], [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]], or [[Bowser]] invincible for a short period of time, giving them the opportunity to dodge enemy attacks; it also, however, makes it so enemies take no damage from counterattacks and most attacks have no effect on the battle (except if the enemy flees or occasionally when the enemy gets defeated from the counterattack normally).

In ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'', Mario and Luigi no longer become transparent when using Boo Biscuits, though they are still invincible during the duration. This, alongside the Protectus [[Battle Plug]], is also the only way to dodge the fire waves that the [[Extension Corps]]'s members use in the final leg of their fight; otherwise, only the [[Guard|Emergency Guard]] is available for damage reduction.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-T_XoHVq8xQ</ref>
