'''Diamond City''' is a city in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. [[Wario]] claims that it is "his city" that he has made into a "sparkling gem of a metropolis." Here, Wario and his friends develop [[microgame]]s for [[WarioWare, Inc.]], and most of them reside here.

==Location==
Diamond City is mentioned by [[Orbulon]] to be located on [[Earth]]. A map of a planet vaguely resembling the real Earth can be seen in [[Wario's House]] in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'', with a more accurate depiction appearing in his house in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', and ''[[Game & Wario]]''. However, a completely different Earth-like planet is seen in the opening and ending cutscene of ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' and in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.
