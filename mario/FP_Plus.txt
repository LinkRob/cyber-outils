'''FP Plus''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. Equipping it increases [[Mario]]'s maximum [[Flower Point|FP]] by five. 

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:FP Plus PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Mario can acquire this badge in three different locations. Mario can purchase this badge at [[Rowf's Badge Shop]] where it is for sale at a price of 150 [[coin]]s. Another FP Plus badge is found in [[Forever Forest]] in the last area that enters the [[Boo's Mansion|Mansion]], Mario can find a rock that makes a weird face which points to the path leading to this badge. The Third and final FP Plus badge is found in [[Pleasant Path]], near the [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]]. [[Bombette]] can blow up a crack in a wall, and behind this wall is a warp pipe leading up to this badge. In this game, wearing all three of the FP Plus badges will allow Mario to go over the maximum amount of Flower Points, 50, by 15 allowing him to max out at 65.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', FP Plus retains its previous effects and can be acquired an infinite number of times. The [[Pianta Parlor]] provides these badges for a price of 100 Piantas if Mario has the [[Special Card]]. [[Rogueport Sewers]] houses this badges as well. This badge is also found in [[Boggly Woods]] . It is in front of the Tree, hidden by falling water.
