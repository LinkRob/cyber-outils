The '''Triforce Tires''' are a type of [[tire]] introduced in the ''[[The Legend of Zelda x Mario Kart 8]]'' content pack of ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and later return in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. They are associated with the [[kart]] body [[Master Cycle]] and the [[glider]] [[Hylian Kite]].
