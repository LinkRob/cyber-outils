The '''Energy Hold''' is a location in [[Bowser's body]] that appears in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. It is one of five major locations that can be explored in Bowser's body including the [[Trash Pit]], the [[Pump Works]], the [[Flab Zone]], and the [[Airway]]. The Energy Hold is also the largest area in Bowser's body.

== History ==
Mario and Luigi head to the Energy Hold after learning that one of the sages for the Star Cures, [[Durmite]], is located here. Mario and Luigi must use teamwork from Bowser who is in the Tower of Yikk to activate and deactivate the Boo-ray Machine in order to access some areas. After finding and defeating Durmite, also known as [[Wisdurm]], they earn the first [[Star Cure]] and leave the Energy Hold.
