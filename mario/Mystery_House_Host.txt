The '''Mystery House host''' is a character making her debut in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. She runs the [[Mystery House]] events.

== Profile ==
The Mystery House Host is honest for a game host. The Mystery House Host also reveals the object that is needed for the task, as seen in the [[Mystery House Throwdown]].
