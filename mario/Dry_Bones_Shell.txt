The '''Dry Bones Shell''' is a power-up that makes its appearance in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

== Description ==
The Dry Bones Shell simply resembles the shell of a [[Dry Bones]]. This item can be found by going into the [[Dry Bones]] menu and selecting the shell.

== In ''Super Mario Maker 2'' ==
[[File:SMM2 Dry Bones Shell screenshot.png|thumb|220px|left|The announcement of the Dry Bones Shell in the ''Super Mario Maker 2'' Nintendo Direct]]
The Dry Bones Shell is available in all the game styles except the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' style. Unlike the [[Spiny Shell]]s or [[Buzzy Shell]]s, the Dry Bones Shells cannot be worn over the player's head, but instead, the player can wear it, like the [[Goomba's Shoe]]. This can be used to surf across [[lava]], water, and [[Poisoned Water|poisoned water]]. The shell can also [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]], and even make the player become temporarily invincible by turning into a pile of bones. The shells can also be kicked around like [[Koopa Shell]]s, but because they are made of bones, they will be destroyed upon coming into contact with a wall.

The Dry Bones Shells can also be used to instantly take out bosses in just one hit by jumping on them with while inside the shell, even bosses like [[Bowser]], as if a [[Thwomp]] or a [[Muncher]] were landing on the bosses. The shell can also float across liquids when placed on kicked on said surfaces.

== Other appearances ==
In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', [[Invincible Mario|Invincible Luigi]] used a Dry Bones Shell from a defeated Dry Bones as a shield during the climax.
