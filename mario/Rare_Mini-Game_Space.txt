'''Rare Mini-Game Spaces''' are an object in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
Rare Mini-Game Spaces are a type of [[space]] with two yellow, five-pointed stars, one of which is larger than the other. The two stars are connected by a curved, orange line on the top, and the bottom line is also curved but is blue in color. At the bottom, there are three orange, apostrophe-like marks.

==In ''Mario Party 6''==
Rare Mini-Game Spaces exclusively appear in the boards of Solo Mode, where one is always the last space on the board. When the player's character lands onto a Rare Mini-Game Space, they end on the board with a new Rare [[minigame|mini-game]] unlocked. However, if the player's character moves past this space, they lose all of the [[coin]]s and mini-games they earned during the board.
