'''Roach Smash''' is a minigame that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player must squish a certain amount of cockroaches that appear on the screen using the touchscreen. The roaches always appear in large numbers allowing the player to quickly smash large numbers of them at once. All difficulites of this game has the player squish a certain amount (usually close to 100 of them) within 45 seconds. Completion of this minigame results in a treasure reward.
