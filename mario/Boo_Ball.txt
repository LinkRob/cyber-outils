'''Boo Ball''' is a recurring object in the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}. It first appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' for [[Nintendo GameCube]].

==Description==
It is a white ball depicting a [[Boo]] face, serving as a decoy of a Boo. In ''Luigi's Mansion'', Boo Balls depict a crudely drawn face that appears to have been drawn from a black marker, but in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', the face depicts the outline of a Boo's face properly.

==In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}==
===''Luigi's Mansion''===
While [[Luigi]] is in a room with a [[Boo]], and the [[Boo Radar]] rapidly blinks red, the furniture next to him may contain either a Boo Ball or a [[bomb]]. It it does occur, this only occurs once while visiting the room, so, but can occur again upon re-entering it. As Luigi moves around and the Boo Radar blinks rapidly a second time, the furniture next to him is guaranteed to have the actual Boo in it. A Boo Ball cannot harm Luigi, and he can vacuum it against the nozzle of his [[Poltergust 3000]] and then release to destroy the Boo Ball.

===''Luigi's Mansion 3''===
There is a Boo Ball in the [[Sandy Grand Hall]] in the [[Tomb Suites]]. Unlike in ''Luigi's Mansion'', the Boo Ball cannot be destroyed if Luigi blows it off the [[Poltergust G-00]]. Instead, Luigi can destroy the Boo Ball by firing it at the top of the pyramid. When the Boo Ball is destroyed, three [[Gold Bat]]s are released, and the player unlocks the achievement "Found and popped the beach ball in Tomb Suites."
