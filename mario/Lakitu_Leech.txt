'''Lakitu Leech''', named '''Free Gift Lakitu''' in PAL releases, is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
When a character uses Lakitu Leech, [[Lakitu]] appears and takes a randomly selected item from a chosen opponent to give to the user.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
Lakitu Leech is one of the items exclusive to [[Perilous Palace Path]]. A character can earn it either from an item given out by [[Yellow Toad]] toward the end or by winning first place in a minigame and selecting the item.

==Trivia==
*This item is functionally similar to the [[Lakitu Orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]'', especially as both involve Lakitu.
