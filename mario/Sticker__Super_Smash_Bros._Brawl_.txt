'''Stickers''' are a collectible item in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. They depict character artworks from other games, several of which are ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' ones. Collecting a sticker adds it to the Sticker Album, where they can be used for decoration on a background. There are a total of 700 different types of Stickers. In [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]], a Sticker can enhance a certain stat of a fighter by applying it to them, although some Stickers can only be applied to certain fighters.

If all 700 stickers are obtained, the player unlocks the Stickers [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]].
