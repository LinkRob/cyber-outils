'''Bells''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Mushroom Zone screenshot.png|thumb|left|A screenshot of the Mid-Point Bell of [[Mushroom Zone]]]]
A Mid-Point Bell acts as a [[checkpoint]], and [[Mario]] restarts from here if he loses a [[extra life|life]] in a [[level]] before re-entering it.
