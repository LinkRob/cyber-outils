The '''Fluffy Bluff Galaxy''' is a [[galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is the first appearance of the [[Cloud Flower]], which turns [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] into their [[Cloud Mario|Cloud]] form, as well as [[The Chimp]]. [[Cosmic Clone]]s also make an appearance in Fluffy Bluff Galaxy.

== Missions ==
=== Search for the Toad Brigade Captain ===
Mario must use the Cloud Flower to get to certain areas. After getting to the [[Toad Brigade]] [[Toad Brigade Captain|Captain]], he tells Mario he could not keep up with The Chimp. Then The Chimp rewards Mario with a [[Power Star]]. After this, the Cloud Flower is put in the Engine Room of [[Starship Mario]].

=== The Chimp's Stomp Challenge ===
[[The Chimp]] challenges Mario to stomp enemies and get 10,000 points in 120 seconds. 100 points are earned for killing an enemy, 200 for the second enemy stomped in a chain, and 300 for every enemy killed thereafter in the chain. 100 points are earned per coin, 10 per star bit, and 1,000 per 1-up. [[Octoomba]]s, [[Elite Octoomba]]s, and [[Goomba]]s are spread throughout the galaxy.

=== Every Planet Has Its Price ===
Mario must feed a hungry luma 100 coins to get to a cloudy tower with a star at the top.

=== Green Star 1 ===
The player will redo the Search for the Toad Brigade Captain mission. Green Star 1 is directly above where the player starts, and merely requires the use of a Cloud Flower to reach it.

=== Green Star 2 ===
The player will redo the Search for the Toad Brigade Captain mission. Green Star 2 is located high above the small water pond across the second bridge. The player can reach the star with the aid of the nearby Cloud Flower, but has to be wary of the shallow water.

=== Green Star 3 ===
The player will redo the Search for the Toad Brigade Captain one last time. The final Green Star is located on the stone mountain to the left of the one that The Chimp is on.

== Locations ==

=== Starting Planet (Cloud Across Valley Planet) ===
This planet is a large landmass with multiple grassy stone mountains. It also has a pond of water and some small buildings and gardens. It has a pit near its middle, ringed by tall cliffs, with a [[Black Hole]] at the very centre. Clouds floating over this valley can be used to safely reach the other side. [[Cloud Flower|Cloud Flowers]] appear prominently here.

=== Water Across Sphere Planet ===
This small planetoid is covered by shallow water, barring where Mario lands. A [[Cloud Flower]] can be used to find a [[Comet Medal]] floating above a metal spike opposite to his landing spot. [[Cosmic Clone|Cosmic Clones]] appear on this planet, but they can be destroyed by finding five [[Star Chip|Star Chips]], which form a [[Launch Star]] that leads to the Cloud Climb Mountain Planet.

=== Cloud Climb Mountain Planet ===
This massive planet has a rectangular piece of grass at the landing spot and a dirt cliff that must be climbed. [[Bomp|Bomps]] appear in this area. Far away from this piece of land lies a set of floating rocky mountains that Mario needs the [[Cloud Flower]] to get to. A [[Black Hole]] can be found between these two lands.

=== Cloud Valley Tower Planet ===
This planet can be reached by feeding a [[Hungry Luma]] 100 [[Coin|Coins]] on a large cloud platform above the starting planet.  As its name implies, it has a very tall triangular tower with clouds and platforms that must be climbed with the [[Cloud Mario|Cloud suit]]. A similar tower is found in the [[Chompworks Galaxy]].

== Trivia ==

*The Pond Planet with the [[Cosmic Clone]]s was originally going to be a lot smaller than its how it currently is, as shown in the E3 2009 trailer.
*This is the only galaxy in World 1 without a Prankster Comet (Besides the Green Comet).
