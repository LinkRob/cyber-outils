The '''4-Person Panel''', also named '''blue switch''', is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==Description==
It is a panel found in various areas. As its name suggests, the 4-Person Panel requires all four [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] to stand on it in order to activate it and open nearby gates. The 4-Person Panel has a rarer variation, the [[2-Person Panel]], which functions similarly but only either the adults, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], or the babies, [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]], have to stand on the 2-Person Panel to activate it.

There are blue [[Warp Pipe]]s with the same four-square image as a 4-Person Panel, and a blue Warp Pipe can also only be activated if all four Mario Bros. stand on it.
