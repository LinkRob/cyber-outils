The '''Monster Mixer''' is an item which appears in the ''[[Nintendo Adventure Books]]'' story ''[[Monster Mix-Up]]''.

== Description ==
The Monster Mixer resembles an oddly-designed plane with propellers at the very ends of the wings. It has two egg beaters on its front capable of causing any entities at its front to [[Amalgamates|fuse together]] randomly. The Monster Mixer was invented by [[Iggy Koopa]] for [[Bowser]] in his scheme to produce more powerful [[Koopa]]s.

Bowser is seen riding the Monster Mixer on the book's front cover. Here, the plane-like build of the Monster Mixer is shown to be yellow and orange.
