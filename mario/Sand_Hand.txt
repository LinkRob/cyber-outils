'''Sand Hands''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Sand Hand has the same color as sand, and its arm appears to be made of quicksand.

===Behavior===
Sand Hands hide under small platforms, where they attempt to grab [[Wario]] to try and drag him into the quicksand. Sand Hands have a relative named [[Water Hand]], which similarly attempt to drag Wario into the water.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Sand Hands are only encountered in one level, [[Desert Ruins]], in quicksand areas during the daytime. Sand Hands are invulnerable to attack and cannot be defeated.
