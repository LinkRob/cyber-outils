The '''Tea Kiosk''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Description==
The Tea Kiosk is a [[shop]] located in the southwest corner of the [[Toad Town Mall]], where it is run by a Yellow [[Toad (species)|Toad]], who sells [[Mushroom Tea]]. The front of the store has a logo of a steaming cup of Mushroom Tea.

==Items==
There are three types of Mushroom Tea sold, all of which are inexpensive and involve the restoration of the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]]'s [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Special Point|SP]].

*Regular Mushroom Tea (5 [[coin]]s): Restores all HP
*Milk Mushroom Tea (8 coins): Restores all SP
*Royal Mushroom Tea (10 coins): Restores all HP and SP
