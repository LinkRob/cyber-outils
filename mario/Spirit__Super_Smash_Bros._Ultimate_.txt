'''Spirits''' are the main collectible item in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. In the story for [[Adventure Mode: World of Light]], they are the different characters and species without their physical form. Each spirit grants a certain type of statistical increase or advantage to a fighter, much like what [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|stickers]] do in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. Spirits could be obtained through a variety of ways, namely as the player's character progresses through World of Light or from winning battles on the Spirit Board. These battles involve an opponent bearing a resemblance or sharing some other common attribute with the spirit in question, such as color.

Several of the spirits fall under the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], which are even the first set of spirits listed in numerical order.
