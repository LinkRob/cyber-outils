'''Mushroom Valley''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''.

==Description==
As shown from the background, Mushroom Valley appears to take place on [[Mushroom Gorge]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. The tennis court is supported on a wooden bridge, and the players play the game on a giant red [[Mushroom Platform]]. There is a wooden scoreboard placed on a blue Mushroom Platform, previously seen in the ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' iteration of Mushroom Gorge. While playing, the ball bounces on the mushroom only slightly, making its properties more comparable to Mushroom Gorge's green Mushroom Platforms in the {{series|Mario Kart}}.

Both Green [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Red Koopa Troopa]]s can be seen surrounding Mushroom Valley.
