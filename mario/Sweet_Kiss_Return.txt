'''Sweet Kiss Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] used by [[Princess Peach|Peach]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Peach blows a sweet kiss at the ball, causing hearts to appear and carry the ball back to Peach so she can hit it. After it is used, the ball is lob shot across the net with a bit of speed.
