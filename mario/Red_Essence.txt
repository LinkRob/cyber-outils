A '''Red Essence''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
A Red Essence is a rather unique item as it makes a single character invincible for at least three turns and removes all status conditions on that character. There are a few ways to obtain a Red Essence. One way is to sleep in the [[Inn]] in [[Nimbus Land]], where [[Mario]] wakes up from a nightmare and the [[Toad (species)|Toad]] gives him a Red Essence. Another one can be found hidden near the [[Save Block]] in the [[Forest Maze]]. Lastly, Red Essences are also an item with the possibility of being won from [[Grate Guy]]'s game.
