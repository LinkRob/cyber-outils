The '''Shooting Star''' is one of [[Mario]]'s three signature [[kart]]s in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', the other two being [[Standard MR]] and [[B Dasher]]. 

== Description ==
This kart is shaped after its namesake. It is a small, bright yellow star, with a center for the character's emblem is, and zig-zag shaped edges behind the star with a small, hollow part for the driver to sit in. 

== In ''Mario Kart DS'' ==
[[File:MKDS Shooting Star.png|thumb|200px|left|''Mario Kart DS'' screenshot of Mario in his Shooting Star in the kart select screen]]
To unlock this, the player must complete all 100cc Nitro Grand Prix cups.

This kart has good [[Acceleration]], but is a very [[Weight Class|Light]] kart and has low [[Items (stat)|Items]]. In fact, the Shooting Star is the lightest kart within the entire game, along with [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Egg 1]], not counting the drivers' weight. Meanwhile, its [[Speed]], [[Handling]] and [[Drift]] are average.

The kart shares its speed with [[Princess Daisy]]'s [[Light Dancer]], it shares its acceleration with [[Luigi]]'s [[Standard LG]] and [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Light Tripper]], its weight is equivalent with Princess Daisy Light Dancer (if Mario is put in the Shooting Star) but is equivalent to Yoshi's Egg 1 (not counting the drivers' weight. It also shares its handling with [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s [[4-Wheel Cradle]] and [[Waluigi]]'s [[Gold Mantis]], as well as the Gold Mantis sharing the Shooting Star's drift. It also shares its low items stat with all other characters' lighter karts.

Toad and [[Wario]] can use the Shooting Star once the player completes all 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix cups, as well as Waluigi's [[Zipper]] kart. All racers can use the Shooting Star once all cups in the Mirror Class have been completed.

Mario in the Shooting Star is the [[Staff Ghost]] used on [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart DS)|Mario Circuit]], with the time of 1:56:533.
