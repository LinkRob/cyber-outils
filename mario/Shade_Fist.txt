|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Shade Fist''' is a move that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is [[Vivian]]'s basic move that requires no [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==History==
To use Shade Fist, the player must press the button that shows up to have Vivian punch an enemy. Shade Fist will always inflict the [[Burn]] status (unless the enemy is immune such as [[Ember]]s). Shade Fist is often cited as one of the most useful moves in the game as it always inflicts Burn even if the enemy is burned already.
