The '''Dizziness Virus''' is an item in the [[arcade]] {{series|Mario Kart}} installments. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and would later return in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
The Dizziness Virus is a [[Blue Virus]] with swirly eyes. It is a lock-on item that, upon hitting a racer, blurs their view and leaves behind afterimages, making it very difficult for the racer to navigate and take proper turns. Any racer affected by a Dizziness Virus is indicated by a spiral effect and the virus floating over their head. Unlike human-controlled racers, CPU racers lose speed upon being hit by a Dizziness Virus. A Dizziness Virus's effects are temporary, but whoever is affected by a Dizziness Virus can get rid of it by bumping into a different racer to pass it over to them.
