The '''Grocery''' is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]''. It's a restaurant shop formerly owned by [[Mario (anime)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (anime)|Luigi]].

==Description==
== History ==

=== ''Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!'' ===
At the Grocery, Mario and Luigi help an old lady named [[Miss Endless]] gather her groceries, but when Mario becomes distracted from the thought of [[Princess Peach (anime)|Princess Peach]] and grabs a lot of random items, Miss Endless becomes angry and leaves. Luigi then notices the pendant Mario has on his hand, greedily recognizing it as a mystical gem passed down from [[Toad's species (anime)|Toad]] legend that can guide the owner to the [[Mushroom Kingdom (anime)|Mushroom Kingdom]], a land of untold riches. While figuring to seek out the kingdom and Mario calls directory assistance, who immediately knows Mario's name and says he will send for him. Suddenly, a [[Haru-ōji|strange blue dog]] enters their store and steals the gem, causing the brothers to chase after it to the desert through a field full of [[Warp pipe (anime)|pipes]].

The Grocery doesn't show up again, until the end of the film. Where Miss Endless returns again to order groceries and sees [[Bowser (anime)|Bowser]] with the [[Koopa Troopa (anime)|Koopa Troopas]] working.
