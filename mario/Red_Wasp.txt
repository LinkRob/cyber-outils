'''Red Wasp''' is [[Rabbid Peach]]'s starting [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Red Wasp is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''Kingdom Battle''. It has medium range and low cover damage. Its hit damage deals 25 to 35 damage.

It has a 10% chance of triggering the [[Super Effect]]. If the Super Effect triggers, it will deal more damage while inflicting [[Honey (status effect)|Honey]] on the foe.
