'''Sparks''', also known as '''ghost bombs''',<ref>Loe, Casey. Versus Books ''Luigi's Mansion'' Perfect Guide. Page 43.</ref> are enemies in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sparks are red, explosive, spike ball [[ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]]s with a tail and pointed nose.

===Behavior===
Sparks are always found in groups of two. They emerge from walls, doors, or even a picture in the hallways of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]]. A Spark automatically explodes after a few seconds. If [[Luigi]] is caught within the blast radius, he loses ten [[Heart Point|HP]]. Sparks also explode and injure Luigi upon contact, including if he vacuums them into his [[Poltergust 3000]].

==History==
[[File:Sparks LM3DS basement.png|thumb|left|''Luigi's Mansion'' (Nintendo 3DS) screenshot of two Sparks in the Basement Hallway]]
There are three groups of Sparks in the mansion, and they are exclusively encountered in the hallways. The first two groups of Sparks are in the 2F Hallway, where one of the groups emerges from the framed mansion photograph on the leftmost side of the hallway, outside of [[Nana's Room]], while the other second group emerges from a wall just before the staircase to the [[Safari Room]]. The last group of Sparks is in the Basement Hallway, where they emerge from a door that leads to a short passage to the [[Secret Altar]].

The only way that [[Luigi]] can defeat a Spark is by spraying either [[water]] or [[fire (element)|fire]] on it, the latter causing it to explode. In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]], Luigi has to use either water or fire on a Spark to include it in the small ghost portrait of the [[Gallery (Luigi's Mansion)|Gallery]].

In the [[Hidden Mansion]] in both PAL releases and the Nintendo 3DS version, Sparks appear more commonly throughout the hallways, and Luigi first encounters them in the 1F Hallway.
