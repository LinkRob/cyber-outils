'''Pengwen''' is a [[penguin]] mentioned by [[Penguin (character)|Penguin]]. She lives in [[Shroom City]] and mentioned in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
While she was only mentioned, she can be described as a [[penguin]].
