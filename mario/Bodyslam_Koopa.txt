'''Bodyslam Koopas''' are a variant of [[Koopa Troopa]] who make a one-time appearance in ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]'' for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]].

==Behavior==
A Bodyslam Koopa repeatedly runs left and right. It sometimes stops moving to slam the ground. [[Mario]] can defeat a Bodyslam Koopa by [[jump]]ing onto it.
