'''Mamar''' is the second [[Star Spirit]] found in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. She also appeared in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==History==
In ''Paper Mario'', Mamar and the seven other Star Spirits had to be saved by [[Mario]]. Like the other Star Spirits, Mamar was trapped and turned into a card. She was held prisoner by [[Tutankoopa]] in [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]]. Upon being rescued, Mamar teaches Mario the [[Lullaby]] [[Star Power]], which can put enemies to sleep if successfully used.
