A '''Honey Super''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario series|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:HoneySuperPM.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', a Honey Super is created by [[Tayce T.]] if [[Mario]] gives her both a [[Super Shroom]] and a [[Honey Syrup]]. This item has the same effect as its ingredients, which is healing 10 [[Heart Point]]s and 5 [[Flower Point]]s making a decent healing item.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
[[File:Honey Super.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', this item is created by [[Zess T.]] using the same ingredients as in ''Paper Mario''. The item retains the same effect as well.

=== ''Super Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', a Honey Super is created by [[Saffron]] for Mario if he gives her a [[Super Shroom Shake]] and a [[Honey Jar]]. A Honey Super restores 20 of Mario's HP, fewer than that of its two ingredients.
