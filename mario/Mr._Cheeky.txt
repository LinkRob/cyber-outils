'''Mr. Cheeky''' is an ape enemy in [[Wario: Master of Disguise|''Wario: Master of Disguise'']].

==History==
These gorilla-like enemies can be found mainly throughout [[Sneezemore Cave]]. They attack by charging at [[Wario]] and attempting to pass wind on him. From Wario's description of them, they appear to be rather old and require to wear dentures. When a Mr. Cheeky is defeat, it will turn into a tiny monkey and run off.
