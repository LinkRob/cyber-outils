'''Poltergust''' is the name given to a series of inventions by [[Professor E. Gadd]] in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, most prominently in the ''[[Luigi's Mansion (series)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' series. It is a series of red [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]]-catching devices that resemble backpack vacuum cleaners and are mainly used by [[Luigi]]. They are based on the Proton Pack from the ''{{wp|Ghostbusters (franchise)|Ghostbusters}}'' franchise. Each of these devices can be used to suck in ghosts and other objects or blow them away, and possesses a [[flashlight]] that takes the place of the nozzle when the latter is not in use. [[Gooigi]] can manipulate his body to form a copy of the Poltergust that he uses.<ref>"[https://luigismansion3.nintendo.com/egadd-research-journal/003 XXX-003 The Birth of Gooigi]". Nintendo.com. Quote: "Just as I expected, Goo began to mimic Luigi's movements—he even transformed a part of his body into a perfect replication of the Poltergust!"</ref>

In the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, starting with ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', Luigi uses one of the main Poltergust models as his [[Final Smash]], in which he uses it to suck up nearby opponents and items and launch them back out.

|style="text-align:center"|[[File:LM Poltergust 3000 model.png|x200px]]<br>[[Poltergust 3000]]
|The Poltergust 3000 is the original version of the Poltergust first seen in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]''. It takes the form of a red vacuum cleaner that can be strapped to a person's back. By sucking [[Elemental Ghost]]s, the Poltergust 3000 is also able to spew fire, water and ice. In the [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|''Luigi's Mansion'' remake]] for the [[Nintendo 3DS]], the Poltergust 3000 can be given the ability to use the [[Strobulb]] function first seen in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.
|-
|style="text-align:center"|[[File:Poltergust 5000 (alt) - Luigi's Mansion Dark Moon.png|x200px]]<br>[[Poltergust 5000]]
|The Poltergust 5000 is an upgraded version of the Poltergust 3000 first seen in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. Compared to its original iteration, the Poltergust 5000 cannot stun ghosts with its flashlight, and must make use of the [[Strobulb]] feature for that purpose. It can also be fitted with a [[Dark-Light Device]], which is able to reveal objects hidden by [[Spirit Ball]]s. When sucking ghosts, the Poltergust 5000 can also perform a more powerful attack known as a Power Surge by charging up a meter.
|-
|style="text-align:center"|[[File:Poltergust G-00 LM3 artwork.png|x200px]]<br>[[Poltergust G-00]]
|The Poltergust G-00, also known as the '''Poltergust 7000''', is the latest version of the Poltergust, first seen in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', but mainly featured in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. It retains all basic functions from the Poltergust 5000, although it can now be used to [[slam]] ghosts into the ground by charging a meter. It also features the addition of the [[Suction Shot]] ability, which allows Luigi to fire suction cups, and the [[Burst]] ability, which allows Luigi to jump while blowing away enemies.<br>The Poltergust G-00's main new ability comes in the form of Gooigi, a gooey clone of Luigi that stored in the device. Additionally, after obtaining the [[Super Suction]], plugging the Poltergust G-00 into special electrical sockets found in certain areas allows Luigi to suck up objects that he normally cannot.

===Other models===
[[File:MKDS Luigi Poltergust 4000 Artwork.png|thumb|[[Luigi]] driving the [[Poltergust 4000]]]]
In addition to the three main models featured in the ''Luigi's Mansion'' series, other models of Poltergust have appeared within the franchise:
*'''PolterGust 1000™:''' A device whose suction is used in the ''[[PolterCue]]'' game to move a billiard ball on a table.
*'''[[Super Poltergust 3001]]:''' An upgraded version of the Poltergust 3000 capable of flying briefly seen in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]].
*'''[[Poltergust 4000]]:''' A [[kart]] that first debuted in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.
**'''[[Ice-blue Poltergust]]:''' A blue variant of the Poltergust 4000 from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.
*'''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit#Karts|Poltergust G-00]] (kart):''' A kart based on the Poltergust G-00 from ''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]]''.
