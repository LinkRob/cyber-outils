'''Strong Slammers''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They are stronger versions of [[Slammer]]s, due to the power crystals found in the [[Secret Mine]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Strong Slammers are identical to Slammers, but they have light-red marks all over their bodies.

=== Behavior ===
Like Slammers, Strong Slammers attack mainly by clapping their hands together to send a powerful red shockwave at Luigi. Strong Slammers are much tougher to vacuum up into the [[Poltergust 5000]], requiring more effort. It is also recommended for Luigi to try and vacuum up more than one Strong Slammer at a time.

== In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}} ==
=== ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' ===
Strong Slammers are first encountered in the Secret Mine, during the mission, [[Hit Rock Bottom]]. The next mission, [[Across the Chasm]], has a Strong Slammer, a [[Strong Gobber]], and a [[Strong Greenie]], all covered in paranormal chains, serve as the sub-bosses. Unlike other Strong Slammers, this one has 250 HP, and is found in the [[Chalet]], but three [[Greenie]]s need to be taken care of before confronting it.

More Strong Slammers appear in various missions of the [[Treacherous Mansion]], and the [[ScareScraper]], when played on expert difficulty or endless mode.

=== ''[[Luigi's Mansion Arcade]]'' ===
Strong Slammers make a reappearance in ''Luigi's Mansion Arcade''. They are encountered in the [[Old Clockworks]] and Treacherous Mansion. They behave like regular Slammers in said game, but their HP is buffed to 35.
