{{Nihongo|'''Wiped Out'''|ピッカピカ窓ふき|Pikkapika mado fuki|Sparkling Window Cleaning}} is a [[Rhythm Minigame|Rhythm]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
[[File:SMP Wiped Out opening.gif|thumb|right|200px|Wiped Out opening.]]
The players place their right hand on the sponge and the camera zooms in. The red arrow points the direction to wipe and the metronome ticks.
{{clr}}
== Gameplay ==
[[File:SMP Wiped Out.gif|thumb|left|200px|Gameplay of Wiped Out.]]
The objective of the minigame is to wipe the soap suds on the windows of a huge work office. The player can wipe the suds using the timing of the song. The player earns three points if they wipe the sud at the right time or one point if they wipe the sud early or late. The player earns no points if they do not wipe off the sud.
{{clr}}
== Ending ==
[[File:SMP Wiped Out ending.gif|thumb|right|200px|Wiped Out's ending.]]
The winner(s) do(es) their victory pose while other players sulk.
{{clr}}
