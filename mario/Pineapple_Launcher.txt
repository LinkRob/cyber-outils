==History==
The Pineapple Launcher can be purchased at [[Funky's Store]] for 3 [[Banana Coin]]s once Chunky is freed in [[Frantic Factory]]. The Pineapple Launcher is the biggest and strongest weapon in the main game being able to take out enemies much easier than the other weapons. However, it also fires the slowest of all of the weapons. The Pineapple Launcher can also be used to hit Pineapple Switches.
