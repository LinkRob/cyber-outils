'''Frozen Fuzzies''', or '''Meringue Clouds-1''', is the first level of [[Meringue Clouds]]. It is unlocked by completing [[Roy's Ironclad Castle]], and its own completion unlocks [[Wiggler Rodeo]] and [[Rainbow Skywalk]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts on a terrain with a mushroom platform above. Four [[Mega Fuzzy|Mega Fuzzies]] and an [[Ice Bro]] are found just ahead. The player must use the Ice Bro's iceballs (or their own, if he/she has an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]]) to freeze the Fuzzies, allowing them to use them as platforms to reach the next area. Two [[? Block]]s are found, with a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] above. Another group of Mega Fuzzies is found, along with two Ice Bros. found above. Two Mega Fuzzies are found circling a platform. An Ice Bro is found in the middle of a group of four Mega Fuzzies. A ? Block containing either a [[Super Mushroom]] or a [[Penguin Suit]] depending on the player's form, followed by another area with Mega Fuzzies and Ice Bros. After another Mega Fuzzy and an Ice Bro, the player reaches the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': After the two Ice Bros. overhead, the first Star Coin is hidden in a cloud.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Just after the first Star Coin, the player must freeze a Fuzzy and ride it down below to collect the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Just after the second Star Coin, the player can either ride a frozen Fuzzy down below the Ice Bro, then jump, or [[Ground Pound]] at the Ice Bro's location to collect the third Star Coin.
