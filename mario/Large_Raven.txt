'''Large Ravens''' are a larger subspecies of [[Raven]] first appearing in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', these Ravens behave similarly to regular Ravens except they’re larger and also much slower. Large Ravens tend to walk around their circular platform like their smaller counterparts, the Ravens and [[Mini-Raven]]s but the platforms they walk around are much larger. They’re also much more durable than regular Ravens too.

==History==
===''Yoshi's New Island''===
Large Ravens first appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', in this game, they are incredibly rare as only one appears in the whole game, near the end of the level [[Eggstraordinary Terrain]]. The Large Raven is seen circling its large circular platform, slowly, due to their size, Large Ravens obviously cannot be swallowed and they’re also immune to [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]-tosses as well, they can only be defeated with a [[Ground Pound]], defeating the Large Raven will cause a ring of [[Coin]]s to appear some of which are actually [[Red Coin]]s which is necessary for attaining 100 Points on the level.

==Trivia==
*The Large Raven being a rare subspecies of a [[Raven]] is similar to the [[Fiery Walking Piranha]]’s appearance in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', the Fiery Walking Piranha, like the Large Raven only appears in one level and only one is seen in the whole game, what’s more is both appeared in the final level before the boss.
*The Large Raven being the largest of the Ravens is similar to the [[Crusher Blargg]] from ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', both are the largest of their respective species which had smaller variants (the [[Blargg]], [[Gargantua Blargg]], and [[Co-Blargg]] for the Crusher Blargg and the [[Raven]] and [[Mini-Raven]] for the Large Raven), both are also very rare enemies with only a few appearing in their respective games.
*The Large Raven is often confused for [[Raphael the Raven]], the leader of the Ravens, this is unlikely as Raphael the Raven is much larger.
