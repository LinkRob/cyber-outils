'''No Koopa''' is a board item in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
No Koopa appears to be a sign with a silhouette of a [[Koopa Troopa]] crossed through with a red line. When this item is active, Koopa Troopa will not be present on the board during gameplay. This means that players crossing the start line will not be awarded coins. However, No Koopa does not work on [[Eternal Star]], as Koopa Troopa does not appear on that board. A similar item called [[No Boo]] is also available to purchase from the Mushroom Shop.

==In ''Mario Party''==
After completing Party Mode, it can be purchased at the [[Mushroom Shop]] for 500 [[coin]]s.

==Nomenclature==
*'''Japanese:''' ノコノコいらず (''Nokonoko irazu''), meaning "No Need for Koopa Troopa"

==Trivia==
*Similar signs with [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s are seen in the code of ''[[Super Mario World]]'', but they [[List of Super Mario World pre-release and unused content|were never used]] in the final game.
*In the [[Donkey Kong Country (series)|''Donkey Kong Country'' series]], the [[No Animal Sign]]s are similar to the No Koopa sign.
