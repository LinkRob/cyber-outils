'''Leafboard Hoard''' is a [[Coin Chaos]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to ride a leaf down a straight wooden track and [[coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]s (worth three individual coins) while avoiding [[Wiggler]]s and [[Prickly Plant]]s. Characters can [[jump]] over obstacles to dodge them, but if they land into one or touch it, they get stunned, briefly halting their movement. Characters can also use one of three [[Barrel Cannon]]s to launch into a line of coins in the air, with some paths having more coins than others.

===Difficult levels===
*Level 1: There are only Yellow Coins and some obstacles.
*Level 2: There are some Red Coins, and some Wigglers are angry and move faster.
*Level 3: There are many Red Coins, although there are also more angry Wigglers as well as Prickly Plants.
