'''Duck Hunt''' is a [[Nintendo Classics]] [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. It shares a name with ''[[w:c:nintendo:Duck Hunt|Duck Hunt]]'', and features a scenario for said game. Like all other microgames from ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', Duck Hunt was directly ported to ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
The gameplay screen is surrounded by a border, presumably of a television screen, and there is a gun pointed at the center of the screen, with a white cursor being shown where the gun is aimed. This gun is likely a second-model [[NES Zapper]], given its dark orange color, but this is not made apparent within the microgame itself. 

==Gameplay==
The player has to press {{button|gba|A}} as soon as a [[Duck (species)|Duck]] flies to shoot it, causing the Duck to fall into the grass. In doing so, the [[Dog (character)|Dog]] is seen holding up the Duck they shot. If the player misses the Duck or does not fire, they lose at the [[microgame]].

The only difference that Level 2 has from Level 1 is that the Duck flies at a higher speed than before. In Level 3, there is not only a Duck that flies at the same speed as in Level 2 but also another one that flies up from the bottom; the player must shoot both to win the microgame.

==Variations==
Other variations of the microgame have also appeared, each utilizing a different control scheme. The microgame's first variation is ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''{{'}}s [[Duck Hunt (WarioWare: Twisted!)|Duck Hunt]], although it involves the use of tilt controls. The second variation is ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''{{'}}s [[Duck Hunt (WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase)|Duck Hunt]], in which the player has to instead shoot a Duck by pointing a [[Wii Remote]] at one. The third variation is [[Duck Hunt (WarioWare Gold)|Duck Hunt]], which combines elements from both the ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' and ''WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase'' iterations of the microgame, although mostly the former one.
