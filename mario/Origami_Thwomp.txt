Origami Thwomps are enemies in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. They are Thwomps that were transformed into Folded Soldiers.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' ==
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'', origami Thwomps appear in Overlook Mountain, where they crash down on Mario when he first ascends the hill leading to the tram stop. They are defeated with a Super Star. Additionally, a kabuki-like design of a Thwomp with its eyes closed appears at the end of the Ninja Attraction. After Mario uses the [[1,000-Fold Arms]] to peel off its face, it changes to its angry expression while a series of walls with similar designs on them attempt to crush Mario while he runs to the exit.

A Big Thwomp with the same appearance as ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' also appears in the Trial of Courage where the player must press A  after the Thwomp drops below the arrow but before hitting Mario.

During the second phase of the final battle with [[King Olly]], he will summon an origami Thwomp towards the end of the phase. Mario must keep using the [[1,000-Fold Arms]] to bash the floor before the Thwomp crushes him.
