'''Super Luigi!''' is Luigi's [[Super Ability]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. It turns [[Luigi]] giant, making it closer to his [[Mega Mario|Mega]] form than his [[Super Mario (form)|Super]] form. Aside from being used by Luigi, Super Luigi! acts the same as [[Mario]]'s Super Ability [[Super Mario!]].
