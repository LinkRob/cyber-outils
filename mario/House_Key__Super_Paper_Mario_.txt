}}The '''House Key''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
House Key is found throughout [[Merlee's Mansion]]. [[Mario]] must use his [[Flip|flipping powers]] to search for the House Keys throughout Merlee's Mansion. One notable key unlocks the door leading to the end of the level but also frees [[Gnaw]] that chases [[Mimi]].
