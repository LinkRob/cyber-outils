'''Speedy Swing Sortie''' is a [[Bonus Area|Bonus Stage]] played by the Kongs in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

== Gameplay ==
Speedy Swing Sortie is a minigame that involves swinging around on vines and collecting coins in midair. The time limit is usually very tight meaning the player must hurry. This minigame is only played by [[Lanky Kong]] and [[Tiny Kong]] in the game. Unlike most other minigames, Speedy Swing Sortie is one of the few ones that cannot be played at [[Snide's H.Q.]] after collecting all 40 [[Blueprint]]s.
