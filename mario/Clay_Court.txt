'''Clay Court''' is a recurring tennis court in the {{series|Mario Tennis}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]]. It also appears in ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]''.

==Description==
The Clay Court essentially has the exact design of a real-world tennis court, namely the ground on which the players play. It also does not have gimmicks of any sort. In its appearances, the Clay Court has a slow ball speed but a weak bounce strength.
