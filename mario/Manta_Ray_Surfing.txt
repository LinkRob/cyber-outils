'''Manta Ray Surfing''' is a challenge to get a [[Power Star]] in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. This challenge is found in the [[Loopdeeloop Galaxy]] and [[Loopdeeswoop Galaxy]]. The challenge is to surf a [[Manta]] named Ray around a course and pass through multiple colored rings in order and then pass through the golden ring within a [[time limit]] to win. The time limit in Loopdeeloop Galaxy is 1:30:00 and the time limit in Loopdeeswoop Galaxy is 3:00:00.
