'''Mark''' is a [[human]] tennis player who appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]] and in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==In the ''Mario Tennis'' series==
In ''Mario Tennis'' for the Game Boy Color, Mark is an unlockable All-Around playable character. He can be unlocked by winning against him in the Mario Tour by Doubles in Varsity Squad.

In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour'', Mark is the coach of the Junior class at the [[Royal Tennis Academy]].
