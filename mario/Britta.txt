'''Britta''', commonly referred to as '''Boss Lady''', is a character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. She is a [[Brock]] that lives on [[Pi'illo Island]] where she is the lead prospector of the [[Dozing Sands]] area.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Britta is a female Brock with brown hair. She also wears a pink hat and pink boots.

===Personality===
Britta is much attractive tempered much like [[Brickle]]. She is very assertive and always yells at her employees to get the job done at a fast pace. Britta can also become greedy when the thought of treasure crosses her mind and often rushes into trouble before thinking.
