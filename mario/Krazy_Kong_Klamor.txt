==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player is armed with a watermelon cannon and must shoot the [[Golden Banana]]s and not any of the five Kongs. Accidentally shooting a Kong will add a point to the target score (even increasing beyond the target goal). This minigame has three different speeds: slow, medium, and high. The speed depends on where Krazy Kong Klamor is played. [[Tiny Kong]] plays this minigame more than any other Kong in the game.

==Trivia==
*This minigame is infamous for being impossible to beat on the Wii U Virtual Console due to a lag. Since the original copy of ''Donkey Kong 64'' runs on the lags in the game, Krazy Kong Klamor as a result becomes off-sync due to the cleaner emulated versions on the virtual console. The only way to win this minigame on higher speeds is to make use of the Pause Trick by pausing as soon the Golden Banana appears, then unpausing and quickly shooting the Golden Banana.
