'''World 1-2''' is the second level in [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Like most other second levels in [[List of Mario games|Mario games]], this level takes place [[Underground theme|underground]]. It has multiple [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Brick Block]]s, and [[POW Block]]s.

== Walkthrough ==

{{Empty section|date=May 2023}}

== Star Coins ==
* '''Star Coin 1''': Not too far after going through the first [[Warp Pipe]], there should be a [[Brick Block|brick]] structure with 3 [[Koopa Troopa]]s in it. Hit the [[POW Block]]s in the structure by either [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] or using a [[Koopa Shell]] to reveal the [[Star Coin]].
* '''Star Coin 2''': Right after the first [[Star Coin]], enter the yellow [[Warp Pipe]]. This will lead you to a room with a [[P Switch|P-Switch]]. Press the [[P Switch|P-Switch]], then run through some [[Warp Pipe]]s. Get onto the upper floor, avoid the [[Koopa Troopa]]s, then get the [[Star Coin]] before the [[P Switch|P-Switch]] runs out.
* '''Star Coin 3''': In order to get this [[Star Coin]], you need a [[Fire Flower]]. Once you get to a [[Fire Pipe]], shoot fire balls into it until a [[Mega Mushroom]] comes out. Take this [[Mega Mushroom]], and start running to the right. You'll then find a [[Star Coin]] trapped in some blocks. Use your [[Mega Mushroom]] to break these blocks, and get the [[Star Coin|coin]].{{NSMB2Nav}}
