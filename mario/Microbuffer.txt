==Appearance==
Microbuffer is a sentient microphone that has a mustache and a tuxedo.

==History==
Microbuffer only appears at the very end of the game where he announces each of the [[Kong Family|Kongs]] and [[King Krusha K. Rool]] as they enter the ring.

==Trivia==
*Its name is a portmanteau of "microphone" and "buffer".
