'''Figure-8 Circuit''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is from the ''Mario'' universe.

==Stage design==
{{sectstub}}

===Features===
*Shy Guy Racers: During this stage, many [[Shy Guy]]s in karts will race along the track. They will first appear from the background and then appear from the left side of the stage going to the right side. The Shy Guy Racers deal moderate knockback if a player should get hit by one and players, in turn, can knock away the Shy Guys.

==Origin==
Figure-8 Circuit is based off on the track of the same name from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. It is the first overall course in that game where the entire course resembles a figure 8. However, despite this, Figure-8 Circuit doesn't have much features from that game besides the stage design.
