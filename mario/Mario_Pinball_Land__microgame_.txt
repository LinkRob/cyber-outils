'''Mario Pinball Land''', titled '''Super Mario Ball''' in Europe, is one of [[9-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''. It is based on the [[Game Boy Advance]] game ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]'', which itself is also titlted ''Super Mario Ball'' in Europe.

==Layout==
The arena depends on the difficulty. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] will be featured in his ball form, while an enemy boss lurks in the arena, although the boss does not attack. Two [[Yoshi Egg|Yoshi Eggs]] will appear to help Mario. The exit is blocked by two flippers (controlled by the Joy-Cons) and, on Level 1, a blue pipe.

==Gameplay==
The player must use their [[Joy-Con]]s as [[Flipper (Pinball)|flippers]] to launch [[Mario]], who is in his ball form, as well as two [[Yoshi Egg]]s at the boss. In the game, the Joy-Con flipper on the left is red, while the one on the right is blue. The boss varies by level, but each of them require three hits to defeat and therefore win the microgame. If either every ball leaves the playing field (before the boss is beaten; impossible on Level 1) or the objective is not met on time, the player loses.

* '''Level 1:''' The boss is [[Petey Piranha]], who stays in the center and does not move. The exit is blocked by a blue [[Warp Pipe]].
* '''Level 2:''' The boss is [[Egyptian Koopa]], who starts in the center and can move around, making him slightly harder to hit. The blue pipe also disappears shortly after the microgame starts, making it possible for the balls to exit the playing field.
* '''Level 3:''' The boss is a [[Big Boo]], who starts in the center and turns to face Mario. It can become invisible temporarily, during which it is invulnerable to attacks. Like level 2, a blue pipe appears at first, but it disappears shortly after the microgame starts.
