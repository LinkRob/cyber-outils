The '''Guard Hall''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is notable for having four Suits of Armor that attempt to attack Luigi by swinging their sword down. The Guard Hall also is accessible from the [[Entrance]] and it leads to the [[Foyer (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Foyer]].

==History==
During the first mission of Gloomy Manor, Luigi heads down the Guard Hall where he has to avoid the Suits of Armor to get into the Foyer.

In a later mission, [[Quiet Please!|A-3: Quiet Please!]], the Suits of Armor actually disappear from the Guard Hall due to the [[Spirit Ball]]s, but using the [[Dark-Light Device]] can reveal them again.
