'''Boo Woods''' is a haunted forests featuring several dead trees, a few gravestones, and most notably [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Luigi's Mansion]]. Boo Woods first appears in the [[Nintendo GameCube]] game ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
In the opening cutscene, [[Luigi]] is seen walking through Boo Woods on his way to a mansion that he had won, supposedly from entering a contest. Two crows are seen in one scene. Boo's Woods is also seen during the cutscene whenever Luigi exits [[Professor E. Gadd's Lab]] to go visit the mansion. From the mansion itself, Boo Woods can be seen from the [[Balcony (2F)|Balcony]] and [[Courtyard (Luigi's Mansion)|Courtyard]].

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''==
Boo Woods is only mentioned by [[Elvin Gadd (past)|Elvin Gadd]] of the past. After E. Gadd's lab is destroyed from a volcanic eruption by [[Thwomp Volcano]], he says that he plans to move to "an affordable lab at the edge of Boo Woods".

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
The outdoor portions of the [[Luigi's Mansion (track)|Luigi's Mansion]] course introduced in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', also returning in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', takes place around Luigi's Mansion.
