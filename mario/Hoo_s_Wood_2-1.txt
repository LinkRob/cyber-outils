'''Hoo's Wood 2-1''' starts the new world and is the first level of the second world, [[Hoo's Wood]] in the game ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Toads ==
* '''Toad 1''': Climb on the tree, until you stop on the orange mushroom. Now glide left with the [[Floatbrella]] skill to make it easier. Jump in the pipe. Go to the right and use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe, to go up the right and go to the left when reaching the top. You can even use the vibe to blow away the Red Paratroopa.
* '''Toad 2''': Go down the pipe above the [[! Box]], keep moving left until you reach the dead end. Now go down the pipe, run to the left and hit the box.
* '''Toad 3''': While going the normal way to complete the level, in the third area, smash the green polka dot blocks and go pass by them.

== Music Score: Ladida Plains 2 ==
In the second area, jump up to the long pipe and keep jumping pass by brown platforms.
