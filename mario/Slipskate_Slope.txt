'''Slipskate Slope''' is an island located on [[Lake Lapcat]], the main location of the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
It is one of the few islands that surrounds the Ruins [[Giga Bell]]. The island has Mario use an [[Ice Skate]] to traverse a straight-forward course with a bunch of [[Dash Panel]]s and rotating tunnels that run into spiked walls.
