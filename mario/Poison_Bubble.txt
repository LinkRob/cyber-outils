'''Poison Bubbles''' are a sub-species of [[Podoboo]] appearing in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. They behave identically to a Podoboo with one major difference. Instead of leaping out of [[lava]], they leap out of [[Poisoned Water|poison swamps]]. They are rare enemies only found in one level, [[Spike Bar Jungle]], Poison Bubbles also share the same immunities as a regular Podoboo and can only be defeated with a [[Starman]].
