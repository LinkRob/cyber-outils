'''Clusters''' are strange, crystal-like enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are only fought in [[Moleville Mines]], and Clusters are only seen in battle with other enemies (usually weaker enemies like [[Bob-omb]]s). Stronger variants of Clusters named [[Pulsar]]s are encountered later on.

==Battle==
In battle, Clusters mainly attack using magical moves. Their main magic attack is [[Crystal]] that can deal moderate damage to one of [[Mario]]'s team. The Cluster's deadliest move is called [[Psyche!]] what has a chance to automatically cause a character to faint. However, the Cluster destroys itself after using this move.

===Strategy===
The Clusters do not have any weaknesses in particular, although their magic defense is rather low. A fully-powered up [[Geno Beam]] can usually take out a Cluster in a single hit.
