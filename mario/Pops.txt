'''Pops''' is a minor character in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. He is an elderly man who serves as mayor of [[Luxeville]].

==In ''WarioWare Gold''==
Pops only appears in the ending, in which he tells [[Lulu]] that Luxeville has installed a modern toilet while she was away trying to retrieve the [[Pot of Luxeville]], formerly the village's only toilet that was stolen by [[Wario]].
