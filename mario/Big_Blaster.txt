'''Big Blasters''', originally named '''Big Berthas''', are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Super Mario RPG|version]]. They are stronger versions of [[Bill Blaster|Blasters]], exclusively encountered in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]].

==Battle==
In battle, Big Blasters mainly attack by firing multiple [[Bullet Bill]]s that can rack up on damage. It can also use a deadly move called [[Blazer]] that may automatically knock out one of [[Mario]]'s party.

===Strategy===
Big Blasters have very high [[Defense]] but low Magic Defense, so it is recommended to use magic attack, especially [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]], since Big Blasters are vulnerable to [[Thunder (element)|Thunder]]-elemental attacks.

===Etymology===
The original name, "Big Bertha," is taken from the {{w|Big Bertha (howitzer)|Big Bertha}}, a German cannon used in World War I.
