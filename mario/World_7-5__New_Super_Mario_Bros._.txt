'''World 7-5''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.|New Super Mario Bros]]''.

==Overview==
Unlike the other levels in [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]], this level features grass.

==Walkthrough==
This level is filled with [[Bullet Bill]]s and [[Bob-omb]]s. Unlike most [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]] levels, this level takes place on an overworld, grassland area and not in the sky. In the hidden area, there are several [[Micro-Goomba]]s, as well as the second Star Coin, blocked behind a pipe. It requires Mario to get the [[Mega Mushroom]] from a ? Block in the hidden area. There is a [[Banzai Bill Cannon]] launching [[Banzai Bill]]s at the very end, right before the [[Flagpole]].
==Enemies==
*[[Bob-omb]]s
*[[Bullet Bill]]s
*[[Koopa Troopa]]s (green & red)
*[[Micro-Goomba]]s
*A [[Banzai Bill]]

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is behind blocks that must be broken by a Bob-omb.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is inside a small Warp Pipe that must be accessed by Mini Mario. However, the player must blow up a block that is blocking the Pipe with a Bob-omb to get inside.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The final Star Coin can be found between several Bill Blasters.

==Secret Exit==
Right before the [[Banzai Bill]], there will be some breakable blocks and a [[Bob-omb]]. Explode the blocks with the [[Bob-omb]], then go down the hidden [[Warp Pipe|pipe]]. You'll reach a cave, then you go to the [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] on the right. Then the [[flagpole]] will be to your right. This will lead you to [[World 7-A (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7-A]].
