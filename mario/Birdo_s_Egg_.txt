'''Birdo's Egg+''' is the enhanced version of [[Birdo's Egg]]. Depending on the driver, if they have the Birdo Egg as their [[Special Skill]], the player can reach a certain level to upgrade to Birdo's Egg+. It appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.
== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Birdo's Egg+ debuted as one of Birdo's special skills in ''Tour'' when Birdo joined [[Holiday Tour (2019)|Holiday Tour]].
