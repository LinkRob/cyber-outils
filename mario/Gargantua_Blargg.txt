'''Gargantua Blarggs''' (also called '''Super Big Blarggs''') are massive [[Blargg]]s that reside in [[lava]]. They debuted in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and have made many appearances in the [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi'' franchise]]. Gargantua Blarggs are typically indestructible and cannot be destroyed by any means.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
[[File:GargantuaBlargg-SMW2.png|thumb|left|158px|A Gargantua Blargg, as it appears in ''Yoshi's Island'']]
Gargantua Blarggs first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''; they return in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They attempt to block [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s path by rising from the lava as he approaches. To pass by a Gargantua Blargg, Yoshi must throw an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] into its mouth. The Gargantua Blarrg will then sink into the lava, allowing Yoshi to continue past it. However, it re-emerges after a few seconds. Unlike the smaller [[Co-Blargg]], Gargantua Blarggs cannot be defeated with an [[Anywhere POW]].

A counterpart of Gargantua Blarggs known as a [[Nep-Enut]] also debuts in this game. Its behavior is similar, although it is blue and resides in [[water]] instead of lava.
{{br|left}}
====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
[[File:YIDS Gargantua Blargg.png|thumb|A Gargantua Blargg from ''Yoshi's Island DS'']]
Gargantua Blarggs return in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', retaining their behavior from the previous game. They only appear in [[Gilbert the Gooey's Castle]]. Just as before, a Gargantua Blarggs will emerge from the lava and attempt to attack Yoshi but they can be sent packing by tossing an egg at it. Hitting a Gargantua Blargg with an egg will unlock it in the [[Island Museum]]. The Gargantua Blargg appears in the subterranean enemy exhibit alongside the [[Co-Blargg]]. A stronger variant of the Gargantua Blargg called the [[Crusher Blargg]] is introduced as well. These Gargantua Blarggs are much taller and can destroy dense rocks. [[Nep-Enut]]s also return in this game.
{{br}}
====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[File:Gargantua Blargg Attacks!.png|thumb|left|[[Cyan Yoshi]] about to attack a Gargantua Blargg in ''Yoshi's New Island'']]
In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', Gargantua Blarggs return, appearing in two levels: [[Gargantua Blargg Attacks!]] and [[Hotfoot Hurdle]]. Just as before, Gargantua Blarggs attack by emerging from the lava and attempting to attack Yoshi. Gargantua Blarggs are once again invincible and cannot be destroyed but they can be sent back into the lava temporarily with an egg toss.
{{br}}
===''Tetris Attack''===
[[File:Gargantua Blargg TA SNES portrait.png|thumb|96px|Gargantua Blargg's VS Mode portrait, from ''Tetris Attack'']]
In the game ''[[Tetris Attack]]'', a Gargantua Blargg appears as a ruler of the "Lava World", being allied with [[Pyro Guy|Flamer Guy]]. In here, as many other old enemies of Yoshi, was shown as a friend of Yoshi, being enchanted by Bowser to work for him. It replaces the flame fairy, Flare, from the original ''[[Panel de Pon]]''.
{{br|right}}

===''Yoshi'' platform series===
====''Yoshi's Woolly World'' / ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''====
[[File:Gargantua Blargg YWW.jpg|thumb|150px|Yoshi outruns a Gargantua Blargg in ''Yoshi's Woolly World'']]
In ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', Gargantua Blarggs merely appear as an outline composed of an orange scarf. It takes three yarn balls to make the Gargantua Blargg hide back down into the lava. They mainly appear in the level [[Lava Scarves and Red-Hot Blarggs]]. There is also one in the fire area of [[King Bowser's Castle (Yoshi's Woolly World)|King Bowser's Castle]]. If Yoshi is given an [[Ice Watermelon]] by the "All-you-can-eat ice watermelons!" [[Power Badge (Yoshi's Woolly World)|Power Badge]], he can use the melon to ultimately take down a Gargantua Blargg with one blow.
{{br|left}}

====''Yoshi's Crafted World''====
[[File:YCW Gargantua Blargg.png|thumb|left|120px|A Gargantua Blargg, as seen in ''Yoshi's Crafted World'']]
Gargantua Blarggs reappear in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'', in the level [[Poochy's Magma Run]]. They are made of bubbled slime and have the same behavior as usual. When a Gargantua Blargg is hit by Yoshi's egg, it spins sinking down. There is also a costume and a craft based on Gargantua Blarggs.
{{br}}
