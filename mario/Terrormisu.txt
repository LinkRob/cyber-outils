'''Terrormisu''' (known by her false name as '''Tiaramisu''') is the main villain of [[Wario: Master of Disguise]].

== Development ==
Terrormisu was created by Nintendo during development and designed and projected by Yutaka Hirata.

=== Physical description ===
In her human form, she has a pink dress similar to that of [[Princess Peach]] and two shadowed, white eyes. In her demon form, Terrormisu still resembles Peach, but has been increased in size and how has pupils in her eyes.

== In ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]'' ==
Terrormisu appears as the final boss.

=== Battle ===
In-battle, she uses forms of three evil masks. In her sad form she appears to be a monster with four blue eyes ([[Genius Wario]] can reveal one of them as a red eye), followed by a sad phanto mask at the top, in her happy form, there are 15 holes that happy masks with eyes appear out of, they are grey at first, but when shot by Cosmic Wario, they Resemble a blue, glowing Phanto, similar to the mask in the sad form, in her final form, an angry red mask can be seen in The background, at the top of the arena is the true angry mask, which is devilish and red.
