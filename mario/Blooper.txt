'''Bloopers''' (often referred to as '''Bloobers'''<ref>''Super Mario Bros.'' English instruction booklet, page 13.</ref><ref>''Super Mario Bros. 3'' English instruction booklet, page 36.</ref><ref>''Yoshi'' instruction booklet, pages 4, 5, 12 on NES and pages 3, 4, 11 on Game Boy.</ref> until ''[[Paper Mario]]'', though Blooper was occasionally used beforehand<ref>Hiroo Tochikubo, ''[[How to win at Super Mario Bros.]]'', Tokuma Shoten, 1987, ISBN 4-19-720003-XC. Page 15.</ref><ref>Tatsumi Yamashita, ''The Official Nintendo Player's Guide'', Tokuma Shoten, 1987, ISBN 999832369X. Page 30.</ref><ref>''Nintendo Power'' Volume 13. Page 8.</ref>) are squid-like creatures that are found mainly in bodies of water. They made their first appearance in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', and various varieties of them have appeared in most [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' games]] since, normally as enemies. ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' was the first game to feature a Blooper as a playable character.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Baby Luigi (Mario Kart 8).png|thumb|A Blooper inking [[Baby Luigi]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'']]
Bloopers are small, white squid-like creatures with a black marking on their face, which resembles a mask. Bloopers come in different sizes and colors, though the original variety shown in ''Super Mario Bros.'' is white and roughly the size of a [[Koopa Troopa]]. They possess varying numbers of appendages, ranging from four to up to ten visible limbs, of various lengths. While most Bloopers lack actual squid tentacles, large Bloopers are sometimes shown to have them, sometimes in great amounts.

===Abilities===
[[File:Moving-Blooper.gif|thumb|left|A Blooper's movement style]]
Some basic abilities that Bloopers have is using their tentacles to help them move and [[swim]] through [[water]]. In some cases, Bloopers use their tentacles to attack. As shown in some games, like the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]], Bloopers can also survive in and out of the water. Bloopers use high-pitched warbling to communicate. Bloopers can also leap from the water and charge into their target, which is also shown in ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]''.

In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', Bloopers are capable of burrowing through the ground, shooting globs of ink, and blasting opponents with energy beams.

Bloopers can squirt ink from between their arms in some appearances, especially in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. In other appearances, such as artwork from ''Mario Kart'', Bloopers squirt ink from a small mouth located near the eyes.

==Main appearances==
===''Super Mario Bros.''===
{{multiframe|[[File:SMB Blooper Sprite.gif]]|align=left|size=30|bg=#2038EC}}
[[File:World 2-2 SMB.png|thumb|200px|A Bloober in World 2-2, the first level in which they are encountered in ''Super Mario Bros.'']]
In ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', Bloobers are first encountered in [[World 2-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-2]]. They are one of the main underwater enemies, besides [[Cheep Cheep]]s, although Bloobers are less common than them.

Bloobers follow [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] continuously, although they cannot touch the ground, a drawback that persists in later 2D installments in the {{series|Super Mario}}. As a result, Bloopers move in a regular zig-zag pattern. Like other enemies, Bloobers damage Mario or Luigi on contact. They can only be defeated from a [[fireball]] thrown by [[Fire Mario|Fiery Mario]], earning the player 200 [[point]]s. It technically is possible to defeat a Bloober by touching it as [[Invincible Mario]], which is not normally possible because the required [[Super Star|Starman]] is never in the same area as a Bloober.

A Bloober serves as a [[fake Bowser]] at the end of [[World 6-4 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-4]], which is revealed as it falls into the [[lava]] if Fiery Mario or Luigi defeats it with [[fireball]]s.
[[File:SMB FDS World Minus 3.png|thumb|200px|left|Flying Bloobers in the Minus World in the Family Computer Disk System version]]
In the [[Family Computer Disk System]] version of the Minus World glitch, flying Bloobers, like the ones in ''The Lost Levels'', appear in World -3, an underground-recolored version of [[World 4-4 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 4-4]]. Since Bloobers use the exact same color schemes as Goombas, these Bloobers are colored light blue.
{{br|left}}
===''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''===
In ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'', Bloobers return as enemies. They now have a flying variety known as [[Pink Bloober]], which swims through the air. Pink Bloobers share a palette with [[Goomba]]s but are still the same coded object as regular Bloobers.

In the remake ''Super Mario Bros. for Super Players'', included within ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]'', Sky Bloobers are depicted off-white to differentiate them from Bloobers again.

===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:SMB3 Blooper.gif|frame|left]]
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', Bloopers return as enemies. They behave the same as before. Defeating a Blooper using a [[fireball]] now earns the player 100 points, slightly less than in ''Super Mario Bros.''.

A few variants of Bloopers were introduced. One of them is [[Blooper Nanny]], which behaves like an average Blooper but is followed by three or four [[Baby Blooper|Baby Bloopers]]. She can send her children in every direction, making it more difficult to swim, and the children return to her after a short time.

The Nintendo Official Player’s Guide for the game marks the first time they’re referred to as <b>Bloopers</b>, however the game’s instruction booklet still uses <b>Bloober</b> as their name.

===''Super Mario All-Stars''===
In the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' versions of ''Super Mario Bros.'', ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels'', and ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', Bloopers are colored pink instead of white.

===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
[[File:SMS Blooper artwork.png|thumb|A Blooper in ''Super Mario Sunshine'']]
Bloopers, also called '''Gesso''',<ref>Bogenn, Tim, and Doug Walsh. ''Super Mario Sunshine'' BradyGames Official Strategy Guide. Page 5.</ref> reappear in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' after an eleven-year absence from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]], albeit with a different look. This is the first game where Bloopers are found on dry land, spitting [[goop]]. Spraying them with water stuns them briefly. Mario can now [[stomp]] a Blooper to defeat it, which yields a [[Coin]]. If one of these Bloopers falls in the water, it disappears in a cloud of smoke. Bloopers can be found in [[Ricco Harbor]], the [[King Boo]] battle in [[Hotel Delfino]]'s [[Casino Delfino]], and [[Noki Bay]]. Specifically, in King Boo's boss fight, Bloopers are one of the enemies that have a chance of appearing after King Boo spins the slots.

The game introduces two Blooper varieties, one of which is [[Jumping Blooper]]s. The other variety, [[Blooper Racer]]s, are a non-hostile species found in Ricco Harbor, where they do not attack Mario and are owned by [[Big Daddy of Blooper-surfing|Big Daddy]], who allows Mario to ride them. Green Bloopers are the slowest, but can be steered the easiest. Yellow Bloopers have average speed and average steering. Pink Bloopers are the fastest, but have poor steering. Most notably, the game introduced a giant Blooper boss named [[Gooper Blooper]], who is fought twice in Ricco Harbor and later in Noki Bay.

In a few [[Mission|episodes]] of Ricco Harbor, boats filled with dead Bloopers and Jumping Bloopers are located near the start of the course.

===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:NSMB Blooper Sprite.png|frame]]
[[File:3-3 NSMB.png|thumb|left|Four Bloopers in ''New Super Mario Bros.''.]]
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', Bloopers appear only in [[World 3-3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3-3]], where several of them appear. Bloopers have the same movements as they did in ''Super Mario Bros.'' and still can be defeated by getting hit with a [[fireball]] or [[Super Star|Star]]. Furthermore, they can be defeated from contact with [[Mega Mario]]. [[Blooper Nanny|Blooper Nannies]] and Blooper Babies also reappear. There is a variation that avoids the player in one of the bonus rooms.

===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[File:Blooper Base.png|thumb|Mario swimming by a Blooper in ''Super Mario Galaxy'']]
Bloopers reappear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They are found in the [[Buoy Base Galaxy]] and the [[Deep Dark Galaxy]]. If Mario approaches a Blooper, it starts to chase him, swimming irregularly, as in the other games. Bloopers can even chase Mario to the surface of the water. If a Blooper hits Mario, it damages him and releases ink. Bloopers can be defeated with a [[Spin]] or a [[Green Shell|Koopa Shell]], but cannot be jumped on.

===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[File:In The Blooper Lair.png|thumb|left|Bloopers in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'']]
Bloopers appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' at [[World 4-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4-4]] and [[World 8-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-4]]. This game introduces a new ability for Bloopers: they are able to hide behind scenery and in [[Warp Pipe]]s. When Mario passes by a Blooper, it quickly shoot out of its hiding spot. Blooper Nannies also appear in this game and also have this ability. Bloopers are resistant to being frozen; if hit by an [[Ice Ball]], they quickly break free from the ice.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[File:Blooper Spin.png|thumb|Mario spins by an underwater Blooper in the Starshine Beach Galaxy in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'']]
Bloopers reappear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', behaving the same way as in the predecessor. They appear only in [[Starshine Beach Galaxy]], specifically in the deepest body of water that surrounds the largest tower in both [[mission]]s. Just as before, Mario can use a [[Spin]] to defeat them.

===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
[[File:World 3-2 SM3DL.png|thumb|left|A Blooper in ''Super Mario 3D Land''.]]
Bloopers reappear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. They behave much the same way as in ''Super Mario Galaxy'' and ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', following Mario or Luigi, except slower and with less accuracy. Bloopers can no longer shoot ink to attack. They also can whirl around underwater. Bloopers can be defeated with a [[Ground Pound]], a [[Boomerang Flower|boomerang]], a [[Fire Flower|fireball]], a [[Super Leaf|tail whip]], the effects of a [[Starman]] or an [[Invincibility Leaf]], or while Mario is in [[Statue Mario|Statue]] form. Additionally, if a Blooper is landed on by Mario or Luigi from above, neither the Blooper or either Mario or Luigi take damage.
{{br}}

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:Blooper Corals.png|thumb|A Blooper in World 3-5, the only level featuring them in ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'']]
Bloopers make another appearance in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Here, they appear only in [[World 3-5 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3-5]], but they do maintain the traits that they had in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''. They can be defeated using the effects of the [[Fire Flower]], [[Mega Mushroom]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Starman]], or [[Gold Flower]] (the last of which also provides bonus [[Coin]]s). Unlike most enemies, Bloopers are not affected by the [[Gold Ring (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|Gold Ring]]. Both Blooper Nannies and Baby Bloopers return again.

===''New Super Mario Bros. U'' / ''New Super Luigi U'' / ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[File:The Blooper's Secret Lair.jpg|thumb|left|Bloopers in Blooper's Secret Lair, a level in ''New Super Mario Bros. U'']]
Bloopers reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', where they behave identically as in earlier ''New Super Mario Bros.'' titles. Bloopers can be defeated from a fireball or Invincible Mario, as usual, but they can now be frozen by [[Ice Ball]]s without breaking free immediately. Bloopers also have their own [[level]], [[Blooper's Secret Lair]], located in [[Acorn Plains]]. They also appear in [[Tropical Refresher]] in [[Sparkling Waters]]. Blooper Nannies and Baby Bloopers make another reappearance.

In ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', Bloopers only appear in the [[Superstar Road]] level [[Star Coin Deep Dive]].

===''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''===
[[File:Blooper SM3DW.jpg|thumb|150px|A Blooper in ''Super Mario 3D World'']]
Bloopers reappear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] port ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. Instead of having their characteristic swimming pattern, Bloopers now turn sideways and charge at Mario's direction when they spot him. As in ''Super Mario 3D Land'', they can be defeated with the same attacks, and can also be defeated with cat claws. They appear only in [[Rammerhead Reef]], [[Gargantuan Grotto]] and [[Pipeline Boom Lagoon]].

===''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS''===
[[File:Blooper Maker World.png|thumb|left|Bloopers in the ''Super Mario World'' style of ''Super Mario Maker'']]
Bloopers reappear as enemies in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]''. They function the same as previous games, except in the ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' game style, since they will not spin around when the vocals occur. They can be placed out of water like ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''; however, they do not award 1000 points when stomped. In the Course Maker, Bloopers can be placed as enemies and can be shaken to turn into a Blooper Nanny. Like many enemies, they can be enlarged by a [[Super Mushroom]] to become a [[Mega Blooper]] and given [[wing]]s to become a [[Winged Blooper]], the latter of which will make them move much faster. They can be placed in [[Warp Pipe]]s, [[Bill Blaster]]s, [[Block]]s, and tracks. The effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]] can also be used to defeat them.

===''Super Mario Run''===
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'', Bloopers do not make a physical appearance, but one appears as statue during the ''Mario Kart'' event, with the Banana and ''Mario Kart'' statues.

===''Super Mario Odyssey''===
Bloopers do not appear physically in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', but a drawing of one appears on [[Princess Peach|Peach]]'s fan when she appears in [[Bowser's Kingdom]].

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
Bloopers reappear in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', retaining much of the same role as in ''Super Mario Maker''. In the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' style, they attack by turning sideways and charging at Mario.

===Etymology===
Their name comes from "bloop", an onomatopoeia for the sound of bubbles breaching the surface of water.
