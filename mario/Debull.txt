'''Debulls''' are a brown bull enemy which appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They are first encountered in [[Boing!]], also being featured within the level's icon.

==Behavior==
A Debull charges in a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s direction to try and hit him. As with most other enemies, if Yoshi is hit by a Debull, he takes damage, and the [[Countdown Timer]] starts.

A Debull is briefly stunned if it either hits a wall or is hit by a [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]. A Debull is stunned for even longer if [[Baby Donkey Kong]] throws an egg at it. Debulls can only be defeated by either going off a cliff or from having [[Baby Bowser]] spit a [[fireball]] at it.

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
Debulls are first encountered in [[Boing!]], in which they even appear within the level's icon. Two of them appear in the land exhibit of the [[Island Museum]].
