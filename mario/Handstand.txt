'''Handstand''' is a move used by [[Mario]] in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]] and later ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''.

==Description==
Mario can execute a Handstand by [[jump]]ing while [[crouch]]ing. In doing so, Mario stands on both hands, his legs pointed into the air. Mario can walk while doing a Handstand, a move known as a '''Handstand Walk'''. While performing a Handstand, Mario can deflect certain objects, such as [[Nitpicker]] eggs, [[Brickman]] bricks, and [[barbell]]s.
