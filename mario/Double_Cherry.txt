'''Double Cherries''' are power-up [[item]]s which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== In ''Super Mario 3D World'' ==
Double Cherries give the player another copy of their character. The player keep making copies of their character until they touch the [[Goal Pole]], or get damaged and lose one copy. When the player has copies, they control them all at once, so some strategy is needed if the players want to keep them alive.

Double Cherries first appear in the level [[Double Cherry Pass]]. [[Meowser]] uses a Double Cherry during the second half of his boss fight.

== In ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' ==
The Double Cherries are only found in two [[level]]s, but act the exact same. Much like most of the other stuff, the Double Cherry is required for puzzles.
