|race = [[Pianta]]}}'''Rocko''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a yellow [[Pianta]] that is a member of the [[Pianta Syndicate]] of west [[Rogueport]].

==History==
Rocko is commonly seen at the [[Pianta Parlor]] as the bouncer. He is stationed there to keep things in order. However, after [[Frankie]] is put in charge of the Pianta Syndicate by [[Don Pianta]], Rocko complains about not having his freak. [[Goombella]] also mentions that Rocko was recently hired to the Syndicate and has a long way to go to the top.
