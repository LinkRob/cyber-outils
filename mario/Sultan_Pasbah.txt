'''Sultan Pasbah''' is a character who only appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Mario's Magic Carpet]]."

==Episode appearances==
==="Mario's Magic Carpet"===
Sultan Pasbah has taken [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] and [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] all captive after the group lands into one of the traps in his palace. The former three are taken to Sultan Pasbah's slave's quarters, while Princess Toadstool is taken to Sultan Pasbah's harem. However, Sultan Pasbah eventually becomes annoyed by Princess Toadstool's constant acting out and puts out an advertisement for her, planning to sell her to the highest bidder, [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]].
