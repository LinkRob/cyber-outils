A '''Froggie Drink''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.
{{br|left}}
==Effects==
Using a Froggie Drink restores 30 [[Heart Point|HP]] to all party members. Froggie Drinks can only be purchased using [[Frog Coin]]s at the shop in [[Tadpole Pond]].
