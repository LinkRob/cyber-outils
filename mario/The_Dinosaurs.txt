'''The Dinosaurs''' are a pair of characters in the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' series who antagonize [[Mona]]. They work for the [[Pizza Dinosaur]] restaurant in [[Diamond City]] and wear exaggerated costumes that appear to parody ''{{wp|Super Sentai}}'' villains. Half of the song [[Mona Pizza (song)|Mona Pizza]] is sung by the Dinosaurs.

[[File:The_Dinosaurs.png|thumb|The Dinosaurs in ''WarioWare: Twisted!'']]
The Dinosaurs first appear in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' in Mona's story. They receive a call at Pizza Dinosaur and immediately begin yelling at the customer, who mistakenly believed them to be [[Mona Pizza]] and quickly hangs up. The Dinosaurs become enraged and attack Mona in large robotic dinosaurs. The Dinosaurs are stopped by [[Mona's Pets]], who destroy their machines. They also have their own [[Pizza Dinosaur (souvenir)|unlockable souvenir]] in the records section; the description of this record is Mona stating "I can't believe those corporate jerks put out a record, too."

In ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', the Dinosaurs appear as members of the band of Mona's rival, [[Vanessa]]. When Vanessa is knocked down to the number 2 hit singer in [[Ear Candy]], the Dinosaurs join her in her attempt at stopping Mona from performing at the [[Hawt House]] by attacking her with an eagle mech.

Although the Dinosaurs do not appear in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', [[Team Dinosaur]] have a similar localized name and also serve as antagonists in Mona's story.
