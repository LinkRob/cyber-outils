'''{{Infobox Enemy|name=Goomba|image=Paper Goomba.png|caption=|first=|games=''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''|homeland=|located=|leader=|species=[[Goomba]]|subspecies=[[Mega Goomba]], [[Accordian Goomba]], [[Paper-Cone Goomba]], [[Small Goomba]], [[Small Goomba Wheel]], [[Small Goomba Gang]]|affiliates=|related=[[Paragoomba]], [[Bone Goomba]]|attacks=Headbutts Mario.|defeated=|comics=}}'''Goombas''' are an enemy that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]].''

== Description ==
They have a brown stubby body with 2 plump feet. Their expression is shown through their angry eyebrows and exaggerated open mouth.

== Stats ==
'''HP:''' 6

'''Attacks:'''

* Head Attack ('''3''')
* Group Attack ('''3''')
* Blowback Attack ('''6''')

[[File:PMCS Goomba Card.png|left|frameless|83x83px]]

=== Price ===
Sell Price: 1 coin

=== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ===
The Goomba is first seen in [[Port Prisma]], but can also be found in [[Daffodil Peak]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Mondo Woods]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], and [[Sacred Forest]]. The ''best location'' to find this card is [[The Golden Coliseum]], as this level contains 53 Goombas in both the playthrough and the post game.
