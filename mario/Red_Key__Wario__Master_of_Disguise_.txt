The '''Red Key''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Red Key, as the same states, is a key that is red in color and [[Wario]] describes it as shiny.

==History==
The Red Key is one of three keys found in [[Allergia Gardens]] (along with the [[Blue Key (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Blue Key]]) and [[Yellow Key]]). It can be used on two different doors of the same color. The Red Key can be found in a purple treasure chest in a room with a bunch of red mushrooms that are used to activate platforms.
