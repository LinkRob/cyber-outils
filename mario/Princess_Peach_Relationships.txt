'''[[Princess Peach]]''' is a kind-hearted, soft-spoken, sweet, cheerful, graceful, girly, and upbeat person. She puts others' needs before her own. The following article details Princess Peach's relationships with other characters.

==Friends/Allies==
===Mario===
{{Q|Thank you, [[Mario]]! Your quest is over. We present you a new quest. [[Breaking the Fourth Wall|Push button B to select a world]].|Peach to Mario, ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''}}
[[File:SM64 Mario Peach.png|thumb|left|Peach and Mario in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.]]
Princess Peach is [[Mario]]'s love interest and they have had a long, positive relationship. The official Mario Portal website as well as [[Nintendo Today!]] mention in the English translation of their character profiles that "Princess Peach and Mario are good friends and help each other out whenever they can". However, the corresponding official original Japanese versions of the same character profiles are more explicit and directly mention that "Mario loves Princess Peach" and that "Mario and Peach are very close, and rely on each other", ultimately confirming that they are much closer than "just friends" in an official capacity.<ref>[https://www.nintendo.com/jp/character/mario/characters/index.html  「ピーチ姫のことが大好きで」 Translation: "He loves Princess Peach"  (Nintendo of Japan 2025)]</ref><ref>[https://www.nintendo.com/jp/character/mario/characters/index.html  「マリオとはとても仲が良くて、お互いを頼りにしています」 Translation: "Mario and Peach are very close and rely on each other."  (Nintendo of Japan 2025)]</ref>
According to lead developer [https://nintendo.fandom.com/wiki/Yoshiaki_Koizumi Yoshiaki Koizumi] in [https://gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2017/06/17/marios-developers-on-hat-capture-and-peachs-love-for-mario.aspx an interview], Mario has been romantically interested in Peach as far back as the original 2D ''Mario'' games, hence why he's always willing to come to her aid and rescue when she needs it.

During the events of first Peach's kidnapping in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', Mario rescued her from Bowser's clutches. Mario has risked his life to save Peach from Bowser since. Peach calls Mario her knight in shining armor in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' while Peach is called Mario's special someone in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They worry over each other when they are apart, and have been shown to have mutual romantic feelings for each other. During the ending to ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' he even attempts to ask for her hand in marriage, but gets turned down because he gets caught one-upping Bowser's proposal. Despite Peach being able to get somewhat annoyed with Mario at times like in the aforementioned incident, where she demands that Mario and Bowser stop fighting over her hand in marriage, it is very rare, and she forgives Mario afterwards.

In many games, Peach also helps Mario when she is around with him. In ''Super Mario World'', Peach helps Mario by providing him with [[Super Mushroom]]s during each phase of the final boss fight, and in ''Super Mario Galaxy'', Peach, despite being kidnapped in a far away place, manages to help Mario by giving him 5 [[1-Up Mushroom]]s in the envelope given by the [[Mailtoad]].

In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', when Mario is in need of rescue, Peach comes to his aid without a second thought and when finally rescuing him at the end of the game, she excitedly jumps into Mario's arms, happily calls his name, dances with him until he carries her in a [https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bridal_carry bridal carry], and finally gives her a bouquet of flowers, all while hearts are shown.

Peach and Mario have also been partnered together in spin-off games many times and appear in merchandise with romantic undertones such as ''[https://store.line.me/stickershop/product/9350/en Super Mario's Relaxing Life]'' LINE stickers, which depict them sitting together staring at the sunset while a heart animation is shown.

===Luigi===
Another of Peach's closest friends is Luigi, who often helps Mario rescue her, and the two share good chemistry in ''Mario Superstar Baseball'' but not in its sequel, ''Mario Super Sluggers''.

There were initial doubts about whether Luigi is attracted to Peach, as in the platformers, Peach kisses Luigi the same way she kisses Mario, and he reacts the same way. However, in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', Peach reacts considerably differently to rescuing Mario and Luigi; excitedly jumping into Mario's arms and happily calling his name after rescuing him, dancing with him until he carries her, and he gives her a bouquet of flowers, all while hearts are shown; while she only dances happily with Luigi. In ''Mario Power Tennis'', when winning a trophy, Peach blows kisses to Mario and Luigi, who both swoon in love, nonetheless, the blown kiss was a response to Mario saying "I love you so much" to Peach. Ultimately, individual interactions in games show that, while they are close friends, there are no romantic feelings between Peach and Luigi, with Mario and Peach being romantically attracted instead.

=== Rosalina ===
Peach and [[Rosalina]]'s relationship is unknown. Rosalina learned Bowser kidnapped Peach and Mario is willing to rescue her. === Princess Zelda (''Twilight Princess'' incarnation) ===
Peach and Zelda are in good terms. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', Peach and Zelda watched a friendly match between Mario and [[Kirby]]. However, a Subspace Bomb interrupts the match, launching Mario away. [[Petey Piranha]] captures Peach and Zelda, forcing Kirby to save either one of the princesses. When Zelda and Peach reunited, they stay together until the end of the story. When Fox McCloud accidentally almost hurts Peach, Zelda (Who was in the form of Sheik) immediately fights him to protect her. Peach however stops the fight by offering tea to both of them, knowing it was an accident and Fox isn’t evil. When Duon forms in front of the group, Sheik and Fox immediately places themselves in front of Peach to keep Peach safe. She, Sheik, Fox, Solid Snake, Lucario, Falco Lombardi, manage to defeat the robot.

==Enemies/Rivals==
===Bowser===
{{Q|I’ve never despised you more than I do right now, Bowser. Hmph!|[[Princess Peach|Peach]] to [[Bowser]], ''[[Fortune Street]]''}}
[[File:SM3DL Prolog4.png|Bowser kidnapping Peach in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.|right]]
Princess Peach hates '''[[Bowser]],''' as Bowser repeatedly kidnaps Peach and attacks her kingdom. In ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', Bowser attacks the Mushroom Kingdom in the hopes in conquering it himself, and captures Peach as she is the only one capable of reversing the damage caused by him. In the end, Mario defeats Bowser and rescues Peach. This culminates in many more kidnapping attempts by Bowser, and Mario ultimately defeats the Koopa king and rescues Peach. In later games, Bowser is revealed to have romantic feelings for Peach, and his motives shift to winning her hand in marriage. In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Peach even discovers Bowser's feelings in [[Bowser's Diary|his diary]].<ref name="PM-BD">{{cite video game| title = [[Paper Mario]]| developer = Intelligent Systems| publisher = Nintendo| date = February 5, 2001| platform = Nintendo 64|level = Princess Peach's Castle| quote = Today was great, diary! I used my castle to lift Princess Peach's Castle way up into the sky! Then I barged into Peach's Castle and beat up Mario! Yeah! Unbelievable, I know! So fun! And there's more! After that, I kidnapped Princess Peach! I couldn't be happier, diary! I hope she likes me...|speaker=[[Bowser's Diary]]}}</ref> In Peach's case, she does not feel the same way, refusing to marry him in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''<ref name="SPM">{{cite video game| title = [[Super Paper Mario]]| developer = [[Intelligent Systems]]| publisher = [[Nintendo]]| date = April 9, 2007| platform = [[Nintendo Wii]]| level = [[The Bitlands]]| quote = That...sham was not a wedding! What are you doing here, anyway, Bowser?| speaker = [[Princess Peach]]}}</ref> and ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.<ref name="SMO"/> Nevertheless, Mario successfully rescues Peach from Bowser every time he kidnaps her. 

Despite her frequent kidnappings,  Peach is not afraid to stand up to Bowser in certain instances. There were rare occasions where Peach was not the kidnapped victim. In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', she was absent in her castle and Bowser ordered the army to kidnap Mario, Luigi and the Toads. Peach and her parasol [[Perry]] travel to [[Vibe Island]] to rescue Mario from Bowser's clutches. In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', Bowser had no plan on kidnapping Peach but the [[Sprixie Princess]]es. Regardless, she and her allies saved the princesses from Bowser.

In {{series|Mario Party}}, like every other character, Bowser can choose to sabotage Peach on boards whenever she lands on the [[Bowser Space]] and depending where her "prize" lands.

Peach is willing to cooperate with [[Bowser]] against more dire circumstances. In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Peach begs Bowser's help to save the worlds; Mario let Bowser know if he refused to help, there will be no kingdom for him to rule and Peach brought up his brainwashed minions to encourage his idea to help. In ''Mario'' spin-offs, Peach and Bowser have equal roles being as playable characters. In ''[[Super Mario Party]]'', if Bowser gets Peach as his ally on boards, she says that she will help Bowser.

=== Cackletta ===
Princess Peach and '''[[Cackletta]]''' do not get along. Cackletta and [[Fawful]] plotted to steal [[Princess Peach's voice]]. Before it happened, [[Prince Peasley]] warned her about their plot. Peach replaced herself with [[Birdo]] before Cackletta and Fawful arrived at the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. While Cackletta and Fawful stole Birdo's voice unaware of Peach's plan, Peach and [[Toadsworth]] were hiding behind the curtains.

Peach was about to tell Mario and Luigi that she was alright, but Bowser came earlier. Meanwhile, Peach arrived at the [[Beanbean Airport]] to tell Mario and Luigi about Cackletta's plot that Peach managed to get away with it.

Cackletta took the form from Bowser into [[Bowletta]] and kidnapped Peach, part of the plan to awaken the [[Beanstar]]. Cackletta told the Beanbean Kingdom to give the Beanstar oud they want Peach to return safely. Later, Cackletta got the Beanstar and Peach hoping to awaken it. Peach was free from Cackletta's clutches along with the Beanstar with the help of Mario and Luigi.

Princess Shroob along with her Princess Peach was travelling in the past while Princess Shroob invaded [[Mushroom Kingdom]] due to her withering [[Planet Shroob|home world]]. They both encountered each other as Princess Peach trapped [[Elder Princess Shroob|Princess Shroob's sister]] in the [[Cobalt Star]] before she captured her. Peach shattered the Cobalt Star with no other way returning Elder Princess Shroob nor to the future. Meanwhile, she managed to invade and capture Peach.

Princess Shroob disguised as Peach to trick Mario, Luigi, [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] into thinking she is the real princess.
