== Friends ==

=== Starlow ===
[[File:MLB Starlow Bowser Touch.png|thumb|left|Starlow touches Bowser, from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.]]
{{Q|Don't touch me! Even [[Starlow|you]] don't get to touch me!|Bowser, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''}}
When inhaled inside of Bowser's body, [[Starlow]] assumes the alter-ego of "Chippy" and presents as a friend to help guide him to fight back against [[Fawful]]. Bowser considers "Chippy" to be a minion, but over time grows to learn to treat "Chippy" with better manners and appreciates "Chippy's" skills, support and company. However, upon learning that "Chippy" is Starlow, Bowser feels angered and betrayed seemingly ending their friendship. 

They would turn up to be hostile foes at [[Pi'illo Island]] during ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', then again regarding the [[Mysterious Book]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

At some point following those events, Starlow and Bowser had begun to try and rebuild their friendship. Bowser appeared comfortable enough to welcome visits from Starlow when she was off-duty and began to call her "Chippy" again. In [[Concordia]] on [[Wayaway Island]] during ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', Starlow visits an injured Bowser when he had formed a tentative truce with [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] against [[Zokket]], after having been beaten by the brothers. When Starlow enters his room to give him a [[Drumstick]], Bowser expresses that not even she gets to touch him, implying she is held in higher regard than others. He also brags about his physical strength and resilience to her, suggesting he cares about how she perceives him. When Mario and Luigi enter after, Bowser gets frustrated and escorts all of them out.

===Kamek===
[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island|''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'']] introduced [[Kamek]], the [[Magikoopa]] who raised [[Bowser]] from infancy and who has been seen interacting with him the most throughout the series. As seen in the [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]] [[Yoshi's Island (series)|incidents]] and [[Shroob]] [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time|incident]], Kamek takes great care of Baby Bowser and worries for his safety, with his devout loyalty continuing into Bowser's adulthood where he is often seen as Bowser's right-hand. Kamek's thought in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' even implies that he considers Bowser to be like a son to him, as despite his brain-washing by the enemies, he thinks, "That's... my child?" when faced with Bowser in battle. The only time a rift has been shown between the two is in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''; after winning a minigame Kamek explains that he took leave of [[Koopa Troop]] when Bowser disappointed him by taking the title of "Game Master", rather than earning it, although the ending states that the two reconciled. Aside from that incident, the two get along well, and Bowser seems to show much more respect to his elderly guardian than to any other of his subordinates.

===Kammy Koopa===
In the first two installments of the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]], Kammy Koopa acts as the right-hand Koopa to Bowser. Kammy deeply respects (and fears) Bowser and tends to call him by various nicknames rather than by his real name. Bowser, however, tends not to show any recpect back to Kammy as he can get annoyed by her at times. In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Kammy always follows the orders of Bowser and their relationship is strictly business. 

However, their relationship is more comical during their time at [[Rogueport|Rougeport]] [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|incident]] as they try to search for [[Princess Peach]] and the [[Crystal Stars]]. Along the way, Bowser and Kammy Koopa run into various of hinderances. At one point, Bowser even fell into the sea and was forced to swim back to Rogueport. After hearing Kammy had a great time at [[Glitzville]], Bowser burned Kammy. However, despite this, Bowser and Kammy get along well as a team and the two of them are always seen together (except when Bowser made it the first time into Glitzville).

===Baby Bowser===
{{Q|Little dude! Get stronger and more evil! Always!|Bowser in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''}}
[[File:YIDS Baby Bowser with Bowser and Kamek.jpg|thumb|Bowser and Kamek meet his past self, from ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.]]
Although technically a [[Baby Bowser|younger version of himself]], Bowser has interacted with Baby Bowser during two occasions of time travel. In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', Baby Bowser insults his future self, who then rashly blasts him out of the castle; later, however, Bowser becomes outraged when he discovers his younger self being bested by [[Yoshi (species)|the Yoshis]] and during the [[Shroob]] [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time|incident]], the two Bowsers do not recognize each other and argue about the ownership of [[Thwomp Volcano]], eventually putting their differences aside to work together to try and defeat [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] and their own [[Baby Mario|baby]] [[Baby Luigi|selves]]. After their defeat, Bowser encourages Baby Bowser to "Get stronger and more evil." as both part ways. In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', he also ambushes Yoshi and Baby Mario just as they were to save the Stork and Baby Luigi again in order to avenge his younger self's defeat.

Despite their tense and conflict relationship, Bowser seems to be a little concerned for Baby Bowser. As it is implied that his advice given to his past self, encouraging to get stronger and more could be the reason Bowser is the way he is now in the present.

=== Elite Trio===
Bowser had mixed and negative receptions towards the [[Elite Trio]] ([[Private Goomp]], [[Sergeant Guy]], and [[Corporal Paraplonk]]). During the [[Fawful]] [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|incident]], he gets angry at them for forgetting the [[Banzai Bill|giant Banzai Bill]] back and assisted Bowser in finding one in order to take down [[Bowser's Castle]] after [[Fawful]] had taken over it. Later, after Bowser found a 2nd [[Star Cure|Star Cute]], without his notice, the Elite Trio push Bowser into a safe and lock him inside defecting to Fawful's side applying they were brainwashed, this angered Bowser as he felt deeply betrayed. This changes when Bowser (with the help of Mario and Luigi) defeated Fawful for good and he allowed the Elite Trio to help in rebuilding his castle. Despite Elite Trio's actions and betrayal, they apologize Bowser and allow to rejoin the Koopa Troop and later become Bowser's elite guards. At some point, the Elite Trio would accompany Bowser in the next [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team|incident]] on [[Pi'illo Island]] as they fought the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] in [[Dreamy Driftwood Shore]]. Bowser then orders the Elite Trio to take down the heroes as they reached a higher area into [[Neo Bowser Castle]], but were failed as a result. 

Despite Elite Trio's incompetence, Bowser did seem a bit proud as he told they did a good job well from their usual standards.

===Antasma===
At first, Bowser saw [[Antasma]] as an annoyance but soon decided to team up with him after the Bat King gave Bowser power. Bowser agreed to help Antasma get the [[Dream Stone]] that aided the both of them in the takeover of [[Pi'illo Island]]. However, this changes Bowser's mind by betraying Antasma, saying he was only using the Bat King to forward his own needs and takes the Dream Stone all for himself, implying Bowser only saw Antasma as a pawn to use in his own evil plans.

===Bowser Jr.===
Bowser definitely shows love for [[Bowser Jr.]], his only son. The two of them are almost always seen together throught the series, involving both of them.

During the [[Isle Delfino]] [[Super Mario Sunshine|incident]], Bowser told Bowser Jr. a lie that Princess Peach was his mother and that Mario kidnapped her. This caused Bowser Jr. to overtake an [[Shadow Mario|alter ego]] to frame Mario and take back Peach for himself. However, at the end of the game, Bowser was forced to admit to Jr. that Peach wasn't his mother, but Bowser Jr. already knew. However, Bowser Jr. says he wants to fight Mario again when he is bigger much to the delight of Bowser. This is proven true as Bowser Jr. would assist Bowser [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|fighting]] Mario in [[Super Mario Galaxy|every]] [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|threat]] he follows.

==Neutral==
===Princess Peach===
The relationship between Bowser and Peach can be easily described as "love/hate". Bowser is madly in love with Princess Peach, even wanting to marry her and make her his queen, but she rejects him every time. This leads Bowser to always kidnapping Peach which leads to the infamous running gag of the Mario series. Bowser, even though being evil, doesn't seem to harm Peach on occasion and would even go out to his way to protect her. Bowser also doesn't tolerate other people kidnapping Princess Peach such as [[Sir Grodus]] and [[Antasma]]. Bowser even goes out to say that kidnapping Peach is his job.

===Wario and Waluigi ===
[[File:Bowser and Wario Bros..png|thumb|Bowser with the [[Wario Bros.]] as her roars at the tv screen, from ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.]]
Bowser can get easily annoyed by the mischievous duo. At one point in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', Bowser usually confronted Wario until he was captured along with Mario and Luigi at [[Princess Peach's Castle]], however Yoshi came to rescue them and stopped him once again. Soon, Bowser would meet Wario and his newcomer Waluigi, when both the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] and [[Wario Bros. (group)|Wario Bros.]] got into a conflict at the Tennis court and was suddenly interrupted by Bowser's arrival along with [[Boo]]. 

In ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]'', Bowser chased them down after they accidentally hit him a black eye with a golf ball. Bowser toss bombs down on them and eventually cornered them. However, a golf ball struck by Mario soon hit Wario and Waluigi in the eye and the golf ball hits the Bob-omb in Bowser's hand causing it to explode on them. In ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', Bowser watched the Wario and Waluigi lose against Mario and Luigi. When the duo falls out into a training room after escaping from the [[police]], Bowser interrogate them, sending to train tennis for hours until they form an alliance to take down the Mario Bros. and the others with Bob-ombs. This fails when Wario and Waluigi's Bob-omb launcher was malfunctioned, sending a Bob-omb flying towards Bowser's balloon crashing down with the trio from an explosion, both knocked out. 

Bowser and his army would invade Wario's [[Wario City|own city]] during the [[Baseball Island]] [[Mario Super Sluggers|incident]] and later defeat him along with Mario and his friends. Later that night, Wario Bros. put a Bullet Bill into the cannon used for [[Bob-omb Derby]], firing it towards Mario and Donkey Kong, Bowser likely presumed see Wario and Waluigi are behind this went to the [[Mario Stadium (baseball stadium)|Mario Stadium]] and hit the Bullet Bill with his bat directly towards the duo, blasting them away from the sky.

==='''Paper Bowser'''===
Bowser has an extreme conflict relationship with his paper counterpart, [[Paper Bowser]]. When Paper Bowser arrived at the castle, he demanded to leave calling him “flimsy knockoff”, they both start argue and fight each other. Nonetheless, he and Bowser team up to kidnap Peach and [[Paper Princess Peach|Paper Peach]] subsequently returning to the castle while sending their armies to slow the trio(Mario, Luigi and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]]) down. As Bowser’s Castle was [[Neo Bowser Castle|upgraded]], both had the same plan: Get the book, finish Mario, Luigi, Starlow and Paper Mario and send them trapped in the book to an empty void.

Like other villains Bowser formerly teamed up, he would also planned to betray Paper Bowser after the trio are defeated. However, the plan foiled as him and Paper Bowser on [[Shiny RoboBowser|their transformation]] were defeated by the trio, leaving Bowser falling off the floating castle.

==Enemies==
===Mario===
{{Q|MAAARIO! Are you seriously trying to start with me again?! I hear about this big meeting, and I'm all ready to act nice... But man, the second I see your face, Mr. Nice Bowser is GONE. Yeah, forget your dumb meeting! I'll pummel you and grab [[Princess Peach|Peach]]!|Bowser, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''}}[[File:SM64 - Mario Bowser Back To Back Artwork.png|thumb|Bowser and [[Mario]], from ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.]]
Bowser and Mario have a long detailed rivalry during the [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]] [[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island|incident]] and [[Shroob]] [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time|incident]], they have been archenemies since they were infants. Bowser always wants to beat Mario himself and greatly dislikes him mainly for taking Princess Peach away from him. After years of being beaten by Mario, Bowser finally receives some redemption in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' as he defeated Mario using the [[Star Rod]]. However, Mario soon defeated him once again and Bowser cries out on how many times Mario has defeated him. Bowser also seems to show jealously towards Mario.

Though, on a few occasions such as in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Bowser would team up with Mario to take down a [[Smithy|bigger]] [[Count Bleck|threat]]. Though Bowser is reluctant, Bowser does see that he cannot take over the world himself if another person is doing so and agrees to team up with his archenemy.

===Luigi===
Unlike with Mario, Bowser doesn't really take Luigi seriously mainly due to Luigi being in his older brother's shadow most of the time. As seen in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', Bowser has a hard time calling Luigi by his real name and calls him by several nicknames such as "Green 'Stache," "Greenie," and "Mr. Green Mario Brother Guy." However, at the end of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', Bowser finally calls Luigi by his name wanting revenge on both him and Mario.
