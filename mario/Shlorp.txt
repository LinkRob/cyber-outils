}}A '''Shlorp''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Shlorps are stronger versions of [[Shlurp]]s that are found in a few areas of the game (starting with [[Downtown of Crag]]). Shlurps move around very slowly on the ground. If a hero gets too close to a Shlurp, it will suck them in and spit them out for damage. Shlurps cannot be damage from the outside due to their extremely hard exoskeletons. The only way to defeat them is to have them swallow [[Boomer]] twice.
