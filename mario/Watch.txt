The '''Watch''' is an item in the PC version of ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''.

==Description==
The Watch is a regular golden {{w|pocket watch}} on a chain.

==In ''Mario's Time Machine''==
In [[London]], during the year 1843, [[Mario]] has to give one of [[Charles Dickens]]'s books to a banker. When the banker is asked why he is so grumpy, he gives Mario the Watch and tells him to leave.

Meanwhile, Dickens' wife, [[Catherine Dickens]], wants to prepare a roast dinner for her husband, Charles, but worries that she might end up being late. Mario has to give the Watch to Catherine, who is then reassured that she is not running late.
