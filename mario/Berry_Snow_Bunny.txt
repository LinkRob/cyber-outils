The '''Berry Snow Bunny''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing a [[Snow Bunny]] and a [[Pink Apple]]. When used, and like other icy items in this game, this recipe freezes all enemies on the screen. The Berry Snow Bunny has the same effect as the Snow Bunny, but this item's effect lasts slightly longer.

When [[Dyllis]] is given both a Snow Bunny and Berry Snow Bunny, she could create a [[Couple's Cake]].
