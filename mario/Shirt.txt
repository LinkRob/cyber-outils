The '''Shirt''' is a clothing item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Despite its name, the Shirt is actually a pair of dark blue overalls, the same appearance as those usually worn by Mario. The Shirt can be bought from [[Toad Town]] for seven [[coin]]s, and equipping it to Mario increases both his [[Defense]] and [[Magic Defense]] by six.
