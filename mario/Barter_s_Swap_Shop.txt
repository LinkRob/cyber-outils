'''Barter's Swap Shop''' is a location in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''.

==Description==
Barter's Swap Shop is run by [[Barter]]. Unlike regular shops, Barter does not sell items, but rather exchanges one of his items for an item that [[Dixie Kong]] and [[Kiddy Kong]] give to him.

Originally, Barter's Swap Shop is in a generic cabin with only a few tools mounted on the wall. In the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake, it has become a storage facility with some boxes lying around and shelves housing some items.

==In ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!''==
Barter's Swap Shop is located in the northern part of the [[Northern Kremisphere]], west of the base of [[K3]].

When the [[Kong]]s first arrive at Barter's Swap Shop, they discover that Barter has a [[No.6 wrench]], which happens to belong to [[Björn]]. However, the only item Barter is willing to trade the No.6 wrench for is a [[mirror (item)|mirror]]. If the Kongs want their mirror back after trading it away, they must purchase it from Barter for ten [[bear coin]]s.
