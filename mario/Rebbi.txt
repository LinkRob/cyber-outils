==Appearance==
Rebbi is a [[Nimbi]] that wears a red robe. He also has zig-zag hair and a mustache.
==History==
Rebbi play a rather important role during Chapter 7-4 during the [[Skellobit]] invasion of [[The Overthere]]. He asks [[Mario]] and the heroes to help him fend of the Skellobits. Once they do, Rebbi gives them the [[Red Orb]] which is needed to build a bridge to [[King Grambi]]'s castle. Rebbi also assists the heroes in fending off the horde of Skellobits who were trying to stop them from accessing the castle.
