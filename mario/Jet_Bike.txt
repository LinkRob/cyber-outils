The '''Jet Bike''' is an unlockable bike body in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== Description ==
The Jet Bike is an orange bike resembles a [[wikipedia:Fighter aircraft|fighter jet]]. The Jet Bike is an unlockable bike in ''Mario Kart 8''. The body part is unlocked at random as the player collects coins in the race.
