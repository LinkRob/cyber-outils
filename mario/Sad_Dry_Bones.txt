'''Sad Dry Bones''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Sad Dry Bones is a variant of [[Dry Bones]] imbued with the [[Gloom]] [[vibe]], and it differs in appearance from being more blue in color and constantly crying.

===Behavior===
A Sad Dry Bones acts similarly to a Dry Bones, including the ability to reassemble itself upon breaking, except a Sad Dry Bones throws three [[bone]]s at once rather than just one.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
[[Image:Sad Dry Bones Ribs.png|thumb|left|200px|Peach sees the first Sad Dry Bones.]]
Sad Dry Bones first appear in [[Shriek Mansion 3-1]], and they are later encountered in [[Bowser's Villa]]. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can defeat a Dry Bones with the effects of the [[Rage]] [[vibe]].
