'''Chomp Walker''' is a single-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character—either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]—takes a [[Chain Chomp]] for a walk in a plains-like area. There are two types of objects found during the walk: bones and pieces of meat. If the Chain Chomp comes across a bone, it becomes distracted and takes a few seconds to eat it. If the Chain Chomp comes across meat, it sprints forward. In addition, whenever the player's character walks onto the grass, their movement is slowed. If the Chain Chomp notices the player's character, it tries to lunge at them. If the character is hit, the player loses at the minigame.

In the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] and Mini-Game Attack modes, the Chain Chomp has to be dragged to the goal within a [[time limit]] of 60 and 40 seconds respectively. In [[Play Land]], the player's character has to try and obtain as many [[point]]s as possible while dragging the Chain Chomp along; every so often, there is a checkpoint that adds ten seconds to the timer.
