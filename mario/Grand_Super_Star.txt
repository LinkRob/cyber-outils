A '''Grand Super Star''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
A Grand Super Star is a [[Skill Item]] that raises the Skill level of a teammate whose Skill level had been increased level 4 using [[Starman|Super Star]]s, including both forms of [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Bob-omb]]s, and all types/forms of [[Lakitu]]s and [[Boo]]s.

A Grand Super Star can be obtained either from opening a [[? Block]] at random, as a prize from the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] minigame, or from defeating a [[Lakitu|Double Lakitu]] with a 10+ Combo attack.
