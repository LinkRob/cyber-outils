'''Mondo Woods''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
The path leading to this location is unlocked after getting the Green Mini Paint Star in [[Château Chanterelle]]. The Mondo Woods unlocks paths leading to [[Sacred Forest]] and [[Dark Bloo Inn]]. The woods have been enlarged, which is later revealed the work of [[Kamek]], who uses his own magic to enlarge all of the whole forest and the [[Goomba]]s.

Late into the Mondo Woods, a giant unpainted coin can be found. After getting the [[Big Paint Star|Yellow Big Paint Star]], the giant color-drained coin will be filled in, allowing Mario to grab it (though it's only worth 1 coin) and get the Blue Mini Paint Star behind it, leading to the Sacred Forest.
