|race = [[Koopa Paratroopa]]|kindred = Elite Trio}}'''Corporal Paraplonk''' is a character that first appears in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' as a boss. He is one of [[Bowser]]'s elite troops and is usually seen with [[Private Goomp]] and [[Sergeant Guy]]. He is also the self-appointed leader of the small group.
==Appearance==
Corporal Paraplonk is a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] with a red shell. He also wears a red bucket on his head. In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', he now wears a blue bucket with Bowser's symbol on it. He seems to be the strongest of the [[Elite Trio]].
==History==
===''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''===
In this game, Corporal Paraplonk is first seen where he notices Bowser wandering around in the woods. Bowser tells Corporal Paraplonk and the others that he is looking for a [[Banzai Bill]] to help knock [[Fawful]] away from his recently, taken-over [[Bowser's Castle|castle]]. Corporal Paraplonk said that one may be deeper in the woods somewhere and Bowser goes out to search. Bowser eventually returns with one and uses Sergeant Guy's cannon to launch the Banzai Bill at Fawful's castle. However, the castle then starts to fly and it crushes Bowser. However, Bowser suddenly grows [[Rump Command|giant]] surprising Corporal Paraplonk. During the first giant battle, Corporal Paraplonk teaches the player how to fight as a giant and coaches Bowser throughout the fight telling him when to attack and counterattack. After the battle, Bowser heads into his castle leaving Corporal Paraplonk and the others outside. Corporal Paraplonk and the rest isn't seen again until when Bowser is searching for a [[Star Cure]] hidden deep in his castle. They eventually find it but have some delays due to a [[Fawful Express]] and Bowser [[Memory Banks|losing his memory]]. After Bowser opens the safe to get the Star Cure, Corporal Paraplonk, along with Private Goomp and Sergeant Guy, push Bowser into the safe and locks him in. Corporal Paraplonk is excited that they will receive praise from Fawful. However, after Fawful is defeated, Corporal Paraplonk and the others beg for Bowser's forgiveness. Bowser does so by letting them help fix up his castle.

In this game, Corporal Paraplonk and the others have been promoted and they help out in Bowser's conquest of the world along with [[Antasma]]. Corporal Paraplonk is then involved in an ambush in the [[Dream World|dream world]] where [[Mario]] was fooled by [[Kamek]] dressed up as [[Princess Peach]]. Corporal Paraplonk, Private Goomp, and Sergeant Guy all fight Mario but they are defeated due to Private Goomp accidentally revealing their weakness. Corporal Paraplonk and the others were then demoted by Kamek to sub down [[Neo Bowser Castle]].

In battle, Corporal Paraplonk attacks with his shell dash, and the target must jump over the shell to avoid damage. If the target lands on the shell, Corporal Paraplonk will be sent into Private Goomp, damaging him, then end up damaging the Goombas in the background.
