This article pertains to the beta elements of ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

== Unused Graphics ==
{| class="article-table"
|+
!Image
!Description
|-
|[[File:SMA2-LuigiJump.png|center|thumb]]
|Some unused sprites of [[Luigi]] jumping. Since he has a flutter jump in this game, like in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', these sprites go unused. However, the big Luigi sprite is briefly used in the 96 Exits cutscene when Luigi is kicking Koopa Masks. Interestingly, these sprites seem to be based on the original version (where Luigi was a palette swap of Mario) instead of Luigi's more unique graphics from ''Super Mario All-Star + Super Mario World''.
|-
|[[File:Sma2 ver.png|center|thumb]]
|A version stamp, which can be found in the files for the title screen. It was probably a leftover from WIP builds of the game as the "'''ver'''." (abbreviation of "Version") didn't exist without an online connection.
|-
|[[File:Sma2 exit.png|center|thumb]]
|An exit sign, which can be found in the files for the map screen. It has a similar appearance to the sign seen at the end of a Ghost House.
|-
|[[File:Sma2 japanesefiller.png|center|thumb]]
|Two blocks of text. The first is just gibberish, while the second translates to "Block Size Transfer".
|}
