'''Whacka Bump ''' is a recurring item in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:WhackBumpPM.png|frame|left]]
In this game, Whacka Bumps can be obtained by [[hammer]]ing a [[Whacka]] found in [[Mt. Rugged]]. Eating a Whacka Bump restores 25 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 25 [[Flower Point|FP]], making it a very effective healing item. However, [[Mario]] can only obtain 8 Whacka Bumps in this game. After that, the Whacka disappears for the rest of the game.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
[[File:WhackaBump_PMTTYD.png|thumb|left]]
Whacka Bumps must now be obtained from a Whacka found on [[Keelhaul Key]] which is rather late into the game compared to the previous game. As usual, only 8 Whacka Bumps can be obtained in this game and they restore 25 HP and 25 FP. Whacka Bumps can now be used in some dishes and may be the result of a cooked [[Mystery]] if Mario is lucky.

In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], a Whacka can be fought as a secret boss in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] uses Whacka Bumps as it's main form of attacking. It does so by headbutting them at Mario. This game also introduces the exclusive Red Whacka Bumps which deal a very high 20 HP of damage to Mario.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
The Whacka Bump can be purchased at the arcade for 300 [[Flipside Token]]s. A Whacka can be found on Chapter 5-1 ([[Downtown of Crag]]) at the far right wall. Mario must use [[Dottie]] to shrink and then flip to 3D. The Whacka is found in the hole. If a Whacka Bump is cooked, it creates a [[Roast Whacka Bump]].
