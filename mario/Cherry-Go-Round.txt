'''Cherry-Go-Round''' is both a 4-player and a [[Duel Minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. As a four-player minigame, it can be played with all four characters landing on the same-colour space and the roulette wheel landing on its name. [[Score Scuffle]] features the four-player version as the third minigame, after [[Domino Effect]] and before [[Study Fall]].

==Layout==
The minigame appears to take place in a bakery, considering the cakes in the background and the characters being on one themselves.

==Gameplay==
Each character has to spin around a bunched pair of cherries as fast as they can for five seconds to build momentum. When the time is up, each character automatically throws their cherries forward, high into the air. Whoever has their cherry thrown the farthest is the winner, and the distance for each thrown is measured in centimeters. If either none of the characters throw their cherries or if they all receive the same score, the minigame ends in a tie, although this is unlikely to occur.

Though the arrows show a clockwise swirl, the player can choose which direction to spin the cherry in.

==Controls==
*Stylus: Spin around a pair of cherries

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
*The minigame's title is a pun on "merry-go-round," but with "cherry" substituting as the first word.
