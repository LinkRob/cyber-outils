'''World 6-Airship''' is the final level in [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. It takes place on an [[Airship]]. [[Bowser Jr.]] is the boss fought at the end.

== Overview ==
It introduces [[Mechakoopa]]s, [[Rocky Wrench]]s and the RemoCon Clown.
==Walkthrough==
Mario begins the level near three spinning wheels, along with a [[? Block]] containing a power-up and two [[Mechakoopa]]s. A [[Rocky Wrench]] is then found on a platform, followed by spinning wheels above a [[Bottomless pit]]. More Rocky Wrenches are then found, along with a pile of [[crate]]s above a [[Warp Pipe]]. The [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] is then found. The [[Red Ring]] is encountered, along with two wheels and a Mechakoopa. A trail of [[coin]]s leading to a Rocky Wrench is found, followed by more crates. Another Rocky Wrench is encountered in front of two [[Brick Block]]s, one of which is a [[Coin Block]]. More wheels above a pit are then found, and two Mechakoopas can be found on them. Two more Rocky Wrenches are found, along with the Warp Pipe leading to Bowser Jr.

In Bowser Jr.'s fight, you must climb into their own [[Hero Car|Clown Car]] and knock Bowser Jr.'s [[Junior Clown Car]] into the electric fence on the walls of the room, or jump on him three times (this can only be done with the [[Propellor Mushroom|Propeller Suit]]). Players must also be careful not to get rammed into an electric fence. As Bowser Jr. takes shocking damage, he gets more aggressive and as a result, it becomes harder to ram him into the electric fences. After Bowser Jr. is defeated, he is caught by [[Kamek]] and ejects Mario and co. from the airship. Upon completion, the Airship leaves and the player unlocks [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7]].

==[[Star Coins]]==
* '''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is blocked by [[crate]]s on a platform with two [[Rocky Wrench]]es. Mario and co. must get it by [[Ground Pound]]ing through the crates.
* '''Star Coin 2''': Shortly after the first Star Coin, there are several crates surrounding a green [[Warp Pipe]]. Mario and co. must Ground Pound into the Pipe to reach an area below decks with several spinning wheels. The second Star Coin is floating between the third and fourth wheels..
* '''Star Coin 3''': Shortly after the [[Red Ring]], the third Star Coin is in the bottom part of a platform with one [[Mechakoopa]]. Mario and co. must [[Ground Pound]] through crates to access it.
