'''Castle Town Item Shop''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
The location is an [[Item Shop]] found on the east side of [[Beanbean Castle Town]], and it specifically has consumable items for sale. The Castle Town Item Shop's stock changes as [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] reach various milestones in their journey.
