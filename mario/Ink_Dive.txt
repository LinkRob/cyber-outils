'''Ink Dive''' is a move in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Ink Dive is a ability that can only be used by [[Blooper]] during a close play, so the player can activate it by pressing the button shown onscreen. In doing so, Blooper squirts ink that completely covers the baseman, allowing Blooper to safely reach the base without being tagged out.
