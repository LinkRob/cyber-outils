'''Niff T.''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Niff T. is a local business man that run's [[Niff T.'s Shop]] in [[Petalburg]]. His shop is the second shop that Mario encounters in the game. He looks exactly like [[Thriff T.]] and similar [[Plenn T.]] because he and [[Thriff T.]] share the same sprite and [[Plenn T.]]'s sprite is basically the same with one small difference in the mustache.

==Trivia==
* His name is a pun of the word "nifty."
