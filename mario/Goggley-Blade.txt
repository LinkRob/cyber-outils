'''Goggley-Blades''' are fish-like enemies in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.
{{br|left}}
==Profile==
===Physical description===
Goggley-Blades have a round body which is darker blue on the top, while their belly is colored light blue. Goggley-Blades have a spike attached to their forehead and yellow webbed feet. They also wear a pair of orange goggles.

===Behavior===
Goggley-Blades act somewhat like [[Fish Bone]]s in that they swim back and forth in a set pattern, but upon noticing [[Wario]], they charge at him in a set direction. Unlike Fish Bones, Goggley-Blades are not defeated upon colliding into a wall, but rather if Wario attacks them from below.
