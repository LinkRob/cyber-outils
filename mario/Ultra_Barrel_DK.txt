'''Ultra Barrel DK''' is an upgraded form of [[Donkey Kong]] in ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]''. It has Donkey Kong equipped with special Ultra Barrel rockets, which can only be acquired after completing all of Candy's Challenges. With the Ultra Barrels, Donkey Kong goes extremely fast, which causes fire to surround him. To equip the barrels, the player must hold down Z while selecting Donkey Kong on the Character Select screen. Ultra Barrel DK has the best stats in the game, with all of them being 5 out of 5. Ultra Barrel DK's bongos are red with white and yellow flames on them. They also have a white engine blower with the DK logo printed on them attached to the sides
