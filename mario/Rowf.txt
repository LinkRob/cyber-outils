'''Rowf''' is a character in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is a [[Doogan]] who runs his own badge shop, aptly named [[Rowf's Badge Shop]], which is located in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. He has a son named [[Rhuff]], who follows him wherever he goes.

==History==
Rowf is always seen at his badge shop, where he sells different badges to [[Mario]] whenever he leaves the area and returns. During the events of Chapter 4, a [[Shy Guy]] had taken Rowf's [[Calculator]], and Rowf needs it returned to him because Rowf relies on his calculator to keep track of his sales and therefore cannot sell anything to Mario without it. After Mario manages to retrieve the calculator from [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]], Rowf rewards Mario the [[I Spy]] badge, allowing him to pick up on hidden items close-by.

During the parade at the end of the game, Rowf and Rhuff can be seen walking in it carrying large bags on their backs but in the opposite direction. They were stopped by [[Moustafa]] and a couple of [[Nomadimouse|Nomadimice]]. It was unknown if Moustafa and the Nomadimice were telling Rowf and Rhuff that they were going in the wrong direction but after their conversation, they continued on, with Rowf and Rhuff continuing to head in the opposite direction of the parade.
