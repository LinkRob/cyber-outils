'''Chocolate Island 4''' is a [[level]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', more specifically the [[world]] [[Chocolate Island]]. Despite its name, Chocolate Island 4 is not the fourth level of Chocolate Island, as it becomes accessible only after completing [[Chocolate Fortress]], but completing Chocolate Island 4 unlocks [[Chocolate Island 5]], following after in numerical order.

==Description==
Chocolate Island 4 is an underground level consisting of chocolate [[lava]] and [[diagonal platform]]s. Only two enemies appear in the level, [[Mega Mole]]s and [[Chargin' Chuck|Clappin' Chucks]], and depending on the path taken, Chocolate Island 4 could be the first level where Mega Moles are encountered.
