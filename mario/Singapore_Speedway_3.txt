'''Singapore Speedway 3''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' that first appeared in the 2023 Winter Tour. As its name implies, it is the third route of [[Singapore Speedway]], placing heavy emphasis on the Chinatown section at the southeast corner of the city.

== Layout ==
Racers on Singapore Speedway 3 start the race facing the opposite direction to the [[Singapore Speedway 1|first]] [[Singapore Speedway 2|two]] routes, akin to [[Bangkok Rush 3]] with [[Bangkok Rush]] and [[London Loop 3]] with [[London Loop]]; according to ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', this is west. After a right turn, they will continue straight onto the Float at Marina Bay, using a ramp to access a flower-shaped platform that they use to get to the ArtScience Museum in front of the Marina Bay Sands resort. The racers can choose to either drive on the museum's canopy or on the area in front of it. Afterwards, they get back to the mainland, heading into the Chinatown area through the entrance far from the [[Finish line|starting line]]. This area has a right turn, then follows with a left turn, then a right turn. When the racers reach the other side, they will end up back at the four-way intersection, approaching from the south. To make their way back to the [[Finish line|starting line]], the racers turn left. (Going straight will lead them onto [[Singapore Speedway 2]], while turning right takes them down [[Singapore Speedway 1]]; those paths are blocked off in Singapore Speedway 3.)

''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' has the racers go through the Chinatown section twice, the first time during the second lap, although they continue heading north through the intersection to switch to [[Singapore Speedway 2]]. On the final lap, the racers make a left at the Float by Marina Bay to follow Singapore Speedway 3, Chinatown included, for the rest of the race.

In both games, the music in the Chinatown changes to a Chinese-sounding version of the standard theme.

== Trivia ==

* [[Madrid Drive]] has a similar phaenomenon with the background music when the racers go through the soccer stadium in [[Madrid Drive 1]] and the Prado Museum in [[Madrid Drive 3]]. When they go through the soccer stadium during races on Madrid Drive 1, the music changes from flamenco music to seemingly made with crowd cheers, and in the Prado Museum of Madrid Drive 3, it becomes quieter.
* The real Chinatown would be at the west side of the track, going off real world geography.
