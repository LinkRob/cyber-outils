{{Nihongo|'''Star Haven'''|星の国|Hoshi no Kuni|Star Land}} is located above the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. It only appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Star Haven is home to the [[Star Spirits]] whom live in the Star Sanctuary to guard the [[Star Rod]] and grant the wishes to the citizens of the Mushroom Kingdom.

== Layout ==
Star Haven has many unique houses that the Star Kids live in, as well as the [[Star Haven Shop]], which sells various items, including the powerful [[Shooting Star (item)|Shooting Star item]].

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
[[Bowser]] attacked the Star Haven prior to the events of ''Paper Mario,'' and stole the Star Rod from the Star Spirits before kidnapping them. [[Mario]], after rescuing the Star Spirits, entered the Star Haven by the [[Star Road]] before traveling to [[Bowser's Castle]].
