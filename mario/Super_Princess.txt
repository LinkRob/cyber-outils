'''Super Princess''' is the super form of [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]]. Toadstool's appearance as Super Princess appeared in "[[The Trojan Koopa]]" in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Retaining the appearance of Toadstool, Super Princess has dark purple on the bottom of her dress and red on the top of her dress.

=== Powers and abilities ===
==== Powers ====
* '''Freeing''': Toadstool can free herself from the ball and the chain in this form, which her normal form cannot do.
* '''Hammer Attacks''': After the Koopas threw the hammers at Toadstool, she can grab the hammers and throw them.
* '''Stomping''':
== Biography ==
{{Spoiler|hide}}
Witnessing [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] troubled with [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s army. Toadstool wished upon a [[Starman]], and the Starman came to her. Grabbing onto Starman, she transformed into Super Princess and broke free from the ball and chain. As Toadstool intervened the scene, The Koopas threw the hammers at Toadstool but she grabbed them and threw it at the tent stands. Mario and Luigi trapped them with the fallen tent. The Shy Guys proceeded to run away but Toadstool stomped on them.
{{endspoiler}}

== Trivia ==
* Unlike Mario, Luigi and Toad's super forms, Super Princess does not grant Toadstool the ability of flight nor the ability to summon [[fireball]]s.
