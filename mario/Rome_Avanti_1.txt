'''Rome Avanti 1''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', first appearing in the Night Tour of 2023 alongside [[Rome Avanti 2]]. As its name implies, it is the first layout of [[Rome Avanti]], taking place in Roma, the capital of Italy.

== Layout ==
Rome Avanti 1's layout brings a stadium called the Colosseo Romano (Italian for Romn Colosseum), which is located in the southeastern area of the city, into focus, and is one of two layouts to visit the southern half of the city. The racers start in the Via del Corso, facing away from a roundabout. After the starting line, they make a slight left turn, followed by an even slighter right turn. They then head up the Colosseo Romano, going through a spiral loop. This loop ends in a ramp that the racers use to get out of the Colosseo Romano, although they need to avoid some bats while going up the loop. After the Colosseo Romano, the racers will go through the Foro Romano (Italian for Roman Forum), where they can use some fallen columns as ramps for [[Trick|jump boosts]]. They take two right turns around il Arco Settimiósio Severósio (the Arch of Septimius Severus), followed by a left turn at la Piazza del Campidoglio (the Capitoline Square). This is followed by a right turn and a left turn leading near the Fontana Nettunósio (Fountain of Neptune) in la Piazza Navona. Afterwards, they must make a right turn to follow the road that leads into a roundabout at la Piazza del Pópolo (Square of the Populace) from the southwest direction, then a sharper right turn to leave the roundabout to the south and get back to the Via del Corso and return to the [[Finish line|starting line]].

In ''Mario Kart 8'', both halves of la Piazza del Campidóglio can be traversed, and the racers can take a shortcut through il Arco Settimiósio Severósio.
