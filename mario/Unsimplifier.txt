'''Unsimplifier''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear Unsimplifier by using only one [[Badge Point|BP]]. While it causes Mario's [[action command]]s to become more difficult to perform, Unsimplifier also increases the amount of [[Star Power]] Mario recovers when using his action commands.

Unsimplifier can be bought from the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] in [[Rogueport]] for fifty [[coin]]s.
