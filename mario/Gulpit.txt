'''Gulpits''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Gulpits are bulky, indigo creatures with long, pink tongues and greenish blushing cheeks.

===Behavior===
Gulpits focus their attacks with their tongue. So long as there are [[Gulpits' Rocks]] lying around, a Gulpit swallows one and spits it out right at [[Mario]]. This can be a large sum of damage, although this depends on the type of rock. Once there are no rocks left, a Gulpit walks up to Mario and lick him, dealing only a little bit of damage.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
They are only encountered in the [[Shiver Region]], specifically throughout [[Shiver Snowfield]] and [[Shiver Mountain]].
