The '''Choco Pasta Dish''' is one of many pasta recipes from the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. [[Dyllis]] can cook this dish simply by mixing a [[Mild Cocoa Bean]] with a [[Fresh Pasta Bunch]]. The meal resembles pasta dipped in chocolate sauce. Using this item restores 5 HP and cures Poison, though both ingredients have the same effect separately.

== Trivia ==
* This is the second chocolate-covered plate made by [[Dyllis]], the first being [[Chocolate Cake]].
