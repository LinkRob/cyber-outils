'''Starbeans Espresso''' is [[bean]] coffee served at [[Starbeans Café]] in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. This coffee is created by giving the [[Starbeans Employee]] a certain amount of beans to make the coffee. Every time [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] makes a coffee, [[Professor E. Gadd]], the owner of Starbeans Cafe, arrives and tastes a small sample before giving them an item. When drank, it permanently increases one of Mario or Luigi's main stats such as HP or ATK.

==Appearance==
The Starbeans Espresso all appears to be a small white cup with the logo on front. It is an parody of Starbucks Coffee in appearance.

===Types of Espresso===
*'''Woohoo Blend:''' This blend is created from 25 Woo Beans. This drink also increases the base HP by 4.
*'''Hoohoo Blend:''' This blend is created from 25 Hoo Beans. This drink also increases the base Bros. Points by 4.
*'''Chuckle Blend:''' This blend is created from 25 Chuckle Beans. This drink also increases the base Speed by 4.
*'''Teehee Blend:''' This blend is created from 25 Hee Beans. This drink also increases the base 'Stache by 4.
*'''Hoolumbian:''' This blend is created from 15 Woo Beans and 10 Hoo Beans. This drink also increases the base Power by 4.
*'''Chuckoccino:''' This blend is created from 15 Woo Beans and 10 Chuckle Beans. This drink also increases the base Defense by 4.
*'''Teeheespresso:''' This blend is created from 15 Woo Beans and 10 Hee Beans. This drink also increases the base of any random stat by 6.

===Items Awarded===
As stated above, every time Mario and Luigi makes a different type of drink each time, E. Gadd will appear and give them a certain item each time. The items include:
*'''[[Greed Wallet|Greed Wallet]]'''
*'''[[Bonus Ring]]'''
*'''[[Excite Spring]]'''
*'''[[Great Force]]'''
*'''[[Power Grip]]'''
*'''[[Cobalt Necktie]]'''
*'''[[Gameboy Horror SP]]'''
