'''Coral''' is a minor reoccurring object in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
Corals tend to serve as minor obstructions in underwater levels that [[Mario]] or another playable character have to swim around. Coral is usually pink in color. In ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', most of the coral appears as a stack of rings.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:CoralSMB.png|thumb|200px|An underwater scene depicting Coral in varying sizes in ''Super Mario Bros.'']]
Coral can be found in [[World 2-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-2]] and [[World 7-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 7-2]], where Mario or [[Luigi]] have to swim around the Coral in their path. It is possible for them to stand on coral, but enemies can swim through it.

===''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''===
Coral appears in [[World 9 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 9]], but at horizontal angle, allowing Mario or Luigi to use them as a platform.

===''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''===
Coral returns in a few underwater [[level]]s, acting the same as before. In [[World 4-4 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 4-4]], there is pink coral designed after its appearance from ''Super Mario Bros.''.

===''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
Coral is featured as a placeable object when designing an underwater area in the ''Super Mario Bros.'' or ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' style. Here, Coral can be formed by placing ground blocks in a stack.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
Coral only appears during [[Bowser]]'s underwater level, [[World 2 (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|World 2]]. Because of Bowser's large size, Coral can serve as a hindrance to him, leaving him open to attack from either a [[Blooper]] or an [[X-Naut]].
