'''Los Angeles Laps 2''' is a course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', first appearing in the third Los Angeles Tour, which took place in 2022. It is actually the third route of [[Los Angeles Laps]] in spite of its number.

== Layout ==
The racers start the course far from the Santa Mónica Pier. After a left turn, they drive straight through a four-way intersection, with the [[Los Angeles Laps 1|Santa Mónica Pier]] on the right. Up ahead, they make a slight left turn to get into the Inglewood Oil Field, passing through an entry gate. There is a fork in the road, but the right path has a branching path that leads to the left path. The paths themselves do not cause slowdowns, but the mud to their sides does. Pump jacks can be seen in this section as obstacles. After a U-turn to the left, more pump jacks can be seen. There is also a large water tower. This is followed by a sharp right, where the racers cross a bridge that passes over [[Los Angeles Laps 3|a road leading away from the Santa Mónica Pier]]. After another left turn, the racers will head back to the [[Finish line|starting line]].

''[[Mario Kart 8]]''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s version of [[Los Angeles Laps]] reserves this route for the final segment of section 2 (which was largely dedicated to [[Los Angeles Laps 3]]) and the majority of section 3. A [[Glide Ramp]] was added after the U-turn, and the racers can pass through some rings to get a boost to their speed in the air. Two grassy shortcuts were added to the area where the original starting line was for racers to use a [[Dash Mushroom]] to get through, and the entry gate has a Mario Kart logo, indicating the start of Section 3. Additionally, arrival at the four-way intersection in section 3 diverts them onto the [[Los Angeles Laps 1|Santa Mónica Pier]].
