'''World 6-1''' is the twenty-ninth level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Layout==
The level begins as [[Mario]] crosses a long-wooded bridge across a vast ocean. Along this bridge are several [[Cheep-Cheep]]s that jump over the bridge attempting to hit Mario. [[Goomba]]s and [[Biddybud]]s also appear on this bridge. After crossing a trip wire at the far end of the bridge, Mario will access the level's [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]]. From here, Mario must traverse a couple more tripwires to make way to the final bridge. After making it nearly half-way across this final bridge, several [[Bubba]] begin to swim out the water and destroy nearly the entire bridge. From the other side of the bridge, Mario can easily climb to the [[Flagpole]].

==Enemies==
Enemies that appeared in World 6-1:
*[[Cheep-Cheep]]
*[[Bubba]]
*[[Goomba]]
*[[Biddybud]]

==Star Medals==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] is found behind the rocks at the very beginning of the level.
*'''Star Medal 2''': By going down from the Checkpoint, Mario can find a [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the second Star Medal in an underwater mini level.
*'''Star Medal 3''': On the third trip wire, Mario can hit the [[Question Block]] to climb to another wire. Here is where the final Star Medal is.
