'''Shout! Factory''' released DVD versions of several DiC-produced cartoons, including the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' ones.
*''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!: Volume 1'' (March 28, 2006)
*''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!: Volume 2'' (October 31, 2006)
*''The Adventures Of Super Mario Bros. 3: The Complete Series'' (June 26, 2007)
*''Captain N And The New Super Mario World'' (November 26, 2007)
These sets were discontinued and rereleased by [[NCircle Entertainment]] in 2012 after Shout!'s deal with Cookie Jar Entertainment (formerly DiC) had expired.
