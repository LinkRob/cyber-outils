'''Fawfulized''' is a term used by [[Fawful]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. Fawfulized creatures are created for the sole purpose of serving Fawful. Fawfulized creatures usually have similar physical features such as green skin and thick glasses. A majority of Fawfulized creatures are mechanical in nature, but there are non mechanical Fawfulized creatures like the [[Fawful Guy]] and [[Magifoofa]] (most non-mechanical minions are actually Fawfulized minions of [[Bowser]]. It is very similar to [[Shroobified]] a method that [[Shroobs]] use.

==Trivia==
*The term is a combination of the words Fawful and Hypnotize.
*The term is a pun of ''fossilised".
*The enemies from the lobby of [[Bowser's Castle]] don't appear to be Fawfulized but moreso brainwashed; such enemies also appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.
