'''Service Ace''' is a restaurant found on the campus of the [[Royal Tennis Academy]]. It first appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]] and returns in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Layout==
===''Mario Tennis'' (Game Boy Color)===
The Service Ace is a white building with a red roof. In front of it is a sign that reads "Cafe."

===''Mario Tennis: Power Tour''===
The Service Ace has been given a makeover. The exterior consists of white and brown walls, a picture of a coffee mug just above the entrance, and a pink, striped roof. In front of the building are two round tables with a chair on the left and right side and an umbrella bearing a light blue and yellow-striped pattern. A green stand in front of the door has the specials.

Inside of the restaurant is a booth with two seats, and further onward are three more tables. Various plants can be seen throughout the restaurant. The bottom-center of the restaurant consists of a small buffet run by two chefs. To the right of the buffet is an exit that leads outside to the two aforementioned tables. Left of the main entrance is a staircase that leads to the Tea Lounge, which has five tables.

==In ''Mario Tennis'' (Game Boy Color)==
When starting the game with either [[Alex]] or [[Nina]], this is one of the areas that [[Emily]] introduces them to.
