The '''Heart Plant''' is a species of plant that is found during the events of ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is a plant that has two leaves at the base, from which a brown question-mark-coiled stem stretches outward.

==Description==
[[File:Jade jungle.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario and [[Watt]] near two Heart Plants.]]
Heart Plants grow all over [[Lavalava Island]], especially deep in the [[Jade Jungle]]. [[Mario]] finds many of them during his search for the [[Fearsome 5|Baby Yoshi]]s that have gotten lost in the jungle. When Mario touches one of them, it uncoils and may drop a [[heart]] that restores 1 [[Heart Point|HP]]. They have an infinite supply of hearts, so every time the player touches a plant, it may give another heart, even if Mario is at full HP.

Heart Plants are difficult to distinguish from [[Hurt Plant]]s, which duplicate their appearance. The only way to find out which type of plant it is to inspect and uncoil it. If it ends up being a Hurt Plant, Mario has to battle it.
