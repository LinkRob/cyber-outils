'''Watermelons''' are objects in certain [[microgame]]s of the {{series|WarioWare}} within the {{franchise|Wario}}.

==History==
===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
In ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', [[Body Block]] involves people keeping a watermelon bouncing around.

===''WarioWare: Touched!''===
In ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', these fruit appear in [[Steel Chef]], [[It Slices, It Dices]], and [[Global Warning]].

===''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''===
In ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', watermelons appear in the microgame [[Universal Marionette]].

===''WarioWare Gold''===
In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', Watermelons retain their appearance in the returning microgames Steel Chef and Universal Marionette, although Body Block is rethemed and does not include watermelons.
