'''Honey-Bees''' are an enemy in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

==In ''Virtual Boy Wario Land''==
They only appear in [[Stage 9 (Virtual Boy Wario Land)|Stage 9]], where they attack [[Wario]] throwing down either a [[watermelon]] or a [[bomb]], the latter of which explodes after a period of time. An artwork of a Honey-Bee shows it carrying the former object. Honey-Bees appear in the foreground, and can neither be touched nor defeated by Wario.
