'''Red Balloons''' are a recurring item in the {{series|Donkey Kong Country}}. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', but make their only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in an issue of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''.

==Description==
Red Balloons are the equivalent to the ''Mario'' franchise's [[1-Up Mushroom]]s, as collecting a Red Balloon earns [[Donkey Kong]] or another [[Kong]] an extra life. In their earlier appearances, Red Balloons, like with [[Green Balloon]]s and [[Blue Balloon]]s, are in the shape of the main protagonist's head and depict a likeness of their face; for instance, in ''Donkey Kong Country'', Red Balloons are shaped like Donkey Kong's head, while in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'', they are instead in the shape of [[Diddy Kong]]'s. Since ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'', Red Balloons have had the appearance of regular red balloons.
