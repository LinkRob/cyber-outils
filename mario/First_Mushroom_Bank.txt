The '''First Mushroom Bank''' is a location in the [[DIC Entertainment]] cartoons ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' and ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
The First Mushroom Bank is a banking corporation situated in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and surrounding areas.

==In other media==
===''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''===
The only appearance of a First Mushroom Bank in the show is "[[The Unzappables]]," where [[Al Koopone]], [[Mouser]], a [[Koopa Troopa]] and [[Tryclyde]] each try to rob the First Mushroom Bank of [[Crime Land]]. After Al Koopone blows up the vault door with a [[Bob-omb|Bob-Omb]], [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and his friends attempt to intercept his attack, but they fail to stop the Koopa Pack because they are wearing [[Unzappable Hat]]s. Later on, when Mario and his group each get Unzappable Hats of their own, they stop the Koopa Pack from robbing the First Mushroom Bank.

===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
Like in ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'', the First Mushroom Bank only appears in one episode, "[[Crimes R Us]]," but it is different than the one in ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' Here, after being taught about bank robbery from [[Crime Wave Clyde]], the [[Koopalings]] pull a bank heist on the First Mushroom Bank, demanding all of the [[Toad (species)|Mushroom People]] to relinquish their valuables. [[Bully Koopa|Bully]] demands for one of the bankers to toss all of the money from a safe into a sack. [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] show up at the First Mushroom Bank in time to make the Koopalings retreat.
