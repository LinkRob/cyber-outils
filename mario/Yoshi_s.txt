'''Yoshi's''' is a fast-food chain that appears in ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. It is named after [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] where it is found throughout the courses and tracks in the game. Most of the employees consists of other [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] but there are also [[Toad (species)|Toads]] and [[Shy Guy]]s that serve as employees as well. The food that is served from Yoshi's are called [[Dash Food]].

==History==
There are many different types of ways that the Dash Food from Yoshi's is served. They can commonly be found outside of establishments found in courses where an employee will have the food ready for any racer to eat. There are also food stands and food trucks where a Yoshi, Toad, or Shy Guy is standing by that will personally hand out a Dash Food to a racer. Rarely, there are also wheeled or stationary kiosks that automatically dispense Dash Food whenever a racer is nearby or not.

Yoshi's is quite the popular food chain in the game as they are found throughout the world. There are also trucks with the Yoshi's logo on it that carry Dash Food, and the logo can be found in other areas as well.
