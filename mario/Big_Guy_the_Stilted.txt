'''Big Guy the Stilted''' is a giant mechanical [[Stilt Guy]] manned by several [[Shy Guy]]s. It is fought by [[Blue Yoshi]] at the end of [[World 4 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 4]], in the [[castle]] level [[Castle of Big Guy the Stilted]]. The boss fight against Big Guy the Stilted takes place on a platform above the [[lava]].
