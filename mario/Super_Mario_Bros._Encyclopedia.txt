'''The Super Mario Bros. Encyclopedia''' (full title: '''Super Mario Encyclopedia: The Official Guide to the First 30 Years'''), also known as '''Encyclopedia Super Mario Bros.''' and originally known as '''Nintendo Official Guide Book Super Mario Bros. Encyclopedia 30th Anniversary 1985–2015''' is an Encyclopedia for [[Mario (franchise)|The Mario Series]] that was created in 2015 by [[Nintendo]] to celebrate the 30th Anniversary of the famous [[Super Mario Bros.]] game for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] and [[Family Computer]] back in 1985. This is an Encyclopedia that’s unique to other Mario Encyclopedias, biggest example is that it uses the [https://www.mariowiki.com Super Mario Wiki] as its main source, which turned out obviously to be a big failure due to it being 2015 and the Mario Wiki is a fan made Wiki so it can be easily vandalized or flat out wrong. The Encyclopedia talks about games from the first Super Mario Bros. to the latest game during 2015.
