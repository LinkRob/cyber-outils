'''Musical Notes''' are a group of five {{w|eighth note}} characters who appear in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. The members include Musical Notes E, G, B, C, D, and lastly F. They are named after a different letter (ranging from B through G). 

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Every Musical Note has the same appearance as an eighth note, although each Musical Note can be distinguished from being a different color. Musical Note E is blue, Musical Note G is green, Musical Note B is purple, Musical Note C is yellow, Musical Note D is orange, and Musical Note F is red. Every Musical Note has a different size, from smallest to biggest: E, G, B, C, D, and F.

===Behavior===
Musical Notes are able to talk to the characters, and each Musical Note often uses words beginning with the same letter as its note. 

==In ''Mario Party DS''==
The Musical Notes are found in [[Toadette's Music Room]]. (The [[Star (Mario Party series)|star]] is a variable price. That is, similar to [[Pagoda Peak]] from ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.) Only one Musical Note appears on the board map at a time, and a character can land on its space to buy a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] from it. Each Musical Note charges a different number of [[Coin]]s, with each larger one requiring more Coins. Musical Note E costs 5 Coins, Musical Note G costs 10, Musical Note B costs 15, Musical Note C costs 20, Musical Note D costs 25, and Musical Note F costs 30. But the star's price never goes over 30 coins. After Musical Note F, the cycle restarts back to Musical Note E and the star goes back to 5 coins.
