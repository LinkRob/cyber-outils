'''Mertropolis''' is a location in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[The Ugly Mermaid]]."

==Description==
Mertropolis is an underwater city protected by an oxygen-containing dome. Though never stated outright, it is implied to be somewhere within [[Water Land]]. Mertropolis is inhabited by fish-like merfolk who each wear a water-filled fishbowl, due to Mertropolis being filled with oxygen. Metropolis is ruled over by [[King Mackerel]] and his daughter, [[Holly Mackerel]].

==In other media==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
In "The Ugly Mermaid" [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] plans to take over Mertropolis using his [[Doom Sub]] and rename it "Kooptropolis", but his plans were foiled by the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] when they used a group of [[Jelectro]]s against his submarine.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Mertropolis's name is a portmanteau of the words "merfolk" and "metropolis," owing to how Mertropolis is a large city inhabited by fish-like merfolk.
