The '''''Dragon Quest''''' franchise is a video game franchise owned by [[Square Enix]]. Its mascot is [[Slime]]. The franchise has made direct crossover appearances with the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] a few times, notably in ''[[Itadaki Street DS]]'' and ''[[Fortune Street]]''. Aside from this, Slime has appeared as a playable character in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', since Challenger's Pack 2, [[Hero]] has been a playable character alongside ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters and various others.
