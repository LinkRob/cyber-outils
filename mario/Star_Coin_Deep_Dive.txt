'''Star Coin Deep Dive''' (also known as '''Superstar Road-3''') is the third level of [[Superstar Road]]. It appears in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

== Layout ==
The player starts in an area with several [[Spiny Cheep Cheep]]s below. A [[Warp Pipe]] will take Luigi to an underwater area. Multiple Pipes will appear, as well as a [[Cheep Chomp]] and a [[Blooper]]. Multiple [[Cheep Cheep]]s guard a barrage of coins and the first Star Coin. Many jet pipes will appear, pushing the second Star Coin. A [[Red Ring]], a Blooper and more Pipes will appear. The third Star Coin is followed by multiple Pipes, some Bloopers and Spiny Cheep Cheeps. Afterwards is a Warp Pipe which takes the player to the Goal Pole.
== [[Star Coin]]s ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In the beginning of the underwater area, several Cheep Cheeps guard the Star Coin. The player must use Fireballs to access it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the first Star Coin, the jet pipes will push it around.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Just like the second Star Coin, jet pipes will push it around in a later segment of the level.
==[[List of Luigi sightings in New Super Luigi U|Luigi Sighting]]==
*A swimming 8-bit Luigi can be found by having the [[Cheep Chomp]] destroy the Brick Blocks under the [[Red Ring]].
