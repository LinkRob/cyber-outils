'''Coins''' are recurring items in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. They appear in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''/''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
Coins appear primarily in special matches known as Coin Battles. Players deal damage to each other, earning Coins that fly out of the struck opponents in the process; players will only lose coins if they are KO'd. The amount of damage and knockback the move deals determines the amount and type of coins produced, which can be bronze, silver, gold, bills (from ''Brawl'' onward), or any mixture thereof. If a player KO'd on the side blast lines of the stage, the player drops some of their coins on the stage, which opponents and teammates can collect. If the player is KO'd or KO'd on the top or bottom blast line, no coins can be snatched, although in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'', coins appear if the player is Star KO'd or hits the screen.

[[File:Coinsmash.jpg|thumb|left|A coin battle, from ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'']]
In ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'', gold Coins are worth ten, silver are worth five, and bronze are worth one. Whenever a player gets KO'ed, they lose half of their coins (rounded down). The player or team with the most Coins when time runs out wins. If a tie occurs, then it will go to [[Sudden Death]].

Coins reappear in the Coin Battle mode for ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', which adds [[Bill]]s to the mix. The values of the Coins are different in this game: Gold coins are worth six Coins, silver coins are worth three, and bronze coins are worth one, while the Smash Bills are worth ten Coins each. If KO'd, players lose half their coins as in ''Melee'', but only up to a maximum of 100.

Coin Battle returns only in ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'', with the coins and bills serving the same purpose as they did in previous games. In the PAL version of ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'', however, the trophy description for the gold earned in various modes of the game refers to them as "Smash Coins", despite both serving different functions.

Coins are also needed to operate the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Lottery|lottery machine]] in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'' for collecting [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|Trophies]]. Similarly, in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', they are used to operate [[Coin Launcher]], where trophies and [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|stickers]] can be obtained.
