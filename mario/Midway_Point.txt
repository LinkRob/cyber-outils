'''Midway Point''' is a gameplay mechanic representing the saving point within a [[level]]. If the player reaches a Midway Point, their progress is saved for that level, and if they lose a life, they continue from that Midway Point. They first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', as [[Midway Gate]]s, and later appeared in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', as [[Checkpoint Flag]]s usable by [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or [[Toad]]. If a character is [[Small Mario|Small]], a Midway Point can change them to their [[Super Mario (form)|Super]] form. Their latest appearance was in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.
