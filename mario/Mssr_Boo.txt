'''Mssr Boo''' (short for '''Monsieur Boo''') is a boss faced by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in the VS COM mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. Mssr Boo is a ghost who wears a red beret. While he has [[Boo]] in his name, Mssr Boo's relationship with the species has not been established. On Normal difficulty, Mssr Boo is the seventh boss fought, but in Hard difficulty it is the first boss.
