'''You Dropped This?''' is a [[Microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' It appears in the level Surfin' Surprise, which [[Jimmy Thang]] has for his level. It uses the Pounce form for its gameplay.

==Layout==
The setting of the Microgame seems to be either the side of a river or the edge of a lake or pond, with the point of view being on land. Not long after the Microgame starts, something will fall in, and a man wearing white fishes the object and another object (The distinguishability varies depending on the present level.) out.

==Gameplay==
To win this Microgame, players have to memorise which object got dropped into the water. If they select the wrong object, they lose.

===Difficulty levels===
The objects get closer in their physical appearance as the game gets harder. On the first level, they are easily distinguishable. On the second level, they have slightly different shapes but the same colour. On the third level, they are the same object, but have different colour patterns.

==Behind the scenes==
===Creation and development===
This Microgame is based on the tale of ''The Honest Woodcutter'', in which the titular character loses his axe and a spirit fishes it out with two different ones, and the woodcutter rejects all the axes except his own, but gets all three of them as a reward. The man who presents the objects seems to be based on said spirit.
