'''Mario Cup''' is a cup in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''. It follows after the [[Toad Cup]], and has a Normal difficulty setting. The tracks shown in this cup are water-related themes, consisting of [[Splash Circuit]] and [[Tropical Coast]].
