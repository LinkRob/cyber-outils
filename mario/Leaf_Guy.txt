'''Leaf Guys''' are enemies and a subspecies of [[Fly Guy]]s that appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' but only on the Boss fight against [[Wiggler]]. Leaf Guys are green Fly Guys with leaves as their propellers.

In battle, two Leaf Guys will support Wiggler by holding a giant [[turnip]] and dropping it on [[Bowser]]. The player must inhale the Fly Guys as Bowser, leaving the turnip falling down into Wiggler, leaving him vulnerable if he is red or damage him if Wiggler is yellow. Then [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] fight the Fly Guys inside [[Bowser's Body]]. They have only one attack, which is flying at both of the Bros., who must counter the attack with their [[hammer]]s. In another one of Wiggler's attacks, he will grab a watering can to grow several plants. Then he will grab them and throw them at Bowser, but some of the plants are Leaf Guys. Bowser must retreat into his shell to avoid getting hit by a Leaf Guy.

Leaf Guys appear one final time in the game in the fight against the [[Tower of Yikk]]. They will aid the tower by holding bombs and dropping them at Bowser if he takes too long to dispose of them with [[fireball]]s. Sometimes, they may carry [[Mushroom]]s.
