'''Onett''' is a recurring [[stage]] in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' as a Melee Stage, in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', and in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
[[File:SSBM Onett Messageboard.png|thumb|The "Onett Message Board" hidden offscreen]]
There are a few features in Onett. The most deadly feature is the the [[Car (obstacle)|cars]] on the bottom of the stage, that will ram into the fighters if they do not evade them on time. There are a couple of trees above the left house, which the player can [[jump]] on to escape their foes. If a player throws an explosive at a car or gets hit by one, the car spins out of control. There is also a three-story medication store with a billboard on the top that falls if it is stepped on too much.

Several other elements of ''EarthBound'' can be seen around the stage, such as a baseball diamond and the crane from the [[w:c:earthbound:Dusty Dunes Desert|Dusty Dunes Desert]].

If the player stands just to the right of the blue house and pauses, they can zoom out to see the "Onett Message Board" to the right. It reads: "''Caution: A black van driven by this guy'' (seen in the picture right of the message) ''has been spotted racing recklessly through town. Be careful!''". This sign cannot be properly read in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' unless the [[Devil]] is summoned as an [[Assist Trophy]] character and moves the stage until the sign becomes visible.

In ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'' and ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', there is an [[onigiri]] behind the flowerbed to the left of the drug store.

===Origin===
The stage takes place at an identically titled [[w:c:earthbound:Onett|location]] appearing in ''[[w:c:earthbound:EarthBound|EarthBound]]''. Onett is originally [[Ness]]'s hometown and starting point, and it appears in [[w:c:earthbound:Eagleland|Eagleland]].

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''Super Smash Bros. Melee''===
Onett is one of Ness's two home stages, aside from [[Fourside]]. As such, all battles involving Ness occur at either of the stages in a single-player mode: [[Classic Mode]], [[Adventure (Super Smash Bros. Melee)|Adventure]], or [[All-Star Mode|All-Star]]. Onett is Stage 9 of Adventure, and the player has to battle three Nesses at once; after defeating them, the player can proceed to Stage 10: [[Icicle Mountain]].

Onett has an alternate track called ''Mother 2'' (the Japanese title of the game ''EarthBound''). It is the overworld solo music from the original ''[[w:c:earthbound:EarthBound Beginnings|Mother]]'', and it also resurfaces in ''EarthBound'' as the music for Ness's house after Ness receives the [[w:c:earthbound:Sound Stone|Sound Stone]] near the beginning of the game.

===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
[[File:SSBB Onett Stage.png|thumb|Onett in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
The unlock match for Ness takes place in Onett.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
Like in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', Onett is where the unlock match for Ness takes place.
