'''Treasures''' are collectible items in the [[Pikmin (franchise)|''Pikmin'' franchise]], debuting in ''[[w:c:pikmin:Pikmin 2|Pikmin 2]]''.

==Description==
The purpose of treasures is different in every game, but a consistency is that all of them have some form of value that plays a role towards the game's objective. In ''Pikmin 2'', treasures are valued in [[w:c:pikmin:Poko|Pokos]], and they pay off the debt of the [[w:c:pikmin:Hocotate Transportation Company|Hocotate Transportation Company]]. In ''[[w:c:pikmin:Hey! Pikmin|Hey! Pikmin]]'' and ''[[w:c:pikmin:Pikmin 4|Pikmin 4]]'', treasures are valued in [[w:c:pikmin:Sparklium|Sparklium]], the power source of the [[w:c:pikmin:S.S. Dolphin II|S.S. Dolphin II]] in ''Hey! Pikmin'' and the [[w:c:pikmin:S.S. Shepherd|S.S. Shepherd]] in ''Pikmin 4''.

A few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-themed treasures appear in the ''Pikmin'' games. ''Pikmin 2'' has [[Decorative Goo]], a tube of acrylic paint branded "[[Mario Paint]]s", and [[Flame of Tomorrow]], a box of cigarettes of the fictional "[[Bowser]] Breath Brand." Neither of the two treasures have ''Mario'' branding in original Japanese releases. Regardless, they are stored in the [[w:c:pikmin:Treasure Hoard|Treasure Hoard]] after being collected. ''Hey! Pikmin'' has several treasures of [[amiibo]] figures from other [[Nintendo]] franchises, predominantly ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', that appear in a [[w:c:pikmin:Secret Spot|Secret Spot]] when the actual figure is scanned in real life: One example is the [[Colossus in Suspenders]], which is a [[Mario]] amiibo figure that appears by scanning one in real life. In his log, [[Olimar]] even comments on the treasures themselves.
