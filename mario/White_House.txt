The '''White House''' is one of the landmark locations within [[Washington, D.C.]] in the [[United States of America]]. It was the home of the then president, [[Abraham Lincoln]]. So far, the White House has made a few other appearances within ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media, [[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!|''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'']] flashback episode "[[Plummers Academy]]", ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Reptiles in the Rose Garden]]" and in ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''.

In "Reptiles in the Rose Garden," [[Bowser|King Koopa]] warps the White House and all the people inside to [[Dark Land]] so that [[Wendy O. Koopa|Kootie Pie Koopa]] can rule over America. [[Mario]] manages to warp it back into Washington, D.C.

In the PC and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] releases of ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]'', Mario has to recover [[Abraham Lincoln]]'s [[Spectacles]] and return it to him within his office.
