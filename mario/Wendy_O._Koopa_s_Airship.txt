'''Wendy O. Koopa's Airship''' is the third overall [[airship]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' that attacked the Sea Side territorial landmark of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

== Layout ==
This airship focuses on its vast array of cannons, sometimes fulfilling puzzling situations for [[Mario]] to dodge and traverse the fortress.

=== Battle ===
The boss of World 3 is [[Wendy O. Koopa]], who utilizes her ill-received [[Magic Wand]] to fire ricocheting rings that bounce off of the airship's room's wall with ease. Two stomps on Wendy's head will induce her to bounce across the room, the third stomp, will render her defeated.
