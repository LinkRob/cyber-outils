The '''Ice Bat''' is an enemy found in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
They are similar to the [[Bat (Super Mario Galaxy)|Bats]] but they're entirely made of ice.

=== Behavior ===
They attack by swooping down on [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) and freezing them. Unlike the Bats, Ice Bats are immune to [[Star Spin]]s and are only drifted back by them. They are also dangerous to touch as they can freeze you if you do. The only way to defeat an Ice Bat is with a [[Fire Flower]].

== In ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ==
Ice Bats are commonly found in the [[Freezeflame Galaxy]] in the Summit section.
