|locked      ={{Y}} }}'''Standard DS''' is one of [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]'s three standard [[List of Karts in Mario Kart DS|karts]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. It has average speed and acceleration and high drift. But it also has poor handling and [[Weight Class|medium weight]] and [[List of Items in the Mario Kart series|items]]. Daisy's other two standard karts are the [[Power Flower (Mario Kart DS)|Power Flower]] and the [[Light Dancer]].
