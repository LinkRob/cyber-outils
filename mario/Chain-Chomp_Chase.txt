'''Chain-Chomp Chase''' is a minigame in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place during [[World 3 (Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix)|World 3]].

==Gameplay==
As stated from the minigame's title, Chain-Chomp Case involves a [[Chain Chomp|Chain-Chomp]] chasing after [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]. The player has to alternately press ← and → on the [[Nintendo GameCube Action Pad]] for ten seconds so that Mario or Luigi can run fast enough to escape the Chain-Chomp. If the Chain-Chomp hits Mario or Luigi, he flies toward the screen, which shatters in a [[Breaking the fourth wall|fourth wall]] reference.
