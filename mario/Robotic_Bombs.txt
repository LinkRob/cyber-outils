'''Robotic Bombs''' are objects found in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. First encountered in [[Old Clockworks]], these speedy explosives will activate with a flash of the [[strobulb]] and come running at [[Luigi]]. They cause 20 [[HP]] of damage to [[Luigi]] if they detonate within close proximity to him. They also stun any nearby Ghosts. In some cases, Robotic Bombs are used to solve puzzles or blow up debris. [[Golden Robotic Bomb]]s are another variety of Robotic Bombs that also cause 20 HP of damage.
