The '''Mother Penguin'''<ref>{{cite manual |name=Super Mario 64 Player's Guide|page=38}}</ref> is a penguin who appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its remake ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. She lives on [[Cool, Cool Mountain]] with her daughter [[Tuxie]].

== Super Mario 64 ==
At Cool, Cool Mountain, Mother Penguin is worried about Tuxie as she went missing and can't remember where she left her. She requests [[Mario]]<nowiki/>to find her. Tuxie can be found on the top of the mountain, near the exact area where Mario starts. Mario must then take Tuxie to the bottom of the mountain where Mother Penguin is. In doing so, Mario earns a [[Power Star]] from her.
