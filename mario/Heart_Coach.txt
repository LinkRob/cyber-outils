'''Heart Coach''' is a Medium Class [[kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' as well as [[Princess Peach]]'s signature kart.

== Description ==
The Heart Coach has a burgundy, dark pink, and light yellow paint job. There are hearts embroidered on the kart. [[Princess Daisy]] has her own equivalent named [[Bloom Coach]], which bears a very similar appearance except it is toned orange with flowers.

== In ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'' ==
The Heart Coach is available from the start. It can be used by any middleweight racer, as well as a lightweight racer if they are paired with a middleweight racer.

The Heart Coach is rated with low speed, high acceleration and average weight. Its weight is tied with [[Mario]]'s [[Red Fire]] and [[Diddy Kong]]'s [[Barrel Train]].

However, despite its rating, the Heart Coach has the worst acceleration out of all four star acceleration karts in-game. Although, it provides the best top speed out of the two star speed karts in-game.

The Heart Coach is used by [[Staff Ghost]]s in [[Time Trial]]s with Princess Peach and Princess Daisy, on the courses [[Peach Beach]] and [[Daisy Cruiser]].
