'''Michael James Vaughn''' (born January 2, 1973) is an American voice actor. He provided the voice for [[Iggy Koopa]] and [[Ludwig von Koopa]] in the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]] series and the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros]]. series. He was replaced by [[Ryan Higgins]] (Iggy) and [[David J. Goldfarb|David Goldfarb]] (Ludwig) in [[Mario Kart 8]].

== Voices ==

* [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]] (2009-2020)
* [[Ludwig von Koopa|Ludwig]] (2009-2020)
