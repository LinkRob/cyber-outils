==History==
The Orange grenade launcher is the strongest weapon in the game as it fires orange grenades. However, this is only present in Multiplayer mode as Krusha is not a main playable character. The Orange grenade launcher is powerful but Krusha must be careful not to hurt himself with one of his own explosions.
