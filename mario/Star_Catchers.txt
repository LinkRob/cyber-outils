'''Star Catchers''' is a Battle minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Layout==
The characters are sitting on a brick chimney of a house during nighttime. The top screen shows the characters whereas the Touch Screen has a fixed view on the night sky, appearing to take place during sunset.

==Gameplay==
Throughout the minigame, star twinkles appear in the sky. The objective for each player is to "claim" the most stars within the 30-second [[Time Limit]]. To claim a star, they must tap one using the [[stylus]] as soon as it appears onscreen before their opponents do. Whenever a character claims a star, they can be seen performing a happy jump on the top screen. Whoever claims to most stars by the end is the winner. The stars claimed by the first player are blue, the second player's are red, the third player's are green, and the fourth player's are yellow.

==Ending==
The star twinkles dim out as they are countered on the top screen. The winner's claimed stars then reappear on the Touch Screen, during which any of the winners perform their victory animation while the losing characters perform their losing animation.
