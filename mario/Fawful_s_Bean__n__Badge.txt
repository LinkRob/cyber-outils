'''Fawful's Bean 'n' Badge''' is an item shop run by [[Fawful]] in [[Princess Peach's Castle Dungeon]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. The store sells a couple of different [[badge]]s, and Fawful takes [[bean]]s for currency instead of [[coin]]s, as implied by the store's title.
