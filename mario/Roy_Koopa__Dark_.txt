'''Roy Koopa (Dark)''' is the dark copy of [[Roy Koopa]], appearing as an enemy and a boss in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' and ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Dark Roy is identical to the real Roy, but his entire body and shades have a dark shade applied to it.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Dark Roy has all the abilities that the real Roy can use.  He fights as a Melee-unit type trooper and his [[Special Skill]] is Spin to Win.

== History ==
=== ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'' ===
Dark Roy first appears in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]''. He replaces the real Roy once he has been recruited in [[Return of the Koopa Cruiser]] and when the real Roy is battled again on [[Goomba's Worth]].

=== ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'' ===
Dark Roy returns in ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. He replaces the real Roy once he has been recruited in [[Better Together]] and is even summoned by the [[Best Fitness Friends (merged form)|Best Fitness Friends merged monster form]], alongside the other dark Koopalings in [[Like Father, Like Son]]. He is fought once again in [[Beat the Minions!]], where he fights as the captain and a troop in rounds 1 and 5 respectively, whilst under the effects of [[Malatone Formula:X]] in both rounds. Dark Roy does not have another dark Koopaling as his [[First Officer]] in round 1, he is the only dark Koopaling that fights alone (not counting the fifth round where he fights alongside the dark copies of the Koopalings and [[Bowser]]).
