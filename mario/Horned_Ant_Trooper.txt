'''Horned Ant Troopers''' is an enemy which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and also appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are a variant of [[Ant Trooper]] that wear spiked orange helmets.

==In ''Super Mario 3D World''==
They first appear [[Ant Trooper Hill]] during a section where [[Mario]] or another playable character can use a [[Super Star]] to blast through several of them. Their spiked helmets make them invincible to aerial attacks like [[Ground Pound]]s or any power-up attacks, except for the Super Star or [[Lucky Cat Mario]].
