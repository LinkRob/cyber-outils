The '''Happy Cape''' is one of [[Geno]]'s personal armors in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the shop in [[Marrymore]] hotel for 38 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Happy Cape on Geno increases his [[Defense]] by 12 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 6.
