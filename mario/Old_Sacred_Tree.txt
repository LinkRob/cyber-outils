The '''Old Sacred Tree''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

== Description==
The Old Sacred Tree has the appearance of a regular oak tree.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
The Old Sacred Tree is found in the [[Sacred Forest]]. When [[Mario]] first approaches the Old Sacred Tree, the [[Red Rescue Squad Apprentice Toad]] pleads to it in hopes of joining the [[Rescue Squad]]. After he leaves, [[Huey]] finds a [[Paint Star Piece]] stuck in the tree's leaves. Mario can use his [[hammer]] or [[Paint Hammer]] to hit the Old Sacred Tree, causing the Paint Star Piece to fall out, as well as a [[Small Goomba Gang]] that [[First Strike]] him. After the battle, if ever Mario hits the Old Sacred Tree with his hammer, a [[Micro Goomba|Small Goomba]] falls out instead.
