Tsuguo Mogami (born January 18, 1980) is a Japanese voice actor. He is the voice of [[Spewart]] in [[Super Mario Odyssey|''Super Mario Odyssey'']]. He is also the voice of [[King K. Rool]] in [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']].
