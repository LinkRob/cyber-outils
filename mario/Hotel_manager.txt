==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Hotel manager is a white-mustached, pink [[Pianta]]. He is also the only Pianta who wears shoes.

===Personality===
In ''Super Mario Sunshine'', as misfortune continues to befall both Sirena Beach and Hotel Delfino, the hotel manager becomes increasingly concerned. As such, he is quick to enlist the help of [[Mario]] to sort out the current predicament on several occasions, though he is not always exactly forthcoming, sometimes requesting Mario's help in indirect or roundabout ways. Nevertheless, he is always appreciative of Mario's assistance, and he often makes a point of telling him so.

==History==
===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
The Hotel manager is first seen in Episode 1 of Sirena Beach, [[The Manta Storm]], where he pleas to Mario to take down the [[Phantamanta|phantom manta ray]] that caused Hotel Delfino to sink in the [[electric goop]] and trapped hotel guests. Mario does so and the Hotel manager awards Mario with a [[Shine Sprite]].

During the next set of Episodes from 2 to 5, the Hotel manager asks Mario to help clear out the [[Boo]]s from the hotel. Mario eventually finds the source of the Boos, [[King Boo (Super Mario Sunshine)|King Boo]], down in the basement of Hotel Delfino and defeats him.

Finally during Episode 6, [[Scrubbing Sirena Beach]], the Hotel manager is in a panic that the electric goop suddenly appear again and asks Mario to clean up the entire beach in [[Time limit|time]]. After Mario accomplishes this task, the Hotel manager awards him with another Shine Sprite.

For the next Episodes, the Hotel manager allows Mario to enter Hotel Delfino anytime.
