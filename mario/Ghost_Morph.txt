'''Ghost Morph''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
Ghost Morph is a [[Special Item (Mario Kart series)|special item]] used by [[Blinky]]. Upon being used, the Ghost Morph turns him and his kart both light blue and translucent temporarily. While Blinky is under the effects of Ghost Morph, he can pass through rivals.
