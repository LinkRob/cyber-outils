}}A '''Copta''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Coptas are stronger versions of [[Choppa]]s that are seen in [[Castle Bleck]] and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Like with Choppas, Coptas can flip in and out of dimensions and attacks by flying toward one of the heroes when they are spotted. Using the [[Pixl]] [[Fleep]] is the most effective way to take out Coptas.
