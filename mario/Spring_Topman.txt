The '''Spring Topman''' is a type of [[Topman]], which are enemies that starred in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''; as such, this enemy is a part of the Topman tribe. It is first seen in the [[Buoy Base Galaxy]] after Mario disables the [[Water Shooter]] system by spinning on a wheel that he finds near the top of the tower. 

==Description and Behaviour==
As their name implies, these Topmans are green, with four yellow chevron arrows around their body. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] can use his [[Star Spin]] to pop their heads off, although a spring connects the head to the body. In this state, the head will flash red, and Mario can jump off the head to bounce higher than his [[Backward Somersault]] can take him. However, bouncing off the Spring Topman will cause Mario to spin and fall slowly, akin to spinning out of a [[Tornado]] or [[Tweester]], or jumping off a [[Sentry Garage]] or [[Spring Robot]]. Mario can escape this state by performing a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] or using his Star Spin again, aside from landing on the ground or in [[Water]]. Additionally, if he shoves the Spring Topman into an [[Electric rail|electric fence]] with a star spin, Mario can destroy it, releasing a [[Coin]].
