A '''Tail Wheel''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
A Tail Wheel is a wooden wheel placed on top of some [[platform]]s. It can only be activated by [[Tanooki Mario]] spinning his tail at it, causing the Tail Wheel to rotate and the platform to ascend. This is required to reach certain areas that are otherwise inaccessible.

Tail Wheels received a successor in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', titled [[Cat Wheel]]s, which instead incorporate usage of the [[Cat Mario|Cat]] power-up, matching the game's cat theme.
