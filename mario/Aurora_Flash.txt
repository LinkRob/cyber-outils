'''Aurora Flash''' is a special attack only used by certain bosses in the game, ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
Aurora Flash can attack all characters on the screen and even puts them to [[sleep]] as a side effect. This move is first seen used by [[Belome]] during the second fight with him. Wearing a Wake-up Pin is a good defence against it.
