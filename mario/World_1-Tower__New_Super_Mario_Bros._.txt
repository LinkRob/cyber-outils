'''World 1-Tower''' is a tower level from the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (DS)|New Super Mario Bros]]., ''with gray walls, and ominous music playing in the background.

==Walkthrough==
At the very start, there is a [[Dry Bones]] and a platform that rises up and down. Right after this, five pairs of walls will crush [[Mario]] if he goes between them as they are closing. On top of the fifth pair, there is another [[Dry Bones]]. There are also numerous [[coin]]s in the area. After this, several walls go up and down one by one, so [[Mario]] has to run below them. Then there is another [[Dry Bones]], and the first [[Star Coin]] in the level. There are even more walls after this, but this time [[Mario]] has to jump on top of them to proceed. After this, [[Mario]] can enter a room with shifting walls and the second [[Star Coin|star coin]]. There is a secret exit here, which can only be reached if [[Mario]] is in [[Shell Mario]] mode. In the room, there is a gap that never closes. [[Mario]] has to enter it and jump upwards, where he will find a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. He will then have to jump onto five more [[Brick Block|blocks]] and start using the Shell Dash. Then, after destroying all the [[Brick Block|blocks]], [[Mario]] has to enter the [[Warp Pipe]]. Then, he will fall to the right after exiting. [[Mario]] must jump onto a [[Brick Block|block]] to obtain another [[1-Up Mushroom|1-Up]]. Then, he will unlock the [[Cannon]], which will take him to [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]]. Then there is the third [[Star Coin]], which can be reached by jumping on top of the walls and avoiding the [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]]. However, to get back to the main level, [[Mario]] needs to avoid being crushed by the walls in the next room. If [[Mario]] chooses to go the normal way, he will either rocket up the tower with a [[Warp Pipe|warp pipe]] or [[Wall Jump|wall jump]] up it. After this, there is a [[Warp Pipe|warp pipe]] which will shoot [[Mario]] up the tower, but this requires shooting at the right time as he can be crushed by the walls here. There is also a [[? Block]] containing a power-up here as well as two [[Super Dry Bones]] closed off, the red door leading to the [[Bowser Jr.]] battle is also here.

The first [[Bowser Jr.]] battle of the game is incredibly easy, [[Bowser Jr.]] will run around the room of the tower trying to run into you. To defeat him, you must stomp on his head, after two hits, Bowser Jr.'s speed will increase. Once he is hit three times, he will be defeated, and he will drag [[Princess Peach]] to [[World 1-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-Castle]].

==Red-flag exit==
In order to reach the [[Red Flagpole|red-flag exit]], Mario will have to be [[Shell Mario]]. In the room with the second Star Coin and shifting blocks, the player can find a gap that never closes on the right side of the room. The player has to enter the gap and jump upwards to find a 1-Up Mushroom, then the player has to jump onto five more blocks. The player has to start the [[shell dash]]. After destroying all the blocks, the player has to enter the red pipe. Next, the player will fall to the right after exiting. The player has to jump onto a block to grab another 1-Up while unlocking the [[Warp Cannon]] which will shoot the player all the way to [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]].

==[[Star Coins]]==
*'''Star Coin 1''': After the part with many moving platforms, there is a large moving platform with a Dry Bones on it, a blue Brick Block and some coins, as well as the first Star Coin. It is easy to grab.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Shortly after the Halfway Point, there is a green Cannon Pipe that blasts Mario or Luigi up. Upon landing, there is a door, in which the player can perform a few Wall Jumps to reach and enter it. Then, the player will be brought to a room with many moving platforms, and the second Star Coin is in the room. Simply jump to it to obtain it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': After getting the second Star Coin, the player is brought out shortly before the Halfway Point. Jump up to some moving platforms and jump every time the platforms on the right move. After that, the third Star Coin is on some blue Snake Blocks.
