'''Big Bubbles'''<ref>Nintendo ''[[Mario Mania]]'' Player's Guide. Page 52.</ref> (also called '''Green Bubbles'''<ref>Nintendo ''Mario Mania'' Player's Guide. Page 82.</ref> or just '''Bubbles'''<ref>''[[Nintendo Power]]'' Volume 28, p. 16</ref>) are obstacles in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. They are huge green bubbles found in two [[level]]s: [[Vanilla Ghost House]] and [[Valley Ghost House]]. They drift in the air, hurting [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] if [[jump]]ed on or touched. Using a [[Spin Jump]], however, allows the player to bounce off them safely, and a [[slide]] or [[Cape Mario|Body Press]] can actually destroy them.
