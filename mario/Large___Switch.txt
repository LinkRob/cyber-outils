'''Large ! Switches''' are objects in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Description==
These large counterparts of [[! Switch]]es are usually orange and on a studded base similar to [[Bowser]]'s collar. It is in the final battle against [[Bowser]] in a few of the games, and activating it defeats Bowser and ends the boss fight. A regular ! Switch comes earlier than a Large ! Switch, though it functions like an [[axe]], causing Bowser to collapse before he re-emerges more powerful during the second part.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
A large ! Switch appears at the end of the second half of the battle against Bowser (as [[Giant Bowser|Super Bowser]]) in {{world-link|8|bowser|World 8-Bowser's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)}}. It is in the area where [[Princess Peach]] is held. Pressing it causes the [[lava]] to drain from the corridor and Super Bowser to disappear with it, ending the battle.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''===
A large ! Switch appears during the fight against Super Bowser in {{world-link|6|bowsern2|World 6-Bowser's Castle}}. It is at the top of a vertical corridor, and activating it causes the floor beneath Super Bowser to collapse, defeating him. A large ! Switch appears at the end of the battle against [[Dry Bowser]] in {{world-link|star|castlen2|World Star-Castle}} and works in the same manner.

===''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''===
A large ! Switch is physically integrated in [[Castle Bowser]], under the chin of his [[Wonder Bowser]] form. It has a much different function than in previous appearances.

During the level [[The Final Battle! Bowser's Rage Stage]], striking the large ! Switch is necessary to damage Wonder Bowser. After five hits, the armor falls off with the Large ! Switch. Defeating Wonder Bowser outright requires activating the [[Bowser Switch]] on top of him instead.
