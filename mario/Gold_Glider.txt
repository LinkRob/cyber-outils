The '''Gold Glider''' is an [[glider]] that debuted in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. It reappeared in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart 7'' ==
The Gold Glider is an unlockable [[glider]] in ''Mario Kart 7''. It can be unlocked by connecting with 100 people using [[w:c:nintendo:StreetPass|StreetPass]], or by getting 10,000 [[coin]]s in the game. The Gold Glider's stats are set to zero.
