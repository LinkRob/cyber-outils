'''Bowser-Ball 'n' Chains''' are a type of [[Ball 'n' Chain]] modeled after [[Bowser]] found in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', they appear as primary obstacles found throughout [[Bowser's Villa]] alongside the [[Podoboo Cog]]s. Bowser-Ball 'n' Chains are seen attached to a block swinging into the background and foreground, if [[Peach]] hits a Bowser-Ball 'n' Chain when it's on the main path, she will take damage. They make brief stops when swinging to one end allowing [[Peach]] to cross safely, Bowser-Ball 'n' Chains are commonly seen in groups requiring some careful timing, there is not attack that can destroy a Bowser-Ball 'n' Chain, the only thing Peach can do is time her movements and avoid them altogether. Peach can jump on the blocks supporting a Bowser-Ball 'n' Chain to use it as a platform.

==Trivia==
*Bowser-Ball 'n' Chains get their name from their resemblance to [[Bowser's Shell]].
