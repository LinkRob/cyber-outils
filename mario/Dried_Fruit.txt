'''Dried Fruit''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dried Fruits are not found in any [[Item Shop|shops]] or other specific places. [[Pokey]]s are found to drop these after battle. A Dried Fruit can be found in the top right areas of [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]].

Dried Fruits restore Mario's health by 15 [[Heart Point|HP]]. Dried Fruits are used with a variety of items to create some [[recipe]]s. Using a few high-class mushrooms with this item can create a [[Volt Shroom]]. Volt Shrooms and this can make [[Thunder Rage]]s. [[Yummy Meal]] is the result of cooking this with a [[Dried Pasta]].
