"'''Plummers Academy'''" is the fortieth animated episode of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the live-action segment "[[Glasnuts]]." In this episode, Mario tells Princess Toadstool and Toad about the day that he and Luigi became plumbers.

==Plot==
[[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and his minions chase the [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] gang around a castle, and end up challenging the Bros. to a fight. Mario pulls out a special golden plunger (while [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] pulls out a golden wrench) which he throws at King Koopa's nose, though he retaliates by opening a trap door underneath the good guys and trapping them in a pit. More concerned with getting the plunger off of his nose, King Koopa and his troops leave the heroes alone in the pit. Unable to get out of the pit, Mario is saddened because he lost his special plunger, which was given to him by the [[Mr. President|President of the United States]]. [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] inquires why Mario received his plunger, so Mario tells the story in an extended flashback sequence.

Mario and [[Luigi]] were starting their first day of "basic draining" at the [[Brooklyn Plumbers Academy]] in [[Brooklyn]], and they were assigned to [[Sergeant Kooperman]], a grueling instructor who has the same voice as King Koopa (and a nose that coincidentally looks similar to [[Wario]]'s). Right off the bat, Kooperman takes a great dislike to the Bros., forcing them to perform labor-intensive assignments, like hauling bathtubs up a fire escape numerous times and digging large pits. On the verge of flunking, the Bros. nearly drown themselves trying to fix some pipes, so they quit the academy.

As they leave the academy, the Bros. notice a statue of the legendary plumber, [[Salvador Drainado]], with a plaque reading "When the plumbing gets tough, the tough get plumbing!", though they opt to become librarians. They then learn that the U.S. President is giving the President of Russia a drive-by tour of Brooklyn. Meanwhile, Kooperman is fixing a sink in a nearby apartment, and when he goes outside to get more drain cleaner, he mistakes a bag of concrete for it. Kooperman soon learns that it's "fast-drying" concrete, which soon causes water to rise from the sewer and flood the street that the President's limo is driving through.

Mario recalls points out that "When the plumbing gets tough, the tough get plumbing!", so he and Luigi don diving equipment and head down into the sewers. Mario uses a jackhammer to chisel through the cement clog, which stops the flooding. Later, the Bros. are treated to a ceremony at the [[White House]], where Mario is awarded his golden plunger, and Luigi his golden wrench, which ends the flashback.

Though Mario is still depressed, [[Princess Peach]] gives the Bros. some encouragement, which prompts Luigi into using his wrench to open a valve and flood the pit, allowing them to escape. Meanwhile, [[Mouser]] is still trying to get the plunger off of King Koopa's nose. The four heroes stuff [[Mouser]] in a closet, while Mario tries to get his plunger back from King Koopa (who's conveniently been tied down). With the Princess's help, Mario gets his gold plunger back, and the heroes leave King Koopa tied down (despite his saying "please"). Wanting to celebrate, Mario requests to have pasta.

==Trivia/Goofs==
[[File:WarioSMBSS.jpg|thumb|The Wario lookalike, the third character from the left]]
*This episode mildly spoofs ''{{w|Police Academy (film series)|Police Academy}}''.
*The two Presidents bear resemblances to [[George H. W. Bush]] and [[Mikhail Gorbachev]], who were the presidents of their respective countries at the time of the episode's airing.
*In some shots Mario's ''golden'' plunger is colored like a regular one.
*The flags on the limo are inconsistent. One of them appears to be the Japanese flag in one shot.
*Somehow, no water leaks out of the mess of pipes the Marios get caught in, despite the big, visible gaps that appear.
*Coincidentally, an overweight plumber with a yellow hat and yellow overalls appears in one of the scenes, somewhat resembling [[Wario]], who would not be introduced until 1992's ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.
