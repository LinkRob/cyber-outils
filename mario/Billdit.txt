'''Billdit''' is a character in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Billdit is a yellow skinned humanoid based off of a electrical outlet, Billdit wears a red aprin that looks like a design of a Hammer and has a white headband with a red "leaf" coming out of his head. He has patches on his low right hand face

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ==
=== Background ===
A woman named [[Chief Willma|Willma]] decided to have two kids, their names where Billdit and Maykit. Willma is a hard worker and is very determined to do everything she can. But when she gets older she cant do all the hard work she could do before. So, she did everything she could and taught Billdit and Maykit how to be just like her. Billdit loves the idea and goes out on adventures.

=== Meeting the Bros. ===
After meeting Willma, she starts to get worried about her kids because of the separation with the islands and is wondering if you could go find [[Maykit and Billdit|them]]. Before you find one of her kids you first have to pick which one you want to run the shop at Shipshape Island. If you chose Billdit, you will head to Raynforst Island. Once there you will meet Billdit who is suspicious of the Bros. after some puzzle solving and linking the Island, Billdit will come onto [[Shipshape Island]] and joins your adventure. After that he starts making equipment for you sucks as [[Hammer]]s, Gloves, and Accessories to help you in combat.
