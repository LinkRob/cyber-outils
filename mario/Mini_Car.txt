==History==
The Mini Car is first seen in [[Frantic Factory]] on his racetrack that only [[Tiny Kong]] can [[Mini-Monkey|enter]]. The Mini Car challenges Tiny to a race and if she wins while collecting the right amount of medals, then she will win a [[Golden Banana]]. The Mini Car later appears in [[Creepy Castle]] where it challenges Tiny to one last race which takes place in Frantic Factory again. After Tiny wins once more, the Mini Car sadly gives over its last Golden Banana.
