'''Koopa Park''' is a recurring golf course in the {{series|Mario Golf}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]] and returns in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''.

==Description==
Koopa Park appears to be located in the wooded lowlands of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], with many lakes and ponds in it.

The ''Mario Golf: World Tour'' version of Koopa Park features improved music and graphics, along with [[Koopa Troopa]] balloons. The evergreens from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' are replaced with more realistic-looking ones in different shades of green and yellow.

==In {{series|Mario Golf}}==
===''Mario Golf'' (Nintendo 64)===
Koopa Park is a golf course that requires 50 Course Points to unlock. Like all courses, it has a par of 72. It has many more trees, bunkers and water hazards than the previous course, [[Toad Highlands]].

===''Mario Golf: World Tour''===
Koopa Park reappears in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'' as additional downloadable content. It may be purchased by means of the Mushroom Pack, along with Toad Highlands and [[Toadette]].
