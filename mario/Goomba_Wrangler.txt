'''Goomba Wrangler''' is a 4-Player and Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Gameplay==
Players must collect [[Goomba]]s that wander on the field by drawing a complete circle around them with the [[stylus]]. However, they have to avoid collecting [[Bob-omb]]s, which take away half of the Goombas they have collected. Brown Goombas are worth one point, while [[Gold Goomba|golden Goombas]], which appear only during the last five seconds, are worth three points. Whoever ends with the most points after the 20 seconds are up wins.

==Ending==
The flowers on the dark patch in the center of the field bloom in the color that corresponds to the winning side.

==Trivia==
*Goomba Wrangler is very similar to the [[w:c:pokemon:Pokémon Ranger (series)|''Pokémon Ranger'' series]] of games, where players capture [[Pokémon]] by making circles around them with the stylus. The French name, Goomba Ranger, seems to reference to the series directly.
