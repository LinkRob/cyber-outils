|An odd splash screen in Simplified Chinese. The upper, larger text is the "Healthy gaming advice", a 28-character health and safety advice that is required to be displayed upon a game's startup by Chinese law. The lower half consists of the publishing house information and a placeholder ISBN with all zeroes (the ISBN will be replaced by the actual one given by the government upon approval). The publisher is the exact same one used by all games published by iQue.


Rough Translation: Healthy Gaming Advice Resist unhealthy games, reject pirated games Be aware of self-protection, avoid being deceived Moderate gaming is good for the brain, addiction to games hurts your body Manage time appropriately, enjoy a healthy life.
