The '''Bubble Berry''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
The Bubble Berry restores five of [[Mario]]'s [[Heart Point]]s. It can only be acquired by smacking a single tree in [[Flower Fields]] with the [[Hammer]]. This tree is located behind the yellow gate in Flower Fields, and can only be reached if Mario has activated the power of the [[Water Stone]] in [[Lily (Paper Mario)|Lily]]'s pond.

In addition to being a healing item, Bubble Berries can be given to the [[Bubble Plant]] located on the northwestern path of Flower Fields. This plant will blow a bubble for Mario which will reach him over the thorns to the [[Sun]]'s [[Sun Tower|tower]].
