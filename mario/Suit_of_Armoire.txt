Heh heh heh!"''|forms = "The Remote Control"|song = A Knight's Choice}}'''Suit of Armoire''' is [[Young Cricket and Master Mantis|Young Cricket]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The microgame is twice as long as the normal microgame time. The objective is to find one of the items in the drawer. If the player opens the correct drawer, the player wins. The player loses if they choose the wrong drawer or waste time.
* '''Level 1:''' There are two drawers.
* '''Level 2:''' There are three drawers.
* '''Level 3:''' Same as level 2, but other items attempt fake-outs.
