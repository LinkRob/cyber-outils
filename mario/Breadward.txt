'''Breadward'''<ref name="spm"/> is a fake [[pixl]] mentioned by [[Mimi]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Profile ==
Not much is known about Breadward, but he is made out of "oats and lard".<ref name="spm">{{cite video game| title = [[Super Paper Mario]]| developer = [[Intelligent Systems]]| publisher = [[Nintendo]]| date = 2007-04-19| platform = [[Wii]]| level = [[Merlee's Basement]]}}</ref>

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
In [[Merlee's Basement]] Mimi disguises as [[Merlee]]; she mentions Breadward
to bribe [[Mario]] and his party within [[Merlee's Mansion]]. In this part of the game, the player is not supposed to accept these offers.
