'''Flowers''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear as interactive objects in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Description==
Flowers often appear as part of the environment, but they can sometimes yield an item, such as a [[coin]]. In the 3D ''Super Mario'' titles, Flowers often appear together in clusters.
