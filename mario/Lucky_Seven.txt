The '''Lucky Seven''' is an item from ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. The item is a red '7'. As its name implies, the Lucky Seven gives the player seven items such as a [[Green Shell]], a [[Red Shell]], a [[Banana]], a [[Bob-omb]], a [[Star]], a [[Mushroom]], and a [[Blooper]]. The items spin around the player. It is also in the title of [[Mario Kart 7]]. The item returns in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Trivia ==
[[File:Lucky7.png|thumb|220x220px|right|[[Princess Peach|Peach]] using the Lucky Seven item.]]
*CPU players cannot get this item, unless a specific type of item in Battle Mode or VS is selected (e.g. Bananas, Red Shells, Mushrooms and Bob-ombs).
*The Lucky Seven is similar to the [[Crazy Eight]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', except the [[Coin]] is not added.
*Other players can use your items if they manage to carefully bump into them. This however only works with the [[Star]], [[Mushroom]], and [[Blooper]].
*In ''Mario Kart Tour'', this is the special item for [[Pauline]] and [[King Boo]].
