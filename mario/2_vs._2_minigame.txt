A '''2 vs. 2 Minigame''' or '''Team Minigame''' is a type of [[minigame]] that appears in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]]. They are initiated when two [[Scoreboard|player panels]] share the same color, which creates two teams of characters. A 2 vs. 2 minigame requires the characters to work together as a team. The team minigames sometimes require the team to work together, or else the minigame will not be able to be completed. For example, in the team minigame [[Dungeon Dash]], the characters will not be able to proceed in the dungeon unless they work together. Other minigames like [[Cake Factory]] require each player to do a specific task in order to complete the minigame. The winning characters of a team in a 2 vs. 2 mini-games will be rewarded the [[coin]]s.
