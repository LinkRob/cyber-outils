'''Shy Guy Bazaar''' (Arabic: خجول جاي بازار) is the final course in [[Mushroom Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. Hence the name, the course is in a bazaar around a [[Shy Guy]] town which is themed Arabian and takes place at nighttime. Houses are shaped like [[Mushroom]]s. Driving into a moving jar will result in Mushrooms, [[Banana]]s, [[Green Shell]]s, and [[Star]]s flying out.
