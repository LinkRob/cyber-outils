The '''Pole Vault''' is an event, only in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]''.

==Layout==
The mode takes place in the [[National Stadium]].

==Gameplay==
When starting the event, the player has to choose the height they want the bar set at, going from as low as 5.50m to as high as 6.30m, each in increments of 0.02m. The player must shake the [[Wii Remote]] and [[Nunchuk]] as fast as possible to build up speed as their character makes their way down the runway. When "Speed Locked" appears, the player must wait for their character to be on the blue/red track and swing the Wii Remote down fast. If the Wii Remote is swung too early or too late, the player receives a foul. The character then lowers their pole, and when it touches the ground, the player has to shake very hard once again to build up power.

After the player jumps, one of four results can occur. They fail if they either hit the bar or if the bar wiggles before falling when touched. If the player either clears the bar or it just wiggles if touched but does not fall, they pass.

The player has three tries to clear the bar. Each time the bar is successfully cleared, it gets a little higher for the next attempt. The goal is to clear the bar as many times as possible before failing three times. Once it is cleared, the player returns to the height selection window. Whoever gets the highest jump without knocking over the pole wins.

==Missions==
Two characters have a mission and specific objective:

*[[Waluigi]]'s second mission has the base objective of clearing the bar three times.
*[[Bowser]]'s first mission involves clearing the bar twice by shaking the Wii Remote and Nunchuk for recoil.

==Trivia==
*This is one of the two events where there is a maximum Olympic Record. The other is the [[High Jump (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games)|High Jump]].
