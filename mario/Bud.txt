'''Bud''' is a character in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. He is from the in-universe [[real world]], where he and his family originate from Wichita, [[Kansas]]. Bud and his family have only appeared in one episode, "[[Mush-Rumors]]," in which they accidentally end up in the [[Mushroom Earth]] when [[Norman]], who is Bud's father, drives into a [[Warp Zone]].
