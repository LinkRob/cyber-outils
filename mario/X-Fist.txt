==History==
The X-Fists only appear during the battle of [[Magnus von Grapple]] near the end of Chapter 2. Magnus always spawns two of these X-Fists to aid him in battle acting as separate foes. The X-Fists only have an HP of two making them very easy to take out. However, if they are not taken out, a single X-Fist can deal four damage to either [[Mario]] or his partner (which is rather high for that early in the game). Later in the game, there are stronger versions of an X-Fist known as [[X-Punch]]es used by the stronger [[Magnus von Grapple 2.0]].
