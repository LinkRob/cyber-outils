The '''Lyrics''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Lyrics can only be obtained after [[Mario]] agrees to find the composer living somewhere in the world to help out the [[Master Poet]] in [[Club 64]]. The Lyrics must be taken to [[Toad in the Desert]] who lives in [[Dry Dry Outpost]] who will then rewrite it into the [[Melody (Paper Mario)|Melody]] allowing Mario to take it back to the Master Poet.
