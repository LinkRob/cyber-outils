The '''Palace Guards''' are [[Toad's species (Illumination)|Toads]] the secure [[Princess Peach's Castle (Illumination)|Peach's castle]] in the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]]. 

== History ==
=== ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' ===
As [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] reach [[Princess Peach's Castle (Illumination)|Peach's Castle]], [[Yellow Toad and Blue Toad (Illumination)|two Toad guards]] outside the door refuse to let them in, Toad distracts them using his frying pan to let Mario sneak in guards inside the castle. Unfortunately for Mario, [[Palace Guard Ensign|the]] [[Green Toad and Purple Toad (Illumination)|ot]][[Palace Guard Corporal|her]] [[Palace Guard Captain|To]][[Palace Guard Sergeant|ad]] [[Palace Guard Liutenant|guards]] saw him and tried to arrest Mario for trespassing the castle, who was looking for the princess herself to talk to, but Peach however, recognized him as [[human]], and not a [[Bowser (Illumination)|monster]], falling too quickly in love with him, because chasing and arresting him was a mistake. Mario explained to them and the princess about the emergency: He and his brother, Luigi fell down this pipe in the sewers underneath Brooklyn, and now Luigi is lost in the Dark lands. Peach said that's it's only a matter of time until Bowser captured him, but she's already on her way to stop him, and she decided to train him in the Training Course, before she decided to take Mario with her or not.

After Peach finally decided to take Mario with her to the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]], the palace guards and the other Toads watched them leave.

A shrunken Bowser is sent to the dungeons as prisoner. In his cell, Bowser playing his piano and singing louder has caused a [[Unidentified Palace Dungeon Guard|Toad guard]] to quiet down as he shuts the door.

The palace guards, with Mario, Luigi, Peach, Toad and Yoshi investigated where the shooting star landed somewhere in the [[Mushroom Forest (Illumination)|Mushroom Forest]]. It turns out it was a Luma that fell from the night sky.
