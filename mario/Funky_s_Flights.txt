'''Funky's Flights''' is a location introduced in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==Description==
Funky's Flights is a flight service run by [[Funky Kong]], who leases his [[Jumbo Barrel]] to [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] to travel between worlds of [[Donkey Kong Island]], bypassing the conventional method of having to defeat a boss first before accessing another world.

In the ''[[Donkey Kong Country (television series)|Donkey Kong Country]]'' animated series, Funky's Flights is located near the beach and docks of [[Kongo Bongo Island]] and has a complex system built into that could raise the entire hut into the air and suspend it there. Besides serving as a flight service, Funky's Flights also makes deliveries for [[Bluster's Barrel Works]].

The interior of Funky's Flights is shown occasionally in the ''Donkey Kong Country'' television show. It was filled with surfer memorabilia, along with other objects like a couch, refrigerator, and paintings. Funky's Flights would later also take place indoors in the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country (Game Boy Advance)|Donkey Kong Country]]''.

== In {{franchise|Donkey Kong}} ==
=== ''Donkey Kong Country'' ===
[[File:Funky's Flights GBC.png|thumb|left|In the Game Boy Color remake]]
Funky's Flights is one of the supporting locations stationed in each of the six [[world]]s. It is where Funky leases his [[Jumbo Barrel]] to Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong, free of charge, so that they can travel to any previously visited worlds via the [[Donkey Kong Island]] map. However, the Jumbo Barrel cannot access any areas that the Kongs have not yet visited, as a way to prevent players from skipping worlds and levels.

In the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake, the location is titled "Funky's Fishing" on the world map, because its purpose is mainly to host a [[Funky's Fishing]] challenge, accessed from a [[Fishing Barrel]]. It is still possible to use the Jumbo Barrel at the location, but it is largely redundant because the player can instantly lease the Jumbo Barrel by choosing the "CALL FUNKY" option on the world map menu.

=== ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' / ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''===
{{main|Funky's Flights II}}
Funky reworked his business practice in time, now offering flight service around [[Crocodile Isle]] in his new [[Funky Barrel]]. Funky's new service was dubbed [[Funky's Flights II]], but with one catch: Funky would charge Diddy and [[Dixie Kong]] two [[Banana Coin]]s for the first use of the Funky Barrel at every location, although any additional use would be free.

=== ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]'' ===
Funky runs his flight service once more, but it is for flying to specific islands. DK and Diddy also have to collect a certain number of [[Oil Barrel]]s before they can use Funky's Airplane to go to an island.

The instruction booklet refers to the service as "'''Funky Flights'''."

==In other media==
===[[Donkey Kong Country (television series)|''Donkey Kong Country'' television series]]===
In the episode "[[Hunka Hunka Burnin' Bluster]]," [[Funky Kong|Funky]] wants to transform Funky's Flights into a restaurant.

== Crossover appearances ==
=== ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' ===
Funky's Flights makes a cameo appearance on the map for [[DK Island (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|DK Island]]. The [[Funky Kong]] [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]] battle appropriately takes place at where Funky's Flights is located on the [[Donkey Kong Island]] map. Once the Funky Kong spirit has been obtained, Funky Kong's Shack opens at the same location.
