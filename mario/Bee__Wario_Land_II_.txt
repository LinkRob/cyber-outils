A '''Bee''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land II]]''. They are oversized bees that attack [[Wario]] with their stinger, causing him to become [[Puffy Wario]]. Wario can defeat a Bee using one of his attacks, although he cannot stun and grab one. Bees cannot sting Wario while he is [[crouch]]ing, except for if he does this while [[jump]]ing.

Bees have their own relatives known as [[Beezley]]s which make an appearance in ''[[Wario Land 4]]'', although Bees themselves do not. The previous game, ''[[Wario Land 3]]'', features white jellyfish enemies that exhibit a similar behavior to Bees, [[Jellybob (Wario Land 3)|Jellybobs]].
