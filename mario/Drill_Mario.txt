'''Drill Mario''' is a power-up in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Description==
===''Super Mario Bros. Wonder / Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park''===
[[Mario]] or another character can become their Drill form by touching a [[Drill Mushroom]]. Drill Mario is capable of protecting himself from attacks coming from the above, drill into breakable objects with his drill, and burrow both underground and into the ceiling. As Drill Mario, Mario wears a Drill helmet and a grey version of his standard form's shirt and a red version of his standard form's dungaree.

The Drill Mario form is the direct successor to when he uses a [[Spin Drill]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

===''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''===
Drill Mario appears in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'' after obtaining a Drill Mushroom while fighting [[Bowser (Illumination)|Dry Bowser]] and [[Wonder Bowser Jr.]]. [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] uses this form to burrow through the ground and attack the Bowsers from various angles.
