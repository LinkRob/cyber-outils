'''Chance Shots''' are a type of move in the {{series|Mario Tennis}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]'' and make a reappearance in ''[[Mario Tennis: Ultra Smash]]'' and ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]''{{'}}s tennis game. They replace the [[Power Shot]]s from ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.
