'''''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games''''' is an Olympic Games title for the [[Wii]] and the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. It is the third installment of the ''Mario and Sonic'' series. Like the previous two installments, it is released for the Wii, but it is also the first in the series to be released for the [[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]]. The game has fifty new sports and twenty characters, though none are newcomers to the series. The game takes place in London, home of the [[wikipedia:2012 Summer Olympics|2012 Summer Olympics]].
