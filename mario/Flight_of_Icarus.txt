'''Flight of Icarus''' is a Boss [[Microgame]] from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'', being featured in the level Caveat Imptor, which [[Ashley (WarioWare)|Ashley]] hosts. It uses the Scales form for its gameplay.

== Layout ==
The Microgame's protagonist has to fly through the canyon to get to the goal. The canyon is littered with various winged monsters and enemies that he needs to avoid. The goal lies at the end.

== Gameplay ==
The player must move their arms so the protagonist can dodge the obstacles and enemies and get to the canyon. If the protagonist hits an obstacle or gets hit by an enemy or an enemy attack, he takes damage; if he is dropped to 0 health, the player instantly loses. If the player lifts their right arm, the protagonist moves left, and vicī versā. When the player flaps both their arms simultaneously, the protagonist flaps his wings, ascending. Contact with the water below means an automatic failure; in multiplayer, if one player falls in, the survivor gets [[Hardlock|hardlocked]] until the round ends.

Level 1 features a short course with few obstacles. Level 2 features a medium-length course with the goal at the top of a vertical segment and more obstacles; during the vertical segment, two winged monsters carry a thorny net to hit the protagonist from below. Level 3 has a long course with the vertical segment in the middle and many more obstacles.
