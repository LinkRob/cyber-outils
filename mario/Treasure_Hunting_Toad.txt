The '''Treasure Hunting Toad''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
The Treasure Hunting Toad is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with blue stripes on his head.

==History==
The Treasure Hunting Toad can be found in [[Moleville Mines]] of [[Moleville]] during the first visit. Later, after [[Punchinello]] is defeated, the Treasure Hunting Toad can now be found in the [[Item Shop]] of Moleville where he will sell his findings to [[Mario]].
