The '''Fishing Hut''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is accessible through a door by the [[Frozen Lake]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
[[Luigi]] only needs to enter the fishing hut once. In mission D-1: [[Cold Case]], he must find a [[Toad (character)|Toad]] painting inside the hut. To do this, shine the [[Dark-Light Device]] on the back wall and point it up. Luigi can return to the Fishing Hut in mission D-3: [[Across the Chasm]] to find a [[Gem]]. Use the [[Poltergust 5000 (object)|Poltergust 5000]] on the snowmobile in the left corner to get the Gem.
