A '''King Bomb''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. King Bombs are the strongest variant of a [[Bob-omb]] that can be encountered in battle, and they are only appear during the battle with [[Magikoopa]] when he summons them.

==Battle==
King Bombs, being Bob-ombs, mainly attack by exploding at one of [[Mario]]'s party. One move in particular they can use called [[Big Bang]] deals massive damage.

===Strategy===
King Bombs are weak to both Jump and Fire attacks meaning any of Mario's special moves will be effective against one. It is highly recommended to take out a King Bomb before it has a chance to use it's Big Bang move.
