The '''Volcano Vase''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Originally, the Volcano Base was inside of a treasure chest that was hidden deep within [[Mt. Lavalava]] and [[Kolorado]] wants it badly. However, the Volcano Base has been pushed out of the erupting volcano and landed somewhere on [[Lavalava Island]]. If Mario heads into the area where he first met [[Sushie]] (when she joined his party), Mario can get the Volcano Vase from inside the chest. Mario can then give the Volcano Vase to Kolorado who, in turn, gives Mario the fourth [[Magical Seed]] which is required to get into [[Flower Fields]].
