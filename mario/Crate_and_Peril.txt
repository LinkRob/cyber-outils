'''Crate and Peril''' is a 1-vs-3 minigame which first appears in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and reappears in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.

== Opening ==
The solo character chases the other three, whom they manage shrink and suck into a box. The camera points inside the box, then the minigame starts.

== Gameplay ==
The solo character has to tilt the box to move around two [[Spiny Shell]]s and try to hit each of the three teammates with them. Whenever a character is hit by a Spiny Shell, they are eliminated from the minigame. If the solo character hits all three teammates, they win, but if the group characters manage to avoid the Spiny Shells for 30 seconds, they win.

== Ending ==
In ''Mario Party 6'', if the team wins, the remaining characters each perform their victory animation, remaining inside the box. If the solo character wins, they perform their victory animation. 

In ''Mario Party: The Top 100'', like the other minigames, an ending scene does not appear.
