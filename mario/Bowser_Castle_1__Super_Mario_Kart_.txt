'''Bowser Castle 1''' is the first Bowser's Castle track of ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' as an [[Extra Cup|Extra Track]].

== In ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'' ==
Bowser Castle 1 is the fourth track of the [[Mushroom Cup]], but in this game, there are five tracks in a cup. It brought forth the traditions of Bowser's Castle's [[lava]] pits, [[Thwomp]]s, and 90 degree turns. There are many [[Jump Pad|jump pads]] and [[Boost Pad|boost pads]] in this track.
