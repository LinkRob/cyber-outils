}}'''Locked Out''' is a Battle mini-game from ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player must find a key to correspond with the doors. There is always one player who is locked out if all doors have been opened. The players can punch and jump for the key, but multiple players can be eliminated at once.

== Controls ==
*{{button|n64|a}} - Grab key
*{{button|n64|b}} - Attack
