'''Seizer''' is the fifth and ninth boss in the Game B mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] whom [[Toad (character)|Toad]] faces against. Seizer is a skeletal {{w|Grim Reaper}}-like character who wears a green robe a carries around a book with him. Seizer can make two full rows of enemies fall all at once in this game.
