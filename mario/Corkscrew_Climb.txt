'''Corkscrew Climb''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Gameplay==
At the start, the minigame selects a random character to be at the front of the line, then the gameplay starts. The characters run around a spiraling building automatically, and the only have to [[jump]] over any [[Amp]]s along the way. If a character touches an Amp, they fall off and quickly run to the back of the line.

When the characters reach the [[finish line]] at the end, the rankings are determined by which order the characters crossed it, so whoever crosses the finish line first wins, with the others following in second, third, and last place respectively.

==Controls==
*{{button|3DS|A}}: Jump

==Trivia==
*Corkscrew Climb, [[Acornucopia]], and [[Piece of Cake]] are the only three Free-for-All minigames that are available from the start.
