|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Flame''' is a [[Fire (element)|Fire]]-type move used certain fire enemies in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Flame is used, a fireball flies over the user for a few seconds then lands over at its target.

==Effects==
Flame can inflict moderate damage to a single member of [[Mario]]'s party. This move is considered a stronger version of [[Drain]].
