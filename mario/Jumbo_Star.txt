The '''Jumbo Star''' is a giant [[Power Star]] obtained by [[Mario]] at the end of ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' after defeating [[Bowser]] in [[Bowser in the Sky]]. Although it counts as a Power Star, collecting the Jumbo Star does not add to the total number of Power Stars collected.
