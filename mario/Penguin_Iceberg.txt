'''Penguin Iceberg''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''.

==Description==
Penguin Iceberg prominently features [[Penguin]]s, who can both be seen on the sidelines as spectators and as line judges. Penguin Iceberg takes place on an ice platform in an arctic sea, between some glaciers. The playing field features a frosty tile pattern design underneath the ice, and everything surrounding the court is covered in snow, including the trees and a few small houses. There is also a [[Snowman]] dressed like [[Mario]], mainly noticeable from the mustache and its dark red bucket with Mario's signature "M" symbol. The Snowman also wears a dark red scarf.
