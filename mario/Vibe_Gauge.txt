The '''Vibe Gauge''' is a game mechanic in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. The Vibe Gauge indicates the duration that [[Princess Peach]] can use the vibe.

== Description ==
[[File:Fury Volcano.PNG|thumb|left|The Vibe Gauge on the top-left of the screen.]]
The Vibe Gauge is a meter on the top-left corner of the screen. It indicates the limit of [[Vibe]] usage. Until the Vibe Gauge is empty, Peach can no longer use any of the Vibes. The player can control Peach to pick up the enemies with [[Perry]] and crouch to help Perry to absorb the enemy. This will fill up the Vibe Gauge.

Peach can go to the shop to buy the [[Vibe Tea]]. The Vibe Tea permanently increases her Vibe Gauge. Each time Peach buys the Vibe Tea, the price increases.
