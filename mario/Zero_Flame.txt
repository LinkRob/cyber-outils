The '''Zero Flame''' is an ability of [[Bowser]]'s in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==In ''Mario Party DS''==
Bowser uses Zero Flame on [[Bowser's Pinball Machine]]. If a player is hit by the Zero Flame, their [[Star (Mario Party series)|stars]] and [[coin]]s are reduced to zero. They lose everything (except items). One can only be hit by the Zero Flame if they land onto one of the three [[Event Space|Green Spaces]] by the plunger. The plunger is most likely to launch the character into the [[Bowser Zone]], but there is a one out of ten chance that it instead launches them into the [[Star Zone]]. All of the Green Spaces in the Bowser Zone activate the Zero Flame.

If the character has a [[Double Dice Set|Double]]/[[Triple Dice Set]], [[Warp Dice Block]], or the [[Star Pipe]], they can easily escape the Bowser Zone and avoid the Zero Flame.

==Trivia==
*A large number "0" (in purple) appears over a character's head when the Zero Flame hits the player.
