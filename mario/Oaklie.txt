'''Oaklie''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He lives in [[Forever Forest]] where he guards the entrance deeper into the maze-like forest.

==Physical description==
Oaklie is a rather unique-looking character that has tan hair and a tan beard. He also has yellow eyes that can be seen from his dark face.

==History==
Oaklie is first seen where he stopped [[Mario]] from venturing any further into Forever Forest. However, Mario says that he was invited to [[Boo's Mansion]] by [[Bootler]] and Oaklie lets him pass. Oaklie then offers one last advice to Mario saying the key to finding the way to Boo's Mansion is to take every path that has different surroundings than the others. Oaklie then leaves and is not seen again until he is visited in the third area in a clearing where he still wishes Mario the best on his adventure.
