'''Cream''' is a food and key item featured in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Cream has to be used as a topping for the [[cake]] that [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] must make for [[Gourmet Guy]] after the events of Chapter 4. It must be added on right after baking the cake and before adding a [[Strawberry]] as the final topping. Doing this correctly will please Gourmet Guy and he will tell Princess Peach and Twink information of the next [[Star Spirits|Star Spirit]].

==In-game description==
''Very rich and and fresh cream.''
