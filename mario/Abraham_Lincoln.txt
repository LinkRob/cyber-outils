'''Abraham Lincoln''' was the sixteenth president of the [[United States of America]] in the real world. He made an appearance in the PC and Super Nintendo releases of ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''. In the game, [[Mario]] had to help Lincoln by recovering his [[Spectacles]], which [[Bowser]] had stolen, preventing Lincoln from being able to issue the Emancipation Proclamation.
