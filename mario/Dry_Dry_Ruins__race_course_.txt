'''Dry Dry Ruins''' is a desert-like area and the first course of the [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. It could be a track associated with [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] due to it having three Yoshi sphinxes. Since the 2023 [[Ninja Tour (2023)|Ninja Tour]], the course has also made an appearance in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

==Layout==
There are [[Pokey]]s here scattered across the track. There is also a lot of sand here that slow racers down. Some pillars here fall from time to time, which racers can do aerial tricks on them. You go inside a ruins site. There are bats all over and Pokey hieroglyphs on the walls. Inside, the music changes, and there are some halfpipes. There is a giant bowl with two Pokeys in it. Sand slowly falls and fills it up, forcing racers to take a longer route in the third lap. This place resembles [[Dry Dry Desert (course)|Dry Dry Desert]] and [[Desert Hills]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' as all three are desert courses.

==Shortcuts==
For the first and second laps, use two [[Dash Mushroom]]s. For the final lap, use one. By the third Lap, there is a column that has fallen over towards the first set of [[Pokey]]s. You need to avoid the bridge and use the mushroom there.
