'''World 4-2''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the second level of World 4, the [[Chai Kingdom]].

==Overview==
World 4-2 is another coastal-themed level where the first area has a bunch of [[Bombshell Koopa]]s around. This level also contains plant-like enemies called [[Pompon Flower]]s that attack by spitting up poisonous pollen at [[Mario]]. They are also unique to this level. There are also [[Nyololin]]s who will try to shoot down Mario with fireballs. Mario must carefully avoid the enemies and falling into the water to complete this level.
