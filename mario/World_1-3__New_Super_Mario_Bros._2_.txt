''''World 1-3 '''is the third [[level]] of [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It is also the level that introduces the [[Super Leaf]]. This level takes place in the overworld, and has multiple trees you can get on by using the [[Super Leaf]]. This level also contains some [[Snake Block]]s, [[Goomba]]s, and [[Koopa Troopa]]s.

== Walkthrough ==
{{sectstub}}

== Star Coins ==
* '''Star Coin 1: '''Once you reach the first runway, run down it with a [[Super Leaf]], then start flying upward. Follow the [[coin]]s, then get on top of the tree. There should be the [[Star Coin]] on the tree.
* '''Star Coin 2: '''After the first [[Star Coin]]'s tree, fly to the next tree, then go down the [[Warp Pipe]]. This [[Warp Pipe]] will launch you to a bonus area. Use the runway in this bonus area to fly using your [[Super Leaf]] to get the [[Star Coin]].
'''Star Coin 3: '''After exiting the bonus area from the second [[Star Coin]], go down, following the [[coin]]s. Then go a little bit left, and break the [[Brick Block|brick]] wall using your [[Super Leaf]] to get this [[Star Coin]].
