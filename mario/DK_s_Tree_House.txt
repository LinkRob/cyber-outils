'''DK's Tree House'''<ref>''Donkey Kong 64'' instruction booklet, page 33.</ref> is a recurring location in the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}. It first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==Description==
DK's Tree House is a wooden hut positioned on a tree within [[Kongo Jungle]] or other interpretations of the location, like [[Jungle (Donkey Kong Country Returns)|Jungle]] in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''. It is where both [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] live.

==In {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}==
==={{series|Donkey Kong Country}}===
====''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''====
[[File:Donkey Kong's Treehouse - Donkey Kong Country.png|thumb|left|Donkey Kong inside of his Tree House in ''Donkey Kong Country'']]
DK's Tree House is the starting point of the game. The house is cozy, with a picture of sailing boats on the wall, a window partially covered by blue curtains, and a vase of flowers on a table. It contains a large amount of [[banana]]s strewn about, an [[Extra Life Balloon]] that can be reached by jumping on a [[Tire]] and a picture of [[Candy Kong]] can also be seen hanging on a wall. DK's Tree House is designed after a regular cabin pattern, being constructed of wooden planks in its entirety.

In the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] and [[Game Boy Color]] versions of ''Donkey Kong Country'', the [[Kong]]s can only enter the tree house once each time they play the first level, [[Jungle Hijinxs (Donkey Kong Country)|Jungle Hijinxs]]. This is changed in the [[Game Boy Advance]] version, where the tree house is redesigned, and has a photograph inside it. Additionally, in that port of the game, after defeating [[Really Gnawty]], when Donkey Kong and [[Diddy Kong]] enter DK's Tree House, they will find [[Very Gnawty]] inside. The beaver then turns around, shocked at the sight of DK, and runs past him out the door.

====''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' / ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]''====
[[File:DK Tree House interior DKCR.png|thumb|250px|Donkey Kong playing a [[Nintendo DS Lite]] inside the tree house in ''Donkey Kong Country Returns''.]]
As in the previous games, it serves as the starting point. Upon starting the [[Jungle Hijinxs (Donkey Kong Country Returns)|first level]], the Kongs must repeatedly punch the Kalimba [[tiki]] until it is knocked out of the house (as it earlier tried to hypnotize Donkey Kong, to no avail); this occurs every time the level is re-played from the beginning.

Starting with this game, the tree house has a brand new design. Its walls are now built of bamboo stems, with the roof being round, thatched and sustained by wooden girnders. In spite of all these traits, DK's Tree House still slightly maintains the furniture from ''Donkey Kong 64'', such as the boombox, hammock, and several barrels. However, it also has two of Donkey Kong's ties hanging from the ceiling, two picture frames, and a television which shows the game's title screen (as well as playing the respective music) if activated by [[Ground Pound|ground pounding]] (in ''Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D'', what appears to be the Japanese version's title screen is shown instead). As in ''Donkey Kong Country'', once the Kongs enter the tree house, they can find and collect an [[Extra Life Balloon]] inside, once for the entire game.

====''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]''====
The treehouse makes a background appearance in the start of [[Homecoming Hijinxs]]. It is also where [[Donkey Kong]] has his birthday, during the opening cutscene. When a strong [[wind]] is caused by the [[Snowmad]]s, he, [[Diddy Kong]], [[Dixie Kong]], and [[Cranky Kong]] leave the treehouse, only to be blown away.

===''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''===
[[File:DKT64.png|thumb|left|200px|Donkey Kong inside his Tree House in ''Donkey Kong 64''.]]
DK's Tree House is located within Kong Isle from [[DK Isles]]. It has many differences compared to the one in ''Donkey Kong Country''. Instead of a bed, this tree house has a hammock. It also has much memorabilia in it, such as [[Cranky Kong]]'s phonograph from ''Donkey Kong Country'' and a red [[girder]] from the original ''[[Donkey Kong (game)|Donkey Kong]]'' arcade game. The tree house also has several [[barrel]]s and [[crate]]s in it, along with old banana peels.

It initially appears during the story intro where Donkey Kong is exercising to the [[DK Rap]] theme on a [[boombox]] until [[Squawks]] arrives to alert Donkey Kong of [[King K. Rool]]. It is the first location where any playable character can be controlled, as the player must move Donkey Kong outside the tree house.

Early screenshots of the game showed that there was going to be a shower stall in DK's Tree House with a poster of [[Banjo]] and [[Kazooie]] on it. The shower and posters were removed in the final version of the game.

==={{series|Mario vs. Donkey Kong}}===
====''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''====
DK's Tree House appears briefly in the prologue. Donkey Kong is relaxing inside it while channel-surfing on his TV, during which stumbles upon a commercial with [[Mini Mario (Mario vs. Donkey Kong)|Mini Marios]].

====''[[Mini Mario & Friends: amiibo Challenge]]''====
DK's Tree House in its ''Donkey Kong Country Returns'' design is a background prop for the levels where [[Mini Donkey Kong]] is playable.

==In {{franchise|Mario}}==
==={{series|Mario Party}}===
====''[[Mario Party 7]]''====
[[File:Party at DK's House MP7.png|thumb|The playable characters and Donkey Kong in front of the tree house in the ending of the ''Mario Party 7'' minigame [[Peel Out]].]]
DK's Tree House appears in the background of two [[Donkey Kong|DK]] [[minigame]]s, [[Jump, Man]] and [[Vine Country]], when players reach the top of the jungle. It looks similar to its appearance in ''Donkey Kong 64''.

====''[[Mario Party 10]]''====
A miniature-sized DK's Tree House is among the decorative elements on the [[Donkey Kong Board]].

==={{series|Mario Kart}}===
DK's Tree House reappears in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', in the background of the course [[DK Jungle (Mario Kart 7)|DK Jungle]]. Its appearance is the same as in ''Donkey Kong Country Returns''.

==In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''==
DK's Tree House makes a background appearance in the intro cutscene, when the [[Toady|Toadies]] capture [[Baby Donkey Kong]].

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
DK's Tree House makes a background appearance in the [[Kongo Falls]] stage of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==In other media==
===''Donkey Kong Country'' television series===
DK's Tree House also appears in the ''[[Donkey Kong Country (television series)|Donkey Kong Country]]'' television series, where it is relatively unchanged except for an elevator that is powered by a strong punch. Sometimes Donkey Kong punches the button too hard, making the elevator slam on entry and startling anyone inside of it.

==Trivia==
*All three versions of ''Donkey Kong Country'' have a different musical theme for the treehouse. The SNES version plays "[[Cranky's Theme]]," the GBC version plays the Title music from ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'', and the GBA version plays "[[Bonus Room Blitz]]."
*[[Nintendo of America]]'s product development division, [[Treehouse]], is codenamed after Donkey Kong’s residence.<ref>[http://kotaku.com/inside-the-treehouse-the-people-who-help-make-nintendo-1301809672 Kotaku: Nintendo's Secret Weapon]</ref>
