The '''Standard Kart S''' is a balanced kart for small characters in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. It is available from the start.

==NPC Use==
[[Baby Mario]] drives a Standard Kart S as a Time Trial ghost for SNES [[Mario Circuit|Mario Circuit 3]] (Nin★iwaco).
