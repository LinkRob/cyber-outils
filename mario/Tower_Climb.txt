'''Tower Climb''' is the fourth [[level]] of [[P. 1-Beginning]] in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Gameplay==
First, you will ride the lifts up and then go down. Then, you go inside the tower and climb it. After, you'll exit the tower and go back outside.

==Have-A-Hearts==
#After completing the [[Broad Jump]] minigame, go to the vase, then, make your way up and you'll find a Have-A-Heart.
#When you use the top exit, eat the tulip, follow the 1st coin path, then, Flutter Jump, to the right to find your 2nd Heart.
#When you reach the Puffing Clouds, go all the way up until you see your 3rd Have-A-Heart.
