The '''Super Nintendo World''' is a real-world theme park area owned by {{w|Universal Parks & Resorts}}. The first area launched in Japan on March 18, 2021, the second area launched in Hollywood on February 17, 2023, and the third and most recent land opened in Orlando on May 22nd, 2025. Although [[Nintendo]] is featured in the name of the theme park, Super Nintendo World is particularly centered around the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], with even its logo being themed as such.

== History ==
The opening was originally set for February 18, 2021, but it was delayed due to Osaka entering a state of [[wikipedia:coronavirus|coronavirus]].<ref name=Klook>{{cite web|url=https://www.klook.com/en-AU/blog/universal-studios-japan-super-nintendo-world-guide/|title=Everything You Need to Know About Super Nintendo World|work=Klook|accessdate=2022-11-25|date=2022-11-16|author=Klook Team}}</ref>

In 2024, [[Universal Studios Japan]] plans to unveil an area introducing [[Donkey Kong]].<ref name=Klook/>
