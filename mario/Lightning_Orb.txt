'''Lightning Orb''' is a special move used by a few enemies in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Lightning Orb is used, a small cyan-colored ball is thrown at its target.

==Effects==
With Lightning Orb, the foe is able to deal moderate damage to one of Mario's party.
