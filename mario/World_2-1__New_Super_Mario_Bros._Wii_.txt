'''World 2-1''' is a level from [[World 2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]].''

==Overview==
This is the First Desert Level. It features [[Sand Geyser]]s and [[Pokey]]s.
==Walkthrough==
The level starts with a [[Koopa Troopa]] walking off a platform onto a [[Sand Geyser]] toward the player. There is then a series of four Sand Geysers; the last is directly below the first Star Coin. After this is another Sand Geyser and a [[Brick Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. Next is a section with geysers and a red [[Warp Pipe]] continuously spawning [[Goomba]]s. There are more geysers, a Boomerang Bro, and the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]].

Continuing forward, there are more geysers, this time guarded by [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, as well as [[Piranha Plant]]s on several of the main platforms. The second Star Coin is in a small indentation in one of the platforms. A section with many Piranha Plants follows; one plant is elevated occasionally by a Sand Geyser and needs to be dodged. Following more geysers, the third Star Coin is obtainable by [[jump]]ing on a red Paratroopa and throwing the shell, or by using a [[Propeller Mushroom]]. After this, there are more geysers, more Koopa Paratroopas, and a second Boomerang Bro that is guarding the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].

==Enemies==

* [[Boomerang Bro]] - 2
* [[Goomba]] - 5 (not including infinitely spawning Goombas from one [[Warp Pipe]] generator)
* [[Koopa Paratroopa|Green Koopa Paratroopa]] - 3
* [[Koopa Troopa|Green Koopa Troopa]] - 1
* [[Koopa Troopa|Red Koopa Troopa]] -  1

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is floating above the pit from which the fifth Sand Geyser emerges. [[Mario]] and co. must wait for the geyser to emerge, then use it to jump up to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is located underneath the platform with the second Piranha Plant. Mario and co. must fall down to the opening in which it rests to get it while the nearby Sand Geyser is inactive.
*'''Star Coin 3''':  The third Star Coin is floating low under a midair platform in the area with no ground and many Sand Geysers. Mario and co. must [[stomp]] the nearby Koopa Paratroopa twice to make it retreat into its shell, then grab the shell and throw it at the Star Coin. Alternatively, the use of the [[Propeller Mushroom]] can also be used to obtain the Star Coin.
