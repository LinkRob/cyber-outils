'''''Donkey Kong Jr. + Jr. Math Lesson''''' (actual romanization: '''''Donkey Kong Jr. + Jr. Sansū Lesson''''') is a game bundled with the Sharp C1 Famicom TV, a television combining the hardware of a television set and the [[Family Computer]], exclusively in Japan. Both the game and the television were both released in October 1983. ''Donkey Kong Jr. + Jr. Math Lesson'' is a Family Computer game, although it never received a standalone cartridge release.

It is a collection of ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|''Donkey Kong Jr.]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. Math]]'' based on earlier prototype builds. Despite this, only sections of both games are featured. It combines half of the 1-player and 2-player modes from ''Donkey Kong Jr.'' and the +-×÷ Exercise mode from ''Jr. Math''. The game can provide for classic arcade excitement and lessons in arithmetic, directed towards a younger audience. No notable changes were made to the details or gameplay mechanics of both titles.
