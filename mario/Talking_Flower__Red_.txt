'''Red Talking Flowers''' are [[Talking Flower]]s that appear in [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Badge]] Challenges of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They appear only in two levels that are not Badge Challenges. They are usually cheering the player on and encouraging them, with very few exceptions.

== In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ==
Red Talking Flowers appear in Badge Challenges. They also appear in a level where you can hit [[ON/OFF Switch|On/Off Blocks]]. They also appear during a Wonder Effect in one level where you can break into where a badge challenge takes place, and they will begin shouting "INTRUDER! INTRUDER!"
