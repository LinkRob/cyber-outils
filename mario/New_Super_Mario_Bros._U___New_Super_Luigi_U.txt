{{Nihongo|'''''New Super Mario Bros. + New Super Luigi U'''''|ニュースーパーマリオブラザーズU + ニュースーパールイージU|Nyū Sūpā Mario Burazāzu Yū + Nyū Sūpā Ruīji Yū}}, alternatively written as '''''New Super Mario Bros. U and New Super Luigi U''''', is a dual compilation pack of [[New Super Mario Bros. U|''New Super Mario Bros. U'']] and [[New Super Luigi U|''New Super Luigi U'']] for the [[Wii U]]. The game was available with only the Mario & Luigi Deluxe Wii U Set (known as the Premium Pack in PAL regions), but it saw an individual release in North America on October 16, 2015. This compilation title can be purchased at retail stores or at the Nintendo eShop. If purchased digitally, the compilation title requires 14.2 GB to be downloaded. As a result, certain Wii U models are not able to download this software without an external storage device.

''New Super Mario Bros. U + New Super Luigi U'' was only available with the Mario & Luigi Deluxe Wii U Set (known for the Premium Pack in PAL regions), but it saw an individual release in North America on October 16, 2015.

== Gameplay ==

=== Changes to ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' ''+ New Super Luigi U'' ===

* The original ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' title screen and game disc have been changed to accommodate for the additional game.

* The box art is identical to the ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' box art, but has a ribbon along the bottom has been added to incorporate the ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' logo, in addition to a picture of [[Luigi]].
* There are 222 bonus videos were included with this version of the game. ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' videos can be found on the [https://www.youtube.com/user/NewMarioUEN/featured official YouTube] channel, while the ''New Super Luigi U'' videos are exclusive to the compilation.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZzUX4BOgMbk] ''New Super Luigi U'' - SuperPlays Collection HD. Youtube. Retrieved August 29, 2025.
</ref>
