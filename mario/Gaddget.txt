[[File:MPA Gaddget Menu Screenshot.png|thumb|240px|A screenshot of the Gaddget selection screen]]
'''Gaddgets''' are miniature toys invented by [[Professor E. Gadd]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. There are 61 different Gaddgets. They can be earned by completing quests in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], winning [[Bowser]]'s [[minigame]]s, and exchanging [[coin]]s for them (with the exception of the secret Gaddget, [[Toad Force V (Gaddget)|Toad Force V]]).

Gaddget is a portmanteau of the surname "Gadd" and "gadget".
