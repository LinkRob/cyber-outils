{{Image}}'''Golden Robotic Bombs''' are similar to normal [[Robotic Bombs]], however, their body is made up of what appears to be gold. They appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. Their style of attack is similar to that of regular Robotic Bombs, however, instead of running at a medium pace towards [[Luigi]] constantly, the Golden Robotic Bombs run in place for a second then sprint at Luigi top speed. They cause 20 HP damage if they hit Luigi. Once they explode, they drop three Gold Bars.
