The '''Antiques''' is a store in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. It appeared in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== Description ==
Antiques consists of antiques which includes [[Super Bell]]s, [[Hammer]]s, [[Music Block]]s and [[Lakitu's Cloud]]s.
