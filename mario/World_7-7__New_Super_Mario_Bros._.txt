'''World 7-7''' is a level from [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Layout==
This level mostly comprises of [[Donut Lift]]s and one-way platforms. There are several Red [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. There are few solid ground areas through the level, and there are several item blocks. Beyond the first third of the level are many moving platforms. There are no secret areas in this level, therefore all the Star Coins are relatively easy to get. At the end of the level, a Red [[Koopa Paratroopa]] can be used to gain height on the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].

==Enemies==
*[[Red Koopa Troopa]]s
*Red [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:'''  Mario must ride the first paddle wheel platform he sees, where he must ride the platform all the way to its lowest point to reach the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' The second Star Coin lies below the platform Mario would normally cross. He should stand on the [[Donut Lift]]s above the Star Coin and wait for them to drop. As he falls, Mario must slide down the wall and [[Wall Jump]] to collect the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3:'''  The third Star Coin is found soon after the second Star Coin, where the screen rises upwards as Mario jumps over a series of Donut Lifts. He should then jump from the top set of Donut Lifts to get the Star Coin above him.
