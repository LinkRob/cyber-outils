{{Nihongo|'''Precision Gardening'''|ダイナミック水やり|Dainamikku mizuyari|Dynamic Watering}} is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
Players are still on their positions while Toads are heard in the the background.

== Gameplay ==
Players must move the Joy-Con up and down to act like a water pump. The faster the players move the Joy-Con, the water will move further and quicker. A rainbow starts to appear slowly when the player nearly reaches the end.

== Ending ==

The player is positioned next to her/his grown flower. The winner does his/her victory pose. New records are mentioned on the top off the screen if the player beats the time.

== In [[Challenge Road]] ==
{{Main|Shell Street/Precision Gardening}}

Precision Gardening is the first minigame challenge in [[Shell Street]].
