The '''Bro Bros.''' are minor characters who serve as a boss enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

== Description ==
The Bro Bros. are a [[Hammer Bro]], a [[Fire Bro]], and an [[Ice Bro]].

== History ==
The Bro Bros. are encountered during the first visit to [[Skorcheen Island]] when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are about to reach the island's lighthouse. The Bro Bros. explain that they were looking for [[Bowser]] (who is on [[Wayaway Island]]). Mario and Luigi explain that they have a mission to reconnect [[Concordia]]'s islands. The Bro Bros. seemingly understand and prepare to leave so Mario and Luigi can continue their mission; however, the Bro Bros. reveal that they are just faking it and ambush the Bros., triggering the fight. Following the battle, the Bro Bros. disappear from the map, likely implying their deaths.

== Combat ==
In combat the Bro Bros. reuse the same tactics as the rest of their species.

=== Solo Attacks ===
During individual attacks, a Bro Bro can throw his respective weapon (The [[Hammer Bro]] uses hammers, the [[Fire Bro]] throws fireballs, and the [[Ice Bro]] attacks with balls of cryokinetic energy.), which must be jumped over (unless he jumps, in which case the projectile flies over the target). He can also throw the projectile high into the air, with the falling projectile having to be repelled by the target. The Hammer Bro signals his target by which hand is used to throw the projectile in the latter attack. The fireballs can potentially [[Burn|set their targets on fire]], while the cryokinetic energy can [[Frozen|trap the victim in ice]].

=== Combo Attacks ===
Occasionally, two Bro Bros. can team up and throw their weapons at the Bros. This must be countered by jumping for fireballs and the cryokinetic energy, while hammers bounce over the victim's head. Additionally, the Fire Bro and Ice Bro can throw fireballs which must be repelled with hammer strikes, after which the Hammer Bro bats them back with his hammer. After enough repulsions, all three will be set on fire and will attack with a charge, which must be repelled with a hammer strike.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The Bro Bros. are named after how the [[Koopa Troop]] members of the [[Hammer Bro]] family have the word "Bro" in their name.
