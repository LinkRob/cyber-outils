'''Letters''' are key items that appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Letters are scattered throughout the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and [[Mario]] must find them all in hidden places. Once he has the letter, the description will tell where the Letter needs to go to, and Mario must have [[Parakarry]] present to be able to deliver the mail. Each time Mario delivers a Letter, he is awarded with a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]].
