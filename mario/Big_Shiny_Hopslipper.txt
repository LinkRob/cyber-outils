With this, Mario can stomp on foes many times with very high damage. Note that this helpful sticker cannot be used on spiked enemies (unless combined with a [[Super Boot]] sticker). This sticker is extremely helpful for boss fights and strong enemies and this sticker can be bought anytime at [[Decalburg]] once Mario reaches World 4 or at [[Rumble Volcano]].
==Description==
Big Shiny Hopslipper stickers are extremely similar to [[Shiny Hopslipper]]s except for the size. They take up four regular sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].
