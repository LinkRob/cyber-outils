'''Platform Push''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
The characters are standing atop various grass-covered block platforms centered within [[Poisoned Water]].

==Gameplay==
The objective for each character is to push all of their opponents into the Poisoned Water within the 30-second [[Time Limit]]. After five seconds, the bordering block platforms begin to sink into the Poisoned Water. Whoever is the last character remaining is the winner. Alternatively, if the time limit runs out, all remaining characters win.
