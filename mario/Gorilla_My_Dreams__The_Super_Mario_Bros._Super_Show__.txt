"'''Gorilla My Dreams'''" is the thirty-seventh live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''.

== Plot ==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] are busy getting ready for a costume ball. Mario decides that he's going to be wearing a bunny outfit and Luigi goes to get his gorilla costume.  As they go to put on their costumes, there is a report on the radio that a gorilla has escaped from the local circus but neither one hears it.  Mario comes out in his bunny costume and a gorilla walks in the door.  Mario insults the gorilla for the ugliness of his suit and the badness of his breath.  In retaliation he's thrown into the wall.

With the gorilla gone, Mario cleans up the mess when Luigi returns in his gorilla outfit.  Mario is upset with him for his antics but leaves to go find his bunny ears.  While Mario is gone, the gorilla appears and Luigi believes it to be Mario.  He too, mocks the gorilla for its ugliness.  Mario returns and finds both Luigi and the gorilla.

Strangely, Mario is unable to decide which is the real gorilla and which is Luigi and all tests fail.  Finally, he decides to leave for the ball.  Luigi goes with him taking the gorilla as his date.
