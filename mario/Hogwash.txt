'''Hogwash''' is a flying pig-like enemy found exclusively to ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]''. They appear to be pink and have wings that possibly resemble the idiom, "If pigs fly". They have similar abilities to [[Necky|Neckies]] as both fly in the skies and go back and forth continuously on in a single direction. They are also known to be one of the few enemies to appear in the ''Donkey Kong Land'' series, but not in the ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' series.
