A '''Stumper''' is an enemy in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Stumpers are tree-like creatures, except they have mouths, spider-like legs, and eyes.

===Behavior===
Stumpers walk around in a set pattern, similar to [[Biddybud]]s, except some Stumpers are stacked on top of each other and their paths can be straight lines.
