'''Haunted Trail''' is the second board for ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

== Description ==
The Haunted Trail is a haunted-themed board. The mid-boss for this board is [[Sledge Bro]] and the final boss is [[King Boo]]. There is also an exclusive Boo space on this board. This board is not available for [[Bowser Party]], like [[Airship Central]].
