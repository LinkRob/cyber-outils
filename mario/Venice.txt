'''Venice''' (Italian: '''''Venezia''''', Veneto: '''''Venesia''''') is a city on [[Earth]] located in Italy. It is well-known for being built over a series of small islands, and is home to numerous canals.

Venice is featured in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[The Venice Menace]]." [[Kootie Pie Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Kootie Pie Koopa]] wants her own waterpark, so [[King Koopa (DiC cartoons)|Bowser]] gives in by promising to warp the entire city of Venice to [[Dark Land (DiC cartoons)|Dark Land]]. However, before Bowser can warp the city, he has to scare everyone away with the [[Doom Sub]].

[[Mario (DiC cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC cartoons)|Luigi]] travel to Venice to fight Bowser, and while they are there, Mario feels a strange feeling in his bones. After finally vanquishing Bowser, a [[Venice pizza man|gondolier]] introduces them to the statues of [[Marco Polo's Plumbers]] in the heart of Venice. Because Mario and Luigi are descendants of the plumbers, Mario is uncontrollably attached to the city of Venice.

Venice also appears on the [[Globulator]], a world map seen in the [[Mario is Missing! (Super Nintendo Entertainment System)|SNES version of ''Mario is Missing!'']]

==Trivia==
*The ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' gameboard [[Grand Canal]] has similarities to Venice.
*In ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', the race course [[Delfino Square]] and the battle course [[Delfino Pier]] have some similarities to Venice; the connection is further implied by the internal name of the latter course being <tt>venice_battle</tt>. Another course similar to Venice, [[Salty Salty Speedway]], appears in ''[[Mario Kart World]]''.
