The '''Booster Course Pass''' is a downloadable content for ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. The DLC contains tracks ranging from ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'' all the way to ''[[Mario Kart Tour]],'' along with a few new ones. Eight courses are released at a time over six waves, this means there will be forty-eight courses in total that will be released. The first wave of the Booster Course Pass launched on March 18, 2022, and the second on August 4, 2022. The third was released on December 7, 2022. Wave 4 was set to release on March 9, 2023, but ended up releasing a day early, on March 8, 2023. [[Birdo]] was added as a playable character, making her the first character added in the Booster Course Pass. Wave 5 released on July 12, 2023 and included the addition of the characters [[Petey Piranha]], [[Kamek]], and [[Wiggler]]. The last one, Wave 6 released on November 9, 2023. It added [[Diddy Kong]], [[Funky Kong]], [[Pauline]], and [[Peachette]] as playable characters
