A '''New Yorker''' is a person based off a real life [[human]] living in [[New York City]]. 

In [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie|''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'']], [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]], [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]], [[Pauline (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Pauline]] , and [[Foreman Spike (Illumination)|Foreman Spike]] are included in this group of people. New Yorkers also appear in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' and '''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' cartoons.

In the games ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'', ''[[Wrecking Crew]]'', and ''[[Mario Bros.]]'', [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Pauline]], and [[Foreman Spike]] are all New Yorkers. In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]],'' [[New Donker|New Donkers]] are based on New Yorkers, as [[New Donk City]] is based on [[New York City]].
