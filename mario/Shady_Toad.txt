The '''Shady Toad''' is a secluded [[Toad (species)|Toad]] merchant in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. He sells things that the player has already collected. Due to some specific characters' dialogue, his business is implied to be illegal. He hides to the left of the [[Sling-a-Thing]] stand in [[Decalburg]], which is convenient, as the player needs to sling Things in order to use them.

== Trivia ==
* The Shady Toad seems to be a spoof on real-life black markets.
* The [[Sling-a-Thing Toad]] may be the Shady Toad, as hinted by him when the player talks to him for the first time.
