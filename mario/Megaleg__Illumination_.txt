'''Megaleg''' is an antagonist set to appear in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. He is seen controlled by [[Bowser Jr. (Illumination)|Bowser Jr.]] when he attempts to Capture Rosalina.

==Description==
The Megaleg largely retains [[Megaleg|its design]] from the  ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]] game;'' however, the movie adaptation provides it a golden frame around his "mouth." It’s legs have also been lengthened, making it differ form the original Megaleg design in the body size-to-leg ratio. It appearance is seen as it being significantly smaller/shorter relative to the game model. During the beginning of the Super Mario Galaxy feature film, the Megaleg is sent on a mission; to infiltrate Princess Rosalina’s ship and take her captive. During this period of infiltration, Rosalina is giving the Lumas a bedtime story, in which the large machine can later be heard walking towards the building her and the Lumas and within. It is after Rosalina floats outside to check the perimeter that the machine engages her not long after coming outside; stating “Princess Rosalina, you’re coming with me” all the while beginning to reach out to her. Just a couple seconds in as the mechanical arm reaches outwards, Princess Rosalina reacts (as expected) with resistance using her magic/powers to damage/halt the Megaleg in its tracks. Throughout the fight, it can be noticed that this specific iteration of the Megaleg is significantly more agile/quick compared to the other iterations; often leading to Rosalina leading to recalculate her attacks. As the fight progresses, one particular Luma stops Rosalina and the Megaleg while they fight, stating that it had a bad dream. With Rosalina distracted, the Megaleg proceeds to take advantage and fires a Bullet Bil barrage towards Rosalina’s/Lumas’ house. Just before the bullet bills (one in particular) breaks in and destroy the entire house. Rosalina captures one/them in a tractor-beam-like magic ray, that allows her To then articulate the Bill’s trajectory to explode elsewhere. While this is all happening, the Megaleg had been recalculating its strategy to retrieve Rosalina as the machine appears to sneak up on her and grab her at the last moment. After being trapped by the large mechanical, three-legged hand, the Megaleg proceeds to bring Rosalina towards its face/body/head, in which the cockpit opens up to reveal [REDACTED BY NINTENDO] as its primary pilot and [REDACTED BY NINTENDO] as its maintainer/copilot. After capturing Rosalina and an unfortunate Luma, the Megaleg and its piloting due/team take off into a large spaceship, in which the ship take off at mega/ultra/hypersonic speed (speed of light). It is later seen destroying Peach’s castle during its invasion, and later exploding within the same large space ship mentioned earlier; causing the ship to stall and shutdown.
