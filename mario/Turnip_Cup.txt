Making its appearance in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', it is a downloadable cup that is part of the [[Booster Course Pass]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ==
The Turnip Cup can be accessed through the Booster Course Pass.
