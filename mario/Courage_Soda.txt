'''Courage Soda''' is an item in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Description==
A Courage Soda is a power-up beverage that, when purchased, causes every enemy defeated from being whacked by [[Perry]] to release a [[Five Coin]]. If several enemies are whacked in rapid succession, the number of coins dropped increases by 2.5 (rounded down) per every two enemies. For instance, defeating two enemies in a row yields two Five Coins, then defeating two more enemies rewards two Five Coins and four [[Coin]]s, and defeating two more enemies yields four Five Coins.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
The Courage Soda can be purchased at the shop for 1,000 coins after completing [[Shriek Mansion]].
