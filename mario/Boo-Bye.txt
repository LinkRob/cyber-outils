'''Boo-Bye''' is the first mini-game select as a choice of the Single-player mini-game. It appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each [[Boo]] has its own color: red, blue, and white. You should lure each Boo to the painting which matches their colors. The game will be over when you touch a Boo.

==Trivia==
*Its name is a pun of "good-bye."
