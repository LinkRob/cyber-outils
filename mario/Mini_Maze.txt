'''Mini Maze''' is a [[Gaddget]] found in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained by completing the quest [[Monkeynapping!?]].

==Layout==
The game features a small maze with a total of a total area of 299 squares (23 squares wide, 13 squares high), with a [[Lakitu's Cloud]] marking the starting point and a [[Fire Flower]] marking the end. The design for the maze itself is very simple, with white squares acting as pathways and black squares acting as walls.

==Gameplay==
Mini Maze involves the player having to reach the end of a small maze. The player can generate a maze by pressing {{button|gba|A}} and press the button again to generate a different maze.
