The '''Kitchen''' is a room in the [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is located near the [[Library]] and the [[Dining Room]]. It is where Luigi first fight [[Greenie]] that wield weapons and defensive objects.

==History==
During [[Quiet Please!|A-3: Quiet Please!]], Luigi must head through the Kitchen in order to get into the Library. However, he is stopped by Greenies that wear pots and pans and carry rolling pins. Luigi vacuums up the Greenies and heads into the Library. Luigi fights [[Hider]]s in this room as well.

A [[Gem]] can also be found here. Luigi must simply open the fridge and vacuum the frozen Gem. He must then carry over to the nearby stove and melt it.
