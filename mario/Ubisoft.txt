'''Ubisoft Entertainment SA''', formerly known as '''Ubi Soft Entertainment SA''', is a video game publishing company that is located in Saint-Mandé, France with developmental studios all around the world. 

Ubisoft has been known for its own popular franchises as well, such as ''Rayman'', ''Tom Clancy's'', ''Prince of Persia'', ''Far Cry'', ''Raving Rabbids'', ''Imagine'', ''Assassin's Creed'', ''Anno'', ''Just Dance'', and ''Watch Dogs''. Later, they have cooperated with [[Nintendo]] to create a crossover series which is later dubbed the ''Mario + Rabbids'' series, having created two games so far.
