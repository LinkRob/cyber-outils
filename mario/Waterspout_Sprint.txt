'''Watersprout Sprint''' is a water level that appears in [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In Fire form, take out the first Big Piranha Plant to get the coin. If not in Fire form, jump on the first Koopa Troopa and throw the shell at the [[Big Piranha Plant]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': In the [[waterspout]] carrying the [[Piranha Plant]] before the Big Piranha Plant, throw a Koopa shell to the coin or swim up the waterspout while avoiding the plant.
*'''Star Coin 3''': By the last three Piranha Plants, get the coin by making a careful jump across, use the Ice or Penguin form to freeze the enemies temperamentally, or use the Fire form to kill the plants to get the final coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Under the red ring shows a mini picture of standard Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' jumping.
