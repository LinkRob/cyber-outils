'''The Sugarstar Trial: Across the Night Sky''' is a level of [[Shining Falls]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level opens with a wave of green [[sugarstar]]s blocking the [[Wonder Flower]]. To access the Wonder Flower, the player must go under long [[cloud block]]s above the sugarstars and move along with the wave of sugarstars. The [[Wonder Effect]] will cause the level to rain [[Super Star]]s with a ten second time limit. Collecting a star resets the timer. The starman effect causes [[coin]]s and [[flower coin]]s to go towards the player. The [[Wonder Seed]] is shortly before the [[Goal Pole]].

==[[10-Flower Coin]] locations==
#A 10-flower coin is on a wavy [[track]] at the start of the level during the Wonder Effect.
#On a wavy track above long cloud blocks is a 10-flower coin.
#A 10-flower coin is on a series of [[lift]]s moving down. The 10-flower coin does not move with the lifts.
