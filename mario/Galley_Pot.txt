The '''Galley Pot''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Galley Pot belongs to the [[Excess Express]]' head chef, [[Chef Shimi]]. During the events of Chapter 6, Chef Shimi's Galley Pot gets stolen leading to the first mystery case of the Excess Express. [[Mario]] soon gets on the case and follows the pot stew stains to [[Heff T.]]'s room and later discovers the Galley Pot in Heff T.'s drawer. It is revealed that Heff T. was so hungry he couldn't wait any longer and stole the Galley Pot when nobody was looking and ate all of the food himself. Mario can then return the Galley Pot to Chef Shimi for a [[Star Piece]].
