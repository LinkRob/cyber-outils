'''4.1''' and '''4.2''' are characters who only appear in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. They are two wolf-like [[Yum Yums]], serving as [[Mona]]'s pets.

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
4.1 and 4.2 are wolves with yellow tops each reading their name respectively. Both of them have brown fur, orange bush eyebrows and long orange whiskers.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
The duo attempted to rescue Art from [[The Dinosaurs]]. In the microgame, the duo were controlling in the microgame off-screen. They were successful attacking them, but were pushed away by The Dinosaurs. Deco ended up rescuing Art instead of 4.1 and 4.2 when he ejected up to the vehicle where Art was held.
