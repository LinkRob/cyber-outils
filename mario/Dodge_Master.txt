'''Dodge Master''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Wearing this badge allows [[Mario]] to use his action commands more easily. It costs Mario two [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear the badge. Mario can obtain the badge by buying it from [[Rowf's Badge Shop]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] for 100 [[coin]]s.

This badge is replaced with [[Simplifier]] in the ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
