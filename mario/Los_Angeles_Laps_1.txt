'''Los Angeles Laps 1''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. As its name implies, it is the first layout of [[Los Angeles Laps]], debuting in the first Los Angeles Tour, which took place in 2020.

== Layout ==
The racers start on the Santa Monica Pier, facing away from a four-way intersection. When the race starts, they drive down the pier a bit before being forced off by a wall. This is followed by a ramp that leads onto the beach. The racers make a slight left to go along the shore, and then find a skatepark with multiple paths and some graffiti drawings. This is followed by a life guard tower which the racers must go around. They then make a left turn. Some [[Sidestepper|crabs]] can be found on this section of the beach, moving side to side on the course as obstacles. The racers make a slight S-curve to get into the city proper. A three-way intersection with the right path leading into the [[Los Angeles Laps 2|Inglewood Oil Field]] lies up ahead; the racers must make a slight left here. The racers then come across the four-way intersection, which they turn left onto to get back onto the pier and back to the [[Finish line|starting line]].

In the [[Nintendo Switch]] version of ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the [[Los Angeles Laps 3|second]] and [[Los Angeles Laps 2|third]] routes of [[Los Angeles Laps]] have been spliced in-between Los Angeles Laps 1's second-to-last and final straightaways, so the racers will have to break their drive through Los Angeles Laps 1 at the four-way intersection by taking a right turn to pass under a bridge that starts section 2, with the home stretch of the final section having them break off from the third route onto Los Angeles Laps 1 to finish the race.

== Trivia ==

* Much like [[Berlin Byways 3]] for [[Berlin Byways]] (as [[Los Angeles Laps 3]] came before [[Los Angeles Laps 2]], much like [[Berlin Byways 2]] came before [[Berlin Byways 1]]), this is the only variant of [[Los Angeles Laps]] to be labelled correctly.
* This is the only variant of [[Los Angeles Laps]] that lacks [[Glider|gliding]]. By contrast, [[Amsterdam Drift 1|the first route]] of the [[Amsterdam Drift]] course has gliding sections, with the [[Amsterdam Drift 2|other]] [[Amsterdam Drift 3|two]] lacking them.
