'''Fire Breath''' is an ability of many characters, especially [[Bowser]]'s, in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. Its first appearance is in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', in which it is used by the [[Fake Bowser]]s and Bowser himself. The ability is also typically usable by [[dragon]]s, even [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] in certain games.
