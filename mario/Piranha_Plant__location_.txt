Found in the [[Desert Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], {{Nihongo|'''Piranha Plant'''|パックンフラワーのまえ|Pakkun Furawā no Mae|Pakkun Flower's Front}} is a location named after [[Piranha Plant|the species itself]].

== Description ==
Piranha Plant is a place of sandy dunes where many Piranha Plants are found.

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
In [[Flowers Are a Blast!]] quest, [[Bob-omba]] requests the player to get her a Piranha Plant and return it to her. The player can drive to this location and grab a Piranha Plant to bring back to her. According to the game script, the Piranha Plant bit the player when they picked it up.
