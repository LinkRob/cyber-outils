|name=Jump x3 Battle Card}}'''Jump x3''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Jump x3 is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s "Jump x3" attack. It is three of the [[Jump (Battle Card)|Jump]]'s scattered on the edges of the card, accompanied by an orange/white checkerboard background.

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Jump x3 is first seen in [[Vortex Island]], but can also found in [[Lighthouse Island]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Green Energy Plant]], [[Mossrock Theater]], and [[The Emerald Circus]].
