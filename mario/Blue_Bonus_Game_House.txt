'''Blue Bonus Game House''' is a building that appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
The '''Blue Bonus Game House''' is a white Toad house with blue stars.

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' the Blue Bonus Game House is a Special building with no rarity. The player can buy the house at the Shop. If the player touches the house, the player will be asked to play the Bonus Game. After the player completes the bonus game, the player cannot play the minigame until six hours pass.

=== Bonus Game ===
The player has 99 seconds the complete the bonus game. The Bonus Game is similar to the bonus game in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. The player must tap the touchscreen to make Mario hit the blocks. After Mario hits the moving blocks, the game determines the amount of coins and Rally Tickets.
