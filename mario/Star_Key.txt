The '''Star Key''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Star Key can be obtained after defeating [[Gloomtail]] in the [[Palace of Shadow]]. After getting the Star Key, [[Mario]] must then head to the top of [[Riddle Tower]] and place the Star Key in the key shaped hole on the pedestal. Doing this will cause eight pedestals with key holes to appear forcing Mario to head downstairs and solve the riddles to get the [[Pedestal Key|keys]].
