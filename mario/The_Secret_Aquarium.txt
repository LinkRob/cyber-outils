'''The Secret Aquarium''' is one of the secret courses in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Layout==
[[File:The Secret Aquarium SM64DS map.png|frame|left|Map of The Secret Aquarium in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'']]
The Secret Aquarium is a large fish tank filled with [[water]]. It utilizes a skybox reminiscent of the one used in [[Bowser in the Sky]]. There is no place for [[Mario]] to refill his air supply to collect [[coin]]s in order to avoid drowning. There are 40 Yellow Coins in rings. Half of the [[Red Coin]]s are in the center of the rings of coins by the windows, and the other half are at the corners of the floor. Like other water-filled areas, The Secret Aquarium has [[fish]] swimming around, creating an ambient effect.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Super Mario 64'' / ''Super Mario 64 DS''==
[[File:The Secret Aquarium SM64DS.png|thumb|[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] swimming in The Secret Aquarium in ''Super Mario 64 DS'']]
The entrance to The Secret Aquarium is located in the room with the [[painting]] into [[Jolly Roger Bay]]. As the room requires three [[Power Star]]s to open the [[Star Door]], The Secret Aquarium therefore requires three Power Stars to enter as well. The entrance to The Secret Aquarium is located through a small hole up above on the wall to the right of the entrance.

The only [[Power Star]] to collect in The Secret Aquarium is the one that appears upon collecting all eight of the Red Coins there. The Power Star spawns above a [[Star Marker]] toward the bottom center of the course.

The Secret Aquarium retains the same layout in ''Super Mario 64 DS'', but the music was changed to the full percussion version of the "[[Dire, Dire Docks (theme)|Dire, Dire Docks]]" theme, which went unused in the original ''Super Mario 64''.

==Trivia==
*This is one of the three courses in both games that doesn't contain any enemies, the others being [[The Princess's Secret Slide]] and [[Tower of the Wing Cap]].
*Because The Secret Aquarium takes place entirely underwater, it is also the only Red Coin course in ''Super Mario 64 DS'' where a [[Bob-omb Buddy]] doesn't show the locations of the Red Coins.
