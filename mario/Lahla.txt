'''Lahla''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Lahla is a teal-colored [[Boo]] who wears cat ears.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Lahla works at the [[Pianta Parlor]] where she acts like the vendor of the casino. Whenever [[Mario]] wins tokens, she gives them to him. She also holds onto any remaining [[Pianta Token]]s if Mario is about to leave the parlor. She has a sister named [[Peeka Boo]] who works nearby at [[Westside Goods]].

Mario can also directly interact with Lahla by turning thin and slipping through the bars in order to get to her. Lahla is surprised by Mario being in there with her, but she offers to tell Mario a little about herself. Lahla will tell Mario more about herself the more he earns high scores during any of the Pianta Parlor games.

==Trivia==
[[File:Peeka1.png|thumb|Lahla in the Japanese version.]]
*In the Japanese version, both Lahla and Peeka wear bunny ears. To avoid {{w|Playboy Bunny}} connotations, this was changed to cat ears in international versions.
