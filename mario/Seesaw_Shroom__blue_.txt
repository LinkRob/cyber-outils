A '''Seesaw Shroom''' is an object in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and its [[reissue|enhanced port]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

== Description ==
Seesaw Shrooms are a light-blue, icy variation of their identically-named [[Seesaw Shroom|regular counterpart]]. Like with regular Seesaw Shrooms, this type tilts slightly from side to side when idle, but balance from whenever there is the weight of playable characters on them. A difference that the blue Seesaw Shrooms have from the regular type is their low traction, likely from having icy properties and appearing in such environments. A character slides across a blue Seesaw Shroom when standing on it unless they are in their [[Penguin Mario|Penguin]] form.

==In ''New Super Luigi U''==
Seesaw Shrooms only appear in one [[level]], [[Cooligan Shrooms]], where they appear as the main feature throughout.
