'''Acrobatic Banana''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] used by [[Diddy Kong]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''. By using it, Diddy bends into the shape of a [[Banana]] and hits the tennis ball, which is then swung back to the receiver in a Banana-shaped curve. Any opponents that return the shot have their controls reversed, and a banana picture shows the effect.
