'''Slide Lift Tower''' is the tower and fourth level of Meringue Clouds in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.
==Walkthrough==
This level is a tower containing [[Fire Bro]]s, as well as [[Slide Lift]]s. They are used throughout the level to progress, and some act as obstacles, as they can crush the player.

The level begins underneath a [[Fire Bro]], and a Slide Lift is found above a pit. A [[Warp Pipe]] leads to another area, where a Fire Bro is found at the beginning. Multiple Slide Lifts are found in the area, and are used to progress vertically. A Fire Bro is found on a row of blocks, which includes a [[? Block]] containing a [[Starman]]. Another Fire Bro is found, as well as another Slide Lift. [[Dry Bones]] and a [[Super Dry Bones]] are found, along with a [[Hidden Block]] on the right wall that contains a [[1-Up Mushroom]] and more Slide Lifts. More Fire Bros are found, as well as a Warp Pipe leading to the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. More Slide Lifts are used to progress, and [[Fire Bar]]s act as obstacles. More Fire Bros, Slide Lifts, and Fire Bars appear in the following section. A [[Huge Fire Bar]] is found before the boss door.

The boss door leads to the battle with [[Kamek]], who creates blocks with his magic. Some of the blocks contain enemies, such as [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]]s, [[Micro Goomba]]s, [[Monty Mole]]s, and [[Bob-omb]]s. When Kamek is hit, he casts magic at the player, which turns the ground into [[Donut Block]]s. He is defeated after being jumped on three times.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In the end of the second area, the Star Coin is sealed in the ceiling by [[Brick Block]]s. The player needs a [[Super Acorn]] or a [[Super Crown]] to break the bricks and reach the coin. The Star Coin can also be reached by bouncing off a [[Fire Bro]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': In the middle of the third area, the player should notice four spinning coins on the right side of the room. Behind those coins is a hidden [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to a secret room. The player needs the Super Acorn or the Super Crown once again to climb the room's Slide Lifts and reach the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Immediately after the second Star Coin, the player should climb up the rightmost chamber by either [[Wall Jump]]ing or using [[Hidden Block]]s to find another secret pipe. The pipe leads to a room infested with Slide Lifts. The player must carefully work their way through the hazardous maze to find the third Star Coin.
