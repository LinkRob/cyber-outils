The '''Grab Badge''' is a of [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In both ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' and its remake, the Grab can be bought from the [[Little Fungitown]] shop for 400 [[coin]]s. It grants the [[Item Grab]] special effect, which causes enemies to drop more items. In the original, the Grab Badge additionally increases [[Power|POW]] by 45. In the remake, the Grab Badge instead increases POW at 52 and [[Bros. Point|BP]] by 10, and can be sold for 100 coins.
