The '''Red Rescue Squad Apprentice Toad''' is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Red Rescue Squad Apprentice Toad has the same appearance as a regular Toad, having red spots on his mushroom cap and wearing a blue vest, but he also wears a pink scarf.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
[[File:PMCS Sacred Forest Toad wish.png|thumb|left|The Red Rescue Squad Apprentice Toad wishing to join the Rescue Squad]]
The Red Rescue Squad Apprentice Toad is first found in the [[Sacred Forest]], pleading the [[Old Sacred Tree]] to become a member of the [[Rescue Squad]].
