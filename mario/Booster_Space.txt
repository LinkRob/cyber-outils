The '''Booster Space''' is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Whenever a character lands on a Booster Space, they obtain a [[Booster (Mario Party: Island Tour)|Booster]], which multiplies each side of a [[Dice Block]] for a turn. A Booster Space is colored blue, and it depicts an image of a Booster.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
Booster Spaces exclusively appear in [[Rocket Road]], where a total of seven of them can be found.
