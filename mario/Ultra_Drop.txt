An '''Ultra Drop''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is a more effective variation of both the [[Mushroom Drop]] and the [[Super Drop]]. Using an Ultra Drop restores sixty [[HP]] for all party members. Certain Item Shops sell Ultra Drops at 100 [[coin]]s each.
