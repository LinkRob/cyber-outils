'''Glitz Pit Security''' are characters who appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They are the main security of the [[Glitz Pit]] of [[Glitzville]], where they escort competitors to the arena.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
All of the Glitz Pit Security resemble each other. They all are blue characters that wear shades and a black suit and also have yellow hair.

===Personality===
The Glitz Pit Security all have stern personalities and take their job very seriously. They don't show much respect for the lower ranked fighters ordering them to follow them and referring to them as "bub". However, Glitz Pit Security show more respect for the higher ranked fighters especially the champion. This is shown when the champion at the time, [[Rawk Hawk]], managed to get one of them to lead [[Mario]] into a locked room.
