'''''Hello, Mario!''''' is both a mobile game and a console game that can be played on [[iOS]], [[Android]], [[Nintendo Switch]] and [[Nintendo Switch 2]] as part of the [[My Mario]] series which is aimed towards younger audiences. They also released a book version of the [[Hello, Mario (book)|same name]]. This game is a similar version to the ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' opening screen where the players can touch [[Mario]] on his face and Mario will react in various ways.

A similar app featuring [[Yoshi]], titled ''[[Hello, Yoshi!]]'', was announced on November 7, 2025, and was released on November 18, 2025 in Japan and on April 9, 2026 in other territories.

== Trivia ==
* The player can interact with Mario's face in a similar way as in [[Super Mario 64|''Super Mario 64's'']] title screen.
* When [[Toad (character)|Toad]] starts making a picture, the [[Plucky Pass Beginnings]] theme can be heard playing as a cameo from [[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker|''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker'']].
