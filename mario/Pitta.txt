==History==
Pitta can be found in [[Flipside]] on the first floor near the store, [[Sweet Smiles]]. Pitta is into the latest trends and fads and often tries to be a part of them. He also spends all of his money on [[Catch Card]]s.
==Trivia==
*His [[Flopside]] counterpart is [[Patta]] who's personality is the opposite of Pitta's.
