'''Mecha-Chomps''' are species of robotic [[Chain Chomp]] dogs that appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' in [[Woohoo Hooniversity]]. In the [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]], they are replaced by [[Mechakoopa]]s.

==Physical description==
Mecha-Chomps actually more closely resemble the real-world dog, but their head looks like a Chain Chomp, with their neck representing the creature's chain. The Mecha-Chomp's face has a screen that functions as an "eye".

==Battle==
In combat, Mecha-Chomps attack by throwing their head forwards to bite the Mario Bro. whose colour is shown on their LED "eye (orange for [[Mario]] and light green for [[Luigi]]). This attack can be dodged with the hammer. Attacking Mecha-Chomps causes them to short circuit and get angry, completely flipping the tell around. Mecha-Chomps cannot be found on the field. A Mecha-Chomp is a Level 15 monster with 36 hit points, an Attack Power stat of 52 (58 upon getting angry), a Defense stat of 56, and a Speed stat of 30 with normal reactions to [[Firebrand|fire]] damage and taking critical damage from [[Thunderhand|lightning]] damage. It gives 26 experience points and 6 coins along with a standard [[Nut]] (3 in 25 kills).
