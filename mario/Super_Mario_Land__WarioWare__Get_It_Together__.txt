'''Super Mario Land''' is a [[microgame]] in [[9-Volt]]'s [[Nintendo Classics]] level in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''

==Layout==
The microgame features a scenario from its namesake [[Game Boy]] game, ''[[Super Mario Land]]'', which it is based on. The first level takes place in [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Land)|World 1-1]], the second level takes place in [[World 3-1 (Super Mario Land)|World 3-1]], and the third level takes place in [[World 4-1 (Super Mario Land)|World 4-1]].

In two-player mode, two more Brick Blocks are placed to the right of the ? Block in Level 2, while an extra Brick Block is placed directly under the ? Block in Level 3.

==Gameplay==
The object is to hit a [[? Block]] as well as collect a power-up item if the ? Block releases one. When the player wins the microgame, [[Mario]] makes an appearance, [[jump]]ing up and down. If the player does not meet the objective on time, they lose.

*'''Level 1:''' The area is spacious and the ? Block is located in the middle of the screen. The player's character has to simply hit the ? Block, which releases a [[coin]].
*'''Level 2:''' The character starts on a high platform, and the ? Block is located next to a [[Brick Block]]. The ? Block releases a [[Super Mushroom]] which the player has to collect after hitting the block.
*'''Level 3:''' There are several [[Warp Pipe]]s, and the ? Block is located to the very right among some Brick Blocks. The ? Block releases a [[Super Star]], which the player has to collect before it falls into the pipe after hitting the block.

The two characters on the highest suitability tier are [[Orbulon]] and [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]. Orbulon is a fit because he can use his Oinker's tractor beam to hit the ? Block and draw in the power-up item. Penny is a good fit because the [[water]] from her [[Jet Tank One]] covers a wide enough range to hit the ? Block and retrieve the power-up item that emerges from it. Meanwhile, [[18-Volt]] is on the lowest suitability tier due to his lack of mobility, and 18-Volt can only retrieve the item by firing his disc at it.

In two-player mode, whoever hits the ? Block in Level 1 or gets the power-up item in Levels 2 or 3 receives an effort point.
