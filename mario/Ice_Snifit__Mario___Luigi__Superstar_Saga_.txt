{{Nihongo|'''Ice Snifit'''|スノームーチョ|Sunō Mūcho|Snow Snifit}} is a parka-laden subspecies of [[Snifit]] encountered in the frozen palace of [[Joke's End]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Profile ==
Ice Snifits use their hurling abilities in the overworld, which can initiate a battle. Ice Snifits sometimes accompany [[Glurp]]s or [[Clumph]]s. Ice Snifits fire [[snowball]]s and shoot ice clumps at [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] remake, Ice Snifits are slightly taller than in the original version with their arms being visible.

=== Strategy ===
Snowballs and icicles can be deflected by Mario and Luigi with the [[hammer]]. Ice Snifits are weak to [[Mario]]'s [[Firebrand|Fire]] attacks, which likely burns them, resulting in an automatic KO. Ice Snifits are most unlikely to be stunned.
