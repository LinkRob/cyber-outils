'''''Bill Bounce''''' was a promotional [[Adobe Flash]] version of the ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' minigame of the [[Bill Bounce|same title]].

==Gameplay==
Unlike the base minigame, [[Mario]] is the only playable character. He has to bounce off of [[Bullet Bill]]s that shoot out of [[Bill Blaster]]s. Every time Mario bounces off of a Bullet Bill, then 10 points are added to the player's score. If the player bounces off of multiple Bullet Bills in a row without touching the ground, then they get 20 points, 30 points, and so on, until 200 points is reached. There is also a timer during the game that lasts for 60 seconds. For every five Bullet Bills stomped consecutively, the player gains 1 second, followed by 2 seconds for the next five Bullet Bills, and so on.

The player needs to earn 2,000 points to clear the game with a satisfactory rating.
