'''Prince Dreambert''', or most simply '''Dreambert''', is the tritagonist of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He is the heir to the throne of [[Pi'illo Castle]] of the [[Pi'illo Kingdom]]. He was trapped in a [[Nightmare Chunk]] when [[Antasma]] crushed the [[Dark stone]] and [[Nightmare Chunk]]s scattered around [[Pi'illo Island]] but was later freed by [[Mario]] in the [[Dream world]]. He then allies with Mario, [[Luigi]] and [[Starlow]] to stop [[Antasma]].


==Profile==
===Physical description===
Prince Dreambert is a [[Pi'illo]] character that wears red clothing with a cape and has a red Pi'illo as a head. His spirit form reduces him to just his head, as is the case with most Pi'illos.

===Powers and abilities===
Like most [[Pi'illo]] citizens, Dreambert possess the ability to fly. He can also enter in and out of people's dreams (mainly [[Luigi]]'s). Prince Dreambert can also turn himself into a [[pillow]] so that Luigi can rest on him activating Dreampoints.

===Personality===
Prince Dreambert appears to be the opposite of [[Starlow]] as having a calm personality. However, he can get serious when it comes to [[Antasma]] since they have a long history with one another. Dreambert is offended when he is name called over his negative traits, seen when [[Starlow]] called him "Forgetbert" due to his frequent memory lapses. He and Starlow also seem to have a bit of a friendly rivalry, like Goombario and Koops from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

Prince Dreambert is the prince of [[Pi'illo Kingdom]] and heir to the [[Pi'illo Castle]] throne.

===The [[Dark Stone]]===
[[File:MLDT Antasma and Dark Stone.png|thumb|200px|[[Antasma]] attempting to destroy the [[Dark Stone]].]]
During the presence of [[Antasma]], [[Antasma]] stole the [[Dark Stone]] and shattered it into pieces. The Dark Stone's pieces became [[Nightmare Chunk]]s; it trapped the [[Pi'illo]] people and Dreambert's soul into the [[Dream World]].<ref name="MLDT">{{cite video game | title = Mario & Luigi: Dream Team| developer = [[AlphaDream]], [[Good-Feel]], [[Nintendo Software Planning & Development|Nintendo SPD Group No.4]]| publisher = [[Nintendo]]| date = 2013-08-11| platform = [[Nintendo 3DS]]| level = Underneath the bridge of [[Pi'illo Castle]]. | quote = We Pi'illos inhabited this [[Pi'illo Island|island]] a long ago. Our [[Pi'illo|people]] were blessed with the ability to travel in and out of the [[dream world]]… Now on the island existed two stones… The [[Dram Stone|Dream]] and [[Dark Stone]]s, both granters of wishes. The Dream Stone was formed of happy dreams… The Dark Stone, manifestation of nightmares… The safekeeping of this stones was tasked to us… But we grew lax, and one day that bat king, [[Antasma]], stole the Dark Stone… He sought to fulfil his own wish! At great risk and great cost, we fought and cornered him… But as Antasma was to be imprisoned in the dream world… He shattered the stolen Dark Stone… And then our true doom came to past. A black rain of nightmare chunks from the Dark Stone fell all over the island… And we were exposed to the chunks, we were all of us petrified…| speaker = Prince Dreambert}}</ref>

===Mario and Luigi===
[[File:MLDT Mario, Luigi freed Dreambert.jpg|thumb|left|200px|[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] freed Dreambert from the [[Nightmare Chunk]].]]
Many years of imprisonment, [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]] and [[Toadsworth]] found a [[Pi'illo]], they brought it to the collection room. A [[Dream Portal]] appeared when [[Luigi]] slept on it, [[Princess Peach|Peach]] was brought by a dark figure into the [[Dream world|Dream World]], convincing [[Mario]] to rescue her. Dreambert's soul appeared to [[Mario]] and told him to rescue him. Meanwhile, [[Mario]] and [[Dreamy Luigi]] freed Dreambert from the [[Nightmare Chunk]] and they returned to the [[Mushroom Earth|real world]]. This shocked everyone present within the area and he explained the events of [[Antasma]].

===Searching for Peach===
Mario, Luigi and Starlow follow Dreambert from the collection room, but lost track of him. After Mario, Luigi and Starlow found a [[Pi'illo]], Prince Dreambert approached them and asked their help on rescuing the Pi'illo. After rescuing a pink [[Pi'illo]], Dreambert and Mario returned to the real world and the group continued progressing to the pipe found at the [[Mushrise Park]].<ref name="MLDT1">{{cite video game| title = Mario & Luigi: Dream Team| developer = [[AlphaDream]], [[Good-Feel]], [[Nintendo Software Planning & Development|Nintendo SPD Group No.4]]| publisher = [[Nintendo]]| date = 2013-08-11| platform = [[Nintendo 3DS]]| level = Underneath the bridge of [[Pi'illo Castle]].}}</ref>

===[[Dream's Deep]]===
After [[Brickle]] gave [[Eldream]]'s Pillow to Mario and Luigi, Prince Dreambert accompanied Mario to the dream world. Dreamy Luigi, Mario and Dreambert chase the [[Dreambunny]] with a nightmare chunk. After rescuing Eldream, himself and [[Dreambunny]] opened the path to the [[Dream's Deep]].

Entering the Dream's Deep, they follow [[Kamek|Peach]]'s voice until they approach Antasma, Peach and [[Bowser]]. After Mario and Dreamy Luigi fought Antasma and Bowser, they returned to the real world. Later, Mario, Dreambert and Peach returned to the real world with Eldream.

Prince Dreambert announced that [[Antasma]] had returned the plague the Pi'illos.<ref name="MLDT2">{{cite video game | title = Mario & Luigi: Dream Team| developer = [[AlphaDream]], [[Good-Feel]], [[Nintendo Software Planning & Development|Nintendo SPD Group No.4]]| publisher = [[Nintendo]]| date = 2013-08-11| platform = [[Nintendo 3DS]]| level = Underneath the bridge of [[Pi'illo Castle]].
| quote = We knew he would live on within the [[dream world]]… we knew he'd wait for his bonds to weaken… And when they did, he would have his chance to return!| speaker = Prince Dreambert}}</ref> Starlow mentioned that Antasma teamed up with Bowser.

===[[Dozing Sands]]===
Brickle opened the [[Mushrise Park]] gate for Mario and company to enter [[Dozing Sands]]. Dreambert accompanied [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] and [[Starlow]] while rescuing P'illos with a Dozite. to the dream world and discovered the [[Luiginary|Luiginary Work]] with Dreamy Luigi, and rescued purple [[Pi'illo]]s with a Dozite to progress to the [[Dream Stone]].

Mario entered the world and accompanied [[Dreamy Luigi]] and Dreambert to find the [[Dream Stone's spirit]]. The spirit kept avoiding the group until it told [[Dreamy Luigi]] that the Dream Stone is found at [[Mount Pajamaja]]. After returning to the real world, they went to [[Wakeport]] in order to progress to [[Mount Pajamaja]].{{Endspoiler}}
