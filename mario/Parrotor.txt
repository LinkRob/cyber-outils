'''Parrotor''' is a light green parrot who serves as the sixth boss of VS COM mode in ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. He is a cross between a parrot and a {{w|monitor lizard}}, with his name being a portmanteau of the two words.
