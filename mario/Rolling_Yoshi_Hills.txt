*''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''|time=100 seconds|located=[[Mushroom Kingdom]]}}'''Rolling Yoshi Hills''' is the fourth level in the [[Acorn Plains]] area of ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
==Features==
The level features rolling round hills, that will roll you either to the left or right. [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] are also found here that can jump high and fly for a short period. There are also some tall pillers/hills that [[Monty Mole|Monty Moles]] hide in, and pop out.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Jump on the first [[Koopa Troopa]] and roll it down the edge on the right leading to the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': While riding on Yoshi (unless you're [[Nabbit]]), jump on (or in if you're Nabbit) the line of [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plants]] and jump to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': While riding on Yoshi, flutter jump (and jump off of Yoshi if you need to) to get the Star Coin above the flagpole. If you don't have Yoshi, triple jump to the coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
On the left of the last POW block, there's an image of what looks like a modern [[Luigi]] doing a pose jump in shadow form on the second to last pillar.
