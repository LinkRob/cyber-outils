}}A '''Zoing-Oing''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Zoing-Oings are stronger versions of [[Sproing-Oing]]s that can be found in [[Castle Bleck Foyer]] and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Zoing-Oings mainly bounce around attempting to harm those that get too close to them. Attacking a Zoing-Oing will cause it to split into three mini versions of itself but they can be taken out easily afterwards.
