'''''Wario's Woods''''' is a [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] puzzle game serving as the system's equivalent to the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[Wario's Woods (Nintendo Entertainment System)|same name]]. The game was released in North America in 1994 and in 1995 in Europe. It was never released on any physical medium in Japan, but was instead available exclusively via the [[Satellaview]] service. This game features [[Toad (character)|Toad]] as the main protagonist for the first time (the others being ''[[Satella-Q]]'' and [[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]] as [[Captain Toad]]).

== Story ==
The story from the instruction booklet:

''The Peaceful Woods was once the friendly home of sprites and other gentle forest creatures. Wario and his band of monsters invaded, though, and spoiled the serenity of the woods. The quiet, woodlands community has now been turned upside-down by these horrifying events. Wario has now named the woods after himself, and peaceful creatures are not welcome. Toad, the Mushroom Kingdom hero, has decided to travel to Wario's Woods and put an end to Wario's evil reign of terror. Toad knows that, with the sprites help, he will be able to rid the forest of Mario's twisted nemesis. Thankfully, the sprites are able to create bombs. It's Toad's job to line up the bombs with the enemies of the woods, thus defeating those evil creatures. By clearing more and more of the woods, Toad steps ever close to the ultimate challenge of facing Wario.''

== Gameplay ==
The game is similair to the [[Wario's Woods (Nintendo Entertainment System)|NES]] version where Toad is the main hero and [[Birdo]] and [[Wanda]] help him with support and bombs to destroy monsters. If the level takes too long, [[Wario]] replaces Birdo temporarily and a [[Pidgit]] replaces Wanda who throws monsters at Toad instead of bombs that will be dropped by a [[Thwomp]] instead. Wario will occasionally body slam at the side of his box making the thwomp ceiling descend.
