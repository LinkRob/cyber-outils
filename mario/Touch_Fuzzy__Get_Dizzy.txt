'''Touch Fuzzy, Get Dizzy''' is the seventh level of [[World 1 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 1]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and the Game Boy Advance version, [[Super Mario Advance 3: Yoshi's Island|''Super Mario Advance 3: Yoshi's'' ''Island'']]. 

== Layout ==
The player first encounters a [[Melon Bug]] on a sloped log. Two more are seen on two other logs, where in the middle is the first [[Smiley Flower]]. At the end of the right slope is a [[Piranha Plant|Wild Piranha]] that the Melon Bug can easily defeat as it rolls down. Two more can also roll into a line of [[Tap-Tap|Tap-Taps]] afterwards. The next section features a locked minigame door, and spots with dirt which Red Yoshi can break through with the Melon hanging. A few red coins can be found in this area, along with the second Smiley Flower and a hidden Winged Cloud containing the door key. After the Middle Ring, you will be in the area full of floating [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]]. The path to the end is rather straightforward, and contains red coins and the last 3 Smiley Flowers.

The next area is set over water, with platforms that bend down as Red Yoshi walks on them. The Goal Ring is at the end. The player can easily collect the two coins underneath the platforms at the beginning and end, although Ground Pounding them can break the platforms, to get the three red coins in a row of 9 below the middle section. If the player throws an egg upwards on top of the middle log, a Spring Ball will fall and will take to another section. There are various flower enemies in the section. On the far right is a Chomp Rock. Rolling it will reveal a hidden Winged Cloud which grows a beanstalk to a cloud section with a trail of zigzagging coins, containing the last red ones of the stage. It leads to another Winged Cloud.
