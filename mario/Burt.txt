'''Burts''',<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 126.</ref> also known as '''Burt Jr.'s''',<ref>''Nintendo Magazine System'' (UK) #41, page 69. "''Drop down the first gap to get the flower and face two Burt Jr’s.''"</ref> '''Bouncing Burts''',<ref>Pelland, Scott ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3 Player's Guide''.</ref> or '''Burt Brothers''' (alternatively '''Burt Bros.'''<ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' Player's Guide. Page 20.</ref><ref>Black, Fletcher. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Pages 40, 42.</ref>), are round, clown-like, peach-colored enemies with large red noses and blue and white striped pants that go up above their hands.

A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] usually encounters a pair of two Burts. Burt always attacks alongside his partner. When he jumps on his partner, who is another Burt, he will gain boost, meaning that he can jump higher. He and his partner will continue this cycle until one is destroyed. If a Burt's partner is destroyed, he will jump nonstop. It takes a ground pound or an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to destroy a Burt. Burts usually leave behind [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]] when defeated. If [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] already has 30 Stars, the Burt will drop [[Coin]]s instead.

==History==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''===
[[File:Burt Classic.png|frame|left]]
Burts debut in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and return in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''; they attack in pairs. After [[Jump|jumping]] on its partner, a Burt will get a big boost, and the same goes for a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] if it jumps on a Burt. If one was defeated, the other would jump non-stop, but if one falls into a [[pit]] or [[lava]], the other will be motionless forever. If defeated, they will give two [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|stars]] or three [[coin]]s. They cannot be eaten, nor can they be defeated when jumped on, but they can be disposed with an [[Yoshi's Egg|egg]] or a ground pound.

[[Burt the Bashful]] was originally a normal-sized Burt, but was made bigger by [[Kamek]].

Sprites found in the source assets of ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' prototypes include a smiling, nightcap-wearing creature otherwise closely resembling Burts, but appears to walk normally.<ref>[https://twitter.com/GamingAlexandri/status/1286766784570437632?s=19 Twitter post showing early Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island tiles]. Retrieved July 25, 2020.</ref>

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
[[File:Burt YIDS.png|frame|left]]
Burt Brothers return in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', retaining their behavior in its predecessor. The [[Big Burt Bros.]] were originally normal-sized Burt Brothers but were made bigger by [[Kamek]].

===''Yoshi's Woolly World'' / ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''===
Burts return in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', retaining their behavior in the previous games. Burt the Bashful also returns, retaining his behavior.

===''Yoshi's Crafted World''===
Though no normal Burts appear in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'', a new inner-tube variant called [[Beach Burt]]s were introduced. A notable Beach Burt named [[Burt the Ball]] acts as the boss in [[Chilly-Hot Isles]] and guards the yellow [[Dream Gem]].

==Trivia==
*Burts are among the few enemies from ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and ''Yoshi's Island DS'' to not return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.
*A Burt is one of the few enemies that resembles a [[Human]].
*[[Beanie (Yoshi's New Island)|Beanies]] from ''Yoshi's New Island'' behave very similarly to Burts, additionally, the first boss of the game is a giant-sized Beanie (named [[Big Beanie]]) just as a Burt is the first boss of the previous ''Yoshi's Island'' games.
