The '''Host Snifit''' is a [[Snifit]] with a pink bowtie. In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', he hosts the game [[Whiffit]], as well as in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'', but in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'', Host Snifit is replaced by a different [[Shy Guy]] who hosts a similar minigame.
