'''...The Tough Get Going''' is the third level of [[Lava Pit (Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle)|Lava Pit]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

==Objectives==
*'''Map 1:''' Defeat nine enemies
*'''Map 2:''' Reach the marked area

==Layout==
On the first map, players will start in an arena area featuring six different platforms. Here, players will encounter two [[Hopper]]s and one [[Smasher]], and more will appears on occasion.

On the second map, players will start in an area that features a linear arena. This map features enemies such as [[Buckler]]s and [[Peek-a-Boo]]s. The objective here is to reach the area indicated in yellow on the map to complete the level.
