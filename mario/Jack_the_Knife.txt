'''Jack the Knife''' is an enemy found in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is a knife that attacks by rotating, aiming at an angle of Wario, and charging forwards. It can only be found on the [[S.S. Caviar]].

== General Description ==
It is a sentient knife about the size of [[Wario]] that attacks by aiming at him and charging against him. Only one instance appears in the game.

== In-game Description ==
{{Quote|These dull and rusted knives are fueled by their own rage over a lifetime of butter-spreading and chicken slicing. Rusted and dull, they know neither remorse nor pity.}}

== Trivia ==
* Its name is a pun on jackknife which is a type of knife that can be folded in.
* According to its game description, it is formed from the rage from a lifetime's worth of butter spreading and chicken slicing.
