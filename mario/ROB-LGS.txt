'''ROB-LGS''', short for ROB-legs is one of [[R.O.B.]]'s karts in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. His others include the [[ROB-BLS]] and the [[Standard RB]].

== Description ==
The kart is quite small, built upon two large machine legs, with a small tire on each, connected to a large tire on the rear.

Just like the ROB-BLS and R.O.B., the ROB-LGS has a different color palette in the Japanese and Korean version of ''Mario Kart DS'', consisting of white and red instead of shades of light and dark gray.

The ROB-LGS is the second bike to appear in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart series]]'', the first being [[Waluigi]]'s [[Zipper]]. However, like the Zipper, it does not have the same abilities as bikes in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart DS'' ==
[[File:ROB-LGSMKDS.png|thumb|left|200px|R.O.B. in his ROB-LGS, in the North American version]]
It is a very heavy kart, only lighter than the [[Dragonfly]] and the [[Tyrant]]. The kart has high top [[speed]], below average [[acceleration]], average [[handling]] (which is unique for heavyweights), but has the lowest [[drift]] in the game, and a high [[Items (stat)|Items]] stat. It is used as the staff ghost for [[Desert Hills]].

The ROB-LGS shares its speed with the Tyrant, its weight rivals the [[Cucumber]], and it shares its handling with the [[4-Wheel Cradle]]. 

This kart can be unlocked for [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Bowser]] if the player completes all cups in the [[Engine Class|150cc]] Nitro or Retro Grand Prix, and can be unlocked for R.O.B. once he is unlocked. All characters can use this kart when all cups in the Mirror Mode have been completed.
