'''Smeeches''' are flying pig-like enemies seen in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They resemble a pig head with wings, large lips, blue beady eyes, and blushed cheeks. Whenever they see [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], they will chase after him and latch themselves onto his mouth. They are for the most part however, relatively harmless enemies.

== In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ==
[[Image:Smeech Fruit.png|thumb|left|A Smeech]]
Smeeches are first seen in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. In this game, they are mainly found in various Galaxies that have [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] such as the [[Yoshi Star Galaxy]], [[Hightail Falls Galaxy]], [[Haunty Halls Galaxy]], [[Starshine Beach Galaxy]], [[Sweet Mystery Galaxy]], and the [[Flash Black Galaxy]]. They are usually seen hovering idly around an area until [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] approaches the area, at which point they will chase after Yoshi. If the Smeech manages to catch Yoshi or if Yoshi attempts to swallow the Smeech, it will latch itself onto Yoshi's mouth and start sucking on him, preventing him from swallowing anything (be it fruits or enemies). Smeeches cannot harm Yoshi but they can be an annoyance if Yoshi doesn't shake them off, a quick [[Head Shake]] is enough to flick one away. Smeeches can also be defeated with mere stomps or [[Star Spin]]s. Also, if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] is to approach a Smeech on their own, the Smeech will turn and flee. If the plumbers dismount Yoshi while a Smeech is on him, the Smeech will detach itself and remain idle for a few seconds before fleeing. Upon defeat Smeeches will drop one [[Coin]] if stomped or three [[Star Bit]]s if spun into. Smeeches commonly appear in Galaxies where Yoshi is primarily used, they are also often seen in certain [[Prankster Comet]] missions.

== Trivia ==
*Smeech is a portmanteau of the words "smooch" and "leech".
*Smeeches bear a striking resemblance to the [[Poink]] enemies from [[Bianco Hills]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', in both their appearance and their method of attack, as these creatures would attach themselves to [[F.L.U.D.D.]]'s nozzle in the same way as Smeeches attached themselves to [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s mouth.
**Smeeches also bear a faint resemblance to [[Love Bubble]]s from ''[[Mario and Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.
*The Smeeches behavior seems to replicate the [[Flipbug]]'s as they also flee from [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] when approached normally however chase them whenever they have special assistance (under the effects of the [[Bee Mushroom]] for the Flipbug and riding [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] for the Smeech) however the difference is the Flipbugs become harmful enemies when chasing the plumbers (as they're under the effects of the power-up) whereas the Smeeches remain harmless.
*If [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] goes up to a Smeech and another one is already on his lips, the Smeech won't take any notice of him and keep hovering around like normal, making an easy target to be stomped on.
*If one manages to reach the end of a level with a Smeech attached to Yoshi, it will show up during their “You got a Star!” animation, still sucking on Yoshi’s mouth.
