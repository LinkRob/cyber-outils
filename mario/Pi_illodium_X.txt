'''Pi'illodium X''' is a much stronger version of [[Pi'illodium]], a powerhouse boss from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It has the same attacks, but it has more health than the regular Pi'illodium. Instead of a gold hue in color, Pi'illodium X features a red base color and a darker gray base. Accessed from the [[Battle Ring]] located in [[Pi'illo Castle]], defeating Pi'illodium in the [[Pi'illo Temple]] after collecting all the [[Ultibed]] parts will unlock the Pi'illodium X.

==Battle== 
The recommended level for this battle is level 40, but is possible at lower levels. Pi'illodium X is a heavy hitter, their attacks hit hard and some are very difficult to avoid, but can be easily shut down by destroying their wings ASAP. [[Slingsniper]] is highly effective at breaking the wings and dealing massive damage to the main body, it's possible to hit the wings and the body at once if you aim very well.
==Moves== 
===Hammer Slam=== 
Pi'illodium X will retract its wings and turn into a giant hammer. One of their eyes will flash red or green (representing Mario and Luigi respectively) and start counting down from 4 to 6 while floating high up and off the screen, then crashing down on the targeted Bro, dealing massive damage and a high chance of inflicting Dizzy.

===Laser Volley===
The screen dims and Pi'illodium X will begin targeting areas that may or may not hit Mario or Luigi, before quickly firing a volley of lasers in the sequence of the scans. Getting blasted by a laser deals moderate damage with a medium chance of inflicting DEF-Down. When Pi'illodium X is grounded, less lasers are fired, and they are fired more slowly. This is the only attack they can use when on the ground.

===Eyepi'illo Bomb=== 
Pi'illodium X rises upwards and far back and starts hitting one of the Eyepi'illos at Mario or Luigi, who must counter it back and forth until Pi'illodium X slips up and gets blown up by it. Getting blown up by the Eyepi'illo deals very high damage to whoever gets hit by it. Whatever the result is, the Eyepi'illo will be destroyed.

===Rocket Barrage===
Pi'illodium X flies upwards and begins flying forward at Mario and Luigi. From there, they'll fire rockets that move slowly or quickly, or charges forward in an attempt to ram them away. It's possible to precisely jump on and counter the ram to immediately end the attack, but the timing is very strict. Getting blasted by a rocket deals moderate damage, while getting rammed deals high damage and removes the target from the attack.

===Eyepi'illo Factory=== 
Whenever there are less than 4 Eyepi'illos currently active, Pi'illodium X may spend a turn creating a new batch of them.

===Self-Destruction===
At critical HP, Pi'illodium X will begin its self-destruct sequence, starting a timer for 45 seconds. If the timer expires and they aren't defeated, Pi'illodium X will explode, dealing extreme damage to both Bros. The battle will still be won if at least one Bro survives the blast.
