The '''Wrestling Mag''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Wrestling Mag is found during [[Toodles]]' [[Trouble Center|trouble]], "I must have that book". [[Mario]] finds the Wrestling Mag with [[Jolene]], who had borrowed it from Toodles. She wanted to "see what the fuss was about". Mario returned the Wrestling Mag to Toodles.
