'''Sea Dragon Wario''' is a form of [[Wario]]'s which only appears in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

==Description==
In this form, Wario's hat becomes a dragon head with the ability to breathe out a stream of fire, although he cannot [[Dash Attack|Barge]] while in this form. Wario can transform into Sea Dragon Wario by collecting a [[Dragon Crystal]].

Sea Dragon Wario has an upgraded form named [[King Dragon Wario]], which combines the abilities of both Sea Dragon Wario and [[Eagle Wario]].
