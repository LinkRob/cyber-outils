'''Ground Pounding''' is a move featured in some platforming titles in the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}. It does not appear as frequently as its [[Ground Pound|equivalent]] in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] and related media, mainly due to [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] using his [[Hand Slap]] attack as a way of pounding the ground instead. The move appears mainly in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'', though as a specialized attack named the [[Simian Slam]] and its two stronger variations, the [[Super Simian Slam]] and the [[Super Duper Simian Slam]].

The Simian Slam functions similarly to [[Mario]]'s [[Ground Pound]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. Its first upgrade, the Super Simian Slam, allows the Kongs to activate blue [[Kong Switch]]es, and the second upgrade, the Super Duper Simian Slam, allows them to activate red switches.

[[King K. Rool|King Krusha K. Rool]] does use actual ground pound attacks during the first and fourth rounds of the final battle at the [[boxing ring]].
