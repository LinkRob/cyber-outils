'''Bucket Men''' are an enemy in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''. They are the successor of [[Dust Man|Dust Men]] from ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bucket Men have a similar appearance to Dust Men in that they are [[Bin]]s with a pair of eyes and feet. Bucket Men have larger eyes than Dust Men, and a Bucket Man's eyes poke forward out from its lid. A Bucket Man also has a pair of brown feet.

===Behavior===
Like Dust Men, Bucket Men disguise themselves as regular Bins. A difference is that Bucket Men poke their eyes out from their lid at times, signaling they are living creatures. When [[Mario]] jumps onto a Bucket Man, it begins to walk around.

==In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''==
[[File:MvsDK 1-4.png|thumb|left|200px|A Bucket Man in Level 1-4 of ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'']]
Bucket Men first appear in [[Level 1-4 (Mario vs. Donkey Kong)|Level 1-4]] and make their final appearance in [[Level 1-4+]]. Mario can defeat a Bucket Man by either hitting it with a [[Hammer]] or by throwing an object at it. Mario can ride on a Bucket Man, which can safely move him across [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]] as it moves around.
