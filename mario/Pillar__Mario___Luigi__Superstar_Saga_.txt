A '''Pillar''' is an object featured during the boss fight against [[Hoohooros]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is a rocky column with [[Hooroglyphs]] on it.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
During his boss fight, one of Hoohooros's actions is hiding behind a Pillar to defend himself against [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s attacks. Though non-sentient, Pillars have only 2 [[Heart Point|HP]] (3 in the remake) and serve more as obstructions that use up a turn of Mario's or Luigi's to open Hoohooros for attack. Whenever a Pillar is destroyed, Hoohooros creates another in its place.
