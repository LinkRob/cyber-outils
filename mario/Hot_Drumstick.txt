'''Hot Drumsticks''' are health-restoring items usable by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its remake ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. Hot Drumsticks are not the strongest drumstick surpassed by the [[Fiery Drumstick]], the TNT Drumstick, and the remake-exclusive Cheesy Drumstick. With the exception of the Cheesy Drumstick, Hot Drumsticks share their design with the other [[Drumstick|Chicken Drumsticks]]. If they are eaten, he immediately gains again eighty HP. Unlike the Fiery and the TNT Drumsticks, they need to purchased from [[Broque Monsieur]]'s item shop for ten coins. [[Sea Pipe Statue|The statue]] has a 100% risk of dropping a Hot Drumstick after a battle in the original game, [[Chuboomba]]s have a 35% risk, [[Toothy|Toothies]] have a 30% risk, and both [[Flifit]]s and [[Treevil]]s each have a 5% risk.
