'''Plack Beach''' is a location in [[Mushroom Kingdom]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and [[Reissue|its]] [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|remake]]. This is where the characters encounter the [[Sea Pipe Statue]] and [[Chakron]].

===History===
[[Bowser]] first comes through here in the beginning of the game. Bowser then meets Broque Monsieur, who is stranded on an island. He pulls the island to the main land with the unknown-to-him help of Mario and Luigi who stimulate his arm muscles. Broque rewards Bowser with the Vacuum Block and teaches him how to use the Vacuum technique. Bowser encounters and fights the Sea Pipe Statue that was brought to life by a Fawfulcopter. After the statue is defeated, palm trees grow to block Bowser's path and the statue starts squirting water, unlocking the Pump Works. Starlow sends Mario and Luigi to investigate the Pump Works. Mario and Luigi eventually make it to the Flame Pipe and fight the Scutlet that was blocking the fire's flow. Starlow then tells Bowser to call her "Chippy", and then Bowser burns down the trees that were blocking his path. He meets again with Broque, who explains that his dog, Broggy, was being disobedient and needed to be taught a lesson. Bowser battles Broggy and wins. Broque and Broggy leave, saying to visit them in Dimble Wood, where they own a shop.

Mario and Luigi later explore Plack Beach, meeting and solving puzzles for Kuzzle, one of which rewards them with the Spin Pipe attack, as well as battling Chakron and procuring his Star Cure.

== Bosses ==
* [[Chakron]]
* [[Sea Pipe Statue]]

==Trivia==
*"Plack" is a pun on dental plaque, a sticky clear film composed almost entirely of bacteria, that can cause tooth decay. This is due to the presence of cavity-ridden tooth-like structures scattered around the beach, as well as tooth/cavity based foes around the area (such as Chuboomba and Toothy).
