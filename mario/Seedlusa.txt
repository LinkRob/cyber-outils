The '''Seedlusa''' is a dangerous enemy from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They are exclusively encountered in the [[Soli-Tree]].

== Description ==
Seedlusas have a design resembling the [[Seedle]]'s, but their body is monochrome and their eyes are red, making them resemble [[Reclusa]] to a degree, and their "leaves" are actually cables with red wirework resembling solidified [[Glohm]]. Since they move away from the Bros. when approached in the field, and have a high chance of fleeing in combat, Seedlusas seem to be cowardly. However, when summoned as part of Reclusa's mob at the top of the Soli-Tree, the creatures appear surprisingly brave in cutscenes, although their behaviour is unchanged in combat.

== Behaviour ==
In the field, Seedlusas hop away from the Bros. when approached. As such, they are one of the few enemies that are unlikely to land a [[First Strike|first strike]] upon making contact; ironically, should they be approached, they are actually more vulnerable to a first strike than most enemies, since they give their pursuers more of a window to hit them before colliding.

=== Combat ===
In battle, Seedlusas can spawn in groups of at least three when not being fought alongside other enemies; any less, however, and they will be aided by either a [[Weeping Trooper]] and/or [[Reclusiff|Reclusiffs]]. Unlike the regular Seedle (or most enemies for that matter), Seedlusas face away from their opposition. A Seedlusa's extremely high defense allows them to take very little damage from attacks, so attacks that hit multiple times are highly recommended against these aberrations; additionally, using the Surprise Iron Ball [[Battle Plug]] can negate the Defense. In combat, the Seedlusa rarely attacks, and has a high chance of [[Flee|running away]]. If it is the only Seedlusa left, it will bury itself into the ground to summon a flower [[Soli-Tree Flower Monster|monster]].

The Seedlusa is a Level 43 monster with 32 hit points, an attack power of 231, a defense of 1746, and a speed of 289. When slain, it gives 490 Experience and 69 Coins, and may drop an Ultra or Max Syrup Jar (2% and 1% chance, respectively).

At the top of the Soli-Tree, [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must battle two waves each of four Seedlusas, the second of which is aided by a single Reclusiff. Afterwards, a cutscene plays where the Bros. get overwhelmed before [[Bowser]] blasts Reclusa's minions in his revenge quest against [[Zokket]] (who is dead) as it orders its army to attack, enraging it.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The Seedlusa's name comes from the name of [[Reclusa]] (who hired them as guards in the [[Soli-Tree]]) mixed with either "seed" or "[[Seedle]]" (due to their resemblance to one).

== Nomenclature ==
Translations of the Seedlusa's name involve the regular Seedle's name or just the word "seed" translated into the same language being combined Reclusa's name:

* Japanese: ''Tanebotchi'' -- Written タネボッチ. Portmanteau of ''tane'' ("seed"; written 種) and the first two syllables of Reclusa's Japanese name, ''Botchiāni''.
* Chinese: ''Zhǒngzi Dúgū'' --  Written 种子独孤. Zhǒngzi (written 种子) means "seed," while ''Dúgū'' (written 独孤) comes from Reclusa's Chinese name, ''Dúgū Yàní'' (written 独孤亚霓).
* Spanish: ''Ysolmilla'' -- portmanteau of ''semilla'' ("seed") and the first two syllables of Reclusa's Spanish name, ''Ysoleth''.
* Portuguese: ''Reklusina'' -- from Reclusa's Portuguese name ''Rekluso''. The final ''-ina'' is a feminine diminutive suffix that was probably derived from ''Sementina'' (the regular Seedle's Portuguese name)
* Italian: ''Ysolpiantagránia'' -- from Reclusa's Italian name ''Ysoll'' and the regular Seedle's Italian name ''Piantagránia''.
* French: ''Solibulb'' (from ''solitaire'' (referencing their master's solitude theme) and ''bulbe'' ("bulb," since they resemble regular Seedles, which are called "''Bulbet''"))
* Korean:  ''Ssias'tori'' -- Written 씨앗토리. from ''ssias'' ("seed"; written 씨앗) and the second and third syllables of Reclusa's Korean name, ''Oetorikeu'' (written 외토리크).

== Trivia ==
