'''Ruby Passage''' is the second world of ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
The [[level]]s of Ruby Passage consist of a mechanical and machinery theme, including its enemies. The Ruby Passage's four levels are [[The Curious Factory]], [[The Toxic Landfill]], [[40 Below Fridge]], and [[Pinball Zone]]. The boss fought at the end of Ruby Passage is [[Cuckoo Condor]], a ghost-like bird creature appearing as a large cuckoo clock. The [[jewel piece]]s of the Ruby Passage appear as a ruby, and the [[CD]]s of Ruby Passage are a red color.
