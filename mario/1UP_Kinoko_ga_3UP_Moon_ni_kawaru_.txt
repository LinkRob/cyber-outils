'''1UP Kinoko ga 3UP Moon ni kawaru!''' is an [[e-Reader]] card, more specifically a [[Switch Card]], for ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It was released exclusively in Japan, as a part of Series 1.

==Description==
1UP Kinoko ga 3UP Moon ni kawaru! can be used in both the main game and in [[World-e]]. By scanning the card, each [[1-Up Mushroom]] turns into a [[3-Up Moon]].

The e-Reader card appears to depict a [[Blue Green Switch]], but with an added plus sign next to the "e." The card is orange and has a red outline of [[Raccoon Mario]], specifically him crouching with his tail extended.
