'''Dimitri''' is a character from ''[[w:c:fireemblem:Fire Emblem: Three Houses|Fire Emblem: Three Houses]]''. He was featured in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]], as of version 7.0.0, and in a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Get It Together!''==
Dimitri appears in Level 2 of the [[Fire Emblem: Three Houses]] [[microgame]]. The objective is for the player's character to select the answer with the correct spelling. If the player selects the correct answer, the phrase "PERFECT TEATIME!" is shown onscreen, but if the incorrect answer is selected, Dimitri does not understand what the player is attempting to say. The same objective is present with [[Edelgard]] in Level 3 except its incorrectly spelled answers are harder to spot at first glance.
