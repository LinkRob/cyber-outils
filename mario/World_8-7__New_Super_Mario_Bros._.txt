'''World 8-7''' is the ninth level of [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Layout==
[[File:Fire Bros in World 8-7.png|thumb|left|The only two Fire Bros. of ''New Super Mario Bros.'' are encountered in World 8-7]]
This level features multiple varieties of [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Hammer Bro]]s. The underground theme plays in this course to fit with the theme of World 8, although it is an athletic level. Also, the gray tileset from World 6 is used instead of the black volcanic one, most likely because there is a thin ledge section.

The level begins with the player needing to clear a gap, with two [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s hovering in between. After a few more gaps and Paratroopas, the player will come across a [[Sledge Bro.]] on top of breakable blocks. Past the Sledge Bro. is a section full of thin ledges that the player must balance on, with several [[coin]]s available for the player to obtain. After the section with thin ledges is passed, the level's [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] is reached.

After the midway point, a pair of [[Fire Bro]]s. will try to attack the player. Within a [[? Block]] that one of them is standing on is a [[Starman]], which can be used to score multiple 1-UPs from the Koopa Troopas in the next area. This area contains vertically- and horizontally-moving platforms, one of which is patrolled by a [[Boomerang Bro]]. Past the Boomerang Bro is solid ground, with a Sledge Bro. in the area.

After getting past the Sledge Bro. and another Boomerang Bro., the player will come across a [[Red Ring]] and a [[Pipe Cannon]]; the latter takes the player to the [[flagpole]] on a path of [[Red Coin|coins]] created by the former. Some Paratroopas are available to give the player a potential boost to the top of the flagpole.

==Enemies==
* [[Boomerang Bro]]s.
* Green [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s
* [[Fire Bro]]s.
* Red [[Koopa Paratroopa]]
* Red [[Koopa Troopa]]s
* [[Sledge Bro]]s.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Underneath the first Sledge Bro.; the player must dash across the single blocks to reach it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Located on the horizontally-moving platform that holds the first Boomerang Bro.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Underneath the second Sledge Bro.; the player must [[Ground Pound]] through the [[Brick Block]]s above it. Alternatively, baiting the Sledge Bro. into jumping onto the Brick Blocks will also work.
