'''Trip''' is a gameplay mechanic in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]].

== In [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]] ==
Trip is a status effect in ''Mario & Luigi'' series. Outside of battles, if the enemy runs into the rear brother or the rear brother touches the enemy, the battle will begin and the brother will trip. Certain enemy attacks can also cause tripping. The brother cannot move until he gets his next turn or an enemy hits him. [[Special Attack|Special Attacks]] are [[Prohibited Command|banned]] in this state.

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', the Bros. have their [[Speed (Initiative)|Speeds]] set to 1 rather than getting tripped if attacked/touched from behind. However, certain attacks can still cause tripping.
== In [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] ==
=== ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' ===
As a gameplay mechanic in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' there is a 1% chance of tripping at the start of a dash input and a 1.25% chance of tripping if you do a turn around while running, effectively preventing players from performing dash-dancing. Terrain that lacks traction increases the chances of tripping up to 5% for icy surfaces. Several of the fighters' attacks may cause their opponent to trip.

=== ''Super Smash Bros. 4'' ===
The tripping mechanic in ''[[Super Smash Bros. 4]]'' no longer occurs when players perform a dash or running-turnaround. The terrain's lack of traction no longer causes tripping. However, fighter attacks can still cause opponents to trip.

=== ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' ===
The tripping in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' has no changes from the game's predecessor.
