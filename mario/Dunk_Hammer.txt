'''Dunk Hammer''' is an overworld [[Action Command]] in ''[[Mario and Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. In the former version, it is named '''Luigi Dunk'''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
In the overworld, [[Cork and Cask]] taught the Mario brothers this technique. [[Mario]] can slam his hammer onto Luigi's head to dunk him underground. From there Luigi can go underneath metal fences. When pressing {{Button|GBA|B}}, Luigi will pop out of the ground again. This technique is used to dig up types of beans. The technique is used in the battle with the [[Chopper Bros.]] attack.

==Trivia==
*A similar move called [[Mole Mario]] can be performed in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', but this time, Luigi buries Mario.
