==Appearance==
Bleu is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that is blue in color. He also wears a purple beret.

==History==
Bleu is an artist that lives in [[Flopside]] with his daughter [[Lily (Super Paper Mario)|Lily]]. Bleu speaks in a French accent. Bleu can also change [[Princess Peach]]'s hairstyles but they turn back to normal once she leaves the area.

==Trivia==
*His name means "Blue" in English which describes his appearance.
*Bleu's [[Flipside]] counterpart is [[Sacre]] which, together, makes a famous French phrase, ''Sacre bleu!''
