The '''Purity Heart''' is an artifact created by [[Merlimbis]] in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' in order to counteract the [[Chaos Heart]]. The Purity Heart is created to be the embodiment of love, the only thing believed to stop the Chaos Heart. According to [[Merlumina]], the Purity Heart was divided into eight [[Pure Heart]]s in order to protect them from evil. The Pure Hearts were taken to different parts of the meta-universe. Once the heroes of light collected all the Pure Hearts, they would combine them to make the Purity Heart, thus shattering the Chaos Heart and stopping [[The Void]].
