The '''Move-It Dunk''' is [[Wario]]'s [[Special Shot]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
To have Wario perform the Move-It Dunk, the player must tap out an uppercase "W," an [[emblem]] often associated with Wario, on the Touch screen twice.

When Wario initiates the move, he dribbles the ball roughly in a W shape, and a small burst of energy occurs each time the ball hits the ground. A blue "W" appears and Wario rushes toward the basket with smoke trailing behind him. He jumps twice, bounces back on his bottom (in the manner of a [[Ground Pound]]), flips in the air and dunks the ball in with fiery streaks shooting out, and a sound like a vase breaking.

If shot outside the three-point line, instead of rushing toward the basket, Wario bounces the ball, jumps and throws the ball with one hand toward the basket. The ball takes on a yellow glow as it goes toward the basket. After a teammate dunks the ball, the streaks appear.
