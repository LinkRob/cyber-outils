'''Dark Koopa''' is a purple-shelled variant of [[Koopa Troopa]] featured as an enemy in both ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and its sequel, ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]].''

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
Dark Koopas are found in only one room of [[Toad Town Tunnels]]. During battle, Dark Koopas will attack just like a normal [[Koopa Troopa]], however, they tend to use an attack with a dizzying move. When Dark Koopas use the dizzying move, it will paralyze [[Mario]] for 2 turns. A Dark Koopa will use its special dizzying attack if it is running while in combat.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Dark Koopas only appear in levels 31 through 40 of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. They lose their trademark ability to make Mario dizzy, making them a much less formidable foe. The game uses the previously unused aerial version of this enemy, the [[Dark Paratroopa]] as it was only a beta element of ''Paper Mario''.
