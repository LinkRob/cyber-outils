'''Squizzard''' is a reptile-like character who appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' as the boss of [[Slipsand Galaxy]].

==Physical description==
He has small arms complete with thick wrists and small hands with only three fingers on each (complete with brown, sharp nails), round eyes with brown eyebrows, a Mohawk-like rock attached to its head, and a two-toothed beak resembling that of a [[Koopa Troopa]]'s.

==Battle==
To defeat it, [[Mario]] needs to grab a [[Fire Flower]] whilst dodging his attacks, and upon the opening of his mouth, shoot it. To attack he throws balls of sand then spiky red and yellow balls then he summons two cannons (Replaced in [[Graffiti]]) when the cannons are destroyed he will throw two bombs.
