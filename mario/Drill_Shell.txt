}}The '''Drill Shell''' is a new [[Bros. Attack]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. Being a [[Special Attack]], it costs five [[Special Point]]s (BP) to perform. [[Luigi]] is its initiator. Unlike most of his Bros. Attacks, this move's target is a single foe.

==Procedure==
Luigi starts the attack by putting the titular item  on his head. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] then picks up Luigi and then dunks him into the target foe. Luigi then has to spin the drill on his head to grind the enemy up to deal further damage.

==Controls==
During the first phase, press A to slam down as the Bros. pass over their target. The second phase is initiated when Luigi slams down. Mash B to continue dealing more damage.

==Ratings==
If he was slightly off target, Luigi will start with an Okay! rating. If he was on-target, the move starts with a Good! rating. Getting 10 hits increases the rating by one level and 20 hits increases the rating by two levels.
