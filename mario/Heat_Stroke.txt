'''Heat Stroke''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' and ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''. The name comes from the name of {{wp|heat stroke|a medical condition}} in which someone's body temperature is dangerously high.

==Opening==
The camera pans in on the area of the minigame, which is on a sun-like star in outer space. The trio drops in on the tower, and the minigame begins.

==Gameplay==
The solo player (who is suspended with balloons tied around their body) is given a [[hammer]] that is to be used to knock the platforms away one at a time. When the solo player knocks a platform away, the trio must [[jump]] to avoid going offscreen with it. The solo player can also fake the swing, tricking the team players into jumping too soon. A team player is eliminated if they go offscreen with a platform or fall onto the sun-like star. Over time, [[Podoboo]]s fall, knocking out any team player who touches them. The solo player wins if they knock out all three team players. The team players win if any survive for 30 seconds and/or get to the final platform.

==Ending==
In ''Mario Party 5'', the solo player's hammer disappears. If the solo player wins, they wave at the screen. If the team of three wins, the solo player's balloons pop, making them fall and get burned by the sun-like star, while the players of the team of three remaining do their winning poses.
