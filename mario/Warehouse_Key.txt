The '''Warehouse Key''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
The Warehouse Key can only be obtained during the "Penguin Murder Mystery" case in [[Shiver City]]. The Warehouse Key is found at the bottom of the frozen lake and [[Mario]] must [[Spin Jump]] the lake three times to break it. Then, Mario must ride on [[Sushie]] and dive underwater to obtain the Warehouse Key. Mario can then use the Warehouse Key to unlock the nearby warehouse which eventually leads to [[Herringway]]'s secret room next door.

==Trivia==
*If Mario attempts to break the icy lake to get the Warehouse Key before starting the "Penguin Murder Mystery" case, the [[Penguin Police Chief]] will kick him back to the main area and the frozen lake will be fixed.
*The Warehouse Key shares its sprite with the [[Odd Key]], the [[Prison Key]], and the [[Storeroom Key]].
