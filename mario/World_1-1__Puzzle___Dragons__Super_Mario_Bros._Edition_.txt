'''World 1-1''' is the first [[level]] of ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. This is the very first stage the player will play, and serves as the main tutorial on how to play the game.

== Encounters ==
Note: Enemies listed in bold will always appear, while enemies that are italicized only have a chance of appearing.

* Encounter 1: '''1 [[Goomba]]'''
* Encounter 2: '''1 Green [[Koopa Troopa]],''' ''1 Goomba''
* Encounter 3: '''1 Green Koopa Troopa, 1 Goomba'''
* Encounter 4 (Boss): '''3 Goombas'''

== Items ==
* '''[[Orb (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|Orbs]]''': Fire, Water, Wood and Heart
* '''[[? Block]] Items''': None.
