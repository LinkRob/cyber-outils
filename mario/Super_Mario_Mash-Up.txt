'''''Super Mario Mash-Up Pack''''' (also known as '''''Minecraft: Super Mario Edition''''') is a free game update which was released on May 17, 2016, exclusive to Nintendo versions of '''Minecraft'''. The mash-up pack comes with a huge ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-themed map and ''Mario''-related skins.
