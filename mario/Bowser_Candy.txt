The '''Bowser Candy''' is a blue [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. This item has a picture of a [[Bowser]] icon on the wrapper. When a character uses it, they transform into [[Bowser]] (complete with a Bowser head, a zipper on the back of the shell, and eyes similar to those of [[Bowser Jr.]] and the [[Koopa Kid]]s from past [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' games]]). The Candy allows the player to roll two [[Dice Block]]s. However, rolling matching numbers will not award a [[coin]] bonus, unlike with [[Twice Candy]]. While in the suit, the player steals two [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s from any opponents they run into. This Candy is unique to only the board [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]]. It normally costs 20 coins if bought at a [[Candy Shop]] (15 in Star Battle Arena), but it will cost only 10 coins if the shop is selling Candies at reduced prices.
