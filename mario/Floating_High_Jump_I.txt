'''Floating High Jump I''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It showcases the [[Floating High Jump]] badge.
==Layout==
The level features many [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[pipe]]s, both of which are usually used for the player to jump over. The level opens with tall pipes with piranha plants the player jumps over to progress. The level transitions to the same idea put the pipes being laid across, and the player must scale the level by jumping on each. Finally, the player must traverse large piranha plants and pipes.
==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s can be obtained in the following order:
#Shortly after the {{lw|? Block}}, the player must jump off of the edge of the following pipe at at maximum height to obtain a 10-flower coin.
#Before the section with pipes laid across, there is a 10-flower coin floating above piranha plants. The player must jump on the small platforms below to be able to reach the 10-flower coin.
#After the [[Checkpoint Flag]], there is a 10-flower coin above a piranha plant. The player must jump off the edge of the previous pipe at maximum height to reach a 10-flower coin.
