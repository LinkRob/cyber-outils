The '''Halloween Tour''' was the twenty-ninth tour of ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', which began on October 21, 2020, and ended on November 3, 2020. It was the first tour to have the same title as a previous tour, while also being the first tour to introduce a course from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', namely [[Maple Treeway]], which acted as the signature course of the tour.
