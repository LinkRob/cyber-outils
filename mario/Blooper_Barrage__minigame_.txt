'''Blooper Barrage''' is a Boss minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. In "Solo" mode, [[Blooper]] was introduced by [[Bowser]] in [[Blooper Beach]].

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the players are on a ship, and they just have to aim the cannon at Blooper, worth +1 point. Beware of the hazardous urchins he throws. Whatever character marks on the corner of square of [[Urchin]], hit the urchin if it is aiming at the player; getting hit takes -1 point. Blooper does slow performance tricks, so hit him as fast as the player can.

When Blooper loses half of his lives, he can squirt his ink on screen; making it harder for players to aim or easier to get hit with urchins. He can even also throws two urchins at once and does quick trick performance.
