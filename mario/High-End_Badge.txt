The '''High-End Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the High-End Badge can be obtained from [[Beanbean Fields]]. Equipping it to either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] increases their [[Bros. Point|BP]] by 12. The High-End Badge also grants the [[POW-Up]] special effect.

In the remake, the High-End Badge is given to the brothers by a [[Monty Mole]] in Beanbean Fields. It now increases BP by 15 and additionally [[Power|POW]] by 50 percent. The High-End Badge can be sold for only three [[coin]]s.
