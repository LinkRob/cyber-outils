==History==
Doe T. is a male [[Toad (species)|Toad]] that can only be found in [[Boggly Woods]] during his [[Trouble Center|trouble]] after it has been accepted. Doe T. wanted help getting to [[The Great Tree]] but enemies were blocking the way. [[Mario]] must defeat all enemies in the area while escorting Doe T. to The Great Tree. After doing so, Doe T. rewards Mario with 20 [[coin]]s. Doe T. remains outside of The Great Tree for the remainder of the game.
