'''Totsumen''' (トツメン, meaning "clash face") are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are red creatures that wear blue masks with knives protruding out from the top.

===Behavior===
Whenever a Totsumen spots [[Wario]], it points its knife at him and charges in his direction at full speed. If Wario gets stabbed by its knife, he takes damage.
