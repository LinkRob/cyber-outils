'''Cher''' is a real-world actress and singer who was fictionally portrayed in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Cher's Poochie]]," her only appearance in the show. Cher has a pet dog named [[Poochie]]. Cher is not portrayed by herself but rather [[Pam Matteson]].
