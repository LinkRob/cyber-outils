'''Blue Spike Top''' is a variant of [[Spike Top]] featured exclusively in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and its sequels.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Blue Spike Top looks like a regular Spike Top except it has a light blue body and a dark blue shell with a relatively similar color to that of a [[Buzzy Shell]] but less dark.

===Behavior===
Blue Spike Tops move around like regular Spike Tops but at a faster speed.

==In the games==
In ''Super Mario Maker'', a Blue Spike Top can be created by shaking a Spike Top while in [[Course Maker]]. Similarly, in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', a Spike Top can be changed to a Blue Spike Top in Course Maker by touching it for a while.
