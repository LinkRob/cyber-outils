'''Primids''' are an enemy species in [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]] of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' working for [[Tabuu]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Primids are green robot enemies with red eyes, black arms, black legs, and a red feather on their head. They wear a pair of thick, light-brown gloves and matching light-brown shoes. Primids are formed out of [[Shadow Bug]]s, which are seen emitting from their back.

===Behavior===
A Primid's only attacks involve punching, although Primids have several variants that have more capabilities.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
Primids are the main troops of the [[Subspace Army]], let alone some of the first enemies encountered in Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary. They are first fought on the [[Midair Stadium]] after the battle between [[Mario]] and [[Kirby]] has concluded.

A Primid is also featured as one of the collectible [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophies]]. A Primid is also seen in a trophy of a [[Big Primid]] as a size comparison.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
A Primid makes a cameo as one of the primary [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirits]].
