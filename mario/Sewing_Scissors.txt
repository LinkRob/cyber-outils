The '''Sewing Scissors''' (known as the <strong>Sewing Clippers</strong> in Europe) are [[Stickers#Thing Stickers|Thing Stickers]] seen in the game ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

== Description ==
The Sewing Scissors are small scissors that are mainly silver in color. The background is orange and only takes one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].

== Notes ==
The Sewing Scissors serve little purpose as it is usually out shadowed by the regular [[Scissors]] sticker. It can be used during the final fight with [[Bowser]] to cut up the [[Whomp]] making it useful thanks to the little room it takes up in the Sticker Album.
