'''Suction Destruction''' is a [[Precision (primary weapon)|Precision]] weapon that resembles a sniper rifle based on a [[Poltergust 5000 (object)|Poltergust 5000]]. [[Luigi]] is the only hero that can use the Suction Destruction. The weapon appears in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:MRKB Suction Destruction screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Suction Destruction in game.]]
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'', Suction Destruction resembles a sniper rifle based on a Poltergust 5000 from [[Luigi's Mansion (series)|''Luigi's Mansion'' series]]. Despite having low cover damage, the user can attack with a long range. The Suction Destruction deals around 35 to 45 damage.
