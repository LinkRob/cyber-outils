The '''Trial Galaxies''' are the three challenge galaxies in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. The Trial Galaxies are unlocked when all three [[Green Power Star]]s are obtained. They are accessed from a small planetoid orbiting the [[Comet Observatory]].
