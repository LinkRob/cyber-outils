'''Ballistic Beach''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

== Opening ==
The captain shoots a [[cannonball]] out at the sea, hitting neither of the nearby beach ball-like balloons. The screen then splits into four to show each of the competitors.

==Gameplay==
Each character has to try and be first to pop both of their beach ball-like balloons by launching cannonballs from their [[cannon]] (which has the appearance of [[Warp Cannon]]s in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''). The left and right buttons on {{button|wii|Pad}} can be used to aim horizontally, and the up and down buttons on {{button|wii|Pad}} can be used to control distance. Only one cannonball can be launched at a time. 

In [[Time Attack (Mario Party 9)|Time Attack]], the object is to hit five balloons. There is a bonus objective in which the balloons must be hit in ten shots or fewer.

==Controls==
* {{Button|Wii|Padleftright}} - Move cannon
* {{Button|Wii|2}} - Fire

==Flavor text==
