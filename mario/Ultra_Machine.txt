'''Ultra Machine''' is [[9-Volt]]'s boss [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''

==Gameplay==
The player has to hit enough baseballs out of the ten in stock to pass the microgame: six balls in level 1; seven balls in level 2; and eight balls in level 3. [[Mario]] appears as a referee at the start and end.
