|fractor=Pascal Casanova}}'''Captain Orion''' is an NPC in ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope''. He is the warden of Pristine Peaks.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Captain Orion is a large and burly Rabbid. He wears a large and dark blue overcoat with yellow epaulets. Orion also has a purple scarf, a captain's hat and big orange mustache.
=== Personality traits ===
Despite his big, strong and tough appearance, Captain Orion is a kind, caring, protective and polite Rabbid. He became on friendly terms with the Heroes quite quickly. Orion also happens to be very commanding, as he commands the heroes to go inside the Winter Palace, along with himself, although it's to save his daughter.   

=== Powers and abilities ===
Captain Orion lacks any sort of special skills, but is shown to be a great traveler. He does lack navigation skills.

== Biography ==
As mentioned above, Captain Orion is a traveler who had escaped from Cursa's wrath, and rescued Rabbid Rosalina. As a result, he became her father figure. He wasn't originally supposed to be at Pristine Peaks, but is very stingy towards buying fuel. Because of this and his lack of navigation skill, they crash-landed at Pristine Peaks, with Captain Orion becoming the warden in the process. Rabbid Rosalina wandered inside of the Winter Palace, and was captured by Midnite. Captain Orion tried to rescue her, but Midnite kicked him out of the Winter Palace.

== In ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'' ==
After the heroes end the eternal winter, they make their way to the Winter Palace to stop another Darkmess Tentacle. At the entrance, the heroes find Captain Orion, frozen in a block of ice, who was trying to enter the Winter Palace. He then asks the heroes to head inside with him and help save Rabbid Rosalina, with Orion joining them.

While exploring the Winter Palace, the group encounters a ghost Rabbid, named Midnite, one of Cursa's Spark Hunters. As she taunts the heroes, she destroys Rabbid Rosalina's knit work, which worries Captain Orion to no end. When the group reaches the top, Midnite speaks surprisingly polite to Edge and then telling the group that she was once friends with Edge, much to the surprise of everyone else. Edge wanted to end the discussion. Midnite then enters the Darkmess Tentacle, and the heroes enter as well.

While inside the Darkmess Tentacle, Captain Orion finds Rabbid Rosalina, and he is very happy to see that she is safe. He attempts to reunite with her, but is captured by Midnite, taunting him in the process. When she sees this, Rabbid Rosalina teams with the heroes to defeat Midnite and save Orion. After Midnite is defeated, Captain Orion is freed, and hugs Rabbid Rosalina. She appears to be embarrassed by this. Edge then shows mercy to Midnite, who then leaves. Captain Orion then tasks Rabbid Rosalina to join the heroes on their adventure, who does reluctantly, but still follows his orders.
