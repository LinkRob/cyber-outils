'''Trashures''' are a treasure-chest like enemy encountered in [[Dimble Wood]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

They also have a subspecies called [[Dark Trashure]]s which are found in the [[Fawful]]-conquered version of [[Princess Peach's Castle]].

== Description ==
Trashures are brown treasure chests with grey rims and eyes on their lid. The golden lock keeping them shock has four arms which grab onto the "mouth."

==Etymology==
Trashure's is a portmanteau of "trash" and "treasure," also referencing the saying "One man's trash is another man's treasure."

==Battle==
Trashures attack by throwing objects at [[Bowser]] with them being a [[cannonball]], a [[Scutlet]] or a golden [[hammer]]. Bowser must counter the items by punching it in the air so it may land on the Trashure.

Sometimes, Trashures throw out [[coin]]s or a [[Mushroom]] which heals 20 HP of Bowser. If Bowser punches a coin, however, it doesn't land on Trashure, but if he does it to a Mushroom, the Trashure has 20 HP of it healed.

When the Trashure is close to fainting, the lock on the enemy's mouth breaks open and multiple [[coin]]s are then found inside. Bowser can then either [[inhale]] all the coins or just continue attacking in order to defeat the Trashure. Trashures are one of the many range of enemies that are partially resistant to [[Fire (element)|fire]], only taking little damage from it despite their wooden body. Trashures also flee a lot during battle, more than likely when they are alone.

[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can battle this enemy in a similar fashion by simply defeating the enemy and can also counter its attacks with their jump. They cannot gain any [[coin]]s from this enemy's critical state unless it throws some at them (except from the loot drop).
