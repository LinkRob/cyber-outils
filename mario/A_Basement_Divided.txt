"'''A Basement Divided''' is the fifty-third live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. In syndication, it was paired alongside the episode "[[Princess, I Shrunk the Mario Brothers]]."

==Synopsis==
An argument brews between [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] and escalates to the point of them having to call a psychotherapist named [[Doc Freud]].

==Trivia==
*The episode's name is a pun on the phrase "a house divided."
