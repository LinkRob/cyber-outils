'''Hyper Goombas''' are an enemy in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and return in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Hyper Goombas are a hyperactive, green variant of [[Goomba]]s.

===Behavior===
Hyper Goombas have the uncanny ability to charge their attack power up from their normal 2, to a deadly 6. Their hit point value is also much higher than a regular Goomba's too.

== In {{series|Paper Mario}} ==
===''Paper Mario''===
Hyper Goombas appear in the third chapter, but only in the second half, at [[Gusty Gulch]]. Along the road to [[Tubba Blubba's Castle]], [[Mario]] encounters this enemy as well as its evolved forms, the [[Hyper Spiky Goomba]] and [[Hyper Paragoomba]]. A few Hyper Goombas could also be found in [[Windy Mill]].

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
Hyper Goombas' appearance and abilities don't change. However, they are more powerful in both their hit points and attack strength. To compensate this increase, the Hyper line of enemies don't appear until chapter 4 of the game, rather than chapter 3. They can found on the [[Twilight Trail]].
