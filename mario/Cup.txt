A '''cup''' represents a series of a few courses in the {{series|Mario Kart}}, usually four. The first four cups appear in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'', including [[Mushroom Cup]], [[Flower Cup]], [[Star Cup]] and [[Special Cup]], the most recurring cups in the series. Aside from the ''Mario Kart'' games, cups have also appeared in some other ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' spinoffs, such as the {{series|Mario Tennis}}, {{series|Mario Strikers}}, and ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]'', in which they instead represent a group of sports stadiums to be played on.
