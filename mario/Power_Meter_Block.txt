A '''Power Meter Block''' is a block used in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It appears in a single level of [[World-e]]: [[Castle Dash]]. When [[Mario]] is standing on a Boost Block, his [[P-Meter]] charges a lot quicker, allowing him to start running in a smaller space than usual.
