'''Crazy Kamek''', named '''Switch Kamek''' in PAL releases, is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
When a character uses Crazy Kamek, [[Kamek]] appears and uses his magic to switch the user's position with an opponent selected through a roulette wheel. Crazy Kamek is functionally similar to the [[Warp Pipe]] and its variants from previous {{series|Mario Party}} installments.

Crazy Kamek has a more powerful variation named [[Chaos Kamek]], which switches the positions of every character.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
Crazy Kamek is one of the items exclusive to [[Perilous Palace Path]], despite Kamek having his own board, [[Kamek's Carpet Ride]]. A Crazy Kamek can be obtained either from being handed out by [[Yellow Toad]] when a character is near the [[finish line]] or by winning a minigame in first place and choosing Crazy Kamek as a prize.
