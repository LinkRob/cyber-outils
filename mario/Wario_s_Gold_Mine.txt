'''Wario's Gold Mine''' is a track in the game ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. It is the last track of the [[Flower Cup]]. It is themed on a Gold Mine. There are no railings for most of the track. There are also Gold Mine Cars which make racers flip over. This track belongs to [[Wario]].

==Shortcuts==
When on a turn where the Gold Mine cars and a Wario sign are, keep straight and go with the GM cars. There are [[Boost Pad]]s and it is shorter than the actual way. Tip: when about to finish on final lap do not use the boost pad as you can finish quicker because the boost up helps after the finish line.

==''Mario Kart 8''==
[[File:MK8 Wario'sGoldMine.png|left|thumb]]
Wario's Gold Mine appears in The Legend of Zelda DLC of ''Mario Kart 8''. It is the first track in the [[Triforce Cup]]. The graphics have been overhauled greatly. Most significantly, the track section inside the mine has become an anti-gravity segment. Additionally, hitting the mine carts now causes an anti-gravity boost rather than spin you out. The [[Shy Guy]]s cheer in the game.
