'''Smash Charge''' is a [[badge]] which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
The Smash Charge allows Mario to charge up his [[Hammer]] for an additional cost of 1 [[Flower Point|FP]] per charge. Smash Charge is ideal for beginners, and since its predecessor, the [[S. Smash Chg.]] badge, costs more FP to use, the Smash Charge can more helpful.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Smash Charge is found in [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]], where it is guarded by a [[Bob-omb]]. This fight can be avoided, and the badge is on the pedestal on the western tower at the very top. [[Mario]] obtains this badge near the end of the chapter as it is in an area that is only accessible later in the fortress.
