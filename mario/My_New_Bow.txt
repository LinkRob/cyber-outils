'''My New Bow''' is one of [[Mona]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''.

==Gameplay==
The girl has to keep the boy infatuated by having her bow stay up on top of her hair. The girl's bow slides down her hair twice during the microgame, and the player has to use their [[Stylus]] to tap the bow so that it goes back up her hair. If the bow is on top of the girl's hair when the timer runs out, the player wins. However, if the bow is not on top, the player loses at the microgame.
