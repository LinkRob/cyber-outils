'''Blow Away Chain Chomp!''' is a [[minigame|mini-game]] played in [[Blizland]] in the [[Adventure Tours]] mode of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for the [[Nintendo DS]]. Completing the minigame is required to bypass a [[Chain Chomp]] obstructing the path.

==Gameplay==
The playable character is [[Wario]], who has to make a [[Chain Chomp]] fall off a square-shaped platform within 45 seconds. To do so, Wario has to use his [[Wario Waft]] ability, which pushes back Chain Chomp, and he must continue to do so until the Chain Chomp falls off the platform. If Chain Chomp touches Wario, he flies off in a very short distance. If the time expires or Wario falls off the stage, Wario loses at the mini-game, but once Chain Chomp falls off the platform, Wario wins.
