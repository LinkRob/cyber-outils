'''Antasmans''' are enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Antasmans are similar in appearance to [[Antasma]]. They also resemble wizards.

===Behavior===
In battle, the Antasmans can heal themselves if they are linked together. They also possess other attacks (all conjectural names) including:

*'''Spell Barrage:''' The Antasmans group together and attempt to ram Mario (fused with [[Dreamy Luigi]]) with their spells pointing upwards. Mario must jump over the Antasman not firing a spell upward to avoid this attack.
*'''Spell Shot:''' The Antasmans charge up their wands and attempt to hit Mario with it. However, if Mario avoids this attack and if there are other Antasmans in range on the other side, the spell with hit them and inflict damage.
*'''Spell Ball:''' The Antasmans will appear in the background and attempt to drop a large, spell ball onto Mario. Mario must hammer it back at them to avoid damage and inflict damage onto them. If Mario fails, Mario will take major damage, in Hard Mode, it's very devastating.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
Antasmans appear only in  [[Dreamy Neo Bowser Castle]], where they are fought in groups. An Antasman can be easily dealt with using a [[Luiginary Attack]], such as the [[Luiginary Ball]] or even [[Luiginary Wall]].
