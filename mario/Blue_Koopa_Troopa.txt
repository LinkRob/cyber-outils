'''Blue Koopa Troopas''' are a color variation of [[Koopa Troopa]]s which first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Blue Koopa Troopas have the same general appearance as Koopa Troopas in general, except they wear a blue [[Koopa Shell]], specifically a [[Blue Shell]].

===Behavior===
In ''Super Mario World'', if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] knocks a Blue Koopa Troopa out of its shell, it becomes a [[Beach Koopa]], but unlike other types, the blue Beach Koopas do not return into their shell but instead try to kick it at Mario or Luigi. They will still attempt to kick a shell, even if it does not belong to them.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario World''===
If [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] eats a Blue Koopa Troopa, he grows [[wing]]s and can fly for a short period of time (or until he spits out the Koopa shell).

===''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''===
Blue Koopa Troopas are very uncommon, to the extent that only one appear in the Ice stage of the [[Mario vs. Luigi]] mode. By attacking the Koopa Troopa, Mario or Luigi can enter its [[Blue Shell]] to become their [[Shell Mario|Shell]] form.
