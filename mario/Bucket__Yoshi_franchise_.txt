'''Buckets''' are an object featured in several ''[[Yoshi (series)|Yoshi]]'' platformers, especially the ''[[Yoshi's Island (series)|Yoshi's Island]]'' series, and first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Description==
Buckets are attached to red pole-like objects hanging from walls or the ceiling. Most of them appear in [[castle]] or [[fortress]] levels. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can shoot an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at a bucket for it to fall off, with some buckets releasing five [[coin]]s upon falling off. Sometimes a bucket is found hanging above [[lava]], and by shooting it into the lava, a Yoshi can use it as a makeshift boat to travel across harmlessly. 

In the ''Yoshi's Island'' series, Buckets have a stone gray appearance with a question mark (possibly a reference to [[? Block]]s) and a black handle hanging from a red pole. In ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', buckets have a more wooden appearance. In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', there are also yellow Buckets.
