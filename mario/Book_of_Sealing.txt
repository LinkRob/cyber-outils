The '''Book of Sealing''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Physical description==
The Book of Sealing looks like a small, brown book with a picture of a Boo on the cover. It is also about the same size as Mario's Sticker Album. It has gold lining around the near edge.

==In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''==
The Book of Sealing is able to capture and seal away Boos. The [[Mansion Steward]] gives Mario the Book of Sealing to capture all 100 of the Boos that were let loose in his mansion. Mario is able to do so but Kamek shows up and uses his magic to turn all 100 Boos into one Big Boo. Mario defeats the Big Boo and the Steward's mansion is ghost free.

==Trivia==
*The Book of Sealing is similar to the [[Poltergust 3000]] from ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' in that both are used to capture Boos in a mansion.
