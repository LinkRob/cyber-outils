'''Wiggler''' is a character that appears in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Super Mario'' series]]. He is a caterpillar-like creature who appears residing in [[Gloomy Woods]]. 

== History ==

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'' ===
[[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] find the Wiggler famished in Gloomy Woods and feed him a [[berry]], replenishing his energy. [[Starlow]] discovers that Paper Kamek used his magic to create piles of trash to block their path. Wiggler, outraged that someone took advantage of his weak state and made a mess of his forest, becomes mad and smashes the rubbish in front of him allowing the trio to progress. Later, the trio finds Wiggler with no energy again, Wiggler cannot move unless they feed him 10 berries. After the trio feed him the berries, Wiggler tells them he has a craving for a dessert. Coincidentally, a [[Yellow Yoshi]] declares he has found a melon, which Wiggler explains is one of his most favorite desserts, and also refuses to move from the spot he is until they feed him his treat.

The trio asks the Yoshi for the melon, but he says no, rather challenging them to a race with the melon as the winning prize. When Mario, Luigi, and Paper Mario beat him, he hands over the melon, which they feed to Wiggler. Wiggler realizes Paper Kamek left more piles of trash and smashes through them. The three help Wiggler by disabling the electric fences [[Bowser]] and his henchmen use to block the path with.

The trio spot Wiggler confronting [[Paper Kamek]], when he uses his magic to make Wiggler big and hostile, [[Paper Kamek]] then taunts Mario, Luigi, and Paper Mario telling them that Wiggler can’t hear then anymore, Paper Kamek then tells Wiggler that Mario, Luigi, and Paper Mario are the real villains and to attack them. The Bros. battle Wiggler and Paper Kamek. After they win, Paper Kamek flies away while Wiggler, battered and bruised, apologizes saying he didn't know what got into him, he thanks the trio for helping him and for all the berries, and passes out, seemingly dead, shortly being carried off into the light by [[Paragoomba]]s. Mario, Luigi, Paper Mario, and Starlow mourn over the loss of Wiggler, but then hear a voice, it was Wiggler, alive, but now in the form of a [[Flutter|butterfly]] named Flutter, Flutter offers to take them to the peak of [[Mount Brrr]]. He remains in this form for the rest of the game and from this point forward whenever the trio speaks to Flutter, he will offer to take them to [[Mount Brrr]], up on the mountain, they can speak to him again to return to [[Gloomy Woods]]. Sometime later, Flutter was present during a [[parade]].

==Trivia==
*This Wiggler is very similar to the [[Wiggler (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|Wiggler]] from ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
**Both tend to speak in the third person.
**Both serve as necessary allies to [[Mario]] during his journey in the forest and ask for some form of help (getting rid of the [[Poisoned Water|Poison]] for the former and clearing away trash for the latter).
**Both have some form of battle although the former was never battled directly but his [[Wiggler Segment|mischievous segments]] were.
**Both transform into a [[Flutter]] later on and help Mario reach a higher, previously unreachable area, [[Bowser Jr.'s Flotilla]] for the former and [[Mount Brrr]] for the latter.
*This Wiggler is also very similar to the [[Wiggler (Mario Party DS)|Wiggler]] from ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. However, while the Wiggler from that game did serve as allies to Mario and his friends, he never passed out nor transformed into a Flutter.
*This is a third time a [[Wiggler|Wiggler species]] helps out the heroes rather than fight them, the other previous two being ''[[Mario Party DS]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
*The [[Paragoomba|Paragoombas]] carrying Wiggler into the light is a nod to how angels are said to carry someone who’s died to Heaven further alluding that Wiggler had seemingly died until he returned as Flutter.
