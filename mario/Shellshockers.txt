|games   = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}The {{Nihongo|'''Shellshockers'''|ザ・ウラウラン|Za Urauran}} (also known as the {{Nihongo|'''Turtles of Hurtle'''|ブレーキのこわれたせんすいかん|Burēki no Kowareta Sensuikan}}) is a team of the [[Glitz Pit]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Shellshockers are team lead by [[Shellshock]] and consist of two [[Shady Koopa]]s (with Shellshock being one of them) and a single [[Shady Paratroopa]]. They are considered a much stronger version of the [[KP Koopa]]s. The Shellshockers place 7th in the Glitz Pit making them one of the stronger teams of the Glitz Pit.
