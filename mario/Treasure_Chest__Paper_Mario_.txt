The '''Treasure Chest''' (also known as the '''Mysterious Treasure Chest''') is an object which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
There were two of these mysterious treasure chests in the game. One of the treasure chests can be found in [[Princess Peach's Castle]] in the storeroom. The other treasure chest can be found in [[Merluvlee]]'s room of [[Shooting Star Summit]]. The function of these treasure chests is to transport badges and items from Princess Peach's Castle to Shooting Star Summit for Mario to use. Princess Peach can do this anytime by finding a badge or item and placing it into the chest for Mario to pick up later.
