'''Inhale''' is an ability given to [[Bowser]] by [[Broque Monsieur]] after the former helped the latter get to [[Plack Beach]] from the [[File:MLBiS_-_Vacuum_Block_Effect_Screenshot.png|thumb|Bowser using Inhale.]]island Broque was stranded on. The ability is given to him in the form of the [[Vacuum Block]]. In battle, Bowser can use it to inhale small enemies and even items, including, but not limited to:
*Lollypops (from [[Chuboomba]]s) (heals 20 HP, but causes enemy to run away crying, gaining Bowser No experience
*Nut (from [[Treevil]]s) (Heals 20 HP)
