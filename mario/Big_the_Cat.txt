'''Big the Cat''' is a character from the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' franchise. He made his first overall appearance in ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Adventure|Sonic Adventure]]'', but Big's first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance is in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]''.

==In {{series|Mario & Sonic}}==
===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'' (Wii)===
Big the Cat appears as a referee for the [[Athletics]] and [[Rowing]] events. He can also be seen behind the podium in the Athletics events during the awards ceremony.

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' (Wii)===
Big the Cat appears as a VIP Member, and along with [[Birdo]] is the only character to give the player a costume as well as 100 Star Points. There is also an unlockable [[Mii]] costume of Big the Cat.

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' ([[Nintendo DS]])===
Big plays a role in [[Adventure Tours]], where he can be found in [[Polastraits]]. Here, Big wonders what is the "sign" near him. [[Amy Rose|Amy]] tries to explain to Big that each mission's details is written there, although because Big does not understand. When Amy completes Big's mission, taking place in [[Alpine Skiing GS]], he understands what Amy was trying to tell him, and rewards the party with a [[Spark Luge]].

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' (Wii)===
Big only makes a cameo appearance as an unlockable Mii costume.

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' ([[Nintendo 3DS]])===
Big has a role in Story mode. In the episode [[To the Ruins!]], Big is searching for [[Froggy]]. [[E-123 Omega|Omega]] is launched at Big by [[Magikoopa]]'s magic, restoring some of Omega's memory. [[Toad (character)|Toad]] thanks Big, and he the group leaves to [[London]]. After this, Big manages to find Froggy, and the pair start making their own way to London.

There is an unlockable badge of Big the Cat that can be obtained from the badge machine.

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Wii U)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' ([[Wii U]])===
Big can be found on [[Copacabana Beach]], in front of the Pousada.

Just as before, there is another unlockable Mii costume of Big.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==

Big makes a cameo in the form of a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]].

===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''===
Big the Cat makes another cameo, this time as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]].
