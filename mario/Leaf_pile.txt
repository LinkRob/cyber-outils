'''Leaf piles''' are recurring objects in the {{franchise|Mario}}.

==Description==
Leaf piles are similar to their real-world counterparts, but leaf piles in the ''Mario'' franchise usually contain an item that can be revealed from it; for instance, in [[Maple Treeway]] from the {{series|Mario Kart}}, driving into a leaf pile can sometimes reveal a [[Dash Mushroom]].
