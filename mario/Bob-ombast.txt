'''Bob-ombast''' is a special move used by [[Admiral Bobbery]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It can be used once Bobbery reaches the [[Ultra Rank]]. Using it costs nine [[Flower Point|FP]], making it the second most expensive move of [[Mario]]'s partners, only behind [[Smooch (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|Smooch]].

==Effects==
To use Bob-ombast, the player must repeatedly press {{button|gcn|a}} to fill up the gauge. Bobbery will slowly walk toward the enemies, dealing damage relative to how far the gauge was filled. If the gauge is filled all the way, Bobbery will explode, dealing 8 points of damage to all enemies. This makes Bob-ombast the most powerful move in the game out of [[Mario]]'s partners. However, due to it costing so much FP to use, it should be used sparingly. Bob-ombast can also flip [[Cleft]]s and other shelled enemies, such as [[Parabuzzy|Parabuzzies]].

Admiral Bobbery can also perform a [[Stylish Move]] if the player presses {{button|gcn|A}} the instant he lands on the ground after exploding.

==Trivia==
*Bob-ombast works similar to [[Bombette]]'s [[Mega Bomb]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
