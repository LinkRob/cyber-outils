'''Tail Thwomps''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. They are a variant of [[Thwomp]] with a tail on their back.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Tail Thwomps have the appearance of a regular Thwomp, but with a [[Raccoon Mario|raccoon tail]] on their backside.

=== Behavior === 
A Tail Thwomp jumps high in the air, floats for a short time using its tail, and then slams onto the ground.

==In ''Super Mario 3D Land''==
There are only two Tail Thwomps, and both are encountered in [[World 5-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 5-1]]. [[Mario]] can defeat a Tail Thwomp by slamming onto it as [[Statue Mario]].
