'''World 6-Castle''' is a level from [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. It is the second to final level of World 6.

==Overview==
In this level, there are Spiked Balls and Giant Spiked Balls that can hurt the player, they can also destroy Dry Bones. It is set above a pool of [[lava]] with moving platforms. The castle features two areas, the first area being composed of pendulum platforms that dip into the lava, and the second area being highly infested with Spiked Balls, which drop from the ceiling in vast numbers.

==Walkthrough==
The level begins near a pendulum platform with ? Blocks above it, one of which contains a power-up. More pendulums follow, with [[Podoboo]]s found jumping out of the lava. Dry Bones on a row of Brick Blocks are found above some Podoboos and platforms. More pendulums follow, along with Spike Balls. The midway point is found at the end of the area, along with a Warp Pipe leading to the next section. Through the Warp Pipe, a Podoboo is found, along with a ? Block containing a power-up. Metal platforms are found moving in the lava, with Spike Balls falling from a spiked platform above. A [[Starman]] is found in a Hidden Block above a Brick Block but be sure to hit it before a Spiked Ball destroys the Block. More pendulum platforms are found, along with some [[Super Dry Bones]]. A ? Block containing a power-up is found above a Hidden Block, followed by the boss door.

You will face [[Morton Koopa Jr.]] again in this castle. The room's floor is composed of six stone-like pillars made of metallic carbonite, which Morton uses in his offensive tactics. When [[Kamek]] casts his magic, at first nothing looks different except the carbonite pillars have slightly lowered. When he gets close enough to you, he jumps and Ground Pounds, causing the pillars on opposite sides of him to shoot up to the ceiling, which kills you if you are crushed. To attack Morton, either stomp on him or toss fireballs a time, Morton also slides in his shell when hit, trying to hit you so be sure to jump over his shell each time it comes at you. Unlike his tower battle, Morton does not use his magic and his ground pounds do not stun players who are on the ground. Upon Morton's defeat by the third hit, the key to leave the castle appears.

==[[Star Coin]]s==
* '''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin floats over a row of Brick Blocks with a Dry Bones on every one. The easiest way to obtain it is to get an Ice Flower, freeze a Dry Bones, then throw it across the row of Brick Blocks, killing all of the Dry Bones and getting the Star Coin.
* '''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is floating high over the swinging platform with three ? Blocks and a Spike Ball. Mario and co. must get on top of one of the ? Blocks and jump from it to the Star Coin.
* '''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is just before the door leading to Morton. Mario and co. must go to the right edge of the final swinging platform, wait for the platform to swing all the way to the right, and jump for the Star Coin.
