The '''Diamond City Audience''' are characters who witness the invention match between [[Penny Crygor]] and [[Dr. Crygor]] at the [[Diamond Academy]] in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. The audience members have an entirely black body, and they are noted for their resemblance to [[Mr. Game & Watch]].
