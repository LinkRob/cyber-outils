'''Super Appeal''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
[[Mario]] can wear the Super Appeal by using one [[Badge Point|BP]]. While wearing it, Mario's "appeal" technique is enhanced.

The Super Appeal is found in the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]], where it can be bought for fifty [[coin]]s. Mario can also purchase these badges at the [[Pianta Parlor]] for 34 [[Pianta Token]]s each; Mario can use this to obtain an infinite number of Super Appeal badges.

Super Appeal has a partner-based variation named [[Super Appeal P]].
