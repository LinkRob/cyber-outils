'''Wuhu Loop''' (known as '''Wuhu Island Loop''' in the British English version) is the first course in [[Flower Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. The track takes place in [[Wuhu Island]]. Unlike the traditional rules of racing in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series, this stage (along with [[Maka Wuhu]] and [[Rainbow Road]]) has a start-to-end-point rule. Instead of doing laps, racers have to get through three parts. The first one to cross the finish line at the end-point wins first.
