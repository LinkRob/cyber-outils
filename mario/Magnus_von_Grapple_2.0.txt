'''Magnus Von Grapple 2.0''' is a vehicle built by [[Lord Crump]] and an upgraded version of the [[Magnus von Grapple]]. It is the boss of Chapter 7 in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Physical description==
He is virtually the same as before, but with stronger attacks, a black paint, paper like limbs and large spikes on the mecha's fists. It also as the number 2 on his chest plate.

===Attacks===
Magnus Von Grapple 2.0 possess many strong attacks this time in its arsenal definitely making it one of the strongest and most difficult bosses to defeat especially having 70 [[Heart Point|HP]].

*'''Drill Attack''': 2.0 flies straight up from its position and flies down right onto both Mario and his opponent dealing damage. This attack is pretty tough to guard against due to the speed.
*'''Fist Rockets 2.0''': Like its original counterpart, 2.0 can launch its fist rockets that will then attack Mario and his team if they are not taken out. 2.0 uses this attack a lot.
*'''Audience Cannon''': 2.0 flies into the audience and sucks them up. Then he fires them all at Mario or his Partner dealing 3 damage for it's every 10 hits. That's a devastating 30 damage if this attack is not blocked. The Audience Cannon is not only 2.0's strongest attack, but it is by far the strongest move in the entire game, surpassing even the [[Shadow Queen]]'s Dark Wave attack and [[Bonetail]]'s Megabreath.

==Strategy==
2.0 is very tough to deal with especially with his damage dealing Audience Cannon attack. Koops is a good choice since his move can protect Mario from the attack. With the fist rockets, Mario should easily take them down with a single Multibounce and continue to Power Smash 2.0 until he is defeated. Just be careful as 2.0's Drill Attack can sometimes flip Koops on his back.
