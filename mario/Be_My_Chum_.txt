'''Be My Chum!''' is a 1-vs-3 Mic [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object for the solo player is to use the [[Nintendo GameCube Microphone]] to call forth enemies for the other three players to avoid. If the Mic is not connected, the solo player instead selects the enemy from a window. The four types of enemies include [[Sushi]]s, [[Cheep Cheep]]s, [[Blooper]]s, and [[Lakitu]]s, with the very latter dropping a [[Spiny Egg]] into the water. The group wins if at least one of them manages to avoid every enemy headed their way within the 30-second time limit, but the solo player wins if all three get hit by an enemy.

If the solo player says "[[Goomba]]," a Goomba jumps in the background, although this does not affect gameplay

==Controls==
;Solo
*[[Nintendo GameCube Microphone]]: Say the name of the enemy to call (either [[Lakitu]], [[Cheep Cheep]], [[Blooper]], [[Sushi]], or [[Goomba]])

;Group
*Control Stick: Move
