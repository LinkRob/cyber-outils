'''Excitement Central''' is the first [[world]] in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
Like the later three worlds, Excitement Central is accessed from its own section of [[Treasure Square]]. Because it is the first world, Excitement Central acts as a tutorial for items and moves. Its two [[level|courses]] are [[Greenhorn Forest]], which can be accessed by a giant tree stump in the middle of the area, and [[Greenhorn Ruins]], located in a temple entrance to the left.

Excitement Central is shown to be in a dense forest with tall trees, wooden bridges over lakes and waterfalls, and even a temple. Once [[Wario]] has cleared both courses, he can fight [[DinoMighty]], whom he has to defeat to recover the first piece of the [[Huge Treasure Chest Key]].
