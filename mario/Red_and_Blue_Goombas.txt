'''Red and Blue Goombas''' are a specific pair of [[Goomba]]s who appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
They serve the [[Goomba King]] and are under orders from [[Bowser]] to prevent [[Mario]] and [[Goombario]] from getting to [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. They fail to protect their monarch and later provide a path to Toad Town via detonation of their own fortress.

The Red and Blue Goombas would later send a letter addressed to Goombario telling him that they no longer want to be evil and, instead, want to be friends with Goombario indicating they had a change of leaf. The Red and Blue Goombas would later appear during the parade where they were seen trying to help free the Goomba King from being stuck in a tree. The Goomba King eventually frees himself and walks off along with the Red and Blue Goombas.

In battle, being Goombas, the Red and Blue Goombas attack with [[headbonk]]s. Although their attack power is the same being 1, their HP is not as Red Goomba has 7 HP and Blue Goomba has 6 HP (due to Red Goomba being the oldest). However, during the battle with the Goomba King, both Red and Blue Goomba have 1 HP due to being weakened from their earlier fight.

===Strategy===
Despite fighting a battle where both opponents have way more than 2 HP, this battle is straightforward. If it has been kept from earlier, Mario can use the [[Fire Flower]] to deal 3 points of damage to the Goombas (though it would be better to have Goombario use it since Mario can already hit hard with his [[Power Jump]] badge). It is recommended to focus on taking out Blue Goomba first since he has lower HP then attack Red Goomba. During the second battle, it is very simple to attack the [[Goomnut]] tree which will send a large Goomnut on top of the Goomba King and Red and Blue Goomba's heads taking out the latter two on the first turn.
