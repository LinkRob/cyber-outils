The '''Blast Barrel''', also named '''Auto-Fire Barrel''', is a type of [[Barrel]] from the [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]]. It first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', having mostly gone on to appear in ''Donkey Kong'' games and seldomly appearing within the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games and its spinoffs.
