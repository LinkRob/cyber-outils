{{Conjectural}}[[File:Rljbljctgwns.jpg|thumb|The glitch.]]The '''Backwards Long Jump/BLJ/Reverse Long Jump '''is a glitch and cheat in the [[Nintendo 64]] game, ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Reverse Long Jump==

The player must do an ordinary long jump, do a still long jump, and steer Mario backwards while pressing the A button rather fast. Mario will do a long jump backwards and, if done rapidly like on a staircase or a tight space, will be shot through walls, due to the game not putting a negative cap on the speed from long jumps. This glitch is most famously used to get up the Endless Stairs without 70 stars and to complete the game with no stars. The glitch breaks Mario's speed, explaining how he shoots through walls. This glitch was fixed in the Shindou version, the DS Remake and the 3D All-Stars Remake, saddening speedrunners.
