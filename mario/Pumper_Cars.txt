}}'''Pumper Cars''' is a Duel minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. This minigame's name is a pun of "bumper cars."

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the players are in handcars and must race to the end. The player must use the Wii Remote with some buttons to even move the handcar. The player who reaches the end first wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} (Up) and {{button|wii|a}}{{button|wii|1}} - Move
