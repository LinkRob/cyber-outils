{{Nihongo|'''Dash and Dine'''|キノピオ食堂|Kinopio Shokudō|Toad Dining Hall}} is a [[2 vs. 2 Minigame|2-vs-2]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The minigame sets in a restaurant.

== Opening ==
Teams turn around to the [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s that are serving. The teams turn back towards the screen and the camera zooms out.

== Gameplay ==
In this restaurant, players must grab the ingredients (lettuce, tomato, or bacon) that the Toad needs in order to create the burger. Players earn a point for each burger. A Toad carrying plates, or a pot is walking while unintentionally blocking the player.

== Ending ==
While the Toads applause, the winning team that does their victory animation. In the background, the losing team is seen rushing and panicking while bringing the ingredients.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – Take/place ingredient

==In-game description==
*''"Fetch ingredients to complete the order!"''
*''"If you grab the wrong ingredient by accident, just put it back where you found it!"''

== In [[Challenge Road]] ==
{{Main|Shell Street/Dash and Dine}}
Dash and Dine is the fourth minigame challenge in Shell Street.

== Etymology ==
The name of this minigame is a reversal of "dine and dash", which is to leave a resturaunt without paying.
