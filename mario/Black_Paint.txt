'''Black paint''' is a substance found in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. It is evil and corrupts the mind. It is also highly toxic, and can be formed when mixing the other six colors together. For the majority of the game, the black paint possesses [[Paper Bowser|Bowser]], and turns him into [[Black Bowser]]. If Mario touches it, he loses 30 HP. If he falls into the black paint in [[Black Bowser's Castle]], Mario will get a game over.
