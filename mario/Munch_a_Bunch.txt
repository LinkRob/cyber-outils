'''Munch a Bunch''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' as a microgame under [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[Nature]] folder and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s microgames instead.

==Gameplay==
The player has to repeatedly press the A button so that [[Chomp the Turtle]] can eat a line of two-dimensional shapes lined against his mouth. The ''WarioWare Gold'' iteration sometimes substitutes some of the shapes with a different object, such as a carrot. If Chomp has eaten every object, the player wins at the microgame, but if unsuccessful, they lose.

Levels 1, 2, and 3 feature six, nine, and twelve objects to eat respectively.

==In ''[[Wario World]]''==
The original iteration of Munch a Bunch is one of the microgames that can played from a [[Game Boy Advance]] using a [[Nintendo GameCube Game Boy Advance Link Cable]]. Munch a Bunch is one of seven microgames in [[Mona]]'s "WarioWare, Inc. trial ver. 4" and the only microgame in [[Kat and Ana|Kat]]'s "WarioWare, Inc. selected 4" set; for the latter set, Munch a Bunch is the only microgame included, and it indefinitely loops, but with more shapes added each time it is played through.
