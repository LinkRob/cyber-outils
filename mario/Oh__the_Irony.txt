'''Oh, the Irony''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', as part of [[Ashley and Red]]'s [[Total Drag]] stage, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Kat and Ana]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player has to drag an iron over the wrinkled parts of the clothes. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame, but if this is not accomplished on time, they lose.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level features a different clothing item, each having an increased number of wrinkles to iron than in the previous difficulty level.

===''WarioWare: Touched!''===
*'''Level 1:''' There is a shirt with a few wrinkles.
*'''Level 2:''' There is a t-shirt with several wrinkles.
*'''Level 3:''' There is a pair of pants with many wrinkles.

===''WarioWare Gold''===
*'''Level 1:''' There is a skirt or kimono with a few wrinkles.
*'''Level 2:''' There is a polo or spacesuit with several wrinkles.
*'''Level 3:''' There is a masquerade dress with many wrinkles.

==Trivia==
*The microgame has the same name as the expression "oh, the irony," but in the form of a pun because the microgame involves the use of an iron.
