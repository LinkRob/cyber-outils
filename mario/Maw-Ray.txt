'''Maw-Rays''' are a relative of [[Unagi]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. They look similar to their relatives but differ in several ways, first, their method of attacking is very similar to a [[Gringill]] or a [[Spike Eel]]. Also, unlike Unagis, they never leave their holes. Maw-Rays also have more jagged scales and their bodies aren't striped.

== In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' ==
Maw-Rays appear in several underwater kingdoms in ''Super Mario Odyssey,'' including the [[Lake Kingdom]] and the [[Seaside Kingdom]]. They are also found within the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], where they pop out from the clouds, revealing that they can survive outside of water.

== In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' ==
A single Maw-Ray also makes an appearance in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. They are voiced by Sarah Silverman and Frank Welker. here, it is seen residing in the ocean on the outskirts of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] swallowing [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Blooper]]s. [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] eventually find themselves swallowed by the Maw-Ray after falling from the [[Rainbow Road]], the duo manages to escape after repairing DK's [[Rocket Barrel]] which the Maw-Ray also swallowed. The Maw-Ray spat out Mario and Donkey Kong as they escape it.
