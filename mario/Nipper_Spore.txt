A '''Nipper Spore''' is an enemy which appears in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming games, and they first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. A [[Nipper Plant]] hatches from one of these.

==In the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
[[File:Nipper Spore SMW2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Nipper Spores first appear in [[The Cave Of Chomp Rock]]. At first, a Nipper Spore slowly descends its to the ground, and upon landing, it instantly hatches. Even touching a Nipper Spore damages the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] and activates the [[Countdown Timer]]. If a Yoshi eats a Nipper Spore, he swallows it automatically, and the Nipper Spore is not converted into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]].

===''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''===
Rather than just fall from above, some Nipper Spores are produced from a [[Nipper Dandelion]], which they are blown off, usually by a [[Windbag]]. Aside from this, Nipper Spores act the same as in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''.
