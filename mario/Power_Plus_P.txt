'''Power Plus P''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear Power Plus P by using six [[Badge Point|BP]], and doing so increases his partners' [[Attack]] power.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Power Plus P can be obtained in a few ways.

* A [[treasure chest]] on the roof of [[Fresh Juice Shop]] in [[Glitzville]].
* Buying it from [[Dazzle]] in [[Rogueport Sewers]] for fifteen [[Star Piece]]s.
* In an invisible ? block on top of [[Herb T.'s Place]] ([[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|Nintendo Switch remake]] exclusive)
* As rare drops from [[Dark Wizzerd]]s and [[Elite Wizzerd]]s
* Stealing Power Plus P using [[Ms. Mowz]] from Dark Wizzerds and Elite Wizzerds
