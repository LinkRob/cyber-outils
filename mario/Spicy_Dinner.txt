The '''Spicy Dinner''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing a [[Big Egg]] or a [[Horsetail]] with [[Hot Sauce]]. This spicy meal temporarily doubles attack.
