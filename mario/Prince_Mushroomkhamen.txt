'''Prince Mushroomkhamen''' is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Mind Your Mummy Mommy, Mario]]." He is the son of [[Queen Mushroomkhamen]] and [[King Mushroomkhamen]], the former of whom mistakes [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] for him being him due to their similar appearances.

Like his parents, Prince Mushroomkhamen is based on real royalty of [[Egypt|Ancient Egypt]]. Their last name is derived from the real {{w|King Tutankhamen}}, a pharaoh of Ancient Egypt, but with the "Tutan" prefix substituted for "[[Mushroom]]" to fit the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' theme.
