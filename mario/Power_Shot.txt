A '''Power Shot''' is a powerful move featured in the ''[[Mario Tennis (series)|Mario Tennis]]'' series and ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' series. They are mostly character-specific, although some Power Shots are shared between playable characters. There are two types of Power Shots: [[Offensive Power Shot]]s and [[Defensive Power Shot]]s.

In ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''{{'}}s story mode, Power Shots are earned by [[Ace]] or [[Clay]] as they progress through the mode.
