'''Wall Lakitus''' are enemies in the [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi'' franchise]]. They are [[Lakitu]] variants that, instead of floating around in [[Lakitu's Cloud]]s, appear hiding behind walls, waiting for a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to approach and then quickly launch a [[Spiny Egg]] at them. They can be defeated by any method of attack: by being [[jump]]ed on, by throwing an [[Yoshi's Egg|egg]] at them, or by [[Swallow|swallowing]] them to turn them into eggs. After a Wall Lakitu is defeated, another will replace it after a short time.

==History==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''===
[[File:Wall Lakitu.png|frame|left]]
[[File:2-7Wall.png|thumb|A group of Wall Lakitus in Lakitu's Wall in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'']]
Wall Lakitus first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|its remake]], where they appear as uncommon enemies. They can first be seen in the level [[Lakitu's Wall]], where they are the main enemy and appear in large numbers throughout. They later reappear in [[The Cave Of The Mystery Maze]] and [[BLIZZARD!!!]] A Wall Lakitu appears in a hole in the walls, looking around for Yoshi, and upon seeing them, they will aim with a blinking crosshair to indicate where they are firing and quickly launch a Spiny Egg in their direction, which will explode upon contact with the ground. Wall Lakitus are usually found in groups of two or more, making it difficult to evade their projectiles. [[Aqua Lakitu]]s, another variant of Lakitus found in the game, behave similarly to Wall Lakitus, with the only difference being that they hide in the [[water]].

===''Yoshi Touch & Go''===
Wall Lakitus appear in ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'', where they primarily appear in [[Yoshi Touch & Go#Modes|Marathon Mode]]. They appear in groups and slowly launch Spiny Eggs at Yoshi . Their Spiny Eggs can be destroyed with eggs, though they cannot be defeated by drawing bubbles around them. The best option is to defeat them by throwing an egg at them, which will give 2 points. Flashing variants also appear; they are worth 4 points.

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
Wall Lakitus reappear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', maintaining their appearance and behavior from ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''. They first appear in [[Big Bungee Piranha's Lair]] and reappear in [[Castle of Priscilla the Peckish]] and in [[Rock 'n' Fall]]. In the [[Island Museum]], Wall Lakitus appear in the [[Island Museum#Airborne-enemy exhibit|airborne-enemy exhibit]].

===''Yoshi's New Island''===
[[File:YNI Wall Lakitu.png|thumb|100px]]
Wall Lakitus appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. Their behavior is similar to how it was in the previous games, with the differences being that their projectiles travel more slowly and can break through destructible terrain. Additionally, their blinking crosshairs no longer have lines. They only appear in the level [[Hidey-Hole Hooligans]], where they are the main enemy again.

===''Yoshi's Woolly World'' / ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''===
[[File:WoollyWallLakituHQ.png|thumb|left|100px|A Wall Lakitu in a climbable wall]]
Wall Lakitus appear in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. They behave similarly to previous appearances; however, they have now completely lost their blinking crosshair. In addition to being found in the usual walls, Wall Lakitus also appear on climbable walls, where they will peek out from the bottom or back to throw Spiny Eggs at the Yoshis. They only appear in the levels [[Scarf-Roll Scamper]] and [[Lakitu Peekaboo]]. After being swallowed and turned into [[yarn ball]]s, they will be yellow.

===''Yoshi's Crafted World''===
[[File:YCW Be Afraid of the Dark 4th SF.jpg|thumb|Two buildings with Wall Lakitus in "Be Afraid of the Dark"]]
Wall Lakitus appear in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'', only in the level [[Be Afraid of the Dark]]. Unlike previous appearances, Wall Lakitus now appear in windows of buildings in the background of the level, rather than in holes, and launch Spiny Eggs toward the Yoshi. Since they are in the background, the only way Yoshi can defeat them is by throwing an egg at them.
