'''Kong Bananza''' is a transformation of [[Donkey Kong]] that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. It is one of the many Bananza forms he can transform into with the aid of [[Pauline]] and is the first of his transformations, being granted to him by the [[Kong Elder]] in the [[Lagoon Layer]] after finding the [[Ancient Record]] for him.

==Physical description==
As Kong Bananza, Donkey Kong is much larger in size and his fur turns into a black and tan color scheme. His eyes now have black and red marks beside them and, most notably, his hair appears to have a bunch of yellow bananas on them. He also has two yellow rings that are shaped like bananas that are on both of his wrists.

==Powers and abilities==
Kong Bananza's primary power is his super strength that is far above Donkey Kong's regular strength. Kong Bananza is able to smash through solid stone which is very useful in the early game where Donkey Kong's base strength makes it difficult to break through tougher terrain; this also makes this form effective versus enemies made of hard materials, such as Sturdy [[Squeeloid]]s and conrete [[Crockoid]]s. Kong Bananza is able capable of charging up a punch that can be let out as a shock wave allowing him to drill through walls or even attack enemies from a distance. When he is granted the ability through the skill menu, Kong Bananza can also passively sense items such as [[fossil]]s and [[Banandium Gems]] which normally requires the use of clapping to do so (which will work for the other Bananza forms as well). After completing the Rehearsal for Kong Bananza, Donkey Kong will be able to transform a small area of terrain in into gold by unleashing a charged punch.

==Other appearances==
===''Super Mario Run''===
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'', there are two statues based after Kong Bananza, the Kong Bananza and Golden Kong Bananza statues. 

=== ''DK Island & Emerald Rush'' ===
The Kong Bananza Statue from Super Mario Run returns in Donkeys Kong Bananza's DLC during an Emerald Rush event, Chip Fever.
