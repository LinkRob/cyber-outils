'''Supernova Sparkle''' is an "ultra-secret special attack" preformed by [[Starlow]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''==
Supernova Sparkle is only shown once, before the boss fights with [[Larry Koopa]] and [[Ludwig von Koopa]]. Here, when [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] are getting beat up in a dark pipe, Starlow uses it to save them so that they can start the battle.
