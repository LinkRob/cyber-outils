'''Bridge Work''' is a Duel minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective for both characters is to stay on the bridge for longer than their rival by avoiding jumping [[Cheep Cheep]]s of varying sizes. After a short time, more Cheep Cheeps begin to [[jump]], and [[Mega Cheep Cheep]]s begin to arrive as well. Whoever gets knocked into the water loses, thereby making the other character the winner. If neither character falls off the bridge within the 30-second [[Time Limit]] or both fall off simultaneously, the minigame ends in a tie.
