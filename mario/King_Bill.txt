'''King Bills''' are a massive indestructible variant of either [[Bullet Bill]]s or [[Banzai Bill]]s. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. King Bills are not only larger and faster than any variant of Bullet Bill but they’re also incredibly dangerous enemies that possess the ability to crush dense stone. Usually, there are [[Coin]] formations shaped like a King Bills to indicate where they’re going to fly allowing [[Mario]] and his friends to steer clear of their path.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''===
[[Image:World 9-8 - King Bill - Destructing Wall.png|thumb|left|A King Bills destroying a wall.]]
King Bills first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', in this game, they only appear once in the game, the final level of the game, [[World 9-8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-8]], around halfway through the level. The level is centered around how the King Bills shoot at the playable characters, showing where they arrive from from in coin formations shaped like a large Bullet Bill (the final one is revealed in the form of [[Blue Coin]]s once a [[P-Switch]] is hit). When [[Mario]] comes close to one, he should generally seek higher ground to avoid being hit by the King Bill when it flies by, King Bills possess the ability to destroy blocks that are otherwise indestructible, while they clear the path for [[Mario]] to move forward, they’re still incredibly dangerous and will damage [[Mario]] if he makes contact with them. King Bills are immense in size, taking up the majority of the vertical screen.

King Bills cannot be defeated, regardless if the player has the effects of the [[Star]] (which merely makes Mario phase through them). However, any King Bills on screen when the [[Goal Pole|Flagpole]] is touched disappears. There is an unused death animation for the King Bills in the game's files suggesting that they were going to be defeatable enemies at one stage.<ref>https://youtu.be/2QtTBBX5AwI?si=GvQCio3pyPTMUexE</ref>

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' / ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' / ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
[[Image:NSMBU Flight of the Para-Beetles Screenshot.jpg|thumb|right|Mario narrowly avoids a King Bill's brutal attack.]]
King Bills appear return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' in these games, they appear in the level [[Flight of the Para-Beetles]], behaving similarly to how they did in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', they fly across the skies creating a huge obstacle as [[Mario]] hops across the [[Para-Beetle]]s, just as before, King Bills cannot be defeated, not even with a [[Star]] which still makes [[Mario]] invincible to them. In this game, they are also engulfed in fire.

King Bills also appear in [[Boost Mode]] however here, they can be defeated by tapping them using the [[Wii U GamePad]] while Mario is in Boost mode.

King Bills are also seen in [[Larry's Trigger-Happy Castle]]. After the underwater section with the [[Torpedo Ted]]s, a massive King Bill chases [[Luigi]] almost immediately after he exits the [[Pipe]], destroying blocks along its way as [[Luigi]] dashes for the [[Boss Door]], the final [[Star Coin]] is also here so Luigi needs to be quick to get it whilst avoiding being hit. Even if [[Luigi]] avoids being killed by the King Bills (upon being hit) or has a [[Star]] which makes him invincible, more keep spawning in endless supplies.

King Bills are also seen in the level [[Para-Beetle Parade]] with a similar role to the level [[Flight of the Para-Beetles]].

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''===
[[Image:P&DSMBE No 68.png|thumb|left|A King Bill seen in-game.]]
King Bills are the final evolution of [[Bullet Bill]] and appear as bosses and in 2 cases, as a normal stage enemy. They will always use "First Strike", dealing a weaker attack when the battle starts, and attack every 2 turns. At half health, they can use "Charge", but this one is different from enemies with a skill with the same name, as it doesn't boost damage by 50%, it doubles it instead. As an ally, King Bill's main attribute is Dark, while his sub-attribute is Fire. His skill is Shield Break, which reduces the [[defense]] of all enemies to 0 for 1 turn. This skill is very handy for defeating enemies with very high defense, including but not limited to [[Spiny|Spinies]], [[Bob-omb]]s, [[Coin Coffer]]s, and more. He requires Green Star Coins to skill up, and has the awakening of Attack Up, increasing all team member's attack by 4% per level.


==Trivia==
*The flame-engulfed King Bills in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' strongly resemble [[Mad Banzai Bill]]s from ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.
*The King Bill is similar to the [[Crusher Blargg]] from ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' as both are the largest variant of their respective species which already have large variants, the King Bill's being the [[Bullet Bill]] and [[Banzai Bill]] and the [[Crusher Blargg]]'s being the [[Blargg]] and [[Gargantua Blargg]] and both are indestructible and possess the ability to destroy normal indestructible blocks and stones.
*In the American Prima Guide for ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', [[Missile Banzai Bill]]s were sometimes mistakingly referred to as King Bills, these errors were fixed in the European version of the guide.
