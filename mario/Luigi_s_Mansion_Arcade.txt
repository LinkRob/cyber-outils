'''''Luigi's Mansion Arcade''''' is a side-game within the [[Luigi's Mansion (series)|''Luigi's Mansion'' series]]. It was both developed and published by [[Capcom]], who first released the game in Japan in 2015, followed by a North American release in 2017. ''Luigi's Mansion Arcade'' is based on ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' in particular. ''Luigi's Mansion Arcade'' is unlike the other ''Luigi's Mansion'' titles in that it features rail shooter gameplay, combined with the usual mechanics of the ''Luigi's Mansion'' series.
