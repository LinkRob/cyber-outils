'''Ghost in the Hall''' is a 4-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. Due to its nature as a free-for-all minigame, it has to be selected from the minigame roulette wheel when all four players land on a space of the same colour.

== Layout ==
The minigame involves a haunted mansion whose layout changes between playthroughs, but there is always a red carpet at the north end which serves as the finish line and a path from the starting point at the south to the finish line.

== Gameplay ==
Characters must choose certain pathways to get to the goal, which is a wide pathway with a red carpet. Some paths contain [[Red Boo]]s that force the character to go in the opposite direction, while others contain doors that open, stunning the character. However, there is always a path leading to the end. The first character to reach the goal wins. There is a horizontal red carpet stretching from the west end to the east end of the mansion halfway through.

== Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The minigame's name comes from the 1989 manga ''Ghost in the Shell'' and "hallway."

== Trivia ==
* The advice claims that the Red Boos stun the player and closed doors merely slow them down; however, closed doors actually stun the player, while Red Boos force them to backtrack. Additionally, the Red Boos are called "pink boos."
* Even if you select [[Boo]] or [[Dry Bones]], they will still get scared by the [[Red Boo|Boos]].
