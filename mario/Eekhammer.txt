The '''Eekhammer''' is a [[Hammer]] type and move in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
== Effect ==
* If the player has good timing, it might hit the enemy multiple times. Each hit does 1 damage.

== Trivia ==
* In its sticking points, it says that this [[Hammer]] really looks like a toy, due to its squeaking sound when it bonks an enemy.
