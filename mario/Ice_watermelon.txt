'''Ice watermelons''', also referred to as '''blue watermelons'''<ref>''Yoshi's New Island'' digital manual</ref><ref>''Yoshi's New Island'' [[List of Yoshi's New Island Message Block hints|Message Block hint]]</ref> (or '''Blue Watermelons'''), are cold and frozen blue [[watermelon]]s found in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' (and [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|its reissue]]) and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] eats one, he will be able to blow three blasts of ice in the original game and five in ''Yoshi's New Island''. The ice can freeze [[List of enemies|enemies]] solid, and they can then be smashed to pieces. In ''Yoshi's New Island'' and ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', enemies that are frozen slide along the ground when touched by a Yoshi.

The '''Super Blue Watermelon'''<ref name=SMW2>''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' [https://www.nintendo.co.jp/clvs/manuals/common/pdf/CLV-P-SADJE.pdf English instruction booklet], page 24.</ref> [[Special Item (Yoshi's Island series)|Special Item]] is available in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'', and the '''All-you-can-eat ice watermelon!''' [[Power Badge (Yoshi's Woolly World)|Power Badge]] is available in ''Yoshi's Woolly World''. Using them automatically places an ice watermelon in the air suspended by a propeller, allowing a Yoshi to have its ability at nearly any point, as new ice watermelons appear following him. In ''Yoshi's Island'', using a Super Blue Watermelon allows a Yoshi to spit ice nine times instead of the usual three.

In ''[[Nintendo Puzzle Collection]]'', ice, fire, and regular Watermelons cascade down the screen when a stage of Round 9 is cleared in the Action Mode of ''[[Yoshi no Cookie]]''.
