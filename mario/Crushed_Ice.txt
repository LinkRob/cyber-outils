'''Crushed Ice''' is a Bowser [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to maneuver an [[ice crusher]] to knock every [[Koopa Kid]] out of the area by ramming into all of them within a [[time limit]] of 99 seconds. If the ice crusher bumps into a wall or a pole, it bounces back. Red Koopa Kids sometimes charge to try and escape the player's character until they hit a wall, during which they become temporarily invulnerable. Blue Koopa Kids have to be hit twice, with the first hit only causing them to slip and become temporarily invulnerable. When every Koopa Kid is eliminated within the time limit, the player's character wins at the minigame, but if time runs out, they lose.

===Controls===
*{{button|gba|Pad}}: Move ice crusher

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, during the [[Chillin' Villain]] quest, Crushed Ice is played at [[Mt. Frostbite]] after the player warms up [[Bowser]] and [[Koopa Kid]] by answering his jokes correctly. After winning Crushed Ice, the player earns the [[Breeze Buddy]] [[Gaddget]].
