'''Magikoopa Mob''' is one of [[Bowser]]'s [[Special Attack]]s in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. Using the move costs 15 [[Special Point|SP]].

==Description==
It involves having [[Magikoopa]]s line up in many designs: curves, L's, horizontal lines, vertical lines, etc. The player has to use the {{button|ds|Stylus}} to trace their position so Bowser can throw fireballs on the Magikoopas, which they then re-use to attack every opponent with their wands. When the wand has a yellow spark over it, Bowser will throw a fireball on it. If all the Magikoopas' wands are touched, they cause more damage than four or three of them alone. After about four times, the Magikoopas walk off stage, exhausted.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
To use this move, Bowser must free all the Magikoopas from [[Peach's Castle]].
