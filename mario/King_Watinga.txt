'''King Watinga'''<ref>''Mario Paint'' English instruction booklet, page 29.</ref> is a large, robotic [[fly (Mario Paint)|fly]] introduced in ''[[Mario Paint]]''.

==History==
===''Mario Paint''===
[[File:King Watinga sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''Mario Paint'', King Watinga is the boss of the [[minigame]] [[Gnat Attack]], where he appears at the end of each level. King Watinga is capable of flight, spawning [[Fly Child]]ren, and shooting out stinger missiles. He turns slightly red after taking 10 hits from the flyswatter, and turns redder after taking 15 hits. After taking 20 hits, he convulses and explodes.

===''Super Mario Maker''===
In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', King Watinga only appears in the third and final level. He only spawns Fly Children and flies off-screen, returning when all the Fly Children are swatted.

Following the release of the version 1.20 update, King Watinga can also appear on the level editor screen if a [[Muncher]] is shaken after flies have already spawned. Angering him by swatting him repeatedly will start a more challenging mode of Gnat Attack. Two King Watingas appear in the final level of this mode.

===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
King Watinga appears in a remake of Gnat Attack called [[Fly Swatter]], a minigame that appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==In other media==
===''Super Mario-kun''===
[[File:King Watinga (Super Mario-kun).png|thumb|Appearance in ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'']]
King Watinga also makes a small appearance in [[Super Mario-kun Volume 5|volume 5]] of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', in which he is brought out of the ''Mario Paint'' game by [[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]]. After colliding with [[Yoshi]], King Watinga fuses with him to become [[Hae Yoshi]]. In the end, he is destroyed, and Mario is able to redraw Yoshi.
