'''Bob-omb Squad''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. In the former game, it is one of [[Wario]]'s minigames, whereas the latter features it as a Variety minigame.

==Gameplay==
The object is for the player to hit the falling [[Para-bomb]]s by using a slingshot to launch small [[cannonball]]s at them. Every Para-bomb has to be destroyed to protect a garden with four [[Power Flower (Super Mario 64 DS)|flowers]]. The player can aim the slingshot using the [[Stylus]] and release to launch a cannonball. A Para-bomb rewards 100 [[point]]s upon being hit, but if any Para-bombs are hit in succession, each one rewards an increasing number of points, the highest being 3,200 points. [[Lakitu]] sometimes appears onscreen, and if he is hit by a cannonball, all Para-bombs onscreen blow up, each rewarding 100 points as if they were destroyed individually.

As the minigame progresses, the speed of the Para-bombs falling increases, making it increasingly difficulty to protect the flowers. Whenever a Para-bomb touches a flower, both it and the flower are destroyed. If all four flowers are destroyed, it is a [[Game Over]].
