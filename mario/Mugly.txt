'''Mugly''' is the first boss appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' who is an unknown species known by anyone currently. Mugly makes his lair in the [[Jungle]] world (the game's first world), more specifically the stage Mugly's Mound. where he can be seen eating some [[banana]]s. [[Kalimba]] hypnotizes him to attack [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]]. [[Thugly]] appears to be the same species as Mugly and is likely to be Mugly's relative.

==Appearance==
Mugly appears to be a banana shaped monster who is yellow. Red retractable spikes are visible on his back. He also has red claws and can turn orange and red when hit enough times by Donkey and Diddy.

==Battle==
When [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] finish all of the levels in [[Jungle]], except for the boss battle, they can see Mugly eating some [[banana]]s until [[Kalimba]] hypnotizes Mugly to attack Donkey and Diddy. 

When starting the battle, Donkey and Diddy must jump on Mugly while he isn't spiked on the back at first after Mugly attempts to ram into Donkey and Diddy. Mugly also lets down his back spikes, giving the Kongs a chance to jump on him as an attack. Sometime, Mugly attempts to crush Donkey and Diddy by jumping high up into the air and landing down again. Donkey and Diddy can also roll into Mugly while not spiked as an attack.

Later in the battle, Mugly turns orange, then red and becomes more aggressive and fast. He also crushes the ground and tries to attack Donkey and Diddy with a shockwave attack.

When Donkey and Diddy manage to jump on Mugly nine times, [[Kalimba]] becomes revealed out of Mugly and injured and Donkey must punch Kalimba and knock him out. After he knocks him out, Donkey and Diddy can access the second world, [[Beach]].

==Trivia==
*In the Prima guide of ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'', Mugly is related to Thugly.
*If Diddy fires peanuts at Mugly or [[Thugly]] in co-op mode, they eat the peanuts and give Donkey and himself to attack.
*Mugly is the simplest boss in the game, due to his attacks being more simplistic.
