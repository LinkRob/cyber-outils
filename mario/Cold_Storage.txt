[[File:Cold_Storage.png|thumb]]'''Cold Storage''' is one of the many rooms in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]''. This room is found in the Basement and it is where you find [[Sir Weston]]. As the name suggests, this room is for the storage of various items requiring cold temperatures and thus, everything in there is frozen. All the items excluding a small fireplace-like item, anyway. [[Luigi]] can use that to retrieve fire power for his [[Poltergust 3000]]. He needs to use fire to defeat Sir Weston and gain access to the final portrait ghost. There are many falling ice spikes in here, so be sure to move every once in a while, and what makes it worse is Weston shoots ice attacks at you through the floor, which is also slippery--the main difficulty in this room.[[fr:Chambre Froide]] [[de:Kühlraum]]
