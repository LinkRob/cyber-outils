'''Snow Go''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Opening==
The view zooms in on the players before zooming out to show the entire course.

==Gameplay==
Each of the characters are riding in their own snowmobile, and they have to try and be the first to complete five laps around a circular track. Every few seconds, two [[Mr. Blizzard]]s in the middle of the course throw snowballs that freeze any player they hit, a concept reused from the ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' minigame [[Mr. Blizzard's Brigade]]. If the player's character is frozen, they have to shake the [[Wii Remote]] to thaw their character. About halfway around the track is very thick snow that slows whoever goes through it. However, the section can be avoided by taking the [[Dash Panel|dash ramp]]. Whoever completes the first five laps first is the winner.

In [[Time Attack (Mario Party 9)|Time Attack]], the object is to instead complete ten laps. There is also a bonus objective that can be accomplished by using the dash ramp on every lap.

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Be the first player to complete five laps. Use the dash ramp to avoid losing speed in the deep snow.''
*'''Controls''': ''Hold the Wii Remote sideways. Press {{button|wii|Pad}} to steer, {{button|wii|2}} to accelerate, and {{button|wii|1}} to reverse. If you get frozen by a snowball, shake the Wii Remote to escape.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a pun on the phrase "no-go," which means something failed.
*Snow Go is one of four minigames to automatically end when a character in first place reaches the goal, the other three being [[10 to Win]], [[Logger Heads]], and [[Pizza Me, Mario]].
*The ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' minigame [[Later Skater]] similarly features a round ice track that the characters have five laps to travel around in, albeit on ice skates.
