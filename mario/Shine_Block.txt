'''Shine Blocks''' are special type of [[block]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They each contain a [[Shine Sprite]] inside.

==History==
Shine Blocks appear throughout the game. Most of them are out in plain sight while others tend to be hidden requiring extra exploration to find. These blocks are required to find to help level up [[Mario]]'s partners.

==Trivia==
*While not necessarily related directly, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' features two variations of the Shine Block named [[Adult Shine Block]] and [[Small Shine Block]].
