'''Dedar''' is a green elf-like character who is the fifteenth boss faced by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in VS COM mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]].

==Physical description==
Dedar has light green skin, and she wears a red shirt with two white buttons, a red pointed hat, and yellow shoes.
