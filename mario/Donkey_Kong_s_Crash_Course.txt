'''Donkey Kong's Crash Course''' is a minigame that can be found in ''[[Nintendo Land]]''. It requires the player to go through many obstacles by tilting the Gamepad. There are 4 courses in the game. You start with the 1st course with 10 areas and Course 2 with also 10 areas, and so on.
