'''Tracks''' are a recurring type of object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. Tracks have also appeared in some of the {{series|Donkey Kong Country}} games.

==Description==
Tracks are a type of thin trail found in midair which objects travel on, most commonly [[lift]]s but sometimes also [[Question Block]]s. A majority of [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] also travel around on a trail. A trail can either have the shape of a closed loop or a path. Certain tracks in the form of a path force any objects or Fuzzies to reverse direction once they reach one end, whereas some other tracks in this form cause them to fall off, in which case [[Mario]] or another playable character risks losing a life.
