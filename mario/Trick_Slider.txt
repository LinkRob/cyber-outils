'''Trick Slider''' is a minigame that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player must use the touchscreen to slide over the pieces to complete the image. There is a total of 8 pieces with one space free allowing the player to move other pieces on. As the difficulty increases, the more scrambled the pieces become forcing the player to make more careful decisions when sliding the pieces around. The time is also always set to 99 no matter what the difficulty setting is. The images can depict either a clove of garlic, [[Wario]]'s cap, or Wario's shoe stepping on a mosquito.
