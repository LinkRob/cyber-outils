'''{{Infobox Enemy|name=Shy Guy|image=PMCS Shy Guy Tea.png|caption=|first=''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''|games=|homeland=|located=|leader=|species=[[Shy Guy]]|subspecies=[[Slurp Guy]], [[Spike Guy]], [[Roller Guy]], [[Sombrero Guy]], [[Soggy Guy]], [[Coal Guy]], [[Pry Guy]], [[Laundry Guy]], [[Shunned Guy]], [[Shield Guy]], [[Silver Guy]], [[Gold Guy]], [[Shy Guy 4-Stack]], [[Shy Guy 5-Stack]]|affiliates=[[The Shy Bandit]]|related=[[Snifit]]|attacks=Rushes over and punches Mario.|defeated=|comics=}}'''Shy Guys''' are a type of enemy that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. A Shy Guy can either have a red, green, blue, yellow, or pink color.

== Description ==
They appear as a red cloaked figure with a white mask with eye and mouth holes. They are [[Black Bowser]]'s minions, and deal HP by punching [[Mario]].

== Red Shy Guy ==
[[File:PMCS Red Shy Guy Card.png|left|frameless|90x90px]]

=== Price ===
Sell Price: 3 coins

=== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ===
The Red Shy Guy is first seen in [[Port Prisma]], but can also be found in [[Ruddy Road]], [[Cherry Lake]], [[Indigo Underground]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Dark Bloo Inn]], [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Toad Trainworks]], [[Tangerino Grill]], [[Sunset Express]], and [[Mossrock Theater]]. The ''best location'' to find this card is [[The Golden Coliseum]], as this level contains 23 Red Shy Guys in both the playthrough and the post game.
