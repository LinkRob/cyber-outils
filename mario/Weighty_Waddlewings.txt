==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Below the second scale go to the wall to the left side of the right part of the scale to get the coin and then wall jump to avoid death.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Land on the left part of the fourth scale after a Waddlewing lands on the right side to easily jump to the scale.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In between the two platforms, make a careful jump to land on the Waddlewing while also getting the Star coin. It would be if you were in Flying Squirrel form or Propeller form.

==Hidden Luigi image==
The last Banzai Bill launcher shows a picture of Luigi's head.
