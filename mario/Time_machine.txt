A '''time machine''' is a hypothetical device that can be used to travel through time, whether backwards or forwards. In ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media, several different time machines have appeared:

*[[Time Machine Robo]], [[Dr. Areku]]'s  machine that can open time portals from the past and the future in the [[Super Mario (Kodansha manga)|''Super Mario'' Kodansha manga]] volume ''[[Super Mario Land 4]]''.
*The [[Time Travel Tube]], the time machine created by [[Ludwig von Koopa|Kooky von Koopa]] in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Toddler Terrors of Time Travel]]".
*[[Time Dokan]], a [[warp pipe]] that [[Kāmenētā]] used to go to the past in ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' [[Super Mario-kun Volume 5|volume 5]].
*An [[Wecker|alarm clock]] from the ''[[Club Nintendo (German magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' comic "[[Super Mario: Verloren in der Zeit]]".
*The [[Timulator]], the time machine created by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario's Time Machine (PC)|Mario's Time Machine]]''.
*[[Taru-gata Machine]], a barrel-shaped time machine used by Mario and Luigi to chase after future Bowser to ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' in ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' [[Super Mario-kun Volume 14|volume 14]].
*[[Prof. E. Gadd's time machine]], the first Time Machine invented by [[Professor E. Gadd]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.
*The [[Warpulator]], the second time machine invented by [[Professor E. Gadd]], used in the end of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.
*The [[Time Obliterator 5.0]], a device created by [[Dr. Crygor]], used by Mike, Penny and himself to explore the rock art of [[Underarm Jungle]] in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''.
