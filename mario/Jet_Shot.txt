The '''Jet Shot''' is [[Diddy Kong]]'s [[Special Shot]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Jet Shot jetpack.png|thumb|left|The Barrel Jet, as seen during Diddy's Jet Shot.]]
The player can have Diddy perform the move by tapping out an uppercase 'M' on the touch screen twice. Diddy Kong begins the move by dribbling the [[basketball]] around randomly. He then causes a [[Barrel Jet]] to appear, and it straps onto his back. Diddy then hovers in the air, does a backwards loop-the-loop and throws the ball at an arc into the basket, with a yellow trail behind it.

If Diddy uses the move outside the 3-pointer line, he throws the basketball towards the hoop, but either of his teammates need to dunk the ball to complete the shot.
