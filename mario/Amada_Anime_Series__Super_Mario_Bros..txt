'''Amada Anime Series: Super Mario Bros.'''  is a show that aired in 1989. It consisted of three episodes based on Japanese folklore and fairy tales, although the third episode was inspired by a Western fairy tale called "Snow White".

== Cast ==
* Toru Furuya as [[Mario]]
* Miyako Endo as [[Princess Peach]], [[Toad (character)|Toad]], [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton Koopa]], and [[Wendy O. Koopa]].
* Masasharu Sato as [[Bowser|King Koopa]], [[Larry Koopa]], and [[Iggy Koopa]]
* Naoki Tatsusa as Luigi, Papa Mario, and [[Ludwig von Koopa|Ludwing Von Koopa]]
* Toshiko Sawada as the narrator

== List of episodes ==
* Super Mario Momotaro
* Super Mario Issun-boshi
* Super Mario Shirayuki-hime
