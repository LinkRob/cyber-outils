'''Arrow Signs''' are a recurring type of object in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], having first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. They most commonly appear in the platforming games, and are also present in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', the [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi'' franchise]], and the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.

==Description==
As with arrows in general, an Arrow Sign points out the direction where [[Mario]] or another playable character must go. In some of the 2D platformers, there is only an arrow sign with a right-pointed arrow, indicating that the exit is nearby.
