'''Stella''' is a character from the ''[[Dragon Quest]]'' franchise. She made her first overall appearance in ''[[w:c:dragonquest:Dragon Quest IX: Sentinels of the Starry Skies|Dragon Quest IX: Sentinels of the Starry Skies]]'', but her first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance is in ''[[Fortune Street]]'', being one of two newcomer playable characters from the ''Dragon Quest'' franchise.
