==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Punch the blocks on the first Big Piranha Plant and get the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After getting the red ring, follow the trail of coins in Flying Squirrel form to get the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Go on the second to last Heavy Parabeetle and ride it down to the Star Coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Below the second King Bill, try to step on every normal Parabeetle to see a mini standard Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' jumping.
