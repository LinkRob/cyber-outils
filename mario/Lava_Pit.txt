The '''Lava Pits''' is the forth and final world in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Located to the west of [[Spooky Trails]], this volcanic world features many lava pools, mine cart tracks, a factory producing mechas, and [[Bowser's Castle]].

== Layout ==
[[Mario]] and his friends land in this world while chasing after [[Bowser Jr.]] and [[Spawny]] and also trying to stop the vortex. After coming out of a mine, Mario and his friends must then fend off against [[Mecha Jr.]] and free Spawny. Afterwards, Yoshi will then join them.

As the team travels further through the area, they will then encounter the physical [[Megabug]], which will capture Spawny right away and spawns [[Bwario and Bwaluigi]], and the [[Lava Queen]]. Once those three are defeated, the team will then discover that the [[Megabug]] has merged with Bowser, becoming [[MegaDragonBowser]]. Once the team defeats MegaDragonBowser, the Megabug is then taken down, and peace is restored to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].
