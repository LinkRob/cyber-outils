'''Figure Skater Peach''' is one of Peach's transformations in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''.

===Appearance===
As a Figure Skater, Peach wears a light blue short dress with a white feather band surrounding the waist, transparent sleeves that have a gradient to blue towards her hands, and white tights that contrast to cyan on her feet. The footwear consists of a set of deep ocean turquoise heels attached to stainless steel skating blades. The dress has a transparent neckline and a high collar resembling a thin blue choker, and her brooch is present on the front. The skirt and gloves have an ice crystal texture on them; the same pattern is present on a long translucent band that drags from her back. Her hair is tied back in a bun with a white feather headdress surrounding it, and she wears blue earrings that look like ice crystals.

==Uses==
Figure Skater Peach is used in the levels A Snow Flower on Ice, A Parade on Ice, and The Dark Ice & the Shadowy Stage. In all three levels, Peach has to use this form to save [[Theet|Theets]] that a figure skater who works for the Sour Bunch has gotten under his control. In order to do so, she has to grab his hand, throw off his balance, and tag every Theet. Once he loses control of them, he must be encircled for a finale.
