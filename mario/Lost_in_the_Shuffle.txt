'''Lost in the Shuffle''' is an unlockable Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera shows the players spending time thinking while the camera zooms to the table. 

== Gameplay ==
The matryoshka dolls will be placed on the table and he will shuffle the dolls that gets faster. The players has 10 seconds to guess which had the largest set of dolls. The player that chose the largest set of dolls wins.

== Ending ==
The winner does her/his victory pose while the losers groan behind her/him.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move cursor
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – Select

==In-game description==
*''"Find the doll with the most mini-dolls inside!"''
*''"Try not to blink during the shuffle!"''
