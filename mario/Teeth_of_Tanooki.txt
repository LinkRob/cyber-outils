The '''Teeth of Tanooki''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Teeth of Tanooki.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Teeth of Tanooki resembles a hand cannon with a design based on the [[Tanooki Suit]]. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has medium cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Teeth of Tanooki deals around 65 to 75 damage. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Honey (status effect)|Honey]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Teeth of Tanooki as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it when the player defeats [[Icicle Golem]].
