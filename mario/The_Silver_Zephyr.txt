[[File:WMoD The Silver Zephyr TV Logo US.png|thumb]]'''''The Silver Zephyr''''' is a television show that is seen and featured in the game, ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is about the adventures of the great thief, [[Count Cannoli]], as the "Silver Zephyr" who goes on many heists.

==History==
During the beginning moments of the game, [[Wario]] can be seen browsing through television channels until he stumbles upon ''The Silver Zephyr'' feeling like the show is a good way to kill time. Wario happens to turn into an episode where Count Cannoli is preparing to steal treasure from the [[S.S. Caviar]]. Wario, who was seeking to become rich at the time, then decides to invent the [[Telmet]], a device that allows him to go in and out of the television at will.

Wario then uses the Telmet to warp himself into the episode of ''The Silver Zephyr''. Wario happens to crash into Count Cannoli who drops his magic wand, [[Goodstyle]], revealing that was the source of his powers. Goodstyle, seeing more potential in Wario, decides to side with him instead transforming him into his own persona, the "Purple Wind." Wario then decides to venture further into the show to claim all of the treasure for himself with Cannoli trying to claim back his wand.

The fate of the show, ''The Silver Zephyr'', isn't mentioned after Wario completes his quest. However, it is heavily implied to be cancelled due to Count Cannoli keeping all of Wario's riches after Wario himself was unable to take his treasure with him into the [[Mushroom Earth|real world]] (as Cannoli was only doing the show to become rich and no longer had access to Goodstyle after the events of the game).
