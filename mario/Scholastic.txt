'''Scholastic''' is a book publisher, typically of educational material, often renowned for the Scholastic Book Fair events they host across schools in America. They have published four ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related books, one of which is on a ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' game while one of them is a guide for drawing [[Nintendo]] heroes and villains in general.

The first ''Mario''-related book from Scholastic is ''[[Super Mario Advance (book)|Super Mario Advance]]'', released in September 2001 as part of their ''You Decide on the Adventure'' series. Following this, Scholastic also released ''[[Warioland 4]]''<!--not a typo, the book is actually formatted with this name--> in 2002, also part of the ''You Decide on the Adventure'' series. A few years later, in January 2006, Scholastic published a chapter book titled ''[[Mario and the Incredible Rescue]]''. Scholastic's fourth and most recent ''Mario''-related book is ''[[How to Draw Nintendo Greatest Heroes & Villains]]'', published in 2007.
