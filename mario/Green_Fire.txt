'''Green Fire''' is [[Luigi]]'s default [[kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is unlocked by winning the [[Mushroom Cup]] in [[Engine Class|50cc]]. It has the same basic appearance as [[Mario]]'s [[Red Fire]], except with a green coloration, but it is slightly quicker and has slightly worse acceleration, and is a bit lighter. The Green Fire is not recommended to use because of its poorer acceleration, and weight compared to most of the medium weight karts, unless the highest weighted character the player plays as has a medium weight, and if the stat that is cared about the most is top speed. It has the same star stats as the [[Bloom Coach]] with 8 stars and both are medium karts.

==Stats==
*Class: Medium
*Speed: 4/5
*Acceleration: 2/5
*Weight: 2/5
