'''Fishin' Boos''' (alternatively '''Fishing Boos''') are ghostly [[Fishin' Lakitu]]s that ride a cloud without a face. They attempt to hit the player character with a blue flame at the end of their fishing lines.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario World''====
[[Image:Fishing Boo Run.png|thumb|left|A Fishin' Boo chasing Mario.]]
Fishin' Boos make their debut in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. In this games, it attacks [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) by hanging a blue fire over his head trying to get him to touch it, they are commonly found in [[Ghost House]]s and relentlessly stalk the plumbers as they travel through the corridors. Fishin' Boos cannot be defeated but the plumbers can use various maneuvers to outrun them.

====''[[Super Mario Run]]''====
[[Image:Fishing Boo Treasure.png|thumb|right|Many Fishing Boos holding different things.]]
Fishin' Boos were added in the version 3.0.4 update. They are featured in the level [[Fishing Boo Taunts You]] as a common enemy. Hazardous flames are not the only things that Fishin' Boos carry on their rods, as they can also carry a [[Super Mushroom]], a [[1-Up Mushroom]] (like with Fishin' Lakitus), a [[Coin]], and sometimes even a [[Starman]]. Fishin' Boos aren't as durable as they were previously and can be beaten with the effects of a Starman or by stomping on their heads. Defeating a Fishin' Boo also reveals it to be a ghostly creature under a sheet.

===In ''[[Nintendo Puzzle Collection]]''===
A Fishin' Boo appears in the game's [[reissue|remake]] of ''[[Yoshi's Cookie]]'', as the fourth non-player opponent in VS. Mode on Hard difficulty.

===In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''===
[[Image:Fishing Boo City.png|thumb|left|Peach's encounter with a Fishing Boo.]]
They return as enemies, with '''Fishing Boo''' now being the spelling of their name. Similarly as in previous appearances, Fishing Boos retain their behavior of holding a fishing rod with a flame and trying to hit [[Princess Peach]] with it. Fishing Boos are commonly found in [[Shriek Mansion]] and [[Bowser's Villa]] and are slightly more aggressive than in ''Super Mario World''. [[Shriek Mansion 3-2]] is the first level where Fishing Boos appear. There is also a sleepy variety called the [[Calm Fishin' Boo]] which sleeps until Peach approaches it. Fishing Boos and Calm Fishin' Boos can both be defeated by Peach's [[Rage]] [[vibe]].

=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' / ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''===
[[Image:Real Reel Fishing Boo.png|thumb|left|A Boo with a Fishing Rod in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'']]
[[Image:Real Reel Fishing Boo DX.png|thumb|right|A Boo with a Fishing Rod in the remake.]]
A literal Fishin' Boo apparently appears with the [[Bonus Level Up Roulette|bonus roulette]] on [[Bowser]]'s level up screen. However, this is a generic Boo with a fishing rod and not the [[Lakitu]] variant. The "Fishin' Boo" fills the [[Lakitu]]'s role on Bowser's level-up screen.
