'''Scarracudas''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are piranha fishes with silver fangs and eyes, purple fins, and yellow lips. Scarracudas have three color variants: green, red, and black. Green is suggested to be the species' standard color, as a green one is featured in its own artwork.

===Behavior===
The Scarracuda color variants differ in behavior, but a consistent trait between every Scarracuda is that they attempt to bite [[Wario]]. Green Scarracudas not only swim around but also [[jump]] out of the water if Wario is nearby. Red Scarracudas only swim around in water. Black Scarracudas are like the green variant except they can be found in [[lava]].

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
[[File:WLSI Mt Lava Lava_Scarracuda.png|thumb|220px|left|A black Scarracuda hitting back Wario in Mt. Lava Lava]]
A Scarracuda has a threat rating of three stars. Wario can defeat one by either running into it using a [[Max Fastosity Dasherator]], using a [[Dash Attack]] on a Scarracuda while it is in midair, or by throwing an enemy at it. Black Scarracudas, being lava-dwellers, are only encountered in volcanic areas, such as [[Mt. Lava Lava]].
