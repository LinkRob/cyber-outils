'''Flower Cup''' is the second cup of the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. The tracks in the course are usually a tad tougher than those in the [[Mushroom Cup]].

It is a standard cup and can always be played from the start of the game with the exception of ''[[Mario Kart 7]].''
