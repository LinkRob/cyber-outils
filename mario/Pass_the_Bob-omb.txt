'''Pass the Bob-omb''' is a [[Captain Command]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
If a player uses Pass the Bob-omb, then a 3-2-1 countdown will being. This leads to a blinking [[Bob-omb]] being dropped on them. The player can hit the Bob-omb towards the enemy captain. Whoever is hit by the Bob-omb when it explodes will be damaged. If the player fails to hit the Bob-omb back, then the Bob-omb will blow up into them.

== Flavor text ==
* '''In-game description:''' Starts a game of catch with the enemy captain using a Bob-omb on the verge of exploding.
