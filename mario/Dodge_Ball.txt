'''Dodge Ball''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. Being hosted by [[Doctor Crygor]], it can be encountered in the Twisted League's Sports level, and is based on [[Foul Balls]], a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''

== Layout ==
Despite sharing its gameplay with its basis, Dodge Ball has a different layout. A girl serves as the protagonist, wearing a white gym shirt and blue gym shorts, an orange hat, and a glasses. The girl will lose the latter two items if she gets struck by the balls.

== Gameplay ==
As with Foul Balls, players have to tilt the 3DS to move the girl's head and avoid the volleyballs, which act like the baseballs from Foul Balls. One must be dodged on the first level, two on the second level, and three on the third level.

If the player wins, other students with show up and cheer and the girl smiles.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The microgame's name comes from the sport Dodgeball, which involves balls as throwing weapons against opponents.
