'''Foot of Fury''' is [[Yoshi]]'s [[Super Strike]] in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Yoshi kicks the [[Soccer Ball]] into the air and jumps with it. Yellow and green sparks and rings appear in the background, then Yoshi kicks the ball with energy. As a result, the ball releases a rain of sparks behind when shot towards the goal.
