The '''Goom Battalion''' is a minor boss from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']] faced on [[Slippenglide Island]]. It is a swarm of [[Goomba|Goombas]] that attacks just before the island's [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Lighthouse]].

== Combat ==
The Goom Battalion fights like regular [[Goomba|Goombas]]. The Goombas can attack by charging, whether in stacks or one at a time. In the latter, some may jump, and those must not be jumped over. The rest must be countered by jumping. The Goombas must be beaten at once, lest more appear. Upon defeating the Goombas, the Koopa Troopa Paratroopa Joint Task Force appears, and has to be beaten next. Each Goomba gives 22 Experience and  Coins upon defeat.
