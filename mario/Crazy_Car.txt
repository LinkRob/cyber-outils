'''Crazy Car''' is one of the [[Job Center]] [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''. It is a recreation of the [[Crazy Cars]] microgame from ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
When creating the microgame, the player has to draw the incoming object that [[Wario]] has to [[jump]] over. Unlike Crazy Cars and its other iterations, Wario instead jumps when the player taps the Touch Screen using the [[Stylus]].
