'''Eekers''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are found in the [[Woohoo Hooniversity]]. Eekers resemble beakers, hence their name, but have red eyes and a grass skirt and two arms with a flame that enables them to heat themselves and power up.

==Battle==
In battle, Eekers accompany [[Virus]]es. They have two attacks.

For the first attack, an Eeker will walk up to the Mario Bros. and perform a short dance as it crosses its arms and swings its body the same amount. After its last swing, the Eeker will move forward; [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] must jump at the right time to counterattack it.

For the second attack, the Eeker stands nearby the Bros. and boils itself, when it reaches above the cap, the liquid sprouts out and lands on Mario or Luigi a few times. Mario and Luigi can counterattack the Eeker by pressing the {{button|gba|A}}<nowiki> Button or </nowiki>{{button|gba|B}} <nowiki> Button (depending on who is attacked) repeatedly to hit the Eeker with their </nowiki> [[Hammer]]s.

If an Eeker is attacked by the Bros. on offense, it will immediately boil.
