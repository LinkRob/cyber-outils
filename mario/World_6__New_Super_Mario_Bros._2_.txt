'''World 6''' is a [[lava]]-filled world serving as the sixth and penultimate [[world]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Completing it unlocks [[World Star (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Star]].

This world is a lava world, similar to [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]] of [[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]] and [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8 of New Super Mario Bros]]., the former from which it reuses its music. This world can be accessed by completing either [[World 5-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5-Castle]] or [[World Flower-Cannon|World Flower-Warp Cannon]]. There are four Toad Houses in this world, two green, one red, and one yellow. There are eleven levels in this world, six of them are the normal levels, one of them is a Ghost House, one of them is a secret level and there is also a [[Rainbow Course]], a Tower and a Castle level. This world has a total of twelve exits. Enemies introduced in World 6 include Volcanic debris, Flame Chomps, Fire Bros., Crowbers, and Bowser sculptures. Bowser is the boss of this world.
