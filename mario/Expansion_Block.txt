The '''Expansion Block''' is an object in the ''[[Yoshi's Island (series)|Yoshi's Island]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', then in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

==Description==
An Expansion Block resembles an [[Egg Block]] except it has dark purple spots. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can [[jump]] under an Expansion Block to expand it to about twice its size for a short period of time. An enlarged Expansion Block allows a Yoshi to get onto higher areas that it cannot otherwise reach.

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
Expansion Blocks are used as early as in the opening level, [[Welcome To Yoshi's Island]].
