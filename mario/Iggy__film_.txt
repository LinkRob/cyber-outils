'''Iggy Koopa''' (also known by his full name '''Ignatius "Iggy" Koopa'''), is a character that appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. (1993 film)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film. He is a [[Dinohattanite]] during his reign and was the cousin as well as the former henchmen of [[President Koopa]]. After evolution, his cousin, [[Spike (film)|Spike]], refers to him as '''Ignatius'''. Iggy is based on the [[Koopaling]] of the [[Iggy Koopa|same name]] While the names are similar, the only thing the two have in common is the fact that he is a high-ranking minion of King Koopa.

== Biography ==

=== Past ===
Sometime prior to the film's main plot line, the portal between the [[Dinohattan]] dimension and [[Earth (film)|Earth]] was inadvertently re-opened by [[Scapelli Construction]]. [[President Koopa|Koopa]] sends Iggy and [[Spike (film)|Spike]] to retrieve [[Princess Daisy (film)|Daisy]] and her [[Meteorite piece|meteorite necklace]], as he needs them both to be able to combine the dimensions and be able to rule over both. Iggy and Spike, unsure of how to find who they are looking for, begin kidnapping [[Brooklyn Girls|various women]] in [[Brooklyn (film)|Brooklyn]], [[New York (film)|New York]], including [[Angelica (film)|Angelica]]. As they're being retrieved by Koopa in hopes it's Daisy.

=== ''Super Mario Bros.'' film ===
Iggy and Spike arrive in Brooklyn, New York, scouting for women to kidnap that may be Daisy. They initially kidnap [[Daniella Verducci|Daniella]] after she is dropped off by Mario at her apartment, though they later come to find Daisy in the New York sewers with Mario and Luigi. They take hold of Daisy, dragging her through the tunnels chased by Mario and Luigi. Luigi reaches out for Daisy as she is being dragged into the Dinohattan portal, snatching the meteorite necklace off her. Iggy and Spike deliver Daisy to Koopa, who becomes enraged that they did not successfully retrieve the meteorite piece. He sends them back out to find Mario and Luigi.
