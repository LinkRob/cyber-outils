{{Nihongo|'''Grinder'''|するどいつめ|Surudoi Tsume|Sharp Talon}} is a physical move used only by [[Birdy|Birdies]] and [[Bluebird]]s in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When Grinder is used, the enemy charges at the selected character and attacks them with its spear causing double the damage. This move is similar to [[Skewer (move)|Skewer]].
