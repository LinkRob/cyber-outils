The '''Great Harmoneel''', or just the '''Harmoneel''', is a giant underground eel in ''Donkey Kong Bananza'', who is the father of the Li'l Eelevators and the husband of the Eelevator. He first appears at the end of the Radiance Layer, having been mind-controlled by the Mind-Bender Mask to serve Void Kong, swallowing Pauline after she and Donkey Kong beat Grumpy Kong. During the Groove Layer, the Harmoneel is freed by Donkey Kong when he plays his favorite song, breaking the Mind-Bender Mask. After apologizing to Donkey Kong, he lets him dive into his stomach to save Pauline.

During the post-game and after finishing the previous five Bananza Rehearsals, the Great Harmoneel hosts a live show at the Heart of Gold in the Planet Core. Seeking ideas for a new song, Donkey Kong and Pauline jump into his windpipe, which serves as the final challenge in the game.

In the ''DK Island & Emerald Rush'' DLC, a statue of the Great Harmoneel can be collected and placed on the West Islet nearby DK Island.

Prior to the Harmoneel's appearance, statues made out of debris in the Landfill Layer depict him, and he is mentioned in the sinkhole to the Racing Layer by Pauline.
