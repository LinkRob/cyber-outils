'''Troopa Pin''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When a character equips the Troopa Pin, their attack and magic power is multiplied by 1.5x. The player can obtain the item by scaling [[Land's End]]'s cliff under 12 seconds.
