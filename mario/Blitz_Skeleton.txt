'''Blitz Skeleton''' is an item featured in [[Adventure Tours]] mode of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for the [[Nintendo DS]].

==Description==
The Blitz Skeleton allows the characters to compete in [[Skeleton]] missions.

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
The Blitz Skeleton is rewarded to the party after they rescue a [[Shy Guy]] and a [[Chao]] on cracked ice. Aside from allowing the characters to compete in Skeleton missions, the Blitz Skeleton is also used to challenge [[Eggman Nega]] a second time.
