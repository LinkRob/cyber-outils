'''Speed-Eater''' is an unlockable minigame in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. 

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a long room filled with several enemies.

==Gameplay==
The player, who takes control of a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], who has to make as many [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s from eating enemies, such as [[Shy Guy]]s, within the [[time limit]]. The time limit is 30 seconds in Easy Mode and 60 seconds in Hard Mode. The Hard Mode is unlockable, and it can be unlocked by collecting all eight [[Baby Coin]]s in [[World 4 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 4]]. Aside from a longer time limit, there are tougher enemies in Hard Mode, some of which Yoshi cannot eat. At the end, the Yoshi is rewarded [[extra life|extra lives]], the number of which depends on how well he did.
