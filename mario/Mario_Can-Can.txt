'''Mario Can-Can''' is a 2-vs.-2 [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each team is in charge of opening a can, done so by [[jump]]ing on or [[Ground Pound|ground-pound]]ing platforms attached to a can opener. As the players continue to jump or ground-pound, the can opener slowly goes around the top of the can, opening it. Players must take turns jumping or ground-pounding in order to get the most leverage from each jump or Ground Pound. The first team to open its can and release the contents wins. If neither team does so within a certain time limit, the minigame ends in a draw.

==Can variants==
*[[Fruit]]s &ndash; Various fruits erupt from the can.
*Candies &ndash; Various sweets erupt from the can.
*Flowers &ndash; Flowers erupt from the can.
*Balloons &ndash; Balloons fly out of the can.
*[[Goomba]] &ndash; Many Goombas or a [[gold Goomba]] erupts from the can.
*[[Thwomp]] &ndash; A Thwomp repeatedly hops out of and back into the can.
*Hammers &ndash; A large hammer followed by small hammers erupt from the can.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Jump
*{{button|gcn|A}} '''→''' {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Ground Pound

==In-game text==
*'''Rules''' &ndash; "''Open the can using the seesaw can opener. Alternate jumps on the seesaw to open the can.''"
*'''Advice''' &ndash; "''A Ground Pound will make the can opener move faster.''"

==Naming==
"Can-Can" comes from the name of {{wp|can-can|a type of dance}}, even though it does not appear in the game.
