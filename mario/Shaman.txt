'''Shamans''' are a species featured in some of the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
Shamans resemble wizards wearing coats that completely cover their body. Because of this, only their eyes are seen.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[File:Shaman SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
Shamans appear as both a friendly species and enemies. In battle, the Shaman's [[Psychopath]] thought is "I'm losing this fight!", and they mainly attack with spells. Shamans can be seen as shopkeepers at the [[Sea]], but as enemies, they are fought at [[Nimbus Land]] and [[Bowser's Keep]]. Due to their high magical defense, the Shamans are very weak to physical attacks so Mario's Jump moves are greatly effective against them.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
[[File:ShamansParade.png|thumb|left|200px|The Shamans during the parade in ''[[Paper Mario]]'']]
Shamans only appear as allies in every ''Paper Mario'' installment except ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. Many Shamans make an appearance, but one of the most notable ones is [[Merlon]], who has a lot more appearances than the others (except for also [[Merlee]]). The Shamans fulfill a variety of purposes such as predicting the next path, finding the location of [[Star Piece]]s, and helping Mario during a point of a battle.
