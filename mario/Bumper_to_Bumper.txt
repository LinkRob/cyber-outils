'''Bumper to Bumper''' is an 8-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame simply involves eight characters trying to use their bumper car (called a "hoverkart" in the instructions) to knock their opponents off into the [[pit]] surrounding the round platform they are on. There is a time limit of 30 seconds, and a character wins if they are either remaining once the [[Time Limit]] is up or if all other characters have fallen into the pit. If all eight characters fall into the pit, the minigame ends as a tie.

==Controls==
*Control Stick or C Stick: Move hoverkart
