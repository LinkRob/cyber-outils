'''Unlockable Characters''' are characters that must be unlocked to select in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' and [[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]] series. [[Super Mario Kart]] and ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'' don't have any unlockable characters. Despite [[Mario Kart: Super Circuit|Super Circuit]] not having any Unlockable Characters it did feature colored Yoshis (light blue, pink and yellow) that are exclusive to Single-Pak multiplayer mode.
