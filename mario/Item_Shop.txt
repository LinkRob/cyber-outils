'''Item Shops''' are stores featured in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. They typically appear in the role-playing games and are where [[Mario]] or another character can purchase items to help them along their journey.

Item Shops appear in {{series|Paper Mario}}, selling items such as the [[Honey Syrup]]s and [[Tasty Tonic]]s. Item Shops in these games are also usually found near [[Inn]]s in towns. In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', Item Shops are replaced with [[Sticker Shop]]s. They also appear in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}} where Mario and Luigi can also buy Gear items such as overalls and gloves. They can also buy Bros. Items which is only featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Item Shops have the same purpose as in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.
