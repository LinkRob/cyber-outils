'''Sugar Rush''' is both a 2-vs-2 and a Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
The setting takes place at a kitchen of a bakery. In this minigame, the two teams will have to make a cake that matches the one shown at the top of the screen. The teammates must work together to quickly match the cake before the other team. The same rule applies to the Duel version. The team or player who matches the cakes two times wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} (Point) - Move cursor
*{{button|wii|a}}/{{button|wii|b}} - Grab/Drop toppings

==Trivia==
*The minigame's title is derived from the phrase "sugar rush," describing someone who gets excessively hyperactive from eating too much sweets.
*''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' features a [[microgame]] with similar gameplay, titled [[Top Notch]].
*In the Star Battle Arena and Party Tent versions, a [[Koopa Troopa]] is the main baker at the bakery (which he is seen putting finishing touches on the cake). In Minigame Tent, a [[Mii]] is the main baker.
