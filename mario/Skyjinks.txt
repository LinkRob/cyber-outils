'''Skyjinks''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to make their way across floating platforms toward the goal. If you fall, you'll lose a [[heart]]. If you have any hearts remaining, you'll return to the platforms. If a character loses all three hearts, they are eliminated from the minigame. In perspective mode, if you fall, the minigame automatically ends and you lose.

==Controls==
The [[Wii Remote]] has to be held sideways. The player has to use the D-Pad to move and press the 2 button to [[jump]] and recover.
