'''Standard RB''' is [[R.O.B.]]'s standard [[kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', the other two being the [[ROB-BLS]] and the [[ROB-LGS]]. It has, along with [[Standard BW]], the highest top speed for standard karts, along with average acceleration and fairly high handling (which is unlike for heavyweights), but having the second worst drift in the game (first being ROB-LGS), and like other standard karts has an average item stat. It's kart color is black with a purple D-pad as an emblem.
