'''Boo-ting Gallery''' is a 2 vs. 2 minigame in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Layout==
Boo-ting Gallery takes place both outside and inside of a mansion, likely based on [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Luigi's Mansion]]. The characters go through three areas: the first takes place outside the mansion's gates; the second within the mansion's foyer; and lastly within a dark cellar.

==Opening==
One team runs up a path leading to the gate of a haunted mansion with some [[Red Boo]]s outside. The Red Boos vanish as the screen splits in two to display both teams. The minigame is played from a first-person perspective.

==Gameplay==
[[File:BootingGallery2.png|thumb|left|250px|The second stage]]
Both teams compete to shoot and defeat all the Red Boos in and around the mansion. The characters have to shoot every red Boo with their gun before they can progress into the next area. There is a cutscene where after clearing the foyer, the team players walk toward the stairs, only for the floor beneath them to give way and drop them into the basement, where a big Red Boo resides. The team that manages to defeat the [[Mega Red Boo]] first and leave the mansion wins.

The minigame has various similarities to [[Spider Stomp]] from ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' and could have possibly been based on it. For comparison, Spider Stomp similarly involves a team having to defeat every [[Scuttlebug]] before they can continue to the next area, with the last area featuring a [[Mega Scuttlebug]] to defeat.

==Ending==
The winning team exits the mansion and leaves through the gates then celebrates. While the winners' names are displayed onscreen, the silhouettes of the losing team are visible through the windows inside the mansion, running as a Red Boo chases them around.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The title of the minigame is a pun on the word "shooting gallery."

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Make your way through the house exterminating Red Boos. The first team to defeat the Red Boo in the basement wins."''
*'''Hint''' &ndash; ''"Teamwork tip! When facing several Red Boos, focus on a different side of the field than your partner."''

==Trivia==
*If one team defeats the final Red Boo just before the other team's camera starts panning in the final cutscene, when it pans back to the middle, the large Red Boo will not appear.
