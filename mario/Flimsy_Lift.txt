'''Flimsy Lift''' is a type of [[lift]] featured in the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
A Flimsy Lift appears stationary in the air. It slowly declines whenever [[Mario]] or another character stands on it. Given that Flimsy Lifts usually appear above pits, this puts the playable characters at risk of losing a life if they stand on it for too long. A Flimsy Lift stops falling once the playable character gets off.

Initially, in ''Super Mario Bros.'', a Flimsy Lift uses the same sprite as a regular Lift, but in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' they are depicted as gray. In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', Flimsy Lifts are light blue.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Bros.''===
Only seven Flimsy Lifts make an appearance. One first appears in [[World 3-3 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 3-3]], followed by four near the end of [[World 6-3 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-3]], and lastly two at the start of [[World 7-4 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 7-4]].

===''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''===
Flimsy Lifts are more common, possibly owing to the increase in difficulty from ''Super Mario Bros.''. A Flimsy Lift appears in at least one level within most [[world]]s.
