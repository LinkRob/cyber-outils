'''Rock Blocks''' are a recurring object in the {{franchise|Mario}} and related media. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Wario]]''.

==Description==
Rock Blocks are one of the tougher varieties of [[block]]s due to being made of stone, typically making them harder to break than other blocks. In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', Rock Blocks have line patterns carved into them just like those of a [[Brick Block]]. A Rock Block can usually be broken by performing a powerful attack or from being under the effects of certain power-ups.
