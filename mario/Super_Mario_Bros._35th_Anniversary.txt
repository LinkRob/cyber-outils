The '''''Super Mario Bros.'' 35th Anniversary''' is a celebration of the first ''Super Mario'' game: ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. The event started on September 30, 2020 and ended on March 31, 2021.

==List of games==
{{See also|Category:Super Mario Bros. 35th Anniversary}}
Here are the games included in the celebration:

*[[Game & Watch]] ''Super Mario Bros.'', ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'', and ''[[Ball]]'' starring [[Mario]].
*''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|Super Mario 3D World for the Nintendo Switch]]'' and [[Bowser's Fury]].
*''[[Super Mario Bros. 35|Super Mario Bros. 35]]''.
*''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]].''
*''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' pixel skin addon.
*35 Speed-run in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]].''
*''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''-''[[Mario (series)|Mario'' tournament]].''
*Mario Splatfest in ''[[w:c:splatoon:Splatoon 2|Splatoon 2]].''
*Mario furniture in ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing: New Horizons|Animal Crossing: New Horizons]].''
*''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' added for Nintendo Switch.
