}}'''Rotation Station''' is a Duel minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In Rotation Station, the two players are racing up a castle-like tower. They must adjust the camera around and jump over gaps in the platforms in order to climb up. The player who reaches to the top first wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} - Camera
*{{button|wii|Pad}} - Move
*{{button|wii|2}} - Jump
