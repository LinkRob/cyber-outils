'''Bean Fruits''' are an item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Bean Fruits appear in underground areas of the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. The [[Luigi Dunk]] move is required to find them. There are a total of seven Bean Fruits, just like with [[Neon Egg]]s, which [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s produce upon eating a Bean Fruit, something that [[Fava]] teaches to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

==Locations==
#The northeastern area of  the Beanbean Kingdom. It is found in the middle of four flowers.
#In the northeastern area of the Kingdom. There is a giant Koopa blocking away which he needs to be push over. Then, Luigi must head under the gate and head further east to fight the [[Piranha Bean]]. After it is defeated, Prince Peasley suddenly appears small and Luigi hammers him to make him big. Prince Peasley then gives Luigi the Bean Fruit.
#Southeast in the Beanbean Kingdom.  It's in the middle of a patch of six flowers. It is past the guards.
#Directly south of the Kingdom. It is located in front of a cactus.
#In the southwestern area of the Beanbean Kingdom, one can be found in the middle of a patch of flowers on a high ledge. Mario and Luigi must jump up another ledge and use the [[Spin Jump]] technique to reach it.
#West of the Beanbean Kingdom, one is found in the middle of a patch of blue flowers.
#Northwest of the Beanbean Kingdom, it is found in the middle of a patch of blue flowers.
