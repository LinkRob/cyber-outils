'''Pushy Cat''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are cats that wear a red uniform and carry a pile of boxes.

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Pushy Cats can be only found onboard the [[S.S. Caviar]] as employees. When they spot [[Wario]], they will attack by pushing the box piles towards him. They are rather weak enemies and can be taken out by any attacks.
