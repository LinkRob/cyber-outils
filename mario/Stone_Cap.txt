The '''Stone Cap''' is an item which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
This item automatically gives [[Mario]] a new ability in which he will turn to stone for a three turns. When turned to stone, Mario cannot attack or do anything for that matter, but he is unaffected by all attacks dealt by opposing forces. Mario can find this item by purchasing it from [[Harry's Shop]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. This shop sells this item for 30 coins.
