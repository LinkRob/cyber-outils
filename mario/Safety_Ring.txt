'''Safety Ring''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
By equipping the Safety Ring on to a character, they are protected from all status ailments and instant death. The Safety Ring can be obtained by finding the secret room in the [[Sunken Ship]].
