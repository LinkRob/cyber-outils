'''Konami Holdings Corporation''', commonly referred to as '''Konami''', is a Japanese entertainment company. It operates as a product distributor (which produces and distributes trading cards, anime, tokusatsu, slot machines and arcade cabinets), video game developer and publisher company. In 2012, they have fully absorbed [[Hudson Soft]] which produced games from the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]].

Konami is famous for popular video game franchises such as ''[[w:c:castlevania:Castlevania (series)|Castlevania]]'', ''[[w:c:metalgear:Metal Gear (series)|Metal Gear]]'', ''[[w:c:suikoden:Suikoden (series)|Suikoden]]'', ''[[w:c:contra:Contra (series)|Contra]]'', ''[[w:c:frogger:Frogger (series)|Frogger]]'', ''[[w:c:gradius:Gradius (series)|Gradius]]'', ''[[w:c:gradius:Ganbare Goemon (series)|Goemon]]'', ''[[w:c:twinbee:TwinBee (series)|TwinBee]]'', ''[[w:c:dancedancerevolution:Dance Dance Revolution (series)|Dance Dance Revolution]]'' and ''[[w:c:silenthill:Silent Hill (series)|Silent Hill]]'' among others. They have developed two games from the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' franchise.

==Games from the Mario franchises==
*'''''[[Mario Roulette]]''''' (1991)
*'''''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''''' (2005)

In addition, three characters from Konami's series appear as guest characters in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. series]]''. These are, [[Solid Snake]] from the ''Metal Gear'' series and [[Richter Belmont]] and [[Simon Belmont]] from the ''Castlevania'' series.
