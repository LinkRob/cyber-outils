'''Parabeanies''' are an airborne variant of [[Beanie]]s and the [[Beanish]] counterparts of [[Paragoomba]]s. They appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Parabeanies directly resemble Beanies, and the only difference with Parabeanies is their white wings about half the size of their bodies, similarly to a [[Paragoomba]]s' relation to [[Goomba]]s. Parabeanies stay afloat, but like Paragoombas, they fall to the ground and become regular Beanies after taking enough damage.

===Behavior===
A Parabeanie attacks by swooping down. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can easily dodge it by jumping at the precise moment.
