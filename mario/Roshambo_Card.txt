|type=Special|desc=Used for Super Roshambo}}'''Roshambo Cards''' are three special [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Cards]] seen at [[Roshambo Temple|Roshambo Temples]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. There are 3 of these cards; rock, paper, and scissors. In Super Roshambo, [[Mario]] must use one of these cards against his opponents to defeat them.
