'''Madam Merlar''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. She is an ancestor of [[Merlon]] whose spirit resides in a hidden room of [[Shiver Mountain]].

==History==
Madam Merlar is first seen after [[Mario]] talks with her after getting past the fake wall. She then tells a long story (that puts Mario to sleep) about her past life before giving him the [[Star Stone]] to help reveal the way to the [[Crystal Palace]].

==Trivia==
*Madam Merlar is the second character in the series to put Mario (and his partners) to sleep with her long stories. The first being [[Merlon]] from the same game and then [[Luigi]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
