{{Nihongo|'''Seaside Area'''|シーサイドエリア<ref>{{cite web|url=https://youtu.be/ZQ3zs226sck|title=一人用マリオパーティアドバンスpart5<nowiki>[字幕]</nowiki>|author=かげらいまどかK|work=YouTube|language=Japanese|date=2017-11-19|accessdate=2023-04-20}}</ref>|Shīsaidoeria|Seaside Area}} is a coastal area in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It appeared in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.
== Description ==
Seaside Area is an area of islands surrounded by water. It consists of places including:
*[[Bowser Game Hall]]
*[[Duel Tower]]
*[[Lakitu House]]
*[[Mario Vaudeville]]
*[[Mushroom Beacon]]
*[[Mushroom Library]]
*[[Mushroom Pool]]
*[[Sandy Beach]]
*[[Sushi Cliff]]

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
The Seaside Area is [[Luigi]]'s starting point in Shroom City.
