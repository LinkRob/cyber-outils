'''Cap Switches''' are a type of [[switch]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
Cap Switches are considerably different between ''Super Mario 64'' and ''Super Mario 64 DS''. In the original, there are three Cap Switches, and they are a [[! Switch]], as indicated by a white "!" on the front. In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', there is only one Cap Switch, and it has a white "?" on the front, indicating that it is a [[? Switch]]. A Cap Switch can be activated by [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] it, and in doing so, every [[Cap Block]] associated with it becomes usable. 

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 64''===
[[File:Vanish Cap Switch model.png|thumb|left|200px|Vanish Cap Switch model]]
Each of the Cap Switches are color-coded, and activating a Cap Switch permanently solidifies every Cap Block of the same color, making it possible to obtain the [[cap]] inside of one. This functionality was carried over from ''[[Super Mario World]]'', in which activating a [[Switch Palace]]'s ! Switch solidifies every [[Dotted-Line Block]] of the same color into ! Blocks.

The first Cap Switch, the '''Wing Cap Switch''', is located on a lower tower structure in the [[Tower of the Wing Cap]]. Activating the Wing Cap Switch solidifies every red Cap Block, allowing Mario to use the [[Wing Cap]] contained within. The second Cap Switch, the '''Metal Cap Switch''', is found in the [[Cavern of the Metal Cap]], on a platform at the leftmost end. By activating the Metal Cap Switch, all green ! Blocks solidify, allowing [[Mario]] to use a [[Metal Cap]] within one. The third and final Cap Switch, the '''Vanish Cap Switch''', is located at the end of [[Vanish Cap Under the Moat]], and activating it solidifies every blue Cap Block, allowing Mario to collect a [[Vanish Cap]] inside of one.

===''Super Mario 64 DS''===
The only returning Cap Switch is the one from the Tower of the Wing Cap, with the location itself being renamed to ? Switch, after the Switch itself. Activating the ? Switch instead has the effect of activating every ? Block, which can be used by any of the playable characters, including Mario, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]].
