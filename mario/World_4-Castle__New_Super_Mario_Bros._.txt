'''World 4-Castle''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.|New Super Mario Bros]]''.  

==Overview==
This is the last level of the fourth world.

==Walkthrough==
[[File:Megagoomba.png|left|thumb|Mario encounters the boss, the Mega Goomba]]
The player starts off on a ledge. They see a platform that swings from left to right, along with a [[? Block]] containing a [[coin]]. The player sees coins floating in the air, as well as another ? Block, this time containing a power-up. The player now has to jump on another swinging platform, which has a [[Dry Bones]] wandering on it. A [[Podoboo]] and another swinging ledge with a Dry Bones can be seen. If the player [[jump]]s when the swinging platform is at its max height, they are able to get their first [[Star Coin]]. The player falls down, and sees a Dry Bones along with two ? Blocks, containing coins. Now, the player sees a couple of [[Thwomp]]s. The player can take two routes from here, by either [[Wall Jump]]ing while collecting coins, or jumping from platform to platform while avoiding a Thwomp. In the lone platform, there is a [[Hidden Block]] with a [[Starman]] inside of it, along with two visible blocks containing coins. The player sees a couple of Dry Bones and a [[Super Thwomp]]. If the player waits while the Super Thwomp is crushing the blocks, they can get their second Star Coin. The player finds a ? Block, containing a power-up. They then encounter more Thwomps and Dry Bones, as well as another Super Thwomp. The player collects some coins and find a Dry Bones here. The player is then at the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] area, and can see a ? Block containing a power-up, a Podoboo, and another ? Block which contains a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The player is on a swinging platform, and is near the last Star Coin. The player must jump from the swinging platform to another, while avoiding a Dry Bones and many Podoboos. The player then sees a boss door, and has to battle the Mega Goomba. After the player has defeated it, they gain access to [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]].

==[[Star Coins]]==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' Mario should jump off the platform when it is at its max height to get to the Star Coin on a ledge.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' Mario should have the Super Thwomp destroy the three rows of blocks that guard the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' After getting on the swinging platform, Mario should jump off of it to land on the ledge below where the final Star Coin is.
