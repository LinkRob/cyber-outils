{{Listnotice|victory animations in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''}}
This applies to characters who rank first, second, third, and fourth place respectively.

== Information ==
If a character wins in any minigame (or gets S-tier in Co-op minigames), then they strike their first place poses as victory animations. But if they lose in them (or get C-tier in Co-op minigames), then they strike their fourth place poses as defeat animations. If they get A/B tier in Co-op minigames, then they strike their second place animations. If they get a tie, then they strike their third place animations. These animations appear normally at the end of a game in the Mario Party gamemode.

==Trivia==
*Several victory and defeat poses have been reused from ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', but the characters have less voice lines.
**In ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', several defeat poses have been reused.
*Diddy Kong's victory poses have been reused from ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.
*Koopa & Shy Guy's 1st place animations have been reused from ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.
*Bowser Jr.'s victory poses have been reused from ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.
