'''Scooter''' is a character who makes a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Too Hot to Handle]]." He is the leader of [[Waki-Waki]] Island. At one point, with the assistance of his friend, [[Bingo]], to try and throw [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] into the volcano as a sacrificial offering to the Fire God. This order was not given by the real Fire God but rather [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] impersonating him.{{stub}}{{TSMBSSNav}}
