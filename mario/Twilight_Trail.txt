'''Twilight Trail''' (ウスグラの道, Usugura no Michi, Dim Trail) is a location that appears in [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]. It is east of [[Twilight Town]] and west of the [[Creepy Steeple]].

== Description ==
Twilight Trail first starts off as an grassland; its colors are orange and brown. Twilight Trail takes place during nightfall. When [[Mario]] reaches the forest area of Twilight Trail, it turns dark and the area's color is blue and purple.

== History ==
{{Spoiler}}
[[Mario]] and his friends had to venture through Twilight Trail several times throughout Chapter 4. This is mainly because Mario had his identity stolen by [[Doopliss]] and Mario had to go back and forth through Twilight Trail to chase Doopliss and find the secret to his name.
{{Endspoiler}}
