'''Apples''' are [[fruit]]s that appear in several [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' games]] with various different uses. They are first seen in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Print World]]''.

==History==
===''Super Mario Bros. Print World''===
[[File:SMBPW Ruler and Apple Border.png|frame|right]]
In ''Super Mario Bros. Print World'', apples are featured part of the selectable border entitled ''Ruler & Apple''.

===''Mario's Time Machine''===

The apple that caused [[Isaac Newton]] to first speculate on the concept of gravity is one of the historical objects stolen by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''. Mario rescues the apple from [[Bowser's Museum]] and manages to return it to 1687 so that it can properly fall for Newton.
{{br|left}}

===''Paper Mario'' series===
====''Paper Mario''====
[[File:Apple (Paper Mario).png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', they are a cheap item found only in [[Boo's Mansion]]. One is able to find Apples by simply [[Spin Jump]]ing on various crates in the right room of the first floor of the mansion. They heal five [[Heart Point|HP]], and they are one of the ingredients used to make [[Apple Pie]]s, the other being [[Cake Mix]].

====''Super Paper Mario''====
[[File:Red Apple SPM.png|frame|left]]
''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' brings back apples; however, in the game, there is no such item named "Apple". Instead, there is a similar item, the [[Red Apple]]. Also, this game introduces new apples, including the taboo [[Black Apple]] (which wakes up [[Princess Peach]]), the luscious but cursed [[Golden Apple]], the mustache-inducing [[Yellow Apple]], the fruit-transforming [[Pink Apple]], and the shrinking [[Blue Apple]]. All apples in this game simply restore five HP, except for the Black Apple, which restores only one when used on a playable character; the above ailments are induced only when they are given to a non-playable character. The more-useful [[Primordial Fruit]] introduced earlier also slightly resembles that game's apples, although the two items' exact relations are unknown.

===''Mario Sports Mix''===
Apples appear in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'', in the basketball minigame called [[Feed Petey]], and give players thirty points if they shoot one in [[Petey Piranha]]'s mouth.

Although apples do not appear in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', the [[Apple Kart]] and two of its variants, the [[Green Apple Kart]] and [[Poison Apple Kart]], are based on apples.

==== ''Mario Kart World'' ====
In ''[[Mario Kart World]]'', in the forest area that leads to [[Acorn Heights]] where a giant [[Wiggler]] dwells and the colorful flowers display, there is a giant red apple that serves as a tunnel due to a hole made by a bite. Drivers can also drive on it as an obstacle. In addition to that, there are boxes of small apples found in [[Koopa Troopa Beach (Mario Kart World)|Koopa Troopa Beach]] and the market area of [[Salty Salty Speedway]] above the high zone of the starting line.

=== ''Donkey Kong Bananza'' ===
The apple also appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'' as a source for [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s health, should it be struck into danger or peril modes. While this fruit has the same color peel, apples come in various sizes.
