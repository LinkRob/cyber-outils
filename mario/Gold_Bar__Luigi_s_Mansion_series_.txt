'''Gold Bars''' are items in the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}. They first appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' for the [[Nintendo GameCube]].

==History==
===''Luigi's Mansion''===
[[File:Gold Bar GBH icon.png|thumb|left]]
In ''Luigi's Mansion'', Gold Bars account for 100,000G each and are often found in [[treasure chest (Luigi's Mansion series)|Treasure Chest]]s. They can still sometimes be found in furniture. Gold Bars are one of three items on the [[Game Boy Horror]] HUD during active gameplay that shows how many [[Luigi]] has collected, with the other two being [[Coin (Luigi's Mansion series)|coins]] and [[Bill|bills]].

===''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''===
Gold Bars return in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'', where they take the same role as they did in ''Luigi's Mansion''. However, they are worth only 20G instead of 100,000G. They are also much more common and can appear after [[Luigi]] vacuums a [[ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] or flashes a [[Gold Spider]], [[Gold Mouse]], [[Gold Bat]], [[Gold Beetle]], or [[Gold Robomb]] with the [[Strobulb]].

===''Luigi's Mansion 3''===
Gold Bars return in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', where they act as they did in ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''. They are rewarded only by defeating [[Gold Goob]]s.
