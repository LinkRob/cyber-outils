'''Roll''' is a move available in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. In order to perform it, the player must [[crouch]] and then either press the Y button or shake the controller. When shaking the controller to roll, [[Mario]] will continually get yellow boosts while rolling, increasing his speed.
