The '''Super Mario Bros. 3 Athletic Theme''' is a song originating from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. As its name implies, it tends to play in levels that prioritise platforming.

== Appearances ==

* ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'': This song plays in the [[Super Mario Bros. 3 (lift microgame)|Super Mario Bros. 3 lift microgame]].
* ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'': [[Melody Pianissima]] may play this theme for her pre-boss minigame in addition to the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' underwater theme.
* ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'': The music that plays when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] rides the Cheep Blimp is an arrangement of the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' athletic theme.
* ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]'': The song Jump! Jump! Jump! is the Athletic Theme remixed in Eurobeat, being played when Mario uses his dancing moves to restore [[Toadette]]'s hostel after she complains about her resort's state.
* ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'': This game includes a whimsical remix called Blue Sky Athletic. This plays in the following levels:
** [[Rolling Green Galaxy]] (when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] is not on the [[Star Ball]])
** [[Beach Bowl Galaxy]] (Twin Fall Lake Planet only)
** [[Honeyclimb Galaxy]] (entire level)
** [[Rolling Gizmo Galaxy]] (ditto from when Mario is not on the Star Ball)
* ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'': The microgame Opening Night may have the player conduct an orchestral arrangement of the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' on the first level.
* ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'': The ''Super Mario Galaxy'' arrangement returns.
* ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'': Some 2D areas in the ''Super Mario Bros. 3''-like world in the Green Energy Plant area play this song.
* ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'': Sky-themed levels in the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' style play this song.
* ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'': This track plays in the first level of the [[Super Mario Bros. 3 (WarioWare: Move It!)|Super Mario Bros. 3 microgame]].
