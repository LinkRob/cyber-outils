'''Loco Express''' is special move used only by [[Booster (character)|Booster]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It also serves as the signature move of Booster and is only seen during the optional battle with him.

== Description ==
When Loco Express is used, Booster rides on a rain train that travels at its target.

== Effects ==
With Loco Express, Booster is able to inflict major damage to a single character.

== Trivia ==
*The move is a pun of "local express."
