'''Madrid Drive 1''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', first appearing in the Summer Tour of 2023. It is the first layout of [[Madrid Drive]].

== Layout ==
Madrid Drive 1 includes a soccer stadium that can be seen at the northern reaches of the map in its route. The racers start by making a slight right (heading northwest), then going through a 270-degree left turn, heading northeast. Afterwards, they drive across a glass bridge, which has ramps at the start and end so the racers can get [[Trick|jump boosts]]. They then make a left turn, going through some city streets, followed by a right turn. Afterwards, they go into a stadium, where [[Goomba]]e play soccer using [[Kuribo's Shoe|oversized shoes]]. The racers can ram the [[Soccer Ball]]s while they head south through the stadium to score extra points. After the racers exit the stadium to the south, they take a right turn to get back to the [[Finish line|starting line]].

== Trivia ==
*When the racers enter the soccer stadium, the music changes to a variant created by a cheering crowd. This is similar to the [[Singapore Speedway 3|Chinatown]] from [[Singapore Speedway]] getting a remix of the standard Singapore Speedway music with a Chinese vibe.
*This is the only variant of [[Madrid Drive]] that does not visit the southern half of the city.
