'''Aboard the Sweet Stuff''' is the first and tutorial [[level]] of ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''. The level takes place on [[Captain Syrup]]'s ship, [[The Sweet Stuff]]. There are signs throughout that teach various moves to [[Wario]], similar to [[Hall of Hieroglyphs]], the first level of ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.
