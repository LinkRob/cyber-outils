The''' Gloomy Manor''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' and ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]''. It is the first mansion to be explored by [[Luigi]].

== Description ==
In the Gloomy Manor, Luigi will receive the [[Poltergust 5000]], the [[Strobulb]], and the [[Dark-Light Device]]. Also in this mansion, Luigi will receive the second piece of the [[Dark Moon]]. This mansion resembles the mansion in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]''.

== In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}} ==
=== ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' / ''Luigi's Mansion 2 HD'' ===
In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' and ''Luigi's Mansion 2 HD'', the mansion appears at the beginning of the game, [[Professor Elvin Gadd]] is working some inventions with some friendly [[Greenie]]s. However, when [[King Boo]] destroyed the Dark Moon, the Greenies became hostile, forcing the professor to escape the manor. Luigi is called upon E. Gadd to locate the second Dark Moon piece.

Luigi retrieves, the Poltergust 5000, Strobulb and the Dark-Light Device, along with capturing ghosts along the way (including three [[Poltergeist]]s).

Later, the whole manor becomes covered with spider webs, but they are soon removed by the ghost-catching plumber.

Luigi then heads to the [[Cellar (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Cellar]] to locate the source of the spider webs in the manor, only to fight a large, spider queen, who is possessed by the [[Grouchy Possessor]].

After retrieving the second Dark Moon piece, fog is cleared to make way to the [[Haunted Towers]], but ghosts have returned to the manor, but are soon cleared out.

=== ''[[Luigi's Mansion Arcade]]'' ===

=== ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' ===
A replica of the Gloomy Manor can be found on the [[Master Suite]], the last floor of [[The Last Resort]]. It can only be examined once. A [[Golden Goob]] will appear after examining it.

Portraits of the Gloomy Manor appear throughout the Last Resort, for example in the hallway on [[RIP Suites|5th floor]] next to the entrance of [[Room 502]] and on the left wall in the [[False Bedroom]] on the [[Twisted Suites|11th]] floor.
