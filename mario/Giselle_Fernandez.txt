'''Giselle Fernandez''' is an American voice actor. Shortly after the release of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', Fernandez revealed on X that they are the voice of [[Princess Daisy]] in the game, succeeding [[Deanna Mustard]].<ref>GZFernVO (October 20, 2023). "[https://twitter.com/GZFernVO/status/1715442836169183277 It’s out! Catch me in #SuperMarioBrosWonder as the voice of the coolest princess (what I’m totally not biased) Princess Daisy! AND YEAH SHE IS PLAYABLE IN THIS ONE! Woo!! :D]" ''X''. Retrieved October 20, 2023.</ref> They're also the voice of [[Baby Daisy]] since ''[[Mario Kart World]]''.
