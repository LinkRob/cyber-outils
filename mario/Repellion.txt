'''Repellion''' is one of the [[microgame]]s within [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player controls [[Wario]], who is piloting a spaceship below a group of flying UFOs hovering around at varying speeds. The objective is to shoot all every UFO using the spaceship's remaining laser blasts. Once every UFO has been destroyed, the player wins at the microgame. If Wario fails to shoot a UFO, his spaceship explodes, causing the player to lose the microgame as a result. Another way for them to lose is if Wario does not shoot every UFO on time.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels feature one, two, and three UFOs respectively.
