|games         = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Shocker''' is a special move used by [[Mallow]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He learns this technique at level 10.

==Description==
When Shocker is used, a large yellow lightning bolt rains down on an opponent.

==Effects==
Shocker is a very strong technique and is able to strike a single foe for high damage. Although this move is powerful and very effective against enemies and/or bosses who are weak to [[Thunder (element)|thunder-based]] attacks (such as [[Belome]]), it lacks the multi-hit properties of [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]].
