'''Luigi in the Cage''' is the first [[mission]] of [[Tick Tock Clock]] in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It replaces [[Roll into the Cage]] from the original ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

== Objective ==
The mission requires playing as Luigi to become [[Vanish Luigi]] and pass through a cage containing the mission's [[Power Star]].
