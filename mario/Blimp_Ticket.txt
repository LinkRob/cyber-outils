The '''Blimp Ticket''' is a key item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Blimp Ticket allows [[Mario]] to ride to [[Glitzville]] via [[Rogueport]]'s blimp. [[Mario]] receives it when he finds [[Francesca Pianta|Francesca]], [[Don Pianta]]'s daughter, and [[Frankie Pianta|Frankie]], her boyfriend.
