Lethal Goob is a kind of rare ghost in'' [[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. It is a form of [[Goob]], but they are brown ghosts glowing red, they will only appear in the [[ScareScraper]]'s twenty floors mod, on the tenth floor. They have 250HP, more than the double life of a Goob. Lethal Goobs appeared after the update in 2020.

== Profile ==
Lethal Goobs is one of the forms of [[Goob]], instead of having blue bodies and white eyes, they have red and black bodies with red eyes. They are defeated in the same way as any kind of Goobs.

==In ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''==
Like all Lethal ghosts, Lethal Goobs can knock out [[Luigi]] in one hit. They won't appear when the doors open, however, they will appear after the door opened for a while. In order to defeat Lethal Goobs, Luigi needs to flash them and vacuum them up.
