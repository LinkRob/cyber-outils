'''Enemy Battles''' are a type of course in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] port ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
Similar to the [[Enemy Course]]s from other {{series|Super Mario}} games, the only objective of an Enemy Battles course is to defeat every enemy within the [[time limit]], upon which a [[Green Power Star|Green Star]] appears, and collecting it ends the course. Unlike Enemy Courses, Enemy Battles are required to be completed in order to progress, and walking to an Enemy Battles course on the map does not cause the player's character to forcefully enter it.

Most Enemy Battles take place in small [[Fortress]]-like rooms with framed images of the [[Bowser]] emblem lining the wall.

==List of Enemy Battles==
Like the standard levels, each Enemy Battle has both its own level number and level name. All Enemy Battles courses have the word "Blockade" in the title. The first three [[world]]s have only one Enemy Battle course, and the following three worlds have two Enemy Battles, with the second one involving fighting [[Fire Bro]]s.

*World 1-A: [[Chargin' Chuck Blockade]]
*World 2-A: [[Big Galoomba Blockade]]
*World 3-A: [[Magikoopa Blockade]]
*World 4-A: [[Brolder Blockade]]
*World 4-B: [[Fire Bros. Hideout 1|Fire Bros. Hideout #1]]
*World 5-A: [[Chargin' Chuck Blockade Is Back]]
*World 5-B: [[Fire Bros. Hideout 2|Fire Bros. Hideout #2]]
*World 6-A: [[Prince Bully Blockade]]
*World 6-B: [[Fire Bros. Hideout 3|Fire Bros. Hideout #3]]
*World Castle-A: [[Brolder Blockade Is Back]]
*World Castle-B: [[Prince Bully Blockade Is Back]]
*World Castle-C: [[Fire Bros. Hideout 4|Fire Bros. Hideout #4]]
