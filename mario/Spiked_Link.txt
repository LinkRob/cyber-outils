The '''Spiked Link''' is [[Bowser]]'s second weapon in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the shop in [[Monstro Town]] for 94 [[coin]]s.

== Description ==
The Spiked Link is a spiked metal ball on a chain. It is one of Bowser's most powerful weapons, although his strongest and ultimate weapon is [[Drill Claw]]. Equipping the Spiked Link increases Bowser's [[Attack]] power by 30.

When using the Spiked Link in battle, Bowser swings it in circles, as he would with a [[Chomp]], and then throws it at the targeted enemy. If a [[Timed Hit]] is performed, the background flashes in white for the last animation, and the Spiked Link's spikes extend the moment it hits an enemy, dealing more damage to it.
