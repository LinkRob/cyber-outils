'''Golden Pipe''' is a recurring item in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
A Golden Pipe is a gold-colored [[Warp Pipe]] functioning similarly to the [[Genie Lamp]]/[[Magic Lamp]], [[Wiggler Capsule]]/[[Flutter Orb]], and especially the [[Star Pipe]] from earlier ''Mario Party'' games ([[Mario Party 2]] through [[Mario Party 7|7]] and [[Mario Party DS|DS]]). Upon being used, the Golden Pipe places its user one [[space]] away from the [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]].

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''[[Super Mario Party]]''===
If the Golden Pipe is used, a character can buy the Star during the same turn unless they roll a 0.

===''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''===
The Golden Pipe can be bought at an [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shop]] for 25 [[coin]]s during the later turns. Because it is impossible to roll a 0, the using the Golden Pipe guarantees the user will land on the [[Star Space]] on the same turn.
