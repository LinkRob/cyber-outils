'''Snifberg the Unfeeling''' is a boss from [[Yoshi's Woolly World|''Yoshi's Woolly World'']]. He is encountered in [[World 5 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 5]] (an icy world) as the area boss, encountered in [[Snifberg the Unfeeling's Castle|his castle at the end of the world]]. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] encounters him as [[Ice Snifit]], but [[Kamek]] uses his magic to enlarge him into a giant.

== Battle ==
The battle with Snifberg takes place within a room with an icy platform that serves as the battlefield. Snifberg starts at the right of the platform, with a [[Spike Trap|bed of spikes]] at his back and another at the back of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].

When the battle beings, Snifberg spins in place as he blows ice, creating an ice barrier, which he uses as a shield against jumps and direct attacks. Snifberg then creates an ice block which he kicks towards Yoshi. He then summons three ordinary snifits. Afterwards, Snifberg creates spikes on his barrier, then proceeds to slam the ground with his last slam having him use his mask's nozzle as a rocket engine to fly high and then slam into the ground. If he isn't attacked, Snifberg will repeat his attack pattern. Yoshi must hit Snifberg the Unfeeling with his [[Yarn Ball|Yarn Balls]], causing the boss to fall onto the spikes which break his ice barrier and render his body vulnerable to [[Ground Pound]] attacks; however, Yoshi must be careful to retreat back to the platform as Snifberg is on a bed of spikes.

When his health reaches a certain point, Snifberg retreats into the background to perform his rage attack, replenishing his ice barrier and spitting spiked chunks of ice which roll down the stairs in the background towards Yoshi. After he finishes, he jumps down and uses a more aggressive variation of his original attack pattern. His ice block kick attack now has him create five ice blocks, with two of them being taller than normal, and his Ice Snifits are summoned at the same time. After Yoshi repeats his strategy once more, Snifberg repeats his rage attack again, then uses an even more aggressive version of his original pattern, with his ice kick attack having him create a stack of five blocks and four spiked ice chunks from his rage attack and his Ice Snifit summon now having five Ice Snifits appear. He must be knocked off the platform into the spikes and then attacked again with Ground Pound attacks until he is defeated.

== Behind The Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Snifberg the Unfeeling derives his name from the word [[Snifit]] merged with "iceberg." His title of "the Unfeeling" comes from cold temperatures causing numbness, with some influence from how lack of emotion is often associated with the cold.

== Nomenclature ==
Snifberg is unnamed in Japanese and Korean, merely being called a "Big Ice Snifit" (Japanese: ''koori no Mūcho Ōkii'' (written 氷のムーチョ大きい ); Korean ''Geodae A-iseu Muuchyo'' (written 거대 아이스 무우쵸)). His Spanish name, ''Friolfiti el Gélido'', means "Cold-Snifit the Frosty," but can also mean "Cold-Snifit the Coldhearted" (''Friolfiti'', "Cold-Snifit," comes from ''frio'' ("(the) cold") and ''Olfiti'' (the Spanish name for the [[Snifit]]).). His Italian name, ''Freddy il Freddoloso'', is a pun on ''freddo'' ("(the) cold"). French refers to the boss as ''Glafit le Givré'', meaning "Ice Snifit the Frosted" (Ice Snifits themselves are called "Glafit" in French.). German refers to him as "Iceberg Snifit," (translated as ''Eisberg-Snifit'').
