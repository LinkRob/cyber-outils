'''Matter''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
Matter is an environmental element functioning like a spotlight, and it makes [[platform]]s and terrain manifest wherever it occupies. When the matter shifts through space, the terrain disappears behind it. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] does not keep up, he falls through the [[Pit|empty space]] below and loses a life.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy''==
Matter appears in [[Ghostly Galaxy]] and [[Matter Splatter Galaxy]], and parts of them feature matter as bouncing droplets that briefly make terrain appear in the spots they strike.

==Trivia==
*A similar concept is incorporated into [[Haunty Halls Galaxy]] and [[Sweet Mystery Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', where some platforms appear only under the shine of [[Bulb Yoshi]]'s light. The enemy [[Mattermouth]] inverts this concept by eating through terrain and leaving empty space in its wake.
