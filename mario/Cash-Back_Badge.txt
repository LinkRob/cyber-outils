The '''Cash-Back Badge''' is a [[badge]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be bought from [[Peach's Castle]] for 800 [[coin]]s. Equipping the badge allows [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] receive the number of coins equivalent to half the value of the items they use.
