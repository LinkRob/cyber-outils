==Effects==
Gold Medals can only be purchased from [[Notso]]'s shop for 500 coins and using a Gold Medal adds 10,000 points to the score. Gold Medals are rather useful for trying to level up quicker but for the rather steep price of 500 coins, it's better to gain the points by defeating enemies.
