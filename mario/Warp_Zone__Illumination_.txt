The '''Warp Zone''' is a magical dimension that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]] movie series.

==History==
In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', the Warp Zone appears as the dimension Mario and Luigi cross when they enter the [[Mysterious Pipe (Illumination)|Mysterious Pipe]]. The Warp Zone contains multiple clouds with Warp Pipes emerging from them. While most of the clouds are pink, the cloud with the pipe that takes Luigi to the [[Dark Lands (Illumination)|Dark Lands]] is a stormy, black cloud.

The Warp Zone later reappears when Mario leads the [[Bomber Bill (Illumination)|Bomber Bill]] into the mysterious pipe. When the Bomber Bill enters the zone, it explodes, causing a shockwave and a massive interdimensional collapse that pulls elements of the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] into [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]].
