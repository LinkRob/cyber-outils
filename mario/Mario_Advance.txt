The '''Mario Advance''' series for [[Game Boy Advance]] were ports of earlier games:
#[[Super Mario Advance]] in March 2001 was a port of the July 1993 [[Super Mario All-Stars]] update of the 9 October 1998 game [[Super Mario Bros. 2]] for SNES
#[[Super Mario Advance 2: Super Mario World]] in December 2001 was a port of the November 1990 game [[Super Mario World]] for SNES
#[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]] in September 2002 was a port of the Aug 1995 game [[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]] for SNES
#[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]] in July 2003 was a port of the July 1993 [[Super Mario All-Stars]] update of the 23 October 1988 game [[Super Mario Bros. 3]]

There is no fifth game in the series.
