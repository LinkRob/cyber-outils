The '''Coliseum''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Description==
The Coliseum is a large arena located in [[Castle MacFrights]]. It is is accessed from the door at the east end of the [[Coliseum Hallway]], but it can also be seen from the [[Coliseum Balcony]]. The arena is pill-shaped and has five windows with wooden doors on them from which arrows come out.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3''==
The boss battle with [[King MacFrights]] takes place here. During this, the stands are filled with cardboard cut-outs of [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]], which cheer for King MacFrights but boo [[Luigi]] throughout the battle.
