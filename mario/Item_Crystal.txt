'''Item Crystals''' (also called '''Ice Crystals''', '''Star Crystals''', or simply '''Crystals''') are three-layer glass crystals found throughout the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games. Most of the time, these crystals contain items such as [[Star Bit]]s, [[Coin]]s, [[1-Up Mushrooms]], or [[Life Mushroom]]s. In some cases, [[Toad (species)|Toads]], [[Luma (species)|Lumas]], and even [[Goomba]]s can be frozen in Item Crystals. Item Crystals can easily be shattered by [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] if he uses a [[Star Spin]], upon breaking on the Item Crystal, the plumbers may obtain the goods which were previously frozen inside. Item Crystals have fairly weak structures and can be shattered in both games with [[Star Spin]]s, the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Rainbow Star]], or [[Rock Mushroom]], other items such as [[Shell]]s, [[Coconut]]s/[[Watermelon]]s, bosses such as [[Dino Piranha]] can also shatter an Item Crystal, crashing through one while using a [[Launch Star]] or [[Sling Star]] is another method to shattering an Item Crystal.

==History==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[Image:Item Crystals.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] near heaps of Item Crystals in the [[Space Junk Galaxy]].]]
Item Crystals are first seen in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', in this game, they are first seen during the [[Star Festival]]. When [[Bowser]] attacks the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] with his fleet of [[Airship]]s, he hails down several [[Meteor]]s on the Kingdom freezing any [[Toad (species)|Toads]] that were hit inside Item Crystals, [[Mario]] cannot do anything to free these Toads at this point however it is assumed they are freed after the universe is reset at the game's end. Item Crystals can be found all over the game and are present in almost every Galaxy and can be easily shattered with [[Star Spin]]s, most of the time, they contain [[Coin]]s, [[Star Bit]]s, a [[1-Up Mushroom]], or a [[Life Mushroom]], in some cases [[Toad (species)|Toads]], [[Luma]]s, and even [[Goomba]]s can be frozen in Item Crystals, freeing Toads and Lumas is usually required to progressing forwards whereas freeing a frozen Goomba will break it free causing it to attack as any Goomba would, freed Goombas can be beaten for [[Coin]]s or [[Star Bit]]s. Some Item Crystals are also octahedron but these ones contain only power-ups such as [[Fire Flower]]s, [[Ice Flower]]s, [[Bee Mushroom]]s, [[Red Star]]s, [[Spring Mushroom]]s, [[Boo Mushroom]]s, and [[Rainbow Star]]s. There are larger Item Crystals which contain frozen [[Launch Star]]s or [[Sling Star]]s, there are also even larger Item Crystals which are much thicker which contain [[Power Star]]s, these Item Crystals require two Star Spins to destroy thus freeing the Star. There are some Item Crystals which contain nothing and mainly only block your path so breaking these is entirely optional. The quantity of [[Star Bit]]s obtained will average from three to ten, and most of the Item Crystals found throughout the game will contain four to six.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[Image:Item Crystals Snow.png|thumb|right|Some Item Crystals seen in the snow park of the [[Freezy Flake Galaxy]].]]
Item Crystals are seen again in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', in this game, they are similar to how they were in the previous game and once again make appearances in virtually every Galaxy however a new type of Item Crystal was also introduced. These Item Crystals in appearance seems to be overall more geometric, unlike the original ones, which are more abstract in shape. They are also non-transparent meaning it is impossible to indicate what Item is inside until the Item Crystal has been shattered. Typically, these Item Crystals contain more [[Star Bit]]s than the transparent ones. These once again contain the usual [[Coin]]s, [[Star Bit]]s, [[1-Up Mushroom]]s, and [[Life Mushroom]]s inside. Whilst [[Toad (species)|Toads]] and [[Luma]]s are never seen trapped in these Item Crystals, [[Goomba]]s still are, in fact, in some cases a [[Flipbug]] can also be inside these Item Crystals. Additionally, [[Yoshi Egg]]s can also be freed from these Item Crystals to use [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in some Galaxies, the [[Boulder Bowl Galaxy]] also has two much larger (almost unbreakable) Purple Item Crystals. These Item Crystals can only be broken by rolling into them twice under the effects of a [[Rock Mushroom]], the first Item Crystal contains a [[Comet Medal]], [[Banktoad]] can be seen trying to shatter this one open (by the orders of the [[Toad Brigade Captain]] while [[Yellow Toad (Toad Brigade)|Yellow Toad]] sleeps beside him) and the second one contains a [[Sling Star]] which leads to the Crystal Planet leading to [[Rollodillo]]'s Lair.
