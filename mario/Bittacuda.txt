}}A '''Bittacuda''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Bittacudas are only found in the [[Tile Pool]] of Chapter 3-2. Bittcudas are often found in groups and attack by biting at one of the heroes. [[Bowser]]'s fire breath is the best way to take out Bittcudas.
