The '''Box''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Box can only be obtained during [[McGoomba]]'s [[Trouble Center|trouble]]. McGoomba tells [[Mario]] to deliver the Box to [[Goomfrey]] in [[Rogueport]]. The true contents of the Box is never revealed as McGoomba doesn't want Mario or anyone else to know what's inside besides Goomfrey.

==Trivia==
*The Box is similar to the [[Tape (Paper Mario)|Tape]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and the [[You-Know-What]] from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' as all of these are side-quest items that Mario isn't allowed to know the contents of.
