'''Flippers''' are an obstacle in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', specifically within the last area of [[Waluigi Pinball]].

==Description==
Flippers automatically move forward then back while remaining locked in a position. It can hit one of the [[Pinball (obstacle)|pinballs]], just like flippers in a regular game of pinball, and doing so causes the pinball to rapidly zoom across the field, putting the racers at risk. Whenever a flipper moves, a green electrical wave can be seen on its black panel on the front. If a racer drives into a flipper, their [[kart]] briefly crashes.
