A '''Dark Eeligon''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are a stronger variant of [[Eeligon]]s.

==History==
Dark Eeligons are only encountered in rooms 62 and 74 of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Dark Eeligons behave the same as their lighter counterparts, except being stronger and having more HP. Oddly enough, Dark Eeligons behave the same as [[Hooligon]]s in contrast to Eeligons as both Dark Eeligons and Hooligons hop on the ground.
