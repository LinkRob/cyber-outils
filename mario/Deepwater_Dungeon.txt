'''Deepwater Dungeon '''is the fifth level of [[World Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Description==
===Features===
Deepwater Dungeon has many moving bodies of water and spiked walls.

==Collectibles==
===Green Stars===
*'''Green Star 1''': After the moving bodies of water, the player will need the [[Cat Mario|cat form]] to reach an invisible wall with the Green Star.
*'''Green Star 2''': After the [[Plessie]] section, the player will once again need the cat form to climb walls to reach the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Before the [[Warp Pipe]] leading to the Goal Pole, the player will need to use the cat form to use the Cloud Cannon, where the player will find the star in Coin Heaven.

===Stamp===
[[File:Deepwater_Dungeon_Stamp.png|thumb]]
In a room with rising water and giant spikes, stay to the far left and collect the stamp at the top of the room.
