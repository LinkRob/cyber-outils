'''Aqua Land''' is a one-time location in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', in which it only appears in the episode "[[Mario of the Deep]]."

==Description==
Aqua Land is based on the sunken city of {{w|Atlantis}}. It is where the [[Mermushroom]]s live, and Aqua Land is ruled over by [[King Neptune]].
