'''Togerobo''' (とげロボ, meaning ''Spike Robot'') is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. It is a robot with a spike ball for a head and spikes on its front. Togerobo attacks by letting its head drop and roll towards [[Wario]].
