'''Sir Goob''' is a ghost in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' and a form of [[Goob]], they only appear on the [[ScareScraper]]'s castle-themed floors. They have 200HP and are wearing medieval-styled clothing.

== Appearance ==
Sir Goob looks like regular Goobs, they have blue bodies, white eyes and mouth. They also wear medieval clothing.

== Description ==
Sir Goobs only appear in ScareScraper's castle-themed floors, they appear in a lot of rooms, unlike [[Slabhammer]]s, they don't appear in rooms that have secret doors. They are defeated in the same way as Goobs.
