==Description==
Paint Guys are [[Shy Guy]]s who carry buckets of paint above their heads.

==Battle==
Paint Guys mainly attack [[Mario]] by trying to throw their buckets of paint on him. The player must press A fast in order to block this attack. If unsuccessful, the Paint Guy will then throw the bucket of paint on Mario and leave him Soggy for a few turns. After the attack, the Paint Guys throw away their buckets and become regular Shy Guys again. Like with Clip Guys, Paint Guys only appear in battle alongside other enemies.
