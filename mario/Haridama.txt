'''Haridama''' are enemies in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Haridama are black fish with large, orange lips.

===Behavior===
Haridama swim back and forth, and they only turn upon reaching a wall. If a Haridama is next to Wario, it exposes its spikes and attempts to charge into him. If Wario bumps a Haridama from either the side or below, it swims forward in a frenzy. If a Haridama is hit from below, it becomes stunned temporarily and floats toward the surface of the water before continuing to swim around.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
[[File:Haridama screenshot.png|thumb|left|Wario encountering a Haridama in ''Wario Land 3'']]
Haridama are first encountered in [[The Pool of Rain]], where three are encountered, and later return in [[Beneath the Waves]] and [[The Big Bridge]], where eight and ten are encountered, respectively. If Wario touches a Haridama, he gets stunned and floats slowly to the surface of the water.
