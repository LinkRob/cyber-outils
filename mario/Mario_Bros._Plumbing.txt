'''Mario Bros. Plumbing'''  is a business owned by [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], located at [[Brooklyn]].

== [[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]] ==
In Mario Bros. we see business do its job: plumbing. [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], an unnamed [[Yellow Mario|third]] and [[Blue Mario|fourth]] brother appear to be removing [[Shellcreeper|Shellcreepers]], [[Sidestepper|Sidesteppers]], and [[Fighter Fly|Fighter Flies]] from the sewers.<gallery>
File:Mario Advance Mario Brothers.png
</gallery>

== In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' ==
Mario Bros. Plumbing is the main setting for every live-action segment in ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''.

== In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' ==
Mario Bros. Plumbing made its appearance at the official course called [[Did Somebody Call a Plumber?]].
