'''Red Goombas''' are an enemy found in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. They behave similarly to [[Goomba]]s with the only difference being is that they walk much slower and make brief stops. They usually appear after stomping on a [[Red Paragoomba]]. Despite the color change, Red Goombas are no stronger than a normal Goomba and can be defeated with any attack. Red Goombas are commonly seen in [[Ladida Plains]], [[Hoo's Wood]], [[Shriek Mansion]], and [[Bowser's Villa]].
