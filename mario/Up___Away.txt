'''Up & Away''' is a Special Move which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. [[Mario]] learns it from [[Kalmar]] after freeing him by defeating the [[Crystal King]] at the [[Crystal Palace]].

== Effects ==
Using Up & Away requires two [[Star Power|SP]] to use. Upon using the move, Kalmar appears and turns all enemies onscreen into stars, causing them to fly away and therefore ending the battle. However, Up & Away has a few drawbacks: it is not effective on bosses; it has a chance of not working on enemies, and using the move results in no [[Star Point]]s being gained. Up & Away should only be used against weak enemies that barely give up any more Star Points, such as [[Goomba]]s.

== Trivia ==
*The nature of Up & Away is similar to [[Lady Bow]]'s [[Spook (move)|Spook]] technique as it scares all of the enemies on the screen away with no Star Point award.
