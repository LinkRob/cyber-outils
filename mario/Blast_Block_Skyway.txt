'''Blast Block Skyway''' is the eighth level of World Flower in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. It is quite similar to [[Beep Block Skyway]], but the [[Beat Block]]s are four times as fast, and their beeping is muted. It can be unlocked by completing [[Shiftier Boo Mansion]], and its completion opens [[Honeycomb Skyway]].

==Features==
The level is similar to the [[Beat Block Galaxy]]'s [[Prankster Comet]] mission [[Silver Stars in Double Time]] from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', where the blocks move faster at the beat.
==Layout==
The course is an altered version of World 4-3: [[Beep Block Skyway]], with [[Double Cherry|Double Cherries]] replaced by [[Super Leaf|Super Leaves]], and the [[Blinking Block|Beep Block]]s switching 4 times faster than before. However, there are no longer beeping sounds to help time jumps. Many [[Spike]]s appear in the level.
==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': At the end of the first area, hovering over a [[? Block]]
*'''Green Star 2''': The second green star is right near the [[Checkpoint Flag]], activated by collecting all 8 [[Green Coin]]s from the Green Star Ring and using [[Propeller Platform]]s (replaced by invisible clouds in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'').
*'''Green Star 3''': In the middle of the Beep Block slide at the end of the level.
