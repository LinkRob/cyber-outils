}}|shows=*''[[Donkey Kong Country (television series)|Donkey Kong Country]]''|comics=*[[Club Nintendo|''Donkey Kong Country'' (Club Nintendo Comic)]]}}The '''Banana Hoard''' is a large stash of bananas belonging to [[Donkey Kong]] that appears in the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong series]]''. It is often the target of [[King K. Rool]] and other villains that tend to steal them forcing Donkey Kong to go out on an adventure and retrieve them.

==Appearances==
===''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''===
This game continues the trend of Donkey Kong's Banana Hoard being stolen. However, [[King K. Rool]] has stolen the Banana Hoard as means of slowing Donkey Kong down while he charges his [[Blast-o-Matic]] to blow up [[DK Isles]]. The bananas from the Banana Hoard are scattered throughout levels and appear as the main collectible items known as [[Golden Banana]]s. After K. Rool has been defeated, Donkey Kong (along with [[Cranky Kong]]) head into the caves where Donkey Kong builds back up his Banana Hoard.
