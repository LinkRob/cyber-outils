'''Secret Specs''' is a collectible item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The item replaces the [[Power Grip]] that also references [[w:c:Metroid:Metroid (series)|Metroid]].

==Usage==
If the item is equipped, the player can see the hidden [[? Block]]s.
