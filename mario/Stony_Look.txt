'''Stony Look''' is a [[Microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''. It is similar in its premise to "[[Stop! Go!]]" from ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]'' and ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', and its gameplay involves the Choo-choo form. [[Wario]] has it in his first set, which is involved in the level Stone-Cold Welcome.

==Layout==
Medusa, who is giant and has glowing red eyes and wears a mask resembling [[Wario]]'s facial features and calls herself "Wadusa," looms in the background on the opposite side of a car to a character who must get to the car. The setting depends on the difficulty.

==Gameplay==
The goal is to walk to the car in the time limit; doing so results in a victory. However, the player must not move when Medusa turns her eyes to the camera. (Only the in-game characters will be subjected to her stare; anyone outside the game is immune.) If she spots a character walking, Medusa will turn them to stone, causing a temporary [[hardlock]] which causes an instant loss. Medusa will look away after a second, giving players plenty of time to run to the car after avoiding her.

===Difficulty Levels===

* '''Level 1 (Easy)''': Medusa does rare fake-outs and rarely stares. The car is rather close.
* '''Level 2 (Hard)''': The car is a bit farther away, and Medusa does fake-outs more often.
* '''Level 3 (Expert)''': The car is way in the distance and Medusa does more elaborate fake-outs.

=== Multiplayer ===
Both players start side-by-side, and must make it to the car while avoiding Medusa. If she catches one character with her stare while the second player escapes, the microgame still ends in a loss.

== Trivia ==

* Originally, Medusa could turn victims to stone without them moving.
