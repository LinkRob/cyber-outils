'''New York City''' is the largest city in the [[United States of America]], that appeared in the [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|''Super Mario Bros.'' film series]] produced by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]] is where [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]], and his brother [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] were born and raised in, before moving to the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]], after saving the town from [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]'s attack.

==Real History==
On September 11th, 2001, in Manhattan New York City, Two planes collided into the twin towers of The World Trade Center, while a third plane crashed into the pentagon, which is the headquarters of the U.S. Department of Defense in Virginia, and a fourth plane crashed in a rural Pennsylvania field during a passenger revolt, where the Flight 93 National Memorial was established near the US Captiol Building also known as the [[White House]], leaving 2,996 people dead, and 6,000–25,000+ injured.
