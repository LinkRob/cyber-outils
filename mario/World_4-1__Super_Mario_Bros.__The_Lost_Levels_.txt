'''World 4-1''' is the first [[level]] of [[World 4 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 4]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the thirteenth level overall in said game.

==Layout==
Moving to the right from their starting position, the player will encounter red [[Piranha Plant]]s and a [[Lakitu]]; there is also a [[Coin Block]] in the bottom [[Brick Block]] of the upper pillar. In the row of [[? Block]]s ahead, there is a power-up in the far-right block, followed by a [[Trampoline|Springboard]] that can help the player get over a large gap. After the gap is a flight of stairs with a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] directly against the back of it. In the next row of ? Blocks, there is a [[Poison Mushroom]] in the far-right block, and there is a power-up in the right block in the next group of ? Blocks. Using said ? Blocks as a platform, the player can reach the pipes above, the left of which is a [[Warp Pipe]] to an underwater area with [[Coin]]s, [[Blooper]]s, and [[Buzzy Beetle]]s that will exit out to a pipe near the one that was entered. In the Brick Block above is a [[Beanstalk]] that leads to a [[Coin Heaven]], though it will drop the player at the end to the stairs with the hidden 1-Up Mushroom from earlier. After the brick with the Beanstalk is a flight of stairs with a [[Goomba]] walking down them, followed by the [[Goal Pole]].
