'''Clams''' are a purple underwater species directly based on their [[wikipedia:clam|real-world counterpart]]. Aside from appearing as enemies in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', Clams have made few appearances in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series. The most recurring clam species is [[Clampy]], which debuted in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 64 / Super Mario 64 DS''===
[[Image:Clam 64.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario near an opening Clam.]]
[[Image:Clam DS.png|thumb|200px|Yoshi near an opening Clam with a Red Coin.]]
Clams are first seen in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and later reappear in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', in this game, they can be found in the waters of [[Jolly Roger Bay]] often storing [[Red Coin]]s, [[Shell]]s, and [[1-Up Mushroom]]s inside, whenever [[Mario]] or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] comes near, they open up their lids allowing the heroes to obtain whatever treasure is inside, after a few seconds however, they will snap their lids back shut. The Clam can damage the heroes if it slams it's lid shut on them or if they are immobile however when their lids are open, they're completely harmless. Clams cannot be destroyed in these games.
{{br}}
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[Image:Clam Beach Bowl.png|thumb|200px|Mario near a Clam.]]
Clams reappear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. In this game, Clams can be found in the [[Beach Bowl Galaxy]] holding [[Star Chip]]s or [[Coin]]s, Clams once again open their lids as [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] approaches and shut them after a while damaging the plumbers if they're snapped, Clams are completely harmless otherwise but other than that, Clams cannot be destroyed. In this game, the Clams now have a pair of eyes.
{{br}}

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[Image:Clam Starshine Beach.png|thumb|left|200px|A Clam in the underwater world.]]
Clams return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' this time only appearing in the [[Starshine Beach Galaxy]] and once again retaining their appearance from before, they are once again found underwater storing items such as [[Coin]]s and open their lids as [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] are near, just as before, Clams can only damage the plumbers as their lid shuts and cannot be destroyed. Clams are much rarer this time as well.
{{br}}
==In ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''==
[[Image:Fortune Island Clam.png|thumb|200px|Wendy emerging from a Clam.]]
Clams appear on [[Fortune Island]]. [[Wendy O. Koopa]] is seen emerging from one as [[Mario]] and [[Huey]] approach the [[Big Paint Star]] ready to engage the two in battle, in battle, Wendy attacks by summoning [[Coin]]s from the Clams and dunking them at [[Mario]] for damage, if Mario fails to block the attack, she can also summon [[Gold Shy Guy]]s, [[Silver Shy Guy]]s, and [[Ice Bro]]s. from the Clams as well, in this game, the Clams return to the initial appearance.
{{br}}
==Trivia==
*The Clams in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' strongly resemble the [[Clampy|Clampies]] which debuted in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' however these aren't the same Clampies as they are white rather than pink and they aren't referred to as such in-game.
