'''Koopa Corps''' is a [[Special Attack]] used by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. The attack costs 10 [[Special Point|SP]], and is unlocked after Bowser frees the Koopas in [[Blubble Lake]].

When Bowser uses this move, a ball of [[Koopa Troopa]]s appears in his hand. Then, Bowser retreats into his shell, and several [[Koopa Shell]]s appear and bounce off a random enemy, damaging them. The player must move Bowser up and down with the {{button|ds|Stylus}} to hit the Koopa Shells back and forth. The Koopa Shells move faster with each hit, and more come in.

==Trivia==
*[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can talk to the Koopas before Bowser releases them. The Koopas will brag to the brothers about how Bowser is so nice to make a great appearance. They then will realize that Bowser did not free them, and will then demand that Mario and Luigi leave them alone.
*In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', this attack is similar to [[Roy Koopa|Roy]] bumping [[Wendy O. Koopa]] trying to attack Mario and Luigi.
