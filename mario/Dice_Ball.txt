The '''Dice Ball''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
The Dice Ball is one of three volleyballs used in the Bonus minigame [[Beach Volleyball (Mario Party 5)|Beach Volleyball]]. It is a combination of a [[Dice Block]] and a volleyball. Every hit, including a serve, causes the Dice Ball to shuffle the numbers around between one and six along with its shuffling sound, just like in a regular Party Mode match. The number shown on the Dice Block is the number of [[point]]s the scoring team earns when the Dice Ball hits the sand.
