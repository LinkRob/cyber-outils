'''Neil''' is a character who appears in ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' series. He is a young child who dreams of becoming the ultimate golfer. He first appears in ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]'', along with [[Ella]], as one of [[Kid]]'s two students, although Neil makes his technical debut in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]'', in which he can become playable if using the Nintendo GameCube Game Boy Advance Link Cable to transfer him over from ''Mario Golf: Advance Tour''.
