The '''Fire Crystal''' is one of the four [[Elemental Crystals]] who fights alongside [[Culex]] in [[Monstro Town]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a sentient crystal associated with the element of fire. The Fire Crystal, like the other three Elemental Crystals, originates from the ''[[Final Fantasy]]'' franchise, but in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'', the Fire Crystal is from the dimension of [[Vanda]], like Culex.

The Fire Crystal, as it name states, is capable of using strong fire-based moves such as [[Flame Wall]] and [[Flame (move)|Flame]]. Though it is weak to Ice-based moves, the Fire Crystal's magical attack is rather high as well as its health, but its defense is quite low.
