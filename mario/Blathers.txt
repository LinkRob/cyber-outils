'''Blathers''' is a character from the ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' series, in which he is the [[w:c:animalcrossing:Museum|museum]] curator. He has made a few cameo appearances in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] as well as the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] and its spinoffs.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Blathers is a dark brown-feathered owl who wears a green bowtie. His face is light-colored and consists of large, white eyes with black pupils, a yellow beak, large, feathered eyebrows, and round, pink cheeks. Blathers' eyebrows are lined with light-brown dots. Being an owl, Blathers has a pair of wings for arms and a pair of yellow talons for legs. A curved, white line is near the tip of both of Blathers' wings. There are three light brown stripes on Blathers' legs and pointed, white toenails. Blathers' chest has a distinct white and light brown-checkered pattern.

===Personality traits===
Blathers has a phobia of bugs, which is mentioned in his various [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] descriptions. As an owl, Blathers is always found sleeping during the daytime.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
Blathers has only made cameo appearances within the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''===
[[File:Blathers SSBB model.png|thumb|left|x200px|A model of Blathers for ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
This is the first time Blathers appears in a game with [[Mario]] in it. He makes a cameo in the background of the [[Smashville]] stage, where he can be seen sleeping if the [[Wii]]'s internal clock is set during daytime. Blathers is also featured as both a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Sticker]] and a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|Trophy]], the latter of which features both Blathers and his sister [[Celeste]].
{{br|left}}
===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''===
Blathers returns a background character in Smashville, and he now also appears in its successor stage, [[Town and City]]. Blathers is now featured as a standalone trophy, unlike before.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Blathers is featured as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Support Spirit]]. His depiction reuses his artwork from ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing: New Leaf|Animal Crossing: New Leaf]]''.
{{sectstub}}

==In {{franchise|Mario}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
In the [[Animal Crossing (track)|Animal Crossing]] track, Blathers is one of the characters from the ''Animal Crossing'' franchise to appear in the background.

===''[[Super Mario Maker]]''===
[[File:Blathers SMM costume.png|frame|left]]
A [[Mystery Mushroom]] [[Costume Mario|costume]] of Blathers can be unlocked by either scanning an [[amiibo]] figure or card of him or at random from clearing the 100 Mario Challenge on any difficulty setting. While playing as Blathers, the player can press Up on the D-Pad to make him read a book.
{{br|left}}
==In ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''==
Scanning a Blathers amiibo unlocks a pattern for the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] designed after Blathers.

==In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''==
[[File:Blathers amiibo sketch WW Gold.png|thumb|200px|The crude drawing of Blathers done by Wario in ''WarioWare Gold'']]
In the [[Wario's amiibo Sketch]] [[souvenir]], if the player scans an amiibo of Blathers, [[Wario]] does a crudely-drawn sketch of him. Unlike his usual depiction, the crude drawing of Blathers shows dots on his belly, below his bowtie, rather than on Blathers's eyebrows.
