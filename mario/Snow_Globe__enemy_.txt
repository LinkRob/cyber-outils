'''Snow Globes''' are enemies in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
As stated in game, Snow Globes are made by the [[Wishstone]] alongside a snowstorm. They only appear in [[Sneezemore Cave]], where the Snow Globes float around in the air until [[Wario]] comes around. Snow Globes behave very similarly to [[Boo]]s as they don't move if Wario is looking at them. When he has their back to them, the Snow Globe will attempt to hit Wario by getting closer to him. Snow Globes can be defeated with any attacks and defeating one results in them turning into water droplets which is often used to bring life to [[Sipping Stalk]]s.
