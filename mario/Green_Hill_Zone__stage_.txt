'''Green Hill Zone''' is a recurring stage in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and returns in both ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
The stage features its classic style and terrain, and familiar Green Hill objects such as palm trees, totem poles, Item Boxes, and spinning flowers. It even features the iconic loop in the background, and [[Miles "Tails" Prower]], [[Knuckles the Echidna]], and [[Silver the Hedgehog]] often make a cameo running through the loop. If the terrain is attacked too often, a section of it collapses, but it can eventually repair itself. A [[Check Point (Sonic the Hedehog franchise)|Check Point]] occasionally appears on the stage, where it temporarily spins when attacked and deals damage to whoever else touches it.

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', the [[Final Destination]] form of Green Hill Zone takes place on a flat platform. The checkpoints also do not appear.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
{{Smashpedia|Green Hill Zone}}
===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
Green Hill Zone is a stage automatically unlocked when the player unlocks [[Sonic the Hedgehog]], whose unlock match also takes place here.

===''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS''===
Green Hill Zone makes a reappearance, but is now available from the start.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
Green Hill Zone appears as a returning stage, available from the start once more.
