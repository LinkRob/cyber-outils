'''Character Museum''' is a mode in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

== Description ==
The Character Museum allows the player to view all of the characters that are in the game. If a character is not unlocked, the character will not show up in the museum. There is a total of fifty-seven characters. The player can view the character's bio. The player can choose to view the character's winning and losing animations. Other characters have more than two animations that can be viewed. The player can choose a character to appear in the Plaza.
