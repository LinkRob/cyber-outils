'''Goomba's Game Board''' is a [[board]] hosted by [[Goomba]] in the [[Mario Party 4 (Flash game)|''Mario Party 4'' Flash game]]. At the center is a giant roulette, similar to the one on [[Goomba's Greedy Gala]], which will send to player to one of the three colored arrow spaces at random. At the top right junction of the board there is a [[Tiny pipe]], which costs one [[Mini Mushroom]] to cross.
