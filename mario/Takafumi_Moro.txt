'''Takafumi Moro''' is a graphic artist at [[1-UP Studio]]. He previously worked for Artoon, [[Sega]], and [[Nintendo]].

==''Mario''-related credits==
* ''[[Nintendo Land]]'' (2012) - 3D Design
* ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' (2013) - Staff (Character Design)
* ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' (Wii U version, 2014) - Staff
* ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' (2017) - Character Art
* ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' (Switch version, 2018) - Staff
* ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' (3DS version, 2018) - Staff
