|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Howl''' is a special attack used only by [[K-9]]s and [[Chow]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
With Howl, it can cause the [[Fear]] ailment to one of [[Mario]]'s party cutting their attack and defense in half. Wearing a Fearless Pin will prevent this.
