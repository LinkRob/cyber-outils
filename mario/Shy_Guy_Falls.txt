'''Shy Guy Falls''' is a course in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is the final course in the [[Flower Cup]].

==Layout==
Shy Guy Falls starts with a left turn on the side of a cliff, then a right turn. The player will end up on a waterfall with boost pads. After the falls, there is a wood bridge, leading to another water fall. After this water fall, there is a glide ramp leading to the side of a cliff again. Near the finish line, there is a hidden grassy shortcut between two sharp rocks that requires a mushroom.
