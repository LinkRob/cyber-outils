'''Condarts Away!''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level features many [[condart]]s. The level opens with three condarts destoring rows of {{lw|Brick Block}}s.

Later in the level is the [[Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will make the level top-down, and the player will walk on the [[semisolid]] in the background while avoiding condarts. Eventually the [[Wonder Seed]] is found surrounded by brick blocks. While the top brick blocks cannot be broken, the side walls can.
==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s are obtainable in the following order.
#To the far right of the first area, there is a condart under a semisolid protecting a 10-flower coin.
#Before the {{lw|Checkpoint Flag}} there is a 2x3 of brick blocks that will be destroyed by a condart if not accessed quickly. [[Jump]]ing on the set of brick blocks and traveling left will reveal two brick blocks. Hitting the one on the left will summon a flower. This will send the player to a secret area that will lead to a 10-flower coin.
