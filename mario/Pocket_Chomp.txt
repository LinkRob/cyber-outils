A '''Pocket Chomp''' is a type of [[Bros. Item]] which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is a [[Chain Chomp]] contained within a [[capsule]]. Upon being used, the Chain Chomp chases [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] and [[jump]]s on the enemy along the way. This can go on up until Mario or Luigi is hit or when defeating the enemy.
