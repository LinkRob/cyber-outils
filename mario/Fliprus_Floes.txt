'''Fliprus Floes''' is the secret level in [[Frosted Glacier]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Go on the crate under the twentieth and twenty-first icicle and have it raise closer to the platform above to reveal a hidden Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Jump over the spike being blown by the first River Piranha Plant.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Jump on the sixth Fliprus and get the third Star Coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
At the beginning of the level, find the hidden block at the same spot of the tree, jump on it, and find another one by just jumping revealing a 1-Up Mushroom and a picture of standard Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' jumping.
