==Effects==
To use a Ghost Shroom, the player must draw circles around an image of a Mushroom. This will summon the Ghost Shroom that will deal damage to any enemies around until it disappears. This item is rather rare as it can be only bought at [[Notso]]'s shop in [[Flopside]].
==Trivia==
*This Ghost Shroom resembles the [[Zombie Shroom]] and [[Ghoul Shroom]].
