The '''Monster''' tires are the default [[tire]]s in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. They are tied with the [[Red Monster]], [[Gold Tires]], and [[Slick]] tires in stats. They have good [[weight]] and [[Off-Road|off-road]], but have some of the lowest [[acceleration]] and [[handling]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' ==
The wheel's design is similar to the [[Tiny Titan]]'s wheels in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. They have high quality speed, weight and off-road. They are tied with the [[Red Monster]] tires.
