'''Mushroom Doom''' is a [[microgame]] featured in [[Dribble & Spitz]]'s [[Fantasy]] level in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to defeat every mushroom monster, either by attacking them directly or knocking a [[Spiked Ball|spiked ball]] onto their head. Once every monster has been defeated, the player wins at the microgame, indicating by a lot of treasure appearing.

If a character touches a spiked ball, they get knocked out, causing them to lose unless the spiked ball is moving in a way that allows for the remaining monsters to be defeated.

In two-player mode, if the players win Mushroom Doom, whoever defeated more monsters receives an effort point. If both players each defeat a monster on Level 1, neither of them receive an effort point because they defeated the same number of monsters.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' There is one monster.
*'''Level 2:''' There are two monsters, both on the same side.
*'''Level 3:''' There are two monsters, each on opposite ends.

In two-player mode, the changes between difficulty settings are different:

*'''Level 1:''' There are two monsters, which are opposite from each other.
*'''Level 2:''' There are three monsters.
*'''Level 3:''' There is one monster, but it hides and peeks out faster. It may also re-emerge from different holes.

In all three two-player difficulty levels, the monsters can hide in their hole for a short period of time.
