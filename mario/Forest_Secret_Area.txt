'''Forest Secret Area''' is a secret [[level]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. It is located in [[Forest of Illusion]], and can be accessed from the secondary exit of [[Forest of Illusion 4]]. Completing the level unlocks the level [[Forest Fortress]].
