'''Stilts''' are a pair of objects used by [[Stilt Guy]]s throughout the {{franchise|Mario}} and {{franchise|Yoshi}}. Stilts first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==In ''[[Paper Mario]]''==
Stilts are only seen used by the Stilt Guys that appear only during the [[General Guy]] fight in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] of Chapter 4. The Stilt Guys attack [[Mario]] by jumping on him with their stilts. The Stilt Guys can be knocked off their stilts reverting them to just regular [[Shy Guy]]s.
