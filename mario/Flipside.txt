}}|games=''[[Super Paper Mario]]''}}'''Flipside''' is a location that appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'']] series. It is a small city that serves outside from other dimensions and is highly geometric in design. Flipside has a sister city, [[Flopside]] that very few know about, with the exception of [[Merlon]] and [[Mario]]. Similarly, the citizens of Flopside know little to nothing about Flipside.

==Description==
Like [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and [[Rogueport]] before it, Flipside and its counterpart [[Flopside]] act as the central hub of the game, with each world or chapter spawning off from it. Whenever Mario obtains a new [[Pure Heart]], he can place one into the correct [[Heart Pillar]] in order to access another world from Flipside (or Flopside, depending on which Pure Heart/Heart Pillar) and unlock more on the two cities. There are many shops and friendly people here. There are also many secrets in Flipside, some of which are necessary to progress.
