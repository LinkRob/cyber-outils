'''Sled Creatures''' are enemies in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are short, big-headed snow beasts.

===Behavior===
Sled Creatures slalom down snowy slopes in indefinite numbers. If a Sled Creature hits [[Wario]], he gets pushed away a significant distance.

==In ''Wario World''==
Sled Creatures only appear in [[Shivering Mountains]]. They are unaffected by [[Wario]]'s attacks, although Wario cannot take damage from a Sled Creature either. There is a chance that a Sled Creature can push Wario far enough to the point of him falling into [[Unithorn's Lair]].

In the following level, [[Beanstalk Way]], there are herds of infinite [[Ram]]s that fulfill a similar role to Sled Creatures.
