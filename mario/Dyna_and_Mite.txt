'''Dyna and Mite''' are a pair of mole characters who appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
Dyna is a female mole who wears a polka-dotted bow whereas Mite is often seen in a small bag behind Dyna.

==History==
Dyna and Mite are siblings that live in [[Moleville]] with their parents. During [[Mario]]'s visit in Moleville, Dyna and Mite explore into [[Moleville Mines]] worrying their parents. Mario and company head down into Moleville Mines defeating the attention-seeking [[Punchinello]] along the way. Dyna and Mite are eventually found near a minecart and everyone escapes from Moleville Mines.

==Trivia==
*Dyna and Mite's names are collectively both named and spelled after dynamite, a type of powerful explosive.
