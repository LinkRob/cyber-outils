'''Hit Parade''' is a [[Job Center]] [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''.

==Gameplay==
While creating the microgame, the player has to draw something that pops out of the holes in the ground during the gameplay. For the microgame itself, [[rabbit]]s ([[mole]]s in the Japanese version) are the default object.

During gameplay, the player has to tap the rabbits with their stylus as they pop out from five holes in the ground. Winning the microgame requires at least three rabbits to be tapped. If time runs out without this goal being accomplished, the player loses.
