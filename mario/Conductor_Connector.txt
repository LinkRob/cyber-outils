The '''Conductor Connector''' is the final [[Special Attack]] gained in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. As with the rest of the Special Attacks in that game, it is a [[Bros. Attack]], although it is not created by [[Technikki]], as she got her spirit trapped in a dream by [[Reclusa]]'s [[Reclusa Dream Flower|flowers]]. It is the counterpart to the [[Mix Tornado]], its use costing 15 [[Special Point|Special Points]]. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] gets access to it when he and [[Luigi]] seek the [[Great Conductor]]'s advice in getting more Bonds after they wind up falling short in breaking into Reclusa's fortress.

== Procedure ==
When the player summons this attack, Mario pulls a Great Conductor plush from his pocket and tosses it into the ground in front of him, teleporting the Great Conductor himself to the battlefield.

=== Phase 1 ===
This attack comes in two phases. During the first phase, Luigi has to pull back to launch Mario with the free end of a plug from [[Concordia's World]] to an orbiting satellite, and Luigi has to pull on his end to stop it so Mario can plug it into the satellite. The player can aim using the left control stick.

=== Phase 2 ===
After connecting the satellites, the Great Conductor will charge a powerful laser from his rear, drawing energy from the satellites to do so, and takes aim at the targeted enemy. The player has to press X when the crosshairs line up in the centre. This attack deals massive damage to the targeted foe with a shockwave spreading to all other foes if the player gets it right. Firing at the wrong time causes the beams to be substantially weakened and hit all enemies with the damage being spread evenly.
