{{Infobox Microgame|name = Cheater!|image = [[File:WWSM Cheater!.png|thumb|250px]]|games = ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''|genre = [[Ashley]]|info = ''"Did you know that opposing sides of a die always equal 7?"''|forms = [[The Discard]]}}'''Cheater!''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The die will be lying flat on a random side. The player must move the [[Wii Remote]] to get the expected number prompted on-screen. By the microgame's description the clue is that the opposite sides equal 7.
*'''Level 1:''' Fair amount of time to find the number. The number is easy to find.
*'''Level 2:''' Less time to find the number. The number is hard to find.
*'''Level 3:''' Short time to find the number. The number is harder to find.
