'''Jabble''' is a young [[Jabbi]] that can be found in [[The Great Tree]]. Jabble appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is also the only known friendly Jabbi as he is friends with [[Punio]].

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
Encountering Jabble is optional but Jabble can be encountered in the [[Item Shop]] of The Great Tree. He is hiding in a corner but he is too afraid to get any closer. Fortunately, Punio manages to convince him that [[Mario]] is not the enemy and Jabble joins the group of Punis. Jabble does not count toward the current number of [[Puni]]s and it is not known whether he helps out in the battles against the other Jabbi.

== Trivia ==
*Jabble is the only known named Jabbi.
*In Super Paper Mario, a plush doll of Jabble can be seen in Fort Francis.
