The center of the plate is a raised semi-circular hatch that resembles the front of a [[Bob-omb]]. 
Bob-omb Dispensers typically appear to provide Bob-ombs for challenges, so Mario has to carry the Bob-omb to destroy a glass cover for either a [[Power Star]],[[Launch Star]] or [[Pipe]]. In ''Super Mario Galaxy'', four of them appear in the same spot in the [[Rolling Gizmo Galaxy]] as obstacles. Mario must avoid them as otherwise they will knock him and the Star Ball he's on off the edge.
