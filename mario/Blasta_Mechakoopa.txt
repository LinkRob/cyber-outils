'''Blasta Mechakoopa '''is a variant of Mechakoopas, first appearing in ''Super Mario Maker 2'' as part of the 3.0 update. Unlike their normal counterpart, Blasta Mechakoopas have a red coloration and wield the ability to launch homing missiles, the missiles will chase Mario.
