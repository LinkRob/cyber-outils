==History==
Wrinky Doors are found throughout the lobbies of worlds and approaching one has the ghost of the deceased [[Wrinkly Kong]] appear to give a Kong a hint for a world. There are five Wrinkly Doors per lobby each with a hint for a Kong.

==Wrinkly Kong's Hints==
===Jungle Japes===
*'''Donkey Kong''': "Get DK's Coconut Shooter from Funky's Store and use it to hit the coconut switches."
