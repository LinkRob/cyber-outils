The '''Watermelon Kart''' is a [[kart]] that appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Watermelon Kart takes its design off the [[Apple Kart]], but this time, based off the design of a {{W|watermelon}}, part its name. The kart's tires are modelled after round, watermelon slices. The star taillights are bright gold.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
As a High-End kart, the Watermelon Kart made its debut during the [[Ocean Tour]]. The kart can usually be obtained by placing high within ranked cups.

When used in races, the Watermelon Kart increases the duration of [[Rocket Start]]s and the points earned for landing them.
