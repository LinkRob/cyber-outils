The '''woman from a [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing (Illumination)|Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]] commercial''' is the minor opening character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
She is a redhead woman who wears a purple sunglasses on her head, purple-striped shirt, a pair of high heels sandals, and her toes are wearing pink toenails polish.

== Real-world influences ==
=== In pop culture ===
After she was presented in the Super Mario Bros. Plumbing commercial, one of the Twitter users informed that [[Jeannie Elias]] (the voice of [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]]) voiced this unknown commercial woman.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://twitter.com/CrazyAssNinten/status/1624931806968524800|title=Jeannie Elias, the voice actor for Princess Toadstool from the Super Mario Bros Super Show, returns to cameo in the Super Mario Bros Movie (2023)|author=Crazy Ass Moments in Nintendo History|work=Twitterdate=2023-02-13}}</ref> This was confirmed to be false when [[Jessica DiCicco]] revealed she is the voice of the "mystery lady in the SMB Plumbing Commercials".<ref name="mystery solved"/>
