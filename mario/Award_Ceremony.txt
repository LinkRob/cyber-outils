An '''Award Ceremony''' is an event in the {{series|Mario Kart}} that occurs after completing one of the cups in [[Grand Prix]]. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'', and has different aspects depending on the appearance.

The ceremony involves the participants ranked first, second, and third respectively, and each of them win a trophy. Any racers ranked fourth place or lower do not take part in the ceremony.

==In ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''==
The ceremony takes place in [[Peach Beach]]. The spectators, consisting of [[Noki]]s and [[Pianta]]s, cheer for the three winning teams. The team in first place is driven around on the [[Parade Kart]] by [[Toadsworth]]. As the three [[kart]]s continue onward, each of them drive into an [[Item Box]], and a trophy pops out for each of them.
