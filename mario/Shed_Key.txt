The '''Shed Key''' is a key item featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. [[Mario]] obtains it after defeating [[Yaridovich]], and with the Shed Key he can unlock the shed in [[Seaside Town]] and free all of the townspeople that were imprisoned there.
