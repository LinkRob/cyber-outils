==History==
Bananaport Pads are found throughout levels on [[DK Isles]]. Bananaport Pads serve as the means for [[Donkey Kong]] and the others to warp around in levels. There are five types of Bananaport Pads each numbered 1-5. There are also two of the same Bananaport Pads per level meaning 10 Bananaport Pads overall. To use a Bananaport Pad, simply head over to it and press {{button|n64|z}}. However, both of the same numbered Bananaport Pads must be activated in order to use them.
==Trivia==
*[[Angry Aztec]] and [[Creepy Castle]] have the most Bananaport Pads of any level as the [[Llama's Temple]] and the crypt also contains its own Bananaport Pads inside.
