'''Jakks Pacific, Inc.''' is an American [[List of Mario merchandise|merchandise]] company that designs consumer products, with a range of products that feature numerous children's toy licenses. The company is named after its founder, Jack Friedman.

Jakks Pacific started producing ''[[Mario (franchise)|Super Mario]]'' products in 2014, which includes figures, playsets, plushes, vehicles, and more.
