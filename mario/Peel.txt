== Description ==
Peel allows the removal of [[Stickers]] that are usually stuck to surfaces. This ability is used by holding the "A" button. Peel is also used to remove [[Bowser Sticker|Bowser&nbsp;Stickers]] which bind [[Toad (species)|Toads]] and block certain pathways/shortcuts. Peel is often used in combination with [[Paperize]]. If Mario leaves a sticker for too long after peeling or enters another area without collecting then the sticker will disappear.

=== Users ===
*'''[[Mario]]''': He is the main and only user of this ability. He pulls of Stickers and other items. It appears that he already has this ability since as soon he woke up after being knocked out by [[Bowser]], he could already pull [[Kersti]] off a sign post.
