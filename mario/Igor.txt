'''Igor''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Biography==
It is implied that Igor was a merchant before he became a [[Boo]] according to [[Goombario]] through his [[Tattle]].

==History==
Igor is a Boo who runs [[Boo's Shop]] in [[Boo's Mansion]]. He has the exact appearance of a regular Boo. Even though Igor is a businessman who is obligated to sell items to customers needing them, Igor is very loyal to [[Lady Bow]] as he did not sell any items to [[Mario]] at the time per her orders. There is also a letter that is addressed to Igor where he mentions is the order list for [[Gusty Gulch]] and gives Mario a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] for delivering the letter to him.
