The '''''DK'' series''' is a spin-off series within the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}. These games star [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], who needs to climb in many places throughout many situations. The series consists of only two titles, ''[[DK: King of Swing]]'' and ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]''.
