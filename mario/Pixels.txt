'''Pixels''' is a 2015 science-comedy film released by Columbia Pictures. The film contains cameo background appearances video game characters like [[Duck Hunt (character)|Duck Hunt]], with some being the villain roles, such as [[PAC-MAN]], and [[Cranky Kong|Donkey Kong]] based off from [[Donkey Kong (video game)|''Donkey Kong'' Arcade]].

==Mario-related elements ==
The film starts with Sam Brenner (Adam Sandler) playing the [[Donkey Kong (video game)|original ''Donkey Kong'' game]] and losing a championship match to Eddie Plant (Peter Dinklage), who uses a  (despite the fact that the actual game does not feature cheat codes and doing so would be counter-productive for an arcade game). ''Donkey Kong'' haunts Brenner as "the one game he sucks at".

When the "arcaders" are called in to train United States Navy SEALs to play arcade games, Ludlow Lamonsoff (Josh Gad) wears a shirt depicting [[Small Mario]]'s [[Jump|jumping]] sprite from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''

Later, an alien that has taken a form based on [[Mario]]'s sprite from Donkey Kong can be seen running and jumping across a street, although none of the main characters are seen interacting with him.

The film's climax has the cast face the leader of the aliens, who has taken a form based on [[Donkey Kong]] and is on a re-creation of [[25m]], with captives on the same platform [[Pauline]] is held captive on in Donkey Kong. As Donkey Kong attacks the group, Brenner has a breakdown when he cannot figure out Donkey Kong's pattern and his insecurity over not being able to win the game gets to him. One of the captives, Matty van Patten (Matt Lintz), then reveals that Plant cheated, giving Brenner the confidence he needs to get to the top and defeat Donkey Kong using a [[hammer]].
