'''Quick Change''' is a badge which appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. Wearing it allows [[Mario]] to swap out his partners without using his turn. Mario's partners can use this effect in battle without using their turn as well.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:Quick_change_badge.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Quick Change costs four [[Badge Point|BP]]. To get Quick Change, Mario must have obtained the [[Super Boots]] or [[Ultra Boots]], and use them tp perform a [[Spin Jump]] three times in a row inside of [[Merlon]]'s house. As a result, Quick Change falls from Merlon's attic. Merlon realizes the power of Quick Change and allows Mario to keep it.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Quick Change now costs seven BP to wear. [[Dazzle]] gives the badge to Mario for only eight [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]].
