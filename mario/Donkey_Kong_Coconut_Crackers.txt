}}'''''Donkey Kong Coconut Crackers''''' was a cancelled [[Donkey Kong]] puzzle game that was going to be released for the [[Game Boy Advance]]. Rare registers a trademark for ''Animal Crackers'' in April 16, 1997. It was developed as ''Donkey Kong Puzzle Paint'' in 2000, and revealed as ''Donkey Kong Coconut Crackers'' in E3 2001, and was originally planned for release in December 7, 2001 in the United Kingdom. It was likely postponed ahead of time due to the forthcoming acquisition from Microsoft in 2002. Eventually, the game was cancelled along with ''[[Donkey Kong Racing]]'' in August 2002 due to [[Rare]]'s move to [[:wikipedia:Microsoft|Microsoft]] in September 2002. The game was later remade as ''[[:wikipedia:It's Mr. Pants|It's Mr. Pants]]'', its trademark was registered on November 9, 2001. The confirmed characters were [[Donkey Kong]] and some [[Kritter]]s. There were known to be five modes in the game.
