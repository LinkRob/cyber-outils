'''Look-This-Way Blocks''', also called '''Atchi Muite Blocks''', are a type of [[block]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Description==
These blocks appear only if a character looks at them and fade once the character stops doing so. Look-This-Way Blocks are featured in small and large varieties.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''==
Look-This-Way Blocks are found only in [[Raarghs in the Ruins]].
