The {{Nihongo|'''Iced Potato'''|さむイモ|Samuimo|Cold Potato}} is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Iced Potatoes can only be obtained by sleeping in the [[Inn]] at [[Shiver City]]. The next morning, an Iced Potato will appear on the nearby table. The Iced Potato only heals 5 [[Heart Point|HP]], which isn't as effective at that point in the game. However, it can be cooked with other items for more effective recovery items. For example, cooking an Iced Potato and a [[Fire Flower]] together results in [[Frozen Fries]], which heals 15 HP.
