'''Renegade Roundup''' is a battle mode which appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

==Gameplay==
Renegade Roundup is essentially the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' equivalent of Cops and Robbers. Each player is always divided into two teams: the red team and the blue team, and both teams take turns being the authorities and the renegades. The authorities must capture every renegade on the battle arena by using the potted piranha plant attached to their vehicles while the renegades need to avoid getting caught. Any captured renegades will be placed within cells located throughout the arena and can be freed by their teammates via driving over switches under the jail cell(s).

The battle will be over by two factors: the authorities win if every renegades are captured while the renegades win if at least one team member is still not caught throughout the duration of the battle.
