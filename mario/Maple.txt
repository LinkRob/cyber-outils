'''Maple''' is an unlockable playable character whose only appearance is in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]]. Maple has an excitable personality, and she can be unlocked by earning 50 birdie badges in Tournament Mode.
