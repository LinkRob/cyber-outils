}}The '''Heavy Fire Bro ''' (unofficially called the '''Blaze Bro''' by fans) is an enemy that first appeared in [[Super Mario Maker 2]]. It is a variant of a [[Sledge Bro]] and a [[Fire Bro]], that throws larger fireballs and just like regular Sledge Bros, they pound the ground, stunning players on the ground. It was revealed in a trailer for ''Super Mario Maker 2'', they can only be placed in the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]] ''style.

== Appearance ==
It looks like a normal [[Sledge Bro]], however, its shell and helmet are red instead of green.
==Powers==
The Heavy Fire Bro has the power to throw an infinite amount of fireballs at the player. Like Sledge Bros, they'll shake the ground whenever they jump, causing the player's character to become stunned, leaving himself/herself open to attacks.[[File:Fatfirebro.png|thumb|A Heavy Fire Bros.'s icon in Super Mario Maker 2]]

==Trivia==
*Despite appearing in the ''Super Mario 3D World'' style, Heavy Fire Bros (or Sledge Bros) haven't appeared in ''Super Mario 3D World''.
