The '''Drill Mushroom''' is a power-up item set to appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It allows the user to transform into [[Drill Mario]], a new form that gives the wearer the ability to burrow into the floor or ceiling or even attack falling [[Spiny|Spinies]] and other tough enemies. In Where the Rrrumbas rule, there’s a secret exit in the ceiling that can be drilled through to reveal.
