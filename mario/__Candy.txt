'''? Candies'''<ref name=Manual>[https://csassets.nintendo.com/noaext/image/private/t_KA_PDF/Wii_Mario_Party_8?_a=DATC1RAAZAA0 ''Mario Party 8'' manual]</ref> are areas that appear on the [[board]]s of ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. They are the successors of the [[Orb Space]]s from ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. Whenever a player passes over a ? Candy, they receive a free piece of [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] at random. Passing a ? Candy does not decrease the steps the player has remaining, since ? Candies do not qualify as [[space]]s. ? Candies disappear on the final turn and no longer give free Candies.

[[Bitsize Candy]] and [[Weeglee Candy]] are obtainable only from ? Candies and cannot be bought in [[Candy Shop]]s.
