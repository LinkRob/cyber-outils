'''Crystal Cup''' is the second cup of the [[Road to the Strikers Cup]] mode in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. It becomes available after completing the [[Fire Cup]]. Clearing the Crystal Cup the first time unlocks both [[Diddy Kong]] and the [[Crystal Canyon]] stadium. Another stadium, [[The Dump]], is unlocked by earning both the Brick Wall and Golden Foot Awards in Crystal Cup.
