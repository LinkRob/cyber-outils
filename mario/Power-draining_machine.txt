==History==
Grubba, a long time ago, was one of the best fighters of his time. However, he soon to retire due to getting old and being unable to keep up with the younger fighters. He then came across a [[Crystal Star]] and thought of the idea to create the Power-draining machine to drain the life out of young, strong fighters and give it to himself making him forever young. He first used this on the original champion of the [[Glitz Pit]], [[Prince Mush]]. In present time, he also drained life from [[KP Pete]] and [[Bandy Andy]] who happened to discover this machine. During the climax of Chapter 3, Grubba (who knew [[Mario]] was searching for the Crystal Star) decided to take him out once and for all and uses the Power-draining machine on himself to become [[Macho Grubba]]. However, Macho Grubba is soon defeated and Grubba himself is taken away with his Power-draining machine presumably being destroyed.
