'''Bopping Toadies''',<ref>''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'' English instruction booklet, page 14.</ref> also known as '''Bopping Frogs''',<ref>[https://www.nintendo.co.jp/character/mario/archives/land2/?lang=en English ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'' entry on the official Mario Portal]. ''nintendo.co.jp''. Retrieved August 13, 2022. ([https://archive.ph/xw7hA Archived] August 13, 2022, 13:51:45 UTC via archive.today.)</ref> are [[frog]]-like enemies from the [[Tree Zone]]'s [[Invincibility!|first stage]] in ''[[Super Mario Land 2 - 6 Golden Coins]]''.

Bopping Toadies have an attack pattern that is very easy to predict and dodge. They lash their tongues out, [[jump]] up a short distance, then lash out their tongues again. However, they stop moments before unleashing their attack. Mario can defeat a Bopping Toady by either [[stomp]]ing it or by throwing a [[fireball]] at it.

By luring a Bopping Toady into a small tunnel, they can get stuck in midair.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HB7tU55FjnE</ref>
