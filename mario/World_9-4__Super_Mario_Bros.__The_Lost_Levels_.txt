'''World 9-4''' is the fourth and final [[level]] of [[World 9 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 9]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the fourth secret level overall in said game. In the original, it uses the gray palette associated with castle levels (and to a lesser extent [[World 6-3 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-3]] and [[World 7-3 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 7-3]]/[[World C-3]] for ''Super Mario Bros.'' and ''The Lost Levels'', respectively), but takes place underwater. This level does not contain any [[Coin]]s, power-ups, [[Poison Mushroom]]s, or secret areas.

==Layout==
[[File:SMAS_LL_World_9-4_Thank_You_Messange.png|thumb|The thank you message.]]
This level takes place entirely underwater. The player will encounter a [[Goomba]], [[Koopa Troopa]], [[Buzzy Beetle]], green [[Koopa Paratroopa]], [[Hammer Bro]]., and a [[Blooper]] before coming to a series of arranged blocks. The blocks read the Japanese characters "アリガトウ!" (Arigatō!), or "thank you", as a [[Fourth wall|message to the players]]. Between the ガ and the ト is a red Koopa Paratroopa, and between the ト and the ウ is a [[Lava Bubble]]. After the exclamation mark is the [[Goal Pole]].

Depending on the version of the ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels'' game the player is playing, the level progression differs after reaching the Goal Pole. In the original and [[Classic NES Series|Famicom Mini]] versions of the game, the player will return to [[World 9-1 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 9-1]] and continue playing World 9 until they run out of lives. In the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' version, the player moves on to [[World A]].
