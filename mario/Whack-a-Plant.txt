'''Whack-a-Plant''' is a 1-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party]]''. Its name and gameplay are based on the arcade game {{wp|Whac-A-Mole|Whack-a-Mole}}.

==Gameplay==
The player simply needs to [[jump]] on the [[Piranha Plant]]s as they pop out from [[Warp Pipe|pipes]] to gain [[Coin]]s. The player can hold down {{button|n64|a}} to jump farther. The number of Piranha Plants increases by one every time they come out, starting at one plant and increasing to eight, filling all but one pipe. One Coin is earned for each Piranha Plant that is stomped. This minigame can give the player up to 36 Coins. Special celebratory music plays if all 36 Coins are collected. If no Coins are collected, the minigame ends in a "Miss!" Unlike in most 1-Player minigames, when this minigame is lost, the player does not lose the usual five Coins.

The minigame can be purchased from the [[Mini-Game House]] for 200 Coins.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|Stick}} &ndash; Move
* {{button|n64|A}} &ndash; Jump
