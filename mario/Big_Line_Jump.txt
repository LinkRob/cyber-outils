|name=Big Line Jump (Battle Card)}}'''Big Line Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Line Jump is the strong line version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a big blue spring boot with a blue/cyan checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 50/250 coins

Sell Price: 25/125 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Line Jump is first seen in [[The Golden Coliseum]], but can also found in [[Vortex Island]], [[Mustard Café]], [[Toad Trainworks]], [[Sunset Express]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
