'''Bouncy Maze''' is an unlockable [[minigame]] which appears in in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Layout==
A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has to make his way through a maze filled with [[Bouncy|Bouncies]] and reach the finish line as quickly as possible. There are [[clock]]s placed along the way, and collecting one adds an extra second to the timer.

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
Bouncy Maze has an easy mode and a hard mode, the latter of which is unlocked by collecting all eight [[Baby Coin]]s in [[World 3 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 3]]. The easy mode has a straightforward path that must be completed within 30 seconds. The hard mode features a longer and windier path that must be completed within one minute.
