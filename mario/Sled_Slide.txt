'''Sled Slide''' is a single-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Opening==
The player's character is inside of a sled, and they ride up to the starting line.

==Gameplay==
The player's character must race around the track inside of their sled. There are arrows along the track showing the direction of the upcoming turns, and if a turn is taken too sharply, the player's character spins out.

In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player's character must complete one lap in 30 seconds to win. In Mini-Game Attack, they must complete three laps in 99 seconds to win. In Play Land, the player's character must complete three laps in the shortest time.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|Pad}}: Left/Right
*{{button|gba|A}}: Accelerate
*{{button|gba|B}}: Brake

==Flavor text==
*In-game description: ''Hop on a sled and race around the rink to win!''

==Trivia==
*The use of {{button|gba|A}} to accelerate and {{button|gba|B}} to brake is just like in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' games.
