The '''Shipshape's Cannon''' (''working name'') is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

==Description==
It is a golden [[cannon]] that is found on [[Shipshape Island]], and is used to launch [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] over the various islands of [[Concordia]]. The cannon is mounted with a telescope over it and the player can zoom and control the movement to search for a nearby location to be fired into.
