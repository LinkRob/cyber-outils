'''Jojora''' is a character in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Her gender is female, which is confirmed in the hints added in the remakes. She appears only in [[Joke's End]]. When the player explores the area for the first time, Jojora appears in some areas and say something.

==Personality==
While little is known about her personality, Jojora seems to be caring about [[Jojora's Friend|her friends]]. Jojora also seems to have a sense of justice, as she tries to attack those who wrong her or her friends.

==History==
Mario and Luigi meet Jojora in the [[Joke's End]]. They mention to her that [[Bowletta]] kidnapped [[Princess Peach]]. However, she has no idea what they are talking about, it's unknown or possible if she allowed [[Fawful]] come in and told her about the Mario Bros. are coming. Nonetheless, she does not help but let them through until the boss battle with herself and [[Jojora's friends|Jojora's friend]]. When Mario and Luigi reach the room where they fight her, she will call up her friend, who assists her in their boss battle.

Upon their defeat, she runs away crying while her friends literally melt.

==Combat==
In Superstar Saga, Jojora has only 50 HP but has extremely high defense, so she can take only one point of damage of she gets hit. However, defeating her is possible with enough effort. She attacks by waving her wand, trying to freeze the heroes.

In Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions, her defense has been lowered to 240, but to make up for this, she has 500 HP. The only time she will exit the battle in either case is if Mario/Luigi smack her wand with his hammer and if the battle ends.
