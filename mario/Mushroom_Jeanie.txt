'''Mushroom Jeanie''' is a character who appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==History==
Mushroom Jeanie is the counterpart of the [[Mushroom Genie]], summoned after the player uses the [[Lucky Lamp]]. Jeanie will then appear and use her wand to move the [[Fake Millennium Star|Millennium Star]] to a different location on the board. This is helpful to players who are too far away from the star or ones who want to prevent nearby opponents from obtaining it.
