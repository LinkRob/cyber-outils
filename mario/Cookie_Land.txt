'''Cookie Land''' is a [[Battle Stage]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. Cookie Land is one of the simplest Battle Stages, and it is round in shape and resembles a cookie.

This battle stage is a circle with chocolate and candy chunks in a pink sky in the background. It has three colorful pads that stop racers in their tracks when hit, like a wall. It appears as if the player can drive on them but you can't, as they are slightly too high. There is also a large circular pad in the center, which is also like a wall. It is tan like a very fancy cookie. The outside edges are "frosted" and it is impossible to fall off.

''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' would later receive a similar dessert-themed Battle Stage of its own, [[Tart Top]].

Cookie Land returns as a [[Retro Stage]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. This is also somewhat similar to [[Big Donut]] from ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.
