'''Mondo''' is the sixth [[level]] of the [[Special World]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==Overview==
Mondo consists of mostly flat terrain, [[Pipe]]s and semi-solid platforms but is complicated by rising and lowering water throughout the stage. This water is full of [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother]]s float above the player. Their platforms are sometimes accessed to progress through the level. After reaching the [[Giant Gate]] at the end of the level, the player can access [[Outrageous]].
