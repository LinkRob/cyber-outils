'''Shocking Arrow Lifts!''' is the first [[mission]] of [[Wet-Dry World]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Layout==
Shocking Arrow Lifts! does not affect the layout of Wet-Dry World, so it can be completed even when selecting any other mission of Wet-Dry World before entering. It is one of the missions to take place in the main area of Wet-Dry World.

==Objective==
[[Mario]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in ''Super Mario 64 DS'') have to use a series of [[Arrow Lift (Super Mario 64)|Arrow Lifts]] to reach a platform with a [[! Block]] containing the [[Power Star]]. To accomplish this task, the [[water]] level has to partially fill the area. If it is not, the [[Crystal Tap]] on the large stone structure can be used to adjust the water level to the appropriate height.

Once the water level is at its appropriate height, Mario has to [[swim]] north to the floating wooden platforms and use them to [[jump]] up onto the smaller platform above with the nearby Arrow Lifts. Mario has to use the Arrow Lifts to reach the platform on the other side while avoiding the [[Amp]] circling the lifts along the way. Once Mario is on the other platform, where there is a [[Kuromame]], jumping at the [[! Block]] produces the Power Star.

There are three other ways to complete the mission, none of which involve the use of the arrow lifts:

*If the water level is below the platforms, Mario can [[Long Jump|long-jump]] to the platform with the Power Star.
*If the water level is at its maximum height, Mario can jump out of the water below the yellow block to destroy it. This method is not usable in ''Super Mario 64 DS''.
*If the water level is at its lowest, Mario can use a [[warp]] by a Crystal Tap to reach the platform with the [[cannon]] and then jump off to reach the ! Block.
