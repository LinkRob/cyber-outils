'''Penguins''' are a species of flightless birds in real life that have sometimes appeared in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], the first instance being in ''[[Pinball]]''.

==Profile==
===Habitat===
Penguins inhabit arctic regions, notably [[Antarctica]], in both real life and the ''Mario'' franchise.

===Variants===
Two species based on real-world penguins have been introduced prior to the Penguins in ''Super Mario 64'', including [[Penkoon]]s from ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and [[Penpen]]s from ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]]. Penkoons are more specifically a hybrid between a penguin and a raccoon.

Later on, with the release of ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', the ''Mario'' franchise went on to introduce their own species of [[Penguin]]s. Because of their similarity to real-world penguins, the Penguins from the ''Mario'' franchise have often substituted for them.

===Notable members===
Similarly, a few characters based on real-world penguins have been introduced prior to the Penguins specific to the ''Mario'' franchise. In the [[Nintendo Adventure Books|Nintendo Adventure Book]] ''[[Leaping Lizards]]'', there is a Penguin named [[Feldspar]], who hosts the [[International Mushroom Games]]. In ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'', there is a boss named [[Penguin (boss)|Penguin]], who is fought in [[Sherbet Land (world)|Sherbet Land]].

==In ''Pinball'' / ''[[VS. Pinball]]''==
[[File:Pinball gameplay.png|thumb|left|200px|A screenshot of the penguins in ''Pinball'', located right above the [[Nintendo]] logo]]
In [[scene A]], there are three penguins, depicted with a dark blue body, that are the default icons on each of the three [[slot window]]s. If the player stops a slot window on a penguin while it is rotating, they earn 500 [[point]]s. If all three slot windows land on a penguin, the [[up-post]] is raised, during which any points earned is doubled.

These penguins later made an appearance in the [[Family BASIC]] accessory for [[Family Computer]], being among the sprites that can be used while programming. The sprites for the penguins in ''Pinball'' were later reused for ''[[w:c:nintendo:Binary Land|Binary Land]]'', for the male and the female penguin, but recolored light green and pink respectively.
{{br|left}}
==In ''[[Mario is Missing!]]''==
Penguins appear in the PC version, as part of the background of Antarctica. The game's narrator states that dozens of Penguins will become homeless if [[Bowser]] successfully melts Antarctica.

==In ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''==
Regular penguins appear in the [[microgame]] [[Penguin Shuffle]], where the objective is for the player to navigate a small, blue-feathered, baby penguin between several adult, black-feathered penguins. In the ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' equivalent, [[Paddle Parade]], the penguins were replaced with ducklings.

==In other media==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
During [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Big Mouth Koopa]]'s reign over [[Antarctica]], he brings an [[Abominable Snowkoopa]] to life in order to terrorize the penguins. Later on, when [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] stole Big Mouth's Wand, he used it to destroy the Abominable Snowkoopa as well as enlarge the penguins, who then angrily chase after Big Mouth.
