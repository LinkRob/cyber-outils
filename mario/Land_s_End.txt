|games =''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' }}'''Land's End''' is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. As its name states, Land's End is the very last piece of land that is connected on the map before [[Nimbus Land]] that's in the clouds and [[Barrel Volcano]] which is over the ocean.

==Description==
Land's End is a very large level that consists of:
*Mountains
*The fields
*A desert
*An underground cave

==History==
After getting defeating [[Yaridovich]] and getting the Star Piece, [[Mario]] and his team head into Land's End. They cross through many areas until they eventually reach underground and to [[Belome Temple]].

They also head back here to get to [[Bean Valley]] which leads up to [[Nimbus Land]].
