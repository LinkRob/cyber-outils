'''Moo Moos'''<ref>Musa, Alexander. ''Mario Kart 8'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 144. "'''''Moo Moos:''' These herbivores lazily wander near and onto the race trace, forcing players to get creative to avoid running into them. Moo Moos can stop even the heaviest racers in their tracks, and are large enough to easily mess with your ideal racing line.''"</ref> (also generically referred to as '''cows''')<ref>''Mario Kart 64'' English instruction booklet. Page 26.</ref><ref>''Mario Kart Wii'' website bio for [[Moo Moo Meadows]]</ref> are moving obstacles that appear mainly in farm-based courses in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. In some appearances, they act as obstacles for drivers. They resemble real life cows, except chubbier, and cuter.

==History==
===''Mario Kart'' series===
====''Mario Kart 64''====
[[File:Moo Moo Farm MK64.png|thumb|''Mario Kart 64'']]
Cows can be seen in the background of the ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'' course named [[N64 Moo Moo Farm|Moo Moo Farm]].

====''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!''====
Although cows themselves do not appear in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', [[Mushroom City]] has a truck advertising the Moo Moo Farm course with the image of a cow.

====''Mario Kart DS''====
Cows once again appear in the background of the [[Classic course|Retro Course]] {{classic course|N64|Moo Moo Farm}}. It is the first game to give them the blue collars with hanging golden bells.

====''Mario Kart Wii''====
[[File:Moo Moo (Mario Kart Wii).png|140px|thumb|A Moo Moo as seen in ''Mario Kart Wii'']]
In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', cows serve as obstacles in the course [[Moo Moo Meadows]]. When hit, they cause the player to spin out. While the ones seen up close use full models, some in the distant background reuse flat textures from ''Mario Kart DS''. A truck in the [[Moonview Highway]] course also has a picture of a cow printed on it with the text "[[List of sponsors debuting in Mario Kart Wii#Moo Moo Dairy|Moo Moo Dairy]]".

====''Mario Kart 7''====
Two cows make a small appearance in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' on a sign in the {{classic course|Wii|Coconut Mall}} course, which reads "Moo Moo Dairy".

====''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''====
Moo Moos return in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' with the return of the Moo Moo Meadows track, this time with Moo Moos of other colors, such as solid black or brown. Also, in [[Toad Harbor]] and the game's rendition of [[Toad's Turnpike]], there are advertising trucks and signs with Moo Moos on them. There is also a sponsor known as [[List of sponsors debuting in Mario Kart 8 and Mario Kart 8 Deluxe#Moo Moo Meadows Milk|Moo Moo Meadows Milk]]. In this game, Moo Moos appear in four different colors; white with blue spots, white with brown spots, black, or brown. The Mii Racing Suit based on a Moo Moo appears in the ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe – Booster Course Pass]]''.

====''Mario Kart Tour''====
[[File:MKT Artwork MooMooMiiRacingSuit.png|60px|thumb|Moo Moo Mii Racing Suit]]
Although Moo Moos themselves do not appear in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', the Moo Moo Meadows Milk sponsor returns in several courses in the game, and the [[Mii|Moo Moo Mii Racing Suit]] and [[Moo Moo Offroader]] are both based on them and were introduced in the [[Animal Tour]].

====''Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit''====
In ''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]]'', the [[Windmill Meadows]] features some Moo Moos as obstacles.

==== ''Mario Kart World'' ====
Moo Moos return in [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']], reprising their role as obstacles in [[Mario Kart Wii|Wii]] [[Moo Moo Meadows]]. They can also be found on routes leading to and from the course. Moo Moos also have their face printed in a few stickers.

In addition, a character named [[Cow (character)|Cow]] who appears to be based on the Moo Moo appears as a playable character (also featured in a few stickers). She originated from a sketch that shows a Moo Moo driving a car, and is the host of the aforementioned Moo Moo Meadows course.

===''Mario Party'' series===
====''Mario Party 3''====
[[File:Moo Moo MP3.png|thumb]]
In ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', cows appear in the background of [[Fowl Play]].

====''Mario Party: Island Tour''====
In ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'', Moo Moos appear on the [[Perilous Palace Path]] board as decoration on the board.

===''Mario Pinball Land''===
In ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]'', there is a rare chance for a cow to pop out of a hole by hitting a [[Monty Mole]]. If Mario hits the Moo Moo before it disappears, he gains 100,000 points.

===''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games''===
Cows reappear in the Wii version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' on Moo Moo Meadows as obstacles in the [[Dream Equestrian]] event.

===''Yoshi'' franchise===
====''Yoshi's Woolly World'' / ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''====
Despite Moo Moos not appearing in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', Moo Moo Yoshi is available as an unlockable Yoshi [[List of Yoshi designs in Yoshi's Woolly World|pattern]] which resembles a cow.

====''Yoshi's Crafted World''====
In ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'', the Moo Moo Meadows Milk sponsor from ''Mario Kart 8'' makes various cameo appearances on the cartons and signs in the background of the flip side of [[Rail-Yard Run]] and [[Go-Go Yoshi]]. As first seen on the front side of Rail-Yard Run, there are also five cardboard Moo Moo [[List of crafts in Yoshi's Crafted World|craft]]s, which serve as [[Blockafeller|Coal Blockafeller]]'s first souvenir request. There is additionally a Moo Moo [[List of costumes in Yoshi's Crafted World|costume]] for Yoshi to wear.

===''Nintendo Badge Arcade''===
Moo Moos appear as badges in ''[[Nintendo Badge Arcade]]'' that the player may collect and decorate their Nintendo 3DS HOME Menu with. They are a part of the ''Mario Kart 8'' set of badges.

===''Minecraft''===
[[File:Minecraft Mario Mash-Up Cow Render.png|thumb|A Moo Moo, as seen in ''Minecraft''.]]
In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''[[Minecraft]]'', the traditional Cow's design from ''Minecraft'' is replaced with the Moo Moo design found in the ''[[Super Mario (franchise)|Super Mario]]'' titles, only with brown spots instead of black spots.  Red Moo Moos with mushrooms on their back replace Mooshrooms.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Moo Moos are based on real-life cows. They originally were based on the white variety with spots, but in later games, Moo Moos began to take designs from other breeds. Their name is derived from the "moo" onomatopoeia, which describes a cow's "voice."

== Nomenclature ==
Moo Moos take their names from doubling the host language's onomatopoeia for "moo." For example, Japanese calls them ''Mōmō'' (written もーもー or もうもう).
