A '''Vomer''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are a variant of [[Dry Bones]] encountered in [[Barrel Volcano]] and [[The Trial Course]] of [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]].

==Battle==
Vomers mainly attack by throwing bones at members of [[Mario]]'s party. A Vomer is invulnerable to physical attacks, but it can still be taken out using either a special attack or [[Pure Water]].
