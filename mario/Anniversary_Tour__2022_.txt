The Anniversary Tour was the seventy-ninth tour of Mario Kart Tour, which began on September 21, 2022 and ended on October 4, 2022. This tour celebrated three years since the release of Mario Kart Tour on September 25, 2019; however, unlike the 1st and 2nd Anniversary Tours, this tour's name did not contain a number representing the anniversary it celebrated.
