'''First Attack''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ===
[[File:First_attack_badge.png|thumb|left]]
First Attack is obtainable at [[Rowf's Badge Shop]] where it can be purchased for 100 [[coin]]s. While wearing this badge, [[Mario]] can automatically defeat enemies whenever he lands a [[First Strike]]. However, the enemy gives Mario no [[Star Point]]s when this is used. This badge can be useful whenever Mario revisits past areas and doesn't have to worry about wasting time fighting against weaker enemies such as [[Goomba]]s that wouldn't yield any Star Points.

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
Mario can purchase this badge from the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] for 100 coins. This badge retains its effects from ''Paper Mario'', and does not apply its effects within the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].
