|race    = |homeland=The overworld|friends=Steve|creator=Mojang|species=Human|enemies=Zombie<br/>Enderman}}'''Alex''' is one of the main playable characters of ''[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft Minecraft]''. She is a female version of [[Steve]] added in 2014.


==Appearances==
On October 1, 2020, Steve was revealed to be the fighter included in Challenger Pack 7, with more information to be revealed prior to Minecraft Live 2020. They are currently the fifth third-party DLC character in the game. Their appearance and animations appear identical to their iteration in Minecraft, with the ability to place blocks, mine for materials, and use items from within the game. Their Final Smash is a giant piston which smashes players into a stronghold filled with TNT, with Creepers detonating the explosives. Alex is one of Steve's alternate costumes, alongside [[Zombie (Minecraft)|Zombie]] and [[Enderman]].
