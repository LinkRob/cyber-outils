'''Sherbet Land''' is the first course of the [[Star Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is the track before [[Mushroom City]]. It also appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe|port]], as the second course of the [[Leaf Cup]], and has the added ability to drive underwater and glide.

==Overview==
[[File:Sherbet Land MK8.jpg|thumb|left|In ''Mario Kart 8'']]
In this course, [[Shy Guy]]s ice skate across the ice at the very beginning of the course. Near the Shy Guys is frozen water which encloses the player into an ice block if dropped into the water. The player can cause the Shy Guys to go into the frozen water by using a Star or a Fireball. There is also an icy cave which can be gone under.

After the cave, the next part is out in the open again, with a path of thin snow and thick snow around the thin snow path. If the player drives into the thick snow, it will slow the kart down and the player will have to return to the thin snow path.

Right after this, the player goes into an area with ice again. This area's path is wide open. Although wide open, there are smiley-faced ice bergs sitting around. If they are touched by characters on the kart, the kart and the characters will freeze for a few seconds and will slow them down.

After this part, the player will drive across the starting/finish line and will go on to his next lap.
