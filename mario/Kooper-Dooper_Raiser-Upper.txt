The '''Kooper-Dooper Raiser-Upper''' is an object in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Up, Up, and a Koopa]]."

==Description==
The Kooper-Dooper Raiser-Upper is an invention made by [[Kooky von Koopa]]. It is inside the [[Doom Ship]], and can shoot lasers that cause anything they hit to rise up to the sky.

==In other media==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
===="Up, Up, and a Koopa"====
[[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and Kooky use the Kooper-Dooper Raiser-Upper on [[Sky Land]]. However, when King Koopa turns up the machine's power, it begins to malfunction, and everything in the air falls down. Kooky later repairs and upgrades the Kooper-Dooper Raiser-Upper, boasting it to now be ten times as powerful. King Koopa goes into [[Mushroom Kingdom|Mushroom Land]], which he conquers for a short while, along with Sky Land.

[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] takes the Kooper-Dooper Raiser-Upper out of the Doom Ship and into [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]]'s floating house. The Kooper-Dooper Raiser-Upper is then fired in reverse on the Doom Ship, causing it to fall into [[Dark Land]]. [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] then uses the Kooper-Dooper Raiser-Upper to lower everything else to their original locations.
