'''The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild''' is one of the [[microgame]]s in [[9-Volt]]'s [[Nintendo Classics]] level in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''. It is both based on and named for the [[Wii U]] and [[Nintendo Switch]] game of the [[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|same name]].

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to use their move to knock a floating object that has been frozen with the [[w:c:zelda:Stasis Rune|Stasis Rune]] multiple times to build kinetic energy up to its maximum capacity. In doing so, the object is sent flying, and the player clears the microgame. For the entire microgame, [[Link]] is shown on the right side of the screen. When the object is sent flying, Link happily jumps once, with his fist raised up.

[[Kat and Ana]] and [[Orbulon]] are both listed in the highest tier of suitability for the microgame. For Kat and Ana, their constant use of jumping and shuriken projectiles allow them to continuously hit the object. For Orbulon's, the tractor beam from his [[Oinker]] enables him to quickly launch the object.

Each difficulty level features a different object to hit: in level 1, it is a large metal box; in level 2, it is a medium-sized boulder; and in level 3, it is a small sphere. The higher difficulty levels are based on how the objects become harder as each one is smaller than the one before.
