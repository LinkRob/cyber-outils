The '''Sticker Fest''' is an event held annually in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]'s town of [[Decalburg]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. The event is hosted by [[Princess Peach]], who welcomes the [[Sticker Comet]], believed to have the ability to grant wishes.
