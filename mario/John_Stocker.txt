'''John Stocker''' (born August 13, 1947) is a Canadian voice actor, who voiced [[Toad]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' and ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and [[Oogtar]] in ''[[Super Mario World (TV series)|Super Mario World]]'', he was also the voice of [[Mouser]], [[Koopa Troopa]], [[Beezo]], [[Flurry]].''
