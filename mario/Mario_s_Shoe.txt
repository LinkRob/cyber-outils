'''Mario's Shoe''' is a recurring item in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They are first seen in ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'', but have their first gameplay role in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Description==
Mario's Shoes are a pair of brown work shoes worn by [[Mario]], and are one of the clothing items part of his main outfit. [[Luigi]] wears a similar pair of his own work shoes.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
Here, Mario's Shoes are known as his [[Boot]]s, which can be upgraded to the [[Super Boots]] and then later the [[Ultra Boots]], each granting Mario a stronger [[jump]] attack than the previous.

==In ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''==
{{quote|Wh-what is this? If it isn't Mario's Shoe! Why, the sole is so worn, as if he's walked a thousand miles!|Madame Clairvoya}}
Mario's Shoe is one of Mario's five missing items, the other four being [[Mario's Cap|Mario's Hat]], [[Mario's Letter]], [[Mario's Glove]], and [[Mario's Star (item)|Mario's Star]]. Like with Mario's other missing items, Luigi has to bring Mario's Shoe to [[Madame Clairvoya]] to help her figure out Mario's whereabouts. Mario's Shoe is found in [[The Twins' Room]], inside of a small [[treasure chest]] that spawns after defeating [[Henry and Orville]]. In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]]'s [[Hidden Mansion]], Mario's Shoe is instead located in the [[Billiards Room]].

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
In installments as early as ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', they are known as boots, and are among the clothing items to have several variations that can be equipped by Mario and Luigi, affecting their abilities accordingly.
