'''Chanterelle''', also referred to as the '''Pop Diva''' by [[Goombario]], is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Chanterelle is a female [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with purple spots on her head. She also wears a purple dress and wears a pink flower in her hair.

===Etymology===
Chanterelle's name is shared with {{w|cantharellus}}, a species of edible mushroom from the real world. Given that Chanterelle is a singer, her name also derives from "chanteuse," a word referring to female singer at nightclubs.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Chanterelle is a famous Toad and she is known for her singing. Chanterelle can be seen at [[Club 64]] where she is hopes to sing some different lyrics for a change. If [[Mario]] brings her the [[lyrics]] from [[Toad in the Desert]] living in [[Dry Dry Outpost]], Chanterelle sings a song for Mario and awards him with the [[Attack FX D]] badge. Chanterelle is also seen during the parade in the end credits and is seen singing during the nighttime part of the parade.
