'''Items''' is a stat in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. The stat is affected by the size of a kart's hind wheels, such as a kart having large hind wheels would have a high item stat—while a kart with small hind wheels has a lower item stat. The higher the stat is, the more [[dash Mushroom|mushroom]]s will be available in a [[Time Trial]], all the way up to 3 mushrooms.
