'''Kiss Thief''' is a default special move learned by [[Ms. Mowz]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Usage==
If successfully timed, Ms. Mowz will kiss the enemy, stealing any items or badges in the process. It mentions the item or badge she stole.
