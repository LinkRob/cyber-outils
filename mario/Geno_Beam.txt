|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}The '''Geno Beam''' is [[Geno]]'s first spell attack from the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Usage==
When the player uses it, Geno jumps and shoots a blue beam. The player also needs to hold Y for power. Depending on where the player starts holding the button, a number of stars will appear on the bottom of the screen. If the player manages to start holding the button when the attacks start, they will get 3 stars and the maximum damage that this attack can do. It looks powerful, but it isn't. It will first do damage from 30 to around 50. As the player levels up, it will get more powerful.
