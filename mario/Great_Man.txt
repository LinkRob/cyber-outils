'''Great Man''' is a [[Treasure (Pikmin franchise)|treasure]] in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Hey! Pikmin|Hey! Pikmin]]''.

==Description==
Great Man is an [[amiibo]] of [[Metal Mario|Silver Mario]], in the same manner that [[Colossus in Suspenders]] is an amiibo of [[Mario]] and [[Priceless Artifact]] of [[Gold Mario]].

==In ''Hey! Pikmin''==
Great Man appears in one of the [[w:c:nintendo:Secret Spot|Secret Spots]] if the player scans an amiibo of Silver Mario. It is valued at 200 [[w:c:nintendo:Sparklium|Sparklium]].
