The '''Abominable Snowkoopa''' is a monstrous snowman created by [[Big Mouth Koopa]] in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[7 Continents for 7 Koopas]]." It was created by him to take over [[Antarctica]] and terrorize the [[Penguin (real world)|penguins]] living there.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
It essentially has the appearance of a regular snowman, complete with a top hat and a scarf, which has a pattern consisting of blue and green squares. 

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Abominable Snowkoopa's name is a pun on the "abominable snowman," but with "Koopa" replacing the suffix "-man."
