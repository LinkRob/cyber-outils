"'''Alligator Dundee'''" is the twelfth live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the animated episode "[[Stars in Their Eyes]]".

== Plot ==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] are busy working on a toilet when their pet, the [[Ratigator]], appears in a very distressed state. He claims that a horrible monster was chasing him through the sewers. [[Alligator Dundee (character)|Alligator Dundee]] enters the basement and explains that he's been on a "sewer safari" and was tracking a horrible creature and tracked it into their basement.

Alligator Dundee gets a net ready that he believes will catch anything. The trio makes their plans to catch the creature. Luigi has second thoughts and asks what the monster looks like. Alligator Dundee shows them a picture of the Ratigator.

The Marios don't want to hurt the Ratigator. Alligator Dundee goes in after him in attempt to catch him. The Marios capture Alligator Dundee and inform him that the Ratigator is their pet. Alligator Dundee claims he didn't know he was tracking their pet. He makes a quick summary of the plot of ''[[Crocodile Dundee]]'' and decides to make a movie with his new name "Ratigator Dundee."

== Trivia ==
* This is the first appearance of the Ratigator.
* When Alligator Dundee shows  Mario & Luigi the picture of the Ratigator, the face of the guy on the left is censored out, for some reason.
