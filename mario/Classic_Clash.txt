'''Classic Clash''', originally titled '''Classic Clash III''', is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', as one of the microgames under [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[Sci-Fi]] folder, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', also as one of their microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player must input a sequence using the {{button|gba|pad}} only that appears above a [[Mario]]-like hero (identified as "Nintenman") to charge and fire a beam of energy at the [[Bowser]]-like enemy to defeat him. If done correctly, the player wins, but if the buttons are not input on time, they lose.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels require the press of two, three, and four buttons, respectively.
