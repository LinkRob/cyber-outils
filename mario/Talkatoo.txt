|alignment=Neutral}}The '''Talkatoo''' is a character that can be found all around ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. When talked to, the Talkatoo will give Mario a clue about the location of a [[Power Moon]]. He can give up to three unique hints about the locations of Power Moons in a specific kingdom at a time. Any Power Moons whose name the Talkatoo gives away will be marked with his head.

== Locations ==
The Talkatoo can be found in the following locations, with his location being marked with a parrot's head on the map (Note that the directions assumes that the top of the map is "north.")

* [[Cap Kingdom]]: At the southern end of the bridge that connects the island that [[Mario (character)|Mario]] starts out on to the "main" island. Mario cannot talk to the Talkatoo during his first visit to the kingdom.
* [[Cascade Kingdom]]: At the southwestern area of the Kingdom.
* [[Sand Kingdom]]: On top of the northwestern building in Tostarena town.
* [[Lake Kingdom]]: On the floor below the display with the Lochlady Dress.
* [[Wooded Kingdom]]: Close to the edge, west of the Iron Road Entrance.
* [[Lost Kingdom]]: After Mario makes his way across the Propeller Pillar, the Talkatoo can be seen on the ridge.
* [[Metro Kingdom]]: At the park where Mario enters the city.
* [[Snow Kingdom]]: In the Shiveria Town, near the entrance to the Snowline Circuit hub.
* [[Seaside Kingdom]]: East of the volleyball court at the western end of the beach, farther than the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. When Dauphin [[Mollusque-Lanceur]] initiates his fight, talking to the Talkatoo will only yield terrified squawking instead of hints.
* [[Luncheon Kingdom]]: Perched on one of the southern buildings in Peronza Plaza.
* [[Bowser's Kingdom]]: At the Keep on top of Bowser's Castle.
* [[Moon Kingdom]]: A little northeast of the [[Odyssey]]'s landing spot.
* [[Mushroom Kingdom]]: Southwest of the bridge over the stream, sitting near it.

==Names in Other Languages==
In Japanese, Korean, and Chinese, the Talkatoo is called the "Hint Bird," with the word "hint" in his name untranslated in Japanese (''Hinto Tori''; ヒント鳥) and Korean (''Hinteu Sae''; 힌트새), although it is translated in Chinese (''Tíshì Nǐao''; 提示鳥). In Spanish, he is called "Cotorrete," from La Cotorra (parrot). In French, he is called "Perrotruc," derived from "perroquet" (parrot) and "Truc," a slang term for an unidentified object. The Talkatoo is referred to as Chiacchierotto in Italian, from chiacchierare, a verb that means "to chat."

==Trivia==
*In "Mario Reads Your Letters," the Talkatoo is referred to as one of [[Mario (character)|Mario]]'s friends, despite his small meetings with Mario. It is possible that Mario was thankful to the Talkatoo for assisting in his journey to find [[Power Moon|Power Moons]], as sometimes the name of the Power Moon in question serves as a hint to the location.
*Throwing the [[Cappy]]-possessed [[Mario's Cap|Mario cap]] being thrown near the Talkatoo causes him to squawk endlessly.
*''Etymology'': His name is a portmanteau of the words "talk" and "cockatoo" (despite his resemblance to a parrot and not a real cockatoo).
*The Talkatoo's messages give away the Power Moon's name but not the location, so he is sort of the opposite to the Glasses Toad of the [[Toad Brigade]], who gives away the location of the Power Moon, but not the name.
