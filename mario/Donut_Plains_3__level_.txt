'''Donut Plains 3''' is a [[level]] of [[Donut Plains (world)|Donut Plains]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. It has a [[time limit]] of 300 seconds.

== Layout ==
[[File:Donut Plains 3.png|thumb|left|200px|Donut Plains 3 in the original ''Super Mario World'']]
The player starts out next to a [[Triple Swing Lift]] followed by a [[lift|Pulley Lift]] and another Triple Swing Lift. After getting through, the player comes to an area with [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. There are also some [[Rotating Block]]s under the player. If the player is not [[Small Mario]] or Small [[Luigi]], they can break the blocks with a [[Spin Jump]]. One of the Rotating Blocks contains a [[Vine]] that leads up to an area with cloud platforms and [[coin]]s. Up ahead are ON/OFF Switches that the player must use to change the path of a Pulley Lift to avoid falling into the [[Bottomless pit]] below them. The Pulley Lift leads the player to the [[Midway Gate]]. Past the gate, [[Fuzzy]]s will start appearing on [[track]]s, often while the player is riding a platform on the same track. After several more platforms, the player hops on one last platform with a [[Bonus Block]] nearby, which guides the player to the [[Giant Gate]].

===Secret area===
In the second half of the level, the player can use a Pulley Lift to reach a yellow [[Warp Pipe]]. This pipe takes them to a [[1-Up Chamber]], where they can win up to five [[Extra life|extra lives]] by hitting [[Prize Block]]s in the correct order.
