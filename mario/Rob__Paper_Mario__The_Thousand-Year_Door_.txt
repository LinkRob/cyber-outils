'''Rob''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a [[Bob-omb]] that lives in [[Fahr Outpost]].

==History==
Rob's only role in the game is where he, [[Fred]], and [[General White]] all operate a giant cannon that can launch [[Mario]] to [[The Moon]]. Oddly, Rob can also judge one's personality by viewing their facial hair.
