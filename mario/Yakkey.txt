'''Yakkey''', called '''Mystical Key''' as an item, is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He also functions as a key item.

==Physical description ==
Yakkey is shaped like a key and is gold in color. He also has long eyebrows and a small red ribbon tied to him.

==History==
{{Q|A key found in Tubba Blubba's room.|Description}}
[[File:PM_Mystical_Key.png|thumb|left]]
Yakkey is first seen after [[Mario]] opens a chest in [[Tubba Blubba]]'s room of his [[Tubba Blubba's Castle|castle]]. Yakkey then admits to Mario that he does unlock the [[Windy Mill|wind mill]] that leads to the [[Tubba Blubba's Heart|secret]] to Tubba Blubba's invincibility. However, Yakkey yells out to Tubba Blubba waking him up in an attempt to allow his boss to chase after them.

==Trivia==
*Yakkey is one of few characters in the series that counts as both a character and an item. Another example is the [[Boo's Portrait]] found earlier in the game in [[Boo's Mansion]] and [[Spangle]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.
*Yakkey's name is a pun of the words "yak," which means to talk a lot, and "key," which he is shaped as and also his function.
