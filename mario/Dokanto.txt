'''Dokanto''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Dokanto is a variant of an [[ant]] with a small cannon attached to its head.

===Behavior===
A Dokanto uses the cannon on its head to shoot pellets at [[Mario]].

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
[[File:Dokanto SML2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Dokanto are only encountered in the [[level]] [[The Ant Monsters]]. Mario can defeat a Dokanto by either [[stomp]]ing it or throwing a [[fireball]] at it.
