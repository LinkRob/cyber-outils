'''Cannon Spaces''' are a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Description==
If a character lands on a Cannon Space, they are blasted to space marked with a reticle of their decision.

==In ''Mario Party: Star Rush''==
Only two each appear in [[World 3-3 (Mario Party: Star Rush)|World 3-3]] and [[World 4-3 (Mario Party: Star Rush)|World 4-3]] of [[Toad Scramble]].
