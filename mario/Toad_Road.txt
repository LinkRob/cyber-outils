'''Toad Road''' is the first board in story mode in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. The player will go up against either [[Shy Guy]] or [[Kamek]] and the possibility of one or two other characters. If the player is paired up with either  [[Shy Guy]] or [[Kamek]], this ensures the player must become the superstar or tie to move on to the next board. The two bosses the player faces here are [[Lakitu]] at the fort and [[Wiggler]] beyond the Bowser Gate. Their battles are pretty easy in comparison to the others but still a challenge. After completing this board the player can move on to [[Bob-omb Factory]]. This is a board that is playable from the start through party mode.

==Trivia==
*The broken bridge is the map-exclusive content of this map (between 41 and 42). Four players must roll a Dice Block and get a total of 16 or more and the captain is allowed to use the 1-10 Dice Block. If you can't pass it, you need to go the path 2.
*Between 60 and 61, if Bowser don't do anything, path 1-J, 62 and 63 will be Shuffle Spaces and path 1-L, path 2-L will be Spin Spaces.
*Two mountains are shaped like the letters M and P, with a small island shaped like the number 9 close to them, which represents ''Mario Party 9''.
