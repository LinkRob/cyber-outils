''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''|levels=9|prewor=N/A|nxtwor=[[World 2 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 2]]}}'''World 1''' is the first world of ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. It is a grassy plain. This world has nine levels: eight are compulsory to complete in order to go to [[World 2 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 2]], but there is one special, optional level (called [[Hang Tongue!]]) which is unlocked after collecting all 40 [[Smiley Flower|Smiley Flowers]] for the world.
