The '''Octovader''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Octovaders are octopus-like enemies that can be first encountered in [[Land's End]]. Octovaders are a stronger variant of [[Octolot]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Octovaders are capable of using a lot of special moves. It can use [[Thunder (element)|thunder]]-based moves such as [[Lightning Orb]], but Octovader's most powerful is [[Drain Beam]] that can attack everyone in [[Mario]]'s party. Octovaders can also use [[Sleep Sauce]] to try and put the party to sleep.

===Strategy===
Octovaders are weak against [[Fire (element)|fire]], so any of Mario's fire techniques, such as [[Super Flame]], are very effective against them.
