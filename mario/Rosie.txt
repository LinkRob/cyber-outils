'''Rosie''' is a minor character in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. She is a sentient red {{w|rose}} who lives in [[Flower Fields]].

==Profile==
===Personality===
Rosie is a very snobbish character and believes she is the prettiest out of all the of the flowers in Flower Fields. Even when Flower Fields was in danger with her fellow flowers needing help, Rosie isn't worried what happens to them and can only think about herself and her own beauty. Even after Mario helps her out, she doesn't thank him and instant focuses on her own beauty making her the only one of the residents of Flower Fields that isn't helpful. She also appears to misinterpret things due to her focusing on her own beauty. In a letter that [[Wise Wisterwood]] sends to Mario, Wise Wisterwood mentions that Rosie was spreading a rumor about Mario being interested in her.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Rosie is found at the end of the hedge maze where she was holding the [[Water Stone]] that is needed to bring back the water in [[Lily (Paper Mario)|Lily]]'s pond. Even though Rosie does show a little concern for Lily, Rosie is not giving up the Water Stone easily and will only trade a prettier item for it. [[Mario]] must talk with [[Posie]] and get the [[Crystal Berry]] from her. Rosie deems the Crystal Berry to be prettier than the Water Stone and quickly trades the item with Mario. Rosie is seen holding the Crystal Berry for the remainder of the game.
