'''Spin-Sation''' is a Game of Fortune [[minigame]] in the [[Mario Party 4 (Flash game)|''Mario Party 4'' Flash game]].

==Gameplay==
At the price of one [[coin]], the player can spin the carnival wheel and have a chance to gain or lose coins. The player may win three, five, or ten coins or may lose five or three coins. There is also a choice for a free spin (equivalent to one coin) and a choice for "nothing." The player can decline to play at any point without losing coins. The player can keep taking turns with the wheel until they reach three separate coin wins, at which point they are sent back to the game board.

==Flavor text==
''Spin the dial to win! Each spin costs 1 coin. Good Luck!''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Its name is a pun on "sensation."
