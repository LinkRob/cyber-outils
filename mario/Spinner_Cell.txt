'''Spinner Cell''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
The solo character's platform lowers to the bottom-right of the stage where other three characters are. The large spiked spinner and the little yellow spinner come out of the background door and move onto the stage, shocking the characters.

==Gameplay==
The solo character takes control of a slow, rotating spiked spinner and a quicker, smaller spinner. If a team member touches the spiked spinner, they are eliminated, while the smaller one just knocks them around. If all three team members are eliminated, the solo character wins, but if at least one team member is remaining when the 30-second [[time limit]] is up, the team wins.

==Ending==
If the team wins, any remaining characters perform their victory animations within the stage. If the solo character wins, the camera moves an angle and zooms in, and they perform their victory pose.

==Controls==
===Solo===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move the large spinner 
* {{button|gcn|C}}: Move the small spinner

===Team===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: [[Jump]]

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''One player controls 2 spinning machines and uses them to attack the other players.''
* '''Advice''': ''The small machine stuns you, but the large one knocks you out of the ring.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a pun on the title of the video game series ''{{w|Tom Clancy's Splinter Cell|Splinter Cell}}''.
