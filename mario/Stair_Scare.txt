'''Stair Scare''' is a Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, Stair Scare is played against [[Whomp (character)|Whomp]] on the first floor of [[Duel Tower]]. When the player's character wins at the minigame, they can continue on to the next floor, and the minigame is unlocked for play in other modes.

==Opening==
Both competitors are standing on a staircase when an earthquake suddenly occurs, followed by the stairs individually falling off of the staircase behind them.

==Gameplay==
The object for both characters is to run up the staircase as quickly as possible as it collapses behind them. Players run by rapidly pressing {{button|gba|A}}. There is a [[Hot Air Balloon|hot air balloon]] at the end, and whoever reaches it first is the winner. Alternatively, a character automatically wins if they are too far ahead of their opponent.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|A}}: Climb stairs

==Flavor text==
*In-game description: ''Uh-oh! Earthquake! Quickly, run up the stairs before they fall apart!''
