The '''Sprixie Princesses''' are rulers of the [[Sprixie Kingdom]], making their debut in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. [[Bowser]] kidnapped the Sprixie Princesses during the events of the game.

== ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' series ==

=== ''Super Mario 3D World''/''Super Mario 3D World'' + ''Bowser's Fury'' ===
The Sprixie Princesses make their debut in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and [[Reissue|its]] [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|remaster]]''.'' In the opening, the green Sprixie Princess (ruler of [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]]) comes out of a clear pipe in the Mushroom Kingdom and warns [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Peach]], and [[Blue Toad|Toad]] that Bowser has taken control of the [[Sprixie Kingdom]] and kidnapped the other princesses. She herself was the only one who escaped his wrath. However, Bowser follows her through the [[Clear Pipe]] and kidnaps her, taking her to the Sprixie Kingdom as Mario and his gang pursues him.

Every Sprixie is first trapped in the final castle level of each [[World]]. When one is freed, she will take out either a wrench or hammer the same colour she is, shake it, and craft a clear pipe to the next world. This stops at [[World Castle]], where the gang frees the Red Sprixie Princess, only for them to be captured all at once by Bowser, who regained consciousness. Using their combined power, he creates both [[World Bowser]] and the sparkling pipe that leads to it.

After [[Meowser]] is defeated, the Sprixie Princesses break out of their jar. Then, after the credits finish rolling, they all go to World 1, where they craft a spaceship to [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]] from a rock. They will then go back to the castles in their respective Worlds and simply flutter around them.

==  ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series ==

=== ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' ===
Sprixie Princesses are featured in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as an a Advanced-tier Support [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]]. The player must use [[Wanda]] and [[Princess Shokora]] Scores to summon the spirit.

Their Spirit's effect provides their user with a Fairy Bottle that can restore damage if the user is at high-enough damage; referencing Bowser trapping the Sprixie Princesses in a bottle from ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== Trivia ==
* Due to the alternate exits in Worlds 1 and 4, you can skip saving the Green and Orange Sprixies entirely. If this is done, there will only be 5 Sprixies in World Castle and Bowser will only kidnap the 5 present. This also affects the credits cutscene, as well as the cutscene where the Sprixies craft the Spaceship.
* The Sprixie Princesses' color schemes represent the respective colors that collectively compose white light.
