'''Ladders''' are a generic object featured in various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related games. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]''.

==Description==
In their appearances, Ladders are generally used by a character to climb up to higher areas, typically in a level or other stage. Ladders are differently colored throughout their appearances, and the ones in ''Donkey Kong'' are colored cyan.
