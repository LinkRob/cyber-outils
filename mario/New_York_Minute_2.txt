'''New York Minute 2''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is the second route of [[New York Minute]], debuting in the Holiday Tour.

== Layout ==
This route has its layout focused more towards Central Park and the city streets, just like [[New York Minute 1]]. The racers start in front of Times Square, taking a left at a Y-shaped junction. After a 150-degree right turn, they go through Central Park, they then take a left turn, going through a 180-degree right turn as they briefly follow [[New York Minute 1]] in reverse. After they pass over a bridge going through the park, the racers make a left turn, lest they accidentally cross the Finish Line in reverse. The road curves right onto a street, where the racers can encounter a [[Glide Ramp]], which they can use to [[Glider|fly]] onto some of the cars. They then encounter a right turn, going down another street with more cars. After another right turn at a T-shaped junction, the racers reach the [[Finish line|starting line]].

''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' for the [[Nintendo Switch]] has this route being the second lap of [[New York Minute]] for the course's appearance in the [[Booster Course Pass]].
