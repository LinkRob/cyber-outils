'''Block and Load''' is a [[1 vs. 3 Minigame|1-vs-3]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. Block and Load derives from "lock and load".

== Opening ==
The blocks fall onto the tower before the game starts.

== Gameplay ==
The objective of the minigame is to shoot knock down blocks by shooting cannonballs. The solo player's shooting is stronger than the team's shooting, but it must cooldown five seconds and it is slower. The team's shooting has no cooldown and the shooting is faster, but its hit is not as strong as the solo player's hit. Either the solo player or the team wins if they demolish the blocks first.

== Ending ==
If the solo player wins, the winner is standing on the platform. The winner does his/her victory animation. If the team wins, they are standing on the floor unlike the solo player. The winners does their victory animation.

*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – Fire cannon
