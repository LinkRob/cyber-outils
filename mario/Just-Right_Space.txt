'''Just-Right Spaces''', spelled '''Just Right Spaces''' in PAL releases, are a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
The Just-Right Space only appears in [[Kamek's Carpet Ride]], and it is featured in two colors, orange and green. A character has to land precisely onto a Just-Right Spaces to proceed, and whoever lands on it receives a 2 [[Power Precision Card]]. After that, all the characters advance to one of the mansions. Inside either mansion, there is another Just-Right Space at the very end, and whoever lands on this Just-Right Space wins the game. If a player uses a card with a higher number than the number needed to reach one, they bounce back until there are no more spaces to move.
