'''Jacks''' are an object originating from the ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' arcade game. Jacks are a variation of [[springboard]]s found only in [[75m]], where they repeatedly enter from the upper-left area of the level around where [[Donkey Kong]] is, one after the other, and do two long bounces across the [[girder]] before falling straight down into a pit. If [[Mario]] gets hit by a Jack, he loses a [[extra life|life]].

Jacks have seldomly appeared outside of ''Donkey Kong''. However, in the [[Game Boy]] remake of the [[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|same name]], if a Jack falls on Mario while he is doing a [[handstand]], it falls off-screen.

They are also featured in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] within the [[75m (stage)|75m]] stage, which first appeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. For this stage, Jacks act the same as in the original ''Donkey Kong'', from which they had their behavior replicated. However, this is a stage feature that only occurs while the 8-bit Donkey Kong is not hiding within the background.
