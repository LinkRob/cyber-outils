==Usage==
The Koopa Kard can be used to help get the coins from a [[Koopa Bank Space]] without having to land on the space itself. This card should be used when a lot of coins is in the bank. The only ways to obtain a Koopa Kard is to either receive it as a gift from [[Toad (character)|Toad]] or find it in a [[Hidden Block]].
