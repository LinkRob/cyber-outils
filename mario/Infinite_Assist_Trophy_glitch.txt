The '''infinite Assist Trophy glitch''' is a bug in the early patches of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. This glitch is performed by [[Isabelle]], where she spawns Assist trophies without any control.

==Overview==
To perform this glitch, two players must use Isabelle as a fighter. Two players must stand to for the [[Assist Trophy]]  between them. Two players must peform Isabelle's side special, [[w:c:supersmashbros:Fishing Rod|Fishing Rod]] at the same time. This glitch cannot be performed on other Assist Trophies such as the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Squid Sisters|Squid Sisters]]. The player can stop this glitch by attacking the player continuously summoning Assist Trophies.

This glitch was fixed on the '''Patch 2.0.0'''.
