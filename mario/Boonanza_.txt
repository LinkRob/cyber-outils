'''Boonanza!''' is a [[Duel Minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective for each character is to round up as many [[Pink Boo]]s into their open corral within a 20-second time limit, with the aim being to round up more than their opponent. When a character approaches a Pink Boo, it moves away from them. Whoever has the most Pink Boos in their corral by the end is the winner, but if both characters have gathered the same number by the end, the minigame ends as a tie.

==Controls==
*Control Stick: Move
