'''Super Plant''' is the twenty-fourth live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the animated episode "[[The Pied Koopa]]."

==Plot==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] are upset because their [[Mama Mario|mother]]'s Basil plant is dying.  However, [[Dr. Toby]] comes in and gives them a secret plant growing formula named the [[Super Plant Growth Formula]]. He cautions the Marios that it's in the experimental stage and that they must read the directions.  Luigi starts to read the directions but Mario grows impatient and dumps the stuff on.  Later, the basil bush becomes becomes a [[Super Plant (character)|Super Plant]] and attacks Luigi.  Mario calls Dr. Toby and is chased by the plant.  Eventually Dr. Toby returns and returns the plant back to normal.

==Trivia==
*Patrick Dempsey plays the Super Plant and is in a full body costume throughout his appearance. He has no speaking roles, and his face is never seen.
