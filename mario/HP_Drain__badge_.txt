'''HP Drain''' is a [[badge]] in the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series. Wearing it allows [[Mario]] to restore [[Heart Point|HP]] every turn, although his [[Attack]] power is weakened. HP Drain has a partner-based variation named [[HP Drain P]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Hp_drain_badge.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', HP Drain can be bought from [[Merlow]] for fifteen [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]]s. Wearing HP Drain costs [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', HP Drain can be found in [[Rogueport]], in the port on the south side of town. Mario cannot get this badge until after Chapter 5, as he needs to his boat form to access the HP Drain. Using a boat panel in the port, Mario can swim around the port to a secret area on the left side of the port with a treasure chest containing this badge. HP Drain now has a lowered BP cost of one.
