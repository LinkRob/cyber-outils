'''WarioWare, Inc.''' is a recurring stage within the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and reappears in both ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. The stage is based on the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]], and it features a few [[microgame]]s that fighters have to play through during various parts the battle; a majority of these microgames are variations from earlier ''WarioWare'' games.

===Overview===
The stage's main setting, the "elevator room", has a simple layout consisting of a main platform that extends below the bottom blast line and four soft platforms, two stacked on either side. Compared to other stages, the blast lines are deceptively close to the camera.
