'''Seedy Pods''' are are a variant of [[Pokey Sprout]] first appearing in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. They resemble blue [[Pokey Sprout]]s and when approached, a Seedy Pod will burrow underground while launching dirt clumps at any would-be attackers. Seedy Pods are often seen bouncing around and singing before being approached, they can only be beaten with ranged attacks.

==History==
===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
Seedy Pods first appear in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. In this game, they can be found in [[Bianco Hills]] and the [[Red Coin Field]]. Seedy Pods are often seen bouncing idly until [[Mario]] approaches at which point they burrow under the ground and start spewing seed-like dirt clumps every which way. Seedy Pods are invincible when underground. To defeat a Seedy Pod, [[Mario]] must user [[F.L.U.D.D.]]'s [[Spray Nozzle]] from afar to stun it and then jump on its head to finish it off, upon defeat, a Seedy Pod will drop a [[Coin]]. In the Red Coin Field, some Seedy Pods also drop [[Red Coin]]s. Seedy Pods can also be beaten by being swallowed by [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].
