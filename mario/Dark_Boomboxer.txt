}}A '''Dark Boomboxer''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Boomboxers are the strongest type of [[Boomboxer]] in the game. They are also rather rare to encounter being found in a few rooms of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Like their counterparts, Dark Boomboxers attack with a powerful soundwave.
