'''Repel Gel''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Repel Gel is one of the most effective items to use during battle, as it causes [[Mario]] to become invisible for three whole turns. In [[Bowser's Castle]], with enough preparation, Mario can defeat the three [[Anti Guy]] unit with this item, as it buys Mario three free turns when used with [[Double Dip]] or [[Triple Dip]].

[[Boo's Sheet]], an even more effective variation, appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It can be bought on the [[Excess Express]] for 10 coins.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Repel Gel is a rare item that cannot be purchased normally in the game. One can be found in [[Gusty Gulch]], in a [[Question Block]] in the center of the road, right by a very short cliff. Another can be found in [[Jr. Troopa's Playground]], but Mario cannot obtain unless he has the [[Super Hammer]] which is obtained from [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]]. With it, Mario can smash the stone block, then jump to reveal the Repel Gel. The third can be found in [[Toad Town Tunnels]]. [[Rip Cheato|Rip Cheato's]] fourth item is a Repel Gel and, like his other items, the Repel Gel costs 64 [[coin]]s. Mario can, however, obtain an infinite number of these. ? Mark [[Li'l-Oink]]s give this to Mario when they leave the pen for another Li'l-Oink to be there. Another Repel Gel can be found in [[Jade Jungle]] in a hidden block by one of the many [[Bellbell Plant]]s found throughout the jungle.

==Trivia==
*Although the Repel Gel doesn't appear in the next game, ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', an item known as the [[Boo's Sheet]] effectively replaces the Repel Gel.
