'''Earth Tremor''' is a special move in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is learned after [[Mario]] gets the first [[Crystal Star]] after defeating [[Hooktail]]. Earth Tremor requires two [[Star Power|SP]] per use.

==Usage==
To use Earth Tremor, the player must press the {{Button|gcn|A}} whenever the line reaches the circle. The more times this is done, the faster the line will go. The player must fill up the gauge up to six times to deal the highest amount of damage (which is 6). Earth Tremor is considered one of the most useful [[Crystal Star]] moves because it pierces enemy defense and can attack all enemies on the screen, including flying and ceiling enemies. However, this can only be achieved by filling the gauge to the highest.
