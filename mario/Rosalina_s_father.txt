'''Rosalina's father''' is a character that first was mentioned in [[Super Mario Galaxy|''Super Mario Galaxy'']].

== History ==
He is mentioned only briefly in the [[Rosalina's Storybook|story book]]. At first, [[Rosalina]] searches for Baby Luma's mother with his telescope before she travels into space with Baby Luma's star ship. When Rosalina and Baby Luma have finished building their house, that is, the [[Comet Observatory|observatory]], they realize that it has become too big for two inhabitants. In addition, Rosalina thinks that her father, [[Rosalina's brother|brother]] and [[Rosalina's mother|mother]] are not with her. But since Rosalina's mother has died in the meantime and the rest of the family does not want to fly away, the ship is soon populated by [[Luma]]s.

== Trivia ==

* In the [[Terrace]], there is a telescope which is implied to be the same one the father owned.
