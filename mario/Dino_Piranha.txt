'''Dino Piranha''' is a large Piranha Plant that resembles to that of a dinosaur and is a boss in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. He makes his home in the [[Good Egg Galaxy]] and is much bigger than his ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' counterpart [[Peewee Piranha]]. The Dino Piranha serves as the first/penultimate boss of Good Egg Galaxy, is the first boss of the Terrace, which is the first area of the game and it is overall the first boss of the game that Mario encounters.

The Dino Piranha is inside an egg on his planet when Mario first encounters him. His egg is cracked when Mario crashes into him with the power of a [[Launch Star]]. The Dino Piranha then starts moving around from inside the egg, and when Mario attacks the tail, this will start the battle properly.

A stronger variation of Dino Piranha appears in the [[Melty Molten Galaxy]] named [[Fiery Dino Piranha]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:SMG Dino Piranha Battle.png|thumb|left|Dino Piranha]]
Dino Piranha's head looks similar to [[Petey Piranha]], despite the fact that the color of its head is purple with white spots when relaxed, as opposed to red with white spots. Its body is much different than Petey Piranha. Dino Piranha's body beneath the giant [[Piranha Plant]] head is that of a dinosaur's, (as would be expected given the name) with a green hue. Its tail has a brown club at the end which may be inspired by the club of a real life {{wp|Ankylosaurus}}. When it first breaks out of its egg, its head is purple, but when enraged, it turns red with anger.

The Dino Piranha is first seen in the [[Good Egg Galaxy]] during the mission [[Dino Piranha (mission)|Dino Piranha]]. The colossal plant-like dinosaur is seen inside his egg until [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] arrives on the planet cracking the egg and awakening the Dino Piranha which proceeds to walk around, after doing a [[Star Spin]] on his exposed tail, the egg shatters revealing the Dino Piranha which chases [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] around the planet, the plumbers can only beat the Dino Piranha by [[Star Spin]]ning by the tail again, the Dino Piranha gets more aggressive and bloodthirsty with each hit (if the Dino Piranha gets a hit on [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] he will knock the plumber high in the air and snicker at the damage dealt). After four hits, the Dino Piranha collapses releasing the first [[Power Star]]. When the [[Speedy Comet]] soars over the [[Good Egg Galaxy]], the Dino Piranha returns initiating [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] to do a rematch with the Dinosaur Veggie under timed conditions. Once done, the plumbers earn a second [[Power Star]]. A stronger relative of Dino Piranha engulfed in fire called [[Fiery Dino Piranha]] appears as a boss much later in the game in the [[Melty Molten Galaxy]].

During the ending cutscene after the Lumas save the Universe from a massive Black Hole that spawns after Bowser meets his downfall, the Dino Piranha can be seen next two a fountain that some [[Pumpkin Head|walking pumpkins]] and [[Mushroom Retainer|Mushroom Retainers]] circle around.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[Image:Dino Piranha Returns.png|thumb|right|Dino Piranha is back.]]
The Dino Piranha returns in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' as one of the five returning bosses (alongside [[King Kaliente]], [[Major Burrows]], [[Bouldergeist]], and [[Fiery Dino Piranha]]) appearing in the [[Boss Blitz Galaxy]]. He is the first of the five bosses to be fought in the Galaxy, [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] immediately lands on his egg the moment they arrive on the Galaxy and once again cracking it (This cutscene replaces the standard "WELCOME TO THE GALAXY!" message that appears in most Galaxies.), they the proceed to [[Star Spin]] the tail end once again shattering the egg revealing the Dino Piranha. The Dino Piranha once again attacks by attempting to trample [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] and gets more aggressive and bloodthirsty with each hit. After four hits, the Dino Piranha is down and releases the [[Launch Star]] leading to [[King Kaliente]]'s planet, the Dino Piranha is fought again during the [[Speedy Comet]]'s arrival on the Galaxy once again being the first boss fought. In addition to the returning [[Fiery Dino Piranha]], [[Peewee Piranha]] another relative of Dino Piranha appears as the game's first boss being fought twice in the [[Sky Station Galaxy]].

===''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games''===
[[Image:Dino Piranha Fires.png|thumb|left|The Dino Piranha makes his strike.]]
In the Wii version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'', Dino Piranha appears as an opponent during the [[Dream Spacewalk]] event. He is located on the Geo Planet (where Mario and Peewee Piranha clashed) in the [[Sky Station Galaxy]] and is out to bring the Galaxy under his control with the [[Luma]]s watching in fear. The main objective of the team of four is to pass through the rings provided by the [[Luma]]s, then perform a Rolling Attack toward Dino Piranha to deal some damage to him. Once the player hits Dino Piranha several times, he will start to spit fireballs at the team, which must be countered with a [[Star Spin]]. The team must use these methods repeatedly to defeat him and save the Galaxy and Lumas from his wrath thus completing the event.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===

==Trivia==
*The Dino Piranha's beta name was '''Piranha Plant Dinosaur'''.
*The Dino Piranha makes a cameo appearance in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' in the event Dream Spacewalk, making his home in the [[Sky Station Galaxy]] on the Geo Planet. The player must defeat him by passing through the rings to gain power. After his [[Heart Point|Stamina]] drops below half, the Dino Piranha will attempt to counterattack with fireballs.
*The Dino Piranha's music is the same as while fighting [[Tarantox]], even though the latter has the [[A Tense Moment|Boss Prelude]] theme which is usually applied to bosses with [[King Kaliente]]'s theme.
*The Dino Piranha is one of four bosses throughout the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' series to be fought in more than one Galaxy and to be fought more then once, being fought two times in the main story mode of Super Mario Galaxy and optionally fought in a [[Daredevil Comet]], as he is fought in the Good Egg Galaxy twice(the first time in the story mode, and the second time in a Comet Trial) and appears once more as Fiery Dino Piranha in the [[Melty Molten Galaxy]].
