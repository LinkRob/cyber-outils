}}'''Kremlantis''' is a half-submerged ocean city located near [[Donkey Kong Island]] that served as the second location of ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]''. 
==Achiterture==
In surface, Kremlantis consists of Greek-style temples with [[Kritter]] heads on pillars. In underwater, it consists of a large series of ruins, a seaweed field and a [[Clambo]] giant statue on end of the map.
==History==
===Background information===
This city was built by [[King K. Rool]]'s ancestors on an islet near Kong Island to serve as a refuge in moment which the pirate [[Snap]]s invaded the [[Crocodile Isle]] for ancient times.<ref>[https://mario.fandom.com/wiki/File:Like_of_Adrian_Smith_in_Twitter_(12,_25,_2023).png Adrian Smith's like on Twitter] (December 25, 2023)</ref> Abandoned because of the flood, that city became a natural habitat for many aquatic animals and of course the ancient temples that survived in the middle of the surface are still standing flooded.

===''Donkey Kong'' franchise===
In ''Donkey Kong Land'', the submerged civilization was explored by DK and Diddy as the second location from [[Cranky Kong]]'s test.<ref>"'''Cranky Kong''': ''I'll call old K. Rool and arrange everything. You'll wake up tomorrow without your bananas.''" - ''Donkey Kong Land'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 1995, p. 3</ref> Here the pair explored the temples that are visible on surface, swam to the underwater ruins full of [[Flapper]]s, dodged the mincers in underwater left by the [[Kremling Krew]], dodged the baskets full of [[Slippa]]s, grabbed a [[Bomb|bomb]] to destroy the stone barrier on map, escaped the [[Nemo]]s and jumped into Swirlwinds until they faced a giant Clambo.
