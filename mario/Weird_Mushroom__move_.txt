'''Weird Mushroom''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can only be used by [[Croco]] during the battle on [[Bandit's Way]].

== Effects ==
When Weird Mushroom is used, a green [[Mushroom]] appears over Croco's head and he eats it restoring 60 of his health and increasing his defense. As stated above, Croco only uses it during the fight with him on Bandit's Way and only uses it one time overall.

== Trivia ==
*Weird Mushroom is similar in appearance to the [[1-Up Mushroom]].
