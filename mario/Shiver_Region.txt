==History==
It is located in the far northeast of the nation. In it resides a single town known as Shiver City which is the home of the [[Bumpty|Bumpties]]. In the northernmost part of the region is Shiver Mountain that contains the [[Crystal Palace]] on the summit, a forgotten shrine that is where the last [[Star Spirit]] is kept. Mario and his partners journeyed up to the Shiver Region in the last chapter of ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Mario had to defeat the mighty [[Crystal King]] in order to save the last of the Star Spirit.
