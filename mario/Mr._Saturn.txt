'''Mr. Saturns''' are alien-like creatures from the [[EarthBound (series)|''EarthBound'' series]]. They reside in a location called [[w:c:earthbound:Saturn Valley|Saturn Valley]], and speak their own language.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
Mr. Saturns are weak tossable items in every game of the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series from ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' onward. While they normally walk around on the stage, Mr. Saturns can be picked up and thrown at another player for minor damage. However, they deal a great amount of [[Shield (Super Smash Bros. series)|shield]] damage; in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', a thrown Mr. Saturn can instantly break another player's shield.

[[Princess Peach|Peach]] (and [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''), when doing her [[Vegetable (move)|Vegetable move]], has a small (1/166) chance of pulling up a Mr. Saturn instead of a vegetable; this is also mentioned in the description for one of the alternate Peach trophies in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U''.

===''Super Smash Bros. Melee''===
Mr. Saturns are the only items in the [[Onett]] stage of [[Adventure (Super Smash Bros. Melee)|Adventure]] mode. He also awards the most bonuses of any other items, including Saturn Ringer, Mr. Saturn Fan, Saturn Siblings, and Mrs. Saturn.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
There is a Mr. Saturn [[List of Mii Headgear|hat]] that [[Mii Fighter]]s can wear, and it can be unlocked for 450 G.

==In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''==
[[File:SMM-MrSaturn.png|frame|left]]
A Mr. Saturn can be unlocked as a [[Mystery Mushroom]] [[Costume Mario|costume]], via the [[List of official Super Mario Maker courses|Event Course]] [[Welcome to Saturn Valley]]. Their appearance is based on the original Mr. Saturn sprites from ''EarthBound''.
