'''Through the Jet Stream'''  is the sixth [[Mission]] (seventh in DS) from [[Jolly Roger Bay]] in [[Super Mario 64]] and it's [[Reissue|remake]]: [[Super Mario 64 DS]].

== Description ==
To collect the sixth/seventh [[Power Star]] of [[Jolly Roger Bay]], [[Mario]] or [[Wario]] must swim through the level to find a jet Stream to collect a Power Star.  The Star is collected differently in both games and have different requirements to collect it as well.  

=== ''In Super Mario 64'' ===
To collect the star in [[Super Mario 64]], Mario needs to use a [[cannon]] found at the start of the level to shoot him across the stage. Then, he can swim down to find a hole in the walls of the level that he can swim through. Once he has gone through the hole, there will be a Jet stream near the middle of the area. Mario has to continuously swim down it until the [[Power Star]] spawns. Once it has spawned, Mario can swim back under the [[Sunken Ship]] and can collect the Star.

=== ''In Super Mario 64 DS'' ===
In [[Super Mario 64 DS]], The stage starts the same as the original version but [[Wario]] must be used to collect the Star and he has to have the [[Metal Cap]] unlocked. When Wario reaches the area where the star spawns in the original game, there is a small platform that contains the Metal Cap box. Then after collecting it, he has to leave the area and collect the Power Star inside of a different Jet Stream under the [[Sunken Ship]].
