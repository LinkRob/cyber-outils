The '''Petal House''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Petal House is is the home of [[Petal Guy (character)|Petal Guy]], located in the [[Jungle Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. The exterior shows the Petal House to be a brown hut with a straw roof and a large flower on the roof's peak, while the interior shows several potted flowers in the background.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Petal Guy spends his time at the Petal House writing poetry for the woman he loves. If the player's character visits the Petal House, Petal Guy immediately asks them for their help. By accepting, this begins the quest, [[Blossom of My Heart]], which involves delivering the [[Poem of Love]] to his romantic interest, [[Flutter (character)|Flutter]].
