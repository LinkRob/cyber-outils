The '''Snow Tour''' is the 38th tour of [[Mario Kart Tour]]. It brings back [[DK Summit]] from [[Mario Kart Wii]] and introduces Penguin Toad as a new unlockable driver. With this tour, the [[Kart Pro]] challenge was also introduced, challenging players to rank first place three times consecutively in multiplayer races. Drivers, karts, and gliders can now be upgraded to level 7, and the maximum tier rank was increased to 70.
