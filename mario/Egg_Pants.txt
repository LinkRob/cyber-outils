The '''Egg Pants''' are a [[clothing]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. They can be bought from present-day [[Princess Peach's Castle]] for 450 [[coin]]s. The Egg Pants can only be worn by either [[Baby Mario]] or [[Baby Luigi]]. Equipping the Egg Pants to either baby raises their [[Defense]] by 38.
