'''Alarm Clock''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

== In ''Wario Land II'' ==
It is a giant alarm clock serve as both the first enemy and boss. While the Alarm Clock does make noise, it does not damage [[Wario]], who can defeat the Alarm Clock by jumping onto it, where its button is located.
