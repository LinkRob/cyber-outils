'''Lemon Candy''' is a [[recipe]] found in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
The Lemon Candy restores 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 15 [[Flower Point|FP]]. It can be cooked by [[Tayce T.]] by giving her a [[Lemon]] and a [[Cake Mix]]. The Lemon Candy can be sold for 15 [[coin]]s.

Coincidentally, Lemon Candy is the favorite candy of the [[Anti Guy]] in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]. It is possible to avoid fighting Anti Guy by giving him Lemon Candy in exchange for the [[treasure chest]] that he guards.
