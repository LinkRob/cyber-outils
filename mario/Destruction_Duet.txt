'''Destruction Duet''' is a [[2 vs. 2 Mini-game]] that appears in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
There are two players on each team. Both teams must try destroy the [[Gray Bowser Statue|Bowser Statue]] as quickly as possible. Each player on a team can distract the other team by attacking them.

The team that destroys the Bowser Statue first wins the minigame.
