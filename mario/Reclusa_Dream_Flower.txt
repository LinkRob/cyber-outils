'''Reclusa Dream Flowers''' are objects that can be found late into the game in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They appear when [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|hijacks]] [[Concordia]].

== Description ==
Reclusa Dream Flowers resemble bluebells, with their petals bearing happy closed eyes in the front and their interiors have yellow plugs in them. Their purpose is to trap their prey in dreams. The size of these flowers can vary depending on the size and shape of their targeted victim's head. These dreams can be escaped by spotting the thread in the illusion and finding the exit. 

== History ==
Reclusa Dream Flowers make their debut when [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Snoutlet]], and [[Cozette]] escape [[Zokket]]'s [[Fortress Zokket|fortress]] after the latter donated the last of his life energy to [[Reclusa]]'s egg so it can break out. Reclusa, upon hatching from its egg, constructs the [[Soli-Tree]] and [[Reclusa World|conquers]] [[Concordia]], trapping many in its path in the Reclusa Dream Flowers. [[Arc]] is the first character to be shown caught, alerting the gang about the Reclusa Dream Flowers before one of them wraps itself around his head. [[Snoutlet]] is reasonably outraged when he hears Reclusa's reasoning that the victims will wither as their minds enjoy the dreams.

Later, as Mario, Luigi, and Snoutlet storm the Soli-Tree, they get caught by some Reclusa Dream Flowers, dreaming that they are in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], which has made peace with [[Bowser]] and the [[Koopa Troop]]. Mario, Luigi, and Snoutlet find that their surroundings are off, escaping by hitting a backwards variant of a [[Mystery Block]], revealing Reclusa controlling the illusion. This allows them to leave the dream and wake up, with the heroes killing the Reclusa Dream Flowers that trapped them, while enraging the friendship-hating supercomputer. After Reclusa's death and destruction, the Reclusa Dream Flowers vanish, releasing those who were trapped in them.

==Trivia==
* Strangely, the flower holding Mario in place does not have the closed eyes on the front while the other grasping Luigi did. This is likely an error.
*Arc is the only character to notice one of the dream flowers shortly before getting trapped, while the other victims did not notice until it was too late.
*Snoutlet is the only trapped victim whose body is never seen under the flower. This is due to him hiding in Luigi's hat during that time.
*Mario, Luigi, and Snoutlet were the only victims to successfully escape from the dream flowers on their own. This is because Snoutlet also ended up being inadvertently caught and him pointing out anything flawed and inconsistent with the illusionary Mushroom Kingdom that Mario and Luigi do not recognize as being part of the real kingdom.
