'''World 1 N-Card''' has two appearances in the first level. It is a match two and win game, with two chances of losing. Items to match: [[Golden Coin|10 Coins]], twenty [[coin]]s, [[Star]], [[Mushroom]], [[Fire Flower]], and a [[1-Up Mushroom|1-Up Chest]].
