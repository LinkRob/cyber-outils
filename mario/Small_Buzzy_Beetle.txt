'''Small Buzzy Beetles''' are a small variation of [[Buzzy Beetle]]s. They only appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
Small Buzzy Beetles are encountered in the [[Sacred Forest]]. They were originally regular-sized Buzzy Beetles, but they, along with the rest of Sacred Forest, were shrunk as a result of [[Kamek]]'s magic. Aside from this, a Small Buzzy Beetle is fought as the second opponent of [[Roshambo Temple 7]].

A [[Small Buzzy Beetle Gang]] consists of several Small Buzzy Beetles. A Small Buzzy Beetle is also included within a [[Buzzy Beetle Tower]].
