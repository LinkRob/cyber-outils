}}A '''Small Sammer Guy''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Small Sammer Guys are a species of [[Sammer Guy]]s only found in [[Sammer's Kingdom]]. Small Sammer Guys are very small and have a low amount of HP (only 1) but they are extremely fast. Small Sammer Guys are often viewed as one of the most troubling Sammer Guy to deal with. An effective way to defeat a Small Sammer Guy is to knock it away with [[Barry]] when one gets close.
