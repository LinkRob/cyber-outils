'''Earth Link''' is a supporting boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It greatly resembles [[Mad Adder]] in terms of color and appearance, and is controlled by [[Cloaker]]. Eventually, [[Mario]] and his party defeat Earth Link in battle.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
===Battle===
Earth Link will only appear if Mario and the others defeat [[Domino]] first instead of Cloaker. Cloaker will hop onto his snake and activates the second phase of the battle. In battle, the Earth Link attacks with a [[Poison (move)|poisonous bite]] and with the deadly [[Carni-Kiss]].

====Strategy====
The Earth Link has much more HP than Domino's Mad Adder snake so it is recommended to take out Cloaker first and avoid the fight with the Earth Link. However, if the Earth Link is fought, Mario's team should focus on magic attacks due to its rather low magic defense. [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] should heal any poisoning when necessary.
