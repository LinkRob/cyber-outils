A '''Metal Block''' is a large [[block]] which appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', large blocks can be breakable once [[Mario]] gets the Ultra Hammer from [[Mt. Lavalava]]. Metal Blocks usually lead to [[Super Block (object)|Super Block]]s. Metal Blocks are very common throughout Mt. Lavalava.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', the Metal Blocks retain their purpose being only broken with the Ultra Hammer. One Metal Block blocks the door containing the Warp Pipe that leads to Chapter 7 (mainly [[Fahr Outpost]]).
