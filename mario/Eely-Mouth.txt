'''Eely-Mouth''' is an eel only appearing in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It lives in the Nokis' ancestral home, a massive underwater chamber below [[Noki Bay]], and its {{wp|gingivitis}} is the cause of the widespread pollution in the area. Eely-Mouth has two separate pairs of eyes on either side of its mouth; one side has a pair of eyebrows, and the other has long eyelashes.

==History==
===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
[[File:SMS Eely Mouth Defeated.png|thumb|200px|left|Mario defeats Eely-Mouth.]]
In ''Super Mario Sunshine'', to purify Noki Bay's [[water]], [[Mario]] must clean Eely-Mouth's eight teeth, three of which are rotten and fall out after being sprayed with [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]]'s water.

At the start of [[Eely-Mouth's Dentist|the episode]], the [[Noki elder]] explains to Mario that he has finally figured out the problem as to why the bay is polluted, and sends him to the ocean floor to fight the giant eel. Due to its intense suffering, Eely-Mouth is aggressive and spins around very fast, and then rise upward and open its mouth. This is when Mario can attempt to clean its teeth, by spraying them with FLUDD. After some time, Eely-Mouth [[whirlpool|sucks in water]], and Mario as well, if he is too close. If this happens, Mario is quickly spat back out and loses air. Due to Eely-Mouth's intense pain, toxic, purple tears regularly come out of its eyes. These tears damage Mario if touched, but refill his breath meter. If Mario sprays the bubbles with water from FLUDD, they pop into a cluster of smaller regular bubbles that do not harm Mario and replenish his air supply. Once all the teeth have been cleaned, Eely-Mouth retreats back into its cave, and spits out its golden tooth, which one of them is revealed to be after being cleaned. The golden tooth turns into a [[Shine Sprite]] that Mario must collect at the bottom. As the eel recedes into its cave, it also leaves behind twenty [[coin]]s in a heart-shaped formation, which float just above the mouth of its cave (collecting them is optional), most likely as a sign of thanks. After Eely-Mouth has completely disappeared, FLUDD comments, "Remember to take proper care of your teeth!"

===''Mario Golf'' series===
In ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]'' and ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]'', the name "Eely-mouth" can appear on the leaderboard.
