'''Seesaw Shrooms''' are an object in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
Seesaw Shrooms are a type of [[Mushroom Platform]] consisting of an orange cap with a circle in the middle. Seesaw Shrooms tilt slightly from side to side when idle, but they balance when there is the weight of the playable characters on them. Enemies and objects do not affect the balancing of the platforms. Seesaw Shrooms come in varying sizes. Seesaw Shrooms have an identically-named icy [[Seesaw Shroom (blue)|blue variant]].

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. U''==
Seesaw Shrooms are the titular feature of the level [[Seesaw Shrooms]], and they later also appear in {{world-link|coin|3|World Coin-3 (New Super Mario Bros. U)}}.
