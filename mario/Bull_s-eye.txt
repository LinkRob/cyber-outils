'''Bull's-eye''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] as a reward from [[Toady (Mario Party Advance)|Toady]] by completing the quest [[Kamek Krew Live!]].

==Gameplay==
Bull's-eye involves throwing darts at a dart board. The player has eight darts, and like in an actual darts, the aim is to throw each dart at the red bull's-eye. The player can use the {{button|gba|Pad}} to aim a dart, and the vertical trajectory can be altered depending on how long {{button|gba|A}} is held. As Bull's-eye is only a simple recreation of a game of darts, there is no reward for getting eight bull's-eyes in a row. There is an additional feature in which the player can press {{button|gba|L}} or {{button|gba|R}} to change the dart color from red, which is its default color, to blue, green, yellow, or gray before circling back to red.
