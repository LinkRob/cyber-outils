'''Bitesize''' is an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Bitesizes are small green-like fish.

=== Behavior ===
The Bitesize attacks [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong]] by biting. These enemies appear alone or in a large school. They can not be defeated by Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong so they must avoid it. However, [[Enguarde]] may defeat them in one strike. Bitesizes do not chase the Kongs or Enguarde but instead move randomly either left or right or back and forth the entire time.

==In ''Donkey Kong Country''==
Bitesizes are encountered in all of the underwater levels except [[Croctopus Chase]].

Bitesizes can also be found in the mini-game [[Funky's Fishing]] in the [[Game Boy Color]] and [[Game Boy Advance]] remake of ''Donkey Kong Country'' where [[Donkey Kong]] must catch as much fish as possible. The Bitesizes can be regular colored or pure red, blue, green, or yellow or even flashing ones.
