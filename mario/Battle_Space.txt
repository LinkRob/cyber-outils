The '''Battle Space''' is recurring [[space]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Description==
The Battle Space typically involves all players wagering a certain number of [[Coin]]s for a Battle Minigame, determined by a roulette (except in ''Mario Party 2''). After the minigame, the coins are distributed among the characters depending on how well they ranked in the minigame. If all players lose a Battle Minigame, the coins return to their original owners.

In ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', ''[[Mario Party 6]]'', ''[[Mario Party 7]]'', ''[[Mario Party 8]]'', and ''[[Mario Party DS]]'', Battle Spaces are dropped from gameplay. In these games, Battle minigames periodically appear in lieu of the usual end-of-turn minigame (only 4-player mini-games in ''Mario Party 5'', ''6'', and ''7''), although it happens entirely by chance (except in ''Mario Party 8'', where it occurs once every 10 turns). Due to their occasional replacement of end-of-turn minigames, earnings from these minigames now are counted towards one's minigame coin total, making them a large influence in the awarding of the [[Bonus Star#Mini-Game Star|Mini-Game Star]].

==History==
===''Mario Party 2''===
In ''Mario Party 2'', Battle Spaces are green spaces depicting a white lightning bolt. When a player lands on this space, [[Goomba]] comes by, gets a certain number of coins from each player and a Battle [[minigame]] begins. Players attempt to battle each other to get the jackpot that the Goomba took: The character in first place gets 70 percent of the coins, while the character in second place receives 30 percent. If the total jackpot is not a multiple of 10, all coins but one will be distributed in this manner, and the remaining coin goes to a random player.

A Battle minigame does not occur if a player lands on this space and there are fewer than 20 coins in play total.

===''Mario Party 3''===
In ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', Battle Spaces retain the same role, except the number of coins is determined by a [[Roulette (Mario Party series)|roulette]] (which would also become the case for later ''Mario Party'' titles) and the space depicts a [[Goomba]].

Goomba has a specific message if there are fewer than 20 coins in play: "Hey! Where are all the coins? No one has even 10 coins! In that case, there'll be no Battle Game! C'mon everyone, get busy!"

===''Mario Party 4''===
In ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', Goomba becomes the host of his own board, and his role as Battle minigame host is filled by [[Bob-omb]]. The space retains its green color and has been changed to appropriately depict a Bob-omb icon. The player who landed on the Battle Space is able to choose the mini-game, unlike other titles.

===''Mario Party 6''===
In ''[[Mario Party 6]]'', Battle Spaces appear only in [[Solo Mode]]. They are now purple with a violet B on them, although the European and Australian versions depict a lightning bolt, more similar to ''Mario Party 2''. Landing on a Battle Space initiates a Battle minigame against the [[Koopa Kid]]s. Unlike other games, Battle Spaces do not have any host, although the Battle Mini-Games themselves are hosted by [[Brighton]] or [[Twila]] in [[Party Mode]].

===''Mario Party 9''===
Battle Spaces return in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', only in a three- or four-player game. [[Hammer Bro]] and [[Fire Bro]] now co-host the Battle minigames, and one will appear whenever a Battle Space is landed on, with Hammer Bro appearing more frequently. Hammer Bro will take three [[Mini Star]]s from each player before starting the minigame, which are wagered, while Fire Bro will instead take a total of ten from whoever places last, and split them between the winners, working the same way as in the original ''[[Mario Party]]'', wherein the loser(s) must give the winner(s) coins after playing a specific minigame. Either Bro will provide extra Mini Stars if at least one player does not have enough Mini Stars, even if players have no Mini Stars at all. The captain will then pick the Free-for-All minigame that gets played, unless the group has already passed the green [[Toad (species)|Toad]], in which case the player in last gets to pick. If everybody ends up in first place or last place, the Fire Bro will not give or take any [[Mini Star]]s away from any players.

If Fire Bro appeared before the start of the minigame, he will reappear on the results screen and attack the player who came last in the minigame with fireballs, which makes them lose their ten Mini Stars. The other three players are then given the amount of Mini Stars distributed depending on the amount of people playing, then (including the one in last place) perform their respective animations for their placing in the minigame. This is one of the only times in the game when an event on the results screen occurs before the actual results appear, the other being [[Bowser Jr.]] coming to give or steal five Mini Stars from the two-player team after winning or losing a Bowser Jr. minigame in Party or Solo Mode, or when a tie occurs in a Jackpot Machine minigame in [[Bowser Station]] and Mini Stars are left over, which a green Toad will distribute to a random player.

Last place does not always mean 4th place; it means the current lowest ranking shown. As such, even if two players tie for 3rd or three players tie for 2nd, it is last place since it is the lowest ranking.

If three players tie for first, the loser loses 9 Mini Stars and the winners get 3 each. On the contrary, if three players tie for last place, then they each lose 3 Mini Stars while the winner gets 9.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bl6eJkUQAk0</ref>
