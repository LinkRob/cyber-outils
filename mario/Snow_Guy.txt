'''Snow Guys''' are enemies in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. They appear in the background of levels, and when they spot [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], an exclamation point appears above their head, and they will start making a snowball, which it will throw at Yoshi. They are invincible, and only appear in [[Poochy and Nippy]] and [[Frustration]].
