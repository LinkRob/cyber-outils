'''Dragon Pot''' is a power-up item in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. It is a [[Power Up Pot]] that transforms [[Wario]] into [[Dragon Wario]], replacing his [[Wario Cap|cap]] with a dragon head capable of breathing flames. The effect of the Dragon Pot lasts only a few seconds. The Dragon Pot also works underwater, but is less effective when used there.
