The '''Study''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. This room resembles any old Study. Luigi can first access the Study after being thrown into the room from the bed in the [[Bedroom (Gloomy Manor)|Bedroom]]. The fireplace will also take Luigi down into the [[Studio]].

==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-2: Gear Up!|A-2: Gear Up!]], Luigi is thrown into the Study from the Bedroom to continue his search for the missing Gear Pieces. However, as he enters any further, [[Greenie]]s and a [[Slammer]] will appear and battle him. Luigi vacuums them all up into the [[Poltergust 5000]] and obtains the Gear Piece. He then steps into the fireplace and enters the Studio.

Once Luigi obtains the [[Dark-Light Device]] in the fourth mission, he can return to the Study to use it on a missing portrait just northwest. After vacuuming up the [[Spirit Ball]]s, he will be awarded with a [[Gem]].
