'''Dark Jawbus''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
They are a dark version of a [[Jawbus]] found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Like with a Jawbus, the Dark Jawbus attacks by extending it's neck making it impossible to jump past it. The only way to defeat a Dark Jawbus is to use [[Mario]]'s [[Flip]] to get past it and then attack its tail (its weak point) until it is defeated.
