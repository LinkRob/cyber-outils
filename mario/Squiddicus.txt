Squiddicus is a giant, orange octopus enemy introduced in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'', its [[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]] [[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D|remake]] and its [[2025]] [[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD|remaster]].
== Description ==
He features spiked, suction‑covered arms, large  eyes, and darker orange wave‑like markings on his head. In that game, he appears only in the level [[Stormy Shore]], serving as the main hazard. Squiddicus later returns in [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]] and its [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze (Nintendo Switch)|Switch port]], appearing exclusively in the [[Sea Breeze Cove]] stage [[Irate Eight]].

== Game Appearances ==

=== ''Donkey Kong Country'' series ===

==== [[Donkey Kong Country Returns|''Donkey Kong Country Returns'']] ====
[[File:Squiddicus Cave.png|left|thumb|249x249px|Squiddicus attacking [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] in a cave, from ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''.]]
In Donkey Kong Country Returns, Squiddicus appears in the level [[Stormy Shore]] as the main obstacle of the level, using his tentacles to block and attack [[Donkey Kong]] and/or [[Diddy Kong]]. He can also destroy the platforms that they are standing on. Squiddicus can not be hurt in this game, and acts as an enemy the player(s) must flee from.

=== [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze|''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze'']] ===
[[File:Squiddicus Underwater.png|right|thumb|249x249px|Squiddicus revealing himself to [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Dixie Kong]], from ''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]''.]]
He later returns in Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze in the stage [[Irate Eight]], this time much larger in size and now faced underwater. He'll use his ink to cover the stage from bellow, and Donkey Kong and/or co. must quickly swim upwards to evade it. He also uses his tentacles to attack, as well as breaking off pieces of the terrain to fall on Donkey Kong and/or co.  At the end of the stage, the player(s) must use a [[Barrel Cannon|barrel cannon]] to launch themselves into chains holding three bombs. This will cause the chains to break and the bombs to fall on Squiddicus. Once all three bombs fall on Squiddicus, he will be defeated and the player(s) will be able to beat the stage.
