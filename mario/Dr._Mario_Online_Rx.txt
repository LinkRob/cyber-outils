'''''Dr. Mario Online Rx''''' is a [[WiiWare]] game part of the [[Dr. Mario (series)|''Dr. Mario'' series]]. The game was developed by [[Akira]] and published by [[Nintendo]]. It was released in 2008, at a price of 1,000 Wii Points. However, since the [[Wii Shop Channel]] was decommissioned in January 2019, the title became no longer available for purchase. Shortly after its release, ''Dr. Mario Online Rx'' was followed by ''[[Dr. Mario Express]]'', a [[DSiWare]] title considered to be a handheld counterpart to the game, given it was also developed by Akira.
