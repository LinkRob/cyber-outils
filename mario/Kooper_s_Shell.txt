'''Kooper's Shell''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is a unique blue shell belonging to [[Kooper]].

==History==
Kooper's Shell is usually seen on Kooper's back; however, during the events of Chapter 1, a [[Fuzzy]] has stolen it and taken it deeper in the forest behind [[Koopa Village]]. [[Mario]] has to chase down the Fuzzy into the forest until it begins to hide around in a series of trees challenging Mario to find him. Mario manages to find the Fuzzy every time until it gives up the shell. Mario then gives the shell back to Kooper, who then joins his party.

==Trivia==
*Kooper's Shell is very similar to [[Madame Flurrie]]'s [[Necklace]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' as they are both items belonging to [[Mario]]'s partners that have been stolen at one point and they join Mario's party once they get them back.
