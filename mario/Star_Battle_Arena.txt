'''Star Battle Arena''' is a solo mode that determines the superstar of the [[Star Carnival]]. The participant that dominates all of their opponents wins a one-year supply of candy as a prize. [[MC Ballyhoo]] hosts the arena in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

== Description ==
The Star Battle Arena is a solo mode that hosts the Star Battle. In the arena, MC Ballyhoo determines the true superstar of the [[Star Carnival]].<ref name="mp81">{{cite video game |title=[[Mario Party 8]] |developer=[[Hudson Soft]] |publisher=[[Nintendo]] |date=May 29, 2007 |platform=[[Wii]]  |level=Star Battle Arena |quote=The Star Battle is the main event! Here we find out who is the true Superstar of the carnival! |speaker=[[MC Ballyhoo]]}}</ref> Depending on which character the player chose, the character will appear on the arena. The superstar earns a super prize, "a one-year supply of [[candy]] as a prize".

The player's character will compete against a CPU-controlled opponent taking place in a board map. The board map is chosen in its respective order. If the player defeats their opponent, the player progresses to fight the next opponent etcetera.
{{Spoiler}}
After defeating the five opponents, [[MC Ballyhoo]] grants the [[Star Rod]] to the player. [[Bowser]] intervenes the Star Carnival and takes the rod. The player will arrive at [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]] and [[Bowser]] will send either [[Hammer Bro]] or [[Blooper]] as the player's opponent. If the player outmatches the opponent, [[Bowser]] returns the Star Rod. Bowser engages the player in a minigame, [[Superstar Showdown]]. After the player defeats Bowser, they return to the Star Carnival and MC Ballyhoo announces the superstar.
{{Endspoiler}}
