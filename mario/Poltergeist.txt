'''Poltergeists''' (known as '''Boffins''' in the European version) are [[ghost]]s that reside in [[Evershade Valley]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They are fought as a mini-boss in [[Quiet, Please!|A-3: Quiet, Please!]] They may also appear as enemies in the bonus missions of [[Gloomy Manor]] and the [[Old Clockworks]]. A large variant of the Poltergeist, known as [[The Brain]], appears as the final boss in the co-op mode, the [[ScareScraper]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Poltergeists are mainly orange in color. They have big, round heads with a purple nucleus in their heads representing their brains.

=== Abilities ===
The Poltergeist has the power to move objects with telekinesis. When Luigi fights the Poltergeist, it throws books at him while he searches for it.

==Battle==
Poltergeists are fought in the Library in this game. They mainly attack by using their psychic powers to throw books at Luigi. These ghosts are smart and they will stay invisible when throwing books and they will sometimes block a Strobe light flash with the book they are holding.  There are other versions of the Poltergeist fought through the game as well, such as the [[Ancient Poltergeist]] and [[Strong Poltergeist]], who have extra abilities, but they are defeated in the same way as the normal Poltergeist. When the [[Strobulb]] is used on it while it is carrying a book, it blocks the light using the book. It also plays the piano during the entire battle. In the Old Clockworks bonus mission, instead of books, it throws the bricks placed around the room. It can even throw books at Luigi while it is being sucked into the [[Poltergust 5000]] to escape.

===Tips===
Luigi must wait until the Poltergeist tosses the book before he can use the Strobe Light flash. Once stunned, Luigi can start sucking up the Poltergeist but he must also be careful. Books will start to fly at him when he does this so he should move around carefully. Fortunately, Poltergeists always drop Hearts whenever they're sucked up, most times fully healing Luigi.
