A '''Flower Tab''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
Flower Tabs are among the [[Flower (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Flower]] items that can increase [[Flower Point|FP]]. Using a Flower Tab increases the party's maximum FP by one. Flower Tabs are often found in [[treasure chest|treasure boxes]] or given to [[Mario]] by certain characters.
