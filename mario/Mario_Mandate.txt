{{Q|[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]] is not an IP that I created. From the position of someone borrowing the IP, I think it’s only natural to show respect to the person who created it, and let that feeling of respect guide us. So when [[Shigeru Miyamoto|Miyamoto-san]], the father of Mario, asks us “could you make a game with only characters from the [[Super Mario (series)|Mario family]]?”, I think it’s only natural for us to give it our best shot. In other words, we are not currently thinking about returning to old NPCs.|Kensuke Tanabe on the development of ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''}}<ref name="first">[https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2016/12/28/afterwords-paper-mario-color-splash.aspx Afterwords – Paper Mario: Color Splash (Game Informer)]</ref>

The '''"Mario Mandate"''' is a colloquial term used by fans to describe what's perceived as a [[Nintendo]]-ordered reduction of creative character designs, original characters and fictional races, and story in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] since the 2010s.<ref name="second">[https://www.gamesradar.com/paper-mario-the-thousand-year-door-remakes-new-toad-could-mean-an-end-to-the-controversial-mario-mandate/ Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door remake's new Toad could mean an end to the controversial "Mario mandate" (GamesRadar)]</ref> Some noticed reductions appear as fan theorized mandates with no confirmation from Nintendo if they ordered them, while other reductions have been officially confirmed by Nintendo developers as mandated restrictions.<ref name="fifth"/><ref name="sixth"/>

==Noticed reductions/replacements==
* In ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', the [[Mushroom Shop Clerk]] is replaced with a generic [[Yellow Toad]]. 
* In the remake ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', the uniforms of the [[Bowser Baddies crew|crew]] of the [[Koopa Cruiser]] and [[Ankoopa]]s are replaced with generic identical [[Koopa Troopa]]s. 
**The [[:image:MLSSBM Koopa Troopa.png|Koopa sprites]] in the remake ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'' differ from the [[:image:MLDT Corporal Paraplonk.png|previous]] [[:image:MLPJ Koopa Troopa.png|sprites]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', and were not reused for convenience.
* In the remake ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the individualized [[Toad (species)|Mushroom People]] in [[Toad Town#Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga|Toad Town Square]] and [[Little Fungitown]] are replaced with generic identical [[Toad (species)|Toads]]. 
**The [[:image:MLSSBM Toad.png|Toad sprites]] in the remake ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'' differ from the [[:image:MLDT Toad.png|previous]] [[:image:MLPJ Toad.png|sprites]] in ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' and ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'', and were not reused for convenience.
* In the remake ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', [[Psycho Kamek]] is replaced with [[Dr. Toadley]].
* In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', all Toads are identical aside from generic red, blue, green, yellow, and purple coloring. 
* In the remake ''[[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|Super Mario RPG]]'', many characters were renamed to more generic names from the [[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars|original game]], notably, Frogfucius being renamed to [[Frog Sage]]. 
==Officially confirmed mandates==
===''Paper Mario'' series===
{{Q|Since [[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]], it’s no longer possible to modify Mario characters or to create original characters that touch on the Mario universe.|Kensuke Tanabe on the development of ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''}}<ref name="third">[https://www.videogameschronicle.com/features/interviews/paper-mario-origami-king/ Paper Mario’s development team lays it all out (Video Games Chronicle)]</ref>

Nintendo developers have officially confirmed mandates in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'']] series. During the development of ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', [[Shigeru Miyamoto]] had asked producer Kensuke Tanabe ''"It's fine without a story, so do we really need one?"'' and ''"As much as possible, complete it with only characters from the [[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]] world"''.<ref name=fourth>[https://www.vg247.com/paper-mario-sticker-star-team-explains-removal-of-rpg-elements Paper Mario: Sticker Star team explains removal of RPG elements (VG247)]</ref> Kensuke Tanabe has since stated that restrictions are enforced by an ''"IP team"'' that is ''"pretty strict"''.<ref name="fifth">[https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2020/08/paper_mario_producer_says_his_team_has_almost_complete_control_over_creative_direction Paper Mario Producer Says His Team Has "Almost Complete Control" Over Creative Direction (Nintendo Life)] “Ever since Paper Mario: Color Splash, we have almost complete control over the creative direction of the game. Mr. Miyamoto checked in on development once or twice, but there were no specific requests to make changes. However, all character designs have to pass a check by our IP team, which is pretty strict.”</ref>
* Original characters and races in ''Paper Mario'' are no longer possible, unless they are outside of the ''Mario'' universe such as sentient inanimate objects like stickers and stationary.<ref name="third"/>
* [[Toad (species)|Toads]] in ''Paper Mario'' can no longer have individual characteristics such as age or gender.<ref name="sixth">[https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2020/07/paper_mario_producer_says_team_is_no_longer_able_to_graphically_represent_individual_characteristics_in_toad_npcs Paper Mario Producer Says Team Is "No Longer Able To Graphically Represent Individual Characteristics" In Toad NPCs (Nintendo Life)]</ref>
* Complicated stories in ''Paper Mario'' are to be avoided.<ref name="seventh">[https://nintendoeverything.com/paper-mario-dev-says-hes-moved-away-from-complicated-stories-and-modifying-mario-characters-for-original-characters/ Paper Mario dev says he’s moved away from complicated stories and modifying Mario characters for original characters (Nintendo Everything)]</ref>

The release of the [[Nintendo Switch]] remake [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'']] appears exempt from these ''Paper Mario'' restrictions by keeping its original character designs, fictional races, and story, however there are no confirmations if only remakes in ''Paper Mario'' are exceptions to the rule.

===''Super Mario Galaxy'' series===
{{Q|“Well, I put a stop to that at the beginning, [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|this time]],” [Miyamoto] said, and for emphasis punched the air with his fist.|[[Shigeru Miyamoto]] on story in the development of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''}}<ref name="eighth">[https://www.wired.com/2009/06/super-mario-galaxy-2/ Miyamoto: Why I Spiked Mario Galaxy 2's Story (Wired)] [https://web.archive.org/web/20140409142922/https://www.wired.com/2009/06/super-mario-galaxy-2/ (archive)] "I told Miyamoto about what Koizumi had said, and he looked slightly taken aback. “He said that?” “Yes.” “Well, I put a stop to that at the beginning, this time,” he said, and for emphasis punched the air with his fist."</ref> 

[[Shigeru Miyamoto]] has expressed ''"I'd like to go with as little story as possible"'' and ''"I've always felt that the Mario games themselves aren't particularly suited to having a very heavy story"'' regarding story in the ''Mario'' franchise.<ref name="eighth"/> In contrast, ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' director Yoshiaki Koizumi stated that he would try to find sneaky ways to add more story to games he worked on without [[Nintendo EAD]] higher ups noticing too much, he also mentioned that these were aspects that Miyamoto wasn't too fond of and occasionally disliked.<ref name="ninth">[https://www.wired.com/2007/12/interview-super/ Interview: Super Mario Galaxy Director On Sneaking Stories Past Miyamoto (Wired)] [https://web.archive.org/web/20140530085716/https://www.wired.com/2007/12/interview-super/ (archive)] “They were always saying, let's not try to push the story forward too much. So I would sort of try to find sneaky ways to get it in without them noticing too much. For example, I always liked the idea of you coming upon another character and hearing little bits of conversation that slowly begin to reveal different parts of the story. And that was the way that I tried to work on Ocarina of Time and Majora's Mask. A lot of the EAD games that do seem to have a lot of story, a lot of that came from my influence. But those are aspects of the games that Miyamoto wasn't nearly as fond of and occasionally didn't like.”</ref> 

In an interview, Miyamoto was taken aback when he learned of what Koizumi had said, and officially stated that he had mandated a stop to it from the beginning of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'''s development.<ref name="eighth"/>
