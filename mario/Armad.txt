The '''Armad''' is an armadillo-like foe from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. Armads make their debut appearance in the level [[Armads on the Roll]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Armads look like the common armadillo, but with orange bodies and shells. Their eyes appear to be conjoined with a black border.
=== Behavior ===
Armads can curl into balls, rolling down slopes in this form.
{{Sectstub}}
== In game ==
Armads are first seen in the level Armads on the Roll, with their last appearance being [[Sunbaked Desert Special Pole Block Allure]].
