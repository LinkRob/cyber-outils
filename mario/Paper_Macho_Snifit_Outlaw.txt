A '''Paper Macho Snifit Outlaw''' is a type of [[Paper Macho Soldier]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. It is encountered in the [[Big Sho' Theater]]. Paper Macho Snifit Outlaw is the only Paper Macho version of a regular Snifit to be encountered.

Together with the Paper Macho Spike Outlaw and the Paper Macho Goomba Outlaw, this enemy tries to stop Mario from advancing to the second floor of the theater. To defeat the enemy, the player must press A as soon as they see "Fire!" The Paper Macho Snifit Outlaw is the strongest of the three enemies on this floor.
