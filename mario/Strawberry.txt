'''Strawberries''' are a type of [[fruit]] found on [[Earth]]. One appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' as a key item, while other strawberries are featured across the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'']] series in minigames.

==History==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Strawberry.png|frame|left]]
In ''Paper Mario'', the Strawberry is an ingredient in the [[Special Strawberry Cake|cake]] that [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] use in an attempt to satisfy [[Gourmet Guy]]'s hunger after the events of Chapter 4. Peach makes the cake after [[Mario]] rescues the [[Star Spirit]] from the [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]. The strawberry is the last item that Peach has to put in the [[cream]], and she adds it after the cream is put on. Adding more than one of these to the cake results in Gourmet Guy spitting it out and Peach having to make the cake again.

===''Mario's Picross''===
In ''[[Mario's Picross]]'', strawberries appear in the Kinoko Course puzzle.

===''Mario Party'' series===
====''Mario Party 2''====
[[File:Strawberry Honeycomb Havoc MP2.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Mario&WarioMakingACake.png|thumb]]
In ''[[Mario Party 2]]'', strawberries appear on the tree in the minigame [[Honeycomb Havoc]], and as the topper for the cake in the minigame [[Cake Factory]].

====''Mario Party 4''====
In ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', strawberries appear in the Bowser [[minigame]] [[Fruits of Doom]], as one of the potential fruits on [[Bowser]]'s list.

====''Mario Party: The Top 100'' / ''Mario Party Superstars''====
In ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', strawberries return in Cake Factory.
{{br|left}}

===''Nintendo Land''===
[[File:NL Prize Strawberry.png|100px|thumb|The strawberry prize]]
Strawberries are one of the types of fruit which appear in the [[Yoshi's Fruit Cart]] attraction in ''[[Nintendo Land]]''. A strawberry also appears as an unlockable [[prize]] at the Nintendo Land Plaza.

===''Princess Peach: Showtime!''===
''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]'' shows there are strawberries in the patisserie stage.

===''Mario Kart World''===
''[[Mario Kart World]]'' shows that one of the pathways to [[Sky High Sundae]] has a tunnel made of a strawberry shortcake. Some of the [[Dash Food]]s prove to be slices of strawberry shortcake for most drivers to obtain pro-racer outfits.

===''Donkey Kong Bananza''===
In ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'', large, cubic strawberries appear in the [[Resort Layer]], alongside pears and [[orange]]s. They are the most abundant of the smaller fruits, with a total of fifteen in the layer. [[Donkey Kong]] can either tear off or [[punch]] their flesh to recover health, or hit a green button on top with a [[Dive Punch]] to cause the entire fruit to explode and reveal whatever is underneath; this includes a [[Treasure Chest]], some [[crate]]s and [[barrel]]s, a [[Banandium Gem]], a [[fossil]], a Shifty Smash challenge, two fragments of a [[Fragmentone]], a [[Hiddentone]], and a [[Bopper Switch]] that can be punched to raise some seafloor ruins for Banandium Gem #29 — Rising Seafloor Ruins. While most of the strawberries are hollow, the strawberry on the island above Ice-Serve Switcheroo is not, and also lacks a Dive Punch button.

==Flavor text==
*'''''Paper Mario'' in-game description:''' ''Bright red strawberries. They look fresh and juicy.''
