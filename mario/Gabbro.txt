'''Gabbro''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
Gabbro is a [[Cragnon]] that was one of many Cragnons captured and bought to [[Floro Caverns]]. However, unlike the majority of them, Gabbro did not get [[Floro Sprout|brainwashed]]. Gabbro is found in a room with one of the [[Pixl]], [[Dottie]]. Gabbro became good friends with Dottie and was torn at the fact that Dottie had to leave due to her duty to help the heroes. Fortunately, mentioning [[Flint Cragley]] gives Gabbro the push to leave since he is a huge fan of Flint. Dottie uses her powers to shrink Gabbro and Gabbro escapes through a small door.
