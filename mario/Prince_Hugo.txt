'''Prince Hugo''' is a one-time character who has a large role in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Sneaky Lying Cheating Giant Ninja Koopas]]." He is a giant [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who has a pet parrot named [[Royal Parrot]], who is also large in size like him. He is the ruler of [[Giant Land]], and seeks [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Toad (DiC cartoons)|Toad]]'s assistance in dealing with the [[Koopa Troop (DiC Cartoons)|Koopa Troop]]'s attack on Giant Land.
