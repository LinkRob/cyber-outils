}}'''Fred''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a light-blue [[Bob-omb]] that lives at [[Fahr Outpost]].

==History==
Fred lives in Fahr Outpost and he plays his role when he, [[Rob (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|Rob]], and [[General White]] all assist [[Mario]] in getting to [[The Moon]]. [[Goombella]], when she [[Tattle]]s Fred mentions she doesn't find him "freaky" despite finding all Freds as "freaky."

==Tattle information==
* "''That's Fred. He's not very freaky for a Fred, though....What? Not all Freds are freaky? News to me.''"
