'''Mixed Signals''' is one of [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player has to tilt the [[Wii Remote]] in directions to adjust the TV antenna until it gets the best reception. If done successfully, the player wins, but if not completed before the [[time limit|timer]] runs out, they lose. 

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level increases how much the reception has to be adjusted. In addition, a different program plays during each difficulty setting, although this does not affect the gameplay.

*'''Level 1:''' The antenna takes little adjustment. A newscast program is being watched, and winning the microgame results in the reporter talking faster.
*'''Level 2:''' The antenna requires more adjustment. The show is of a bird on a tree, and winning the microgame results in the bird flying out of the television.
*'''Level 3:''' The antenna requires a lot of adjustment. A choir appears on the television, and winning the microgame results in the TV opening up and one of the members jumping out.
