'''Water Elemental Ghosts''' are little ghostly balls of water with yellow eyes, that clearly represent the water element. These little ghosts make their appearances in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|Luigi's Mansion]]''. They usually can be found in sinks, or foutains or any wet place. They can be sucked up by the [[Poltergust 3000]], and used for different quests around the mansion. Sucking these up, you can defeat other ghosts in the mansion, like the Tempor Terror and Spark.

==Trivia==
*Water Elemental Ghosts slightly resemble [[Lava Bouncer]]s from the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games.
