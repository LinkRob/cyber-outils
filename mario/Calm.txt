'''Calm''' is one of the four [[vibe]]s in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. Unlike other vibes, it does not grant Peach a special ability but gradually restores Peach's Health Points.

== Description ==
If Princess Peach took several hits from enemies or harmful objects, she can use the Calm vibe to restore her HP. Like other vibes, it will use her [[Vibe Gauge]].
