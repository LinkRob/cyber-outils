==Appearance==
Chap is a young [[Flip-Flopian]] that is green in color.

==History==
Chap can be found on the second floor of [[Flipside]]. Chap often states how peaceful Flipside is. He even goes on to say how he wished something would happen. However, after the events of the game, Chap comments how Flipside is peaceful again but appears to come to terms with that.

==Trivia==
