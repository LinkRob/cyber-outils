A '''Far-Away High Security Prison Facility''' is a location in the mid-credits scene of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', where Fox McCloud sent Bowser and his son, Junior to prison far away, before heading back to the Lylat System.

==Trivia==
*The prison facility looks like the shelf of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] cartridges.
*It might be in the same place as the penguins live or another galaxy.
