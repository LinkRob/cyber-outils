== Biography ==
Diddy Kong is one of the many [[Kong]]s in the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]]. He is present in the match between [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]], sitting between [[Dixie Kong (Illumination)|Dixie]] and [[Chunky Kong (Illumination)|Chunky]]. He is the only Kong left cheering in the crowd once [[Cranky Kong (Illumination)|King Cranky]] orders everyone to simmer down. Cranky then angrily says "That means you, Diddy Kong!". Diddy sheepishly apologizes and stops, with Dixie giving him a disdainful look.

He is shown to play the bongos.
