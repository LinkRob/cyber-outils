The '''Monettes''' are a cheer-leading team for the football game in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. Captained by [[Mona]], it is a group of four blond girls with beedy eyes. They are shown competing against Team Dinosaur. The Team was originally called the Diamond City Roughs.
