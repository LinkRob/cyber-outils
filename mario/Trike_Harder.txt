{{Nihongo|'''Trike Harder'''|３輪車レース|Sanrinsha rēsu|Tricycle Race}} is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The name is a pun of "try harder".

== Opening ==
The camera pans from a high angle, zooming to a long shot then panning across the players on trikes.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to be the first player to reach the finish line. The player must move the controller in a circular motion to pedal the tricycle. The minigame ends when the third player passes through the finish line.

== Ending ==
The camera aims at the winner who does her/his victory pose. The players that passed the finish line (except 4th place) looks at the victor.
