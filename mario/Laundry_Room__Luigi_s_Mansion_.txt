The '''Laundry Room''' is one of the rooms featured in [[Area Two]] of ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]]. Completing the room is optional, as the door to the [[Butler's Room]] is always unlocked, but [[Luigi]] is required to collect [[Mario's Cap|Mario's Hat]] in the washing machine to present to [[Madame Clairvoya]].
