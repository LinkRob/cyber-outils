'''World D-3''' is the third level of [[World D]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]].''

==Layout==
The player begins next to a bunch of six [[Bill Blaster|Turtle Cannon]]s, and in the third slot between them is a [[Brick Block|brick]] with a power-up. After a pipe with a [[Piranha Plant]], there are two [[Hammer Bro]]thers. that will accuse at the player. Before the next Turtle Cannon, there is a row of [[Hidden Block]]s that can be old as cover against the next bunch of Hammer Brothers. After another bunch of six Turtle Cannons is a [[Springboard]] that the player can old to reach the top of a pillar with a Red [[Koopa Troopa]] on it, which can be used to hit the Hammer Brother below. Next is another Piranha Plant, three more Turtle Cannons, two Hammer Brothers, and a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] before Turtle Cannons in a stair generation that directs to the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].
