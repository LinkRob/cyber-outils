'''Penguin Mario''' is a form of [[Mario]] wearing a [[Penguin Suit]]. It first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' and reappears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and its variations ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

The Penguin Suit is helpful for walking on slippery ice, sliding (which allows the player to slide into nearly anything and destroy it; this is useful when wanting to get into secret areas), faster swimming (similarly to the [[Frog Suit]]), and the player can throw [[Ice Ball]]s, like [[Ice Mario]].

Penguin Luigi is a playable driver in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]].''
