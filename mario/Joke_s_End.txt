'''Joke's End''' is a location featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and [[Reissue|its]] [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]]. It is a frozen palace where [[Jojora]] and [[Jojora's friends|her friends]] live. It is also the sixth world of the [[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser|''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'']] campaign.

==History==
According to Jojora, Joke's End was once guarded and owned by her friends' cousin's ancestor who owned it originally long ago.<ref>[[AlphaDream]] (November 17, 2003) ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. [[Game Boy Advance]] "[[Jojora]]: Eh? Me? I'm Jojora. I'm the buddy of the cousin of the descendant of the spirit who once guarded this palace."</ref> What happened to them now is unknown.

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ===
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] arrive in Joke's End, as they make it to the entrance at the palace gates, they are stopped by [[Jojora]], a mysterious fairy-like yet bossy girl who guards the area. She tells them to meet at the gates, although she does this by toying against the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] by traps, but later gets humiliated when they clear her challenge despite her being the one who literally told them to do it. After the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] make their way through the entire palace, Jojora suddenly invites them for a tea party in her room. Telling them that she admires their perseverance and soon asks which four of [[Jojora's friends|her "friends"]] they would like to meet for tea. After the brothers select one, the chosen friend falls down from the ceiling and fights alongside Jojora against the brothers. As the Mario Bros. defeat Jojora and her friends, the friend melts away, causing Jojora to flee away crying, return back to call the bros "jerks" loudly before leaving again. 

As the Mario Bros. proceed, they encounter [[Fawful]], who tells them to give him the [[Beanstar]] for [[Princess Peach]]. Luigi goes in and gives a [[Fake Beanstar]], but Fawful recognizes this as a trick, destroys the Fake Beanstar and takes out Luigi, taking the real one from his [[suitcase]]. As Luigi unlocks the door to let Mario in, he tells him the trick failed, making Luigi feel upset. Mario soon finds a Peach's dress from a suicase creates a backup plan: Luigi disguise himself as Princess Peach and pretend to act like her. Mario heads to the final top area of the palace, where he follows Fawful and encounters [[Bowletta]] holding a crying, Princess Peach. Mario demands them to give the princess back, only to refuse. Suddenly, "Princess Peach" (under Luigi's disguise) comes in and fools Bowletta that the princess they captured was an impostor. Frighthered, Bowletta drops Peach and takes the impostor "Peach" away. Mario jumps out from electrocution as Peach gets up and gives Mario a kiss as they leave. 

=== ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'' ===
[[Captain Goomba (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Goomba]] and [[Elite Captain Quartet|his minions]] arrive to Joke's End at near the palace entrance, they find the [[Koopa Cruiser]] in ruins, so they continue to defeat [[Koopa Troop|brainwashed minions]] to gather repair tools, unaware they are being spied on by [[Roy Koopa|Roy]]. After having enough allies, Roy launches a surprise attack only to get defeated as Roy comes to his senses and later joins the squad. The minions soon stop a bomb the [[Elite Trio]] had placed around the cruiser. With the cruiser fully repaired, the squad is able to launch as they get to [[Bowser's Castle (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Bowser's castle]].

== Description ==
Joke's End is an icy island reached by Mario late in his adventure by using Luigi as a surfboard to traverse the large north-eastern ocean. Joke's End contains an eerie blue tower where Mario and Luigi must travel through multiple levels of complex traps and puzzles, and increasingly challenging (and surreal) enemies, while being coaxed on by a mysterious floating woman named [[Jojora]] who appears to be a magician of some kind.

==Trivia==
*As Jojora and her friends are so far, the only remaining inhabitants of Joke's End, it is unknown if other ppl from the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] live here. As it has been implied that there are or have been more people like Jojora and her friends living in Joke's End despite not showing them in the game. Although at least two residents: a [[beanish]] resident and a [[presenter]], seemed to most likely living in this area.
*The name of this area actually will mark the end of the theming for the locations of the Beanbean Kingdom. As every other major area is named after a form of laugh, Joke's End is the only one that isn't. This is fitting considering Joke's End is the last major location explored in the game (not counting [[Bowser's Castle]] as it was only flown and not actually part of the Beanbean Kingdom).
