An '''Imomushi''' is a green caterpillar enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land 4]]'', exclusively in [[Wildflower Fields]]. Imomushi cannot damage [[Wario]], and they merely just inch around slowly. Like most other enemies, Wario can pick up an Imomushi if it is stunned and then throw it. Imomushi can be defeated from any of Wario's attacks.

In S-Hard mode, Imomushi do not appear, likely owing to their harmless nature, and either red [[Spear-Mask]]s or [[Totsumen]] appear in their place.
