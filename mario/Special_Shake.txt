The '''Special Shake''' is an item and a [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
A Special Shake can be created by [[Tayce T.]] using a numerous amount of combinations which mainly involve using [[Melon]]s and [[Jammin' Jelly|Jammin' Jellies]] as the first ingredients. Although, it's simple to just cook a Melon that can be found on [[Lavalava Island]] as the Jammin' Jellies are very rare items in the game. Using a Special Shake restores 20 [[Flower Point|FP]].
