The '''Lunar Stone''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Lunar Stone can be found in a room in [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]] being guarded by [[Stone Chomp]]s. It then must be used with the [[Diamond Stone]] and the [[Pyramid Stone]] to access [[Tutankoopa]]'s room.
