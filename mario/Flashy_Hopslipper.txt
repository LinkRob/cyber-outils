'''Flashy Hopslipper''' is a type of [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Description==
With a Flashy Hopslipper, [[Mario]] can stomp on a single enemy many times for much stronger damage. Flashy Hopslipper stickers are similar in appearance to Shiny Hopslipper stickers except for the diagonal rainbow background. They only take up one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].

==In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''==
Flashy Hopslipper stickers are very rare, and can be only found by upgrading a [[Shiny Hopslipper]] sticker in certain levels. Flashy Hopslipper stickers are very helpful for boss fights especially like [[Petey Piranha]] when their defenses are weakened.
