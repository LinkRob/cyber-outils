A '''Coco Pop''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can be created by baking together a [[Cake Mix]] and a [[Coconut]]. Cake Mixes can only be found in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] while Coconuts must be harvested in a lone tree on [[Lavalava Island]] (in [[Yoshi's Village]]).

==Effects==
The Coco Pop can restore only 3 [[Heart Point|HP]] but 15 [[Flower Point|FP]] as well. The Coco Pop can be useful mainly for restoring FP. However, considering [[Mario]] can only obtain Coconuts on [[Lavalava Island]] which is during Chapter 5, the Coco Pop's usage may not be as effective during that stage of the game.
