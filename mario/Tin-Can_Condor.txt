The '''Tin Can Condor''' is the first major boss in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''. He makes his home in Big Paper Peak. His boss stage is named [[The Tin-Can Condor]], after him. Defeating him nets the blue [[Dream Gem]].

== Profile==
===Physical description ===
The Tin Can Condor has a tin can for a body and a balloon for a head. He takes his design from a Native American. His clothing includes a war-bonnet and quillwork earrings. [[Kamek]] summons him by blowing up a balloon and placing craft materials on a tin can.

=== Powers and abilities===
The Tin Can Condor starts off the battle by laying blue, spiky eggs that roll towards Yoshi. Yoshi must grab a magnet and toss it at his nemesis as he flies over head. Once he gets hit with the magnet, the Tin Can Condor falls over. This will cause his bonnet to fall off, revealing a white bandage. Yoshi can damage him with a ground pound. This must be done three times.

The Tin Can Condor can also pound the ground and fly with paper airplanes. There are also yellow eggs with bounce and red eggs which roll faster.

==Biography==
The Tin Can Condor attacks the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] after being summoned by Kamek. However, he is soon defeated, and Yoshi gains the blue Dream Gem.
