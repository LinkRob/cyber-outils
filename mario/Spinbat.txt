'''Spinbats''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Spinbats hang from the ceiling. When [[Wario]] gets nearby, the Spinbat drops to the ground in a spinning motion.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Spinbats have a threat rating of two stars. They are encountered in cave and haunted mansion [[level]]s, and Spinbats are first encountered in [[Savannah Valley]]. If Wario is beneath a Spinbat, he takes damage, but if he dodges a Spinbat, it gets stuck into the ground. If Wario leaves a Spinbat alone, it flies back to its roost. A Spinbat can be defeated from any attack.
