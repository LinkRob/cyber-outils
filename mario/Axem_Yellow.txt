'''Axem Yellow''' is a member of the [[Smithy Gang]]'s [[Axem Rangers]], a major antagonist and boss in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its upcoming [[Super Mario RPG|2023 remake]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Axem Yellow, along with the other Axem Rangers, are first seen after ambushing [[Mario]] in [[Barrel Volcano]] stealing his [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]] in the process. They all race to the top and board on their battleship, the [[Blade]], in attempt to get away from Mario and his friends. However, Mario and company managed to catch up to the Axem Rangers and eventually defeated them in battle.

===Battle===
Axem Yellow is the muscle of the Axem Rangers and, thus, has the highest attack and defense of the group. Axem Yellow mainly attacks with physical attacks, but will occasionally hurl a toxic bubble at one of Mario's parties. He also has one special move, [[Body Slam (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Body Slam]], that can deal a lot of damage to a single character.

====Strategy====
Axem Yellow is weak against [[Jump]] attacks, meaning Mario's Jump techniques will be effective against him. Axem Yellow is also one of the least threatening members of the Axem Rangers as most of his attacks can be easily blocked. Thus, he should be one of the last members of the Axem Rangers that should be targeted (with [[Axem Red]] being the very last one).
