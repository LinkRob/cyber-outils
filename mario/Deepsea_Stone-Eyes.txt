'''Deepsea Stone-Eyes''' is the fifth level of [[Soda Jungle]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
==Layout==
The level starts in a jungle near a [[? Block]] and a [[Stone-Eye]] with [[coin]]s above it. A [[Warp Pipe]] is found nearby, and the coins move to form a line leading to it. The pipe leads underwater to an area with some Stone-Eyes and [[Fish Bone]]s. A group of Fish Bones is found around a ? Block containing either a [[Super Mushroom]] or an [[Ice Flower]] depending on the player's current form. Two pairs of moving Stone-Eyes colliding with each other are found, along with another group of Fish Bones. More colliding Stone-Eyes are found, along with Fish Bones. After the last pair of Stone-Eyes, a Warp Pipe is found. Entering the pipe leads the player back outside, where they find the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': After going underwear, pass through the first moving statue and get the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The coin is on the left by the exit pipe.
*'''Star Coin 3''': After grabbing the second Star Coin, move away from the statue quickly and go to a hidden alcove revealing a hidden pipe. Go in it and get the coin over the giant figure of Luigi.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Under the third Star Coin shows a giant standard Luigi from [[Super Mario Bros.|''Super Mario Bros.'']] dying.

==Secret exit==
Before thinking about going into the first pipe, ground-pound the statue on the wall revealing a secret path to the secret exit. The path leads out of the haunted area if found or else it'll lead a path back to the last two levels of the area.
