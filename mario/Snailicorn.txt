'''Snailicorns'''<ref>Loe, Casey. ''New Super Mario Bros.'' Player's Guide. Page 12.</ref> are enemies in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Snailicorns are large snail-like creatures with two small green feet and a horn on their head.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros.''==
[[File:NSMB Snailicorn.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Snailicorn.png|thumb|A Snailicorn in World 5-B]]
They appear in only two ice [[level]]s, [[World 5-3  (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5-3]] and [[World 5-B (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5-B]]. Snailicorns walk from side to side on the [[platform]] they are found on and turn around to avoid edges if necessary, similarly to [[Red Koopa Troopa]]s. If a Snailicorn sees [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], it charges in a straight line and attempts to damage him with its horn. They can be very dangerous, for neither Mario or Luigi can [[jump]] on them to defeat them, and [[fireball]]s are ineffective. They can also be annoying, as they are quite large in both height and girth, and are usually found near cliff edges, and under low ceilings, so there's not much leg room for the plumbers. 

[[Image:Snailicorn.jpg|thumb|left|Mario stomping on a Snailicorn.]]
Snailicorns are not, however, indefeasible, as jumping on them sends them back a few feet, and this tactic can be used to send them off of a cliff nearby. Mario or Luigi can [[stomp]] a Snailicorn to push it back, possibly into gaps within platforms or [[pit|bottomless pits]], and [[Ground Pound]]s can push Snailicorns a greater distance. Jumping on them at the last minute is a quick and easy strategy. Snailcorns can be defeated with the effects of a [[Blue Shell]], a [[Mega Mushroom]], or a [[Starman]], tossing a [[Shell]] at one can also defeat a Snailicorn.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Their name is a portmanteau of "snail" and "unicorn."

{{Nomenclature/Snailicorn}}

==Trivia==
*The game's Japanese internal filename for Snailicorn is a romanization of the Japanese name for [[Bully]], suggesting that it was originally designed as one before being changed to a horned snail. This explains why the two species act in a similar manner. Given that Snailicorn has one horn and only appears in ice levels, it may have specifically been modeled after [[Chill Bully]].
*The Snailicorn's attack pattern is reminiscent of the [[Cleft]] species in the ''[[Paper Mario]]'' series.
