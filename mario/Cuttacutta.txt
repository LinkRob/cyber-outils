'''Cuttacuttas''' are an enemy in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Cuttacuttas are mechanical, red [[crab]] toys.

===Behavior===
Cuttacuttas roll from left to right and can take [[Lift|elevator]]s. If a Cuttacutta sees a [[Mini]], it extends one of its claws five times to try and break the Mini, then moves in the opposite direction.

==In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''==
Cuttacuttas appear only a few stages of [[Tropical Island]], including [[Room 2-5 (Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis)|Room 2-5]], [[Room 2-8]], and [[Room 2-9]]. Cuttacuttas can be permanently defeated from either a [[Hammer]] or a [[Fire Flower]]. In the [[Construction Zone]], Cuttacuttas can be used in the Tropical Island construction kit, [[Special Kit 1]], [[Special Kit 2]], and [[Special Kit 3]].
