The '''Mega Shirt''' is one of the armor items exclusively used by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from a shop in [[Moleville]] for 22 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Mega Shirt increases Mario's [[Defense]] by 18 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 10. Unlike other armor items, the Mega Shirt's Magic Defense rating is not exactly half of its Defense rating.
