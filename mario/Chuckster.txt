'''Chucksters''' are a variant of [[Pianta]] exclusively appearing in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Personality traits===
Their main role is to throw [[Mario]] on to certain areas that he cannot otherwise reach on his own.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Chucksters can be found in a few different areas on [[Isle Delfino]]. In the hub area, [[Delfino Plaza]], one Chuckster can be found on a roof close to the [[Cannon (Super Mario Sunshine)|cannon]] leading to [[Pinna Park]]. If Mario pays the Chuckster a single coin, he will chuck Mario over to a window that leads to a [[Shine Sprite]].

Several Chucksters can be found during [[Pianta Village]]'s fifth [[mission|episode]], [[Secret of the Village Underside]]. Mario must talk to some specific Chucksters to have them throw him to a certain spot. Mario must face an angle to be thrown in the direction he wishes to travel in.
