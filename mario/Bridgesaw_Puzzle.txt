'''Bridgesaw Puzzle''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective for each character is to use puzzle pieces to create a bridge across a small gap containing [[Poisoned Water]].
{{sectstub}}

==Controls==
*[[Stylus]]: Tap to select, then slide to the right

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on "jigsaw puzzle," but with "bridge" as the prefix of the first word because the minigame involves creating a bridge.
