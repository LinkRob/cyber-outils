The '''Stormstar Sea''' is one of the five seas visited in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is the last sea to be explored properly, with its area engulfing the centre of [[Concordia]] as well as a region between the [[Lushgreen Sea|Lushgreen]] and [[Gulchrock Sea|Gulchrock Seas]].

== Major islands ==
Two major islands native to this sea can be found.

* [[Conductor Island]]: This island has a forest where the [[Great Conductor]] makes his home. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] first wakes up here after his and [[Luigi]]'s arrival in [[Concordia]], and they later come here after defeating [[Glohm Bowser]] and escaping [[Fortress Zokket]] when [[Zokket]] attacks with a [[Glohm]] wave.
* [[Jellyfish Island]]: This island has a dance hall that [[Groovemaster Dyode]] owns.

== Islets ==
Two islets can be found in this area:

* Shrimpee Islet
* Mykroh Islet

== Other Locations ==

* [[Fortress Zokket]]: Not a true island, but the description calls it one. [[Zokket]] uses this flying castle as his base of operations. This area is accessed after the Bros. upgrade the engine to [[Shipshape Island]]. After Zokket's death, the fortress is replaced by the [[Soli-Tree]].
* The [[Soli-Tree]]: [[Reclusa]]'s fortress, which it created to mock the [[Uni-Tree]]. Only visited during the [[Reclusa World]] chapter. After Reclusa meets its demise, the Soli-Tree is destroyed, and the player can park Shipshape Island at the centre of the ocean to restore [[Concordia]].
