'''Master Crash''' is a character in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a [[Bob-omb]], and fights in the minor league at the [[Glitz Pit]] with his team, [[Bob-omb Squad]]. He is one of the first to greet [[Mario]], and stays in the minors throughout the chapter. Occasionally, Mario can see him fighting in the arena against another minor league fighter.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Master Crash has the same appearance as a regular Bob-omb in the game.

===Personality traits ===
Master Crash has a speech impediment in which he says words such as "BA-BOOM" at the end of his sentences, much like the Bob-ombs of [[Fahr Outpost]]. Unlike [[Cleftor]], Master Crash is friendly to Mario as soon he entered the minor league.
