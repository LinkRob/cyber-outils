'''World 3-2''' is a level from [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.   

==Overview==
This snow-covered level marks the first appearance of [[Bullet Bill]]s and [[Bill Blaster]]s in the game and introduces a new variant of [[Hammer Bro]], the [[Ice Bro]]. Its completion unlocks [[World 3-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3-3]], {{world-link|3|ghost|World 3-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)}}, {{world-link|3|tower|World 3-Tower (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)}}, and an [[Enemy Course]].
==Walkthrough==
The stage starts with snow-covered hills patrolled by [[Goomba]]s and three Bill Blasters, along with the first [[Star Coin]]. The next area has an Ice Bro, more Bill Blasters and a [[Block]] containing a [[Starman]]. Located nearby are platforms with coins and a [[Yoshi Egg]]. The players then pass through an area infested with Bill Blasters and moving platforms, after some Brick Blocks, followed by the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].

*[[Ice Bro]]s - 2
*Red [[Koopa Troopa]]s - 3
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is floating high above a platform with a red [[Koopa Troopa]]. Mario and co. must bound off the Koopa Troopa to reach the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Not far after the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], there are four Bill Blasters surrounding a green [[Warp Pipe]]. This pipe leads to an area with three more Bill Blasters and the second Star Coin which is floating high-up in the air. Mario and co. can reach it by bounding off the Bullet Bills that are launched from the Bill Blasters.
*'''Star Coin 3''': After exiting the area with the second Star Coin, Mario and co. should jump up to the nearby midair platform with the red Koopa Troopa, then to the moving platform with the [[Ice Bro]] near another midair platform, and finally to the midair platform above which the third Star Coin rests.
