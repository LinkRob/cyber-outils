'''Flipstings''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Flipstings are yellow scorpion-like creatures with light blue eyes. They also have a red variant.

===Behavior===
Like scorpions in general, Flipstings can use their stinger as a means of attack. Red Flipstings can cling to a ceiling and pierce it with its tail to strike the floor above it.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Flipstings have a threat rating of two stars. Because of their stinger tail, [[Wario]] cannot jump on a Flipsting or even perform a [[Dash Attack]] on it from the front. By performing an [[Earthshake Punch]], Wario can put these enemies into their shells, making them safe to attack or throw at other nearby enemies.
