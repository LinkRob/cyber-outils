'''Toadette Kart''' is an unlockable light class [[kart]] which appears in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is the signature [[kart]] of [[Toadette]], and looks almost exactly like the [[Toad Kart]] used by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] except it is pink. The Toadette Kart has maxed out [[Acceleration]] but with the worst speed.

Toadette Kart can be unlocked by winning first place on [[Mirror Mode]] of [[Mushroom Cup]]. It has the same stats as [[Baby Mario]]'s [[Goo-Goo Buggy]] and [[Paratroopa]]'s [[Para Wing]].

==Stats==
*Class: Light
*Speed: 1/5
*Acceleration: 5/5
*Weight: 2/5
