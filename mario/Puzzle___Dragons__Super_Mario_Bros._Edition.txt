'''''Puzzle & Dragons Z + Super Mario Bros. Edition''''' is a puzzle game for the [[Nintendo 3DS]] released in 2015. It is a crossover between the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'']] series and the ''Puzzle & Dragons'' series by [[wikipedia:GungHo Online Entertainment|GungHo Online Entertainment]]. The game follows similar mechanics of regular ''Puzzle & Dragons Z'' games, including an overworld and story, only this time with additional characters from the ''Mario'' series. The game was developed by GungHo Online Entertainment and published by [[Nintendo]].

== Story ==
One day in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], [[Orbs]] suddenly started to appear around [[Princess Peach|Peach's]] [[Peach's Castle (Super Mario Odyssey)|Castle]]. Mario was invited to the castle in order to observe the Orbs. However, when he arrives, Peach has already been captured by [[Bowser]] and his minions. A nearby [[Toad (character)|Toad]] gives Mario a letter from the Koopa King himself, daring the plumber to follow him to his own castle. The Toad also explains that [[Kamek]] is abusing the power of the Orbs, causing the entire Kingdom to overflow with them. Then he and Mario decide that using the Orbs' power themselves was the sure way they could save Princess Peach.

== Gameplay ==
The game plays similarly to other games in the ''Puzzles and Dragons'' franchise, players have a 6x5 board of different orbs that they can drag around, the types being {{Color|Fire|red}}, {{Color|Water|blue}}, {{Color|Wood|green}}, {{Color|Light|yellow}}, {{Color|Dark|purple}}, and Heart. The goal is to match 3 or more orbs to deal damage or heal with Heart orbs, and to clear as many in one turn. The more combos and orbs the player clear, the more damage they will deal and/or the more HP they heal. Matching 5 orbs in one combo will turn that attributed attack into a Mass Attack, which will hit all enemies that are currently alive. 

If the player manage to clear 7 combos, they will be rewarded with a Super Orb, which deals 3x as much damage that multiplies with itself. Successfully clearing 10 combos will not only give out another Super Orb, but will also cause any enemies defeated within this turn to drop their Rare Items if they have any. Players can move as many times as they want within a timeframe, which starts at 5 seconds but can be altered by certain skills and awakenings. Each stage has a number of battles they must clear, each battle having different sets of enemies that must be defeated in order to move on, and the types of orb vary from stage to stage.

Whenever players defeat an enemy, at the end of the encounter the enemy will give them EXP and Coins, and any items they may have dropped. At the end of each stage is a boss fight against more powerful foes, beating the boss will clear the stage, the score will be tallied up for your Skill Points (average combo and Rare Item count), Speed Bonus (how fast the player cleared the stage), and continues used. The next stage will be unlocked if it was the player's first clear of the stage. Some stages have secret exits, these requires the creation of orbs that normally don't show up in the stage, requiring the use of skills to get to these levels.

===Bosses===
Major bosses
The following bosses are unique in that they are one of a kind, and will never join the player under normal conditions. When these bosses do join the player, the player is not able to part ways or depower any of them, and they stop appearing as enemies as well.

[[Larry Koopa]] (World 1-Castle and World 1-Airship Boss)
Joins the player as an ally after ★World 1-Airship is completed.
[[Lemmy Koopa]] (World 2-Castle and World 2-Airship Boss)
Joins the player as an ally after ★World 2-Airship is completed.
[[Morton Koopa Jr.]] (World 3-Castle and World 3-Airship Boss)
Joins the player as an ally after ★World 3-Airship is completed.
[[Wendy O. Koopa]] (World 4-Castle and World 4-Airship Boss)
Joins the player as an ally after ★World 4-Airship is completed.
[[Iggy Koopa]] (World 5-Castle and World 5-Airship Boss)
Joins the player as an ally after ★World 5-Airship is completed.
[[Roy Koopa]] (World 6-Castle and World 6-Airship Boss)
Joins the player as an ally after ★World 6-Airship is completed.

Generic Bosses
The following generic enemies "only" appear as bosses. Unlike the major bosses, these bosses have a very small chance to join the player when defeated, and they will always appear as enemies, regardless of whether or not they are in the player's party.

Boom Boom (World 1-Tower, World 2-Tower, World 3-Tower, World 4-Tower, World 5-Tower, World 6-Tower 1, World 7-Tower 1, and World 8-Tower 1 Boss)
After Bowser Jr. joins the player, Boom Boom replaces him as the boss of World 6-Tower 2, World 7-Tower 2, and World 8-Tower 3.
After Bowser joins the player, Boom Boom replaces him as the boss of World 8-Bowser's Castle.
After Dry Bowser joins the player, Boom Boom replaces him as the boss of ★World 8-Bowser's Castle.
Blooper Nanny & Babies (World 4-5 Boss)
Blooper & Cheep Cheep (World 4-7 Boss)
Boo Trio (World 5-Ghost House, World 7-Ghost House, and World 8-Ghost House 1)
Blooper & Cheep Cheeps (World 5-5 and World 8-6 Boss)
Wiggler & Flame Chomp (World 5-6 and World 8-4 Boss)
After Larry Koopa joins the player, Wiggler & Flame Chomp replaces him as the boss of World 1-Castle.
Petey Piranha (World 5-7 and World 8-3 Boss)
After Lemmy Koopa joins the player, Petey Piranha replaces him as the boss of World 2-Castle.
Flutter & Flame Chomp (World 5-8 and World 8-5 Boss)
After Iggy Koopa joins the player, Flutter & Flame Chomp replaces him as the boss of World 5-Castle.
Spiny Egg & Lakitu (World 6-3 and World 7-10 Boss)
After Morton Koopa Jr. joins the player, Spiny Egg & Lakitu replaces him as the boss of World 3-Castle.
Bullet Bill & Goomba (World 6-4 Boss)
After Morton Koopa Jr. joins the player, Bullet Bill & Goomba replaces him as the boss of World 3-Airship.
