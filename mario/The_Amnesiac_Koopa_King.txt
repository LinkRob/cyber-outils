'''The Amnesiac Koopa King''' is the ninth level of [[Hoohoo Mountain]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 7.

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains 2 [[Tanoomba]]s, 5 [[Hammer Bro]]s, and 1 [[Scaratroopea]]. The enemy captain is a Hammer Bro.

The 2nd battle contains  2 [[Troopea]], 2 Scaratroopeas, and 4 Hammer Bros. The enemy captain is a Scaratroopea.

The final battle contains  2 Troopeas, 2 Scaratroopeas, 3 Hammer Bros, and 1 Tanoomba. The enemy captain is the Tanoomba.
