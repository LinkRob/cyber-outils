'''Bub-ulbs''' are a recurring, plant-based species in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
They are a species of flowers that are green in color with a flower stemming form their heads. They each have petals with different ones varying in colors.

=== Habitat ===
Bub-ulbs are found in [[Flower Fields]].

==History==
===''Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', there are four Bub-ulbs, each having a different flower petal. When [[Mario]] encounters a Bub-ulb and talks to it, it gives him him a [[Magical Seed]]. Mario must collect all four of these seeds in order to gain access to the [[Flower Gate]], the way into Flower Fields.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Bub-ulbs can be found in [[Petalburg]]. One Bub-ulb, by the name of [[Bub-ulber]], will give Mario a [[Dried Bouquet]] in exchange for a [[Hot Dog]] after completing his [[Trouble Center|trouble]].
