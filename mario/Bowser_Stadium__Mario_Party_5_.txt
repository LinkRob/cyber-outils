'''Bowser Stadium''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
Bowser is an arena which resembles [[Bowser's Castle]]. It is filled with [[lava]] around the edges and in the center, acting as a hazard for the characters.

==In ''Mario Party 5''==
Bowser Stadium is an arena in [[Super Duel Mode]]. If a character touches the lava, they lose one [[heart]], unless the vehicle is equipped with either the [[Lakitu Cloud]] or the [[E. Gadd Jet]] tires.
