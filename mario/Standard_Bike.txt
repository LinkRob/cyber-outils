The '''Standard Bike''' is a bike that first appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' and later in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. In ''Mario Kart Wii'', it appeared in three different versions, the [[Standard Bike S]], for the lightweight characters, the [[Standard Bike M]], for the middleweight characters, and the [[Standard Bike L]], for the heavyweight characters. In ''Mario Kart 8'' and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', it appeared as a Bike part, unlocked from the start. It has average stats in ''Mario Kart Wii'', and it does not affect character stats in ''Mario Kart 8'' and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''.
