'''Natural Power''' is an item in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Description==
Natural Power quickly recovers [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Vibe Gauge]] while she is idle., but only if she is not under the effects of any [[vibe]].

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Natural Power can be bought in the shop for 3,000 [[coin]]s after [[Giddy Sky]] becomes accessible.
