The '''New Year's 2020 '''is an unlockable High-End glider from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It was meant to celebrate the upcoming year 2020.

== Description ==
New Year's 2020 is a version of the [[Parafoil]]. Coincidentally the [[Holiday Tour]], the tour this glider is present in, ends on the [[New Year's Tour (2020)]]. The glider has a special skill called [[Mushroom Plus]], which increases the chances of getting a [[Mushroom]].
