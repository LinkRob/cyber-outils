'''Mushroom Marauder and Jake the Crusher Fungus''' are a pair of two muscular [[Toad (species)|Toad]] wrestlers who make a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Tag Team Trouble]]." They are [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]]'s cousins, and he has at least one more [[Toad's cousin|cousin]] from another episode, "[[Mind Your Mummy Mommy, Mario]]." A flyer from the [[Mushroom Wrestling Federation]] features both Mushroom Marauder and Jake the Crusher Fungus on it.
