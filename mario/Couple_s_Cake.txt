'''Couple's Cake''' is a recipe in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It works very similar to a [[Slow Shroom]] except it only affects [[Mario]]'s partners. Couple's Cake first appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
[[File:CakeCouplesTTYD.png|thumb|left]]
Mario receives a Couple's Cake for completing the [[Trouble Center|trouble]] titled "Looking for a gal." According to [[Rogueport Sewers]] resident [[Wonky]], Couple's Cakes remind [[Zess T.]] of her late husband, so she is hesitant about making them. This is a coincidence to [[Tayce T.]]'s story about [[Cake]]; she won her now deceased husband over it.

==In ''Super Paper Mario'' ==
A Couple's Cake can be made by [[Dyllis]]. It takes a [[Snow Bunny]] and [[Berry Snow Bunny]] to make. It can then be cooked with a [[Poison Mushroom]] to make a [[Trial Stew]]. If he eats it, [[Mario]] is granted 5,000 points.
