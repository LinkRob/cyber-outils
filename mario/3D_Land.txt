{{Nihongo|'''3D Land'''|3Dランド|3D Rando|3D Land}} is a recurring plains-themed stage in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. The stage is based on [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 1]] from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Layout==
3D Land is an auto-scrolling stage based off of the plains-themed aesthetics from ''Super Mario 3D Land''.
{{sectstub}}

==Origin==
3D Land has aspects of many levels from ''Super Mario 3D Land'' from the first area with [[Princess Peach's Castle]]'' in the background being World 1-1 and valley area based off on World 8-2.
{{sectstub}}
