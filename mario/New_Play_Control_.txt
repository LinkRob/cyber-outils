'''New Play Control!''' is a series of [[Wii]] games that were originally for the [[Nintendo GameCube]], but feature enhanced controls that make use of the [[Wii Remote]] and [[Nunchuk]]. Two ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related games were released within the series, ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]''.
