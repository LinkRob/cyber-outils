'''Big Bad Bugaboom''' is the third [[Power Star]] mission of [[Honeyhive Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Here, the galaxy has been infested by [[Mandibug]]s, and there is a boss fight against [[Bugaboom]] at the end.

==Layout==
The only two planets that are accessible are the Starting Planet and the one with Bugaboom.
{{sectstub}}

==Objective==
Upon reaching the starting planet, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] must progress their way forward, avoiding any Mandibugs, [[Boulder]]s, [[Slurple]]s, and [[Piranha Plant]]s they come across. After making his way down the waterfall, Mario or Luigi have to make their way through the tunnel to meet [[Queen Bee]]. When she is helped out, Queen Bee summons a [[Launch Star]] that leads to the Mandibug Planet, where Mario has to fight Bugaboom. To defeat Bugaboom, Mario or Luigi has to [[Ground Pound]] on the monster's back three times, earning him a [[Power Star]] after the last strike.

== Soundtrack ==

* Before the [[Bugaboom]] begins his boss fight, fight, a fast-paced version of the [[Honeyhive Galaxy]]'s theme plays.
* During the first phase of the Bugaboom's battle, [[King Kaliente]]'s theme plays.
* The Bugaboom's own theme plays starting from the second phase. This song is a higher-pitched, slightly faster remix of King Kaliente's theme.
