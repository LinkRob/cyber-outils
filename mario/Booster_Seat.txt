The {{Nihongo|'''Booster Seat'''|スーパーベビィカー|Sūpā Bebī Kā|Super Baby Car}} (known as the '''Baby Booster''' outside North America) is a small kart with good handling in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== Description ==
Booster Seat is a light and slow kart. The top splits in half and opens when an item is thrown or when the player performs a trick. It is available at the start of the game.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' ==
The Baby Booster appears as a kart for lighweights. It is available at the beginning of the game.

=== NPC Use ===
In [[Moo Moo Meadows]], the normal staff ghost (Nin☆YuNya) uses [[Baby Daisy]] on a Booster Seat.
