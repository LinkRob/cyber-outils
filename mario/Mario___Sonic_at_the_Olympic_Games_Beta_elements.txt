== Unused Characters (Wii) ==
There is In-game data which reveals that Silver, Jet, Cream, Big, Espio, Rouge, [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], [[Birdo]], and a [[Goomba]] were originally planned to be playable characters for this game. However, they all were then scrapped during the game's development and are only accessible through hacking. Although Jet, Donkey Kong, and Silver seem to have been completed, Birdo will be in a T-pose when loaded into the game.
