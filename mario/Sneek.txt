'''Sneeks''' are rather common rat enemies found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' They represent easy enemies, which the Kongs are able to defeat just by jumping on it.

== Behaviour ==
Sneeks often wander around a set path. They hurt the player if it touches them. There are also Sneeks running in small, wooden wheels in the level [[Squeals on Wheels]]. When encountered with them, [[Ellie the Elephant]] is frightened and runs away. Because of this, Sneeks are, however, more threatening than the previous games' simple enemies [[Neek]] and [[Gnawty]].<br />
