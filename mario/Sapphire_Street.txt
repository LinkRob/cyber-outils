'''Sapphire Street''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Description==
Sapphire Street is one of the streets located in [[Diamond City]].

==In ''WarioWare Gold''==
Sapphire Street is where [[Jimmy T]]'s story, [[Night on the Town]], takes place. In the opening cutscene, Jimmy T is walking down Sapphire Street when a stray [[Cat (WarioWare Gold)|cat]] jumps into his hair. [[Amy and Mimi]] notice the cat and ask Jimmy if they can take pictures, to which he agrees, and player plays through Jimmy T's microgames while the three take photos in Sapphire Street. In the ending cutscene, when Amy and Mimi are done taking photos, the latter sends the best photo, which is one of the cat. At first, Jimmy is shocked but then brushes it off after Amy and Mimi leave.

In the ending credits, Sapphire Street is shown in front of an image of Jimmy T and the cat.
