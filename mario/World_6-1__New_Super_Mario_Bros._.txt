'''World 6-1''' is the first level on world 6. It appears first and only in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

== Layout ==
This level takes place on a rocky mountain, and introduces [[Bill Blaster Turret]]s which shoots out [[Bullet Bill]]s from multiple directions. Players will navigate the mountain through thin ledges and alcoves, while also encountering red and green [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s.

== [[Star Coin]]s ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Upon crossing the third [[Bill Blaster Turret]], Mario should [[Wall Jump]] on the alcove leading to a trail of [[Coin]]s, where the first Star Coin is found.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is before the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]]. Mario should just simply jump to the left to get it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Before the flagpole, Mario should take the lower route and go on a thin ledge and hang on it to collect it, while also avoiding the Bullet Bills.
