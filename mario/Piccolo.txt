'''Piccolo''' is a character in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
She is an optional musical [[Pixl]] who is able to play different music for each character. Her music is able to unlock a few hidden secrets like a large red block with her image on it.

Piccolo is a secret pixl found in [[Flopside]] that takes a very long journey to find. After the long journey Merlee gives the player a key for a house in Flopside where Piccolo is hidden.

== Etymology ==
The word "Piccolo" refers to a light flute, a musical instrument. In Italy, Piccolo is known as Doremì, another musical term.
