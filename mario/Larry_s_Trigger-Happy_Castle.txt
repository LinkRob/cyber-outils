|game = ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''}}'''Larry's Trigger-Happy Castle''', or '''{{world|Waters|castle}}''', is the [[Castle]] level of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by completing [[Dragoneel Depths]] and its own completion will unlock [[All Aboard!]]
==Layout==
[[Luigi]] begins the level in front of an area that fire [[Banzai Bill]]s. Some [[Brick Block]]s and a [[? Block]] are found, followed by two [[Bill Blaster Turret]]s. A pit of water with [[Torpedo Ted]]s in it can be found in between them. Two Brick Blocks, one of which contains a power-up, can be found. A [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[Mini Mushroom]] is above the Brick Blocks. Luigi can climb up the Brick Blocks to a mini [[Warp Pipe]], leading to a hidden area with [[coin]]s. A [[Warp Pipe]] is found, and it leads to an underwater area. Two groups of Brick Blocks are found along with some [[Red Skull Box]] that release [[Targeting Ted]]s. A Warp Pipe that leads to the next area is then found. A ? Block is found next to a wall made of Brick Blocks. A [[King Bill]] then destroys the wall, and Luigi must jump on Brick Blocks floating above the water to avoid it. The boss door is then found. Luigi must fight [[Larry Koopa]], who battles the same way he did in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Jump on the first Bullet Bill cannon and jump to the Star Coin over the gap in between the cannons.
*'''Star Coin 2''': In the underwater part, activate the P Switch and swim to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': After exiting the underwater part of the level, jump on the three platforms and jump to the Star Coin before the King Bill destroys them.

==Hidden Luigi image==
After going in the underwater section (not in the Mini pipe), hit the Brick Block with a P Switch on it and let a Torpedo Ted hit that block to activate it. After that, swim above the exit pipe as fast as you can to reveal a picture of standard Luigi from [[Super Mario Bros.|''Super Mario Bros''.]] swimming.
