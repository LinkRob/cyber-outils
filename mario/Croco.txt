'''Croco''' is a crocodile villain who appears throughout ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He is fought as a minor boss twice. Croco frequently steals items from other characters.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Croco is a purple crocodile who wears white gloves and a red top hat on his head.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
The first encounter with Croco is at [[Mushroom Kingdom]] in [[Bandit's Way]]. He steals a [[Frog Coin]] from [[Mallow]] and Mario, and Mallow chases him through the outskirts of town. Later, they battle Croco to regain Mallow's Frog Coin. When Mario and Mallow defeat him, Croco states that the coin is stupid.

Croco is re-encountered at [[Moleville Mines]], where he steals all of [[Mario]] coins and runs around in a square. Mario can either chase Croco or wait for him to return. Afterwards, Mario regains his coins.

Croco can also be seen for the last time in [[Bowser's Keep]]. This time, he actually helps Mario and his team by selling them items and gear. Croco soon leaves right after Mario has brought items from him and leaves the room. Croco also appears near the end of the game where he is now living with the Yoshis on [[Yo'ster Isle]]. He also appears to have made friends with [[Boshi]]. Croco also makes a cameo appearance in the parade at the end of the game.
