'''Sad Nipper Plant''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Sad Nipper Plant is a variant of a [[Nipper Plant]] imbued with the [[Gloom]] [[vibe]], hence their sad face. Sad Nipper Plants have a white face and a green stem and leaves.

===Behavior===
Like with regular Nipper Plants, a Sad Nipper Plant jumps up and down, but when a Sad Nipper Plant is approached, it tends to jump higher and attack while sad. A Sad Nipper Plant's actions are shown to be jumping up and down, left and right repeatedly.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Sad Nipper Plants first appear in the [[level]] [[Ladida Plains 1-7]]. They also appear in other levels such as [[Hoo's Wood]]. It is harder for [[Princess Peach|Peach]] to hit a Sad Nipper Plant than a normal one due to its methods of attack, but most attacks can still defeat a Sad Nipper Plant. In the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]], its name is spelled '''Sad N. Plant'''.
