'''Aunt Luigeena''' is a one-time character who appear in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Mama Mia Mario]]." Aunt Luigeena is the aunt of both [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. She, like Luigi, is also portrayed by [[Danny Wells]], likely for comedic effect. [[Mario]] is afraid of Aunt Luigeena.
