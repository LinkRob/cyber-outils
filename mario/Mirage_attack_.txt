|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}{{Nihongo|'''Mirage attack!'''|ミラージュアタック！|Mirāju Atakku!}} is a special move used only by [[Yaridovich]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
With Mirage attack!, Yaridovich is able to create a [[Yaridovich Clone|clone]] of himself to aid him in battle. However, the Yaridovich Clone is much weaker and shouldn't be a problem to take down.

==Trivia==
*A similar move called [[Multiplier!]] is used by the [[Machine Made]] Yaridovichs. However, Multiplier! turns the Yaridovich Machine Made into four [[Drill Bit]]s.
