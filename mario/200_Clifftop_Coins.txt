'''200 Clifftop Coins''' is a Coin Collection Challenge in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

== Description ==
The player's character has to [[Wall Jump]] while collecting [[coin]]s throughout the challenge. [[Piranha Plant]]s, [[Spike Top]]s, and a [[Fuzzy]] are encountered as enemies, as well as three [[Skewer]]s as obstacles. Completing the challenge requires the player to have anywhere between 160 to 200 coins by the time they reach the [[Goal Pole]]. If the player touches any of the enemies and obstacles or the time limit reaches zero, they lose at the challenge.
