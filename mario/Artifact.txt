The '''Artifact''' is a key item that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is a small, ancient object.

== History ==
The Artifact can be found in [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]] in the same room with the [[Super Hammer]]. The Artifact isn't important for the story and is completely optional to pick up; however, the Artifact can be given to [[Kolorado]] who is in the first area of [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]] to earn a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] from him.
