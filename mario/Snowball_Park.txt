|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Snowball Park''' is the first level of World 3 in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|port]].

==Features==
The level features a snow-based course, with certain areas that are made of ice. The main enemy here are Snow Pokies and Skating Goombas, and the ice can make avoiding these enemies difficult, but not impossible. The selected character can also ride in the [[Ice Skate|ice skates]]. Behind a tree at the beginning is a Glide Box.

== Green Stars ==
*'''Green Star 1''': Destroy the group of Para-Biddlybuds to get the star hidden in an alcove.
*'''Green Star 2''': Pass the red ring, chase down the [[Rabbit]] to get the star (use the ice skate to make it easier).
*'''Green Star 3''': Above the trees, use the [[Propeller Block]] to reach up to the star. Alternatively, climb the right tree while in Cat form to climb up the wall; or be in Tanooki form to wall-jump and then flutter to the star.
