'''Wrecking Crew''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''. As its name implies, it is based on the original ''[[Wrecking Crew]]'' game. [[9-Volt]] hosts it in his Spintendo Classics stage.

==Layout and Gameplay==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] is poised atop a platform surrounded by eight bombs that he can whack with his [[Hammer]] to destroy the ladders leading up to it. [[Eggplant Man]]s will climb up these ladders to his platform, their goal being to knock him off. To with, the player must help Mario down the ladders with Eggplant Mans on them with his [[Hammer]]. If he gets caught, the player loses.

===Difficulty Levels===
*'''Level 1 (Easy)''': Mario must repel a single Eggplant Man.
*'''Level 2 (Hard)''': Mario must repel two Eggplant Mans.
*'''Level 3 (Expert)''': Four Eggplant Mans will climb the ladders, but not all of them will attack.

==Trivia==

* The microgame's description refers to the ladders as stairs and the [[Eggplant Man]] enemy as a singular character.
