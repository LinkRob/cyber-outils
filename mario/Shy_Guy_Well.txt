'''Shy Guy Wells''' are characters in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Description==
Shy Guy Wells are statues resembling [[Shy Guy]]s that only appear in [[Shy Guy's Jungle Jam]]. When a character lands on an [[Event Space]] near the statue, a Shy Guy asks them, "What kind of wish do you have?". The player can answer with "a fun wish"  or "a sad wish". Either answer may please or anger the Shy Guy Well at random. If the Shy Guy Well is pleased, the game continues as normal, but if angered, the Shy Guy Well causes the large Shy Guy statue at the back of the board to begin "crying", which prevents access to the opposite side of the board via bridge or raft for one to three turns due to the rising tides it creates.
