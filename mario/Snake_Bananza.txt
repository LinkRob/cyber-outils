'''Snake Bananza''' is a transformation of [[Donkey Kong]] that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. It is one of the many Bananza forms he can transform into, and is his fifth and final Bananza transformation unlocked in the game, being granted to him by the [[Snake Elder]] in the [[Radiance Layer]] after finding the pieces of the [[Ancient Record]] for him.

==Physical description==
Snake Bananza is quite unique in appearance as Donkey Kong, despite being a snake, still has his normal form's large arms in this form (albeit covered in scales) with a large upper body. However, his lower body is still curled much like with the snakes and his color scheme is dark green and black. He also has yellow stipes throughout his body resembling bananas.

==Powers and abilities==
Snake Bananza's primary ability is being able to jump much higher than before. This can be done by charging up his jump then unleashing it to allow Donkey Kong to go through certain structures. There is even an ability that can be unlocked from the skill tree that allows Snake Bananza to charge a jump a second time while in midair. However, Snake Bananza's other known ability is being able to slow time with a glare. After charging this up as well, Donkey Kong will be able to unleash a glare that is able to slow everything down making it useful for getting past obstacles that move too fast for him to navigate through in his other forms. This glare ability is even useful against bosses especially ones that move fast such as [[Muckety Muck]]. After completing the Snake Bananza Rehearsal, Donkey Kong will now be able to transform the area he is in into gold by jumping and punching down at it.

== Trivia ==

* Quite uniquely, [[Pauline]] shows no trepidation before she starts singing to awaken this transformation.
* In this form, Donkey Kong resembles a Yuan-Ti Abomination from ''Dungeons & Dragons'', albeit carrying no weapons.
* This is the only bananza of a predatory animal.
