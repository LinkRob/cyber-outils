The {{Nihongo|'''Frying Pan'''|フライパン|Furaipan|Frying Pan}} is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
The Frying Pan belongs to [[Tayce T.]], the resident chef of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. However, during the events of Chapter 4, the [[Shy Guy]]s stole the Frying Pan from Tayce T. and ran away from her house. [[Mario]] headed into [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] and retrieved it. After doing so, Tayce T. baked a [[Cake]] for him, which is required to get past [[Gourmet Guy]].
