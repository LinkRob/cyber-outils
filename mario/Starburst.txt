'''Starburst''' is a [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Spark]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''. It is first encountered in the prologue escaping the wrath of [[Cursa]], and joins the team in the tutorial stage, formally being recruited in the stage Headlong into the Unknown!

== Profile ==

=== Physical Description ===
As Starburst is a Spark, he looks like a regular yellow [[Luma]], though with the face of a blue-eyed Rabbid. He also has Rabbid ears.

=== Powers and Abilities ===
Via his Turbocharge power, Starburst can power the gang up within range of his user. Doing so allows them to deal more damage. Feeding Starburst will increase the damage bonus.
