'''Madrid Drive 2''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', first appearing in the Summer Tour of 2023. It is the second layout of [[Madrid Drive]], hence its name.

== Layout ==
Madrid Drive 2's layout focuses on el Mercado Santósio Miguelósio (the Market of San Miguel), la Plaza Mayor (the Major Plaza, or Town Square) and el Parque Retiro (the Retiro Park), the former two of which inhabit the map's southern region and the latter of which is at the far east. The racers start in the opposite direction that they did in [[Madrid Drive 1]]. After a slight left turn followed by a right turn, they will encounter a [[Glide Ramp]], which they use to [[Glider|fly to the south over a lake]] in el Parque Retiro. While the racers fly over the water, they can pass through fountains to get an additional [[Trick|jump boost]]. The racers turn right whereupon they find another ramp, which they use to cross a fountain, landing back in the city streets. After a right turn, the racers will enter la Plaza Mayor from the south, where they must avoid oversized [[Wiggler]]s that go around King Philip III's statue. The racers come out of the northwest exit, and then make a slight right, passing through el Mercado Santósio Miguelósio, where they must avoid [[Thwomp]]s. This is followed by a 180-degree right turn and a slight left in order to get back to the [[Finish line|starting line]].

In ''Mario Kart 8'', this route is shown being driven backwards, since Mario is shown in the trailer gliding over the water from the south end rather than the north.
