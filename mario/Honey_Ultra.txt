A '''Honey Ultra''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario series|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:HoneyUltraPM.png|thumb|left]]In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', this item is a recipe made by [[Tayce T.]], a chef in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. This item is only created by cooking an [[Ultra Shroom]] with some [[Honey Syrup]]. Once created, this item will restore fifty [[Heart Point]]s and five [[Flower Point]]s, the same amount as the two items that were used to make it.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', this item is created by [[Zess T.]] who will create the item for [[Mario]] if she is given an Ultra Shroom and a Honey Syrup. This item has the same effects as its ''Paper Mario'' counterpart.

=== ''Super Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', there is oddly no Honey Ultra as mixing an [[Ultra Shroom Shake]] and a [[Honey Jar]] will make a [[Honey Super]] instead.
