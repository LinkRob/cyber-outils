'''Fluzzard''' is a shy but speedy bird who appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Super Mario Galaxy 2 (Nintendo Switch)|port]].

==History==
===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[File:SMG2 Fluzzard.png|250px|thumb|left|[[Mario]] riding on Fluzzard]]
Fluzzard appears in only two [[Galaxy|galaxies]]: the [[Wild Glide Galaxy]] and the [[Fleet Glide Galaxy]]. In both galaxies, [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) must participate in the [[Fluzzard gliding]] [[minigame]], during which Fluzzard acts as a form of transportation for the player. As such, gliding with Fluzzard is the only way to progress through these two galaxies.

Fluzzard is found at the beginning of the [[level]], and an orange [[Jibberjay]] tells Mario that Fluzzard is very shy and cannot fly very well, but he can glide. The Jibberjay then notices that Fluzzard seems to be OK with Mario and asks Mario if he will fly with Fluzzard. The Jibberjay then gives Mario a tutorial on controlling Fluzzard and tells him to try to fly through the big sun circle at the end of the race. After Mario completes the race, the Jibberjay gives him a [[Power Star]]. Fluzzard later reappears in the Fleet Glide Galaxy and flies again with Mario. After Mario completes the initial races in both galaxies, Fluzzard can race the [[Black Jibberjay]] to see who is faster.

===''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''===
Fluzzards are also sighted during the scene in which Toad and Peach land in the Gateway Galaxy in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
His name is a portmanteau of "flutter" and "buzzard."

==Trivia==
*Fluzzard is comparable to the [[w:c:zelda:Loftwing|Loftwing]] from ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword]]'' in both design and means of control.
*When Mario or Luigi collect a [[Green Power Star]] while gliding with Fluzzard, Fluzzard suddenly vanishes, leaving behind a few pink and green feathers while the player character gets the Star.
