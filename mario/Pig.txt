'''Pigs''' are a real-world animal that have sometimes appeared in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They also have some fictional varieties and created for ''Mario''-related media, typically ones that are anthropomorphic and capable of speaking in English.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
Pigs make a somewhat large role during "Chapter 4: For Pigs the Bell Tolls". During the events of that chapter, the citizens of [[Twilight Town]] known as [[Twilighter]]s are being turned into Pigs by [[Doopliss]]. It should be noted that even non-Twilighters such as [[The Traveling Sisters Three]] and [[Dupree]] are turned into Pigs during their visits in Twilight Town. After Doopliss is defeated, the Pigs turn back into their respective characters.

A little earlier before this, another pig is seen named [[Mr. Hoggle]] who owns the hot dog stand in [[Glitzville]]. However, unlike the pigs that appeared when Doopliss was around, Mr. Hoggle is an anthropometric pig that is able to speak full sentences and stand on two legs.
