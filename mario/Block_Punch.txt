'''Block Punch''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is available from the start, so Block Punch does not have any unlock criteria.

==Description==
Block Punch supports up to four players, although only on a single system. The Gaddget features a cube in the center and a retractable red boxing glove in each of the four diagonal corners.

The object for each player is to break the cube before their opponents. There are three types of blocks: wooden, which is the weakest; metal, which takes more hits to break; and steel, which takes the most hits to break. Whoever breaks the cube is the winner.
