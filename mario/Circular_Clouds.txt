<strong>Circular Clouds</strong> are harmless enemies that appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. They appear in certain areas where certain platforms or gimmicks are required to advance forward. During that time, they appear in a spirit-like form if you try using the Joy vibe and get close to them (as they're normally near the desired destination) they will cling on to [[Peach]], turn blue and weigh her down and then switch off her Joy Vibe so this is where you have to find a different alternative.

== Trivia ==
*These enemies were most likely created so that you can't use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy vibe]] as an alternative to get certain collectibles.{{SPPNav}}
