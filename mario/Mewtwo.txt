'''Mewtwo''' is one of the [[Pokémon]] from the ''Pokémon'' series. It has also appeared in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, beginning with ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''.

==Biography==
Mewtwo is a genetically created Pokémon that was created from Mew's cells. Mewtwo is a Legendary Pokémon and one of the first revealed in the entire series. Mewtwo is a very powerful Psychic-type Pokémon that is well known for it's fierce power in and out of it's series.
Thanks to it's starring role in ''Mewtwo Strikes Back'', ''Genesect and the Legend Awakened'' and ''Mewtwo Strikes Back Evolution'', Mewtwo has became one of the most popular Pokémon of all time. Mewtwo has been featured in many other movies and made a single appearance in the anime where Giovanni used it to effortlessly defeat Gary. Mewtwo is one of two Pokémon to have two different Mega Evolution forms with the other one being [[Charizard]]. Many fans were upset that Mewtwo wasn't included in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' (despite actually being one of the worst characters to use in ''Melee'') but rejoiced when Mewtwo was announced as the first DLC character to be released in ''Super Smash Bros. 3DS/Wii U''.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
[[File:Mewtwo SSBU.png|thumb|left|''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' artwork]]
Mewtwo made its ''Super Smash Bros''. series debut as an unlockable character in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee''. It can be unlocked after playing 20 cumulative hours of Versus Mode matches (5 hours for 4 players) or playing 700 Versus Mode matches. 

Mewtwo was later announced in the ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U: 50-Fact Extravaganza'' as a playable character in ''Smash 4'', becoming the first fighter in ''Super Smash Bros.'' history to be be made available as downloadable content.

Mewtwo returns as an unlockable character in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' but is playbale without DLC.

==Trivia==
*Mewtwo is the only playable Legendary Pokémon in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series. As such, it is also the only playable Pokémon without evolutionary relatives.
*Mewtwo is the only genderless playable character from the ''Pokémon'' series.
*Mewtwo's Mega Evolutions are tied with Mega Rayquaza for the highest base stat total of all Pokémon, with 780, excluding non-playable Eternamax Eternatus.
