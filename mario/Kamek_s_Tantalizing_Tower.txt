{{Nihongo|'''Kamek's Tantalizing Tower'''|カメックのゴージャスタワー|Kamekku no Gōjasu Tawā|Kamek's Gorgeous Tower}} is an unlockable board in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The board takes atop a golden tower within a city, with a Kamek-themed roulette at the top. This roulette activates every time a player lands on its event space or collects a star, randomizing the cost of the next star.
