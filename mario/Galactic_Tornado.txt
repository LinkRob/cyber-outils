'''Galactic [[Tornado]]es''' are enemies which debut in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They make their first appearance in the [[Dusty Dune Galaxy]]'s first mission, [[Soaring on the Desert Winds]]. This [[Tweester]]-like enemy will propel [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] into the air where they might have the reach a platform. After going through a Galactic Tornado, the character may have to catch onto a [[Launch Star|Launch]] or a [[Sling Star]]. Some Galactic Tornados have rocks orbiting them, hindering the character's ability to step into them.

Galactic Tornadoes later appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', in [[Melty Monster Galaxy]]'s first mission, [[Magnificent Magmaw Sea]]. The variation with the rocks does not return.
