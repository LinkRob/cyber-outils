'''Drybake Desert''' is the first level of World 2 in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

== Layout ==
Drybake Desert looks similar to [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]] from the [[Paper Mario|game]]. It's mostly covered with sand with cacti all around. There are also pyramids in the background. In addition to this, there is a cave located in this level.

== History ==
After getting the first [[Royal Stickers|Royal Sticker]], Mario heads off into the vast sands to continue his search for another one. Mario fights though many new enemies including [[Pokey]]s, [[Paragoomba]]s, and the unique [[Sombrero Guy]] . Once more, Mario encounters the [[Travelling Toad]]. He also nearly gets stuck in quicksand but manages to escape. Eventually, he ends up in front of a giant pyramid. Mario must make his way through, around and up the pyramid and finds the Comet Piece to complete this rather long level.

== Secret Area ==
The secret area in this level is located at the bottom of a slide between to cacti ledges. There are many Stickers located in this area. There is also a Tablet Piece scrap that will be useful later.
