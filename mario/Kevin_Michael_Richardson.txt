'''Kevin Michael Richardson''' (October 25, 1964) is an American actor and voice actor who voiced [[Kamek (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Kamek]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' and ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. Outside of ''Mario'', he is best known for voicing [[w:c:disney:Captain Gantu|Captain Gantu]] in the ''[[wikipedia:Lilo & Stitch (franchise)|Lilo & Stitch]]'' franchise, Temutai in Kung Fu Panda: Legends of Awesomeness, [[w:c:mortalkombat:Goro|Prince Goro]] in the 1995 ''[[wikipedia:Mortal Kombat (1995 film)|Mortal Kombat]]'' movie and [[w:c:spongebob:Seagull|Seagull]] in the [[w:c:spongebob:The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge Out of Water|''The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge Out of Water'']].
