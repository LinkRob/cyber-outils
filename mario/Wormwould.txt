'''Wormwould''' is a giant worm who appears as a boss in [[The Grasslands]] in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. Here, [[Wario]] has to defeat him to recover the [[Music Box|Yellow Music Box]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
As indicated by his sprite, Wormwould is a large worm with a red body with light pink stripes and large, light green lips.

===Powers and abilities===
Wormwould's only attack consists of spitting rocks at Wario, who can defeat Wormwould by [[Ground Pound]]ing his head three times.

===Etymology===
Wormwould's name is a pun on "{{w|wormwood}}," a medicinal herb used to kill worms and other parasites.
