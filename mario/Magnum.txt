|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Magnum''' is a special move used only by [[Smithy]]'s Tank head in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Magnum is used, a golden [[Bullet Bill]] is fired at a single target.

==Effects==
With Magnum, Smithy may be able to cause instant death to one of Mario's party. Wearing a Quartz Charm or the Jinx Belt can prevent this.

==Trivia==
*This move is similar to [[Blazer]] used by [[Big Bertha (Bill Blaster)|Big Berthas]].
