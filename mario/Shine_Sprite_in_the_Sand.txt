'''Shine Sprite in the Sand''' is one of the secret [[Shine Sprite]]s found in [[Delfino Plaza]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Objective==
[[Mario]] has to go to the beach next to the lighthouse. Just before the lighthouse is a hidden [[Shine Painting]], which Mario can reveal by having [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] spray water at it. Once the Shine Painting is fully revealed, a [[Shine Sprite]] emerges from it and flies off hover just above a pillar near the [[Warp Pipe]] leading to [[Super Slide]].

Mario can complete the mission at any time except while Delfino Plaza is flooded. During this, if a glitch is used to walk underwater, the Shine Painting is in a different location, around where the umbrellas are, and spraying it releases a [[coin]] instead.
