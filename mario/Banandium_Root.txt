* [[King K. Rool]]}}The '''Banandium Root''' is the main focus item of the game [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza''.]] It is found in the [[Planet Core]] and is a magical [[Banandium]] object that grants its wielder any wish they desire.

== Description ==
The Banandium Root's appearance is a chain of golden bananas that twists and loops around itself.

== Usage ==
The Banandium Root can grant the wish of anyone who obtains it. It will normally reject wishes made for evil and even imprison the one making such a wish while the Root sleeps. But if it has slumbered for a long period, it will lack discernment and grant any wish it hears.

== History ==
The Banandium Root resides in the [[Heart of Gold]], a massive sphere of pure [[Bananergy]] within [[Mushroom Earth]]'s [[Planet Core]]. It awaits those who seek to have a good wish to be fulfilled. It is the source of all Banandium Gems and Banandium Gold, which extend from the core outwards to even the surface.[[File:Planet Core-Map.jpg|thumb|left|The Banandium Root dwells in the Heart of Gold, the very center of the planet.]]

Well before the events of the game, a [[King K. Rool|certain surface dweller]] forced their way into the Planet Core. Because of their rottenness, the Banandium Root rejected their wish and sealed them away. 

Much later, the [[Void Kong|president]] of [[VoidCo]] plots to access the Root's wish-granting power, and tunnels through [[Ingot Isle]] using a massive aggregate of stone to reach the Planet Core. He needs the magical properties of [[Pauline|Pauline's]] voice to breach the seal, which he mistakes for the Banandium Root itself. When the seal is ruptured, the freed surface dweller enters the Root's sanctum and wishes to amplify the power of their rottenness and consume the world with it. Once defeated, [[Donkey Kong]] and Pauline have their wishes granted and make for the surface. 

Upon blasting through the surface, the Root is stolen once more. The surface dweller uses it to instantly seize total control over [[New Donk City]], remaking it in their own image. Despite being empowered even more by the Banadium Root, the surface dweller is defeated once again.
