The '''Dyllis Special''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing a [[Roast Shroom Dish]] with a [[Fruity Hamburger]]. This recipe restores 60 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
