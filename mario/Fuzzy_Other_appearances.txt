Fuzzies have made only one appearance outside the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] thus far.

==''Wario'' franchise==
===''WarioWare: Get It Together!''===
[[File:WWGIT-Nintendo-01.png|thumb|A Fuzzy in level 1 of the New Super Mario Bros. U microgame in ''WarioWare: Get It Together!'']]
In ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'', Fuzzies are featured in the [[microgame]] [[New Super Mario Bros. U (microgame)|New Super Mario Bros. U]], where the goal of the player's character is to avoid them while collecting [[coin]]s (of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]). If the character has an attack, they can use it to defeat the Fuzzies. However, if the character gets hit by a Fuzzy, they lose the microgame. In two-player mode, the microgame is not immediately counted as a loss if a character touches a Fuzzy; in this case, Fuzzies have to defeat both characters for it to count as a loss.

In level 1, the player's character has to avoid one Fuzzy traveling along a single track in a level of [[Rock-Candy Mines]]. In level 2, the character has to avoid two Fuzzies moving on either single or separate tracks in a level of [[Meringue Clouds]]. In level 3, there are three Fuzzies to avoid in a sky-themed level, where they move on either one or separate tracks.

Fuzzies are also present in level 2 of the [[Super Mario World (WarioWare: Get It Together!)|Super Mario World]] microgame, set in an area based on [[Donut Plains 3 (level)|Donut Plains 3]]. Fuzzies have a behavior based on their appearance from the [[Super Mario World|source game]] of the same title.
