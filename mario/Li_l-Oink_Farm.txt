The '''Li'l-Oink Farm''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Located in the southern area of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]], this farm is home to [[Li'l-Oink]]s. The farm can only hold a maximum of ten Li'l-Oinks and when the eleventh Li'l-Oink makes its way into the farm, the first and oldest Li'l-Oink leaves the farm and drops an item behind. However, if Mario goes into the pen, all the Li'l-Oinks will run away. The Li'l-Oink farm can hold many different breeds of Li'l-Oinks. The farm is entertainment to a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who watches the Li'l-Oinks.

==Trivia==
*The Li'l-Oink Farm gets mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' where a Toad by the name of [[Pine T.]] manages to get a job watching over the Li'l-Oinks of the farm.
