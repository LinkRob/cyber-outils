'''Koopa Troopa's Game Board''' is a [[board]] hosted by [[Koopa Troopa]] in the [[Mario Party 4 (Flash game)|''Mario Party 4'' Flash game]]. The only junction in the board is a [[Ukiki]]'s [[Banana Peel]] Junction, similar to the ones on [[Koopa's Seaside Soiree]], which will randomly send the player up or down. Unlike arrow spaces on other boards it does not count as a space for movement.
