The '''Mushroom Earth'''<ref group="notes">"Mushroom Earth" is a descriptive name as there is no official name for the planet other than it being described as '''"an Earth"''' where the '''Mushroom Kingdom''' is from.</ref> is '''an alternate Earth''',<ref name="an">[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OD_K8veKC14&t=538s "Perhaps the best way to say it is that the human characters you're encountering here and Mario also live on ''an'' Earth together"] [[Nintendo]] developer Kenta Motokura ''Super Mario Odyssey'' Gameplay with Developers at E3 2017 – Nintendo Minute. Youtube. Retrieved 10 April 2024.</ref> which encompasses the [[Mushroom World]] continent, which encompasses the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] where [[Mario]] and his friends live. It is a similar version of the real [[Earth]]. The planet is the setting of many ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games and in some media and games, it has a [[Warp Pipe|connection]] to our [[Earth]], with certain real world locations between the two [[w:c:Tropedia:Merged Reality|merged]] in some instances.

==Description==
A notable continent on the planet is called the [[Mushroom World]] which houses the country of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

Other lands that are associated with the Mushroom Kingdom exist outside of the Mushroom World continent but on Mushroom Earth planet, such as the [[Beanbean Kingdom]], [[Sarasaland]], [[Dinosaur Land]] (which includes [[Yoshi's Island]]), and [[Isle Delfino]]. 

It is however, harder to determine if locations in the ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' franchise, such as [[Donkey Kong Island]] or [[Crocodile Isle]], or in the [[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]] series, particularly [[Diamond City]], are located on Mushroom Earth planet, some fans suggest it is sensible to assume that they are, because several characters appear in the ''Mario'' franchise.{{Citation needed}}

The Mushroom Earth planet is orbited by a vast chain of small planetoids, as seen in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. [[Mario]] appears to be able to breathe normally despite the lack of oxygen in this game, though in others he cannot. This may be explained by the presence of the Luma in his cap.{{clear}}

==History==
===Past===

====''Yoshi's Island series''====
The games ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', ''[[Yoshi's New Island|Yoshi’s New Island]]'', ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' have all confirmed that [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] first lived in Mushroom Kingdom. 

====''Mario  Luigi Partners in Time''====
''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' shows that the [[Shroobs]] of [[Shroob Planet]] discovered the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] on the Mushroom Earth through a telescope and invaded the planet. The Shroob Planet was dying and the Shroobs were defeated during their invasion.

=== Present===
===''Super Mario'' series===

====''Super Mario Bros.''====
''Super Mario Bros.'' takes place after the events of ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]'' as [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] had traveled through one of the [[Warp Pipe]]s from the sewers of [[New York City]], [[Earth]] and returned to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], Mushroom Earth.<ref>[https://www.ndw.jp/mario-interview-230425/2/ インタビュー  マリオ映画公開記念！宮本茂さんインタビュー 制作の始まりから驚きの設定まで] (April 25, 2023). ''Nintendo Dream Web''. Retrieved April 25, 2023</ref>

====''Super Mario World''====
[[File:SMW Mushroom Earth.png|thumb|200px|The Mushroom Earth in ''Super Mario World'']]

The planet appears in official art for ''[[Super Mario World]]''. It depicts several islands around [[Dinosaur Land]].

The events of the game are only set around [[Dinosaur Land]] specifically, which is away from the continent [[Mushroom World]]. 


====''Super Mario 64''====
[[File:SM64 Mushroom Earth.png|thumb|left|200px|The Mushroom Earth in ''Super Mario 64'']]
In ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', Mario uses [[painting]]s in [[Peach's Castle]] to travel to different parts of the world within the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

The planet itself appears on the box art, although only the Mushroom Kingdom is visited. It depicts several of the locations in it such as [[Princess Peach's Castle]] and [[Cool, Cool Mountain]].

====''Super Mario Sunshine''====
Mushroom Earth appears as a flight map setting on the [[Toad Express]], flying its destination to Isle Defino.

During the opening cutscene it is shown that many islands around the Earth's continent are surrounded by archipelagos shaped like animals. These include as: a dolphin (north), a crab (southwest), a sea turtle (east), a jellyfish (northeast), a mola (northwest) and a small fish (south). 

It was implied Isle Delfino is located in the Earth's equator at the center left, however this was later changed from an official atlas promotional map from [[Super Mario Odyssey]], placing it in the southern hemisphere, northwest of the Mushroom Kingdom.<ref>''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' untextured shape on the globe</ref>

====''Super Mario Galaxy''====
[[File:MushroomWorld.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The first detailed full depiction of the Mushroom Earth first shown in ''Super Mario Galaxy'']]

Due to those two games being set in space, the Mushroom Earth is often seen in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|its sequel]]. In the former game, it is shown primarily in the opening and ending cutscenes, where it is the location where the Star Festival is held, and is first shown from space when Bowser kidnaps Peach and removes her castle from its foundation and carries them both into space, causing Mario to follow them into space as well. Additionally, Mushroom World is the setting of [[Grand Finale Galaxy]], the final galaxy in the game due to it involving Mario returning to the Mushroom Kingdom.

====''Super Mario Galaxy 2''====
[[File:Mushroom Earth-SMG2.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The retelling of the [[Star Festival]] in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' has the Earth in a cloudy Nebula-like space]]
The Mushroom Earth also returns in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', where it is the main setting of the game's first world, while other real places in space are featured as the settings of later worlds (such as the Solar System in World 2, the Orion Arm in World 3, the Milky Way galaxy in World 4, the Local Group in World 5, and a black hole/quasar in World 6) except for World S, which features a planet with star-shaped islands instead, as well as in the backgrounds of the first two Bowser Jr. galaxies (Bowser Jr.'s Boom Bunker is the exception, which features a Sun in the background instead).

====''Super Mario Odyssey''====
[[File:Mushroom Earth SMO.png|thumb|right|150px|The map of Mushroom Earth in ''Super Mario Odyssey''.]]
Mario travels across the Mushroom Earth in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

====''Super Mario 3D World''====
An unspecified Earth also appears in the backgrounds of [[World Star]], [[World Mushroom]], and [[World Flower]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', again due to those levels being set in space, explaining [[Rosalina]]'s appearance in this game.

===''Mario Kart'' series===

====''Mario Kart DS''====
Official artwork for ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' depicts various characters racing and battling on the Earth's surface, visually demonstrating the worldwide scope of the [[Nintendo DS]]' Wi-Fi capabilities. 

====''Mario Kart Wii''====
In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', Earth appears while the player is waiting in the Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection and in the [[Rainbow Road]] course of the Special Cup. The planet is located in the background of the course, underneath the track. If the players accidentally fall off the course, they will fall to the void by the gravity of the planet, instead of floating in space.

====''Mario Kart 7''====
In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', like ''Mario Kart Wii'', Earth appears while the player is waiting in the Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection. It is also there in the Mario Kart Channel.

====''Mario Kart 8''====
In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', Earth is still shown when the player plays online. Also, like Rainbow Road in ''Mario Kart Wii'', ''Mario Kart 8's'' Rainbow Road is set above Earth, along with a [[Blooper]] spaceship and MKTV satellites floating in space.

===''Mario Party'' ===
In ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' (in a minigame in the latter), the Earth can be seen. In ''[[Mario Party DS]]'', there is another minigame where players run on a globe and shoot opponents.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
[[File:The Overthere,SPM.jpg|left|thumb|The Overthere planet, from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.]]
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Mushroom Earth was under the invasion of [[Count Bleck]], an evil aristocrat who plans to end all worlds using [[Chaos Heart]]. He kidnaps Princess Peach and then takes Luigi, Bowser and his whole troops to [[Castle Bleck|his castle]]. In Chapter 7-3, if either Mario, or other member jumps on the giant cloud "[[Cyrrus]]", they will be sent into orbit and back down to the planet of [[The Underwhere]] and [[The Overthere]], which is seen in the background. The planet bears quite similar to that of Mushroom Earth.

===Other appearances===
[[File:DK Bananza - Underground World Layers.png|right|thumb|The interior structure of the world is a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollow_Earth Hollow Earth], as seen in Donkey Kong Bananza]]

In ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'', the Mushroom Earth is revealed to be a [[wikipedia:Hollow_Earth|hollow earth]]. Its interior is known as the [[Underground World]], with 16 Layers boasting a variety of different biomes leading to the [[Heart of Gold]], the center of the [[Planet Core|planet's core]].
<br>

[[Wario]] refers to the area below the Awazon as "deep within the Earth" in the manual of ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

During the events of the [[Club Nintendo (magazine)|Club Nintendo comics]] "Donkey Kong in: Banana Day 24", aliens erroneously took Earth for a giant coconut, and since they adored this kind of fruit, they took the planet with them, pulling it away from the sun. This resulted in a sudden drop of temperature all over the planet. When [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and some of his friends talked to the aliens, they returned Earth back to its original position.

In the comic "[[Die Jagd nach dem Nintendo 64: Krawall im All]]", from the same magazine, Mario and Luigi traveled through space in search for a new spaceship. They finally found it on Earth (which Luigi referred to as "Planet '''Terra'''").

In ''[[Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]'', Mushroom Earth can be seen in the background during Asteroid Belt and the fight with Sumo Kong.

==In other media==
===== ''Super Mario Bros.'' (film)=====
{{Main|Dino World (film)|}}
[[File:Dinohattan Globe-SMB1993FILM.jpg|thumb|The Mushroom Earth, or "Dino World", in the ''Super Mario Bros.'' 1993 film.]]
In the ''[[Super Mario Bros. (1993 film)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film, an alternate, unnamed version of Earth is revealed to exist known as the '''[[Dino World (film)|Dino World]]'''; this parallel Earth was brought into creation when a meteorite struck the true Earth, causing spatial and dimensional disturbances, resulting in the creation of the alternate Earth, which the dinosaurs, at least those who weren't killed, and a few other creatures were flung to.

On this Earth, various types of dinosaurs evolved into humanoid creatures, though some, such as [[Yoshi]], seemed to have retained their reptilian forms. Aside from one rundown city, [[Dinohattan]], this Earth is a desert wasteland, lacking various resources.
