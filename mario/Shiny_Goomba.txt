'''Shiny Goombas''' are enemies in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Physical description==
Shiny Goombas look exactly like regular Goombas except they're shiny in color.

==Battle==
In battle, Shiny Goombas act like regular Goombas except they are a little stronger. They are also commonly fought in groups especially with five of them. They are only found in certain levels of World 4 and are commonly found near [[Snow Spike]]s on top of their snowballs that are being hurled at Mario. They are also found near [[Shiny Koopa Troopa|Shiny Koopas]]. Shiny Goombas also tend to drop Shiny-related stickers when defeated.
