'''Bowser's Body''' is one of the main settings in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] find themselves trapped there toward the beginning of the game when [[Bowser]], affected by the machinations of Fawful, invades [[Princess Peach's Castle]] and inhales up the brothers along with many others. While inside Bowser, Mario and Luigi use a series of bodily functions to traverse the environment and help Bowser.

Later in the game, Mario, Luigi, and [[Starlow]] discover [[Warp Pipe|pipes]] inside Bowser that lead to various locations in the [[Mushroom Kingdom|outside world]].
