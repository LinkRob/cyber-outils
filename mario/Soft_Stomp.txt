'''Soft Stomp''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Effects ==
[[Mario]] can wear this badge by using one [[Badge Point|BP]]. While worn, Mario can use a new attack in battle that lowers his enemy's defenses. Mario can use this attack by paying two [[Flower Point|FP]] in battle.

Soft Stomp is found in the center of the network of tunnels within [[Rogueport Sewers]]. In the large plaza with a shop, [[Merluvlee]]'s, and a large pool of shallow water, Mario can go into this water and find the Soft Stomp in a treasure chest. Soft Stomp is also purchased from the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] for a price of 75 [[coin]]s.
