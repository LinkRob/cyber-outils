}}'''Frozen Assets''' is a Duel minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the players must break open ice pillars in order to get crystals that are inside of them. The players can use a variety of moves to break open the ice pillars including punches, kicks, and even a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]]. Not all ice pillars contain crystals as some may hold nothing at all. The large Freezie at the top of the stage will cause more ice pillars to fall on the ground. The player to find five crystals first wins.

== Ending ==
The player who lost the minigame gets frozen in ice.
