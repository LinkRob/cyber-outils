The '''Slide Attack''', also spelled '''Slide-attack''', is a move in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!]]''.

==Description==
Slide Attack can only be used by the back driver in co-op mode. It involves performing a quick sliding punch to either the left or right, by pressing {{button|gcn|L}} or {{button|gcn|R}} respectively. If Slide Attack hits another racer, they spin out, and the character who used Slide Attack can even steal any item they have. On certain courses, depending on where Slide Attack is used, it can put the user and their partner at risk of falling off of an edge.
