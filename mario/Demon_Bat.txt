'''Demon Bats''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Demon Bats are bats with a round body, a pair of wings, two big ears on top of their head, and a large spear-pointed tail. Demon Bats also wear pointed shoes. In their artwork, Demon Bats are shown to have a dark green body, and their shoes are dark purple.

===Behavior===
Demon Bat fly around in the air, and if [[Wario]] is within range, a Demon Bat swoops down to try and attack him with its pointed tail.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
Demon Bats are first encountered in [[Course No.04]] of [[Rice Beach]]. They are then later encountered in [[level]]s of [[Stove Canyon]].
