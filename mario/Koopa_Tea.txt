The '''Koopa Tea''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', the Koopa Tea can be simply made by having [[Tayce T.]] brew a [[Koopa Leaf]]. In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', the rules are the same except that the Koopa Leafs are replaced with [[Turtley Leaf]]s. In both games, the Koopa Tea can restore 5 [[Heart Point|HP]]. A Koopa Tea is needed for one of [[Koopa Koot]]'s many favors.
