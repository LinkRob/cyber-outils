'''Frogs''' are one of the real-world species in the [[Diddy Kong Racing (series)|''Diddy Kong Racing'' series]]. They do not often appear in the main ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' franchise, though [[Winky]] is featured as an [[Animal Friend]] in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', and both [[Frogoon]]s and [[Hopgoon]]s are enemies in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' and its remakes.

==History==
===''Diddy Kong Racing'' / ''Diddy Kong Racing DS''===
In ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and [[Diddy Kong Racing DS|its remake]], several frogs live in the pond in the hub. One of these frogs, who has a tell-tale red crest upon its head, is actually [[Drumstick (character)|Drumstick]], who has been put into frog form by [[Wizpig]]. He can be freed if the player runs over him with a car.

===''Diddy Kong Pilot'' (2001)===
[[File:Frog DKP 2001.png|thumb|left]]
Frogs appear in some of the unfinished swamp courses of the 2001 ''[[Diddy Kong Pilot (2001)|Diddy Kong Pilot]]''. They are merely rendered as static sprites and do not have any behavior, so it is unclear whether Frogs were intended to be ambient or have a role in the swamp courses.
