'''Reclaim the Red Goblet!''' is the fourth level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 11.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 8 [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. The enemy captain is a Koopa Paratroopa.

The final battle contains 8 [[Para-Beetle]]s. The enemy captain is a Para-Beetle.

After the final battle the player recruits a Koopa Paratroopa (Red) and a Para-Beetle.

The player may replay the battle to recruit up to one Koopa Paratroopa and or Para-Beetle.
