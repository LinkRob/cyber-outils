'''Goomba Gold Rush''' is a Coin Chaos minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. It, [[Tiles and Tribulations]], and [[Rolling Rumble]] are the only three Coin Chaos minigames available after the Coinathlon mode is unlocked.

==Layout==
Each character is on a platform with a cannon in a desert area, although only the character being played is shown. The cannon is mounted on a wooden railroad track, on which it can move left and right.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Goomba Gold Rush minigame mode.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Minigame mode gameplay]]
The characters have to shoot [[cannonball]]s at [[Goomba]]s out on the field. At times, the Goombas are lead by a [[Bob-omb]]. Destroying a Bob-omb defeats all Goombas following it and any other Goombas nearby. Players earn [[coin]]s by destroying these targets, which enable them to advance in the Coinathlon game mode. In levels 2 and 3, [[Red Coin]]s, worth three coins, can appear among the Goombas. The targets also move faster in Level 3.
{{br|left}}
