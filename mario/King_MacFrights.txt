'''King MacFrights''' is the sixth boss in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
King MacFrights is a medieval ghost wearing a king's shirt, a gold crown, and red gloves. He has red eyebrows and beard with white eyes and mouth.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3''==
King MacFrights is encountered on [[Castle MacFrights]], the sixth floor of [[The Last Resort]]. [[Luigi]] has to battle him for the seventh floor [[elevator button]]. King MacFrights has 200 [[Heart Point|HP]].

=== Battle ===
Luigi first sees King MacFrights standing on the balcony of the castle with two Goobs standing beside him. MacFrights then tants Luigi with the seventh floor's elevator button. Now, Luigi has to go through the castle to reach MacFrights' arena. When Luigi arrives, King MacFrights gets enraged and wears his big armor and rides around the arena. A herd of ghosts will cheer for him. The audience will cheer when King MacFrights hit Luigi, they will boo Luigi if Luigi attacks King MacFrights. He will order arrows to fire which Luigi needs to dodge. MacFrights later rides faster towards Luigi, when you see he rise his head, flash him quickly so he will be stunned. After stunning him, you have to fire a plunger at his armor to slam his armor to the ground, Repeat the steps and his armor will be destroyed. The battle gets into phase two. During phase two, King MacFrights will attack with his sword and shield, Luigi has to wait for him to spin and get dizzy. After he spins, you can flash and suck him. You will get the seventh floor's elevator button when he is beated. The ghosts will also Luigi when MacFrights gets sucked up.
