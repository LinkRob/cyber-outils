'''Rumble Fishing''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
There are [[Dragoneel]]s jump out of the water and dive in the water. Players on [[Lakitu's Cloud]]s holding fishing rods appear on the screen and the minigame starts.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to get the longest [[Dragoneel]] in this minigame. The player should move around to find the Dragoneel, There are ripples on the water that indicates where it is. The stronger rumbles hint that the Dragoneel is longer. The player can shake the Joy-Con to claim the Dragoneel. Whoever fished out the longest Dragoneel is the winner.

== Ending ==
The winner poses his/her 1st/2nd place victory pose from the eariler games on top of Lakitu's Cloud. Other players are standing on different small islands and poses their losing animation.
