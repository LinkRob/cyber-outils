The {{nihongo|'''Rubber Mushroom'''|きのこ|Kinoko}} is an item which appears in the ''[[w:c:nintendogs:Nintendogs|Nintendogs]]'' games for the [[Nintendo DS]]. While it has not made any ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearances, the Rubber Mushroom is a [[Super Mushroom]] in the form of a dog toy.

==Description==
The Rubber Mushroom is based on the Super Mushroom's design from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', specifically in that it has a yellow cap with red spots, a design similarly applied to the Super Mushroom furniture items in earlier ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' games. The Rubber Mushroom can bounce around a lot.

In ''[[w:c:nintendogs:Nintendogs + Cats|Nintendogs + Cats]]'', the item was renamed to "Mushroom Toy," and was changed to purple with white spots. Aside from a change in its design, the item remains essentially the same.
