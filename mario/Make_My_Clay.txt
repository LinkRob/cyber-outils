'''Make My Clay''' is one of the [[microgame]]s under [[Dr. Crygor]]'s [[Reality]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must repeatedly press {{button|gba|A}} for a puddle of clay on the [[moon]] to grow into an alien life-form before a meteorite falls from the top of the screen, crushing it. The player if the meet this objective but lose if they do not.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels require {{button|gba|A}} to be pressed four, six, and eight times respectively.

==Trivia==
*The name is a pun on the phrase, "Go ahead, make my day," but substitutes "day" for "clay," the main feature of the microgame.
