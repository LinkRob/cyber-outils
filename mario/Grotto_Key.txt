The '''Grotto Key''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Grotto Key is found in the storeroom of the [[Pirate's Grotto]] on an upper ledge. In order to get to it, [[Mario]] must first have either [[Koops]] or [[Admiral Bobbery|Bobbery]] activate the switch from a distance and then stand on the wooden lift and ride it up. Mario then can jump onto the barrels to get to the Grotto Key, which unlocks a door allowing further access into the Grotto.
