The '''Bucket''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Bucket can be obtained from the [[Mayor Penguin]] of [[Shiver City]] who gives it to [[Mario]] once he obtains the [[Scarf]] from [[Merle]] of [[Starborn Valley]]. The Bucket must then be used on a [[snowman]] on [[Shiver Snowfield]] while the Scarf being used on another to help open the way to [[Shiver Mountain]].
