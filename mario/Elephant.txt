'''Elephants''' are [[Earth|real-world]] animals that have sometimes appeared in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, their first time being in ''[[Wario World]]'' as an enemy.

==Behavior==
in ''[[Wario World]],'' elephants are very aggressive and resilient. An Elephant tries to attack [[Wario]] by charging up and ramming into him.

In ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]],'' elephants are sentient, speaking creatures that live in the Tempest Layer. 

==In ''Wario World''==
Elephants are only encountered in [[Wonky Circus]]. They are one of two enemies that a [[snake charmer]] can be found riding, the other one being a [[Cobra]]. Elephants are invincible while attacking, and can be defeated from either a [[Piledriver]], [[Wild Swing-Ding]], or [[Mega Toss]].
