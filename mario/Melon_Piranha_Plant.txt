'''Melon Piranha Plant''' is a variant of [[Piranha Plant]] introduced in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It is capable of spitting watermelon seeds, much like in the {{series|Yoshi's Island}} series, where a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can spit watermelon seeds while a [[watermelon]] is in its mouth. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] can stomp on the seeds that these creatures spit without taking damage. Melon Piranha Plants have a head pattern that makes their head resemble a watermelon due to having thick, dark green squiggly lines, which alongside the seeds they spit is how these creatures got their name.
