|notes = 8 in total. All need to be place in the pedestals to unlock the final area of the Palace of Shadow.}}The '''Pedestal Keys''' are key items that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==

The Pedestal Keys resemble the [[Palace Key]]s except the Pedestal Keys are blue in color instead of gray.

==History==

There are eight Pedestal Keys in total that can be found and they can only be obtained by solving the various of riddles in the [[Palace of Shadow]]'s [[Riddle Tower]]. After placing the [[Star Key]] on the top, it will activate the eight pedestals where the Pedestal Keys must be place in. After placing all of the Pedestal Keys in place, the final area of the Palace of Shadow will be unlocked.
