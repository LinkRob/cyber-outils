The '''Planet Core''' is a location within the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'']] and [[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]] franchises. While not explicitly shown in most games, it is the penultimate location in [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']], serving as the game's final sublayers of the [[Underground World]]. The deepest layer is also the [[Banandium Root]]'s home.

== History ==
Before the events of the game, [[King K. Rool]] and his minions stole bananas from [[Donkey Kong Island]] and fled to the Underground World in search for the [[Banandium Root]]. But because K. Rool's wish was evil, the Root rejected him. His body was then used as a seal to block access to the Core from the Forbidden Layer. This prevented the Kremlings from leaving the Planet Core and anyone else from entering it.

[[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Pauline]] eventually reached what they thought was the Banandium Root, which [[Void Kong]] attempted to use to fulfill his dreams of becoming rich, but the so-called Banandium Root turned out to be K. Rool's belly. Awoken from his slumber after Void Kong turned Donkey Kong to stone with his remote and Pauline freed Donkey Kong with her singing, K. Rool tossed Void aside and jumped into the core to seize the Banandium Root. 

[[Heart of Gold|There]] he and Donkey Kong faced off, but K. Rool is defeated, at which Donkey Kong wishes for bananas while Pauline wishes to return home. The Banandium Root combines both wishes, forming an elevator of bananas that they ride on. However, King K. Rool is not dead yet, reawakening to give chase.

After K. Rool is defeated for good, the Planet Core can be visited again.
