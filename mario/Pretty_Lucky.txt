'''Pretty Lucky''' is a [[badge]] which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:Pretty_lucky_badge.png|thumb|left]]
In ''Paper Mario'', Pretty Lucky gives [[Mario]] the ability to be "lucky" in combat. Being "lucky" is when an enemy unexpectedly misses its attack. The word "lucky" flashes and [[Mario]] tips his hat in relief. This badge is primarily an upgraded version of the [[Close Call]] badge. There is only one copy of this badge in this game. This copy is found in [[Merluvlee and Merlow's House]] at [[Shooting Star Summit]] and it is priced at five [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]]. Pretty Lucky costs only three [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear. An upgraded form of the badge exists in the form of [[Lucky Day]].

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', Mario can get an unlimited amount of these badges. With the help on [[Ms. Mowz]], Mario can steal Pretty Lucky badges from several enemies. This badge costs two [[Badge Point]]s to wear in this game. This badge does not work on Mario's partners so he will have to get the [[Pretty Lucky P]] badge to protect his party. Later on, Mario can venture off into the [[Rogueport Sewers]] and find Pretty Lucky in an invisible block on the deepest area. Mario can also find it in [[Dazzle]]'s inventory, and she sells it for three Star Pieces rather than five in ''Paper Mario''.

Pretty Lucky was also given a partner-based variant, the [[Pretty Lucky P]].
