The '''3-Up Moon Block''' is a [[block]] introduced in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and again in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is very infrequent, in secret places that are usually hard to find, and can found by using the [[Wii U GamePad]] in [[Boost Mode]]. The player must tap on it to make it pop up, and players using a [[Wii Remote]] need to reach it to pick up the [[3-Up Moon]] inside quickly before the block will retire. There is a 3-Up Moon Block in every level in the game.

In the [[Nintendo Switch]] port, ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', they were removed alongside Boost Mode, and 3-Up Moons are instead obtained by hitting [[block]]s containing [[1-Up Mushroom]]s with [[Toadette]].
