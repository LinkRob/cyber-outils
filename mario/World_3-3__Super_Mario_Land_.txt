}}'''World 3-3''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the third and final level of World 3, the [[Easton Kingdom]].

==Overview==
World 3-3 takes place in the skies with some trees in the distance. [[Ganchan]]s return in this level, attempting to crash into [[Mario]]. There some other enemies present in this level as well, but the real challenge is avoiding falling into the pits. Mario does this until he reaches the end of the level and faces the boss, [[Hiyoihoi]]. Hiyoihoi is a large Tokotoko with legs and attacks by throwing Ganchans at Mario. After defeating Hiyoihoi, Mario advances to the final world, [[Chai Kingdom]].
