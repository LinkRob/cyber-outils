This is a list of [[Saffron]] and [[Dyllis]]' recipes in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. There are a total of 96 recipes in the game. The recipes are listed by the game's order, except that they are separate due to different chefs that can create different dishes. Note: Dyllis' recipes can go together in a different way, for example: '''[[Shroom Shake]]''' and '''[[Smelly Herb]]''' works the same with '''Smelly Herb''' and '''Shroom Shake'''.
