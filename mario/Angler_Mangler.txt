'''Angler Mangler''' is a mini-boss in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==In ''Wario World''==
Angler Mangler is encountered twice in [[Shivering Mountains]]. Whenever [[Wario]] approaches Angler Mangler, it seals him into a wall of ice. If Wario touches the ice, he becomes frozen, and the player has to wiggle the Control Stick to free Wario. Angler Mangler can also create a line of ice chunks to freeze Wario. Upon taking damage, Angler Mangler spits out several ice chunks in all directions.

Angler Mangler cannot take damage from any of Wario's physical attacks. During battle, Angler Mangler summons [[Barrel Buster]]s at Wario, who can attack Angler Mangler by stunning a Barrel Buster and throwing it at the light dangling from Angler Mangler's head. For the first battle, it takes three hits to defeat Angler Mangler, but the second battle slightly increases this to five hits.
