The '''Cyborca''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. Their name is pun on cyborg and orca.

==History==
Cyborcas are blue, whale-like creatures with a metal cap and propeller. They attack by shooting projectiles at [[Wario]]. Cyborcas can only be found in [[Sweatmore Peak]] and are rather rare enemies to encounter as only five of them can be found throughout the area.
