'''Beanerang Bros.''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. In the original version, they are named '''Boomerang Bros.'''.

==Profile==
They are the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] counterpart of [[Boomerang Bro]]s. What is different about their appearance is that they are tall and lanky, with a purple, pointy helmet and a sprout growing out of it, purple shells, black shades and white gloves. Their boomerangs are brown with a pink stripe. In the remake, they wear purple shoes.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
[[File:BoomerangBroMLSS.png|frame|left]]
Beanerang Bros are found in [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] and fight with [[Elite Gritty Goomba]], [[Elite Troopea]]s, [[Sand Cheep]]s and [[Sharkbone]]s.

===Battle===
They fight with attacking the Bros. with their boomerang. If the right side of their shades twinkle, it will hit [[Mario]], but if it twinkles on the left side, it will hit [[Luigi]]. These attacks should be avoided by jumping. They can also immediately throw their boomerangs, or fire them off-screen to attack them from behind.

In the international versions of ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', Beanerang Bros are healed with fire attacks; but in the Japanese version of the game and the 3DS remake, they take normal damage from it.

==In ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser''==
Beanerang Bros appear as enemies in the side story for the 3DS remake, ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]''. They are Ranged troopers, and their Special Skill is Piercing Projectile. They are strong against [[Goomba Tower]]s and [[Pokey]]s.
