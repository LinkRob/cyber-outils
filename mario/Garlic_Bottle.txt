The '''Garlic Bottle''', also named '''Garlic Pot''', is one of the [[Power Up Pot]]s featured in both ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. Collecting one transforms [[Wario]] into [[Bull Wario]]. If Wario is in his [[Small Wario|Small]] form, a Garlic Bottle instead changes him to his usual form, similar to [[Super Mushroom]]s from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]].
