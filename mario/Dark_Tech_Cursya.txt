}}A '''Dark Tech Cursya''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Tech Cursyas are only found in a certain amount of rooms in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Dark Tech Cursyas are stronger versions of [[Tech Cursya]]s and like with them, touching a Dark Tech Cursya directly will result in the victim being inflicted with the [[No Skills]] status. Attacks from a distance such as [[Bowser]]'s fire breath is recommended.
