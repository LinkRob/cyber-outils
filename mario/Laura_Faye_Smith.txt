'''Laura Faye Smith''' (born May 10, 1971) is the current voice actor for [[Rosalina]], replacing [[Kerri Kane]] as of 2013 starting in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. She also plays [[Baby Rosalina]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' and Male Kana in ''Fire Emblem Fates''. She also does some work in theatre.
