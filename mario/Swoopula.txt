'''Swoopula''' is an enemy which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They are a stronger gray variant of [[Swooper]] usually found in dark castles.

==History==
Swoopulas are rather troublesome foes that have only one attack: sucking two HP from [[Mario]] and co. or four in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''. It is located in the [[Crystal Palace]] in ''Paper Mario'' and the [[Palace of Shadow]] or the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''. Swoopulas hang from the ceiling and Mario must get them off by using a hammer attack outside of battle or a partner attack.

Much like with [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]], wearing the [[Zap Tap]] is extremely effective against Swoopulas as they cannot latch onto Mario to suck his health and will take damage instead. Likewise, eating a [[Volt Shroom]] will yield the same results as well.
