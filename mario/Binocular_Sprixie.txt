The '''Binocular Sprixie''' is a character that appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. Her first appearance is in the first stage of the level. Hence her name, she is a Sprixie who tends to carry a blue binocular set with her everywhere she goes.
== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
The Binocular Sprixie is a small pink [[Sprixie]].

===Behavior===
When [[Mario (character)|Mario]] approaches her location on his way through the [[Sprixie Kingdom]], the Binocular Sprixie will look through her binoculars towards the path he must take. Her locations are the following:
*[[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]]:
**[[Super Bell Hill]]: At the checkpoint section. She looks toward the goal when spoken to.
**[[Mount Beanpole]]: At the start of the level, perched on the trees at that location. She directs Mario up the hill.
*[[World 2 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 2]]:
**[[Conkdor Canyon]]: At the checkpoint area, atop a palm tree near a spring. She directs Mario northeast.
*[[World 3 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 3]]:
**[[Switchboard Falls]]: Near the middle of the level. She must be spoken to if Mario wishes to see the [[8-bit Luigi Figure]].
*[[World 4 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 4]]:
**[[Ant Trooper Hill]]: Near the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]] on a raised platform. She directs Mario to east, along the cliff.
*[[World 5 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 5]]:
**[[Sunshine Seaside]]: On a cloud platform. Here, she directs Mario north.
**[[Sprawling Savanna]]: On a raised platform that can be found directly in the middle of the large section.
*[[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]]:
**[[Ty-Foo Flurries]]: Near the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]] area. She directs Mario north.
**[[Bullet Bill Base]]: Just before the [[Warp Box]]. She directs Mario east through the second section.
*[[World 7 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 7]]:
**[[Boiling Blue Bully Belt]]: South of the first area's northern wall.
*[[World Bowser]]:
**[[Cookie Cogworks]]: South of the big cookie gear climbing section. She directs Mario up the wall.
*[[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]]:
**[[Rolling Ride Run]]: Just before the [[Warp Box|teleporter]].
