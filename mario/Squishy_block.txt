'''Squishy blocks''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.

==Description==
Squishy blocks are pinkish-orange [[block]] platforms. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] pushes a squishy block, he can move it. When a squishy block is pushed against a wall, it squishes against it, growing taller and thinner. If a squishy block is [[Ground Pound (move)|ground-pounded]], the opposite occurs. Squishy blocks can be used to travel over [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]].

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
[[File:Squishy block YISMA3 screenshot.png|thumb|left|A transparent squishy block in Endless World of Yoshis in ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'']]
Squishy blocks are uncommon, and they appear in only three [[level]]s: [[Chomp Rock Zone]], [[Sluggy the Unshaven's Fort]], and [[King Bowser's Castle]]. In ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'', there is a unique transparent squishy block in [[Endless World of Yoshis]].
