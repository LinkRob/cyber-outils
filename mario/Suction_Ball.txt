'''Suction Ball''' is the [[Star Pitch]] of [[Birdo]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces her [[Weird Ball]] from ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
Upon using Suction Ball, Birdo first throws the baseball as she normally would, but then attempts to inhale it back into her mouth. As a result, the baseball temporarily moves away from the batter, who should only swing the ball when it returns their way, otherwise they get a strike.
