'''Piranha Patch''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Opening==
A flower bud blooms into a pink flower, then the camera then zooms out to show the entire field.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Piranha Patch Perspective Mode.png|thumb|left|250px|Gameplay in Perspective Mode]]
The object is for the characters to earn the most [[point]]s by collecting flowers within the thirty-second [[time limit]]. There are two types of flowers: pink flowers, which are worth +1 point, gold flowers, which are worth +3 points, and [[Piranha Plant]]s, which are worth -1 point. Piranha Plants, pink flowers, and gold flowers all start out growing from the same type of bud. Flowers disappear five seconds after sprouting, while Piranha Plants disappear only three seconds after sprouting. Characters move slower in the tall grass section, where gold flowers most commonly spawn. Whoever ends with the most points after 30 seconds is the winner.

Piranha Patch also appears in [[Perspective Mode]], in which the camera is zoomed in on the character, making it harder to tell if there are any flower buds nearby.

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules:''' ''Gather as many flowers as you can. Pink flowers are worth one point, and gold flowers are worth three. Be careful: you'll lose a point for each Piranha Plant you pick by mistake!''
