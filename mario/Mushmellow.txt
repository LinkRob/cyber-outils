The '''Mushmellow''' is a [[kart]] appears in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appeared in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

== Description ==
The kart itself seems to be similar to the [[Egg 1]] as they both are a character's trademark as a kart itself. The kart appears to slightly resemble a [[Mushroom Car]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', and is shaped like a [[Super Mushroom]] with small wheels.

The kart's name is a portmanteau of the words "mushroom" and "marshmellow".

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart DS'' ===
[[File:MKDS Mushmellow.png|thumb|left|Toad in his Mushmellow in ''Mario Kart DS''.]]
The Mushmellow is [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s first kart of choice, alongside his [[Standard TD]] and the [[4-Wheel Cradle]]. The Mushmellow features great [[acceleration]], but has low [[speed]], [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]] and [[Items (stat)|items]] stat, whilst [[handling]] and [[drift]] are above average.

Its weight rivals Yoshi's Egg 1, its handling rivals [[Luigi]]'s [[Streamliner]], and its drift rivals [[Bowser]]'s [[Hurricane]]. It shares its low items stat with each character's lighter karts.

During gameplay, only Toad can use the Mushmellow. However, if the player completes all 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix cups, Yoshi and [[Dry Bones]] can use this kart. To let any character use this kart, the player must complete all cups in the [[Engine Class|Mirror Mode]].

Toad in the Mushmellow is use as the staff ghosts for courses [[Shroom Ridge]], {{classic course|GCN|[[Baby Park]]}} and {{classic course|GCN|[[Mushroom Bridge]]}} with an allotted times of 2:05:123, 0:50:920 and 1:30:600 respectively.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
[[File:MKT Toad and Toadette 2.jpg|thumb|x200px|Toad in his Mushmellow racing with [[Toadette]] on {{classic course|3DS|[[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart 7)|Mario Circuit]]}} in ''Mario Kart Tour''.]]
The Mushmellow returns as a normal kart. It appears to use the same tires as the [[Pipe Frame]]. The game also adds in two new variants of the Mushmellow, one of which being the [[Pink Mushmellow]], which uses designs based of [[Toadette]] and is also classified as a Normal kart, and the [[Cheermellow]], which was part of the [[Winter Tour]] as a High-End kart.
