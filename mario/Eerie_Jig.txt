'''Eerie Jig''' is a special move used by [[Remo Con]]s and [[Puppox]]s in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
The enemy walks up to its target and does a "dance" in front of them. Eerie Jig can turn one of [[Mario]]'s party members into a [[Scarecrow]], similarly to [[S'crow Bell]].
