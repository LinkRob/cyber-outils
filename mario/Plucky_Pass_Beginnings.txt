'''Plucky Pass Beginnings '''is the first course in Episode 1, and subsequently, of ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''. This level is set on an island in the sky with the Toad Brigade, one Shy Guy, and a bridge leading up to where the Power Star is.

==Super Gems==
*'''Super Gem 1''': Walk up the alcove near where you start.
*'''Super Gem 2''': After plucking the first blue switch, pluck a [[Turnip]] and throw it at the wall of eight Brick Blocks and one POW Block.
*'''Super Gem 3''': Walk to [[Blue Toad]].
==Optional Mission==
*Captain Toad must uncover the Golden Mushroom in this course. To accomplish this, Captain Toad should use the Super Pickax in this course to destroy the Brick Block below, revealing an alcove where the mushroom can be found. When collected, the objective is checked off on the page this level was on.{{CTTT Nav}}
