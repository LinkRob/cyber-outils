|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}'''Little Mouser's Shop''' is a location that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is an [[Item Shop]] run by a [[Little Mouser]] in [[Dry Dry Outpost]]. It contains the cheapest items available and, in return, purchases items from Mario for a very cheap price. Buying a [[Dusty Hammer]] then a [[Dried Shroom]] from this shop in this order grants Mario to speak with [[Moustafa]].

==Trivia==
*Little Mouser's Shop is one of two Item Shops where it is required to be visited (as it's needed to speak with Moustafa). The other being [[Harry's Shop]] as it is required for Mario to get the [[Toy Train]] in order to travel throughout the different stations in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]].
*The method of purchasing two items before speaking with a certain individual is used again in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' when one wants to speak with [[Don Pianta]] in [[Rogueport]].
