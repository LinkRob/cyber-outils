The '''Bubble Plant''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
The Bubble Plant is a blue plant with a unique shape having four petals and a head that allows him to blow out bubbles.

==History==
The Bubble Plant lives in [[Flower Fields]] in the upper-left area. When [[Mario]] first comes across him, the Bubble Plant offers his assistance but only if Mario feeds him a [[Bubble Berry]] first. When following the set path, Mario should already have acquired the Bubble Berry and giving it to the Bubble Plant allows it to blow a bubble to cross over the thorns. After [[Lakilester]] has joined the party, the Bubble Plant's services are no longer required as the former can cross over spikes/thorns with his ability.
