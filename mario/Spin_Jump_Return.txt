The '''Spin Jump Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] used by [[Mario]] in both ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Mario spins in the manner of a cyclone to head directly in front of the tennis ball and then returns it to his opponent. However, the Spin Jump Return fails if the ball is too far away, being one of few Defensive Power Shots to have this status.
