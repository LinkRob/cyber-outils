'''S. Jump Charge''' is a [[badge]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. One can wear this badge by paying three [[Badge Point|BP]] and using four [[Flower Point|FP]] in battle. If [[Mario]] wears this badge, It charges after his jump attack power, can received by 3.

S. Jump Charge is obtainable in [[Cloudy Climb]]. In the first area of the climb, Mario can scale a tower of clouds floating in the air. Then he can use a moving cloud to access another cloud floating in the air. Here, Mario gets the S. Jump Charge Badge.
