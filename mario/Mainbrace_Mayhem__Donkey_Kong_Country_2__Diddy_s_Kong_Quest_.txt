'''Mainbrace Mayhem''' is the second level appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''. This level also appears as the second in [[Gangplank Galleon]]. It comes after [[Pirate Panic (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Pirate Panic]] and before [[Gangplank Galley (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Gangplank Galley]].

==Overview==
This level is the first appearing rigging level in the game. It has [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] scaling multiple ropes and ship masts in order to progress. Two new enemies, [[Click-Clack]]s and [[Klinger]]s, make their first appearance in this level. The returning enemies are [[Klomp]]s and [[Neek]]s.
