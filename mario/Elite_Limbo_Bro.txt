'''Elite Limbo Bros.''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Mario and Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are a stronger variant of [[Limbo Bro]]s, with which they originally shared a name with, but were renamed to Elite Limbo Bros. in the remake for differentiation.

==Physical appearance==
An Elite Limbo Bro has a body resembling that of a [[Sledge Bro]]'s, or more specifically a Limbo Bro, from which Elite Limbo Bros. can be visually distinguished with their green skin, black helmet, and a purple flame on their torch.

==Battle==
They are found in [[Teehee Valley]] when [[Mario]] arrives there after the [[Joke's End]] without [[Luigi]], but they do not appear on maps. Elite Limbo Bros. accompany [[Elite Dry Bones]]. They attack faster and stronger than regular Limbo Bros..
