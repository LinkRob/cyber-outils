'''EXP Badge A''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' that is a more powerful variation of the [[EXP Badge]]. It can be bought from [[Fawful's Bean 'n' Badge]] for 70 [[bean]]s. Equipping the EXP Badge allows the user to earn 40 percent more [[Experience Point|EXP]] at the end of a battle if they defeat an enemy using a [[Bros. Item]], whereas the EXP Badge only allowed for a 20 percent increase.
