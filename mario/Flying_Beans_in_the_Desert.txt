'''Flying Beans in the Desert''' is the third level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 18.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 1 [[Gritty Goomba]], 3 [[Scaratroopea]]s, and 4 [[Lakipea]]s. The enemy captain is a Scaratroopea.

The 2nd battle contains 1 Gritty Goomba, 2 [[Fly Guy]]s, 3 [[Parabeanie]]s, and 2 Lakipeas. The enemy captain is a Parabeanie.

The final battle contains 2 Gritty Goombas, 2 Beanies, 3 Fly Guys, and 1 Scaratroopea. The enemy captain is the Scaratroopea.

* Fly Guy (Lv: 20-20+)
