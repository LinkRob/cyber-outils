'''Giant Bowser X ''' is a giant form of [[Bowser X]] and stronger variant of [[Giant Bowser]] who appears as a boss in the Battle Ring in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. As with the previous battle, Bowser uses the same attacks, though they are a bit faster, stronger and some have slightly different patterns. 

==Trivia==
*Giant Bowser X also has previously appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' as an attack for Bowser X.
