'''Barbos''' is the mother of all the [[Lurchin]]s in the [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]]. She first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'', as the boss of [[Razor Ridge]], then in ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'' as the boss of [[Cape Codswallop]]. In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3 (Game Boy Advance)|Donkey Kong Country 3]]''{{'}}s [[Game Boy Advance]] remake, Barbos was repurposed as the boss of [[Pacifica]], in favor of Razor Ridge's version-exclusive boss [[Kroctopus]]. In all three appearances, Barbos is fought by [[Enguarde]], given that [[Dixie Kong]] and [[Kiddy Kong]] are incapable of fighting underwater enemies on their own.
