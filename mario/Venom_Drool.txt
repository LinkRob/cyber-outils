|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Vemon Drool''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by a few enemies and bosses in the game.

==Effects==
When Venom Drool is used, a large bubble heads toward the target and bursts open releasing poison on them. Of course, this [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]s whoever is hit by this attack (unless they are wearing an item such as a Safety Pin). This move is similar in appearance to [[Viro Plasm]].
