'''Hyper Spiky Goombas''' are a type of [[Goomba]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. They are olive colored and sport a spike on their head.

Like regular Hyper Goombas, Hyper Spiky Goombas have acquired an ability to charge their attack power. Using this technique, their attack skyrockets to 9.

== Appearances ==
=== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ===
Hyper Spiky Goombas are referred to as "Hyper Spiked Goombas". These enemies have harnessed an ability to charge up their attack and wear a helmet with a spike on it, making them a somewhat formidable foe. They are found along with the other two types of Hyper Goombas, [[Hyper Goomba]] and [[Hyper Paragoomba]], at [[Gusty Gulch]] during chapter 3.

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
Hyper Spiky Goombas are slightly stronger than their ''Paper Mario'' counterparts, but they appear later on during chapter 4. Like the other Hyper enemies in the game, Hyper Spiky Goombas are found along the [[Twilight Trail]].
