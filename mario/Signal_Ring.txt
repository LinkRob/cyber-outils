'''Signal Ring''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
Any user can equip the Signal Ring. In doing so, the Signal Ring makes a noise to indicate that [[Mario]] is close to a hidden [[Treasure chest|treasure box]]. The player can obtain the item after defeating [[Valentina]]; the player can go to the far right house in [[Nimbus Land]].
