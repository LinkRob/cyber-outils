'''Kleptoads''' are enemies exclusively appearing in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They are only seen in [[Hightail Falls Galaxy]] and are located on the secret planet, which a [[Hungry Luma]] creates after being fed with enough [[coin]]s.

==History==
=== ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
When encountered, they will start hopping around their small planet. To get the mission's [[Power Star]], the player must acquire five [[Silver Star]]s, which are carried by several Kleptoads. They can be defeated by jumping on them or being eaten by [[Yoshi]], but once one is defeated, a new one will emerge from the ground again.

== Appearance==
Kleptoads are anthropomorphic frogs with blue skin and tan on their belly marks. They also have white, vertically stretched eyes with black pupils. They wear white gloves & their feet have purple flippers.
