'''Glacial Meltdown''' is a [[minigame]] from ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
The game's objective is to eliminate the others by knocking them off the ice. The characters are on ice, so their traction is reduced. Once his opponents lose their footing, the player who survives the onslaught wins the minigame. [[Lakitu]] may appear, sometimes throwing [[Buzzy Beetle]]s as he watches.

==Controls==
The [[Wii]] Remote is held sideways. The Plus Control Pad is used for movement. To jump, press the 2 button. To attack, press the 1 button.

==Trivia==
*Normally, [[Lakitu]] tosses [[Spiny|Spinies]]. However, this was changed to where he tosses [[Buzzy Beetle]]s as his projectiles, presumably to make a stompable enemy.
