'''Chomp''' is an attack used by [[Wario]] in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, since his playable debut in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. It is his standard special move.

==Description==
The move involves Wario opening his mouth and biting forward. It cannot damage an opponent unless they are next to him. Wario's mouth remains open for as long as the special button is held. By grabbing an opponent, Wario can continuously bite on them, dealing a small amount of damage in each time, but the number of times Wario can bite is relative to the opponent's damage percentage.

Aside from biting opponents, Wario can also use Chomp to eat items, even certain projectiles. Larger items take a longer time for Wario to swallow, and if he eats an explosive, Wario takes 6% damage.
