A '''Miracle Capsule''' is a type of [[Orb|capsule]] that appears only in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. Unlike other capsules, a Miracle Capsule cannot be thrown onto the [[board]] or used by the player on themselves (though it can still be thrown away). If a player obtains three Miracle Capsules, [[Eldstar]] appears, and all of the first-place player's [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s are given to the player in last place. Only the player in last place can receive a third Miracle Capsule, but Miracle Capsules can be collected by any player if stolen by [[Lakitu Capsule]]s or similar, though they still give the first-place player's Stars to the player in last rather than the player who collected the Miracle Capsules.

If the player in first place gets all three Miracle Capsules, the capsules are spent and nothing happens.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TTjG4mEuSB4</ref> If two or more players are tied for first place, a Miracle Capsule chooses a target at random.
