'''Tragic Carpet Ride''' (renamed '''Mind Over Mattermouth''' in Australian and European releases) is a general minigame in'' [[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Opening==
The characters enter a room on a carpet. They gasp as the floor around the carpet dissipates.

==Gameplay==
[[Mattermouth]]s enter the room and start to eat the floor away. The characters must avoid falling into the holes that the Mattermouths leave behind, otherwise they fall down, eliminating them. If a character touches a Mattermouth, they become stunned. Either the last character to fall or the last character remaining wins.

==Controls==
*{{Button|3ds|Stick}} - Move
*{{Button|3ds|A}} - Jump

== Etymology ==
The name, Tragic Carpet Ride is a pun of "magic carpet ride". The PAL name is a pun "mind over matter."

== Trivia ==
*The music, Danger Closing In is reused in [[Tox Box Shocks]].
