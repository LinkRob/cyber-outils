'''3D Green Shell''' is a [[Bros. Attack]] used in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', and it costs 4 BP to be used. The Attack Pieces to obtain the 3D Green Shell are found in [[Mushrise Park]], which are required to all be collected to proceed through the game. It can only target enemies on the ground. The difference between 3D Green Shell and its [[Red Shell]] counterpart, [[3D Red Shell]], is it hits the enemies more randomly and [[Luigi]] initiates and ends the attack.

==Usage==
Upon using the attack, [[Mario]] and Luigi will run into the foreground or FG, where Luigi will take out a Green Shell and kick it at one of the enemies. Mario will kick the Green Shell back at the enemies after it rebounds off the enemy towards him, and Luigi will do the same, this repeating three more times and each time both bros. move toward to the enemies a bit. After the Green Shell hits nine times, Mario will then kick the Green Shell towards Luigi, who blasts it at one of the enemies, ending the attack. If all enemies are defeated before the attack ends, it will end prematurely. When the shell comes to Luigi than press {{button|3DS|B}} and when it comes to Mario press {{button|3DS|A}}.

Striking three, five, nine, and ten times with the Green Shell will yield an "OK!", "Good!", "Great!" and "Excellent!" rating respectively.

== Later Appearances ==
This attack was going to be put into ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', but was cut out for unknown reasons in favour of the [[Fire Flower]]. It returns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', with the number of kicks reduced from 11 to 7, and its final kick now strikes all enemies at once. [[Luigi]] also uses an animation similar to his Super Strike from the [[Mario Strikers (series)|''Mario Strikers'']] games. During the attack, the enemies turn toward the south when Mario and Luigi kick shell.
