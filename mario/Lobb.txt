A '''Lobb''' is an enemy which made its only appearance in ''[[Mario Clash]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Lobb MC sprite.png|frame|left]]
A Lobb is a black box with a skull-and-crossbones emblem on the side. On top of the box is a hole that a white gloved hand can move in and out of. There are also two red wheels attached to the side of the Lobb.

===Behavior===
Their description of being a black box with a retractable hand makes them similar to [[Torpedo Base]]s from ''[[Super Mario World]]'', but instead of throwing [[Torpedo Ted]]s, a Lobb throws [[Big Steely|Iron Ball]]s at [[Mario]].
