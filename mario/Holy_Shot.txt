'''Holy Shot''' is [[White Mage]]'s [[Special Shot]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
White Mage can perform Holy Shot if the player taps out the shape of a star shape on the Touch Screen twice. The move is the White Mage's equivalent to [[Black Mage]]'s [[Meteor Shot]].

White Mage begins the shot by levitating the ball in a circle around her, as four blue symbols magically appear on the ground around her. The symbols start emitting white columns of light and eventually become a bluish-white disk. White Mage touches the ball and casts it high into the air. The entire screen turns white, and the ball comes back down, creating a brilliant blueish-white light show when it enters the basket.

If Holy Shot is performed from outside the three-point line, the ball is cast toward the basket with a gleaming white and blue trail, and it does not leave the screen. Like other Special Shots, if Holy Shot is performed outside the three-point line, a teammate needs to dunk the ball to complete the shot and make the light show appear.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
"Holy" refers to the most powerful [[w:c:finalfantasy:White Magic|White Magic]] spell from the ''[[Final Fantasy]]'' franchise, [[w:c:finalfantasy:Holy (ability)|Holy]]. It was introduced in the first ''[[w:c:finalfantasy:Final Fantasy|Final Fantasy]]'' game and is usually the strongest offensive spell that White Mages can use.
