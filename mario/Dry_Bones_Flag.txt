'''Dry Bones Flag''' is one of the three flags hidden by the [[Three Musty Fears]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The Dry Bones Flag in particular is hidden by [[Dry Bones]], and [[Mario]] can find it by examining his bed in [[Mario Bros. House|Mario's Pad]].
