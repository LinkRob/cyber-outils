'''Big Tail Goombas''' (also called '''Grand Tail Goombas''') are enemies which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' and return in all four {{series|Mario & Luigi}} games for [[Nintendo 3DS]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Big Tail Goombas are a variant of both a [[Grand Goomba]] and a [[Tail Goomba]], and they have a large tanooki tail on their backside. Big Tail Goombas almost always have an angry look on their face, like many types of [[Goomba]]s in general.

=== Behavior ===
Big Tail Goombas use similar attacks to that of a regular Tail Goomba, except they can float much higher, and because their tail is larger, a Big Tail Goomba can swing it a wider range.

== In ''Super Mario 3D Land''==
[[File:Grand Tail Goomba - World 1-1 - Super Mario 3D Land.png|thumb|left|A Big Tail Goomba in World 1-1.]]
Here, they are named Grand Tail Goomba, and are first encountered in [[World 1-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 1-1]]. Big Tail Goombas most prominently appear in [[World 5-5 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 5-5]], where some drop a [[Super Leaf]] upon being defeated. Grand Tail Goombas behave identically to regular Tail Goombas but their tail swing is much stronger, has slightly larger range, and they can also fly higher than a Tail Goomba as well. Grand Tail Goombas however, are no more durable than their regular-sized counterparts and can be defeated with any attack.
{{br|left}}

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''===
Big Tail Goombas first appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' in the ''Mario & Luigi'' series. They are only fought in [[Dreamy Neo Bowser Castle]]. Big Tail Goombas are also never seen on the overworld and are only seen in battle alongside [[Antasman|Antasmen]], [[Lakitu]] and [[Spiny]]s.

==== Battle ====
Big Tail Goombas mainly attack by flying at Mario and then attempt to crash down on him. This attack can be counter-attacked with swinging the hammers.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''===

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''===
Big Tail Goombas appear exclusively in the side story, ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'', as both enemies and recruitable allies. They fight as Melee troopers, and their [[Special Skill]] is [[Spin Cycle]]. The in-game description is "Bulky blokes with high POW and HP. They can sweep up foes by spinning their tails.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''===
[[File:BJJBigTailGoomba.gif|right]]
Big Tail Goombas exclusively appear in the side story ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. Like in ''Minion Quest'', they are enemies and recruitable allies. They are once again Melee troopers and retain Spin Cycle as their Special Skill. They are weak against yellow [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Ninji]]s. Unlike in ''Minion Quest'', only two Big Tail Goombas can be recruited at once.
