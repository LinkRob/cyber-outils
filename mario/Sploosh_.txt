'''Sploosh!''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Objective ==
The player can complete the quest at [[Sushi Cliff]] at [[Seaside Area]]. [[Sushi (character)|Sushi]] tells the player someone fell off the cliff before starting the quest. [[Shroomlock]] emerges out of the water to tell the player someone pulled him into the water. Given the three suspects, [[Cheep Cheep (character)|Cheep Cheep]], [[Blooper (character)|Blooper]] and [[Dolphin (character)|Dolphin]]. Sushi remembers all of their stories, so the player does not need to visit them to ask.

{{Spoiler}}
Given the stories from Sushi, the emphasis is on the body parts. Blooper for tentacles, Dolphin for fins, and Cheep Cheep for gills. Since Shroomlock was pulled by someone, this can be done by using arms making Blooper the real culprit. Blooper said that it was meant to be a prank and apologised to Shroomlock.
{{Endspoiler}}

After completing the quest, Sushi will grant the player the [[Digitizer]] [[Gaddget]].
