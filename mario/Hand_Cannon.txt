The '''Hand Cannon''' is one of [[Geno]]'s personal weapons in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the shop in [[Nimbus Land]] for 105 [[coin]]s, and equipping it on Geno increases his [[Attack]] power by 45. When using the Hand Cannon in battle, Geno shoots out three large bullets from his right elbow.
