|games = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}The '''Rafters''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is located in the highest point of Gloomy Manor and it is the first place where the player must use Gyro controls to move Luigi across narrow beams.

==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-5: Sticky Situation|A-5: Sticky Situation]], Luigi must head into the Rafters in order to finish up the web cleaning through the entire mansion. Luigi must carefully cross over the narrow beams to clean the last bit of webs before completing the mission.

There is also a [[Gem]] located in the room. Luigi must cross over the narrow beams until he reaches an area with a portrait of cheese. The Gem is located right on the waredrobe.[[fr:Grenier]][[de:Dachstuhl]]
