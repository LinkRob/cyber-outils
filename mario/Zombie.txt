A {{Nihongo|'''Zombie'''|ゾンビ|Zonbi|Zombie}} is an undead species of humanoids which appear in the [[Wario Land (series)|''Wario Land'' series]] as enemies. They first appear in ''[[Wario Land II]]''. Zombies have grey or green skin color and live in dark areas.

==In the ''Wario Land'' series==
===''Wario Land II''===
Zombies appear as enemies to [[Wario]]. Zombies will throw their heads Wario becomes [[Zombie Wario]] if he comes in contact with the zombies. Their heads only appear at first glance. If Wario approaches the head, the zombie will spawn and throw their heads three times and continue walking. Wario can defeat zombies by attacking them. However, they will respawn in a few seconds.

===''[[Wario Land 3]]''===
Zombies return as enemies. Like the previous game, Wario becomes Zombie Wario if they touch him. While the zombies retain their behavior, zombies are quicker and more unpredictable than the previous game. Zombie Wario, [[Hot Wario]] or thrown enemies can defeat the zombie.
