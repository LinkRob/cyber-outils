'''Sir Lance-A-Lot''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. 

==History==
They are robots with armor that wield lancers and attack by charging at [[Wario]]. Sir Lance-A-Lots are creations of [[Carpaccio]] that serve him in his quest. They are rather common enemies in the game being first encoutered in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]] and later again in [[Sneezemore Cave]], [[Sweatmore Peak]], and an area leading up to [[Carpaccio's Lab]]. Sir Lance-A-Lots can be defeated with any attack, but Wario must take care not to dash into them while their lances are pointing forward or he will take damage instead.

==Trivia==
*Their name is a pun of Sir Lancelot, a legendary knight.
