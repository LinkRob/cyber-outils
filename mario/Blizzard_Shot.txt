The '''Blizzard Shot''' is an [[Offensive Power Shot]] which exclusively appears in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. It is used by [[Elroy]] in Story mode, and can be unlocked for [[Clay]] and [[Ace]] to use once certain prerequisites have been met. 

==Description==
Upon activating Blizzard Shot, the user spins toward the ball while the background turns into ice. They then hit the ball to the other side of the court. If a receiver returns the shot, they get pushed back and become temporarily frozen within an ice cube, during which they are unable to move.

Clay or Ace must have at least L6 Speed, L6 Response, and L7 Spin before Blizzard Shot can be unlocked for them to use.
