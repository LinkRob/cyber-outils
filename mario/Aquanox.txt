'''Aquanox''' is an aquatic translucent blue [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Spark]]. It appears in [[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope|''Mario'' + ''Rabbids Sparks of Hope'']].

== ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'' ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope|Sparks of Hope]]'', the player can recruit Aquanox after completing a Battle Quest, [[Light Your Fire]]. The Spark does not belong to any hero, meaning any hero can use Aquanox in their team. Its skills consist of [[Splash Attack]] and [[Splash Protector]] to help [[Mario]].
