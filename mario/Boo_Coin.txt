A '''Boo Coin''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

== Description ==
Boo Coins allow for a few types of allied enemies to transform into stronger forms, although doing so requires at least a certain number of Boo Coins.

[[Boo]] is one enemy that can be transformed via a Boo Coin, as spending three Boo Coins turns it into Boo Trio, which in turn can become Boo Crew by spending another six Boo Coins. [[Whomp]] is another enemy that can be transformed from Boo Coins, as spending six of them upgrades it to Whomp & Boos.

==In ''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''==
Boo Coins can be obtained by either defeating [[Boo]]-type enemies or winning them in a [[Toad House]]'s Lucky ? Blocks minigame.
