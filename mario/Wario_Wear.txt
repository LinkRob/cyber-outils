'''Wario Wear''' is a [[microgame]] featured under [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame features a blank white background. The only other features are Wario, who is styled as a paper puppet on a stick, and his clothes falling from above.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of Wario, whom they have to move left and right so that he can get dressed into his clothes as they fall from above. Touching a clothing item automatically puts it onto Wario. Some of Wario's clothing falls slowly from the top of the screen. If Wario successfully lands into every clothing item, the player wins the microgame, but if he misses one, the player loses.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' Wario must grab his shirt, which falls in a straight line. The microgame starts with Wario in only his underwear.
*'''Level 2:''' Wario must grab his shoes and pants, both of which fall in a zigzag. The microgame starts with Wario in only his underwear and shirt.
*'''Level 3:''' Wario must grab his hat and jacket. The hat falls down fast and straight, while the jacket falls in a zigzag, like level 2's clothing items. The microgame starts with Wario wearing his shirt, pants, and shoes.
