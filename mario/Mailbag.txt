The '''Mailbag''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Mailbag can only be found in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] after a [[Shy Guy]] stole it from the [[Postmaster]] at the [[Post Office]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] during the events of Chapter 4. While the Mailbag is optional and does not affect the main storyline, [[Mario]] cannot read his partner's letters without it. If Mario returns the Mailbag to the Postmaster, he rewards Mario with one of the [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]].
