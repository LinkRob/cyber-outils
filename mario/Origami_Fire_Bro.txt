'''Origami Fire Bros.''' are enemies which first appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are [[Fire Bro]]s. in the form of [[Folded Soldiers]].

===Behavior===
In battle, Fire Bros. attack using either [[Fire Throw]], involving their traditional behavior of throwing [[fireball]]s, or [[Super Bro Throw]], which involves lifting another enemy and throwing it at Mario, damaging Mario but defeating the enemy in the process. Much like [[Boomerang Bro]]s. and [[Ice Bro]]s., Fire Bros. have their own unique move, [[Hot Bro Throw]], a variation of Super Bro Throw that first puts the grabbed enemy ablaze it is thrown at [[Mario]].

==In ''Paper Mario: The Origami King''==
Origami Fire Bros. first appear on the path toward the [[Fire Vellumental]] and later return in the [[Sea Tower]].
