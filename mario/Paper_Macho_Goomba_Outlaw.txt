A '''Paper Macho Goomba Outlaw''' is a variation of the [[Paper Macho Goomba]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' that appears in the Big Sho' Theater. Together with the Paper Macho Spike Outlaw and the Paper Macho Snifit Outlaw, this enemy tries to stop Mario from advancing to the second floor of the theater. To defeat the enemy, the player must press  as soon as they see "Fire!" The Paper Macho Goomba Outlaw is the weakest of the three enemies on this floor.
