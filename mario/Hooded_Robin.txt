'''Hooded Robin''' is a character from [[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!|''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'']] who appears as the titular protagonist of the episode [[Hooded Robin and His Mario Men]], the only episode he appeared in.

== Profile ==

=== Personality ===
Being based on the legendary hero Robin Hood, Hooded Robin has similar personality traits to him, being a vigilante who steals from the rich and helps the poor. When [[Bowser]], as the Sherrif of Koopingham, steals [[Coin|Coins]] from Sharewood Village's inhabitants, Hooded Robin makes it his mission to take those Coins back, turning him into an enemy of Bowser.

=== Appearance ===
Hooded Robin resembles an [[Albatoss]], albeit with an anthropomorphic build and human-level intelligence. He has red feathers and a pale red underbelly, and wears a similar green cap to Robin Hood with a feather in it. He carries a brown bow, and a plunger.

== History ==
Hooded Robin makes his debut when some [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa Troopas]] that [[Bowser]] had employed as his "police force" capture [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], and Kinopio (referred to as "[[Toad (character)|Toad]]" in the show). What seems to be Bowser's voice then rings out, telling the Koopa Troopas to get every daisy in Sharewood Forest and bring them to Bowser. When Mario asks why Bowser would tell his minions to fetch flowers, Hooded Robin reveals the voice to really be his, albeit a perfect imitation of Bowser's. Hooded Robin then proceeds to free the group with one of his arrows, which cuts the rope that hoisted them into the air. As the group befriends Hooded Robin, the bird leads Peach, Toad, and the Bros. to Sharewood Village, where it is revealed that Bowser had taken the village's [[Coin|Coins]]. The heroes agree to help him in his mission.

Hooded Robin leads the group to the toll booth near Koopingham Castle, where Bowser and his minions have set up their base. Hooded Robin and his new allies proceed to ambush the Koopas, but a Koopa Troopa tricks Hooded Robin into caging himself in the wagon where Bowser had stashed the money. Hooded Robin is subsequently brought to his prison, a bird cage in Koopingham Castle, though Mario and his friends spring him free upon breaking their way in.

Freed from his bondage, Hooded Robin proceeds to help the group find where the stolen money is kept. The group finds a vault containing the coins, but [[Fryguy]] is guarding them. Inspired by his ability to imitate Bowser, Princess Peach proposes that Hooded Robin could distract Fryguy by disguising himself as a female.  Hooded Robin and Toad proceed to disguise themselves as a "[[Frygal]]" to distract Fryguy and lure him. However, their disguise fails and Fryguy, in his rage, attacks Hooded Robin. Hooded Robin lures Fryguy into the vault where the money is kept, but Fryguy gets his revenge by smelting the money with his fire breath, which Hooded Robin finds to his horror. Mario and Luigi use a water pipe to drain the liquefied money and turn it back into actual coins, siphoning them back to Sharewood Village so Hooded Robin can complete his mission. His mission complete, Hooded Robin proposes a feast for defeating Bowser.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Hooded Robin is based on the legendary hero Robin Hood. His name and hat are also derived from Robin Hood.

== Trivia ==

* The episode's planned title may imply that Hooded Robin was originally going to be Robin Hood himself before he was changed to an avian design.
