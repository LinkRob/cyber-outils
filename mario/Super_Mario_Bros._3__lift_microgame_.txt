'''Super Mario Bros. 3''', originally titled '''Super Mario Bros. 3-Lift''', is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'', as one of the microgames under [[9-Volt]]'s [[Spintendo Classics]] folder, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', now as one of [[18-Volt]]'s microgames.

==Layout==
The microgame is based on ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''{{'}}s original [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version. It features [[Mario]] standing on a [[Rail Lift]] placed on a circular [[track]] in an area with a clear blue sky consisting of only two clouds. There is a [[Warp Pipe]] at the bottom on the left and right sides each, but neither of them can be used during the microgame.

The HUD from ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' is displayed at the bottom. In ''WarioWare: Twisted!'', only the right side of the player display at the bottom-left corner is visible, due to it being obscured by a [[Bomb (WarioWare series)|bomb]]. In ''WarioWare Gold,'' the counter is no longer obscured, and it is shown to display an "M," for Mario.

==Gameplay==
The player has to move the Rail Lift that [[Mario]] is on to prevent him from being hit by enemies, which can include [[Bullet Bill]]s, jumping [[Cheep Cheep]]s, and [[Venus Fire Trap]]s. In ''WarioWare Gold'', [[Rocky Wrench]]es are another enemy that has to be avoided.

*'''Level 1:''' The Rail Lift has a big rotating capacity, making it easy to avoid enemies.
*'''Level 2:''' The Rail Lift has a smaller rotating capacity, making it harder to avoid enemies.
*'''Level 3:''' While the layout is shared with level 1, the player now also has to protect [[Luigi]], who is standing on a separate Rail Lift.
