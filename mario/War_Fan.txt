The '''War Fan''' is a weapon only usable by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the shop in [[Nimbus Land]] for 100 [[coin]]s. The War Fan has an [[Attack]] rating of 60 points.
