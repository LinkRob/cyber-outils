'''Warp Tunnel''' is a special technology used by the spaceship [[WM ARC]] during [[Mario]] and the others' journey throughout the galaxy in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''. The Warp Tunnel serves as a major plot device.

== Usage ==
According to [[Jeanie]], due to the sheer distance to [[Cursa]]'s stronghold, using the warp tunnels to quickly travel through the galaxy is the only viable option, which requires powers from purified [[Darkmess]] energy collected in the regions the spacecraft landed. The tunnel are also two-sided and will not close after passing through, enabling the player to travel back to past areas.
