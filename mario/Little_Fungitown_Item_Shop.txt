'''Little Fungitown Item Shop''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
The location is found near the entrance to [[Little Fungitown]]. Like the [[Castle Town Item Shop]], the Little Fungitown Item Shop sells consumable items, but its stock is updated less often because Little Fungitown is first accessed in a later part of [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s journey than [[Beanbean Castle Town]], where the Castle Town Item Shop is. The Little Fungitown Item Shop's inventory is only updated twice, adding only one item each time.
