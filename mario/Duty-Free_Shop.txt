The '''Duty-Free Shop''' is an area on the [[MSS Sea Star|cruise ship]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.  Here, the player can look at records, collectible figurines, sound effects that the player has collected with [[Cruise Mileage Point]]s. There is a computer that is used to purchase souvenirs, sound effects, characters, and [[minigame]]s, as well as a [[Travel Diary]], which holds game information. The shop is hosted by [[Toadsworth]]. There is a picture of [[Peach's Castle]] in the back.

When looking at souvenirs, if players say "Surprise" in the microphone, the object plays an animation.
