'''Honeybee Hive''' ('''Honeybee House''' in Australia and Europe) is one of the three new battle stages in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. It is [[Queen Bee]]'s stage.

The stage is in a bee hive with [[Honeycomb Wall]]s. Honey may drip from the ceiling occasionally, making honey puddles appear on the floor, which, if racers drive over them, can cause the racers to slow down. [[Item Box]]es are found throughout the stage. In the middle of the stage, there is a pipe that shoots strong gust of air out, taking drivers who drive over it to a different part of the stage. The place is covered with [[Stingby|Stingbies]].If you drive into a Stingbee, they will cause you to spin-out.
