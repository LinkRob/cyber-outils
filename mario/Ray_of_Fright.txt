'''Ray of Fright''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
The solo character tries to hit the team of three with lasers, surprising each of them in the process, but is unsuccessful at hitting them.

==Gameplay==
Each of the three team members must avoid touching the light lasers blasted from the solo character's laser cannon. The light lasers ricochet off the surrounding walls, and they last for only a few seconds and extend to only a certain length before then. The solo character can rotate the cannon, allowing them to attack from different angles. If a team member is hit by a laser or touches a glowing end of the cannon, they are eliminated. The solo character wins if they eliminate the team members, although if at least one team member remains when the 30-second [[time limit]] is up, the team wins.

==Ending==
If the solo character wins, the game camera focuses on them as they perform their victory animation from inside of the laser cannon. If the team wins, they each perform their respective victory animation in the arena.

==Controls==
===Solo===
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Fire the laser
* {{button|gcn|R}}: Rotate clockwise
* {{button|gcn|L}}: Rotate counterclockwise

===Team===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''One player controls a laser cannon. The other three have to avoid getting zapped by blasts of light!''
*'''Advice 1''': ''The player controlling the laser should consider the angles of the arena walls when firing.''
*'''Advice 2''': ''Don't touch the glowing end of the laser cannon, or you're out of the game.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on the phrase "ray of light."
