'''Snowman Wario''',<ref>''Wario Land 3'' instruction booklet. Page 24.</ref><ref>''Wario Land 4'' European instruction booklet. Page 22.</ref> also known as '''Snow Wario''',<ref>''Nintendo Power Advance'' V.3. Page 52.</ref><ref>[http://web.archive.org/web/20021024232133/http://www.nintendo.com:80/cs?rh=viewgps&sucessURL=/games/gamepage/strategy.jsp&parentId=629&xslt_gps_topic_id=0&xslt_gps_subtopic_id=7&xslt_gps_header_id=-1& Official North American webpage]</ref>, is one of [[Wario]]'s many reactions in the [[Wario Land (series)|''Wario Land'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Wario Land 3]]'' and returns in ''[[Wario Land 4]]'' and ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Description==
Snowman Wario is triggered by having a snowdrift fall on Wario's head. He is then covered in snow, reducing his walking speed and [[jump]]ing height, but allowing him to defeat enemies when he touches them. Also, in a similar manner to rolling, if he steps on a slope, he will become an uncontrollable rolling snowball that is able to break [[Snowman Block]]s. When he hits a wall in this state, he will return to normal.
