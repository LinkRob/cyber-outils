The '''Black Jibberjay''' is a [[Jibberjay]] and a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. He is the fastest of the Jibberjays as well as their leader. He races [[Mario]] on two separate occasions, and he and the other Jibberjays send Mario a letter, inviting him to do so beforehand each time.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
The Black Jibberjay is first encountered in the [[Wild Glide Galaxy]]. He is raced during the mission [[Jungle Fluzzard Race]], in which Mario has to ride [[Fluzzard]] to the finish line at the end before Black Jibberjay and the other Jibberjay contestants in order to win. When Mario wins, the Black Jibberjay rewards Mario with a [[Power Star]] as a trophy and declares him the new leader of the Jibberjays.

Mario races the Black Jibberjay a second time in the [[Fleet Glide Galaxy]], during the mission [[Fastest Feathers in the Galaxy]]. Unlike last time, Mario only races the Black Jibberjay, not the other Jibberyjay racers. When Mario wins again, the Black Jibberjay gives him another Power Star.
