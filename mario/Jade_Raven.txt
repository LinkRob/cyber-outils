The '''Jade Raven''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Its appearance is similar to that of a [[Raven]].

==History==
The Jade Raven is obtained from the [[Yoshi Chief]] after rescuing the [[The Fearsome Five]] after they got lost in [[Jade Jungle]]. The Yoshi Chief tells [[Mario]] that, if he needs help to get into [[Mt. Lavalava]], he should use the Jade Raven. The Jade Raven can be used on a large statue of a Raven deep in the Jade Jungle, and doing so is necessary in order to speak with the leader of the Ravens, [[Raphael the Raven]].
