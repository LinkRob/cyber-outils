'''1-UP Studio''' (formerly known as '''Brownie Brown''' until 2013) is a video game company entirely owned by [[Nintendo]]. It is based in Tokyo, Japan, and was founded by many previous [[Square Enix]] staff that retired due to creative differences. The company has also developed mainly JRPGs for handheld game systems.

==History==
Part of the development for ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' was outsourced to 1-UP Studio in order to have the game released for the 2011 Holiday season. Director [[Koichi Hayashida]] supervised the team to make sure that they would understand Nintendo's philosophy in designing games and work in harmony with the main development team. 1-UP Studio was also the developer for ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.
