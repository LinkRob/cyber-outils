'''Timing is Money''' is an unlockable Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The minigame derives from "time is money".

== Opening ==
The roulette is active and the camera zooms out. The players look pleased before the minigame starts.

== Gameplay ==
Players can collect [[coin]]s by [[Ground Pound|ground pounding]] at the right time. The player may get 1 coin, 2 coins, 3 coins, 5 coins or a [[Bob-omb]]. Players will lose all of their coins if they ground pound resulting a Bob-omb.
== Ending ==
At the end of the minigame, if the player ground pounded after the game ends, the roulette will stop moving. If the player had not ground pounded after the minigame, the roulette will still spin. Players that successfully collected coins will do their victory pose, while the players that have 0 coins will do their losing pose.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} + {{button|ns|jc-right}} – Ground Pound
