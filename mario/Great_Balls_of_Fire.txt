The '''Great Balls of Fire''' are large, powerful [[fireball]]s which are featured prominently in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Fire of Hercufleas]]." Their name is a reference to the {{wp|Great Balls of Fire|Jerry Lewis song of the same name}}.

==Description==
Located in the city of [[Mythis]], the Great Balls of Fire are guarded by the hero [[Hercufleas]], who keeps them in his acropolis home. The Great Balls of Fire are evidently more powerful than regular fireballs, as they are shown to explode violently upon contact with an object, creating an explosion powerful enough to destroy a stone wall and pillars.

The Great Balls of Fire can also be controlled mentally by whoever is in possession of them, as [[King Koopa (DiC cartoons)|King Koopa]] is shown to make several of the Great Balls of Fire chase [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] by simply concentrating and making gestures with his hands. The Great Balls of Fire are also finite, meaning that they will eventually be extinguished if used too many times. If the Great Balls run out, they can apparently be ignited once more by simply placing fire in the urn the Great Balls of Fire are kept in.

==Trivia==
*During the fight against [[King Koopa (DiC cartoons)|King Koopa]], the song "{{wp|Great Balls of Fire}}" is played, though it is edited out for later airings with "[[It's a Dog's Life]]" from ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.
