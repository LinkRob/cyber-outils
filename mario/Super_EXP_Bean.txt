'''Super EXP Beans''' are an obtainable item in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Locations==

Super EXP Beans can be gained by completing levels. Better Super EXP Beans are gained in harder levels.

*Super EXP Bean S can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 11 or higher.
*Super EXP Bean M can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 16 or higher.
*Super EXP Bean L can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 25 or higher.
*Super EXP Bean DX can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 30 or higher.

==Types==

===Super EXP Bean S===

The Super EXP Bean S gives one recruited troop 30 EXP. 

===Super EXP Bean M===

The Super EXP Bean M gives one recruited troop 300 EXP.

===Super EXP Bean L===

The Super EXP Bean L gives one recruited troop 2000 EXP.

===Super EXP Bean DX===

The Super EXP Bean DX gives one recruited troop 5000 EXP.

==Flavor text==

*The Super EXP Bean S in-game description is: "A bean that grants 30 EXP, regardless of unit type."
*The Super EXP Bean M in-game description is: "A bean that grants 300 EXP, regardless of unit type."
*The Super EXP Bean L in-game description is: "A bean that grants 2,000 EXP, regardless of unit type."
*The Super EXP Bean DX in-game description is: "A bean that grants 5,000 EXP, regardless of unit type."
