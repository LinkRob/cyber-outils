|fp      = 0}}'''Shroom Choco-bars''' are items found in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are upgraded versions of [[Sweet Choco-bar]]s, healing 15 HP instead of 5. They can be used to feed [[Squirps]] in Chapter 4-2, as well as Sweet Choco-bars and [[Golden Choco-bar]]s. They can also be made by [[Dyllis]] by mixing a [[Mild Cocoa Bean]] and a [[Shroom Shake]].
