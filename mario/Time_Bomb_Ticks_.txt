'''Time Bomb Ticks!''' is a minigame in ''[[Mario Party-e]]''.

==Gameplay==
Time Bomb Ticks! is a two-player minigame in which the first player plays as [[Waluigi]] and the second player plays as [[Mario]]. Waluigi must stop the clock by pressing buttons in the correct order from 1 to 16. Whatever time is left on the clock is given to Mario, who must do the same thing as the clock counts down. If Waluigi hits the numbers in the wrong order, the time remaining for Mario increases, and if Mario hits the wrong order, the remaining time decreases even faster. Waluigi or Mario can win the minigame by either stopping the clock or by getting the other character to blow it up by not pressing the buttons in time.
