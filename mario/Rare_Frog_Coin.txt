The '''Rare Frog Coin''' is a particular [[Frog Coin]] owned by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The Rare Frog Coin has the same appearance as a regular Frog Coin, but it plays a unique minor role during the story.

==History==
[[Frogfucius]] gave it to Mallow so that he can buy a [[Cricket Pie]]. At one point, [[Croco]] steals the Rare Frog Coin, and both [[Mario]] and Mallow recover it after chasing Croco down [[Bandit's Way]]. Once they recover the Rare Frog Coin, Mario and Mallow can trade it in at the shop in [[Toad Town|Mushroom Kingdom]] for a Cricket Pie.
