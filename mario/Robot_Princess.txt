The '''Robot Princess''' is a robot created by [[Kooky von Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Kooky von Koopa]] in the likeness of [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]]. The Robot Princess only appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Reign Storm]]." The reason why the Robot Princess was created was so that [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] could attempt to take over the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
The Robot Princess bears a near-exact appearance of Princess Toadstool aside from bolts and hinges on her body.
