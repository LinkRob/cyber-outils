'''Whimsical Waters''' is a board in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Description==
The board takes place in an underwater area inhabited by [[Dragoneel]]s. The mid-boss is a [[Mega Cheep Chomp]], while the boss at the end is a [[Mega Blooper]]. Many of the gimmicks involve Dragoneels, such as one where players choose a chest.
