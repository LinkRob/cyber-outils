The '''Ultrasyrup Jar''' is an item featured in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. When consumed, the Ultrasyrup Jar restores a certain amount of [[Bros. Point|BP]] to its user.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' / ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''==
In these games, they are called '''Ultra Syrups'''. When one consumes it, it restores 100 [[Bros. Point|BP]], or 55 BP in the Japanese version. They are sold at the [[Castle Town Item Shop]] for 80 coins after [[Princess Peach]] is kidnapped. [[Birdo]] has a chance of dropping the item with 100%, [[Magikoopa]]s has 22.58% chance, [[????]]'s Sharkbone variants have 16.13% chance and [[Sniper Bill]] with 12.9% chance.

In the remake, the Ultrasyrup Jar restores 60 BP, slightly more than the original's Japanese version.
