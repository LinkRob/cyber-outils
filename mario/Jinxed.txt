|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Jinxed''' is a special attack used only by [[Jinx]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Jinxed is used, Jinx will attack his target with many attacks ending with a dropkick.

==Effects==
With Jinxed, Jinx can deal moderate damage to one of Mario's party. Also being a physical attack, this move is blockable. Jinxed is similar to [[Triple Kick]].
