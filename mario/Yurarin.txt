'''Yurarins''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. Yurarins are seahorse-dragon enemies that are only encountered in [[World 2-3 (Super Mario Land)|World 2-3]] of the [[Muda Kingdom]]. Their leader is [[Dragonzamasu]].

==History==
Yurarins float slowly in the water. [[Mario]] can very easily swim by them or shoot missiles at Yurarins using his [[Marine Pop]] to defeat them. Defeating a Yurarin earns the player 400 [[point]]s. Yurarins have stronger variants named [[Yurarin Boo]]s, which appear in the same level and shoot [[fireball]]s to attack. Both Yurarins and Yurarin Boos share the same sprites.
