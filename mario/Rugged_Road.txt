==Layout==
Unlike all of the other levels in World 5 so far, Rugged Road doesn't not take place in the jungle. It mainly takes place on a long, dirt road covered with lava within the mountains.

==History==
After exploring the [[Chomp Ruins]], [[Mario]] clears through the jungle and steps into the heat of Rugged Road. As he treads across the burning path, Mario must avoid falling rocks that rain in from above. Mario eventually works around the hot path and nabs the [[Comet Piece]] to clear the level.
