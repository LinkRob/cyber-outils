'''Pine T.''' is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
He is the father of [[Pine T. Jr.]] After [[Mario]] took on Pine T. Jr.'s [[Trouble Center|trouble]], he found Pine T. lying unconscious on the ground of Floor 18 of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] revealing he did so to gather treasure in hopes of supporting his family after losing his previous job. Mario rescued him and completed Pine T. Jr.'s trouble. Pine T. Sr. then intends on finding a job instead of putting his health at risk.

As later revealed in a email sent by his son, Pine T. Sr. managed to get a job working on the [[Li'l-Oink Farm]]. Pine T. Jr. mentions that his father has been recharged thanks to his new job and his son intends on helping him more.
