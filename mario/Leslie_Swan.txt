'''Leslie Swan''' (born June 8, 1970) is a former employee at Nintendo. She work as the Senior Editor of ''Nintendo Power'', and a Nintendo of America localization manager at Treehouse for numerous titles both within and outside the ''Super Mario'' franchise. She was also the voice of Princess Peach in [[Super Mario 64|''Super Mario 64'']], [[Mario Kart 64|''Mario Kart 64'']], ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]].'' She was later replaced by [[Jen Taylor]] in [[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]].

She would eventually resigned as localization director in 2015 for all ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]] tiles,'' though she remains in Nintendo of America.
