'''Petbottom''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Petbottom resembles a bottle full of a pink liquid, with a sharp spear attached to the front of the cap. They have a pair of legs, allowing them to walk back and forth.

===Behavior===
A Petbottom can damage [[Wario]] from the spear attached to it.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Wario only encounters Petbottoms in the [[Ruby Passage]]. [[Wario]] can defeat a Petbottom by either [[Ground Pound (move)|Smash Attacking]] it from above, [[Dash Attack|ramming]] it from behind, or by performing a nearby [[Super Smash Attack]], in which case the Petbottom is flipped upside-down, allowing Wario to attack it from below.
