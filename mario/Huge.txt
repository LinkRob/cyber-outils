==History==
When someone has the Huge status, their attack power will be increased by two for a couple of turns. The [[Power Punch]] item is a way to get the Huge status. Enemies such as [[X-Naut]]s and [[Magikoopa]]s can inflict Huge on themselves and others (though X-Nauts mainly do it to themselves as they drink potions).
