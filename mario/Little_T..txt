|race = [[Toad (species)|Toad]]}}'''Little T.''' is a character from ''[[Paper Mario (game)|Paper Mario]]''. He is a young [[Toad (species)|Toad]].

==History==
Little T. is never directly encountered in game, but he does send [[Mario]] a [[letter]] with a picture of himself on it. Little T. mentions that he is training at [[Toad Town Dojo|The Dojo]] where he hopes that he can defeat [[Bowser]] and save [[Princess Peach]] one day.
