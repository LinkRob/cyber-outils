== About ==
Cat Piranha Creepers are Cat Versions of [[Piranha Creeper|Piranha Creepers]] that first appeared in [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]].






== Appearance ==
Their appearance is similar to [[Piranha Creeper|Piranha Creepers]], but they now have purple fur with white spots on their flower & white-tipped cat ears.
