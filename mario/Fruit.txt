'''Fruits''' are an organic real-world food which have appeared throughout the {{franchise|Mario}} and its spinoffs. They make their overall debut in ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]]''. Most fruits in ''Mario'' media exist in the real-world, such as [[apple]]s and [[pineapple]]s, although fictional fruits have also occasionally appeared, such as [[Heart Fruit]]s in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. Fruits tend to be a favorite food of [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]], something that was established in ''Yoshi's Story''.

Some notable appearances of fruit in general include ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'', and even a few crossover games such as ''[[Nintendo Land]]'' and ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]''.

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', there is a round-shaped item named Fruit that appears inside of [[Yoob's Belly]]. A [[Yellow Yoshi]] eats it to gain the strength to help push a [[Chomp Rock]]. 

In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', the [[Fruit Cup]] was added in as part of the [[Booster Course Pass]]. There are also fruits in ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' as well.
