'''Piranha Plants on Parade''' is the second [[level]] of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==

At the start, there is a row of  13 [[Jump Block]]s, and a pipe with a [[Trottin' Piranha Plant]]. Further along are [[Block]] and a [[? Block]]. There is a coin in the Block, and a [[Fire Flower]] in the ? Block. 

Past the Blocks, there is a row of 14 Jump Blocks. Above the 4th-11th Jump Blocks are 8 [[Coin]]s. At the end up the Jump Blocks is another pipe with a Trottin' Piranha Plant. 

Further along, there are 3 ? Blocks, with Coins, and there is a Block above the middle ? Block. In the Block is a flower that releases 10 Coins, and 1 Wonder Coin. To the right is a pipe with a Trottin' Piranha Plant, and a red pipe that leads into the background.  

In the background, there are 3 ? Blocks, with the middle one containing a Fire Flower. The 2 on the outside contain Coins. To the left is a flower that releases 12 Coins onto the 7th-19th Jump Blocks of a 21 Jump Block run. Further left is a set of 4 pipes with 6 Coins above them.  

Back in the foreground, to the right of the red pipe, are 2 ledges with 2 Coins between them. Below the ledges are 2 Trottin' Piranha Plants. To the right of the Trottin' Piranha Plants is a row of 19 Jump Blocks. To the right is a [[Checkpoint Flag]].   

To the right of the Checkpoint Flag is a row of 10 Jump Blocks.  Inside each of the Jump Blocks is a Coin. Above the 5th-7th Jump Blocks is a row of 2 Blocks, with a ? Block in the middle. In the ? Block is a Fire Flower. Above the Blocks is a ledge that has Trottin' Piranha Plants. There is a pipe facing down on the right of the ledge that lets out Trottin' Piranha Plants, and a pipe on the left that intakes Trottin' Piranha Plants.   

To the right is a row of 12 Jump Blocks with a pipe that contains a Trottin' Piranha Plant on the end. To the right is another pipe with 2 Coins above it.   

===Wonder Seed===
By activating the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]], the screen begins scrolling right, and singing Trottin' Piranha Plants emerge from pipes.

In between a pipe, and another on the right, the Wonder Flower moves along a set of 9 Jump Blocks. After activating the Wonder Flower, the 3 pipes on the right of it drop down. To the right of the pipes is a set of 10 Jump Blocks followed by a pipe, with a Flower Coin above the 2nd-9th Jump Blocks. After the pipe is another set 9 Jump Blocks, with Flower Coins on the 2nd-8th Jump Blocks. This is followed by 3 more pipes. Above, are 5 more pipes, where Trottin' Piranha Plants pop out. 3 Trottin' Piranha Plants also come out of the  3 pipes near the Wonder Flower, as those pipes rise into the air.    

The 3 pipes to the of the set of 9 Jump Blocks pop up, and the 1st one releases a Trottin' Piranha Plant. Above, there are two pipes. The one on the right, which has another pipe to the right of it, releases three Trottin' Piranha Plant to walk around on the ceiling, while the one on the left has the Trottin' Piranha Plants exit the area. 2 green pipes, and 1 red pipe appear from the foreground, and these can be moved through. Below is a set of 9 Jump Blocks, with a Flower Coins on the 2nd-8th Jump Blocks. This is immediately followed by 2 normal pipes popping up, and 1 large pipe popping down.    

This is followed by 2 sets of 8 Jump Blocks, with the second set being lowered one block from the 1st. The 1st set contains 7 Flower Coins on the 2nd-8th Jump Blocks, while the 2nd set contains Flower Coin from the 1st-7th Jump Blocks. A Large Trottin' Piranha Plant appears from the large pipe. Below the 2 sets of jump blocks are 4 Large Trottin' Piranha Plants. Above the Jump Blocks is a are 2 Long ? Blocks. The 1st one is above the 8th Jump Block of the 1st set through the 1st and 2nd Jump Blocks of the 2nd set. The 2nd Long ? Block is above the 3rd-5th Jump Blocks of the 2nd sets.   

This level contains a 3rd Wonder Seed by completing the [[Red Flagpole]]. The Red Flagpole can only be accessed by starting the [[Wonder Effect]], or by using the Add ! Blocks [[Badge]].

== [[10-Flower Coin|10-flower coin]] locations ==
The first 10-flower coin can be found after entering a red [[Warp Pipe]] which teleports the player to the background of the level.

The second 10-flower coin is found at the end of a row of [[Marimba Blocks]] which can be found right before the [[Checkpoint Flag]].

The last 10-flower coin can be found in an alcove guarded by [[Trottin' Piranha Plant|Trottin' Piranha Plants]].

==Trivia ==
*This is the first level in ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' to have a [[Red Flagpole]].
*This level's title is a reference to the level in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' titled [[Piranha Plants on Ice]].
