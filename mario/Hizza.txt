'''Pizza''' is a character who is only mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Hizza is a large, two-headed snake that [[Luigi]] encounters during his [[Super Luigi|journey]] in the [[Waffle Kingdom]] to collect the pieces of the [[Marvelous Compass]]. Hizza was known to terrorize the people of [[Plumpbelly Village]] forcing them to offer up maidens to it to ensure the people won't be hurt. Eventually, the daughter of the mayor of Plumpbelly Village was offered up next, but Luigi decides to take her place to trick the snake. Accompanied by [[Jerry (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|Jerry]], Luigi heads for Hizza's lair to fight the large snake. Eventually, Luigi was able to defeat Hizza by tricking it's heads into eating each other and Luigi is rewarded with a piece of the Marvelous Compass by the mayor.
