'''Airship 1-Up Rally''' is a 1-Up Rally Challenge in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. It appears to take place at [[The Mighty Cannonship]].

== Objective ==
The object for the player's character is to collect as many [[extra life|1-Ups]] within 40 seconds. This can be accomplished by jumping on the [[Mechakoopa]]s or [[cannonball]]s to get 1-Ups. The player succeeds long enough, they earn a gold medal.
