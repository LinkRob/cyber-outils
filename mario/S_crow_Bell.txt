|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''S'crow Bell''' is a special move used by [[Sling Shy]] and [[Carroboscis]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
With S'crow Bell, the user is able to turn one of [[Mario]]'s party into [[scarecrow]]s making them only use special moves. Wearing a Trueform Pin can counter this status ailment, though.
