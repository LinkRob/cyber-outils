'''Abby Trott''' is a voice actress and singer who lives in Los Angeles at California.<ref name="at-twitter"/> She is known for voicing Ivy in ''Carmen Sandiego'' and [[w:c:fireemblem:Annette|Annette]] from ''[[w:c:fireemblem:Fire Emblem: Three Houses|Fire Emblem: Three Houses]]''. Abby Trott was also known to be the English vocalist for [[Lifelight]] for ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
