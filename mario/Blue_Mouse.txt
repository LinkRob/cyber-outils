'''Blue Mice''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Blue Mice are a type of [[Mouse (ghost)|Mouse]] [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] with a light-blue body.

===Behavior===
Blue Mice only travel along the floors in most of the hallways.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Blue Mice, like their [[Purple Mouse|purple]] counterpart, are usually encountered in the hallways. If [[Luigi]] touches a Blue Mouse, he takes five [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage. Because Blue Mice lack any HP, they can be instantly vacuumed into the [[Poltergust 3000]], like with Purple and [[Gold Mouse|Gold Mice]] as well. A Blue Mouse can be defeated instantly using any element.

In the PAL version's [[Hidden Mansion]], Blue Mice frequently appear throughout the different rooms in general.
