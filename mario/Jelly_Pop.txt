'''Jelly Pop''' is a [[recipe]] item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Jelly Pops can be made by giving [[Tayce T.]] a [[Jammin' Jelly]] and a [[Cake Mix]]. Jelly Pops restore a total of 64 [[Flower Point|FP]], more than any other item that restores FP. This number could be a reference to the [[Nintendo 64]], the console that ''Paper Mario'' was initially released for.
