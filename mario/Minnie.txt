==History==
Minnie is a young [[Flip-Flopian]] that can be found on the second floor of [[Flipside]]. She often believed in fortune telling until to the end the game, she learns not to rely on them so much. She could not find her path, so she decided to ask the fortune-teller.

==Trivia==
*Her [[Flopside]] counterpart is [[Winnie]].
