'''Apple Drop''' is one of the [[microgame]]s part of [[Ashley]]'s Food level in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to make every [[apple]] fall off the tree, either by touching them or shaking the tree. In Level 1, there are five apples to shake down, in Level 2 there are seven apples, and lastly in Level 3 there are eleven apples.

[[Kat and Ana]], [[Orbulon]], and [[Red (WarioWare series)|Red]] are listed as best suited for the microgame. Kat and Ana's constant jumping and their shuriken projectiles allow them to reach the apples more easily. For Orbulon, he can use his [[Oinker]]'s tractor beam to quickly knock the apples off the tree. Red's [[bomb]] can shake the tree very fast. [[Mike]] and [[Ashley]] are listed as bad fits, although not on the lowest tier, because their respective projectiles do not have the efficiency to shake the tree by a lot.

In two player mode, Levels 1 and 2 are the equivalent of Levels 2 and 3 in single-player respectively. Level 3 features fifteen apples on the tree. Whoever drops more apples receives an effort point.

There is one Mission associated with the microgame titled Tranquil Tree, in which the player must have [[Mona]] complete the microgame on Level 3 without shaking the tree.
