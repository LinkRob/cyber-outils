'''Blue Donut Lifts''' are a blue variety of the [[Donut Lift]] found in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', they function similarly to the regular Donut Lifts with a major difference being that they're much more lighter and fall with an instant step.

==History==
===''Super Princess Peach''===
Blue Donut Lifts appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. In this game, they can be found in several levels of the game's eight worlds functioning similarly to the brown [[Donut Lift]]s however unlike those Donut Lifts, the Blue Donut Lifts are much lighter and will fall the moment [[Peach]] stands on them at an exteremly quick speed. They do respawn after a few seconds allowing Peach to make another attempt at crossing if she slips up, due to their rapid falling speeds, [[Peach]] will either need to consistently [[jump]] across the Blue Donut Lifts to make it across or run along them using the [[Gloom]] [[Vibe]]. Most of the time, crossing the Blue Donut Lifts is necessary for progressing through the level however in some cases, Blue Donut Lifts often set the path where a [[Toad Box]] or other item is located, other times, Peach can use the Blue Donut Lifts to descend to lower platforms which were previously inaccessible.

==Trivia==
*Blue Donut Lifts are similar to the [[Donut Lift]]s found in the 3D ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' games in the sense that they fall immediately upon being stood on however, those Donut Lifts still fall at a slow speed unlike the Blue Donut Lifts which fall quickly.
