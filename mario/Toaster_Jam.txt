'''Toaster Jam''' is one of [[Orbulon]]'s [[microgame]]s featured in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The microgame is essentially an extension of the [[Butterfingers (microgame)|Butterfingers]] microgame from ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', but it also involves having to insert the toast into the toaster. The player first has to rotate the bread so that it can fit into the toaster, during which the microgame command is "Toast!". After that, the player has to catch it when it gets out, and the microgame's command changes to "Grab it!". By catching the toast, the player wins the microgame, but if they either do not put the toast into the toaster or do not catch the toast when it jumps out of the toaster, they lose.
