==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Candy Canes are found on [[Pleasant Path]] at the intersection (where west leads to [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]], south leads to [[Koopa Village]], and east leads to [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]]). If [[Mario]] walks around a Candy Cane, a special theme will play and the Candy Cane will release restoring hearts making it a useful way to restore HP without needing to stay at a Toad House (although, the nearest Toad House isn't too far from the only Candy Canes in the game and they can still restore FP and [[Star Power]] as well).

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Candy Canes return in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' where only one is found in [[Petal Meadows]] right before [[Petalburg]]. Mario can no longer circle around the Candy Cane for recovery hearts but he can hammer it ten times for a [[Horsetail]] which can be used in recipes.

==Trivia==
*Candy Canes were originally going to be in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. However, they were taken out in the final game.
