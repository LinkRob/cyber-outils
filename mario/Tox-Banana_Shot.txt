'''Tox-Banana Shot''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
Tox-Banana Shot is a variation of the [[Banana Shot]], reflected from its very similar item icon. As such, Tox-Banana Shot is used very similarly except its metal bazooka fires a Poison Banana.

When the racer uses a Tox-Banana Shot, the Poison Banana locks on to a nearby racer and is launched at them. Aside from being hit, causing the targeted racer to spin, they also become [[Poison (status effect)|poisoned]], during which the racer's steering wheel shakes briefly. If the Poison Banana is launched at an object, it drops to the ground, where it awaits a racer to drive over it.
