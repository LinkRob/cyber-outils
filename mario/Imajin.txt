'''Imajin''' is an Arabian boy and a protagonist in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'', a game released overseas as ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Imajin has a mother and father, aptly named [[Mama (Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic)|Mama]] and [[Papa (Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic)|Papa]] respectively, and he has a twin brother and sister named [[Poki and Piki]] respectively. Aside from this, Imajin has a girlfriend named [[Lina]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Imajin is a dark-haired boy who wears Arabian clothing consisting of a white turban with a jewel in the center, yellow knickerbockers, a red jacket, and red pointy shoes.

==History==
Imajin is one of four playable characters in ''Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic'', the others being Mama, Papa, and Lina. Together, they go on a journey to rescue Poki and Piki from [[Wart|Mamu]], who had dragged them into a storybook. Gameplay-wise, Imajin is the average character, and he does not have unique abilities of his own.

In ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', Imajin is replaced by [[Mario]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Imajin's name is possibly a play on the word "imagine."

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', one of the names that can be generated from pressing the "RANDOM NAME" button is IMJIN, a reference to Imajin.
