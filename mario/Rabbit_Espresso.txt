The '''Rabbit Espresso''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. It can only be bought from a coffee shop in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario: The Origami King)|Toad Town]] for fifty [[coin]]s.

==Description==
The Rabbit Espresso increases [[Mario]]'s running speed for 10 seconds. It has a counterpart called [[Turtle Tea]].
