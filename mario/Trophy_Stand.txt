'''Trophy Stands''' are items in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

==Description==
Trophy Stands have the appearance of a lower part of the [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]], having a circular, golden shape and the design of a ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' emblem on it. A Trophy Stand can be picked up and thrown in the manner of a Frisbee disc, and if it hits an enemy, they combine to form a trophy, which can be picked up and collected.

==In ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''==
[[File:Trowlon trophy SSBB screenshot.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Kirby and Peach approach a trophy of a Trowlon, after they used a Trophy Stand on it, in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
Trophy Stands appear only in stages of [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]]. The player's fighter is required to use Trophy Stands on enemies, because this is the only way to obtain each one's corresponding trophy. However, in order for a Trophy Stand to be effective on an enemy, its health meter has to be low. Trophy Stands can also be used on a boss, if its health is lowered enough. If a boss is captured using a Trophy Stand, a lower-pitched version of the capture sound effect plays instead. Even if the boss is captured, the screen still eventually fades out as if it had been defeated normally, and if the player's character does not claim the boss's trophy quickly enough, it is not counted toward being collected and added to the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Vault|Vault]].

A few enemies cannot be converted into a trophy from a Trophy Stand, such as [[Jyk]]s, which are invincible.
