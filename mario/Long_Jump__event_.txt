'''Long Jump''' is an event in the [[Mario & Sonic (series)|''Mario & Sonic'' series]] where characters compete to jump the farthest. The first appearance was the ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'', followed by ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]], [[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' and ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''.
