The '''Vault Guard''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]], and even uses the same sprite as regular members of the species.

==History==
The Vault Guard guards the vault of the castle in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. He also helps explain how the [[Treasure chest|treasure boxes]] work to [[Mario]]. Despite being a guard, the Vault Guard is rather cowardly, as he hid from [[Mack]]'s invasion instead of helping to fight them off.
