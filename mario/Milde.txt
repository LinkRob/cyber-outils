'''Mildes''' are enemies in the [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi series'']], debuting in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. 

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
Mildes are round, link pink creatures with purple feet. They also have a larger variant named [[Puchipuchi L]], which can also be found where Mildes appear.

===Behavior===
They are basic enemies that simply march forward. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can quickly defeat a Milde by either [[jump]]ing on it or from using another attack.

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
Mildes are primarily encountered in [[Marching Milde's Fort]] in [[World 4 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 4]]. The fort is the home of the world's mid-boss [[Marching Milde]], whom [[Kamek]] enlarges just before the boss fight begins. Up to eight Mildes can appear during the battle, as [[Yellow Yoshi]] has to [[Ground Pound]] Marching Milde, splitting her into two Puchipuchi Ls, which themselves are each split into four Mildes upon being Ground Pounded. The boss fight ends once every Milde has been defeated.

==In ''Yoshi's New Island''==
Mildes only appear in two levels, [[Furious Fred de Fillet's Castle]] and [[Spray Day Mayday]].
