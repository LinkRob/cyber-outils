An '''Electric Clown Fence''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Behavior==
An Electric Clown Fence is an enemy that obstructs a path.

==In ''Wario World''==
Electric Clown Fences are only encountered in [[Wonky Circus]], where three of them can be found.  [[Wario]] has to try to hit one of the moving targets on the fence by throwing a [[Clown]] or a different enemy at it. Wario can destroy a target by hitting it a total of three times or from one [[Mega Toss]]. Whenever Wario hits a target, it becomes smaller and moves faster, making it harder to hit.
