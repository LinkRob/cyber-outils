"'''Mario of the Deep'''" is the forty-seventh animated episode of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the live-action episode "[[Two Bums From Brooklyn]]."

== Plot ==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] arrive in [[Aqualand]] to defeat [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]], known throughout the episode by his alias [[Barra-Koopa]], who has captured [[King Neptune]]. As they travel along, they discover a [[Blooper]] that is attacking a [[Mermushroom]]. The Mario Brothers confront the Blooper and after a brief fight, Mario manages to get rid of it by hitting it with a discarded anchor.

Meanwhile, Barra-Koopa tries to mock King Neptune but finds the sea king is far too easy going to be hurt by his words. Barra-Koopa is informed of the Marios' appearance and gets ready to get rid of the Marios.

The Marios search the ocean floor and discover that the Mermushrooms are being forced to bring all the gold coins from sunken ships to Barra-Koopa.  The Mario brothers fight some of the Bloopers and the Princess dresses up as a female Blooper distracting three of them.  While the Bloopers are defeated, Barra-Koopa manages to capture his enemies in a net.  He then locks them up in a Trouter and [[Piranha Plant]] infested ship.

The group manages to escape the [[Trouter]]s and [[Piranha Plant]]s but are then confronted by a [[Clawgrip]].  They manage to escape this creature too, and get out of the ship. The Mermushroom shows the group where King Neptune is being kept and they free him.  King Neptune then attacks Barra-Koopa's palace with a whale and the Marios come in armed with swordfish. After a brief fight, the Marios manage to beat Barra-Koopa's henchmen and drive him off with a swordfish constantly poking him.

== Trivia/Goofs ==
* This is the only ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' episode to take place entirely underwater.
* Barra-Koopa is based on the fish called barracuda, and and King Neptune is named after the Roman god of the {{w|Neptune (mythology)|same name}}.
* This is the first time Blooper is shown to be able to talk.
* The Marios have to jump over their enemies. It seems they forgot they were underwater and could just ''swim'' over them.
* The plumber's log number, H2O, is the chemical formula for water.
* When the Mermushroom says her people were taken by King Koopa that means she is a servant to her king, not royalty.
