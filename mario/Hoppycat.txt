'''Hoppycats''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Hoppycat screenshot.png|thumb|left|250px|A screenshot of several Hoppycats in ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']]
Hoppycats are mollusk-like creatures that wear a green shell with a few spikes on it. In a level towards the end of World 3, however, an orange Hoppycat can be seen that is holding the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]].

===Behavior===
Like with [[Skipsqueak]]s, Hoppycats jump at the same time as the player's character. Due to their spiky shell, Hoppycats cannot be jumped on; however, they can be destroyed by certain other methods, such as when Mario uses his trunk as a whip when he [[Elephant Mario|is an elephant]].
{{br|left}}

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Their name is a portmanteau of "hop" and "copycat," referring to how a Hoppycat jumps at the same time as the player's character.
