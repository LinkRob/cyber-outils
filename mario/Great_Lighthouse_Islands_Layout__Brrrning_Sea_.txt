== Starting Area (Entrance) ==
Unlike the first two [[Great Lighthouse Islands]], [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Connie]] can walk straight through the front door upon their arrival. The [[Concordian Peddler]] appears close to the Great Lighthouse's entrance, having set up one of his shops nearby.

== Main Elevator ==
When Mario, Luigi, and Connie reach the main elevator, they get ambushed and enslaved by [[Mettozokettohei#Togezokettohei|Togezokettoheitachi]] and [[Mettozokettohei#Ringuzokettohei|Ringuzokettoheitachi]]. They are taken down to floor B1, where Connie is separated from the Bros. and gets put to work making [[Glohm]]. She can be found later on B1 after the Bros. spring themselves free.

== Floor B1 ==

=== Guard Area ===
Mario and Luigi must sneak past some guards. Luigi must then stuff himself into the barrel at the far end and use it to get to a switch. Upon doing so, the Bros. can escape.

=== Main Elevator Station ===
Mario and Luigi return here after they escape the Guard Area. They can find a note from [[Patriarc]] throwing jabs at how [[Zokket]] enjoys taking care of the egg in his fortress at the northern entrance.

There are many blocks of ice blocking several paths, indistinguishable from the ice chunks that can be destroyed with fireballs. After Mario and Luigi [[Bomb Bud|destroy]] the blockage on Floor B2, much of the ice melts.

=== Glohm Pipeworks ===
North of the elevator station lies the room where slaves produce more [[Glohm]]. [[Connie]] is rescued here, and a switch can be activated to allow access to Floor B2.

== Floor B2 ==

=== Main Elevator Station ===
To get to the next area, Mario and Luigi must plug in three plugs that act as keys to the gate to the [[Pipegunk]]'s lair.

=== The Pipegunk's Lair ===
This area is blocked off until Mario and Luigi plug in three plugs to unlock the gate.

== Top Of The Lighthouse ==
This area cannot be visited until Mario and Luigi kill the Pipegunk. When they reach the area, Connie will use her [[Wattanist]] power to reactivate the Great Lighthouse, unlocking the path to the [[Gulchrock Sea]].
