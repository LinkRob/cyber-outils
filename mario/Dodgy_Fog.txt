|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Dodgy Fog''' is a special move used by [[Madame Flurrie]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Flurrie can use this move once she reaches the [[Ultra Rank]] and it requires 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Effects==
To use Dodgy Fog, the player must tilt the {{button|gcn|stick}} in the directions that is shown on the screen at the right moments. The more correctly it's put in, the longer Dodgy Fog lasts. Dodgy Fog gives [[Mario]] the [[Dodgy]] status which allows him to evade enemy attacks but not all of the time. This move can be useful against tough, late game bosses or in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].

==Trivia==
*Dodgy Fog is very similar to [[Lakilester]]'s [[Cloud Nine]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' as they're both cloud-based techniques that gives Mario a chance to evade an enemy's attack.
