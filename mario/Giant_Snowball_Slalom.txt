'''Giant Snowball Slalom''' is one of [[Wario]]'s minigames in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It is a harder variation of [[Snowball Slalom]].

== Gameplay ==
The game follows the exact same rules as before, except this time, the player must roll the [[Headless Snowman]]'s head through a much longer course, and there are more rocks and [[Penguin]]s along the course, which can slow down and shrink the snowman.

== In-game instructions ==
: ''"Roll the snowball over a longer course! As before, avoid rocks because they'll slow you down."''
