'''Moonsnakes''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description=== 
Moonsnakes have a similar appearance and behavior to the [[Spike Bar]] obstacles seen in other games; however, they appear to be made out of stone or rock instead of metal and have a base made out of Moon Rock. They come in different lengths and some have more than one set on a base. Moonsnakes have a white octagon for their "main" segment with the rest of them being composed of dark grey orbs with grey-ish spikes poking out. 

===Behavior===
Moonsnakes are not a living enemy and function more as an obstacle as they rotate around a block in a clockwise or counter-clockwise direction, although they can be seen rolling without spinning in some instances. They often appear on the ground.

==In ''Super Mario Odyssey''==
Moonsnakes are encountered in the [[Seaside Kingdom]], [[Snow Kingdom]], [[Moon Kingdom]], [[Dark Side]], and [[Darker Side]]. If [[Mario]] makes touches a Moonsnake, he takes damage from the fire. Moonsnakes cannot be destroyed.
