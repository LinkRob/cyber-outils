'''Miner Setbacks''' is a [[Co-op Minigame|Co-op]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The camerea pans from the goal, where the [[Spike]]s are waiting to the players that are holding the cart with crystals.

== Gameplay ==

Every player are holding each side of the cart with crystals, they must work their way to the finish line avoiding the [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plants]] coming out of the pipe, otherwise they will be stunned for a few seconds. There is a meter that shows how close the team is to the goal. 
== Ending ==

The players does their victory or losing animations depending on the rank.
==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move

==In-game description==
*''"Carry the cart to the finish line!"''
*''"Watch out for Piranha Plants!"''

== Etymology ==
Its name is a pun of "minor setbacks".
