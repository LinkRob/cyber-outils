}}'''Candy Shakedown''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
Inside a bright room, the players are looking through the jar with candies. The jar shakes and the other players' screens appear.

== Gameplay ==
Players must unwind the lid by pressing the right button and shake to remove candies in the jar. The first player who shakes all of their candies wins the minigame. The finished players must wait for other players to empty their jar until one player has not completed emptying their jar.

== Ending ==
The winner does his/her victory pose while other players are talking to each other.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|joy-con-l}} / {{button|ns|joy-con-r}} (vertical) – Shake bottle
