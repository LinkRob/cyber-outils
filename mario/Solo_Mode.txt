'''Solo Mode''' is a special mode exclusive to ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Features==
Solo Mode is entirely different from [[Story Mode]] that appears in the other Mario Party games. First of all, there is no set story and the player must compete in only three boards as many times as they want. The main function of Solo Mode is to unlock Mini-games very quickly and earn coins.
