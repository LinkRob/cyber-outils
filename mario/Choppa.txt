}}A '''Choppa''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Choppas are strange, alien-like enemies that live on [[Planet Blobule]]. They often fly around erracticly but when they spot someone, they will fly toward them attempting to damage them. Choppas can actually flip in and out of dimensions making them one of the few enemies in the game to do so. Choppas can also be found in the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]].
