'''Mega Rush P''' is a [[badge]] which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is the partner-based variant of [[Mega Rush]].

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear the Mega Rush P by using one [[Badge Point|BP]]. While doing so, Mario's partners gain four Attack points whenever they are in [[Peril]].

Using his Paper Mode, Mario can slim down and wiggle his way into [[Mayor Kroop]]'s garden in [[Petalburg]] to find the Mega Rush P.
