The '''Mario Kart 2016 Calendar''' is an officially [[Nintendo]] licensed products designed by [[Celina Carvalho]] and published by [[Wikipedia:ABRAMS|ABRAMS]] on 2015. The calendar is based off ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:Mario Kart 2016 Calendar back.jpg|150px|thumb|left|Back cover for the calendar.]]
The calendar uses ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' artworks of racers. Racers [[Luigi]], [[Mario]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and [[Princess Peach]] are present on each month. Various ''Mario Kart 8'' items also appear on each month. The front cover of the calendar uses the ''Mario Kart 8'' box art.
