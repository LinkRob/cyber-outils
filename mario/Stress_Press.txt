'''Stress Press''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is rewarded by [[King Bob-omb|Big Bob-omb]] for clearing the [[Big Boss Bob-omb]] quest.

==Gameplay==
The Gaddget is intended to function as a stress reliever. At first, the player has to select an object to smash with a [[hammer]], and it can be either both a [[Warp Pipe]] and a [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]] molded in clay, an alarm clock, a piggy bank, or a diamond. Once selected, the item is moved under the hammer, which the player can then use to smash the object. The alarm clock, piggy bank and diamond break into bits when crushed, while the clay Warp Pipe and Mushroom are simply flattened, making them more comparable to stress relief toys. In addition, smashing the piggy bank reveals a [[coin]] amongst the pieces.
