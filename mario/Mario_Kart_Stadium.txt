'''Mario Kart Stadium''' is a course in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is the first course in the [[Mushroom Cup]], let alone the first in the game overall.

==Layout==
After passing several ads, your driver takes a sharp right turn towards some [[Item Box]]es. There is then another right turn, but you have the option to go on three boost pads to the left. You can also cut through the grass here with a Mushroom.

You then come to a street where an anti-gravity section begins. You can take a left turn, or you can use a Mushroom to go on a nearby trick ramp. After a right turn, the course starts curving upwards. You then turn left towards a gliding area. 

The gliding ramp leads you to yet another trick ramp as well as three more boost pads. To the left, you can use a Mushroom to cut a corner quickly. You then pass the finish line.
