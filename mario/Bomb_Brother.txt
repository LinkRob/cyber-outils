A '''Bomb Brother''' is one of the many bosses that can be found in the [[ScareScraper]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Profile==
A Bomb Brother has all of the physical appearances to normal [[Greenie]]s; however, their bodies are yellow instead of green.

There are also several different themed variations, such as [[Fright Knight]], [[Blue Pimpernel]], [[Spookumber]], [[Spooky Spook]], [[Dreadonfly]], [[Horrorca]], [[Bad-minton]] and [[Awelligator]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
A Bomb Brother may be found at random within floors that are a multiple of five.

===Battle===
Bomb Brothers will always appear in twos. They will carry large bombs in their hands, so the [[Luigi]]s will have to wait for an opening to stun and capture them. Whenever it is being vacuumed, it will drop small bombs which can blow up at a short rate.

Their health points range from 250, 300 or 400, depending on  the difficulty. Whenever they are fought, they will be assisted by normal of masked and shielded variations of Greenies and [[Strong Greenie]]s.
