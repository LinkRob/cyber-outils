'''Super Dive''' is a move in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', in which it is named '''Sliding Catch''', and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'' under its current name.

==Description==
Super Dive is a fielding move that allows the character to dive a distance to catch a ball. Super Dive only sometimes activates, and it is signified by a sparkling light surrounding the fielder's baseball glove body and a sound effect.

In ''Mario Superstar Baseball'', Super Dive can be used by either [[Mario]], [[Princess Daisy]], [[Wario]], [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]], [[Shy Guy]], [[Dry Bones]], [[Noki]], or [[Toadette]]. In ''Mario Super Sluggers'', only a few characters can perform a Super Dive, specifically Daisy, Shy Guy, and [[Baby Daisy]], the latter being the only character who newly receives the ability, because Baby Daisy is a newcomer.
