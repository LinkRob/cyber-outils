'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a course of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
==Layout==
The level heavily features [[Spike]]s, as well as sinking [[Pipe|pipes]], with them being the namesake of the level.

Near the end of the level is a [[Talking Flower]] on a sinking pipe. Standing on the sinking pipe will send the player to a secret area with a [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will spawn spikes throwing [[Spiked Ball]]s to the area where the player is. The pipes the player is standing on will move with the music.

== [[10-Flower Coin]] locations ==
Touching a [[Wow Bud]] protected by a Spike on a sinking pipe will summon a pipe that will lead you to an area in the background with the first 10-flower coin.
A spike is protecting the second 10-flower coin on a row of [[Marimba Blocks]]. An [[Elephant Fruit]] or a [[Fire Flower]] can be used to reach the 10-flower coin without taking damage.

Touching a Wow Bud to the right of three [[Piranha Plants]] will spawn the final 10-flower coin.
