'''Yes, You Can!''' is one of [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. 

==Gameplay==
The player has to use a can opener to open a can. To accomplish this, the player has to whirl the [[Wii Remote]] around as if they were using an actual can opener. If the player accomplishes this within the [[time limit]], they wins, but if they do not, the player loses.

===Difficulty levels===
The can is less opened with each increased difficulty level than in previous ones. The contents revealed from opening the can also differ per difficulty level:
* '''Level 1''': Can is three-quarters open. Opening the can reveals its contents to be three ducks.
* '''Level 2''': Can is five-eighths open. It reveals an airplane and clouds.
* '''Level 3''': Can is half open. It shows a spinning ballerina.
