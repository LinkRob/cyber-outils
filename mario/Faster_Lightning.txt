'''Faster Lightning''' is a giant snail and a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[A Toadally Magical Adventure]]." He is the pet of the [[Wizard King of the West]], who uses him as his steed.

==Physical description==
Faster Lightning has a dark green body and wears a shell with a pattern of magenta and light pink swirls.
