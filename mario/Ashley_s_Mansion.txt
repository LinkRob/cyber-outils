'''Ashley's Mansion''' is the residence of [[Ashley and Red]] in [[Diamond City]]. Its only appearances are in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]], and Ashley's Mansion first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

== In ''WarioWare: Touched!'' ==
Ashley's Mansion was the main setting for the game. Inside the mansion, Ashley was stirring a cauldron and mixed the ingredients, but the spell failed.

== In ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'' ==
Ashley's Mansion appears as the setting for Ashley and Red's story.

== In ''[[Game & Wario]]'' ==
Ashley's Mansion appears as a setting before the [[Ashley (Game & Wario)|Ashley]] minigame. It only appears before Ashley and Red fell into the wrong place in the book and after Ashley and Red escapes the book.

==In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''==
Ashley's Mansion is the setting for Ashley and Red's story.
