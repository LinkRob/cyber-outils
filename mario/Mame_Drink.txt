The '''Mame Drink''' is one of [[Mametchi]]'s five special items in [[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]].

==Description==
The Mame Drink is a recovery item that, if used, removes all bad conditions from the player's [[kart]], such as square tires, burst tires, or [[Thunder Cloud]]. In addition, the Mame Drink provides a shield for approximately half a second upon use. With good timing, this can be used to block incoming items or be invulnerable to the [[Time Bomb]]'s explosion.
