'''Gus''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
He is a member of the [[Robbo Gang]], led by [[Ishnail]], on the east side of [[Rogueport]]. He is also an optional boss whom [[Mario]] can fight. He always guards the entrance to his gang's territory and will charge anyone 10 [[coin]]s. Of course, you can always not pay and not pass, or fight Gus, which is recommended so that Mario can gain control of the east side for free throughout the whole game. Gus and Mario have a comical conversation if Mario decides to fight him and wins.
