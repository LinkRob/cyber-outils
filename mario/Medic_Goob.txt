Medic Goob is a kind of ghost in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', it is a kind of rare ghost.

== Appearance ==
Like regular [[Goob]]<nowiki/>s, Medic Goobs have white eyes, except they have green body instead of blue. They are defeated in the same way as Goobs.

== In ''Luigi's Mansion 3'' ==
Due to the fact that Medic Goobs are rare ghosts, it is only possible for them to appear on [[ScareScraper]]'s fifth floor in ten, fifteen and twenty floors mode, tenth and fifteenth floor in twenty floors mode. [[Luigi]] must flash them and vacuum them up, they will be captured in the same way as regular Goobs. However, Medic Goobs are able to heal other ghosts' lives, so they might also heal other kinds of rare ghosts in the room.
