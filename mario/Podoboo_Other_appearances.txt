==''Yoshi'' franchise==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
[[File:Lava Bubble SMW2.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Podoboo SMW2 YI.png|thumb|A Podoboo Leaps.]]
Podoboos, known as '''Lava Bubbles''' for this game,<ref>''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide, page 127.</ref> appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They share the same behavioral patterns as in ''Super Mario World'', leaping vertically out of lava, only much slower than before. In some cases, Lava Bubbles jump in arcs sideways, and if they land on solid ground while doing so, they bounce along it.

[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can defeat Lava Bubbles by either [[swallow]]ing them or throwing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them. If Yoshi [[swallow]]s a Lava Bubble, he can [[Fire Breath|spit fire]], similar to the effects of the [[fire watermelon|red watermelon]]. If a Yoshi touches a Lava Bubble, this knocks [[Baby Mario]] off his back, triggering the [[Countdown Timer]], and Yoshi has to retrieve him.

Lava Bubbles are primarily found in levels such as [[Hookbill the Koopa's Castle]] and [[The Very Loooooong Cave]].

The game introduces a related species named [[Lava Drop]], which tend to move around horizontally or vertically similar to a [[Cloud Drop]].

====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
[[File:LavaBubble YIDS.png|frame|left]]
[[Image:Podoboo Up Hop.png|thumb|[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] (and [[Baby Peach]]) fluttering over two Podoboos.]]
Podoboos return in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', retaining their behavior from the previous game. Most Podoboos are seen leaping straight up out of lava, although the game also introduces new variants of Podoboos that can leap sideways, and some that can even bounce off walls and leap much slower. Just as before, Podoboos can be defeated by either swallowing them or tossing [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] at them. Once again, swallowing a Podoboo grants [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] the ability to spit fire.

Podoboos are among the few enemies that a Yoshi only has to simply encounter for the Podoboos to appear in the [[Island Museum]], meaning that a Yoshi does not have to throw an egg at it. In the Island Museum, Podoboos fall under the subterranean-enemy exhibit.

Podoboos can be found in underground volcanoes and castles, such as [[Yoshi on Stilts]], [[Six-Face Sal's Fort]], and [[Hurry and Throw!]].

===''Yoshi's New Island''===
While Podoboos themselves don’t appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', [[Lava Drop]]s do, they now behave identically to Podoboos in this game by leaping out of lava.

==''Donkey Kong'' franchise==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series===
====''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''====
[[File:MvsDK Podoboo.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Podoboo Hop Leap.png|thumb|Podoboos as seen in ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''.]]
Podoboos appear as enemies in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''. In this game, they appear as obstacles in [[Fire Mountain (world)|Fire Mountain]] and even more in [[Fire Mountain Plus]]. Podoboos can be seen leaping out of the lava pits around the various rooms, behaving the same way as in the 2D titles of the ''Super Mario'' series. If Mario touches a Podoboo, he gets burned and loses a life instantly.

In the [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], Podoboos are depicted after their common design.

====''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''====
[[File:Podoboo Mini.png|thumb|A Podoboo as seen in ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''.]]
Podoboos return as obstacles in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''. They behave identically as in the previous game and are commonly found in levels of [[Lava Dome]]. They are depicted more closely after their appearance in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', without the eyes. Podoboos are also placeable enemies in the [[Construction Zone]] in the Lava Dome Kit as well as [[Special Kit 2]] and [[Special Kit 3]]. Just as before, Podoboos cannot be defeated, and if a [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Mario]] touches a Podoboo, he is immediately scorched.

==''Wario'' franchise==
===''WarioWare: Get It Together!''===
In ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'', Podoboos appear in level 3 of the [[Super Mario Bros. (WarioWare: Get It Together!)|Super Mario Bros.]] and [[Super Mario World (WarioWare: Get It Together!)|Super Mario World]] microgames.

==Crossover appearances==
===''The Legend of Zelda'' series===
Podoboos are one of several ''Mario'' enemies to appear in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], specifically appearing in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords|The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords]]'', and ''[[w:c:zelda:Cadence of Hyrule: Crypt of the NecroDancer feat. The Legend of Zelda|Cadence of Hyrule: Crypt of the NecroDancer featuring The Legend of Zelda]]''. In the first three, they appear in sidescrolling segments and act just like in the ''Super Mario'' games. In the ''Oracle'' games, a subspecies of the Podoboo, [[Podoboo Tower]], exists in the subterranean world of [[w:c:zelda:Subrosia|Subrosia]] in ''Oracle of Seasons'', and in the burning ruins of [[w:c:zelda:Symmetry City|Symmetry City]] in ''Oracle of Ages''. In ''Four Swords'', where they are known as '''Bubbling Lava''',<ref>Stratton, Bryan, and Stephen Stratton. ''The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past/Four Swords'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide. Page 176.</ref> they hop after the players, splitting into two small, quickly ricocheting ones when attacked. In ''Cadence of Hyrule'', they instead move around walls like [[Spark]]s.

Both ''The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening'' and ''The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords'' feature giant Podoboo-like bosses as well, with the former game having [[Hot Head]] and the latter having [[Gouen|Gōen]].

===''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games''===
Podoboos appear in the [[Bowser's Castle]] segment of [[Mario's Figure Skating Spectacular]] in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'', where the skaters jump over and past them during parts of the routine.

==Guest appearances==
===''Kaeru no Tame ni Kane wa Naru''===
[[File:KnTnKwN Lava Bubble.png|frame]]
In ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kaeru no Tame ni Kane wa Naru|Kaeru no Tame ni Kane wa Naru]]'', Podoboos appear with their usual design as obstacles in sidescrolling areas. They jump from lava as they normally do, but sometimes travel in wide arcs, turning sideways as they do so.

===''Minecraft''===
In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''[[Minecraft]]'', [[w:c:minecraft:Magma Cube|Magma Cubes]] are replaced by Podoboos.
