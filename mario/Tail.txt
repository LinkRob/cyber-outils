|name=Tail (Battle Card)}}'''Tail''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.
==Description==
The Tail is a defensive card that gives [[Mario]] an extra line of defense when it's the opponents turn. It is a tan/brown striped tail with an orange/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 80 coins (painted)

Sell Price: 40 coins (painted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Tail is only obtainable in [[Fort Cobalt]].
