The '''Seven Wonders of the Glitz Pit''' is a term for the secrets of the [[Glitz Pit]] and they are seen in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. The Wonders are told by [[Bandy Andy]] who has been doing research on them every time he leaves the locker room.
==The Wonders==
===The Sealed Wall===
*'''Bandy Andy''': "''They say...there's a sealed wall in the minor-league locker room... Some say...the remains of fallen athletes are stored in there...''"
*'''Meaning''': There is a giant yellow block in the minor league locker room that can be broken once Mario obtains the Super Hammer.
===The Man-Eating Toilet===
*'''Bandy Andy''': "''They say...in one of the arena's bathrooms, a man-eating toilet lurks... Some say...that anyone who sees this toilet never sees anything again...''"
*'''Meaning''': After Mario gets locked into one of the minor league locker rooms on the other side (the blue side), there is a toilet he can take that acts like a Warp Pipe which leads into the major league room of the red side.
