The '''Adult Trousers''' is a [[clothing]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. As the name suggests, the Adult Trousers can only be equipped by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] but neither [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]]. The Adult Trousers are obtained from the [[Koopaseum]]. Equipping the Adult Trousers increases its user's [[Defense]] by 50, [[Speed]] by 10, and [[Stache]] by 5.
