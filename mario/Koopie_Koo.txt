'''Koopie Koo''' is a female [[Koopa Troopa]] character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and it's [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. She is [[Koops]]'s overprotective girlfriend.

==Appearance==
Koopie Koo, as stated above, is a female Koopa Troopa who wears a teal-colored shell that has pink sleeves. She also wears white and pink shoes and has orange hair which is tied with a pink ribbon.

==Personality==
Koopie Koo is often a nice character and is quite devoted to her boyfriend, Koops. She is also very protective over him as seen when she tried to convince Koops not to go after Hooktail. Even with Koops deciding to go with Mario to fight the dragon, Koopie Koo gets upset for his decision and attacks the both Koops and Mario. However, Koopie Koo still loves Koops and mentions she misses him when she sends Mario an email.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Koopie Koo pleads with Koops not to join Mario's team and go slay [[Hooktail]] to avenge his father, but Koops is adamant about his plans. She loves Koops not matter what; he needn't prove anything to her in the game. Although Koopie Koo loves Koops unconditionally, Koops still wants to change himself and become a brave hero. She consents to his leaving for the journey. After Koops left [[Petalburg]] with [[Mario]], she waits for Koops to return home as a stronger Koopa. After the [[Shadow Queen]] is defeated, Koopie Koo reunites with Koops being proud of him for finishing his journey and the two continue to leave peacefully in Petalburg.

Koopie Koo also sends Mario an email later in the game saying she misses both Mario and Koops. She mentions that she misses Koops' scent so much, she decided to go to his house to be reminded of him. She also brings up that, with Koops' father Koopley, staying at his house now, it's starting to smell a lot like him (before being disgusted at the thought that she may also be smelling Hooktail's gut from him). However, she says that she'll get used to it saying that Koopley may become her father in the future.
