'''''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''''' is the [[Nintendo DS]] handheld release of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', following after the handheld ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' as the second and last Nintendo DS title in the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' series. It was created to coincide with the [[wikipedia:2010 Winter Olympics|2010 Winter Olympics]] in Vancouver, Canada. The game is similar to the original, but with different events, new characters and several big changes in gameplay and control schemes. Like its predecessor, ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' received a dual release on both the [[Nintendo DS]] and [[Wii]].

Unlike the Wii version, there is a single-player [[Adventure Tours]] mode revolving around a plot involving [[Bowser]] and [[Dr. Eggman]]'s scheme to melt all the snow on [[Winter Island]] by capturing the [[Snow Spirits]] to cancel the 2010 Winter Olympics in favor of their own.
