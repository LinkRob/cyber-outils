'''Flower Fuzzies''' are a variant of [[Fuzzy]] appearing  in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|it’s remake]]. Unlike regular Fuzzies which are capable of sucking out [[HP]], Flower Fuzzies prefer to drain their victims' [[FP]], with the drained FP, a Flower Fuzzy can become more powerful.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
During a battle with Flower Fuzzies, [[Mario]] can get attacked by one of them and lose not only [[HP]] but also [[FP]]. The Flower Fuzzy then gains 3 FP. Once this enemy has this FP, he has the deadly power to strike Mario and his partner with lightning. If Mario has no FP left, the Flower Fuzzy will start sucking up 3 of his HP instead like a normal [[Fuzzy]]. This combination of factors makes the Flower Fuzzy surprisingly dangerous if not taken down quickly enough. These enemies are native to [[Keelhaul Key]], but can also be located in the middle section of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Although Flower Fuzzies don’t appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', a similar variant called the [[Pink Fuzzy]] does, Pink Fuzzies are simply stronger variations of Fuzzies.

==Trivia==
*The Flower Fuzzy is the only enemy in the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series that’s capable of draining [[FP]] rather than [[HP]] (although it will start draining HP if [[Mario]] or his [[partner]]s have no FP).
**In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], the Flower Fuzzy is now one of two enemies that can drain FP since the added hidden boss, [[Prince Mush]] can also drain FP as well.
