The {{nihongo|'''Town Area'''|タワーエリア<ref>{{cite web|url=https://youtu.be/9nYActoJ5l0|title=一人用マリオパーティアドバンス字幕プレイ part6|work=YouTube|language=Japanese|date=2016-03-07|accessdate=2023-04-19}}</ref>|Tawā Eria|Tower Area}} is an area of [[Shroom City]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
This will be the player's starting location if they choose [[Mario]]. This area is located east of the [[Horror Area]] and [[Desert Area]], and north-east of [[Seaside Area]]. Hence its name, it looks like an ordinary town, and also serves as the main part of Shroom City, as it consists of 17 places and 18 quests.
