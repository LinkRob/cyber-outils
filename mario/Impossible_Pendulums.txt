'''Impossible Pendulums''', is the eighth level in the ninth world of ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', [[Superstar Road]]. It can be accessed upon collecting all the Star Coins in [[Peach's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Peach's Castle]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts on a platform with the level background very similar to that of [[The Final Battle (New Super Luigi U)|The Final Battle]]. A few platforms and several rotating [[Ball 'n' Chain]] pendulums are just ahead along with some [[? Block]]s. After the first [[Star Coin]] and a swinging pendulum, more swinging pendulums and a [[Red Ring]] will appear. The second Star Coin and more pendulums are ahead. Afterwards, a [[Bony Beetle]] and a pendulum appear just before the [[Warp Pipe]] which will transport the player to rotating pendulums, the third Star Coin and the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Near the beginning, the player needs to jump over the first swaying pendulum and land on the platform with the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the Red Ring, the player needs to drop down to the platform in the middle to receive the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': After going through the Warp Pipe, the Star Coin will be rotating in sync with the pendulums.
==[[List of Luigi sightings in New Super Luigi U|Luigi Sighting]]==
At the last Ball 'n' Chain inside the castle, the player needs to head down to the bottom right corner to find an 8-bit Luigi in a secret area.
