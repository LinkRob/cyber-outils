The {{Nihongo|'''Frost-Frosted Cake'''|純白のケーキ|Junpaku no Kēki|, lit. Snow-White Cake}} is a key item in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It is a large cake decorated with a snowflake motif.

== Appearance ==
It resembles a towering, fourth-tiered cake. The apical tier of the cake is decorated with a Shiverian sign topper. Each of the bottom layers of the cake feature an assortment of snowflake shaped sweets.

== Inform ==
The cake is very popular at weddings by tradition as told in the Snow Kingdom's brochure. It is the kingdom's first-place prize for the Bound Bowl Grand Prix. Only the Shiverian Elder can make the Frost-Frosted Cake.

== History ==
During the events of the game, the cake is stolen by Bowser and the Broodals as a wedding cake for Princess Peach's forced marriage to the Koopa king. After Mario defeats Bowser in the Moon Kingdom, the Frost-Frosted Cake is the only wedding item that does not return to its original location.
