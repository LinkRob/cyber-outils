'''Gleam Glacier '''is the sixth world of [[Super Princess Peach|''Super'' ''Princess'' ''Peach'']]''. ''It is an ice and snow-themed world, located in [[Vibe Island]], with a color combination of green, blue and purple. It has several sad and angry enemies in the world, unlike the previous World, not too many new enemies were introduced here. The most common enemies are [[Spike (enemy)|Spikes]], [[Walrus]]ses, [[Mad Spike]]s, and [[Flurry|Flurries]]. There are also obstacles such as [[Snowman|Snowmen]] and [[Freezie]]s which block [[Peach]]'s path and Peach must use her abilities to get past them. The world also makes use of the snowy and icy environments around it. The world's boss is the icy [[Blizzaurus]].
