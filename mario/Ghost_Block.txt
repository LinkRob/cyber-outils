'''Ghost Blocks''' (also called '''Ghost Question Blocks''' or '''Ghost ? Blocks''') are possessed [[Question Block]]s that appear in various [[Ghost House]]s of the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' series. They look very similar to Question Block with an obvious difference being the fact that their "?" doesn't move. They usually appear in groups and in the same formations as a group of Question Block however when approached, they glow pink and lift themselves into the air whilst making a very eerie sound before attacking, a trait shared with the [[Ghost Vase]]s. If they hit [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]], the plumbers will take damage so they will either need to jump out of the way or duck for cover to avoid them, if the Ghost Block hits a wall or flooring, it will shatter releasing a [[Coin]], the same is true if it hits Mario/Luigi however this will deal damage so avoid this, a Ghost Block can also be destroyed with the effects of a [[Starman]]. In their debut, Ghost Blocks are incredibly rare only appearing in [[World 7-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7-Ghost House]] in a secret room near the second [[Star Coin]]. They are much more common in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' as they appear in [[Haunted Shipwreck]], [[Haunted Cargo Hold]], [[Spinning Spirit House]], and [[Vanishing Ghost House]].
