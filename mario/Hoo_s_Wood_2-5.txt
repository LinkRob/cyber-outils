'''Hoo's Wood '''is the fifth level of [[Hoo's Wood]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]].''

== Toads ==
* '''Toad 1''': Cry while running the long falling bridge and jump over [[Paratroopa]]s to go pass by. Then rage at the [[Fangs]] to shoo them away and jump over the pipe and hit the ! Box.
* '''Toad 2''': Jump on top of the [[Perry Block]] or green platform and run on the top floor and dash until you find the ! Box.
* '''Toad 3''': Spin the windmill with [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe, then fall down top the bottom, go to the left and jump over the pipe and hit the ! Box.

== Puzzle Piece ==
Climb down the ladder and run to the right to the dead end and get the puzzle piece.
