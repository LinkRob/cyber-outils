'''Water Barrels''' are a type of [[barrel]] featured in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
Water Barrels contain water, and [[Mario]] can pick up a Water Barrel then throw it, causing water to splash around everywhere. A Water Barrel can optionally be used to clean large piles of [[Goop]], as it covers a wider area than any of [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]]'s nozzles. If Mario [[Ground Pound|ground-pounds]] a Water Barrel, FLUDD's water supply is replenished.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
In [[Sirena Beach]]'s sixth episode, [[Scrubbing Sirena Beach]], Mario can optionally use Water Barrels to more quickly clear the [[electric goop]], therefore helping him in the timed challenge.

In [[Pianta Village]]'s fourth episode, [[Chain Chomp's Bath]], Mario can use a single Water Barrel to instantly cool down the [[Chain Chomp]].
