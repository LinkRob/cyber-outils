'''Frost T.''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Frost T. is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with green spots on his head and can be seen with white hair. He also wears a blue sweater.

==History==
Frost T. is first seen at the beginning of the game talking with [[Russ T.]] during [[Princess Peach]]'s party at her [[Princess Peach's Castle|castle]]. Later, Frost T. can be found in his hometown of [[Starborn Valley]]. He is also known to have traveled to places with [[Mayor Penguin]] and has a nephew named [[Mini T.]] who lives in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]].

Frost T. is also involved in the chain [[letter]] side-quest where he received a letter from his nephew, Mini T. Frost T. remarks on how Mini T. and [[Dane T.]] always love to get into mischief, but always love to write letters to him. Frost T. also mentions having a wife and daughter before asking Mario to give a letter to [[Goompapa]] in [[Goomba Village (location)|Goomba Village]]. He then specifies that it is for his daughter, [[Goombaria]], but remarks that her father can explain it better to her.
