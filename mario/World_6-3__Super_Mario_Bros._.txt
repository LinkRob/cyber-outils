'''World 6-3''' is the third level of [[World 6 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 6]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. It is the only "ashen" level.

== Layout ==
The player begins on a stretch of land, shortly before approaching the first platform. A vertically moving [[lift]] is placed after the third platform; it can help the player leap over a tall platform, followed by a [[Springboard|jumping board]] and three horizontally moving lifts. Above the third lift is a [[? Block]] containing a [[Power-Up Panels]]. 

Ahead is a vertically moving lift followed by two sets of scale lifts. This is where [[Bullet Bill]]s will begin to appear. Those are the only enemies encountered in this level. Here, some more platforms await, along with another jumping board that will help the player reach a horizontally moving lift and a scale. Finally, four platforms lie ahead, which will fall upon being stepped on. Just ahead is the [[flagpole]].

== Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' ==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 21000.

=== Red Coin locations ===
* Red Coin #1 is located on the platform following the first jumping board, underneath the lifts.
* Red Coin #2 is located under the vertically moving lift following the ? Block.
* Red Coin #3 is located on the tall platform following the second scale lift.
* Red Coin #4 is located on the platform following the second jumping board, under the lift.
* Red Coin #5 is located beneath the final platform.

=== Yoshi Egg location ===
* The Yoshi Egg is found inside a [[Hidden Block]] left of the platform underneath the third scale lift.
