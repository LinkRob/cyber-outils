A '''Buzzy Shell''' is the protective shell worn by [[Buzzy Beetle]]s. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

Buzzy Shells are a lot like [[Koopa Shell]]s except they grant its wearer, in this case a Buzzy Beetle, invulnerability to [[fireball]] attacks from [[Mario]] or another character in their [[Fire Mario|Fire]] transformation. In several 2D [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] installments, [[jump]]ing on a Buzzy Beetle causes it to retreat into its shell, and the Buzzy Shell can then be kicked to slide along the ground in the same manner as a Koopa Shell.
