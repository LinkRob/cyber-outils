'''Swaphopper''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

Swaphoppers are bug-like creatures with a spike on their head. The upper part of their body is blue and orange (like Flaptacks), and they have plunger-like feet of the same color. They also have two glowing blue antennae.

They have a big spike on their head, so if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] jump on them they will get hurt. They jump sideways and are always in Mario or Luigi's direction. They are invincible until Mario or Luigi hits a [[Gravity Switch]], and the Swaphopper's head and spike will be upside-down. Jump on them when the head is upside-down and they will be defeated.
