'''Standard DK''' is [[Donkey Kong]]'s standard [[kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', with his other karts being the [[Rambi Rider]] and the [[Wildlife]]. It is a yellow kart, with DK's logo on it in red. The kart has great [[Speed]], average [[Acceleration]], poor [[Handling]], good [[Drift]], and good [[Items (stat)|items]].

It shares its speed with [[Wario]]'s [[Dragonfly]], its acceleration with the Wildlife, Wario's [[Brute]] and [[Standard WR]], [[R.O.B.]]'s [[Standard RB]] and [[ROB-LGS]]. It shares its weight with [[Luigi]]'s [[Streamliner]], [[Waluigi]]'s [[Standard WL]] and [[Zipper]], and it shares its handling with the Rambi Rider, [[Bowser]]'s [[Standard BW]] and [[Hurricane]].

At for the majority of the game, only Donkey Kong can use this kart, but once all cups in the Mirror Class have been completed, all characters can use this kart.

Donkey Kong later got a [[Standard Kart L]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.
