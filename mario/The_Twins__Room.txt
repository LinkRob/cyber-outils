'''The Twins' Room''' is one of the many rooms in [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|the mansion]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]]. It is located in [[Area One]], but [[Luigi]] cannot enter the room until he is in [[Area Three]].

==Description==
[[File:TheTwins'Room3DS.png|thumb|The Twins' Room in the Nintendo 3DS remake]]
The Twins' Room is small in size and filled with children's toys, such as trucks on the shelf, a helicopter mobile hanging from the ceiling, and a globe on a desk near the door. A pair of tennis rackets are in the corner next to a desk cluttered with books and a poster of the "Monsters" is on the wall. A [[Speedy Spirit]] is hiding in the bunk beds.

==History==
When Luigi enters The Twins' Room, he fights the room's occupants, the twins [[Henry and Orville]]. After Luigi captures the twins, he receives [[Mario's Shoe]].
