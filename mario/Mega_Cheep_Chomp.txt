{{nihongo|'''Mega Cheep Chomp'''|巨大バクバク|Kyodai Bakubaku}} is a character who appears in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. He is an enlarged [[Cheep Chomp]], and has the appearance as such. Mega Cheep Chomp is the boss of [[Whimsical Waters]], and his boss minigame is [[Mega Cheep Chomp's Shell Shock]].

==Battle==
The Mega Cheep Chomp's minigame, Mega Cheep Chomp's Shell Shock, involves the players firing Koopa shells from cannons at it to deal damage. Each player has three Koopa shells, and they try not to have the Mega Cheep Chomp face them when time runs out, otherwise resulting in the Mega Cheep Chomp lurching out of the water and landing on the player it is facing, deducting points. Every time a player hits him with a Koopa shell, it rotates, changing the player it will attack. When it is mad, three [[Cheep Cheep]]s will appear and swim around it, deflecting shots from cannons. They can be eliminated by shooting shells at them. They can be eliminated by shooting shells at them. The Cheep Chomp's rotations from damaging him cause his facing to cycle P1 > P2 > P4 > P3 > P1, with the cycle repeating.

When he is defeated, the Mega Cheep Chomp will faint. His corpse then explodes, revealing the [[Mini Star]]s in his possession.
