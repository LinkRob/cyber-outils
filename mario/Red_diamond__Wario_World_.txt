A '''red diamond''' is an item in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==In ''Wario World''==
Red diamonds are a type of [[treasure]] found in the sub-levels accessed from wooden and steel [[trapdoor (Wario World)|trapdoors]]. [[Wario]] has to collect the red diamonds so that the [[Stone Doohickey]] guarding the door to the [[level]]'s boss disappears.

Although each level has eight red diamonds, not every one has to be collected to get rid of a level's Stone Doohickey, and the two levels of each [[world]] have a minimum requirement of red diamonds that increases by one in the next two levels of a world: [[Excitement Central]]'s levels, [[Greenhorn Forest]] and [[Greenhorn Ruins]], require at least three red diamonds; [[Spooktastic World]]'s levels, [[Horror Manor]] and [[Wonky Circus]], require at least four; [[Thrillsville]]'s levels, [[Shivering Mountains]] and [[Beanstalk Way]], require at least five; and lastly, [[Sparkle Land]]'s levels, [[Mirror Mansion]] and [[Pecan Sands]], require six red diamonds.
