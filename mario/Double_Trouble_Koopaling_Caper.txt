'''Double Trouble Koopaling Caper''' is the fourth level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 29.

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains 2 [[Mechakoopa]]s, and 6 [[Dry Bones]]. The enemy captain is a Dry Bones, and he knows [[Chance]] and [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 2 Mechakoopas, 5 Dry Bones, and [[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]]. The enemy captain is Lemmy, and he knows [[Rally]], Chance, and [[Parry]].

The 3rd battle contains 6 Mechakoopas, and 2 Dry Bones. The enemy captain is a Mechakoopa, and he knows Rally.

The final battle contains 5 Mechakoopas, 2 Dry Bones, and [[Ludwig von Koopa|Ludwig]]. The enemy captain is Ludwig, and he knows Rally, [[Call for Backup]], and [[Lightning (move)|Lightning]]. Call for Backup calls a [[Para-Beetle]] or a [[Lakitu]].

After the final battle, the player recruits Lemmy (Lv. 30) and Ludwig (Lv. 30).

==Possible Recruits==

*Mechakoopa (Lv. 29)
