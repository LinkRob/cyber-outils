A '''Dash Space''' is a type of [[space]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. They appear in both ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Dash Space.png|thumb|left|''Mario Party 9'' sprite]]
Dash Spaces are colored light green and depict two dark blue arrows on them. If a Captain lands onto a Dash Space, they get to roll the [[Dice Block]] again.

[[Advance Space]]s from certain other ''Mario Party'' games are very similar to Dash Spaces, even having their name in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'', except an Advance Space moves a character forward by the same number of spaces as on the Advance Space.
