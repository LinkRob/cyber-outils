'''Sammy Rai''' is a one-time character who briefly appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. He is a big man in a samurai costume who sings traditional Japanese ballads. His name is a pun on "samurai."

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
Sammy Rai only briefly appears in the opening scene for [[Mona]]'s stage, where he is announced as the week's highlight sensation on [[Ear Candy]] by [[Ken the Reporter|Ken the VJ]]. However, his music can be heard in the end cutscene of [[Mike]]'s story, when Mike and [[Dr. Crygor]] perform a karaoke duet.
