}}A '''Chromeba''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Chromebas are paler, stronger versions of [[Growmeba]]s that are mainly found in [[Castle Bleck]] (specifically [[Castle Bleck Foyer]]). Chromebas, like all other Growmebas, have the ability to duplicate themselves and defeating them requires taking out the original Chromeba.
