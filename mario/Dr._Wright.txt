'''Dr. Wright''' is a character from the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] version of ''[[w:c:nintendo:SimCity|SimCity]]''. He is loosely based off of [[Wikipedia:Will Wright|Will Wright]], the original designer for the ''SimCity'' series. In the game, he serves as an adviser to the player.

Following his ''SimCity'' debut, Dr. Wright appeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' as a collectible [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]], and then went on to be an [[Assist Trophy]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. In his appearances as an Assist Trophy, Dr. Wright's attack is the same. Once spawned on stage, using his pointer stick, he pulls out a skyscraper from the ground, attacking any enemy players it touches. The city he summons varies in size and power.
