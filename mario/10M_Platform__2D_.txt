'''10M Platform''' is one of the 2D Tokyo 1964 events in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''.

==Controls==
All of the controls involve pressing a button while a character jumps off the high dive.

*[[Joy-Cons]]: Any of the buttons
*Control Stick: Rotate clockwise or counterclockwise
*L button
*R button
