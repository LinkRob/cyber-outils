==Description==
Megaflash Clone Jump stickers are very similar to Flashy Clone Jump stickers except for the large size. They take up nearly six sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].

==Notes==
Megaflash Clone Jump stickers are Mario's strongest Clone Jump sticker. They are very rare and can be bought in only one area in the entire game: [[Rumble Volcano]].
