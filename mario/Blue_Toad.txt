'''Blue Toad''', also known as just '''Toad''' in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe|New Super Mario Bros. U Delux]]<nowiki/>e'', is a character who appears in [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' series]]. He is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who joins the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] along with [[Yellow Toad]] to rescue [[Princess Peach]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Blue Toad has the appearance of a generic colored Blue Toad with blue spots on his mushroom cap, as his name would suggest. Despite this, he and other generic Toads are still labeled as "[[Toad (species)|Toad]]" in various Mario games.

===Personality===
Blue Toad is shown as a courageous character, who able to venture alongside the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] on their dangerous mission to save [[Princess Peach]] from [[Bowser]] and [[Koopa Troop|his minions]]; a force that most [[Toad (species)|Toads]] tend to be afraid of. He also shown to be kind and compassionate as seen in the ending of the game where he happily invites [[Luigi]] to ride in his and [[Yellow Toad]]'s hot air balloon after he had been abandoned by [[Mario]] and Peach. In the introduction of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', he shown to be even more finicky than his partner Yellow Toad as he's seen to be in an extreme alarm when Bowser's mechanical arm pulled away the Princess.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
[[File:Blue Toad jumping NSMB Wii artwork.png|thumb|135x135px|left|''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' artwork of Blue Toad jumping]]
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''===
Blue Toad first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. He attends [[Princess Peach]]'s birthday party at [[Princess Peach's Castle|Peach's Castle]] along with [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] and [[Yellow Toad]]. The party is later interrupted by the [[Koopalings]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] disguised as a large birthday cake in order to kidnap the princess. Blue Toad along with Mario, Luigi and Yellow Toad ran to rescue her. Together they travel through eight different [[World|worlds]], stopping [[Bowser]] and [[Koopa Troop|his minions]] and saving Peach. After Bowser is defeated and the princess is saved, the two Toads go on a hot air balloon, they also invite Luigi to join their balloon after his balloon was left with Mario and Peach onboard.

Blue Toad can be a playable character in multiplayer and appears in the ending cutscene and the credits.{{br|left}}

=== ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''/''[[New Super Luigi U]]/[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' ===
[[File:Blue Toad Main Artwork.png|thumb|x200px|Blue Toad artwork, from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'']]
Blue Toad appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. In these games he acts the same. In the opening cutscene, the Toads are seen with the Mario Bros. and Princess Peach attending a lunch at Peach's Castle. However, their peaceful together is soon interrupted by the sudden appearance of multiple [[Airship|airships]] consisting of Bowser and the Koopalings. Bowser use a giant mechanical arm, he pulls away Peach's chair from harm and instead gave a mighty punch at the heroes, causing them to be knocked out.

Bowser used the mechanical arm to scoop up Blue Toad, and his friends and threw the heroes far across the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. Upon crashing into the [[Acorn Tree]] consisting of [[Super Acorn|Super Acorns]] and falling back to the ground, the four heroes soon get up and see Peach's Castle getting invaded by Bowser's forces in the far distance. Deciding to take action, Blue Toad and his friends run across the plains in the start of a new adventure.

=== ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]/[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'' ===
[[File:Toad Artwork - Super Mario 3D World.png|thumb|Blue Toad appearance, from ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''|left]]
Blue Toad appears for the first time as a playable character in a 3D Mario game along with [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] and [[Princess Peach|Peach]] on the mission to save the [[Sprixie Princesses|sprixies]] from [[Bowser]]. He is characterized by his exceptional speed, making him the fastest runner in the group. However, his jumping ability is slightly lower than the other characters, requiring more precision in platforming sections. In the game he is just labeld as [[Toad (species)|Toad]] compared to his previous mario bros playable apearences where he is called as Blue Toad.

While Blue Toad does not possess any unique powers, his agility makes him a strategic choice for time-based challenges and speedruns. His design is consistent with earlier appearances of multicolored Toads and does not distinguish him with a separate backstory or personality in-game.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
Blue Toad makes a reappearance, although [[Toadette]] replaces him in the opening cutscene, ending cutscene and the credits. Blue Toad is still playable, but he must be selected by holding ZL while Yellow Toad is selected. 

=== [[Super Mario Maker 2|''Super Mario Maker 2'']] ===
Blue Toad returns as a playable character labeled as [[Toad (species)|Toad]] alongside Mario and Luigi similair to [[Super Mario 3D World|super mario 3D World]] roster while [[Princess Peach|Peach]] has been replaced with [[Toadette]]. Each game style (''[[Super Mario Bros.|Super Mario Bros]].'', ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', and ''[[Super Mario World]]'') provides Blue Toad with unique sprites, with ''[[Super Mario World|Super Mario World's]]'' being entirely original as toads didn´t apear in that game. This is the first game where Blue Toad can use the [[Frog]], [[Cape Feather|Cape]], [[Balloon Mario|Balloon]], and [[Builder Mario|Builder]] forms. His Fire form in ''Super Mario 3D World''-style gameplay has changed from Yellow Toad’s coloration to Blue Toad’s, though his pants remain white.

=== [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']] ===
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', Blue Toad is a playable character with a new style and a lower-pitched voice.

==Other appearances==

=== '''''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''''' ===
Blue Toad appears in a total of four [[Battle Card]]s.
=== '''''Mario Sports Superstars''''' ===
Blue Toad appears in two different collectible cards as Blue Penguin Toad and Blue Flying Squirrel Toad.
=== '''Nintendo 3DS''' ===
Blue Toad and the other heroes of ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' were shown as some of the featured characters in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] tech demo. He was riding a [[Pink Yoshi]], similarly to the game's box art. Eventually, this tech demo screen featuring Blue Toad and his friends was later featured as a puzzle in [[StreetPass Mii Plaza]].

==='''''New Super Mario Bros. Mii'''''===
Blue Toad, [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Yellow Toad]], and [[Mii|Miis]], were also seen and playable in the Wii U demo ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Mii]]''.

==='''''Taiko no Tatsujin Wii: Minna de Party Sandaime''''' ===
Blue Toad alongside Yellow Toad, Mario, Luigi, the Koopalings, and various other characters from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' made cameo appearances in the Wii game ''Taiko no Tatsujin Wii: Minna de Party Sandaime'' where the characters can be seen dancing to the various tracks featured in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.

=== ''Mario Tennis Fever'' ===
Blue Toad appears alongside [[Yellow Toad]] appear as support characters. In Adventure mode, he and Blue Toad help [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] on their quest to the island.

==In other media==
===''[[Super Mario-kun]]''===
Blue Toad along with Yellow Toad are featured in the 41st and 42nd volumes of ''Super Mario-kun''. As the story is based on ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', Blue Toad and Yellow Toad are introduced as Mario's new accomplices. Throughout the adaption of the two issues, Blue Toad plays an important role by journeying alongside Mario, Luigi, and Yellow Toad on their quest to rescue Princess Peach from Bowser and his forces.

Both Blue Toad and Yellow Toad are seen in their propeller forms on the cover of the 41st issue of ''Super Mario-kun''.

=== [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] ===
Blue Toad apears playable with only his alterego forms: Builder, Astronaut, Cat and Pit Crew forms.

=== ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' film series ===
In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', a [[Yellow Toad and Blue Toad (Illumination)|yellow and blue toad guard]] stops [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] from entering [[Princess Peach's Castle (Illumination)|Princess Peach's Castle]]. Due to their coloration, occupation, and having spotlight than other Toad guards, it is possible that them together are considered references to Yellow Toad and Blue Toad respectively.
