'''Tryclyde''' is an antagonist in ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''. He is based on his [[Tryclyde|main counterpart]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Tryclyde looks similar to his game counterpart, but the most noticeable different is that the left head wears glasses.

=== Personality traits ===
Tryclyde's heads are given distinct personalities: the middle one is big and a bully to the other heads, the left one is smart (hence his glasses), and the right one is dumb. The three heads always bicker with each other, causing one of them to bite the other; when this happens, the middle head (having the pain receptors) always hurts itself. Their bickering is what helps them to be defeated.

=== Powers and abilities ===

== Comic appearances ==
Tryclyde appears in volume 8 of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''. Mario, Luigi, and [[Yoshi]] encounter Tryclyde in a pyramid. Tryclyde burns Mario and Yoshi, but Luigi has a [[Frog Suit]]. He gives Mario a [[Magical Potion]]. Tryclyde steals it from him, but drops it on Mario, enabling Mario to recover from a [[Mushroom]]. Mario then defeats Tryclyde by throwing [[Mushroom Block]]s at him.

Tryclyde appears as part of [[Wart]]'s ultimate form during the fight. He appears to be jutting from Wart's head.
