The '''Star Shrine''' is a cosmic temple that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is located on the peak of [[Star Hill]], and is where the [[Star Gate]] is located. The [[Elder Shrooboid]] is the boss of the area.
