'''Treasure Hunt''' is the first level of ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'' overall. It is also the only level where [[Pak E. Derm]]s appear. The hidden Heart Coin letter for this stage is Y.

== Fruits ==
* [[Apple (Yoshi franchise)|Apple]]
* [[Banana (Yoshi's Story)|Banana]]
* [[Watermelon]]
* Melon
* Grapes
* Heart Fruit

==Happy Hearts==
* First one can be found near Poochy.
* Second one can be found on the tree with "orange leaves".
* Third and last one can be found in the area that needs platforms.
