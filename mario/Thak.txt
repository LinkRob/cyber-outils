'''Thak''' is the eleventh boss whom [[Toad (character)|Toad]] faces in the VS COM mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. Thak is a blue ogre-like monster with orange hair and a horn on top of his head.
