'''Superstar Road''' is a world in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', after the player finishes the game. If the player gathers all the [[Star Coins]] in any other world, then he or she will have access to a level depending on which world. The Toad Houses in this world also contain items that could not be accessible before, the [[Propeller Mushroom]] and the [[Penguin Suit]]. It is a spiritual successor to the previous secret worlds and Star Roads alike. In ''New Super Luigi U'' and ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe'', the Propeller Mushroom and the Penguin Suit can be found in regular levels as well.

The world has nine regular levels and four Toad Houses.
