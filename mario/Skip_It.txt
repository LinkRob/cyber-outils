'''Skip It''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective of the microgame is to jump over the rope the expected amount of times by either jumping in real life in rhythm of the rope in the [[Form Baton|Big Cheese]] Form or flicking the Wii Remote to imitate a jumping motion. The player needs to jump once, twice, or thrice, depending on level to win. If they fail to jump over the rope, the microgame ends in a loss.
*'''Level 1:''' The drawn girl is skipping the rope. The player must jump once over the rope. If the player wins, the crown drops down to her head with her delight. If the player loses, she trips forward and cries.
*'''Level 2:''' A green female hippopotamus is skipping the rope. The player must jump twice over the rope. If the player wins, she will go to the screan and kiss it, leaving a lipstick mark. If the player loses, her make-up and eyes wil fall off and leave behind beady eyes.
*'''Level 3:''' A sun being on legs clothed in a toga is skipping the rope. The player must jump thrice over the rope. If the player wins, a body with a moon head will fall and attach to its body as they celebrate. If the player loses, the legs will be removed from the head as it falls, impaling the ground.
