'''Twisted Mansion''' is a course in  ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is the third course in the [[Flower Cup]].

== Layout ==
Twisted Mansion starts outside of a creepy mansion, similar to Luigi's Mansion. Once you go in, there are waves on the track. the track splits into 2, goes on the walls, and merges together. Then, there is a sharp turn, leading into water. Underwater, there are fishbones that you have to watch out for, or you may get bit. After a few 90 degree turns, you will go to some glide ramps, leading to the outside, where there are hammers smashing to the ground. Getting hit by a hammer has the same effect as getting hit by a Thwomp.. After that, you go back into the mansion, do a sharp turn, and go back out to the finish line.{{Sectstub}}
