'''Ball o' String Wario''' is a transformation of [[Wario]] which only appears in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Description==
The transformation involves Wario transforming into a yellow yarn ball, something that occurs if he comes into contact with either a [[Silky]]'s or an [[Anonster]]'s silk. As Ball o' String Wario, he rolls back and forth off walls uncontrollably. Wario eventually unravels and becomes dazed, then returns to normal. The Ball o' String form is required for breaking [[Yarn Block]]s.
