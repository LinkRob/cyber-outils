'''Gusty Gulch''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is a canyon-like desert located east of [[Forever Forest]] and [[Boo's Mansion]], and it has [[wind]] blowing harshly throughout (hence the "gusty" part of the name).

==In ''Paper Mario''==
In Gusty Gulch, there is a small, rundown village where the inhabitants are [[Boo]]s. They live peacefully here, but [[Tubba Blubba]] usually comes down from his [[Tubba Blubba's Castle|castle]] and eat a Boo every day. [[Mario]] comes here in search of a way to solve the mystery of his [[Tubba Blubba's Heart|invincibility]] and defeat him and claim the third [[Star Spirit]] from [[Lady Bow]]. There is a large [[Windy Mill|windmill]] located in the western part of this area. The "hyper" series of enemies are found in and around this area, and they are quite powerful in battle.
