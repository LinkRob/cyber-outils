'''Sort or 'Splode''' is a [[minigame]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', as one of [[Mario]]'s minigames, and returns in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', under the Variety category of minigames.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a factory, and is shown from an overhead view. At the upper-center is a conveyor belt where the [[Bob-omb]]s and [[Bob-omb Buddy|Bob-omb Buddies]] emerge from. In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', the floor is light blue and laminated, but in ''New Super Mario Bros.'', this was changed to metal flooring.

==Gameplay==
Sort or 'Splode is a minigame in which the object is to place the [[Bob-omb Buddy|Bob-omb Buddies]] into the red section and the black [[Bob-omb]]s into the black section. To do so, the player can use their [[Stylus]] to drag a Bob-omb into the appropriate section.

As the player progresses into the minigame, more Bob-ombs begin to emerge from the conveyor belt and move at a faster rate. If a Bob-omb explodes, either from being placed into the wrong section or remaining out in the open for too long, the minigame ends with the number of Bob-ombs being counted. If a Bob-omb is placed into the wrong section, any other Bob-ombs within that section explode as well, and they are not counted in the Bob-omb total at the end.

If the player manages to put forty Bob-ombs into a section, the Bob-ombs within that section are taken out and counted toward the game score to allow for more Bob-ombs to be placed there.

==Trivia==
*In ''New Super Mario Bros.'', there is a 1-vs-1 multiplayer minigame with similar gameplay, [[Bob-omb Sudden Death]], which has a similar layout and the same metal flooring as Sort or 'Splode.
