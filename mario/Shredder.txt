|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Shredder''' is a special move used by [[Smithy]] and [[Culex]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Shredder is used, a purple/pink light envelops the party.

==Effects==
Shredder is a move that basically nullifies any stats increase that the party may have received such as the attack and defense power-up from [[Geno Boost]]. This move also works against certain badges that grant stats increase.
