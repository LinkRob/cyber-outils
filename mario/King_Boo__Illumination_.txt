'''[[King Boo]]''' is a minor antagonist in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' seen having a cameo during the wedding scene.

==Biography==
He is the monarch of the [[Boo]]s, and is seen in the background of the wedding between [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] and Peach. He presumably flees upon King Bob-omb's fuse being lit. He was the only attendant of the wedding who didn't attack Peach after she freezes Bowser. He disappears when the wedding is crashed.

Hinting that Luigi's Mansion film is confirmed, King Boo will play a major role in the upcoming spin-off with a true appearance from Dark Moon and Luigi's Mansion 3. It's unknown if King Boo is the same one from the wedding like in the film.

==Trivia==
* Interestingly, he is the only attendant of the wedding who didn't attack Peach after she froze Bowser (with the exception of King Bomb-Omb, as he didn't attack Peach because he was sleeping). If there'll be a movie based on he game "Luigi's Mansion", it might be foreshadowing of his possible betrayal of Bowser.
*In the video games King Boo has been established as Luigi's Nemesis. In the movie the two never interact.
