'''Chief Leyden''' is a character from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Chief Leyden is a green-skinned [[Concordian]] man. He has bushy white eyebrows and a white moustache. He wears a blue long-sleeved robe with a red belt tied around his waist and dark blue trousers under the bottom half of his robe.

=== Personality ===
Leyden is portrayed as an incompetent leader, as his son and assistant describes him as sleeping on the job and being unable to change the bathroom lightbulb on by himself in his diary. Despite this, Leyden does care for his son and assistant.

== History ==
Leyden is first seen outside his house on [[Bulbfish Island]], surrounded by the islanders who blame him for stealing the Bulbfish eggs. Leyden declares his innocence and flees, running inside his house. Inside, he tasks [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] (who had arrived on the island) with finding the real culprit. Mario and Luigi deduce Leyden's assistant to be the perpetrator during their investigation, but Leyden is told by his assistant that [[Princess Burnadette]] of [[Skorcheen Island]] and [[Prince Chilliam]] from [[Slippenglide Island]] are the thieves, leading to their arrest.

The assistant is later caught as the perpetrator and confronted atop his boss's house, and threatens to throw the eggs into the sea. The assistant admits to how much he hated Leyden and schemed to usurp him. Leyden arrives, expressing his disappointment (though without surprise) that his son would do such a thing. However, Leyden accepts that he is at fault for being a poor parent. Leyden and his son apologise for their faults, but a fight breaks out. After the fight dies down, Leyden's son is sentenced to doing his father's work.

Leyden is also the central character for the "Leyden's Piscine PR Plans" side quest, where planning to find a new fish for Bulbfish Island to produce in hopes of gaining the respect and praise of his citizens and son. After receiving an Offin and an Egoby from the Bros., he rewards them with three Ultra Mushrooms and a Max Syrup Jar.

During the [[Reclusa World]] chapter of the game, Leyden gets captured by one of [[Reclusa]]'s [[Reclusa Dream Flower|flowers]] and trapped in his own fantasy. This is relayed by the [[IDLE]] gang when [[Snoutlet]] considers getting a [[Bond (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Bond]] from Leyden and his assistant.

Leyden can be seen in [[Club Dyode]] on [[Jellyfish Island]] during the credits, where he converses with another Concordian man.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Leyden derives his name from the Leyden jar.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Chief Leyden is called ''Rōden'' (written ロウデン); his name is written in Katakana, but is otherwise identical to ''rōden'' ("leaking electricity"; written 漏電). His Chinese name, ''Lòudiàn'' (written 鏤殿), is a homophone of ''lòudiàn'' ("leaking electricity"; written 漏電). Korean refers to him as ''Nucheon'' (written 누천), a mutation of ''nujeon'' ("leaking electricity," written 누전) His Portuguese name is ''Geravô'', from ''gerador'' ("generator") and ''avô'' ("grandfather"). Italian refers to him as ''Lúcio'', meaning "Lucius" and derived from ''luce'' ("light"). French refers to him as ''Milleyd'', from the Slavic name ''Milan'' and "LED" (an abbreviation of "light-emitting diode"). In German, his name is ''Energopio'', from ''Energie'' ("energy"). He is called "Batrei" in Dutch, from ''batterij'' ("battery").
