A '''Dark Cursya''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Dark Cursyas are darker, stronger versions of [[Cursya]]s.
=== Behavior ===
Dark Cursyas carry any curse of the other Cursyas in the game excluding the troublesome [[Back Cursya]]. The best way to take them out is to attack from a distance with [[Bowser]]'s fire breath.

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
Dark Cursyas are enemies in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are only found in the [[Pit of 100 Trials#Flopside|Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]].
