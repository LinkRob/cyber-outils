The '''Dried Bouquet''' is an item which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
It is received after completing [[Petalburg]] resident [[Bub-ulber]]'s [[Trouble Center|trouble]] titled "The Food I Want." [[Mario]] trades a [[Hot Dog]] for a Dried Bouquet. When used, these flowers restore one [[Heart Point]]. Dried Bouquets' main usage is being used to create many [[recipe]]s. Bub-ulber will continue to give Mario Dried Bouquets as long he brings him Hot Dogs in return.
