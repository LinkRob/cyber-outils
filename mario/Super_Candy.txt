'''Super Candies''' are items in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Description==
Super Candies are a variant of [[Candy (Mario & Luigi series)|candy]] which can restore more [[Heart Point|HP]] than its base counterpart. They have the appearance of an orange star. It can be used by either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to restore roughly 60% of their maximum [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Bros. Point|BP]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
Super Candies are dropped by various enemies.
