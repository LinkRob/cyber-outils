'''Konotako''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are winged, bomb-like octopu monsters with several suction cups on their undersides.

===Behavior===
Konotako attack [[Wario]] by hovering over him and then self-detonating. A Konotako can use its suction-cups to latch on to Wario's head.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
Konotako are encountered in two [[level]]s, [[Course No.10]] and [[Course No.13]].
