The '''Super Star Cup''' is a [[cup]] in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Strikers''==
Super Star Cup is a Super Cup that can be unlocked by being one of the top three winners of the [[Super Flower Cup]]. It is basically the same as the [[Star Cup]] but with return matches. Eight teams participate in the Super Star Cup, and there are 14 rounds played. If the player is one of the top three winners, the [[Super Bowser Cup]] is unlocked. Furthermore, if the player wins the Super Star Cup in first place, the "Weak Goalies" cheat is unlocked.
