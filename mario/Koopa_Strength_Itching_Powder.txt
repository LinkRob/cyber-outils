'''Koopa Strength Itching Powder''' is an item in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Jungle Fever]]."

==Description==
It is an itching powder of [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s. Whoever is doused with the Koopa Strength Itching Powder becomes increasingly itchy, to the point of it becoming unbearable.
