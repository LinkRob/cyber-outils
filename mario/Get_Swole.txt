'''Get Swole''' is one of the [[microgame]]s in [[Jimmy T]]'s Sports level in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''

==Gameplay==
This microgame features a man holding a weight, and the character is different between each difficulty level. The player has to lift the man's arm twice, either through direct contact or from using attacks, to win the microgame. If unsuccessful, the player's character loses at the microgame.

In two-player mode, the arm may appear on the opposite side. Whoever lifts the arm twice receives an effort point, but if each player's character lifts the arm only once, no effort points are awarded.

=== Suitability ===
[[Orbulon]] is on the highest suitability tier because he can lift the arm using his tractor beam.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The [[w:c:rhythmheaven:Wrestler|Wrestler]] from the ''[[w:c:rhythmheaven:Rhythm Heaven (series)|Rhythm Heaven]]'' game [[w:c:rhythmheaven:Ringside|Ringside]] is holding a dumbbell, which the player's character has to help him lift, and the Wrestler's arm takes little movement to lift. Winning on this difficulty level may cause the [[w:c:rhythmheaven:Reporter|Reporter]] to appear.
*'''Level 2:''' The player has to help Hercules lift a fish. Hercules's arm takes more movement to lift than the Wrestler's. Winning on this difficulty level may cause the cherub to appear.
*'''Level 3:''' The player has to help a man with a horse's head lift a tree. This man's arm takes takes a lot of movement to lift. Winning the microgame may cause a white horse to appear.
