A '''Sniper Bill''' is a large, flexible [[Bullet Bill]] that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its remake, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They wear brown fingerless gloves and wield [[Super Scope]]s, holding them in a manner akin to a sniper's positioning while wearing a grimace on their faces. In the remake, Sniper Bills no longer have a mouth nor brown fingerless gloves, and more resemble a standard Bullet Bill.

Sniper Bills are found in [[Bowser's Castle]], where they do not appear in the overworld, and are fought alongside [[Boo]]s and [[Magikoopa]]s.

==Battle==
[[File:MLSS Sniper Bill battle.png|thumb|left|A Sniper Bill and [[Magikoopa]] battle in the original game]]
Sniper Bills attack [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] by firing smaller Bullet Bills from their Super Scope at the Bros., then flying at them like a regular Bullet Bill. If the scope on Mario or Luigi is small, the bullet hits before the Sniper Bill; if the scope is large, the Sniper Bill will hit first. The attack can be avoided and countered by jumping, though the Sniper Bill will take no damage if hit and simply "walk" back to its original position. They are healed by fire, but are weak to thunder.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', their attack pattern remains the same as in the original, though they will now take damage if countered.
