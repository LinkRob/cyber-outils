A '''Hop-Chop''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. Hop Chops are first encountered in [[Big Bounce Byway]] in [[World 4 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 4]] and can be found later in [[Searchlight Sneak]] in [[World 5 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 5]].

== Description ==
Hop-Chops appears to be a green springboard with angry red eyes and sharp teeth; their jaws are separated by springs with yellow joints.

== Behaviour ==
Typically encountered in groups, Hop-Chops attack their prey by chomping. Stomping on or attacking certain Hop-Chops will turn them into a normal springboard that the characters can use to jump on and bounce into the air. However, the Hop-Chop will revert after some time.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The name Hop-Chop comes from "chop-chop" and "hop."

== Trivia ==
* When jumped on, it makes the noise when jumping on a spring in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.
* It has the same eyes as a Bully's.
* Strangely, ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' uses the plural form of the name in the singular. This could be an error.
