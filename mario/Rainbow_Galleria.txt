'''Rainbow Galleria''' is a board that appears in [[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]. It takes place in a shopping mall that consists of a ground floor and two elevated floors, which can be climbed up towards or climbed down from through pink and blue escalators. Players can also use the elevator to go between the ground floor and top floor. In this board, a flash sale will occasionally take place, in which Stars and items can be purchased for half the price for a limited amount of turns. There appear to be a number of shops, such as one with a [[Peepa]] and a [[Boo Bell]] design motif. There are also four colored stamp stations (green, blue, pink, and orange) located throughout the board.
