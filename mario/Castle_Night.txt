'''Castle Night''' is one of the [[Gaddget]]s in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. The player can unlock it in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], where they receive it from [[Mechakoopa (character)|Mechakoopa]] after correctly answering a few of his math questions.

==Description==
The purpose of Castle Night is display a [[castle]] against a starry sky with [[firework]]s. The main thing that the player can do is customize the castle with lights, flowers, and fireworks. They can also toggle between turning the lights on and off by pressing {{button|gba|A}}, have flowers appear in the bushes by pressing {{button|gba|B}}, or detonate two sets of fireworks by pressing {{button|gba|L}} and {{button|gba|R}}.
