'''World 5-A''' is the first bonus level in [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''It can be unlocked by spending five [[Star Coin]]s. 

==Layout==
This level has [[Seesaw Shrooms|Stretch Shrooms]]. Mario must time his jumps, because not all of the platforms move at the same time as each other. After the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], Mario can get the first [[Star Coin]] by going down to a Stretch Shroom. He needs to hit a [[! Switch]] to return. Just after that, Mario should find a single block. If he hits it by running and jumping, a [[Beanstalk]] pops out, taking him to an area with the second Star Coin. The player should later see two Mushroom Platforms that are able to touch each other. Mario should stand in between them and jump when they shrink, getting the last Star Coin. Then, Mario reaches the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''' - Mario should go to the Stretch Shroom near the red [[! Switch]]. After it expands, he can quickly grab the first Star Coin located to the right.
*'''Star Coin 2''' - After hitting the block by either running and jumping or with a Koopa [[shell]], Mario can climb the beanstalk and collect the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''' - Right after the Stretch Shrooms near the Goal Pole contract, Mario should land on a lower Stretch Shroom to get the final Star Coin.
