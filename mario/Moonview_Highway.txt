'''Moonview Highway''' is the second track of the [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== Layout ==
The first portion of the course is set in the outskirts of a city, being highly reminiscent of a [[wikipedia:touge|touge]]. This leads through a toll booth into the highway section of the track, which contains multiple [[Boost Pad|boost pads]] and several tricky turns. The traffic is constant throughout with the hazard on the track; cars, trucks, and [[Bob-omb Car]]s are persistent obstacles, seeming to increase with each lap. The former two can flatten racers in a head-on collision and bowl them over in a side or rear collision. Bob-omb Cars are dangerous due to explosions every time they're hit by a racer. The music is very much like fast city jazz. There is piano and a jazzy saxophone that plays fast harmonies. At a point of the track, you will always hear a police cop siren passing by. You will also always hear a train going over the bridge which you drive under, and, like the police car, it cannot be seen. Traffic travels in both directions. In normal mode, traffic drives on the left side, while in mirror mode, this is reversed and traffic drives on the right side. As you enter the city and there are two lanes, cars and trucks can also change between these lanes at will, it even changes the music to soothing. The world record for this race is 01:44.120 - it was won by someone in the USA. There is also a building in the course with [[Princess Peach]] on it and there are some words like "moon".

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Moonview Highway appears in the tour after the [[Mario vs. Luigi Tour (2023)|Mario vs. Luigi Tour]] called the [[Night Tour (2023)|Night Tour]]. A notable change on the billboard of Peach depicts her afraid of Bowser including the billboard showcases a musical.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ==

Moonview Highway appears in the [[Feather Cup]]. Like most tracks in the [[Booster Course Pass]], most assets were taken from ''Tour''.
