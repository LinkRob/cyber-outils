'''Pokey''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. He is a representative of his species of the [[Pokey|same name]], and bears an identical appearance to them.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Pokey appears in the [[Desert Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], introducing himself as the desert's guardian. Pokey has three riddles, which he calls the "Riddles of Mystery," and if the player answers all three correctly, Pokey rewards them the [[Tap-Tap Sumo]] [[Gaddget]].
