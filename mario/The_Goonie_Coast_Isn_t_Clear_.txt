'''The Goonie Coast Isn't Clear!''' is the second level of [[World 3 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 3]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', a [[Pink Yoshi]] takes control in this level. This level takes place on a beach with many pirate ships manned by [[Pirate Guy]]s and armed with [[Bill Blaster]]s on the ready. Hence the level's name, there are many [[Goonie]]s flying through the open skies, this level also marks the debut of [[Blooper]]s, [[Boss Bass]]es, and [[Bullet Bill]]s. This level also autoscrolls all throughout requiring Yoshi to keep moving as he progresses and make quick movements to avoid falling behind.

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, as the level moves, advance to the right collecting [[Coin]]s as you move along the path, from here, hop onto the boat and beat the [[Pirate Guy]] and [[Tube Guy]] here, from here, hop onto the island and hit the [[Fly Guy]] above to grab it's [[Red Coin]], kill the [[Piranha Plant]] at the island's end and jump to the next set of boats. Around the boats are [[Pirate Guy]]s and [[Tube Guy]]s, beat these pests and jump by one of the boat flags to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]], hit it to obtain some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and continue to the right, avoid the [[Cheep Cheep]]s as you reach the [[Stork Stop]], tag the [[Stork Stop]] to switch to [[Baby DK]] (if you haven't already) and climb the vine to reach the ship's head (hopping to collect the [[Smiley Flower]] by the neck as you do) (you can still reach the ship with other babies via the [[Flatbed Ferry]] but it is trickier as it does require some waiting). 


Note: As you get on the ships, there are several routing paths you can take on the masts and the decks, due to the level's scrolling nature, you typically are only able to take one path, below will list all the ideal paths to take which contain the key collectibles.

Upon reaching the ship's "head" advance to the right and remain on the deck watching out for the [[Pirate Guy Stack]]s as you go, from here, drop down the flooring and move to the right corner to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]], hit it to get another [[Smiley Flower]], from here, hop up the thin mast and grab the [[Red Coin]] atop it (or toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at it), from here, advance to the right and hit the [[! Switch]] to activate the switch blocks, run across them and collect the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]], hit the [[Winged Cloud]] at the path's end to get yet another [[Smiley Flower]], once done, hop up to the [[Donut Lift]]s before the switch blocks deactivate and jump the next ship from here, watch out for the [[Pirate Guy]]s and [[Pirate Guy Stack]]s ([[Baby DK]]'s egg tosses can make quick work of these pests) and jump to the mast, from here, grab the two [[Red Coin]]s here, you can climb the vine if you want to (there is a hidden [[Winged Cloud]] containing [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] by the ship's flag), once this is done, drop down to the decks and ground pound the two [[Stake]]s, there are [[Red Coin]]s hidden inside these [[Stake]]s, after they've been ground pounded, advance to the right and tag the [[Stork Stop]] to switch to [[Baby Peach]], use her abilities to float with the wind. 

As the screen scrolls and as you glide across the skies, collect the numerous [[Coin]]s, three of these [[Coin]]s at the top near the end are [[Red Coin]]s, additionally, there is also a [[Smiley Flower]] at the end so be sure to collect this too (also watch out for the [[Goonie]]s near the end), (if you decide to take the lower path for whatever reason, you will have to swim across waters with [[Blooper]]s constantly hopping out, additionally there are also [[Bullet Bill]]s being fired out of a [[Bill Blaster]] on the island as well as [[Pirate Guy]]s and [[Tube Guy]]s to watch out for). Once you reach the boat, hop by the flag to reveal another [[Winged Cloud]], collect the [[Coin]]s that pop out and beat the remaining [[Pirate Guy]]s and from here, advance to the next area.

Upon entering the next area, tag the [[Middle Ring]] and collect the [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] (or [[Coin]]s) that emerge from the [[Tube Guy]]s and [[Pirate Guy]]s in the area, from here, hop onto the boat and jump by the flag to reveal yet another hidden [[Winged Cloud]], once hit, it will reveal some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] (or [[Coin]]s if you have 30 [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]]) for you to collect, once you collect these, beat the [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[Pirate Guy]]s on the small island and tag the [[Stork Stop]] here (but don't switch out if you're already [[Baby Peach]] because you still need her), hit the [[! Switch]] to activate the wind from the [[Pipe]] on the island and use the wind to glide in the air and collect the [[Coin]]s, (the four [[Coin]]s closest to the top center are [[Red Coin]]s (if you lose momentum, hop on the [[Goonie]]s and collect the [[Coin]]s this way but be sure to stay ahead of the scrolling screen)). From here, drop down to the boat below, beat the [[Pirate Guy]]s, and hit the [[Baron Von Zeppelin]]s as they're carrying a [[Spring Ball]] and a [[Large Spring Ball]] respectively, use either [[Spring Ball]] to reach the [[Donut Lift]]s above (there is also a hidden [[Winged Cloud]] in the ship's "neck" containing some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] (but you will have to act quickly to get this one due to the scrolling screen), once you reach the ship, tag the next [[Stork Stop]] on a platform above and switch to [[Baby DK]], from here, advance to the right onto the ship's deck and watch out for the [[Needlenose Plant]] below, from here, climb the vine and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] by the ship's flag to get a [[Key]], also be sure to watch out for the [[Bullet Bill]]s, from here, drop down and smash the [[Box]] in the small ship-pit to get some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]]. After this, hop across the [[Donut Lift]]s to reach the next ship.

Once you hit the deck, collect some [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] from the [[Egg Plant]] on this ship, from here, advance to the right and switch to [[Baby Peach]] at the next [[Stork Stop]], once done, continue moving to the right and once the lower path comes into view, drop down and grab the [[Baby Coin|Baby Peach Coin]] and use the [[Key]] you obtained earlier to enter the door below.

Once below-decks, hop up to in the center-parting to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]] which contains a 1-Up, from here, drop down and jump to the top right platform in the corner and hop up to the corner of the platform to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]] which contains a [[! Switch]], hit the switch to turn all the [[Dash Coin]]s in the area into [[Coin]], collect all these [[Coin]]s and jump to the top left platform in the corner to reveal another [[Winged Cloud]], this one contains a [[Key]] which is needed to enter the door to the right where you can play a minigame ([[Bouncy Maze]]), once done, move to the left and tag the [[Middle Ring]] before exiting the room through the door.

Once you're back above decks, grab the [[Smiley Flower]] by the door (the screen won't start scrolling again until you jump to the upper ledge), once you jump to the ledge and grab the [[Red Coin]], from here, hop up the mast and toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] above to reveal a [[Large Spring Ball]], use it to reach a higher area in the clouds, in this area, there is a [[! Switch]] which makes all the [[Coin]]s visible (none of these [[Coin]]s are [[Red Coin]]s but they are useful for earning extra lives), tag the [[Morph Bubble]] to switch to the [[Yoshi Helicopter]] and collect all the [[Coin]]s, once you've gotten everything, exit the area through the [[Pipe]] to the right.

Once you return to the ship, advance to the right whilst avoiding the remaining [[Bullet Bill]]s and [[Pirate Guy Stack]]s, from here, move to the right and descend down the path and hop to the stone slab in the water (also beat the [[Pirate Guy]] on it), hit the [[Egg Block]] to fill up your supply but be sure to jump off it as the slab starts to slowly sink in the water after you stand on it, from here, hit the [[Baron Von Zeppelin]]s in the sky holding 1-Ups but be sure to watch out for the [[Grenade]]-carrying [[Baron Von Zeppelin]]s (be sure to stay out of the water and make leaps on the slabs, a [[Boss Bass]] is patrolling the waters here and it would love to eat you). Hit the [[Winged Cloud]] in this area to raise all the stone slabs and collect the final three [[Red Coin]]s along them (there is also a 1-Up-containing hidden [[Winged Cloud]] on the lowest slab to the right), once you reach the upper ledge, move with the screen to the right and watch out for the [[Pirate Guy]], [[Pirate Guy Stack]], and the [[Bill Blaster]] firing [[Bullet Bill]]s as you move towards the level's end. Once done, jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
On the final ship (the one with the [[Egg Plant]] at the beginning), tag the [[Stork Stop]] and switch to [[Baby Peach]], once you do, move with the screen to the right and upon passing the lowest [[Bill Blaster]], wait for the lower decks to come into view as the screen scrolls revealing the [[Baby Coin|Baby Peach Coin]], drop down to the ledge to grab the [[Baby Coin]].
