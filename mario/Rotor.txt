The '''Rotor''' is an object in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' that appears in the [[Old Clockworks]] Mansion. It is needed to open the [[Clock Tower Gate]] door. [[Luigi]] first sees it in the grasp of a [[Strong Scarer]] who is trying to hide it in C-5: [[Piece at Last]]. After finding the [[Toad]] Maintenance worker and capturing all the [[Ghosts]], Luigi gets the Rotor and can use it to open the Clock Tower Gate Door in C-Boss: [[Showtime]]. Other rotors appear in several other mansions however this is the only one that is classified as a "Rotor" and it is also the only one that isn't already attached.
