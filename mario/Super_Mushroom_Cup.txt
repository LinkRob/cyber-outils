The '''Super Mushroom Cup''' is an unlockable Super Cup in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''.

==Description==
The Super Mushroom Cup is basically a double version of the [[Mushroom Cup]]. It consists of four teams participating in six different rounds.

==In ''Super Mario Strikers''==
The Super Mushroom Cup can be unlocked by beating the [[Bowser Cup (Super Mario Strikers)|Bowser Cup]]. When the player wins the Super Mushroom Cup in first place, they unlock the "Perfect Strike" cheat.
