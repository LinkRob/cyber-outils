'''Sneak 'n' Snore''' is a 4-player mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. It is similar to [[Barrel Peril]] from ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
Four players wear barrels. They must attempt to sneak past a [[Chain Chomp]] to escape the dungeon. If a player presses his or her button at one end, he or she must go back to the other side of the room to the open door. When the Chain Chomp awakens, which it does repeatedly, it rejects any exposed player from the [[minigame]] by dropping him or her in a [[Warp Pipe]].

The player who presses the button and gets to the open door first wins Sneak 'n' Snore.
