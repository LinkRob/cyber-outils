'''Starlow''' (known as '''Chippy''' by [[Bowser]]) is the deuteragonist of the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}. She first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. Starlow is a Star Sprite who helps [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] by giving tips and carrying items, as well as possessing special powers.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Starlow appears as a yellow ball with two brown feet and a pale-yellow star on top.

===Personality traits===
{{main|Starlow/Relationships}}
Starlow has a very upbeat personality, since she always seems to be in a good mood. However, she mainly gets annoyed whenever Bowser is acting pushy, or when Luigi is acting scared and cowardly. She is also very sarcastic. Her relationship with Luigi starts out far colder in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'', with her often expressing annoyance at his cowardice and focusing her attention on Mario. Even so, she acknowledges that he is a hero like his brother, and in Mario & Luigi: Dream Team, she is much more open with him, saying he looks cute when he sleeps and expressing genuine concern when his dreams seem to be troubling him. Starlow claims she is jealous of Luigi's ability to sleep. Her attitude towards Luigi remains similar in ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'' and ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'', as she treats Luigi with more respect while still occasionally making fun of him.

Starlow shares a very good friendship with Peach, with the two being very like-minded and even being able to produce powerful combo attacks together. Starlow holds a high amount of respect for the Princess, and puts her safety before anything else.

===Powers and abilities===
Like [[Tippi]], [[Kersti]], [[Huey]], [[Pixl]]s, [[Pi'illo]]s and [[Dreambert]], Starlow has the ability to float, and she uses this ability to move around freely in midair. She can also open the Star Menu, which is similar to [[Stuffwell]] from the previous games, except it has demos of the special attacks Mario and Luigi have unlocked. In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'', Starlow is shown to be quite strong, as she could carry both Mario Bros. easily in the boss fights again Bowser, the [[Dark Star]], and the [[Dark Star Core]]. In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'', Starlow can summon a gust of [[wind]], but she only ever does so one time.

When she teams up with Peach, she can move heavy objects with telekinesis and shoot a pink laser from her star.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'', Starlow is also shown to be powerful when she attacks [[Larry]] and [[Ludwig]] in Twinsy Tropics Dungeon using her "[[Supernova Sparkle]]". She can also be able to hold onto ledges using her teeth, when [[Kamek]] was trying to blow them away.

== History ==
=== {{series|Mario & Luigi}} ===
==== ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' ====
[[File:Starlow Saved Screenshot - MLBiS.png|thumb|left|Starlow thanking Mario for saving her.]]
Starlow is sent to Princess Peach's meeting as a representative of the Star Sprites. Starlow believes that the cause of the [[Blorbs]] was from [[Blorb Mushroom]]s sold by a [[Fawful|man in a white cloak]]. Later, she is inhaled into [[Bowser's body]], along with Mario, Luigi, and other citizens of the Mushroom Kingdom. When Mario finds her, she is trapped by an amoeba who is holding her inside him. She remarks on how the amoeba is tickling her with rage.

Starlow has the same purpose as [[Stuffwell]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' by being Mario and Luigi's main assistant during their journey. Also like Stuffwell, she holds Mario and Luigi's items and equipment. However, Starlow has a demo version of all the abilities learned throughout the brothers' adventure, unlike Stuffwell. She can combine her powers with [[Princess Peach]] to telekinetically control people, such as Bowser, and also help Mario and Luigi to perform certain action commands. Starlow often talks to Bowser from inside of him to give him information. Starlow uses Chippy as her name for Bowser, not wanting him to know that she is helping the Mario Bros. She also helps Mario and Luigi in their encounter with Bowser. She can also save the player from nearly unavoidable attacks from bosses like Bowser, the [[Dark Star]] and the [[Dark Star Core]], by having Mario and Luigi grab her feet so she can fly up to the top screen.

Starlow appears to have negative feelings towards Luigi, and this negativity towards him sometimes generates humorous results, such as when Starlow angrily knocks Luigi into [[Alpha Kretin]] after Luigi suggests that Starlow fight it alongside Mario rather than him. She only demonstrates this when Luigi is acting particularly cowardly. 

If the bros. travel to [[Bowser's Castle]] and engage in the optional boss fight against the Shroobs, Starlow expresses curiousity on how they handled this fight as babies. Starlow immediately expresses confidence that Mario was the one who came out on top overall. This somewhat upsets Luigi and Mario explains quickly that Luigi did just as much as himself. It is unclear how Starlow felt hearing this. It is unclear whether this gave her more confidence in Luigi's abilities or if she still lacks confidence in him based on his prior cowardly actions.

At the end of the game, when Starlow and the other protagonists are ejected out of [[Bowser's body]], Bowser almost immediately recognizes her voice as "Chippy" and gets furious, deciding to pick a fight with her, Mario, and Luigi. When [[Bowser's Castle]] and [[Princess Peach's Castle|Peach's Castle]] were remodeled, Starlow and [[Birdley]] deliver a present to Bowser from Peach as a thanks for all he did, and they also have a small chat, ending with Starlow teasing Bowser, who then demands her to leave his castle, which she does.

==== ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' ====
[[File:Luigi&StarlowDreamTeam.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Starlow watching over [[Luigi]] sleep, from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.]]
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', Starlow was invited to [[Pi'illo Island]] to represent the Star Sprites. Like in the previous game, she acts as Mario and [[Luigi]]'s partner and guide.

This time around, she serves another purpose rather than just keep Mario and Luigi's supplies safe. She keeps an eye on Luigi while he's asleep in other [[Pi'illo]]s that were [[Nightmare Chunk|chunked]], whereas [[Mario]] and [[Prince Dreambert|Dreambert]] enter the [[Dream World]]. When [[Dreamy Luigi]] senses, does, or feels something, he causes Luigi's in-dream reactions by pulling and tapping on various parts of his face and moving his hand towards buttons. Her looking down on Luigi remains, and Luiginary Works are discovered when she pulls on Luigi's mustache because she is mad at him for causing all the trouble. However, their chemistry develops between the two as the game progresses.

When Bowser is confronted at the top of [[Neo Bowser Castle]] at the ending, together with Peach under [[Prince Dreambert]]'s command, Starlow destroys the [[Dream Stone]], only for its broken shards to be inhaled by Bowser, leading to his transformation into [[Dreamy Bowser]].

==== ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'' ====
In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'', Starlow is the only character exclusive to appear in the game. Like in the previous games, she still acts as the brothers' guide. However, she is the one who gives out the fighting tutorial, unlike in the predecessors where it was [[Toadsworth]] instead.

Starlow helps Mario and  Luigi to search for missing [[Paper Toad|Paper Toads]] that are scattered across the Mushroom Kingdom, while deal with Bowser alongside his [[Paper Bowser|his paper counterpart]] and [[Koopa Troop|his entire army]]. As Mario, Luigi and Starlow enter [[Sunbeam Plains]], they encounter two [[Paper Goomba|Paper Goombas]], the trio crumples [[Crinkle Goomba|one]] and battles them defeated. After two Paper Goombas leave, Starlow tells the duo she has a weird shivery feeling hoping it was just a breeze and not something more ominous, possibly preferring to what's happening at Bowser's Castle. As they proceed, they soon encounter Crinkle Goomba again, only this time more Paper Goomba reforcements as they use a special move called "Super Goomba Super Wheel" and ultimately defeat Mario and Luigi. Starlow tells Mario and Luigi to keep it together not let it get them go down. Crinkle Goomba responds the weaskest of them are million times stronger than Mario and Luigi. Starlow tells him big words for a little goomba, almost as big as their eyebrows. She tries to get Mario up, but to her shock notices [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] float by grab the Paper Goombas and defeat them. Mario, Luigi and Starlow join Paper Mario throughout their journey, while also rescue Princess Peach and [[Paper Princess Peach]].

==== ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' + ''Bowser's Minions''====
===== ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'' =====
Starlow had already arrived in the Mushroom Kingdom well before the [[blorbs]] [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|epidemic incident]]. While enjoying a vacation at the [[Gwarhar Lagoon]], she is convinced by her admirer, [[Spangle]], to enter [[Wendy O. Koopa]]'s beauty contest along with two [[Starkiss]]es. Starlow reluctantly accepts, but she is then captured, along with a [[Bob-omb]], by [[Hermie III]], who mistakes them for Christmas decorations. Starlow manages to free both herself and the Bob-omb, and as she argues with Spangle for not saving her, [[Bowser's Minions]] find out about Wendy's contest. [[Captain Goomba (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Goomba]] also comments that he wouldn't mind having Dorastella as a teammate. Starlow, Spangle, and the two Starkisses all win the contest ultimately, after the Goomba Squad retreats when Wendy is freed from her brainwashing.

Starlow can be unlocked as a recruitable ally after completing [[Showdown Time!!]], along with [[Prince Peasley]]. Her special skill is Dive Attack, in which she dives into an opponent from above.

==== ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' + ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'' ====
===== ''Bowser Jr's. Journey'' =====
As Mario and Luigi encounter and confront Bowser Jr. and Kamek in Bubble Lake, Starlow quickly stops them, telling they have no time to fight while trying to search for the second [[Star Cure]], while also pretend they didn't see "Bowser's kid". This triggers Junior, saying he's more than just a kid and tells what she is calling her a "yellow ball with feet". Starlow rudely confronts him, saying she has a shiny star on her head, both continue to argue and stare eye to eye angrily at each other. Kameks immediately stops them, telling him they do not have time to deal with the trio. Starlow snaps herself out of it, agreeing with Kamek, as she and the Bros. duo leave them. Before leaving, Starlow gives Junior a peace offering and tells that Bowser is "[[Giant Bowser|working hard]]" and that Junior should be proud of him. She even tells they're alike. When Junior asks where his father is, she tells he'll will find out soon enough, she then leaves.

==== ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ====
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', Starlow is swept away with the rest of the Mushroom Kingdom residents into the [[Concordia]], reuniting with Peach on [[Merrygo Island]]. Sensing that Mario and Luigi are nearby, Starlow flies across the [[Color-Full Sea]] to look for them until she stops to rest on [[Shipshape Island]]. She finds Mario and Luigi, informing them that Peach is waiting for them on the island. Once recovered, Starlow and Peach befriend the [[IDLE]]s, later becoming members of the team and setting off with them to scout [[Dorrie]] in the [[Brrrning Sea]].

Starlow's role of being Mario and Luigi's main companion is fulfilled by [[Snoutlet]] instead.


==Crossover appearances==
=== {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} ===
==== ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' ====
Starlow appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a support spirit. Her spirit can be obtained by battling in [[World of Light]] or battling at the Spirit Board. Starlow's spirit is purchasable from the Vault Shop for 1000G. In World of Light, her spirit can be found at the Dark Realm in the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Mysterious Dimension|Mysterious Dimension sub-area]], her spirit can be found in the quiz, "Which of these is a Nopon?" (as a wrong answer).
