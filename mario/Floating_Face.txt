'''Floating Face''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

== Profile ==
This enemy is found in only one level, Wario's Castle. The Floating Face's are found in a small room in Wario's Castle. They can be defeated by stomping on them or tossing fireballs at them. Floating Faces bounce off when they hit a surface.<ref name="SMBE">''They bounce off the walls in their rooms - Super Mario Bros. Encyclopedia (description of the Floating Face)''.</ref>

== Trivia ==
* A myth was formed that there is a golden Floating Face in a secret room in Wario's Castle. It was said that killing the golden Floating Face will grant the player 10000000 points and the choice to play as [[Wario]]. This is obviously not true.
* The Floating Faces have a striking resemblance to Wario himself.
