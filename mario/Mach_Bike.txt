'''Mach Bike''' is a medium [[bike]] which appears in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== Description ==
The Mach Bike is a fast bike with the highest drift and mini-turbo stats in ''Mario Kart Wii''. It does not have the same speed as the [[Flame Runner]], but the outstanding mini-turbo boosts compensates for this.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' ==
The Mach Bike is one of the mediumweight bikes available at the start of the game.

The Mach Bike is the vehicle used by the expert [[Staff Ghost]]s' character at [[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart Wii)|Luigi Circuit]], [[Moonview Highway]] and [[Daisy Circuit]], specifically [[Luigi]] by Nin★Uta♪, [[Princess Peach]] by Nin★Koh, and [[Princess Daisy]] by Nin★Taeko, respectively.

== In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' ==
[[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]] rides a Mach Bike in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' which references her ''Mario Kart Wii'' artwork. She used it to drive over [[Rainbow Road]].

== Real-world influences ==
The Mach Bike has been considered the most optimal bike in ''Mario Kart Wii'' due to its high speed and high drift. [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] is the most common racer used for the Mach Bike for having the highest speed of all mediumweights.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.reddit.com/r/MarioKartWii/comments/k1j1cu/best_mach_bike_characters_besides_daisy_and_why/|title=Best mach bike characters besides Daisy and why?|work=Reddit|accessdate=2023-05-29}}</ref> The Mach Bike had world records on tracks requiring a lot of drifting or that rely more on the Mini-turbo stat than the speed stat, such as [[Mario Circuit 3]].
