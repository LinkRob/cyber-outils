==Effects==
Eating a Town Special only restores 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]. Cooking a Town Special requires a [[Primordial Fruit]] and must be given to [[Saffron]]. However, a Primordial Fruit restores 10 HP which is more than a Town Special. Because of this, it is only recommended to cook a Town Special to fill up the recipes list.
