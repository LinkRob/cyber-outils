'''Red Grabbing Ghosts''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Red Grabbing Ghosts are a variant of regular [[Grabbing Ghost]]s, and the only difference that Red Grabbing Ghosts have with their appearance is that they have a red body instead of a lavender one.

===Behavior===
Like their regular counterpart, Red Grabbing Ghosts sneak behind [[Luigi]] before trying to grab him. Like its [[Mirror Ghost]] and [[Cinema Ghost]] relatives but unlike regular Grabbing Ghosts, a Red Grabbing Ghost can inflict damage onto Luigi by grabbing him, but a Red Grabbing Ghost eventually lets go once it has damaged him enough.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Red Grabbing Ghosts are first encountered in the [[Storage Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Storage Room]]. They have 20 [[Heart Point|HP]], exactly twice the amount of a regular Grabbing Ghost's 10 HP.
