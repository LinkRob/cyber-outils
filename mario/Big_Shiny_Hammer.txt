The '''Big Shiny Hammer''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' and is the second strongest regular [[Hammer]] sticker available.

== Description ==
The Big Shiny Hammer is very rare, only being sold in [[Outlook Point]], [[Decalburg]], and [[Rumble Volcano]]. It has a four - star attack power. This hammer does very good damage to ground or low altitude enemies. It can be sold for 13 [[Coin|coins]], and purchased for 25.

== Sticking Points ==
''A big, shiny Hammer boasting big, shiny damage. Definitely do not miss your swing, or you'll be really bummed out.''
