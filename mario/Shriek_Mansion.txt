'''Shriek Mansion''' is the third world of ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It is a location in [[Vibe Island]] in an haunted building with many puzzles, mazes, and spooky activity and surroundings. The mansion has many rooms and balconies. It also introduces several new enemies such as [[Boo]]s and [[Dry Bones]]. Fittingly, the boss of this world is the regal leader of the [[Boo]]s himself, [[King Boo]].
