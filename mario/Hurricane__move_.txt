'''Hurricane''' is a special move used by [[Lakilester]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can be used once Lakilester reaches the [[Ultra Rank]] and it requires 5 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.
==Usage==
To use Hurricane, the player must repeatedly press the {{button|n64|a}} and {{button|n64|b}} buttons at the same time to fill up the gauge. After a little while, Lakilester's cloud will blow a great wind that may blow away all enemies from the battle. However, this doesn't work on bosses and no [[Star Point]]s are earned from this.
==Trivia==
*[[Madame Flurrie]]'s [[Gale Force]] move from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' is similar to Lakilester's Hurricane move.
*Like [[Cloud Nine]], Hurricane makes use of Lakilester's cloud in battle.
