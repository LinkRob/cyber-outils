'''Gold Micro Goomba''' is a variant of both a [[Gold Goomba]] and a [[Micro Goomba]] featured as an enemy in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Gold Micro Goombas are Micro Goombas under the effects of a [[Gold Ring]], and act in the same manner but yield more [[coin]]s upon being defeated.
