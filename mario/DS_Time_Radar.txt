The '''DS Time Radar''' is one of [[Professor E. Gadd]]'s inventions which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==Description==
The DS Time Radar is a handheld device both named after and having the appearance of a [[Nintendo DS]]. The DS Time Radar has the ability to pinpoint which time period that one is stuck within.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''==
The DS Time Radar is used by E. Gadd on two separate occasions. The first occasion is after [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have defeated the [[Junior Shrooboid]], and E. Gadd uses the DS Time Radar to try and find which time period [[Princess Peach]] is in. At first, one of the [[Toad (species)|Toads]] worried about Princess Peach believes that E. Gadd made it up. The DS Time Radar reveals Peach to be in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] of the past. The second occasion is when E. Gadd powers on his [[Hydrogush 4000]].
