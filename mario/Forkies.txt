'''Forkies''' (both singular and plural) are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are rather unique-looking creatures which wield forks in battle, and are a stronger variant of [[Jawful]]s. Forkies are encountered in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]], where they usually fight alongside [[Star Cruster]]s and [[Tub-O-Troopa]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Forkies can attack physically by using their forks. However, they can use a move called [[Storm]] which can deal high damage due to their high magic attack.

===Strategy===
Forkies have no weaknesses and their magic defense is rather high so [[Mario]] and his team should stick to using physical attacks.
