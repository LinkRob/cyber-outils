The '''Star Shot''' is a game mechanic which appears in ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]''.

==Description==
The Star Shot is slightly stronger than [[Max Charge Shot]]s, that pushes the opponent if he/she hits the ball. To perform it, the opponent's ball must be on the air or the gauge must be filled one-third to make the Star Point appear. The player has to stand on the Star Point marked on the court. Once the ball is close to him/her, the player must return the ball at a high speed.

If the player performs the Star Shot and the opponent misses it, the player or team will be announced as the '''Star Shot Winner'''.
