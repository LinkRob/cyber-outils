'''Snack Sucker''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' It is hosted by [[Ashley (WarioWare)|Ashley]] the witch, being in the Food category. It makes its return in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', where [[Orbulon]] takes over as its host, with its location being in his Fantasy stage.

== Layout ==
In ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'', the [[Snack Sucker (character)|titular being]] appears in a sky with clouds. He manifests as a disembodied head with black eyes and rests at the top of the screen. In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', the setting is changed to a pink sea of clouds, and the Snack Sucker now appears at the side of the screen with a different design, with his mouth being changed to a funnel, and a series of black tentacles can be found hanging from his bottom side.

== Gameplay ==
The gameplay is the same between both versions. Snacks will be drawn towards the Snack Sucker, as well as non-edible objects which must be tapped to prevent him from eating them. The player will be defeated if they knock out a snack or let the Snack Sucker eat a non-food item.

In the first difficulty level, the Snack Sucker will attract three items. Level two has four items, and level three has five items.
