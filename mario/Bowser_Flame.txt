{{image}}The '''Bowser Flame''' is one of [[Bowser|Bowser's]] most used and most powerful attacks.

In both ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', it is Bowser's standard special attack.

The one time when a player can use this attack on will in a game original to the [[Mario]] Universe is during the Mario RPG series. Bowser charges up the flame in his mouth and then at the right time, a button must be pressed or an action must be done in order for the flame to deal megamassive damage to an opponent. In some cases, the flame may K.O. one of more opponents in its process. The flame is one of the only attacks that Bowser knows that can attack all enemies on the screen, but the player must keep notice that there are some enemies that are completely impervious to fire and some that are partially resistant to fire.
