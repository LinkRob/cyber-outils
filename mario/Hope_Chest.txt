The '''Hope Chest''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is given to [[Princess Peach]], and by extension the player, from [[Mr. I (character)|Mr. I]] by completing the [[Love That Princess!]] quest.

==Description==
The Hope Chest is a gag item intended to test the players' affinity for each other. One player has to lock a [[treasure chest]] by inputting a series of five buttons, then the other player has to guess and provide the correct input to unlock the chest, revealing a [[heart]].
