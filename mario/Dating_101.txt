'''Dating 101''' is a downloadable [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''.

==Gameplay==
The microgame starts with a girl telling the player to meet her at a certain location and at a certain time. After the girl leaves, a clock appears, starting at 1 and ticking as time passes. To win the microgame, the player has to tap the correct location at the right time on the clock, which will make the girl happy, and the player wins at the microgame. If either the incorrect location is tapped, the correct location is tapped at the wrong time, or the player waits too long, the player loses at the microgame, with the girl looking sad as she reminds the player of what the appointed time was.
