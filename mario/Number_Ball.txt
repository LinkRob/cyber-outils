'''Number Balls''' are an object in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Description==
Number Balls are a type of ball that strongly resemble {{w|billiard ball}}s, and each Number Ball shows a one-digit number, ranging from one to five. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can pick up a Number Ball simply by touching it and throw it like an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], with the intended purpose being for a Number Ball to land into its corresponding slot. Once every Number Ball is in their slot, something can activate, such as a door, allowing the Yoshi to continue further into the level. If a Number Ball is thrown anywhere outside of its slot, it respawns where it was originally found.

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
Number Balls appear in a few levels, including [[Number Ball Special]], [[Six-Face Sal's Fort]], and [[Find the Number Ball!]] Most Number Balls are well hidden.
