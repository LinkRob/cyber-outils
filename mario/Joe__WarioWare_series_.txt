'''Joe''' is a dog businessman who exclusively appears in the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' games, in which he debuted in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', and has returned in nearly every ''WarioWare'' game since. Throughout his appearances, Joe has run several different businesses, earning him a variety of titles, such as '''Gelato Joe''' when running the [[Gelateria]] or '''Pizza Joe''' when running [[Mona Pizza]] (even though [[Mona]] is the restaurant's mascot and namesake).
