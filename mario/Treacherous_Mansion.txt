The '''Treacherous Mansion''' is the fifth and final mansion of ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. The Treacherous Mansion is also the largest mansion, and it is most notable for having a total of three bosses. It is where Luigi and E. Gadd learn that [[King Boo]] is the mastermind behind the destruction of the [[Dark Moon]], and he has [[Mario]] yet again.

==Description==
The Treacherous Mansion is a massive sized mansion. It also has a museum theme containing many Exhibit-related rooms.
