The '''Secret Mine''' is the fourth mansion that [[Luigi]] explores in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' and ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]''.

== Description ==
The Secret Mine is mainly based on a snow theme. It also has the theme of an old mining place where the ghosts mainly the [[Boo]]s go to harvest crystals. It also contains new creatures called Fuzzballs.

==''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' / ''Luigi's Mansion 2 HD''==
Luigi comes to the Secret Mine in search of the fifth [[Dark Moon]] piece. He rescues a [[Toad]] assistant, who reveals that the Boos are using power-enhancing crystals to further strengthen the ghosts.

[[Professor Elvin Gadd]] later locates the Dark Moon piece at the [[Workshop (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Workshop]]. However, he has to break paranormal chains blocking the [[Gondola]] created by a [[Strong Greenie]], a [[Strong Slammer]] and a [[Strong Gobber]].

Once at the Workshop, Luigi defeats the [[Shrewd Possessor]] and obtains the fifth Dark Moon piece.
