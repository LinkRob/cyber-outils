== Description ==
The 2.5 [[Jakks Pacific]] figure line debuted in 2014 and is sold in waves at a variety of different stores: Walmart, Target, GameStop, Walgreens, Five Below, Party City, and others. Product releases and packaging vary, including checkout designs, blister packs, and figure with item packs.
