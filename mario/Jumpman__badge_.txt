'''Jumpman''' is a [[badge]] which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear the Jumpman by using two [[Badge Point|BP]]. While wearing the badge, Mario's attack increases, although he cannot use his [[hammer]] attacks during battle. However, Mario can still deal the first strike using a hammer attack from the overworld.

[[Charlieton]], a salesman who appears in [[Rogueport]], will sell this badge to Mario for 180 [[coin]]s.

== Etymology ==
In contrast to the badge [[Hammerman]], this badge is named Jumpman as Mario jumps instead of hammering and vice-versa. Jumpman is a reference to Mario's original nickname "Jumpman."
