'''Sir Worth-A-Lot''' is an enemy from ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are variants of [[Sir Lance-A-Lot]]s that have golden armor.

==History==
Sir Worth-A-Lots are the strongest members of [[Carpaccio]]'s group as thy can only be encountered late into [[Wario]]'s adventure as they are found in [[Carpaccio's Lab]] and [[Allergia Gardens]]. Like with Sir Lance-A-Lots, Sir Worth-A-Lots can attack by charging at Wario with their lances. Wario can defeat them with any of his attacks, but must be careful not to charge into them when their lances are pointing forward. According to Wario, Sir Lance-A-Lots are promoted into Sir Worth-A-Lots after achieving five straight years of perfect attedence.
