'''''Donkey Kong Adventure''''' is a side story from [[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]] including [[Donkey Kong (series)| Donkey Kong]] elements, such as [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] himself, plenty of DK related themes and weapons, and even references too.

==Gameplay==
Almost exactly like the base game, except for a few added techniques, like [[Grab & Launch (Donkey Kong Adventure)|Grab & Launch]], [[DK Ramps (Donkey Kong Adventure)|DK Ramps]], and [[Dandelions (Donkey Kong Adventure)|Dandelions]].
