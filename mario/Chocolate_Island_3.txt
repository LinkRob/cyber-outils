'''Chocolate Island 3''' is a [[level]] of [[Chocolate Island]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

It is a daytime sky-themed level where [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] has to use several [[Single Swing Lift]]s to get across gaps. A few types of enemies are encountered along the way, including [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Beach Koopa|Koopas without a Shell]], [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, and [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]]. A [[Cape Feather]] can help Mario or Luigi skip large portions of the level, with even [[? Block|Prize Blocks]] containing them.
