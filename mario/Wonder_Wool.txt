[[File:Green_Wonder_Wool.jpg|thumb|The Green Wonder Wool]]'''Wonder Wool''' is one of the types of collectibles found in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. There are five Wonder Wools per level and a total of 275 Wonder Wools in the entire game. If all five Wonder Wools are collected in a level, a new Yoshi design will be unlocked.
==Story==
The [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] were turned into Wonder Wool by [[Kamek]]. Kamek accidentally drops many pieces of the Wonder Wool into many of the worlds. The remaining Yoshis must get back the pieces to remake all the Yoshis.
