A '''Wheelie''' is a technique that can be done on a [[bike]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. Players flick the Wiimote upward or press up on the D-pad on a [[Nintendo GameCube Controller]] or [[Classic Controller]] for the biker to get up on one wheel, increasing their speed but decreasing their control. While doing a wheelie, players are more affected by contact with other vehicles. Braking or a down-flick of the Wiimote/pressing down on the D-pad stops the wheelie. Wheelies can only be done while boosting in ''Mario Kart 8''.
