'''Accountant Assault''' is a [[microgame]] in [[Wario]]'s [[Touch Training]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Gameplay==
Using a calculator, the player has to complete an expression shown on the blackboard on the top screen before time runs out. They can also tap and enter the answer by tapping the = button on the calculator. Accountant Assault has twice the length of an average microgame to give the player a few extra seconds to solve the expression. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame, but if the expression is not solved on time, they lose.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The expression consists of single-digit numbers.
*'''Level 2:''' The expression consists of single-digit and double-digit numbers.
*'''Level 3:''' The expression consists of double-digit and triple-digit numbers.
