'''Step It Up''' is one of six mini-game modes in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
*Be the first player to win a certain number of minigames to become the champion!
*The player can choose 3 wins, 5 wins, 7 wins with Free-for-all Minigame or 1-vs-Rivals Minigame. At the start, random player will spin the Minigame Wheel to choose a minigame. From that point goes on, the last place player will choose the minigame. If many players are tied for last place, one random player will spin the wheel.
*Whittles can also be seen in this mode in the background. Each time you win, you'll climb stairs. If you win enough times, you'll reach the top, where Whittles congratulate you and you'll see a gold or sliver statue of your character behind it depending on the wins being set to 5 or 7. When playing with 3 wins, the statue doesn't show.
*Like other minigames, complete this minigame you'll get 10 Party Points.
