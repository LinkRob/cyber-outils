'''Bumpers''' are an object which appear in a few [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] installments. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
A Bumper is an object which bounces back [[Mario]] or another character if they touch it. In their debut appearance, ''New Super Mario Bros.'', Bumpers have the appearance of semi-translucent yellow bubbles containing a plant-like design within them. Since their second appearance in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', Bumpers are donut-shaped object, although the specifics of their design varies depending on the game style.

==In ''Super Mario Maker''==
Bumpers can be placed in a level via shaking a [[Grinder (obstacle)|Grinder]]. When the player makes contact with one, they will be bounced in the direction that they hit it from. In the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' style, Bumpers appear with green and white stripes and two eyes with a similar look to [[Mr. Eraser]]. In the ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' style, they appear with a white and lime-green checker pattern with the same eyes as the previous look. In the ''[[Super Mario World]]'' style, they appear with a pink and white diamond pattern, this time with two beady-looking eyes. In the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' style, they have the same orange and yellow star pattern as [[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]]'s [[Circus Ball]]s.
