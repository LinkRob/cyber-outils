'''Whirly Fly Guys''' are a rare variant of [[Fly Guy]] featured exclusively in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', and make a reappearance in both ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

==Behavior==
Whirly Fly Guys differ from Fly Guys in that they repeatedly flash between different colors. A Whirly Fly Guy is not instantly defeated by an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] thrown by a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] or [[watermelon]] seeds spit at one, but instead this causes the Whirly Fly Guy to start dropping [[coin]]s. After enough hits, the Whirly Fly Guy is finally defeated, and the Yoshi earns one or two extra lives in the process.

In ''Yoshi Touch & Go'', Whirly Fly Guys have a different behavior of hiding in the grass at first, and when a Yoshi approaches a Whirly Fly Guy, it quickly flies off. If Yoshi manages to defeat the Whirly Fly Guy, the player earns four [[point]]s.
