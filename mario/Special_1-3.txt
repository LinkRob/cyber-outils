'''Special 1-3''' is the forty-ninth level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Layout==
Like the previous Special levels, the layout of Special 1-3 takes after [[World 2-3 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-3]], however, new platforms have appeared. The level begins with a [[Question Block]]-shaped platform, followed by a [[Goomba]]-shaped one. As the second platform suggests, Goombas appear on the platform as they pop out of the [[Baddie Box]]es nearby. The third platform is a Cloud-shaped platform where [[Mario]] can collect several [[coin]]s and a [[Super Leaf]] (if he went through the red loop). From here, Mario can use the cannon to land aboard the [[Bullet Bill]]-shaped platform. Like the Goomba Platform, several Bullet Bills appear to attack Mario. In addition, in the line of fire of the Bullet Bills, Mario can hit several floating Question Blocks, there are also some small spaces that Mario can only fit into by crouching. The [[Cheep Cheep]]-shaped platform Mario accesses next, like the previous enemy-shaped platforms, contains a number of Cheep Cheeps, though the Cheep Cheeps only jump out of the water near the Question Blocks on the platform, there are also a few P Buttons here. Using the cannon here, Mario (or [[Luigi]]) finds the final platform, a mock-up of a ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' styled [[Flagpole]] scene.

==Enemies==
Enemies that appeared in Special 1-3:
*[[Goomba]]
*[[Bullet Bill]]
*[[Cheep Cheep]]

==Star Medals==
*'''Star Medal 1''': While in the first cannon, Mario can shoot to the [[Fireball]]-shaped platform to his left to find the first [[Star Medal]].
*'''Star Medal 2''': While aboard the Cloud platform, Mario can use the cannon to shoot the next Fireball-shaped platform to his left and find the level's second Star Medal.
*'''Star Medal 3''': Like the first two coins, Mario can obtain the final Star Medal by using the cannon aboard the Cheep Cheep platform and shooting the Fireball-shaped platform to his right.
