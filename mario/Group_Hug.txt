|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''|fp/bp = 4 [[Flower Point|FP]]}}'''Group Hug''' is a special move used by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. This move requires 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Description==
When Princess Toadstool uses this move, she goes in front of the other two party members and a blue light sparkles around them.

==Usage==
With Group Hug, Toadstool is able to heal everyone on the team for moderate amount of HP and even quires status ailments.

==Trivia==
*Because of its properties, Group Hug is considered one of the most valuable moves in the game along with [[Come Back]].
