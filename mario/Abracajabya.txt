The '''Abracajabya''' is a boss from [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']], created by [[Grumpy Kong]] and used by the [[Void Company]] to poison the [[Forest Layer]], hindering [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Pauline]]'s progress toward the [[Planet Core]].

== Description ==
The Abracajabya is a gargantuan flower-like monster resembling a witch. It has green and pink petals and yellow eyes and a purple stem.

== History ==
The Abracajabya was created by [[Grumpy Kong]] while Donkey Kong and Pauline made their way through the [[Forest Layer]] on their way to the [[Planet Core]]. Eventually, it was destroyed. It has a statue that can be collected and placed in the West Ruins area north of the Jungle Ruins on [[Donkey Kong Island]] in the ''DK Island & Emerald Rush'' Downloadable Content Campaign.

== Battle ==
The Abracajabya is fought throughout the Forest Layer. It attacks by sprouting up a distance from [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and shooting large thorns at him. Donkey Kong must use these horns to make his way to its head, which houses its weak spot, a bud that must be attacked to deal damage to the boss. As he climbs the large thorns, Donkey Kong must avoid smaller thorns. Donkey Kong can also try to dodge the thorns the Abracajabya shoots out with his [[Ostrich Bananza]] transformation using the updrafts from fans scattered throughout the arena if the [[Ostrich Elder]] bestowed it to him.

At the 70% and 35% health thresholds, the Abracajabya shakes Donkey Kong off and the cycle repeats. Upon defeat, it collapses, allowing him to proceed.

== Behind the Scenes ==
The Abracajabya is based on a flower. The name is a pun on the phrase "abracadabra" and "jab you."
