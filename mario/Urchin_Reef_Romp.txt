'''Urchin Reef Romp''', also known as, '''Sparkling Waters-2''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is the second course of [[Sparkling Waters]].

==Features==
The level takes place underwater and the player must avoid the [[Urchin]]s found in this level. There are also some rapids that can pull the player to a bottomless pit.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Before entering the first pipe, pass the pipe to reveal a hidden area. Wall jump and then run left to the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Go under the eighth Mega Urchin while following the current to the Star Coin. Swim up afterwards to avoid death while also avoiding the Mega Urchin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In Fire form (unless you're Nabbit), go to the trail of Urchins right after the eighth Mega Urchin and shoot fireballs at the Urchins to get the Star Coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Near the P Switch after the third Star Coin shows a pretty big picture of standard Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' jumping.
