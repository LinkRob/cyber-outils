The '''Waluigi Court''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' for [[Nintendo 64]].

==Description==
Waluigi Court is a tennis court belonging to [[Waluigi]]. Like with [[Wario & Waluigi Court]] and [[Wario Court]], the background of the court features [[treasure chest]]s and ruins in a desert setting. The ground of the court depicts artwork of [[Waluigi]] holding his racket. This court has the strongest bounce with the fastest ball speed.

==In ''Mario Tennis'' (Nintendo 64)==
Waluigi Court is an unlockable court that the player can unlock by winning the third round of the [[Medallion Match]] minigame in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]], then transferring the data through a [[Transfer Pak]]. Because the Transfer Pak is incompatible with [[reissue]]s of ''Mario Tennis'' for Nintendo 64, Waluigi Court remains exclusive to the original version.
