'''Tracey Moore''' is a Canadian voice actress. She provided the voice for [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World (show)|Super Mario World]]'',  following after [[Jeannie Elias]], who previously voiced Princess Toadstool in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. 

Aside from voicing Princess Toadstool, Moore was also known for her role as Sailor Moon and the singing voice of Strawberry Shortcake in ''{{w|Strawberry Shortcake's Berry Bitty Adventures}}''.
