'''Winner's Wheel''' is an Item Mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the player must stop the wheel to get the item they want. However, there is no real strategy to this mini-game and must stop the wheel when they feel like it. As long the wheel doesn't stop on the Baby Bowser, the player will leave with an item.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|a}} - Stop Wheel

==Trivia==
* Winner's Wheel is a parody of the popular game show ''Wheel of Fortune''.
