A '''Glurp''' is an enemy appearing in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]]. It is a blue gelatinous slime-like monster encountered only in [[Joke's End]].

== Battle ==
One of a Glurp's attacks is charging up and burping out a toxic pink cloud. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] must dodge the cloud by [[jump]]ing over it, otherwise it may [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] them. The other attack used by a Glurp is making its way up to Mario or Luigi to try and bite them, resulting in either brother's [[Defense]] being lowered. While a Glurp attempts this, Mario or Luigi can protect themselves from damage by hitting the Glurp with their [[hammer]], resulting in it taking damage itself. While not an attack, there is a possibility of a Glurp dividing itself into two identical copies upon taking damage.

Glurps are extremely resistant to all [[Jump]], solo [[Hammer]], and regular [[Chopper Bros.]] attacks. Glurps can be defeated with other hammer attacks, although their main weaknesses are the [[Firebrand]] and [[Thunderhand]] attacks.
