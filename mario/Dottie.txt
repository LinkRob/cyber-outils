'''Dottie''' is a [[Pixl]] and one of [[Mario]]'s partners in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Dottie has the ability to shrink Mario and his partners down to an incredibly small size. She is originally found in the fifth chapter of the Land of Crag, helping a [[Gabbro|Cragnon]] hide from the [[Floro Sapien]]s. Her personality reflects the archetype of a relaxed spiritualist, or a hippie.
