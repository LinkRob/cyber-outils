'''Happy Birthday''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' as one of [[Mona]]'s microgames and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Wario Deluxe]]'s.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of a character who blows a constant stream of air, and the character has to blow out every [[candle]] before time runs out. If done successfully, an applause plays, indicating that the player has won. In ''WarioWare: Twisted!'' this is accomplished by tilting the character's head, while in ''WarioWare Gold'', it is by tilting the table.

In ''WarioWare: Twisted!'', each increased difficulty level has an older character, weakening the strength of their blows, as well as the number of candles present. Specifically, the first difficulty level features a child, who has to blow out three candles; the second features an adult who has to blow out five candles; and the third is an old person who has to blow out sixteen candles.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', the player takes control of [[Wario]], who has to blow out one, two, and three candles while rotating the table, respectively. The player can optionally blow into the system's Mic to increase the strength of the blow.
