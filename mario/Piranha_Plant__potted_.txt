A '''Piranha Plant''', sometimes distinctly named '''Potted Piranha Plant''', is an item in which a regular [[Piranha Plant]] sits within a pot. It originally appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and its sequels as [[Princess Peach]]'s special item. The Piranha Plant has also appeared in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and the [[Wii U]] version of ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'', followed by the mainline ''Mario Kart'' games with ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
In ''Mario Kart Arcade GP'', ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]'', using Piranha Plant eats the items of Princess Peach's opponents.

In ''Super Mario 3D World'', while the player's character carries a Piranha Plant, it automatically lunges itself at enemies along the way to eat them. It can also stretch itself out to nearby [[Green Star]]s, including some that would otherwise be out of reach. A Piranha Plant can be defeated like a regular enemy by jumping on it. In multiplayer mode, if a Piranha Plant lunges itself at another player's character, it merely knocks them aside instead of powering them down.

In ''Mario Kart 8'' and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', a Piranha Plant lunges itself at nearby racers, a behavior derived from its appearance in ''Super Mario 3D World'', as the user drives along. As with other items, the effect of a Piranha Plant is only temporary, so it disappears after a while.

==In the games==
In ''Super Mario 3D World'', the potted Piranha Plant first appears in [[Shadow-Play Alley]], being one of the signature features of the level. It also appears in this level in the Wii U version of ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''.
