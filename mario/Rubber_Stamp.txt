'''Rubber Stamp''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' It uses the Knight form for its gameplay, and [[Wario]] has it in his second set, used in the Lava at First Sight level which is "hosted" by [[Volcano Wario]].

== Gameplay ==
The player controls an employee who holds a stamp that leaves a mark shaped like [[Wario]]'s nose and moustache. The employee has to press his stamp on the postcards, done when the player moves the Joy-cons downward, and has to do so five times to win. Stamping the desk causes a loss.

=== Difficulty Levels ===
Harder levels will add bacon, with the easy level having only postcards, the hard level throwing in a single bacon piece, and the expert level having two pieces of bacon. Stamping the bacon will cause a loss.

== Trivia ==

* The microgame's music is identical to Memory Match's theme.
