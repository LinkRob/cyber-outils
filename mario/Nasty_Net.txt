'''Nasty Nets''' are an object exclusively appearing in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[The Ugly Mermaid]]."

==Description==
Nasty Nets are a type of net that can be launched from the [[Doom Sub]]. The main purpose of a Nasty Net is to catch and hold enemy targets. Upon hitting its target, a Nasty Net wraps itself around it entirely.

A Nasty Net is fairly durable and does not conduct electricity, revealed when [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] managed to collect a school of [[Jelectro]]s in one without getting shocked. Nasty Nets are tough, so many cannot destroy it, although a [[Chain Chomp]] can chew through a Nasty Net in one bite.
