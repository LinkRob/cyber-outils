The '''Penguin Champ''' (also known as the '''Big Penguin''') is a [[Penguin]] who appears in [[Cool, Cool Mountain]] of ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. He races with [[Mario]] on the ice slide. The Penguin Champ slides on his belly really fast, but if [[Mario]] wins against him he'll reward Mario with a [[Power Star]]. However, if Mario in any way cheats to get there much sooner, the Penguin Champ gets mad and refuses to hand Mario the Power Star.
