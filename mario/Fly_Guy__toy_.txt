'''Fly Guys''' are an enemy in ''[[Mario and Donkey Kong: Minis on the Move]]''. They are the [[mini]] counterparts of the species of the [[Fly Guy|same name]].

==In ''Mario and Donkey Kong: Minis on the Move''==
Fly Guys are enemies in the [[minigame]] [[Fly Guy Grab]], in which the [[stylus]] must be used to aim and launch the claw on the bottom screen to hit the Fly Guys on the top screen.
