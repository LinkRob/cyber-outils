'''Koopa Village''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
After getting past the [[Koopa Bros.]] disguised as [[Toad (species)|Toads]], [[Mario]] is able to walk through [[Pleasant Path]] in order to continue his journey to [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]]. However, before approaching the fortress, Mario must visit Koopa Village and help them from the turmoil that the [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] have caused.

In the eastern part of Koopa Village, Mario will find a house that is shaking. Inside, he will find a shell-less Koopa named [[Kooper]]. Kooper's shell has been taken by a [[Fuzzy]] and he desires to get it back. The Fuzzy runs out of Kooper's house and Mario must chase after it.

In order to get Kooper's shell back, Mario must partake in a guessing game with the Fuzzies. Mario must guess what tree the Fuzzy has relocated in. After guessing correctly four times, the Fuzzy declares they surrender. However, when you try to leave this location, the Fuzzies take back their surrender and challenge Mario to a fight. After beating the Fuzzies, Kooper requests to join Mario on his adventure.

Naturally, there are other residents of Koopa Village. Most notably, one of them is the famous explorer, Professor [[Kolorado]], who often is seen out of the village due to going on many adventures. He often leaves his [[Kolorado's Wife|wife]] behind much to her annoyance due to his tendency to leave without warning. Another Koopa by the name of [[Koopa Koot]] lives in the house further east where he often tries to get others to do favors for him. However, Koopa Koot himself appears not to be as popular with other characters as he is often described as being incredibly selfish. Also, once the Koopa Bros. have been defeated, the freed [[Bob-omb]]s will now live at Koopa Village.

Koopa Village is also one of many locations that [[Chuck Quizmo]] can appear at. He will always be found to the far east side of Koopa Village (right near Koopa Koot's house next to the blue Warp Pipe once it has been activated). Chuck Quizmo can also appear in the first area right beside the [[Save Block]].
