'''Mega Blooper's Bayside Bop''' is a Boss Battle minigame featured in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Gameplay==
It is different from other boss minigames in that rather than fighting [[Mega Blooper]], the characters each only have to bop a beach ball with the same rhythm demonstrated by [[Blooper]]s each round. The goal is still for the characters to get the most [[point]]s. Hitting the beach ball to a beat earns a character points: two points if timed perfectly or one point if they are slightly early or late with their timing although failing the timing results in losing a point. The characters switch positions each round.

Being a minigame that does not involve fighting Mega Blooper, reaching the halfway point in the minigame, which is by reaching the white line halfway through the top-right bar, instead speeds up the tempo rather than have Mega Blooper fight more aggressively. Furthermore, a character earns three points at the end by completely filling the bar rather than by dealing the finish attack on Mega Blooper.
