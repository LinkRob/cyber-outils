A '''Pokey Sprout''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. Here, they are commonly encountered in [[Bianco Hills]] and [[Pinna Park]]. Pokey Sprouts have an identical appearance to a [[Pokey]]'s head. They hide in the ground amongst the flowers but as soon as [[Mario]] approaches them, they reveal themselves and attack. Mario can defeat them by either stomping on them or using [[F.L.U.D.D]]'s to spray them at a wall, doing this can cause a Pokey Sprout to splat and drop three [[Coin]]s which can be very crucial for obtaining those 100-Coin [[Shine Sprite]]s.

==Trivia==
*The overworld sprite of Pokeys in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' appears identical to a Pokey Sprout.
*''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' includes a similar variety to the Pokey Sprout called the [[Pokey Head]]. These Pokeys behave similarly but they're green and lack any spikes.
