'''Harisu''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Harisu is a hedgehog with retractable spikes.

===Behavior===
If [[Wario]] attempts to [[jump]] onto a Harisu, it extends its quills and starts to run around quickly.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
Harisu can be found in levels of [[Sherbet Land (world)|Sherbet Land]]. They are first encountered in [[Course No.15]]. Wario can defeat a Harisu through most means, such as from using a [[Dash Attack]].
