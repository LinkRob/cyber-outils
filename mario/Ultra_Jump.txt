|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Ultra Jump''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is used by [[Mario]] once he reaches level 14 and requires 11 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Effects==
When Ultra Jump is used, Mario will proceed to jump on every enemy on the screen for high damage for as long the player keeps the action command going. Ultra Jump is much stronger than [[Super Jump]] and can be very useful against strong enemies or bosses that are weak to jump attacks.
