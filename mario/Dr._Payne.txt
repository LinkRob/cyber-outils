'''Dr. Payne''' is a dentist who runs his own dental clinic, aptly named [[Dr. Payne's Dental Clinic]], in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Dr. Payne is a bald man with a white beard. He resembles the [[Sewer Guru]], though no connection between the two has even been established.

===Etymology===
Dr. Payne's name is a pun on the word "pain." It can be inferred that the "pain" refers to toothaches given Dr. Payne's job as a dentist.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
Dr. Payne's only appears during cutscenes of [[Wario]]'s story. During the intro, Dr. Payne had fixed Wario's teeth, which had become rotten and painful from eating too much chocolate, and he instructed Wario to stop eating any more sugary foods. In the ending, Wario buys ten pies from [[the Sweet Spot]] and eats them, which immediately causes his cavities to return. The pain causes Wario to launch all the way back into Dr. Payne's Dental Clinic, where Dr. Payne had been expecting Wario to return because of Wario's irresponsible nature.
