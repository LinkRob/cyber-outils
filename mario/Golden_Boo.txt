==Appearance==

The Golden Boo looks exactly like a regular [[Boo]] except it's golden in color. The golden boo drops [[coin]]s once it is defeated.

==In battle==

Although it's different in color, the Golden Boo's attacks are exactly the same as a regular Boo both in style and power. They attack by slapping Mario or scaring him.
