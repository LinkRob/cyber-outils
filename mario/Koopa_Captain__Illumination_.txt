The '''Koopa Captain''' is a minor antagonist in Illumination's 13th feature film ''{{w|The Super Mario Bros. Movie}}'', an adaptation of Nintendo's ''{{w|Mario (franchise)|Mario}}'' video game franchise. He is the low-ranking commander of the [[Koopa]] Army who attempts to help [[Bowser (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Bowser]] conquer the Mushroom Kingdom and the world.

==Appearance==
The Koopa Captain is a Koopa Troopa with yellow scales and brown eyes. He wears an eye patch on his right eye (though it's completely fine), complete with a red shell, red spiky shoulder guards, and red boots.

==Biography==
===Past===
Not much is known about his past, but considering his low status, he most likely went through rigorous training and expertise. he probably volunteered to be in the army and did whatever he could to obtain his rank as captain.

===''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''===
He is first seen invading the Penguin Kingdom along with his army, the [[Koopa General]], [[Kamek (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Kamek]], and Bowser, in a bid to steal the Super Star stowed away in the Penguin Kingdom's castle, leading the other Koopa soldiers into their offense positions. When Bowser burns down the castle and finds the superstar, the captain and the other [[Koopa Troopa]] soldiers capture the penguins while the general restrains the Penguin King, who unsuccessfully escapes the Koopa's grasp. 

After the invasion, he is seen at a rock party (celebrating said invasion) with the general where Bowser makes a speech to the army about his plan to marry [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]].

Later, the captain is seen again riding on his kart to ambush Mario and the Kong Army on Rainbow Road. He fires a green shell to knock a few Kongs off the [[Rainbow Road]].

The captain is last seen again leading the group of Koopa soldiers to surround Princess Peach and Toad upon their arrival in the Mushroom Kingdom, ready to assist Bowser for his forced proposal to Peach into a royal wedding. When Peach surrenders and turns herself in, the captain and his comrades give evil smirks on their faces after forcing Peach to marry Bowser.
