'''Under the Angry Sun''' is the fourth level of the Story Mode of ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. It is a ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''-style level set within a desert.

==Features==
The level involves [[Mario]] riding a track platform to the [[Goal Pole]] while avoiding the [[Angry Sun]]. Winged [[Propeller Mushroom]]s make an appearance, and these enable Mario to reach [[coin]]s that are otherwise unreachable as well as [[1-Up Mushroom]]s within [[Question Block|? Blocks]].
