'''Rupees''' are a recurring item in the [[The Legend of Zelda|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]. They have made a few appearances in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media, such as being the replacement for [[coin]]s in [[Hyrule Circuit]] in both ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

Rupees are green and resemble a sexagonal gemstone. Originally, Rupees varied in their value, which can be told by their colour, but the ''Mario Kart 8'' version is always green and is worth a single coin.

The name Rupee comes from "ruby."
