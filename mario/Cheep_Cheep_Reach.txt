'''Cheep Cheep Reach''' is a [[Coin Chaos]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each character is equipped with a fishing line with a white glove at the end, very similar to the one used in the ''[[Mario Party]]'' minigame [[Cast Aways]] as well as the ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' minigame [[Hand, Line and Sinker]]. With it, the characters need to catch [[Cheep Cheep]]s to earn [[coin]]s, but must avoid grabbing the [[Urchin]]s, which instead stun them temporarily. Cheep Cheeps are worth one coin each while [[Gold Cheep Cheep]]s are worth ten each. Gold Cheep Cheeps are larger and faster than regular Cheep Cheeps. At times, a [[Red Coin]] in a [[bubble]] can be caught, and it is worth three coins.

The player can hold down {{button|3ds|A}} longer to grow the character's fishing rod before it is thrown, increasing how many Cheep Cheeps can be caught in the glove when it is thrown. However, the glove can only grow up to a certain size, and if the player holds the button for too long, the glove shrinks to its smallest size before continuing to grow again.

==Levels==
*Level 1: There are only Cheep Cheeps, Gold Cheep Cheeps, and Urchins.
*Level 2: Red Coins in bubbles sometimes appear.
*Level 3: Many Red Coins appear alongside an increased number of Cheep Cheeps and Urchins.

==Controls==
*Press and hold {{button|3ds|A}}: Grow glove
*Release {{button|3ds|A}}: Throw glove
*{{button|3ds|Stick}}: Aim where the glove lands (a control not mentioned by the game)

==Flavor text==
*Instructions: ''Grab the Cheep Cheeps!''
