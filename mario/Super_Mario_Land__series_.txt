The '''''Super Mario Land'' series''' is a series of three 2D Mario platformers released for the [[Game Boy]].

==Games==
===''Super Mario Land''===
{{main|Super Mario Land}}
[[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] has been kidnapped by [[Tatanga]] of [[Sarasaland]]; it is up to [[Mario]] to rescue her. This first game is one of the best-selling games for the [[Game Boy]], having exceeded 18 million copies. ''Super Mario Land'' spans four [[world]]s, each having three [[level]]s. It features different enemies, bosses, and locations than those in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. Power-up items are limited to the [[Super Mushroom]] and [[Superball Flower]], and the only collectible items are [[coin]]s and [[1-Up Heart]]s.

===''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''===
{{main|Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins}}
[[Wario]] makes his first-ever appearance as the main enemy here, and has taken over Mario's castle. This is the only ''Mario'' game to feature zones that you can play in any order (excluding the final zone) instead of worlds. The game features a unique power-up item named the [[Carrot]], which turns Mario into [[Bunny Mario]].

===''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''===
{{main|Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3}}
Wario is the now the main protagonist in this game. It marked the start of the {{series|Wario Land}} and the end of the ''Super Mario Land'' sub-series.
