'''Return Postage''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It can be obtained by defeating [[Bonetail]], the boss of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear the Return Postage by using seven [[Badge Point|BP]]. While Mario is wearing a Return Postage, all direct attackers to Mario receive damage equal to half of the damage they dealt. This attack does not inflict damage to enemies using projectile attacks.
