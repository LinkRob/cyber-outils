'''Graffiti Swing''' is [[Bowser Jr.]]'s [[Star Swing]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces his [[Killer Jr. Ball]] move from ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', which itself is also replaced by the [[Graffiti Ball]] for when Bowser Jr. is pitching.

==Description==
When Bowser Jr. initiates Graffiti Swing, he twirls his [[Magic Paintbrush]] in his fingers and streaks paint all around him to create the effect. A fake ball lands first, splattering the landing spot with paint and temporarily stunning any fielders who try to catch it. A few seconds later, the actual ball then lands. Characters who can move around in midair, such as [[Paragoomba]], are unaffected by Graffiti Swing.
