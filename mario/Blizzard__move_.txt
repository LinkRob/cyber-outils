|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Blizzard''' is a special move use by certain enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
With Blizzard, the enemy can strike all characters on screen dealing high damage. The Blizzard itself shows a screen of snow blowing hard on the screen. A similar move called [[Snowy]] can be used by [[Mallow]], though the description is different.
