The '''Anytime Egg''' is a [[Special Item]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', and it can be acquired from various [[Bonus Challenge]]s or by winning a [[Mini Battle Game]].

The Anytime Egg can be used via the pause screen. In doing so, the current [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s supply of [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] is refilled to six. Using the item is redundant if the Yoshi already has six Yoshi Eggs.
