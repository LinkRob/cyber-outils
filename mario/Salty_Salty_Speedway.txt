'''Salty Salty Speedway''' is the second course of the Banana Cup in ''[[Mario Kart World]]'', and is the third checkpoint of the [[Spiny Rally]]. 

==Description==
Salty Salty Speedway is set in a city named Lagoon City which located just east of the large bay in the south of the game's map. It is full of canals for much of its area. Lagoon City and the course features a statue of [[Birdo]] as a mermaid.

== Layout ==
The course has racers drive on the canals for much of its area, and is the only course in the game where players start the race with their karts in their boat mode. This course also appears as a stage in Battle mode. An arrangement of the theme for Delfino Pier from ''Mario Kart Wii'' plays on this version of the course.

Outside of ''Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit'', this is the first new course in the series' history to be in the Banana Cup.
