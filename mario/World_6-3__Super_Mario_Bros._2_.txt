'''World 6-3''' is the third [[level]] of [[World 6 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]] in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its Western conversion ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' as well as the eighteenth level overall. A second boss fight against [[Tryclyde]] takes place at the end, although in ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'', the second battle against [[Mouser]] takes place here as the original location where he was fought, [[World 3-3 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3-3]], instead features a battle against [[Robirdo]].
