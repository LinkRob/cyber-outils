'''Thwomp Thump''' is a [[Captain Command]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==

If a player uses Thwomp Thump, then they will call a [[Thwomp]] to fall down on all ground units. They can drop the Thwomp at any time, and there is a 3 second countdown after the player starts the drop. If an enemy captain uses Thwomp Thump, then the player will not see a countdown, or know when they drop it. Each squad can jump to avoid the Thwomp. 

==Flavor text==

*'''In-game description:''' "Drops a mean Thwomp that deals heavy damage to everyone on the ground! Can be avoided by jumping."
*'''Guide advice:''' "The impact of a Thwomp thumping deals damage to all troopers on the ground. Press the A Button right as the Thwomp falls to move your allies out of the way and avoid receiving damage."
