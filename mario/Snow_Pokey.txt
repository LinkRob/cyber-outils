A '''Snow Pokey''' is a variant of [[Pokey]] which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Profile==
A Snow Pokey has the characteristics of a Pokey and a [[Mr. Blizzard]]. It is found in snowy environments, a contrast to how Pokeys are generally encountered in hot desert areas. A Snow Pokey has a snowy, white body, black eyes with white pupils, a carrot nose, icy spikes, and wears a red bucket with a yellow handle as a hat.

== In ''Super Mario 3D World'' ==
Snow Pokeys reside in the snowy levels of [[World 3 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 3]] as well as [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]], [[World Mushroom (Super Mario 3D World)|World Mushroom]], and [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]]. Whenever a Snow Pokey takes notice of the player's character, they begin moving after them. A Snow Pokey can be defeated through the use of several power-ups, including [[Fire Flower]]s, [[Starman|Super Star]]s, [[Super Leaf|Super Leaves]], [[Invincibility Leaf|Invincibility Leaves]], [[Boomerang Flower]]s, [[Super Bell]]s, and [[Lucky Bell]]s.
