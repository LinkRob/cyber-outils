The '''Coin Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be bought at [[Peach's Castle]] for 300 [[coin]]s. Equipping it doubles a character's number of [[coin]]s at the end of a battle if they defeated an enemy using a [[Bros. Item]].
