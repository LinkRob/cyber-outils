'''Cooligan Shrooms''' is a level of the [[Frosted Glacier]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Tilt the second or third platform to make one of them lean to the Star Coin. You can even jump on a Cooligan or from the second "?" block to get the coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Jump on the platform on the line of Cooligans in between the first and second red pipes and jump on one of them. It could be in easier in Ice or Penguin form which can freeze a Cooligan to destroy the others including itself.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In Ice or Penguin form, freeze a Cooligan sliding on the Munchers and ride it to the Star Coin to jump off right away.

==Hidden Luigi image==
On the mushroom like platform to the left of the final Star Coin shows a picture of standard Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' sitting on a pole.
