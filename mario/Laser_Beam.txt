'''Laser Beam''' is a special ability in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and reappears in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Whenever a character with the Laser Beam ability throws the ball to home base, the ball travels a lot faster than normal while sailing at a much lower arc.

==In {{series|Mario Baseball}}==
===''Mario Superstar Baseball''===
The Laser Beam can only be used by [[Waluigi]], [[Bowser]], [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], and the [[Pianta]]s. Laser Beam's speed differs depending on who uses it, with [[Waluigi]]'s Laser Beam being one of the faster ones.

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
Laser Beam is instead used by [[Wario]], [[King K. Rool]], and the Piantas, who are the only characters to retain the ability from ''Mario Superstar Baseball''. Each character now throws their Laser Beam at the same speed.

In Challenge mode, there is a scout mission in [[Wario City]] in which the object is to get the runner out using a Laser Beam throw three times. In doing so, [[Boo]] joins [[Mario]]'s party.
