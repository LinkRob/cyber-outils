'''Helen''' is a female non-playable golfer who appears in ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''. She is one of [[Kid]]'s students, along with [[Neil]], [[Ella]], and [[Buzz (character)|Buzz]].

==Physical description==
Helen has shoulder-length light green hair somewhat wavy. Helen wears a dark red shirt matching her eye color, although the sleeves of her shirt are black with two dark red stripes on them. Her shorts are purple, and her shoes are brown.
