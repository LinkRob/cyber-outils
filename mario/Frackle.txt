'''Frackle''' is a little "organism" resting on [[Fracktail]]'s back in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
In the second boss battle in the game, Mario had to use the [[pixl]] [[Thoreau]]'s power to grab the Frackle and smush the antenna 3 times. Later in the game, you must take on [[Wracktail]] in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] which involves the twin brother [[Wrackle]].
