'''Combo Attack''' is a bonus challenge in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. The player must pass through rings to gain points.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
'''Combo Attack''' appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' as a bonus challenge. The player's objective is to increase the  combo counter to reach a higher rank. The stars ranked depends on the highest combo the player made. The player gains points through the following:
* [[Rocket Start]]
* [[Ramp]]
* [[Coin]]
* [[Boost Pad]]
* [[Mini-Turbo]]
* [[Mushroom]]
Depending on the cup the player chose, the must complete three courses to do a bonus challenge. If Combo Attack is one of the bonus challenges, the game will choose the course, driver, kart and glider.
