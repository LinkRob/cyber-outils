The '''Slow 'Shroom Orb''' ('''Sluggish 'Shroom Orb''' in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'') is an orb in both ''Mario Party 6'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. It is light blue in color. When used, it slows down the speed of the [[Dice Block]] tremendously and rolls the number in order, allowing players to choose any number from 1 through 10 to roll. Players do this by hitting {{button|gcn|A}} when the number below the desired outcome is shown on the Dice Block; if a player wants to get a three, then the player is to hit {{button|gcn|A}} when the Dice Block shows a two for example. They are one of the few [[Orb]]s used in the [[Mario Party 6#Solo Mode|Solo Mode]] in ''Mario Party 6''. In ''Mario Party 7'', they do not appear in the boards [[Pagoda Peak]] and [[Pyramid Park]].

This item has a similar effect to the [[Slowgo Candy]] from ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' (though the Slowgo Candy reduces the maximum roll, where only numbers 1 though 5 appear) and the [[Slow Dice Block]] from ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. Since it essentially allows the player to choose the number that they would like to roll, it is also functionally similar to the [[Custom Dice Block]] introduced in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'', though this item does not require the player to time hitting the Dice Block.
