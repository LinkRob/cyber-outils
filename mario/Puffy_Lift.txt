'''Puffy Lifts''' are pink/yellow cloud platform-like creatures made of clouds and cakes with eyes in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They appear in World 2 [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in level named [[Up 'n' Down with Puffy Lifts]]. When the player's character or enemies step on them they will get squished, they get squished even more when players [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] them, and when the stuff is removed from the lift, it rises again. One of the [[Wonder Effect]]s involves transforming into a Puffy Lift.

When player's turn into a Puffy Lift unlike the Puffy Lift Platform, player's are almost invincible, can move & guide spike ball, player's also able to destroy the spike balls if player's jump on them or crush them in the wall. However, like the Puffy Lift Platform, players can also get squished by spike balls step on them, and when the spike balls is removed from player's, player's rises again. If players get fully squished, they lose a life.
