'''Ohlaka Island''' is a minor location in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Description==
It is an island lined with {{w|moai}} that have different types of head accessories.

==In ''WarioWare: Get It Together!''==
In the intro to [[Orbulon]]'s stage, [[Culture]] is the last of three locations where Orbulon steals artifacts from. After stealing from [[Takechiko City]], Orbulon warps to Ohlaka Island in his [[Oinker]]. He hovers over the golden moai and uses the Oinker's tractor beam to steal it. Orbulon boastfully says that he is absorbing culture. While the golden moai is being drawn into the Oinker, a fossil, a palm tree, and a stone arch also go up into it. After stealing the golden moai and the other items drawn into the Oinker's tractor beam, Orbulon warps away.
