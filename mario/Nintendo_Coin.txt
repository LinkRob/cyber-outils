The '''Nintendo Coin''' is a special item that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
The Nintendo Coin, along with the [[Rareware Coin]], are needed to get the final [[Boss Key]] to free [[K. Lumsy]] from his prison. The Nintendo Coin can be won by [[Donkey Kong]] by playing the [[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong arcade machine]] located in [[Frantic Factory]] a second time (as the first time results in a [[Golden Banana]]). The Nintendo Coin replaces [[Pauline]] during the second major play of ''Donkey Kong''.
