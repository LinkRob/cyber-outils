The '''1-10 Dice Block''' is a special item that only appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

== ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' ==
In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'',  the 1-10 Dice Block has a chance of rolling from 1 to 10. The player can obtain the 1-10 Dice Block by landing [[Dice Block Space]], landing on a [[Lucky Space]] to [[Toad House]] or landing on [[Spin Space]] whether it is "Dice Block Shuffle", stealing Dice Block or receiving one. This [[Dice Block]] was used in [[Toad Road]] by the captain to cross the bridge. It was also in [[Bowser Station]] for the Bingo game.
