'''Imperial Poobah''' is a character in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Plumbers of the Year]]." He is a plumber who works for the [[Grand Order of Plumbers]].

==Episode appearances==
In "Plumbers of the Year," Imperial Poobah showed up at [[Mario Bros. Plumbing]] to bestow the title of "Plumbers of the Year" to [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]]
