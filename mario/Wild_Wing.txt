The '''Wild Wing''' is a kart in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'']] series first appearing in [[Mario Kart Wii]].

== Description ==
The Wild Wing resembles a professional tuner racecar, with an aerodynamic front. There are feathery wings on the rear sides of the kart, next to the rear fender, and below it are two dual exhaust pipes at the back. The design of the Wild Wing features a striped design.

=== Color schemes ===
In ''Mario Kart Wii'', there are several color schemes of the Wild Wing for each character.
* [[Mario]]'s Wild Wing has a red body, with black/white stripes and fenders, gold exhaust system, orange headlamps, and blue seat accents.
* [[Luigi]]'s Wild Wing has a dark green body, with dark blue stripes and fenders, teal-blue stripe through the middle, silver exhaust system, azure headlamps, and yellow seat accents.
* [[Princess Peach]]'s Wild Wing has a pink body, with white stripes and fenders, gold exhaust system, magenta headlamps, and dark pink seat accents.
* [[Princess Daisy]]'s Wild Wing has an orange body, with amber/white stripes, black fenders, copper exhaust system, yellow headlamps, and greenish-teal seat accents.
* [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s Wild Wing has a white body, with green/white stripes and fenders, red stripe through the middle, gold exhaust system, light orange headlamps, and dark yellow seat accents.
* [[Birdo]]'s Wild Wing has a purple body, white black/white stripes and fenders, silver exhaust system, violet headlamps, and turquoise seat accents.
* [[Diddy Kong]]'s Wild Wing has a black body, with dark brown/red stripes and fenders, copper exhaust system, light orange headlamps, and light brown seat accents.
* [[Bowser Jr.]]'s Wild Wing has a yellow body, with green/dark red stripes, black fenders, bronze exhaust system, orange headlamps, and red seat accents.
* Male [[Mii]]'s Wild Wing has an azure body, with white stripes and fenders, gold exhaust system, yellow headlamps, and turquoise seat accents.
* Female Mii's Wild Wing has a white body, white pink stripes and fenders, light beige stripe through the middle, silver exhaust system, light orange headlamps, and white seat accents.

== ''Mario Kart'' series ==
=== In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' ===
[[File:MKW Mario Holding Bob-omb on Wild Wing Artwork.png|thumb|150px|right|Mario in his Wild Wing, about to throw a [[Bob-omb]].]]
The Wild Wing makes its debut in ''Mario Kart Wii''. This kart is available from the start. This kart offers good [[speed]], [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]], [[drift]] and [[mini-turbo]] attributes, whilst hindering [[acceleration]], [[handling]] and [[off-road]] attributes.

It is the third fastest medium vehicle in game, the sixth fastest vehicle overall, and the heaviest medium vehicle. Its weight is equivalent to the [[Wario Bike]]. This is unique as the Wild Wing is almost impossible to be knocked about by most other vehicles within the heavyweight class, like the [[Jetsetter]].

However, the Wild Wing provides the worst acceleration of all vehicles in the medium class, and provides the third lowest acceleration overall.

If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] use the Wild Wing, the kart's weight is exceed the [[Offroader]] by one point. Additionally, if [[Princess Daisy]] were to use the Wild Wing, the kart's top speed would exceed the [[Blue Falcon]] and [[Flame Runner]]'s default top speed by one point.

In [[Time Trial]]s, the Wild Wing was used by Nin☆MUGI☆ as Daisy in [[Coconut Mall]] and by Nin☆Dai8 as [[Mario]] in [[Mario Circuit|<small>GCN</small> Mario Circuit]].

=== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
After much absence,  The Wild Wing returns in Mario Kart Tour, where it is classified as a High-End kart this time around. It uses the Standard tires from [[Mario Kart 8]] and its [[Nintendo Switch]] port [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]. However, unlike its Mario Kart Wii appearance, it only has Mario's color scheme no matter which driver is using it, and its wings no longer move. The Wild Wing increases the duration of speed boosts resulted from Dash Panels and the points earned for using Dash Panels.
