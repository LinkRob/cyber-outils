The '''Hidden Room''' is an optional room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Hidden Room is a small room with seven red [[treasure chest]]s, three of which contain [[treasure]]. The room's floor and walls are golden, and the entire room sparkles with gold dust. The Hidden Room also two vases and a [[Mirror (object)|mirror]] on the south wall only visible when using the [[Game Boy Horror]].

The Hidden Room is similar to the [[Sealed Room]], which also has a sparkling, golden wall, and both rooms are optional and hidden.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Hidden Room first is first accessible during [[Area Two]]. It can be accessed by inspecting the mouse hole in the [[Butler's Room]] with the [[Game Boy Horror]], activating a wind-like vacuum that directly sucks [[Luigi]] into the Hidden Room.

Four of the treasure chests each contain a [[Flash]] (each replaced with a [[Ghost Guy]] in the PAL releases' and Nintendo 3DS version's [[Hidden Mansion]]). After Luigi captures every Flash, the room's lights turn on, and a green treasure chest appears; this is the only treasure chest that contains a [[Blue Stone]], a [[Green Stone]], and a [[Red Stone]].

During the [[blackout]], two [[Blue Twirler]]s, one [[Grabbing Ghost]], and several [[Bat]]s appear here, but unlike before, none of them are revealed from a treasure chest. However, one of the treasure chests has a [[Speedy Spirit]], exclusively appearing here during the blackout.
