The '''Rocket Blast''' is the second [[Bros. Attack]] that is obtained in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', replacing the [[Bye-Bye Cannon]]. Hence its name, it uses a [[Rocket]]. The move costs 5 [[Special Point]]s (BP) to use, and it is found in [[Doop Doop Dunes]].

==Gameplay==
[[Mario]] starts this attack by summoning the move's titular [[Rocket]]. He and [[Luigi]] then board the rocket and blast into the sky.

As they come crashing down, the Bros. have to stomp on the target enemy. The order will either be Luigi and then Mario, or Mario then Luigi. If the two successfully stomp, the second Bro. takes off on the rocket and have to steer it into the enemies, blowing up the rocket and dealing fire damage to anything caught in the blast.

The initial two stomps target a single foe, while the fiery explosion hits multiple foes.

==Ratings==

* '''Okay:''' One of the Bros. fail to successfully stomp on the enemy.
* '''Good:''' Both of the Bros. successfully stomp on the enemy, but completely miss every enemy with the rocket's explosion.
* '''Great!:''' Both of the Bros. successfully stomp on the enemy, and hits at least one enemy with the rocket's explosion.
* '''Excellent!:''' Both of the Bros. successfully stomp on the enemy, and hits every enemy with the rocket's explosion.
