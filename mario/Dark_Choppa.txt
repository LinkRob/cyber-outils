}}A '''Dark Choppa''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Choppas are darker versions of [[Choppa]]s that can only be found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Like with the regular Choppas, Dark Choppas attack by flying toward one of the heroes. They can be easily taken out with [[Fleep]].
