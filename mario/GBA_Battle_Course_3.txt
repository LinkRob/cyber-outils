'''Battle Course 3''' is one of the three battle courses appearing in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. The stage is similar to [[Peach Circuit]] in terms of appearance.

[[File:BattleCourse3GBASC.png|thumb|200px|left|Gameplay in ''Mario Kart: Super Circuit'']]
The stage itself appears to take place in a sunset-area plains like how it appears in the Donut Plains-related levels. In the center of the course is a circle-shaped area of sand with four grass areas around it. There are also multicolored walls.

The stage reappeared in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. The stage also appeared in a tournament on [[Mario Kart Channel]]. Players had to drive through ten gates which were placed all throughout the stage. The [[Wii Wheel]] was the only acceptable controller in this competition. This appeared as the second competition of May 2008, and it later appeared as the second competition of May 2010. It appeared a third time as the second competition of November 2011.
