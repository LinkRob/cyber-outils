'''Fury Volcano''' is the fourth world of ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' and it is located on [[Vibe Island]]. It is based on a lava-theme, with many of the enemies being fiery and stronger. The world itself contains heaps of [[lava]], Tiki Statues, rooms with smoke, and more. The most common enemies of this world is [[Podoboo]]s and [[Rex]]es. The boss of this world is [[Wiggler]].
