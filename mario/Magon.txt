'''Magons''' are an enemy encountered throughout ''[[Wario World]]''. They attack [[Wario]] by swiping their claws at him. Magons are basic enemies, and Wario can quickly defeat one from a single hit. Magons are first encountered in [[Greenhorn Forest]].
