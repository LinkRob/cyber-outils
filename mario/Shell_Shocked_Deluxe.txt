'''Shell Shocked Deluxe''' is a Toad's Rec Room [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. Hence the name, it’s gameplay is similar to [[Shell Shocked]].
__NOTOC__
== Opening ==
The player chooses a map. If four players are involved, the players can choose to arrange the maps with the [[Nintendo Switch]] screens. The players confirm when they are ready.

The camera zooms to the player who positions their tank the camera pans to the other players and zooms out before the minigame starts.

== Gameplay ==
The player's objective is to destroy their opponents' tanks to gain the most points. If the player is hit, they will be launched and return to their pad. The minigame ends after the time runs out.

== In-game description ==
''"Whoever destroys the most rival tanks wins!"''
