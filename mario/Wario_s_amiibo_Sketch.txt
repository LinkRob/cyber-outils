'''Wario's amiibo Sketch''' is a [[souvenir]] in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. Here, the player can scan an [[amiibo]] for [[Wario]] to do a crude drawing of. A notable exception is the Wario amiibo, as Wario does professional drawings of himself. The player can only scan up to three amiibo a day.
