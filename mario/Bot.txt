A '''Bot''' is gelatinous enemy from {{series|The Legend of Zelda}}, in which they first appear in ''[[w:c:zelda:Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]]''. It made its first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in ''[[Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up''==
[[File:SMBFWIGU Medical Doctor Nurse.png|thumb|left|200px|A Bot being X-ray scanned by Dr. Mario and Nurse Toadstool]]
A Bot appears in two of the pages, "Medical Doctor / Nurse" and "Construction Worker / Police." In the former, a Bot can be seen being scanned by an X-Ray machine used by [[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]] and [[Nurse Toadstool]]. In the latter, a Bot wearing a hard hat can be seen to the left using a pneumatic drill. The description even states that Bot's jelly-like body makes it ideal from him to use.
{{br|left}}
==In {{series|NES Remix}}==
Bots appear in some of the ''Zelda II: The Adventure of Link''-based challenges in ''[[NES Remix 2]]'', with these challenges also carrying over to ''[[NES Remix Pack]]'' and some to ''[[Ultimate NES Remix]]''.
