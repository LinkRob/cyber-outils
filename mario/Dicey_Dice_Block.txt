The '''Dicey Dice Block''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
The Dicey Dice Block is a small [[Dice Block]] only used by a [[Bob-omb]] while following one of the characters. Three of a Dicey Dice Block's sides depict a 0, while the other three depict a -1, with the latter number only subtracting one [[space]] from the player character's total roll for that turn. A Dicey Dice Block is dark gray, and each side's number is purple with a thick, black outline.

==In ''Super Mario Party''==
Bob-ombs, and therefore the Dicey Dice Block, only appears in [[King Bob-omb's Powderkeg Mine]] for Mario Party mode and [[Gold Rush Mine]] for Partner Party mode.
