'''Cat Pom Pom''' is the cat transformation of [[Pom Pom]] who first appears in ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

== In ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury'' ==
Like [[Cat Boom Boom]], Cat Pom Pom is fought as a boss in [[Clawswipe Colosseum]].
