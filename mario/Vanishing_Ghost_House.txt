'''Vanishing Ghost House''', or '''{{world|Clouds|ghost}}''', is the [[Ghost House]] of [[Meringue Clouds]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by completing  [[Stonecrush Tower]] and its own competion unlocks [[Above the Bouncy Clouds]], [[Flame Chomp Ferris Wheel]] and [[Three-Headed Snake Block]].

==Layout==
The player comes in the Ghost House, with the platforms disappearing behind them. The [[? Block]]s and an [[Ghost Block]] are found, followed by a moving platform. More ? Blocks and Ghost are encountered as the platform moves to the right. Another disappearing platform is found, and is followed by another moving platform, which moves quickly downward. Another section of disappearing platforms and Ghost Blocks follows. A fake [[Warp Door]] appears at the end of the area, with a real Warp Door found in a fake wall. The door leads to the area with the [[Goal Pole]], where another Ghost Block and ? Blocks are found.

==Star Coins==
#The first [[Star Coin]] is located near the beginning of the level, above the rotating platform. The player must jump on top of a ? Block in order to collect the Star Coin.
#The second Star Coin is found prior to the door out of the ghost house, and the player can either glide from the visible door or hop on a ? Block to collect it.
#Under the door out of the Ghost House is a hidden area with the third Star Coin behind stairs.

==Secret Exit==
After the second rotating block drops down, the player should jump to the left to find a secret door. This leads to the secret platform, which accesses a shortcut to [[Ludwig's Block-Press Castle]].

==[[List of Luigi sightings in New Super Luigi U|Luigi Sighting]]==
*If the player takes the secret exit route, they will see a picture of [[King Boo]] and Luigi on a sign, which references their artwork from ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.
