'''Locomotionless''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Locomotionless begins at the [[Train Station]], after the player's character inquires about [[Shy Guy (Mario Party Advance)|Shy Guy]]'s favor. Shy Guy explains that the train station has run out of [[coal]] for its train. Because of this, Shy Guy wants the player's character to retrieve some coal for him, vaguely specifying that it is located in the [[Town Area]] but not in the item shop, [[Junk]]. The scene then returns to the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] map. The coal is located in the [[Vending Machines]], where they can be picked up for free, but if the player's character returns without first having obtained some coal, Shy Guy tells them the exact location. When the player's character returns with coal, Shy Guy happily rewards them with the [[Cake Maker]] [[Gaddget]], ending the quest.
