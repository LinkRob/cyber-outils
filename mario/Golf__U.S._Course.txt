'''''Golf: U.S. Course''''' is a golf game starring [[Mario]]. It was released for the [[Family Computer Disk System]] in Japan on June 14, 1987, as a follow-up to ''[[Golf: Japan Course]]'' and as an installment of the {{series|Golf}}. In 1991, ''Golf: U.S. Course'' received its own indirect follow-up titled ''[[NES Open Tournament Golf]]''.

''Golf: U.S. Course'' is the first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game to have support for more than two players, although only in the form of alternating turns.
