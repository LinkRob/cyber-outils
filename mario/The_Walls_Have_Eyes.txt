==Features==
{{Empty section|date=May 2023}}

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In the third moving on the lower row reveals a Star Coin in it. Ground pound that statue and get the coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the group of moving statues, ground-pound the statue that doesn't have a tree on it and jump to reveal a vine that reveals a hidden area to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': After escaping the area where the second Star Coin is, go in between the two statues on the right and wall jump on those statues to avoid death.

==Secret Exit==
Ground pound the hidden brick blocks used as a big platform near the mini pipe to reveal a hidden area with a pipe in it. Go into the pipe which is a cannon revealing to a secret area with a hidden flagpole. The flagpole leads to the level [[Slippery Rope Ladders|Layer-Cake Desert-Secret: Slippery Rope Ladders]].

==Hidden Luigi image==
In Mini form, go in the mini pipe. In the area, run up the statue and ground pound it to reveal an image of Super Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' crouching.
