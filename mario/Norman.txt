'''Norman''' is a one-time character featured in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Mush-Rumors]]." He is the father of [[Bud]] and [[Missy]], who are his son and daughter respectively, and the husband of their [[Bud and Missy's mother|mother]]. All of them are from the in-universe real-world location of Wichita, [[Kansas]], but during a road trip to [[Wild Waldo's One-of-a-Kind Amusement Park]], Norman accidentally drives through a [[Warp Zone]], taking them to the [[Mushroom World]].
