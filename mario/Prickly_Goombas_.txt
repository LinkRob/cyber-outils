}}'''Prickly Goombas!''', or '''{{world|Frost|3}}''', is the third level of [[Frosted Glacier]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Freezing Rain Tower]], and its own completion opens the path to [[Scaling the Mountainside]] and [[Icicle Caverns]], both of which are sometimes blocked by a [[Cooligan]] enemy course. This level is where Nabbit appears again. 

==Layout==
The player begins the level on a ground entirely out of ice, nearby two [[Venus Fire Trap]]s in [[Warp Pipe]]s. A [[? Block]] is found above a regular [[Piranha Plant]], containing a [[Super Mushroom]] or a [[Fire Flower]], depending on the player's form. Another Piranha Plant is found, along with a [[Prickly Goomba]] found next to a Venus Fire Trap. Some rolling hills, Prickly Goombas, and Venus Fire Traps are found. A Venus Fire Trap is found in a Warp Pipe, followed by a ledge with a Prickly Goomba and three regular [[Goomba]]s on it. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is found, along with another ? Block. A rolling hill with a Goomba on is found, and a ? Block containing a [[Yoshi Egg]] is hidden behind a wall at the end of the hill. A [[Fire Bro]] is found, followed by a rolling hill with Prickly Goombas on it. Mario then arrives at a ? Block and a Venus Fire Trap. A group of Prickly Goombas is found below a platform, with a Fire Bro burning their shells open. Another Prickly Goomba is found with a Fire Bro, followed by a rolling hill and a row of Piranha Plants in Warp Pipes. A group of rolling hills, Venus Fire Traps, and regular Piranha Plants are found, with the [[Goal Pole]] at the end of the area.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first [[Star Coin]] is located through a [[Warp Pipe]] above a platform. Some Goombas and a Prickly Goomba are located on the platform, and must be defeated in order to access the Warp Pipe.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the [[Checkpoint Flag|Midway Point]], there is a pipe reachable only by a hidden Yoshi that is hidden in a wall. Through the Warp Pipe, a row of Prickly Goombas must be jumped on using Yoshi in order to reach the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is the air after the second Star Coin, above a Fire Bro and a row of Prickly Goombas.
