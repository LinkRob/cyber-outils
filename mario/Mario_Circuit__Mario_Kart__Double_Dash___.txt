'''Mario Circuit''' is the second course of the [[Flower Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' and the home course of [[Mario]]. It is the first Mario-themed track to feature a [[Chain Chomp]], and Mario Circuit also features [[Piranha Plant]]s that lunge at nearby racer as well as [[Goomba]]s patrolling the sandy hills area. In multiplayer mode, the first set of [[Piranha Plant]]s are gone.

This track appears as the final retro course of the [[Leaf Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', as '''{{classic course|GCN|Mario Circuit}}''', having little difference from the original game. The graphics are improved and you can now perform [[Trick]]s off of the bumps in the Goomba area and on the bridge/ramp right before the starting line.

==Trivia==
*This Mario Circuit was originally going to be in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', but was replaced by [[Mushroom Bridge]] probably either in honor of its uniquely added [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart DS)|Mario Circuit]] or to avoid using all three circuit courses from ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'' as a Retro Course in a single ''Mario Kart'' game.
*This was originally the first track in the [[Mushroom Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' (as seen in the Multi-Game Demo Disc Demo), but was changed to [[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Luigi Circuit]] for unknown reasons.
