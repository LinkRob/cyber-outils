'''Serponts''' are a type of snake enemy in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]].

They move forward slowly until the player breaks the pot which they wear on their head. Then, they chase the player at a high speed. Serponts are violet with a yellow underside. Fireballs are ineffective against them when they are wearing a jar.

== Trivia ==
According to Nintendo<ref>https://www.nintendo.com/jp/topics/article/f6d2505a-dd79-410d-b350-b2b2036213f0</ref>, they wear a pot as they enjoy dark and narrow places.
