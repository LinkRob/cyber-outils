'''World 2-4''' is the name given to the fourth [[level]] of the second [[world]] in several {{series|Super Mario}} games and some related media:

*[[World 2-4 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-4]], a level in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''
*[[World 2-4 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 2-4]], a level in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''
*[[World 2-4 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 2-4]], a level in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''
*[[World 2-4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-4]], a level in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''
*[[World 2-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2-4]], a level in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''
*[[World 2-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-4]], a level in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''
*[[World 2-4 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 2-4]], a level in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''
*[[World 2-4 (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|World 2-4]], a level in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.
*[[World 2-4 (Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS)|World 2-4]], a level in ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]''.

Several games have a fourth level in their second world, but are not named World 2-4:
