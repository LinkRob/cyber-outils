'''Treasure Tree''' is a Mini Mini-Game in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Description==
Treasure Tree is a [[minigame]] that can only be played on the western side of [[Shy Guy's Jungle Jam]].

==Gameplay==
Playing the minigame requires a character to be in their [[Mini Mario|Mini]] form, otherwise they pass by Treasure Tree upon encountering it on the board map. If the character is mini, a [[Shy Guy]] talks to the character and allows them to play.

In the minigame, three different items attached to wooden stakes that drop down and swing from left to right, similarly to a pendulum. The player's character has to jump by pressing {{button|gcn|A}} and grab one of the items within five seconds. Once the character has collected an item, is gets added to their inventory.
