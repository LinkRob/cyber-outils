'''Kung Fu Koopas''' are [[Koopa Troopa]]s who are skilled in kung fu, and they only appear in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. They are apprentices of [[Koopa Master]].
{{br|left}}
==In ''Mario Party 7''==
Kung Fu Koopas appear in the board [[Pagoda Peak]], where they run the [[Orb Shop|House of Orbs]].

Among the items for sale in the [[Duty-Free Shop]] is a trophy of a Kung Fu that can be bought for 500 [[Cruise Mileage point]]s.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Kung Fu Koopas have the appearance of Green Koopa Troopas but with an added orange robe.

=== Powers and abilities ===
In their trophy, Kung Fu Koopas are said to train day and night, showing that they are dedicated to kung fu.
