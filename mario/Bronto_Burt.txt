'''Bronto Burt''' is an enemy from the ''Kirby'' franchise. They appear in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related crossovers, most notably the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, and make their first ''Mario''-related appearance in the [[Nintendo 64]] game ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bronto Burts are pink, insect-like creatures with an ovular body and two pairs of small, bug-like wings on its back. It possesses black eyebrows, specifically giving them an angry appearance, and a pair of orange feet.

===Behavior===
In the ''Kirby'' games, some Bronto Burts fly around repeatedly, while others remain perched on the ground and only take flight when [[Kirby]] approaches them.

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''{{'}}s [[Smash Run]] mode, Bronto Burts exhibit unique behaviors. While flying around, a Bronto Burt attacks with its wings by spinning around, dealing several hits that each have low knockback and only deal 5% damage. A Bronto Burt has either one of three flying patterns: it either goes directly toward the player's fighter; flies in a sine wave-like motion; or slowly falling to ground and remaining still until the fighter approaches, upon which the Bronto Burt launches itself up and starts flying horizontally, just as some do in the ''Kirby'' games.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''Super Smash Bros.''===
Bronto Burts make a cameo appearance in the background of the [[Dream Land (Super Smash Bros.)|Dream Land]] stage.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''===
Bronto Burts retain their cameo appearance in the background of the returning Dream Land stage.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''===
A Bronto Burt is featured as both a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] and a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]].

===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''===
[[File:Bronto Burt SSB4 3DS.jpg|thumb|A Bronto Burt as a Smash Run enemy in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'']]
Bronto Burts are one of the enemies featured in [[Smash Run]], and have once again received their own trophy. A fighter can easily defeat a Bronto Burt via nearly any means.

Bronto Burts retain their background appearance in Dream Land, now featured as downloadable content.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''===
While Bronto Burts neither return as a Smash Run enemy (let alone the mode in general) or a trophy, they retain their background cameo in the downloadable Dream Land stage.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
A Bronto Burt appears as a support-class [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]]. Aside from this, Bronto Burts retain their background cameo in the downloadable Dream Land stage yet again.

==In ''[[NES Remix 2]]''==
Bronto Burts appear in some of the ''Kirby's Adventure'' challenges. One first appears in Stage 1-3, in which it is one of the enemies that [[Kirby]] has to defeat using his Beam [[Copy Ability|copied ability]].
