'''Pagoda Peak''' is a board in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Pagoda Peak - Mario Party 7 (Solo Board).png|thumb|left|The board in Solo Cruise]]
Pagoda Peak resembles an ancient Chinese mountain—said to be 4,000 years old—blended with Japanese-style architecture. The board is set in a small town featuring two dojos, including one at the mountain's summit, and is surrounded by rugged, mountainous terrain. [[Kung Fu Koopa]]s run the [[Orb Hut]]s, while an elderly Koopa named [[Koopa Master]] resides at the top of the mountain.

In Party Cruise mode, Koopa Master awards a character who reaches the summit with a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] in exchange for the number of [[coin]]s displayed above his house (10, 20, 30, or 40). Each time a Star is purchased, the price of the next Star increases by 10 coins, up to a maximum of 40 coins. After a Star is bought for 40 coins, the price resets back to 10 coins.

==Solo Cruise==
In the Solo Cruise version of the stage, the player or the opponent wins by acquiring one Star from Koopa Master. And the star is 100 coins. Whoever reaches Koopa Master --at the top of the board-- with 100 coins (and makes it first) wins this stage. If a player reaches Koopa Master with fewer than 100 coins and cannot afford the Star, he allows them to roll a Coin Block to earn more coins. After that, he sends them back to the start. If the player or the opponent gets to Koopa Master with 100 coins, they get the star, and they win.

==Events==
[[File:Pagoda Peak Logo.png|thumb|Logo]]
*Landing on the [[Event Space|Green Space]] near the start prompts [[Toadsworth]] to ask if the player wants to use one of his bottle rockets. If the player accepts, they can choose which rocket to ride. The character is then tied to the chosen rocket, which either transports them to a higher part of the board or sends them back to the start.
*Landing on a Green Space next to one of the Gongs of Fate causes Koopa Master to appear and allows the player to strike the gong, randomly changing the current Star's price. He then gives the player some coins afterward.
*At the Green Space next to Kung Fu Falls, Koopa Master appears and instructs the player to try to collect the coins that flow from the falls. The player must jump and kick between platforms to gather coins while avoiding falling logs. If the player collects all of the coins that appear, Koopa Master rewards them with a Star (or 50 coins in Solo Cruise).
*The Green Space next to Blazing Rock has one or more players attempt to extinguish the flames using a large fan by repeatedly pressing the appropriate buttons. If they succeed, they can enter the cave and emerge with a capsule, which may contain coins or even a Star, depending on how quickly they put out the flames. They may also receive a Master Koopa T-Shirt, which has no value.
*Landing on the Green Space on the Chinese dragon's tongue causes it to pull the tongue into its mouth with the player(s) still on it, and it spits them out in a blue fireball, sending them all the way back to the start.

===Bowser Time===
When [[Bowser Time]] occurs, [[Bowser]] does one of four things:
He takes a souvenir photograph of all the players and forces each of them to pay 10 or 20 coins for it. He then leaves without giving anyone the photo. This occurs on all boards except for [[Bowser's Enchanted Inferno!]]. The backdrop for this board is based on the classic Chinese novel ''{{wp|Journey to the West}}''.
He flattens one of the Orb Huts and replaces it with one of his own, called Bowser City. [[Koopa Kid]] stops any player who passes by and throws them into the shop, where Bowser sells them either a worthless item or a [[Koopa Kid Orb]] for 20 coins. Koopa Kid then either steals or uses the orb. This also occurs on [[Grand Canal]] and [[Pyramid Park]].
He leaps off the mountain and smashes through one of the bridges, preventing players from crossing. Rolls that would normally take players over the bridge now stop at the space just before it. The bridge reappears at the start of the next turn.
He jumps off the mountain and lands in a lower area, causing a tremor that sends all players falling to a lower part of the board—similar to the giant tree's sneeze in [[Towering Treetop]] from ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Trivia==

*When [[Bowser Time]] occurs, Bowser appears on top of the mountain instead of at the start of the board.
