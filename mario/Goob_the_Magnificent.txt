'''Goob the Magnificent''' is a form of [[Goob|Goobs]] in [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']] that can only be found in [[Twisted Suites]]-themed [[ScareScraper]] floors. They have 200 HP, which is twice that of regular Goobs'. 

== Appearance ==
Unlike regular Goobs that are completely naked, Goob the Magnificents have magician tuxedos on, and a bow tie properly tied on each of their necks. The rest of their physical features are same as regular Goobs'.

== In [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']] ==
Like all the other ghosts that were added since version 1. 3. 0, Goob the Magnificents only appear in Twisted Suites-themed floors. Therefore, a player in the team must let Luigi have the magician costume on in order to get to Twisted Suites-themed floors. Goob the Magnificents act exactly same as regular Goobs and are captured in the same ways.
