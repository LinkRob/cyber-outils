'''Truffle Towers''' is a small castle in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''. It is where the [[Music Key]]s are located.

==In ''Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix''==
At the start, [[Waluigi]] breaks into Truffle Towers to steal the Music Keys with the goal of becoming the best dancer. [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] arrive at Truffle Towers in an attempt to stop Waluigi, but the doors are locked. Once Mario and Toad get the key from a shop run by [[Lakitu]], they enter Truffle Towers and challenge Waluigi to a dance-off. After winning, Mario (or Luigi) and Toad recover the first Music Key and set off to find the rest.

Truffle Towers is seen again in the ending, when all of the Music Keys are returned to it.
