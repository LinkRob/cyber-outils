|name=Hopslipper x2 (Battle Card)}}'''Hopslipper x2''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Hopslipper x2 is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s "Long Jump x2" attack. It is two of the [[Hopslipper (Battle Card)|Hopslipper]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied by a green/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 12 coins (unpainted)

Sell Price: 6 coins (unpainted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Hopslipper x2 is first seen in [[Marmalade Valley]], but can also found in [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Plum Park]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Lighthouse Island]], [[Toad Trainworks]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
