The '''Yoshi Cup''' is a recurring [[cup]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It debuts in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
The Yoshi Cup has a [[Yoshi Egg]] as its emblem. The [[Egg Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' also has a Yoshi Egg as its emblem, except it leans to the right, rather than left.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''===
The Yoshi Cup is the first of the two easy cups, as well as the first cup in the game overall. The cup contains two [[Yoshi]]-themed courses, both of which are numbered in the same way as the courses in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. The layout of the two courses in this cup would later be reused for [[Peach Castle (race course)|Peach Castle]] and [[Kingdom Way]] in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
The Yoshi Cup is introduced in the [[New York Tour]]. Here, courses featured in this cup are treated as Yoshi's favored or favorite courses.
