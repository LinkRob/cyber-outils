|race = [[Hammer Bro]]}}'''Mr. Hammer''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario (game)|Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Mr. Hammer is a [[Hammer Bro]] that runs the [[64th Trivia Quiz-Off]] in [[Princess Peach's Castle]] after the events of Chapter 5. After [[Princess Peach]] runs into him and a [[Koopatrol]] in a room, instead of reporting her to [[Bowser]] or [[Kammy Koopa]], Mr. Hammer allows Peach to compete in his quiz show alongside two Koopatrols named [[Spiky Tom]] and [[Spiky John]]. After the quiz show, Mr. Hammer gives Peach a [[Sneaky Parasol]] for just entering, but also a [[Jammin' Jelly]] if she places in first.
