'''Spicy Soup''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario series|''Paper Mario'' series]]. The item is a basic recipe created primarily by cooking a [[Fire Flower]].

==In ''Paper Mario''==
[[File:SpicySoupPM.png|thumb|left]]In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Spicy Soup can be created if [[Mario]] gives [[Tayce T.]] a Fire Flower. She will give Mario this soup which can be used to recover four points of HP and FP.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', the item is made in the same way. Mario must give [[Zess T.]] a Fire Flower. In this game, the Spicy Soup has been greatly upgraded. Instead of healing Mario slightly, this item gives the same effect as [[Maple Super]], recovering ten points of HP and FP, with a bonus of plus one point to his attack temporarily.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Mario can no longer create this item using a Fire Flower as Fire Flowers are absent from the game. Instead, it is created by way of giving a [[Fire Burst]] to [[Saffron]] in [[Flipside]]. Mario will be given this item which restores eight points of HP.

In addition to being a regular item, Mario must use this item after finding [[Princess Peach]] in Flipside some time in the game. Peach falls ill and must be woken up with something zesty, which Mario finds in the Spicy Soup.
