'''Cogs''' are a type of object featured in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'', and ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' platforming games. Cogs first appear in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Description==
Cogs are generally used to power mechanisms, which can be dangerous for the playable characters as Cogs often put them at risk as they move around. Cogs come in varying sizes, and some of the larger ones can lift the characters up to a higher area.

==In the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]==
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''===
Cogs are featured in {{world-link|1|castle|World 1-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)}}. Here, [[Mario]] or other playable characters are required to travel across them in order to progress. If a character is crushed by a Cog, they instantly lose a life.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''===
Cogs make an appearance within [[Crushing-Cogs Tower]]. They retain their general functionality from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', but are now used to travel up within a [[tower]] level and fight [[Boom Boom]] at the end.
