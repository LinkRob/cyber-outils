'''Slamming blocks''' are blocks that appear only [[Fuzzy Time Mine|one level]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. They are yellow platforms that either moves out of walls, hit a wall or another slamming block, and then it will retract. Some of these blocks have [[Green Star]]s behind them.
