The '''Ampberry''' is an item from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They are fruits that grow on Wayaway Island.

== Description ==
Ampberries are heart-shaped fruits with a circuit pattern on their "peel."

== Uses ==
Ampberries are known for their ability to cure [[Glohm]] infection, as Doctor Vulko displays in his treatment of [[Prince Chilliam]] during the Brrning Sea chapter after [[Extension Corps|Ten]] turns the latter into a Glohm zombie. During combat if one brother contracts Glohm, Mario and Luigi can use Ampberries to cure the Glohm infection, which is represented by the eponymous status effect, and can immediately use them whenever the player applies the appropriate [[Battle Plug]].

== Location ==
Ampberries are found commonly in Wayaway Island, where [[Bowser]] stockpiles a lot of them in his castle.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Ampberries take their name from the word "ampere" (a unite that is used to measure the strength of an electric current) and "berry" (due to their nature as fruit).

== Nomenclature ==
In the Japanese and Korean dubs, Ampberries are called "semiconductors" (''Handōtai'' (written ハンドータイ) and ''Bandoche'' (written 반도체), respectively. In Chinese, they are called ''Bàndǎo Guǒ'' (written 半导果), a portmanteau of ''bàndǎotǐ'' ("semiconductor"; written 半導體) and ''guǒ'' ("fruit"; written 果). In Spanish, they are called ''Melóhmio'', a blend of ''melón'' ("melon") and ''óhmio'' (which refers to the unit "ohm" which measures electric resistance).
