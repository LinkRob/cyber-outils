The '''Hoolumbian''' is a type of blend in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Usage==
The blend can be obtained in the [[Starbeans Cafe]]. To have the [[Starbeans Employee]] create the Hoolumbian, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must give 15 [[Woo Bean]]s and 10 [[Hoo Bean]]s to him. Using Hoolumbian on either Mario or Luigi permanently increases his [[Power]] rating by 4, later lowered to 2 for the remake.
