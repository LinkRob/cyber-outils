|race = Unknown (possibly [[Dry Bones]])}}The '''S.S. Chuckola Crew''' are minor characters that are seen in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They are a bunch of skeleton characters that are alive. They follow under the orders of the [[S.S. Chuckola Captain]].
==History==
The S.S. Chuckola Crew are seen on the [[S.S. Chuckola]] where one of the crew members took one of the [[Beanstar]] pieces that [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] need. One of the members agree to give it to him after freeing another one of their members, [[Bloat]], from the wall. Mario and Luigi done so but the explosion causes a bunch of sand to flood into the ship causing it to sail into the water but eventually crashes into a rock. The ship then sinks to the bottom of the ocean and it is unknown what became of the S.S. Chuckola Crew.
