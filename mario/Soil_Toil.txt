'''Soil Toil''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a straightforward dirt path within a garden.

==Gameplay==
There are two teams, each with two characters. Both characters have to cooperatively drive and steer a car over to the finish line at the end, with one of controlling the left front wheel while the other controls the right front wheel. To go forward, the player use their Stylus to turn the wheel on the Touch Screen forward. The car moves in the direction that the slower wheel moves, so each teammate has to moderate the speed in which the wheel spins while driving around. Driving into one of the carrots protruding from the ground or a patch of mud slows the car's movement. Whoever crosses the finish line first wins.

==Controls==
*[[Stylus]]: Spin the car's wheel
