Here are the quotes of [[Mario]] in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]''.

* "Mario!" (selected)
* "Yoo-hoo!" (called in an event or passing through a ring in Dream Platform)
* "Ya-hoo!" (great start in race events)
* "Oh, nice." (landing in the water in Dream Platform)
* "Oh, no." (said three different ways)
* "Pull!" (selecting a heartbeat in Skeet)
* "What-a happened?" (missing all targets in Skeet)
* "Fantastico!" (shooting all targets in Skeet)
* "Oh, no! I lost!" (ending in 4th-8th place in an event or failing a mission)
* "Yes!" (said four different ways)
* "Whoo-hoo-hoo-hoo!" (shouting in Hammer Throw)
* "Yeah! I'm the winner!" (ending in 1st place in an event)
* "That's it, so nice!" (breaking a record)
* "Whoo-hoo!" (jumping in Long or Triple Jump)
* "Whoa!" (losing balance)
* "Aw, mamma mia!" (4th to 8th in Awards Ceremony)
* "Oh. Mario will get you next time!" (2nd or 3rd in Awards Ceremony)
* "Whoo-hoo! I'm the winner!" (1st in Awards Ceremony)
