The '''Happy Shirt''' is a type of armor only usable by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the shop in [[Marrymore]]'s hotel for 38 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Happy Shirt increases Mario's [[Defense]] by 24 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 12, which is the same for [[Mallow]]'s [[Happy Pants]].
