'''Luigi's''' (known by the Japanese name '''Luigip''') is a sponsor based on [[Luigi]]. It appeared in ''Mario Kart 64''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'' ==
Luigi's appeared as a sponsor in signs and trackside banners in different courses.
=== List of appearances ===
* Trackside banners in [[Luigi Raceway]]
* Signs in [[Moo Moo Farm]]
* Signs in [[Kalimari Desert]]
* Signs in [[Choco Mountain]]
* Signs in [[Mario Raceway]]
* Signs in [[Royal Raceway]]

== Etymology ==
The Japanese name, Luigip derives from [[wikipedia:Agip|Agip]].
