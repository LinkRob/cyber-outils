'''Traced Memories''' is a minigame that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player must use the touchscreen to trace over an image that appears on the screen. The player must make sure to draw as much in the lines as possible. There will also be laser beams that move up and down on the screen and turn on and off at times. If the player touches a beam, they will lose a [[Wario]] hat. If they lose all Wario hats, then they will fail at the minigame. After the player has traced the image, they can press the "OK" button to confirm their selection. If they complete it correctly, then they are rewarded with treasure. Traced Memories can have up to 12 different images that can be traced.
