'''Scooter Commuter''' is a [[microgame]] featured under [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[Sci-Fi]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
A character on a scooter drives off of a ramp to launch in midair. While the scooter is in midair, the player must use the {{button|gba|Pad}} to turn it left or right until the scooter is at a precise angle so that it lands on both wheels. In doing so, the player wins at the microgame, but if the scooter does not land properly, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The target is an uphill road, which is at nearly the same angle as the motorcycle after it jumps, so the player only has to turn the scooter slightly.
*'''Level 2:''' The target is a flat platform, and the player has to turn the scooter horizontally, requiring slightly more turning than in the first level.
*'''Level 3:''' The target is a downhill ramp, which inclines at the opposite angle of the scooter when it goes off the ramp. As such, the player has to turn the scooter a lot for it to land correctly.
