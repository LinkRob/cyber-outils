*''[[Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''|firstfilm=''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'' (2026)|films=''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''}}'''Flying Mario''' is one of the seven powers available for use in the popular [[Wii]] title ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.
==Appearance==
As Flying Mario, Mario's shirt color remains the same but his hat and overalls turn into a black color while his buttons turn to a red color. His gloves also glow and leaves behind a red trail as he flies. When Luigi gets it he changes into a black hat and overalls and he keeps his green shirt, like Mario.
==Effects==

Flying Mario is also one of the most unique powers in the game as it can be only used in the [[Comet Observatory]]. Other than that, the only other area without hacking in which it can be used is in the second mission of the [[Gateway Galaxy]]. The [[Red Star]], which transforms [[Mario]] into Flying Mario, is unlocked once the [[Red Power Star]] is obtained. Flying Mario does not appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', however, it is still in the game's coding and can be used with hacks.


==Trivia==
*Flying Mario can be considered ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'''s version of [[Wing Mario]], who is from ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.
