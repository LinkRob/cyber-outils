'''Bull Wario''' is a powered-up form of [[Wario]] featured in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

==Description==
This form allows Wario to instantly destroy [[block]]s by [[Dash Attack|charging]] into them. Wario can become Bull Wario by touching a [[Viking helmet]], or a [[Garlic Bottle]] in ''Virtual Boy Wario Land'' if he is in his [[Small Wario|Small]] form.

==In the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]==
[[File:Bull Wario sign MK7.png|thumb|left|Bull Wario's cameo in the Wario Shipyard course in ''Mario Kart 7'']]
Bull Wario later makes a cameo appearance in [[Wario Shipyard]] course in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', also returning in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' starting with the [[Wario vs. Waluigi Tour]]. Here, it is shown pointing downward to [[Dash Panel]]s up ahead.
