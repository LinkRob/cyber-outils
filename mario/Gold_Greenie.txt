'''Gold Greenies''' are enemies in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They are the rarest [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]], appearing in a few rooms in different mansions.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Gold Greenies are very similar to [[Greenie]]s except they are golden in color.

===Behavior===
Gold Greenies, unlike all other ghosts in the game, spend their time actually trying to get away from Luigi rather than attack him. This is an exception in the [[Secret Mine]] mansion where two Gold Greenies attempt to attack Luigi with hockey sticks. Gold Greenies have a total of 20 HP which is the double amount from a regular [[Greenie]]. Whenever Luigi manages to vacuum up a Gold Greenie, he will be awarded with a lot of money. If Luigi doesn't catch them up in time, they will fly up and away, forcing you to restart the current mission if you want to get the money from them.
