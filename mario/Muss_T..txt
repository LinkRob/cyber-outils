'''Muss T.''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Muss T. is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with the exact same appearance as the average member of his species.

===Etymology===
His name is a pun of the word "musty," a word used to describe something stinky, which is ironic because Muss T. loves to stay clean.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Muss T. is seen near the large hole where [[Princess Peach's Castle]] used to be. He serves no other role except for a [[letter]] that can be delivered to him. In his [[Tattle]], [[Goombario]] also mentions that Muss T. loves to go for walks, regardless of weather, and often cleans a lot for some reason.
