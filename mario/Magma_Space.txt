A '''Magma Space''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Description==
Magma Spaces are a type of [[space]] that only appear in the second part of [[Magma Mine]], explored after defeating the mid-boss. This space does not appear after the Almost There mark.

Whenever a Captain lands on a Magma Space, the [[lava]] rises an additional two, three, or four spaces, depending on which number is printed by the plus sign on the space, in addition to the lava rising a default number of two spaces. The +4 Magma Space is the most rare; the +3 Magma Space is less rarer; and the +2 Magma Space is the least rare of the three. If the magma reaches the cart, the Captain loses half of their mini stars. Those not the Captain wave their hands when the Magma is about to catch up.

==Trivia==
*There is an [[Mario Party 9/Beta elements|unused]] +5 Magma Space in the game's coding. A possible reason it went unused is because landing on a +5 Magma Space would make it too difficult for the characters to escape the lava.
