'''Goombas vs. Goombas''' is the eighth level of [[Hoohoo Mountain]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 5.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 4 [[Beanie]]s and 4 [[Hammer Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Hammer Bro.

The 2nd battle contains 2 Beanies, 3 [[Bob-omb]]s, and 3 [[Paragoomba]]s. The enemy captain is a Paragoomba.

The final battle contains 5 [[Goomba]]s, 2 Hammer Bros, and [[Private Goomp]]. The enemy captain in Private Goomp.

After the final battle, the player unlocks two Paragoombas, and one Goomba.

If the player replays the level, then they can also unlock up to one Paragoomba, one Goomba, and one Hammer Bro, each time.
