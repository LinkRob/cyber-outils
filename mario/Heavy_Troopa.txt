'''Heavy Troopas''' are a fattened variant of [[Koopa Paratroopa|Sky Troopas]] which appear as an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are similar to [[Tub-O-Troopa]]. Heavy Troopas are only encountered in [[Nimbus Land]] during [[Valentina]]'s occupation of the area and in [[Bean Valley]]. 

A Heavy Troopa's only attack is launch itself at their target, and it takes two turns on its end before the Heavy Troopa can use the move.
