'''#6 Wendy's Castle''' is the [[castle]] [[level]] of [[Chocolate Island]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. It can be accessed after completing either [[Chocolate Island 5]] or the [[Chocolate Secret]].

== Layout ==
The level starts off with Mario avoiding a Dry Bones, jumping on a spring (also avoiding a [[Spike Pillar]]) and moving on to where he has to avoid two Grinders. He needs to move forward and avoid two [[Podoboo]]s. Then avoid the next set of the Grinders and Spike Pillars, get to the Midway Point and go to the next room. Mario then has to avoid the Hotheads and Sparkies and jump on the moving platforms to get to the Boss Door

== Boss battle ==
Mario has to avoid two [[Zigzag Podoboo]]s in the fight. Mario needs to jump on Wendy O. Koopa three times to defeat her much like how [[Lemmy Koopa]] was fought (she is the red one as seen in the image). Two yellow clones of her will also come out of the Warp Pipes so Mario has to make sure that he doesn't accidentally jump on them. Once Wendy O. Koopa has been stomped on three times, she falls into the lava, and Mario moves onto the [[Sunken Ghost Ship]].

== Ending Message ==
After Wendy is defeated and put the egg he rescued on the ground, he uses a mop to literally wipe away the pixels that were once Wendy O Koopa’s Castle from existence.

''Wendy O. Koopa in Castle #6 has sung her last song. Mario must meet the challenge that is now before him. There is a sunken ship that appears to be a gateway to the Valley of Bowser.
