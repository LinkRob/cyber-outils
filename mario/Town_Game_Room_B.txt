The '''Town Game Room B''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Town Game Room B is a gambling hall located in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], next to a similar building, [[Town Game Room A]]. In the background of Town Game Room B, various slot machines with blue sides and green borders are lined across a yellow-and-green checkered wallpaper, and a red stool sits in front of every slot machine. Two potted plants resembling small trees can also be seen near the front.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
[[Amp (character)|Amp]] is the only known visitor of Town Game Room B, where he keeps losing at the minigame [[Match 'Em]]. If the player agrees to try and win on his behalf, this initiates the quest [[Losing Streak]], the player needs to win at least one more [[Coin]] in Match 'Em than what they began with. Once this objective is fulfilled, Amp resumes gambling, and the player unlocks the minigame as a reward.
