'''Winkles''' are a race of snail-like creatures that inhabit the reaches of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] and only appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They own a sports recreational center called [[Winkle Colosseum]], which is also located in Chucklehuck Woods, and invite [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to play a game of Chuckola Bounce for 500 [[coin]]s. This mini-game is mandatory, as the reward is a [[Winkle Card]] that allows Mario and Luigi to traverse towards a fallen piece of the [[Beanstar]].

Winkles have tan-ish bodies and orange, blue, or pink shells. À la the [[Whittle]] species from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', their grammar is often broken, although the Winkle who hosts the Chuckola Bounce minigame is able to speak properly.
