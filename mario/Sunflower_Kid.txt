The '''Sunflower Kids''',<ref name="Prima 101">Hodgson, David S J, Bryan Stratton, and Stephen Stratton. ''Super Mario Sunshine'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide. Page 101.</ref> also known as '''Kid Sunflowers'''<ref>Hodgson, David S J, Bryan Stratton, and Stephen Stratton. ''Super Mario Sunshine'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide. Page 20.</ref> and '''Sunflowers''',<ref name="Prima 101"/><ref>Bogenn, Tim, and Doug Walsh. ''Super Mario Sunshine'' Official Strategy Guide. Page 81.</ref> are sunflowers found in the beach area of [[Pinna Park]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. Their guardian is a giant sunflower named the [[Great Sunflower]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sunflower Kids have the appearance of a regular sunflower but with black eyes with white pupils and black freckles.

==History==
===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
The Sunflower Kids are featured in Episode 4: [[The Wilted Sunflowers]] of Pinna Park in ''Super Mario Sunshine''. Here, the [[Snooza Koopa]]s are destroying the roots of the Sunflower Kids and the Great Sunflower, causing them to wilt. Whenever [[Mario]] defeats a Snooza Koopa, the Sunflower Kid near it is no longer wilted. When Mario defeats the last Snooza Koopa, the Great Sunflower is restored, and it happily rewards Mario a [[Shine Sprite]].

===''Minecraft''===
In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''[[Minecraft]]'', [[w:c:minecraft:Sunflower|Sunflowers]] are replaced by Sunflower Kids.
