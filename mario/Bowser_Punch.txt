The '''Bowser Punch''' is one of the attacks that [[Bowser]] starts off with in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

== Description ==
The Bowser Punch is an extremely powerful attack and can K.O. an opponent if used correctly. During the attack, Bowser charges up his fist and then when {{button|ds|X}} is pressed, the attack is initiated. If the player forgets to press {{button|ds|X}}, then Bowser falls on the opponent dealing miniscule damage. If {{button|ds|X}} is pressed, though, when Bowser's arm is far back, the most powerful his is dealt and can possibly K.O. the opponent. This attack is as famous as Bowser's legendary [[Bowser Flame|flame]].
