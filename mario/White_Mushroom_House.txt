A '''White Mushroom House''' is a special type of [[Toad House (Super Mario Bros. 3)|Toad House]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It can appear in any world except [[Dark Land|World 8]] if the player collects at least a certain number of [[coin]]s in a specific level of that world. Unlike regular Toad Houses, a White Mushroom house consists of only one treasure box, which contains a [[P-Wing]] (in odd-numbered worlds) or an [[Anchor]] (in even-numbered worlds).
