"'''Bad Rap'''" is the thirty-second episode of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the live-action segment "[[Caught in a Draft]]."

== Plot ==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] arrive in [[Rap Land]], where the people are no longer rapping. Upon their arrival they see that the people are being hypnotized into bringing their gold [[coin]]s to [[Rappin' Koopa]] ([[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s alias for the episode), who has taken over the radio signals. While trying to figure out what to do, they are approached by the [[Flab Boys]] who are unaffected by Rappin' Koopa because of their wearing headphones. The Flab Boys explain what happened and agree to help.

In order to infiltrate the place the Princess is disguised as a member of Rap Land and brings in a gold coin that secretly conceals Toad. Unfortunately, when the bag is thrown with the others, Toad squeaks tipping off Rappin' Koopa to their trick. The Princess is captured but Toad is ignored.  Mario and Luigi attempt to save the Princess but fail to get in before the doors are closed.

Inside Rappin' Koopa's hideout, Rappin' Koopa has both the Princess and [[King James]] tied up on a giant record player. He activates the record player expecting the needle to finish off his prisoners.  Fortunately, Toad sneaks in and deactivates the machine when Rappin' Koopa leaves. With both King James and the Princess freed, they return to the Marios who had stopped for lunch. It's determined that they must unplug Rappin' Koopa's source of power and find the giant plug. Mario volunteers to unplug it but is electrocuted when he touches it. The electricity turns him into [[Fire Mario|Super Mario]], who successfully gets rid of Rappin' Koopa's power. Rappin' Koopa is none to happy about this and prepares to attack.

Rappin Koopa arrives with a legion of his [[Koopa Troopa]]s and a squadron of [[Bob-omb]]-carrying [[Albatoss]]es.  Fortunately, the heroes had been preparing for the attack. The albatosses are defeated by exploding records launched at them and the Koopa Troopas are bowled away by the Flab Boys. Rappin' Koopa is then defeated and pulls out a [[Magical Potion]], which he uses to create a door to escape through.

With Rappin' Koopa gone, a "new" song is written and all of Rap Land dances to the show's theme song, the "[[Mario Rap]]".

== Trivia/Goofs ==
* This is the only ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' episode to feature African American characters, King James and the Flab Boys.
* King James is a based on soul legend {{w|James Brown}} who was influential in the development of rap music, and the Flab Boys on the band known as {{w|The Fat Boys}}.
* This is the first time Toad's tendency to squeak when squeezed is recognized by the characters.
* The only time Mario is shown to become Super Mario through electrocution.
* Luigi offers Mario spaghetti but the dish looks like a pizza.
* After Koopa escapes into the warp zone the door disappears but a door shaped hole is left behind.
* The first time the characters of the show get to hear their own theme song.
* When King James first puts a record in his shooting stereo device, a closeup of his right hand is seen. At this moment, the ring that was on in left hand is now on his right. Similar inconsistencies also occur, such as his ring disappearing completely.
