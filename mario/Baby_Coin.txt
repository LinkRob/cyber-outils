'''Baby Coins''' (also called '''Character Coins''') are a type of large [[Coin]] found in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. These coins are found throughout the levels of the game, each level contains one Baby Coin. The Baby Coins resemble a large sparkling coin with one of the babys' face imprinted on it, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can only collect a Baby Coin if he is carrying the correct Baby seen on the coin (otherwise, a dotted outline will appear in the Baby Coin's place). Baby Coins usually require the respective baby's ability to obtain and unlike regular coins, cannot be obtained with [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]]. In the earlier worlds of the game, there is usually a [[Stork Stop]] not to far away from the Baby Coin however, starting with [[World 4 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 4]], the Baby Coins start requiring some extra skill to reach. Every 8 Baby Coins that are collected will unlock a hard minigame in the minigames mode (with the exception of the last one where the last 7 Baby Coins are required instead). The Baby Coins can only be collected by [[Baby Mario]], [[Baby Peach]], and [[Baby Donkey Kong]] respectively and there are 39 Baby Coins in all with 13 each being divided amongst the three babies.
