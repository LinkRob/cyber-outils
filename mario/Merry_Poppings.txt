'''Merry Poppings''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Opening==
The players are floating in the sky with balloons, ready to shoot blowguns to try to pop each other's balloons.

==Gameplay==
The two dueling characters are suspended in the sky by the strings of 30 balloons. Each player is given a [[blowgun]], which they shoot from to pop each other's [[balloon]]s. The first player to pop all 30 of their adversary's balloons wins. If five minutes pass and neither character is eliminated, the minigame ends in a draw.

==Ending==
When the losing player runs out of balloons, they emit their "blown away" cry while falling. A "poof" is then heard when the losing player falls through the clouds. The winning player waves while saying their victory quote. However, if five minutes pass, all balloons of both players detach.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|A}} '''+''' {{button|gcn|B}} (simultaneously) &ndash; Shoot blowgun

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Pop all of your opponent's balloons with a blowgun before he or she pops all of yours."''
*'''Advice''' &ndash; ''"The secret is pressing the buttons 30 times before your opponent does!"''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The minigame title is a pun on the name of the fictional character [[wikipedia:Mary Poppins (character)|Mary Poppins]].
