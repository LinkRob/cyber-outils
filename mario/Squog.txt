A '''Squog''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are a stronger, faster, and greener variant of [[Squiglet]]s. They first appear in [[Land of the Cragnons|Chapter 5]].



==History==
Squogs are the second-strongest version of [[Squiglet]]s in the game and can be first seen in the [[Gap of Crag]]. These green Squiglets attack by spitting rocks at their targets. Squogs also move faster than Squiglets as well.
