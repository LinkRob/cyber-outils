'''Warp Dice Blocks''' are items only appearing in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

== Description ==
The Warp Dice Block appears as a rainbow-colored Dice Block with white edges. It has no numbers anywhere on the dice.

== In ''[[Mario Party DS]]'' ==
The Warp Dice Block can be purchased at the [[Monty Mole]]'s [[Item Shop]] for 2 [[coin]]s, or from random events on specific boards. When used, it warps the player to a random space, then they continue their turn as usual. Computer players are programmed to never buy or use the item.

=== Strategy ===
This item can serve as a risk-take should you be the furthest away from getting a [[Star]] and want to take your chances to move to a closer spot. This item can make you or break you depending on its usage and what turn it is used on. This item can especially be helpful for [[Kamek's Library]] should the opportunity arise.
