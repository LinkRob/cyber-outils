A '''Big Cosmic Clone''' is a variant of a [[Cosmic Clone]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' as an enemy.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Big Cosmic Clone resembles its regular counterpart, both being purplish-black with yellow eyes and having the shape of [[Small Mario]], except a Big Cosmic Clone is larger, as its name would suggest.

===Behavior===
Like regular Cosmic Clones, a Big Cosmic Clone follows after [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to mimic his movements. Due to their size, Big Cosmic Clones can break [[Brick Block]]s, something that regular Cosmic Clones cannot do. Like their regular counterpart, Big Cosmic Clones can be defeated by either touching the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]] to end the level, touching it while [[Invincible Mario|invincible]], or from reaching certain areas within a level that Big Cosmic Clones cannot continue to. For the former two methods, a Big Cosmic Clone yields ten [[coin]]s upon being defeated.
