{{Nihongo|'''Echofinder'''|ちょうおんぱ|Chou Onpa|Ultrasonic Waves}} is a special attack used by certain enemies and bosses in the game, ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
'''Echofinder''' is a neutral attack that can affect the player's status. When the enemy uses Echofinder, a bunch of yellow/orange circles form around the targeted player. It can stop the selected character from using special attacks for a while and even inflicts damage. However, the player can negate Echofinder with armor like the [[Lazy Shell]].
