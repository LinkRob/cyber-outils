'''Cranky's Kong Pads''' are special pads that appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

== History ==
These pads are found throughout levels and each of them have a certain Kong's face on them. These are mean to activate special abilities that the Kongs must purchase from [[Cranky's Lab]].

=== Types of Cranky's Kong Pads ===
*'''[[Donkey Kong]] Pad''' - This activates [[Baboon Blast]] where Donkey Kong blasts into the sky.
*'''[[Diddy Kong]] Pad''' - This activates the [[Simian Spring]] ability where Diddy Kong can leap to high places.
*'''[[Lanky Kong]] Pad''' - This activates [[Baboon Balloon]] allowing Lanky Kong to float upwards.
*'''[[Tiny Kong]] Pad''' - This activates [[Monkeyport]] where Tiny Kong can warp to certain areas. This is needed to access the final level: [[Hideout Helm]].
*'''[[Chunky Kong]] Pad''' - This activates [[Gorilla Gone]] where Chunky Kong can turn invisible allowing him to sneak past enemies and see invisible things.
