'''Spear-bots''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Spear-bots are robots with a cylindrical body. Their torso is light green, while their head, rounded hands, and feet are all light yellow. A Spear-bot has a pair of eyes on its head but no other facial features. Spear-bots have a compartment on their chest containing a large, yellow spear.

===Behavior===
A Spear-bot can extrude and retract its spear at will. Whenever [[Wario]] is nearby, a Spear-bot faces him and sticks out its spear, during which it also moves faster. After some time, a Spear-bot retracts its spear and slows its movement again.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Spear-bots are first encountered in [[The Peaceful Village]], where Wario can find two of them. Aside from Spear-bots, Wario also encounters two variants of them along his journey, [[Fire Robota]]s and [[Beam Robota]]s. If Wario is poked by a Spear-bot's spear, he gets stunned temporarily.
