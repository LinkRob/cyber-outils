'''Monochrome Explosion''' is the fifth level of [[Joke's End]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 24.

==Layout==
The 1st battle contains 1 [[Buzzy Beetle]], 2 [[Bomb Boo]]s, and 5 [[Boo]]s. The enemy captain is a Boo.

The 2nd battle contains 3 Bomb Boos, and 5 Boos. The enemy captain is a Boo.

The 3rd battle contains 1 Buzzy Beetle, 2 Bomb Boos, 4 Boos, and 1 [[Big Boo]]. The enemy captain is a Big Boo, and he knows [[Rally]] and [[Deny]].

The final battle contains 1 Buzzy Beetle, 6 Bomb Boos, and 1 [[White Magikoopa|Magikoopa (White)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows Call for Backup and Deny. Call for Backup calls a [[Koopa Paratroopa]], or [[Para-Beetle]].
