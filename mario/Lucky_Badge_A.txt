'''Lucky Badge A''' is a stronger variation of [[Lucky Badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It greatly increases the likelihood of getting [[Lucky]] hits. The Lucky Badge A can be obtained from [[Shroob Castle]].
