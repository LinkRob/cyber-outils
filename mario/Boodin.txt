|race = [[Flip-Flopian|Flopsider]]}}'''Boodin''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Boodin is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that is green in color. He wears shades and has a mustache.

==History==
Boodin lives in [[Flopside]] and runs a [[Catch Card]] shop known as [[Fondest Hopes]]. He is known to like collecting cards and often hangs out with others to play cards with them.

==Trivia==
*Boodin's [[Flipside]] counterpart is [[Hoodin]].
