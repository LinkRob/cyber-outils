'''Clean Team''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
Both teams are walking in front of a hotel when they spot the dirty windows. On each side, the player with the bottle sprays foam on a window, and the player with the towel wipes it clean.

==Gameplay==
Both teams must try to be the first to clean the mud off every dirty window on a hotel building. Each team has one character with a spray bottle and another with a towel. To clean a window, the character with the spray bottle must spray foam onto it, then the character with the towel must wipe the foam off to finish cleaning it. The first team to clean every window wins.

During the day, [[Goomba]]s are each shown from the cleaned windows, but during the night, [[Koopa Troopa]]s are shown instead. There are no gameplay differences between the daytime and nighttime variations of the minigame.

==Ending==
The winners stand in front of the cleaned hotel as they perform their victory animations.

==Controls==
===Player with the spray bottle===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Move
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Spray foam

===Player wiping the windows===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Move
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Wipe the window

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Team up with a partner to fight window grime! The first team to clean all the muck off their windows wins!"''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"Buckets cannot be passed. Plan your route to avoid wasting valuable time!"''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The title of the minigame is a play on the phrase "dream team," with the first word replaced by "clean" because it involves two teams competing to clean a hotel building.
