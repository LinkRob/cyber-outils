A '''tiny pipe''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Description==
A tiny pipe is a type of [[Warp Pipe]] that obstructs certain paths on a [[board]], although a tiny pipe has a small hole that a character can optionally pass through if they are under the effects of either a [[Mini Mushroom]] or a [[Super Mini Mushroom]]. Tiny pipes often lead to special [[space]]s or a Mini Mini-Game, or they can be simply used as shortcuts.

==In ''Mario Party 4''==
Tiny pipes appear in the different boards. The [[Extra Room]] board, [[Mini Board Mad-Dash]], has a total of three tiny pipes, more than the number of tiny pipes that the other boards have.

In the [[Mario Party 4 (Flash game)|''Mario Party 4'' Flash game]], tiny pipes appear on [[Goomba's Game Board]] and [[Boo's Game Board]]. [[Mario]] can traverse through if he has a Mini Mushroom, much like in ''Mario Party 4'' itself.
