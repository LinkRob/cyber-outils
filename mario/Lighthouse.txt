'''Lighthouses''' are a location in ''[[Bowser's Fury]]''. They appear on all major islands in [[Lake Lapcat]], and Lighthouses are colored around the region's [[Giga Bell]]. A lighthouse is covered in [[goop]] at first but can be fixed using [[Cat Shine]]s.
