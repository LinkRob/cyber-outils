'''Spooky''' is an enemy which only appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. A Spooky is actually either a [[Shy Guy]] or a [[Bandit]] wearing a disguise, depending on the action; if a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] either eats or [[Ground Pound]]s on a Spooky, the disguise disappears to reveal a [[Shy Guy]], but if a Spooky lands on Yoshi to steal Baby Mario, it is revealed to be a Bandit.
