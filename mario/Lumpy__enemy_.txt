{{nihongo|'''Lumpy'''|つまりカエル|Tsumari Kaeru}} is a large frog enemy which first appears in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. Lumpys are very lazy, and their only ability is to block [[Wario]]'s path. In order to pass through them, Wario must either use a [[Ground Pound]] or roll off of a slope to move them. To defeat a Lumpy, Wario must touch one while in either his [[Hot Wario|Hot]], [[Vampire Wario|Vampire]], or [[Ice Skatin' Wario|Ice Skatin']] forms.

In ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]'', a Lumpy appears as a character, and has a role during Story mode.
