The '''Star Tournament''' is an unlockable challenge from ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''.

==Description==
The Star Tournament includes the same competitions and courses, only with a more intricately designed and difficult way of play. The winds are much stronger and blow the golf ball much farther, the [[Chain Chomp]]s have longer chains, there are more trees and rough, [[Tournament Green]]s are used instead of regular greens, [[Boo]]s affect the golf ball more drastically, [[Bunker (obstacle)|Bunker]]s are deeper, it rains more on the courses, and the computer competition is more brutal. Players also usually have to have Star Characters in order to play successfully.

To unlock the Star Tournament, the player must first win all competitions of the regular tournament. The player must be skilled and know how to guide the ball in all sorts of circumstances in order to win the Star Tournament.
