The '''secret passage''' is a wide road taken by Mario, Princess Peach, Toad, Donkey Kong, Cranky Kong, and the members of the Kong army as a shortcut to the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] in ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''. The path ends with a ramp that leads to Rainbow Road. The secret passage is located in the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]] and lies in between mountains and waterfalls.
