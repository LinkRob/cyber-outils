'''Spike Traps''' are a hazardous obstacle debuting in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', they are orbs that have [[Spike (hazard)|Spikes]] all around them, Spike Traps are typically found in large groups requiring some tactical manuevers to avoid. Spike Traps are also capable of floating as well in a similar fashion to [[Grinder (obstacle)|Grinder]]s. Just like regular [[Spike (hazard)|Spikes]], Spike Traps will power-down [[Mario]] if he touches them.

==History==
===''Super Mario Maker / Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS / Super Mario Maker 2''===
[[Image:Spike Trap Bonanza.jpg|thumb|left|[[Mario]] in a maze of Spike Traps in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' style.]]
Spike Traps appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as it's [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|two]] [[Super Mario Maker 2|sequels]] as placeable objects. Spike Traps can be placed in almost any theme of any style and act as obstacles to avoid (as a smaller alternative to the [[Grinder (obstacle)|Grinders]]). Spike Traps can be placed along the ground or in the air, enemies can also walk along them however if [[Mario]] touches a Spike Trap, he powers-down or dies. Unlike most spiked hazards, [[Mario]] cannot safely [[Spin Jump]] on Spike Traps however [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can walk along them safely. [[Mario]] however can only walk along them if he is under the effects of a [[Starman]]. In the underwater theme for the ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' style, Spike Traps are replaced with [[Jelectro]]s.

===''Super Mario Run''===
[[Image:Spike Trap Run.png|thumb|x200px|[[Luigi]] near some Spike Traps in ''Super Mario Run'']]
Spike Traps return in ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' this time being found in a few courses most notably in the [[Black Coin]] variations. Just as before, Spike Traps can be seen floating idly and will damage those who land on them or run into them from below or the sides, Spike Traps often guard [[Black Coin]]s and other items and the use of a [[Starman|Star]] is often required to run along the Spike Traps, additionally, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can easily run along the Spike Traps harmlessly (however the sides are still harmful to the Yoshis).
