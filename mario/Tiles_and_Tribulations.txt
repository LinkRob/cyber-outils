'''Tiles and Tribulations''' is a Coin Chaos minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. It, [[Rolling Rumble]], and [[Goomba Gold Rush]] are the only three Coin Chaos minigames available after the [[Coinathlon]] mode is unlocked.

==Gameplay==
Players have to rotate tiles of various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' enemies to match three or more in a row to eliminate them and earn some [[coin]]s. Whenever tiles are eliminated, any tiles above it fall into the space below, and more tiles appear to fill the remaining opening on the top. Eliminating [[Bob-omb]] tiles causes them to explode, destroying any nearby tiles as well. Item-related tiles can appear if the character collects enough coins, and such tiles also have to be matched in a row of three to obtain the coins.

==Levels==
*Level 1: Tiles of [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Boo]]s, and [[Piranha Plant]]s are present.
*Level 2: [[Red Coin]] tiles appear.
*Level 3: There are more Red Coin tiles, and [[Buzzy Beetle]] tiles are also included.

==Controls==
*{{button|3ds|B}}: Rotate left
*{{button|3ds|A}}: Rotate right

==Flavor text==
*''Match three!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a pun on the phrase "trials and tribulations," referring to a test of patience and endurance.
