The '''Gulchrock Sea''' is one of the five major seas explored in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is the fourth sea to be visited, following the [[Brrrning Sea]] to the south, and is unlocked when [[Connie]] reactivates the latter's Great Lighthouse after the [[Pipegunk]]'s defeat. The Gulchrock Sea is in Concordia's northeastern corner, east of the [[Lushgreen Sea|Lushgreen]] and [[Stormstar Sea|Stormstar Seas]].

== Major Islands ==

=== Offandon Island ===
Unlike its predecessors, the Gulchrock Sea has a single major native drifting island in its waters: [[Offandon Island]]. [[Bowser]] brought this island under his control, and his minions are inhabitants here. Bowser and his minions have turned the Concordians native to the island against [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

== Non-native Islands ==
When Mario and Luigi make their way to the Gulchrock Sea, Bowser attacks Shipshape Island, disconnecting [[Twistee Island]] and [[Heatfreeze Island]]. The two must be reconnected after Mario and Luigi escape Offandon Island and link it.

== Islets ==
The Gulchrock Sea has the highest number of islets, with a total of seven:

* Cozee Islet
* Paquette Islet
* Meeger Islet
* Mynor Islet
* Nanoh Islet
* Teensi Islet
* Daintye Islet

== Great Lighthouse Island ==
The Gulchrock Sea's [[Great Lighthouse Islands|Great Lighthouse Island]] becomes viable for visitation when Mario and Luigi have gotten a pile of [[Ampberry|Ampberries]] and taken them to [[Doctor Vulko]]. After they deal with a few [[Mettozokettohei|Mettozokettoheitachi]] and [[Glohm]]-infused [[Gabon|Gabons]], they can access the second floor, where [[Zokket]] has stashed his documents, which contain notes about the rebirth of a monster called "[[Reclusa]]" and plans to dispose of the Mettozokettoheitachi and the [[Extension Corps]] after it awakens. Afterwards is the top floor, where [[Connie]] activates the Great Lighthouse. After she does so, she and the Bros. spot [[Fortress Zokket]] releasing [[Glohm]].

After Reclusa reawakens and conquers [[Concordia]], [[Cozette]] heads to this island, where she can be paid a visit during a sidequest and reveals that this is where she discovered the fiend's egg.

== Trivia ==
