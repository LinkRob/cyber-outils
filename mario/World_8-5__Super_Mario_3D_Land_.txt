'''World 8-5''' is the forty-third level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
The level opens alongside a large brick wall within a castle. Mario must traverse several small blocks and platforms &ndash; some cracking and some flipping &ndash; in order to reach the other side of the lava-filled abyss below. The level sports a short top-down effect to the level while moving down a stairway of cracked platforms, and afterward, Mario must dodge the attacks of several [[Bullet Bill]]s while moving along [[Flip-Swap Platform]]s. Mario then can use the pole to reach the level's [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]].

From here, Mario must move along a large "O-shaped" bridge of flip panels with [[Rocky Wrench]]es throwing wrenches at Mario. Along the second staircase of cracked blocks, Mario reaches the last portion of the level &ndash; a bridge of flip panels with [[Banzai Bill]]s shooting over. Across the final bridge of flip panels, Mario is confronted by a single Rocky Wrench and beyond it, is the [[Flagpole]] of the level.

== Enemies ==
Enemies that appeared in World 8-5:
* [[Paragoomba]]
* [[Bullet Bill]]
* [[Rocky Wrench]]
* [[Banzai Bill]]

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] of the level is found on the blue side of one of the first flip panels in the level.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is on top of the center Rocky Wrench just past the level's checkpoint.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The final Star Medal is obtained within the Mystery Box of the level which is located before the portion of the level including Banzai Bills.
