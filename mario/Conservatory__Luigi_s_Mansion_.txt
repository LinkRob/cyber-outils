'''Conservatory''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Conservatory is a music room which houses a variety of musical instruments. The instruments consist of the following: a saxophone, a harp, an upright bass, three kettle drums, a xylophone, and a grand piano. [[Luigi]] can examine any of the instruments except the grand piano to play a tune, which is the [[Ground BGM (Super Mario Bros.)|Ground Theme]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' while the room is dark.

==History==
The Conservatory first becomes accessible in [[Area Two]], after obtaining the [[Key]] from [[Shivers]] in the [[Butler's Room]]. [[Luigi]] has to interact with each of the instruments except the grand piano. Upon doing so, a complete arrangement of the Ground Theme starts playing. Luigi then has to go to the grand piano, where [[Melody Pianissima]] appears. After Luigi answers Melody's trivia question correctly, she begins to send music sheets to attack him, and Luigi first has to vacuum up the music sheets into his [[Poltergust 3000]] before he can also do so with Melody. After Luigi captures Melody, the lights turn on, and a [[treasure chest]] with the [[Key]] to the [[Dining Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Dining Room]] appears.

During the [[blackout]], three [[Gold Ghost]]s and one [[Purple Puncher]] can be found in the Conservatory. A [[Speedy Spirit]] also appears here exclusively during the blackout, and Luigi can find it by examining the seat in front of the piano.
