'''Geno Blast''' is one of [[Geno]]'s spell attacks in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==​Usage==
Upon using the move, Geno shoots a thin white line up in the skies and makes colorful explosions. Its timing is the same as the [[Geno Beam]]'s one, which is a star meter.
