'''Purple Coin Pipe''' is a Special building that appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
The '''Purple Coin Pipe''' is a purple [[Pipe]] with a [[Star]] engraved on the pipe.

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' the Purple Coin Pipe is a Special building with no rarity. The player can buy it at the Shop after collecting all the [[Purple Coin]]s excluding [[World Star (Super Mario Run)|World Star]]. If the player touches the Purple Coin Pipe, the game will ask the player if they want to play the special course, but colored coins will not be counted on the records.
