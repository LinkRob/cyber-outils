'''Aqualea''' is a mermaid who appears in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] release of ''[[Wario's Woods]]''. 

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Aqualea is a mermaid who has blue hair, with two small fins in place of ears, and a pink tail. She is slightly taller than [[Toad (character)|Toad]].

=== Personality traits ===
Aqualea is prideful, believing herself to be irresistible to her opponents. Before battle, Aqualea is shown clenching her fists down. If she loses, she has her hands on her, shaking her body left and right while bearing a nervous expression on her face. If she wins, Aqualea raises her hands up into the air happily.

== In ''Wario's Woods'' ==
Here, Aqualea is fought as the eighth boss in VS. COM mode.

== Trivia ==
*The [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version of ''Wario's Woods'' has its own mermaid boss named [[Mad]], although the relation between her and Aqualea has never been elaborated upon.
