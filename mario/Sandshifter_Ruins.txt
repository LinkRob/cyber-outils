==Layout==
Like the last two levels, Sandshifter Ruins is mostly of sand. However, the area is slightly darker since it takes place inside the ruins.

==History==
[[Mario]] heads deep into Sandshifter Ruins in order to continue his search for the [[Royal Sticker]]s. He battles through enemies and slides through sands. He eventually reaches a room full of [[Dry Bones]] where the [[Comet Piece]] awaits and the end of the level is there.

==Secret Exit==
The second Secret Exit in this game is located in Sandshifter Ruins. Mario must enter a room after falling through a hole for the second time. After getting the Comet Piece through an hidden opening, Mario must Paperize an area next to the spike pit and place the Comet Piece leading straight to the fourth level Damp Oasis.
