The '''Beanstar Pants''' are a [[clothing]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
Beanstar Pants can be bought from the clothes store of [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 500 [[coin]]s. In the Japanese version, the Beanstar Pants cost 1,750 coins, significantly more than in international releases. Despite this, instead of increasing [[Defense]] by 60 like in international versions, the Beanstar Pants grants only increases Defense by 55 in the Japanese version.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Beanstar Pants only become available in the clothes store of Beanbean Castle Town once the [[Beanstar]] has been restored. It costs 780 coins to buy Beanstar Pants, and they can be sold for 180 coins. It was now made only usable by [[Luigi]]. Aside a stat change of increasing [[HP]] by 15 and [[Defense]] by 41, the Beanstar Pants were repurposed into temporarily granting [[DEF-Up]] at the start of the battle, meaning Luigi receives a 50% increase of his Defense during this time.
