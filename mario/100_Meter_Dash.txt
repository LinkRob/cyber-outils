'''100 Meter Dash''' is one of [[Jimmy T]]'s [[microgame]]s featured in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player must wait until the pistol fires, then mash {{button|3DS|A}} for their character to keep dashing until they cross the finish line. Their character has a magenta silhouette to distinguish from the other runners, who have a black silhouette. If the player presses the A button before the pistol fires, the crowd boos, and the player loses at the microgame.

''WarioWare Gold'' has a mission associated with the microgame, 100 Meter Champ, in which the player's character has to cross the finish line before the in-game clock reaches nine seconds.

===Difficulty levels===
The speed of the player's opponents differs in each difficulty level.

* Level 1: The opponents run slowly.
* Level 2: The opponents run relatively fast.
* Level 3: The opponents run very fast.
