An '''Albatross''' (also known as a '''Double Eagle''') is a golf term for scoring under three below a par. Albatross is the hardest achievement to accomplish in the ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' series.

== Description ==
Albatross is three below a par making it difficult to achieve in ''Mario Golf'' series. It requires a character that can shoot the ball to a greater distance. If the player manages to obtain an Albatross, they will receive -3 points.
