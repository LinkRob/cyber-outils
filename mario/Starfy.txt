'''Starfy''' (スタフィー) (or "Prince Starfy") is the titular star of his own series. His first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance is in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', although in the form of a cameo. At one point during ''[[Densetsu no Stafy 3]]'', [[Wario]] assists Starfy on his journey. Prior to the release of ''[[w:c:starfy:The Legendary Starfy|The Legendary Starfy]]'', his name overseas was '''Stafy''', after his Japanese name.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Starfy has the appearance of a yellow, five-pointed star with two blue eyes and a mouth that usually smiles unless disturbed or hurt in any way.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
Starfy has a cameo appearance on a movie poster in [[Yoshi Theater]] reading "LEGEND OF STAFY", being a translation of ''Densetsu no Stafy.''

== In ''[[Densetsu no Stafy 3]]''==
<!--Only add information about when Wario assists Starfy.-->
Starfy is assisted by [[Wario]] at points during his adventure.
{{sectstub}}

== In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''==
[[File:Starfish SPP sprite.png|frame|left]]
Starfy appears as an enemy called a [[Starfish]], with no design changes except for the fact that he wears sunglasses. It describes him, "A star-shaped foe rumored to be the prince of a kingdom". This describes a bit of Starfy while he was a prince. Starfish first appears in the level [[Hoo's Wood 2-5]].

==In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''==
[[File:MarioMakerStarfy.png|frame|left]]
Starfy is featured as one of the [[Mystery Mushroom]] [[Costume Mario|costumes]]. His can be unlocked by completing the "[[Starfy: Prince of Pufftop]]" [[List of official Super Mario Maker courses|Event Course]].

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''===
[[File:Starfy SSBB screenshot.png|thumb|200px|''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' screenshot]]
Starfy (using his Japanese name “Stafy”) is featured as an [[Assist Trophy]] character. Unlike most other ones, Stafy is vulnerable to taking damage from opponents and being launched out of the stage. To compensate, Stafy can utilize a variety of moves, such as a spin attack. There is also a collectible [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|Trophy]] of Stafy.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''===
Starfy returns as an Assist Trophy, acting the same as before. Both games also feature him in the form of a Trophy. He now uses his English name.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Starfy retains his role as an Assist Trophy. Graphical changes show that the inside of his mouth is a red color rather than yellow and his face changes into an agonized look when he is attacked. He also cameos as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]] along with other characters from his home series.

== Trivia ==
*During an interview with [[Nintendo]], they were asked whether Starfy was a star or starfish. They explained that he is neither.
