A '''crew''' within the [[Koopa Troop|"Bowser Baddies"]] man the [[Koopa Cruiser]].<ref name="first">[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]] "Hey! You! Are you signing up to join my crew of Bowser Baddies?"</ref> They are made up of [[Koopa Troopa|Koopas]] typically dressed in aviation jackets and bomber hats.

Following [[Bowser]]'s temporary alliance with the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', they escort the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] in flight to the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] alongside their barreled [[Goombas]] who are stowed away. In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' they also escort [[Baby Mario]], [[Baby Luigi]], [[Baby Peach]], and [[Toadsworth the Younger]] to [[Bowser's Castle]].



==History==

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''===
[[File:Koopa Cruiser interior M&LSS.png|left|thumb|The crew on one of the Cruiser's decks.]]
Following [[Bowser]]'s temporary alliance with the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], he assembles a crew of his [[Koopa Troop|"Bowser Baddies"]] at [[Toad Town#Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga|Toad Town Square]]. One of them is lost and [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can direct him to the assembly area. At the assembly area, Bowser introduces his [[Koopa Cruiser]]. The crew board the ship and it takes off into the air.

Aboard the ship, the crew teach tutorials to the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] on jumping, blocks, and how to fight. One of them also takes passport photos for the Bros. in order to cross the border. Some of the crew are seen doing duties like moving crates and stowing Goombas into barrels. After the Koopa Cruiser is destroyed by [[Fawful]], wounded Koopa survivors are found in [[Stardust Fields]]. They ask the Bros. to find Lord Bowser and mention that they haven't finished paying off the Koopa Cruiser.{{clear}}

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''===
[[File:KoopacruiserPiT.jpg|thumb|One of the crew members on the Cruiser of the past.]]
The crew of the Koopa Cruiser are shown in the past. [[Baby Bowser]] evacuates [[Baby Peach]], [[Baby Mario]], [[Baby Luigi]], and [[Toadsworth the Younger]] from the [[Shroob]] invasion. On board, the crew teach the baby Mario Bros. how to jump, use blocks, and navigate obstacles as they are called to the bridge. 

The pilots of the ship fire the Koopa Cannon to destroy pursuing [[Shroob UFO]]s. Eventually they run out of ammo and a missile sent by [[Princess Shroob]] from [[Shroob Castle]] hits the Koopa Cruiser. The Koopa Cruiser then crashes into [[Bowser's Castle]].{{clear}}
