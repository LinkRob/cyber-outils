'''Gummits''' are gummy enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Gummits are based on the moles in {{w|Whac-A-Mole}} challenges, although Gummits are green mole-like gummies with cheeks, a snout, a belly, and three strands of hair on their head.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
Gummits, along with their [[Golden Gummit]] and [[Spiky Gummit]] variants, only appear in [[Freezy Flake Galaxy]], during the mission [[The Chimp's Skating Challenge]], and in [[Shiverburn Galaxy]], during the mission [[The Chimp's Ultimate Skating Challenge]]. Both missions involve the [[the Chimp]] putting [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] on an ice skating challenge to reach at least a certain number of [[point]]s from defeating Gummits and their variants within the [[Time Limit]]. Unlike most enemies, Gummits cannot harm Mario or Luigi, although Spiky Gummits can. Mario or Luigi earn ten points whenever they defeat a Gummit from [[skate|skating]] into it and therefore kicking it into the air.
