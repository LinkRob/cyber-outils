The '''Dragon Shot''' is an [[Offensive Power Shot]] in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Dragon Shot used.png|thumb|left|200px|Clay hitting the ball]]
The move can be learned by [[Clay]] or [[Ace]] once they have reached L5 Muscle and L2 Body. When either character uses Dragon Shot, the ball starts to absorb energy before being hit toward the opponent's side of the court with minimal curve. While the move is used, the ball resembles a dragon's head until the opponent returns it, upon which the opponent gets knocked back a far distance.
