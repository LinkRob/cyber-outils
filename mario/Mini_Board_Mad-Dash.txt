'''Mini Board Mad-Dash''' is a [[board]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==In ''Mario Party 4''==
Mini Board Mad-Dash is exclusively accessed from the [[Extra Room]], like with [[Mega Board Mayhem]]. [[Thwomp]] says that he constructed the board within two months, an achievement that he is proud of.

Each character begins with 100 [[coin]]s, and their goal is to have the highest coin total by the end. Unlike regular boards but also like Mega Board Mayhem, there are no [[Star (Mario Party series)|stars]], [[Star Space]]s, special events, or [[minigame]]s at the end of each turn. However, landing on a [[Bowser Space]] can still trigger a [[Bowser]] minigame if Bowser decides upon the Bowser Mini-Game event. Mini Board Mad Dash has a long stretch in its uppermost section that only consists of [[Red Space]]s, Bowser Spaces, and [[Warp Space]]s. The area can be avoided by using [[Mini Mushroom]]s or [[Super Mini Mushroom]]s to access the three [[Gate (Mario Party 4)|Gates]], which lead to the inner part of the board with an [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shops]] and several [[Blue Space]]s. The [[Mushroom Space]]s here only award characters with Mini Mushrooms, unlike other boards.

Like with Mega Board Mayhem but unlike other boards, Item Shops always have five of the same [[item]] for sale, instead of a selection of five different items, and they can only sell [[Warp Pipe]]s, [[Sparky Sticker]]s, Super Mini Mushrooms, and [[Super Mega Mushroom]]s.

==Trivia==
*Mini Board Mad-Dash has the most Gates compared to the other boards.
