'''Skillit''' is a character appearing in [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team|''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'']]. He is a [[Shelltop]] in [[Wakeport]] who manages the [[Mad Skillathon]] minigame.

== Profile ==

=== Personality ===
Skillit is known for his enthusiasm, being the host for the Mad Skillathon's events, and his first encounter with [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] sees him disappointed with Skillit believing the two are comedians due to their lack of skill, but Skillit changes his mind when the duo show their [[Bros. Attack|Bros. Attacks]]. Afterward, he seems confident the Bros. have what it takes to participate in the Mad Skillathon. Skillit tends to sprinkle his speech with slang words like "dawg" and "playa."

=== Appearance ===
Skillit has a blue shell on his head, blue clothes with a red bow tie, brown shoes, and black shades that obscure his eyes. In his hand is a microphone.
