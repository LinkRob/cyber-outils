'''Ropes''' are a recurring object within the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', and several variations of Ropes have appeared throughout ''Mario''-related media, such as [[Tightrope]]s and [[Wire]]s.

== In ''Super Mario World''==
A Rope is attached to a mechanism on a trail on which it moves back and forth repeatedly, either horizontally or vertically. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can grab on to a Rope, proving mainly beneficial for if they have to move across a [[pit]]. Mario or Luigi cannot grab on to one while riding [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].
