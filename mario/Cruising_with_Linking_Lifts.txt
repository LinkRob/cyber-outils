'''Cruising with Linking Lifts''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
==Layout==
The level is based around [[Linking Lift]]s. The player rides on a platform made on linking lifts connected to a [[track]]. Individual blocks in the lift will disappear. There are stray linking lifts that the player must hit to extend their platform.

Shortly after the [[Checkpoint Flag]], there is a [[Wow Bud]] which will summon the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]] when hit. The [[Wonder Effect]] will make each linking lift not on the track much longer. The wonder effect will summon a [[Lakitu Trio]]. The lakitu trio will throw a bevvy of items, including small [[flower coin]]s, [[coins]], [[1-Up Mushroom]]s, [[Wonder Token]]s, [[Elephant Fruit]], and even a [[Talking Flower]]. The player must collect all five wonder tokens to summon the [[Wonder Seed]].
==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s can be obtained in the following order:
#A [[condrop]] will drop a 10-flower coin onto a pair of linking lifts above a few [[Cloud Block]]s.
#A wow bud is located to the far right above a [[pit]], which will summon a 10-flower coin. To reach the wow bud, the player must hit every linking lift available. This requires an [[Elephant Fruit]] to collect the linking Lifts blocked by [[Brick Block]]s.
#During the wonder effect, a 10-flower coin is located on a track at the top of the screen.
