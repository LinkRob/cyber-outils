'''Invisibility''' is a [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Badge]] from [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]].

== Abilities ==
The Invisibility Badge makes the character of your choice invisible, so that you can only tell where you are by looking at the puffs of dust, placing [[Standee|Standees]] or using stickers.
