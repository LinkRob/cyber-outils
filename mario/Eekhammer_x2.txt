|name=Eekhammer x2 Battle Card}}'''Eekhammer x2''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Eekhammer x2 is the fast version of [[Mario]]'s "Hammer x2" attack. It is two of the [[Eekhammer (Battle Card)|Eekhammer]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied by a red/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 15 coins (unpainted)

Sell Price: 8 coins (unpainted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Eekhammer x2 is first seen in [[Plum Park]], but can also found in [[Sacred Forest]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Lighthouse Island]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Toad Trainworks]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
