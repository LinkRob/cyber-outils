'''Construction Site''' is the main setting of ''[[Donkey Kong (game)|Donkey Kong]]'' and its [[Game Boy]] [[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|version]]. It takes place on an incomplete building under construction. The Construction Site consists of [[25m]], [[50m]], [[75m]], and [[100m]], and in the Game Boy version, the Construction Site doubles as a remake of the original ''Donkey Kong'' and as a prologue before branching out into a larger game with the first actual world, [[Big-City]].
