'''Mega Spikes''' are a larger variant of [[Spike]]s featured in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. They make their debut appearance in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''

==In ''Mario Sports Superstars''==
A Mega Spike is fought as a boss in the [[Road to Superstar]] mode. Its attacks involve summoning [[Brick Block]]s and [[Giant Spiked Ball]]s from the sky.

==In ''Super Mario Maker 2''==
Just like other enlarged variants of enemies, Mega Spikes are created by using a [[Super Mushroom]] on their parent species, which in their case are Spikes. Mega Spikes act the same as regular Spikes, with size being their only difference, specifically in that a Mega Spike spits out a Giant Spiked Ball to attack.
