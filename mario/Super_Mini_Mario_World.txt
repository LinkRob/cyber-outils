'''Super Mini Mario World''' is a theme park featured in the [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (series)|''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series]]. It was designed and is owned by the [[Mario Toy Company]] to center it around the [[Mini-Mario]] toys. Super Mini Mario World first appears in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]'' and makes a reappearance in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!]]''.
