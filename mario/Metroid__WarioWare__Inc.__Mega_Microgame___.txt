'''Metroid''' is a [[microgame]] found in [[9-Volt]]'s folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''. The microgame re-enacts the fight between [[Samus Aran]] and [[Mother Brain]] in ''[[w:c:metroid:Metroid (game)|Metroid]]''.

The sound effects in the microgame are slightly different, with the Missile shots sounding more like when the [[w:c:metroid:Kiru Giru|Kiru Giru]] from ''[[w:c:metroid:Metroid: Zero Mission|Metroid: Zero Mission]]'' is pushed back. The microgame uses the [[w:c:metroid:Tourian (theme)|Tourian theme]] instead of the [[w:c:metroid:Zebetite (theme)|Zebetite theme]] like in ''Metroid''. There is also a slight alarm blaring when Mother Brain is destroyed, likely referencing the [[Family Computer Disk System]] version.

==Gameplay==
The goal is for Samus to shoot a total of five [[w:c:metroid:Missile|Missiles]] to defeat Mother Brain, with the first missile being used to destroy Mother Brain's [[w:c:metroid:Control Capsule|Control Capsule]]. There are [[w:c:metroid:Cannon|Cannons]] that fire, although they cannot hit Samus's position. Samus is wearing the [[Varia Suit]] and has 99 Missiles and 8 [[w:c:metroid:Energy Tank|Energy Tank]]s but zero [[w:c:metroid:Energy|Energy]]. Samus cannot move around, although she can turn into her Morph Ball form and back.

In the second and third difficulty levels, Mother Brain fires two or three projectiles at Samus. This causes a failure if they touch her, but Samus can destroy them with one missile or dodge them using the Morph Ball. [[Rinka]]s are also present in both difficult levels, and they can block Missiles as well as hit Samus. Taking a hit from a Rinka only causes Samus to shake.
