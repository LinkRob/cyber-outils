The '''Cursed Dice Block''' is a recurring type of [[Dice Block]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party: Star Rush''===
A Cursed Dice Block only appears in [[World 2-2 (Mario Party: Star Rush)|World 2-2]] and [[World 2-3 (Mario Party: Star Rush)|World 2-3]] of [[Toad Scramble]]. Here, a Cursed Dice Block comes from obtaining a false duplicate of a character, which is actually a [[Peepa]] in disguise. The Peepa bestows a curse on the team in the form of a Cursed Dice Block, which has two 0's and four -1's; the former does nothing while the latter subtracts from the player's total roll by one. After a set period of time, the Peepa disappears.

===''Mario Party Superstars''===
Here, the Cursed Dice Block functions similarly to the [[Poison Mushroom]] from ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. The item's user can either forcefully apply it to another character for a turn or on themselves. A Cursed Dice Block only allows a character to roll from 1 to 3 during that turn. It is possible to cancel the effect of a Cursed Dice Block by using a [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]], [[Double Dice]], [[Triple Dice]], or [[Custom Dice Block]]. After being used, the Cursed Dice Block disappears.

On [[Yoshi's Tropical Island]] and [[Peach's Birthday Cake]], if a character passes by [[Bowser]], he sometimes forcefully sells them a Cursed Dice Block at a considerably higher price than at the [[Item Shop]], where it instead costs three [[coin]]s to purchase.
