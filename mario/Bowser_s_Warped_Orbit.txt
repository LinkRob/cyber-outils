'''Bowser's Warped Orbit''' is [[Bowser]]'s [[board]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. This board can unlocked if the player completes the [[Star Battle Arena]].

==Description==
[[File:MP8 Bowser's Warped Orbit Logo.png|left|thumb|280px|The Bowser's Warped Orbit logo.]]
Unlike boards themed around [[Bowser]] in previous ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' games, this board does not have a [[lava]] theme. Instead, this board is a space station located in [[Outer Space|outer space]]. Additionally, [[Kamek|Magikoopa]] and [[Toady|Toadies]] appear in shops and board events, rather than underlings like [[Goomba]]s and [[Koopa Kid]]s which appeared on Bowser's boards in previous games. The objective here is to steal [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s. There are no stars, so players --on Battle Royale-- start out with five (ten Stars for each team on Tag Battle mode), but the handicap setting can add more starting [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]]. 

This board plays similarly to [[Snowflake Lake]] from ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and [[Pyramid Park]] from ''[[Mario Party 7]]'', but instead of [[Chain Chomp]]s to steal Stars, the player must use special [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] to steal Stars from their opponents: [[Bullet Candy|Bullet]] and [[Bowser Candy]]. These candies are specific to only this board. The Bullet Candy lets the player roll three Dice Blocks but steal one Star, while the Bowser Candy lets the player steal two Stars but roll two Dice Blocks.

[[File:BowserWarpedOrbit (Star Battle Arena).png|left|thumb|250px|Star Battle Arena layout.]]
If the player lands on a [[DK Space]] causes [[Donkey Kong]] to appear on a [[Iron Barrel|Steel Barrel]] satellite, and give the player a free Star (a piece of Candy in Star Battle Arena). This adds the Star to the circulation. Landing on the [[Bowser Space]] causes Bowser to come down from his observation tower to steal a Star from the player (although if the player has no Stars, Bowser will give them ten [[Coin]]s instead). This usually removes the Star DK added to the circulation (reducing the stars back its original number). So in the case of a DK Space, new stars can be added. That is, or in the case of a Bowser Space, new stars can be taken away. ([[DK Space]]s are the only way where new stars can be added.) 

Landing on the [[Lucky Space]] takes the player to [[Shy Guy]]'s Space Buffet. The Lucky Space on this board has a path of [[Blue Space|Blue Spaces]] (each with three coins). However, it does not lead to a free star. Instead, the player is able to collect three free pieces of Candy by passing [[? Candy|Candy Space]]s (which are Bullet and/or Bowser Candy --both unique to only this board). So in the end, the player's inventory is going to be filled with Bullet and/or Bowser Candy (by passing Candy Spaces). 

One of the [[Event Space|Green Space]]s on the board causes Bowser to come down and chase the player all around the board in a complete loop. Anyone that is in their path will also get chased down (this essentially just moves all the players involved to the same space). The Green Space next to [[Kamek|Magikoopa]] makes him use his magic to shuffle everyone's Candies around, like he did back in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. The Green Spaces --arrow spaces in Star Battle Arena-- next to the Warp Tube<ref>''Mario Party 8'' PRIMA Official Game Guide, page 50.</ref> (underneath the observation tower) it causes players to travel through the tube and reach to the other side of the board. And they collect 20 coins along the way. This is useful for traveling to candy shops to buy more [[Bullet Candy|Bullet]] and [[Bowser Candy]] by landing on this Green Space, going into the Warp Pipe, and collecting 20 coins along the way. (In Star Battle Arena, the tube --in this version-- contains nothing. Because it --the tube-- is part of the board's path. It is not a shortcut.) All the other Green Spaces make Bowser reverse the direction of the flow of the board.

Another feature of the board is the three directional buttons (two in Duel Battle), which players encounter when the path splits into two. When a player steps on one, it makes the player go in a random direction: left or right; this resembles the gimmick of [[Backtrack]], a duel board in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', there is a similar board to Bowser's Warped Orbit called [[Bowser Station]], as both boards are owned by Bowser and are in space.

===Shy Guy's Space Buffet===
[[File:Shy Guy's Space Buffet.png|thumb|[[Yoshi]] at Shy Guy's Space Buffet]]
Shy Guy's Space Buffet is the Lucky Space area of Bowser's Warped Orbit. [[Captain Shy Guy (Mario Party 8)|Captain Shy Guy]] flies the player here upon them landing on the Lucky Space. This area is quite small, mostly consisting of a few Blue Spaces --this path is four Blue Spaces long-- each with three coins (and the player can get a total of 12 coins by passing the Blue Spaces). Unlike on all other boards (with a Lucky Space), the lucky area, it does not result in a free Star. Instead, players get to collect three free pieces of Candy by passing [[? Candy|? Candies]], all of which are either Bullet and/or Bowser Candy. When the player makes it to the end, Captain Shy Guy takes them back to the Lucky Space.

==In ''Mario Party 8''==
Bowser's Warped Orbit is the only unlockable board in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. In order to to have it playable in Party Tent mode, the player must unlock Bowser's Warped Orbit by completing it in the [[Star Battle Arena]] once, and thereby the mode itself.

Bowser's Warped Orbit has a role toward the end of [[Star Battle Arena]]. When [[MC Ballyhoo]] is about to give the player's character the [[Star Rod (Mario Party 8)|Star Rod]] as a reward for winning the five previous [[board]]s, [[Bowser]] shows up and steals it, then promptly leaves with it into Bowser's Warped Orbit. The player hops on a platform and follows Bowser there. MC Ballyhoo is upset about what had just occurred, and Big Top flies MC Ballyhoo up to Bowser's Warped Orbit, where the duo retain their usual role of hosting the board.

==[[Star Battle Arena]]==
In Star Battle Area, the player or the opponent wins by reducing the other's stars to zero stars. (That is, by using [[Bullet Candy|Bullet]] and [[Bowser Candy]]. And both of these candies are only hidden on this board.) Players start with three Stars each. 

In subsequent playthroughs, the opponent is random unless the player does Star Battle Arena with the first unlockable character. If they do Star Battle Arena with the first unlockable character, the second one is forcibly the opponent. After defeating their opponent, the player's character can fight Bowser in a void-like arena, where the boss minigame [[Superstar Showdown]] takes place. The player gets the first unlockable character and they are playable in the game. That is, for doing Star Battle Arena with any of the starter characters. So now, they have the first unlockable character. But in order to unlock the second one, they must complete Star Battle Arena with the first. 

Unique to this stage, for the [[DK Space|DK]]/[[Bowser Space]]s, Donkey Kong gives away candy while Bowser steals a star from circulation (resulting with less stars in play). The player or the opponent (if they reduce the other's stars to zero) can get a total of six stars. But if Bowser steals the one star, this results in less stars (five instead of six).
