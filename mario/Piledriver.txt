'''Piledriver''' is one of three [[Mad Move]]s usable by [[Wario]] in ''[[Wario World]]'', the other two being [[Mega Toss]] and [[Wild Swing-Ding]].

==Description==
The move involves Wario spinning into in the air and then pounding the enemy headfirst into the ground, something that instantly defeats most enemies. Performing a Piledriver also generates a shockwave that can damage or defeat any nearby enemies.

Like [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]], Piledriver can be performed by pressing {{button|gcn|R}} upon [[jump]]ing into the air, but Piledriver can only be executed if Wario is holding an enemy. Another similarity between a Piledriver and a Ground Pound is that both can breaking open [[Trapdoor (Wario World)|trapdoors]], although steel trapdoors can only be opened using a Piledriver.
