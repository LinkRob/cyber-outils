'''Elite Goombule''' is a variant of [[Goombule]] and, by extension, a [[Goomba]] which appears an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. They appear as a boss in the [[Trash Pit]].

==Physical description==
Elite Goombules resemble a palette swap of their counterpart, with a blue body and a lime green yolk-like orb at their heart, but a protective black shell appears on their head.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] only encountered two Elite Goombules during their journey, after obtaining all ten [[Attack Piece]]s in the [[Trash Pit]]. An Elite Goombule's helmet-like shell protects it from any [[jump]] and [[hammer]] attacks, and it can therefore only take damage from a [[Green Shell]] attack.

An Elite Goombule is an Level 2 enemy has 16 hit points with an attack power of 15, a defense of 13 (999 against jumps and hammers), a defense of 13, and a speed of 12, taking normal damage from fire and dropping 2 coins. When slain, it may drop a [[Mushroom]] or [[Syrup Jar]] (1/4 and 3/10 chance, respectively).

== Behind the Scenes ==
As with regular Goombules, Elite Goombules are based on cells.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, the Elite Goombules have the name ''Katabō'', from a pun on ''katai'' ("hard"; written 硬い) and ''Saibō'' ("cell"; written 細胞). Their Spanish name, ''Aceroomba'', comes from ''acero'' ("steel") and English "Goomba." Italian calls them ''Goómbuli Eletti'' ("Elite Goombules" (''Goómbulo'' means "Goombule," being based on ''Glóbulo'' ("globule")); singular ''Goómbulo Eletto''). In French, their name is ''Goomblindé'', of unknown origin. German translates their name as ''Abwehrzell-Gumba'', meaning "Defense Cell Goomba." In Korean, they are called "Armed Goombules" (''Mujang Sepoba'', written 무장세포바; ''Mujang'' means "armed"), despite not carrying any weapons or having arms.
