A '''Double Medal''' is an item in the [[Coinathlon]] mode of ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Description==
Upon being activated, a Double Medal temporarily doubles the number of [[coin]]s that the user obtains. While the effects of a Double Medal are active, there is a blue aura around the top screen. When the Double Medal is about to run out, the blue aura flickers and eventually vanishes.

If a [[Diddy Kong]] [[amiibo]] is used in Coinathlon, Diddy Kong always receives two Double Medals when he starts out.
