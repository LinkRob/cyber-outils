{{Nihongo|'''Bowser's Kingdom'''|クッパの国|Kuppa no kuni|, lit. '''Bowser Country'''}}, also known as the '''Well-Defended Castle Floating Among Glowing Clouds'''{{Citation needed}} is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' and the homeland of [[Bowser]]. It is a vast realm based on Japan. Its most notable city is [[Bowser's Castle (Super Mario Odyssey)|Bowser's Castle]], which serves as the kingdom's main battle area in the game.

The kingdom is notable for being primarily inhabited by [[Stairface Ogres]]. [[Crazy Cap|The shop]] is ran by people though. The primary currency of the Bowser's Kingdom are purple [[Regional Coin|Bowser's Oblong]], however, they also accept regular gold coins.

==Description==
Unlike most games, which feature a Western European design for his kingdom, this kingdom is a lush, flowering traditional Japanese style, with a concentric series of walls and baileys, a traditional garden and tea house, and a central keep. Instead of the usual [[lava]], the castle's moats are filled with poison.

==Paintings==
There are two paintings in this kingdom. One is located at the left building near the Main Courtyard Entrance's Checkpoint Flag, at the left side of the wall. It takes the player either to the Snow Kingdom Clifftop at the [[Snow Kingdom]] or the Diving Platform at the [[Seaside Kingdom]], and it can be accessed after completing the main game.

The other painting is located at the Island in the Sky, which can only be reached from a painting in the [[Cascade Kingdom]].

==Power Moons==
{{main|Bowser's Castle (Super Mario Odyssey)}}
There are a total of 64 [[Power Moon]]s (61 regular Power Moons and 1 [[Multi Moon]]) in this kingdom. During the player's first visit to this kingdom, they can collect 37 Power Moons and the Multi Moon. After the player defeats Bowser in the [[Moon Kingdom]], 7 more Power Moons become available to collect, including one from the kingdom's Hint Art and one from using a painting found in the [[Cascade Kingdom]]. The Moon Rock Moons will also be available, adding 17 additional Power Moons.

==Trivia==
*Many ornaments strewn throughout the kingdom, such as the [[wikipedia:Nobori|nobori]] and the [[wikipedia:Traditional lighting equipment of Japan#Chōchin|chōchin]], contain the kanji 寿 (''Kotobuki''), which means "long life". Some chōchin additionally contain the compound 空覇 (can be read as ''Kuppa'', Bowser’s Japanese name), literally meaning "celestial supremacy".
*The kingdom's industry (hanafuda cards) may be a reference to Nintendo's origins as a card company.
