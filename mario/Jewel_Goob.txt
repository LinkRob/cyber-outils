'''Jewel Goob''' is a kind of special ghost and a form of [[Goob]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', it can be found on every floor of [[The Last Resort]] but not in [[ScareScraper]]. Jewel Goobs have can have 80, 100 or 120 HP.

==Appearance==

Jewel Goobs have blue body with white eyes and mouth, same as Goobs, but Jewel Goobs have crystalline body which regular Goobs don't have. They often hide in secret places like [[Golden Goob]]s.

==In the game==

If [[Luigi]] finds one Jewel Goob, it will run around screaming, trying to escape, Jewel Goobs do not take damage while being vacuumed and will only take damage if they are slammed. Unlike the other floors which only has one Jewel Goob, the 13 floor which is the Fitness Center has two Jewel Goobs.
