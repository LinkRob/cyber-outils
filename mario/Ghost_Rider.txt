'''Ghost Riders''' are ghost-like creatures that assist the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] during their journey in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. They only appear in one level, [[Ghost Castle]]. Ghost Riders act as platforms for the Yoshis to step on, allowing them to safely move across hazards and even progress higher into the area.
