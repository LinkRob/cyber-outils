'''World 3-Cannon''' is the [[Warp Cannon]] level of [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. It only consists of a [[Warp Cannon]] in a snowy area, and entering it allows the player's character to skip over to [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]].
