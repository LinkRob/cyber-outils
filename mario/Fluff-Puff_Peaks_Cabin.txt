'''Fluff-Puff Peaks Cabin''' is a [[Poplin House]] in [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. After taking to the Poplin for the first time, he will give you a [[Wonder Seed]] and unlock a path to the rest of Fluff-Puff Peaks. If returned to after defeating [[Bowser Jr.]] the poplin will thank the player for making the mountain safe and talk about visiting the summit together.
