'''Flashy Iron Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Description==
Flashy Jump Stickers are similar in color to Shiny Iron Jump stickers, except they have a diagonal, rainbow background. They take up about one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].

==Notes==
Flashy Iron Jump stickers are ideal for battle strong spiked-enemies. However, they are much rarer as the only way Mario could get a Flashy Iron Jump sticker is to upgrade a Shiny Iron Jump sticker in a certain level ([[Shy Guy Jungle]] is a great level to do this in).
