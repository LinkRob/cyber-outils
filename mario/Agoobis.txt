'''Agoobis''' is a ghost in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' and a form of [[Goob]]. They only appear in the [[ScareScraper]]'s Egypt- themed floors. They have 200 HP. 

== Appearance ==
Like regular Goobs, Agoobises have blue bodies with white eyes and mouths, but they have 200 HP and wear ancient Egyptian clothing.

== Description ==
Like the new ghosts that only appear after the update in 2020, Agoobises will only appear on Egypt-themed floor, they will hide in a random room and wait for the players. They act and are defeated in the same way as regular Goobs.
