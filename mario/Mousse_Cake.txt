'''Mousse Cake''' is a [[recipe]] item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
If [[Mario]] cooks some [[Cake Mix]] by himself, he can create a Mousse Cake. This item restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]]. It appears to be a fluffy pink cake with white and pink frosting and a pink line across it.

During the events of the game, a [[Bub-ulb]] from [[Petalburg]] sends a message from the [[Trouble Center]] for Mario to get two [[Hot Dog]]s and a Mousse Cake for himself. If Mario gives the requested items, the Bub-ulb will reward him with an unlimited supply of [[Dried Bouquet]]s in exchange for Hot Dogs.

It has a very similar appearance to the [[Mousse]] from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', which can be cooked by [[Dyllis]] and cures [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] in addition to healing 15 HP.
