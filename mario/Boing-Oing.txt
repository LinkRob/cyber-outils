A '''Boing-Oing''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Boing-Oings are stronger versions of [[Sproing-Oing]]s and can be found on [[Planet Blobule]]. Boing-Oings mainly bounce around and can damage someone if they get too close to it. Boing-Oings will split into three Mini-Boings but they can be easily defeated.
