'''Speed''' is a stat in all the ''[[Mario Kart series|Mario Kart]]'' games. Normally, speed depends on the size of the engine, and therefore the size of the [[kart]]. The bigger the engine/kart is, the faster it will go.

Usually, the better speed a kart has, the slower its [[Acceleration|acceleration]] is. If a kart is big and fast, when getting hit by an [[Item Box|item]], the kart will take a little bit longer to get back up to full speed.

Although this is normally common sense in the games, there are exceptions. Not always do karts need to be big in order to be fast. For example, the [[B Dasher]] (from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and as a [[Retro Kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'') is not a big kart, and yet it is very fast.
