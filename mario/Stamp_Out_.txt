'''Stamp Out!''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Layout==
[[File:Stamp Out drawing pad texture.png|thumb|left|200px|Texture used for the drawing pad]]
The minigame takes place on a giant drawing pad depicting some hills and clouds with eyes and also what appears to be [[Princess Peach's Castle]].  It is placed on a carpet-like floor with a yellow and light blue-checkered pattern. Various toys appear next to the drawing pad, such as a yellow car, a few crayons to the left, a [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]] stamp on its side, and a few building blocks, but they are only for decoration.

==Opening==
A drawing pad, alongside an assortment of toy-like objects, falls onto a carpet-like floor. The four players piloting stamp machines then fall onto the drawing pad.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Stamp Out MP4.png|thumb|In ''Mario Party 4'']]
Each character uses a stamp machine, which is basically a cross between a pogo stick and a stamp. As characters bounce around the board, their machine leaves a trail of stamps in their specific color: red, blue, yellow, or light pink. The objective is for characters to fill more of the drawing pad with their color than their opponents. Characters can charge their stamp machine for longer [[jump]]s and stamp over their opponents' patterns to override them.

After the 30-second [[time limit]] is up, the minigame calculates the percentage of each color on the drawing pad. The character who has stamped the most of the board in their color wins. If no character manages to cover at least 5% of the board, the minigame ends in a [[draw]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Its name is a play on a phrase that means "to put out or extinguish by stepping on forcefully with the feet" or "to stop or destroy by force."
