The '''Mushroom Field''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Mushroom Field is one of the locations in the [[Desert Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. There are stone walls circling around a sand floor and small flags outlining the perimeter, as well as smaller fields marked for measuring a hammer's thrown distance. The Mushroom Field hosts the Hammer Camp, where others can practice their hammer throwing skills. [[Hammer Bro (Mario Party Advance)|Hammer Bro]] is the only character known to attend the Mushroom Field.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
When the player's character attends the Mushroom Field, they find Hammer Bro, who urges them to try out hammer throwing. Accepting the offer initiates the [[Hammerama]] quest, where Hammer Bro and the player's character compete to throw the farther hammer on a couple of the smaller fields. When the player's character throws their hammer the farthest, Hammer Bro rewards them the [[Mini B-Ball]] [[Gaddget]] as a trophy.
