The '''Polluted Piranha Plant''' is an enemy from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. They can be found throughout many locations on [[Isle Delfino]], the first of which is at [[Delfino Airstrip]], where Mario obtains the first [[Shine Sprite]]. They appear to be [[Piranha Plant]]s made up of the [[Goop]] which is polluting the Island and are destroyed by shooting them in the mouth when it's open with [[F.L.U.D.D.]] three times (one takes six hits). Polluted Piranhas can be found in [[Delfino Airstrip]], [[Delfino Plaza]] ([[Grand Pianta Statue]], the [[Boathouse]], and the [[Lighthouse]]), and [[Bianco Hills]] (Episode 1).
