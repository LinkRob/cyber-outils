'''Luigi Circuit''' is a track in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. It is the first course in [[Lightning Cup]], and instead of being on a sunny day, is a rainy day. Puddles of rain can cause players to slip out. This course reappears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' as a [[Classic course|Retro Course]] in the [[Leaf Cup]].

In the background is a small farm with a satellite and a giant blimp displaying Luigi's picture and his name. In the DS version, his pose on the blimp is different.

The track theme for Luigi Circuit is shared with [[Peach Circuit]] and [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|Mario Circuit]].
