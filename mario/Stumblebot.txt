'''Stumblebot''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' as [[Dr. Crygor]]'s boss [[microgame]], and Stumblebot reappears in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', where it is repurposed as a regular microgame, instead being one of [[Ashley and Red]]'s.
