The '''Mudroom''' is a room in the [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is to the right of the [[Entrance]]. There is also a door that can be [[Strobulb]]ed to gain access to the [[Mudroom Exterior]] where Luigi can pick up a [[Gem]] and money.

== History ==
During the first mission [[Gloomy Manor#A-1: Poltergust 5000|A-1: Poltergust 5000]], Luigi must head into the Mudroom in order to get the Key to unlock the [[Guard Hall]]. Luigi must point the [[Poltergust 5000]] up toward the ceiling fan to make the Key fall down. Luigi can then use his newly acquired Key to unlock the way to the Guard Hall.
