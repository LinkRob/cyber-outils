'''Captain''' is a term for the leader of the team. In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', the term is used on boards.

== Description ==
Whoever is the captain of the vehicle in ''Mario Party 9'' and ''Mario Party 10'' gets the opportunity to roll the [[Dice Block]]. The captain will move the vehicle depending on how many spaces they move. Depending on the space they land, the event will affect them. If the captain lands on a space that triggers the [[Captain Event]], they are in charge of selecting the choices for the players and the options. If they land on the [[Boss Space]], they will get captain bonus at the end of the boss minigame.
