'''Ray''' is a friendly manta ray who appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', in both the [[Loopdeeloop Galaxy|Loopdeeloop]] and [[Loopdeeswoop Galaxy|Loopdeeswoop]] galaxies. It is used to race around two galaxies and beat the time, 1:30:00.

== Description ==
Ray is a dark purple [[Manta Ray]]. His upper side is covered in purple spots, and his eyes are surrounded by orange rings. His lower side is tan.

== Usage ==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] has to ride Ray through the course to get the [[Power Star]]. Falling off results in an instant loss and the loss of a life. The record is 90 seconds.

To accelerate, one must press A. Twisting the Wii Remote is how the player steers Ray, and shaking the remote causes him to jump.{{Super Mario stub}}
