So, when the right card comes your way, grab it by the lapels and shake it senseless!"''|forms = [[The Discard]]}}'''In the Cards''' is one of [[Ashley]]'s microgames from ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.
==Gameplay==
The microgame starts with cards facing upright and the player is told which card they must find; then the cards are flipped. The player must pick it up when the expected card comes to the player's hand. The player loses the microgame if he or she picks up the wrong card, or if the microgame runs out of time.
*'''Level 1:''' There are three cards. If the card is chosen correctly, the two band members play.
*'''Level 2:''' There are three cards. If the card is chosen correctly, two magicians appear.
*'''Level 3:''' There are four cards. If the card is chosen correctly, six magicians appear.
