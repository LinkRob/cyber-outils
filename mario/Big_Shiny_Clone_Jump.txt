'''Big Shiny Clone Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Description==
Big Shiny Clone Jump stickers look similar to [[Flashy Clone Jump]] stickers except for the large size. They take up four sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].

==Notes==
Big Shiny Clone Jump stickers are very helpful for fighting a mob of enemies and certain bosses. Like with the Flashy Clone Jump stickers, Big Shiny Clone Jump stickers can be bought anytime at [[Shaved-Ice Cave]].
