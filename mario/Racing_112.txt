'''Racing 112''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', as one of the microgames under [[9-Volt]]'s [[Nintendo Classics]] folder, and Racing 112 returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[18-Volt]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
Racing 112 features a snippet of the 1978 classic [[Nintendo]] game, ''[[w:c:nintendo:Color TV-Game Racing 112|Color TV-Game Racing 112]]'', and the object is to drive past many race cars without crashing into them. If the player successfully dodges every car until time runs out, they win at the microgame, but if their car crashes into any of the other cars, they lose.

===Difficulty levels===
The changes between difficulty levels differ between the microgame's original and ''WarioWare Gold'' iterations.

In ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', the first level requires three cars to be avoided; the second level requires six cars in pairs to be avoided; and the third level is similar to the second level, except the opponent cars move left and right rather than stay in one lane like in the first two levels.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', the first level requires only two cars to be avoided; the second level requires four cars driving in pairs to be avoided; and the third level is similar to the second level, except the opponent cars move left and right rather than stay in one lane like in the first two levels.
