The '''Koopa Bank''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is located in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City]]. The manager of the bank is [[Koopa (Mario Party Advance)|Koopa]], who has a large, blue vault to store several [[coin]]s.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
[[File:Koopa Bank MPA screenshot.png|thumb|left|Shroomlock and Toadsworth in the Probably a Robbery? quest.]]
During [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, most of the coins are apparently stolen from the Koopa Bank after the power is suddenly shut off. Koopa soon starts panicking, and asks the player to help when they arrive. The quest that follows, [[Probably a Robbery?]], involves [[Shroomlock]] telling the player to interrogate the three other people who were in the bank at the time of the crime, [[Goombob]], [[Paratroopa (character)|Paratroopa]], and [[Mr. I (character)|Mr. I]], who have all left the bank and returned to their homes. Mr. I and Goombob both state that they heard various sounds around the bank, whereas Paratroopa states that he saw the gleam of the coins; as the power was out, it was pitch black, meaning that Paratroopa could not have seen the coins unless he was the culprit. Once the player returns to the bank to inform Koopa and Shroomlock of this, Paratroopa immediately flies in to apologize, saying that he only intended it as a prank and that he can return the coins immediately. Shroomlock decides not to arrest Paratroopa, and the two leave the bank. Koopa thanks the player for helping him by giving them the [[Card Trick]] [[Gaddget]].
