'''World Flower''' is the second bonus world in [[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]. There are a total of nine levels and three Toad Houses in this world. This world has a total of eleven exits. There are three normal levels, a Ghost House and a Castle on the main path and an additional two levels along with a Warp Cannon which can be found by secret exits in the other levels. The three Toad Houses includes two that are red and one which is green. Porcupuffers are the only enemies introduced in the world. The boss of World Flower is Lemmy Koopa, who is fought at the castle at the end, and defeating him will take the player to World 5. The Warp Cannon in this world takes the player immediately to World 6, thus skipping World 5 as well.

==Appearance==
This world's appearance looks similar to [[World Mushroom (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Mushroom]], but with a bright green background, and Fire Flower's in the background.
