The '''Ancient Waterworks''' is a location in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Layout==
The Ancient Waterworks is a very large ruins located deep within a forest. There is also a large waterfall where opening certain doors allow water to pass through.

==History==
===Episode: 6: A Flower That's Drunk Up Power!===
[[Wario]] and [[Goodstyle]] continue their hunt for the [[Wishstone]] pieces where the third one is stated to be located within the Ancient Waterworks. After solving the many puzzles of the large ruins, Wario defeats [[Ka-Bloom!]] at the end and obtains the third piece of the Wishstone.

===Special Episode 3===
Wario and [[Count Cannoli]] appear to be continuing their rivalry where they ended up in the Ancient Waterworks once again. [[Carpaccio]] suddenly shows up and tells the two thieves that he needs pieces to his invention and whoever finds them first gets to have it.
