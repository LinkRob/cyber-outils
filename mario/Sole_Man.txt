'''Sole Man''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', as one of the microgames under [[Mona]]'s [[Strange]] folder, and Sole Man later remade in ''[[Game & Wario]]'', in which it is part of [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s stage, [[Gamer]].

==Layout==
In the ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' iteration, the microgame takes place on a beach, where several [[Fronk (species)|Fronk]] appear in the background.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of [[Fronk]], who is followed by a large shadow, followed by a foot falling from above. The player has to move Fronk away from the foot and avoid him from being crushed. The foot drops twice before the microgame ends. If Fronk is not crushed, the player wins the microgame, but if he is, the player loses.

In the ''Game & Wario'' version, a different character is played in each difficulty level, and Fronk is only played in the first difficulty level.

===Difficulty levels===
====''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''====
The size of the foot increases with each difficulty level.

*'''Level 1:''' The shoe is someone's sneaker.
*'''Level 2:''' The shoe is a hiker's boot, which covers a bit more of the screen.
*'''Level 3:''' The shoe is a fat person's sandal, which covers most of the screen.

===''Game & Wario''===
[[File:G&W Stomp.png|thumb|200px|''Game & Wario'' level 3 gameplay]]
*'''Level 1:''' A [[Fronk (species)|Fronk]] must avoid being squished by a person's foot. The setting is a dirt road.
*'''Level 2:''' [[Wario-Man]] must avoid being squished by a dinosaur's foot. The background is a city on fire.
*'''Level 3:''' A person in a [[Goomba]] suit must try to be stomped by [[Mario]]'s foot. The setting is based on ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
[[File:WarioWare-Dodge1-SSBB.png|thumb|200px|left|The microgame in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''.]]
Sole Man is one of the microgames featured in the [[WarioWare, Inc. (stage)|WarioWare, Inc.]] stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. Just as before, the object is to dodge the stomping foot, just like in the original microgame. If a character is stomped, this has the same effect as a [[Pitfall]], with the character becoming partially buried and incapable of moving temporarily. Just as in the original microgame, any character crushed by the foot loses.
