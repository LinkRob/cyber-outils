==Features==
Sprawling Savanna Rabbit Run is an alternate version of [[Sprawling Savanna]], taking place during the daytime and includes only the large field and the cliff.

==[[Green Star]]s==
*'''Green Star 1''':Catch the [[Big Rabbit]]. It can be very tricky to catch as it is extremely fast. There are Dash Panels scattered across the field to use to your advantage.
*'''Green Star 2''': On the right of the cliff, there is a Clear Pipe leading to a cloud platform with the second Green Star floating above it.
*'''Green Star 3''': Can only be acquired with Blue Toad. After making it to the top of the cliff, there is a Blue Toad Switch to the left, guarded by Conkdors. Press the switch and the final Green Star will appear on one of the moving cloud platforms.
