'''The Tough-As-Nails Squad''' is the eighth level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 13.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 7 [[Buzzy Beetle]]s and 1 [[Green Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Green)]]. The enemy captain is a Buzzy Beetle and it knows [[Deny]] and [[Rally]].

The final battle contains 7 [[Elite Troopea]]s and 1 Magikoopa (Green). The enemy captain is a Elite Troopea and it knows Rally and Deny.

After the final battle, the player recruits a Buzzy Beetle.

The player can replay the level to unlock up to 1 Magikoopa (Green), and or Buzzy Beetle.
