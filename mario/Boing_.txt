'''Boing!''' is the fifth level of [[World 2 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 2]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', a [[Purple Yoshi]] takes control in this level. This level takes place in an open canyon filled with [[Bouncy|Bouncies]] which can be used to bounce to higher ledges and also around the place. This level also makes use of several other platforms and gimmicks and marks the debut of [[Debull]]s, [[Eggo-Dil]]s, [[Bandit]]s, and [[Fleeper]]s in this game.

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the right and hit the [[Egg Block]] to fill your egg supply and hit the [[M Block]]s here for some [[Coin]]s and two [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] respectively (if you're not using [[Baby Mario]] at this point, you can easily use the [[Stork Stop]] to switch to him and then hit the [[M Block]]s below). Next, advance to the right and hit the [[Fly Guy]]s carrying [[Coin]]s before hitting the [[Smiley Flower]] in their air with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], next grab the [[Coin]]s in the coin lot above the [[Bouncy]] as two of the coins in this lot are [[Red Coin]]s, after this, move to the right and pound the [[Stake]] by the [[Bouncy]] for a [[Red Coin]], then, bounce to the [[Ferris Wheel]] to reach the upper ledge where another [[Ferris Wheel]] is with some [[Coin]]s, progress onward and hop on the manual [[Ferris Wheel]]s and use one of them to reach the [[Red Coin]] atop it, then, drop down and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] for some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] before continuing, jump by the [[Bouncy]] and collect the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]] by it and bounce on the top of the [[Bouncy|Bouncies]] to reach the upper ledge, switch to [[Baby Peach]] at the [[Stork Stop]] and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] here to activate the wind to reach the upper platform with the [[Pipe]], enter the [[Pipe]] to reach a room with an [[Eggo-Dil]] and three [[Winged Cloud]]s, the [[Winged Cloud]]s contain a [[Smiley Flower]], [[Coin]]s, and [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] respectively (if you're short on eggs, just slurp up some of the Eggo-Dil's petals), after collecting these, drop down the lower paths to collect the [[Coin]]s and a [[Red Coin]], there is also a [[Stake]] here (before pounding the [[Stake]], it's best to wait until all of the [[Eggo-Dil]]s petals are removed or else they will fall with the ground pound) making dodging them difficult). Pound the [[Stake]] to get a [[Red Coin]], after this, exit through the [[Pipe]] and drop back down to the main path and advance to the right (but be sure to switch to [[Baby Mario]] as well before you do).

In the next area, advance to the right and pound the [[Stake]] to get the [[Red Coin]] inside it, drop down and advance to the right to get the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]] along the path but watch out for the [[Debull]]. Advance to the right along the [[M Block]]s to get the [[Baby Coin]] at the end and hop up to the upper platforms and be careful of the [[Eggo-Dil]], advance to the right and watch out for the [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Woozy Guy]]s, there is also a [[Baron Von Zeppelin]] here carrying a [[Key]], jump to it to snag the [[Key]], hit the soft stone to drop the [[Stork Stop]] down to the main ground so you can switch to [[Baby Mario]] and get the [[Baby Coin]] (if you haven't already), once done, progress to the left and watch out for the [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Woozy Guy]]s as you traverse the hill and enter the hut to play the [[Bouncy Maze]] minigame. Once done, advance upward and once you reach the platform with the arrow sign by it, flutter to the left to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]], hit it with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to get some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], progress to the right and watch out for the [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Debull]]s here and also hit the [[Fly Guy]]s in the air to obtain their treasures, there are also [[Fleeper]]s to watch out for so be careful of these guys, there are four [[Stake]]s on this plateau but only the third one contains a [[Red Coin]], watch out for the [[Debull]] at the end and exit the area through the right.

In the next area, tag the [[Middle Ring]] and the [[Tap-Tap]] here should disappear and turn into a [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Star]], ground pound the [[Stake]] to get a [[Red Coin]] and tag the [[Stork Stop]] to switch to [[Baby Peach]], jump across the [[Bouncy|Bouncies]] while avoiding the [[Debull]] below, use the next [[Bouncy]] to spring to the top of the tall [[Stake]], grab the [[Smiley Flower]] on top of it and pound the [[Stake]] all the way down to reveal a [[Winged Cloud]], hit the [[Winged Cloud]] with an egg to reval a [[! Switch]], hitting this switch will temporarily activate a gust of wind which propels you to the upper platform, move to the left while collecting [[Coin]]s but watch out for the [[Bandit]] because it will steal your baby if you give him the chance ([[Bandit]]s can be killed with either three stomps or one ground pound), from here, enter the [[Pipe]].

In this secret area, jump on the [[Flatbed Ferry]], as it moves, use the wind to glide yourself as you grab the [[Coin]]s (none of them are [[Red Coin]]s) and at the tracks end, there is a [[Smiley Flower]] for you to grab (if you fall, you will land on some clouds below, if this is to happen, advance to the right and exit through the [[Pipe]] and make another attempt), once the goodies have been claimed, fall through the [[Flipper]]s and jump down the [[Pipe]] below to return to the main level.

After this, return back to the main path and drop down below the [[Ferris Wheel]] to grab four [[Coin]]s and a [[Red Coin]] while avoiding the [[Bandit]], after this, jump back up to the [[Ferris Wheel]] and to the upper ledge, be careful of the [[Eggo-Dil]] and to get the 1-Up from the [[Fly Guy]] below, either toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] or hop on it then flutter back up to the ledge before you die. Next, advance to the right and use the [[Bouncy|Bouncies]] to reach the upper ledges whilst collecting the [[Coin]]s. In the pen here guarded by two [[Stake]]s on either side, there is a line of [[Coin]]s with three of them being [[Red Coin]]s but watch out for the [[Debull]] and [[Bandit]], as well as the yellow [[Shy Guy]] on the platform, once done, advance to the right to reach the next area.

In the next area, use the [[Egg Block]] to fill your egg supply and tag the [[Stork Stop]] to switch to [[Baby Mario]], (while there is a flurry of wind in the next area making it good for [[Baby Peach]], [[Baby Mario]] is the better choice as most of the [[Donuts and Eggs|next level]] will require him and his abilities). Move to the right and drop down to grab the [[Smiley Flower]] which is inside the [[Winged Cloud]] which can be hit with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], from here, bounce on the [[Bouncy|Bouncies]] to reach the higher ledges and grab the [[Coin]]s, trigger the hidden [[Winged Cloud]] here for some bonus [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and grab the three [[Red Coin]]s after sliding through the moving [[Bouncy|Bouncies]], next, jump on the next [[Bouncy]] to grab the [[Coin]]s and the last two [[Red Coin]]s, then use the next [[Bouncy]] to propel to the [[GOAL! Ring]], jump through it to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
You will need [[Baby Mario]] for this [[Baby Coin]], if you're not using [[Baby Mario]] at this point, jump to the upper platforms and break the soft stone with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to drop the [[Stork Stop]] down to you and switch to [[Baby Mario]], run to the left and drop down to the bridge of [[M Block]]s, run across the bridge to claim your prize.
