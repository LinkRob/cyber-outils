'''Mario Finale''' is [[Mario]]'s [[Final Smash]] in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Effects==
[[File:Mario Finale SSBB.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario using Mario Finale in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
When Mario Finale is used, a roar of flames spirals over a fair distance damaging opponents caught in it. The move itself doesn't have any knockback but if this move is used near the edge of a stage and an opponent is caught in the middle, they will most likely be KO'd. In ''Super Smash Bros. 3DS/Wii U'', the Mario Finale is a tad stronger and covers more distance.

==Trivia==
*The Mario Finale may be based on Mario's [[Ultra Flame]] from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.
