The '''Nose Deck''' is a location in [[Bowser's body]] in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. Like the [[Rump Command]], the Nose Deck is one of the most visited locations in Bowser's body. It is located right above the [[Airway]] and right below the [[Memory Banks]]. It is also located in Bowser's nose.
==History==
Mario and Luigi visit the Nose Deck several times whenever Bowser breathes in pollen and cannot get his sneeze out. Mario and Luigi must then compete in a mini-game where they must attack simluators inside Bowser's nose a certain number of times in order to get him to sneeze. This is also used to get the [[Tower of Yikk]] out of the water which ends up crushing Bowser urging Mario and Luigi to go into the Rump Command once more.
