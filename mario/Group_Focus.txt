'''Group Focus''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Group Focus costs two [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear, and it allows Mario's partners to use focus. This effect does not allow them to use [[Star Spirit]]s' abilities, nor is it affected by [[Deep Focus]].

Group Focus is purchased from Rowf at his [[Rowf's Badge Shop|badge shop]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] for 100 [[coin]]s. This badge, however, does not allow them to use the [[Star Spirits]]' abilities.
