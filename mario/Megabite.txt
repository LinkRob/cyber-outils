}}A '''Megabite''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Megabites are very strange, skull-like enemies that are found in various of locations in the game (even as early as [[Yold Desert]]). Megabites only have an [[Heart Point|HP]] of 4 but they take only 1 point of damage no matter how strong the attack is. Megabites tend to fly around out of reach in areas and swoop down for a surprise attack. Upon defeat, Megabites tend to drop items such as [[Shooting Star (item)|Shooting Star]]s.
