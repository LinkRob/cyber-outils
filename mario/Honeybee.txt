The '''Honeybees''' are a species of [[bee]]s that appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. In both games they are found in [[galaxy|galaxies]] with plentiful honey, and are ruled over by the [[Queen Bee]].

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
=== ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ===
Bees appear in many areas, such as [[Honeyhive Galaxy]], and at the third [[Sea Slide Galaxy]] star. Unlike previous games, Bees are the same size as Mario, and rather friendly allies who serve the [[Queen Bee]]. They also do not roam around on all four feet, like previous games. In [[Honeyhive Galaxy]], they teach the player about the effects of [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]], such as destroying the circular blocks.

=== ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ===
Bees retain their role from ''Super Mario Galaxy''. They also appear in various similar [[galaxy|galaxies]], such as [[Honeybloom Galaxy]]. Once [[Mario]] has cleared a special mission, [[The Secret Wall Jump]], a Bee joins on [[Starship Mario]].
