|type = 2 vs. 2 mini-game}}'''Slot Synch''' is a 2 vs. 2 mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this game, two teams attempt to match up the same two characters on a dice block. Each character has a different point system with [[Toad]] being worth 3 points, [[Koopa Troopa]]s being worth 2 points, [[Goomba]]s being worth 1 point, and [[Koopa Kid]] being worth -1 point. Whichever team has the most points by the end of the game will win.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|a}} - Hits the dice block.
