'''Cheepskipper'''<ref>Loe, Casey. ''New Super Mario Bros.'' Player's Guide. Page 14.</ref> is a unique purple-and-white [[Mega Cheep-Cheep]] with yellow fins and two sharp fangs protruding from its bottom lip. Its sole appearance to date is as the [[List of bosses|boss]] of [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' Unlike with typical [[Cheep Cheep|Cheep-Cheep]]s in the game, its pectoral fins are short and round instead of wing-like, its dorsal fin is wavy and thin, and its caudal fin has two lobes instead of three. Cheepskipper attacks at first by jumping out of the water and bouncing once on the bridge and back into the water. Cheepskipper always bounces out from right to left. When Cheepskipper jumps out, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] have their chance of jumping on it to attack it. Normal Cheep-Cheeps also launch themselves at Mario/Luigi as well. These small fish are swift and attack in large numbers, making them rather hard to dodge. After jumping on it three times, or hitting it with nine fireballs, Cheepskipper is defeated and gives Mario and Luigi the key to access [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]].

==Battle==
Cheepskipper is a formidable foe so play close attention to his attacks. During the battle, he is aided by several smaller [[Cheep-Cheep]]s which hop out of the water to attack (these Cheep-Cheeps can be annoying to avoid but can be defeated with any attack, it's highly recommended to take out the Cheep-Cheeps as they can become a nuisance if left alone). Cheepskipper will either hop straight out at you or he will bounce along the bridge before dipping back into the water below. Whenever the Cheepskipper jumps across the bridge, stomp on him, do this three times to finish him off. Additionally, ground pounding him twice, pelting him with [[fireball]]s or stomping him with the effects of a [[Mega Mushroom]]. If you've entered the fight as [[Shell Mario]], you can also slide back and forth and hit him as he bounces along the bridge, this also makes you impervious to the leaping Cheep-Cheeps making defeating them a whole lot easier, upon defeat Cheepskipper will explode and leave behind the key providing access to [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]].
