'''Dashing''' is a recurring move in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] and related media. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. Dashing is the same thing as when a character runs, although Dashing specifically refers to the move used during gameplay. Aside from moving faster, Dashing allows a character to jump a slightly greater distance than they are otherwise capable of doing or possibly allows them reach the end of a [[level]] more quickly.
