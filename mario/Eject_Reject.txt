'''Eject Reject''' is a downloadable [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It was released on June 24, 2009 in Japan; May 24, 2010 in North America; and June 25, 2010 in Europe. It later returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Wario Deluxe]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
In ''WarioWare: D.I.Y.'', the player is shown a sign with a cake on it, a conveyor belt, and a boxing glove. The player must punch off every cake different from the cake on the sign. The cakes are {{w|kagami mochi}}, and the non-cake items are incomplete kagami mochi, [[poop]], green snakes with brown stripes, and snowmen. There are a few different kinds of cakes, but at least one is always identical to the cake on the sign. If the player punches off every incorrect cake while retaining the correct cakes, they win at the microgame. However, if the opposite is done, the player loses at the microgame.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', [[Wario]] has to remove any objects that are not cakes, which are instead either a white snake or a {{w|Kin no unko}}. In level 1, there are two or three non-cake objects; in level 2 there are four or five non-cake objects; and in level 3, there are six or seven non-cake objects.
