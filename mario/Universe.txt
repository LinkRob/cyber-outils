== Description ==
Outer Space is filled with a vast, possibly infinite space, much of which is still unknown to this day. It is filled with an incalculable number of [[Star|stars]], [[Prankster Comet|comets]], [[Star (species)|celestial beings]] and [[Shroob|powerful creatures]]. Even though, Mario visited [[List of galaxies|inhabited planets]], [[Flipside|other]] [[Concordia's World|dimensions]] during his travels, there are many undisclosed planets they didn't discovered such as the [[Shroob Planet]], it is possible many other undisclosed, yet inhabited planets outside of Mushroom Earth with unknown lifeforms also exist within this universe.

===Present===
In the present times, many conflicts between Bowser and Mario would take place on either the [[Earth|real world]] or the Mushroom Earth with the fate of the universe often resting on the outcomes of those conflicts. Despite this, Mario and his allies would always find a way to win and preserve the safety of their home for another day. As the conflicts on them intensified however, Mario and its various inhabitants would be drawn into said conflicts. 

There are also other present conflicts occurred outside of the Mushroom Kingdom happening at the same time, such as [[Donkey Kong]] confront [[King K. Rool]], [[Ghastly King]] and [[Tiki Tak Tribe|other]] [[Snowmad Tribe|tribes]] on [[Donkey Kong Island|his island]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] having [[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy|his own adventure]] in Yoshi's Island and [[Wario]] [[Wario Land (series)|hunting for gold]] as well as working on [[WarioWare (series)|his own company]].

[[File:Outer_Space.jpg|left|thumb|250x250px|Mario flying in space, from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.]]
During [[The Void]] [[Super Paper Mario|incident]], Mario's universe was faced with another destruction, this time a mysterious man named [[Count Bleck]], who plans to use the [[Chaos Heart]] to destroy all dimensions. Mario alongside [[Tippi]], Luigi, Princess Peach and Bowser travel to other worlds and foiled Count Bleck, however, one of his minions, [[Dimentio]] steals the Chaos Heart at his own will only to be foiled by the heroes, saving all dimensions using the [[Pure Heart|Pure Hearts]].
