'''Lava Dome''' is the fifth floor in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''. It is [[lava]]-themed, and as such, the [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Marios]] have to avoid fiery and lava obstacles.
