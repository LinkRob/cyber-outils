The '''Super-Special Tummy Tonic''' is an object in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Description==
The Super-Special Tummy Tonic was created by [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]. It functions as an antidote for the painful side effects of [[Tonally Transformative Elixir]].

==In ''WarioWare Gold''==
In the opening cutscene of Penny's story, when Penny tested the Tonally Transformative Elixir on Dr. Crygor, he got extreme stomach pain, so she gets out the Super-Special Tummy Tonic and gives it to him. When this occurs, the player plays through Penny's set of microgames.

The Super-Special Tummy Tonic ends using curing Dr. Crygor of his stomach pain, but it did not immediately get rid of his higher-pitched voice. However, Dr. Crygor is later heard talking normally in future scenes, although it is unclear how his voice returned to normal.
