The '''Star Rod''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
The Star Rod is a magic wand that appears as the reward for becoming the Superstar of the [[Star Carnival]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==History==
After the player completes the game's Star Battle mode, [[MC Ballyhoo]] awards the player not only a year's supply of [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] but also the Star Rod itself, which he had been carrying in Big Top all this time. However, [[Bowser]] swoops down and steals the rod, claiming he is the real Superstar. The player then chases Bowser to his [[board]], [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]], where the rod is recovered after the player beats one of Bowser's minions, and used to create energy balls that attack Bowser in [[Superstar Showdown]].
