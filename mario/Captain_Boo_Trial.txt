'''Captain Boo''' is the tenth level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 35.

==Layout==

Is this level, the only ally on your team is [[Captain Boo]].

The 1st battle contains 2 [[Parabeanie|Parabeanies]], 1 [[Piranha Bean]], 2 [[Beanerang Bro|Beanerang Bros]], and 1 [[Elite Troopea]]. The enemy captain is the Elite Troopea.

The 2nd battle contains 2 Parabeanies, 2 Piranha Beans, 3 beanerang Bros, and 1 Elite Troopea. The enemy captain is the Elite Troopea.
