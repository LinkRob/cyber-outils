'''R.O.B. Block Set''' is one of [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Layout==
It features an overhead view of [[R.O.B.]] and the tray with blocks from ''[[w:c:nintendo:Stack-Up|Stack-Up]]''.

==Gameplay==
The gameplay differs in each, but all of them have involve R.O.B. having to grab a block.

In Level 1, the player must have R.O.B. select either a red, blue, green, or yellow block.

In Level 2, there is only one block, and R.O.B. has to move it to the designated spot. 

In Level 3, the gameplay is similar to Level 2, except there is more than one block, and R.O.B. has to pick up a specific one to move to the designated spot.
