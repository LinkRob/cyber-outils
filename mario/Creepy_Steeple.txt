The '''Creepy Steeple''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
As the name suggests, this old mansion is filled with traps and creepy creatures, such as [[Boo]]s and [[Swooper]]s. The fourth [[Crystal Star]] is also here, and can be obtained by defeating the master of the manor, the [[Duplighost]] named, [[Doopliss]]. The only way to get here is to lake the long and winding [[Twilight Trail]], which begins in the nearby village of [[Twilight Town]].

The Creepy Steeple is mainly a big maze,with a well outside the Steeple where, if [[Mario]] jumps in, leads to an underground area. This is also a parrot living underground, and is the only person who knows Doopliss's name.
