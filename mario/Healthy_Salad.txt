'''Healthy Salad''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
[[File:Healthy_Salad.png|frame|left]]
[[Mario]] can use the Healthy Salad to restore fifteen of his [[Flower Point|FP]] and cure him of [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] if he has been inflicted with it.
{{br|left}}
===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Healthy Salad makes a reappearance. Mario can obtain this item by [[Saffron]] who will make this item by cooking a [[Fresh Veggie]]. Unlike its previous appearance, instead restores fifteen [[Heart Point|HP]], although it can still cure poison.
