A '''Pale Piranha''' is an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They are black and white [[Piranha Plant]]s found in [[Boggly Woods]] and [[The Great Tree]].

==History==
[[File:Pale_Piranha screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|The Pale Piranha on the Mind-Bogglers team]]
Pale Piranhas have four [[Heart Point|HP]] and have an attack power of two. [[Mario]] cannot [[jump]] on Pale Piranhas unless he uses the [[Spike Shield]] badge. Unlike more powerful Piranha Plants featured later on, Pale Piranhas do not have any breath attacks, and only attack Mario with their bites.

During Chapter 3, Mario meets a fighting team called the "[[Mind-Bogglers]]" in the [[Glitz Pit]]. This fighting team features a Pale Piranha, a [[Pider]], and a [[Cleft]]. The Pale Piraha also appears to be the leader of the Mind Bogglers.

==Trivia==
*Various languages, such as Japanese, French, German, and Italian, give them the same name as Piranha Plant in their respective language.
