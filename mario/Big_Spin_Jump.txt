|name=Big Spin Jump (Battle Card)}}'''Big Spin Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]].''

==Description==
The Big Spin Jump is the strong drill version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a big red boot with gray trims and a drill underneath it. There is a red/pink checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 20/80 coins

Sell Price: 10/40 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Spin Jump is first seen in [[Fortune Island]], but can also found in [[Toad Trainworks]], [[Sunset Express]], and [[The Emerald Circus]].
