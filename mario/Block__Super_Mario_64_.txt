'''Blocks''', also known as '''boxes''', are a type of object that first appear in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Description==
They are regular-looking [[block]]s, and some of them contain three [[coin]]s. In ''Super Mario 64'', aside from being larger size, blocks have an identical appearance to the throwable [[Crate]]s. [[Mario]] can stand on top of a block as a platform. In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', they have the appearance of an orange brick instead.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 64''===
Blocks are scattered throughout the various courses, where they can be broken by most of [[Mario]]'s attacks. In certain courses, such as [[Dire, Dire Docks]] and [[Wet-Dry World]], Blocks appear only while an [[! Switch]] is active. In these cases, the blocks are always empty, and they are used more as platforms than containers.

===''Super Mario 64 DS''===
For this appearance, they are named '''Bricks''', due to having a similar design to them. As such, Bricks have an entirely difference appearance from the Crates. In rare cases, a Brick contains a [[Super Mushroom|mushroom]]. Like with Mario, [[Luigi]] and [[Wario]] can destroy Bricks using most of their attacks, although [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] cannot break them with any of his attacks unless he has eaten a mushroom. The game introduces [[Black Brick]]s, which can only be destroyed by Wario.

==In ''[[Mario Party]]''==
Boxes appear in the [[minigame]] [[Box Mountain Mayhem]], where the goal is to destroy as many boxes as possible, as many of them contain coins and [[Coin Bag]]s. There are also [[Crazed Crate|Whomp Block]]s disguised as these boxes, which move the characters who attack them away from the mountain of boxes.
