'''Sugar''' is a character who is the proprietor of [[Club Sugar]]. Though it is a recurring location, Sugar himself only appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare: Twisted!''===
Sugar is seen in the ending scene for [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s stage. He informs the Thangs that their dinner is ready. Some time later, Sugar gets irritated at the Thangs that they are not coming for dinner because they are too busy dancing, and causing their dinner to become cold. Some time later, Sugar closes the club for the night and forces the Thang Family to leave.

===''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''===
Despite not appearing, Sugar is listed within the cast credits as '''Sugar the Club Owner'''.
