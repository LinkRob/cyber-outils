'''Electric Groove Fish''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', as one of [[Orbulon]]'s [[microgame]]s, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Mona]]'s microgames.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to guide the electricity from the electric fish to the city.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The path is simple. The path needs a few presses.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The path is moderate. The path needs more presses.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The path is complex. The path needs many presses.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
Instead of the city above it, the city is next to the electric fish. If the game is won, the city will light up and the lights also appear in the sky.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The path needs five presses. If won, the [[Mischievous Moon]] and the stars will appear in the sky.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The path needs eleven presses. If won, the train will appear which the stars are train tracks in the sky.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The path needs fifteenth presses. If won, the dolphin and an island will appear along with the circles within the sky.
