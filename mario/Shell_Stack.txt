'''Shell Stack''' is a single-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to [[jump]] onto [[Koopa Shell|shell]]s as they are shot out of [[Warp Pipe|pipes]] horizontally. As more shells are jumped onto, the pipes begin shooting out the shells at an increasing speed. The color of the pipe and shell determines the shell's speed, with [[Green Shell]]s being the slowest, followed by [[Yellow Shell]]s, then Orange Shells, and lastly [[Red Shell]]s, which are the fastest. If the player's character either misses or gets hit by a shell, the minigame ends.

In the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player's character must stack ten shells within 60 seconds to win. In Mini-Game Attack, the objective is the same, but the time limit is halved to thirty seconds. In both modes, either being hit by a shell or not successfully jumping onto a shell only stuns the player's character for about three seconds.
