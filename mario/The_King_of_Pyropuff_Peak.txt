'''The King of Pyropuff Peak''' is the first boss level in [[Episode 1 (Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker)|Episode 1]] of ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

== Layout ==
This level features the first boss level in ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker'', with [[Draggadon]] as its boss.

==Super Gems==
*'''Super Gem 1''': Go in front of the fourth barrier before getting hit by Draggadon's fire.

*'''Super Gem 2''': After climbing the first ladder, go back the second ladder to reveal a moving wall connected to another ladder. Climb that ladder to the level of the Super Gem.

*'''Super Gem 3''': Before climbing the fourth ladder, pluck the stem closest to it to reveal a Super Gem.

==Optional Mission==
*Collect 45 coins throughout the level to complete the optional mission.
