Treasure Ships replace hammer Bros on maps under a criteria. Your coin counter has to be a multiple of 11. Then the second last digit of your score has to match the coin counter. If your score is 1236940, your coin counter has to be 44. Then you end a level on an even number like 236. The treasure ship will also appear if your coin counter is 88 and your score is 2530. You would have to end a level on an odd number like 251. There are 168 coins on this slow moving ship. There's a hidden life between the two windows right before the pipe. These treasure ships can only appear in worlds 1, 3, 5, and 6. You will fight two boomerang brothers at the end and you will get the item that the hammer bro would've had. (Star, hammer, cloud, P Wing, Music Box)
