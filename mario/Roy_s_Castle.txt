'''Roy's Castle''' is the fifth castle of ''[[Super Mario World]]''. It is located at the end of the [[Forest of Illusion]].

== Layout ==
The player starts in a room where there is lava on the floor, there is a [[Snake Block]] that moves the player past the lava and the spike room. (Mario must follow it to avoid the lava and spikes.) The player needs to be careful though, because [[Zigzag Podoboo]]s and Fireballs can damage the player and even lose a life if they don't have a power-up. Once the player is in the spike room, spikes will fall and the player can fall onto the spikes if they are not careful. Once they are out of the spike room, they will be in a room with lava (but there is a bridge) with [[Podoboo]]s, fireballs and [[Bowser Statue]]s. The player has to avoid the hazards and get to the boss door.

The battle with start with two pillars crashing down so that the player cannot escape from the battle. Then [[Roy Koopa]] will move towards the left wall and climb up it much like in [[Morton Koopa Jr.]]'s battle. When he's on the ceiling, he will fall down when the player gets close. If he is stomped on, the walls will move closer (but will not collide). There are two ways to defeat Roy Koopa. One way is to simply just stomp on him three times. Another way is to throw two fireballs at Roy Koopa and stomp on him once. Both ways will defeat him. [[Mario]] will then have rescued one of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s friends, then they will move onto the next world, [[Chocolate Island]].

== Ending Message ==
After Mario has defeated Roy Koopa and rescued the egg, he jumps on the bomb detonation switch but it didn't explode. Mario walks up to the castle to see what went wrong. But as soon as he did he got caught in the explosion.

''Mario found his way through the Forest of Illusion and has put an end to Roy Koopa of castle #5. Onward to the dangerous (but tasty) Chocolate Island!''
