'''Lightning Cup''' is a recurring cup in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It has appeared in six games: ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

In ''Super Circuit'', Lightning Cup is the third cup in the game, but in ''Mario Kart DS'', ''Mario Kart Wii'', ''Mario Kart 7'' and ''Mario Kart 8'', it consists of retro tracks. It is the counterpart of [[Special Cup]]. In every ''Mario Kart'' installment, the Lightning Cup's emblem is the Lightning Bolt.
