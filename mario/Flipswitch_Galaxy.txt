The '''Flipswitch Galaxy''' is a [[galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It can be accessed from the [[Terrace]] once [[Mario]] has at least seven [[Power Star]]s.

==Description==
Flipswitch Galaxy is the smallest galaxy in ''Super Mario Galaxy'', consisting of only one planet composed of 18 [[Flipswitch Panel]]s that [[Mario]] must step on. Once every Flipswitch Panel has been turned yellow, a [[Power Star]] appears. The background is a scene from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', as shown in the pictures. They periodically switch between variations of [[World 2-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-1]] and [[World 1-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-2]]. The level features a [[Jump Beamer]] that produces circular lasers which can damage Mario. Some Flipswitch Panels are also covered with moving spiked platforms that can hurt Mario.

Before Mario lands on the Starting Planet, he flies past 49 [[Brick Block]]s floating in space, which cannot be reached other than through hacking. ===Starting planet===
[[File:SMG Flipswitch Galaxy Starting Planet.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario in the Flipswitch Galaxy]]
[[File:SMG Flipswitch Galaxy Block Planets.png|thumb|200px|Inaccessible Brick Blocks]]
The Starting Planet is the largest planet in the Flipswitch Galaxy and the only accessible one. It is covered in [[Flipswitch Panel]]s (18 of them exactly) and several hazards such as spiked platforms and electrical barriers. The platforms and electrical barriers move around the planet creating hazards for Mario to dodge as he goes through them.

The only other resident of the planet is a Jump Beamer that releases circular shockwaves as Mario goes through the planet. Mario must flip all the Flipswitch Panels from blue to yellow and then, when they are all flipped, they will turn green. Once the Flipswitch Panels are green the laser-shooting Jump Beamer will turn into a trampoline and release a [[Power Star]] for Mario to get. The electric barriers and spiked platforms will also be deactivated.

==Trivia==
*The music used for this galaxy (as well as many other galaxies) is a cover of the underground theme from ''Super Mario Bros.'' This music returns in many later 3D platformers, such as ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. These versions use slight variations, such as an additional, quieter beat in the formerly-silent areas.
