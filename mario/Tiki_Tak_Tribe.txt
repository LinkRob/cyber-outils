The '''Tiki Tak Tribe''' is an ancient evil hive mind of Tikis lead by [[Tiki Tong]] and is the main antagonistic force in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' and its [[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D|3D]]/[[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD|HD]] [[Reissue|remake]]. 
==Tikis==
Tikis are creatures often made of wood or stone, they often have their faces painted, and most of them have feathers. Many of them are shaped like musical instruments, most of them being [[wikipedia:Membranophone|membraphone]]s. There are some that have firepower and are completely immune to lava, except those that are made of wood.
==History ==
The tribe was inaugurated when Tiki Tong created [[Donkey Kong Island]] and Tikis. The tribe expanded throughout the island where tyranny reigned and enslaved all animals with their hypnotic powers. The first collapse of the Tiki Tak Tribe was the [[Kong]] rebellion, since the Kongs were immune to the hypnotic power of the Tikis.
