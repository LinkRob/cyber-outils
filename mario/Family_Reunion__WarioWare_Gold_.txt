'''Family Reunion''' is one of [[Orbulon]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:WWG Family Reunion Level 3.png|thumb|left|200px|Level 3 gameplay]]
A family of a specific animals are watching television, but one of them is a different species and has to be removed by the player. In doing so, the player wins at the microgame, but if they do not meet this objective, the player loses at the microgame.
{{br|left}}
===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' There is a family of sheep and a wolf, who can easily be distinguished from having a much different appearance than the sheep.
*'''Level 2:''' There is a family of dogs and a cat. It is slightly more difficult to distinguish the cat from the dogs, especially because the dogs can be differently colored.
*'''Level 3:''' There is a family of dalmatians and a cow, who is difficult to distinguish because it has the same color and pattern as the dalmatians.
