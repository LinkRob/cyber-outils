'''Goomba Lagoon''' is a board that appears in [[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]. It is set on a large tropical island, featuring a shipwreck and a large volcano shaped like a [[Goomba]]. The tide changes on this board, causing various aspects of the board to change as it is played, both aesthetically and physically, changing the available paths. Additionally, the volcano in the center of the board may erupt, spewing out many [[Gold Goomba]]s.
