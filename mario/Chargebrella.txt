{{Nihongo|'''Chargebrella'''<ref>{{cite video game| title = [[Super Princess Peach]]| developer = Nintendo SPD, TOSE| publisher = Nintendo| date = February 26, 2006| platform = Nintendo DS}}</ref>|	ためうちカッサー|Tameuchi Kassā|Muster Perry}} is one of Perry's forms in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Description ==
Chargebrella can be purchased for 700 coins in the shop. Perry charges his attack before firing the ball-shaped energy. The ball-shaped energy travels a short distance. Perry can perform this move to flip switches and strike enemies.
