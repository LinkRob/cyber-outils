'''Green Peppers''' are items in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]] which first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. Like their counterpart [[Red Pepper]]s, Green Peppers are rarely found outside of [[Item Shop]]s.

==Appearance==
Green Peppers and Red Peppers resemble one another, and their respective colors are their only difference in appearance.

==Usage==
Green Peppers are mainly used to help raise Mario or Luigi's defense for a few amount of turns. However, they can also lighten Mario or Luigi making them act quicker to avoid attacks when jumping. These peppers can be used with Red Peppers to cancel out the effects and have an attack and defense boost for a short time.
