The '''Barrel Train''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It appears only in the [[board]] [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]].

==Description==
The Barrel Train is a locomotive operated by [[Donkey Kong]]. It appears whenever a player lands on a [[DK Space]], replacing the train's current locomotive, which could be either [[Shy Guy]]'s standard one or [[Bowser]]'s [[Bad-Breath Express]]. In Battle Royale and Tag Battle, the first player who reaches the Barrel Train is given a free [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] from Donkey Kong. In Duel Battle and Star Battle Arena, Donkey Kong doubles the player's [[coin]] total, but he gives 10 coins to players who have none.

The Barrel Train leaves either after someone reaches it, with the normal locomotive replacing it afterward, or if a character lands on a [[Bowser Space]], in which case the Bad-Breath Express replaces the Barrel Train instead.

==Names in other languages==
