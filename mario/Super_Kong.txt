'''Super Kong''' is a Kong-member of the Kong family as he appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''. Super Kong really isn't playable nor is he an enemy but he another of Nintendo Super Guides that helps the player throughout the level, which is similar to the [[Cosmic Guide]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

When Super Kong throws a [[DK Barrel]], he will be joined by [[Super Diddy Kong]] who resembles [[Diddy Kong]] with white fur and a blue shirt and hat.

==Trivia==
*With his white fur, Super Kong slightly resembles the [[Mini Donkey Kong]]s when they’re under the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] however the difference is the Fire Mini Donkey Kongs retain their red ties while Super Kong has a blue tie.
