|game = ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''}}'''Spine Coaster Stowaways''' is the sixth level of [[Rock-Candy Mines]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked when [[Smashing-Stone Tower]] is completed using the Secret Exit, and its completion unlocks [[Sumo Bro's Spinning Tower]].
==Layout==
The level starts in a mountainous area with a [[Bonecoaster]] which [[Luigi]] and company must ride to get to the end of the stage. [[Poisoned Water]] is found at the bottom of the area throughout the level. The Bonecoaster begins with a slope, leading to a section where it dips into the poison. A [[Big Piranha Plant]] is found, followed by [[Brick Block]]s and a [[P Switch]] on a track. The P Switch causes [[Blue Coin]]s to appear in the following section, where more slopes and Big Piranha Plants are found. [[Piranha Plant]]s on Brick Blocks are found, two of which land on the Bonecoaster. A Big Piranha Plant is found, along with some Brick Blocks on tracks that shoot [[coin]]s into the air. [[Venus Fire Trap]]s are found, followed by another section above the poison. After the Bonecoaster track ends, a quick-moving [[lift]] is found, along with a Big Piranha Plant and the [[Goal Pole]].

The player also can use the [[P-Acorn]] to beat the level without ever riding the Bonecoaster.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': This star coin will appear above your coaster early on and will follow you for a while so pick it up at your convenience. 
*'''Star Coin 2''': Hit the P switch and wait for the coaster to move up again and there will be a coin at the next apex.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Near the end of the level the coaster will dip into the purple and you'll be jumping over it and a star coin will appear during one of your jumps.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Near the first Star Coin, an 8-bit Luigi is on the ceiling of the course.
