'''Luis Guzmán''' (born August 28, 1956) is a Puerto Rican actor. His career spans over 40 years and includes a number of films and television series. He has appeared in Paul Thomas Anderson's films ''Boogie Nights'' (1997), ''Magnolia'' (1999) and ''Punch-Drunk Love'' (2002), and Steven Soderbergh's films ''Out of Sight'' (1998), ''The Limey'' (1999) and ''Traffic'' (2000).

He was Gomez Addams in ''Wednesday'' as well.

He plays [[Wart (Illumination)|Wart]] in [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'']].
==Connections==
*He co starred with Toad actor [[Keegan-Michael Key]] in the movie ''Keanu'', and with Fox McCloud actor [[Glen Powell]] in the movie ''Fast Food Nation''.
*He guest starred in an episode of ''Community'', starring [[Donald Glover]], which also mutually guest starred [[Jack Black]] and [[Brie Larson]].
