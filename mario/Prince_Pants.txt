'''Prince Pants''' is an armor which is only usable by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is the strongest armor usable by Mallow. The Prince Pants can be obtained from [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] by buying it for 100 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Prince Pants increases Mallow's [[Defense]] by 48 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 24.
