'''Bumblebees''', alternatively named '''bees''', are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bees have fat, cylindrical-like bodies and are slightly orange. They roam around on all four feet.

==In ''Yoshi's Story''==
[[File:Bumblebee Yoshi's Story sprite.png|frame|left]]
Bumblebees are encountered in [[Cloud Cruising]], where they can be found in two areas. The first area features an unlimited number of Bumblebees as the gimmick of the red [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] next to the second [[Miss Warp]], where the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] must dodge them while [[sniff]]ing for [[coin]]s. In the second area, as the Yoshi rides the green [[dragon]], he encounters bumblebees along the way. A Bumblebee can be defeated by either being [[jump]]ed onto or eaten.
