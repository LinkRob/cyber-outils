'''Bombs''' are enemies from the ''[[Final Fantasy]]'' franchise. They appear in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bombs have often varied in appearance throughout the ''Final Fantasy'' games, but in ''Mario Hoops 3-on3'', a Bomb appears as a large, orange flame with an angry expression, yellow eyes with black sclera, and a pitch black opened mouth. In [[Rainbow Road (Dribble Race course)|Rainbow Road]], Bombs are featured in various colors. 

===Behavior===
Bombs jump out of the [[lava]] along with several [[Podoboo]]s. A Bomb floats over the court, and the player can make it explode by touching it with their [[Stylus]].

==In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Bombs are encountered in the [[Bowser's Castle (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Bowser's Castle]] and [[Rainbow Road (Dribble Race course)|Rainbow Road]] courts. If a Bomb explodes, any character caught within range of the explosion gets burned and starts running around, as if they had touched a Lava Bubble. If a character touches a Bomb, they get knocked back and lose the ball.
