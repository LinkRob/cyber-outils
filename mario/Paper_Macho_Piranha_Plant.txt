'''Paper Macho Piranha Plants''' are a variant of both [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[Paper Macho Soldier]]s featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. They are three-dimensional, made out of papier-mâché, and are only encountered in the [[Spring of Jungle Mist]]. Even upon being defeated, another Paper Macho Piranha Plant does not appear in its place.
