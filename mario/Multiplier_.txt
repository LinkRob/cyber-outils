'''Multiplier!''' is a special move used only by [[Machine Made]] [[Yaridovich]]es in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
With Multiplier!, the Machine Made Yaridovich is able to split itself down into either four [[Jabit]]s or four [[Drill Bit]]s.

==Trivia==
*This move is similar to [[Mirage attack!]] used by the real [[Yaridovich]].
**Even both of this moves end with an exclamation point.
