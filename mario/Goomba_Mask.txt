A '''Goomba Mask''' is an item in the {{series|Super Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''

== Description ==
Goomba Masks sometimes appear after the player has defeated a [[Goomba]]. They can be worn on the player's head allowing them to disguise themselves as a Goomba. This also makes the player hardly noticeable.

== Trivia ==
* Goomba Masks should logically lose their function if their wearer attacks an enemy, but this does not occur.
* There is a similar transformation that [[Mario (character)|Mario]] can encounter in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' whenever he [[Wonder Flower (item)|triggers]] certain [[Wonder Effect]]s; as a result of one effect, Mario could be [[Goomba Mario|transformed into a Goomba]].
* A paper-machê variation appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. When [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] wears it, he pretends to stumble drunkenly. [[Olivia]] then laughs at his antics.
