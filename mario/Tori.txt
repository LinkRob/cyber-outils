'''Tori''' is an unlockable tennis player in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. Tori is of the technical type, and she can be unlocked by winning against her in a singles match. Tori has [[Elroy]] as her tennis partner.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Tori wears a light green sports bra, a black miniskirt with a light green stripe on the left and right each, black tennis shoes with light green shoelaces, and a light green wristband on her right arm. Tori has short hair, colored purple in her artwork but hot pink in the game.
