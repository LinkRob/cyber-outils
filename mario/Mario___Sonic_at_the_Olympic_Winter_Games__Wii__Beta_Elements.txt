This article pertains the beta elements of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''{{'}}s [[Wii]] version.

== Alpine Skiing Downhill Test Levels ==
There are two Test Levels for Alpine Skiing Downhill found within the game's files, which were used simply for testing purposes as the name suggests.

=== AlpineTest01 ===
A simple slope that was possibly use to test the controls for the event. There is no audience, gates, or an ending for the level. A model of a helicopter is also visible, which might be unused in the final game.

=== AlpineTest02 ===
This test map doesn't seem to load correctly as there are four pathways disconnected away from one another. The area where the player spawns in has four ramps, along with some clock-like graphics to their right.
