'''Maizō''' are [[crab]]-like enemies in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Maizō is a six-legged crab. In its artwork, a Maizō is shown to have a red body.

===Behavior===
A Maizō always carries a [[10-Coin|10 Gold Coin]]. At first, a Maizō remains hidden in the sand until [[Wario]] performs a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] nearby, and in doing so, a Maizō jumps out of the sand to reveal itself. After doing so, a Maizō becomes briefly stationary before rushing to try and escape Wario. Even if a Maizō flees offscreen, it eventually respawns in the sand.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
Maizō are encountered in the [[Rice Beach]] [[level]] [[Course No.06]]. Wario can obtain a Maizō's 10 Gold Coin by using a [[Dash Attack|Body Slam]] onto it.
