Lethal Oozer is a kind of ghost in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', it is a form of [[Oozer]]. Like [[Lethal Goob]]<nowiki/>s, [[Lethal Hammer]]<nowiki/>s and [[Lethal Slinker]]<nowiki/>s, Lethal Oozers are black ghosts glowing red. They have 200 HP.

== Appearance ==
Lethal Oozers have black bodies and red eyes, their bodies are glowing red. The way to defeat them is same as the way to defeat regular Oozers.

== In ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' ==
Lethal Oozers can knock out [[Luigi]] in one hit if Luigi gets knocked by the objects they throw, The way to defeat them is to flash them and vacuum them up.

== Trivia ==

* Lethal Oozers were added in version 1. 4. 0.
