A '''Digga''' is a drill-like enemy that debuts in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. Diggas earned their name from a pun on "digger". [[Mario]] first encounters them in the [[Spin-Dig Galaxy]]. He can destroy them by [[Jump|jumping]] on them, or with the spin-drill. [[Digga-Leg]] also summons Diggas when he is fought. When their stamina is drained as a result of this, Diggas will disappear and leave behind a coin. 

==Appearance==
Digga slightly resembles a drill. He has two angry eyes and a drill on his "nose". He constantly has an angry expression. He is completely limbless, but can still move by burrowing through the ground.
