'''Butterfingers''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', as part of [[Mona]]'s [[Strange]] folder, and returns as one of her microgames in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Layout==
The original iteration of the microgame features only a black background and a surrounding brown border. In ''WarioWare Gold'', the black background was replaced by an acid green display with windows.

== Gameplay ==
The player controls the hand, which has to catch the toast that pops out of the toaster. If done successfully, they win, but if not, they lose.

* '''1st level difficulty:''' There is no bite on the toast.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' There is a small bite on the toast.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' There is a big bite on the toast.
