A '''Diamond''' is a very valuable type of gemstone from the real world. They have made some appearances in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, most notably within the ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' franchise. Diamonds first appear in ''[[Mario is Missing!]]''.

==In ''Wario'' franchise==
===''[[Wario's Woods]]''===
A Diamond of a certain color appears whenever [[Toad (character)|Toad]] takes out four enemies with a [[bomb]]. If Toad clears the Diamond, every enemy with the same color is also eliminated.

===''[[Wario Land 4]]''===
[[File:Diamond WL4 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Diamonds are light blue gemstones worth 1,000 [[coin]]s. Because of this, Diamonds are often placed in out-of-reach or hidden areas.

{{br|left}}
==In ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''==
Various types of diamonds—[[Blue Diamond]]s, [[Gold Diamond]]s, and [[Red Diamond (Luigi's Mansion)|Red Diamonds]]—are among the collectible items that contribute to rank of [[Luigi's NEW Mansion]] during the ending.
