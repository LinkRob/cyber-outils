The '''Fourth-Degree Card''' is an optional key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
The Fourth-Degree Card can be obtained at [[The Dojo]] after defeating [[The Master]] a second time. It simply serves as proof that Mario defeated The Master, and does not affect gameplay in any way.
