The '''Tiny Tug''' is an unlockable [[kart]] part in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:Mk7_-_Metal_Mario_Battling.png|thumb|180px|right|[[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]] using the Tiny Tug, equipped with [[Monster (tires)|Monster]] tires in [[Balloon Battle]] on [[Battle Course 1|<small>GBA</small> Battle Course 1]].]]
It resembles a tug boat, yet is much smaller. The Tiny Tug gives a boost in [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]] and [[Off-Road|off-road]], whilst lowering [[Speed]]. In terms of hidden stats, the Tiny Tug offers a boost in sea speed and sea handling, whilst lowering air speed, [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] and [[drift]].

== In ''Mario Kart 7'' ==
The Tiny Tug can be unlocked by collecting [[coin]]s.

The kart is [[Princess Daisy]]'s signature kart. As a computer-controlled racer, she will use this kart body, equipped with [[Slim (tires)|Slim]] tires and the [[Paraglider]]. 

In [[Time Trial]], [[Princess Peach]] uses this kart body for the Expert [[Staff Ghost]] for [[Cheep Cheep Lagoon]], using the [[Roller]] tires and the Peach Parasol. Princess Daisy also uses the Tiny Tug for the Expert Staff Ghost on [[Daisy Cruiser|<small>GCN</small> Daisy Cruiser]], equipped with the [[Sponge]] tires and the [[Peach Parasol (glider)|Peach Parasol]].
