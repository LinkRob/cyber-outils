The '''Waluigi Calendar''' is one of [[Waluigi]]'s birthday presents in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Description==
The Waluigi Calendar is a purple calendar themed after Waluigi. The upper half of the calendar depicts a portrait of Waluigi, while the lower half displays a calendar page of January 2002. It is implied from the in-game description that every page on the calendar is a Sunday, even though the texture still shows all seven days of the week.

==History==
Waluigi can obtain the present from [[Toad]] by completing [[Toad's Midway Madness]] in Story Mode and defeating him in the minigame [[Bowser Bop]].

==Flavor text==
''This is Waluigi's calendar. Every day is Sunday?!''
