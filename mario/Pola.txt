'''Pola''' is a [[snow spirits|snow spirit]] in the [[Adventure Tours]] mode in the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
Pola is the third snow spirit encountered by [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]]. They first find Pola being guarded by [[Dry Bowser]] at the end of [[Sparkleton]] 2. After defeating Dry Bowser, they free Pola from its floating prison, and it joins the group as [[Sparky (character)|Sparky]] leaves. Furthermore, by freeing Pola, the player gets the Pola Emblem.
