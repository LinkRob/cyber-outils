'''Chargin' Chuck Blockade''', or '''World 1-A''', is the [[Enemy Battles]] course of [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its port ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

== Features ==
The only feature of this level are the two [[Chargin' Chuck]]s, who have to be defeated within the 200-second [[time limit]].

== Green Star ==
The first and only [[Green Power Star]] appears upon defeating both Chargin' Chucks within the time limit.
