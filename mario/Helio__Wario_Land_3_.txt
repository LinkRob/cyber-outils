'''Helio''' is a boss featured in both ''[[Wario Land 3]]'' and in ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]'', the former in which it is named '''Yellow Belly'''.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
[[File:Helio_WL3.png|frame|left]]
Helio is encountered by [[Wario]] in the [[Desert Ruins]]. It has arrowheads for arms, and if Wario touches them, he turns into [[Puffy Wario]]. To attack Helio, Wario must [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] on its head to reveal an accordion-shaped device from its head and then Ground Pound that to attack Helio. When Wario has done this three times, Helio is defeated.

==In ''Dr. Mario 64''==
Helio is fought as a boss during Dr. Mario's story, and he is among the playable characters in the Vs. modes.
