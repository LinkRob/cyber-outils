'''Heatfreeze Island''' is a major location from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is the second major island to be found in the [[Brrrning Sea]], and the first to play off the temperature theme of the sea. Heatfreeze Island is also the tenth major drifting island to be connected to [[Shipshape Island]], and one of two to be linked more than once, the other being [[Twistee Island]]. Its icon has two houses that look like persons, with the one on the left being white with a chimney that looks like a pipe on top and the other being maroon with a volcano-esque chimney.

== History ==
When [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] arrive here, they find that the path to the lighthouse has been blocked by arguing inhabitants from the Freezin and Boilin villages, who are in a dispute over which recipe is better. Mario and Luigi taste both recipes, declaring their verdict: both taste good when eaten together. The arguing villagers, dissatisfied with the verdict, head back to their respective villages to make something better, giving Mario and Luigi access to the Lighthouse. Relinking the Lighthouse gives them access to [[Skorcheen Island]] and [[Slippenglide Island]], which comes in clutch when Ten of the [[Extension Corps]] injects [[Glohm]] into [[Prince Chilliam]], since [[Doctor Vulko]] makes his home on Skorcheen Island and can find a cure. After the Bros. link Skorcheen and Slippenglide to Shipshape Island, [[Prince Chilliam]] and [[Princess Burnadette]] host their wedding on Heatfreeze island, after which [[Connie]] has a flashback to when she got mittens from her mentor [[Cozette]].

Upon entry into the [[Gulchrock Sea]], [[Bowser]] attacks [[Shipshape Island]] when the vessel catches his attention, and his surprise attack disconnects Heatfreeze Island, after which some [[Koopa Troop]] soldiers invade it. After the Bros. connect [[Offandon Island]], they head to Heatfreeze Island to stop Bowser's minions from taking it over. Mario and Luigi are taught the [[Fire & Ice]] Bros. Move as they go to the Lighthouse to reconnect Heatfreeze Island. When the Bros. reconnect the island, Bowser's minions leave. The state of the island is determined by whether the Bros. reconnected [[Twistee Island]] (which also got severed from Shipshape during Bowser's surprise attack) beforehand or not; if the Bros. did not, Chilliam and Burnadette defend their respective peoples. If the Bros. did reconnect Twistee, the villages will be overrun with Koopa Troop goons. Reconnecting this island beforehand also determines whether the [[Mettozokettohei|Mettozokettoheitachi]] that invade Twistee manage to infect the villagers of Twistee with Glohm or not; if not, they become minibosses there.

== Layout ==

=== Starting Area ===
The island's starting point is at the south, opposite to the mountain.

=== Plaza ===
Just to the north of the starting point and between the Boilin and Freezin Villages is the plaza. [[Prince Chilliam]] and [[Princess Burnadette]] host their wedding here.

=== Boilin Village ===
To the east of the plaza, southeast of the mountain, lies a village surrounded by lava. When the [[Koopa Troop]] takes over, Princess Burnadette defends this village.

=== Freezin Village ===
To the west of the plaza, southwest of the mountain, lies a village in perpetual winter. When the Koopa Troop takes over, Prince Chilliam defends this village.

=== Mountain ===
Getting to the Heatfreeze Lighthouse requires scaling a mountain. The first section is icy and contains a spiked variety of the [[Icead]] enemy from [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team|''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'']]. A few dormant [[Curlup|Curlups]] are here.

==== Cave ====
Inside the mountain is a cavern flooded with lava. Mario and Luigi must drain the lava, then roll a large boulder down the slope to clear the path to get to the other end.

=== Path to the Lighthouse ===
Upon exiting the cave, Mario and Luigi must use the [[Spin Jump (Mario & Luigi series)|UFO Spin]] to cross a lake of [[Lava]] and reach Heatfreeze Island's lighthouse.

=== Lighthouse ===
Upon reaching the [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Lighthouse]], Mario and Luigi can pull the plug and guide it to [[Shipshape Island]]'s [[Uni-Tree]], leading to Heatfreeze Island's reconnection.

== Nomenclature ==
In the Japanese version of the game, Heatfreeze Island's name is ''Kandān'' (written カンダーン), from ''Kan'' ("cold"; written 寒) and an elongation of ''dan'' ("heat"; written 暖). In Chinese, it is called "Cold and Heat" (Hán'nuǎn; written 寒暖). Its Spanish name, ''Glava'', comes from an amalgamation of ''glaciar'' ("glacier") and English "lava." The Island's Italian name, ''Gelavica'', is derived from ''gélido'' ("gelid," "freezing," "frosty") and ''lávica'' ("volcanic," "lavic"). The French dub calls it ''Glagmatoll'', a blend of ''glace'' ("ice") and the English words "magma" and "atoll." In Dutch, its name is ''Warmkoude'', a blend of English "warm" and the Dutch word ''koude'' ("cold"). It has its name transliterated in Korean, yielding ''Hi-teu-peuri-jeu'', written 히트프리즈.
