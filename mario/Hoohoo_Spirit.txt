{{Infobox Item|name = Hoohoo Spirit|caption = Sprite of the Hoohoo Spirit in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.|image = [[File:MLSS Hoohoo Spirit.png]]|first = ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' (2003)|games = ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''<br />''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''|type = Key item}}'''Hoohoo Spirits''' are spirits in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and reappear in the [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]].

== History ==
Hoohoo Spirits appear in [[Hoohooros]]' first challenge which Mario and Luigi must collect ten Hoohoo Spirits in thirty seconds. They player loses when they did not collect all of it before time runs out, or they fall off the ledge. After the first challenge, they must fight against Hoohooros.

In the remake, the challenge extends to thirty-five seconds.{{M&LSSNav}}
