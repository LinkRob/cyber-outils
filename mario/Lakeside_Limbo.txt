'''Lakeside Limbo''', (called 湖のほとり ''Mizūmi no Hotori'' in Japan), is the very first level in [[Lake Orangatanga]] and ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. The level [[Doorstop Dash]] come after it.

==Overview==
This level has the events taking place at the lake. At the lake, a lot of boardwalks are seen structured inside of the water. As this level is the first in the game, it designed to be the easiest. It has five [[Sneek]]s, six [[Kobble]]s and a single [[Booty Bird]] as the enemies. Also, [[Koin]] is in the level holding the [[DK Coin]]. Near the ending of the level, [[Ellie the Elephant|Ellie]] can be found.
