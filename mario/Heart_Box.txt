'''Heart Boxes''' are recurring items in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

Found in various locations, these revitalizing boxes restore [[Mario]]'s [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]] levels. In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', these items cost [[coin]]s to use. In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', Heart Boxes return to being free with every usage. They are usually found with [[Save Block]]s but some Heart Boxes are found by themselves either before a boss or strong enemy fight.
