'''Big Battlefield''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U ]]'' and reappears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Stage design==
Big Battlefield, as it's name states, is basically [[Battlefield]] but much larger in size. This was because of the new 8-Player Smash feature allowing up to 8 players to fight on this stage. Other than that, this stage has all other features of Battlefield.
