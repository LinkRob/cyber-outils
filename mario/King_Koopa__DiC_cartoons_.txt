'''King Bowser Koopa''' is the main antagonist of the three Mario animated series: ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show]]'', ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', and ''[[Super Mario World (TV series)|Super Mario World]]''. He is the tyrannical ruler of Dark Land, leader of the Koopa Pack, father of the [[Koopalings|Koopa Kids]] and arch-nemesis of [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], Toad and Yoshi. who rules over a vast army.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
King Koopa looks quite different from the [[Bowser|original version of Bowser]], King Koopa is a green alligator-like Koopa, he has a spiked turtle shell, a pair of horns and a small golden crown on top of his head, his belly is a deeper yellow and the whites of his eyes were also yellow, He also received a single pair of spiked bands on his wrists, with the spikes on said bands being colored yellow.
=== Personality traits ===
{{sectstub}}

== Biography ==
According to "[[The Adventures of Sherlock Mario]]", Koopa is a cross between a lizard and an inferior species of toad ([[Toad (species)|no relation]]). In his youth, he got the lowest grades in school, to the point that he holds the world's record for flunking kindergarten the most times, and likely as a result, was shunned by his peers.

== Trivia ==
* This incarnation of King Koopa combines the physical and personality traits of both [[Bowser]] and [[Wart]]. Having both the physical appearance and goal of kidnapping Princess Toadstool of the former and the color scheme and crown of the latter. Not to mention having the [[8-bits]] among his minions, who served Wart in [[Super Mario Bros. 2]].
