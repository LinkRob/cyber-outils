The '''Telmet''' is a device created and used by [[Wario]] in the game ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
The Telmet was made after Wario developed an idea after watching an episode of ''[[The Silver Zephyr]]'' and wanted to be part of the heist to claim all of the treasure for himself. Wario then heads into his room to create the Telmet which he then uses to successfully enter his television.

As stated by Wario himself, the Telmet's main function is to teleport himself in and out of his television. Wario activates it by using a remote and pointing it at the screen before clicking a button. After doing so, Wario is then transported into his television. Wario always wears the Telmet whenever he is seen napping on his chair during the interludes between other episodes.
