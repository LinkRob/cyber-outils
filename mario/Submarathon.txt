'''Submarathon''' is a 2-vs.-2 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Layout==
The background basically shows a peaceful sea world, with [[fish]] and [[Blooper]] creatures swimming around in clean water. It is also full of other kind of life, such as corals and algae.

==Opening==
The minigame starts with a [[clam]] onscreen. It then spits out the red team members in their [[Cheep Cheep]] submarine. The screen then splits, showing both teams.

==Gameplay==
The minigame consists of two teams of two players, who must race to the finish by riding Cheep Cheep-shaped submarines, one red and the other blue. The interior of the submarine is also visible, as the center of the submarine is really a big window which protects the characters from the water but offers a great visibility The upper half of the screen shows the red team, while the lower half shows the blue team, and the divider contains a small straight line that shows how far each team is from the goal. If a player's hands get tired from repeatedly pressing the buttons, they can swap out with their teammate and take a rest. The players must reach the goal by taking turns pumping the lever to accelerate their submarine, though it is faster to reach the goal if the player that starts on the lever continues the whole way through. The first team across the goal wins. If neither team reaches the goal within five minutes, the minigame ends in a draw.

==Controls==
===Player controlling lever===
*{{button|gcn|L}} '''+''' {{button|gcn|R}} (Simultaneously) &ndash; Accelerate
*{{button|gcn|Y}} &ndash; Switch places

===Resting player===
*{{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Blink eyes/Move lips

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Race your submarine to the finish line. One character uses the lever, while the other one rests. Whoever finishes first wins."''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"Switching is the key! When one character gets tired, switch places with the other character."''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The minigame's name is a portmanteau of the words "submarine" and "marathon."

==Trivia==
*If [[Waluigi]] wins, he holds his arms in a different position than he does in other minigames. The pose he holds his arms in is identical to the pose of his secondary winning animation.
*It is the only minigame in ''Mario Party 5'' in which the song "In Calm Water" plays; said song is a cover version of "[[Underwater BGM (Super Mario Bros.)|Underwater BGM]]" from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''
