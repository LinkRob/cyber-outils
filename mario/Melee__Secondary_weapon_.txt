'''Melee''' is a type of [[List of weapons in Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|primary weapon]] used by [[Mario]] and [[Rabbid Mario]].

== Description ==
All Blasters have a strong resemblance to a [[hammer]]. It targets foes within 8 cells of the user. Around the user, it creates a shockwave. The damage is dealt depending on how close to target is to the user (ex. the closer the target is to the user, the more damage it receives).
