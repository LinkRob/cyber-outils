'''Giga Bowser''' is the [[Final Smash]] of [[Bowser]] that first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. It made a reappearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]''.

==Effects==
When Giga Bowser is used, Bowser turns into [[Giga Bowser]], and cam use a variety of elemental attacks. Giga Bowser won't flinch from attacks but can still take damage. Giga Bowser is especially useful for small stages where he can get a lot of hits in. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', Bowser takes a little longer to transform into Giga Bowser, and the transformation itself lasts for a shorter period of time.

==Trivia==
*Giga Bowser is based on the boss character from ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''.
*Giga Bowser is one of three Final Smashes of the Mario series to be a transformation. The other two are [[Wario-Man (Final Smash)|Wario-Man]] and [[Super Dragon]]. However, [[Bowser Jr.]] joins in with the ability to become [[Shadow Mario]] in the latest installment.
*When used in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U'', Bowser retains his original moves from ''Super Smash Bros. Melee''.
