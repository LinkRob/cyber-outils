'''Pogo Guys''' are enemies in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Pogo Guys are apes that wield a hammer and wear a helmet.

=== Behavior ===
A Pogo Guy attacks by swinging its hammer at [[Wario]], turning him into [[Bouncy Wario]] temporarily; this role would later be used by [[Hammer-bot]]s in ''[[Wario Land 3]]'' instead. Pogo Guys cannot be defeated.
