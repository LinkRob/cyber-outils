{{Nihongo|'''Space Food'''|うちゅうしょく|Uchū Shoku|Space Food}} is a type of food in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
[[File:SpaceFoodTTYD.png|thumb|left]]
Space Food has the same properties as a [[Mushroom]] (heals five [[Heart Point|HP]]) except it also makes the user [[Allergic]], meaning they are immune to status effects, including [[Power Lift]]. Space Food is made by [[Zess T.]] by mixing a [[Dried Bouquet]] with just about any other healing item; anything else will result in a [[Mistake]]. Oddly enough, cooking the Mistake with another [[Dried Bouquet]] will make Space Food. The most efficient way of cooking Space Food is by mixing a Dried Bouquet with a [[Dried Shroom]], as these only cost two [[Coin]]s and are very close to Zess T.'s house.

If Mario sleeps at the [[Inn]] in [[Fahr Outpost]], a Space Food will be on the table when he wakes up. The Inn costs fifteen coins for the night, and the Space Food sells in the neighboring shop for ten coins, so in effect, the player can stay at the Inn for five coins. On the Moon's surface, Space Food heals 50 HP, but in the [[X-Naut Fortress]], it heals 20 HP.

==In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
Space Food restores ten HP normally, but restores fifty HP in [[Outer Space]], including the [[Whoa Zone]].
