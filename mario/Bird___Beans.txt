'''''Birds & Beans''''' is a [[DSiWare]] game released for the [[Nintendo DSi]] as a launch title. It is a two-for-one [[reissue|remake]] of two ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' minigames, [[Pyoro (minigame)|Pyoro]] and [[Pyoro 2]]. Similarly, the DSiWare title ''[[Paper Airplane Chase]]'' is a remake of another ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' minigame, [[Paper Plane (minigame)|Paper Plane]], also originally from said game.

In ''Bird & Beans'', the player is given the option to play either ''Pyoro'' or ''Pyoro 2''. Both star [[Pyoro]], who has to eat and shoot out vegetables to earn [[point]]s while avoiding taking damage from them himself.
