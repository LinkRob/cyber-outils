The '''Phony Ball''' is a special move used by [[Wario]] in the [[Mario Baseball (series)|''Mario Baseball'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' as Wario's [[Star Skill]], and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'' as his [[Star Pitch]], with [[Phony Swing]] now being his [[Star Swing]].

==Description==
===''Mario Superstar Baseball''===
If Wario uses the move while pitching, he throws both a regular baseball and a clove of [[garlic]], confusing the batter as to which one to hit. If Wario uses the move while batting, he hits both a clove of garlic and a baseball, likewise confusing the fielders as to which ball they should catch. The ball is hit as a high fly ball, and splits into the real ball and the garlic shortly before hitting the ground. Attempting to catch the garlic temporarily stuns the fielder.

[[Waluigi]]'s Star Skill, the [[Liar Ball]], is similar, except Waluigi throws an eggplant instead of garlic. Compared to the Phony Ball, the Liar Ball travels a shorter distance if Waluigi uses it while batting.

In Challenge mode, the [[Gnarly Garlic]] power-up must be bought first before Wario can start using Phony Ball.

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
The Phony Ball operates a bit differently. Instead of throwing a garlic with the real baseball, Wario now throws two baseballs, one of which is a fake. The balls move at relatively slow speeds, and if the batter swings at the fake ball, this gets them a strike.
