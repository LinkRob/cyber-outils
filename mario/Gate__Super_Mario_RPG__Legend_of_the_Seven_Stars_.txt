The '''Gate''' is the gloomy dimension that is home to [[Smithy]] and various other enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It serves as an entryway between [[Smithy Factory]] and [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] through [[Exor]]. The Gate consists of filled dark smog and half-broken machines floating in a void, and it is patrolled by various [[Smithy Gang]] members and several ghosts.

==Trivia==
*Although the Factory and the Gate are separate locations, the [[Save Block]]s identify the Gate as being the Factory itself.
