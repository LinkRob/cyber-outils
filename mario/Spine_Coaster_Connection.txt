'''Spine Coaster Connections''', is the first level of [[Superstar Road]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked when all of the [[Star Coin]]s in [[Acorn Plains]] are collected. [[The Final Battle (New Super Luigi U)|The Final Battle]] in [[Peach's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Peach's Castle]] must also be completed to unlock Spine Coaster Connections. 
==Layout==
The level begins on a platform with a [[? Block]], leading up to a [[Bonecoaster]]. The Bonecoaster travels down a section of track with a row of [[coin]]s, followed by a [[Mega Fuzzy]] and groups of [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]]. More Bonecoasters follow, along with another Mega Fuzzy. A section of three Bonecoasters then follows, with Mega Fuzzies blocking the path throughout the area. The section leads to a single Bonecoaster, which takes the player to the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Before jumping off the second Bonecoaster, the Star Coin appears right above a Fuzzy.
*'''Star Coin 2''': In the set of three Bonecoasters, the Star Coin appears above the middle one.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Shortly after the second Star Coin, this Star Coin will appear above the bottom Bonecoaster.
==[[List of Luigi sightings in New Super Luigi U|Luigi Sightings]]==
*Shortly after the first Fuzzy, an 8-bit Luigi will appear in the grassy land above.
