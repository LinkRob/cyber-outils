'''Ludwig's Thump Castle Hotel''', labeled '''Ludwig's Castle Hotel''' on its entrance, is a location in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''.

==Description==
Ludwig's Thump Castle Hotel is a horror-themed [[castle]] owned by [[Ludwig von Koopa]]. It features [[door]]s capable of vanishing.

==In ''Hotel Mario''==
Ludwig's Thump Castle Hotel is the fifth [[Koopa hotel|hotel]]. It features [[Dry Bones|Skeleton Koopa]]s, [[Boo|Boo Buddies]], [[Big Boo]]s and [[Pat the Bat]]s. The doors stop vanishing once [[Mario]] obtains [[Bowser's Ghost Cologne]].

There are nine regular stages and a boss stage titled [[Ludwig's Frantic Lab]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Ludwig's Thump Castle Hotel is likely named after {{w|Golden Nugget Atlantic City|Trump's Castle}}.
