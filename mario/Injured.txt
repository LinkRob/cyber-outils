==History==
Injured is a rather troublesome status effect. This condition only happens when one of [[Mario]]'s partners is attacked directly by an enemy. When they are attacked for a certain amount of damage, then that partner is knocked out for that number of turns. For example, if [[Goombario]] is attacked by an enemy for 2 points of damage, it will result in him being out for 2 turns. Fortunately, not every enemy in the game can attack Mario's partners; however, when it happens, Mario will be alone for a certain number of turns. This status condition is commonly caused by bosses such as [[Buzzar]], the [[Big Lantern Ghost]], and [[Bowser]] himself. The attack is similar to the [[Dizzy]] status effect in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'']] games.

Injured no longer appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' as Mario's partners have their own [[Heart Point|HP]].

==Attacks that can cause Injured==
* [[Buzzar]]'s wind attack
* [[Tutankoopa]]'s magic attack
* [[Big Lantern Ghost]]'s lantern shine attack
* [[Lava Bubble]]'s spit fire attack
* [[Bowser]]'s [[Star Rod]] shine attack
