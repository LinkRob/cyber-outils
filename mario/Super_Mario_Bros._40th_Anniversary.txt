The '''''Super Mario Bros.'' 40th Anniversary''' is an ongoing event which started since September 2025, celebrating 40 years with the 1985 release of the original ''[[Super Mario Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]''<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HXxeiXTF0t0 スーパーマリオブラザーズ40周年 Nintendo Direct 2025.9.12]. YouTube (Japanese). Retrieved October 9, 2025</ref>

The celebration was formally announced on the [[Nintendo Direct]] as it is followed from previous entry celebrations of the [[Super Mario Bros. 20th Anniversary|20th]], [[Super Mario Bros. 25th Anniversary|25th]], [[Super Mario Bros. 30th Anniversary|30th]], and [[Super Mario Bros. 35th Anniversary|35th]] anniversaries of the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]''.''
