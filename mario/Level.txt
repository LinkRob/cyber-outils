'''Levels''', titled '''stages''' or '''courses''' in some games, are a type of location in various ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' platformers, and sometimes other genres, in which the goal is to reach the end while avoiding various enemies and obstacles along the path. For the most part, levels consist of items to collect.

== Description ==
Levels appear in as early as the first ''[[Donkey Kong (game)|Donkey Kong]]'' arcade game, with the first stage being [[25m]] and the last one being [[100m]].

In most ''Mario'' games since ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', levels are accessed and grouped within [[world]]s.
