A '''Sad Cheep Cheep''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Sad Cheep Cheep is a variant of [[Cheep Cheep]], which it resembles, except Sad Cheep Cheeps are seen constantly crying, due to being imbued with the [[Gloom]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
Sad Cheep Cheeps can [[jump]] out of the water, like with regular Cheep Cheeps, except a Sad Cheep Cheep can leap higher because it uses its tears to propel itself while jumping.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
[[File:Sad Cheep Cheep Jumps.png|thumb|left|200px|A screenshot of a Sad Cheep Cheep in ''Super Princess Peach'']]
Sad Cheep Cheeps first appear in the [[level]] [[Wavy Beach 5-1]], and they appear in the worlds [[Wavy Beach]], [[Gleam Glacier]], and [[Bowser's Villa]]. Like with regular Cheep Cheeps, a Sad Cheep Cheep can be defeated from a [[stomp]], a whack from [[Perry]], [[Chargebrella]], or the effects of the [[Rage]] vibe.
