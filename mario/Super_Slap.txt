The '''Super Slap''' is one [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]]'s personal weapons in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be obtained in [[Bowser's Keep]]. Equipping the Super Slap increases Princess Toadstool's [[Attack]] power by 70, making it one of her strongest weapons, surpassed by only the [[Frying Pan (Princess Peach)|Frying Pan]]. The Super Slap can be sold for 49 [[coin]]s, but it cannot be replaced afterward.
