'''Icicles''' are obstacles in the [[Summit]] stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and later ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. If a fighter is nearby, the icicle attempts to drop on to them. Icicles are indestructible and cannot be melted.

[[Huge Icicle]]s of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] appear in the [[Mushroom Kingdom U]] stage of ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''.
