'''World 3''' is the third world of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. 

== Description ==
It is a grassland that is set at nighttime. Other than the castle, it consists of two ground levels and one level set in some treetops. It also marked the first appearance of the [[Hammer Brother]]s.

On the map in ''Deluxe'', there are several trees and some water. To the far west is 3-1, and from there, the player is lead east, following a simple path through 3-2, 3-3, and 3-4. There are no [[pipe]]s on this map.
