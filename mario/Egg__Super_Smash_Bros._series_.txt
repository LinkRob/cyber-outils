'''Eggs''' are items in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]]. They appear in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''. Fighters can either eat eggs to restore some [[HP]] or throw them at opponents to inflict minor damage. While [[Egg]]s do appear in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], the Egg [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'' lists ''Super Smash Bros.'' as its origin game.
