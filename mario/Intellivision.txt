The '''Intellivision''' is a game console released by Mattel in 1979 in North America, in 1981 in the UK; in 1982 in South Africa, Germany, France, and Japan; and in 1983 in Brazil. Two games from the Mario series, [[Donkey Kong (video game)|''Donkey Kong'']] and [[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|''Donkey Kong Jr.'']], were released for this system. However, a change in the system's ROM set prevented Donkey Kong from being playable on the Intellivision II which was released in 1983. A separately-sold adapter allowed the system to play games that were made for the [[Atari 2600]].

The console was discontinued in 1990.
