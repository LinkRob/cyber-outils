'''Taj the Genie''' is a blue elephant genie who resides on [[Timber's Island]] and has the ability to do magic such as teleport.

== History ==
=== In ''Diddy Kong Racing/Diddy Kong Racing DS'' ===
Taj first appears in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' as an ally to [[Timber the Tiger|Timber]] and his friends to kick [[Wizpig]] off [[Timber's Island]] and free the control of [[Tricky the Triceratops|Tricky]], [[Bluey the Walrus|Bluey]], [[Bubbler the Octopus|Bubbler]], and [[Smokey the Dragon|Smokey]]. In this game, Taj challenges the player to a vehicle race one at a time. First, he does the car, then the hovercraft, and finally the plane. He can also change the player's vehicle of his/her choice. He also gives the player a [[Golden Balloon]] when completing a race or a [[T.T. Amulet]] when completing a certain challenge.

Taj reappears in the [[Diddy Kong Racing DS|DS remake]] where he remains the same, but with a few differences; he no longer hums along with the theme song and he doesn't ask the player if he can help him/her further after choosing something, meaning he only does one thing at a time. Taj is also an unlockable character in this game who can be unlocked by completing Adventure Mode for the first time. He also has two new challenges; one where the player has to blow out all the blowtorches on the island, and another where he/she must collect ten [[Taj Token]]s before Taj does himself.

=== In ''Donkey Kong Racing'' ===
Taj was going to appear in the cancelled sequel to ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'', ''[[Donkey Kong Racing]]'' as he was racing [[Donkey Kong]] in a forest-like area on a [[Zinger]]s and was racing Donkey Kong and [[Diddy Kong]] on [[Rambi the Rhinoceros|Rambis]] in the beginning of the trailer. He also was considerably going to be a playable character in the game.

== Trivia ==
*Taj can be heard singing in the background in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' when near him, singing along with the theme song of [[Timber's Island]]. This was removed in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]''.
*Taj's name appears on the ''[[Tetris Attack]]'' high score board. It is unknown if this is the current Taj or a different one.
*When the player plays as Taj in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]'' and talks to the other Taj, he says he must be going crazy by talking to himself.
*Taj's name is derived from the [[wikipedia:Taj Mahal|Taj Mahal]].
