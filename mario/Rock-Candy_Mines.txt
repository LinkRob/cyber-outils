'''Rock-Candy Mines''' is the mountain world of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and their [[reissue|remake]] ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. Rock-Candy Mines lies east of [[Acorn Plains]], [[Sparkling Waters]], and [[Soda Jungle]], southeast of [[Layer-Cake Desert]], [[Frosted Glacier]], and [[Meringue Clouds]], and south of [[Peach's Castle]]. Rock-Candy Mines can be entered by clearing Soda Jungle's castle, or completing the secret worlds in Frosted Glacier and Sparkling Waters.

==Levels==
The levels are different in each game, although both have a total of ten levels, including seven normal stages, two Tower levels, and a Castle level.
