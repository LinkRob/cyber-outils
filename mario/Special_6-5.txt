'''Special 6-5''' is the fifth level of [[Special 6]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is based off of [[World 8-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 8-4]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts off on a platform. Going right leads to part of the platform that will detach itself and start moving past some [[Prickly Plant]]s and [[Boo]]s. After this segment is a [[Arrow Platform]] that goes past more Boos and some moving stones. After a [[Checkpoint Flag]] and a rotating platform, with [[Big Boo]]s and a [[Starman]] on it, another platform moves past some [[Prickly Plant]]s, some [[Peepa]]s, some Boos and some Big Boos. After this, the platform will drop and the player will be next to the door. Outside is another moving platform that leads to the [[Goal Pole]].

==[[Star Medal]]s==
*'''Star Medal 1:''' Floating to the left of the [[Roulette Block]].
*'''Star Medal 2:''' On the rotating platform with the [[Big Boo]]s and the [[Starman]].
*'''Star Medal 3:''' In a small opening above some [[Coin]]s, before the door.
