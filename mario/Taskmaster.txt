'''Taskmaster Toad''' is a variant of [[Toad (character)|Toad]] who appears exclusively in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' story mode. He functions as a supporting character, assigning "jobs" to [[Mario]] as part of the game's story. These jobs consist of custom-built levels that must be completed to fund the reconstruction of [[Princess Peach's Castle|Princess Peach’s Castle]]. He works alongside [[Toadette]] who is called chieff in the story mode, and their teamwork drives the rebuilding effort, blending leadership and cooperation. Taskmaster Toad also interacts with Mario and the rest of the [[Toad (species)|Toad]] crew, keeping the operation on track with diligence and care.

== Physical description ==
Physically, Taskmaster Toad is distinguished by his gray hard hat with a brown chin strap, suggesting a supervisory role on a construction site. He also wears a brown utility belt, reinforcing his role as the administrator of building tasks. Despite being similar in appearance to standard Toads, these construction-themed accessories mark him as a unique character within the game’s cast. The Toad crew members who assist in the reconstruction often wear badges featuring Taskmaster Toad’s emblem, subtly indicating his supervisory role within the team.{{SMM2Nav}}
