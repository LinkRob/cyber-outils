'''Z-Bananas''' (also called '''Z Bananas''') are a type of item in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Description==
Z-Bananas appear as purple-colored bananas that emit a light purple glow. They appear only on the [[DK's Jungle Ruins]] board, where they function as the direct equivalent of [[Mini Ztar]]s. The difference is because Z-Bananas and [[Banana]]s replace the [[Mini Ztar]]s and [[Mini Star]]s found on the other six [[board]]s, so Bananas have a similar function to Mini Stars in DK's Jungle Ruins. Like Bananas, Z-Bananas appear singularly or in bunches across the board. Collecting a single Z-Banana causes the player to lose one banana, while collecting a bunch causes them to lose three. It is possible to receive Z-Bananas during the second [[Captain Event Space|Captain Event]] of DK's Jungle Ruins, but there are none present in the [[Boss Battle Space|boss minigames]] of the board.
