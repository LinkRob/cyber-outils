'''Belltop''' is a character who appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Belltop resembles a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with a giant counter on his head. He also carries a blue whistle in his mouth and two rods where he bangs himself with.

==In ''Mario Party 3''==
Belltop only appears on [[Duel Mode]] boards where he is set on certain locations. Whenever a player passes through him, a countdown begins and when it hits zero, a mini-game occurs where one of the players could earn 20 or more coins. Also, after every mini-game win, Belltop will dispense coins to the winner after [[Tumble]] hits him.
