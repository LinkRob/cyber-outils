'''DK Dirt Pile''' is an object in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==Description==
DK Dirt Piles are brown and have the initials "DK" on them. Each one contains a [[5-Banana Coin]], which can be unearthed if the active [[Kong]] uses the [[Super Slam]], as the resulting shockwave erodes the DK Dirt Pile. DK Dirt Piles appear in every area and inside [[K. Lumsy's Prison]].
