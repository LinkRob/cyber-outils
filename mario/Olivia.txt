{{nihongo|'''Olivia'''|オリビアー|Oribia|lit. Olivia}} is the {{W|deuteragonist}} of ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. She is the sister of [[King Olly]], although it was later revealed that he essentially created her. She is also the main companion to [[Mario]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
In her basic form, Olivia is a yellow origami figure wearing a yellow crown, yellow robe and brown shoes. She has brown eyes, fair skin and yellow hair. Olivia's traits include her folded crown and hair, her yellow robe, and her spiky, triangular limbs.

=== Personality traits ===
Olivia is a friendly and a cheerful person.
{{Spoiler}}
{{Q|You'd think I'd be used to facing art and office supplies by now... but this one is SUPER scary!|Olivia against [[Stapler]], [[Origami Castle]], ''Paper Mario: The Origami King''}}
Despite fighting against different members of the [[Legion of Stationery]], she felt intimidated with the Stapler.
{{Endspoiler}}

=== Powers and abilities ===
* '''[[w:c:powerlisting:Origami Physiology|Origami Physiology]]''': Olivia is an origami girl folded by her brother, King Olly. Olivia can transform into large origami creatures such as the [[Vellumental]] on certain [[Magic Circle]]s. This can be done on the field or in battles.
**'''[[w:c:powerlisting:Origami Manipulation|Origami Manipulation]]:''' Olivia can fold herself into Vellumentals. Olivia can also fold other characters. As seen in the [[Origami Castle]], Olivia transformed [[Bowser]] to origami to defeat King Olly. Olivia and Mario assisted Bowser to defeat Olly.
* '''Singing''': Olivia sang a song called [[My Heart's a Burnin']] to summon a [[Fire Vellumental Cave]].

== Biography ==
=== [[Princess Peach's Castle]] ===
{{Spoiler}}
In the castle dungeon, Mario freed Olivia from the sealed wall and she joined forces with Mario. Escaping the dungeon, Mario and Olivia found Bowser clinging on the peg and Olivia alarmed Mario about the [[Folded Soldiers]]. Mario freed Bowser from the peg and joined Mario and Olivia to escape the dungeon. Mario exited the dungeon and a yellow folded Shy Guy and [[Princess Peach|Origami Peach]]. The yellow folded Shy Guy asked Mario if he wants to join their cause. Regardless of Mario's answer, it ordered the Folded Soldiers to fight Mario. Olivia cheered for Mario to defeat the Folded Soldiers. After defeating the Folded Soldiers, the yellow folded Shy Guy introduced himself as [[King Olly]] as it shocked Olivia. Olivia persuaded King Olly to stop. King Olly fulfilled his promise to create a new origami kingdom. A [[Shy Guy]] on a [[Koopa Clown]] Car came to Mario, Olivia and Bowser's aid and escaped the castle. However, Mario, Olivia, Bowser and the Shy Guy fell off the Koopa Clown Car into different locations.

=== Whispering Woods ===
After Mario and Olivia fell into [[Whispering Woods]]. Before Mario could reunite with Olivia, she warned Mario about the bottomless pit and gave him an [[Paper Mario: The Origami King|Empty Confetti Bag]]. Mario filled the bottomless pit with confetti and freed Olivia who was stuck on the ground.

=== Origami Castle ===
Olivia travels with Mario on his journey, explaining the battle system and how to fill in Not-Bottomless Holes. Olivia was responsible for naming [[Bob-omb (Paper Mario: The Origami King)|Bob-omb]] "Bobby", and enables Mario to use Magic Circles. Olivia is usually very cheerful, but when Bob-omb sacrifices himself to save her from a boulder in Sweetpaper Valley she becomes uncharacteristically sad. Olivia only cheers up when Mario uses a Goomba Mask to make a funny face, making her realize that Bob-omb wouldn't want her to be sad. She can also take on the appearances of the Vellumentals, and transforms into a giant hammer for Mario to use in the final fight with King Olly.

After the battle, Olivia fears she went too far with her hammer attack on King Olly, realizing that she mortally wounded her brother. She notices the mark on his body and reads it for him, telling him that it is a sweet message from the Origami Craftsman. King Olly regrets his villainy and makes a final request to Olivia to fold his remains into the final crane for the Thousand Cranes technique to amend his wrongdoings. Olivia is deeply upset as King Olly dies. With the help of the Origami Craftsman, Olivia finishes folding the final crane for the Thousand Cranes technique. Olivia wishes to undo all the origami Olly ever folded. This also erases her existence, since Olly was the one who first folded her. At the end of the game, Mario reminisces over Olivia while he and Peach watch fireworks.

In the game's 100% ending, a miniature, inanimate version of Olivia created by the Origami Craftsman appears in the scaled-down model of Origami Castle, alongside a similarly scaled down version of her brother, fulfilling the role their creator had originally intended for them.
{{endspoiler}}

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Olivia is featured as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]]. Because Olivia is not a spirit available at the start of the game, she does not appear in [[Adventure Mode: World of Light]], but she appears on the Spirit Board, giving a player an opportunity to fight in a spirit battle and obtain her spirit. Olivia can sometimes appear in the shop for 1000G.

If the player equips her spirit, she increases the player's airborne defenses, a possible reference to her floating ability in ''The Origami King''.
