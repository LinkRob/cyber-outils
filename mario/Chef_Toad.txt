'''Chef Toad''' is a character who appears as the head chef of Toadstool Cafe at [[Super Nintendo World|''Super Nintendo World'']].

==Appearance==
Chef Toad is a male Toad whose mushroom cap is red with white round spots, and he wears a white chef's hat on top of his cap. His principal attire features a white chef's robe with yellow buttons, and a red scarf surrounds his neck.

==''Super Nintendo World''==
Chef Toad was first introduced in Super Nintendo World at Universal Studios Japan. He appears in an animated scene as a window from a waiting area near the cafe. In the scene, Chef Toad walks into the view and begins preparing food, then notices guests through the window, greets them, and introduces them to the cafe.

There is merchandise involving Chef Toad, which feature plush dolls, key chains, figurines, and medallions, all of which can be found exclusively at a souvenir shop known as the 1-Up Factory in Super Nintendo World.
