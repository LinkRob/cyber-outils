'''''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''''' is a {{series|Super Mario}} game for the [[Nintendo Switch]]. It is the first new 2D ''Super Mario Bros.'' game since ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It was revealed in a [[Nintendo Direct]] on June 21, 2023. The game was released internationally on October 20, 2023.

In addition to being backwards compatible on the [[Nintendo Switch 2]], an enhanced version of the game for the system titled ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park]]'' was released on March 26, 2026 as part of the [[Super Mario Bros. 40th Anniversary|''Super Mario Bros.'' 40th Anniversary]] celebration.<ref>Nintendo of America (September 12, 2025). [https://youtu.be/tWIEv_aksvo?t=843 Nintendo Direct 9.12.2025] ''YouTube''. Retrieved September 12, 2025.</ref>
==Gameplay==
''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' is a platformer game that includes playable characters such as [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Princess Daisy]], [[Blue Toad]], [[Yellow Toad]], [[Toadette]], [[Nabbit]] and the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]. Their moveset features standard abilities, for instance the Wall Jump and the Ground Pound. The Yoshis retain their Flutter Jump, ability to use their tongue, and can also be used as a mount, and they and Nabbit do not take any damage from enemies. The players can receive new abilities by consuming power-ups, both returning and brand-new ones, such as an elephant-themed fruit.

The signature feature of the game is the Wonder Flower, a power-up that changes the world around Mario and friends in unique and strange ways, examples including bringing Warp Pipes to life and causing themselves and elements of the stage to stretch upward. Also revolving around the "Wonder" theme are the Wonder Seeds and purple coins, prominent collectibles. Another notable addition are Talking Flowers, non-playable characters that populate the levels and comment on the events and gimmicks nearby.

The game has a very expansive world map, allowing you to walk around it in open area's much like ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. Unlike most Mario games, the game allows you to choose the levels you want to play, and collect the wonder seeds, to unlock levels and progress. The game also includes [[Poplin]]s, the residents of the flower kingdom. You can find them around the map and talk to them. Or you may even find them running a [[Poplin Shop|shop]], where you can buy [[1-Up Mushroom|1 ups]], badges, or standee's. 

The game takes place in a location distinct from its predecessors and foregoes some of their established elements while featuring new level themes, such as autumnal forests and savannas. The hub consists of a contiguous world map, similar to that of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', where each level is represented by a 3D modeled icon, as in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. Also similar to both games, levels have unique names. Each one is also assigned a difficulty rating in-game, a feature only found previously in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''{{'}}s Story Mode.

Unlike every other 2D side-scrolling ''Super Mario'' game, excluding ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', there is no [[time limit]], so the characters can explore levels for as long as desired.

==Development==
[[File:1600px-SMBW Warp Concept.jpg|thumb|260x260px|Masanobu Sato's proposed concept of warping the player after collecting an item. This would eventually become the final game's [[Wonder Effect|Wonder Effects]].]]
On June 21, 2023, a [[Nintendo Direct]] has showcased ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''.<ref name="nintendo direct">{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s7t5jnpkCkI|title=Nintendo Direct 6.21.2023 - Nintendo Switch|work=YouTube|author=Nintendo of America|date=2023-06-21|accessdate=2023-06-21}}</ref> On August 31, 2023, more of ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' was shown in a 15-minute-long Nintendo Direct specifically focused on it. ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' also supposedly never had a deadline, as producer Takashi Tezuka stated: “I wanted to prevent people from saying, ‘We won’t make that deadline, so that’s why we didn’t do it—we can’t do it,’”

In regard to the new badge system, producer Tezuka had this to say: “It felt more like maybe something that you would see in a Zelda title, it didn’t really feel very Mario-esque … Mario gameplay is a lot more straightforward and simple.” According to Tezuka, the simple formula of older Mario games just isn't enough anymore. “When Mario first came out, of course, everything was new, so we really didn’t have to explain anything or convince people to do that play style,” Tezuka says. “We provided the game, and people were able to come up with their own play styles.”

The team also wanted to make a more unique and repayable experiences. Tezuka said, “If someone plays through a course, finishes it, moves on, and never comes back, that makes me a little sad. I really want players to play courses again and again.” For ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'', the development team took big risks. Instead of following the limitations of Mario, Tezuka urged developers “If you think it looks cool, it’s going to be fun. Do it.”

== Story ==
<blockquote>Welcome to the [[Flower Kingdom]], a not so distant land just beyond the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. [[Mario]] and friends were invited here by the benevolent [[Prince Florian]]! But [[Bowser]], the King of The [[Koopas]], had other plans. With just a single touch of the mysterious [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]], Bowser merged with Prince Florian's castle! Thanks to his newfound power, chaos spread across the land! 

[[Castle Bowser|Bowser]] lands in the middle of the [[Petal Isles|Pedal isles]], protected by his [[Cloud Piranha Plant|Cloud Piranha Plants]], as he prepares for his [[Bowser's Epic Wonder|"big wonder]]". but wait! it seems the royal seeds can destroy Cloud Piranha Plants! 

Now it's up to Mario and friends to find the royal seeds, stop Bowser, and save the Flower Kingdom!</blockquote>[[File:1600px-Flower Kingdom SMBW.jpg|left|thumb|256x256px|The main playable characters, Poplins, and Prince Florian in the [[Flower Kingdom]] in the opening cutscene.]]
[[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Princess Daisy]], [[Yellow Toad]], [[Blue Toad]], and [[Toadette]] are invited to the [[Flower Kingdom]], a neighbor to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] ruled by [[Prince Florian]]. Florian shows off the Wonder Flower to his visitors, when suddenly, [[Bowser]], [[Bowser Jr.]], and [[Kamek]] arrive. Bowser takes hold of the Wonder Flower, merging him, his Koopa Clown Car, and Florian's castle, the Flower Castle, to become Castle Bowser; he proceeds to spread havoc across the entire kingdom, including trapping the kingdom's inhabitants, the Poplins, in their homes. Not wanting to let this stand, Mario and company eagerly decide to help save the Flower Kingdom and set off with Florian, who opts to be the gang's guide, to stop Bowser's plans once again. The group is closely followed by four [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] and [[Nabbit]].

Mario and his allies journey through Pipe-Rock Plateau, Fluff-Puff Peaks, Shining Falls, Sunbaked Desert, Fungi Mines, and Deep Magma Bog, collecting the Royal Seed from each to remove the Cloud Piranhas protecting Castle Bowser, defeating an enhanced Bowser Jr. in four corrupted palaces and disabling three flying battleships summoned by Kamek in the process, before confronting Bowser within the castle from Petal Isles. After getting through some more of his defenses, Mario and his allies eventually defeat Castle Bowser, preventing him from using Wonder powers to terrorize the universe, and saving the Flower Kingdom, leaving the heroes, along with Florian and the Poplins, to celebrate.

Meanwhile, a post-credits scene shows Kamek attempting to carry Bowser Jr. and Bowser, who has reverted to his normal form, in the Koopa Clown Car; however, the weight is too heavy for Kamek, and he sinks, causing them to crash into some hills near the Flower Castle.

==Characters==
===Playable characters===
[[File:SMB Wonder playable characters.jpg|thumb|All twelve playable characters in ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']]Counting the colored Toads and Yoshis separately, ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' features 12 playable characters, the most playable characters in a mainline ''Super Mario'' game. Among them, Daisy and the Yoshis are playable for the first time in a home console ''Super Mario'' game after being previously playable in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. Most characters have the same attributes and play identically aside from revealing different Hidden Character Blocks. The Yoshis and Nabbit do not take damage from most obstacles and enemies, but cannot transform using power-ups. Additionally, Nabbit does not flinch from attacks, while the Yoshis retain their ability to Flutter Jump, use their tongues, and allow other characters to ride them. The random option on the selection screen cannot select the Yoshis or Nabbit.

=== Badges ===
A new element to the 2D ''Super Mario'' series in this game is badges, which are similar to the badges from the ''Paper Mario'' and ''Mario & Luigi'' series as well as the Power Badges from ''Yoshi's Woolly World''. Obtained either by completing a Badge Challenge or buying them with flower coins in a Poplin Shop, the player can equip their character with a badge before starting a course or upon losing a life.  They can dramatically alter the gameplay experience when equipped, either making the experience easier or harder depending on the badge. There are a total of twenty-four badges, including nine Action Badges, which add new abilities to the characters, eleven Boost Badges, which assist the player during gameplay, and four Expert Badges, which grant an advanced skill to the characters.

== Worlds ==
[[File:1600px-SMBW Flower Kingdom Map.jpg|thumb|281x281px|A map of the [[Flower Kingdom]]]]
The game is set in the Flower Kingdom, and has eight different worlds, consisting of six main, numbered worlds, a central world to which all of them connect called the Petal Isles, and the Special World. Each world contains a mix of both regular levels and smaller, bonus levels, of different types:

* '''Break Time!''' levels are short levels with a specific objective to obtain the Wonder Seed.
* '''Badge Challenge''' and '''Expert Badge Challenge''' levels involve using the abilities of a particular badge to reach the goal. The player is also rewarded with the badge upon completing the level. Players temporarily start the level in their Small form, and failing the challenge will not cause any lives to be lost. Despite their short length, they still hold three 10-flower coins in them, but unlike regular levels, the 10-flower coins are not saved if the player fails the challenge.
* '''Wiggler Race''' levels involve racing a Wiggler wearing roller skates to the goal.
* '''KO Arena''' levels involve defeating a gauntlet of enemies in succession, similar to some of the Mystery House levels in ''Super Mario 3D World''. These levels also have three 10-flower coins, which are awarded based on how fast the player completes the gauntlet.
* '''Search Party''' levels involve finding five hidden Wonder Tokens to obtain a Wonder Seed.

There is also a special bonus level known as Bonus: Coins Galore!, which contains many coins and flower coins for the player to collect. It appears when the player(s) receive(s) a Game Over, which causes a golden Propeller Flower to appear on the world map.

Each regular level contains two Wonder Seeds, one of which is obtained by finding the level's Wonder Flower and reaching the end of the Wonder Effect section, and the other is obtained by reaching the end of the level and grabbing the Goal Pole. Some levels also have a hidden third Wonder Seed, which is obtained by finding a secret exit. Bonus levels and palaces only have one Wonder Seed, the former of which is obtained by completing the level, and the latter of which stems from the Wonder Flower, while the end of palaces instead awards the player with a Royal Seed. Additionally, every regular level, Badge Challenge, and KO Arena level hides three 10-flower coins, which behave similarly to the Star Coins from the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' series, the Star Medals from ''Super Mario 3D Land'', and the Green Stars from ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

Each location of the Flower Kingdom has a corresponding color for its Wonder Flowers, Wonder Seeds, and Wow Buds.

== Standee's ==
Standee's can be placed around levels. in online play, standee's serve as a re-spawn point, that you can go to if you turn into a ghost. the more standee's you buy in the poplin shop, the more you can add to your collection. in the poplin shop, you can buy a standee for purple coins. you can buy a character specific standee, or a random standee.

== Music ==
The music for this game was composed by Koji Kondo, Shiho Fujii, Sayako Doi, and Chisaki Shimazu. The game features a full [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder Official Soundtrack|soundtrack]] like most modern Nintendo titles. On November 1st, 2024, ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' was added to Nintendo Music.

== Real-world influences ==
=== In pop culture ===
The Nintendo Direct of ''Wonder'' received mixed reactions of excitement and concern. Fans took interest with Princess Daisy being playable, a new elephant power-up, and even the art direction. Fans were concerned noticing Mario's voice is off, pointing out the voice is different from [[Charles Martinet]], which was replaced by [[Kevin Afghani]] (which the latter theory would later become true as of August 21, 2023). 

== amiibo ==
Selecting the "Scan amiibo" option in the pause menu while in a course will allow the player to scan an amiibo to receive a power-up, which differs when using certain amiibo. nly one amiibo can be scanned for the duration of a course unless it is either restarted or the player loses a life. The power-ups received are as follows:

* Elephant Mario: An Elephant Fruit
* Poplin & Prince Florian: A Bubble Flower
* Captain Toad & Talking Flower: A Drill Mushroom
* Other amiibo: A Fire Flower.

== Promotion ==
[[File:1200px-SMBW Trading Cards.png|thumb|286x286px|The promotional image showing all of the possible cards]]
By pre-ordering the game through GameStop, customers received an exclusive sticker set depicting the playable characters, a Wonder Flower, and a Talking Flower.

By pre-ordering the game through Walmart's website, customers received a trading card pack, each containing ten cards randomly pulled from a total of 20 different cards to get. All of the possible cards that were distributed in the card packs include 15 base cards, 4 holofoil cards, and a limited edition holofoil card depicting Elephant Mario, with only 1,000 produced.

At Target, an exclusive shadowbox collectable depicting the game's main artwork could be received with the purchase of the game in-store.

By pre-ordering the game through Best Buy's website, customers received a free art print with the game's main artwork.

In Canada, customers were given a free Elephant Mario keychain as a pre-order bonus for the game at GameStop. Those who pre-ordered the game at Walmart received an exclusive pop-out magnetic set.

An Elephant Fruit stress ball was initially announced as a pre-order bonus for certain retailers in multiple regions, including the My Nintendo Store in Europe (alongside pins of an Elephant Fruit, Talking Flower, and Wonder Flower), GAME in the UK, and JB Hi-Fi in Australia, though it was later canceled due to production issues. My Nintendo Store customers instead only received the pins, while the stress ball was replaced by the same pins with GAME and a pin of Elephant Mario with JB Hi-Fi. A bundle containing the game, a Talking Flower resin figure, a 2D diorama, and magnets using the game's promotional artwork is also available at the My Nintendo Store in the UK and Ireland. Customers in Australia who pre-ordered the game through EB Games received the same pins as Europe's My Nintendo Store and GAME.

In Italy, a "''Super Mario'' Immersive Hub" was open in Milan on October 14 and 15, in which the game's demo could be played. A promotional event made in collaboration with soccer club Catania FC took place at Stadio Angelo Massimino in Catania on October 22.

Advertisements for the game were shown in Times Square.

From September 1, 2023 to April 26, 2024, a series of articles titled 「『スーパーマリオブラザーズ ワンダー』のへ」 (Into the World of Super Mario Bros. Wonder) or simply 「ワンダーのへ」(Into the World of Wonder) was released on the official Japanese Nintendo website. The series consisted of eighteen volumes, detailing various aspects of the gameplay and story of the game.<gallery>

=== Awards and acknowledgements ===
''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' was nominated for five awards at The Game Awards 2023, in the following categories:

* "Game of the Year"
* "Best Game Direction"
* "Best Art Direction"
* "Best Family Game"
* "Best Multiplayer"

The game would go on to win "Best Family Game", though it lost "Game of the Year" and "Best Multiplayer" to ''Baldur's Gate 3'' and "Best Game Direction" and "Best Art Direction" to ''Alan Wake 2''.

=== Sales ===
''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' sold 4.3 million units worldwide within the first two weeks, making it the "biggest release" and "fastest-selling" ''Super Mario''-related title ever.

As of March 31st, 2025, the game had sold 16.03 million units worldwide.

==Trivia==
*On August 21, 2023, it was announced that [[Charles Martinet]] is retiring from providing voice acting for the ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' games.<ref>[https://deadline.com/2023/08/charles-martinet-nintendo-mario-1235523914/ Charles Martinet Retires From Longtime Voice Role Of Nintendo’s Mario]</ref> This means that ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' is the first game to feature [[Kevin Afghani]] as the new voice of [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].
**In addition, this is also the first game to feature [[Giselle Fernandez]] as the new voice of Princess Daisy, replacing [[Deanna Mustard]].
*This is the second Mario platformer game to feature Daisy as a playable character, the first being ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.
**This is the first console Mario platformer to feature Daisy as a playable character .
**This is also Daisy's third appearance in a mainline Mario game, The first being her debut in [[Super Mario Land|''Super Mario Land'']] and the second being ''Super Mario Run'' (which is officially considered mainline by Nintendo).
*Excluding ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe'' (which is a direct port), ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' is first original 2D ''Super Mario'' series platformer to be released on the Nintendo Switch.
*Not counting entries in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', its follow-ups, and the [[iOS]]/[[Android]] release ''Super Mario Run'', this is the first 2D ''Super Mario'' game since ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' (''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' if not counting ports) to not use the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' label.
**It is the first game with ''Super Mario Bros.'' in the title to not use the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' label since the sub-series inception.
*This is also the first game since ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' in the ''Super Mario'' series where [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is an individual playable character rather than playing his usual role as a rideable sidekick.
*This game has the most playable characters in mainline Mario game since ''Super Mario Run'', as this game has 12 playable characters: Mario (1), Luigi (2), Peach (3), Daisy (4), Light Blue Yoshi (5), Red Yoshi (6), Green Yoshi (7), Yellow Yoshi (8), Blue Toad (9), Yellow Toad (10), Toadette (11), and Nabbit (12).
*[[Nabbit]] and [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] are shown to be "Easy-Mode Characters" as revealed at the Super Mario Bros. Wonder Direct. This is second time Nabbit has been declared an "Easy Mode" Character and the first time for Yoshi.
**However, during certain Wonder Effects such as the one in Maw Maw Mouthful, the Yoshis and Nabbits can still be eaten by Maw Maws.
*This is the second game where Mario can transform into a [[Goomba]]. The first time was in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.
*This is the first game to have characters walk while crouching, and characters that can throw Fire Balls while crouching.
**Some characters ''can'' walk while crouching in [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'']], however.
*''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' has sold 4.3 million copies within 2 weeks, making this one of the best sales performances of a ''Super Mario'' game.
*This game was released on the same week as ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Superstars|Sonic Superstars]]''.
