'''Rocket Bucket''' is an object in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Description==
It is an aircraft that [[Wario]] can use to fly to otherwise unreachable elevations. The player can have elevate the Rocket Bucket by pressing and holding {{button|wii|2}} and steer it by tilting the [[Wii Remote]]. To have Wario exit a Rocket Bucket, the player must press Up on the Control Pad.

Wario can break the Rocket Bucket by either [[Dash Attack|charging]] into it, attempting to fly it to the next screen, or by touching any surface covered in [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]].
