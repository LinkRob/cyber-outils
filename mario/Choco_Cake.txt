'''Choco Cake''' is a dark [[cake]] [[recipe]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''

== In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
Choco Cake is made by [[Zess T.]]. Using it restores five [[Heart Point|HP]] and fifteen [[Flower Point|FP]].

Mario can also receive this cake as a reward for solving [[Toadia]]'s trouble.
