The '''Cobalt Necktie''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It can only be obtained by making a different kind of [[Starbeans Espresso|espresso]] at [[Starbeans Cafe]] for the sixth time. [[Professor E. Gadd]] then appears to give the Cobalt Necktie to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

==Appearance==
Despite the name, the Cobalt Necktie resembles a bow tie more of a regular necktie. It also appears to be gray in color.

==Usage==
While equipped, the Cobalt Necktie doubles the regular amount of 'Stache Points. This is useful as the more 'Stache Points, the luckier Mario or Luigi are.
