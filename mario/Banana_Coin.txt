===''Donkey Kong 64''===
Banana Coins are very common in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' and come in five different colors: yellow for [[Donkey Kong]], red for [[Diddy Kong]], blue for [[Lanky Kong]], purple for [[Tiny Kong]], and green for [[Chunky Kong]]. Collecting these items are required as they allow the Kongs to buy potions from [[Cranky Kong]], instruments from [[Candy Kong]], and weapons and gear from [[Funky Kong]]. They are often scattered about throughout levels usually found around banana bunches. Finding a rare [[5-Banana Coin]] adds 5 regular Banana Coins to the Kong that picked it up.
