'''Allegra''' is an allied NPC in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'', making her only appearance in the second DLC titled [[The Last Spark Hunter]]. She is the warden of the jungle-like [[Melodic Gardens]].

==Profile==
{{Sectstub}}

===Appearance===
Allegra is somewhat tall and lean. She has black stringy hair and blue eyes and purple face paint and wears a leafy dress. She also wears green arm bands and a necklace with rocks on it.

===Personality traits===
Allegra is first seen as a cautious character, as she spies on [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Bowser]], [[Beep-0]], [[JEANIE]], [[Rabbid Mario|and]] [[Rabbid Luigi|the]] [[Rabbid Peach|five]] [[Rabbid Rosalina|Rabbids]] [[Edge|allied]] with them during the group's exploration of the Melodic Gardens. She later warms up to the heroes after witnessing them save the musicians. She cares for everyone allied with her, especially the Dorrie she keeps as a pet, as when she reunites with him, she hugs the creature. Allegra is not afraid of danger, and she will do anything to protect her friends and the Melodic Gardens.

===Powers and abilties===
{{Sectstub}}

==Biography==
Allegra makes her debut as the heroes traverse through the Melodic Gardens and she watches their exploration. After they save the Rabbid Musicians, Allegra comes out of hiding and they become friends. As she does so, Allegra reveals what is happening on the planet, including a new [[Spark Hunter]], whose existence [[Edge]] knew nothing of. The heroes join Allegra to help her find a friend of hers, but then, [[Kanya]] then appears and reveals that Allegra's friend may be in danger. This devastates Allegra, causing her to run off while Kanya summons a robot version of [[King Bob-omb]] that she sics on the heroes.

After destroying Kanya's robot, the heroes later reunite with Allegra as she reunites with her friend, who is revealed to be Dorrie. [[Kanya]] then attacks the heroes with a mecha version of [[King Bob-omb]]. During the battle, the heroes free the Dorrie, and Kanya is defeated for good as a result, causing the music and biophonies (one of which Allegra shows as a reward) to be restored to the Melodic Gardens.

==In ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope''==
Allegra's Memory biography reads:

''"Allegra is a fascinating study. An eco musician of rare ability, she is perfectly in tune with the natural world - and even her skin's pigmentation adapts to its mood. She wields her bio tuner like a baton, somehow bringing a unified vision to the collective sounds of an entire biome. It's her life's calling. It is not difficult to understand why, yet she is still somehow a mystery to me. One I fear I may never solve."''

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Allegra is named after the Italian musical tempo "Allegro," whose name comes from an Italian word meaning "happy" (''allegro'', to be specific).

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Allegra is called Aregura (written アレグラ), which has the same roots as her English name. In Chinese, she is called Āléigélā (written 阿雷格拉). In Spanish, the Italian name is retained, but pronounced "Al-YEH-grah." Italian uses the same name as the English name, with the same pronunciation ("Al-LEH-grah"). For some reason, the name is not translated properly into Portuguese.
