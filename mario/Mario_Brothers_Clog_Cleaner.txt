'''Mario Brothers Clog Cleaner''' is an item which only appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[The Marios Fight Back]]." It is a creation of [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]].

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
During "The Marios Fight Back," Mario and Luigi brag to [[David Horowitz]] that the Mario Brothers Clog Cleaner is an effective product capable of dissolving any plumbing [[clog]] in three minutes or less. The effects of a Mario Brothers Clog Cleaner is not seen by itself, but it is shown that using the Mario Brothers Clog Cleaner with the [[Mario Brothers Plant Food]] causes a clog to mutate.
