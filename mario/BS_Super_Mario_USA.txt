'''''BS Super Mario USA''''' is a [[Satellaview]] game that serves as a storyline sequel to ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It was the first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' platformer to use the Satellaview's Soundlink features. Its graphics are mostly the same as the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' version of ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', but it had new levels and a new plot, comparable to ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. ''BS Super Mario USA'' was broadcast in four parts during spring 1996, exclusively within Japan as the Satellaview had never been released overseas.

==Story==
Shortly after the events of ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', [[Mario]] and his friends [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]] and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] believed [[Subcon]] was all just a dream and move on. However, [[Wart]] returns and overthrows the [[Ōsama|King of Subcon]] in the heroes' absence. Mario and his friends must once again team up and face the forces of Wart, including the [[8 bits|8-bits]]: [[Birdo (species)|Birdo trio]], [[Mouser]], [[Tryclyde]], [[Fryguy]] and [[Clawgrip]]. Both the heroes and their villains can use the power of the stars ([[Satellaview|satellites]]) to their advantage. In addition, Mouser has stolen the [[Ōgon no Mario Zō|golden Mario statues]], symbols of peace, and hid them all throughout Subcon.<ref>July 18, 2020.
