'''Minions''' are a species from the ''{{w|Despicable Me}}'' franchise. Following the film series' success, they have become mascots for [[Illumination Entertainment]] in general. Minions have sometimes been used to help market ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', but they do not appear within the film itself.

==In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''==
While Minions do not appear in the film itself, one is seen during the opening logo for Illumination. Here, a Minion is seen riding a [[kart]] at a starting line, with the letters of the Illumination logo lighting up similar to the countdown lights held by [[Lakitu (referee)|Lakitu]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}} since ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. The letters of the logo light up in order of "ILLU," "MINA," and lastly "TION". The Minion revs the engine before the second set of lights are lit, causing his kart to backfire, a reference to an unsuccessful [[Rocket Start]]. Frustrated, he slams the front the kart with his fist before it finally blasts off, causing the Minion to be dragged along with it as he screams the company’s name.

In a teaser trailer for the film, Minions are among the Illumination-owned characters seen headed to the theaters to watch ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''.
