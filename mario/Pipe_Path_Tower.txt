'''Pipe Path Tower''' is an island located on [[Lake Lapcat]] in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
Pipe Path Tower is one of the few islands that surrounds the Wasteland [[Giga Bell]]. The island is a large tower with [[Clear Pipe]]s surrounding it. During the final fight against [[Giant Bowser]], all of the Clear Pipes are absent.
