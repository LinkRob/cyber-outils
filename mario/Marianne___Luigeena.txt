"'''Marianne and Luigeena'''" is the fourth live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is associated with the animated episode "[[Mario's Magic Carpet]]."

==Plot==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] have their cousins [[Marianne]] and [[Luigenna]] coming over for lunch. They also invite [[Lyle Alzado]] to lunch. They receive a call from their cousins and in spite of Luigi's protests Mario tells them about their special guest.  Mario and Luigi decide they must keep their cousins a good distance between their cousins and Lyle Alzado.

However, the Marios have to leave to pick up refreshments.  While they are gone Lyle shows up and their cousins show up soon after. The two women are crazy about Lyle and start to flirt with him.  Lyle tries to leave but they won't let him.  Marianne sends Luigenna away and sits with Lyle offering to go dancing with him.  Luigi shows up and apologizes. Mario also returns just in time for Marianne and Luigenna to say good-bye because they just spotted [[Mel Gibson]]. Lyle claims to be disappointed as he was looking forward to going dancing, although Mario and Luigi believe he's really joking.

== Trivia ==
* This is the first time that [[Lou Albano]] and [[Danny Wells]] portray alter-egos of Mario and Luigi, respectively.
* Marianee and Luigenna are the first relatives of the Marios that are seen.
