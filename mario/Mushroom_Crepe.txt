A '''Mushroom Crepe''' is a [[recipe]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. They appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', being named '''Shroom Crepe''' in the former game.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
[[File:Shroom_Crepe_TTYD.png|frame|left]]
Shroom Crepes restore 30 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 20 [[Flower Point|FP]] upon use, making them very effective. However, making one requires a [[Cake Mix]] and an [[Ultra Shroom]].

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Mushroom Crepes make a reappearance. They now only restore 20 HP and no longer any FP, but were given an added effect of puring [[Poison (status effect)|poison]]. A Mushroom Crepe can be made from a Cake Mix and a [[Shroom Shake]]. Mushroom Crepes can also be used in the [[Fruity Cake]] recipe by mixing it with a [[Peachy Peach]].
