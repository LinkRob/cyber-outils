The '''ticket''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], for the [[Kind Goomba]] quest, [[Goomba (Mario Party Advance)|Goomba]] asks the player's character to retrieve the item for them at the [[Train Station]] in exchange for a [[Gaddget]]. When the player's character arrives there, [[Shy Guy (Mario Party Advance)|Shy Guy]] asks them what they want, and when they mention Goomba's errand, he quickly gives the ticket to them. When the ticket is given to Goomba, he happily rewards the player with the [[Egg Roll (Gaddget)|Egg Roll]] Gaddget.
