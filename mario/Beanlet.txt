'''Beanlets''' are creatures that make their appearance in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They serve as cute and puppy-like domestic pets for the people of the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. Five of them are lost by a [[Beanish]] Person and he requests for [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to help find them. They are all located in [[Beanbean Castle Town]], on top of houses or in small nooks. Once all five are given back to their owner, he rewards them with a [[Golden Mushroom]].

After the second invasion of the Beanbean Castle Town caused by [[Bowletta]], this sidequest becomes [[Missable content|permanently missable]].
