The '''Master Cycle Zero''' is a vehicle featured in ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild]]'', and is in turn based on the [[Master Cycle]] from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. The Master Cycle Zero was added as a [[kart]] part in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''{{'}}s version 1.6.0 update. The [[Ancient Tires]] are its associated wheels, and the [[Paraglider]] is its associated [[glider]].
