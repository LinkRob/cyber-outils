*[[Yoshi Kid]]|enemies=[[Doopliss]]}}'''Gloomer''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and its [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. He is a male [[Twilighter]] that lives in [[Twilight Town]].

==History==
Gloomer is the first Twilighter that greets [[Mario]] and his friends as soon they enter Twilight Town for the first time. However, before he can say anymore, he was transformed into a [[pig]]. After Mario has defeated [[Doopliss]], Gloomer turns back to normal. After the events of Chapter 4, he gives another cheer for Mario (though he seemed a bit exhausted to say it).

==Trivia==
*Oddly, when Gloomer was transformed into a pig, he was known as '''Freddy the Pig'''. This also implies that Gloomer's name is also '''Freddy'''. His German name is '''Freddy''', too.
