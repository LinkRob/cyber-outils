'''Old Red-Mugs''' are enemies in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Old Red-Mugs are [[Whomp]]-shaped enemies made up of several gray bricks. One of their sides has a small, red face in the center.

==Behavior==
An Old Red-Mug is located around a pit, where it blocks [[Wario]]'s path.

==In ''Wario World''==
Old Red-Mugs appear only in the level [[Greenhorn Ruins]], and only three of them are encountered. Wario can defeat an Old Red-Mug by either punching its face three times or [[Mega Toss]]ing another enemy at it
