'''Stampede!''' is the name of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Final Smash]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It replaces the [[Super Dragon]] move from earlier ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' installments.

==Description==
[[File:Stampede SSBU Move List graphic.png|thumb|left|200px|Move List graphic]]
The move is a directional Final Smash in that, when activated, Yoshi charges forward, so the move is only effective if he hits at least one opponent. Upon hitting some opponents, the scene is changed to a canyon-like area, where numerous [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] arrive to run over the character(s), a scene derived from the ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' opening cinematic, although Yoshis do not run over anyone in the cinematic. A puff of sandy fog partly obscures the Yoshis in the path behind the upper slope where the opponents lie. Stampede! deals a lot of damage to whoever is caught within the move.
