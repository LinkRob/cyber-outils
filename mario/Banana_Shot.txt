The '''Banana Shot''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
The Banana Shot is a a metal bazooka loaded with a [[Banana]]. It is a lock-on item which, when used, launches a Banana toward its target. When the targeted racer is hit, they spin out. If the Banana is launched against an object, it drops to the ground and functions the same as a regular Banana.

==Trivia==
*Banana Shot has a stronger variation named [[Tox-Banana Shot]].
