'''Cheep Cheep Check''' is a 1-vs.-3 minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place at an aquarium with [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Clampy]]s inside.

==Gameplay==
The objective for the rivals is to count the number of Cheep Cheeps, while the solo character has to try and mess up their count, which they can do by scaring the Cheep Cheeps and opening the two Clampys. Once 15 seconds have passed, the rivals have a limited time to calculate how many Cheep Cheeps are inside the tank. After this, the number of Cheep Cheeps is unveiled. If the rivals guess correctly, they win, but if they are incorrect, the solo character wins.

==Controls==
===Solo character===
*{{button|wii|A}} − Scare Cheep Cheeps or open Clampys

===Rivals===
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} − Move cursor
*{{button|wii|A}} − Select input for the number of Cheep Cheeps
