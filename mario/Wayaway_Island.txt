'''Wayaway Island''' is a major location from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. Despite often being grouped with the [[Gulchrock Sea]], it is technically not part of [[Concordia]] due to its distance from the rest of the islands and the lack of a lighthouse. [[Bowser]] and his [[Koopa Troop|forces]] have a base on this island, which has to be visited to get a large pile of [[Ampberry|Ampberries]] so [[Doctor Vulko]] can cure the Concordians that have been infected with [[Glohm]] without worry of his Ampberry reservoir running low.

== History ==
Prior to the events of the game, [[Bowser]] and his forces were drawn to [[Concordia]] when the [[Great Conductor]] opened a portal to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] so [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] could save the land after [[Zokket]] sundered it with his desecration of the [[Uni-Tree]]. Bowser and his forces immediately conquered Wayaway Island, hoarding the Ampberries growing there, but other than that, Zokket's attack did not affect the island majorly. From here, Bowser and his army conquered the [[Gulchrock Sea]], but ended up at odds with the [[Zokket Army]].

Mario and Luigi would eventually have to get more Ampberries when [[Doctor Vulko]] notices his Ampberry supply running low, so they infiltrate [[Offandon Island]] (which they had previously escaped from after getting marooned thanks to Bowser's attack) to get information on the whereabouts of Wayaway Island. To get to Wayaway Island, they have to use a [[Warp Pipe]] on [[Gulchrock Sea#Islets|Meeger Islet]] and another to Mynor Islet.

Upon reaching the island, Mario and Luigi storm the fortress, hoping to strike a deal where Bowser hands some of his Ampberries, but their plan fails when Bowser's hatred of their guts overrides this option and he chooses violence. In the chaos, the [[Extension Corps]] kidnaps [[Bowser Jr.]] Bowser, realising that he condemned his son to a kidnapping, storms [[Fortress Zokket]] to get his child back as the Bros. get information on the Ampberry stash's whereabouts by a [[Goomba]].

Mario and Luigi later return here to get a part of Bowser's [[Airship]] to upgrade [[Shipshape Island]]'s engine, which allows access to Fortress Zokket after Zokket blocks his lair off with a Glohm wall. Bowser can also be visited in his throne room, recovering from his wounds incurred during [[Glohm Bowser]]'s boss fight.

== Layout ==

=== Starting Area (Wayaway Island Castle Gate) ===
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] start at the south end of the area. To the west is the [[Concordian Peddler]], who is ready to sell items.

=== Crossing ===
To the north is a four-way crossing, with the south path leading back to the south, the west path leading to a few [[Mystery Block|Mystery Blocks]], the east path leading to a long Mystery Block, and the north path leading inside the fortress.

=== Fortress ===

==== Ampberry Stash ====
Bowser keeps the Ampberries here.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Wayaway Island's name comes from "away," which can be used to refer to things at a distance.
