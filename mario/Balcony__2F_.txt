'''Balcony''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Balcony is a small terrace on the second floor of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]], and it provides a view of the [[Graveyard]] and the [[Boneyard]]. The Balcony is visible from the hole in the [[Master Bedroom (Luigi's Mansion)|Master Bedroom]]'s right window.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Balcony can be entered from the door on the left side of the [[Wardrobe Room]]. The Balcony only features a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] and some small plants that can be watered to obtain some [[treasure]].
