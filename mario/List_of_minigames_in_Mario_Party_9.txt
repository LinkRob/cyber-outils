''[[Mario Party 9]]'' is a Nintendo [[Wii]] game released in 2012. There are 82 mini-games, with Free-For-Alls (44), 1 vs Rivals (10), Bowser Jr. (10), and Boss Battles (14). There are also 4 Extra mini-games that can be played on Extras. You must unlock these to play them in free for all.
