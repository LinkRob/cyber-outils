The '''Flutter Dunk''' is [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Special Shot]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Yoshi perform it by tapping out an uppercase "M" twice on the Touch Screen. Yoshi initiates the Flutter Dunk by dribbling in an ''M''-shape, which coats the ball with [[water]]. Yoshi then [[Flutter Jump]]s into the air while a rainbow trails behind him and forcefully dunks the ball in the hoop, followed by a rainbow-colored ring appearing.

If Yoshi executes the move from outside the 3-point line instead, he shoots the ball, with the rainbow trail behind it, towards the hoop. When a teammate dunks it in the hoop, the rainbow ring appears.

==Trivia==
*The sound that occurs during the Flutter Dunk is Yoshi's flying sound in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.
