The '''Shiny Fire Flower''' is an item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. It is a stronger version of the [[Fire Flower]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''===
In this game, the Shiny Fire Flower appears as one of many [[Stickers]] throughout the game. They can be first purchased in the [[Item Shop]] on [[Whammino Mountain]] for 30 coins. Naturally, this sticker is stronger than the regular Fire Flower sticker and is very effective against [[Dry Bones]] and ice-related enemies such as [[Ice Bro]]s. However, it is ineffective against fire-related enemies or aerial enemies. The Shiny Fire Flower sticker also has a few other uses in the game where in [[Bouquet Gardens]], [[Mario]] can [[Paperize]] a Shiny Fire Flower sticker, alongside two other flower-related stickers, to help bloom a [[Toad]]'s garden resulting in two copies of each sticker to be created. The Shiny Fire Flower sticker can also be donated to the [[Sticker Museum]].

===''Paper Mario: The Origami King''===
Shiny Fire Flowers are now regular items in this game rather than stickers. Like with before, they are simply stronger versions of the basic Fire Flowers where Mario can deal up to 36 points of damage to his foes. Shiny Fire Flowers can only be purchased in the item shop in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario: The Origami King)|Toad Town]] for 760 coins.
