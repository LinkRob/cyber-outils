'''Dreampoints''' are locations appearing throughout ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' that takes [[Mario]] to a special spot in the [[Dream World]]. The main difference that Dreampoints have from [[Luigi]]'s dreams is that Dreampoints do not have any [[Pi'illo]]s to rescue.

== List of Dreampoints ==
*[[Pi'illo Castle]]: The [[Battle Ring]] is a Dreampoint. There is also one in the exhibit room.
*[[Dozing Sands]]: This is where Dreampoints are first introduced. Mario goes in this Dreampoint to find out where the [[Dream Stone]] is.
*[[Wakeport]]:
**Near [[Kylie Koopa]]'s house, [[Big Massif]] is sleeping on a Dreampoint.
**After his liberation, the [[Bedsmith]] can no longer be slept on. However, the bed in [[Pi'illoper]]'s basement gets converted into a Dreampoint, allowing the player to get the [[Luiginary Wall]] if they missed it.
*[[Mount Pajamaja]]: At the top of the mountain, Mario must go inside a Dreampoint and wait out the [[Dreambeats]].
*[[Driftwood Shore]]: Mario and Luigi are required to get three Dream Eggs to expand the Dream World, making them go on four different Dreampoints.
*[[Somnom Woods]]: A Dreampoint at the [[Pi'illo Temple]] is used to summon the [[Zeekeeper]].
*[[Neo Bowser Castle]]: There is one next to a floating [[Koopa Clown Car]], two Dark-Stone shaped ones, and there is even one in [[Bowser]]'s Room.
