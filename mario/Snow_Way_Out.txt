}}'''Snow Way Out''' is a 1 vs. 3 minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. This minigame's name is a pun of the phrase "no way out."

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the single player is inside a snow machine while the three players are on top of a roof of a house. The single player much attempt to shoot snowballs at the three players which, if they are hit by one, will turn them into ice and slide off the roof. The three players much simple dodge the snowballs for 30 seconds to win. The single player can even shoot ice on the roof which will make that small area slippery for the three players. The three players must be sure to stay far from each other to increase their chances of winning.
