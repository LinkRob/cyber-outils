|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Doom Reverb''' is a special attack used by enemies (mainly [[Shy Guy]]s) in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Doom Reverb is used, a bell appears overhead of the selected character while chiming a tone.

==Effects==
With Doom Reverb, it may cause the [[Silence]] ailment to one of [[Mario]]'s party. Wearing a special item like a Wake-Up Pin can cancel out this effect.
