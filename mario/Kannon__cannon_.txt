'''Kannons''' are objects in the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''.

==Description==
Kannons are regular black [[cannon]]s with a coded design in various ''Donkey Kong'' titles produced by [[Rare]]. They are generally used to shoot [[kannonball]]s.

==Appearances==
===''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' / ''Donkey Kong Land 2''===
In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'', cannons,<ref>''Donkey Kong Land 2'' [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Virtual Console]] electronic manual, tab 9: "Progressing Through Stages"</ref> primarily spelled as '''kannons'''<ref>''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' instruction booklet, page 17.</ref> To activate a kannon, [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong]] must throw a [[kannonball]] into it, and then enter it; it launches the Kongs to a [[Bonus Area]]. They first appear in [[Mainbrace Mayhem (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Mainbrace Mayhem]], and only appear in levels with kannonballs (not being shot). There is also a [[Kremling]] enemy named [[Kannon]], whose name is based on the kannon it carries around.

===''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!''===
[[File:DKC3 cannon.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'', a kannon pops up on top of [[Belcha's Barn]] after [[Dixie Kong]] and [[Kiddy Kong]] defeat [[Belcha]]. The kannon blasts them to the start of [[Lake Orangatanga]]. The kannon does not appear in the [[Game Boy Advance]] [[Donkey Kong Country 3 (Game Boy Advance)|version]], as it returns the [[Kong]]s to the main [[Northern Kremisphere]] map automatically instead.

===''Donkey Kong 64''===
In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'', a single Kannon is located in a small room near the start of [[Gloomy Galleon]]. The kannon is only usable by [[Chunky Kong]], who place a [[Kannonball]] inside of it to try and fire at [[target]]s on a wall within a [[time limit]]. If he meets the objective, Chunky earns a [[Golden Banana]].

==Flavor text==
