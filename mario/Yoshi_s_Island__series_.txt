'''''Yoshi's Island''''' is a series of platforming games starring [[Yoshi]], and involves various [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] on an adventure traveling with [[Baby Mario]] on their back.

There are currently three installments:

*''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''
*''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''
*''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''
Wave four of the [[Booster Course Pass]] brought a Yoshi's island-themed race track, but you'd need to purchase both ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' and the booster course pass if you wanted to race on it.

''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'' is not recognized as a main installment, but rather a spinoff within the ''Yoshi's Island'' series.
