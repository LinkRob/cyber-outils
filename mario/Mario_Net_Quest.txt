'''''Mario Net Quest''''' is an {{w|Adobe Shockwave}} game created as a result of a partnership between [[Nintendo]] and IBM. The game is based on ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', the latest ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' game released at the time.

==Gameplay==
''Mario Net Quest'' is a whac-a-mole-style game in which certain objects have to be clicked on to gain points; clicking a [[Coin]] is worth 500 [[point]]s, a [[Red Coin]] is worth 1,000 points, and a [[Power Star]] is worth 1,500.

The player has to avoid clicking enemies as they result in the loss of points; clicking a [[Bully]] causes 500 points to be lost; a [[Goomba]] causes 1,000 points to be lost, and a [[Boo]] causes 1,500 points to be lost.
