|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Sweet Feast''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. [[Mario]] can learn this move once he gets the [[Crystal Stars|Sapphire Star]] after defeating [[Cortez]] in Chapter 5. This move requires 5 [[Star Power|SP]] to use.
==Effects==
Sweet Feast is an upgraded [[Sweet Treat]] and works the same controls-wise. However, the icons for Mario, his partners, the flowers, and the [[Poison Mushroom]]s move by much faster. This time, there are larger icons that restores more HP or FP when struck.
