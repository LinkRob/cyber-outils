}}A '''Jawbus''' is an enemy found in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They resemble a blue wheel with four spikes and a joyous face in the center. It also dons a yellow and red flashing tail which is the enemy's weak spot. When approached, A Jawbus will push its "face" out of the center wheel and form a dinosaur looking face with a snapping mouth. Getting around the creature is futile as it can extend its neck. Only by flipping to 3-D and walking behind it to its still-Remaining weak spot you can defeat it. Its name refers to its large snapping jaw.

'''Catch Card Info''': Max HP: 5, Attack: 1. Count on a Jawbus to stick its neck out for you. Or at you.
