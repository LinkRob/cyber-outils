===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In this game, the Spring Jump can be used once [[Mario]] gets the [[Ultra Boots]] from [[Riverside Station]] in Chapter 6. To use the Spring Jump, the player must press and hold {{button|gcn|a}} and spin the control stick to have Mario jump very high. He is then able to grab onto pipes or knock of things off of high ledges such as keys and treasure chests.
