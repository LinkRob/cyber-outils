'''Fruit Factory''' is a [[Transfer Pak]] minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]. It plays an arrangement of the overworld theme from ''Yoshi's Story''.

==Description==
The player plays as [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], who can earn [[point]]s by hitting the ball at revolving panels depicting either an [[apple]], a [[banana]], or a [[melon]], worth one, three, and five points, respectively.
