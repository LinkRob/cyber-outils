'''Merluvlee''' is a recurring character in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. She is a fortune teller from the Ancients.

== In ''[[Paper Mario]] ''==
Merluvlee is located in a [[Merluvlee and Merlow's House|house]] at [[Shooting Star Summit]] along with her brother, [[Merlow]]. As a fortune teller, she can tell you the location of [[Super Block]]s, [[Badge]]s, [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]]. However, Star Pieces obtained by answering [[Chuck Quizmo]]'s quizzes, or by doing [[Koopa Koot]]'s favors are "out of her reach" and she gives you a refund as she can only tell the locations of Star Pieces that are hidden or under a panel.

{{Q|She's a gorgeous lady named Merluvlee. I can't believe I'm in the same room with her! She foresees events and uses her magic powers to find what you're looking for. She's very accurate. I think I'm in love... She's incredible! Pinch me, will you, Mario?|Tattle}}

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]] '' ==
[[File:MerluvleePMTTYD.png|thumb|139x139px]]
Merluvlee only has the ability to locate Star Pieces and [[Shine Sprite]]s, and no longer does this for Super Blocks and Badges. In addition, Merluvlee can also tell the heroes where they should go.

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]] '' ==
Merluvlee lives in [[Flipside]], and only has the ability to tell the player where the heroes should go or what they should do next.
