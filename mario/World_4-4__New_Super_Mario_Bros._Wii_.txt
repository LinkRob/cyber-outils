'''World 4-4''' is the fourth level of [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 

==Overview==
This was the first appearances of [[Invincible Mario]], [[Spiny Cheep-Cheep]], and [[Blooper Nanny]].
==Walkthrough==
This level begins near a [[Warp Pipe]], which takes the player underwater. [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Mega Cheep Cheep]] are found next to a [[? Block]]. The next section contains [[Spiny Cheep Cheep]]s, along with [[Blooper]]s and a [[Blooper Nanny]] in Warp Pipes. More Bloopers are found hiding behind scenery, along with a [[Red Ring]]. A Warp Pipe blowing a current of bubbles is before the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]]. Following the midway point, more Warp Pipe currents and Bloopers are encountered. A yellow Warp Pipe is found under a row of [[Brick Block]]s, leading the player to a bonus area, where one of only two [[Cheep Chomp|Cheep-Chomp]]s in the game can be found. The next section contains many Bloopers, as well as Warp Pipe currents, which can be disabled by a [[P Switch]] on the ceiling. A [[Starman]] can be found in a ? Block on the end of a row of Brick Blocks. Many Warp Pipes are found throughout the section, as well as ? Blocks that contain more Starmen if the player is [[Invincible Mario|invincible]]. A Warp Pipe is found at the end of the area along with a Blooper, taking the player out of the underwater area. The [[Goal Pole|flagpole]] then follows.

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Not long after the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], there is a [[P Switch]] on the ceiling. Mario and co. should activate the P Switch to both summon [[Coin]]s and stop the nearby [[Warp Pipe]]s from blowing wind, then follow the track of Coins to an upside-down Yellow Pipe. This Pipe leads to an area where the first Star Coin is floating high in the air. Mario and co. should hit one of the [[? Block]]s (both if not Super) to get a [[Penguin Suit]] and freeze the nearby [[Cheep Cheep]]. This gives Mario and co. a platform to jump from and collect the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Not far from the Pipe that leads from the area with the first Star Coin, the second Star Coin is floating in between two Green Pipes. Mario and co. must swim down to it while avoiding any [[Blooper]]s or Cheep Cheeps.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Just before the exit Pipe at the end of the level is a platform protruding from the ground. Inside this platform is a hidden area, which contains the third Star Coin.
