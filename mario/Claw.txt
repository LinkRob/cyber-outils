|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Claw''' is a special attack used only by [[Chow]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
The enemy charges at one of [[Mario]]'s party and makes a slashing sound.

==Effects==
With Claw, it does twice the amount of damage to a single character. This attack can be guarded against.
