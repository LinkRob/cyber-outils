The '''Mirror Bathroom''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Description==
The Mirror Bathroom is a bathroom in the [[Twisted Suites]], located on the eleventh floor of [[The Last Resort]], and is east of [[False Bedroom]]. It has a painting on the back wall reflected in the mirror.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3''==
When entering the Mirror Bathroom, [[Luigi]] must use the [[Dark-Light Device]] on the painting reflected on the mirror so that it releases the yellow "gem". There is a hidden pressure plate near the tub, which reveals a [[treasure chest]] filled with [[Gold Bar]]s.

After the door ways were scrambled by [[Nikki, Lindsey, & Ginny]], Mirror Bathroom is instead east of the [[Bladed Bedroom]]. When Luigi enters the Mirror Bathroom again, he is attacked by three [[Oozer]]s, all of which Luigi has to vacuum into his [[Poltergust G-00]] before he can continue.
