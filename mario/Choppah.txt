{{Nihongo|'''Choppah'''|バズメット|Bazumetto}} is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They have orange spiked helmets on their heads. They have propeller-like wings around their necks, allowing them to fly. They appear in [[Honeybloom Galaxy]], [[Sweet Mystery Galaxy]], [[Honeyhop Galaxy]], [[Flip-Out Galaxy]], and [[Grandmaster Galaxy]].

== Trivia ==
* Its head resembles a [[Penkoon]] from ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.
* Choppah's German name is "Heli-Monty". Translated, it means "Para - Monty", which Coo incidentally is simple to [[Monty Mole]]'s name. Although, it is unknown if they are related or not.
