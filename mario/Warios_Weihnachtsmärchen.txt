'''''Warios Weihnachtsmärchen''''' is an 8-page comic in the June 1995 issue of the ''[[Club Nintendo (German magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' magazine in Germany. It is based on the [[Charles Dickens]] story ''{{w|A Christmas Carol}}''.

== Description ==
The story stars [[Wario]] and takes place during [[Christmas]] season, where runs his own shop that sells [[bomb]]s, [[Warios Bombenladen]]. Wario's only employee is [[Bomberman]], whom he mistreats. [[Kid Klown]] enters the shop, wanting to purchase a "surprise bomb." Bomberman asks Wario what he should give the customer. Wario takes Kirby, who is imprisoned in a small cage, paints him black, and wraps Kirby into a small present. When Kid Klown gets home and realizes that Kirby is in the present, both of them, including Kirby, are furious with Wario. As Bomberman works up the confidence to request days off, Wario becomes furious at Bomberman and fires him.

When Wario lies down in his bed and goes to sleep in the evening, the [[Geist der Vergangenheit|Ghost of Past]], who resembles [[Toad (character)|Toad]], appears. Wario is deeply frightened by the Ghost of Past, who shows Wario his past, where [[Baby Wario|Wario]] and [[Baby Mario|Mario]] are building sandcastles as babies. Wario is unpopular due to always being mean, whilst Mario is popular. The ghost also shows Wario and Mario at their graduation ball, where [[Princess Peach|Toadstool]] only dances with Mario because he was much nicer to her, even though Wario liked Toadstool a lot. In the present, Wario begins to cry, admitting to his nasty behavior, when the ghost of the past disappears.

Wario is next met by the [[Geist der Gegenwart|Ghost of Present]], who is actually [[Link]]. He shows Wario what the other characters think of him today. Bomberman is seen sitting alone in front of his Christmas tree, crying and complaining that he cannot afford any Christmas presents. Wario is also shown Kirby and Kid Klown, both of whom are angrily throwing darts at a poster of Wario's face. Wario claims that he did not intend for the events to occur and that he only wanted to set things right.

Wario is lastly visited by the [[Geist der Zukunft|Ghost of Future]], who resembles the Grim Reaper. The Ghost of Future shows a vision, there are two children ask the salesman if they have ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' games. The salesman simply asks what they want to do with this mean guy that nobody can stand, to which the two children agree and ask for the latest ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game instead. Wario is then shown a horrific vision of a cemetery, where there is an abandoned and neglected tombstone of him on the hill. The Ghost of Future advises Wario to change his fate by starting right now to prove to the others that he is a good person.
[[File:Warios Weihnachtsmärchen Christmas.png|thumb|250px|A reformed Wario shouts "Merry Christmas!" with the others at Mario's christmas party]]
Wario rushes off immediately, angry at himself and admitting to his wrongdoings. Wario vows to set things right and sets off to make amends. Wario goes into his bomb shop, where he finds Bomberman and tells him that he may not only work for him again but also receive off days. Wario leaves behind the beaming and thankful Bomberman and heads off to see Kid Klown and Kirby, whom he brings home-baked cookies to. Lastly, Wario goes to see Mario at the latter's [[Mario Bros. House|home]], having no one to celebrate Christmas with. When Wario knocks on the door, he is greeted by Mario, who is dressed as [[Santa Claus]]. Wario admits to Mario about his bad behavior and apologizes. Wario is forgiven by Mario, who invites Wario into his home to celebrate Christmas with [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], Toadstool, [[Luigi]], [[Donkey Kong]], and [[Diddy Kong]]. Wario and the others are last seen shouting "Merry Christmas!" as they enjoy their Christmas together.
