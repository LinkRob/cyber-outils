'''Hulu''' is a female [[bamboo dancer]] who appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. She runs a dance school called [[Dance Stage]] in the [[Jungle Area]] of [[Shroom City]], along with two [[Spear Guy]]s.

For her quest, [[Jungle Jive]], the player's character has to follow her lead while dancing. If done successfully, Hulu gives the player the [[Mini Bowling]] [[Gaddget]].
