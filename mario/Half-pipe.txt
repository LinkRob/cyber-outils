'''Half-pipes''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

==Description==
A racer can drive off the side of a half-pipe to perform a [[trick]] in the air to gain a brief speed boost upon landing.
