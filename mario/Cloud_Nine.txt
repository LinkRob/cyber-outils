'''Cloud Nine''' is a special move usable by [[Lakilester]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. The move becomes available once Lakilester has reached the [[Super Rank]]. Cloud Nine requires 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Usage==
To use Cloud Nine, the player must press the {{button|n64|a}} button at the right moments to increase the number of turns. With Cloud Nine in effect, [[Mario]]'s evasiveness increases allowing enemies to sometimes miss him. This can be useful against a horde of strong enemies and is recommended against the [[Anti Guy]]s.

==Trivia==
*Cloud Nine is one of four partner moves in ''Paper Mario'' to affect Mario directly, the other three being [[Lady Bow]]'s [[Outta Sight]], [[Watt]]'s [[Turbo Charge]], and [[Sushie]]'s [[Water Block]].
