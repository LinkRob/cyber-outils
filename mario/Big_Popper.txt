'''Big Popper''' is a single-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place on a flat, horizontal plane with a wall on each side. [[Princess Peach's Castle]] is visible in the distant background.

==Opening==
The player's character is standing in the center of the area as a [[bubble]] descends. They immediately walk over and fan the bubble into the [[Spike (Mario Party Advance)|spike]], popping it.

==Gameplay==
The player has to pop bubbles by using a fan to make the bubbles move to the spike to earn [[point]]s. Fanning more bubbles into the spikes consecutively causes the point multiplier displayed on the spiked wheel to increase, going from "×1" to "×3" to "×5" to "×7" and finally to "×10," though this decreases over time if more bubbles are not popped before. If a bubble hits the ground, the player loses ten points. After one minute has passed, the minigame ends.

In the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the goal is to earn 70 points, while in [[Mini-Game Attack]], the goal is to reach 200 points. If the player does not reach the required number of point on time, they lose.

In Play Land mode, over time, the sky turns orange, where more bubbles fall faster. As time passes, more bubbles appear on the field; amongst these are red bubbles, which not only increase points if popped but also increase time.

==Controls==
* {{button|gba|Pad}}: Move left or right
* {{button|gba|A}}: Swing fan

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules:''' ''Fan the falling bubbles into the spikes. Pop 'em to earn points!''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The minigame title is likely a pun on the signer [[wikipedia:The Big Bopper|The Big Bopper]].
