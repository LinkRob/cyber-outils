'''Bomberland'''<ref>"[[Wario]] has stumbled upon the secret gateway into [https://bomberman.fandom.com/wiki/Planet_Bomber Bomberland]!" – Back cover. ''[[Wario Blast: Featuring Bomberman!]]''. [[Nintendo]] (English).</ref> is a location setting of ''[[Wario Blast: Featuring Bomberman!]]''.

==Description==
Bomberland is where the events of ''[[Wario Blast: Featuring Bomberman!]]'' takes place. It is inhabited by [[Madbomber]]s, including [[Bomberman]] himself.

== History ==
=== ''Wario Blast: Featuring Bomberman!'' ===
In his search for treasure and new lands to conquer, [[Wario]] finds an extra-dimensional portal connecting [[Mushroom Earth|his world]] to [https://bomberman.fandom.com/wiki/Planet_Bomber Bomberland]. Wario decides to go there to plunder the people and treasure. He is then surrounded against Bomberman and several Madbombers.<ref>1994. ''[[Wario Blast: Featuring Bomberman!]]'' instruction booklet. [[Nintendo|Nintendo of America]] (American English). Page 4.</ref>

==Trivia==
*Bomberland is the setting of the majority of the [https://bomberman.fandom.com/wiki/Bomberman_(series) ''Bomberman'' series].
