A '''Super Drop''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is a slightly more effective variation of the [[Mushroom Drop]] but is still surpassed by the [[Ultra Drop]]. Using a Super Drop restores thirty [[HP]] for all party members. Certain Item Shops sell Super Drops at 50 [[coin]]s each.
