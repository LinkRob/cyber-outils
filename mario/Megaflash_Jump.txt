'''Megaflash Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It is the strongest variant of the [[Jump]] sticker.

== Appearance ==
The Megaflash Jump is very similar to [[Flashy Jump]] stickers except the rainbow background goes horizontally rather than diagonally.
