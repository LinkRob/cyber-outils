'''DK Pass''' is a snowy track owned by [[Donkey Kong]] which appears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', as the first course in the [[Star Cup]]. It reappeared in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' as the second course in [[Leaf Cup]]. It is similar to [[DK Summit]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== Shortcuts ==
Before the descent down the mountain, there is a wide corner with a snowy mound on its inside. If racers head up this mound, they can collect an [[Item Box]] which always has [[Triple Mushroom]]s, a [[Star]], a [[Red Shell]], or just a [[Mushroom]], in it. The chances of getting a Mushroom or a Red Shell is pretty slim.

== Changes from ''Mario Kart DS'' to ''Mario Kart 7'' ==
*Wooden ramps added.
*A boost pad replaced with a gliding pad.
*The [[Item Box]] on a snowy hill having a less likely chance of gaining a [[Star]].
*It is snowier.
