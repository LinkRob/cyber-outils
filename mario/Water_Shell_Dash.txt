'''Water Shell Dash''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] used by [[Koopa Troopa]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Koopa Troopa goes into his [[Koopa Shell|shell]], moves over to the ball in a slide of water and hits it. Water Shell Dash can only be used if the tennis ball is at ground level.
