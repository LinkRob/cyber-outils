==''Mario'' game appearances==
===''Paper Mario'' series===
====''Paper Mario''====
[[File:PMFuzzy.png|frame|left]]
[[File:PaperFuzzyBoss.png|thumb|A Fuzzy from ''Paper Mario''.]]
Fuzzies appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', in which a large group of them inhabit the woods behind [[Koopa Village]]. Fuzzies attack by draining [[Heart Point|HP]] from [[Mario]], although by wearing the [[Zap Tap]] badge, Mario becomes invulnerable to Fuzzies. Outside of battle, Fuzzies move about by hopping, and can secrete webs from their bodies to hang from in a similar manner to {{wp|silkworm}}s or {{wp|spider}}s.

When Mario first reaches Koopa Village, he finds it under attack by a horde of Fuzzies who are harassing the [[Koopa Troopa]] inhabitants of the village. After Mario defeats the apparent leader of the Fuzzies, who stole the Blue Shell of [[Kooper]], the Fuzzies "promise" to behave and stop attacking the village, at least for a while. There is also a piece of [[Toad Town News]] that warns of the Fuzzies' invasion.

A Fuzzy can also be fought in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] if [[Princess Peach]] tells [[Bowser]] that a Fuzzy is one of Mario's worst fears. Afterward, [[Kammy Koopa]] warps a Fuzzy into the Toy Box to guard a [[treasure chest]] and defeat Mario.

Two variants also appear: the [[Forest Fuzzy|Forest Fuzzies]] of [[Forever Forest]], and the [[Jungle Fuzzy|Jungle Fuzzies]] of [[Lavalava Island]]. A similar enemy known as the [[Swoopula]] also acts and attacks like a Fuzzy. 

An [[Paper Mario/Beta elements|aqua-colored Fuzzy]] was intended to appear, but it never made it into the final version.

====''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''====
[[File:TheFuzz.png|thumb|The Fuzz fighting team ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''.]] 
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Fuzzies are semi-common enemies. They have the same parasitic method of attack as in the first ''Paper Mario'', which can now also harm Mario's [[partner]]s.

Fuzzies are first encountered in [[Shhwonk Fortress]], often within the underground sewers. A Fuzzy later appears as a member of [[the Fuzz]] fighting team in the [[Glitz Pit]]. Fuzzies also appear in the [[Pit of 100 Trials (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|Pit of 100 Trials]], on floors 1 through 9.

A few Fuzzy varieties make an appearance. There are [[Green Fuzzy|Green Fuzzies]] and [[Flower Fuzzy|Flower Fuzzies]], the latter replacing the similar Jungle Fuzzies from the previous game. Some more types of Fuzzies are introduced, including the mini-boss [[Gold Fuzzy]] and a large group of Fuzzies known as the [[Fuzzy Horde]]s.

====''Super Paper Mario''====
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Fuzzies appear as enemies on the [[Planet Blobule]]. Unlike previous titles of the {{series|Paper Mario}}, Fuzzies attack other creatures by bouncing into them, instead of draining HP. Besides [[Pink Fuzzy]], the game introduces the most lethal type of Fuzzy, the [[Dark Fuzzy]], which appears only in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]].

====''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''====
Fuzzies make another appearance in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. They are one of the few enemies in the game to retain their appearance from the first three ''Paper Mario'' games. They only appear in level 4-5, [[Whiteout Valley]], where they ride the ski lift bar, coming from the front and back. They often appear in groups of 2-4. Once a Fuzzy reaches Mario, it jumps up and falls onto him. However, Mario can avoid a Fuzzy by either swinging his chairlift out of the way or jumping to knock the Fuzzy away while it sits directly above his head. If Mario is [[frozen]] by an [[Ice Bro]] and a Fuzzy lands on him, the Fuzzy scores a [[First Strike]] on Mario.

In battle, a Fuzzy can only simply bounce and slam into Mario. However, if a Fuzzy is hit and not defeated, it multiplies into two Fuzzies, which have full [[HP]]. However, Fuzzies have very low HP, and most attacks can defeat them in a single shot.

====''Paper Mario: Color Splash''====
[[File:PMCS Fuzzy.png|thumb|left|100px|Fuzzy sprite from ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'']]
Fuzzies reappear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'', maintaining their appearance and attacks from the previous games. They once again attack [[Mario]] by bouncing on his head and by multiplying. A difference from before is that if there are multiple Fuzzies, they may also form a group attack, and in some cases perform a blowback attack, which causes more damage. However, by [[jump]]ing on a Fuzzy or hitting it with a [[hammer]] as a [[First Strike]] outside of battle, Mario can defeat the Fuzzy instantly without engaging in a battle.

Fuzzies can be encountered in [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Redpepper Volcano]], [[Toad Trainworks]], and [[Sunset Express]]. A Fuzzy is also the first-round opponent in [[Roshambo Temple 5|Roshambo Temple #5]]. Additionally, Fuzzy Stacks can be found in Redpepper Volcano and Toad Trainworks.

====''Paper Mario: The Origami King''====
[[File:OrigamiFuzzy.jpg|thumb|100px|An origami Fuzzy]]
[[Origami Fuzzy|Origami Fuzzies]] appear as enemies in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. However, Mario cannot enter battle with them, and they instead only act as obstacles in the overworld. Mario will lose HP if he collides with them, but can defeat them by hitting them with the Hammer.
{{br|right}}

===''Mario Party'' series===
====''Mario Party: Island Tour''====
[[File:Buzz a Fuzzy.png|thumb|Several Fuzzies in the minigame Buzz a Fuzzy in ''Mario Party: Island Tour'']]
Fuzzies make an appearance in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''. Fuzzies are most notably featured in the minigame [[Buzz a Fuzzy]], in which players have to use suctions to capture as many Fuzzies as they can, and whoever gets the most wins. A Fuzzy also appears as one of the tiles in the minigames [[Match Faker]] and [[Tap Dash]].
{{br}}
====''Mario Party 10''====
[[File:MP10Fuzzy.png|thumb|left|Artwork of Fuzzy in ''Mario Party 10'']]
[[File:Fuzzy Fliers.png|thumb|The characters dodging Fuzzies in the minigame Fuzzy Fliers in ''Mario Party 10''.]]
Fuzzies appear again in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. They are a hazard on the [[Airship Central]] board, where they can take away [[Mini Star]]s. Just as the previous ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' appearance, Fuzzies are featured in their own minigame, [[Fuzzy Fliers]], alongside [[Mega Fuzzy|Mega Fuzzies]], where they are obstacles that weaken the [[Lakitu's Cloud]]s used by the players' characters upon contact. Fuzzies are also a selectable enemy in the minigame [[Meanie Match]].
{{br|left}}
====''Mario Party: Star Rush''====
[[File:Dodge Fuzzy Get Dizzy.png|thumb|left|Toad spinning and dodging Fuzzies in the minigame Dodge Fuzzy, Get Dizzy in ''Mario Party: Star Rush''.]]
''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'' features Fuzzies as the primary obstacles of yet another minigame, [[Dodge Fuzzy, Get Dizzy]]. Fuzzies move in a rail, and the characters must spin past them to progress up the tower. If a character is hit by a spinning Fuzzy, they become stunned for a few seconds, which also provides a significant advantage for their opponents.
{{br|left}}

====''Super Mario Party''====
[[File:Screenshot 10 - Super Mario Party.png|thumb|Fuzzies in the minigame Fuzzy Flight School in ''Super Mario Party'']]
In ''[[Super Mario Party]]'', following a brief precedent set by the previous three ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' titles, Fuzzies are obstacles in another minigame of their own, [[Fuzzy Flight School]], where the characters must fly their planes around the Fuzzies to dodge them. If a character is hit by a Fuzzy, they slow down briefly.

A Fuzzy can also appear in [[Absent Minded]] as a selectable option.

===''Mario & Luigi'' series===
====''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''====
[[File:FuzzbushFuzzy.png|thumb|Fuzzies and a Fuzzbush in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''.]]
Fuzzies appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' as projectiles that are spawned by the [[Fuzzbush]]. Upon being spawned, Fuzzies slide along the ground at [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], who must jump over them. Unlike other Fuzzies, these can [[poison (status effect)|poison]] either [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bro]]. Unlike in other games, Fuzzies are colored purple.

====''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''====
While regular Fuzzies do not appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', [[Paper Fuzzy|Paper Fuzzies]] appear as uncommon enemies.
{{br}}
====''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''====
[[File:Fuzzbush Fuzzy DX.png|thumb|right|A Fuzzbush attacks with a Fuzzy in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'']]
Fuzzies return in the remake ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They have the same role in the base game but now utilize their common design, instead of appearing purple. Fuzzies are also commonly used by Fuzzbushes in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign. 

===''Mario Kart'' series===
====''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''====
In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', a Fuzzy appears on the logo for the sponsor [[Lists of sponsors|Fuzzy Battery]]. Fuzzies physically appear in {{classic course|Tour|[[Rome Avanti]]}} in the ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe – Booster Course Pass|Booster Course Pass]]'' for ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. They function identically to their ''Mario Kart Tour'' appearance.

====''Mario Kart Tour''====
[[File:MarioKartTour_Fuzzies.png|thumb|Fuzzies in ''Mario Kart Tour'']]
Fuzzies once again cameo in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' on Fuzzy Battery signs, which can be seen in [[Waluigi Pinball]] and the new [[New York Minute]] courses. They make their first physical appearance within the series in [[Rome Avanti|Rome Avanti R/T]] while they ride along tracks. They also appear inside a yellow pipe section in [[Piranha Plant Pipeline]] and in {{wp|Piazza Navona}} in Rome Avanti 3.

{{br}}
===''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''===
[[File:Fuzzy Under.png|thumb|Toadette walking by several Fuzzies in ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker'']]
Fuzzies appear in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' and its Nintendo Switch and Nintendo 3DS [[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker (Nintendo Switch / Nintendo 3DS)|ports]]. They can only be defeated using a [[Super Pickax]] or an [[Invincibility Mushroom]], and unlike most enemies in that game, do not react in any way to the touch screen / pointer interaction.

Fuzzies appear in the levels [[Clear Pipe Puzzleplex]], [[Biddybud Snow Stroll]], [[Poison Canal Cannon Run]], [[Shadow-Play Alley]] and [[Clear Pipe Cruise]].

===''Dr. Mario World''===
Fuzzies appear in ''[[Dr. Mario World]]'' as assistants. Their stage mode effect is to grant a chance that the first three [[capsule]]s will be of matching color, where both halves will be changed to the same color based on the left half's color. The effect has a 40% chance to activate on the first level and an 80% chance to activate at the maximum level. Their versus mode is to instead clear a column on the user's side of the stage if the opponent uses a skill. Two of them are rewarded as assistants, where each are obtained by clearing the first areas of [[World 22]] and [[World 29]].
