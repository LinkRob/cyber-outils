'''Sydney Sprint 1''' is a race track from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', first appearing in the Sydney Tour of 2021, which spanned from the 21st of April to the fourth of May. As its name implies, it is the first variant of '''Sydney Sprint'''.

== Layout ==
Racers on the course start in Port Jackson, facing north. The racers start with a sinusoidal right curve and a left turn through the aforementioned Sydney Opera House, tagging a glide ramp to cross the water.  They then go through a clockwise 270-degree turn, heading southwest down the Port Jackson Bridge.  They turn left at the intersection to the southwest, heading southeast down a road and turning right, heading south briefly before the road goes through a wide left turn, approaching the starting line from the south.

This route is used for the first lap of [[Sydney Sprint]].
