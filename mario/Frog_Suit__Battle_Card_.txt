|name=Frog Suit (Battle Card)}}'''Frog Suit''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Frog Suit is a defensive card that gives [[Mario]] an extra line of defense when it's the opponents turn. It is an empty green frog costume with a green/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 80 coins

Sell Price: 40 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Frog Suit is first seen in [[Sunglow Ridge]], but can also found in [[Fort Cobalt]] and [[Toad Trainworks]].
