'''Star Road''' is a plot-essential location featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is also the homeland of the [[Star Spirit]] named [[Geno]].

Normally, Star Road is where others' wishes are granted, in the form of a [[Wish Star]], but at one point, [[Exor]] shattered the Star Road, breaking it into seven [[Star Piece]]s that [[Mario]] and his party have to retrieve.

==Trivia==
[[File:Star World SMW map.png|thumb|200px|left]]
The Star Road from Super Mario RPG looks almost identical to the [[Star World]] from [[Super Mario World]], so much so that they even have the same background with half the screen covered by clouds and the other half by space and stars, with [[Mushroom Moon|the moon]] even on the same position on the screen. They both look like giant Stars with litle Stars at each point of them and also one at the center (The Last One).
