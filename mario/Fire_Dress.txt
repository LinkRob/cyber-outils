The '''Fire Dress''' is a type of armor for [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be obtained by either buying it from [[Hinopio]] in [[Barrel Volcano]] or obtaining it from [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]]. Equipping the Fire Dress to Princess Toadstool increases her [[Defense]] by 42 and her [[Magic Defense]] by 21, exactly half of the Defense.
