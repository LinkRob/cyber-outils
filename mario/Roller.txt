The '''Roller''' wheels are default wheels in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. They are the smallest wheels in the game. They improve [[acceleration]] and [[handling]], but hinder the kart's [[speed]] and [[weight]].
The Roller wheels return in ''Mario Kart 8 ''and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe ''where they now underwent a heavy aesthetic design; unlike in ''Mario Kart 7'', the Roller wheels have a red and yellow paint job.
