|effects = Allows Tiny to hover in the air.}}'''Pony Tail Twirl''' ('''''Roundum Roundus''''') is a special move used by [[Tiny Kong]] in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. Tiny can learn this move after buying a potion from [[Cranky Kong]] in [[Frantic Factory]].

==Usage==
To use Pony Tail Twirl, the player must press the {{button|n64|a}} button then {{button|n64|a}} again while holding it to maintain the move for a short time. This move is very useful as it allows Tiny to hover from one area to another. Aside from the final fight with [[King Krusha K. Rool]] and the second fight with [[Dogadon]] requiring [[Chunky Kong]]'s [[Hunky Chunky]], this is the only ability in the game that is required for a boss fight (with [[Mad Jack]] as it is needed to help Tiny hover from platform to platform).
