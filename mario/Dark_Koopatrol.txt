'''Dark Koopatrols''' are a [[Koopa Troopa]] variant and an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Like the [[Dark Koopa]] and its [[Dark Paratroopa|Paratroopa counterpart]], Dark Koopatrols are native to the dark tunnels of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. The Dark Koopatrols possess all the abilities of a regular [[Koopatrol]], but have a higher attack and defensive power. Their hit points are also more than three times that of a regular Koopatrol.

There is one named Dark Koopatrol known as "[[The Koopinator]]." He is the second to last fight in the [[Glitz Pit]], the fighting tournament during chapter 3 of ''Paper Mario: The Thousand Year Door''.
