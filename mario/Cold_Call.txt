'''Cold Call '''is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
At a random point, the phone will ring. The player must pick it up when it rings. The person's voice comes from the [[Wii Remote]].
*'''Level 1:''' The telephone is black. The telephone will ring.
*'''Level 2:''' The telephone is white. The telephone will ring. At a point, it will stop ringing.
*'''Level 3:''' The cellphone will ring. The player must pick it up and press the button to answer.
