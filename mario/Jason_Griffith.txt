'''Jason Adam Griffith ''' (born November 29, 1980) is a voice actor who was best known as the voice of [[Sonic]] in the ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic the Hedgehog (series)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' series. He also voices [[Shadow the Hedgehog]] and [[Jet the Hawk]] as well. He has voiced Sonic and Shadow in both ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' and ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]] ''and voiced them one more time in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]. ''But later in the year 2010, SEGA announced they were replacing the entire voice cast. He was replaced by [[Roger Craig Smith]] who now voices Sonic and was replaced by [[Kirk Thornton]] who now voices Shadow in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]. ''And he was also replaced by [[Michael Yurchak]] who now voices [[Jet the Hawk]].'' ''He is currently now on the Pokémon anime series voicing Ash' traveling companion Cilan.
