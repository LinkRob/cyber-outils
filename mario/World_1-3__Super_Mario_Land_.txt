}}'''World 1-3''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the third and final level of World 1, the [[Birabuto Kingdom]].

==Overview==
World 1-3 has a very distinct Egyptian-theme to it containing many hieroglyphics on the walls. This area also has narrow walkways with [[Warp Pipe]]s containing [[Piranha Plant]]s in them. There are also [[Gao]]s here that attack by spitting fireballs at [[Mario]]. Near the end of the level, Mario will fight his first boss of the game, [[King Totomesu]]. After defeating King Totomesu, Mario will proceed to find a [[Fighter Fly|Fly]] disguised as [[Princess Daisy]] and moves on to the next world, the [[Muda Kingdom]].
