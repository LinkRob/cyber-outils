'''Soul Bubbles''' are a stronger variant of [[Love Bubble]]s which appears as an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Soul Bubbles can be distinguished from Love Bubbles from having a cream-colored body and green eyes.

[[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]] encounter the Soul Bubble in [[Shroob Castle]]. Their attacks are faster than the standard [[Love Bubble]]; the only difference between attacks is that while Love Bubbles circle the brothers a preset number of times, the number of loops made by Soul Bubbles varies.

The Soul Bubble can heal other enemies in battle, just like the [[Love Bubble]]; however, the yellow enemies heal more HP than their original relatives. Also, the Soul Bubble can revive other enemies that've been defeated by the brothers.
