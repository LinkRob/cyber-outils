'''Ghost Piranhas''' (also called '''Polterpiranhas''' or simply '''Ghosts''') are enemies that appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They attack by trying to swallow Yoshi while flying in the air. They only appear in two [[level]]s, [[Hector the Reflector's Haunted House]] and [[The Cave That Never Ends]].

Ghost Piranhas are among the several Piranha Plant variants to receive a passing mention from [[Viridi]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

== Appearance ==
Ghost Piranhas are undead Piranha Plants colored cobalt blue with wings.

==Trivia==
*The Prima Guide sometimes refers to Ghost Piranhas as Polterpiranhas.
