"'''The Beauty of Kootie'''" is one of the episodes of ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It is paired with the segment "[[Mind Your Mummy Mommy, Mario]]."

==Synopsis==
[[Wendy O. Koopa|Kootie Pie]] transforms into a human in another scheme to stop [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]].
