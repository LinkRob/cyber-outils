'''Deep-Black Jungle Drift''' is the third [[level]] of [[World Mushroom (Super Mario 3D World)|World Mushroom]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Features==
The level is a shorter remix of [[Deep Jungle Drift]]. It looks nearly pitch-black and resembles a [[Ghost House]].
{{sectstub}}

==Green Stars==
*'''[[Green Power Star|Green Star]] 1''': Near the start of the area, above a series of chain-links.
*'''Green Star 2''': Floating above an invisible platform in the second area.
*'''Green Star 3''': Above the Warp Box leading to the Goal Pole. The player's character has to use the [[Light Box]]es and wall jump off the wall to reach it.
