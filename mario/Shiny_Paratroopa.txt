'''Shiny Paratroopas''' are an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Shiny Paratroopas are a variant of both [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s and [[Shiny Koopa Troopa]]s, and they resemble the former, but with an added sheen akin to those of shiny [[stickers]].

===Behavior===
A Shiny Paratroopa's only attack is dive bombing into [[Mario]]. If a Shiny Paratroopa takes damage during battle or a [[First Strike]] is landed on it on the field, it loses its wings, turning the Shiny Paratroopa into a [[Shiny Koopa Troopa]].

==In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''==
Shiny Paratroopas are only encountered in [[Rugged Road]].
