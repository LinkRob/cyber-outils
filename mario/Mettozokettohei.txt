The '''Mettozokettohei''' (pluralised as either "'''Mettozokettoheitachi'''," "'''Mettozokettoheira'''," or just "'''Mettozokettohei'''") is an enemy from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They serve as the footsoldiers of [[Zokket]]'s [[Zokket Army|army]].

== Description ==
Mettozokettoheitachi are small, grey [[Goomba]]-like monsters that resemble HDMI connectors and stand on two legs. Their faces have black eyes with yellow pupils. Some of them wear helmets that resemble silver cages.

== Behaviour ==
If a Mettozokettohei spots [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] in the field, it will charge.

Mettozokettoheitachi wear helmets to boost their defense; this helmet can be knocked off with sufficient damage, but a new one will be dropped down onto their heads after a few turns. One of their attacks is a charging attack done in two rows, and one from each lime will attempt to charge into the target.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyhWkdZGh00</ref> If a Mettozokettohei uses this attack, all Mettozokettoheitachi who have not attacked yet will lose their next turn. Another one of their attacks is charging into the target, which must be countered via hammer strikes. Without their helmets, Mettozokettoheitachi jump onto their target. However, if they have a helmet, they just charge.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrK4eqkLeVI</ref>

=== Statistics ===
As with other enemies that can be found in multiple locations, Mettozokettoheitachi have their stats changed depending on their location. On Lottacoins Island, the Mettozokettohei is of Level 15. It has 56 hit points, an attack power of 55, a defense of 18, and a speed of 66. When slain, the creature gives 29 experience and drops 12 coins, in addition to potentially a [[Nut]] (2%), Super Nut (1% chance), Lottacoins Lever (11%), or a Zokket Alloy (11%).

At the [[Color-Full Sea]]'s [[Great Lighthouse Islands|Great Lighthouse Island]], the Mettozokettohei is of Level 17, and its health, attack, defense, and speed are boosted to 68, 64, 21, and 71, and its death gives 39 experience, 14 coins, and a possibly a Nut, Super Nut, or Zokket Alloy, but the Lottacoins Lever cannot be dropped here.

On Daintye Islet, the Mettozokettohei's level is raised to 43, with 397 hit points, an attack power of 243, a defense of 97, and a speed of 224, giving 43 experience and 5 coins when slain along with possibly a Nut, Super Nut, or Zokket Alloy.

== Variants ==
Mettozokettoheitachi have several variants.

=== Togezokettohei ===
{{Infobox Enemy|name=Togezokettohei|first=''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''|affiliates=[[Zokket]]<br>[[Zokket Army]]|leader=[[Zokket]]|species=Mettozokettohei|location=[[Lottacoins Island]] (as minibosses)<br>[[Great Lighthouse Islands]] of the [[Color-Full Sea|Color-Full]], [[Brrrning Sea|Brrrning]], and [[Gulchrock Sea|Gulchrock Sea]]s<br>[[Fortress Zokket]]|defeated=Any non-jumping attacks (with sword held up)<br>Any attacks (with sword held down)|attacks=Sword charge<br>Stab}}
A red variant with a jack plug-like arm used as a sword can be encountered. They are called "Togezokettoheitachi" (Togezokettohei in the singular) can be found in the Great Lighthouses of the Color-Full and Brrning Seas, as well as Lottacoins Island and Fortress Zokket. These enemies use their spikes to stab their target in one of their attacks. This must be blocked with a hammer. Another attack has them charge at their target multiple times not unlike [[Big Massif]]'s [[Hooraw]] disciples in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', though this is more akin to the [[Sorrow Fist]], as the Togezokettoheitachi raise their jack plug sword upward for the first few charges, and then lower the sword the last time they charge. At low health, Togezokettoheitachi stab their swords upward, [[Spiny (Enemy type)|countering jump attacks]].

As with other enemies that appear in multiple locations, Togezokettoheitachi have their stats changed at several points depending on their location. The first encounters with Togezokettoheitachi function like minibosses, putting them at Level 16 with a health maximum of 113, an attack power of 57, a defense of 466, and a speed of 50, giving 66 experience and 25 coins along with a [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]] (3%), Super Mushroom (1%), or Lottacoins Lever (11%). Later encounters have them function as common enemies, with the ones in the Great Lighthouse Island of the Color-Full Sea being Level 18 monsters with 136 hit points, an attack power of 66, a defense of 54, and a speed of 58, giving 88 experience and 29 coins along with the possibility of a Super Mushroom, Ultra Mushroom, or Zokket Alloy. In the [[Brrrning Sea]]'s Great Lighthouse Island, the Togezokketohei is of Level 26, with 2533 hit points, an attack power of 106, a defense of 94, and a speed of 7, and gives 223 experience and 49 coins along with the possibility of a Super Mushroom (3%), Ultra Mushroom (1%), or a Zokket Alloy (26%) when slain. At the [[Gulchrock Sea]]'s Great Lighthouse Island, Togezokketoheitachi are of Level 34, with 432 hit points, an attack power of 158, a defense of 149, and a speed of 123, and their deaths give 556 experience and 76 coins. along with the possibility of a Super Mushroom, Ultra Mushroom, or Zokket Alloy. In Fortress Zokket during the Gulchrock Sea chapter, they are of Level 35, with 457 hit points, an attack power of 165, a defense of 157, and a speed of 128, and when slain, they give 600 experience, 80 coins, and possibly a Super Mushroom or Ultra Mushroom. On Shrimpee Islet, they are of Level 40 and have 612 hit points, an attack power of 204, a defense of 168, and a speed of 154, giving 678 experience, 101 coins, and possibly a Super Mushroom, Ultra Mushroom, or Zokket Alloy. On Daintye Islet, they are of Level 43, with 712 hit points, an attack power of 231, a defense of 194, and a speed of 170, giving 613 experience and 86 coins when slain along with the same non-coin loot drops as in the Gulchrock Sea's Great Lighthouse Island. During the second visit to fortress zokket, they are of Level 38, with 544 hit points, an attack power of 187, a defense of 182, a speed of 143, and rewards of 594 experience, 92 coins, and possibly a Super Mushroom, Ultra Mushroom, or Zokket Alloy (16%).

=== Jettozokettohei ===
{{Infobox Enemy|name=Jettozokettohei|first=''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''|affiliates=[[Zokket]]<br>[[Zokket Army]]|leader=[[Zokket]]|species=Mettozokettohei|location=[[Fortress Zokket]]|attacks=Stomp<br>Spike ball drop<br>Swoop|defeated=any jumping-based attacks}}
There is also an aerial variant with arms instead of legs and a jetpack. Specimina of this variant of Mettozokettohei have the name "Jettozokettohei" ("Jettozokettoheitachi" or "Jettozokettoheira" in the plural), and since they have jet packs, they [[Flying (Enemy type)|cannot be hit by ground-based weapons]]. Jettozokettoheitachi can drop spiked balls on their target; this must be blocked with a hammer strike and is followed by the Jettozokettohei charging the target and having to be repelled itself. Another attack has the Jettozokettohei performing a stomp attack, which must be repelled with a hammer strike.

=== Būmeranzokettohei ===
{{Infobox Enemy|name=Būmeranzokettohei|first=''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''|affiliates=[[Zokket]]<br>[[Zokket Army]]|leader=[[Zokket]]|species=Mettozokettohei|location=[[Great Lighthouse Islands]] of the [[Color-Full Sea|Color-Full]] and [[Brrrning Sea]]s<br>[[Bulbfish Island]] (as a miniboss)<br>[[Heatfreeze Island]] (as a miniboss)<br>[[Fortress Zokket]]|attacks=Boomerang Strike<br>Boomerang Double Curve|defeated=any attacks}}
There is also a white variant that wields a boomerang. They are called "Būmeranzokettohei" in the singular and "Būmeranzokettoheitachi"/"Būmeranzokettoheira" in the plural. When Būmeranzokettoheitachi throw their boomerangs, the Bros. must jump over the boomerang. If the Būmeranzokettohei leans back before it throws its boomerang, the boomerang will attack one Bro. and must be avoided entirely. Otherwise, the boomerang attacks the opposite Bro. who can stomp on it to send it back after the first Bro. dodges it.

=== Ringuzokettohei ===
{{Infobox Enemy|name=Ringuzokettohei|first=''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''|affiliates=[[Zokket]]<br>[[Zokket Army]]|leader=[[Zokket]]|species=Mettozokettohei|location=[[Heatfreeze Island]] (as a miniboss)<br>[[Great Lighthouse Islands]] of the [[Brrrning Sea|Brrrning]] and [[Gulchrock Sea]]s<br>[[Fortress Zokket]]|attacks=Laser Ring Crunch<br>Laser Ring Salvo|defeated=any attacks}}
Ringuzokettoheitachi are a green Mettozokettohei variant with glowing arms. They attack by summoning a ring around their target. The target has to smash the machine travelling around the ring with his hammer, lest he gets blasted by a laser. Another attack summons vertical laser rings. The short, low ones must be vaulted. This is followed by a big laser ring that must be jumped through. Other Mettozokettohei variants can give them more moves by accompanying the Ringuzokettohei.

=== Reviving Mettozokettohei ===
In Monster Mania: Level 5, a stronger version of the regular Mettozokettohei can be fought. These must be beaten at once, summoning reinforcements to attack should one survive. The [[Monster Maniac]] states that these Mettozokettoheitachi consumed too many [[1-Up Mushroom]]s before the fight, implying that their reinforcement summon ability simulates a resurrection power.

These Mettozokettoheitachi are of Level 42 with 994 hit points, an attack power of 240, a defense of 93, and a speed of 218, and their deaths give 170 experience and 40 coins.

=== Zetsuentaizokettoheitachi ===
All five versions have Glohm-infused variants.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Mettozokettoheitachi are named after their allegiance to [[Zokket]], with the "hei" part of their name coming from the Japanese word for "soldier"; as such, the ''zokkettohei'' (written ゾケットへい) part of their name literally means "Zokket Trooper." The origin of the "metto" (written メット) part still has yet to be revealed, but it is implied to come from the second syllable of "helmet" (referencing the fact that they, or at least the regular version, wear helmets; this is also similar to the [https://megaman.fandom.com/wiki/Mettaur Mettaurs] from the ''Rockman'' games, whose name's first syllable comes from the second syllable of "helmet.").

Togezokettoheitachi have a Japanese translation of "spike" (''toge''; written トゲ) in their name replacing ''metto''; this is a reference to the jack plug sword they hold.

Jettozokettoheitachi have a Japanese transliteration of "jet" (''jetto''; written ジェット) replacing ''metto'' in their name. This references the jetpacks they use to fly.

Būmeranzokettoheitachi have a rough transliteration of "boomerang" (''būmeran''; written ブーメラン) into Japanese in their name replacing ''metto''. This references the boomerangs they use to attack their prey.

Ringuzokettoheitachi have a Japanese transliteration of "ring" (''ringu''; written リング) in the name replacing ''metto'', referencing how they can summon rings to attack their prey.

== Nomenclature ==
=== As a whole ===
In the Chinese dub, Mettozokettoheitachi are litterally called "Zokket Troopers" (''Zhuókǒngzuò Bīng'', written 卓孔座兵). Their Spanish name is ''Shockdado'', which comes from a pun on ''Shocke'' (corruption of ''choque'' ("shock"), which [[Zokket]] has as his Spanish name) and ''soldado'' ("soldier"). Mettozokettoheitachi are called "''Zoldado''" in the Portuguese dub, from ''soldado'' ("soldier"). In Italian, they are called "''Skagnozzo''," from Zokket's Italian name ''Skonnet'' (which in turn comes from the verb (''di'')''sconéttere'' ("to disconnect") and a corruption of ''scagnozzo'' ("follower," "minion," "lackey"). In French they have the name ''Voldat'', a pun on "volt" (a measurement of voltage) and ''soldat'' (French for "soldier"). In Dutch, Mettozokettoheitachi are called ''Shoqx-strijderen'', a calque of their Chinese name. German calls them ''Stekselen'' (singular ''Steksel''; from ''Stekdov'' (Zokket's German name)).

=== Regular Mettozokettohei ===
In the Chinese dub, the regular Mettozokettohei is called ''Gāngkuī Zhuókǒngzuò Bīng'' (written 钢盔卓孔座兵), which literally means "Helmet Zokket Trooper." In Spanish, it is referred to as ''Shockdado Casco'', wich ''casco'' meaning "helmet." In Italian, it is called ''Skognozzo Elmo'', with ''Elmo'' meaning "helmet." In French, its name is ''Voldat Casqué'', which means "helmeted Mettozokettohei." Dutch translates the enemy's name as ''Shoqx-helmstrijder'', literally "Zokket Helmet Trooper." (''Helms'' means "helmet.") In German, the enemy is called ''Kappsteksel'', from ''Kapp'' ("cap," "hat") and ''Steksel'' (referring to Mettozokettoheitachi as a whole).

=== Togezokettohei ===
In Chinese, Togezokettoheitachi are literally called "Spike Zokket Troopers," (''Jiāncì Zhuókǒngzuò Bīng'', literally 尖刺卓孔座兵). Spanish translates their name as ''Shockdado Clavija'' (''Clavija'' is a translation of "plug," making the name literally translate as "Plug Zokket Trooper." The Portuguese translation of their name notes the jack plug arm being a spear for them to attack their prey, resulting in ''Zoldado Lança''. Italian translates their name as ''Skagnozzo Spina''. In French, they are known as "Mettozokettohei Swordsmen" (Voldat Éspéiste). Dutch translates their name as ''Sqox-steekstridjer'' (literally "Zokket Stab Trooper"). German translates the name as ''Stechsteksel'', with the ''Stech'' part coming from ''Stechen'' ("to poke").

=== Jettozokettohei ===
In Chinese, the Jettozokettohei is literally called "Jet Zokket Trooper" (''Pēnshè Zuókǒngzuò Bīng''; written 喷射卓孔座兵), and its Spanish, Portuguese, and German names are calques of this:
*Spanish: ''Shockdado Propulsor''
*Portuguese: ''Zoldado Jato''
*German: ''Strahlsteksel''

In Italian, French, and Dutch, it gets less-direct translations, resulting in ''Skagnozzo a Razzo'' ("Mettozokettohei to Rocket"), ''Voldat Flottant'' ("Floating Mettozokettohei"), and ''Shoqx-zweefstrijder'' ("Zokket Hover Trooper").

=== Būmeranzokettohei ===
The Būmeranzokketohei's Chinese name is ''Huíxuánbiāo Zhuókǒngzuò Bīng'' (written 回旋镖卓孔座兵), which literlly means "Boomerang Zokket Trooper." It is referred to using a calque of this name in the Spanish dub (''Shockdado Bumerán''). In Portuguese, it is called ''Zoldado Bume'', with ''bume'' being a clipping of ''bumerangue'' ("boomernag"). Its Italian name, ''Skagnozzo Chakram'', incorporates the English word "chakram," which is a disk-like throwing weapon from India (referencing how it wields a circular boomerang). Its French and German names come directly from this, but with corruption on the "chakram" part, resulting in ''Voldat Achakram'' and ''Schockramsteksel'', respectively. Its Dutch name, ''Shoqx-werpstridjer'', literally means "Zokket Throw Trooper."

=== Ringuzokettohei ===
The Ringuzokettohei is referred to as "Ring Zokket Trooper" (''Yuánhuán Zhuókǒngzuò Bīng'', written 圆环卓孔座兵) in the Chinese dub. It is referred to as "''Shockdado Aro''" in the Spanish dub, literally "Large Ring Mettozokettohei." In Portuguese, it is referred to using a calque of its Chinese name (''Zoldado Anel'', with ''anel'' meaning "ring"). Its Italian name, ''Skagnozzo Laser'', literally means "laser Mettozokettohei." French translates the enemy's name as ''Voldat Phospho'', with "Phospho" coming from English ''phosphorescent'' ("having the property of emitting light for a period of time after the source of excitation is taken away"). Dutch uses ''Shoqx-ringstridjer'' (literally "Zokket Ring Trooper").
