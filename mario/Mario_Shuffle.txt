'''Mario Shuffle''' is a mode in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. One or more players can play Mario Shuffle.

== Description ==
The player selects three characters on their team. The player can add their [[amiibo]], the character will bring a special item.

In Mario Shuffle, the player's objective is the move all of their three pieces to the other side of the board before the opposing team. Before the game starts, the game determines the team that starts with two teams rolling the [[Dice Block]]. The team with the highest number starts the game.

The player rolls the Dice Block. With the two numbers, they must choose one of the two pieces to move forward. If an amiibo has a special item, the player can press {{button|3ds|x}} to use it. However, the amiibo figure can use the item once. The yellow squares do not affect the piece. The blue space makes the player move forward with a certain number of spaces. The red space makes the player move back with a certain number of spaces. The "!" purple space sends the piece back to the start.

The first team to have all pieces at the end of the board wins the game.
