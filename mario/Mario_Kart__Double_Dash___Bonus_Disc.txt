The '''''Mario Kart: Double Dash!! Bonus Disc''''' is a bonus disc included with certain copies of ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It features five playable demos, one of which is of ''[[Mario Party 5 Demo|Mario Party 5]]'', and six videos of non-''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. By using a [[Nintendo GameCube Game Boy Advance Link Cable]] to connect to ''Fire Emblem'' for [[Game Boy Advance]], 14 exclusive items and two songs can be unlocked within the game.

== Demos Included ==

=== ''Playable demos'' ===

* ''[[Mario Party 5 Demo|Mario Party 5]]''
* ''[[F-Zero]] GX''
* ''Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles''
* ''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron III'' 
* ''Sonic Heroes'' 

=== ''Videos'' ===

* ''1080° Avalanche''
* ''Pokémon Colosseum''
* ''Kirby Air Ride (also included a Kirby: Right Back At Ya! promo)''
* ''Harry Potter: Quidditch World Cup''
* ''Spongebob Squarepants: Battle for Bikini Bottom''
* ''NBA Live 2004''

=== Bonus Features ===

* Fire Emblem (requires the [[Nintendo GameCube Game Boy Advance Link Cable]] and a [[Game Boy Advance]] to use)
