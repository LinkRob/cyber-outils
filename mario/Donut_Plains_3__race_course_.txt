'''Donut Plains 3''' is the first course in [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. It is the last of the three Donut Plains ([[Donut Plains 1]] and [[Donut Plains 2]]). Like the previous two, it is based on the [[Donut Plains (world)|Donut Plains]] from ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Layout==
[[Monty Mole]]s appear as obstacles on the track.
[[File:GBA DP3.png|thumb|200x200px]]

===''[[Mario Kart 8]]''/''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe|Deluxe]]''===
In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', in the [[Banana Cup]], with remastered graphics and remixed music. The racer can resist the water unlike other games. It is also a [[Battle Mode|battle]] round.[[File:RosalinaMK8DXDP3.PNG|thumb|130x130px|left]]

=== ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' ===
This track returns in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', with the Monty Moles removed, as part of Extra [[Star Cup]].
