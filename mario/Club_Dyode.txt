'''Club Dyode''' is major a location visited late in [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. It is a dance hall located on [[Jellyfish Island]].

== Description ==
Hence its host island's name, Club Dyode looks like a jellyfish, being pink and bulbous with pipes resembling tentacles on the icon of the island. A palm tree, star, and cup with a straw in it can be seen on the dance hall's "dome."

== History ==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] come here after [[Doctor Vulko]] recommends consulting [[Adaphne]] for help breaking through [[Fortress Zokket]]'s barrier. However, [[Club Dyode Ticket|tickets]] are required for entry. Mario and Luigi dance with [[Groovemaster Dyode]], who hands the tickets over when they complete a dancing minigame. Mario and Luigi enter the club with the tickets, whereupon Adaphne appears on a floaty in the pool, much to a staff member's annoyance. Adaphne is later woken up when someone lands in the water so hard she gets woken up by his splashing. Mario and Luigi then help make some Jellyfish Pizza to satisfy her cravings and win her heart and aid.

The club is one of the few locations that is not affected after [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|hijacks Concordia]] after the [[Stormstar Sea]] chapter.
