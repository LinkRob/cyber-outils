The '''Crawly Kart''' is a high-end kart in [[Mario Kart Tour]] that was added with the 2020 [[Halloween Tour]]. It provides a Slipstream boost allowing for better combos and a longer boost time.


== Description ==
The Crawly Kart is a rather large black kart with yellow stripes and parts being its "legs". It represents a big spider, possibly referencing the [[Scuttlebug]]. The emblem of the driver driving it is located near the exhaust pipes and on the cars bonnet. It has two car lights on the front and has a for arachnids typical large "abdomen" with black and yellow stripes on the back. The seat the driver sits on is orange with a pattern of a cobweb on it.
