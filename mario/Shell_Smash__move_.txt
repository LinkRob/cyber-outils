'''Shell Smash''' is [[Koopa Troopa]]'s [[Skillshot]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Upon initiating Shell Smash, Koopa covers the ball with a giant white shell and kicks it toward the goal. When the [[Kritter|Goalie]] blocks the ball, he becomes stunned, while the ball flies across the stadium. Unlike [[Dry Bones]]'s Skillshot, [[Shocker (Mario Strikers Charged)|Shocker]], the ball flies in a random trajectory. Shell Smash can be used even when far away from the Goalie. An advantage that Shell Smash has over [[Birdo]]'s [[Extreme Egg]] is that the shell can pass through characters and stun them without breaking.
