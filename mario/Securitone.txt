The '''Securitone''' is a character from [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']] who works as a guard in the Forbidden Layer.

== Description ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Securitone is a tall, black [[Fractone]] who has pink/blue eyes.

=== Personality ===
Like most of the [[Fractone|Fractones]] who live in the Forbidden Layer, the Securitone is worried about what lies ahead, so he angrily tells [[Donkey Kong|DK]] and [[Pauline]] to leave. However, he will accept offers of gold.

== History ==
The Securitone can be found guarding several checkpoints in the Forbidden Layer. Every time he appears, he demands a fee in gold. Upon being paid, the Securitone departs to the next checkpoint (if any), creating a region around his old post causing thrown chunks of terrain to leave behind Passage Stone trails which can be used as bridges. 

The first checkpoint has the Securitone charge 2000. At the second checkpoint, the fee jumps up to 5000. At the third checkpoint, the fee jumps up to 9999. There are also optional areas where the Securitone charges 1,000 gold.
