'''Yoshi Park 1''' is a course in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''. It is one of two courses in the [[Yoshi Cup]], the other one being [[Yoshi Park 2]]. Yoshi Park 1's design is based on both the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]] and ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.
