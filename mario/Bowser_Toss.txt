'''Bowser Toss''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. This minigame may have been inspired by how [[Mario]] defeats [[Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Gameplay==
Both players have 10 seconds to spin their Bowser dolls around repeatedly. The Bowser dolls are thrown when the 10 seconds are up. The angle and spin of the Bowser doll affect how far the Bowser doll is thrown. Whoever throws their Bowser the farther distance wins the minigame. If both players throw their Bowser dolls the same distance or not at all, it is a draw. If a player is not actively spinning their doll when time runs out, they drop the doll and "MISS" appears in place of their distance.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|Stick}} &ndash; Adjust angle
* {{button|n64|A}} (Repeatedly) &ndash; Spin Bowser
