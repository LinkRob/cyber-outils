'''Pyramid Scheme''' is a 2-vs.-2 [[coin]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in an Egyptian tomb-like setting.

==Gameplay==
Both characters on each team have to collect coins and the lesser-common [[Coin Bag|coin bags]] (worth five coins each) as they fall from above. Each team has to try and collect the most coins within a 30-second [[time limit]]. Each of the coins and coin bags have a shadow to indicate their landing spot. The only way for a character to lose is by not collecting any coins.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''Work with a partner to catch the coins raining down from above!''
* '''Advice''': ''The coins make shadows when they are about to fall. Bags are worth 5 coins, so catch them at all costs!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on the phrase {{w|pyramid scheme}}, referring to a type of illegal investment scam.
