'''Astro Goombas''', also called ('''Goombeetles''', '''Robot Goombas''', '''Metallic Goombas''' or '''Octobeetles''') are a type of [[Goomba]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are depicted as wearing metallic astronaut helmets, to prevent them from getting stomped on, red or yellow eyes, and Goomba-like bodies and legs. In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', Astro Goombas have both larger and brighter eyes.

===Behavior===
Like regular Goombas, a Astro Goomba attacks by charging at [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]. Astro Goomba cannot be defeated by directly [[jump]]ing or even [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]]ing them, as a Astro Goomba's protective helmet protects them from said attacks. A Astro Goomba can be defeated from a [[Star Spin]], followed by either jumping or Ground Pounding it. Alternatively, a [[Bullet Bill]] can be lured over to hit them. In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', the [[Co-Star Luma]] can knock over an Astro Goomba.

==Locations==
In ''Super Mario Galaxy'', Astro Goomba appear in [[Bowser Jr.'s Robot Reactor]], the [[Battlerock Galaxy]], [[Bowser's Dark Matter Plant]], [[Bowser Jr.'s Lava Reactor]], and the [[Dreadnought Galaxy]].

In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', Astro Goomba appear in [[Bowser Jr.'s Fiery Flotilla]], [[Beat Block Galaxy]] and [[Chompworks Galaxy]].
