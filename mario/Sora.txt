'''Sora''' is the main protagonist of the crossover video game series, ''[[wikipedia:Kingdom Hearts|Kingdom Hearts]]'' by [[wikipedia:Disney|Disney]] and [[Square Enix]]. The series pits him fighting alongside and against various Disney and ''Final Fantasy'' characters, including [[Cloud Strife]] and [[Sephiroth]]. Sora wields a Keyblade as his weapon of choice. Sora is the last downloadable fighter in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Profile==
Sora lived on [[w:c:kingdomhearts:Destiny Islands|Destiny Islands]] with his friends [[w:c:kingdomhearts:Riku|Riku]] and [[w:c:kingdomhearts:Kairi|Kairi]], Sora ended up embarking on a grand adventure the night his home was attacked by the Heartless. During this, he obtained a weapon called the Keyblade. After his world was swallowed by the darkness, he found himself in Traverse Town, where he met [[w:c:kingdomhearts:Donald|Donald]] and [[w:c:kingdomhearts:Goofy|Goofy]]. With the Keyblade and the help of his new friends, Sora protects the worlds from the Heartless and other threats presented by [[w:c:kingdomhearts:Maleficent|Maleficent]] and [[w:c:kingdomhearts:Master Xehanort|Master Xehanort]].

== Game appearances ==

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
On October 5, 2021, Sora was announced to be the last DLC character releasing for ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. He was released on October 18, 2021 with his home stage [[Hollow Bastion]]. According to Sakurai in Sora's reveal trailer, Sora was the real winner of the Smash Bros. Fighter Ballot back in 2015 for ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U|Super Smash Bros. for Wii U and Nintendo 3DS]]''; though the contest concluded with [[Bayonetta]] being added to the game, Sakurai stated that this was because Sora was not considered a realistic option at the time. He has costumes based on his appearance from ''[[w:c:kingdomhearts:Kingdom Hearts|Kingdom Hearts]]'', ''[[w:c:kingdomhearts:Kingdom Hearts II|Kingdom Hearts II]]'', ''[[w:c:kingdomhearts:Kingdom Hearts: Dream Drop Distance|Kingdom Hearts: Dream Drop Distance]]'', and ''[[w:c:kingdomhearts:Kingdom Hearts III|Kingdom Hearts III]]'', with an alternate appearance for his ''Kingdom Hearts'' design which resembles his look in Timeless River.

==Trivia==
* Sora is the third fighter in the ''Smash Bros.'' franchise to be owned by a company that does not specialize primarily in game development, after Banjo & Kazooie and Steve (all of whom are owned by Microsoft), that being The Walt Disney Company.
* Sora is the first character from a Square Enix developed game to be fully voiced in English, as Cloud, [[Hero]], and Sephiroth are only voiced by their Japanese voice actors ([[w:c:kingdomhearts:Takahiro Sakurai|Takahiro Sakurai]], [[wikipedia:Mitsuki Saiga|Mitsuki Saiga]], and Toshiyuki Morikawa), in place of their English actors (Steve Burton, Rasmus Hardiker, and George Newbern).
**Also, as his English voice actor, [[wikipedia:Haley Joel Osment|Haley Joel Osment]], is also a screen actor and has starred in several films, most notably ''[[wikipedia:Forrest Gump|Forrest Gump]]'' and ''[[wikipedia:The Sixth Sense|The Sixth Sense]]'', this also makes Sora the first playable character to have a "celebrity voice actor", as this is often known as.
**Sora is also the fourth Square Enix character in ''Smash''.
