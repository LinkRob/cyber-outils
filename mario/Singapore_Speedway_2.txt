'''Singapore Speedway 2''' is a race track from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', first appearing in the Ocean Tour from July 2022 to August of the same year. As its name implies, it is the second layout of [[Singapore Speedway]]. Singapore Speedway 2's route focuses on the Gardens by the Bay, which is located at the northwest corner of the city.

== Layout ==
Racers start in the same spot that they did for [[Singapore Speedway 1]], on the Esplanade Bridge, but when they get to the four-way crossing, they take the north path, gliding over the water. They can hit some fountains for [[Trick|Jump Boost]]s. When they reach the other side, the racers will pass between the [[Singapore Speedway 1|Marina Bay Sands]] resort's raised swimming pool and the [[Singapore Speedway 3|ArtScience Museum]]. They will touch down and go down an elevated path through the Gardens by the Bay. Conveyor Belts similar to the ones from [[Toad's Factory]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' can be found here. When the racers exit this section, they will pass through the Helix Bridge, passing the Float at Marina Bay. They make a right and then turn left.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the racers visit this route after they pass through the [[Singapore Speedway 3|Chinatown]] the first time on Lap 2, which sticks to this route thereafter (in order to facilitate the transition, when they approach the intersection from the south, they will continue north. The final lap also starts with this route, but rather than turning right to continue back south to the starting line, the racers turn left when they reach the Float at Marina Bay, switching over to [[Singapore Speedway 3]].

== Trivia ==

* In reality, the Gardens by the Bay would be at the southeast corner.
