'''Gondola Glide''' is a 2-vs-2 mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
Both teams of two characters each glide forward on their gondola, advancing through the first gate right as the mini-game begins.

==Gameplay==
The object for both teams is to reach the end first. Both teams have to repeatedly press whichever button appears onscreen (either {{button|gcn|A}} or {{button|gcn|B}}) to move their gondola faster, although pressing an incorrect button slows the gondola. Whenever a team and their gondola goes through a star, they have to start repeatedly pressing the other button. Whoever reaches the goal at the end first is the winner, but if no one gets there by five minutes, the mini-game ends in a tie.

==Ending==
The winning team's gondola pulls to a stop atop a snowy hill, then both characters on the team each perform their victory animation there.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|B}} {{button|gcn|A}} (tap repeatedly): Move forward
