'''Graveyard''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' where [[Bogmire]] is located at. This place is also the final area of Area 2.

==History==
===''Luigi's Mansion''===
When [[Luigi]] first comes here via the [[Boneyard]], Luigi must walk to a grave to summon [[Skeleton Ghost]]s to appear. He must suck the three in his [[Poltergust 3000]] then a strange glowing aura glowed around [[Bogmire]]'s grave. Luigi must walk to it and summon Bogmire to fight him. After the defeat of Bogmire, Luigi obtains the [[House Key]] to Area Three.
