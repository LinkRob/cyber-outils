'''London Loop 1''' is a race track that comes from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. Hence its name, it is the first layout of the [[London Loop]] course, which takes place in London, England.

== Layout ==
London Loop 1's focus is on the more metropolitan areas in London. The racers start by taking a right turn off the Westminster Bridge, where they will encounter a roundabout. After this, they take a left turn followed by a wide right curve. This is followed by a left turn. Afterwards, the racers will make a right curve through the Leadenhall Market, with an unused left path being blocked off by a fence. After the racers get through the market, they make a slight left and then a sharp right around a grassy path where a [[Chain Chomp]] resides. This area is followed by a drawbridge similar to the one in [[Mario Kart DS|DS]] [[Delfino Square]]. After the racers get through this section, they make a 90-degree right turn to stay on the road, going past the London City Hall. (There is another road that is blocked off but could otherwise be accessed via a sharper right turn; this belongs to [[London Loop 3]].) This is followed by a straightaway where the racers have a view of a Ferris wheel called the "London Eye," and a monument called "The Shard." The racers will find another 90-degree left turn, followed by a right turn not long after to get back onto the Westminster Bridge, where the [[Finish line|starting line]] awaits.

''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' has this layout for the first lap of [[London Loop]]. In this version, racers can perform [[Trick|jump boosts]] off the centre roundabout while going through this area. [[Arrow Field]]s replace the fences, as the racers would go through [[London Loop 2]] and [[London Loop 3]] later. Additionally, while no fence blocks off the left path in the Leadenhall Market, if the racers drive down this path, they will go out-of-bounds when they get far enough down the path, similarly to what happens if they decide to continue down the highway in [[Mushroom City]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.
