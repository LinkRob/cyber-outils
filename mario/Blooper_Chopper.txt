A '''Blooper Chopper''', named '''Division Blooper''' in PAL releases, is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
The item has the exact appearance of a [[Blooper]]. When a character uses a Blooper Chopper, they call on a [[Blooper]] to ink the opponent, halving the total roll amount of the opponent's turn. A Blooper Chopper has an opposite counterpart named the [[Starman|Super Star]], which instead multiplies a character's dice roll by two.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
Blooper Choppers are among the many items that exclusively appear in [[Perilous Palace Path]]. A Blooper Chopper can be won after winning a minigame and selecting it.

*Description: ''Halve the number your opponent rolls.''
