'''Perspective Mode''' is one of four extra mini-games in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
*Only one human player will play this extra minigame. The new view in this mode boosts the difficulty in 10 different minigames. There are some minigames have rules exclusive to this mode.
*Only one minigame will unlock at first, beat the minigame will unlock the next minigame. You need to get the first place in those minigames. You can't choose COM players or COM Difficulty, but it's much easier than Master.
