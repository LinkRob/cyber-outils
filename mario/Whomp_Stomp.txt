'''​Whomp Stomp '''is a''''' ​''Mid-boss Battle '''​mini-game from ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. In "Solo" mode, it appears in [[Bob-omb Factory]] at the mid-boss fort. In "Party" mode, it appears as one of three games that can be selected there, and has a 50% chance to show up at other boards' mid-boss fort. As its name implies, a [[Whomp]] stars as the players' opponent.

== Introduction ==
A [[Whomp]] that serves as the players' opponent is dropped onto a platform, scaring the player that he lands behind with his entrance.

==​Gameplay==
The minigame is a game with luck with some skill involved. You have to pick a number between 0 or 1. You have 10 seconds to decide. After the players pick their numbers, those who picked 1 will turn the lever, with each lever pulled rotating the platform. Afterwards, the Whomp will fall onto his face, crushing the character in front of him beneath himself; this takes away a point in later rounds if the target had at least 1 point and prevents them from attacking the Whomp. Players who did not fall victim to the Whomp's attack climb onto him and attack by pounding his back with [[Ground Pound]] strikes, earning 3 points per strike to his back.
===​''Rage''===

As with other bosses in the game, the Whomp will rage when his health falls below half. As Whomp rages, he'll add +1 or +2 turns by stomping on the number before the players decide on a number.

== Ending ==
The Whomp is shown lying on his face, exploding afterwards.

==​Rules==
''"Players each choose 0 or 1 turns of the platform. Try picking a number that keeps you from winding up under Whomp! Players left standing can [[Ground Pound|ground-pound]] Whomp for points!"''

== Trivia ==

* This is one of three boss minigames in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' where at least one player is bound to get hurt, due to the [[Whomp]] always attacking the player in front of him. The other two are [[Spike Strike]] and the four-player version of [[Chain Chomp Romp]].
** [[Mega Cheep Chomp's Shell Shock]] from [[Mario Party 10|''Mario Party 10'']] also has at least one player getting hurt.
