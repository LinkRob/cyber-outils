'''Doll Boy''' is a giant {{w|Daruma doll}} boss who appears in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Doll Boy is fought in [[The Volcano's Base]]. During its battle, [[Wario]] has to strike the [[barrel]]s that Doll Boy is standing upon when they flash. Once all of them are gone, Doll Boy turns green and begins moving around the area, attempting to hit Wario with its [[hammer]]s. If one hits Wario, he becomes [[Spring Wario]] that forces Wario out of the battle, therefore resetting it when he returns. Once Wario jumps on Doll Boy, he can then defeat it entirely.
