'''Red Coin Blocks''' are a type of object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==Description==
Red Coin Blocks are dark red blocks with a light pink [[coin]] symbol embedded on them.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
Red Coin Blocks appear in certain locations of the [[Dream World]], such as [[Dreamy Somnom Woods]] and [[Dreamy Wakeport]]. When a Red Coin Block is activated, eight [[Red Coin]]s appear, all of which have to be collected to spawn an [[! Block]].
