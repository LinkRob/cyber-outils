'''Gamewatch Boy''' is a line of miniature LCD wristwatches designed in the likeness of a [[Game Boy]]. They were produced by Hong Kong-based company [[Mani Industries]] during the 1990s. Gamewatch Boys are possibly based on [[Nelsonic Game Watch]]es as they feature a built-in game. Only games were produced as part of the Gamewatch Boy line of systems, both of which are ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' ones: ''[[Super Mario Race]]'' and ''[[Dr. Mario (Gamewatch Boy)|Dr. Mario]]'', released in 1992 and 1993 respectively.
