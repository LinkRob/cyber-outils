The '''Ostrich Elder''' is a character that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. She is one of the five Elders that can bestow the ancient power of [[Bananza]] upon [[Donkey Kong]] and she rules over the [[Forest Layer]] of the [[Underground World]].

==Appearance==
The Ostrich Elder is a very large and elderly ostrich whose feathers are mainly grey and black in color. The tips of her wings contains bundles of old, yellow bananas as well as a bunch of them she has underneath her beak. She also wears large, pink headphones and sneakers.

==History==
The Ostrich Elder is first seen where Donkey Kong and [[Pauline]] manage to make it through the toxic Forest Layer and check themselves into the Elder's [[Eggshell Hotel]]. The Ostrich Elder informs the two adventurers that an [[VoidCo|evil group]] was responsible for polluting the Forest Layer with poison which makes traveling around to stop them. The Ostrich Elder then lets them know that they will need her Bananza powers, but will need to find the pieces of her [[Ancient Record]] that was scattered throughout the Eggshell Hotel by [[Void Kong]]. After Donkey Kong and Pauline retrieve the pieces, the Ostrich Elder preforms her number and allows Donkey Kong to make use of her [[Ostrich Bananza]] form allowing Donkey Kong to take flight to avoid the poisons.
