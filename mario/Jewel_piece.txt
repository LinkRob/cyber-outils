'''Jewel pieces''' are an item in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
[[File:WL4 Crescent Moon Village jewel piece found.png|thumb|left|Wario discovering a jewel piece in [[Crescent Moon Village]] in ''Wario Land 4'']]
There are four jewel pieces in each of the regular [[level]]s. They are quarter pieces that form a complete [[jewel]] upon being combined. [[Wario]] has to collect every jewel piece in a passage so that he can fight the boss at the end. During a level, the player can check which jewel pieces they have obtained by pressing {{button|gba|Start}} to access the Pause menu. Before Wario has completed a level, the jewel pieces he found are gray on the pause screen, but after Wario completes the level, the jewel pieces are colored in.

The appearance of a jewel piece depends on which passage it is found in, including emerald jewel pieces in [[Emerald Passage]] levels, ruby jewel pieces in [[Ruby Passage]] levels, topaz jewel pieces in [[Topaz Passage]] levels, and sapphire jewel pieces in [[Sapphire Passage]] level. In addition, there are four purple jewel pieces in [[Hall of Hieroglyphs]] and four golden jewel pieces in the [[Golden Passage]].
