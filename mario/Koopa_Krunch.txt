'''Koopa Krunch''' is a single-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
Koopa Krunch features gameplay comparable to that of ''[[Tetris]]'' and ''[[Dr. Mario (game)|Dr. Mario]]'', namely from how the player has to match three identically colored [[Brick Block]]s to earn [[point]]s while preventing the structure from reaching the top of the grid. [[Green Shell]]s sometimes fall into the grid, and they take up the same space as a Brick Block. Green Shells can only be removed by using a [[Red Shell]] to knock out as many of them as possible.

In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player must earn 300 [[point]]s to win. In Mini-Game Attack, the object is to instead earn 1,000 points. In Play Land, the gameplay only ends when the Brick Blocks reach the top of the screen, meaning the object is to get the highest score possible. As time passes, the Brick Blocks fall down increasingly faster, and blue Brick Blocks begin to fall down the grid as well.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|Pad}}: Move blocks
*{{button|gba|A}}: Flip blocks
*{{button|gba|B}}: Flip blocks

==Flavor text==
*Rules: ''Connect 3 identical blocks to clear them. Clear shells with red ones.''
