The '''ColecoVision''' is a game system released by Coleco in August 1982 and by CBS Electronics in July 1983. It was the first game system to feature a home version of [[Donkey Kong (video game)|''Donkey Kong'']], which came packed with the system. It was followed a year later with the release of [[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|''Donkey Kong Jr.'']] for the ColecoVision. A separately-sold adapter allowed the ColecoVision to play games made for the [[Atari 2600]].

After three years, the ColecoVision was discontinued in August 1985.
