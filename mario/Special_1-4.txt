'''Special 1-4''' is the fiftieth level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
As with the Special levels thus far, the layout of Special 1-4 is designed after [[World 6-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 6-1]], though, the [[Cheep-Cheep]]s of the original level are replaced with [[Boomerang Bro.|Boomerang Bros.]] and [[Paragoomba]]s. The level, like World 6-1, features long bridges and tightropes that Mario must cross to reach the far end with the [[Flagpole]].

== Enemies ==
Enemies that appeared in Special 1-4:
* [[Boomerang Bro.]]
* [[Paragoomba]]

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] in the level is found on top of the largest rock at the very beginning of the level.
*'''Star Medal 2''': Once across the first large bridge, Mario can wall-kick the rocky wall to grab the second Star Medal.
