'''Spiky Goombas''' (also called '''Spiked Goombas''' or '''Spiny Goombas''') are enemies in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', in which they are named '''Spiked Goombas'''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Spiked Goombas are [[Goomba]]s with a spiked hat on their head.

===Behavior===
A Spiked Goomba's attack style is to jump on [[Mario]] and injure him using their spikes. They have an additional point of attack damage.

== In {{series|Paper Mario}} ==
=== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ===
Spiked Goombas appear on the path back to [[Goomba Village]]. They are the second enemy that Mario encounters, excluding [[Jr. Troopa]]. Spiked Goombas are underlings to the [[Goomboss|Goomba King]] but do not assist him in battle.

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
Spiky Goombas can be found [[Rogueport Sewers]] and in [[Petal Meadows]]. After [[Bowser]]'s underlings take over the Rogueport Sewers, Spiky Goombas are rarely found. On occasion they will appear in battle with a [[Hammer Bro]] or a [[Magikoopa]].

=== ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ===
They have been renamed back to Spiked Goombas, which reappear as rather uncommon enemies. They still cannot be jumped on, and the assistance of [[Pixl]]s such as [[Boomer]], [[Carrie]], and [[Cudge]] is required to defeat them. Bowser's [[Fire Breath]] is another effective way to take out Spiked Goombas. In one of his quotes, [[Professor Frankly]] calls them '''Spiny Goombas'''.
