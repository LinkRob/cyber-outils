A '''Lever Switch''' is an object in a few {{series|Super Mario}} installments. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
Like switches in general, when a Lever Switch is pulled, a certain event occurs as a result. Lever Switches can have a variety of different effects, depending on where they are located. Some examples of a Lever Switch's effects are either turning on lights in dark areas or causing entire [[platform]]s to move forward or backward.

In ''Super Mario Galaxy'', a Lever Switch can be activated by [[Star Spin|spinning]] into it. In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', a Lever Switch can also be activated either with [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s tongue or if the second player uses [[Co-Star Luma]] to spin into the Lever Switch. In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', a Lever Switch can be activated by throwing [[Cappy]] at it.
