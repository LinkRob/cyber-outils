A '''Marshmallow Block''' is an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
Marshmallow Blocks are a type of [[block]] featured in the sublevels beneath wooden and steel [[Trapdoor (Wario World)|trapdoors]]. Marshmallow Blocks disappear almost immediately upon being touched, so [[Wario]] has to be careful and agile while traveling across the platforms.
