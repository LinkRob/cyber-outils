'''Meanie Match''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a library and has a podium in the middle with a color wheel consisting of twelve colors. A total of fifteen enemies are present: a [[Shy Guy]], a [[Fuzzy]], an [[Urchin]], a [[Hammer Bro]], a [[Bullet Bill]], a [[Koopa Troopa]], a [[Dry Bones]], a [[Blooper]], a [[Huckit Crab]], a [[Waddlewing]], a [[Goomba]], a [[Cheep Cheep]], a [[Boo]], a [[Goombrat]], and a [[Bob-omb]].

==Gameplay==
The objective is to remember which colors the enemies are on and drag them to their appropriate colors. The order of play is randomly decided. If a character brings an enemy to the appropriate color, they move on to the next round, otherwise, they are eliminated. Whoever is the last character remaining is the winner. If none of the players drag an enemy within the [[time limit]], all of them are eliminated, and as such, the minigame ends in a [[draw]].

Meanie Match has an associated challenge named Perfect Placement, in which all four characters must drag the enemies to their correct positions without making any mistakes.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} − Move cursor
*{{button|wii|A}} − Select enemy

==In-game text==
''Grab an enemy with {{button|wii|A}}, and drag it to its place!''
