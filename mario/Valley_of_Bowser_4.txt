'''Valley of Bowser 4''' is the fifth [[level]] of the [[Valley of Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can access here once they have completed [[Valley of Bowser 3]].

==Layout==
The level starts with a [[Diggin' Chuck]] and a [[Koopa Paratroopa|Red Koopa Paratroopa]]. After that the player will find another Diggin' Chuck next to a blue [[Warp Pipe]] and a Yellow [[! Block]]. Then there is some [[lava]] and more Koopa Paratroopas, followed by a green Warp Pipe and a [[? Block]] that contains a [[Vine]]. If the player goes down the Warp Pipe, there will be an icy cavern (with one [[Dragon Coin]] in GBA version). a few Spike Tops and Cheep Cheeps that swim in the water. There is also a ? Block that contains a [[Super Mushroom]] or a [[Cape Feather]]. On the other side there is another Warp Pipe that backtracks to the blue Warp Pipe that they saw before.

Up the [[Vine]], the player will find a ? Block that contains a [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. The player can either choose to dive down the small pit, or to break the [[Rotating Block]]s to go down. There is a hidden [[1-Up Mushroom]] if the player chooses to go down the yellow blocks. Later, the player will find the [[Midway Gate]], and many more Diggin' Chucks.

There is a [[Key]] encased in a closed off receptacle inside of a wall just before the exit. The Key can only be obtained via Yoshi's tongue to grab it through the wall. The [[Keyhole]] leads to [[Star Road (Super Mario World)|Star Road]], [[Star World 5]], and the [[Front Door]] of [[Bowser's Castle]]. If the player chooses to go through the [[Giant Gate]], they can continue onward to [[Larry's Castle|#7 Larry's Castle]].
