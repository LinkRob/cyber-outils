'''I Hate Bananas''' is one of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player has to tilt the system to aim a crosshair at a [[banana]] on a person's head. They then have to shoot the [[banana]] by pressing {{button|gba|A}}. If the player shoots the banana, they win, but if they do not shoot it within the time limit, they lose.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' There are three characters, only one of whom has a banana on their head.
*'''Level 2:''' There are four characters.
*'''Level 3:''' Same as level 2 but the four characters are all walking.
