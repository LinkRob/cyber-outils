'''Elvin Parsley''' is a character in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Elvin Lives]]."

==Profile==
Elvin Parsley is a blatant parody of [[Elvis Presley]], both in appearance and from being the king of rock and roll. However, unique to Elvin is the fact that he is also the king of [[Sock Hop Land]].

===Physical description===
Like his real-world equivalent, Elvin Parsley has a black pompadour hairstyle. He wears a red jacket and black jeans. Elvin is constantly twitching, and he moves his hips around recklessly.
