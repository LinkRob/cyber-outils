'''Kooper''' is a major character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Koopa wears a dark blue shell with matching shoes and a red bandana around his neck.

===Powers and abilities===
Kooper can launch himself to reach far away objects and items

===Etymology===
Kooper is a pun on the common name "Cooper," but with the "C" substituted for a "K," similar to some other Koopa-related names.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
Kooper is a friendly Koopa Troopa who lives in [[Koopa Village]]. He is missing his [[Kooper's Shell|shell]], which had been stolen by a [[Fuzzy]]. Once [[Mario]] retrieves Kooper's shell from the Fuzzy, Kooper joins Mario's party as his second partner.

Kooper has four different shell-based attacks. He can retract into his shell and hit the first enemy. Much like the first attack, the second attack also allows him to retract into his shell and attack, although this attack will harm all ground enemies. While the first two attacks are default, the last two require the use of a [[Super Block]] to be used. After using a Super Block once, Kooper is able to use his shell to create a tornado to attack the enemies with it. If Mario uses the [[Ultra Stone]] and another Super Block on Kooper, he will have the ability to transform into a fire shell to do an attack to the ground enemies.

Kooper idolizes the famous archaeologist, [[Kolorado]], and leads his expedition crew to [[Shiver City]] in order to make it to the [[Crystal Palace]] after the events of the game. However, Kooper leaves the expedition after receiving an invitation to [[Princess Peach]]'s party from [[Parakarry]]. Kolorado also follows suit after getting an angry letter from his [[Kolorado's wife|wife]] who was upset at him for leaving without warning again.
