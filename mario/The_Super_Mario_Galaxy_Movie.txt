{{Nihongo|'''''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'''''|ザ・スーパーマリオギャラクシー・ムービー|Za Sūpā Mario Gyarakushī Mūbī}}, originally going to be called “Super Mario World”, is an animated film based on the ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' [[Super Mario (series)|series]] and a second entry in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|film series]] by [[Illumination Entertainment]] and [[Nintendo]], as well as a sequel to [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie|''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'']].<ref name="MAR10 Day 2024">{{Cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vfjmQlWRHgQ|title=MAR10 Day 2024|author=[[Nintendo of America]]|date=March 10, 2024|accessdate=March 10, 2024|work=YouTube}}</ref> The sequel was announced on [[Mario Day]], 2024. The animated film was released on April 1, 2026, is directed by Aaron Horvath and Michael Jelenic and is based on the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Super Mario]]'' [[Mario (franchise)|game series]].<ref name="MAR10 Day 2024">{{Cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vfjmQlWRHgQ|title=MAR10 Day 2024|author=[[Nintendo of America]]|date=March 10, 2024|accessdate=March 10, 2024|work=YouTube}}</ref>. Despite being a Mario themed movie, several non-Mario characters appear like [[Fox McCloud]], [[Mr. Game & Watch]], [[R.O.B.]] and [[Pikmin (species)|Pikmin]]. This movie marks the debut of [[Baby Toad]] in the series.

The film was mainly inspired by ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''<ref name=SepDirect>{{Cite web|date=September 12, 2025|author=Nintendo of America|url=https://www.youtube.com/live/tWIEv_aksvo|video-clip=5:16|title=Nintendo Direct 9.12.2025|publisher=YouTube|accessdate=September 14, 2025}}</ref> and has been described by [[Shigeru Miyamoto|Miyamoto]] as the "main event" of the [[Super Mario Bros. 40th Anniversary|''Super Mario Bros.'' 40th Anniversary]] celebration.<ref name=SepDirect/>

==Confirmed premise==
[[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] join up with [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]], [[Yoshi (Illumination)|Yoshi]], [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]], and [[Rosalina (Illumination)|Rosalina]] on a quest into the far reaches of space and across the galaxy in order to face against [[Bowser Jr. (Illumination)|Bowser Jr.]], [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]'s son who plans to retrieve and free his father from being shrunken and imprisoned.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Dunn |first=Jack |date=November 12, 2025 |title='Super Mario Galaxy Movie' Trailer: Mario Heads to Space, Meets Brie Larson's Rosalina and Benny Safdie's Bowser Jr. |url=https://variety.com/2025/film/news/super-mario-galaxy-movie-trailer-rosalina-brie-brie-larson-bowser-jr-1236574637/ |access-date= November 13, 2025}}</ref>

===End-Credits Scenes===
In the first post credit scene of the movie, Bowser (now as his Dry Bowser form), along with his son Bowser Jr. are in a [[Far-Away High Security Prison Facility|high security prison]], along with [[Lumalee (Illumination)|Lumalee]] (as the role of a prison officer) who says they shall look forward to "misery and death" during their stay scaring both father and son. There is also Fox McCloud leaving the facility (accompanied by a Toad before leaving) and saying he is about to "head home" after Princess Rosalina's mission. 

In the second post credit scene of the movie, a Ukiki tries to steal a suitcase from a female [[Whittle]], only to be punched by a white gloved hand. Then the Whittle thanks that person, who reveals to be [[Princess Daisy (Illumination)|Princess Daisy]], which may indicate that there may be a 3rd Mario Movie or another Mario character themed movie in the future starring Daisy as one of the main characters.

On March 10, 2024, in a celebratory [[Mario Day]] video, the sequel was officially announced to be in development at [[Illumination Entertainment]] with an intended release date of April 3, 2026. Aaron Horvath and Michael Jelenic will return to direct the film.<ref name="MAR10 Day 2024" />

On October 2, 2024, [[Keegan-Michael Key]], who voiced [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]] in ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'', gave vague details on the film's successor.<ref>"[https://www.mensjournal.com/streaming/the-super-mario-bros-movie-2 The Super Mario Bros. Movie 2 Features More Easter Eggs for Hardcore Fans]", article by ''Men's Journal''.</ref> According to Key, the movie will have a wider scope across the [[Mario (franchise)|Mario franchise]], with characters from older games appearing.

On May 13, 2025, it was accidentally confirmed that the film's sequel is named ''Super Mario World'', a reference and sharing the same title with [[Super Mario World|the video game]] [[Super Mario World (television series)|and the TV series with the same name]]. Unfortunately, it was later removed.<ref>[https://nintendoeverything.com/ NintendoEverything] (May 13, 2025). [https://nintendoeverything.com/super-mario-world-is-the-title-of-the-mario-movie-sequel/ Super Mario World is the title of the Mario movie sequel]</ref><ref>[https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/nbcuniversal-presents-2025-upfront-content-slate- NBCUniversal’s 2025 Upfront Showcases Expansive Entertainment, Sports and News Offerings | NBCUNIVERSAL MEDIA] Retrieved May 13, 2025. ([https://web.archive.org/web/20250514011753/https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/nbcuniversal-presents-2025-upfront-content-slate- Archived] May 14, 2025 via Wayback Machine.)</ref>

In July 2025, some Old Spice bathroom products were leaked, and the title change was revealed to be based on the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games. Many Reddit users debunked this as inauthentic at first, but the leaks were proven real once the title of the movie was officially announced and Old Spice registered trademarks for the names of the products in the leak. In October 2025, images of a commercial featuring [[Lumalee (Illumination)|Lumalee]] were leaked and it was revealed that Old Spice had withdrawn their trademarks for the products, possibly cancelling the partnership for unknown reasons. 

On September 12, 2025, Nintendo announced the movie title as "''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''." <ref>[https://deadline.com/2025/09/the-super-mario-galaxy-movie-sequel-cast-1236529859/ ‘Super Mario Bros’ Sequel Gets Title: Here’s Who Is Coming Back | Deadline Hollywood] Retrieved September 12, 2025.</ref>

The first trailer, released on November 12, 2025 in a special [[Nintendo Direct]] presentation, revealed the voice castings of [[Brie Larson]] and [[Benny Safdie]] as [[Rosalina (Illumination)|Rosalina]] and [[Bowser Jr. (Illumination)|Bowser Jr.]] respectively.<ref name="Trailer1">{{Cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yqjMaKQskW8|title=The Super Mario Galaxy Movie Direct 11.12.2025|date=November 12, 2025|work=YouTube|publisher=[[Nintendo of America]]|accessdate=November 14, 2025}}</ref>

On December 11, 2025, during the Game Awards, a teaser on Mario and Luigi fighting Bowser Jr. was shown.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jw6vtgE76gw|title=The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|Mario & Luigi vs. Bowser Jr.|date=December 11, 2025|work=YouTube|publisher=Illumination}}</ref>

The second trailer, released on January 25, 2026 in a special Nintendo Direct presentation.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_2YywtBYAaw|title=The Super Mario Galaxy Movie Direct 1.25.2026|date=January 25, 2026|work=YouTube|publisher=[[Nintendo of America]]}}</ref>

The third and final trailer was released on March 9, 2026 in a special Nintendo Direct presentation.<<ref name=MarchDirect/>
