'''Bath Bombs''' are an obstacle in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in the fifth wave of ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''{{'}}s [[Booster Course Pass]] and return in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''{{'}}s [[Vacation Tour]].

==Description==
Bath Bombs are obstacles found in the bathtub section of the [[Squeaky Clean Sprint]] course. They emit a rising cloud that pushes drivers upward, allowing them to perform a [[Jump Boost]], similarly to what a [[current]] allows for. Bath Bombs act as walls, so whoever drives into one is slowed down.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''===
In Squeaky Clean Sprint, Bath Bombs can be launched in the air by either driving into them or by hitting them with a [[Spiny Shell (blue)|Spiny Shell]] or a [[Super Horn]]. Bath Bombs are unaffected by other items, such as [[Green Shell]]s and [[Red Shell]]s, which either rebound off of them or break on contact.

===''Mario Kart Tour''===
Bath Bombs also appear in Squeaky Clean Sprint R. They generally function the same, but the differences that Bath Bombs have from ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' is the method in which they are taken out. Bath Bombs cannot be taken out if simply bumped into; instead, a driver either has to boost into it with a [[Mushroom]], ram into it while under the effects of a [[Mega Mushroom]] or a [[Bullet Bill]], or hit it with either a [[Bowser's Shell]], Super Horn, [[Giga Bob-omb]], or [[Super Bell]]. Drivers are awarded bonus points if they take out a Bath Bomb.
