==History==
Tinga runs the [[Inn]] of [[Flipside]] and is always seen with a smile on her face. The Inn Tinga runs used to be her family's mansion, but decided to remodel it to have others stay in it. She only charges 5 [[coin]]s per stay, restoring HP in the process.
==Trivia==
*Tinga's [[Flopside]] counterpart is [[Inga]].
