'''Keyzer''' is a sleepy ghost character who assists [[Wario]] in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. Keyzer has a [[key]] for a nose and lips, and uses it to unlock doors.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
[[File:WL4-Captain Steals Keyzer.PNG|thumb|left|A ghost steals Keyzer from Wario]]
Keyzer is found in all 18 stages, and it is always found asleep at first until Wario gets near, causing Keyzer to wake up. Keyzer then follows Wario around for the remainder of the stage, and even leaves into the [[Vortex]] with him at the end. Afterward, back in the passage, Keyzer travels over to the locked door and unlocks it, allowing Wario to progress to the next stage. However, Keyzer disappears after unlocking the door, and does not return in the same stage afterward.

[[Crescent Moon Village]] features a [[Ghost (Wario Land 4)|ghost]] who is interested in Keyzer. It tries to steal Keyzer throughout the stage, and even moves around faster while holding Keyzer to try and prevent Wario from rescuing it. Even after Wario rescues Keyzer, the ghost attempts to kidnap it again.
