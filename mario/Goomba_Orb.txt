The '''Goomba Orb''' is an [[orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. It is the successor to the [[Goomba Capsule]] from ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', and was later replaced by the [[Spear Guy Orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Orb Goomba - MP6.png|thumb|left|Screenshot]]
If an opponent lands on a [[space]] with a Goomba Orb, a [[Goomba]] appears and makes them give a number of [[Coin]]s to the owner of the space, which is determined by the [[Dice Block]]. The possible amounts are three, five, ten, fifteen, and twenty. A Goomba Orb costs ten coins to buy at the [[Orb Shop]], or five coins during a flash sale.
