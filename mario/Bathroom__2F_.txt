'''Bathroom''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Bathroom is very similar in design to the [[Bathroom (1F)|Bathroom on the first floor]]. On the left side, there is a bath on the left side with a shower head above it. The bath is covered by white shower curtains, which [[Luigi]] can pull back using his [[Poltergust 3000]] to reveal the bathtub. On the right side is a sink combined with a [[mirror]]. The only differences that the Bathroom's design has from the one on the first floor are the cabinet on the left wall and different floor tiles.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
[[File:Bathroom 2F curtains LM 3DS.png|thumb|left|200px|Luigi in the Bathroom in the Nintendo 3DS version]]
The Bathroom is first accessed in [[Area Three]]. When [[Luigi]] enters the room, he has to pull back the shower curtain, which depicts a silhouette of the bathtub, to reveal the [[Portrait Ghost]], [[Miss Petunia]], starting the battle. After vacuuming Miss Petunia into his [[Poltergust 3000]], a [[treasure chest]] containing the [[key]] to the [[Billiards Room]] appears.

A [[Water Elemental Ghost]] can be summoned from the shower head. During the [[blackout]], a [[Blue Twirler]] can also be encountered here.
