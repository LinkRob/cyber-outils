'''Roll Out the Barrels''' is an Item [[minigame]] found in [[Pirate Land]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and a regular item minigame in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', where it is unnamed. Its name is derived from a lyric from the "{{wp|Beer Barrel Polka}}."

==Gameplay==
[[File:MPS Roll Out the Barrels.png|thumb|left|200px|Roll Out the Barrels in ''Mario Party Superstars'']]
Before the minigame begins, items are placed in six [[barrel]]s, which shuffle themselves around quickly. When the minigame begins, the player then has to choose between said six barrels. If the player chooses the barrel with [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]] in it or runs out of time, they get nothing.

In ''Mario Party Superstars'', [[Bowser Jr.]] takes the place of Baby Bowser, and the pirate theme in the background was replaced by a grassland theme.

In ''Mario Party 2'', the minigame can be purchased from [[Woody]] for 400 [[coin]]s.
