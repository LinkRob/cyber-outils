'''Goose''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and it's [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].

==History==
Goose is a [[Craw]] member of [[Ishnail]]'s [[Robbo Gang]] and possibly the brother of [[Gus]] and [[Garf]]. He operates the [[Trouble Center]] on the east side of [[Rogueport]]. If the player chooses to, [[Flurrie]] can blow on the back of the Trouble Center and reveal the hidden back door of it. He wants to keep secret the fact that the Robbo Gang operates the Trouble Center. He offers [[Mario]] a bribe, but he does not accept it. [[Goombella]] mentions that despite his looks, Goose is smart which may contribute to why he is the one managing the Trouble Center.
