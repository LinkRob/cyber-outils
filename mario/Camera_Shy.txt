'''Camera Shy''' is a four player/duel minigame from ''[[Mario Party DS]].''

==Opening==
The Camera zooms in on the inside of a cornfield, with the tiny characters holding cameras.

==Gameplay==
The goal is to snap a picture of all three opponents in the corn maze while trying not to be caught on their cameras.

==Ending==
Whoever takes pictures of all other characters faster will have theirs shown, and the winning character will do their victory pose with their pictures in front of them.

==Rules==
*'''Rules''' – "''Take a photo of everyone else in the maze. Your target must be in focus, so take the photo only when the frame turns green.''"
*'''Tips''' – "''Your flashbulb needs time to recharge after every use, so make sure you have a shot in focus before you shoot.''"
