'''Shiny Paragoombas''' are a variant of both [[Paragoomba]]s and [[Shiny Goomba]]s which appear as an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. They are encountered in [[Snow Rise]], [[Ice Flow]], and [[Whiteout Valley]].

Shiny Paragoombas appear in different context in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', as a shiny variation of [[Paper Paragoomba]] named (Shiny) Paper Paragoomba.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The only physical difference that Shiny Paragoombas have from regular Goombas is the sheen on their body, hence their name.

===Behavior===
A Shiny Paragoomba can deal more damage than regular Paragoombas. [[Mario]] can turn a Shiny Paragoomba into a Shiny Goomba by using a Jump type [[Stickers|sticker]].
