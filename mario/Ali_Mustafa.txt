'''Ali Mustafa''' is an Egyptian tie salesman who made a one-time appearance in the ''[[Club Nintendo]]'' comic "[[Mario's Picross (comic)|Mario's Picross]]."

==History==
In the comic, [[Mario]] is stranded and dehydrated from being in the desert. Along the way, Mario meets Ali Mustafa, who tries to sell him ties, claiming it is Egypt's best. Mario gets infuriated, as he wants a drink, so he shoos away Ali Mustafa. Mario later finds out that a cocktail bar requires him to wear a tie in order to enter.

==Physical description==
Ali Mustafa has a short curled mustache. He wears an orange robe and a teal-colored turban with a red, circular gemstone on the front, above which is a red, a yellow, and a green feather. He wears light yellow sandals.
