'''Icicle Mites''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Icicle Mites have the appearance of large, walking icicles with eyes.

===Behavior===
If stunned and embedded into the ground, an Icicle Mite can be used by [[Wario]] as a small platform. The effect is temporary because after fifteen seconds, the Icicle Mite explodes.

==In ''Wario World''==
Icicle Mites are only encountered in the cave portions of [[Shivering Mountains]]. They can only hurt Wario if he jumps on their sharp point on top. Wario can stun an Icicle Mite by hitting it three times and embed it into the ground by using his [[Piledriver]] move.
