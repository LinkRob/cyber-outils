'''Wood Blocks''' are an object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
Wood Blocks are a hard, indestructible type of [[block]] used mainly as a platform. They function identically to [[Hard Block]]s, which Wood Blocks even replace in some of their appearances, such as ''Super Mario Bros. 3''. Sometimes, if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] runs into the side of a Wood Block, he gets bounced away. Some Wood Blocks are located near [[pit]]s as an obstacle. Some Wood Blocks contain a power-up item, similarly to certain [[? Block]]s, but a Wood Block can release its power-up item upon being touched.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
Wood Blocks are found in several levels. A [[Chain Chomp]] is usually found attached to a Wood Block. There are larger Wood Blocks in the fourth world, [[Giant Land]].

In the remake ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', a few [[World-e]] levels—[[Classic World 1-1]], [[Classic World 1-2]], and [[Classic World 2-2]]—use Hard Blocks instead of Wood Blocks, due to being a recreation of a level from ''Super Mario Bros.''.

===''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''===
Wood Blocks mostly appear instead of Hard Blocks, owing to the game's ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' theme.
{{sectstub}}

===''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
Wood Blocks are placeable objects in the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' style, where they replace the Hard Blocks, similarly to how [[Gray Block]]s replace Hard Blocks in the ''[[Super Mario World]]'' style.
