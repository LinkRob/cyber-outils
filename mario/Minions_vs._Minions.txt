'''Minions vs. Minions''' is the second level of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 6.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 8 [[Lakipea|Lakitpeas]]. The enemy captain is a Lakitpea.

The 2nd battle contains 8 [[Paratroopea|Paratroopeas]]. The enemy captain is a Paratroopea.

The final battle contains 4 Lakipea and 4 Paratroopea. The enemy captain is a Lakipea.

After the final battle, the player unlocks [[Lakitu]].

No other allies can be gained by replaying the level.
