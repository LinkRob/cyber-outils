'''Gold Banzai Bills''' are golden counterparts of [[Banzai Bill]]s and large counterparts of [[Gold Bullet Bill]]s which first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''==
Whenever [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] touches a [[Gold Ring (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|Gold Ring]], every Banzai Bill turns into a Gold Banzai Bill. They behave exactly like normal Banzai Bills, the only difference being that they leave behind a two-row trail of [[coin]]s in their wake. A Gold Banzai Bill can be defeated using most attacks, and grants 20 coins upon being defeated.
