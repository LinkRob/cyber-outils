|games     = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Body Slam''' is a physical move used only by [[Axem Yellow]] and [[Remo Con]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
When using Body Slam, the foe leaps up into the air and delivers an attack to a single member of [[Mario]]'s party for high damage. Being a physical attack rather than a magical one, it can be blocked against.
