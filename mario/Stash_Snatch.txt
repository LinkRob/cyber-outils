==Gameplay==
In this minigame, [[Chunky Kong]] must navigate through the maze and collect 6 coins and reach the end goal within the time limit. There are several [[Kremling]]s that will try to impede Chunky's quest. This minigame is rather unique as it's the only minigame in the game that is played once and by only one specific Kong.
