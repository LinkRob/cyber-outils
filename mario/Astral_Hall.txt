The '''Astral Hall''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Astral Hall contains two drawers on either side of the room, five candelabra in the center, and a large crystal chandelier hanging in the middle of the candles. If Luigi leaves the room after clearing it, the candles become lit, although he cannot extinguish them, even when releasing [[water]] through the [[Water Elemental Ghost]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Astral Hall is accessed in [[Area Three]] of [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Luigi's Mansion]]. If [[Luigi]] continues through the door on the right, he is looped back through the entrance to the room as a short tune plays. Luigi has to release fire via the effects of the [[Fire Elemental Ghost]] to light all the candelabra. Once the candles are lit, Luigi can enter the center of the room for an event to occur: the candle flames turn purple and suddenly extinguish while the door leading to the [[Observatory]] appears with a seal over it. Luigi is then ambushed by [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]], including four [[Ghost Guy]]s, two [[Gold Ghost]]s, and one [[Grabbing Ghost]]. Once Luigi has vacuumed up every ghost, the lights turn on, and the door to the Observatory is unsealed.

If Luigi visits Astral Hall during the [[blackout]], there are six Ghost Guys to fight.

In the PAL releases' and the Nintendo 3DS version's [[Hidden Mansion]], there are a lot more ghosts, including four [[Garbage Can Ghost]]s, two Grabbing Ghosts, two [[Red Grabbing Ghost]]s, three Gold Ghosts, and two [[Mr. Bones]].
