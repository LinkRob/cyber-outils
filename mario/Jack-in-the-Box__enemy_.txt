'''Jack-in-the-Box''' (ビロロン, ''Biroron'') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. This enemy resembles the toy of the {{w|jack-in-the-box|same name}}, and it appears after hitting a [[Question Block|? Block]]. A jack-in-the-box bounces around wildly, and [[Mario]] can defeat it by either jumping onto it or from shooting a [[fireball]] at it as [[Fire Mario]].
