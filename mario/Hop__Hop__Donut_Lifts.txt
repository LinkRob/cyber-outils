'''Hop! Hop! Donut Lifts''' is the fifth level of ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.

== Layout ==
The player starts off on a small grassy platform before jumping onto some [[Donut Lift]]s nearby. The player can either choose to follow the Donut Lift path or go underneath it and follow a path full of [[Flatbed Ferry|Flatbed Ferries]]. Both paths lead to another small grassy platform before leading to a [[Paddle Platform|paddle wheel]]. The player can choose to use the paddle wheel or follow the Donut Lift path again. The player will then be on another small grassy platform and they will need to jump onto another Donut Lift path.

After the path, the screen will start to scroll upwards and the player will have to use [[Expansion Block]]s, Donut Lifts and Flatbed Ferries to move upwards. The player will then reach the [[Middle Ring]] and they will have to use a paddle wheel to move sidewards to reach the next part of the level, which is a climb up some grassy platforms. After these platforms is a Donut Lift path that leads to the [[GOAL! Ring]].
