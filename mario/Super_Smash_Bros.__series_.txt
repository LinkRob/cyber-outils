The '''''Super Smash Bros.'' series''' is a series of fighting games published by [[Nintendo]]. It currently has six installments: ''[[Super Smash Bros.]] ([[Nintendo 64]])'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]] ([[Nintendo GameCube]])'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]] ([[Nintendo Wii]])'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]] ([[Nintendo 3DS]]), ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]] (Nintendo [[Wii U]])'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]] ([[Nintendo Switch]]). All fighters have 2 distinct KO sounds from Brawl to Ultimate. KOs like Standard KO, Star KO, & Screen KO. In Melee, Several characters yell out two of strong knockback groans. Every fighter has 3 distinct victory poses in all five games. Every character has 2 different idle poses.

== Gameplay ==
This game series mainly involved mostly Nintendo-related characters mainly those from the ''Mario'', ''Kirby'', ''The Legend of Zelda'', and ''Pokemon'' series fighting it out in battle. For Single-player mode, there is usually three modes that are playable: Classic Mode, Adventure Mode (Melee and Brawl only), and All-Star Mode (''Melee'', ''Brawl'', and ''Smash 4''). The more popular Battle Mode is a multiplayer mode where everyone can join in on the fight. Also note that certain characters must be unlocked by special ways in order to play as them. Only in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' where one can play the Subspace Emissary in order to unlock every character in that game. Also, [[Mega Man]], the [[Villager]], [[Rosalina]] and [[Luma]], [[Duck Hunt (character)|Duck Hunt]], [[Greninja]], [[Lucina]], [[Palutena]], [[Bowser Jr.]], the Wii Fit Trainer, and more are new additions to ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS and Wii U''. The [[Inkling]]s from the ''Splatoon'' series are new additions to ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' for the Nintendo Switch. [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Donkey Kong]], [[Kirby]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], Pikachu, Jigglypuff, [[Link]], [[Fox McCloud|Fox]], [[Samus Aran]], [[Captain Falcon]], and [[Ness]] are the only characters to have appeared in every ''Super Smash Bros.'' game so far who are often referred to as "The Original 12."
