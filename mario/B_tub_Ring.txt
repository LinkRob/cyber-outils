'''B'tub Ring''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When a user equips the B'tub Ring, the item halves elemental damage and decreases magic attacks by 50%. The item can be obtained within [[Marrymore]]'s shop. The B'tub Ring is also needed to hatch the [[Mystery Egg]] for the [[Lamb's Lure]]. The B'tub Ring is only usable by the [[Princess Peach|Princess]].
