The '''Coin Box+''' is the enhanced version of the [[Coin Box]] in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is an exclusive item that appears on certain drivers.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Being an exclusive item, if the driver has a [[Coin Box]], the player can enhance the Coin Box into the upgraded version. Compared to the Coin Box, there will be a chance that the Coin Box+ will spit out [[Red Coin]]s which is worth twice than the coin.
