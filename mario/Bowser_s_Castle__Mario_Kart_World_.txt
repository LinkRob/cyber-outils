'''Bowser’s Castle''' serves as the concluding track of the [[Lightning Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' and is the launch point for the [[Moon Rally]]. Located in the shadowy volcanic area in the northwest of the game’s map, it introduces several innovations compared to previous [[Bowser’s Castle]] tracks, adopting a more industrial and futuristic aesthetic. This course deviates from the traditional placement of Bowser’s Castle just before Rainbow Road in the main console ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series and is the first new addition to the [[Lightning Cup]] since ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]].''

Situated atop a high hill, the castle’s design mimics an erupting volcano, complete with a Bowser-shaped mushroom cloud. The entrance gate resembles that of Bowser’s Castle from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and is predominantly black and gray, contrasting with the usual brown hues of earlier courses. Parts of the track are set in a stormy area akin to [[Cloudtop Cruise]] from ''Mario Kart 8'', where racers receive a continuous speed boost, accompanied by flame and spark effects on their vehicles.

== Course Layouts ==
The race begins on a straight path with two downward slopes, followed by a brief split section in a hexagonal form. Next is a slanted section of the track: it initially ascends, then curves left, with the slope running perpendicular to the road. Another left turn is followed by a right turn, with the angle between them sloping downhill. A perpendicular segment leads to a left turn, where a [[Glide Ramp]] propels racers through a [[lava]] tunnel like a cannon. At the top, there’s a broad right turn flanked by lava floor hazards. Two drops lead to a U-turn with lava obstacles. A Glide Ramp takes racers to a secluded pair of overlapping U-turns, concluding with a final jump to the finish line. This is one of the few Bowser’s Castle tracks without [[Thwomp]]s, though the [[Kamek]] item can summon them here.

=== Routes ===
There are three distinct paths to enter Bowser’s Castle, depending on the route chosen to reach the course.

*Coming from Wario Stadium, Choco Mountain, or Toad’s Factory, players enter after passing through Big Donut and ascending either side of the volcano’s base.
*Coming from Airship Fortress, players enter the course exclusively via the left side of the volcano’s base.
*Coming from Dry Bones Burnout or Mario Circuit, players enter the course exclusively via the right side of the volcano’s base.
