'''Diamond Knoll''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Description==
Diamond Knoll is an area of [[Diamond City]] with an abundance of grass.

==In ''WarioWare: Twisted!''==
During [[Kat and Ana]]'s stage, [[Tap Out]], the duo and their classmates in [[Diamond City Kindergarten]] take a field trip to Diamond Knoll. The teacher instructs the students to not go wandering off, an order soon disobeyed by Kat and Ana as they go exploring with their pets [[Shadow the Dog|Shadow]] and [[Shuriken the Falcon|Shuriken]]. While Ana is distracted by a flower, Kat provokes several [[bee]]s within a [[beehive]], and the bees angrily chase after the two of them. Kat and Ana come across a wooden cabin, where they themselves inside, only to then be ambushed by the [[Diamond Troll]].

In the story's ending cutscene, Kat and Ana are rescued by their pets, Shadow and Shuriken, who break into the hut and land onto the Diamond Troll. The group then leaves the hut only to find that the bees have been waiting outside for them. The group runs away from Diamond Knoll while being chased by the bees once more.
