|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' (2013)}}'''Really Rolling Hills''' is the fourth level of World 2 in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The level revolves around the mechanic of the rolling hills, similar to [[Yoshi Hill]] from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. A new enemy by the name of [[Skipsqueak]]s, as well as their other variant, [[Spiny Skipsqueak]]s, dominate the hills, jumping on them in a steady fashion.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': In Cat form, go in the first clear pipe and climb the ledge to get the star.
*'''Green Star 2''': In the underground section, take the wrap pipe to the right of a line of purple bricks to a secret area. Light up each tile to get the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Right after that wrap pipe, fall in between two of the three wheels to get a star from below.
