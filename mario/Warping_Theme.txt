The "'''Warping Theme'''" is a six-note melody which first appears in the first ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'', followed by its ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' debut in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

== In {{series|The Legend of Zelda}} ==
===''The Legend of Zelda''===
The Warping Theme is played whenever Link uses the [[Recorder]].

=== ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time]]'' ===
The Warping Theme is the melody used in the title screen's music. It is also extended beyond its usual six notes.

=== ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap|The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap]]'' ===
Similar to its other appearances, the Warping Theme is associated with Link's ability to travel throughout the overworld. The theme can be played using the [[w:c:zelda:Ocarina of Wind|Ocarina of Wind]].

==In ''Super Mario Bros. 3''==
An arrangement of the Warping Theme plays whenever [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] use the Warp Whistle, the game's iteration of the Recorder, to be warped over to the [[Warp Zone]].
