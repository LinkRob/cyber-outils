A '''Stumper''' is an enemy in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

==In ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''==
Mud Troopers slowly walk towards you, the light temporarily rids of it, but it will come back; you can defeat it permanently by stomping on it or using the [[Super Pickax]]
