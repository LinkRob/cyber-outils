'''World 7-4''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. It is the fourth level found in World 7. It is a short level, consisting many [[Super Mushroom|Mushrooms]] and Spin Blocks.

==Enemies==
*[[Koopa Troopa|Red Koopa Troopa]]

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is on top of the right-most mushroom platform at the beginning of the level. It must be accessed with a Spin Block.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is on a wider mushroom platform to the upper-left of the first one.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is at the very top-left of the level, followed by a path of [[Coin]]s that lead to the Red Coin Ring.

==Secret Exit==
The secret exit requires Mario to be in his Mini form. The player needs to hop on a Flying ? Block, then around a pillar to reach a mini Warp Pipe, and going into the Pipe will take Mario to the secret exit, which leads all the way to the castle.
