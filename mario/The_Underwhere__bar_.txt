'''The Underwhere''' is a location that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==History==
The Underwhere is a milk bar that can be found in [[Flipside]] and is owned by [[Garson]]. As The Underwhere sells milk, the player cannot buy anything here. However, they can speak with Garson who will offer random gossip for 10 coins each. After [[Super Dimentio]] has been defeated, [[The InterNed]] will appear and will play any song for 10 coins. There is also a hidden golden warp pipe that can be found via [[Mario]]'s [[Flip]] ability that leads to the [[Flipside Arcade]].
