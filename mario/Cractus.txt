'''Cractus''' is a character in the ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' franchise. It first appears in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
Cractus is a large, drooling {{wp|Venus flytrap}} with leaf-like appendages.

==Appearances==
===''Wario Land 4''===
[[File:WL4CractusSprite.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Cractus boss fight WL4.png|thumb|[[Wario]] battling Cractus in ''Wario Land 4'']]
Cractus first appears as a boss in ''[[Wario Land 4]]'' and is one of the four non-first/final bosses of the game in which the Player(s) can fight in any order. It is the guardian of the [[Emerald Passage]]. 

Upon starting the battle, Cractus is sleeping, ignoring Wario's presence. To force it to battle, Wario must simply ram the vase holding it. This will cause Cractus to wake up. In normal mode, the [[time limit]] of the battle is 4:00; in Hard mode it is 3:00; and in S-Hard mode, it is 2:00.

==== Strategy ====
To damage Cractus, Wario must [[Ground Pound]] its head. Touching its gaping mouth or spiky arms will damage Wario. Initially, Cractus will position itself extremely low, where Wario can damage it with a simple jump followed by a ground pound. As Cractus is damaged, it will increase the height of its patrols (also increasing the range of its attacks) and the speed of its attacks. If Wario is climbing the vines before Cractus begins its patrol, it will glide across the top of the room while dropping drool in set intervals.

Each time Cractus is damaged, it becomes enraged while drooling for a brief moment of time. If Wario touches this drool, he will turn into [[Zombie Wario]], which relieves it. [[Hotaru]] will appear from the east whenever Wario is turned into a zombie to help him resume the fight. If the drool is avoided, Cractus will follow Wario and attack with homing jabs, and will only be relieved when it successfully damages him or if Wario manages to go behind it. It is possible to damage Cractus in this state, but it requires precise timing. After enough hits, Cractus is defeated and Wario is rewarded with the creature's [[legendary treasure|Crown]].

There are unused sprites of Cractus without the crown around its body.

===''WarioWare: Move It!''===
[[File:Cractus WWMI screenshot.png|thumb|left|230px|Cractus in ''WarioWare: Move It!'']]
In ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'', Cractus is the main antagonist of [[Kat and Ana]]'s story, with changes to his design: the leaves around his head are straighter and no longer wilted, he is rooted to the ground therefore he can no longer fly, and he lacks bald skin and has a jagged mouth instead of a straight mouth with sharp teeth. During the events of their stage, The Grand Parfait Adventure, Kat and Ana follow a parchment, but Leo steals it as he wants the parfaits as well. Leo gets a dose of karma when he gets eaten by Cractus. Kat and Ana battle Cractus and several carnivorous plants that aid him, though Cractus becomes enraged when they fell one of his minions. He then sics them on the twins, commencing a battle.

During the intermission to their stage, Kat and Ana face Cractus' minions while the monster himself waits in the background. If the player wins a microgame, Kat and Ana fell the minion, but if the player fails, they get mauled instead. During the Boss Microgame, Cractus himself confronts the duo, with the same reactions as his minions.

After the player clears the stage, Cractus is defeated and loses consciousness from his wounds, though whether he actually did survive or not is unclear. Leo escapes from the monster's jaws, salty over his failure to get the parfaits, while Kat and Ana enjoy a parfait feast.

==In other media==
===''Super Mario-kun''===
[[File:Cractus SuperMarioKun.jpg|thumb|Cractus in ''Super Mario-kun'']]
Cractus makes an appearance as a boss in the ''Wario Land 4'' arc of volume 28 of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''. Wario sees the boss's crown, but he then notices Cractus sleeping next to it. Wario attempts to grab the crown, but the boss awakens and snaps it from Wario and then attacks him. After it drools on Wario, Wario becomes Zombie Wario. Cractus continues attacking Wario. Wario throws [[Mad Scienstein|Arewo Shitain-hakase]] and causes light to shine on Wario, reverting him to normal. When Wario attacks Cractus by ground-pounding, Arewo Shitain-hakase throws fruit at him, transforming Wario into [[Fat Wario]]. Fat Wario then falls into Cractus's gaping mouth, crushing and defeating it.
