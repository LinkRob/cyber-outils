The '''Elder Key''' is a special item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It unlocks the door to a room in [[Booster Tower]] with an imprisoned [[Chomp]]. The Elder Key is obtained by completing a small game involving portraits of [[Booster (character)|Booster]] and his ancestors.
