'''Yellow Shells''' are a type of [[Koopa Shell]] worn by certain [[Koopa Troopa]]s. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. When [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] has a Yellow Shell in his mouth, he becomes heavy and can create [[sand cloud]]s upon hitting the ground.
