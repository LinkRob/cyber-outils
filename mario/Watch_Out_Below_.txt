'''Watch Out Below! '''is the second level of [[World 1 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 1]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and the Game Boy Advance version, ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. It features [[Pink Yoshi]] as the playable [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. Watch Out Below! features [[Incoming Chomp]]s as the main obstacle, and the level title indicates the Pink Yoshi must be cautious of them.

==Layout==
[[File:Watch Out Below GBA.png|thumb|left|Watch Out Below! in ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'']]
Pink Yoshi starts in an overworld location. Pink Yoshi needs to move forward and avoid the first Chain Chomp. When Pink Yoshi gets to the first set of [[coin]]s (some are [[Red Coin|red coins]]), Pink Yoshi needs to be aware, because more Chain Chomps come crashing down. Pink Yoshi then needs to hit the first question cloud with a [[Yoshi Egg]] and get the flower that comes out of it. A Chain Chomp will come crashing down over the flower though, so Yoshi needs to be aware when gets it. Pink Yoshi then needs to get the next set of coins (being aware of the Chain Chomps) and hit the next question cloud (once he is in that location, Chain Chomps will only crash down behind Pink Yoshi, but they can't go beyond that). Pink Yoshi needs to get the 5 stars that come out of the question cloud and get the Middle Ring. Then a star will come out of a tunnel so Pink Yoshi must get it. Then Shy Guys will come out of the Warp Pipe if Pink Yoshi doesn't have all eggs. The player must then hit through the white bricks to get the red coins (all of the coins are red coins). After that, they need to break open the next set of white bricks to make the bouncy arrow get down so they can jump on it.

The player will then be in a new area, they have to search everywhere to find all the red coins, flowers and stars. They also need to hit the helicopter box to turn Pink Yoshi into a helicopter. He can fly in all directions and get flowers and red coins. At the end, the player needs to go down the Warp Pipe. After that, they have to hit the question cloud with a Yoshi Egg to get the final flower and hit the Propeller Shy Guy with another egg to get a 1-UP. The player needs to then get the three coins (all of them are red coins) and avoid the last Chain Chomp. After that, they need to hit the goal and will get the bonus game if they're lucky (if they collected any flowers). Pink Yoshi will give his Yoshi Eggs (if he has any) and [[Baby Mario]] to [[Light Blue Yoshi]]. Light Blue Yoshi and Baby Mario will then move on to the next level, [[The Cave of Chomp Rock]].
