'''Wario Factory''' is a basketball court in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It is also the first court of the [[Star Tourney]]. ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'' features a court with the [[Wario Factory (Mario Sports Mix)|same name]], and it could possibly be the same as this one.

==Description==
Wario Factory takes place in a factory with a gold-plated floor and walls. Wario Factory takes place on a wide lift that moves up and down perpetually. The movement of the court does not affect gameplay directly, but whenever the court stops moving, two hatches on each side of the court open, and [[Bob-omb]]s start to walk out onto the court from the conveyor belts. Either team can pick up and throw a Bob-omb to explode on their opponent. The backboard of the basketball hoop is a gear with a "W" [[emblem]] slightly above it.
