'''Luigi Statues''' are collectible items in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' that become available after a player collects all 13 [[Gem]]s in a mansion. There are 5 statues total.

==History==

Once a player finds all 13 Gems in each mansion, they are rewarded with a crystalline statue of Luigi that is visible in [[E. Gadd's Vault]]. They can be viewed in the Gem collection area. The statue appears to be made of a crystal material. The Gems appear to be encrusted around the wooden base of each statue.
