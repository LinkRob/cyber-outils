'''Dizzy Attack''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Found on [[Pleasant Path]], this badge is based on [[Mario]]'s spin technique. This badge can be found inside the first red [[Question Block]] Mario encounters. Dizzy Attack allows Mario to use an attack to make the targeted enemy [[dizzy]] for two turns, although this only works if Mario uses his spin technique to enter a battle. Dizzy Attack requires two [[Badge Point|BP]] to use.
