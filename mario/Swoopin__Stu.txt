A '''Swoopin' Stu''',<ref>Hodgson, David S J, Bryan Stratton, and Stephen Stratton. ''Super Mario Sunshine'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide. Page 17.</ref> also known as '''Name Kuri'''<ref>Bogenn, Tim, and Doug Walsh. ''Super Mario Sunshine'' BradyGames Official Strategy Guide. Page 5.</ref> is a type of transparent creature that appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
Swoopin' Stus can be found wherever there is [[goop]]. They are named for the [[Strollin' Stu]], but they do not closely resemble them, nor are they related, instead appearing as round slug-like creatures. They come in a large variety of colors, as well as some size variation, although they all act exactly the same. For the most part, unless Swoopin' Stus see [[Mario]], they will generally wander in an outward direction without any real, apparent purpose and will not create a larger "goop area" unless they fall off a ledge and pop on impact.

Once a Swoopin' Stu spots Mario, it will turn white for a second before making a "bwooww-wop" noise and swooping at him, then splattering, leaving goop behind.

Swoopin' Stus can be destroyed with a blast of water from [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] or by being [[jump]]ed on, though the latter method will leave behind a puddle of goop that can spawn more Swoopin' Stus. This can be avoided, however, if they are jumped on while they are in the air. [[Stomp]]ing on them also causes them to occasionally drop [[Water Bottle]]s. Eventually, Swoopin' Stus will pop even without provocation but will not leave goop.

==Appearances==
===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
[[File:SMS Noki Bay Graffiti Enemies.png|thumb|Swoopin' Stus in [[Noki Bay]]]]
In ''Super Mario Sunshine'', since Swoopin' Stus spawn from sources of goop, they are most prominent during battles with [[Polluted Piranha Plant]]s, from which they spawn from their base, as well as [[Petey Piranha]]. Swoopin' Stus can be found in [[Bianco Hills]], [[Ricco Harbor]], [[Sirena Beach]], [[Noki Bay]], and [[Pianta Village]], the last of which shows they can also emerge from [[Fiery Goop]].

In beta builds, Swoopin' Stus were originally intended to have a more slug-like design; they had a wider bottom, no head tentacles, and made no sound. Swoopin' Stus did not even turn white to relay that they are pouncing.

===''Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour''===
In ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]'', the name "Gooble" appears on the leaderboard.
