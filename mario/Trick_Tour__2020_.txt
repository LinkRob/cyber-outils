'''Trick Tour''' is the sixteenth Tour that began at April 22, 2020 and ended on May 5, 2020. This tour mainly features Super Mario Maker 2's final major update (V3.0.0). 

It was the second Tour in a row that did not make any new courses and the first to give specific Variants of Tracks in the case of R/T. This was included in nearly every retro and RMX Tracks in R/T versions which excluded [[Rock Rock Mountain]] and introduced this variant to [[Vanilla Lake 1|SNES Vanilla Lake 1]] and [[Bowser Castle 2 (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|GBA Boswer Castle 2]]. 

This tour features [[Builder Mario]] and Builder Toad as new characters with the background of the menu featering [[Princess Peach's Castle]] in [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart 7)|3DS Mario Circuit]] as the land mark for this tour.
