==History==
Dodgy is a very useful status effect to have. This effect can be given to [[Mario]] and his partners but certain enemies can have this effect as well. When someone is affected with the Dodgy status, they will sometimes avoid hits from enemies. Using a [[Repel Cape]] is the most common way to get the Dodgy effect. Using [[Madame Flurrie]]'s special move, [[Dodgy Fog]], gives the Dodgy status as well. [[Magikoopa]]s are enemies that can give Dodgy to other enemies or themselves and bosses such as [[Macho Grubba]] can give Dodgy to himself.
