'''Lightning Score Striker''', named '''-3 Lightning''' in PAL releases, is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
[[File:MPIT Lightning Striker.png|thumb|200px|left|[[Wario]] being struck by the Lightning Score Striker in ''Mario Party: Island Tour'']]
A Lightning Score Striker has the exact appearance of a [[Lightning]] item from the {{series|Mario Kart}}. If a character uses a Lightning Score Striker, the opponent is shocked, and their dice roll is subtracted by three. There for, if the opponent rolls anywhere between 1 or 3, they cannot move during that turn.

The Lightning Score Striker has an opposite counterpart named [[Dash Mushroom]], which instead adds 3 to the user's dice roll.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
Lightning Score Striker is one of the items exclusive to the [[Perilous Palace Path]] board. It can be obtained by winning first place in a minigame and then selecting the Lightning Score Striker.

==Flavor text==
*In-game description: ''Subtract 3 from your opponent's roll.''
