'''Village Square''', named '''Mushroom Village''' in its debut appearance, is a village located by a river bank and inhabited by [[Toad (species)|Toads]] in ''[[Mario Party]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. [[Toad (character)|Toad]] Acts as the guide around the area, which is a hub for the different modes.
