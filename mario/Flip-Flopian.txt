The '''Flip-Flopians''' are a race of characters that appear in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Flip-Flopians are polygonal bipeds who has different colors. Flip-Flopians have counterparts who have personalities contradicting each other.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Flip-Flopians do not have any special powers but they have their own talents like normal humans such as cooking and fortune-telling.

=== Habitats ===
Flip-Flopians live in different towns called [[Flipside]] and its sister city, [[Flopside]].

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
Most Flip-Flopians are found in [[Flipside]] and [[Flopside]]. Some Flip-Flopians talk to the player and others offer help to the player such as [[Saffron]] and [[Dyllis]] cooking meals for the player.
