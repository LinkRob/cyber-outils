==History==
The Feather Bow is Tiny's personal weapon that can be purchased at [[Funky's Store]] for 3 [[Banana Coin]]s. Tiny wields a small bow that fires strong feathers from a distance. The Feather Bow can be used to press Feather Switches and defeat enemies. The Feather Bow can also be used in the final fight with [[King Krusha K. Rool]] to damage his toes.
