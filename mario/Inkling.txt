}}An '''Inkling''' is a playable character that originally appears in ''Splatoon'' series. They make appearances in the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario series]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros. series]]''.
== Appearances ==
===''[[Super Mario Maker]]''===
An Inkling Girl and Boy made a cameo appearance as a [[Mystery Mushroom]] costume.

===''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
The Inklings appear as playable characters in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. The player can both select the female and male versions of the Inklings (they are classified as different characters with differen statistics), as well choosing different colored versions of them.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
The Inklings appear as unlockable playable fighters in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''. The initial teaser trailer for ''Ultimate'', before its title was revealed, featured the default female and male Inklings in their designs from the original ''Splatoon'', though they were not explicitly confirmed to be playable until Nintendo's E3 2018 presentation. Inkling can potentially be the first character unlocked in the game as the first character in [[Samus Aran|Samus]]' Classic Mode character unlock tree.

==Trivia==
*Inkling appears as a playable guest character in ''Taiko no Tatsujin: Drum 'n' Fun!'', alongside [[Kirby]], with "Splatoon 2 Medley" also being one of the game's songs.
*Inkling is the only character to have male and female alternate costumes where both genders have the same voice actor.
