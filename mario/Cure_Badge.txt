'''Cure Badge''' is a [[badge]] item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be bought from [[Peach's Castle]] for 500 [[coin]]s. Equipping it restores five [[HP]] to its user each turn. It has a more effective variant known as [[Cure Badge A]].
