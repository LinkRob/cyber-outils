'''Biting Bullet Bills''' are [[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island/Beta elements|unused]] enemies in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] game ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. These red [[Bullet Bill]]s come from similarly-colored [[Bill Blaster]]s, and they dynamically chase after [[Yoshi]] while their mouth makes a two-frame chomping motion. It slows down over time until it ceases to function, quickly shrinking in blue and vanishing in a generic pop. They can be defeated by simply [[stomp]]ing or [[swallow]]ing them; it looks like a regular Bullet Bill if spat back out, but it will fly downwards. In the [[Game Boy Advance]] port, ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', they are used and make their only appearance in the level [[Endless World of Yoshis]].

While these specific Bullet Bills have yet to reappear in the series, this concept was reused for subsequent appearances of [[Missile Bill]], which originally only switched their direction once.
