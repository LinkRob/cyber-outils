'''King Purple Coin Coffers''' are Dark/Dark attribute enemies and are found as early as World 7-Ghost House, and can also be encountered in World 8-Ghost House 2. A King Purple Coin Coffer is the boss of Special World course ★World 7-8. As teammates, their Awoken Skill is '''Dark Boost''', and may provide ATK+ bonuses when depowered.
