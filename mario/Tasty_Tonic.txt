'''Tasty Tonic''' is a recurring item in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:TastyTonicPM.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Tasty Tonics are very common in the game being sold in nearly every [[Item Shop]]. Drinking a Tasty Tonic cures [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] and shrinking status. Tasty Tonics can also be used in a variety of [[recipe]]s. A stronger version of the Tasty Tonic is called the [[Super Soda]].

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Tasty Tonics return in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', having the same role as before. They can only be bought in a few [[Item Shop]]s, where they are sold for 3 [[coin]]s.
