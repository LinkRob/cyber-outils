|race = [[Toad (species)|Toad]]}}The '''Toad waitress''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. She is a purple [[Toad (species)|Toad]] that is employed on the [[Excess Express]] as the waitress.

==History==
The Toad waitress is commonly seen walking around in the kitchen of the game. During the second day, [[Doopliss|someone]] has stolen her [[Shell Earrings]] and asks [[Mario]] to retrieve them. Her Shell Earrings came from her ex-boyfriend who is a [[Little Mouser|Squeek]] that is seen in [[Podley's Place]] in [[Rogueport]].
