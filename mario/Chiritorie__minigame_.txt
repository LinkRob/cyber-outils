'''Chiritorie''' is an unlockable two-player versus minigame which appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. It is a variation of the [[microgame]] of the [[Chiritorie (microgame)|same name]], and features both players controlling their own [[Chiritori]], with player one's being red and player two's being blue. Chiritorie can be unlocked by completing [[Jimmy T.]]'s story.

==Gameplay==
Chiritorie is a two-player minigame played from the same system, with the first player having to hold {{button|gba|L}} to move their [[Chiritori]] whereas the second player has to hold {{button|gba|R}} to move theirs. The object for both players is to collect a hundred pieces of crumpled paper lying around, and the first one to do so is the winner. As the crumpled pieces of paper are vacuumed up, more appear, ranging from one through five pieces of crumpled paper grouped together along with six appearing as a big clutter.

By default, both the red and blue Chiritoris spin around in place, with a yellow arrow indicating the direction in which they are facing and therefore move while the player presses down their button. A Chiritori can only move in one direction, and hitting a wall or object causes it to start moving in the opposite direction. 

[[Jimmy T.]] appears onscreen at random, and he walks slowly in a linear direction. If a Chiritori vacuums Jimmy T., it malfunctions, and some of the crumpled paper it collected falls onto the ground, being taken away from their total and potentially allowing the other Chiritori to vacuum up the trash.
