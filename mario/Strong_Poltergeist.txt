The '''Strong Poltergeist''' ('''Strong Boffin''' in European localizations) is a sub-boss fought in the [[Treacherous Mansion]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. He is encountered in the mansion's third mission, [[A Train to Catch]].

== Profile ==
The Strong Poltergeist resembles a normal [[Poltergeist]], but has the stony-like texture marked all of his body, like all of the other strong ghosts.

== In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' ==
[[File:Strong Poltergeist taking book.png|left|thumb|249x249px|Strong Poltergeist stealing the yellow book]]
In mission E-3 "A Train to Catch," The Strong Poltergeist appears in the study, stealing a book that Luigi needs to open the secret entrance to the [[Train Exhibit]]. After taking the book, he will flee to the Space Exhibit, where Luigi must go to fight him. In the room, there is a moving telescope that Luigi must look through. After looking out of it, Luigi will be teleported into space on the ring of a planet. 

=== Battle ===
During battle, the Strong Poltergeist attacks by throwing stars at [[Luigi]]. He will remain invisible, but the player can tell where the Poltergeist is by the stars lighting up or by using the [[Dark-Light Device]]. When he is not holding a star, the player can stun him using the [[Strobulb]] and capture him. The Strong Poltergeist has 200 HP. 
[[File:Strong Poltergeist Laugh.png|left|thumb|249x249px|The Strong Poltergeist laughing at Luigi while throwing bombs and summoning beetles]]
While capturing him, the ring will start spinning, causing Luigi to lose his suction and freeing the Poltergeist. The Poltergeist will enter the asteroid, throwing out bombs and beetles. He cannot be stunned once in the asteroid. The Strong Poltergeist will do this two times in all. Once the Poltergeist is defeated, Luigi is teleported back into the Space Exhibit and the yellow book is obtained.
