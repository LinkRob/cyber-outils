These are party games referencing {{series|Mario Party}}, including by having similar forms of gameplay.

==References==
* ''Rio'': A party video game based on the film with the same name. It has 40 minigames. It also has three bonus minigames.
* ''Shrek Super Party'': A party video game based on the film Shrek. It has 30 minigames and a board game.
* ''SpongeBob SquarePants: Lights, Camera, Pants!'': A party video game based on the show SpongeBob SquarePants. It has 30 minigames.
