==Layout==
The Bafflewood is mainly a forest level mainly green and blue in color. Many trees can be seen in the level and in the background.

==History==
After being separated from the [[Wiggler]], [[Mario]] becomes lost in a dense forest. Mario treads to the maze-like forest and fights enemies until he reaches the end very shortly. He grabs the [[Comet Piece]] and completes the level.
