'''Bulb Berries''' are a power-up item that Yoshi can use in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''  and are one of three power-ups for Yoshi in along with the [[Dash Pepper]] and the [[Blimp Fruit]]. They are yellow berries that cause [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] to change into [[Bulb Yoshi]], which is a version of Yoshi that glows yellow. Once the berry is eaten, the light that comes from Yoshi reveals secret pathways and allows Yoshi to walk on said pathways. The effect of a Bulb Berry is temporary, so it wears off after a certain amount of time, or if [[Mario]] is hit by an enemy. The player can extend the time that the power up can be used by having Yoshi eat another Bulb Berry before the time expires.

=== Level Appearances ===

* '''Haunty Halls Galaxy''': In the level "A Glimmer of Bulb Berry"
* '''Sweet Mystery Galaxy''': In the level "Bulb Berry's Mysterious Glow" where almost the entire galaxy requires the Bulb Berry to light up platforms.
