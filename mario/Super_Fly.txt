A '''Super Fly''' is an enemy in the [[Beanbean Castle Sewer|sewers]] of [[Beanbean Castle]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. Super Flies are upgraded versions as well as palette swaps of [[Fighter Fly|Fighter Flies]], being purple with blue antennae, yellow hands, and yellow shoes. They resemble the classic design of the Fighter Flies from ''[[Mario Bros. (game)|Mario Bros.]]'' Super Flies have more [[Heart Point|HP]], are stronger, and are faster than Fighter Flies.

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', Super Flies' antennae and shoes are green, insead of blue and yellow formerly.
