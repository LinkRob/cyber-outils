The '''PAC-MAN Cup''' (also written as '''Pac-Man Cup''') is a cup which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
Generally, the PAC-MAN Cup only consists of courses focused on [[PAC-MAN]] himself.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart Arcade GP''===
This is the fourth of the six cups. The first course in Pac-Man Cup is [[PAC Mountain]], and the second and final one is [[PAC-Labyrinth]]. Both courses are specifically based on the ''PAC-MAN'' games.

===''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''===
Here, PAC-MAN Cup is the first master cup. It consists of the same two courses as in ''Mario Kart Arcade GP''.

===''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''===
Like in ''Mario Kart Arcade GP'', the PAC-MAN Cup is the fourth cup, although it consists of two different courses, the first one being [[PAC-MAN Stadium]], followed by [[NAMCO Circuit]], the latter of which is based on [[Bandai Namco Entertainment Inc.|Namco]]-owned properties in general.
