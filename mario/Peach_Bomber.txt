}}'''Peach Bomber''' (also '''Daisy Bomber''' when used by [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]) is [[Princess Peach|Peach]] and Daisy's side special move since ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''.

== Description ==
Peach/Daisy performs this move by hitting her opponents with her rear. If she comes in contact with the opponent, the opponent will be sent launching to the air, and Peach twirls and lands. If the opponent outpaces the move, Peach/Daisy will be left open.

The move can be used to tech chase opponents. Peach bounces off the opponent's shield when performing Peach Bomber, making the safe for shield pressure.

=== Customization ===
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for 3DS|3DS]]'', Princess Peach has customizations of the Peach Bomber.
* '''Flower Bomber''': When Princess Peach successfully performs this move, a flower is planted on the victim's head. If she misses, Peach has to wait before she can do anything.

* '''Flying Peach Bomber''': When Princess Peach successfully performs this move, it produces more knockback than the default. If she misses, Peach has to wait longer before she can do anything, leaving big openings.

== Trivia ==
* Peach Bomber is Peach's only special move that does not originate in any ''Mario'' game.
