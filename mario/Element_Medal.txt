The '''Element Medals''' are a series of three items in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
There are three types of Element Medals: the [[Fire Element Medal]], the [[Water Element Medal]], and the [[Ice Element Medal]]. Whenever [[Luigi]] collects one of these Element Medals, he can vacuum in an [[Elemental Ghost]] of the corresponding type, giving his [[Poltergust 3000]] a limited supply of that element. However, the Poltergust 3000 is not instantly given a supply of an element when Luigi first obtains the corresponding Element Medal. Somehow, the effects of an Element Medal are not shown in a [[Mirror (object)|mirror]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Each of the three Element Medals are obtained in a certain room, within a large [[treasure chest]] that has to be revealed by capturing every [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] in the room. The Fire Element Medal is in the [[Mirror Room]], and it is first Element Medal discovered by Luigi. The second one is the Water Element Medal, found in the [[Kitchen (Luigi's Mansion)|Kitchen]]. Lastly, the Ice Element Medal is in the [[Tea Room]].
