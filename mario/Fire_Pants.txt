The '''Fire Pants''' are a type of armor usable by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It increases his [[Defense]] by 42 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 21. The Fire Pants can be obtained by either buying it from [[Hinopio]] in [[Barrel Volcano]] or from [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]].
