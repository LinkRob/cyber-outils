'''{{world|coin|4|}}''' is the fourth level of [[World Coin]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Layout==
This is a desert-based level that takes place underground. The whole level is completed using a moving platform. There are many Sand Geysers that the player can jump on. The primary enemies are [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. [[Propeller Mushroom]]s are the power-up encountered here.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': At the beginning of the level, over some [[Hidden Coin|Dash Coins]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': Halfway through the level, at the top of Dash Coins and brown platforms.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Suspended in midair. A [[Koopa Shell]] or Propeller Suit is recommended.
