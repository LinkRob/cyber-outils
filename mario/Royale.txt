The '''Royale''' is a [[kart]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

== Description ==
It resembles a pink-toned classic Rolls-Royce.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart DS'' ===
[[File:RoyaleMKDS.png|thumb|left|200px|[[Princess Peach]] in her Royale in the character select screen]]
The Royale is one of [[Princess Peach]]'s three signature karts, the other two being [[Standard PC]] and [[Light Tripper]]. The Royale offers amazing [[Drift]] and [[Items (stat)|Items]], but lacks in [[Speed]] and [[Handling]], while [[Acceleration]] and [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|Weight]] are average.

Its acceleration is tied with the [[B Dasher]], and its handling is shared with Wario's [[Dragonfly]]. It shares its items stat with each character's heavier karts. 
This kart, and the [[4-Wheel Cradle]] are the heaviest karts within the lightweight class. However, the Royale also features the lowest acceleration and handling out of the karts in the lightweight class.

[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and [[Toad]] can use the Royale once the player completes all cups in the 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix, and all racers can use the Royale once all cups in the Mirror Mode have been completed.

During races, a CPU controlled Peach will choose the Royale to race with more frequently than her other karts.

Peach in the Royale is used for the staff ghosts for courses [[Delfino Square]], [[Peach Gardens]] and {{Classic course|GBA|[[Peach Circuit]]}}, with the allotted times of 1:54:601, 1:52:989 and 1:12:011 respectively.

=== ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
[[File:MKT 3DS Rainbow Road Racers.jpg|thumb|150px|Princess Peach in her Royale racing with Mario and [[Diddy Kong]] on {{Classic course|3DS|[[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 7)|Rainbow Road]]}}]]
The Royale makes a reappearance, now being classified as a Super kart. The game also introduces two variants, a white variant called the [[White Royale]], which appeared in the [[Paris Tour (2019)|2019 Paris Tour]], and also a blue variant called the [[Blue Royale]], which appeared in the [[Sydney Tour|first Sydney Tour.]] Both of these variations are classified as Super karts.
