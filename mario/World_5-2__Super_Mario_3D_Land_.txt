'''World 5-2''' is the twenty-fourth level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
The level features several top-down rooms in a maze-like pattern, filled with traps, lava pits, and enemies. The first few rooms lead in a straight path, featuring spiked bars and [[Spiny|Spinies]]. Upon reaching a circular room with a spinning spiked bar, Mario will find the level's [[Checkpoint Flag]] by going up. At the end of this next room, there is a [[Question Block]] that will send [[Mario]] into a brief bonus level to try obtaining a [[Star Medal]]. Following this room is one with a long-spiked bar that moves at a fast pace. Past here, Mario finds the last room of the level. This room holds a doorway leading to the [[Flagpole]] of the level.

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': In the circular room, Mario finds the first Star Medal by going down.
*'''Star Medal 2''': If Mario is [[Fire Mario]], he can continue going right from the very beginning and light the four torches in a room with a steel gate. The second Star Medal is on the other side.
*'''Star Medal 3''': In the room with the long-spiked bar, Mario can obtain the final Star Medal at the far end.
