'''Jelly Super''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:Jelly_Super_(PM).png|frame|left]]
Jelly Super is a [[recipe]] that [[Mario]] can make by giving a [[Jammin' Jelly]] and a [[Super Shroom]] to [[Tayce T.]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. This item has the same effects as its ingredients respectively, and a Jelly Super restores ten [[Heart Point|HP]] and fifty [[Flower Point|FP]].

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
Jelly Super is created by giving [[Zess T.]] the same ingredients required in ''Paper Mario''. The Jelly Super retains the same effects as in ''Paper Mario''.
