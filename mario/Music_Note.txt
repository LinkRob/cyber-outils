'''Music Notes''' (sometimes called '''Rainbow Notes''' or simply '''Notes''') are multicolored {{w|eighth note}}s that appear as an item in some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, namely a few 3D {{series|Super Mario}} platforming titles. Music Notes first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
Music Notes usually appear when [[Mario]] or another playable character touches a [[? Coin]], although the ones in [[Gold Leaf Galaxy]] appear if Mario rings a bell with the [[Star Cursor]]. Afterwards, the Music Notes line up, and Mario will need to grab all of them. The background music also mutes when the Music Notes are activated.

Mario must collect all of these notes in order to get anything from [[Power Star]]s, to [[1-Up]]s, to activating a [[Launch Star]] to a new area. In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', collecting every Music Note always spawn a [[Power Moon]]s, and they are instead activated from touching a [[Treble Clef]].
