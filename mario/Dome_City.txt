'''Dome City''' is a location in the [[Super Mario World (television series)|''Super Mario World'' television series]]. It is the home of [[Oogtar]] the [[Cave People|cave boy]]. Like the map screen for [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]], it is a cluster of domes, many of which are buildings. The domes appear to be based off of the spotted stacks seen in the background of several levels of ''[[Super Mario World]]'', such as [[Yoshi's Island 1]].

The domes come in all shapes, colors, and sizes. [[Mario]]'s house has a moat and windows, as seen in the image on the right. The palm trees are notably similar in the style of the [[Forest of Illusion]]'s trees, having jack-o'-lantern-like faces.
