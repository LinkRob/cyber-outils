'''Dash Socks''' are an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Description==
The Dash Socks are mainly used to run around the field at high speech, but their effects can also bloom [[Spinny Flower]]s for [[Coin]]s or allow the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] to use a [[catapult]]. The Dash Socks can be activated by holding {{button|3ds|x}} after performing a [[Team Jump]]. If the Dash Socks are used to [[dash]] into an enemy on the field, the three Mario Bros. perform a [[First Strike]] by dashing straight into the field enemy on the battle screen and damage it. Using the Dash Socks on a spiked enemy is ineffective because it this damages the Mario Bros. when the battle starts.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''==
[[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] get them from a trio of [[Paper Toad]]s in [[Sunbeam Plains]]. The Dash Socks cannot be used while [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are separate from [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] in [[Gloomy Woods]], or if Paper Mario is in a secluded area by himself.
