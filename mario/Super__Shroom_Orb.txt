A '''Super 'Shroom Orb''' is an [[Orb]] item in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' as the '''Super Mushroom Capsule'''.

==Description==
The Super 'Shroom Orb contains a gold [[Super Mushroom]] and grants the player three [[Dice Block]]s, allowing them to move up to 30 spaces. If the player rolls three identical numbers, they receive a bonus of 30 coins, while rolling three 7s awards 50 coins instead.

In ''[[Mario Party 8]]'', the Super 'Shroom Orb is replaced by the [[Thrice Candy]], which has an identical function.

==Appearances==
===''Mario Party 5''===
In ''Mario Party 5'', the Orb can be either used on the player at the cost of ten [[coin]]s or thrown onto a [[space]] on the [[board]].

===''Mario Party 6''===
The Super 'Shroom Orb returns in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and is available on all boards. Starting with this installment, it can only be used on the player and can no longer be placed on the board. It costs 20 coins at various [[Orb hut]]s, but the price is reduced to 15 coins if the player is in third place and to 10 coins if the player is in fourth place.

===''Mario Party 7''===
In ''[[Mario Party 7]]'', the Super 'Shroom Orb is available on every board except [[Windmillville]]. In eight-player mode, it allows the team to roll six Dice Blocks, although the maximum movement is still 30 spaces. If all six Dice Blocks show the same number, the team is awarded 300 coins.

==Probabilities==
{{main|Triple Dice#Probabilities}}
As the Super 'Shroom Orb functions the same as [[Triple Dice]], the probabilities of rolling certain numbers are the same.
