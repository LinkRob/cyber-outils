'''Super Jump Punch''' is the up special move used by [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]] in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. The move first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'', although it is first used by Dr. Mario in the sequel ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''.

==Description==
Super Jump Punch doubles as both an attack and a recovery move. If Mario, Luigi, or Dr. Mario hit an opponent using the move, [[Coin]]s fly out from the opponent for a few seconds before the opponent is launched. The move is based on the 2D sidescrolling {{series|Super Mario}} games, particularly whenever Mario or Luigi hit a [[Block]] containing a Coin. The Coins are only for aesthetic, and they have no gameplay purpose.
