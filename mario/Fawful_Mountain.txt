The '''Fawful Mountain''' is a giant obstacle that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|3DS]] [[reissue]]. It is a large mountain resembling [[Fawful]]. It is involved in the boss fight against the [[Fawful Express]].

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' ==
=== Battle ===
The Fawful Mountain serves as a protection for the Fawful Express. When the Fawful Express hides inside the Fawful Mountain, it can restore its [[Heart Point|health]]. The mountain is severely weak against [[Bowser]]'s [[Fire Breath]].

==== Attacks ====
The Fawful Mountain has the ability to perform a Body Slam. The player then needs to push the Mountain away by using the touchscreen. If the player succeeds the Fawful Mountain will collide against a tunnel.

The Mountain can also call upon clouds that resemble Fawful to help him. White cloud heal him while grey clouds attack [[Bowser]]. Bowser then needs to shoot fire breath to the direction the clouds are currently located in.
