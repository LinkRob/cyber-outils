'''Hot Dogs''' are an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Hot Dog.png|frame|left]]
Hot Dogs are bought at the hot dog stand in [[Glitzville]], where they are sold for ten [[coin]]s each. The item heals five [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]]. As part of a trouble posted at the [[Trouble Center]], if Mario takes two Hot Dogs and a [[Mousse Cake]] to [[Bub-ulber]] in [[Petalburg]], the [[Bub-ulb]] gives Mario a useful [[Dried Bouquet]] which can be used in a lot of recipes. Mario can also get more Dried Bouquets from Bub-ulber in exchange for Hot Dogs (as this is the only way to get Dried Bouquets in the game).

One of the other known forms of Hot Dog was the [[Southern Fried Egg Dog of Tastiness]], a Hot Dog made with an egg from a "southern island," possibly either [[Lavalava Island]] or [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]]. However, the Yoshi Egg that [[Mr. Hoggle]] planned to make the Hot Dog out of did not cooperate as it broke open with, revealing the [[Yoshi Kid]] and resulting in no Southern Fried Egg Dogs of Tastiness being sold.

Another form of Hot Dog involved mixing the [[Businessman]]'s [[Hot Sauce]] with another of [[Mr. Hoggle]]'s Hot Dogs, the [[Fat Dog]], to create a Hot Dog known as the Tongue Blistering Fat Dog, but it is unknown if it ever happened.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
The Hot Dog makes a reappearance in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' as a [[recipe]]. [[Dyllis]] can mix a [[Power Steak]] and a [[Fresh Veggie]] to create a Hot Dog, or it can be bought at an [[Itty Bits]] in The [[Overthere]] for 120 coins. Using a Hot Dog restores 20 HP and cures Poison.
