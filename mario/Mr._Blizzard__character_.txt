'''Mr. Blizzard''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mr. Blizzard is the namesake character representative of his [[Mr. Blizzard|species]], which he shares an appearance with.

===Behavior===
Mr. Blizzard is fond of baseball, and his description in ''Mario Party Advance'' mentions that he is a member of the [[Snowtown Ice Stars]] team and that he regularly plays at the [[Ice Stadium]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
[[File:Mr Blizzard MPA screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Mr. Blizzard telling the player's character that he is busy practicing his pitches]]
The player's character encounters Mr. Blizzard in the Ice Stadium, where he is , and is annoyed if he's approached while practicing. Once he asks what the player is doing there, they ask him to pitch some snowballs at them, and accepting this starts his associated quest, [[Cool as Ice]].

In his quest, Mr. Blizzard says that he will reward the player's character with a [[Gaddget]] if they score a home run. Once they do, Mr. Blizzard passes it off by saying that he was not trying, but still rewards them the [[Mini Slugger]]. After this, Mr. Blizzard continues practicing his pitches.
