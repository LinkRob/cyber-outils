'''Badge Points''', also known as '''BP''', are a recurring feature in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. Badge Points enable [[Mario]] to equip [[badge]]s, with each individual one coming with a set number of Badge Points needed to be used.

== In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
[[File:BadgePointPM2.png|thumb|left]]
In [[Paper Mario]], at the beginning of the game, the amount of Badge Points Mario possesses is three and goes up by three every time he levels up and chooses to upgrade BP. Mario can acquire a maximum of 30 Badge Points.

In [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]], Badge points work similarly to those in the previous game, however Mario can reach a maximum of 99 instead of 30. Additionally, Mario's partners can use [[:Category:Partner Badges|partner badges]], but they take from Mario's BP to use them.
