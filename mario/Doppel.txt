'''Doppels''' are ghost enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are found in [[Smithy Factory]], often alongside other ghost enemies. Doppels are relatives of [[Shadow (enemy)|Shadows]].

==Battle==
In battle, Doppels are fast and can attack with physical attacks that deal high damage. They can also use special attacks such as [[Echofinder]] to weaken [[Mario]]'s party.

===Strategy===
Being ghosts, the Doppel's only weakness is [[Pure Water]] which should be used given their high [[HP]].
