A '''Gold Piranha Plant''' is an enemy which first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Gold Piranha Plants are a variant of [[Piranha Plant]], which they resemble, except a Gold Piranha Plant has a golden body while still retaining the same white spots on their head and white lips as regular Piranha Plants have.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''==
[[File:GoldPiranhaPlantNSMB2.png|thumb|left]]
Every Piranha Plant becomes a Gold Piranha Plant while the [[Gold Ring (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|Gold Ring]] is in effect. They release coins from the pipes where they are in when defeated. Aside from that, they can be defeated the same way like ordinary Piranha Plants.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''[[Mario Party 10]]''===
Gold Piranha Plants appear in the minigame [[Pipe Sniper]], in which shooting one with an orb earns that character three [[point]]s, instead of one point like with regular Piranha Plants.

===''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''===
Gold Piranha Plants appear in the minigame [[Piranha Plantemonium]], in which shooting one earns a character three points, instead of one point like with a Piranha Plant. Large Gold Piranha Plants with a health bar can occasionally appear, and require several hits to take down. Whoever defeats it earns ten points.
