'''Flitter''' is an enemy in the [[Donkey Kong (series)|''Donkey Kong series'']]. It is an enemy that flies in the air and resembles a blue dragonfly. This enemy can stay and fly in one position or fly in a straight line left or right until the Kongs lose them. Flitters have very similar abilities to [[Hogwash]]es and [[Necky|Neckies]] as all three fly in a straight pattern or stay stable in the air. They can also be used to access high areas when jumped on. 

Flitters reappear in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'' where they have no major changes and once again reappear as enemies on [[Crocodile Isle]]. They also appear in the trailer for ''[[Donkey Kong Racing]]'', but it is unknown what their role is.

Flitters reappear in ''[[DK: King of Swing]]'' and ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]'' as enemies when they are charged into instead of being jumped on, only leading into the result of [[Donkey Kong]] becoming injured. They have the same traits in ''DK: Jungle Climber''.
