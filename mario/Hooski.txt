'''Hooskis''' are a species that appeared in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They live mainly on [[Pi'illo Island]] of the [[Pi'illo Kingdom]] and they are remarkably similar to [[Hoohooligan]]s from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

== Appearance ==
Hooskis are similar in appearance to Hoohooligans except they're brown in color.

=== Notable Hooskis ===
*[[Big Massif]]: The oldest of the Massif Bros. He, at his own will, went to the [[Dream World|dream world]] to that he can get stronger. He was eventually fought and defeated and went back to the real world to aid in the tour of [[Mount Pajamaja]].
*[[Lil' Massif]]: The youngest of the Massif Bros. He asked [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to get his brother in order to start the tour.
*[[Beef-Off#Hooraws|Hooraws]]: A group of martial artists that are only fought in [[Dreamy Wakeport]]. They must be defeated in order to fight Big Massif.
