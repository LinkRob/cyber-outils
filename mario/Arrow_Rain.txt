|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Arrow Rain''' is a special move used only by [[Smithy]]'s magic head in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Arrow Rain strikes all characters on the screen for high damage. There is no blocking against this attack and is similar to Smithy's other attack, [[Sword Rain]].
