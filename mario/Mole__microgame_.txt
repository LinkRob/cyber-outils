}}'''Mole''' is [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s microgame in ''[[Game & Wario]]'' and reappears as the only original microgame from ''Game & Wario'' as [[Mona]] microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player has a few seconds to study the person's face. Then they are questioned by the number of moles. The character smiles and the player wins the game when they click the correct answer.
=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
The microgame has a character in each difficulty.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The character is a girl which has no distractions.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The character is a trembling old man.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The character is a woman whose makeup had melted that her eyes are identical to moles.
