'''World 2-4''' is the fourth and final level of [[World 2 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 2]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the eighth level overall in said game. This level reappears in ''[[All Night Nippon: Super Mario Bros.]]'' as World 5-4.

==Layout==
The player starts at the top of some stairs, which lead into a passage the player needs to jump through, avoiding the [[Koopa Troopa]] above. The passage ends at a drop into a [[lava]] pit, which the player needs to avoid by moving right as they fall. After a row of [[Goomba]]s and Koopa Troopas, there is a [[Fire Bar]] before a lava pit with a set of stairs across it, above which and against the wall is a [[Hidden Block]] with a power-up. Next is a hallway split between an upper and lower path, the top contains more Fire Bars and the bottom contains small lava pits, and a [[Lava Bubble]] jumps between both. After the hall are large lava pits with Fire Bars between them and Lava Bubbles jumping out of them before the [[Fake Bowser]].
