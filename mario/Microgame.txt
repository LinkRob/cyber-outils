'''Microgames''' are very short minigames lasting for only a few seconds and a core feature within every ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' game, first appearing ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. Microgames come in different genres and settings depending on their hosts. They rely on the player's quick reflexes, especially on higher difficulty settings, which often add an extra task. Increased difficulty with microgames is also featured in the form of speeding up the gameplay. Before a microgame starts, the player's only instruction is provided in the form of a single verb with an exclamation mark at the end (usually; some use phrases or nouns).
