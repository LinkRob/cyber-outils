'''VoidCo''' (also called '''Void Company''') is the main antagonistic group that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. Lead by [[Void Kong]] alongside his associates [[Grumpy Kong]] and [[Poppy Kong]], VoidCo's main goal is to drill through [[Ingot Isle]] to reach the [[Planet Core]] in order to earn a wish for riches.
