The '''Ukiki Robber''' is a minor character from [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'']]. He is a thief working for [[Wart]] in the [[Wart's Casino|casino]] within the [[Gateway Galaxy]].

== Profile ==

=== Personality ===
The Ukiki Robber is manipulative, using his cuteness to lure unsuspecting targets close to him, allowing him to get his hands on his target's belongings.

=== Appearance ===
The Ukiki Robber is indistinguishable from the other [[Ukiki|Ukikis]].

=== Powers and Abilities ===
While the Ukiki Robber is not particularly strong, he is fast and manipulative.

== History ==
The Ukiki Robber first appears stealing the backpack of [[Toad (character)|Toad]] while the latter and [[Princess Peach]] are visiting the [[Gateway Galaxy]] in their search for someone who can get to [[Planet Bowser]]. As the robber escapes, he is chased by Toad and Peach into a hidden futuristic city inhabited by [[Koopa Troop]] members, leading the two into a [[Wart's Casino|casino]] that [[Wart]] and his goons run. After Peach and Toad defeat Wart, Toad retrieves his backpack. The Ukiki makes one last appearance during the second credits scene, using his cuteness to manipulate a [[Whittle]] and steal her belongings, but [[Princess Daisy]] attacks him, defeating the Ukiki Robber for good.
