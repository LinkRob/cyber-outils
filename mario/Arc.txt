'''Arc''' is a character from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. He is the son of [[Patriarc]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Arc is a small, green-skinned Concordian boy. His outfit consists of a dark blue beret with a compass attached to it and a sailor's uniform.

=== Personality ===
Arc is knowledgeable on ocean currents, due to his goals of following in his father's footsteps as an explorer.

== History ==

=== Backstory ===
Prior to the game's events, Arc found his spyglass after [[Technikki]], who created it for a contest in what would later become Merrygo Island, threw it into the sea after she lost.

Later, when [[Zokket]] tore [[Concordia]] apart, Arc and his father were stranded on Raynforst Island, and later kidnapped by [[Snaptor|Snaptors]] taken to one of the Great Lighthouses. Arc escaped when his father stuffed him into a capsule, which would later wash ashore on [[Shipshape Island]] after the Bros. link Rumbla Island to it.

Arc makes his first appearance in the game when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]], having linked Rumbla Island, spot Arc's capsule and receive a map from him. The map shows the locations of the other islands, where [[Maykit and Billdit]] ended up. Upon getting their hammers from the first child of [[Chief Willma]], Mario and Luigi bust open Arc's capsule. Following his liberation, Arc explains how to use the map, which proves key in finding the second child.

Throughout the game, Arc serves as the player's guide on currents. Additionally, there is a side quest involving his spyglass, which he cares a lot about. The Bros. can find it on Raynforst Island.

During the [[Reclusa World|final leg of the game]], Arc ends up one of [[Reclusa]]'s victims after it wakes up and captures many in its [[Reclusa Dream Flower|flowers]]. He breaks free after the Bros. defeat and kill Reclusa. During the ending cutscene, Arc and [[Furen]] hang out, when Patriarc returns. Arc and his father hug as they reunite.

== Behind the Scenes ==
=== Etymology ===
While the name itself is a common name, where Arc derives his name from can be any number of several sources, with one referring to a curved line relating to his expertise in mapping, Noah's Ark (a Biblical ship), an electric arc (an electrical breakdown of a gas that produces a prolonged electrical discharge), or an island arc (referencing Arc's ability to track drifting islands on currents).

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Arc is known as "Sensamīru" (written センサミール), a portmanteau of a transliteration of "sensor" and ''miru'' ("to look"; written 見る). His Chinese name, ''Shàn'guān'' (written 善觀), literally means "good look." Spanish translates his name as ''Simonel'', from the male given name ''Simón'' ("Simon") and ''timonel'' ("helmsman"). His Portuguese name is ''marú'', from ''marujo'' ("sailor"). Italian calls him ''Poletto'', a diminutive of ''polo'' ("pole" in the geographic sense referencing his cartographer job), although it could also be a combination of ''Paoletto'' (a diminutive of ''Páolo'' ("Paul")) and ''polarità'' ("polarity" in the electric sense). In French, his name is ''Archibald'', which, while a given name, could reference arcs in the same senses as his English name. Dutch translates his name as ''Elmus'', which is a reference to a "Saint Elmo's fire," referring to electrical discharges seen around pointed objects, caused bio ionization of the atmosphere during a storm. In German, he is referred to as ''Stromas'', from ''Strom'' ("electricity"). Arc has the name ''Senseo'rukeu'' (a portmeanteu of a Korean transliteration of "sensor" and "look"; written 센서루크) in the Korean dub.
