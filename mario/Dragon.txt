'''Dragons''' are one of the creatures that assist the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] during their journey in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. They are serpentine-like dragons that a Yoshi can ride on to travel across areas that the Yoshi cannot do by themselves. A Dragon only appears in two stages, [[Cloud Cruising]] and [[Magma Castle]]. The former level features a Dragon with a light green body, and it moves the Yoshi across the air, while the latter level features a Dragon with a red body that carries a Yoshi across the [[lava]].
