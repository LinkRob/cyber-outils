'''''Unjust Desserts''''' is the eleventh book in the ''[[Nintendo Adventure Books]]'' series. The book was written by {{wp|Matt Wayne}} and published in March 1992.<ref name="English"/> Its title is a pun on the phrase "[[wiktionary:just deserts|just deserts]]."

==Plot==
Mario, Luigi, Princess Toadstool, Toad, and the Mushroom King are holding a party for Yoshi's birthday, and all of their Toad and dinosaur friends are there to celebrate. But when Yoshi suddenly eats the mysterious purplish cherry on his birthday cake, he begins to eat everything in sight: pizza, ravioli, everything he can, and then he eats Luigi. This stops the devouring problem, but now they have a new dilemma on their hands—how to get Luigi out of Yoshi's stomach.

A mushroom named Fungus Cap tries to convince them not to call the Yoshi doctor, Doctor Drake. Instead, he suggests they give Yoshi a pill from a bottle he has in his hands. The heroes sense something strange about this Toad, and Mario soon notices that he is not what he seems, because it is Bowser. The Koopa King reveals himself, saying he had fun with Yoshi and his evil cherry, and escapes through a window.

Meanwhile, they call Doc Drake, who arrives at the birthday hall promptly. He tells Mario to drink his special shrinking potion, which will make him a lot smaller, allowing him to go into Yoshi and retrieve Luigi. Mario drinks it, and the Princess places him in Yoshi's mouth. Mario slips down into his stomach, carrying the shrinking potion for Luigi to drink.

But, of course, there's more trouble: Bowser Koopa has been shrunk and is in Yoshi's stomach too. While Mario and Bowser duel, Luigi hears that Yoshi should eat a flower called a threelip, which should make Yoshi spit Luigi out. Yoshi eats the threelip, and spits out Luigi, Mario, Bowser, and Bowser's troops. Mario uses a spare fire flower to rid Bowser's troops, and Bowser retreats.

Meanwhile, Princess Toadstool shouts to Mario, telling him to get to some magic daisies, which are near him, but are very high up. Using a tanooki suit, Mario reaches the daisies, takes a bite out of one of the petals, and grows back to his original size, returning everything to normal.
