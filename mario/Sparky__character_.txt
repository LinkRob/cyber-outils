'''Sparky''' is a [[snow spirits|snow spirit]] in the [[Adventure Tours]] mode in the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sparky is a pink snow spirit who has the same snowflake-like shape of the other snow spirits.

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''==
Sparky is the snow spirit of [[Sparkleton]] and the second one encountered by [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]]. The two characters first find Sparky under the protection of [[Eggman Nega]]. After defeating him, they free Sparky and it joins the team until the next snow spirit is saved, as then it will go back to Sparkleton. If the player has 4 pink crystals, it can break ice walls.

After Sparky returns to Sparkleton, the player obtains the Sparky Emblem.
