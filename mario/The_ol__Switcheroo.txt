'''The 'ol Switcheroo''' is one of the [[World-e]] levels in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', in both the Japanese and North American versions. For the [[Wii U]] [[Virtual Console]] and [[Nintendo Switch Online]] ports, it is included in the base game in all regional releases.

==Layout==
The level takes place in both a desert area with some waterfalls and inside of a castle-like environment underground area. In the latter environment, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] has to activate a [[P Switch]] to cross a wide [[lava]] pit, since all the [[coin]]s above the lava turn into [[Brick Block]]s.
