'''World 5-3''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Enemies==
*[[Goomba]]
*[[Koopa Troopa|Green Koopa Troopa]]
*[[Koopa Troopa|Red Koopa Troopa]]
*[[Snailicorn]]

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' Slide down the hill until you see ice blocks, when you do, jump on them and grab the Star Coin Midair.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After getting the [[Checkpoint Flag|checkpoint]], go until you see a 1 block tunnel, then duck and slide into the tunnel after this the Star Coin is inside a cage of [[Brick Block|bricks]].
*'''Star Coin 3''': After the second, you should find the first Snailicorn go until you see a shaft wall jump up to get the Star Coin.
