The '''Rolling Green Galaxy''' is a galaxy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It vastly resembles a golf-course, and in it, [[Mario]] balances on a large ball, and needs to maneuver the ball through the galaxy in order to locate the hole that will open it. Doing so will enable him to receive the [[Power Star]]. To keep the ball stationary, the player will need to keep the [[Wii|Wii Remote]] facing up like a joystick; tilting it any direction will result in Mario moving in that direction.

== Missions ==
=== Rolling in the Clouds ===
In this mission, you have to stand on top of a rolling ball with a star inside it. You must roll the ball through several obstacle courses, including sections with ? Coins that spawn starbits and goombas. Once you get to the end, the ball explodes and you can collect the Star.

== Music ==

The music here is a remix of music that plays in the obstacle course levels of ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.
