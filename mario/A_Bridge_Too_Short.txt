'''A Bridge Too Short''' is a luck-based single-player [[Donkey Kong]] [[minigame]] featured in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. Its title is a pun on the 1974 book ''[[wikipedia:A Bridge Too Far (book)|A Bridge Too Far]]'' by {{wp|Cornelius Ryan}}.

==Opening==
The player walks up to the first pair of [[bridge]]s, the screen splits to show Donkey Kong, and then the game starts.

==Gameplay==
The player races against Donkey Kong to reach the finish line. The player has to pick one of the two bridges to cross. There are four pairs of bridges that lead to the finish line, each pair having a correct bridge and a hidden broken bridge. If the player crosses a bridge and it suddenly breaks, they lose some time to get to the other bridge. If the player picks the correct bridge, they proceed to the second pair of bridges and so on until they reach the finish line. The player must do so before Donkey Kong to win.

Donkey Kong usually takes a little while to choose a bridge. Whenever the player makes the same bridge choices as Donkey Kong, if his bridge breaks, so does the player's. As a result, the player can potentially avoid falling behind if they let Donkey Kong choose a bridge first, but the player still has to be ahead of him to win the minigame.

==Trivia==
*This is the only single-player Donkey Kong minigame from ''Mario Party 7'' not to return in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.
