'''Bronze Coin''' is an item in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
A Bronze Coin is a type of [[Coin (Wario Land 4)|Coin]] worth fifty [[point]]s upon being collected. They have the second lowest value out of any Coin, behind [[Small Coin]]s, which are only worth ten points. A Bronze Coin can be obtained by defeating minor enemies and breaking blocks. In [[Pinball Zone]], they can also be obtained by throwing a [[Chomp ball]] into a [[pinball tulip]].
