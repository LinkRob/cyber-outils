'''Music Mischief''' is a [[microgame]] featured in [[Mona]]'s [[That's Life]] level in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to remove a pair of headphones or earbuds from a boy's ears so that the music stops disturbing his two classmates next to him. If done successfully, the player wins the microgame, and the boy gives a shocked reaction while his peers act relieved.

[[18-Volt]], [[Orbulon]], and [[5-Volt]] are listed as the three most suitable characters for the microgame: 18-Volt can repeatedly shoot at the earbuds or headphones to knock them away; Orbulon can vacuum either the headphones or earbuds into his [[Oinker]], and 5-Volt's warp has enough force to knock away either the headphones or earbuds.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The boy is wearing a pair of headphones.
*'''Level 2:''' The boy is wearing a pair of earbuds, which must be taken out separately from each other.
*'''Level 3:''' Features the same setup as Level 2, except the boy moves his head left and right.

In two-player mode, the boy wears a pair of shining sunglasses that also has to be removed during Levels 2 and 3. Whoever removes more objects in either of the two levels receives an effort point.

==Mission==
*'''Hip Hop''': [[Lulu]] has to complete Level 3 by removing the earbuds using only her [[Ground Pound (move)|ground pound]].

==Trivia==
*The music that the boy listens to is an excerpt of [[Mona Pizza (song)|Mona Pizza]].
