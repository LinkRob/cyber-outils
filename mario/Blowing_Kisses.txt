'''Blowing Kisses''' is a [[Microgame]] from [[WarioWare: Move It!|''WarioWare: Move It!'']]. It is set in [[Jimmy Thang]]'s folder (and therefore can appear in the levels Surfin' Surprise and Pool Party Panic), and uses the Lovestruck form for its gameplay.

== Layout ==
A redheaded princess serves as the microgame's protagonist, and she wears a long-sleeved blue dress. The floor is lavender with two spots and purple trails leading from the princess to them; each spot is dark purple until a frog is on it, at which point it will turn yellow.

== Gameplay ==
To win, the player must help the princess blow a kiss toward a cursed prince, who is trapped in frog form. If the player succeeds, the prince returns to his true form.

=== Difficulty Levels ===
Levels 2 and 3 have rats that serve as obstacles. The rats move back and fourth, but their movement speeds are the same. The player is defeated if the princess kisses a rat, in which case she turns the rat into a witch, who turns the princess into a frog in retaliation.
