'''Bounce''' is a status effect in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
There are weapons that can inflict Bounce on a foe. Whoever is affected by Bounce will be flung into the direction from where they were hit. Bounce will inflict an "out of bounds" damage bonus to the foe. The target becomes vulnerable to  line-of-sight attacks.
