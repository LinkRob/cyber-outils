'''Scooter Pursuit''' is a 4-player minigame that appears in the game ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
The players are located in a large field and are riding on scooters to battle other players. The player will earn points if he/she can manage to hit the other players with cannonballs in the field. If a player is hit by a cannonball, then the target will be stunned for a moment and cannot shoot cannonballs and soon after the player will recover. The layout of the field has circular platforms which can prevent the players from shooting cannonballs through it. The player with the most points will win the game.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|wiimote}} - Tilt to Move
*{{button|wii|2}} - Shoot

==Trivia==
*The gameplay of the minigame is somewhat similar to the ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' minigame, [[Cannonball Fun]]. However, the mini-game has different mechanics.
