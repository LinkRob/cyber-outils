'''Shrink Stomp''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and later returns in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Shrink Stomp costs one [[Badge Point|BP]], and while wearing it, [[Mario]] can unleash a new [[boots|jump]] attack that can shrink enemies in battle for two [[Flower Point|FP]]. When an enemy or even Mario is shrunk, their attack is lowered by 2.

== In ''Paper Mario''==
Shrink Stomp is found in [[Toad Town Tunnels]]. This badge is guarded by a [[Blooper]], however, this badge could be guarded by the [[Electro Blooper]] or the [[Super Blooper]], depending on which have been defeated at the time Mario and his party enter this area.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
Shrink Stomp is found in [[The Great Tree]]. It is also in the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]], where it costs 75 [[coin]]s to purchase. Shrink Stomp retains all of its powers and effects from in ''Paper Mario'' as well. Wearing multiple Shrink Stomp badges can result in more damage from the attack.
