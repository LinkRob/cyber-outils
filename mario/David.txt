'''David''' is a common first name used for boys. Several people involved with ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' and related media have "David" as their first name:

*[[David Horowitz]], a news reporter who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[The Marios Fight Back]]," being portrayed by himself.
*[[David J. Goldfarb]], a voice actor who portrays [[Ludwig von Koopa]].
*[[David Cooke]], a voice actor who portrays [[Morton Koopa Jr.]]
*[[David S. J. Hodgson]], a strategy guide author for [[Prima Games]].
*[[David Wise]], a British composer who works for [[Rare Ltd.]].
