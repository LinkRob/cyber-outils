The '''Jump Seal''' is an item which appears in both ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
The Jump Seal is a lock-on item that, upon hitting a racer, attaches to their kart, weighing them down and preventing them from being able to [[hop]], and therefore [[drift]], temporarily.
