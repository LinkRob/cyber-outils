'''Sparring Wario''' is the boss [[microgame]] of [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of [[Wario]], who has to to destroy a punching bag. The only action Wario can perform is punch, which is done by pressing {{button|gba|A}}. Whenever Wario hits the bag, it swings higher to the right. After a number of hits, the bag swings high enough to go all the way around. When this occurs, Wario automatically charges a finishing move, and he has to execute it with proper timing to destroy the punching bag, causing it to break off into segments. Wario is shown performing a victory animation, and the player wins at the microgame. If Wario gets smacked by the punching bag or if he does not perform any action, the bag detaches from its chain and rolls toward Wario, knocking him over and causing the player to lose at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
The punching bag becomes heavier with each difficulty level, and therefore requires more punches from Wario to be destroyed.

*'''Level 1:''' It takes five punches to destroy the punching bag.
*'''Level 2:''' It takes seven punches to destroy the punching bag.
*'''Level 3:''' It takes nine punches to destroy the punching bag.
