{{Stub}}[[File:Endless Challenge (Easy).jpeg|250px|thumb|Endless Challenge on Easy difficulty]] '''Endless Challenge''' is a game mode in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' in which the player starts with a certain amount of lives (depending on difficulty) the player then can play an endless amount of user-generated levels until they lose all their lives. The objective of the mode is to complete as many levels as possible.
