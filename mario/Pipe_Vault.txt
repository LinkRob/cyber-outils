'''Pipe Vault''' is a location in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
The Pipe Vault is an underground area that could be based on [[World 1-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-2]] from the original ''[[Super Mario Bros]]'', especially as the Underground Theme plays here.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[Mario]] and his team decide to take a brief detour through the Pipe Vault. Mario avoids and battles many classic enemies along the way. Eventually, Mario reaches the end and can now access [[Yo'ster Isle]].
