'''Candles''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''. Like [[Oil]]s, Candles just float up and down and injure [[Mario]] if he touches them. A Candle can only be defeated by using a [[hammer]]. Candles make a reappearance in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''.
