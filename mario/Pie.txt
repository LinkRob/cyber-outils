'''Pie''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and its two sequels, ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
A Pie can be thrown at another racer and, if hit, the racer's screen becomes covered in pie cream. In this regard, the Pie is similar to the [[Blooper]] item in the mainline ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' games, as using a Blooper also obscures a racer's view, which in the Blooper's case is from squirting ink.

''Mario Kart Arcade GP DX'' introduces a similar pie-based item named the [[Pie Bazooka]], which can throw a Pie forwards.
