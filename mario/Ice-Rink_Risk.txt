'''Ice-Rink Risk''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'', and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''; in the former two games, the minigame title is spelled '''Ice Rink Risk'''.

==Layout==
The game takes place in a tall enclosed blue- and yellow-bricked ice rink with a [[Spiny]] imprinted in it.

==Gameplay==
[[File:MP3 Luigi Hurt by Spiny Artwork.jpg|150px|thumb|left|Artwork of [[Luigi]] and a Spiny Shell based on the minigame]]
Players have to avoid being hit by [[Spiny Shell]]s while running away on the slippery ice that the players are on. One Spiny Shell is dropped onto the rink at the start, followed by another 10 and 20 seconds in. Players can also [[jump]] on other players to stun them, making them more likely to be hit by Spiny Shells. Any players standing after 30 seconds win.

This may cause them to infinitely fall, which means they can no longer play.
