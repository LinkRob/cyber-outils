=== Pinna Park Beach ===
Files and text strings for a scrapped shine sprite, that seems to be gathered across four episodes, According to the text string, it's mostly related about a family of tourists making their arrival to Hotel Delfino.

In the first one, a mother (who is a tourist) noticed her daughter has gone missing and asks Mario to find her. The text also has interactions with other NPCs, which never happens in the final game outside of cutscenes. It's also possible that human girl (from Spaceworld 2001 Trailer) and the cut Ice Cream cart are connected to this event.

In the second one, it explains a Pinata who's guarding a flood gate (most perversely a dam) as it becomes old and falls apart. Mario could at one point interact with the gate too.

In the third one, [[Nico]] tells Mario that her father has been missing for quite a while when he was on his way to get ice cream, having the player to then start searching for his whereabouts.

In the fourth one, the mayor is having a difficult time with a mess on Delfino Plaza and (asking for Mario's help) is hoping to stick with the five star ranking streak the island has been getting.

There's also a side mission where the player can help someone named "Rownie". If the player decides to help the character, Mario will then receive a Reverse Nozzle, but will break if it is damaged. As Rownie explains what the nozzle does (and given the name is temporary), this is an early name for the Turbo Nozzle. However, none of Nozzles get broken upon Mario getting hurt referring to a feature that was scrapped in early development.
