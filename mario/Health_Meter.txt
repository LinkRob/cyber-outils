The '''Health Meter''' is an on-screen indicator of how much health [[Mario]] or another playable character has remaining. The first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game it appears in is ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', but it was carried over from ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]''. Many games feature their own specific Health Meters, such as the [[Power Meter]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

Health Meters typically do not appear in the 2D [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] installments because the health is instead represented by the form that Mario or another playable character is in.
