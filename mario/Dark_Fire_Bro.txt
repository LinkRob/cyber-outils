'''Dark Fire Bros.''' are enemies in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are stronger versions of [[Fire Bro]]s that can only be found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]].

==History==
Dark Fire Bros. attack by spitting out fireballs that can deal a lot of damage. They can be dealt with by almost any attack but players should be wary of their long distance fireballs. [[Princess Peach]]'s parasol is a good method to avoid taking any damage from the fireballs and quickly defeating them.
