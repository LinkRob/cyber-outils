'''Goobs''' are the most common kind of ghost in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', it is the first kind of ghost fought in the game. They behave in an identical to [[Greenie]]s in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. Goobs always act mischievous and attack [[Luigi]] by punching. They have 100HP.

== Profile ==
Goobs are blue ghosts with white eyes and mouth. In the [[Grand Lobby]], there were Goobs disguised into human staffs, they were wearing masks with the [[Steward]]. The Goobs show their true form when Luigi enters the Lobby during night. Like Greenies, they sometimes use weapons or clothing to protect themselves, such as mask, shield or sunglasses.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3'' ==
In the beginning of the game, Goobs disguised themselves as human staffs. After the hotel's "makeover", Goobs are rid of their disguises and wreak havoc around the hotel, appearing on the various floors of The Last Resort.

During [[Amadeus Wolfgeist]]'s second phase, he summons [[Twirling Goob]]s wearing masks which attack by spinning.

[[Director Morty]] filmed a movie which involved Luigi fighting a Goobs in a kaiju costume.

During [[DJ Phantasmagloria]]'s fight, Goobs appear dressed as hip-hop dancers, which will try and hide the 15th floor's elevator button in their hoods.

At the end of the game, it was revealed that the ghosts of The Last Resort were being controlled by [[King Boo]]'s gem. With the hotel destroyed, the reformed ghosts, including Goobs help Luigi and friends to rebuild the hotel.
