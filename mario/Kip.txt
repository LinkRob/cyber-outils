Kip is a Kremling child that appears as a playable character in ''Donkey Kong Barrel Blast''. He was made to rival Diddy Kong, and as such he has the same racing stats as him. His relationship with [[Kass]] is unknown. His PAL trophy in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' states that he likes donuts, rushes into situations, and is cherished by the Kremlings because of his young age.
