'''Pretty Plaza Panic''' is a level found in World 3 of the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The level is basic, featuring some rotating platforms, that are a quarter or half circles. It also has some fountains that spurt out water, and some pillars that lead to the top of the flagpole. Also, near where you find the stamp, there is a log type floating bridge to cross, but it is optional to cross it.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Jump over the fence behind the star of the level and on the invisible cloud with a green star.
*'''Green Star 2''': After crossing the first set of spinning platforms, catch the [[Rabbit]] on the second non-spinning platform to get the next star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Jump on the pillar below the Lakitu Cloud to get to the cloud area. Get to the end to get the star.

==Stamp==
[[File:Pretty_Plaza_Panic_Stamp.png|thumb]]By the second set of spinning platforms, go on the small platforms and hit on the left the button as Peach to get the Small Peach stamp.
