==History==
Lanky can purchase his Grape Shooter at [[Funky's Store]] once he is freed by [[Donkey Kong]] in [[Angry Aztec]] and has 3 [[Banana Coin]]s. Lanky wields a blowgun that is made of bamboo and can shoot out grapes. Lanky will then be able to press Grape Switches and defeat enemies from afar.
