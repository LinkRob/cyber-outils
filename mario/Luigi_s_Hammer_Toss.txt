'''Luigi's Hammer Toss Game''' is a [[Super Mario Bros. Watch]] released by [[Nintendo]] in 1990. The game has you play as [[Mario]]'s brother [[Luigi]] where you earn points by moving Luigi left and right to deflect the [[Hammer]]s thrown by two [[Lakitu's Cloud|cloud]]-riding [[Hammer Bro|Hammer Bros.]] with his shield. This is the first game starring Luigi, followed by ''[[Mario is Missing!]]'' in 1992.
