'''Anvil''' is an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]''.

==Physical description==
They are turquoise anvils with a pair of wings.

== In ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]'' ==
Anvils are enemies that assist [[Wendy O. Koopa]] during her boss fight. The player can control [[Mario]] to shoot the anvils, causing them to drop downwards, depending on their position. If an anvil is shot above Wendy, it falls onto Wendy, injuring her.
