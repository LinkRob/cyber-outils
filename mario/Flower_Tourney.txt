The '''Flower Tourney''' is the second tournament in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It is unlocked by completing [[Mushroom Tourney]]. It consists of four courts: [[DK Cruiser]], [[Luigi's Mansion (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Luigi Mansion]], [[Daisy Garden]], and the unlockable [[Malboro Garden]]. Completing the Flower Tourney unlocks the [[Star Tourney]].

==Unlockables==
Various content can be unlocked by completing Flower Tourney on a certain difficulty or by obtaining a bronze, silver, or gold prize at minimum, depending on the item. 

*[[Dixie Kong]]: Complete on normal difficulty with at least a bronze prize.
*Daisy alternate costume: Complete on normal difficulty with at least a silver prize or higher.
*Watermelon Ball: Complete on normal difficulty with a gold prize.
*[[Boo]]: Complete on hard difficulty with at least a bronze prize.
*[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] alternate costume: Complete on hard difficulty with at least a silver prize.
*Dice Ball: Complete on hard difficulty with a gold prize.
