The '''Daytripper''' (also known as the '''Royal Racer''' in British English releases of ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'') is a kart that appears in the {{series|Mario Kart}}.

== Description ==
It is an old-fashioned, decorated, horse-drawn coach, with two wings equipped on each side and the back tires are larger than the front. The characters' emblem is located on the sides and back of the kart.

== In {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
=== ''Mario Kart Wii'' ===
[[File:MKW Day Tripper.png|thumb|160px|Princess Peach's Daytripper in ''Mario Kart Wii''.]]
The Daytripper debuted in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', as an unlockable kart. To unlock it, the [[Leaf Cup]] in [[Engine Class|150cc]] must be completed in first place on the, or if 2,700 races are played. The kart is based for [[Princess Peach]] and [[Princess Daisy]]. Its [[bike]] counterpart is the [[Sugarscoot]].

The Daytripper offers good [[acceleration]], [[handling]], [[Off-Road|off-road]] and [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]], but has below average [[speed]] and [[drift]]. It also has slightly above average [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]].

The Daytripper was used by Normal Staff Ghosts, Nin★Toki with Princess Daisy, with the allotted time of 01:56.822 in [[Daisy Circuit]] and Nin★HIRO with Princess Peach in [[Peach Beach|<small>GCN</small> Peach Beach]], with the allotted time of 01:32.233.

==== Color schemes ====
*[[Mario]]: Red and white body, gold back.
*[[Luigi]]: Green and white body, silver back.
*Princess Peach: Pink and white body, white back.
*Princess Daisy: Orange and white body, gold back.
*[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]: Teal green and white body, lime green back.
*[[Birdo]]: Dull purple and white body, dark pink back.
*[[Diddy Kong]]: Black and white body, brown back.
*[[Bowser Jr.]]: Yellow and white body, light brown back.
*Male Miis: Dodger blue and white body, dark yellow back.
*Female Miis: White and hot pink body, brown back.

=== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ===
[[File:MKT Assorted Karts.jpg|thumb|150px|Princess Peach in the Daytripper racing with Mario, Yoshi, [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Shy Guy]] in ''Mario Kart Tour''.]]
The Daytripper returns in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', where it is classified as a Super kart. The tires used are the [[Wood]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It no longer has color swaps for each character like in ''Mario Kart Wii'', with the Daytripper using Peach's pink version. Also, it is no longer called the Royal Racer in the British English version, and is just called the Daytripper in all versions.
