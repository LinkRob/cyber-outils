The '''Primordial Dinner''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] cooked by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing a [[Primordial Fruit]] and a [[Shroom Shake]]. This dinner restores fifteen [[Heart Point]]s and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
