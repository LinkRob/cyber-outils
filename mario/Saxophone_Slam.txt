'''Saxophone Slam''' is [[Tiny Kong]]'s special musical move from ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. Tiny can use this move once she buys it and her saxophone from [[Candy's Music Shop]] in [[Angry Aztec]].

==Usage==
When Saxophone Slam is used, Tiny takes out her saxophone and she plays a jazzy theme. This can be used on her music pads to trigger events or to defeat enemies. This technique is very useful in taking out the [[Giant Kosha]] from [[Crystal Caves]].
