'''''Mario Mania''''' is a real-world book published by ''[[Nintendo Power]]'' in 1991. It is primarily devoted to ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' content, having a detailed strategy guide for ''[[Super Mario World]]'', and the first 32 pages explore the the history of [[Mario]] up to that point.
