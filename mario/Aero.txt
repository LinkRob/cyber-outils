'''Aeros''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', although they cannot be engaged in a battle.

==History==
[[File:aero sprite.png|frame|left]]
Aeros are first seen raining down in [[Rose Town]] just as soon [[Mario]] and [[Mallow]] enter. A [[Toad (species)|Toad]] reveals that the Aeros have landed on the other citizens and caused paralyzing meaning they're unable to move. Mario and Mallow decide to head into the [[Forest Maze]] to find the source of the Aeros. Eventually, they find the source of the Aeros coming from [[Bowyer]] who is a member of the [[Smithy Gang]]. [[Geno]] has also recently appeared and demands Bowyer to hand over the [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]]. Mario and Mallow soon assist Geno in fighting Bowyer. After Bowyer is defeated and Geno tells Mario and Mallow the importance of the Star Pieces, a lone Aero heads back to [[Smithy]] to tell him about the Star Pieces.

==Trivia==
*If an Aero hits Mario, he is only temporarily paralyzed.
