The '''4-Wheel Cradle''' is the third [[kart]] for [[Toad]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', the [[Mushmellow]] and the [[Standard TD]] being the others. 

== Description ==
This kart is in the shape of a crade that has four wheels (hence the name).

== In ''Mario Kart DS'' ==
[[File:4-Wheel Cradle - Kart Select (Toad) - Mario Kart DS.png|thumb|left|200px|Toad in his 4-Wheel Cradle]]
The 4-Wheel Cradle is unlocked among every other third kart by completing all Nitro Grand Prix cups in the [[Engine Class|100cc engine class]].

This kart is slow, but has good [[Weight]], and [[Acceleration]], has average [[Handling]] and [[Drift]], followed by a excellent [[Items (stat)|Items]] stat.

The kart's weight is equivalent to [[Princess Peach|Peach]]'s [[Royale]] (not counting the drivers weight), its handling matches [[R.O.B.]]'s [[ROB-LGS]], and its drift rivals [[Bowser]]'s [[Tyrant]].

Mario and Peach can use the 4-Wheel Cradle once the player completes all 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix cups, and all racers can use this kart once all cups in the Mirror Class have been completed.
