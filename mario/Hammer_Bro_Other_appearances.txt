==In spinoff franchises==
===''Yoshi's Safari''===
[[File:Hammer Bro Yoshi Safari.png|thumb|A Hammer Brother from ''Yoshi's Safari''.]]
In ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]'', Hammer Brothers, as well as Boomerang Brothers and Sledge Brothers, occasionally appear as mini-bosses. Hammer Brothers attack Mario and [[Yoshi]] by quickly throwing hammers at them, which need to be either dodged or knocked away by a blast of Mario's [[Super Scope]]. To defeat a Hammer Bro, Mario has to blast it with the Super Scope repeatedly.

===''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''===
Though Hammer Brothers do not make a direct appearance in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'', the [[Spiked Koopa]] boss has the Japanese name ｢トゲブロス」 ''Toge Burosu'' ("Thorn Bros.") and wears a helmet vaguely like a Hammer Brother.

==Crossover appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
====''Super Smash Bros.''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'', Hammer Bros. occasionally appear in the background of the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Super Smash Bros.)|Mushroom Kingdom]] stage.

====''Super Smash Bros. Melee''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', Hammer Bros. have the same role as before, appearing only in the background of the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Super Smash Bros. Melee)|Mushroom Kingdom]] stage.

====''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''====
[[File:Hammer Bro SSBB artwork.png|thumb|200px|left|Artwork of a Hammer Bro. from ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
[[File:Hammer Bro Brawl.jpg|thumb|200px|A Hammer Bro as an Assist Trophy in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
[[File:Hammer Bro SSBB Subspace screenshot.png|thumb|200px|Hammer Bro in Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary of ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', Hammer Bros. appear as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|trophy]], an [[Assist Trophy]], and an enemy in [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]]. Hammer Bros. are the only entities to take on the role of both an Assist Trophy and an enemy in The Subspace Emissary. 

As an Assist Trophy character, upon being summoned, the Hammer Bro throws hammers at opponents, damaging them. The Hammer Bro is invincible, like with most other Assist Trophy characters.

In Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary, Hammer Bros. behave similarly to their Assist Trophy counterparts, but if a fighter stands too close to a Hammer Bro, he hits that fighter with his head. Unlike their Assist Trophy counterparts, Hammer Bros. can be damaged and defeated. In one cutscene, a Hammer Bro is seen driving a [[cargo]] with [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s stolen [[Banana Hoard]], and Donkey Kong and [[Diddy Kong]] manage to track down the Hammer Bro.
{{br}}
====''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U''====
[[File:Hammer Bro SSB4 3DS.jpg|thumb|200px|left|A Hammer Bro in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'']]
[[File:Hammer Bro SSB4 Wii U.jpg|thumb|200px|A Hammer Bro in ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'']]
Hammer Bros. reprise their role as Assist Trophies in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''. They once again attack by jumping and throwing hammers at fighters, only this time the Hammer Bro can be attacked, like the Hammer Bros. in the previous game's The Subspace Emissary campaign. After enough damage has been dealt, the Hammer Bro flips upside-down and falls off-screen, much like in ''Super Mario Bros.''

Hammer Bros. also appear as enemies in [[Smash Run]] mode in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS''. They act just like their Assist Trophy counterparts, although defeating them in this mode causes them to bounce around rather than fall off-screen immediately.
{{br}}

====''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''====
[[File:Hammer Bro SSB Ultimate artwork.png|thumb|150px|A Hammer Bro as an Assist Trophy]]
Hammer Bro reappears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as an [[Assist Trophy]]. He also appears as an Advanced-class Grab-type primary [[spirit]], which also grants the user slightly increased jump height. In the corresponding spirit battle, the player has to fight [[Yoshi]] on the [[Golden Plains]] stage, with the Hammer Bro Assist Trophy appearing when the opponent is highly damaged.

The core extracted from a Hammer Bro spirit can be used with a Reporter & Wrestler core to summon [[w:c:nintendo:Masked Dedede|Masked Dedede]]'s spirit.

===''Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix''===
[[File:HammerBroGuardsDDRMarioMix.png|thumb|left|upright=0.85|Hammer Bros. in ''Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix''.]]
In ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]'', two Hammer Brothers block the entrance of [[Mushroom Park]] to stop Mario (or [[Luigi]]) and Toad from getting the next [[Music Key]] from Wario. The Hammer Bros. only allow them to pass if Mario dances against them and wins. If the Hammer Bros. lose, Mario (or Luigi) can get into Mushroom Park.

===''Minecraft''===
In the Super Mario Mash-up for ''[[Minecraft]]'', [[w:c:minecraft:Zombie|Zombies]] are replaced by Hammer Bros. Additionally, [[w:c:minecraft:Zombie Villager|Zombie Villagers]] are replaced by Hammer Bros wearing the Villager's clothing. Hammer Bro also appears as a playable skin.

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''===
[[File:Museum_Sneak_M&S_2020_Hammer_Bro.png|thumb|250px|A Hammer Bro entering Stage 4 of Museum Sneak in ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020'']]
Hammer Bros. only appear in two [[2D Event]]s, [[Volleyball (Tokyo 1964)|Volleyball]] and [[Marathon (Tokyo 1964)|Marathon]], and the [[Tokyo 1964]] minigame [[Museum Sneak]]. Like several other ''Mario'' characters and species in the [[Tokyo '64]] game system, Hammer Bros. reuse their sprites from ''Super Mario Bros.''.

Unlike their other appearances, Hammer Bros. neither use hammers nor have any association with them. In Museum Sneak, some time after [[Mario (character)|Mario]] starts a stage, a Hammer Bro with a flashlight enters the area. It moves around faster than the other enemies. In Volleyball, five Hammer Bros. serve as [[Bowser]]'s teammates. In Marathon, several Hammer Bros. are among the 68 participants, and they move at a similar fast speed as the Hammer Bro in Museum Sneak.
