'''Fake Yoshis''' (also called '''Copycat Yoshis''' or '''Phony Yoshis''') are a type of [[Shy Guy]] that possesses the ability to transform into a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] complete with a fake [[Baby Mario]] on his back first appearing in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. In their true form, they are Shy Guys wearing green and white robes (in the same color of a [[Yoshi Egg]]) but then quickly transform into a duplicate of Yoshi and Baby Mario mimicking all his movements in the same way he moves (although the Fake Yoshis are unable to [[Flutter Jump]] and will descend in a failed attempt to do so). As Fake Yoshis are typically closed off, they’re usually harmless.

==History==
===''Yoshi's New Island''===
[[Image:Fake Yoshi Mimic.jpg|thumb|left|[[Pink Yoshi]] moving with a Fake Yoshi.]]
Fake Yoshis first appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', in this game, they primarily appear in [[Inside the Outside]], three Fake Yoshis are found in three rooms and each room has a [[Message Block]] explaining the Fake Yoshi, hitting the [[Winged Cloud]] in the room will cause the Shy Guy to appear in the closed off area and take on its Fake Yoshi form, in each of the rooms, the Fake Yoshi will copy Yoshi's exact movements, the goal is to trick the Fake Yoshi, in each of these rooms is a pit of [[Spike (hazard)|Spikes]], Yoshi must move around his area and get the Fake Yoshi above to mimic him in doing so, using the environment of the closed off area against the Fake Yoshi and moving to lead the Fake Yoshi into the Spikes, once the Fake Yoshi hits the Spikes it returns into its Shy Guy form, dies, and several collectibles are revealed including [[Coin]]s, [[Red Coin]]s, and [[Smiley Flower|Flowers]], beating the Fake Yoshis is not required for level progression however the items it leaves behind upon defeat are required for attaining 100 Points in a level often making defeating a Fake Yoshi necessary.

==Trivia==
*Regardless of the color of the Yoshi it’s copying, the Fake Yoshi will always appear as a [[Green Yoshi]].
*Fake Yoshis are similar to the [[Cosmic Clone]]s from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' as both are duplicates of [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]]/[[Yoshi]] that copy his every move however there is a difference in that Cosmic Clones are actually harmful enemies while the Fake Yoshi is harmless mostly due to being in a closed off area. Additionally, both leave behind key items upon defeat, the Cosmic Clones all turn into [[Star Bit]]s once the [[Power Star]] or [[Launch Star]] appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' or leave behind [[Coin]]s upon either being beaten with a [[Star]] or touching the [[Goal Pole]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' and the Fake Yoshis leave behind [[Coin]]s, [[Red Coin]]s, and a [[Smiley Flower|Flower]] upon defeat.
*The Fake Yoshi being vulnerable to Spikes is somewhat of an oddity as most [[Shy Guy]]s (the Fake Yoshi's true form is a Shy Guy) are actually immune to Spikes.
