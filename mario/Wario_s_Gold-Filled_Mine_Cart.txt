'''Wario's Gold-Filled Mine Cart''' is a type of [[Mine Cart]] featured in [[Wario's Gold Mine]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

==Description==
Mine Carts can be seen making their way along the mine tracks in Wario's Gold Mine. They contain gold and bear [[Wario]]'s signature "W" [[emblem]] its left and right sides each. In ''Mario Kart Wii'', Wario's Gold-Filled Mine Carts are obstacles, and if a racer drives into one, their [[kart]] tumbles over. In the ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' iteration of the course, they no longer serve as obstacles and hover on [[anti-gravity]] rails, so a racer can use one as a [[Spin Boost Pillar]] by driving into it.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', similar mine carts appear in [[Wario Shipyard]] around the sunken ship area, possibly referencing the mine carts in Wario's Gold Mine.
