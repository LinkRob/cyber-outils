'''Hoppos''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]].'' They return in ''[[Mario Tennis Fever]]'' as a Wonder Affect. 

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Hoppos are spherical purple hippos that are light orange on top of their back. The insides of a Hoppo's ears are also light orange. A Hoppo has two visible, round teeth on its upper jaw, on the left and right sides respectively.

===Behavior===
Hoppos roll along the ground, and the characters can bounce onto one.
