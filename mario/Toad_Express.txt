The '''Toad Express''' is [[Princess Peach|Peach]]'s private airplane. It has only appeared a few times, the first being in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. Here, [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Mario]], [[Toadsworth]], and some [[Toad (species)|Toads]] used the the Toad Express to go on a vacation and travel to [[Isle Delfino]].
