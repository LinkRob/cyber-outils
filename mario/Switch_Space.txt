The '''Switch Space''' is a [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
There is only one Switch Space, and it appears halfway within [[Banzai Bill's Mad Mountain]]. Whoever reaches the space first [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounds]] it, causing the [[Banzai Bill]] to switch from the lower [[Banzai Bill Cannon]] to the one at the top of the mountain. After being activated, the Switch Space becomes a [[Green Space]], and whoever is hit by a Banzai Bill in the upper second half of the board flies back to this space.
