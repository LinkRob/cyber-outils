'''Athens Dash 2''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. Hence its name, it is the second layout of [[Athens Dash]].

== Layout ==
The area visited by Athens Dash 2 will be an area off to the north of the Akrópolis.

The racers start the race inside the Parthenon, where they did for [[Athens Dash 1]], only they will be facing the four-way intersection to the east of the Parthenon instead of the stairs to the west. The racers turn left at the four-way intersection (The right path leads south and goes towards a cliff that overlooks the Théatron Dionusósio, while Athens Dash 1 comes from the east path, which is up ahead according to which direction they approach the intersection from.), where they pass the Erekhtheîon and encounter a [[Glide Ramp]] that leads to Monastiráki street. After a left turn and a right turn, the racers will head up the mountain back to the top. The racers will have to contend with spherical rolling [[Boulder|stone]]s on their way back up; touching a rolling stone will cause them damage, but a racer who uses a [[Super Star]] can smash through the stones without worry for extra points. The stones take their designs from the stones that roll down [[Bowser Castle (Mario Kart 8)|Bowser Castle]]. Upon making their way past the rolling stone section, the racers will encounter the steps leading back up to the Parthenon. After they go back up the steps, the racers just have to avoid some columns in the road before crossing the [[Finish line|starting line]] to complete a lap.

This route is used for the final lap of [[Athens Dash]] after the second lap is completed.
