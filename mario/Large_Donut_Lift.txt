'''Large Donut Lifts''' are an object in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

==Description==
Large Donut Lifts are similar to regular-sized [[Donut Lift]]s, which they function identically to, but the difference is that a Large Donut Lift is larger. Just like a regular Donut Lift, when a Large Donut Lift is stood on, it will start shaking and when stood on for too long, it will descend only to respawn shortly after.

==In ''Yoshi's New Island''==
Large Donut Lifts can be found in several of the levels as an alternative to the smaller Donut Lifts, most notably as an additional platform used during the battle with [[King Clawdaddy]]. The Large Donut Lifts function identically to the regular Donut Lifts. They shake when stood on and if [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] remains on one, it will descend downward only to respawn. They are also noticeably larger allowing more leg room than a smaller Donut Lift.

==Trivia==
*The Large Donut Lifts are about the same size as the [[Donut Lift]]s in the 3D ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' games however those are regular Donut Lifts as they're considered such by the games.
