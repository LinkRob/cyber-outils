'''Silver Medals''' are an item in ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''.

==Description==
A Silver Medal is a type of [[Happiness Medal]], ranked below the [[Gold Medal (Yoshi Topsy-Turvy)|Gold Medal]]. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can obtain a Silver Medal if he completes a mission with a score near the requirement set by the [[Yoshi Island Spirits|Spirits]]. A Silver Medal also gives the player two chances in the [[Bonus Chance!]].
