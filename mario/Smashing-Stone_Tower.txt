'''Smashing-Stone Tower''' is the tower and third level in Rock-Candy Mines in the game ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Layout==
The level starts in a tower with [[Grrrol]]s and platforms that must be climbed up to progress. Two Grrrol generators are found on either side of the area. Two more Grrrol generators are in the section following, and [[Hidden Block]]s below them must be hit in order to cause the Grrrols to destroy [[Brick Block]]s blocking the path. [[1-Up Mushroom]]s can be found in some of the Hidden Blocks. A large section of Brick Blocks follow, and is cleared out by two [[Mega Grrrol]]s. [[Pipe Cannon]]s are found, and lead to the boss door where [[Boom Boom]] awaits. [[Kamek]] transforms Boom Boom's arms into wings prior to the battle. He must be hit three times in order to be defeated.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first star coin is between two Grrrol dispensers.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second star coin is in a secret area behind the [[Luigi]] face.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third star coin is above the [[Pipe Cannon]] that launches the player to the secret exit, two Brick Blocks guard it.
==Secret Exit==
The secret exit is found by entering a pipe near the end of the level. Once there, the player must perform a [[Ground Pound]] in the slanted area. The secret exit leads to [[Spine Coaster Stowaways]].
==Hidden Luigi image==
The hidden alcove hiding the second Star Coin is stylized using Luigi's face instead of Bowser Jr's.
