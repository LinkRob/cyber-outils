'''Kuribo''' is a [[Sockop]] character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Kuribo has the same appearance as a Sockop. He is both fought and named this during battle.

=== Personality ===
Kuribo is shown to be greedy, as shown that he wants to "chug" [[Mario]] and the [[Attack Piece Block]] he has.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
Like the other Sockops, Kuribo lives in [[Dimble Wood]]. His distinction from regular Sockops is only showing during regular dialogue. In one part, he and two other Sockops are seen arguing over who gets to chug [[Mario]]. One of the Sockops names Kuribo a "hog" because it wants to chug both Mario and [[Luigi]] even after doing so for an [[Attack Piece Block]]. After Mario manages to rescue Luigi, Kuribo and the other Sockops team up to battle the duo.

== Behind the scenes ==
=== Etymology ===
Kuribo has the same Japanese name as a [[Goomba]]. Because Kuribo is a Sockop, its name specifically references the [[Goomba's Shoe]], named "Kuribo's Shoe" in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. This reference was not present in the original Japanese version.

==Trivia==
*His name is also a reference of how Luigi is shown to walk on thorns while inside the shrunken-down Boss Sockop.
