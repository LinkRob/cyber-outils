The '''Megaton Dinner''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Effects==

Made from a [[POW Block]], this meal damages all enemies on-screen by shaking the ground. The major difference between this and the POW Block is that the dinner item does not require the player to input any commands; Mario simply carries out the attack animation and delivers the damage instantly.
