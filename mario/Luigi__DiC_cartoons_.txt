'''Luigi''' is a protagonist that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World (TV series)|Super Mario World]]''. He appears in live-action and cartoon episodes.


==Profile==
Luigi is the younger brother of Mario. He lives in [[Brooklyn]], New York with his brother Mario.
===Physical appearance===
Luigi is a taller and thinner Italian man with black hair, black eyebrows and a thick black mustache with green eyes. He wears a green cap and overalls. He has a long-sleeved blue shirt and white gloves, which is the opposite of his gaming counterpart's green shirt and blue overalls, matching his appearance in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. In his Super form, he wears a white cap and overalls and a long-sleeved green shirt, which is the opposite of Fire Luigi's current color scheme since ''[[Super Mario World]]'', matching his sprite in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

=== Personality traits ===
Luigi is optimistic, quirky, creative, and kind but he is a bit shy and sensitive like his video game counterpart.

=== Paraphernalia ===
*'''Plumbing Tools'''
**'''Wrench:''' Luigi uses the wrench to screw/unscrew pipes.

== Behind the scenes ==
=== Voice ===
[[Danny Wells]] voiced Luigi in ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''. In ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3'' and onward, [[Tony Rosato]] portrayed Luigi.
