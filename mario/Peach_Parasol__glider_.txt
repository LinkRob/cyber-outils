The '''Peach Parasol''' is an unlockable [[glider]] that made its first appearance in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

== Appearance ==
As the name applies, this glider is a pink umbrella owned by [[Princess Peach]]. It gives a little [[acceleration]] boost, but lacks [[weight]]. Similar to the [[Birthday Girl]] [[kart]] body part, its color scheme only changes when a specific female character uses it.

=== Color schemes ===
==== ''Mario Kart 7'' ====
* [[Daisy]] - Orange umbrella with pale orange tips.
* [[Rosalina]] - Turquoise umbrella with pale turquoise tips.
* [[Honey Queen]] - Purple umbrella with pale purple tips.

==== ''Mario Kart 8 / Deluxe'' ====
* [[Daisy]] and [[Baby Daisy]] - Orange
* [[Rosalina]] and [[Baby Rosalina]] - Turquoise
* Other racers - Pink
