'''Wario's House''' is a reoccurring location in the ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' franchise. Since its first appearance in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', it has been the home of [[Wario]], mainly within the {{series|WarioWare}}, and replaces [[Wario Castle]] from some of Wario's earlier appearances.

==''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Wario's House reappears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]'' with a bigger role than usual. It starts with Wario watching television until he watches the program, ''[[The Silver Zephyr]]'', and gets an idea to go into the television show itself to get rich. Wario then invents the [[Telmet]] inside of his room and promptly enters the television For now on, whenever Wario completes a level, he'll come back to his room and rest on his couch before going into a different episode.
