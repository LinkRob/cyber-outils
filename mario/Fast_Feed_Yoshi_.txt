'''Fast Feed Yoshi!''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party-e]]''.

==Layout==
The background shows a light green and blue-checkered background. The [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and [[Shy Guy]]s, which are all purple, are designed after their sprites in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object is for Yoshi eat two [[Coin]]s from a rotating spinner. Yoshi has to avoid eating a [[Shy Guy]], as this uses up a turn.
