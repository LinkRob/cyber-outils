'''Bowser Slots''' (stylized as '''BOWSER Slots''') is the Item [[minigame]] of [[Bowser Land]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
To earn an item, the player has to match three pictures of the desired item by stopping each reel. If the player successfully matches three of an item, it pops out of the slot's head, and the player wins the item. However, if they do not, a spring with [[Bowser]]'s head on it pops out of the slot's head and bounces back and forth until the game fades out; thus, the player gets nothing.

The minigame can be purchased from [[Woody]] for 980 [[Coin]]s.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|A}} &ndash; [[Jump]]

==In-game text==
* '''Game Rules''' &ndash; ''"It takes well-timed jumps to line up 3 of a kind. Match them up to win!"''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"There are always 2 of one kind in a row on each block. Watch closely when jumping. You miss if you don't get 3."''
