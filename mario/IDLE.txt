The '''IDLE''' gang is an allied group from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They are four Concordian children who wish to help out in [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Snoutlet]], and [[Connie]]'s mission to stitch [[Concordia]] back together. They are temporarily joined by [[Princess Peach]], [[Starlow]], and [[Dorrie]] after the reactivation of [[Lottacoins Island]]'s [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Lighthouse]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The IDLE Concordians look different, but their designs are stereotypical of Concordian humanoids, with faces resembling an electrical outlet. Each one has a yellow-orange band tied around a different part of his or her body.

Ireen, the leader, is a short, pink Concordian who wears a hot pink dress. Her band is worn as a hair bow around her antenna, and she has reddish-pink eyes, each one with a visible eyelash that also doubles as eyebrows (pointing upwards when she gets angry or has a determined expression and pointing downwards when sad or scared). She has similar proportions to many child characters including [[Connie]] and [[Arc]]. A side quest reveals that Ireen roped the boys into the group by fighting them.

Dulles is a lanky green Concordian with dark green eyes and similar proportions to tall characters like [[Cozette]] and [[Technikki]]. He has his band tied around the green antenna on his head like Ireen, and his outfit consists of a green top and dark green trousers. Dulles' eyes are slanted outward near the top most of the time, but anything scaring him or making him sad causes their angle to invert.

Loog is a short blue Concordian with dark blue eyes who wears a blue tank top and dark blue trousers and a scar on one cheek. His band is worn like a scarf. He is the only Concordian to have round eyes, presumably to evoke BS546 sockets.

Ellow is a large, stocky yellow Concordian (not unlike the [[Bulbfish Island]] guard and [[Princess Burnadette]]) with orange eyes who wears an orange top and brown trousers. His band is worn like a cape. His eyes are always closed, resembling Type G outlets.

=== Personality ===
The IDLE gang is willing to help out, though their Japanese biography describes them as not being very good at it. However, their willingness to drive Technikki's rocket through a wall around the Great Lighthouse of Color-Full Sea to help [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] storm it and their numerous scouting missions reveal that they are very brave. The four kids are also cooperative despite their occasional bickering (which mellows out later), and repeatedly insist that their name is "IDLE" with capital letters should one mistakenly claim that the group is "idle" without capitals.

== Membership ==
The members of IDLE are individually named Ireen (leader), Dulles, Loog, and Ellow. The four members have bands around different parts of their body.

=== Temporary Members ===
Princess Peach, Starlow, and Dorrie join the team temporarily. Peach wears her band around her right arm, and Dorrie wears his around his neck. Starlow does not wear one, presumably due to her small size.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The name "IDLE" comes from the word "idle," which is ironic considering the quartet's drive to help out.

== Nomenclature ==

=== The Entire Group ===
In the Japanese dub, the group is called the Lingering Groop (''Guzuguzu Dan''; written グズグズ団). Their Chinese name, ''Tuōlatuōla Tuán'' (拖拉拖拉团), is formed from a calque of the Japanese name. The Spanish dub calls them "The Slackers" (''Los Remolones'', which contains ''molón'', a colloquialism for "cool"), and in Portuguese, their name, ''Estáticos'', is translated as "Statics." Their Italian name, ''N.E.T.T.Y.'', is a pun on ''inetti'' (plural of ''inetto'' ("incapable," "inept")), while French translates their name as ''Les ZÉROS'', which means "Zeroes" (the plural of a pejorative term meaning "someone of little importance"). In the German dub, they are known as Team WYRG, derived from ''würg'' ("choke"). Their Dutch name, Team G.A.A.P., comes from ''gaap'' (which refers to the act of yawning).

=== Individual Members ===
The individual members also take their names from the team's letters (or syllables, depending on the language).

==== Ireen ====
In the Japanese dub, Ireen is called ''Gumi'', from the first syllable in ''Guzuguzu'' ("IDLE") with the Japanese female name ending ''-mi'' (written み) tacked onto the end. In Chinese, she is called ''Tuōmǐ'' (拖米), a calque of the Japanese name. The Spanish dub gives her the French masculine (ironically for Ireen name ''René'' (meaning "reborn"), from the first syllable in ''Remolones''. Her Portuguese name is ''Esther'', derived from the first syllable of ''Estáticos'' while being a proper name. Italian gives her the English name "Nelly," a diminutive of "Eleanor," while also coming from the first letter of the entire group's name. In French, she is called "''Zeboce''," which is a pun on "the boss" reflecting how she leads IDLE; the first letter of the name forms the Z in ''ZÉROS''. In Dutch, Ireen is called "Gussie," her name coming from the first letter in ''G.A.A.P.'' (the group's full name). She has the name ''Wibke'' in German, from the first letter in ''WYRG''.
