'''Bramball Woods''' is a level seen in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]. ''
==Features==
It is forest-themed, though it is more foggy and darker than the average forest scenery and the only course with Bramballs. The course itself features and purely consists of many Bramballs. Spinning Coins and Dash Coins are common. Many platforms made of Brick Blocks are found as the player gets close to a specific area through the level. Giant Brick Blocks and similar types are present.

Some areas starting from the middle of the level contain many Donut Lifts, where Stalking Piranha Plants and more Bramballs walk by.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Totally visible between two structures of Brick Blocks, found just after the first Warp Pipe of the level.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Shortly after the first Star Coin and right before the Checkpoint Flag, hidden in a wall to the right of the Bramball.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Below a Donut Lift structure, guarded by a Bramball.
