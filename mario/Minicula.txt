'''Miniculas''' are vampire bat enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
A Minicula can turn [[Wario]] into [[Bat Wario]] by touching him, in the same manner as a [[Kobatto]] can turn Wario into [[Vampire Wario (form)|Vampire Wario]] in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. Miniculas cannot be defeated, although they do not act hostile toward Wario.

Miniculas are the most common in the [[Sapphire Passage]], where they appear in a majority of its levels, including [[Crescent Moon Village]], [[Arabian Night]], and [[Hotel Horror]]. Their only appearance outside of the Sapphire Passage, as well as their last one altogether, is the [[Golden Passage]].
