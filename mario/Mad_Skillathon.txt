The '''Mad Skillathon''' is a challenge mode from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It can be found in the southern portion of [[Wakeport]], and manifests as a talent show run by a [[Shelltop]] man named [[Skillit]]. As its name implies, the Mad Skillathon involves an endurance challenge with various [[Special Attack]]s, more specifically [[Bros. Attack]]s, not unlike the [[Cholesteroad]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

==Bros. Attacks Involved And Challenges==
The Bros. Attacks have their gameplay modified slightly from how they function in actual combat. Getting 200 points nets the B rank, which gives 100 Coins. Scoring A rank, at 500 points, gives 300 coins. Getting S rank gives an exclusive gear piece. Getting the S rank for all challenges gives the Pro Wear. If the player passes multiple thresholds, the prizes from passing them are combined for that run.

If the player gets an equal score to their previous score or worse, that score is ignored and Skillit tells the player to try again next time. Getting a better score than their previous high score will cause the new score to become the new high score, though the player will still need to manually save with a [[Save Block]]. The maximum score possible is 999 points.
