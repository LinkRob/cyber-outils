'''Bucket Head''' is a snowman-like enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bucket Head's torso and head are both white, and its head is usually large in proportion. On its face, Bucket Head has eyes, a mouth, thick black eyebrows, and the skull emblem of the [[Black Sugar Gang|Brown Sugar Pirates]]. Bucket Head has round red feet, yellow gloves, and a small red bucket on its head.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name Bucket Head refers to the small bucket that it wears on its head.
