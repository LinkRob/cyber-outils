'''Stone Wheels''' are objects which appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. Stone Wheels are very usual all over the game, and they are destroyed by [[Ground Pound]]ing on the wheels, normally leaving an item. Continuously, the items inside the wheel are [[Star Bit]]s. Unlike innumerable objects that manipulated with a Ground Pound, they have the Ground Pound symbol on the wheel. It will observably split, and after being break up, the rock still cotyledon tiny pieces on the level, it did not serve any purpose. It also known from turn aside [[Sproutle]]s from growing, as a [[Bee]] states In the [[Honeyhive Galaxy]]. The wheel can also sometimes to be seen in stacks. According to another Bee in the Honeyhive Galaxy, they can drop down from the sky, similar to a [[Meteor]].
