'''Para-Goombas''' are a variant of [[Flying Goomba]]s which make their first and main appearance in ''[[Mario Clash]]''. Para-Goombas have a thorn on their head that protects them from [[jump]] attacks. To defeat a Para-Goomba, [[Mario]] has to throw a [[Green Shell|Turtle Shell]] at it, causing the Para-Goomba to become stunned, and then hit the Para-Goomba again to defeat it.

In the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]], a Para-Goomba appears in level 3 of the [[Mario Clash (microgame)|Mario Clash]] [[microgame]], as the enemy that Mario has to defeat by throwing a Turtle Shell at it. In ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', the Para-Goomba only has to be hit once, but in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', it has to be hit twice, like in ''Mario Clash''.
