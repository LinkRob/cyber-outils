'''Cake Factory''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. It later returns in both ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the two teams must try to beat the other by making cake. One of the players has to place the cake while the others have to place the strawberry on top. They must be careful as if two cakes are placed on each other or if the strawberry is placed first and then the cake, it will not count as a point and precious seconds are wasted. The team that builds the most cakes by the end of the time limit wins.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|a}} (hold) - Grab item
* {{button|n64|a}} (release) - Place item
