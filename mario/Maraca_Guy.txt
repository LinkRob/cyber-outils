'''Maraca Guy''' is a [[Shy Guy]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It usually appears alongside [[Sombrero Guy]]s and [[Accordion Guy]]s. There is only one Maraca Guy that can be found and it is in the World 2 level [[Yoshi Sphinx]].

==Appearance==
The Maraca Guy looks exactly like a regular Shy Guy. It wears a sombrero and carries two maracas.
