'''Shiny Paper-Cone Goombas''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. They are very rare enemies that are fought in a few levels of [[World 4 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|World 4]].

== Appearance ==
Shiny Paper-Cone Goombas are similar to [[Paper-Cone Goomba]]s except that they are more shinier in color.

== Battle ==
In battle, Shiny Paper-Cone Goombas attack by charging at Mario with their pointy heads. However, their attack is much higher and jumping is useless (unless using an Iron Jump sticker). It is recommended to take this strong enemies out with Shiny-based Hammer stickers.
