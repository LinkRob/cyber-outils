'''Oerlikon''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are a variant of [[Spiny]] first encountered in [[Barrel Volcano]] and later again in [[The Trial Course]] of [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]].

== Battle ==
In battle, Oerlikons mainly attack by shooting their spikes at one of [[Mario]]'s party members.

=== Strategy ===
Oerlikons are weak to [[Ice (element)|ice]], so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] is very effective against them.
