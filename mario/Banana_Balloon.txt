==History==
The Banana Balloons are found throughout world of [[DK Isles]]. The Banana Balloons come in five different colors: yellow for [[Donkey Kong]], red for [[Diddy Kong]], blue for [[Lanky Kong]], purple for [[Tiny Kong]], and green for [[Chunky Kong]]. The Banana Balloons must be busted open with the respective color Kong's shooter (meaning for the yellow ones, Donkey Kong must pop it with his [[Coconut Shooter]]). Busting a Banana Balloon adds 10 regular Bananas to the count.
