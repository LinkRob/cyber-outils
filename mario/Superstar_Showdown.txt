'''Superstar Showdown''' is the final [[minigame]] being played at the end of [[Star Battle Arena]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. [[Bowser]] is good to his word and gives the player back the [[Star Rod (Mario Party 8)|Star Rod]]. At the last second, Bowser has second thoughts and challenges the player to the final match. The player is pitted against him in space. If the player is defeated at any time, the player can try again. If the player starts again, Bowser keeps the damage the player has dealt to him. 

This minigame is unlocked in [[Free Play Arcade]] only when Star Battle Arena is completed.

==Opening==
Bowser and the player are in space, and the player has the Star Rod in their hand. Bowser is riding in the [[Koopa Clown Car]], and the player is on a star-shaped platform.

==Gameplay==
===Phase 1===
[[File:Superstar Showdown 1.png|thumb|left|First phase]]
Bowser has 10 [[Heart Point|HP]], and the player has five. While Bowser blasts [[fireball]]s, the player must hold the [[Wii#Wii Remote|Wii Remote]] up and swing it around to charge up the Star Rod, while dodging Bowser's flames. After it is charged, the player must swing down their arm to throw the energy ball. After the player hits Bowser three times, the game goes to Phase 2.

===Phase 2===
Bowser has two [[Bullet Bill]] launchers, which fire around six Bullet Bills each time. After the player hits him three times again, the battle enters the final phase.

===Phase 3===
[[File:Superstar Showdown 3.png|thumb|Third and final phase]]
In addition to the weapons Bowser had before, the Koopa Clown Car now has a laser blaster, which rotates, sending beams out. The only way to dodge these is to get in between two of them. The Bullet Bills are now colored red and move faster. When Bowser has one HP left, he fires a large fireball in addition to the other attacks. After the player blasts Bowser one more time, the battle ends.

==Ending==
After being hit by the final blast from the Star Rod, Bowser, still inside the Koopa Clown Car, is sucked downward into a [[black hole]]-like vortex.

==Controls==
* '''Wii Remote'''
** Move left/right
** Hold down {{button|wii|A}}/{{button|wii|B}} to build up energy
** Throw energy ball
* '''Buttons'''
** Press {{button|wii|A}}/{{button|wii|B}} to raise the rod
