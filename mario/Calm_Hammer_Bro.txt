'''Calm Hammer Bro''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Calm Hammer Bro is a variant of [[Hammer Bro]] imbued with the [[Calm]] [[vibe]], although Calm Hammer Bros. have the same appearance as their parent species.

===Behavior===
At first, a Calm Hammer Bro can be fond sleeping, but when [[Princess Peach|Peach]] approaches, the Calm Hammer Bro wakes up and starts throwing its [[hammer]]s, like what Hammer Bros. do. However, a Calm Hammer Bro does this either slowly or quickly, depending on how long it has been awake.   

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
[[Image:Clam Hammer Bro. Sleeps.png|thumb|left|Peach near a sleeping Calm Hammer Bro.]]
Calm Hammer Bros. are first encountered in [[Wavy Beach 5-2]]. They are encountered in several areas, often near Hammer Bros. Peach can keep a Calm Hammer Bro from waking up by slowly walking toward it and then attacking. A Calm Hammer Bro can be stunned from a [[stomp]] attack, and other attacks can instantly defeat it.
