'''World 8-5''' is a level from [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8]] which is found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Layout==
This level takes place around a volcano. There is a lot of lava, as well as many Wobble Rocks. [[Podoboo]]s jump out of the lava, and generic enemies (such as [[Goomba]]s and [[Koopa Troopa]]s) also appear in the level. Since this level is relatively short, there is no [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]].
==Walkthrough==
In this lava-filled stage, head to the right, jumping from platform to platform while avoiding the Podoboos and lava. Head right and hit the ? Block above the large wobbling platform to get an item. There will be brown platforms balancing on tiny bases, and they will fall over shortly after you step on them, so be quick to avoid sinking with it. Continue right and drop to a lower ledge to get Star Coin #1 on the way (it's below the platform with two ? Blocks).

Up ahead, hit the ? block on the next large platform to get another item. Hop along a series of platforms, then jump on the wobbling platform at the top of those steps. Jump to the lower platform to the right, then stomp a Koopa Troopa and kick its shell to the left to receive Star Coin #2. Go right and jump along a bunch of low-lying platforms, defeating a Koopa Troopa along the way.

At the end of this stretch, you will reach a slightly raised platform. On that one, dash and jump to the higher ledge, then jump from that to reach Star Coin #3. Now, go into the pipe ahead to reach the final area. When you emerge, go right to the [[flagpole]] and exit this stage. 
==Star Coins==

*'''Star Coin 1''': Above a wobbling platform below some stone blocks. Drop down to the platform to obtain the Star Coin then jump back up to the higher ground before the platform sinks.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Below the balance beam dangling above the lava. Use a Koopa Troopa's shell and toss it at the Star Coin to obtain it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Near the exit is a higher platform, charge up a run and jump to the higher platforms. The final Star Coin is atop the highest platform but be careful as you only have one shot at this.
