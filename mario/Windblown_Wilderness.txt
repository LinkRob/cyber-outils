'''Windblown Wilderness''' is the third level of [[World 2 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 2]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', a [[Cyan Yoshi]] takes control in this level. The level takes place in a vast canyon with many pits, hence the level's name, there is a lot of wind blowing in various directions and the level makes use of [[Baby Peach]]'s wind-gliding abilities. There are also [[Balloon]]s which can be seen gliding through the level, Yoshi can hitch a ride on these, [[Gusty|Gusties]] are also seen flying through the level making for an obstacle as Yoshi soars the skies.

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the right and hop onto the [[Countdown Platform]]s to earn some [[Coin]]s, hop down the [[Pipe]] (if you want to), then start running across the long set of [[Countdown Platform]]s until you reach the Stork Stop at the end, switch to [[Baby Peach]] (if you haven't already) and use the her abilities to glide with the wind (other babies can also be used to reach the upper platforms via the use of the [[Balloon]]s but this will take heaps longer), glide up to the highest platform and then flutter to the left, there's a [[Winged Cloud]] here, hit it with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to get  the [[SStar (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] inside. After this, advance to the right and along the main path (don't worry none of the [[Coin]]s along the [[Countdown Platform]]s are [[Red Coin]]s so you should be fine here), hop across the Countdown Platforms and watch out for any incoming [[Gusty|Gusties]], toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the [[Winged Cloud]] in the corner to earn the [[Smiley Flower]] here, after this drop down the pit (but not all the way down or else you'll die) as there are two [[Red Coin]]s dangling near the bottom (alongside some [[Coin]]s) grab these, and then glide the wind back up to the platforms, glide to the top platform (once again none of the [[Coin]]s along the [[Countdown Platform]]s are [[Red Coin]]s) and then exit through the right.

Once in the next area, tag the [[Middle Ring]] and advance onward, there are a few [[Shy Guy]]s here, you can either beat them or avoid them, there's also a [[Stork Stop]] here (but chances are you're still [[Baby Peach]] so you won't be needing it at this point), next, glide across the wind gliding past the [[Countdown Platform]]s until you reach the upper ledge, there is an exit leading to the next area here but use the next wind gust to glide to the platform with the [[Pipe]] (there is a [[Stork Stop]] in this pipe), after this, use the next wind gust to glide to the rock platform above and from there, use the next wind gust to reach the top ledges (whilst grabbing the aligned [[Coin]]s and two [[Red Coin]]s), each step here has a [[Shy Guy]] (making a total of three [[Shy Guy]]s on each side), atop the left step, there is a [[Winged Cloud]] high above, hit it with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to reveal a [[! Switch]], hit the switch to activate the wind, glide with the wind (grabbing the [[Coin]]s as you go) and reach the topmost ledge to grab the [[Baby Coin]], after this, go back down and climb the right stairs and exit through the right tagging the [[Middle Ring]] on your way out through the [[Flipper]]s.

If you chose to take the lower exit instead, you will end up on a track with several [[Nipper Plant]]s coming your way, [[Nipper Spore]]s can also be seen raining from the skies and there is a [[Piranha Plant]] and a [[Windbag]] (which blows [[Nipper Spore]]s from the [[Nipper Dandelion]]) here. Beat these foes and jump on the body of the dinosaur skeleton to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]], hit it with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to get some more [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], continue along the path and tag the [[Middle Ring]], from here, there is also a [[Pipe]] (which leads to another area with a [[Stork Stop]]) and then hop across the [[Countdown Platform]]s while avoiding the [[Gusty|Gusties]] and reach the right ledge which has a [[Winged Cloud]] containing [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] you can either exit by hopping up to the platform to the right or through the left [[Pipe]] which leads to an area with a [[Stork Stop]] and an [[Egg Block]] which can fill your egg supply, either way, you should end up in the next area.

In the next area, drop down or advance to the left (depending on which path you took) and enter the door, you will arrive in a secret area with the [[Kangaroo]], hop into her pouch and hop and glide across the skies collecting [[Coin]]s as you go (there are no [[Red Coin]]s here) however this time, there are actual pits which can kill you just the same so avoid these. Once you've made it across, you will reach an upper ledge which contains a [[Winged Cloud]] that contains [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], from here, drop down and advance to the right hopping across the [[Countdown Platform]]s (if you took the lower path, the [[Kangaroo]] room would be your only way of reaching these) hop across each platform but be careful of the onslaught of [[Gusty|Gusties]] coming your way as they can knock you off the platforms. At the end, there is a long set of [[Countdown Platform]]s, run across these (hit the [[Winged Cloud]]s above, one contains [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and the other has [[Coin]]s), collect the [[Coin]]s along the platform track as the three at the end are [[Red Coin]]s, a [[Smiley Flower]] awaits at the track's end, collect this too. After this, hop down to the main track and watch out for the [[Windbag]]s perched on the Dino-Skeletons that blow [[Nipper Spore]]s at you, from here, exit through the right.

In the next area, you will see some [[Glide Guy]]s that start attacking, beat these guys and glide across the wind and hop along the platforms, there are two blowing wind gusts, blow with the one to the right and grab the [[Smiley Flower]] in the upper alcove, one you have this, either blow with the left wind gust or move past the wind gusts to the left and hit the [[! Switch]], once the switch blocks are activated, hop across them and reach the dirt chunk at the top, toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the dirt chunk above as a [[Spring Ball]] is perched on it, use the [[Spring Ball]] to reach the upper platforms, there are two [[Winged Cloud]]s in this niche (one is in the left corner at the bottom and the other is on the rock platform) one contains [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] while the other has [[Coin]]s, there is also a stash of [[Coin]]s in this nook but none of them are red, once done, drop down to the niche's bottom and exit through the [[Flipper]]s (don't hop up to the top or else you may miss the [[Red Coin]]s, use the wind to glide up to the top through the [[Coin]] lines collecting four [[Red Coin]]s as you go. In the next area, there is a [[Egg Block]], hit it for some eggs and jump across the [[Donut Lift]]s in this area, the top set of [[Donut Lift]]s have three [[Red Coin]]s so be sure to obtain these, above you here are a few [[Baron Von Zeppelin]]s, the first two are carrying a [[Grenade]] and [[Needlenose]] respectively and will drop them on you once you get underneath, there is also a [[Baron Von Zeppelin]] carrying a 1-Up so be sure to hit this one with an egg, additionally, there are also three [[Baron Von Zeppelin]]s carrying a box, this will take three egg tosses, once all three Barons are wiped out, the Box drops and can be ground pounded for some bonus Stars, after collecting all the goodies, exit through the right.

In the next area, you can advance to the right, you will come across a [[Lakitu]] who tosses some [[Spiny Egg]]s at you (you can also enter the [[Pipe]] where yet another Stork Stop can be found (ideally, [[Baby Peach]] should be used to finish this level but other babies can be used as well by stealing [[Lakitu]]'s cloud and riding it to the top). Once done, advance to the left and glide the wind upward collecting the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]] in the line, hop to the left ledge and toss an egg at the [[Winged Cloud]] above for some more Stars, jump to the right platform and glide the wind through the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]], glide the wind gusts to the right and glide upward, there is a Smiley Flower here, grab it and toss eggs at the two [[Winged Cloud]]s here as they contain 1-Ups, below, there is a stash of [[Coin]]s which you can grab, drop back down to the main paths and glide the leftmost wind and follow the three [[Coin]] lines aligned with each gust, there is one [[Red Coin]] in each gust grab them and hop to the ledge, use the [[Lakitu's Cloud]] waiting here to zoom with the wind and grab the final [[Red Coin]] at the end of the [[Coin]] trail, after this, jump to the end platform and jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
You will need [[Baby Peach]] for this [[Baby Coin]], in the second area, instead of exiting through the right at the lower platforms, glide the wind and fly to the upper platforms until you reach the twin staircases with [[Shy Guy]]s, climb the left staircase and toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the [[Winged Cloud]] to reveal a [[! Switch]] hit the switch to activate the wind and glide with both wind sets (collecting [[Coin]]s as you go) to grab the [[Baby Coin]] at the top, the wind is only temporary so you will have to be quick about reaching the top ledge.
