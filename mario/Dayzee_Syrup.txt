'''Dayzee Syrup''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Effects==

It is made out of a [[Dayzee Tear]]. It takes the appearance of any other syrup item, but this one is colored blue. As one of the more useful items in the game, since Dayzee Tears are difficult to find, it doubles the Attack power of [[Mario]] and his friends and gradually restores [[Heart Point|HP]].
