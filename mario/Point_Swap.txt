A '''Point Swap''' is an item found in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Using a Point Swap switches the value of [[Mario]]'s or his partner's [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]]. If Point Swap is cooked with another item, it usually results in one that can restore the other stat.
