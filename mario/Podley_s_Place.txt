'''Podley's Place''' is a bar and [[Inn]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Downstairs is the bar, where [[Podley]] himself resides during the story. He serves [[Chuckola Cola]] to the folks of the bar. A staircase downstairs in the bar leads to the inn upstairs. In the inn, [[Mario]] can sleep in the bed to restore all [[Health Point]]s, [[Flower Point]]s, and [[Star Power]]. If he has an [[Inn Coupon]], he can sleep for free. [[Toadsworth]] resides in the inn for the whole game.
