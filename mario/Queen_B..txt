'''Queen B.''' is the third boss in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' resembling a giant [[Zinger]]. Queen B. is found in [[Vine Valley]]. Queen B. tries to attack [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] with her massive stinger. Like regular [[Zinger]]s, Queen B. cannot be jumped on due to the spikes on her back. The Kongs must attack Queen B. with a barrel, making her angry and red and bounces across the entire area. The Kongs cannot attack Queen B. in this state, due to being invincible in this mode. After a while, Queen B. calms down and turns yellow again. She can also have a barrel be thrown at her again. The Kongs must repeat these step four more times to defeat Queen B. and move on to [[Gorilla Glacier]].

In the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake, Queen B. has regular [[Zinger]]s surrounding her instead of bouncing up and down multiple times by herself an having to wait a certain amount of time to defeat Queen B. When all of the Zingers surrounding Queen B. become defeated, Donkey and Diddy can attack Queen B. with a barrel.
