The '''Boomerang Flower''' is a power-up item which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It can be obtained from certain [[Question Block]]s or from defeating a [[Boomerang Bro]]. It reappears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' as one of the new items.

==Description==
Boomerang Flowers are white, and are shaped a lot like an actual boomerang, with it curving down to make a 'v' shape. It also has a bright blue stripe across one end. Like most power-ups, the Boomerang Flower has two long black eyes as well.

The Boomerang Flower grants the user the ability to throw boomerangs but only one at a time unlike the Boomerang Bros. This power-up is mainly useful for attacking enemies from a distance and grabbing distance items such as [[Green Power Star|Green Star]] or a [[Star Medal]].
