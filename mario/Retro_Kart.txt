A '''Retro Kart''' is a [[kart]] that returns in a newer ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' game from an older installment. While this was technically the case for karts used by characters in earlier installments, the term for Retro Karts themselves was first used in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

==Retro Karts in ''Mario Kart 7''==
*[[Egg 1]] (returned from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'')
*[[Barrel Train]] (returned from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'')
*[[B Dasher]] (returned from ''Mario Kart DS'')
*[[Pipe-Frame]] (returned from ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'')
