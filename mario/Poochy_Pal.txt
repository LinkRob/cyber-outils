'''Poochy Pal''' is a [[Gaddget]] found in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is unlocked by completing the [[Nerd Force V]] quest.

==Description==
[[Poochy]] appears in this Gaddget to accompany the player. The player can only see what Poochy does in the backyard, which includes sleeping, entering into a [[Warp Pipe|pipe]], etc. If the player presses the {{button|gba|A}} button, a [[butterfly]] or bagworm appears. The butterfly flies from one side of the screen to the other, and the bagworm drops from the tree on a thread.
