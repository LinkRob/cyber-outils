'''Mari''' is a blue female [[Luma]] who first apears in a storybook of [[Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2|''Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2'']].

== Profile ==

== History ==
Mari is mentioned in [[Rosalina's Story|Rosalina’s illustrated storybook]] in Part 1. It is a children’s book that explains [[Lubba]]’s past. There are four chapters in total, and each chapter is unlocked one by one as the game progresses.

Lubba was originally a blue Luma named Lu and lived happily with his twin sister Mari, along with many other [[Luma|Lumas]] and [[Rosalina]]. While Lubba was shy, Mari was extroverted and impulsive. However, one day, Mari suddenly became a star, and the twins were separated. Lu had already heard from Rosalina the reason why Lumas become stars, but he was shocked to learn that Mari had become a star without any conditions or qualifications, so he went to visit Rosalina. Rosalina told him that Mari had left the cycle and would return someday, but that it could take a hundred, a thousand, or even ten thousand years, and Lu despaired.

Lu spent 100 years alone in sadness and solitude, but Mari never returned. Worried that his sadness would be a burden to Rosalina, Lou decided to leave her and build his own spaceship to search for Mari. As time passed, Lou grew and turned purple, his other friends became stars, and he occasionally saw Rosalina, but his search for Mari was in vain. Thus, 1,000 years passed without Mari.

Mari spent all that time alone, gradually losing her memory and becoming an eccentric Luma who desired her own death, since for a Luma death is not the end but just another phase. She gave up on that idea when, from far away in space, she saw a nostalgic purple light coming from Lubba. She decided to let that light guide her and set out on another journey.
