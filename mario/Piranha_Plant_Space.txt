A '''Piranha Plant Space''' is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
If a character lands on a Piranha Plant Space, then a [[Piranha Plant]] eats them up and sends them back a random number of spaces, ranging between one and six.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
Piranha Plant Spaces only appear in two boards, [[Perilous Palace Path]] and [[Kamek's Carpet Ride]].
