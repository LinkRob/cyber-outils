'''Ink Pasta''' is a pasta [[recipe]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Inkpasta.PNG|frame|left]]
Ink Pasta is made by [[Zess T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is made by mixing [[Inky Sauce]] and [[Fresh Pasta Bunch|Fresh Pasta]]. It restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 30 [[Flower Point|FP]] and sells for 80 [[coin]]s.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
The Ink Pasta reappears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' as the '''Ink Pasta Dish'''. It can be made by [[Dyllis]] in [[Flopside]], using the same ingredients as in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', but Fresh Pasta is renamed [[Fresh Pasta Bunch]]. Ink Pasta restores 10 HP and cures [[Poison (status effect)|poison]], being a lot more effective than the two ingredients separately.
