'''Snow Land''' is the first course of the [[Star Cup]] of ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', much like that of [[Sherbet Land (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Sherbet Land]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. This course is snow/ice-themed, meaning that it will get slippery for the player. The main hazards are ice patches, snowmen and penguins.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ==
Snow Land is part of Wave 2 of the [[Booster Course Pass]]. Unlike the original, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe's version of Snow Land features elevated roads and a bridge at the starting line. The U-turn in the middle of the track now features an ice path with a pool of water in the middle. The penguins now are fully animated, occasionally sliding along the ice similar to the skating Shy Guys from GCN Sherbet Land. 

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Snow Land is one of the tracks added to [[Mario Kart Tour]] in it's 2022 Holiday Tour Update. The course design is mostly unchanged from the Mario Kart 8 Deluxe version with some smaller changes. Firstly, the road was changed to resemble the Game Boy Advance version. Penguins now roam at the start of the track. A ramp was also added just before the entrance of the thin ice basin, along with the giant watery hole from its previous iteration now being changed to a thick patch of ice. Two Dash Panels were added onto the ice ramp leading back to the icy road and two snowmen mark the off-road shortcut.{{MKSCNav}}
