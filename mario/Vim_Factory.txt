The '''Vim Factory''' is a factory located in the center of [[Toadwood Forest]], which is part of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. The factory's primary purpose is to extract "[[Vim]]" from [[Toad (species)|Toads]] for the [[Shroob]]s to use as an energy source. The factory is powered by Toads strapped to trees.

Protecting the factory are two [[Hammer Bro]]s., called the [[L33t Hamm3r Broz.]] whose minds are being controlled by Shroobs through helmets. The Hammer Bros. kidnap [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] upon the bros. arrival. After [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] defeat them, these Hammer Bros. teach them how to use a hammer in return. The babies then rescue Mario and Luigi from the tree in the middle of the factory. [[Swiggler]] is the main boss of the Vim Factory residing on the Vim Factory's tower, and the guardian of the second piece of the [[Cobalt Star]]; it transports the fuel to the [[Shroob UFO]]s. Upon defeating it, however, [[Baby Bowser]] arrives and takes both the shard from Swiggler and the one the bros. already got back in [[Baby Bowser's Castle]].
