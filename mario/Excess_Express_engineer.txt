|race = [[Toad (species)|Toad]]}}The '''Excess Express Engineer''' is a minor character that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is the main engineer of the [[Excess Express]] and is always seen running the train.

==Appearance==
The Excess Express Engineer is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] that appears to wear a black hat on his head. He also wears a black jacket to match his hat.

==History==
The Excess Express Engineer's role in the game is somewhat significant as his [[autograph]] is needed in order to please [[Bub (character)|Bub]]. The Engineer feels touched that someone wants an autograph of him. On the third day, all of the passengers have gone missing and as soon the Engineer talks with [[Mario]], a bunch of creatures known as [[Smorg (species)|Smorgs]] will infest the outside of the train. The Engineer urges Mario to deal with them as he must stay behind to keep control of the train. After Mario has defeated the [[Smorg]], the Excess Express is saved and the Excess Express Engineer is pleased thanking Mario for his help.
