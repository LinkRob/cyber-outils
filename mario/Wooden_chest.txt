A '''wooden chest''' is an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
A wooden chest has the appearance of a wooden container with a square-shaped base, and it is edged in dark metal rims, giving it a rustic look. Wooden chests contain common items such as [[garlic]] or [[coin]]s, or even a [[bomb]], which is an object.

==In ''Wario World''==
[[Wario]] can force open a wooden chest can using any attack, revealing its contents. If Wario reenters a wooden or a steel [[trapdoor (Wario World)|trapdoor]] after having completed the sub-level inside, a wooden chest with coins takes the place of where the [[Red diamond (Wario World)|red diamond]] used to be.
