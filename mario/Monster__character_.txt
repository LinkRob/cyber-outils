'''Monster''' is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[The Marios Fight Back]]." It is a giant creature covered in brown hair, and is a giant counterpart of [[Clog]] mutated after taking [[Mario Brothers Clog Cleaner]] and [[Mario Brothers Plant Food]].
