The '''Blue Coin Block''' is an object which appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
Whenever [[Mario]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in the remake) find a Blue Coin Block, they can [[Ground Pound]] it to have a nearby trail of a few [[Blue Coin]]s appear. Each Blue Coin Block spawns a specifically number of Blue Coins, most commonly either six or seven. A ticking sound plays while the Blue Coins appear, as the player's character has only a short amount of time to collect them. When the Blue Coins are about to disappear, the ticking sound plays faster, and the Blue Coins start to blink. After being used, a Blue Coin Block does not reappear unless the player resets the level.

The use of Blue Coin Blocks are not tied to obtaining any particular [[Power Star]], but given that Blue Coins are worth five [[Coin|Yellow Coins]] each, collecting the Blue Coins can be necessary to acquire a course's [[Power Star|100-Coin Star]].

A Blue Coin Block appears in nearly every main course except [[Bob-omb Battlefield]], [[Snowman's Land]], and [[Tall, Tall Mountain]]. [[The Princess's Secret Slide]] is the only secret course to have a Blue Coin Block.

==Locations==
*[[The Princess's Secret Slide]]: When Mario enters the location, he can find a Blue Coin Block to the right.
*[[Whomp's Fortress]]: The Blue Coin Block is found near the [[Bob-omb Buddy]]
