'''Grabbit''' is a single-player minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is for the player's character (either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]) to sneak up on the [[rabbit]]s and catch them within the [[time limit]]. Whenever the player's character is close to a rabbit, the rabbit gets alerted (indicated from a red exclamation mark in a speech) and retreats into its burrow. Aside from moving around, the character can also perform actions such as running and diving. If the character hits a wall while diving, they become stunned for a few seconds. Each time the player's character catches a rabbit, they earn one [[point]].

In the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, seven rabbits must be caught within 30 seconds. In Play Land, the character must catch as many rabbits as possible. In Mini-Game Attack, the player's character has to catch five rabbits within 30 seconds. In Play Land, catching a rabbit adds more seconds to the timer.
