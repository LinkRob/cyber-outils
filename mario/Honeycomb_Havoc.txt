'''Honeycomb Havoc''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]'', ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'', and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. It is a variation of the classic game {{wp|nim}}.

==Gameplay==
The players are standing in front of a giant tree that contains [[beehive|honeycomb]]s, [[fruit]]s, and (in the ''Mario Party 2'' version only) [[coin]]s. The player has to catch fruit in their basket by hitting a slow [[Dice Block]] and getting either a 1 or 2 each time. However, players have to avoid the honeycombs. When a player gets a honeycomb, some [[bee]]s come out and chase the player out of the minigame. If a player hits a 2 on the Dice Block but gets a honeycomb first, only the honeycomb will count while the fruit or coin will remain on the tree for the next player. The last player standing wins.

In the [[Mini-Game Coaster]], the human player has to avoid getting a honeycomb to win.

The minigame can be purchased from [[Woody]] for 150 coins.

==Trivia==
*In ''Mario Party 2'' and ''Mario Party: The Top 100'', there is a [[glitch]] that happens when a player rolls a 2 on the Dice Block. After the first fruit is in the basket, the character quickly turns right and then left again. This does not affect gameplay, but it was later fixed in ''Mario Party Superstars''.
