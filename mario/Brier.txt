'''Brier''' is an enemy in ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Brier is a floating, round, light green creature covered in thorns about the size of its main body. They also have a red variation.

===Behavior===
Briers continually pulse their thorns in and out, and their thorns can pop any [[bubble]] drawn onto it. The only difference between the regular and red Briers is that they each pulse their thorns at opposite intervals. Briers have a weaker variant named [[Marucchi]] that can be defeated from a drawing a bubble around it.

==In ''Yoshi Touch & Go''==
Briers are common enemies that are encountered in nearly every level, although red Briers appear only in Challenge and Vs. Battle modes. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can defeat a Brier by throwing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at it. If Yoshi throws an egg at a Brier while its spikes are retracted, the egg goes through it, but if an egg hits a Brier while its thorns are out, both the egg and the Brier get destroyed.

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''===
A Brier makes a cameo appearance as a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]], which depicts its ''Yoshi Touch & Go'' artwork In [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]], the sticker can be applied to any fighter to increase their launch resistance by 7 points.
