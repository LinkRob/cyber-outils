'''Green Toad''' is a color variant of the [[Toad (species)|Toads]] who apear as NPCs and citizens of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. They have green spots on their mushroom head and match the color vest they wear. Green Toads are typically portrayed as diligent workers, often seen excavating items from sandy terrain or hauling heavy objects with surprising strength and efficiency.
