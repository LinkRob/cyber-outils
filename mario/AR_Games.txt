'''''AR Games''''' is a preloaded app on [[Nintendo 3DS]] units consisting of a compilation of several augmented reality mini-games and simple tools.

==Description==
Every Nintendo 3DS system comes bundled with all six cards specifically intended for use with ''AR Games'', and the cards depict [[Mario]], [[Link]], [[Kirby]], [[Pikmin]], [[Samus Aran|Samus]], and a [[? Block]] from the {{series|Super Mario}} respectively. By scanning the cards, real time graphics are augmented onto live footage. It is also possible to take 3D photos of Nintendo characters, using any to all six AR Cards and their Miis.

Some of the minigames in ''AR Games'' feature ''Mario'' content, most notably Fishing, which has [[Cheep Cheep]]s, [[Blooper]]s, and [[Fish Bone]]s as possible fish to catch.
