A '''Smash Ball''' is a recurring item in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.

== Description ==
Smash Balls are rainbow-colored orbs that float around in the sky. They closely resemble the ''Super Smash Bros.'' franchise's logo. When someone breaks their outer crust, the Smash Balls shatter. The person who opened them will gain a powerful [[Final Smash]], making Smash Balls one of the most powerful items in the series. There is also a [[Smash Bomb|fake variation]] whose powerful energy rushes outward in a Supernova-esque explosion when it is broken.

== In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} ==
Smash Balls first appear in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. They float around the stage randomly, but can suffer knockback. When their outer layer is broken, the Smash Ball's opener will start to glow rainbow and can unleash a powerful Final Smash.

Smash Balls reappear in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', retaining their functionality from ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''.

Smash Balls made their last appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' so far. There is also the [[Smash Bomb]], which resembles them, but is very volatile and is known to explode.

== Nomenclature ==
In most languages, Smash Balls have their name translated literally:

* Chinese:  Luàn Dòu qiú (written as 亂鬥球)
* Dutch: Smashbol/Smashbal (''masc.'') 
* French:  Balle Smasche (''fem.'')
* German:  Smash-Ball (''masc.'')
* Spanish: Bola Smás (''fem.'')

Japanese and Korean give them transliterated names, resulting in:

* Japanese: Sumasshu Bōru (written as すますしゅぼうる in the Hiragana script and as スマッシュボール in the Katakana script)
* Korean: Seumasi Bol (written as 스매시 볼 in the Hangul script)

In Italian and Russian, their name is changed to either "Smash Orb" or "Smash Sphere" (''Sfera Smasci'' and ''Smesh-sfera'' (written as 	Смеш-сфера)), and they are treated as feminine regarding grammatical gender.
