'''King Red Coin Coffers''' are Fire/Fire attribute enemies and are found as early as World 7-9, and may also appear in World 7-10 and World 8-9. A King Red Coin Coffer may also randomly appear in the boss battle of Special World course ★World 5-4. As teammates, their Awoken Skill is '''Fire Boost''', and may provide ATK+ bonuses when depowered.
