}}'''Attack FX E''' is a [[badge]] that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Attack FX E is found in [[Shiver City]], one of the last settlements [[Mario]] visits. The badge is an attack FX badge so it does not have a [[Badge Point|BP]] cost and it changes the sound [[Mario]]'s [[hammer]] and [[Boots|jump]] attacks that written as from Yoshi's Squeaks.

It is located in the building on the west side of Shiver City that does not have a door leading inside. There is, however, a building adjacent to it, and inside of this building is a flight of stairs leading to its second floor. When Mario gets near one of the windows inside of this house, he can open the window to the outside. From here, Mario can access to the adjacent house, and enter it through its windows. Inside of this house is the Attack FX E badge.
