'''Breeze Buddy''' is a [[Gaddget]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is rewarded by [[Bowser]] for completing the quest [[Chillin' Villain]].

==Description==
Breeze Buddy is intended to function like a fan, although only within the game itself. Turning on the fan shows an animation of it blowing cool air. The player can toggle the speed in which the fan blows air and turn it off.
