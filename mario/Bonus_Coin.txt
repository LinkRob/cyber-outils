A '''Bonus Coin''' is a rare type of [[Coin]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. They resemble a regular coin but with a smiling face on them.

The only time Bonus Coins appear is from a [[Yoshi Cloud]], which drops ten of them, one at a time, then flies away. Upon touching the ground, a Bonus Coin bounces once before falling offscreen. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] collect all ten Bonus Coins, the Yoshi Cloud rewards them with a [[1-Up Mushroom]].
