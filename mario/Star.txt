'''Stars''' (alternatively referred to as '''Starmen''', '''Star Bonuses''',<ref>''Nintendo Magazine System'' (AU) Issue #49, page 39.</ref> '''Invincible Starmen''',<ref>Cain, Christine. ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide. Page 10.</ref> '''Invincibility Stars''',<ref>''Mario Pinball Land'' instruction booklet, page 23.</ref> '''Super Stars''',<ref>Bueno, Fernando. ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' European PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 13.</ref> or '''Superstars'''),<ref>[[:File:Stargazer Superstar.jpg|"''YOU FOUND A SUPERSTAR!''"]]—message in the ''[[Stargazer]]'' activity on the [[Play Nintendo]] website</ref> are items used in many ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, including the ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' series and the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Description==
[[File:Starman Classic.png|thumb|A Starman appearing in ''Super Mario Bros.'']]
A Star generally appears as a five-pointed star with beady eyes, most consistently yellow in color. If the player gets a Star, they will become [[Invincible Mario|invincible]]. The user becomes temporarily invulnerable to all damage, being able to defeat almost any enemy. However, hazards that would be fatal regardless of power-ups ([[Lava]], [[Poisoned Water]], [[pit]]s, [[Time Limit|time running out]], getting crushed, etc.) will still defeat the user, even with the Star. In addition, the black [[goop]] in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' mode in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'' will cause the player to lose their invincibility if touched while also taking damage, as if they took damage without the power-up.<ref>Fracktail (February 22, 2021). [https://twitter.com/Fracktail/status/1363738193497972738 oh ok #SuperMario3DWorld #BowsersFury #NintendoSwitch]. ''Twitter''. Retrieved February 23, 2021.</ref> The projectiles or enemies that come into contact with the user of the Star while it is activated are destroyed, damaged, or knocked off the screen (depending on the type of enemy and what game it is). The Star also increases the speed of the user substantially (again, depending on the game). It is the first ''Mario'' power-up with a pair of eyes.

Starting with ''[[Super Mario World]]'', if the player collides with and defeats eight enemies while invincible (five in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''), they will get an [[extra life]]. The [[point]] chain as of that game is 200 - 400 - 800 - 1,000 - 2,000 - 4,000 - 8,000 - 1UP - 1UP - 1UP... As the player continues defeating enemies, they will keep getting extra lives for each additional enemy defeated until the Star's power wears off and they are no longer invulnerable, with the exception of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', where after the player gets an extra life, the chain of points restarts (...4,000 - 8,000 - 1UP - 200 - 400...). Touching another Star while already invincible will prolong the effect and allow the player to continue getting more lives.

==Main appearances==
===''Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:Starman SMB.jpg|thumb|100px|left|A Starman in ''Super Mario Bros.'']]
[[File:Starman SMB.gif|frame]]
Starmen made their debut in the game ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', first found in [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]]. It is a very rare item, only found in some levels. When [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] gets it, they will turn invincible, allowing them to destroy enemies and go through hazards without getting hurt (though they will still lose a life if they fall in a [[pit]] or run out of time). However, the star's power is only temporary, and it will disappear after a certain amount of time. Invincibility is indicated by Mario's flashing sprites and the Starman theme which replaces the current soundtrack.
{{br}}

===''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''===
[[File:Starman Hard.png|thumb|200px|Mario about to collect a Starman in ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels'']]
The Starman returns in the same manner as everything else that returns in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''. They still grant Mario invincibility and the ability to defeat any enemy he touches. Starmen are much less common and are found mainly in [[Hidden Block]]s.

===''Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:SMB2 Star.gif|frame]]
Starmen return in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', but due to the absence of [[? Block]]s, the only way to get a Starman is to collect five [[Cherry|cherries]] in a level. The stars have the exact same effect as in the previous games; it grants the player invincibility and the ability to defeat any enemy. Instead of bouncing, a Starman floats upwards from the bottom of the screen in a zig-zag fashion.

===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:SMB3 Star.gif|frame]]
[[File:Starman Music.png|thumb|left|200px|A Starman spawned from a Jump Block in ''Super Mario Bros. 3'']]
Starmen are more common in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. Just like the preceding games, they grant Mario invincibility, but in addition, Mario can run slightly faster than normal. However, unlike most other games, the star's power lasts shorter than normal. If Mario jumps when he is not [[Small Mario]], he does flips in the air. Certain enemies are not affected by the effects of a Starman, like [[Jelectro]].

This game also introduces '''Continuous Starman'''<ref>M. Arakawa. ''Super Mario All-Stars'' Player's Guide. Page 113.</ref><ref>Peterson, Erik. ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3'' Player's Guide. Page 10.</ref> chains, where certain ? Blocks that normally contain [[coin]]s will contain additional stars instead if Mario is invincible when hitting them.

If Mario is fast or efficient enough, he can complete large sections of a level while invincible. This is achievable on [[World 3-2 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|Worlds 3-2]], [[World 5-1 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|5-1]], [[World 5-7 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|5-7]], [[World 7-3 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|7-3]], and [[World 7-7 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|7-7]], and bar [[P-Wing]] use, it is the only way to complete the last of these levels.

Starmen also appear as icons in the [[goal (Super Mario Bros. 3)|goal]] roulette, which must be hit in order to clear a level. If the player gets three Stars (easily done by running at max speed and jumping to the goal at a 45° angle), they receive five [[Extra life|extra lives]].
{{br|left}}
===''Super Mario Land''===
[[File:Star SML sprite.png|21px|right]]
[[File:Starman N-A.png|thumb|left|200px|A Starman being found in ''Super Mario Land'']]
Stars make a return in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. They are incredibly rare, appearing only in a few levels and mainly within Hidden Blocks. Unlike most games, Starmen are also much smaller and lack eyes. If Mario does not collect the Star, it falls out of the screen.

The Starman theme is different than usual, as it is composed of two loops of an excerpt from the famous "Galop infernal" from ''{{wp|Orpheus in the Underworld}}'' by {{wp|Jacques Offenbach}}, a tune most commonly associated with the {{wp|Can-Can|can-can}}.
{{br|left}}

===''Super Mario World'' / ''Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2''===
[[File:SMWStar.gif|frame|left]]
[[File:Starman SNES Island.png|thumb|Mario about to collect a Starman in ''Super Mario World''.]]
Super Stars are very rare in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', as in the preceding games. They are mostly found in [[Roulette Block]]s but are rare otherwise, occasionally appearing in ? Blocks. The Super Star grants the same invincibility effects as before and enables Mario or Luigi to defeat enemies that are normally invulnerable, such as [[Blargg]]s, [[Lava Bubble|Podoboo]]s, [[Boo]]s, and [[Thwomp]]s.
[[File:SMW Star.png|frame|left]]
The main difference is that Mario or Luigi scores a combo, similar to a shell or a jump combo, when he defeats enemies. This results in increasing point values for successive enemies, eventually gaining [[extra life|extra lives]] starting on the eighth, which became a recurring trait of the power-up, including in remakes of ''Super Mario Bros. 2'' and ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' where this trait was not present.

The music for [[Star World]] is a slowed version of the Super Star theme.

The Nintendo Official Player’s Guide for the original game refers to them as <b>Super Stars</b> while the Player’s Guide for the [[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2|remake]] reinstates the <b>Starman</b> name.
{{br}}
===''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''===
[[File:SML2-SuperStar-animated.gif|32px|left]]
[[File:Starman Gray.png|thumb|200px|A Star in ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'']]
In ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'', Stars do not move when freed from blocks. They can also be gained by defeating 100 enemies, making them slightly more common than before. Stars turn Mario invincible and allow him move faster, much like in previous games, though he gains extra lives from the fifth enemy defeated onwards, rather than the eighth. Stars are depicted after their appearance in ''Super Mario World''.

As with ''Super Mario Land'', the Star theme is a unique composition.

===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:NSMB Star.gif|frame|left]]
Starmen make Mario or Luigi invincible in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', as always. Mario and Luigi can run much faster while invincible, though the star's power does not last as long (only about 10 seconds). In certain levels, Starmen have the added ability of spawning a trail of [[Silver Coin]]s behind Mario or Luigi as he moves around.

The game introduces a feature that transforms some coin-containing ? Blocks into blocks containing Starmen while Mario or Luigi is under the invincibility effects of a Starman. Once the Starman's effects run out, the ? Blocks revert to containing coins.

The star is the second rarest item (after the [[Blue Shell]] power-up) and can only be found in some levels, such as [[World 1-1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|Worlds 1-1]], [[World 1-2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|1-2]], [[World 4-6 (New Super Mario Bros.)|4-6]], [[World 4-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|4-Castle]], [[World 6-2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|6-2]], [[World 7-3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|7-3]], [[World 8-4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|8-4]], and [[World 8-7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|8-7]]. Starmen sometimes appear in hidden ? Blocks, though they can usually be found in Roulette Blocks and occasionally in visible ? Blocks.

Starmen are the only power-up items that do not appear in [[Toad House]]s, cannot be stored as a [[item storage|Stored Item]], and do not appear in [[Red Flying ? Block]]s.

The Starman is also available in the [[Mario vs. Luigi]] mode, potentially appearing when Mario or Luigi collects eight [[coin]]s. Mario or Luigi has a higher chance of receiving a Starman if they are losing. A different type of star, the [[Big Star]], also appears exclusively in the multiplayer mode. Mario or Luigi must collect a certain number of them (three, five, or ten) to win.

===''Super Mario Galaxy'' series===
{{main|Rainbow Star}}
Although Stars do not appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', Rainbow Stars share the same function and also play the invincibility theme while under the effect of one.

===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[File:Starman Light.png|thumb|Mario illuminating a dark area under the effects of a Star in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.]]
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', Stars are mostly found through [[Roulette Block]]s or just regular [[? Block]]s. They behave very similarly to the previous game, but they now have the ability to light up dark places entirely, noticable especially in [[level]]s such as [[World 2-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2-3]]. The Super Star chain gimmick also returns from ''New Super Mario Bros.'', allowing Mario, Luigi, [[Yellow Toad]], and [[Blue Toad]] to hit certain ? Blocks while under the influence of a Super Star to spawn another one instead of coins. In multiplayer, one of the playable characters can share the effects of the Super Star by having a character currently affected by it carry one who is not. The theme cover is also different.

The player can now obtain them from [[Toad House]]s and store them in the inventory. Up to 99 Super Stars can be stored in the inventory alongside other power-ups by winning them from [[Power-Up Panel]]s. Furthermore, the protagonists can receive a Super Star from a [[Yellow Toad House]].

===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
[[File:Star3DLand.png|thumb|200px|Model from ''Super Mario 3D Land'']]
[[File:Starman Clouds.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario obtaining a Super Star in a Coin Heaven in ''Super Mario 3D Land''.]]
Super Stars make their 3D ''Super Mario'' debut in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. Like in ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'', in order to start getting extra lives for hitting enemies, the player only needs to hit five enemies, instead of the standard eight. Super Stars can be found in both ? Blocks and Roulette Blocks. They are most commonly found at the entrance of [[Coin Heaven]]s. From there, the Super Star can be used to knock away enemies along the path.

The effects of the Super Star are similar to that of the [[Invincibility Leaf]], except the latter's effects last until the level is either exited or completed. Additionally, unlike the Super Star, the Invincibility Leaf does not grant extra lives upon defeating a chain of enemies.

As the power-up begins to wear off, the music speeds up, similar to the effect of the Rainbow Stars in the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' games. Unlike those games, however, the music accelerates during the second bar rather than the fifth, as seen in both this game and ''Super Mario 3D World''. In the [[List of Super Mario 3D Land pre-release and unused content#Demo|E3 2011 demo version]], it sped up during the fifth bar, like in ''Super Mario Galaxy''.

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:Starman Secret.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario finding a Starman in ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'']]
Stars reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', functioning and behaving as in the previous 2D ''Super Mario'' games. They can be found mostly in Hidden Blocks or Roulette Blocks and rarely appear in ? Blocks, following the precedent set in ''New Super Mario Bros.'' The coin block-chain feature returns from past installments where Question Blocks normally containing Coins can contain Stars instead if Mario or Luigi is under the effects of a Star. The contrasts between the Star and the Invincibility Leaf from ''Super Mario 3D Land'' are retained. The effects of the [[Mega Mushroom]] also features a sped up theme when its effects are about to expire, similar to the Star.

The player can store a Star in the [[item storage]] exclusively from the [[World Star (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Star]] Toad House by obtaining all the [[Star Coin]]s and the [[Moon Coin]]s.
{{br}}

===''New Super Mario Bros. U'' / ''New Super Luigi U'' / ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[File:Starman Shore.png|thumb|A Super Star in ''New Super Mario Bros. U'']]
The Super Star returns in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', retaining the same attributes it had from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. The Super Star is once again used to illuminate dark areas, like in [[Light Blocks, Dark Tower]]. As with previous ''New Super Mario Bros.'' titles, Super Stars can spawn from certain ? Blocks in succession if the player character is currently under the invincibility effects of a Super Star.

In [[level]]s, Super Stars are found in ? Blocks, Hidden Blocks, and [[Coin Block]]s. Super Stars can also be obtained from [[Toad House]]s as well as a [[treasure chest]] that appears from completing an [[Enemy Course]]; in ''New Super Mario Bros. U'', the Super Star has a very high chance of appearing, but in ''New Super Luigi U'' and ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe'', the possibility has been lowered in favor of other items such as the [[Propeller Mushroom]], [[Mini Mushroom]], and the [[Super Crown]]. A Super Star can also be earned in the inventory by reaching the [[Flagpole]] with the final digits at "99".

The [[Boost Star]] in ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' looks nearly identical to the Super Star, but the only difference is that it is purple in color.

===''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''===
[[File:Super Star Artwork - Super Mario 3D World.png|thumb|200px|A  Star from ''Super Mario 3D World'']]
[[File:Starman Cave Find.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario discovering a Star in ''Super Mario 3D World''.]]
Stars reappear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|its port]]. They work similarly as in ''Super Mario 3D Land'' though the sharing feature from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' is retained: when one player acquires a Star, they can touch other players and make them invincible as well. They award an extra life for every eighth enemy defeated on contact. Stars do not appear as frequently as they do in ''Super Mario 3D Land'' but can still be found in ? Blocks, Hidden Blocks, and Roulette Blocks. The contrasts between the Star and the Invincibility Leaf from ''Super Mario 3D Land'' and ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'' apply once more.

''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury'' introduces another power-up item titled the [[Invincibility Bell]], which grants invincibility like the Invincibility Leaf, only combined with the effects of the [[Super Bell]] instead of the [[Super Leaf]].

In the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury'', like all the other power-up items, Stars can be obtained randomly by collecting 100 coins.

===''Super Mario Maker'' subseries===
[[File:Super Starman Maker.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario discovering a Super Star in ''Super Mario Maker''.]]
In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', Super Stars are among the various elements that can be placed in levels. They can be placed either inside ? Blocks or directly on the field, the latter marking the first instance in a ''Super Mario'' title where they appear openly in levels. Super Stars are one of three power-up items available in every game style, with the [[Super Mushroom]] and [[Fire Flower]] being the other two. Super Stars function as they do in other ''Super Mario'' titles, granting the player temporary invincibility and the ability to defeat enemies on contact.

===''Super Mario Run''===
[[File:Starman Dual.png|thumb|Luigi about to grab two Stars in ''Super Maruo Run'']]
Stars reappear in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. It is now only one of two power-up items, the other being the Super Mushroom. Stars can be found in ? Blocks or, for the first time, by collecting every [[Red Coin]] spawned from a [[Red Ring]].

This time around, the user of the item becomes slightly magnetic as well as invincible, as coins will be drawn towards them. [[Black Coin]]s are resistant to this force and will simply shake left to right. In Toad Rally, collecting a Star starts [[Coin Rush]] mode, with the meter glowing white until the effects wear off.

=== ''Super Mario Bros. 35'' ===
Starmen appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 35]]'' and work exactly like in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' The Item Roulette has a chance of granting the player a Starman.

=== ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ===
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', the Star returns as a power-up item. It retains its capability to draw in coins nearby from ''Super Mario Run''.
