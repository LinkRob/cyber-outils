'''Happy Heart''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. By equipping this badge, [[Mario]] has his [[Heart Point|HP]] gradually restored during battle. The Happy Heart has an [[Flower Point|FP]]-based equivalent named [[Happy Flower (badge)|Happy Flower]].

==In ''[[Paper Mario]]''==
[[File:Happy_heart_badge.png|thumb|left]]
There are two Happy Heart badges. Mario can obtain one of these badges from [[Merlow]] at [[Shooting Star Summit]] who will trade eight [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]] for this badge. The second is found in [[Jade Jungle]] at [[Raphael the Raven]]'s giant tree. Mario can wear this badge for three [[Badge Point|BP]].

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Happy Heart instead costs two BP to wear, just like for the Happy Flower badge. Mario can find the Happy Heart at [[Petal Meadows]] and purchase it from [[Dazzle]] for four Star Pieces. Happy Heart now has an variation named [[Happy Heart P]], which can be equipped on one of Mario's partners.
