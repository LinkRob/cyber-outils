'''Rainbow Note Blocks''' are rare blocks which lead to secret areas, before returning the player to where they left.

== Appearances ==
===Super Mario Bros. 3===
The Rainbow Note Blocks do not actually display rainbow colors in the game. They look like a light red [[Note Block]]. When jumped on, the player will be propelled to a secret area, a [[Coin Heaven]]. This area is filled with coins, and occasionally hosts a [[1-Up Mushroom]].
===Super Mario 3D Land===
In certain area, Mario or Luigi must locate one of the Rainbow Note Blocks in order to gain one of the three [[Star Medal]]s. When Mario or Luigi arrives in the secret area, they will get a [[Rainbow Star]] and have to race to the other side through many enemies. If they do it right, they will gain a 1-Up Mushroom in addition to the Star Medal. 

== Trivia ==
*In Super Mario 3D World, the Cloud Cannon replaces the Rainbow Note Block. It leads to a secret area which is the same format to those in Super Mario 3D Land.
