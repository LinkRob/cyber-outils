'''Fondest Hopes''' is a shop that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Fondest Hopes is a [[Catch Card]] shop that is run by [[Boodin]] in [[Flopside]]. This shop is notable for selling [[Catch Card SP]]s which is one of the few ways to get that item. Most Catch Cards can be bought here and the price depends on how rare it is. Boodin can also by any extra Catch Cards.
