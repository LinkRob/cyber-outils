Stay in line and don’t fall out of order!"''|forms = "The Big Cheese"}}'''Marching Orders''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in  ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
To player is in the middle of the line. The player starts in "The Big Cheese" form, then drops his or her arms and supposedly swinging the [[Wii Remote]] to move. When it prompts the player to "March!", then march by swinging the arms. When it prompts the player to "Stop!", the player must stop. The time must run out while the player is not hitting anyone. If they bump another, the microgame ends in a loss.
*'''Level 1:''' One stop.
*'''Level 2:''' Two stops.
*'''Level 3:''' Two stops and a faster pace. The line is small.
