'''Lord Fredrik''', also known as '''Lord Fredrik the Snowmad King''' or simply '''Fredrik''', is the main antagonist and the final boss of [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze|''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze'']] and [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze (Nintendo Switch)|its port]]. He is the leader of the [[Snowmad Tribe]], a tribe of arctic and antarctic animal seafarers based on stereotypical depictions of vikings.

== Profile ==

=== Personality ===
While his personality is not known, it is known that Fredrik is a ruthless individual. His mannerisms during the fight show him to be somewhat violent when something incites his anger. Despite his general hatred for the Kongs, Fredrik does seem amused to see them in his fortress, which is actually his command ship stationed on top of [[Donkey Kong Island]]'s volcano.

=== Physical Appearance ===
Lord Fredrik is a massive walrus-like creature with dark grey skin and a black moustache. He wears a large double-horned helmet with a pale blue, rhombus-shaped crystal on the front, a large white fur cape, bracelets with rhombus-shaped crystals similar to the one on his helmet across the surfaces, a white fur skirt held up by a belt with the Snowmad emblem in front and smaller, circular crystals across the strap and black briefs.

== History ==

=== In ''Donkey Kong: Tropical Freeze'' ===
During the introductory cutscene to [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze|''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze'']], Fredrik is informed by one of his minions of [[Donkey Kong Island]] (where [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] is celebrating his birthday), and leads the Snowmad Tribe there. He then blows into his enchanted horn, which summons an ice dragon that blows the Kongs away from the island, which the Snowmads proceed to invade by stationing their command ship on top of the island's volcano to construct their fortress. The ice dragon that Fredrik summoned with his horn then proceeds to throw the island into a state of winter.

[[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and his friends later return to reclaim their island. When the Kongs reach his lair, Fredrik is amused and instigates a fight for control of the island. The Kongs defeat Fredrik and send him flying through his command ship (destroying it as well as the Snowmad fortress) and into the sea where his minions' remaining ships are stationed, ending the Snowmad Tribe's reign over Donkey Kong Island; it is unknown if Fredrik survived. Donkey Kong then restores his home's climate with Fredrik's horn, which the Snowmad King had dropped during his downfall.

==== Combat ====
Lord Fredrik is fought in a lava cavern that he dropped the Kongs into, with the Kongs standing on a platform in the lava.

===== Phase 1 =====
Lord Fredrik begins the battle by jumping into the background of his arena and increasing in size, blowing into his horn to summon balls of ice energy at Donkey Kong, who must dodge them. Some of them will spill out Snowmad minions that must be thrown into the background to hit Fredrik; however Fredrik will run side to side to dodge being pelted by his minions, pausing after some time to adjust his helmet.

===== Phase 2 =====
After getting pelted by enough Snowmads, Fredrik will shrink back to his regular size and return to the foreground, charging DK to spear him with his helmet. DK must jump over Fredrik and land on his back to deal damage. If his first charge misses and he is not hit, Fredrik may turn around and attempt another charge. If he stops the pattern, he will jump up and land somewhere else on the platform, sinking part of it into the lava and moving on to another part. After enough hits to his back, Fredrik reassumes his giant form and retreats to the background.

===== Phase 3 =====
This phase is identical to the first phase, save for a new attack where Fredrik summons ice dragons from his horn. These dragons are preceded by ice shards falling from the top of the screen, showing where their dives come from. If the dragons miss DK, they freeze part of the platform, making manoeuvring harder.

===== Phase 4 =====
As with phase 3 with the starting phase, this phase matches phase 2, but Fredrik now performs his charge attack at longer, faster intervals. After enough damage, he begins performing a spinning jump, which creates lava pillars that lift up all of the platform except for a piece that he is standing on. The pillars will eventually subside, after which the pieces of the platform fall back to their spots after remaining airborne for a few seconds.

===== Phase 5 =====
This phase is identical to phase 3.

===== Phase 6 =====
This phase is identical to phase 4, but Fredrik can also summon ice dragons from his horn. The dragons must be jumped or ducked to avoid damage, telegraphed by their altitudes.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Lord Fredrik takes his design from the walrus, much like the Waldough enemy (a member of the Snowmad Tribe). His name is either Norwegian or Swedish, and is cognate with English ''Frederick'', from Latin ''Friderīcus'', from Proto-Germanic ''Friþurīks'' ("peaceful king," which is ironic, considering Fredrik's violent personality).

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese and Russian, Lord Fredrik is simply referred to by transliterations of his name (''Furedorikku'' and ''Fredrik'', respectively; written フレドリック and Фредрик). His Spanish name, ''Frigorico'', is a pun on ''frigorífico'' ("refrigerator"), and the given name ''Federico'' ("Frederick," "Fred"), and his title, ''El Rey Morsário'', comes from ''morsa'' ("walrus") and ''corsário'' ("corsair"), essentially meaning "The Walsair King." In Italian, his name is ''Renaldo'' ("Reynold"), and his title is "the king of ices." French translates his name as ''Frighorriffik'', a corruption of ''frigorifique'' ("refrigerating") that combines it with ''horrifique'' ("horrific") or ''horreur'' ("terror," "horror"). His German name, ''Seine Frostigkeit König Qual'', means "His Frostiness King Torment."
