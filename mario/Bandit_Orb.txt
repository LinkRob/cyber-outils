The '''Bandit Orb''' is an [[orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
The Bandit Orb can only be thrown to a [[space]] on the [[board]]. If a character lands on a space triggered by a Bandit Orb, [[Bandit]] appears at one of the [[windmill]]s. Bandit steals their coins from that windmill and gives them to the owner of the space. There is a possibility that Bandit does not steal any coins, if he goes to a windmill without any coins.

==History==
The Bandit Orb only appears in [[Windmillville]], where it costs 15 [[coin]]s to purchase from an [[Orb Shop]].
