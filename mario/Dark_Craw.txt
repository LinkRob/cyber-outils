'''Dark Craws''' are one of the enemies featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
A Dark Craw is first encountered in the [[Glitz Pit]] of [[Glitzville]] under the name [[Craw-Daddy]]. It is one of the last opponents that [[Mario]] can face in the tournament. The Dark Craw is a tough opponent and it fights by throwing or rushing it's spears at Mario (much like [[Gus]]).

More Dark Craws can be encountered in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] between floors 61 and 69. Dark Craws are very common on these floors and are often fought in groups with themselves or other enemies.
