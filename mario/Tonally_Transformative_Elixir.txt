The '''Tonally Transformative Elixir''' is an item which only appeared in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Description ==
The Tonally Transformative Elixir will cause anybody who drinks it to have their voice changed. It also causes some kind of stomache illness if taken in large doses.

== History ==
In [[Penny's Lab]], Penny Crygor created the Tonally Transformative Elixir to become a mega pop star. She used her grandfather, [[Dr. Crygor]] as a test subject. The results proved to Penny that Crygor's voice became high-pitched but gave him a stomachache. Penny created a [[Super-Special Tummy Tonic]] to ease the pain which cured Crygor's stomachache.

== Trivia ==
* Of the many elixirs that Penny has, this is the only elixir that is mentioned in WarioWare Gold by [[Penny Crygor]].
