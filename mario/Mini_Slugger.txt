The '''Mini Slugger''' is a [[Gaddget]] found in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is rewarded by [[Mr. Blizzard (character)|Mr. Blizzard]] for completing the [[Cool as Ice]] quest, which similarly has a simple baseball theme to it.

==Gameplay==
Mini Slugger is one of the Gaddgets designed for two players on a single system. One player has to pitch either a slow ball or a fast ball, while the other tries to hit the incoming throws by swinging the bat. Any of the hit balls stick to the walls and the four [[Bowser Statue]]s positioned around the field. After the batter misses six balls, the field resets itself.
