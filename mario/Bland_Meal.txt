'''Bland Meals''' are one of the [[recipe]]s made by [[Tayce T.]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Befitting it's name, the Bland Meal is a very basic item, only helpful early on. However, this item cannot be obtained until Chapter 4. [[Mario]] heals 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 10 [[Flower Point|FP]] upon consuming this meal. There are numerous of ways to cook this item up. This is one of the frequent items that come out of random recipe mixing as well and it doesn't sell for much in [[Item Shop]]s.
