'''Ice Smash''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
While wearing this badge, [[Mario]] can unleash a new [[hammer]] attack that can freeze his opponents for three [[Flower Point|FP]].

Mario can obtain multiples of this badge on his adventure. One of these badges is found in [[Creepy Steeple]]. Another Ice Smash is found in [[Rogueport]] at the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] where it costs 75 [[coin]]s to purchase.
