The '''Swooper''' [[glider]] (known as '''Swoop''' in PAL regions) is an unlockable glider in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. Hence the name, it is a glider that resembles a [[Swooper]] enemy. It has good [[acceleration]], but lacks [[weight]]. Its stats are the same as the [[Paraglider]].
