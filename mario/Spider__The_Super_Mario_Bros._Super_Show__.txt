'''Spiders''' are an antagonistic species that make a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Count Koopula]]."

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are large, black arachnids with red legs. Spiders wear a gas mask resembling that of a [[Snifit]]'s, although they are not shown to spit bullets from it.

===Behavior===
Spiders are shown to make chirping noises.

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
After jumping down a laundry chute and landing into Count Koopula's basement, [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] are confronted by Spiders. Before the spiders get a chance to attack, Princess Toadstool scares them off by having a [[Venus Fire Trap|Magic Piranha Plant]] spit two fireballs at them. One of the spiders gets hit in the abdomen and explodes.
