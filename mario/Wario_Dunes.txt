'''Wario Dunes''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''.

==Description==
Wario Dunes takes place a desert where cacti and an Egyptian pyramid appear in the background, similarly to the [[Wario Court]] from ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' for [[Nintendo 64]]. Aside from this, there is another pyramid at the front of the court as well as unlit [[Torch (Super Mario series)|torches]]. Red, Green, Blue and Pink [[Shy Guy]]s surround the court. Wario Dunes has a slow ball speed and a weak bounce strength, due to the sandy environment.

==In ''Mario Tennis Open''==
Wario Dunes is used for the [[Flower Cup]] tournament.
