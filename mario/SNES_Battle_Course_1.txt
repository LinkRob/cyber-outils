'''SNES Battle Course 1''' is the first of four [[Battle Stage]]s in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. This course's theme vastly resembles that of the [[Donut Plains]] and [[Mario Circuit]] tracks. It returns in the battle mode for ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' with updated graphics, [[Item Box]]es, larger size, [[Boost Pad]]s, and [[Glide Ramp]]s.
