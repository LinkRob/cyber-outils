'''RoboUrchins''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
RoboUrchins are mechanical urchins that can only be found on [[Sweatmore Peak]]. They are similar to [[Lurchin (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Lurchin]]s, but smaller and younger. They also stay in place and can only damage [[Wario]] if he comes into contact with them. RoboUrchins can be taken out using [[Captain Wario (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Captain Wario]]'s missiles.
