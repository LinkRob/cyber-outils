The '''Three Little Pigheads''', also named the '''Three Little Pigs''', are a trio of pigs fought as the boss of [[Mario Zone]] in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. Here, they are fought together within the level [[Final Bosses: Three Mean Pigs!]].

The Three Little Pigheads are based on the ''{{w|The Three Little Pigs}}'' fairy tale. Furthermore, each of the Three Little Pigheads are hiding within their own birdhouse-like structure from the ceiling, in the likeness of a straw house, a wooden house, and a brick house respectively, much like in ''The Three Little Pigs''.
