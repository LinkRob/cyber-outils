'''Faraway Oasis''' is a track in ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. It is the second track of the [[Shell Cup]].

== Description ==
Faraway Oasis is a large savanna with a track that is themed after safari. It is habitable for animals in a woodland-grassland biome with rivers where drivers can cross in.
