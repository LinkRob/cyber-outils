'''Tsūdebiru''' is a mini-boss in ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tsūdebiru is an orange, jar-like creature.

===Behavior===
Tsūdebiru produces [[Tako]] and attacks by launching waves of them at [[Mario]] and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].

==In ''Yoshi's Safari''==
Tsūdebiru is encountered in the underwater portion of [[Crescent Coast]]. After taking several hits from the [[Super Scope]], Tsūdebiru is defeated, dropping behind foamy bubbles in the process.

==Trivia==
*Tsūdebiru is the only mini-boss who is not fought twice in ''Yoshi's Safari''.
