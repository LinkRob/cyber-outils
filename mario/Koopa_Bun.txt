A '''Koopa Bun''' is a [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It can be made by [[Zess T.]] by giving her a [[Turtley Leaf]] and a [[Keel Mango]].

==Effects==
Eating a Koopa Bun restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]]. In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', there is a similar item named [[Koopa Dumpling]], which replaces the Koopa Bun.
