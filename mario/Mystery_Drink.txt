A '''Mystery Drink''' is an item in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Description==
A Mystery Drink randomly increases or decreases [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s dance meter at the start of the song.

==History==
In Story Mode, the Mystery Drink can be bought at any shop for ten [[coin]]s.

==Nomenclature==
*'''Japanese:''' ランダムドリンク (''Randamu Dorinku''), meaning "Random Drink"
