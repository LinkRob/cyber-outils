'''Tornado Swing''' is the [[Star Swing]] used by [[Luigi]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:Tornado Swing usage.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Luigi sends the Tornado Swing off into the field]]
Upon using Tornado Swing, Luigi spins around with his bat to create a tornado, which the ball becomes trapped in. The tornado then travels across the field, briefly stunning whoever it hits. After a few seconds, the tornado disappears, and the ball lands onto the ground.
