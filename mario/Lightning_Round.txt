'''Lightning Round''' is an unlockable Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
A [[Sumo Bro]] standing on top of a platform approaches a large platform and performs two sparks. Players land down to the platform.

== Gameplay ==
Players can collect coins where it appears on the platform while [[Sumo Bro|Sumo Bros.]] strikes sparks. Players lose three coins when sparks come on contact.

== Ending ==
The Sumo Bros. leave the area. The players with coins do their victory animations while players with no coins do their losing animation.

*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
