Arakawa. ''Donkey Kong 64'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1999, p. 79 - "'''''BLUEPRINT DUSTUP:''' The bats in the area are aggressive, and Skeleton Kremlings are trudging around.''"</ref> (also called '''Bones''', '''Krossbones''' and '''Bone Kritters''') are enemies that appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. They are skeletal [[Kritter]]s that are first found in [[Fungi Forest]] during nighttime and later at [[Creepy Castle]] as the most common enemies there. They are rather uncommon enemies to encounter overall due to appearing late in the game.

==History==
Bones tend to patrol an area until they spot a Kong. When they attack, Bones mainly swing their bone club. However, this attack tends to miss especially against smaller Kongs like [[Diddy Kong]] and [[Tiny Kong]]; because of this, Bones may, instead, deliver a short-ranged kick that is effective and certain to hit up close. If a Bones are attacked once by a weak attack, it's head will fall off but will still be able to move around easily. After enough hits, they will be defeated.
