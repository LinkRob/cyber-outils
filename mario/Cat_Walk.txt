'''Cat Walk''' is a [[microgame]] of [[Mona]]'s stage in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''

==Gameplay==
The player controls a light-blue bipedal cat, whose goal is to cross a yellow bridge made out of some wooden logs from the roof of [[WarioWare, Inc.]] to a ten-story building on the opposite side. The player has to tilt the system so that the cat maintains its balance while crossing and does not fall off. If successful, the cat claps its paws as a spotlight shines on it.

The bridge becomes gradually shorter with each difficulty level, making it increasingly hard for the cat to maintain its balance. The bridge is made of seven, four and two rows of wood in the first, second, and third difficulty levels, respectively.
