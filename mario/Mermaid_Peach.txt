'''Mermaid Peach''' is a power-up in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''

==Overview==
Mermaid Peach involves [[Princess Peach]] being a mermaid with a light-blue tail. She can use a singing voice, similarly to {{w|Ariel (The Little Meramid)|Ariel}} from Disney's ''{{w|The Little Mermaid}}''.

===Physical appearance===
As a mermaid, Peach wears a frilled, strapless metallic swimsuit top, with a turquoise layer under a lavender one, that exposes her midriff and her belly button, a scaled mermaid tail that has a gradient from turquoise to magenta, and a pearl necklace with her brooch as a pendant. She also wears pearl earrings and a gold bracelet on each wrist, and her hair is kept down by default, although it is a little messier.
