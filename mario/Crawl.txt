'''Crawling''' is a move in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It is done by holding Z in the N64 version or R in the DS version, then moving in any direction. [[Mario]] or other characters will perform a crawl walking slowly with their arms and knees. Crawling is used to scale some steep slopes and walk carefully on sloppy floors.
