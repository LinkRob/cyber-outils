'''HP Plus P''' is a [[badge]] found in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It enables each of [[Mario]]'s partners to have their max [[Heart Point|HP]] increased by 5, although at a cost of 6 [[Badge Point|BP]]. Like most badges, HP Plus P can be stacked. HP Plus P is the partner-based variation of [[HP Plus (badge)|HP Plus]].

==Locations==
This badge can be obtained through several means:

* [[Mario]] uses [[Yoshi (partner)|Yoshi]] to reach a box located on the second floor of the Secret Room in [[Glitzville]].
* Mario can find another one by reaching the end of the road that leads to [[Fahr Outpost]]. Enemies can occasionally be found with the badge, and [[Ms. Mowz]] is needed to steal it from them.
* Mario can purchase the badge for 200 [[Pianta Token]]s at the [[Pianta Parlor]].
