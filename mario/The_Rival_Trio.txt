'''The Rival Trio''' is the sixth level of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 8.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 7 [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Sergeant Guy]]. The enemy captain is Sergeant Guy. Sergeant Guy's backup is a [[Fly Guy]] or [[Spear Guy]].

The 2nd battle contains 7 [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s and [[Corporal Paraplonk]]. The enemy captain is Corporal Paraplonk. His special move is Ultimate Shell Shock, and deals damage to several of the player's allies.

The final battle contains 7 [[Goomba]]s and [[Private Goomp]]. The enemy captain is Private Goomp. His special move is [[Deny]], and at the end Private Goomp engages in 1v1 combat with the player. 

After the final battle, the player unlocks a Goomba and a Paratroopa.

The player can replay the battle to unlock up to two Goomba, Shy Guy, and or Koopa Paratroopa.
