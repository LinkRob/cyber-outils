'''Shortfuses''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Shortfuses are living, orange [[Barrel Cannon]]-like enemies with two brown feet and a fuse on their fuse. Shortfuses also have red and golden color variations.

===Behavior===
A Shortfuse first ignites its fuse before shooting [[Spiked Ball|spiky balls]] at [[Wario]]. Regular Shortfuses only shoot spiky balls while standing in place, while the red variety is able to [[jump]].

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Shortfuses have a threat rating of two stars. They are commonly found in [[Ratl Ruins]].
