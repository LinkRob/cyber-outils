The '''Rain Cloud''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and its sequels, ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
The Rain Cloud is a light-blue [[Lakitu's Cloud]] with a water drop on top. Upon using the item, a cloud appears above the opponent's head, and the resulting rain makes it harder for them to control their [[kart]].

Similar items include the [[Snow Cloud]] and [[Thunder Cloud]].
