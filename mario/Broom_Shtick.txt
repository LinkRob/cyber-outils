'''Broom Shtick''' is one of [[Young Cricket]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. The microgame is similar to ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''{{'}}s [[Balancing Act (WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!)|Balancing Act]] and ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''{{'}}s [[Balancing Act (WarioWare: Twisted!|Balancing Act]] and [[On a Roll]], as all three also involve an objective of balancing an object.

The microgame's name is a pun of "broom stick".

==Gameplay==
The player must balance the broom while time is running. If the time runs out while the broom is balancing, the player wins. If the broom falls, the player loses.

*'''Level 1:''' The broom is blue. Its height is long and length is thin. It is easy to balance.

*'''Level 2:''' The broom is pink. Its height is shorter and length is longer. It is harder to balance it.

*'''Level 3:''' The broom is yellow. Its height is short and length is long. It is hard to balance it.
