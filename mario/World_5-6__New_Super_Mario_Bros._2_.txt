'''World 5-6''' is the level of [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. This level is based upon filled with many moving platforms. The [[Piranha Plant]] is the frequent enemy in this level. 

If the player travel through the air while as [[White Raccoon Mario]], he can notice any coins on the highest point of spikes.

==Layout==
At the starting of this level, Mario slump the cliff. There is a blue pipe inside with a [[Piranha Plant]] and any [[? Block]]s. It holds a [[Fire Flower]] at the middle blue pipe.  Afterwards, a stair-like orange platform area with a red [[Koopa Troopa]] on each platform below it are two crossing blue pipes inside with Piranha Plants. At the end, a red Koopa Troopa between two [[Brick Block]], one of which it is a [[Coin Block]] that can right on a [[Gold Block]].  Descend, there is a red pipe holding a [[Venus Fire Trap]]. Further down, there is a set of rotating platforms, with a blue pipe holding a Piranha Plant, and a [[Super Piranha Plant]]. Even further down, there is a many rotating platforms, few [[Goomba]]s, and a yellow pipe with a [[Red Ring]] above the rotating platforms. If the player moves to the right, they will be met with another Big Piranha Plant, but this Big Piranha Plant is look after an [[! Pipe]] that produces a [[Mega Mushroom]]. The player will be met with a red Venus Fire Trap pipe and more rotating platforms, and will reach the ground. The player must use rotating platforms to turn across, but the left Venus Fire Trap has a red Koopa Troopa on the rotating platforms. The player will be met some bricks, a [[Star Coin]], and a [[! Switch]] on the left. It construct the blocks appear under the rotating platforms. The player can then turn right to realize a [[Checkpoint Flag|checkpoint flag]] and some ? Blocks, one of [[Question Block|Question Blocks]] contains a Fire Flower. As the player goes to the right, they will be met with some Goombas and pipes, except this time, red pipes hold Venus Fire Traps, blue pipes hold normal Piranha plants, and yellow pipes are safe. The player will eventually end up at a set of rotating platforms on a track with a green pipe they can move into, a [[Big Venus Fire Trap]], and more pipes below it. Next, there is a Goomba and a Star Coin, but it has stone blocks above the Star Coin. The player must move up the pipes to earn to the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Just before the checkpoint, the Star Coin is seen to the right of the spinning platforms and above spiked platforms. The player must hit the red switch to safely collect this coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the checkpoint, Mario or Luigi must find a green pipe that will take them underground to a room with the star coin. The player needs to use a springboard or [[White Raccoon Mario]] to reach it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Just before the flagpole, the Star Coin is buried underneath stone blocks. The player can only collect it with the [[Mega Mushroom]].
