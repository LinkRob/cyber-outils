'''Movements''' is a room located in [[Old Clockworks]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is accessible only in Mission C-Boss: [[Showtime]]. It has a door from the [[Clock Tower Gate]] and a door leading to the [[Belfry Tower]].

==History==
Once [[Luigi]] enters the Movements, he must use the [[Poltergust 5000]] on three rotors to move the base platform up to the door leading to the Belfry Tower. There are several [[Spider]]s and lots of treasure on the way up. The mansion's thirteenth [[Gem]] is in the Movements. Just walk over to the far right window on the base floor and use the Poltergust 5000 and the Gem will be pulled through the window and towards you.
