'''''Donkey Kong''''' is a video game franchise preceding the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. This video game series mainly features [[Donkey Kong]] as the main character. Usually, he is featured either as the main protagonist or antagonist.

==Overview==
Being a major series, the ''Donkey Kong'' series also has other series of games within it as well. The main backbone of the ''Donkey Kong'' series is the [[Donkey Kong Country (series)|''Donkey Kong Country'' series]] which is a series of 2-D platformers with many aspects taken from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. There are also many other spin-off series within the ''Donkey Kong'' series, one of which is the [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (series)|''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series]], following after the [[Donkey Kong (series)|''Donkey Kong'' series]], which features [[Mario]] as the main protagonist and Donkey Kong as the main antagonist (though is usually more portrayed as sympathetic rather than truly bad).
