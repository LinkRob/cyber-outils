Below is a list of [[minigame|mini-game]]s found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. There are 71 [[Minigame|mini-games]] found total. There are 20 4-Player mini-games, 10 1 vs. 3 mini-games, 10 2 vs. 2 mini-games, 8 Battle mini-games, 10 Duel mini-games, 6 Item mini-games, 3 Rare mini-games, and 4 [[Game Guy]] mini-games. The following is a list of all of them. Mini-games marked in gold are [[coin]]-collecting mini-games.
