An '''Ice Bomb''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
[[File:Ice Bomb SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
An Ice Bomb is a very effective item capable of dealing 140 [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage to all enemies onscreen. An Ice Bomb is exceptionally effective on enemies vulnerable to ice-elemental attacks, most of them being [[Fire (element)|fire]]-elemental enemies, as the Ice Bomb deals twice the amount of damage to them. Ice Bombs are ineffective on ice enemies.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Ice Bombs are rather rare items in the game as they can only be purchased in the [[Item Shop]] in [[Seaside Town]] during [[Yaridovich]]'s takeover. There, Ice Bombs cost 250 [[coin]]s apiece. Certain enemies such as [[Muckle]]s may also drop Ice Bombs upon being defeated.
