'''Lava Drops''' (also called '''Fire Drops''') are a sub-species of [[Cloud Drop]] and a relative of [[Podoboo]] featured in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==In {{series|Yoshi's Island}}==
In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', they commonly appear in fire-themed levels, [[fort]]s, [[castle]]s, and underground volcanoes. Lava Drops are very common in [[World 6 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 6]]. Lava Drops behave identically to Cloud Drops moving in the up, down, left, and right in the air to try and hit a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to knock [[Baby Mario]] off his back, they are commonly seen in groups making avoiding them a challenge, they can be somewhat annoying as they are impervious to all of Yoshi's attacks including swallowing and even [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] throws. The only way defeat a Lava Drop is by passing through a [[Middle Ring]] or from using a [[Winged Cloud]].

Lava Drops have since reappeared in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' with the same role as in the original. In the latter, they behave identically to [[Podoboo]]s by hopping out of pits of [[lava]] in addition to retaining their behaviors from previous games. Lava Drops also appear in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.
