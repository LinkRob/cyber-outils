Edge is a strong honor-bound warrior Rabbid. She is very confident in her skills but far from arrogant. She has made plenty of close friends while having a fair share of enemies as well. 

==Friends/Allies==
==='''[[Rabbid Mario]]'''===
[[File:Rabbid Mario and Edge.jpg|thumb|left|Rabbid Mario tries to get Edge to join the dance party.]]
Edge basically sees Rabbid Mario as her friend, though Rabbid Mario was clearly in love with Edge once he saw her defeat the Stooges that were attacking Twinkle. Edge does not show any interest in romance and has other things to worry about and when Rabbid Mario tries various tactics to win her over, which often ends with her getting annoyed or ignoring him, some of them involve Rabbid Mario creating a muscular snowman, telling her that they "make quite a pair", and when she confessed to have been the commander of the Spark Hunters, he lies about her nearly fainting at the sight of his enormous biceps. He is also seen glaring at Beep-0 after he gets too suspicious of her which resulted in her getting angry as if he is sticking up for her. Even after Edge reveals her origins near the end of the game, Rabbid Mario shows that he unconditionally loves Edge, and the revelation didn't bother him since her heroic deeds she does for the greater good overshadow anything bad she did as a Spark Hunter. However, in Melodic Gardens, Edge and Rabbid Mario can be seen smiling at each other after they save the planet from Kanya and during the end of the staff credits of ''Rayman in the Phantom Show'' Edge can be seen genuinely smiling at Rabbid Mario when he shows everyone the gift Rayman gave him during his adventures alongside Rabbid Peach, which implies that she might have feelings for Rabbid Mario, though this remains ambiguous. 

==='''[[Mario]]'''===
Edge has only a few interactions with Mario, and she appears to have the most respect for the plumber and it is shown just before the ''Courageous Duo'' when she insists on taking out a Darkmess Puddle in Barrendale Mesa and she and Mario have it covered, which Beep-0 comments that the two of them complement each other due to their courage and refusal to back down from any challenge. Mario is shocked to learn about Edge having once been a Spark Hunter, but nonetheless still accepts her due to her proving herself as a hero he sees her as. 

==='''[[Rabbid Luigi]]'''===
While the two of them do not have very many interactions, Rabbid Luigi immediately grew to respect Edge when he watched enthusiastically as she defeats the Stooges that were attacking Twinkle. Unlike Rabbid Mario, Rabbid Luigi's friendship with Edge is more platonic and they seem to get along just fine and Rabbid Luigi, alongside the heroes, refuse to cast her out upon learning who she originally was and assure her that she is still their friend. 

==='''[[Luigi]]'''===
Edge and Luigi don't have very many direct dialogues exchanged between them. Luigi is amazed and nervous when he first meets Edge and he was willing to let her join their crusade to stop Cursa by any means possible and seems intrigued by her familiarity with the Spark Hunters, which he later becomes surprised upon learning that she was one before she left, and he seems to like her. During the very beginning of ''The Last Spark Hunter'', Edge and Luigi, alongside Princess Peach, are seen interacting with each other. 

==='''[[Rabbid Peach]]'''===
Edge's first impression has resulted in Rabbid Peach being so shocked and envious that she ends up dropping her smartphone, and glares at Edge as Twinkle approached her, though she does not show as much animosity towards her as she once did Princess Peach previously and is willing to tolerate her presence. Even though their interactions were limited, they are comfortable enough around each other, and they are both unimpressed with Rabbid Mario's snow display to them and she is also seen glaring at Beep-0 when he gets too suspicious of Edge, who has clearly agitated her. Even when she learns that Edge was once the leader of the Spark Hunters, she helps comfort Edge that they still have their trust and accepts her as part of their family. 

==='''[[Princess Peach]]'''===
Edge and Princess Peach don't interact very much despite them being on the same team, and Peach is shown to be somewhat nervous around her upon their first meeting, though through various battles, they become more comfortable around each other and both of them are happy to fight alongside each other. At the very beginning of ''The Last Spark Hunter'', Edge can be seen interacting with Peach and Luigi. Like the rest of the heroes, the princess is accepting towards Edge and her being at the Mushroom Kingdom indicates that she is hospitable towards her and lets her become an official resident in her kingdom. 

==='''[[Rabbid Rosalina]]'''===
Edge and Rabbid Rosalina very rarely interact with each other, but Edge is shown to be very protective of her when she tells Midnite to release her from captivity and goes an extra mile to ensure Rabbid Rosalina's safety as well as Captain Orion. They do appear to work together just fine. 

==='''[[Rosalina]]'''===
Edge seems to be concerned for Rosalina's fate when it was found that she was possessed by Cursa the entire time and when they do finally meet each other after setting her free, Edge is shown to be very respectful towards her and tells her that her old friends are seeking a different profession before deciding to stay with her new friends to see if she can't keep air in their lungs.

==='''[[Beep-0]]'''===
Out of all of her allies, Beep-0 is one of the few allies whom she conflicts with the most. Even as Beep-0 tolerates her, he steadily grew suspicious of her as the mystery of her relationship with the Spark Hunters unravels while also being somewhat afraid of her when he stammers as he speaks with her in the beginning and after a tense confrontation and it's usually when she gets mad. She is often the person who corrects him once Beep-0 makes an unrealistic assumption about their enemies giving up and she is known to be irritated by him, with notable cases when he asks her if she knew a way to get through Cursa's barrier and awareness of her familiarity with Midnite and Bedrock, she became furious and tells him off for prying into her business. After fighting her Darkmess doppelganger, Beep-0 became concerned for her as she admits having once been a Spark Hunter and assures her that they have a hundred reasons to believe her. 

==='''[[JEANIE]]'''===
The interactions between Edge and JEANIE are extremely limited though according to JEANIE's Memory Log about her, she closely monitored Edge due to her mysterious nature and hasn't been able to determine if she can be trusted, with the only information available is that she wields the Flying Blade as it were a part of her. She does trust her, but due to her being new to emotions as an AI, she struggles if it was due to instinct, wishful thinking, hope or all three. After Edge finally came around to revealing her origins, she is the last person to comfort her that she is part of their family, and she doesn't have to keep secrets from them. 

==='''[[Twinkle]]'''===
With Edge's mission being to save the Sparks from Cursa and the Spark Hunters, she saved Twinkle from falling into the grasp of Cursa's minions by throwing the Flying Blade at one of them, before taking out the rest of them. Twinkle starts out terrified of her, when it screamed in fear when she glanced in its direction, fearing the worst. Edge shows herself to be understanding of what Twinkle went through when she smiles and stabs the blade into the floor instead and reaches out her hand to show that she is not a threat to it, which Twinkle cautiously approaches. When the Giant Wildclaw arrived, Twinkle hides behind Edge's back for protection, to convey its trust for her and has no problems being around her since then.

==Neutral==
==='''[[Bowser]]'''=== 
The relationship between Edge and Bowser is ambiguous as they barely interact with each other directly. Edge had referred to Bowser as a round fella when asked if they are friends with him during his fight with Bedrock and as she claims that she isn't a friend of the latter, Beep-0 lets her know that he isn't either due to his history with kidnapping Princess Peach and antagonizing Mario and the Mushroom Kingdom. In Terra Flora, Bowser and Edge share a bubble with each other with the latter glaring at the former and they each have Darkmess versions of themselves that they fight in Cursa's stronghold. In ''The Last Spark Hunter'', both of them were paired to handle a Darkmess Puddle and Bowser is happy to fight alongside her seeing that she is a capable fighter. At the end of the game with Cursa gone, Bowser ends his alliance with the heroes, though it has yet to be seen if their relationship was affected as a result. 

==='''[[Augie]]'''===
Despite their limited interactions, Augie is already fearing for the fate of Beacon Beach and his earlier encounter with the Spark Hunters that threatened him made him paranoid. After the Sunrise Temple is brought to the surface and the heroes enter it, Edge watches them and seeing that she was noticed by him, she shushes him and grins to intimidate him into keeping quiet before she enters, which makes Augie worried about the heroes. Once Twinkle is saved, Augie becomes scared of her and calls her a Spark Hunter which she corrects him and gives a proper introduction. 

==Enemies/Rivals==
==='''[[Cursa]]'''===
What is currently known is that Cursa is responsible for Edge's creation among the rest of the Spark Hunters, and she was the only one who was given an edge with enough free will in order to be a proper leader to the Spark Hunters, however that same free will given to her ended up being the basis of her betraying Cursa due to her seeing just how evil it was and grew to despise her creator for its how monstrous it was to the Sparks that it absorbed and ends up leaving it behind to save the Sparks and keep them out of its reach. Cursa is also shown to view Edge as a mere pawn who serves its ambitions and upon learning of her treason, it creates Kanya in her place and held an intense grudge against her and has no qualms with hurting or killing her when it came across her again. 

==='''[[Spark Hunters]]'''===
Having been the leader within their ranks, Edge's friendship with the Spark Hunters depends on how they got along before and after she left. After seeing Cursa for what it was, she tried to tell them about it, which they ended up turning against her. 

==='''[[Midnite]]'''=== 
She has the most friendship with Midnite as shown when they had a brief friendly interaction between each other, before Edge made it clear that she won't rejoin Cursa's forces which they get into a heated argument. After Midnite's defeat, despite Edge having Midnite at her mercy, she chose to spare her life to show that she still cares for her despite putting through much pain, which Midnite becomes remorseful before leaving. 

==='''[[Bedrock]]'''===
Edge's relationship with Bedrock is somewhat vague due to their more limited interactions, other than that both of them do know each other very well. Bedrock did not take Edge's treachery very well when she left the Spark Hunters and is shocked to see Edge within Mario's ranks during her battle with Bowser which got her distracted long enough for Bowser to strike her. After she was defeated, she glares at Edge as she was escaping but turns somewhat remorseful when she does not return the same angry look.

==='''[[Daphne]]'''===
Out of all the Spark Hunters, it was pretty clear that Daphne is the only Spark Hunter whom Edge is the most stressed and anxious about as she knows that she is one of the most devious tricksters within the Spark Hunter ranks and as Daphne is very sadistic, she is the only one who can provoke Edge to anger the most. It was pretty clear that both of them do not get along.

==='''[[Kanya]]'''===
Due to Edge already having left the Spark Hunters, Kanya was created to take her place which left Edge with no knowledge of her existence. As a result, Edge becomes shocked and confused when Allegra, the Melodic Gardens warden, informs the heroes about Kanya, as she tells her that they defeated all of the Spark Hunters and is unable to comprehend the existence of another Spark Hunter. When Edge does meet Kanya, it was clear that both sides do not like each other at all and neither side have any existing friendship between them, and Edge is later seen being sympathetic to the Allegra's friend's plight and also exhibiting strong silent anger that Kanya would stoop so low to trap the innocent Dorrie. Once Kanya is defeated and blown off the planet, Edge isn't seen showing any pity for her, which is more due to her not having any emotional connection with her at all.
