The '''''Mario Artist'' series''' is a series of eight ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-themed creativity games for the [[Nintendo 64DD]], released exclusively in Japan. Because the Nintendo 64DD was a commercial failure, only four of these games were released whereas the other four were canceled. The first installment, ''[[Mario Artist: Paint Studio]]'', is a successor to ''[[Mario Paint]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]].
