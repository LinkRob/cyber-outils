|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Peepa's Fog Bog''' is a level of [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The level starts out with a puzzle themed area. You have to use the correct doors to get to certain areas. You also can find Snow Pokeies and Peepas. The next area is a river ride area, followed by a platform that will rise and lower into fog, making it a bit hard to see. The final area is similar to the first, except there is nothing you need to do here except get to the flagpole.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Near the top left corner of the first room is Captain Toad. Use a star (can be found in a grave inthe right corner of the room) to kill the Peepas surrounding him to earn the Green Star.
*'''Green Star 2''': Inside the real mystery house box after the Checkpoint. The Green Star disappears if a player is standing in the snow floor, but reappears if they walk back to the starting area. It's obtained by grabbing a snowball and throwing it to the Green Star while standing on the area which doesn't have snow floor.
*'''Green Star 3''': Near the fake mystery house box after the Checkpoint.
