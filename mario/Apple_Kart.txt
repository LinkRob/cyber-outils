The '''Apple Kart''' is a [[kart]] appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The kart takes its design of an [[Wikipedia:Apple|apple]]. The driver's seat is designed after the inside core of an apple, and the taillights are modelled after stars. Its worth noting that the kart's name is a pun on "apple tart". The kart uses the [[Wood]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

The Apple Kart has many different variations based of other types of fruits, such as the [[Green Apple Kart]], the [[Posion Apple Kart]], the [[Pumpkin Kart]], the [[Gold Pumpkin Kart]], and the [[Watermelon Kart]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The Apple Kart made its debut into the game during the [[Valentine's Tour]]. It is known for being the signature kart to [[List of characters in Mario Kart Tour/Baby Peach|Baby Peach (Cherub)]]. This kart increases the duration of [[Tricks|Jump Boosts]] and the points earned for performing them.
