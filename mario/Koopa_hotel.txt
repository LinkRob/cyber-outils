The '''Koopa hotels''', or simply '''hotels''', are a series of locations in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''.

== Description==
The Koopa hotels are a series of seven hotels found in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. Each of them are owned by a different [[Koopalings|Koopaling]] except for the last one, which is [[Bowser]]'s. [[Iggy Koopa]] is the only Koopaling to not own a hotel, and rather appears as a mini-boss in Bowser's.

==List of hotels==
*[[Morton's Wood Door Hysteria Hotel]] 
*[[Roy's HardBrick Hotel]]
*[[Larry's Chillton Hotel]] 
*[[Lemmy's High-ate Regency Hotel]] 
*[[Ludwig's Thump Castle Hotel]]
*[[Wendy's Blitz Snarlton Hotel]] 
*[[Bowser's Seizures Palace Hotel]]

==In ''Hotel Mario''==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] make their way through each of the hotels on a journey to rescue [[Princess Peach|Toadstool]].
