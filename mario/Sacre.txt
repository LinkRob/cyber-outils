'''Sacre''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==History==
Sacre is an artist that lives in [[Flipside]] with his daughter [[Ellie (Super Paper Mario)|Ellie]]. Sacre was once very daring but decided to take an easier route in painting. However, he has not been too successful in his art.
==Trivia==
*Sacre's [[Flopside]] counterpart is [[Bleu]]. Together, their names form the phrase "''Sacre bleu!''"
