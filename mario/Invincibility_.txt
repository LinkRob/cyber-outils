'''Invincibility!''' is the first level of the [[Tree Zone]] in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Layout==
[[File:Ragumo SML2 screenshot.png|thumb|left|A Ragumo in the hollow portion of the tree trunk]]
[[Mario]] begins in a flat area without any enemies. Soon after, he comes across what appears to be the roots of a tree trunk. Mario can travel over the trunk, where he encounters [[Bopping Toady|Bopping Toadies]], [[Spikey (Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins)|Spikeys]], and [[Kyotonbo]], or he can go inside the hollow area, where there are a few Bopping Toadies and a few [[Ragumo]]. Several holes are on the surface of the tree trunk, specifically twenty-six, and jumping down any of them leads into the hollow portion of the trunk.

Above the fourth and seventh holes are a few [[Hidden Block]]s that lead Mario up to an [[Empty Block]] with a [[1-Up Heart]] on it. A few Hidden Blocks are also above the nineteenth hole, but these lead up to a platform of Empty Blocks with a [[Coin]] stationed both on top and between each one. After this is a series of coins arranged in a shape of a down arrow, indicating that Mario to jump down. On the ground, right before the third Hidden Block is a Spikey and a [[Starman]] placed on two Empty Blocks. The [[Mid-Point Bell]] is in the middle of the fourteenth hole, and between it and the nineteenth hole is a large space inside the trunk with a row of 12 [[? Block]]s, one of which has either a [[Super Mushroom]] or a [[Fire Flower]], depending on Mario's form.

Starting from the nineteenth hole, the space inside the log narrows, and a few of the following enemies consist of Bopping Toadies and a Kyotonbo at the very end. Right after the twenty-sixth hole, and therefore the end of the tree trunk, is the end of the level, but Mario first has to jump out of the hollow area of the trunk in order to reach the end. Mario can optionally reach the [[Bonus Bell]] by either jumping to it from three [[Brick Block]]s or by jumping from two Hidden Blocks placed right against the tree, and the upper Hidden Block contains a 1-Up Heart. At the bottom of the tree is an opening that serves as the level exit, and it directly leads into the next level, [[In the Trees]].
