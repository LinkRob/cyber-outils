'''Walrusses''' are a type of [[Walrus]] found in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. They are rare giant-sized Walruses that are only found in [[Gleam Glacier]]. They are also huge, strong and can make "surprises", the Walrusses are usually dwelling below the snowy/icy ground but as soon as [[Peach]] approaches, they poke their heads from the ground, burst from the ground with a very high leap and start endlessly flailing their arms around upon landing while remaining in place. Walrusses will damage Peach upon contact, while extremely tough-looking and immune to jumps, Walrusses are not indestructible and Peach can easily beat one by whacking it with [[Perry]] three times or running through it while [[Rage|enraged]], the use of [[Chargebrella]] can also be used to bring a Walruss down. Upon defeat, they appear to shrink and flip away. Peach can also jump over a Walruss too in which case, the Walruss will turn around and flail at her from the other side. A sleeping Walruss can also be seen on the second-half of Gleam Glacier's map, this Walruss however appears to be harmless.

==Trivia==
*It's name is a pun on the animal it is, "Walrus" and "Russ" (the name).
*Walrusses are the biggest normal-enemy in the game which explains why they appear on the same page as the bosses in the Glossary.
*The Walrusses facial expressions are similar to the [[Broozer]]'s from ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', additionally, their arm-flailing tactics are similar to the Broozer's method of attack however the difference is the Walruss doesn't move as it flails its arms unlike the Broozer.
*The Walrusses could be related to the [[Fliprus]]es from the later games.
*According to the game's glossary, they spend time on the ice eating Snow Cones.
