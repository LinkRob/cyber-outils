{{nihongo|'''Rozary'''|ロザリィ|Rozarii}} is a female golfer who made her only appearance in the Japan-exclusive game ''[[Mobile Golf]]''.

==Profile==
According to the ''Mobile Golf'' website, Rozary is of Hispanic origin. Unlike most ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters, Rozary's name is of religious origin, as it is a pun on the {{w|rosary}}, a set prayers dedicated to {{w|Mary, mother of Jesus|Mary}} in {{w|Catholicism}}, a major religion amongst Hispanic people in real life.

Aside from golfing, some of Rozary's other interests include music and dancing, giving her a sense of rhythm.
