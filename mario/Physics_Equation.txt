The '''Physics Equation''' is an item in ''[[Mario's Time Machine (Nintendo Entertainment System)|Mario's Time Machine]]'' for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]].

==Description==
It is a scrap of paper that holds [[Albert Einstein]]'s famous formula for {{w|mass–energy equivalence}}, ''E=mc<sup>2</sup>''.

==In ''Mario's Time Machine'' (Nintendo Entertainment System)==
The Physics Equation is stolen by [[Bowser]] when he time-travels back to 1905. The Physics Equation can be found in [[Bowser's Museum]] with some other stolen historical artifacts.

Later on, after [[Mario]] defeats the [[Koopa Troopa|Koopas]] guarding the Physics Equation, he has to time-travel with it to 1905 and return it to Albert Einstein.

In the news article following Mario's return of the Physics Equation, there is a tongue-in-cheek statement saying that physics students will have another reason to pull their hair out.
