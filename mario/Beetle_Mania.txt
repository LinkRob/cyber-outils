'''Beetle Mania''' is an optional minigame in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a shooter in which a [[beetle (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|beetle]] shoots stars at [[Red Shell|red Koopa shells]].

==Layout==
The minigame utilizes two layouts, one of which is based on the [[level]] [[Yoshi's Island 2]] from ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and another of which depicts a dark green sky with smiling clouds on the upper half. The Yoshi's Island 2 background is shown if either [[Bowser]] or [[Mallow]] are in the middle slot, while the dark green sky background is shown if either [[Geno]] or [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] are in the middle slot.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
The minigame can be purchased from the [[Beetle Mania Toad]] at the [[Toad Town|Mushroom Kingdom]] [[inn]] for 500 [[Coin]]s. [[Mario]] has to talk to the Beetle Mania Toad a certain number of times before he is asked if he wishes to buy the minigame. Saying "yes" adds "game" to the 'X' menu.

==Etymology==
The minigame's name references {{w|Beatlemania}}, a pop culture trend revolving around {{w|The Beatles}}.
