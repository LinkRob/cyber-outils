'''Pokey Corral''', titled '''Pokey Peril''' in European and Australian releases, is a General minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Introduction==
A [[Pokey]] head emerges from the ground as the players jump into the arena. They run into the center near the Pokey head and the minigame begins.

==Gameplay==
The characters must avoid the Pokeys as they appear from the ground. If a character touches a Pokey, they fall off the arena and are eliminated from the minigame. Whoever is the last character standing wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|3ds|Stick}}: Move

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules (American English)''': ''Avoid the Pokeys, and keep your footing on the moving sand. Last player standing wins.''
*'''Rules (British English)''': ''Try not to fall off the pillar, and be the last one standing!''

==Trivia==
*The American English name is a reference to the {{w|O.K. Corral (building)|O.K. Corral}}, a building in {{w|Arizona}}. Because the location is relatively unknown outside of America, this may be why European and Australian versions of the game use a different name.
