'''Port Prisma''' is the first location, and is the hub, of ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. It is a ransacked village destroyed by [[Koopa Troopa|Koopas]] and [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]]. It is home to the [[Prisma Fountain]] with missing spots for the corresponding [[Big Paint Star|Big Paint Stars]]. As the player progresses through the game, the fountain can be fully restored, and allows access to the game's final boss. The port is divided into a harbor and 3 colored districts. There are two [[File:PMCS Red Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|27x27px]]  [[File:PMCS Blue Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|27x27px]] [[Mini Paint Star|Mini-Paint Stars]] in this level.

== Description ==
The story begins when [[Mario]] and [[Princess Peach]] are summoned to Port Prisma after a mysterious and alarming letter arrives. The letter is a colorless toad, with the stamp revealing that the Prisma Fountain has been drained of color, and the once-thriving town of Port Prisma has begun to fade into dullness. The colors that were once so central to the town’s vitality have disappeared, leaving the town in a sad and monochrome state.

After the opening cutscenes are played, [[Mario]] arrives at The Harbor District. Leaving the ship and heading to the right reveals a [[hammer]] on some boxes. Obtaining the [[hammer]] gives [[Mario]] a unique mechanic that allows crates to be destroyed and turned into [[File:PMCS Coin.png|frameless|21x21px]] [[Coin|coins]]. Hitting the crate that blocks the stairs reveals the terrifying ruins of a once happy town. After viewing the ghosttown, the Emercency Recovery Unit (ERU) appears in the [[Prisma Fountain]]. After following the instructions given, a paint can is revealed from below. Hitting it with a hammer reveals a living paint can named Huey.

The early stages of the adventure involve Mario and Huey teaming up with the variety of quirky characters of Port Prisma to investigate the source of the color drain and find out who is behind it. The game introduces new mechanics through the use of color, with Mario utilizing his paint hammer to restore color to objects and Toads that have lost their hues. This color-based mechanic becomes the central gameplay mechanic of the rest of the game, but introducing it quite well in this first level. Port Prisma is not just a starting point, but a vital chapter in ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'', serving as the beginning of Mario and Huey’s journey to save the entire island from the strange, color-draining forces.

==Locations==
===The Red District===
[[File:PMCS Port Prisma Empty Prisma Fountain.png|border|frameless|457x457px|right]]This district is the central area of Port Prisma. [[Super Flag|Super Flags]] signify every completed achievement, and celebrations are held here when [[Big Paint Star|Big Paint Stars]] are returned.
====The Prisma Fountain====
The [[Prisma Fountain]] resides in the middle, which holds [[Huey]] in the beginning of the game. This fountain houses all 6 [[Big Paint Star|Big Paint Stars]] of every color. Once a Big Paint Star is recovered, you can restore your [[HP]] in the fountain everytime you visit '''Port Prisma'''. Hitting specific [[Big Paint Star|Big Paint Stars]] will grant that color of [[paint]] infinitely.
[[File:PMCS Port Prisma Harbor District.png|border|frameless|400x400px|center]]

=== The Harbor District ===
The Harbor is the first playable location in the game, and where Mario gets his <u>[[Paint Hammer]]</u>. As the game progresses, we are introduced to [[Know-it-All Toad]] and [[Wringer]].
[[File:PMCS Port Prisma Yellow District.png|border|center|frameless|420x420px]]

===The Yellow District===
This district is focused on housing and fighting. Most [[Toad (character)|toad's]] live in this district, and also where [[Princess Peach]] is temporarily living in. This is also where [[Princess Peach|Princess Peach's]] assistant [[Toad (character)|toad]] gets their color slurped from a [[Slurp Guy]], commencing the first battle in the game.[[File:PMCS Dojo - Action Toad.png|alt=Action Toad.png|border|left|frameless|81x81px]]
====The Dojo====
The Dojo is located in the Yellow District, and is later the entrance into the [[Prisma Museum]]. [[Action Toad|Action toad]] is the inhabitant of the Dojo. He incourages you to train your attacks, such as [[Jump (PMCS)|Jump]], [[Block (PMCS)|Block]], [[Hammer (PMCS)|Hammer]], and [[Fire Flower (PMCS)|Fire Flower]].
[[File:PMCS Prisma Museum.png|border|left|frameless|144x144px]]

====Prisma Museum====
In the Dojo, theres a secret museum where you can donate basic, thing, and enemy [[Battle Card|battle cards]]. When you donate a certain amount of cards, you will be able to see old concept arts for the game.
[[File:PMCS Rescue Squad Chief.png|border|left|frameless|105x105px]]

==== Rescue Squad Area ====
This is an recovery area for lost [[Rescue Squad]] members. The Rescue Toads are divided into 5 squads with a corresponding color (Blue, Red, Yellow, Green, and Purple). These toads are lost, and will report to the [[Rescue Squad Chief]] in this area. The chief will count how many are still missing for a particular group. If a [[Rescue Squad Captain]] is found, all [[Rescue Squad]] toads will report to his locations, even if there are still some missing.
[[File:PMCS Port Prisma Piper tree flowers.png|border|left|frameless|235x235px]]

==== The Meeting Spot====
On the highest spot of the district, there is a meeting place for an old friend group. As the game progresses, more is revealed about this sidequest, including [[Piper|Piper's]] backstory with his 5 [[Toad (character)|toad]] friends.  
 
[[File:PMCS Port Prisma waterwheel.png|border|center|frameless|420x420px]]

===The Blue District===
This district has more buy and sell places located here. It is also home to the waterwheel, and upon being fixed grants access to the first [[File:PMCS Red Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|20x20px]] [[Mini Paint Star]].

==== Prisma Cardware ====
[[File:PMCS Prisma Cardware.png|center|frameless|420x420px]]
Prisma Cardware, also known as "Card Shop", is a shop to buy [[Battle Card|Battle Cards]] using [[Coin|coins]]. This shop is unlocked after Mario repaints the shop owner. As [[Battle Card|Battle Cards]] are unlocked, they will appear as purchasable items.

==== Prisma Postal ====
[[File:PMCS Prisma Postal Toad.png|center|frameless|420x420px]]
[[Prisma Postal]] is located across the bridge of this district. Mario will receive letters throughout the game and can be read in here. This is shown as an icon on the map, as well as a shimmer on the envelope in front of the building.

==== Port Prisma Café ====
[[File:PMCS Prisma Cafe menu.png|center|frameless|420x420px]]
The [[Prisma Café|Port Prisma Café]] is located to the far left of the district. At first, the [[Prisma Café Toad]] is seen sad, not wanting to work where its too busy. After returning to [[Ruddy Road]] and talking to the [[Ruddy Road Café Toad]], they both switch locations, unlocking the cafe in this district. Here, you can purchase a [[Hearty Latte]] for 70 coins and [[Prisma Juice]] for 120 coins.

== Walkthrough #1 - [[File:PMCS Red Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|38x38px]] [[Mini Paint Star|Mini-Paint Star]] ==
After progressing through the Yellow District and wringing out Huey, the stairs will be destroyed and become unusable. Continuing into the Blue District, you'll need to color more colorless Toads. Once the green gate is colored and opened, you'll encounter a Goomba standing behind it. After defeating the Goomba, head up the stairs and make your way to the right side of the map. After a few more battles and restoring more colorless Toads, you'll face off against a Slurp Guy, whose defeat reveals the solution to the watermill problem. Once Mario defeats the Slurp Guy and uses the Paint Hammer on the waterfall, the water will flow, carrying Mario down the stream. With the waterfall restored, the toilet paper barrier will unravel, unlocking a staircase leading to the first [[File:PMCS Red Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|27x27px]] [[Mini Paint Star|Mini-Paint Star]]. Collecting the [[File:PMCS Red Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|27x27px]] [[Mini Paint Star|Mini-Paint Star]] will clear the level.

== Walkthrough #2 - [[File:PMCS Blue Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|38x38px]] [[Mini Paint Star|Mini-Paint Star]] ==
Upon entering Crimson Tower, recoloring the Toad reveals that he is the bridge builder and can repair the bridge in Port Prisma. After returning to Port Prisma and heading to the Blue District, the bridge will be fixed, unlocking new areas to recolor. In this newly accessible area, you'll find the [[File:PMCS Blue Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|27x27px]] [[Mini Paint Star|Mini-Paint Star]] just out of reach on the roof. After restoring a few more spots and speaking with the Port Postal Toad, he will fix the sign above his post office. Next, stand to the right of the red Toad near the bridge and use the Cutout mechanic. Doing this will reveal the cutout section needed to progress. Once you reach the roof, the Card Connoisseur Toad will appear and ask to inspect your cards. While this is happening, a battle will begin, and you’ll learn the Battle Spin mechanic. After receiving your cards back, you can color a few more spots and finally obtain the [[File:PMCS Blue Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|27x27px]] [[Mini Paint Star|Mini-Paint Star]]. Collecting the [[File:PMCS Blue Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|27x27px]] [[Mini Paint Star|Mini-Paint Star]] will clear the level.
