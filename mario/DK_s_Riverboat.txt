'''DK's Riverboat''' is a vehicle in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

== Description ==
DK's Riverboat is a wooden boat used by [[Donkey Kong]] exclusively in [[Castaway Bay]]. It is depicted with a yellow star emblem incidentally resembling the ones on [[Diddy Kong]]'s shirt.

== In ''Mario Party 6'' ==
A playable character can land on a riverboat for Donkey Kong to give them a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] for 20 [[coin]]s. Whether the character accepts or declines, Donkey Kong then takes them to the [[Start Space]]. If a character lands on a [[Event Space|? Space]], the riverboat switches places with [[Bowser's Battle Yacht]].
