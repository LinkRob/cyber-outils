A '''Poundette''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Poundettes are hammer-like enemies that work for the [[Smithy Gang]] in [[The Factory]], and they are also assistants for the [[Director]]. Poundettes are a stronger variant of [[Pounder]]s, which they resemble, albeit with a pink body.

==Battle==
Like with Pounders, Poundettes mainly attack by pounding themselves at one of [[Mario]]'s party. They are also able to toss hammers and one attack may even inflict the [[Fear]] status on one of Mario's party members.

===Strategy===
Poundettes are weak to thunder attacks as well meaning [[Mallow]]'s thunder techniques are effective against them.
