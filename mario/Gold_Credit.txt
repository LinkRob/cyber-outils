The '''Gold Credit''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Gold Credit is given to [[Mario]] as gift from [[Koopa Koot]] after completing his favor that involves getting him a [[Life Shroom]]. The Gold Credit can be used in the [[Playroom]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] to play the [[Smash Attack]] game. The Gold Credit is similar to the [[Silver Credit]] which was already obtained earlier.
