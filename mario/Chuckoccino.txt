The '''Chuckoccino''' is a type of blend in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Usage==
The blend can be obtained in the [[Starbeans Cafe]]. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must have 15 [[Woo Bean]]s and 10 [[Chuckle Bean]]s for the [[Starbeans Employee]] to create it. Chuckoccino permanently increases Mario or Luigi's [[Defense]] points by 4 (2 in the remake).

==Trivia==
*The name is a portmanteau of "chuckle" and "cappuccino".
