'''Stump Petuni''' is a minigame which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Stump Petuni becomes available after chapter two and when Mario has saved the The Great Tree. The minigame involves [[Mario]] asking Petuni questions, which is done by selecting four words, each from an option of three words, and the fourth and final word ends with a question mark. If Mario asks Petuni the question, "Mario likes which person the most?" Petuni should answer "[[Princess Peach]]" and gives Mario a [[Mystic Egg]]. If Mario asks her any other question, she will not know the answer, mainly because they are nonsensical.
