'''Spike Eels''' (also called '''Purple Eels''') are gigantic, lazy-eyed eels that are encountered in the underwater areas of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' such as [[World 3-2 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 3-2]] and [[World 7-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 7-1]]. Despite their huge size, Spike Eels are not the most dangerous enemies in the game, and they can be killed by hitting it fifteen times with a [[boomerang]] (as [[Boomerang Mario]]) or [[fireball]]s (as [[Fire Mario]]). A Spike Eel can be instantly defeated by slamming it from above with [[Statue Mario]] or from using a [[Starman]] or [[Invincibility Leaf]].

==Trivia==
*The Spike Eel is the third major Eel species to be introduced in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, the first was the [[Unagi]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and the second was the [[Gringill]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', the fourth would be the [[Maw-Ray]] which made its debut in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', Maw-Rays resemble Unagis but behave very similarly to both Gringills and Spike Eels.
