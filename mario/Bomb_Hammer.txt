'''Bomb Hammers''' are [[Luigi's Mansion 3#Rare Ghosts|rare ghost]]s that only appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. They are [[Hammer (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Hammers]] which are glowing yellow. They can attack by spawning bombs; they also send out a burst of bombs when Luigi or Gooigi slams them to the ground. Bomb Hammers can't be found in [[The Last Resort]], they are only located in the [[ScareScraper]] (ten floors mode). They have 350HP.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Bomb Hammers are a form of Hammers, but instead of being red and having white eyes, they are glowing yellow and have blue eyes.
===Behavior===
Bomb Hammers won't always appear on the fifth floor of the ScareScraper in the ten floors mod; they appear randomly. To capture them, [[Luigi]] has to suck them up in the way he sucks up Hammers. Flash them and stun them first, and suck them from the back. Bomb Hammers will spawn bombs that Luigi has to avoid, be careful when slamming them because the bomb Hammers will send out a burst of bombs when slammed.
