|caption   = [[Mario]] deep in Shy Guy Jungle.}}'''Shy Guy Jungle''' is the first level of World 5 in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Layout==
Shy Guy Jungle is an very dense jungle surrounding with many vegetation. It is mainly green and brown in color.

==History==
After getting the fourth [[Royal Sticker]], [[Mario]] travels far into a dense jungle to begin his search for another one. This jungle has many Spear Guys attempting to slow Mario down. Eventually, Mario encounters three Spear Guys holding Bridge Part scraps which Mario has to chase down and defeat them. After more exploring of the Shy Guy Jungle, Mario finds the Comet Piece and completes the level.

==Notes==
Mario must have the Goat Thing Sticker in order to complete this level. This sticker can be obtained in the level [[Snow Rise]] after watching [[Birdo]]'s song. Mario can also use the Vacuum or the Upright Vacuum.
