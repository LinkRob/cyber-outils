A '''Chomp Call''' is a recurring item in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Description==
The Chomp Call is a [[Chain Chomp]]-shaped whistle that calls four small Chain Chomps to surround the host and [[Star Exchange|Star Space]] of the [[board]]. It is similar to the [[Lucky Lamp]] from ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. In ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'', the Chomp Whistle becomes flat on both sides and is colored navy blue instead of black.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party 4''===
In ''Mario Party 4'', when using a Chomp Call, four small [[Chain Chomp]]s are called around the Star Space. The Chain Chomps scare each other, frightening the Star in the process and causing it to move. A Chomp Call costs 15 [[coin]]s to purchase at an [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shop]].

===''Mario Party 5''===
In ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', Chomp Calls are not board items and are used only in the minigame [[Chomp Romp]]. Here, players must use Chomp Calls to lure a Chain Chomp from the top of a playground to the bottom, attempting to reach the end before their opponents.

===''Mario Party: The Top 100''===
The Chomp Call is reintroduced as an item in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'', where it functions identically as in ''Mario Party 4''.

===''Mario Party Superstars''===
In ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', the Chomp Call returns as an item. It appears in every board except [[Peach's Birthday Cake]], as the Star is always in a fixed location. It also costs only 7 coins in every board except [[Woody Woods]] Forest Shop, where it is six coins instead.

===''Super Mario Party Jamboree''===
In ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'', the Chomp Call is an item on every board except [[Mario's Rainbow Castle]], as it also features the Star in a fixed location. The Chomp Call now costs 6 coins.

While playing with Pro Rules, a player can select a Chomp Call as their starting item of choice, unless they are playing on Mario's Rainbow Castle.
