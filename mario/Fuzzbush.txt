The '''Fuzzbush''' is an enemy found in [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

== Battle ==
Fuzzbushes accompany [[Chuck Guy]]s during the battle. It comes close to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]; they attack with [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] that are either going to Mario or Luigi. Mario and Luigi must dodge the Fuzzies by jumping and they cannot counterattack them. While Luigi was battling by himself, the Fuzzbushes still thrust Fuzzies, although, Mario was absent in battle.

=== Strategy ===
Fuzzbushes are weak to Mario's [[Firebrand]] attack. It takes regular damage with other attacks.
