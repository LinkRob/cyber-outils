'''Sandy Shine Sprite''', alternatively titled '''Buried Treasure!''', is the bonus tenth episode of [[Gelato Beach]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Layout==
Sandy Shine Sprite is one of two extra [[Shine Sprite]]s of Gelato Beach that can be obtained during any episode, the other one being the 100-[[Coin]] Shine Sprite. As such, because Sandy Shine Sprite is a bonus episode, it does not affect the layout.

==Objective==
[[Mario]] has to spray a [[Dune Bud]] next to the [[springboard]] behind the tower, to reveal an opening in the sand. Mario is given a limited time to go down a sandy staircase leading to some Coins and spray the wall to reveal a [[Shine Painting]]. The Shine Painting gradually reveals itself upon being sprayed, and once the Shine Painting is fully revealed, a Shine Sprite appears for Mario to collect.
