'''Smilaxs''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
Smilax look extremely similar to smaller [[Piranha Plant]]s.

== Battle ==
Smilaxs only appear in the fight with [[Megasmilax]]. They mainly attack by biting a member of [[Mario]]'s team. Mario and his team must also fight a group of Smilaxs as a [[Shy Away]] keeps watering them until the Megasmilax grows. Even after the Megasmilax is defeated, the Smilaxs must be defeated as well in order to end the fight.

=== Tips ===
Like with the Megasmilax, Smilaxs have a weakness to [[Ice (element)|Ice]], so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] move will be very effective against them.
