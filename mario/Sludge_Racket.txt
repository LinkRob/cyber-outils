The '''Sludge Racket''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] used by [[Petey Piranha]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
When performing the move, Petey swallows his tennis racket, hits himself in the stomach, and launches a [[Goop|sludge]]-covered racket at the ball to send it back to the receiving end. If any receiver gets caught within the sludge, they are slowed down.
