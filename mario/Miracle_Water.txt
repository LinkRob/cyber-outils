The '''Miracle Water''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Miracle Water can only be obtained once [[Mario]] retrieves the [[Water Stone]] from [[Rosie]] and gives it to [[Lily (Paper Mario)|Lily]]. The Miracle Water is the last of three items needed to access [[Cloudy Climb]] along with the [[Magical Bean]] and the [[Fertile Soil]]. However, Mario and co. must now bring back the [[Sun (character)|Sun]] by destroying the [[Puff Puff Machine]] first. After that is done, Mario must then place the Magic Bean, Fertile Soil, and the Miracle Water on a dirt patch to have the large [[beanstalk]] grow, opening up access to Cloudy Climb to confront [[Huff N. Puff]].
