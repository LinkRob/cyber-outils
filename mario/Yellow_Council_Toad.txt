The '''Yellow Council Toads''' are minor characters in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. They are the [[Toad General]]'s supporting [[Toad Council]] memebers. One of them asked their princess what they're gonna do if the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] can't be destroyed, and she doesn't know how to stop Bowser, because the Toads are adorable. They were forced to evacuate with the others along with their general, since Bowser is on his way.
