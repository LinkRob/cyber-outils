'''Tutankhammer''' is a variant of [[Hammer (enemy)|Hammer]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. They are bandaged-like mummies. Tutankhammers will only appear on the [[Tomb Suite]]-themed floors on [[ScareScraper]]. They have 300 HP.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Tutankhammers' bodies are bandaged,  however, the rest of their body are same as normal Hammers.

=== Behavior ===
They attack, act, their voice, and the way they are vacuumed are in the same way as Hammers.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3'' ==
Tutankhammers usually are in rooms that have a secret door which can only be found by the [[Dark-Light Device]], There must be at least one [[Luigi]] that wears the mummy clothing, the players can capture Tutankhammers if they are found, but capturing them is not necessary. Only the player that captured them in their vacuum will get them in E Gadd's gallery.

==Trivia==
*Tutankhammers only appear after the update during 2020.
*The name "Tutankhammer" is based on a pharaoh called {{w|Tutankhamun}}.
