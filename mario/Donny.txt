'''Donny''' is a character in ''[[Unjust Desserts]]''.

==In ''Unjust Desserts''==
Donny is a [[Micro-Dolphin]] who lives in a pool of [[Koopa-Kola|Koopa Kola]] inside of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s stomach. He is the first Micro-Dolphin that [[Mario]] meets while he is shrunk down and wandering inside Yoshi's belly. Donny is amused at Mario when he tells Donny that the dolphins are living inside of Yoshi, and refuses to believe him. Nonetheless, Donny and the other Micro-Dolphins help Mario move across the Koopa Kola lake.
