A '''Spiky Parabuzzy''' is an enemy which first appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is a hybrid of a [[Parabuzzy]] and a [[Spike Top]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Spiky Parabuzzies are first encountered in [[Riverside Station]]. Due to them having spikes on their heads, they cannot be jumped on (unless [[Mario]] has the [[Spike Shield]] badge equipped). If Mario doesn't have the Spike Shield badge, then the best way to defeat Spiky Parabuzzies is either with the [[Hammer Throw]] badge or the special move [[Earth Tremor]]. Spiky Parabuzzies also appear in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] in the later floors. When jumped upon, Spiky Parabuzzies lose their wings and become Spike Tops.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
[[File:Spiky_Parabuzzy_Card.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Spiky Parabuzzies are encountered in the much later levels of [[Overthere Stair]] and [[Castle Bleck]] usually near a pit.

===''Super Mario Maker''===
Spiky Parabuzzies can be created in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|version]] by adding wings to a Spike Top. They will always fly in a straight line and jumping on them is impossible except without a [[Goomba's Shoe]] or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].
