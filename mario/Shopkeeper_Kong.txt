The '''Shopkeeper Kong''' is a minor character from ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' who gets one of his [[Banana|bananas]] stolen by [[Sports Coat Kong|the Driver]]. The Driver eats that banana, then throws it on the road, causing [[Swanky Kong (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Swanky Kong]] to crash.
