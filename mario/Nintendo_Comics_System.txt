'''''Nintendo Comics System''''' is a series of comic books published by [[Valiant Comics]] in 1990 and 1991. They were produced as a result of a licensing deal with [[Nintendo]]. The ''Nintendo Comics System'' stories featured a variety of Nintendo properties, such as ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', and ''[[Metroid]]''.

Valiant released most of their ''Mario'' comics as part of the [[Super Mario Bros. (comic series)|''Super Mario Bros.'' comic series]], which are based on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]]'s three ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' games: ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. Aside from the usual returning characters, the ''Super Mario Bros.'' comics also has some unique characters such as [[Stanley the Talking Fish]], [[Wooster]], and [[Dirk Drain-Head]].

The ''[[Game Boy (comic series)|Game Boy]]'' comic series supposedly focuses on the [[Game Boy]] in general, although it has only four issues, all specifically focused on ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' and featuring [[Tatanga]] as the main antagonist. 

In [[Sweden]], these comics were published in [[Nintendomagasinet]].
