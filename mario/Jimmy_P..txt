'''Jimmy P.''' is a character who first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Jimmy P. is based on [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]], whom he shares a first name with. He strongly resembles Jimmy T., but he has a yellow afro, a pink nose, a blue shirt, white pants, and tanned skin.

===Personality traits===
Unlike Jimmy T., Jimmy P. never goes to [[Club Sugar]], but instead goes into a similar club named [[Club Spice]].

==Biography==
Little is known about Jimmy P., and it is also unknown what his last name is short for.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''===
[[File:Legendary Dog Dancers and Jimmy P.png|thumb|200px|Jimmy P. dancing with the Legendary Dog Dancers in ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'']]
Jimmy P. has his own stage, titled [[Canine Crazed]], which is both parallel to and a harder version of Jimmy T.'s [[Feline Fever]] stage. In it Jimmy P. befriends the [[Legendary Dog Dancers]] while making his way to Club Spice, just as Jimmy T. did with the [[Legendary Cat Dancers]] on his way to Club Sugar.

===''[[WarioWare Gold]]''===
While Jimmy P. does not appear in the game, Jimmy T's character trailer shows a photo of him and Jimmy P. shaking hands, which suggests that they are friends.

==Trivia==
*Jimmy P. is the only character (not counting some of [[Wario]]'s alter-egos) who previously had his own stage in the {{series|WarioWare}} to neither return in ''WarioWare Gold'' nor ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.
