'''World 3-2''' is the second normal level in [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It is an underwater level in a beach area. This level can be accessed by completing [[World 3-1 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3-1]]. There is only one exit, which unlocks [[World 3-3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3-3]]. 
==Layout==
Mario begins in front of a pool of water where there is a small island in the middle of the water being circled by [[Snake Block]]s. A red Warp Pipe takes Mario underwater. A few ? Blocks can first be found occasionally blocked by Snake Blocks above a [[Whirlpool]] which will disappear after a short while and then reappear again, like in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. One of the ? Blocks contain a [[Fire Flower]]. Then, he will come across more Snake Blocks, [[Urchin]]s and [[Cheep Cheep]]s. After that, another set of Snake Blocks enclosing a Whirlpool with the first Star Coin can be found. This is followed by an [[! Pipe]]. More Snake Blocks and Cheep Cheeps can be found, along with [[Mini Urchin]]s, followed by the checkpoint. Another Fire Flower can be found in a ? Block after that. This is followed by more Cheep Cheeps and Snake Blocks. A [[P Switch]] appears on a Warp Pipe on the ceiling, which causes blue coins to appear in an area where Spiny Cheep Cheeps which follows Mario are swimming in. Following that are Snake Blocks where several Urchins are found. Not long after, the blue exit Warp Pipe can be found. Back in the overworld, a hidden block containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] can be found on a cliff to the left of Mario. Several Snake Blocks can be found circling several floating platforms above water, followed by the flagpole.
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is early in the level. The player may want to wait for the Whirlpool to disappear before grabbing it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the checkpoint, the player should enter the first yellow Warp Pipe which is enclosed in Snake Blocks and in between water currents. In the next area, the player should navigate around the Snake Blocks to reach the Star Coin located in the middle, where a [[Spiny Cheep Cheep]] which follows Mario will be guarding it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The last Star Coin is located in an area enclosed with Snake Blocks above an Urchin near the exit pipe.
