An '''Egg Bomb''' is a recurring item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It replaces the [[Egg Missile]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Egg Bomb.png|left|thumb]]
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', an Egg Bomb is obtainable from [[Zess T.]] who will cook this item up for if he gives her a Mystic Egg and a Fire Flower. [[Mystic Egg]]s are somewhat hard to obtain so it can tedious obtaining this item. Like the Egg Missile, this item inflicts six damage to a single enemy in a battle.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' adds a new way of using this item. It, unlike its original counterparts, attacks all enemies on the screen. This item is cooked in a similar fashion to its previous versions, only [[Fire Flower]] is replaced by [[Fire Burst]].
