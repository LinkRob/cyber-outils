|name=Spin Jump (Battle Card)}}'''Spin Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Spin Jump is the drill version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a red boot with gray trims and a drill underneath it. There is a red/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 10/30 coins

Sell Price: 5/15 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Spin Jump is first seen in [[Sunglow Ridge]], but can also found in [[Marmalade Valley]], [[Kiwano Temple]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Mustard Café]], and [[Redpepper Volcano]].
