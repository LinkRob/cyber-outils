'''Mario Bros.''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', based on the [[Mario Bros. (game)|game]] of the same name. It is one of [[5-Volt]]'s microgames.

== Gameplay ==
In this game, the player controls [[Mario]]. The objective here is to collect a [[coin]]. Sometimes, the player must collect it before [[Luigi]] does. There are three difficulty levels:
*'''Level 1 difficulty''' – There is no appearance of Luigi.
*'''Level 2 difficulty''' – Luigi will appear and move at a moderate speed.
*'''Level 3 difficulty''' – Luigi will appear once again, this time moving at a quicker pace.
