'''Bulrush Coming Through!''' is a course in [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']]. 

This level consists mainly of [[Bulrush]]es and [[Swirlypod]]s. There are also many trees and Hard Blocks scattered around the course. There is one [[Checkpoint Flag]], after which a few [[Trompette]]s appear.

The [[Wonder Flower]] is found after the Checkpoint Flag in some Hard Blocks. A Bulrush will break these blocks, releasing the Wonder Flower, summoning a herd of Bulrushes. After following the herd, they will knock down the Goal Pole, and make their way to the course's secret exit.

== [[10-Flower Coin|10-flower coin]] locations ==
The first coin can be found behind some blocks that are only breakable by [[Bulrush|Bulrushes]]. The player must lure a Bulrush to break said blocks to obtain the coin.

The second coin can be found in a sub-area accessed via [[Ground Pound|Ground Pounding]] a tree. After clearing the sub-area, the 10-flower coin can be found at the end.

The final coin, similar to the first coin, is found in a corner obstructed by blocks. The player must lure a Bulrush to break said blocks to obtain the coin.

== Trivia ==
There are 8 Bulrushes in the Bulrush herd during the Wonder Effect.
