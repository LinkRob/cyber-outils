A '''Mad Big Boo''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. 

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mad Big Boos are a variant of both [[Mad Boo]]s and [[Big Boo]]s alike, and like regular Big Boos, a Mad Big Boo is white, it has white teeth, a magenta tongue sticking out, and light red or light orange eyes surrounded by a light purple eyeshadow, similarly to [[Boolossus]] from ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''. A Mad Big Boo's eyes are the only way it differs in appearance from regular Big Boos.

===Behavior===
Mad Big Boos are imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]]. Mad Big Boos act the opposite of regular Big Boos in that whenever [[Princess Peach|Peach]] looks at a Mad Big Boo, it quickly moves toward her, but if she is not facing a Mad Big Boo, it does not move after her.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Mad Big Boos first appear in the level [[Shriek Mansion 3-5]]. Like with their regular counterpart, Mad Big Boos cannot be defeated from the Rage vibe.
