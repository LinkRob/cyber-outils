'''Piston Lifts''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
Piston Lifts are a pair of two thin [[Mushroom Platform]]s that act similarly to a balancing scale, or a [[Scale Lift]]. When [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] lands onto a Piston Lift, it sinks whilst its paired platform rises.
