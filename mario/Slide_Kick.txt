The '''Slide Kick''' is a move in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
The Slide Kick can be performed by [[Mario]] in ''Super Mario 64'', as well as [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], and [[Wario]] in ''Super Mario 64 DS''. To perform a Slide Kick, the character has to be [[dash|running]], and while the character does this, the player has to hold {{button|n64|Z}} and press {{button|n64|B}} in ''Super Mario 64'', or hold {{button|ds|R}} ({{button|ds|B}} in Dual-Hand Mode) and press {{button|ds|A}} in ''Super Mario 64 DS''. In doing so, the character does a short [[slide]] on the ground with his foot outstretched, although a Slide Kick can be performed for longer if done on a hill.

Most enemies can take damage upon being hit a Slide Attack. In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', if Yoshi uses a Slide Kick, enemies such as [[Boo]]s are unaffected by it, because Yoshi is weaker than the other playable characters.
