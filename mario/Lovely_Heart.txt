The '''Lovely Heart''' is an [[item]] which appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
The Lovely Heart allows [[Princess Peach]] to use her [[Star Skill]], [[Heart Ball]] in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]]. The Lovely Heart has the appearance of a red heart surrounded by a gold border. The Lovely Heart can be bought at the shop for 200 [[coin]]s if either Peach is the team captain or if she has been recruited into the player's team.
