'''Donkey Kong Jungle''' is a baseball stadium in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
Donkey Kong Jungle is the home stadium of [[Donkey Kong]]. The main hazards are the large [[barrel]]s shot out at the fielders from two [[Barrel Cannon]]s on each side of the back of Donkey Kong Jungle. If a barrel hits a character, they spin out for a few seconds. The only other obstacles are [[Klaptrap]]s, which appear in the river, slowing any fielders they bite.

The background features some objects from some earlier ''[[Donkey Kong Country (series)|Donkey Kong Country]]'' titles, including a [[millstone]] and a temple structure from ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' and [[Swanky's Sideshow]] tent from ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. There is also a large billboard depicting [[Swanky Kong]] in his original artwork for ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!''. There is a volcano in the background, not unlike but not the same as the volcano in [[DK Mountain]], but it is only part of the scenery.

==In ''Mario Superstar Baseball''==
During Challenge mode, if Donkey Kong's team, [[DK Kongs]], is not chosen by the player at the start, they are competed against in Donkey Kong Jungle. The stadium is also where the [[Barrel Batter]] minigame is hosted.
