'''Bangkok Rush 1''' is one of the three layouts of [[Bangkok Rush]], a race track in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. As the track's first layout, it made its debut appearance in the Bangkok Tour.

== Layout ==
The track's layout is concentrated to the southern half of the city, which includes the Chao Phraya River. Racers start this course at the Grand Palace beside a building with two elephant statues, with the Wat Arun visible ahead. After a gradual left turn, they travel across the Chao Phraya River (which is too shallow to trigger the [[Propeller (Mario Kart 7)|propeller]] unless the player triggers a glitch by turning right as they enter the racer). Rowboats appear with ramps on their front; the racers can use them to get [[Trick|Jump Boost]]s. After a sharp left turn, the racers will be facing the Democracy Monument. However, they must take a right just before they meet it onto a road through the Maeklong Railway Market. The canopies can be driven on. The racers make two close left turns, after which they encounter a staircase that acts as a ramp. They can choose to stay on the road or go up the stairs; if they do the latter, they will drive on the BTS Skytrain tracks. Regardless, they must make a left turn next to the giant Ganesha statue and the Phra Buddhasaiyas followed by a right turn. to get back to the [[Finish line|starting line]].

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the racers go through this route for the first lap of [[Bangkok Rush]].
