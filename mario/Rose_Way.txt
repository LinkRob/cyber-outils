|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Rose Way''' is a location leading from [[Tadpole Pond]] to [[Rose Town]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. This area is known for having many [[Treasure Box]]es around this area with five total, all guarded by [[Shy Guy]]s. Four of the boxes contain [[coin]]s while the final one contains a [[Mushroom]] that fully heals all of Mario's party's HP and FP. Many people visit here because of its value of finding money. [[Crook]]s, however, scare away people coming in this area.

After the venture throughout Rose Way by reaching the end of it, Mario and co. can see [[Bowser]] and his [[Koopa Troop]] doing a training camp.
