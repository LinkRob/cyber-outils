The '''Koopa Trace-Walking Host''' is a character from ''Super Mario Odyssey''. He is a Koopa Troopa who hosts the [[Koopa Trace-Walking]] minigame.

== Appearance ==
The Koopa Trace-Walking Host is a standard Koopa Troopa, although he has different shell colours and boot colours and clothing every time he appears. In the Sand Kingdom, the character has a red shell and red boots and on his head is a sombrero. When Mario meets him in the Snow Kingdom, the Koopa Troopa has a blue shell and boots and has his head wrapped in a snow hoodie. In the Moon Kingdom, the Koopa Trace-Walking Host has a purple shell and boots and on his head is an astronaut's helmet.

== Role ==
The Koopa Trace-Walking Host is the host of the Koopa Trace-Walking minigame. If spoken to, he allows players to play the minigame. Players have to complete his minigame to get their reward. However, if enemies approach him, they can cause the Koopa Trace-Walking Host to panic, locking the minigame until the enemies leave or die.
