'''Iron Pants''' is a clothing item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is only usable by [[Mario]], and has the [[Heavy G]] special effect.

== In the games ==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', equipping the Iron Pants increases Mario's [[Defense]] rating by 70, or 80 in the Japanese version.

In the remake, the Iron Pants can be obtained from a [[Monty Mole]] in [[Beanbean Fields]]. The Iron Pants' Defense rating was increased even further to 99. It can be sold at a low price of three [[coin]]s.
