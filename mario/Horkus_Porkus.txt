'''Horkus Porkus''' (also known as '''Phil''') is an enemy from [[Wario: Master of Disguise|''Wario: Master of Disguise'']]. He is the statue of a God who can look straight into the heart of all men. He rises up and down and will try to crush Wario. He takes multiple hits to destroy. 

== In-game description ==
''The statue of a god who can look straight into the hearts of all men. Due to a tragic spelling error, most people call him "Phil".''

== Etymology ==
The name is a pun of "hocus pocus" and "Horus", a falcon-headed god.
