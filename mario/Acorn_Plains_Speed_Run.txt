'''Acorn Plains Speed Run''' is a Time Attack Challenge in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

==Description==
Taking place in [[Acorn Plains Way]], the challenge involves having to reach the [[Goal Pole]] within at least 70 seconds. Goombas, Waddlewings, and Piranha Plants are encountered along the way.
