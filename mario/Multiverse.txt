'''Multiverse''' is a location that appears in the ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' series. It is a collective term which involves all universes that exist, the most famous of which being [[Mario]]'s [[Universe|home universe]].

== Description ==
The multiverse is the collective term for all universes that exist, including timelines. The Mario series has one primary universe that focus several events happening in games and spin-offs in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'', ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' and ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' series.

The ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series is set in an alternate universe where events from the games happened in a [[Mysterious Book|book]]. The paper world acts the same way as the main world, however, it is unknown if characters like [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] or [[Wario]] exist. Although [[Luigi's Diary]] mentions [[Mario Golf (series)|Golf]], [[Mario Tennis (series)|Tennis]] and [[Mario Party (series)|Parties]], implying they do exist in the paper reality.

== Game appearances ==

=== ''Mario'' games ===
In the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' series, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] met their [[Baby Mario|past]] [[Baby Luigi|counterparts]] and [[Time hole|time traveled]] to the past to defeat the [[Shroob|Shroobs]]. They also encounter [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] and [[Paper Princess Peach|other]] [[Paper Toad|characters]] from ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' universe, who visited the [[Mushroom Earth|real world]] to fight against [[Bowser]], [[Paper Bowser|his paper self]] and [[Koopa Troop|his army]].{{Expansion}}
=== Other games ===

* In ''[[Wario Blast: Featuring Bomberman!]]'', Wario heads into a [https://bomberman.fandom.com/wiki/Planet_Bomber different universe], where he encounters [[Bomberman]].
* In ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series, many other franchises coexist, including characters from the ''Mario'', ''Donkey Kong'', ''Yoshi'', ''and Wario'' series. As such, the term [[w:c:supersmashbros:universe|universe]] is used by the ''Super Smash Bros.'' fans to collectively refer to all content from a specific franchise present in the series, not to other universes like the [[w:c:supersmashbros:World of Trophies|World of Trophies]], [[w:c:supersmashbros:Subspace|Subspace]] and [[w:c:supersmashbros:Mysterious Dimension|Mysterious Dimension]]. Each universe has a symbol representing it with the ''Mario'' franchise's symbol being a [[mushroom]], the Yoshi franchise's symbol being a [[Yoshi Egg|yoshi egg]], the ''Donkey Kong'' and Wario franchise's symbols being a letter.
* In ''[[NBA Street V3]]'' and ''[[SSX on Tour]]'', characters from the ''Super Mario'' series exist in the [[Earth|real world]], where they encounter characters from ''[[wikipedia:NBA Street (series)|NBA Street]]'' and ''[[wikipedia:SSX|SSX]]''.
* The ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series features multiple characters from different franchises and other worlds as part of the racetracks.
* The ''[[Itadaki Street DS|Itadaki Street]]'' series features ''Super Mario'' and ''[[wikipedia:Dragon Quest|Dragon Quest]]'' as part of the same universe.
* In [[Mario Hoops 3-on-3|two]] ''[[Mario Sports Mix|Mario Sports]]'' games features characters from ''[[wikipedia:Final Fantasy|Final Fantasy]]'' appear in the Mario universe to steal a cup.
* In ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' series, characters from the ''Super Mario'' and ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic the Hedgehog series|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' series exist together in the real world, where they travel other countries to participate the [[wikipedia:Olympic Games|Olympic Games]].
* In ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Lost World|Sonic Lost World]]'', [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] visits [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]] from via [[Warp Pipe|Warp pipe]] to [[w:c:sonic:Sonic's universe|Sonic's universe]], where he travels there to save the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]].
* In ''[[Mario + Rabbids (series)|Mario + Rabbids]]'' series, ''Super Mario'' and ''[[wikipedia:Rabbids|Rabbids]]'' are part of the same universe, though not set in the main universe.<ref>[https://www.reddit.com/r/Marioverse/comments/1ebh08f/the_mario_rabbids_games_are_not_canon_davide/?utm_name=web3xcss&rdt=54558 The Mario + Rabbids games are not canon (Davide Soliani Interview).] Retrieved April 21, 2026</ref>
