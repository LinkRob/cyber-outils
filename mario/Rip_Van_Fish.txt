'''Rip Van Fish'''<ref>M. Arakawa. Nintendo ''Mario Mania'' Player's Guide. Page 106.</ref> (also pluralized '''Rip Van Fishes'''<ref name=Prima>Stratton, Bryan. ''Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide. Page 14.</ref>) are fish enemies who spend most of their time sleeping. They make their first and main appearance in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. Rip Van Fish are large blue fish with orange fins, tail, and lips as well as a pair of big eyes.

Rip Van Fish is named after the literary character {{wp|Rip Van Winkle}}, who fell asleep for twenty years.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario World''====
{{multiframe|[[File:RipVanFish-SMW.png]] [[File:RipVanFishSwimmingR.gif]]|align=left}}
[[Image:Blurp Waters.png|thumb|right|Mario near some Rip Van Fish.]]
Rip Van Fish first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' commonly appearing in underwater levels. They are seen sleeping but as [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] approach, they wake up and give chase, they don't stop chasing the plumbers until they've killed them. However, if Mario/Luigi get too far from a Rip Van Fish, they will fall back asleep. While not much of a threat alone, they sometimes appear in groups which can make them very dangerous especially as they are faster than most fish enemies and the slow swimming Mario. Mario and Luigi cannot defeat these enemies unless they have a [[Fire Flower]], use a [[Starman]] or have their [[Yoshi]] eat them. If Mario/Luigi is under the effects of a Starman, the Rip Van Fish will flee from him.

Rip Van Fish can also be awakened by the whistle of a [[Chargin' Chuck]]. While Mario can only awaken one fish at a time as he walks by, the whistle can awaken all the fish in an area which can make them a major threat.
{{br|left}}

===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island/Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''===
[[Image:Rip Van Fish Dark.png|thumb|right|Yoshi near a Rip Van Fish and Piscatory Pete.]]
Rip Van Fish also appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' where they move left to right (or up and down or even in a pattern) in [[Yoshi Submarine|Submarine]] sections. In this game, Rip Van Fish aren't seen sleeping but are rather moving around until Yoshi's approaches at which point they charge at him at a quick pace. You can defeat them by shooting missiles at that them in your Submarine form. A faster golden variant of Rip Van Fish which actually chases you called [[Piscatory Pete]] also appears, this variety can move in any direction and not just up, down, left, or right. Both varieties are primarily found in levels of [[World 3 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 3]].

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
[[Image:Rip Van Fish DS.png|thumb|left|Yoshi swimming above a Rip Van Fish.]]
The Rip Van Fish also appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', in this game, they are practically invincible due to Submarine Yoshi being unable to shoot Missiles. They behave as they did previously (moving up and down or left and right) and ramming into Submarine Yoshi upon sight. In this game, Rip Van Fish are very rare enemies only found in one level, [[Island of Peril]]. Due to being located underwater, to earn the Rip Van Fish in the [[Museum]], all you need to do is encounter the enemy as you cannot toss [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] at them, they are located in the Aquatic Enemy Exhibit. The [[Piscatory Pete]] also returns in this game.

====''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''====
The Rip Van Fish reappear in the underwater portions of the [[World-e]] level [[Sea to Sky]] in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. They act the same as in ''Super Mario World''.

===''Super Mario World'' television series===
[[File:RipVanFishCartoon.jpg|thumb|A Rip Van Fish from the ''Super Mario World'' cartoon series episode, "King Scoopa Koopa".]]
Rip Van Fish also appeared in the [[Super Mario World (television series)|''Super Mario World'' television series]] episodes "[[King Scoopa Koopa]]" and "[[Mama Luigi]]".

In "King Scoopa Koopa", after leaping down a [[Warp Pipe]] in the [[Star Road (Super Mario World)|Star Path]] to escape several cave people transformed into [[Chickadactyl]]s, Mario and [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] end up in the ocean. Seeing a Rip Van Fish near, Mario and Princess Toadstool attempt to tiptoe past the Rip Van Fish, only for it to wake up, leading to the latter chasing them. Fortunately, Mario and Princess Toadstool struck into the Warp Pipe before the Rip Van Fish can get them.

In "Mama Luigi", [[Luigi]] and [[Yoshi]], after falling into the ocean, end up waking a nearby Rip Van Fish, which proceeds to chase Luigi and Yoshi. Attempting to escape the Rip Van Fish, Luigi and Yoshi enter a nearby, underwater tunnel and begin swimming furiously, avoiding obstacles such as [[? Block]]s and [[Urchin]]s. Eventually, the Rip Van Fish, having apparently grown bored of chasing Luigi and Yoshi, quickly stops and begins to nap once again, allowing Luigi and Yoshi to get away.

===''Mario's FUNdamentals''===
In ''[[Mario's FUNdamentals]]'', Rip Van Fishes appear as a playing card when the player plays Go Fish with Mario.

===''Mario Family''===
[[File:RipVanFish MarioFamily.png|thumb|A Rip Van Fish in ''Mario Family'']]
A Rip Van Fish appears as a sewing design in ''[[Mario Family]]''.

===''Nintendo Puzzle Collection''===
Rip Van Fish reappears in the [[Nintendo GameCube]] remake of ''[[Yoshi's Cookie]]'' in ''[[Nintendo Puzzle Collection]]'', as the second opponent in the game's VS. Mode if the player plays on Hard mode.

*The Rip Van Fish is similar to the [[Spiny Cheep-Cheep]] from the later games starting with ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' as both are purple fish that relentlessly chase the heroes upon sight. It is possible that the Spiny Cheep-Cheep has replaced the Rip Van Fish.
*The Rip Van Fish are among one of the few enemies from ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and ''Yoshi's Island DS'' to not return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.
