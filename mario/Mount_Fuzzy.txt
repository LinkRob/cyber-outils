'''Mount Fuzzy''' is the first level in [[Rock-Candy Mines]] in the game ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Layout==
The player starts the level near a [[Fuzzy]] on a track, and he must climb up platforms to reach to top of the area. On the left side is a [[Hidden Block]] with a [[Mini Mushroom]]. A Fuzzy circling a platform is found, along with a Fuzzy around a [[? Block]] and some [[Brick Block]]s. Three more Fuzzies are found circling platforms, followed by a cave containing four Fuzzies riding a track around rows of Brick Blocks. A [[Koopa Troopa]] is also found. There is a [[Mini Pipe]] that leads to a hidden area with [[Jump Block]]s, [[coin]]s and three ? Blocks that contain [[1-Up Mushroom]]s. Two Fuzzies and a [[Green Ring]] are found, followed by more platforms. Three Fuzzies on a track are found near two groups of Brick Blocks, followed by the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Follow the Fuzzies in the first circle and wall jump to get the coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Use a Flying Squirrel suit or the Ice Flower (from the green ring) to collect this Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Freeze a Fuzzy and use it as a platform to reach the Star Coin. 
==Hidden Luigi images==
If you jump when you got Star Coin 1, you will see this Hidden 8-bit Luigi.
