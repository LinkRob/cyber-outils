A '''Gradual Syrup''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:GradualSyrupTTYD.png|frame|left]]
In this game, consuming a Gradual Syrup restores 2 [[Flower Point|FP]] every turn for 10 turns. Gradual Syrups can be purchased in the [[Deepdown Depot]] for 15 [[coin]]s.
{{br|left}}
===''Super Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Gradual Syrups now restores [[Heart Point|HP]] over time due to the game lacking FP. In order to get a Gradual Syrup in this game, [[Mario]] must take a [[Long-Last Shake]] and a [[Peachy Peach]] to [[Dyllis]] of [[Flopside]] to have her make it into one.
