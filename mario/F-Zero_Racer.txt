'''F-Zero Racers''', also named '''F-Zero machines''', are the racing vehicles used in the ''F-Zero'' series. The [[Blue Falcon]], being [[Captain Falcon]]'s vehicle, is the most notable vehicle amongst the F-Zero Racers. The F-Zero Racers also make a few appearances in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] as well as several games within the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]].

The Blue Falcon makes a cameo appearance in the German ''[[Club Nintendo (magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' comic ''[[Super Mario: Im Rausch der Geschwindigkeit]]''. Additionally, during the ending of a chapter in [[Super Mario-kun Volume 4|volume 4]] of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', Mario and Yoshi are chased by F-Zero Racers.
