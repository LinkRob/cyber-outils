'''Mighty Peach''' is one of Peach's transformations in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''

===Appearance===
In her Mighty form, Peach is equipped with a black bodysuit with pieces of armor overlapping. It has the shape of a sleeveless minidress with a black midsection and a silver belt that has a round yellow button at the center, knee-length wedged boots, white gloves, and telescopic sleeves that can turn into parabolic antenna-like devices, capable of generating electricity. She wears a clear light blue visor on her eyes, held up by two red antennas that cover her ears, and her hair is tied up in a ponytail with a red band.

==Abilities==
In her Mighty form, Peach can pick up big and heavy things, including meteors, vehicles, and even UFOs.
