'''Paper Plane''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', being one of the microgames part of [[Mona]]'s [[Strange]] folder. Paper Plane has an extended [[minigame]] variation in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', also titled [[Paper Plane (minigame)|Paper Plane]]. In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', the microgame received a [[reissue|remake]] titled [[Ground Control]].

==Layout==
There is a [[Paper plane (object)|paper plane]] flying from above in some sort of building, and barriers sticking out from either wall.

==Gameplay==
Using the Control Pad, the player must steer the paper plane around the obstacles until the microgame timer expires, and if done successfully, the player wins at the microgame. If the paper plane touches one of the obstacles, it is destroyed, and the player loses at the microgame.

*'''Level 1:''' The obstacles are very thin.
*'''Level 2:''' The obstacles are twice the original size.
*'''Level 3:''' The obstacles are three times the original size, making them very thick.
