'''Bowser's Sub''' is a submarine featured in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its remake, ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. The submarine plays a prominent role in the first mission of [[Dire, Dire Docks]], entitled [[Board Bowser's Sub]].

Here, [[Mario]] has to climb atop the sub and reach the [[Power Star]] atop its bow. Though little is known about the sub, it can be assumed that Bowser's troops are in it. The sub disappears after Mario defeats [[Bowser]] in the [[Bowser in the Fire Sea]] level. In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', a [[Bill Blaster]] is added on the sub.
