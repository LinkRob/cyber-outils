The '''Super Flower Cup''' is a [[cup]] in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Strikers''==
Super Flower Cup is a Super Cup that can be unlocked by winning in the top three of the [[Super Mushroom Cup]]. Super Flower Cup is essentially the same as the [[Flower Cup]], but also includes matches. Six teams participate in the Super Flower Cup, and there are ten rounds played. Winning in the top three places unlocks the [[Super Star Cup]]. Furthermore, if the player wins in first place, they unlock the "Field Tilt" cheat.
