'''Rally Block''', also named '''Count Block''', is a yellow [[block]] with a digital counter. It first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', and it has returned in various ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' series installments.

== Description ==
The Rally Block is a yellow block with a digital counter. If Mario or Luigi hits the block, the brothers will position themselves further from each other. Mario and Luigi must hit the block to gain [[coin]]s. The counter increases when the brother hits the block.

Since ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Rally Block has the same function in all of its appearances.
