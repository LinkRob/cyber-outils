'''Hop, Drop, and Roll''' is a 1-vs.-2 or a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object for the solo character is to eliminate all of their rivals within a 30-second [[time limit]]. The solo character has control over the [[Bill Blaster]]s and can decide when [[Bullet Bill]]s are fired from them; additionally, [[Grrrol]]s spawn every three seconds and roll out on their own. Rivals have to avoid the Grrrols or Bullet Bills, otherwise they get eliminated. If the solo character eliminates every rival within 30 seconds, they win, but if at least one rival survives until the end, their side wins.

Its name is a pun on the phrase "stop, drop, and roll."
