The '''Toy Train''' is both a key item and a transportation which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
In order to get further into [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]], the Toy Train is needed to get around. However, the Toy Train was stolen and has been stored in the storage room of [[Harry's Shop]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. In order to get the Toy Train, [[Mario]] must have the [[Storeroom Key]] first. After getting the Toy Train, Mario must place it in Shy Guy's Toy Box before going into there. After the Toy Train has been placed, Mario can now head further into Shy Guy's Toy Box to find the next [[Star Spirit]].

==Trivia==
*The Toy Train is one of two trains that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. The other being the [[K64]].
