The '''Red Key''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
The Red Key is on the second floor in [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. Here, the Red Key is carried by a [[Fawfulcopter]], which [[Bowser]] chases to the highest spot on the cliff using his [[Spike Ball (move)|Spike Ball]] ability. Bowser can hit the Fawfulcopter for the Red Key, and with it, Bowser can open the red door he encountered earlier and rescue his [[Magikoopa]] minions.
