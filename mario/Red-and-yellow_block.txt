'''Red-and-yellow blocks''',<ref>''New Super Mario Bros. Official Player's Guide'', page 109.</ref> or '''red Blocks''',<ref>''Nintendo DS Pocket Guide'', pages 35 and 100.</ref> are objects in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Description==
Red-and-yellow-blocks are switches that control [[platform]]s. They are suspended in the air like [[block]]s, but they can be struck an innumerable number of times. Striking a red-and-yellow block once triggers nearby platforms to start moving or materialize. Striking it a second time makes them stop or disappear. The type of platforms impacted depends on the [[level]].

The switches first appear in {{world-link|2|towern|World 2-Tower (New Super Mario Bros.)}} after the level's [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]]. Striking them causes [[rotating platform]]s to materialize, allowing [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) to reach one of the level's [[Star Coin]]s and the boss room with [[Bowser Jr.]] Striking the switches again causes the rotating platforms to disappear. Red-and-yellow blocks subsequently appear in {{world-link|8|castlen|World 8-Castle}}. Striking them causes nearby [[Lift|Flatbed Ferries]] to move, a function similar to [[ON/OFF Switch]]es from ''[[Super Mario World]]''. Striking the red-and-yellow blocks is necessary to collect all of the level's Star Coins.
