{{nihongo|'''Crabs'''|カーニィー|Kānyī}} are an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Crabs are members of the species of the {{w|crab|same name}}. They hide their face in their shell-like head. It is hard to tell a Crab's real color due to its black and white artwork.

=== Behavior ===
Crabs attack [[Mario]] by stretching their claws at him upon noticing him.
