A '''Paper Macho Goomba''' is a large [[Goomba]] that is a [[Paper Macho Soldier]].
== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' ==
Paper Macho Goombas are located at the middle of [[Toad Town]]. The first one will be eating a building. The first time you hammer the Paper Macho Goomba, a little flag will pop up on their head and will charge after you even faster. If it manages to come in contact with Mario, he loses seven lives. After the Paper Macho enemy is defeated, you'll earn confetti to help fill in holes to earn [[Coin]]s.
