'''Cuby''' is a [[snow spirits|snow spirit]] in [[Adventure Tours]] mode in the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
It is the snow spirit of [[Cubyrinth]], hence the shortened "Cuby" for its name, and the fourth one encountered by [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]]. Cuby is first found in [[Polastraits]], guarded by [[Eggman Nega]]. After defeating him, they free Cuby and it joins the team after Pola stays in Polastraits. If the player has four yellow crystals, it can break the ice walls in Cubyrinth.
