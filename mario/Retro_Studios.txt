'''Retro Studios''' is an American video game developer based in Austin, Texas. It was founded by Jeff Spangenberg in 1998 as an independent development studio to create games exclusively for [[Nintendo]]. It initially started with four projects for the [[Nintendo GameCube]], but all of them were cancelled. It was eventually bought out by Nintendo to become a first-party developer and a division of Nintendo. Its first major release was ''Metroid Prime''. Its first title related to Mario was ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' for the [[Wii]].
