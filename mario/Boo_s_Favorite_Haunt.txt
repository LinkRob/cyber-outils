}}'''Boo's Favorite Haunt''', is the ghost house course of [[Soda Jungle]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by completing [[Stone-Snake Tower]], and its own completion will unlock [[Painted Pipeworks]] and [[Deepsea Stone-Eyes]].
==Layout==
The level starts in a ghost house, near [[Boo Buddies|Circling Boo Buddies]] around a [[? Block]]. Some moving platforms are found above a [[pit]], and multiple groups of Circling Boo Buddies are found as well. A ? Block containing either a [[Super Mushroom]] or a [[Super Acorn]] depending on the player's current form. A stationary platform is found, with a group of Circling Boo Buddies floating in various directions. There is a [[Hidden Block]] that contains a [[Starman]]. Some swinging platforms surrounded by Circling Boo Buddies are found, with moving platforms in between. At the end of the area, a group of Circling Boo Buddies is found around a door. Entering the door leads the player outside to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Jump over the second group of Circling Boo Buddies and land on a safe spot.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Jump over the eighth group of Circling Boo Buddies and land on a safe spot.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Avoid the last group of Circling Boo Buddies and make a big jump to the right wall having a coin nearby.

==Hidden Luigi image==
On the wall by the door leading to the secret exit shows a framed picture of modern Luigi.

==Secret Exit==
Under the third Star Coin leads a door leading to the secret exit. The path will lead to [[Para-Beetle Parade|Soda Jungle-Secret: Para-Beetle Parade]].
