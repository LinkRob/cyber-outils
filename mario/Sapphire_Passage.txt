'''Sapphire Passage''' is a location in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
The Sapphire Passage is one of six passages in the [[Golden Pyramid]]. It is represented by a blue panel at the bottom-left depicting a menacing face. It consists of four levels—[[Crescent Moon Village]], [[Arabian Night]], [[Fiery Cavern]], and [[Hotel Horror]]—most of which have a horror theme, and concludes with a boss fight against [[Catbat]], a large, purple, ghost-like cat with a bat statue on its head. The jewel pieces in this passage are sapphires, hence its name, and the [[CD]]s are blue.
