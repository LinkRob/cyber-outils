The '''Dragonfly''' is an unlockable [[kart]] in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]].

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', the Dragonfly is a light red tractor, with a brown seat for the driver. The wheels have a yellow rim with a red outline, with the front two wheels being slightly smaller than the rear wheels. The character's emblem, normally Wario's, is located on the hood.

In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', the Dragonfly gets a darker shade of red, and the wheels are equally sized and are a dark gray version of the [[Monster (tires)|Monster]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', with a red rim and the ''MARIOKART'' logo adorned on them. The seat for the driver is also now dark gray. The character's emblem will also appear on the back of the kart, and there is also the addition of a yellow mower at the back.

== In ''Mario Kart DS'' ==
[[File:MKDS Dragonfly.png|thumb|200px|right|Wario and his Dragonfly on the kart selection menu.]]
The Dragonfly first appears in ''Mario Kart DS'', as an unlockable kart for [[Wario]]. It is unlocked by completing the [[Grand Prix|Nitro Grand Prix]] in [[Engine Class|100cc]] along with all the other unlockable karts in the game.

The Dragonfly is second [[Weight|heaviest]] kart only being lighter than the [[Tyrant]], so its ideal for knocking into other karts. It's stats, except [[weight]] and [[Items (stat)|items]] are all slightly lower than his other karts.

It shares its handling with [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Royale]], and it shares its drift with [[Dry Bones]]' [[Banisher]]. It shares its items stat with all other characters' heavier karts.

[[Luigi]] and [[R.O.B.]] can use the Dragonfly once all cups in the 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix have been completed. All other characters can use this kart once all cups in Mirror Mode have been completed.

The Dragonfly is used with Wario as the [[Staff Ghost]] for [[Choco Mountain|<small>N64</small> Choco Mountain]].

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
[[File:MKT Amsterdam Tour Week 2 Drivers.jpg|thumb|200px|left|Daisy (Farmer) on the Dragonfly.]]
Dragonfly appears as a kart in [[Amsterdam Tour]]. It is the signature kart of [[Daisy (Farmer)]]. The Dragonfly contains a special skill called [[Slipstream Plus]].
