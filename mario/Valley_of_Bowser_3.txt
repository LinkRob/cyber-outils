'''Valley of Bowser''' is a [[level]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', more specifically [[Valley of Bowser]]. Despite its name, Valley of Bowser 3 is not the [[world]]'s third level, as it is only unlocked after completing [[Valley Ghost House]], although completing Valley of Bowser 3 unlocks [[Valley of Bowser 4]], which comes after in numerical succession.
