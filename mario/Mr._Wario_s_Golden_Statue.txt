'''Mr. Wario's Golden Statues''', simply titled '''Gold Statues''' or '''Wario Statues''', are an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
In each course, a Mr. Wario's Golden Statue has been split into eight parts known as [[Statue Piece]]s by the [[Black Jewel]]'s magic and scattered throughout the course. As such, [[Wario]] has to obtain the eight Statue Pieces to complete a statue, and in doing so, half a heart is added to his health bar. Given that there are eight Mr. Wario's Golden Statues, his health bar can increase by up to four hearts.
