The '''Troopa Shell''' is a weapon usable by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is the empty shell of a [[Koopa Paratroopa|Sky Troopa]] with the wings still intact. It can be bought from the shop in [[Seaside Town]] for 90 [[coin]]s after [[Yaridovich]]'s occupation has ended. The Troopa Shell has an [[Attack]] power of 50.
