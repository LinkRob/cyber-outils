'''Rams''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are {{w|Sheep|ram}}s with light brown fur and long horns.

===Behavior===
If a Ram is provoked by [[Wario]], it leaps and tries to crash down horns first onto him. Rams are also encountered in large stampedes.

==In ''Wario World''==
Rams only appear in [[Beanstalk Way]]. Some of them appear individually, but an indefinite number can be found stampeding down mountains, much like the [[Sled Creature]]s in [[Shivering Mountains]]. A Ram within a stampede can push Wario into a lower part of level or into the [[Unithorn's Lair]] if he crashes into them.

To defeat a Ram, Wario must first [[punch]] the Ram a few times to stun it. Afterward, Wario must use one of his [[Mad Move]]s—[[Piledriver]], [[Wild Swing-Ding]], or [[Mega Toss]]—to finally defeat it. However, Rams in a stampede are invincible and cannot be defeated.
