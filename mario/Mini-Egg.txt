*[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' (Nintendo Switch)]]}}'''Mini-Egg''' is a special move used by [[Yoshi (Partner)|Yoshi]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Yoshi can learn this move once he reaches the [[Super Rank]] and requires 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==History==
To use Mini-Egg, the player must press {{button|gcn|a}} when the star lights up to have Yoshi lay eggs. At the Super Rank, Yoshi lays up to three eggs but once he reaches the [[Ultra Rank]], he can lay up to four. After laying the eggs, Yoshi will then throw them at random enemies shrinking them and causing damage. This move can be useful but due to the randomness of this ability, it's recommend not to be used that much.
