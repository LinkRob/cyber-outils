'''Clay''' is a playable character who appears in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour''==
In the Story mode, the player can select either Clay or [[Ace]] to compete in the [[Island Open]] and eventually the [[Peach Tournament]]. Clay is the character's default name, but the player can decide on his name, and can input up to five letters.
