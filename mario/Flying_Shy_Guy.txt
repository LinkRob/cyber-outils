'''Flying Shy Guys''' are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Flying Shy Guy has the same appearance as a red-robed Shy Guy.

===Behavior===
A Flying Shy Guy rides a [[Magic Carpet]] around, and this is their only distinction from regular Shy Guys.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
Flying Shy Guys are encountered by racers in [[Shy Guy Bazaar]], during the [[glider]] portion. In ''Mario Kart 7'', if a racer crashes into a Flying Shy Guy, they immediately lose speed and begin falling. In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', while they still lose speed as a result, the racer can continue gliding along.
