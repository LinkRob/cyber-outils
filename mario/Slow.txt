'''Slow''' is a status effect that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==History==
Slow is a rather rare status condition with only [[Beldam]] being the only one who can inflict it directly. It works the opposite of [[Fast]] as those affected with Slow won't be able to attack every other turn. This status is rather troublesome as Mario will only encounter it during an outnumbered fight.

In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Slow is rare as well with only [[Cursya]]s being able to inflict it. [[Count Bleck]] uses an attack that slows down everything around him as well.
