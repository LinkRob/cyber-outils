The '''Hylian Kite''' is a type of [[glider]] featured in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' (from the ''[[The Legend of Zelda x Mario Kart 8]]'' content pack) and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. The Hylian Kite is dark blue in color, and bears a red [[w:c:zelda:Royal Crest|Royal Crest]] symbol on top. For the most part, its stats are average. The Hylian Kite is associated with the [[kart]] [[Master Cycle]] and the [[tires]] [[Triforce Tires]].

In ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', Hylian Kite is included in the base game, and can be unlocked at random by collecting [[coin]]s.
