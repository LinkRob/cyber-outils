|caption = [[Mario]] just entering Wiggler's Tree House}}'''Wiggler's Tree House''' is the third level of World 3 in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Layout==
Wiggler's Tree House is located in the middle of [[The Bafflewood]] with the surroundings having the same color. The house itself is huge.

==History==
After exiting the maze forest, [[Mario]] finally reaches Wiggler's Tree House. He enters the enormous tree and works his way upward. Unfortunately for the [[Wiggler]], [[Kamek]] shows up and uses his magic to split the Wiggler's body segments apart sending them across different areas. Mario agrees to help the Wiggler put himself back together and grabs one of the three [[Comet Piece]]s in the room and finishes the level.

==HP-Up Heart==
Mario can find a [[HP-Up Heart]] on the second level of Wiggler's Tree House. However, Mario must have a Secret Door sticker in order to get to it.
