This article pertains to the beta elements of ''[[Mario's Super Picross]].''

== Unused Music ==
There are two unused songs found within the game's data. What it purpose it might've had is unknown. All songs can be heard at the '''Select-A-Data''' '''Screen''' using the Pro Action Replay code '''C02B79??''' (where '''"??"''' is one of the below). All the songs have looped/non-looping versions and subtracting or adding '''40''' from the current song's value will play them.
