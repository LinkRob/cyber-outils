'''Candies''' are special items in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
As Candies replace the [[Orb]]s of ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]'', players can collect and eat the Candies found throughout the [[board]]s to either help themselves or hinder the progress of their opponents. Upon eating a Candy, the player is granted a specific ability for their current turn. Their physical appearance may also change. A single player on Star Battle Arena or Battle Royale (via Party Tent), they can hold up to three pieces of candy. In Tag Battle (via Party Tent), a team can hold up to six. If a player has too much Candy, they (the player) can discard one of their candies. That is, to make room for the new treat.

While free Candies can be earned by passing over [[? Candy|? Candies]], there is no way to determine which Candy a player will receive upon crossing one. However, players can also visit [[Candy Shop]]s, where they can purchase a Candy of their choice in exchange for [[coin]]s. Unlike the Orbs and capsules of the three previous installments, Candies cannot be thrown onto [[Blue Space]]s and [[Red Space]]s to create [[Character Space]]s or roadblocks; as such, Candies instead function like the items from [[Mario Party|the]] [[Mario Party 2|first]] [[Mario Party 3|four]] [[Mario Party 4|installments]] of the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series (if counting just the home console games).

Additionally, the grand prize awarded to the winner of [[Star Battle Arena]] is a year's supply of Candy.

==Variations==
===Red Candies===
Red Candies affect the [[Dice Block]].

====Twice Candy====
{{main|Twice Candy}}
[[File:TwiceCandyMP8.png|100px|thumb]]
'''Twice Candy''' allows the player to roll two Dice Blocks instead of one when eaten. If both dice roll the same number, the player gets 10 coins. Rolling two 7s gives the player 30 coins. This Candy costs five coins to buy in the shop.

====Thrice Candy====
{{main|Thrice Candy}}
[[File:Thrice Candy.png|100px|left|thumb]]
'''Thrice Candy''' allows the player to roll three Dice Blocks instead of one when eaten. If all the dice roll the same number, the player gets 30 coins. Rolling three 7s gives the player 100 coins. This Candy costs 15 or 20 coins to buy in the shop.

====Slowgo Candy====
{{main|Slowgo Candy}}
[[File:Slowgo Candy.png|100px|thumb]]
'''Slowgo Candy''' allows the player to be able to roll a Dice Block that scrolls through the numbers more slowly, but the numbers allowed to be hit are 1&ndash;5 instead of 1&ndash;10. This candy is useful for special spaces. If the player is near [[DK Space|DK]] or [[Lucky Space]]s, they can use this candy to get on to them. The Candy costs 10 or 15 coins in the shop.
{{br}}

===Green Candies===
Green Candies affect the player and/or the other players just before the player hits the Dice Block.

====Springo Candy====
{{main|Springo Candy}}
[[File:Springo Candy.png|100px|left|thumb]]
'''Springo Candy''' allows the player to move freely to another player's space. The player must first throw a dart at a wheel with the other characters' faces on it. Depending on whom the dart hits, the player will spring (using their spring-like body) to that player's space. In Tag Battle Mode, the player can select one of the players only on their opposed team. After that, the player will hit the Dice Block. The Candy costs 10 or 20 coins if bought at a shop.

====Vampire Candy====
{{main|Vampire Candy}}
[[File:Vampire Candy.png|100px|thumb]]
'''Vampire Candy''' allows the player to turn into a vampire-like version of themselves and steal coins from other players. First, the player must throw a dart at a wheel, with the section it lands on determining how many coins they will steal. Teammates cannot have their coins stolen in Tag Battle Mode. The Candy costs 10 or 15 coins if bought at a shop.

====Cashzap Candy====
{{main|Cashzap Candy}}
[[File:Cashzap Candy.png|100px|left|thumb]]
'''Cashzap Candy''' allows the player to attack a player and destroy their coins but not steal them. The player must throw a dart at a wheel with the other characters' faces on it, and, depending on whom the dart hits, the player will attack that other player with lightning, causing them to lose half of their coins. The Candy costs about 15 or 20 coins to buy.
{{br}}

===Yellow Candies===
Yellow Candies affect the player and/or the other players while the player moves across the board.

====Bowlo Candy====
{{main|Bowlo Candy}}
[[File:Bowlo Candy.png|100px|thumb]]
'''Bowlo Candy''' allows the player to turn into a ball version of themselves (similar to [[Mario]]'s form in ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]'') and steal 10 coins from each player (except their own teammate in Tag Battle Mode) they hit while moving across the board. The Candy costs 5 or 15 coins if bought at a shop.

====Bloway Candy====
{{main|Bloway Candy}}
[[File:Bloway Candy.png|100px|left|thumb]]
'''Bloway Candy''' allows the player's legs to turn into a mini-tornado and sends any opponent they touch while moving across the board back to the start. It costs 5 or 15 coins if bought at a shop.

====Bitsize Candy====
{{main|Bitsize Candy}}
[[File:Bitsize Candy.png|100px|thumb]]
'''Bitsize Candy''' allows the player to turn into an 8-bit version of themselves. While moving across the board, the player can hit a block from each space they pass that releases three coins. This is useful for collecting enough coins to buy a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s-up ahead, such as in [[DK's Treetop Temple]], in [[King Boo's Haunted Hideaway]], on [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]], and in [[Koopa's Tycoon Town]]. The Candy can be obtained only from [[? Candy|? Candies]]. This candy is also the biggest price of coins (at 25). Because on [[DK's Treetop Temple]] and [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]], if the [[Spear Guy]] or [[Holly Koopa]] decides to buy the player's Bitsize Candy and gives them coins in return, they give the player 25 coins. 

====Weeglee Candy====
{{main|Weeglee Candy}}
[[File:Weeglee Candy.png|100px|left|thumb]]
'''Weeglee Candy''' allows the player to turn into three tiny versions of themselves and steal a random Candy from each player they pass by while moving across the board. If one passes a character with no Candy, nothing occurs. Like Bitsize Candy, Weeglee Candy cannot be bought from shops and can be obtained only from ? Candies.
{{br}}

===Blue Candies===
Blue Candies are similar to Yellow Candies, but blue ones have a much greater effect on other players.

====Thwomp Candy====
{{main|Thwomp Candy}}
[[File:Thwomp Candy.png|100px|thumb]]
'''Thwomp Candy''' allows the player to turn into a stone head, and any players they pass by while moving across the board each lose half of their coins. This Candy costs 15 or 20 coins if bought at a shop.

====Duelo Candy====
{{main|Duelo Candy}}
[[File:Duelo Candy.png|100px|left|thumb]]
'''Duelo Candy''' allows the player to roll two [[Dice Block]]s and duel the first player they encounter while moving across the board. Whoever wins the Duel minigame gets to throw a dart at a wheel with coins and Stars. Depending on what the dart hits, the loser gives coins or Stars to the winner of the minigame. After dueling, the player ends their turn on that space, lose their dice rolls, and land on the same place as the other player. The Candy costs 10 or 20 coins if bought at a shop.

====Bowser Candy====
{{main|Bowser Candy}}
[[File:Bowser Candy.png|100px|thumb]]
'''Bowser Candy''' allows the player to transform into [[Bowser]], roll two [[Dice Block]]s, and steal two Stars from each player that they pass. This Candy is found only in [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]]. It costs 15 or 20 coins if bought at a shop (10 coins for last-place players).

====Bullet Candy====
{{main|Bullet Candy}}
[[File:Bullet Candy.png|100px|left|thumb]]
'''Bullet Candy''' allows the player to transform into a [[Bullet Bill]]-like version of themselves, roll three Dice Blocks, and steal one Star from each player they pass by. This Candy is found only on Bowser's Warped Orbit. It costs 15 or 20 coins if bought at a shop (10 coins for last-place players).
{{br}}
