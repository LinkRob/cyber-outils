{{Nihongo|'''Sushie'''<ref name="PaperMario"/>|おプク|Opuku|Madame Cheep}} is a Cheep-Cheep that becomes Mario's seventh partner in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Sushie can go into the water and having Mario ride on her through the water. She sometimes watches over Yoshi kids in the [[Yoshi Village]]. At the end of the game, Sushie is present the Yoshi kids in [[Jade Jungle]], then in [[Princess Peach's Castle]], and later on the Star Wagon with the other party members in the game.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Sushie is a purple [[Cheep-Cheep]] with orange hair, lips and tail.

=== Personality traits ===
Sushie is sensitive and soft-hearted. She loves romantic stories that she cries "at the top of her fin".<ref name="PaperMario"/><ref name="PaperMario2">{{cite video game| title = [[Paper Mario]]| developer = [[Intelligent Systems]]| publisher = [[Nintendo]]| date = 2001-02-05| platform = [[Nintendo 64]]|quote = Uh-huh... Oh... Oh! You're going to [[Mt. Lavalava|the volcano]] because you want to save [[Princess Peach|the princess]] from some [[Koopa Troop|bad guys]]? What...a touching story! Romantic stories like that... just touch a soft spot in this heart of mine. That does it! I can't let you go alone! I, Sushie, will be by your side until the end!|speaker = Sushie}}</ref> Sushie loves "taking care of others",<ref name="PaperMario1">{{cite video game| title = [[Paper Mario]]| developer = [[Intelligent Systems]]| publisher = [[Nintendo]]| date = 2001-02-05| platform = [[Nintendo 64]]|quote = You needn't worry, [[Mario]]! I love taking care of others! If you plan on getting through this [[Jade Jungle|jungle]], you need me anyway.|speaker = Sushie}}</ref> however, the Yoshi kids described her to be "bossy"; depicting she is not "succeeding".

===Power and abilities===
Sushie specializes water attacks.<ref name="PaperMario"/> Sushie can swim across water and dive under obstacles.

== Biography ==
{{Spoiler}}
Sushie has a daughter named [[Sashimie]]. A letter sent to Sushie by her daughter reveals that Sashimie will have a baby soon, which will make Sushie both a mother and a grandmother.
=== Yoshi Village ===
In the [[Yoshi Village]], Sushie asked Mario to help find the five missing Yoshi kids. During this quest, she becomes a partner of Mario to assist him in finding the Yoshi kids. When returning to Sushie with the Yoshi kids, Mario commented that he is rescuing [[Princess Peach]] from the [[Koopa Troop]] thus she praised the story and joined Mario's party.
{{Endspoiler}}

== Appearances ==
===''Paper Mario''===
Sushie appeared in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' as a playable character. She will join Mario's party after Mario returns the [[Yoshi kid]]s.

=== ''Super Paper Mario'' ===
Although Sushie does not fully appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', she appears as a [[Catch Card]]. Sushie's photo is also found on the picture. The Catch Card can either be obtained in the Card Shop, or after completing Room 80 in [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]].

==Other media==
===''Super Mario-Kun''===
Sushie appeared in the ''[[Super Mario-Kun]]'' [[Super Mario-Kun Vol 27|volume 27]], performing her role in ''Paper Mario''.
