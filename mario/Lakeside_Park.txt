[[File:MKSC Lakeside Park.png|thumb|Lakeside Park]]'''Lakeside Park''' is a track from ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. It is the first course in the [[Special Cup]]. The biggest hazard of this track is the two volcanoes. During the first lap the volcanoes are dormant but during the second lap, the volcanoes start to erupt and spit out lava which falls from the sky. If a racer is hit by the lava the racer will spin out.

== Trivia ==
* Although it is not confirmed by [[Nintendo]], many fans believe this track is [[Donkey Kong]]'s course in ''Mario Kart: Super Circuit'', due to the track resembling his head.
* In ''Mario Kart Tour'', it is the final retro track to be added to the game.
