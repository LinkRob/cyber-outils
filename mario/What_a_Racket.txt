'''What a Racket''' is a [[1 vs. 3 Minigame|1-vs-3]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
A bomb with a parachute lands on the middle of the line and explodes. The camera zooms out and players prepare with their rackets.

== Gameplay ==
The aim of the minigame is to hit the parachuting bombs to the other side to gain points. The solo player holds a large racket that gives the player enough time to hit the bomb and can rally the maximum of four bombs. The team holds regular-sized rackets that the team can rally one bomb. If the bomb stuns the player if it explodes close to the player.

== Ending ==
The rackets disappears from their hand and the camera zooms to either the solo player or the team if they win the minigame. The winners does their victory animation while losers are heard sulking.
