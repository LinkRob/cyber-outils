'''Mrs. President''' is a one-time character in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', having made an appearance in "[[Reptiles in the Rose Garden]]." She is the wife of [[Mr. President]], and unlike him, her full appearance is shown on-screen. Mrs. President is based on {{w|Barbara Bush}}, who was the First Lady and wife of {{w|George H. W. Bush}} at the time of ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''{{'}}s run, let alone [[DiC Entertainment]]'s ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' cartoons in general.
