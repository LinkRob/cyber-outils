==''Yoshi'' franchise==
===''Yoshi''===
[[File:Yoshi NES gameplay.png|thumb|A Type from ''[[Yoshi (game)|Yoshi]]''.]]
Bloobers make an appearance as one of the six falling objects in ''[[Yoshi (game)|Yoshi]]''. Bloober's sprite in this game is similar to its sprite from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

===''Yoshi's Safari''===
[[File:Blooper Yoshi's Safari sprite.png|frame|left|]]
In ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]'', yellow-colored Bloobers appear as flying enemies who will try to harm Mario and [[Yoshi]] by ramming them; these Bloobers must be defeated by blasting them repeatedly with Mario's [[Super Scope]]. The mech built and utilized by [[Iggy Koopa]] is the [[King Blooper]], which resembles a large Blooper.

===''Yoshi's Cookie'' remake===
A Blooper makes a small appearance in the ''[[Nintendo Puzzle Collection]]''{{'}}s version of ''[[Yoshi's Cookie]]''. They appear in the normal difficulty, being the aquatic enemy that replaces Cheep Cheep. In Hard difficulty, there is a [[Rip Van Fish]] instead.

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' features Bloopers appearing in early stages of [[World 3 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 3]], where they will try to attack a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] by hopping up and down in bodies of water. Bloopers can be easily defeated by being eaten, hit by an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], or [[jump]]ed upon. Three of them appear in the Aquatic Enemy Exhibit of the [[Island Museum]]. Eating them won't make Yoshi produce an egg.

===''Yoshi's New Island''===
A few Bloopers appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' as enemies in [[Spray Day Mayday]]. Specifically, they appear during the level's [[Submarine Yoshi]] section, where they try to swim into Yoshi in the same way as in previous appearances. Bumping into a Blooper causes Submarine Yoshi to slow down briefly, but the enemies can be defeated by shooting them with a missile.

===''Yoshi's Crafted World''===
{{Main|Takko}}
[[File:YCW Alien Squid.png|thumb|A Takko in-game]]
[[Takko]], a new variant of Bloopers, appear in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''. They do not attack or harm Yoshi, and cannot be defeated or turned into eggs; they can instead be used as springboards. When used in this manner, Takko expel ink, which scares away Shy Guys. Yoshi can also carry Takko in his mouth, and spit them into a new position. However, if a Takko is left in the same position for too long, it will teleport back to its original spot.

==''Super Princess Peach''==
[[File:Blooper SPP sprite.png|frame|left]]
Bloopers reappear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', where they are found in the [[Subrella]] sections of the game. They can be defeated simply by blasting a bubble at them with the submarine. There are also new sub-species of them called [[Glad Blooper]]s. Gooper Blooper also makes an appearance, as the boss of [[Wavy Beach]].

==''Donkey Kong'' franchise==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''===
[[File:MvDK2 Bloopers.png|thumb]]
Bloopers make their first appearance in the ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''. They first appear in [[Tropical Island]] as small variations, in Room 3. Initially, they seem stationary, blocking the [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Marios']] way, but as the Mini Marios approach them, they jump and hover for a few seconds, allowing the toys to pass underneath them.

==''Wario'' franchise==
===''WarioWare: D.I.Y.''===
[[File:Blooperwwdiy.png|thumb|left|175px|Blooper in ''WarioWare: D.I.Y.'']]
A Blooper appears in its original ''Super Mario Bros.'' appearance in the ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' [[microgame]] named after itself, [[Blooper (microgame)|Blooper]]. In the microgame, Mario must avoid the Blooper while trying to collect all the [[coin]]s. They are also the icon used for one of the 8-bit instruments in the [[Super MakerMatic 21#Record MakerMatic|music creator]].

==Crossover appearances==
===''Excitebike: Bun Bun Mario Battle''===
[[File:Sfami-me.png|thumb|]]
In ''[[Excitebike: Bun Bun Mario Battle]]'', various Bloobers can be observed in the audience.

===''Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix''===
[[Mega Blooper|Big Blooper]] and [[Blooper Baby|mini-Blooper]]s appear in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''. Despite this, Bloopers do not appear.

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
====''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''====
A Blooper [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. It can be obtained when it appears at random in one of the game's single-player modes.

====''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', a regular Blooper also appears as a trophy. In addition, a Big Blooper based on the boss from ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' appears as a hazard in the [[Paper Mario (stage)|Paper Mario]] stage. 

====''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', a regular Blooper appears as a Novice class Shield-type primary [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]]. When equipped, the fighter's weight is reduced, increasing their speed but making them easier to launch. In [[Adventure Mode: World of Light|World of Light]], the spirit can be found in the Light Realm. The corresponding spirit battle has three tiny [[Inkling]] boys as opponents on the [[Delfino Plaza (stage)|Delfino Plaza]] stage. Additionally, the Big Blooper returns on the Paper Mario stage.
{{br}}
===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
[[File:PDSMBE-W2-3.jpg|thumb|left|A Blooper in ''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition'']]
Bloopers are opponents found in water-themed levels of ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. Their attribute is Water (which means they are weak to the Wood attribute, but strong against Fire), and they are overall weak enemies; however, Bloopers' related specimens, the [[Blooper Nanny|Blooper Nannies]], are also present in the game and are enforced a bit by their [[Blooper Baby|babies]]. Bloopers attack with ink, and when they are allies to the player, they can use the "Ink Attack" Skill once every 11 turns, which multiplies the player's attack points by 1.5, but reduces their team's total HP to 1. Three [[Cheep Cheep Coin]]s can be used on any Blooper to make it evolve into "Blooper & [[Cheep Cheep]]", and with six more, evolves into "Blooper & Cheep Cheeps".
{{br|left}}

===''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games''===
Bloopers appear as an item in [[Football Plus]] in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]''. In this [[Plus Event]], the Blooper obscures the view of all members of the opposing team for a short amount of time, except for the goalkeeper who remains unaffected.

===''Mario + Rabbids'' series===
====''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle''====
[[File:MRKB bloopers.jpg|thumb|Mario, [[Luigi]], [[Rabbid Peach]] and [[Beep-0]] standing next to frozen Bloopers]]
Bloopers make minor appearances throughout [[Sherbet Desert]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. A few of them are blocked within ice formations during the second level, [[Undergarment Gulch]]. Pictures of Bloopers can be seen on boxes at the beginning of the level [[Call of Sherbet Mountain]]. A Rocket known as the [[List of weapons in Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle#Blooper Shooter|Blooper Shooter]] has a design resembling a Blooper.

====''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope''====
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'', two Bloopers appear swimming in the helmet of the giant diver suit at [[Mt. Spout]]. They can be seen performing the same charging attack from ''Super Mario 3D World''.

==Guest appearances==
====''The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening''====
[[File:TLOZ LA remake Bloopers.jpg|thumb|left|Bloopers as they appear in the remake]]
Bloopers, originally known as Bloobers,<ref>M. Arakawa. ''The Legend of Zelda – Link's Awakening'' Player's Guide. Page 100.</ref> appear as enemies of the hero [[Link]] in the game ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening]]''. These Bloopers look almost identical to the ones in the ''Super Mario'' franchise, with their proportions most closely resembling the ones in ''Super Mario Bros. 3''. Bloopers return in the [[Nintendo Switch]] remake where they can also be caught in the [[w:c:zelda:Fishing Hole|Fishing Hole]] of [[w:c:zelda:Mabe Village|Mabe Village]], while a figure of one can be won from the [[w:c:zelda:Trendy Game|Trendy Game]] after clearing the [[w:c:zelda:Catfish's Maw|Catfish's Maw]] and placed in [[w:c:zelda:Old Man Ulrira|Ulrira]]'s house. The text that appears upon acquiring the figure reads, "''You got a Blooper figure! It's not so menacing outside of the water.''" while its actual description reads, "''It's a Blooper figure. Squids are the hot new thing with kids today.''" a reference to the ''[[Splatoon (series)|Splatoon]]'' series.
{{br}}

====AR Games====
[[File:AR Games Blooper.jpg|thumb|A Blooper in the Fishing AR Game]]
On the [[Nintendo 3DS]], the Fishing minigame of ''[[AR Games]]'' includes a [[Lakitu]] fishing and catching a Blooper. The player can also catch Bloopers when Lakitu begins fishing.

====''Splatoon'' series====
In ''[[w:c:splatoon:Splatoon|Splatoon]]'', some images of 8-bit Bloopers can be seen on the walls in various multiplayer stages like [[Urchin Underpass]]. In ''[[w:c:splatoon:Splatoon 2|Splatoon 2]]''{{'}}s [[w:c:splatoon:Octo Expansion|Octo Expansion]] DLC, [[w:c:splatoon:Footloose Station|Footloose Station]] contains a platform shaped like a Blooper's sprite from ''Super Mario Bros.''

====''Minecraft''====
In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''[[Minecraft]]'', [[w:c:minecraft:Squid|Squids]] are replaced by Bloopers. [[w:c:minecraft:Glow Squid|Glow Squids]] appear as green Bloopers.

====''Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp''====
[[File:ACPC Blooper.png|thumb|left|50px]]
Bloopers, alongside [[Cheep Cheep]]s, [[Cheep Chomp]]s and [[Eep Cheep]]s, were among the creatures that could be caught in the Fishing Tourney #12 for ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp|Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp]]'' on March 14, 2019, as part of the ''Super Mario'' crossover event for [[Mario Day]]. A Blooper balloon attached to a [[Brick Block]] could also be obtained as special furniture after reaching the 1,500, 1,800, 2,200, or 2,800 cm milestone.

==In other media==
====''Monopoly Gamer''====
In ''[[Monopoly Gamer]]'', a Blooper face appears on the "Power-Up Die". A player who rolls a Blooper can normally steal two Coins from any other player. Some characters have a "Power-Up Boost" that enhances or changes this power.
