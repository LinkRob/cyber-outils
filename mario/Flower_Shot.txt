'''Flower Shot''' is [[Princess Daisy]]'s [[Special Shot]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The Flower Shot is performed by tapping out a triangle twice, just like for other female characters' Special Shots in ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''.

Daisy begins the shot by dribbling the ball in a triangle, while a small field of flowers grows at her feet. She then spins slowly into the air and magically casts the yellow-glowing ball into the hoop. For added effect, lightly colored petals trail the ball, and large flowers bloom when the shot goes in. If a character touches Daisy while she performs Flower Show, they lose their [[coin]]s.

If Daisy uses Flower Shot outside the 3-pointer line, the visuals are not changed, but a teammate needs to [[jump]] to catch the ball and dunk it.
