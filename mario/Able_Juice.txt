The '''Able Juice''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and the [[remake|Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)]].

==Description==
[[File:AbleJuice-Sprite-SMRPG.gif|left|Able Juice's sprite.]]
Upon using Able Juice, it restores any abnormal conditions (Fear, Silence, Mushroom, Scarecrow, Sleep, Poison) during battle. Able Juice is rarely found in [[? Block]]s and as rewards. It appears as a blue mug with a straw and a little star.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
An Able Juice is sold in several [[Item Shop]]s, as early as the one in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], at a price of four [[coin]]s. Able Juices can be sold for two coins each.
