'''Powert''' is a character in ''[[Mobile Golf]]''. He is a muscular golfer from [[Egypt]], and his artwork shows him riding a camel.

==In ''Mobile Golf''==
Powert is the champion of the third tournament whom the player has to win against in single mode. Powert is one of the four human golfers players must face in four singles tournaments.
