The '''Chancellor''' is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He is the advisor to [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], a role that would be taken over by [[Toadsworth]] in later games.

==Appearance==
[[File:Chancellor SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
The Chancellor is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with green spots on his head. He also has a brown mustache and wears a green jacket.

==History==
The Chancellor will give [[Mario]] the World Map allowing him to view other places.  Later in the game, the Chancellor refuses to let Princess Toadstool leave due to her being kidnapped by [[Bowser]] earlier. This is due to his over-protectiveness of the princess.
