A '''Turtley Leaf''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are only found in [[Petalburg]] in a bush near [[Kroop]]'s house.

==Appearances==
=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
[[File:TurtleyLeaf_PMTTYD.png|frame|left]]
Eating a Turtley Leaf by itself only restores 3 [[Flower Point|FP]]. The main advantage of a Turtley Leaf is that it is used in a lot of dishes.
{{br|left}}
=== ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ===
The Turtley Leaf reappears in ''Super Paper Mario''. Turtley Leaves are dropped by [[Koopatrol]]s or [[Koopa Troopa]]s. The Turtley Leaf instead lessens the amount of damage the user takes by half for ten seconds.

==Trivia==
*The Turtley Leaf is very similar to the [[Koopa Leaf]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
