'''Valley Fortress''' is a [[level]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. It is the last [[fortress]] level of the game, specifically located in the [[Valley of Bowser]]. It can be accessed after completing [[Valley of Bowser 2]], and completing the Valley Fortress unlocks the [[Back Door]]. Like other fortress levels, Valley Fortress has four [[Reznor]]s to fight at the end.
