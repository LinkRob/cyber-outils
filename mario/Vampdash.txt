'''Vampdash''' is a character appearing in [[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope|''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'']]. It is found in the Pristine Peaks Secret Zone, and is one of many [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Sparks]] who can be recruited.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Vampdash is a somewhat tall purple Spark who rides a purple bat-shaped leaf. As with all sparks, he has rabbit ears straddling the tuft at the top of his body from his Luma half, and his face resembles that of a Rabbid, with bulging blue eyes and a mouth.

=== Powers and Abilities ===
Vampdash is able to use the Vamp Dash ability, which increases the damage his user deals with with their dashes, and applies a [[Lifesteal|life drain]] effect to the attack, allowing the user to heal themself by dashing through enemies. The user also gets the Athlete power with him in their possession, increasing the damage done by move abilities. Players can feed Vampdash [[Star Bit|Star Bits]] to upgrade his powers.

Vampdash's user deals damage with their dashes equal to the base damage multiplied by a number equal to 1.3 + his level/10, and healing 39% the base damage + an additional 3% * his level, and their move ability damage is increased by 10% + 5% * his level. (For example, at Level 1, Vampdash applies a 1.4 multiplier to his user's dash attack and they heal by 42% the base damage, and move ability damage is incrased by +15%. These increase to 1.5x, 45%, and +20% when Vampdash's Level reaches 2, 1.6x, 48%, and +25% when his Level is 3, and so on.)

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Vampdash is called "Vamp Slider" (''Vanpu Suraida''; written ヴァンプスライダ), referencing his life draining power and how the heroes perform a slide during their dash attacks. In Chinese, he is referred to as the "Soaring Blood-Stealing Star" (''Dào Xuè Biāo Xīng''; written 盜血飆星). In Spanish, he is called ''Vampiacelerón'', from ''vampiro'' ("vampire") and ''acelerón'' ("dash"). In Portuguese, he is called ''Velodracul'', from ''veloz'' ("fast") and the character Dracula. In Italian, he is called ''Vampiscívolo'', from ''vampiro'' ("vampire") and ''scívolo'' ("slide"). In French, he is called ''Vampirush'', from the English words "vampire" and "rush." German translates his name as ''Drakularasa'', from the mythical vampire Dracula and the verb ''rasen'' ("to go faster than permitted," "to race").
