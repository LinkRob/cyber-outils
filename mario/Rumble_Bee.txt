The '''Rumble Bee''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Rumble Bee.

== Description ==
In ''Kingdom Battle'', Rumble Bee resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack with a medium range. The Rumble Bee deals around 55 to 65 damage. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Honey (status effect)|Honey]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Rumble Bee as an option among the Blasters. After completing the [[Red Coin]] challenge and unlocking the Battle HQ, Mario can obtain this blaster.
