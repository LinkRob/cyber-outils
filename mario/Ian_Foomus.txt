'''Ian Foomus''' is a character that appears in the remake of ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Appearance==
Ian Foomus is a [[Little Mouser]] that is purple in color. He also wears a grey cap that has a green pencil on top of it and carries a blue book.

==History==
Ian Foomus can only be found at the [[Trouble Center]] in [[Rogueport]] where he offers to give out advice on how to complete certain troubles. However, he always requires a coin payment in order to give away his information. According to [[Goombella]], Ian Foomus doesn't help out others willingly and only does so if they pay him beforehand.

==Etymology==
Ian Foomus' name is a play on the word "infamous." This is referencing on Ian referring to himself as "the infamous info mouse."

==Trivia==
*Ian Foomus is very similar to [[Wonky]] as they are both characters that charge 5 coins to reveal information. Ian Foomus himself was also added as a way to help players solve troubles if they are having trouble with them.
