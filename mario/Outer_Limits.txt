The '''Outer Limits''' (also known as the '''Space Byway''') is a location in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is located deep within [[Outer Space (Super Paper Mario)]] and serves as the main setting for Chapter 4-3.

==History==
===Chapter 4-3: The Gates of Space===
The heroes make it back into [[Outer Space|space]] and [[Squirps]] informs everyone that they are finally in the Space Byway. He says they'll make it to the [[Whoa Zone]] from here but [[Tippi]] doesn't believe him fully due to the odd name. Squirps says to just trust him and they all head on forward. Eventually, they come across a [[Space Gate]] that can only be opened by placing Squirps in the spot. The heroes soon reach one where it requires two Squirps and Squirps soon grows hungry and is too hungry to move from the spot. Mario and the others are forced to head out into space and find the [[Twinkle Mart]] to buy a [[Sweet Choco-bar]]. After giving Squirps one, he multiples into two and opens the Space Gate. The heroes head on and finally make it to the Whoa Zone.
