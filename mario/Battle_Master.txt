The '''Battle Master''' is a Toad who appears in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake of Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]].

== In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
The {{PAGENAME}} is found in various locations across the game. Talking to him you can ask for him to give you battle strategies or practice battle skills.

When practicing battle, you can practice guarding and action commands for moves.
