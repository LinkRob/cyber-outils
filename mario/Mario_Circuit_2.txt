'''Mario Circuit 2 '''​is the final track for the [[Mushroom Cup]]. It belongs to [[Mario]], hence the name.

== Layout ==
In ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'', it has a hazard, [[Oil Slick]]. On the first and second turns, there are coins. The next turn to the left, straight ahead are 12 [[Dice Block]]s. The next turn to the left, the right side of the road, there is [[coin]]s behind the [[Oil Slick]]. Turning left and right, there are three coins by the edge. Oil Slick is straight ahead while the right turn has coins lined up in a zig-zag. The 135'''° '''degree turn has coins on the left while the 45'''° '''turn has a ramp where players can go over and the coins are on the right edge. The right angle turn to the left, straight ahead is the finish line.

===Mario Kart: Super Circuit===
The course returns in the game for the [[Extra Flower Cup]] as the first course. It sets in daytime while the coins are placed on different spots.

=== Mario Kart 7 ===
It is the second course of the [[Banana Cup]]. The place sets on the sunset while other coins are being placed. The course has a [[Glide Ramp]], while on the left turn, you can do a trick on the ramp while gliding.
