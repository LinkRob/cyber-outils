'''Wanna Slice?''' is one of [[Wario-Man]]'s [[microgame]]s featured in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. There is also a microgame of the [[Wanna Slice? (WarioWare: Touched!)|same name]] in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', also featured as Wario-Man's microgames.

==Gameplay==
Using the [[Wii Remote]], the player must select every pizza slice so that they can be eaten. One of the slices is made of metal, and it cannot be eaten. If the player does not eat every slice before the timer expires or if they select a metal slice, they lose at the microgame.
