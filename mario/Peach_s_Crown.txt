'''Peach's Crown''', or '''Crown''', is an object worn by [[Princess Peach]] in a majority of her appearances. It occasionally has a role of being a lost item that [[Mario]] has to retrieve.

==In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''==
[[File:Crown SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
The Crown, as it is called in-game, is one of the items that Princess Peach loses during her forced wedding with [[Booster (character)|Booster]]. The Crown is eventually discovered to be on Booster's head.

==In ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''==
When [[Grape (character)|Grape]] invaded the [[Sparkle Theater]], Peach lost her tiara again, as it was flying out of the theater, but [[Toad]] caught it while he and a couple other [[Toad (species)|Toads]], and a couple of [[Theet]]s were blasted out of the entrance, before the doors were locked. In the end, after defeating the [[Sour Bunch]] and Madame Grape, Toad returned it to Peach during the credits.
