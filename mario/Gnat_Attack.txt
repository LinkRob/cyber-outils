'''Gnat Attack''' is a minigame first appearing in ''[[Mario Paint]]'', and the goal is to swat as many [[Fly|flies]] as possible. Gnat Attack has also appeared in the {{series|WarioWare}} and in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as of version 1.2.0.
