A '''Final Smash''' is a special move in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, first appearing in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. It is generally used by fighters when they hit the [[Smash Ball]]. The Final Smash can deal high knockout depending on the percentage like [[Mario Finale]]. Some Final Smashes defend the player such as [[Peach Blossom]].
