'''Mr. Game & Watch''' is a character appearing in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. 

== Appearance ==
Mr. Game & Watch looks nearly identical to his video game counterpart. He is a black silhouette of a character with a large nose, sometimes with a hammer or a parachute.
