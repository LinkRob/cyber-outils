'''Wario's Ladder''' is a minigame that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player must guide [[Wario]]'s icon to the gem at the end of the path. There are five rows of wires and each one have certain connections to other wires. Wario's icon will follow down and across the wires until it reaches it's destination. The player, at the start of the minigame, has either 10 or 15 seconds to draw extra lines to guide Wario's icon to the treasure before it goes off on it's own. The player can also press the "OK" button if they feel they have the correct path created before the time is up. The player must be careful not to guide Wario's icon to broken wires or it will result in a loss of this minigame.
