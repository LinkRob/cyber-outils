The '''Peach Tart''' is a [[recipe]] which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Peach_Tart_TTYD.png|thumb|left]]
The Peach Tart made when [[Zess T.]] mixes a [[Peachy Peach]] and [[Cake Mix]]. This dessert randomly gives the player a [[Sleep]]y, [[Dodgy]], or [[Electrified]] status in battle.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', the same recipe is used by [[Dyllis]]. A Peach Tart instead restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (status effect)|poison]].
