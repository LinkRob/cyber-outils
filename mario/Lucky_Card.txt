The '''Lucky Card''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'', exclusively appearing in Minigame Match mode.

==Description==
Using the item triples the chance of the user's minigame being selected.

==Flavor text==
''Triples the chances of playing the minigame you selected.''
