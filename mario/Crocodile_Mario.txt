"'''Crocodile Mario'''" is the fiftieth animated episode of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the live-action segment "[[Rowdy Roddy's Rotten Pipes]]."

==Synopsis==
When Koopa steals a town's magical statue that repels crocodiles, Mario and Luigi have to retrieve it and save the citizens of Outback from the hungry crocodiles.

== Plot ==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] arrive in [[Down Under Land]] in search of a [[Magic Statue]] that could help ward off [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]].  However, as soon as they arrive, [[Kangaroo Koopa]] (King Koopa's alias during the episode) arrives and steals the magic statue (which looks like an animated version of {{w|Paul Hogan}} in his Crocodile Dundee character).  With the magic statue gone, the crocodiles of the area start attacking the city.  Mario and Luigi manage to fight off one crocodile but there are several more.  The Princess, Toad and the rest of the inhabitants of the city are forced onto the roof of the General Store while the Mario Brothers have to fight off the rest of the crocodiles.

Outnumbered, the Marios use a plunger as a pogo stick and leap away to the swamp to find Kangaroo Koopa, which they must do soon because the crocodiles are slowly chewing the General Store away. Finding a raft, the Marios go down the river but are soon attacked by a group of [[Birdoroo]]s. Unfortunately, Kangaroo Koopa notices the Mario Brothers coming and has his [[Koopa Troopa]]s throw boomerangs at them. To make matters worse, Mario and Luigi are heading straight for some rapids.  They crash but Mario manages to get himself and his brother to shore.

Back in the city, the crocodiles have nearly chewed the general store away, forcing the refugees to hide on a tree.  Of course, the crocodiles start chewing on that too.

The Marios are in trouble, as they have no way of finding Kangaroo Koopa.  Fortunately, they spot a herd of crocodiles running from something. Mario deduces that the crocodiles are running from the magic statue and they follow the crocodile tracks to Kangaroo Koopa.  Unfortunately, when they take the statue, Luigi trips and alerts him to their presence. The Marios play "Koopa in the Middle" and keep the statue away from Kangaroo Koopa.

At last, the statue is brought back to the town and given to the Princess. The Princess replaces the statue on its pedestal and it scares off the crocodiles. Unfortunately for Kangaroo Koopa he happens to be in their way and is chased off.  With Kangaroo Koopa gone and the Town saved, Mario wants to throw "a pizza on the {{w|barbecue|barbie}}."

== Trivia/Goofs ==
* Princess Toadstool was not voiced by [[Jeannie Elias]] in this episode.
* The episode's title is a parody of the 1987 film ''{{w|Crocodile Dundee}}''.
* Everything in Down Under Land is upside down, and is a parody of Australia.
* The eggs on the Marios' raft inexplicably disappear and reappear from time to time.
* The size of the statue keeps changing, in some scenes its smaller than Mario and in others, it's as big as King Koopa.
