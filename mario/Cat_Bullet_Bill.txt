'''Cat Bullet Bills''' are enemies that first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. They are [[Bullet Bill]]s after being powered up with a [[Super Bell]].

==Physical description==
Cat Bullet Bills have the base design of a Bullet Bill, except they have black and white cat ears and a tail. Their upper half is colored black, whilst the lower is white. They home in on [[Mario]] or another playable character, much like [[Missile Bill]]s or Bullet Bills from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]/[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''===
[[File:CatBulletBill.png|thumb|left|100px]]
Like normal [[Bullet Bill]]s, Cat Bullet Bills are fired from Bill Blasters (making a meowing sound effect while doing so). However, unlike normal Bullet Bills, Cat Bullet Bills will lock onto, and follow the player, making them similar to the Missile Bill. However, unlike Missile Bills, Cat Bullet Bills always follow a horizontal path; they cannot travel vertically.

Cat Bullet Bills will sometimes drop a Super Bell when defeated. In the level The Bullet Bill Express, [[Cat Banzai Bill]]s appear, which act as red Banzai Bills.

=== [[Super Mario Maker 2]] ===
They appear in ''Super Mario Maker 2'' as enemies that can be added to courses, where they replace [[Missile Bill]]s in the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' theme.

== Trivia ==
* While a Cat Bullet Bill appeared on the early box art for ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'', they were removed on the later box art and do not appear in the released game; instead, they were replaced with [[Missile Bill]]s.
