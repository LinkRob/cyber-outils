'''Cleftor''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a [[Hyper Bald Cleft]] with its own name and personality.

==Personality==
Cleftor's personality is the exact opposite of [[KP Pete|King K]]'s. Cleftor doesn't care much about making friends as he considers everyone else opponents that need to be defeated in battle. Cleftor doesn't think much about [[Mario]] and even believes that Mario defeating his team was of pure luck on their end. However, Cleftor soon grows to respect Mario, especially after he saves the world.

==History==
Cleftor is only seen at the [[Glitz Pit]] of [[Glitzville]] where he stays in the minor league. He is the leader of his team, the [[Punk Rocks]].
