==Layout==
This level is very similar to Spiky Spike Bridge. However, spikes will come up on the bridge if the player is in the searchlights from [[Searchlight Sneak]] and will remain there until the player leaves the range of said searchlights, making this the only hybrid level. In this case, the level is a hybrid of Spiky Spike Bridge and Searchlight Sneak.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Near the end of the first section, to the left of the nearby [[Fire Bro]], is a bridge. The player must traverse it and climb up the pillar to reach the first green star.
*'''Green Star 2''': After the [[Checkpoint Flag]], the player must climb up a nearby pillar guarded by a Fire Bro. in order to claim the second Green Star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Near a large spiked platform, a bridge made of [[Donut Block]]s leads to it.
