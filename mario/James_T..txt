'''James T.''' is the younger brother of [[Jimmy T.]] in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. He first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' along with his sister, [[Jamie T.]], although neither of them have made any major appearances. James's mother is [[Mama T.]] and his father is [[Papa T.]].

==Physical description==
James wears an orange afro wig, a blue vest, a white shirt, red pants, and white, pointed shoes. He is shorter and chubbier than Jimmy.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
James is first seen in Jimmy T.'s stage [[Dance Club Rub]] along with his other family members, dancing at [[Club Sugar]]. James hosts his own [[microgame]] set, James's Mix, featuring [[Kat and Ana]]'s, [[Ashley]]'s, and [[Dr. Crygor]]'s [[microgame]]s.
