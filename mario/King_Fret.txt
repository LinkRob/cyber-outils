'''King Fret''' is the king of [[Jewelry Land]], the father of [[Prince Pine]], and a friend of [[Princess Peach]] in ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
King Fret wears a white robe with the red outline. He wears a yellow crown and has yellow hair.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Safari''===
In ''Yoshi's Safari'', King Fret and Prince Pine are kidnapped by [[Bowser]] as part of his attempt to take over Jewelry Land. Bowser also steals twelve [[magic gem]]s, splitting the kingdom into the [[Light Realm]] and the [[Dark Realm]]. Peach sends [[Mario]] and [[Yoshi]] to help, and they rescue King Fret in the [[Light Realm]]. He then asks them to save Prince Pine, who was still being held captive in the Dark Realm. After their Kingdom is saved, King Fret is last seen alongside Prince Pine thanking Mario and Yoshi and bidding them farewell as they returned to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

==In other media==
===''Super Mario-kun''===
In ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' [[Super Mario-kun Volume 9|Volume 9]], King Fret's kingdom is affected by the chaos that is afflicting the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and everyone in his kingdom is turned into an electric appliance: he himself is turned into a walking television, while his son is turned into a [[Super Scope]]. [[Luigi]] and [[Birdo]] are also affected and turned into robotic heads; while Mario and Yoshi go to solve the situation, Fret keeps Luigi and Birdo company by letting them watch TV on himself. Once the chaos is stopped, King Fret returns to normal.
