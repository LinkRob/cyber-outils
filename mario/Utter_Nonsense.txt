'''Utter Nonsense''', titled '''Soundalike Star''' in PAL releases, is a General [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Opening==
A roulette spins from the ten playable characters. After a few seconds, it stops on a specific character's image, with that character being the one whom the player has to imitate.

==Gameplay==
Like in [[The Choicest Voice]], the system's built-in mic has to be used to imitate the selected character's voice. Utter Nonsense is the only minigame that can only be played using two to four human players. Before the players have to attempt to imitate a character's voice, that character's voice clip is played, followed by a three-second countdown. Once each player has imitated a character's voice, the minigame rates the voice recording from a score of 0 to 100. Whoever gets the highest score is ranked the winner, and their imitation is played.

==Controls==
*Built-in mic: Imitate a character's voice

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''' (American English): ''Listen to the character's voice, and then perform your best impression.''
*'''Rules''' (British English): ''Listen to the character's voice and do your best impression!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is shared with the phrase "utter nonsense," meaning something false or silly
