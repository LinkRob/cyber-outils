|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'''}}'''Shell Slam''' is a special move used by [[Koops]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It can be used once Koops reaches the [[Ultra Rank]] and it requires 6 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==History==
To use Shell Slam, the player must rapidity press the control stick toward the left to fill up the gauge. Depending on how much the gauge is filled, Koops can deal up to 6 points of damage to all ground foes even piercing defense. This move can be very useful in dealing with strong, ground enemies with a lot of defense such as [[Dark Bristle]]s.

==Trivia==
*This move is similar to [[Parakarry]]'s [[Air Raid]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
