The '''Bowser Mobile''' is one of [[Bowser]]'s vehicles. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and returns in its [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. The Bowser Mobile resembles a purple spiked muscle car with a Bowser head on the front.

==Attacks==
*'''Drive Thru: '''Bowser only keeps the car in pilot mode while dodging many hazards or pits.

;With Bowser
*'''Soccer Bomb Throw: '''Bowser will throw soccer ball-shaped bombs to harm Mario. However, they can be thrown back at Bowser to damage him.(in other talking mode is actually the car)
*'''Big Jump: '''Bowser will make his car jump through pits or lava pillars or lava harming fences to not damage his car.
*'''Dash Mode-ON: '''After taking six hits the car will be severely bashed, and Bowser will make the car go faster.
