A '''Bullet Bill Orb''' is an [[Orb]] that appears in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. 

==History==
===''Mario Party 5''===
[[File:Bullet Bill Capsule.jpg|thumb|''Mario Party 5'' artwork]]
In ''Mario Party 5'', where the Orb is known as a '''Bullet Bill Capsule''', it can be thrown onto a [[space]] on the [[board]], or used on the player for five [[coin]]s. It allows players to ride [[Bullet Bill]] the number of spaces as determined by the [[Dice Block]], taking 30 coins from everyone in its path, making it the equivalent of the [[Bowser Suit]]. As a drawback, when players use this item, they cannot experience board events, nor can they choose their direction at junctions.

===''Mario Party 6''===
In ''Mario Party 6'', players can use a Bullet Bill Orb only on themselves, free of charge (it costs 20 coins, or 10 during a flash sale, at an [[Orb hut]]). The effect is still the same, except players steal only 20 coins from opponents; it also allows players to pass roadblocks without triggering them. Using a Bullet Bill Orb near the [[Star Space]] causes players to fly past it.
