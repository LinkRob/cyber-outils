'''Bounce Bros.''' is [[Luigi]]'s first [[Bros. Attack]] that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is learned alongside [[Splash Bros.]] in the [[Stardust Fields]]. The move can be activated during Luigi's turn. It costs four [[Bros. Point|BP]] in Mode 1 and Mode 2, but costs three in Mode 3.

In Bounce Bros., the move involves Luigi leaping onto [[Mario]], who throws him into the air. When Luigi is right above him, Mario leaps into the air to grab him. The two then do a flip and Luigi slams into the enemy with his hands. The buttons that must be pressed are {{button|gba|B}} to let Luigi hop on Mario, {{button|gba|A}} to have Mario jump and catch him, and {{button|gba|B}} again to have them both slam down.

It takes eight uses of Bounce Bros. before learning the Advanced Command. In the advanced command, Mario and Luigi separate, each tumbling toward an enemy. The commands are {{button|gba|A}} to initiate the attack, {{button|gba|B}} to separate the Bros., {{button|gba|B}} again when Luigi nears the enemy he's targeting, and {{button|gba|A}} again when Mario nears the enemy he's targeting. If there's only one enemy, the commands are the same, but the Bros. obviously will both target that enemy.
