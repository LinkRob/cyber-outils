'''Tiny''' is used in the name of a few different subjects:

*[[Tiny Kong]], a [[Kong]] who first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.
*[[Tiny (ailment)|Tiny]], a status effect from the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].
*[[Tiny (Mario Golf series)|Tiny]], a character from the [[Mario Golf (series)|''Mario Golf'' series]].
*[[Tiny Wario]], a form of Wario similar to [[Small Mario]].
