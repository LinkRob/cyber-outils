== Description ==
The '''Snow Area''' is a snowy area within the Shroom City. In that area, [[Mr. Blizzard]] and [[Penguin (character)|Penguin]] live there. The Snow Area has three different locations:

* [[Ice Game Room]]
* [[Ice Stadium]]
* [[Mt. Frostbite]]
