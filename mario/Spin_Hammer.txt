|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Spin Hammer''' is a move that [[Mario]] uses in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Mario can use this move once he gets the [[Super Hammer]] from [[Podler]] in Chapter 3.
==History==
To use Spin Hammer, the player must press and hold {{button|gcn|b}} to have Mario concentrate then spin the {{button|gcn|stick}} to have Mario twist up. Then, release the control stick to have him spin around. This will allow Mario to destroy stronger blocks.
