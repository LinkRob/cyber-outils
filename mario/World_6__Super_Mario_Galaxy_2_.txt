'''World 6''' is the sixth [[world]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. As with the other worlds, World 6 consists of six regular [[galaxy|galaxies]] and one boss galaxy. [[Bowser]] is fought as the boss at the end. From the world map, a large orange-red [[Black Hole]] is shown, but it is not shown to affect any of the galaxies on the map.

==Galaxies==
*[[Melty Monster Galaxy]]
*[[Throwback Galaxy]]
*[[Clockwork Ruins Galaxy]]
*[[Battle Belt Galaxy]]
*[[Slimy Spring Galaxy]]
*[[Flash Black Galaxy]] ([[Hungry Luma]])
*[[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]]

== Description ==
==== [[Melty Monster Galaxy]] ====
This galaxy is completely covered in lava, with a magma tide that rises and falls every so often. The main obstacle here is the magma and its tide. As the name suggests, the most common enemies in the galaxy include giant lava monsters known as [[Magmaargh]]s and [[Magmaw]]s.

==== [[Clockwork Ruins Galaxy]] ====
This galaxy takes place in a temple-like area with statues of many creatures. The galaxy includes new gimmicks such as rolling Millstones and walls that push the player out of the gravitational force. Pegs are also introduced in this level and are used to reach the Power Star.

==== [[Throwback Galaxy]] ====
This galaxy is a redesigned [[Whomp's Fortress]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. The music is a jazzy arrangement of the original [[Super Mario 64 Original Soundtrack|Super Mario 64 Main Theme]] as the original version was also played on [[Whomp's Fortress]]. Although the course is generally identical, a few minor changes have been made.

==== [[Battle Belt Galaxy]] ====
The galaxy is unique in that in order for [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to advance, he will need to defeat every enemy on a planet. The enemies start easily with foes such as [[Goomba]]s, Urchins, [[Li'l Cinder]]s, and [[Crabber]]s. However, the planets will get progressively more complex with enemies like [[Jack O' Goomba]]s, [[Boo]]s, [[Li'l Brr]]s and [[Undergrunt]]s.

==== [[Flash Black Galaxy]] ([[Hungry Luma]]) ====
This galaxy takes place in a large mansion. However, everything is pitch black except for the occasional second of lightning, which temporarily lights up the area. In addition, many gaps are in the ground. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is found here and the level makes extensive use of the [[Blimp Fruit]]. Common enemies and obstacles here include Mines, [[Jack O' Goomba]]s and [[Flaptack]]s.

==== [[Slimy Spring Galaxy]] ====
This level mostly takes place underwater. It makes extensive use of the [[Green Shell]] to move around underwater. Common enemies here include [[Snoodle]]s, Urchins and [[Boo]]s.

==== [[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]] ====
This is the final main galaxy in the game and where [[Bowser]] is fought for the final time. The galaxy uses elements from every part of the game such as [[Snake Block]]s and the [[Spin Drill]]. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] will also be needed to bypass certain obstacles, but he cannot be used in the final battle.

The power-ups found here are the [[Cloud Flower]], [[Blimp Fruit]], [[Spin Drill]] and [[Dash Pepper]]. The galaxy has many obstacles such as lava and electricity. Common enemies here include Magikoopas, Banzai Bills, Hammer Bros., Boomerang Bros. and Magmaarghs. Lumas are also found here, often cheering Mario on.
