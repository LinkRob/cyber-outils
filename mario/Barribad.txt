[[Dark Barribad]]}}A '''Barribad '''is an enemy that appears in [[Super Paper Mario|''Super Paper Mario'']]''.

==History==
It attacks in a way similar to the [[Yux]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''; by shooting round rings at the player. Barribads can also create a shield around themselves, which can passed by flipping in 3D with [[Mario]].

There are two strongers versions. One in [[Castle Bleck Interior]] called [[Sobarribad]]. And the other in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]] named [[Dark Barribad]].
