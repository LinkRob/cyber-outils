'''Forest of Perpetual Night and Terrible Dangers''' is a location in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[King Mario of Cramalot]]."

==Description==
The Forest of Perpetual Night and Terrible Dangers appears to be inhabited by several creatures, only ever shown by their eyes. Like haunted-themed areas in general, the trees of the Forest of Perpetual Night and Terrible Dangers have no leaves, but additionally appear very disfigured. There is a lake in the middle of the Forest of Perpetual Night and Terrible Dangers. The Forest of Perpetual Night and Terrible Dangers is near [[Cramalot Castle]].
