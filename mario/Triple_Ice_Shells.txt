The '''Triple Ice Shells '''are smaller versions of the [[Ice Shell]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. They, if thrown, behave similar to the [[Ice Shell]] except that they are three small Ice Shells and because of that they can hit three people in total.
