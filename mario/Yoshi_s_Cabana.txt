|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}'''Yoshi's Cabana''', also known as '''Yoshi's Shop''', is a location that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is an [[Item Shop]] run by a light blue [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] on [[Lavalava Island]] within [[Yoshi's Village]]. The inventory of Yoshi's Cabana mainly consists of items that were sold in previous Item Shops making it the first Item Shop in the process to not sell any unique items. Buying the [[Snowman Doll]] from here will be useful for the numerous of fire-based enemies in [[Mt. Lavalava]].
