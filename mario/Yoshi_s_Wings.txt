'''Yoshi's Wings''', also referred as '''Wings''', is a power-up which appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==In ''Super Mario World''==
Yoshi's Wings is a power-up that [[Mario]] and [[Yoshi]] can obtain in only a few levels. In doing so, Yoshi becomes a [[Winged Yoshi]], turns blue and flies up to [[Coin Heaven]]. After this, a [[Blue Yoshi]] becomes available for the next level. In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version, Yoshi's color does not change while he is wearing the wings.

== In [[Super Mario World (TV series)|''Super Mario World'' television series]] ==
Magic Wings, as they are called, appear in the episodes "[[Ghosts 'R' Us]]", "[[Born to Ride]]" and "[[The Night Before Cave Christmas]]". They have the same function as in ''Super Mario World''.
