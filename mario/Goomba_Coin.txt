A '''Goomba Coin''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
Goomba Coins can transform certain enemies into stronger forms. They can be obtained by defeating Goomba-type enemies or by winning them in the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] game in [[Toad House]]s.

A Goomba can be transformed into a 2-Goomba Tower by using three Goomba Coins, and it can be further transformed into a 3-Goomba Tower by spending six more Goomba Coins. A [[Bullet Bill]] can also be transformed by spending three Goomba Coins, specifically into a "Bullet Bill & Goomba," which itself can be further transformed into a "Bullet Bill & Goombas" by spending six more Goomba Coins.
