'''Balloon Burst!''' is a [[minigame]]s in ''[[Mario Party-e]]''. 

==Gameplay==
There is a [[Wario]] balloon, and players take turns inflating it using the {{button|gba|A}} button. When either player releases the button, the other player takes their turn inflating the balloon. Once the balloon inflates to a certain point, both the music and the background change. The balloon eventually pops, and whoever pops it loses at the minigame.
