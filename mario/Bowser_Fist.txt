The '''Bowser Fist''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It increases POW by 10 (30 in Japanese versions). It is dropped by [[Morton Koopa Jr.]] during his battle.
