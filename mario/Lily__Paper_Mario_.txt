'''Lily''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Physical description==
Lily is a large, pink [[Wikipedia:Water lily|water lily]].

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Lily lives in the far east area of [[Flower Fields]]. She is often worried and when [[Mario]] meets her, she is in deep thought of her stolen [[Water Stone]]. Mario must track down the stone in order to refill the water passes in the area. Once the water stone is placed in the pedestal by Lily, Mario can gain access to the [[Bubble Berry]], an item that can give Mario a short flotation power.
