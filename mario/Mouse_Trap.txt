'''Mouse Trap''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', featured as part of [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[Nature]] folder.

==Gameplay==
There is a mouse running from side to side, and the player has to drop the dome when the mouse is directly under it to trap it. If done successfully, they win at the microgame. If the player either does nothing or drops the trap and misses, they lose.

Each difficulty level makes the dome shorter and therefore harder to trap the mouse into.
