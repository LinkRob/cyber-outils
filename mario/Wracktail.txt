''"The ancients locked me in this wretched place! I am the wrathful god, Wracktail!"'' -Wracktail

'''Wracktail''' is an optional boss from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. He is the final fight of and is found at the bottom of the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Wracktail is very similar to [[Fracktail]] as they both have a similar attitude and they talk in very similar ways, except that Wracktail talks rather rational, unlike his counterpart, who maniacally shouts as a result of being manipulated.

<br />

== History ==
One key difference from Fracktail is his color. The fight, itself, is primarily the same, except this one's more difficult since Wracktail is ten times stronger. [[Frackle|Frackles]], Fracktail's very small minions who can mean trouble in large numbers, are replaced with the [[Wrackle|Wrackles]], who have the white and pink color scheme that Wracktail has rather than the red and yellow one that Fracktail has. Wrackles serve the same purpose as their counterparts, but are slightly stronger. Overall this fight is much easier than the final fight of the previous game's Pit of 100 Trials. Wracktail is replaced with a mysterious shadow creature for the Flopside Pit of 100 Trials.
<br />

== Trivia ==

* Unlike Fracktail, Wracktail didn't suffer from a programming error. Instead, he was programmed with the intention of being aggressive, once woken from his slumber.
