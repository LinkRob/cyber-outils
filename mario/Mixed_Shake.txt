The '''Mixed Shake''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Effects==

It is a juice with pieces of fruit in it. It is made by mixing [[Peach Juice]] with [[Mango Juice]]. This drink restores 20 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
