'''Hammerswing Hangout''', is the fourth level of [[Superstar Road]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by collecting every Star Coin in [[Frosted Glacier]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts on icy terrain with a few [[? Block]]s and a [[Warp Pipe]] leading inside a tower. Inside are two [[Hammer Pendulum]]s, swinging back and forth, and scrolling upward. Some platforms and a [[Fliprus]] are just above. After the first Star Coin, a [[Green Ring]] will appear. Some more platforms and Flipruses will be above. After more platforms, Flipruses and the second and third Star Coins, a [[Warp Pipe]] will appear, and it will lead the player to the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Near the beginning of the level, this Star Coin will be on the left near a Fliprus.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the Green Ring, the third Fliprus on the right is just below the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': To the left side, shortly after the second Star Coin.
