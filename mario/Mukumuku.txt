'''Mukumuku''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are gopher-like creatures only encountered on [[Star Hill]]. Mukumukus are commonly found poking their heads out on the overworld until they encounter [[Mario]] in a battle.

==Battle==
Mukumukus mainly attack by throwing several items at someone (similar to [[Croco]]'s [[Chomp (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Chomp]] move). Mukumukus can also use their own cookies named [[Muku Cookie]]s to summon more Mukumukus into battle.

===Strategy===
Mukumukus are weak against [[Fire (element)|Fire]], so Mario's fire techniques are very effective against them.
