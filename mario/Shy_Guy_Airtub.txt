The '''Shy Guy Airtub''' is a vehicle from the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'']] franchise. They first appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', but can be first battled as regular enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

== Description ==
In their debut appearance, Shy Guy Airtubs appeared as a small airships piloted by [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] with a single cannon on their front. ''Dream Team'' redesigns them to be slightly bigger and adds cannons on the side, with three Shy Guys apiece. This design returns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'' gives them two Shy Guys.

== Combat ==
Since Shy Guy Airtubs are considered to be background enemies, they do not appear on the field. They may randomly appear in a fight located in the [[Ultibed]] parts' areas.

Shy Guy Airtubs have two attacks, with their first attack being their main attack which is done on their turn and involves them opening fire with their side cannons. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must use their hammers to repel these [[Cannonball|Cannonballs]]. If they are destroyed by these cannonballs (or a [[Taunt Ball]]), the Shy Guy Airtubs crash into the ground in the background, and their [[Shy Guy]] passengers ditch the wreckage and join the fight.

Shy Guy Airtubs only use their second attack when the Bros. use a Taunt Ball to call them over. Here, the Airtubs fire cannonballs from their main cannons, one at each Bro. Before the attack, the Airtubs' Shy Guy pilots point at the Bros. The Bro. who gets pointed at by the most Shy Guys will be the first target. The cannonballs can be deflected with hammer strikes, but their spikes and size increase the damage taken from a botched counterattack; conversely, the Shy Guy Airtubs take more damage from getting hit by their own deflected cannonballs in this attack. If the Shy Guy Airtubs are defeated by blocking this move, they crash in the foreground, dealing damage to all other enemies that were in the foreground of the battle.

=== Statistics ===
Shy Guy Airtubs are of Level 27, with 123 hit points at full health, and their attack, defense, and speed are 245, 83, and 110, respectively. Destroying them yields 250 experience (300 with the Quick Level Rank-Up Bonus), 240 coins, an [[Ultra Candy]] in 4 out of 5 item drops, and a Deluxe Iron Ball Hammer in 1 out of 5 item drops.

== Other Appearances ==

=== In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'' ===
''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' features some Shy Guy Airtubs bringing [[Toadette]]'s [[Papercraft|Papercrafts]], which are destroyed by [[Papercraft Bowser]], though she gets her revenge by creating [[Papercraft Fire Mario]] using Fire Ore.

=== In ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ===
''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'' features some Shy Guy Airtubs fighting [[Zokket]]'s [[Zokket Army|forces]], but when [[Bowser]] sees [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] during his assault on the [[Zokket Army]], some of the Airtubs break formation to strike [[Shipshape Island]]. The Airtubs also appear during the fight with [[Glohm Bowser]] (a monster that was created when [[Zokket]] blasted [[Bowser]] with his Glohmatic Quad-Ray), and can be commandeered with [[Luigi Logic]], leading to a shooter segment. If Luigi tries to commandeer the vehicle while Glohm Bowser is still raging, it will destroy the Airtub, foiling the Luigi Logic. Their only attack is to fire cannonballs that have to be repelled with a hammer strike. In this game, Shy Guy Airtubs are of Level 36 with an Attack Power of 173 and a Speed of 133. The values for their health and defense are unknown. In this game, they do not drop rewards when destroyed.

== Variations ==
Shy Guy Airtubs have two variations that can be found later in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

=== Shy Guy Airtub R's ===
Shy Guy Airtub R's are only seen throughout [[Kamek]]'s third maze and the third map in [[Neo Bowser Castle]]. They have the same attacks as their weaker counterpart, but their colour is changed to purple, and their stats and those of their own Shy Guy pilots (who themselves are yellow) are stronger, with a Level of 35, 178 hit points, an attack power of 364, a defense of 119, a speed of 144, an experience drop of 500 (600 with Quick Level), and a loot drop with 160 coins, a [[Star Candy]] in 1 out of 5 drops, and a Deluxe Soft Hammer in 1 out of 20 drops.

=== Shy Guy Airtube Deluxes ===
During the final battle, [[Dreamy Bowser]] can summon Shy Guy Airtub Deluxes (Shy Guy Airtub DX for short). Shy Guy Airtub Deluxes look different from their weaker cousins, having a jet black paint job, and their front cannon is stylised to resemble [[Bowser]]'s head. When it appears, the Shy Guy Airtub Deluxe will chase Luigi who must dodge the fireballs launched from its front cannon. Mario (who gets furious at Dreamy Bowser during this attack) must attack the Airtub withh his hammer. Shy Guy Airtub Deluxes have the same background side cannon attack as the weaker versions, but with a middle cannon that serves as a distraction. The Shy Guy Airtub Deluxe is Level 36 with 240 as the hit point maximum, an attack power of 449, a defense, a defense of 288, a speed of 145, and no experience or loot drops.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Shy Guy Airtubs are called "Shy Guy Flying Boats" (''Heihō Hikōtei'', written ヘイホーひこうてい), since they are essentially flying boats. Their Spanish name, ''Helibote Chico Tímido'', literally means "Shy Guy Heliboat"; ''Helibote'' is a combination of English "boat" and ''hélice'' ("propeller"). Portuguese calls them ''Helibarco do Masquito'', with ''Helibarco'' coming from ''helicóptero'' ("helicpoter") and ''barco'', a synonym of ''nave'' ("boat"). In Italian, their name is ''Aeronave Tip Tímido'', meaning "Shy Guy Airship." In French, they are called ''Vaisseau Maskass'' ("Shy Guy Boat"). In German, Shy Guy Airtubs are called ''Shy Guy-Flugwanne'', or "Shy Guy Flight Tub."
