The '''Shiverian Elder''' is an elderly, race-loving Shiverian that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. He lives in Shiveria, also called the [[Snow Kingdom]], and is encountered in the Snowline Circuit hub.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Shiverian Elder is an elder who sits in a brown rocking chair and wears red glasses. He is larger than the average Shiverian, and his face has a moustache that goes below his chin. His outfit consists of the same wooly one-piece that his fellow Shiverians wear, although his hands are visible.

== In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' ==
When Mario first arrives at the Snowline Circuit, the Shiverian Elder states that the Bound Bowl Grand Prix cannot start without one more Shiverian Racer. [[Mario]] is then able to reach the dark room to his upper-left, where one [[Shiverian Racer|lonely Shiverian]] resides, who can be [[Capture|captured]]. Capturing the Shiverian and talking to the Shiverian Elder lets Mario enter the race as the Shiverian. After Mario wins, the Shiverian Elder starts referring to this specific Shiverian as a "Supersonic Snowman" to show his pride in the racer.
