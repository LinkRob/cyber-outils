'''Maple Super''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:Maple Super.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', [[Mario]] can have [[Tayce T.]] create a Maple Super by providing a [[Super Shroom]] with a [[Maple Syrup]] as the ingredients. The Maple Super combines the effects of both ingredients, meaning it restores 10 [[Flower Point|FP]] and [[Heart Point|HP]] each.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Mario can acquire a Maple Super by giving [[Zess T.]] a Super Shroom and a Maple Syrup, the same ingredients as in ''Paper Mario''. Maple Syrup also retains the same effects as before.
