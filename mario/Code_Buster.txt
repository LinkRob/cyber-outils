'''Code Buster''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'' as one of [[Orbulon]]'s microgames, categorized under the [[IQ]] folder, and later returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', in which it is one of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player must input a sequence of buttons indicated onscreen, varying between any of the D-Pad button or the A button, which themselves vary between each difficulty level. If the player presses the wrong button or holds the button in wrong order, they lose at the microgame.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', inputting the buttons is linked to a specific objective, which include unlocking a door in level 1, defeating a dragon in level 2, and destroying a gate in level 3.

==Trivia==
*This is the only microgame in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' and ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!'' to make use of the B button, although pressing it results in losing a microgame.
