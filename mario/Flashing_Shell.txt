A '''Flashing Shell''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]].

==Description==
A Flashing Shell is a type of [[Koopa Shell]] that quickly alternates between several colors. It is created whenever a yellow [[Beach Koopa|Koopa without a Shell]] climbs into an empty shell of any color. A Flashing Shell attempts to hit [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] by spinning around and dashing in his direction. A Koopa without a Shell cannot be ejected from a Flashing Shell.

Like with other Koopa Shells, the Flashing Shell can defeat enemies along the way, and even though a Flashing Shell cannot be shown, the player can still receive a combo in increasing [[point]] values for enemies successively defeated by a Flashing Shell and [[extra life|extra lives]] starting with the eighth enemy.

==In ''Super Mario World''==
Flashing Shells first appear in [[Donut Plains 4]]. If Mario or Luigi [[jump]] onto a Flashing Shell, they bounce upward, but this does not destroy the Flashing Shell. A Flashing Shell can only be destroyed by either a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] eating it, a [[Spin Jump]], [[Starman|invincibility]], or [[Cape Mario|Caped Mario]]'s Body Press maneuver. If a Yoshi eats a Flashing Shell, regardless of their color, he gains all three shell abilities—[[sand cloud]]s, [[Fly (move)|flying]], and [[Fire Breath|breathing fire]]—for as long as the Flashing Shell remains in his mouth.

During [[Fall]], just like with other Koopa Shells, Flashing Shells are redesigned to resembles Mario's face, but in the case of Flashing Shells, it still quickly alternates between several colors.
