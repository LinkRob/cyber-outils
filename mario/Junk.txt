'''Junk''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Junk is located in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. Its owner and sole employee is [[Paratroopa (character)|Paratroopa]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
If the player's character enters Junk while not on a specific mission, Paratroopa informs the player that the store has not yet opened.

Junk has a notable role in during two quests. In [[Nerd Force V]], the player's character has to buy a [[Toad Force V Figure]] so that they can be allowed into the [[TFV Fan Club]]. In [[Bestest Buds]], the player's character has to choose between a [[Bracelet]], a [[Necklace (Mario Party Advance)|Necklace]], and a [[Ring (Mario Party Advance)|Ring]] as the best gift for [[Bowser]].
