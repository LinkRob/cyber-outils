'''Tall Order'''<ref name="NintendoTreehousePt2">Nintendo. (June 15, 2018). [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EVaIpD8q79Y Super Mario Party Gameplay Pt. 2 - Nintendo Treehouse: Live | E3 2018] YouTube. Retrieved September 9, 2018.</ref> is a [[2 vs. 2 Minigame|2-vs-2]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The background takes place at [[Frosted Glacier]].

== Opening ==
The cutscene shows two players standing on the ice shaving objects that are on top of a cloud. Two [[Penguin|penguins]] move the metallic bowl under the players.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to create a tall ice sculpture in order to win the minigame. However, the penguins are slightly moving the bowl, players must shave the ice as fast as possible but also time themselves. After thirty seconds passed, ice sculptures are measured.

== Ending ==
The team's screen takes the whole space. The team does their victory animations while the other team can be heard sulking.
