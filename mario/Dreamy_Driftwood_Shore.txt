'''Dreamy Driftwood Shore''' is a location that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It is where [[Mario]] and [[Dreamy Luigi]] meet the [[Seadring]]s for the first time. It is the [[Dream World|dream world]] version of [[Driftwood Shore]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
Mario heads into Dreamy Driftwood Shore with [[Princess Peach]] as an attempt to hide her from [[Antasma]] and [[Bowser]]. However, they quickly realize that the area is too small and they meet a Seadring named [[Seadric]]. Seadric says that in order to help expand Dreamy Driftwood Shore, they need to collect [[Dream Egg]]s from his other siblings. Mario, joined by Dreamy Luigi, go out and gather the Dream Eggs from [[Seabelle]], [[Seatoon]], and [[Seabury]]. Seadric then eats all of the Dream Eggs and helps expand the area to its limits. As Mario and Dreamy Luigi head deeper, it was revealed that Princess Peach was actually [[Kamek]], which means that the real Princess Peach is already with Bowser. Mario and Dreamy Luigi soon find themselves fighting [[Elite Trio|Bowser's "elite" troops]]: [[Private Goomp]], [[Sergeant Guy]], and [[Corporal Paraplonk]]. Mario and Dreamy Luigi then defeat the troops where [[Kamek]] angers at them, demotes the trio and leave the dream world. Disappointed, [[Prince Dreambert]] tells Mario to leave the dream world to come up with another plan.
