A '''Zess Dynamite''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Zess Dynamite is a battle item created by [[Zess T.]] herself, as its name would suggest. It can be made from cooking a [[Coconut Bomb]] and an [[Egg Bomb]] together.

== Effects ==
The Zess Dynamite is a very powerful item and using it summons an image of Zess T. who then unleashes a large explosion dealing seven points of damage to every enemy on the screen. This make it a very useful item and one of the most powerful in the game.
