'''Talkie Walkie''' is a 1-vs.-3 Mic mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object for the solo character is to reach the [[treasure chest]] at end, while the team has to try and prevent them from reaching there. The player has to say commands into the [[Nintendo GameCube Microphone|Mic]] (or select from a blue window if a Mic is not connected) for their character to move, while the teammates try to throw [[Spiny Egg]]s at the solo player. The solo character wins if they manage to reach the treasure chest, but the team wins if either the solo character is hit by a Spiny Egg, the solo character fails to reach the treasure chest within the 60-second [[time limit]], or if the solo character falls off the stage.

==Controls==
===Solo===
* "Forward": Move character forward
* "Move back": Move character backward
* "Right": Move character right
* "Left": Move character left

===Team===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Move the cursor
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Throw a [[Spiny Egg|Spiny Ball]]

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''One player speaks commands to reach the goal. The other three will try to hit them with a Spiny!''
* '''Advice 1''': ''The solo player should watch the other players' cursors to know where to move next.''
* '''Advice 2''': ''The solo player can't take orders while moving. When your character stops, speak another order into the Mic.''
