'''Bowser's Bird Cage''' is Bowser's first prison in the mid-credits scene of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

The cage was fire proof and contained only a tiny piano for company.

It was later replaced by his [[Bowser's Miniature Castle| playhouse-sized castle]].
