The '''Big House''',<ref>(June 10, 1998). [https://web.archive.org/web/19980610065405/http://www.nintendo.com/n64/super_mario64/15-2.html Course 15 - Star 2: The Big House in the Sky]. ''Nintendo: Super Mario 64 Strategy'' (Internet Archive: Wayback Machine). Retrieved March 15, 2022.</ref> also referred to as the '''Cloud House''',<ref>''Super Mario 64'' Player's Guide, pages 119 and 120.</ref> is a large floating building located in [[Rainbow Ride]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
The Big House can be reached by riding the [[magic carpet]] to the right of the course's starting point, then riding the carpet to the right of the spinning [[platform]]s, and then riding the carpet to the right at the end of the second carpet's path. On the Big House balcony is a [[warp]] that brings the player to the top of the maze of Rainbow Ride. Beneath the magic carpet's path next to the balcony is a wooden bridge, and a [[Star Switch]] is at the end of it in the DS version. Inside the Big House are a short stairway, a black-and-white checkerboard floor, a large piece of wood with five [[coin]]s on it, and a fireplace that acts like a [[flamethrower]], as well as a [[Cap Block|red block]] containing a [[Power Flower (Super Mario 64 DS)|Power Flower]] in ''Super Mario 64 DS''.

The magic carpet that leads to the Big House takes the player through it twice. The player can avoid the fireplace flame by staying to the back of the carpet until the flame goes out, then going to the carpet's leading edge. After going through the building the first time, the carpet takes the player to two large blue blocks around the building. The player must [[jump]] onto the blocks to avoid being pushed off into the [[pit|abyss]] and then return to the carpet before it disappears. On top of the first block is a circling [[Amp]], and on top of the second one are five coins. The player can collect five more coins on the carpet's path right before it goes through the Big House the second time. As the carpet goes through the Big House again, it takes the player to the roof. On the roof are the [[Power Star]] for the [[mission]] [[The Big House in the Sky]] and a [[! Block|yellow block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom|1-UP Mushroom]], as well as a [[Star Sphere]] for the mission [[Switch Star of the Manor]] in ''Super Mario 64 DS''.
