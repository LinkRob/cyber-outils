'''Remo Con''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are strange, puppet-like creatures only found in [[Booster Tower]]. Remo Cons have stronger variants called [[Puppox]]es.

==Battle==
In battle, Remo Cons attack mainly by dropping [[hammer]]s at a party member. They are also able to use [[Body Slam (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Body Slam]] to deal much more damage and [[Eerie Jig]] to transform someone in [[Mario]]'s party into a [[scarecrow]].

===Strategy===
Remo Cons are weak to [[Fire (element)|fire]]-elemental attacks, so Mario's fire techniques such as [[Fire Orb]] and [[Super Flame]] are very effective against a Remo Con.
