'''Stay Tuned''' is one of [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must tune the instrument by aligning it in the red zone. If done successfully, they win the microgame, but if this is not done within the [[time limit]], they lose.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels involve tuning a guitar, a bass, or a violin respectively, each requiring more tuning than the previous instrument.

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name is a pun on the phrase "stay tuned," a phrase used to encourage a viewer to keep watching a program or series for follow-ups.
