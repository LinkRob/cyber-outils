'''Pipe Cannons''' are a type of [[Warp Pipe]] which appear in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], especially the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
[[File:SMW Yoshi's Island 1 Pipe Cannon.png|thumb|left|220px|Fire Mario shooting out of a Pipe Cannon in ''Super Mario World'']]
Throughout their appearances, Pipe Cannons generally have the same appearance as a Warp Pipe, and are told apart from their difference in functionality. Whenever a character enters a Pipe Cannon, the Pipe Cannon shoots them out for a certain distance to another area. In ''Super Mario World'', a Pipe Cannon has a different appearance in that it is larger, thicker, and always slanted.

''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' features a transparent type of Pipe Cannon known as a [[Clear Pipe Cannon]], which is attached to the end of certain [[Clear Pipe]]s and behaves more like the ''Super Mario World'' variant. Additionally, when a character enters a [[Cloud Cannon]], a small Pipe Cannon appears on top of the Cloud Cannon and blasts the character up to a [[Coin Heaven]].
{{br|left}}
==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario World''===
This version of Pipe Cannons cannot be directly entered, and [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] are instead shot out of one after they exit a certain area. Two [[small pipe]]s act as a supporting structure for a Pipe Cannon, which launches anyone shot from them at an angle.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''===
Mario or Luigi can now enter the Pipe Cannon to be blast off to a different area, usually one that is otherwise inaccessible, or to collect [[coin]]s or sometimes even a [[Star Coin]] that cannot be reached otherwise. Some Pipe Cannons, such as in [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6]], adjust which direction they are facing before launching Mario or Luigi.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''===
Pipe Cannons act the same as in ''New Super Mario Bros''. In the minigame [[1-up Blast]], [[cannon]]s similar to a Pipe Cannon can be found. except their aim can be adjusted manually, and the purpose is for a character to shoot out at the balloons, each worth the number of [[1-Up]]s shown on them, as they make their way out.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''===
Pipe Cannons act the same as in previous ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' series installments. They are also used to reach the [[Rainbow Course]].
{{sectstub}}

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' / ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' / ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
Pipe Cannons make yet another reappearance, retaining their functionality from earlier games. Aside from appearing in levels, Pipe Cannons also appear on the overworld, one in each world, and the player's character can use them to more quickly travel to any previously visited worlds.
