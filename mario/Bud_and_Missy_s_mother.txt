'''Bud and Missy's mother''' is a character who makes a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Mush-Rumors]]." She is the wife of [[Norman]] and the mother of [[Bud]], her son, and [[Missy]], her daughter. Bud and Missy's mother is the only character within their group who does not have a given name. She and her family are from the in-universe real-world location of Wichita, [[Kansas]], and during a road trip, they accidentally end up in the [[Mushroom Earth]] when Norman drives into a [[Warp Zone]].
