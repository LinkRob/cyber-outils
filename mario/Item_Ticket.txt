The '''Item Ticket''' is a collectible in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' which gives the driver access to items.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Item Tickets can be used in races. The button for the Item Ticket is available if the player does not have an [[Item Box]]. Once the player uses the Item Ticket, the driver will gain items depending on how many slots the driver has. player can have The player can use the Item Ticket once per race.
