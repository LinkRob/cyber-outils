'''Trompettes''' are a type of enemy in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|Super Mario Bros. Wonder.]]

{{Infobox_Enemy|name=Trompette|games=Super Mario Bros. Wonder|related=[[Goomba]], [[Mumsie]]}}

Trompettes are weak and walk back and forth along the ground. They have many different heights, and when stomped on, they fall.

There are two types of Trompette, brown Trompettes and purple Trompettes. Brown Trompettes walk off cliffs while purple ones turn around.

Brown Trompettes first appear in Bulrush Coming Through! They also appear in the The Sharp Trial: Launch to Victory and POOF! Badge Challenge Crouching High Jump I. Purple Trompettes appear only in Bulrush Coming Through! and POOF! Badge Challenge Crouching High Jump I.

== Trivia ==
According to Nintendo<ref>https://www.nintendo.com/jp/topics/article/f6d2505a-dd79-410d-b350-b2b2036213f0</ref>, the heights of Trompettes grow as they get older.
