'''Fawfulcopters''' are an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They resemble [[Fawful]], and they have a top section with Fawful eyes and a saucer bottom. It has a retractable mouth on the bottom, where it comes out from a rod. 

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
Fawful often uses a Fawfulcopter to communicate to [[Bowser]] in various points during his journey. Fawfulcopters also appear in [[Princess Peach's Castle|Peach's Castle]], where they hold keys to opening certain doors in the castle. Bowser must chase the Fawfulcopters to get their keys. Upon being caught, a Fawfulcopter hands over their key and flees. As enemies, Fawfulcopters are encountered in [[Bowser Path]].

===Battle===
A Fawfulcopter appears in battle alongside [[Chain Chawful]]s, in which the Fawfulcopter floats on the top screen. Bowser can [[inhale]] a Fawfulcopter, allowing [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to fight the Fawfulcopter inside [[Bowser's body]].

Instead of attacking, a Fawfulcopter flashes a red light to summon more Chain Chawfuls. The Fawfulcopter also uses this move inside of Bowser's body, although it does not summon any enemy. A Fawfulcopter can escape Bowser's body if Mario and Luigi do not destroy it in two turns. If a Fawfulcopter appears while Mario and Luigi are battling a Chain Chawful, they cannot attack the Fawfulcopter, and they instead have to quickly defeat the Chain Chawful before another one is summoned by the Fawfulcopter.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''==
Aside from appearing as enemies in the ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' remake, Fawfulcopters are also very common enemies in the ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' mode. They take critical damage against [[Swooper]]s, [[Broozer]]s, and red [[Para-Biddybud]]s. A Fawfulcopter's [[Special Skill]] is [[Discord]], which can lower the [[Attack|POW]] of whoever takes damage from the attack.
