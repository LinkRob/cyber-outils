|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Switch Shock Circus''' is the first level located in [[World Flower (Super Mario 3D World)|World Flower]] of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The level is a remixed version of [[Switch Scramble Circus]] and shares many of the same elements, although having more enemies within the stage.
==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': In a [[Mystery Box]] behind the starting location. The player must turn on four switch panels on the wall to earn the Green Star.
*'''Green Star 2''': The second Green Star can be collected by obtaining all eight [[Green Coin]]s from the [[Green Ring]].
*'''Green Star 3''': At the end of the level, floating right of the ledge. The player must use a baseball or a Boomerang Flower to obtain this Green Star.
