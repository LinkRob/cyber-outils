A '''Bracer''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
The Bracer is a drink that can boost defense by 100% for a single battle making it a very good item to have. However, Bracers are very rare to get as they can only be purchased at a Frog Coin shop and by defeating certain enemies.
