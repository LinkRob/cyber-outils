==Description==
Donut Lifts are named for their round shape (except for the top) with a hole in the middle. If [[Mario]] or another character stands on a Donut Lift for more than one second, it drops below. Because of this, the character must constantly [[jump]] when crossing Donut Lifts to prevent them from dropping. Sometimes, a playable character has to drop with a Donut Lift to reach otherwise unreachable lower platforms or collectible items like [[Star Coin]]s. Donut Lifts change color while being stood on, typically to red or white. Donut Lifts first appeared in the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' series but have also appeared in a couple ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' games as well. In the 3D games, Donut Lifts have more of an aligned 3D appearance but still retain their traditional behavior.

==Mainline appearances==
===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:SMB3 NES 5-5 Screenshot.png|thumb|left|Donut Lifts as seen in [[World 5-5 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 5-5]].]]
In ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', Donut Lifts are commonly found in [[level]]s of [[Sky Land]] and [[Pipe Land]]. If stood on, a Donut Lift begins to shake, and if Mario or [[Luigi]] does not get off of the Donut Lift after one second, it falls into a [[pit]] below. Because of this, Mario or Luigi has to jump as much as possible while crossing the Donut Lifts. Donut Lifts do not regenerate after falling.

In ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', there are [[Spiky Lift|spiky variants]] in [[Bowser's Last Stand]] that function identically to Donut Lifts, except they are covered in [[Spike (obstacle)|spikes]].

===''Super Mario 64''===
[[File:Donut Lift Skies.png|thumb|right|[[Mario]] on the Donut Lifts in ''Super Mario 64''.]]
Donut Lifts return in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', where they are named Falling Blocks.<ref name=sm64/> They have a longer, almost rectangular shape compared to previous appearances. Falling Blocks appear only in [[Rainbow Ride]], where Mario must [[jump]] over most of them while riding a [[magic carpet]] on a rainbow trail. There are also Falling Blocks that function as a bridge to the area with the [[pyramid platform]]s. If stood on, a Falling Block descends about a few meters before disappearing. Unlike in ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', Mario has to distance himself from a Falling Block for it to regenerate.

===''Super Mario 64 DS''===
[[File:Donut Lift Rainbow Skies.png|thumb|left|[[Luigi]] hops across the Donut Lifts in ''Super Mario 64 DS'']]
In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', Donut Lifts return with the same role as in the original ''Super Mario 64'', except their wooden texture is more prominent.
{{br|left}}
===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:NewDonutLifts.png|thumb|left]]
[[File:Donut Lift DS.png|thumb|[[Luigi]] on some Donut Lifts in ''New Super Mario Bros.'']]
Donut Lifts make another appearance in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. They are commonly found in a few levels of [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3]], [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 4]], [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]], and throughout [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]]. Donut Lifts retain their core function, shaking then falling after being stood on by Mario or Luigi for a second, but unlike before, they turn red when stood on, making it more evident when they are activated. Like in ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', once a Donut Lift falls, it never regenerates. Mario or Luigi has to ride partway down on some Donut Lifts to reach certain [[Star Coin]]s. [[Mini Mario]] can safely traverse across Donut Lifts without triggering them to fall.

===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[File:Donut Lift Plateau.png|thumb|left|Mario and his friends around some Donut Lifts in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.]] 
Donut Lifts reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. Unlike in the first ''New Super Mario Bros.'', Donut Lifts now regenerate a few seconds after falling. Donut Lifts are commonly found in castle levels and are featured during the boss battle in [[World 5-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5-Castle]]. They also appear in various levels of [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7]].

There is also a variety named [[Donut Ledge]]s, which are yellow bars with grooves on their ends, allowing Mario or another playable character to hold on them similarly to a [[Cliff Ledge]]. When Mario walks over or hangs on them, they work identically to Donut Lifts, turning red for a second before falling. Donut Ledges are found only in [[World 4-2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4-2]].
{{br|left}}
===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
[[File:Donut Lifts 3D.png|thumb|right|Mario jumps at some Donut Lifts in ''Super Mario 3D Land''.]]
Donut Lifts are seen again in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. In this game, they appear in various sky-themed levels, such as [[World 3-3 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 3-3]] and [[Special 5-1]]. Donut Lifts retain their design from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' but have a rubbery appearance rather than wooden. Donut Lifts are often very close together and, as usual, descend shortly after they are stood on, requiring [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to run across them immediately. A Donut Lift that has been stood on specifically descends to the bottom of the level and then respawns in its original location afterward. There is also a rock variation with the same effect.

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:Gold Donut Lift Caves.png|thumb|left|Mario near some Donut Lifts in ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''.]]
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', Donut Lifts can be found in several levels and retain their functionality from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''. This game also introduces a new variety of Donut Lift which is longer, despite being larger, these Donut Lifts function almost identically to their smaller counterparts.

The game introduces a glacial variety named [[Icy Donut Lift]]s. Other than being light blue and slippery from being covered in [[ice]], they share every characteristic of the normal Donut Lifts.

===''New Super Mario Bros. U'' / ''New Super Luigi U'' / ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[Image:Donut Lift Sky Beach.png|thumb|right|The Mario Crew near some Donut Lifts at the Beach Skies.]]
Donut Lifts return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. In these games, they retain their functions from the previous games, turning red upon being stood on and then descending after some time, only to respawn shortly after. [[Kamek]] also uses them in his battles (in the levels [[Slide Lift Tower]] and [[Stone Crush Tower]]) by firing spells at the solid floor Mario can stand on. When Kamek's magic projectiles hit the solid floor, they may turn it into Donut Lifts. Donut Lifts are also found in sky-themed levels, such as [[Above the Cheep Cheep Sea]]. For the first time in the series, [[Mini Mario]] can also cause Donut Lifts to descend by standing on them.
{{br|left}}
===''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''===
[[File:SM3DW World3-2.png|thumb|left|Donut Lifts in the level Chain-Link Charge in ''Super Mario 3D World'']]
Donut Lifts are seen again in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' as well as ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. They retain their appearance and function from ''Super Mario 3D Land'', but they now turn red when stepped on, similar to their appearance in the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' games. Once a Donut Lift descends to the bottom, it will disappear and respawn back in it's original location. The heroes will need to either run across the Donut Lifts or jump around them to survive. Donut Lifts are commonly found in sky-themed levels, such as [[The Great Goal Pole]], but they also appear in various other levels.

In ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'', several sections are composed mostly of Donut Lifts, requiring [[Mario]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] to run quickly to make it through.

===''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS''===
[[File:Donut Lift Classic.png|thumb|Mario using the Donut Blocks to reach the lower paths in ''Super Mario Maker''.]]
Donut Lifts (here named '''Donut Blocks''') reappear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', with the same functions as in the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' series. For the first time in any game of the ''Super Mario'' series, [[Thwomp]]s and [[Super Thwomp]]s can trigger Donut Blocks to fall.

Additionally, as Donut Blocks are absent in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World]]'', they have been given new sprites for those styles. Their ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' sprites have been updated, sporting a lighter color scheme.

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', Donut Blocks retain their role from the original ''Super Mario Maker''. In the added ''Super Mario 3D World'' style, Donut Blocks are completely solid and cannot be jumped through, and they take on a more square design than the original game the style is based on.

===''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''===
Donut Lifts return in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', with the same behavior as before. Wider versions with various widths also appear, all behaving the same as the standard block.
