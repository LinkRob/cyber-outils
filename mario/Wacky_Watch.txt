The '''Wacky Watch''' is a rare item found only in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Description==
Upon use, the item resets the turn counter to five turns remaining. This usually shorten the games, although it lengthens it if used within the final five turns. Even if a Wacky Watch is activated on the final turn, the current turn still treats certain conditions as if it were the last turn, such as [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shops]] remaining closed.

The Wacky Watch can only be obtained via a [[Hidden Block (Mario Party series)|Hidden Block]]. It is the only item of the game that cannot be obtained from an Item Shop or received from an [[Item Bag]].

==Profile==
*'''Manual description''' (page 23): ''To turn back time or set it forward, use the Wacky Watch to jump to the last five turns in the game.''
