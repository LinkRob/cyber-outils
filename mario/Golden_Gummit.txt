'''Golden Gummits''' are a variant of [[Gummit]]s and an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
Golden Gummits, like Gummits and [[Spiky Gummit]]s, only appear in only in [[Freezy Flake Galaxy]], during the mission [[The Chimp's Skating Challenge]], and in [[Shiverburn Galaxy]], during the mission [[The Chimp's Ultimate Skating Challenge]]. Golden Gummits are more rare than regular Gummits, and retreat into their hole more quickly. Upon defeating a Golden Gummit, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] earn fifty [[point]]s.
