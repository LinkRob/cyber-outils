'''Spoiled Rotten''' is the first boss of ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Spoiled Rotten resembles a large round eggplant creature. She has youthful facial features with a sleepy expression, wears sneakers and carries a doll. While she is angered, Spoiled Rotten has large, veinous eyes and a wide mouth filled with fangs.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
[[File:WL4-Eggplant Warrior Screenshot.png|frame|left|[[Wario]] battling Spoiled Rotten and the two [[Eggplant Warrior]]s.]][[File:WL4_SpoiledRotten_Map.png|thumb|300px|Map of the level]]
Spoiled Rotten guards the [[Entry Passage]]. [[Wario]] faces her straight after the first level, the [[Hall of Hieroglyphs]]. The arena appears to be some kind of kitchen, with an oven and well-stocked shelves of food. There is also a television with a [[Nintendo GameCube]] on the left side of the field.

Spoiled Rotten is accompanied by two [[Eggplant Warrior]]s that Wario must defeat. To attack Spoiled Rotten, Wario can simply hit her by [[Dash Attack|ramming]] its body. After hitting the creature seven times, Spoiled Rotten's appearance turns savage. In this state, Wario cannot hit her from the front, as this will cause damage to him. Instead, Wario must jump over the boss and hit her in the back. Once she is defeated, Wario can access any of the four main [[Wario Land 4#Passages and levels|Passages]].

The time given to defeat the boss varies on the [[Wario Land 4#Game mode|game mode]].

*[[File:WL4-Normal Mode Sprite.png]]: the time given is 1:00.
*[[File:WL4-Hard Mode Sprite.png]]: the time given is 0:30.
*[[File:WL4-S-Hard Mode Sprite.png]] (unlockable): the time given is 0:15.

Unlike other bosses in the game, Spoiled Rotten cannot be fought again after beating the game.
