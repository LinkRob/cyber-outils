'''Secret Chippie Decoder Ring''' is an item in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Chippie Chipmunks]]."

==Description==
It is a special brown, plastic ring shaped like a chipmunk's head, and it is worn by prestigious members of the [[List of implied organizations#Chippie Chipmunks|Chimpmunks]] group, such as [[Mr. Gibbel]], who is never seen without it.
