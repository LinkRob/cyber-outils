The '''Cricket Pie''' is a special item featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the [[Toad Town|Mushroom Kingdom]]'s item shop using [[Mallow]]'s [[Rare Frog Coin]]. The Cricket Pie can then later be given to [[Frogfucius]], who gives Mallow the [[Froggie Stick]] weapon in exchange.
