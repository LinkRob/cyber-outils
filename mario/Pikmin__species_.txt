'''Pikmin''' are a small plant-based species from the franchise of the [[Pikmin|same name]]. They have appeared in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media, including crossovers such as the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]].

==Background==
Pikmin are a plant-animal hybrid species that inhabit the planet [[w:c:nintendo:PNF-404|PNF-404]]. They spend time buried in the ground as [[Sprout (Pikmin franchise)|sprouts]] before they are [[pluck]]ed out of the ground by a [[leader]] to be used for certain tasks such as battles.

==History==
===''Luigi's Mansion'' (Nintendo GameCube)===
In ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' for the [[Nintendo GameCube]], Pikmin are seen in a preview trailer included within the game. It is technically their first appearance in a game, predating their formal debut in the first ''Pikmin''. They do not retain this appearance in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]] or even the [[Nintendo Classics]] port on [[Nintendo Switch 2]].

===''Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour''===
[[File:MGTT Pikmin.jpg|thumb|200px|Pikmin flying outside of yellow flowers in ''Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour'']]
In ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]'', Pikmin make a cameo appearance where if the [[golf ball]] lands in a patch of flowers, then red, yellow, and blue Pikmin fly out.

===''Super Mario Maker''===
In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', Pikmin is one of several [[Costume Mario|costumes]] that [[Mario]] can take on in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' style. This costume in particular shows a Red Pikmin, a Blue Pikmin, and a Yellow Pikmin, the only three types in the first ''[[w:c:nintendo:Pikmin (game)|Pikmin]]'' game.

While the costume is in effect, ''Pikmin''-related sound effects can play. The "[[Player Down (Super Mario Bros.)|Player Down]]" tune is replaced by the Pikmin's own death cries, and the "[[Course Clear (Super Mario Bros.)|Course Clear]]" fanfare is replaced by the tune that plays after obtaining an important item in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Pikmin 3|Pikmin 3]]''. Pressing  causes the red and blue Pikmin to [[Maturity|mature]] into flowers. When underwater, all three Pikmin are encased in a [[bubble]].

===Unused appearances===
A group of Pikmin and Olimar were originally planned to appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' whenever a new type of coffee was made at the [[Starbeans Cafe]]. He would call Mario 'Marlio', an anagram of Olimar, and mistake Luigi for a dangerous beast and order his Pikmin to attack him.
