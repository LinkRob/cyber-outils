The '''Wedding Ring''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Wedding Ring is lost by [[Francesca Pianta]] in [[Keelhaul Key]]. Francesca Pianta says that she does not want to leave the island without it, and counts on [[Mario]] to find it. Mario eventually finds the Wedding Ring near the cave.

Later in the story, [[Frankie]] posts a trouble in the [[Trouble Center]]. He asks Mario to find his missing Wedding Ring from earlier. Mario finds it for him and earns the [[Gold Card]].
