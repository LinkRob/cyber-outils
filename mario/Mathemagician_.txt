'''Mathemagician!''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The quest is initiated by [[Mechakoopa (character)|Mechakoopa]] at the [[Mushroom Library]]. He introduces himself as a mathematician, prompting the player to ask for some questions about math. Mechakoopa then poses the player three questions based on simple arithmetic, promising to give the player a [[Gaddget]] if they answer all three correctly. Each question is presented alongside three potential answers, and the player must choose the correct answer from the options. Once the player correctly answers all questions, Mechakoopa is overjoyed by their perfect score, calling them a "prodigy," and rewards them with the promised Gaddget, [[Castle Night]]. However, if the player incorrectly answers any of the questions, Mechakoopa kicks the player's character out of the library, forcing them to restart the quest.

The quest concludes with Mechakoopa explaining that most of the residents in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] obtained a Gaddget after they fell from the sky, and he wishes the player luck as they attempt to collect every fallen Gaddget, finishing the quest.
