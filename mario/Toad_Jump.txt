'''Toad Jump''' is a minigame in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Gameplay ==
[[Toad (character)|Toad]] has to [[jump]] over enemies, such as [[Spiny|Spinies]], on his way to the end goal. Toad runs by default, and the only control is blowing into the system's Mic to make Toad [[jump]]. The height and distance in which Toad jumps depends on how long the player is blowing into the Mic.
