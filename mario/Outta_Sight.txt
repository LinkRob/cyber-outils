|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Outta Sight''' is a special move used by [[Lady Bow]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It also serves as her field technique and it also requires 2 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Usage==

When used in battle, Outta Sight allows Lady Bow to hide [[Mario]] for a turn letting him avoid all enemy attacks. This is also useful against enemies or even bosses that uses a very strong move on the next turn. However, the only negative side to this is that Lady Bow loses her turn to attack on the next turn. As a field move, Outta Sight will allow Mario to pass through barriers while moving on conveyor belts. Outta Sight also hides Mario from enemies allowing him to sneak on by and avoid a battle.

==Trivia==

*Outta Sight is a pun of the words "out of sight" which may allude to the phrase "out of sight, out of mind".
*[[Veil]], which is [[Vivian]]'s technique, is very similar to Outta Sight which will later appear in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' as both moves allows for Mario to hide safely from enemies and characters.
*The [[Pixl]], [[Slim]], from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' also does something similar as he virtually makes Mario and the others invisible while turning sideways.
