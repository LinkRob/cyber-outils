'''Spear Thicket''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Spear Thicket is an area consisting of tall, dark green blades of grass. Two trees are visible in the near background.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Spear Thicket is in the [[Jungle Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It is where [[Spear Guy (character)|Spear Guy]] is found. He is interested in dueling characters who have a mustache, so if either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] visits Spear Thicket, Spear Guy wants to duel them, but if either [[Princess Peach]] or [[Yoshi]] visits, Spear Guy dismisses them because neither of them have a mustache. If Mario or Luigi accept Spear Guy's challenge, this starts the [[Mustached Hero!]] quest. During the minigame, either Mario or Luigi faces off against Spear Guy in the minigame [[Tank-Down]], and the reward for winning is the minigame itself.
