A '''Koopa Zapper''' is a hazard featured in [[Koopa Cape]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. A few of them are specifically found within the underwater tunnel. Although the course reappears in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', Koopa Zappers do not.

It is a zapper with a [[Green Shell]] in the center and three electrical prods that rotate in a clockwise formation. If a character touches a Koopa Zapper, they spin out and temporarily shrink to a smaller size, like what the [[Lightning]] item does.
