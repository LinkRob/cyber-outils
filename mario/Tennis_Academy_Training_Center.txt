The '''Tennis Academy Training Center''' is a training center which first appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]] and returns in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. It is located on the campus of the [[Royal Tennis Academy]]. In the latter game, several minigames can be played here.
