'''Dodge Balls''' is one of [[Wario]]'s [[microgame]]s featured in the [[Intro Games]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player has to move a car around an arena and avoid being squished by a few boulders, which have a white and black-patterned appearance, giving them a soccer ball-like appearance. Levels 1, 2, and 3 feature two, three, and four boulders to avoid, respectively.
