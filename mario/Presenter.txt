'''Presenters''' (also simply referred to as '''Moles''') are a friendly species of [[Monty Mole]] appearing exclusively in the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They can be found all over the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] typically appearing in closed off areas or high platforms that [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must work their way to. When [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] reach a Presenter, the Presenter will congratulate them for making it all the way to his location and give them a prize (which often times is a very rare item such as a [[Gold Mushroom]] or a [[1-Up Super]] or even a piece of Gear or a Badge). The Plumbers will often times need to use their abilities to reach a Presenter, typically Presenters can be found in underground caves (such as 2D pseudo-scrollers) but sometimes they can be found aboveground as well.

In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] remake, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', Presenters are replaced by bow-tie-wearing [[Monty Mole]]s which retain the same role as the Presenters in the original.

==Locations==
Here is a list of the locations and rewards for the 11 Presenters found throughout the game:

===Presenter #1:===
Requirement: Bros. Hand Power Moves (specifically [[Fire Tickle]])

Reward: [[Golden Mushroom]]

Location: [[Hoohoo Village]] - Knock over the [[Ankoopa]] using the [[Fire Tickle]] to find this Presenter.
===Presenter #2:===
Requirement: [[Ultra Hammer]]s

Reward: [[Max Nut]]

Location: Base of [[Hoohoo Mountain]] - Use [[Ultra Hammer]]s to smash the black rocks, clearing the way to this Presenter near the entrance of [[Guffawha Ruins]].
===Presenter #3:===
Requirement: Bros. [[Hammer]] Moves

Reward: [[Golden Mushroom]]

Location: [[Beanbean Fields]] - West of the entrance to [[Beanbean Castle Town]], [[Luigi]] can burrow under a fence after performing a [[Luigi Dunk]]. Complete the sidescrolling course inside the cave to find this Presenter.
===Presenter #4:===
Requirement: Hand Powers

Reward: [[Red Pepper]], [[Green Pepper]]

Location: [[Beanbean Fields]] - South of the entrance to [[Teehee Valley]], find a hidden section from the northeast corner. Grow the plant roots into flat trees by drinking water and tickling [[Mario]]. One of the trees leads to this Presenter on a higher ledge.
===Presenter #5:===
Requirement: Bros. [[Hammer]] Moves

Reward: [[1-Up Mushroom]]

Location: [[Beanbean Fields]] - South of the entrance to [[Teehee Valley]], enter a cave in the northwest corner. [[Hammer]] Mario into [[Mini-Mario]] to pass through a small hole and complete the sidescrolling course to find this Presenter.
===Presenter #6:===
Requirement: Bros. Hammer Moves

Reward: [[High-End Badge]]

Location: From Presenter #5, backtrack to the last moving platform as Mini-Mario and follow the overhead path eastward to reach this Presenter in the hidden niche.
===Presenter #7:===
Requirement: Hand Powers

Reward: [[Wool Trousers]]

Location: [[Beanbean Fields]] - South of the entrance to [[Yoshi Theater]], ignite the electrical orb with Luigi's [[Thunderhand]] Power to access a cave. Use [[Simu-Block]]s to create bridges and reach this Presenter.
===Presenter #8:===
Requirement: Bros. Hand Power Moves

Reward: [[Iron Pants]]

Location: Beanbean Outskirts - West of the area south of the entrance to [[Teehee Valley]], complete a series of obstacle courses, leading to a cave. Hammer Mario into [[Mini-Mario]], pass through a small hole, and complete the sidescrolling course to find this Presenter.
===Presenter #9:===
Requirement: [[Ultra Hammer]]s

Reward: [[1-Up Super]]

Location: [[Oho Ocean]] - Use Luigi's surfboard to reach the northwest corner of the surface. Fall into the [[Whirlpool]] and swim south to find a [[Yellow Pipe]] that leads to a lone island where this Presenter awaits.
===Presenter #10:===
Requirement: [[Ultra Hammer]]s

Reward: [[Red Pepper]], [[Green Pepper]]

Location: [[Joke's End]] - Travel west from the [[Save Album]] and use [[Spin Jump]] to follow the moving [[Whirlwind]] west through the air. At the end, you'll find this Presenter.
===Presenter #11:===
Requirement: Access to [[Bowser's Castle]] at the end of the game

Reward: [[1-Up Super]]

Location: [[Bowser's Castle]] - Past the door to [[Lemmy Koopa]]'s room, continue east where the [[lava]] pool resumes. Use the moving whirlwind to guide you east through the air, and you'll discover this Presenter.

==Trivia==
*With their sunglasses and wide smiles, Presenters strongly resemble smaller versions of [[Mega Mole]]s.
*If the Plumbers talk to a Presenter again after already earning the prize, the Presenter will call them "greedy" before declining to give them anything more.
*It is widely assumed that the [[Monty Mole]]s in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' are actually a redesign of Presenters (similar to how the [[Mawful Mole]]s in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' were redesigned to more closely resemble regular Monty Moles) however it is confirmed that these are actually regular [[Monty Mole]]s and not Presenters.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20210810004852/https://play.nintendo.com/news-tips/tips-tricks/mario-luigi-superstar-saga-bowsers-minions-tips-tricks/</ref>
**Similarly, the [[Ankoopa]]s from the original ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' were replaced with [[Gargantua Koopa Troopa]]s in the [[Reissue|remake]].
