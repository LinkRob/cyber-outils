'''Wonder Seeds''' are a collectable item in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. Each of the Royal Seeds is simply different color that are corresponded to each of the world, Blue: [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]], Turquoise: [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]], Red: [[Shining Falls]], Yellow: [[Sunbaked Desert]], Green: [[Fungi Mines]], Purple: [[Deep Magma Bog]], Orange: [[Petal Isles]], and White: [[Special World (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Special World]].

==Description==
A Wonder Seed can be collected during a [[Wonder Effect]] or from a [[Poplin]]. Collecting a Wonder Seed causes a [[Wonder Effect]] to end.

Each normal level contains at least two Wonder Seeds. One is given after completing a level and the other is found after triggering the [[Wonder Effect]], which ends after the Wonder Seed is found. Secret exits contain a third Wonder Seed.

Levels without a [[Wonder Flower]] contain only one Wonder Seed. The last level of each world gives the player a [[Royal Seed]], which they can use to defeat [[Cloud Piranha Plant|Cloud Piranhas]] surrounding [[Castle Bowser|Wonder Bowser]].

There are a total of 225 Wonder Seeds in the game, and collecting all seeds will award a medal to the player's  save file.

== Trivia ==
[[The Final Battle! Bowser's Rage Stage]] is the only level with no Wonder Seeds.
