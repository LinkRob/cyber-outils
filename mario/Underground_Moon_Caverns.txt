}}The {{Nihongo|'''Underground Moon Caverns'''|
地下の月の洞窟|Lunam sub cavernis|lit. '''Moon Caverns'''}}, . is a location in the [[Moon Kingdom]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It is a vast realm on the Moon. It's the place set to hold a cave in the [[Moon Kingdom]], it also holds a bunch of [[Power Moon|Power Moons]] and [[Regional Coin|Regional Coins]].The Cave also contains a bunch of Enemies to Capture.

==Description==
The Underground Moon Caverns is a Spooky Cave Located in a open wall near the "Quiet Wall" Checkpoint, Head North from the Checkpoint and you will slide to the Underground Moon Caverns Location, It's Also Located in the Upper Ledge after you used the Trampoline, Once Done, There should be a Door in the upper ledge, Which will lead you to the Madame Broode Battle after you've Completed the Main Battle, There's Not Much To describe for now.

==Trivia==
*The Place When Mario Captures Bowser is actually based off of Underground Moon Caverns after you've completed Bowser's Fight and Harder Fight.
