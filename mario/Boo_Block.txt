'''Boo Blocks''' (also called '''Boo Buddy Blocks''' or '''Block Boos''') are enemies which first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. They are [[Boo]]s with the ability to turn into blocks.

==In ''Super Mario World''==
Boo Blocks are only encountered in some of the [[Ghost House]] levels. Similar to regular Boos, Boo Blocks will only approach [[Mario]] when his back is turned. However, when Mario is looking at them, the Boo Blocks will take the form of a solid block that Mario can actually stand on. This is required to clear a certain Ghost House.
