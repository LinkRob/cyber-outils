'''Secret Scroll 2''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== History ==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can go to the second room to the right of the [[Hammerhead Bros.]]'s new home. A [[Thwomp (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Thwomp]] requests the Mario Bros. to break one of the three stones. The Secret Scroll 2 appears after winning the minigame. Upon reading the scroll, the brothers learn the [[Cyclone Bros.]] technique.
