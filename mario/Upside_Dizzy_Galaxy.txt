The '''Upside Dizzy Galaxy''' is a galaxy in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is located in [[World 5 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 5]]. This galaxy is similar to the [[Rightside Down Galaxy]] from [[World 1 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 1]], but is more difficult.

===Green Star 1===
The first [[Green Star]] is located about halfway through the level in the main planet. At this section, the walls are blue (i.e. gravity pulling down), but patches of the wall with gravity pulling up make it possible for Mario to jump to the left into the blue section, and Wall Jump to reach the Power Star.
