'''Speedy Spin''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Found at [[Rowf's Badge Shop]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]], [[Mario]] will find this badge on sale for fifty [[coin]]s. This badge requires one [[Badge Point|BP]] to use.

Equipping the Speedy Spin allows Mario to cover greater distance with the [[Spin Dash]]. It can prove useful for traveling around places quicker and avoiding enemies.
