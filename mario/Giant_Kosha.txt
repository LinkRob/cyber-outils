==History==
The Giant Kosha is a large [[Kosha]] that is found at the highest point of [[Crystal Caves]]. The Giant Kosha will, at times, smash it's large club on the ground causing boulders to rain down and damage the Kongs. Only [[Tiny Kong]] is able to defeat the Giant Kosha as her [[Monkeyport]] ability is the only way to reach it. The Monkeyport pad that leads to the Giant Kosha is located within an igloo that can only be broken after [[Chunky Kong]] obtains a specific [[Golden Banana]]. Once Tiny gets up there, she can easily take out the Giant Kosha with her [[Saxophone Slam]]. Once the Giant Kosha is taken out, it will not respawn freeing Crystal Caves of the constant earthquakes.
