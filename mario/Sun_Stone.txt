The '''Sun Stone''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Sun Stone is used alongside the [[Moon Stone]] to gain access to [[Hooktail Castle]]. They are both located in the [[Shhwonk Fortress]].
