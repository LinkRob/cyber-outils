'''Ace''' is one of the tennis players competing at the [[Island Open]]. She is a playable character in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]'' in the story mode.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Ace is a brown-haired girl who wears a red and white sporting outfit.

=== Personality traits ===
Ace is a tomboy with a short temper.

=== Powers and abilities ===
* [[w:c:powerlisting:Tennis Combat|'''Tennis Combat''']]: Ace is skilled in tennis.

== Biography ==
{{sectstub}}

== In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour'' ==
Ace is a playable character in ''Power Tour''. In the story mode, the player can choose either Clay or Ace as the main character. If she is chosen as the playable character, Clay will be her partner vice versa.
