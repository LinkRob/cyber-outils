'''The Trouble with Toads''' is the second level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 19.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 2 [[Sharpea]]s, 3 [[Spike]]s, and 3 [[Fire Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Fire Bro.

The 2nd battle contains 1 [[Rex]], 4 Sharpeas, 3 Fire Bros. The enemy captain is a Sharpea.

The final battle contains 3 Sharpeas, and 5 Rexes. The enemy captain is a Rex.

== Possible recruits ==
* Fire Bro (Lv: 19-20)
* Spike (Lv: 19-20)
