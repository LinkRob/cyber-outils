'''Danger High and Low''' (or '''World 4-3''') is the third level of World 4 in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. 

==Layouts==
<tabber>
|-|Pink Coins = The level starts with a single [[Buzzy Beetle]]. The player can kick it into a [[Piranha Plant]] and the [[Muncher]]s below. Soon the player will reach the first Pink Coin atop some [[Brick Block]]s, accessible via wall jumping. The player will then enter a [[Warp Pipe|warp pipe]], and approach a series of falling Buzzy Beetles. The second Pink Coin is momentarily afterwards, just above a row of Brick Blocks; the player must jump over the Piranha Plant and then ledge-grab to reach it. Another warp pipe appears, followed by a Buzzy Beetle shell that the player can kick. The player will pass three Piranha Plants and promptly reach the third Pink Coin atop some Brick Blocks. Another warp pipe is entered, which leads to an area with [[trampoline]]s and even more Buzzy Beetles. The fourth Pink Coin is found immediately afterwards atop a row of Brick Blocks between some back arrow blocks over some Munchers. After entering yet another warp pipe, an [[! Switch]] is encountered, and if hit, it will drop many Buzzy Beetles. The fifth and final Pink Coin can be found immediately after the second ! Switch, and the player simply has to jump to get it. The player will then enter one last warp pipe and jump across some platforms to the [[Goal Pole]].
|-|Purple Coins = The level starts with a single Buzzy Beetle. The player can kick it into a Piranha Plant and the Munchers below. Soon the player will reach the first Purple Coin, which they can reach by jumping off of a Buzzy Beetle. The player will then enter a warp pipe, and approach a series of falling Buzzy Beetles. The second Purple Coin is momentarily afterwards, just between two rows of [[? Block]]s and Brick Blocks; the player must jump over the Piranha Plant and then jump on the lower row to reach it. Another warp pipe appears, followed by a Buzzy Beetle shell that the player can kick. The player will pass three Piranha Plants and promptly reach the third Purple Coin below some Brick Blocks. Another warp pipe is entered, which leads to an area with trampolines and even more Buzzy Beetles. The fourth Purple Coin is found immediately afterwards above some back arrow blocks over some Munchers. After entering yet another warp pipe, an ! Switch is encountered, and if hit, it will drop many Buzzy Beetles. After hitting the second ! Switch, the player must follow the solid blocks to a ? Block containing the fifth and final Purple Coin. The player will then enter one last warp pipe and jump across some platforms to the Goal Pole.
|-|Black Coins = The level starts with a single Buzzy Beetle. The player must kick it into a Piranha Plant and the Munchers below in order to collect the first Black Coin. The player will then enter a warp pipe, and approach a series of falling Buzzy Beetles. The second Black Coin is momentarily afterwards, just below two rows of ? Blocks and Brick Blocks. The player will then enter a warp pipe, and can collect the third Black Coin if they midair spin at just the right moment. Just after kicking a Buzzy Beetle shell, the player will pass four Piranha Plants and some Brick Blocks just before another warp pipe is entered. The warp pipe leads to an area with trampolines and even more Buzzy Beetles. The fourth Black Coin is found immediately afterwards, and the player must carefully jump on a Buzzy Beetle and use the back arrow block above to obtain it. After entering yet another warp pipe, an ! Switch is encountered, and if hit, it will drop many Buzzy Beetles. After hitting the second ! Switch, the player must jump into a gap in the platforms directly towards a Buzzy Beetle to get the fifth and final Black Coin. The player will then enter one last warp pipe and jump across some platforms to the Goal Pole.
</tabber>
