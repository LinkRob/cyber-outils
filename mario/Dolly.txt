The '''Dolly''', also known as the '''Peach Doll''', is a key item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
This doll, owned by [[Goombaria]], got stuck in a tree in [[Jr. Troopa's Playground]] (due to [[Jr. Troopa]] himself as [[Goombario]] mentioned Jr. Troopa likes to pick on the Goombas). After [[Mario]] retrieves the [[Hammer]] from a bush, he is able to strike a tree with it. When the tree is hit, the Dolly will fall down the tree.

After the fight with [[Jr. Troopa]], Mario is able to bring the Dolly back to Goombaria. To show her gratitude, she grants Mario a [[Star Piece]] and a kiss.

==Trivia==
*Similar looking dolls have been seen in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', and ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''.
