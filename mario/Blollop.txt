==Appearance==
Blollop is a young, female [[Blobule]] that is yellow in color.

==History==
Blollop, when first talked to, figures that the heroes are searching for the [[Pure Heart|Treasure of Space]] and recommends them to go and talk to [[Blappy]].

==Trivia==
*Blollop is the only known female Blobule.
