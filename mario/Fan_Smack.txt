|textcolor = ff66ff}}'''Fan Smack''' is a special move used by [[Lady Bow]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Lady Bow can use this move once she is upgraded to the [[Ultra Rank]] and it requires 5 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Usage==

To use Fan Smack, the player must repeatedly tap the control stick in the left direction ({{button|n64|stickleft}}) to fill up the gauge. Once it's filled (or some time as passed), Lady Bow will smack the enemy up to five times inflicting 2 points of damage (10 [[Heart Point|HP]] in all). Fan Smack is, indeed, a strong move, but it's practically useless against enemies with a defense of 2 or higher. Fan Smack is basically a more stronger version of [[Smack]].

==Trivia==

*This is, by far, the strongest move used by one of Mario's partners in the ''[[Paper Mario]]'' series.
