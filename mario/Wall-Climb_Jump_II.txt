'''Wall-Climb Jump II''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It showcases the [[Wall-Climb Jump]] badge.
==Layout==
The level starts filled with two block high, one block wide platforms the player must wall-climb jump off. Afterwards, these very platforms on [[track]]s begins. Finally, there are ten block high, one block wide platforms rotating around the center.
