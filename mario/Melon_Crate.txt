==History==
Melon Crates are fairly rare in the game with one crate found per level (aside from [[Hideout Helm]] where there are no Melon Crates present). Melon Crates can be busted open with any attack and contain four Watermelon slices. This can be prove useful when a Kong has taken a lot of damage and need a quick restore of health (though jumping in a [[Tag Barrel]] is better as Tag Barrels are more common than Melon Crates and doesn't require much effort to get into).
