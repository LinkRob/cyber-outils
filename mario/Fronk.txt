'''Fronk''' is a character in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]] and a pet of [[9-Volt]]'s. He is part of the species of the [[Fronk (species)|same name]]. Fronk makes his first appearance in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. 

==Physical description==
Fronk is a small, yellow creature with a pixelated appearance, supposedly based on sprites from earlier games or {{w|pixel art}} in general. He has an identical appearance to his species, namely those who are also colored yellow, 

==In the ''WarioWare'' series==
===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
[[File:Chicken Race start.png|thumb|left|Fronk and 9-Volt starting Chicken Race in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'']]
Here, he is named '''Shag''', but would later be renamed Fronk in subsequent appearances due to Shag being a vulgar word in British English. Shag is seen during 9-Volt's third intermissions, where he is pet by 9-Volt, causing him to sweat. Fronk also appears in the two-player game [[Chicken Race]], where he competes against 9-Volt.
{{br|left}}
===''WarioWare: Twisted!''===
Fronk has his own set of microgames titled Frantic Fronk. However, unlike the other microgame hosts, Fronk's microgames randomly appear in any stage starting with [[Jimmy T.]]'s, although they are more likely to show up in later stages. Fronk's microgames have a shorter time limit than the others and therefore require quicker reaction.

===''WarioWare Gold''===
During 9-Volt's stage, Fronk tries to help 9-Volt do math in class by telling him to see it as a role-playing game. As the player plays through 9-Volt's microgame set, both Fronk and 9-Volt make their way through an RPG-style map that presumably appears within 9-Volt's imagination.

Similar to ''WarioWare: Twisted!'', Fronk has his own set of microgames but does not have his own stage for them. In Story mode, his microgames appear at random during the Dancing Team and Potluck Gang stages. One of his microgames, [[Tread Carefully (microgame)|Tread Carefully]], could usually be found in Wario Deluxe's stage, in the part where Wario Deluxe halts all further instructions of what controls needed to be used for the microgames. It can also appear in the Dancing Team ans Potluck Gang stages, although it would be very unlikely.
