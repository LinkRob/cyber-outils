'''Super Pickaxes''' ('''Super Pickax''' in the USA) are objects in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

== Usage ==
Super Pickaxes can be plucked from the ground similar to [[Turnip]]s. If [[Captain Toad]] picks one up, he will swing it repeatedly in front of him, breaking certain objects in his way such as [[Brick Block]]s. After a certain amount of time, the pickax will disappear. The Super Pickax can also be used to defeat enemies, and it works in a similar way to a [[Starman]].
