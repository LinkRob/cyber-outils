'''Dyllis Deluxe''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing a [[Shroom Steak]] with a [[Gorgeous Steak]]. Considered a legendary recipe, the Dyllis Deluxe restores 90 [[Heart Point|HP]] and sells for 225 [[coin]]s. This recipe cures the most HP out of all of the other recipes in ''Super Paper Mario''.
