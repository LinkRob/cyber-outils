The '''Sweet Daytripper''' is a red, pink and white [[Daytripper]]. It made its debut as a kart in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Sweet Daytripper is a variant of the Daytripper. It made its debut in the [[Peach Tour]].

=== Tour appearances ===
* [[Peach Tour (2020)]] (Spotlight, Peach Pipe 1) (Peach Pipe 2)
* [[Sunset Tour (2020)]] (Princess Pipe)
* [[Peach vs. Daisy Tour (2021)]] (Spotlight, Peach vs. Daisy Pipe 1)
* [[Wedding Tour (2021)]] (Open-Wheel Pipe)
* [[Princess Tour (2023)]] (Spotlight Shop)
