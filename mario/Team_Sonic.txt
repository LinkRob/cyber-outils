'''Team Sonic''' is a group of ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' characters in the {{series|Mario & Sonic}}. They compete in the [[Olympic Games]] alongside [[Team Mario (Mario & Sonic)|Team Mario]].

== Trivia ==
* [[Sticks the Badger|Sticks]] and [[E-123 Omega|Omega]] mark the only Team Sonic characters to not appear in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''.
* [[Cream the Rabbit|Cream]] was removed from Team Sonic. Instead, she appears in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]'' as non-playable character.
