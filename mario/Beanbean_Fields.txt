'''Beanbean Fields''' (referred to as '''Beanbean Outskirts''' in the original) is the grassy place around Beanbean Castle Town. It appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its remake, [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]].

They borders [[Hoohoo Mountain]] and [[Teehee Valley]], [[Chucklehuck Woods]], [[Oho Ocean]], and [[Beanbean International Airport]]. [[Woohoo Hooniversity]] is located in the southeast corner of the region, while [[Yoshi Theater (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Yoshi Theater]] lies in a box canyon. [[Warp Pipe]]s can be found in the cave on the beach.

They first reach Beanbean Fields after plummeting to the bottom of [[Hoohoo Mountain]]. It most importantly be obedient to a hub, since areas of the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] are connected to the field. After the [[Beanstar]] breaks, one of the pieces coastlines in [[Harhall's Studio]]. However, [[Harhall]] finds the piece first, and he won't give it away unless [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] support him with his project. They also stop by the fields to explore for the [[Bean Fruit]], which are needed to get [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] at the Yoshi Theater to lay their [[Neon Egg|egg]]s.

The [[Hammerhead Bros.]] first met in the house in [[Hoohoo Village]], locomote here, making its new home in a cave of Beanbean Fields. Mario and Luigi can stop by them here after mastering at [[Oho Oasis]] to receive the [[Super Hammer]]s. They can come back after rebuilding the Beanstar to give [[Ultra Hammer]]s, which are called for [[Joke's End]].
