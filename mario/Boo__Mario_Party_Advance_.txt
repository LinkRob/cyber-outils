'''Boo''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Boo is the namesake character representative of his [[Boo|species]]. He has the same appearance as a regular Boo.

===Behavior===
Boo appears in a few haunted locations, such as [[Boo Cemetery]] and the [[Horror Condo]], alongside a few other Boos.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
When the player's character visits Boo Cemetery, they are greeted by four Boos, and their associated quest is [[True-Blue Boo]].

During the True-Blue Boo quest, the Boos are about to tell the player's character about a crime that had occurred recently. [[Shroomlock]] arrives to try and help solve the crime, and the Boos mention that the [[Golden Boo Statue]], a symbol of their friendship, is lost. When Shroomlock suspects it was stolen, the Boos become worried and ask the player's character to help find it. At this time, the Boos realize that there are four of them in their group rather than the usual three. Shroomlock tells the Boos that one of them stole the Golden Boo Statue and is an imposter among them. Shroomlock then asks the players' character to select the Boo with a slightly different moveset and rhythm, and selecting the correct impostor reveals Boo to be [[Koopa Kid]], who flees but leaves behind the Golden Boo Statue. The Boos reward the player with the [[Predictotron]] [[Gaddget]] for their help.

In the epilogue, it is stated that the Boos now regularly haunt Horror Condo.
