'''Spiky Joe''' is a character in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a [[Red Spike Top]] and the leader of the [[Tiny Spinies]] team.

==History==
Spiky Joe can be seen in the major league room in the [[Glitz Pit]] of [[Glitzville]] where he is actually one of the nicer members. When he loses to [[Mario]], he simply says that he needs to train more.
