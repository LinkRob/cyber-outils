'''World 0-1''' is the first board of the first world, [[World 0]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. It is a plain board with a few features. The first board teaches how allies are used in the board.

== Spaces ==
=== Boss Spaces ===
*'''Boss 1: '''The first boss space is located in front of the three rocks. The player rescues a [[Star]].
*'''Boss 2: '''The second boss space is located two spaces behind the three rocks and is on the middle.

=== Ally Spaces ===
*'''Ally 1: '''An ally is three spaces west and one space south from the rock on the left.
*'''Ally 2: '''Another ally is three spaces east and one space south from the rock on the right.
*'''Ally 3:''' An ally is two spaces east and three spaces south from the rock on the left.
*'''Ally 4:''' Another ally is two spaces west and three spaces south from the rock on the right.
