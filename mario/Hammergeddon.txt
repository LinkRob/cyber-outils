'''Hammergeddon''' is a Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It involves two characters fighting through the use of throwing [[hammer]]s at one another.

==History==
At the [[Mushroom Beacon]] in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], [[Blooper (character)|Blooper]] challenges the player to this minigame. By clearing Hammergeddon, they unlock the minigame.

==Gameplay==
Both characters start out with three [[heart]]s each. They must avoid the hammers thrown by their opponent or even themselves, as this can damage them. If a character is hit by a hammer, they lose a heart. Both characters have the ability to jump, and the direction in which their hammers are thrown can be either straight upward or in an arc formation across the arena. 

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|Pad}}: Move
*{{button|gba|A}}: Jump
*{{button|gba|B}}: Throw hammer
