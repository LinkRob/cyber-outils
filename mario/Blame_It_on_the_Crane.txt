'''Blame It on the Crane''' is a 1 vs. 3 Player mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. It later returns in both ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''. The name of this [[mini-game]] is derived from the song "[[wikipedia:Blame It on the Rain|Blame It on the Rain]]," which was featured in "[[Kootie Pie Rocks]]," an episode of ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', in which the rock band [[Milli Vanilli]] guest-starred.

==Gameplay==
One player controls the crane while the trio is in color-coordinated balls on a circle-shaped platform. The platform spins counterclockwise. Seven [[Shy Guy]]s / Koopa Troopas are also in similar-colored balls. The solo player must grab the players on the platform while it spins around. When a player is grabbed by the crane, he or she loses. Grabbing a Shy Guy or Koopa Troopa results in nothing but less time.

If at least one of the three players has not been picked up by the crane in the time limit, their team will win. If all of the three players are picked up, the solo player wins.
