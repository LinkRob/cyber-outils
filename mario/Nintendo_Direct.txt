{{nihongo|'''Nintendo Direct'''|ニンテンドーダイレクト|Nintendō Dairekuto}} is a series of online presentations from [[Nintendo]] in which they reveal various upcoming information about their games and other products. The first Nintendo Direct occurred on October 21, 2011. Most Nintendo Direct presentations include ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' content within its line-up or as the sole focus, such as the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' trailers.
