==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
DEF-Down returns in this game under the name '''Soft'''. When someone has the Soft status, their defense is lowered by 3 for a few turns. The best way to Soften an enemy is to use the [[Mr. Softener]] item which can be bought in certain item shops.
