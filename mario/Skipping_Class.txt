'''Skipping Class''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
*Press the buttons in the order on-screen to perform jump-rope tricks. The faster you enter the button combinations, the more point you'll earn.
*Press slowly and carefully, because pressing just one button wrong, you get no points.

==Controls==
Hold the Wii remote sideways. Press the buttons that shows on-screen.
