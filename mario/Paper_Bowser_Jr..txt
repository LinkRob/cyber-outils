'''Paper Bowser Jr.''' is a character and antagonist that appears in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. He is the paper form of [[Bowser Jr.]] from a [[Mysterious Book|different dimension]] and is one of the more major antagonists. Bowser Jr. and Paper Bowser Jr. are best buddies.
