The '''Ice Snifit''' is an enemy from [[Yoshi's Woolly World|''Yoshi's Woolly World'']]. They are encountered in [[World 5 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 5]], which takes the form of an icy tundra.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Ice Snifits resemble ordinary Snifits, but with blue parkas.

== Behaviour ==
Ice Snifits attack by shooting a freezing mist from their nozzles. If [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] gets caught in this freezing mist, he will be frozen into a solid block of ice and slide away until he falls into a body of water or bottomless pit or collides with a ledge.

== Notable Members ==
One Ice Snifit named [[Snifberg the Unfeeling]] is encountered at his castle at the end of World 5. He is empowered by [[Kamek]] and can summon an ice barrier around himself.
