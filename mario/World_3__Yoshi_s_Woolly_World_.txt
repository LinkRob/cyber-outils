''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''|levels=9|prewor=[[World 2 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 2]]|nxtwor=[[World 4 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 4]]}}'''World 3''' is the third world of [[Yoshi's Woolly World|''Yoshi's Woolly World'']]. It is mostly a cloud-themed world. It contains nine levels: eight are compulsory, but there is one special, optional level (called [[Woollet Bill's Last Ride]]) that can be unlocked by collecting all 40 other Smiley Flowers from the previous levels of World 3. Completing this world unlocks [[World 4 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 4]].
