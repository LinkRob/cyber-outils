'''Cosmic Luigi''' is a character from [[Super Mario Galaxy]] and replaces [[Cosmic Mario]] when playing as [[Luigi]]. Cosmic Luigi is very similar to his counterpart with the only difference being is that he long jumps a lot, and takes shortcuts, making him bit harder to beat than Cosmic Mario however he does show a few weaknesses in the race such as accidentally slipping into honey, taking longer routes allowing you to slip right past him if he's beating you. His voice is the same as Cosmic Mario's, and when he appears, "Mario" is replaced by Luigi in the Prankster Comet mission.
