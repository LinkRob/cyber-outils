'''Fresh Juice''' is an item and a [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
A Fresh Juice restores 5 [[Flower Point|FP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]. It works like a [[Super Soda]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Fresh Juices can be made in a variety of ways, although the easiest method is having [[Zess T.]] cook just a [[Honey Syrup]].
