'''World 3''' is the ice-themed [[world]] and third world in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. [[Lemmy Koopa]] is fought as the boss here.

== Description ==
World 3 introduces the [[Penguin Suit]], and it is the only world in the game that has a switch capable of changing the obstacles in a level (similar to a [[Switch Palace]]). On the map is an area with ice that has two [[Ice Bro]]s. on it. If [[Mario]] or another character walks into an Ice Bro, they have to fight and defeat them in an [[Enemy Course]]. Accessing the second part of the level requires traversing a waterfall. World 3 has a [[Warp Cannon]] that leads to [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]].
