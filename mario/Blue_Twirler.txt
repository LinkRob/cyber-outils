'''Blue Twirler''' is a type of [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|Ghost]] featured in some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. It first appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' for [[Nintendo GameCube]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Blue Twirlers have a light blue body and yellow eyes, and they are somewhat bulky.

===Behavior===
Blue Twirlers use their large fists to create shock waves.

==Appearances==
===''Luigi's Mansion''===
[[File:Blue Twirler.jpg|thumb|''Luigi's Mansion'' artwork]]
In ''Luigi's Mansion'', Blue Twirlers are one of the largest, heaviest and most powerful non-portrait ghosts found in the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]]. Blue Twirlers have large fists that they use to make shock waves, that spread around and take ten [[Heart Point|HP]] if they hit Luigi. They have thirty HP each (45 in the PAL and 3DS Hidden Mansion), which can be whittled down by suction or elements. 

===''Mario Power Tennis''===
In ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', a Blue Twirler is one of the Ghosts that can appear in [[Luigi's Mansion Court]].

===''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''===
[[File:MKAGP2_GhostTwirler_YoshiPark.png|thumb|left|Graphic of a Blue Twirler in ''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2'']]
In ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', a Blue Twirler appears as of the ghost artworks in the Horror House section of [[Yoshi Park 2]].

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
[[File:GhostK-MSS.png|thumb|Blue Twirlers in the Ghost K minigame in ''Mario Super Sluggers'']]
In ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'', Blue Twirlers appear in the [[Luigi's Mansion (baseball stadium)|Luigi's Mansion]] stadium. They reside within the graves, and if a fielder runs onto them, a Blue Twirler comes to stun them. Blue Twirlers appear in only red, green, and yellow varieties, despite their name. Blue Twirlers are also featured in the minigame [[Ghost K]].

===''WarioWare: Get It Together!''===
In ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'', Blue Twirler can only appear in level 3 of two-player mode, along with a [[Gold Ghost]] and a [[Purple Puncher]], and both players have to reveal all three Ghosts.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
In the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, Blue Twirler is depicted alongside a Gold Ghost and a Purple Puncher in the [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|Ghosts]] [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' as well as another in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''.
