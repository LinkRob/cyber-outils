'''Budget Dentistry''' is one of [[Kat & Ana]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
Using the [[Wii Remote]], the player has to aim at the flashing spots on the person's teeth to fill them in. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame, indicated by the uvula opening up and something flying out of it.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level adds another filling than the previous, and all three difficulty levels show a different animation when completing the microgame.

*'''Level 1:''' There is one filling. Completing the microgame causes several flowers to fly out of the character's mouth.
*'''Level 2:''' There are two fillings. Completing the microgame causes several leaves to fly out of the character's mouth.
*'''Level 3:''' There are three fillings. Completing the microgame causes several pink roses to fly out of the character's mouth.
