'''Larson''' is a [[Bandit]] who appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and it's [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]

==History==
Larson scammed [[Goomther]] with a fake credit card and was going to continue his devious actions, but Goomther reported him to the [[Trouble Center]]. Larson then started hiding in the back alley of [[Rogueport]] to avoid getting caught.

If [[Mario]] accepts Goomther's plea from the Trouble Center, Mario and his partners must chase Larson around Rogueport. After he is caught the third time, Goomther appears to act upon his revenge. However, Larson is never brought to his official justice, and will continue hiding in the back alley for the rest of the game. [[Goombella]] mentions that Larson always pulls scam and is constantly trying to avoid the law, meaning he will continue his devious schemes.
