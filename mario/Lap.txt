A '''lap''' is a term in the {{series|Mario Kart}}, let alone racing in general, that refers to the action of going around a track once. Most tracks in most games are completed in three laps, but there are exceptions.

==Exceptions==
#All ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'' tracks have 5 laps. The lap count for tracks that are featured as [[Retro Stage]]s in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' from this game were changed to 3.
#''[[Baby Park]] has ''7 laps in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', due to its small size. The lap count was changed to 5 in ''Mario Kart DS'' but changed back to 7 in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.
#''[[Wario Colosseum]]'' has two laps in ''Double Dash!!'', due to its large size.
#In ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', the number of laps can be changed from 3 to 5 during Quick Play mode. Normally in the [[Grand Prix]], three laps are done on the regular tracks and five laps would be used on the [[Retro Stage|Extra Track]]s.
#In ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'', the number of laps may be freely changed from a range of 1 to 9 during [[VS Race]].
