The '''Glasses''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Glasses can only be found after accepting one of [[Koopa Koot]]'s favors. The Glasses can be found in a bush in [[Koopa Village]]. After giving the Glasses back to Koopa Koot, he will reward [[Mario]] with a [[coin]]. The Glasses is the second lost item belonging to Koopa Koot that can be found in a bush in Koopa Village with the other being the [[Empty Wallet]].
