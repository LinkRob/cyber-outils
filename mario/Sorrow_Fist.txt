He is one of the [[Beef-Off#Hooraws|Hooraws]] that must be fought in order to obtain a [[Hooraw Medal]] to speak with [[Big Massif]].

==Appearance==
Sorrow Fist is a Hooraw that wears blue clothing. He also appears to have a sad face hence his name.

==Battle==
During his battle, the Sorrow Fist has the highest stamina, second-to-highest power, and the second-to-lowest defense and speed.

For the battle conditions for Sorrow Fist, [[Mario]] can only win by achieving Excellent attacks.

===Attacks===
*'''Arm Flail''': Sorrow Fist will dash at Mario, swinging his arm. Mario should avoid this attack since it hurts even landing on top of him, except if the boss keeps his hands lower than where his head is.
*'''Shell Smack''': Sorrow Fist summons a shell and smacks it towards Mario, who must bat it away with the hammer.

===Tips===
The fight against Sorrow Fist isn't tough. One should only use [[Luiginary Attack]]s if they are great at achieving Excellents at them, but the big tip is that any easy attack is good for this battle.
