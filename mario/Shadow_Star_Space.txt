'''Shadow Star Spaces''' are a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
Shadow Star Spaces are essentially the opposite of a [[Star Space]]. If a character reaches the space, [[Bowser]] gives them a [[Ztar|Shadow Star]], causing them to lose one [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]], or 20 [[coin]]s if they do not have any Stars. Like the Star Space, reaching a Shadow Star Space does not count towards the [[Dice Block]] roll, and even walking up to a Shadow Star Space triggers the effects of it.

==In ''Mario Party 6''==
Shadow Star Spaces only appear in two boards, [[Castaway Bay]] and [[Clockwork Castle]], but only during certain circumstances. In Castaway Bay, the Shadow Star Space appears when Bowser and [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] shift their position around, while in Clockwork Castle, Bowser replaces Donkey Kong at night and alters the Shadow Star Space with him when he moves.
