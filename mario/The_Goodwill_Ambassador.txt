'''The Goodwill Ambassador''' is a disguise taken on by [[Cackletta]] at the start of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. At first she comes to bring a gift from [[Queen Bean]], but turns out it was a trap to steal [[Princess Peach's voice]].

== Plot ==
Cackletta disguised as "The Goodwill Ambassador" to take Peach's voice, replacing her speech with literal explosive vocabulary.

{{spoiler|hide}}
After [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] cleared up the [[Piranha Plant]]s at the [[Beanbean Airport]], the plane arrived. Peach, Toadsworth and [[Prince Peasley]] confirmed that Cackletta took [[Birdo]]'s voice instead. Peasley warned Peach and Toadsworth about Cackletta plotting to steal Peach's voice. Instead, they had Birdo pretend to be Princess Peach while the real Peach and Toadsworth hid behind the curtains.
{{endspoiler}}
