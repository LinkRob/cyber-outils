== Overview ==
'''Crouch High Jump''' is a [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Badge]] in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]].

== Ability ==
If the player makes their character [[Crouch]] for a short period of time, a large [[Jump]] will be activated.
