'''Spangle's Honey Pie''' is the third level of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 15.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 2 [[Pokey]]s and 6 [[Starkiss]]. The enemy captain is a Starkiss and knows [[Rally]] and [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 3 Pokeys. 4 [[Spear Guy]]s, and 1 Starkiss. The enemy captain is the Starkiss and knows [[Chance]] and Deny.

The final battle contains 3 Pokey, 2 Spear Guys, and 3 Starkiss. The enemy captain is a Starkiss and knows Deny and Rally.

After the final battle, the player recruits a Pokey.

The level can be replayed to recruit up to 2 Spear Guy and/or Pokey.
