'''Ace Coins'''<ref>''Super Mario Advance'' instruction booklet, page 27.</ref> are large, red [[coin]]s with an "A" engraved on them. They only appear in ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'', as the successor to the [[Red Coin]]s from the Challenge mode of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''.

There are 100 Ace Coins in total, five in each [[level]]. The player is required to obtain them to fully complete, or "ace," the level. Once every Ace Coin has been collected, the player's character receives an [[extra life]]. Ace Coins are often near risky areas where one of the playable characters can take damage or lose a life. Whenever a level is replayed, every collected Ace Coin becomes transparent and only rewards points when collected. If the player gets a [[Game Over]] in a level that has not yet been completed, they lose their progress of collecting Ace Coins within that level.

Ace Coins have a very similar purpose to [[Dragon Coin]]s from ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and [[Advance Coin]]s in [[World-e]] of ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.
