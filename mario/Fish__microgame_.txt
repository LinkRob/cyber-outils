'''Fish''' is one of the [[microgame]]s in [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s stage [[Gamer]] in ''[[Game & Wario]]''. It is also available as a [[Cluck-A-Pop]] souvenir, titled '''BALLOON FIGHTER: Fish'''.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of a fish, which has to swim to eat three worms within the [[time limit]]. In doing so, the player wins at the microgame. In Levels 2 and 3, if the fish eats a worm lingering from a fishing line as bait, the fisherman catches the fish, causing the player to lose at the microgame. In Level 3, the player can also lose if the fish gets eaten by an eel.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' There are five worms.
*'''Level 2:''' Same as level 1 except one of the worms is bait.
*'''Level 3:''' There are six worms, one of which is bait. Another worm is next to a giant eel that eats it, and the eel attempts to eat the fish if it gets close.
