The '''Black B Dasher''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Black B Dasher is a black and gold coloration of the [[B Dasher]]. It uses the [[Pipe Frame]]'s tires, like the B Dasher and its color variations.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Black B Dasher first appeared in the [[New York Tour]], as a High-End kart. It increases the duration of [[Dash Panel]]s speed boosts and the points increased for hitting one.
