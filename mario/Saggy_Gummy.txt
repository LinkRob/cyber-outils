'''Saggy Gummy''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and its two sequels, ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
The Saggy Gummy is a lock on item that, upon hitting its target, briefly slows them before stretching their [[kart]] both horizontally and vertically.
