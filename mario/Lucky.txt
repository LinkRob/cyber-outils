'''Lucky''' is a character that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|its]] [[Reissue|remake]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Lucky is a brown [[Bulky Bob-omb]]. His size is smaller than a regular Bulky-Bomb.

=== Personality traits ===
Lucky is a friendly Bulky Bob-omb. He is furious when he catches anybody cheating in the game.

== Biography ==
Lucky hosts the lottery drawing located in West [[Rogueport]]. He allows Mario to see if he's lucky enough to win an item.

== Trivia ==
*If the player changes the date on their Nintendo GameCube, Lucky will close the lottery. He will snap and say, ''"Can'ttakeanymoreno"'' and ''"Nevertrustanotherperson"'' if the player admits to changing the clock.
