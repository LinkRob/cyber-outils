'''Pull It Together''' is a [[Team Minigame|Team]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The minigame is played like the tug of war.

== Opening ==
The camera pans closer to the players. The team picks up the rope before the game starts.

== Gameplay ==
The players must mash the button as instructed to pull the rope; the flag marks the goal. Whoever's line the flag crosses, the team wins. If the flag does not pass either team, either team's side to flag is above wins the minigame. The winning team cheers while the losing team sulks.

== Ending ==
The winning team poses their victory while the other team performs their losing animation.

==Controls==
*Mash {{button|ns|jc-right}} – Pull

==In-game description==
*''"Pull your rivals over the line!"''
*''"Mash the button as fast as you can!"''

== In pop culture ==
Pull it Together's minigame challenge in Master Challenge Road is infamous with the player's strategy to use the knuckle of the finger to beat the minigame, as a result, it can cause blisters to the player.
