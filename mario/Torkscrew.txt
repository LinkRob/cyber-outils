'''Torkscrew'' '''''is a boss that appears in''''' '''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It lairs in [[Dozing Sands]].

Torkscrew is first seen where he devours a [[Pillow|pillow]] (actually a petrified [[Pi'illo]] folk) and digs away to make his getaway. Mario and Luigi used the [[Drilldigger|drill machine]] to make him spit it out but soon, it then eats [[Britta]]. Mario and Luigi, infuriated, engage the Torkscrew in battle.

==Appearance==
Torkscrew is a strange, mole-like monster. He has a red coloration, a drill on his head, and large teeth and claws. The [[Battle Ring]] gives him a purple coloration.

==Battle==
In battle, Torkscrew normally cannot be jumped on. He mainly uses his attack:
*'''Drill Spin:''' Torkscrew will dig underground and will drill into either Mario or Luigi. This can be avoided with a jump. If a bro. gets stuck, the Torkscrew runs, causing an inverse [[Chase Attack]]. Then, the other bro must chase that bro will collecting the lost coins and avoiding the objects that Torkscrew tosses.
*'''Drill Dive:''' Torkscrew will jump up high and attempt to dive down at either Mario or Luigi with his drill. One of them must use his hammer to block this attack. A successful counterattack will mean Torkscrew trips, rendering himself vulnerable to jumps.
*'''Team Attack:''' Other enemies will go after one bro while Torkscrew goes after the other. Mario or Luigi must jump at the correct time to avoid damage.

===Strategy===
Torkscrew is a heavy hitter so it is recommened to use [[Bros. Attack]]s in this fight. Remember that the drill on his head acts like a spike so jump-based attacks are useless. [[3D Red Shell]] and [[3D Green Shell]] are the best attacks to use in this fight. [[Fire Flower]] is also recommended because it can deal high amounts of damage to Torkscrew. Countering the Drill Dive allows him causes him to become vulnerable to jumps.
