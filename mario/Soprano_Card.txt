The '''Soprano Card''' is a key item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. With it, [[Mario]] can purchase items from the Juice Bar at a discounted price, and it also allows him to buy [[Kerokero Cola]] there. The Soprano Card can be obtained from [[Toadofsky]] by playing the [[Starslap]] song at [[Melody Bay]].
