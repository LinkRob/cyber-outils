'''Bees''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
A Bee tries to attack [[Mario]] by exiting its hole and slowly homing in on him. When a Bee is defeated, the next one comes out of its hole to try and attack Mario.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
Bees are only encountered in the level [[Honeybees (level)|Honeybees]], where they are located near walls with eight holes in them, each housing a different Bee. Mario can defeat a Bee using a [[jump]], but a [[fireball]]s is ineffective against them. After Mario defeats all eight Bees or at least one Bee from either a [[Spin Jump|spin jump]] or touching it while [[Invincible Mario|invincible]], no more Bees come out of the wall.

Bees have an undead variant called [[Skeleton Bee]]s, and a fat variant named [[Būichi]].
