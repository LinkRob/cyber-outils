The '''Earth Vellumental Temple Path''' is a collection of exhibits in the [[Earth Vellumental Temple]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' ==
The 1st Exhibit costs 10 coins and is called "Temple Origins".  The 1st part reads, "Legend says the spirit of the dirt, known as the Earth Vellumental, lives in this very cave." The 2nd part reads, "It is a mighty spirit, imbued with the power to lift the very ground!" Part 3 reads, "It is no wonder that the meek and modest Koopa Troopa venerate it so, via pilgrimage and gifts."

The 2nd Exhibit costs 10 coins and is called, "Shellvation is Near." The 1st part reads, "To one with a creative mind, these fortuitously formed formations may resemble the Earth Vellumental." The 2nd part reads, "Indeed, one may pay respects to its hallowed shell in this very spot as many Koopa Troopas do." Part 3 reads, "Notably, the Earth Vellumantal's shell is even stouter than this rock, which is itself impervious to damage."
[[File:Paper Mario- The Origami King, Earth Vellumental Temple 3rd Exhibit Part 4.jpeg|left|thumb|329x329px|Exhibit 3 Part 3]]
The 3rd Exhibit cost 10 coins and is called, "Pain Point." The 1st part reads, "It's said this claw-shaped gouge was created by the Earth Vellumental in an epic battle...Mario?" The 2nd part reads, "Hey? it's really you, Mario...you've got to help me!" At this point, Mario frees the Toad who is stuck in the exhibit, and the Toad continues the story, with part 3 saying, "Oh yeah. They think that this giant gouge in the wall was made by the Earth Vellumental." Part 4 continues with, "But! This part is important. They also said that its claws were, like, its weakest attack." Part 5 reads, "The attack that will really get you is when it launches rocks out of its mouth. So, uh, watch out for that, I guess." 

The 4th Exhibit cost 10 coins and is called, "Footsteps of the Elders." Part 1 reads, "The following section of the path has been left in its historically authentic uneven state." Continued by part 2 reading, " Please watch your step."

The 5th Exhibit is called, "Behold: The Spirit Itself." The 1st part reads, " You are looking at a holy image representing the full, glorious form of the Earth Vellumental itself." Followed by part 2 reading, "Although the spirit is fully invincible kindly direct your attention now to the beast's great tail.
