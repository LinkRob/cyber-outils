The '''Work Pants''' is an armor which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Unlike most armor, the Work Pants can be equipped by any character, and doing so increases the user's [[Defense]] by 15 and their [[Magic Defense]] by 5. The Work Pants can be bought from a shop in [[Moleville]] for 22 [[coin]]s.
