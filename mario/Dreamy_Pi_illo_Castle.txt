*[[Princess Peach]]|final state=Intact}}'''Dreamy Pi'illo Castle''' is a location in the [[Dream World]] that is seen in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It is the first place visited in the dream world and is an alternate version of [[Pi'illo Castle]]. It is also where [[Mario]] first meets up with [[Dreamy Luigi]] and [[Dreambert|Prince Dreambert]].
==Layout==
Being a dream world location, Dreamy Pi'illo Castle is basically a 2-D version of Pi'illo Castle.
==History==
Mario heads into Dreamy Pi'illo Castle after a [[Antasma|strange, shadow creature]] has taken [[Princess Peach]] away. Mario runs into [[Dreamy Broque Monsieur]] and soon, Dreamy Luigi. The two follow keep exploring around the 2-D castle where they eventually corner the creature that still has Peach. The shadow creature then fights Mario and Dreamy Luigi but uses [[Dreamy Mario]] to fight them instead. Mario and Dreamy Luigi defeat Dreamy Mario, but the creature has taken Peach somewhere [[Dream's Deep|deeper]] into the dream world.{{Mario & Luigi: Dream Team Nav}}
