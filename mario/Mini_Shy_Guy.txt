'''Mini Shy Guys''' are [[Shy Guy]] toys which appear in the [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (series)|''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series]]. They usually pace back and forth, but they’re still hazardous as they can break a [[Mini Mario]] or prove troublesome to [[Mario]] himself. A Mini Shy Guy can be defeated using a [[hammer]]. Mini Shy Guys effectively fill the role of [[Goomba]]s in the [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (series)|''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series]].

==History==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''===
[[Image:Mini Shy Guy GBA.jpg|thumb|left|Mario near a Mini Shy Guy.]]
[[Image:Mini Shy Guy Switch.jpg|thumb|right|Mario doing parkour on a Mini Shy Guy.]]
Mini Shy Guys first appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'' and later appear in its [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (Nintendo Switch)|its Switch remake]]. In these games, they commonly moving back and forth and are often seen walking along [[Spike (obstacle)|Spikes]]. Mario can hop on a Mini Shy Guy to safely traverse the Spikes. Mini Shy Guys can also be defeated with [[Hammer]]s. If Mario is hit by a Mini Shy Guys, he dies.

Mini Shy Guys first appear in the [[Level 1-1 (Mario vs. Donkey Kong)|very first level]], where they patrol areas in front of garbage cans which can be used to attack them, and continue to appear occasionally in subsequent levels. Exclusively in the Spooky House boss stage, Level 4-DK, Shy Guys are transformed into [[Polterguy]]s and vice versa by [[Donkey Kong]]'s [[Color Switch|switches]]. Remarkably, throughout the entirety of [[Level x-7]], a Shy Guy needs to be used to cross beds of spikes.

Certain types of Shy Guys wield weapons and have different colors and behavior from the standard red Shy Guys; these include [[Katakata Kaen Heihō]], [[Katakata Yarihō]], and [[Katakata Spanner Heihō]].

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''===
[[Image:Mini Shy Guy DS.jpg|thumb|right|A Mini Mario about to approach a Mini Shy Guy.]]
Mini Shy Guys return in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''. In this game, they appear in several levels and act as a common enemy for the Minis. Mini Shy Guys now pause for an extended period of time after reaching a wall and turning around. They can also be dropped if they are standing on [[Pink Block]]s and are able to walk along [[Spike (obstacle)|Spikes]]. If a Mini is to hit a Mini Shy Guy, they will break instantly. A Mini can hop on a Mini Shy Guy to hitch a ride across hazards such as Spikes. Dropping a Mini Shy Guy into a pit of spikes or into a pool of water by getting rid of any Pink Blocks they were standing on is a good strategy. Mini Shy Guys can also be defeated with [[Hammer]]s or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]]. Whenever the Hammers are being used by a Mini, the Mini Shy Guys will move faster. Mini Shy Guys appear on every floor except for [[Lava Dome]] and [[Jungle Hijinks]]. Three subspecies of Mini Shy Guys also appear in this game, these are [[Mini Snifit]]s, [[Jetpack Guy]]s, [[Mummy Guy]]s, and [[Phantom Guy]]s. Mini Shy Guys can also be placed in the construction zone in all kits (including the [[Special Kit 1|three]] [[Special Kit 2|special]] [[Special Kit 3|kits]]) with the exception of the [[Lava Dome]] kit and [[Jungle Hijinks]] kits. Mini Shy Guys are also the subject of the minigame [[Shy Guy Smash]] which in each world, the player has to hit a number of Mini Shy Guys for points whilst avoiding the [[Mini Bob-omb]]s.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!''===
Although Mini Shy Guys do not appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!]]'', a subspecies of them called the [[Mini Spear Guy]] does. Mini Spear Guys are hazardous to jump on due to the spear they are holding.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!''===
[[Image:Mini Shy Guy DSI.jpg|thumb|left|A [[Mini Mario]] holding [[Hammer]]s near a Mini Shy Guy .]]
Mini Shy Guys return in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!]]''. In this game, they retain their behaviors from previous games, they commonly follow set paths and can be dropped to lower paths by removing the [[Red Girder]]s they walk across, Mini Shy Guys can walk along spikes just as before. Due to the difference in gameplay, Mini Shy Guys are hazardous to stand on, and any [[Mini]] that does so is destroyed. Mini Shy Guys can still be destroyed with [[Hammer]]s or by Minis sliding into them from a slope. Additionally, Mini Shy Guys can also be defeated by shooting into one from a [[Cannon]]. Later in the game, in Super Skywheel, Shy Guys can fill Shy Guy Blocks, becoming solid platforms. Just as before, Mini Shy Guys speed up while a Mini holds a hammer. [[Super Skywheel]] also introduces [[Shy Guy Block]]s, which are transparent at first but when a Mini Shy Guys walks into one, it becomes a solid cube, allowing the Minis to walk across them harmlessly. Mini Shy Guys appear in the [[Construction Zone]] and are available by default.

Shy Guys appear early in the game, starting with [[Area 1-5]] where the player learns that it is possible to destroy enemies using Hammers.

===''Mario and Donkey Kong: Minis on the Move===
[[Image:Mini Shy Guy 3DS.jpg|thumb|left|A [[Mini Toad]] holding [[Hammer]]s, near a Mini Shy Guy.]]
Mini Shy Guys are seen again in ''[[Mario and Donkey Kong: Minis on the Move]]''. They appear in several levels along the terrain, acting as obstacles for the Minis to avoid. If a Mini hits a Mini Shy Guy, it breaks, which causes a [[Game Over]]. Mini Shy Guys can once again be defeated with Hammers.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars===
[[Image:Mini Shy Guy U.jpg|thumb|right|A [[Mini Mario]] and [[Mini Peach]] near a Mini Shy Guy.]]
Mini Shy Guys also appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars]]''. In this game, Mini Shy Guys appear in several levels, retaining their behavior from before. They are also introduced in a similar stance in [[Level 1-3 (Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars)|Level 1-3]], where a Mini can pick up a pair of Hammers and destroy the Mini Shy Guy present in the level.

They are once again hazardous to touch from above or to the side destroying a Mini on contact. However, Mini Shy Guys can be beaten by sliding into one down a slope or with [[Hammer]]s. Just as before, Mini Shy Guys move faster while a Mini is holding Hammers. Mini Shy Guys can be dropped to lower floors by removing any [[Red Girder]]s or [[Pink Block]]s it may be standing on. Mini Shy Guys can also walk on [[Spike (hazard)|Spikes]] too.

===''Mini Mario & Friends: amiibo Challenge''===
In ''[[Mini Mario & Friends: amiibo Challenge]]'', Mini Shy Guys once again appear as enemies. In addition to being vulnerable to Hammers or a slope slide, Mini Shy Guys can be defeated by [[Eat Enemies|eating]] them with [[Mini Yoshi]], or less commonly by being hit with [[Mini Bowser]]'s [[Bowser Bomb]].

==Trivia==
*It is unknown if the [[Mario Toy Company]] created the Mini Shy Guys, but it can be assumed that they were not because they appear to be enemies of him. Mini Shy Guys still have wind-up keys at the back, just like a Mini Mario.
