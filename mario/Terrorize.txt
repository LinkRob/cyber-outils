|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Terrorize''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by [[Bowser]] from the start and requires 6 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Effects==
When Terrorize is used, a [[Big Boo]] is summoned to the field. This move has a 90% chance to inflict the [[Fear]] status on all enemies on the screen. This is one of Bowser's more useful moves in the game.
