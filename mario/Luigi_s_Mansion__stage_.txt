'''Luigi's Mansion''' is a stage seen in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', and later reappears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
[[File:SSBB Luigi's Mansion stage.png|thumb|left|230px|Luigi's Mansion in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
The stage takes place at a simplified version of [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Luigi's Mansion]]. It features five condensed rooms from the game of the [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|same title]]: [[Nursery]], the [[Kitchen (Luigi's Mansion)|Kitchen]], the [[Study (Luigi's Mansion)|Study]], the [[Ball Room]], and the [[Foyer (Luigi's Mansion)|Foyer]]). Fighters can destroy a part of the mansion by attacking the pillars in the separate rooms. When a fighter does this, a few [[Boo]]s will appear and then quickly fade away. If fighters destroy all the pillars, mansion becomes flat, although it does rebuild itself later. The pillars on the bottom floor cannot be destroyed until the pillars above are. If all the fighters are not in any of the rooms and the mansion is whole, it shows the front of the mansion.

The Ω form of the stage takes place on the roof of the mansion. The interior is no longer accessible, but fighters can wall jump off of the walls.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
In ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', the stage is unlocked after three matches are played as [[Luigi]], and it is where his unlock match takes place. In ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'' and ''Ultimate'', the stage is available from the start.
