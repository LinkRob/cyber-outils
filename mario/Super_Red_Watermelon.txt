The '''Super Red Watermelon''' is an item available in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. It is one of the rewards for clearing a minigame. The Super Red Watermelon is essentially the [[fire watermelon]] in the form of a [[Special Item]], as using a Super Red Watermelon from the Pause screen puts a fire watermelon in the playable [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s mouth. A Super Red Watermelon can shoot up to nine blasts of fire unlike regular fire watermelons, which can only shoot three blasts. Similarly, the [[Super Blue Watermelon]] grants nine blasts of ice from an [[ice watermelon]] instead of just three.
