The '''Dark Lands''' is a location that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. It is a haunted area inhabited by [[Dry Bones]], [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] and [[Snifit|Snifits]].

== Description ==
The Dark Lands consists of leafless hollow trees, many lava flows and volcanoes. Many small castles and palaces can be found here, so the Dark Lands are not ''completely'' devoid of architecture. However, the castles, much like everything else, are broken down, abandoned, and almost completely lifeless.

== History ==
As [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] was separated from [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]], Luigi was warped in the Dark Lands. Some [[Bat (Super Mario Galaxy)|Bats]] firghtend him flying out of a tree. Once he met a Dry Bones shortly after, he got chased down by a whole group of them, almost dying multiple times, whether it be by a Dry Bones or by [[lava]]. However, Luigi managed to find a [[Abandoned Castle|castle]] and shut the door between him and the Dry Bones. Unfortunately, Luigi doesn't have much time to rest, as a group of [[Shy Guy (Illumination)|Shy Guys]] and a [[Snifit (Illumination)|Snifit]] capture him behind him instead.

Later, they take Luigi on their blimp to [[Bowser's Castle (Illumination)|Bowser's Castle]]. Along the way, Luigi looks at the castle he just got taken away from, and remembered a time when he was a kid. He builds a toy castle, but it gets kicked down by a kid bully. However, the kid bully was driven away by Mario. This thought made Luigi start to miss his brother, while the Shy Guys made way toward Bowser's Castle.

== Trivia ==
=== References in video games and other media ===
* Luigi exploring the Dark Lands is a possible reference to [[Luigi's Mansion (series)|''Luigi's Mansion'' series]], the game series where Luigi's adventures are explored in dark and haunted places, specifically mansions.
* The Dark Lands may be a reference to [[Dark Land]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', and can be considered the movie equivalent of it.
