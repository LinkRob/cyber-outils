'''Toad Balloons''' are an item in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. They only appear in the [[Enemy Course]]s, which appear in every [[world]] except [[World 9 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9]]. Toad Balloons are round, blue balloons depicting an upset [[Toad (species)|Toad]]'s face. In an enemy course, the player's character must collect eight of these for a wooden [[treasure chest]] to appear. Collecting all eight Toad Balloons also causes any remaining enemy to be defeated. By opening the treasure chest, the Toad is freed, he provides three [[Super Mushroom]]s as a reward, and the [[level]] ends.
