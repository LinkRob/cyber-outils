'''World Crown''' is the fourth/final Special World and the twelfth and final world overall in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.  

== Description ==
It is unlocked after every [[Green Power Star]] and [[Stamp]] from the previous eleven worlds are collected, as well as getting the [[Gold Flag]] by reaching the top of the [[Goal Pole]] for every level. It takes place in the farthest reaches of space, with [[Luma]]s and [[Star Bit]]s floating about. 

This world contains three final courses: the last standard course, the last [[The Adventures of Captain Toad]] course, the last [[Mystery House]]. It also has the final [[Sprixie House]]. 

Its shape is based on its name, as the world resembles a golden crown.

==Courses==
*[[Champion's Road]]: The final course in ''Super Mario 3D World''
*[[Captain Toad's Fiery Finale]]: The final The Adventures of Captain Toad course in ''Super Mario 3D World''. It houses a Stamp.
*[[Mystery House Marathon]]: The final Mystery House in ''Super Mario 3D World'', with 30 Green Stars and a Stamp.

==Trivia==
*Once the Player(s) reach World Crown, the Course Lists for all Worlds, as well as the collected collectible preview for a stage in the overworld, gets updated to show off which characters have completed which courses, which helps with unlocking the "Clear Every Course with [X character]" Character Portrait Stamps.
**Champion's Road and Mystery House Marathon count towards this requirement as well, with standard exception with Captain Toad's Fiery Finale.
*The music played in World Crown is the theme of the [[Comet Observatory]] after its complete restoration from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.
*This is the only World that features [[Stamp]]s in every stage.
*Ironically, despite World Crown being the final world of ''Super Mario 3D World'', it is the smallest world in the game, and the only one to have both a Captain Toad Course and a Mystery House.
