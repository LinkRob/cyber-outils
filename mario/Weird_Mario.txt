'''Weird Mario''' is the form [[Mario]] take in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. The Mario troupe can use this form after picking up a [[Weird Mushroom (item)|Weird Mushroom]], a power-up found exclusively in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' style.

==Description==
===Appearance===
Mario becomes taller and skinnier after picking up the Weird Mushroom.
