'''Koopa Beach''' is a beach in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]'' and ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''. It is a playable court in the [[Mushroom Tourney]] and the [[Mushroom Cup]], respectively. 

== Game appearances ==
=== ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3'' ===
[[File:MH3o3 Koopa Beach Ship.png|thumb|left|In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3'']]
In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3'', a pirate ship can be seen on the water in the background that can sometimes shoot bombs in the field. If a player gets hit by it, they fall down and lose the ball. Also, players move slower when playing in the surf on either side of the field. In the background, there are two surfboards that have an [[Mario|M]] and [[Luigi|L]] on them, and the court does not have an audience. There are no [[Koopa Troopa|Koopas]] on the field, only a green shell design hanging over the court.

It is possible that Koopa Beach is a throwback to the Koopa Beach courses, [[Koopa Beach 1]] and [[Koopa Beach 2]] from ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''.

[[Peach Field]] can be seen in the background.

=== ''Mario Sports Mix'' ===
In ''Mario Sports Mix'', the court is renamed '''Koopa Troopa Beach'''. All sports can be played in this court, a trait shared with many other courts. [[Birdo (species)#Mario Sports Mix|Birdo]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Mii]]s and [[Pianta]]s can be seen in the background of this court.

The main feature of this court are the constant waves, which brings both [[Green Shell]]s and [[Coin]]s to use for the players. There are no features that can directly harm players in this stage. On a  side note, [[? Panel]]s never appear in this court regardless of settings. This makes Green Shells the only available items here.

In basketball, volleyball, and dodgeball, Koopa Troopa Beach is the second court in the Mushroom Cup, and can be played from the start. However, the hockey version is part of the Extra Cup and the player needs to unlock it by using alternate paths in the Hockey Tournament Mode.

In Volleyball mode, Koopa Troopa Beach is one of the two stages to change the volleyball's color, in this case, blue. The other is [[Western Junction]].
