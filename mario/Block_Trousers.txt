The '''Block Trousers''' is a type of [[clothing]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is not sold in stores, and the Block Trousers can be obtained in [[Thwomp Volcano]], and there is a 1.5% chance of a [[Thwack]] dropping a Block Trousers. The Block Trousers can only be equipped by [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], not their baby counterparts [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]]. Equipping the Block Trousers increases [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s [[Defense|DEF]] by 70.
