'''World 8-Airship''' is a level in [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' and the final airship level in the game.

== Overview ==
This Airship is where [[Princess Peach]] is being held. Bowser Jr. will give all he has got to stop Mario from reaching Peach.

== Walkthrough ==
The level begins near some [[crate]]s, as well as a screw that moves a Screwtop Platform blocking the path when the player spins on it. Two [[Mechakoopa]]s also come from a Screwtop Platform above. A section of [[Cannon]]s firing [[Cannonball]] follows. More crates, as well as a Cannons that fires giant cannonballs, are followed by a few more Cannons. More crates and Mechakoopas are found, along with three Cannons firing giant cannonballs. A Screwtop Lift is found, along with a Cannon firing giant cannonballs. After that, there is a [[Warp Pipe]] along with three rotating Cannons, leading the player to the next area below decks. A screw is used to lower hanging platforms, followed by another screw that controls Screwtop Platforms. A Warp Pipe leads the player to the next section of the level.

A screw that controls [[coin]]s is found, along with a Mechakoopa. A [[Red Ring]] is found, along with another screw that controls a metal platform. Another Screwtop Lift is found, with a Screwtop Platform also controlled by a screw in the way. The [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] then follows. Yet another Screwtop Lift is found, leading to a platform with a Mechakoopa on it. A screw that controls a large section of Screwtop Platforms, with some crates, a Mechakoopa, and the Warp Pipe leading to Bowser Jr. are found at the end.

In the end room, there is the final showdown against Bowser Jr. Bowser Jr. prepares to fight with his broken and damaged Junior Clown Car, but upon noticing Bowser's [[Koopa Clown Car]] also in the room, kicks away his personal Clown Car using his father's instead. He attacks Mario with fireballs and [[Spiked Bomb]]s, which can be used to defeat him by [[Ground Pound]]ing the ground which wobbles the ground flinging the bombs into the air. After the second hit, the Koopa Clown Car begins releasing larger bombs. After the third hit with a [[Giant Spiked Bomb]], Bowser Jr. is defeated. His defeat provides access to {{world-link|8|bowser|World 8-Bowser's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)}}, the site of the final showdown against Bowser where [[Kamek]] has whisked [[Princess Peach]] away to.

==Star Coins==
* '''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is floating high up in the air in the area with several [[cannon]]s. To obtain it, Mario and co. can either fly up to it with a [[Propeller Suit]], or jump up to it with the [[cannonball]]s or a well-timed [[Triple Jump|triple jump]].
* '''Star Coin 2''': Just after the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] are two platforms with two [[Mechakoopa]]s and the second Star Coin trapped inside. Mario and co. must ride the nearby Screwtop Lift under them and subsequently jump at the right time to get it without touching a Mechakoopa.
* '''Star Coin 3''': A Propeller Suit is required for this one. Soon after the second Star Coin, there is a lone screw. Mario and co. must spin on it to make the third Star Coin emerge from under the nearby platform, jump to it, and activate the Propeller Suit to fly to safety before falling. Alternatively, a KO'd Mechakoopa can be tossed at the Star Coin as well.
