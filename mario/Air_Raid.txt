'''Air Raid''' is a special move used by [[Parakarry]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Parakarry can use this move once he reaches the [[Ultra Rank]] and it requires 6 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Usage==
To use Air Raid, the player must tap the control stick in the left direction repeatedly ({{button|n64|stickleft}}) as Parakarry flies around the screen. As the gauge is filled, all enemies on the screen will be dealt 6 points of damage. Air Raid can be described as an upgraded [[Shell Shot]].

==Trivia==
*The way the Parakarry zooms around the screen as the gauge fills is similar to [[Sushie]]'s [[Tidal Wave]] move.
