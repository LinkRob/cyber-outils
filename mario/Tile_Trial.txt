'''Tile Trial''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is unlocked by completing the [[DVD for Me]] quest, given to the player as a reward from [[Ninji (character)|Ninji]].

==Description==
Tile Trial is a {{w|sliding puzzle}} with 15 tiles and an empty slot. The sliding puzzle image is of a [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]]. Just like a regular sliding puzzle, the player has to rearrange the panels in the correct order until they correctly form an image of the Mushroom. By pressing {{button|gba|R}}, the player can see an image of how the resulting image should look. The placement of the tiles are randomized whenever the Gaddget is started.

==Trivia==
* The Gaddget's name is a pun on [[Time Trial]], a mode in the {{series|Mario Kart}} and racing games in general.
