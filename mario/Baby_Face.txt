The '''Baby Face''' is a recurring character in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' and ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' franchises. He first appears in ''[[Mario Paint]]''.

==In {{franchise|Mario}}==
===''Mario Paint''===
[[File:Baby Face Mario Paint sprite.png|frame|left]]
In the drawing board, the Baby Face can be placed onto the canvas as a stamp. In the music composition mode, the Baby Face is one of the instruments that can be placed onto the score. When a Baby Face is played, it makes a sound similar to a baby's voice. This voice has since appeared in several other games.

In [[Gnat Attack]], a winking Baby Face is the fifth icon added to the top-left corner of the screen, after completing levels 1 through 3 five times in a row.

===''[[Mario Artist: Polygon Studio]]''===
[[File:Baby Face Polygon Studio.png|thumb|200px|The Baby Face in ''Mario Artist: Polygon Studio'']]
The Baby Face floats the background of the model creation menu, and clicking on the Baby Face plays its sound effect from ''Mario Paint''.

==={{series|Super Mario}}===
====''[[Super Mario Maker]]''====
[[File:SMM SFX 9.png|thumb|left|100px]]
The Baby Face appears as a placeable [[List of sound effects in Super Mario Maker|sound effect]], named '''Baby'''. When activated, the Baby Face appears briefly, and the sound effect plays the same sound as in ''Mario Paint''. Some of the game's [[List of Super Mario Maker stamps|stamps]] depict the Baby Face making different expressions.
{{br}}

====''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''====
The Baby Face returns as a [[List of sound effects in Super Mario Maker 2|sound effect]], functioning identically to its appearance in ''Super Mario Maker''. However, if used in night levels, the sound effect is pitch-shifted, and occasionally plays a sound of a baby (more like an adult) screaming instead.

==In {{franchise|Wario}}==
==={{series|WarioWare}}===
====''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''====
[[File:Baby Face WarioWare DIY.png|frame|left]]
The Baby Face is a stamp in the [[Game MakerMatic]] mode of the [[Super MakerMatic 21]]. It also appears in the downloadable [[microgame]] [[Boxed Up (WarioWare: D.I.Y.)|Boxed Up]], as one of the objects that has to be put into its corresponding box.
{{br|left}}

====''[[Game & Wario]]''====
[[File:Baby Face Game & Wario.png|thumb|250px]]
Baby Faces appear in two microgames in the [[Gamer]] stage. In [[Super Fly Swatter]], there is a possibility of the Baby Faces replacing the [[Fly (Mario Paint)|flies]] from ''Mario Paint''. In [[Windbag (microgame)|Windbag]], Baby Faces fall from the sky in level 1, along with [[Spiked Ball|spiked balls]]. However, unlike spiked balls, Baby Faces fall more quickly and suddenly, and only replace the player's head if they hit them, without causing them to lose the microgame.

====''[[WarioWare Gold]]''====
[[File:Baby Face Shave the World.png|thumb|left|200px]]
The Baby Face appears in a few microgames. In [[Shave the World]], the Baby Face can appear on the third difficulty level, as one of the faces possibly revealed by shaving the Earth. In [[Tiptoe Titan]], the Baby Face is one of the characters who can walk under [[Wario]]'s feet. In [[Wario Interrupts]], one of [[Wario Deluxe]]'s distractions replaces every sound played during a microgame with the Baby Face's sound effect.

Aside from microgames, the Baby Face also appears in [[Wario Kard]], as an enemy which only uses scissors or paper.

====''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''====
[[File:Baby Face Break Room.jpg|thumb|250px|The Baby Face customization option in the Break Room in ''WarioWare: Get It Together!'']]
A special customization option named Baby Face appears in the [[Break Room]], unlocked after reaching level 6. When this customization is applied to a crew member, they adopt a red and white color scheme, and their voice is replaced with the Baby Face's sound effect.
