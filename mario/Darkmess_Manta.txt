The '''Darkmess Manta''' is a malevolent entity that appears early in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

==History== 
The Darkmess Manta appears just as [[Mario]] and his friends were about to relax. It begins to infect the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] with Darkmess and Mario tries to warn the distracted [[Rabbid Peach]], but the latter ends up getting abducted by the entity and [[Princess Peach]] helps Mario board the Manta to save her Rabbid counterpart. Mario and [[Beep-0]] managed to rescue Rabbid Peach and they fight the strange minions which are revealed to belong to [[Cursa]], who is trying to find [[Starburst|one]] of the [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Sparks]] that escaped from it. The team managed to rescue Mario, Rabbid Peach, and Beep-0 while avoiding Cursa's astral hands.
