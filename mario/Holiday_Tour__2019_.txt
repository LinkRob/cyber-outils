'''Holiday Tour''' was the seventh Tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] that began on December 17, 2019 and ended on December 31, 2019. 

This Tour event took place during the Holiday's in which Yoshi (Reindeer) and pauline (party Time) where featured after Christmass theme. Just like [[London Tour]] and [[Winter Tour (2019)]], festive trees where added on all tracks featured in the first week. Hitting the tree will give the player "Happy holidays" bonus points.

This Tour added [[Birdo]] who becomes playable again in the [[Mario Kart (series)]] since [[Mario Kart Wii]]. This tour also added: [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 7)|3DS Rainbow Road]] from [[Mario Kart 7]], a variant of [[New York Minute]]: [[New York Minute 2]] and introduced R/T variants of [[Mario Circuit 1|SNES Mario Circuit 1]], [[Kalimari Desert|N64 Kalimari Desert]] and [[Cheep Cheep Lagoon|3DS Cheep Cheep Lagoon]].
