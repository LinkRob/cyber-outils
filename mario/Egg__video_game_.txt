'''Egg''' is one of the [[Game & Watch]] titles included in ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 3]]'', both in its original and remade incarnations, the latter featuring ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' content. 

== Original ==
In the original version you play as a wolf trying to take chicken eggs after they lay them.
{{Sectstub}}

== Modern ==
In the modern version of the game you play as [[Cyan Yoshi|Yoshi]] using his tongue to eat treats made by Mario.
