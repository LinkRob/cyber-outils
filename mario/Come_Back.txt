{{Nihongo|'''Come Back'''|おねがいカムバック|Onegai Kamu Bakku|Please Come Back}} is one of [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]]'s special moves in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
Come Back has the same effects as the [[Life Shroom]] and the [[Pick Me Up]]. It can be used to restore the life of fainted characters. It appears to be a blue light, and a flashing star following the selected character.

If the player [[Timed Hit|times this]] right, when the character is revived he will receive his full HP. If it isn't timed right, the character will receive back a half of his HP. To time it, just press any of the letter buttons right after the star hits the character you are reviving.

==Trivia==
*The [[Pick Me Up]]s overshadows Come Back, because they do not need timing.
