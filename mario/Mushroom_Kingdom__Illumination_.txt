'''Mushroom Kingdom''' is a location that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. It is a landscape region of the [[Mushroom World (Illumination)|Mushroom World]] continent where many [[Toad's species (Illumination)|Toad]] residents and [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]] live.

== History ==
The Mushroom Kingdom first appears when Toad offers to take Mario on a tour straight to the princess' castle, going through several obstacles along the way. When they arrive at the castle, two Toad guards halt them from passing through, so Toad distracted them while signaling Mario to pass by and enter the castle; which only leads to him being detected by others inside.

Meanwhile, the Mushroom Kingdom's monarch, Princess Peach was observing with other Toads about Bowser obtaining the Super Star, and his plan to head over to their kingdom, with the assumption that he will destroy it, much to the others horror. Peach plans on traveling to the Jungle Kingdom to acquire their army, before encountering Mario. Upon finding out he needs help to rescue his brother, Peach agrees on one term; complete her obstacle course. This takes Mario the rest of the day, and until next morning to complete, but shows Peach that he is worthy enough to join her travels, and eventually leave the castle and kingdom.

The kingdom later appears when Peach and Toad lose Mario and the army, which prompts them to quickly ride back there to warn the others about Bowser's arrival and evacuate them to the forest. Soon enough, Bowser's fortress lands and the Koopa King himself attempts to forcefully propose Peach to marriage, leaving the fortress standing above the kingdom.

Mario, alongside Donkey Kong, soon arrive back at the Mushroom Kingdom and battle Bowser's army to reach the fortress, where a wedding is being held between Peach and Bowser. Sometime after his wedding being crashed, Bowser orders a Bomber Bill to be launched directly at the Mushroom Kingdom, having Peach gasp in shock, and for Mario to reroute it to his warp pipe.

After Bowser's defeat, the Koopa King was shrunk as punishment, and transported into a birdcage inside the kingdom castle, while Mario and Luigi moved into a house of their own in the kingdom.
