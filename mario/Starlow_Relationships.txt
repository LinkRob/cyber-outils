== Mario ==
[[File:Starlow Saved Screenshot - MLBiS.png|thumb|Starlow thanking Mario, from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''|left|163x163px]]
Starlow is one of [[Mario]]'s friends and closest ally that she admires Mario's bravery by treating him in respect and kindly, while also teaching basic fights and skills on their journey. Although Starlow would not hesitate to speak her mind if she needs to. In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', Mario saves Starlow from an amoeba attack.

== Luigi ==
[[File:Luigi&Starlow-Paper Jam.jpg|thumb|left|[[Luigi]] and Starlow in [[Gloomy Woods]], from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''|163x163px]]
{{Q|Luigi, listen. You can do it! I know you can save [[Mario|both]] [[Paper Mario (character)|Marios]]! I mean, you're Luigi, man! You're tall! You've got a sweet mustache! You had, like, [[The Year of Luigi|a whole YEAR named after you]]... And, well... GOOOOOOO, LUIGI! Plus, I mean... it's basically Luigi RPG now!|Starlow, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''}}
Starlow and [[Luigi]] are good friends and often help each other. However, Starlow relationship with Luigi starts out far colder since their [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|first adventure]]. Unlike Mario, who she admires and respects him, Starlow often expresses her annoyance at Luigi's cowardice, as shown when Luigi tells Starlow to fight [[Alpha Kretin]] instead of himself and she angrily pushes him. Even when Luigi feels shiver due to the [[Airway (Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story)|Airway]] being cold with [[Bowser]] inhaling ice from a defeated [[Midbus]], Starlow seriously tells Luigi not to be cold.
[[File:Luigi&StarlowDreamTeam.jpg|thumb|Starlow watching over Luigi being asleep, from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.|right|163x163px]]
Although Starlow claims to have a jealously and negative strained relationship towards Luigi, she does have some care for him after learning from his bravery against [[Shroob]]s in [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time|the past]] and for his worried concerns about what Bowser will do to [[Paper Toad]]s he kidnapped. Starlow also helped keeping an eye on Luigi in his sleep as Mario and [[Prince Dreambert]] head to [[Dream World]]. Unaware of the dangers going on there, Luigi on his emotions quickly tells Starlow to press down on the L on [[Luigi's Cap|his cap]], causing [[Dreamy Luigi|his dreamy counterpart]] and [[Luiginoid Formation|multiple ones]] to become [[Giant Luigi]].

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', Starlow even understands Luigi being scared of ghosts and encourages to let go of his fear and be a hero to rescue [[Mario]] and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] from [[King Boo]] and [[Boo|his army]] in [[Gloomy Woods]]. As time passes on, it is possible Starlow attitude towards Luigi changed as she now treats Luigi with more respect, though while still occasionally mocking him.

== Prince Dreambert ==
Starlow gets along well with [[Prince Dreambert]], although his lapses in memory over key pieces of information cause her great frustration, leading her to even call him "Forgetbert" on occasion. Nonetheless, she joins Dreambert with Mario and Luigi throughout the adventure while face [[Antasma]] and Bowser. 

Starlow and Dreambert are alike in many ways, both share a similar behavior expression towards other people and their experience against villains: Starlow with Fawful and Bowser, whereas Dreambert with Antasma.

== Bowser ==
[[File:M&LBIS+BJJ Starlow Final Cutscene.jpg|thumb|Starlow meeting [[Bowser]] as she leaves a surprise gift for him in front of [[Kamek]] and the [[Elite Trio]], from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.|left|163x163px]]
When inhaled inside of Bowser's body, [[Starlow]] assumes the alter-ego of "Chippy" and presents as a friend to help guide him to fight back against [[Fawful]]. Bowser considers "Chippy" to be a minion, but over time grows to learn to treat "Chippy" with better manners and appreciates "Chippy's" skills, support and company. However, upon learning that "Chippy" is Starlow, Bowser feels angered and betrayed seemingly ending their friendship. 

They would turn up to be hostile foes at [[Pi'illo Island]] during ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', then again regarding the [[Mysterious Book]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

At some point following after those events, Starlow and Bowser had begun to try and rebuild their friendship. Bowser appeared comfortable enough to welcome visits from Starlow when she was off-duty and began to call her "Chippy" again. In [[Concordia]] on [[Wayaway Island]] during ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', Starlow visits an injured Bowser when he had formed a tentative truce with [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] against [[Zokket]], after having been beaten by the brothers. When Starlow enters his room to give him a [[Drumstick]], Bowser expresses that not even she gets to touch him, implying she is held in higher regard than others. He also brags about his physical strength and resilience to her, suggesting he cares about how she perceives him. When Mario and Luigi enter after, Bowser gets frustrated and escorts all of them out.

== Bowser Jr. ==
[[File:Starlow and Bowser Jr..jpg|thumb|Starlow and [[Bowser Jr.]] fight on eye to eye, from [[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]].|left|163x163px]]
Starlow knows about [[Bowser]]’s son, Bowser Jr. Like Bowser, Starlow considers his son enemies as she has some animosity towards him. In ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'', when Starlow calls Bowser Jr. “Bowser’s kid”, he angrily calls her a “yellow ball with feet”, causing a conflict between the two. Nonetheless she tells Bowser Jr. encourage his journey even praising his father slightly by telling him that he is "[[Giant Bowser|working hard]]" (implied to be referring to his [[Giant Bowser|Super Bowser form]]).

She doesn’t encounter Bowser Jr. until [[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam|later events]].

== Fawful ==
[[File:M&L-BIS Fawful demise.jpg|thumb|The trio confront an unstable Dark Fawful, from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'']]
{{Q|Look give up already. Seriously, get over it. [[Fawful|You're]] done with evil.|Starlow, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''}}
Starlow had a little interaction with Fawful before his demise. During the [[Fawful]] [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|Incident]], Starlow and the others find out Fawful was responsible for [[Blorbs]] cause which directly sends Starlow alongside [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]] and [[Toad (species)|few]] [[Toadsworth|others]] inside [[Bowser's Body]]. When Starlow hears about Fawful's plan to awaken the [[Dark Star]], she helped the Bros. as well as Bowser to stop his plan. After Bowser finally defeated Fawful in his [[Dark Fawful|dark form]] turned into dark tiny insect like. Starlow wonders about Fawful's behavior, only to realize he has most of the Dark Star power inside him, making her terrified.

However, Starlow's fear became true as Fawful was swallowed by [[Dark Bowser]] to complete his whole form. With no choice, Bowser would soon fight against his dark self in a final showdown, while Starlow, Mario and Luigi fight against both the Dark Star and Fawful. After they successfully defeated them, Starlow angerly demands a disembodied Fawful to give up his evil ways and disappear for good to which he faces remorse and accepts it. Fawful, in a psychotic smile way gives the trio one last ditch attempt to self-destruct himself, causing a massive explosion that sends Starlow, the Bros. and everyone else out of Bowser's body.
