'''Mousersaurus Rex''' is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' animated episode "[[Quest for Pizza]]." He is a giant red dinosaur who resembles [[Mouser]]. He assists [[Alley Koopa]], [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s alias used throughout the episode.
