A '''Recovery Boost''' is a technique featured in every Mario Kart game since ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. After falling off the track or going out of bounds, [[Lakitu]] will fish you out and drop you back on the course. If you press the accelerate button as soon as you hit the ground after being dropped, you will receive a small boost to get you going, about the same as a [[Mini-Turbos|mini-turbo]]. This is very helpful to avoid losing further time after a recovery since you would need to accelerate from zero, which can take a while for low acceleration karts.
