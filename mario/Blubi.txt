|race = [[Nimbi]]}}'''Blubi''' is a character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Blubi is a [[Nimbi]] that has a thick mustache. He also wears blue robes fitting his title as the "Blue Sage."

==History==
Blubi is first discovered frozen in ice in [[The Overthere]]. After [[Bowser]] frees him from his icy prison, Blubi is grateful but has amnesia. Fortunately, [[Luvbi]] manages to snap him out of it for a second and Blubi gives the [[Blue Orb]] to the heroes.
