|name=Big Hammer (Battle Card)}}'''Big Hammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Hammer is the strong version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a big brown hammer with an orange/tan checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 18/75 coins

Sell Price: 9/38 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Hammer is first seen in [[The Crimson Tower]], but can also found in [[Mondo Woods]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Green Energy Plant]], and [[Black Bowser's Castle]].
