The microgame shares the same music with [[Sticky Shift]].

==Gameplay==
The player must grate the whole object before time runs out; otherwise, it ends within a loss. The player must move the [[Wii Remote]] back and forth in "Thumb Wrestler" form.
*'''Level 1:''' Grate a small object which can be a cellphone or an alarm clock.
*'''Level 2: '''Grate a medium object, a fridge or safe.
*'''Level 3:''' Grate a large object, Earth or a building.

===''WarioWare Gold''===
The game's music changed. The microgame belongs to [[Kat and Ana]] who belongs to the "Touch League". This microgame's genre is "That's Life". The player must grate the object left and right rapidly before time runs out.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The player must grate a simple object, a carrot.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The player must grate a medium-sized object, a washing machine.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' the player must grate a medium-sized object, saucer.
