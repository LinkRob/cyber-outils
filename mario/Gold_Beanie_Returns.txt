'''Gold Beanie Returns''' is the fourth level of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 16.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 4 [[Sharpea]]s, 2 [[Paratroopea]]s, 1 [[Boomerang Bro]], and 1 [[Clumph]]. The enemy captain is the Clumph.

The 2nd battle contains 4 Clumphs, 3 Paratroopeas, and 1 Boomerang Bro. The enemy captain is a Clumph.

The final battle contains 3 Clumphs, 3 Paratroopeas, 1 Boomerang Bro, and 1 [[Gold Beanie]]. The enemy captain is the Gold Beanie.

The level can be replayed to recruit up to 1 Boomerang Bro.
