'''Bridget the Baker''' is a character in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical appearance===
Bridget has dark blue hair, in which she has a red flower pin. Bridget wears turquoise pants and a long-sleeve shirt, over which she wears a yellow apron.

===Personality===
Bridget is able to fulfill a variety of careers, being not only a baker but also a singer.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
[[File:Luna.png|thumb|left|As a singer, Luna is her stage name.]]
In [[Wario]]'s stage, [[Touch Training]], after a dentist visit from [[Dr. Payne]], Wario goes to the [[Sweet Spot]], ignoring when Dr. Payne told him to not eat more sweet foods, and he asks Bridget for ten pies to eat. After one bite, Wario immediately gains cavities again, and the resulting pain sends Wario flying back to Dr. Payne's clinic. Bridget, seemingly oblivious to Wario's pain, gives him a friendly goodbye and asks him to come again soon.

She later appears in [[Mona]]'s stage, [[Cute Cuts]], under the stage name '''Luna'''. She appears on the show [[Ear Candy]] as one of the artists to have cracked the Top 5, appearing at number four while singing a short portion of [[Ashley's Theme]]. Her song is also featured in [[Ashley & Red|Ashley]]'s stage.

==Quotes==
*"Thank you, come again!"
