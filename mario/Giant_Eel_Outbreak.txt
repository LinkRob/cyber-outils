'''Giant Eel Outbreak''' is the only mission of [[Drip Drop Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and its high-definition [[Reissue|remaster]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]''.

==Layout==
[[Gringill]]s are the main obstacle encountered. Aside from them, there are also [[Torpedo Ted]]s, [[Urchin]]s, and [[Puffer Fish]].

==Objective==
[[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] has to defeat three Gringills by throwing either a [[Green Shell]] or a [[Red Shell]] at each one. In doing so, a [[Power Star]] appears at a sunken ship for Mario and Luigi to collect.

==''Super Mario Galaxy''==
The reason for the mission is because the Gringills have infested the planet's waters, and the [[Penguin]]s cannot go fishing because the fish are being eaten by Gringills.
