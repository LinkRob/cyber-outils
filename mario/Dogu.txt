'''Dogu''' is a clay figure and a character who appears in ''[[Wrecking Crew '98]]''.

==Etymology==
Dogu is named for the fact that he is a {{w|Dogū}}, humanoid and animal figurines created by Japan several centuries ago.

==In ''Wrecking Crew '98''==
[[Mario]] can face off against Dogu in Stage D once he has won against the other secret opponents, [[Onigiri]], [[Onnanoko]], and [[Oyazi]]. Once defeated, Dogu becomes unlocked for play in both VS. Mode and Tournament Mode.
