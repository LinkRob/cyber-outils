The '''Diamond City Roughs''' are a team of football players who appear in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', during [[Mona]]'s stage, in which they compete against [[The Dinosaurs]]. They have the [[Monettes]], led by Mona, for a cheerleader. One of the Diamond City Rough's players is [[Boy]], and he has a crush on Mona.

==Description==
The Diamond City Roughs dress in blue uniforms. Their helmet has a white star on it.
