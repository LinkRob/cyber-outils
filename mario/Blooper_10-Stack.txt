'''Blooper 10-Stacks''' are an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. They are a variant of [[Blooper]] consisting of ten Bloopers in a stack, but this number can be gradually reduced by attacking the Bloopers, each having 1 [[Heart Point|HP]]. Blooper 10-Stacks are encountered in [[Lighthouse Island]].
