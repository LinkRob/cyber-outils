'''Nurse Toadstool''' is an alias of [[Princess Peach]] in the [[Dr. Mario (series)|''Dr. Mario'' series]]. Although she is [[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]]'s assistant, Nurse Toadstool does not have much of a major role in her appearances. She mostly just appeared in artwork associated with games such as ''[[Dr. Mario (video game)|Dr. Mario]]'', which is her technical debut as she is featured in the backstory within the game's instruction booklet.

==Physical description==
Nurse Toadstool wears a pink nurse's uniform instead of a dress and a nurse's hat in place of her crown. She also wears pink sandals.

==Other appearances==
In the ''[[Club Nintendo (magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' comic "[[Süße Weihnachten]]," Nurse Toadstool can be seen assisting Dr. Mario.
