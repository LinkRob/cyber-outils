'''Wall Ball''' is a minigame in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. In both games, Wall Ball is hosted within [[Princess Peach]]'s stadium, [[Peach Garden]] from ''Mario Superstar Baseball'' and [[Peach Ice Garden]] from ''Mario Super Sluggers''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Wall Ball Superstar Baseball.png|thumb|250px|left|Wall Ball in ''Mario Superstar Baseball'']]
In both games, the minigame involves four character each competing to earn the most [[point]]s, by throwing a ball into a row of Brick Walls each round. Whoever ends with the most points wins.

===''Mario Superstar Baseball''===
In ''Mario Superstar Baseball'', the minigame is turn-based, and the points are represented by [[coin]]s. There are three types of walls: a Brick Wall, a ♪ Wall, and a Bowser Wall. Each player tries to hit the ♪ Wall, which is worth 100 points. Brick Walls are worth five points each, and Bowser Walls have to be avoided because if a character throws a ball into one, they lose half of their coins, which are equally divided amongst the other characters. A meter is displayed on the right side of the screen, and it shows how strong or weak they will throw the ball.

The camera is shown at an angle from the brick wall, with the characters standing in the back.

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
In ''Mario Super Sluggers'', the minigame is no longer turn-based, and the game camera is fixed on the characters. The minigame now uses actual points for each character's score. The walls are now known as blocks, and the only returning one is the Bowser Block. The other two are the Green Blocks and Pink Blocks, the latter having Princess Peach's tiara on top, although there are regular light blue blocks as well, essentially replacing the Brick Walls from ''Mario Superstar Baseball''. Green Blocks are worth a small bonus if hit last, while Pink Blocks are worth a higher bonus if hit last, essentially replacing the ♪ Wall from the previous game. If a character hits a Bowser Wall, they lose fifty points instead.

==In ''Mario Superstar Baseball''==
In [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]], Wall Ball, like other four starting minigames, [[Bob-omb Derby]], [[Chain Chomp Sprint]], [[Piranha Panic]], and [[Barrel Batter]], can be played up to three times as an opportunity to obtain additional [[coin]]s. Here, the player has to verse against three [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s.
