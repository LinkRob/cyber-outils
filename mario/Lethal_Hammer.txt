'''Lethal Hammer''' is a rare ghost in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' and a variant of a [[Hammer (enemy)]]. They only appear in [[ScareScraper]]'s ten floors or twenty floors mod. They have 350HP.

==Appearance==

Lethal Hammers have black and glowing red bodies instead of red bodies, they have red eyes, black eyebrows, two tails and red mouths.

==In ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' ==

Like all the other Lethal ghosts, Lethal Hammers can knock out [[Luigi]] in one hit. Lethal Hammers only appear in ScareScraper's fifth floor in ten floors mod, fifth, tenth and fifteenth floor in twenty floors mod. They will appear after the door opens, in order to defeat them, Luigi has to flash them and vacuum them up.
