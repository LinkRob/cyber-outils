'''Shadow the Dog''' is the pet dog of [[Kat and Ana]] who appears in the earlier ''[[WarioWare]]'' series, having made his debut appearance in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. Shadow has the ability to transform into a giant sword.
