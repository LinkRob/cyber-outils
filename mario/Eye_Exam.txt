'''Eye Exam''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is unlocked by clearing the [[Weeping Thwomp]] quest.

==Description==
Like all other Gaddgets, the Eye Exam was invented by [[Professor E. Gadd]]. The tool initializes with a right-side up C zoomed in to 20/200, though it is also able to alter itself to 20/100, 20/60, 20/50, 20/40, 20/30, 20/28, 20/25, 20/22, and 20/20. These increments tally up to ten optional sizes in total. Once the Gaddget is zoomed in to the maximum level it can reach, the c-shaped silhouette can be changed into [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], followed by [[Princess Peach]], then [[Luigi]], and finally [[Mario]]. While the down button is used to revert the images back, only the c-shaped silhouette can be altered. If the player presses the A button eight times, the device allows the c-shaped silhouette to be reversed one whole time.
