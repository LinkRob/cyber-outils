'''Game Boy - Nintendo Classics''', formerly referred to as '''Game Boy - Nintendo Switch Online''', is an application for the [[Nintendo Switch]] and [[Nintendo Switch 2]]. For the former, it was released alongside [[Game Boy Advance - Nintendo Classics|Game Boy Advance - Nintendo Switch Online]] on February 8, 2023 in the Americas and February 9, 2023 in most other regions, shortly following its announcement in the [[w:c:nintendo:Nintendo Direct|Nintendo Direct]] on February 8, 2023; it was later released on September 9, 2025 in Taiwan.<!-- The app may officially launch in Malaysia, the Philippines, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, and Thailand in the near future as Nintendo Switch Online is confirmed for these markets. --> It is available for all [[Nintendo Switch Online]] subscribers and features a library of [[Game Boy]] and [[Game Boy Color]] games, which can be played in the Game Boy, [[w:c:nintendo:Game Boy Pocket|Game Boy Pocket]], or Game Boy Color screen filters. Multiplayer-compatible games such as ''[[Tetris]]'' can be played with other Nintendo Switch devices locally or online.<ref>Nintendo of America (February 8, 2023). [https://youtu.be/_-KAU3bK1Y8 Nintendo Switch Online - Game Boy & Game Boy Advance Announcement - Nintendo Direct 2.8.23]. ''YouTube''. Retrieved February 8, 2023.</ref>

The European versions of the games are available on the North American version of the app.

Special color palettes that would be used on the Game Boy Color by holding down specific buttons when starting the games, or [[Super Game Boy]]–specific color palettes, are not available. There is also no [[Transfer Pak]] connectivity with [[Nintendo 64 - Nintendo Classics]].

The app's version 3.0.0 update changed the app's subtitle from "Nintendo Switch Online" to "Nintendo Classics". It also added button remapping, updated the "Reproduce classic feel" screen filter, and changed the app's startup splash screen to use the sound effect of a [[coin]] from ''[[Super Mario Land]]'', rather than the Game Boy and Game Boy Color's startup logo, to match with the other Nintendo Classics apps. Like the [[Game Boy Advance - Nintendo Classics]] and [[Nintendo GameCube - Nintendo Classics]] apps, the Game Boy's original startup screen can be seen by holding any direction on {{button|ns|leftstick}} upon startup, with the startup being different depending on which "Game screen" option has been chosen. On the Nintendo Switch 2, the application now runs at a resolution of 1080p in handheld mode. There were no changes for resolution in TV mode and the controls page still shows a Joy-Con or a Nintendo Switch Pro Controller even when a Joy-Con 2 or a Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller is connected, unlike [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Classics]] onward.

==Default button mapping==
Despite the fact that only one controller can be connected to the app at a time and multiplayer requires each player to own a console, the app allows players to use a single Joy-Con.
