A '''Mermushroom''' is a variant of the [[Toad (species)|Mushroom People]] featured exclusively in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Mario of the Deep]]." They live in [[Aqua Land]], where they are ruled by [[King Neptune]].
