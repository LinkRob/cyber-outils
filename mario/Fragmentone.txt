'''Fragmentones''' are puzzle-shaped [[Fractone|Fractones]] that can be found in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. 

== Appearance ==
Fragmentones are Fractones whose main body has lost pieces far from itself, giving them a shape resembling a lone puzzle piece. Their missing portions become sentient, semicircular pieces called '''Fragments'''. Once brought back to the main body, they merge into a single seamless entity again.
