'''Club 64''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is located at the docks of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and the known residents are the [[Club 64 Bartender|Bartender]] and [[Chanterelle]]. It appears to be a small café and is the only one found in the game.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Visiting Club 64 isn't necessary for the main story, but it does serve some purpose for obtaining an optional [[badge]]. At one point in the game (around Chapter 4), the [[Master Poet]] will request [[Mario]] to find some lyrics from the [[Toad in the Desert|famous composer]] living in [[Dry Dry Outpost]]. After Mario retrieves the [[Lyrics|lyrics]], Chanterelle will sing her new song and after she is finished, she will give Mario the [[Attack FX D]] badge.
