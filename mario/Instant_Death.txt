An '''Instant Death '''attack is a type of an attack that is mostly seen in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

As its name tells, the character on that is used dies instantly, no matter what is his or her HP, Defense, Magic Defense etc. There are no more than 5 of these in the game. An example of an attack is Jinx's [[Silver Bullet]] and [[Smithy]]'s Tank Head's Magnum.
