'''Rhinestone Goomba''' is an enemy found exclusively to ''[[Hotel Mario]]''.

==History==
[[File:RhinestoneGoombaBox.png|thumb|left|A Rhinestome Goomba on Hotel Mario's boxart.]]They resemble the famous rock 'n' roll singer, [[wikipedia:Elvis Presley|Elvis Presley]] who is deceased. They have similar talents to normal [[Goomba]]s, with them walking back and forth left and right indefinitely until [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] jumps on them. They are quicker than normal Goombas however.
