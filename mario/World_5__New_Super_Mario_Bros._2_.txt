'''World 5''' is the fifth [[world]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It can be unlocked by completing [[World 4-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 4-Castle]] or [[World Flower-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Flower-Castle]]. The castle boss of this world is [[Ludwig von Koopa]].

== Description ==
World 5 is a sky-themed world, similar to how [[Sky Land]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' is the fifth world of said game. This world features many [[Mushroom Platform]]s and sky towers. It has six normal levels, one unlockable level, a [[Ghost House]], a [[Tower]], a [[Castle]] and a Rainbow Course level, making up eleven levels from this world, similar to the amount of total levels from [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' Additionally both worlds have the Ghost House done before the Tower level if taking the normal route. World 5 has four [[Toad House]]s, and shares the same music theme as [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7]] from ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

World 5 is the second normal world to combine two world themes into one, with that being [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]] combining both beach and jungle themes, World 5 combines both sky and mountain, though only [[World 5-6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|one level]] is mountain-themed.

Completing this world unlocks the final normal world, [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]].
