'''Venus Ice Traps''', also named '''Ice Piranha Plants''', are enemies that first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. They are the ice-based equivalent of [[Venus Fire Trap]]s, and spit [[Ice Ball]]s, which are capable of freezing [[Mario]] or another playable character in an ice cube. Venus Ice Traps can commonly spit either one or two [[Ice Ball]]s at a time.

==Description==
Venus Ice Traps are similar in appearance to [[Piranha Plant]]s except that they can spit ice balls. Some may also hang upside down.

==Appearances==
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]] / [[New Super Luigi U]] / [[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
[[Image:Venus Ice Trap - Ice Cream Freeze.png|thumb|left|A Venus Ice Trap spitting an Ice Ball while perched upside-down]]
Venus Ice Traps are first seen in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and later appear in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. They behave similarly to a [[Venus Fire Trap]] however, they spit [[Ice Ball]]s rather than [[Fireball]]s. There are only a few on the levels and one of them is actually required to earn a [[Star Coin]]. Mario can defeat them in any way (ranging from the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]])  but the best way to deal with them is to eat them with a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. After Yoshi captures a Venus Ice Trap, he can spit out the ice ball as a projectile to freeze other nearby enemies. Venus Ice Traps only appear on the first level of [[Rock-Candy Mines]]: [[Fuzzy Clifftop]] and in levels of the  [[Frosted Glacier]] such as [[Broozers and Barrels]]. They can be dealt with in the same way as a regular [[Piranha Plant]].

==Trivia==
*Venus Ice Traps are similar to [[Frost Piranha]]s from the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series as both are [[Piranha Plant]]s with the ability to spit ice. However, there are several key differences, namely how Frost Piranhas can spit clouds of ice whereas Venus Ice Traps only spit out [[Ice Ball]]s. Additionally, Frost Piranhas are blue in color while Venus Ice Traps have the same color as a regular [[Piranha Plant]].
