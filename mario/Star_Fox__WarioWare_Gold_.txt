'''Star Fox''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. As its name implies, it is set in the [[w:c:starfox:Star Fox (series)|franchise of the same name]], more specifically ''Star Fox SNES'', the first game of the series.

== Gameplay ==
[[w:c:starfox:Fox McCloud|Fox]] must steer his [[w:c:starfox:Arwing|Arwing]] to dodge obstacles and enemies; this is done by tilting the 3DS. If the player presses the A button, Fox can shoot enemies. The number of enemies and obstacles goes higher with more enemies. If Fox gets shot down, the player loses.

The rules say: ''"Tilt the Arwing to avoid the obstacles! You can even shoot down enemies while you're at it!"''
