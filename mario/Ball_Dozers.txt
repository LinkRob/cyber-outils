'''Ball Dozers''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. Its name is a pun on "{{wp|bulldozer}}s," a type of construction vehicle.

==Opening==
The view changes from the bottom of the area to the top. The players then emerge from the sides of the screen, and the minigame begins as the balls roll into the arena from [[Warp Pipe]]s.

==Gameplay==
The players must use their [[hammer]]s to smash the pegs impeding their balls' progress and allow them to roll to the bottom of the pinball machine-like area. The team players have a much bigger blue ball, while the solo player has a much smaller orange ball. The side whose ball reaches the bottom first wins.

==Ending==
The winning side's ball rolls down the pipe at the bottom of the area. They then perform their victory animations in the center of the area. If the minigame ends in a tie, both teams perform their defeat animations.

===Glitch===
*If one team's ball rolls down the pipe after the timer hits 0, a glitch will occur where the team that got their ball in the pipe doing their victory animation(s), while the other team can be seen performing their defeat animation(s).
**The minigame can also be seen reloading to the winning cutscene status and the screen is still split into two, due to the trigger of the black fade, different camera position and single screen for the winning cutscene not activating.
**Noticeably, the winning team can always be seen teleporting to the left screen. Even if the team players (on the right screen) win, they can be seen teleporting to the left screen, while the solo player (on the left screen) teleports to the right screen.
**Despite all this, the minigame still ends in a tie and the tie music will play, as well as the word "Tie" showing up on the screen and the announcer calling it out.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8wfe0aojfzI&t=396s</ref>

* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Swing hammer
