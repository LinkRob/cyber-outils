'''Big Bob-omb's Revenge''' is the fourth [[Mission]] of [[Bob-omb Battlefield]] exclusive only for ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]].''

== Description ==
To collect the fourth [[Power Star]] of [[Bob-omb Battlefield]], either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Wario]], or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] (wearing a [[cap]] of the previous three characters) must scale up the mountain for a second time to fight [[King Bob-omb]] for another [[Power Star]]. If the Cannon is unlocked, you can aim ajacent to the island (by making sure the top arrow is aligned with the bottom of the island) and after launching, you will be sent flying and you can steer your character to land atop the mountain. The fight is a continuation of the original fight from [[Big Bob-omb on the Summit]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. Big Bob-omb will not use his Bob-omb army against you but will try to pick you up and throw you off the mountain, this time, he’s a lot more aggressive, Mario, Luigi, or Wario can beat him by picking him up from behind and throwing him down three times, to make the Star appear however if they were to throw him off the mountain, he will jump back up, scold them for their lack of honor and it won’t count as a hit.
