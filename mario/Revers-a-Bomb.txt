'''Revers-a-Bomb''' is a recurring 2 vs. 2 minigame in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
In Revers-a-Bomb, the two teams must blow each other up by sending [[Bob-omb]]s their way. The Bob-ombs can be switched back and forth by pressing the switches forcing the players to work together frantically to ensure their side doesn't get blown up. The players must also be careful not to directly get hit by an exploding Bob-omb or they will be stunned for a few seconds. Both teams have a total of 10 hits to take. The team that takes out the other first or has the highest point count at the end of the minigame wins.

==Controls==
The controls are the same in all three iterations of Revers-a-Bomb.

*Control Stick: Move character
*A button: Hit switch
