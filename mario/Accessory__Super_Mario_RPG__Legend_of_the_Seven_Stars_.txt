An '''Accessory''' is a type of item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
An '''Accessory''' is a type of item that only appeared ''Super Mario RPG''. Accessories raise stats or prevent status effects in battle. Every accessory has their own criteria of obtaining the item.
