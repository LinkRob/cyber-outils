The '''Dreambeats '''are a type of music created by [[Antasma]] that make the hearer fall asleep randomly. Some such as Pi'illos, Starlow (possibly because she doesn't appear to have ears), and Antasma are unaffected by their tone. [[Bowser]] too was unaffected but this was mostly due to him covering his ears.

In the story, once [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Dreambert]], [[Starlow]], and the [[Big Massif|Massif]] [[Lil' Massif|Bros.]] reach the top of [[Mount Pajamaja]], [[Bowser]] and Antasma are planning to blow the Dreambeats out of some speakers.

This easily knocks out [[Kamek]], [[Corporal Paraplonk]], [[Sergeant Guy]], and [[Private Goomp]] and Luigi almost faints from the music but safely falls asleep at a [[Dreampoint]] allowing [[Mario]] to escape to the [[Dream World]]. Various residents of [[Wakeport]] along with [[Brickle]] in [[Mushrise Park]] and [[Broque Monsieur]] could also hear from [[Pi'illo Castle|P'illo Castle]]. They all fell asleep to the music.

Mario is forced to go into a nearby [[Dreampoint]] in order to wait the Dreambeats out. Unfortunately, the Dreambeats cause the Dream Portal to close, trapping Mario and [[Dreamy Luigi]].
