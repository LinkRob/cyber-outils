'''20-Coin Hexes''' are a type of [[Hex]] which appear in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Description==
As with any other Hex, a 20-Coin Hex can only be obtained from a [[Hex Area]] at random by whoever passes by there. A 20-Coin Hex can be placed on a [[Blue Space]] or [[Red Space]] up to five [[Space]]s from the user. The 20-Coin Hex is an equivalent of the [[10-Coin Hex]] in that it steals [[Coin]]s, twenty in the 20-Coin Hex's case, from an opponent to give to whoever placed it or their team. If the user or a teammate lands on a 20-Coin Hex, they instead receives five Coins. Like any other Hex, after the 20-Coin Hex is activated, the Space that it was on returns to normal.
