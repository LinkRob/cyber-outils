'''Star Fox''' is a microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', serving as the boss microgame of [[9-Volt]]'s level, The Multiplayer Test. Hence its name, it involves the [[w:c:starfox:Star Fox SNES|first game]] of the ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox (series)|Star Fox]]'' franchise.

== Gameplay ==
[[Fox McCloud|Fox]] and his friends have to make their way through a level of Star Fox. After they get through the game, they encounter a massive version of the Robotic Operating Buddy, which wields a gun similar to a NES Zapper. Fox has to destroy the blocks that surround the monstrous Robotic Operating Buddy. The first level has few enemies with only one block having to be destroyed, the second has more enemies with two blocks, and the final level has many more enemies and obstacles with all four blocks being present. If Fox gets shot down, the player loses the microgame.

== Trivia ==
*The flashing blocks reference the game ''Stack Up'' that Robotic Operating Buddies are used for.
