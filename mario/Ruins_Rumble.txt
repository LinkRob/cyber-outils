'''Ruins Rumble''' is a 1-vs-Rivals mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Opening==
An overhead shot of the entire field is shown before the in-game camera adjusts its focus to more closely show a frontal view of the characters.

==Gameplay==
The object for the solo character is to eliminate the other characters using their vehicle, including any [[Dry Bones]] in the path. If there are three rivals, more Dry Bones are placed along the path. Meanwhile, the rivals have to try and avoid the solo character until the 30-second [[time limit]] is up. The solo characters moves more quickly than their rivals, meaning the others have to often turn between paths as they avoid the solo character. If a rival touches the solo player's vehicle, they are eliminated, upon which they are knocked off the field and onto the side area that borders the area where gameplay takes place. If the solo character hits every rival and Dry Bones within 30 seconds, they are ranked the winner. If at least one rival or even at least one Dry Bones is not eliminated by the time the thirty seconds are up, the rivals win.
