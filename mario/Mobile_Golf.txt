'''''Mobile Golf''''' is a spinoff installment of the ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' series. It was released for the [[Game Boy Color]] on May 11, 2001, exclusively in Japan. The game is compatible with the [[Mobile GB Adapter]]. The game is mainly centered around the characters from the [[Game Boy Color]] game ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]''.
