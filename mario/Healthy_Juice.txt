'''Healthy Juice''' is an item made by [[Tayce T.]], a chef in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. She creates this item by combining a [[Special Shake]] with either a [[Stinky Herb]], [[Dried Shroom]], or [[Strange Leaf]].

==Effects==
This delicious and nutritious drink restores 40 [[Flower Point|FP]] making it a very effective healing item in terms of FP. When Tayce T. finishes cooking this, she will say that this one of her specialties. This item can be sold for 25 [[coin]]s at most retailers across the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. Since this item requires two ingredients, [[Mario]] must have also acquired the [[Cookbook]] from [[Gourmet Guy]] in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]].
