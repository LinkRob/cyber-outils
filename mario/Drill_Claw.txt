'''Drill Claw''' is a weapon item used exclusively by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. There is only one Drill Claw, and it is found in a [[treasure chest]] at the end of a battle course in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]]. Bowser can equip the Drill Claw to increase his [[Attack]] power by 40.
