'''Lightning Shark''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] weapon that is designed after a hand cannon with average power and range. [[Mario]] can use the Lightning Shark. The weapon appears in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:MRKB Lightning Shark screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Lightning Shark in-game.]]
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Lightning Shark resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack with a medium range. The Lightning Shark deals around 25 to 35 damage.
