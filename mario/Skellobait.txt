}}A '''Skellobait''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Skellobaits are commonly seen in [[The Overthere]] during [[Bonechill]]s invasion. Skellobaits are dropped by [[Skellobomber]]s and walk around on the ground. When Skellobait spots one of the heroes, it will breathe ice on them causing [[Frozen|Freeze]] if it connects.
