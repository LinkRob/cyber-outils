'''Red Blocks''' are a type of [[block]] which appear in a few {{series|Super Mario}} games. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
At first, Red Blocks start out as red [[Dotted-Line Block]]s, but once [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] hit a [[! Switch]], they solidify into Red Blocks. In ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Run]]'', this effect is only temporary, but in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', Red Blocks are instead activated from giant ! Switches on the overworld.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
Red Blocks only appear in [[World 3-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3-4]] and [[World 3-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3-5]]. As such, they are instead activated and deactivated by giant ! Switches on the [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3]] map. Activating the Red Blocks are required to reach a certain [[Goal Pole]] in each level: the main Goal Pole in World 3-4 and the [[Red Flagpole]] in World 3-5.

===''Super Mario Run''===
Red Blocks most prominently appear in [[Red Block Run]], and they can also be found in [[Switch Ghost House]].
