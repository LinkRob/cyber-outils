'''Fret''' is a young man who wanders around [[Flipside]]. He is a minor character in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Fret is a young man. He is a teal [[Flip-Flopian]].

=== Personality traits ===
Fret is laid-back. He likes to write down the things he wants to do before his game ends but never took action.

==History==
Fret lives in [[Flipside]]. He plays minigames and held high scores. He played as many games he can before the world ends. He is grateful after the heroes save the worlds.
