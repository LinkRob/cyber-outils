'''Sleepy Time''' is a special move used by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Toadstool learns this move at level 11 and requires 4 FP to use.

==Usage==
With Sleepy Time, Toadstool is able to put an enemy or enemies to sleep by summoning a bunch of sheep onto the field. In order to use Sleepy Time, the player must rotate the control pad to make the technique more effective against multiple enemies.

==Trivia==
*The [[Sleepy Sheep]] from the ''[[Paper Mario series]]'' may have been based on Sleepy Time.
