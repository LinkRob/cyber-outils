The '''Coconut Bomb''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Zess T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
This recipe deals seven [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage to the enemy. When the Coconut Bomb is mixed with an [[Egg Bomb]], a [[Zess Dynamite]] is created.
