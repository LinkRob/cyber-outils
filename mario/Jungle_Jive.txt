'''Jungle Jive''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The quest takes place at the [[Dance Stage]], where it begins after the player's character accepts [[Hulu]]'s offer to dance. Hulu asks the player's character to follow her lead as they dance, which involves her shouting a button that the player must quickly input: {{button|gba|a}} for dancing left, {{button|gba|b}} for dancing right, and {{button|gba|pad}} for jumping and spinning into the air. If the right commands are input successfully, Hulu congratulates the player's character at their dancing ability and rewards them with the [[Mini Bowling]] [[Gaddget]], ending the quest. However, if the player misses too many of Hulu's commands, she applauds them for their effort but not their skill, and she encourages them to restart the quest.
