'''Ring My Bell''' is one of the [[microgame]]s in [[Jimmy T]]'s [[Sports]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
A small, white, stick figure-like character is jumping up and down on a trampoline. The player must press {{button|gba|A}} when the trampoline is fully depressed to make the character spring up and hit the bell.

Levels 1, 2, and 3 involve the man having to bounce up 30, 45, and 60 feet respectively.
