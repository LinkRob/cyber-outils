'''Sub-Goomba Xs''' are the stronger variants of [[Sub-Goomba]]s fought in the [[Battle Ring (Mario & Luigi: Dream Team)|Battle Ring]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They are fought in the Normal Class 6 battle alongside the [[Elite Trio X]], and like them, have no change in appearance from who they are based on, but have increased stats.
