'''Bridges''' are a type of platform in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
A Bridge is attached from one side of a wall or platform to typically another one to allow for characters to safely move across the gap between. In the ''Super Mario'' series, Bridges are often used like a regular [[Semisolid Platform]], and while several are attached to a wall, there are several that are only built partway across, leaving a gap. Certain [[level]]s such as [[Butter Bridge 2]] of ''Super Mario World'' feature Bridge segments in midair that [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can jump from.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario World]]''===
Bridges prominently appear in [[Butter Bridge 1]], [[Butter Bridge 2]], and [[Cheese Bridge Area]]. These Bridges are segmented and made out of logs.
