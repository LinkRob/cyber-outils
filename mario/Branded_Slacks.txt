The '''Branded Slacks''' are a [[clothing]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''==
The Branded Slacks can be bought from the [[Shroom Shop]] in [[Peach's Castle]] for 350 [[coin]]s. They can only be worn by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], not their baby counterparts [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]]. Wearing the Branded Slacks increases its user's [[Defense|DEF]] by 35.
