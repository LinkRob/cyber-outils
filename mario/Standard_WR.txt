The '''Standard WR''' is the standard kart for [[Wario]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. Wario also has the [[Brute]] and the [[Dragonfly]] as his karts. This kart is [[Speed|fast]], [[Weight|Heavy]], and has medium [[Drifting|Drift]]. On the other hand, it's [[Acceleration]] and [[Handling]] is poor.

This kart is purple in color. The [[Standard Kart L]] replaces this kart in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' and is yellow instead of purple.
