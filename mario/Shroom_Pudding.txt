'''Shroom Pudding''' is a recipe made by [[Dyllis]] in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Effects==

It is made by mixing a [[Big Egg]] with a [[Shroom Shake]]. Called a "shroomtastic pudding", it restores 25 HP and cures poison.
