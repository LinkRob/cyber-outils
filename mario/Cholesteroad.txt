The '''Cholesteroad''' is a series of events found in [[Bowser's body]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|3DS port]].

== Description ==
The Cholesteroad is a combination of 'cholesterol' and 'road'. Here, it is where the Mario Bros. can test out their [[Bros. Attack]]s, and serves as the equivalent to [[Bowser]]'s session with [[Broque Madame]], as those sessions include testing out Bowser's [[Brawl Attack]]s.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' ==
To unlock the Cholesteroad, first the Mario Bros. must defeat the [[Scutlet (boss)|Scutlet]] that was blocking Bowser's [[Flame Pipe]]. These events are spilit between two categories, '''Endless Mode''', which allows the player to test out five of the Bros. Attacks against and endless supply of [[Cholesteron]]s, and '''Juuuuump Mode''', in which the Bros. will practice with the [[Jump Helmet]] move and measure how far they can maintain their distance.

Like with Broque Madame's sessions for Bowser, the Bros. are ranked on their progress and how long they can make it to the harder difficulties, as well as rewards depending on how many points they can stack up.
