'''Gooeys''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

== Description ==
Gooeys are small, ghost-like enemies that can be found in various areas of [[Blowhole Castle]]. Gooeys often act on their own especially while invisible. To defeat a Gooey, [[Wario]] must first make them visible via hitting a switch as [[Cosmic Wario]] then shooting them down.
