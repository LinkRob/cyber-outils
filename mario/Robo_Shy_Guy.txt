|gender= N/A}}'''Robo Shy Guys''' are [[Shy Guy]]s that appear in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "Robo Koopa." [[Toad (character)|Toad]] and [[Princess Peach]] have been kidnapped by Robo Koopa. The [[Mario]] Bros. go through the sewer system to try to save Toad and Peach. They are greeted by Robo Shy Guys who try to stop them. They are quite easily defeated. After that, they never appear again.{{stub}}
