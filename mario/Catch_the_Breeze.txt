'''Catch the Breeze''' is the fifth level of [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', a [[Purple Yoshi]] takes control in this level. This level takes place in the grassy fields of [[Yoshi's Island]] with a nice view of the sea and introduces [[Stork Stop]]s and the ability to switch between babies, the level also introduces [[Baby Peach]] and her ability to use her pink umbrella to fly with the wind. After the completion of this level, [[Stork Stop]]s will appear in previous levels allowing you to obtain collectables you weren't able to collect before.

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the right and be sure to enter the first [[Pipe]] there (spawning [[Shy Guy]]s), this pipe will lead you to a secret area with a [[Smiley Flower]], grab the Flower and exit the area to return to the main level, next jump in between the blue "cheese blocks" and in the right corner, there is an invisible [[Winged Cloud]], use an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to hit it and get some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], after this, hop back up the platforms to reach the [[Stork Stop]], after an introduction, use the [[Stork Stop]] to call the [[Stork]] over and hop up to the [[Stork]] to switch to [[Baby Peach]], [[Baby Peach]]'s abilities allow you to fly across the skies with the wind (but her [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]-tosses aren't as strong and they instantly break upon impact (without ricocheting)), use her abilities to glide with the wind collecting the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]s on your way up. 

Upon reaching the top of the wall, in the next area, tag the [[Middle Ring]] and use the next wind gust to reach the higher ledges to grab some [[Coin]]s, [[Red Coin]]s, and a [[Smiley Flower]], drop back down, avoid the [[Shy Guy]]s, and use the wind again to reach a higher ledge, collect the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]s up here, a [[Smiley Flower]] is also seen to the left, grab that as well, and then advance to the right and defeat the [[Piranha Plant]] in the area, hit the idle [[Winged Cloud]] to get some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], then drop down and grab the [[Coin]]s and hit the next [[Winged Cloud]] to get some more [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]]. Beat the [[Crazee Dayzee]]s, [[Piranha Plant]]s, and [[Shy Guy]]s in your way and advance to the right and use the wind to glide upward (while avoiding the [[Glide Guy]]) and trigger the invisible [[Winged Cloud]] to appear, hit the [[Winged Cloud]] with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to get some more [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], after this, advance to the left and glide up across to the right collecting the [[Coin]]s and three [[Red Coin]]s in the direction, then drop down to the ledge into the small niche, there is an invisible [[Winged Cloud]] here, hit it with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to get a [[Smiley Flower]], next use the blowing wind to hop onto the [[Donut Lift]]s and hop across each set to reach a high alcove with two [[Coin]]s, two [[Red Coin]]s, and the [[Baby Coin]]. After collecting the goodies, hit the [[Winged Cloud]] in the area to get some more [[Coin]]s and the return back to the main path. Drop down and avoid or beat the [[Shy Guy]]s, [[Woozy Guy]]s, and [[Piranha Plant]]s and continue onward using the wind to get the aligned [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]s while reaching a ledge which contains more [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]s, drop back down to grab another row of [[Coin]]s and use the next wind gust to glide up higher to get some more [[Coin]]s and a [[Red Coin]] then reach the ledge to get some [[Coin]]s and hit a [[Winged Cloud]] for a prize, drop down to get some more [[Coin]]s and then use the large wind gust at the end to reach an alcove halfway, avoid the [[Piranha Plant]]s, [[Crazee Dayzee]]s, and [[Glide Guy]]s here and grab the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]s as well as hitting the [[Winged Cloud]] for a 1-Up. After this, glide even higher, to grab the final [[Red Coin]] and flutter to the left to grab some [[Coin]]s and a [[Smiley Flower]], after this, flutter back to the right to reach the high ledge with the [[GOAL! Ring]], jump through the ring to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
You will need [[Baby Peach]] for this [[Baby Coin]], after using the wind gust to get the three [[Red Coin]]s, use the wind to reach the [[Donut Lift]]s floating upahead, hop from each [[Donut Lift]] to reach a ledge, on this ledge is a [[Winged Cloud]] along with some [[Coin]]s, [[Red Coin]]s, and the [[Baby Coin]] at the end.
