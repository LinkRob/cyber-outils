A '''Grab Bag''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

== Description ==
When a Grab Bag is used, it takes away all of the user's current in favor of a [[Star Pipe]], a [[Block Sensor]], and a [[Triple Dice Set]].

==In ''Mario Party DS''==
Grab Bags are featured in turns right before the [[Last Five Turns Event]], only ever seen if the host of the game selects 20, 25, or 30 turns. It can be either be bought at the [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shop]] for 25 [[Coin]]s or obtained from a [[Hidden Block (Mario Party series)|Hidden Block]] at random.
