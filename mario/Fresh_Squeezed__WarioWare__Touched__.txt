'''Fresh Squeezed''' is a [[microgame]] found in the [[List of WarioWare: Touched! microgames#Cute Cuts|Cute Cuts]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''
==Gameplay==
The objective of the game is to milk the cow by pulling its udder toward the bucket to fill it up. 

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The player must milk 3 times in a bucket.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The player must milk 4 times in a thick bottle.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The player must milk 5 times in a milk bottle.
