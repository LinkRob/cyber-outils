'''Sky Survivor''' is a mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the player is up above the clouds, riding a machine that is shaped like a Koopa Shell with wings. In this game, the player must avoid the obstacles, which are spiky red balls, while flying in the sky. IF a player hits an obstacle, then the player's health will deplete. If the player depletes all of the character's HP, then the player loses. The more farther the player goes in the mini-game, the harder it gets for the player to dodge the obstacles. If both of the players manage to make it to the finish line, then the player with the most HP remaining will win. However, if both players manage to make it to the finish line with the same remaining amount of HP, then the game will result as a draw.
