'''Pair 'Em''' is a gambling [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], during the quest [[Game Mage]], [[Kamek]] has the player play Pair 'Em. During this quest in particular, the player's character begins with 30 coins, and the reward for winning is the minigame itself.

==Gameplay==
Pair 'Em involves the player having to match cards to win [[coin]]s, but they need at least one trio of each picture. ? cards occasionally appear, and the type of card that they are is not revealed until the cards are placed down. If all the cards are put down and matched, then the numbers of coins they are worth are doubled for all suits. The minigame ends when all cards have been placed or when every space has been filled. To win the minigame the player has to end with at least one coin more than what they began with.

===Values===
These are the cards and their corresponding value from matching three of them:

*[[Lakitu's Cloud]]: two coins
*[[Yoshi Egg]]: three coins
*[[Mushroom]]: four coins
*[[Fire Flower]]: five coins
*[[Star]]: ten coins
