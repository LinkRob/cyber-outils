'''Infernal Tower''' is a board in Solo Mode of ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
Infernal Tower is the [[Bowser]] board of ''Mario Party 6''. It is decorated with his personal icon and surrounded by [[lava]], much like other Bowser boards in the {{series|Mario Party}}. The player's character begins at the bottom and must end at the [[Rare Mini-Game Space]] on top of the tower.

Infernal Tower is the longest board in ''Mario Party 6'' and therefore gives the player more opportunities to collect [[minigame]]s in one playthrough than [[Thirsty Gulch]] and [[Astro Avenue]]. The [[Event Space|? Space]]s are in front of steel doors depicting a faint Bowser [[emblem]]. Landing on one causes a [[Chain Chomp]] to come out and bash the player back to start, allowing for more potential minigames to collect.

If the player's character moves beyond the Rare Mini-Game Space, they stop at a small platform. This activates a trap, causing a [[cage]] to fall on the player, who is then carried away by Bowser in his [[Koopa Clown Car]]. In the process, the player loses all of the minigames and [[coin]]s they had collected on the board.
