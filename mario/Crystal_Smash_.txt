'''Crystal Smash!''' is [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]'s [[Super Ability]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
When Daisy performs this super ability, she summons orange crystals from the ground. Whomever comes in contact with the crystals will be sent flying in the air. The opponents will be stunned for a few seconds.
