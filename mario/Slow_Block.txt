A '''Slow Block''' is an orange [[Dice Block]] that appears in ''[[Mario Party]]''. It resembles the [[! Block|yellow block]]s of ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' but with a number on each surface instead. The Slow Block is bought at the [[Mushroom Shop]] for 100 [[coin]]s. It is the opposite of the [[Speed Block]], which rolls an 8, 9, or 10, since when it appears on a player's turn, the player can roll only a 1, 2, or 3. [[Poison Mushroom]]s have a similar effect in [[Mario Party 3]]

A Slow Block has a four on the top of the die, even though it allows the player to roll only a 1, 2, or 3. This appears to be due to its artwork being a recolor of the artwork for the [[Plus Block]] and [[Minus Block]].
