'''Mount Must Dash''' is level 6 of World 3 in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. The theme in the level and music resembles the [[Super Mario Kart|''Super Mario Kart'']]'s [[Mario Circuit]] tracks.

==Features==
{{sectstub}}The level design is shaped like a mountain with colored fences akin to the fences in Mario Circuit. Throughout the level are dash panels and jump panels. The only enemies featured in the level are [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plants]] and Para-Biddybuds.

==[[Green Star]]s==
*'''Green Star 1''': When the path splits, take the lower one to get the star.
*'''Green Star 2''': After climbing the long hill after the checkpoint, go to the [[Mystery Box]] in the hole past the hill. Once there, catch the [[Rabbit]] and the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 3''': After the stamp, be in Cat form to climb up the ledge to the right of the last [[Warp Box]] to get the star.

==Stamp==
[[File:Mount_Must_Dash_Stamp.png|thumb|left|the rabbit stamp]]After the second star, jump on the next springboard to jump on the bouncy platforms reaching towards the Rabbit Stamp.
