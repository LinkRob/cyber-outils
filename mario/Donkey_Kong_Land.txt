'''''Donkey Kong Land''''' is a video game for the [[Game Boy]] released in 1995 that features [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] as the playable characters. It has ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'' as the sequels to this game and ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' as the semi-sequel.

==Story==
[[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] tell their latest adventure to [[Cranky Kong|Cranky]] about what happened. [[Cranky Kong|Cranky]] then becomes jealous of all of Donkey and Diddy's success and makes a bet with [[King K. Rool]] to once again steal the [[banana]]s from the [[Donkey Kong's Banana Hoard|Banana Hoard]] as Cranky thinks Donkey and Diddy are not able to complete the game on an [[Game Boy|8-bit handheld]].
