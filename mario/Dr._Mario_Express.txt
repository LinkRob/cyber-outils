'''''Dr. Mario Express''''' is a [[DSiWare]] title developed by [[Akira]] and published by [[Nintendo]]. It is part of the [[Dr. Mario (series)|''Dr. Mario'' series]]. It was originally released on the [[Nintendo DSi Shop]] as a game that could be downloaded for 500 DSi Points. ''Dr. Mario Express'' first released in Japan in 2008, then English-speaking countries in 2009, and lastly by Nintendo's Chinese subsidiary, [[iQue]], in China in 2010. It was [[reissue|ported]] to the [[Nintendo 3DS]]'s [[Nintendo eShop|eShop]] on its date of launch, in 2011.

''Dr. Mario Express'' is considered the handheld counterpart of the [[WiiWare]] title ''[[Dr. Mario Online Rx]]'', a game also developed by Akira shortly beforehand.
