'''Hectic Highway''' is a [[microgame]] which appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', under [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] folder. A similar microgame titled [[Outta My Way (WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase)|Outta My Way]] would go on to be released as one of [[Wario-Man]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player has to use the left and right arrows of the {{button|GBA|Pad}} to have [[Wario]] steer his vehicle around any ongoing traffic. Another microgame from ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', [[Racing 112]], features a very similar objective.

===Difficulty levels===
Each increased difficulty level has Wario ride a larger vehicle, giving him less space to avoid traffic.

*'''Level 1:''' Wario drives a small motorbike.
*'''Level 2:''' Wario drives a medium sports car. Aside from a larger size, the sports car also drives faster than the motorbike, and there is more traffic to avoid.
*'''Level 3:''' Wario drives a large monster truck, which moves at around the same speed as the sports car. The traffic also instead features larger vehicles and sometimes a dormant bulldozer.
