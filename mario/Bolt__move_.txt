{{Nihongo|'''Bolt'''|でんげき|Dengeki}} is a move used by certain enemies and bosses in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
With Bolt, the foe strikes a single member of [[Mario]]'s party for moderate damage. Bolt could be considered a lesser version of [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]].
