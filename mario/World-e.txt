'''World-e''' is a world exclusive to ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. The [[level]]s could only be accessed using [[e-Reader]] cards via the e-Reader peripheral, some exclusively released in Japan, fewer accessible in North America, and none in Europe due to the e-Reader not being released there. In the [[Wii U]] [[Virtual Console]] version, all the levels were made accessible from the start, without having to use an e-Reader.
