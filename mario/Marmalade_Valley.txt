'''Marmalade Valley''' is an orange level in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. There is one orange Color Star to be found. It is an excavation site where Toads discovered an entire fossil of a [[Draggadon]], who later recolor and revive with the orange Big Paint Star. This is also the first area where the Sunset Express train can be seen.
