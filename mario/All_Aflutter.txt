'''All Aflutter''' is one of [[Mona]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''.

==Layout==
There are four speech bubbles, each saying the word of the direction the speech bubble is pointed. There is also a circle in the center with a gap, often being flown over and obscured by a [[butterfly]].

==Gameplay==
To win the microgame, the player must tap the speech bubble that says which direction the gap in the center circle is facing. If either time runs out or an incorrect speech bubble is selected, the player loses at the microgame.
