'''Walruses''' (トドデス (Tododesu)) are real-world animals that appear as an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]]. They appear first in the second level of the seventh stage, excluding the Construction Site. They protect the key by wandering around levels. When they see [[Mario]], they charge at him, with fangs. Mario can stand on it, but unlike most enemies, they can't be lifted because they are extremely heavy.
