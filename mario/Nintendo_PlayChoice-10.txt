'''Nintendo PlayChoice-10''' is an arcade machine created by [[Nintendo]] in August 1986. It consists of ten [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games, hence the "ten" in the title. The games included were more similar to their home console versions compared to [[VS. System]] variations of the games. ''[[NES Open Tournament Golf|Mario's Open Golf]]'' is a rare example of a PlayChoice-10 variation with significant alterations.
