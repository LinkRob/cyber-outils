The '''Biddybuggy''' ('''Buggybud''' in Europe/Australia) is a kart found in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]] and [[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is designed in the likeness of a [[Para-Biddybud]].

==Stats==
{{sectstub}}

==In ''Mario Kart Tour''==
The [[kart]] can easily be unlocked. It retains its exact appearance from ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''.

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Biddybuggy is featured as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]].
