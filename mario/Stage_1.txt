|game    = ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]]''}}'''Stage 1''' is the first level in ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]]''.

==Appearance==

Stage 1 is a large stage with many climbable vines around. There are also four small trees at the bottom of the screen that acts as platforms.

==Hazards==
*'''[[Snapjaw]]s''': The only enemy in this level. Three red Snapjaws are already present on the stage but [[Mario]] can summon blue colored ones to attack Donkey Kong Jr. They can be taken down by dropping fruit on them but they should be avoided.
