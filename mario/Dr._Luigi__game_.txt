'''''Dr. Luigi''''' is a game for the [[Wii U]]. It was released as part of the [[Year of Luigi]] series.

==Release==
This game is available for download on [[Nintendo eShop]]. It was released for North America on December 31, 2013 for $14.99.

==Gameplay==
The gameplay of ''Dr. Luigi'' is the same as the classic NES game [[Dr. Mario (game)|''Dr. Mario'']] and ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]''.
