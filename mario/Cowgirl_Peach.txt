'''Cowgirl Peach''' is a power-up in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''

==Overview==
Cowgirl Peach involves [[Princess Peach]] being dressed as a cowgirl, wielding a lasso, and even riding wooden horses.

===Physical appearance===
In her Cowgirl form, Peach wears a fawn leather cowboy hat, a fringed gilet of the same material on a sky-blue short-sleeved skirt, and long blue jeans with beige flared chaps covering the legs. On her feet are brown cowboy boots with low heels of a lighter color, and on her hands are fawn wrist-length leather gloves. The hat is adorned with a burgundy stripe on the bottom, loops of rope sewn into the brim, and a pair of bull horns. She also wears a red scarf, held by her brooch on the back, a dark brown belt with a round silver buckle, and silver hoop earrings. Her hair is worn in a low, messy ponytail.
