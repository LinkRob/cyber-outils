}}A '''Rawbus''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Rawbus is a stronger version of a [[Jawbus]] that can be first found in the [[Gap of Crag]] (Chapter 5-2). The Rawbus attacks by stretching out their necks and biting making it difficult to go past them. The best way to take it out is by using [[Mario]]'s [[Flip]] to get behind it and attacking the weak point on its tail until it's defeated.
