{{Nihongo|'''Water Blast'''|すいじょうきばくはつ|Suijōki Bakuhatsu|Vapor Explosion}} is an attack used by multiple bosses in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Water Blast deals medium or high damages to all party members. When used, Water Blast causes spouts of water to appear everywhere on screen with steam rising upwards, followed by a giant explosion.
