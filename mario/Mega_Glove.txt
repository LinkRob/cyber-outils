The '''Mega Glove''' is a weapon used by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from a shop in [[Nimbus Land]] for 102 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Mega Glove increases Mario's [[Attack]] power by 60.
