'''Pinballs''' are an obstacle which first appear in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

==Description==
Pinballs are very similar to [[Big Steely|Big Steelies]] except they are featured in [[Waluigi Pinball]] and its sports stadium of the [[Waluigi Pinball (court)|same name]], where they are hazards that the characters have to avoid.
