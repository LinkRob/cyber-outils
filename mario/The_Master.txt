'''The Master''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
He is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who owns [[The Dojo]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and is the strongest fighter throughout the entire area. Although he is old in appearance, The Master is very capable in a fight. He has high health and devastating attacks. After Mario defeats The Master's students, [[Chan]] and [[Lee]], he decides to fight [[Mario]] himself. The first fight is pretty easy, since all he does is punch Mario. When he wins, Mario gets the [[Third-Degree Card]]. Mario can challenge The Master again to get the [[Fourth-Degree Card]]. This time The Master transforms into a healthy yellow Toad with in a fighter's stance. The Master's [[Heart Point|HP]] is 75, and he does a double attack combo with a damage of 11.

In the third and final fight, he is more powerful. He has a yellow aura surrounding him and has 99 HP. If Mario is able to defeat The Master, he will grant Mario the diploma.

According to a Toad near the Odd House, The Master and [[Tayce T.]] are siblings and that Tayce T. used to live with The Master at The Dojo before she moved out to focus on her cooking. [[Russ T.]] also revealed that The Master and [[Goompa]] used to train together which is hinted at the beginning of the game where Goompa teaches Mario the basic fighting commands.

==Strategy==
There is a very effective way to help defeat the Master in his third form very easily. Mario should be equipped with the [[Zap Tap]] badge. That way, whenever The Master is about to use his Combo Move, the Zap Tap badge will disrupt his attack sequence only landing one hit with it. Also, using an [[Ultra Stone|ultra-ranked]] Watt with her [[Flower Point|FP]] saving charge will help big time in the fight. Mario should keep up with the Mega Smashes until The Master falls. It is heavily recommended fighting The Master for the final time after Mario has completed Chapter 7.

==Trivia==
*His students, Chan and Lee, are quite obviously based on {{w|Jackie Chan}} and {{w|Bruce Lee}} respectively.
*The Master himself appears to be based on {{w|John Kreese}} from the ''[[wikipedia:The Karate Kid (franchise)|Karate Kid]]'' franchise as well as ''[[wikipedia:Cobra Kai|Cobra Kai]]''. Both are powerful, highly trained, Martial Arts masters who show no mercy to their opponents.
*In his final form, the Master is actually stronger than the final boss, [[Bowser]] (in his powered-up state). While both have 99 HP, the Master has more powerful attacks than Bowser. Much like [[Bonetail]] in [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|the sequel]], the Master is the strongest opponent in the game and is completely optional.
*The Master is similar in many ways to the character [[Jinx]] from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Both Jinx and the Master are expert martial artists who train disciples in schools. Additionally, [[Mario]] has to fight both Jinx and the Master three times each, with every battle getting progressively harder in each case.
*In his third form, the difficulty of beating the Master is comparable to or harder than fighting Bowser for the final time in the game, since the former has the potential to do more damage than the latter per attack, and regenerates more often.
*The Master, along with Lee and Chan, are some of the few characters that don't appear in the parade at the end. This is mainly because the encounter with The Master is optional and does not affect the main storyline.
*The Master is one (if not the only) of few Toads who is fought as a boss. The other notable example is [[Prince Mush]] from the remake of ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' who is also a seasoned warrior.
