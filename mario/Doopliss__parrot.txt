'''Doopliss' Parrot''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a parrot belonging to [[Doopliss]].

== Profile ==
Doopliss' Parrot is a green parrot that has yellow spots on his chest and wings. He also has a red beak and rosy pink cheeks.

== History ==
[[Mario]] and [[Vivian]] travelled to the [[Creepy Steeple]] where they eventually find the Parrot. However, he seems to act like a normal parrot at first until Vivian uses her [[Veil]] techique to spy on him. Doopliss' parrot beings to complain on how his master, Doopliss, locked him away for revealing his name. The parrot was shocked after Mario and Vivian come from the shadows saying he wants a cracker.
