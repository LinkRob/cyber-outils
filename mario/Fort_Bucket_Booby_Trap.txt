'''Fort Bucket Booby Trap''' is the first fort level in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' and the fourth overall level.

==Layout==
[[Yellow Yoshi]] starts in an area with an [[Egg Block]] and a [[Bucket (Yoshi's Island series)|bucket]], followed by swinging hammers and pits of [[lava]]. Enemies such as [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Lava Drop]]s are interspersed between these obstacles. A [[Whirly Gate]] will turn Yellow Yoshi into a [[Hot-Air Balloon Yoshi|Hot-Air Balloon]], after which he has to navigate a vertical tunnel filled with [[coin]]s and [[Fly Guy]]s. At the end, he is warped back to his normal form and transported into an area with a [[Middle Ring]].
Ahead are an [[Egg Plant]] and a series of bridges over lava, where [[Co-Blargg]]s lunge at Yellow Yoshi. These are followed by several [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s and, at the end, another Egg Plant and a golden bucket that drops a [[Spring Ball]] when hit with an egg. The Spring Ball helps Yellow Yoshi jump over a ledge where the boss door is located.

Past the door, the Yoshi engages in a boss fight against [[Kamek]]. In this room, there are three golden buckets aligned high above the ground which drop missiles when hit. Kamek, who stands on a platform of [[! Block]]s, attacks by shooting magic at one of the golden buckets to release a missile (or targets Yellow Yoshi directly if he stands underneath him), following with two spells that create Shy Guys before teleporting to another spot, always under a golden bucket. Yellow Yoshi must hit a bucket and release a missile onto Kamek to inflict damage on him. After the first and second hits, Kamek will fly on a broom past the buckets, causing them to drop missiles onto the floor. After enduring three hits, Kamek is defeated.

Above the blocks is a hidden Winged Cloud that, when revealed, can be hit to release a Spring Ball that bounces the Yoshi to the pipe. The Smiley Flower is then found in yet another hidden Winged Cloud on the right side of the bonus room.
