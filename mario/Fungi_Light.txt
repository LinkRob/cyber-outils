The '''Fungi Light''' is a unique object appearing in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. One is owned by [[Mario]] and can be found in [[Mario Bros.' House|his home]]. If Mario turns off the Fungi Light, he goes to sleep and recovers all of his [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]]. Coincidentally, the other Fungi Light is located at the most expensive inn in the game, the suite in [[Marrymore]]. Of course, turning off the light and taking a rest will recover all of the party's HP and FP. However, use of this lamp will cost two hundred coins and an additional one hundred coins with each subsequent night stayed in the suite.

In [[Monstro Town]], Mario has to turn off an [[Amanita|actual fungus]] before he can go to sleep.
