{{Infobox Location|image = [[File:Rump Command.png|thumb]]|caption = ''[[Mario]]'' and ''[[Luigi]]'' playing the minigame.|located = [[Bowser's Body]]|first = ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''|games = ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''}}The '''Rump Command''' is an area in [[Bowser's body]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. It becomes accessible to the player once [[Bowser]] gets crushed by [[Bowser's Castle|his own castle]]. It is where [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] go to revive Bowser in any situations where he is crushed by something heavy. In those situations, there is an [[Emoglobin]] that allows Mario to save the game, as well as two [[Evoglobin]]s. There are also two boats that Mario and Luigi board to save Bowser.



==Minigame==
In this minigame, Mario and Luigi move left and right in the boats while shooting adrenaline to fill up a meter. Mario must shoot the red orbs, and Luigi must shoot the green orbs. When the meter fills up, a special blue orb appears that shoots red and green orbs. The blue orb can be shot by both bros, but it must be shot several times to win. When it gets shot enough times, Bowser gets revived and becomes giant.
