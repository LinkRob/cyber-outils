'''Monty Mole''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' and a representative of his species of the [[Monty Mole|same name]]. He is found in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]].

Monty Mole is a gambling addict, having wasted a lot of money on its games, and asks for the player character to help get him out of his slump. Monty Mole has a habit of making dirt-related puns, especially "down-to-earth." In doing so, Monty Mole rewards the player with the minigame [[Scratch 'Em]], which he frequently loses.

Monty Mole is playable in two later home console games in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series (''[[Super Mario Party]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'').

==Trivia==

*Monty Mole is the third non-human mammal character to be playable in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series after [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]]. While he is a mole, [[Donkey Kong]] is an ape (a gorilla), and [[Diddy Kong]] is a monkey (a spider monkey).
