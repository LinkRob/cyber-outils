{{Nihongo|'''Horror Area'''|ホラーエリア<ref>{{cite web|url=https://youtu.be/yzfYbXJ0W44|title=1人用マリオパーティアドバンス字幕プレイpart1|language=Japanese|date=2017-10-04|work=YouTube|accessdate=2023-04-19}}</ref>|Horā Eria|Horror Area}} is a a haunted area located in the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It appeared in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Description ==
Horror Area is a haunted area with leafless trees and graves. On the west of Horror Area is [[Snow Area]], east of Horror Area is [[Town Area]] and south of Horror Area is [[Desert Area]].

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
Horror Area is [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s starting point in Shroom City. The following places appear in Horror Area:
* [[Boo Cemetery]]
* [[Mr. I (location)|Mr. I]]
* [[Horror Condo]]
