'''Waki-Waki''' is a one-time location which appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Too Hot to Handle]]." It is a volcanic and tropical island inhabited by a Hawaiian-inspired tribe of [[Toad (species)|Mushroom]]s. The location is ruled over by [[Scooter]] and the [[Fire God]].
