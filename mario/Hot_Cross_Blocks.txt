'''Hot Cross Blocks''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] found in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.<ref>Mario Party Legacy (September 1, 2024). [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XmFC5mnXSLk&t=352s PAX West 2024 Demo - Super Mario Party Jamboree (Improved Audio)]. ''YouTube''. Retrieved September 6, 2024.</ref>

==Opening==
The camera pans from the exit door to the characters, each performing an animation to show their readiness, before the camera fixes to a bird's-eye view.

==Gameplay==
Each character has to select one of the four block patterns on the right to walk on. If two or more characters choose the same block pattern, none of the characters receive it. Similarly, in cases where a character selects a block pattern which is not connected to their block path, the block pattern sinks immediately after being applied. Whoever reaches the other side first is the winner.

==Ending==
The characters stand at the exit door, performing their winning or losing animation, depending on their performance in the minigame.

==In-game text==
*'''Description''' &ndash; "''Choose block patterns to build a path across the lava. If you choose the same block pattern as someone else, no one gets it! Blocks that don't connect will sink.''"
