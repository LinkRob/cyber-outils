'''[[Princess Daisy]]''' is an sassy and boisterous tomboy with a feisty attitude.

== Family ==
=== Father ===
Daisy has a father who may be the king and also rule [[Sarasaland]]. However, she mentioned him in ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' running off. Notably, Daisy mentions ''"Augh! I don't believe this! I've never lost - not even to my dad!"  ''while losing in a minigame.

== Allies ==
=== Luigi ===
Daisy and Luigi have been a couple since NES Open Tournament Golf where Daisy takes the role of being Luigi's caddie.
One of the most notable hints concerning Luigi and Daisy being a couple is the giant statue of Luigi and Daisy in Daisy Circuit in Mario Kart Wii. There is also a statue of '''[[Baby Luigi]]''' and '''[[Baby Daisy]]''' behind the statue of adult Luigi and Daisy.

=== Mario ===
{{sectstub}}

=== Princess Peach ===
Princess Daisy and '''[[Princess Peach]]''' are close friends. Daisy moved into [[Mushroom Kingdom]] while Peach had missed female companionship.

=== Toad ===
[[Toad (character)|Toad]] has interacted with Daisy multiple times, clearly establishing their friendship. In ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', Daisy happily talks with Toad and Birdo. They fly together in ''Mario Party: Island Tour''. In ''Super Mario Sluggers'', Toad sits next to Daisy in anticipation after Luigi gets knocked down. In the festival-ending cutscenes, they are seen sitting next to each other again, watching fireworks with other friends. ''Mario Party 8'' refers to them as Bloomy Shroomy. In the ''Mario Kart Tour'' Bangkok Tour artwork, they hang out together on a boat loaded with fruits. In ''Mario Kart World'', they chat with Toadette in the post-credit cutscene before smiling for a group photo.

===Toadette===
Similair with [[Toad (character)|Toad]], Toadette also has interacted with Daisy a lot of the times, she has been described to be avid friends with Daisy. The two would garner different titles for being a tag team duo in the ''Mario Party'' games. In ''Mario Party 6'', Daisy and Toadette were known as the Shopping Buddies, while in ''Mario Party 8'', they were known as the Perfumed Shroomy. The two were also on the Peach Monarchs baseball team in ''Mario Superstar Baseball'' and ''Mario Super Sluggers'', having good chemistry in both titles respectively.

During the [[Ocean Tour]] event in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', both Daisy and Toadette were entrusted Sailor outfits.

Daisy has been described as very loyal and caring towards Toadette, while Toadette expresses just about the same sympathy towards Daisy, which makes their friendship constantly stable.
