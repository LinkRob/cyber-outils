The '''Sweet Sweet Galaxy''' is a galaxy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It appears after the [[Hungry Luma]] next to the [[Terrace]] has been fed 400 [[Star Bit]]s. It features moving platforms with cookie cutter-shaped holes in them and appears to be made up primarily of titanic sweets and desserts. If [[Mario]] falls through a hole he will fall into a pit and die. The stage also has [[Electric rail|electric rails]] which have to be jumped over; if Mario touches one, he will get stunned temporarily and lose one point of health. Not only that, but there's a good chance he will fall through a hole after touching an electric barrier as it is on a moving platform.

The music of the Sweet Sweet Galaxy is a remix of the second overworld theme from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

== Missions ==
=== Rocky Road ===
Mario has to run and jump forward while avoiding holes and electric barriers until he gets to the cake with the [[Power Star]] on top of it. The first part has 2 electric barriers with the road moving against you.  The second part has the road moving to the side with the electricity on the side. After this, there is a checkpoint and a brown section that moves all around. Finally, the road has parts where the floor is gone and the holes are the floor!
