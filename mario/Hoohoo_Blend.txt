The '''Hoohoo Blend''' is a type of blend in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Usage==
The Hoohoo Blend can be obtained in the [[Starbeans Cafe]], where making it requires giving 25 [[Hoo Bean]]s to the [[Starbeans Employee]]. When either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] drink a Hoohoo Blend, his maximum [[Bros. Point|BP]] is increased by four, or two in the remake.
