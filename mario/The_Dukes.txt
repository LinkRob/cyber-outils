'''The Dukes''' is [[Rabbid Mario]]'s default weapon in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
The Dukes appears in ''Sparks of Hope'' as Rabbid Mario's weapon. The player can change the skins of The Dukes. Rabbid Mario will be unlocked once Rabbid Peach is saved from the Darkmess Manta, in order to unlock The Dukes since Rabbid Mario owns this weapon.

== Statistics ==
The Dukes can repeatedly attack enemies in close range. Even if they are behind full cover, Rabbid Mario can still damage them if he is close enough.
