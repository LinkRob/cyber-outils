The '''Fortune Teller''' is a minor character from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']] who can be encountered in the side quest Feelings of Dread.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Fortune Teller appears as a [[Concordian]] woman with an orb on her head. She has an alternate form that resembles a [[Boo]].

== History ==
The Fortune Teller appears in the side quest Feelings of Dread, where she appears on the southeastern part of [[Shipshape Island]], which can be accessed from the lower decks via the [[Bros. Ball]] move. En route to the fortune teller, [[Luigi]] jumps in fright at seemingly nothing, while [[Mario (character)|Mario]] trips. Eventually, they reach the fortune teller and hear from her that they must be cursed. The fortune teller explains that the curse stems from [[Gulchrock Sea#Islets|Nanoh Islet]], after which the Bros. are sent on their way.

Mario and Luigi use [[Shipshape Island]]'s cannon to get to Nanoh Islet, where a [[Boo]] blocks their path. The Boo, who acts as a minor, optional "sub-boss," splits into six and attacks in that form, only to be defeated.  Despite his defeat, the Boo enjoyed the battle and tells them to pass his regards to his contact.

Mario and Luigi return to the fortune teller, getting their reward: the Bros. Hammer Deluxe. The fortune teller then turns into a Boo, claiming that the Boo on Nanoh Islet likes fighting before she fights away, expressing her hopes for more persons to take the bait.
