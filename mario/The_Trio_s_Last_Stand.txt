'''The Trio's Last Stand''' is the eighth level of [[Joke's End]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 28.

==Layout==
The 1st battle contains 5 [[Koopa Troopa]]s, 2 [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s (Red), and [[Corporal Paraplonk]]. The enemy captain is Corporal Paraplonk, and he knows [[Shell Shock]] and [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 1 [[Fly Guy]], 2 [[Shy Guy]]s, 4 [[Bandit]]s, and [[Sergeant Guy]]. The enemy captain is Sergeant Guy, and he knows [[Call for Backup]] and Deny. Call for Backup calls a Bandit or Shy Guy.

The final battle contains 2 [[Paragoomba]]s, 3 [[Goomba Tower]]s, 2 [[Big Tail Goomba]]s, and [[Private Goomp]]. The enemy captain is Private Goomp, and he knows [[Clash of Captains]] and Deny.

After the final battle, Corporal Paraplonk, Sergeant Guy, and Private Goomp rejoin the squad.

==Trivia==

*Corporal Paraplonk, Private Goomp, and Sergeant Guy, can not be used in any battles, between the time this level is unlocked, and the first time it is cleared.
*While Ice Bros and Goombas do not appear in the level, they can still be recruited.
