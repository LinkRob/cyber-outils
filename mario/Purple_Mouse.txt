'''Purple Mice''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Purple Mice are a type of [[Mouse (ghost)|Mouse]] [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] with a purple body.

===Behavior===
Like with [[Blue Mouse|Blue Mice]], Purple Mice travel along the ground, although Purple Mice have the added ability to travel up walls and ceilings.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Purple Mice are most often encountered in hallways, like with Blue Mice. If [[Luigi]] touches a Purple Mouse, he takes five [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage, also just like with Blue Mice.

==Flavor text==
*[[Gallery (Luigi's Mansion)|Gallery]] (Nintendo 3DS version): ''A purple ghost mouse that can scurry through walls.''
