'''Pipsy the Mouse''' is a playable character in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]'' who helps [[Diddy Kong]] and friends kick [[Wizpig]] out of [[Timber Island]]. Pipsy has as much acceleration and handling as [[Tiptup the Turtle|Tiptup]] and [[Drumstick the Rooster|Drumstick]]'s karts have. In the intro screen of ''Diddy Kong Racing'', Pipsy is seen playing a steel drum.
