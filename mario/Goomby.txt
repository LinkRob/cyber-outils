'''Goomby''' is a character who appears in the ''[[Nintendo Adventure Books]]'' story ''[[Monster Mix-Up]]''. He and his sister, [[Foomby]], lead the [[Amalgamates|Goomba Troopa]] pack.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Like the the Goomba Troopa, Goomby resembles a normal [[Goomba]], but his head resembles a [[Koopa Troopa]]'s. The Goombombs resemble him and Foomby, but their pose is different from theirs. His pose while he and Foomby are being captured is looking back with both feet out.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Goomby has the ability to summon [[Goombomb]]s which, he and his sister used to their advantage to stop [[Mario]] from juggling them.

== Biography ==
Goomby and his gang of [[Amalgamates|Goomba Troopae]] try to kidnap [[King Toadstool]]. Mario suggests that Goomby and Foomby's troops will fall if their leaders are captures, but is proven wrong when his victims's lackeys kidnap the Mushroom King anyway. There is a puzzle regarding this. Goomby and Foomby and their [[Goombomb]]s shuffle about. Mario must find their identities. If Mario finds the twins, he knocks them out and tosses the Goombombs away. The Goombombs will explode if Mario mistakens THEIR identities for the Goombada siblings.
