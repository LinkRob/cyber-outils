'''Shipshape Island's Workshop''' is a location from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']], stationed on [[Shipshape Island]]. It can be found near the back middle of the vessel and is run by [[Maykit and Billdit|Maykit]].

== Description ==
The workshop is held by white posts, and its roof is green, lime green, and white. A stone table appears at the front that Maykit uses to craft items from the resources that she is given. Just outside of it is a sign with a blue hammer.

== History ==
The workshop is opened when [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Snoutlet]] reconnect [[Twistee Island]], which was severed from the rest of [[Concordia]] after [[Zokket]] sundered the [[Uni-Tree]] for his god, [[Reclusa]]. From there, Maykit manages it and can craft items from the various materials that she is given.

After the game ends, both of [[Chief Willma|Willma]]'s children are seen manning the shop (implying Maykit started a partnership with her brother), and are doing business with a pink-skinned [[Concordian]] girl and lavender-skinned Concordian girl, the latter presumably being the entemophobic merchant Spiralia.
