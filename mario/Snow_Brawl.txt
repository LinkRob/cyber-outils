'''Snow Brawl''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. It later returns in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Opening==
The three teammates try to throw [[snowball]]s at the solo character, but they miss. The solo character runs into the back corner of their side, and four [[Ukiki]]s jump into the arena. During the nighttime in ''Mario Party 6'' only, [[Shy Guy]]s appear instead.

==Gameplay==
The object of the minigame is to hit all playable characters on the other side of the field with a snowball. The solo character is assisted by four computer-controlled Ukikis. In ''Mario Party 6'' only, during the nighttime, the solo character is assisted by Shy Guys instead. Each player must first create a snowball before they can throw it. Any players hit by a snowball become encased in solid ice, eliminating them from the minigame.

In order to win, the solo character must hit all team players, and the three opposing players must hit the lone player (the four Ukikis or Shy Guys do not need to be hit to win). The first side to fulfill their objective wins. However, if either [[Time limit|time]] runs out or if both the solo character and the last remaining teammate freeze are each hit by a snowball simultaneously, it is a [[draw]].

==Ending==
If the solo character wins, they and any Ukikis or Shy Guys who are not frozen each perform a victory animation. If the team of three wins, any teammate not frozen performs their victory animation, while any Ukikis or Shy Guys who are not frozen perform their sad poses. If time runs out, the remaining characters each perform their losing animation.

==In ''Mario Party 6''==
The [[Miracle Book]] page "Yoshi's Revenge" depicts a scenario from the mini-game where [[Mario]], [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], and [[Toadette]] have frozen all of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Ukiki]]s, but activating the page provides Yoshi with [[Shy Guy]]s to counter the team of three.

===Etymology===
The title of the minigame is a portmanteau of "[[snowball]]" and "brawl."
