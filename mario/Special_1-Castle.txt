'''Special 1-Castle''' is the fifty-first level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. Upon beating the level, [[Luigi]] becomes a playable character, and can be used to play in any level of the game.

== Layout ==
In design and layout, the level is based on [[World 1-Castle (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 1-Castle]], however, [[Mario]] has only 30 seconds on his clock to beat the level. To compensate this, the level is riddled with [[+ Clock]]s to raise Mario's timer. After passing through the first half of the level, Mario is confronted by [[Dry Bowser]], and a [[#Boss Battle|battle]] similar to those against [[Bowser]] throughout the game ensues.

== Enemies ==
Enemies that appeared in Special 1-Castle:
* [[Prongo]]
* [[Podoboo]]
* [[Firebar]]
* [[Hammer Bro.]]
* [[Thwomp]]

== Boss Battle ==
Dry Bowser's attack patterns are virtually the same of the Fake Bowser fights from the game. Cosmetically, Dry Bowser's breath attacks are a blue flame whereas the original Bowser attacks are a fiery orange. Dry Bowser is defeated in the same way Fake Bowser is &ndash; by using the switch at the end of the bridge to his castle. By the switch, Mario can also obtain a 100-second + Clock.

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] can be obtained by jumping on top of the long [[Question Block]] past the large gears early in the level.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is found above a + Clock after a confrontation with two Hammer Bros.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The final Star Medal is above the second Thwomp of the level.
