The '''Pipe Room''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Pipe Room contains a crate, a bucket, a river of water, and a frozen barrel. Aside from this, the Pipe Room is mostly empty, and its only [[treasure]] is a single [[Gold Bar]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Pipe Room is accessed in [[Area Four]]. The [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] here include green [[Flying Fish]], [[Grabbing Ghost]]s, and [[Red Grabbing Ghost]]s. However, defeating them is optional, because [[Luigi]] just has to use an [[Ice Elemental Ghost]]'s effects to freeze the river of water so that he can turn off the valve on the upper left side. By doing so, the flow of water to the right is deactivated, revealing a [[treasure chest]] with the [[Key]] to the [[Cold Storage]].

In PAL releases' and the Nintendo 3DS version's [[Hidden Mansion]], the ghosts encountered here include three [[Purple Puncher]]s, two [[Purple Bomber]]s, two Red Grabbing Ghosts, and three [[Blue Twirler]]s.
