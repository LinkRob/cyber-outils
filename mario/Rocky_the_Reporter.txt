'''Rocky the Reporter''' is a one-time character who appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. He is a dalmatian news reporter who fills in for [[Ken the Reporter]], whom he is based on, because Ken instead serves as the host for [[Ear Candy]].

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
Rocky appears at the end of [[Wario-Man]]'s stage, in front of a crowd of people outside [[Toy Express]], where he reports on the success of the new hit game ''WarioWare: Touched!''.
