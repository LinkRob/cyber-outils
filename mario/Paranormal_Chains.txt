== Description ==
The paranormal chains appear in mission E-3 "Across the Chasm." There are two different uses of the paranormal chains in the mission, one for the strong ghosts and one for the Gondola entrance. For the ghosts, they wrap the chains around them, and it acts as a power boost, giving a [[Strong Greenie]], [[Strong Slammer]], and [[Strong Gobber]] much more HP than they would have without it. For the Gondola entrance, it acts as a blockade created by the chained strong ghosts to prevent [[Luigi]] from entering the Gondola. There's a green one that's created by the Strong Greenie, a red one that's created by the Strong Slammer, and a yellow one that's created by the Strong Gobber.



== The Chained Ghosts ==
The chained ghosts is a trio of ghosts that consists of a [[Strong Greenie]], [[Strong Slammer]], and [[Strong Gobber]], all wrapped in chains. These ghosts appear right before Luigi is about to enter the Gondola, creating paranormal chains to block Luigi from the entrance. After doing this, the ghosts will separate and flee into three separate rooms. Luigi must find and capture each one in order to regain access to the Gondola.

=== Strong Greenie ===
[[File:Chained Greenie.png|thumb|391x391px|The chained Strong Greenie staring at Luigi after opening the window|left]]
The [[Strong Greenie]] is found in the Smuggler's Hideout hiding in a suit of armor. In order to get him out, Luigi must use the Dark-Light Device to reveal another suit of armor wielding a spear. Interacting with that armor will cause it to lunge its spear into the armor where the ghost is hiding, removing the helmet from it. After that, the Strong Greenie will scare Luigi before opening a nearby window, allowing three [[Sneaker|Sneakers]] in. Luigi must capture all three Sneakers first, then the Strong Greenie will appear. Once he is captured, the green paranormal chain will disappear.

=== Strong Slammer ===
[[File:Strong Slammer.png|thumb|391x391px|The chained Strong Slammer bursting from the fireplace]]
The [[Strong Slammer]] is found in the Chalet, hiding in the cabinet on the far-right side of the room. Once revealed, he will flee into the fireplace and three Greenies will appear to fight Luigi. Once they are captured, the Strong Slammer will burst from the fireplace to face Luigi himself. Once captured, the red paranormal chain will disappear.



=== Strong Gobber ===
[[File:Screenshot 2026-02-10 095947.png|thumb|391x391px|Luigi capturing the chained Strong Gobber|left]]
The [[Strong Gobber]] is found hiding behind some planks in the Basin, preventing Luigi from stunning him with the [[Strobulb]]. He's invisible, but his reflection can be seen in the ice on the ground along with an invisible [[robomb]]. Luigi must use the [[Dark-Light Device]] to reveal the robomb, before flashing it with the Strobulb to activate it, causing it to explode and stun the Strong Gobber. This will cause the Strong Gobber to flee, and three [[Hider|Hiders]] will appear that need to be captured. Once they are, the Strong Gobber will appear in the middle of the room to fight Luigi. Once defeated, the yellow paranormal chain will disappear.

Once all three paranormal chains are gone, the Gondola will be accessible to Luigi once again.

== Trivia ==

* Despite the unique appearances and HP differences, the three chained ghosts do not have a unique name or spot in the vault.
* These ghosts act as sub bosses in the game, but they aren't the only strong ghosts that act as sub bosses. A [[Strong Sneaker]] in the mission C-5 "Piece at Last" also acts as a sub boss.
* This is the first appearance of the [[Strong Gobber]] in the Dark Moon Quest.
* The paranormal chains have no explanation on how they exist or why only these three ghosts can wear them.
* The chained Strong Gobber has the most health out of any other ghosts in the Dark Moon Quest, excluding [[Creeper|Creepers]] and [[Possessor|Possessors]], whom have infinite HP
