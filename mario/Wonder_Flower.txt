The '''Wonder Flower''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It is one of the central mechanics of the game. 

== Description ==
Wonder Flowers have a light green stem and leaves, and their petals are blue with pulsating petals, with a white centre and black eyes. Collecting one triggers [[Wonder Effect]]s, capable of altering level mechanics or transforming the player's character.

== In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' ==
One Wonder Flower acts as the game's catalyst. [[Bowser]] gets his hands on this Wonder Flower, merging himself and his [[Koopa Clown Car]] with [[Prince Florian]]'s castle. He becomes a [[Bowser Castle (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|flying fortress]] to conquer the Flower Kingdom. their are multiple colors

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
Blue Wonder Flowers appear during the second promotional ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' event from December 20, 2023 to March 15, 2024 in the Toad Rally mode. They replace and behave identically to Coin Rush Stars, activating Coin Rush when the meter is filled up while also changing all coins into Gold Goombas. A Wonder Flower also appears in the icon for version 3.1.0 of the game and the event returned from March 5th, 2026 to April 3rd, 2026 celebrate the upcoming [[Nintendo Switch 2]] [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park|Edition]].
