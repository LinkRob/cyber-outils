'''The Dump''' is a soccer stadium in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
The Dump is a stadium covered in mud and [[Warp Pipe|Pipes]]. Despite the presence of mud, the stage is not slippery and does not slow down the players. A big ditch is located in the center, where the ball slows and stops, making it very hard to pass the ball when in the center. Like most other stadiums, The Dump has its own [[electric rail]].
