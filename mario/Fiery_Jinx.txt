'''Fiery Jinx''' (まほうのほのお, Magic Flame) is an attack used by [[Vivian]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
This attack is only accessible by leveling Vivian up to the [[Super Rank]]. Fiery Jinx costs six [[Flower Point|FP]] to use. This attack is executed by pressing either the {{System Buttons|gcn|A}}, {{System Buttons|gcn|B}}, {{System Buttons|gcn|X}}, or {{System Buttons|gcn|Y}}; depending upon how many buttons are hit correctly, this attack does greater or less damage. This attack will also set all enemies on fire. Vivian sometimes uses this move against [[Mario]] and his party while fighting alongside her sisters. If Fiery Jinx is used against fire-based enemies such as [[Ember]]s or [[Lava Bubble]]s, their [[Heart Point|HP]] is instead restored, due to their invulnerability to fire.
