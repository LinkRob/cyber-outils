'''Bonfire Blocks''' are a type of [[block]] exclusively found in the [[Wario Land (series)|''Wario Land'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]'', and have gone on to appear in every subsequent installment within the ''Wario Land'' series.

==Description==
Bonfire Blocks are a red block depicting a flame on them. They can only be destroyed from using fire, specifically either [[King Dragon Wario]] or [[Sea Dragon Wario]] in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]'' or, as of ''[[Wario Land II]]'', [[Flaming Wario]].
