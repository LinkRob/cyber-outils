'''Ghost Guess''' is a 1-Player [[minigame]] that is found in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Opening==
The minigame takes place in a cave that contains a circle of medium-sized rocks around a slightly illuminated center, where the player hears a series of ghost-like screeches. The [[Boo]]s then pop up as a circle, surrounding the player. The minigame then starts.

==Gameplay==
The player's objective is to find the leader in the Boo circle within an undefined limited amount of time. The player needs to carefully find the lead Boo by looking at the shadows, figuring out which Boo is moving first. As time passes, the group of surrounding Boos slowly closes in to capture the player. If the player picks the correct Boo, all the Boos disappear and the player wins the minigame. However, if the player guesses incorrectly or does not choose in time, all the Boos enclose them, making them disappear with the Boos and lose the minigame.

On [[Mini-Game Island]], this minigame is featured as World 4-4.

The minigame can be purchased from the [[Mini-Game House]] for 100 [[Coin]]s when it is available for purchase.
