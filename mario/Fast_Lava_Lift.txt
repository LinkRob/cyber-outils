A '''Fast Lava Lift''' is a variation of the [[Lava Lift]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', the latter of which gave it a distinct name.

==Description==
A Fast Lava Lift has the appearance of a regular Lava Lift, except it is light-blue. Fast Lava Lifts act similar except they move at a fast speed.
