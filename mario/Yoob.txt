'''Yoob''' is a giant [[Yoshi]]-like creature created by the [[Shroob]]s that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. He starts out as a large magenta and green [[Yoob Egg|egg]], where it becomes an attraction that tourists can see for five [[coin]]s. When Yoob hatches from the egg, he begins eating the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s, as well as other creatures (off-screen), as evidenced by the bones and [[Koopa Troopa]]-like [[Dry Bones|skeletons]] found in his [[Yoob's Belly|belly]] when the brothers explore it.

==Appearance==
Yoob resembles a magenta Yoshi, except he is much larger and fatter (and gets even bigger when the Shroobs zap it), has black spines, a black saddle, blue shoes, a green underbelly, black irises, red pupils, and pincers for hands. Yoob bears a loose resemblance to {{fandom|barney|Barney|Barney the Dinosaur}}, the host of the educational children's programs ''{{fandom|barney|Barney & The Backyard Gang}}'' and ''{{fandom|barney|Barney & Friends}}''. As a matter of fact, a ''[[Nintendo Power]]'' issue predating the release of ''Mario and Luigi: Partners in Time'' mentioned the similarities in a beta screenshot of the game.<ref>"Big purple dinosaurs have the power to mesmerize babies. That's how they lure them close enough to eat them." - ''Nintendo Power'' Volume 193, page 64.</ref>

==History==
===''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''===
[[File:YoobAttacks.png|thumb|left|Yoob appears in the Yoshi village.]]
The creature known as Yoob originally starts out as an abnormally large, pink-and-green [[Yoshi Egg]]. One day, this egg arrives on Yoshi's Island (presumably placed on the island by the Shroobs). Although no one knows where it comes from, the egg instantly becomes a tourist attraction. For five [[Coin]]s, tourists can actually witness the enormous egg close-up. However, the egg soon hatches, and Yoob is released. Since Yoob is apparently programmed by the [[Shroobs]] to eat Yoshis, it starts terrorizing the island, eating every Yoshi in sight. The remaining Yoshis flee to safety.

When the four [[Mario]] brothers (Mario, [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]]) first arrive on Yoshi's Island, the place is deserted. Most of the Yoshis have already been eaten. The few survivors are hiding in a small tree hut. When Mario, Luigi, and the babies discover the survivors, they hear a loud roar which prompts the Yoshis to flee.

Yoob is first seen by the plumbers after discovering the group of Yoshis still hiding on Yoshi's Island. The plumbers flee back inside the house as Yoob quickly eats all of the Yoshis, including [[Yoshi]] and [[Kylie Koopa]], although Kylie is quickly spat out due to being a Koopa. Yoob then leaves the area in search of more food, though he is later seen eating and spitting out [[Kamek]] after the quartet find him on the island. After the adult and baby Mario Bros. defeat Kamek and corner Baby Bowser at the cliff, Yoob climbs to where they are, and two [[Shroob UFO]]s appear and enlarge him. Yoob then proceeds to eat [[Baby Bowser]], [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]].

Once inside [[Yoob's Belly]], the brothers discover that Yoob does not have a normal digestive system. The group (except Baby Bowser) helps some of the previously swallowed Yoshis move forward in Yoob's stomach by pushing a [[Chomp Rock]], which clears a path that lets them walk through Yoob's intestines. They then discover that part of Yoob's Belly is a factory for trapping Yoshis. The factory, maintained by [[RC Shroober]]s, stores Yoshis in [[Yoob Egg]]s, then places them on a conveyor belt that takes them to [[Sunnycide]]. The group helps the Yoshis push the Chomp Rock away, then find [[Toadbert]], who has forgotten everything that happened after he traveled back in time, but is still taking care of some trapped Yoshis. Toadbert tells them that Sunnycide is guarding the exit, and they go to confront it. Sunnycide uses the Yoob Eggs in battle, and likewise, the heroes can free the Yoshis by breaking the eggs. Eventually, they defeat Sunnycide, and everyone, including the Yoshis, Toadbert, and Baby Bowser, escapes Yoob through the digestive tract, while Yoob seems to stop functioning altogether.

If Mario and Luigi return to Yoshi's Island, they can find Yoob's now-deactivated body attached to the side of a cliff. In fact, the Mario brothers can actually reenter Yoob's body to find items, [[badge]]s, and [[bean]]s the heroes may have missed during their first time through though they must exit through the digestive tract again.

===''Super Mario-kun''===
[[File:YoobSMK36.jpg|thumb|The Yoob and his backstory in the ''Super Mario-Kun'' volume 36.]]
In the ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', Yoob appears in volume 36. Unlike in ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'', Yoob originally was just a giant, carefree, friendly Yoshi who is gluttonous. He is used as a vessel for Sunnycide, as Sunnycide takes control of the giant Yoshi and turns him into a Yoob. After the brothers get eaten, they defeat Sunnycide inside, which reverts the Yoob to his normal state. The giant Yoshi then eats the Cobalt Star Shards, which causes the brothers to panic, and ends the chapter. They manage to get the shards out of him.

==Trivia==
*When the babies dig underground or attack with the hammer inside [[Yoob's Belly]], Yoob can be seen wincing in pain on the top screen. He also does this when the babies open a part in his belly by hammering the buttons and when the Yoshis roll the Chomp Rock down his stomach, creating a path through his insides. This stops after Sunnycide is destroyed.
*In the original version of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', an enemy named [[Fawful Guy]] has an attack where it is eaten and turned into a discolored Yoshi egg by an off-screen creature, whose roar is identical to Yoob's and the egg's color is identical to the eggs Yoob creates, too. It is unknown if it is really Yoob, but it is implied that it is or possibly a [[Fawfulized]] version of Yoob.
*[[Kylie Koopa]] christened the giant Shroob Yoshi "Yoob". It is unknown if the Shroobs themselves gave it a different name upon creation.
*It's unknown whether the Yoob is a bio-mechanical lifeform created from scratch or a Shroob Yoshi from the [[Shroob Planet]].
*There is a chance that the [[Shroob Rex]] was inspired by [[Yoob]], with a strong resemblance of each other.
