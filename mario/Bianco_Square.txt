'''Bianco Square''' is an area of [[Isle Delfino]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It is where the [[Grand Pianta Statue]] located, but [[Mario]] first has to defeat a brown [[Polluted Piranha Plant]] for it to rise out from the ground. Bianco Square also appears in various other iterations of Delfino Plaza, such as [[Delfino Plaza Court]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' or within the [[Delfino Plaza (stage)|Delfino Plaza]] stage in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.
