The '''Hidden Block Card''' is a recurring item in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Description==
Using the item causes a [[Hidden Block (Mario Party series)|Hidden Block]] to appear, and it rewards either [[coin]]s or a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]], depending on where the roulette lands; if the roulette lands on the transparent area, it awards coins, but if it lands in the smaller white area, the reward is Star.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Super Mario Party''===
Hidden Block Cards are exclusive to the Mario Party mode.

===''Mario Party Superstars''===
A Hidden Block Card can only be obtained from either an [[Item Bag]] or during the [[Last Five Turns Event]].
