'''Larry Koopa (Dark)''' is a enemy and boss in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' and ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. He is the dark alter-ego of [[Larry Koopa]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Dark Larry is identical to the real Larry, except the colors of his hair, face, shell and body is are darker shades, and he gains yellow eyes.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Dark Larry has all the abilites that Larry uses. He fights as a Ranged-unit type trooper, and his [[Special Skill]] is Throw the Fight.

== History ==
=== ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'' ===
Dark Larry Koopa first appears in ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser''. In ''Minion Quest'', he replaces Larry once he has been recruited in [[Larry's Fortress]], and after being fought in [[Goomba's Worth]].

=== ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'' ===
Dark Larry Koopa returns in ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey''. He replaces Larry after he is recruited in [[Lollygagging Larry]]. After the [[Best Fitness Friends]] [[Best Fitness Friends (merged form)|fuse into one]] in [[Like Father, Like Son]], they create dark doppelgangers of the [[Koopalings]]. Dark Larry also appears in [[Beat the Minions]]. He is fought in round 4, as the captain with [[Dark Morton Koopa Jr.|Dark Morton]] as his first officer, both infected with [[Malatone Formula:X]]. He later joins the other dark Koopalings and the dark counterpart of [[Bowser]], with all of them taking Malatone Forumla:X once more. Despite their efforts, they are defeated by [[Bowser Jr.]] and his army.
