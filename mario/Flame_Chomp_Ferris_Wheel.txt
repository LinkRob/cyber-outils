'''Flame Chomp Ferris Wheel''', or '''Meringue Clouds-5''', is the fifth level of [[Meringue Clouds]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by completing [[Vanishing Ghost House]]'s main exit. Clearing the level gives access to [[Three-Headed Snake Block]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts on a portion of the ground, followed by four rotating platforms, acting as Ferris Wheels, above a pit. [[Flame Chomp]]s are found throughout the section, and a [[? Block]] containing a power-up is at the center of the second Ferris Wheel platform. Another portion of terrain appears, followed by Ferris Wheels with six platforms on them, as well as [[Big Piranha Plant]]s and more Flame Chomps. Another section of Ferris Wheel platforms follows, along with a [[Red Ring]]. Flame Chomps appear throughout the section, and the [[Goal Pole]] is found after the rotating platforms.
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is located at the bottom of the third Ferris Wheel platform, and can be collected by riding on the platform.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is located on a Ferris Wheel in the section of the level with Big Piranha Plants, and the player must jump onto the Ferris Wheel and ride it to collect the Star Coin
*'''Star Coin 3''': Right before the Goal Pole, the player can either glide with the [[Flying Squirrel Mario|Flying Squirrel suit]] or as [[Peachette]] or bounce off of the Flame Chomp to reach the third Star Coin.
