'''World 3-1''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the first level of World 3, the [[Easton Kingdom]].

==Overview==
[[Mario]] starts this level near some Easter Island heads in a plains-like area. This level introduces a new enemy called [[Batadon]]s. They are flying stone heads that try to damage Mario usually over pits. [[Tokotoko]]s are also new enemies that attack by dashing into Mario. [[Bombshell Koopa]]s are also common enemies in this level as well.
