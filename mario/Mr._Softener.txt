'''Mr. Softener''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
[[Mario]] or his partners use a Mr. Softener, it reduces the enemies' defenses by 3. Mr. Softeners can be purchased at [[Niff T.'s Shop]] of [[Petalburg]] for 8 [[coin]]s. Cooking a [[Courage Shell]] and a [[Point Swap]] together is another way of acquiring Mr. Softeners.
