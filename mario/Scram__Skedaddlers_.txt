'''Scram, Skedaddlers!''' is the 3rd level of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] and also of [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']].

==Layout==

===Enemies===
The main enemies in the level are [[Skedaddler]]s, however a couple [[Koopa Troopa]]s can be seen throughout the level. There are also many crates and pipes scattered around the course, with one section consisting of many [[Marimba Block]]<nowiki/>s and another with many trees. The single [[Checkpoint Flag]] is found after a formation of 5 [[Pipe|pipes]] of different colours. The [[Goal Pole]] is guarded by another [[Skedaddler]], which destroys the peanut crates leading up to it.

===[[10-Flower Coin]]<nowiki/>s===
The 1st 10-Flower Coin appears above a Skedaddler's head, who will run down the length of a stretch of [[Marimba Blocks]]. Right before the [[Checkpoint Flag]] is a red pipe that leads to the 2nd 10-Flower Coin. The red pipe is covered by several [[Crate]]s. The last 10-flower coin is found in a tunnel, blocked by a peanut crate.
