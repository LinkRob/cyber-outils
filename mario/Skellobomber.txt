}}A '''Skellobomber''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Skellobombers are flying [[Skellobit]]s that are first encountered in [[Overthere Stair]] and later in [[Castle Bleck]]. They serve under [[Bonechill]] and they mainly attack by tossing their heads at one of the heroes. They can regrow their heads immediately. Skellobombers can also toss [[Skellobait]]s to aid them in battle.
