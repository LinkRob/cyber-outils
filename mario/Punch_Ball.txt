'''Punch Balls''' are items that only appear in ''[[Punch Ball Mario Bros.]]'', a game based on the original ''[[Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
These balls come in two colors, red or green. They can be used by players to throw at opponents. This will stun opponents, giving [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] the opportunity to defeat them.
