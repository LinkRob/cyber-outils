'''Soggy Guys''' are types of [[Shy Guy]] found in the game ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

== Appearance ==
They are Shy Guys that had fallen in the water. They are seen in Cherry Lake. The first one Mario encounters gives him an extra card slot after Mario defeats a [[Shy Stack|5-Shy Stack]]. They later appear in the [[Indigo Underground]] and [[Violet Passage]].
