}}The '''Post Office''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Found in northern [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]], this building is where [[Parakarry]] works at before temporary leaving to find his missing letters. It is also were his boss, the [[Postmaster]], works at as well. It is here that [[Mario]] can read letters addressed for the partners in his party.
