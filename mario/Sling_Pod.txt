A '''Sling Pod''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
Sling Pods are stretchable white balls of web with sticky properties, so [[Mario]] can attach himself to a Sling Pod before being launched. Once Mario is attached, the player has to use the [[Star Pointer]] to pull back on the Sling Pod in the direction opposite of where they want to launch Mario. When the stretched back Sling Pod is released, Mario is launched in that direction.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy''==
[[File:Sling_Pod.png|thumb|left|Mario pulling back on a Sling Pod in the Sling Pod Galaxy]]
Sling Pods can be found in [[Space Junk Galaxy]], the [[Sling Pod Galaxy]], and the [[Ghostly Galaxy]]. The Sling Pod Galaxy is named for Sling Pods appearing throughout, more prominently than in the other two [[Galaxy|galaxies]]. The only [[mission]] of Sling Pod Galaxy, [[A Very Sticky Situation]], involves using Sling Pods and [[Pull Star]]s alike as Mario makes his way to the [[Power Star]].
