'''Dual Slinger''' is [[Mario]]'s default weapon in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
The Dual Slinger appears in ''Sparks of Hope'' as Mario's weapon. The player can change the skins of the Dual Slinger. The player does not need to unlock the Dual Slinger since it is available at the start of the game.

== Statistics ==
The Dual Slinger can fire at two targets, or one target to deal twice as much damage.
