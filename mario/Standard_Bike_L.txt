The '''Standard Bike L''' is a Bike that appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. In ''Mario Kart Wii'', bikes were the big, new thing. This Standard Bike L is, as you'd expect, a very standard bike.

== Mario Kart Wii ==
This bike can be ridden by large weight characters. Including [[Wario]], [[Waluigi]], [[Donkey Kong]], [[Bowser]], [[King Boo]], [[Rosalina]], [[Funky Kong]], and [[Dry Bowser]].
