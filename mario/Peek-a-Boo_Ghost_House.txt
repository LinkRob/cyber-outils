'''Peek-a-Boo Ghost House''' is a [[level]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Go on the platform with the second Star Coin on it then head left near the ceiling and run left until you find a hidden door. Scurry up the platforms and jump to the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Wall jump the shaft above the first group of Circling Boo Buddies to get the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Go in the structure in the second group of Circling Boo Buddies to get the coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
In the background to the right of the circling boo buddies shows a spider web with a picture of Luigi form.

==Secret Exit==
By the flagpole of the first Star Coin shows a passageway to [[Fliprus Floes|Frosted Glacier-Secret Fliprus Floes]].
