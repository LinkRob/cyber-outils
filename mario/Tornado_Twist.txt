'''Tornado Twist''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] used by [[Fly Guy]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Fly Guy turns upside down and spins his propeller to create a tornado, puts it onto the ball, and hits it at the receiver, causing them to briefly spin within the tornado.
