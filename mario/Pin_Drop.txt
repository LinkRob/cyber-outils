'''Pin Drop''' is a [[Microgame]] from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'', being featured in the level Lava at First Sight, which involves the second set of microgames [[Wario]] hosts. It uses the Scales form for its gameplay.

== Layout ==
The microgame involves a vertical chamber. [[Wario]] floats down using a parachute, with some light so players can see what he is coming up on.

== Gameplay ==
The player has to tilt their body so Wario can dodge the spikes on his way down. Harder levels feature larger amounts of spikes for Wario to dodge, and if a spike jabs him, the player loses.
