A '''Snapdragon''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are plant-like enemies encountered on [[Rose Way]]. Snapdragons have a stronger variant named [[Fink Flower]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Snapdragons attack physically by lashing their leaves at a party member. They can also use [[Pollen Nap]], which can put one of [[Mario]]'s party members to sleep.

===Strategy===
Snapdragons, being plants, are weak to [[Fire (element)|fire]], so any of Mario's fire techniques are effective against them.
