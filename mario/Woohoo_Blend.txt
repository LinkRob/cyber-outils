The '''Woohoo Blend''' is a type of blend which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Usage ==
The Woohoo Blend can be bought in the [[Starbeans Cafe]], but [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must have at least 25 [[Woo Bean]]s (or 10 in the remake) for the Woohoo Blend to be made. When Mario or Luigi drinks Woohoo Blend, it adds 4 HP to their total, or 2 HP in the remake.
