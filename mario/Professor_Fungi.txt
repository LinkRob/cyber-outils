'''Professor Fungi''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Professor Fungi is a [[Purple Toad]] with a white mustache and beard.

==In ''Mario Party 2''==
[[File:FungiJapan.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Professor Fungi with a pipe in the Japanese version of ''Mario Party 2''.]]
Professor Fungi is found in Option Lab, typing the player's records on his typewriter (identified as a "computer" in-game). He types how many times the player played the [[board]]s, how many times the characters won, minigame records that the characters will attempt to beat the record in the minigame, and [[Driver's Ed]] records when the player unlocks the minigame as they can attempt to beat the record when playing the minigame.

He also has an unnamed assistant with a garbage can next to him. If the player deletes their save file, Professor Fungi's assistant throws the player's records in the trash can.

In the Japanese version, Professor Fungi has a pipe smoker, but it was removed in releases outside of Japan to avoid any references to tobacco.
