'''TNT Drumsticks''' are items that they are expendable by [[Bowser]] has access in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]] and its remake ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.'' TNT Drumsticks are the strongest form of drumsticks, ban the Cheesy Drumsticks in the reboot, surpassing  the [[Hot Drumstick|Hot Drumsticks]] and the [[Fiery Drumstick|Fiery Drumsticks]]. If they consumed, Bowser instantly put back 280 Heart Points, creating the powerful item. It can be conduct from [[Broque Monsieur]]'s item shop afterhand the boss fight at the [[Tower of Yikk]] for sixty [[Coin|coins]]. Few enemies have a risk of dropping one of them after fighting them: [[Choomba|Choombas]] and [[Toothy|Toothies]] have a 30% risk, and [[Crawful|Crawfuls]] and [[Jailgoon|Jailgoons]] have a 5% risk.
