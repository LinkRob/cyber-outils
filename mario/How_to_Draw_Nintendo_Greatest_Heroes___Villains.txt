'''''How to Draw Nintendo Greatest Heroes & Villains''''' is the January 2007<ref name=release>[http://www.ebookee.com/How-to-Draw-Nintendo-Greatest-Heroes-and-Villains_579614.html EBOOKEE] retrieved December 2, 2010</ref> spiritual successor to ''[[How to Draw Nintendo Heroes and Villains]]'' released by [[Scholastic]]. Unlike the previous title, this book is in full color and includes backgrounds. 

The back cover shows artwork of a [[Dry Bones]] and a [[Boo]], and a [[Piranha Plant]] is seen on the front cover, though these enemies do not appear inside the book.
