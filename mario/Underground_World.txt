The '''Underground World''' is the name for the [[wikipedia:Hollow_Earth|hollow]], internal structure of the [[Mushroom Earth|''Mushroom Earth'']] and serves as the main setting for the game, ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. It's numerous layers are most easily traversed by the enormous vertical tunnels connecting each layer to the next. At its [[Planet Core|center]] is the [[Heart of Gold]].
