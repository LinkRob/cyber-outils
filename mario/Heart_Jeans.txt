The '''Heart Jeans''' is a clothing item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. In both of its appearances, the Heart Jeans can only be worn by [[Luigi]].

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Heart Jeans can be bought from the clothes shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 120 [[coin]]s, or 110 coins in the Japanese version. Equipping it to Luigi increases his [[HP]] by 9 and his [[Defense]] by 10.

In the remake, the Heart Jeans is instead obtained at [[Woohoo Hooniversity]]. It no longer increases HP, but its Defense rating was more than doubled to 26. Heart Jeans was also given the [[Mushroom Recover]] special effect so that any [[Mushroom]] item used on Luigi can additionally cure any status effect if he has any. Heart Jeans can be sold for only three coins.
