'''Chomp Rock 'n' Roll''' is the second [[level]] of [[World 1 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 1]] in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

==Layout==
The level starts with a [[Chomp Rock]] and some [[Shy Guy]]s, followed by a pipe that produces [[Mega Guy]]s for [[Yoshi (species)|Pink Yoshi]] to turn into [[Mega Eggdozer]]s. One Mega Eggdozer must be used to destroy a [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] that blocks an opening to a [[Whirly Gate]]; within the Whirly Gate is a power-up that turns Pink Yoshi into a [[Mine-Cart Yoshi|Mine-Cart]], and he must reach the end gate by navigating a series of platforms populated with Shy Guys. Past this is a [[Middle Ring]] and an [[Egg Plant]]. A second Chomp Rock is found in the next area, and Pink Yoshi must use it to clear a path through several walls of soft rock and a bed of [[thorn]]s, leading to the level's [[GOAL! Ring]].

==Flowers==
#Located in a secret room shortly after the beginning of the level. Above the space where the level's first Chomp Rock comes to a stop, there is a hidden area where Pink Yoshi must [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] onto some soft rock and enter a door. On the other side, he must defeat all the Shy Guys on a series of platforms to produce the Smiley Flower; he can do so using another Chomp Rock.
#Found at the end of the Mine-Cart Yoshi course, beside the goal ring. Pink Yoshi must jump to collect it.
#Inside a [[Winged Cloud]] above the Chomp Rock after the Checkpoint Ring.
#Found in a hidden area in the ceiling after the upward slope leading to the walls of soft rock. Pink Yoshi can access it by jumping off a Chomp Rock or an enemy.
#Found in an underground area accessed via the last pipe in a row of five pipes near the end of the level. Pink Yoshi must first clear the thorns at the pipe's entrance.
