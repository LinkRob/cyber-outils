'''The Traveling Sisters Three''' are a trio of [[Toad (species)|Toad]] sisters who appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They frequently appear throughout the game.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
The Traveling Sisters Three are first seen in [[Petalburg]] where they are freaked out by the flirtatious [[Dupree]]. They appear in every area up until the final one where they stay on [[Keelhaul Key]] for the remainder of the game.

==Trivia==
*Oddly enough, during the events of Chapter 4, The Traveling Sisters Three are turned into [[pig]]s. This make them one of few non [[Twilighter]]s to be turned into pigs with the other being [[Dupree]].
*The Traveling Sisters Three are a reference to the [[Three Beautiful Sisters]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
