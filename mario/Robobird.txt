'''Robobirds''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profiles==
===Physical description===
Robobirds are blue-gray, mechanical airborne creatures with a drill for a beak and a jetpack on their back.

===Behavior===
A Robobird flies around using a jetpack on its back. At first, a Robobird flies in one spot in midair, but if [[Wario]] gets close to a Robobird, it charges toward him in a straight line. A Robobird can pass through walls and hurt Wario upon contact. Unlike most enemies, Robobirds can respawn in the area Wario is currently in, compared to how most support enemies only respawn if Wario leaves the area before returning there.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Robobirds are only encountered in the [[Ruby Passage]] levels during the Hard and S-Hard modes. Robobirds can be defeated with a jump attack, a [[Ground Pound|ground pound]] or by throwing something at them. Unlike most other enemies, a Robobird does not yield any [[Coin (Wario Land 4)|coins]] upon being defeated.
