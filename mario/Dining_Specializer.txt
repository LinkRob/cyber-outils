The '''Dining Specializer''' is a gadget that resembles a [[Nintendo DS]]. The player can find the Dining Specializer at [[Saffron]]'s restaurant, [[Sweet Smiles]] and [[Dyllis]]'s restaurant, [[Hot Fraun]]. It appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
The '''Dining Specializer''' resembles a [[Nintendo DS]]. The player can find the Dining Specializer at [[Sweet Smiles]] and [[Hot Fraun]]. The player must find the [[Cooking Disk]]s to unlock recipe guidelines.
