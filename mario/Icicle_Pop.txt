The '''Icicle Pop''' is a [[recipe]] made by [[Zess T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Mixing an [[Ice Storm]] and [[Honey Syrup]] creates an Icicle Pop. Icicle Pops can be used both outside and in battle. If used outside of battle, the pop casually restores fifteen [[Flower Point|FP]]. However, if used in battle, it will restore 15 FP, but will [[Frozen|freeze]] the player.

The English description for this item erroneously states that it restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] instead of 15 FP, a possible translation error.
