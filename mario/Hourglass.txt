'''Hourglass''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
As the name implies, Hourglass can be used to measure time. Three hourglasses with different times are displayed; one minute, three minutes, and five minutes. The hourglasses flip after one side is completely empty. No buttons need to be pressed to activate or flip the hourglasses. Once one side of an hourglass becomes empty, the player can rotate the system.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The Hourglass was invented by [[Professor E. Gadd]] as a way to tell time. It can be obtained from [[Bowser]] as a reward for completing the quest [[Boss Bowser]].
