'''Master Mantis''' is a recurring character in the {{series|WarioWare}}. He first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Master Mantis is an elderly man with long, gray hair, a round, red nose, and a downward-pointed mustache. He wears a purple martial arts uniform.

===Relationships===
Ever since [[Young Cricket]] was a young child, Master Mantis has been his martial arts teacher. The two are very frequently seen together and always share a stage in the ''WarioWare'' games.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''===
Master Mantis shares a stage with Young Cricket, [[Enter Young Cricket]], although named after the latter. In the opening cutscene, while walking down [[Park Street]], Master Mantis and Young Cricket smell dumplings being served at a nearby food stand. In the ending cutscene, after Young Cricket angered the [[Park Street Residents]] by skipping to the front of the line, he returns to Master Mantis and tells him that they should get in line, to which Master Mantis agrees.

===''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''===
Master Mantis co-runs the [[Assembly Dojo]] alongside Young Cricket. Master Mantis is also one of the characters who appears as a comic stamp.
