The '''Gate Handle''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Gate Handle can be found in a room of the [[Pirate's Grotto]] and it can be used to raise the floodgate near the entrance of the area. [[Mario]] will then be able to access the lower areas of the Pirate's Grotto in his boat form.
