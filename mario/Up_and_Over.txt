Tonde Nobotte Norikoete|Jump, Climb Up and Over}} is known as '''World 1-1'''. '''Up and Over''' is the first level in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

==Layout==
'''Up and Over''' takes place in the plains. In the mode of [[Pink Coin]]s, there are two [[Question Block]]s that contain [[Mushroom]]s. The player will encounter two short ledges that he/she can vault with five coins each. The player will land onto the ground, encountering a [[Goomba]] and a green [[Pipe]]. There is a bottomless pit that can be avoided and another ?Block containing on [[coin]]. The player will encounter three Goombas and a [[Pause Block]]. The top ? Block contains one coin. The first [[Brick Block]] contains nothing while the three ? Block contains one coin each, and the last Brick Block contains five coins. After the player jumps over the tall ledge, there are two ? Blocks: the left ? Block containing a coin while the right ? Block containing a Mushroom. There are arrows pointing ↗ and →. The arrow pointing → leads to five coins above the path and two Goombas. The arrow pointing ↗ leads to a Koopa Troopa then the Pink Coin along with the Pause Block.

|-|Pink Coins = The level starts with a [[Super Mushroom]]. After that, the player can see an arrow block pointing right, which spawns four coins, and the player can see a [[Goomba]] and the first [[Pink Coin (Super Mario Run)|Pink Coin]]. To collect it, the player must do a high jump. Afterwards, there is a [[Warp Pipe]] and a [[? Block]], with the block having a [[Coin]] in it. After them, three Goombas appear alongside two [[Brick Block]]s, four ? Blocks, and the second Pink Coin. The player must jump over the blocks to get it. After that there are two ? Blocks, one of them has a Coin and the other one has a Mushroom. There's an arrow block pointing ↗, followed by a [[Koopa Troopa]] and the third Pink Coin, then three pipes and the fourth Pink Coin. The player must do a high jump on the third pipe to get it. After that there are two ? Blocks, but one of them is out of reach for the player, and three Goombas as well as a Warp Pipe. After that, there are five Goombas in a row and the fifth and final Pink Coin. The player must do a high jump when jumping on fifth Goomba to get it. There is also a Koopa Troopa and two [[Semisolid Platform]]s before the player gets to the [[Goal Pole]].
|-|Purple Coins =
The level starts with a Super Mushroom. After that, the player can see an arrow block in the air pointing ↘, which spawns four coins, and the player can see a Goomba and the first [[Purple Coin]]. To collect the Purple Coin, the player must jump from the Goomba. Afterward, there are a Warp Pipe and a ? Block, with the block having a [[Coin]] in it. After them, three Goombas appear alongside five Brick Blocks, three ? Blocks, and the second Purple Coin. The player must perform a high jump over the blocks to get the Purple Coin. After that there are two ? Blocks, one of them has a Coin and the other one has a Mushroom. There's an arrow block pointing ↗, followed by a group of Goombas and the third Purple Coin in a pit, then three pipes. After that there are a Goomba, two ? Blocks, and the fourth Purple Coin, which the player must perform a high jump to collect. After going past three Goombas, Mario arrives at a stack of two Semisolid Platforms with Goombas on them, with the second one having a Brick Block above it. The player must reach the second Semisolid Platform and hit the Brick Block to collect the fifth and last Purple Coin. There is also a Koopa Troopa and two Semisolid Platforms before the player gets to the [[Goal Pole]].
|-|Black Coins = The level starts with a Super Mushroom. The first [[Black Coin]] is located next to a mid-air wall near the beginning. The player must wall-jump off of it to obtain the Black Coin. After a Goomba, a pipe, and three Goombas, the second Black coin will be on a Cloud Platform with two other Coins. The player must jump on top of the two [[? Block]]s underneath, then jump and Spin-Jump (if the player is playing as [[Yoshi]], Flutter-Jump) to obtain the Black Coin. The third is in a pit much similar to its Purple counterpart. The player must jump and slide down the right wall to get it, then quickly wall-jump out of the pit. The fourth Black Coin is located on another Cloud Platform. The player must jump onto the ? Block underneath, and quickly wall-jump to the coin. If playing as Yoshi, Flutter-Jumping will make this process easier. Near the end of the course, there will be two [[Brick Block]]s with a ? Block in between them. The player should break the left Brick Block to obtain the fifth and final Black Coin.
