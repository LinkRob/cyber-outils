The '''Cashzap Candy''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Cashzap Candy.jpg|thumb|left|Artwork]]
The Cashzap Candy is a [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] with a green wrapper depicting a [[Lightning]] on the front. If a character uses a Cashzap Candy, lightning first surrounds them, inverting their colors, then the user goes to where the target is and electrocutes them, taking away half their [[coin]]s, although the user does not receive them. Afterward, the character returns to their original [[space]], similar to the effects of using the [[Vampire Candy]].

==''Mario Party 8''==
In [[Party Tent]], the player has to first choose which opponent to target on a spinning dart wheel. In Tag Battle, players can destroy coins from one random opposing team character. In [[Star Battle Arena]], they do not spin a dart wheel because they always have only one opponent.

Cashzap Candy can be obtained at [[DK's Treetop Temple]], [[King Boo's Haunted Hideaway]], and [[Koopa's Tycoon Town]].
