'''Last Stand P''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is the partner-based equivalent of [[Last Stand]]

==Effects==
Last Stand P, like Last Stand, costs only one [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear. While worn, Last Stand P halves the damage [[Mario]]'s partners take while they are in [[Danger]].

Last Stand P is found twice. One of these badges is found in [[Hooktail Castle]]. It is found near the end of the castle, near the top of the right tower. Near the top, Mario can use a purple block to move down in this badge's area. Mario can then use Koops to grab this badge. Another Last Stand P is purchased from the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]], where it is sold for fifty [[coin]]s.
