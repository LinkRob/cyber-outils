'''Macro Zone''' is one of the zones featured in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It takes place within a giant house and a yard, with [[Mario]] being at an ant-sized proportion. The [[Sewer Rat]] is the boss of Macro Zone.
