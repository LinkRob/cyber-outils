'''Mayor Penguin''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is the mayor of [[Shiver City]] and lives there along with his [[Mayor Penguin's Wife|wife]]. He is also best friends with the famous novelist, [[Herringway]].

==Appearance==
Mayor Penguin is a light-blue [[Bumpty]] with large, brown hair.

==History==
Mayor Penguin is first seen where he was knocked out at his home in Shiver City. Many, including his wife, thought he was murdered and that [[Mario]] was to blame. However, after Herringway was brought to the scene, Mayor Penguin suddenly wakes up and reveals that he accidentally knocked himself out while trying to get a present for Herringway. Mayor Penguin apologizes for the misunderstanding and allows Mario to leave Shiver City. Later, Mayor Penguin gives Mario the [[Scarf]] which is needed alongside the [[Bucket]] to access [[Shiver Mountain]] and, eventually, the [[Crystal Palace]] where the final [[Star Spirits|Star Spirit]] rests. Mayor Penguin is seen much later during the celebration at [[Princess Peach's Castle]] as one of the many attendees there.

A letter can also be delivered to Mayor Penguin by using [[Parakarry]] and finding the letter in a tree on [[Shiver Snowfield]] (after hammering the tree three times). Mayor Penguin says that the letter is addressed from [[Yoshi Village]] and remarks he doesn't know where it is due to the village being far away. Mayor Penguin simply states he will read the letter later and gives Mario a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] as a reward.
