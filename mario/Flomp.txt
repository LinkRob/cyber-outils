A '''Flomp''' (also called a '''Flimp''') is an enemy debuting in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
A Flomp is a floating [[Thwomp]] shaped like a platform. It has a green outer edges with spikes sticking out. However, they have a green octagonal frame adorned with spikes and two screws on the sides, making it resemble a watch, possibly referring to how timing is important when dealing with Flomps.

=== Behavior ===
[[File:SMG2 Angry Flomp.png|thumb|left|200px|''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' model of a Flomp with an angry face, shown before flipping]]
Flomps are floating platform enemies that like to sleep. Flomps can be used as regular platforms, with some revolving mildly in one spot whereas others move from side to side. However, at set intervals, a Flomp makes a rattling sound, its sleepy face turns into an evil grin, and the Flomp shakes a bit before quickly flipping over and resuming its original position.
{{br|left}}
== In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
Flomps are found at [[Bowser's Lava Lair]] and [[Grandmaster Galaxy]]. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] has to jump from Flomps in order to progress. If Mario or Luigi is standing on a Flomp when it flips, he get flung back into [[lava]], [[space]], [[Black Hole]], or possibly another Flomp if he [[Star Spin|spins]] in midair. Flomps cannot be defeated in any way.

== In ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' ==
[[File:Flip Out MPIT.png|thumb|200px|''Mario Party: Island Tour'' screenshot of Flomps in Flip Out]]
Flomps appear in the minigame, [[Flip Out]], titled "Let Sleeping Flomps Lie" in PAL releases. Here, the characters must use Flomps and regular platforms to cross a chasm to the finish line. If a character is on a Flomp as it flips, they are flung three spaces backwards.
