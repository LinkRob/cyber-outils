The '''Fire Element Medal''' is an item in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
[[File:Poltergust 3000 fire LM artwork.png|thumb|left|200px|Artwork of the Poltergust 3000 shooting a blast of fire for ''Luigi's Mansion'']]
The Fire Element Medal is one of the three [[Element Medal]]s, the other two being the [[Water Element Medal]] and the [[Ice Element Medal]]. The Fire Element Medal in particular allows the [[Poltergust 3000]] to vacuum in a [[Fire Elemental Ghost]], giving it a limited supply of fire, which can either light candles, melt [[ice (element)|ice]], or burn [[fake door]]s. Releasing fire is required to defeat a [[Flash]], as it is needed to expose its heart.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Fire Element Medal is the first Element Medal obtained. It is contained within a large [[treasure chest]] in the [[Mirror Room]], where it is revealed after vacuuming up every [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]]. Like with the other two Element Medals, the Fire Element Medal's effects are not shown in a [[Mirror (object)|mirror]].
