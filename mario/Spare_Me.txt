'''Spare Me''' is one of the [[microgame]]s under [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s [[Sports]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
An overhead view of a gray bowling lane is shown, with a red bowling ball on the left and one to three bowling pins on the right. The trajectory is made up of separate red dots.

==Gameplay==
The goal is to bowl over every bowling pin, just as in real bowling. To accomplish this, the player must press {{button|gba|A}} to launch the ball when the trajectory properly lines up with the bowling pins. It is possible for the bowling ball to roll into the gutter, and a knocked-over pin can hit other pins to knock them over as well. Once every bowling pin has been knocked over, the player wins the microgame. If they do not roll over all or any of the bowling pins, they instead lose.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level adds another bowling pin that has to be knocked over.

*'''Level 1:''' There is only one bowling pin to knock over.
*'''Level 2:''' There are two bowling pins to knock over.
*'''Level 3:''' There are three bowling pins to knock over.
