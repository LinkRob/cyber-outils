==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': When entering the underwater part of the level, jump on the three platforms and jump to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After passing the poles near the former spot of the first Star Coin, swim underwater under the three same-sized spinning platforms to get the Star Coin that moves under the platforms while avoiding the Spiny Cheep-Cheeps.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Jump on any of the last three spinning platform (or the last pole in the area) to get the Star Coin over the second to last spinning platform.

==Hidden Luigi image==
When entering the pipe leading into the ship, go underwater and swim to the left wall which shows a picture of Super Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' swimming.

==Secret Exit==
When reaching to the green exit pipe leading to the original, go under water and avoid the Spiny Cheep-Cheeps to find a secret red pipe by the right wall leading to the hidden flagpole. The flagpole leads to [[Beanstalk Jungle|Sparkling Waters-Secret: Beanstalk Jungle]].
