'''Green Toad and Purple Toad''' are [[Palace Guards (Illumination)|Palace Guards]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. They were laughing at each other, telling jokes, until they mistakenly chased Mario for unkowningly, trespassing the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]]. Later, they watch Peach, and Mario leave on a journey to the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]].
