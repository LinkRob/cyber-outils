'''{{world-link|4|ghostn|World 4-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros.)}}''' is the [[Ghost House]] of [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 4]] found in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

== Layout ==
[[File:4-GH_NSMB.png|thumb|left|[[Fire Mario]] collecting the first [[Star Coin]]]]
The player starts off with a series of [[Semisolid Platform]]s with [[Boo]]s to avoid. Next, striped platforms can be found which take [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to the next segments, some of which include [[Balloon Boo]]s to avoid. The player should take the [[Warp Pipe]] by pressing the [[P Switch]] on the third segment. The player should then find a red [[! Switch]] to make red blocks appear and allow access to a [[Door]], as a Balloon Boo chases the player.

In the next room, the player must head to the left to press another ! Switch to make two red blocks appear, allowing access to another Warp Pipe which will take Mario or Luigi another level up whilst avoiding another Balloon Boo. The player must use the gray [[? Block]]s to get up another floor and take the Door. This leads to outside of the mansion, where the [[Goal Pole]] can be found, unlocking [[World 4-5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 4-5]].

=== Secret exit ===
Within the same room, Mario or Luigi must be in [[Mini Mario|Mini-form]] must get to the top of the Warp Pipe, and Wall Jump to get to some higher blocks and more platforms, and find a door to go through, where the player will find the red-flag exit. This will unlock the Cannon level, in which the next level after will be [[World 7-1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7-1]].

== [[Star Coin]] locations ==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' There are two way to collect the first Star Coin. The player can go through a door in the first segment and press the [[? Switch]] there, making four ? Blocks appear, tied to long ropes. Mario or Luigi must stand on them and once the countdown ends, they'll lift Mario or Luigi up, teleporting them to a higher level and allow access to the first Star Coin. Or, by taking the first striped platform, the player can simply find an accessible route upwards by finding Hidden Blocks and destroying some [[Brick Block]]s.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After reaching the second room, by pressing the ! Switch, Mario or Luigi should [[Wall Jump]] on red blocks nearby to get through a Semisolid Platform, then wall jump again to get the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In the same way the player tries to find the secret exit, instead of going through the door, Wall Jump a few times to find the final Star Coin.
