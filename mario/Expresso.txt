'''Expresso the Ostrich''' is an [[Animal Buddy]] to the [[Kong Family]] appearing in the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' series.

==Games==
===''Donkey Kong Country/Land''===
Expresso makes his first appearance in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' as one of the [[Animal Buddy|Animal Buddies]] to help [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] out on their adventure to stop the [[Kremling Krew]]. He can flap throughout the air temporarily and even has his own [[Animal Token|token]]. Due to the tallness of Expresso, some enemies, such as [[Klaptrap]]s pass right through him.

He reappears in ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'' acting the same way as in its semi-sequel, but can defeat enemies when jumping on them.

===''Donkey Kong Country 2'' (Game Boy Advance)===
Expresso reappears in the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' as a racing bird acquired by [[Cranky Kong|Cranky]] inside of [[Monkey Museum|his hut]]. Unfortunately, he was weak so [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] had to gain [[Golden Feather (Donkey Kong)|feathers]] to power up some of his stats. When racing, he races against red, blue and a green coloration of Expresso with different names. When winning first place all across [[Crocodile Isle]], his image is saved inside of the [[scrapbook]].

===''Donkey Kong Racing''===
Expresso can be seen at the beginning of the trailer in ''[[Donkey Kong Racing]]'' where thousands of them can be seen running.

===''Diddy Kong Pilot'' (2003 build)===
Expresso appears in the 2003 build of ''[[Diddy Kong Pilot (2003 build)|Diddy Kong Pilot]]'' making numerous cameos in desert levels. There is even a smaller and bigger variation of him in later desert levels.
