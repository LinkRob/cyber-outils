'''Star Carnival Bowling''' is a one-to-four-player [[minigame]] found only in the [[Extras Zone]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. Players can use either characters from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] or [[Mii]]s from their [[Wii]] console. This minigame resembles the [[w:c:nintendo:Bowling|Bowling]] event in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Wii Sports|Wii Sports]]'', except players bowl with the {{button|wii|A}} button instead of the {{button|wii|B}} button.

==Opening==
A closeup of the ten pins that are set up at the end of the player's bowling lane is shown. The camera then zooms backwards until it is behind the player, who is holding a bowling ball, ready to bowl. The minigame then begins.

==Gameplay==
Players can choose between either five or ten frames of bowling and play by swinging {{button|wii|Wiimote}} while holding {{button|wii|A}} to throw the ball down the lane into the ten pins at the end. Just like in the actual game, players score [[point]]s based on how many pins they are able to successfully knock down (up to 300 points). A spare adds 10 points plus the number of pins knocked down in the player's next throw. A strike adds 10 points plus the number of pins knocked down in the player's next two throws. If they want to check out the current score, players can press {{button|wii|B}} at any time before they continue on with their turns.

==Ending==
The camera pans from left to right across the scoring screen above the lane, showing players' final scores for the game. The camera then pans to the player(s), who do(es) their winning animation(s). In the multiplayer version, the losing players do their losing animations (this also happens if the player gets zero points in the single-player version).

==Controls==
* '''Wii Remote'''
** {{button|wii|A}} &ndash; Hold to swing
* '''Buttons'''
** {{button|wii|Pad}} &ndash; Move left/right
** {{button|wii|A}} &ndash; Hold to swing
** {{button|wii|B}} &ndash; Score
