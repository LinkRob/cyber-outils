'''Glad Red Koopas''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. 

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are a variant of [[Red Koopa Troopa]]s imbued with the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
Glad Red Koopas act similarly to Red Koopa Troopas, with a difference being that Glad Red Koopas skip happily due to being under the effects of the Joy vibe.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
A Glad Red Koopa first appears in the level [[Ladida Plains 1-2]].

==Flavor text==
*''[[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]]: ''A Koopa Troopa who has learned to jump happily.''
