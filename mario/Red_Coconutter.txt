'''Red Coconutter''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is a stronger and faster variant of [[Coconutter]] that wears red tribal clothing.

It is encountered in [[Thwomp Volcano]], fighting alongside [[Thwack]]s and [[Tanoomba]]s. Red Coconutters can throw their spears, as well as shoot multiple darts from a blowgun. When a Red Coconutter throws its spear, its shining eye indicates whether it will attack either [[Mario]] and [[Baby Mario]] or [[Luigi]] and [[Baby Luigi]]. Its spear-throwing attack has a chance of [[Poison (status effect)|poisoning]] whoever it hits.
