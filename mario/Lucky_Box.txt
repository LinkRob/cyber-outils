The '''Lucky Box''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
Like the [[Coin Box (Mario Party series)|Coin Box]], the Lucky Box stores coins and [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s, but after every round of partying, the player earns an additional 10% coin bonus from that round's total.

The Lucky Box can be purchased from the [[Mushroom Shop]] in [[Village Square|Mushroom Village]] for 400 [[coin]]s.
