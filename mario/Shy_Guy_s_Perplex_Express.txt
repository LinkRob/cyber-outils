'''Shy Guy's Perplex Express''' is a [[board]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
[[File:StarBattleArena ShyGuyPerplexExpress.png|thumb|The Duel Battle and Star Battle Arena variant]]
Shy Guy's Perplex Express is set on a moving passenger train. The board consists of the inside and the top of the train's cars (there is also a secret area called Payday Waystation). The steam locomotive is driven by a [[Shy Guy]] engineer, and the train cars themselves are inhabited by [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, and Shy Guys. The objective on this board is to get to the front of the train, where the Shy Guy engineer gives away a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] for 20 [[coin]]s. The board is a one-way board, without junctions. Green [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] cannot be obtained on this board.

Players who land on a [[DK Space]] cause the train to be switched with [[Donkey Kong]]'s [[Barrel Train (Mario Party 8)|Barrel Train]]. The player who makes it to Donkey Kong first gets a free Star. Shy Guy's train comes back after Donkey Kong gives a Star away. When players land on a [[Bowser Space]], [[Bowser]] comes on his train, the [[Bad-Breath Express]]. If players meet Bowser, he takes a Star away from them. In Star Battle Arena, Bowser steals half of the player's coins. However, when a player comes without a Star or coins in Star Battle Arena, Bowser gives the player 10 coins. The [[Lucky Space]] causes the train to stop and let the player off at [[Payday Waystation]], which has many coins and a free Star.

===Green Spaces===
*If a player lands on the [[Event Space|Green Space]] in front of Holly Koopa, she offers to buy one of their Candies for several coins. The number of coins offered is linked to the Candy's cost when bought at a [[Candy Shop]]. Players in low positions (3rd/4th in Battle Royale) receive higher offers than players in high positions (1st/2nd in Battle Royale).
*The Green Space in front of the Shy Guy chef's heat vent causes the player to be sucked up to the roof of the train and vice versa for the Green Space in front of the vent on the roof. 
*The Green Space on the roof of the green car makes the Shy Guy conductor come and ask for the player's assistance. He informs the player that Holly Koopa's Candy was stolen and says that either [[Bandit]] or [[Little Mouser|Mowz]] did the act. The player must then choose a car where they think the perpetrator is. If they are correct, Shy Guy rewards them with 20 coins (10 only in the Star Battle Arena, due to the winning requirement).
*Green Spaces in the red car, on top of the yellow car (before the vent Green Space) as well as on top of the indigo car will cause [[Kamek|Magikoopa]] to appear and use his magic to move the front car to the back of the train, and move the other cars upward to the front.
*The Green Space on top of the red car will cause the characters on top to be hit back to the start by a bar, causing them to lose 5 coins each.
*The Green Space on top of the light blue train car causes a Shy Guy to throw a [[coin bag]] into the engine's firebox, which then causes coins to erupt from the smoke stack and land on players on the top, with each player receiving between 2 and 10 coins. Players in lower positions will receive a higher number of coins.

==Controversy==
{{Main|List of controversies#Mario Party 8}}
In the United Kingdom, various copies of ''Mario Party 8'' were recalled due to this board featuring a scene where [[Kamek|Magikoopa]] would alter the order of the cars, chanting, "[[Magikoopa]] magic! Turn the train [[wikipedia:Spastic (word)|spastic]]! Make this ticket tragic!", with "spastic" being a highly offensive word for someone with cerebral palsy or a person who acts clumsily or foolishly. Later copies changed this line by replacing "spastic" with "erratic". Later American copies even had the line changed completely to "Let me use my magic to make this all a little more interesting!"<ref>[https://tcrf.net/Mario_Party_8#Regional_Differences ''Mario Party 8'']. ''The Cutting Room Floor''. Retrieved July 04, 2024.</ref>

==[[Star Battle Arena]]==
In Star Battle Arena, the player or the opponent wins by acquiring one Star from [[Holly Koopa]]. And the star is 50 coins. (This is similar to the stage [[Goomba's Booty Boardwalk]]. And again, it is similar to [[Pagoda Peak]] --from Solo Cruise in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''-- only the star --on the Mario Party 7's Solo Cruise version of [[Pagoda Peak]]-- it is 100 coins.) Players who meet the conductor get 10 coins from him. (That is, if they get to Holly with less than 50 coins and can't pay for the star.) Only three passenger cars appear in Star Battle Arena. Donkey Kong also doubles the player's coin total, depending on how many coins they have by the time they get to him. If the player has zero coins, however, he gives them 10 coins instead
