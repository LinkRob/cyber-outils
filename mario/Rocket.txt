The '''Rocket''' is an item in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. It first appears in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its Western conversion ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==In the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]==
===''Super Mario Bros. 2''===
A Rocket appears in only three levels, [[World 4-1 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 4-1]], [[World 4-2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 4-2]], and [[World 7-1 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 7-1]]. One of the playable characters—[[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]], or [[Toad (character)|Toad]]—can pluck a Rocket out of the ground. In doing so, they enter it automatically and blast upward to the next part of the level. There, the player's character automatically ejects from the Rocket after reaching a certain height, followed by the Rocket exploding itself.

Rockets appear as white with red fins and three boosters coming from their base.

===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''===
Rockets are used to transport to one of the [[Special World]]s. The first Rocket appears in [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]], where the [[Sprixie Princesses]] construct it for [[Mario]] and the other playable characters after they rescue the Sprixie Princesses in [[World Bowser]]. The Rocket in World 1 leads to [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]], where a Rocket to [[World Mushroom (Super Mario 3D World)|World Mushroom]] can be accessed after meeting the necessary prerequisites, mainly from completing every level in World Star. The third Rocket is in [[World Flower (Super Mario 3D World)|World Flower]], and it leads to [[World Crown]] and becomes accessible from fulfilling similar prerequisites.

===''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''===
Here, they are named '''Mini Rockets'''. These items can be captured, and launching them transports Mario to a bonus area. They are portrayed as white with red fins and a single booster, and are now about taller by half than Mario himself.
