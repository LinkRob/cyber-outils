'''Poison Puffs''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Poison Puffs are the most powerful and dangerous variant of [[Ruff Puff]]s. They only appear in the deepest levels of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]], where they are fought several times. Poison Puffs fly very fast, and can charge themselves up for one of the most devastating attacks in the game, which is unleashing a lethal poison gas on [[Mario]] and his active partner, dealing 10 damage to both of them and [[Poison (status effect)|poisoning]] them.
