|gender=Male}}'''Theodore "Ted" Nicoloff''', better known as '''Walker Boone,''' (born May 4, 1944 - January 29, 2021) was a Canadian voice actor who provided the voice work of [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|Super Mario World]]'', following after [[Lou Albano]], who previously voiced Mario in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''.

Aside from voicing Mario, Boone was also known for his role as Commander Lynch in ''{{w|Star Trek: The Next Generation}}''. Walker Boone died on January 29, 2021 at the age of 76. His death was not announced until August of that year, and his cause of death was not reported.
