==''Mario'' appearances==
===''Mario Kart'' series===
====''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe / Mario Kart Tour''====
[[Image:Nep-Enut YI Switch.jpg|thumb|left|[[Waluigi]] gliding across the chasm as a Nep-Enut can be seen in the background.]]
[[Image:Nep-Enut YI App.jpg|thumb|right|A Nep-Enut moving through the water as [[Blue Birdo]] glides the skies.]]
A single Nep-Enut appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' in the new [[Yoshi's Island (Mario Kart series)|Yoshi's Island]] course which came with the [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe – Booster Course Pass|Booster Course Pass DLC]] for the former and a free update for the latter, the Nep-Enut can be seen in the background of the course moving through the ocean waters on the outskirts of the course, it can be seen as racers glide across the chasm towards the final bend and even through the cave section, the Nep-Enut doesn’t have any impact on the race however and mostly sticks to the background.

==''Yoshi'' appearances==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
[[Image:Nep-Enut SNES.jpg|thumb|left|[[Cyan Yoshi]] taking aim at a Nep-Enut threatening him.]]
Nep-Enuts first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and later appeared in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. In these games, Nep-Enuts can be found in a few levels of [[World 3 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 3]] with their most prominent appearance being [[Nep-Enut's Domain]]. Nep-Enuts are seen lurking below the water until a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] approaches at which point, the Nep-Enut will emerge from the water blocking his path. The Nep-Enut won’t try to attack Yoshi directly but hitting it will cause damage and cost Yoshi a lot of [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]] so it’s imperative to avoid it. Nep-Enuts are very dangerous foes as if Yoshi hops into the lake it resides in and it knocks [[Baby Mario]] off his back, it can very well get Yoshi in a lock hitting him constantly thereby making it impossible for him to get out of the water to retrieve [[Baby Mario]]. Nep-Enuts are also indestructible and there is no attack that can destroy them however tossing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at a Nep-Enut will cause it to retreat beneath the surface of the water.

A particular Nep-Enut in the level [[What's Gusty Taste Like?]] lives in a secret underground area, and is located entirely underneath terrain and [[Semisolid Platform]]s, making it untouchable when it is not in its risen form. The [[Nintendo Power|Player's Guide]] specifies this one as a [[Submarine Nep-Enut]],<ref name=guide2>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 128.</ref> though the actual behavior between the two is largely similar.
{{br|left}}
====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
[[Image:Nep-Enut DS.jpg|thumb|right|[[Green Yoshi]] and [[Baby DK]] near a snarling Nep-Enut.]]
Nep-Enuts return in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', in this game; they behave very similarly to how they did in the previous game however this time, Nep-Enuts are much more rare with only one appearing in the whole game, it can be found in [[Up the Creek]] towards the end of the second section. Just as before it will emerge from the water and block [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s path and even attempt to attack if he falls in the water however the game is much more forgiving this time as the Nep-Enut can no longer get Yoshi in a lock. Yoshi once again cannot defeat a Nep-Enut however he can send it back into the water with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] toss, hitting the Nep-Enut with an egg will also unlock it in the [[Museum]], the Nep-Enut can be found in the tropical section of the Aquatic Enemy Exhibit. Strangely enough, hitting a [[Gargantua Blargg]] with an egg will also unlock the Nep-Enut in the Museum in addition to the Gargantua Blargg, likewise, hitting the Nep-Enut with an egg will unlock both it and the Gargantua Blargg in the Museum (depending on whichever comes first).

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[File:Nep-Enut in YNI.png|thumb|Yoshi approaches a Nep-Enut in ''Yoshi's New Island'']]
Nep-Enuts return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', where they prominently appear in [[Rise of the Nasty Nep-Enuts]]. Nep-Enuts are much larger and far more aggressive in this game as they chase [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] slightly faster than in previous games, Nep-Enuts will once again block Yoshi's path and try to damage him if he comes near, multiple Nep-Enuts can be found in some lakes now, just as before they are indestructible and are vulnerable to [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] however this time, Nep-Enuts take three egg tosses to send back into the water rather than one. 

===''Yoshi'' platforming series===
====''Yoshi's Woolly World'' / ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''====
[[File:WoollyNep-EnutHQ.png|thumb|left|120px|A Nep-Enut in the [[Scrapbook Theater]] in ''Yoshi's Woolly World'']]
In ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', Nep-Enuts appear only in the course, [[Frozen Solid and Chilled]]. By tossing three [[Yarn Ball]]s into them, Yoshi is able to stun the Nep-Enuts for a few moments. They spit out [[Shy Guy]]s at Yoshi. Nep-Enuts can be instantly frozen with an [[Ice Watermelon]]. They can be climbed on during their frozen state, but release themselves from the ice after a short while. However, Nep-Enuts can be defeated permanently if Yoshi [[Ground Pound|pounds]] them while they are frozen by the effects of an [[Ice Watermelon]].
{{br|right}}
====''Yoshi's Crafted World''====
[[File:Nep-enut YCW.png|thumb|120px|A Nep-enut, as it appears in ''Yoshi's Crafted World'']]
In ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'', Nep-Enuts appear only in the level [[Pirate Pier]]. They are made of bubbled slime and have the same behavior as usual. If hit with an egg, they will briefly retreat spinning below the water.

==Behind scenes==
===Etymology===
The name "Nep-Enut" is an anagram of "{{wp|Neptune (mythology)|Neptune}}", the Roman god of the sea.

==Trivia==
*Nep-Enuts are one of the few enemies in the original ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' that cannot be beaten with a [[Winged Cloud Maker]] or an [[Anywhere POW]].
*A Nep-Enut can also be seen on the boxart for both ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' despite not being a very common enemy in either game.
