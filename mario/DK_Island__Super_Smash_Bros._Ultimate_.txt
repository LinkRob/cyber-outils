'''DK Island''' is a dungeon which appears in the Light Realm of [[Adventure Mode: World of Light]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
The location is themed after ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' in general and, to some lesser extent, the [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]]. Although the location is named DK Island, after [[Donkey Kong Island]], it actually takes place within a redrawn map of [[Kongo Jungle]], the first world of ''Donkey Kong Country''. All six [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirits]] are part of the ''Donkey Kong'' franchise. After defeating each spirit, dots appear on screen and continue their way on to the next spirit, a reference to ''Donkey Kong Country''{{'}}s world map progression system.

Aside from this, there is a shop run by [[Funky Kong]] named Funky Kong's Shack, appropriately located at [[Funky's Flights]] on the map. It becomes available after acquiring the Funky Kong Master Spirit in the fifth and penultimate spirit battle, which too was accessed where Funky's Flights is located on the map.

After defeating every spirit, [[Diddy Kong]] appears at the end of the path, where he is being held as a captive fighter.
