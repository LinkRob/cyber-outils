'''Strange Forest''' is a location in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
Strange Forest takes place in an isolated forest. It has no structural hazards, but it has an intricate design. Throughout the map are several tree barriers elevated on small platforms scattered throughout the entire map, and the characters therefore have to travel around them. There are bushes on the map that the player can run through. There is a river running through the middle of the course. The river can only be crossed using either of the two bridges, one of which is to the very left of the area, and the other bridge is located closer to the middle.

Like all other Coin Hunter courses, this map has two sets of connecting Warp Pipes. The first Warp Pipe is at the bottom-left of the area, and it transports whoever is in it to the upper-left area. Similarly, the second Warp Pipe is found at the bottom-right of the area, and whoever enters it is taken to the upper-right area.

Strange Forest consists of fifteen randomly positioned [[? Panel]]s.

==In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Like [[Mario Classic 2-2]], Strange Forest is an unlockable course in Coin Hunters mode. Both can be unlocked by winning five games in Coin Hunter mode. Whoever collects the most coins within the [[time limit]] of three minutes and twenty seconds is the winner.

==Trivia==
*Strange Forest is the only Coin Hunters course that is not based on a [[level]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.
