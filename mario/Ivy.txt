'''Ivies''' are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''.

== Profile ==
Ivies debuted in the level [[Spike the Piranha (level)|Spike the Piranha]] in [[Yoshi's Crafted World]].
They are attached to [[Spike the Piranha]] but seem to think independently from him.
=== Physical description ===
Ivies are made up of a [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plant-colored]] [[Party Ball]], acorns and thorns.
=== Behavior ===
Ivies Dispense [[Spiked Ball|Spiked Balls]] and [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]], slide across the ground, emerge from the ground and generally stick upright.
== In game ==
Ivies are found in the level [[Spike the Piranha (level)|Spike the Piranha]]. In the 2nd and 5th phase of [[Spike the Piranha|Spike the Piranha’s]] boss battle, Ivies slide across the ground in a wave like formation to attack [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], in the 3rd phase, Ivies dispense [[Spiked Ball|Spiked Balls]] that [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] has to jump over and [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] that Yoshi can eat and in the 6th phase, 8 Ivies emerge from the ground to coax Yoshi into the centre which is where [[Spike the Piranha|Spike the Piranha’s]] head emerges from the ground too. Ivies can be defeated by one [[Egg Throw]].
