The '''Lab''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is located southwest of the [[Common Hall]] and directly below the [[Secret Pocket]]. The Lab is also the place where Luigi first obtains the [[Dark-Light Device]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
During [[Gear Up|A-2: Gear Up]], Luigi must head into the Lab in order to fight one of the [[Slammer]]s that has made off with a gear. After vacuuming it up, Luigi is then awarded with the gear.

During [[Visual Tricks|A-4: Visual Tricks]], Luigi must head here to get the Dark-Light Device. However, as Luigi heads toward the Lab, the door to it suddenly disappears and E. Gadd tells Luigi that he has to find another way around. Luigi eventually enters the Lab through a fireplace from the [[Parlor (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Parlor]] and locates the Dark-Light Device. However, the [[Greenie]]s aren't going to have him get it so easily and proceed to fight him while wearing shades. Luigi vacuums up the Greenies and earns the Dark-Light Device.
