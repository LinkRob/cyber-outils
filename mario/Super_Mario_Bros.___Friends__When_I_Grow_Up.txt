'''''Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up''''' is a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-themed digital coloring game for the [[MS-DOS]]. It is aimed toward younger audiences. ''Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up'' was released in 1992 in America, and was developed by Brian A. Rice, Inc. and published by Merit Software. The game doubles as edutainment as it teaches about various real-world careers.

The game features some animated sequences in it - most of which are on common careers, such as Judge, Copper, Waiter, etc. The game includes [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], various [[Toad (species)|Toad]], and [[Bowser]] (albeit in his video game/cartoon form). Some of the careers that Mario takes on, such as [[Dr. Mario (video game)|Dr. Mario]], are from other ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', whereas aliases such as [[Farmer Mario]] are exclusive to the game.

Although it is a ''Mario'' game, there are still some ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' cameos, such as [[Link]] in the "Chef/Waiter" and "Travel Guide" pages, a [[Bot]] in the "Medical Doctor / Nurse" page, and both a [[Moblin]] and [[Daira]] in the "Teacher" page.
