The '''Lakitu Info Center''', spelled '''Lakitu Info Centre''' in PAL releases, is a series of locations in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Description==
A Lakitu Info Center appears in most major areas. The party can take on any Toad rescue missions or even redo ones that they have previously completed. Many of the Toad rescue missions have alternate hard mode versions, and completing them earns the player Medals, a certain number of which gets them an exclusive [[Battle Card]].

Within each Lakitu Info Center is a [[Warp Pipe]] leading to an [[Arcade (Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam)|arcade]], where one can participate in a variety of minigames based on previous boss battles, [[Bros. Attack]]s, [[Trio Attack]]s, and [[Papercraft]] battles, and purchase [[Lakitea]].
