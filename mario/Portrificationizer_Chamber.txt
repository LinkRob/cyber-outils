The '''Portrificationizer Chamber''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Portrificationizer Chamber only contains the [[Ghost Portrificationizer]], and it was designed to contain it.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The only times that [[Luigi]] visits the Portrificationizer Chamber is after completing an area by defeating its boss. Like with [[Professor E. Gadd's Lab]], Luigi cannot freely roam around the Portrificationizer Chamber.

The final cutscene takes place in the Portrificationizer Chamber, where the Ghost Portrificationizer is used in reverse to transform [[Mario]] from a [[Mario's Painting|painting]] and back into his usual self.

==Trivia==
*In the original ''Luigi's Mansion'', there is an unused text string named "Ghost Portrificationizer Room," suggesting this was likely the intended name.
