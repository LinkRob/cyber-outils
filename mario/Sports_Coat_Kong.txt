The '''Sports Coat Kong''', also known as the '''Driver''', is a minor character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== Biography ==
The Sports Coat Kong resides in the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]]. When Princess Peach knock on the gates to the entrance, he led her, Mario and Toad, to see King [[Cranky Kong (Illumination)|Cranky Kong]] at his [[Cranky Kong's Palace|palace]], in order to stop the evil [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] from ruling the [[Mushroom Earth (Illumination)|world]] and the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]]. Accepting their request, he driver offers them a ride to see Cranky Kong. As the three heroes hop into his kart, he starts to speed through the kingdom. During his drive, he also snags a banana from a local merchant, eats it, before discarding the peel on the road, which causes another kong, Swanky Kong to slip on the banana peel and crash into a nearby hut. After reaching a ramp, Sports Coat Kong activates his glider to breeze right through the palace entrance, before Mario, Peach and Toad hop off. Sport Coat Kong then proceeds to drive off to an unknown location afterwards.
