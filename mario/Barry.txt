'''Barry''' is a [[pixl]] found in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. His name derives from "barrier" that prevents movements or access which Barry prevents the enemies or bosses' attacks from hitting the player.

== Personality ==
According to [[Tippi]], Barry is embarrassed not knowing that Mario was a hero.

==History==

Barry is an optional pixl obtainable by going into Bitland after defeating Francis. He can be found in the bush where the player had started. When the chapter is first started, he gives Mario or Peach some tips, but he/she assured him that they do not need tips. After joining the party, he can form a spiked barrier around the player, which can be used to attack or counterattack enemies with good timing.
