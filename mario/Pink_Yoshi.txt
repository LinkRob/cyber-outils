'''Pink Yoshis''' (also erroneously referred to as '''Magenta Yoshis''') are a color variety of [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] debuting in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They are known for being rather lazy and sleepy and are often times seen taking naps, despite this, they are just as adventurous as the other Yoshis.


==History==
===''Yoshi'' franchise===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
[[Image:Pink Magenta Yoshi.png|thumb|left|The "Magenta" Pink Yoshi in the original.]]
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Correct.png|thumb|right|The Pink Yoshi's correct color in the GBA remake.]]
Pink Yoshis are first seen in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. In this game, they are playable characters in the second level of each world. They retain the abilities of the other [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s being playable in [[Watch Out Below!]], [[The Baseball Boys]], [[Jungle Rythm]], [[The Cave Of The Lakitus]], [[Ride The Ski Lifts]], and [[The Cave Of The Bandits]].

In the original ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', the Pink Yoshi's coloring was a more magenta color in-game however this was fixed in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' where he is now a truer pink color.


====''Tetris Attack''====
[[File:TA4.png|thumb|left]]
Two baby Pink Yoshis make a brief appearance on the title screen in ''[[Tetris Attack]]''. Alongside [[Yoshi]] himself, a [[Blue Yoshi]], and a [[Purple Yoshi]].
{{br}}

====''Yoshi's Story''====
[[File:YI_PinkYoshi.png|left|thumb|A Pink Yoshi from ''Yoshi's Story'']]
[[File:Pink Yoshi Flutter Jump.png|right|thumb|125px|A ''Yoshi's Story'' [[Flutter Jump]]]]
In ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', a Pink Yoshi is among the [[Baby Yoshi]]s the player can choose from before starting a level. It wears yellow shoes and a red saddle in this game, a color scheme also present in most later games, excluding ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It does not differ from other playable Yoshis apart from its color and diet.

Pink Yoshi is featured on the cover art picture of the North American ''Yoshi's Story'' cartridge.

====''Yoshi Touch & Go''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Touch.png|thumb|left|The Pink Yoshi running across the plains.]]
In ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'', you can acquire a Pink Yoshi by achieving a score of at least 80, but less than 100 points in the vertical falling parts of the game. Here, the Pink Yoshi can carry a total of 30 [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] and is the third slowest Yoshi, being faster than the [[Cyan Yoshi]] but slower than the [[Blue Yoshi]].

====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Turn.png|thumb|left|The Pink Yoshi makes his turn.]]
Pink Yoshis return in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' once again retaining the same role as before, in this game, they appear in the second level of each world, these being [[Hit the M Blocks!]], [[Underground Mysteries]], [[The Goonie Coast Isn't Clear!]], [[High-Speed Cart Race]], and [[Goonie Heights]]. A Pink Yoshi is also seen during the final battle as [[Baby Peach]]'s Yoshi. The Pink Yoshi is playable during the fight if Yoshi enters the boss door with [[Baby Peach]].
{{br|left}}

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Switch.png|thumb|left|The Pink Yoshi's Turn.]]
Pink Yoshis are seen again in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. In this game, they are once again playable in the second level of each world. They once again retain their primary role from the previous games in the series. Also, upon completing a special level, the [[Green Yoshi]] will pass [[Baby Mario]] over to a Pink Yoshi rather than another Green Yoshi. Pink Yoshis are playable in the second level of each world, these being [[Chomp Rock 'n' Roll]], [[Inside the Outside]], [[Ukiki Jammin']], [[Underground Pokey Patrol]], [[Fine Feathered Friends or Foes?]], and [[Bandit Valley]].

====''Yoshi's Woolly World'' / ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Yarn.jpg|thumb|left|Yarn Pink Yoshi.]]
Pink Yoshi is back in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' as a playable character. If you pick file two, Pink Yoshi is the default playable character, alongside [[Red Yoshi]], who is always playable. The Pink Yoshi does not play differently from the other Yoshis in the game. If the game is not played on file two, Pink Yoshi can be unlocked as a playable character in two ways, the hard way is by completing [[King Bowser's Castle (Yoshi's Woolly World)|King Bowser's Castle]] and the easy way is by scanning a Pink Yarn Yoshi [[amiibo]].

====''Yoshi's Crafted World''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Craft.png|thumb|left|A Crafted Pink Yoshi.]]
Pink Yoshis return in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'' retaining the same role as in previous games, they are among one of the selectable Yoshis and can be played as in any level of the game.

===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Isle.png|thumb|right|The Pink Yoshi on Yoster Isle.]]
Several Pink Yoshis appear as non-playable characters on [[Yo'ster Isle]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Unlike the other Yoshis in this game, they have blue shoes, shells, and scales on their backs. One Pink Yoshi can store any Yoshi Cookies [[Mario]] and his allies currently have from winning Yoshi races but he can only store up to 200, any more than that, Mario will have to withdraw one.

===''Paper Mario: Color Splash''===
[[Image:Paper Pink Yoshi.png|thumb|right|The Pink Yoshi about to make his strike.]]
Pink Yoshis are seen again in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' alongside numerous other [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] encaged in the [[Emerald Circus]], unlike most other games, the Pink Yoshis are actually wild creatures who get a kick out of swallowing anyone nearby. After [[Lemmy Koopa]] disappears during his performance, all the Yoshis escape, during [[Mario]]'s battles in the Circus Tent, a Pink Yoshi may show up to swallow him, if successful, it will spit out Mario for some damage and also cause him to lose his [[Battle Card]], if Mario blocks the attack, the Pink Yoshi leaves harmlessly.

===''Mario Party'' series===

====''Mario Party''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Mini Island.png|thumb|right|A Pink Yoshi on the island.]]
A Pink Yoshi appears in ''[[Mario Party]]'', it can be seen on the [[Yoshi's Tropical Island]] sitting on a small deserted island surrounded by [[Whirlpool]]s.
{{br|left}}

====''Mario Party Superstars''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Leaf Sail.png|thumb|left|Pink Yoshi leaf sailing.]]
A Pink Yoshi appears in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'' on the returning ''[[Yoshi's Tropical Island]]'' board. Unlike last time, this time, the Pink Yoshi is seen resting on a leaf raft on the water.

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
Pink Yoshi also appears in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] as one of Yoshi's recolors. It first appeared as a member of the Yoshi Team encountered in the 1-Player mode in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'', and became a playable palette swap in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

===''Mario Kart'' series===
====''Mario Kart: Super Circuit''====
[[Image:Colored Yoshis GBA.png|thumb|left|The four colored Yoshis.]]
A Pink Yoshi appears as a playable character in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' exclusive to the multiplayer mode when playing with players that don't have ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' inserted in their [[Game Boy Advance]]. Player 2 is always a Pink Yoshi.

====''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''====
[[File:Pink Yoshi Cartoon.png|thumb|Art of a Pink Yoshi from ''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''.]]
A Pink Yoshi, alongside various other Yoshis, cameos in the first lap of [[Yoshi Park 1]] in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', where pictures of them pop out of egg props.

====''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Racing.jpg|thumb|right|The Pink Yoshi Racing.]]
A Pink Yoshi appears as a playable character in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' as of the November 2014 update. The Pink Yoshi (alongside several other colored Yoshis) can be unlocked by purchasing both DLC Packs. The Pink Yoshi can be selected through a dropdown menu which comes up when selecting Yoshi. In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', the Pink Yoshi and all the other colored Yoshis are unlocked by default and don't need to be purchased. The Pink Yoshi is player-exclusive meaning CPU Pink Yoshis never race.

====''Mario Kart Tour''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Tour.png|thumb|right|Pink Yoshi's unlock screen.]]
Pink Yoshi appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' as an unlockable racer, in this game, he can be unlocked by purchasing him from the shop for [[Coin]]s or as a mystery prize from the [[Pipe]]s. His Special Item is the [[Heart]].
{{br}}

===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Sunshine''====
[[Image:Pink Yoshi Sunshine.jpg|thumb|right|The Pink Yoshi on the roof with Mario.]]
A Pink Yoshi appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. In this game, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] will hatch as a Pink Yoshi upon eating a [[Coconut]] or [[Banana]]. A Yoshi can also become a Pink Yoshi if it eats the aforementioned fruits (this tactic is required for beating the "Yoshi's Fruit Adventure" mission in [[Ricco Harbor]]). Whenever a Pink Yoshi spews juice at an enemy, it turns into a pink platform which moves vertically upward, this can be useful for reaching platforms or ledges that are too high up otherwise.

====''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''====
[[Image:Yoshi Plains Ride.png|thumb|right|Pink Yoshi amongst several other Yoshis.]]
Pink Yoshi makes an appearance in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' as one of the rideable Yoshis alongside the [[Green Yoshi]], [[Yellow Yoshi]], and the [[Cyan Yoshi]]. He retains the same abilities as the other Yoshis, being able to swallow enemies, stomp on spiked enemies harmlessly, and he can also swallow [[Berry|Berries]], swallowing five will award the plumbers with either a power-up or a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The Pink Yoshi is [[Blue Toad]]'s signature Yoshi, [[New Super Mario Bros. Wii/Beta elements|originally]], a [[Red Yoshi]] was going to appear in the Pink Yoshi's place. The Pink Yoshi is always the fourth Yoshi color to hatch.
{{br}}

===''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''===
[[Image:Yoshi's Island Past.jpg|thumb|right|The Pink Yoshi along with the rest of the Yoshis giving [[Baby Bowser]] [[Yoshi Cookie]]s as they think he saved them.]]
Pink Yoshis are seen again in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. In this game, they are seen in [[Yoshi's Island]], and they (along with the rest of the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]) are eaten by the colossal [[Shroobified]] Yoshi, [[Yoob]]. When [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]] end up inside [[Yoob]], they find one of the Pink Yoshis stranded on a platform above some spikes so they create a bridge for him to escape and he helps by helping push the [[Chomp Rock]] down Yoob's belly to open the path forward, another Pink Yoshi can be seen trapped inside a [[Yoob Egg]] which the baby plumbers must break open with their [[Pump Mario|water-spitting powers]]. Finally, another Pink Yoshi is seen during the battle with [[Sunnycide]] as the final Yoshi that needs to be freed to knock the [[Chomp Rock]] over his head to reveal his weak point. After [[Sunnycide]]'s defeat, the plumbers, and all the Yoshis including the Pink Yoshis escape [[Yoob]]'s belly.

==Trivia==
*Pink Yoshi is featured on the cover art picture of the North American ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'' cartridge.
*On the world map of ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', a [[Red Yoshi]] is erroneously in the Pink Yoshi's place while the Pink Yoshi is in the [[Red Yoshi]]'s place on the seventh level. This error was fixed in the GBA remake ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' and both Yoshis are in their correct placements on the world map.
*In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', if the [[Yoshi Kid]] takes 15-18 minutes to hatch, it hatches as a Pink Yoshi Kid. The Pink Yoshi Kid has green hair, and red polka dotted pants.
*A Pink Yoshi, alongside various other Yoshis, cameos in the first lap of [[Yoshi Park 1]] in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', where pictures of them pop out of egg props.
