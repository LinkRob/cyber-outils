}}'''Chuckoroks''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They appear in the Chuckorok Bounce minigame of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the [[Winkle]] area's [[Winkle Colosseum]]

Chuckoroks bear a resemblance to [[w:c:zelda:Octorok|Octorok]]s from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' series. Their main attack is firing a laser. During the minigame, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have to bounce the balls back at them.
