'''Wreck-It Ralph''' is a 2012 computer-animated comedy film created by [[wikipedia:Disney|Disney]] and [[wikipedia:Walt Disney Animation Studios|Walt Disney Animation Studios]]. The film contains cameo background appearances of several video game characters, this includes the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES controller]], [[Bowser]], and the [[Super Mushroom]] item.

== Plot ==
The film focuses on Wreck-It Ralph, the arcade villain of the platform game ''Fix-It Felix, Jr.'', is frustrated and he doesn't want to be a bad guy anymore much to everyone's negative attitude. Ralph would go on a quest to find a medal to prove he is not a bad guy.

== Mario-related elements ==

* [[Bowser]] appears as a member of the Bad-Anon villains support group. When Wreck-It Ralph expresses interest to no longer be a bad guy anymore, Bowser reacts in shock by breathing fireballs. Bowser also appears at the end of the film clapping at Ralph for his heroism.
* The [[Super Mushroom]] appears in Tapper's lost-and-found box when Ralph was searching for a medal to prove he wasn’t a bad guy.
* The [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES controller]] appears as the vault to Sugar Rush's CPU room, where King Candy makes a code to open the vault.

==Trivia== 
*[[Mario]] along with [[Luigi]] were going to appear in the first movie, but never made it as writers stated they couldn't think of a suitable way to incorporate them.<ref>https://www.wired.com/2012/10/interview-with-rich-moore-and-clark-spencer-the-director-and-producer-of-wreck-it-ralph/%3C/ref%3E</ref> There was also plans for [[Piranha Plant]] initially set to make appearance in the film, but were eventually cut. Instead, they brought Bowser.
**Ralph’s voice actor, John C. Reilly joked that the Mario Bros. do not appear because [[Nintendo]] stated a high fee to legally add them to the movie.
**Mario was also originally going to appear in the sequel ''Ralph Breaks the Internet'', but never got included again.<ref>https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/ralph-breaks-the-internet-mario-cameo/</ref><ref>https://youtu.be/j3R1WNEopvA?si=pQa7wBohgeJq8SLc</ref> However a [[? Block|? Blocks]] is shown in the movie.
*Despite Mario not appear in ''Wreck-It Ralph'', he is mentioned by Felix during the party.
