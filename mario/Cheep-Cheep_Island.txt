'''Cheep-Cheep Island''' is the third course in [[Lightning Cup]] of ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. This course is beach/tropical-themed, being named after [[Cheep-Cheep]]s, much like [[Cheep Cheep Beach]] and [[Cheep Cheep Lagoon]]. The course holds a series of islands, connected by boardwalks and wooden bridges, with crabs scattered along the beach roaming around, along with the Cheep Cheeps themselves jumping out of the water in the background.
