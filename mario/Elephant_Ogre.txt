'''Elephant Ogre''' is a character featured in [[Kat]] and [[Ana]]'s story in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. Here, he attacks the [[Diamond Dojo]] during the nighttime. Elephant Ogre is a massive red-skin beast who carries a black spiked club with him. He has his own child named [[Li'l Ogre]].
