'''Popple's Partner, Part 2''' is the eleventh level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 25.

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains 2 [[Parabeanie]]s, 5 [[Beanerang Bro]]s, and 1 [[Elite Troopea]]. The enemy captain is the Elite Troopes.

The 2nd battle contains 2 Elite Troopeasm 5 Beanerang Bros, and 1 Parabeanie. The enemy captain is the Parabeanie, and he knows [[Deny]].

The final battle contains 1 Elite Troopea, 2 Parabeanies, 3 Beanerang Bros, [[Birdo]], and [[Popple]]. The enemy captain is Popple, and he knows Deny and [[Rally]].
