'''Muffy''' (known as '''Tina''' in the PAL version) is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Biography==
Muffy lives in [[Flipside]] on the first floor, where she always tries [[Saffron]]'s recipes. She also has a sweet tooth often tasting desserts and making them as well.

==Trivia==
*Her [[Flopside]] counterpart is [[Buffy]].
