'''Cat biddybluds''' are a variant of [[Biddybud|biddyblud]] which appear in the [[Bowser's Fury|''Bowser's Fury'']] mode of [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|''Super Mario 3D World'' + ''Bowser's Fury'']]. They resemble biddybluds, but have fur, ears, and a tail, which is white-tipped at the end.
