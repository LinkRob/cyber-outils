'''DK Dash Attack''' is a move used by [[Baby Donkey Kong|Baby DK]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Description==
DK Dash Attack involves Baby DK performing a shoulder charge, just as [[Wario]] can do with his [[Dash Attack]] in the {{series|Wario Land}}. The player can have Baby DK execute the move by pressing down on the {{button|ds|Pad}} and {{button|ds|Y}}. DK Dash Attack is capable of defeating nearly every enemy, and it can also break open a [[crate]]. In instances when there is a ceiling straight above the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] and Baby DK, the latter has to use DK Dash Attack because they cannot [[jump]] across.
