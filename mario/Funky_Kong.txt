'''Funky Kong''' is one of the [[Kong]]s featured in the [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]]. He first appeared in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''. In the first two ''Donkey Kong Country'' games, Funky Kong allowed players to travel to any world they had previously visited. In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'', Funky runs a boat rental service that has to be utilized in order to advance in the game. In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'', Funky Kong ran an armory shop that allowed the Kongs to purchase weapons and upgrades from. Ever the jack-of-all-trades, this has made Funky an entertainer, mechanic, surfer, and salesman.

==Appearances==
===''Donkey Kong 64''===
Funky Kong reappears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' as a major ally to the Kongs. Unlike any of his previous appearances in the ''Donkey Kong Country'' series, Funky Kong in this game runs an armory known as [[Funky's Armory]] and has more of a military-like appearance instead of his usual "surfer dude" or mechanic gimmicks. Funky Kong sells the Kongs their various of weapons and ammo that is required in the game. He can also refill their ammos anytime if they are low free of charge. Funky also offers several upgrades, namely an increased ammo belt, homing ammo, and even a sniper mode that is required to hit targets from a fair distance away. During the end moments of the game after K. Rool has been defeated by the Kongs, Funky was the one to deliver the finishing blow onto K. Rool while the latter was distracted by [[Candy Kong]] by launching a giant boot onto K. Rool. During the end credits of the game, Funky can be seen dancing on a high ledge.

===''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''===
This is the first appearance of Funky Kong in a ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' game, let alone the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] in general. Funky Kong is a large type character and must be unlocked by unlocking four Expert Staff Ghost data in [[Time Trial]]s. He has a speed bonus of 4, which makes him a very popular character to use in time trials with fast heavy bikes. He is considered as the best character to pick among fans in combination with the [[Flame Runner]] as he has the greatest speed stat when in a wheelie.

Funky has his own [[Battle Stage]] named [[Funky Stadium]]. The big barrel monitor above in the center of the stadium shows his icon.

=== ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'' ===
Funky Kong is an unlockable playable team player in ''Mario Super Sluggers'', where he has good batting and decent fielding skills, while lacking in pitching and running skills. He is the only Kong in the game who bats with his right hand, while fielding with his left hand. He has good chemistry with all the Kongs and bad chemistry with the Kremlings. He is unlocked after he joins the player in Challenge Mode after saving him from a [[Fire Bro]]. He then joins the others to take down [[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] After Donkey Kong's team is complete Funky and the rest of the DK wilds can be faced in a baseball game in DK jungle. While not appearing in the ending Funky and the other Kongs presuminly take part in the celebration [[Princess Peach]] throws for Baseball island.

===''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]] (+Funky mode)''===
In the first game, without Funky mode, Funky Kong owned his own shop named [[Funky's Fly 'n' Buy]], where he sold useful items to the Kongs, such as balloons.

In the [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze (Nintendo Switch)|version]], Funky Kong is the star of [[Funky Mode]], in which is an easy pick good for beginners or players who want to relax while playing, as he is the most versatile and easiest choice to play the game, as he can double jump, hover, and has five hearts. Along with him he carries his trusty surfboard.

=== ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
Funky Kong returns in ''Mario Kart Tour'' as a High-End driver, being made available on May 20, 2020. He was part of the first week's spotlight of the [[Jungle Tour]], alongside the Bolt Buggy and the Banana Wingtip. Funky also returned as part of the 2nd week spotlight of the 2020 Los Angeles Tour. Starting with the 2021 Cat Tour, he is now a regular High-End driver, and starting with the [[Bowser vs. DK Tour]], he has his own self-named cup. Funky Kong's special item is the [[Banana Barrels]], which is shared with Diddy Kong.

=== ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ===
Funky Kong is set to be added to ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' in Wave 6 of the [[Booster Course Pass]], returning from ''Mario Kart Tour''. Funky was added on November 9 2023
