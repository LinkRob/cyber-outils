'''Balloon Bang''' is a multiplayer minigame hosted by [[Dr. Crygor]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
One player plays [[microgame]]s from ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' while the others each use a pump to inflate a red balloon on top of a television screen reading "WARIO." One player has to complete at least one microgame before the next character can have a turn. Whoever is playing a microgame when the balloon pops loses and is sent off to the moon.

==Trivia==
*In the ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!'' instruction booklet, one of Dr. Crygor's pieces of advice is for a player to scream "BLAMMO" at the top of their lungs while timing the balloon's inflation to scare other players. Dr. Crygor goes on to say that the method proves successful in lab experiments.
*According to Dr. Crygor, Balloon Bang was created based on the theory of balloon entropy.
