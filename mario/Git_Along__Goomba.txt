'''Git Along, Goomba''', titled '''Goomba Rodeo''' in the British English version, is a General [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place on top of a barrel, on which several [[Goomba]]s are standing, in a desert.

==Opening==
Players use their lassos to catch one Goomba each. Many Goombas appear from the manholes on the platform.

==Gameplay==
The player must spin their lasso by drawing circles on the bottom screen using their [[Stylus]]. They will then throw it to the platform in the middle of the top screen, capturing any Goombas within the circle. The longer the player spins, the bigger the lasso's range will be. More Goombas frequently emerge from the manholes. The competitor with most Goombas when time runs out is the winner.

==Flavor text==
===Controls===
*'''American English:''' Stylus &ndash; Draw circles to widen the lasso, and then slide to throw
*'''British English:''' Stylus &ndash; Draw circles and slide to throw

*'''Rules''' &ndash; '''American English''' ''"Use your lasso to catch as many Goombas as possible."''
*'''Rules''' &ndash; '''British English''' ''"Throw your lasso to catch more Goombas than your opponents! Draw circles on the Touch Screen to widen your lasso and slide the stylus in the direction you want to throw it."''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The American English name is a pun on the stereotypical wild western phrase "Git along, little dogies."
