'''Deep Jungle Drift''' is a [[level]] of [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Layout==
The level starts on a raft surrounded by [[Poison Water]] in a jungle-like area. After avoiding some [[Stingby|Stingbies]] and moving platforms, the player is able to reach the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. This is followed by some more Stingbies as well as platforms with [[Piranha Creeper]]s on them. A downwards current brings the player to a [[Warp Box]], which transports the player to a platform with the [[Goal Pole]].

==Green Stars==
*'''[[Green Power Star|Green Star]] 1''': Before the Checkpoint Flag, go through one of the [[Clear Pipe]]s to get the Star.
*'''Green Star 2''': The player's character, in their [[Cat Mario|Cat]] form, must climb up the roof after the Checkpoint Flag. There, they must activate a [[Green Star Ring]] and collect all eight [[Green Coin]]s for the Green Star to appear.
*'''Green Star 3''': The player's character must climb up a wall next to the Warp Box to find the Green Star on their way up.

==Stamp==
