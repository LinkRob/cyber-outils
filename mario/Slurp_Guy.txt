|comics=}}'''Slurp Guys''' are a type of [[Shy Guy]] that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. A Slurp Guy can either have a red, blue, or yellow color.

== Description ==
Their appearance is that of a normal [[Shy Guy]], the difference being that they can also suck out [[Mario]]'s paint with their plastic straws. The color of the Slurp Guy determines which color paint they will steal from you, and upon doing so will heal them to full HP.

== Stats ==
'''HP:''' 14

'''Attacks:'''
* Attack ('''5''')
* Group Attack ('''5''')
* Blowback Attack ('''12''')
* Straw Attack (heals itself while reducing the correlating paint color of Mario)
* Straw Attack [''when Mario has no paint of that correlating color'']
* Last Straw Attack (heals itself while reducing the correlating paint color of Mario)

== Red Slurp Guy ==
[[File:PMCS_Red_Slurp_Guy_Card.png|left|frameless|90x90px]]

=== Price ===
Sell Price: 3 coins

=== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ===
The Red Slurp Guy is first seen in [[Port Prisma]], but can also be found in [[Ruddy Road]], [[Indigo Underground]], [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Toad Trainworks]], and [[Tangerino Grill]]. The ''best location'' to find this card is [[Sunglow Ridge]], as this level contains 4 Red Slurp Guys in both the playthrough and the post game.

== Blue Slurp Guy ==
[[File:PMCS Blue Slurp Guy Card.png|left|frameless|90x90px]]

=== Price ===
Sell Price: 3 coins

=== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ===
The Blue Slurp Guy is first seen in [[Cherry Lake]], but can also be found in [[Fort Cobalt]] and [[Mossrock Theater]]. There is no ''best location'', as each level contains two Blue Slurp Guys in post game.

== Yellow Slurp Guy ==
[[File:PMCS Yellow Slurp Guy Card.png|left|frameless|90x90px]]

=== Price ===
Sell Price: 3 coins

=== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ===
The Yellow Slurp Guy is first seen in [[Cherry Lake]], but can also be found in [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Toad Trainworks]], and [[Mossrock Theater]]. The ''best locations'' to find this card is [[Cherry Lake]], [[The Crimson Tower]], and [[Fort Cobalt]], as these levels contain 2 Yellow Slurp Guys in post game.
