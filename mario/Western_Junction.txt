'''Western Junction '''is a court which appears in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''. It can be used to play Basketball, Hockey, Volleyball, and Dodgeball, with the latter having to be unlocked first.

== Design ==
The court takes place in a desert near a train station being run by [[Shy Guy]]s. There are many features in this court, including the constantly rotating turntables that can make players run faster or slower, depending on the direction they are running. The other being the train that occasionally passes through the court and knocks players out of the way. Shy Guys on the train will throw either Bananas or coins at the players, either harming them or helping them.

This court also appears in the [[Bob-omb Dodge]] party game.
