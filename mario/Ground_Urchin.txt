}}A '''Ground Urchin''' is a variant of Urchin that appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They look like normal Urchins except that they are red.
==History==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
They appear in [[Freezeflame Galaxy]]. They stay in one spot, and will chase Mario when they notice him. They can be defeated with a fireball.
===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
They reappear in this game behaving exactly like in the previous game. They only appear in [[Battle Belt Galaxy]].
