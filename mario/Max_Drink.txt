A '''Max Drink''' is an item in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Description==
Max Drinks are a type of energy drink that fully maxe out [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s dance meter when starting a dance.

==History==
In Story Mode, Max Drinks can be bought at any shop run by a [[Lakitu]] for 50 [[coin]]s. Up to three Max Drinks can be carried at a time.
