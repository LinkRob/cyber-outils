'''Rail-Yard Run''' is the first (and only) level of [[Sunshine Station]] in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''.

==Layout==
The [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] unravels the rolled path before starting the level. The player will come pass the three [[coin]]s. There are three coins above the red and green [[Shy Guy]]s. The train comes out of the tunnel.
