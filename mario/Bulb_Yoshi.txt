'''Bulb Yoshi''' is one of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s power ups in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. To access this form, Yoshi has to eat a [[Bulb Berry]]. Upon doing so, Yoshi illuminates yellow, and [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can see and access passages that are otherwise invisible and intangible.

Unfortunately, unlike with the baby variant, this power up is only temporary. It also gradually runs out, with the light becoming smaller and dimmer over time. In order to prolong the effects, Yoshi will have to continue to consume Bulb Berries.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', there are Baby Bulb Yoshi which functions nearly identically. They are [[Yellow Yoshi|Yellow Yoshis]] that glow, the only difference being Baby Bulb Yoshis do not show invisible platforms.
