The '''Spear''', also known as the '''Torpedo''' outside North America, is a heavy bike in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== Description ==
The Spear resembles a {{w|Moto Guzzi V8}} with exhaust pipes sticking out. The emblem is located on the front and sides of the bike.

The Spear is unlocked by unlocking 12 Expert Staff Ghosts in Time Trials, winning 200 WFC races (now impossible) or by playing 3,600 races.

[[Dry Bowser]] and [[King Boo]] use the Spear in a [[Time Trial]] course for the Expert Staff Ghost for [[Grumble Volcano]] and {{classic course|SNES|[[Ghost Valley 2]]}} by Nin★aki and Nin★sira■ respectively.

Its [[kart]] counterpart is the [[Jetsetter]].

== Statistics ==
The Spear is the [[speed|fastest]] bike in the game and has a high [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]] stat tied with the [[Jetsetter]] and is the second heaviest bike in the game, but it also has a low [[acceleration]] stat tied with the [[Wild Wing]] and a low [[drift]] stat. Additionally, the Spear has the worst [[handling]], [[Off-Road|off-road]] and [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] stats among all bikes in the game. Its speed will increase if [[Funky Kong]] is used in it. If [[Waluigi]] is used in the Jetsetter, its drift stat will exceed that of the [[Daytripper]] and [[Magikruiser]].
