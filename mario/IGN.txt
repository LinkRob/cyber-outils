'''Imagine Games Network''', more commonly abbreviated to just '''IGN''', is an online multimedia news and reviews website for games and movies. It is notorious for having enormous FAQs and guides for games.

The website<ref name="IGN">http://www.ign.com</ref> comprises a plethora of channels — each to its own subdomain — each covering separate areas of entertainment. Its game related channels feature those for the PC, the [[Nintendo DS]] and [[Nintendo DSi]], the Xbox, the PlayStations, and many more; and also has channels on movies, music, gear and technology, sports, comic books, television, and even more.
