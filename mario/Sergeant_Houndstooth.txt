}}'''Seargeant Houndstooth''' is an enemy found in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]] in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. 

==History==
He is a security guard that usually appears along with [[Dave]]. When a [[Nigel B. Whistlebottom]] blows a whistle. He will enter the room attack by either commanding Dave to attack [[Wario]] or by rolling into him. Like with all of the guard-related enemies, Sergeant Houndstooth cannot be defeated but any attack from Wario can stun him for awhile.
