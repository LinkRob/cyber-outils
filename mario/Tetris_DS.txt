'''''Tetris DS''''' is a Touch! Generations puzzle game developed and published by [[Nintendo]] for the [[Nintendo DS]]. The aesthetic and themes are largely based on [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games, many of which are ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, and the cover art depicts graphics from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Modes==
===[[Marathon Mode]]===
A 20-level mode with each level being themed on a specific game. The first ten levels are themed after either ''Super Mario Bros.'' or ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

===Mission Mode===
Has a backdrop based on ''The Legend of Zelda''.

===Push Mode===
Has a backdrop based on ''Donkey Kong''.

===Touch Mode===
Has a backdrop based on ''Balloon Fight''.

===[[Catch Mode]]===
A level based on ''Metroid''.

===Puzzle Mode===
A level based on ''Yoshi's Cookie''.
