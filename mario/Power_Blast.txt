A '''Power Blast''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Drinking a Power Blast increases the entire party's attack power by 50%. This item is very useful for tough boss fights. Power Blasts can be purchased at the [[Tadpole Pond]] shop for 5 [[Frog Coin]]s.
