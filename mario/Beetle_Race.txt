The '''Beetle Race''' is a [[minigame]] that takes place on [[Booster Hill]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. [[Mario]] is required to have the [[Beetle Box]] to play the minigame, as he needs to use it to catch [[beetle]]s. Playing Beetle Race costs 100 [[coin]]s the first time but 50 coins in subsequent plays.
