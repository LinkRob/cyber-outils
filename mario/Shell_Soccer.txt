'''Shell Soccer''' is an Extra minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. It returns in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'' as a Sports & Puzzles minigame.

==Gameplay==
The four players are each divided into a team of two. The minigame involves them having to kick a [[Koopa Shell]] into the opposing team's set of seven [[Goomba]]s. Whichever team is first to knock out all of their rivals' Goombas wins the mode.
