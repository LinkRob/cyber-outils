An '''Anglefish''' is an enemy appearing in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Anglefishes have teal, triangular bodies with white wings and red feet on the back of their body and red-lidded eyes near the front of their nose.

===Behavior===
An Anglefish attacks by jumping out of the water, trying to spear the player. They can be defeated when stomped on.

==In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''==
Anglefishes live in water pools, and attack prey by jumping out of the water. They are introduced in the level [[The Anglefish Trial: Ready, Aim, Fly!]]

==Trivia==
===Etymology===
The name "Anglefish" comes from "angler fish" (or perhaps "angelfish") and "angle."{{SMBWNav}}
