'''Lucky Bros.''' is a [[badge]] item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In both versions, it is an item dropped by [[Trunkle]], and equipping it grants the [[Lucky Attack]] special effect.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', Lucky Bros. increases [[Power|POW]] rating by 26 (24 in the Japanese version). In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', it instead increases POW rating by 36 as well as [[Heart Point|HP]] by 15, and in the same version, Lucky Bros. can be sold for three [[coin]]s.
