'''{{world|flower|cannonn2}}''' is the Warp Cannon level of [[World Flower (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Flower]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Clearing this stage allows the player to access [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]]. 

==Layout==
The level begins with [[Mario]] beside the Warp Cannon in the generic world. Entering the Warp Cannon begins a countdown, and then Mario is blasted into the course. In this level, Mario needs to navigate through [[Parabomb|Para-bombs]] and rotating and stationary electric [[Amp]]s, while avoiding falling into the large abyss. A single [[Red Ring]] can be found halfway through the course. After this, the level ends.
