{{Nihongo|'''Snow Whirled'''|まわってスノーボード|Mawatte sunōbōdo|Snowboarding Around}} is a 4-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' that later returned in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' ==

=== Opening ===
The camera pans from the finish line to the players who are on a platform standing on their snowboards.

=== Gameplay ===
The player's aim is to rotate as many times as possible before they land to the ground. The player must press the buttons in order to rotate.

=== Ending ===
The player with the highest score pose their victory while the players sulk for their loss.

=== Text ===
*'''Rules''': ''Press the buttons in order to spin your snowboard in midair. Whoever pulls off the biggest spin wins!''
*'''Advice''': ''Press the buttons in order, but don't rush it!''

=== Controls ===
*{{button|gcn|A}} {{button|gcn|B}} {{button|gcn|Y}} {{button|gcn|X}} - Press in order

== In ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' ==
While there are no mechanical changes to the minigame. The players now wave at the screen after they finish their turn.

=== Text ===
*'''Description''': ''Out-spin everyone else on your snowboard!''
*'''On-screen''': ''Do midair spins on your snowboard!''

=== Controls ===
* {{button|3ds|A}}/{{button|3ds|B}}/{{button|3ds|Y}}/{{button|3ds|X}} - Press in sequence to rotate
