'''''Mario Sports Superstars''''' is a [[Nintendo 3DS]] sports game released in 2017. It is the second ''[[Mario Sports (series)|Mario Sports]]'' game, following after ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'' for the [[Wii]]. ''Mario Sports Superstars'' features five different sports, including soccer, tennis, golf, baseball, and horse racing. The game is developed by [[Bandai Namco Games]] and [[Camelot]] (Golf and Tennis).

==Sports==
*Tennis
*Golf
*Baseball
*Horse Racing
*Soccer

===Supporting characters===
Supporting characters are represented by different icon outlines than the main characters. Boom Boom and Pom Pom have hexagonal outline while the rest are represented by regular dots. In baseball, generic characters have a green circle behind their icons. Supporting characters are playable in sidekick roles in sports such as soccer.
