The '''Birthday Girl Rosalina''' is a kart which appeared in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', and appears as a separate vehicle in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:RosalinaKart7.png|thumb|200px|right|Rosalina next to her Birthday in ''Mario Kart 7''.]]
The Birthday Girl Rosalina is based of Rosalina's color scheme for the [[Birthday Girl]], having a cyan and azure blue color scheme, as opposed to the default pink and red color scheme. In ''Mario Kart Tour'', it is equipped with the Birthday Girl's tires.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' ==
When Rosalina is unlocked by winning the [[Star Cup]] on [[Engine Class|150cc]], only she can use the cyan and azure blue Birthday Girl. This kart is a little heavier and is faster than [[Princess Peach|Peach]] and [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]'s Birthday Girl karts, due to Rosalina being classified as a Cruiser-weighr driver. This kart and its color scheme is used for the Normal Staff Ghost for [[Music Park]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
This kart debuted in the [[London Tour]] in 2019. Now, this color scheme is no longer restricted to Rosalina, and any other character can use it. Whenever the kart is used, it can increase the duration of [[Drafting|Slipstream]] boosts and the points earned for getting them.
