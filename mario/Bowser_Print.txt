'''Bowser Print''' is a [[Gaddget]] of [[Bowser]]'s handprint in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is received from Bowser by clearing the quest [[Final Showdown]]. Bowser egoistically boasts that it is a "one of a kind."

==Description==
Bowser Print only consists of a red handprint of Bowser's on a golden border frame. Unlike several other Gaddgets, there are no controls.
