'''''Yoshi''''' is a video game franchise which is a spinoff of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. It usually revolves around [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] or even more [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]. It began with the puzzle game ''[[Yoshi (video game)|Yoshi]]'' game, shortly after Yoshi's debut in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

== Gameplay ==
Most ''Yoshi'' games are platformers, and the main goal is to reach the goal ring at the end. There is a specific series titled ''[[Yoshi's Island (series)|Yoshi's Island]]'', and its games usually revolve around this while carrying around [[Baby Mario]] on the Yoshis' backs. Yoshi can also be played independently as shown in [[Yoshi's Story|''Yoshi's Story'']].
