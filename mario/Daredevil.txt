'''Daredevil''' is one of [[Jimmy T]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player has to control a character to run under an iron bar and cross to the other side without getting crushed. With each difficulty setting, the size of the iron bar is increased, to the point of covering most of the screen in level 3.
