'''''Excitebike: Bun Bun Mario Battle''''' was a game available for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super Famicom]]'s [[Satellaview]] accessory. It is a variation of ''[[w:c:nintendo:Excitebike|Excitebike]]'', albeit with ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' characters. The game was released in four different episodes, each during a specific week in 1997.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Excitebike Mario Battle gameplay.png|thumb|left|200px|Gameplay screenshot of the characters racing one another in ''Excitebike: Bun Bun Mario Battle'']]
The concept of the game was unchanged except for a "SUPER" mode where the player has unlimited turbo, as well as the addition of coins. The coins are spread out on the courses and increase top speed.

==Characters==
In ''Excitebike: Bun Bun Mario Battle'', the playable characters were gradually added over the four weeks. They consisted of [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Toad (character)|Toad]], [[Wario]], [[Princess Peach]], and [[Yoshi]]. The former three were added in episode 1, Wario was added in episode 2, Princess Peach was added in episode 3, and Yoshi was added in episode 4, although Luigi was removed.
