'''World 5''' is the fifth world in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. It is a jungle-themed world with lakes of purple toxic water, and its boss is [[Iggy Koopa]]. This [[Warp Cannon]] leads to [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8.]] It is recommended you get all [[Star Coin|Star Coins]]. The background music of World 5 was remixed for ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]'s'' location of the same name.
