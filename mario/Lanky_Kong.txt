'''Lanky Kong''' is an [[Kong]] character with huge arms where he can walk with them, cross slippery ramps, hit his enemies and press buttons from a greater distance. He is the buffoon of [[Kong Family]] in [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]].

His funny way as a clown, he is liked by everyone as [[Chunky Kong]] & [[Kiddy Kong]]. He really likes to mess with the [[Kremling]]s in which they are victims of his antics and even [[King K. Rool]] is humiliated by him.
==Characteristics==
Lanky is a Kong orangutan with pale pink skin, orange fur, blue eyes and wears a red clown nose. His outfit is a short white shirt, blue pants with plaid patch (red & white), blue suspenders and yellow buttons.

==History==
===Background information===
Lanky was born in an island belonging to [[DK Isles]] called Utan. Tired of the boring ambience from his home island, he decided to move to the [[Donkey Kong Island|main island]] of the archipelago and start his career as a clown.{{Citation needed}}

===''Donkey Kong'' franchise===
====Canon====
===== ''Donkey Kong 64'' =====
In ''Donkey Kong 64'', Lanky was the second Kong to be imprisoned by the [[Kremling Krew]] until he was saved by [[Donkey Kong]] in [[Angry Aztec]] when he activated the coconut [[Target Switch]] opening his captivity. All of his color-coded items are blue, he walk with hands by OrangStand, he gets the Grape Shooter, his instrument is a trombone, his pad is Baboon Balloon and his barrel power is OrangSprinty. Lanky battle with [[Puftoss]] and [[King Kut Out]]. In the round 3, he defeats K. Rool (with a spotlight on head) playing his trombone to chase him and slip on banana peel several times.

===== Donkey Kong Bananza =====
In ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'' there is a company called "Lanky: No Style. No Grace. Funny Face.", which is a reference to Lanky's verse of the [[DK Rap]]. 

He later received a statue on [[DK Island]], obtainable by collecting 1,000,000 emeralds in Emerald Rush in the So Strong It's Funny event.  

====Spin-off====
===== Donkey Kong Barrel Blast =====
In ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]'', Lanky Kong is fourth playable character from selection in which his '''Boost''' is 2 out of 5, '''Speed''' is 4 out of 5 and '''Agility''' is 1 out of 5. His counterpart is the [[Klump]].

*<u>'''Nintendo Player's Guide'''</u> (page 5): ''This knuckle-dragging Kong looks like a clown, and it's more than just a coincidence. Think of him as a twisted twig on a distant branch of the family tree. Kremlings and Klaptraps may snicker at his goofy gait as he ambles in their direction, but there's nothing funny about a hyperextended sucker punch.''
*<u>'''Nintendo Player's Guide'''</u> (page 118): ''Long-limbed Lanky Kong has a distinct advantage in close- and mid-range encounters with his spin attack. In the Battle Arena, or on platforms in Monkey Smash, that can give you a big advantage. Lanky has some good jump attacks, as well.''
*<u>'''Prima Official Game Guide'''</u> (page 11): ''This Kong is rubbery and goofy. Those long arms may seem clumsy, but Lanky can use them well. Just like his relatives, the orangutan has moves that let him get to places others can't reach. He's not the fastest of the crew, and he can't jump the highest, but Lanky's got all the skills he need to help take on K. Rool.''
*<u>'''German website'''</u>: ''To the satisfaction of the other Kongs, Lanky has been at home on the DK islands for some time now. Originally he comes from the more distant Utan island, where relatives of the Kongs live.<br>The society there was much too boring and conservative for Lanky, however, so that he was attracted to the slightly chaotic members of the Kong clan.<br>Here he feels much more comfortable, since his constant silliness is always appreciated, especially by Chunky and Kiddy.
