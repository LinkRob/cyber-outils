The '''Meat Pasta Dish''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. This recipe takes the appearance of [[Spaghetti]] topped with meat sauce. It is made by mixing a [[Fresh Pasta Bunch]] with a [[Power Steak]]. This heavenly dish restores 18 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
