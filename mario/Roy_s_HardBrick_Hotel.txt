'''Roy's HardBrick Hotel''' is a location in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''.

==Description==
Roy's HardBrick Hotel is one of seven [[Koopa hotel]]s, and it is owned by [[Roy Koopa]], as its name would suggest. The name is a pun on the {{w|Heartbreak Hotel}}, a fictional hotel mentioned in a song by [[Elvis Presley]]. As its name suggests, Roy's HardBrick Hotel is built mainly out of bricks.

The enemies in Roy's HardBrick Hotel include [[Ninji|Mini Ninjas]], [[Parabomb]]s, [[Koopa Troopa|Koopas]] and [[Goomba]]s. It has nine regular stages and a boss stage named [[Roy's Rowdy Rooftop]].

==In ''Hotel Mario''==
[[File:Toasters Hotel Mario.png|thumb|left|200px|''Hotel Mario'' screenshot of Mario discovering that the flickering lights are a result of too many toasters plugged in]]
Roy's HardBrick Hotel is the second hotel visited in ''Hotel Mario''. After [[Morton Koopa Jr.]] is defeated in his [[Morton's Wood Door Hysteria Hotel]], [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] falls into the arms of [[Roy Koopa]], who takes her to his hotel.

From outside, the lights of Roy's HardBrick Hotel are flickering inexplicably, leading [[Luigi]] to question [[Mario]] how they can find Princess Toadstool with the lights going out. Mario believes there to be a switch inside Roy's HardBrick Hotel that would solve the problem. Later on, Mario discovers that the flicker is caused by the excessive usage of electricity in the toaster room, where numerous toasters toast [[Bowser's Sourpuss Bread]].
