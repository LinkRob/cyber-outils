'''''NES Remix 2''''' is the sequel to ''[[NES Remix]]''. Like its predecessor, ''NES Remix 2'' was released for the [[Wii U]] exclusively through the [[Nintendo eShop]]. ''NES Remix 2'' was released in April 2014, a few months after ''NES Remix''{{'}}s release in December 2013. Both ''NES Remix 2'' and ''NES Remix'' were packaged as a part of the retail Wii U title, ''[[NES Remix Pack]]'', also released in 2014.
