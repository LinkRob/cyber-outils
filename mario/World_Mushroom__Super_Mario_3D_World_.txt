'''World Mushroom''' is the second Special World and the tenth world overall in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. It sits adjacent to [[World Flower (Super Mario 3D World)|World Flower]], made up of remixes of previous courses. It is unlocked after clearing [[Cosmic Cannon Cluster]] in [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]]. This world contains seven regular courses and one Mystery House.
