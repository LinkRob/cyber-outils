'''Cat Boom Boom''' is a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', specifically within the game ''[[Bowser's Fury]]''. He is a cat variation of [[Boom Boom]].

== In ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''==
Cat Boom Boom is fought twice in the [[Clawswipe Colosseum]]. The first encounter, titled "Clash with Cat Boom Boom," is based on Boom Boom's battle in [[World 2 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 2]] and [[Boss Blitz]]. The second encounter, titled "Final Bout with Cat Boom Boom," is based on Boom Boom's battle in [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]].
