A '''Moldy Mush''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
A Moldy Mush is a very old and moldy [[Mushroom]]. In its artwork, a Moldy Mush is shown to have four green stains on it.

==Effects==
A Moldy Mush restores only 1 [[Heart Point|HP]] to a single member of [[Mario]]'s party, making it more of a gag than an actual item. It cannot be used during battle. The Moldy Mush is a prize after playing one of [[Grate Guy]] or [[Knife Guy]]'s games.
