An '''Elixir''' is a recovery item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
In its artwork, an Elixir is depicted as a white tadpole-like creature smiling, while their sprite shows an entirely different appearance of a blue cup with a musical note (♪) printed on it.

==Effects==
Using an Elixir restores 80 [[HP]] for all party members. However, getting an Elixir requires the party to have an [[Alto Card]] to get into the [[Juice Bar]] in [[Tadpole Pond]]. There, an Elixir can be purchased for 42 [[coin]]s.
