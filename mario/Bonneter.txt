{{Nihongo|'''Bonneters'''|カブロン人|Kaburon-jin|Bonneton People}} are the natives of the Cap Kingdom introduced in the game ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. They are the hat ghosts who live in the [[Bonneton|Bonneton area]] of the Cap Kingdom.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Bonneters are hat like ghosts, usually black top hats.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Bonneters can take appearances of other hats and have the ability to capture beings.

== Habitats ==
Bonneters take great pride in building their homes and do not mind people climbing up on top of them, perhaps being atop of private property comes naturally to these hat-like people. Mario can see Bonneters running the local [[Crazy Cap]] in kingdoms that do not have citizens, such as the [[Cascade Kingdom]]. After defeating the final boss, Mario will find Bonneters in most kingdoms, as with natives of other kingdoms.
