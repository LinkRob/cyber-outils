==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
They are encountered at [[Chucklehuck Woods]], [[Woohoo Hooniversity]], and [[Joke's End]], with each location changing the Wall Face's appearance. When a nearby [[Pressure Plate]] is stepped on, the Wall Face opens its eyes and mouth to shoot either a fireball (at Chucklehuck Woods and Joke's End) or a lightning bolt (at Woohoo Hooniversity). If [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are standing on it, they are hit by the blast and sent flying back. If Luigi steps on the Pressure Plate while inside a [[barrel]] (achieved with [[Dunk Hammer]]), the blast is rebounded. If the Wall Face launches a fireball, the rebound destroys it and usually opens a door in the process. If it launches a lightning bolt, it bounces perpendicular to the Wall Face.
