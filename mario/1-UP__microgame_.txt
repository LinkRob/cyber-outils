'''1-UP''' is a downloadable [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It was first released in Japan on May 20, 2009, then in North America on April 19, 2010, and lastly in Europe and Australia on May 21, 2010.

==Layout==
1-UP is based on ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', and reuses assets from said game.

== Gameplay ==
The object is for [[Mario]] to receive a [[1-Up]], whether by collecting three [[Coin]]s or obtaining a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The player takes control of Mario, who is riding a [[lift]]. They have to tap Mario to have him jump onto another lift. The player then has to tap a [[? Block]], where a 1-Up Mushroom emerges. After this, the player has to tap a [[Coin Block]] at the right timing so that the 1-Up Mushroom bounces off of it and Mario can collect it, resulting in a win. Alternatively, because Mario starts with 97 Coins, the player can hit the Coin Block three times for Mario to get a 1-Up. If the microgame timer runs out or if Mario falls off a lift, the player loses at the microgame.
