|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Recover''' is a special move used by certain enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Recover is used, healing stars appear on the selected target.

==Effects==
With Recover, the user is able to heal itself in battle. If the user has allies, it can use Recover on them as well. This move is comparable to [[Mallow]]'s [[HP Rain]] and [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]]'s [[Therapy]]. [[Mega Recover]] is a stronger version of Recover.
