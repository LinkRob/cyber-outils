'''Coin Shower Flower''' is a 1-vs-3 [[Coin]]-collecting [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Gameplay==
The solo player standing on the giant tilting sunflower can get the Coins that are dropped on the tilting flower, while the other three players have to use leaves as boats around the [[water]] to try to reach the Coins the solo player misses. If the player is standing on the center of the tilted flower, no Coins will pop out, and if the player on the flower falls into the water, the minigame will end in a "Miss!"

The minigame can be purchased from the [[Mini-Game House]] for 50 Coins when it is available for purchase.

On [[Mini-Game Island]], the player is the singular player and has to collect at least 20 Coins to clear the minigame.
