'''Waluigi's Reign''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party-e]]''.

==Gameplay==
It is a two-player minigame in which each player takes turns running [[Mario]] through a shower of [[Coin]]s (including [[Red Coin]]s) and [[Hammer]]s dropped by [[Waluigi]]. Mario has to try and collect the Coins while avoiding the Hammers. Each yellow coin is worth one [[point]], while each [[Red Coin]] is worth five points, and each player has to compete to try and collect more Coins during their turn than the other player. If Mario touches a hammer or the [[time limit|timer]] runs out, the current player's turn ends.
