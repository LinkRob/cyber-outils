==History==
Getting the Golden Card is completely optional to the game. To get the Golden Card, [[Mario]] must head into The Overthere bar in [[Flopside]] and then [[Flip]] into 3-D and head past [[Carson]]. He will then end up in a hidden room that contains the Golden Card (and a [[Cooking Disk]]). The Golden Card will allow the heroes to play the game Hammer Whacker at the [[Flipside Arcade]].
