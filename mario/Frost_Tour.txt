'''Frost Tour''' was the fiftieth tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] which began on August 11, 2021 and ended on August 24, 2021.

This Tour introduced [[Penguin Mario]] that made it's first debut in [[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]] along WIth [[Penguin Luigi]] and [[List of characters in Mario Kart Tour/Toad#Penguin Toad|Penguin Toad]] who return as drivers since their last apearence in [[Snow Tour]]. The tour added a variant of [[Vanilla Lake 1]] from [[Super Mario Kart]] called [[RMX Vanilla Lake 1]].
