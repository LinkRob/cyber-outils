'''Lucky Badge''' is a type of [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Using it greatly increases the likelihood of [[Lucky]] hits. The Lucky Badge can be bought from [[Peach's Castle]] for 700 [[coin]]s or obtained in the [[Thwomp Caverns]]. The Lucky Badge has a more effective variation named [[Lucky Badge A]].
