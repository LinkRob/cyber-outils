'''Moogles''' are a species originating from the [[Final Fantasy (franchise)|''Final Fantasy'' franchise]] made by [[Square Enix]]. They have appeared in nearly every ''Final Fantasy'' game since their debut in ''[[w:c:finalfantasy:Final Fantasy III|Final Fantasy III]]'' to the point of becoming a series mascot. The first appearance of a Moogle in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] is in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

Moogle's design in the ''Mario'' franchise appears to be a mix between its recurring design starting from ''Final Fantasy III'' and its bigger, puffier-eared one from ''Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles''. A Moogle with a similar design, apart from differently colored wings, appears as the logo for the "Moogleworks" shop in ''Final Fantasy XIII''.

==In {{franchise|Mario}}==
===''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''===
A Moogle appears as one of five playable characters from the ''Final Fantasy'' series, the others being [[Ninja (character)|Ninja]], [[Black Mage]], [[White Mage]], and [[Cactuar]]. Moogle is unlocked by earning a silver trophy in the [[Star Tourney]] on the Normal difficulty. It is classified as a Tricky character.

===''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''===
Moogle and the other four ''Final Fantasy'' characters return as playable characters. Moogle is unlocked by beating the Mushroom Cup Tournament of any sport. Moogle retains its Tricky classification, having very weak power and a speed below average, but it makes up for those weaknesses with a high technique stat. Unlike most characters with high technique attributes, Moogle's full power throws are not curved.

==In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''==
A Moogle appears alongside a [[w:c:finalfantasy:Chocobo|Chocobo]] as a neutral, primary [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]], which was included in the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Spirit Board|Spirit Board]] for Challenger Pack 8. Moogle's representative fighter in the spirit battle is a gray [[Kirby]]. If the spirit is equipped to a fighter, their speed is increased.
