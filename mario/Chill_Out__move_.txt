|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Chill Out''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can be used once [[Mario]] defeats [[General Guy]] and frees [[Muskular]] during the events of Chapter 4. It requires 2 [[Star Power|SP]] to use.

==Effects==

With Chill Out, Muskular appears in the screen and forces all of the enemies to "chill out" lowering their attack power by 3 for a few turns. This is very useful against heavy-hitting enemies such as the [[Anti Guy]]s. However, Chill Out doesn't work well on bosses and should be saved only for very tough enemy fights.
