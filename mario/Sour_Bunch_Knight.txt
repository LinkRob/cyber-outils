'''Sour Bunch Knights''' are enemies from ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]'' that work as agents of the [[Sour Bunch]].

== Description ==
Sour Bunch Knights are short, humanoid members of the [[Sour Bunch]]. They have knight attire and carry swords.

== Behaviour/Combat ==
Sour Bunch Knights use their sword to attack their targets. Any attack can destroy a Sour Bunch Knight, causing them to collapse.

== Variants ==

=== Sour Bunch Spearman ===
Sour Bunch Spearmen use spears rather than swords. Their attacks involve a charging rush.

=== Big Sour Bunch Knight ===
A Big Sour Bunch Knight is featured as a boss in [[The Castle of Thorns]]. He sends two Sour Bunch Spearmen during the first phase, and attacks directly during his second phase. His weapon is a hammer, and Princess Peach has to stomp his head to stun him, then strike his vulnerable body before the stun wears off. When he is defeated, the path clears.
