The '''Piranha Plant Capsule''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
The Piranha Plant Capsule can be thrown onto a space or activated by the user for five [[coin]]s. Any player who lands on a space with the Piranha Plant Capsule is chewed up and spat out by a [[Piranha Plant]], causing them to lose half of their coins. If the player has an odd number of coins, they lose the larger portion (rounded up). Even if a character uses the Piranha Plant Capsule on themselves, the same negative effect occurs.

If a player is already on a space containing a Piranha Plant Capsule and an opponent lands on it, the Piranha Plant chews only the opponent while the original player reacts with surprise.

The Piranha Plant Capsule is replaced by the near-identical [[Piranha Plant Orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.
