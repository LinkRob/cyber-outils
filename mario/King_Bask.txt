'''King Bask''' is a character mentioned during the events of ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]''.

== History ==
King Bask is mentioned by [[Aster]]. He was a former king of the Kingdom of Bask. Many years before the game's events, Bask heard of the [[Lucien]]'s power and tried to get his hands on that power, but the racket used his power to destroy the Kingdom of Bask the moment he was captured. Bask, in an effort to save his kingdom, divided the racket's power between five Power Stones, and hid the racket himself in a secret room.

== Nomenclature ==
In the Japanese version of the game, King Bask is referred to as ''Soru'', from either the Latin translation of "sun" or the Spanish translation (''sōl'' or ''sol''). His German, Spanish, and Italian names are all ''Solarius'', due to the possible relationship of the Lucien's name to Lucifer as the personification of the morning star. French translates Bask's name as ''Rakhetaton'', forming a pun on ''raquette'' ("racket") and the name of the pharaoh [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhenaton Akhenaton Amenhotep IV]. Russian does not give him a name, but refers to him by the title of "King of the Sun" (''Korol' Solntsa'', written Король Солнца).
