|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Super Flame '''is a fire magic move used by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is the last move Mario can learn, learned at level 18. The move is stronger than both [[Super Flame]] and [[Fire Orb]].

==Effects==
When the move is used, Mario will shoot large amount of [[fireball]]s at the enemy, dealing great damage.

== Trivia ==
* This is the most powerful fire magic move in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''.
* It costs 14 [[Flower Point]]s to learn this move.
