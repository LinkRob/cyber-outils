'''Robo No.1 '''(ロボNO.1) is an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for the [[Game Boy]]. It makes one single appearance in Tower 9-6 of the game near the top of the level.

== Profile ==
===Physical Description===
Robo No.1 resembles a knight, but it's name suggests a relation to robots.

===Behavior===
Robo No.1 walks back and forth on a platform, turning around upon reaching the edges.

==In ''Donkey Kong'' (Game Boy)==
Though it is classified as an enemy, only one Robo No.1 ever appears, at the top of [[Stage 9-6]], where it guards the last pole that [[Mario]] uses to swing over to [[Pauline]]. Robo No.1 can be stood on and also picked up and thrown, but if Mario touches a Robo No.1, he loses a [[1-Up|life]]. The Robo No.1 cannot be defeated because there are no projectiles nearby to use against it.
