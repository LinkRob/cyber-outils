'''Daves''' are police dogs in [[Wario: Master of Disguise]]. 

==History==
They appear along with [[Sergeant Houndstooth]]s when a [[Nigel B. Whistlebottom]] blows a whistle. They attack by punching [[Wario]] and are also invincible. They can temporarily be stunned by attacks allowing Wario to get by them unscathed. Dave, like with Sergeant Houndstooths and Nigel B. Whistlebottom are only found in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]].
