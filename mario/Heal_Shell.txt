'''Heal Shell''' is a type of armor only usable by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] for 100 [[coin]]s. Equipping it increases Bowser's [[Defense]] by 24 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 12.

Although it has Heal in its name, the Heal Shell does not have any healing capabilities.
