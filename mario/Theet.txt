The '''Theet''' is a creature from ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]'' They are featured as employees of the [[Sparkle Theatre]], taking various roles such as staff and cast members, but are also featured as spectators.

== Description ==
Theets have light yellow skin, two black beady eyes, a bulbous nose, and no mouth. Their nose flashes different colours that depend on the Theet's mood.

== History ==
The Sparkle Theatre is run by Theets, but during the events of the game, [[Grape (character)|Madame Grape]] and [[Sour Bunch|her minions]] try to rob them of the theatre, and [[Princess Peach]] and [[Stella]] team up with the Theets to reclaim the theatre.

A Theet also appears in a recipe book in [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]. 

== Variants ==

=== Knight Theet ===
Official artwork of the Theet has a knight-themed Theet with a glowing red nose.

=== Mer-theet ===
These are a merfolk variant of Theet that can be found in underwater areas.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Name ===
Theets take their name from "theatre," referencing their role as employees in the theatre.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Theets are called "Kyasuton" (written キャストン), which comes from "cast." Their Chinese name is "Kǎsīdùn" (written 卡司顿), from the Japanese name, and the Japanese name is also transliterated into Korean, yielding "''Kae-seu-teun''" (written 캐스튼). In the Spanish dub, they are called "''Bambalino''" (from ''bambalina'' meaning "backdrop" in the sense of "background"), while in Portuguese, they are called "''Ribaltino''" (from "''ribalta''," which means "stage"). In Italian, they are called "''Teatrì''" (from ''teatro'', "theatre"), and in French, they are called "''Théâtrin''," from "''théâtre''" ("theatre"). In the German dub, Theets are called "''Teatri''," from "''Theater''" ("theatre"), and in Russian, they have the name ''"Teatral'ka''" (written Театралька), which is derived from ''teatral'nyy'' ("theatrical," written театральный).
