'''Poltergust''' can refer to not only the line of [[Poltergust]] models but also more specific types of models:

*[[Poltergust 3000]] - The first version of the Poltergust ever created. It appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'', ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]'', and ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''.
*[[Super Poltergust 3001]] - The second version of the Poltergust. It appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.
*[[Poltergust 4000]] - A version of the Poltergust that is both a [[kart]] and a vacuum. It debuts in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and reappears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.
**[[Ice-blue Poltergust]] - A blue variant of the Poltergust 4000 in ''Mario Kart Tour''.
*[[Poltergust 5000]] - An upgraded version of the Poltergust that appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]''.
*[[Poltergust G-00]] - A further upgraded version of the Poltergust that appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
*[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit#Karts|Poltergust G-00]] - A kart which resembles the model from ''Luigi's Mansion 3'' and was added to ''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]]'' in the 2.0 update.
