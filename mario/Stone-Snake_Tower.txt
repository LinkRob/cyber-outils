}}'''Stone-Snake Tower''' is a level found in [[Soda Jungle]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Features==
The level starts in a tower with a large [[Snake Block]] and a [[Mega Grrrol]] found behind [[Brick Block]]s. A [[Mega ? Block]] is found above a Brick Block containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The Snake Block and the [[Poisoned Water]] begin to rise, and [[Grrrol]]s are spawned from the wall. A [[Red Ring]] is found, followed by another Mega Grrrol in front of a [[P Switch]]. A pair of [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s also appears, and the Snake Block begins to rise. A second Snake Block is then found, followed by the boss door, which takes the player to [[Boom Boom]]. [[Kamek]] causes him to grow larger before the battle. He must then be hit three times in order to be defeated.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Wall jump when reaching the right wall for the first time while avoiding the Grrrols above.
*'''Star Coin 2''': When the Mega Grrrol breaks the blocks leading to the P Switch, hit that switch and follow the path of blue coins leading to the Star Coin above. Jump at the right moment to avoid getting hit by the [[Spiked Ball]]s.
*'''Star Coin 3''': When seeing another stone-snake, jump over it right after to a hidden alcove and wall jump to the coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
In the hidden passage below the third Star Coin shows a picture of Super Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' crouching.
