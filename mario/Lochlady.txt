'''Lochladies''' are mermaid-like creatures native of the [[Lake Kingdom]] that appear in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

== Appearance ==
They are found in blue and pink color schemes. Each of them wear a coral headdress and a pair of golden bracelets.

== Description ==
No male Lochladies appear to exist. Lochladies are known for their strength and flexibility, which lend to their professions as accomplished designers.

== ''Super Mario Odyssey'' ==
Bowser invades their kingdom to steal their beloved Lochlady Dress for his wedding with Princess Peach.


At the wedding a few Lochladys are seen and say that they were brought by force and want to go home
