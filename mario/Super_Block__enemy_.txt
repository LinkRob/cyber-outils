}}The '''Super Block''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
The Super Block is a toy enemy that is only seen in [[Frantic Factory]]. It is seen with the other toys forming the [[Toy Monster]] while [[Chunky Kong]] becomes [[Hunky Chunky]]. The Super Block forms the main head of the Toy Monster. Once the Toy Monster is defeated, the Super Block along with the other toys disappear as well.
