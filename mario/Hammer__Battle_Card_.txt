|name=Hammer (Battle Card)}}'''Hammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Hammer is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a brown hammer with a orange/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 5/20 coins

Sell Price: 3/10 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Hammer is first seen in [[Port Prisma]], but can also found in [[Ruddy Road]], [[Indigo Underground]], [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Château Chanterelle]], [[Marmalade Valley]], [[Kiwano Temple]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Plum Park]], [[Sacred Forest]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Toad Trainworks]], and [[Tangerino Grill]].
