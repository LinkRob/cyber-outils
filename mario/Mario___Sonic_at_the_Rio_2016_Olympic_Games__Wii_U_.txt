'''''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games''''' is the [[Wii U]] home console release of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'', released in June 2016. It is the fifth home console installment of the {{series|Mario & Sonic}}, following after ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]''. This game takes place in Rio, Brazil, during the [[wikipedia:2016 Summer Olympics|Rio 2016 Olympics]] that occurred in August 2016. The game is the first ''Mario & Sonic'' home console installment to release after its handheld counterpart, in this case being the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|version]] of the same name.

The game received an [[arcade]] tie-in titled ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games Arcade Edition]]''.
