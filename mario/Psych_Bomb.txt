|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Psych Bomb''' is a special moved used by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Toadstool learns this attack at Level 18 and this is her only offensive special attack. This move takes 15 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use. It was a good combination with Bowser Crush, Geno Blast or Geno Flash when using on boss battles, e.g. Culex.

==Description==
When Psych Bomb is used, red bombs are thrown at enemies.

==Effects==
To use Psych Bomb, the player must press the Y button to build up damage. This move can cause major damage to all enemies on the screen.
