|first = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}The '''Lobby''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is located near the [[Foyer]] and has an elevator that leads down into the [[Cellar]]. The Lobby also leads out to a higher area of the Library. There is also a door that leads to the [[Balcony]] from this room.
==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-3: Quiet Please!|A-3: Quiet Please!]], Luigi heads into the Lobby but has a run in with some [[Greenie]]s but he vacuums them up into the [[Poltergust 5000]]. A [[Slammer]] with some Spiders soon appear on the elevator but Luigi defeats them as well. Luigi then heads out to the upper portion of the Library.

Once Luigi reaches the final mission [[Gloomy Manor#A-Boss: Confront the Source|A-Boss: Confront the Source]], he gets directly [[Pixelator|pixelated]] to the Lobby where he can use the Elevator Key to get down to the Cellar.
