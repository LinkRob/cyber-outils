The '''Jet Tank One''' is a water blaster invented by [[Penny Crygor]] in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Description==
Penny uses the Jet Tank One whenever the player plays as her in a [[microgame]]. The player can tilt the {{button|ns|leftstick}} in a direction to have Penny spray water toward that direction as the recoil pushes her into the opposite direction. The water sprayed from the Jet Tank One serves as projectiles capable of affecting objects in the microgames. The player can press {{button|ns|a}} to have Penny hover in midair using the Jet Tank One and then press it again to cancel the move.

==In ''WarioWare: Get It Together!''==
During the story mode, Jet Tank One is first unveiled when Penny finishes constructing it in her [[Penny's Lab|lab]]. While testing out the Jet Tank One, Penny receives a call from her grandfather, [[Dr. Crygor]], to notify her that they are stuck in a game and need her to help them escape. With the Jet Tank One, Penny quickly flies to [[WarioWare, Inc.]] and enters the game that [[Wario]] and the others are inside of.

Upon arriving, Penny forms the [[Golden Watering Can]] and fills it with water from the Jet Tank One, at which point it floats toward a nearby toilet and waters it, causing a massive [[beanstalk]] to grow from the toilet.
