The '''Starfish''' is an enemy appearing only in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. The enemy is based on [[Starfy]], the main character of ''[[w:c:starfy:The Legendary Starfy (series)|The Legendary Starfy]]'' series, as referenced by its glossary description. It reuses various animations and sprites of Starfy from his games. It has pink cheeks and wears black sunglasses, referencing Starfy's hobby of wearing and collecting sunglasses.

Although the Starfish does not attempt to harm [[Princess Peach]], she still takes damage when coming into contact with it. The game describes it twice as an enemy; in the game's glossary, it is described as "a foe", and in all its locations, there is a message box stating it to be an "odd enemy".

Out of all the enemies in the game, the Starfish is the hardest enemy to find and defeat. It appears in a specific area within three levels: [[Hoo's Wood 2-5|2-5]] in [[Hoo's Wood]], [[Wavy Beach 5-5|5-5]] in [[Wavy Beach]], and [[Gleam Glacier 6-4|6-4]] in [[Gleam Glacier]]. There is a low probability of seeing the Starfish when the player enters the specific area it is located, so repeated entry to the area might be needed to cause it to appear. To defeat it, players must attack it in each of these three places. After defeating it in all three places, the Starfish will be added to the Glossary. Once it takes damage in an area, it will not appear in that area again.

In Hoo's Woods, it swims back and forth in a shallow area of water, and eventually will jump up into the sky and leave. Players must hit it or jump on it as it jumps up.

In Wavy Beach, it is asleep on the sand. The player must walk slowly up to it, then attack it, so it does not wake up and flee. It has a mat on it, and upon waking it up, the mat is lifted up, showing a drawing of [[w:c:starfy:Mattel|Mattel]] on it.<ref>[https://twitter.com/MarioBrothBlog/status/1322965232742993921 Supper Mario Broth on Twitter]. Retrieved November 1, 2020.</ref>

In Gleam Glacier, it stands on a ledge above the player. It will leave after a few seconds. To reach it, players must use the [[Gloom]] vibe.
