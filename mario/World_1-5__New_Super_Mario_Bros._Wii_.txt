'''World 1-5''' is a level from [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 

<br />
==Layout==
The level starts out on a large [[Mushroom Platform]], with three rotating mushroom blocks serving as the first obstacles. More regular Mushroom Platforms follow. Above these, there are three sets of [[Brick Block]]s running along lines. One of these is a [[? Block]], which contains a [[Propeller Mushroom]]. There are then two large green rotating mushroom blocks, the second of which contains the second [[Star Coin]] at the edge of it. After that, there are some purple Mushroom Platforms and a mechanism that changes the platform height by shaking the [[Wii#Wii Remote|Wii Remote]] while the player stands on top of the oversized screw. These platforms start off in such a position where the player needs to either activate the screw or have a Propeller Mushroom handy. If the player raises the fourth platform high enough, they are able to obtain a [[Red Ring]] from an elevated yellow platform.

Next there is the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], after which there is a set of Brick Blocks guided by a line moving notably faster than the ones seen earlier. There are a few blocks in the middle of this line and some [[coin]]s floating above, as well as a single red Koopa Troopa on the blocks. There is another line that two separate Brick Blocks follow circumnavigating a green Mushroom Platform. Standing on one of the Brick Blocks allows the player to touch another Red Ring. The rest of the level contains more rotating mushroom blocks, which lead to the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]], guarded by Koopa Paratroopas.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In the area with three rail rides (two with a Brick Block and a ? Block, and one with two Brick Blocks), Mario and co. must get on top of one of the Brick Blocks on the middle rail ride, then [[jump]] up to a platform directly above. Hitting one of the Brick Blocks on the right of the platform will reveal a [[Beanstalk]], which leads to a secret area. The first Star Coin is then surrounded by several [[coin]]s and seven rail-riding ? Blocks, which must be used to reach the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is located shortly after the exit drop from the area with the first Star Coin. To get it, Mario and co. must get on the right edge of the green mushroom block, descend to the Star Coin, then immediately jump to safety before falling. It can also be obtained with a [[Propeller Mushroom]] or by throwing a Koopa shell at it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is located in the long row of red mushroom blocks, floating above a Paratroopa. Mario and co. must jump over the Paratroopa to get it.
