}}A '''Dark Cherbil''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Cherbils are only found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]] and are stronger versions of [[Cherbil]]s. Dark Cherbils can only be found in a few rooms of the Flopside Pit of 100 Trails. Like with Cherbils, Dark Cherbils attack by expelling gas at its target. However, Dark Cherbils are able to use the gas attacks of [[Ice Cherbil]]s and [[Poison Cherbil]]s allowing them also inflict freeze and poison on its targets.
