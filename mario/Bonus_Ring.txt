The '''Bonus Ring''' is an item that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It can only be obtained by making [[Starbeans Espresso]] at [[Starbeans Café]] for the second time. [[Professor E. Gadd]] will appear and hand over this accessory to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

It is derived from the [[w:c:starfox:Supply Ring|Supply Ring]] within the ''[[Star Fox]]'' series. The Supply Ring itself was planned to make an appearance within ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', along with [[Fox McCloud]] and various other [[Nintendo]] characters, before being removed. For the final release, the Supply Ring was renamed to the Bonus Ring to disconnect it from the ''Star Fox'' series.

==Appearance==
The Bonus Ring is a pentagon-shaped ring that is gold in color.

==Usage==
When equipped, the Bonus Ring doubles the amount of Experience Points won at the end of battles. This can be very useful to level up quicker and should be equipped against bosses and a horde of enemies.
