'''Wiggler Bell''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Description==
The Wiggler Bell is a gold bell that depicts [[Wiggler]]'s face. It is usable at only the [[Mega Wiggler's Tree Party]] board. Upon being used, the Wiggler Bell awakens Mega Wiggler, initiating the Wiggler's big move event, where Mega Wiggle moves around to the other side of the board and falls back asleep. The Wiggler Bell functions as the equivalent of [[Event Space]]s placed next to bells.

==In ''Super Mario Party Jamboree''==
The Wiggler Bell can be bought for five [[coin]]s at either [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shop]] at Mega Wiggler's Tree Party.
