'''Zipper''' is [[Waluigi]]'s unlockable Exclusive [[Kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

== Description ==
The Zipper resembles a three-wheeled chopper motorcycle. Notably, this is the first [[bike]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}, but it does not have the same abilities as the bikes used in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart DS'' ==
[[File:MKDS Zipper.png|thumb|200px|left|Waluigi on his Zipper on the kart selection menu]]
It is unlocked when either the player completes all cups in the [[Engine Class|100cc]] [[Grand Prix|Retro Grand Prix]] to unlock Waluigi, or when the player completes all cups in the 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix. By thie latter method, [[Toad (character)|Toad]] and [[Wario]] can use the Zipper, just like how they can use Mario's [[Shooting Star (kart)|Shooting Star]]. All other characters can use this kart once all cups in Mirror Mode have been completed.

The Zipper has all-round average and balanced stats, with the exception of [[Items (stat)|Items]], which have an excellent stat. It shares its speed with [[Luigi]]'s [[Streamliner]], and it shares its handling and drift with [[Mario]]'s [[B Dasher]]. It shares its items stat with all other characters' heavier karts.

The Zipper is used with Waluigi as the [[Staff Ghost]] for {{Classic course|SNES|[[Choco Island 2]]}}.
