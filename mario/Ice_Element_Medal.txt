The '''Ice Element Medal''' is an item in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Ice Element Medal is one of the three [[Element Medal]]s, the other two being the [[Fire Element Medal]] and the [[Water Element Medal]]. The Ice Element Medal in particular allows the [[Poltergust 3000]] to vacuum in a [[Ice Elemental Ghost]], giving it a limited supply of ice.

Most of an Ice Element Medal's purposes are for fighting [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]], as it can cool [[Miss Petunia]]'s hot bath, freeze the individual [[Boo]]s during the [[Boolossus]] battle, and expose a [[Blue Blaze]]'s [[Ghost's heart|heart]], which is protected by the element of water. The stream of ice can optionally be used to slow the movement of [[Boo]]s. The only time the Ice Element Medal is not used on ghosts is when freezing the water in the [[Pipe Room]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Ice Element Medal is the third and final Element Medal obtained by [[Luigi]]. It is contained within a large [[treasure chest]] in the [[Tea Room]], where it is revealed after vacuuming up every [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]]. Like with the other two Element Medals, the effects of the Ice Element Medal are not shown in a [[Mirror (object)|mirror]].
