'''Think Tank''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
The solo character controls a large, slow tank, while each of the team members control their own small, fast tank. The large tank has ten [[Heart Point|hit points]], while the smaller ones have three each. The team members, using their tanks, each have to try and destroy the solo character's tank and vice versa. The solo character wins if they eliminate all three team members, but the team wins if they manage to destroy the solo character's tank. If neither side defeats the other within five minutes, the minigame ends as a tie.

==Ending==
All characters stand in front of their tanks while performing their winning or losing animations, depending on their status. Any eliminated tank appears wrecked with smoke coming out of it. If the minigame ends in a tie, all characters perform their losing animations.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Fire

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a pun on "{{w|think tank}}," an organization that conducts research and advocacy, and a military tank.
