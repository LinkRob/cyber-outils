|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Pollen Nap''' is a special move used by [[Fink Flower]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Pollen Nap is used, a faded yellow light comes down on the target with pollens in it as well.

==Effects==
With Pollen Nap, the Fink Flowers are able to put one of [[Mario]]'s party to sleep. However, this can be cured with an item like an [[Able Juice]].
