'''Rustle Burrow''' (known as '''Scuttlebug Burrow''' in Europe and Australia) is the seventh level of World 3 in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]].''

== Layout ==
Rustle Burrow mainly takes place in a deep, underground pit. It is covered with cobwebs and enemies, mostly [[Scuttlebug]]s. Stickers that Mario drops while falling are found on ground. The stickers [[Mario]] dropped are all in various rooms.

== History ==
Mario chases the Wiggler Segment until it falls down a pit under a spiderweb. As he goes down, Mario will not only lose his Hammer and Stickers, but [[Kersti]] is also captured by the [[Scuttlebug]]s. Mario notices all of his Stickers on the ground and collects them. While traveling, Mario notices the Wiggler Segment behind a web but has to come back to it later. He is eventually reunited with Kersti. Mario fights more Scuttlebugs and eventually gets his Hammer back. After he battles and defeats the [[Big Scuttlebug]], he knocks away the web with the Wiggler Segment and it joins him. He goes back and takes the Comet Piece and leaves the level, the segment leaving to catch the boat in the harbor.
