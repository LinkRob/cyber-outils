The '''Snow Cloud''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and its two sequels, ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
The Snow Cloud is a white Lakitu's Cloud with a red bucket for a hat. The Snow Cloud is used similarly to a [[Rain Cloud]], although it locks on to another racer and freezes their kart's tires while snowing upon them. As a result, the racer loses traction and slows them down.
