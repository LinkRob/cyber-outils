'''Thunder Rage''' is an item which appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
[[File:Thunder Rage (Paper Mario).png|thumb|left]]
Thunder Rage is similar to the [[Thunder Bolt]] item in that it deals 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage to an enemy, except the Thunder Rage affects every enemy in battle. Thunder Rage can be purchased from the [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] [[Item Shop|shop]] in [[Harry's Shop]] for 35 [[coin]]s.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
Thunder Rage retains the same effects it had in the last game, and is found in various shops later in the game. This item is, as stated above, the same as it was in the last one. [[Mario]] can obtain this item from [[Zess T.]] by mixing a [[Point Swap]] and a [[Shooting Star]]. This, however, is not one of the many recipes for the recipe book.

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
Thunder Rage is given to Mario from various enemies upon their death or it can be purchased at the [[Flopside]] Shop. This item is priced around 60 [[coin]]s. Thunder Rage deals damage like it did in the last games, but does more or less, depending on how well-performed the action command was. To use this item, the player must shake the [[Wii Remote]] up and down to fill the meter. For how much the meter goes to the right, the more damage this item does. This item, like other attack items, deals damage to all enemies on screen. Thunder Rage deals 2-8 damage to every enemy regardless of their defense points.
