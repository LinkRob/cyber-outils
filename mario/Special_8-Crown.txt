'''Special 8-Crown''' is the secret final level in [[Special 8]], and the true final level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' overall. It is unlocked once the player has five stars on their profile. This level contains no [[Star Medal]]s, and also contains no [[Checkpoint Flag]]. It is the hardest level within the game.

==Layout==
[[File:SM3DL S8-Crown.png|thumb|left|A Big Cosmic Clone chasing Mario]]
The player starts off on a platform next to some [[Toad (species)|Toads]], [[Hammer Bro]]s., and [[Boomerang Bro]]s. At the end of the platforms is a [[Warp Box]] that leads to an area that contains several platforms along with some [[Goomba]]s and several other enemies. At the end of this section is a [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to a boss fight with [[Boom Boom]] and [[Pom Pom]] (with Pom Pom's boss theme used). After the player defeats them, a Warp Pipe appears.

In the next area is a [[Switchboard]] that goes past some [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]], some [[Tail Bob-omb]]s, and some [[Fire Bar]]s before leading to a Warp Box. In the next area are several [[Donut Block|Donut Lift]]s and Fire Bars with a Warp Box at the end. In the next segment is a [[Big Cosmic Clone]], some [[Burner]]s, and some [[Chain Chomp]]s with two [[Wallop]]s guarding the Warp Box.

In the next area is a switch that activates some [[Flip Panel (platform)|Flip Panel]]s, which spell out "THANK YOU!!" and then lead to a Warp Box that goes to the final area with the [[Goal Pole]].
