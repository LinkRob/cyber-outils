|caption   = [[Mario]] standing on a crate in Bowser Jr.'s Flotilla}}'''Bowser Jr.'s Flotilla''' is the second level of World 6 in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Layout==
This level takes place high in the skies. The battleship highly resembles the airships from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==History==
[[Mario]] and [[Kersti]] are flying on [[Flutter]] toward Bowser's Castle until [[Bowser Jr.]] once again gets in their way. Mario must board the Flotilla and fight his way toward the Koopa King's son. Mario eventually reaches the Koopa Prince and the two get into their final fight. Bowser Jr. tries to one up Mario by having a defensive shield but the plumber hero strikes back easily with the Tail or Shiny Tail Stickers. Bowser Jr. is defeated and he is launch away from his Flotilla with Mario taking the Comet Piece and completing the level ready to face his arch-enemy, [[Bowser]], in the final battle.
