'''Defeat Bomb Flapper!''' is a [[minigame]] in the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player plays as [[Waluigi]] in this minigame, which takes place in [[Cubyrinth]] in [[Adventure Tours]] mode. The player moves with the control pad and picks up [[Bob-omb]]s with the A button. The player must pick up black Bob-ombs dropped by [[Bomb Flapper]] and throw them back at it several times within 45 seconds. Bomb Flapper will occasionally drop green Bob-ombs, which chase the player. The bombs will turn red and explode within a certain amount of time. If a Bob-omb explodes on Waluigi, he will be stunned for a few seconds, costing the player valuable time.

==Flavor text==
''Throw back Bob-ombs to reduce Bomb Flapper's HP to 0! Green Bob-ombs will chase you!''
