A '''Ring Beamer''' is an obstacle which appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They are dome-shaped installations that fire plasma rings when provoked.

== Description ==
Ring Beamers have yellow dome-shaped heads atop their disk-shaped body. This head has several spikes protruding from them; therefore, the Ring Beamer cannot be jumped onto.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Ring Beamers first appear in [[Toy Time Galaxy]]. They are immobile and shoot a beam that will damage [[Mario]] if he touches it. A Ring Beamer cannot be defeated.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
Ring Beamers appear in [[Shiverburn Galaxy]] and [[Grandmaster Galaxy]], and they act the same as in ''Super Mario Galaxy''. A Ring Beamer can now be immobilized by the [[Co-Star Luma]], but it can still not be defeated.

== Variants ==
Ring Beamers belong to an enemy class that attacks using shockwaves. Others in this class include the [[Jump Beamer]] and [[Pulse Beam]], the former being susceptible to being stomped and used as a [[Springboard]] (although this merely stuns the Jump Beamer and the Jump Beamer is effectively immortal) and the latter being a "manually activated" variant in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

== Trivia ==

* Certain enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'' which have been poisoned with [[Glohm]] can launch similar plasma rings when slain.
