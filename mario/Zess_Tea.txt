A '''Zess Tea''' is an item and a [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is one of many dishes created and named after [[Zess T.]] herself, although Zess Tea in particular has the same pronunciation as her name. Zess T. can brew Zess Tea if [[Mario]] gives her a [[Golden Leaf]] from the [[Creepy Steeple]].

==Effects==
Drinking a Zess Tea replenishes 20 [[Flower Point|FP]] making it a useful FP recovery item. However, a [[Maple Syrup]] is a better option since it is easier to get as it can be purchased in some [[Item Shop]]s.
