'''Shiny Hopslipper''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Description==
A Shiny Hopslipper allows [[Mario]] to jump on a single foe with high damage. This sticker is very helpful for fighting strong, normal enemies such as [[Boomerang Bro]]s, but is still not intended to be used on enemies with a high [[Defense]] rating. Shiny Hopslipper stickers are identical to regular [[Hopslipper]] stickers, except they are shinier and have a silver background.

==In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''==
At first, the Shiny Hopslipper is rare, but it becomes very common after [[Mario]] reaches [[World 5 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|World 5]].
