'''Wario Land''' is [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

== Layout ==
The microgame takes place in ''[[Super Mario Land 3: Wario Land]]''.

== Gameplay ==
With the form of "The Boxer", the player controls [[Wario]] who is holding a [[Pirate Goom]]. The player must thrust their [[Wiimote]] forwards for Wario to throw the Pirate Goom at an enemy.
* '''Level 1''': It is easy to defeat the enemy.
* '''Level 2''': Either the enemy jumps, flies or walks away from Wario. The enemy is harder to hit than the previous difficulty.
* '''Level 3''': Wario has to wait for the enemy to approach before he throws the Pirate Goom.
