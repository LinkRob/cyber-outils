'''Blizland''' is the sixth and final area in the [[Nintendo DS|DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''. The [[snow spirits|snow spirit]] of this area is [[Blizza]]. This area was turned into a mixture of [[Bowser's Castle]] and Dr. Eggman's Factory by [[Bowser]] and [[Dr. Eggman]].

There are numerous enemies in the area. The robot Omega is found after the first ice wall. Upon defeating him in the Intense Short Track, Omega disappears to prepare to battle Dr. Eggman. Shortly after, a Chain Chomp can be found blocking the way. After Wario knocks it off the bridge, the team can proceed. [[Frosty]] is held hostage by [[Dry Bowser]] here. After Dry Bowser is defeated and Frosty is freed, the team faces off against Bowser and Dr. Eggman in the Blazing Bobsleigh, Fever Hockey, and Extreme Snowboarding.
