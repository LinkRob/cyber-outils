'''Hösndösn''' is a location in the ''[[Club Nintendo (magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' magazine "[[Super Mario: Verloren in der Zeit]]."

==Description==
Hösndösn is a mysterious planet inhabited by the alien pair [[Hösn and Dösn]] as well as a [[human]] [[Klösn]]. The planet's name combines the names of the alien pair Hösn and Dösn.

Hösndösn is similar to [[Earth]] or the [[Mushroom Earth]]. Aside from Klösn, Hösndösn is inhabited by several humans, and the planet has its own Olympic Winter Games-like event, the "intergalactic Olympics." The inhabitants speak a similar language as Mario's, but with the vowel "o" substituted for the umlaut "ö."

Contrarily, Hösndösn has notable differences from Earth, such as the presence of several moons, one of which is extremely large, and an overall rocky and barren terrain akin to the Moon. It also features futuristic buildings and flying objects.
