A '''current''' is a flow of water, varying from slow to fast speeds. Currents appear in various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, mainly the platforming titles. Currents, or rather the effects of them, first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', but they are first visibly shown in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. While currents cannot hurt [[Mario]] or another character, the fast-moving ones can push them toward an obstacle or pitfall or sometimes prevent them from continuing in a certain direction, similar to stronger [[wind]]s.
