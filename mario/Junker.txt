The '''Junker''' is a boss that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|the 3DS remake]], and is the fourteenth boss of the game. It's a robotic trash can that was created by [[Midbus]] to defeat [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]], but it was destroyed not long after.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Junker resembles a pink trash can. He has golden eyes. His arms are similar to vacuums. His [[Junker X|X counterpart]] gives him a blue body and his eyes a pink coloration, but in both forms, he has brown foot-like shoes.

== History ==
Junker is a boss in [[Peach's Castle Garden]].
{{Sectstub}}

=== Combat ===
Whenever he starts his next turn, the Junker calls forth some [[Junker Can]]s. These monsters are sentient cyan trash can-like monsters that have golden eyes, though in [[The Gauntlet]], their body turns brown and their eyes pink. The Junker himself has the following moves:
*First, Junker can blind a Mario brother by stomping the ground, knocking a trash can onto him. Junker will then try to crush him with [[Thwomp]]s pulled from inside his "mouth", though that Bro. can stop his movement by jumping; he must use that to avoid getting brained for massive damage.
*Second, Junker will pick a Junker Can up and shake it, causing all sorts of objects to fly out. The [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Poison Mushroom]]s have to be dodged by ducking (no input required), boxers do not deal damage to the Bros., and the rest must be avoided by jumping. Any [[Bob-omb]]s must be avoided entirely; humorously, it will occasionally let out old underwear. Poison Mushrooms are exclusive to the 3DS remaster.
*Third, Junker will throw some balls toward the Bros, who must mount them. After two, Junker will get mad and charge, though the target can avoid this by jumping over him.
*Junker can also incapacitate [[Luigi]] by sucking him up with his vacuum arms and trapping him in a Junker Can. This always hits but Luigi may sometimes catch the rim to make his getaway quickly, but if he fails, Luigi will be trapped, so Mario will have to free him by defeating a Junker Can that has him. If Mario dies with Luigi trapped, the Junker automatically wins. In the 3DS version, Luigi can get away if the player mashes the B button quickly enough.

On the initiative count of 75 (113 with the Challenge Medal; The [[The Gauntlet|Gauntlet]] bumps this count up to 152 (228 with the Challenge Medal).), the Junker Cans will charge, though this can be blocked with a hammer. Killing a Junker Can will force the Bro. who was responsible to block a [[Chain Chomp]], a [[Fish Bone]], or a [[Nooz]], which can all be blocked with the hammer and have only one hit point.
*Fish Bones circle their target and then ram him after a random number of loops (always at least 2).
*Chain Chomps simply charge at their target. If this hits, the target may be [[Poison (status effect)|poisoned]], taking damage over time.
*Noozes hover above their target and then fall, sometimes hover in order to throw off the victim's counter. In the 3DS remaster, any Nooz that targets [[Mario (character)|Mario]] has a red cockscomb.

Junker is a level 26 monster that has 1,445 hit points (2,168 with the Challenge Medal), 120 power (300 with the Challenge Medal), 89 defense (134 with the Challenge Medal), 48 speed (72 with the Challenge Medal). He gives 6,000 experience and drops 900 coins (1,350 with the Challenge Medal) and a Max Nut and Special Gloves upon defeat. He is immune to being stunned, the KO effect, and the burn effect, but can still suffer Stat Downs. His X form has 3080 hit points (4620 with the Challenge Medal), 179 Power (448 with the Challenge Medal), 129 Defense (194 with the Challenge Medal), and 123 Speed (185 with the Challenge Medal) and drops 9000 coins upon being beaten the first time as well as the Challenge Medal.

== Trivia ==
* Before the battle with Junker, Midbus refers to the robot as the '''Super Ultra Great Mega Trashy Monster Junker Bot''', in battle, this is just shortened to Junker, it's unknown if the former is actually it's real name or an instance of Midbus giving an unnecessarily long name for something (something he has been known to do).
* Junker slightly reassembles to [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Trash_Basher Trash Basher], a monster from the Anime based off of the ''Kirby'' series, ''Kirby: Right Back At Ya!'', he is also similar to said monster, in that they are both Trash Cans and can launch out trash.
* The Junker is one of three bosses ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' to have an attack that cannot be avoided conventionally, with the other two being [[Fawful]]'s [[Vacuum Helmet]] and the [[Fawfulized|Fawfulised]] [[Princess Peach's Castle (boss)|Princess Peach's Castle]]. In his case, the Junker is able to suck Luigi in with one of his arms. (The attack can be avoided in the 3D remaster by mashing the B button.)
** The Junker is also the fourth boss in the series overall to have an unavoidable attack, the other bosses being the following:
*** During her second fight in Bowser's Castle in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', [[Cackletta]] summons a Time Bob-omb that drops the Bros. to 1 hit point, triggering the second phase of her battle (third in the 3DS version.)
*** When the Bros. fight the three [[Shroob|Shroobs]] during their investigation of [[Hollijolli Village]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', the [[Shroob UFO|Shroob Saucer]] that was watching the fight drops a bomb onto the battlefield, allowing the Shroobs to escape. This Saucer is absent when the babies come.
*** If the [[Elder Shrooboid]] is not brought down within four turns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', he automatically wins as the Shroob Saucer that he summoned will drop a bomb onto the battlefield. More time can be bought by countering his Spiked Ball attack and sending it into the Shroob Saucer, which (on top of knocking him over and disabling his Spiny trait) causes the counter to reset to four.
*** The Vacuum Helmet is able to [[Prohibited Command|steal]] the Bros.'s [[Action command|Action Commands]] in ''[[Mario  Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.
*** [[Bowser]] steals [[Bros. Attack|Bros. Attacks]] as a counterattack during his [[The Gauntlet|Gauntlet]] [[Bowser X|fight]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. (He did not do this in [[Toadley Clinic]].)
*** During the [[Robo-Drilldigger|Robo-DrillDigger]]'s story fight in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', the first time the robot launches his plasma ball, the [[Giant Luigi|supersized]] [[Luigi]] will be unprepared to retaliate. This does not occur later, as Luigi sees the attack coming in and uses his hammer to deflect. The unavoidable version is cut in the Battle Ring.
*** When it is near death in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', the [[Pi'illodium]] will activate its self-destruct attack. The Bros. will have to kill the boss within 40 seconds of real time.
*** [[King Boo]] can sometimes kidnap [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] during his ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' fight.
*** [[King Bob-omb]] will explode on his next turn if his timer hits 0 in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. (If any Bro., Paper Mario included, survives, no [[Experience Point|experience]] or loot will be gained.)
