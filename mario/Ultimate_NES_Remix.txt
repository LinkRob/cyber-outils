'''''Ultimate NES Remix''''' is a [[Nintendo 3DS]] compilation title part of the ''[[NES Remix (series)|NES Remix]]'' series. It was first released in 2014 in English-speaking countries, then in Japan in 2015. Unlike ''[[NES Remix Pack]]'' for the [[Wii U]], which consists of both ''[[NES Remix]]'' and ''[[NES Remix 2]]'' in their entirety, ''Ultimate NES Remix'' only consists of a select number of challenges from each game, effectively making it a "best of" compilation.
