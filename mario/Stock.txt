'''Stock''' is a minor character that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==History==
Stock is seen during Chapter 2-3 where he was being held prisoner in [[Merlee's Mansion]] by [[Mimi]]. Stock can be found in a room with other inmates but Stock himself can only be seen in 3-D. Stock can give [[Mario]] the information to the safe that contains 1,000,000 Rupees but for a rather steep price of 10,000 Rubees (players can easily just get the combination by looking it up). After Chapter 2, Stock is desperate to get out of the mansion and is even willing to pay the heroes to help him out.
