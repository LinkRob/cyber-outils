A '''Galley Cat''' is an enemy from ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Galley Cats are cats that wear a green waiter uniform. 

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Galley Cats can only be found on board the [[S.S. Caviar]] as rare enemies (as only four can be found). It attacks by throwing a plate of food like a boomerang toward [[Wario]]. They are rather weak enemies and can be taken out with any attacks.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
A Galley Cat's name is a pun of "alley cat" and "galley," a name referring to a kitchen on a boat.
