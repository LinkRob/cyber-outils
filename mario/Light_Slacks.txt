The '''Light Slacks''' is an unused clothing item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. Had it been used, the Light Slacks would have increased [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s [[Defense]] by 48 and grant the [[Light G]] special effect. According to its description, the Light Slacks is made from [[Parabeanie]] wings.
