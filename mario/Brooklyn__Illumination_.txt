*''[[A Hero Like No Other]]''|first=''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''}}'''Brooklyn''' is a location that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series producted by [[Illumination]].

==History==

=== ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' ===
Both [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] reside in Brooklyn, starting their own business, the [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing (Illumination)|Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]], after resigning from the their boss, [[Foreman Spike (Illumination)|Foreman Spike]] and his company. However, they have a mishap during a plumbing job at a couple's place, and the two come home that night, exhausted. After Mario leaves the dinner table, he starts to watch the news about a flood, and he and Luigi go into the sewers to investigate, but both end up being sucked into a [[Mysterious Pipe (Illumination)|Mysterious Warp Pipe]], away from their home city.

Brooklyn later appears at the climax of the film, where after Mario lures a [[Bomber Bill (Illumination)|Bomber Bill]] into the same warp pipe where he entered the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] at, it sucks in everything and everyone, Mario and his friends have to pity off against an enraged [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]], who has the intent of killing them. Mario and Luigi eventually manage to get the [[Super Star (Illumination)|Super Star]], and proceed to take out Bowser and [[Koopa Troop (Illumination)|his army]], leading to them, alongside the residences of the Mushroom Kingdom to be hailed as heroes. After this point on, the damages Brooklyn sustained from Bowser and the Koopa Troop are likely being reconstructed.

In the end, Mario and Luigi decide to stay in the Mushroom Kingdom, but have a warp pipe installed so that they can travel back to Brooklyn anytime they want. Meanwhile, a [[Yoshi (Illumination)|Yoshi Egg]] can be seen hatching within the Brooklyn Sewers. In a flashback, Yoshi explained to Mario and Luigi his visit in Brooklyn, before he was trapped in the blocked [[Mysterious Pipe (Illumination)|pipe]] in the [[Sand Kingdom (Illumination)|Sand Kingdom]].
