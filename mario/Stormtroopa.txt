'''Stormtroopas''' are a variant of [[Koopa Troopa]]s which only appear in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show]]'' episode "[[Star Koopa]]."

==Etymology==
Their name is a play on [[w:c:starwars:Stormtrooper|Stormtroopers]] from the ''[[w:c:starwars:Star Wars|Star Wars]]'' franchise, with the "-troopa" spelling being taken from Koopa Troopa.
