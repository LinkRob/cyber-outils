|notes = Sometimes blows out [[coin]]s.}}A '''Trumpet Plant''' is a species of plant that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==

Trumpet Plants can only be found on [[Lavalava Island]] deep within [[Jade Jungle]]. When [[Mario]] either examines or hammers a Trumpet Plant, it will make a trumpet-like sound and blows out stardust and sometimes coins. This is the only function of the Trumpet Plants in the game.
