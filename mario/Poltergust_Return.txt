'''Poltergust Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] used by [[Luigi]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Luigi throws his racket in front of the ball, and the [[Poltergust 3000]] sucks both to his position as the ball is sent over the net. After Luigi performs the move, the ball is shot across the net with a slice.
