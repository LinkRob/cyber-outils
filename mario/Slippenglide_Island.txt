'''Slippenglide Island''' is a major location from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. Found drifting in the [[Brrrning Sea]] following the sundering of [[Concordia]], it is the twelfth island to be connected to [[Shipshape Island]]. As its name suggests, the island perpetually goes through a winter climate, being covered in snow. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can head to this island after connecting [[Heatfreeze Island]], although [[Skorcheen Island]] (which is unlocked along with Slippenglide) is recommended to visit beforehand. [[Bouncy Bloom]] trampolines appear here, but have been frozen in snow; they can be thawed out using the UFO Spin's Vacuum technique.

== History ==
After the Bros. connect [[Heatfreeze Island]], the [[IDLE]] gang discovers the [[Extension Corps]]'s hideout on Slippenglide Island (where Ten has set up his laboratory) during their expedition. Mario and Luigi can go to this island before [[Skorcheen Island]], or vicī versā, though picking this island second is recommended due to the enemies being of Level 24 and 25, while Skorcheen's inhabitant enemies have a Level of around 23. When the Bros. arrive, they discover that most of the Concordians that live here including the [[Slippenglide King|king]] have been infected with [[Glohm]] (save for [[The Glohm Dodging Slippenglider|one who expresses grief at what is going on around him]] and another who serves as a shopkeeper).

Mario and Luigi find the IDLE group near the laboratory to the west, along with Ten in the lab. The Bros. chase Ten up a mountain, upgrading their [[Bros. Ball]] to push through a strong wind that otherwise blocks them off. Ten escapes, while the Bros. investigate his laboratory, only to find not much information about [[Glohm]] or [[Zokket]]'s plans.

Mario and Luigi encounter some [[Koopa Troop]] soldiers, defeating them and pressing on to the Lighthouse.

When [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|hijacks Concordia]], Mario and Luigi return here to find the Extension Corps and get a [[Bond (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Bond]], which is necessary to enter the [[Soli-Tree]]. The Extension Corps is in Ten's laboratory.
