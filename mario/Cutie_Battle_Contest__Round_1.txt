'''Cutie Battle Contest, Round 1'''  is the seventh level of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 17.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 3 [[Clumph]] and 5 [[Boomerang Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Boomerang Bro, and he knows [[Deny]]. 

The final battle contains 6 Clumph and 2 Boomerang Bros. The enemy captain is a Clumph and he knows [[Rally]].

After the final battle, the player recruits 2 Boomerang Bros.

The battle can be replayed to recruit up to 2 Boomerang Bros.
