The '''Fresh Pasta Bunch''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. In the former game, it is named '''Fresh Pasta'''.

== Description ==
Fresh Pasta Bunches are a restorative item with a different effect between their two appearances. In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', using Fresh Pasta restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 5 [[Flower Point|FP]], whereas in ''Super Paper Mario'', Fresh Pasta Bunch restores only 5 HP, but were also given the effect of curing any character inflicted with [[Poison (status effect)|poison]].  

Fresh Pasta Bunches are usable in a variety of dishes with a significantly better recovery effect.
