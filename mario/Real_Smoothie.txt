'''Real Smoothie''' is an 8-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a smoothie factory, with an [[orange]]-producing machine on the left side and a [[strawberry]]-producing machine on the right.

==Gameplay==
Both characters on each of the four teams have to cooperatively make moothies. The character on the left has to grab the oranges, while the character on the right has to grab the strawberries, and both of them automatically throw the fruits into the blenders. If a character grabs the wrong fruit, the smoothie briefly stops being produced. Any team that has the most fruit in their blender by the end of the 30-second [[time limit]] wins.

==Ending==
Both characters on each winning team does their respective factory continues to operate. Contrarily, both characters on each losing team does their losing animation as their factory shuts down. It is possible for all four teams to win, in which case every character performs their respective victory animation.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|L}} / {{button|gcn|R}}: Grab fruit

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''Throw fruit into the blender! The player on the left takes oranges, while the one on the right does strawberries.''
* '''Advice''': ''Don't grab your partner's fruit by mistake, or you'll lose valuable time.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on the phrase "real smooth."
