'''Cannon Spaces''' are an object in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Description==
If a character lands on a Cannon Space, they get to fire [[cannonball]]s at [[Bowser]]'s [[airship]], damaging it. After Bowser's airship is hit by ten cannonballs, the last character to land on a Cannon Space is awarded ten [[Mini Star]]s before everyone continues to the Homestretch.

==In ''Mario Party 10''==
Cannon Spaces appear only on the board [[Airship Central]], where there are sixteen of them, all on a circular stretch just before the Homestretch. Of the sixteen Cannon Spaces, twelve of them fire two cannonballs each, while the remaining four fire four cannonballs each.
