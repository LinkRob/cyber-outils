'''Simu-Block''' is a red and green block in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
The Simu-Block is a half-red and half-green block. There are two pairs of Simu-Blocks that are found together. Mario and Luigi must hit the Simu-Blocks at the same time to activate the puzzle.
