'''Bēro '''(ベーロ, ''Bēro'') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It is a living Japanese paper lamp that remains still and attacks by sticking its tongue out. This enemy cannot be destroyed, but can be used as platforms. 

Bēro has also reappeared in [[Super Mario-kun Volume 8|volume 8]] of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''. They are handing bad food to Wario.

==Trivia==
*Bēro is based on a Japanese folklore monster, {{w|Chōchin-obake}}, a Japanese paper lamp who comes to life on its one hundredth "birthday".
