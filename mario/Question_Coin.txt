'''Question Coins''' (also known as '''? Coins''') are large coins that appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and its [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|sequel]]. They are usually found out in the open or in bonus rooms. Their appearance is a large rotating golden coin with a ? on both sides.

== Description ==
They cause items to appear when collected. Sometimes, they make a chain of [[Music Note]]s appear (collecting all these can yield a [[1-Up Mushroom]]), or other times they may merely generate [[Star Bit]]s. They may also be found in a chain, where collecting one Question Coin makes another one appears, and collecting the final one in the chain triggers a different item appearance. One example of this is in the [[Gusty Garden Galaxy]], where the coins allow a [[Rainbow Star]] to spawn. This allows the player to destroy the [[Golden Chomp]], revealing a [[Power Star]]. Most of the Question Coins have a time limit, the player has to collect all of the collectibles before time runs out.
