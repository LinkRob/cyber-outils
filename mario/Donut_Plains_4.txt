'''Donut Plains''' is the fourth main level of [[Donut Plains (world)|Donut Plains]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and the [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

== Layout ==
The player starts the level and has to run to where the [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] is before it goes up, there is also a yellow [[Koopa Troopa]] that tries to damage the character while chasing them in its [[Koopa Shell|shell]]. If you go to the top of the pipe. you can either jump down (avoiding the [[bottomless pit]]) and start jumping over the next set of warp pipes (some of them will automatically go up, and some have [[Piranha Plants]] in them). If the player jumps up, they will get the first [[Dragon Coin]] and needs to move on to the set of warp pipes.
