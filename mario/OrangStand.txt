'''OrangStand''' ('''''Palmus Walkum''''') is a special move used by [[Lanky Kong]] in game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. Lanky can purchase a potion containing this move from [[Cranky Kong]] at [[Angry Aztec]] for 3 [[Banana Coin]]s.

==Usage==
To use OrangStand, the player must hold the {{button|n64|z}} button while pressing the {{button|n64|b}} button to have Lanky stand on his hands. OrangStand allows Lanky to walk up steep slopes that none of the other Kongs can do. This ability is needed to help free [[Chunky Kong]] from his cage in [[Frantic Factory]]. Lanky later receives an upgrade to OrangStand known as [[OrangStand Sprint]] that allows Lanky to move very fast and maintain the OrangStand for as long he as [[Crystal Coconut]]s.
