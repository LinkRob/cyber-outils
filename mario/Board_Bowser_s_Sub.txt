'''Board Bowser's Sub''' is the first mission of [[Dire, Dire Docks]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. Completing this mission is required to access [[Bowser in the Fire Sea]]. The objective is to collect a [[Power Star]] aboard [[Bowser's Sub]].

In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', Mario is the only character who can play through Board Bowser's Sub the first time, as the [[Big Star Door]] only allows him to enter if Bowser in the Fire Sea is not yet completed. The mission can later be replayed with either [[Luigi]], [[Yoshi]], or [[Wario]] once he has been unlocked later on.  

==Layout==
Mario has to swim all the way down to a wide tunnel, with a ring of [[coin]]s surrounding it, and then continue down it until he reaches a wide area. Mario must swim all the way up to find Bowser's Sub, then find a way on it. The Power Star is found floating slightly above Bowser's Sub, and Mario can jump at the Power Star to obtain it, ending the mission.
