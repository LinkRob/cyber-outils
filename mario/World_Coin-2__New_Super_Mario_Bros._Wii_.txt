|time=500 seconds}}'''{{world|coin|2|}}''' is the second level of the [[World Coin]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 
==Layout==
This level is a replica of [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' with a few differences such as added [[Star Coin]]s, small obscured areas, a [[? Switch]], the removal of the bonus room and blocks and coins that move and appear (respectively) upon approach. The primary enemies are [[Goomba]]s.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In the beginning of the level, within the fourth pipe is where the first Star Coin is.
*'''Star Coin 2''': In between the third set of the blocks arranged like stairs.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In the air. Getting to a certain spot causes [[Brick Block]]s and [[? Block]]s to appear and create stairs to the Star Coin.
