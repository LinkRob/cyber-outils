'''Goomba Boards''' are a type of [[wooden cutout]] made in the likeness of a [[Goomba]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==In ''Super Mario 3D Land''==
[[File:Goomba Board SM3DL.png|frame|left]]
Goomba Boards are one of three different wooden cutouts that make an appearance, along with [[1-Up Mushroom Board]]s, and [[Pipe Board]]s. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can break a Goomba Board either from being in their [[Tanooki Mario (power-up)|Tanooki]] form and spinning into a Goomba Board using their tail or by [[roll]]ing into or [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] a Goomba Board.
{{br|left}}
==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
Goomba Boards act as obstructions for racers to drive into. They make their ''Mario Kart'' debut in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', in which they appear in [[Piranha Plant Slide]]. This course returns in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', along with the Goomba Boards.

In ''Mario Kart Tour'' driving into a Goomba Board, let alone a wooden cutout in general, causes a character to spin out unless they are under the invincibility effects of a [[Starman|Star]].
