'''Joy''' is one of the four [[vibe]] moves featured in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It is used by [[Princess Peach]] and [[Hoo]].

Several enemies in the game, such as [[Red Koopa]]s, [[Red Paratroopa]]s, [[Bob-omb]]s, [[Para-bomb|Parabomb]]s, [[Piranha Plant]]s, [[Blooper]]s, [[Torpedo Ted]]s, [[Fang]]s, and [[Ptooie]]s are affected by the Joy vibe. Bosses like [[Hoo]], [[Blizzaurus]] and [[Bowser]] can use the Joy vibe.

== Strategy ==
Peach can use the Joy vibe will start to make a cyclone; press {{button|ds|A}} to fly up. This strategy can be used to be immune and clear the smoke away where some areas cause smoke which will cause damage. Joy is also used for reaching higher places.
