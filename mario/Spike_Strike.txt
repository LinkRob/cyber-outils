'''Spike Strike '''is a Mid-boss Battle mini-game. In "Solo" mode, it appears in [[Magma Mine]] at the mid-boss fort. In "Party" Mode, the minigame always appears at the mid-boss fort in [[Magma Mine]] and has a 50% chance to appear at the other boards' mid-boss forts. Its titular boss is an oversized [[Gabon]] named [[Spike]].
==​Intro==
Spike drops down onto a small hill poking above the magma with four chutes dropping down, his entrance shaking the ground a bit. He then spits out four [[Spiked Ball|Spiked Balls]], one at each chute.

==​Gameplay==
In this game, Spike spits out the spike balls; then he stomps to reveal the suggestion between the hammer and the stick. Each of them worth differently:
*[[Golden Hammer]] is worth +3 points.
*[[Hammer|Normal Hammer]] is worth +1 point.
*A Stick is worthless, taking away -1 point.
Picking nothing will also cause you to lose a point. During the first round, there will be enough hammers for everyone, with one being a golden hammer. Afterwards, the number drops.

===​Rage===
When Spike rages, he stomps really hard and the lava flows to shorten your time to select an object.

== Ending ==
Spike falls onto his back and poofs.

== Rules ==
''"Grab a hammer as fast as you can to knock the rolling [[Spiked Ball|Spiked Balls]] back at [[Spike]]! Gold hammers are worth three points, iron hammers are worth one, and branches aren't worth anything!"''

==​Controls==
'''Wii Remote '''​should be held vertically.
*Use {{button|Wii|A}} to select the object.
*Point at the '''sensor bar''' with your {{button|Wii|Wiimote}}.

== Trivia ==

* This is one of three boss minigames from ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' where at least one character is bound to get hurt, the other two being [[Whomp Stomp]] and [[Chain Chomp Romp]]. This is because less players can choose [[Hammer|hammers]] after the first round after Spike sends his balls rolling.
* Picking a branch when Spike sends his [[Spiked Ball|Spiked Balls]] rolling will display a character icon with a sad expression that does not appear anywhere else in the game.
