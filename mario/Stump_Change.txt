'''Stump Change''' is a [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
Donkey Kong rolls on a [[barrel]] and gets a [[banana]]. At end of the stump, Donkey Kong jumps onto a smaller one and eats the banana. The characters, barrels, and bananas then fall from above onto giant tree stump, where the gameplay takes place.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to roll through the bananas to collect them. There is a [[Banana Bunch]] in the middle, and it is worth five bananas. Like with other Donkey Kong minigames, each banana collected is worth a [[coin]] after the minigame. If a character falls off the stump, their barrel breaks, and that character is eliminated. The minigame ends once the 15-second [[time limit]] is up.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Roll

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''Hop on the barrel and roll over the bananas to collect them.''
* '''Advice''': ''If you fall off the stump, your game will end. Stay up there!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on the phrase "chump change," referring to a very small amount of money.
