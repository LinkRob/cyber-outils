'''Gold Boos''' are golden variants of [[Boo|Boo's]] that first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== History ==
===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:Gold Boo 2.png|thumb|Some Gold Boos]]
Gold Boos first appear in ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'' when [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] touch a [[Gold Ring (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|Gold Ring]] inside a [[Ghost House]]. They begin to flee upon turning gold, leaving a trail of coins behind for the player to pick up. Just like all the other golden enemies, they give five coins to the player upon defeat. [[Big Boo]]'s also have the same effect and turn into Gold Big Boos.

===''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''===
A Gold Boo appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' in the Mansion Safe in [[The Enigmansion]], where it acts identically to a regular Boo, and is even named as such, but drops twelve coins after the battle.

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', a gold variant of [[Captain Boo]] is unlocked whenever the Boo amiibo is scanned in the [[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser|''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'' mode.]]

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey'' ===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'', a figure of a Gold Boo appears in the [[Tower of Yikk|Tower of Yikk's]] office, among with other Boo figures like [[Boo]], [[Big Boo]], [[Tail Boo]] and [[Bomb Boo]], unlike the original game.
