'''Camelot Software Planning​''' (originally called '''Sega CD4''' or '''Sonic! Software Planning''') is a game designer company that developed most of the ''[[Mario Sports (series)|Mario Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Golf series|Mario Golf]]'' games, as well as a [[Game Boy Advance]] title ''Golden Sun''. They also created the character [[Waluigi]] for the [[Mario Sports (series)|''Mario Sports'' series]].
