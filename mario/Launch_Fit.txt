'''Launch Fit''', also known as '''Launch Diet''', is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Description==
The Launch Fit is a type of launching board that [[Bowser]] can use to launch into the air in the manner of a [[cannonball]], destroying various obstructions along the way. To use it, Bowser must stand on the Launch Fit and unless his [[Fire Breath]] on a small [[bomb]] on the back of the board to light its fuse. In doing so, the bomb explodes, causing the Launch Fit to launch Bowser all the way over to the other side.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
A Launch Fit can be found in [[Dimble Wood]] and [[Blubble Lake]].
