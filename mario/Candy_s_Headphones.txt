==History==
Candy's Headphones are often found semi-hidden throughout all levels (except [[Jungle Japes]] and [[Hideout Helm]]). Touching Candy's Headphones restore all of the Kong's musical instrument (although, this can also be done by visiting [[Candy Kong]] in her [[Candy's Music Shop|music shop]]). Also, a jingle plays whenever a Kong touches the headphones.

==Trivia==
*Oddly, even though Candy's Headphones appear in [[Fungi Forest]], Candy's Music Shop doesn't appear there itself.
