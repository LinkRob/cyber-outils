The '''Punch-Out!! franchise''' is a series of games starring [[Little Mac]]. [[Mario]]-related media has appeared in many of these games.

Games

* '''[[Punch-Out!! (arcade)]]''' (1984) - [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Cranky Kong|Donkey Kong Sr.]] and [[Donkey Kong Jr. (character)|Donkey Kong Jr.]] can be seen in the crowd.
* '''[[Super Punch-Out!! (arcade)]]''' (1984) - The same four characters can be seen in the crowd again.
* [[Punch-Out!! (Nintendo Entertainment System)|'''Punch-Out!! (NES)''']] (1987) - [[Mario]] can be seen as the referee in this game.
* '''[[Punch-Out!! (Wii)]]''' (2009) - [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] is one of the fighters in the game, and Doc Louis references [[Mario]] in one of his lines.
