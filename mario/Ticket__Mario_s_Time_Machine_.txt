The '''Ticket''' is an item in ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]'', exclusively within PC releases.

==Description==
The ticket is a lecture ticket that grants entry to one of [[Michael Faraday]]'s Christmas Lectures.

==In ''Mario's Time Machine''==
In 1831, Mario receives the ticket from a youth, who had it as a spare ticket, after Mario said that he had never heard one of Faraday's lectures. Meanwhile, [[Charles-Gaspard de la Rive]] lost his own ticket, so when Mario gives it to de la Rive, he thanks Mario and proceeds to further discuss about Faraday and his scientific experiments.
