'''Pink Bloobers''' are a variant of [[Blooper|Bloober]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical appearance===
In their original ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels'' appearance, these Bloobers use the same sprite as the Bloobers but use the same palette as [[Goomba]]s, giving them a light pink-brown color. In the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' [[reissue|remake]], all Bloopers, including the airborne ones, are colored pink. In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]'', the Pink Bloobers are off-white, closer to their original coloration and being visually distinct from Bloobers again.

===Behavior===
They act the same as Bloopers but swim in midair rather than underwater.

==In ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''==
[[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can defeat a Pink Bloober through various means, including [[jump]]ing onto it, throwing a [[fireball]] at it (in their [[Fire Mario|Fire]] form), or touching it while under the effects of [[Invincible Mario|invincibility]]. The player earns 1,000 [[point]]s each time a Pink Blooper is defeated.
