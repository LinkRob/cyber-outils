== Plot for "Boom!! One Shot Kill! A Plan to Join Forces" ==
Mario and Luigi ride onto Yoshi and they approached a castle. Yoshi can read the sign that was in [[Yoster language]], the sign read "HELP". Before they could proceed, [[pipe]]s scattered around them. Bowser summoned through a hologram of the pipe. Bowser told Mario, Luigi and Yoshi that there is no escape because the pipes surrounded them. Bowser summoned a squad of [[Piranha Plant]]s and left. Mario used Yoshi as bait to Piranha Plants, but his plan failed. Mario and Luigi threw fireballs, but the Piranha Plants extinguished the fireballs with watering cans. Mario performed the "SNES Controller Nunchuck" and injured the Piranha Plants. The Piranha Plant transformed into an [[Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant]]. After the Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant performed the "[[Iron Root Kick]]" onto Mario, it opened its mouth to eat the trio. Mario spot its weak point through the uvala. Mario and Yoshi's "[[Mario & Yoshi's Rolling Attack]]" puzzled the Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant and broke his head. Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant falls in defeat. Yoshi and Mario got their bodies tangled, Yoshi said that they should use the technique again.
