'''Flip Panels''' are a type of platform which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
When the green "!" switch of a Flip Panel is pressed, several connected green panels, each depicting a face, are created. [[Mario]] or another character can then move across these panels to travel across [[pit]]s or other hazards, similar to [[Snake Block]]s. Some seconds later, the panels turn dark green, open their mouth, and start to disappear one at a time. When every panel has disappeared, Mario or another character can step on the "!" switch to create the Flip Panels again.

==In ''Super Mario 3D Land''==
[[File:W2-2 Flip Panels SM3DL.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario walking across Flip Panels in [[World 2-2 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-2]] of ''Super Mario 3D Land'']]
Flip Panels appear in [[World 2-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-1]], [[World 2-2 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-2]], [[World 4-5 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 4-5]], [[Special 2-5]], [[Special 6-3]], and lastly {{world-link|s8|crown|Special 8-Crown}}, where they are not used as platforms but rather to spell out a message to the player, reading, "THANK YOU!!"

==In ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''==
Flip Panels appear in the minigame [[Blown Hover]] as an obstacle that players have to avoid.

==In ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''==
Flip Panels retain their functionality from ''Super Mario 3D Land'', although the green "!" button that produces the panels now turns orange upon being pressed. Flip Panels appear in [[Flip Panel Footpath]], [[Secret of the Golden Realm]], [[Mummy-Me on Flip Panel Footpath]], and [[Flip Panel Panic]].

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
Flip Panels appear in the stage [[3D Land]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. They are featured as platforms in the first scrolling section of the stage.
