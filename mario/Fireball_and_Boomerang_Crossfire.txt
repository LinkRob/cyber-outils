'''Fireball and Boomerang Crossfire''' is the first level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 27.

==Layout==
The 1st battle contains 4 [[Fly Guy]]s and 4 [[Fire Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Fire Bro and he knows [[Deny]] and [[Chance]].

The 2nd battle contains 4 Fly Guys and 4 [[Boomerang Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Boomerang Bro.

The final battle contains 4 Fly Guys, 1 Fire Bro, 2 Boomerang Bros, and 1 [[Big Boo]]. The enemy captain is the Big Boo, and he knows [[Rally]] and Deny .

==Possible recruits==

*Fire Bro (Lv. 27)
*Boomerang Bro (Lv. 27)
