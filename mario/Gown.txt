The '''gown''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
The gown is a pale blue gown with a white collar at the neck, and it turns its wearer into a partially visible apparition-like form. The gown is one of [[Professor E. Gadd]]'s inventions, and he introduces it at [[Starbeans Cafe]]. However, [[Luigi]], having a fear of [[ghost]]s, vacuums E. Gadd up into the [[Poltergust 3000]].
