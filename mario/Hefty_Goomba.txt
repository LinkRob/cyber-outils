A '''Hefty Goomba''' (also called a '''Medium Goomba''') is a type of [[Goomba]] that appears in various 2D ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' games. They are bigger than a standard Goomba but smaller than a [[Grand Goomba]].

Hefty Goombas appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. In this game, they are only seen in two levels. The [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1]] Enemy Course and [[World 8-2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-2]]. When stomped on, they split into two standard Goombas. Stomping on a Grand Goomba will split it into two Hefty Goombas. Throwing fireballs at a Hefty Goomba can also take it out.

Hefty Goombas also appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' with the same role as before. They are seen in [[Soda Jungle]] in the level [[Jungle of the Giants]]. They also appear in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' in the level [[Giant Swing-Along]].
