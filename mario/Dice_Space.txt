A '''Dice Space''' is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
A Dice Space is one of the less common spaces. If the player's character lands on one, a [[Dice Block]] appears above their head, allowing them to have another turn, but without using any [[Mushroom]].
