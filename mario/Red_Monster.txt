The '''Red Monster''' wheels are unlockable wheels in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. They have good [[speed]], [[weight]], and [[Off-Road|off-road]], but lack good [[acceleration]] and [[handling]] stats. 

Comparing it to the [[Monster (wheels)|Monster]] tires, the Red Monster's acceleration is a little bit better, yet it is slightly lighter. They are tied with the Monster tires for the best off-road in the game and are also tied with the Monster tires, the [[Slick]] tires, and the [[Gold Tires]], for the worst handling.
