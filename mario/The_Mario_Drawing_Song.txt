The '''Mario Drawing Song''' is a song created in [[w:c:nintendo:Flipnote Studio|Flipnote Studio]] by [[Nintendo]] to promote the “Make Your Own Mario Flipnote” for the [[Super Mario Bros. 25th Anniversary|''Super Mario Bros.'' 25th Anniversary]]. Part of this song was arranged for the [[Special 8]] map theme in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. The Mario Drawing Song also appeared at the end of ''[[The Cat Mario Show]]''. It can also be listened to on ''[[Game & Watch: Super Mario Bros.]]'' by holding the A button on the digital clock.
