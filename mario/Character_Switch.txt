A '''Character Switch''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
A Character Switch is a type of [[P Switch]] with either [[Mario]]'s, [[Luigi]]'s, [[Princess Peach]]'s, [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s, or [[Rosalina]]'s [[emblem]] printed on it: Mario's emblem depicts a red "M" emblem; Luigi's depicts a green "L" emblem; Peach's depicts an emblem of her [[Peach's Crown|crown]]; Toad's depicts his head; and Rosalina's depicts a [[Grand Star]].

The Character Switch can only be pressed and activated by the character whose emblem is shown can press the switch. When the switch is pressed, in most cases a [[stamp]] appears, but the switches can also reveal a [[Green Star]] or a line of [[coin]]s.
