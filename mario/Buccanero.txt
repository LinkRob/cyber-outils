'''Buccanero''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Buccaneros are a variant of [[Bandinero]]s that wield a sword. They have a purple body, a lot of which is covered by a yellow vest with a white stripe in the center. A Buccanero also wears a yellow bandana-cap, gray pants, and yellow shoes.

===Behavior===
If a Buccanero is near Wario, it swings its sword wildly to try and attack Wario. This attack is similar to the [[Kutlass]]es from ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
A Buccanero has a threat rating of two out of three. They are rare enemies, and only appear in the tutorial area, [[The Sweet Stuff]], and sometimes during the boss fight against [[Large Fry]]. If Wario touches a Buccanero from the side, he takes damage, but Wario can defeat one by [[jump]]ing onto it.
