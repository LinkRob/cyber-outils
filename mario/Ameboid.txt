{{nihongo|'''Ameboid'''|プヨメーバ|Puyomēba}}, is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Battle==
This enemy appears to be a {{w|Amoeboid}}, due to its behavior or a [[Goomba]] made entirely of blue-colored gelatin. When failed to be defeated in a single attack, this foe splits into five, all having identical stats in which is considered an insignificant annoying enemy. This foe is strong against jumping attacks, but weak to [[Fire (element)|fire]] attacks.

In the overworld, Ameboid disguises itself as a blue spot. When closer approached, it reveals itself as an Ameboid.
