'''Fort Flaptrap''' is a location in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
Fort Flaptrap is one of [[Lake Lapcat]]'s islands, and it is one of the few to surround the Lakeside [[Giga Bell]]. Fort Flaptrap has a lighthouse on top and several [[Flip-Swap Platform|Red-Blue Panels]], some of which lead down into pits of [[lava]].
