The '''Teeheespresso''' is a type of blend in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Its name is a portmanteau of "teehee" and "espresso."

==Usage==
The blend can be obtained in the [[Starbeans Cafe]]. The player must have 15 [[Woo Bean]]s and 10 [[Hee Bean]]s for the [[Starbeans Employee]] to create it. The blend boosts 6 points on any stat, or 3 points in the remake.
