'''Seth Rogen''' is a Canadian-American actor and filmmaker who voiced [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== Trivia ==
* Bebop in ''Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutant Mayhem'' is also voiced by Seth Rogen, Donkey Kong's voice actor.
* He co stars with [[Donald Glover]] and [[Keegan-Michael Key]] in The Lion King.
