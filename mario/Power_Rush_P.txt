'''Power Rush P''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Effects ==
The Power Rush P has the effects of a [[Power Rush]] except it only affects [[Mario]]'s partners. Power Rush P costs one [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear, and in doing so, Mario's partners are given two extra [[Attack]] points for each one equipped if they are at five or less [[Heart Point|HP]].

The Power Rush P can be bought from the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] for 50 [[coin]]s. Mario can obtain infinite Power Rush Ps at the [[Pianta Parlor]], where it is sold for 34 [[Pianta Token]]s each.
