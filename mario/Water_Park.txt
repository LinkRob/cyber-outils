'''Water Park''' is a aquatic park-themed course in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is the second course in the [[Mushroom Cup]], and second overall in the game.

==Layout==
At the beginning of the race, your driver will enter the entrance for the water/amusement park. It sort of resembles the inside of a submarine. After exiting the submarine area, there will be a right turn towards a trick ramp, but not before passing an anti-gravity booster. 

The driver then drives underwater and has to navigate around (or hit since you're in anti-gravity) a moving submarine. You come out of the water and there is an upwards-left turn. Here, you'll find some item boxes as well as another submarine you have to dodge.

You then enter another underwater section where there is an amusement park ride. After taking a left turn, there are more item boxes to collect before a gliding section that takes you out of the water. You have to dodge a ticket booth and a ferris wheel while gliding before landing and crossing the finish line.
