'''Ringmaster''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. The microgame shares the same music with [[Undercover Agent]] and [[Flask Me Later]].

== Gameplay ==
Three hands will be shaking bells in a pattern. When it comes to their turn, they must hit in a beat on time by hitting on the wrist or shaking the [[Wii Remote]]. If the player does not do their part, the composer will point at them.
*'''Level 1:''' The beat will have a shake.
*'''Level 2:''' The beat will have two shakes.
*'''Level 3:''' The beat will have three or four rapid shakes.
