'''Funky Kong''' is a [[Kong]]. He appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Funky Kong's appearance strongly resembles his [[Funky Kong|video game counterpart]] in artwork for [[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]].

=== Powers and abilities ===
* '''Vehicular Mastery''': Funky Kong is skilled at driving a kart.

== Biography ==
Funky Kong is one of the Kongs that reside in [[Jungle Kingdom]].

== Trivia ==
* Funky Kong's presence in Rainbow Road may reference him being the most used racer in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' for world records.
