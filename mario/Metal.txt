'''Metal Tires''' are a set of unlockable [[tire]]s in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. They are shaped like a four-blade boat rotor and, as their name suggests, have a silver-gray metal running surface. Metal tires add significant weight. This is the same weight as [[Monster Tires]] and [[Hot Monster Tires]], albeit slightly faster. But they also significantly impede traction and acceleration. Your stats are shared with Gold Hoop. In ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', CPU [[Iggy]], [[Bowser]], and [[Dry Bones]] use Steel Drivers, [[Metal Mario]] uses [[Tri-Speeder]] or [[Jet Bike]], and [[Dry Bowser]] uses [[Bone Rattler]] and [[Pink Gold Peach]] uses this tire on the Comet.
