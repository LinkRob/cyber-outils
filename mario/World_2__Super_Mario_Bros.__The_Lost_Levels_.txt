'''World 2''' is the second world in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''. This world takes place at nighttime, and is the first one in the game to do so.

==Level information==
===World 2-1===
The player starts off and go across a bridge. There, they meet up with 4 [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, and find 3 [[Question Block|? Blocks]], 2 containing [[coin]]s, and the middle one containing a power-up. Then, the player finds a couple of [[Brick Block]]s, the middle one containing a [[Star]]. The player can go through the [[Warp Pipe]] under the Stone Blocks, which leads them to a Bonus Room. The player encounters a couple of [[Piranha Plant]]s along the way, and has to jump from pipe to pipe to cross the water. The player must jump in a moving platform, which carries them across the water as well. The player here has two choices; either jump from the green [[Trampoline]], or take the platform. The player finds a Piranha Plant, along with two [[Hidden Block]]s beside it, and a Brick Block containing a [[Beanstalk|Vine]] that leads to [[Coin Heaven]]. The player now finds four ? Blocks, the first one containing a [[Poison Mushroom]], and the other ones containing coins. Another Trampoline can be seen here. The player encounters some [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s here going horizantally. Then, the player reaches solid ground, and a couple more Koopa Paratroopas can be seen here. Now the staircase along with three [[Goomba]]s is shown. The player need to slide down the [[Flagpole]] to complete the level, and the player gains access to World 2-2.

===World 2-2===
The player finds a Koopa Paratroopa right when they enter the level. After, Piranha Plants along with Goombas and Koopa Troopas appears, with three coins floating in the air. The player must jump across a gap, and he or she eventually finds a Piranha Plant here.

===World 2-3===
Like 2-3 of the original ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', this level consists of jumping from bridge to bridge while dodging jumping [[Cheep-Cheep]]s. It also contains many Koopas and an airborne [[Blooper]].

===World 2-4===
On level 2-4, it is possible to get a 1-Up by kicking the Koopa down the narrow passageway near the start of the level and allowing its shell to run into and knock out other enemies. There is a hidden power-up near the staircase before the section of the level with many [[Fire Bar]]s and [[Podoboo]]s and lastly, False Bowser next to the ax. After clearing the level, the player must head to [[World 3 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 3]].
