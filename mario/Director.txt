The '''Director''' is a worker of [[Smithy Factory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He serves under the [[Factory Chief]] and [[Smithy]] himself, but is higher-ranked than the [[Clerk]] and [[Manager]]. The Director is also one of the bosses whom [[Mario]] and his [[party]] battle.

==Battle==
During his battle, the Director is accompanied by [[Poundette]]s.
