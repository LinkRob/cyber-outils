'''Middle Rings''' are a recurring type of object in the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', then later return in its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', followed by ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', which names them '''Checkpoint Rings'''.

==Description==
A Middle Ring marks a [[checkpoint]] partway through most [[level]]s, so if a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] loses a life some time after passing through a Middle Ring, he can restart from where the Middle Ring had been activated. In addition, passing through a Middle Ring increments the [[Countdown Timer]] by ten and turns all onscreen enemies into [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|stars]]. Despite their name, Middle Rings are not always found exactly midway through a level, but sometimes either shortly after the starting point or before the end, including just before a red [[door]] leading to a boss fight. Some levels consist of several Middle Rings, in which case losing a life resets the Yoshi from the Middle Ring he most recently activated. If the player gets a [[Game Over]] or the Yoshi completes the level, the effects of the Middle Ring wears off.

In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and ''Yoshi's Island DS'', if a Yoshi loses a life some time after reaching a Middle Ring, the Yoshi retains his star count (including the extra ten from reaching the ring) and [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] that he had when he activated it. This is not retained in ''Yoshi's New Island'', in which losing a life after activating a Middle Ring instead resets the Countdown Timer to its default number of ten and Yoshi's egg count to zero.
