'''Sweet Treat''' is a special move in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Sweet Treat costs only 1 [[Star Point|SP]] to use, and [[Mario]] acquires it once he activates the [[Magical Map]] at [[The Thousand-Year Door]].

==Usage==
In order to use this move, the player must flick the Control Stick in the left direction to have Mario throw the smaller [[Crystal Star]]s. Also using the cursor, Mario must throw the Crystal Stars at the smaller images of either himself, his partner, or Flowers. Mario must be careful not to hit the Poison Mushrooms or he will be stunned for a few seconds. After the move is finished, Mario, his partner, and his [[Flower Point|FP]] will be healed depending on how many times he hit the images.

There is an upgraded version of this move which is known as [[Sweet Feast]] which is acquired after getting the fifth Crystal Star.

== Trivia ==

* This is the only special move that does not require a crystal star to unlock.
