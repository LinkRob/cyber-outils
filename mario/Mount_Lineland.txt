'''Mount Lineland''' is a location that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It serves as the main setting of Chapter 1-2.

== Layout ==
As the name states, Mount Lineland is mainly a mountain-based level that eventually leads into a small town.

== History ==
=== Chapter 1-2: Afooting in the Foothills ===
After clearing through [[Lineland Road]], Mario and [[Tippi]] continue their quest through Mount Lineland. After scaling the mountain, Mario and Tippi end up in a small town. Mario and Tippi soon meet with [[Thoreau]], the throwing [[Pixl]], who joins them on their quest. Mario and Tippi eventually meet with [[Old Man Watchitt]] in order to have [[Green (Super Paper Mario)|Green]] lower the bridge. He lets down the bridge and Mario and Tippi exit Mount Lineland and into [[Yold Desert]].
