'''Desktop Golf''' is a type of [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is unlocked by completing the quest [[Swimmin' Wimp]].

==Summary==
The Gaddget features a typical golf course-like setting. It involves the player having use five golf balls, all shown in a purplish-velvet bar. The player must  attempt to get these balls inside of the hole, like the rules of typical golf. The hole is on a literal green field. There are also two bushes, one being on the left side and the other being on the right side. Aside that, two areas filled with sand can be found under the green field. If a ball is successfully hit into the hole, it is given back to the player. After all five balls are used up, the Gaddget resets.
