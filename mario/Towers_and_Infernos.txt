'''Towers and Infernos''' (known as '''Sinking Feeling''' in the American version) is an Extra minigame that appears in Mario Party: Island Tour. To be able to play this minigame, the player will need a "?" Block AR Card. The AR card acts like the tower, so if the player rotates the AR card, the tower will be rotated.

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a play on the title of the 1974 film ''The Towering Inferno''. The American English name is derived from the phrase "sinking feeling".
