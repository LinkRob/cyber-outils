'''Twink''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Born in [[Starborn Valley]], Twink ascended to [[Star Haven]] immediately before [[Bowser]] had taken the [[Star Rod]] from the [[Star Spirit]]s. Twink helps [[Mario]] and [[Princess Peach]] throughout the course of the game. Twink frequently relays information that Princess Peach has learned to Mario, often regarding the Star Spirits.

Star Kids in Starborn Valley speak of Twink, often suggesting that he had harbored a relationship with them all prior to his ascension to Star Haven.

Right near the final showdown with Bowser, Twink actually gets into a fight with [[Kammy Koopa]]. He, at first, barely does anything to the elderly Koopa but fortunately, Peach has been focusing her wishes into Twink making him stronger and stronger each turn. Eventually, he powers up to where Kammy Koopa's attacks doesn't even harm him anymore and he knocks Kammy out. Twink also aids Mario during the final battle with Bowser with the [[Star Beam]] attack. After Bowser is defeated, Twink decides to head back home hoping he would become a Star Spirit someday. Twink is also seen again at the very end of the parade where he flies over Peach and Mario one last time before leaving into Star Haven.
