'''Red Electrokoopas''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Like [[Blue Electrokoopa]]s, Red Electrokoopas are quadrupeds, something that [[Koopa Troopa]]s also were in their original appearances. A Red Electrokoopa wears a large, red shell with yellow spots, and it has three spikes on top, between which it can generate blue electricity in the shape of a triangle.

===Behavior===
Red Electrokoopas are found climbing around on [[Chain-Link]]s.
