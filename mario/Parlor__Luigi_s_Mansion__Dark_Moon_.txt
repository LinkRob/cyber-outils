The '''Parlor''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is where Luigi first sees the mysterious [[Spirit Ball]]s. The Parlor is located near the [[Master Hall]].
==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-2: Gear Up!|A-2: Gear Up!]], Luigi must head into the Parlor to obtain a gear piece. He must use the [[Poltergust 5000]] to make the phonograph play and cause two [[Greenie]]s and a [[Slammer]] to appear. Luigi vacuums them up and obtains another Gear Piece.

During [[Gloomy Manor#A-4: Visual Tricks|A-4: Visual Tricks]], Luigi must head into the Parlor after the door to the [[Lab]] disappears. As Luigi heads into the Parlor, he spots Spirit Balls fazing through a grandfather clock and makes it disappear. The fire of the nearby fireplace is put out and Luigi uses it to get into the Lab.
