'''Paper Toads '''are recurring characters and incarnations of [[Toad (species)|Toads]] first appearing in'' [[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

== History ==
Paper Toads once appear coming out of the [[Mysterious Book|magical book]] ending up interacting with the real world. They seem afraid of the real world often hiding in areas throughout the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. The trio ([[Mario]], [[Luigi]] and [[Paper Mario]]) were required by [[Toadette]] to find them during their missions which they can begin at the [[Lakitu Info Center]]. These were required to build the [[Papercraft]]s. After the trio saved all of the Toads, they were rewarded with the [[Trio Attack]]. As the story progresses, most of the Paper Toads befriended their real-life counterparts. Even they had their critical part during the plot being the "Toad Stairs" taking them from [[Mount Brrr]] to [[Neo Bowser Castle]]. Every time there is a Papercraft boss battle, they are seen carrying the Papercrafts from under.
