'''Protobatters''' are creatures with clubs that appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. They are located inside [[Pump Works]].

Protobatters attack by spitting out balls of batter, and hitting them with their club at the bros., which can be countered with a hammer. They also attack by slamming their clubs on the ground, which creates a shockwave that can be countered by [[jump]]ing.

A blue, and slightly more stronger variant known as [[Pwallop]] also appears in the game.
