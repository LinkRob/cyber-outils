A '''Gold Dice Block''' is a type of [[Dice Block]] which first appears in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'', having a different function in each game.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party: Island Tour''===
It is a Bonus Dice Block obtained by whoever finishes a minigame for Bonus Dice Blocks in first place in [[Perilous Palace Path]], or in last place in [[Bowser's Peculiar Peak]]. A Gold Dice Block is rolled alongside a Dice Block, and its sides are each numbered 1 through 6, just like regular Dice Blocks.

===''Mario Party: Star Rush''===
The Gold Dice Block only appears when [[amiibo]] are used. If an amiibo has save data from ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' on it and the game detects a [[Gold Token]] before starting [[Toad Scramble]], [[Balloon Bash]], or [[Mario Shuffle]], a Gold Dice Block is added as an item at the start of the game. A Gold Dice Block can roll either a 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, or a 9.
