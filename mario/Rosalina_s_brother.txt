'''Rosalina's younger brother''' is a character who was first mentioned in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

== History ==
His role in the play is similarly minor to that of [[Rosalina's father|his father]]; he makes one appearance in the [[Rosalina's Storybook|story book]] and is mentioned shortly after the [[Comet Observatory|observatory]] is built. When they were children, he went sledding with [[Rosalina]].

== Trivia ==
In ''[[Rosalina's Story]]'', it is possible to notice that she uses a red cap and her brother a green one, which is very similar to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].{{SMGNav}}
