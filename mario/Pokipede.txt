'''Pokipedes''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Pokipedes are [[Wiggler]]-like caterpillars with anywhere between three to seven segments. A spiked helmet covers all but their last segment. Green Pokipedes sport a green spiked helmet, while Red Pokipedes have a red one, and the former type wears blue boxing gloves, while the latter wears red boxing gloves. 

===Behavior===
Pokipedes move along rails and punch everything in front of them, as well as destroy [[snow block]]s in their path. Red Pokipedes move much faster than Green Pokipedes and can destroy [[Hard Block]]s as well.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''==
Pokipedes the main and titular feature of [[Pokipede Pass]]. A playable character can defeat a Pokipede by either stomping on their single uncovered segment, hitting the segment with three [[fireball]]s, or by attacking them with [[Elephant Mario]]'s trunk.
