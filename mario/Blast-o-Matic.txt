The '''Blast-o-Matic''' is a weapon that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==Description==
The Blast-o-Matic is a large laser gun created by [[King K. Rool]] that is located within [[K. Rool's Mobile Island Fortress]], and it is capable of destroying islands.

==History==
During the events of the game, K. Rool created the Blast-o-Matic in attempt to destroy [[Donkey Kong]] and his friends once and for all. The Blast-o-Matic is always set to point at [[DK Isles]] should Donkey Kong or the others fail on their quest. However, fortunately for the Kongs, [[Snide]] has created a way to at least slow down the Blast-o-Matic involving collecting [[Blueprint|blueprints]] throughout the journey. Collecting all of the blueprints in the game results in the Blast-o-Matic being slowed down by one minute per blueprint. There are 40 blueprints in all in the game and 10 minutes are already added to the time meaning the Kongs have 50 minutes to clear through [[Hideout Helm]] to shut of the Blast-o-Matic. The Kongs can do so by completing challenging mini-games in the Blast-o-Matic generator room. After doing so, the Blast-o-Matic is successfully shut down and K. Rool makes a run for it with his primary goal failed.

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
The Blast-o-Matic returns as K. Rool's [[Final Smash]] under the same name. Staying true to the original, K. Rool operates the Blast-o-Matic by pressing the red button (he does so repeatedly in this game as well) which activates the blaster. Except now, the Blast-o-Matic actually makes contact and blasts any fighters caught in the blast, all while destroying DK Isles at the same time.

==Trivia==
*The Big-o-Blaster from another Rare game, ''[[w:c:banjokazooie:Banjo-Tooie|Banjo-Tooie]]'', is very similar to the Blast-o-Matic as they are both weapons of mass destruction that must be shut down as the primary objective of the game.
*Even after the Blast-o-Matic has been shut down, if the player chooses to quit after beating the game, the Game Over screen will still show K. Rool about to destroy DK Isles with the Blast-o-Matic.
