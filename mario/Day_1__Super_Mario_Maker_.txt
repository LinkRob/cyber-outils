[[File:Super_mario_maker.jpg|thumb|Mario in ''Super Mario Maker'']]'''Day 1''' is the first day when you start creating a course on ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. When you start Day 1, you will 12 objects that you have. If you're creating a little bit of your course, then a message will pop up and it will say: Oh! A delivery truck has arrived! And then it will say: New course elements are now available! (Spend at least five minutes creating a course to queue the next delivery.) It will also say "Or would you like to play a sample course?". You could either press 'Create' or 'Play'. If you press Create, then you will get the new items. If you press Play, then you will be taken to the sample course "Ground". When you complete Ground, then you will unlock the new items as the same thing as when you press Create.
