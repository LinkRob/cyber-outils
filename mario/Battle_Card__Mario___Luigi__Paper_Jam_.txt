'''Battle Cards''' are items which only appear in two ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'', [[Crinkle Goomba]] gives [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] their first Battle Cards. They cost [[Star Point]]s and can heal, damage, grant [[Bros. Point|BP]], give [[Experience Point|EXP]], lower or raise stats, and lower the enemy levels. The brothers acquire the aforementioned Star Points by successfully performing attacks.
