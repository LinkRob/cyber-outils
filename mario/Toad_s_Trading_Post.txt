'''Toad's Trading Post''' is a shop run by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. When the player comes pass by, he/she may refuse or buy one of the items on stock. The player can buy one item what he/she can afford. The player can also choose to view the map before deciding to buy an item or pass by.
