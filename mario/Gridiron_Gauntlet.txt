'''Gridiron Gauntlet''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
Players look around at the crowd while standing on the grid-like area. The [[Chargin' Chuck]]s ram and the players avoid the Chargin' Chucks.

== Gameplay ==
The players have three lives in the grid-like area. Players can push others and dodge Chargin' Chucks.

== Ending ==
The winner(s) do their victory pose on the field/yard.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
