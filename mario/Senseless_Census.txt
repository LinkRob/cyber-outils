'''Senseless Census''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera pans the camera in the train car where all Toads are doing their own thing. The players enter the train car and the game starts.

== Gameplay ==
The players' objective is to find the number of Toads to win the game. The players have a time limit and they can move around to count the number of Toads. After the time runs out, it shows the number of Toads and the player wins with the closest number.

== Ending ==
The winner(s) do(es) their victory pose while other players are talking to each other or a Blue Toad. Winners may be replaced by Blue Toads.

== Controls ==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
*{{button|ns|jc-top}} – Increase count
*{{button|ns|jc-bottom}} – Decrease count
