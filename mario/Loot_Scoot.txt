'''Loot Scoot''' is a [[microgame]]s featured under the [[Anything Goes]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', and it later returns as a boss microgame of [[Wario]]'s [[Mash League]] in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Gameplay==
Wario has to escape from a moving rock while running with a bag of treasure. The player must press {{button|gba|A}} for Wario to perform a [[Dash Attack]] on a wall in his way. If Wario gets caught by the moving rock, the player loses at the microgame.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', Loot Scoot was lengthened due to being a boss microgame. After breaking through walls as usual, Wario then has to break a fist-shaped rock above him, by performing an upper Dash Attack at it. In doing so, Wario somehow [[jump]]s high enough to get onto the upper floor. From there, he has to defeat a few [[Mummy|mummies]] in his path, then jump off a giant [[Springboard]] to cross a very large gap, enabling him to reach the other side. In Levels 2 and 3, there are at least two mummies as well as two walls having to be bashed simultaneously.

===Difficulty levels===
In the ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' iteration of Loot Scoot, Wario has to bash through one, two, and three walls in Levels 1, 2, and 3 respectively.

In the ''WarioWare Gold'' iteration of Loot Scoot, Level 1 features two walls and two mummies, Level 2 features three walls and three mummies, and Level 3 features four walls and four mummies. Every difficulty level features only one fist-shaped rock.
