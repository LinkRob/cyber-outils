}}'''Swinging with Sharks''' is an Item mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the player must swing on a swing and try to land on one of the items that appear on barrels. The height of the swing is the key to getting a desired item as the higher the swing, the further the player goes and the lower the swing, the shorter the player goes.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|a}} - Jump

==Trivia==
*Although this mini-game uses the term "sharks," the sharks of the Mario series are referred to as [[Sushi]]s.
