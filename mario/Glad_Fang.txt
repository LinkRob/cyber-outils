A '''Glad Fang''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Glad Fang is a variant of [[Fang]] imbued with the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] [[vibe]]. It appears more content than a regular Fang, as shown from its eyes.

===Behavior===
A Glad Fang flies around aimlessly, unlike Fangs, which target [[Princess Peach]].

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Glad Fangs are first encountered in [[Ladida Plains 1-7]].
