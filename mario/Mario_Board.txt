The '''Mario Board''' is a board in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
[[File:Mario Board screenshot.jpg|thumb|left|A screenshot of the Mario Board]]
The board is based on some of the earliest levels from the 2D {{series|Super Mario}} installments, most notably [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1]] from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' or the overworld levels in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. It is also based around [[Mario]] himself. The music for this board is a remix of ''Super Mario Bros.''{{'}}s [[Ground Theme]].

The Mario Board displays several [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Block]]s, and rolling hills to the side. A decorative stand showing part of an overworld level from ''Super Mario Bros.'' is in the upper-left corner, and players can hit a [[? Block]] here to acquire an item, including [[Coin]]s, a [[Super Mushroom]], or a [[Mega Mushroom]]. Three models resembling Mario also appear on the board, which depict different [[Dice Block]]s, which can be purchased using Coins upon being landed on.

==In ''Mario Party 10''==
The Mario Board is exclusive to [[amiibo Party]] mode. It can be played on by scanning the Mario [[amiibo]] figure via the [[Wii U GamePad]].

==Board-exclusive events==
===Power-ups===
When passing the upper top-left corner, the player can hit one [[? Block]] out of three to earn a random item: one to three [[Coin]]s, which are spent on a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]; a [[Super Mushroom]], turning the user large and causing them to steal two coins from each opponent while moving around the board; and [[Mega Mushroom]], which has a similar effect to a Super Mushroom but turns the user even larger and gives them 8-10 more coins while moving around the board.

===Block Shop===
Upon landing on an [[Steal Space]], the player has an opportunity to purchase one of the [[Special Dice Block]]s.
