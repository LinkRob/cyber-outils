|first = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}'''Under the Stairs''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. This room can be accessed from the [[Patio]] and has a door leading behind the Fish Tank in the Foyer. It is one of the smallest rooms in Gloomy Manor.
==History==
Luigi can get into Under the Stairs once he acquires the [[Dark-Light Device]]. In [[Gloomy Manor#A-5: Sticky Situation|A-5: Sticky Situation]], Luigi can head out to the Patio and reveal a hidden door that leads to Under the Stairs. From there, Luigi can enter through another door to the [[Foyer]] to get the [[Gem]] that was hidden behind the fishtank.
