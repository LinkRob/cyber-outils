The '''Legendary Dog Dancers''' is a group consisting of fourteen dancing dogs in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
The Legendary Dog Dancers only appear in [[Jimmy P.]]'s [[Canine Crazed]] stage, being the direct parallel of the [[Legendary Cat Dancers]] in [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s stage, [[Feline Fever]].

In the opening cutscene of Canine Crazed, Jimmy P. notices a dog while walking down a sunny street. Jimmy P. gives the dog a bone and continues walking, but he is then followed by a group of dogs, the Legendary Dog Dancers. They begin to dance with Jimmy P. and go to [[Club Spice]] with him.

During the stage, while the player plays through Canine Crazed, four dogs each represent the player's chances during the stage, while one dog is in a mirror ball, where it either dances or falls if the player wins or loses a [[microgame]], respectively.
