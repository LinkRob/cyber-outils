The '''Giant Eggs''' are a massive variation of [[Yoshi Egg]]s that mainly appears in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can obtain a Giant Egg from eating [[Fat Guy]], converting it into a Giant Egg in the process. If a Yoshi throws a Giant Egg, it gives off a shockwave attack that defeats all enemies on-screen, turning them into [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]]. A Giant Egg is the only way to completely defeat an [[Eggo-Dil]] so that it does not return.

There are red Giant Eggs that are only used in the boss fight against [[Baby Bowser]], after [[Kamek]] had turned him into a giant. During this part, Giant Eggs are instead provided by [[Baron von Zeppelin]]s, and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can attack Big Baby Bowser by throwing these Eggs at him, until he is eventually defeated by Yoshi.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 64]]''===
[[File:Yoshi's Valley MK64 Giant Yoshi Egg.png|thumb|left|200px|Yoshi facing the Giant Egg of Yoshi Valley in ''Mario Kart 64'']]
A Giant Egg is featured as an obstacle in [[Yoshi Valley]], near the last stretch before the grassy area and the [[finish line]]. The egg slowly moves around in a circular pattern. If a racer drives into the Giant Egg, it squishes them temporarily. The Giant Egg is hard to avoid, given its large size and how it takes up most of the space in which it moves.

===''[[Mario Kart DS]]''===
In [[Yoshi Falls]], a Giant Egg sits in the middle of a lake, but it has no effect on the race, as it is normally unreachable.

===''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
The Giant Egg returns as an obstacle in the games' iteration of {{classic course|N64|Yoshi Valley}}. The Giant Egg has lost a lot of mass, making it easier to avoid, and some [[Coin]]s now appear in its trail.
