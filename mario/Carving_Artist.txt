{{Infobox Microgame|name = Carving Artist|image = [[File:WWSM Carving Artist.png|thumb|250px]]|games = ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''|genre = [[Ashley]]|info = ''"We're looking forward to seeing your latest masterpiece! But, um, you've only got five seconds to make it."''|forms = [[The Thumb Wrestler]]|song = I'm A Sculptor}}'''Carving Artist '''is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. It is a pun of ''starving artist''.
==Gameplay==
There is some material in front of you. The player must chisel to create a sculpture.
*'''Level 1: '''The material is wood, the sculpture will be a bird or a totem.
*'''Level 2: '''The material is ice, the sculpture will be a bunny or a robot.
*'''Level 3: '''The material is a rock, the sculpture will be a face or a human figure.
