'''Squitter the Spider''' is a spider [[Animal Buddy]] that first appeared in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''. He appears to be an orange spider with black spots on him. He resides on [[Donkey Kong Island]] and is an ally to the [[Kong]]s. He has the ability to spew out spider webs and make them web platforms or just as a weapon to attack his enemies. He cannot defeat enemies by jumping on them, but can use his webs to defeat them. 

In ''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'', Squitter is first encountered in [[Crocodile Cauldron]] where helps [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] cross many levels in that world. He mainly appears in levels of [[Krazy Kremland]] and he has his own level, [[Web Woods]] found in [[Gloomy Gulch]]. He reappears in the pseudo-sequel, ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'' and first appears in the world, [[Krem Cauldron]] and, therefore remains the same.

He reappears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' where he keeps his previous abilities from ''Donkey Kong Country 2/Land 2'' and, once again, helps the primates on their adventure to stop [[King K. Rool|Baron K. Roolenstein]]. He also has the ability to jump on enemies that Dixie and [[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] can defeat via jump. He has a level dedicated to himself, [[Krack Shot Kroc]] where he takes place in the factory. He reappears, once again, in ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'' where he remains the same as all three previous games he has appeared in. This is his most recent appearance as a non-cameo. 

He makes a cameo appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' as a collectible [[trophy]].
