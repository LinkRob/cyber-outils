'''Wild Dino Rhinos''' are an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Wild Dino Rhinos are a variant of [[Dino Rhino]]s, which they have an identical appearance to.

===Behavior===
Wild Dino Rhinos are trained by [[Animal Trainer Shy Guy]]s. When Wild Dino Rhinos are in battle with an Animal Trainer Shy Guy, they attack by charging at [[Mario]] once. If an Animal Trainer Shy Guy is defeated before a Wild Dino Rhino, it enters an enraged state and attacks by charging into Mario twice, [[jump]]ing onto him, and then [[Fire Breath|breathing fire]] onto him. When another Animal Trainer Shy Guy enters the battle to replace the previous one, the Wild Dino Rhinos calm down.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
Wild Dino Rhinos are only encountered in [[the Emerald Circus]], where two of them and an [[Animal Trainer Shy Guy]] are the second group of enemies that [[Mario]] must fight on stage.
