'''World 5-3''' is the third level of [[World 5 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5]] in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its Western conversion, ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. In the original, an albino [[Mouser]] was fought as a boss here, but in ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', [[Clawgrip]] appears as the boss instead.
