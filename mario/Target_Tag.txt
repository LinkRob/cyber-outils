'''Target Tag''' is a 4-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
Each of the characters jump into their respective [[cannon]]s and are shot into the air.

==Gameplay==
The characters must try to touch the [[target]]s as they soar through the air to get the number of [[point]]s displayed on each target they touch—either 5, 10, 30, or 50. The characters can easily push their opponents out of the way while attempting to touch the targets, especially for targets worth more points, as they are smaller. The characters must avoid the large [[Bowser]] targets, as these cost them all of their points. Any characters who end with the most points win.

Target Tag is one of the minigames featured in [[Decathlon Castle]]. Here, the player's objective is to get as high of a score as possible to earn as many points as possible, up to 1,000.

==Ending==
[[File:Target Tag Ending.png|thumb|The ending of Target Tag]]
The minigame ends right before the characters land on a safety [[trampoline]] depicting a cannon and a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]. The scene changes to show the characters perform their respective winning and losing animations.
