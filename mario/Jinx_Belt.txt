'''Jinx Belt''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The item functions like the [[Attack Scarf]], but anyone in [[Mario]]'s party can use it.

== Description ==
When any user equips the Jinx Belt, the item protects them from instant death like the [[Attack Scarf]]. It also increases both their [[Attack]] and [[Defense]] by 27 and their [[Speed]] by 12. The Jinx Belt is obtained after defeating [[Jinx]] in all three battles.

The Jinx Belt can be sold for 800 [[coin]]s, making it one of the most valuable accessories, the other being the [[Ghost Medal]]. Once sold, the Jinx Belt cannot be replaced.
