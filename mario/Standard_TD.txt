The '''Standard TD''' is [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s standard [[kart]] appearing in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. His non-standard karts are his [[Mushmellow]] and his [[4-Wheel Cradle]]. The Standard TD is heavily based of the [[Toad Kart]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', as it has similar shape, style and color. The only difference is that it no longer features the handlebar.

== Statistics ==
[[File:StandardKartTD.png|thumb|150px|right|Data-rendered model of the Standard TD.]]
The Standard TD features great [[Acceleration]], but is [[Speed|slow]] and is farily [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|Light]]. Its [[Handling]], [[Drift]] and [[Items (stat)|Items]] are averagely balanced.

Its weight is equivalent to [[Luigi]]'s [[Poltergust 4000]] and [[Dry Bones]]' [[Banisher]] and it shares its drift with [[Bowser]]'s [[Standard BW]]. It shares its items stat with all other characters' standard karts.

== Successors ==
The [[Standard Kart S]] replaces this kart in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.
