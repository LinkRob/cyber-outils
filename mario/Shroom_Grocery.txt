'''Shroom Grocery''' (temporary called '''Shy Guy's Shop''') is a location that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. 

==History==
Shroom Grocery is an [[Item Shop]] and the first major Item Shop that appears in the game. It is run by a regular [[Toad (species)|Toad Shopkeeper]] and is located in the first area of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. During the events of Chapter 4, a [[Shy Guy]] takes over Shroom Grocery (even renaming it to "Shy Guy's Shop") not wanting to sell items to Mario. However, Mario can simply attack the Shy Guy to scare it off allowing the shopkeeper to regain control over his shop.
