The '''Lushgreen Sea''' is the first sea visited in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is located in the northwest corner of [[Concordia]]. It is to the west of the [[Gulchrock Sea]], north of the [[Color-Full Sea]], northwest of the [[Brrrning Sea]], and west by northwest of the [[Stormstar Sea]].

== Description ==
As its name implies, the Lushgreen Sea primarily contains islands that have an abundance of plant life.

== Islands ==

=== Major Islands ===
There are four major islands:

* [[Rumbla Island]]: An island of artisans. The Bros. are free to discover this island when they reach [[Shipshape Island]] the first time. When the Bros. connect this island, Twistee Island and Raynforest Island can be discovered.
* [[Twistee Island]]: A tourist hotspot known for the Twisten Sprout. Loved by dancers. [[Maykit and Billdit|Maykit]] is trapped here. To discover this island, [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have to link Rumbla Island. When the Bros. reconnect this island with Shipshape Island, Maykit will set her shop here, provided they did not reconnect Raynforst Island beforehand. When they link Raynforst Island afterward, [[Gorumbla]] will go on a rampage.
* [[Raynforst Island]]: An island with a rainforest with rain perpetually falling. Billdit is trapped here. To discover this island, Mario and Luigi have to link Rumbla Island. When the Bros. reconnect this island with Shipshape Island, Billdit will set his shop here, provided they did not reconnect Twistee Island beforehand. When they link Twistee Island afterward, [[Gorumbla]] will go on a rampage.
* [[Florall Island]]: An island with strikingly beautiful flowers that grow everywhere. Reconnecting this island allows this sea's Great Lighthouse Island to be visited. To unlock this island, Gorumbla has to be beaten after linking Twistee Island and Raynforst Island.

=== Great Lighthouse Island ===
As with the next three seas, the Lushgreen Sea contains a Great Lighthouse. To get to the Lushgreen Sea's [[Great Lighthouse Islands|Great Lighthouse Island]], [[Florall island]] has to be linked.

=== Islets ===
Three islets can be found in the Lushgreen Sea. They can be first visited after [[Technikki]] or [[Adaphne]] adds an engine to [[Shipshape Island]].

* Dropplet Islet: Three [[Shower Fish|Shower Fishes]] infected with [[Glohm]] can be found here. They have to be beaten to get the [[Mystery Block]] that holds a Blocky Hammer Wear DX.
* Morsel Islet: This islet contains a course where Mario and Luigi have to use Bros. Panels to get to a [[Mystery Block]].
* Smahl Islet: This area has a Coin Competition Block containing 2,954 [[Coin|Coins]].

== Rare Reefs ==
Three [[Rare Reef|Rare Reefs]] can be found here:

* Weeping-Lion Reef: A reef resembling a lion.
* Pensive Reef: A reef resembling a Concordian person pondering something.
* Squiddish Reef: A reef resembling a [[Blooper]].
