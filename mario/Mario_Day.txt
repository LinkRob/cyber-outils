'''Mario Day''' (also written as '''Mar10 Day''', '''MAR10 Day''', '''MAR10 DAY''', and '''Mario day'''), or '''National Mario Day''', is a real-life event.

==Description==
Mario Day is an annual celebration of [[Mario]] and the {{franchise|Mario}} at large. It occurs on the 10th of March each year because its shortened form, Mar 10, has "10" looking like "IO, hence "Mario."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nintendo.com/whatsnew/happy-mar10-day-have-fun-with-this-stache-of-mario-games/|title=Happy MAR10 Day! Have fun with this ‘stache of Mario games.|accessdate=2023-02-25|date=2021-03-10|author=Nintendo}}</ref> People celebrate Mario Day by either dressing up as Mario, having ''Mario''-themed parties, among similar activities.

While Mario Day was not originally created or declared by [[Nintendo]], it was unofficially celebrated by many ''Mario'' fans. Since 2015, Nintendo has officially observed the holiday and started celebrating annually by promoting ''Mario'' games and holding ''Mario''-themed events.

In the early 2020s, Mario Day had few real-world events from Nintendo and at large due to the COVID-19 pandemic. 

==2015==
In 2015, Nintendo of America posted a tweet on their {{w|Twitter}} account that wished the YouTube channel {{w|Smosh|Smosh Games}} a happy Mario Day and asked what their favorite moment in a ''Mario'' game.<ref>{{cite web|author=Nintendo of America| date=March 10, 2015 | url = https://twitter.com/NintendoAmerica/status/575329015466229760 | quote = Hey, @SmoshGames, Happy #MAR10 Day! What’s your favorite Mario moment of all time? Tell us with #MyMario!|title=Post|publisher=Twitter}}</ref>

Nintendo UK also posted a tweet celebrating the holiday by asking users which ''Mario'' game they were playing on that day.<ref>{{cite web |author=Nintendo of UK |url= https://twitter.com/NintendoUK/status/575250781928034304 | quote =It’s Mar 10. Happy #MarioDay! If you’re playing a Mario game today, let us know which one! |title=Post |publisher=Twitter}}</ref>

==2016==
In 2016, Nintendo celebrated the holiday by sponsoring a number of sweepstakes on Twitter. To enter one such contest, users had to change their profile picture to a given image (shown here) and post about their favorite ''Mario''-related memory using the hashtags "#MAR10Day" and "#SweepsEntry." For each sweepstake, the prizes were: both a [[Super Mario Bros. 30th Anniversary|''Mario'' 30th Anniversary]] T-shirt and a Mario plush toy, a [[Wii U]] Deluxe Set with ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', or a ''Mario'' keychain and a copy of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

A video titled "[[Celebrate Mar. 10 - Mario Day!]]" was released on Nintendo's official YouTube channel. In it, a person in a Mario costume walks around Nintendo's office, turning various objects into ''Mario''-related objects, including hats on workers and screens spelling "Mario." The person also adds ''Mario''-related objects to the office such as several ''Mario'' plushies to a person's desk.

==2017==
In 2017, Nintendo celebrated the holiday by adding a free ''Mario''-themed frame to Facebook and the Messenger Camera that could be used from March 10 at 5 a.m. PT until March 12 at 11:59 p.m. PT., by putting several ''Mario'' games on sale on Amazon, and by having several [[My Nintendo]] rewards for ''Mario'' titles such as a 30% off discount on ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. Nintendo also worked with the [[Starlight Children's Foundation]] by providing new designs for its gowns while making a donation to create 2,000 gowns. [[MAR10 Day 2017 - Mario Quiz|A quiz]] was published that year on the [[Play Nintendo]] website in celebration of Mario Day.

==2018==
[[File:Mario GoogleMapsPromo.jpg|thumb|The ''Mario Kart'' promotion on Google Maps]]
In 2018, Nintendo partnered with Google adding Mario in Google Maps, where the user can have Mario drive around in a [[Pipe Frame]]. Users had to look for a [[? Block]] next to the start button when the start and destination fields were filled.

There was an event in ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' titled "It's All About Mario Event," where all items relating to [[Mario]] are in fixed appearance rates in [[Bonus ? Block#Super Bonus Game events|Super Bonus Game]]. Nintendo also offered a special 50% discount for the full game.

In ''[[w:c:nintendo:Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp|Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp]]'', items from the ''Mario'' franchise were available to craft for a limited time.

==2019==
Nintendo released an episode of Nintendo Minute on its official YouTube channel titled "Mar10 Day Character Bracket: Mario’s Enemies - Nintendo Minute." In this video, series' hosts Kit and Krysta rank several ''Mario'' enemies based on several categories, such as which would be the most annoying pet. Nintendo of America made a celebratory tweet for the holiday asking how fans will celebrate Mar10 Day. Several ''Mario''-themed activities occurred at [[Nintendo New York]] and were sponsored by Frito-Lay Variety Packs.

The event "It's All About Mario Event" returned in ''Super Mario Run'', and the player was given 10 Rally Tickets as a log-in bonus.

In ''Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp'', Mario's hat and outfit were gifted for all players, and several ''Mario''-themed wallpaper and flooring were available for crafting. Several of the furniture from the prior ''Mario''-related event returned, and the Fishing Tourney 12 for the game featured [[Cheep Cheep]]s, [[Eep Cheep]]s, [[Cheep Chomp]]s, and [[Blooper]]s as catchable fish.

==2021==
[[File:Mario Day 2021.png|thumb|Mario Day 2021]]
Aside from various events relating to the [[Super Mario Bros. 35th Anniversary|''Super Mario Bros.'' 35th Anniversary]] event such as those included in ''Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp'' and ''Animal Crossing: New Horizons'', a 35% off sale was held for ''Super Mario Maker 2'', ''Mario Tennis Aces'', ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', and ''Super Mario Party''. The [[Mario Tour (2021)|Mario Tour]]'s starting date in ''Mario Kart Tour'' also coincides with Mario Day.

A special [[Super Mario 3D World (Know Your Nintendo)|''Know Your Nintendo'' video]] was published and features five questions about the ''Super Mario'' series.

==2022==
[[Super Nintendo World]] in Universal Studios Hollywood was announced to open in 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://pokemonblog.com/2022/03/10/mar10-day-2022-the-first-us-super-nintendo-world-is-set-to-open-at-universal-studios-hollywood-in-2023/|title=MAR10 Day 2022: The first US SUPER NINTENDO WORLD is set to open at Universal Studios Hollywood in 2023|accessdate=2023-02-26|date=10-03-2022|work=Pokémon Blog|author=Blogger}}</ref>

Three new LEGO ''Mario'' sets and a LEGO [[Princess Peach]] were announced.<ref>{{cite web|author= NintendoAmerica |url= https://twitter.com/NintendoAmerica/status/1501890635468611588 | quote=Fun-packed new #LEGOSuperMario Expansion Sets and characters are coming later this year, including the fabulous new LEGO Peach! #Mar10Day|title=Post|publisher=Twitter}}</ref>

==2023==
[[File:Mario Day 2023 infographic.jpg|thumb|An infographic posted by Nintendo of America for Mario Day]]
In 2023, Nintendo issued an official motto for the event, "'''Every Day Is a Mario Day'''," used in some promotional material, including a song themed around Mario Day.

On March 1, a ''Mario''–themed party was held at Nintendo New York, featuring special guests Mario, Luigi, and Ryan Kaji from the YouTube channel {{w|Ryan's World}}. Visitors could meet Ryan and play Nintendo Switch games with him.

In ''Mario Kart Tour'', the [[Mario Tour (2024)|Mario Tour]] began on March 7 and concluded on March 21.

The final trailer of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' premiered in a dedicated Nintendo Direct presentation on March 9, 14:00 PT. The movie was also promoted through a cast interview with Fandango and a theme song singalong on ''The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon''. An event promoting the film in ''Super Mario Run'' began on March 10 and ended on May 31.

Wave 4 of the ''[[Booster Course Pass]]'' of ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' was released on March 10.

The "Dry Bowser Castle Battle" LEGO ''Mario'' expansion set and LEGO Donkey Kong were also announced.

==2024==
{{Quote|Oh yeah! Mario time!|[[Kevin Afghani]], 2024 Mario Day video<br>[[File:Mar10 Day Oh Yeah Mario Time.ogg]]}}
From March 3 to April 1, users with a [[Nintendo Switch Online]] membership who played ''[[Super Mario World]]'' on the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Switch Online]] application would earn My Nintendo Platinum Points to redeem ''Super Mario World''-themed icons for their Nintendo Switch profile. A video titled "Mario Through the Years - A Mar10 Day Celebration" was published on Nintendo of America's YouTube channel on March 5, showcasing games in the {{series|Super Mario}} available on the Nintendo Switch at the time.

Several rewards for My Nintendo were made available for Mario Day, such as a 14-day free trial for Nintendo Switch Online available from March 3 to March 17 and sweepstakes for LEGO ''Mario'' sets (from March 5 to April 16) and Princess Peach-themed clothing by BlackMilk (from March 5 to April 23).

A free digital guidebook to ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' was made available on March 5 for users with a Nintendo Account.

"The Bowser Express Train," "King Boo's Haunted Mansion," and "Battle with Roy at Peach's Castle" LEGO ''Mario'' expansion sets were announced on March 9 together with a teaser for LEGO ''Mario Kart'' sets.

A video presentation was published by Nintendo on March 10, where Shigeru Miyamoto and Chris Meledandri announced that Illumination is producing another movie "based on the world of ''Super Mario Bros.''" which was later revealed to be ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. The presentation had further ''Mario''-related included release dates for ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' for the Nintendo Switch, as well as the addition of ''[[Dr. Mario (video game)|Dr. Mario]]'', ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'', and ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' to the [[Game Boy - Nintendo Switch Online]] application.

The online ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' Open March 2024 Tournament was held on March 10 from 12:00 to 18:00 PT, with the top 310 players receiving 1,000 My Nintendo Gold Points.

From March 10 to March 16, physical editions of certain ''Mario'' games were discounted by up to $20 at select retailers.{{citation needed}} A $25 retailer gift card was given upon purchase of a Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch – OLED Model, or Nintendo Switch Lite system between the same dates.

==2025==
''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' has a [[My Nintendo Mario Kart 8 Deluxe Community Challenge|community challenge]] for players to participate in one million laps in overall total among participants from March 4 to March 17 to receive 310 platinum points as a participation award. A 14-day trial period for [[Nintendo Switch Online]] was also issued during the same time.

On March 4, [[Play Nintendo]] website was updated to list older activities related to Mario Day and the ''Mario'' franchise at the top.

Nintendo partnered with the airline company {{w|JetBlue}} to create a customized {{w|Airbus A320 family|Airbus A320}} called the [[Cloudtop Cruiser]].

[[Nintendo Sound Clock: Alarmo]] was released to retailers on March 9.
