}}'''Daffodil Peak''' is a yellow level in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. It has a mountainous environment, and you usually travel through pipes to get from one place to another. There was a legend about a Mountain Sage residing in this area, which turned out to be the yellow Chosen Toad, who needs his head repainted in order to recover from his amnesia.
