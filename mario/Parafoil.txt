The '''Parafoil''' (known as the '''Paraglider''' in the American English version) is a [[glider]] that appears in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart series]]''. It debuted in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

== Description ==
In ''Mario Kart 7'', the Parafoil is colored sky blue with yellow stripes on each side.

As of ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', its design is white in the center, and blue, pink and yellow stripes on each side.

== In {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
=== ''Mario Kart 7'' ===
It gives a boost in [[acceleration]], but lowers [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]]. It shares its traits with the [[Swooper (glider)|Swooper]].

=== ''Mario Kart 8'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ===
In both games, the Parafoil is available from the start. Its stats are identical to ''Mario Kart 7''.

=== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ===
It returns in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', taking its redesign from ''8'' and ''Deluxe''.
