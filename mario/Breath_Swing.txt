'''Breath Swing''' is the [[Star Swing]] of [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Bowser uses his [[Fire Breath]] on the ball and then hits it, launching three [[fireball]]s across the field, one of which is the actual baseball. If a fielder touches one of the fireballs, they get burned. As the baseball slows its descent, the fire engulfing it starts to fade away, revealing the baseball.
