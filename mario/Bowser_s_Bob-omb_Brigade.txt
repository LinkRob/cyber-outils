'''Bowser's Bob-omb Brigade''' is a level in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. It is located in [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]].

== Layout ==
Bowser's Bob-omb Brigade features tank like vehicles and carts with moving spikes on the front. At the beginning, there are Blocksteppers, who make walking on the ground a bit difficult. On most carts, Hammer Bros. and Fire Bros. can be found.

== Collectibles ==
=== [[Green Star]]s ===
*'''Green Star 1''': Near where the first Hammer Bro. is, there's a long item box that gives Bob-ombs. Use these on the nearby large boxes to create steps to reach the first star.
*'''Green Star 2''': Near where a gap comes between the bridge, grab a Bob-omb, and go to the cart with the stone box, and use the bomb to blow it up, revealing the second star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Close to where the boss is, there is another large item box. Ground pound on it to make it go over the edge of the bridge to grab the last star.

=== Stamp ===
[[File:Bowser's_Bob-omb_Brigade_Stamp.png|left]]
*Using a Bob-omb from where you grabbed the last star, throw it at the all behind the pipe to get the stamp.
