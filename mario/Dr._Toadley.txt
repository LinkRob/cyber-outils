'''Dr. Toadley''' is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who appears to be some kind of shrink or fortune-teller. He inhabits [[Toad Town]].

== Profile ==
Dr. Toadley, unlike most Toads, wears a purple robe around his entire torso and head. He has a mask around his mouth, and yellow earrings hanging from his ears. 

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==

=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''/''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|+ ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'']] ===
During the top [[Mushroom Kingdom]] officials meeting to discuss the [[Blorbs]], Dr. Toadley sends a representative, who ends up leaving the before [[Bowser]] inhales everyone. He is later seen at [[Toadley Clinic]] when [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] arrive there to take down the blockade in front of [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. He sends them on a mission to find the [[Star Cure]]s and ultimately the [[Miracle Cure]].

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''===
Dr. Toadley appears in the revamped version of [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]], replacing the role of [[Psycho Kamek]].
