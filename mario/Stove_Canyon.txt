'''Stove Canyon''' is the fourth [[world]] in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. It takes place within a volcanic area, with [[lava]] appearing in every [[level]]. The world's boss is the [[Devil's Head]], and defeating him allows Wario to access [[SS Tea Cup]].
