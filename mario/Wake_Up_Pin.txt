'''Wake Up Pin''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When the user equips the Wake Up Pin, they are not harmed by any [[Sleep]] or [[Mute]] attacks. The Wake Up Pin can be purchased for 42 [[coin]]s at either [[Rose Town]] or in [[Seaside Town]] after [[Yaridovich]]'s occupation there has ended.
