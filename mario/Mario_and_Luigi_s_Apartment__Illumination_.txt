'''Mario and Luigi's Apartment''' is an apartment complex in [[Brooklyn]] that [[Mario and Luigi's Family|Mario and Luigi's family]] resides within in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== History ==

=== ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' ===
After their first failed plumbing job, [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] arrive back at their apartment for dinner, as they are greeted by their [[Mario and Luigi's Family|other family members]]. However, as they settle down, with Mario questioning them if they saw their commercial, both Uncle Tony and Arthur start to make fun of the brothers, while their mother supports the brothers. As Mario askes his father for his opinion, he disapproves with it, thinking that Mario is nuts for resigning from his previous job and taking Luigi down with him. Finally having enough, Mario excuses himself from the dinner table and silently storms to his room, much to the others confusion.

Mario then plays Kid Icarus in his bedroom, before switching over to the news as Luigi enters. Luigi then reassures Mario that he isn't bringing him down with him, before Mario continues to watch the news to discover that a flood is currently happening.

Later in the climax of the movie, the apartment reappears again, as the other family members start to notice rumbling from inside the complex. During Mario and Luigi's [[Super Star (Illumination)|invincibility run]], both Papa and Mama Mario watch from the window of the apartment, before the brothers are eaten by a Piranha Plant, and spat back out, sending them into awe.
