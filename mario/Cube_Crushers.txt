'''Cube Crushers''' is a 2-vs.-2 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. 

==Layout==
The minigame takes place within three ice-filled areas within a refrigerator. The top screen has two mini-maps, one for each team, and is used to see the characters' progress to the end as well as the current area they are in. At the end of each side is a pile of ice cubes with a yellow flag on top, representing the end goal.

==Gameplay==
The objective for both teams is to escape from the refrigerator first, by getting through all three sections. Each section has several [[ice cube]]s in the path. The light blue ice cubes can be destroyed from a single [[punch]], although not the more resistant white ice cubes. Each room has two red buttons at the end, one on each side of the door. When each teammate reaches a red button, they must punch them at the same time to open the door. A team cannot continue to next area until both teammates enter the door.

Some of the light blue ice cubes are [[Question Block|? Blocks]], depicted with a red question mark, and punching one reveals either a purple [[spiky ball]] or a [[Starman|Star]]. The purple spiky ball temporarily encases the character within ice, making them unable to move, but they can be freed if either their teammate punches them or if they press {{button|ds|B}} repeatedly. The Star makes the character temporarily [[Invincible Mario|invincible]] and enables them to break through any ice cube more quickly, including the stronger white ones.

This minigame does not appear in the Simple Set.

==Ending==
The characters on the winning team each do their victory animation on an open door of an ice maker, in front of a pile of ice cubes.
