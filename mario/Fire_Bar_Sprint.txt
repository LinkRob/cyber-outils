'''Fire Bar Sprint''', is a sixth level in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by collecting every [[Star Coin]] in [[Rock-Candy Mines|Rock Candy Mines]]. As the name implies, the course is based around [[Fire Bar]]s.

== Layout ==
The player starts in a rocky area. They come across a giant [[? Block]] and several giant [[Fire Bar]]s. More Fire Bars appear, along with two sets of Brick Blocks that the player must run over. The second Star Coin is located at the end of the first set of blocks. Many more Fire Bars then appear. Beyond them, the player finds the [[Goal Pole]].

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': After some Fire Bars. It will be in the way of the player’s jump.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the first set of Brick Blocks the player must run over, The player must jump to obtain this Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Near the Goal Pole, the player must [[Ground Pound]] on Brick Blocks to obtain the third Star Coin.
==[[List of Luigi sightings in New Super Luigi U|Luigi Sighting]]==
*At the end (after the third Star Coin), the player should Ground Pound on the Brick Blocks before the Goal Pole to reveal an 8-bit Luigi.
