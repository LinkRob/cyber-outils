'''Garlic Dispensers''' are an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
A Garlic Dispenser has the appearance of a red vending machine with a face and a giant head of garlic on top. It sells [[garlic]] to [[Wario]], which can heal some of his health.

Whenever [[Wario]] approaches a Garlic Dispenser, it makes certain gestures to try and allure Wario into spending his money. When Wario [[punch]]es a Garlic Dispenser, he receives a head of garlic in exchange for some of his [[coin]]s, with garlic becoming more expensive in later courses. In some courses, a Garlic Dispenser increases the price of garlic by either five or ten coins for each subsequent one bought.
