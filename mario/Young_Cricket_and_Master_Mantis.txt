'''[[Young Cricket]]''' and his martial arts teacher, '''[[Master Mantis]]''', are a duo in the {{series|WarioWare}}. Both of them first appear in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. Both of them are martial artists, with Young Cricket being a devoted student of Master Mantis's. The two of them are employees for [[WarioWare, Inc.]].
