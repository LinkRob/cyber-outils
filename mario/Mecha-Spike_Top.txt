A '''Mecha-Spike Top''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mecha-Spike Tops are a variant of [[Spike Top]]s, which they resemble, but walk in a robotic manner and have a glossier body.

===Behavior===
Mecha-Spike attack by walking into [[Princess Peach|Peach]] aimlessly.

== In ''Super Princess Peach'' ==
Mecha-Spike Tops are first encountered in the [[level]] [[Wavy Beach 5-1]]. They are also encountered in [[Fury Volcano]] and subsequent [[world]]s. Mecha-Spike Tops also appear in various minigames. They behave similarly to their organic cousins and like them, can grip to the surface of the platforms they're on. Mecha-Spike Tops can only be destroyed from using the Rage vibe. Mecha-Spike Top also has a variant imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]], [[Mad Mecha-Spike Top]].
