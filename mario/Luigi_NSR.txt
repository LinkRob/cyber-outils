'''Luigi NSR''' is a [[w:c:rocketleague:Vehicles|Battle-Car]] released on November 14, 2017, exclusively to the [[Nintendo Switch]] version of ''[[w:c:rocketleague:Rocket League|Rocket League]]''. It is available from the start, and can only be used by Blue team players.

==Decals==
The Luigi NSR is not compatible with any sort of [[w:c:rocketleague:Decal|Decals]] or [[w:c:rocketleague:Paint|Paint]].

==Trivia==
*The in-game files refer to Luigi NSR as "Calzone2".
*When Luigi NSR jump, the sound when [[Mario]] jumps in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' can be heard.
*Luigi NSR can only equip "Luigi" as [[w:c:rocketleague:Topper|Topper]]
*On other platforms, Luigi NSR is seen as an [[w:c:rocketleague:Octane|Octane]] instead.
