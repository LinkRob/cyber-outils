'''Parasol Plummet''' is a 4-player bonus mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, players must try to grab as many [[coin]]s as they can while using [[parasol]]s during their descent. They can fall slowly or drop down quickly to grab the coins and [[coin bag]]s, worth five coins each. They must watch out as [[Hammer Bro]]s. will try and drop hammers on them, causing players not to pick up any coins. As this is a bonus mini-game, everyone wins as long they have at least one coin.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Move
*{{button|n64|a}} - Open/Close parasol
