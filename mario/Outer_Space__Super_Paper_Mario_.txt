'''Outer Space''' is a location that appears in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Super Mario'' series]]. It is the primary setting for Chapter 4 and the main setting for Chapter 4-1.

==History==
=== ''Super Paper Mario'' ===
==== Chapter 4-1: Into Outer Space ====
[[Mario]], [[Tippi]], [[Princess Peach]], and [[Bowser]] all set out for the next [[Pure Heart]] but are suddenly dumped into space. Knowing they need oxygen, Tippi warps everyone back to the [[Flipside]]. After getting a [[Helmet]], Mario and company head back into space where they eventually find a spaceship with a young alien named [[Squirps]]. Squirps knows of the Pure Heart and agrees to take them there (though not fully knowing how to get there). After exploring through space around a bit, the heroes decide to take a quick stop on [[Planet Bolube]].
