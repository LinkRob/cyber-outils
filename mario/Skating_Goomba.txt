'''Skating Goombas''' are a variant of [[Goomba]] featured in the snow-themed locations in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

== Physical description ==
Skating Goomba has the same appearance as a normal [[Goomba]] except their feet are not visible because they ride around in an [[Ice Skate]].

== In the ''Super Mario'' series ==
=== ''Super Mario 3D World/Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury'' ===
Skating Goombas are found in snow-themed levels specifically Snowball Park and Ty-Foo Flurries they skate along the ice minding their own business when they spot the player it will immediately pursue them by skating after them which can hurt the player on contact.

It can be defeated with most forms of attack some of them can even drop their Ice Skate upon defeat.

In the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign, Skating Goombas retain their same behavior but gain a cat-like appearance such as cat ears and whiskers they are only found in [[Slipskate Slope]].
