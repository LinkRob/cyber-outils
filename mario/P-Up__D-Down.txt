'''P-Up, D-Down''' is a [[badge]] which appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. [[Mario]] can wear this badge by paying two [[Badge Point|BP]], and in doing so, Mario lowers his defense points for an increase in his attack points.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:P_up_d_down_badge.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Mario finds this badge at the [[Crystal Palace]], in the upper section. Here, Mario can push the little statue near the end of the palace and reveal a secret hole leading to this badge.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Mario can acquire this badge at [[Riverside Station]]. In addition to being found in Riverside Station, Mario can also obtain the badge an infinite number of times by way of using [[Ms. Mowz]] to steal from [[Elite X-Naut]]s and [[Dark Koopatrol]]s.
