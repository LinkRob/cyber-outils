'''Toad Highlands''' (stylized as '''Toad HighLands''') is a recurring golf course in the {{series|Mario Golf}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]] and later returned in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'' as a downloadable course.

==Description==
Toad Highlands is a basic course with a few water hazards and bunkers. It is located near the mountains of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] or possibly even on them directly. Toad Highlands belongs to [[Toad (character)|Toad]], as per the name. In the ''Mario Golf: World Tour'' version of Toad Highlands, there are Toad-shaped hedges and [[Toad House]]s.

==In {{series|Mario Golf}}==
===''Mario Golf'' (Nintendo 64)===
Toad Highlands is the only default course. It must be played first to unlock [[Koopa Park]].

===''Mario Golf: World Tour''===
Toad Highlands returns as a downloadable course, through either the Mushroom Pack or Season Pass DLC.
