A '''Cyclone Generator'''<ref>''Wario World'' Player's Guide. Page 98.</ref> is an object introduced in a ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' game, ''[[Wario World]]'', before later returning in a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game, ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
Cyclone Generators are weathervane statues designed in the likeness of a bird with brown feathers and green eyes. A Cyclone Generator appears to have a featherless neck.

A Cyclone Generator has the ability to produce a sand [[tornado]], spinning around whatever gets caught within it.

== Game appearances ==
=== ''Wario World'' ===
[[File:Cyclone Generator Wario World.png|thumb|left|200px|A Cyclone Generator in Pecan Sands in ''Wario World'']]
In ''[[Wario World]]'', Cyclone Generators only appear in the level [[Pecan Sands]]. If [[Wario]] attacks a Cyclone Generator, it produces a cyclone that causes Wario to spiral upward and therefore reach higher areas that he cannot get to otherwise.

=== ''Mario Superstar Baseball'' ===
In ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', Cyclone Generators are featured as one of the gimmicks in [[Wario Palace]] alongside [[Chain Chomp]]s. If the baseball lands near a Cyclone Generator, it produces a cyclone, in which the ball spins around for a few seconds, then the cyclone disappears and flings the ball in a random direction.
