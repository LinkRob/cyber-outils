'''Clubosaurs''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description==
A Clubosaur is a triceratops that wields a club. A Clubosaur can alternate between being two-legged and four-legged, being the latter if it is not holding a club.

===Behavior===
A Clubosaur can charge up for a few seconds and then go on a brief rampage, swinging its club frantically. A Clubosaur is invincible while using the attack. After being punched by [[Wario]] three times, a Clubosaur loses its club and becomes four-legged. In this state, the Clubosaur uses a different attack in which it charges at Wario.

==In ''Wario World''==
Clubosaurs are only found in [[Excitement Central]]. Every Clubosaur starts off holding a club, protecting it from taking damage from any of Wario's frontal attacks. After Wario hits a Clubosaur three times, it loses its club. After three more hits, the Clubosaur lies on the ground stunned, and Wario can pick it up. Upon doing so, Wario can then defeat the Clubosaur by either throwing it at another enemy or using a [[Mad Move]] on it.

Clubosaurs have a few behavioral variants, as all of them have almost the same behavior, including [[Skelosaur]]s in [[Horror Manor]], [[Gatorbaby|Gatorbabies]] in [[Wonky Circus]], [[Polar Bear]]s in [[Shivering Mountains]], [[Grizzly|Grizzlies]] in [[Beanstalk Way]], [[Masked Clubber]]s in the [[Mirror Mansion]], and [[Club Mummy|Club Mummies]] in [[Pecan Sands]].
