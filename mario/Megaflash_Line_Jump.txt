'''Megaflash Line Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. These stickers are more powerful versions of the [[Line Jump]] sticker, a sticker that allows [[Mario]] to attack all enemies with a repeating jump attack.

== Appearance ==
The Megaflash Line Jump sticker looks like a combination of the [[Flashy Line Jump]] and [[Big Shiny Line Jump]] stickers in terms of size and background color. It takes up nine sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].
