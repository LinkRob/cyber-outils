'''Flower Jar''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
Flower Jar is one of the [[Flower (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Flower]] items that increases [[Mario]]'s maximum [[Flower Point|FP]], specifically by three. Flower Jars are often found by defeating certain bosses.
