'''Magma Flow''' is one of the courses in [[Star Sprint]] mode of ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Layout==
The object is for players to avoid obstacles along the way, including fire and [[Bullet Bill]]s being fired from a cannon. When encountering a burned portion of the road, the player must use the command "Mario Party" via the [[Nintendo GameCube Microphone]] to call on allies to cool the road. After continuing past more obstacles, the player's character stumbles upon a locked door, and have to use the "Mario Party" command once more for the allies to come and knock the door down. After this is the [[finish line]].
