'''{{world|5|5}}''' is the fifth normal level of [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. This level is based upon [[Bullet Bill]]s, and also includes [[Snake Block]]s. 
==Layout==
[[Mario]] begins the level next to [[Snake Block]]s that can be jumped on to reach a [[Warp Pipe]]. Jumping onto the platform to the right of the Snake Blocks causes a group of [[coin]]s to appear under the pipe. When the pipe is entered, Mario enters an area with a [[Bill Blaster Turret]] and a group of [[? Block]]s over a Snake Block. Another Snake Block with a Bill Blaster Turret and a [[Coin Block]] above it is then found. Mario then arrives at a Bill Blaster Turret found next to a [[Brick Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. Another Bill Blaster Turret is found, surrounded by two rising walls of Snake Blocks. Mario then arrives at the [[Checkpoint Flag]], which is nearby four ? Blocks, one of which containing a power-up. Two Snake Blocks with [[Note Block]]s in between them are encountered, along with a Bill Blaster Turret. A [[Red Ring]] is found above Note Blocks, in between two Bill Blaster Turrets. More ? Blocks and a Bill Blaster Turret are found, followed by a [[Banzai Bill Cannon]]. The [[Goal Pole]] is then found.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Early in the level, the player must climb on the first [[Bill Blaster Turret]] to make the first Star Coin appear.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the midpoint flag, the player must enter into the green pipe between the two Bill Blasters to be blasted into the sky. The Star Coin is at the end of this sky area.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At the end of this level, where the [[Banzai Bill Cannon]] is, the last Star Coin can be found. The player must jump on a [[Bullet Bill]] to avoid falling into a pit.
