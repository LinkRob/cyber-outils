'''Graffiti Ball''' is the [[Star Pitch]] of [[Bowser Jr.]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Bowser Jr. uses his [[Magic Paintbrush]] to cover the baseball with paint before throwing it at the batter. During the process, the screen is covered with a splat of paint, somewhat like the effect of the [[Blooper]] item from the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]], making it harder for the batter to hit the baseball headed their way. The baseball does not travel fast, and can be hit with proper timing.
