|first = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}The '''Secret Pocket''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is argubly the smallest room in Gloomy Manor and it is located in the [[Common Hall]] between the rooms leading to the [[Lab]] and the [[Parlor]].
==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-2: Gear Up!|A-2: Gear Up!]], Luigi has to go into the Secret Pocket in order to obtain a Key to unlock the way into the [[Master Hall]].
