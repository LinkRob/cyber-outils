The '''1-2-3 Dice Block''' is a special item which first appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and reappears in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. It can be obtained by landing on a [[Dice Block Space]] or [[Spin Space]]. It can be also found in the [[Toad House]].

== Description ==
The player can use the 1-2-3 Dice Block to roll from 1 to 3.

== In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' ==
=== Boo's Horror Castle ===
The [[Scaredy Rat]] used this Dice Block in the [[Captain Event]] of [[Boo's Horror Castle]].

=== Blooper Beach ===
It was also used in the two Captain Events of [[Blooper Beach]] by the player's choice.

=== Bowser Station ===
If [[Bowser]] intervenes the event, he will give the captain the 1-2-3 Dice Block leaving it with its bad luck in the third Captain Event of [[Bowser Station]].
