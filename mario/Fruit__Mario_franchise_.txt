'''Fruits''' (also named '''Apples''', '''Nuts''', and '''Yoshi Fruit''') are a unique type of [[fruit]] in the {{franchise|Mario}} often eaten by [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]. They are usually red in color, and often yield some type of item. Berries first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

== In {{series|Super Mario}}==
=== ''Super Mario World'' ===
[[File:Red Berry SMW sprite.png|frame|left]]
There are three types of Berries, and [[Yoshi]] can eat one either by extending his tongue out at it or by [[jump]]ing at it. Yoshi can eat berries for the value of one [[coin]].

The standard Berries are named '''Red Berries'''. If Yoshi eats ten of them within a [[level]], he lays an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] which a [[Super Mushroom]] hatches from. In the remake ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]'', a different power-up hatches from the egg depending on the Yoshi's color: [[Red Yoshi]] hatches a [[Fire Flower]], [[Blue Yoshi]] lays a [[Cape Feather]], and [[Yellow Yoshi]] lays a [[Starman]].

The second variation is [[Pink Berry|Pink Berries]], and eating two of them lays a [[Yoshi Cloud]] that releases ten [[Bonus Coin]]s that can be collected for a [[1-Up Mushroom]].

Lastly, there are [[Green Berry|Green Berries]], the most rare variety encountered only in the level [[Funky]]. Eating a Green Berry adds 20 seconds to the time.

=== ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' ===
Berries specifically appear in levels with Yoshi. Eating five red Berries causes Yoshi to produce an item that Mario can use to progress through the remainder of the level.

The beta version featured a [[Yoshi Cookie]], the equivalent of five Berries.

=== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' ===
Here, the berries are red, and referred to as '''Fruit''' in the game manual. Like in ''Super Mario World'', Yoshi can eat Fruit by either touching it or from extending his tongue out at it. A difference from before is that doing so releases [[Star Bit]]s.

==In ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''==
Fruits appear in [[Yoshi's Mountain Race]] and give a brief speed boost to the player's Yoshi who ate it. Three of them are placed in front of a player's character and their Yoshi if they initiated the minigame.
