'''Chaindeliers''', originally named '''Kinklinks''',  are a species of [[Chain Chomp]]s that act as supports for chandeliers. They appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

During the beginning of the game, [[Mario]] fights [[Bowser]] on top of chandeliers held by Chaindeliers. When Mario returns later on, the Chomps are replaced with [[Chandeli-ho]]s.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Their newer name, "Chaindelier," is a portmanteau of "chain" and "chandelier."
