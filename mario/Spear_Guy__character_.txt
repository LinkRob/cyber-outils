'''Spear Guy''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' and a representative of the species of the [[Spear Guy|same name]]. Spear Guy has the same appearance as his species.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Spear Guy is found in the [[Spear Thicket]], where he wishes to challenge a character to a duel, but only if they have a mustache, otherwise Spear Guy deems them unworthy of being his opponent. As such, only [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can challenge Spear Guy to a duel, starting the [[Mustached Hero!]] quest, not [[Princess Peach]] or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. The duel takes place in the minigame [[Tank-Down]], and the reward for winning is being allowed to keep the minigame.
