A '''Shine Replay''' occurs in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' when you return to a mystery level where [[Shadow Mario]] takes [[F.L.U.D.D.]] and have to get the [[Shine Sprite]] without it. After completing the Shine Replay, you will get another shine. Usually, in Shine Replays, you will be able to use [[F.L.U.D.D.]], and it often is a red coin challenge.

==When Occur==
*After 1-3
*After 1-6
*After 2-2 (Blooper Surfing replay)
*After 2-4
*After 3-1
*After 4-2
*After 4-6
*After 5-2
*After 5-4
*After 6-6
*After 7-5
