The '''Linguini Empire Colosseum''' is a large, spacious colosseum located in the [[Linguini Empire]]. It is a one-time location that only appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Great Gladiator Gig]]." [[Brutius Maximus Grouchimus]] acts as the guardian of the Linguini Empire Colosseum.

Just as the Linguini Empire is based on the {{w|Roman Empire}}, the Linguini Empire Colosseum is based on the {{w|Colosseum}} of {{w|Ancient Rome}}, although the Linguini Empire Colosseum features more of cylindrical appearance, with an opening on top and several smaller openings around the side of the building.
