The '''DEF-Peak Badge''' is a type of [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be bought from [[Gritzy Caves]] for 700 [[coin]]s. Equipping the item increases its wearer's [[Defense]] to 150% in battle for three turns, although the wearer faints afterward.
