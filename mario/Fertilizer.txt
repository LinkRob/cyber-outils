'''Fertilizer''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==History==
The Fertilizer obtained in the the middle of the south-east corner of [[Nimbus Land]], after [[Mario]] takes an invisible path to a [[Beezo|Shy Away]] and corners it. The Shy Away gives Mario a Fertilizer in exchange for allowing it to leave. A Fertilizer, as well as a [[Seed (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Seed]], can then be taken to the [[Gardener]] in [[Rose Town]], which excites him because he had been looking for both items for a long time. The Gardener takes both the Fertilizer and Seed and puts them in a nearby pot to water. A [[beanstalk]] then grows out, allowing Mario to reach two [[Lazy Shell]]s.

Both the Fertilizer and Seed are obtained only once, so discarding either one makes it impossible to obtain the Lazy Shell.
