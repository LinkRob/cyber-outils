'''Cinder Toad''', named '''Hinopio''' in the original game, is a supporting character in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|Nintendo Switch version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Cinder Toad is a tall, muscular adult [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who wears a black vest, sunglasses, and a beard and mustache, making him resemble a stereotypical biker. Cinder Toad's head is also black with red flame patterns on the underside, and he has a heart tattoo on his left shoulder.

==Biography==
Cinder Toad formerly lived at the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], but moved to [[Barrel Volcano]] to run his own shop.<ref>''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' Player's Guide, page 91.</ref>

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[File:SMRPG Cinder Mart.png|thumb|left|200px|Cinder Toad in ''Super Mario RPG'' for the Nintendo Switch]]
On the advice of [[Queen Nimbus]], [[Mario]] and his party visit Cinder Toad before they proceed to face the [[Czar Dragon]]. Cinder Toad runs the [[Cinder Mart]], which sells weapons and armor, and there is also a usable [[inn]].
