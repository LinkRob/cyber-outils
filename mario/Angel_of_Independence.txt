The '''Angel of Independence''' is a real-world landmark and a column in downtown [[Mexico City]]. It was built to honor those who fought for the Mexican Independence. The Angel of Independence appears in all versions of ''[[Mario is Missing!]]''.

==In ''Mario is Missing!''==
The Angel on top of the column had been stolen by [[Koopa Troopa]]s, causing the column to close to visitors, so [[Luigi]] went to find the Angel and return it. His reward for doing so was 1,500 dollars plus a bonus of 2,400 dollars.
