Toadstool's ??? is a rather unique item that can only be encountered once in the game. Toadstool's ??? can be found in [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]]'s room between a blue chair and a fireplace. Later, when Toadstool joins the party, if Mario finds Toadstool's ???, Toadstool will scold at Mario for going through her things.
