'''Mort T.''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who bears an exact resemblance to the average member of his species.

==History==
Mort T. runs the [[Toad House]] in [[Koopa Village]]. There is a [[letter]] found in [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]] that can be delivered to him which turns out to be from his [[Mort T.'s wife|wife]]. He mentions that he loves his wife dearly and gives [[Mario]] and [[Parakarry]] a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] for their troubles.
