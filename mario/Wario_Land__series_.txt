'''''Wario Land''''' is a series of seven platforming games starring the anti-hero of the series [[Wario]]. The games were released primarily on the [[Game Boy]] series of handhelds, with the exception of ''[[Wario World]]'' and ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]'', which were released on the [[GameCube]] and [[Wii]], respectively.
