'''Star Points''' are recurring items in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and serves as the series' version of [[Experience Point]]s for this game and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Appearing after an enemy is defeated, this item allows [[Mario]] to level up once he receives 100 Star Points. Once Mario gains 100 Star Points, he has the option to [[level up]] his [[Heart Point|HP]] by five, his [[Flower Point|FP]] by five, or his [[Badge Point|BP]] by three.

The amount of Star Points given to Mario depends on the difficulty of the enemy. As he continues to level up, the Star Points he receives lessens.

==Trivia==
*The [[Shadow Queen]] is the only final boss of the series to give out a Star Point at the end of the battle (as most final bosses do not give any form of experience points).
