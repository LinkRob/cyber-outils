'''Awesome '''is the fourth level in the [[Special World]] in the game ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Overview==
Awesome consists of mostly slippery terrain covered with [[Beach Koopa]]s, [[Rex]], and Yellow Koopas, leading into a platforming section with [[Paratroopa]]s, [[Banzai Bill]]s and [[Cheep Cheep]]s. There is a [[Starman]] located in a [[? Block]] near the end that makes the last section easier. After reaching the Giant Gate, the player can access [[Groovy]].
