'''Para-Podoboos''' are a [[Wings|winged]] variety of [[Podoboo]]s found in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. They behave much like [[Zigzag Podoboo]]s in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', as they fly diagonally and bounce off of walls, but instead of sinking into lava, Para-Podoboos now bounce off the surface of it.
