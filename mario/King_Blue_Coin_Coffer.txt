'''King Blue Coin Coffers''' are Water/Water attribute enemies encountered in as early as World 7-10, and can also be found in World 8-6. A King Blue Coin Coffer may also randomly appear in the boss battle of Special World course ★World 7-8. As teammates, their Awoken Skill is '''Water Boost''', and may provide RCV+ bonuses when depowered.
