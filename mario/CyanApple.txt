A '''Cyan Apple''' is an item found in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
One is found on a tree in the [[Overthere Stair]]. It restores 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]. If [[Princess Peach|Peach]] tries to eat this fruit, she will shrink to about [[Mario]]'s size when he has been shrunk by [[Dottie]]. After a few seconds, the effect wears off Peach.

If one of the Apples is given to [[Saffron]], she will make it into [[Sky Juice]].
