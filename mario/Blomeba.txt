A '''Blomeba''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are stronger versions of [[Growmeba]]s which can be found in a few locations, such as [[The Bitlands]] and [[Floro Caverns]]. Blomebas can duplicate themselves like their weaker counterparts. However, its clones are also capable of duplicating. In order to defeat the Blomebas, the player must attack and destroy the original and then the clones will fall as well.
