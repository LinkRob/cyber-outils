'''White Magikoopas''' are enemies which appear in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
As the name suggests, White Magikoopas are simply Magikoopas who wear a white robe. This kind of Magikoopa, however, is one of the most powerful, having the ability to heal his allies. He has a couple of different attacks so [[Mario]] should attack these guys first in the battle. A few of these pests can be found in the [[Mt. Lavalava]] area as well as the dark halls of the [[Crystal Palace]].

In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Mario can find one of these guys while battling his way through the [[Glitz Pit]]. Their fighting ability doesn't change, nor does the uncanny ability to heal himself and his allies.

==''Minion Quest/Bowser Jr.'s Journey''==
White Magikoopas are obtainable Ranged minions in [[Minion Quest]] and [[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]. Their [[Special Skill]] is [[Feed the Speed]], which raises the [[Speed]] stat of any unit close to them; this is changed to a [[Defense]]-raising Skill in Bowser Jr.'s Journey.
