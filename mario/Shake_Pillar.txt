'''Shake Pillars''' are an object in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Description==
Shake Pillars are obstructions that [[Wario]] encounters throughout his journey. Wario can only move a Shake Pillar by performing either an [[Earthshake Punch]] or a [[Super Smash Attack]]. Upon being activated, a Shake Pillar slides up or down for a short while before returning to its initial position. A Shake Pillar can lift Wario upon rising, often allowing him to reach otherwise inaccessible areas. If Wario is squished by a Shake Pillar, he is temporarily [[Flat Wario|flattened]], but a Shake Pillar can destroy enemies and certain objects by crushing them.

Shake Pillars vary in design between levels, although they sometimes even have differing designs within a level. However, a consistency with every Shake Pillar is that they bear an exclamation mark. Shake Pillars also vary in size and movement, but they otherwise function the same.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Shake Pillars are found in most [[level]]s except [[Subwarine]] and boss levels. The levels [[Whoopsy Desert]] and [[Disturbing Tomb]] feature pyramid-shaped Shake Pillars with a hollow center, and raising them allows Wario to access to the inner room that typically has [[Coin Bag (Wario Land: Shake It!)|coin bags]] or a [[Max Fastosity Dasherator]].
