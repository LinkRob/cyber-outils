==Appearance==
Bozzo is a young [[Flip-Flopian]] that is tan in color.

==History==
Bozzo lives in [[Yold Town]] with his grandfather, [[Old Man Watchitt]]. Bozzo is often very curious and hyperactive which leads to him getting into trouble. As stated by [[Tippi]], being the grandson of the mayor of Yold Town, Bozzo may become mayor in the future.
