|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Storm''' is a special move that appears in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by a few enemies and bosses in the game namely the [[Elemental Crystals|Earth Crystal]].

==Effects==
When Storm is used, a large, wavy column of electricity is shown over the targeted character. This attack can deal moderate to high electrical damage. The unused enemy, Mastablasta, was going to use this move.
