A '''Favorite Fruit''' is a game mechanic in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Description==
Each [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has at least two Favorite Fruits, which can be either [[apple]]s, [[banana]]s, [[grape]]s, [[melon]]s, and [[watermelon]]s. All Yoshis have melons as one of their Favorite Fruits. If a Yoshi eats their Favorite Fruit, they receive three times as many [[Heart (Yoshi's Story)|hearts]] and health than is granted by other fruit.

The Favorite Fruit of a Yoshi is often similarly colored to them, such as how [[Red Yoshi]] likes apples and [[Yellow Yoshi]] likes bananas. [[Black Yoshi]] and [[White Yoshi]] have every fruit as their Favorite Fruits. 

The Yoshis prefer the [[Lucky Fruit]], which is chosen at random when starting a new game, over their Favorite Fruits. The Lucky Fruit rewards eight hearts and fully restores the [[Smile Meter]].
