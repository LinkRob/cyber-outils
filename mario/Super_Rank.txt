The '''Super Rank''' is a rank that Mario's partners can achieve once they have been powered-up with a [[Super Block]]. This was first introduced in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. This allows them to use more moves in battle and their attack power increases as well. Getting to the Super Rank in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]] ''is now different. Now, Mario must speak with [[Merlon]] who lives next door to [[Professor Frankly]] in [[Rogueport]] and give him [[Shine Sprite]]s in order to power up his partners. Unlike in the previous game, Mario's partner's HP also increases.
