'''A Quick Dip in the Sky''' is the seventh level of Meringue Clouds in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Spinning Spirit House]] and its own completion unlocks [[Snaking Above Mist Valley]].

== Layout ==
This level is set in a cliff area. The first section is inhabited by several red [[Koopa Troopa]]s, while the second area has two circling [[Water Bubble]]s that surround a small piece of land, along with two [[Big Piranha Plant]]s. After reaching the [[Checkpoint Flag|checkpoint]], [[Mario]] will need to climb a bunch of giant bubbles while fending off against [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]]. A [[Warp Pipe]] is located at the very top. This will lead the player to the [[Goal Pole]] area, where a single [[Koopa Paratroopa]] is found.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' Before the Midway Point, it can be seen moving around the Big Piranha Plants. The player must defeat them to obtain it.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' Seen under a group of Koopa Paratroopas, inside a fake wall.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' Between two Fuzzies in the last section before the Goal Pole.
