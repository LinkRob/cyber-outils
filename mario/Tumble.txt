'''Tumble''' is a "magical die" that the fake [[Millennium Star]] brought to life,<ref>''Mario Party 3'' North American instruction manual, page 8.</ref> replacing [[Toad]] as the host of ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tumble is stout with a round head, upon which is a blue die with white pips. Because he lacks arms or legs, his head floats in the air beside and above a pair of white gloves and large red shoes.

===Personality===
Tumble is typically cheery and optimistic, but in Party Mode of ''Mario Party 3'', he sometimes acts worried whenever the location of the [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] changes, worried that the characters are unable to reach the Star, even if it is nearby.

==History==
===''Mario Party 3''===
[[File:Tumble2.png|thumb|''Mario Party 3'' model]]
In ''Mario Party 3'', Tumble not only explains [[minigame]]s and controls but also manages the setup of a Battle Royal or Duel Board. Whenever the [[Fake Millennium Star|Millennium Star]] moves, Tumble also shows the players the new location of it. He hosts minigames in [[Duel Mode]], excluding those from [[Game Guy]].

In [[Story Mode]], Tumble and the fake Millennium Star both give out a specific [[Star Stamp]] to the player whenever the have won at a [[board]].

At the end of the game, the top of Tumble's dice cap opens to reveal the real Millennium Star. After the credits, a blue die is shown landing near a "Mario Party" board game, implying he had returned to his original form.

===''Mario Party Advance''===
[[File:Tumble MP Advance sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'', Tumble makes a reappearance, this time as the host of the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode. He guides the player character ([[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]), informing them of basic mechanics such as moving around in their car and the types of [[space]]s. Comparable to Duel Mode, Tumble also hosts regular minigames in the mode, either at the end of certain turns or by landing on a [[Mushroom Space]], and he gives the player two [[mushroom]]s if they win the minigame. If the player runs out of mushrooms, Tumble notifies them, followed by a [[Game Over]].

Tumble also suggests areas where the player can start new quests. Until their first quest is complete, the playable character is restricted to a specific starting location: Mario to the [[Goomba House]], Luigi to the [[Mushroom Library]], Peach to the [[Jungle Game Hut]], and Yoshi to the [[Boo Cemetery]]. Tumble prohibits them from visiting other areas until these respective quests are finished.

In the Japanese version, after completing a quest, Tumble informs the active character that the day’s adventure is over and prompts them to record the events in their diary, encouraging them to do better the next day.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Tumble's name is derived from how dice tumble after being tossed.
