'''Wario's Warehouse''' was a series of articles formerly on Nintendo of Europe's website during the early 2000s. They were co-written by Mark Green and Richard Moulton in the real world,<ref>https://pixelatron.com/blog/about/</ref> but the articles themselves are written from [[Wario]]'s perspective. The main purpose of each article was to provide information on a game released around the time of the article's publication, although they also consisted of unique facts on Wario and [[Waluigi]]. One of the last articles was Wario giving gameplay advice on ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==List of articles==
The articles are listed in the presumed chronological order in which they were posted on Nintendo of Europe's website.
