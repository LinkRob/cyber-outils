'''Origami Rocky Wrench''' is an origami variant of [[Rocky Wrench]] who appear exclusively in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. They are ordinary Rocky Wrenches who were folded and brainwashed by [[King Olly]] to attack other people.

==History==
Origami Rocky Wrenches appear exclusively in the beginning of the [[Spring of Rainbows]] cave. In the overworld, they function how normal Rocky Wrenches did in the previous two ''Paper Mario'' games, throwing wrenches at [[Paper Mario|Mario]] if he gets near. Mario can dodge the wrenches by either jumping over them or destroying them with his hammer. If Mario leaves the area, the origami Rocky Wrench will go back to its hole.

In-battle, origami Rocky Wrenches have three attacks, Pass Belt, Wrench Throw, and Spanner Throw. When using Pass Belt, the origami Rocky Wrenches go under the ground to pop up and launch Mario in the air, doing damage each time the origami Rocky Wrenches launch him. When using Wrench Throw, the origami Rocky Wrench throws a standard wrench at Mario. When using Spanner Throw, the origami Rocky Wrench throws a golden colored wrench in an arch (similar to the hammers thrown by [[Hammer Bro]]s.).

==Trivia==
*According to their description, origami Rocky Wrenches seem to attack people unprovoked. However, they seem to have a personal vendetta against Mario particularly.
*In the concept art, it is revealed that origami Rocky Wrenches lack any legs.
