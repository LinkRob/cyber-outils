The '''Mecha Castle''' is a level in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'' as well as the first one of the final chapter, [[P. 6-Finale]]. Mecha Castle is widely considered to be hardest of the castle levels on the final chapter. It is home to many [[Sword]]s and other machinery, a lot of which is hazardous.
