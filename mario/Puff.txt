'''Puff''' is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who lives in the [[Mini-Game House]] within [[Village Square|Mushroom Village]] in ''[[Mario Party]]''. She has a crystal ball, and looking into it shows a list of the available [[minigame]]s that can be replayed.
