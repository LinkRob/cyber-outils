The '''Carrot Kart''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

==Description==
The kart resembles a [[Wikipedia:Formula One|Formula One racecar]] with a [[Wikipedia:Carrot|carrot]] theme. It is equipped with the [[Slick]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

==In ''Mario Kart Tour''==
The Carrot Kart is a High-End kart, introduced in the 2019 [[Paris Tour]]. It is the signature kart for [[Shy Guy (Pastry Chef)]] and [[Nabbit]]. The Carrot Kart increases the duration of [[Mini-Turbo]] speed boosts and the points given for performing one. There is a variaton of the Carrot Kart, named [[Radish Rider]].

==Trivia==

*The Carrot Kart is one of the several karts with a design influenced by the Forumla One racecar. The others are the [[B Dasher]] first appearing in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', the [[Sprinter]] first appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', the [[Circuit Special]] debuting in ''Mario Kart 8'', the Radish Rider and the many color variations of the '''B Dasher Mk. 2''' appearing in ''Mario Kart Tour''.
