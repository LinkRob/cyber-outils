'''The Sweet Stuff''' is a pirate ship owned by [[Captain Syrup]] in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
The Sweet Stuff is the first area visited by [[Wario]]. It only consists of a tutorial level, [[Aboard the Sweet Stuff]], and a shop run by Captain Syrup, the [[Pirate Shop]]. The shop takes place within a cabin of the ship.
