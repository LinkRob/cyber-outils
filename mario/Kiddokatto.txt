'''Kiddokatto''' (キッドカット)  is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It is a toy soldier that acts like a [[Goomba]], walking back and forth and attack by simply walking into Mario, damaging him. It can be defeated with any attack. It is similar to a [[goomba]], just faster.
