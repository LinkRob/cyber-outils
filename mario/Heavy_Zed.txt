'''Heavy Zeds''' are a species of [[owl]]s that assist [[Mario]] during his journey in the [[Tree Zone]] in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. Heavy Zeds are generally found asleep, but if Mario approaches one, it wakes up. A Heavy Zed has a flat head that Mario can stand on to have it carry him to another location.
