'''Mari-Lui Badge''' is one of the [[badge]]s featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The badge's name combines those of [[Mario]]'s and [[Luigi]]'s, but with the omission of the "o" and "gi" at the end of their names, respectively.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Mari-Lui Badge can be bought from [[Little Fungitown]]'s clothes shop for 190 [[coin]]s. Equipping it increases [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s [[Power|POW]] rating by 20 and grants the [[BP-Auto Gain]] special effect. In the Japanese version, the Mari-Lui Badge is more than three times as expensive, with its cost being 600 coins.

In the remake, the Mari-Lui Badge is instead obtained at the [[Guffawha Ruins]]. The Mari-Lui Badge was given a higher POW rating of 38, and its special effect was changed to [[BURN Counter]]. The Mari-Lui Badge can be sold for only three [[coin]]s.
