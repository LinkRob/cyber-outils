'''Heart Pants''' is a clothing item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', in which it appears in [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]]. The Heart Pants can only be equipped by either [[Baby Mario]] or [[Baby Luigi]], and in doing so, that baby's [[HP]] is increased by 10 while their [[Defense]] is increased by 5.
