'''Jelly Ghosts''' are a gelatinous variant of [[Boo]]s encountered in the [[Jelly Pipe]] in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. A Jelly Ghost hides within the floors and ceilings, and if a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] approaches, it comes out to try and damage the Yoshi.
