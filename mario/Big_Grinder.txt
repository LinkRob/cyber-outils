'''Big Grinders''' are an object in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==Description==
Big Grinders are a larger counterpart of regular [[Grinder (obstacle)|Grinder]]s, and aside from being a larger size, Big Grinders share an appearance with them. Big Grinders act the same as regular Grinders, and like them, Big Grinders can only be defeated by touching it while under the [[Invincible Mario|invincibility]] effects of either a [[Starman|Super Star]] or an [[Invincibility Leaf]]. Big Grinders tend to come from the ceiling or from underwater and chase [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] for a while before retreating.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''==
Big Grinders appear throughout {{world-link|3|castlen2|World 3-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}}. They also appear in the [[Third Course (Impossible Pack)|third level]] of the [[Impossible Pack]].
