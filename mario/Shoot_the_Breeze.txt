{{Infobox Microgame|name = Shoot the Breeze|image = [[File:WWSM Shoot the Breeze.png|thumb|250px]]|games = ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''|genre = [[Ashley]]|info = ''"Hit the targets to knock 'em down! You've got unlimited ammo, so fire away! {{button|wii|A}} or {{button|wii|B}} Fire"''|forms = [[The Remote Control]]}}'''Shoot the Breeze''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. It is a pun of "shoot the breeze", a casual chat. [[Can Shooter]] is a mini-game which is an extended version of this microgame.

==Gameplay==
The objective of the microgame is to shoot all objects onscreen. If time runs out while some objects are not shot, the player loses the mircogame. If the player shoots all objects before time runs out, the player wins the microgame.
*'''Level 1:''' The player must shoot two cans.
*'''Level 2:''' The player must shoot three cans, or a moving mechanical mouse.
*'''Level 3: '''Shoot the robot four times to get it offscreen.
