'''World Bowser''' is the eighth and final main world of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It follows after [[World 7 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 7]]. It is a large amusement park built in the likeness of [[Bowser]]. Unlike its predecessors, this world is not ruled by the [[Sprixie Princesses]] at all. There is a volcano in the back, and it sits high above the main map. World Bowser contains eight standard courses, two Boss Arenas, a Mystery House, a [[Toad]] House, and [[Bowser's Castle]]. The world is powered by the [[Sprixie]] Princesses, who are liberated when the world is completed.

==Bosses==
*[[Pom Pom]] (Encountered in the world's train level, titled The Bowser Express)
*[[Motley Bossblob]] (Can be encountered in the second boss stage, Motley Bossblob's Encore.)
*Queen [[Hisstocrat]] (Found in Hisstocrat Returns.)
*[[Meowser]] (Encountered in the game's final level, The Great Tower of Bowser Land)
This world is the only Story Mode world without an Enemy Blockade.
