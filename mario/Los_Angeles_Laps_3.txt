'''Los Angeles Laps 3''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', first appearing in the second Los Angeles Laps Tour, which took place in 2021. Despite the number "3" in its name, it is really [[Los Angeles Laps]]' second route, with [[Los Angeles Laps 2]] (which was released seven months after 3 was released) being the actual third route.

== Layout ==
Racers on this course start just before the four-way intersection, making a left turn near the Tongva Park and then a slight right as they go under a [[Los Angeles Laps 2|bridge]] and then left to go through the Los Angeles Convention Centre. The road curves right under the convention centre's canopy, followed by a slight left curve. There, the racers find a [[Glide Ramp]] that they must use to [[Glider|fly]] over the Dodger Stadium. Coins can be found here, arranged to form the outline of a baseball. After the gliding section, the racers make two left turns before continuing down a curvy road towards Two Rodeo Drive; a [[Dash Mushroom]] can be used to cut the corner at the start of the curvy road. Following Two Rodeo Drive, the racers make a sharp left turn, going straight at a three-way intersection and arriving at the [[Finish line|starting line]] just afterwards.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the shortcut is grassy. While most of the first section sticks to [[Los Angeles Laps 1]], when the racers reach the four-way intersection, they will be forced onto this route to finish up the section, with section 2 starting under the bridge, which was given a Mario Kart logo on the side that faces the pier. When the racers have travelled through this route and have reached the four-way intersection again, they will be forced to go straight, diverting onto [[Los Angeles Laps 2]]. A ramp replaces the original version's starting line.
