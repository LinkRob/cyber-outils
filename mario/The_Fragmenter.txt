'''The Fragmenter''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use The Fragmenter.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', The Fragmenter resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Fragmenter deals around 30 to 40 damage. When the Super Effect triggers, it can inflict [[Honey (status effect)|Honey]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use The Fragmenter as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it after purchasing the Pixel Pack DLC.
