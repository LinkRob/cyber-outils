'''Shadows''' are a recurring species in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]].

==Description==
Shadows are described as beings of pure darkness and tend not to have a true physical form. Shadows are beings that can control or manipulate darkness and tend to live over a long period of time. Shadows could include beings of nightmare (such as [[Antasma]]), beings of evil (such as the [[Shadow Queen]]), or beings of pure darkness (such as [[Shadoo]]).

Shadows could also include reoccurring characters in the series that can turn into a being of Shadow by a special method. A good example of this is [[Bowser Jr.]] who is able to turn into [[Shadow Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' by the use of the magic paintbrush. [[Fawful]] is another example who manipulated the [[Dark Star]] until becoming [[Dark Fawful]] and, eventually, a being of [[Dark Star Core|pure darkness itself]]. The [[Cosmic Clone]]s from the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy series]]'' are also good examples of Shadows that are enemies.
