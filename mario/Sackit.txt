'''Sackit''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Sackits are thieving enemies which are encountered on [[Star Hill]] and in [[The Trial Course]] of [[Bowser's Keep]]. They are stronger versions of [[Crook]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Sackits only attack by hurling knives at one of [[Mario]]'s party members. A Sackit may sometimes decide to [[flee]] from battle.
