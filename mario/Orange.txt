An '''orange''' is a type of citrus fruit from [[Earth]]. It appears in a few games of the {{series|Mario Party}}.

==Other appearances==
[[File:Orange Honeycomb Havoc MP2.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Mario Party 2]]'', oranges appear on the tree in the [[minigame]] [[Honeycomb Havoc]].

In ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', oranges appear in the [[Bowser]] minigame [[Fruits of Doom]] as one of the potential fruits on Bowser's list.

[[Donkey Kong Bananza]] has cubic oranges found in the Resort Layer under the planet where Pauline and [[Kong Family|the Kongs]] live.
