'''Jigsaw Jumble''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''Mario Party: The Top 100''


==Gameplay==
*Find the missing pieces in each of the five puzzles. Be the first to complete them all to win!
*If you find the correct piece, place it in the picture instantly. If you don't know anything to pick after three seconds, just go trial-and-error. In [[Time Attack]] mode, you need to place only correct pieces to get the bonus objective cleared.

==Controls==
Hold the Wii remote vertically.

{{System Buttons|wii|A}} - Select puzzle piece
