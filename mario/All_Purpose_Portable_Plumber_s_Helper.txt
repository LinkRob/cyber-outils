'''All Purpose Portable Plumber's Helper''' is a one-time item which appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Mighty McMario and the Pot of Gold]]." It is a {{w|swiss army knife}}-like device briefly used by [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] to cut a chain keeping [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] attached to [[King O'Koopa]]'s throne.
