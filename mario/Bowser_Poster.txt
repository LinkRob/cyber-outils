The '''Bowser Poster''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Bowser Poster is a [[poster]] featuring a green checkered background with [[Bowser]]'s face. Activating the Bowser Poster changes the title screen to have a green checkered background with flying balloons resembling Bowser's head.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The Bowser Poster can be bought for 1,500 coins in Challenge Land.
