{{Nihongo|'''Plush Crush'''キャッチャーパニック|Kyatchā Panikku|Catcher Panic}} is a 1-vs-3 [[:Category:Minigames|minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

== Opening ==
Three players are shown outside of a toy machine, with the solo player inside of the toy machine, with the camera zooming in on the solo player as multiple toys of [[Bowser]], a [[Boo]], a [[Goomba]], a [[Cheep-Cheep]], a [[Whomp]], and a [[Shy Guy]] drop down.

== Gameplay ==
For solo play, the player must avoid being crushed by one of the toys, which the other players will try to pick up, lasting 30 seconds. If the player survives 30 seconds without being squished, they will win the minigame, but only one toy is enough for the team to win the minigame.

For the three other players, they must co-operate to drop a toy on the solo player.

== Ending ==
If the team wins, they'll do their winning animations, with the toy that crushed the solo player remain in the same position. If the solo player wins, they'll do their winning animations with three different toys behind them.

===Group (3 players)===
*'''Rules''': ''"Drop a toy on *insert opponent name* to win! Work with the other players to trap and squish the target."''
*'''Tips''': ''"It takes time to drop a toy, so work with your teammates to cut off *insert opponent name*'s escape routes."''
