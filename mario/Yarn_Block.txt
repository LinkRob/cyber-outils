'''Yarn Blocks''' are a type of object in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Description==
Yarn Blocks are purple [[block]]s depicting a silk ball on the front, and often block a path. They can only be broken by [[Ball o' String Wario]] rolling into them, therefore enabling [[Wario]] to progress further. Yarn Blocks are comparable to [[Snowman Block]]s in that both are a type of block that can only be destroyed from one of Wario's reactions while he is rolling uncontrollably.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Yarn Blocks make an appearance in [[The Grasslands]], [[The West Crater]], and [[Tower of Revival]]. Additionally, there are objects in [[Desert Ruins]] and [[Beneath the Waves]] that act the same as Yarn Blocks despite lacking a yarn ball icon.
