The '''Thwomp Candy''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
It is a blue [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] with a picture of a rock on the wrapper. When a player uses it, they transform into a [[Thwomp]]-like stone head of themselves. Should the eater bypass another player whilst in this state, that player loses half of their coins. Unlike all other blue-colored Candies, this Candy does not allow the user to roll multiple [[Dice Block]]s. It can be found on [[Goomba's Booty Boardwalk]], [[King Boo's Haunted Hideaway]] and [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]]. It typically costs 15 or 20 [[coin]]s.
