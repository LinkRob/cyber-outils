A '''Mame Block''' (otherwise known as a '''Present''') is one of the three [[Mametchi]]'s special [[Items (stat)|Items]] stat in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''. The item works in a similar approach of a [[Banana]] when the player uses it. When a driver runs over the item, the player will spin out for a moment and lose time during the race.
