A '''Sheep Attack''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. 

==Effects==
A Sheep Attack is like a [[Lamb's Lure]] except it affects all enemies onscreen instead of just one. Sheep Attack has similar artwork to the Lamb's Lure except with a more menacing facial expression, likely owing to how it is more effective. Using a Sheep Attack turns all enemies on the screen into sheep, which then fly away, ending the battle. Like the Lamb's Lure, the Sheep Attack is not effective against bosses or larger enemies. A Sheep Attack can be obtained by using a Lamb's Lure 48 times.
