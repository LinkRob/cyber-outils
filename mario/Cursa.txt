{{Nihongo|'''Cursa'''|クルサ<ref>{{cite web|url=https://topics.nintendo.co.jp/article/810b91f0-e3b3-4292-a06a-a6fb93718570|title=宇宙を舞台にマリオとラビッツが戦略バトル『マリオ＋ラビッツ ギャラクシーバトル』は今冬発売。|accessdate=2022-03-11|date=2022-06-28|work=Nintendo}}</ref>|Kurusa|Cursa}} is the main antagonist of ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''. It is  a mysterious and malevolent entity who wishes to cause the galaxy to be thrown into chaos<ref name="SOH website">{{cite web|url=https://www.ubisoft.com/en-au/game/mario-rabbids/sparks-of-hope|title=Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope|accessdate=2022-10-12|work=Nintendo|quote=Cursa's evil force is running rampant throughout the universe. It wants to consume the mysterious Sparks—uncanny and powerful creatures formed by the fusion of Lumas and Rabbids—and it threatens to destroy all who stand in its way.}}</ref>, and consumes [[Luma]]-[https://raymanpc.com/wiki/en/Rabbid Rabbid] hybrids called Sparks to pursue such goals.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Cursa is a tall entity who possess a red eye (though it's not known if it has two eyes and only shows one of them). It wears a purple cloak, and can project ghostly hands called Astral Arms to attack its enemies. When its vulnerable side is shown, its eye is blue.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FN48p_QXBUk|title=Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope - Story Trailer - Nintendo Switch|accessdate=2022-10-12|date=2022-09-30|work=YouTube}}</ref>

[[File:MRSOH Cursa true form.png|left|thumb|Cursa's true form.]]
Once [[Rosalina]] freed herself from Cursa's control, its true form is revealed. It is a floating purple entity with a face that has a mask with a close resemblance to a [[Rabbid]].
===Personality traits===
To pursue its own goals, Cursa will never  hesitate to kill anyone who will stand in its way.<ref name="SOH website" /> In addition, it is largely cruel and evil; and will always accept any challenge of killing anyone.

==Biography ==
{{spoiler|hide}}
Cursa was once [[Megabug]]. [[Megabug]] was defeated in the process by [[Mario]] and his team. Despite being defeated, Megabug's fragment remained as it floated through the galaxy. As it merged with the stellar debris, it regained it feelings. With a desire to grow, it consumed light energies from the galaxy leading to the rebirth of Cursa.<ref name="Rosalina's backstory">{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=be-JY7CIS24|title=Mario + Rabbids: Sparks of Hope - Rosalina's Backstory|accessdate=2022-10-19|date=2022-10-19|author=YTSunny|work=YouTube}}</ref>

Cursa was intrigued by the powers of the light energy produced by the [[Luma|Lumas']] joy with the [[Rabbid]]s that they was drawn to the [[Comet Observatory]]. While approaching the observatory, it masked its presence that [[Rosalina]] could not sense its arrival. When Rosalina noticed Cursa's arrival at the last moment, they shielded the Lumas and Rabbids from Cursa. While the Rabbids and Lumas were saved, resulted the Rabbids and Lumas to merge into [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Sparks]]. In the meantime, Rosalina merged with Cursa. Despite Cursa being able to control Rosalina, it can feel her rebelling against the union in hopes of counting on Mario and his crew to save her and the Sparks. Cursa's goal is to gather the Sparks and absorb their energy to crush Rosalina's will. Cursa then created an army consisting of corrupt Rabbids, brainwashed minions of [[Bowser]] and [[Spark Hunter]]s.<ref name="Rosalina's backstory"/>
{{endspoiler}}

==In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
Cursa makes its appearance against Mario, Beep-0 and Rabbid Peach. The player cannot fight against Cursa, but against the Goombas it brainwashes.

===Battle===
Cursa appears as the final boss of ''Sparks of Hope''. It has three phases for its defeat. The battle against Cursa is fought with the entire party being available, albeit with three on each battlefield.

====Phase 1====
The first phase of the battle pits the gang against Cursa's arms. These arms will float over one target, counterattacking Team Jumps and attempts to jump from jump pads, but their main weakness is the spikes that can be spawned from using said jump pads. If it tries to attack someone who positions themself near said spike, the Astral Arm will take damage rather than actually attacking.

====Phase 2====
Cursa is fought in phase 2, where their body can be targeted. Cursa's attacks include an energy beam that can cause Exhaustion.
====Phase 3====
During this phase of the fight, [[Rosalina]] will break free of Cursa's control. She reforms her body and generates three gargantuan copies of her wand, which hover in between Cursa and the heroes. Attacking the wands will grant energy to the wands passed over to Rosalina; the stronger the attack, the greater the boost. The wands will reach their maximum charge after around 6000 to 7000 damage's worth of energy. Cursa still uses the energy beam from before. When Rosalina's gauge is fully charged, she will use the energy to attack Cursa.
