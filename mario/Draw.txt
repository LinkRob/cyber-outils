A '''Draw''' is an event in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It usually occurs if none of the characters win a [[minigame]], but there are cases of it occurring if all characters win. Draws are in every ''Mario Party'' game except for a select few and act similarly to the Tie event in the series; their ending screen has the word "Tie!" or "Draw!" appear, but sometimes, such as in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', everyone is declared the winner. 

There are certain games in the ''Mario Party'' series where the draw does not appear, including ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'', ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'', and ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''. If a draw occurs in any of the aforementioned games, all of the characters remain in the same place while nothing happens.
