==Appearances==
===''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''===
In this game, Steel Kegs can be only found inside of a mill of [[Fungi Forest]]. [[Chunky Kong]] must destroy some Steel Kegs within the time limit to get a [[Golden Banana]].
