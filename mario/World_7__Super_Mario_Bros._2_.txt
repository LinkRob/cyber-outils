'''World 7''' is the seventh and final world in the game ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', following [[World 6 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]]. Taking place high above the clouds and in the sky, this is where [[Wart]] resides.

==Level information==
===World 7-1===
{{main|World 7-1 (Super Mario Bros. 2)}}
The level begins with the player having to climb a ladder. Instead of dodging [[Albatoss]] dropping [[Autobomb]]s over blocks that blow up, creating pits, hitch a ride on the back of an Albatoss heading left. After jumping over multiple columns, the player will reach a plant, which, if plucked, will spawn a rocket that will take the player to the next area in the level. From here, the player will have to dodge [[Snifit]]s, [[Shy Guy]]s, [[Tweeter]]s, and one [[Ninji]] to advance to the next area. This area involves climbing multiple ladders, dodging multiple [[Spark]]s, some [[Hoopster]]s, and another Snifit until reaching a door, which leads to the end of the level. Here the player fights a green [[Birdo (species)|Birdo]].

===World 7-2===
{{main|World 7-2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)}}
In World 7-2, the final stage of the game, it takes place in a massive castle belonging to none other than Wart. This is the longest level in the game, and it features multiple fights against [[Birdo]]. Also, it features a fight with [[Hawkmouth]] at the end, which comes to life and attacks the player before allowing them to advance to the final room, where Wart is fought. The Hawkmouth can be defeated by throwing the [[Mushroom Block]]s in the room at it. Hitting it three times immobilizes the Hawkmouth, allowing the player to enter it and head to Wart's room. Wart attacks the player by spitting bubbles. He must be defeated by throwing [[vegetable]]s that come from the [[Dream Machine]] in the room into his mouth when he opens it to spit bubbles. Once this is done six times, Wart is defeated, and the good [[Subcon (species)|inhabitants]] of [[Subcon]] can be freed.
