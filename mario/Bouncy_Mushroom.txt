'''Bouncy Mushrooms''' are objects that can be bounced off of.

== In ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' ==
In ''New Super Mario Bros.'', these mushrooms first appear in [[World 1-5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-5]]. They are pink, and enemies can walk on them without bouncing.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' ==
In these games, ''Bouncy Mushrooms ''(or [[Trampoline Mushroom]]s) appear in [[Mushroom Gorge]]. The Bouncy Mushrooms are red, and in ''Mario Kart 7'', there is a blue mushroom which will allow the player to [[Glider|glide]]. There are also some green [[Mushroom Platform]]s. In later titles, these mushrooms are remodeled to look like different objects, but act the same as Bouncy Mushrooms.
