'''Wario Dance Company''' is the boss [[microgame]] of [[Tiny Wario]]'s stage, [[Forever Form Baton]], in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. As such, it is the final mandatory boss microgame, but the penultimate boss microgame if counting [[Boom Box (WarioWare: Smooth Moves)|Boom Box]] from [[Orbulon]]'s optional stage, [[The Secret of the Balance Stone]].
