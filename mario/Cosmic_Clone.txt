'''Cosmic Clones''' are galactic copies of [[Mario]] that are very similar to [[Cosmic Mario]] which appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Behavior==
If Cosmic Clones appear in a level, they mirror [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s moves exactly, albeit delayed by a few seconds. Even if Luigi is being played, the Cosmic Clones still resemble [[Mario]]. The clones copy Mario and Luigi's every move, even if the landscape changes in between their target and the clones themselves (such as a platform falling away). Clones also wall jump off of thin air if the wall disappears. Cosmic Clones automatically disappear when Mario or Luigi complete the [[level]].

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
Levels can spawn a neverending Cosmic Clones, although Mario or Luigi usually die before too many fill the screen, or the game lags too much after when so many appear on screen. If Mario activates a [[Sling Star]] or a [[Power Star]], the clones disappear. 

==In ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''==
[[File:CosmicClone3DLand.png|left|thumb|x150px|A Cosmic Clone from ''Super Mario 3D Land'']]
Cosmic Clones also appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' only in levels of the Special worlds. They often chase Mario or Luigi throughout certain levels especially [[Ghost House]]s and Castle levels. Cosmic Clones appear in two sizes: big and small. These Cosmic Clones will keep on chasing Mario or Luigi until they finish a certain level or reach a certain point in a level. They will also do everything Mario or Luigi does including "floating" without a Leaf. At some points, Cosmic Clones are needed to destroy pillars in order to continue the level. Cosmic Clones can be defeated if Mario or Luigi touch if while under the effects of a [[Starman|Super Star]]. If that happens, they drop three [[coin]]s in their small size, or ten if they are giant.
