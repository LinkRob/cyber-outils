'''Paidan''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Paidan is a spider with mandibles on the front and a few black stripes on its back.

===Behavior===
Paidan move up and down, and can use their mandibles to damage Wario.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
Wario only encounters Paidan in [[Course No.30]]. Whenever Wario touches a Paidan's back, it is defeated.
