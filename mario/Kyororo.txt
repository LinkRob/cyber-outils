'''Kyororo''' (キョロロ, ''Kyororo'') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It is a cyclops that at first stays still looking back and forth but when [[Mario]] approaches it, it will charge at him at full speed.
