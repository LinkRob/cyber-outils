The '''Studio''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It can be first accessed from a fireplace located in the [[Study (Gloomy Manor)|Study]].
==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-2: Gear Up|A-2: Gear Up]], Luigi is thrown into the Studio to look for the final Gear. However, [[Hider]]s attempt to stop Luigi but Luigi vacuums them up into the [[Poltergust 5000]]. Soon after, the [[Slammer]] will appear and Luigi vacuums it up as well and gets the final Gear.

There is also a [[Gem]] located in this room. Luigi has to check out the camera to spot the Gem and then vacuum up the web and get the Gem.
