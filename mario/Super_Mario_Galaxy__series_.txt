'''''Super Mario Galaxy''''' is a sub-series of the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. It is mainly a 3-D adventure game starring [[Mario]] (and [[Luigi]] at a certain point in the game) where he must rescue [[Peach]] from the clutches of [[Bowser]]. There are only two games from the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' series: ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' (both released for the [[Wii]]). ==Gameplay==
In both games, Mario mainly retains some of his techniques that were in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' which include the Wall Jump and the Long Jump. In the Galaxy games, Mario (and Luigi) has a new move called the Spin where it serves as his main means of attack. Spinning is also used to activate Sling Stars and Launch Stars to transport them to a different planet. The player can also point the Wii Remote at the screen to quickly collect Star Bits and feed Star Bits to Lumas. 

Controls are a little different in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' as the player can now use [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in certain levels. Like always, Yoshi can be used to eat up enemies and unlocked certain areas. He is even used to battle certain bosses. This game also as a new mode where if Mario fails a mission over five times, then a [[Cosmic Clone]] called the [[Cosmic Spirit]] will take over and lead Mario right to the Power Star earning him a Bronze Star.
