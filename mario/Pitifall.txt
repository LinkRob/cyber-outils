'''Pitifall''' is a Duel [[minigame|mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
Pitifall is a luck-based mini-game in which the objective is to swing from a rope and land on the unseen platform. There are three ropes, each held by a [[Shy Guy]], and both characters each have to choose one of them. In doing so, the characters swing, holding on to their rope and automatically letting go upon reaching the end. If both characters do not land on the platform, a [[Mecha Fly Guy|Fly Guy]] randomly picks one of them up to put them there. Regardless, whoever lands on the platform is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move the cursor
*{{button|gcn|A}}: Choose a rope

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Choose one of the three ropes. Whoever chooses correctly and swings to the platform wins!''
*'''Advice''': ''If neither player swings safely to the platform, a Fly Guy will rescue just one of them.''
