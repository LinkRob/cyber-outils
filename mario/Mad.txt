{{nihongo|'''Mad'''|メイドー|Meidō}} is a mermaid who is the third boss faced by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]].

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
[[File:Mad_WarioWoods_NES sprite.png|frame|left]]
Mad is a mermaid who has long green hair and a red tail. She wears red wristbands, a yellow tiara, a pearl necklace, and a red bra.

=== Power and abilities===
Mad has the ability to freeze Wario's helper.
{{br|left}}
== Trivia ==
*The [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version of ''Wario's Woods'' features its own mermaid boss named [[Aqualea]], although the relation between the two has never been properly established.
