'''Badge Bandits''' are a purple variant of [[Bandit]]s exclusive to ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They have the uncanny ability to steal not only [[Mario]]'s [[badge]]s but also his partners' as well.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Badge Bandits are only encountered in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. Once they have a badge in hand, Badge Bandits flees from combat. If they are successful, [[Mario]] must fight the Badge Bandit again. Using the special attack of [[Ms. Mowz]], Mario can reclaim the stolen badges.
