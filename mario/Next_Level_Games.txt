'''Next Level Games''' is a Canadian video game developer. They were founded in 2002, and have produced some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. Since January 5, 2021, Next Level Games became a fully owned subsidiary of [[Nintendo]]'s.<ref>{{cite web| url = https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2021/210105e.pdf| title = Notification of the Acquisition of the Canadian Software Developer, Next Level Games| accessdate = September 25, 2023| author = Nintendo| date = January 5, 2021| month = 01| format = PDF}}</ref>

==''Mario''-related games==
In summary, Next Level Games created the {{series|Mario Strikers}} and the sequels to the original ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''. The first ''Mario'' game created by Next Level Games is ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'', released in late 2005.
