}}'''Bob-omb Barrage''' is a [[1 vs. 3 Mini-game]] that appears in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
One player is in a little boat. He or she can move around. The other three players must hit that player with a [[Bob-omb]].

To win the mini-game, either one of the three players must hit the other player with a Bob-omb, or the one player must survive the time limit for the mini-game without being hit.

==Controls==
===Single Player===
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Move

===Three Players===
*{{button|n64|stick}} (flick) - Throw Bob-ombs
