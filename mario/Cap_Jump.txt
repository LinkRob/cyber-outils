'''Cap Jump''' is a jump technique performed in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

== Description ==
Mario can perform the Cap Jump by pressing and holding {{button|ns|y}} (or {{button|ns|jc-left}} when using the Joy-Con) to step on [[Cappy]].
