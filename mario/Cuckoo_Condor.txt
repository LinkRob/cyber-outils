'''Cuckoo Condor''' is a boss in the [[Game Boy Advance]] game ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. It guards the [[Ruby Passage]] and is one of the four bosses after [[Spoiled Rotten]] in which the Player(s) can tackle in any order.  

== Appearance ==
Cuckoo Condor lives up to both parts of its name. At first, the boss is concealed within a large weaponized cuckoo clock-like mech, but is later on to be reviled to be a Condor-like creature. 

== Strategy ==
The pendulum of the clock features a large claw that it uses to pick [[Wario]] up and bring him to the large saw blades bordering the arena to inflict damage. It is difficult to escape this grasp, and impossible unless Wario idddds seized near the center of the screen. Wario must [[Dash Attack|ram]] the large claw that it uses to attack, causing it to swing up and damage the boss.

As the boss takes damage in this form, it will start using its buzz and electrical saws to attack Wario. After taking enough damage, the clock is destroyed, revealing the condor's true form underneath. The small spinning bird on top of the clock remains as a headpiece for the exposed Condor. 

In this state, Cuckoo Condor will lay [[Egg (Wario Land 4)|egg]]s, which hatch chicks upon touching the ground. To damage the Cuckoo Condor, Wario must pick up these eggs before they hit the ground, then power throw the egg onto its head. As damage is dealt over time, the Cuckoo Condor begins to lay eggs at a significantly faster rate (and moving increasingly faster), forcing Wario to time his throws properly to defeat the boss.

After enough hits, Cuckoo Condor is defeated and Wario is rewarded with the creature's [[legendary treasure|Earrings]].

==Time given in different modes==
