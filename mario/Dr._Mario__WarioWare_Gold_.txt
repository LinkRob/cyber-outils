'''Dr. Mario''' is one of [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It is based on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] [[Dr. Mario (game)|game of the same name]].

==Gameplay==
The object is for the play to tilt the system to correctly align a [[Capsule]] so that it takes out three [[Virus]]es at once. The microgame is based on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[Dr. Mario (video game)|same name]], and it has a very similar objective to the ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' microgame of the [[Dr. Mario (WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!)|same name]], aside from the tilt controls. Another difference is that ''WarioWare Gold''{{'}}s Dr. Mario features a zoomed in display.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The capsule only consists of one color, and the Viruses are arranged horizontally.
*'''Level 2:''' The capsule has two colors, and the Viruses are arranged horizontally.
*'''Level 3:''' The capsule has two colors, and the Viruses are arranged either vertically or horizontally on the second-to-bottom row.
