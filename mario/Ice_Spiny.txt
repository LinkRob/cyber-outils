'''Ice Spinies''' are an enemy in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Ice Spinies are a variant of [[Spiny (Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix)|Spinies]], but they have a significantly different appearance of being flat purple chunks of ice with large sharp icicles growing from the top surface.

===Behavior===
Ice Spinies are featured in two different speeds, similarly to red and green Spinies. If an Ice Spiny is stepped on, it explodes, causing the dance meter to drop significantly.

==In ''Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix''==
Ice Spinies can be briefly found in the background of [[World 4 (Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix)|World 4]], where they are avoided while descending the mountain. Like red and green Spinies, Ice Spinies make a significant appearance only in the Mush Mode versions of "[[Rendezvous on Ice]]" and "[[Midnight Drive]]."
