{{Nihongo|'''Waddlewing Warning!'''|ズングリからのドングリ|Zunguri kara no donguri|Acorns from Waddlewing}} is the first level in [[Acorn Plains]] of ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

== Layout ==
The level features [[Waddlewing|Waddlewings]], that will fly, and sometimes land, and walk. Most of the Waddlewings hold [[Acorn Mushroom]]s, and will drop them when jumped on. There are also ledges that will rise and fall, similar to a [[Rise of the Piranha Plants|level]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

== Collectibles ==
=== Star Coins ===
* '''Star Coin 1''': After crossing the first pipe, use the Flying Squirrel suit to swoop up to the Star Coin or use Luigi's super jump at the right time to get on the highest of the first three platforms.
* '''Star Coin 2''': After crossing the second Piranha Plant, go to the second incline platform and glide under that platform to get the Star Coin and also swoop up to the platform to avoid falling through death.
* '''Star Coin 3''': After crossing the third incline, glide through the final trail of coins, avoid the wall of Waddlewings, and swoop up to the Star Coin above the flagpole.

== Luigi sighting ==
[[File:Waddlewing Warning NSLU start.png|thumb|The Luigi sighting at the start of the stage]]
At the beginning of the stage, there's a giant standard [[Luigi]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', introducing the game ''New Super Luigi U'' hanged from a billboard.
