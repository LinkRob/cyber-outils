'''Dark Lakitus''' are a variant  ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Dark Lakitus are found only in the deep reaches of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]], within the seventh area. They are also accompanied by [[Sky-Blue Spiny|Sky-Blue Spinies]]. Some Dark Lakitus can have a [[Thunder Rage]] or a different [[Thunder]]-based item.
