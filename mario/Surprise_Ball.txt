A '''Surprise Ball''' is an object which only appears in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. They are round red spheres with a white exclamation mark painted on each of them. Surprise Balls are found in midair, and a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can extend its tongue out at one to launch up onto the Surprise Ball and use it as a small platform.
