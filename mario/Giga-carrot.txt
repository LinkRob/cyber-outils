The '''Giga-carrot''' is an object which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'', including the latter's [[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]] campaign.

==Description==
The Giga-carrot is a giant carrot grown by several generations of [[Wiggler]]s at the [[Wiggler Family Farm]].

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''===
During [[Bowser]]'s search for a [[Banzai Bill]] to launch into [[Bowser's Castle]], due to being taken over by [[Fawful]], Bowser tries to pull out the Giga-carrot. With unknowing help from [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] in the [[Arm Center]], Bowser manages to pull out the Giga-carrot successfully. This angers a [[Wiggler]], who tells Bowser that he has to eat the Giga-carrot because he pulled it out, and the Wiggler describes the Giga-carrot as being grown for generations within his family.

Bowser does not like vegetables, so he is hesitant to eating the Giga-carrot, but ends up doing it. Bowser receives more unknowing help from Mario and Luigi in the [[Gut Check]], where they help Bowser digest chunks of the Giga-carrot that he had eaten. This occurs during a minigame in which the player has to use the Stylus to tap the Giga-carrot chunks that line up on the Bottom Screen.

When Bowser eats the entire Giga-carrot, the Wiggler turns red with anger, so Wiggler essentially had Bowser eat the Giga-carrot to try and make Bowser realize how mad he is for pulling out the Giga-carrot. This is followed by a boss fight of Bowser against the Wiggler.

===''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''===
It is one of the ingredients that [[Bowser Jr.]] has to use to make the [[Skeletone Formula:D]]. The Giga Carrot is also shown to have a bite mark when [[Bowser]] finds it, this bite mark was from when [[Bowser Jr.]] and his team ripped a part of the Giga Carrot for the Skeletone Formula.
