The '''Bull's Eye Banazi''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
This kart is a variation of the [[Bullet Blaster]] that has its design based of the [[Bull's-Eye Banzai]].

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
This kart debuted in the [[New York Tour]] in 2019. When used, this kart helps to increase the duration of [[Rocket Start]] boosts and the points earned for performing them.
