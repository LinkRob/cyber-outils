A '''Bomb Oozer''' is a kind of [[Luigi's Mansion 3#Rare_Ghosts|rare ghost]] in [[Luigi's Mansion 3]], it is a form of [[Oozer]]. Like [[Bomb Goob]]s and [[Bomb Hammer]]s, Bomb Oozers have glowing yellow bodies with blue eyes and white mouth. Due to the fact of being rare ghosts, Bomb Oozers will only appear in [[ScareScraper]] in the ten or twenty floors mod. They have 200HP.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Same as Oozers, Bomb Oozers have yellow bodies, but their bodies are glowing yellow.

=== Behavior ===
Unlike most ghosts, Bomb Oozers have blue eyes with white mouth. The way to defeat Bomb Oozers is same as the way as Oozers.

== In ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' ==

When Bomb Oozers appear, they will hold bombs to prevent [[Luigi]] from flashing them, Luigi has to use a burst to let them drop their bombs. They will throw bombs to attack Luigi, while being sucked, they will throw more bombs at a time. They are defeated in the same way as Oozers.
