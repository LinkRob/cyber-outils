'''Water''' is a drink and key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Water is only seen when [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] have to bake a [[cake]] for [[Gourmet Guy]]. Water can be acquired from the nearby sink. Adding Water is not recommended as doing so causes the cake to become ruined.
