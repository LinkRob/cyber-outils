'''DK Cruiser''' is a basketball court owned by [[Donkey Kong]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
DK Cruiser takes place on a large wooden raft drifting down a river. On each side, there are hollowed-out hoops depicting the "DK" [[emblem]]. [[Barrel]]s occasionally roll across the stage and squish characters nearby, causing them to lose [[Coin]]s and other items. [[Banana]]s also sometimes fall on the court.

DK Cruiser does not return in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'', but there is a similar court, [[DK Dock]].

==Flavor text==
*'''Manual:''' ''Barrels come rolling into your way when you pass by the waterfall. And watch it—sometimes bananas get thrown in from the jungle.''
