'''Mace Guys''' are the [[Ball 'n' Chain]]-wielding variant of [[Shy Guy]]s. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', and make a majority of their appearances in the [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi'' franchise]].

== Physical description ==
Mace Guys have an entirely identical appearance to Shy Guys, with the only difference being the mace that they carry. Some Mace Guys carry a larger mace than others.

== Appearances ==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
Mace Guys are relatively uncommon enemies that appear in only three [[level]]s: [[Sluggy The Unshaven's Fort]], [[Raphael The Raven's Castle]], and [[Ultimate Castle Challenge]]. Mace Guys attack by swinging their [[Ball 'n' Chain|mace]] at a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] and [[Baby Mario]]. If Yoshi is hit, he takes damage, and the [[Countdown Timer]] is triggered with [[Baby Mario]] in a [[bubble]].

In the [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', Mace Guys also appear in an additional secret level, [[Endless World of Yoshis]].

===''Yoshi Topsy-Turvy''===
Mace Guys appear as enemies in seven missions. 

===''Yoshi's New Island''===
Mace Guys appear as uncommon enemies throughout the game. 

===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
Mace Guy appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' as a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]], which increases the POW of a fighter's weapon by 22 when equipped.

== Trivia ==
*Despite the official artwork depicting the Mace Guy with a black ball with white spikes, the actual sprite is light blue/white with yellow spikes.
*Despite the English translation referring to the enemies as 'Mace Guys,' the weapon they wield is actually a flail.
**The original Japanese name is 鉄球ヘイホ (Tekyū Heihō) which can be translated to 'Wrecking Ball Shy Guy'
