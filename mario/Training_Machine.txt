The '''Training Machine''' is a key item in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Description==
The Training Machine is an item that greatly resembles a [[Nintendo DS]].

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
The Training Machine can only be obtained from [[Bestovius]] during the [[Piccolo]] quest. Bestovius will give it to [[Mario]] and the others after he is given the [[You-Know-What]] from [[Old Man Watchitt]]. The Training Machine must then be delivered to [[Merluvlee]]. Merluvlee is pleased by her gift and gives the [[Crystal Ball]] in return.
