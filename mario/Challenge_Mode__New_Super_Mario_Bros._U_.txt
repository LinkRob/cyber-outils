'''Challenge Mode''' is a mode in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. There are five different types of challenges; [[Time Attack (Challenge Mode)|Time Attack]], [[Coin Collection (Challenge Mode)|Coin Collection]], [[1-Up Rally (Challenge Mode)|1-Up Rally]], [[Special (Challenge Mode)|Special]] and [[Boost Mode Challenges]]. As you complete challenges, more are unlocked with higher difficulty. The difficulty is measured using stars. Depending on how good the player performs, they will receive a medal. Gold, Silver or Bronze.

== Categories ==
===Time Attack===
Mario must complete the levels in as quickly as possible. Each Time Attack level is inspired by levels in the main game.

===Coin Collection===
A series of challenges involving collecting or dodging coins. Mario is on a timer.

===1-Up Rally===
Mario must complete challenges revolving around jumping on enemies to attempt to get 1-Ups.

===Special===
Various different challenges similar to those above but don't fall under the other categories.

===Boost Pad Challenges===
A series of challenges requiring the [[Boost Pad]] for completion.
