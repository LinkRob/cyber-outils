'''''Super Mario Compact Disco''''' was a soundtrack released by the Ambassadors of Funk. It consists of 20 songs based around scores from various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games such as ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''.

==Tracks==
* Opening Credits 
* Super Mario Bros. Theme 
* Radio Compact Disco 1 
* Super Mario Land Theme 
* Radio Compact Disco 2 
* Go, Mario, Go! 
* King of the Koopas
* Radio Compact Disco 3 
* Mario & Yoshi music 1
* Invincible Mario 
* Super Mario World theme
* Radio Compact Disco 4 
* Save Me (With Your Charm)
* Radio Compact Disco 5 
* Super Mario Kart theme
* Super Mario USA theme
* Radio Compact Disco 6 
* Six Golden Coins
* Radio Compact Disco 7
* Mario Theme (Reprise)

==Trivia==
[[File:Super odd Super mario land music video flimed at Chessington World of Adventures|thumb|right|335 px]]
* The song Save Me (With Your Charm) mentions Kinopio, [[Toad (character)|Toad]], as an antagonist. Even though he is a protagonist in the entire series.
* The lyrics to Super Mario USA aren't even related to the [[Super Mario Bros. 2|game]] it's based on.
* [[Princess Daisy]] appears as the damsel in distress in almost all the songs, even though she was only in Super Mario Land.
* Super Mario Land was the only track to get a music video.
