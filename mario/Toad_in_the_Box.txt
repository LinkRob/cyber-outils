'''Toad in the Box''' (stylized as '''TOAD in the Box''') is a 4-Player [[minigame]] that appears in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. Its name is a pun on the name of the toy the {{wp|jack-in-the-box}}.

==Opening==
The players fall from above onto a stage, followed by the blocks. The minigame then starts.

==Gameplay==
Players have to hit the spinning blocks and get a [[Toad]] face each time. Each time a player gets a Toad face, the spinning block gets a little faster. The floor below them also rises one level. If one of the players hits the spinning block and gets something other than a Toad face, the player gets stunned for a moment: Getting a [[Piranha Plant]] figure causes it to hit the player once, getting a [[Chain Chomp]] figure causes it to hit the player twice, and getting a [[Bowser]] figure causes [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]] to come down and smash the player. The first player who gets five Toad faces wins. If nobody does so within a certain time limit, the minigame ends in a draw.

The minigame can be purchased from [[Woody]] for 150 [[Coin]]s.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|A}} &ndash; [[Jump]]
