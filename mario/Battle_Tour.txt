The '''Battle Tour''' was the eightieth tour of [[Mario Kart Tour]], which began on October 5, [[:Category:2022 games|2022]] and ended on October 18, 2022. It was rerun from October 4, [[:Category:2023 games|2023]] to October 17, 2023 as the game's hundred-and-sixth tour overall and the first tour following the end of new in-game content.
