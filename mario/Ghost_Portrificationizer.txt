The '''Ghost Portrificationizer''' is an invention of [[Professor E. Gadd]]'s which appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Ghost Portrificationizer works in conjunction with the [[Poltergust 3000]]. On the left end of the machine, the Poltergust 3000 can be attached to release [[ghost]]s into it. The Ghost Portrificationizer takes [[Portrait Ghost]]s in particular and converts them into portraits to be hung in the [[Gallery (Luigi's Mansion)|Gallery]]. The machine also works in reverse, as shown during the ending, in which [[Luigi]] used the Ghost Portrificationizer to free [[Mario]] from the painting he had been trapped in by [[King Boo]].
