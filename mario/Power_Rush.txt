}}'''Power Rush''' is [[badge]] which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
In ''Paper Mario'', Power Rush is found in [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. To get this badge, [[Princess Peach]] must grab the Power Rush inside of the castle's library and send it in the large chest with a star on it to the identical one in [[Shooting Star Summit]]. Like the other badges in the castle, if [[Mario]] does not get them from the chest, he can find them inside of the castle. Power Rush costs one [[Badge Point|BP]] for Mario to wear, and doing so increases Mario's [[Attack]] power by 2 while he is in [[Danger]].

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', Power Rush is found in the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]], where it can be bought for fifty [[coin]]s. An unlimited number of Power Rushes can be obtained via the [[Pianta Parlor]], where it is priced for 34 [[Pianta Token]]s each.

The Power Rush has the same effects as in ''Paper Mario'', but unlike its previous appearance, the Power Rush has a "D" on it instead of a "P." It is also among the badges to have received a partner-based equivalent, the [[Power Rush P]].
