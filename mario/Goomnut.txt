'''Goomnuts''' are items which appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Goomnuts can be found very early on in the game, only after [[Mario]] gets the [[Hammer]]. There is a lone tree located in [[Goomba Village]]. By smashing this tree, a Goomnut will fall and it can be picked up by Mario. These items are very weak, giving Mario only 3 points of [[Flower Point|FP]]. However, their purpose is only useful early in the game where Mario's FP is only at 5 and later on as an ingredient for some recipes.

==Trivia==
*While Goomnuts do not appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', they are mentioned by [[Goombella]] during one of her [[tattle]]s in a certain room of the [[Riverside Station]] when she says it smells like rotting Goomnuts.
