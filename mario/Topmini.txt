A '''Topmini''' (also called a '''Mini Topman''') is a type of [[Topman]], and is the smallest of the bunch  It makes its first appearance in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and reappears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They don't have any weapons to hurt Mario but makes him spin and push him around.

==Design==
Topminis have a yellow body, a black spinner on the bottom covered in gold bulbs, and two big eyes.

== Behaviour ==
Like their bigger cousins, Topminis attack by spinning into Mario. This attack only serves to push him around. Mario can destroy them with his [[Star Spin]], causing the creature to release a [[Star Bit]]. Topminis can also be destroyed by shooting their heads with a Star Bit, in which case they drop three more Star Bits.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Topminis take their name from the prefix ''mini-'', from the Latin word ''minus'', which means "small." The prefix is changed to a suffix to form a pun on [[Topman]].

== Nomenclature ==
Topminis are implied to be babies due to their name translations, with the Japanese translation being ''Bebī Begoman'' (written ベビーベーゴマン), with ''Begoman'' being the name of the regular [[Topman]] and ''Bebī'' coming from English "baby." Spanish calls the enemy ''Nenetrompo'', from ''nene'' (a synonym of ''infante'' ("baby")) and ''trompo'' (a synonym of ''peón'' ("top")). In Italian, the Topmini is called ''Robinfante'', from ''roboto'' ("robot") and ''infante'' ("baby"). German calls the enemy ''Mini-Begoman'', with ''Mini-'' coming from Latin ''minus'', like with the English name.

== Trivia ==
