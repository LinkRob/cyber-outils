'''Oho Jees''' are characters and enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They live peacefully on [[Oho Oasis]] near the [[Fire Palace|Fire]] and [[Thunder Palace|Thunder]] temples. In ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'', one Oho Jee was shown in [[Cavi Cape]], implying there are more Oho Jees living in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. The Oho Jees often use incorrect grammar and say very bizarre and mysterious things when spoken to.  

According to one of the radios on Oho Oasis, a researcher was transformed into an Oho Jee after being attacked by red Jee when it accidentally set on fire.

==Physical description==
Normal Oho Jees can come in red or blue. Two unique Oho Jees, who are colored green and purple and have afros, are found on each of the two Surfing Spot islands.

==Battle==
Oho Jees become enemies if Mario or Luigi use either the [[Firebrand]] on a red Jee or [[Thunderhand]] on a blue Jee. In battle, Oho Jees mainly attack by spinning multiple times at Mario or Luigi. The Red Oho Jee attacks with a circular pattern at Mario and Luigi. Blue Oho Jees move around in a diamond shape.

The Oho Jees are sometimes very stupid in battle. They may run away and (if the running fails) come back saying "I no believe!" (a reference to their species' name) or hum a song, which slightly heals the brothers. Also, if they are healed too much, they will blow up and the battle will end. After defeat they will lay on the ground seemingly dead only to jump back up and say they were just kidding and that they're fine.

===Strategy===
Red Oho Jees are weak to Luigi's Thunderhand, and vice versa for blue ones.

*In the Japanese version of the original ''Superstar Saga'', the red Jee to the northwest of the [[Fire Palace]] will explicitly mention wishing to become mayor of "Oppai village".
