'''Coin Rush''' is a new mode introduced in [[New Super Mario Bros. 2|''New Super Mario Bros. 2'']]. It is a single-player mode that can be unlocked by defeating the [[Castle]] levels or the [[Cannon]] levels in World 1. In Coin Rush you play as [[Mario]], or a White Racoon Mario. You must race through random levels through the game and collect as many [[coin]]s as you can on each level. The levels act like normal levels in the game, except the time to complete the course is severely shortened down to one-hundred seconds. But you can expand the time by making it past Checkpoint Flags. The only levels that are not selected in Coin Rush are World 3-B (due to the required use of the [[Mini Mushroom]]), and the castles from World 6 and World Star (both due to the length of the [[Bowser]]/[[Dry Bowser]] battles).

Once the player defeats [[Bowser's Castle]] in World 6, you can get a [[Gold Flower]] in your inventory. You can only use the flower once though. Dying in one level ends the entire mode and you must start over. Collecting 100 coins does not give you an extra life, as it only adds to your coin total.

The total of number of coins that can be collected is 30,000; once that number is reached, no more coins will be added to your total.
