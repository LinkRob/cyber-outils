The '''Untitled Donkey Kong film''' is in production at [[Universal Pictures]].<ref>[https://www.comicbasics.com/donkey-kong-movie-reportedly-in-development-potential-release-date-revealed/ ‘Donkey Kong’ Movie Reportedly in Development, Potential Release Date Revealed - IMDb]</ref> It is upcoming animated film based on the [[Donkey Kong (series)|''Donkey Kong'']] video game series and a third entry in the [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|''Super Mario Bros.'' film series]] by [[Illumination|Illumination Entertainment]] and [[Nintendo]], as well as a sequel and spin-off to ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' with [[Seth Rogen]] reprising his role as [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]]. The film is so far, currently in development since November 14, 2025.

== Confirmed cast ==

* [[Seth Rogen]] as [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]]
* [[Fred Armisen]] as [[Cranky Kong (Illumination)|Cranky Kong]] 

==Development==
On April 23, 2026, an official document by Universal with all their upcoming films were posted on their website, specifically their Spanish division. One film listed under "UNTITLED ILLUMINATION/NINTENDO EVENT FILM" is seen, with a release date for April 12, 2028.<ref>[https://www.universalpictures.es/fechasdeestreno/Estrenos-Universal.pdf UNIVERSAL PICTURES INTERNATIONAL SPAIN]</ref> Although there is currently no confirmation for this yet, it is heavily implied the unknown listed film here is in fact the ''Donkey Kong'' film, mostly due to their similar release dates.
