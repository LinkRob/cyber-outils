The '''Boss Key''' is an important item which appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. They are one of the primary collectible items, along with [[Golden Banana]]s.

==History==
As the name states, Boss Keys can be earned from defeating the major bosses from the game after [[Troff]] and [[Scoff]] open the way to the boss. There are eight Boss Keys in total and each of these keys are needed to unlock the large locks on [[K. Lumsy]]'s cage. Collecting all of the Boss Keys frees K. Lumsy who stops [[King K. Rool]] from escaping allowing the Kongs to take him down in the final showdown.

==Trivia==
*The jingle that plays for collecting a Boss Key is the exact same as the one for collecting a Golden Banana.
