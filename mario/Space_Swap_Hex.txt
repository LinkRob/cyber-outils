The '''Space Swap Hex''' is a type of [[Hex]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. The player who owns it can choose to place it on any [[Blue Space]] or [[Red Space]] within five spaces from their current location. If another player then lands on it, they swap places with the player who placed it. If the owner lands on it, they will simply receive 5 [[Coin|coin]]s. Players can get this hex by either randomly obtaining it by passing through a [[Hex Area]], or stealing one using the [[Snag Bag]]. Computer players cannot use this Hex, similar to how they never use [[Warp Dice Block]]s.
