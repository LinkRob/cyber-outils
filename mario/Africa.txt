'''Africa''' is a real-world continent found on [[Earth]]. Its first appearance in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media is the ''[[Saturday Supercade]]'' episode "[[Hairy Parent]]," but the first appearance of Africa in a ''Mario'' game is ''[[Mario is Missing!]]''. [[Egypt]] is the most famous country within Africa and the most recurring within ''Mario''-related media. 

==Appearances==
==={{series|Mario Discovery}}===
====''Mario is Missing!''====
In ''[[Mario is Missing!]]'', [[Luigi]] visits a few cities in Africa on his journey to rescue [[Mario]], including:

*[[Nairobi]], Kenya
*[[Marrakech]], Morocco ([[PC]] version only)
*[[Cairo]], Egypt (PC and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] versions only)

====''Mario's Time Machine''====
In all versions of ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]'', Egypt is featured as one of the locations that Mario travels to.

===''Mario Kart'' series===
While not appearing in gameplay, Africa, along with Earth's other continents, is featured on the online racing menus in the entries of the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]] that support online play, including ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' along with its remake ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

===''Mario Party DS''===
In ''[[Mario Party DS]]'', Africa makes a minor appearance in [[Globe Gunners]] on the globe and its corresponding top screen map.

==In other media==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
In ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[7 Continents for 7 Koopas]]," when [[King Koopa (DiC cartoons)|King Koopa]] tries to conquer the entire [[Earth|Real World]], he puts [[Kooky von Koopa]] in charge of Africa. Kooky uses his [[magic wand]] to turn all of Africa's animals (including a lion, giraffe, zebra, gorilla, and an African elephant) into stuffed animals. [[Luigi]] later steals Kooky's wand and turns the stuffed animals back to normal. The animals then proceed to chase Kooky.
