'''Porta-Gust''' is a [[Gaddget]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is given as a reward from [[Snifit (Mario Party Advance)|Snifit]] for completing the [[Condo of Mystery!]] quest. The name Porta-Gust is a portmanteau of "portable" and "[[Poltergust 3000|Poltergust]]."

==Usage==
The Gaddget is a ghost hunting simulator that functions like the [[Game Boy Horror]]'s [[Boo Radar]] and [[Poltergust 3000]] from ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''. The Porta-Gust shows a radar, and to approach a [[Boo]], the player must use the {{button|gba|Pad}} to press and hold the directions as their corresponding button lights up in the lower-left corner. The graph increasingly reacts the closer to a Boo it is. When the graph starts to shake violently, the player must press and hold {{button|gba|A}} as soon as they see the Boo on the screen. In doing so, the Boo gets sucked into a jar capable of holding up to eight Boos. The player can press {{button|gba|B}} to release a Boo, which is required if the maximum capacity is reached so that the player can catch more Boos.
