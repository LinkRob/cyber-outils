'''Super Cheese''' is an item that appears on [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]. It is used to restore BP and replace [[Supersyrup Jar]]s.

== Description ==
The Super Cheese is exclusive to [[Bowser]]. Like the [[Supersyrup Jar]], if [[Bowser]] eats the Super Cheese, he gains 35 [[Bros. Point]]s.
