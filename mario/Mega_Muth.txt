A '''Mega Muth''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They appear in [[Castle Bleck]] at the [[Castle Bleck Inner Sanctum]]. The Mega Muth is a powerful version of a [[Muth]].

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
Mega Muths are more powerful versions of [[Muth]]s encountered only in [[Castle Bleck Inner Sanctum]] of Chapter 8-4. Possessing a massive 200 [[Heart Point|HP]], Mega Muths are one of the strongest enemies in the entire game. Since only [[Mario]] is around at the time during Chapter 8-4 and [[Bowser]] was out of the party, it's highly recommended to use the [[Flip]] ability to go by them. Especially since Mega Muths are found by other Mega Muths and other enemies as well. If one does want to fight a Mega Muth for points, then safely using [[Boomer]] from a distance is the best strategy.
