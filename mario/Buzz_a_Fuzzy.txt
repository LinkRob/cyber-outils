{{Nihongo|'''Buzz a Fuzzy'''|チョロボンアーチェリー|Chorobon ācherī|Fuzzy Archery}} ('''Ready, Aim, Fuzzy!''' in British English) is a General minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must use the [[Nintendo 3DS]] to aim at the Fuzzies. They then pull back on the "Circle Pad" ready their bow, and release it to fire. The player that has fired at the most Fuzzies in three rounds wins.

==Controls==
* {{Button|3ds|tilt}} – Aim
* {{Button|3ds|stick}} – Fire

== Flavor text ==
*'''Rules''': ''Please pay attention to your surroundings and be careful when moving your system. Aim by moving your system, and try to hit as many Fuzzies as possible with an arrow. If the gameplay becomes unstable, place the system on a flat surface to recalibrate. You can also press down on {{button|3ds|Pad}} at any time to center your view.''

==Trivia==
*This is the first minigame in the {{series|Mario Party}} to include Fuzzies, which usually appear in the {{series|Super Mario}} and {{series|Paper Mario}}.
