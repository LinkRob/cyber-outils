'''No Boo''' is an [[item]] in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

== Description ==
No Boo is a sign depicting a silhouette of a [[Boo]] crossed through with a red line. It prevents the character from being assisted by Boo when dropping by its space, specifically to steal [[coin]]s or [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s from another characters.

==In ''Mario Party''==
No Boo can be bought at the [[Mushroom Shop]] for 500 [[coin]]s after completing Party Mode. The item does not work at [[Peach's Birthday Cake]] because there is no Boo there.
