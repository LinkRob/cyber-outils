The '''Universe''' is all of space and time and their contents that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]]. It is a dimensional vast expanse of space and matter that is home to [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and many others. 

== Description ==
The universe is a large space with so many galaxies, so many systems, and so many planets, moons, stars, comets, and asteroids, with the most notable ones being [[Earth (Illumination)|Earth]] and [[Mushroom Earth (Illumination)|Mushroom Earth]].
