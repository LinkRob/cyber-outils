The '''Spiky Stardust Defense System'''  (abbreviated to '''SSDS''') is a procedure in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' found only in [[Stardust Fields]].

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''/''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' ==
It serves as a tutorial for using [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s [[jump]]s in tandem. The obstacle is a series of glowing [[spike (hazard)|spikes]] that extend from one side to another. If either bro lands on it they both go flying to just before the obstacle.
