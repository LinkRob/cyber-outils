The '''Super Poltergust 3001''' is an advanced version on the [[Poltergust 3000]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' which was invented by [[Professor E. Gadd]].

== Description==
The Super Poltergust 3001 functions like the Poltergust 3000 except [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can ride on it and it can go high. The Super Poltergust 3001 appears to have the ability to vacuum up [[ghost]]s. Professor E. Gadd can be seen riding on the Super Poltergust 3001 after taking a drink at [[Starbeans Cafe]].

== Trivia ==
*Professor E. Gadd created another rideable vacuum, that is the [[Poltergust 4000]], although in the form of a vehicle used by Luigi in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. The regular Poltergust 3000 can be ridden once in the PAL version of ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' during the battle with [[Boolossus]] in the Hidden Mansion.
