'''Snivel Disobedience''' is a [[microgame]] found in the [[Cute Cuts]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''

==Gameplay==
The objective of the game is to use the stylus to cut the blonde woman's hanging snots before the time runs out. If this is done, the game is won. 

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The woman holds still as her snot dangles down.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The woman's snot dangles up and down.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The woman moves as her snot dangles left and right.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name is a pun on the onomatopoeic word "snivel" and the term "{{wp|civil disobedience}}".
