'''Music Pads''' are special pads that appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

== Description ==
Music Pads are found throughout levels in [[DK Isles]] (including in DK Isles itself). To use a Music Pad, the Kongs must first visit [[Candy Kong]] in her [[Candy's Music Shop|music shop]] to purchase an instrument that can be used on a specific pad. Playing an instrument on a Music Pad will tigger a certain event.

== Types of Music Pads ==
*'''Bongos:''' Used for [[Donkey Kong]]'s [[Bongo Blast]].
*'''Electric Guitar:''' Used for [[Diddy Kong]]'s [[Guitar Gazump]].
*'''Trombone:''' Used for [[Lanky Kong]]'s [[Trombone Tremor]].
*'''Saxophone:''' Used for [[Tiny Kong]]'s [[Saxophone Slam]].
*'''Triangle:''' Used for [[Chunky Kong]]'s [[Triangle Trample]].

== Trivia ==
*Lanky's Music Pad is the only one in the game that appears during a boss fight (as four of them appear during the third round fight with [[King Krusha K. Rool]]).
