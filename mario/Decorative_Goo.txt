The '''Decorative Goo''' is a [[Treasure (Pikmin franchise)|treasure]] which first appears in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Pikmin 2|Pikmin 2]]''.

==Description==
The Decorative Goo is a tube blue acrylic paint with a blue cap. It has the logo "[[Mario]] Paints," a reference to the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] game ''[[Mario Paint]]'', contains blue paint, and reads "Made in USA" in small text at the bottom right of the label reading "Blue." In the Japanese version, the Decorative Goo is a generic branded, which it would become in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Pikmin 4|Pikmin 4]]'' altogether.

The [[w:c:nintendo:Treasure Hoard|Treasure Hoard]] icon of the Decorative Goo uses its generic design from the Japanese version in original releases of ''Pikmin 2''. This was changed in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Pikmin 1+2|Pikmin 1+2]]''.

==In ''Pikmin 2''==
[[File:Decorative Goo location.png|thumb|left|250px|The location of the Decorative Goo in ''Pikmin 2'']]
The Decorative Goo is located in a pool area in the northern area of [[w:c:nintendo:Awakening Wood|Awakening Wood]]. It is surrounded by a blue stain of sorts, presumably the contents of the Decorative Goo. [[Captain Olimar|Olimar]] and [[w:c:nintendo:Louie|Louie]] need to have at least ten [[Blue Pikmin]] with them to retrieve the Decorative Goo.

In North American releases only, the Decorative Goo hovers above the ground in the Treasure Hoard.
