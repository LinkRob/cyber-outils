'''Fink Flowers''' are flower-like enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are found at [[Land's End]] and [[Bean Valley]]. Fink Flowers are also a stronger variant of [[Snapdragon]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Fink Flowers can attack physically by whipping their wines at one of [[Mario]]'s party. They can also use moves such as [[Pollen Nap]] and [[S'crow Dust]] which can put someone to sleep or turn them into a scarecrow. They also then to [[Recover|heal themselves]] whenever their HP gets low. Fink Flowers are also known for their high HP.

===Strategy===
Fink Flowers, being plants, are weak to [[Fire (element)|Fire]] moves, so [[Mario]] should use his [[Super Flame]] on them. Having one party member wear a [[Trueform Pin]] and another wear a [[Wake Up Pin]] protects them from Fink Flower's attacks.
