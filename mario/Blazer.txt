|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Blaster''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is only used by [[Bill Blaster|Blaster]]s and [[Big Bertha (Bill Blaster)|Big Bertha]]s.

==Effects==
When Blaster is used, a large [[Bullet Bill]] is fired at the target. If it connects, the target will be instantly knocked out making Blaster one of the one-hit kill moves in the game. Blaster can be guarded against or wearing a Quartz Charm or Lazy Shell will prevent the one-hit kill effects.
