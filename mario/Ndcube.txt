'''Ndcube''' is a Japanese video game developer that has developed every game in the {{series|Mario Party}} beginning with ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. They replaced [[Hudson Soft]] as the new developers of the series after Hudson Soft had been acquired by [[Konami]].

==History==
The company was founded on March 1, 2000 through a joint venture between Nintendo and advertising firm Dentsu, hence the Nd in the name.

==List of ''Mario'' games==
*2012 - ''[[Mario Party 9]]''
*2013 - ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''
*2015 - ''[[Mario Party 10]]''
*2016 - ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''
*2017 - ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''
*2018 - ''[[Super Mario Party]]''
*2021 - ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''
*2024 - ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''
