A '''Spiked Gloomba''' is a [[Gloomba]] that has a small hat with a spike on top of its head. It is essentially a "Dark [[Spiked Goomba]]." It is stronger than both the Gloomba and the Spiked Goomba. They wear their spiked helmets to avoid being jumped on by [[Mario]], however, if Mario is equipped with the [[Spike Shield]] badge or has a similar effect, Mario can jump on Spiked Gloombas with no trouble.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:Spiked Gloomba PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Spiked Gloombas made their debut. They are found in the [[Toad Town Tunnels]]. These enemies can be found throughout the upper portions of the sewers primarily and may tag along with other enemies on the lower levels.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', they appear in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. They first appear in the second area of the pit, levels eleven through twenty, and afterward, may tag along with other enemies on the next ten levels.

=== ''Super Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Spiked Gloombas appear within the Pit of 100 Trials, like in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''.
