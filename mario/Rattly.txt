'''Rattly the Rattlesnake''' is a rattlesnake-species [[Animal Buddy]] being a part of the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' series. He seems to be a replacement of [[Winky the Frog|Winky]].

==Games==
===''Donkey Kong'' series===
====''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''====
Rattly first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' first appearing in [[Topsail Trouble (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Topsail Trouble]] and making its final in [[Animal Antics (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Animal Antics]].

In the game, Rattly is known for its incredible heights in jumping and reaching areas usually that cannot be accessed by [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] or [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] themselves. Sometimes is required to reach these areas whether it allows it to advance in the level or access a [[DK Coin]] or a [[Bonus Level]]. This is similar to how [[Squitter the Spider|Squitter]]. It can simply do this by his incredibly springy coil. It can also bounce up and down rapidly by its [[supercharge]] attack by bouncing even higher. Its level [[Rattle Battle (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Rattle Battle]] is entirely dedicated to itself. It can even defeat [[Zinger]]s by jumping on them.

====''Donkey Kong Land 2''====
Rattly reappears in the semi-sequel ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''.

In the game, Rattly apparently has survived the sinking of [[Crocodile Isle]] (his hometown) and has come to once again assist Diddy and Dixie. Instead, his supercharge jump button is SELECT.
