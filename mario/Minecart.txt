==Appearances==
===''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''===
The Minecart return in this game as part of the [[Mine Cart Ride]] minigame. Only [[Diddy Kong]], [[Chunky Kong]], and [[Donkey Kong]] (in that order) are able to ride in the Minecarts of this game. As Diddy is the weaker of the Kongs and must jump out of the Minecart to avoid obstacles, Donkey Kong and Chunky are strong enough to jump with their Minecarts (DK actually has to jump to switch tracks during his Ghost Train Ride).
