A '''Lucky Fruit''' is a gameplay mechanic which appears in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==In ''Yoshi's Story''==
Before starting Story Mode and Trial Mode, a roulette wheel consisting of [[apple]]s, [[banana]]s, [[watermelon]]s, and [[grape]]s appears onscreen. Pressing the A button or Start slows down the roulette wheel until it stops on one of the four regular [[fruit]]s. The selected fruit becomes the Lucky Fruit, which becomes the "yummiest" fruit for the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] up to the end of their adventure. The Lucky Fruit rewards the Yoshi with eight [[heart]]s and recovers every petal on their [[Smile Meter]], even if the Yoshi color does not ordinarily like the fruit.

Each level features twelve of the selected Lucky Fruit to find. In Super Happy mode, if Yoshi does a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]], all [[Shy Guy]]s onscreen turn into the Lucky Fruit.
