'''Katsini''' is the first boss whom [[Toad (character)|Toad]] faces in the VS COM mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]].

==Physical description==
Katsini is a purple humanoid character dressed in a suit and a top hat, and he carries a red cane around with him. Katsini has two fangs sticking from his lower jaw. Katsini bears a resemblance to [[Tatanga]], but no connection between the two characters has ever been established.
