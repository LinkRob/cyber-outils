'''Bomb Mario''', (or alternatively sometimes called '''Bomb Wario'''), is a powerup first appearing in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

In this game, [[Mario]] takes on this form. [[Wario]], later, appears in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and later on takes on the formation to delete files. He also does this in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. This formation cannot be used in gameplay.

This method is only used to erase save files by selecting a pipe and exploding underneath the pipe. This then results into a normal formation when blasting out. Same method goes with Wario.
