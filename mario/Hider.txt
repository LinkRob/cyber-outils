{{Nihongo|'''Hiders'''|ルハイド|Ruhaido}} are enemies that appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Hiders are slim, blue ghosts with a long tail. They also have a stronger variant, known as [[Strong Hider]]s.

=== Abilites ===
Hiders have the ability to hide in furniture and throw various objects at [[Luigi]]. When found out of the item hiding spot, they will flee to another piece of furniture. Despite Hiders having only 15 HP, they have high speed.

== In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' ==
[[File:A group of Hiders.png|thumb|185px|right|A group of Hiders stealing the pinwheel vanes in the Courtyard of the Haunted Towers.]]
Hiders debuted as enemies in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They appear as common ghosts found in all mansions, including [[ScareScraper]]. Their most notable role is in the [[Haunted Towers]], in the mission, [[The Pinwheel Gate]], where five Hiders steal the pinwheel vanes, forcing Luigi to capture them all and retrieve back the vanes.

== In ''Luigi's Mansion Arcade'' ==
Hiders return in as enemies in ''[[Luigi's Mansion Arcade]]'', appearing in the [[Gloomy Manor]] and the [[Treacherous Mansion]]. They behave in the same manner as they did in ''Dark Moon''.

== Trivia ==
*In ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', yellow ghosts known as [[Oozer]]s, are comparable to Hiders, although Oozers are much more aggressive than Hiders.
