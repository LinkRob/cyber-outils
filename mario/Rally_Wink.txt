'''Rally Wink''' is a special move used by [[Goombella]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''

==Description==
The move costs 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use, and it becomes usable once Goombella reaches the [[Ultra Rank]]. The player can use Rally Wink by repeatedly pressing {{button|gcn|a}} and {{button|gcn|b}} to fill up the gauge, causing Goombella to give Mario an extra chance to attack. Rally Wink works similarly to the [[Fast]] status.
