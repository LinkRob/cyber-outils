'''Midnite''' is one of the secondary antagonists in ''[[Mario + Rabbids: Sparks of Hope]]'' (alongside [[Bedrock]] and [[Daphne]]). This Rabbid makes her lair in the Winter Palace within Pristine Peaks, where she imprisons a Rabbid version of [[Rosalina]] that joins the party to help them fight Midnite just prior to the battle. Midnite also apparently used to be friends with [[Edge]], who left her. (Midnite puts it as "ghosted.") When she is defeated, Midnite will surrender a [[Darkmess]] crystal to the party.

According to her biography, Midnite is not really a ghost, but her genetic code was merged with mist, hence her phantasmal appearance.

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
Midnite has a Rabbid's upper body combined with a ghostly tail. Her outfit consists of a strapless frilly dress. One of her hands has a paper Japanese fan.

== In ''Mario + Rabbids: Sparks of Hope'' ==
=== Battle ===
Midnite is fought in three phases. Throughout these three phases, she moves by teleporting from place to place (thus ignoring "Line-of-sight" moves while she moves provided she wasn't shoved into them) and snipes at enemies from a distance, à la the Peek-a-boo Rabbid from ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]''. For every 1/3 HP she loses, Midnite switches the positions of the library shelves, making the battlefield trickier to navigate. Bombs are found in all three phases of the battle. [[Thwomp]]-carrying Rabbids that can flatten the heroes are found during the first and third phases of the battle; in the second phase, their place is stolen by Lone Wolf Rabbids, and in the third, both Rabbid variations appear. Midnite doesn't summon new enemies to replace those that were defeated.

== Memory Bio ==
Today, we encountered a bioform more terrible than any of Cursa's minions thus far - a Spark Hunter named Midnite.
An analysis revealed that Midnite is the result of a merge between Rabbid DNA and "a cold mist during the dead of night that chills to the bone." The combination is cunning, alluring, and highly treacherous.

==Behind the scenes==
=== Etymology ===
Midnite derives her name from "midnight."

===Nomenclature===
In Spanish, Midnite is called "Medianocta," which comes from Spanish Medianoche, which is a literal translation of "midnight." Her Portuguese name is "Madruguita," derived from Madrugada, which means "Dawn." Her French name is just the French term for "midnight," Minuit.

== Trivia ==
* Midnite is the only Spark Hunter that doesn't have legs. She is also the only one who has the ability to fly.
** Midnite is also one of two bosses ([[Cursa]] is another) who has these traits as well as the only one who is immune to Line-of-sight moves normally.
* The song that plays during the first phase of Midnite's battle is done in a swing style.
