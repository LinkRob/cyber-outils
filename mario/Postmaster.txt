|race = [[Koopa Paratroopa]]}}The '''Postmaster''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is the boss of [[Parakarry]] and his [[Post Office]] can be found within [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]].

==Appearance==
The Postmaster is an elderly [[Koopa Paratroopa]] that has a mustache. He also has a blue shell, wears a blue hat, and wears glasses that hides his pupils.

==History==
The Postmaster can be talked to anytime to read certain letters given to only [[Mario]]'s partners (as letters to Mario can always be found at his [[Mario's House|house]]). However, during the events of Chapter 4, the Postmaster's [[mailbag]] gets stolen by a [[Shy Guy]] (which makes reading the mail impossible to do at the time). If Mario retrieves the Mailbag for him, the Postmaster will reward Mario with a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]].
