'''Isaac Newton''' is a real-world person best known for developing three fundamental laws of motion. He made his first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearance in ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''.

==In ''Mario's Time Machine''==
In the [[MS-DOS]] and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] versions, [[Bowser]] travels to 1687 in his [[Timulator]] and steals the [[apple]] that Isaac Newton should have observed to assist his contemplation on gravity and puts it among the artifacts in [[Bowser's Museum]]. Mario time-travels to 1687 to return the apple himself, and after learning about its historical importance, Mario put the apple back into the tree so it could later fall for Newton.

In the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] [[Mario's Time Machine (Nintendo Entertainment System)|version]], Mario instead gets his information from [[Message Block|info boxes]] to learn about Newton and the apple. He also does not return the apple to Newton directly, but rather to the tree. The return of the apple sparks Newton's interest in studying gravity, leading to the publication of [[Principia|"The Principia"]], as well as other works, such as "Optics".
