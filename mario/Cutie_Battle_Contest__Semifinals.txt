'''Cutie Battle Contest, Semifinals''' is the ninth level of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 19.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains  2 [[Elite Pestnut]]s and 6 [[Boomerang Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Boomerang Bro.

The 2nd battle contains 3 Elite Pestnuts, 1 [[Koopa Paratroopa]], and 4 [[Starkiss]]es. The enemy captain is a Starkiss.

The 3rd battle contains 6 Elite Pestnuts, and 2 Starkisses. The enemy captain is a Elite Pestnut.

The final battle contains 2 Elite Pestnuts, 2 Koopa Paratroopas, 1 Starkiss, 1 Boomerang Bro, 1 [[Magikoopa]] ( Green), and [[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]]. The enemy captain is Wendy, and she knows [[Deny]] and [[Pass the Bob-omb]].

After the final battle, the player recruits Wendy.

The battle can be replayed to recruit up to 1 Koopa Paratroopa, Boomerang Bro, and/or Magikoopa (Green).

== Trivia ==
* While the Chargin' Chuck is said to be in the level, they can not be found in any of the battles.
