'''Paint balls'''<ref>''Mario Power Tennis'' instruction booklet, page 24.</ref> are objects in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Paint balls appear only in the [[Minigame|Special Game]] [[Artist on the Court]]. They come in varying colors and appear out of [[Warp Pipe]]s for the player's character to hit with their [[tennis racket]] into the colorless sections of a mural. Paint balls essentially function like a bucket fill tool in photo editing software, filling in the sections they hit, and even if a paint ball is hit incorrectly, the mural remains unaffected.

Once every specifically colored portion of a mural is filled in, paint balls stop appearing from the Warp Pipes in identical colors, so the variety of paint ball colors decreases as gameplay progresses.
