The '''Boo-Away Orb''' is an [[Orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. This is the successor to the [[Boo Repellent]], [[Gaddlight]], and [[Bone Capsule]] (from [[Mario Party 3]], [[Mario Party 4|4]], and [[Mario Party 5|5]]).

==Description==
The Boo-Away Orb can only be activated when [[Red Boo|Pink Boo]] comes to steal a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] or [[coin]]s. It produces a [[flashlight]] that scares Pink Boo away. The Boo-Away Orb is functionally similar to the [[Boo Repellent]] from ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' and the [[Gaddlight]] from ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', as both are used by a character scare off the regular [[Boo]] sent by their opponent. The Boo-Away Orb is functionally comparable to the [[Snack Orb]], which is also used automatically to protect the user from losing a Star, in this case from a [[Chain Chomp]].

The Boo-Away Orb and Gaddlight are identified as the same subject in Japanese versions. The flashlight within the Boo-Away Orb looks very similar to the Gaddlight, even depicting the same icon of a cross icon over a Boo, except the flashlight in the Boo-Away Orb is yellow.

==In ''Mario Party 6''==
The Boo-Away Orb is only obtainable in [[Towering Treetop]] and [[Castaway Bay]] because they are the only two [[board]]s with a Pink Boo House, where Pink Boo can be summoned. When the targeted opponent uses their Boo-Away Orb automatically, the character who enlisted the help of Pink Boo effectively wastes 5 coins if they asked her to steal coins or 40 coins if they asked her to steal a Star.
