'''Underwater Mines''' are enemies which first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Underwater Mines are green {{w|naval mine}}s with some golden plating. They are attached to a vertical chain. Underwater Mines have a pair of eyes, but they often keep theirs closed.

===Behavior===
Underwater Mines have the ability to regenerate themselves after detonating, making them practically invincible in this regard.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Underwater Mines are encountered in [[Deep Dark Galaxy]] only. Underwater Mines have airborne counterparts known as [[Floating Mine]]s, which are in more galaxies than them.

===''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''===
Underwater Mines are encountered in one [[galaxy]] only, [[Cosmic Cove Galaxy]], similarly to ''Super Mario Galaxy''. Floating Mines also make a returning appearance, still more common than Underwater Mines as before.
