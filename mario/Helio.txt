A '''Helio''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are small orbs of fire encountered in [[Barrel Volcano]] during the fight with [[Czar Dragon]].

== Description ==
Originally, Helios looked like brownish-red circles with a bright orange spot near the upper left of their sprite. The Nintendo Switch remake designs them in a manner reminiscent of magma, being brown striated with orange.

== Battle ==
Helios can only be summoned into battle by the [[Czar Dragon]]. Helios only attack by dashing or exploding at one of [[Mario]]'s party. They are not capable of any other special attacks.

=== Strategy ===
Helios are weak against [[Ice (element)|HP]] moves, so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] is very effective on them. This is not usually necessary because Helios have very low HP and can be taken out by any damaging move.
