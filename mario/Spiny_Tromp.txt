A '''Spiny Tromp''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
Spiny Tromps are large, spiky and round species of [[Thwomp]]s that are only found in [[Mount Lineland]] and [[Underwhere Road]]. Spiny Tromps are invincible and cannot be defeated in any way and they are too large to jump over. The only way to get by Spiny Tromps is to use [[Mario]]'s [[Flip]] ability in order to get by them.
