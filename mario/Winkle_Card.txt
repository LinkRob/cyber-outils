The '''Winkle Card''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is a treasure to the [[Winkle]] race.

==Physical description==
The Winkle Card appears to resemble a Winkle shell that is purple in color.

==Effects==
The Winkle Card is mainly used to grant access further back of the [[Winkle Colosseum]]. However, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must first play the mini-game [[Chuckola Bounce]] before they can do so. After they do, they can access the back area where the [[Beanstar]] piece rests.
