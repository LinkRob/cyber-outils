'''Mario Stadium''' is a sports stadium which appears in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''.

==Description==
Mario Stadium is a standard court featuring no gimmicks. All four sports can be played here, including Dodgeball, Basketball, Volleyball, and Hockey. The tutorial films take place here, just like [[Mario Stadium (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Mario Stadium]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. Mario Stadium is one of the selectable courts in the [[Bob-omb Dodge]] minigame, the other two being [[Ghoulish Galleon]] and [[Western Junction]].
