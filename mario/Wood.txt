The '''Wood''' ('''Wooden''' in Australia and Europe) wheels are unlockable wheels in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. They have good [[acceleration]] and [[handling]], but lack good [[speed]] and [[weight]]. The wheels are similar to the wheels of the [[DK Jumbo]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.
