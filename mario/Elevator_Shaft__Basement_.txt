The '''Elevator Shaft''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Description==
The Elevator Shaft is located on the [[Basement (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Basement]] of [[The Last Resort]], directly east of the [[Garage (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Garage]] and northwest of the [[Service Stairwell]]. The Elevator Shaft has several [[Spider (Luigi's Mansion series)|spiders]] and [[Spider Web]]s. It also has a ladder that leads up to two higher floors, but only the upper one is reachable. There is a painting that [[Luigi]] can use the [[Dark-Light Device]] on to make [[coin]]s appear.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3''==
The Elevator Shaft is an optional room. Luigi can only access the Elevator Shaft once he has learned the [[Suction Shot]], allowing him to pull off the panel in the northeast corner of the garage. On the top floor of the Elevator Shaft, [[Gooigi]] can slip through the grate to drop down to the floor directly below, where he can collect a blue [[Gem (Luigi's Mansion series)|gem]].
