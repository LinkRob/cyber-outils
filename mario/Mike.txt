'''Mike''' is a karaoke robot created and programmed by [[Dr. Crygor]] in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. He first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Biography==
Mike was created to serve as an assistant for Dr. Crygor, though Mike does not always get along with him.
{{sectstub}}

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mike is a blue, humanoid robot. His chest has two yellow speakers, outlined by orange speaker boxes. There are two microphones mounted onto a yellow mic stand on Mike's head, pointing left and right each, and both microphones have a yellow handle. Aside from the speaker box, Mike's eyes and eyebrows are also orange. Lines are shown on Mike's body where his joints would be located.

Originally, in ''WarioWare: Touched!'', the orange parts of Mike's body are colored red.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare: Touched!''===
In the opening for Mic's story, [[Mic Rocking]], Dr. Crygor decides to leave the [[Dr. Crygor's Lab|lab]], and programs Mike to clean it while he is gone. When Mike blows away some dust, his karaoke programming overrides his cleaning software, compelling Mike to host a party, so Mike blasts off into space and hosts a karaoke tournament on a planet with [[Alien Bunny|Alien Bunnies]]. During this, the player plays through Mike's [[microgame]]s, all of which all involve the use of the Nintendo DS microphone.

In the ending of Mike's story, after Mike wins the competition, Dr. Crygor is flown to the planet by [[Dribble and Spitz]] and tells Mike that it is time for them to return home. Back at the lab, Dr. Crygor wishes to sing a duet with Mike. Mike ends up insulting Dr. Crygor's singing,stating that it is "99.78% awful," starting an argument between the two of them. After this, Mike goes to the [[Hawt House]] to join the other characters.

===''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''===
Mike is first seen in [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s microgame set [[The Invent-Off]], both as a framed picture and cheering on Dr. Crygor in the [[Invent-Off]] event. Upon winning, Penny uses one of Mike's head-mounted microphones to sing, and Mike is shown dancing alongside her and Dr. Crygor.

Later, during the [[The Kelorometer]] microgame set, Mike assists Dr. Crygor in showing off the latter's latest invention, the [[Kelorometer]], a machine designed to burn "kelories" (a pun on calories).

===''[[Game & Wario]]''===
Mike is the host of the multiplayer minigame, [[Disco]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Mike's name is pronounced the same as "mic," a shortening of "microphone;" this acknowledges how Mike is a karaoke robot who has two microphones on his head.
