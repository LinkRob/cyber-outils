'''Bunny Belt''' is a single-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Opening==
The player's character walks up to the toy-making controls.

==Gameplay==
The player has to correctly input a sequence of buttons displayed on the panel above the controls to create a bunny doll. They must do this as much as possible within the [[time limit]]. If the player presses the wrong controls, the bunny doll comes out looking like something else unless they press {{button|gba|B}} to restart the process. 

In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the object is to create ten bunny dolls within thirty seconds. In Mini-Game Attack, the time limit is reduced to twenty seconds. In Play Land, whenever ten bunny dolls are created, the sequence of button for creating the bunny dolls are replaced by different controls, and this also increases the timer by 10 seconds.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|Pad}}: Press switch
*{{button|gba|A}}: Make toy
*{{button|gba|B}}: Restart toy

==Flavor text==
*In-game description: ''Press the + Control Pad and the A Button in order to manufacture cute dolls.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a pun on "money belt," but with the first word substituted for "bunny," because the minigame is about creating bunny dolls.
