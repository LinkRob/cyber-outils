The '''Turbo Track''' is a secret course in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. To access it, [[Mario]] must have the [[Rocket Nozzle]] and use it to charge into the [[Turbo-bashable doors]] between the [[Isle Delfino Police]]. In doing so, Mario is taken into the Turbo Track, where he can collect a [[Shine Sprite]] at the end of a series of platforms.
