'''Upward Mobility''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. It has a similar music to [[Skyjinks]] and gameplay to ''[[Mario Party 3]]''{{'}}s [[Awful Tower]].

==Gameplay==
*Use the [[White-Green Checkered Block|Jump Blocks]] to bounce up to the goal high above the clouds!
*To finish this quickly, you should only bounce once on each Jump Blocks and ignore any other blocks.

==Controls==
The game requires the [[Wii Remote]] to be held sideways.

*D-Pad: Move
*2 button: Jump
