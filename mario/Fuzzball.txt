'''Fuzzballs''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Fuzzballs are ball-shaped creatures covered in purple fur. They have light-blue feet, which are not covered in hair, and a Fuzzball's only visible facial feature is its wide mouth.

===Behavior===
Fuzzballs cannot damage [[Luigi]] on contact or from attacks, but they instead chase Luigi around and get in the way of the [[Poltergust 5000]]. If Luigi uses his Poltergust 5000 to pick up a Fuzzball, he can shoot it around the room to stun it temporarily.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
Fuzzballs mainly appear in the [[Secret Mine]]'s [[Chalet]]. They very rarely appear in the [[Treacherous Mansion]]'s [[Dark Age Exhibit]] when interacting with the furniture. Fuzzballs have a variant named [[Gold Fuzzball]], which turn into regular Fuzzballs after being thrown around three times.
