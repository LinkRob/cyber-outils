A '''nubbin''' is an object which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Description==
Nubbins are found all throughout [[Bowser's body]]. They appear red and swollen, although [[Mario]] can use his [[hammer]] to hit a nubbin to affect the environment in some way, such as by causing a platform to appear or a path to open.
