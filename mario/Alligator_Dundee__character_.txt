'''Alligator Dundee''' is a one-time character who appears in the titular ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Alligator Dundee (episode)|Alligator Dundee]]." He is a parody of {{w|Michael "Crocodile" Dundee}} of ''{{w|Crocodile Dundee}}''.

==Episode appearances==
In "Alligator Dundee," he chases a monster into [[Mario Brothers Plumbing]], and they decide to help him capture it. Unbeknownst to Alligator Dundee, the monster turns out to be [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s friend, [[Ratagator]]. Mario and Luigi stop him, and he apologizes, changes his name to '''Ratagator Dungee''', and decides to make a movie about his life.
