'''Singapore Speedway 1''' is a race course from the mobile game ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is the first route of Singapore Speedway, and its path focuses strongly on the Marina Bay Sands resort.

== Layout ==
The racers start this course on the Esplanade Bridge, with a four-way intersection up ahead. The south path at the right heads into the Chinatown, while the north path to the left ends at a ramp; both paths are blocked. After the racers get through this section, they will encounter a slight right turn, then be forced to the left. After the racers get through this turn, they will spot a [[Cannon (Mario Kart series)|cannon]], which they use to [[Glider|fly]] up to a swimming pool on top of the Marina Bay Sands resort that they drive through. The route here curves to the left. When the racers get to the other side, they will encounter a [[Glide Ramp]] that they use to [[Glider|fly]] over the water and a soccer field, touching down at the other side of the field. The racers can go through some rings here for a speed boost. When the racers touch down at the southwest corner of the course, they will find one more left turn before reaching the [[Finish line|starting line]].

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the racers will go through this route for the first lap of [[Singapore Speedway]] (although the north and south paths are no longer blocked though they [[Arrow Field|still count as out-of-bounds]] at this time), then drive down it some more when they start Lap 2, going into the Chinatown from the "far entrance."
