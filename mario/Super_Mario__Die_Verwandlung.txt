'''''Super Mario: Die Verwandlung''''' is a 4-page comic in the January 1993 issue of the German ''[[Club Nintendo (German magazine)|Club Nintendo]]''. It was later reprinted in the January 1996 issue.

== Description ==
One morning, [[Mario]]'s alarm clock rings like every other day. Mario gets up, yawning, and thinks about a nightmare he had. Mario also complains that he feels somehow disorganized and makes his way to the bathroom. When Mario looks in the mirror, he is shocked because his pixels acted on their own last night and he is completely pixelated. Mario searches his house for his missing pixels, but when he cannot find them, Mario consults [[Dr. Light]] and informs him about his change.
[[File:SM Die Verwandlung page 3 panel 6.png|thumb|200px|left|Mario is accidentally duplicated into Wario from Dr. Light's machine]]
Mario hurries to see Dr. Light but not before putting a paper bag over his head so that no one on the street will recognize him. Most are scared by the paper bag except for a kid, who tells his mom that he wants to look like that, but his mom tells him not to look at Mario. Mario arrives to his destination and notes that he had not run this fast since [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] proposed to him. The door to Dr. Light's office is opened by [[Mega Man]], who says that the carnival is not for another four weeks, until he recognizes Mario under the bag. Dr. Light sighs, thinking it is someone complaining about plastic surgery again, and says he did his best, thinking Mario is [[Link]]. Mario reveals himself to Dr. Light and affirms that he has a problem with his pixelated appearance. Dr. Light, who is somewhat distracted, mishears Mario, thinking that he said "pimples" instead of "pixels," while Mega Man interjects with the suggestion of "Oil of Mega Man" for dry skin. Dr. Light suddenly remembers Mario's actual of missing pixels, though still refers to him as Link. Without further ado, Dr. Light puts Mario on one of his inventions and explains his plan to duplicate Mario and put him back together as one.

However, when Dr. Light flips the switch and starts the machine, this results in a serious mistake of creating [[Wario]] instead of a second Mario. Wario repeatedly swears how evil he is while he smashes the door and runs out. Mario, Mega Man and Dr. Light give chase, and Mario manages to catch Wario with a large net. Dr. Light devises a new plan to fix the mistake by using a [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. Both Mario and Wario are given a Super Nintendo Entertainment System Controller each and are instructed to press the Start button at the same time. The plan is successful, as Mario returns to normal and Wario has disappeared. Mario is now properly recognized by Dr. Light again.

Just as everything seems resolved in the end, the telephone rings suddenly and Dr. Light goes to answer. The call is from [[Luigi]], who notifies that he has become black and white, to the irritation of Dr. Light, Mario, and Mega Man.
