'''On the Mark''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' as one of [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s microgames, named '''Chalk Full''' and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Kat and Ana]]'s microgames. The original microgame's name is a pun on the phrase "chock full".

==Gameplay==
The player has to use the [[stylus]] to rub across the drawing, which can sometimes be one of [[Mario]], to erase it from the blackboard. In the ''WarioWare Gold'', a whiteboard is used instead, and Mario is no longer a possible drawing, although one of his [[Mario's Cap|cap]] is.

===Difficulty levels===
====''WarioWare: Touched!''====
*'''1st level difficulty:''' There is one drawing to erase.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' There are four drawings to erase.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' Same as Level 2, but some chalk starts to draw more on the board.

====''WarioWare Gold''====
*'''1st level difficulty:''' There is one small drawing to erase.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' There is one medium drawing or three small drawings to erase.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' There is one large drawing or three small drawings to erase, and there is a chance of a whiteboard marker drawing a line on the board.
