==History==
Rulers are minor enemies that only appear in [[Frantic Factory]]. There are only seen after [[Chunky Kong]] releases a bunch of toy enemies from the toy box with his [[Primate Punch]]. Two Rulers move rather quickly toward Chunky but they can be easily taken out with any attack. Rulers are also used as arms for the [[Toy Monster]].
