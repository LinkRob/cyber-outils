'''KP Koopas''' are a variant of [[Koopa Troopa]] featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
KP Koopas and regular Koopa Troopas do not have that many differences from one another, namely in battle. These guys have the same stats as well as the same attitude. The KP Koopas are fought while participating in the [[Glitz Pit]]. These guys are minor league fighters who teach [[Mario]] about what the Glitz Pit is about and who is all in it. One of the members of the "KP Koopas" is a [[KP Paratroopa]].
