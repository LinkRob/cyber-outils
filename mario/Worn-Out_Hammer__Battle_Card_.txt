|name=Worn-Out Hammer (Battle Card)}}'''Worn-Out Hammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Worn-Out Hammer is the weakest version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a brown hammer with a white patch on the corner. There is an orange/white striped background.
==Price==
Buy Price: N/A

Sell Price: 1 coin

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Worn-Out Hammer is first seen in [[Port Prisma]], but can also found in [[Ruddy Road]], [[Cherry Lake]], [[Indigo Underground]], [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Sacred Forest]], and [[Cobalt Base]].
