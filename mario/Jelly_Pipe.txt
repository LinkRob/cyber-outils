'''Jelly Pipe''' is the third [[level]] of [[P. 2-Cavern]] in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. In the level, the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has to navigate through a sewer filled with [[jelly]].

Jelly Pipe has a variety of newspapers in the background displaying several words that reference various [[Nintendo]] characters and products, including [[Luigi]], [[Wario]], [[Mario]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], [[Nintendo 64|N64]], [[Pikachu]], [[Nintendo]], and [[Yoshi]]. These same newspapers also appear in [[Torrential Maze]].
