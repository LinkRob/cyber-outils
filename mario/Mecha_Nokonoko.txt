'''Mecha Nokonoko''' is a vehicle and boss in ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]''.

==Description==
Mecha Nokonoko is a very large [[Koopa Troopa]]-based submarine with a turret mounted on its back. It is four-legged like the Koopa Troopas in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==In ''Yoshi's Safari''==
Mecha Nokonoko is the eleventh boss, being fought at the end of [[Dark Sea]].
