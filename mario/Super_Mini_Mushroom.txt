The '''Super Mini Mushroom''' is a [[Mushroom]] that only appears in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. It is a glowing [[Mini Mushroom]] overlaid by a metallic-like material.

==Description==
The Super Mini Mushroom is an item that turns its user into [[Mini Mario|a pint-sized version of themselves]], similar to the normal Mini Mushroom, which only lets the player move five or fewer spaces. The Super Mini Mushroom, although more expensive and rare, overcomes this problem by allowing the player to roll two [[Dice Block]]s numbered 1 through 5. And it is possible to roll the normal number of spaces (up to 10) with this item. However, the player can't get a total of "1" (as there are two 1-5 Dice Blocks instead of one). They can only get a total of 2-10. The effects of the Super Mini Mushroom wear off when the player moves the number of spaces decided by the Dice Blocks. If the player rolls any matching pairs of numbers (two "1's", "2's", "3's", "4's", or "5's") they get a bonus 10 coins. 

Super Mini Mushrooms can be won during the [[minigame]]s on the board, the [[Lottery Shop]], or through the [[Item Shop]]s. Unlike the [[Mini Mushroom]], this item --along with the [[Super Mega Mushroom]]-- cannot be found on a [[Mushroom Space]] nor can its effect be inflicted on opponents (with a [[MiniMega Hammer]]).

While mini, players can play special pint-sized minigames if they pass certain locations on the board. Depending on the game, the player has a chance to win an [[item]] or some [[coin]]s. Additionally, a player can pass through [[tiny pipe]]s, too small for normal sized players. The reactions to players by characters on the board or the effect of a board event on the player may also change.
