'''Mushroom Stadium''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Mushroom Stadium is a stadium located in the [[Desert Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It has a red dome exterior that covers a set of stands and a pair of straight {{w|track and field}} lanes. It is where [[Bullet Bill (character)|Bullet Bill]] goes for training.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
[[File:A Speeding Bill race.png|thumb|left|Mario and Bullet Bill racing one another in ''Mario Party Advance'']]
Mushroom Stadium is where Bullet Bill's quest, [[A Speeding Bill]], takes place. In it, the player's character has to race Bullet Bill from one end of the stadium to the other. Mushroom Stadium is also featured in the [[Kamek Krew Live!]] quest, in which the player's character must find a venue where the [[Kamek Krew]] can perform. Bullet Bill allows the Kamek Krew play at the Mushroom Stadium because he is a big fan of theirs.
