'''Ba-dum BUM!''' is the first level of [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. It takes place in the overall grassy plains of [[Yoshi's Island]] with a good view of the sea. The level acts as a tutorial for how to utilize [[Yoshi Egg]]s as well as other actions such as dashing. 

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the right and here you will come across two [[Shy Guy]]s and a [[Crazee Dayzee]], either beat or ignore the enemies and continue onward, from here, grab the [[Flower (Yoshi's Island)|Flower]] and then hit the [[Winged Cloud]] and climb the stairs formed by it to reach the three [[Red Coin]]s and the [[Flower (Yoshi's Island)|Flower]]. After obtaining the collectibles, continue right, kill the chomping [[Piranha Plant]] and grab the [[Red Coin]] atop the Pipe, enter the Pipe and hit a [[! Switch]] to make some [[Coin]]s appear, use [[Baby Mario]]'s dash ability to collect them all. After this, exit the cavern and continue along the main path. Be sure to get the [[Winged Cloud]] containing the [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] if you haven't already and drop down to get the next [[Flower (Yoshi's Island)|Flower]]. From here, kill the [[Piranha Plant]] and jump on the [[Ferris Wheel]] and obtain the [[Red Coin]]s around it, continue along the path (underneath the "cheese"-like block, there is a hidden [[Winged Cloud]] containing some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] so be sure to hit it). Continue onward and grab the next [[Flower (Yoshi's Island)|Flower]] above a [[Pipe]] where [[Shy Guy]]s endlessly spawn out of. Another [[Winged Cloud]] can be seen here, hit it to make a [[Flower Plant]] grow, jump along the leaves to reach the higher portion of the level, turn left and hit the next [[Winged Cloud]] to make some stairs appear, climb the stairs to the island which contains a hidden [[Winged Cloud]] containing some more [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and a [[Winged Cloud]] above containing the final flower [[Flower (Yoshi's Island)|Flower]]. There is also a [[Fly Guy]] here carrying a 1-Up, use any attack to steal the extra life from him. From here, continue back along the main path, the [[Ferris Wheel]] contains some more [[Red Coin]]s, get the coins and tag the [[Middle Ring]] to earn some extra [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], watch out for the [[Windbag]] which spews out [[Nipper Spore]]s at you and from here, hit the [[Winged Cloud]]s for some more treasures while avoiding some [[Glide Guy]]s and [[Woozy Guy]]s, get the last of the [[Red Coin]]s and then jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
You will need [[Baby Mario]] to obtain this [[Baby Coin]]. After the [[Middle Ring]] past the [[Windbag]], there is a [[Warp Pipe]] enter the [[Warp Pipe]] to find a secret area with the [[Baby Coin]] inside.
