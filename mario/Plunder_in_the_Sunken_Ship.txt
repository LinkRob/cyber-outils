'''Plunder in the Sunken Ship''' is the first [[mission]] of [[Jolly Roger Bay]] in [[Super Mario 64]] and it's [[Reissue|remake]]: [[Super Mario 64 DS]].

== Description ==
To collect the first [[Power Star]] of [[Jolly Roger Bay]], [[Mario]] must go into the water and swim down towards a sunken ship. Once there, Mario has to lure the [[Eel]] out of his spot so he can swim inside of the ship. While he's inside the ship, Mario must open a chest so the water will lower and to make the Star accessible. At the top of the room there will be a yellow box with a Star inside it and Mario can climb the small platforms to collect the Star.
