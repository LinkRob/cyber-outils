The '''Talking Parrot''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
The Talking Parrot is sold in the [[Toad's Shop|Mushroom Shop]] in [[Village Square|Mushroom Village]] for 50 [[Coin]]s. After being purchased, the Talking Parrot is sent to the [[Option House]]. Every time the player selects the Talking Parrot, it says a random quote from the vast variety of voice clips in the game. However, this is not a sound test, for there is not an option to select a specific clip the player wishes to hear.

A similar object is the [[Parrot Box]] that comes from ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. Both of these objects are similar except that the player can now pick a certain character voice instead of hoping that the Talking Parrot says the quote they want, and the Parrot Box can also be used for free.
