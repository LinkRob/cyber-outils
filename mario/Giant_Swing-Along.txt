==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Jump on the third Koopa and spin the shell to the left to reveal a vine on the block it was standing on. Climb the vine to a hidden passage and use the other Koopa to destroy the smaller brick blocks and get the Dtar Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Be in Super form to destroy the brick blocks below the sixth Piranhacus Giganticus.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Jump on any of the last three Koopas to and push it to the right and it knock down the plants and get the Star Coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
While growing the vine leading to the first Star Coin, look at the picture down below on the left showing a picture of standard Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' dying.
