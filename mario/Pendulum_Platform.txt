'''Pendulum Platforms''' are a type of object which appear in the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' sub-series]]. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Description==
Pendulum Platforms almost exclusively appear within [[castle]] and [[tower]] levels. They hang from the ceiling, where they swing back and forth like a pendulum, as their name suggests. [[Mario]] or another playable character often has to wait for a Pendulum Platform to sway completely to one side before he can continue to another platform either above or to the right of him.
