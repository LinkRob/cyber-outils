'''Body Builder''' is a 2-vs.-2 mini-game which appears in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each player on a team takes turns stopping a turning wheel on the right spot to pick a part and build a robot. The glowing spot indicates which part goes next on the robot. Whichever team gets the four robot parts first wins the mini-game.
