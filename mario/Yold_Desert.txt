The '''Yold Desert''' is a location in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is found within [[Lineland]] and is the main setting for Chapter 1-3. Its name comes from the article "the" (though written as "ye") and "old".

== History ==
===Chapter 1-3: The Sands of Yold===
Following his journey through [[Yold Town]], Mario runs along the desert until he meets [[O'Chunks]], who was sent by Count Bleck to defeat Mario. Mario eventually outpowers O'Chunks, who retreats after the battle. After some more exploring, Mario comes upon a blue platform which requires the player to press the 1 button and the - button the same time when Mario is on the platform. The entrance to the Yold Ruins is revealed after this has been done.
