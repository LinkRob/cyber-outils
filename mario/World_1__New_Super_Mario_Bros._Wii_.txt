'''World 1''' is a grassland [[world]] that is the first world of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

World 1 consists of six regular [[level]]s, a [[Tower]], a [[Castle]], and [[Warp Cannon]]. The boss of the world is [[Larry Koopa]]. There are three [[Mushroom House]]s, two of which are red and one is gold, as well as [[Princess Peach's Castle]], where various video clips can be watched about how playing the game. The two red [[Toad House]]s become accessible upon completing [[World 1-2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1-2]] and [[World 1-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|1-4]] respectively, and the gold [[Toad House]] can be unlocked by completing [[World 1-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1-3]].
