}}'''Rings of Fire!''' (or '''World 5-4''') is the fourth and final level of World 5 in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. 

==Layouts==
<tabber>
Pink Coins = The level begins with two [[Dry Bones]] as well as a [[Ring of Fire]] with a [[Super Mushroom]]. The first Pink Coin is just up ahead, inside a moving Ring of Fire. After passing another Dry Bones, regardless of which path the player takes, they will be led to the Rings of Fire with the second Pink Coin. The player will approach a [[Red Ring]] and collect some [[Red Coin]]s to get a [[Starman]]. The player must then jump through a Rings of Fire to collect the third Pink Coin. Afterwards, the player must jump up to the center and follow a platform to a Rings of Fire with the fourth Pink Coin. The player will then follow a series of Rings of Fire, with the fifth and final Pink Coin in the very last Rings of Fire. Afterwards, the player will approach two more Rings of Fire, along with [[Fake Bowser]]. Just like in the [[Bowser's Castle Hangout|previous]] [[Fire Bar Castle! Youch!|two]] castle levels, the player must avoid Fake Bowser and hit the ax, clearing the level.
|-|Purple Coins= The level begins with two Dry Bones as well as a Rings of Fire with the first Purple Coin. A Super Mushroom can be found just up ahead, inside a moving Rings of Fire. After passing another Dry Bones, regardless of which path the player takes, they will be led to the Rings of Fire with the second Purple Coin. The player will approach a Red Ring and collect some Red Coins to get a Starman. The player must then jump through a Rings of Fire to collect the third Purple Coin. Afterwards, the player must jump up to the center and follow a platform to a Ring of Fire with the fourth Purple Coin. The player will then follow a series of Rings of Fire, with the fifth and final Purple Coin in the second to last Ring of Fire. Afterwards, the player will approach two more Rings of Fire, along with Fake Bowser. Just like in the previous two castle levels, the player must avoid Fake Bowser and hit the ax, clearing the level.
|-|Black Coins = The level begins with a Dry Bones as well as a Ring of Fire with a [[Hidden Coin]] that turns into the first Black Coin; the player must then use the back arrow block to truly collect it. A Super Mushroom can be found just up ahead, inside a moving Ring of Fire. After passing another Dry Bones, regardless of which path the player takes, they will be led to the Ring of Fire with the second Black Coin. The player will approach a Red Ring and collect some Red Coins to get a Starman. The player must then jump through a Ring of Fire and jump off of the [[Hard Block]] to collect the third Black Coin. Afterwards, the player must jump up to the very top and follow a platform to a Ring of Fire with the fourth Black Coin. The player will then follow a series of Rings of Fire, with the fifth and final Black Coin in the very last Ring of Fire. Afterwards, the player will approach two more Rings of Fire, along with Fake Bowser. Just like in the previous two castle levels, the player must avoid Fake Bowser and hit the ax, clearing the level.
