'''Shaker''' is a special move used by [[Boomer (boss)|Boomer]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Shaker is used, the selected target is hit and the impact is described as a blue star.

==Effects==
Shaker is an instant death attack meaning it will automatically take down one of [[Mario]]'s party. Blocking the attack at the right moment or wearing the Quartz Charm are the only ways to avoid being damaged by this attack.

==Trivia==
*The description for Shaker is very similar in appearance to [[Jinx]]'s [[Silver Bullet]] techique.
**Coincidentally, both of these attacks are instant death attacks used by only one character in the game.
