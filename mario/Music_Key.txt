'''Music Keys''' are items in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Description==
The Music Keys give the power of music, and they are locked up safely until [[Waluigi]] steals them from [[Truffle Towers]] and takes one for himself, releasing the other three into the ocean, [[World 3 (Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix)|Wario's carnival]], and a huge [[Freezie]]. After the keys are stolen, [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) and [[Toad]] attempt to retrieve them from Waluigi. Mario and Toad eventually do so and return to Truffle Towers, but [[Bowser]] appears and steals the Music Keys. Mario and Toad follow him to [[Bowser's Castle|his castle]] and steal the keys back. After losing a [[Bowser's Castle (Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix)|dance-off]] to Mario, Bowser confesses he was going to use the Music Keys to fix his tone-deafness. Mario then uses the keys to turn the area surrounding Bowser's Castle into a green field, causing everyone to start dancing. Afterwards, the Music Keys are returned to Truffle Towers.
