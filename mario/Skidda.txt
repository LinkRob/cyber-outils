'''Skiddas''' are purple variants of [[Kremling|Kremlings]] found throughout the [[Northern Kremisphere]] in [[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!|''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!'']] and it's [[Donkey Kong Land III|Game Boy Advance remake]]. They attack [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] and [[Kiddy Kong]] by sliding towards them on slippery ice.

== History ==
Only appearing in 3 of the games' levels, Skiddas are one of the rarest enemies to encounter, and the only levels they appear in are snow-themed:

* [[Skidda's Row]]
* [[Tearaway Toboggan]]
* [[Lemguin Lunge]]
