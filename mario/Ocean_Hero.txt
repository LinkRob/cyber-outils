'''Ocean Hero''' is a [[microgame]] featured in [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[Nature]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place at the bottom of an ocean, where there is garbage and sometimes sea creatures.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to manually collect all of the garbage by moving them to the net. Alternatively, they can attack the garbage into the currents pointed toward to the net, although the player's character is also affected by the currents. Once all the garbage has been caught, the player wins at the microgame, but if they fail to gather all the trash, they lose.

In two-player mode, whoever cleans more garbage receives an effort point.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' There are three plastic bags to catch and a few currents.
*'''Level 2:''' It also has three plastic bags, although there are four bundles of ropes and currents that form a loop. Sea snakes sometimes appear instead of ropes.
*'''Level 3:''' There are five plastic bags to catch and currents that form two loops. Jellyfish sometimes replace some of the bags.

In two-player mode, there are several differences:

*'''Level 1:''' There are five plastic bags, like Level 3 in single-player, and a simple arrangement of currents.
*'''Level 2:''' It also has five plastic bags, but there are seven bundles of ropes and a more complex arrangement of currents.
*'''Level 3:''' There are nine plastic bags and a complex arrangement of currents.
