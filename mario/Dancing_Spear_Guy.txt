'''Dancing Spear Guys''' are a variant of [[Spear Guy]]s that make their debut in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. Here, they have a pattern of repeatedly dancing while holding up a tall spear. Dancing Spear Guys also appear in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]].
