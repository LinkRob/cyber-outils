The '''Shop Key''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Shop Key is a [[key]] that can be found on [[Twilight Trail]] behind a log blocking the way. The only way to get the Shop Key at that point is to use [[Koops]]. After retrieving the Shop Key, [[Mario]] can then unlock the door in the [[Twilight Town]] [[Item Shop]] to get into the storage room where a Black Chest lurks. Unlocking it with the [[Black Key]] (that is also found on Twilight Trail but in a small shed) will allow Mario to use the Paper Tube ability.
