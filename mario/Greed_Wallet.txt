The '''Greed Wallet''' is a special item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It can only be obtained by making [[Starbeans Espresso|espresso]] at [[Starbeans Cafe]] for the first time. In doing so, [[Professor E. Gadd]] then appears and gives the Greed Wallet to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

==Appearance==
The Greed Wallet, though despite its name, resembles a bag rather than a wallet.

==Usage==
When equipped, the Greed Wallet doubles how many coins Mario and Luigi earn after each battle. This can be especially helpful when trying to buy better items or enter the [[Winkle Colosseum]].
