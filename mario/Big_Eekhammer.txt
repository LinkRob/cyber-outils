|name=Big Eekhammer (Battle Card)}}'''Big Eekhammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Eekhammer is the strong and fast version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a big red hammer with a red/pink checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 15/60 coins

Sell Price: 8/30 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Eekhammer is first seen in [[Mondo Woods]], but can also found in [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Lighthouse Island]], [[Fortune Island]], and [[Mustard Café]].
