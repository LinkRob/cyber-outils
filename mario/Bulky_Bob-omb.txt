'''Bulky Bob-ombs''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. 

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bulky Bob-ombs are basically larger, pink [[Bob-omb]]s.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
In battle, Bulky Bob-ombs mainly attack by exploding at [[Mario]] and his team. If a Bulky Bob-omb explodes and it's near other Bulky Bob-ombs, an exploding chain will start and Mario and his team will be damaged by each explosion, something they cannot guard themselves against.

===Tips===
The best way to deal with Bulky Bob-omb is to use moves that doesn't cause any explosions. [[Koops]]'s [[Power Shell]] is one of the best ways to take out a group of them while Mario can use [[Earth Tremor]] or any other non-fire or explosion type moves.
