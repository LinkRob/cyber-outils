'''Chain Chomp''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. He is a representative of his own species, [[Chain Chomp]], and has an identical appearance to them. Chain Chomp's quest is titled [[Chomper Stomper]]. In it, Mario has to defeat Chain Chomp in the Duel minigame [[Chain Saw]]. In the ending, it is stated that Chain Chomp took [[Whomp (character)|Whomp]]'s place as the new guardian of [[Duel Tower]]'s first floor.
