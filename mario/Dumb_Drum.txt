'''Dumb Drum''' is the fifth boss in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' resembling a giant [[Oil Drum]]. Dumb Drum appears in the world [[Kremkroc Industries Inc.]] It appears to have a skull and two crossbones along with red eyes put on it. Like regular [[Oil Drum]]s, Dumb Drum spits out enemies.

Dumb Drum attempts to smash down on [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] a couple of times, then sends out enemies. The first enemy Dumb Drum sends out are [[Kritter]]s. After that, the Kongs must continue avoiding Dumb Drum and defeat the [[Klaptrap]]s, [[Army|Armies]], [[Klump]]s, and [[Slippa]]s. If the Kongs manage to defeat all of the enemies, Dumb Drum collapses and the [[Kong]]s can move on to [[Chimp Caverns]].

In the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake, Dumb Drum must be attacked by [[TNT Barrel]]s five times to collapse after spewing out an enemy species to make it collapse.

== Trivia ==
*In the SNES version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', Dumb Drum makes strange noises when hitting the ground.
