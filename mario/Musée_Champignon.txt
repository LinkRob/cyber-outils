'''Musée Champignon''' is a museum in [[Toad Town (The Origami King)|Toad Town]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''.

== Rooms ==
=== Lobby ===
When Mario enter the museum, he enters the Lobby. Here Mario is treated by its receptionist Toad. The receptionist desk nears [[Captain T. Ode]] submarine display and a white [[warp pipe]] to the Warp Pipe Room.

On the walls of the Lobby are the Framed Prints. They stores the print of the biggest fish Mario caught.

From the Lobby, Mario can enter the other rooms using doors.

=== Art Gallery ===
In this room, Mario can spend his Toad Points to unlock concept art of the game. Toad Points can be earned by rescue Toads.

=== Origami Toad Gallery ===
This room showcase different Origami Forms of each Toad that Mario have encountered or rescued.

=== Treasure Gallery ===
Mario can find Collectible Treasures in various places around the game world.
In this gallery, player can view all found treasures, and also discover some trivia about them. Collect all 120 treasures to earn a Trophy.

=== Origami Character Gallery ===
This Gallery shows all the Folded Soldiers that Mario have encountered. Defeat at least one of each Folded Soldier per Streamer, to complete the gallery. By completing the gallery, player earn a Trophy.

=== Sound Gallery ===
This Gallery is a place storing all the music heard in the game. To unlock the sounds, repair all of the Not-Bottomless Holes per area.
