'''Jump Shoes''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
The Jump Shoes are only usable by [[Mario]]. When Mario equips the Jump Shoes, he can use [[Jump]] attacks against any foe. Enemies invulnerable or resistant to Jump attacks take regular damage from Mario when he uses the Jump Shoes. The Jump Shoes can be purchased in [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and [[Rose Town]], as well as [[Seaside Town]] after [[Yaridovich]] is defeated.
