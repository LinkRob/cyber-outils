'''Yoshi''', also known as '''Yoshi Kid''', is [[Mario]]'s fourth party member in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He joins when [[Mario]] rescues the egg from the hot dog stand at [[Glitzville]]. He has the standard design of a Yoshi with the shoes, but also has unique elements, having a random hairstyle on his head. and wears a polka-dotted diaper/pants of varying colors, depending on the color he hatches with.

==Biography==
{{Spoiler}}
Mario found the young Yoshi's egg and co in [[Glitzville]] where it was going to be cooked by [[Mr. Hoggle]] to make his "[[Hot Dog|Southern Fried Egg Dog of Tastiness]]". The egg appeared to be alive and did not stay still. After Mario caught it, Mr. Hoggle allowed him to keep the egg. After Mario battled the [[Iron Cleft]]s, the egg hatched into a Yoshi Kid where it was then named by Mario. The little guy would prove to be a loyal ally to Mario who he would continue to refer to as "The Great Gonzales" and would eventually help him become the champ of the Glitz Pit and defeat the [[Shadow Queen]].

After Mario and his party defeated the Shadow Queen. the Yoshi became a regular fighter in the [[Glitz Pit]] under alias of "The Great Gonzales Jr." in honor of Mario and hopes to defeat him in the ring some day.
{{endspoiler}}

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
Yoshi Kid, a newborn Yoshi, appears as a partner for Mario. Mario can recruit Yoshi Kid to his party after he defeated Iron Clefts and the egg hatches. Like all Yoshis, this baby Yoshi can lay and throw eggs at enemies, can carry Mario around (even though Mario is too big for him) to help him move faster, flutter jump, ground pound, and eat enemies with his tongue. All of his attacks only deal 1 point of damage, but they are also multi-hit attacks, making them pretty damaging.

== In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Although Yoshi Kid does not physically appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', he does make a cameo in the form of a [[Catch Card]]. He can also be seen in one of the posters in [[Fort Francis]].

==Colors==
[[File:Yoshi Kids.png|thumb|left|300px]]

Yoshi will come in one of seven colors determined by the amount of time that passes between the point where its egg joins Mario's party atop the Hot Dog Stand. His color palettes, hairstyles, and diaper color are:


* Green, with red spiky hair, an orange diaper, and red shoes.
* Red, with green spiky hair, a black diaper, and green shoes.
* Blue, with pink spiky hair, a turquoise diaper, and pink shoes.
* Orange, with light-blue spiky hair, a green diaper, and light-blue shoes.
* Pink, with a spike of green hair, a red diaper, and pink shoes.
* Black, with red spiky hair, a gray diaper, and yellow shoes.
* White-Blue, with a curl of yellow hair, a pure white diaper, and pink shoes.

==Trivia==
*Yoshi Kid's Glitz Pit origins resulted in him calling Mario by the name "Gonzales" throughout the whole course of Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door, except for six times. Although the use of "Mario" in the ending was deliberate and even played somewhat of a role in Yoshi Kid's character development, the other listings were likely a mistake on the developers' part. These are the following times Yoshi Kid calls Mario by his actual name:
**At the ending of the game
**In [[Grubba|Grubba's]] office when reading his secret paper about the machine under the ring
**At [[Twilight Town]] when the steeple bell rings, changing Mayor Dour into a [[pig]]
**At [[Keelhaul Key]] after [[Frankie]] says "I love you" 100 times
**At the very end of Chapter 5 when the [[Pianta|Piantas]] say they're staying on Keelhaul Key
**If Mario brings him along to the top of [[Riddle Tower]] for the first time.
*Yoshi is one of two Yoshis that can be seen outside battle in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''; the other one is seen on the Excess Express, as an adult.
*During the 2nd fight with [[Doopliss]], after defeating [[Goombella]], [[Koops]], and [[Madame Flurrie|Flurrie]], Yoshi Kid will appear, but, no matter what you named him, his enemy name is always "Yoshi".
*The Italian version of Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door refers to him specifically as a '''Miniyoshi''', further explaining that Miniyoshis are Yoshis just hatched from the eggs.
*Yoshi Kid also appears as a sticker in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and as a trophy in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''. 
**In the ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'' trophy, he is referred to as '''Mini-Yoshi'''. This name is non-canon because in ''The Thousand-Year Door'', the naming screen didn't have enough spaces for the Yoshi to be called that.
**Yoshi Kid and [[Goombella]] are the only ''Paper Mario'' partners to appear in the ''Smash Bros.'' series as trophies. [[Vivian]], despite not having a trophy, appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a Spirit.
*Yoshi Kid is the only character in the entire ''Paper Mario'' series that can be named.
