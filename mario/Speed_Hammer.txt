{{Infobox_Enemy|image = Speed Hammer.png|games = [[Luigi's Mansion 3]]|located = [[ScareScraper]]|species = ghost|leader = [[Hellen Gravely]]|attacks = punching}}Speed Hammer is a kind of rare ghost in [[Luigi's Mansion 3]], it is a form of [[Hammer (enemy)]]. Speed Hammers only appear in the [[ScareScraper]]'s ten floor mod, Speed Hammers have 350HP.

==Appearance==

Speed Hammer is a form of a Hammer. Although it is also red, it's glowing red. It has white eyes and mouth, same as regular Hammers. Speed Hammers can move faster than the regular Hammers.

==Battle==

When [[Luigi]] reaches the fifth floor of the ScareScraper in ten floor mod, Speed Hammer is possible to appear based on the game chooses a kind of rare ghost randomly. Luigi and the other players have to flash the Speed Hammers as fast as they can because Speed Hammers move faster than regular Hammers. Speed Hammers have to be defeated in the same way: flashed and be sucked from their back.
