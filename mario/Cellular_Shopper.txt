The '''Cellular Shopper '''is an item found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. The item can be purchased at [[Toad (character)|Toad's]] shop for 5 coins, won in an Item Mini-Game, received for answering Toad's question or via an [[Item Bag]].

==Usage==
This item allows the player to call either Toad or [[Koopa Kid|Koopa Kid's]] shop during a turn and lets them purchase an item if they wish. The cellular Shopper is beneficial if a player is nowhere near an Item Shop on the board. The Cellular Shopper is [[Princess Daisy]]'s favorite item.
