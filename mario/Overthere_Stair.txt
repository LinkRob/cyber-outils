The {{Nihongo|'''Overthere Stair'''|スカイロード|Sukai Rōdo|Sky Road}}<ref>{{cite video game |title=[[Super Paper Mario]] |developer=[[Intelligent Systems]] |publisher=[[Nintendo]] |date=2007-04-19 |platform=[[Wii]] |level=Overthere Stair |language=Japanese}}</ref> is a long path that leads from the [[Underwhere]] up to the [[Overthere]]. It is found in chapter seven of the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
The '''Overthere Stair''' takes place in the sky. It is a fluffy road<ref name="spm">{{cite video game |title=[[Super Paper Mario]] |developer=[[Intelligent Systems]] |publisher=[[Nintendo]] |date=2007-04-19 |platform=[[Wii]] |level=Overthere Stair |quote=This fluffy road leads to the Overthere Stair. You met up with Peach again here among the clouds. Be careful not to fall between the clouds...}}</ref> made of clouds. If the person is not careful, they can fall between the clouds.<ref name="spm"/> The Overthere Stair links from [[The Underwhere]] and [[The Overthere]].

There are many enemies there, and lots of clouds that can bounce Mario really high up. There are also many doors that lead to [[Apple]]s such as the [[Red Apple]], [[Blue Apple]], and [[Black Apple]], all of which heal [[Mario]] and [[Four Heroes of Light|co.]]. The apples have strange effects on users.

== History ==
Mario, [[Luigi]], [[Bowser]], [[Tippi]] and [[Luvbi]] travelled out the Underwhere to the Overthere. They find [[Princess Peach]]<ref name="spm"/> sleeping beside the tree and near the forbidden [[Golden Apple]]. Mario and party searched for the correct apple that can wake her from her deep sleep. Finding the [[Black Apple|correct apple]], Peach woke up and rejoined the party. Mario's party climbed up the Overthere Stair; [[Cyrrus]] helped Mario and his party to reach extreme heights. Mario and his party entered the Overthere.

== Trivia ==
* The Overthere Stair is the longest level in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
