'''Sumo Bro's Spinning Tower''' (also referred as Rock-Candy Mines-Tower) is the tower level of [[Rock-Candy Mines]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Layout==
This tower begins near a platform with a [[Sumo Bro]] on it. A [[? Block]] is found above the Sumo Bro. A [[Fire Bar]] is found, along with a [[Screwtop Lift]] that the player must use to ascend. A ? Block is found, along with another moving platform with a Fire Bar at either end of the track. On the left wall is a [[Hidden Block]] that contains a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. Another ? Block is found, along with a Screwtop Lift with a [[Dry Bones]] on it. Two Fire Bars are encountered as the Screwtop Lift moves up, where another platform with a Dry Bones is found. A Sumo Bro is found above the Screwtop Lift as it rises. A Screwtop Lift with three Sumo Bros. above it must then by used. This leads to the boss door, with a Sumo Bro found above. After entering the boss door, [[Boss Sumo Bro]] must be fought.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In about midway, the player must jump when they are at the middle of the track holding the Screwtop Lift. They must then jump, and wall-jump to get the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': In the place where three Sumo Bros try and electrocute the player, the player must eliminate the last Sumo Bro, and jump to the platform to get the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is located at the very top of the tower. The player must reveal the three hidden blocks right near the boss door, one containing a power-up; then jump up the blocks, and jump again to reveal another set of blocks. They then must eliminate the Sumo Bro on top, then jump to get the final Star Coin.
==Hidden Luigi image==
An 8-bit Luigi is seen right on top of the tower, when the player grabs the third Star Coin.
