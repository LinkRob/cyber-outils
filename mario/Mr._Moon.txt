'''Mr. Moon''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mr. Moon has the appearance of a yellow crescent moon with a pair of eyes, a pointed nose, and a mouth.

===Behavior===
Mr. Moon appears at nighttime in certain areas. It attacks by flying around at [[Wario]] and spitting shooting stars at him.

== History ==

=== ''Wario Land 3'' ===
Mr. Moon only appears in two [[level]]s, [[The Vast Plain]] and [[The Colossal Hole]], exclusively at nighttime, with [[Sun (enemy)|sun]] taking its role at daytime instead. If Wario touches one of Mr. Moon's shooting stars, he briefly transforms into [[Electric Wario]]. In The Colossal Hole, if Wario is below the sky, Mr. Moon does not pursue him. Mr. Moon is invincible, so it cannot be defeated.
