'''Fetch Quest''' is a [[Co-op Minigame|Co-op]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
In a cave, the players look around and were shocked when the diamonds fell to the ground.

== Gameplay ==
Players have sixty seconds pick up the diamonds and place them on the center. Players can also pass diamonds to each other. There are [[! Switch]]es that must be stood for other players to grab the diamonds.

== Ending ==
*Players does their victory animation while the door is open.
*Players does their losing animation while the door is closed.
