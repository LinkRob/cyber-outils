The '''''Mario Party 3 Original Soundtrack''''' is a two-disc set containing every piece of music and sound byte in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. The soundtrack was released on March 31, 2001, and contains all of the same music as [[Toad's Music Box]] in [[Peach's Castle]]'s Sound Room in the game, as well as a few extra tracks.
