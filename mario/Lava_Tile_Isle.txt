'''Lava Tile Isle''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame involves players trying to knock each other off moving [[Grindel]]s into [[lava]]. Characters can [[jump]] on, [[punch]], and [[Ground Pound|hip-drop]] each other. Grindels that wobble are about to move, meaning players should move to another area to stay in the minigame. The last player standing wins. However, if two or more players remain after time runs out or if all the players fall into the lava, the minigame ends in a draw.

The minigame can be purchased from [[Woody]] for 150 [[Coin]]s.
