'''Log Chop''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', under [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s [[Sports]] folder.

==Layout==
A martial artist is standing behind a log laying atop two cinder blocks. On the right side is a power meter, with twelve colored blocks with a repeated pattern of filling in from bottom to top then disappearing from top to bottom.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to have the martial artist chop the log in half, which is accomplished by pressing {{button|gba|A}} when the meter is in the red. In doing so, the player wins at the microgame. If they press the button while the meter is in either the light green or yellow zone, the man's karate chop is not strong enough to cut the log in half, causing the player to lose at the microgame. Another way of losing is if the player does not press {{button|gba|A}} before the timer runs out.

''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' has a few other microgames that make use of the same power meter, including [[Bam-Fu]], [[Zero to Hero]], and [[Shingle Smasher]].

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1''': Three segments at the top of the meter are red.
*'''Level 2''': Two segments at the top of the meter are red.
*'''Level 3''': Only the uppermost segment of the meter is red.
