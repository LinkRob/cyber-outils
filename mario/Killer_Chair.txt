A '''Killer Chair''' (also called a <b>Flying Chair</b> or simply a <b>Chair</b>) is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its remake ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Chairs have the appearance of regular wooden chairs.

===Behavior===
When [[Mario]] or another character approaches a Killer Chair, it rises into the air, rapidly rotates, stops moving for a few seconds, then hurls itself to where the character is The Killer Chair continues to move in its projected direction until hitting either the floor or a wall, at which point it disappears. Overall, the Killer Chair's movements are comparable to that of a [[Bookend]]'s. It is neither shown nor explained how Chairs manage to move.

==Super Mario 64 / Super Mario 64 DS==
[[File:Chair 64.jpg|thumb|left|A Chair in ''Super Mario 64'', about to hurl itself in Mario's direction]]
There are only two Killer Chairs in total, and both of them appear in [[Big Boo's Haunt]]. Both Killer Chairs only appear inside the mansion, in the second room entered to the left. If a Killer Chair hits Mario (or [[Luigi]] or [[Wario]] in ''Super Mario 64 DS''), he loses two units from his [[Power Meter]].
