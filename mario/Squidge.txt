The {{Nihongo|'''Squidge'''<ref name="dkcmanual">{{cite manual |game=Donkey Kong Country |page=30}}</ref>|スクイッジ|Sukuijji|Squidge}} is a squid-like enemy found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''. 

== Description ==
A '''Squidge''' is a light blue squid with eyes on top of its head. They are underwater enemies<ref name="dkcmanual"/> that appear in many underwater levels. They only wave from side to side in the water upside-down or right-side up. Squidges can sting Kongs. [[Enguarde the Swordfish|Enguarde]] can defeat Squidges by stabbing them.
