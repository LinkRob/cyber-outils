The '''Starshroom''' is a mushroom-shaped starship named by the [[Luma]]s and [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who built it. They found it in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They appear once [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] rescue the [[Toad Brigade]] in [[Honeyhive Galaxy]]. Starshrooms take their designs from a standard mushroom. They appear in red, green, turquoise, purple, dark blue, cyan, and yellow.

== In {{series|Super Mario}} ==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Named by Lumas and Toads who built the Starshroom, the Starshrooms appeared in different galaxies and the Comet Observatory.
==== Locations ====
*[[Comet Observatory]]: After Mario completes the [[Space Junk Galaxy]], he can see the red Starshroom parked in the garage.
*[[Sweet Sweet Galaxy]]: Around the galaxy.
*[[Space Junk Galaxy]]: On top of the tower planet. There is also a yellow one that spins near the starting planet (which is mostly destroyed save for the core, which is shaped into another starshroom.
*[[Bubble Breeze Galaxy]]: The Toad Brigade crashes the red starshroom onto the Starting Planet.
*[[Ghostly Galaxy]]: This serves as the Starting Planet's "Landing Platform".
*[[Drip Drop Galaxy]]: A sunken starshroom, previously from a previous brigade, can be found in the planet's core.
*[[Sea Slide Galaxy]]: Mario can walk on them in his search for the five [[Silver Star]]s/100 [[Purple Coin]]s that are scattered around the Sea Slide.
*[[Bonefin Galaxy]]: Mario lands on a derelict green Starshroom on his way into the galaxy.
*[[Deep Dark Galaxy]]: Parked in the starting area, next to the [[Cannon (Super Mario Galaxy)|Cannon]].

===''Super Mario Odyssey''===
Starshrooms made two appearances in ''Odyssey''.
==== Locations ====
*[[Honeylune Ridge]]: In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', there is a Starshroom on the moon, it is a [[Crazy Cap]] store.
*[[Mushroom Kingdom]]: In Super Mario Odyssey, there is a Starshroom in [[Toad Town]], it is also a Crazy Cap store.

==Trivia==
*The Starshroom in the [[Bonefin Galaxy]] seems to have been abandoned and gone derelict, as the player cannot find any [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s anywhere in the galaxy.
