'''Bobolink''' is a character who is mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Bobolink is a female [[Bob-omb]] that was mentioned by [[Swob]] as he expressed his love for her. Swob even wrote a special message to Bobolink on the 50th floor of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] asking to her to marry him. Swob did this because he heard that any wish will come true by writing a message on the 50th floor of the Pit of 100 Trials. However, this turns out to be a lie and Swob posts a trouble at the [[Trouble Center]] wanting someone to remove the graffiti. [[Mario]] can accept this request and use [[Admiral Bobbery]] to remove the graffiti off of the wall.
