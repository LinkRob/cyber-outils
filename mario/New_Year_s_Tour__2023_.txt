'''New Year's Tour''' was the eighty-sixth tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] that began on December 27, 2022 and ended on January 10, 2023. This tour is also a rerun that started on December 26, 2023 to January 9, 2024 and on December 24, 2024 to January 7, 2025.

This tour mainly features R/T variants on [[Classic course|Classic Courses]], from [[Sky Garden|GBA Sky Garden]], [[Cheep-Cheep Island|3DS Cheep-Cheep Island]], [[Bowser Castle 3 (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|GBA Bowser Castle]], [[Baby Park|GCN Baby Park]],[[Koopa Cape|Wii Koopa Cape]] and [[Piranha Plant Slide|3DS Piranha Plant Slide]]. [[Kadomatsu]] makes a return from the previous New Year's Tours theme since [[New Year's Tour (2020)|2020]] starting from 1 January, 2023.

The first half of the tour, players can recieve a [[Black Mii Suit]] and the [[Purple Dozer]] in the highest placement of Ranked Cup. The second half of the tour features [[Green Toad (Pit Crew)]] and the [[? Block Mii Suit]] in high ranked Cup placement rewards.
