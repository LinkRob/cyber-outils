A '''1-Vs-3 Space''' is a type of [[space]] in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and return in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', having a slightly different role between the games.

==Description==
1-Vs-3 Spaces are found in the [[Solo Mode]] boards in ''Mario Party 6''. Landing on these makes the player play a 1-vs-3 [[minigame]] with the [[Koopa Kid]]s.

In ''Mario Party 9'', 1-Vs-3 Spaces appear on every [[board]] instead of certain solo boards, and they have been renamed to '''1-vs-Rivals Spaces'''. When playing with less then four players, they are 1-vs-2 Spaces, but still serve the same effect. If only two players are playing, no 1-vs-Rivals Spaces appear.
