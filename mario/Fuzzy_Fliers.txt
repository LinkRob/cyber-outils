'''Fuzzy Fliers''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] appearing in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to ride their [[Lakitu's Cloud|cloud]] to the top of a tower-like structure. They must prevent their cloud from evaporating by collecting clouds along the way, with small clouds adding four seconds to a character's cloud, while big clouds add seven seconds. [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] are the main obstacle, and they can be found riding on [[track]]s. If a character touches a Fuzzy, they lose three seconds from their timer. If a cloud runs out of time, it evaporates, eliminating whoever is in it. Whoever either reaches the end first or is the last character remaining is the winner.

Fuzzy Fliers has an associated challenge named "Flying High," in which the for characters must reach the goal without losing their clouds.

Fuzzy Fliers has a [[Reverse Minigame]] variation where whoever is eliminated first is the winner, who is also rewarded 20 [[Mini Star]]s. The minigame does not immediately end, even after the first character is eliminated.
