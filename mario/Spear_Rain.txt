|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Spear Rain''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is only used by [[Smithy]] with his Magic Head.

==Effects==
When Spear Rain is used, a bunch of spears rain down on [[Mario]]'s party damaging them all for high damage. Despite being called "Spear Rain," the objects that rain down resemble more of tridents.

==Trivia==
*[[Sword Rain]] is similar in appearance to Spear Rain.
