The '''Fractones''' are a race of gemstone creatures that appear in [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']].

== Description ==
Fractones come in various shapes and colours, though they tend to be blue, and have expressive eyes. They can change their shape and form; however, some of them maintain one form which matches their occupation or imitates a local lifeform. A destroyed Fractone can repair itself, though segments that get too far away may leave the main body with gaps until they return.

Fractones tend to speak in the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nosism first person plural], and tend to refer to each other using they/them pronouns. Fractones tend to be friendly, though the ones in the [[Forbidden Layer]] are more hostile. In the Planet Core, the Fractones have difficulty speaking, stating that they had not used speech in a long time.

History

A Fractone named [[Exploritone]] set out to find the origin of his race, eventually discovering that they originate in the [[Planet Core]]. [[Exploritone#Saga of the Exploritone|Logs of his journey]] can be found throughout the game.

== Notable Members ==

* '''[[Exploritone]]''': An adventurer who set off to the planet core to discover where the Fractone race comes from.
* '''[[Architectone]]''': A house-shaped Fractone who creates living spaces called Getaways that have beds that [[Donkey Kong (character)|DK]] and [[Pauline]] can rest in to replenish their health.
* '''[[Styletone]]''', '''[[Stufftone]]''', and '''[[Chiptone]]''': The three shopkeepers found throughout the underground world. They each except a different form of payment ([[Fossil|Fossils]], [[Banandium Gold]], or [[Banandium Chips]], respectively) and sell different things. 
* '''[[Constructone|Constructones]]''': Fractones that can clear bedrock, build barrel cannons, or do other construction-related actions for a fee of [[Banandium Gold|gold]]. 

== Behind the Scenes ==
The name "Fractone" comes from "fractal" (a geometric shape with detailed structures at small scales) and "tone".

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Fractones are called ''Wareruya no tamitachi'' ("the breakable persons," written ワレルヤの民), with ''Wareruya'' coming from ''wareru'' ("to break"; written 割れる) and -''ya'' (a suffix that denotes a personality trait; written 屋) and therefore roughly meaning "breakable." Their Spanish name, ''Fractono'', is a calque of the English name (''fractal'' + potentially ''trono''), and in Italian, they are called "''Frattale''" (from ''frattale'' ("fractal")),
