'''Classic Clash I''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', being a part of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[Sci-Fi]] folder.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place in a field, where on the left side is a [[Mario]]-like hero (later named "Nintenman" in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''{{'}}s [[Classic Clash]]), while on the right side is a [[Bowser]]-like enemy.

==Gameplay==
The Mario-like hero has to dodge the Bowser-like enemy's attacks, which is either kicking rocks or spitting flames. If done successfully, the player wins, but if the Mario-like hero is hit by either attack, the player loses.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1''': The hero has to jump over a rock. 
*'''Level 2''': The hero has to either duck under the enemy's flames or jump over two rocks. 
*'''Level 3''': The hero has to either duck under the enemy's flames or jump over three rocks.
