'''Rome Avanti 3''' is a race track from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is one of the three routes of [[Rome Avanti]], and was released in the 2023 Anniversary Tour.

== Layout ==
Rome Avanti 3 starts in the same direction as Rome Avanti 1, facing south according to the ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' map. Not long after the racers start, they will be forced to turn left, taking a path going southeast. The path curves to the right, going southwest through the colosseo via a bridge. After the racers get over the bridge, they turn right. The road curves slightly to the left, before the racers take a sudden sharp left with the road curving right, then take another left. After the racers get through this section, they turn right before coming across a three-way intersection, turning left.  They then take a right turn to drive northeast at the Piazza Navona. Eventually, they will reach the Porta del Pópolo's roundabout, turning right and taking through south exit to get back onto the Via del Corso, crossing the finish line soon after.

Both halves of la Piazza del Campidóglio can be traversed in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.
