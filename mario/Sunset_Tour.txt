The Sunset Tour was the thirtieth tour of ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', which began on November 4, 2020, and ended on November 17, 2020. The tour introduced [[Sunset Wilds]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' (making this the first GBA course and the first course not from ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' to make its classic course debut in ''Mario Kart Tour''), which acted as the signature course of the tour.
