'''Daisuke Ito''' is a character designer at [[Nintendo EPD]]. He is also the current voice actor for [[Draggadon]].

* ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' (2013) - Staff
* ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' (2014) - Character Motion Design
* ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker (Wii U)]]'' (2014) - Staff, [[Draggadon]] (uncredited), [[Gold Draggadon]] (uncredited)
* ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' (2014) - Character Motion Design

* ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]'' (2017) - Character Animation
* ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' (2017) - Character Art, Modeling Suppport
* ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker (Switch)]]'' (2018) - Staff, [[Draggadon]] (uncredited)
* ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker (3DS)]]'' (2018) - Staff, [[Draggadon]] (uncredited)
