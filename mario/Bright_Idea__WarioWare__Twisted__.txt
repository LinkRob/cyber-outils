'''Bright Idea''' is one of [[Jimmy T]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player has to tilt the system to screw in a light bulb so that it turns on. If done correctly, the player wins, and a random scene is also shown, which varies depending on the difficulty level. If the time is up and the light bulb is not on, they lose.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The light bulb has a small head. Turning it on shows a cat about to eat the last fish.
*'''Level 2:''' The light bulb has a slightly bigger head. Turning it on shows a man sweating in a costume.
*'''Level 3:''' The light bulb has an even bigger head. Turning it on shows a woman who [[Breaking the fourth wall|kisses the screen]].
