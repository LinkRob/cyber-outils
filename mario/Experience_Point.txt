An '''Experience Point''' is a type of statistic as the fundamental of the ''Mario'' role-playing games. Experience points can be earned by defeating enemies or consuming items.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' ===
'''Experience Points''' debuted in ''[[Super Mario RPG: The Legend of Seven Stars]]''. The player is rewarded experience points after defeating all enemies in the battle.

=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Mario can earn experience points by defeating enemies.

=== ''Mario & Luigi'' series ===
==== ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ====
Mario and Luigi can earn experience points in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They can claim it by defeating enemies in battle.
