The '''Washroom''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Washroom resembles the one on the [[Washroom (1F)|first floor]], except a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] does not appear in it, and there is a "Monsters" poster instead of a medicine cabinet. In the Nintendo 3DS version, the poster depicts a field instead.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Washroom is a room part of [[Area Three]]. Whenever [[Luigi]] approaches the toilet tank, in it, a [[Water Elemental Ghost]] emerges from it. A [[Heart]] also usually appears from the sink upon examining it. The Water Elemental Ghost is necessary for Luigi to release [[water]] onto the [[Temper Terror]] to douse the fire protecting its [[Ghost's heart|heart]] and therefore defeat it. When Luigi defeats the Temper Terror, the lights turn on, and a [[Treasure Chest|treasure chest]] containing some money appears.

During the [[blackout]], if Luigi visits the Washroom, he can encounter two Temper Terrors.

==Trivia==
*If Luigi pulls the "Monsters" poster for too long, it snaps back and changes into a [[Boo]] poster that says "Get Out of Here!", making Luigi fall on his back and lose five [[Heart Point|HP]]. The "Monsters" poster is also found in The Twins' Room, and the Boo poster appears when the projection screen in the [[Projection Room]] is vacuumed; discovering both gives the player an achievement in the remake.
