'''Ultra Cheese''' is an item that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. They replace [[Ultrasyrup Jar]]s from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

==Usage==
Ultra Cheeses are used to restore 60 BP for [[Bowser]]. The Bros. can't use cheese.
