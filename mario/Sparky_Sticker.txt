The '''Sparky Sticker''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==History==
Sparky Stickers are type a sticker depicting an [[Amp]]. They are roughly the size of a [[space]]. A Sparky Sticker can be bought at an [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shop]] for 15 [[coin]]s. A Sparky Sticker is used by placing the sticker on the space that the user is currently on. If an opponent passes by the space with a Sparky Sticker, they get shocked and lose ten coins. The Sparky Sticker also forcefully turns opponents under the effects of a [[Mini Mushroom]] or [[Mega Mushroom]] back to normal size. 

The placement method for the Sparky Sticker would later be used in the following three ''Mario Party'' games (''[[Mario Party 5]]'', ''[[Mario Party 6]]'', and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''), where characters can throw/place [[orb]]s on spaces and characters that touch (and/or pass through in ''Mario Party 6'' and ''Mario Party 7''{{'}}s case) will feel the effects of that orb. This has a similar function to placing red orb on a [[Blue Space]] or [[Red Space]] and making it into a roadblock. However, this is the only item that can be placed on the space the player is standing on, as opposed to Blue Spaces or Red Spaces ahead of the player.

The Sparky Sticker is [[Waluigi]]'s favorite item.
