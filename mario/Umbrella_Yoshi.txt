The '''Umbrella Yoshi''' is one of the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] transformations found only in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. It only appears in two levels, [[Bounceabout Woods]] and [[Snag-Stitch Mountain]].

==Controls==
The player must use the A button to open and close the umbrella to dodge enemies and collect beads under a time limit. When they reach the end, they move onto the next part of the level.
