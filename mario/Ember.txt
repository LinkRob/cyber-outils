'''Embers''' are enemies in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and return in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They are blue flame-like ghosts that are a variant of [[Lava Bubble (Paper Mario)|Lava Bubble]]s, which they look almost identical to, given they are a small flame with demonic eyes. Embers are often seen resting in dark areas and tend to attack any intruders that come by. Being composed of fire, Embers are naturally immune to jumps however they are unable to withstand most other attacks especially if these attacks are ice-based. Embers behave similarly to Lava Bubbles however they also possess another ability which involves duplicating itself.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
=== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ===
[[Image:Ember Old.jpg|thumb|left|An Ember from ''Paper Mario''.]]
Embers are basically blue [[Lava Bubble (Paper Mario)|Lava Bubble]]s, given that they have better stats and a different look. They are found in only one place in the game, but there are three repeatable fights along the way. On the pathway up into [[Star Haven]], [[Star Road]], Mario finds these powerful fire beasts. If [[Mario]] attacks one with [[Kooper]] or his [[Hammer]], then they will split into two Embers so be warned. Due to their fiery nature, Embers are obviously immune to jumps so it is advised to use other types of attacks against them instead.

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
[[Image:Ember New.jpg|thumb|An Ember]]
Embers return in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', in this game, they (along with the [[Lava Bubble (Paper Mario)|Lava Bubble]]s) are ghosts of lost sailors from the dreaded [[Pirate's Grotto]]. They are also servants of [[Cortez]]. When [[Flavio]] and his crew are nearing [[Keelhaul Key]], Embers emerge from the darkness of the seas and sink the Ship. In battle, Embers attack by blowing fireballs at [[Mario]] and his allies in an attempt to [[Burn]] them. When using Cortez's ship, the [[Black Skull]], to escape, these fiery fiends attack the [[X-Naut]] battle ship. Since they appear much earlier in the game than their ''Paper Mario'' counterparts, they have weaker stats as well.
