A '''Dusty Hammer''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. They can only be found and bought in an [[Little Mouser's Shop|Item Shop]] in [[Dry Dry Outpost]]. Given they only deal 1 point of damages to enemies, they are pretty much useless items to use in battle. They can be thrown to damage flying and ceiling enemies acting much like the [[Hammer Throw]] badge. A Dusty Hammer is also one of the items that needs to be purchased in order for [[Mario]] to speak with [[Moustafa]].

==Description==
Dusty Hammers look like regular Hammers except they have a white patch on it. They also greatly resemble [[Worn-Out Hammer]]s seen in a later ''Paper Mario'' installment, ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
