'''World 3-4''' is the fourth [[level]] of [[World 3 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 3]] and the fourteenth overall level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
The level begins with [[Mario]] crossing a tightrope along some towering snow-capped platforms. After passing a giant spiked ball, Mario finds a [[Warp Box]] that leads him further into the level. Along this tight rope after the Warp Box is a music box that can lead Mario into [[Coin Heaven]]. Passing a tight rope nearby leads to the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]] of the level. Several spiked balls wait for Mario on the final leap of the level as past the giant balls, is the [[Flagpole]].

==Star Medals==
*'''[[Star Medal]] 1''': Near the beginning of the level is a tall tree. Climbing the tightropes, Mario can find the first [[Star Medal]] around the top of the tree.
*'''Star Medal 2''': At the end of the level's Coin Heaven, accessed by jumping off of a [[Rainbow Note Block]].
*'''Star Medal 3''': In the middle of the level is a moving [[Question Block]]. Mario should take a ride and not hit the block. This leads to a teleportation box and the final Star Medal.
