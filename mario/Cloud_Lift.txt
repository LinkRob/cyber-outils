'''Cloud Lifts''' are a type of [[Semisolid Platform]] mainly featured in the 2D {{series|Super Mario}} installments. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
Ever since their first appearance, Cloud Lifts have been used by [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and other playable characters as platforms for moving across certain sky areas. Cloud Lifts generally move in a fixed position, either horizontally or vertically.
