'''Mystic Eggs''' are items which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Using a Mystic Egg restores Mario's [[Heart Point|HP]] by 5, just like the [[Egg]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]''. The Mystic Egg is used in several [[recipe]]s as the Egg was as well as ones newly introduced in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''.

==History==
Mystic Eggs are found in the [[The Great Tree|Great Boggly Tree]], but they cannot be obtained until a little later in the game. [[Mario]] can find [[Petuni]], a female [[Puni]], at the start of the tree, after Chapter 2 is complete and ask her if she needs to do anything. Petuni will give Mario a very small quiz called "Stump Petuni." Upon solving it, Mario receives a Mystic Egg.
