'''Mother Brain''' is the main antagonist of the ''Metroid'' franchise, having first appeared in the game of the [[w:c:metroid:Metroid (game)|same name]]. Mother Brain has made cameos and a few mentions in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media, in which she is first mentioned in the ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]'' comic "[[Duh Stoopid Bomb!]]," whereas Mother Brain's first physical appearance in a ''Mario''-related game is ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''==
Mother Brain is briefly mentioned by [[Samus Aran]] in the guest room of the [[Princess Peach's Castle|Mushroom Castle]], where Samus says she is resting up before battling Mother Brain. 

==In ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''==
[[File:Metroid WWIMM screenshot.png|thumb|left|Samus facing Mother Brain in the Metroid microgame in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'']]
Mother Brain is featured within the [[microgame]] [[Metroid (WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!)|Metroid]], which is a recreation of the final boss fight in the original ''Metroid''. As such, Mother Brain's sprite is reused from the first ''Metroid''. To win the microgame, Samus must blast Mother Brain repeatedly with missiles while dodging Mother Brain's attacks to defeat her.

== In the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]]==
[[File:Mother Brain SSBU screenshot.jpg|thumb|A screenshot of Mother Brain in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'']]
As of ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', Mother Brain is featured as an [[Assist Trophy]]. Her first ''Super Smash Bros.'' appearance is in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', in which Mother Brain makes a cameo appearance as a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]].
