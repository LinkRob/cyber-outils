'''Guitar Gazump''' is [[Diddy Kong]]'s special move from the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. It is Diddy's musical instrument attack that can be bought from [[Candy's Music Shop]] with his guitar.

==Usage==
When Guitar Gazump is used, Diddy plays a rock-based song on his guitar and can be used on his music pad or to defeat all enemies in the area. Guitar Gazump has numerous of different uses compared to most musical instruments in the game such as being needed to unlock the tunnel in [[Angry Aztec]] gaining access to the second half of the level.
