'''Fluff-Puff Peaks Palace''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level features many [[Bone Piranha Plant]]s and sinking [[pipe]]s. All enemies are spawned by smoke clouds. The level features puzzles using sinking pipes.

Eventually the player will reach the [[Wonder Flower]], but it is unreachable. The player must stand on a sinking pipe at the right time to bring the Wonder Flower above the [[semisolid]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will make the player stretchy and all objects a silhouette. The [[Wonder Seed]] is between Bone Piranha Plants shortly before the boss.

Finally, is [[Bowser Jr.]]. The battle takes place on five connected platforms that individually move up and down. After one hit, he will transition into [[Wonder Bowser Jr.]] In this state, he will transform the platforms into ice and honey.
==[[10-Flower Coin]] locations==
#A 10-flower coin is underneath a semisolid with [[Hammer Bro]]s. on top of the semisolid.
#A 10-flower coin is underneath a semisolid and is seemingly unreachable. To reach it, the player must stand on the blocking pipe to the right to sink the pipe and reach the 10-flower coin.
#Shortly after the previous 10-flower coin, is another underneath a bridge. To reach the coin, the player must crouch and walk though a crack. What makes this hard to notice is that the player will likely encounter this during the Wonder Effect, and the player will not know the blocks are actually in the background.
