'''Flopside Tower''' is a location that appears only in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is a very large tower located in [[Flopside]].

==History==
Flopside Tower can be visited once the player has access to Flopside. Flopside Tower can be visited but not much can be done there until towards the very end of the game. Once all of the [[Pure Heart]]s have been placed away, the final door leading to [[Castle Bleck]] will appear at Flopside Tower. Flopside Tower is naturally the Flopside counterpart of [[Flipside Tower]].
