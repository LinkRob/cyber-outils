The '''Bullet Candy''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
It is a blue [[Candy]] that depicts a [[Bullet Bill]] on the wrapper. If a character uses it, their torso becomes the back part of a rocket, somewhat like a Bullet Bill, and they can hit three [[Dice Block]]s. However, unlike with [[Thrice Candy]], there is no [[coin]] bonus for rolling the same number three times. As the user moves along the board while the Bullet Candy is in effect, they steal one [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] from any opponents they move into.

==In ''Mario Party 8''==
The Bullet Candy is unique to only the board [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]]. And it is worth 15 or 20 coins if bought at a [[Candy Shop]] but can sometimes cost only 10 coins. It can be obtained for free via [[Bowser's Warped Orbit#Shy Guy's Space Buffet|Shy Guy's Space Buffet]].
