==History==
Novbi is a [[Nimbi]] that is in control of the elevator in the main area of [[The Overthere]]. However, during the first visit, Novbi is frozen solid requiring [[Bowser]] to help defrost him. Novbi is grateful for the assistance and allows the heroes to use the elevator whenever they want.
