The '''Royal Dress''' is an armor only usable by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] for 100 [[coin]]s. The Royal Dress increases the Princess's [[Defense]] by 48 and her [[Magic Defense]] by 24.
