The '''Miracle Book''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
It is a 20-page pop-up book. When the Miracle Book is opened, there are images of the playable characters playing various [[minigame|mini-games]]. If the player presses {{button|gcn|A}}, an effect is shown, which varies depending on the page shown.

==In ''Mario Party 6''==
The Miracle Book can be bought at the [[Star Bank]] for ten [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]]. However, the individual pages also have to be bought there, and their price varies from 20 to 40 Stars. Filling the Miracle Book is one of the requirements to completing the game.

Once every page is collected, [[Brighton]] and [[Twila]] sees how much work [[Mario]] and his friends have invested into putting an end to their feud. As a reward, Brighton and Twila reconcile and send the Stars scattering across the skies.
