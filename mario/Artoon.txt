'''Artoon''' was a Japanese game developer who developed both ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', both of which are titles within the ''[[Yoshi (series)|Yoshi]]'' franchise. Since 2010, the company has become defunct and merged into AQ Interactive.
