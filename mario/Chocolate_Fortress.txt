'''Chocolate Fortress''' is a [[level]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', more specifically the [[fortress]] level of [[Chocolate Island]]. [[Dry Bones]], [[Thwimp]]s, and [[Thwomp]]s are encountered, and there are several hazardous [[Spike (Super Mario World)|Spikes]] to avoid along the way. Like other fortress levels, four [[Reznor]]s are the mini-bosses fought at the end.
