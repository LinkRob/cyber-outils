'''Red Paragoombas''' are enemies in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
[[File:Red Paragoomba SPP sprite.png|frame|left]]
They are red variations of normal [[Paragoomba]]s.
{{br|left}}
=== Behavior ===
They have the ability to fly and hop around but lack the ability to drop [[Micro-Goomba]]s on Peach. Any attack can defeat a Red Paragoomba, stomping on a Red Paragoomba will turn it into a [[Red Goomba (enemy)|Red Goomba]].
