The '''Sewer Guru''' is a character who appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Profile==
The Sewer Guru is an old angel who lives in the sewers of [[Diamond City]]. The Sewer Guru is bald, has a thick beard and mustache, a white halo above his head, and he wears a white toga.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
[[File:Sewer Guru screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|The Sewer Guru asks Wario if he dropped the two Game Boy Advance units or the "fancy one."]]
The Sewer Guru appears during [[Wario]]'s story and in the ending for [[Wario-Man]]'s story.

In the intro for Wario's stage, when Wario drops a stolen [[Game Boy Advance]] and Game Boy Advance SP down a manhole by accident, the Sewer Guru emerges and asks Wario if he dropped the two Game Boy Advances, which is in the Sewer Guru's left hand, or a console with two screens and no buttons based on the [[Nintendo DS]], which the Sewer Guru holds in his right hand. Wario hops into the sewer and steals all three from the Sewer Guru.

In the ending of [[Wario-Man]]'s stage, Wario-Man falls into the same manhole that the Sewer Guru emerged from. Wario jumps out of the manhole, back to his normal form, and the Sewer Guru flies out, infuriated at Wario, who fell onto him. In an act of revenge, the Sewer Guru strikes Wario with his wand to [[Flat Wario|flatten]] him, after which the Sewer Guru smiles.

==Trivia==
*The Sewer Guru's Japanese name {{nihongo|かみさま|Kamisama}} translates to "God."
