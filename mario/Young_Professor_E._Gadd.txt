'''Young Professor Elvin Gadd''' is a character who appears in the [[Nintendo DS]] game, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. He is the younger version of [[Professor Elvin Gadd]]. Young E. Gadd is encountered in [[Thwomp Volcano]], just before a massive explosion. Mario, Luigi, Baby Mario, and Baby Luigi all find him in his lab at the bottom of the volcano. The reason he was at Thwomp Volcano was to study the behavior of some [[Thwomp]]s. During the eruption, Young E. Gadd's lab is destroyed by Thwomps that fall through the building, including a massive, flaming one that is about to explode. Seeing [[Baby Mario]] douse the giant, flaming Thwomp, the memories of his present self suddenly start to be rearranged, and he has the idea of building the [[Hydrogush 4000]].

He looks the same as his "future" counterpart, the only difference being his brown-black hair.

==Trivia==
*Of all the characters who have younger selves, Young E. Gadd is the only one that does not interact with his present-day self.
