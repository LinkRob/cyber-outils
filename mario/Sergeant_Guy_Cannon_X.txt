The '''Sergeant Guy Cannon X''' is an upgraded version of the [[Sergeant Guy Cannon]]. It only appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
Sergeant Guy Cannon X is fought in Class 6 of the [[Battle Ring (Mario & Luigi: Dream Team)|Battle Ring]]. It has the same appearance as Sergeant Guy Cannon, with the only difference being that Sergeant Guy Cannon X has increased stats.
