'''Mario Dice Block''' is [[Mario]]'s [[Dice Block]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The Dice Block reappeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a spirit.

== In ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' ==
Mario Dice Block is [[Mario]]'s [[Dice Block]]. If the player has Mario as an ally or Mario as their character, the player can use his Dice Block. The Mario Dice Block is a well-balanced die according to the game. His numbers are 1, 3, 3, 3, 5, 6.

== In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' ==
The Mario Dice Block appears as a spirit in ''Ultimate''. The spirit was added during version 2.0.0. Despite the spirit's name is Dice Block, the game uses the Mario Dice Block's artwork for the spirit. The player can obtain the spirit at the Vault Shop for 500 Gold or win the spirit battle on Spirit Board.
