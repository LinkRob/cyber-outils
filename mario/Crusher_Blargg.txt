'''Crusher Blarggs'''<ref>''Yoshi's Island DS'' Internal Filename: CrusherBlargg</ref> are enemies in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They are much stronger than a standard [[Gargantua Blargg]] and also possess the ability to crush dense rocks. Crusher Blarggs are the largest and strongest species of [[Blargg]]s and hence are completely indestructible, there is no attack that can destroy them.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
Crusher Blarggs appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', in this game, they are incredibly rare enemies with only two appearing in the whole game, a Crusher Blargg behaves almost identically to a [[Gargantua Blargg]], emerging out of [[lava]] to attack but they are not only much taller making a much larger hazard but, true to their name, possess the ability to crush dense rocks, this makes them very dangerous as they can destroy the flooring [[Yoshi]] stands on knocking him into the lava if he isn’t careful. They can also make traversing the terrain impossible if they destroy enough of the walkway, their height also creates a danger as Yoshi must be close to the top to be out of reach, Crusher Blarggs are completely invincible and there is no attack that can destroy one however if hit with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], they can be sent back in the [[lava]] temporarily, doing this will also cause the Crusher Blargg to appear in the [[Island Museum]], Crusher Blarggs appear in the Giant Enemy Exhibit. Crusher Blarggs are only found in two levels of the game, [[Yikes! Boiling Hot!]] and [[Number Ball Special]], both of which are bonus levels.

==Trivia==
*Crusher Blarggs are the only enemy in the game to not appear in any normal levels, being exclusive to Bonus Levels.
*Crusher Blarggs are similar to the [[King Bill]]s from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. Both are the largest variants of their respective species (the [[Blargg]]s for the Crusher Blargg and the [[Bullet Bill]]s for the King Bill) and both also had a variant that is larger than the origin species (the [[Banzai Bill]] and [[Gargantua Blargg]] respectively) but smaller than the Crusher Blargg and King Bill themselves, additionally, both are primarily found in bonus levels.
*Various guides for ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' referred to Crusher Blarggs as '''Gargantua Blarggs'''.<ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' Player's Guide. Page 54. "''Watch out for the Gargantua Blargg that crashes up through the rocks.''"</ref><ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' Player's Guide. Page 82. "''Quickly shoot the Gargantua Blargg with an egg to stun it, then jump up the platforms to reach door i.''"</ref><ref>Black, Fletcher. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 185. "''There is a flower on the post next to the chambers' right wall, but watch out for the Gargantua Blargg that rises out of the lava-filled floor.''"</ref>
