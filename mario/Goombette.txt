'''Goombette''' is an enemy found in the [[Sunken Ship]] with three being found with [[Hidon]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are very similar to [[Micro-Goomba]]s and are stronger than a regular [[Goomba]] despite being a smaller size.
