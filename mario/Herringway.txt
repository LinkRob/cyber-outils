'''Herringway''' is character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Herringway is a dark blue [[Bumpty]] with thick eyebrows.

==Biography==
Herringway is a famous novelist who lives in [[Shiver City]] and is best friends with the [[Mayor Penguin|mayor]]. Herringway's house is the middle one the eastern area of Shiver City. Herringway locks himself away in a secret room so that nobody disturbs him while he is writing.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
Herringway was eventually found by [[Mario]] and his friends, who suspect that Herringway murdered the mayor. Herringway is shocked to hear this and runs to the mayor's house to the scene. The [[Penguin Police Chief]] believes that Herringway murdered the mayor and Herringway protests that he and the mayor were best friends and would never do that. However, Mayor Penguin soon wakes up and it was revealed that he accidently knocked himself out while trying to get a present for Herringway. Herringway gladly takes the present and leaves back for his house.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
Herringway is only mentioned a few times. His novels are said to be widely read. In [[Poshley Heights]], [[Goombella]]'s [[tattle]] reveals that at least three of the penguin residents living there are Herringway's relatives which hints that their relations to Herringway may aided them in living in the richest town.
