'''Circus Kongs''' are a recurring enemy in the {{series|Mario vs. Donkey Kong}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Circus Kongs are [[monkey robot]]s with a near-identical appearance to a [[Mini Donkey Kong]], except Circus Kongs wear a necktie with a "C," given that their first name starts with that letter.

===Behavior===
If any [[Mini]]s come into contact with a Circus Kong, it throws them upward and juggles them back and forth, causing the Minis to remain suspended in the air. In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis'' and ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!]]'', a Circus Kong can only carry up to two Minis at a time, and any additional Minis will be thrown aside and briefly stunned if they touch the Circus Kong. A Circus Kong cannot damage a Mini unless it throws them into a harmful object.

Circus Kongs can stop juggling the Minis if another Mini uses [[Hammer]]s or [[Fire Flower]] against one, causing the Circus Kong to flip over and flail around temporarily and allowing the Minis to move past it. An exclamation mark appears over a stunned Circus Kong shortly before it flips back upright.
