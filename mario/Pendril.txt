'''Pendril''' is an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. Pendrils appear in [[Plack Beach]], where they can only be fought by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

== Physical description ==
They are blue penguins with drills replacing their beaks.

==Battle==
They battle [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] by popping out of the ground before nose-diving into one of them. Occasionally, when they re-surface, a rock becomes stuck on their nose. If either Mario or Luigi attack them with the rock stuck to them, the rock shatters and a [[Bob-omb]] runs at the one who attacked. Eventually the Pendril re-enters the underground without the rock.

== Trivia ==
* Pendril's name is a portmanteau of "penguin" and "drill".
* If the player hacks the game with [[Bowser]] battling one, when it attacks, as the Pendril ends the attack, the game will freeze, forcing the player to reset.
