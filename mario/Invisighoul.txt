'''Invisighouls''' are ghosts which appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', exclusively within the level [[Hector the Reflector's Haunted House]]. They attack by picking up various furnitures and tossing them at [[Yoshi]], the furniture can be eaten and produced into an [[Yoshi Egg|Egg]].

Hence their name, Invisighouls are invisible, they are only seen in the mirror's reflection during the battle with their leader [[Hector the Reflector]]. Eleven Invisighouls appear in the [[Island Museum]], under the Haunted House Exhibit.

==Etymology==
The name Invisighoul is a portmanteau of the words "invisible" and "ghoul."
