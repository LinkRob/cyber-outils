'''Grand Comet Medal''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
A Grand Comet Medal is a Skill item that raises the Skill level of a teammate whose Skill level had reached level 4 using [[Comet Medal]]s, such as both forms of [[Rosalina]] and all variations of [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Blooper]]s. A Grand Comet Medal can be obtained either at random from opening a [[? Block]], winning it from the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] game in a [[Toad House]], or from defeating "Blooper & Cheep Cheeps", [[Cheep Chomp]]s, and "Dry Bones & Cheep Cheep"s with a 10+ Combo attack.
