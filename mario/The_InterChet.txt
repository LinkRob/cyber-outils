'''The InterChet''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The InterChet is a 3-D block that is red and black in color. His "faces" all appear to have mustaches on them.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
The InterChet runs the [[Flipside Arcade]] in [[Flipside]]. He runs both the games and the rewards center for the heroes to play and cash in on prizes. However, there are hints that The InterChet is a con artist as his quote mentions robbing.

==Trivia==
*It is also stated that The InterChet created [[The InterNed]] who was first seen in [[Merlee's Mansion]].
