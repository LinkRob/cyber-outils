The '''Loopdeeswoop Galaxy''' is a galaxy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It is one of the [[Trial Galaxies]]. It is a harder version of the [[Loopdeeloop Galaxy]].

== Missions ==
=== The Galaxy's Greatest Wave ===
[[Mario]] must complete the track in only 3 minutes to get the [[Power Star]] from the [[Penguin Coach]]. If Mario finishes under 1:30 and doesn't talk to the coach, back where the younger [[Penguin]]s where will be a [[Sling Star]] where Mario can go around the galaxy to get a large amount of [[Star Bit]]s.
