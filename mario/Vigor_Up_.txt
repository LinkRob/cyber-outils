'''Vigor Up!''' is a special move from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It doubles the attack points of the enemy used the move.

==Trivia==
* A very small number of enemies can use that move.
* [[Jinx]] is the only enemy that can use both this move and [[Valor Up]].
* The right pronunciation for that word is with the I as it is in the alphabet, with the O said very quietly and without the R.
