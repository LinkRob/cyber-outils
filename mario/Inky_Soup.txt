'''Inky Soup''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Effects==

It is made from [[Inky Sauce]]. A look-alike of the [[Spicy Soup]], the Inky Soup does not look flavorsome. It appears inky, and is served with a wooden bowl and metal spoon. This soup restores fifteen [[Heart Point]]s, twelve more than the ingredient used in the making, and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
