'''World 9-2''' is the second level of [[World 9 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 9]] and the second secret level overall in said game. Like [[World 9-1 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|the previous level]], it uses overworld tiles underwater, which in the original, leads to many graphics appearing with unusual colors, due to many secondary tiles using the same palette as the [[coral]] tiles.

== Layout ==
[[File:SMB TLL World 9-1 Mario swimming.png|thumb|200px|Mario swimming]]
This level takes place entirely underwater. There are many pipes with [[Piranha Plant]]s situated over a large chasm, with [[Lakitu]] traveling along the center of the stage until the player reaches the [[Goal Pole]]. From halfway through the level to the end, there are additional platforms with pipes and [[Coin]]s.
