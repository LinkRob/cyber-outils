'''Boss Basses''' are enemies that appear in the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' series, as enemies that attempt to swallow the player whole. They are first seen in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', in some levels they jump out of the water to eat the player. They are very relentless enemies and aggressively chase [[Mario]] until they've eaten him, luckily, a Boss Bass can easily be defeated with most attacks but a new one will commonly take its place. Boss Basses are related to [[Big Bertha (fish)|Big Bertha]]s which are the female equivalents who protect the [[Baby Cheep]]s underwater while the Boss Bass hunts for it's prey on the water's surface.

==History==
===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:BossBassEatingMario.png|thumb|left|A Boss Bass in ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'']]
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', the Boss Bass quickly swims along the water's surface in [[World 3-3 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|Worlds 3-3]] and [[World 3-8 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|3-8]], trying to leap out and eat [[Mario]] if he gets too close. It defeats Mario instantly in one gulp regardless of his form. In both of the stages it appears in, the water level rises and sinks, making Mario an easy target. Throwing blocks, shells, [[hammer]]s, and [[fireball]]s at the Boss Bass can defeat it, as well as tail attacks and using a [[Super Star|Starman]], but it eventually comes back. However, in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', if the player has turned on [[Orange Switch|the e-Reader card]] that makes enemies hit by [[fireball]]s turn into [[coin]]s, and the player successfully hits the Boss Bass with a fireball and collects the coin, the Boss Bass does not return for the rest of the level.
{{br|left}}

===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:CartoonBigBertha.jpg|thumb|left|160px|A Boss Bass from "[[Never Koop a Koopa]]".]]
Boss Basses make appearances in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', where they usually chase after Mario and his friends. It first appears in "[[Reptiles in the Rose Garden]]" chasing fish.

===''Mario Teaches Typing''===
[[File:Boss Bass MTT sprite.png|frame|right]]
In the second mode of ''[[Mario Teaches Typing]]'', Underwater World, the game repeatedly loops footage of [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] or [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] swimming away from either a Boss Bass or a [[Blooper]]. Their appearance is slightly different: their fins are cyan, they have several sharp teeth rather than a single blunt tooth, and have round pupils with eyebrows instead of squinting pupils.
{{br|left}}

=== ''Mario Kart DS''===
[[Image:Boss Bass DS.png|thumb|A Jumping Boss Bass.]]
A Boss Bass appears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' in the remake of {{classic course|N64|[[Banshee Boardwalk]]}}. It can be seen hopping over the bridge periodically, the Boss Bass's jumps are high rendering it completely harmless. It usually jumps whenever players race by the bridge and is also seen jumping during the course's introduction. It replaces the [[Giant Cheep-Cheep]] from the original course in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

=== ''Yoshi's Island DS'' ===
[[Image:Boss Bass Yoshi.png|thumb|left|A Boss Bass as seen in ''Yoshi's Island DS''.]]
The Boss Bass returns in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', appearing in many levels of [[World 3 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 3]]. The Boss Bass attacks by hopping out of the water and if [[Yoshi]] is below the Boss Bass' mouth during its descent, the Boss Bass will eat [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and the baby killing them instantly. The Boss Bass cannot be destroyed but it can be made to appear in the [[Island Museum]] by tossing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them, Boss Basses are seen in the Tropical Section of the Aquatic Enemy Exhibit. Their leader [[Bessie Bass]] appears as the mini boss of World 3. Bessie Bass is the only Big Bertha in the game and the only one that can actually be defeated and also the one Big Bertha that can't kill Yoshi instantly.
{{br}}

===''Super Paper Mario''===
While Boss Basses don’t appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Boss Basses, they are mentioned. In [[The Underwhere]], a [[Shayde]] tells another one of three stories about how he lost a [[extra life|life]]. The second story involves him falling into the sea from a [[Warp Pipe|pipe]], but instead of drowning, he can apparently swim as well as a Cheep Cheep. However, a Boss Bass thought that since he swam like a fish, he was one himself, and the huge fish ate him. In the Japanese version, a shark is mentioned instead. The other mention of a Boss Bass is via the 77th [[Sammer Guy]] [[List of Sammer Guys#Sammer.27s Endgame .28Rounds 76-100.29|Ripper Van Fish]], who mentions counting Boss Basses to sleep if spoken to after defeat.
