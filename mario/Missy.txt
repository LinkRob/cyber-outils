'''Missy''' is a young girl who appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Mush-Rumors]]." She has a brother named [[Bud]], an [[Bud and Missy's mother|unnamed mother]], and a father named [[Norman]]. The whole family is from Witchita, [[Kansas]] in the in-universe real world, although end up in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] during a car trip to [[Wild Waldo's One-of-a-Kind Amusement Park]].
