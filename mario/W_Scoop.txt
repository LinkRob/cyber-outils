'''W Scoop''' is a television show that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is a news show that is hosted by an unnamed woman who talks about [[Wario]].

==History==
During the opening of the game, Wario can be seen watching ''W Scoop'' and is upset to hear that it didn't give him any updates of treasure for him to steal. Wario then changes the channel to ''[[The Silver Zephyr]]'' that interests him more and eventually causes him to invent the [[Telmet]] and enter the show. ''W Scoop'' isn't seen again after this point.

==Trivia==
*The woman that hosts ''W Scoop'' bears a resemblance to [[Mona]] from the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' series.
