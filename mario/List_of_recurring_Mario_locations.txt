!Mushroom Kingdom
----
[[File:Mushroom Kingdom.png|145x145px]]
|The [[Mushroom Kingdom]] is the main location of many of the ''Mario'' games. It is ruled by [[Princess Peach]] but is regularly invaded by [[Bowser]]. Many other major places are within the Mushroom Kingdom's borders, such as [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. The landscape of the Kingdom includes forests, deserts, snowy areas, beaches, mountains, and plains. The Mushroom Kingdom is also inhabited by many species including [[Human]]s, [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa]]s, [[Toad]]s and [[Bob-omb]]s. The Mushroom Kingdom's also hosts a variety of sports events, such as kart-racing, tennis, golf and baseball.
|<center>''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''</center>

!Princess Peach's Castle
----
[[File:NSMBU Normal Peach's Castle.png|145x145px]]
|[[Princess Peach's Castle]] is where Princess Peach lives. It resides in the capital of the Mushroom Kingdom [[Toad Town]]. Its first appearance was in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', where Bowser invaded, kidnapped the Princess and turned all the paintings into worlds where he hid the [[Power Star]]s. The castle has even been removed from its position several times, due to Bowser using some form of power to do so. Several race tracks also go past the castle, such as [[Royal Raceway]] and [[Mario Circuit]].
|<center>''[[Super Mario 64]]''</center>
|-
!Bowser's Castle
----
[[File:BowserCastleMK7.png|145x145px]]
|[[Bowser's Castle]] is the headquarters of the [[Koopa Troop]] and [[Bowser]]. When Bowser invades the Mushroom Kingdom he often takes Peach to this castle and Mario has to travel there to get her back. Bowser is known to have several other castles aside from this one. Bowser's castle was also attacked in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', when the sword [[Exor]] landed on the castle, scattering most of the Koopa Troop and destroying the bridge connecting the castle to the rest of the world. Several heroes including Mario, Peach and Bowser later rectified this.
|<center>''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''</center>
|-
!Toad Town
----
[[File:MarioCircuit MKWii.png|145x145px]]
|[[Toad Town]] is the capital city of the Mushroom Kingdom. It serves as the gateway to Princess Peach's Castle. It has been taken over by Bowser several times and was mostly destroyed by an [[Shroob|alien]] attack, but in each instance it was saved and restored, Toad Town has still remained a bright and colourful place. Toad Town's inhabitants include Toads, Koopas, and Shy Guys (indirectly).
|<center>''[[Paper Mario]]''</center>

!Yoshi's Island
----
[[File:YoshislandDS.png|145x145px]]
|[[Yoshi's Island (place)|Yoshi's Island]] is the native location of the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] and a part of [[Dinosaur Land]]. This island has been invaded by Bowser several times along with the rest of Dinosaur Land, but has always been saved by Mario and/or Yoshi. Yoshi's island is also the main location in the [[Yoshi (series)|''Yoshi'']] series, where Yoshi has adventures there. The island has even had several different names, such as Yo'ster Isle and [[Lavalava Island]].
|<center>''[[Super Mario World]]''</center>

!Donkey Kong Island
----
[[File:Donkey Kong Island DKCR.png|145x145px]]
|[[Donkey Kong Island]] is an island that is home to [[Donkey Kong]] and various other Kongs. It first appeared in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' and is the main setting of the [[Donkey Kong (series)|''Donkey Kong'']] series. The island has frequently been invaded by [[King K. Rool]] and the [[Kremling|Kremlings]], [[Tiki Tong]] and the [[Tiki Tak Tribe]], and [[Lord Fredrik]] and the [[Snowmads]]. It has also appeared in several ''Mario'' spinoffs.
|<center>''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''</center>

!Luigi's Mansion
----
[[File:Luigi's Mansion location.png|145x145px]]
|[[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Luigi's Mansion]] is a massive [[Ghost House]] built by the [[Boo]]s working for [[King Boo]] as part of a trap to capture the Mario Bros. This plot was foiled by [[Luigi]] and [[Professor E. Gadd]]. The mansion has also been used a racetrack and the location of several sports events. The mansion has many rooms including a kitchen, a dining room, a tea room, and a music room.
|<center>''[[Luigi's Mansion]]''</center>

!Isle Delfino
----
[[File:Over Delfino.png|145x145px]]
|[[Isle Delfino]] is a large dolphin-shaped island located outside the Mushroom Kingdom. It is a popular holiday resort populated by [[Pianta]]s and [[Noki]]s. Some of its attractions include a [[Pinna Park|theme park]], a [[Hotel Delfino|luxury hotel]] and a [[Delfino Square|race track]]. In ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', Mario, Peach and Toadsworth visit the island as part of a vacation but discover it has been mysteriously vandalized by [[Shadow Mario|someone who looks like Mario]]. Mario is then forced to clean up the Graffiti with the help of [[F.L.U.D.D.]], which gives time for Mario's imposter to kidnap Peach. This imposter is revealed to be [[Bowser Jr.]] disguised as Mario using a magic paintbrush. Mario then has to recover all the [[Shine Sprite]]s which have been stolen by Bowser Jr. to light up the island again.
