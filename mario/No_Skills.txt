'''No Skills''' is a special status condition that is commonly seen in the RPGs.

==Appearances==
===''Super Mario RPG''===
In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', [[Bowyer]] of the [[Smithy Gang]] can disable moves based on which buttons they are attached to. <!--Add how long the button is disabled-->
===''Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', No Skills is a rather rare condition in the game. When [[Mario]] is hit by a move that causes No Skills, then he cannot use a certain skill for a number of turns. This includes using his jump, hammer, or even items. All three can occur at once which can be very troublesome as it leaves Mario without an option to attack forcing him to rely on his partners. [[Spy Guy]]s, [[Fuzzipede]], and [[Bowser]] himself are the only ones that can cause No Skills in the game. Mario will get his commands back over time.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', No Skills return still as a rather rare condition. The only difference now is that Mario's partners can be affected as well. No Skills is caused by [[Bowser]] again but now by the three dragons in the game: [[Hooktail]], [[Gloomtail]], and [[Bonetail]].
