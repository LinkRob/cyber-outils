'''SMB2 Mushroom''' (known as '''Super Mario Bros. 2 Mushroom''') is a mushroom that appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

== In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' ==
In ver 3.0, the player can pick it up and become a ''Super Mario Bros. 2'' version of themselves. This gives them the ability to ride on enemies and throw them. Big enemies, flying enemies or even [[Chain Chomp]]s. But only available in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' style.

== Origin ==
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', you have to find a bonus room, find the mushroom, pick the mushroom to get defense. The characters in the original game can stand on enemies or throw enemies. They could also pick up vegetables and throw at enemies.
