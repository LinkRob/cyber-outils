'''Finger Shot''' is one of [[Geno]]'s personal weapons used in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be either obtained from [[Gaz]] or by buying it in the [[Moleville]] shop for 50 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Finger Shot on Geno increases his [[Attack]] power by 12. During battle, the Finger Shot enables Geno to shoot bullets at enemies.
