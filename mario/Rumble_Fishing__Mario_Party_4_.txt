'''Rumble Fishing''' is a Battle minigame in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera pans a shot of the bright sun before tilting down the players on the dock.

== Gameplay ==
Each player has their own fishing line, when they feel a bite indicating the rumble feature, they reel the rod rapidly and the player who reels the rod the fastest, results in a big [[Cheep Cheep]].

== Ending ==
The players turn around with them celebrating their victory while the losers sulk with disappointment.
