The '''Mushroom Wrestling Federation''' is a wrestling organization based in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] that made a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Tag Team Trouble]]." They are the hosts of the [[Tag Team Wrestling Tournament]], which had a grand prize of a one million [[Coin|Gold Coins]]. The only known employee is the [[Mushroom Referee]], who is both the host and referee of the tournament.

[[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s cousins [[Mushroom Marauder and Jake the Crusher Fungus]] are featured on a poster from the Mushroom Wrestling Federation.

The name "Mushroom Wrestling Federation" is a play on the real {{w|WWE|World Wrestling Federation}} (WWF), as it was called at the time, before rebranding to World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE).
