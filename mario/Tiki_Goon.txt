'''Tiki Goon''' is a Tiki enemy which first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tiki Goons are wooden, bongo-shaped creatures. Their facial features include a square hole for a nose, yellow pupils, and black sclera. Tiki Goons wear a cloth on their head, a striped skirt, and four red feathers sticking from the left and right sides of their head each.

===Behavior===
Tiki Goons roam back back and forth in a set pattern in ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' and ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games alike.

==In ''Donkey Kong Country Returns''==
Tiki Goons are one of the first enemies encountered, as they are first encountered in the very first level, [[Jungle Hijinxs (Donkey Kong Country Returns)|Jungle Hijinxs]]. [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] can defeat a Tiki Goon by either [[jump]]ing onto it, by using [[Rambi the Rhinoceros|Rambi]], or throwing a [[barrel]] at it.

===Donkey Kong Country Tropical freeze===
A piece of concept artwork in '[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]] shows two Tiki Goon carcasses lying at the base of a large rock. The artwork is unlocked in the image gallery after collecting all Puzzle Pieces in [[Grassland Groove]].

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 7]]''===
Tiki Goons are obstacles encountered in parts of the track [[DK Jungle (Mario Kart 7)|DK Jungle]]. If a racer drives into a Tiki Goon, they spin out and lose three [[coin]]s.

===''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
Tiki Goons return as obstacles in {{classic course|3DS|DK Jungle}}, acting the same as before. In the original [[Wii U]] version, the player unlock a Tiki Goon [[stamp]] by winning against the Staff Ghost of {{classic course|3DS|DK Jungle}}.

==In ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''==
[[File:DKCR microgame.png|thumb|250px|The Donkey Kong Country Returns microgame in ''WarioWare: Get It Together!'']]
A Tiki Goon appears in level 1 and sometimes level 2 of the [[Donkey Kong Country Returns (microgame)|Donkey Kong Country Returns]] [[microgame]] as an enemy which has to be defeated.
