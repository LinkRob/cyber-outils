'''''Mario no Photopi''''' is a creativity game for the [[Nintendo 64]]. It was released on December 2, 1998, exclusively in Japan. It is the only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game to have compatibility with SmartMedia cards, from which digital photos can be transferred to the game and other devices.
