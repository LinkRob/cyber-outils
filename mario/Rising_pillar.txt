The Rising Pillar is an object in [[Super Mario 3D World]] and [[Reissue|it's remake]]: [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]. It resembles a stone pillar that rises as the player interacts with it

== In Super Mario 3D World ==
In the level [[Mount Beanpole]], near the top of the level, there is an Rising Pillar that the player can scratch on with a [[Super Bell]] to get to a cloud for a [[Green Star]]. When the player scratches it with the Cat Suit, it will rise into the air until it reaches it's maximum height. The level: '''[[Fire Bros. Hideout 2|Fire Bros. Hideout #2]]''' also includes a Rising Pillar which is used to defeat the [[Fire Bro]] and [[Goomba Tower]] for a [[Green Star]].

== In Bowser's Fury ==
The shines, "Climb to the Shine" and "Spin, Scramble, Shine" can be accessed by [[Cat Mario]] by scratching on the wheel on the pillar and climbing up them for these two shines.
