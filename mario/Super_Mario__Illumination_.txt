'''Super Mario''' is a form that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[illumination Entertainment]] and it spin-offs. It is a transformation of [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]], when touching a [[Super Mushroom (Illumination)|Super Mushroom]].

==Appearance==
When Mario eats a super mushroom, his body grows larger and stronger. He can punch, stomp blocks, beat enemies and run fast.

==Weaknesses==
Mario would take one hit damage from an enemy, return back to his regular form as shown during his [[Training Course (Illumination)|training]].

==History==
===Film series===
====The Super Mario Bros. Movie====
During [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]]'s Training Course, she begs Mario to eat the Super Mushroom as he doesn't want to despite his hatred of mushroom food. Mario would go super form as he attempts to reach the flag unsuccessfully many times. Later, Mario would transform into a super form again before fighting against Bowser's minions alongside [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] in the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] in order to stop [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]].

==Trivia==
Unlike in the games, Super Mario is treated as a power-up of Mario's default form as opposed to it being Mario's default size, as [[Small Mario]]'s signature shrunken proportions was not present when Mario is depowered. Super Mario is depicted as twice the size of normal Mario.
