The '''Happy Shell''' is a type of armor only usable by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It essentially has the same appearance as his [[Bowser's Shell|shell]], as shown from the artwork. The Happy Shell can be bought at [[Marrymore]]'s hotel for 38 [[coin]]s. Equipping it on Bowser increases his [[Defense]] by 6 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 3.
