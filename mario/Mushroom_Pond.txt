The '''Mushroom Pond''' is a location in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It is a pond located west of the [[Odyssey]] with a waterfall that pours water into the pond. It is directly in front of the kingdom's [[Koopa Freerunning Ranking Board]]. Inside the lake is a blue [[Dorrie]] with three regional coins on its back, and a small nook with three more regional coins. Under the waterfall is a [[Warp Pipe]] which leads into a room with a giant painting. Entering the painting leads to the rematch against [[Mollusque-Lanceur]].
