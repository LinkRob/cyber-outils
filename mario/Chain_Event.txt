'''Chain Event''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
*Slide down the chain and dodge the Spike Balls. Be the first player to reach the ground to win! The last place player, if they make it to the ground before the results screen comes, will do a groan without a sound.
*To defeat COM (Very Hard) and COM (Master) in this minigame make sure you don't hit any of the Spiked Balls, which is extremely difficult.
*In reverse minigame, it is the player who takes the longest to slide down the chain to win.

==Controls==
Hold the {{button|Wii|Wiimote}} vertically. Shake it left to move the character left and right to move them right.
