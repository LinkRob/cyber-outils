'''Giant wheels''' are round stone sculptures with menacing faces painted along their rolling surface originating in ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Heroes|Sonic Heroes]]'' as the final, chasing obstacle of [[w:c:sonic:Ocean Palace|Ocean Palace]]. Their only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance is in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]''.

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Wii)==
They appear in both [[Dream Alpine (Individual)|Individual]] and [[Dream Alpine (Team)|Team]] versions of [[Dream Alpine]], which takes heavy influence from Ocean Palace, despite being labeled as [[w:c:sonic:Seaside Hill|Seaside Hill]]. Here, they slowly roll either across the path or down the path against the racers, contrary to their original role as fast pursuers.
