The '''Fuzzy Dress''' is a personal armor of [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]]'s featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from a shop in [[Nimbus Land]] for 70 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Fuzzy Dress increases Princess Toadstool's [[Defense]] by 36 and her [[Magic Defense]] by 18.
