'''Super Jump''' is a move in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Super Jump is a fielding ability that allows the user to [[jump]] higher than others, allowing them to catch a ball at a certain height, including low home run balls, that cannot be caught otherwise. A Super Jump can be used by either [[Luigi]], [[Waluigi]], [[Baby Luigi]], [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]], [[Paragoomba]], [[Boo]], or [[King Boo]].
