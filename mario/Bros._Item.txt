'''Bros. Items''', also known as '''Bros. Attacks''',<ref>[https://cdn02.nintendo-europe.com/media/downloads/games_8/emanuals/nintendo_ds_21/Manual_NintendoDS_MarioAndLuigiPartnersInTime_EN.pdf ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'' English manual], pages 20 and 23</ref> are special [[item]]s used by [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Bros. Items are different from regular items in that they are used for attacking one or multiple enemies at once (oftentimes with a specialized [[jump]] attack), and depending on the item, they require at least two characters or all four characters to be active in battle when utilized. Bros. Items are introduced by [[Toadsworth the Younger]] in [[Hollijolli Village]] when Baby Mario and Baby Luigi fight the three [[Shroob]]s in the village, with the [[Green Shell]] item, and later on the player obtains different kinds of Bros. Items, either from [[? Block]]s or buying them from [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time/Shops|item shops]].

Bros. Items replace [[Bros. Attack]]s from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', though they are replaced by the very same attacks in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' (being named "[[Special Attacks]]" in that game), and in all subsequent ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' games (''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'').
