'''Boosters''' are an item in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Boosters are used to multiply the values of a [[Dice Block]], but a red-faced 0 always remains on the Dice Block. If a Dice Block lands on the 0, the Boosters are used up, but one or a few Boosters can still be received if the character is either on a [[VS Space]] and wins the minigame, on a [[Booster Space]], or at the start of the board.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
Boosters are only used in [[Rocket Road]]. It is possible for a character to win if they get a 25 from using four Boosters, due to the board's length.
