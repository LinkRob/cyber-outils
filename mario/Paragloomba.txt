A '''Paragloomba''' is an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are a variant of [[Gloomba]], which Paragloombas have the same appearance as but with added wings, like with [[Paragoomba]]s to [[Goomba]]s.

===Habitats===
Like Gloombas, Paragloombas can only be found in certain underground areas. They are also stronger than Gloombas.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
Paragloombas can be found in [[Toad Town Tunnels]] usually alongside Gloombas and [[Spiky Gloomba]]s. In battle, they have an [[Heart Point|HP]] of, an [[Attack]] of 2, and no [[Defense]].

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Paragloombas are found on the lower floors of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. Paragloombas retain most of their overall stats except for their Attack, which has been changed to 3. Both the log and tattle for a Paragloomba suggests that its wings are moldy.
