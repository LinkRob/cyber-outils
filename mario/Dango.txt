'''Dango''' are a variant of [[Strollin' Stu]] and an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It carries ten regular Strollin' Stus atop it.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
[[File:Giant Stu.png|thumb|left|200px|A Dango on its own, upon being flipped over]]
A Dango appears in the amusement park area of [[Pinna Park]], underneath a wooden bridge near the [[Roller Coaster]]. [[Mario]] can use [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] to spray and knock down every Strollin' Stu stacked onto Dango to then [[jump]] onto the Dango itself. A Dango drops a [[Blue Coin|blue coin]] the first time Mario defeats it.
