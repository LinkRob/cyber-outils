'''Airbrushers''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a living room with some paint on the floor.

==Opening==
Both characters on each team are holding a pair of large wings, and are sprung off of a paintbrush as the minigame starts.

==Gameplay==
After springing off a paintbrush at the start, each team, consisting of two members, has to cooperatively use a pair of large, feathery wings to collect [[Coin]]s within the air. There are a few [[Red Coin]]s, worth five individual Coins.

Like any other minigame that involves collecting Coins, there is no winning or losing criteria, and each character at the end performs their victory animation if they have collected at least one Coin, otherwise they do their losing animation.

==Controls==
*{{button|ds|A}}: Flap wing

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''You and your partner each take one wing as you flap toward floating coins. Red coins are worth five times as much as the normal ones.''
*'''Tips''': ''To get the higher coins, you and your partner must each repeatedly flap your wing at the same time.''
