{{quote|Try these fresh red peppers! ...Oh, who am I kidding? In this haze, these peppers look totally unappetizing!|Pianta|Super Mario Sunshine}}
'''Peppers''' are one of the [[fruit]]s in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' at [[Delfino Plaza]]. If [[Yoshi]] eats one, he turns [[Purple Yoshi|purple]], allowing him to spit [[juice]] of the same color. Peppers are the only type of fruit that Yoshis never ask for to hatch out of their [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s.

Later on, during the mission [[King Boo Down Below]] at [[Sirena Beach]], Peppers are essential to attacking [[King Boo (Super Mario Sunshine)|King Boo]]. By throwing a pepper at his tongue, King Boo is stunned, allowing [[Mario]] to damage him with another fruit.
