'''Nicole Eggert''' is a famous actress who portrays herself in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Neatness Counts]]." Nicole's reason for visiting [[Mario Bros. Plumbing]] is because she was looking for a plumber to fix her kitchen sink.
