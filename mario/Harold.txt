==History==
Harold lives in [[Flopside]] with his wife [[Gertrude]] for sixty years. Harold has been described as "old school" and rather keeps most things to himself. After completing the game, it is revealed that Harold managed to buy Gertrude a gift which is a wedding ring that he couldn't afford at the time.
