'''Bone-Dry Dunes''' (written as '''Bone Dry Dunes''' in the British English version) is the second race track of the [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== Layout ==
Bone-Dry Dunes is mainly a desert-themed course, although unlike [[Dry Dry Desert]], which gives off an Egyptian feel, this track is more like an Arabian town. There is also a structure near the beginning that resembles [[Dry Bowser]].

== Real world influences ==
=== In pop culture ===
There was a Twitter post from Nintendo UK who asked about one of the options that is a course in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. As a result, most people incorrectly respond to "Dry Bone Dunes".<ref>[https://twitter.com/NintendoUK/status/1346858866441650182 Without checking, which of these is a course in Mario Kart 8 Deluxe?]</ref>

== Trivia ==
* Bone-Dry Dunes' theme is a remix of [[Moo Moo Meadows]] theme.<sup>[''[[wikipedia:Wikipedia:Dubious|dubious]]'']</sup>
** Both of the soundtracks have the violin incorporated in the melody, although the violin in Bone-Dry Dunes' music has a brief melody.
*[[Dry Bones]] appear as obstacles, although in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', he is a playable character.
** After his appearance in ''8 Deluxe'', the staff ghost used Dry Bones for the 200cc [[Time Trial]]s.
