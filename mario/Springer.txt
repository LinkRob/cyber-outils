A '''Springer''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are a stronger variant of [[Shyster]]s that work for the [[Smithy Gang]], and Springers are encountered within the [[The Factory]].

==Battle==
Like with Shysters, Springers mainly attack by hopping at at party member for damage. However, they can also use the move [[Somnus Waltz]] in order to put someone to sleep.
