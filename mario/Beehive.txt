'''Beehives''' are the homes for [[bee]]s in both the real world and the {{franchise|Mario}}. Their first appearance in a ''Mario''-related title is ''[[Donkey Kong 3]]'', but the first time a Beehive appears in the ''Mario'' franchise is ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==In ''Donkey Kong 3''==
Beehives are obstacles knocked on by [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] to dispatch the bugs. In the [[greenhouse (blue)|blue]] and [[greenhouse (gray)|gray greenhouse]]s, Donkey Kong uses his fists on the beehive, and in the [[greenhouse (yellow)|yellow greenhouse]], he uses [[coconut]]s.

At the yellow greenhouse, [[Stanley]] has to force Donkey Kong into a beehive at the top, followed by the beehive falling onto his head. In the [[arcade]] version, this is followed by a cutscene of Donkey Kong twitching and scrambling to get the beehive off. Donkey Kong falls down to the ground and the beehive breaks open from impact, revealing Donkey Kong to have a partially swollen face and a puffy right eye.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''===
The level [[Honeybees (level)|Honeybees]] takes place inside of a giant beehive.

===''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''===
Beehives are obstacles in a few of the levels. They appear in episode 8 in [[Bianco Hills]], and every episode in [[Gelato Beach]] and [[Pianta Village]]. If [[Mario]] gets near a beehive or knocks it down by spraying it, the bees will swarm him, causing some damage if he gets stung. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can eat the bees, and if he manages to, a [[Blue Coin]] is revealed.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''[[Mario Party 2]]''===
[[File:Honeycomb Havoc MP2.png|thumb|left|200px|A honeycomb in the tree in ''Mario Party 2'']]
Beehives, called Honeycombs, appear in the minigame, [[Honeycomb Havoc]]. They will appear in a line of fruits, and the players will jump under a numbered block to cause the items to fall. If the honeycomb falls onto a player, the bees will swarm them, eliminating them from the game.

===''[[Mario Party 7]]''===
[[File:MP7 Big Dripper.png|thumb|200px|Beehives in Big Dripper in ''Mario Party 7'']]
Many beehives appear in the [[Big Dripper]] minigame. The stage has a group of beehives on the ceiling, and two giant beehives are on the sides. 
{{br|left}}

==In ''[[Paper Mario]]''==
[[File:Beehive Paper Mario.png|thumb|200px|left|A beehive in the Forever Forest area in ''Paper Mario'']]
A beehive appears in the [[Forever Forest]] area. If Mario hits the tree with his hammer, it will fall, and a [[Bzzap!]] will come out and challenge Mario.

==In ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''==
Beehives, or '''hives''', are objects in the [[Grassy Greens Stage]]. Enemies named [[Bumbler]]s are inside of them.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
[[File:Beehive SSBU artwork.png|thumb|left|The ''Animal Crossing'' Beehive in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''.]]
The [[w:c:animalcrossing:Beehive|Beehive]] from the ''Animal Crossing'' franchise appears as an item in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. When a beehive is picked up and thrown, bees come out to swarm the fighter and inflict damage steadily. If the hive is attacked, the bees target whoever hit it.
