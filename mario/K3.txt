K3 is the fifth world in [[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]] on the SNES version however on the Gameboy Advance version you can go to this world, [[Pacifica]], and [[Razor Ridge]] in any order. It has 6 stages, the boss of the region is [[Bleak]] and the [[Brother Bear's House]] is [[Blizzard's Basecamp]]
