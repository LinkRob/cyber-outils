}}'''Gold Rings''' are objects that appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. When [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] jumps through a Gold Ring, all the enemies change into golden enemies for a certain amount of time. When an enemy is defeated, a small amount of coins will be collected and thrown Koopa shells make trails of coins. Some enemies, like [[Bullet Bill]]s and [[Banzai Bill]]s leave trails wherever they go.

==Trivia==
The Gold Ring has a striking resemblance to the Rings of the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' franchise. They serve a similar function especially to Warp Rings.
