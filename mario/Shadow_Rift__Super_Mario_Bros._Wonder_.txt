The '''Shadow Rift''' is an enemy from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', being featured as the titular enemy of [[Beware of the Rifts]].

== Profile ==

=== Appearance ===
Shadow rifts are irregularly-shaped black blobs. Their bodies have a swirling pattern.

=== Behaviour ===
Shadow rifts move around in various directions, attempting to consume [[Mario (character)|Mario]].

== In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' ==
Shadow rifts only appear in the level Beware of the Rifts, the fourth course of World 5, [[Fungi Mines]].
