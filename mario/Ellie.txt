'''Ellie the Elephant''' is an [[Animal Buddy]] who is an elephant in the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' series.

==History==
===''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!''===
Ellie first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble]]'' as an uncommonly found [[Animal Buddy]]. Here, Ellie has the ability to suck in water and then spit it out of her trunk to defeat other enemies. She also has the ability to carry [[barrel]]s with her trunk (even from a far length). She is also known to have a fear of [[Sneek]]s and runs away whenever she sees one.

Also, this fear of Sneaks has Ellie run away uncontrollably in [[Stampede Sprint]] from a trio of Sneaks at the start of the level. She also fights [[Squirt]].

===''Donkey Kong Land III''===
Unlike most characters who play the same role in this game, Ellie has a different role. She no longer has a fear of [[Sneek]]s, has an indefinite amount of water supply. Ellie, however, can no longer stick [[Barrel]]s in her trunk. Since Squirt is not in the game, Ellie does not fight him.

===''Donkey Kong Racing''===
Ellie was to be a rideable [[Animal Buddy]] as seen in the trailer for ''[[Donkey Kong Racing]]'' until the game ended up as vaporware. She is seen in the ending scene with [[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] riding her along with many other foes following the two of them.

===''Donkey Konga''===
Ellie makes a cameo appearance in ''[[Donkey Konga]]'' where she appears at the bottom of the screen dancing to beats played.

=== ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' ===
Ellie made a cameo in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a spirit. The artwork from ''Donkey Kong Country 3'' is reused. To claim her spirit at the [[Adventure Mode: World of Light|World of Light]], the spirit can be found at the Light Realm. The opponents are made up of four Puppet Fighters cloned from [[Squirtle]]. The opponents favor neutral specials as the only condition. Squirtle's neutral special, [[w:c:supersmashbros:Water Gun|Water Gun]] mimicks Ellie's ability to squirt water out of her trunk.
