'''Spade Panels''' are a type of [[panel]] featured on the world map in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and its [[reissue]]s. Accessing one allows [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to play a minigame hosted by a [[Toad]] in which there are three slots that spin like a slot machine between the panel of a [[Mushroom]], a [[Fire Flower]], and a [[Starman]] each. The player has to try and line up all three panels to show a matching image. The reward for doing so is [[extra life|extra lives]]; getting a complete Mushroom, Fire Flower, or Starman picture rewards Mario or Luigi with 2, 3, and 5 extra lives, respectively, similar to the reward given by a [[level]] [[goal]] by getting the same picture in three consecutive levels.
