'''Toad Rally Tickets''', known as Rally Tickets, are a type of item in Super Mario Run. Rally Tickets are used to participate against other players in Toad Rally. A Rally Ticket is used for every match is played. Anytime the player runs out of them, Toad Rally can't played until you need to earn more the Rally Tickets. They can be come together in several different ways, collecting all of the color coins in a level (tickets for each set), playing the mini-games, buying them in the shop via linking a My Nintendo account, and from blocks in the kingdom. There is no Rally Ticket is used during a Friendly Run, but no Toads can be gained nor lost if the player either wins or loses the match. As of the version 3.0.4 update, players have to use Rally Tickets in order to play a round of the Remix 10 mode.
