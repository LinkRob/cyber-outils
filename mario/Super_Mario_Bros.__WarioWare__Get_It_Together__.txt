'''Super Mario Bros.''' is a [[microgame]] part of the [[Nintendo Classics]] set in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame is based on and takes place within the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[Super Mario Bros.|same name]], specifically at the end of [[World 8-4 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 8-4]]. Aside from a stretched 16:9 display, the only difference with the location's layout is a ramp added near the center of the [[bridge]]. The name "MARIO" at the top-left of the HUD is left unchanged.

==Gameplay==
The object is for the player's character to defeat [[Bowser]]. They can do so by either attacking him with a certain number of projectiles, upon which he tumbles over into the [[lava]], or by touching the [[axe]]. If Bowser is defeated using the former method, he falls into the lava in the same manner as being defeated from [[Fire Mario]]'s [[fireball]]s in ''Super Mario Bros.'' itself. If the player's character does not defeat Bowser or gets defeated by him, the player loses at the microgame.

Bowser's attacks differ between each difficulty level. In Level 1, Bowser only breathes out bursts of fire. In Level 2, he throws [[hammer]]s. In Level 3, he not only utilizes both attacks, but a [[Podoboo]] sometimes jumps out of the lava.

[[Orbulon]] and [[5-Volt]] are listed as the characters suited for the microgame, as Orbulon can use his beam to vacuum up any obstacles, including Bowser, while 5-Volt can teleport over to the Axe at the end. [[9-Volt]] and [[Kat and Ana]] are listed as the least suitable. For 9-Volt, this is because he can possibly crash into Bowser with his skateboard, hence why a ramp was added into the bridge, and has to time Bowser's jumps before grappling the [[Ring (WarioWare: Get It Together!)|ring]] that carries him over to the Axe on the other side. For Kat and Ana, their uncontrollable jumping can possibly cause them to jump into Bowser's fireballs, and they cannot throw shurikens with precision.
