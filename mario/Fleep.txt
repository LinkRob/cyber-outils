'''Fleep''' is a [[pixl]] partner in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Fleep can first be found inside the lavatory in the second level of chapter four. Fleep has locked in the bathroom for fifteen hundred years due to the lack of toilet paper. Once Mario finds a piece of paper for Fleep, he will join Mario's Party.

Fleep has the ability to flip panel of the background to reveal hidden items. While this is only used a few times in story mode, it becomes essential if Mario uses treasure maps to find rare items.
