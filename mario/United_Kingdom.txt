The '''United Kingdom''' is a real-world country which has made some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearances, most commonly its capital city of [[London]]. The United Kingdom made its first ''Mario''-related appearance in ''[[NES Open Tournament Golf]]''.

==In ''NES Open Tournament Golf''==
The United Kingdom is one of three playable golf courses, titled the '''UK Course''', the other two being [[Japan]], the [[United States of America]], and one of six courses in the Japanese version. Like the other golf courses, the United Kingdom has 18 holes. The UK Course is the largest of the courses in both Japanese and international versions.

==In {{series|Mario Discovery}}==
===''[[Mario is Missing!]]''===
In all versions of the game, [[Luigi]] visits London on his journey to rescue Mario. The [[Globulator]] shows some other cities in the United Kingdom, although Luigi cannot travel to them. In PC versions, the five other cities include {{w|Aberdeen}}, {{w|Belfast}}, {{w|Edinburgh}}, {{w|Glasgow}}, and {{w|Nottingham}}. In the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version, besides London, only {{w|Gibraltar}} is shown.

===''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''===
All versions of the game feature at least one location in the United Kingdom that Mario can time-travel to via the [[Timulator]], during a certain year, but they differ between versions. The only location within the United Kingdom that Mario can travel to in the PC, [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]], and [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] versions is [[Cambridge]], during 1687. In PC and Super Nintendo Entertainment System releases, Mario can also travel to [[Stratford-upon-Avon]], during 1601 and [[London]] (referred to as [[England]] in the Super Nintendo version) in 1595; the PC version also allows Mario to time-travel to London during the years 1831 and 1843. In the Nintendo Entertainment System [[Mario's Time Machine (Nintendo Entertainment System)|version]], Mario can time-travel to the United Kingdom itself in 1602.

==In ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''==
The United Kingdom is one of the countries on the [[Globe]] souvenir, which shows a picture of the British flag, the {{w|Union Jack}}, and lists the country as "Great Britain."

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' features [[London Loop]], [[London Loop 2]], and [[London Loop 3]], all of which take place in the United Kingdom's capital city of London. London Loop itself returns in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''{{'}}s third wave of the [[Booster Course Pass]].

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]''===
The game takes place in the United Kingdom, more specifically London, just as the real-life {{w|2012 Summer Olympics}} did.

==Trivia==
*In the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Adventures of Sherlock Mario]]," the episode takes place in [[Victoria]], a location based on the United Kingdom, specifically London during the {{w|Victorian Period}}.
*Similarly, in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', [[Bonneton]] in the [[Cap Kingdom]] is also based on the United Kingdom during the Victorian Period.
