'''Future Dream''' is a board in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

== Description ==
Future Dream is set in the [[Dream Depot]], the main setting of the game. The setting is themed after a space station, with rocket ships, teleportation devices, and shortcuts to navigate through the different stations. In ''Story Mode'', the board is only made up of one large space station. In ''Party Mode'', there are three space stations, one in the direct center of the board, and one on the west and east sides. There are floating platforms that can be used by players to be able to get from one station to another.

=== [[Happening Space|? Spaces]] ===
* There are [[Happening Space]]s located on the central platform, which are next to three rocket ships. Upon landing on these spaces, each rocket takes players to either the west or east platforms' landing pads.
* The west space station includes a rocket ship game, where the player controls the levitation of their ships to collect extra [[coin]]s from UFOs. 
* On the east space station, there are two more Happening Spaces next to a pair of robot arms. This will activate a slot machine minigame. By performing expertly in this minigame by properly lining up all of the slots, they can win a [[Mushroom Capsule]] as their consolation prize.
* Lastly, there are Happening Spaces next to the air taxis on the west and east platforms. These spaces will allow players to ride the air taxis from one station to the other, but they must pay fee of ten coins first.

==Trivia==

*Future Dream is similar to [[Space Land]] (from ''[[Mario Party 2]]''). That is because both of these boards take place in outer space and follow the classic ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series formula (that is, passing a [[Star Space]] and trading 20 [[coin]]s for a [[Star (Mario Party series)|star]]). 
*This is one of the three boards in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' which are similar to boards from ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. The other two are [[Pirate Dream]] and [[Bowser Nightmare]] (which are similar to the ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' boards [[Pirate Land]] and [[Bowser Land]]).
