'''Cutie Battle Contest, Round 2''' is the eighth level of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 18.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 2 [[Pokey]]s, 3 [[Chargin' Chuck]]s, and 3 [[Para-Beetle]]s. The enemy captain is a Chargin' Chuck.

The 2nd battle contains 5 Pokeys, and 3 [[Spear Guy]]s. The enemy captain is a Pokey, and he knows [[Chance]] and [[Deny]].

The final battle contains 1 Pokey, 1 Chargin' Chuck, and 6 Para-Beetles. The enemy captain is a Para-Beetle, and he know [[Rally]] and Deny.

After the final battle, the player recruits a Spear Guy, Pokey, and a Para-Beetle.

The battle can be replayed to recruit up to 1 Spear Guy, Pokey, Chargin' Chuck and/or Para-Beetle.
