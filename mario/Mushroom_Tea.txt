'''Mushroom Tea''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Description==
There are three variations of Mushroom Tea: Regular, which restores all [[Heart Point|HP]]; Milk, which restores all [[Special Point|SP]]; and Royal, which restores all HP and SP. Each type of Mushroom Tea is consumed right after being bought, so they cannot be stored within the inventory.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
Each type of Mushroom Tea can be bought from a different location. The regular type can be bought at the [[Tea Kiosk]] in the [[Toad Town Mall]] for five [[coin]]s. Milk Mushroom Tea can also be bought at the Tea Counter, but for eight coins. Lastly, Royal Mushroom Tea can be bought at the Tea Kiosk for ten coins, or obtained for free at the [[Star Shop]].
