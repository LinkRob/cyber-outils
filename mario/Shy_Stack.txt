'''Shy Stacks''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Each Shy Stack consists of four different [[Shy Guy]]s stacked together, and it is one of the toughest Shy Guy variants. [[General Guy]] uses these powerful guys only once, as the last defense with his battle with [[Mario]]. These minions each have a slingshot, and deal a point of damage, although they inflict four total with one single hit. These are also the Shy Guys with the third-highest hit point value, General Guy and [[Anti Guy]] being the highest in the order of lower to higher.
