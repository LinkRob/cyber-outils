The '''Vampire Candy''' is a green [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It has a picture of a [[Swooper]] on the wrapper, and when a player uses it, they transform into a vampire. As a "Vampire" character, they spin the dart wheel to determine how many [[coin]]s they will steal from each of their opponents. They will then steal the determined coins by flying into their opponents. In Tag Battle, players can steal coins only from the opposing team and not their own teammate, since each pair of teammates shares its coin and [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] total. Vampire Candy can be found on [[Goomba's Booty Boardwalk]], [[King Boo's Haunted Hideaway]], [[Koopa's Tycoon Town]] and [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]]. This Candy's effect is similar to that of the [[Vacuum Orb]] from ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

The player transformed into a vampire will have a purple hue on them, as well as red eyes with yellow sclera.
