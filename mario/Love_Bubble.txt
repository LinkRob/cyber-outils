'''Love Bubbles''' are strange, pink, spherical creatures with wings and two round feet. They have two "X"s for eyes (which become spirals when they take damage) and a small smile. They are encountered in the [[Toad Town]], [[Star Hill]] and [[Star Shrine]]. In the field, Love Bubbles generally fly around aimlessly, but intruding upon their territory may cause them to attack the Bros. with a divebomb. They appear as enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. 

In battle, a Love Bubble employs a variety of different attacks. They cannot be hit by ground-based weaponry since it can fly. First, a Love Bubble may fly high in the air then dive bomb into a [[Mario]] brother. However, before attacking, the Love Bubble with lift up one of its feet to indicate which Bro. it will dive bomb. If it lifts up its right foot, the Love Bubble will attack Mario; if left, it will attack [[Luigi]].

Instead of dive bombing, the Love Bubble may attempt to attack both Mario Bros. To do this, the creature will fly in front of the brothers, hovering either low to the ground or high in the air. If low to the ground, the Love Bubble will attempt to fly into both Mario Bros. Thus both Mario Bros. must [[jump]] to dodge this attack. However, if the Love Bubble is hovering high in the air, it will fly down into one Bro. then fly back up. Thus only the first Bro. should jump while the second Bro. remains still. If both jump, the second Bro will be hit during the Love Bubble's ascent. Again, the Love Bubble's feet will indicate which Bro. will be attacked first.

A Love Bubble may turn into a glowing sphere and circle around the Mario Bros. Eventually, the Love Bubble will home in on one Bro. and strike. The player can counter this attack with a [[Hammer]] swing as the winged creature circles around the Mario Bros.  Depending on which Mario Bro. counters, if Luigi hits it, it will be flying upward and eventually crash into the ground. If Mario hits the moving sphere with his hammer, it flies like a baseball and gets back to its battle spot after when going off the screen. This is helpful when fighting the [[Elder Shrooboid]] later in the game when it uses its spiked ball attack. In the Japanese and European versions the Love Bubble will always fly up into the air when hit regardless of which Bro. hit it. The Love Bubble will never use this move when the Plumbers are fighting without the babies.

However, despite all these attacks, the Love Bubble's signature technique is healing other enemies. When attacked, a Love Bubble will heal one-fourth of the damage inflicted to another monster. As such, all Love Bubbles should be eliminated before they can heal their allies in battle.

A stronger version called the [[Soul Bubble]] appears later in [[Shroob Castle]].
