'''Tolielip''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tolielip is a [[wikipedia:narcissus (plant)|narcissus]], but he looks like a [[wikipedia:tulip|tulip]]. He has orange petals.

===Etymology===
Tolielip's name is a combination of "to lie," referring to Tolielip's lies, and tulip.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Tolielip lives [[Flower Fields]] in the main area along with [[Wise Wisterwood]]. He often tells secrets that are mostly made up of lies. [[Tattle|Tattling]] him with [[Goombario]] confirms that Tolielip is a well-known liar.
