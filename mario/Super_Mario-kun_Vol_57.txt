'''''Super Mario-kun Volume 57''''' is the fifty-seventh volume of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' based on ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''.

== Description ==
'''''Super Mario-kun Volume 57''''' is the fifty-seventh volume of the ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' illustrated by Yukio Sawada and published by Shogakukan. The comic book is based off the ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' events starring [[Olivia (Super Mario-kun)|Olivia]] and [[Mario (Super Mario-kun)|Mario]].
