'''Mug Shot''' is a [[Job Center]] [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''.

==Gameplay==
Mug Shot is based on the ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' of the [[Mug Shot (WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!)|same name]], and it has the same objective. Before the microgame starts, the player has to draw the glass that will slide across the table. During the microgame, the player must use their [[Stylus]] to tap the glass at the right timing in order for Wario to catch it. If the glass slides past Wario, they lose at the microgame.
