The '''Heart of Gold''' is the center of the [[Planet Core]] inside the [[Mushroom Earth]].

== Landscape ==
[[File:Heart of Gold (outside).jpg|Heart of Gold on the map screen|thumb|left]]
The Heart of Gold can be reached by [[minecart]] from the Climactic Cliff at [[Underground World|Sublayer]] 1609. The Heart itself is a massive orb of Bananergy endlessly pouring out of the [[Banandium Root]], a mystical object which will grant any good wish. The golden streams projecting out the Root become the Banandium Gold and Banandium Gems found in all above layers all the way to the surface.

== Trivia ==
* This locale is mentioned in Pauline's song, [[Breaking Through (Heart of Gold)]].
