The '''You Decide on the Adventure''', also known as '''Choose Your Own Adventure''', is a series of gamebooks, published in [[2002]] and [[2003]] by [[Scholastic]] and based on video games for [[Game Boy Advance]] and [[Game Boy Color]]. Two of them are based on ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and another two on ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]''.
