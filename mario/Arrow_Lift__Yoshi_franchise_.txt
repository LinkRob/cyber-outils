An '''Arrow Lift''' is an object in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming games. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Description==
An Arrow Lift is a wheel-shaped platform depicting an arrow that constantly spins around until a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] jumps on it, upon which the Arrow Lift stops spinning and begins traveling in the direction shown by the arrow.

==In {{series|Yoshi's Island}}==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
'''Spinning Wheels''', as they are named, are first used in [[Salvo the Slime's Castle]]. Spinning Wheels come in light brown and light blue varieties.

===''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''===
For their reappearance, they were renamed to '''Arrow Wheel''', and serve the same purpose as before.

===''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''===
Arrow Lifts retain the same purpose again. They have now been named Arrow Lift, revealed from a [[Message Block]].

==In ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''==
Arrow Lifts make their first appearance outside the ''Yoshi's Island'' series.
