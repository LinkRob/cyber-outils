'''Misty''' is a minor character in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Misty is a young [[Flip-Flopian]] that is blue in color.

==History==
Misty can be found in [[Flopside]] on the first floor. She is very nice and polite and is often seen asking her grandmother, [[Ruby]], how she lived for so long. Mitsy's [[Flipside]] counterpart is [[Betsy]].
