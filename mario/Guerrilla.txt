'''Guerrillas''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are only encountered in the [[Forest Maze]]. Guerrillas are weaker and earlier versions of [[Chained Kong]]s, also enemies from the same game, even though they both bear a very similar appearance.

==Battle==
In battle, Guerrillas can only attack physically but their attack power is extremely high.

===Strategy===
Guerrillas are weak to Fire so Mario should use his [[Super Flame]] move to injure them. Double teaming the Guerrillas are also a good idea.

==Trivia==
*Like the Chained Kongs, Guerrillas resemble [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], with their [[Psychopath]] thought even suggesting this as it says not to confuse them with him.
*Their name is a pun of "gorilla," a type of primate.
