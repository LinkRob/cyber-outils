The '''Ancient Tires''' are a type of [[tire]] featured in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', in which they were introduced in the version 1.6.0 update. They are associated with the [[kart]] body [[Master Cycle Zero]] and the [[glider]] [[Paraglider (The Legend of Zelda)|Paraglider]].
