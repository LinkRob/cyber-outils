'''Water Skipping'''<ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!'' Player's Guide, page 6.</ref> is an ability exclusively performed by [[Kiddy Kong]] in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' This move allows him to bounce on [[Water|water]], which is effective for reaching far or higher areas that the [[Kong]]s cannot reach themselves. This ability is required to reach a [[Bonus Level]] in [[Tidal Trouble]] and [[Riverside Race]]. Water Skip can be performed by performing a [[Roll Attack|roll]] onto a body of water and pressing {{button|SNES|B}} ({{button|GBA|A}} in the [[reissue|Game Boy Advance version]]) right before Kiddy touches the water surface. Kiddy can bounce twice in a row, the second bounce always being higher.

The move returns in ''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]'', where [[Donkey Kong]] and a partner can do so by [[Kong Roll]]ing  onto a body of water; the bounce height is more consistent in this game. In the [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze (Nintendo Switch)|Nintendo Switch port]], [[Funky Kong]] can do this by himself, though he surfs using his surfboard instead.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n_B2cS0wyWU&t=9s</ref>
