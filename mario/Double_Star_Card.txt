The '''Double Star Card''' is an recurring item in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Description==
The Double Star Card doubles the number of [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] that a character can obtain at once, provided that they have enough coins to purchase both.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party: Star Rush''===
The Double Star Card appears in [[Balloon Bash]] mode, where a character can obtain it by either landing on a [[? Block]] if they are in a lower position or by purchasing them for 5 [[coin]]s at a [[Shy Guy Shop]]. A Double Star Card doubles the number of [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] that can be earned from landing on a [[Star Balloon]], provided the character has enough coins. The Double Star Card is stackable, so characters can earn up to 12 Stars for 120 coins if used at a triple Star Balloon.

===''Super Mario Party'' / ''Mario Party Superstars''===
In both games, if the character reaches the [[Star Space]] with a Double Star Card, then [[Toadette]] provides them with the option to either purchase one Star at the usual price or to obtain double the number for double the price, like in ''Mario Party: Star Rush''.
