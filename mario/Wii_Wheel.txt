The '''Wii Wheel''' is an accessory for the [[Wii]] designed for ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. It was released standalone and bundled with certain copies of the game. The Wii Wheel is used so that players can controller their racer as if they were using a steering wheel. A single Wii Wheel is sold with the game, and additional Wii Wheels must be purchases separately.
