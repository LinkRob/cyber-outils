'''Brianne Sidonie Desaulniers''', better known as '''Brie Larson''' (born October 1, 1989) is an American actress who is the voice of [[Rosalina (Illumination)|Rosalina]] in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''.

== Trivia ==

* [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'']] marks Larson's debut voice acting in an animated movie.
* Larson is a huge [[Nintendo]] fan and has previously described her love for the ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' franchise.
* Larson once kicked her first boyfriend out for accusing her of taking ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' too seriously.
* She previously worked with [[Chris Pratt]] (voice of [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]]) in ''Avengers: Endgame''.
