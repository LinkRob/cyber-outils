A '''Mad Green Paratroopa''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mad Green Paratroopas are a variant of [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, the green type in particular, but have bright red skin, an angry face, and smoke coming out of their ears, indicating that they are imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
Mad Green Paratroopas fly around in the air, and whenever Peach approaches a Mad Green Paratroopa, it tries to attack by quickly flying at her.

== In ''Super Princess Peach'' ==
Mad Paratroopas first appear in the [[level]] [[Ladida Plains 1-9]], and make a reappearance in several other levels. In the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]], their name is spelled '''Mad G. P-Troopa'''. A Mad Green Paratroopa can be defeated from any attack.
