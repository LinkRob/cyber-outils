|textcolor = ffffff|first=''[[Paper Mario]]'' (2000)}}'''Tattle''' is a move used by both [[Goombario]] and [[Goombella]] in the games ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Usage==

===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
In this game, Goombario simply uses Tattle to give information on an enemy or boss. This move also allows the player to see the enemy or boss' current [[Heart Point|HP]] similar to the [[Peekaboo]] badge. Outside of battle, the move is also used to give information on the places he and [[Mario]] have visited. He can also give information on characters and objects that requires Mario to be standing right beside them.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
In this game, Tattle has become a little more complex to use. Tattle still uses 0 [[FP]], but the player must press the A Button once the cursor lands on the enemy or boss. Goombella then gives information on the foe and then foe's HP is revealed. Much like the previous game, the move is also used to give information on the locations, people, and objects.

==Trivia==
*[[Tippi]] (and later [[Tiptron]]) both use a techique similar to Tattle in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
