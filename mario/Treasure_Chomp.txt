'''Treasure Chomps''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are living red [[treasure chest]]s based on {{w|Mimic (Dungeons & Dragons)|mimics}}. Treasure Chomps have the same base appearance as a regular treasure chest in the game but with added black, beady eyes and and mouth where it is opened.

===Behavior===
At first, a Treasure Chomp appears the same as a treasure chest, but if [[Wario]] tries either use a [[Dash Attack]] or [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] on it, the Treasure Chest bites at him.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Treasure Chomps are only encountered in [[Boogie Mansion]]. Wario can only defeat a Treasure Chomp by feeding it a [[bomb]], destroying it from within and allowing Wario to obtain its treasure.
