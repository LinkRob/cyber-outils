'''Boogie Mansion''' is the third secret level of [[Quiver Cliffs]] in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''. It is both the final secret level as last one to feature platforming elements. Boogie Mansion can be unlocked by obtaining the [[Secret Map]] of [[Bad Manor]].

== Trivia ==

* Although you have to rescue a Pink Merfle in this level, but when you open the cage, a Black Merfle appears instead.
* This is the only level where you use a bomb to open the Treasure Chomp.
