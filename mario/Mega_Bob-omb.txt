'''Mega Bob-ombs''' are a larger more powerful variety of the [[Bob-omb]] appearing in several games. Mega Bob-ombs typically behave similarly to regular sized Bob-ombs with their only prominent difference being their size. Additionally, due to their larger size, their explosions are also heaps larger capable of causing heaps more destruction than a regular Bob-omb. Due to their size, Mega Bob-ombs can come in mobile forms or rolling forms and can typically be defeated in the same ways as a regular Bob-omb. Mega Bob-ombs have also made several prominent appearances in the ''[[Mario Sports (series)|Mario Sports]]'' series.

==History==
===''Super Mario Strikers''===
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Walks.png|thumb|left|The Mega Bob-omb near a Blue Birdo.]]
[[Image:Wario Mega Bob-omb.gif|thumb|right|[[Wario]] with a Mega Bob-omb.]]
Mega Bob-ombs appear in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''. In this game, they appear as items that can be obtained by the players, Mega Bob-ombs function similarly to the standard [[Bob-omb]]s with the primary exception being that upon usage, only one Mega Bob-omb rains down (unlike the regular Bob-ombs where multiple rain down). A Mega Bob-omb's explosion radius is larger than that of a normal Bob-omb making it more useful in slipping up any rivals however, the Mega Bob-omb can also affect a player's teammates as well so be careful not to slip up any teammates by accident. When [[Wario]] loses, he also pulls out a Mega Bob-omb which proceeds to explode on him.
{{br}}
===''Mario Strikers Charged''===
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Fall.png|thumb|right|A Mega Bob-omb falls onto the field.]]
Mega Bob-ombs return in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]'' with the same overall functions and roles as in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''. In this game, they are once again obtainable items and just as before, unlike the normal-sized [[Bob-omb]]s where multiple can rain down, only one Mega Bob-omb rains down and it's explosion radius is much bigger and can knock over any players within the radius, just as before, the Mega Bob-ombs can knock over teammates so proceed with caution when placing it.
{{br}}
===''Mario Sports Mix''===
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Cannons.png|thumb|left|A Mega Bob-omb being shot out of the Cannons with some smaller ones.]]
Mega Bob-ombs appear again in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''. In this game, they can be seen in the minigame [[Bob-omb Dodge]]. During the minigame, Mega Bob-ombs are shot out of the cannons around the sides of the arena alongside dozens of normal-sized [[Bob-omb]]s however the Mega Bob-ombs are far more dangerous than the normal-sized Bob-ombs and can cause players to take up to 25 points of damage.
{{br}}
===''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''===
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Chase.png|thumb|left|The Elite Trio chases Mario with a Mega Bob-omb.]]
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Castle.png|thumb|right|Mario and Luigi sidestep a rolling Mega Bob-omb.]]
Mega Bob-ombs appear again in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. In this game, they can be seen during the battle with The [[Elite Trio]]. One of their attacks involves the trio chasing [[Mario]] with a Mega Bob-omb, Mario runs whilst avoiding the [[Goomba]]s charging at him from behind, if Mario is hit, by the Mega Bob-omb, he will take heavy damage however if he dodges the charging Goombas for long enough, the Mega Bob-omb will explode doing heavy damage to the pursuing Elite Trio. Mega Bob-ombs are later seen in [[Neo Bowser Castle]] as a hazard that [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must avoid while progressing through the castle, the Mega Bob-ombs can be seen rolling along the tracks the plumbers are walking across. Typically, they must seek shelter in the various alcoves off the tracks whilst the Mega Bob-ombs pass. If the plumbers are hit by a Mega Bob-omb, it will explode sending them flying back to their starting location, when appearing in large numbers, the Mega Bob-ombs can be annoying to avoid as they are usually in narrow areas so the plumbers must time their movements to get by unscathed. Mega Bob-ombs are also involved in various puzzles throughout the castle where the plumbers must change the strength of the wind ducts to make the Mega Bob-ombs roll down the correct tracks to use them to open the path forward. After [[Antasma]]'s defeat, Mega Bob-ombs are also seen being rolled down by the [[Elite Trio]] as a final attempt to stop [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] from reaching the Castle's Tower where [[Bowser]] is, later, when the plumbers are escaping [[Neo Bowser Castle]], several Mega Bob-ombs can be seen bouncing along the tracks which they must avoid. 

The Mega Bob-ombs in [[Neo Bowser Castle]] have textured-on feet and their 3D appearance also sticks out more.
{{br}}
===''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''===
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Slides.png|thumb|left|Mega Bob-ombs sliding down the sandslide.]]
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Battle.png|thumb|right|Mega Bob-ombs being used as ammo.]]
Mega Bob-ombs are seen again in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. In this game, they can be seen in the minigame [[Safe by a Sandslide]] as obstacles that must be avoided as [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] are rescuing the [[Paper Toad]]s sliding down the sandslide, if they are hit by a Mega Bob-omb, they lose a life-point. If they lose all three, they lose the game. Mega Bob-ombs are also seen in the minigame [[Bombs Away (minigame)|Bombs Away]] as obstacles used by [[Bowser]] and his [[Koopalings]] which must be tossed back. Mega Bob-ombs are also smaller in this game than in the previous game.
{{br}}
===''Super Mario Maker / Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS / Super Mario Maker 2''===
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Classic.png|thumb|right|A Mega Bob-omb in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' style.]]
Mega Bob-ombs appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as it's [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|two]] [[Super Mario Maker 2|sequels]]. In these games, they can be made by giving a [[Super Mushroom]] to a [[Bob-omb]]. Mega Bob-ombs behave identically to standard Bob-ombs with the major difference being their size. Just like a regular Bob-omb, Mega Bob-ombs can be ignited with stomps or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] which can be used to destroy blocks and enemies. Their explosions are also larger making them capable of destroying more objects around them. Mega Bob-ombs can also be given wings to become [[Winged Mega Bob-omb]]s and can also be destroyed with the effects of a [[Starman]].
{{br}}
===''Paper Mario: Color Splash''===
[[Image:Paper Mega Bob-ombs Sleeping.png|thumb|left|Many Mega Bob-ombs Sleeping.]]
[[Image:Paper Mega Bob-ombs Ignited.png|thumb|right|The Mega Bob-ombs have been ignited.]]
Mega Bob-ombs also appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. In this game, they are seen all around [[Black Bowser's Castle]] as possible weapons and ammo. Many are seen around the [[Black Paint]] Cauldron sleeping. [[Mario]] must lure a normal-sized [[Bob-omb]] to the sleeping Mega Bob-ombs. Upon being ignited, the Mega Bob-ombs will create a large chain reaction exploding the entire room flooding it with Black Paint which slowly starts to consume the room as [[Mario]] and [[Huey]] make their escape.
{{br}}
===''Mario Tennis Aces''===
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Pirate Shy Guy.png|thumb|right|A Black Shy Guy with a Mega Bob-omb.]]
Mega Bob-ombs appear in ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]'' as weapons used by the [[Black Shy Guy]] boss during the final phase of the minigame [[Shy Guy's Train Tussle]]. When the Black Shy Guy's health is fully depleted, he will make one last ditch attempt at the players by tossing a Mega Bob-omb at them which they all must deflect back at him to win the battle, after one player deflects the Mega Bob-omb, the Black Shy Guy will deflect it to another player who must also deflect it, once all four players have deflected the Mega Bob-omb, the battle is won.
{{br}}
===Other Appearances===

===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[Image:Mega Bob-omb Surprise.png|thumb|left|A Mega Bob-omb surprises King Koopa.]]
Despite not appearing in the game ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', a Mega Bob-omb appears in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode [[Crimes R Us]]. In the episode, after the [[Koopalings]] attempt to rob the bank (the [[Mushroom Kingdom Treasury]]), they are blindsided by [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Crime Wave Clyde]] (who has betrayed [[Bowser]] and the [[Koopalings]]), when [[Bowser|King Koopa]] pulls up the moneybags and opens them, he instead sees the tied up [[Koopalings]] in one bag and a Mega Bob-omb in the other. The Mega Bob-omb proceeds to explode into fireworks as the [[Airship]] [[Bowser|King Koopa]] is piloting retreats.
