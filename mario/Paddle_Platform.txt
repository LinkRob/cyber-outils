The '''Paddle Platform''' consists of several platforms in a ring. It debuted in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

== Description ==
The Paddle Platform is made up of several platforms in a ring. Depending on which platform the player stands on, the platform will move left or right (if player stands on right platform, the Paddle Platform will move to the right, vice versa).
