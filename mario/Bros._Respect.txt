'''Bros. Respect''' is one of two unused [[badge]]s in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', the other being [[Bros. Life]]. Had it been used, Bros. Life would have granted the special effect [[BP-Auto Gain]], which restores two [[Bros. Point|BP]] of its user each turn, plus an increase in [[Power|POW]] by 72; as such, Bros. Respect is essentially a more effective variation of Bros. Life. It does not appear anywhere within the files of the remake ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.
