This pages lists '''glitches''' that can occur in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Glitches==
===Orb glitch===
In [[Yoshi's Island 1]], while riding [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], grab a [[Fire Flower]] get to the end of the level and stand on top of the [[Warp Pipe|green Warp Pipe]]. Then press X and A together while holding right. This causes [[Mario]] to spin jump off Yoshi and killing a [[Rex]], spawning a [[coin]], which is collected on Yoshi's tongue. After Mario collects it, a [[Chargin' Chuck]] should spawn on Yoshi's tongue. If Mario doesn't take damage, when Yoshi eats a Chargin' Chuck, he'll have a [[Magic Ball]] in the item box. However, if he does take damage and Yoshi eats a Chargin' Chuck, the game will crash.
