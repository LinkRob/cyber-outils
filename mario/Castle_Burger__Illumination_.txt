'''Castle Burger''' is a location that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. Its a fast-food restaurant that Mario and Luigi pass by the place on their way to arrive at a [[BrKLnCouple|client's house]], with the former sliding down from the sign pole alike a [[Goal Pole]].
