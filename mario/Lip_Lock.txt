|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Lip Lock''' is a special move used by [[Madame Flurrie]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Flurrie can use this move once she reaches the [[Super Rank]] and it requires 3 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==History==
To use Lip Lock, the player must hold {{button|gcn|a}} until the red light shows. Flurrie will then kiss the enemy, draining some of their HP while piercing their defense, and adding it to her own HP. This move can be useful for restoring Flurrie's HP when other options to heal are not available (especially useful in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]).
