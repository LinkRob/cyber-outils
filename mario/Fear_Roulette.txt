|games     = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Fear Roulette''' is a special move that is used only by the [[Ding-A-Ling]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Fear Roulette is used, a red crystal appears on the screen.

==Effects==
With Fear Roulette, the crystal selects its victim and when it hits, it may cause [[Instant Death Attack|Instant Death]]. Wearing an item such as a Quartz Charm can cancel out this effect.

==Trivia==
*The red crystal used in the attack resembles the [[Elemental Crystals|Fire Crystal]] that is seen during the fight with [[Culex]].
