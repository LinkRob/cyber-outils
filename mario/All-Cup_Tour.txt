The '''All-Cup Tour''' is an unlockable cup in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.

==In ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!''==
The All-Cup Tour is the fifth cup, and it can be unlocked by winning first place in 150cc [[Special Cup]]. The All-Cup Tour differs from the other cups in that the racers have to race on every track from the four previous cups—[[Mushroom Cup]], [[Flower Cup]], [[Star Cup]], and [[Special Cup]]—rather than on a selection of four unique courses. This is reflected by All-Cup Tour's name as well as its logo, which consists of each of the other four cup symbols.

The game randomizes the order in which the courses are raced on, although it always begins with [[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Luigi Circuit]], the first course overall, and concludes with [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Rainbow Road]], the final course overall.

Completing All-Cup Tour in 150cc unlocks [[Mirror Mode]]. Beating the All-Cup Tour in Mirror Mode unlocks the [[Parade Kart]]. Completing All-Cup Tour in 50cc and 100cc does not unlock anything specific, but completing All-Cup Tour in all four engine classes is necessary for unlocking the special title and ending screens.
