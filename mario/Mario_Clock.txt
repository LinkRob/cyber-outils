'''''Mario Clock''''' is a clock application released as a [[DSiWare]] title for the [[Nintendo DSi]] in 2009, then for the [[Nintendo 3DS]]'s [[Nintendo eShop]] at launch in 2011. It features graphics and sound effects from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' like its calculator counterpart, ''[[Mario Calculator]]''. ''Mario Clock'' has an ''[[Animal Crossing]]''-themed counterpart titled ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing Clock|Animal Crossing Clock]]''.
