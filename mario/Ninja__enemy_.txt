A '''Ninja''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are warriors that work for the [[Smithy Gang]], and Ninjas are encountered in [[The Factory]].

==Battle==
Ninjas mainly attack by throwing knives at one of [[Mario]]'s party members. Although they have no special attacks, Ninjas make up for it with their high attack and many resistances. When a Ninja's HP gets low, they will perform a desperate attack that can do a lot of damage to a character.
