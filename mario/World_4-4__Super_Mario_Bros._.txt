'''World 4-4''' is the fourth level of [[World 4 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 4]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''.

== Layout ==
The player starts at the top of some stairs, followed by two small lava pits. Following this are two different paths, the top one being the correct one. In remakes, a chiming sound will be heard, as an indicator that the player has chosen the correct path. This top path consists of several small gaps, followed by a [[Fire-Bar]] further down.

Next up are three paths, with the bottom one being the correct one. This bottom path is straight path down, with the only enemy being another Fire-Bar. This path will immediately lead the player to the [[fake Bowser]].

== Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' ==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 17000.

=== Red Coin locations ===
* Red Coin #1 is located between two pillars from the top path in the first puzzle.
* Red Coin #2 is located over the pit of lava before the second puzzle. The player must perform a corner jump to collect it.
* Red Coin #3 is located at the beginning of the center path of the second puzzle.
* Red Coin #4 is located in the air before the boss area. Below it is a [[Hidden Block]].
* Red Coin #5 is located in front of the fake Bowser.

=== Yoshi Egg location ===
* The Yoshi Egg is found inside a Hidden Block left of the single block, before the second puzzle.
