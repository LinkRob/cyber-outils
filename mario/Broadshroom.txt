'''Broadshroom''' is a location mentioned in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== History ==
Broadshroom is mentioned by [[Eve]] while in the middle of her "Tell that person..." [[Trouble Center|trouble]]. Not much else is known about Broadshroom except that Eve used to have a lead role while living there before quitting and settling down in [[Twilight Town]]. [[Podley]] never mentions Broadshroom directly, but he does reveal that he and Eve used to be a famous actor and actress couple before he decided to go down a simpler path in his life.

== Trivia ==
*Broadshroom is most likely a reference to Broadway, a famous location in New York City known for it's actors.
