'''''WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase''''' (also known as '''''WarioWare: Do It Yourself - Showcase''''' in Oceania and Europe) is an add-on title for ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''. It is a [[WiiWare]] title released for the Wii Shop Channel in 2009 in Japan and in 2010 in North America, Europe, and Australia. With ''WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase'', players can use [[microgame]]s and other content from ''WarioWare: D.I.Y'', but it is also featured as a game of its own.
