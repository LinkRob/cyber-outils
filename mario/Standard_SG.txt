'''Standard SG''' is a [[kart]] used by [[Shy Guy]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. 

== Description ==
It is a white kart, with the emblem using a cartoon depiction of Shy Guy's mask. This kart and the Shy Guy are only available in [[DS Download Play]] by the racers that do not have the game cartridge. As such, it is impossible for the Standard SG to use a different emblem.

It shares all its stats with [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Standard YS]], so it excels in [[acceleration]] and [[handling]], whilst it has low [[drift]]. It is also a [[Weight Class|light]] kart, but its weight is average, along with [[Speed]] and [[Items (stat)|Items]].
