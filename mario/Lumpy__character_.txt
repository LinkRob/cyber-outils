'''Lumpy''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. He is a green [[Ratooey]] that can be seen near the docks of [[Rogueport]] after the events of Chapter 1.

==History==
During the game, Lumpy asks someone to invest in some coins to him so he could go do [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]] to find oil. Mario can pay up to 100 coins three times to Lumpy in a grand total of 300 coins. If Mario gives him 100 coins, Lumpy will pay Mario 300 coins later. If Mario pays him 200, he'll get 600 back. If he gives him 300 coins, he will get 999 coins back.
