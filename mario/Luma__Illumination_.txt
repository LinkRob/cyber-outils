'''Lumas''' are a species of [[Star (species)|stars]] that appear in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''.

==Voice==
*[[Virginia Dare Jelenic]] (Yellow Luma)
*[[Pepper Jelenic]] (Various and [[Baby Luma (Illumination)|Baby Luma]])

==Appearance==
Lumas are star-shaped creatures who come in many colors. In the second movie they have a mouth much like [[Lumalee (Illumination)|Lumalee]] does in the first, a notable difference with their video game counterparts. In comparaison with their games counterparts, their only differences are mouths presence and better 3D designs.
