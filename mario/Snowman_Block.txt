'''Snowman Blocks''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Wario Land}}. They first appear in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Description==
Snowman Blocks can only be broken by having [[Snowman Wario]] roll into them, typically from a slope, although the ability to break Snowman Blocks also depends on the size of Snowman Wario's snowball. Snowman Blocks appear in snow-themed and ice-themed [[level]]s.

Snowman Blocks have a different appearance in each of their appearances. In ''Wario Land 3'', Snowman Blocks are purple and have a picture of a snowman imprinted on them. In ''[[Wario Land 4]]'', they are blue blocks with a light-blue border, and instead depict a picture of a snowball. In ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]'', Snowman Blocks sport a metallic appearance and instead depict a snowflake.

==In {{series|Wario Land}}==
===''Wario Land 3''===
Snowman Blocks only appear in one [[level]], [[The Frigid Sea]]. There are darker purple Snowman Blocks that require Snowman Wario's snowball to be larger to break than the lighter purple Snowman Blocks.
