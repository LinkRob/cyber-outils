'''Buzz Bombers''' are enemies found within the [[Sonic the Hedgehog (franchise)|''Sonic the Hedgehog'' franchise]]. They are [[Badnik]]s made by [[Dr. Eggman]] that are based on {{wp|bee}}s, though they more closely resemble {{wp|wasp}}s due to their blue color and elongated bodies. In almost all appearances, they fly forward through the air, occasionally stopping to shoot bullets out of their stingers in a downward-diagonal pattern.

Buzz Bombers make one ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in the [[Tokyo Sky Flight]] minigame of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''. They use their original sprites and a variation of their typical attack pattern, pursuing the player while shooting in more angles than before, eventually flying away if not destroyed. The minigame also features yellow versions of Buzz Bomber that movies quicker and flees after making one shot. Both types are sometimes armed with [[w:c:sonic:Crawl|Crawl]]'s [[Bumper (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Bumper]], like many other Badniks in the minigame.
