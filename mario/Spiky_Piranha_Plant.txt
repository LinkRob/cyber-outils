'''Spiky Piranha Plants''' (also called '''Spiked Piranha Plants''' or '''Spiny Piranha Plants''') are a rare sub-species of [[Piranha Plant]] found in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', they appear to be related to both [[Prickly Piranha Plant]]s and [[Super Piranha Plant]]s. They have a green thorny stem that supports their red thorn-covered black head. When they see [[Mario]] they will slam their head to the ground trying to hurt Mario similar to a [[Prickly Piranha Plant]] however, Spiky Piranha Plants are far more dangerous as due to the red thorns, [[Mario]] cannot stomp on its head. [[Mario]] must use the means of various projectiles to bring down a Spiky Piranha Plant.

== In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ==
[[Image:Spiky Piranha Plant Attacks.png|thumb|left|The Spiky Piranha Plant gets ready to attack!]]
Spiky Piranha Plants are first seen in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. In this game, they are incredibly rare enemies as only one appears in the entire game. It is encountered in the [[Sky Station Galaxy]] and is seen guarding the [[Key]] which opens the barrier where the [[Power Star]] is being kept. When [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] approaches the Spiky Piranha Plant, it attacks by slamming down at the plumbers similar to the [[Prickly Piranha Plant]] however unlike the Prickly Piranha Plant, the Spiky Piranha Plant is adorned in red spikes making it hazardous to jump on, plus it is immune to [[Star Spin]]s making it immune to virtually all of the plumbers' primary attacks. To beat a Spiky Piranha Plant, the plumbers will need to whack a nearby [[Stretch Plant]] into it as it attacks.

== In ''Super Smash Bros Ultimate'' ==
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', [[Piranha Plant]] becomes a small Spiky Piranha Plant for its side smash attack. In [[Palutena's Temple]], Spiky Piranha Plants (just called '''Spiny Piranhas''') are mentioned briefly by Viridi during Palutena's Guidance dialogue for Piranha Plant.

==Trivia==
* While Spiky Piranha Plants are immune to most attacks, they can still be stunned by the [[Co-Star Luma]] which can make targeting it easier.
* Spiky Piranha Plants slightly resemble the [[Inky Piranha Plant]]s and [[Fire Piranha Plant]]s from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.
* Hacking the game shows that Spiky Piranha Plants can also be beaten with [[Shell]]s.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EU0LD1TUKdI 1:19 in shows that Spiky Piranha Plants are susceptible to Shell attacks.]</ref> This remains unused in the base game as Shells are never seen in Galaxies with Spiky Piranha Plants.
