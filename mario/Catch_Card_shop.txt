A '''Catch Card shop''' (also referred as '''card shop''' by [[Tippi]]) is a shop that owns [[Catch Card]]s. Catch Card Shops appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
[[Catch a Dream]] and [[Fondest Hopes]] are '''Catch Card shops'''. Customers can buy or sell [[Catch Card]]s within the shop.
