==Effects==
In the game, they replace the role of Eggs from the previous ''[[Paper Mario series|Paper Mario]]'' games. These items are very helpful as they are dropped by many different foes and can be purchased from the Itty Bits in [[Flopside]]. This item, ulike the 5 HP the [[Egg]] recovers, heals up 12 HP. Like all other healing items in this game, Big Eggs do heal poison as well.
