'''Broom Zoom''' is a single-player [[minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Layout==
The player's character—either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]] or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]—rides a broom in a starry night sky. 

==Gameplay== 
The objective is for the player's character to fly their broom through [[ring]]s to earn as many [[point]]s as possible within the [[time limit]]. Flying through rings in succession doubles the number of points earned from flying through the previous ring. There are several [[Brick Block]]s and some [[Amp]]s placed along the way, and crashing into one slows the character and causes them to lose their combo.

In the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player must earn at least 5,000 points to win.
