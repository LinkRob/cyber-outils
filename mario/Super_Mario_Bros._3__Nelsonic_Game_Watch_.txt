'''''Super Mario Bros. 3''''' is a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game released within the [[Nelsonic Game Watch]] line of wristwatches. It was released in 1990, and is directly based on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[Super Mario Bros. 3|same name]]. The game is alternatively titled '''''Super Mario 3''''', as shown on the bezel, or '''''Super Mario III''''' in the included instruction booklet.
