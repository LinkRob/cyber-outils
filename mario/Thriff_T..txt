'''Thriff T.''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Thriff T. is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who works alongside [[Plenn T.]] at the shop in [[Rogueport]]. He looks just like Plenn T., who is his older brother. He eventually moves to [[Keelhaul Key]] to start a [[Keelhaul Galleria|shop]] there.
