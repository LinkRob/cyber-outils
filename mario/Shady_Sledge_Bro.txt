[[File:Shady_Sledge_Bro.png|thumb]]The '''Shady Sledge Bro''' is a mini-boss found in the [[Dark Bloo Inn]] and Vortex Island in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. He is exactly the same as a regular sledge bro. except he has higher health.
