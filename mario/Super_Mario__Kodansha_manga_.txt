The '''''Super Mario Manga''''' (or '''''KC Mario''''') is a manga created by [[Kazuki Motomaya]], and published by Kodansha as a part of the [[wikipedia:Comic BomBom|Comic BomBom]] series, ''Deluxe BomBom'' series, and ''KC Deluxe''.

== Description ==
The ''Super Mario Manga'', or ''KC Mario'', is a manga created by Kazuki Motomaya, and published by Kodansha as a part of the [[Wikipedia:Comic_BomBom|Comic BomBom]] series, Deluxe BomBom series, and KC Deluxe. ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3|The Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' volume was part of the Comic BomBom series, all side chapters were part of the Deluxe BomBom series, and ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' and all volumes after it were published under the "KC Deluxe" title.
