}}'''Pink Coins''' are coins in ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' that give 2 [[Toad Rally|Toad Rally tickets]] when all 5 are collected in a row.

==Overview==
These coins is an easy collection item for players for starters in World Tour.

These coins are replaced with [[Purple Coin|Purple Coins]] once the player obtains all 5, and the pink coins can never be collected again.
