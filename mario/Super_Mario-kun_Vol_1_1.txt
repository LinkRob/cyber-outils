== Plot for "Save Princess Peach!! Start of a new Adventure!!" ==
{{Spoiler}}
[[Luigi (Super Mario-Kun)|Luigi]] witnesses [[Mario (Super Mario-Kun)|Mario]] on the water. Luigi attempted to rescue Mario until Mario showed his [[Frog Suit]] revealed that he bribed Luigi. A [[Goomba]] disguised as [[Princess Peach (Super Mario-Kun|Princess Peach]], but Mario already knew Goomba disguised as Peach. Grabbing [[coin]]s, Mario questioned Goomba about Peach. [[Bowser (Super Mario-Kun)|Bowser]] talked through his mechanized doll and told Mario that he kidnapped Peach to Mario's shock, Bowser intimidates Mario with his [[Koopa  Troop|army]]. Mario tried to destroy the doll and the doll self-destruct. Mario and Luigi ran and found injured [[Yoshi (Super Mario-Kun|Yoshi]] on the ground. Mario and Luigi cured Yoshi; Yoshi thanked the brothers and introduced itself. Yoshi claimed that a [[Bowser|giant monster turtle]] made a ruckus and took the [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s, Mario and Luigi joined forces with Yoshi.

Bowser's reinforcements of [[Super Koopa]]s arrived. Mario and Luigi burned the Super Koopas with fireballs. Mario finds a [[Cape Feather]] and transformed into [[Cape Mario]]. Mario toyed the Super Koopa; the hotheaded Super Koopa attacked Mario and Luigi, but the brothers fled. Mario and Luigi encountered a [[Dino-Torch]] and it burned Mario's cape. Before the Dino-Torch could breathe fire, Mario slapped the paper on its face. Mario and Luigi encounter a [[Chargin' Chuck]], but failed to defeat it. The Chargin' Chuck through Mario off the cliff. Yoshi grabbed onto Mario to save him, while they were falling, you swallowed a blue turtle to fly. After Mario and Yoshi defeated the Chargin' Chuck, Mario, Yoshi and Luigi continued their adventure.
