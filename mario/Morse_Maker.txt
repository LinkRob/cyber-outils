'''Morse Maker''' is a [[Gaddget]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained from [[Fly Guy (character)|Fly Guy]] by clearing the quest [[Hey, UFO!]].

==Description==
The Gaddget allows the player to input a series of letters, numbers, or symbols to then have it played back in {{w|morse code}}. By pressing {{button|gba|B}}, the player can remove either some letters or all of them and write a different morse code.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|pad}}: Select a letter, number, or symbol
*{{button|gba|A}}: Input a letter, number, or symbol
*{{button|gba|B}}: Undo last change
*{{button|gba|R}}: Manual Morse
*{{button|gba|L}}: Play Morse Message
