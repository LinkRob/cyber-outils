'''Cape Codswallop''' is a location existing in the ''Donkey Kong'' series.

==Description==
Cape Codswallop is a location appearing to take place on a peninsula with lakes surrounding it. There are also windmills existing around the area, where some levels may take place in.

==In the Games==
===''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]''===
Cape Codswallop first appears in ''Donkey Kong Land III''.

This area is the very first world in the game. It features simple areas for [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] and [[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] to navigate through. It is simply a replacement to [[Lake Orangatanga]] from ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. The boss in the area is [[Barbos]].
