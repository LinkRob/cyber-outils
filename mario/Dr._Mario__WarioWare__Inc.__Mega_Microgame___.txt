'''Dr. Mario''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''. It is one of [[9-Volt]]'s microgames, appearing under the [[Nintendo Classics]] folder.

==Gameplay==
The microgame re-enacts a scenario from the original ''[[Dr. Mario (game)|Dr. Mario]]'', and the player has to correctly place a [[capsule]] to clear the bottle of [[Virus]]es and win the microgame. If done quickly enough, the Virus in the magnifying glass disappears right before the microgame ends. If the capsule was incorrectly placed, the player loses the microgame, which itself acts as if the player got a [[Game Over]], with the magnified Virus laughing and [[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]] shrugging.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The capsule must be placed on top of the Viruses.
*'''Level 2:''' The capsule must be placed between the Viruses horizontally.
*'''Level 3:''' The capsule must be placed between the Viruses vertically.
