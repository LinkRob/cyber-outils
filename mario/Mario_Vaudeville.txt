{{Nihongo|'''Mario Vaudeville'''|マリンえんげいじょう<ref>{{cite web|url=https://youtu.be/ZQ3zs226sck|title=一人用マリオパーティアドバンスpart5<nowiki>[</nowiki>字幕<nowiki>]</nowiki>|work=YouTube|language=Japanese|date=2017-11-19|accessdate=2023-04-19}}</ref>|Marin engei-ba|Marine Theater}} is a location which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is found within the [[Seaside Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. Despite its name, Mario Vaudeville is not indicated to be owned by Mario.

==In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''==
A particular [[Dolphin (character)|Dolphin]] often tries to perform stand-up comedy here, but has little success at doing so. If the player's character (either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], or [[Princess Peach]]) is willing to help Dolphin, this starts the quest [[Comedy Bomb]]. In it, the player's character has to laugh during the punchlines and appropriate moments, as doing so attracts other [[Dolphin]]s over to Mario Vaudeville to hear the Dolphin character's stand-up comedy. As a reward for clearing the quest, the player receives the [[Snow Globe (Gaddget)|Snow Globe]] [[Gaddget]].
