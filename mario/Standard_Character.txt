A '''Standard Character''' (also '''Starter character''') is a term used to classify characters in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]],  ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party series]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros. series]]''. Standard characters are available at the start of the game. 

Listed below are the Starter Characters. (Note: There are no [[Unlockable Character]]s in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' and are all Standard)
