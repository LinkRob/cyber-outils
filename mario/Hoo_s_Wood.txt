'''Hoo's Wood''' is the second world in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It is a forest-themed world with many tall trees and high canopies, the world itself consists of rivers and grassy walkways, several new enemies are also introduced in this world as well, Hoo's Wood is also home to [[Hoo]], a large Owl. [[Hoo]] is also the ruler, protector, and boss of this world.
