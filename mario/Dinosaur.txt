'''Dinosaurs''' are prehistoric creatures which lived millions of years ago in the real world. Representations of both real-world dinosaurs, such as [[T-Rex]]es, and in-universe dinosaurs, such as [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]], have sometimes appeared in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. Dinosaurs first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', namely those specific to the ''Mario'' franchise, while realistic dinosaurs have appeared in early as ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''.

For ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' games, sauropod-based Dinosaurs make an appearance in certain [[Dino Domain]] courses in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]''. They are peaceful although serve as an obstruction to racers when they walk along the road. The world's boss [[Tricky the Triceratops|Tricky]] is a dinosaur himself, specifically a triceratops.
