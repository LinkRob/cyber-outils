They appear for the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' game styles.

== Description ==
Rotten Mushrooms appear into four different variants correlated to the [[1-Up Mushroom]]s. They replace the [[1-Up Mushroom]]s when in the Ground Night Theme. The Rotten Mushroom stalks the player, and will jump a few block if adjacent to a wall. The Rotten Mushroom negatively affects the player, different to the Super Mushroom. When it touches the player, he/she will become small or die.<ref name="SMM2"/>

The Rotten Mushroom will run from the player after they use the [[Starman|Super Star]].<ref name="SMM2"/>
