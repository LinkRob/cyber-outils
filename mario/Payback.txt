==History==
When someone has Payback in effect, attacks will be countered. This mainly includes direct attacks. There are a few ways one can get the Payback status. The best way is to use a [[Spite Pouch]]. [[Admiral Bobbery]]'s [[Hold Fast]] special move gives him the Payback status as well. [[Sir Grodus]] is a notable boss that can give himself the Payback status while [[Poison Puff]]s are some of the only enemies in the game that can give themselves Payback.
