The '''Voltage-Proof Coil''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Voltage-Proof Coil is a coil that is yellow in color and has a little diamond on the top of it.

==History==
The Voltage-Proof Coil is a rather special item that can be found in [[Carpaccio's Lab]]. With it, [[Wario]] will not take damage from the electrical currents in the level anymore allowing him to access new areas. The Voltage-Proof Coil can be found in a room with numbered switches that requires [[Arty Wario]] to draw blocks on top of them in order to reveal the chest with the coil inside.
