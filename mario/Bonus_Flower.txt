A '''Bonus Flower''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Bonus Flowers can only be found after defeating enemies in battle. After defeating a certain enemy, a type of Bonus Flower may emerge. There are five different types of Bonus Flowers:

*'''HP Max''': This Bonus Flower fully restores the [[HP]] of a single character.
*'''Attack Up''': This Bonus Flower increases the attack power of a single character by 50% for the remainder of the battle.
*'''Defense Up''': This Bonus Flower increases the defense of a single character by 50% for the remainder of the battle.
*'''Lucky''': This special Bonus Flower allows the player to play a minigame. Here, the player has a chance to double their [[coin]]s or [[EXP]]. If they get it correct, their coins or EXP is doubled. If they get it wrong, then they gain no points or EXP from that battle.
