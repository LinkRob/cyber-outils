'''Cheepers Keepers''', titled '''Hook, Line and Cheep Cheep''' in the British English version, is a general [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place on a dock in front of a beach, which has a field consisting of short grass and several palm trees behind it.

==Opening==
After the characters are first shown, something catches each one's fishing hook, revealed to be a [[Cheep Cheep]] as each character reels them out before throwing their fishing rod hook back into the water.

==Gameplay==
The minigame is divided into three rounds, and in each one, players must press a certain button as many times as they can within five seconds for their character to catch the most Cheep Cheeps. Whoever has reeled out the most Cheep Cheeps by the end is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|3ds|A}} {{button|3ds|B}} {{button|3ds|Y}} {{button|3ds|X}}: Catch Cheep Cheep

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''' (American English): ''Press the selected button as fast as you can to reel in Cheep Cheeps.''
*'''Rules''' (British English): ''Press the displayed buttons as many times as you can before time runs out. The more you press it, the more Cheep Cheeps you'll catch!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on the saying "Finders Keepers," with the "Cheep" prefix referring to a Cheep Cheep.
*The British English name "Hook, Line and Cheep Cheep" is a pun on the phrase "Hook, line, and sinker," which was previously used as a pun for the ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' minigame [[Hand, Line and Sinker]].
