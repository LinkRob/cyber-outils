A '''Frozen Coin''' is a type of object featured in the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
A Frozen Coin is a [[coin]] encased within an [[Ice Block]]. The coin within the Ice Block can only be collected by melting the Ice Block itself. A Frozen Coin can generally be melted using [[Fire Mario]]'s [[fireball]]s, but it can be melted by fire in general.
