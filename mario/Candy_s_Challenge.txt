'''Candy's Challenge''' is a location ran by [[Candy Kong|Candy]], exclusive to the [[Game Boy Color]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

In this area, [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] had to do six challenges in each world to receive a [[Banana Coin]] if they complete it. Once they finish the challenge, they are unable to do it again.
