'''Snowboarding Shy Guys''' are a variant of [[Shy Guy]]s encountered in a few snowy courses in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

==Description==
Snowboarding Shy Guys snowboard across the half-pipe area of a course, performing tricks as they go up a ramp. If a racer drives into one a Snowboarding Shy Guy, they briefly spin out. A Snowboarding Shy Guy can be taken out from an offensive item or by touching it while invincible.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart Wii''===
[[File:Shy Guy Snowboarder MK Wii screenshot.png|thumb|left|150px|A Shy Guy Snowboarder in ''Mario Kart Wii'']]
Here, they are named '''Shy Guy Snowboarders''' in the Prima guide. They are encountered in the last portion of [[DK Summit]], just before the [[finish line]].

===''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
Shy Guy Snowboarders can be seen at [[Mount Wario]], in the background of the slalom and mogul portions in the final section. They are not encountered as obstacles, unlike DK Summit. In addition, a [[stamp]] of a Shy Guy Snowboarder can be obtained in the original ''Mario Kart 8'' by winning a [[Time Trial]] race against Mount Wario's [[Staff Ghost]].

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
Since this game, they have been renamed to Snowboarding Shy Guys. They return in DK Summit, having the same role as before.

===''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' - [[Booster Course Pass]]===
Just as in ''Mario Kart Tour'', Snowboarding Shy Guys are encountered in DK Summit, retaining their role once more.
