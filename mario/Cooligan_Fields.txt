'''Cooligan Fields''', or '''Frosted Glacier-2''', is the second level of [[Frosted Glacier]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. It is opened via completion of [[Spinning Star Sky]], and its completion unlocks a [[Cooligan]] enemy course and [[Freezing Rain Tower]].  

==Layout==
The level begins with a section with icy ground. A [[? Block]] containing a power-up is found, along with a [[Cooligan]]. More Cooligans are found along with a [[Coin Block]]. A structure of [[Brick Block]]s is found, as well as a ? Block and more Cooligans. A snowy section of the ground with Cooligans follows, along with another icy section with a [[Warp Pipe]] that spawns Cooligans. Multiple long groups of Cooligans are found as the player progresses. Another Warp Pipe that spawns Cooligans and a [[Hidden Block]] with a [[1-Up Mushroom]] are found with the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. Another Warp Pipe spawning Cooligans is found, along with a [[Red Ring]] and another Hidden Block with a 1-Up Mushroom. Large blocks of act as platforms above a pit, followed by more Warp Pipes that spawns Cooligans. Brick Blocks, ? Blocks, and coins are found in between two Warp Pipes, and are located near more Cooligans and ice blocks. Ice blocks that move up and down are found above a pit. A section of multiple Cooligans is found, leading to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In plain sight, the first Star Coin is located under a row of [[Brick Block]]s.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is found between two green [[Warp Pipe]]s, in a set of [[coin]]s. The player must first break the [[Brick Block]]s barricading the area in order to reach it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is found above a Brick Block after a series of coins. The player can reach the platforms by bouncing off of a Cooligan or another character.

==''Monopoly Gamer''==
Cooligan Fields is the eighth Property on the game board in ''[[Monopoly Gamer]]''. It is one of two properties in the Orange Color Set (along with [[Frosted Glacier]]).
