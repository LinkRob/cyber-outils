'''Prince Bully Blockade is Back''' is the second blockade level in [[World Castle]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== Layout ==
This level is a rematch fight again [[Prince Bully]]. It is the same as [[Prince Bully Blockade]], except that Prince Bully will shoot out more fireballs than last time.

==Green Star==
*'''Green Star 1''': Defeat Prince Bully to get the Star.
