'''Bowser Castle Court''' is an unlockable tennis court in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Bowser Castle Court takes place in [[Bowser's Castle]], as the name suggests. It takes place on a platform suspended over a pool of [[lava]] by large chains, and the flooring has a stone texture. In standard matches only, there is a wide red carpet depicting [[Bowser]]'s face on each side. [[Mecha-Bowser]] watches over the court, while [[Mechakoopa]]s and [[Magikoopa]]s spectate.

In the gimmick version of the court, the court tips when players move around, making it difficult for them to keep their footing and hit the ball.

==In ''Mario Power Tennis''==
Bowser Castle Court can be unlocked by winning any Tournament in the Singles Tournament. It is also where the minigame [[Mecha-Bowser Mayhem]] takes place.

If the player waits long enough during the introduction of the stage, the version of [[Bowser's Keep]] from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' is visible beyond the walls early in the intro.
