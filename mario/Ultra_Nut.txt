'''Ultra Nuts''' are a type of [[nut]] and an [[Heart Point|HP]]-restoring item in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. In each of their appearances, Ultra Nuts use the same sprite as other nuts.

==In the ''Mario & Luigi'' series==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', using an Ultra Nut restores 100 HP of both [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. Ultra Nuts can be bought at the [[Castle Town Item Shop]] for 140 [[coin]]s each. In the Japanese version, Ultra Nuts instead restore 75 HP, and the [[reissue|remake]] lowers this even further to 65 HP.
