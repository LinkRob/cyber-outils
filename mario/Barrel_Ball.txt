'''Barrel Ball''' is the [[Star Pitch]] of [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Upon using Barrel Ball, Donkey Kong throws a [[barrel]] instead of a ball. If the batter hits the barrel, it breaks, and the ball is hit. The batter has to hit the barrel while their swing is charged, otherwise the baseball only a short distance, making it easy for fielders to get them out.
