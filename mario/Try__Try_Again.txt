'''Try, Try Again''' is the main battle theme from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It is composed with synths, drums, horns, flutes, strings, and bells. The track runs at around 140 to 150 beats per minute and lasts for just under 2 minutes with 62 bars, with an 8-bar intro with the first two bars having 100 to 110 beats per minute. Being an instrumental song, it has no singer of its own. Its melody comes partially from the title theme, [[Your Dream Adventure]].

== Victory in the Dream World ==
In the Dream World, the song has been shortened to 48 bars with a duration of 80 seconds, with a tempo of 144 beats per minute, and has the techno theme. At bars 13 to 20, the chord progression is changed, and at bars 32 and 40, the F-minor chord is removed, with the F-major chord extending to that bar to fill the gap. The first four bars have been removed. Both versions have C major as their theme.
