|damage = 0}}'''Mango Juice''' is an item and recipe that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Drinking a Mango Juice restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]] making it an effective recovery item. Mango Juices can be made by giving [[Saffron]] a [[Keel Mango]]. Keel Mango's can be purchased at [[Itty Bits]] in [[Downtown of Crag]].
