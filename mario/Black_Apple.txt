==Effects==
It restores one HP and cures the poison ailment. A Black Apple can be found by hammering a tree in the Overthere Stair. The apple is also a cure for Princess Peach after she eats the Golden Apple and is plunged into a hundred-year sleep (there are other fruits that awaken her, but only temporarily and with humorous side effects such as changing her into an actual peach.)

The Black Apple is also mentioned to taste bad, and Peach was irritated at her friends when they forced her to eat it. The Black Apple is also referred to as "taboo fruit".
