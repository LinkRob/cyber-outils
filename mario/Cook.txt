'''Cook''' is a mole-based enemy in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Cooks have a light pink body with round, white hands, dark pink blush on its cheeks, and brown feet. Cook wears some chef clothing, including a white chef's hat and a white apron.

===Behavior===
Cooks carry a piece of [[cake]] on a fork. They do not harm Wario, but they throw a cake that, if eaten by Wario, turns him into [[Fat Wario]].

===Variants===
They have a variant named [[Doughnuteer]]s, which appear in ''[[Wario Land 3]]'', although Cooks themselves do not make an appearance.
