'''Isthmus Be the Way''' is a [[Co-op Minigame|Co-op]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. It is a pun of the phrase, "this must be the way" and "isthmus". If one starts to wave the Joy-con before the bomb is passed to the player, an easter egg will appear, that character will also wave.

== Opening ==
A Toad waves while the camera pans to the starting player.

== Gameplay ==
Each player is standing on the platform waiting for the player to pass the bomb until they reach the Toad. If the player falls off, the players sulk while the player that fell is placed back to the place they last fell.

== Ending ==
The Toad puts the bomb in the cannon which shoots out the bomb and all fireworks explode in the sky.
==Trivia==
*If the party was in Africa's Serengeti Park, Toad and Toadette (Who are the hosts of Super Mario Party) will want this minigame cancelled, because the fireworks at the end will make all the animals (Including: antelopes, lions, zebras, giraffes, leopards, warthogs, rhinos, and even elephants) feel threatened and stressed.
