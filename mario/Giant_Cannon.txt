The '''Giant Cannon''' is a large cannon that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The '''Giant Cannon''' is located in [[Fahr Outpost]] where [[Mario]] and company wanted to use it to get to [[The Moon]]. However, the [[Fahr Outpost Mayor|mayor]] claimed there were no such cannon until [[Admiral Bobbery]] yells at him saying he has seen the cannon himself before. Even so, the mayor refuses to let the heroes use the Giant Cannon until get they permission form [[Goldbob]] and [[General White]]. Once they do, an army of [[Bob-omb]]s load into the Giant Cannon and blast Mario and the others to The Moon.
