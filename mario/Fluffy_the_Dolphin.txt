'''Fluffy the Dolpin''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
Fluffy the Dolphin is a rather young Dolphinian that can only be found during the fight with [[Stuffy the 64th]] in [[Blowhole Castle]]. According to [[Wario]], Fluffy the Dolphin is the next in line to the throne which possibly makes it related to Stuffy the 64th. In battle, Fluffly the Dolphin will often spawn in the air to shoot energy balls at Wario. Although they can be taken out with any attack, it is recommenced to use [[Cosmic Wario]]'s lasers to shoot down Fluffy the Dolphin from a distance.
