The '''Palace Guard Captain''' is a Green Toad, and the leader of the other [[Palace Guards (Illumination)|Palace Guards]] of the Mushroom Kingdom in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. After hearing that Mario is trespassing the castle, just by the time he reached to Peach, he with his [[Palace Guard Liutenant|liutenant]], his [[Palace Guard Ensign|ensign]], this [[Palace Guard Corporal|corporal]], and his [[Palace Guard Sergeant|sergeant]] tackled, and tried to arrest him, but Peach orders them to release him, before the captain stomped on Mario. And then she recognized and fell in love with him as another human, and not a monster.
