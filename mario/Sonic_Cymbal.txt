'''Sonic Cymbal''' is a pair of cymbals used as a weapon by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is obtained from a certain [[Treasure chest]] within [[Bowser's Keep]]. Equipping the Sonic Cymbal to Mallow increases his [[Attack]] rating by 70.
