'''Electro Pop''' is a recurring [[recipe]] item in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:ElectroPopPM.png|thumb|left]]
Electro Pops can be made by giving [[Tayce T.]] a [[Volt Shroom]] and a [[Cake Mix]]. Electro Pops replenishes 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and temporarily [[Electrified|electrifies]] [[Mario]].

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Electro_Pop_TTYD.png|thumb|left]]
Electro Pops serve the exact same role, but it is instead colored black. They can still be made from combining a Volt Shroom and a Cake Mix (which can be purchased at the [[Pianta Parlor]]). They still restore 15 HP and electrifies the user.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
Unlike the past two games, Electro Pops are no longer made from combining a Volt Shroom and a Cake Mix, and a single [[Thunder Rage]] is the only ingredient required to make an Electro Pop. However, this item no longer restores the user's HP but now just electrifies them for a set amount of time. Its icon is nearly identical to the one in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''.
