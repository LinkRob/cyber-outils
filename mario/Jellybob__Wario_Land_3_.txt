'''Jellybobs''' are floating white jellyfish enemies encountered in ''[[Wario Land 3]]'' and an opponent featured in ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]''. In the former game, they are named '''Pneumos''' and possess a horn-like nose.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
If a Pneumo stings Wario, he becomes [[Puffy Wario]]. Jellybobs act the same as the [[Bee (Wario Land II)|Bees]] in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.
