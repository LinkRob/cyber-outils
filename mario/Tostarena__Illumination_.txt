'''Tostarena''', also known as the ''Gateway to the stark beauty of the desert.'' is a location in the [[Sand Kingdom (Illumination)|Sand Kingdom]] in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Movie series]].

==History==
When [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] arrived in the town, they asked its inhabitants where a blocked [[Mysterious Pipe (Illumination)|Warp Pipe]] is, and they answered that its in the Inverted Pyramid.
