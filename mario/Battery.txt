'''Battery''' is an invention used to power electronics. Two types of batteries appear the role-playing games:

*[[Battery (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Battery]], an item in the [[Mysterious Mine Carts]] minigame of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''
*[[Battery (Paper Mario series)|Battery]], an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]], originally named "D-Cell Battery."
