The '''Long Jump''' is one of [[Mario]]'s moves, used especially in the 3D platforming games of the {{series|Super Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

== Description ==
Mario and [[Luigi]] can use it to clear long gaps to reach far away platforms, while still staying close to the ground for obtaining [[coin]]s. To use it, Mario must run forward and, while he's running, the player must press 'Z' and then the 'A' button. Mario will then be able to propel himself forward. He usually shouts, "Yahoo!' when doing the Long Jump.

This move was eliminated from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', because [[F.L.U.D.D.]]'s [[Hover Nozzle]] will carry Mario across the gaps he'd normally have to jump across.

[[Donkey Kong]] can also use a Long Jump in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

Mario is able to do the Long Jump once more in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]], and [[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.
