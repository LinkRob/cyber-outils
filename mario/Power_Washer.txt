'''Power Washer''' is a 1-vs-3 minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Gameplay==
The team of three are standing on a dish, while the solo character operates a water sprayer to try and spray them off. Any team member sprayed off the dish gets eliminated from the minigame. A second water sprayer is activated shortly before time runs out. If at least one team member is on the dish when the 30-second [[time limit]] expires, the team wins, but if all three characters are sprayed off the dish, the solo character wins.

==Controls==
*[[Stylus]]: Spin sprayers (solo)
*D-Pad: Move (team)
*{{button|ds|A}}: [[Jump]] (team)
