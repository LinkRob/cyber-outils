The '''Blue Mushroom House''' is a house in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', only obtainable after beating the [[World 8-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8-Castle]]. It will replace the first 1UP house in World One. It is used to customize the touch screen on your [[Nintendo DS|DS]] while playing in that particular file. You may pay 20 [[Star Coin]]s for one skin for the bottom screen, and you may pay for as many of the skins as you want.
