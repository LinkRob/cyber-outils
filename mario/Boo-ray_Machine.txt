The '''Boo-ray Machine''' is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Description==
The Boo-ray Machine is a machine that [[Yikk]] built before his death. It resembles a [[Boo]] and has a light-blue LED screen on the front that gives Boo-like facial expressions. The Boo-ray Machine projects a light that goes through one's body and displays a view of their x-ray against the wall. In the original game, the Boo-ray Machine can be activated by having [[Bowser]] [[punch]] a red button on the back of the machine, but in the remake, the machine is instead activated by standing on a platform behind it.

The machine's name is a pun on "x-ray machine," but with [[Boo]] substituting for the "X" in the first word.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
When Bowser first finds the Boo-ray Machine, Yikk specifically explains to him that the light makes the insides of one's body visible, and that it shows [[Boo]] transparency waves, giving off light and other special properties.

The Boo-ray Machine is necessary for [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to explore further into the [[Energy Hold]]. If Bowser keeps the Boo-ray Machine activated, Mario and Luigi can possibly find hidden blocks somewhere, but if Bowser deactivates it, several Boo-like blocks may appear as platforms.
