|time=500}}'''{{world|flower|A}}''' is a level in [[World Flower (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Flower]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' that is unlocked by locating the secret exit in [[World Flower-1 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Flower-1]].
==Layout==
The level takes place underground in an icy area. Mario begins on an unslippery platform above the ice. A [[Koopa Troopa]] can then be found on the ice, below two ? Blocks and a Brick Block, one of which contains a [[Super Leaf]]. After several small gaps, several [[Buzzy Beetle]]s can be found on the ceiling. What follows is a large abyss that an ice platform crosses over. Several Buzzy Beetles and Koopa Troopas will fall from smaller ice platforms during the ride. [[Spike Top]]s will then begin to appear. At the end of the ride is the checkpoint. Another Super Leaf can be found in a ? Block after that. Several gaps from platform to platform appear next which have Spike Tops and Buzzy Beetles on them. In between the gaps are rectangular icy [[Donut Block]]s. Further on, several one-way gates appear, followed by another moving ice platform. Koopa Troopas, Buzzy Beetles and Spike Tops can be found along the ride. Several rectangular icy Donut Blocks are also in place during the ride. At some points, these platformed will have to be ducked under or jumped over. On the platform below, the exit Warp Pipe can be found. Back outside, another moving ice platform spans an abyss. A single square ice platform can be found during the ride before the [[Goal Pole]].

Completing this level will unlock [[World Flower-Cannon]] and a [[Toad House|Green Toad House]].
