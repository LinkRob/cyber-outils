'''Right Oar Left?''' is a 2-vs.-2 minigame from ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. The name is a pun of "right or left".

==Opening==
The screen divides into two to show each team. On each side, both teams paddle a [[Dorrie Boat]] through the tunnel, and the camera focuses behind them.

==Gameplay==
The two teams must compete to reach the end of the cave first. The players on each of their teams must work together steering their Dorrie Boat away from obstacles especially the flames. Steering the boat into one of the flames will cause the boat to be stopped for a short time. The team that reaches the end first wins.

==Ending==
[[File:MP4 Right Oar Left Ending.png|thumb|left|200px|[[Wario]] and [[Waluigi]] in the ending]]
The outside of the cave is shown, and the winning team rows toward the camera and celebrates. If the minigame ends in a draw, the boats stop.

*{{button|gcn|b}} - Row left oar
