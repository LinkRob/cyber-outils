The '''Doot-Doot Sisters''' are a group of two female [[Pianta]]s who have made a few appearances within ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. They are street performers who are often accompanied by a blue male Pianta who plays the ukelele. The Doot-Doot Sisters are colored yellow and pink respectively, and often end their sentence with "doot." The Doot-Doot Sisters appear to be popular throughout [[Isle Delfino]], although they do not like several of their fans.
