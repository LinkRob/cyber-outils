'''Super Catch''' is a move in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. It is the only fielding move to not return in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
A Super Catch is a fielding ability that can be used by either [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Diddy Kong]], or [[Toadsworth]]. It allows the character to catch the ball without fumbling it, unless it was a [[Star Swing]] from either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]].
