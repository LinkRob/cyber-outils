'''Single Swing Lifts''' are a type of [[lift]] which appear exclusively in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==Description==
A Single Swing Lift consists of a rotatable platform attached to a block by a few circles. They rotate in a clockwise formation.
