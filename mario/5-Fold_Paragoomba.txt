In the very first level of [[World 2 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|World 2]] Mario will quickly find [[Travelling Toad|a toad]] being bullied by five paragoombas. If Mario intervenes and refuses to pay them in coins then they will merge to form a 5-Fold Goomba and fight him.



==Behavior==
The 5-Fold Paragoomba is basically five regular [[Paragoomba]]s folded together into one. They connect themselves into a large, circular object that floats in the air. They can be confusing when the player tries to attack them.

==Battle==
The 5-Fold Goomba is a much stronger version of Paragoombas. They attack Mario by rolling into him. Since they are in the air, Mario must resort to using Jump-related Stickers to damage them.
