'''Sluggies''' are hairy slug enemies from the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They come in two colors: pink with red spots and yellow with blue spots. Pink appears to be the primary color for Sluggies, given that it has standalone artwork for ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and is the only color for Sluggy in ''Yoshi's New Island''.

===Behavior===
Sluggies can fall onto a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] from a ceiling. The pink Sluggies do not move while on the ceiling whereas the yellow ones do so slowly. If a Yoshi is nearby, a Sluggy attempts to land on him. Both types of Sluggies move while on the ground.

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
Sluggies only appear in one level, [[Sluggy The Unshaven's Fort]]. It also is where a giant Sluggy named [[Sluggy the Unshaven]] is fought.

==In ''Yoshi's New Island''==
Sluggies appear in the level [[Ready, Steady, Go!]].
