'''Boomerangs''' are an object thrown by [[Boomerang Bro]]s since ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. Just like a [[Hammer Bro]]'s [[hammer]]s or a [[Fire Bro]]'s [[fireball]]s, touching a Boomerang Bro's boomerang damages [[Mario]] or another playable character. Boomerangs also appear when [[Boomerang Mario]] throws them.
