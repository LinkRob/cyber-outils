The '''Big-POW Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is the [[Power|POW]]-based equivalent of the [[Big-DEF Badge]].

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''==
The Big-POW Badge can be acquired either from buying it at the [[Shroom Shop]] for 450 [[coin]]s or from a [[? Block]] in [[Gritzy Caves]]. If equipped, the Big-POW Badge temporarily increases attack power by 60% if getting a "Great" when using a [[Bros. Item]].
