The '''Sticky Glove''' is a weapon used by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the shop in [[Nimbus Land]] for 98 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Sticky Glove increases Mallow's [[Attack]] power by 60.
