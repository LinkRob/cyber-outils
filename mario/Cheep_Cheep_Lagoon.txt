'''Cheep Cheep Lagoon''', titled '''Cheep Cheep Cape''' in the British English version, is the third course of [[Mushroom Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

== Description ==
The player will drive beneath the water beneath the '''Cheep Cheep Lagoon'''. Hence the name, it takes place in a lagoon. After the big underwater portion of the stage, players go into a cave. This cave has another underwater area to dive. Although the player can dive into the water, the player can [[glide]] over it.
