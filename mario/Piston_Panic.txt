The '''Piston Panic''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Piston Panic.

== Description ==
In ''Kingdom Battle'', Piston Panic resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack with a medium range. The Piston Panic deals around 30 to 40 damage. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Bounce (status effect)|Bounce]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Piston Panic as an option among the Blasters. Like the [[Piranha Pelter]], the player has to defeat [[Pirabbid Plant]] to unlock the Piston Panic.
