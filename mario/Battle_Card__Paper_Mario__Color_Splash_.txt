|activated by=}}'''Battle Cards''' are vital attacking components in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. These cards are used by [[Mario]] in battles against enemies and bosses encountered in levels. Battle cards can be obtained from most [[? Block]]s, some colorless spots, and sometimes enemies. These battle cards can also be bought at [[Prisma Cardware]] in [[Port Prisma]], and thing cards can be bought from the [[Wringer]]. You can also donate some of your unused battle cards when you unlock the [[Prisma Museum]].

== Types ==
There are three different types of Battle Cards. These include:

* Basic cards (60 total)
* Enemy cards (72 total)
* Thing cards (27 total)

Each type of card is used in their own way, typically with their own unique attacking ability. Basic and Enemy cards are mainly used for attacking purposes, while the Thing cards are seen more as progressional elements rather than just being an attacking card. 

== Basic Cards ==
These cards are used in boss or enemy battles as a main attacking element. Basic cards consist of anything from boots to hammers, flowers, pow blocks, and can even be used as defensive items. These cards can come in 4 different types: the worn-out version, normal version, big version, and huge version. These cards can be defined by the different backgrounds on the cards themselves. The bigger and better conditioned card will give a greater effect when used.
