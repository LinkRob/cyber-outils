|name=Huge Iron Jump (Battle Card)}}'''Huge Iron Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Huge Iron Jump is the super-strong iron version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a huge blue-gray boot with an blue/cyan spiral background.
