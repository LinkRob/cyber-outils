'''Bubbas''' are enemies that appear in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'']] franchise. They are a variant of the [[Cheep Cheep]], and are big pink or orange fish, usually wearing sunglasses and thus resembling [[Blurp|Blurps]]. Bubbas are similar in their behaviour to the [[Boss Bass]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' as well as the [[Cheep Chomp]] in later games, trying to [[Mario]] if he gets near them for a certain amount of time.

==History==
===''Super Mario 64''===
Two Bubbas appear in [[Tiny-Huge Island]] and they cannot be defeated similar to most aquatic enemies in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. If Mario is eaten, he will lose a life, regardless of his current health. In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', they are replaced by [[Big Bertha (fish)|Big Berthas]].

===''Mario Party''===
[[File:Bubba's Trick.png|thumb|Bubba's Trick event in ''Mario Party'']]
A single Bubba appears in the original [[Mario Party|''Mario Party'']], where if characters stop on a [[Green Space|Happening Space]] on [[Yoshi's Tropical Island]], a gameboard in that game, he would eat [[Toad]], switching Toad's and [[Bowser]]'s positions. Bubba lacks his trademark shades in this game, thus looking more like [[Bub (enemy)|his smaller counterpart]].

===''Mario Golf''===
[[File:BubbaGolf.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]], either a Bubba or [[Blurp (Yoshi's Story)|Blurp]] will jump out of the water if the ball lands in it. They are also much smaller in this game.

==Trivia==
*The [[Cheep Cheep]]s from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' are often mistaken as Bubbas, but they do not wear sunglasses and are smaller in size.
