'''Orbison''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Orbisons are creatures which resemble a magic staff. They are first encountered in [[Nimbus Land]] and later appear in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] as well. Orbisons are a stronger variant of [[Orb User]]s.

==Battle==
Orbisons, unlike with the Orb Users, only have recovery moves for special attacks. However, Orbisons also have massive defense but low HP just like with the Orb Users.

===Strategy===
Orbisons are weak to [[Jump]] meaning [[Mario]]'s jump techniques are useful against them. [[Ultra Jump]] is more recommended as Orbisons still have massive defense.
