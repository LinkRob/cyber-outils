'''Don the Sparrow''' is a brown sparrow character who appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. Don appears in the various character's stories, having various, usually minor, roles between them. For instance, in [[Kat and Ana]]'s story, Don delivers a letter on Ana's behalf to call for Kat's help in dealing with [[Boneheads]].
