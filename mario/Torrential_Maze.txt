The '''Torrential Maze''' is the level that is on Page 2 on ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

== Collectibles ==
*'''♥ 1:''' When you reach The Message Block that talks about the ghosts, go to the 1st Opening on the right and your Heart will be there.
*'''♥ 2:''' Before the 1st Jelly Ghost, There is an opening to the left. Go there and you'll see a ? Bubble. Pop it and out comes the 2nd Have-A-Heart.
*'''♥ 3:''' In the area where the 4th Ms. Wrap, Go to the top left opening and you will find your 3rd Heart.
