'''Undergrunts''' (also simply referred to as '''Moles''') are mole-like enemies first appearing in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. These moles are typically seen digging beneath the ground leaving stoney dirt trails in their wake. Undergrunts wear helmets adorned in spikes making them susceptible to damage from above and are usually seen below the ground making hitting them rather difficult. Undergrunts are vulnerable to earthquakes and can be forced aboveground and then stunned by [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]]s and then from there, beaten with [[Star Spin]]s, the Undergrunts are also known to constantly harass the [[Star Bunny|Star Bunnies]] forcing [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] to eliminate them to free the bunnies.

==History==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[Image:Undergrunt Garden.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]]'s encounter with an Undergrunt.]]
Undergrunts are first seen in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. In this game, they can be found in the [[Gusty Garden Galaxy]] during the mission "The Dirty Tricks of Major Burrows" where they are found digging up the [[Star Bunny|Star Bunnies]] gardens. They are seen tunneling below the ground with only their helmets visible and cannot be attacked in this state (if they hit [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]], they will briefly pop their heads out of the ground taunting him before burrowing back beneath the ground), [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] must [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] near an Undergrunt to force it aboveground rendering it dizzy and from here, the plumbers can [[Star Spin]] near one to finish it off for a [[Coin]], typically, beating Undergrunts is required to advance onward. Undergrunts are later seen in the [[Gold Leaf Galaxy]]. A relative of the Undergrunt called the [[Monty]] also appears in several other galaxies. [[Undergrunt Gunner]]s also appear in the game as bosses and mini-bosses piloting the [[Bubble Cannon]]s and [[Cannon (boss)|Cannons]]. The Undergrunts are lead by [[Major Burrows]] who appears as a boss in the [[Gusty Garden Galaxy]].

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[Image:Undergrunt Planet.png|thumb|right|[[Mario]] stuns the Undergrunts.]]
Undergrunts return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' where they retain their behavior from the previous game (tunneling beneath the ground leaving dirt trails in their wake) however this time, they're much more rare with only three appearing in the whole game, they are only seen in the [[Battle Belt Galaxy]]. Undergrunts can once again be beaten by [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] near them and the preforming a [[Star Spin]] to finish them off however this time, they leave behind three [[Star Bit]]s instead of a [[Coin]] upon defeat. [[Undergrunt Gunner]]s also return in this game this time as regular enemies. [[Major Burrows]] also makes a return in this game as one of the five returning bosses from the [[Boss Blitz Galaxy]].

==Trivia==
*The Undergrunt's "defeat-reward" switching from a [[Coin]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' to three [[Star Bit]]s in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' is similar to the [[Jammyfish]] switching from a [[Coin]] to 10 [[Star Bit]]s
**In contrast, both are similar to how the [[Mandibug]]'s "defeat-reward" was switched from three [[Star Bit]]s to a [[Coin]].
*Undergrunts are the first major mole species since the [[Monty Mole]] to appear in a mainstream ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' game. Despite this, Undergrunts are unrelated to [[Monty Mole]]s and are just an entirely separate mole species.
