'''Mario''' better known as '''Jumpman''' is the one of the main protagonists of the 1983-84 Ruby Spears Saturday morning cartoon series ''[[Saturday Supercade]]''.

==Profile==

===Physical Description===
This incarnation of Mario marked the first appearance of Mario's current color scheme (red shirt and blue overalls), predating Mario's standard outfit in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' by five years. He notably has darker skin tone, smooth mustache, and is also taller being nearly as tall as Pauline.

==Personality traits==
Compared to his [[Mario|video game counterpart]], who is more heroic, this version of Mario's presented as a more gruff, closed-minded, short-tempered individual rather than the unambiguously heroic figure that he is today. He's often very desperate since he wants to bring Donkey Kong back to his circus. Despite his short temper, he still remains one of his heroic qualities. He's a pretty straight forward person and never backs down from a challenge. He's also protective and brave and will do whatever it takes to save [[Pauline (Saturday Supercade)|Pauline]] whenever she gets snatched by [[Donkey Kong (Saturday Supercade)|Donkey Kong]] or other enemies.
