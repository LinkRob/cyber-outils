|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Meteor Swarm''' is a very powerful special attack used only by [[Smithy]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When Meteor Swarm is used, multicolored rainbow stars start raining down across the screen.

=== Effects ===
With Meteor Swarm, Smithy is able to inflict heavy damage to all of Mario's party. Because of Meteor Swarm primarily, it is impossible to beat Smithy without Peach, even at level 30. If not blocked, it does 150+ damage to everyone, easily causing a Game Over.
