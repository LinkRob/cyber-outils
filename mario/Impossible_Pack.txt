The '''Impossible Pack''' is the tenth and final downloadable course pack for [[Coin Rush]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', although it released the same day as the ninth pack, [[Mystery Adventures Pack]]. The icon shows [[Dry Bowser]]'s emblem on a red and black-striped background, and the 10 indicates that Impossible Pack is the tenth course pack.
