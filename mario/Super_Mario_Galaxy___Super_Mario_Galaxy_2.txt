'''''Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2''''' is a compilation title consisting of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It was released on October 2, 2025 for the [[Nintendo Switch]] as well as an enhanced version for the [[Nintendo Switch 2]].<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tTYux25TW8Q Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2 – Nintendo Direct 9.12.2025]</ref> as part of the [[Super Mario Bros. 40th Anniversary|''Super Mario Bros.'' 40th Anniversary]] celebration. It is the second release of ''Super Mario Galaxy'' for the Nintendo Switch, after 2020's ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]''. First announced during the September 2025 Nintendo Direct, both games can also be purchased separately on the Nintendo eShop.

== Changes from the version of ''Super Mario Galaxy'' included in ''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'' ==

=== Gameplay ===

* An update, when played on the [[Nintendo Switch 2]], allows the second player in both games to use Joy-Con 2 mouse controls.
* As with ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', the games feature an Assist Mode that allows the player to start with six health as if they collected a [[Life Mushroom]], allows them to recover health by remaining stationary, and automatically returns them to solid ground if they fall into the void. Switching between Assist Mode and Normal Mode returns the player to the last checkpoint, resetting collectibles and enemies as if the player had died.
* ''Rosalina's Storybook'' includes an additional chapter for ''Super Mario Galaxy'', and a brand new storybook is added to ''Super Mario Galaxy 2.''
* The soundtrack for both games can be accessed and listened to from their respective title screens.
* [[Amiibo]] are now supported, and, like in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', scanning them gives the player in-game items.
* Although only pressing {{Button|ns|a}} is required to start the game as in ''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'', {{Button|ns|b}} on the title screen no longer emits a yellow glow effect that appears around the logo in either game.
* When saving, an icon appears at the bottom left corner of the screen. The player can freely move around while the game is saving.
* The player can now use {{Button|ns|leftstick}} to navigate menus, a change taken from the Wii version of ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.
* Collecting a Life Mushroom when the player has six segments of health now gives an extra life, another change taken from the Wii version of ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.
* Many menu options accessed by pressing {{Button|ns|minus}} in ''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'' are now accessed through a pause menu. As a result, players now cannot start or exit Co-Star Mode in situations where they cannot pause the game.
* The player can now tilt the console to move the Star Pointer instead of having to touch the screen, and press {{Button|ns|r}} to activate the star cursor while playing in handheld mode.

=== Visuals ===

* An update, when played on the Nintendo Switch 2, allows the games to run at 4K in docked mode and 1080p in handheld mode.
* The UI of both games has been updated.
** The HUD font used for the Life Meter, currencies, race ranks, and the "Welcome to the Galaxy!", "3", "2", "1", "Go!" (''Super Mario Galaxy''), "Start!" (''Super Mario Galaxy 2''), "Time's up!", "Too bad!", "Goal!", "Game!", "You lose", "Game Over", "You Got a Star!", "You Got a Grand Star!", and "The End" text has been replaced with the modern ''Super Mario'' font introduced in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', and appears less glossy than in the originals. In addition, the aforementioned text is now stylized in all caps.
* Most environmental and character textures have been replaced with higher-resolution versions.
* The opening pre-rendered cutscene of ''Super Mario Galaxy'' has been re-rendered at a higher resolution with certain textures replaced with higher-resolution versions. The aspect ratio has also been changed from 2.20:1 to 1.78:1, matching the gameplay aspect ratio and showing more on the top and bottom.

=== Text and translations ===

* Both games are now localized in Dutch and Brazilian Portuguese.
** Both games have been re-titled in Korean from their original ''Super Mario Wii'' moniker to the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' moniker, making the Korean versions consistent with other regions, and simultaneously dropping the [[Wii]] branding.
* In the Korean version, KoreanNGL is now used as the body text font instead of KoreanSDNR for both games.
* In the Italian version, mission titles and dialogues containing descriptive names for enemies and bosses or dated translations of their names are now updated with their most recent iteration.

== Amiibo ==
Two new amiibo figures are set to release on April 2, 2026, to coincide with the game and the theatrical release of the 2026 animated film, ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'': one depicts [[Mario]] being launched from a Launch Star, while the other depicts [[Rosalina]] reading to a group of Lumas. Scanning any Mario or Rosalina amiibo grants a [[Life Mushroom]] or [[1-Up Mushroom]], respectively. Additionally, amiibo figures from the ''Super Mario'' franchise grant coins, while all other compatible amiibo grant Star Bits.

== Reception ==

=== Critical reception ===
The compilation has received positive reviews. Several critics opine that the games have both aged well, while criticism has been directed at the controls and lack of fully remade visual assets.
