'''''Super Mario Galaxy Original Soundtrack''''', also titled '''''Super Mario Galaxy Official Soundtrack''''', is a soundtrack album consisting of various songs from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It was released on January 31, 2008, originally exclusive to [[Club Nintendo]] members, but was later [[reissue]]d on ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]'' alongside the ''[[Super Mario 64 Original Soundtrack]]'' and the ''[[Super Mario Sunshine Original Soundtrack]]''. Many of the songs featured on the ''Super Mario Galaxy Original Soundtrack'' were performed by the [[Mario Galaxy Orchestra]] band.
