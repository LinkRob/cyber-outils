'''Frosted Glacier''' is the snow/ice themed world of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. The world lies northeast of [[Layer-Cake Desert]], [[Sparkling Waters]], and [[Acorn Plains]], north of [[Soda Jungle]], and northwest of [[Rock-Candy Mines]] and [[Meringue Clouds]]. The world is covered in a blanket of snow, with scattered pine trees and mountains throughout. This world, along with Sparkling Waters, are both available to visit after Layer-Cake Desert. The player can choose one or the other, which can result in a complete playthrough without visiting the world.

There are a total of 9 levels in the world, 5 of them are normal stages, 1 Tower level, 1 [[Castle]] level, 1 Ghost House, and 1 secret level. The sky for some normal levels in this world has stars, a moon and auroras.
