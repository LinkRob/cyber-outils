'''Concordia's "World"'''<ref>[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]] [[Connie]]: "Now, let's head to where the Soli-Tree was! Aim shipshape toward the center of the world!"</ref><ref>[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]] "Center of the World: The heart of Concordia, where the Uni-Tree should be."</ref> is a location that appears in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]. It is a home planet of [[Connie]] and many others as well as a separate dimension far away from the [[Mushroom Earth]]. 

== Description ==
Most of the Concordia planet is largely unknown. It is similar world, but unconnected to [[Mushroom Earth|Mario's]]. Its most prominent location is [[Concordia]], the home planet of [[Connie]] and [[Concordian|her species]].

== History ==
The [[Great Conductor]] used his powers to summon [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and their friends to his Concordia's World due to their [[Bond (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Bonds]].
