'''Thought Peek''', formerly titled '''Psychopath''', is one of [[Mallow]]'s spells in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and the [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].

==Usage==
When Mallow uses it, the screen darkens and two lights circle around the enemy while drums play in the background. After that, a cloud with a question mark appears on top of the enemy's head. When the cloud vanishes, the enemy's remaining HP is being displayed in blue numbers. If you press any button (e.g. [[Timed Hit|time it]] right), right before the cloud appears, the player will hear a "Ding" sound, as usual, and a dialogue line appears, displaying what the enemy thinks. Doing this is sometimes useful because it can reveal weaknesses. This attack is a Time Level 1 attack.
