'''Green Pokeys''', originally known simply as '''Pokeys''', are a green variant of [[Pokey]]s exclusive to the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' and return in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Green Pokeys are just like normal Pokeys, being tall creatures made of individual round body segments, giving them a wiggly structure. They have a green body, based on regular Pokeys' appearance in early media, such as ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', and also have some black spikes on their bodies.

=== Behavior ===
Unlike yellow Pokeys, Green Pokeys lack spikes, are able to be jumped on, and have more [[HP]]. Green Pokeys attack by toppling on top of Mario; sometimes, Mario gets crumpled if he does not block the attack. Each time a Green Pokey loses a body segment, its attack goes down. Green Pokeys are aided by [[Spiny|Spinies]], [[Paragoomba|Paragoombas]], and [[Sombrero Guy|Sombrero Guys]].

The [[Tower Power Pokey]] can also summon these Pokeys during the boss battle against it. However, they only have 8 HP each.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''===
[[File:Greenpokeypmss.png|thumb|left|200px|Green Pokeys in the overworld]]
Green Pokeys (under the name '''Pokey''' in this game) appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' as common enemies throughout [[World 2 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|World 2]]. They appear in several levels of World 2 including [[Drybake Desert]], [[Yoshi Sphinx]], [[Sandshifter Ruins]], [[Damp Oasis]], and [[Drybake Stadium]], Pokeys can appear in the overworld and will chase [[Mario]] on sight to engage him in battle, they may also emerge from [[Quicksand]] rivers and ride them, along with showing up in battles with other enemies, in battle, Pokeys have 20 [[HP]] which is four more than a regular [[Pokey]] but they lack spikes on their head making them susceptible to [[Jump]] attacks. The use of [[Shiny Jump]]s can be used to chip away good chunks of HP from a Pokey, Pokeys typically attack by toppling themselves on [[Mario]] which may [[Crumpled|crumple]] him if he doesn’t [[Guard|block]] so be careful, when battles take place in Quicksand, the Pokeys won’t sink either. Pokeys are vulnerable to most attacks however and defeating them generally isn’t too hard. [[Tower Power Pokey]] may also summon some Green Pokeys to assist him in battle (but he will only do this if he’s just a head or has one segment revealed). The Green Pokeys that assist the Tower Power Pokey are slightly weaker than most others and only have 8 HP. 


===''Paper Mario: Color Splash''===
They return as enemies, and were renamed '''Green Pokeys''' to distinguish them from the yellow Pokeys. Green Pokeys show up alongside yellow Pokeys in the [[Mustard Café]] area, and behave as in their previous appearance. A Green Pokey is the first-round opponent in [[Roshambo Temple 6|Roshambo Temple #6]].
