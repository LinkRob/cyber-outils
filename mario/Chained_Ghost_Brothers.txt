== Description ==
[[File:Paranormal Chains.png|thumb|370x370px|Paranormal chains blocking the Gondola entrance]]
The Chained Ghost Brothers is a trio of ghosts that appear in [[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]. The trio consists of a Strong Greenie, a Strong Slammer, and a Strong Gobber, all wearing paranormal chains. These chains make them stronger. The Chained Greenie has 100 HP, as opposed to the 50 HP that [[Strong Greenie|Strong Greenies]] have, the Chained Slammer has 250 HP, as opposed to the 150 HP that [[Strong Slammer|Strong Slammers]] have, and the Chained Gobber has 300 HP, as opposed to the 200 HP that [[Strong Gobber|Strong Gobbers]] have. They act as sub-bosses located in the Secret Mines on mission E-3 "Across the Chasm" where they use their ability to create paranormal chains to create a blockade in front of the entrance to the Gondola, preventing [[Luigi]] from entering.

== How to Defeat ==
After blocking the entrance, the Chained Ghost Brothers will flee and separate, each entering a separate room. Each ghost has their own colored chain blocking the entrance. The Chained Greenie has a green chain, The Chained Slammer has a red chain, and The Chained Gobber had a yellow chain. Luigi has to capture the ghost corresponding with their certain cored chain to remove it. 

=== Chained Greenie ===
[[File:Chained Greenie.png|thumb|320x320px|The Chained Greenie staring at Luigi after opening a window]]
The Chained Greenie can be found in the Smuggler's Hideout. In order to reveal him, Luigi must use his [[Dark-Light Device]] to reveal a suit of armor resting on the ground. After this, he must interact with a nearby suit of armor holding a spear, which will cause it to lunge the spear into the resting suit of armor's helmet and remove it. Doing this will cause the Chained Greenie to jump-scare Luigi before opening a nearby window to allow three [[Sneaker|Sneakers]] in. After defeating the Sneakers, the Chained Greenie will reveal himself, allowing for his capture. After defeating him, the green paranormal chain will disappear. 

=== Chained Slammer ===
[[File:Chained Slammer Scare.png|left|thumb|320x320px|The Chained Slammer scaring Luigi]]
The Chained Slammer can be found in the Chalet. Luigi can find him by locating a shaking cabinet on the far-right side of the room. After searching it, the Chained Slammer will scare Luigi before fleeing into the fireplace. Three Greenies will appear out of the fireplace to attack Luigi. Once they are captured, the Chained Slammer will burst out of the fireplace to face Luigi himself. Once captured, the red paranormal chain will disappear.


=== Chained Gobber ===
[[File:Screenshot 2026-02-10 095947.png|thumb|320x320px|Luigi capturing the Chained Gobber]]
The Chained Gobber can be found in the Basin hiding behind wooden boards. You can spot him by using the reflection in the ice, along with a [[robomb]]. Luigi must use the [[Dark-Light Device]] to reveal the robomb before using the [[Strobulb]] to activate it. Once it explodes, it will stun the Chained Gobber, causing him to flee. Luigi must then defeat three Hiders. Once defeated, the Chained Gobber will drop from the ceiling to fight Luigi. After Luigi defeats him, the yellow paranormal chain will disappear.


Once all three ghosts are captured and all three chains disappear, the Gondola entrance will become accessible.

== Trivia ==

* Despite the visual and HP difference, the Chained Ghosts only register as normal strong ghosts in the vault.
* The Chained Gobber has the most overall HP out of any ghost in the Dark Moon Quest, only tied with [[King Boo]].
* The Chained Ghost Brothers are not the only strong ghost that act as sub-bosses. In the Old Clockworks mission "Piece at Last," a [[Strong Sneaker]] acts as a sub boss for that mission well.
* The Chained Gobber is the first Strong Gobber Luigi encounters.
