'''Chris Pratt''' is an American actor and comedian who voiced [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' and ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. Aside from ''Mario'', he is best known for playing [[w:c:jurassicpark:Owen Grady|Owen Grady]] in ''[[w:c:jurassicpark:Jurassic World (film)|Jurassic World]]'', [[w:c:garfield:Garfield|Garfield]] in ''[[w:c:garfield:The Garfield Movie|The Garfield Movie]]'', [[w:c:marvelcinematicuniverse:Star-Lord|Peter Quill/Star-Lord]] in the ''[[w:c:disney:Marvel Cinematic Universe|Marvel Cinematic Universe]]'', [[w:c:thelegomovie:Emmet Brickowski|Emmet Brickowski]] in ''[[w:c:thelegomovie:The Lego Movie|The Lego Movie]],'' and [[w:c:pixar:Barley Lightfoot|Barley Lightfoot]] in ''[[w:c:pixar:Onward|Onward]]''.

==Early life==
Pratt was born in Virginia, Minnesota on June 21, 1979. During the time of his birth, his mother worked at a Safeway supermarket, and his father worked in mining and later in remodeling houses. His mother is of Norwegian descent. At age seven, Pratt and his family relocated to Lake Stevens, Washington. In high school, Pratt participated in a youth wrestling tournament, shot put, and track & field. He graduated from Lake Stevens High School in 1997.

After dropping out of community college, Pratt began working as a door-to-door discount ticket salesman and an evening stripper. He eventually arrived at Maui, Hawaii, being homeless, spending most nights in an abandoned minivan and sleeping inside a tent. At age 19, Pratt worked as a waiter at a Bubba Gump restaurant.

==Acting career==
Pratt made his acting debut in 2000 where he portrayed Devon in the short film ''Cursed Part 3''. At the time he was working at Bubba Gump, Pratt met actress and director Rae Dawn Chong, who would cast Pratt for ''Cursed Part 3'', which was filmed in Los Angeles, California.

In 2011, Pratt gained a little more attention after portraying Scott Hatteberg in ''Moneyball'', a sports drama film based on the 2002 Oakland Athletic baseball team. In 2014, Pratt's fame rose to higher ranks after portraying Emmet Brickowski in ''The LEGO Movie''. During that same year, he portrayed Peter Quill the Star-Lord in ''Guardians of the Galaxy''.

2015 marked the year where Pratt made his first true breakthrough by portraying Owen Grady in ''Jurassic World'', the first film of the Jurassic Park sequel trilogy.

=====Official film critics=====
While the ''Los Angeles Times's'' Katie Walsh praised [[Jack Black|Black]]'s performance as Bowser, she criticized both Pratt and [[Charlie Day|Day]]'s lead performances of Mario and Luigi as being "so unremarkable that it could have been anyone at all".<ref>[https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/movies/story/2023-04-04/super-mario-bros-movie-review-chris-pratt-voice Review: "Wahoo!" isn’t Quite Right for Mildly Amusing, Hectic "Super Mario Bros. Movie"]</ref> Calum Marsh wrote for the ''New York Times'' criticizing Pratt's performance as "grating" and "unctuous".<ref>[https://www.nytimes.com/2023/04/05/movies/the-super-mario-bros-movie-review.html The Super Mario Bros. Movie Review: This Ain't No Game]</ref> Kristy Puchko from ''Mashable'' commended the fan service and music but was critical of the characters and noted Pratt's lack of accent, commenting "whatever voice Pratt is doing isn't Brooklyn, and it isn't anything particularly specific, consistent, or exciting".<ref>[https://mashable.com/article/the-super-mario-bros-movie-review 'The Super Mario Bros. Movie' Review: What if Mario had Daddy Issues?]</ref> ''Screenrant's'' Molly Freeman described most of the voice cast as "fun" with Black's Bowser being a standout, while noting that Pratt's performance was "not so bad as to be distracting, but not strong enough to be at all interesting either".<ref>[https://screenrant.com/super-mario-bros-movie-2023-review-movie/ The Super Mario Bros. Movie Review: Proof That Beloved IP Isn't Enough]</ref> Josh Spiegel from ''SlashFilm'' criticized Pratt's performance as Mario as being "blandly heroic".<ref>[https://www.slashfilm.com/1247880/super-mario-bros-movie-review-unsurprising/ The Super Mario Bros. Movie Review: Nintendo's Big Screen Adventure Thrives On Being Unsurprising]</ref> ''IGN's'' Tom Jorgensen had slight criticism towards Pratt and Day's attempt at New York accents for Mario and Luigi as "certainly not going to be taking home any commendations from the good people of Brooklyn" and stated "There’s definitely been a flattening of the more cartoonish qualities". However, he applauded the actors for embodying each character's heroism and for a grounded delivery that "balances well with the fantastical trappings of the Mushroom Kingdom".<ref>[https://www.ign.com/articles/the-super-mario-bros-movie-review The Super Mario Bros. Movie Review]</ref> Brian Tallerico of ''Roger Ebert'', a self-described lifelong fan, wrote that the vocal performances were "uniformly mediocre".<ref>[https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/the-super-mario-bros-movie-movie-review-2023 The Super Mario Bros Movie Review 2023]</ref>
