'''Floaters''' are a species of statue-like entities that assist [[Wario]] during his journey in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. A Floater carries an arrow on the bottom, indicating the direction in which it moves, and Wario can ride a Floater to travel across gaps. Similar to [[Directional Lift]]s from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', Wario can change the direction in which the Floater moves by jumping and landing back onto it.
