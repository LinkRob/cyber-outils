'''Rowf's Badge Shop''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Located in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]], this [[badge]] shop is run by [[Rowf]] and his son [[Rhuff]]. Here [[Mario]] can buy various badges for prices ranging from fifty to 200 [[coin]]s.

== Inventory ==
Each time Mario visited the shop, there will be three badges available. The badges are placed at random, but some can only be purchased after the [[Calculator]] has been returned.
