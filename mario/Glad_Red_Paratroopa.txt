A '''Glad Red Paratroopa''' (spelled '''G. R. P-Troopa''' in the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]]) is a variant of a [[Koopa Paratroopa|Red Koopa Paratroopa]] imbued with the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] [[vibe]] and an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Glad Red Paratroopa has the same appearance as their regular counterpart, but have a happy look on their face.

===Behavior===
A Glad Red Paratroopa flies around in a figure-8 pattern. [[Princess Peach]] can stomp on one to turn it into a [[Glad Red Koopa]], just as how stomping a regular red Paratroopa turns it into a [[Red Koopa Troopa]].
