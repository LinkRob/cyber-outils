'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[KO Arena]] course of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It can only be accessed after clearing [[Mountaineering!|Wiggler Race: Mountaineering!]] as it is located in an underground subarea. This level has one [[Wonder Seed]].

== Layout ==
The start point of the level is an area with many pipes which drop out [[Fire Flower|Fire Flowers]] and [[Elephant Fruit|Elephant Fruits]] infinitely, and one pipe taking the player to the start of the first room.
The first room contains 15 [[Goomba|Goombas]], some of which are sleeping. Once all the Goombas have been killed by the player, the gate to the pipe which takes the player to the next room unlocks.

The second room contains eight [[Trottin' Piranha Plant|Trottin' Piranha Plants]] which spawn from yellow pipes at the top of the level and walk to the bottom of the level. They can be defeated with the powerups given or [[Swirlypod]] shells spawning from pipes at the bottom of the level. The room contains many [[Semisolid Platform|Semisolid Platforms]] and [[Marimba Blocks]]. Once all the Trottin' Piranha Plants have been killed by the player, the gate to the pipe which takes the player to the final room unlocks.

The final room contains five [[Bulrush|Bulrushes]], which spawn on trees. Once all the Bulrushes have been killed by the player, the gate to the pipe which takes the player to the end room unlocks.

The ending room contains a treasure chest with a [[Wonder Seed]] inside, and five [[Talking Flower|Talking Flowers]] that say "Congratulations!" simultaneously.

== 10-Flower Coins ==
According to the time taken, [[10-Flower Coin|10-Flower Coins]] will drop from pipes on the ceiling. The timings are as follows:

* <180 seconds: 1x 10-Flower Coin
* <120 seconds: 2x 10-Flower Coins
* <70 seconds: 3x 10-Flower Coins
