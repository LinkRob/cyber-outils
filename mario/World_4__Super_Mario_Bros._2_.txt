'''World 4''' is the fourth of the seven [[worlds]], following [[World 3 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]], in the game ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. The world has a unique snow and ice theme to it, and introduces the player to the [[Flurry]] and [[Autobomb]] enemies. The boss of this world is [[Fryguy]], who is an intentionally out-of-place boss for this world, being a fire creature in an ice-covered world, before [[World 5 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5]].

==Levels==
===World 4-1===
The first part of the level consists of dodging [[Flurry|Flurries]] while sliding on icy surfaces and jumping over pits. At the far right, there is a plant that turns into a [[Rocket|rocket ship]] when pulled up, which takes the player to the next part of the level. Level 4-1 is notable for being the only level in the entire game without a boss, instead it has a long area filled with many enemies, with the boss music playing in the background. The [[Crystal Ball]] is lying unguarded on the ground at the end.

===World 4-2===
World 4-2 is the only level in the game that has [[Whale]]s in it. The level starts underground, and has a vine that the player needs to climb to the first main part of the level. The player then needs to slide, duck, and jump along the ice while dodging a flock of [[Beezo]]s. After going through a doorway, the next section has the whales in it. The water coming through the blowholes can hurt the player if the player hits it from the sides, but the player can stand on top of it. After picking up the plant that turns into a rocket ship, the player must go to the next area and approach the last room where a red Birdo is located. After she is defeated, the player will head to World 4-3.

This level also has a hidden warp to [[World 6 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]]. After the player enters the area with the whales, look for a [[Potion|magic potion]]. Carry it forward until there is a vase. Enter the vase after crossing over to the dream world, and the player will warp to World 6.

===World 4-3===
In world 4-3, a pink [[Birdo (species)|Birdo]] is strangely seen as a seemingly normal enemy early in level, and she reappears after being defeated whenever the player reenters the room it is in. This allows it to be defeated an infinite number of times for a massive amount of points. The actual purpose of this out-of-place Birdo is for the player to ride on one of her eggs across the otherwise impassable ocean to reach the main dungeon where Fryguy's lair is located. After defeating Fryguy, the door appears, advancing the player from World 4 to [[World 5 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5]].
