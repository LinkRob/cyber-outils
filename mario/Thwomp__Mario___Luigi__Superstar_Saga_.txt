'''Thwomp''' is a non-playable character who first appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It is a representative of its species of the [[Thwomp|same name]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
The only two Thwomps of ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' live in the new home of the [[Hammerhead Bros.]] from the [[Hoohoo Mountain]] to [[Beanbean Kingdom]] outskirts. The [[Gray Rock]] leading to the first Thwomp and [[Black Rock]] leading to the second Thwomp can be destroyed after the [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s hammers are upgraded to the level required for each of the types of rocks. Each Thwomp reveals himself in a featureless room.

In each respective room, he explains that if [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] want to play the game, they will be charged a certain number of coins to have a chance at winning a prize from the three summoned stones. The three summoned stones are actually a gimmick as each stone have the same result, which is randomized when the bros enter the thwomp's room.

The first room, which requires a [[Super Hammer]] to break the [[Gray Rock]] blocking it, charges 200 coins with the possible results being nothing, 200 coins, 300 coins, or [[Secret Scroll 1]]. The [[Secret Scroll 1]] can only be obtained once, so only the 3 remaining results are possible after it is obtained.

The second room, which requires an [[Ultra Hammer]] to break the [[Black Rock]] blocking it, charges 500 coins with the possible results being nothing, 500 coins, 800 coins, or [[Secret Scroll 2]]. The [[Secret Scroll 2]] can only be obtained once, so only the 3 remaining results are possible after it is obtained.

In the [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]], he looks like a normal Thwomp, although slightly thicker.
