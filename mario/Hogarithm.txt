A '''Hogarithm''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Hogarithms are a stronger variant of [[Pigarithm]]s only seen in [[Castle Bleck]], specifically [[Castle Bleck Interior]]. Like Pigarithms, Hogarithms mainly move around in a set pattern. Attacking a Hogarithm causes it to become smaller and faster, and defeating it altogether causes several [[coin]]s to appear.
