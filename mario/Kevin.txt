'''Kevin''' is one of the [[human]] tennis players unique to the [[Mario Tennis (series)|''Mario Tennis'' series]]. He appears in both ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]] and in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. Unlike many of the other tennis players, Kevin is not playable in either game. In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour'', Kevin is the coach of the Varsity Class in the [[Royal Tennis Academy]].
