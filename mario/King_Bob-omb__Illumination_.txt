'''King Bob-omb''' is a minor antagonist in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
King Bob-omb resembles his [[King Bob-omb|video game counterpart]], except that he has a fuse on top of his head, like regular [[Bob-omb|Bob-ombs]].

=== Powers and abilities ===
==== Weaknesses ====
* '''Flammability''': If King Bob-omb's fuse is exposed to fire, he will explode.

== Biography ==
In [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]'s wedding, King Bob-omb is one of the many other enemies that attended. When the fight started between Bowser and [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]], Peach threw a fired spear at a sleeping King Bob-omb, and his fuse lit, awakening him in fear. He desperately tried to put out the fire, only to explode seconds later, much to his chagrin. He ends up blowing Peach into the stairs, making her lose her [[Ice Flower]] power-up.
