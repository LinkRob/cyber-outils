'''Pauline's lost items''' are belongings that belong to [[Pauline]]. In its debut, ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]],'' it was called '''Parasol, Hat & Bag'''.

== In ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' ==
If [[Mario|Jumpman]] collects the Parasol, Hat & Bag, he gains bonus points.

== In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''==
After helping Pauline in finding all three objects, the player gets rewarded with a [[Power Moon]].
