'''Mummers''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mummers are purple creatures wrapped in bandages, giving them the appearance of a mummy. If a Mummer's bandages are removed, they are revealed to be skinny instead of fat.

===Behavior===
They walk slowly and breathe poisonous gas at [[Wario]], which hurts him.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
A Mummer has a two-star threat rating. To defeat a Mummer, Wario must first execute an [[Earthshake Punch]], causing its bandages to fall off and making the Mummer temporarily dizzy. While a Mummer is dizzy, Wario can defeat it using a [[Dash Attack]].
