[[File:Mario_Kart_DS_Shell_Cup_trophy.jpg|250px|thumb|The Shell Cup trophy in ''Mario Kart DS'']]
The '''Shell Cup''' is the first [[Retro Cup]] featured in every mainline [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]] installment since ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. It is based on the [[Green Shell]] item. The Shell Cup features mostly simple tracks from previous ''Mario Kart'' installments, making it the [[Mushroom Cup]]'s counterpart in this regard. The Shell Cup is followed by the [[Banana Cup]].
