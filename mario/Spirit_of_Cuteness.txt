The '''Spirit of Cuteness''' is one of the [[tiny spirits]] in ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Spirit of Cuteness is a round, flying pink creature with white wings, flower imprints all over her body, and red spirals in her cheeks. She also makes a buzzing sound while flying.

==History==
[[File:Spirit of Cuteness screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|A screenshot of the Spirit of Cuteness introducing herself to Yoshi]]
In ''Yoshi Topsy-Turvy'', [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] first meets the Spirit of Cuteness in the [[Tale of the Spirit of Cuteness]]. She is the first of the tiny spirits whom Yoshi encounters in the [[Forbidden Pop-Up Book]].

After introducing herself, the Spirit of Cuteness tells Yoshi that [[Bowser]] turned the [[Eggling]]s into [[apple]]s and asks him to save them. If Yoshi rescues enough Egglings in a mission, she gives Yoshi a Happiness Medal. Once Yoshi receives at least two Happiness Medal, the Spirit of Cuteness allows him to progress to the next page. She occasionally appears in the other spirits' tales, giving Yoshi more missions to save more Egglings.

In the ending, she and the other spirits thank Yoshi.
