'''Marianne''' is a character in the "[[Marianne & Luigeena]]" live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. She and her sister, [[Luigeena]], are the cousins of [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]].
