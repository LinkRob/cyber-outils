}}'''Rolltergeist''' is an enemy in [[Wario: Master of Disguise|''Wario: Master of Disguise'']]. 

==History==
Rolltergeists are small rolls of bread with eyes that attack by slowly rolling toward [[Wario]]. These unique enemies can only be found as a cluster in a secret room of [[Blowhole Castle]] that be accessed from pressing a [[Spirit switch]]. Any attacks are able to defeat a Rolltergeist and defeating them always results in two gems being collected. Oddly enough, the secret room that the Rolltergeists can be found in can be accessed using precise usage of [[Arty Wario]]'s blocks and doing so reveals that the Rolltergeists aren't affected by the spirit switches compared to all of the other enemies in Blowhole Castle. It is unclear if the developers wasn't intending for this room to be accessed by other means than the spirit switches or not.

==Trivia==
*"Rolltergeist" is a play on the words "roll" and "poltergeist."
