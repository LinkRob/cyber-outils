|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Flame Wall''' is a [[Fire (element)|Fire]]-type move used by certain enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Flame Wall is used, the screen becomes engulfed in flames and the flames move from right to left during the animation.

==Effects==
Flame Wall can damaged all characters for moderate damage.

==Trivia==
*In the Virtual Console version of this game, Flame Wall has been darkened to avoid the risk of seizures.
