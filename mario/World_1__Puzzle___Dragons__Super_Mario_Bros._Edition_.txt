{{DISPLAYTITLE:World 1 (''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition'')}}

'''World 1''' is the first world of ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. As the very first world, it serves as a mostly introductory world where the difficulty isn't very hard at all. Completing 1-Tower unlocks Yellow [[Toad]], and 1-Airship unlocks Super Mario and Super Luigi.
