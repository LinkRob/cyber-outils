A '''Blewbird''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Blewbirds are black-shelled blue avian creatures that have a red beak.

=== Behavior ===
Blewbirds shoot a projectile from their beak. This projectile extends into a horizontal pole upon collision with a wall. Mario is able to use this horizontal pole as a platform.

== In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ==
Blewbirds appear prominently in the level [[Blewbird Roost]].

== Variants ==
Bubble-blowing variants can be found during certain [[Wonder Effect]]s.

== Behind the scenes ==
=== Etymology ===
"Blewbird" is a pun on the verb "to blow" and "bluebird."
