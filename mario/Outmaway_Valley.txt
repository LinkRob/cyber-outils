'''Outmaway Valley''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
==Layout==
The level features many [[Outmaway]]s pushing [[Ice Block]]s. The level immediately showcases how Outmaways function by having one push an ice block over the player's head.

Later in the level, is a lone large ice block. Breaking the large ice block with cause the ground to glow yellow. Ground pounding here will summon the [[Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will turn the ground the player is standing on into a large snowball. The snowball with move right, and will picking up [[:Category:Objects|objects]] along the way, including the [[Goal Pole]]. After the wonder effect expires, the player will be able to walk to the [[Red Flagpole]].

==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s are obtainable in the following order:
#There are two Outmaways pushing a large ice block back and forth. There is a [[handle]] above the area where this is happening. Pulling the handle will reveal a [[10-Flower Coin]].
#Falling in a [[pit]] after a blocked yellow [[pipe]] will lead to a secret area with a 10-flower coin.
#There is an Outmaway pushing large ice blocks that come from the ground. The ice blocks eventually fall into a pit. Jumping off the ice block as it is falling will allow the player to get a 10-flower coin
