The '''Time Kinoko''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Wario]]''.

==Description==
Time Kinokos refill [[Time Limit|Time Gauge]], functioning similarly to [[+ Clock|Clocks]] in some of the other games. Time Kinokos have a red cap with white spots, the same as with standard [[Mushroom]]s and [[Super Mushroom]]s.

==In ''Mario & Wario''==
Time Kinokos are rare items that only appear in a few courses. To obtain one, [[Wanda]] has to guide her character to walk over to it.
