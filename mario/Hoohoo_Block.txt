A '''Hoohoo Block''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
The Hoohoo Block is needed so that the [[Hammerhead Bros.]] can create [[hammer]]s for [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. Hoohoo Blocks are located at the peak of [[Hoohoo Mountain]] which Mario and Luigi must climb up. After defeating [[Dragohoho]] and freeing [[Prince Peasley]], Hoohoo Blocks head down the river and land in the Hammerhead Bros.' home where they make the hammers for Mario and Luigi.

==Trivia==
*Despite their name, Hoohoo Blocks are not really cuboid. Hoohoo Blocks are small, round purple rocks that have faces on them.
