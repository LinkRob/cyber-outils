'''''Donkey Konga: The Hottest Hits''''' is a promotional soundtrack from Nintendo of Europe consisting of seven songs featured in ''[[Donkey Konga]]''. It was released in 2004.

==Track listing==
One of the songs is from the {{series|Super Mario}}, two are from ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' (one pertaining to a ''Mario'' stage in the game), one is for ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'', and three are from a ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' game.
