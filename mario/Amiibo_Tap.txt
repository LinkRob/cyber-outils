'''amiibo Tap''' is one of [[9-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object is to move the [[amiibo]], which can either be a [[Mario]], [[Samus Aran|Samus]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] amiibo, chosen by the microgame at random, to the [[New Nintendo 3DS]].

In level 2, random object appears alongside the amiibo. The random objects include either a duck, a cup of tea, a bird in a nest, an egg, a tomato, or a turtle. In level 3, one of the random objects from level 2 appears, now as well as a fake Mario amiibo.
