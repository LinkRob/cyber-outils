'''Wario's mother''' is the unseen mother of [[Wario]] who appears in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', during the blooper reel. During one part, Wario gets a call on the phone while lifting weights, while doing so he learns it is his mother. He then says "Mom? I told you not to call me!" This may imply their relationship is strained, though it could also be because Wario was busy lifting and reminded his mother in advance.
