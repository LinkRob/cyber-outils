A '''Springy Flower''' is a flower which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Springy Flowers first appear in the [[Honeyhive Galaxy]], during the first episode [[Bee Mario Takes Flight]]. To use Springy Flowers, [[Mario]] has to use his [[Star Spin]] close to their stem, causing him to be sent up the Springy Flower as if it were a [[Sproutle Vine]]. In the third episode, [[Big Bad Bugaboom]], Mario can either use the Springy Flowers or the [[Bee Mario|Bee]] power-up to make it to [[Bugaboom]]'s area.

Springy Flowers return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', retaining their design and utilization from before. Their first appearance in this game is the [[Rightside Down Galaxy]].
