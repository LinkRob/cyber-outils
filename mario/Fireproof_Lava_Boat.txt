'''Fireproof Lava Boat''' is a vehicle in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Too Hot to Handle]]."

==Description==
The Fireproof Lava Boat is a small, inflatable raft unaffected by heat or [[lava]]. It is owned by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]].
