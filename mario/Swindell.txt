'''Swindell''' is a character in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a [[Bandit]] who lives on the east side of [[Rogueport]].

==History==
Swindell can always be seen standing outside where he talks about how much he likes the [[Robbo Gang]]. Swindell also seems to hate the [[Pianta Syndicate]] but has admitted that he has not seen [[Ishnail]], [[Don Pianta]], nor [[Frankie]] personally. However, at the end of the game, Swindell declares that they are not as cool as [[Mario]] who had just recently saved the world.

==Etymology==
His name is a pun on the word "swindle," meaning for someone to gain money from someone through deceptive means.
