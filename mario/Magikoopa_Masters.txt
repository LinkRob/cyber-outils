The {{Nihongo|'''Magikoopa Masters'''|レッドデビルズ|Reddo Debiruzu|Red Devils}} (also known as the {{Nihongo|'''Underworld Servants'''|ちぬられしじごくの使い|Chinurareshi Jigoku no Tsukai}}) is a team of the [[Glitz Pit]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Magikoopa Masters are a team of three [[Magikoopa]]s: a [[Red Magikoopa]], a [[Green Magikoopa]], and a [[White Magikoopa]]. They are mainly ranked 5th making them one of the strongest teams in the Glitz Pit. Oddly, they are sometimes ranked 4th or 6th. After the Magikoopa Masters are defeated, [[Bowser]] storms in demanding a battle with [[Mario]].
