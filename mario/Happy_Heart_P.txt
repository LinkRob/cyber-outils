'''Happy Heart P''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is like the [[Happy Heart]] badge but its use instead extends over to [[Mario]]'s partners.

==Effects==
Mario can wear Happy Heart P by using two [[Badge Point|BP]], and while wearing this badge, Mario's partners restore one [[Heart Point|HP]] every turn in battle.

This badge is not only purchased from [[Dazzle]] for four [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]], but Mario has to find this badge as well. It is located in [[Rogueport Sewers]] where Mario can find a large staircase and by using [[Flurrie]], he can reveal a secret treasure chest with this badge inside.
