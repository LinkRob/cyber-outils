'''Fury Calico Cat Parents''' are fury versions of [[Calico Cat Parent]]. They are bigger versions of [[Fury Cat]]s. They are black with red eyes and ears, have spiked tails and fur on their heads and backs, and have a red aura emanating from them. When Fury Bowser appears, all kittens and calico cat parents on Lake Lapcat turn into Fury Cats and chase after Mario if he is nearby, damaging him if they make contact . Fury Cats are virtually invincible, though hitting them with fireballs or boomerangs knocks them a short distance away, and clawing at them as Cat Mario may stun them for a moment. Mario can also cause them to poof away by coming into contact with them as Invincible Mario or Lucky Cat Mario. If a Fury Cat is pushed into water or lava by Mario, Plessie, or Bowser Jr., it will poof away and not return until Fury Bowser returns to his inactive state. When this occurs, all Fury Cats will return to normal, and those that disappeared will respawn in their original location.
