'''Crash Test, Dummy!''' is one of [[Mona]]'s [[microgame]]s, under the [[Strange]] folder, in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame displays a side view of a crash dummy in the driver's seat of a car.

==Gameplay==
Shortly after the microgame starts, the car shakes and then crashes, and right before it crashes, the player must press {{button|gba|A}} to deploy the air bag. If done successfully, they win at the microgame, but if done incorrectly, they lose at it.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' It takes little time for the car to stop after it starts shaking.
*'''Level 2:''' It takes longer for the car to stop after it starts shaking.
*'''Level 3:''' It takes even longer for the car to stop after it starts shaking.
