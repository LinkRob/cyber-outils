A '''Straw Head''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Straw Heads are undead, scarecrow-like enemies only encountered in the [[Sunken Ship]].

==Battle==
In battle, Straw Heads are capable of using a couple of nasty tricks. Their signature attack is [[S'crow Funk]] which can turn a party member into a scarecrow. They can also use [[Dark Claw]] which can [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] a party member.

===Strategy===
Straw Heads are weak to thunder attacks meaning any of [[Mallow]]'s thunder techniques are effective against them. Also, since Straw Heads are undead enemies, a [[Pure Water]] can be used on them to defeat them instantly.
