'''Chocolate Island 5''' is a [[level]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', more specifically the [[world]] [[Chocolate Island]]. Although it is not the fifth level in succession, Chocolate Island 5 can be accessed after completing [[Chocolate Island 4]], following after in numerical order. Chocolate Island 5 is the penultimate level of Chocolate Island, as completing it unlocks [[Wendy's Castle|#6 Wendy's Castle]].
