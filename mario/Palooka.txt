The '''Palooka''' is an enemy from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

== Description ==
Palookas are sentient red punching bags that wear yellow boxing gloves and sport yellow dot eyes surrounded by a black hourglass-shaped marking. They have a single leg that they hop around on.

== Behaviour ==
In the field, Palookas hop around and charge up to punch intruders they spot.

=== Combat ===
Palookas have two attacks, both of which must be countered with hammer strikes and potentially knock the target out of the battle for a few turns. Their first attack is an uppercut which must be counterattacked several times to stop it. The second attack involves them guarding before the guard is released to punch, not unlike the POW [[Pep]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

== Variants ==
A stronger version of the Palooka called Palooka R can be encountered in [[Fortress Zokket]]. These have blue skin, but their palette remains the same in other areas. Both versions are also infused with [[Glohm]] after [[Reclusa]] awakens; while regular Glohm-infused Palookas still haunt Skorcheen, Glohm-Infused Palooka Rs appear in the [[Soli-Tree]].

== Stats ==
Regular Palookas are Level 24 enemies with 493 [[Heart Point|hit points]], an [[Power|attack power]] of 101, a [[defense]] of 51, and a [[Speed (Initiative)|speed]] of 83, giving 218 [[Experience Point|experience]], 53 [[Coin|coins]], and possibly a [[1-Up Mushroom]] (5% of drops), a set of Liberation Wear (2% of drops), or a sample of [[Crafting Materials (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Skorcheen Lava]] (23% of drops) when slain. Upon being infused with Glohm, their level jumps up to 40, with 1652 hit points, an attack power of 220, a defense of 126, and a speed of 193, giving 1322 experience and 197 coins when destroyed.

Palooka R's are of Level 38 with 1224 hit points, giving 891 experience and 138 coins upon defeat. Upon being infused with Glohm, their level jumps up to 43 with 1947 hit points, an attack power of 249, a defense of 146, and a speed of 213, giving 1593 experience and 224 coins when destroyed.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Palookas are based on a punching bag. Their name is a word that refers to an inferior/average prizefighter.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Palookas are called ''Nagurōbu'' (written ナグローブ), from ''naguru'' ("to punch"; written 殴る) and a transliteration of "glove." Their Chinese name, ''Jīdǎ Quán'' (written 击打拳), literally means "punching fist." Spanish translates their name as ''noquete'', from ''noquear'' ("to knock out") and the diminutive suffix "-''ete''." Portuguese refers to them as ''Murrilo'', from ''murro'' ("punch"). In Italian, their name is ''Nocchetto'', from ''nocca'' ("knuckle"). French translates their name as ''Pawplum'', from ''poids plume'' ("featherweight"). In the Korean dub, they are known as ''peokgeulleobeu'' (written 퍽글러브), from ''peok'' (onomatopoeia for punching, written 퍽) and a transliteration of "glove."
