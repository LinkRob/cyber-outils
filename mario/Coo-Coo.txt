'''Coo-Coos''' are a featherless chicken-like enemy found in {{w|grandfather clock}}s within [[Stage 10 (Virtual Boy Wario Land)|Stage 10]] of ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]'', although Coo-Coos themselves are based on {{w|cuckoo clock}}s.

Whenever a Coo-Coo emerges from a clock, which occurs whenever the long hand circles around the clock face a few times, it drops an egg containing a [[Bomb (enemy)|Bomb]]. [[Wario]] can defeat a Coo-Coo by either [[Dash Attack|barging]] it or from his flames as [[Sea Dragon Wario|Sea Dragon]] or [[King Dragon Wario]].
