'''Hammer and Sic 'Em''' is a [[Team Minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera zooms out from the center of the circular arena while the teams are aligned to their side.

== Gameplay ==
The team must pop their rivals balloons with their hammer. Allies help the team players to pop other rivals balloons. If they hit a player with a hammer, they will be stunned for a few seconds and will get up. Whomever's balloon remains present wins the minigame.

== Ending ==
The winning team performs their victory and the losing team performs their losing animation.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – Strike
