|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''HP Rain''' is a special move used by [[Mallow]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Mallow learns this move at level 3 and requires 2 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Description==
When HP Rain is used, a white cloud appears and will drop blue rain onto the selected character.

==Effects==
With HP Rain, Mallow is able to heal someone for moderate HP. He can also heal himself and if the player presses the Y button just before it move ends, they will regain more HP. However, HP Rain doesn't restore much HP than [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]]'s [[Therapy]].
