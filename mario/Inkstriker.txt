The '''Inkstriker''' is an veichle that is exclusive to ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. The Inkstriker is based on the Inkstrike special in the original ''Splatoon'' game. It is unlocked when a certain number of coins are collected, but it is randomized. It's stats are identical to the [[Mach 8]] and [[Sports Coupe]] from the same game.
