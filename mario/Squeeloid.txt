The '''Squeeloid''' is an enemy from [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']]. They first appear in the Canyon Layer stage.

== Description ==
Squeeloids are bipedal monsters that resemble boars. They sport two small legs that can become wheels when they need to run, and have a small pink tail that resembles a bomb fuse.

== Behaviour ==
Squeeloids attack their prey by turning their feet into wheels and charging at their target. [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] can punch them to launch them. When a launched Squeeloid hits an object, it will explode, destroying any destructible objects caught in its blast radius and damaging enemies in that area. Other enemies can detonate Squeeloids by hitting them.

== Variants ==

=== Ice Squeeloid ===
Found in the Freezer Layer, these Squeeloids function just like their regular counterparts, but have shells made of snow and ice, and their explosions can cool lava to stone and put out fire.

=== Spiked Squeeloid ===
Some Squeeloids have thorns (which function like spikes) on their bodies, and therefore cannot be launched by direct contact attacks. They can be launched or detonated by other means, however, and behave the same.

=== Salt Squeeloid ===
Some Squeeloids, mainly those in the Feast Layer, have salt in their shells. When launched at muck, they can destroy it.

=== Sturdy Squeeloid ===
Some Squeeloids have harder materials in their shells, and can only be launched by the Kong Bananza transformation.

== Appearances In Battle Challenges ==
Ten regular Squeeloids appear in the first Battle Challenge of the Canyon Layer.  The Canyon Layer's second Battle Challenge has five Sturdy Squeeloids. Eight Ice Squeeloids appear in the Freezer Layer's sixth Battle Challenge. Five Spiked Squeeloids appear in the Forest Layer's first Battle Challenge. The second Battle Challenge of the Tempest Layer has four Sturdy Squeeloids.

== Behind The Scenes ==
"Squeeloid" is based on the word "squeal" (the sound that pigs make), with the replacement of the A with E based on the word "wheel."

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Squeeloids are called ''Sukīro'' (written スキーロ), from the word "squeal" and possibly "roll." Spanish translates their name as ''Jabalóide'', from ''jabalí'' ("warthog"), ''bala'' (a translation of "cannonball"), and the suffix -''óide'', which denotes likeness to something. Portuguese refers to them as ''Bramidóide'', from ''bramido'' (which refers to an animal's bellow) and the suffix -''óide'' (which delotes likeness to something). In Italian, their name is ''Verruóide'', from ''verro'' (a translation of "pig"), ''ruota'' (which can either mean "wheel" or "he/she/it spins"), and the suffix -''óide'' (which denotes likeness to something). French translates their name as ''Grouikoïde'', from ''grouik'' ("oink") and the suffix -''oïde'' (which denotes likeness to something). In German, they are called ''Wuzoid'', from ''Wutz'' (a regional term for "pig") and the suffix -''oid'' (which denotes likeness to somethig).
