A '''Goomba Mummy''' is a variant of [[Goomba]] which exclusively appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Ten Koopmandments]]." Only one makes an appearance, and it is seen chasing [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]], and [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] through a pyramid in [[Pyramid Land]].
