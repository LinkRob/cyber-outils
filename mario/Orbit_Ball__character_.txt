'''Orbit Ball''' is a character in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Orbit Ball is a light-skin ball with two small, beady eyes, two horizontal eyebrows, a wide, red mouth. He often smiles, revealing two buckteeth on his upper jaw.

===Behavior===
Orbit Ball is shown to be briefly, as in [[Galaxy Bounce]], when he reaches his home planet, he gives the "V" peace sign.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
Orbit Ball is exclusively featured as the playable character of both the [[microgame]] [[Galaxy Bounce]] and the souvenir of the [[Orbit Ball (souvenir)|same name]]. In Galaxy Bounce, the object is to draw lines to keep Orbit Ball bouncing upward until he reaches his home planet, but if Orbit Ball misses a line and falls down offscreen, the player loses at the microgame. In the Orbit Ball souvenir, the gameplay is very similar, except it only ends when Orbit Ball misses a line and falls offscreen.
