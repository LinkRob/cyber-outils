'''Carroboscis''' are carrot-like enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', exclusively at [[Booster Pass]]. They never appear on the field and are only fought alongside other enemies (mainly [[Spikester]]s).

==Battle==
In battle, Carroboscis attack physically by leaping into the air and attacking its victim. A Carroboscis can also use many spells such as [[Doom Reverb]] or [[Spore Chimes]].

===Strategy===
A Carroboscis's weakness are Mario’s Jump and Fire moves, so using those on them is recommended.
