'''Boot Camp''' is the boss [[microgame]] of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s stage, [[Tomorrow Hill (stage)|Tomorrow Hill]], in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player controls their [[Mii]], who first has to make their way through the assault course. For this half, the [[Form Baton]] utilizes The Mohawk form. There are wooden fences which either need to be ducked under or jumped over. After this is the second half, during which the form switches to The Elephant, and the player has to shoot fruit in the giant nose boss's nostrils before it runs into them.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' Short assault course where, during the second half, the player's Mii shoots bananas while battling the nose, which does not attack them.
*'''Level 2:''' Longer assault course where, during the second half, the player's Mii shoots cherries, and the nose attacks by shooting at them.
*'''Level 3:''' Long assault course where, during the second half, the player's Mii shoots peaches. Like in level 2, the nose attacks, but it is closer to the player's Mii than in previous difficulty levels.
