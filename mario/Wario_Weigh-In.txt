'''Wario Weigh-In''' is one of [[Wario-Man]]'s [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''. As such, it appears in the Spandex Challenge level. Its gameplay is emulated by [[Curls and Weigh]] in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Gameplay==
Here, [[Wario]] has gotten fat and must exercise to reduce his weight. There is a limit, though. If he has not exercised enough or has exercised too much, you lose. The size of the acceptable weight range typically increases on easier difficulties, with the starting point usually being closer in as well. (i.e. 275 and down to 100 to 200 on Level 1, 325 and down to 100 to 175 on Level 2, and 375 and down to 100 to 150 on Level 3).
