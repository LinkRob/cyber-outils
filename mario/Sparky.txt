'''Sparky''' is a variant of [[Podoboo]] featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They appear in [[Pipe Vault]] and [[Moleville Mines]]. There are also two Lava Bubble variants, the [[Lava Blubble]] and [[Lava Babble]], which can be found in [[Booster Tower]] and [[Barrel Volcano]], respectively.

In [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|the game's remake]], they were referred to as '''Lava Bubbles''' to be closer to the Japanese name.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
A Sparky can attack Mario by using [[Drain]] or [[Flame (move)|Flame]]. It can also spin its body into a [[party]] member. A Lava Bubble can also use the special attack [[Hot Shot]], which allows it to shoot a small fire orb at a party member. They are invulnerable to [[Fire (element)|fire]]-elemental attacks, although unlike other fire-based enemies, Sparkies are not invulnerable to [[Mario]]'s [[jump]]. Lava Bubbles typically fight in groups of one to three.
