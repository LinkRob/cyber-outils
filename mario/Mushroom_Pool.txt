The '''Mushroom Pool''' is a location featured in the [[Seaside Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is where [[Cheep Cheep (character)|Cheep Cheep]] takes swimming lessons from his [[Coach (Mario Party Advance)|Coach]], the only character known to work at the Mushroom Pool.
