'''''Mario Undōkai''''' is an [[arcade]] game created by [[Banpresto]]. It involves the use of a dance pad for a few different minigames, although the overall game is not focused on music rhythm. Its use of a dance pad predates the commercially released ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.
