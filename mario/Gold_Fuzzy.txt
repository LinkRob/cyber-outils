The '''Gold Fuzzy''' is a mini-boss encountered in Chapter 1 of ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Physical description==
As suggested by its name, Gold Fuzzy has the exact appearance of a [[Fuzzy]] except it is golden in color.

==History==
The Gold Fuzzy is first seen after [[Mario]] gets both the [[Sun Stone]] and [[Moon Stone]] and tries to leave [[Shhwonk Fortress]]. The Gold Fuzzy sees Mario as a trespasser and engages him in battle. Gold Fuzzy was soon defeated, and he and his [[Fuzzy Horde]] leave.

==Battle==
During battle, the Gold Fuzzy can attack by simply jumping at Mario or his partner. Gold Fuzzy is the only Fuzzy in the ''Paper Mario'' series to not drain HP while attacking. The Gold Fuzzy can also summon a Fuzzy Horde that attacks with Fuzzies leaping at their target (they do not drain HP as well).

===Stats===
*HP: 10
*Attack: 1
*Defense: 0

===Strategy===
The best tactic to defeating the Gold Fuzzy is to focus on the Gold Fuzzy itself. Do not bother targeting the Fuzzy Horde as they have double the amount of HP the Gold Fuzzy does (20 HP) and they are defeated anyway after the Gold Fuzzy is defeated. The Gold Fuzzy and the Fuzzy Horde's attacks are simple to guard against making this battle rather easy.
