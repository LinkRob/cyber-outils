'''Crank to Rank''' is a 4-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party 8]]'', later reappearing in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.

==Gameplay==
Near Princess Peach's castle, the four players are seen standing in front of their flagpoles and must put their hands on the wheel on the flagpole. Then, the four players must crank the flagpole The first player to get their flag to the top of the flagpole will win the mini-game.
