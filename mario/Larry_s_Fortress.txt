'''Larry's Fortress''' is the eighth level of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 10.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 3 [[Parabeanie]]s, and 5 [[Lakitu]]. The enemy captain is a Parabeanie.

The 2nd battle contains 7 Parabeanies and 1 Lakitu. The enemy captain is the Lakitu.

The final battle contains 4 Lakitu, 1 Parabeanie, 2 [[Spear Guy]]s, and [[Larry Koopa|Larry]]. The enemy captain is Larry. Larry's [[Captain Command]] is [[Rally]] and [[Deny]].

After the final battle, the player unlocks Larry.

The player can replay the battle to unlock up to one Spear Guy and or Lakitu.
