The '''Meteor Curling Stone''' is an item in [[Adventure Tours]] of the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==Description==
The Meteor Curling Stone is required to challenge [[Dry Bowser]] in [[Curling Bowling]].

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
A [[Lakitu]] gives it to [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] after completing two tasks in any order: one is a [[Short Track 500m]] mission in [[Icepeak]], and the other is an [[Intense Short Track]] mission in [[Blizland]].
