'''Hot Fraun''' is a [[Flopside]] restaurant owned by [[Dyllis]] and it only appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
After completing Chapter 5,  [[Mario]] and [[Tippi]] can go to the Flopside which was available afterwards. One of the places were '''Hot Fraun'''. Mario comes in and talks to Dyllis who tells him to bring two ingredients to create a dish.{{SPMNav}}
