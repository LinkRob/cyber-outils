==''Yoshi'' franchise==
===''Yoshi''===
Hidden within the data of the [[Game Boy]] version of ''[[Yoshi (game)|Yoshi]]'' is an unused Bullet Bill which would've functioned similar to the [[Goomba]]s, [[Blooper]]s, [[Boo]]s, and [[Piranha Plant]]s in both that game and the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] version.<ref>[https://tcrf.net/Yoshi_(Game_Boy) Yoshi (Game Boy) - The Cutting Room Floor)]. ''TCRF''. Retrieved October 20, 2021.</ref> It is fully functional, though it turns into garbage graphics when it settles on a plate.

===''Yoshi's Safari''===
Bullet Bills are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]''. They are present in a few levels. They come at [[Mario]] head on and are shot down with one hit from the [[Super Scope]]. The Turtle Cannons are not visible, the bill's come from somewhere off screen. In this game the Bills come at the player head on, a perspective rarely seen of them.

===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
[[File:Bullet Bill SMW2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Bullet Bills appear as enemies in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|remake]], having their usual behavior of firing out of Bill Blasters and traveling straightforwardly. However, Bullet Bills look entirely different from their design, with features including a light-green body and a visible mouth with sharp teeth. A Bullet Bill can not only be defeated from being [[stomp]]ed, like in the {{series|Super Mario}}, but also from being swallowed by a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] and converted into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]. Alternatively, if a Yoshi catches a Bullet Bill into his mouth, he can spit it out as if the Bullet Bill were shot out of a Bill Blaster.

The game introduces two variants of Bullet Bill. There are yellow Bullet Bills called [[Bouncing Bullet Bill]]s, which can bounce off of walls, and red Bullet Bills called [[Biting Bullet Bill]]s, which home in on a Yoshi, the latter of which only appears in the remake though exists unused in the original.

Unused data shows that Bullet Bills had their usual black color, and they would have been able to bite while moving.<ref>{{cite web |url= https://tcrf.net/Development:Super_Mario_World_2:_Yoshi%27s_Island/Sprites#killer.CGX |title= Development:Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island/Sprites#killer.CGX |accessdate=2024-03-29 |format= Webpage |work=TCRF }}</ref>

====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
[[Image:Bullet Bill YIDS.png|frame|left]]
Bullet Bills make another appearance, but are now colored black like in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. A Bullet Bill can be defeated from using the same methods as in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''. While throwing an egg at a Bullet Bill or a Bill Blaster does not not affect them, this is still required for them to appear in the [[Island Museum]]. Bullet Bills first appear in the level, [[The Goonie Coast Isn't Clear!]].

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
Bullet Bills return as enemies, retaining their behavior from earlier installments of the ''Yoshi's Island'' series. Bullet Bills are first encountered in [[Diagonal Valley Rally]], followed by [[Hop 'n' Pop Till You Drop]], [[Flatbed Ferry Freefall]], [[Snow Go Mountain]], and lastly [[Spray Day Mayday]].

===''Yoshi'' platforming series===
====''Yoshi's Story''====
[[File:Yoshis Story Bullet Bill.png|thumb|120px|A Bullet Bill in ''Yoshi's Story'']]
[[File:BulletBillYS.gif|frame|left]]
In ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', Bullet Bills follow the same pattern as in other games. However, this game introduces tongues for the mechanical irritants. The Turtle Cannons of this game are slightly different in appearance and the Bullet Bills are golden in color.

====''Yoshi's Woolly World'' / ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''====
[[File:YWW Bullet Bill.jpg|thumb|200px|A Bullet Bill as seen in ''Yoshi's Woolly World'']]
Bullet Bills, along with Bill Blasters, make an appearance in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', adopting a knitted appearance rather than the usual metallic look. A new type of Bullet Bill known as [[Woollet Bill]]s also appear. While the artwork depicts them nearly identical to their ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' appearance, they take their main series design in-game.

====''Yoshi's Crafted World''====
Bullet Bills return in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'' with their modern appearance in the level [[Spinwheel Shuffle]].

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
{{Smashpedia|Bullet Bill}}
====''Super Smash Bros.''====
[[File:SSB Bullet Bill model.png|thumb|left|64px]]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'', one of [[Master Hand]]'s attacks involves firing pairs of Bullet Bills from his fingers. They are gold and resemble their design in the early ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platformers. In later games, Master Hand shoots generic bullets for this attack.

====''Super Smash Bros. Melee''====
In earlier revisions of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', the [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] of the Banzai Bill enemy from [[Princess Peach's Castle (Super Smash Bros. Melee)|Princess Peach's Castle]] is identified as "Bullet Bill" when the language is set to English. This was fixed in version 1.02 and the PAL version to match the Japanese text. Furthermore, in all revisions, the Banzai Bill's [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] incorrectly lists ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' as its game debut, when that is actually the debut of Bullet Bills. Banzai Bills actually debut in ''Super Mario World''.

====''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''====
[[File:Bullet Bill SSBB screenshot.png|thumb|Bullet Bills in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', Bullet Bills are encountered as enemies in a few stages of [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]], in which they are part of [[Bowser]]'s troops and, by extension, the [[Subspace Army]]. Bullet Bills first appear in the introductory cutscene for [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]], in which the latter destroys three Bullet Bills, causing them to explode.

During gameplay, a Bullet Bill appears onscreen from the right and continues traveling to the left, similarly to the 2D installments of the ''Super Mario'' series. Bullet Bills are shown to be larger than usual, but not to the extent of Banzai Bills. The player's fighter can no longer defeat Bullet Bills by jumping on them, but they can still use other physical moves to attack and eventually defeat them. A few Bullet Bills home in on the player's character rather than continuing to travel in a straight line, and they can be temporarily deflected.

====''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS''====
[[File:Bullet Bill SSB4 3DS.jpg|thumb|left|A Bullet Bill in Smash Run mode in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'']]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', Bullet Bills appear as an item in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} for the first time. Once picked up, the player briefly transforms into a Bullet Bill and attacks by ramming into opponents at full speed, comparable to the Bullet Bill item in the ''[[Mario Kart]]'' series. If the user hits a solid wall, including ceilings and floors, they explode, dealing additional damage to nearby players within the blast radius.

Bullet Bills are also enemies in [[Smash Run]]. They slowly fly toward the player and inflict damage by hitting them. Bullet Bills are also in an event that involves defeating as many enemies as possible, in which they also do not home in on opponents.
{{br}}
====''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U''====
[[File:Bullet Bill SSB4 Wii U.jpg|thumb|The Bullet Bill item in ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'']]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', Bullet Bills have the same role as in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'', minus the absence of the Smash Run mode. 

====''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''====
[[File:Bullet Bill SSB Ultimate artwork.png|thumb|left|''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' artwork]]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', in addition to reprising their role as items, Bullet Bills are also featured as a Novice-class Attack-type primary [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]], which can be enhanced to a [[Banzai Bill]] upon reaching level 99. In [[Adventure Mode: World of Light]], the spirit is encountered on the [[Light Realm]] map, and the puppet fighters are four small [[Captain Falcon]]s fought on the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Super Smash Bros.)|Mushroom Kingdom]] stage. Bullet Bill items spawn throughout the spirit battle.

A Bullet Bill appears as one of the drawings made by [[Nikki]], an [[Assist Trophy]]. After it is fully drawn, it launches off, damaging any opponent in its path.

===''Mario & Sonic'' series===
====''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'' (Nintendo DS)====
In the [[Nintendo DS]] [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Nintendo DS)|version]] of ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'', Bullet Bills appear as enemies in [[Airship (minigame)|Airship]]. They appear on most difficulty levels except level 1, in which there are only [[Egg Flapper|Flappers]].

====''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Wii)====
In the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', Bullet Bills appear in [[Dream Snowball Fight]], where they periodically appear in the area and knock down characters when they hit them, but can be turned around by hitting them with [[snowball]]s.

There is the large-sized [[Big Bullet Bill]] who appears as a rival in [[Skeleton (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games for Wii)|Skeleton]] in Single Festival and [[Bobsleigh (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games for Wii)|Bobsleigh]] in Team Festival. Big Bullet Bill also appears in the [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Nintendo DS version]] as a rival in [[Cubyrinth]] in [[Adventure Tours]] mode, although regular Bullet Bills do not appear in the Nintendo DS version at all.

====''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games'' (Nintendo 3DS)====
A Bullet Bill appears in the Story Mode of the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|version]] of ''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games'', where Bowser Jr. uses it to travel from one location to another. In [[Junior Hits England]], Bowser Jr. gets on a Bullet Bill to depart for [[London]], and in the opening cutscene of the next episode, [[Junior, Super Hero?]], Bowser Jr. accidentally crashes his Bullet Bill into both an [[Egg Pawn]] and an [[Egg Flapper]], defeating both of them.

A [[List of badges in Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|badge]] of a Bullet Bill can also be obtained from the badge machine.

====''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games''====
In ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'', Bullet Bills are featured prominently in the [[Bullet Bill Sledge Race]] event, where each two-seater bobsleigh is pulled by a pair of harnessed Bullet Bills, based on how reindeer pull [[Santa Claus]]'s sleigh.

====''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games'' (Nintendo 3DS)====
Bullet Bills appear as an item in [[Boxing Plus]] in the Nintendo 3DS version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'', where they deplete the opponent's [[Guard Gauge]]. Also, in [[Rhythmic Gymnastics Plus]], some Bullet Bills appear during the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' composition on expert difficulty, where they occasionally fire from offscreen to reveal the button required to input after traveling over their marker.

===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
Bullet Bills make an appearance as enemies in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. Their main attribute is Dark.

As enemies, Bullet Bills sometime deliver first strike blows, and tend to attack every single turn. They can even increase the ATK of every enemy on the field, making their attacks deal more damage than normal to the player.

As allies, Bullet Bills have a skill that halves the defense of all the enemies for three turns.

A Bullet Bill can be transformed into a [[Banzai Bill]] by giving it four [[Fire Flower]]s. Alternatively, giving a Bullet Bill three [[Goomba Coin]]s transforms it into a mash-up enemy, called Bullet Bill & [[Goomba]] (a solo Goomba on a Bullet Bill blaster). Six more Goomba Coins transforms Bullet Bill & Goomba into Bullet Bill & Goombas (two Goombas on a Bullet Bill blaster).

Bullet Bills have ATK as their highest stat, while their HP and RCV are very low.
