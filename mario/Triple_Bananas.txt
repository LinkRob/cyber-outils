'''Triple Bananas''' are an item which appear throughout the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They function like a regular [[Banana Peel|Banana]] item, but the user is given three Bananas, and upon using the item, these Bananas trail behind their [[kart]]. The Triple Banana is similar to the [[Triple Green Shells]] and [[Triple Red Shells]], which grant three [[Green Shell]]s or [[Red Shell]]s that circle the user's kart, respectively.

Triple Bananas first appear in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'', and have since replaced the [[Banana Bunch]]es from ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'', which function the same but yield five Bananas instead of three.
