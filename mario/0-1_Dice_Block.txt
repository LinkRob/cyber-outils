The '''0-1 Dice Block''' is an obtainable item in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

== Description ==
The player can obtain the 0-1 Dice Block by landing on a [[Dice Block Space]], [[Lucky Space]] or the [[Toad House]] or [[Spin Space]] by getting a chance of that Dice Block. Rolling a 0 will not make you move and any spaces will not affect the player. Rolling a 1 will move them 1 space and they are affected by spaces as normal.
