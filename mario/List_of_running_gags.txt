This article shows all of the '''running gags''' that appear throughout the {{franchise|Mario}} and media in relation to it. This could range from a series, such as ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'', to an entire franchise in general.

==Throughout the ''Mario'' franchise==
*[[Bowser]] kidnapping [[Princess Peach]] with [[Mario]] (and/or [[Luigi]]) having to save the day. This occurs in many games and is usually the main plot of the entire series.
*Peach getting kidnapped in general whether if it's by Bowser or some other character (like [[ Sir Grodus]], [[Princess Shroob]], [[Antasma]], etc.)
*In most games, the final level of a certain video game is usually [[Bowser's Castle]] (or any other Bowser-related location such as [[Neo Bowser Castle]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'').

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
*There are several instances of characters [[breaking the fourth wall]].
*The opening of the game takes place at [[Mario's Pad|Mario and Luigi's house]] (with the exception for ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'').
*A parade happens at the end of the game.
**This originates from [[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]
