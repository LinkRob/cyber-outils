The '''Toad General''' is a major character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', and its sequel ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
He is a blue Toad, sporting glasses and a bowtie. He also wears a shirt under his vest that looks similar to [[Toadsworth]]’s.

=== Speech ===
The Toad General speaks in a deep and more serious voice, in contrast to other Toads’ higher pitched voices.

== Biography ==
Before the movie began, a very young [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]] was found by other Toads who raised her in the Mushroom Kingdom. After things such as training went as planned, the Toad General crowned Peach as princess of the Mushroom Kingdom.

{{Quote|Good luck, [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess]]. For ALL [[Toad's species (Illumination)|our]] sakes.|Toad General, ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''[[File:Mario Movie But Only Toad General mp3cut.net 1 .ogg|300px]]}}
At Peach's castle, The General announces that Bowser has found the Super Star from the Snow Kingdom and will destroy the entire Mushroom Kingdom, explaining and demonstrating through an interactive 3D map (made of voxelized  versions of each kingdom) to the rest of his council and Princess Peach. Although [[Cranky Kong (Illumination)|Cranky Kong]] doesn't make alliances, he wished Princess Peach the best of luck on convincing him to save her kingdom, even with the help of [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]], to find his missing brother [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]]. But when Peach returned with the Kong army captured by Bowser, she told him and the other Toads to evacuate the kingdom into the forest. After Bowser's defeat and feeding of a [[Mini Mushroom]], the general and all the other Toads returned to the Kingdom.
