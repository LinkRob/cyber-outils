Winged Traitors is the 3rd level in [[Hoohoo Mountain]]. The level of the enemy squad is 2. It appeared in [[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]].

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 4 [[Paragoomba|Paragoombas]] and 2 [[Beanie|Beanies]]. The enemy captain is a Paragoomba.

The final battle contains 5 Paragoombas and 2 Beanies. The enemy captain is a Paragoomba.

After the final battle, the player unlocks 3 Paragoombas. If the player replays the battle, then they can unlock 2 more Paragoombas.
