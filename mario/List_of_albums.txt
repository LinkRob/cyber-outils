{{listnotice|albums featuring music from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] and related media}}

[[Nintendo]] has not published official sheet music, but it has officially licensed ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii: Simplified Piano Solos]]''. High demand for sheet music of the ''Mario'' franchise has led a number of fans to release their own arrangements online, though the fans often simplify or interpret the original version rather than accurately transcribe it.<ref>[http://www.mariopiano.com Mario Piano Sheet Music]</ref>
