The '''Hamburger''' is a recipe that can be made by [[Saffron]], the chef in [[Flipside]].
==Effects==

It is made by cooking only a [[Power Steak]]. It is simply the meat of a full hamburger with sauce on the top. When [[Dyllis]] mixes a Hamburger and Power Steak together, a [[Gorgeous Steak]] is created. The Gorgeous Steak is an item required to make the most powerful healing item in the game too. This item sells for around forty [[coin]]s. Like all healing items in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', it not only restores the amount said, in this case being twenty, it also restores poison.
