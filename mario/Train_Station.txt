The '''Train Station''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Train Station is a smoke-covered area where a black train can be seen docked in the background. Large, sturdy arches are suspended over the entire station. The only employee and likely owner of the Train Station is [[Shy Guy (Mario Party Advance)|Shy Guy]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The Train Station is located in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. At some point, the train runs out of [[Coal]], so when the player's character visits the Train Station, Shy Guy asks for their help to find Coal. If the player's character agrees to help, this starts the [[Locomotionless]] quest. Shy Guy cannot leave his post at the Train Station, hence why he requests the player's character to obtain Coal at the nearby [[Vending Machines]]. Once some Coal is brought to Shy Guy, he can start up the train again, and the player is rewarded the [[Cake Maker]] [[Gaddget]].
