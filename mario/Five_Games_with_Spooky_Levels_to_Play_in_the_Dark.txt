"'''Five Games with Spooky Levels to Play in the Dark'''," also spelled "'''5 Games with Spooky Levels to Play in the Dark'''," is a video uploaded to [[Play Nintendo]]'s YouTube channel on October 14, 2014. The video later received a successor titled "[[5 Games with Spooky Levels to Play on Halloween!]]" in 2018.

==Description==
[[File:It's time to grab a flashlight.jpg|thumb|left|180px|Thumbnail]]
The video shows footage of haunted levels in five different games. While it is not Halloween-themed, the video's haunted theme is because it was uploaded during the month of October. In order, the five main games featured include ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'', ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', ''[[w:c:kirby:Kirby: Triple Deluxe|Kirby: Triple Deluxe]]'' (the only non-''Mario'' game in the video), ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and lastly ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'', prior to its release.

The video is mainly themed after ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''. The first thing shown after the ESRB's "Everyone" certificate is a cutscene from ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' of Luigi entering the [[Gloomy Manor]] the first time and the doors closing behind him. After this, the game's theme song plays in the background throughout most of the video. It stops playing right before the screen at the end showing the games available.

At the very end of the video, a clip from ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' is shown in which Luigi looks up the [[Eerie Staircase]] before the [[Harsh Possessor]]'s sudden appearance, scaring Luigi.
