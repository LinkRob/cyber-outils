'''Toxic waste''' is a hazardous object which appears in some ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' games. It first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and returns in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.
