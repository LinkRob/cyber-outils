'''Glass Blocks''' are an object in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Description==
Glass Blocks have the appearance of small, translucent [[block]]s. [[Wario]] can pick up a Glass Block to either throw at enemies or to break [[Enemy Block]]s. A Glass Block is very fragile, due to being made up of glass, and it breaks upon either falling to the ground or being attack. A Glass Block can respawn if Wario goes off-screen and then returns to where the Glass Block was found.

==In ''Wario Land II''==
Glass Blocks first appear in the level [[Defeat Four Ducks!]]. They appear in various rooms throughout [[Syrup Castle]]. Wario has to use the Glass Blocks to knock down one of the four [[D.D.]]s.
