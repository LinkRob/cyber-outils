'''Queen Mushroomkhamen''' is a who made a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Mind Your Mummy Mommy, Mario]]." She has a son named [[Prince Mushroomkhamen]] and a husband named [[King Mushroomkhamen]]. She mistakes [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] for being her son, Prince Mushroomkhamen, due to them having a similar appearance. While King Mushroomkhamen is never seen, Queen Mushroomkhamen states that [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] looks like him.

Like her husband and son, Queen Mushroomkhamen is based on real royalty of [[Egypt|Ancient Egypt]]. Their last name is derived from the real {{w|King Tutankhamen}}, a pharaoh of Ancient Egypt, but with the "Tutan" prefix substituted for "[[Mushroom]]" to fit the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' theme.
