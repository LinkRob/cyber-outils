'''Flit''' is one of the [[human]] tennis players featured in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. Flit takes tennis seriously, and he is intensely determined to rise through the ranks. At first, Flit had [[Skipper]] as his doubles partner, but he was replaced by [[Roy (Mario Tennis: Power Tour)|Roy]] later on.
