'''Nintendo Monopoly''' is a [[Nintendo]]-themed variation of [[wikipedia:Monopoly|Monopoly]].

== Description ==
The game features characters from Nintendo franchises, including ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', and ''[[Animal Crossing]]''.
