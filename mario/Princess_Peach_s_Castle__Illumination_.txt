==History==
===''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''===
Peach's Castle is first seen when Toad points to Mario in the distance that the princess is located there, and they head towards that general direction to see her. After travelling through Toad Town, they go through a series of warp pipes before finally making it to the top. The Toad Guards who were guarding the doors refused to let them in, so Toad distracted them by prepping some cooked vegetables, allowing Mario to bypass them and make it in.

However, he is quickly detected by some other guards inside the castle, before encountering the princess herself. Meanwhile, the princess, Peach views an incoming attack from Bowser, and tells her Toads about her plan to travel to the Jungle Kingdom for assistance. When she walks out, Peach then finds Mario, whom explains to her that he needs her help to rescue his brother. Peach then takes Mario to the back of the castle to an obstacle course to see if he is worthy enough. Eventually seeing his skills, Peach agrees to take him along.

Sometime later during the movie, after losing Mario and the Kong army, Peach drives back to her castle to denounce the loss of the army, much to the others' shock. She then orders the Toads to evade the castle and retreat into the Mushroom Forest as Bowser arrives.

After Bowser's wedding fails before the climax, the Koopa King orders a Bomber Bill to be launched at the castle's location, much to the monarch's distress. Mario then safely redirects the missle before it could come into contact with the castle.

In the post-credits of the movie, it is revealed that after Bowser was shrunken, he was transferred to a birdcage inside the castle, with the only thing accompanying him being his piano. Here, he is seen playing on it before being shushed by a Toad guard for playing too loudly.

===''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''===
In the sequel, the caslte was abducted by a giant [[UFO]], controlled by Bowser Jr. trying to get his father out of prison, forcing all the Toads to evacuate, except for Mario, Luigi, and Yoshi. After the battle, [[Megaleg (Illumination)|Megaleg]] was destroyed and Bowser Jr. was back on the ship, and the castle crashed and was destroyed on [[Honeyhive Galaxy (Illumination)|Honeyhive Galaxy]].

After Rosalina is saved, the Lumas ask to see Peach's Castle, but Luigi claims that it "died" (in reference to its destruction in the Honeyhive Galaxy). But in the end with Rosalina's help Mario, Luigi, Yoshi, and Peach put her castle back together.
