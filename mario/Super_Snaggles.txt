'''Super Snaggles''' (also called '''Yellow Snaggles''' or '''Jumping Nibbles''') are a yellow subspecies of the [[Snaggles]] first appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''. They behave almost identically to regular Snaggles although periodically, they also possess the ability to preform much faster and higher jumps making them a more complicated obstacle to dodge. They are immune to all direct attacks but vulnerable to projectiles such as [[Barrel]]s.

==History==
===''Donkey Kong Country Returns / Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D / Donkey Kong Country Returns HD''===
Super Snaggles first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' and later go on to appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD]]''. In these games, they commonly appear as enemies in the [[Beach (Donkey Kong Country Returns)|Beach]] and the [[Ruins (Donkey Kong Country Returns)|Ruins]] lingering in the water and leaping out periodically attempting to bite [[Donkey Kong]], [[Diddy Kong]], and [[Dixie Kong]] as they leap across, however, unlike a regular Snaggles, a Super Snaggles may also leap with a fast and high jump which can throw the Kongs off, they commonly appear below [[Climbing Grass]] attempting to knock the Kongs into the water below. Super Snaggles are commonly invincible however they can be beaten with the use of a [[Barrel]]. In ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD]]'', Super Snaggles also appear in the new level [[Gushin' Geysers]].

===''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze''===
Super Snaggles (under the name '''Yellow Snaggles''' in this game) reappear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]'' and [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze (Nintendo Switch)|it's Switch port]], in these games, they behave identically to how they did in the previous game, taking leaps out of the water in attempt to hit [[Donkey Kong]], [[Diddy Kong]], [[Dixie Kong]], and [[Cranky Kong]] and just as before, posses the ability to make very high and fast leaps to throw the Kongs off, just as before, a Yellow Snaggles can be beaten with a [[Barrel]] but if the Kongs are near one and DK preforms a [[Kong POW]] with either Diddy, Dixie, or Cranky, the Yellow Snaggles can turn into either an [[Extra Life Balloon]], a [[Yellow Heart]], or a [[Banana Coin]] depending on who DK's current partner is at the time. When the Kongs swim underwater, a Yellow Snaggles will chase after them although the Yellow Snaggles is much more aggressive than a regular Snaggles underwater but can still be beaten with a whack from Cranky's cane. Yellow Snaggles appear in [[Lost Mangroves]], [[Sea Breeze Cove]], and [[Juicy Jungle]].

==Trivia==
*The Prima Guide for ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'' referring to Super Snaggles as Jumping Nibbles is an oddity as leaping is a common trait shared among the Snaggles species, however it’s naming could also be due to its high jumping abilities.
*The Super Snaggles is one of the few ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' enemies to return in ''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]''
