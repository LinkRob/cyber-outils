'''Cheep Cheep Coin''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
A certain number of Cheep Cheep Coins can be used to transform a few certain different types of enemies into stronger forms, including a [[Red Koopa Troopa]] into a "Red Koopa Troopa & Cheep Cheep" for three Cheep Cheep Coins as well as a [[Blooper]] into "Blooper & Cheep Cheep," also for three Cheep Cheep Coins, and it is possible to upgrade it to "Blooper & Cheep Cheeps" by using six more.

Cheep Cheep Coins can be obtained by defeating certain enemies or by winning them in the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] game in [[Toad House]]s.
