'''Plucky''' is an allied character in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''<nowiki>'s</nowiki> Nintendo Switch 2 port, ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park]]''. He is the pet bird of the [[Toad Brigade]], and is used as an information gatherer. Like the [[Recruit Poplin]], Plucky is not featured in the base version of ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''.

==Profile==
===Personality===
Due to his inability to speak, Plucky is largely enigmatic regarding his personality. However, his actions show him to be quite loyal to the [[Toad Brigade]] as well as brave (hence his name). 

=== Physical Appearance ===
Plucky is a yellow bird who wears a red kerchief. His beak is dark brown.

== History ==

=== In ''Super Mario Run'' ===
Plucky made his debut in [[Super Mario Run|''Super Mario Run'']], which featured a statue that depicts him carrying [[Captain Toad]]. Plucky's appearance here is to advertise his role in ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park''.

=== In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park'' ===
Plucky makes his actual debut in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park'']], where he can be found at the Toad Brigade Training Camp perched on a mailbox where the [[Recruit Poplin]] sends his data on the [[Koopalings]]. He also appears in the courses where [[Mario (character)|Mario]] confronts the Koopalings themselves, where Plucky drops a [[Hatena Capsule]] that contains a [[Power-up]], such as an [[Elephant Fruit]] in [[Lemmy Koopa]]'s stage or a [[Super Flower Pot]] in [[Wendy O. Koopa]]'s stage.
