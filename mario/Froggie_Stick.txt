The '''Froggie Stick''' is one of [[Mallow]]'s personal weapons in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be obtained from [[Frogfucius]] by trading him a [[Cricket Pie]] for it. Equipping the Froggie Stick increases Mallow's [[Attack]] power by 20. The Froggie Stick can be sold for 18 [[coin]]s, although it cannot be replaced.

The Froggie Stick has a stronger variation named [[Ribbit Stick]], which uses the same sprite as the Froggie Stick.
