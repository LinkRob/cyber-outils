|first = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' (2013)|games = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}The '''Front Yard''' is an area found in the [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It marks the first-ever area explored by [[Luigi]] in this game.

==History==

During the first mission [[Gloomy Manor#A-1: Poltergust 5000|A-1: Poltergust 5000]] Luigi makes his way to the front door of the mansion. However, it is locked but soon, a mouse runs out holding the key with its tail. Luigi catches the key and unlocks the front door to Gloomy Manor leading to the first official room: the [[Entrance]].

Unlike most areas in the game, the Front Yard is actually one of few that is visited once since after the first mission, E. Gadd starts pixelating Luigi directly to the Entrance.
