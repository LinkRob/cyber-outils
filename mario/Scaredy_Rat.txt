'''Scaredy Rats''' are a recurring variant of [[Little Mouser]] in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==In [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' sub-series]]==
===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[File:Scaredy Rat NSMB Wii render.png|frame|left]]
Scaredy Rats are commonly found in [[Ghost House]]s. They are quite the annoyance as they are usually seen running around on short edges where players could easily get knocked back from them. If Mario stomps on them they start running fast requiring a second stomp to defeat them.
{{br|left}}
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''===
Scaredy Rats retain a similar purpose as in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''. They only appear in Ghost Houses and jumping out from dark edges.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''[[Mario Party 9]]''===
A Scaredy Rat appears during a Captain Event on the [[Boo's Horror Castle]] map. In this event, a Scaredy Rat steals five [[Mini Star]]s from every character and tries to escape by rolling [[Dice Block]]s. If the Scaredy Rat is caught by the current captain, it loses the stolen Mini Stars, but if the Scaredy Rat makes it to the end, it successfully steals every Mini Star that it took.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''===
[[File:Scaredy Rat PMSS.png|frame|left]]
Scaredy Rats only appear in [[The Engimansion]]. They are usually seen running around in the hallways. Unlike most enemies, Scaredy Rats more actually ingore Mario not bothering to chase after him in hopes of a fight. In battle, Scaredy Rats have two attacks: a simple Jump attack and an charge attack that steals one of Mario's stickers. If Mario doesn't defeat the Scaredy Rat in time, it will run off with his Sticker.
{{br|left}}
==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''==
[[File:Scaredy Rat M&LPJ sprite.png|frame|left]]
A Scaredy Rat was seen in the attic of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] when Luigi and Toad spotted it. The Scaredy Rat started to chase around Toad and frightens Luigi to where he bumps into the bookcase behind him and releases all of the Paper characters from the book.
