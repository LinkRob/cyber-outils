'''Blanka '''is a beast-like character hailing from Brazil from the [[Wikipedia:Street Fighter (series)|Street Fighter]] series. He is [[Mario]]'s third opponent in the ''[[Club Nintendo (magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' comic "[[Super Mario Klemp-Won-Do: Muskeln sind nicht alles!]]."


== In ''[[Club Nintendo]]'' ==
The third fight to win Street Fighter II grand master starts, and [[Toad]] arrives to assist Mario. Mario is given [[bananas]], and he spreads them across the ring's floor. Blanka accidentally slips on one of the banana peels and falls right out of the boxing ring, getting himself disqualified.

Blanka was also one of the fighters that laughed at Mario before the tournament started.

== In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' ==
Blanka appeared as a spirit in ''Ultimate''.
