An '''Enemy Course''' is a type of level featured in a few [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] games. Enemy Courses first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as its variation ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and [[reissue|remake]] ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
Enemy Courses are single-screen areas that [[Mario]] or another playable character forcefully enter if they bump into an enemy on the world map. There, they are forced to fight one or more enemies of that type. Although the playable characters are forced to enter an Enemy Course upon encountering an enemy, the areas themselves are optional, because they can be skipped if the playable characters manage to avoid the enemy on the world map. If Mario or another playable character defeats every enemy in an Enemy Course, they are rewarded a power-up item, and the enemy stops appearing on the world map. If Mario or another character lose a life in an Enemy Course, they are knocked back to where they came from on to the world map, where the enemy continues to roam around.

==History==
===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', every single World (except [[World 9 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9]], has at least one Enemy Course, in the enemy course, when [[Mario]] and his friends run into an enemy on the map, they are pulled into a small level with that enemy and must collect 8 [[Toad Balloon]]s within the Time limit of 100 seconds, there are three variations of each enemy course too, if Mario and his friends have a power-up they can eliminate the enemies easily, when the enemies are destroyed, getting the Toad Balloons is much easier, but some enemies may respawn or come in infinite supplies, once all eight Toad Balloons are collected, a [[Treasure chest]] will appear and when opened, a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] will emerge and award three [[Super Mushroom]]s which are added to your inventory, the enemy will also be eliminated from the world map for a few levels too. If [[Mario]] and his friends are under the effects of a [[Star]] on the world map, they can plow through an enemy on the map without being dragged into the enemy course.
