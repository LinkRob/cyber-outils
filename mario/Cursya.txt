A '''Cursya''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is a monster that will inflict curse when touched. The name may be a pun of the phrase, "curse you".

==History==
Cursyas are rather troublesome enemies if one isn't careful around them. Cursyas are found in many areas of the game including [[Merlee's Basement]] and [[Floro Caverns]]. Touching a regular Cursya directly will cause the victim to be slowed down. There are also different versions of Cursyas in the game. The most dangerous one is the dreaded [[Back Cursya]] as touching it directly sends the player back to [[Flipside]]. Whenever dealing with Cursyas, it is highly recommended to attack from a distance. Using [[Bowser]]'s fire breath or [[Boomer]] are the best methods to taking out Cursyas without fearing of being cursed.
