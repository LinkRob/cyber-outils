'''Bubbles''', also known as '''Groopy'''<ref>''VS. Clu Clu Land'' / ''Clu Clu Land D'' "All Star Casts" attract mode</ref> or '''Gloopy''',<ref>''Arcade Archives Clu Clu Land'' manual</ref> is a red bubble fish who appears in the [[Nintendo]] game ''{{wp|Clu Clu Land}}''. She has also made various appearances in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]].
==History==
===''VS. Pinball''===
[[File:VS. Pinball gameplay.png|thumb|170px|''VS. Pinball'']]
Bubbles appears in the international version of ''[[VS. Pinball]]'', replacing the red [[Achilles]] from the Japanese version as the character that returns the ball into play if the player hatches three pink [[chick]]s and the ball falls below the bottom of [[scene B]]. This appearance predates her appearance in ''Clu Clu Land'' by about a month. She is depicted wearing no gloves and blue or green shoes instead of red gloves and orange shoes.

Since ''[[Arcade Archives]] Pinball'' only emulates the original Japanese version of ''VS. Pinball'', Bubbles does not appear in that rerelease.

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
Bubbles makes a cameo in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] and in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a [[spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]].

According to series director [[Masahiro Sakurai]], before the [[Ice Climbers]] were chosen to represent the "retro" gaming era, Bubbles was considered as a potential character instead, among other candidates such as the fighter from ''[[w:c:nintendo:Urban Champion|Urban Champion]]'', [[Balloon Fighter]] from ''[[w:c:nintendo:Balloon Fight|Balloon Fight]]'', an [[Excitebike Racer]], and Ayumi Tachibana from the ''[[w:c:nintendo:Famicom Detective Club|Famicom Detective Club]]'' series. Sakurai decided against Bubbles because he did not know how she would fight.<ref>Soma (April 29, 2016). [http://sourcegaming.info/2016/04/29/duflupdate/ The Definitive Unused Fighters List in Smash]. ''Source Gaming''. Retrieved August 15, 2021.</ref>

====''WarioWare: Twisted!''====
Bubbles appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' in [[Clu Clu Land (WarioWare: Twisted!)|Clu Clu Land]], one of [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s. The player must rotate the screen to guide Bubbles to the item. If Bubbles misses the item, the player loses the microgame.

====''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''====
Bubbles appears in another microgame named [[Clu Clu Land (WarioWare: Smooth Moves)|Clu Clu Land]] in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', where she must navigate through the level while avoiding [[Unira]]s and black holes.

====''WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase''====
In ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]'', Bubbles appears in [[Clu Clu Land (WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase)|Clu Clu Land]], a microgame in 18-Volt's microgame set. The microgame involves Bubbles and some [[Unira]]s among some turnposts. Bubbles moves across the area, and the player must press {{button|wii|A}} while pointing a turnpost near Bubbles. She grabs the turnpost and changes her direction. The player must avoid the on-screen Uniras until time is up.
{{br}}

===''DK: King of Swing''===
[[File:DKKOSBubblesSprite.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Bubble DKKoS select.png|frame]]
Bubbles ends up on [[Donkey Kong Island]] during the events of ''[[DK: King of Swing]]''. Bubbles is the most well-rounded character in the game, and she has balanced stats of four in both [[Jump]] and Attack. To unlock Bubbles, the player must clear Diddy Kong Mode and collect all 24 medals. Bubbles can only be used in Jungle Jam Events.

===''Super Mario Maker''===
[[File:SMM-SMB-Bubbles.png|frame|left]]
Bubbles appears as an unlockable [[Mystery Mushroom]] costume for [[Costume Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. The costume was added in version 1.40, and can be unlocked by clearing [[Super Mario Maker#100 Mario Challenge|100 Mario Challenge]] on Super Expert difficulty.
