'''Helipopper''' is a Battle [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
The camera turns around from the front to the back of the first character's helicopter, then the screen divides into four to show all four characters.

==Gameplay==
The characters each have to fly around in a [[helicopter]] and pop [[balloon]]s in the sky to earn [[point]]s within the 45-second [[time limit]]. There are some balloon bunches in the area worth three points. The characters sometimes encounter a [[Bullet Bill]], which they must avoid, otherwise it stuns them temporarily. Whoever ends with the most points wins, but if every character ends with the same score, the minigame ends in a [[draw]].

Helipopper is one of the minigames that can appear in [[Decathlon Castle]], where the object is to pop as many balloons as possible to earn as many points as possible, up to 1,000.

==Ending==
The screen switches to the winner's helicopter, shown at a top-diagonal view. If more than one character has won, the screen stays split, but only to show the winners at a top diagonal view. The losing characters' helicopters are seen falling to the ground. If a draw occurs, all of the helicopters fall to the ground.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''Fly the helicopter and pop as many balloons as possible.''
* '''Advice''': ''Try to hit the balloon bunches. They are worth 3 points each.''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The minigame's name is a pun on "helicopter" and "pop."
