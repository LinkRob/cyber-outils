'''Jelly Shroom''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:JellyShroomPM.png|thumb|left]]
Jelly Shroom is a [[recipe]] that [[Mario]] can help make by giving a [[Jammin' Jelly]] and a [[Mushroom (Paper Mario series)|Mushroom]] to [[Tayce T.]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. This item has the same effects as its ingredients respectively, and the Jelly Shroom restores five [[Heart Point|HP]] and fifty [[Flower Point|FP]].

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
Jelly Shroom is created by [[Zess T.]] using the same ingredients required in ''Paper Mario''. The Jelly Shroom also retains its effects from ''Paper Mario''.
