'''Croctopus''' is an aquatic enemy found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''. It comes in two variants, appearing as blue and yellow or purple and yellow.



Croctopus always chases [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong]]. The blue and yellow type follows them along whatever path they take, but the purple and yellow type have a specific path along which they swim. Croctopus rotates its body as it swims, making it very speedy. Even [[Enguarde the Swordfish|Enguarde]] cannot defeat it.
