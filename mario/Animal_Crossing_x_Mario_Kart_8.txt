'''''Animal Crossing x Mario Kart 8''''' is one of the downloadable content packs for ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. It was released on April 23, 2015. The pack was included in the base game for ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. Most of its content is focused on the ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' franchise, and includes a [[Animal Crossing (track)|course]] themed after the series. The pack also introduces [[Villager]] and [[Isabelle]], alongside [[Dry Bowser]] from the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise.
