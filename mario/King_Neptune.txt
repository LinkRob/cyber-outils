'''King Neptune''' is a one-time character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', in which he only appears in the episode "[[Mario of the Deep]]." King Neptune is the ruler of [[Aqua Land]], inhabited by [[Mermushroom]]s, although King Neptune is closer to a regular merfolk.
