The '''Doom Ship''' is an [[Airship]] used by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It has heavy artillery like a normal airship, but also possesses a warp drive, meaning it can warp to other locations. [[Ludwig von Koopa|Kooky von Koopa]] is credited to be its creator according to the bonus disc on the Shout! Factory DVD set. It has a submarine version called the [[Doom Sub]].
