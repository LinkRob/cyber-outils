'''King Sammer''' is the ruler of the [[Sammer's Kingdom|Sammer Kingdom]]. He only appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Biography==
King Sammer controlled legions of [[Sammer Guys]] who are sworn to protect him. When Mario enters the Sammer Kingdom, King Sammer initiates a challenge to Mario forcing him to face his one hundred Sammer Guys before the King handed over the [[Pure Heart]]. Mario accepts the challenge. 

[[Mimi]] put King Sammer in slumber. At the 26th gate, Mimi impersonated King Sammer, who fought Mario and co. Not long after this fight, [[The Void]] destroys King Sammer and his Kingdom. After returning to the world after its destruction, Mario retrieves the Pure Heart after a fight with [[Mr. L]].

After all worlds are saved, Mario can take on the challenge again. Once all the 100 Sammer Guys are defeated, King Sammer will congratulates tone of the [[Four Heroes of Light]] and granted the [[Catch Card]]s.

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
After all worlds are saved, the player can take on the challenge again. Once all the 100 Sammer Guys are defeated, King Sammer will congratulate the player and reward him with a few [[Catch Card]]s with characters from [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]].
