'''World 8-3''' is the third level of [[World 8 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 8]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. The player is given a time limit of 300 seconds to complete it.

== Layout ==
This is an overworld course that features several castle backdrops. The player will encounter several [[Hammer Brother]]s throughout, plus several [[Bullet Bill]]s and [[Bill Blaster]]s. There are only three [[pipe]]s, each of which contain [[Piranha Plant]]s and there are no secret areas. Unlike other typical courses, at the end is nothing other than a few floating stone blocks that the player must carefully leap across to reach the [[flagpole]].

== Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' ==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 28000.

=== Red Coin locations ===
* Red Coin #1 is located at the beginning, directly above the fortress. To the left is a Hidden Block that will help the player reach it.
* Red Coin #2 is located where the player encounters the first duo of Hammer Brothers. It is above the top row of Brick Blocks.
* Red Coin #3 is located on the small stone steps.
* Red Coin #4 is located where the player encounters the second duo of Hammer Brothers. It is found inside a new [[Coin Block]], in the center of the top row of Brick Blocks.
* Red Coin #5 is located at the end of the course, above the floating stone blocks.

=== Yoshi Egg location ===
* The Yoshi Egg is found inside a [[Hidden Block]], located directly above the left of the castle backdrop, following the second set of Hammer Brothers.
