A '''Rob-omb''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Rob-ombs are a variant of [[Bob-omb]] that can be found in [[Booster Tower]] and [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]].

==Battle==
In battle, Rob-ombs, like most Bob-omb varieties, only attack by self-destructing and dealing high damage to a single character.

===Strategy===
Rob-ombs are weak to both [[Jump]] and [[Fire (element)|fire]] attacks, so any of [[Mario]]'s special moves are effective against them. It is recommended to take out Rob-ombs before they explode.
