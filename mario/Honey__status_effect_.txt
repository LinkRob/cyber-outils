'''Honey''' is a status effect in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
There are weapons that can inflict honey on a foe. Whoever is affected by Honey cannot move for one turn. Honey will last until the next turn. An ally performing a team jump on the inflicted target can remove Honey.
