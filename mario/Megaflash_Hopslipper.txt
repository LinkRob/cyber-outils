'''Megaflash Hopslipper''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It's Mario's strongest Hopslipper sticker and is very powerful and rare. One can only be found in [[Chomp Ruins]] in a secret room. Megaflash Hopslipper combined with a [[Super Boot]] sticker is ideal for the final fight with [[Bowser]].
==Description==
The Megaflash Hopslipper is a much larger version of a [[Flashy Hopslipper]]. It takes up over four sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].
