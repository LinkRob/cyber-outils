==Effects==
These items are only made by [[Tayce T.]], the cook in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. [[Mario]] helps creates this item by collecting both a [[Stinky Herb]] and a [[Egg]] (which are found in [[Flower Fields]] and [[Mt. Rugged]] respectfully). Then, he must give both of these items to Tayce. Tayce T. cooks Mario's items and gives him the Boiled Egg in return. This item recovers 8 [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]], more than the two items to make it give.
