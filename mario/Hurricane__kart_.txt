The '''Hurricane''' is one of [[Bowser]]'s karts in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', with the other two being [[Tyrant]] and his standard kart, [[Standard BW]].

== Description ==
It is based of the [[Koopa Clown Car]], equipped with dual engine propellers, and a larger propeller in the middle with two split sides for Bowser's emblem (unless it is changed by the player).

== In ''Mario Kart DS'' ==
[[File:MKDS Hurricane.PNG|thumb|left|Bowser in his Hurricane]]
The Hurricane is unlocked by completing all Nitro [[Grand Prix]] cups in [[Engine Class|100cc]]. At first, only Bowser can use this kart, but [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[R.O.B.]] can use the Hurricane once the player completes all Nitro or Retro Grand Prix cups in 150cc, and all other racers can use the Hurricane once all cups in the Mirror Class have been completed.

It has the second highest top speed in the game only being rivaled by R.O.B.'s [[ROB-BLS]], and has low acceleration, low handling, high drift, and low items. Its generally better than Bowser's other karts with the exception of weight and items.

Its other stats that match is drift which rivals [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s [[Mushmellow]], and its items stat matchs with all other characters' lighter karts.

Bowser in the Hurricane is used as the staff ghost for courses [[Airship Fortress]], with a time of 2:07:748, and [[Bowser's Castle]] with a time of 2:19:661.
