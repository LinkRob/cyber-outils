|name=Huge Jump (Battle Card)}}'''Huge Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Huge Jump is the super-strong version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a huge brown boot with a orange/tan spiral background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 36/170 coins

Sell Price: 18/85 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Huge Jump is first seen in [[Green Energy Plant]], but can also found in [[Black Bowser's Castle]].
