'''Wiggly''' is a character and boss that only appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He is a large male [[Wiggler]] that was seen in [[Wakeport]] behind [[Pi'illoper]]'s house. Prior to his battle, Wiggly wanted to build a his flower garden but [[Popple]] (who intended to rob the home) was convinced that Wiggly was actually a security guard hired by Pi'illoper. Due to this, when [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] arrive in the area to reach the thouse, Popple (who formed a "truce" with the plumbers) caused Wiggly to attack Mario and Luigi with his rudeness and brash remarks. After the battle, Popple insults Wiggly for making a flower garden, only to be chased off before he can rob the place; Popple quickly calls it quits with the [[Pi'illo Island|island]] soon after.

== Appearance==
Wiggly resembles an ordinary Wiggler except that he's a little bigger in size.

==Battle==
In battle, Wiggly mainly attacks by launching shockwaves (which must be jumped) at Mario and Luigi (and Popple too). Wiggly constantly gets annoyed by Popple where he will turn red and begin to chase Mario and Luigi. His other attack comes if Popple climbs up and tries to pick the flower from him: Wiggly uses the shockwave attack and rears back, causing the Shadow Thief to fall like a meteor. Mario and Luigi can use their hammer to block this.

===Tips===
The fight with Wiggly isn't tough except that Popple is a constant annoyance where he will jump on Mario or Luigi's backs causing them to jump slower or even knock one of them away when Wiggly charges at them. Using Bros. Moves such as [[Bye-Bye Cannon]] is very useful in this fight. Take note that as soon Wiggly is defeated, Mario and Luigi must then fight Popple right after.

===Stats===
In battle, Wiggly is considered to be a Level 26 enemy that has 1485 HP, 256 Power, 188 Defense, and a Speed of 78. When defeated, he drops 400 [[Coin]]s and has a 1/2 chance to drop a regular [[1-Up Mushroom]] and a 3/10 chance to drop a [[Max Star Candy]].
