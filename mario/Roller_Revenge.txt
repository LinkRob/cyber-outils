'''Roller Revenge''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Gameplay==
The characters, who are on a square-shaped platform, have to dodge [[Spike Roller]]s that [[Spike]]s throw at them. Every character starts off with three [[extra life|lives]], and a character loses one if they either off the stage or get hit by a Spiked Roller. If a character loses all of their lives, they are eliminated. If a character falls off and still has health remaining, they return in a [[bubble]], similarly to ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. Whoever is either the last character remaining or has most lives remaining when the 60-second [[time limit]] runs out is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|3DS|Stick}}: Move
*{{button|3DS|B}}: Attack
*{{button|3DS|A}}: Jump
