A '''Count-Lift''', sometimes named a '''Countdown Platform''', is an object which first appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
A Count-Lift is a dirt-like platform covered with grass on the top, somewhat like a dug up patch of dirt, and has a countdown timer in the middle. Whenever [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] [[jump]] onto a Count-Lift, it moves to the right in a straightforward path while counting down. When the timer reaches zero, the Count-Lift falls. A Count-Lift can only be stood on for a few seconds, with the exact number usually being one second.

==In the games==
In ''Super Mario World'', Count-Lifts only appear in one level, [[Valley of Bowser 3]].

In ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 3]]'', a Count-Lift makes an appearance in the modern remake of [[Turtle Bridge]].
