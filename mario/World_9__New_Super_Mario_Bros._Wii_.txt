'''World 9''' is the final world and a secret world of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. The world is set in space and on a giant rainbow star. Each level in the world is gated and cannot be played until all [[Star Coin]]s from a certain world are found. Therefore, it is optional to play through. There are, just like most other worlds, eight levels. World 9-8, the final one, has giant [[Bullet Bill]]s called [[King Bill]]s. These things cannot be stomped on or defeated but can be evaded. 

== How to unlock ==
The World is unlocked when [[Bowser]] is defeated. Although to unlock the levels, Mario must collect all the star coins in one world to unlock one level.
