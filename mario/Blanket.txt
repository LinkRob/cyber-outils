|notes = Must be taken to the conductor after receiving it from [[Ghost T.]]}}The '''Blanket''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
While [[Mario]] is aboard the [[Excess Express]], he discovers that the [[Excess Express Conductor|conductor]] is a Blanket short. Mario searches for suspicions in the whole train. In Cabin 004, he uses [[Vivian]]'s [[Veil]] to go underground. [[Ghost T.]] appears after a few seconds. He asks Mario to find his long-lost [[Ragged Diary|diary]] in the baggage room. Mario returns it, receives the Blanket, and returns the Blanket to the conductor.
