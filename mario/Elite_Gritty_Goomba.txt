'''Elite Gritty Goombas''' are enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are stronger variants of [[Gritty Goomba]]s, and originally had the same name as them in the original [[Game Boy Advance]] version. Elite Gritty Goombas are encountered at [[Gwarhar Lagoon]].

Elite Gritty Goombas, like Gritty Goombas, are covered in bandages and have a spike with a flag strapped to their back. The only visual differences with Elite Gritty Goombas are that their bandages are brown and that the flag wrapped around their spike is green.

==Battle==
Elite Gritty Goombas are accompanied by [[Elite Troopea]]s. They do not appear on the map. They have the same behavior as the Gritty Goombas, but they are faster.

===Strategy===
They take regular damage on any attack but they cannot be stomped.
