'''Pirate Guys'''<ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' Nintendo Player's Guide. Page 61.</ref><ref>''Yoshi's Island DS'' [[Prima Games|Prima]] guide, page 153.</ref> are seafaring types of [[Shy Guy]]s appearing as enemies in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They wear white robes and either a red, blue, green, or yellow bandanna.

==History==
In ''Yoshi's Island DS'', Pirate Guys appear along with those who [[Pirate Guy Stack|stack]] themselves on top of each other. They are like normal Shy Guys, except they are all white with purple shoes, wear differently colored bandanas and appear in beach-themed stages. They can be found in the level [[The Goonie Coast Isn't Clear!]].
