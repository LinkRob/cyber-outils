'''Jackpot+ Space''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Description==
Jackpot+ Spaces a rare type of [[space]] exclusively appearing on [[Bowser Station]]. There is either a five or an eight printed by a Jackpot+ Space's plus sign, and landing on one adds the corresponding number of [[Mini Star]]s to the [[Jackpot Machine]], and when it reaches twenty Mini Stars, a Free-for-All [[minigame]] is initiated for players to win the Mini Stars.
