==Effects==
Miracle Dinners are a rather unique dish as they can only be made from [[Mystery|Mystery Boxes]]. Eating a Miracle Dinner restores only 1 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]] but adds 2000 points. Making a Miracle Dinner is very difficult as cooking Mystery Boxes usually lead to [[Mistake]]s or other dishes. [[Saffron]] is the chef that can make Miracle Dinners.
