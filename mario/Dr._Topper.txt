'''Dr. Topper''' is a green [[Hammer Bro]] character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==History==
Dr. Topper is only seen in [[Bowser's Keep]] late into [[Mario]]'s adventure, and he is working with [[Smithy]]. He can be found in the area with the six red doors, where he acts in charge of the puzzle rooms. Mario is forced to participate in Dr. Topper's various games, and Mario has to complete at least four out of six challenges to be able to continue his journey.
