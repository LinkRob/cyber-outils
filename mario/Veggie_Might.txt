'''Veggie Might''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', categorized under [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[Nature]] folder.

==Gameplay==
The player must move their character and have them pull out vegetables as soon as they sprout. If done successfully, they win, but if this condition is not met on time, they lose.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels require that one, two, and three veggies be pulled out, respectively.
