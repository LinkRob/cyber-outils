|race = [[Bumpty]]}}The '''Penguin Patrol '''is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He lives in [[Shiver City]] where he works as the city's chief of police.

==Appearance==
The Penguin Patrol is a green [[Bumpty]] that only wears a brown hat.

==History==
The Penguin Patrol was called to the scene where it was suspected [[Mayor Penguin]] was murdered by [[Mario]] in his home. However, Mario finds a note saying "[[Herringway]]" and the Penguin Patrol agrees to let Mario find Herringway. After Herringway was brought up to the scene, the Penguin Patrol quickly assumed that Herringway murdered him, but Mayor Penguin soon wakes up later. After hearing from the Mayor that he accidently knocked himself out when trying to retrieve a present for Herringway, the Penguin Patrol dismisses the scene and tells Mayor Penguin to be more careful.
