==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Galaxy''====
[[File:SMG Tropic Planets.png|thumb|left|200px|Prickly Plants in the [[Good Egg Galaxy]] in ''Super Mario Galaxy'']]
In ''Super Mario Galaxy'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Super Mario Galaxy (Nintendo Switch)|port]], Prickly Plants damage [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] when touched, and the only way to destroy them is by attacking them with a [[Stretch Plant]] or [[Star Spin]]ning a [[coconut]] into them. There are four types: the standard variant, two [[Giant Prickly Plant|larger variant]]s, and another large version that can fly with spinning leaves. The grounded larger variants, after being destroyed by a coconut, reveal a [[Sling Star]] or [[Launch Star]] leading to another planet. The floating variants serve as obstacles when a [[Floaty Fluff]] is used.

Prickly Plants appear in the following list of galaxies and missions. If no mission is stated, Prickly Plants appear during all missions: 
*[[Gateway Galaxy]] ("[[Gateway's Purple Coins]]"; normal)
*[[Good Egg Galaxy]] (normal ones appear in all missions, a large grounded one appear in "[[King Kaliente's Battle Fleet]]")
*[[Gusty Garden Galaxy]] (normal ones appear in all missions but "[[Purple Coins on the Puzzle Cube]]"; large flying ones appear in "[[The Dirty Tricks of Major Burrows]]", "[[Gusty Garden's Gravity Scramble]]", and "[[The Golden Chomp]]")
*[[Dusty Dune Galaxy]] ("[[Sunbaked Sand Castle]]", "[[Bullet Bill on Your Back]]"; giant and normal, both grounded)
*[[Toy Time Galaxy]] ("[[Bouncing Down Cake Lane]]"; normal)
*[[Bowser's Galaxy Reactor]]

====''Super Mario Galaxy 2''====
[[File:SMG2 Luigi Tall Trunk Galaxy slide.png|thumb|200px|Chains of prickly plants around [[Tall Trunk Galaxy]]'s slide in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'']]
In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', Prickly Plants act the same as before, but they can also be destroyed by having [[Yoshi]] eat them or by rolling into them in [[Rock Mario|Rock form]]. When touched by the [[Co-Star Luma]], Prickly Plants retract their spikes, allowing Mario or Luigi to pass over them safely without needing to destroy them. 

Different variations are used in this game compared to the predecessor; aside from the normal type, there is also a floating type of the normal size that appears for segments with [[Cloud Mario]] or [[Blimp Yoshi]]. Additionally, the slide in the [[Tall Trunk Galaxy]] features a few unique variations of its own, such as enormous patches filling pits in the slide (which are rendered as flat textures rather than fully modeled), chains of five or eight plants in a row either along part of the slide's length or around part of the round shape of the slide (with each chain being treated as a distinct object in the data rather than each plant), and similar chains of twelve taller—but not wider—plants near the end of the slide that are harder to [[jump]] over.

Besides the above-mentioned Tall Trunk Galaxy-exclusive variations, Prickly Plants appear in the following list of galaxies and missions. If no mission is stated, Prickly Plants appear during all missions: 
*[[Yoshi Star Galaxy]] ("[[Saddle Up with Yoshi]]", "[[Spiny Control]]"; grounded)
*[[Boulder Bowl Galaxy]] (grounded)
*[[Cosmic Cove Galaxy]] ("[[Exploring the Cosmic Cavern]]"; grounded)
*[[Tall Trunk Galaxy]] (grounded ones appear in "[[Tall Trunk's Big Slide]]" and "[[Tall Trunk's Purple Coin Slide]]", flying ones appear in all missions)
*[[Cloudy Court Galaxy]] ("[[Head in the Clouds (mission)|Head in the Clouds]]"; flying)
*[[Starshine Beach Galaxy]] ("[[Climbing the Cloudy Tower]]"; grounded)
*[[Slipsand Galaxy]] ("[[Squizzard's Sandy Sinkhole]]"; grounded)
*[[Melty Monster Galaxy]] ("[[A Stroll Down Rolling Lane]]"; flying)
*[[Boss Blitz Galaxy]] (grounded)

====''Super Mario 3D Land''====
[[File:SpikyPlant3DLand.png|100px|thumb|A Prickly Plant in ''Super Mario 3D Land'']]
Prickly Plants later appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', being first encountered in [[World 4-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 4-1]]. As of this game, their thorns are stubbier and are arranged in a globe, instead of being grown from the plant's calyx like in the previous games; their leaves also continuously rotate around their stems. They can be destroyed with projectiles (such as the [[Fire Mario|Fire form]]'s [[fireball]]s), a tail whip in [[Tanooki Mario|Tanooki form]], by landing on them in [[Statue Mario|Statue form]], or by touching them while [[Invincible Mario|invincible]].

===''Mario Party'' series===
====''Mario Party: Island Tour''====
[[Image:Goomba To Maneuver.png|thumb|left|[[Peach]] keeping her [[Goomba]]s away from the Prickly Plants as [[Mario]] and [[Boo]] get hit by the Prickly Plants, [[Waluigi]] can be seen at the back too.]]
In ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'', Prickly Plants (referred to as '''Thorny Flowers''' in the European and Australian versions of the game) make an appearance in the minigame [[Goomba to Maneuver]], scattered on the path that the player needs to follow. The player needs to maneuver a line of [[Goomba]]s between them. Goombas that touch a Prickly Plant are flung away, reducing the number of Goombas in the line, the player who had the most Goombas remaining is the winner, additionally if a player themselves is hit by a Prickly Plant, they slow down. Prickly Plants appear in both normal and big sizes, with bigger ones becoming more common as the minigame progresses.
{{br}}

====''Mario Party: Star Rush''====
In ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'', Prickly Plants appear as obstacles in the minigame [[Leafboard Hoard]].
