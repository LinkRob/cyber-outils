==Effects==
The HP Plus can permanently increase [[Mario]]'s [[Heart Point]]s by 5. However, this item is very rare to get in the game.

==Trivia==
*In the European version of [[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]], [[HP-Up Heart]]s are called HP Pluses.
