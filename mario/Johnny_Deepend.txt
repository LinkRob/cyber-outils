'''Johnny Deepend''' is a boss in [[Luigi's Mansion 3]], he is a swimming athlete on the thirteenth floor, which is the fitness center. [[Luigi]] recieves the fourteenth floor's elevator button after beating him, he has 60 HP.

==Appearance==

Johnny Deepend appears to be a swimming athlete wearing green swimsuit and swimming cap with a pair of goggles. He has pink skin.

==Battle==

Johnny Deepend will attack Luigi with polo balls when he enters the room, Luigi has to avoid the balls and find the right time to fire a ball at Johnny. After stunning Johnny with the ball, Luigi has to use [[Gooigi]] to rech a wheel that will drain the water out of the pool, while using Gooigi, Johnny will ignore Luigi and spat water at Gooigi, so Gooigi has to find the right time to hide behind the pillar and the right time to open the drain.

Once the water is drained, Johnny Deepend will be stuck in the open drain, groaning, Luigi has to fire another ball at him then suck his goggles off, once his goggles are vacuumed, Luigi can flash him and vacuum him. Johnny will make some poses before getting vacuumed. Luigi will recieve the fourteenth floor's elevator button after defeating him, however, [[Polterkitty]] will swallow the button in the elevator again, causing Luigi to deal with her for the second time.
