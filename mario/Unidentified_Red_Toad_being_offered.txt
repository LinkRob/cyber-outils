The '''Red Toad being offered''' is a minor character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. When some of the inhabitants of [[Mushroom Heights (Illumination)|Mushroom Heights]] dropped some coins, while [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] was taking Mario to the Palace, Mario gave this Red Toad one of the dropped coins, with respect.
