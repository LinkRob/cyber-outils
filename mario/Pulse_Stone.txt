The '''Pulse Stone''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Pulse Stone can only be obtained once [[Mario]] talks with [[Moustafa]] (after giving him a [[Lemon]] under his "Sheik" disguise). As Moustafa says, the Pulse Stone is the key to finding [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]] located somewhere in [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]]. While walking through Dry Dry Desert, the Pulse Stone beeps, signaling how closer Mario is to reaching Dry Dry Ruins. Once the Pulse Stone is beeping at its fastest, Mario must place it in the nearby stone pedestal to make Dry Dry Ruins rise from the sands. The Pulse Stone's usage is over and is permanently left in the stone pedestal.
