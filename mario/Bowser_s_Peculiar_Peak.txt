'''Bowser's Peculiar Peak''' ('''Bowser's Bizarre Volcano''' in British English) is the final board in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''. It is owned by [[Bowser]], and is unlocked by playing nearly every board except [[Shy Guy's Shuffle City]] at least once.
