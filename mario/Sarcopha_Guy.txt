'''Sarcopha Guys''' are enemies in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are mummies often found hiding in a golden sarcophagus.

===Behavior===
Sarcopha Guys usually hide within their own coffin, but whenever [[Wario]] gets close, the Sarcopha Guy jumps out and tries to damage him.

== In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Sarcopha Guys are only encountered in [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]]. They can be defeated from any attack except [[Sparky Wario]].

==Flavor text==
''This mummy got his name from the titanium-alloy sarcophagus he was discovered in. He's a bit bashful, so he doesn't get out much.''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
A Sarcopha Guy's name is a play on "sarcophagi," the plural form of "sarcophagus," which are tombs that store mummies.
