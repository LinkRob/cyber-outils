'''World 8-6''' is a level in [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.
== Overview ==
It is similar to [[World 8-6 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8-6]] from ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

== Walkthrough ==
This level takes place in a volcano with rising [[Lava]]. [[Propeller Mushroom]]s are the main power-up in this level.

The level begins near two [[? Block]]s, as well as two lava pits and a [[Podoboo]]. A [[Trampoline]] allows the player to progress, where a [[Koopa Troopa]] and another lava pit with a Podoboo are located. Another Trampoline leads to a ? Block and a [[Brick Block]] structure above the lava. The player then encounters a [[lift]] with a Trampoline on it, moving over a pit of lava with three Podoboos. More Trampolines are found, along with a [[P Switch]] that causes [[Blue Coin]]s to appear. Trampolines are used to reach a [[Warp Pipe]] at the top of the area, leading to the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]].

A [[Swinging Hammer]] allows the player to cross a pit of lava containing Podoboos. More ? Blocks are found, followed by a swinging chain. A [[Red Ring]] is found, along with three more Podoboos. Two more chains appear, allowing the player to reach a higher platform. Another Swinging Hammer then allows the player to reach a Warp Pipe, leading outside of the volcano. After a pit of lava, the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]] is found.
== Enemies ==

* [[Podoboo]]s - 15
* Red [[Koopa Troopa]]s - 7

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Above the moving platform with the [[Trampoline]] are two paths, and the first Star Coin is located in the left one. The player can either use the Trampoline or a [[Propeller Suit]] to reach it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is located in the next set of split paths after the one with the first Star Coin, on the left. Mario can either fly to it with a Propeller Suit, or wall kick to it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': ''(pictured)'': The third Star Coin is to the left of the exit [[Warp Pipe]], surrounded by eight other [[Coin]]s. The player can again use a Propeller Suit to reach it, or jump on the Swinging Hammer as it swings to the left.
