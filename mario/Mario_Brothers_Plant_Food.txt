The '''Mario Brothers Plant Food''' is an item which only appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[The Marios Fight Back]]."

==Description==
On its own, the product seems to be used as regular plant food, but it is shown that combining it with [[Mario Brothers Clog Cleaner]] causes a [[Clog]] to mutate into a [[Monster (character)|Monster]].
