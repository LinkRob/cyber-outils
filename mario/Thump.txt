'''Thumps''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Thumps are large, [[Thwomp]]-like rocks.

===Behavior===
Thumps can be used as floors, but they still act as obstacles by blocking hidden areas beneath them.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Thumps are mainly encountered in [[Fury Volcano]], and they first appear in the [[level]] [[Fury Volcano 4-2|4-2]]. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can pound a Thump down from the effects of [[Rage]], and if she knocks a Thump all the way into the ground, it shatters. Thumps are one of the enemies to not have an entry in the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] due to being considered more of an roadblock/obstacle than an actual enemy.
