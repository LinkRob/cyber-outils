A '''Co-op minigame''' is a type of [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Description ==
Players work together to win the minigame. After playing the minigame, they will be ranked S, A, B or C.

They also appear in the '''River Survival''' mode. Players can hit balloons to win and add more time.
