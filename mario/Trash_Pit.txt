'''Trash Pit''' is the first area found in [[Bowser's body]] in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', located in his colon. Enemies here include [[Goombule]]s, two [[Elite Goombule]]s and [[Spike Blop]]s.

In this area, [[Mario]] and everyone else in [[Princess Peach's Castle]] become inhaled by [[Bowser]] and fall into [[Bowser's Body|his body]]. Mario, [[Luigi]] and Starlow apparently landed in this area and had to venture their way out of it.

During gameplay, many [[Goombule]]s can be found here to get in the bros. way. [[Hammer]]s can also be obtained near the end of this area as there are crates blocking the area and Mario and Luigi need to break them with their hammers. Ten [[Attack Piece]]s must be obtained here to gain the [[Green Shell (attack)|Green Shell]] attack.

Mario also had to free Luigi and Starlow who were in trouble as Starlow and Luigi had their heads trapped in bubbles and Luigi's in a [[pipe]]-shaped one.
