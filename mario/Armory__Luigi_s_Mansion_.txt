The '''Armory''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Armory is most noted for containing several suits of armor, many of which hit Luigi with their clubs if he stands in front of them, even while the lights are still on. The room also contains five [[Treasure chest|chest]]s, three of which contain [[treasure]]. On the left side is some crates, but none of them contain anything, and there is a covered [[mirror]] hanging on the north wall.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Armory is visited during [[Area Four]]. [[Luigi]] has to examine the objects for each of the [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] to come out. Two [[Purple Puncher]]s are each contained in a treasure chest, and a [[Blue Twirler]], a [[Red Grabbing Ghost]], a [[Garbage Can Ghost]], and a [[Gold Ghost]] can each be brought out from a certain suit of armor. After Luigi captures the ghosts, a treasure chest with the [[Key]] to the [[Pipe Room]] appears.

In PAL releases' and the Nintendo 3DS version's [[Hidden Mansion]] mode, the Armory consists of four [[Purple Bomber]]s, two [[Flash]]es, and six [[Blue Mouse|Blue Mice]].
