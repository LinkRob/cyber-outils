'''Donkey Kong Country''' is one of [[5-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Gameplay==
The microgame is based on the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] game ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''. It features [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] in a [[Barrel Cannon]] (although it uses the sprite of a [[Blast Barrel]]), and he has to launch out of it and collect a line of three [[banana]]s in the air. If done successfully, the player wins the microgame, but if either time runs out or Donkey Kong misses the bananas, the player loses.

As with any other microgame, Donkey Kong Country has differences between its difficulty levels. In level 1, the Barrel Cannon slowly moves vertically. In level 2, it does the same but at a faster speed. In level 3, the Barrel Cannon rotates in a ninety-degree arc.
