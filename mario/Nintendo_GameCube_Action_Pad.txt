The '''Nintendo GameCube Action Pad''' is a dance pad controller for the [[Nintendo GameCube]]. It was created by [[Konami]] specifically for ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]'', the only game it is compatible with.

==Description==
The pad features eight buttons: the directional inputs, the {{button|GCN|A}} and {{button|GCN|B}} buttons next to the up button, and the {{button|GCN|Z}} and {{button|GCN|Start}} buttons on the top corners of the pad. In the center is a light-blue silhouette of [[Mario]], specifically the ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' artwork of him performing a [[Sweep Kick]], but made to appear as if he is breakdancing.
