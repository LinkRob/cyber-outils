'''Planet Bowser''' is the main base of operations for [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]], his [[Bowser Jr. (Illumination)|son]] and their army in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. It contains a large amusement park and the Boomsday Weapon, which Bowser Jr. plans to use to destroy the universe. In the end, however, after the Bowser family's defeat by [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]], the base was transformed into a garden planet.
