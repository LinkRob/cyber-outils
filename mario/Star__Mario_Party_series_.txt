''Not to be confused with [[Power Star]] or [[Starman]].''

'''Stars '''are the main objects (and collectibles) of value in the [[Mario Party|''Mario Party'']] series. The main objective of the Mario party games is to collect the most stars. In [[Mario Party 9|''Mario Party 9'']], [[Mario Party 10|''10'']], and [[Mario Party: Island Tour|''Island Tour'']], Stars --along with [[Coin]]s-- are succeeded by [[Mini Star]]s and [[Mini Ztar]]s (except in ''Mario Party 9''<nowiki/>'s [[DK's Jungle Ruins]] --where [[Banana]]s and [[Z-Banana]]s replace Mini Stars and Mini Ztars but have the same effect as Mini Stars and Mini Ztars from previous boards). They (stars and coins) return in [[Mario Party: Star Rush|''Mario Party: Star Rush'']], [[Mario Party: The Top 100|''The Top 100'']], [[Super Mario Party|''Super'']], [[Mario Party Superstars|''Superstars'']] and ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree|Jamboree]]''. And by extension, ''Super'', ''Superstars'', ''Jamboree'' both revert back to the classic ''Mario Party'' series formula.

==Importance==
Stars are the objective of the Mario Party games. Other than being used in recreations of fun, there are other importance's in Stars.

In [[Mario Party 5|''Mario Party 5'']], the stars has to be created in order to give the characters power in order to stop and defeat [[Bowser]] in destroying all dreams.

In [[Mario Party 6|''Mario Party 6'']], Brighton and Twila are arguing, and [[Mario]] suggests collecting Stars in order to end their feud. In the game's epilogue, Brighton and Twila see how desperate they are to end their feud, so they stop fighting for the sake of everyone else.

==Methods==
There are different methods of collecting stars throughout the ''Mario Party'' series.
*The Star will teleport to a different spot in the board. The players have to run to the Star and pay 20 coins to receive it. After that, the Star is teleported somewhere else in the board. This is the most common method of collecting stars.
*The Star will stay in one spot, but the price for the Star will be different every time. This concept was introduced in ''Mario Party 6''.
*The Star will be hidden in one out of three places, and the player has to go to one of the places, and hope they get lucky. This concept was introduced in [[Mario Party 7|''Mario Party 7'']].
*All of the players have stars in the beginning. They must steal the Stars from each other. This concept was introduced in ''Mario Party 6''.
*The Stars are located in multiple places, but the players must invest coins to get them. The person who invests the most coins gets the stars. This concept was introduced in ''Mario Party 7''.
