'''Blue Podoboos''' are a blue variant of [[Podoboo]] that first appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. Sometimes they have additional abilities, such as being able to home in on a target, but in most games they behave almost identically to a standard Podoboo, only faster and with the ability to jump out of [[blue lava]].

==Appearances==
===''Super Princess Peach''===
[[Image:The Blue Podoboo.png|thumb|left|A Singular Blue Podoboo]]
Blue Podoboos first appear in ''Super Princess Peach'' as enemies appearing in [[Bowser's Villa]]. They hop out of blue lava and hover above it. When [[Princess Peach]] approaches a [[torch]], the Blue Podoboo slowly follows after her. Peach must lead the Blue Podoboos to every unlit torch, as lighting all of them in a room is necessary to open up certain doorways. Blue Podoboos can be defeated by either Peach running into them, even as it harms her in the process, or from entering a torch.

===''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury'' ===
[[Image:Cool Blue Podoboos.png|thumb|right|Blue Podoboos from ''Super Mario 3D World''.]]
In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its port ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', Blue Podoboos are more common than they were in ''Super Princess Peach''. There are now stages dedicated to blue lava and the Blue Podoboos one example being Castle-4: [[Boiling Blue Bully Belt]]. Blue Podoboos can no longer be defeated. Also, unlike ''Super Princess Peach'', Blue Podoboos no longer home in on [[Mario]] [[Luigi|or]] [[Peach|his]] [[Toad|friends]]. Instead, they now leap faster than a standard Podoboo (and sometimes higher too).

==Trivia==
*In the minigame [[Hot Rope Jump]], from ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', there are Podoboos that appear to be blue at the start of the minigame, but they are not the same Blue Podoboos since they can change their color throughout the minigame.
