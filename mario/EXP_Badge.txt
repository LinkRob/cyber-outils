'''EXP Badge''' is a type of [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be bought from [[Peach's Castle]] for 350 [[coin]]s. Equipping the EXP Badge allows the user to earn 20 percent more [[Experience Point|EXP]] at the end of a battle if they defeat an enemy using a [[Bros. Item]]. The EXP Badge has a more powerful variation, the [[EXP Badge A]].
