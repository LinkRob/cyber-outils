A '''Tornado''' is an offensive item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and its sequels ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
Using a Tornado fires it in a straight line, similar to a [[Green Shell]], that stops upon touching a wall. For all items that can be shot straight, a crosshair appears once the player gets it in order to enhance aim. Once the Tornado is used, any [[kart]] caught within the Tornado's path gets spun out and flown into the air, akin to what a real tornado does, except for only one second. As a result, their kart is slowed down. The Tornado can go through many karts and therefore affect several within its path.
