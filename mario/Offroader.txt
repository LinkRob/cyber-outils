The '''Offroader''' is a large [[kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. Its bike counterpart is the [[Wario Bike]].

== Description ==
The Offroader resembles a dune buggy with bullhorns on the front, a spare tire on the back, and a flag on the left side.

==Stats==
The Offroader has the best [[acceleration]] and [[handling]] among all large karts in the game and is the [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|second heaviest]] vehicle in the game behind the Piranha Prowler. It has barely above average [[Off-Road|off-road]] capabilities despite its name, but it has the best off-road among all large karts in the game. Its [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] is above average. However, it is the [[Speed|slowest]] large kart in the game and has the worst [[drift]] among all karts in the game.{{MKW Kart Stats/Offroader}}
