'''Jump! Jump! Jump!''' is a course of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level opens with a [[Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will spawn outlined blocks that become tangible along with the music, then become outlined and intangible after a few beats.

The level opens with slower music and steps to teach the player how the mechanic works. The music starts to get faster and instead of steps, it is platforms the player must [[jump]] across platforms. The music then gets even faster and instead of jumping, the player must sprint at max speed. The level then returns to jumping, but this time the platforms are two blocks long instead of four. The level ends with having the player run on the blocks, then normal ground. Grabbing the [[Wonder Seed]] by jumping.
