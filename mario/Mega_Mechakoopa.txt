'''Mega Mechakoopa''' is a large [[Mechakoopa]] character who appears as a miniboss in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', within its own boss minigame, [[Mega Mechakoopa's Swing & Stomp]]. Here, the characters must make their way up to Mega Mechakoopa's head and land on it to deal damage.
