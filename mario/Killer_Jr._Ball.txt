'''Killer Jr. Ball''' is the name of [[Bowser Jr.]]'s [[Star Skill]] in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. He uses it while either pitching or batting, upon which the baseball turns into a [[Bullet Bill]] wearing his bib. Aside from this aesthetic difference, Killer Jr. Ball is essentially the same as [[Bowser]]'s Star Skill, [[Killer Ball]]. In Challenge mode, the [[Jr. Mask]] must be bought before Bowser Jr. can start using the move.

If Bowser Jr. uses Killer Jr. Ball while pitching, the Bullet Bill slowly approaches the home plate, does a loop and then, and then dashes. If the move is used while batting, the Bullet Bill charges across the field. If it is caught by a fielder, they are pushed a distance back.

Killer Jr. Ball is one of the Star Skills to not return in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. Here, Bowser Jr. instead uses the [[Graffiti Ball]] for pitching and the [[Graffiti Swing]] for batting.
