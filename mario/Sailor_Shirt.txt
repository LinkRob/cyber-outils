The '''Sailor Shirt''' is an armor only usable by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the [[Shaman]] at the [[Sea]] or in the [[Sunken Ship]] for 50 [[coin]]s after [[Yaridovich]] has been defeated. Equipping the Sailor Shirt to Mario increases his [[Defense]] by 30 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 15.
