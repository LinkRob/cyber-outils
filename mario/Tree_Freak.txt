A '''Tree Freak''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Tree Freak is a large tree with green leaves and spiked fruits growing on their branches.

==In ''Wario World''==
Tree Freaks only appear in [[Beanstalk Way]], where three are encountered by [[Wario]]. While fighting a Tree Freak, Wario can [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] near it to shake the ground, causing the tree to drop spiked fruit. The Tree Freak's vulnerable spot is a small bud on the front, which Wario can attack by picking up a fruit on its spikeless side and then throwing it at the Tree Freak. Each Tree Freak takes one more hit than the previous one to defeat: the first Tree Freak takes one hit; the second one takes two hits; and the third one takes three hits.
