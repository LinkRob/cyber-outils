'''Grunt''' is a one-time character from ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. He is a [[Super Troopa]] who appears in the episode "[[Mario and Joliet]]," in which [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] entrusts him to guard the cell where [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]], and [[Romano]] are imprisoned.
