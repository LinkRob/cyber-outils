'''Penguin Pushers''' is a [[Co-op Minigame|Co-op]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The mother [[penguin]] waddles out of the igloo and calls out to the baby penguins.

== Gameplay ==
Players must fence in baby penguins to their mother in sixty seconds. Players win if they manage to fence all baby penguins before sixty seconds pass.

== Ending ==
If players are successful, the players will do their victory animations, seconds animations or third animations while the penguins are cheering.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move

==In-game description==
*''"Corral the penguins and push them to the pen!"''
*''"The baby penguins will waddle away from you if you chase them."''
*Tips: ''"It's easier to herd more penguins out if one person stands by the exit!"''

==Trivia==
*Penguin Pushers contradicts the minigame Pushy Penguins, which instead involves the characters having to avoid being pushed off by penguins.
