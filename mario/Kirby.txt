'''[https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Kirby Kirby]''' is the titular main protagonist of the ''[[w:c:kirby:Kirby (series)|Kirby]]'' series and one of [[Nintendo]]'s stars. He made his debut in the 1992 [[Game Boy]] title ''[https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Kirby%27s_Dream_Land Kirby's Dream Land]''. Kirby lives in [[w:c:kirby:Dream Land|Dream Land]], a country on the star shaped planet of [[w:c:kirby:Planet Popstar|Pop Star]], and usually has to save his home from whatever evil that attacks it. He has a main role in all the ''Kirby'' games, which usually include [[Meta Knight]] and [[King Dedede]]. He was created by [[Masahiro Sakurai]], who also created the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], where he appears to be among its central characters. Kirby is famously known for his cute appearance and being one of the most powerful protagonists in Nintendo's history, among the likes of [[Mario]] and [[Link]], the latter being the main character of ''[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda_(series) The Legend of Zelda]'' series.

Kirby has made a handful of appearances alongside Mario, most notably in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series.

== Profile ==
Kirby is a resident of the yellow, star-shaped planet Popstar, and the planet's consistent hero. His round, pink form is only 20 cm (8 inches) in height, though his dimensions are obviously larger in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series and some other media. Living in the peaceful kingdom of Dream Land, he defends his home against foes such as his "arch-frenemy" King Dedede, and the malevolent, shadowy [[w:c:kirby:Dark Matter|Dark Matter]], among others. Kirby is generally depicted with an easygoing, childlike personality, but he is also shown to be determined and heroic when the situation calls for it. He also loves to sleep and eat massive quantities of food,

=== Powers and abilities ===
Kirby's main ability is to swallow his opponent, not unlike [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], except Kirby inhales his target rather than uses a long tongue. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]],'' he can copy his attacker's ability but lose his. For example, for [[Captain Falcon]], Kirby can Falcon Punch and Falcon Kick but at the cost of his Hammer, Ninja and Swallow moves. He has standard punch and kick, but in ''Super Smash Bros.'' he can Spin and Spin Kick to do more damage. His sucking ability is a bit like Yoshi's. His Final Smash in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' is [[Cook Kirby]], where he puts everyone or a couple of his opposition into a cooking pot. After a couple of stirs they fly off the screen or just fall down depending on damage. When the cooking pot vanishes, it leaves food to up the health of the character who eats it. In subsequent ''Super Smash Bros.'' series installments, Kirby's Final Smash was changed to [[Ultra Sword]] instead because the Cook Kirby's actions were relegated to the [[Chef Kawasaki]] [[Assist Trophy]] instead.

== In the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series ==
[[File:KirbyBrawl.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Kirby in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
Kirby had appeared in every installment of the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series. In the first two games, he is the franchise's sole representative. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', he was joined by fellow characters [[Meta Knight]] and [[King Dedede]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', Kirby's Final Smash was changed to Ultra Sword. He reappears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a playable fighter. He appears in the [[Adventure Mode: World of Light|World of Light]] mode as the sole survivor of Galeem's initial attack. Kirby can now inhale certain projectiles, and his dash attack is once again the flame variant used in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''.

== Trivia ==
*At 20 cm (8 inches) tall, Kirby is the second shortest playable character in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series. Due to being ≈1.9 cm (≈0.75 inches) tall, [[Olimar]] is the canonically shortest character as of ''Brawl''.
*All of Kirby's victory poses are variations of the "Kirby Dance", victory dances he performs upon completing a level or defeating a boss in his home series.
*Although Kirby is canonically male, due to his feminine appearance, he is sometimes mistaken as a female by fans.
*Alongside ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media, Kirby also makes an appearance in ''The Legend of Zelda'' franchise as well. In the Game Boy title ''[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Link%27s_Awakening Link's Awakening]'' as well as its [[Nintendo Switch]] remake, he can be found as a foe for Link in Eagle's Tower, though if he swallows the hero in green, he simply spits him back out. However, the Kirby in-game is not technically the actual character, as they are known as "[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Kirby Anti-Kirby]", a corrupt, hostile version of himself. This has led to later ''Kirby'' games since ''[https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Kirby_Super_Star Kirby Super Star]'' to have enemies with sword swallowed and copied by Kirby to gain the [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Sword Sword] ability, where he gains a green floppy hat and sword techniques like Link does.
*Out of the four main characters (Kirby Bandana Waddle Dee Meta Knight and King Dedede) Kirby is the only one that has not had an antagonistic role.
**He is also the only Kirby fighter in Smash Bros that was never a villain.
*Super Smash Bros. is the first media in which Kirby had a voice actor, contrary to popular belief that it was the ''Kirby: Right Back at Ya!'' anime.
