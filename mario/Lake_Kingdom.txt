The {{Nihongo|'''Lake Kingdom'''|湖の国|Mizuumi no Kuni|, lit. '''Lake Country'''}}, also known as '''The Land of Beauty and Clear Water''' is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It is a vast realm in the form of an lake. Its most notable city is [[Lake Lamode]], which serves as the kingdom's main playable area in the game.

The kingdom is notable for being primarily inhabited by [[Lochlady|Lochladies]]. The primary currency of the Lake Kingdom are purple [[Regional Coin|Fish Scale]], however they also accept regular gold coins. Due to its architectural style and many islands, it seems to be based on Greece. The name "Lake Lamode", however, comes from French ''à la mode'', meaning "in fashion" or "stylish". The name of the Lochladies and the presence of Dorrie (a reference to the Loch Ness Monster) make a connection to Scotland.


==Concept and creation==
The building decorations, the rocks shaped like a drape and the light shaped like a lace were all designed following the motif of the wedding dress.

The aquatic plants of the kingdom were designed following the motif of the sequin, and the design was completed by adding glitter that made them flicker in the water.

== [[Painting]] ==
There are two paintings in this kingdom. One is located inside a hole at the fishing pond near where the [[Fishin' Lakitu]] is. The painting takes the player to the Southwestern Floating Island at the [[Sand Kingdom]] or the Floating Sky Island at the [[Luncheon Kingdom]]. If the Lake Kingdom was selected first, this painting will lead to the Sand Kingdom, although if the [[Wooded Kingdom]] was selected first, this painting will lead to the Luncheon Kingdom, the painting can be accessed at any time.

The other painting is found on the Viewing Balcony, which can only be accessed from either the [[Metro Kingdom]], the [[Seaside Kingdom]], or the [[Snow Kingdom]]. If the Lake Kingdom was selected before the Wooded Kingdom, and the [[Snow Kingdom]] was selected before Seaside Kingdom, then the painting will be in the Seaside Kingdom. If the Lake Kingdom was selected before the Wooded Kingdom, and the [[Seaside Kingdom]] was selected before the Snow Kingdom, then the painting will be in the Snow Kingdom. If the Wooded Kingdom was selected before the Lake Kingdom, then the painting will be in the Metro Kingdom.

==Power Moons==
{{main|Lake Lamode}}

There are a total of 44 [[Power Moon]]s (41 regular Power Moons and 1 [[Multi Moon]]) in the Lake Kingdom. 25 Power Moons and the Multi Moon can be collect during the player's first visit to this kingdom can be collect, including one from the kingdom's Hint Art. The player can also collect a Power Moon if they travel to this kingdom using a [[painting]] found in a future kingdom. After the player defeats Bowser in the [[Moon Kingdom]], 6 more Power Moons become available to collect. The [[Moon Rock]] moons will also be available to destroy, adding 9 additional Power Moons to be accessible.
