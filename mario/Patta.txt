'''Patta''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
Patta is a very young [[Flip-Flopian]] who can be found in [[Flopside]] near [[Hot Fraun]]. Patta dislikes trends but also hates being left out of them.

== Trivia ==
*Patta's [[Flipside]] counterpart is [[Pitta]].
