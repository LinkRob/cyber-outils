'''Shriek Mansion 3-4''' is the fourth level of ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. The level starts of with her in a dark area with a light on herself.

== Toads ==
*'''Toad 1''': Jump on the brown platforms to reach the top and move left.
*'''Toad 2''': Get in the purple pipe, then do tricks when sliding, after getting out of the purple pipe, go to the left to save the Toad.
*'''Toad 3''': Climb up the ladder, then go to the left before the platform disintegrates.

== Items ==
*'''Winged Basket''': Grab the Koopa or the shell and go to the right and hit it.
*'''Winged Basket 2''': Explore through doors, until you find a foggy room and find the deep pit.
*'''Puzzle Piece''': Climb on the ladder to the top and move left.
