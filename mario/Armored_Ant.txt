'''Armored Ants''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are related to the [[Shogun]]s. Armored Ants are encountered in [[Barrel Volcano]] and [[The Trial Course]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are ants that wear a suit of armor and wield an axe.

==Battle==
In battle, Armored Ants mainly attack physically with their axes but are capable of using magical moves such as [[Vigor Up]] or [[Endobubble]].

===Strategy===
They are weak against Ice moves so [[Mallow]] should use his [[Snowy]] move against them. Since they are wearing a suit of armor, Armored Ants are resistant to [[Fire (element)|Fire]] attacks, meaning [[Mario]] has to not use any Fire-based moves but rather stick to physical attacks.
