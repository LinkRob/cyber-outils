The '''Shadow Doppelgangers''' from ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'' are shadow clones of various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' and ''[[Sonic the Hedgehog (franchise)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' characters, including [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Wario]], [[Waluigi]], [[Bowser]], [[Bowser Jr.]], [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]], [[Miles "Tails" Prower|Tails]], [[Amy Rose]], [[Knuckles the Echidna|Knuckles]], [[Shadow the Hedgehog|Shadow]], [[Vector the Crocodile|Vector]], [[Silver the Hedgehog|Silver]], [[Blaze the Cat|Blaze]], [[Metal Sonic]], and [[Doctor Eggman|Dr. Eggman]].

== In ''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games'' ==
They are Shadow Doppelgangers of Team Sonic and Team Mario . They appear in Legends Mode where they challenge Team Sonic and Team mario in Multiple Events and retreat to the platform after each characters win.
