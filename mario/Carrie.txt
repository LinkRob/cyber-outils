'''Carrie''' is a Pixl that only appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' and is one of [[Mario]]'s partners. She is found in [[Fort Francis]], in the dungeon.

== Abilities ==
She helps Mario by acting as a hovering platform to bring him across spikes and other obstacles and to help him move faster. Carrie can also be used to damage spiked enemies such as [[Spiny|Spinies]] without taking damage. Mario is also able to go 3D while using her. Also, [[Bowser]] can ride on Carrie and even use his powerful fire breath making it one of the best combos in the game as Carrie makes Bowser agile and faster. This is very useful against most of the boss fights in the game especially against the final boss, [[Super Dimentio]].
