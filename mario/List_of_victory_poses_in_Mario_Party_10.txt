{{Listnotice|victory animations in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''}}
This applies to characters who rank first, second, third, and fourth place respectively.

==Information==
If a character wins in 1-vs.-3 and 2-vs.-2 minigames, then they strike their first place poses as victory animations. But if they lose in them, then they strike their fourth place poses as defeat animations.

In [[Bowser Mini-game|Bowser minigames]], a different victory theme will be played after they are finished. If a player doesn't lose any lives, then they strike their second place poses. If a player loses hearts, then they sulk. If a player's heart count reduces to zero, they pass out and spin around.

At the end of every Bowser minigame, Bowser scratches the screen. If Bowser fails to damage a single player, then the regular victory theme plays and everybody else strikes their first place poses. If Bowser successfully damages at least one player, then he chuckles. If every single player loses all of their hearts, or if Bowser Jr. wins in an extra minigame, then the defeat theme will play.

==Trivia==
*These victory poses loop endlessly:
**Luigi's 4th place animation
**Yoshi's 3rd and 4th place animations
**Wario's 1st place animation
**Waluigi's 1st place animation
**Rosalina's 1st place animation
**Donkey Kong's 1st, 3rd and 4th place animations
**Spike's 2nd and 3rd place animations
**Bowser's losing animation
**Bowser's winning animation in amiibo Party
**Bowser Jr.'s winning and losing animations
*Yoshi's 4th place animation references him running away after he gets hit in the ''Mario'' platformers.
*Donkey Kong's 1st place animation references him slamming down on the ground in the ''Donkey Kong Country'' games.
*The winning, 2nd place, and losing animations are reused in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.
*In ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', all characters but Birdo doing their new winning animation and retains their 2nd place/losing animation.
