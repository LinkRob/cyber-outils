A '''Minigame Space''', also spelled '''Mini-game Space''', is a recurring type of [[space]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party''===
Landing on a Minigame Space causes a single-player mini-game to occur, so only one character has a chance to earn extra [[coin]]s.

===''[[Mario Party 3]]''===
Minigame Spaces only appear Duel Mode boards. Landing on one will start a Duel Minigame where one of the two players has a chance to earn ten coins to help keep their partners. Since Duel Mode does not have minigames at the end of turns, landing on a Minigame Space is one of two ways to play a minigame, the other being going past [[Belltop]] five times.
