|name=Big Hammer x2 (Battle Card)}}'''Big Hammer x2''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Hammer x2 is the strong version of [[Mario]]'s "Hammer x2" attack. It is two of the [[Big Hammer]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied with an orange/tan checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 50 coins (unpainted)

Sell Price: 25 coins (unpainted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Hammer x2 is first seen in [[Redpepper Crater]], but can also found in [[The Emerald Circus]] and [[Black Bowser's Castle]].
