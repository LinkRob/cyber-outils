'''Roxanne''' is a woman who only appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Cyrano de Mario]]." She was an old friend of [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]]'s. She has since been married and has five children. Roxanne was portrayed by [[Vanna White]] of ''{{w|Wheel of Fortune}}'' fame.
