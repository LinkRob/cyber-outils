|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Mystery House Claw Climb''' is a level of [[World Bowser]] in the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.
==Features==
This level is a mystery house with entirely [[Cat Mario|Cat Suit]] themed challenges. It is a very good way to get free bells, if you win.

==Green Stars==
All of the stars require the player to be in Cat form.
*'''Green Star 1''': In Section 1, climb up to the third floor on the right to get the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 2''': In Section 2, climb the wall on the right and run to the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 3''': In Section 3, climb up the red and blue wall made out of [[Beat Block|Beat Blocks]] before it switches color and get the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 4''': In Section 4, cross the right side of the stage and climb the north wall to get the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 5''': In Section 5, climb up to the top floor on the left and get the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 6''': In Section 6, break the purple Brick Blocks, climb the wall on the right side, and break the other purple Brick Blocks to get to the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 7''': In Section 7, climb up any of the Beat Blocks before it switches color and get the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 8''': In Section 8, climb up the middle of the north wall to get the star within ten seconds. Just avoid falling into the lava.
*'''Green Star 9''': In Section 9, break the purple Blocks and/or use the [[POW Block]]s to move break them faster to get the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 10''': In Section 10, climb the blocks and don't stay on them for too long to the star within ten seconds.
