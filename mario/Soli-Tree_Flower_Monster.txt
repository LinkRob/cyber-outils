During combat, [[Seedlusa|Seedlusas]] have a chance to inter themselves into the ground. If that happens, a monster that resembles a flower grows out of the ground where they once stood. These enemies are only encountered in the [[Soli-Tree]], as are Seedlusas.

== Description ==
These unnamed enemies are monstrous sentient flowers with a grey-ish white stem. Their head has a black face with red eyes and black petals with red streaks stretching from the head to the face. The enemy has two stubby arms that can stretch to great lengths, tipped with black five-petalled flowers for "hands." At the base is a series of red roots that act as legs. The enemy's colours are the same colours that [[Seedlusa|Seedlusas]] have for their own palette.

== Combat ==
These enemies are completely absent from the overworld, since their spawning criteria involves a [[Seedlusa]] burying itself.

The enemy has three attacks. In the first attack, it can plant its arms into the ground, causing five flowers to bloom around the Bros. The flowers have their blooming order selected randomly, and will launch shockwaves that must be jumped over in the same order after they bloom. The second attack is an orbit run which has the enemy run around the Bros. and occasionally ram into them, not unlike [[Glohm Bowser]]'s Death Shell attack; the ramming charge must be countered with a hammer strike.

After a few turns, the monster can perform a powerful move, firing out lots of dark spores that deal tremendous amounts of damage. After this move, the enemy will kill itself, ending the fight if all other enemies have been killed.

This monster, who is of Level 44, has 3,086 hit points, but low defense, in contrast to the Seedlusa, with attack power and defense stats of 265 each and a speed of 266. If slain before it can unleash its dark spore attack, the creature gives 2601 Experience and 360 Coins, dropping a Full-Belly Wear DX with a 2% chance.
