'''Electroshock''' is a [[Thunder (element)|Thunder]]-based move used by certain enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When Electroshock is used, a stream of electricity rains down on a single target and a circle of electricity forms around the target.

== Effects ==
Electroshock can inflict moderate damage to a single character. This move is considered a weaker version of [[Static E!]].
