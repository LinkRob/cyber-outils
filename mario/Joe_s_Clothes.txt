'''Joe's Clothes''' is a shop in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It is a shop owned and managed by [[Manager Joe]], and it can be found in [[Diamond City]]. Joe's Clothes is the main setting for [[Mona]]'s story.

== In ''WarioWare Gold'' ==
Mona plans to have a party and also for a dress. Manager Joe helps her, he tells her to close her eyes and imagine her perfect dress. She describes it as "extravagant, like a parfait and cute like a plushy" and "Super cool like [[Wario]]". She mentions that he is her "fashion icon". She also mentions that she like Joe's tie, because it screams like fashion. While he plans for Mona's dress, she is interested with another dress. Joe thinks he should wear the tie.

Mona starts the microgame gameplay trying out dresses if she likes it or not. They were successful to get her the perfect dress. Mona asks him to join her party, and Joe accepts her request. She realises she is late to her own party and she drives the motorcycle leaving Joe behind.
