'''Swallowing''' is a recurring ability used by the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
The move involves a Yoshi catching either an enemy or an item with its tongue and then swallowing it. In ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platformers, swallowing an enemy converts it into a [[Yoshi Egg]]. The {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} has a move known as [[Egg Lay]], in which Yoshi swallows a fighter to convert them into an egg temporarily.
