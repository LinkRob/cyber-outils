'''All or Nothing''' is a badge that appears in the [[Paper Mario series|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:All or nothing badge.png|thumb|left]]

In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', All or Nothing is one of the many badges that can be purchased from [[Rowf]], the [[Rowf's Badge Shop|badge shop]] owner. This badge in particular costs 100 [[coin]]s, however this is one of the few badges that are only accessible after the completion of Chapter 4.

The badge is one of the many badges in the game that increases Mario's attack. This badge costs 4 BP to be used and doesn't always work. The badge may give [[Mario]] 1 point to his attack, but only if he completely fulfills the action commands for his moves.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', All or Nothing is located within the [[Palace of Shadow]]. The badge, itself is found inside of the red [[Question Block]] inside a room with spikes that come up from the ground. Like in ''Paper Mario'', this badge costs four BP to wear and increases the damage Mario does so long as he performs the action command correctly.
