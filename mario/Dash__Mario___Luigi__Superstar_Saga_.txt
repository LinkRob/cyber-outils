'''Dash''', also named '''Fire Tickle''', is an overworld technique in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
[[Mario]] can use this move while he is behind [[Luigi]]. Hold {{button|gba|A}} to charge up the [[Firebrand]], then release the button to make both Bros dash quickly in one direction. This technique is used to knock over [[Ankoopa]]s and push [[cannonball]]s out of the way.
