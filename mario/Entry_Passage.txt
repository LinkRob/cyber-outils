The '''Entry Passage''' is the first passage visited by [[Wario]] in the [[Golden Pyramid]] in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. It only consists of a tutorial level, [[Hall of Hieroglyphs]], and a boss fight against [[Spoiled Rotten]], which becomes accessible upon collecting the purple [[Jewel]].
