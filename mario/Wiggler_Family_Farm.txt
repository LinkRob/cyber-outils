The '''Wiggler Family Farm''' is a large area within [[Dimble Wood]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|its]] [[Reissue|remake]]. It is owned by generations of a [[Wiggler]] family. The [[Giga-carrot]] was grown here before it had been pulled out by [[Bowser]].
