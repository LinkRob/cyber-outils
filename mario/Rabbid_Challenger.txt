'''Rabbid Challengers''' are [[Rabbids]] that challenge you to do their challenges. In [[Mario + Rabbids: Kingdom Battle]], there's a difficulty level that goes from ''Very Easy'' to ''Ultimate''; but in the [[Donkey Kong Adventure DLC]], there is no difficulty, until you get to the [[bad banana]] challenges.
