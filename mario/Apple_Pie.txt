The '''Apple Pie''' is an item and [[recipe]] made by [[Tayce T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
This dessert appears as a tart filled with cooked apples. It is made by giving Tayce T. an [[Apple]] and [[Cake Mix]], which are found in [[Boo's Mansion]] and [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] respectively. An Apple Pie restores 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 15 [[Flower Point|FP]], and is sold at all stores for ten [[coin]]s.
