'''Copacabana Beach''' is a real-world location which has made a few appearances in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media, the first time being in ''[[Mario is Missing!]]'' for [[MS-DOS]].

==Description==
Copacabana Beach is a beach located in [[Rio de Janeiro]], [[Brazil]].

==In ''Mario is Missing!''==
[[File:CopacabanaBeach_MIM.png|thumb|left|''Mario is Missing!'' (SNES) photograph]]
It is one of the three landmarks in Rio that [[Koopa Troopa]]s steal artifacts from.
