'''Toriuo''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Toriuo sprite.png|frame|left]]
Toriuo are a hybrid of a bird and a fish and therefore have the bodily features of both.

===Behavior===
Much like [[Cheep Cheep]]s, they [[jump]] out of a body of water and attack anything in their way. However, Birdfish glide in the air a bit longer than Cheep Cheeps, using their wings to fly through the air.

==History==
Toriuo only appear in the [[Hippo (level)|Hippo]] level. Mario can defeat a Toriuo by either [[stomp]]ing it or by throwing a [[fireball]] at it.
