The '''Best Fitness Friends''' (often shortened to '''BFF''' and also referred to as the '''Brute Force Federation''') are a trio of minions that serve Fawful. They only appear in ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. Their real group name is the Brute Force Federation.

== Members ==
[[File:MLBISBJJBestFitnessFriends.png|thumb|The Best Fitness Friends merged monster form]]
*'''[[Dieter]]:''' the Ranged-type member. He can speak German and is very vain.
*'''[[Beef]]:''' the Melee-type member. He was absent during the first half of the game, with [[Ludwig von Koopa|Ludwig]] being his doppelganger.
*'''[[Kaley]]:''' the Flying-type member. 
*The three members can [[Best Fitness Friends (merged form)|fuse into a monster]].

== ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'' ==
The trio are seen first after [[Bowser]] leaves his castle in the cutscene where [[Fawful]] reveals his plan to take over it. He then sends the BFF to introduce the [[Skeletone Formula:D]] as a cure for the blorbs within the [[Koopa Troop]] army. Bowser Jr. and subsequently all the [[Koopalings]] that are guarding the castle eventually went off to find the ingredients, leaving only the minions within the castle, thus succeeding in the take over. Later on, Fawful is implied to have gotten the [[Vacuum Shroom]] from Dieter, who insults Fawful's other henceman Midbus as a slow pig in a cutscene of [[Dimble Wood]]. While Bowser Jr.'s party was searching for the Wigglers' Giga Carrot, they shadowed them in order to steal the ingredients once all are gathered. [[Ludwig von Koopa|Ludwig]] senses this and departs, later on becoming Beef's doppelganger. Kaley then leaks info of Fawful's invasion onto Bowser's Castle to the remaining Koopalings, so [[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]] and [[Larry Koopa|Larry]] depart from Bowser Jr. in order to investigate this.

Upon returning to Bowser's Castle, which was now been invaded by Fawful's army, Wendy and Larry manage to save [[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]] whom was recently beaten up by Dieter. However, the trio end up being trapped in barrels by Dieter, but when a giant-sized [[Bowser]] lifts his castle up from the ground, only Wendy's barrel remained, which "Beef" picked up.

The Best Fitness Friends later spy on Bowser Jr.'s group (which has no been diminished to [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton]], [[Kamek]], and [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]] (in which he is later forced to leave by Bowser Jr.) through [[Cavi Cape]]. After Iggy departed from the group, he was pushed off a cliff by the trio.

== Trivia ==
* In the Japanese version, they are called '''SS Company''' (short for Super Suppiler Company or Shin Satsu Company. Shin Satsu means 'Instant Kill).
*The Best Fitness Friends are among the few villains in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise who have never met [[Mario]] himself.
*Their abbreviated name "BFF", is often famously short for the phrase "Best Friends Forever" which is used to express that two (or more) individuals are lifelong friends. In more recent year's it's often used to refer to a person's best friend.
