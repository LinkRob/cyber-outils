==Gameplay==
The player must hold the Wii Remote like "The Waiter" form and tap it with the other hand to beat it. When a note lands on the castanet, hit the Wii Remote. As a result, it turns into a frog and it jumps out, otherwise the note is eaten by the snake. If the microgame is won,cherry blossoms bloom and shrink.
*'''Level 1:''' Hit the beat once.
*'''Level 2:''' Hit the two notes.
*'''Level 3:''' Hit the three notes.
==Trivia==
*[[Mona Pizza]]'s theme from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' was played on the second level.
