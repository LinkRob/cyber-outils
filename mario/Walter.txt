==History==

Walter can be found living in [[Flipside]] with his wife [[Gladys]]. Walter appears not to have the best of memory due to his old age. However, Walter is a lover of both food and games as he has several high scores in the [[Flipside Arcade]].

*Walter's [[Flopside]] counterpart is [[Harold]].
