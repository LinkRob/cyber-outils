'''Super Hammers''' are items in the [[Game Boy]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]''. In addition to being able to defeat enemies, Super Hammers can break through brick walls and some floors. They are introduced in the [[Desert]] in [[Stage 5-1]]. Mario cannot jump while holding a Super Hammer, but like regular hammers, he can throw them upward, allowing him to take them to a higher platform.
