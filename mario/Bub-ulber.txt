'''Bub-ulber''' is a character that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a [[Bub-ulb]] that lives in [[Petalburg]]. He always have the craving for food.

== History ==
Bub-ulber's role in the game isn't that significant but he does have a [[Trouble Center|trouble]] that he needs done. Bub-ulber requests some [[Hot Dog|hot dogs]] and a [[Mousse Cake]]. The hot dogs can be bought at [[Glitzville]] for 10 coins (make sure to buy two hot dogs since he will ask for another after he ate the first one) and the Mousse Cake can be made by giving [[Zess T.]] a [[Cake Mix]] that can be bought at the [[Pianta Parlor]] for 6 Pianta Tokens. After completing his request, Bub-ulber will give him a [[Dried Bouquet]].

[[Mario]] can return to Bub-ulber anytime to receive more Dried Bouquets if Mario brings him more hot dogs.
