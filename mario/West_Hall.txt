The '''West Hall''', titled '''West Hallway''' in PAL releases, is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Description==
The West Hall is one of the rooms in the [[Haunted Towers]]. It acts as a hallway between a few rooms, and it has three doors, with the left one leading into the [[West Bedroom]], the right one leading into the [[Crow's Nest]], and the north one leading into the [[West Bathroom]]. The West Hall serve little other purpose, although [[Spider (Luigi's Mansion series)|spiders]] can appear here, and overgrown plants cover portions of the room. The West Hall can either be entered from the left door in Crow's Nest or the right door inside the West Bedroom.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
In B-4: [[Pool Party]], [[Luigi]] has to enter the West Hall to then enter the West Bathroom. However, the West Bathroom door is locked, so Luigi first has to obtain its [[key]] before he can unlock the door and enter the West Bathroom.

In B-5: [[Doggone Key]], Luigi has to fight three [[Creeper]]s in the West Hall before he can catch the [[Polterpup]] in the West Bedroom.

In the bonus mission [[Hostile Intrusion]], the West Hall is one of the rooms that could have several [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] in it.
