'''Orange Juice Generators''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
Orange Juice Generators are a zigzag, sponge-like type object that blocks certain things from being accessible, such as a [[Blue Coin]]. If [[Mario]] touches one, he takes damage. An Orange Juice Generator can only be dissolved if [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] spits [[juice]] at it, although the Orange Juice Generator returns after Mario leaves and re-enters the location.

==Locations==
There are a total of six Orange Juice Generators. A majority of them appear in Delfino Plaza.

*On a small island near Delfino Plaza, blocking the Warp Pipe to the [[Lily Pad Ride]].
*In Delfino Plaza, behind the top of the police station's tower, where it covers a small opening with a Blue Coin.
*In Delfino Plaza, on a building near the [[Cannon (Super Mario Sunshine)|cannon]] that leads to [[Pinna Park]]. This Orange Juice Generator also covers a small opening with a Blue Coin.
*In [[Ricco Harbor]]'s eighth episode, [[Yoshi's Fruit Adventure]], blocking the entrance to a cage containing a [[Shine Sprite]].
*In [[Gelato Beach]], covering a [[Dune Bud]] to the left of the [[Sand Cabana]]
*In [[Pianta Village]]'s fifth episode, [[Secret of the Village Underside]], covering the entrance to a secret level on a green mushroom platform.
