'''Elevator lifts''' are objects in [[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]. This will start whenever the player steps on them either up or down. They must land them again to swap their direction. A certain variant also exists, and it continuously goes right while the player changes its  path, though it vanishes it reaches a certain distance. They appear in [[World 6-B (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6-B]] and the second level of the Coin Challenge Pack A, where the elevator lifts are fairly common throughout both levels.
