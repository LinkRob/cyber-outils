'''Turtles''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Turtle has a blue body, a green shell, yellow claws, and a yellow beak.

===Behavior===
A Turtle always carries a large chunk of ice at first, and they attempt to hinder [[Wario]]'s progress. If a Turtle loses its ice chunk, it becomes defenseless.

==In ''Wario World''==
Turtles are only encountered in [[Shivering Mountains]]. [[Wario]] can destroy a Turtle's ice chunk using either his [[Dash Attack]] or one of his [[Mad Move]]s. While a Turtle is unguarded, Wario can pick it up and throw it.

==Trivia==
*Their Japanese name, ノッコー (''Nokkō'') suggests that they are a variant of [[Koopa Troopa]]s.
