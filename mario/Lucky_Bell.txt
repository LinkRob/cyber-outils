The '''Lucky Bell '''is a new power-up in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' that transforms [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Toad]], [[Princess Peach]], or [[Rosalina]] into their [[Lucky Cat Mario|their Lucky Cat forms]]. 

It is called a '''Lucky Bell''' because when the character [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounds]], they will transform into a gold cat statue of themselves and a trail of [[Coin]]s is made as they fall. It also grants them temporary invincibility for as long as the player holds one of the Ground Pound buttons, which is similar to the [[Tanooki Suit]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', and the [[Statue Leaf]] from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. Lucky Bells make their debut in [[Spiky Mount Beanpole]] in [[World Mushroom (Super Mario 3D World)|World Mushroom]].
