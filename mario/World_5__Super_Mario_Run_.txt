'''World 5''' is the fifth world of ''Super Mario Run''. Although its exact terrain is unknown, it contains four levels that consist of the sky, desert, mansion, and castle themes, respectively. This world is available after completion of World 4, and unlocks [[World 6 (Super Mario Run)|World 6]] upon its own completion. 

==Levels==

===[[Lakitu's Revenge]]===
A sky level with Spiny-throwing Lakitus, moving platforms, and Fuzzies.

===[[Pokey Vaulting]]===
A desert level with Pokeys, Koopa Paratroopas, and moving platforms.

===[[Boohind Lock and Key]]===
A mansion level with Boos and Stretches.

===[[Rings of Fire!]]===
A castle level with Rings of Fire, Podoboos, and Dry Bones, as well as the game's fifth boss battle, against Fake Bowser.
