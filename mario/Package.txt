The '''Package''' is a key item which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
The Package can only be obtained once [[Mario]] accepts one of [[Koopa Koot]]'s many favors. Mario must head to [[Gusty Gulch]] and retrieve the Package from one of the [[Boo]]s living there. Mario must wait a little while (usually requires leaving a few screens and coming back) to receive the Package and once he does, he can take it back to Koopa Koot to be awarded with a [[coin]].

== In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
{{Q|An item Goldbob asked you to deliver to General White.|Description}}
In this game, the Package is part of a [[Trouble Center|trouble]] requested by [[Goldbob]] of [[Poshley Heights]]. He requests [[Mario]] to deliver an important Package to [[General White]]. However, this involves going on another wild goose chase to search for General White until the Package can be delivered to him. Mario is then rewarded with 64 coins from Goldbob.

==Trivia==
*In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', the Package uses the same sprite image for the [[Box]].
