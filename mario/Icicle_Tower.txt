'''Icicle Tower''' is a level in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

== Layout ==
{{Sectstub}}

== [[Star Coin]]s ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': On the second lifting platform, go on the right side of it and go in the hidden passageway almost at the top while avoid the icicles to get the coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Jump on the right side of the platform with the first three Dry Bones to destroy them with the icicles while avoiding them to get the Star Coin easily.
*'''Star Coin 3''': On the last elevator platform, go the left side of it to move to the hidden passageway with the Bony Beetles. Go near them to have them destroyed by the icicles and get the Star Coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
At the beginning of the level, jump on the "?" block (and maybe the "M" block to make it easier) and wall jump to the hidden area with a platform and a picture of Super Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' crouching.
