'''Circling Boo Buddies''' are many [[Boo Buddy|Boo Buddies]] in a circular formation found in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', they also appear in a line formation sometimes by the name of Boo Buddy Snake. These [[Boo]]s chase [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] during game-play to slow them down and make the player halt. The Boos cannot be beaten by any means and you are only to escape them. These Boos usually appear in darker and gloomy levels like [[Ghost House]]s in ''Super Mario World''.

These Boos, years later, appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' with an identical role. In these games, the only way to defeat them is with a [[Starman]]. The Circling Boo Buddies usually retain their spinning patterns (sometimes moving outwards and inwards) but they never leave their general location. They are only found in Ghost Houses.
