{{Nihongo|'''Nobuyuki Hiyama'''|檜山 修之|Hiyama Nobuyuki}} (born  August 25, 1967) is a Japanese voice actor at [[Arts Vision]]. He is the current voice actor for [[Topper]] and the [[Tostarenan]]s, having voiced them since their original appearances in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.
