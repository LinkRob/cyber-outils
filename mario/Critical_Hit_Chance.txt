The '''Critical Hit Chance''' is a weapon skill in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
Critical Hit Chance boosts the weapon's chance of landing a critical hit on an enemy.
