*[[Tenderling]]s|caption=[[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Starlow]] and [[Princess Peach]] in Flab Zone, from [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' + ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'']]}}The '''Flab Zone''' is an area inside of [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. In this area, [[Princess Peach]] gets kidnapped once again, this time, not by Bowser. She gets kidnapped by six [[Alpha Kretin|Beta Kretins]].

In this area, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must trek through a maze similar to [[Joke's End]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. There are ten [[Attack Piece]]s here which, when collected, grants the player the [[Jump Hat]] special attack.

After trekking the maze, the player must put up a difficult fight with the Beta Kretins, who melded together to form the all powerful Alpha Kretin. After destroying it, Peach is saved and the adventure switches to Bowser's role.
