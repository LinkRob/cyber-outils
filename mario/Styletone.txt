'''Styletone''' is a character appearing in [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']].

== Description ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Styletone takes her design from a T-shirt. They are magenta in colour, and possesses pink eyes like other Fractones.

== In ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'' ==
Styletone serves as a shopkeeper who runs a clothing store chain called Style Shops and sells outfits that [[Donkey Kong (character)|DK]] and [[Pauline]] can purchase by handing over fossils.
