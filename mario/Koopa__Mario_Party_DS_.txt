'''Koopa''' is a supporting character who appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. He is a regular [[Koopa Troopa]] with a [[Green Shell]], and his grandfather is [[Koopa Krag]].

==''Mario Party DS''==
Koopa only physically appears in Story mode. He is first seen when [[Mario]] and the others make their way to [[Kamek's Library]], informing the group that [[Kamek]] had imprisoned his grandfather into a book. Koopa asks Mario and his friends to help free Koopa Krag. At the end, after Kamek is defeated and Koopa Krag is freed, both Koopa and Koopa Krag reward the characters the fourth [[Sky Crystal]].
