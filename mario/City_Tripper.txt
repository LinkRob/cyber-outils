The '''City Tripper''' is a [[bike]] introduced in the ''[[Animal Crossing x Mario Kart 8]]'' downloadable pack for ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and reappears in the base game for ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is frequently associated with [[Isabelle]], namely the light-green color City Tripper. The vehicle has also made some appearances in the ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' series, as '''Isabelle Scooter''', in which it is an obtainable furniture item.
