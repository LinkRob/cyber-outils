'''Petal Guy''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is the namesake character representative of his species of the [[Mufti Guy|same name]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Petal Guy has the same appearance as a regular, red-robed member of his species.

===Personality traits===
Petal Guy is lovestruck, as he has a crush on [[Flutter (character)|Flutter]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, when the player's character arrives at Petal Guy's home, [[Petal House]], he asks them to deliver his [[Poem of Love]] to Flutter, starting the quest [[Blossom of My Heart]]. When the player's character finds Flutter in her home, which is on the third floor of [[Mushroom Condo]], Petal Guy also arrives, saying that he was searching for his love. Petal Guy proceeds to recite the poem, hoping that Flutter will love him. This is unsuccessful, as Flutter flies away from Petal Guy. Even after being rejected, Petal Guy proclaims that he is not giving up on his love, and rewards the player the [[Bead Machine]] [[Gaddget]] for helping him.

In the game's ending, it is revealed that Petal Guy gave up on poetry in favor of starting carpentry.
