'''Kooper's mother''' is a character that is mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
As the name states, she is the mother of [[Kooper]], one of [[Mario]]'s partners. Kooper's mother is mentioned in a letter addressed to Kooper that he can receive from [[Kolorado's Wife]]. Kolorado's wife mentions that Kooper's mother visited her the other day and the two of them sat down and had some [[Koopa Tea]] together. Kooper's mother wished to see her son, but was disappointed to hear he was still traveling.
