'''Sphere Mongers''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The minigame's name is a pun of "fearmonger".

== Opening ==
The player picks up the metallic spheres and drops it into their hole. The camera zooms out before the game starts.

== Gameplay ==
Players ride on UFOs that have magnets attached to the bottom. The objective is to collect many metallic spheres and drop them in the player's colored hole within a certain time limit. Silver spheres are worth one point while golden spheres are worth three points. The player with the most points wins once the minigame ends.

== Ending ==
The players fly away, and the winner's UFO flies to the screen and do their victory animation while sitting.
