'''Drop Shot''' is a [[1 vs. 3 Minigame|1-vs-3]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The [[Bomb]] appear first where they are laying  on the conveyor. The [[Spike]] picks the bomb up for the player before the minigame starts.

== Gameplay ==
The solo player can move the cursor and aim at its own targets while Spike throws the bomb to the player. The team must evade the player's throw until thirty seconds pass. If the player does not hit the bomb, the bomb explodes behind.  When the player manages to hit the target, the team player flies to the air and the torches light up. The team players that are already hit are seen dizzy behind the fence.

== Ending ==
If the solo player wins, the winner does her/his victory animation while the teams groan behind the fence and the Spike cheers. If the team wins, the camera is aiming at the team that do their victory animations while the solo player keeps swinging his/her racket and the Spike sulks.
