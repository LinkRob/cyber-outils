}}A '''Dark Dayzee''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
Dark Dayzees are stronger, darker versions of [[Crazee Dayzee]]s and can be only found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Dark Dayzees, like with Crazee Dayzees, attack with a singing attack that can travel a good distance. Dark Dayzees are very powerful as they have more HP than even [[Amazee Dayzee]]s. Using [[Bowser]] is the best way to take them out.
