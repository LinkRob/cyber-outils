'''Akiki''' is a tomboyish [[Ukiki]] and the niece of the previous mayor of the [[jungle area]], whose name is simply Ukiki. She appears only in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==History==
[[File:Akiki MPA screenshot.png|thumb|left|240px]]
In ''Mario Party Advance'', she believes that her uncle has been kidnapped and asks the player and [[Shroomlock]] to find him. The message Ukiki has left Akiki read, "bIg teAM cAT THElf bLAKE." - the capital letters spell "I am at the lake," referring to [[Loch Dorrie]]. Once the player finds Ukiki, he tells the player that he faked his kidnapping, wanting to play a joke on Akiki.
