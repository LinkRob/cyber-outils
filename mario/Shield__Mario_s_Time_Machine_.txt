The '''Shield ''' is an item in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] and PC versions of ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''.

==Description==
It is the shield that [[Joan of Arc]] used during her battles. In both versions of ''Mario's Time Machine''. its design features a blue sword on a white background, with brown specks on the shield's upper half.

==In ''Mario's Time Machine''==
The Shield is among the items that [[Bowser]] stole throughout history to add to his [[Bowser's Museum|museum]]. [[Mario]] has to time travel back to [[Orleans]] to return the Shield, but because he does not know whom the Shield belongs to, Mario has to ask the inhabitants of the camp about the current situation. When Mario shows the Shield to the [[Duke of Alençon]], he tells Mario that the Shield belongs to Joan of Arc and that it has to be instantly be brought to her. Once Mario returns the Shield to Joan, she quickly takes it and begins preparations for the assault on Orleans.
