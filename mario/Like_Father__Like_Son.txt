'''Like Father, Like Son''' is the eighth level in [[Bowser's Castle]], and the final level in the main storyline of ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Overview==
[[Bowser Jr.]], [[Kamek]] and the [[Koopalings]] march to the [[Best Fitness Friends]], who they have been waiting for in the throne room. [[Dieter]] asks Bowser Jr. the [[Koopa Troop]] are evil minions. Bowser Jr., confused after realizing their efforts on kidnapping [[Princess Peach]] and fighting with the [[Mario]] [[Luigi|Bros.]], he asks the other Koopalings for an answer, but all of them are unsure. When asked by Dieter again, Bowser Jr. replies saying good and evil are simplistic, outdated terms.

The fight then commences. Bowser Jr. and his squad will fight [[Fawfulcopter]]s, [[Borp]]s, [[Choomba]]s, [[Magifoofa]]s, [[Crawful]]s and the Best Fitness Friends themselves in rounds 1-4. After being defeated in the first round, they all take [[Malatone Formula:X]] and threaten Doom upon them, [[Fawful]] and the entire [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

After being defeated in the fourth round, all three members [[Best Fitness Friends (merged form)|merge into a monstrous entity]]. In the final battle, it creates dark clones of the Koopalings, but Bowser Jr. says he's had enough of their tricks and attempts to harm the Koopa Troop, and they'll have to answer to him.

Afterwards, the Best Fitness Friends are defeated for good. Bowser Jr. then collects the [[Skeletone Formula:D]] supplements. The Koopalings also witness the [[Dark Star]]s defeat from [[Princess Peach's Castle]], and are aware of [[Bowser]]'s efforts of defeating the [[Dark Star (character)|Dark star]]. The minions then celebrate their victory. As Bowser returns, injured from a fight with the Mario Bros., the Koopa Troop rebuild the castle and repair the damages made, whilst Bowser Jr. feeds the Skeletone supplements to the blorbed [[Goomba]]s, curing them. Everyone then lifts Bowser Jr., proud of his efforts leading the Koopa Troop to victory.
