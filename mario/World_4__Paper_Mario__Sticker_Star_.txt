'''World 4''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It is the fourth [[world]] explored by [[Mario]].

==Description==
World 4 is an ice-themed world featuring snow and several icy peaks. Its boss is [[Mizzter Blizzard]], who disguised himself as the Bowser Snow Statue. World 4 is located west of the [[Decal Channel]], and it is only accessible by using the ship at [[Surfshine Harbor]].

Unique enemies found in World 4 include [[Snow Spike]]s and [[Ice Bro]]s. Shiny enemies such as [[Shiny Goomba]]s and [[Shiny Koopa Troopa]]s are also first encountered here.
