'''Large Fry''' is the penultimate boss in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
He is a large, light green fish dressed as a chef.

===Powers and abilities===
In its boss battle, Large Fry showcases its ability to perform a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]], causing two sacks of enemies to drop down. A variety of enemies can appear in these sacks, including [[Bandinero]]s (red or yellow), [[Buccanero]]s, [[Shortfuse]]s, [[Nosedozer]]s, or [[Recapitator]]s. Large can also dash from one side of the screen to the other, and he glows green before doing so. Large Fry may also spit out giant homing missiles.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
[[File:Large Fry WLSI battle.jpg|thumb|left|Wario fighting Large Fry in ''Wario Land: Shake It!'']]
Large Fry is fought by [[Wario]] in [[Quiver Cliffs]]. Wario can dodge Large Fry's charging attack by [[duck]]ing. To damage Large Fry, Wario has to throw an enemy into his mouth, causing Large Fry to spit out a [[bomb]], which Wario must then throw back in. When the bomb explodes inside of Large Fry, he drops to the floor, leaving him open to one of Wario's attacks. Defeating Large Fry rewards the player with the Large Fry Emblem and access to the [[Shakedown Schooner]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Large Fry's name is play on the phrase "small fry," but with the word "Large" used to refer to its large size.
