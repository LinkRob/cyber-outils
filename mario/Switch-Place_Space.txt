A '''Switch-Place Spaces''', named '''Front to Back Space''' in PAL releases, is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Switch-Place Spaces only appear in the board [[Star-Crossed Skyway]]. If a character lands onto one, the current [[Mini Star]] or [[Mini Ztar]] holder at the [[Star Stage]] is launched by the star underneath it to the back of the line, so the next character in line is available to give their Mini Stars or Mini Ztars to whoever makes it to the Star Stage next.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
There are a total of ten of Switch-Place Spaces on Star-Crossed Skyway, specifically in the second, third, and fourth quarters of the board.
