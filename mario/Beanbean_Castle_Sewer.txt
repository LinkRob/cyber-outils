'''Beanbean Castle Sewer''', originally pluralized as '''Beanbean Castle Sewers''', is the waterworks found in the underground of [[Beanbean Castle]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|its remake]].

== History ==
The Beanbean Castle Sewer runs the water supply of the castle and [[Beanbean Castle Town]]. [[Cackletta]] locked [[Lady Lima]] and several more of the castle's inhabitants in the sewer.

When [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] arrive at the castle, Cackletta, disguised as Lady Lima, pretends not to trust them so that they can go into the sewer and fix the plumbing to prove that they are truly who they say they are. Once the real Lady Lima is free, she thanks them. However, fixing the sewer's pipes turns off the [[Beanstar]]'s protection and is vulnerable to theft. Lady Lima tells the Mario Bros. that the person they encountered whom they thought to be Lady Lima was Cackletta.

==Trivia==
*In the original Japanese version of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', there is a [[Heart Block]] in the Beanbean Castle Sewers, giving [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] the opportunity to fully restore their [[HP]] and [[BP]]. While it was removed from other regional releases, the Heart Block was reinstated in the [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]].
