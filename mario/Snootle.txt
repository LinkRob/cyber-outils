'''Snootles''' are green, circular enemies introduced in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|Super Mario Bros. Wonder.]]

Snootles appear only in the level [[Valley Fulla Snootles]]. Snootles jump and can also bounce off of walls. A big Snootle can also be found in Valley Fulla Snootles. When Snootles hit items or other enemies, they bounce off them.
