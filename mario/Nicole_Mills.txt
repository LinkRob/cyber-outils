'''Nicole Mills''' is a Canadian voice actress, art director and graphic designer.  She was the temporary voice for [[Princess Peach]] when [[Jen Taylor]] left. She was also the original voice of [[Baby Peach]] in [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]. Both roles would be taken over by [[Samantha Kelly]] in [[Mario Strikers Charged]] and [[Mario Kart Wii]].

== Voices ==

* [[Princess Peach]] (2005-2007)
* [[Baby Peach]] (2005)

== Roles ==

* ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'' – [[Princess Peach]]
* ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' – Princess Peach / [[Baby Peach]]
* [[New Super Mario Bros.]] - Princess Peach
* ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]'' – Princess Peach (shared with Samantha Kelly)
