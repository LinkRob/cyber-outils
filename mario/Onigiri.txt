'''Onigiri''' is an opponent faced by [[Mario]] in ''[[Wrecking Crew '98]]''. It is a living ball of rice, more specifically {{w|onigiri}}, which it is named after.

==In ''Wrecking Crew '98''==
Onigiri is the opponent of Stage A and the first secret opponent fought by Mario. To face Onigiri, [[Koopa Troopa]] has to be defeated within four minutes to unlock Stage A. Onigiri itself can be unlocked as a playable character in multiplayer by defeating it.
