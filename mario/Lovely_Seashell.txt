The '''Lovely Seashell''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The Lovely Seashell is a key item found on [[Sandy Beach]]. [[Goombob]] asks the player's character to obtain the Lovely Seashell for him, as Goombob wants to give it as a gift to his crush, [[Goombetty]]. When the player's character returns with it to [[Goombob Manor]], Goombob excitedly asks for the Lovely Seashell, at which point Goombetty walks in. She notices the Lovely Seashell, prompting Goombob to give it to her and ask her out on a date. Goombetty says that the Lovely Seashell is pretty and agrees to Goombob's proposal, and because of this, Goombob happily rewards the player the [[Dessert Menu]] [[Gaddget]].
