|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Dizzy Shell''' is a special move that is used in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is one of [[Kooper]]'s moves and he can only use it once he reaches the [[Super Rank]]. This move requires 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Usage==

With Dizzy Shell, the player must press the A Button repeatedly in order to power up this move. After the gauge is filled (or about five seconds have past), Kooper will attack all ground enemies on the screen with the chance of inflicting the [[Dizzy]] ailment on them. However, this move doesn't work on airborne enemies and it doesn't damage enemies at all.

==Trivia==

*[[Dark Koopa]]s also use a move similar to the Dizzy Shell.
