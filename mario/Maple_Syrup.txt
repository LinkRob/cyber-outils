'''Maple Syrup''' is an item featured in some of the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games. Drinking it regenerates [[Flower Point|FP]], the number of which varies between the games. Maple Syrup is a stronger version of [[Honey Syrup]].

==Appearances==
===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
Maple Syrup debuts in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It restores 40 FP when used. It is green in color and comes in glass bottles. It can be bought at shops in [[Moleville]], [[Marrymore]], [[Seaside Town]], the [[Sea]]/[[Sunken Ship]], [[Monstro Town]], [[Nimbus Land]], [[Barrel Volcano]], [[Bowser's Keep]], and the [[Factory]].

===''Paper Mario'' series===
Maple Syrup returns in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', being a regular item that is also used in several recipes. This item restores 10 FP and can be purchased in the shops in [[Harry's Shop]] of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]], [[Star Haven]], and [[Shiver City]] in ''Paper Mario'', and [[Twilight Town]], the [[Excess Express]], and [[Fahr Outpost]] in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', each at different prices. In the latter game, Maple Syrup is now a more realistic brown color and comes in pitchers.
