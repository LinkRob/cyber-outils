|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Captain Toad Takes a Spin''' is the second to last [[Captain Toad]] [[Captain Toad Courses|course]] to be played in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The level features P-Switches, that when stepped on, turn the course on its sides. The main enemy here is Fizzlits, who can spread across Captain Toad's path. If he gets shrunk, there's a mushroom hidden somewhere in the course. The player should see it by turn the game pad around.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': After hitting the first P Switch, avoiding the Fizzlit and get the star behind it.
*'''Green Star 2''': After getting the first star, drop down to the left side without hitting a P Switch to get the Star.
*'''Green Star 3''': After hitting the second P Switch, go to the bottom floor and run up the ramp to get the star.
*'''Green Star 4''': After hitting the third P Switch, avoid the two Fizzlits blocking the path and get the star.
*'''Green Star 5''': Hit the fourth P Switch at the right moment to avoid being attacked by the Fizzlit and get the star.
