'''World 1-4''' is the fourth and final level of [[World 1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. It is the first castle level in the game and the entire series.

== Layout ==
The player starts in an elevated area with stairs that lead downwards, eventually ending at a [[lava]] pit. Following this is a platform surrounded by lava. It features a [[Fire-Bar]], along with a [[? Block]] above it, which contains a power-up. Up ahead is a corridor of Fire-Bars attached to the ceiling. After this is another room with Fire Bars attached to both the ceiling and the floor. Next up is a room where the player will find several [[Hidden Block]]s before the bridge where the [[fake Bowser]] is found. Above the bridge is a horizontally moving platform.

== Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' ==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 13000.

=== Red Coin locations ===
* Red Coin #1 is located above the first ? Block in the level.
* Red Coin #2 is located in the hallway with the Fire-Bars.
* Red Coin #3 is located within the following area, with the Fire-Bars.
* Red Coin #4 is located in the same area as the third coin.
* Red Coin #5 is located just above the fake Bowser.

=== Yoshi Egg location ===
* The Yoshi Egg is found inside the Hidden Block in the large empty space that has other Hidden Blocks, just before the fake Bowser.
