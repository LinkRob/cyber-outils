'''''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games''''' is a game for the [[Nintendo 3DS]], developed by [[Sega]] and produced by [[Nintendo]]. It was released shortly after its [[Wii]] [[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|counterpart]], and is the third handheld installment of the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' series. The game has 50 new sports and 20 characters, though none are newcomers to the series. The game takes place in London, home of the [[wikipedia:2012 Summer Olympics|2012 Summer Olympics]].
