'''Mushroom World''' is the name of the continent on the [[Mushroom Earth]] that encompasses the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], where [[Mario]] and his friends live. It is the setting of many ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. 

==Description==
[[File:SMB3sevenCountries.png|The eight worlds of ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' being described as countries in the instruction booklet|thumb|left]]
The [[Mushroom Kingdom]], [[Star Haven]], as well as all the lands are featured in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', are all a part of the Mushroom World continent. The Mushroom Kingdom forms the entrance of it.

The eight worlds of ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' are all kingdoms, or "countries" as stated by [[Iggy Koopa]] in the manual, and each are ruled by a separate [[king]]. The manual notes "The Mushroom Kingdom forms an entrance to the Mushroom World".

In the three DIC Entertainment cartoons, there are many lands located in the Mushroom World continent that so far have ''not'' appeared in any game, such as Dome City, several dark forests, and the Mushroom Sea.

The Mushroom World also consists of the many areas and levels from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' starting from Mario's house.{{clear}}


== Locations ==
There are many lands associated with the Mushroom Kingdom and thus the greater Mushroom World continent. These lands appear in the Mario series of games, as well as some spin-off titles. Some are from the ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' franchise.

*Mushroom World (continent)
**[[Mushroom Kingdom]] &ndash; One of the largest kingdoms on the planet. The Mushroom Kingdom is a frequently troubled but beautiful land home to Mario and Luigi and ruled by Princess Peach.
***[[Boo Woods]] &ndash; A dark, spooky, haunted woods and the location of [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Luigi's Mansion]].
***[[Mushroom Woods]]
***[[Lava Land]]
***[[Star Haven]] &ndash; The city of the [[Star (species)|Stars]] high in the sky (possibly in orbit) of Mushroom World and home of the powerful [[Star Rod]].
***[[Rogueport]] &ndash; A rough-and-tumble town in the outskirts of the Mushroom Kingdom.<ref>[https://twitter.com/NintendoAmerica/status/1775568951835083009 Nintendo of America Twitter account] [https://web.archive.org/web/20240403213001/https://twitter.com/NintendoAmerica/status/1775568951835083009 (archive)] ''"I am now on holiday traveling in the Mushroom Kingdom. In my travels, I came into possession of a mystical map...a treasure map, actually. It was inside a box I got from an old merchant in a town called Rogueport [...] I've included the map with this letter, so please bring it with you when you come. I'll meet you at Rogueport"'' [[Princess Peach]]</ref>
**[[Dark Land]] &ndash; The cloudy, volcanic homeland of the Koopas and Bowser's headquarters.
***[[Koopa Kingdom]] - A kingdom of [[Koopa]]s, [[Goomba]]s, [[Bob-Omb]]s, [[Boo]]s and many other races ruled by [[Bowser]].
**[[Water Land]] &ndash; The land's coastal areas.
**[[Giant Land]] &ndash; A land inhabited by enormous animals.
**[[Grass Land]] &ndash; A large and lush prairie country located somewhere near the Mushroom Kingdom.
**[[Ice Land]] &ndash; Mushroom World's shimmering polar lands.
**[[Desert Land]] &ndash; An ancient desert kingdom dotted with ruins.
**[[Sky Land]] &ndash; The vast blue sky of Mushroom World is home to a kingdom in the clouds.
**[[Pipe Land]] &ndash; A small island group covered in warp pipes of all shapes and sizes.
*[[Beanbean Kingdom]] &ndash; The Mushroom Kingdom's closest neighbor, bordering the Mushroom World continent by the Mushroom Kingdom, home of the [[Beanish|Bean people]].
