'''36-Volt Man''' is an object in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Description==
36-Volt Man is a fictional video game for the [[Nintendo GameCube]]. Little of the game is shown, but it is known to star the titular 36-Volt Man, and the game reuses sound effects from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
36-Volt Man is featured in the opening cutscene for [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s stage, [[Retro Action]]. Here, 36-Volt Man's release in [[Toy Express]] is announced, and a crowd of people arrive to purchase the game, including 9-Volt and 18-Volt. 9-Volt manages to acquire a copy, and he and 18-Volt rush back to [[5-Volt's House|9-Volt's House]] to play the game together. 9-Volt and 18-Volt end up playing 36-Volt Man throughout the night. After the two complete 36-Volt Man, they celebrate, with 9-Volt spinning records while 18-Volt dancing behind him.

When replaying Retro Action and clearing the boss [[microgame]], a short segment is sometimes shown of 9-Volt and 18-Volt playing 36-Volt Man, and it has the caption "I wish I could play games all day!".
