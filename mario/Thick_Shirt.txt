The '''Thick Shirt''' is an armor only usable by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the shop in [[Rose Town]] for 14 [[coin]]s. Equipping it increases Mario's [[Defense]] rating by 12 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 8.
