'''Mine Cart Owner '''is a supporting character in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

== Appearance ==
The Mine Cart Owner is an old [[Beanish]] man who owns the [[Mysterious Mine Carts]] with his Beanish dog.

== History ==
He appears inside [[Hoohoo Mountain]] as [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] entered in the mountain. He asked the Bros. if the care to ride the carts. He appears riding on wheels pulled by his dog during the tutorial. When the Bros. agree, they ride on the carts doing the mini-game. After completing the mini-game, he appears on the other side.
