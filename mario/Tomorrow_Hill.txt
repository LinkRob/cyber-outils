'''Tomorrow Hill''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Description==
It is a hill in the northwest of [[Diamond City]]. The location can also be seen when zoomed in on Diamond City's map. According to a nearby sign, one can see tomorrow from it.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
During [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s stage, [[Tomorrow Hill (stage)|Tomorrow Hill]], the duo has to drive a young woman named [[Alien Mitzi|Mitzi]] in their [[Dribble Taxi|taxi]] to Tomorrow Hill. The player plays through Dribble and Spitz's microgames as they make their way to Tomorrow Hill, during which the song of the [[Tomorrow Hill (song)|same name]] can be heard playing on the taxi's radio. In the ending, the duo reach their destination and drop off Mitzi, who reveals herself to be an alien when a [[UFO]] arrives to pick her up.
