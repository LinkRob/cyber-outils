'''Castaway Bay''' is a [[board]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Castaway Bay night.jpg|thumb|left|At nighttime]]
This game board is made up of three main islands and a port where the players start at. It is a one-way path (with junctions) to the [[Star Space]], where the characters would get on a boat belonging to either [[Donkey Kong]] or [[Bowser]]. Players who make it to [[DK's Riverboat]] are met by Donkey Kong, who gives the character a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] for 20 [[coin]]s. He then gives the character a safe ride back to the start. However, characters who stop at [[Bowser's Battle Yacht]] encounter [[Bowser]], who gives them a [[Ztar|Shadow Star]] that takes away a Star or 20 coins if they do not have a Star. The objective is very similar to [[Mario's Rainbow Castle]] from the original ''[[Mario Party]]'', where the end rotates between [[Toad (character)|Toad]] handing out a Star and Bowser a Ztar.

At night, an [[Alien (Mario Party series)|alien]] in a [[UFO]] can be seen on the eastern island. During the day, the UFO is wrecked in the water near the southeast corner of the island. Neither the alien nor UFO have any effect on gameplay.

===? Space events===
[[File:Ukiki Castaway Bay.png|thumb|200px|The ? Space event featuring a Ukiki]]
If a character lands on the [[? Space]] at any of the ports with a post and a bell, they hit the post, ring the bell, and the system switches the day/night cycle. Bowser's Battle Yacht and DK's Riverboat are switched around. One of these ? Spaces is right next to the current boat, adding to the suspense over who receives a Star and who does not.

If a character lands on the ? Space in front of the [[tree]] on the first island, [[Ukiki]] appears and asks if the player wants to play a game. If they agree, the character plays a short minigame where the goal is to try and catch the coins thrown by Ukikis while avoiding the [[Spiny Egg]]s, which stun the character temporarily. Ukiki usually throws in one or two [[coin bag]]s as well. The minigame lasts for ten seconds. There is no counter that keeps track of how many coins the character, with their total being shown only when the minigame is over.

The ? Space at the third island near the Star Space has two functions depending on the time of [[day]]. If it is [[daytime]], players have the choice to pay their respects to the Great [[Mushroom]] Spirit to get an [[Orb]] or coins. At night, the Mushroom statue is replaced by a [[Goomba]], and players have the choice to pay respects to the Great Goomba Spirit. The outcome is always [[Paragoomba]]s taking characters to exchange positions on the board, regardless of whether they decide to pay respects or not.

===Other events===
On the northern island there is a river flowing downstream. Players passing by are stopped by a [[Shy Guy]] who asks if they want to go [[raft]]ing for ten coins. Accepting takes players downward near the Star Space. If DK's Riverboat is docked at the Star Space, [[Bowser]] fires [[cannonball]]s at rafting players. If hit, the character loses five coins, and their turn ends prematurely.

Castaway Bay is one of two boards to feature [[Red Boo|Pink Boo]], other than [[Towering Treetop]]. A character can have Pink Boo can steal either coins or a Star from their opponent as long as it is nighttime.

==Spaces==
Because of Bowser and Donkey Kong's unique roles on the board, there are neither [[Bowser Space|Bowser]] nor [[DK Space]]s on Castaway Bay.

==Trivia==
*This is the only board in ''Mario Party 6'' where Bowser can be seen during the day. Conversely, it is also the only board where Donkey Kong can be seen at night.
**However, no [[DK|DK Space]]s nor [[Bowser Space]]s appear, due to Donkey Kong and Bowser's unique role on this board. 
***The day cycle uses the [[DK's Riverboat]]. And every three turns, it switches to night and players use the [[Bowser's Battle Yacht]]. 
*A slower-paced rendition of this board's music is used on the main menu of ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.
