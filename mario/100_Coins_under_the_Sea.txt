'''100 Coins under the Sea''' is the fifty-seventh course in the Story Mode of ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. This course is underwater-themed and is in the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' style. It was made by Eccentric Millionaire, and has a difficulty rating of 2/4 stars.

==Layout==
The level starts off with the player under the water. The player needs to get the [[coin]]s and avoid the Cheep Cheeps. They will also have to go through switching blocks. If [[Mario]] keeps on swimming, he'll get to the first [[Question Box]]. If he hits it, he will get a [[Fire Flower]] (even if he's Small Mario). If Mario gets the Fire Flower, he will turn into [[Fire Mario]]. Mario has to keep on swimming through the level, grabbing all the coins, and avoiding underwater enemies. He will then have to go through transparent pipes to continue the level. He will then have to avoid Bloopers while grabbing coins and going through the pipes. Then eventually, Mario will reach the [[Goal Pole|goal]], and complete the level.

==Description==
''"Could someone indulge me by playing this course? It's simple. Collect 100 Coins and reach the goal. But watch out: I added Cheep Cheeps, Bloopers, and other traps!"''
