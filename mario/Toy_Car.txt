'''Toy Car '''(トイカー, ''Toi Kā'') is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. It is a mini purple car driven by a miniature [[Wario]], similar to the [[Wario Car]]. A Toy Car has a spike on the front, and it moves back and forth trying to harm Wario with it.
