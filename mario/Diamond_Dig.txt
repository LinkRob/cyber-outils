'''Diamond Dig''' is one of the [[microgame]]s under [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place during the daytime. The surface is composed of dirt, which has a small gap within it.

==Gameplay==
As [[Wario]] falls, the player has to move him so that he can safely land in the gap to collect a yellow [[diamond]]. If Wario lands on the dirt, the player has only a brief second to move him toward the dirt until Wario can no longer move. If Wario does not land in the gap with the diamond, the player loses at the microgame. In Levels 2 and 3, there is an additional stone block obstacle that the player has to navigate Wario around.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1''': There are no obstacles.
*'''Level 2''': There is a small stone block placed above the yellow diamond.
*'''Level 3''': There is a long stone block placed above the yellow diamond.

==Trivia==
[[File:BLOCK minigame Polygon Studio.png|thumb|250px|The BLOCK minigame in ''Mario Artist: Polygon Studio'']]
*Diamond Dig originated in the form of a minigame in ''[[Mario Artist: Polygon Studio]]'', titled "BLOCK."
*Diamond Dig is one of three Introduction microgames to not appear in either [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s, [[Kat and Ana]]'s, or Wario's second stage, the other two being [[Wario Wear]] and [[Busted! (WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!)|I Spy]].
