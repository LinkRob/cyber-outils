|image = [[File:PlessiePlungingFalls.png|250px]]}}'''Plessie's Plunging Falls''' is the fourth level of [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]] of the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. This level introduces [[Plessie]].

==Features==
The main feature of this level involves riding Plessie through the level. The players must move Plessie around as he can jump and move quickly over speed boosts.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Jump off the first ramp as Plessie.
*'''Green Star 2''': After passing fifteen unknown enemies, go into the secret hiding behind a waterfall.
*'''Green Star 3''': Jump at an accurate distance from the center of the last ramp as Plessie.

==Stamp==
[[File:Plessie's_Plunging_Falls_Stamp.png|thumb]]After passing fifteen of the Splounders, stick to the main course on the windy path and get the stamp of Plessie.

==Hidden Luigi Image==
Immediately after the first set of boost panels, there is an 8-bit Luigi floating on the surface of the water.
