'''Vampire Wario''' is a counterpart of [[Wario]] featured as a character in ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|port]] included within ''[[Nintendo Puzzle Collection]]''. Unlike ''[[Wario Land 3]]'', in which [[Vampire Wario]] is the name of a transformation, the one in ''Dr. Mario 64'' is both a standalone character and a transformation of Wario's.

==History==
[[File:Vampire Wario DM64 icon.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]'', if the player is playing as [[Dr. Mario]] and has not lost or rematch at any point in normal mode or greater, Wario will eat the [[Capsule#Dr. Mario 64|Megavitamins]] and transform into a vampire and a match will ensue. Defeating him in one battle will unlock Vampire Wario as a playable character. In multiplayer modes, Vampire Wario has the highest-level AI along with [[Rudy]] and [[Metal Mario]]. Vampire Wario's losing animation has him transform into a bat, while his winning animation has him cackling.
