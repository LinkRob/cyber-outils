'''Ankoopas''' (also known as '''Giant Turtles''' or simply '''Turtles''') are a species of fat [[Koopa Troopa]]s found in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They can be knocked over by using [[Mario]]'s [[Firebrand]] and then having [[Luigi]] ram into it. In the [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]], they are replaced by [[Gargantua Koopa Troopa]]s.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''==
They appear in caves and other places around the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] and recite the same line. They block paths in different paths of the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. After Mario and Luigi master the [[Fire Tickle]], they can knock down Ankoopas. After Mario and Luigi knock down the Ankoopa, it expresses shock and moves out of their way.

=== Locations ===
Below is a list of all 9 of the the Ankoopas, their locations, and what they're blocking the path to.

'''Ankoopas #1 and #2'''<br>
Path Blocked: Progression<br>
In the Jellyfish Sisters Cave on the [[Fire Tickle]] tutorial side.

'''Ankoopas #3 and #4'''<br>
Path Blocked: Progression<br>
East of the path to the [[Relaxation Room]].

'''Ankoopa #5'''<br>
Path Blocked: [[Presenter]] holding a [[Golden Mushroom]]<br>
In a cave near the waterfall at the back of [[Hoohoo Village]] accessable through a cave hut.

'''Ankoopa #6'''<br>
Path Blocked: [[Yoshi Theater]]<br>
North of [[Beanbean Castle Town]] in the [[Beanbean Fields]] near the [[Tanoomba]]s.

'''Ankoopa #7'''<br>
Path Blocked: Alternate path to [[Teehee Valley]] / [[Piranha Bean]]'s area.<br>
North East of [[Beanbean Castle Town]] at the beach of [[Beanbean Fields]] on the bridge above (knocking over this Ankoopa is optional but works as a shortcut and more convenient route).

'''Ankoopa #8'''<br>
Path Blocked: [[Chucklehuck Woods]], [[Winkle Colosseum]]<br>
West of the area in [[Chucklehuck Woods]] with the two tiny holes accessible by [[Mini-Mario]] near several [[Question Block]]s.

'''Ankoopa #9'''<br>
Path Blocked: None<br>
In the house closest to [[Starbeans Café]] in [[Beanbean Castle Town]]. This Ankoopa isn't blocking a path but rather just lives in the house and as a result, cannot be knocked over.


== Etymology ==
Ankoopa's name is a combination of "anchor" and "Koopa". An anchor is a heavy object attached to a vessel, relating to Ankoopa as being heavy to move.
