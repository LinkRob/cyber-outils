A '''Dark Paragoomba''' is a dark variant of a [[Paragoomba]] that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They have 10 HP and 2 Attack. They only appear in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]] in rooms 3, 4, 14, 24, and 47. Similarly to their Paragommba counterpart, they bounce around their location.
