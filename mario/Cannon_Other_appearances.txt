In ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for the [[Game Boy]], cannons appear in a variety of forms. In the [[Desert]], the cannons have a dated look taken from the Middle Ages (the image on the left); on the [[Airplane]], cannons are made out of steel and look more contemporary (middle image). Both types of cannons retain the same basic function and fire out bullets that look like [[Bullet Biff]]s, acting similar to the seed-spitting [[Dōryī]] except they sometimes fire two shots at a time. In later Airplane levels, different cannons charge up energy balls and routinely change their angle of fire, shooting diagonally and upwards as well (the image on the right). If Mario gets hit by any of their projectiles, he loses a life.

===''Donkey Kong 64''===
A single cannon appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' in the [[Gloomy Galleon]] stage. To use it, [[Chunky Kong]] must put a cannonball into it. He must use the cannon to fire cannonballs at moving targets on the wall. By firing at enough of them, Chunky is awarded with a [[Golden Banana]].

===''Donkey Kong Country'' (Game Boy Color)===
In ''[[Donkey Kong Country (Game Boy Color)|Donkey Kong Country]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]], the cannons of the [[Gangplank Galleon|Gang-Plank Galleon]] appear as targets in the final stage of [[Crosshair Cranky]], where they must be destroyed before they fire on the player.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series===
====''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''====
[[File:Boss Game 2 MvDK2.png|thumb|120px|A cannon in ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis'']]
Cannons<ref>''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis'' North American instruction booklet, page 18.</ref> appear in boss fights against [[Donkey Kong]] in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''. The player utilizes these weapons to launch [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Marios]] directly at Donkey Kong to inflict damage.

The mechanism operates differently depending on the stage layout. On odd-numbered floors, the cannon attaches to the base of a circular, gear-like object that moves left and right along a pulley system connected to the gear. On even-numbered floors, the gear features a distinct appendage with the cannon fixed directly onto its end.

To operate the device, the player uses the [[stylus]] to drag its handle for aiming and then taps the Attack Button labeled "SHOOT". Furthermore, the player can hold {{button|ds|L}} or {{button|ds|R}} to activate the Boss Cannon Indicator, which provides a visual guideline that indicates the trajectory a Mini Mario will travel when fired. If the cannon shoots a Mini Mario onto a platform, a nearby item falls onto Donkey Kong to damage him.

The promotional [[Adobe Flash]] game ''[[Cannon Kaos|Mario vs. DK 2: Cannon Kaos]]'' includes a cannon as a central gameplay mechanic.

====''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!''====
In ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!]]'', cannons function identically as in ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis'', being used to shoot Mini Marios at Donkey Kong in boss battles.

====''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!''====
[[File:MvsDKFinalBattle.png|thumb|left|120px|Final boss cannon in ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!'']]
[[File:Mobile and Static Cannons.png|thumb|100px|Single-direction cannon (left) and multidirectional cannon (right)]]
Cannons appear as common objects in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!]]'', first appearing in [[Cannon Cove]] as the main object. They also appear as the main weapon against the final boss, like in the previous games. There are two types: multidirectional cannons and cannons that aim in one direction, with the latter moving vertically or horizontally.
{{br}}

====''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars''====
Multidirectional Cannons reappear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars]]'', where they act the same as in their previous appearance. [[Cannon Kong]]s, Kong toys with cannons for bodies, also appear.

==''Yoshi'' franchise==
===''Yoshi Topsy-Turvy''===
Cannons appear in ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''. [[Yoshi]] can go inside one by [[jump]]ing at its top, and by tilting the [[Game Boy Advance]] to the left or the right, the cannon tilts to the respective direction.

==''Wario'' franchise==
===''Wario: Master of Disguise''===
In ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]'' a golden cannon known as [[Blamses]] was a popular attraction. There are also two other cannons: the [[Sphinx Jr.]] and [[K9 Cannon]].

==Crossover appearances==
===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''===
[[File:M&SOWG Cannon.png|thumb|A cannon in ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''.]]
During [[Adventure Tours]] mode in the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', cannons are used to launch Mario or [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] to a different island after they saved a [[snow spirits|snow spirit]].

===''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle''===
Cannons make their return in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'', albeit redesigned to resemble [[Rabbid]]s. They launch Mario and company to another part of the map, where they may find a path forward or a secret. Blue cannons launch Mario and company to a bonus area where all [[Blue Coin]]s must be collected to gain a new weapon. Large cannons send Mario and company to their respective worlds.
