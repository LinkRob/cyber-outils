'''Valor Up''' is a Special strike from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It will double the defense of the enemy used it.
==Trivia==
* A small number of enemies can do this.
* [[Jinx]] is the only enemy in the game, that can use both this move and [[Vigor Up!]].
