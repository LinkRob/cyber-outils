{{Nihongo|'''Cheese Land'''|チーズランド|Chīzu Rando|Cheese Land}} is the third course in the [[Flower Cup]] of ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', and as its name implies, the track is made of yellow cheese, complete with cheese mountains in the background.

== Layout ==
Its hazards are [[Little Mouser]]s which spin racers out of control if touched. In the far distance an odd, greenish planet can be seen. It is probably unknown if this area is supposed to be a moon, due to old tales about the moon being made out of cheese. Lots of turning is needed to finish this course.

== In ''Mario Kart 8''/''Deluxe'' ==
The track returns in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', with the same layout. However, [[Chain Chomp]]s replace the [[Little Mouser]]s. The track now also has more of a valley theme than before. The [[Glide Ramp]] replaces the second ramp. While the other off-road paths have two [[Glide Ramp]]s resembling pizza. The bottomless pit replaces the off-road path.
