The '''Wondrous Dice Block''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Description==
The Wondrous Dice Block is [[Rosalina]]'s unique [[Dice Block]], and it has a light blue border corresponding to the color of her outfit. In [[Toad Scramble]], if Rosalina is in the party, she can use her Wondrous Dice Block to roll either a 5, a 6, or a variable number corresponding to the character's current ranking (e.g. characters in first place would have a 1 as the number) to add to their total roll.

The Wondrous Dice Block and [[Princess Daisy]]'s [[Friendly Dice Block]] are the only Dice Blocks to have slots for a variable number dependent on the character's current status.
