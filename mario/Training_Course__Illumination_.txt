The '''Training Course'''<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yP-Qduvw9zY|title=The Super Mario Bros. Movie | Princess Peach Training Course Clip|author=Illumination|date=2023-04-04|accessdate=2023-05-23}}</ref> is an obstacle course in the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] featured in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== History ==
Since [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]] was a child, the [[Toad's species (Illumination)|Toads]] raised her and Peach trained on the obstacle course.

Peach then tests [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] with it to see if he is worthy of coming along with her to the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]]. Even though Mario isn’t doing well at the beginning, he eventually almost completes it only for a Piranha plant to ambush him, so Peach takes him along anyway.

==Physical appearance ==

The Training Course consists of [[Donut Lift]]s, [[Brick Block]]s, [[Lift]]s, [[Bill Blaster]]s, [[Fire Bar]]s, and other obstacles.
