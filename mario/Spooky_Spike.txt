'''Spooky Spike''' is a single-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Opening==
[[Boo]] spikes a ball at the player's character, who deflects it from the ground.

==Gameplay==
The minigame is essentially a game of volleyball against a Boo. The player's character has to [[dive]] to hit the [[beach ball]]s spiked by Boo for [[point]]s. Boo spikes a ball either in front, behind, to the left, or to the right of the player's character. The player can look at the shadow on the ground to determine where the ball will land. Once the player has obtained a certain number of points, they win.

In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player's character must spike ten balls in 30 seconds to win. In Mini-Game Attack, they have to spike fifteen balls instead. In Play Land, the object is to earn as many points as possible, and the minigame only ends when a ball hits the ground.

==Controls==
