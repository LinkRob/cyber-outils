'''Paper Trail''' is one of the [[Wario|WarioWatch]] [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player has to tilt the system to rapidly to roll out all of the toilet paper. There is a timer at the top-left that starts at four seconds.

*'''Level 1:''' The roll of toilet paper is short.
*'''Level 2:''' The roll of toilet paper is longer.
*'''Level 3:''' The roll of toilet paper is even longer.
