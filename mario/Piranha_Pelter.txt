The '''Piranha Pelter''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Piranha Pelter.

== Description ==
In ''Kingdom Battle'', Piranha Pelter resembles a hand cannon with a design based on [[Piranha Plant]]. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack with a medium range. The Piranha Pelter deals around 30 to 40 damage. When dealing damage to foes, it can inflict [[Honey (status effect)|Honey]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Piranha Pelter as an option among the Blasters. After defeating [[Pirabbid Plant]], Mario can obtain this blaster.
