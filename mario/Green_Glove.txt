'''Green Gloves''', also referred to as a '''Green Glove Baseball Boy''',<ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'' Player's Guide. Page 94. "''You’ll have to rely on a Green Glove Baseball Boy to hit a platform Switch.''"</ref> are green [[Bandit]]s that hold baseball gloves. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==History==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
[[File:Green Glove SMW2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Baseball Boys.png|thumb|200px|Green Gloves' first appearance in [[The Baseball Boys]] in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'']]
In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and its port ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', Green Gloves are the catchers of the Baseball Boys, and use teamwork with their yellow-colored counterparts, [[Slugger]]s. If [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] throws an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] toward one, they will catch the egg, aim at Yoshi, and throw it back at a high speed. If Yoshi is hit, it can knock him off a platform and into a bottomless pit or spikes.

[[Ground Pound]]ing their heads or stepping on them thrice will defeat them. However, they are immune to a Yoshi's tongue, so eating them is impossible. They can also be used to reach items like [[Flower]]s that are out of reach by ricocheting an egg off a wall.

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
[[File:Green Glove YIDS.png|frame|left]]
In the [[Island Museum]] of ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', there are five Green Gloves and two [[Egg Plant]]s.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', a Green Glove and a Slugger appear as a [[spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]].
