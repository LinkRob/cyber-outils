'''World 6-1''' is the first level in [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]] in [[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]. 

==Layout== 
[[Mario]] begins near a number of [[POW Block]]s, which he may activate to get [[coin]]s and other items from nearby [[Brick Block]]s. Shortly thereafter is a [[Red Ring]] Mario may pass through to make eight [[Red Coin]]s appear nearby, half of which parachute down from up above along with [[Volcanic Debris]]. Just after this is the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. After this, there is a [[? Switch]] which, when hit, causes several platforms to appear ahead, allowing Mario to cross over the lava while collecting coins. However, volcanic debris may destroy blocks or damage Mario. The [[Goal Pole]] is just ahead.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' The first Star Coin is on top of a platform near the start of the stage. Mario may use a Buzzy Beetle shell to get up to it.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' Near the end of the section after the Checkpoint Flag is a protected Star Coin. Mario must wait for volcanic debris to free it before it can be collected.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' Right next to the stairs near the Goal Pole is a hidden block containing a [[Vine]]. Mario must climb it to get to a hidden area with the third and final Star Coin.
