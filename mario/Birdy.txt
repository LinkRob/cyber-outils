'''Birdies''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They serve as bodyguards for [[Valentina]]; though, they despise her due to her overworking them. They aided in Valentina's attempted takeover of [[Nimbus Land]] who attempted to use [[Dodo]] to impersonate [[Mallow|Prince Mallow]]. However, their plan was foiled by [[Mario]], Mallow, and the others. They look like birds, hence the name.

==Battle==
In battle, Birdies can attack physically with their spears. They can use a special attack called [[Grinder (move)|Grinder]] that deals high damage.

===Strategy===
Birdies are weak to ice, so Mallow's [[Snowy]] is an effective way to damage them.
