'''Hopslipper''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' and a [[Battle Card]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
Hopslipper stickers look like slippers with springs at the bottom of them. They are green in color and only take up one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]]. With a Hopslipper, [[Mario]] can jump on a single enemy many times, making them similar to the [[Power Jump]] [[badge]] from earlier ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' games.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''===
Hopslippers are very common, and they can be bought in most [[Sticker Shop]]s. However, the regular Hopslipper is not very effective on enemies with a higher [[Defense]] rating.

===''Paper Mario: Color Splash''===
Aside from now being a Battle Card, Hopslippers retain their role from ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''.
