'''Toadsworth the Younger''' (also known as simply '''Young Toadsworth''') is a character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' He is [[Toadsworth]] from the past, but when time traveling, he becomes a younger counterpart of Toadsworth. In the game, he and his older self teach both the [[Mario|adult]] and [[baby Mario]] [[Luigi|bro]][[Baby Luigi|theirs]] special moves.

==Appearance==
Toadsworth in his younger self has a beige mushroom cap with brown round spots. The differences from his future self is that his msutache is brown, the vest is red with yellow trim linings. He does not wear a purple dress shirt, nor is he equipped with glasses or a cane.

==History==
===''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''===
Toadsworth invited [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] at [[Princess Peach's Castle|Baby Peach's Castle]] to play with her due to her tantrum, until he sees all [[Toad (species)|Toads]] knocked out by [[Baby Bowser]] and easily captures [[Baby Peach]]. To his horror, Baby Bowser attacks Toadsworth with fire, fainting him just as Baby Mario begins to fight Baby Bowser. Shortly after the battle, Toadsworth wakes up and grabs Baby Peach, he soon finds out both [[Toad Town]] and the castle are invaded by the [[Shroob]]s in their [[Shroob UFO|saucers]] taking every Toad inside. Toadsworth and the babies follow Baby Bowser safety through to the [[Koopa Cruiser]]. As more Shroob saucers arrive to attack the cruiser, Toadsworth takes a crying Baby Peach to the front command room, abandoning the baby bros. As the Koopa Cruiser shoot three Shroob saucers and accidently fall down towards the ruins of [[Hollijolli Village]], Baby Bowser and [[Bowser Baddies crew|his crew]] saw the Shroobs defeated two people, he strictly orders to leave them. However, Toadsworth strongly disapproves this, replying to Baby Bowser that abandoning those villagers is reprehensible, causing Baby Peach to cry louder. At Hollijolli Village, he helps giving the baby bros. [[Green Shell]] to defeat the remained Shroobs. After the Shroobs are defeated, Toadsworth investigates the unconscious villagers and to their surprise, it is revealed those two are actually the adult [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] from the future.
