A '''Mystery Egg''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
At first, the Mystery Egg, serves no use, but if [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] is equipped with the [[B'tub Ring]] and uses a Mystery Egg ten times, it becomes a [[Lamb's Lure]]. A Mystery Egg should only be used by Princess Toadstool, otherwise it just buzzes. The Mystery Egg can be purchased from the [[Treasure Hunting Toad]] for 200 coins.
