'''Vanna T.''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Vanna T. is a female [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with wears a red suit with a blue tie. The mushroom part of her head has red, heart-shaped spots, making her one of the only Toads to not have round-shaped spots on the mushroom part of their head.

=== Etymology ===
Vanna T.'s name is a pun of the word ''vanity'' (although, she seems to have the opposite nature). Vanna T.'s name could also be a reference to {{w|Vanna White}}, the assistant of {{w|Pat Sajak}} on the game show ''{{w|Wheel of Fortune}}''. 

== History ==
Vanna T. is often seen whenever [[Mario]] participates in [[Chuck Quizmo]]'s quizzes. Whenever Mario answers a question correctly, she can be seen clapping for him before Chuck Quizmo gives him a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]]. Vanna T. eventually sends Mario a [[letter]] thanking him for participating in Chuck Quizmo's quizzes. Vanna T. is seen again at the end of the game where she is standing beside Chuck Quizmo near [[Merlon]]'s house in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] thanking Mario for participating in her boss's quizzes (which is the only notable time Mario is able to directly interact with her). Vanna T. also appears in the parade where she claps for Chuck Quizmo after he sets off a firework from his hat.
