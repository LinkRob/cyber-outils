'''Boom Box''' is a [[minigame]] featured under [[Yoshi]]'s section in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It revolves around finding the two tunes that sound the same. Like [[Tox Box Shuffle]], [[Mario]]'s head appears on the upper screen.
