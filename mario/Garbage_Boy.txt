'''Garbage Boy''' is a character who appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. He is always found in a trash can, which he likely lives in, and uses it as a disguise to his convenience.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
Garbage Boy is featured in [[Mona]]'s stage. During the intro, Garbage Boy only appears within the background of [[Mona Pizza]], popping in and out of his trash can while Mona performs with her band.

Later on, in the end story scene shown after the player has completed Mona's set of [[microgame]]s, Garbage Boy is seen again in the [[Hawt House]], after Mona dashes out, realizing she still has to deliver pizzas. Garbage Boy makes pokes his head out of the trash can and moves up to the microphone, but just before he is about to say something, Garbage Boy gets kicked aside by [[Vanessa]], disguised as Mona, who is booed and has objects thrown at her.
