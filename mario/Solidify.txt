|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Solidify''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is used by a certain number of enemies and bosses in the game.

==Effects==
When Solidify is used, three ice pillars appear on all of the party members damaging them in the process. This move deals moderate damage making it not too threatening of a move. Solidify is considered a weaker version of [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] move.
