'''Men'ono''' (メン斧, meaning "ax face") is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Men'ono uses its axe to try to chop [[Wario]] and injure him when he is in-sight. It releases a [[Bronze Coin]] upon being defeated. They appear to be related to [[Onomī]], another enemy that carries an axe.

===Behavior===
A Men'ono has pink cloak, brown shoes, and carries an axe with it. It also has cyan-colored eyes.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Men'ono are encountered in two [[Sapphire Passage]] levels, [[Crescent Moon Village]] and [[Hotel Horror]], as well as the [[Golden Passage]].
