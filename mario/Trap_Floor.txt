'''Trap Floor''' is a Bowser [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, Trap Floor is the last minigame played. It is played at the [[Pipe House (Mario Party Advance)|Pipe House]], during the quest [[Final Showdown]], in which [[Bowser]] challenges the player's character to the minigame for the final [[Gaddget]]. Upon winning, the player is awarded both the minigame and the [[Bowser Print]].

==Gameplay==
The object is to open the trapdoors to drop all the [[Koopa Kid]]s to the floor below and eventually into the [[pit]]s below the door. The traps that are activated depends on which button is pressed: {{button|gba|A}} opens the blue traps; {{button|gba|B}} opens the red traps; and {{button|gba|R}} opens every trap, although this can only be used once. If a Koopa Kid falls through a trapdoor and the one below is not opened, the Koopa Kid falls and bounces onto the ground beside it, dazed. Green Koopa Kids move faster than the red and blue Koopa Kids. If a Koopa Kid reaches the player's character, it [[Fire Breath|breathes fire]] at them, and the player loses at the minigame. If all thirty Koopa Kids are defeated, the player's character wins at the minigame.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|A}}: Blue traps
*{{button|gba|B}}: Red traps
*{{button|gba|R}}: All traps

==Flavor text==
*'''In-game description:''' ''Open the trapdoors to make the Koopa Kids fall all the way to the bottom!''
