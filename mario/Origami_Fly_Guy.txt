== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' ==
'''Fly Guy'''s make their ''Paper Mario'' series debut in ''Paper Mario: The Origami King''. They, like many of the Koopa Troop, are transformed into Folded Soldiers by King Olly. Their design in the series now matches their appearance in various modern ''Mario'' games. They surround the Sea Tower, and can be defeated without going into battle by ramming into them with the boat after it gets an engine upgrade, although defeating them this way will not register them for the Musée Champignon gallery.
