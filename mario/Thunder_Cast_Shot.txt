'''Thunder Cast Shot''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] of [[Wario]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Wario straps a mechanized mallet to his back that hits him, electrocuting it and himself. Afterward, if the receiver hits the tennis ball, they become electrocuted.
