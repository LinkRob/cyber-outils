'''Danger, Bob-omb! Danger!''' (known as '''Run, Bob-omb, Run!''' in multiplayer) is an Action [[minigame]] that appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

== Gameplay ==
The player sheers a [[Bob-omb Buddy]] detour and stay away from balls of fire and the flame breath sputter by a [[Bowser Statue]]. The player controls him by using the [[Stylus]]. Leave a mark on a ball of fire or the flame breath ideals him to glow and shatter immediately, closing the minigame.

In the multiplayer version, each player has three aliveness. Like in individual player mode, if one of an [[Bob-omb]]s touch a piece of hazards, they erupt and defunct. When all Bob-ombs lose all of their lives, the last Bob-omb standing is the winner of this mini game. The minigame also has  modifiable difficulty. The tremendous it is, fireballs increases and the more frequent Bowser puffs in that place will be.

== In-game instructions ==
*'''Single player:''' ''"Slide over the Touch Screen to guide one the Bob-omb out of the way of the incoming fireballs. Watch out for Bowser's flame breath too. How long can you survive?"''
*'''Multiplayer:''' ''"Slide over the Touch Screen to guide the Bob-omb out of the way of the incoming fireballs. Watch out for Bowser's fiery breath, too. Lose three Bob-ombs, and you're out. The last Bob-omb standing wins."''
