'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[Badge Challenge]] course in [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. This level has one [[Wonder Seed]] and is unlocked after completing [[Mountaineering!|Wiggler Race: Mountaineering!]]

== Layout ==
The course has many large, patterned blocks in red, yellow and green, which form many blockades that the player needs the badge to clear. Also on the course are many [[Semisolid Platform|Semisolid Platforms]] and [[? Block|? Blocks]] to help the player.

== [[10-Flower Coin]] locations ==
The first coin can be found on top of a green patterned block on the left wall of the course. The player must wall-climb jump on the thin section jutting out and collect it.

The second coin can be found above a small, square yellow block that is rotating near the middle part of the course. It can be obtained after jumping off a large green patterned block moving side to side.

The last coin is at the very top of the course, above and to the left of the [[Goal Pole]]. The player must Wall-Climb Jump up the red block wall from a group of blocks to the left of the Goal Pole to reach it.
