'''Cloud Climb''' is a single-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Cloud Climb higher area.png|thumb|left|200px|Luigi in a higher area of the course]]
The player's character has to bounce on a large series of clouds to get to the [[finish line]] at the top. There are some [[Amp]]s circling the area, and if the player's character touches it, they are stunned temporarily, causing them to fall in the process. There are also [[springboard]]s placed along the path, and the player's character can use these to bounce higher.

In the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] and Mini-Game Attack modes, the player's character must make it to the goal within the [[time limit]], which is 60 seconds in Shroom City mode but 30 seconds in Mini-Game Attack. In Play Land, the object is to get to the top in the quickest time, and the course's length is extended as well.

==Controls==

* {{button|gba|A}}: [[Jump]]
