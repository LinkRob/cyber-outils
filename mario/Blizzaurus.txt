'''Blizzaurus''' is a large, light-blue ice dragon. She is the sixth boss of ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. Blizzaurus is fought at [[Gleam Glacier]].

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
Blizzaurus is a giant light-blue ice dragon.
{{Spoiler|hide}}
Her second form is a small blue fairy.
{{Endspoiler}}
===Personality traits ===
While little is known about her personality, she can be seen happy as a fairy.

=== Powers and abilities ===
* '''Ice Manipulation''': Blizzaurus can summon icicles or a freezing energy beam.
{{spoiler|hide}}
* '''Transformation''': She can transform into a fairy.
* '''Flying''': In her fairy form, she can fly in the air.
{{endspoiler}}

== In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' ==
After [[Princess Peach]] enters the Gleam Glacier Palace, Princess Peach finds the kidnapped Toad, but Blizzaurus surprises her by freezing Peach and then ramming into both her and the Toad, and challenges her to battle.

===Battle===
====Phase 1====
At the start of her battle, Blizzaurus protects herself with a shield. When attacking, she will attempt to ram Princess Peach, summing three icicles or spewing a freezing energy beam from her mouth that will freeze Peach. To beat Blizzaurus, she is weak against the [[Rage|Rage Vibe]], which breaks her protective barrier and cause damage to her, it takes three hits to take out her barrier.

==== Phase 2 ====
{{Spoiler}}
After her first form is left with three lives, Blizzaurus transforms into a fairy, once again inside a shield. She will fly above Peach, making Peach's target hard to aim. In this form, Blizzaurus will attack by either firing small energy balls in a row of three, or by summons two giant icicles onto the ground. Though Peach can jump on top of the icicles and use her Rage vibe to break Blizzaurus' shield making her vulnerable.

Upon her defeat, Blizzaurus explodes into snow, giving away the kidnapped Toad and the way to [[Giddy Sky]].
{{Endspoiler}}

== Trivia ==
* Blizzaurus' name is a portmanteau of "blizzard" and "saurus."
* Blizzaurus is the first and only boss in ''Super Princess Peach'' who can change forms.
* Unlike all of the bosses, [[Hoo]], [[Army Bro]] and Blizzaurus are bosses that made their debut in ''Super Princess Peach''.
* Blizzaurus second form bears a strong resembles to a Toad, which hints that Blizzaurus might be a uneck race of Toads.
* If counting the first two hits on the barrier as part of her hit-points, then Blizzaurus has the third most hit-points out of any boss in ''Super Princess Peach'', with 15 in total.
