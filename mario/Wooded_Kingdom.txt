}}The {{Nihongo|'''Wooded Kingdom'''|森の国|Mori no Kuni|, lit. '''Forest Country'''}} is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. Located on the [[Mushroom Earth]], it is a vast realm in the form of a dome forest. Its most notable city is [[Steam Gardens]], which serves as the kingdom's main playable area in the game.

The kingdom is notable for being primarily inhabited by [[Steam Gardener]]s. The primary currency of the Wooded Kingdom is purple [[Regional Coin|Bolt Nuts]]; however, they also accept regular gold coins.

==Paintings==
There are two paintings in this kingdom. One is located near the Forest Charging Station, and it can be accessed easily by throwing Cappy to the Scarecrow that activates the Wooded Kingdom Timer Challenge 2 Power Moon, as well as the Scarecrow that activates the Wooded Kingdom Timer Challenge 3 Power Moon. By going to the top of the platforms and then jumping to the surface the player will find a cube-shaped structure in which the painting is. It can take the player to either the Southwestern Floating Island at the [[Sand Kingdom]] or the Floating Sky Island at the [[Luncheon Kingdom]], depending on whether the [[Wooded Kingdom]] or the [[Luncheon Kingdom]] was selected first. If the [[Wooded Kingdom]] was selected first, the painting will lead to the [[Luncheon Kingdom]] however if the [[Lake Kingdom]] was selected first, the painting will lead to the [[Sand Kingdom]], either way the painting can be accessed after defeating [[Spewart]].

The other painting is located inside the Iron Cage, which can only be accessed from a painting at either the [[Metro Kingdom]], [[Snow Kingdom]] or [[Seaside Kingdom]]. If the [[Lake Kingdom]] was chosen before the [[Wooded Kingdom]], the painting will be in the [[Metro Kingdom]] painting, if the [[Wooded Kingdom]] was chosen before the [[Lake Kingdom]], and the [[Snow Kingdom]] was chosen before the [[Seaside Kingdom]], the painting will be in the [[Seaside Kingdom]]. If the [[Wooded Kingdom]] was chosen before the [[Lake Kingdom]], and the [[Seaside Kingdom]] was chosen before the [[Snow Kingdom]], the painting will be in the [[Snow Kingdom]].

==Power Moons==
{{main|Steam Gardens}}

There are a total of 80 [[Power Moon]]s (74 regular Power Moons and 2 [[Multi Moon]]s) in the Wooded Kingdom. During the player's first visit to this kingdom, they can collect 47 Power Moons and the 2 Multi Moons, including one from the kingdom's Hint Art. The player can also collect a Power Moon by traveling through a painting in a future kingdom (the [[Metro Kingdom]], [[Seaside Kingdom]], or [[Snow Kingdom]]). After the player defeats Bowser in the [[Moon Kingdom]], 4 more Power Moons become available to collect. The [[Moon Rock]] will also be available to destroy, causing 22 additional Power Moons to be accessible.
