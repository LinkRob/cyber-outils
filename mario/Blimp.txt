'''Blimps''' are a type of transportation appearing both in real life and in the {{franchise|Mario}}. In the latter, blimps first appear in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', and have since gone on to appear mainly in the {{series|Mario Kart}}.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
A Blimp appears in the background of certain courses. It typically depicts a logo of a fictional sponsor, although in ''Mario Kart Tour'', there is one in Singapore Speedway bearing the game's logo.

==In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''==
There is a blimp named the [[Cheep-Cheep Blimp]], which takes Mario to [[Glitzville]].

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''==
There is a blimp named the [[Zeeppelin]], which takes the protagonists to [[Pi'illo Island]].
