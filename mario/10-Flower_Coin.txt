A '''10-Flower Coin''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Description==
A 10-Flower Coin is a variation of a [[10-Coin]] that has a value of ten [[Flower Coin]]s. They replace the [[Star Coin]]s from the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' sub-series, as ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''{{'}}s levels each similarly have only three 10-Flower Coins to collect. If the 10-Flower Coin is collected again during a replay of a level, it instead grants three Flower Coins. The [[sensor]] badge will alert the player in a 10-Flower Coin is nearby.
