==History==
Sunbi is one of the first [[Nimbi]]s met in [[The Overthere]] when [[Mario]] and the other heroes arrive. Sunbi was being attack by two [[Skellobit]]s. When Mario and the others help him, Sunbi gives them the [[Door Key]] to advance further in The Overthere.
==Trivia==
*Sunbi's name is a reference to the day of the week, Sunday.
