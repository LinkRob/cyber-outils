|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Viro Plasm''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can only be used by [[Ameboid]]s and [[Zeostar]]s.

==Effects==
When Viro Plasm is used, a small, blue bubble heads toward the target and bursts open. This releases a substance that [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]s whoever is hit by this unless they're wearing an item such as a Safety Pin.
