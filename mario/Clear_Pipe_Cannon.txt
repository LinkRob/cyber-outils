'''Clear Pipe Cannons''' are an object which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Description==
A Clear Pipe Cannon is a type of [[cannon]] attached to the end of certain [[Clear Pipe]]s, and entering one automatically launches the characters to a different section of a course.
