A '''Train Bandit'''<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 128.</ref> is a chalk [[Shy Guy|Shy-Guy]] etched onto certain walls in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its remake ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.

==History==
When [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has transformed into the [[Train (form)|Train]], Train Bandits come to life and chase after him. They are limited to the walls' borders and stop moving when Yoshi returns to his normal state. The only ways to defeat a Train Bandit are by using the [[Winged Cloud Maker]], [[Anywhere POW]], or by smashing a [[POW Block]] when they are on-screen, with the latter only being possible in the [[Game Boy Advance]] version, in [[Go! Go! Morphing!]].
