'''Diamond Broadcasting System''', abbreviated '''DBS''', is a location in the {{series|WarioWare}}. The location first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' and returns in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Description==
Diamond Broadcasting System is a television station located in [[Downtown Diamond City]]. The broadcasting station appears as a tall structure of red and cyan with an antennae and the initials displayed.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare: Twisted!''===
[[File:DBS WW Touched map sprite.png|frame|left]]
In [[Wario-Man]]'s story, the '''Diamond City Broadcasting''', as it is named in this game, has a breaking news story from [[Ken the Reporter]], who announced the outstanding sales of [[Wario]]'s latest game, which has apparently surpassed all sales records.

Due to its close proximity to [[Club Sugar]], Diamond City Broadcasting may be spotted during the openings for certain other stories at nearby locations, including Wario's, [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s, [[Dr. Crygor]]'s, and [[9-Volt]]'s, as well as [[WarioWatch]].

===''WarioWare: Touched!''===
Diamond Broadcasting System appears in the opening of [[Mona]]'s story, [[Cute Cuts]]. One of its broadcasts is ''[[Ear Candy]]'', a music show hosted by Ken, who goes by the alias "Ken the VJ" for this job. He reports the weekly top five music artists. [[Vanessa]], who is watching the broadcast, becomes infuriated when Mona surpasses her as top artist of the week.
