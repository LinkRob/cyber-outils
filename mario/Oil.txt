'''Oil''' (オイル ''Oiru'')'' ''is an enemy in [[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|''Donkey Kong'']] and [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong|''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'']].
[[File:Oil Artwork.png|thumb|179x179px|Oil's artwork]]
It is an aura of fire with the text Oil that simply floats up and down. It does not attack [[Mario]] but can still harm him if he touches it. It can only be defeated with a [[hammer]].{{Stub}}
