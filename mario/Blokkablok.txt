The '''Blokkablok''' is a enemy in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. They are rather uncommon during the earlier and later parts of the game but appear frequently in between.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
It is a snake with several [[Brick Block]]s and [[Question Block]]s as segments, with the Question Blocks containing power-ups such as [[Super Mushroom]]s.

===Behavior===
Blokkabloks aren't very aggressive but they do move around in a set pattern formation making right and left turns and deal damage if you touch them or their spiked blocky segments preventing any jump attacks; Blokkabloks are also immune to the effects of the [[Fire Flower]]. However, Blokkabloks are not indestructible, and can be defeated by jumping into and destroying all its ? Block and Brick Block segments. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] is under the effects of the [[Starman]] or the [[Invincibility Leaf]], they can defeat a Blokkablok by touching it. A Blokkablok releases several [[Coin]]s upon being defeated.

==Trivia==
*Blokkabloks are similar to [[Pile Driver Micro-Goomba]]s (from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''), [[Blockhopper]]s (from ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'') and [[Fake Block]]s (also from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'') as they're all different types of species in the form of blocks or brick blocks.
