|name=Eekhammer x3 Battle Card}}'''Eekhammer x3''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Eekhammer x3 is the fast version of [[Mario]]'s "Hammer x3" attack. It is three of the [[Eekhammer (Battle Card)|Eekhammer]]'s scattered on the edges of the card, accompanied by a red/white checkerboard background.
