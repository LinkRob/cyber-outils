A '''Double Item Box''' is a feature introduced in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. When the player's kart collides with it, both characters will receive an item. If you've already got an item, it'll only give you one. These item boxes can show up randomly in the place of a regular Item Box, or through a special passage (e.g. thin road in [[Luigi Circuit]], Warp pipe in [[Peach Beach]], etc.).

Double Item Boxes were removed for the next few ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' installments, as not only was the double character feature removed in those games, but one could also hold out an item to make them hold two at once. Because the Double Item Box was removed, all the special passages in [[classic course]]s were removed also.

The Double Item Box returned in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', for the first time since its debut in ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!''.
