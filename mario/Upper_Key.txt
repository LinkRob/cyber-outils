The '''Upper Key''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Upper Key is a cyan-colored key with the shape of a ghost on the end of it.

==History==
The Upper Key is one of two main keys that can be found in [[Blowhole Castle]] along with the [[Lower Key]]. As the name states, the Upper Key can be used on a door in the upper area with an image of it next to it. The Upper Key can be found in a purple treasure chest in the room directly below the dark room where the Lower Key is found.
