A '''Meowbomb''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Meowbombs are one of [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]]' many creations of [[Fort Francis]]. Meowbombs are summoned in battle by Francis and explode after a certain amount of time. Meowbombs can be used to damage Francis by using [[Thoreau]] to grab one of them and throw it into Francis. Meowbombs are also fired by [[SurpriseMeow]]s.
