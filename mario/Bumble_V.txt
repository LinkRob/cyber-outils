The '''Bumble V''' is a [[kart]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

== Description ==
It is in the shape of a bumblebee, with its stinger at the front, with four wings , tailights resembling honeycombs and its respective yellow and black stripes.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 7]]''===
[[File:Yoshi MK7.png|thumb|150px|[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in the Bumble V, about to launch a [[Red Shell]].]]
The Bumble V is one of the kart parts. It is the signature kart for both [[Queen Bee|Honey Queen]], although [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s promotional artwork of the game also feature him with the Bumble V kart.

It gives a boost in [[acceleration]] and [[Off-Road|off-road]], but lowers [[weight]] and [[handling]]. In terms of hidden stats, it gives a boost in air speed and handling, [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] and [[drift]].

The Bumble V kart body is favored by the CPU-controlled [[Wario]] and Honey Queen.

The Normal Staff Ghost for [[Maka Wuhu]], Ret☆Reed, uses Yoshi in the Bumble V, equipped with Sponge tires and the Flower Glider, with the allotted time of 01:50.607, and the Expert Staff Ghost for [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 7)|Rainbow Road]], Nin☆Asakur, uses Honey Queen in the Bumble V, equipped with [[Standard (tires)|Standard tires]] and the Flower Glider, with the allotted time of 01:52.077.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
The Bumble V returns in ''Mario Kart Tour'', where it is classified as a High-End kart. It is equipped with the [[Pipe Frame]]'s tires in the game. The game also introduces two new variants, one being the [[Queen Bee (kart)|Queen Bee]], which uses the opposite colors with more black stripes than yellow, and the [[Rose Queen]], with has pink gold stripes.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' ==
[[File:MLPJ_Yoshi_LV2-5_Card.png|thumb|right|Yoshi in the Bumble V as a Battle Card.]]
The Bumble V appears as a [[Battle Cards|Battle Card]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. It is a Level 2 [[Character Card]], which can be used to deal eight points of damage to on screen enemies.
