==Features==
The level features [[Pendulum Platform|Pendulum Platforms]]. The area is very dark and there is almost no light in the castle, except for few several lights that are either static or swinging. A [[Red Ring]] is found, and is followed by a segment of Pendulum Platforms above a pit.  [[Lemmy Koopa]] boss fight is exactly the same as in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Over the first wobbling platform with a [[Dry Bones]] is a smaller wobbling platform with a Star Coin over it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': When having the [[Flying Squirrel Mario|Flying Squirrel suit]] or the [[Propeller Mushroom|Propeller Suit]], go under the wobbling platform to get the coin and swoop up to avoid death.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Use the [[P Switch]] to cross over the [[Muncher|Munchers]], but at the third [[Brick Block|Brick Block platform]], ground pound the bricks over the gap of the line of Munchers and go through a secret passageway leading a pipe that leads to a hidden area with the Star Coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
On the right wall near the entrance leading to [[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]] is a picture of [[Super Mario (form)|Super Luigi]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''
