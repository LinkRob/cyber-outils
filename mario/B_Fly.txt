'''B Flies''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' and ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
B Flies are large flies with a hairy belly and four wings attached to their back. Although their sprites are nearly identical in ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'' and ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'', artwork from each game reveals a distinct difference which is not conveyed by their in-game sprites: Bee Flies from ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'' have a proboscis, but their counterparts from ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'' have a tongue sticking out of a regular mouth instead.

===Behavior===
B Flies are rare enemies which are generally idle at first but flee upon the sight of [[Mario]] or [[Wario]].

==History==
===''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''===
Bee Flies first appear in ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'', where they can be found in the [[Tree Zone]] level [[The Exit]] and the [[Macro Zone]] levels [[In the Syrup Sea]] and [[Fiery Mario–Special Agent]]. They typically remain in one place when undisturbed, but they quickly fly off in a diagonal manner when approached by [[Mario]]. Their runaway flight is just as fast as Mario's top running speed.

===''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''===
Bee Flies reappear in ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'', behaving similarly to their previous incarnation. They are found in the first three stages of the [[SS Tea Cup]]. They are also given an animation when idle and fly away in a curved angle. They do not move if [[Wario]] crouches, which makes it possible for Wario to jump on them. If Wario manages to jump on one, a [[10-Coin|10 Gold Coin]] appears. They are said to be companions of their ''Super Mario Land 2'' counterparts.<ref name=DaijitenWario/>
