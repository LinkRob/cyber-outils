'''Boo's Game Board''' is a [[board]] hosted by [[Boo]] in the [[Mario Party 4 (Flash game)|''Mario Party 4'' Flash game]]. Similar to [[Boo's Haunted Bash]], the board features three invisible bridges that will periodically disappear and reappear, restricting movement on the board. Unlike the bridges on Boo's Haunted Bash, these ones count as a full space for movement. At the bottom left junction of the board there is a [[Tiny pipe]], which costs one [[Mini Mushroom]] to cross.
