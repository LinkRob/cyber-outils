'''Perilous Palace Path''' is a board featured in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''. It is a long board where the goal is to be the first to reach the castle at the end. Perilous Palace Path takes place in a grassy area, and hills from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' can be seen in the background.
