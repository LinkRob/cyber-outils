The '''Curlup''' is an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

== Description ==
Curlups are enemies resembling armadillos. They have yellow bodies, black eyes with golden pupils, and brown shells.

== Behaviour ==
In the field, Curlups can be found sleeping. If not, they roll at [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] if approached.

=== Combat ===
Curlups start the battle sleeping if they were encountered in that state. If attacked while sleeping, they awaken and gain an [[POW-Up|attack power boost]]. Curlups cannot act while sleeping.

Curlups can attack by slamming the ground, creating shock waves with each slam. The shock waves must be vaulted. They can also roll directly at the Bros., and must be repelled with the hammer. If their rolling charge is repelled, Curlups will perform the attack on the opposite Bro. Getting hit will result in the hit Bro. being knocked off-screen for a few turns (That Bro. will be treated as dead for the purposes of [[Special Attack|Special Attacks]] but cannot be affected by items.); if the other Bro. got knocked off-screen, that Bro. instead rejoins the combat with the hit Bro. staying in battle.

Curlups are of Level 22 with 353 hit points, and their attack, defense, and speed are 87, 75, and 92, respectively. Defeating them gives 301 Experience and 78 coins along with the following items:

* 1-Up Mushroom (6% chance)
* En Garde Boots (2% chance)
* Heatfreeze Ore (27% chance)

== Variants ==
A stronger version of Curlup called the Curlup R can be found on [[Brrrning Sea#Islets|Petit Islet]], where the [[Monster Maniac]] summons one as an optional boss, claiming it ate a lot of [[Bean#Types of Beans|Heart Beans]]. Curlup R's can be destroyed easily by fire, despite their overpowered health. Glohm-infused Curlups appear later when [[Reclusa]] goes wild, and can increase their stats.

The Curlup R is of Level 40 with 35000 hit points, an attack power of 210, a defense of 168, and a speed of 154. It gives 1271 Experience and 1000 Coins when destroyed.

The Glohm-infused Curlup is of Level 40 with 1285 hit points, an attack power of 163, a defense of 210, and a speed of 231. When destroyed, it gives 1589 experience, and its loot drop consists of 237 coins in addition to the following items:

* 1-Up Mushroom (6%)
* En Garde Boots (1%)
* Heatfreeze Ore (27%)
* Glohm Dust (12%)
* Glohm Fragment (14%)
* Glohm Crystal (16%)

== Behind the Scenes ==
The Curlup is named after the phrase "curl up," referring to their ability to roll up into a ball-like form.

== Names in Other Languages ==
In Japanese, Curlups are known as ''Nerumajiro'' (written ネルマジロ); this is derived from from ''neru'' ("sleep," referring to their tendency to be found sleeping; written 寝る) and ''arumajiro'' (from a somewhat incorrect transliteration of "armadillo" (written アルマジロ (The proper transliteration would be ''āmadjiryo'', written アーマディリョ.)) and a synonym of ''yoroi-nezumi'' ("armadillo," literally "armored mouse/rat"; written 鎧鼠)). Chinese translates their name to ''Shuìmó Qiúyú'' (literally "Sandman Armadillo," in reference to them being armadillo-like creatures that may sleep; written 睡魔犰狳), and Korean uses ''seullipadillo'' (written 슬리파딜로), a portmanteau of "sleep" and a botched transliteration of "armadillo." In Spanish, the enemy is called ''Repantín'', from ''repantigar'' ("to slouch"). It uses ''tatunino'' as its name in Portuguese, from ''tatu'' ("armadillo") and ''tanino'' ("tannin") due to its colour. In Italian, the enemy's name is ''Dormidillo'', from ''dormire'' ("to sleep"). Curlups are called ''Tadoudou'' in French, from ''tatou'' ("armadillo," reflecting their design) and ''doudou'' ("plush doll," reflecting their sleepy nature). They go by ''Gürtelmuff'' in German, literally meaning "belt muff" or "belt stench."
