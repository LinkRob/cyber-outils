'''Koopa Legends''' is a book that [[Koopa Koot]] asks for in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Koopa Legends is one of many items that Koopa Koot wants [[Mario]] to retrieve for him as one of his favors. This item is obtained in [[Koopa Village]] which is found in [[Kolorado]]'s house, which is only two houses left of Koopa Koot's. Entering the house and engaging conversation with his [[Kolorado's Wife|wife]], will give Mario the book. When she gives it to Mario, Kolorado's wife tells him that "it has weird things in it like firmer shells in 30 days".
