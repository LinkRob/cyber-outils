'''Mushroom Boy''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
Mushroom Boy is a male [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with red stripes on his mushroom cap.

==History==
In the game, Mushroom Boy used to live in [[Rose Town]] but decided to move to [[Seaside Town]] to open his very own [[Item Shop]]. Unlike most other Item Shops, Mushroom Boy's Shop doesn't sell the usual items and weapons. Instead, [[Mario]] can give Mushroom Boy rare [[Mushroom]]s to trade for a useful item, even a [[Flower Tab]]. However, if Mario offers to trade an ordinary Mushroom, the Mushroom Boy does not give him anything.
