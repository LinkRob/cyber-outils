A '''Gnawsher''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Gnawsher is a spherical enemy with wings and golden teeth. They also appear as a red variant.

===Behavior===
A Gnawsher moves through the air and chomps through blocks, items, or small enemies. How the Gnawshers behave depends on their colour. When the player approaches the red variant, the Gnawsher starts to follow the player in attempt to attack. Like other enemies, Gnawshers can be defeated by being attacked by a power-up or jumped on.

A swarm of them appears in Gnawsher Lair during a [[Wonder Effect]]. The Gnawshers can be stunned in this case by hitting a red-colored [[POW Block]].

== Behind the scenes ==
=== Etymology ===
Their name is derived from the word "Gnaw", which means to chew at something persistently, and "Gnash."
