The '''Spin Dash''' is a move in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. This move allows [[Mario]] to gain distance by spinning. When used, Mario can spin past enemies or spin into battle. When the [[Speedy Spin]] [[badge]] is used, the distance that the Spin Dash covers will increase. When the [[Dizzy Attack]] badge is equipped, Mario is capable of using the Spin Dash to enter battle and make enemies dizzy upon entering battle.
