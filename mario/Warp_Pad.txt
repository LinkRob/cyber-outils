'''Warp Pads''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Description==
Warp Pads are small, metallic, circular switches on the ground that are always featured sets of two. A Warp Pad has a dark red base, and it has a light-green, tube-like structure protruding from it. A Warp Pad model shows that the tube-like component is silver on the inside and has a small gold circle in the middle. Contained within the tube structure is a small, white orb that radiates light, and a curved beam of light extends from it over to that of its paired Warp Pad.

When a Warp Pad is activated, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] temporarily transforms into a ball of light that travels along the curved light beam until he reaches the second Warp Pad, at which point Mario or Luigi transforms back into his usual self. Warp Pads allow either for quick travel between areas, usually those at different elevations, and using Warp Pads are often required to reach otherwise inaccessible areas.
