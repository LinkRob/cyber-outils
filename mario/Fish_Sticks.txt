'''Fish Sticks''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. Its name comes from the name of the real-life food {{wp|fish finger|fishsticks}} (also known as "fish fingers"), which are battered slices of fish.

==Opening==
A [[Fishin' Lakitu]] flies down and catches a [[Cheep Cheep]] with its fishing rod. The four players then walk up from separate ports where their boats are docked.

==Gameplay==
Players attempt to catch Cheep Cheeps on the ends of the various scattered fishing rods surrounding a pool of water in the middle of the platform to earn [[point]]s. There are twelve rods total. When a fishing rod sinks down, this signifies that a Cheep Cheep has bitten the line. Red Cheep Cheeps are worth one point, and [[Gold Cheep Cheep|larger golden ones]] are worth three points. The player(s) with the most points at the end of the minigame win(s).

==Ending==
The camera zooms in on the location of the winner, who then performs their victory animation as the losers do their losing animations. If there is more than one winner, the camera does not zoom in.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Move
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Check line
