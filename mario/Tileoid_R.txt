}}A '''Tileoid R''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Tileoid Rs are [[Tileoid]]s that are found mainly in the [[Whoa Zone]] (which is different from most other Tileoids as they are commonly found in the [[Dotwood Tree]]). Tileoid Rs move around on the floors, walls, and ceilings damaging anyone that gets in their way. Tileoid Rs have the second highest HP of the Tileoids second only to the [[Dark Tileoid]].
