'''Space Fantasy''' is a song in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It plays at a speed of 72 beats per minute at 4/4 time and lasts 26 bars, covering 1 minute and 26 2/3 seconds. It is in the key of A Major. The song has a relaxing, cosmic feel that typically appeals to its locations:

*[[Good Egg Galaxy]] (Flipswitch Area, Pill and Star Planets)
*[[Space Junk Galaxy]] (Yoshi Planet)
*[[Battlerock Galaxy]] (Minefield Saucer Area and Blast Missile Planet. A remixed, more battle-oriented theme with the same tempo and duration plays elsewhere in the level, typically near the [[Battlerock]] itself.)
*[[Sling Pod Galaxy]] (Entire Level)
*[[Bubble Breeze Galaxy]] (Entire Level)
*[[Ghostly Galaxy]] (Matter Splatter Mansion Planet)
*[[Gusty Garden Galaxy]] (Disk Planets)
*[[Boo's Boneyard Galaxy]] (Entire Level)
*[[Deep Dark Galaxy]] (Pukupuku Pampol and Marble Planets)
*[[Dreadnought Galaxy]] (Beam (including Starting Planet) Planets, Cube, Aqua (before flood), and Sphere Planets. A remixed, more battle-oriented theme with the same tempo and duration plays elsewhere in the level, including the Aqua Planet after it floods.)
*[[Matter Splatter Galaxy]] (Entire Level)
