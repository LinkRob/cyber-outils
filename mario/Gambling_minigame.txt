A '''gambling minigame''' is a type of minigame that only appeared in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Description ==
In gambling minigames, the player always uses coins while abiding by the rules of the minigame in hopes of earning more coins. The player will lose the minigame if they do not gain coins while their coin count is zero.
