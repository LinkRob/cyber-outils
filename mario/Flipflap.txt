==Appearances==
===''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''===
Flipflaps are rather uncommon enemies in this game. They appear in only two levels: [[Fungi Forest]] (at night) and [[Creepy Castle]]. Flipflaps are often confused with regular bats that appeared in earlier levels which may catch players off-guard. Flipflaps simply attack by swooping down at a Kong biting them. In Fungi Forest at night, they replace the [[Zinger]]s.
