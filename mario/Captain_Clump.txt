'''Captain Clump''' is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Pirates of Koopa]]." He is a stereotypical pirate, having an eyepatch, a hook in place of a hand, a large peg leg, and a parrot, whose name is [[Patches]].
