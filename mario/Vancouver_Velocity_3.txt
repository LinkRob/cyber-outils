'''Vancouver Velocity 3''' is a race course that first appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is one of the three layouts of [[Vancouver Velocity]] and made its debut in the Autumn Tour for 2022.

== Layout ==
Racers start Vancouver Velocity 3 next to the Vancouver Convention Centre, facing the same direction that they do in the [[Vancouver Velocity 1|first route]], facing the Vancouver Olympics Cauldron; however, they go straight and therefore do not go towards the cauldron at all. Instead, they go into the Massey Tunnel, going into a wide left turn, before passing the Vancouver Public Library and driving down the library's Central Branch, which is lined with stores. After a wide right, the racers turn left into the Rogers Arena. There is a stand in the middle of the arena. The second half is essentially [[Vancouver Velocity 2]] done backwards; the racers leave the stadium, passing by the statue of Roger Neilson on their way out. The course directs them to the left and down more streets of the city before a long straightaway to the [[Finish line|starting line]].

''[[Mario Kart 8]]''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s [[Nintendo Switch|Switch]] version has this route for the final lap of [[Vancouver Velocity]]. Additionally, racers whose gliders break during the [[Cannon (Mario Kart series)|cannon]] section from [[Vancouver Velocity 2]] on the second lap can continue onto Vancouver Velocity 3 instead of being counted out of bounds.
