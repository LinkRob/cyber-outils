'''Dixie Kong''' is a female [[Kong]]. She appears in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' as part of the Jungle kingdom.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Dixie Kong resembles her [[Dixie Kong|video game counterpart]]. Unlike her appearance in video games, she wears a pink singlet with a white star.

== Biography ==
Dixie Kong lives in the [[Jungle Kingdom]]. She was present in the audience when she watched a duel between [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and standing next to Diddy.
