The '''unbeaten spokesman''' is a character who appears alongside [[Bowser]], [[Lakitu]], [[Duck Hunt|the dog from Duck Hunt]], and Smick in a series of Australian commercials for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]], cross promoting ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', ''Gyromite'', and ''Duck Hunt''. He resembles {{wp|Max Headroom}}.

In his [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qq3FIgEsAEo appearance], he and the other characters appear as CGI characters. They mock the viewer in distorted voices, challenging them to complete the games featured with the repeated taunt of "You cannot beat us". It's mentioned that in ''Super Mario Bros'', a player can "score one million" and "discover new worlds".
[[File:We do not like losing.png|thumb|left|200px|The spokesman rebuffs a child after he defeats [[Bowser]] by blasting his chair into space.]]
In a [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ohtTXbY3w0 later live-action commercial], a child can be seen playing ''Super Mario Bros.'' while imitating the voices from the computer-animated commercial. When he defeats [[Bowser]] and proudly shouts "beatcha!", the spokesman sends a gust of wind into his room that leaves him spinning, before blasting him into space.
