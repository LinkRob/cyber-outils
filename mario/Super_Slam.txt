'''Super Slam''' is a special move in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==Description==
To have a [[Kong]] charge the move, the player must hold {{button|n64|B}}. In doing so, small orange orbs of energy start to surround the Kong. Once the orbs begin to spiral around the Kong, the player can release the button to have the Kong unleash a shockwave, the color of which varies depending on which Kong is using the move. The Super Slam uses one [[Crystal Coconut]] per use.

The resulting shockwave can unearth a [[5-Banana Coin]] from a [[DK Dirt Pile]] within its range and instantly defeat most enemies that touch it, including [[Klump]]s, [[Kosha]]s, [[Klobber]]s, and [[Kasplat]]s. However, the Kong gets damaged if they perform a Super Slam on certain enemies, such as [[Kaboom]]s, [[Purple Klaptrap]]s, and the [[Toy Monster]]. Purple Klaptraps are entirely unaffected by the Super Slam.

==In ''Donkey Kong 64''==
The Super Slam is not available from the start, but rather one provided by the [[Banana Fairy Princess]]. She provides the move to [[Tiny Kong]], although any Kong can use the move afterward, after Tiny agrees to help find the missing [[Banana Fairy|Banana Fairies]].
