'''''Mario Sports''''' is a loosely defined series used to refer to ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games in which the characters can player in a variety of sports. It is not to be confused with the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' series, which is a crossover series featuring both ''Mario'' and ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' characters competing in a variety of Olympic sporting events.

The first installment is ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]'' a [[Nintendo DS]] game developed by [[Square Enix]] and released in 2006, the second installment is ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'', a [[Wii]] game developed by Square Enix and released in 2010. The third and most recent is ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]'', a [[Nintendo 3DS]] game developed by [[Camelot]] and released in 2017.
