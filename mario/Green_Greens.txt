'''Green Greens''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', then reappeared in the subsequent title, ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. It later reappeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is from the ''Kirby'' universe.

==Stage design==
The stage has three platforms. Two of the platforms have nothing to offer except to fight on. Blocks tend to fall in the gap between the platforms. Whispy Woods is featured on the platform in the center.

===Features===
The stage has [[Whispy Woods]], a boss from the ''Kirby'' series, who occasionally blows on the characters. In between the platforms there are bombs and boxes, breaking the boxes and the bomb boxes will make them explode and cause damage to the player(s) in a certain vicinity.

==Origin==
[[w:c:kirby:Green Greens|Green Greens]] is the name of a stage in ''[[w:c:kirby:Kirby's Dream Land|Kirby's Dream Land]]''.
