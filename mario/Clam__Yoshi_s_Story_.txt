'''Clams''' are a type of enemy featured in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. Here, they only appear in the level [[Lots O'Fish]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Clam YS sprite.png|frame|left]]
They have a colorful yellow and light purple shell, a pair of googly eyes with light blue circles, and an entirely red mouth.
{{br|left}}
===Behavior===
Clams act like underwater [[Warp Pipe]]s in that they blow a current of underwater air upwards, something that they stop doing after a given time. Clams cannot be defeated
