'''Swanky's Sideshow''' is really a competitive game between [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]]/[[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] and [[Cranky Kong|Cranky]] run by [[Swanky Kong]] in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version, it is replaced by [[Swanky's Dash]].

This game costs two [[Bear Coin]]s to play. If Dixie or Kiddy win, they earn a variety of prizes, such as [[banana]]s, [[Banana Bunch]]es, [[Bear Coin]]s, and [[Life Balloon]]s.

==Gameplay==
In this game, [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]]/[[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] must challenge [[Cranky Kong|Cranky]] to throw balls at the targets (with a [[Kremling]] making a cameo on it) to see who is able to break the most targets. Apparently, like usual, Cranky rambles a lot if he loses.

There are three versions of the game to play; the first is "Head to Head" on which Cranky and the monkeys must compete in a match and miss as few targets as possible. Hitting a target gives points to the meter. Whoever gains the filled meter first wins the minigame.

The second version is "Race to 25". This minigame has Dixie/Kiddy race Cranky upon seeing who can hit twenty five targets first.

The third version is "Endurance". In this version, Dixie/Kiddy must race Cranky to see who is able to hit the most targets before missing one. Whoever misses the first target loses.

==Easter Eggs==
[[File:Swanky's Sideshow - Competing Background - Donkey Kong Country 3.png|thumb|left|The full view of the background in the competing area.]]

While not many easter eggs are found in this area, they are mainly plush toys in the background scenery of the competing match. Their purpose is not known, but could've possibly been used for prizes for winners.

On Dixie/Kiddy's side, two full [[banana]]s and a banana peel can be seen in the background. As with the dolls, there is a regular teddy bear along with a [[Squitter the Spider|Squitter]] and [[Enguarde the Swordfish|Enguarde]] plush toys in the background.

On Cranky's side, four full bananas are seen lying around in the background. Also, another normal teddy bear can be seen aside a [[wikipedia:doll|doll]]. It is unknown what, if anything, the doll is a reference to. There is also a [[Squawks the Parrot|Squawks]] plush on the left side.
