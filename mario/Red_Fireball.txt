The '''Red Fireball''' is an item in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
As shown from its icon, the Red Fireball has the appearance of a regular fireball but with two eyes. The center of its body is light orange whereas the outer part is a darker orange. Two drips appear to be dropping from its tail.

==In ''Mario Super Baseball''==
The Red Fireball only appears in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge]] mode. It allows Mario to use his [[Star Skill]], [[Fireball]], in the mode. The Red Fireball can be bought from the item shop for 200 [[coin]]s if either [[Mario]] is the team captain or if he has already been recruited to the player's team.
