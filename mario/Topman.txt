'''Topmen''' are enemies which first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They first appear in the [[Battlerock Galaxy]].

==Behavior==
To attack, Topmen use their spin to knock their opponent into dangerous objects around them. [[Mario]] can beat these enemies at their own game by using his [[Star Spin]] to send them into deadly hazards within their vicinity, damaging the creatures with their body sparking and shaking erratically. Their short circuits can also potentially short out any [[Electric rail|Electric rails]] they get knocked into. When it takes enough electrical damage from being tossed into a power line by Mario, a Topman dies and goes into meltdown, leaving a [[Coin]] among its remains.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Topmen can be found at [[Battlerock Galaxy]], [[Buoy Base Galaxy]], and the [[Dreadnought Galaxy]]. They are prominent in the Battle Rock Galaxy's third mission, Topmaniac and the Topman Tribe, and in the Dreadnought Galaxy's third mission, Revenge of the Topman Tribe, in which their leader, [[Topmaniac]], appears as a boss. He can also summon them to his side in battle.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
Topmen can be found in the [[Space Storm Galaxy]]. They are used to clear the secret mission [[Space Storm Galaxy#C'Mere, Topman|C'mere, Topman]], in which they are the main feature.
