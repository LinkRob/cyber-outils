The '''Fire Sumo''' is a minor character from the ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|Super Mario World]]'' television series. He is a [[Sumo Bro]] that faces [[Luigi]] in the episode [[Mama Luigi]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
The Fire Sumo looks just like the rest of the [[Sumo Bro]]s. His skin is orange and his shell has a yellow plastron. His shoes are red and on his head is a white spike that acts as a horn. The area around his mouth is red.

=== Personality traits ===
Due to his lack of screentime, the Fire Sumo has an unknown personality, but his willingness to attack Luigi without question shows him to be loyal to [[Bowser]].

=== Powers and abilities ===
The Fire Sumo can create fissures with his stomps. Unlike most [[Sumo Bro]]s, he is not shown making the fire waves that are spawned by them in the games.

== Biography ==
The Fire Sumo makes his debut during Bowser's raid on Dome City in the episode [[Mama Luigi]], at the start of the story that [[Luigi]] tells [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. During his attempts to escape the raid (meanwhile, [[Mario (character)|Mario]] gets attacked by a [[Magikoopa]] known as a "Koopa Wizard" and a [[Banzai Bill]] called a Magnum Bill and [[Princess Peach]] gets dragged off by a [[Chargin' Chuck]] squad known as the Koopa Football Players), Luigi runs into the Fire Sumo, who stomps the ground, causing a fissure to open up. Luigi escapes him by jumping into the fissure, using a [[Magic Balloon]] that he finds mid-descent to get to a skull raft. The Fire Sumo does not appear again, implying Mario had dealt with him off-screen.

== Episode appearances ==
The Fire Sumo only appears in [[Mama Luigi]].

== Trivia ==
*The Fire Sumo is the first [[Sumo Bro]] to be given a name.
