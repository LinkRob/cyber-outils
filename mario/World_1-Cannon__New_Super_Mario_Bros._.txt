'''World 1-Cannon''' is the [[Warp Cannon]] level or [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. It can be unlocked by finding the [[Red Flagpole|secret exit]] in [[World 1-Tower (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-Tower]].

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the right and climb up the staircase to reach the Cannon, enter the Cannon (as you would a [[Pipe]] and the Cannon will launch [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] off into the distance sending him to [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]].
[[File:NSMB1-Cannon.png|thumb]]

If World 5 hasn’t been unlocked yet, [[Bowser Jr.]] will automatically flee here with [[Princess Peach]] and he will no longer be present at the [[Castle]]s of incomplete worlds completely omitting any cutscenes that would’ve played in Worlds prior to World 5. Sequence breaks can be avoided by completing the level after you unlock World 5 normally, the level's completion is required to get three Stars on a savefile and it does show up among the completed levels of the game.

Like all the other Warp Cannon levels, this level contains no [[Star Coin]]s.

==Trivia==
*Snowy Mountains can be seen in the background of the level along with the sky's hue being slightly more of a bluish gray, this is the background of World 5, the world the Warp Cannon leads to.
