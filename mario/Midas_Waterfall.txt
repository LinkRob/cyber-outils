'''Midas Waterfall''' is a location in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It also appears in the book ''[[Mario and the Incredible Rescue]]''.

==Description==
It is a waterfall where water flows down into the [[Midas River]].
