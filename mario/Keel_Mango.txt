A '''Keel Mango''' is a tropical fruit found in the trees of [[Keelhaul Key]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:KeelMango_PM2.png|frame|left]]
The Keel Mango is found in the trees of [[Keelhaul Key]], although is not available when the player first arrives at Keelhaul Key. In fact, it does not become available until after the player has completed the chapter, reached the [[Princess Peach]] intermission after Chapter 5, and returned to [[Rogueport]]. This is possibly to ensure that the player gives the [[Coconut]] to [[Flavio]] in exchange for the [[Chuckola Cola]], as giving him any other food item results in him asking for something more tropical. Since Keel Mangoes are also tropical, this may be why they were made unavailable at this point in the game. Once the [[Warp Pipe]] leading to Keelhaul Key has been activated, it is a very simple matter to get Keel Mangoes &mdash; a tree containing them is very near the Warp Pipe. Keel Mangos can be used to make several deserts if cooked by [[Zess T.]]. The mango itself cures 5 HP.

In the Japanese version, the player can get the Keel Mango from the tree anytime.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
They reappear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' that can be dropped by [[Putrid Piranha]]s or by the [[Downtown of Crag]].
