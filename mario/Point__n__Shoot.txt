'''Point 'n' Shoot''', named '''Seaside Snapshot''' in PAL releases, is a General [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame is divided into three rounds. Characters have to take snapshots of certain characters around a town. They must then take a picture of one of the three required ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' enemies, such as [[Jumbo Ray]] or [[Cheep Cheep]], to receive [[point]]s. Whoever ends with the most points is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|3ds|Tilt}}: Adjust the view
*{{button|3ds|A}}: Take a snapshot
