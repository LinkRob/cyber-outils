The '''Golf Outfit''' is a costume that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It gives [[Mario]] clothing based on his appearance in ''[[Family Computer Golf: U.S. Course]]'' and ''[[NES Open Tournament Golf]]''. The Golf Outfit is paired with the [[Golf Cap]].
