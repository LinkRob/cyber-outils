'''Bowser's Airship Sneak''' is a Special challenge in the [[Challenge Mode (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Challenge Mode]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

==Objective==
The player has to get through the first area of the course [[The Mighty Cannonship]] without touching any enemies, [[coin]]s and [[cannonball]]s.  If they do so, they fail the challenge.
