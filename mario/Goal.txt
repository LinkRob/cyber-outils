A '''Goal''' is the end point of a [[level]], usually an object that has to be activated. Goals first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', in the form of [[Goal Pole|Flagpoles]]. Since then, Goals in general have gone on to appear in several ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related games.
