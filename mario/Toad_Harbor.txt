}}'''Toad Harbor''' (known as '''Toad Harbour''' in British English) is a course in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is the second track in the [[Flower Cup]].

Toad Harbor takes place within a seaside city, with some elements taken from various real world cities such as a downward slope and trolley cars similar to San Francisco, as well as a statue of [[Princess Peach|Peach]] resembling the [[Statue of Liberty]].
