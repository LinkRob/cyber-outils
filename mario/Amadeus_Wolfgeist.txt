'''Amadeus Wolfgeist''' is a boss appearing in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. He makes his lair on the Great Stage, which is on the Last Resort Hotel's fourth floor. [[Luigi]] fights him for the sixth floor's elevator button during his journey through the hotel. He has 450HP.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Amadeus is an elegantly dressed ghost bearing an appearance to that of a normal pianist. He has silver, almost gray hair that is mostly straight except for the back of his hair. His hair bursts into flames when his anger is encited, an example being during the cutscene that plays during his final phase which has him possess his piano.

=== Personality traits ===
Though he likes playing his piano, this character appears rather irritated when Luigi makes his way into the Great Stage. However, Amadeus treats his battle as part of his performance, until Luigi pushes him over the edge by sucking up the [[Twirling Goob]]s.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Amadeus Wolfgeist seems to be able to possess things. While he was in his first phase, the chairs were ripped from their seats. Amadeus then directed the chairs into Luigi. During his final phase, Amadeus can possess his piano. This turns him into a [[Mad Piano]]. Unlike most Mad Pianos, Amadeus can shoot the keys of his piano like bullets and also throw bombs.

== Biography ==
Amadeus Wolfgeist was a great piano player before he died. His spirit haunted the Last Resort hotel, posing as his former self. When [[King Boo]] took over the hotel, Amadeus found his alignment swaying to evil. Eventually, Luigi cornered and fought him within the Great Stage, capturing him on [[Professor Elvin Gadd]]'s request and freeing [[Toad (character)|Toad]] whose spirit Amadeus had in his custody, trapped in a painting.

== Battle ==
=== Combat ===
When Luigi makes his entrance, Amadeus Wolfgeist is busy playing his piano. He later scares Luigi away by playing the piano rapidly when Luigi tries to reach [[Toad]]'s painting.

==== Phase 1 ====
During his first phase, Amadeus does not directly fight. Instead, he continues playing the piano. However, the seats will be torn from their foundation and attempt to fly towards Luigi's direction. Luigi can avoid the chairs by constantly running.

==== Phase 2 ====
[[File:Amadeus.png|thumb|Amadeus Wolfgeist getting mad.]]
During his second phase, Amadeus summons five [[Twirling Goob]]s. They have 100 hit points just like their normal [[Goob|counterparts]]. However, their masks protect their eyes from [[Strobulb]]'s flashing, so Luigi has to knock their masks off with his [[Poltergust G-00]]. After sucking them in, Amadeus gets mad and the battle gets into phase three.

==== Phase 3 ====
During his final phase, Amadeus possesses his piano, becoming a [[Mad Piano]]. His main attack during this phase involves him slamming down hard on Luigi to crush him. This deals decent damage, but Amadeus will reveal himself upon whiffing his slam attack. Once he is flashed, he will retreat to the grand stage. He will fire bombs from his mouth. These bombs will flash red before their detonation. Luigi must grab a bomb with his vacuum and fire a bomb at the boss's mouth to stun him, then slam him and render him vulnerable. Upon losing a quarter of his health to the Poltergust G-00's suction, Amadeus will repeat his attack pattern, but will add shooting piano keys as bullets to his arsenal. Amadeus Wolfgeist has 450 hit points in combat. Once he is defeated, the boss will take a bow before being sucked in.

== Behind the scenes ==
=== Creation and development ===
Hence the name, the character is based off [[wikipedia:Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart|Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart]], a famous composer in Austria.

=== Etymology===
'''Amadeus Wolfgeist''' is a reversal of ''Wolfgang Amadeus''. Based on a famous composer in the Clasical period, [[wikipedia:Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart|Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart]], he was known for composing symphonies, orchestras and concertos composing more than six hundred works.

== Trivia ==
*Amadeus Wolfgeist bears similarity to [[Melody Pianissima]], who was fought in the first ''[[Luigi's Mansion (series)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' [[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|game]]. They both play the piano and fight with music-based attacks. However, Melody Pianissima is an easier boss since she does not fight until Luigi answers a quiz, and Luigi just has to suck up her music sheets (which have only one hit point apiece) and then vacuum her up.
