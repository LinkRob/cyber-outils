'''Groovy''' is the fifth level of the [[Special World]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==Overview==
Groovy consists of mostly flat terrain with light slopes and many different forms of [[Koopa Troopa]]s, as well as [[Piranha Plant]]s, [[Pokey]]s and [[Volcano Lotus]]es. There are also a couple of [[Chargin' Chuck]]s with baseballs at the end. After reaching the [[Giant Gate]] at the end of the stage, the player will unlock [[Mondo]].
