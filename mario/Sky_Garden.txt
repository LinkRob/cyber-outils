'''Sky Garden''' is the second track of the [[Lightning Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. The track is in the sky and is cloud-themed. On the sides of the tracks are tan-colored clouds which slow down the [[kart]] and may save a one from falling off. Some [[Jump Pad]]s can be seen here, and one of them allows you to pick up an item box. Vines are shown growing from underneath. The vines do not affect the racer, but stop him as if he ran into a wall. These can be avoided easily as they are only at the edges of the course.

There are many shortcuts here by cutting through clouds from ramps, but are only possible by using one or two [[Mushroom]]s. The one with the most impact is to use a Mushroom when going on the first very small ramp which can save up to seven seconds of time.

== In ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' ==
This track returns in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' as the same in the Lightning Cup, but there is an added shortcut this time where the player can use a Mushroom to hop to the other side.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ==
Sky Garden is set to return in ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' as part of the Booster Course Pass, appearing in the Lucky Cat Cup.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Sky Garden's track released in the [[Sky Tour]] as of February 22, 2022. The Sky Tour trailer uses its music.

==Trivia==
*This course is the only course in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series to appear in the same cup twice, unless SNES Mario Circuit 3 counts: it was in ''Mario Kart: Super Circuit''{{'}}s [[Extra Lightning Cup]] and ''Mario Kart Wii''{{'}}s Lightning Cup. 
*''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' remixed some songs, Sky Garden's music was one of them for the credits. 
* In the ''Mario Kart Tour'' T version, you can jump off the moving platform near the end and pass between the 2 vines on the left. This allows you to go directly to the glider section.
