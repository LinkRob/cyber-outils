'''Magic Projectiles''' (also simply called '''Projectiles''' and sometimes called '''Magic Blasts''' or '''Fireballs''') are a type of energy beam conjured from the wands of the [[Koopalings]] or [[Magikoopa]]s often used as a weapon against [[Mario]] and his friends, they first appeared in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and since then have gone on to appear in several later games as well. Magic Projectiles are typically depicted as a colorful fireball-like beam with shapes fluttering around it, in other games however, they merely consist of shapes. Magic Projectiles are harmful to touch however they’re generally easy to avoid and can be extinguished with certain counter-projectiles. Magic Projectiles dissipate when touching a wall, ceiling, or the ground.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Bros. 3 / Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''====
[[Image:Magic Projectiles SNES.jpg|thumb|left|Mario dodging Magic Projectiles shot by [[Roy Koopa]] in the original.]]
[[Image:Magic Projectiles GBA.jpg|thumb|right|[[Iggy Koopa]] firing a Magic Projectile as seen in the remake.]]
Magic Projectiles first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and later go on to appear in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. In these games, they are fired by the [[Koopalings]]' wands in battle, [[Larry Koopa]], [[Morton Koopa Jr.]], [[Iggy Koopa]], [[Roy Koopa]], and [[Ludwig von Koopa]], the Projectiles resemble white orbs that fly toward [[Mario]], the Projectiles can be dealt with by avoiding them, the Koopalings become more advanced in firing them as the battle progresses, [[Wendy O. Koopa]] and [[Lemmy Koopa]] are the only two Koopalings that don't use Magic Projectiles, instead they use [[Candy Ring]]s and [[Circus Ball]]s instead. In the [[GBA]] [[Reissue|remake]], [[Magikoopa]]s can also fire Projectiles as well however they look slightly different to the Koopalings' Projectiles.

====''Super Mario World / Super Mario Advance 2: Super Mario World''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile SNES.jpg|thumb|right|A Magikoopa firing a Projectile at Mario.]]
Magic Projectiles reappear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and later go on to appear in ''[[Super Mario Advance 2: Super Mario World]]''. In these games, they are fired by [[Magikoopa]]s from their wands, unlike the previous game, Magic Projectiles this time are a formation with various shapes moving around them, Mario must avoid the Magic Projectiles to avoid taking damage, the Koopalings don't use Magic Projectiles in these games due to not possessing [[Magic Wand]]s.

====''Super Mario Galaxy''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile SMG Red.jpg|thumb|left|[[Kamella]] firing a Red Magic Projectile at [[Mario]].]]
[[Image:Magic Projectile SMG Green.jpg|thumb|right|And [[Kamella]] firing a Green Magic Projectile at [[Mario]].]]
Magic Projectiles return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. In this game, they are commonly fired by [[Magikoopa]]s, the Red Magic Projectiles they fire are damaging upon contact but when they hit the ground, they manifest into rolling fireballs which can be very tricky to avoid especially when there’s not much space. Keeping distance from the landing point of the Projectiles is a good strategy to avoiding them. [[Kamella]] also fires Magic Projectiles from her wand, she fires the common Red Magic Projectiles which transform into fireballs that can split into copies of two or three. Additionally, Kamella may also fire Green Magic Projectiles which turn into [[Green Shell]]s upon landing, these are more of a help than a hindrance as these Shells are necessary for defeating Kamella.

====''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile Wii.jpg|thumb|right|[[Iggy Koopa]] firing a Magic Projectile at [[Mario]] from his carriage.]]
Magic Projectiles return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. In this game, they are fired by the [[Koopalings]] from their [[Magic Wand]]s, and additionally, each Projectile has a different color depending on the Koopaling that fired them, [[Larry Koopa]] fires Cyan Magic Projectiles, [[Roy Koopa]] fires Purple Magic Projectiles, [[Iggy Koopa]] fires Green Magic Projectiles, [[Morton Koopa Jr.]] fires Pink Magic Projectiles, and [[Ludwig von Koopa]] and [[Kamek]] both fire Blue Magic Projectiles, additionally, the way the Projectiles move can differ depending on the Koopaling that fired them, Iggy's Projectiles are faster than most other Koopalings and Ludwig fires homing Magic Projectiles in his first battle and a row of four Magic Projectiles during his second battle, additionally, [[Kamek]]'s Projectiles are capable of destroying the floor and transforming it into either [[Coin]]s or an enemy. Once a Koopaling is hit twice, they will fire two Magic Projectiles. Additionally, in [[World 2-Tower (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2-Tower]], Roy fires Projectiles from the tower's top as [[Mario]] and friends climb the Tower and in [[World 5-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5-Castle]], Iggy will fire Projectiles at the heroes for most of the level, firing them more rapidly towards the end. Magic Projectiles are not indestructible in this game as the use of [[Ice Ball]]s can cancel them out. In the games files, there exists unused Cyan Projectiles that came offscreen during a level implying that Larry was once going to fire them offscreen too. In this game, the Magic Projectiles resemble a colored fireball like beam with shapes flowing around it which would go on to become its standard appearance in most future games.

====''Super Mario Galaxy 2''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile SMG2.jpg|thumb|left|A Magikoopa firing a Magic Projectile to knock [[Mario]] off the [[Sand Bird]].]]
Magic Projectiles return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. In this game, they are once again fired by [[Magikoopa]]s and can turn into fireballs upon landing however more commonly a Magic Projectile may turn into a [[Micro Goomba]] or a [[Li'l Cinder]] upon landing. Magic Projectiles can be dealt with in the same ways as before however this time, a Magikoopa's Projectiles can also temporarily remove the body segments off the [[Sand Bird]] in the [[Slipsand Galaxy]] which can make them quite dangerous. Since [[Kamella]] doesn't return in this game, the Green Magic Projectiles that turn into [[Green Shell]]s are also absent from this game.

====''Super Mario 3D Land''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile 3D.jpg|thumb|right|Two Magikoopas firing Projectiles.]]
Magic Projectiles return in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', in this game, they are once again fired by [[Magikoopa]]s as primary weapons, in this game, they appear as purple energy beams and they also travel at a slower pace than before. Magic Projectiles can be tricky to avoid especially when they are launched in areas with tight terrain but in most cases, they can be skirted around, when [[Mario]] is [[Statue Mario]], the Projectiles have no effect on him.

====''New Super Mario Bros. 2''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile 3DS.jpg|thumb|left|[[Larry Koopa]] firing a bouncing Magic Projectile.]]
Magic Projectiles are seen again in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. In this game, they are once again fired by the [[Koopalings]] during their battles, [[Roy Koopa]], [[Iggy Koopa]], [[Larry Koopa]], and [[Ludwig von Koopa]] all use Magic Projectiles during their battles. They function as they did before however Roy is the only one who fires his in the traditional sense as Iggy only starts firing his Magic Projectiles after receiving a hit, Larry's Magic Projectiles have the ability to bounce off walls, and Ludwig is able to fire multiple Magic Projectiles, additionally, [[Morton Koopa Jr.]] now uses his [[Magic Wand]] to fire [[Spiked Ball]]s rather than Magic Projectiles. Although the [[Ice Flower]] and [[Penguin Suit]] are absent from this game, the use of a [[Gold Flower]] can still destroy a Magic Projectile.

The Prima Guide for this game generically refers to Magic Projectiles as '''Fireballs'''.

====''New Super Mario Bros. U / New Super Luigi U / New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe====
[[Image:Magic Projectile U.jpg|thumb|right|Ludwig and his clones firing Magic Projectiles to confuse Mario.]]
Magic Projectiles also appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. In these games, they are once again used by the [[Koopalings]] and [[Kamek]] during their respective boss battles however this time, with the Koopalings possessing new weapons instead of their [[Magic Wand]]s, only three Koopalings still retain their use of Magic Projectiles, [[Larry Koopa]], [[Iggy Koopa]], and [[Ludwig von Koopa]], Larry fires his Projectiles in a straight line while Iggy fires a volley of Green Magic Projectiles along with a Red Magic Projectile resembling a [[Podoboo]], if this one hits the [[Lava]] below, it will emerge as a [[Magmaargh]]. Ludwig will split himself into clones who will all fire multiple Magic Projectiles, the real Ludwig always fires three Magic Projectiles while the clones fire two Magic Projectiles. Just as before while under the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]], the use of [[Ice Ball]]s can cancel out a Magic Projectile.

====''Super Mario 3D World / Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury====
[[Image:Magic Projectile 3DU.jpg|thumb|left|Magikoopas firing Projectiles at [[Peach]].]]
Magic Projectiles are seen again in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. In these games. They are fired by [[Magikoopa]]s and are now a lighter pink color, just as before, Magic Projectiles are hazardous to touch however when in Statue form while under the effects of a [[Lucky Bell]], the Projectiles have no effect. The Magic Projectiles are also slower in this game than in other games making dodging them slightly easier. In the [[Bowser's Fury]] side story of the [[Reissue|port]], [[Cat Magikoopa]]s also fire Magic Projectiles.

====''Super Mario Maker / Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS / Super Mario Maker 2''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile Maker.jpg|thumb|right|An 8-bit [[Larry Koopa]] firing a Magic Projectile in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' style of ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.]]
Magic Projectiles appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as its [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|two]] [[Super Mario Maker 2|sequels]] in these games, they are fired by [[Magikoopa]]s, [[Mega Magikoopa]]s as well as [[Koopalings]] [[Larry Koopa]], [[Roy Koopa]], [[Iggy Koopa]], and [[Ludwig von Koopa]], Magic Projectiles can be seen in all four game styles resembling mere orbs in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' styles while resembling an energy ball in the ''[[Super Mario World]]'', ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', and ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' styles. The Projectiles can be dealt with in the same ways as other games.

===''Paper Mario'' series===
====''Paper Mario''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile PM.jpg|thumb|left|A Magikoopa firing a Magic Projectile at Mario.]]
Magic Projectiles appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' this time as a common weapon used by the [[Magikoopa]]s. In battle, they may fire a Magic Projectile at Mario which causes 3 points of damage. If Mario [[Guard]]s against it, he can take less damage. [[Kammy Koopa]] may also use Magic Projectiles in her boss fight as well.
{{br}}
====''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile PMTTYD.jpg|thumb|left|[[Kammy Koopa]] firing a Magic Projectile at Mario.]]
[[Image:Magic Projectile PMTTYD HD.jpg|thumb|right|A [[Red Magikoopa]] firing a Magic Projectile at Mario.]]
Magic Projectiles return in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|its Switch remake]] once again as a weapon used by [[Magikoopa]]s. During battle, they will fire a spell at [[Mario]] or his partners which launches a Magic Projectile doing damage to them however they can [[Guard]] against it for less damage or [[Superguard]] to nullify all damage. [[Red Magikoopa]]s, [[Green Magikoopa]]s, and [[White Magikoopa]]s can also use Magic Projectiles additionally, [[Kammy Koopa]] uses them too in her boss fight.

====''Super Paper Mario''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile SPM.jpg|thumb|right|A Magikoopa firing a Magic Projectile at Mario as Mario destroys another.]]
Magic Projectiles are back in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' once again fired by [[Magikoopa]]s, in this game, the Magic Projectiles now resemble red hexagons and are slightly larger but still slightly easy to avoid. [[Dark Magikoopa]]s also possess the ability to fire Magic Projectiles, their Projectiles are much more powerful and dangerous.
{{br}}

====''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile PMSS.jpg|thumb|left|Kamek firing a Magic Projectile at Mario.]]
Magic Projectiles are seen again in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. In this game, they are fired by either [[Kamek]] or his [[Magikoopa]]s, in battle, Kamek has two types of Magic Projectiles, the yellow ones which simply do damage or the red onesvwhich can temporarily put [[Mario]] to [[Sleep]] for a few turns. Both of these can have their damage lessened and effects nullified by [[Guard|Blocking]].

====''Paper Mario: Color Splash''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile PMCS.jpg|thumb|right|Larry Koopa firing a Magic Projectile.]]
Magic Projectiles appear again in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' this time primarily being used by the [[Koopalings]] and [[Kamek]] in their battles, the Koopalings and Kamek may fire a Magic Projectile at Mario to deal some damage, additionally, in his boss fight, [[Larry Koopa]] may fire a Projectile at the chimney of the [[Sunset Express]] to heal some HP.
{{br}}

===''Mario & Luigi'' series===
====''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile PIT.jpg|thumb|left|Kamek firing a Magic Projectile to begin the boss battle.]]
Magic Projectiles appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. In this game, they are fired by [[Kamek]] to create enemies such as [[Goomba]]s and [[Boo]]s in [[Baby Bowser's Castle]]. Additionally, Kamek may also use Magic Projectiles during his boss fight on [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]].
{{br}}
====''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story / Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile BIS.jpg|thumb|left|A [[Magifoofa]] firing a Magic Projectile.]]
[[Image:Magic Projectile BIS DX.jpg|thumb|right|A [[Magifoofa]] firing a Magic Projectile as seen in the remake.]]
Magic Projectiles return in  ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' as well as ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' in these games, they are prominently fired by [[Magifoofa]]s to initiate battles against [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], additionally, a Magifoofa can also fire petrified shapes at the plumbers which resemble the shapes around a Magic Projectile. Additionally, the [[Magikoopa Mob]] uses a volley of Magic Projectiles against foes.

====''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile DT.jpg|thumb|right|[[Kamek]] getting ready to fire a Magic Projectile.]]
Magic Projectiles return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' once again used by [[Kamek]] in his various boss battles in [[Dreamy Neo Bowser Castle]]. Kamek will use these as his primary means of attack [[Mario]] and [[Dreamy Luigi]] and they must either jump to avoid them or Hammer them back. Kamek’s duplicates may also use Magic Projectiles as well.
{{br}}

====''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile PJ.jpg|thumb|left|[[Larry Koopa]] firing a Magic Projectile at [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] who proceeds to dodge it.]]
Magic Projectiles return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' being used by [[Larry Koopa]], [[Ludwig von Koopa]], and [[Iggy Koopa]] during their respective battles, additionally, [[Kamek]] and [[Paper Kamek]] may also use Magic Projectiles in their battles, the Koopalings and Kamek will fire their Projectiles at [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] and they must dodge them by jumping or they will take some hefty damage.
{{br}}

====''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile SS DX.jpg|thumb|right|[[Ludwig von Koopa]] firing Magic Projectiles at the plumbers.]]
Magic Projectiles are seen again in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. In this game, they are used by [[Ludwig von Koopa]] during his battle in [[Bowser's Castle (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Bowser's Castle]]. In the battle, Ludwig will use his wand to create a huge energy ball which will hover above the battlefield and he will proceed to fire waves of Magic Projectiles at [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] who then must destroy them with their [[Hammer]]s before launching the Energy Ball back at Ludwig.

{{br}}


===''Mario Party'' series===
====''Mario Party DS''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile PDS.jpg|thumb|left|[[Kamek]] firing Magic Projectiles at [[Princess Peach]].]]
Magic Projectiles appear in ''[[Mario Party DS]]'' this time being used by [[Kamek]] during his boss fight in [[Book Bash]], when the player reaches Kamek, he will fire Magic Projectiles at them in attempt to damage them as they try to ink his face.

{{br}}

====''Mario Party 10''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile MP10.jpg|thumb|right|[[Kamek]] firing Magic Projectiles at everyone.]]
Magic Projectiles are seen again in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. In this game, they are fired by [[Kamek]] during his boss fight in [[Kamek's Rocket Rampage]]. Kamek will fire them frequently throughout his boss battle and being hit by one will dock a point so evasion is very important during this minigame, when Kamek is angry, he will fire more Projectiles.
{{br}}

===''Princess Peach'' series===
====''Super Princess Peach''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile SPP.jpg|thumb|left|[[Giant Kamek]] firing Magic Projectiles at [[Princess Peach]] who is slipping away.]]
Magic Projectiles appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' this time as a weapon used by the [[Giant Kamek]], the boss of [[Giddy Sky]]. In battle, Giant Kamek will launch three Projectiles at once to hit [[Peach]] who must avoid them, when Giant Kamek is down to two lives, he will start firing five Projectiles at once. The Projectiles aren’t too hard to avoid however.
{{br}}


===''Yoshi'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island / Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile YI.jpg|thumb|right|[[Kamek]] firing Magic Projectiles at [[Yoshi]].]]
Magic Projectiles appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. In these games. They are fired by [[Kamek]] as [[Yoshi]] moves down the final corridor with [[Baby Mario]], if they hit Yoshi, they can knock Baby Mario off his back, if the Projectiles hit a block, it will turn into an enemy.
{{br}}

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[Image:Magic Projectile YNI.jpg|thumb|left|[[Kamek]] firing a couple Magic Projectiles at [[Yoshi]].]]
Magic Projectiles are back in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. In this game, they are fired by [[Kamek]] during his various boss fights in the Forts. Once again they can knock [[Baby Mario]] off [[Yoshi]]'s back and can also function like [[Yoshi Egg]]s as they can move along the tracks of [[Arrow Cloud]]s. The Magic Projectiles in this game resemble their appearance from the more recent mainstream ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games and when Kamek has been hit twice, he will start shooting two Magic Projectiles at once.

==Trivia==
*In most games, the [[Koopalings]]' Magic Projectiles look slightly different from the [[Magikoopa]]s' Magic Projectiles however they’re still composed of the same energy as they all have the same shape formations.
