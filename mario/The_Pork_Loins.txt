'''The Pork Loins''' is a four-piece rock band in the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' series. They first appear in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', as an unnamed band in the [[microgame]] [[Garage Rock]], then in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' where they score third place on [[Ear Candy]]'s top five singers.
