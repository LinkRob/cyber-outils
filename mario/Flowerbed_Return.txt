The '''Flowerbed Return''' is [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]'s [[Defensive Power Shot]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

== Description ==
This Power Shot is used by Daisy. When Daisy's meter is full, the player can perform this move when the ball is behind her. Daisy's Power Shot can miss the ball with a far distance. When Daisy performs it, she spins and leaps among the flowers rising from the bed. While she is in the air, she hits the ball.
