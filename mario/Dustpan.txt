'''Dustpan''' is [[18-Volt]]'s boss [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It is designed after the [[Chiritorie (microgame)|Chiritorie]] [[microgame]] from ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', and it even has "Vacuum!" as its command.

==Layout==
Like the Chiritorie microgame, Dustpan displays [[Chiritori]] and the objects on a black background, although the background is instead colored teal and has a wooden frame surrounding there the gameplay takes place.

==Gameplay==
The player controls Chiritori, which has to vacuum every crumpled piece of paper while avoiding a [[Green Fireball]] moving around.
