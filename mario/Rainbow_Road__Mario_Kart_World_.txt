'''Rainbow Road''' is a track from [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']], featured in the [[Special Cup]] as the final track as well as the final track of the game as a whole. It is located south of the island where the game takes place, and, alongside the other tracks in the Special Cup, is unlocked by completing all of the previous cups from [[Mushroom Cup]] to [[Lightning Cup]].

== Description and Layout ==

=== Starting Area ===
The racers start on a tiled road before tagging a glide ramp, after which they go on a straightaway and head into the water.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref> This area is skipped if the player disables intermissions in Versus Mode.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hZvYdv2SR1U</ref>

=== Water Area ===
In this section, the racers drive through water to get to a [[Glide Ramp]]. This leads to a Warp Zone.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref> This area is skipped if the player disables intermissions in Versus Mode.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hZvYdv2SR1U</ref>

=== Warp Zone ===
To get to Rainbow Road, the racers must follow a light beam with multiple colours.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref> This area is skipped if the player disables intermissions in Versus Mode.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hZvYdv2SR1U</ref>

=== Landing Platform ===
After warping, the racers reach a circular platform with a rainbow path leading away from it.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref> If the player disables intermissions in Versus Mode, this is where they start.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hZvYdv2SR1U</ref>

=== Turning Rainbow Path Area ===
After the landing platform, the racers make a slight left to wind up for a 180 degree right turn, followed by a slight left turn that leads into a straightaway with some ramps. The path winds left and right while ascending, leading into a 315-degree right turn that leads into a Glide Ramp.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref>

=== Split Path Zone ===
After landing from the glide ramp, the racers take a left turn which leads into a straightaway with ramps. The path splits, and the racers can choose either path, with both winding around before converging at a section gate that leads to the Glide Ramp that segues into the next area.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref>

=== Crystal River Ride Island ===
The racers visit an island that has a river that they drive through and blue and purple crystals. The river winds through the island before the racers take another winding rainbow path to leave it. The path splits in two and the branches reunite before a section gate and a Glide Ramp to the next area.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref>

=== Space Station ===
This area is a space station where racers race through a winding road before going through a rainbow path in [[Anti-gravity]]. Eventually, they reach a glide ramp that leads to the next area.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref>

=== Snake Path Zone ===
When the racers land, they will find that the road splits again, and the paths wind around even more chaotically. This section is also in Anti-gravity. There are also [[Dash Panel|Dash Panels]] and conveyor belts. When the paths reunite, the racers go through another glide ramp.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref>

=== Homestretch ===
This is another winding path that the racers have to drive through before taking another Glide Ramp.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref>

=== Goal Gate ===
This course has a floating ring-like goal gate like [[Bowser Castle (Mario Kart 7)|Bowser Castle]] from [[Mario Kart 7|''Mario Kart 7'']]. The racers must drive through it to finish the course.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4SMmi5m66aY</ref>

== Trivia ==

* This is the only [[Rainbow Road]] to finish in a different location to where it started.
* This is the only course in the game to have proper [[Anti-gravity]]. However, the karts do not have their physics changed.
* This is the only course that isn't selectable in Free Roam. (As of January 2026)
