The '''Royal Flail''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Royal Flail is a small flail that is light blue and yellow in color.

==History==
The Royal Flail is an important item that can be found in [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]]. In order to access it, [[Wario]] must transverse through dark rooms until he reaches one with two [[Scare-Ohs!]] in it. After defeating the Scare-Ohs!, the purple chest containing the Royal Flail will appear. The Royal Flail can then be used on a mural that unlocks a door allowing Wario to proceed.
