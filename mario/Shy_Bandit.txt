'''The''' '''Shy Bandit''' is an enemy that can occasionally appear on the world map in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

== Stats ==
'''HP:''' 26[[File:PMCS Shy Bandit Battle.png|thumb|450x450px]]'''Attacks:'''

* Attack ('''5''')
* Blowback Attack ('''12''')
* Straw Attack (heals itself while reducing the paint of Mario)
* Last Straw Attack (heals itself while reducing the paint of Mario)
* Blind Attack (causes Blind status)
'''Sell Price:''' ?

== Prism Island ==
When he appears, [[Mario]] must catch and defeat him before he slurps up all the color in an area. If the Shy Bandit is successful, he will reset the progress of that specific level down to 0% (with some exceptions). If the player reaches the card before the Shy Bandit, the player will receive a [[File:PMCS_Coin.png|frameless|20x20px]]<small>x</small>300 bonus. If the Shy Bandit is stopped after sucking some paint, a fight will begin.

== Battle ==
Once the player reaches his location, he will either spew paint that blinds you or summon 3 enemies to attack you. The blindess attack inflicts blind status, causing a missed attack no matter what card is played. Defeating him will also grant a [[File:PMCS_Coin.png|frameless|20x20px]]<small>x</small>300 bonus.
