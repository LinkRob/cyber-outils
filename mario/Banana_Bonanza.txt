'''Banana Bonanza''' is a [[Bonus Game]] that can be found in various levels of ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]''. It is sometimes accessible from [[Bonus Barrel]]s.

== Gameplay ==
The minigame starts with a [[Barrel]] roulette. When the player presses the {{button|DS|A}} button, the roulette stops on a small, medium, or large Barrel, determining the size of the barrel used by Donkey Kong. His objective is to collect as many falling [[Banana]]s and [[Banana Bunch]]es into the Barrel as possible within a time limit of ten seconds. When the minigame ends, Donkey Kong hides in the Barrel, and the number of collected Bananas are revealed as they fly out. Donkey Kong gets an [[Extra Life]] for every 100 Bananas that he collected.

==Browser game==
[[File:BananaBonanza PC.png|thumb|left|Adobe Flash version]]
An {{wp|Adobe Flash}} version of this minigame, known as '''''Banana Bonanza!''''', was playable on the official ''DK: Jungle Climber'' website. The browser version is largely the same as the original minigame, although it does not have a visible time limit.
