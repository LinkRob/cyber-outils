'''Mushroom dealer Pianta''' is a character in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. He is a light blue [[Pianta]] who carries mushrooms around on his back. He can be found in [[Delfino Plaza]] during various parts of [[Mario]]'s journey. Most of the time he  gets caught on fire.
