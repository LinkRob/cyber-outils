The '''Coatroom''' ('''Cloakroom''' in European releases) is a room of [[Gloomy Manor]] featured in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It can be accessed from the [[Foyer]], and inspecting the toilet takes [[Luigi]] directly to the [[Bathroom (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Bathroom]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
During [[Poltergust 5000 (mission)|A-1: Poltergust 5000]], Luigi heads into the Coatroom to vacuum up the [[Greenie]]s in the mansion. Luigi manages to get into the Bathroom by using the toilet and finds the Greenies in a shower. The Greenies escape back into the Coatroom where Luigi follows and vacuums them up into the [[Poltergust 5000]].

Luigi can go back into the Coatroom during [[Quiet Please!|A-3: Quiet Please!]] to find and catch a [[Gold Greenie]] to earn a lot of money.
