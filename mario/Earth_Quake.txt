The '''Earth Quake''' is an item which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
This item could also be seen as an upgraded version of the [[POW Block]] item as it turns [[Mario]]'s shell-based enemies like [[Buzzy Beetle]]s and [[Koopa Troopa]]s on their shells. Since this is a regular item, it can just be purchased, but it only works once. Another unfortunate oversight with this item is that midair enemies are unaffected by its attack.
