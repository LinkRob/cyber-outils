The '''Icead''' is an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They are an ice-elemental [[Magmad]] variant.

== Description ==
Iceads are slug-like creatures that resemble blue mounds that get darker near the bottom and have yellow eyes that are contorted into a sad expression, hence why they are named "Icead." They have a sub-species in [[Concordia]] that has a spiked head.

==Behaviour ==
Like their fiery counterparts, Iceads can be found on [[Mount Pajamaja]]. Their behaviour in the field is similar to that of a [[Magmad]]; getting too close to an Icead will cause them to engulf themselves in sheer cold and chase after the Bros. Eventually, the creatures will become vulnerable to an [[First Strike|ambush]] as their sheer cold wears off.

=== Combat ===
Both species fight together in tandem. The Iceads have the same attacks that their fiery counterparts do, but their attacks cannot inflict [[Burn]]. Additionally, due to their ice element, they take more damage from the [[Fire Flower (move)|Fire Flower]] [[Special Attack]].

While the Mario brothers can stomp the Iceads properly and safely, Magmads cannot be stomped on. Magmads and Iceads become each other when damaged, provided the creature survives.

==== Statistics ====
The Icead is a Level 18 monster that has 125 hit points, an attack power of 137, a defense of 98, and a speed of 70, giving 50 Experience and dropping 20 Coins along with a Super Candy in 1 out of 20 drops and a Secret Box in 1 out of 100 drops.

A spiked variant of Icead can be found in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. This version, like the Magmad, which returns in the same game, [[Spiny (Enemy type)|cannot be jumped on safely]]. It can be found on [[Heatfreeze Island]] and [[Slippenglide Island]]. This may be a regional variant, much like the [[Hoohoo Mountain]] and [[Teehee Valley]] [[Dry Bones]] (which are both quadrupedal on top of the former lacking a shell) compared to the bipedal Dry Bones of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. These spiked Iceads whack their target with their head spike; this can be blocked with a hammer strike. They can also use a strafing cold breath attack which must be jumped over. If there is more than one, two spiked Iceads can bounce a snowball between themselves; this must be blocked with a hammer strike.

==== Glohm-infused Spiked Iceads ====
A [[Glohm]]-contaminated variant can be found haunting Slippenglide. This version may occasionally lose a turn. When [[Reclusa]] hijacks [[Concordia]], this "Glohm Icead" can also be found haunting Heatfreeze Island, but both must always take a turn, and can duplicate themselves, shuffling among the fakes to save their own skin. The [[Pipegunk]] can take on a form similar to this type of Icead if it is exposed to cold air.

===Statistics===
The Heatfreeze Island version is of Level 21, with 141 hit points, an attack power of 83, a defense of 88, and a speed of 69, and gives 143 Experience, 40 coins, and possibly a [[Mushroom|Super Mushroom]], an Ultra Mushroom, and/or some [[Crafting Materials (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Heatfreeze Ore]] upon defeat, just like the [[Magmad]]. The Slippenglide Island version is of Level 24 with 175 hit points, an Attack of 97, a Defense of 105 , and a Speed of 95, with an experience reward of 127 and a loot drop of 31 coins along with possibly a Super Mushroom (3%), an Ultra Mushroom (1%), and/or a sample of Slippenglide Snow (33%).

Glohm Iceads on Slippenglide Island have the same loot drops as their counterparts (barring giving 48 Coins when destroyed) but can also drop some Glohm Dust (3%) or a Glohm Fragment (7%) in addition to the regular version's loot drops before [[Reclusa]] awakens; their stats are otherwise boosted compared to the clean spiked Iceads, with a Level of 24, 329 hit points, an Attack of 99, a Defense of 116, and a Speed of 95, giving 200 Experience. On Heatfreeze Island after Reclusa goes on a rampage, their stats are identical to the Glohm Magmad on Heatfreeze Island (734 hit points, Attack 212, Defense 302, Speed 185, giving 1627 Experience and 242 Coins when destroyed along with probably an Ultra Mushroom, a Max Mushroom, some Heatfreeze Ore, a Glohm Fragment, a Glohm Crystal, or some Glohm Dust). On Slippenglide Island, the only change is the Heatfreeze Ore loot drop being changed to a sample of Slippenglide Snow.

==Behind the Scenes==

===Etymology===
The Icead's name comes from a pun on "ice" (reflecting the creature's ice element) and "sad" (due to the apparent sad eyes face).

==Nomenclature==
In Japanese, Iceads were known as "Aisuns" (''Aisun'', written アイスン), derived from "ice." Their Chinese name, ''Róngbīng Guài'' (written 融冰怪), literally means "Melting Ice Monster." In Spanish, they are known as ''Gelus'', which could either come from ''gélido'' ("icy") with the Latin suffix ''-us'' or was wripped straight from the Latin translation of "ice," which is ''gelus''. In Portuguese, they are called "''Gélidus''," from ''gélido'' (icy) and the Latin suffix ''-us''. Their Italian name, ''Ghiàccide'', comes from ''ghiáccio'' ("ice") and the suffix ''-ide'' (which denotes that something pertains to another), which means that language literally calls them "Pertaining to Ice." Their French name, ''Givrus'', comes from ''givre'' ("frost") with the Latin suffix ''-us''. In German, they are called ''Frostirren'' (singular ''Frostirr''; from English "frost" and ''Irrlicht'' ("will-o'-wisp"). Their Korean name, ''Aiseun'' (written 아이슨), comes straight from the Japanese name. In Russian, their name is ''Ledun'' (written Ледун), derived from ''lyod'' ("ice"; written лёд) and the Russian masculine noun ending ''-un'' (written -ун).
