'''Flytraps''' are an enemy in the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}. They first appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Flytraps are based on {{w|venus flytrap}}s, but are significantly larger, have a pair of yellow eyes, and have a mouth wide enough to chomp onto a [[human]]s.

===Behavior===
A Flytrap attacks by lunging at [[Luigi]] and then attempting to eat him, but it is possible for Luigi to break free of the Flytrap.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''===
[[File:Flytrap about to chomp LMDM.png|thumb|left|200px|A Flytrap about to munch on Luigi in the [[Courtyard (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Courtyard]] area in ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'']]
Flytraps appear in [[Haunted Towers]] and [[Treacherous Mansion]]. A Flytrap can be stunned briefly using the [[Strobulb]], but they are otherwise unaffected by Luigi's usual attacks, especially because Flytraps are not among one of the [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] enemies. To defeat a Flytrap, Luigi needs to pull a spiky fruit off a nearby plant using his [[Poltergust 5000]] and shoot it at the Flytrap, which then eats it and dies.

===''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''===
[[File:Dr Potter and Flytrap LM3.jpg|thumb|250px|''Luigi's Mansion 3'' screenshot of Dr. Potter with his Flytrap]]
One Flytrap makes an appearance in the [[Garden Suites]], where [[Dr. Potter]] uses a watering can to transform a sapling into a Flytrap.
