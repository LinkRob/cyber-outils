'''Backwards Bill''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Backwards Bill is an item with the exact appearance of a [[Bullet Bill]], reminiscent of its ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' artwork, that only appears in [[Perilous Palace Path]]. A Backwards Bill functions similarly to the [[Setback Shell]], except it knocks the selected opponent back five [[space]]s instead of two like the Setback Shell. A [[Golden Dash Mushroom]], which allows the user to move an extra five spaces, has the opposite function of a Backwards Bill.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
The characters have a chance of winning a Backwards Bill from either an [[Item Space]] or from winning a [[minigame]] and selecting the item.
