A '''Smiley Flower''', sometimes named '''Flower''', is one of the main items within the [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi'' platforming games]], having first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They have made a few cameo appearances in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]] and the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]].

==In the ''Yoshi'' franchise==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
[[File:Smiley Flower SMW2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Here, they are named '''Special Flowers''', and there are five of them to collect in each [[level]]. Once each Special Flower is obtained in a level, the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] earns a [[extra life|1 up]]. The main purpose of a Smiley Flower is to contribute to the [[completion]] score of a level, with each Special Flower being worth 10 points, making for a total of 50; for a complete score, a Yoshi has to also collect thirty [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]] and twenty [[Red Coin]]s, all worth a point each, making for the other fifty points.

Each Special Flower is added to the [[GOAL! Ring]], which spins around as a roulette whenever a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] jumps through one, and if the GOAL! Ring lands on a Smiley Flower, the Yoshi can continue to a [[Bonus Challenge]].

There exists an enemy called [[Fooly Flower]] that mimics the Smiley Flower.

===''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''===
Smiley Flowers have the same purpose as in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''.
