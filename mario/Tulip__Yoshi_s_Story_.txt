The '''Tulip''', also named the '''Turbo Tulip''', is a type of item which appears in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. Using one turns a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] into a [[Yoshi Egg]] temporarily, upon which he can roll around and access certain areas that he could not otherwise go to.

In ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]], Tulips appear in the background of the [[Mario's Star (golf course)|Mario's Star]] golf course.
