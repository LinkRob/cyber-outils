'''Poki and Piki''' are Arabian twins who appear in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'', released overseas as ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Poki and Piki are the children of [[Mama (Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic)|Mama]] and [[Papa (Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic)|Papa]] and brother and sister to [[Lina]] and [[Imajin]].

==Physical description==
Both characters wear pajamas, with Poki being a boy whose pajamas are blue and Piki being a girl whose pajamas are pink.

==In ''Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic''==
In the intro, while reading a storybook, Poki and Piki are captured by [[Wart|Mamu]], and must be rescued by their family. The twins are seen again much later during the ending.

Like their other family members, Poki and Piki do not return in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Both of them are not replaced by any character in particular.
