'''Liz''' is a character who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[No Way to Treat a Queenie]]." She is a [[Brooklyn]] resident who believes herself to be the Queen of England. Liz lives in an asylum due to her unusual behavior. Liz's name is a shortening of "Elizabeth," a name traditionally given to the Queen of England.
