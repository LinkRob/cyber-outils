'''Paper Kamek '''is a recurring character and a boss in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Appearance ==
Paper Kamek is a [[Magikoopa]] somehow similar to [[Kamek]]. He does, however, have a white outline and a more simplified appearance.

==History ==
The Bros. and Paper Mario first finds Paper Kamek in [[Doop Doop Dunes]] with Kamek. They argued continuously, but eventually planned to have [[Papercraft Kamek]] to destroy the trio who is on top of [[Papercraft Luigi]]. However, they end up defeated by the trio.{{Sectstub}}
