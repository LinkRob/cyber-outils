'''Slippas''' is a type of snake enemy originating from ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', and returning in ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]''. Slippa is found exclusively in various cave levels, making them rare, but in truth they are no more harmful than the [[Gnawty]] enemy.

Slippa's main attack pattern is to simply slither forwards. Slippa make hissing noises when nearby to show it's presence. In some areas, these enemies can spawn infinitely from oil barrels. Slippa can be defeated with a simple roll or jump.
