The '''Red Jar''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Red Jar can only be obtained after accepting one of [[Koopa Koot]]'s many favors. Koopa Koot seeks this rare item and sends [[Mario]] to [[Dry Dry Outpost]] to get it. In order to get the Red Jar, Mario must head into [[Little Mouser's Shop]] and buy a [[Dusty Hammer]], a [[Dried Pasta]], another Dusty Hammer, and a [[Dried Shroom]] all in that order to receive the Red Jar from the [[Little Mouser (character)|shopkeeper]]. After giving it to Koopa Koot, the old [[Koopa Troopa]] rewards Mario with three [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]].

==Trivia==
*The Red Jar is the last item that is needed to give to Koopa Koot therefore ending the favor sequence for the remainder of the game.
*The method to receive the Red Jar is done in a similar manner in order to see [[Moustafa]].
*The Red Jar can be obtained even before accepting Koopa Koot's request if the player knows the purchasing method ahead of time.
