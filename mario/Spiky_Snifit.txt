A '''Spiky Snifit''' is an enemy which appears in [[Teehee Valley]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They have the appearance of a cactus with a [[Snifit]]'s mask.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ==
Spiky Snifits are found in battles in Teehee Valley that accompanies [[Gritty Goomba]]s or the [[Anuboo]]. They throw spike balls at [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] that the Bros. should jump over (if the Spiky Snifits vibrate their mouth) or it goes over the Bros. (if you don't see the creatures vibrate their mouths). Spiky Snifits unexpectedly sometimes shoot out a [[Super Mushroom]], which restores [[HP]] when touched, at times. Prior to launching their projectile, Spiky Snifits shift. Revealing their right arm means that Mario will have to defend against them, and waving their left arm means that Luigi is their target.

Spiky Snifits are weak to Mario's [[Firebrand]] technique. They cannot be jumped on.
