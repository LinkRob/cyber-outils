'''Sand clouds''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Yellow Yoshi sand cloud SMW.png|thumb|left|200px|''Super Mario World'' screenshot of Yellow Yoshi defeating a [[Rex]] with sand clouds]]
Sand clouds are puffs that a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] of any color can create if they have either a [[Yellow Shell]] or a [[Flashing Shell]] and land after performing a [[jump]], defeating any enemies nearby. A [[Yellow Yoshi]] can create sand clouds regardless of which color the Koopa Shell in its mouth is.
