|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Full House''' is a special move used only jesters in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Full House is used, the Jester throws a card at its target. It appears as an Ace card.

==Effects==
Full House can attack a single character for double the damage. This attack can be blocked against to avoid high damage.

==Trivia==
*Full House is based on a Full House from a Poker game.
