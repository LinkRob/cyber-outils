'''Skedaddlers''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Skedaddlers are rodent-like creatures with orange and white fur and wear a small, black shell on their back.

===Behavior===
A Skedaddler attacks by spitting seed-like projectiles. A Skedaddler stands in place until approached by either the player's character or an incoming [[Koopa Shell]], upon which the Skedaddler flees in the opposite direction and runs up to the point of reaching a dead end. If a Skedaddler is a significant distance away, it stops running and goes back to spitting its projectiles. Some Skedaddlers hold an item over their head or have [[coin]]s trailing behind them.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''==
Skedaddlers are the titular feature of their own level, [[Scram, Skedaddlers!]]. They also appear in [[Wiggler Race Mountaineering!]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Its name is a pun on the word "skedaddle," meaning to hurry away.
