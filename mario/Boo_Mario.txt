'''Boo Mario''' is a transformation featured within the [[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|''Super Mario Galaxy'' series]]. It has the self-explanatory purpose of transforming [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] into a [[Boo]], which is done by touching a [[Boo Mushroom]].

== In the ''Super Mario'' series ==
=== ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ===
This form first appeared in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' in the [[Ghostly Galaxy]]. The player can have Boo Mario float around by pressing {{Button|wii|a}} repeatedly, and also make him turn invisible by shaking the [[Wii Remote]]. While Boo Mario is invisible, he can pass through through solid walls. The [[Boo]]s in the area that Boo Mario is in will become infatuated and follow him around. Mario reverts to his regular form if he either touches a source of light, water, or an enemy.

Boo Mario uses this transformation to race the [[Spooky Speedster]] in one mission to gain a Power Star. In another, he uses it to rescue his brother, Luigi, imprisoned in a haunted mansion. If Mario tries to talk to Luigi as Boo Mario, he’ll accidentally scare him, so Mario must turn back to his human form to reassure his twin.

=== ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ===
The Boo Mushroom also appears in [[Haunty Halls Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

== Other appearances ==

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
Boo Mario appears as a spirit in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. If the player obtains the spirit, the player's jump and move speed will increase, but will be easier to be launched.
