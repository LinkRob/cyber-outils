The '''Plumber's Periscope''' is an item in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Recycled Koopa]]."

==Description==
The Plumber's Periscope is a regular periscope owned by [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]].

==Episode appearances==
In "Recycled Koopa," after [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], Luigi, [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] and [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] clear out the [[Koopa trash|garbage]] dumped into [[Princess Peach's Castle|Mushroom Castle]], they believe the [[Koopa Troop]] will reattempt it, at which point Toad brings out Luigi's Plumber's Periscope in a cardboard box and gives it to Mario to spy on the Koopalings and Bowser in order to find out. With the Plumber's Periscope, Mario looks into a nearby [[Warp Pipe]] and notices that [[Roy Koopa|Bully]], [[Wendy O. Koopa|Kootie Pie]] and [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Big Mouth Koopa]] are employing [[Sledge Bro]]thers to dump garbage through a Warp Pipe leading to another land. In response, Mario and Luigi jump through the Warp Pipe to [[Bowser's Castle|King Koopa's Castle]], planning to put an end to the Koopalings and their pollution.
