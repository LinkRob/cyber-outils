The '''Gelateria''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Description==
The Gelateria is a restaurant in [[Diamond City]] owned by [[Joe|Gelato Joe]], and it is also where [[Mona]] works at in the game.

==In ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''==
Mona's stage is centered around her having to get to the Gelateria on time before it opens. Her boss, Gelato Joe, clocks in at 7:53. However, Mona is a distance away, and zooms down the road on her [[Mona's scooter|scooter]], anxiously trying to arrive on time and prompting the police to give chase. In the ending cutscene, Mona arrives to the Gelateria to clock in at 7:59, precisely a few seconds before the Gelateria officially opens at 8:00, leaving Mona relieved.

After the ending cutscene of every [[microgame]] host's stage, starting with [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]], a birds-eye-view scene is shown where the character goes down to the Gelateria, where they hang out for the remainder of the game. Following this is a close-up scene of the Gelateria, where each character there has their own animation, such as how Jimmy T is seen grooving from the bottom-right open window of the Gelateria. [[Wario]] is the last character to arrive at the Gelateria, where he does not show up until his second stage is completed.

==Trivia==
*The music during the recurring scene of the characters at the Gelateria is a shortened version of "Mr. Ether & Planaria" from ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.
