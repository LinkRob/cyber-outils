'''Noobis''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Behavior ===
It is a statue that attacks by trying to crush Wario. They can only be found in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]] usually alongside [[Horkus Porkus]]. They can be defeated by jumping on them or ramming into them three times.

== Etymology ==
Its name is a pun on the Egyptian god, Anubis, and the word "noob" which is a popular gaming term that is often used as an insult toward others.
