'''Chomps Jr.''' is one of several aquatic enemies in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]''. Smaller and faster than the larger [[Chomps]], Chomps Jr. swims around more aimlessly than its counterpart. This makes it a bit more dangerous than Chomps, but unlike Chomps, it tends to be more of a loner and doesn't always appear in 'packs.' This enemy can be defeated by [[Enguarde the Swordfish]] stabbing them.

Chomps Jr. was going to appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Racing]]'' until the game became cancelled as they were seen in underwater races.
