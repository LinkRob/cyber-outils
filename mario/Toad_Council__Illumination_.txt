The '''Toad Council''' is a group of eight Toads, including the [[Toad General]] as their leader, who appear in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

==History==
The Council had a meeting, where their General announces that [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] stole the [[Super Star (Illumination)|super star]], planning an invasion, and forced wedding at [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|their kingdom]]. Even with the star's power making him invincible all the Toads and their kingdom will be destroyed. [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]], however planned to convine [[Cranky Kong (Illumination)|Cranky Kong]] and his kong army to assist them, to defeat Bowser once and for all. But when she returned with the army captured by Bowser, the council and the other Toads were forced to evacuate, while she and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] buys them some time. With the mushroom kingdom and [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]] saved by Bowser's shrunken defeat, the council returned to the castle, for the [[Mario (Illumination)|Super Mar]][[Luigi (Illumination)|io Brothers]] to live in [[Mario Bros. House (Illumination)|their own new house]].
