'''Professor E. Gadd's Research''' is the ninth level of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 11.

== Layout ==

The 1st battle contains 8 [[Boo]]s. The enemy captain is a Boo, and he knows [[Sneak Attack]].

The final battle contains 3 Boos, and 5 [[Tail Boo]]s. The enemy captain is a Tail Boo, and he knows [[Rally]].

== Possible recruits ==

* Boo (Lv. 11)
* Tail Boo (Lv. 11)
