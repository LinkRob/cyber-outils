'''Oranges''' are [[fruit]]s featured in a few games of the {{franchise|Yoshi}} as well as the [[Yoshi's Fruit Cart]] attraction in ''[[Nintendo Land]]''.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Woolly World''===
In ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', orange slices appear in [[Bonus Game (Yoshi's Woolly World)|Bonus Games]]. They are worth fifty beads, like other fruits.

===''Yoshi's Crafted World''===
In ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'', five orange papier-mâché [[List of crafts in Yoshi's Crafted World|crafts]] appear on the flip side of [[Jungle Tour Challenge]]. They are one of the souvenir hunt requests from the [[Rumble Jungle]] [[Blockafeller]].
