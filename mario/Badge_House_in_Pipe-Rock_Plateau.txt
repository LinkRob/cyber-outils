'''Badge House in Pipe-Rock Plateau''' is located in [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It is a [[Poplin House]] and requires three [[Wonder Seed|Wonder Seeds]] to unlock. After clearing this level, an open area containing many courses is unlocked.

After entering the house, the Poplin gives the player a Parachute Cap badge. A small area is then opened for the player to try out the badge. The player leaves by going to the far right of the area.{{SMBWNav}}
