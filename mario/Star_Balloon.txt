A '''Star Balloon''' is a recurring object in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.

==Description==
Star Balloons are a type of balloon shaped exactly like a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] with a string attached to it. Characters can find Star Balloons when they float down to a [[space]] on the [[board]]. Star Balloons serve a similar role to [[Star Space]] of preceding ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' games, although function differently than them: When a character passes a Star Balloon, they are offered to purchase a Star for 10 [[coin]]s, although some Star Balloons offer two to five Stars instead of one, in which case the character can purchase a multiple of them at an added price of 10 coins each. Whenever Star Balloons are used up, they vanish, but more Star Balloons eventually replace them on the board.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party: Star Rush''===
Star Balloons exclusively appear in Balloon Bash mode.

===''Mario Party: The Top 100''===
Star Balloons exclusively appear in Minigame Match mode.
