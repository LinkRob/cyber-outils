'''Inga''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Inga is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that is purple in color an has orange hair.

=== Personality traits ===
Inga is known for being cunning.

==History==
Inga ran the inn of [[Flopside]]. Her inn resembled a haunted mansion and has three different packages that can be purchased for a certain amount of coins. Purchasing a certain package will also have Inga cook up a certain item in the morning. After resting up, Inga will mention she had cooked breakfast but has hidden it away. The only way to find it is to use [[Mario]]'s Flip ability to find the item in 3-D.

==Trivia==
*Her [[Flipside]] counterpart is [[Tinga]].
