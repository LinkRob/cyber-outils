|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}'''Fire Shell''' is a special move used by [[Kooper]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can only be used once Kooper reaches the [[Ultra Rank]] and it requires 5 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use. This is also Kooper's strongest move.

==Effects==

To use the Fire Shell, the player must repeatedly tap the control stick to the left to fill up the bar (similar to the [[Dizzy Shell]] move). Once it is filled, Kooper will spin into all ground based enemies for fire and high damage. The Fire Shell is very helpful against ice enemies and [[Dry Bones]]. The Fire Shell may also cause [[burn]] damage to enemies.

==Trivia==
*Kooper's Fire Shell is similar in function to [[Vivian]]'s [[Fiery Jinx]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
