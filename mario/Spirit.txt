'''Spirit''' is a stat that was first introduced in [[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]. 
==History==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
The Spirit stat only applies to playable characters in Minion Quest mode. It determines the probability of a trooper using their [[Special Skill]]. Spirit can be increased in battle by using the [[Captain Command]] Rally.
