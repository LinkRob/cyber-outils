|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Sporocyst''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can only be used by [[Amanita]]s.

==Effects==
With Sporocyst, the Amanita will be able to turn one of [[Mario]]'s party into a [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]] making them useless in battle until the effects wear off. Wearing accessories such as the Trueform Pin or Lazy Shell armor are good ways to prevent being turned into a mushroom.
