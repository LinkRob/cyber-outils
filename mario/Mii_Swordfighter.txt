'''Mii Swordfighter''' is one of the many playable characters in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]''.

==''Super Smash Bros. 3DS/Wii U''==
A Mii Swordfighter, known as the Mii Sword Fighter in the PAL version, is one of the three different types of Mii Fighters that appear in ''Super Smash Bros. 4'', along with Mii Brawlers and Mii Gunners.

It has overall average mobility. Of the three Mii types, the Mii Swordfighter has the median dash, the fastest walking speed, and the lowest jumps, as well as being the only Mii type that is incapable of wall jumping. 

==''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''==
Mii Swordfighter reappeared as a playable character in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. They were confirmed as a playable character on June 12, 2018, along with the other Mii Fighter types, Brawler and Gunner. Mii Swordfighter is classified as fighter #52.
