A '''Fire Shooter''' (also called a '''Fire Blaster''' or a '''Fire Cannon''') is an obstacle that first appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They resemble the [[Flash Liquidizer Ultra Dousing Device]], but they are red and shoot fire from their turret. They are completely indestructible and [[Mario]] cannot destroy them. Their relatives include [[Water Shooter]]s and [[Electricity Shooter]]s.

== In {{series|Super Mario}} ==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[Image:Fire Shooters.jpg|thumb|left|[[Mario]] near two Fire Shooters.]]
Fire Shooters are encountered in [[Bowser's Star Reactor]], [[Deep Dark Galaxy]], [[Toy Time Galaxy]], and in the [[Undergrunt Gunner Planet]]. There are two variants of this obstacle. One that emits flame streams, and the other shoots fireballs. They are often found in groups together, Fire Shooters are indestructible and cannot be destroyed in any way.
{{br}}
===''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''===
[[Image:Fire Shooter Chasm.png|thumb|right|Mario gliding by the Fire Shooters.]]
Fire Shooters reappear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', in this game, they can only be found in [[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]]. Notably, the game only features those which shoot out fireballs. They appear as an obstacle near the beginning as [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] is riding the [[Floaty Fluff]] down the lava falls. The Fire Shooters shoot their fireballs upward, if [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] is hit by one of these fireballs, the Floaty Fluff will disappear sending them plummeting down to a [[lava]] below.
{{br}}
==Trivia==
*Fire Shooters function very similarly to the [[Flamethrower]]s found in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', additionally both also have an instance where they used as makeshift fire streams, the Flamethrower being used in [[Rainbow Ride]] in the large manor's fireplace and the Fire Shooters being used inside the "mouth" of the [[Heavy Metal Mecha-Bowser]].
