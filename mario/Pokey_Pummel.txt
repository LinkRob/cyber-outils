'''Pokey Pummel''' is a recurring 4-player minigame in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Layout==
It takes place in the desert, and it features a few [[Pokey]]s.

==Opening==
The players are standing in a desert when four Pokeys rise out of the ground. [[Hammer]]s then appear in each of the playable characters' hands.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is for the characters to use their hammer to swing at the segments of a Pokey's body. By hitting a segment enough times, the character can send it flying off into the distance; in ''Mario Party 7'' and ''Mario Party Superstars'', each segment requires five hits, while in ''Mario Party: The Top 100'', each segment needs to be hit three times. The last segment to whack away is the Pokey's head.

To win, a character must be the first to whack every segment of a Pokey off into the distance. However, if none of the Pokeys are knocked away completely within five or three minutes in ''Mario Party 7'' or ''Mario Party Superstars'', respectively, the minigame ends in a [[draw]]. The shortest possible time is 1.083 seconds.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ancLxG5zpLw</ref>

In the [[Decathlon Castle]] version in ''Mario Party 7'', the player's objective is to knock out the Pokey's segments as fast as possible to earn as many points as possible, up to 1,000. It also appears in the Half Decathlon of ''Mario Party: The Top 100'' as the second minigame.

==Ending==
[[File:PokeyPummel End.png|thumb|250px|The ending to Pokey Pummel in ''Mario Party 7'']]
In ''Mario Party 7'' and ''Superstars'', as soon as one player finishes, the other Pokeys sink into the ground, and the winner celebrates; in ''Mario Party 7'', the camera zooms in on them. If a tie occurs in both games, all players will perform losing animations.

==Trivia==
*This minigame's background is reused for [[Flash Forward]] in ''Mario Party: The Top 100'' and ''Mario Party Superstars''.
*In the ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' version of the minigame, the Pokeys had a pink flower on their heads and black spines which, at the time was their standard appearance since ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', the Pokeys now have green spines and a set of spikes on their heads which had become their standard appearance since ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.
