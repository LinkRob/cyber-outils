{{Nihongo|'''Mega Monty Mole's Maze Mischief'''|巨大チョロプーの空中めいろバトル|Kyodai Choropū no kūchū meiro batoru|Giant Monty Mole's Sky Maze Battle}} is a boss minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Opening==
It starts off with [[Mega Monty Mole]] standing at the end of the maze. It then, zooms out, showing the maze (it goes fast enough where you can't see the route to the end) then it shows the characters at the start.

==Gameplay==
{{Sectstub}}
The characters have to make their way through a maze.

==Controls==
 − Navigate maze (Note: The players will automatically fire the cannons if they reach them.)

==In-game description==
''"Race through the maze and fire the cannons! Be first, and fire the big one!"''
