'''Crackpots''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Crackpots are octopus-like creatures which somewhat resemble an [[Octorok]], and they wear a pot. Crackpots are featured in maroon and purple color variations, with the former wearing an orange pot while the latter wears a gray pot.

===Behavior===
Crackpots attack by shooting [[Spike Ball|spiky ball]]s at [[Wario]]. After this, it retreats into its pot. Maroon Crackpots only spit out one spiky ball at a time, while the purple variation spits out three at a time.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
[[File:WLSI Gurgle Gulch Crackpot purple.png|thumb|left|250px|A purple Crackpot spitting a spiky ball at Wario in [[Gurgle Gulch]] in ''Wario Land: Shake It!'']]
Crackpots have a threat rating of two stars. If a Crackpot is stunned, Wario can pick it up and shake its pot, causing the Crackpot to pop out and shoot in the direction Wario is facing; this can be used to defeat other enemies.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Crackpots have the same name as "crackpot," which describes someone who has implausible beliefs and refuses to acknowledge it as such.
