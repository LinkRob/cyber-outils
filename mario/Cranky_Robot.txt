'''Cranky Robot''' is one of [[Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must twist the [[Wii Remote]] to wind up the robot. Its action depends on which way the player winds it: if the player rotates to the right, the robot its arms up and down, but if they rotate left, the robot tilts itself left and right while waving its arms.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels require one, two, and three rotations of the Wii Remote respectively.
