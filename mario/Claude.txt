'''Claude''' is a character from ''[[w:c:fireemblem:Fire Emblem: Three Houses|Fire Emblem: Three Houses]]''. He was featured in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]], as of version 7.0.0, and in a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

== In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' ==
Claude appeared as a spirit during the 7.0.0 update. [[Byleth]] has an alternate costume that represents Claude and uses his Hero Relic called [[w:c:fireemblem:Failnaught|Failnaught]].

==In ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''==
[[File:WWGIT FE Three Houses level 1.png|thumb|left|Claude's appearance in ''WarioWare: Get It Together!'']]
Claude appears in Level 1 of the [[Fire Emblem: Three Houses]] [[microgame]].
The objective is for the player's character to select the answer that compliments Claude while avoiding the ones that insult him. This is unlike Level 2 and 3, featuring the other two house leaders, [[Dimitri]] and [[Edelgard]] respectively, in which the answer with the correct spelling must be selected. If the player selects the correct answer, the phrase "PERFECT TEATIME!" is shown onscreen, but if the incorrect answer is selected, the text says that Claude's feelings were hurt.
