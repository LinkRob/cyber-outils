'''Paint Misbehavin'''' is a 2-vs.-2 and Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' and a 2-vs.-2 minigame in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. Its name is a pun on the title of the song "{{wp|Ain't Misbehavin' (song)|Ain't Misbehavin'}}."

==Opening==
The players are shown standing on a desk with art materials. A group of eleven [[Goomba]]s on a stack of books comes toward them as they hop into their paintball machines, and the game starts.

==Gameplay==
Each team or player competes to paint the eleven Goombas roaming around the square stage in their color by shooting paintballs at them. Goombas can be "repainted" if hit by a different-colored paintball. The paintball machines are restricted to opposite sides of the stage, and teammates cannot move past each other. Hitting opponents with paintballs stuns them briefly. The team or player that has more Goombas painted in their color when time runs out wins. A tie can occur only if an even number of Goombas is painted.

In ''Mario Party Superstars'', a "score" sound plays each time a Goomba is hit. Characters also cheer whenever they hit a Goomba.

==Ending==
[[File:Paint Misbehavin' duel results.png|thumb|[[Toad]] is seen shooting [[Wario]] after winning.]]
On the stage, the winning character(s) celebrate(s) as they walk back to the paintball machine(s), while the losing player(s) groan(s) before they run around to avoid being shot at by the winning player(s). If the minigame ends in a tie, all characters will do their losing animations.
