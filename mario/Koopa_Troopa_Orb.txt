The '''Koopa Troopa Orb''' is an [[Orb]] item in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Koopa Troopa Orb effect MP6.png|thumb|left|200px|Koopa Paratroopa about to switch Toadette's position with Wario]]
The Koopa Troopa Orb is the successor of the [[Paratroopa Capsule]] from ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', despite still featuring a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] instead of a [[Koopa Troopa]]. A character can only place a Koopa Troopa Orb on a [[space]] as a roadblock trap, so if an opponent passes a space with a Koopa Troopa Orb, Paratroopa comes to switch their position with whoever placed the orb.

==In ''Mario Party 6''==
A Koopa Troopa Orb costs ten [[coin]]s to purchase at the [[Orb Shop]]. If a character is under the effect of the [[Metal Mushroom Orb]], Paratroopa gives up at trying to lift them, considering them to be too heavy.
