A '''Splorch''' is mantle-like enemy in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. Splorches make their first appearance in the first [[Enemy Battles|Enemy Battle]] of [[World 7 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 7]], which is the second Brolder Blockade in the game and is unlocked alongside the second Prince Bully blockade by completing [[Boiling Blue Bully Belt]].

Like some other elements of ''Super Mario 3D World'', Splorches pay homage to ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', in this case [[Fryguy]], whom they resemble. They can be pushed back by projectiles as well as being hit. They hop out of the [[lava]] and whenever they see [[Mario]] or another character, they roll towards them leaving behind a trail of lava in their wake. Their fire trails can deal damage, so pursuing them is unadvised. While they're not much of a threat alone, they are often found in groups of 2 or 4 which can make them very dangerous. 

The only way to defeat these enemies is with an invincible-like item such as the [[Starman]] or the [[Invincibility Leaf]]. Plus ground pounding on a Splorch while using [[Lucky Cat Mario|Golden Statue Mario]] can also dispose of one. Defeating Splorches causes them to explode.

Splorches sport a frown upon spawning, but their expression changes to a nasty-looking smile in attack mode. When they get hit, they start frowning again.

==Trivia==
*Some Splorches are also seen hopping out of blue lava but despite this, they retain their usual red color unlike the [[Blue Podoboo]]s.
*''Etymology'': Splorches derive their name from the term "torch" (which as a noun refers to either a handheld stick with a flame on the end or is the British term for what Americans call a "flashlight" and as a verb means "to burn") and "sploosh" or "splash" (which are both onomatopoeias for a solid object hitting a liquid material, with "sploosh" typically denoting harder splashing).
