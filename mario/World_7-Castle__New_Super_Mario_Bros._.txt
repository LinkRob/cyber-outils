'''World 7-Castle''' is the castle level and the final level in [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]] in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' 

== Overview ==
In this castle, [[Mario]] must travel on moving [[Snake Block]]s, whilst dodging enemies and obstacles like [[Amp]]s or [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s.

== Walkthrough ==
Mario must cross through some platforms, finding a single coin in a blue [[? Block]], and ride his first Snake Block. Through this segment, you must avoid Amps, spikes and [[Thwomp]]s. You can also collect the first [[Star Coin]] here, and find another ? Block with a [[Super Mushroom]] or [[Fire Flower]].

After crossing the [[Checkpoint Flag]], you'll ride a second snake block through a segment of several Ball 'n' Chains. Upon this segment, you'll also find the second Star Coin guarded by a Ball 'n' Chain. Afterwards, you'll enter the final segment featuring [[Burner]]s and more spikes. To get the final Star Coin, you will have to make a risky jump as the Snake Block collapses, get it and land safely onto the platform or else fall into the pit below and lose a life, forced to restart from the midway-point.

=== Boss battle ===
[[File:NSMB W7-Castle.png|thumb|right|[[Lakithunder]].]]
Upon entering the boss arena, which takes place outside of the castle, climb upwards with the blue platforms, and you can head left to find an invisible ? Block with a Super Mushroom or Fire Flower. Going right will lead Mario to the boss fight against [[Lakithunder]], a [[Lakitu]] on a [[Thunder Cloud]] with the ability to shoot lightning and can throw [[Spiny Egg]]s. As par usual with any other boss, Lakithunder is defeated by 3 normal jumps, 2 [[Ground Pound]]s (3 as [[Mini Mario]]), 9 [[Fireball]]s, 3 [[Blue Shell]] hits, and 1 jump from a [[Mega Mushroom]]. Defeating him will grant Mario access to the final world of the game, [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8]].

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In the first half of the level, near the beginning; the player must jump towards it while the Snake Blocks are turning away from it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Shortly after the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]]; it is on top of a block that a Ball 'n' Chain rotates on.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At the very end of the level; as the blocks are collapsing, the player must continue standing on top of them and make a risky jump to collect the coin and make it onto the ground.
