The '''[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_Switch_2 Nintendo Switch 2]''' is a ninth-generation hybrid console and the direct successor to the [[Nintendo Switch]]. It was released on June 5, 2025, with ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' of the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series as a launch title, and is the eighth and latest international [[Nintendo]] home system.

Despite being brand new and already sold more than 3 million units on its first day, the Switch 2 has quickly sparked controversy among Nintendo fans in the United States for having a high retail price over previous consoles, with its ''base'' launch costing $449.99 (and video games as much as $80 each), making itself the most expensive ever by the multi-billiondollar company. For comparison, its predecessor has its introductory price being $299.99 and the OLED model usually $349.99. The likely reason is inflation and the ongoing tariff crisis within the country. In Japan, the price of the Nintendo Switch 2 is ¥49,980, which is around $350.

Another criticism among consumers is the system's hardware capabilities are not a dramatic increase compared to the first Switch, and it still falls short to the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_5 Sony PlayStation 5], causing some to believe this hybrid console is a repeat of the transition of the [[Wii]] to [[Wii U]].

== Features ==
The Nintendo Switch 2 supports the same three playing styles as the original Nintendo Switch: TV mode (with the console placed in the Nintendo Switch 2 Dock), tabletop mode (with the console placed on a flat surface via the kickstand and the Joy-Con 2 detached), and handheld mode (with the console held in the player's hands and the Joy-Con 2 attached). The console consists of a 7.9-inch LCD screen with a resolution of 1080p, supporting up to 120 frames per second and HDR. hen playing in TV mode, the Nintendo Switch 2 Dock allows up to 4K resolution for compatible games. The speakers have enhanced audio, allowing for 3D audio in games that utilize it. The console also features a built-in microphone, which is able to cancel out background noise and only detect the player's voice, even at a distance; and an extra USB-C port at the top, which can be used for charging in tabletop mode or to attach a USB-C camera. The kickstand now extends across almost the full width of the console; the angle at which it is reclined can be adjusted. The Nintendo Switch 2 has an internal storage capacity of 256 GB, eight times more than the 32 GB on the base and Lite models of the Nintendo Switch. While standard microSD cards can still be used for captures on the Nintendo Switch 2, software will only download to a microSD Express card.

=== GameChat ===
'''GameChat''' is an online communication feature that allows players to conduct voice and video calls with other players around the world while playing games, accessed by pressing the  button. Players speak through the Nintendo Switch 2's built-in microphone, and, using a Nintendo Switch 2 Camera, can also appear on-screen during a call session. Through GameChat, players can also share their screen to other participants.

=== GameShare ===
'''GameShare''' allows one Nintendo Switch 2 to locally and wirelessly share compatible games with other Nintendo Switch or Nintendo Switch 2 systems, in a similar manner to Download Play on the [[Nintendo DS]]. Through GameChat, one Nintendo Switch 2 can share a game with other Nintendo Switch 2 systems online. All compatible software can GameShare to a Nintendo Switch, including Nintendo Switch 2-exclusive games.

=== Language support ===
The Nintendo Switch 2 does not feature region-locking, in the same vein as its predecessor. The system can be changed to any of sixteen languages available at launch:

* Chinese (Simplified)
* Chinese (Traditional)
* Dutch
* English (United Kingdom / United States)
* French (Canada / France)
* German
* Italian
* Japanese
* Korean
* Portuguese (Brazil / Portugal)
* Russian
* Spanish (Latin America / Spain)

A separate model for Japan exists which only supports the Japanese language and Nintendo Accounts set to Japan.

The Nintendo Switch 2 is the first Nintendo console to support the Thai and Polish languages. Both languages were added in a firmware update on November 10, 2025, though no games featured support for them at the time. Moreover, the system UI still displays in American English if set to Thai, and British English if set to Polish. ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'' received a free update on December 22, 2025 adding Thai support, making it the first Nintendo-published game to support the language. Furthermore, both ''Donkey Kong Bananza'' and ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' received free updates adding Polish support on January 21, 2026.

Unlike on Nintendo Switch, the regional dialects for English, French, Portuguese and Spanish are no longer dependent on console region and can now be chosen individually regardless of whatever region the Nintendo Switch 2 is set to.

== Peripherals ==

=== Joy-Con 2 ===
[[File:Switch 2 Joy Con.jpg|thumb|255x255px|The Joy-Con 2]]
The Nintendo Switch 2's '''Joy-Con 2''' controllers, included with the console, are a direct evolution of the Nintendo Switch's [[Nintendo Switch|Joy-Con]]. They retain most of the original Joy-Con's features, excluding the IR sensor on the Joy-Con (R). Notably, they are connected directly to the sides of the console via magnetic, gendered plugs rather than vertical slide locks, and can be easily detached with a button press.

Both Joy-Con 2 feature optical sensors on their sides, allowing them to be set down and used as computer mice. The Joy-Con 2 (R) also features a new  button, which brings up the GameChat menu. The Joy-Con 2 are overall bigger than their predecessors, with both sticks and the {{Button|ns|sl}} and {{Button|ns|sr}} buttons being made larger in particular.

=== Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller ===
[[File:Switch 2 Pro Controller.jpg|left|thumb|The Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller]]
The '''Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller''' is a more traditional controller for the system, with it being similar to the Nintendo Switch Pro Controller. Various new features have been added that were not present on the Nintendo Switch Pro Controller, such as the  button to access GameChat, GL and GR buttons that can be used to assign existing button controls, and an audio jack. The Nintendo Switch logo has also been replaced by the Nintendo Switch 2 logo, the top of the controller and the inner area of the analog sticks are gray instead of black, and the casing is primarily surrounded by solid plastic instead of transparent plastic. The battery is also no longer user-replaceable.

=== Nintendo Switch 2 Camera ===
The '''Nintendo Switch 2 Camera''' is a USB-C peripheral that is able to capture only player's face and body for video calls through GameChat or games that make use of the camera such as ''Super Mario Party Jamboree – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV''. It is a 1080p wide-angle camera and also includes a privacy shutter.

An officially licensed, Piranha Plant–themed camera manufactured by HORI was also released on launch day. In the UK, it was made available on the My Nintendo Store. This camera only supports 480p resolution.

=== USB mouse ===
A USB computer mouse can be plugged into one of the USB ports of the Nintendo Switch 2, replacing the Joy-Con 2 mouse mode in specific games. Like on the Nintendo Switch, the only ''Super Mario''-related software that is compatible with a USB mouse is Super Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Classics, which includes ''Mario Paint'', ''Mario's Super Picross'', and ''Mario & Wario'', emulating the Super NES Mouse.

=== Nintendo Switch 2 Edition games ===
The Nintendo Switch 2 Edition of a Nintendo Switch game features enhanced content and/or visuals, such as frame rate improvements and new gameplay content that makes use of Nintendo Switch 2 features. For players who already own a Nintendo Switch game that has a Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, an upgrade pack that includes the new content can be purchased on the Nintendo eShop, on the My Nintendo Store, or at retailers. Save data from the original Nintendo Switch game is cross-compatible in most cases, and Nintendo Switch 2 Edition software can also be played on Nintendo Switch if the cartridge is inserted, without the enhancements.

=== Backwards-compatible games ===
All Nintendo Switch ''Super Mario'' games are backwards-compatible with the Nintendo Switch 2, with improved loading times and frame rates. ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'', ''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'', ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury'', ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'', ''Super Mario Odyssey'', ''Super Mario Party Jamboree'', and ''Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2'' require software updates to run optimally on Nintendo Switch 2, and ''WarioWare: Move It!'' requires the Nintendo Switch Joy-Con (R) to play microgames that utilize the IR Motion Camera. Applications to play classic games such as Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Classics are also available on the Nintendo Switch 2.

Additionally, the following games and applications have received free updates that take advantage of the system's new features and/or superior hardware:

* ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''

* ''[[Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''
