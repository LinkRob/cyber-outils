The '''K64''' is a train that is located between [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and the train station found at [[Mt. Rugged]]. It is seen in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. In the game, [[Mario]] has to use this train engine to transport him to Mt. Rugged, which is the only way to be able to reach [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]]. In order to get the engine going, Mario has to get rid of a rock blocking the railroad with [[Bombette]]. After this, Mario is able to ride the engine whenever.

==History==
The K64 comes from ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. In [[Kalimari Desert]], many trains looking similar to this one are found driving around the railway tracks on this course. Players must avoid hitting them when they pass by (sometimes even having to wait for it to pass by before going again) or else the player will flip out and slow down.

== Trivia==
*'''K64''' is a reference to [[Kalimari Desert]] and [[Nintendo 64]].
*The K64 appears to be an 0-6-2 tank engine or an American type steam locomotive.
