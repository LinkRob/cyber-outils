'''Reign Storm''' is the first segment of the fourth episode of ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and the seventh episode overall. The episode's name comes from reign and rainstorm.

==Plot==
When [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] take a vacation in Hawaii, [[Kooky von Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Kooky]] uses a robot princess to try to trick the [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Bros.]] and take over the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

==Trivia==
*This episode featured the song "Girls Just Want to Have Fun" (by Cyndi Lauper).
*This was the first episode to have Princess Toadstool and Toad wearing swimsuits, the second episode was [[7 Continents for 7 Koopas]].
*This was the first time Princess Toadstool ever wore a swimsuit as well as having bare feet. The second time was in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'', the third time was in the third time was in [[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games|''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games'']], and the fourth time was in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''.
