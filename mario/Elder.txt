The '''Elder''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
The Elder is an elderly [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with dark green spots on his head. [[Yaridovich]]'s disguise looks similar to the Elder, except his body is gray and his vest is dark red.

==History==
The Elder is the elder of [[Seaside Town]], with many looking up to him. During the events of the game, the Elder and the other citizens of Seaside Town were taken hostage by [[Yaridovich]], a member of the [[Smithy Gang]]. After [[Mario]] defeats [[Jonathan Jones]] and gets a [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]], Yaridovich reveals himself to Mario and demands the Star Piece, threatening to otherwise hurt the hostages. After Yaridovich is defeated, the Elder and the other Seaside Town citizens are freed and the Elder offers his thanks to Mario.
