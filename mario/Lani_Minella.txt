'''Lani Jean Minella''' (born July 28, 1950) is an American voice actress and voice director. She provide the voice for [[Larry Koopa]], [[Morton Koopa Jr.]], [[Wendy O. Koopa]] and [[Lemmy Koopa]] in the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]] series and the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros]]. series. She was replaced by [[Michelle Hippe]] ([[Larry Koopa|Larry]]) [[David Cooke]] ([[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton]]) [[Ashley Flannegan]] ([[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]]) and [[Carlee McManus]] ([[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]]) in [[Mario Kart 8]].
