'''Arrow Lifts''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
[[File:SM64 WDW Arrowlift.png|thumb|left|200px|Arrow Lifts, as they appear in the original ''Super Mario 64'']]
Arrow Lifts are gray [[block]]s with two joined blue arrows that point in opposite ends, either horizontally or vertically. If [[Mario]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in ''Super Mario 64 DS'') steps on an Arrow Lift, it moves back and forth in a set path indicated by its two facing arrows. Upon reaching the end of its path, an Arrow Lift stops moving for two seconds before returning to its original position. After another two seconds, the Arrow Lift traveling on its set path again, repeating the process.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Super Mario 64'' / ''Super Mario 64 DS''==
Arrow Lifts exclusively appear in [[Wet-Dry World]], where there are five in total. Arrow Lifts are the main feature of the course's first [[mission]], [[Shocking Arrow Lifts!]], where a series of Arrow Lifts have to be used to reach a [[! Block]] containing the mission's [[Power Star]].
