An '''Elite Boom Guy''' is a stronger variant of [[Boom Guy]] featured in the [[Gritzy Caves]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. They can be distinguished from their blue clothing, whereas Boom Guys wear red clothing. Like their parent species, Elite Boom Guys carry a [[Bill Blaster]] on their head, and they can shoot [[Bullet Bill]]s from it.

It is unknown whether they are wild or serve the [[Shroob]]s. If one loses its launcher a pair of blue [[Fly Guy]]s will come and drop a new one on its head; this has a higher likelihood of success than for a regular Boom Guy.
