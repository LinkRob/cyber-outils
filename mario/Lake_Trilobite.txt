'''Lake Trilobite''' is a body of water found on [[Dinosaur Land]]. In ''[[Dinosaur Dilemma]]'', [[Mario]] can visit it if he decides to look for [[Luigi]] and the missing [[dinosaur]]s there, and he ends up here if he goes searching for [[basket of apples|apples]]. If he goes to the lake while searching for the dinosaurs, he sees a group of them on the other side of the lake alongside the also-missing [[Yoshi]]. Mario decides to make his way to the other side, which can be done by either directly swimming there or riding a flimsy raft. By solving a puzzle that involves arranging a group of letters into the names of dinosaurs, the hint of "NO BOAT" is given, recommending that Mario swim across. If he still uses the raft, it quickly breaks under his weight, and he passes out in the lake, only to later wake up in the basement of a nearby mansion. If he swims across, he dives in and proceeds to collect a group of [[coin]]s on the lakebed (the exact number is determined by another puzzle). After Mario swims across the lake, or after he collects the apples, he ends up next to the group of dinosaurs, who are noticeably hungry. If he has the basket of apples, the dinosaurs reveal themselves to be [[Mega Mole]]s in disguise and immediately eat him; if he does not have any apples, he wanders around with the other dinosaurs (who are actually dinosaurs and not Mega Moles in disguise) away from the lake in search of food.
