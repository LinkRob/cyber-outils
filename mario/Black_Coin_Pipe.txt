'''Black Coin Pipe''' is a Special building that appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
The '''Black Coin Pipe''' is a black [[Pipe]] with a [[Star]] engraved on the pipe.

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' the Black Coin Pipe is a Special building with no rarity. The player can buy it at the Shop after collecting all the [[Black Coin]]s in all levels excluding levels in [[World Star (Super Mario Run)|World Star]]. If the player touches the Black Coin Pipe, the game will ask the player if they want to play the special course, but colored coins will not be counted on the records.
