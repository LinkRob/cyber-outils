'''Disco''' is [[Mike]]'s minigame in ''[[Game & Wario]]'', for the [[Wii U]]. It is one of the four minigames (alongside [[Islands]], [[Fruit (minigame)|Fruit]], and [[Sketch]]) that is exclusively multiplayer.

==Trivia==
*The character models for the two players resemble Elvis Presley and Michael Jackson.
