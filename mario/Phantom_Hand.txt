'''Phantom Hand''' is a floating, disembodied glove that point to secrets in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

== History ==

=== ''New Super Mario Bros.'' ===
They only appear in the {{world-link|7|ghostn|World 7-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros.)}}, and their only purpose is to point out the location of an invisible block containing a [[Question Switch]] moving back and forth to the player. They are not enemies, meaning [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are not injured if they touch one. Upon the block's discovery, the Phantom Hands will remain idle pointing at the [[Used Block]].

In the game's files, it is shown that there are invisible variations of other enemies and objects that went unused such as [[Podoboo]]s, [[Bowser Jr.]], and various platforms and the Phantom Hands were intended to point to the locations of these as well.
