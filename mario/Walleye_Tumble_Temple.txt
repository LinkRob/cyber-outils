==Super Gems==
*'''Super Gem 1''': After falling down, either throw a turnip at the first Walleye when it blocks the doorway and walk pass it, or walk through the doorway and go on the rotating platform to the Super Gem.
*'''Super Gem 2''': After crossing the other side of the stage, go through the other side of the hidden tunnel to reveal a hidden dead end. Pluck the stem three times to reveal the Super Gem.
*'''Super Gem 3''': After reaching the top of the other side of the stage, pass the first Walleye and turn left.
==Optional Mission==
*Don't break the unstable bridge leading towards the third Super Gem.{{CTTT Nav}}
