'''''Sonic the Hedgehog''''' is a video game franchise from [[Sega]]. In the early 1990s, ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' was intended to compete with the {{franchise|Mario}}, but years later, after Sega became a third-party, a dedicated crossover series starring ''Mario'' and ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' character was created, {{series|Mario & Sonic}}. Aside from this, since ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' has been represented within the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.
