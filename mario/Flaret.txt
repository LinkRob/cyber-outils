'''Flarets''' are round fireballs that used by [[Bowletta]] during her battle at [[Bowser's Castle]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its remake ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. As such, they only appear in [[Bowser's Castle]].

== Description ==
Originally, Flarets were yellow-ish orange circles, and they had a sexagonal tesselation akin to a honeycomb. In the 3DS remake, Flarets have their design changed to resemble a soccer ball with more defined graphics.

==Battle==
Bowletta shoots out Flarets to attack. After a few turns, she absorbs a Flaret to restore 35 [[Heart Point|HP]], or 99 HP in the remake. When she performs a powerful fire breath attack, she destroys any and all Flarets that are still on the battlefield. A Flaret by itself has 10 hit points, an attack strength of 95, a defense strength of 160, and an initiative speed of 300, and drops no rewards when destroyed.

===Strategy===
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must prepare to jump over Flarets, although some of them might jump over the Bros, who must duck under them instead (no inputs required). Flarets can be healed by Mario and Luigi's [[Firebrand|Fire]] and [[Thunderhand|Thunder]] attacks. The [[Splash Bros.]] [[Bros. Attack|attack]] is the only jump attack that Mario or Luigi can use without taking damage. The only actual way for them to attack a Flaret is by hitting it with their [[Hammer]].

==Names in other languages==
In Japanese, Flarets are referred to as ''meramera'', which means "flaring up" and is written as メラメラ in Katakana. Their Chinese name, Chìrè Huǒqiú, literally means "burning hot fireball". In German, they are called ''flamy'', which may come from "flame". Their Spanish name, ''Brisatún'', comes from "brasa" (ember), and the Italian versions of the game call them ''Fjammello'', which is a genderswap of ''Fjammella'' (little flame).
