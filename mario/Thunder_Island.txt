'''Thunder Island''' is a stadium in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Thunder Island is a dangerous arena with stormy weather and surrounding sides, so if the player's character falls off, they have to wait temporarily before their character respawns back onto the field. The [[Wind]] expelled from the tornado is hazardous, as it slows characters and blows them closer toward the edge. Additionally, the wind can blow various objects, including cows, tractors, and even [[Mega Cheep Cheep]]s onto the field. If a character is hit, they fall to ground and become stunned for a few seconds.
