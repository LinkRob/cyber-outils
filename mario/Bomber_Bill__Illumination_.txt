[[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]'s '''Bomber Bill''' is a weapon from ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' from 2023. It is a massive [[Bomber Bill]] that was summoned when King Bowser flips out over his wedding being crashed.

==Description==
Bowser's Bomber Bill looks typical, but is actually much larger than their parent species, the [[Banzai Bill]], their size being more comparable to the [[King Bill]]. Additionally, they can hover in place to make sharp turns akin to a [[Missile Bill]], something a regular Bomber Bill cannot, as seen when the Bomber Bill that got launched stopped just before it smashed [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]]'s [[Princess Peach's Castle (Illumination)|castle]] after getting its eye whipped by Tanooki Mario’s tail.

==History==
After [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]] rejects his wedding proposal, freezing his body in ice as she does and blowing up [[King Bob-omb (Illumination)|King Bob-omb]] with a burning spear, [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] flies into a rage. [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] arrives on the scene and rescues his brother [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]]. The three and [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] celebrate their victory, but Bowser escapes from his icy prison, declaring that the Bomber Bill must be launched. The massive warhead is then shot from a massive volcano-esque cannon on the back of the ship. Due to the cannon being aimed straight up, it has to curve downwards to strike the castle. Mario, getting an effective time boost from this, tries to get its attention, succeeding by using his tail as a whip on the weapon's eye. This causes it to fly in Mario's direction, but the red-clad plumber uses this to his advantage, luring the missile into the [[Mysterious Pipe]] (the same one Mario came out of when he first arrived) and saving the castle from its explosion. However, it causes the warp zone to collapse as it explodes, which results in the castle and everyone on it being sucked into [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]], where the final battle occurs.

==Trivia==
*The Bomber Bill seems to be somewhat sentient, as if it was not sentient, it would have smashed Princess Peach's castle whether it got smacked across the eye or not.

==Behind the Scenes==
===Name===
Bomber Bills take their name from the convention of [[Bullet Bill]] subspecies having a common name at the end of the word with an alliterative word describing a weapon at the start.
