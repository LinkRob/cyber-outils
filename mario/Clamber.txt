'''Clamber''' is a move in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Clamber is only usable by [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], [[Diddy Kong]], [[Dixie Kong]], and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in ''Mario Superstar Baseball''. In ''Mario Super Sluggers'', every Kong, with the addition of [[Funky Kong]], can use the move, although it is no longer possible for Yoshi to.

Using Clamber allows the user to climb up a wall, especially to catch hard-to-reach balls that are about to become home runs. If the baseball is caught, the character does a big play.

==In ''Mario Super Sluggers''==
There is a scout mission revolving around the use of Clamber. It takes place in [[DK Jungle (baseball stadium)|DK Jungle]], and the player's character has to catch a home-run ball a few times.
