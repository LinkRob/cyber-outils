'''Lakitu Valley''' is a golf course in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''.

==Description==
Lakitu Valley is a basic golf course featuring no ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-based gimmicks, and its only obstacles are trees and water, much like real-world golf courses. Despite Lakitu Valley's name, no [[Lakitu]]s are shown to inhabit here.
