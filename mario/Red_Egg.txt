'''Red Eggs''' are a type of [[Yoshi Egg]] featured as an item in the [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi'' franchise]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==In the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]==
Red Eggs are among the eggs that can be carried around by a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. Throwing a Red Egg at an enemy causes two [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]] to be released. There are red-spotted [[Egg Block]]s that turn into a Red Egg when jumped at, like yellow ones that turn into a [[Yellow Egg]] upon being jumped at. Furthermore, if a regular Yoshi Egg hits a surface twice, it becomes a Red Egg, but if it bounces too much, the Red Egg falls offscreen.

==In ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''==
Like in the ''Yoshi's Island'' series, a Red Egg is created if a regular Yoshi Egg bounces off a surface twice. Throwing a Red Egg at an enemy automatically earns the playable Yoshi three [[heart]]s.
