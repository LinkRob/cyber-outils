'''Tap Dash''', titled '''Find It First''' in PAL releases, is a General [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Opening==
A ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' species selected by the game at random slowly rotates around on the top screen. A hand icon is shown on the Touch Screen, it moves to the panel matching the species, and taps it.

==Gameplay==
One of the ''Mario'' species is displayed on the top screen, and each player must quickly tap the one specific panel out of sixteen that depicts the species. Whoever taps the correct icon first earns nine points, followed by six points for second, three for third, and one point for fourth. There are three rounds, each lasting five seconds, and the next one begins after every player taps an icon or when the five-second time limit for that round runs out. If a character either touches an incorrect panel or does not touch any panel within the time limit, they receive no points for that round. Whoever has the most points after the third round concludes is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|3ds|Stylus}}: Touch a panel
