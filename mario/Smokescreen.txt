The '''Smokescreen''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in both ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
If a character uses Smokescreen, they throw it backward as the item begins to produce smoke around the lane of the course. If a character gets in this smoke, their view is blocked temporarily.
