|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Spritz Bomb''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by certain enemies and bosses in the game.

==Effects==
When Spritz Bomb is used, a bunch of small bombs rains down on the target. This move deals twice the normal damage making it a rather strong move. This move is one of [[Axem Black]]'s signature moves.
