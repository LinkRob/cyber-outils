'''DK Country''' is one of [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. Despite its name, the microgame is based on ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'', not ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==Layout==
[[Diddy Kong]] is inside a moving [[Bonus Barrel]], under a red [[Zinger]] and six [[banana]]s, three on the left and right sides each. A sky background and sea can be seen in the background, just as in ship-themed levels of ''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''.

==Gameplay==
The player must tap the Bonus Barrel, which unusually functions like a [[Barrel Cannon]], to shoot Diddy up to collect the bananas. The player loses if either the bananas are not collected in time or if Diddy touches the Zinger.
