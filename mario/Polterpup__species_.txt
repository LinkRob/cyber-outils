'''Polterpups''' are a species of [[ghost]] dogs which first appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They have a character representative of the [[Polterpup|same name]], who has appeared more than the other Polterpups.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Polterpups have a white, slightly translucent body with a glow around it. A Polterpup's eyes are white and emit a bright radiance. Polterpups have a rounded head, and unlike real-world dogs, they do not have ears. Each Polterpup wears a collar which has an identification tag hanging from it, and the ones in the [[ScareScraper]] each wear a blue collar whereas the Polterpup character wears a red collar.
