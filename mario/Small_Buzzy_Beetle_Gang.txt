A '''Small Buzzy Beetle Gang''' is a variation of [[Buzzy Beetle Gang]] instead consisting of several [[Small Buzzy Beetle]]s. They only appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Small Buzzy Beetle Gangs attack [[Mario]] by ramming him using their [[Buzzy Shell|shell]], dealing 1 [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage each. While this does very little damage, the large number of Small Buzzy Beetles within a Gang can deal lots of damage to Mario.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
Only one Small Buzzy Beetle Gangs is encountered in the [[Sacred Forest]], specifically in a small cave, where they guard the fifth [[Paint Star Piece]]. In battle, [[jump]] and [[hammer]] attacks do very little damage to Small Buzzy Beetle Gangs, and they are resistant to [[Fire Flower]]s. However, Small Buzzy Beetle Gangs are weak to [[Ice Flower]]s, [[POW Block]]s and [[Things]].
