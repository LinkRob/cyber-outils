{{Nihongo|'''Brigadier Mollosque-Lanceur III'''|ホーダン伯爵|Hōdan Hakushaku|, lit. Drainage Cannonball}}''', Dauphin of Bubblaine'''|(alternatively spelled '''Mollusque-Lanceur'''; French for "'''Mollusk Launcher'''") is a giant, well-dressed, purple octopus who appears as a boss in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. He initially appears atop the large glass chalice in the [[Bubblaine]], sucking the water from it with a bendy straw, as a [[Bubblainian]] explains when [[Mario]] makes his way to the kingdom. This has caused fewer tourists to come to Bubblaine. In terms of his appearance, Mollusque Lanceur and a common enemy called an [[Astro-Lanceur]] seem related.

Mollusque Lanceur serves as the primary boss of [[Seaside Kingdom]] and the game’s fourth major boss, overall, he is either the overall tenth or eleventh boss of the game depending on what order Kingdom the Player(s) go, he later on appears in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] as one of the six rematch harder versions of their fights. 

== Profile ==
=== Personality traits ===
Mollusque-Lanceur seems to be arrogant, as he seems determined to keep the Sparkle Water. His pompousness leads him to get angry from [[Mario]] and [[Cappy]] trying to knock the boss off his pedestal.

== In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' ==
Mollusque-Lanceur is featured as the boss of [[Seaside Kingdom]].

=== Battle ===
Mollusque Lanceur is unique due to his ability to interact with [[Mario]] before his battle (not counting when the [[Cookatiel]] blows Mario off the Stupendous Stew bowl in the [[Luncheon Kingdom]]. In order to stop Mollusque-Lanceur, Mario must hit several glass case-protected Ground-Pound Switches scattered throughout the kingdom. This will cause a glob of lava to be shot onto his head. Mollosque-Lanceur will attempt to stop him by launching shell-based Floating Mines that explode after a short period into the water near Mario. Once Mollosque-Lanceur has taken enough damage, he will leave the tower to confront Mario directly, now with a large puddle of lava on his head, and without his hat.

In battle, Mollosque-Lanceur swims around, occasionally spitting a Floating Mine or drill-like cone shell out of his mouth. To defeat him, Mario must capture a Gushen with his hat and use its water to clear the lava off of the boss's head, exposing it. From there, Mario must keep pounding Mollusque-Lanceur's head with the water (Mollusque-Lanceur will not heal when his head is sprayed, however.), until eventually, the boss is defeated. But sometimes, he will spin to escape Mario's attacks. Mollusque-Lanceur is invulnerable in this state. Starting from his second phase, Mollusque-Lanceur can be hit with a ground pound, which does much more damage. Upon defeat, Mollusque-Lanceur will be shown gripping his head in pain. Mario, in his normal state, will appear on top of Mollosque-Lanceur's head, which rapidly begins to inflate and glow, before violently exploding, sending Mario and the Multi Moon flying over the chalice, where they are suspended by its water.

In the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Super Mario Odyssey)|Mushroom Kingdom]] rematch, Mollusque-Lanceur is fought in the sky. His battle is the same, other than the fact that there is no solid ground (Which makes ground pounding Mollusque-Lanceur risky, if not fatal to the player) and due to the rain, the Gushens have their water supply refilled at all times.

==Behind the Scenes==
===Etymology===
"Mollusque-Lanceur" means "mollusk launcher" in French.

==Nomenclature==
In Japanese, Chinese, and Korean, Mollusque-Lanceur is called "Count Cannonball" (Japanese: (''Hōdan Hakushaku'') written 砲弾伯爵; Chinese: ''Pàodàn Bójué'' (written 炮彈伯爵); Korean: ''Potan Baekjak'' (written 포탄백작)). In Spanish, he is called "Brigadier Pulpierre III," with Pulpierre coming from ''pulpo'' ("octopus") and the French name ''pierre'' ("Peter"). His name in Italian is "Capitaine Pulpidù," with ''capitaine'' actually being French (The actual Italian translation of "captain" is ''capitano''.), and Polpidù coming from the French surname ''Pompidou'' mixed with Italian ''polpo'' ("octopus").French calls him "Bombopode, Duc de Pétillance," with ''Duc de Pétillance'' meaning "Duke of Bubblaine" and "Bombopode" coming from a meger of ''bombe'' ("bomb," "explosive") and ''céphalopode'' ("cephalopod"). In German, the character is called "Kunibert von Krak," meaning "Kumibert von Octo."

==Trivia==
* Mollusque-Lanceur is the only boss whose battle theme can be heard in 8-bit mode.
* Mollusque-Lanceur is one of six bosses that can only be beaten if the player capture their own minions. Mollusque-Lanceur himself can only be beaten if the player capture a Gushen to spray his head to remove the lava. Players have to hit [[Knucklotec]]'s face (his weak point) with his own fist to deal damage to him. [[Torkdrift]] floats high enough so the player need to capture an [[Uproot]] to smash his glass core, and jumping at him will not do anything. The [[Mechawiggler]] is normally invulnerable, but he can be beaten if the player capture a [[Sherm]] to shoot at his glass cores. The [[Cookatiel]] can only be beaten by capturing a [[Podoboo]] and stomping on his head. The [[RoboBrood]] can only be damaged if the player capture a [[Pokio]] to pierce the robot's leg armour, and [[Madame Broode]] can be defeated if the player capture her [[Chain Chomp]] and smack her face with his body.
* The quote that Mollusque-Lanceur says upon getting hit by the first cork resembles "Ah, oui, oui," a nod to his French heritage.
* Mollusque-Lanceur is the only boss whose arena spans the entire kingdom. This does not apply to his rematch, however. He thus has the largest boss arena.
