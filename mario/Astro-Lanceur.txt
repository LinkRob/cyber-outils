'''Astro-Lanceurs''' are alien enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

==Description==
Resembling Dauphin [[Mollusque-Lanceur]] of Bubblaine, these cephalopod monsters are grey octopuses with glowing blue eyes and a funnel mouth and the [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] symbol on top of their head. They wear a space helmet that has a nozzle out the front so they can shoot seashell missiles.

==In ''Super Mario Odyssey''==
Found only in the [[Moon Kingdom]]'s Honeylune Ridge area, Astro-Lanceurs attack by throwing a seashell out of their mouth, like [[Mollusque-Lanceur]] does in his boss battle. Mario can destroy the creatures with a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] on their head. This causes their space helmet to shatter as well as forcing the Astro-Lanceur to the floor. Mario has his jump height increased, allowing him to stomp on the Astro-Lanceur with ease. After the Astro-Lanceur's defeat, their body explodes into eight [[Coin|coins]] that land in a ring centered at the location of their demise.
