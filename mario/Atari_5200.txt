The '''Atari 5200''' was a game system released by Atari in October 1982 and discontinued on May 21, 1984. Due to the Atari 5200's lifespan, only one ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game had ever been released for this system: ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]'' A separately-sold adapter allowed the system to play games that were made for the [[Atari 2600]].
