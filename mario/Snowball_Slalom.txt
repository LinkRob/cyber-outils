'''Snowball Slalom''' is one of [[Wario]]'s [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and an Action minigame in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. In the former game, the minigame has a harder variation titled [[Giant Snowball Slalom]]. Snowball Slalom plays the background music theme of [[Cool, Cool Mountain]] and [[Snowman's Land]].

== Gameplay ==
Using the [[stylus]], the player has to guide the [[Headless Snowman]] down a mostly straightforward icy path to the [[finish line]] as fast as possible within 20 seconds. As the Headless Snowman rolls, he increases in size. Rocks and [[Snowman|Snowmen]] are obstacles along the way, and hitting them shrinks the Headless Snowman and slows his movement. The Headless Snowman can also be slowed if the player taps him with the stylus.

In ''New Super Mario Bros.'', Snowball Slalom is featured as both a single-player and multiplayer minigame, the latter of which supports two to four players. The single-player mode plays mostly the same way, but adds [[penguin]]s as obstacles, like in Giant Snowball Slalom. In multiplayer, Snowball Slalom instead involves each player racing their own Headless Snowman, and whoever reaches the end first wins; alternatively, if no one makes it to the end within 60 seconds, the player who traveled the farthest wins.

== In-game instructions ==
* '''''Super Mario 64 DS'':''' ''Rub the Touch Screen to roll the snowman's head to the goal before time runs out! Rocks will slow you down, as will touching his head!''
* '''''New Super Mario Bros.''''' 
** '''Single-player:''' ''Rub the Touch Screen to roll the snowman's head to the goal before time runs out! Rocks will slow you down, so avoid them!''
** '''Multiplayer:''' ''Rub the Touch Screen to roll the snowman's head to the goal! The one who gets there first is the winner.''
