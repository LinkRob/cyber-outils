'''Metal Detectors''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera zooms closer to the players that are holding shovels.

== Gameplay ==
In this minigame, players can get coins. The player can find coins via the Joy-Con's rumble with the coin amount being random. Pokeys appear as hazards, the player loses three points if he/she comes in contact.

== Ending ==
Players face towards the screen, the players with coins do their victory animation and the players sulk when they with no coins.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – Dig

==In-game description==
*''"Dig up the most coins!"''
*''"When the rumble at your feet is the strongest, dig!"''
