'''Uncle Kong''' is a [[Kong Family|Kong]] who appears in the Japanese Strategy Guide for the game [[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest|Super Donkey Kong 2: Dixie & Diddy]]. Uncle Kong gave the player tips on how to be the game. This Kong has never appeared again. He is not a member of the [[Kong Family]], and he sells [[Banana|bananas]] in Osaka.

== Trivia ==

* His nickname "UK" might be a pun as his creator, [[Rare|Rareware]], is a company from the United Kingdom, which also goes by the nickname "UK".
* Although he’s specified to be an uncle, it never says who he is the uncle of.
