'''Johnson''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is an [[X-Naut]] that serves under [[Grodus]].

==History==
Johnson plays a very minor role in the game as he was addressed by [[Lord Crump]] at the beginning of the game where [[Mario]] and [[Goombella]] went. Johnson, however, didn't know where they went much to the distain of Lord Crump.
