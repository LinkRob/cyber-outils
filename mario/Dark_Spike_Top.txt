}}A '''Dark Spike Top''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Spike Tops are stronger and darker versions of [[Spike Top]]s that are found in rooms of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. They have rather high stats making it a rather troublesome enemy to deal with. The best way is to flip them over with an explosion from [[Boomer]] and then attack their defenseless bellies.
