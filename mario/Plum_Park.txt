'''Plum Park''' is a [[level]] of the purple region in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. The Balloons [[Things|Thing]] can be found here. 

== Layout ==

=== Starting Area ===
A Dark Bloo Inn staff member can be seen here, having failed to see the teapot needed for the ghost Toads at the [[Dark Bloo Inn]], so Huey encourages [[Mario (character)|Mario]] to continue looking.

=== Main Area ===
Much of the water has been contaminated by poison, and Mario must avoid falling in; if he falls in, Mario will take 2 poison damage.

=== Side Path ===
Mario can use his [[Hammer]] in the starting area to access this path, which leads to the bucket area.

=== Bucket Area ===
This area is situated to the south of the hedge maze, and has buckets that can be used as boats to ride over the poison.

=== Hedge Maze ===
This area lies to the north. The right exit leads to the bucket area.

=== Concert Stage ===
Toad #1 of the Yellow [[Rescue Squad]] can be found here, instructing Mario to find the teapot prior to leaving to see his captain in [[Sacred Forest]]. When Mario enters the area, [[Birdo (character)|Birdo]] performs on stage with some [[Koopa Troopa]] dancers. The Balloons [[Thing]] appears here for him to collect.

=== Teapot Area ===
This area is behind the concert stage. Mario must knock the wall down with a hammer strike. The Teapot Thing appears here, but when Mario tries to grab it, [[Petea Piranha]] appears, offended to find his sweet time being interrupted by Mario, and attacks him in retaliation.
