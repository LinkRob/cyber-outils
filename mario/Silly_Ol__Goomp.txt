'''Silly Ol' Goomp''' is the sixth level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 20.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 8 [[Goomba]]s. The enemy captain is a Goomba, and he knows [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 8 [[Pestnut]]s. The enemy captain is a Pestnut, and it knows [[Chance]] and Deny.

The final battle contains 4 [[Gritty Goomba]]s, 3 [[Paragoomba]]s, and [[Private Goomp]]. The enemy captain is Private Goomp, and he knows [[Clash of Captains]].

After the final battle, the player "recruits" Private Goomp (Lv. 22).

== Possible recruits ==
* Goomba (Lv. 21+)
* Paragoomba (Lv. 21+)
