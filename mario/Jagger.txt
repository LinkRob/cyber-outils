'''Jagger''' is a friendly [[Terrapin]] character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He has the same appearance as Terrapins in general. He is [[Jinx]]'s martial arts student. At one point, [[Mario]] and his party can engage in a friendly battle with Jagger at [[Monstro Town]].

==Etymology==
Jagger is likely named after the rock musician {{w|Mick Jagger}}.
