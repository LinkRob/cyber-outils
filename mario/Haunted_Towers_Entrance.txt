'''Haunted Towers Entrance''' is an area/room in [[Haunted Towers]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
It is the first area explored in Haunted Towers and heading through the gate immediately takes [[Luigi]] into the [[Courtyard]].

During B-1: [[A Job for a Plumber]], Luigi is first sent to the Haunted Towers Entrance to start his short journey to the Hydro Generator. Luigi can use his [[Strobulb]] on the plants outside for money and head through the gate to the Courtyard.

When Luigi first arrives in this area during B-3: [[Graveyard Shift]], two [[Venus Flytrap]]s suddenly appear and attempt to eat Luigi. Luigi must water a Spike Ball plant and defeat them before processing any further.

Also, during B-4: [[Pool Party]], if Luigi checks out the nearby tree, a [[Gold Greenie]] will appear and Luigi can capture it for lots of money.
