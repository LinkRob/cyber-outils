'''Juice''' is a type of liquid that can be spit out by [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It has the ability to wash away [[Orange Juice Generator]]s as well as transform certain enemies into [[Yoshi Platform]]s. Yoshi has only a limited supply of juice, shown on the bottom-right corner on the HUD, and if it runs out, Yoshi returns into becoming a [[Yoshi Egg]]. The juice is the same color as Yoshi while he is spraying it.
