A '''Torch''' is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
Torches are a lamp-like switch that can only be activated using [[Mario]]'s [[Firebrand]]. They have a counterpart named [[conductor]]s, which similarly can only be activated using [[Luigi]]'s [[Thunderhand]].
