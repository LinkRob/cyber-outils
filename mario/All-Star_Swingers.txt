{{Nihongo|'''All-Star Swingers'''|リズムでバッティング|Rizumu de battingu|Batting with Rhythm}} is a [[Rhythm Minigame|Rhythm]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. If someone hits too hard, the game will warn the player, but the message will annoy the players. CPU had a benefit on this, because they are automatic, they don't "see" the message because they are "AI". There are three kinds of balls: white, pink, and zig-zag.

== Opening ==
The players pose with their bat with two hands or one. The metronome ticks and the red curved arrow moves.

== Gameplay ==
[[File:SMP All-Star Swingers.gif|thumb|right|Gameplay of All-Star Swingers.]]
The objective of the minigame is to hit the ball at the rhythm of the song. If the player hit the ball at the right time, the player earns three points and the ball. If the player hits the ball too early or late, the player earns a point. If the player misses the ball, the player does not earn points.

== Ending ==
The winner(s) do(es) their victory pose while other players sulk.
