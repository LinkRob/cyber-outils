'''Paper Luigi''' is a paper incarnation version of [[Luigi]] based on his appearance in the [[Paper Mario|''Paper Mario'' series]] that came out of a [[Mysterious Book|magical book]]. He appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'' ===
Paper Luigi is an independent character from Luigi and appears only after the player unlocks the Music Player after beating [[Shiny RoboBowser]], though he is mentioned by [[Starlow]] at the beginning of the game when [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] first meets [[Mario]] and Luigi, and confuses Luigi with Paper Luigi. A [[Paper Toad]] in [[Princess Peach's Castle|Peach’s Castle]] also mentions him after being rescued. In the [[Music Player]] menu, he appears to sleep in [[Twinsy Tropics]] while the trio are on their adventure.

Though Paper Luigi does not have a role in the story, what happens to him is unknown. It is likely he returns back to the paper world off screen.

Playing the Music Player, Paper Luigi has different reactions. The first song plays relating to [[Princess Peach]], and hearts begin to appear as he listens to the song. When the second song plays relating to [[Bowser]], Paper Luigi is shown to sweat in fear.
