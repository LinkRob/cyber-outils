'''Pretty Lucky P''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is the partner-based variant of [[Pretty Lucky]].

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear this badge by using two points of his [[Badge Point|BP]]. While worn, Pretty Lucky P gives Mario's partners the ability to be "lucky" in combat, allowing enemies to unexpectedly miss their attacks. This badge is an upgraded form of the [[Close Call P]] badge, the partner variant of [[Close Call]].

Mario can buy Pretty Lucky P at the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] for 150 [[coin]]s.
