'''Bubble Dayzees''' are a variant of [[Crazee Dayzee]] which appears exclusively within the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. It made its debut appearance in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They can be distinguished from Crazee Dayzees from their light purple petals, although the main difference with Bubble Dayzees is that they spit out [[bubble]]s, hence their name.
