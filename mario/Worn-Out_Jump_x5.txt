|name=Worn-Out Jump x5 (Battle Card)}}'''Worn-Out Jump x5''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Worn-Out Jump x5 is the weakest version of [[Mario]]'s "Jump x5" attack. It is five of the [[Worn-Out Jump]]'s scattered on the card. There is an orange/white striped background.
