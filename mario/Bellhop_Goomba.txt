'''Bellhop Goombas''' are enemies which only appear in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''. Here, they are encountered in [[Wendy's Blitz Snarlton Hotel]], where they work for [[Wendy O. Koopa]]. Bellhop Goombas can only go back and forth, acting the same as [[Goomba]]s, with the only difference being that they are wearing hotel worker outfits.
