==Features==
This level is a far shorter remix of [[Shadow-Play Alley]]. It features new enemies such as a [[Goomba Tower]] and [[Boomerang Bro|Boomerang Bros]].

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Above the door leading into the alley. The player needs to grab the [[Boomerang Flower]] on top to get this Green Star.
*'''Green Star 2''': Right to the left of the [[Goomba Tower]] required to open the Warp Box.
*'''Green Star 3''': On the ledge right of the aforementioned Goomba Tower.
