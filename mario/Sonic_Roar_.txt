'''Sonic Roar!''' is the [[Super Ability]] used by [[Bowser Jr.]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Upon using his move, Bowser Jr. lets out a very loud roar loudly, sending a sonic wave in front of him. The sonic wave shrinks whoever it hits temporarily, regardless whether they are on Bowser Jr.'s team or not. If a character has been shrunk, they move much slower and cannot kick the ball due to their small size.

Bowser Jr.'s [[Mega Strike]] similarly involves a loud burst of sound.
