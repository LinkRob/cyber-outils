'''Fortress Fortress''' is a [[level]] of the [[Forest of Illusion]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. It is a secret [[Fortress]] level that can be accessed by completing [[Forest Secret Area]], and completing Forest Fortress unlocks [[Star World 4]].
