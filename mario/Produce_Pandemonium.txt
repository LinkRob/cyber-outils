'''Produce Pandemonium''' is one of [[Wario]]'s [[microgame]]s category under the [[Anything Goes]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
As [[Wario]] is driving his [[Wario Car|car]], he has to dodge the fruit that drops from the sky. If he gets hit by a fruit, the player loses the microgame. In all difficulty levels, Wario has to dodge at least one fruit. In Level 1, there is one more fruit to dodge, and Levels 2 and 3 each add another fruit to dodge than the preceding difficulty level, respectively.
