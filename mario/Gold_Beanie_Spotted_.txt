'''Gold Beanie Spotted!''' is the fifth level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 21.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 8 Spikes. The enemy captain is a Spike.

The 2nd battle contains 7 Piranha Bean, and 1 Fire Stalking Piranha Plant. The enemy captain is the Fire Stalking Piranha Pl.

The final battle contains 2 Scaratroopeas, 3 Spikes, 2 Piranha Beans, and a Gold Beanie. The enemy captain is the Gold Beanie.

== Possible Recruits ==

* Spike (Lv.21)
* Fire Stalking Piranha Pl. (Lv. 21)
