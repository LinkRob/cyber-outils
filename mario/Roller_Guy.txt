'''{{Infobox Enemy|name=Slurp Guy|image=PMCS Roller Guy.png|caption=|first=''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''|games=|homeland=|located=|leader=|species=[[Shy Guy]]|subspecies=|affiliates=|related=|attacks=Rolls into Mario using a powerful ring.|defeated=|comics=}}'''Roller''' '''Guys''' are a type of [[Shy Guy]] that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. A Roller Guy can either have a red, green, or pink color.

== Description ==
Their appearance is that of a normal [[Shy Guy]] inside a green rolling ring. They attack by running you over and ramming you to deal damage.

== Stats ==
'''HP:''' 8

'''Attacks:'''

* Attack ('''12''')
* Group Attack ('''24''')
* Blowback Attack ('''12''')
* Counter Attack ('''12''')
