The '''Grudge Metal''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Grudge Metal.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Grudge Metal resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has medium cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Grudge Metal deals around 155 to 165 damage.

It has a 50% chance of triggering the Super Effect. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Bounce (status effect)|Bounce]] dealing 198 damage.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Grudge Metal as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it after the player defeats [[Phantom (Rabbid)|Phantom]].
