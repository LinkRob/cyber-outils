'''Prohibited Command''' is a status effect that can be inflicted on the Mario Bros. in the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' games. It involves one or more [[Command Block|actions]] being disabled and is similar to [[No Skills]] from the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series.

==History==

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
There are two instances of this status effect occurring.

*The [[Boo|Boos]] in Bowser's Castle can mug the Mario Bros. for [[Command Block|Command Blocks]] if their charge attack hits. Defeating the Boo responsible restores the Command Blocks that it stole. If the Boos steal every Command Block from both Bros., the Mario Bros. automatically lose unless their counterattacks can help win the fight.
*When [[Popple]] uses his stealing attack during his boss battles in the Winkle region of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] and in the underground ruins in western [[Teehee Valley]], Hammers can be stolen. [[Popple]] telegraphs this by turning his back to the Bros. before making his move.

===''Mario & Luigi:  Bowser's Inside Story''===
During the battle versus [[Fawful]], the Vacuum Helmet Headgear that Fawful wears on his head may suck up the Items or [[Special Attack]] command blocks, prohibiting them until either destroyed or a few turns pass.

Bowser can also counterattack Special Attacks by devouring them during his optional battle in the [[The Gauntlet|Gauntlet]] (this game's version of the Battle Ring).

===''Mario & Luigi:  Dream Team''===
When battling [[Thunder Sass]], Mario needs to rely on counters to defeat him. To make up for this, though, Thunder Sass has his [[Heart Point|health]] and [[defense]] stats reduced.

Bowser Jr. steals [[Bros. Attack|Bros. Attacks]] by tickling the move's user with his Paintbrush.

The [[Tiny (ailment)|Tiny]] status effect can also prohibit the use of [[Special Attack|Special Attacks]].

===''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''===
The wardens of the [[Twinsy Tropics]] dungeon mug the Bros. for Command Blocks in an event in ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''. Upon their defeat, the wardens (reluctantly) return the Command Blocks.

In his [[Fury (status effect)|rage state]], Kamek may disable the Mario Bros.'s Command Blocks.
