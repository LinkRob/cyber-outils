'''Delfino Square''' is the second track of the [[Flower Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. It is the second track in the {{series|Mario Kart}} to take place on [[Isle Delfino]], the first being [[Peach Beach]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' ==
[[File:Delfino Square - Luigi Racing - Mario Kart DS.png|thumb|left|[[Luigi]] racing in Delfino Square.]]
This track features the Grand Pianta Statue, a drive through the alleyways of Delfino, and a drawbridge. The track is themed around Delfino Plaze from [[Super Mario Sunshine]] Boxes can be found on some sections of the track that house a [[Dash Mushroom|Mushroom]], but crashing into them head-on will stop a kart dead in its tracks. Shortcuts can be found, the first one is using a Mushroom over the water after exiting the alleyways and landing on the bridge, which has another item box. Another is seen behind the boxes. Using a Mushroom will get through the dirt and mud that is waiting there. Before the drawbridge, there is water on one side, which allows racers to hit each other off and lose time. The track's staff ghost uses Peach in the [[Royale]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' ==
[[File:Delfino_Square.PNG|thumb|''Mario Kart Wii'' screenshot showing an overview of Delfino Square before the race begins]]
Delfino Square appears as a [[Retro Stage]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' as the third track of the [[Banana Cup]]. This Retro Stage's staff ghost uses Wario in the [[Wario Bike]] (Nin★iwaco) and the [[Honeycoupe]]/Dragonetti (Nin★TARO).
