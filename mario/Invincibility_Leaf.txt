The '''Invincibility Leaf''' is a power-up item which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
When using an Invincibility Leaf, the user changes into their [[White Tanooki Mario|White Tanooki]] form, which is a variation of the [[Tanooki Suit]] with the invincibility properties of a [[Starman]], albeit without a [[time limit]]. When Mario or Luigi complete a level while in their White Tanooki form, the effects of the Invincibility Leaf wear off, changing them into their standard [[Tanooki Mario (power-up)|Tanooki]] form. Unlike a Starman, an Invincibility Leaf does not grant any added speed, jump velocity, or increments of [[point]]s from defeating enemies in succession. Just like the Starman, an Invincibility Leaf does not grant invulnerability from falling into a [[pit]], touching [[Poisoned Water]], or [[lava]].

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''===
The Invincibility Leaf only appears after [[Mario]] loses five lives in a level that he has not yet completed. The Invincibility Leaf does not appear when revisiting a completed level or a level within a Special World. This means [[Luigi]] cannot obtain it unless any levels in the regular world are skipped, such as from using a [[Warp Zone]].
If this power-up appears, even if it is not collected, the stars on the save file cannot become shiny.

=== ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' ===
The Invincibility Leaf has the same effect as in ''Super Mario 3D Land'', but it instead changes Mario or [[Luigi]] into their [[White Raccoon Mario|White Raccoon]] form. The effects of the Invincibility Leaf cannot kill [[Iggy]]'s [[Chain Chomp]]. The Invincibility Leaf cannot be replaced by any other items while it is active.

===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''===
Invincibility Leaves return as a power-up item, and they appear after a player has lost on a certain level a certain number of times.
