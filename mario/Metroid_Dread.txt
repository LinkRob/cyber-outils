'''Metroid Dread''' is one of [[9-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''. It is based on the [[Nintendo Switch]] game of the [[w:c:metroid:Metroid Dread|same name]] and uses the Archer form for its gameplay.

==Gameplay==
[[Samus Aran|Samus]] is running either left or right (Her direction matches which dominant hand the player uses.), and the player has to crouch when she is below breakable blocks so that she can grab an [[Energy Tank|energy tank]]. If done with good timing, the player wins at the microgame while Samus blasts through the blocks and with a Shinespark, grabbing the tank. However, if the player crouches either too early or too late, they lose at the microgame; crouching too early results in Samus crashing into the ceiling and being attacked by an enemy (causing the Power Suit to burst), while crouching too late (or not at all) results in her falling off the platform and into the [[Bottomless Pit|abyss]] ahead of it.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels feature a crouching area that is six, five, and three blocks wide, respectively.
