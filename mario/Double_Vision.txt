[[File:Double_Vision.jpg|thumb]]'''Double Vision''' is a 2 vs 2 mini-game in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

== Gameplay ==
There are two teams who draw specific parts of a picture which consists of a [[Cheep Cheep]], a [[Goomba]], a [[Boo]], or a [[Wiggler]]'s head. The team which drew better wins.
