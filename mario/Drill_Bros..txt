'''Drill Bros.''' is a recurring move in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' with the name '''Baby Drill''', as it is only usable by [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]]. It later returns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' as one of [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s moves.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''===
Baby Drill is learned by Baby Mario and Baby Luigi in [[Vim Factory]] shortly after they receive their [[hammer]]s from the [[L33t Hamm3r Broz.]]. Drill Bros. allows the babies to burrow underground, which can allow them to slip through certain tight spaces. However, Baby Drill cannot dig through hard materials like metal. If the babies surface while underneath a spot marked with an "X," they can receive a [[bean]]. In the field map, if the babies emerge from under an enemy, it gets launched into the air and falls back down stunned, which can potentially allow for a better [[First Strike]].

===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''===
Drill Bros. is learned by Mario and Luigi at the [[Energy Hold]]. Here, when the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] [[Spin Jump (Mario & Luigi series)|spin]] into a drill-like bone inside the Energy Hold, they find a very small space. Mario and Luigi then get the idea to drill into the ground using their hammers, therefore learning Drill Bros. and being able to use it for the rest of their journey.

Drill Bros. allows the Mario Bros. to either traverse under small and tight areas that not even [[Mini-Mario]] can travel through, activate certain parts inside [[Bowser's body]], and dig out beans. Outside of [[Bowser's body]], the location of a bean is represented with an "X," like in ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'', but in Bowser's body, the location of a bean is indicated by sparkling spots. Drill Bros. can also be used to collect [[Mushroom Ball]]s in [[Toad Town Mall]], and the Drill Bros. move later has to be used in the [[Lumbar Nook]] to alleviate the pain in [[Bowser]]'s back.
