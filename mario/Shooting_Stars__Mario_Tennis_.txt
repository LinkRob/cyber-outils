'''Shooting Stars''' is a [[minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]. It stars [[Luigi]] as the playable character.

==Gameplay==
Luigi has to hit the tennis balls shot from the [[Ball Bot]] at a star above the court. Luigi gets one [[point]] each time he hits the star and six by breaking it. The minigame ends Luigi misses hitting the ball even once.

If Luigi gets over 60 points, the [[Castle Court]] is unlocked.
