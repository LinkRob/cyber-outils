The '''Red Potion''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Red Potion is one of four potions that must be used to create the invisibility potion for [[Princess Peach]] so she can sneak inside of [[Sir Grodus]]' room. After getting the [[Data Disk]] for [[TEC-XX]], Peach must then drink the Red Potion to become visible again.
