'''Mass A-peel''' is a [[Donkey Kong]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Layout==
Mass A-peel has the same setting as the other two Donkey Kong minigames, in a jungle with a [[DK's Tree House|tree house]] and a circus tent resembling [[Swanky's Sideshow]] in the background. During gameplay, Donkey Kong hangs from a vine and swings above the player characters while eating [[banana]]s.

==Gameplay==
Bananas are scattered all over the place, and players have ten seconds to collect as many as possible while avoiding [[Banana Peel|banana peel]]s. Touching a banana peel causes that player to slip, losing time. Small bananas are worth a single [[coin]], while [[Banana Bunch]]es are worth five coins. The minigame ends early if all the bananas are collected.

The title of the minigame is a play on "mass appeal".
