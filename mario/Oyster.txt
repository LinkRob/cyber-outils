'''Oyster''' (also called '''Klam''') is an clam-like enemy that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

== In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' ==
These large, clam-like enemies are only found in [[Gloomy Galleon]] inside of a large treasure chest that only [[Tiny Kong]] can get into. There are several Oysters located in the treasure chest and each of them has a pearl that belongs to the [[Mermaid]]. [[Tiny Kong|Tiny]] must retrieve the pearls from the Oysters when they open their mouths. Tiny must also be careful not to stay too close to the teeth as if she is near them as the Oyster chomps down, she will take damage.
