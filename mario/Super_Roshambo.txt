'''Super Roshambo''' is a tournament that takes place at the [[Roshambo Temple]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. The tournament has three rounds. To play the game, players choose one of three cards: rock, paper, and scissors. Once a player has selected a card, it will be shown face down. The cards can be flipped over after the Ro-Sham-Bo! signal. There are three ways to win: Rock beats scissors, scissors cut paper, and paper covers rock. The player can win coins and a card if Mario defeats all three opponents but if he loses, then he can pay coins or collect a Mini Paint Star to play again.
