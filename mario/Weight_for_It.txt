'''Weight for It''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
A character knocks a ball past their rival, surprising them, before taking their position in the center of the left platform as [[Goomba]]s spectate.

==Gameplay==
Both characters have to use their [[hammer]]s to send as many of the seven weighted balls to the opposite side as possible. If a character is hit by a ball, they become stunned for a few seconds. Once the 15-second [[time limit]] runs out, whoever has fewer weighted balls on their side wins.

==Ending==
The winner does their victory animation as their opponent's side of the stage falls into the pit, taking the opponent with them.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Swing hammer

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''Hit balls with a hammer to roll them toward your rival. The player with the least amount of balls wins!''
* '''Advice''': ''Hit the balls! Quickly! It's easy and fun!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on the phrase "wait for it."
