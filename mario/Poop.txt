'''Poop''' is bodily waste converted from food digested by living creatures. It also exists within the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise and some related media.

Aside from being the hatching location of [[Baby Yoshi]]s, [[Yoshi Egg]]s have also been represented as poop, an example first introduced in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', in which a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can eat an enemy to digest it in under a second and poop it out as a Yoshi Egg; this move is known as [[Egg Lay]], which is also one of Yoshi's attacks in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, but the main difference is that the fighter inside of the Yoshi Egg is capable of escaping.

==In ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''==
Poop has been suggested to appear in ''Super Mario Sunshine'', specifically in the form of brown [[Goop]], although this implication is mainly because one of the enemies, [[Coo Coo]]s, drop splats of brown Goop as they fly around; this is unlike birds in the real world, which only poop while perched.

==In [[Wario (franchise)|''Wario'' franchise]]==
Poop has most frequently appeared in the ''Wario'' games.

In ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!|Made in Wario]]'', Poop is one of the objects that has to be avoided in the [[microgame]] [[Fruit Fall]]. In its overseas release, ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', this was replaced with diamonds.

Poop is featured in two ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' microgames; in [[Power Line Pigeons]], Wario has to avoid being pooped on by pigeons, and in [[What Goes In...]], the objective is to safely guide food through a human's digestive tract so that it comes out as poop.

In ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', Poop is one of the usable objects in the microgame [[Crowd Control]] to scare others with.

Poop also has a role in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. Here, if [[Arty Wario]] messes up a drawing, it turns into a living piece of poop with a pair of eyes and legs, and the poop proceeds to run away. Poop is one of the items featured in the minigame [[Trash It]], in which it must be guided to a toilet.
