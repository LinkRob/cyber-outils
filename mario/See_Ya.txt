A '''See Ya''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
When a See Ya is used, [[Mario]] and his party can flee from any battle without worrying about failing. However, a See Ya does not work on bosses or scripted fights. A See Ya can be purchased at [[Frogfucius' Student]]'s shop in [[Seaside Town]] for 10 [[Frog Coin]]s.
