'''Rainbow Flutter''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Yoshi performs a [[Flutter Jump]] in the air and then hits the ball, which leaves a rainbow trail behind it. It will temporarily slow down anyone who manages to return the ball. This may be the inspiration for the [[Flutter Dunk]] from ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

This power shot is able to hit the ball even if it is very high in the air, and is in fact the only Offensive Power Shot capable of doing this. Returning a lob with this power shot when its at the highest point in the air will make the ball fly over small characters, making them unable to return it.

A similar move as a Super Move is present in [[Table Tennis - Singles]] in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]''.
