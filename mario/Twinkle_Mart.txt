|first = ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''}}'''Twinkle Mart''' is an item shop that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Twinkle Mart can be found deep within the [[Outer Limits]] and is being run by [[Howzit]]. Twinkle Mart only sells the three chocolate bar items of the game. It is required to go here the first time in order to get a chocolate bar for [[Squirps]].
