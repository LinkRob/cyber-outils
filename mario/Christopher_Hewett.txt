'''Christopher Hewett''' (April 5, 1921 - August 3, 2001 [aged 80]) was a British actor who was best known as the titular butler on ''Mr. Belvedere''. He played King Koopa in the ''[[Mario Ice Capades]]'' TV special. He died on August 3, 2001.
