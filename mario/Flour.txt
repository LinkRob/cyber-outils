'''Flour''' is a food and key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Flour is a key ingredient for a [[cake]] that [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] must bake for [[Gourmet Guy]] after the events of Chapter 4. It is added before the cake gets baked, along with [[Butter]], [[Cream]], [[Milk]], [[Strawberry|Strawberries]], and [[Sugar]]. Correctly making the cake will please Gourmet Guy and will give Peach and Twink information on the next [[Star Spirits|Star Spirit]].
