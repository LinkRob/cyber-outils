'''Shroom Steaks''' are items in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. They are usually very potent healing items.

==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
[[File:Shroom Steak.png|frame|left]]
Shroom Steaks are powerful healing items that are only obtainable by cooking. [[Tayce T.]] will make this delicious item for [[Mario]] if he gives her an [[Ultra Shroom]] and one of many mushroom items. By cooking these shrooms up, Mario can obtain this item. Although it doesn't heal more HP than the Ultra Shroom, a Shroom Steak does heal 30 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 10 [[Flower Point|FP]].

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
[[File:Shroom Steak (TTYD).png|frame|left]]
The Shroom Steak is nearly as helpful as in ''Paper Mario''. This item retains the same effect of healing 30 HP and 10 FP from ''Paper Mario''. Since it is a lot easier to obtain an Ultra Shroom than before, the Shroom Steak is a much more prominent and powerful item. Although, just cooking an Ultra Shroom with a [[Maple Syrup]] will create a much more powerful item, the [[Maple Ultra]]. If Mario wants this item in this game, he must ask [[Zess T.]] to cook one of his Ultra Shrooms.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Shroom Steak is now created by cooking up an [[Ultra Shroom Shake]], which can be easily purchased, making Shroom Steaks more accessible than before. Shroom Steaks now restores 70 HP, more than double of what it was before, and can cure [[Poison (status effect)|Poison]]. Shroom Steak is the second most powerful healing item, second to only the [[Dyllis Deluxe]].
