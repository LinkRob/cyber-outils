'''Dark Bloo Inn''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
Dark Bloo Inn is a blue area. When Mario encounters this level, something is amiss. Inside, a tuxedo Toad at the door tells Mario about a strange phenomena happening, objects flying about or the phone ringing without anyone at the other end. Huey and Mario have to go through the Inn and splat paint on six Toad ghosts to reveal their contribution to the chaos under a time limit (When you don't help all Six Toads, the Inn will go into a time loop, if this happens repeatedly, Huey might tell Mario they might be in a time loop). Once they are found, they gather in the Inn's dining room and want Mario to commence the tea party they promised to have when they were alive a long time ago. Mario and Huey find the Teapot thing from the [[Plum Park]]. Then Mario can serve the six Toads tea and allow them to ascend to heaven. They appear in the painting.

After the story, the Inn reopens, and Mario is free to visit and relax, restoring HP.
