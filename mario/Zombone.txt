'''Zombone''' is the undead version of the [[Czar Dragon]] and a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[File:Zombone SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
Zombone is fought in [[Barrel Volcano]], right after he is defeated in his original Czar Dragon form, upon which Zombone rises back out of the [[lava]] as a living skeleton.

===Battle===
Zombone posses many strong attacks that can inflict damage to all of Mario's party. Its signature attack, [[Boulder (move)|Boulder]], can deal the highest damage, effectively replacing the Czar Dragon's [[Water Blast]].

====Strategy====
Zomebone actually possess two weak points: Thunder and Jump. This means that Mario's Jump moves and [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt]] is useful here. Mallow is useful from the start since the Czar Dragon's weak point was Ice which [[Snowy]] comes in handy. [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] should heal when necessary.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Zombone's name is a portmanteau of "zombie" and "bone."

==Trivia==
*Zombone is similar to two latter bosses in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games:
**[[Bonetail]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' in that both of them are skeletal dragons.
**[[Dry Bowser]] from ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' in that both of them had an original form until falling out the lava and coming back as a living skeleton.
