'''Mario Wings to the Sky''' is the fifth [[mission]] of [[Bob-omb Battlefield]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]],'' its [[Nintendo DS|NDS]] [[Reissue|remake]], ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and its 2021 enhanced port, [[Super Mario 3D All-Stars|''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'']]. 

== Description ==
This level is exactly the same in both ''Super Mario 64'' and its remake. Mario has to fly through five rings of coins in the air near the island using the [[Wing Cap]] (aquired after collecting ten Power Stars (14 in the remake), going into the castle and looking up when there is sunshine beaming down on the rug) and collect all five middle coins of the rings to make the Power Star appear. Taking off from the Island in the Sky, he has to use the Wing Cap and shoot himself from a cannon to have enough speed to hit all the rings, as well as being able to fly upwards. The Power Star appears in the meadow near the Star Marker. It is also possible to use the cannon on its own to go through all the rings, but this takes good aiming and can't be done in one try. Through this method, it’s also possible to use [[Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] to get the Star in the remake.
