'''Bullet Bill''' is an item in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
The item is a regular [[Bullet Bill]], as shown from its icon. The Bullet Bill only appears in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge mode]], in which it must be purchased from the [[Item Shop]] so that [[Bowser]] can use his [[Star Skill]], [[Killer Ball]], if he is either on the player's team or the team captain himself. The Bullet Bill costs 200 [[coin]]s to buy.
