'''Spiky Gummits''' are a variant of [[Gummit]] and an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Spiky Gummit has a purple body, yellow eyes, and a few yellow spikes placed around its body.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
Spiky Gummits are encountered in [[Freezy Flake Galaxy]], during the mission [[The Chimp's Skating Challenge]], and [[Shiverburn Galaxy]], during the mission [[The Chimp's Ultimate Skating Challenge]]. They appear here along with Gummits and [[Golden Gummit]]s, but unlike them, Spiky Gummits must be avoided by [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], otherwise they get knocked back, costing them at least a second in the timed challenge. Despite possessing spikes over their body, Spiky Gummits cannot damage Mario or Luigi.
