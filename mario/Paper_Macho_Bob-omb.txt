A '''Paper Macho Bob-omb''' is a type of Paper Macho Soldier and rare enemy in ''Paper Mario: The Origami King''. They only appear in Hotfoot Crater alongside small Paper Macho Goombas and Paper Macho Lava Bubbles, collectively referred to as "Paper Macho Crater Crashers" in their Collectible Treasure name in the Musée Champignon. They parachute down from the top of the volcano; once they land, they act like regular Bob-ombs, chasing Mario and soon exploding. They can only be defeated by letting them self-destruct, as they are immune to the hammer. The resulting explosion is used to break any rocks blocking Mario's path, though it deals 15 HP damage to him if he is too close.
