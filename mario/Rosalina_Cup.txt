'''Rosalina Cup''' is a cup with [[Rosalina]] representing as an icon or name. It appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
Rosalina Cup has an icon representing for itself. Like all cups, the cup contains four courses per tour depending on its appearance on each tour.
