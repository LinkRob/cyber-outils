The '''Master Poet''' is a character in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is a very skilled poet who is found in [[Club 64]] of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]], where he works with [[Chanterelle]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Master Poet is an elderly [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with green spots on his head. He wears a green jacket and has white, curly hair.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
The Master Poet isn't significant to the story, but he is needed to obtain the [[Attack FX D]] badge. After Mario completes Chapter 2, he can talk with the Master Poet who requests new lyrics from the [[Toad in the Desert]] living somewhere in the world. If Mario brings the [[lyrics]] from Toad in the Desert who lives in [[Dry Dry Outpost]], the Master Poet will conduct it and Chanterelle will sing. [[Goombario]] mentions in his [[Tattle]] that the Master Poet is in love with [[Chanterelle]]. The Master Poet also speaks in rhymes to fit in with his personality.
