A '''Book''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
Books are only found in [[Creepy Castle]] in the library. Once [[Donkey Kong]] enters the library, he'll eventually find a hidden room with a [[Golden Banana]] at the end. However, there are many Books flying in the hall requiring Donkey Kong to use his [[Strong Kong]] ability to get by them to get to the Golden Banana. The Books cannot be defeated by any means making them one of few invincible enemies in the game.
