'''Tough Coffee''' is an item in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Description==
When bought, Tough Coffee permanently increases [[Princess Peach]]'s life meter by half a heart.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Tough Coffee can only be bought four times, increasing in price each time it is bought. Tough Coffee costs 250 [[Coin]]s when first bought; 500 the second time; 1,000 the third time; and 1,500 the fourth and final time.
