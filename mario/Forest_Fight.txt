'''Forest Fight''' is the ninth level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 14.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 5 [[Boo]]s and 3 [[Fire Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Boo who knows [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 1 [[Chuck Guy]], 3 [[Para-Beetle]]s, and 4 Fire Bros. The enemy captain is a Para-Beetle who knows [[Chance]].

The 3rd battle contains 2 Chuck Guys, 4 [[Koopa Paratroopa|Koopa Paratroopa (Red)]], and 2 Fire Bros. The enemy captain is a Chuck Guy who knows Deny.

The final battle contains 1 Chuck Guy, 1 [[Boo]], 1 Para-Beetle, 1 Koopa Paratroopa, 3 Fire Brros, and [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]]. The enemy captain is Iggy, and he knows [[Chance]].

After the final battle, the player recruits Iggy. 

The level can be replayed to unlock up to one Boo, Koopa Paratroopa, Fire Bro, and or Para-Beetle.
