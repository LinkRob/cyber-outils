The '''Mushroom Bank''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
The Mushroom Bank is a building shaped like a mushroom. It also has large coins around the roof and the word "Bank" at the very top.

==In ''Mario Party''==
The Mushroom Bank can be visited anytime and is where the player can store their coins, stars, etc. The player can also come here to buy items.
