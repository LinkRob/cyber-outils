'''Mastery Gems''' are items that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. 

==History==
Whenever [[Wario]] obtains a Mastery Gem, he will be able to upgrade a certain disguise allowing for more functions with it. Mastery Gems can be found in certain levels inside green treasure chests and the player must play the minigame [[Color Me Happy]] in order to obtain the Mastery Gem.
