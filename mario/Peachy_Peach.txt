A '''Peachy Peach''' is an item which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Peachypeachttyd.png|frame|left]]
Peachy Peaches by themselves can only restore 1 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 2 [[Flower Point|FP]], but they are an ingredient usable in several dishes. The only way for [[Mario]] to receive Peachy Peaches is to sleep in the [[Twilight Town]] [[Inn]], where a Peachy Peach is left on the table whenever he sleeps there.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
Peachy Peaches are more useful than in the previous game, this time restoring 7 HP and even having the ability to cure [[Poison (status effect)|poison]]. Peachy Peaches also retain their usefulness in being used in a lot of recipes that can be beneficial to Mario's journey. Unlike the previous game, Peachy Peach in this game can be purchased in [[Item Shop]]s, though, Mario can rest in an Inn to receive some as well.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', giving [[Princess Peach]] a [[Pink Apple]] while she is asleep temporarily turns her into a Peachy Peach; the same thing happens with [[Cyrrus]] in the [[Overthere Stair]].
