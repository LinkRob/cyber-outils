'''Goomba Village''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's objective is to choose the house with the highest amount of Goombas to win. Players will observe the [[Goomba]]s as they run into different [[Toad House]]s. After all Goombas enter a house, players have a choice between five houses. If a player chooses a house, other players cannot choose the house they chose. After the players choose a house or ten seconds pass, the Goombas will come out of the house as the number of Goombas are counted. The player with the highest count wins the minigame.
