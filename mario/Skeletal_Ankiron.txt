'''Skeletal Ankiron''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Skeletal Ankiron is a variant of [[Ankiron]] with a body composed of skeleton.

===Behavior===
The only difference between a Skeletal Ankiron and an Ankiron is its appearance, so Skeletal Ankirons act the same as Ankirons.

==In ''Wario World''==
A Skeletal Ankiron only appears in [[Horror Manor]], guarding a steel trapdoor where the course's final [[Red Diamond]] and [[Gold Statue]] can be found.
