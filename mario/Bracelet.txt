The '''Bracelet''' is a key item in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In the [[Bestest Buds]] quest, the Bracelet is one of three items mentioned by [[Bowser]] while lounging at the [[Bowser Pad]], with the other two items being the [[Ring (Mario Party Advance)|Ring]] and the [[Necklace (Mario Party Advance)|Necklace]]. The Bracelet becomes available to purchase at [[Junk]] after Bowser asks the player to guess whether he wants the Ring, the Necklace, or the Bracelet the most. Bowser mentions that he prefers rings to bracelets but that that he likes bracelets and necklaces equally, meaning the Ring is the item that has to be bought.

If the player's character makes the mistake of purchasing a Bracelet and brings it to Bowser, he is shown to hate it, and the player is forced to restart the quest. In spite of this, Paratroopa said earlier that Bowser is going to appreciate the Bracelet.
