{{Nihongo|'''Hamma, Bamma, and Flare'''|トリプルブロス|Toripuru Burosu|Triple Bros}} (also known as the {{Nihongo|'''Big Bad Brute Bros.'''|ミラクルトライアングル|Mirakuru Toraianguru|Miracle Triangle}}) is a team of the [[Glitz Pit]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== History ==
Hamma, Bamma, & Flare consists of a [[Hammer Bro]], a [[Boomerang Bro]], and a [[Fire Bro]]. The leader of this team is [[Hamma Jamma]] who comes from a long line of fighters (most notably his [[Hamma Jamma's grandfather|grandfather]]). This team is ranked 3rd of the Glitz Pit making them one of the strongest teams.

== Trivia ==
*Bamma and Flare are the only Boomerang Bro and Fire Bro that appear in the entire game.
