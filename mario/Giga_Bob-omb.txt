'''Giga Bob-omb''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

==Description==
The Giga Bob-omb is a type of [[Bob-omb]] with a bluish color, a menacing glare, and metallic legs and feet. Just below its eyes is a furnace-like cage that resembles a mouth. A Giga Bob-omb is very big, even moreso than King Bob-omb.

==In ''Mario Kart Tour''==
A Giga Bob-omb is the [[Special Item (Mario Kart series)|special item]] of [[Toad (species)|Purple Toad (Pit Crew)]], [[Princess Peach|Peach (Yukata)]], [[Cat Mario|Cat Luigi]], [[King Bob-omb]], and [[Shy Guy|Shy Guy (Gold)]] as well as the [[Mii|King Bob-omb and Purple Mii Racing Suits]]. When used, the Giga Bob-omb bounces forward on the course briefly before exploding, producing a blast radius much larger than a regular [[Bob-omb]]'s. Unlike regular Bob-ombs, a Giga Bob-omb cannot be thrown backwards, and if a Giga Bob-omb is held for too long, it automatically explodes, knocking out the user. When Giga Bob-omb+ is active, the Giga Bob-omb is even larger and produces an even bigger blast radius.
