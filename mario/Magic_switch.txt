'''Magic switches''' are a set of eight different-colored tiles with symbols on them in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. They are featured in the [[DK's Stone Statue]] party board's boss [[minigame]], [[Hexoskeleton]]. The character has to [[Ground Pound|ground-pound]] specified tiles in a particular order to damage [[Dry Bones]] with magic. After being hit three times, Dry Bones collapses, and [[Donkey Kong]] is restored to his original state. [[Diddy Kong]] then hands over a [[Sky Crystal]] as thanks. There is also a boss trophy of a magic switch, obtainable by beating Dry Bones three times.
