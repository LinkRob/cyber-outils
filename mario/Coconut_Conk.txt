'''Coconut Conk''' is a 1-vs-3 [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame takes place on a deserted island with the three team players in the six [[coconut]]-filled palm trees and the solo player in the [[barrel]] in the sand. The team players must [[Ground Pound|ground-pound]] the trees to hit the solo player with coconuts, while the solo player has to avoid being hit. The team players must avoid ground-pounding each other, or the flattened character is temporarily stunned. If one of the team players hits the solo player with a coconut, the team players win, but if the solo player avoids being hit by a coconut for the entire time limit, that player wins.

===Trio Challenge===
In ''Mario Party Superstars'', this minigame is available in Stage 2 and Stage 5 of the [[Mt. Minigames#Trio Challenge|Trio Challenge]] in [[Mt. Minigames]].

In Stage 2, human players play as a team against a Hard CPU solo opponent, and the rank requirements are:
* A: Player scores a hit on a rival within 10 seconds
* B: Player hits within 20 seconds or CPU hits within 10 seconds
* C: Player hits within the time limit or CPU hits within 20 seconds
* D: CPU scores a hit within the time limit

In Stage 5, a single human player plays against Hard CPU opponents, and the rank requirements are:
* A: Win

* {{button|n64|Stick}} &ndash; Move

====Solo Side (1 player)====

====Team Side (3 players)====
*{{button|ns|stick}} (Left and Right) &ndash; Move
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} &ndash; Jump
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} '''→''' {{button|ns|jc-right}} &ndash; Ground Pound
