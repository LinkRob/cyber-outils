'''Snoozorbs''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. They are large robots used by the [[Shroob]]s as guards in [[Shroob Castle]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Their appearance is similar to a grey orb with a single golden leg supporting them and two golden arms.

===Etymology===
Snoozorb is a portmanteau of the words 'snooze' and 'orb'.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''==
[[File:When Snoozorbs Attack.PNG|frame|left]]
Usually, Snoozorbs are found sleeping, but if the Bros. approach them, the monster will wake up and attack. They attack by either shooting a laser beam or changing into a ball and rolling into the Bros. As with other enemies, Snoozorbs give two hints on how they attack. First, they will blow some steam. If the steam comes out the right of their head, the Snoozorbs will attack [[Mario]] and if the steam comes out the left, the target is [[Luigi]].

If the Snoozorb does its rolling attack, it will roll toward the appropriate brother, who must use a [[Hammer]] strike to fend off the enemy's attack. If the Snoozorb is planning on firing a laser, it charges, gathering energy. If the energy goes straight in, Mario or Luigi must [[Jump]] to avoid the attack. If it spirals, however, the attack will pass overhead. Snoozorbs sometimes direct their laser towards both Bros.

After Snoozorbs have taken enough damage, they awaken and start attacking multiple times in a single turn.
