'''Challenges''' are achievements in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Each one requires a condition to be meet. These can range from recruiting Minions, receiving EXCELLENT ratings, defeating enemies, using beans, or clearing quests. There are 10 medals for each difficulty (bronze, silver, or gold). Even if a Challenge is completed, the game will not register it until a level has been won.

==List==
=== Bronze ===
*Recruit 20 Goomba Minions (Goombas, Paragoombas, Big Tail Goombas, Private Goomp, and Goomba Towers all count as a Goomba Minions)

*Recruits 20 Koopa Troopa Minions (Koopa Troopas, Koopa Paratroopas, Koopa Paratroopas (Red), Corporal Paraplonk, and Captain Koopa Troopa all count as a Koopa Troopa Minions)

*Recruits 20 Shy Guy Minions (Shy Guys, Spear Guys, Fly Guys, Bandits, Sergeant Guy, and Captain Shy Guy all count as Shy Guy Minions)

*Recruits 20 Boo Minions (Boo, Bomb Boo, Tail Boo, Big Boo, Broozer, and Captain Boo all count as Boo Minions)

*Use beans to fortify 50 times

*Defeat 300 enemies

*Recruits 20 spiky Minions (Spinies, and Pokies both count as spiky Minions)

*Receive 100 EXCELLENT ratings

*Clear 50 quest

*Recruits 50 Minions

=== Silver ===
*Recruit all types of Goomba Minions (Requires Goombas, Paragoombas, Big Tail Goombas, Private Goomp, Goomba Towers, and Captain Goomba)

*Recruit all types of Koopa Troopa Minions (Requires Koopa Troopas, Koopa Paratroopas, Koopa Paratroopas (Red), Corporal Paraplonk, and Captain Koopa Troopa)

*Recruit all types of Shy Guy Minions (Requires Shy Guys, Spear Guys, Fly Guys, Bandits, Sergeant Guy, and Captain Shy Guy)

*Recruit all types of Boo Minions (Boo, Bomb Boo, Tail Boo, Big Boo, Broozer, and Captain Boo)

*Recruit all types of Melee troopers (Requires Captain Goomba, Captain Koopa Troopa, Goomba, Big Tail Goomba, Goomba Tower, Koopa Troopa, Broozer, Buzzy Beetle, Spiny, Pokey, Chain Chomp, Chargin' Chunk, Bob-omb, Mechakoopa, Private Goomp, Morton, and Roy)

*Recruit all types of Flying troopers (Requires Captain Boo, Paragoomba, Koopa Paratroopa, Koopa Paratroopa (Red), Boo, Bomb Boo, Tail Boo, Big Boo, Fly Guy, Para-Beetle, Lakitu, Magikoopa (Blue), Corporal Paraplonk, Bowser Jr., Starlow, and Prince Peasley)

*Recruit all types of Ranged troopers (Requires Captain Shy Guy, Dry Bones, Shy Guy, Spear Guy, Bandit, Hammer Bro, Fire Bro, Ice Bro, Boomerang Bro, Fire Stalking Piranha Plant, Spike, Magikoopa (White), Magikoopa (Red), Magikoopa (Green), Sergeant Guy, Larry, Wendy, Iggy, Lemmy, and Ludwig)

*Defeat 1,000 enemies

*Clear 100 quests

*Recruit 100 Minions

=== Gold ===
*Recruit "that guy" (Requires the player to play [[Believe in Bowser]] to recruit [[Bowser Jr.]]

*Learn all Captain Commands (Requires Deny, Rally, Chance, Play Dead, Lightning, Parry, Pass the Bob-omb, Thwomp Thump, Clash of Captains, Call for Backup, Sneak Attack, and Shell Shock)

*Perform all skills (See Skills Below)

*Recruit all characters

*Use beans to fortify 200 times

*Receive 500 EXCELLENT ratings

*Raise a Minion's level to 40

*Defeat 1,500 enemies

*Clear 200 quests

*Recruit 150 Minions
