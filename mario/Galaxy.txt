'''Galaxies''' are locations featured as courses in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They can be accessed via [[Dome]]s in the former game and [[World]]s in the latter.

== In {{series|Super Mario}} ==
=== ''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Galaxies contain either seven [[Power Star]]s, six Power Stars, or one Power Star.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2===
Galaxies contain either three Power Stars and three [[Green Power Star|Green Power Stars]], or two Power Stars and two Green Power Stars.
