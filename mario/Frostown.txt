'''Frostown''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for the [[Nintendo DS]].

==Description==
Frostown is a town and [[Frosty]]'s home. It features a Gallery, a totem of emblems, a broken mechanism, and a [[Thwomp]].

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
Frostown is the first area of [[Adventure Tours]]. [[Toad (character)|Toad]] is the first character encountered by [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog]], and he joins them after some persuasion. [[Princess Peach]] can make the Thwomp happy so that it moves, opening up the path and to an area where [[Knuckles the Echidna]] is. [[Miles "Tails" Prower]] is also found here, and he will fix a broken mechanism at the very start once he sees a similar working mechanism in [[Polastraits]]. [[Sparky (character)|Sparky]] is held hostage by [[Eggman Nega]] here. When Mario and Sonic's team defeat Eggman Nega in [[Nordic Combined]], Sparky is freed, Eggman Nega teleports away, and the heroes proceed to [[Sparkleton]].
