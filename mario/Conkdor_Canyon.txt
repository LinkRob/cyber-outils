'''Conkdor Canyon''' is the first level of [[World 2 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 2]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It can be accessed after completing [[Bowser's Highway Showdown]], and completing Conkdor Canyon unlocks either [[Puffprod Peaks]] or [[Shadow-Play Alley]]. Conkdor Canyon also appears as a [[Bonus Episode (Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker)|Bonus Episode]] in the [[Wii U]] version of ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' if it is being played on a Wii U that also has save data for ''Super Mario 3D World''.

==Features==
The most notable feature of this level is the [[Conkdor]]s. This level is mainly desert based and the players must work their way up the structure to complete the level.

==Collectibles==
The following are exclusive to ''Super Mario 3D World''.

===Green Stars===
*'''[[Green Power Star|Green Star]] 1''': In [[Cat Mario|Cat]] form, climb the wall to the right of the first Conkdor.
*'''Green Star 2''': Use the seventeenth Conkdor to destroy the block closest to the flagpole way ahead, by giving it your attention, facing you.
*'''Green Star 3''': Destroy the last Conkdor to have [[Captain Toad]] give you the star.
