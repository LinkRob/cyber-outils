'''Bedrock''' is one of the secondary antagonists in ''[[Mario + Rabbids: Sparks of Hope]]'' (alongside [[Midnite]] and [[Daphne]]).

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Bedrock is a giant muscular cubic-looking Rabbid. She wears a black Greco-Roman wrestling outfit on which a dark fuchsia seatta can be slightly discerned, has pink hair with two (cubic) pigtails on either side, yellow eyebrows, and wears pink foundation and light blue lipstick.

=== Personality ===
{{Sectstub}}

== History ==
Bedrock is an entity born from the fusion of [[Rabbid]] DNA with a block of granite. Her massive body, as hard as stone, and her blocky pink hair are her most recognizable physical traits. Apart from that, she also wears a pink and black Greco-Roman wrestling outfit. Bedrock is one of the [[Sparks Hunters]] created by Cursa to hunt down [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Sparks]] across the galaxy.

She appears mainly in [[Paletteville]], capital of [[Palette Prime]], where [[Mario]] and his friends caught her in the middle of a duel against [[Bowser]]. Disturbed to see her former friend [[Edge]] at Mario's side, she finally got the better of Bowser and threw him into a [[Darkmess Tentacle]], into which she plunged in turn. Defeated by the combined efforts of Bowser and the other heroes, Bedrock managed to escape with the help of Daphne.

She reappeared one last time alongside [[Midnite]] at Cursa's Fortress, where their mistress almost crushed them with her hand as punishment for their failures. After Cursa's defeat, Edge suggests that his former friends have survived and will have to find a new profession.

==In ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope''==
=== Combat ===
The fight takes place in a circular arena divided into 4 sections, with Bedrock at the center. Bedrock's allies are mainly [[Goomba]]s, but also [[Stooge|Stooges]] and [[Depleter|Depleters]], plus other henchmen arriving via portals. Bedrock has extremely high resistance, especially to the effects of [[Burn]], so that most attacks inflict virtually no damage. Her main technique, [[Solo Stomp]], is to leap onto a target within her reach. Her imposing stature enables her to destroy obstacles and push back characters standing in her way via the [[Cover Crasher]] effect. Finally, she possesses a passive behavior called Repercussion. When attacked, she jumps in place, causing a shockwave on landing that indiscriminately injures enemies and allies alike, before heading for the attacker.

The only way to beat Bedrock is to lure her into each of the 4 sections of the arena, in the center of which are explosive barrels hidden under leaves, then have her activate them by crashing to the ground. The resulting explosion removes Bibi's 1/4 health and sends her back to the center of the arena, as well as destroying the section where the barrels were located.
