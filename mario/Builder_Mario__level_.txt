'''Builder Mario''' is an official course in Course World in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. It is the fifth level published by NintendoJP, with the "standard" tag. It is set in the desert theme for both the main area and sub area in the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' style.

==Layout==
This level is made up of mechanics introducing the [[Super Hammer (Super Mario Maker 2)|Super Hammer]]. To start, there are two [[? Block]]s, one of which contains a Super Hammer. [[Mario]] needs to power up into [[Builder Mario]], then use his hammer to clear away the [[Rock Block]]s up ahead. He can also do so with the [[Thwomp]]s nearby. He can go through the [[Clear Pipe]], then bounce off the [[Mushroom Trampoline]] over the [[Big Thwomp]]. Then there is a Pipe giving out Super Hammers. Along with four [[Big Spiny|Big Spinies]] roaming the area, there are three sets of different blocks, including Rock Blocks, ? Blocks, and [[Brick Block]]s. The pipe leading to the sub area is just ahead.

The pipe leads to a [[Bill Blaster]] shooting out Super Hammers. There are many Rock Blocks ahead of Mario he needs to clear away with his hammer. After a while, he gets a [[Checkpoint Flag]] along with a ? Block containing a Super Hammer. The next obstacle is made up of Rock Blocks, Brick Blocks, and red [[POW Block]]s. There is also a [[Cloud Lift]] below Mario. As Mario progresses, he comes across an area with [[! Block]]s. There is a nearby Bill Blaster which shoots out coins. At the end, Builder Mario must create [[Builder Box]]es to enter the pipe leading back to the main area.

In the main area, there is a nearby red POW Block Mario can get, then hit the ! Block to get up to the [[Goal Pole]].
