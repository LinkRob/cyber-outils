'''Circus Balls''' (also called '''Beach Balls''', '''Magical Balls''', '''Rubber Balls''', or simply '''Balls''') are a type of yellow bouncy ball with stars imprinted on them that are primarily used by [[Lemmy Koopa]]. During his battles, Lemmy is often seen balancing on a Circus Ball and he can spawn Circus Balls from his wand which [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] must dodge and bounce upon to reach Lemmy. The Circus Balls are rather bouncy so the plumbers will spring back if they are hit. Outside battles, Circus Balls are sometimes used as a means of reaching high platforms.

==History==
===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[Image:Circus Ball Classic.jpg|thumb|left|Lemmy tossing his Circus Balls around the room.]]
Circus Balls are first seen in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. In this game, they are mainly seen during the battle with [[Lemmy Koopa]] (at the end of the [[Ice Land]]). In this game, Lemmy can be seen riding on a Circus Ball and from it, he will launch Circus Balls at [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] (rather than the usual [[Magic Projectile]] attacks the other [[Koopalings]] possess). If the plumbers are to be bumped by a bouncing Circus Ball, they will be bounced back so the plumbers must dodge and bounce on the Circus Balls to reach Lemmy. When Lemmy Koopa is hit, he will bounce in his shell before donning a new Circus Ball, after the hits, Lemmy falls with his Circus Ball and the plumbers emerge victorious.

===''Super Mario World''===
[[Image:Circus Ball Roll.png|thumb|right|Iggy Koopa rolls a Circus Ball.]]
Circus Balls reappear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' however this time, they are not used by [[Lemmy Koopa]] but rather, [[Iggy Koopa]] and [[Larry Koopa]] use them instead. During their respective battles, Iggy and Larry will try to toss Circus Balls at the plumbers which they must dodge. If they are hit, they will be bounced back which makes it easier to fall in the [[lava]] so avoid them at all costs as you go to jump on Iggy/Larry's head.

===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[Image:Circus Ball Ice Bounce.png|thumb|left|Lemmy tossing Circus Balls at the Mario Crew.]]
Circus Balls appear again in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', in this game, they are once again utilized by [[Lemmy Koopa]] in his battles in [[World 3-Tower (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3-Tower]] and [[World 3-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3-Castle]]. In these games, Lemmy balances atop a Circus Ball whilst spawning several bouncing Circus Balls from his wand, if the heroes are hit by a Circus Ball, they will be bounced back and may slip off the icy edges so be careful. Whenever Lemmy is stomped, his Circus Ball falls as he takes damage, when Lemmy emerges from his shell, he gets a new Circus Ball. As the battle progresses, Lemmy will shoot more Circus Balls. In [[World 3-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3-Castle]], there are several Circus Balls seen bouncing around which the plumbers can use to reach higher platforms. Also in the same level during the boss fight, Lemmy rides on a [[Large Circus Ball]] which makes reaching him more difficult.

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[Image:LemmyKoopaNSMB2.png|thumb|right|Lemmy about to launch a Circus Ball at Mario from his own.]]
Circus Balls return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' once again being utilized by [[Lemmy Koopa]] in his battle in [[World Flower-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Flower-Castle]]. During the battle, Lemmy will ride on a Circus Ball while tossing more Circus Balls at [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]]. The plumbers must dodge and bounce on these Circus Balls and avoid falling off to hit Lemmy. They can also bounce off a Circus Ball to hit the switch on the room's roof to change the conveyor belt's direction. Just as before, being hit by a Circus Ball will bounce the plumbers around, when Lemmy is hit, his Circus Ball will drop and he will spin in his shell before utilizing a new Circus Ball. Circus Balls can also be seen around [[World Flower-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Flower-Castle]] as well where they can be used to bounce to higher platforms.

===''New Super Mario Bros. U / New Super Luigi U / New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[Image:Lemmy Circus Ball Circus Bombs.png|thumb|left|Lemmy shocked at the appearance of the Mario Crew from his Circus Ball.]]
Circus Balls also appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' once again being used by [[Lemmy Koopa]] in his boss fights in [[Lemmy's Swingback Castle]] and [[Lemmy's Lights-Out Castle]]. During the battle, Lemmy rides his Circus Ball as he attacks but this time, he is seen tossing [[Circus Bomb]]s at the heroes rather than Circus Balls. These variations of Circus Balls explode on impact so avoid them at all costs. With every hit, Lemmy's Circus Ball will drop as he spins in his shell before retrieving a new Circus Ball.

===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
[[Image:Circus Ball Lemmy Combat.png|thumb|right|Little Lemmy Koopa on his Circus Ball.]]
Circus Balls appear again in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]'' once again being utilized by [[Lemmy Koopa]]. Most of Lemmy's attacks often involve him utilizing the Circus Balls, additionally, Lemmy is also seen riding a Circus Ball.

===''Paper Mario: Color Splash''===
[[Image:Lemmy Circus Ball Act.png|thumb|left|Lemmy's putting on a show with his Circus Ball performance.]]
Circus Balls are seen once again in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' where they are once again utilized by [[Lemmy Koopa]]. In this game however, they have a different design (having red and green stripes) highly resembling a beachball. In the [[Emerald Circus]], Lemmy is often seen using the Circus Ball during his performances and acts. He also uses it in his boss battle, to beat him, [[Mario]] will need to use jump attacks to attack him from way atop the Circus Ball. Halfway through the battle, Lemmy ditches the Circus Ball for a much more powerful variety called the [[Wonder Ball]]. To pop it, Mario will need to use a [[Disco]] card to distract Lemmy and the [[Ice Pick]] to pop the Wonder Ball.

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
[[Image:Super Lemmy Maker 2 The Circus Construction.png|thumb|right|Little Lemmy rides on his Circus Ball again!]]
Circus Balls are seen yet again in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. In this game, they are once again used by [[Lemmy Koopa]] and he is seen riding a Circus Ball by default. Lemmy also tosses Circus Balls from his magic wand which [[Mario]] can dodge or bounce upon. If [[Mario]] is hit by a Circus Ball, he will be bounced back. They can once again be bounced upon to reach Lemmy. The Circus Balls function similarly to [[Bumper (Super Mario Maker)|Bumpers]].

==Trivia==
*Most Circus Balls have a few stars imprinted on them but the one [[Lemmy Koopa]] is seen riding in [[World 3-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3-Castle]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' has stars imprinted all over. This is primarily because it was later enlarged into a [[Large Circus Ball]] by [[Kamek]].
*The Circus Balls slightly resemble the Pixar Ball from the movie ''[[wikipedia:Toy Story|Toy Story]]''.
