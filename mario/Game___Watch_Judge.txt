'''Game & Watch Judge''' is one of the [[microgame]]s featured during [[9-Volt]]'s [[Nintendo Classics]] level in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''. It is based on the [[Game & Watch]] game ''[[Judge]]''.

==Gameplay==
Two characters from ''Judge'' are each holding a numbered sign, and the player's character has to select which sign displays the greater number by either touching or attacking it.

If the greater number is selected, the character with that number hits the other character with their hammer, and the character with the lower number lifts their legs into the air. A hand giving a thumbs up is shown at the bottom-right to indicate that the player has won at the microgame. By selecting the character with the lower number, they attempt to attack using their hammer, only to be quickly attacked by their opponent instead. A hand shaking with all fingers slightly bent is shown to indicate that the player has lost the microgame.

In two-player mode, whoever selects the correct sign receives an effort point, but whoever selects the wrong sign loses an effort point.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' Each sign displays a number, and the highest possible number is 9.
*'''Level 2:''' Each sign displays dots, and the highest possible number is 6.
*'''Level 3:''' The signs display addition expressions. The highest possible sum is 5.
