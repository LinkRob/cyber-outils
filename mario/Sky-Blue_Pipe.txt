'''Sky-Blue Pipes''' (also called '''Sky-Blue Spiny Eggs''') are a variant of [[Spiny Egg]] found in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and it’s [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|switch remake]], they are found in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] and are thrown by [[Dark Lakitu]]s. When a Sky-Blue Pipe hatches, it turns into a [[Sky-Blue Spiny]]. In battle, a Sky-Blue Spiny can curl up into a Sky-Blue Pipe for a turn rendering it invincible to attacks, Sky-Blue Pipes also do more damage than regular [[Spiny Egg]]s but have the same weaknesses, [[Superguard]]ing is very effective against a Sky-Blue Pipe.

==Trivia==
*Despite their name, Sky-Blue Pipes aren’t actually Sky-Blue at all and are actually a royal blue.
*The name "Sky-Blue Pipe" comes from a translation error, the Japanese Name for "Spiny Egg" (''Paipo'') was confused with ''Paipu'' (the Japanese name for [[Pipe]]), this mistranslation was fixed in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].
