'''Evil Woody'''<ref>"''Many of the Stars are only a few steps away from a junction that changes at least once a turn. There are three exceptions: the Star after the Evil Woody, the one near the lake on the way back to the Start, and the one by the Piranha Plants near Woody in the bottom left corner.''" Hodgson, David and Bryan Stratton. ''Mario Party 3'': [[Prima Games|Prima's]] Official Strategy Guide. Page 32.</ref> is [[Woody]]'s counterpart featured in a few games of the {{series|Mario Party}}. He first appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Profile==
Contrasting with Woody's perpetually happy expression and friendly attitude, Evil Woody has a perpetually angry expression and a hostile attitude. His color palette is also an inversion of Woody's color palette, being blue with purple leaves.

==History==
===''Mario Party 3''===
[[File:Evil Woody MP3.png|thumb|left|Evil Woody in ''Mario Party 3'']]
In ''Mario Party 3'', Evil Woody is found in [[Woody Woods]]. If a player lands on one of the three [[Event Space|? Space]]s surrounding Evil Woody, he will award them a Minus Coin Fruit (resulting in a loss of five [[coin]]s) or a Reverse Block Fruit allowing the player to roll again but go in reverse. If the player does not choose either fruit, Evil Woody will always give them a Reverse Block Fruit.

===''Mario Party 6''===
[[File:MP6 EvilWoody.png|thumb|Evil Woody in ''Mario Party 6'']]
Evil Woody appears in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' on the board [[Towering Treetop]]. He appears at night to replace Woody at the top of the [[board]]. Whenever a character lands on one of Evil Woody's [[Event Space]]s, instead of him giving coins as Woody does, Evil Woody instead takes away coins by dropping [[Spiny|Spinies]] on every character. Unlike in ''Mario Party 3'', Evil Woody appears to be an alterego of Woody that comes out at night in this game rather than simply being an evil version of him.

===''Mario Party Superstars''===
Evil Woody reappears in the returning board Woody Woods in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. In addition to reprising his role from ''Mario Party 3'', he also appears at the end of a game to drop large purple fruit onto the losing players in order from last to second. This time around, the Minus Coin Fruit takes away a random number of coins ranging from 1 to 15, and if the player does not choose either of his fruits, Evil Woody will make them suffer the effects of both fruits. Also, the victim adopts a sad expression on their face whenever they roll Evil Woody's [[Dice Block]], and instead of the victim going in reverse on their own, a swarm of [[Swooper]]s appears, dragging the victim backwards before dropping them on the ground.

==Trivia==
*The structure of Evil Woody's Japanese name is intentionally like that of [[Wario]] and [[Waluigi]], being a portmanteau of the Japanese word ''warui'' ({{ruby|悪|わる}}い), meaning "bad," and the name of their good counterpart.
*How Evil Woody replaces his counterpart in ''Mario Party 6'' is unknown, as he is established as a separate being in ''Mario Party 3''.
