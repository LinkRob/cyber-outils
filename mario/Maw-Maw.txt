'''Maw-Maws''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are orange and maroon tadpole-like creatures.

===Behavior===
In their natural state, Maw-Maws wander around at plodding speeds. However, if it is provoked, a Maw-Maw chases and eats whatever comes into its path, including other enemies (This will immediately defeat the enemy that became the Maw-Maw's prey.). After chasing for a period of time, a Maw-Maw eventually becomes exhausted and stops.
