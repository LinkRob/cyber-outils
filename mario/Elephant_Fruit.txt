The '''Elephant Fruit''' is a power-up in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It is a red fruit with a white underside that transforms [[Mario]] into [[Elephant Mario]].

== Trivia ==
* In real life, there is a real fruit named the {{w|Dillenia indica}}, nicknamed the "Elephant apple."

== Trivia ==

* When Mario eats an Elephant Fruit and transforms into an Elephant, he says "Wowie-zowie!"
* The Elephant Fruit's appearance resembles an apple with ears, eyes and a trunk.
* The Elephant Fruit's power-up sound effect is similar to an original power-up sound effect, but instead, it uses horns and trumpets to resemble the trumpet of the real-life elephants.
