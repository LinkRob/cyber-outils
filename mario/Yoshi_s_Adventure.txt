'''Yoshi's Adventure''' is a ride attraction in [[Super Nintendo World]]. It opened in Universal Studios Japan in March 18, 2021 and will open in Universal's Epic Universe in summer 2025.

== Description ==
The theme of the ride is [[Yoshi's Island (series)|Yoshi's Island]], which consists of [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s and [[Egg]]s. The ride will involve turning and sudden stops. It also involves strobe effects and loud noises which may not be comfortable for ear-sensitive visitors and may disturb assistance dogs.<ref name="usj"/>

In Universal Studios Japan, the minimum height is 92cm. Otherwise, that said guest must be accompanied with another guest.<ref name="usj"/>
