'''Ice Slide, You Slide''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

== Layout ==
The minigame takes place on slippery ice surrounded with snow and trees.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the characters have to jump over the [[Snowball]]s and slide under the [[Fliprus]]es since hitting either of them slows down whoever hit it. The first character to get to the end wins.

The players must flick their [[Wiimote]] to jump over Flipruses. After jumping over Flipruses, the screen will command the players to shake their Wiimotes until they reach the finish line.

== In-game description ==
* ''"Run, jump, and slide to the goal!"''

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|B}} − Duck
*{{button|wii|wiimote}} − [[Jump]]/Gain momentum at the homestretch

== Etymology ==
The name is a pun of "I slide, you slide".
