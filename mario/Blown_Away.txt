'''Blown Away''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. Its name comes from a term that refers to someone who is very impressed.

==Gameplay==
The minigame takes place on a large floating light-pink cloud. Each player must knock their opponent off by using an air bazooka. Players can also make a bigger blast by holding the {{button|gcn|a}} button. The bigger the blast, the farther the player on the receiving end flies back when hit. The player who knocks their opponent off the cloud wins. If neither player has been knocked off the cloud within the time limit or both players fall off simultaneously, the minigame is a draw.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Move
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Fire bazooka
