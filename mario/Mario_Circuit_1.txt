'''Mario Circuit 1 '''is a course that first appeared in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'' and reappeared in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', [[Mario Kart DS]], ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart World]]''.

==''Super Mario Kart''==
In ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'', Mario Circuit 1 is the first course of the [[Mushroom Cup]]. When they turn left they pass [[coin]]s. During the second turn, the pipes are the hazards. After them, they pass the items and then there is a shortcut where they can cut through the sand.
==''Mario Kart: Super Circuit''==
In ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', Mario Circuit 1 is part of the [[Extra Mushroom Cup]]. The background is based on [[Peach Circuit]] and the pipe hazards were removed.

==''Mario Kart DS''==
In ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', Mario Circuit 1 is the first course in the [[Shell Cup]]. In [[Time Trial]]s, the Staff Ghost uses [[Mario]] in the [[Standard MR]]. In [[Mission Mode]], mission 1-8 is played on this course, where [[Mario]] has to perform at least 4 [[Mini-Turbo|powerslides]].

==''Mario Kart Tour''==
This track returns in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' as part of the [[New York Tour]], while receiving R, T, and R/T variants like the other tracks that appear in this game. There is an added Glider ramp in the R and R/T variant.

==''Mario Kart World''==
{{Main|Mario Circuit (Mario Kart World)}}
This track returns in ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. Unlike the previous installments, the track consists of two Mario Circuits ([[Mario Circuit 2]] and [[Mario Circuit 3|3]]) combined in order.
