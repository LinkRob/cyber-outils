'''Drago''' is a green dragon and a character who appears in ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]]. Here, Drago is the sixth boss fought by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in Game B of the Round Mode.

==Profile==
===Physical appearance===
Drago has green skin, and his belly is covered in yellow scales. His wings are pink.

===Powers and abilities===
Drago's main attack is turning red and breathing out fire, which turns every bomb onscreen into an enemy. Aside from this, Drago also has the ability to teleport.
