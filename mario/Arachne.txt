'''Arachnes''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They have a yellow and black-striped body, and their appearance resembles that of a real tarantula.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Arachnes are only encountered in [[Rose Way]].

==Battle==
In battle, Arachnes mainly attack with physical moves or with their sole special move, [[Venom Drool]], which may [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] one of [[Mario]]'s party members.

===Strategy===
Arachnes are weak against [[Ice]], so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] move is effective against them. However, considering that Snowy is learned later than when Rose Way is first accessed, such an opportunity is unlikely. Bringing [[Able Juice]]s can help in case a party member becomes poisoned.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Arachne is named after {{w|Arachne}}, a character from Greek mythology who was turned into a spider.

==Trivia==
*An Arachne's [[Psychopath]] quote is based on the song "{{w|Banana Boat Song|Day-O}}," which makes reference to a deadly black tarantula.
