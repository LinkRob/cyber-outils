'''Fuzzler''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== Appearance ==
Fuzzlers are a hybrid of [[Wiggler]]s and [[Fuzzy|Fuzzie]]s. That are predominant at [[Switchboard Falls]]. Fuzzlers are found on tracks. When it detects [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Toad (character)|Toad]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]] or [[Rosalina]], it chases after the player and attacks by contact. However, they can be defeated by players in their [[Invincible Mario|invincible form]] or they may fall off the track. {{SM3DWNav}}
