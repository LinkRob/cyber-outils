}}A '''Dark Reversya Cursya''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Dark Reversya Cursya is only found in certain rooms of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. They are darker versions of [[Reversya Cursya]]s. Like with them, Dark Reversya Cursyas inflict the reverse curse on those who touch them directly which reverses controls for a while. It is recommended to take the Dark Reversya Cursyas down from a distance.
