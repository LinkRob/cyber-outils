'''Balloon Boos'''<ref>Loe, Casey. ''New Super Mario Bros.'' Player's Guide. Page 11.</ref> are [[List of enemies|enemies]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are [[Big Boo]]s with pink cheeks, and their tails resemble balloon knots, akin to their name.

==History==
[[File:BalloonBoo NSMB.png|thumb|Some Boos and a Balloon Boo in ''New Super Mario Bros.'']]
Balloon Boos appear only in one level, {{world-link|4|ghostn|World 4-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros.)}}, where five of them are encountered. They appear large at first, but when looked away from, they approach the player while expelling air and shrinking; otherwise, they stop and inflate by inhaling air at a rather slow rate, until they have assumed their massive appearance once more, by doing so, it could end up bumping into the plumbers even without moving, making him dangerous in tight spaces. Balloon Boos continue to chase [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] even when they have reached their minimum size. The air that a Balloon Boo inhales does not affect Mario or Luigi. Balloon Boos can only be defeated with the effects of a [[Blue Shell]], [[Starman]], or a [[Mega Mushroom]].

== Trivia ==
*In the game's files, there exists an unused variety of the Balloon Boo which doesn't deflate/inflate it's size and slowly chases Mario when his back is turned like a normal [[Boo]]. Said Balloon Boo moves much slower than a normal Boo however, this Balloon Boo could be considered an unused [[Big Boo]] as it doesn't change it's size.
*When [[Mega Mario]] (or Mega Luigi) collides with a Balloon Boo, it pops and soars away in circles, somewhat reminiscent to destroying a [[Goal Pole|Flagpole]] with this same form.
*In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', the [[Fwoosh]] enemy looks similar to a Balloon Boo.
