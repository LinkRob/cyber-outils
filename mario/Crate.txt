A '''Crate''' is a wooden box featured as a recurring object featured in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] and its spinoffs. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==In the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]==
===''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''===
Crates are objects featured as platforms in the [[airship]] levels.

===''[[Super Mario 64]]'' / ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''===
[[Mario]] can either pick up and throw or punch crates to have it release a few [[coin]]s.

There is also variant named the [[Crazy Crate]] which upon being picked up by [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]] or [[Wario]] in the [[reissue|remake]]), bounces around a few times before landing, releasing coins in the process like regular crates.

==In the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]==
===''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' / ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''===
[[File:Crate SMW2YI sprite.png|frame|left]]
Crates contain either a few [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]] or a [[Key]]. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can break open a Crate by performing a [[Ground Pound]] on it, something that was later made possible in later installments of the ''Super Mario'' series.
