The '''Fan''' is a [[Stickers#Thing Stickers|thing sticker]] that appears in the {{series|Paper Mario}}.

==Description==
The Fan sticker has light-blue fan pieces and a black base. This sticker is extremely large in size, taking up sixteen regular sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].

==History==
===Paper Mario series===
====''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''====
In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', the Fan is found in W1-2, [[Bouquet Gardens]]. It produces a large breeze that destroys a Toad's flower garden and blows Goombas towards Mario. 

====''Paper Mario: Color Splash''====
In ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'', the Fan is found at Cherry Lake. It produces a breeze that blows Shy Guys towards Mario and lifts the bridge to Justice Toad.

====''Paper Mario: The Origami King''====
The Fan returns in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'', although not as a collectible item. When Mario completes a puzzle on Bonehead Island, the Fan is seen rising up in the background, accompanied by music similar to its appearance in ''Sticker Star''.
