'''Bazooka Shy Guys''' are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bazooka Shy Guys are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] with an identical appearance to regular Shy Guys, except they are equipped with a bazooka launcher. Like several Shy Guy variants, the color of a Bazooka Shy Guy's robe varies in color.

===Behavior===
When a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] is near a Bazooka Shy Guy, it methodically shoots explosive [[cannonball]]s at the Yoshi, trying to damage him.

==In ''Yoshi's Crafted World''==
Bazooka Shy Guys are encountered in [[Mr. Geary's Factory]] and [[Floppin' and Poppin']]. A Yoshi can defeat Bazooka Shy Guy using any attack and convert it into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]].
