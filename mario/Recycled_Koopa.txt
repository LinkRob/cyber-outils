"'''Recycled Koopa'''" is the twenty-fourth episode of ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

== Blurb ==
[[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] orders the [[Koopalings]] to pick up the garbage around the castle. Being lazy, they deposit into a warp pipe that leads to [[Princess Peach's Castle|Princess Toadstool's castle]], which spreads through the entire [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and the others soon find the source, and decide to steal a Koopaling's [[Magic Wand]] to send it back to [[Bowser's Castle|Castle Koopa]]. After arriving at the castle, Mario steals Hop's wand. Mario find [[Super Leaf|Super Leaves]] that he and the others use to gain Raccoon Power and fly back to Toadstool's castle, while they avoid getting blasted by the Doom Ship. Mario uses the wand to send the garbage (as well as the [[Doom Ship]]), back to Castle Koopa.

Although the Mushroom Kingdom is back to normal, Mario spies on the Koopas, only to see that [[Sledge Bro]]s. are just tossing the garbage down another pipe. Mario and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] go to investigate, and when they go down the other pipe, it warps them to [[Brooklyn]]. Not only is the Koopa garbage spreading throughout their hometown, but it's turning people everywhere into [[Koopa Zombie]]s. Meanwhile, the Koopas are watching everything from the Doom Ship, where King Koopa gets the idea to make the zombies his slaves.

The Bros. attempt to clean up all the garbage, but King Koopa orders the zombies to stop them. After a chase sequence, the Bros. finally ditch the zombies. But now, Mario is slowly starting to turn into a Koopa Zombie himself. The Bros. manage to build a vehicle called the [[Trash Smasher]], but in the end, Mario becomes completely zombified. Luigi uses the Trash Smasher to recycle all the garbage into into practical goods, which turns everyone back to normal. King Koopa tries to pollute the city with more garbage, but the Marios fly after him in the Trash Smasher.

== Synopsis ==

At Kastle Koopa, King Koopa orders Bully,Kootie Pie, Big Mouth, Hop, and Hip Koopa to clean the various rubbish around the castle. He warns them that if they don't, they won't be able to cause mischief for a week. As Koopa departs, Bully quickly relaxes in a hammock, telling the others to start cleaning. Frustrated by his laziness, Big Mouth threatens to tell Koopa about Bully's delay, but Bully remains unaffected. At that moment, Kootie Pie picks up a tin can, dismissing it by claiming she broke a nail and is too fabulous to work. Hearing this, Bully insults her, saying he thought she was part of the trash. Kootie Pie retaliates by throwing sludge in his face. He brushes it off and throws a [[Green Shell]] at her, accidentally hitting Big Mouth. Koopa then mentions that he hopes they're busy cleaning, to which Bully responds affirmatively and tosses some trash into a [[Warp Pipe]].


Meanwhile, at [[Princess Toadstool's Castle|Peach's Castle]], [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Toad]], and [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] are walking through the garden when garbage suddenly falls out of an overhead pipe. The flow of trash eventually halts, but the garden is ruined. When the princess asks who could be responsible, Mario picks up a statue of King Koopa. As they discuss their options, Luigi sarcastically suggests using a [[magic wand]] to clear the mess. Mario agrees and decides to retrieve the wand from one of the Koopalings, then they jump through a Warp Pipe to do so. Back at Kastle Koopa, the five Koopalings show Koopa the now-cleaned castle grounds, each claiming they did the most. Suddenly, the group emerges from the Warp Pipe outside the castle, with Koopa and the Koopalings in pursuit. Luigi advises them to hide inside a nearby suit of armor, which they do successfully. During the chase, Mario manages to grab Hip's wand as they pass by.

As the group flees from the castle, some Koopalings notice Hip's wand in their possession. Mario, Luigi, Toad, and Princess Peach then collect Super Leaves from nearby blocks. Donning Raccoon Suits, they take to the air and fly away. Koopa responds by preparing his Doomship to follow them. In the Mushroom Kingdom, Mario, Luigi, Toad, and Princess Peach continue flying as the Doomship catches up, launching [[Bob-omb]]s. After hitting Luigi's tail, Koopa orders [[Paragoomba|Para-Goombas]] to attack. Toad and the princess manage to evade and disable two enemies, but they notice the garbage around their castle emitting a foul odor, threatening to spread across the kingdom.


They land on the garbage heap, where Mario chants an incantation that gathers all the trash into a giant ball, which also engulfs Hip's wand. Mario tries to retrieve the wand but is stopped by Luigi, preventing him from being sucked in. The garbage ball is then launched out of the castle and crashes onto the Doomship, weighing it down and causing it to crash back at Kastle Koopa. Big Mouth claims the trash is recent, but Koopa is skeptical and orders everyone to dispose of it again to prevent recurrence.

At Princess Toadstool's Castle, as Mario, Luigi, and the princess enter, she questions whether they've learned their lesson. Toad then appears, handing Luigi his [[Plumber's Periscope]] to check on Koopa. Mario looks into the periscope via a Warp Pipe and sees [[Sledge Bro]]thers dumping more boxes of trash into it. Realizing they haven't learned their lesson, Mario informs the others. The princess tells them to go stop the trash dumping while she and Toad stay behind to guard the castle. The brothers arrive just as Bully orders the Sledge Brothers to continue dumping trash, who are exhausted from the task. Mario and Luigi look down the pipe to see where the trash is going, but the Sledge Brothers accidentally drop them inside the pipe without noticing.

Mario and Luigi fall into [[Brooklyn]]. As trash continues falling from the sky, they use trash can lids to shield themselves from panicked citizens passing by. Luigi spots a tail that looks like Koopa's and, upon pulling it, discovers it's actually a lookalike in business attire, who walks away muttering "Koopa." After seeing a cab driver pick up some trash and turn into another lookalike, they realize the trash is transforming citizens into [[Koopa Zombie]]s. They rush to [[New York City]]'s city hall to find the [[Mayor of New York City|mayor]], only to see him turning into a zombie too. Recognizing that the trash is spreading to the [[Earth|real world]], they rush to clean it up.

Meanwhile, on the Doomship, Bully, Kootie Pie, and Big Mouth inform Koopa about the destructive power of the garbage on Earth's inhabitants. Big Mouth rants about how they want Koopa to be their leader, but gets a can shoved onto his head. Struggling to remove it, Koopa orders them to keep dumping trash into the real world, aiming to conquer it. Upon arriving, King Koopa commands the zombie horde to attack Mario and Luigi, as displayed on a banner. He also dumps trash along with weapons like [[Blooper|Bloober]]s to aid the attack. The horde, along with enemies like [[Bob-omb]], [[Dry Bones]], and [[Fire Snake|Firesnake]], chases the brothers through various locations, including a sewer and a subway station, with both hiding in piles of garbage to evade capture.


After escaping, Mario laments about Koopa, unaware that he has grown a tail resembling Koopa's. Luigi notices and alerts him, causing Mario to panic. They realize they must eliminate all the trash before Mario is fully transformed. Rejecting magic, they settle on a plan. Meanwhile, Koopa fires his [[Crud Cannon]] from his modified Doomship, which hovers over the Statue of Liberty, now resembling Koopa. The cannon spews garbage across Brooklyn, transforming animals into creatures like Paragoombas and [[Spiny Cheep Cheep]]s, including Mario himself, while he and Luigi work on their [[Trash Smasher]]. Before Mario is completely transformed, he asks Luigi to finish the device.


With the Trash Smasher ready, Luigi pilots it to vacuum and recycle the city’s garbage, reversing the transformations of the residents, including Mario. However, Koopa resumes dumping trash over Brooklyn. Determined to stop him permanently, the brothers upgrade the Trash Smasher to fly, allowing them to pursue the Doomship. They successfully chase Koopa out of Brooklyn, with the standard Statue of Liberty commenting on their efforts.

==Trivia/Goofs==
* When King Koopa leaves the Koopalings to pick up the trash, Bully's head quickly jumps behind his back for one frame.
* When King Koopa leaves his castle to get Hip's wand back, all the trash is already back at his castle.
* The giant garbage ball that hits King Koopa's Doom Ship causes him to land back at his castle, however, it should have really landed back at the Mushroom Kingdom.
*This is the only time outside the ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' in which King Koopa says "Attack!"
