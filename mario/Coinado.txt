The '''Coinado''' is an item that allows the player to steal coins from another rival in the ''[[Mario Party series]]''. It made its debut in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

== Description ==
The Coinado resembled a tornado capturing [[coin]]s in front of a purple/magenta spiky shape in ''Star Rush''. In ''[[Super Mario Party]]'', the Coinado is a jar with a blue label of a tornado capturing coins.

== In ''Mario Party: Star Rush'' ==
[[File:Coinado.png|thumb|right]]
The Coinado can be placed in a space on a board somewhat like the [[Hex|Hexes]] from [[Mario Party DS]]. The player who lands on the hidden space with a Coinado loses five coins.

== In ''Super Mario Party'' ==
[[File:SMP Boo using Coinado.png|thumb|165px|[[Boo]] using the Coinado.]]
The user of Coinado can choose who to steal coins from the rival. The Coinado will take five to ten coins from the said opponent.
