Orbs are items that appear mainly in the Puzzle and Dragons series. They made their Mario debut in Puzzle and Dragons: Super Mario Bros Edition.

== Appearance and Types ==
There are currently six known types of Orbs.
* Fire Orb: a red-colored Orb with a Fire Flower pattern.
* Water Orb: a blue-colored Orb with a Penguin Suit pattern.
* Wood Orb: a green-colored Orb with a Tanooki Leaf pattern.
* Light Orb: a golden-colored Orb with a Super Star pattern.
* Dark Orb: a violet-colored Orb with a Poison Mushroom pattern.
* Heart Orb: a pink-colored, square-shaped Orb with a heart pattern.

== Powers/Abilities ==
Orbs must be moved into connected lines if the player wants to use them. Five of the Orbs (Fire, Water, Wood, Light, Dark) allow monsters on the player's team to attack, while Heart Orbs restore the team's collective HP.

== Trivia ==
* Heart Orbs are the only square-shaped Orbs to exist in-game. (But if they're square-shaped, they should technically be called Heart Cubes.)
* The Dark Orb is the only Orb with the pattern of a power-up that does not appear in the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros. series]].
