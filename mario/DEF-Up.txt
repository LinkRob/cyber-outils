{{sectstub}}
===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
DEF-Up appears only in this game of the ''[[Paper Mario series]]''. DEF-Up as usual raises defense of the user. DEF-Up can be achieved in a number of ways including using [[Courage Shell]]s or the special move [[Power Lift]] (that also raises attack).
