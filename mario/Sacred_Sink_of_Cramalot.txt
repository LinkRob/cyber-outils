The '''Sacred Sink of Cramalot''' is an object in [[Cramalot]]. It appeared in "[[King Mario of Cramalot]]" in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''

== Description ==
Legend says the one who pulls the [[Golden Plumber's Helper (King Mario of Cramalot)|Golden Plumber's Helper]] from the Sacred Sink of Cramalot can defeat the [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and save [[Cramalot]]. This is based on the legend of {{w|Excalibur}}.
