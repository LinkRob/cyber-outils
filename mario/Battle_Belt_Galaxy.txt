The '''Battle Belt Galaxy''' is a [[galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is located in [[World 6 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 6]], the setting of the chapter Bowser In Our Sights. As its name implies, the Galaxy is essentially a huge gauntlet, since Mario has to defeat enemies (such as [[Goomba]]s) to advance. Mario can access it with at least 65 [[Power Star]]s in his possession, including the first one from the [[Throwback Galaxy]].

==Power Stars==
===Mini-Planet Mega-Run===
{{main|Mini-Planet Mega-Run}}
This mission is available from the start when Mario unlocks this Galaxy. In order to advance, he must defeat every enemy. At the end of the level, Mario must face four [[Silver Chomp]]s, who serve as the boss of the level. After Mario defeats them, he can grab the key and save the [[Power Star]].

===Mini-Planet Daredevil Run===
{{main|Mini-Planet Daredevil Run}}
Same as Mini-Planet Mega-Run, but the Hungry Luma that inhabited the grassy plateau on the ice planet is gone, as is the Gobblegut Planet, forcing Mario to head for and defeat the Silver Chomps, [[Coin]]s do not appear, and if anything hurts him, a life is automatically lost.

===Snacktime for Gobblegut===
{{main|Snacktime for Gobblegut}}
If Mario scoops up at least 20 [[Coin]]s, he can feed them to a [[Hungry Luma]] that lies on a grassy plateau on the ice planet and therefore spawns a bonus planet. When Mario makes his way there, [[Gobblegut]] returns to claim his [[Fiery Gobblegut|revenge]].

===Green Star locations===
All three [[Green Star]]s are on top of shields that guard either [[Warp Pipe]]s (or, in the case of the last one, the [[Power Star]]). The first one can be obtained from either Mini-Planet Mega Run or Snacktime For Gobblegut, but the latter two are restricted to Mini-Planet Mega Run.

# After his arrival on the pumpkin-shaped planet with the walking flame-producing pumpkins, Mario must perform a backflip onto the shield that guards the Warp Pipe that leads into the planet, then perform a second one to get the [[Green Star]]. Grabbing the key will cause the shield to vanish, prohibiting him from grabbing the Star.
# Same thing, but on the grassy planet where the [[Undergrunt]]s are digging their trenches.
# Same thing, but on the steel planet where the [[Silver Chomp]]s are fought.
