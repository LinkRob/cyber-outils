'''Frogoons''' are a type of enemy which first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Frogoons are light green frogs with dark green spots on their back. They have a yellow variant named [[Hopgoon]].

In the {{series|Mario Kart}}, Frogoons do not have the ability to inflate themselves, and instead only hop around. In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', Frogoons make two consecutive jumps before turning around, while in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', they only jump once.

== History ==

=== ''Donkey Kong Country Returns'' (3D/HD) ===
In ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' and it [[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D|3D]]/[[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD|HD]] [[Reissue|remaster]] version, Frogoons appear as enemies together. They repeatedly jump up and down in one spot. They can inflate their body while in midair to hover up slightly higher. A Frogoon can be defeated from being [[jump]]ed onto, causing it to deflate and blow away offscreen. A minor detail is that Frogoons sometimes try to catch and eat a small fly between their jumps.

=={{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 7]]''===
[[File:DK Jungle Frog.jpg|thumb|200px|A Frogoon hopping toward Donkey Kong in DK Jungle in ''Mario Kart 7'']]
Frogoons are one of the obstacles to appear in [[DK Jungle (Mario Kart 7)|DK Jungle]]. If a racer drives into a Frogoon, they spin out and lose three [[coin]]s, like with a [[Tiki Goon]].

===''[[Mario Kart 8]]''/''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
Frogoons retain their role of being obstacles in {{classic course|3DS|DK Jungle}}. 

== Other appearances ==
=== ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'' ===
Frogoons appear in certain difficulty levels of the [[Donkey Kong Country Returns (microgame)|Donkey Kong Country Returns]] microgame, as one of the enemies that have to be defeated to move on.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Their name is a portmanteau of "frog" and "goon."
