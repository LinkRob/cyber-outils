'''Power Play''' is the third level of [[Joke's End]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 26.

==Layout==
The 1st battle contains 6 [[Broozer]]s and 2 [[Clumph]]s. The enemy captain is a Broozer.

The 2nd battle contains 5 Clumphs and 3 [[Ice Snifit]]s. The enemy captain is a Clumph, and he knows the moves Chance and Deny.

The final battle contains 3 Broozers, 3 Clumphs, and 2 Ice Snifits. The enemy captain is a Broozer, and he knows the moves Rally and Deny.

*Broozer (Lv. 26)
