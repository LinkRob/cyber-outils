The '''Waste Basket''' is an object used in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and is available at the start of the game. [[Mario]] can only hold 29 items in this game. As such, if Mario is carrying the maximum amount of items and picks up a new item, he must decide which item to discard. However, the player cannot throw away important items.

In [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|the Switch remake]], the Waste Basket does not exist. Instead, consumables each have different quantity caps that vary based on the item (e.g., Mario can carry up to 10 [[Mushroom]]s at a time, but only up to 3 [[Croaka Cola]]s at a time). Any excess items are automatically transferred to a storage box in [[Mario Bros.' House#Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars|Mario's Pad]]. Unique, formerly-disposable items such as the [[Bright Card]] and [[Fertilizer]] are placed into a Special Items tab, where they cannot be discarded or sold.
