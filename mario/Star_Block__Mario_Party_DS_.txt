The '''Star Block''' is a rare type of [[Hex]] in the game ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. The player who possesses it can choose to place it on any [[Blue Space]] or [[Red Space]] within five spaces from their current location. Then, if anyone lands on it, a golden [[Dice Block|Block]] appears with the numbers 1-3 on it. The player then strikes the block, receiving the amount of [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] indicated. Players can get it in a [[Hex Area]] or receive it from the [[Snag Bag]].

An identical golden block containing one to three Stars can also appear as a [[Hidden Block (Mario Party series)|Hidden Block]], though this is a rare occurrence.
