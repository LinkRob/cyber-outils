The '''Silver Credit''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Silver Credit is given to [[Mario]] by [[Koopa Koot]] after doing the [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]]'s favor that involves giving him a [[Sleepy Sheep]] to help him sleep. The Silver Credit can then be used in the [[Playroom]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] to play the Jump Attack game. A similar item called a [[Gold Credit]] can also be received from Koopa Koot later in the game.
