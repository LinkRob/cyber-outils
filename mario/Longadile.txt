}}A '''Longadile''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Longadiles are stronger versions of [[Longator]]s that are first seen in [[Castle Bleck]] mainly in [[Castle Bleck Interior]]. Like with Longators, Longadiles attack by stretching out their heads toward their target. Longadiles are rather troublesome enemies during Chapter 8-3 as they are often found beside pits meaning if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] gets hit by one, they will fall into a pit taking extra damage. This is especially dangerous in the room with the many [[Dimentio]] clones where they must avoid them and the Longadiles. Longadiles can also be found in the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]].
