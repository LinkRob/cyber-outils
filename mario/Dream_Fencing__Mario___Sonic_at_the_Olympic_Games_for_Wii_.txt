'''Dream Fencing''' is an unlockable [[Dream Event]] in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' for the [[Wii]]. It can be unlocked by completing Comet Circuit.

== Layout ==
Dream Fencing takes place at Dual Wharf Fencing Beach.

== Gameplay ==
Dream Fencing has strong similarities to regular fencing, except it more closely resembles one-on-one matches in fighting games, as the characters have health bars and their goal is to deplete the opponents' health completely and defeat them instead of score a certain number of hits. Each of the characters have their own special move.

Four players duke it out in a tourney tree-style competition aiming for the top three spots, like with [[Dream Table Tennis (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games for Wii)|Dream Table Tennis]], [[Singles (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games for Wii)|Singles]], and regular [[Individual epée (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games for Wii)|Individual epée]].

When a character uses a special move, their icon of the top part of their face flashes across the screen, and they unleash an attack that can only be blocked if the opponent strikes the player as they charge the attack. A character can be knocked out of the arena, causing them to take some damage. If an opponent if forced to back up as far as possible, the other character can use a special move and blast them out of the stage. If the knocked off character has enough health remaining, the fight continues with both characters set to their starting positions.
