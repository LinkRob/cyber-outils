'''Fried Eggs''' are a [[recipe]] item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
=== ''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Fried Egg PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Fried Eggs give [[Mario]] 10 [[Heart Point|HP]], the same as a [[Super Shroom]]. These items are only obtainable from [[Tayce T.]] who Mario must ask to cook up one of his [[Egg]]s (which are also rare items that can only be found in a few areas in the game mainly in one of the bushes at [[Mt. Rugged]]. These items don't sell for very much, around 6-7 [[coin]]s.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Fried Egg.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Fried Egg still gives Mario only ten HP, so it is not all that great. Also, the [[Mystic Egg]] item is more difficult to obtain than the Egg item was in ''Paper Mario''.
{{br|left}}
===''Super Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Fried Eggs are created by cooking a [[Big Egg]] which replaces the previous egg items and restores twelve HP. Fried Eggs are slightly stronger, restoring fifteen HP.
