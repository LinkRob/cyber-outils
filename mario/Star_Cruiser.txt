The '''Star Cruiser''' is a vehicle in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Description==
The Star Cruiser is a small blue ship with wings on it. It also depicts a star on the front.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
After [[Mario]] saves all of the [[Star Spirit]]s and meets them at [[Star Haven]], the Star Cruiser is soon created for him and his [[party]] to access [[Bowser's Castle]]. While flying through the skies on the Star Cruiser, other citizens see it as a shooting star and they make some wishes upon it. Soon, the Star Cruiser lands in Bowser's Castle at the entrance. Mario can leave Bowser's Castle at any time by riding on the Star Cruiser.

The Star Cruiser is later seen during the parade at the end. Mario's partners are seen on top of it doing different poses.
