'''Pak E. Derms''' are an elephant enemy featured in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. Aside from this, they have also made a few cameos in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]].

==In ''Yoshi's Story''==
Pak E. Derms only appear in the first [[level]], [[Treasure Hunt]]. Here, they carry a large stop sign in their trunk to try and stop [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] from getting past them. Pak E. Derms do not utilize any attack, making them more of an obstacle. To bypass a Pak E. Derm, Yoshi must [[Ground Pound]] right next to it, causing the Pak E. Derm to fall over very briefly.
