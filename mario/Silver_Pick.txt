A '''Silver Pick''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
It is a small, silver fork with two prongs and a [[Spike]] on the handle.

==In ''Super Mario Party''==
The Silver Pick is exclusively usable on [[Megafruit Paradise|Watermelon Walkabout]] in the Partner Party mode. Here, when a character with the Silver Pick lands on any of the three "To the plateau!" [[Event Space]]s, the Silver Pick sticks on the slice of watermelon in front, raising it upwards and allowing the character to climb onto a higher, square-shaped area that has a few [[coin]]s, [[Red Coin]]s, and a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]].
