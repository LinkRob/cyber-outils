The '''Super Blue Watermelon''' is an item available in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. It is one of the rewards for clearing a minigame. The Super Blue Watermelon is essentially an [[ice watermelon]] in the form of a [[Special Item]], as using a Super Blue Watermelon from the Pause screen puts an ice watermelon in the playable [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s mouth. A Super Blue Watermelon can shoot up to nine blasts of ice unlike regular ice watermelons, which can only shoot three blasts. Similarly, the [[Super Red Watermelon]] grants nine blasts of fire from a [[fire watermelon]] instead of just three.
