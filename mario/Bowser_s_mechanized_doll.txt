[[Bowser (Super Mario-kun)|Bowser]] owns a '''mechanized doll''' that made its appearance in ''[[Super Mario-kun Vol 1 1]]''.

== History ==
=== Save Princess Peach!! Start of a new Adventure!! ===
After Bowser kidnapped [[Princess Peach (Super Mario-kun)|Princess Peach]], he sent a mechanized doll to speak to [[Mario (Super Mario-kun)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Super Mario-kun)|Luigi]] that he kidnapped the princess. After Bowser finishes his talk with Mario and Luigi, the doll self-destructs in front of the Mario brothers.
