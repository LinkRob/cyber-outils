''''' Super Mario Sound Collection: Happy! Mario 20th Super Mario Bros. [Nintendo DREAM Vol. 139]''''', alternatively titled '''''Happy! Mario 20th - Super Mario Sound Collection''''', is a soundtrack consisting of music from the first three {{series|Super Mario}} games (''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'') as well as ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It only came bundled with the 139th issue of ''Nintendo Dream'', and it was exclusively released in [[Japan]] to celebrate the {{series|Super Mario}}'s [[Happy! Mario 20th|20th anniversary]] there.
