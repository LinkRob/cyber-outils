A '''completion ticket''' is an item in [[Adventure Tours]] mode of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for [[Nintendo DS]].

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
In [[Frostown]], a [[White Shy Guy]] gives a completion ticket to [[Mario]] or [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] if any of the player's characters lose at an event a certain amount of times. A completion ticket can be used by pressing {{button|ds|L}} on the mission attempt screen, and in doing so, the mission is skipped. If a completion ticket is used, the mission board will display a black star instead of a silver or gold star.
