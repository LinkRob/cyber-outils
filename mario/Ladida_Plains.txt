'''Ladida Plains''' is the first world of ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', located in [[Vibe Island]]. Perry will teach Peach how to use him as a weapon and use Peach's [[vibe]]s. The boss of this world is [[Petey Piranha|Boss P. Plant]].
