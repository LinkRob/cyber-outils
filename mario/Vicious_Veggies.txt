'''Vicious Veggies''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' As one of [[Kat]] and [[Ana]]'s microgames, it can be found in the Tap Out level in that game. In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', the microgame is now in [[Shag]]'s folder and can be encountered in both the Dancing Team and Potluck Stages due to its genre being changed to "Pop-up."

== Layout ==
The microgame's protagonist is an astronaut who wears a blue and white space suit. The Earth is positioned behind his back, with the microgame taking place in outer space just outside the atmosphere of the Earth. In ''WarioWare Gold'', he has a [[Fronk]] mask.

== Gameplay ==
To win, the player has to time their press of the A button so that the astronaut can repel the vegetables that are attacking the Earth. If the vegetable reaches the Earth, the player loses.
