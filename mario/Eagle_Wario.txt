'''Eagle Wario''' is a transformation of [[Wario]]'s which only appears in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. It allows Wario to fly a short distance and move faster while [[Dash Attack|barging]]. Aside from this, Eagle Wario is lighter, and can escape from quicksand fairly quickly.
