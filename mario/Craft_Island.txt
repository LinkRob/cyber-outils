}}'''Craft Island''' is a location found in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. It is located in the [[Handmade Ocean]] and serves as the central hub of the game. The [[Yoshi Hut]], the [[Amiibo Hut]], and the [[Scapbook Theater]] can also be found around Craft Island. A fourth building, called the [[Boss Tent]], can also be unlocked after defeating the final boss.
