}}A '''Tileoid Y''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Tileoid Ys are members of the [[Tileoid]] family that are found in the [[Whoa Zone]] and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Tileoid Ys move around very fast on the floors, walls, and ceilings. Coming into contact with one will result in being damaged.
