'''New York Minute 1''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', first appearing in the New York Tour. It is the first route of New York Minute, hence the number.

== Layout ==
Much like [[New York Minute 2]], New York Minute 1 brings New York City's Central Park into its focus. The racers start in front of Times Square in between two three-way intersections, with a Y-shaped junction in front and ahead of a T-shaped junction. When the racers get the green light, they take a right at the Y-shaped junction, then make a slight left. Afterwards, the racers go through Central Park, making a 270-degree wide left turn. The road curves to the right later, with a four-way intersection up ahead; the racers go straight forwards, with the Rockefeller Centre up ahead. Due to the entrance being blocked, they turn left, then turn right when they reach a road with cars, the Empire State Building up ahead. The racers make a sharp right turn to go down another street, whereupon they encounter the T-intersection that they started in front; the racers make a right turn there to get back to the finish line.

The [[Booster Course Pass]] exclusive to the [[Nintendo Switch|Switch]] version of ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' has this route being the route that the racers use to traverse [[New York Minute]]'s first lap.
