|games =''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' }}'''Merlee's Mansion''' is a location in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It serves as the setting for Chapter's 2-2 and 2-3 and is located within [[Gloam Valley]]. This eventually leads to the [[Merlee's Basement|basement]] at the end. During the events of Chapter 2, [[Mimi]] takes over Merlee's Mansion where [[Merlee]] hid in the toilet in the female bathroom.

==Description==
Merlee's Mansion is a large mansion with elegant decor. It contains many rooms and a safe in the second part of it. Merlee has also large pets called [[Gnip]]s that will kick out any intruder, but only for their own safety. Her largest pet, [[Gnaw]], her favorite and watches over the door leading to the safe area.

==History==
===Chapter 2-2: Tricks, Treats, Traps===
Mario and company reached Merlee's Mansion but cannot find Merlee. Mario and company passed by Gnips. Mario and the others run into Mimi who was Merlee's handmaid. Mimi tells Mario and the others that Merlee is in the far upper right door but warned them not to go into any of the other doors. The group ended up findin traps, but managed to et the [[House Key (Super Paper Mario)|House Key]]. Mario has unlocked the door that also freed Gnaw who started to chase Mimi. Tippi wondered why Gnaw chased Mimi. The group proceeded further into the mansion.

===Chapter 2-3: Breaking the Bank===
Unfortunately, the trip further through Merlee's Mansion was halted after Mario accidentally destroyed an expensive vase. Furious Mimi (who has somehow evaded Gnaw) rushes out and orders Mario to pay 1,000,000 [[Rubee]]s for it. However, seeing this took awhile, Mario and company searched around for some answers. There were hints toward a safe upstairs that contained many Rubees inside blocked by electric barriers. However, after searching, Mario and company run into another Pixl, [[Slim]], whose ability helped them to get past the electric barriers. Mario and company cracked the safe with the correct code. Mario and company went to the [[Rubee Savings and Loan]] to pay off teir debt for Mimi. However, it surprised Mimi that broke her curse and mysteriously exploded. Mario and company found out she put a curse in the mansion, Tippi questioned further who was Mimi, then they trek down to the [[Merlee's Basement|basement]] to find Merlee.
