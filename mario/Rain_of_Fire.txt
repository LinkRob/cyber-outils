'''Rain of Fire''' is a Bowser [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. Its name is a pun on the title of Disney's {{wp|Touchstone Pictures|Touchstone}} film ''{{wp|Reign of Fire (film)|Reign of Fire}}''.

==Opening==
[[File:Rain of Fire MP5 - Bowser lighting cannon.png|thumb|left|Bowser breathes fire at the fuse of the cannon.]]
This minigame, like all other [[Bowser]] minigames in ''Mario Party 5'', takes place on a purple mountainous statue of Bowser, this time on the head. Bowser [[Fire Breath|breathes fire]] at the fuse as it burns to the cannon. Subsequently, a firework shoots high into the air and explodes, starting the minigame.

==Gameplay==
As the fireworks rain down, players must avoid the flaming debris. Players must watch the shadows in order to avoid the debris as it falls. Even after it has touched the ground, it still remains there for about a second before disappearing. Players must dodge two waves of flames in this manner. Touching just one piece of debris eliminates the player. Any players that survive both waves of flames are safe from Bowser.
