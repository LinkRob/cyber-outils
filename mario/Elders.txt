The '''Elders''' are five major supporting protagonists in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. They can be climbed upon by Donkey Kong. 

== Appearance ==
The Elders are all giant creatures with bananas for hair or feathers. They all wear headphones and sit on a large throne in front of a disc. They are each the species of whoever lives in their layer. All the males (Kong, Zebra, and Snake) have beards made of bananas.
