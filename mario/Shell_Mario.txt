'''Shell Mario''' and '''Shell Luigi'''<ref>''New Super Mario Bros.'' instruction booklet. Page 24.</ref> (also known as '''Koopa Mario''' and '''Koopa Luigi''') is a power-up which first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Description==
[[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can become his Shell form by wearing a [[Blue Shell]]. While the appearance is consistent, the abilities of Shell Mario are different entirely from ''New Super Mario Bros.'' to ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', though both involve entering the shell in some form. ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' references Shell Mario's abilities by giving the player the ability to enter shells briefly.

==''New Super Mario Bros.''==
The main ability of Shell Mario or Shell Luigi is the [[Shell Dash]], which involves him sliding around in his shell, which can be done automatically by either running briefly or crouching on a downward [[slope]], helping him to defeat enemies (excluding [[Whomp]]s) and break [[Brick Block]]s. Both [[Triple Jump]]s and [[Double Jump (consecutive)|Double Jump]]s are harder for Shell Mario to perform because the consecutive jumps before Shell Mario is forced into his shell.

Shell Mario has some other abilities. He can [[swim]] faster, like [[Frog Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', and Mario can also [[duck]] into the shell, like [[Hammer Mario]], another power-up from said game. Shell Mario is safe from most attacks while ducking except [[Bob-omb]] explosions, [[Spiked Ball]]s, [[hammer]]s thrown by [[Hammer Bro]]s. or [[Sledge Bro]]s., [[boomerang]]s from [[Boomerang Bro]]s., and the [[volcanic debris]] from [[World 8-8 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8-8]]. If Shell Mario falls into water while spinning, he begins to swim. Shell Mario can change to any other power-up by touching its item, except the lower tier [[Super Mushroom]],

==''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
In the skies of [[Toad Town]], after Mario or Luigi strikes the first [[Shell Block]] there, the [[Koopa Paratroopa Trio]] arrive to explain Blue Shells and the resulting Shell form. Mario is the only character who can become his Shell form. Shell Mario wears a heavy Blue Shell, so if he [[jump]]s while wearing it, he loses the shell.

On the field, Shell Mario can be hit by Luigi's hammer, causing Shell Mario to spin a short, straight distance before returning to his starting position. Shell Mario is required to hit certain [[block]]s and levers that are unreachable otherwise.
