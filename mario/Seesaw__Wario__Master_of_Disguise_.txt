A '''Seesaw''' is an object that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
Seesaws can only be found in [[Ancient Waterworks]] throughout certain parts of the level. The Seesaws can be activated by using [[Arty Wario]]'s blocks on the opposite side from [[Wario]] in order to propel him upwards. This is most notably used in the boss fight with [[Ka-Bloom!]] in order to chase it to the upper area of the arena in order to damage it.
