'''Mario Chase''' is a minigame that can be found in ''[[Nintendo Land]]''. This minigames revolve around Miis dressed as Toads and a Mii dressed as Mario. The Miis dressed as [[Toad (species)|Toad]] have the goal of chasing and catching the [[Mii]] dressed as [[Mario]], and the Mario Mii had thw goal of surviving until the time runs out. Deoending on how many Toad characters there are, there can also be [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] that help the Toads by stunning Mario and announcing his location, but they only appear if there aren't many Toads. The courses have three levels, and each is split in to four sectors, helping the Toad(s) find Mario.
