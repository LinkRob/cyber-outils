'''Pine T. Jr.''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Pine T. Jr. is a young [[Toad (species)|Toad]] and is the son of [[Pine T.|Pine T]]. [[Mario]] takes on his [[Trouble Center|trouble]] titled "Help my Daddy!". Mario revives his father on floor 18 of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] and earns the [[Silver Card]] which can be used at the [[Pianta Parlor]] in [[Rogueport]].

In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]] of this game, Pine T. Jr. sends an email to Mario saying how his father got a job caring for the [[Li'l-Oink]]s of [[Li'l-Oink Farm]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. Pine T. Jr. says he still isn't good at studying, but still intends to do so to help out his father.
