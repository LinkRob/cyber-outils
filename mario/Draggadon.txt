'''Draggadon''' is a large [[dragon]] who appears as a boss in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' and returns in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. It has a larger variant named [[Gold Draggadon]].

== In ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''==
Draggadon is fought as a boss a few times.

With Draggadon defeated by [[Toadette]], looking for [[Captain Toad]], Draggadon gave her a ride to leave Pyropuff peak.

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
After [[Larry Koopa]] has been defeated and the [[Big Orange Paint Star]], the Big Orange Paint Star sprinkles paint all over [[Prism Island]] and on Draggadon. At first, Draggadon was a fossil, but when the Big Orange Paint Star sprinkled the paint over him, Draggadon came to life. His homeland is [[Redpepper Volcano]].
