'''Star Way''' is a location which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Layout==
Star Road is mainly a large pathway made of stars that eventually leads up to [[Star Haven]]. The only enemies that are present are [[Ember]]s.

==History==
Star Way can be accessed once [[Mario]] rescues all seven of the [[Star Spirits]]. When Mario arrives at the top of [[Shooting Star Summit]], the Star Spirits inform Mario he must make his way to [[Star Haven]]. They create a path in the middle of the platform, which leads to Star Way. Star Way is a very short path composed of stars that eventually spiral upwards. Blue [[Ember]]s can be encountered on Star Way that commonly blend in with the atmosphere, but they should pose no threat to Mario when he makes his way there.
