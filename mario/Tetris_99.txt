'''''Tetris 99''''' is a ''Tetris'' game for the [[Nintendo Switch]]. It was first released in early 2019 as a digital download, and it later saw a physical release the same year. ''Tetris 99'' includes a variety of themes, including some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related ones, especially in the limited-time Maximus Cup events, which are themed after a certain game, such as ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' or ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.
