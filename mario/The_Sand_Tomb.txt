'''The Sand Tomb''' is a stadium in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
The stadium takes place in a desert, and [[Thwomp]]s appear in the middle of the stadium. There is also light quicksand on the side areas, and whoever runs on it is slowed down, except the [[Kritter]] goalies. Like most other stadiums, The Sand Tomb features an [[electric rail]] on the side, which temporarily stuns whoever touches it.
