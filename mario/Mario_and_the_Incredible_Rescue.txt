'''''Mario and the Incredible Rescue''''' is a short chapter book written by Tracey West and published by [[Scholastic]] in January 2006. It is part of the ''Nintendo Heroes'' series of books, and features settings from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' alike.

==Synopsis==
[[Bowser]] uses an old [[The Book of Spells|Book of Spells]] to create a hoard of [[Boo]]s, which attack the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. He then kidnaps [[Princess Peach]] while her [[Toad (species)|guards]] are besieged by the specters. The only way [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can get past the ghosts guarding [[Bowser's Keep]] and save Peach is by collecting six special [[Mushroom]]s.

==Stickers==
Similar to other ''Nintendo Heroes'' books, ''Mario and the Incredible Rescue'' came included with a series of stickers. The stickers depict artwork from either ''Super Mario 64'' or one of the earlier ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' games, including ''[[Mario Party]]'', ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', and ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.
