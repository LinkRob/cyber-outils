'''Hot Cogs''' is the third level of Peach's Castle in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. 

==Layout==
The player starts in a lava cave with a [[Cog (obstacle)|Cog]] turning clockwise, and a [[Podoboo]] is jumping around the gear. A [[? Block]] containing a power-up is found, and is followed by another gear and Podoboo. A section of the ground is found, and a [[Goombrat]] is found on it. Another Goombrat is found, along with a row of Podoboos below a row of [[Brick Block]]s with a [[Red Ring]] above them. Another section of gears follows, with more sideways-jumping Podoboos found. Another section of the ground follows, along with a Goombrat and some Podoboos. Six more gears and some Podoboos are found in the section leading to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': At the very first gear, the player must wait for it to rotate a bit to reveal the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the second gear, the player must head to the area below the arrow sign and continue moving right. The Star Coin is located over a gear, almost completely submerged in lava.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The player must have an [[Ice Flower]]. Then, when they player encounters the third Goombrat, they must use Ice balls to defeat the Podoboos, accessing the Star Coin.

==[[List of Luigi sightings in New Super Luigi U|Luigi Sighting]]==
*When Luigi reaches an arrow sign, he must break the hidden Brick Blocks to reveal a crouching 8-bit Luigi.
