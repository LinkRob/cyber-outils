'''Pole Block Passage''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level features many [[Pole Block]]s. The level opens with the then unfamiliar pole block, forcing the player to hit the block as there is no other way to progress. The rest of the level uses the pole block in different ways.

Later in the level is another pole block. When hit instead of spawning a pole, it will spawn the [[Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will summon various long poles that bend. In addition, five [[Wonder Token]]s spawn that the player will need to collect to obtain the [[Wonder Seed]] and end the wonder effect.

==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s can be obtained in the following order.
#Before the first yellow [[pipe]], is 10-flower coin that can be easily reached by hitting the pole block to the right.
#A 10-flower coin is hovering over a [[pit]]. Hitting the pole block that is over the pit will allow the player to hang down to reach the 10-flower coin.
#Shortly after the previous 10-flower coin, is a pole block that is too high to reach by [[jump]]ing. Grabbing a [[Koopa Shell]] and throwing the shell upwards will allow the player to enter a pipe, which will send the player to a secret area. In the area, there are two pole blocks. Hitting either and climbing upwards will give access to a 10-flower coin.
