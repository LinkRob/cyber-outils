'''Emperor Ed''' is an elderly [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who featured as the ruler of [[Sky Land]] in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Up, Up, and a Koopa]]." He has not made any other appearances. During the episode, Emperor Ed is kidnapped by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and [[Ludwig von Koopa|Kooky von Koopa]] in their scheme to take over Sky Land.
