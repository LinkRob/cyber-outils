'''13-Amp''' is a character who appears in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. She first appears in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Profiles==
===Physical description===
13-Amp is a teenage girl with bright green eyes, puckered lips, and long, floor-length silver hair. Her clothing includes dark blue pants, a white shirt with dark blue sleeves, a pair of purple shoes, a purple wristband on each wrist, and a purple cap with a dark blue bill and the number 13, referencing her name.

===Powers and abilities===
13-Amp is a skilled rapper, and she boasts herself as being a "rapping phenomenon." In 13-Amp's rank C character card, it is said that she is the best rapper in her high school, although keeps it a secret from her parents because they may not approve of it.

==In the ''WarioWare'' series==
===''WarioWare Gold''===
13-Amp first and most notably appears during [[18-Volt]]'s segment. In the opening, on [[Emerald Street]], 13-Amp stole [[Ralphie]]'s [[Nintendo 3DS]], and when 18-Volt comes, Ralphie asks him to get it back from her. 13-Amp is challenged to a rap battle by 18-Volt, a skilled rapper like her, for Ralphie's games. The conditions are that if 13-Amp loses the rap battle, she has to return Ralphie's games to him, but if 18-Volt loses, she gets to take his games as well. As the player plays through 18-Volt's microgame set, 13-Amp is seen rapping with 18-Volt, with [[9-Volt]] appearing as the DJ in the background. Once the microgame set has been completed, 13-Amp and 18-Volt leave, both hoping to do a rematch again soon. 13-Amp is shocked that she was bested by 18-Volt when he tells her that he is in fourth grade.

13-Amp makes her second appearance as the DJ for [[Club Joe]] during the first stage of the Ultra League.

13-Amp makes her brief third appearance during the end credits in an image, where she is holding a Nintendo 3DS while standing next to 18-Volt.

===''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''===
She has a more minor role. 13-Amp is seen with 18-Volt in some images in his gallery. Here, the two of them are shown together on friendlier terms, suggesting that they had become friends some time after they first met in ''WarioWare Gold''.
