The '''Town Game Room A''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Town Game Room A is a gambling hall that appears in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], next to a similar location, [[Town Game Room B]]. The background of Town Game Room A shows pink and yellow electronic slot machines in front of a dark checkered wall with neon stars above them. [[Monty Mole (character)|Monty Mole]] frequently drops by here to gamble.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
[[File:Monty Mole rejoicing MPA.png|thumb|left|200px|Monty Mole rejoicing after the player wins Scratch 'Em during the Winners Keepers quest in ''Mario Party Advance'']]
In Shroom City mode, the quest [[Winners Keepers]] takes place in Town Game Room A, where the player has to win the gambling minigame [[Scratch 'Em]] on Monty Mole's behalf, by ending with at least one more [[coin]] than what they began with. Once this is done, the player the minigame itself is unlocked as a reward.
