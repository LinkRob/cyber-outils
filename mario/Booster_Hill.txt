'''Booster Hill''' is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
Booster Hill is mainly a very long and large hill.

==History==
After defeating [[Knife Guy]] and [[Grate Guy]], Mario chases after [[Booster (character)|Booster]] who has ran away with [[Princess Toadstool]]. However, Booster runs fast and has [[barrel]]s and his [[Snifit]] minions to slow Mario down. Mario manages to avoid danger and chases Booster into [[Marrymore]].

Mario can also come back here after reaching [[Seaside Town]], in order to play the [[Beetle Race]] minigame where he has to catch [[Beetle (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|beetles]]. Mario must have the [[Beetle Box]] in order to compete in the Beetle Race.

==Trivia==
*If one looks closely, [[Bowser's Keep]], the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], and [[Barrel Volcano]] can be briefly seen.
*Booster Hill is the second location in the game to be mainly a "mini-game" with the first being [[Midas River]].
