'''Multi-Vators''' are a type of [[platform]] introduced in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''

==Description==
Multi-Vators are yellow platforms with an orange, single-digit number printed on it to indicate the minimum number of characters who have to stand on the Multi-Vator to activate it. Upon being activated, the Multi-Vator moves, usually to a hidden area or collectible. Multi-Vators generally appear in courses with [[Double Cherry|Double Cherries]] so that a character can use [[Double Mario|clones]] to meet the requirement of a Multi-Vator by obtaining Double Cherries. In ''Super Mario 3D World'' and ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury'', Multi-Vators are usually easier to activate in multiplayer mode, which involves two to four players playing the game.

==In ''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''==
The first Multi-Vator appears at the end of [[Double Cherry Pass]], and it requires four characters to ride it to the level's third [[Green Star]].

[[Beep Block Skyway]] is the next course to have a Multi-Vator, which is located near the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. It requires four characters to stand on it, like the Multi-Vator of Double Cherry Pass, and using this Multi-Vator leads to the [[Stamp]].

Three Multi-Vators appear in [[Backstreet Bustle]], each having a different number requirement: the one requiring two characters goes from the beginning to the area in the middle; the Multi-Vator requiring three characters leads to the third [[Green Power Star|Green Star]]; and the Multi-Vator requiring four characters leads to the second Green Star.

Near the end of [[Cakewalk Flip]] is a Multi-Vator that requires two characters, and using it leads to the course's Stamp.

Near the end of [[Rolling Ride Run]] is a Multi-Vator that requires two characters, and using it leads to the third Green Star. Given its two-character requirement and appearing near the end, this Multi-Vator's purpose can be considered similar to that of Cakewalk Flip's.

==In ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''==
A single two-character Multi-Vator appears in [[Double Cherry Spires]], where it is used to obtain the [[Power Star]]. Multi-Vators also appear throughout [[Multi-Vator Mayhem]], primarily for [[Captain Toad]] to progress throughout the level, and these Multi-Vators are retained in [[Boo Spotting at Multi-Vator Mayhem]].
