The '''Big-DEF Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is the [[Defense]]-based counterpart of the [[Big-POW Badge]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''==
The Big-DEF Badge can be bought for 400 [[coin]]s at the [[Shroom Shop]], and there is also one in a [[? Block]] in [[Gritzy Caves]]. Using the Big-DEF Badge temporarily adds defense when getting a "Great" when using a [[Bros. Item]].
