'''''Mario Calculator''''' is a calculator application released as a [[DSiWare]] title in 2009 for [[Nintendo DSi]] units, then [[reissue|ported]] to the [[Nintendo 3DS]]'s [[Nintendo eShop]] at launch, on June 6, 2011. ''Mario Calculator'' is the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' equivalent to ''[[Animal Crossing]]''{{'}}s ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing Calculator|Animal Crossing Calculator]]'', and has a clock application counterpart titled ''[[Mario Clock]]''. Both ''Mario Calculator'' and ''Mario Clock'' reuse graphics and sound effects from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

Aside from serving as a calculator, ''Mario Calculator'' also features a unit converter.
