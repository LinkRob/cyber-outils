'''Toad Cup''' is a [[Cup]] from the [[Mario Kart (series)]] that features [[Toad (character)|Toad's]] Icon on it to represent him.  

== In ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]'' ==
The cup first apeared in [[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]] that apears as the first one you can can chose, despite the name, neither of the two cups hosted apeared to be based on [[Toad (character)|Toad]], but instead based on [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. This cup features: [[Peach Castle (Mario Kart Arcade GP DX)|Peach Castle]] and [[Kingdom Way]]. 

== In [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] ==
In [[Mario Kart Tour]] the Toad cup returns as it begins in [[New York Tour]]. These cups are mainly favored or favorite for [[Toad (character)|Toad]].
