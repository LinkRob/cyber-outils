The '''A Midsummer Night's Dream Op. 61: Wedding March''' is an instrumental song composed by Felix Mendelssohn. It was used for [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]'s, and [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]]'s forced wedding at [[Bowser's Castle (Illumination)|Bowser's Castle]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.
