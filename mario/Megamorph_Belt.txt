The '''Megamorph Belt''' is one of [[Bowser]]'s weapons in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Description==
Unlike with the [[Minimizer]], Bowser does not reveal it until close to the very end of the game, after [[Donkey Kong]] accidentally breaks the Minimizer by bumping into Bowser and stepping on it. Bowser uses the Megamorph Belt's power to transform into [[Blockhead Bowser]], a form of blocks encasing Bowser. The weak spot of Blockhead Bowser is the golden Bowser Block, which the player has to attack to defeat Bowser in [[Bowser's Block Party]].
