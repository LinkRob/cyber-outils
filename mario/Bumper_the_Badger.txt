'''Bumper the Badger''' is a playable character in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]'' who appears to be a badger with black and gray fur and a red belt with red gloves on. He also wears a helmet. Bumper, along with the others, were called to save [[Timber's Island]] from the evil [[Wizpig]].
