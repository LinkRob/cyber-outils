'''Spiky Topman''' is variant of [[Topman]] which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
The Spiky Topman is a red Topman with a spike on its head, making it the most harmful Topman variant.

=== Behavior ===
Spiky Topmen are found near shock barriers. They will attempt to push the victim to the barriers. Their bumping attacks does not harm the victim. However, [[Mario]] can defeat them by perform a spin attack to push the Topmen to the barriers. Mario cannot stomp on the Topman due to the spike on their head that can harm him.

==Other appearances==
===''Mario Kart Wii''===
[[File:Spiky Topman MKWii screenshot.jpg|thumb|200px|left|[[Diddy Kong]] facing off against a Spiky Topman in ''Mario Kart Wii'']]
Topmans also appear in an online event on the [[Galaxy Colosseum]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. The object was for the player to push all the Spiky Topmen off the stage, and more Spiky Topmen appear in each wave. Since the discontinuation of [[Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection]] on May 20, 2014, the Galaxy Colosseum, and therefore Spiky Topmen, can no longer be accessed in the game without the use of modded software such as Wiimmfi.
