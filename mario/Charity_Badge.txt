The '''Charity Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in both ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Charity Badge is sold at the clothes shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]]. It can be bought for 140 [[coin]]s, and it increases either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s [[Power|POW]] by 15 (16 in the Japanese version).

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Charity Badge becomes available for purchase after defeating the [[Chuckolator]]. It is still priced at 140 coins, but now increases POW by 23, as well as [[BP]] by 5 and [[HP]] by 10. The Charity Badge can be sold for 35 coins.
