'''Lakitus in Pipes''' are a variant of [[Lakitu]] that dwell inside of certain [[Warp Pipe]]s. Lakitus in Pipes similarly toss [[Spiny Egg]]s at [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], and have made their only appearance in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. Mario or Luigi can defeat a Lakitu in a Pipe by [[jump]]ing onto it, earning the player 200 [[point]]s. Lakitus in Pipes are encountered in [[Vanilla Secret 2]] and [[Forest of Illusion 4]].

Lakitus in Pipes have green eyes unlike their regular counterpart. This distinction is also shared by the [[Lakitu (referee)|Lakitu]] referee in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'', whose head is essentially the same as that of a Lakitu in a Pipe's.
