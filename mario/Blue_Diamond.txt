'''Blue Diamonds''', alternatively named '''Big Jewels''', are an item in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
Blue Diamonds are a type of [[treasure]] often found in green [[Treasure Chest]]s, along with two [[Gold Bar]]s. [[Bill]]s, and [[Coin]]s, after vacuuming up an optional [[Portrait Ghost]]. Each Blue Diamond is worth 2,000,000G.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
There are a total of five Blue Diamonds found in the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]]. The first Blue Diamond can be obtained in the [[Dining Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Dining Room]].

In the PAL releases' [[Hidden Mansion]], there are instead thirty Blue Diamonds, as [[Speedy Spirit]]s and [[Gold Mouse|Gold Mice]] now always drop a Blue Diamond upon being vacuumed into the [[Poltergust 3000]].
