==Appearance==

The Power Platform is a large arena that has the appearance of [[Bowser]]'s [[Koopa Clown Car]].

==History==

After [[Mario]] defeats Bowser after using the [[Star Spirits]] to nullify the power of the [[Star Rod]], Bowser retreats with [[Princess Peach]] to the Power Platform. After luring Mario to it, Bowser has [[Kammy Koopa]] use her magic to power the Power Platform and ends up increasing Bowser's sjze and power. Thanks to the power-up, Bowser was no longer affected by the [[Star Beam]]. However, the Star Beam was eventually upgraded to the Peach Beam and Bowser was defeated. Due to all of the power used, the Power Platform self-destructs sending Bowser and Kammy Koopa flying off and even destroys [[Bowser's Castle]] in the process.
