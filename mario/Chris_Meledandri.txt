'''Chris Meledandri''' is the CEO of [[Illumination Entertainment]]. He is an American producer and writer known for ''Despicable Me'' (2010), ''The Lorax'' (2012), ''Despicable Me 2'' (2013), ''The Secret Life of Pets'' (2016) and ''Sing'' (2016). Before founding Illumination, he worked for 20th Century Fox's animation division where he oversaw the production of ''Anastasia'' (1997), ''Titan A.E.'' (2000), ''Ice Age'' (2002), ''Robots'' (2005), ''Ice Age: The Meltdown'' (2006) and ''Horton Hears A Who!'' (2008), after that he left 20th Century Fox for Universal to found Illumination.
