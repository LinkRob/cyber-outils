'''Land of Flying Blocks''' is the first [[level]] of [[Meringue Clouds]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Flight of the Para-Beetles]] or [[Roy's Conveyor Castle]] and its own completion unlocks a red Toad House, [[Seesaw Shrooms]], and [[Switchback Hill]].

==Overview==
The course's main gimmicks are blocks that appear when the player reaches a certain spot, and a ridable cloud that the player must use to navigate across the course.

==Walkthrough==
The level begins with some [[Waddlewing]]s, as well as [[Mushroom Platform]]s. A cloud platform is then found, and is used to navigate across the area. Throughout the area, multiple [[coin]]s in bubbles and blocks that appear when the player reaches a certain spot are found. Some [[Piranha Plant]]s are found in [[Warp Pipe]]s, and one if found on a [[lift]]. Some structures of [[Brick Block]]s appear, along with more Waddlewings and a Piranha Plant on a lift. A [[Trampoline]] is found in a [[? Block]]. A group of Piranha Plants in Warp Pipes then follows. The cloud then reaches a group of Mushroom Platforms, and a [[Warp Pipe]] is at the end of the area. The following area begins with the [[Checkpoint Flag]] and another moving cloud platform. A [[Big Piranha Plant]] is located above, and a Koopa Troopa and another Big Piranha Plant are on lifts. A large structure of Brick Blocks appears, as well as a [[Red Ring]]. Another Mushroom Platform is found, along with another cloud. It moves through a section containing multiple Big Piranha Plants, and leads to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
* '''Star Coin 1''': In a series of pipes and Piranha Plants is a lone red Warp Pipe which leads to the area with the first Star Coin. Flying Squirrel Mario is required to get this Star Coin.
* '''Star Coin 2''': Immediately after the first Star Coin, above a trio of Brick Blocks.
* '''Star Coin 3''': Blocked off by a group of Brick Blocks. The player needs to destroy the blocks with a Ground Pound in order to get the third Star Coin.
