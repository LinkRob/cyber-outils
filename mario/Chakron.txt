'''Chakron''' is a character from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. He is the third and final Star Stage, the first two being [[Wisdurm]] and [[Kuzzle]] in order. He is found on top of a mountain in [[Plack Beach]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Chakron is an octopus with eight pink arms and lips. He has a round sphere face and a gold star on his forehead.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
[[File:Chakroad M&LBIS screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Chakron's voice in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'']]
Chakron makes his debut during the Bowser-wakes-up-in-[[Cavi Cape|Cavi-Cape]] chapter, contacting [[Bowser]] to explain to him the purpose of [[Chakroad]]s, when Bowser first discovers one in [[Cavi Cape]]. After the Mario Bros. get the first two [[Star Cure]]s, they are told by [[Dr. Toadley]] to climb a large mountain whose top houses Chakron's abode in [[Plack Beach]]. The two make their way here, finding him at the top Chakron explains that he has frozen in a pose for 1,000 years, gathering knowledge from the stars. He offers them the third Star Cure if they can use an attack that would shake the ground and break his pose. After unsuccessfully trying to imitate Chakron's pose, Luigi tries to tickle Chakron, to the annoyance of the latter.

Mario and Luigi have to battle Chakron at first, to try and free Chakron from his pose. However, the match ends after three turns, since it is not successful. Chakron informs the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] that there is an attack in [[Dimble Wood]], [[Snack Basket]], to use on him in battle and free Chakron of his pose. However, the Mario Bros. are required to get an Excellent rating in order to win the battle. After the attack is used, Chakron will flip out of control and fall back on the ground, then he will then slither towards the edge and fall off. Chakron survives and he finds a new pose that satisfies him, which he plans to hold for another 100 years. Chakron then gives Mario and Luigi the last Star Cure and offers to teleport them back to [[Toad Town]]. Talking to him thereafter will also trigger this. Should the player accept, the camera pans up and then down, showing that everyone except Chakron vanished. It then cuts to Toad Town where the Mario Bros. are shown materialising.

Chakron last appears during the ending, after Peach's cake is shown, and his mysterious voice appears, congratulating the player. He shows the player's ranks. Unless the player reached Rainbow Rank (Final Boss Rank for Bowser) for everyone, Chakron claims to be less than amazed, but gives the player another chance to try again.
