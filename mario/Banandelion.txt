'''Banandelions''', alternatively spelled '''banande lions''' and also referred to as '''dandelions''', are tall flowers appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]'', ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. When a Banandelion is spun next to, this gives the player a jump boost.
