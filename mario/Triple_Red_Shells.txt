The '''Triple Red Shells''' are an item in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

==Description==
The Triple Red Shells are the [[Red Shell]] equivalent of the [[Green Shell]]. By using the Triple Red Shells, three [[Red Shell]]s circle the user's kart, acting as a shield that can block incoming items, namely Green Shells or Red Shells, but it cannot block a [[Blue Spiny Shell]]. With the Triple Red Shells out, the player can use the Red Shells individually.

In ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', the Triple Red Shells are a [[Special Item]] of [[Koopa Troopa]] and [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]], as well as [[Piranha Plant]] and [[King Boo]], who can use nearly any Special Item.
