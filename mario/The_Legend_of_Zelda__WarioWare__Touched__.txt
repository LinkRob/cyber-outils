'''The Legend of Zelda''' is one of [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s featured in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', within the [[Retro Action]] set. The microgame is based on both the first ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'' and ''[[w:c:zelda:Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must use their {{button|ds|stylus}} to tap any [[Fairy (The Legend of Zelda)|fairy]] onscreen to catch them and restore [[Link]]'s health.

In most cases, when the player completes the microgame, a part of the environment moves away, revealing Link hold up his [[Nintendo DS]] treasure triumphantly. If the Level 1 is themed after ''Zelda II: The Adventure of Link'' is completed, Link is shown briefly running by.

=== Level difficulties ===
* '''Level 1''': The player must catch one Fairy in a setting based on either ''The Legend of Zelda'' or ''Zelda II: The Adventure of Link'', chosen by the game at random. 
* '''Level 2:''' Two moving Fairies must be caught in either a dungeon or the overworld, selected by the game at random.
* '''Level 3''': Similar to Level 2 except the dungeon features [[Like Like]]s while the overworld features [[Moblin]]s.
