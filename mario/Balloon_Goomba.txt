'''Balloon Goombas''' are enemies appearing in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' they are Goombas attached to a pink, blue or yellow balloons they float from the bottom of the screen all the way to the top if [[Mario]] jumps on a Balloon Goomba the balloon will burst becoming and acting like a regular [[Goomba]] they are only encountered in the level [[Skyward Stalk]]. Balloon Goombas are among the few enemies to not reappear in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
