'''Mega Quake''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is a more powerful variation of both the [[Quake Hammer]] and [[Power Quake]] badges. The badge does not return in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' because [[Mario]] can equip multiple Quake Hammer badges to be given the same effects of a Mega Quake.

==Effects==
Mario can wear Mega Quake by using three [[Badge Point|BP]]. In doing so, he can perform a special [[hammer]] attack for seven [[Flower Point|FP]] that affects all enemies in the battle.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Mario can acquire Mega Quake at [[Rowf's Badge Shop]] by paying 200 [[coin]]s. This badge can prove useful as it gives Mario an option to strike at all grounded enemies for a high amount of damage. This is especially useful against enemies such as [[Dark Koopa]]s and [[Spike Top]]s as it can not only damage through their high defenses, but flip them over their backs as well.
