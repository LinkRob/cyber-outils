'''Rolling Egg Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Yoshi hops into a [[Yoshi Egg]] and rolls over to the ball If players play the doubles tournament and complete both the [[Mushroom Cup]] and the [[Flower Cup]], they can power up the Rolling Egg Return to have Yoshi change into one of seven other colors (aside from green) every time he uses it, including [[Red Yoshi|red]], [[Yellow Yoshi|yellow]], [[Cyan Yoshi|light blue]], [[Blue Yoshi|blue]], [[Pink Yoshi|pink]], [[Black Yoshi|black]], and [[White Yoshi|white]].

==Trivia==
*Yoshi's action of rolling around in an Egg is similar to the [[Egg Roll (move)|Egg Roll]] move in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.
