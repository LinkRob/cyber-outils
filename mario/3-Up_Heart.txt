A '''3-Up Heart''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' and ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Description==
It is a large, heart-shaped item that is similar to the [[1-Up Heart]] except the 3-Up Heart grants three [[1-Up|extra lives]], much like the [[3-Up Moon]] from ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'', a 3-Up Heart has the base appearance of a 1-Up Heart, but with a "3" written on it. In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'', a 3-Up Heart has a blinking appearance.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
[[File:3-Up Heart SML2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
3-Up Hearts can only be obtained in [[minigame]]s.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
3-Up Hearts are instead found in hard-to-reach areas and secret rooms.
