'''Glare Desert''' is a court in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
Glare Desert is a basketball court taking place at a desert. Besides the environment, the only difference of Glare Desert from other basketball courts is that a [[tornado]] is its only stage hazard. The tornado moves along the field randomly, knocking away characters who touch it.

Glare Desert plays the same music as [[Mario Stadium (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Mario Stadium]].

==In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Glare Desert is one of the unlockable courts. The player can unlock it by scoring at least 800 [[point]]s while playing on [[Jr. Street]] in the [[Star Tourney]]. Glare Desert is classified as being a Star Tourney court, despite it never being played on during the Star Tourney.

Glare Desert is also where [[Cactuar]] can be unlocked. At first, Cactuar has his head stuck in the ground. After being dribbled on for a while, Cactuar pops up and runs away, then he is unlocked.
