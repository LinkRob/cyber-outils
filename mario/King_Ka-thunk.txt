'''King Ka-thunk''' is a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
As his name suggests, King Ka-thunk is a square-shaped creature attached to a large [[Ka-thunk]]. King Ka-thunk has a blue color matching that of a Ka-thunk's, and it has a set of seven golden spikes protruding at the front, one on each side and another in the middle. King Ka-thunk also has a pair of owl-like eyes surrounded by red outline, a set of four-fingered red hands, and red feet. King Ka-thunk's back appears to be pink and rubbery, and it has a white X-shaped bandage indicating that it is his vulnerable spot.

===Attacks===
*'''Ka-thunk Crush:''' He uses the Ka-thunk to try and crush the player(s). However, doing so also exposes King Ka-thunk's back, leaving it open to attack.
*'''Platform Drop:''' This attack is similar to the Ka-thunk crush, although this attack will cause platforms to fall into the void, creating less spaces for the player(s) to move.

==In ''Super Mario 3D World''==
[[File:King Ka-Thunk.png|thumb|220x220px|Kin Ka-thunk angered.]]
King Ka-thunk is the main boss of [[World 5 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 5]]. Here, he is encountered in [[King Ka-thunk's Castle]] at the end, where he holds a violet [[Sprixie Princesses|Sprixie Princess]] hostage. King Ka-thunk is the sixth boss overall, and he is later fought at in the [[Boss Blitz]].

=== Strategy ===
King Ka-thunk is rather straight-forward in terms of strategy; he can be defeated by either [[jump]]ing or [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] on his back after attempting to crush the player's character. A red shadow appears on the ground a few seconds before King Ka-thunk does his attack, indicating where he will land. As King Ka-thunk takes damage, the platforms fall, limiting the movement of the player's character.

== Trivia ==
* The battle agents King Ka-thunk is very similar to [[Whomp King]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', in that both battles are against larger versions of either an enemy or object that also has the ability to slam down on the player.
** Coincidentally, in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', Whomp King will have a red shadow that appears before preforming a slam, much like King Ka-thunk.
