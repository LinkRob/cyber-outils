'''Rubee Savings and Loan'''<ref>{{cite video game |title=[[Super Paper Mario]] |developer=[[Intelligent Systems]] |publisher=[[Nintendo]] |date=2009-04-09 |platform=[[Wii]] |level=Rubee Savings and Loan}}</ref> is a small bank located in [[Merlee's Mansion]]. It appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
The Rubee Savings and Loan is a small bank in [[Merlee's Mansion]]. [[Mimi]] owns the bank. The wall has a square clock and decorated with posters.

== History ==
{{Spoiler}}After [[Mario]] broke the vase, Mimi forced Mario and Peach to work off their one million Rubee debt. Mimi placed a curse that prevents Mario from returning to [[Flipside]]. Mario and [[Tippi]] talked to a mansion slave. He told Mario that he heard Mimi whisper the numbers in her sleep. The numbers hinted Mario and Tippi to unlock the safe with one million Rubees. Mario and Tippi went to Rubee Savings and Loans to pay their debt. Mimi realized Mario and Tippi broke the curse and fakes her death.

{{Endspoiler}}
