The '''Heart Bean''' is a consumable that made its first appearance in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

== Description ==
The Heart Bean is a bean that boosts stats' of a Mario brother's [[HP]].

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''/''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' ==
Heart Beans are functioned to boost the HP of a certain user.

In the remake, the Heart Bean is a pink bean to differentiate from the rest of the beans.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' ==
The Heart Bean is a pink bean that increases the HP stat of the user.
