'''O-Zone''' is a Duel [[minigame|mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
All the spaces are covered, then they are covered and spun around on the platform they are on in a 360-degree turn.

==Gameplay==
The minigame is somewhat based on {{w|tic-tac-toe}}. The characters have to [[Ground Pound (move)|ground-pound]] spaces an O under them. If a character ground-pounds a space with an "X," lightning bolts strike the character, stunning them for a few seconds. Some spaces do not have a letter underneath them, and ground-pounding them has no effect. It is possible for a character to ground-pound their opponent to stun them temporarily. Whoever gets three O's out of five on the board is the winner. If neither character ground-pounds three O's within the 60-second [[time limit]], the minigame ends in a [[Draw|tie]].

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: [[Jump]]
* {{button|gcn|A}} (while in midair): Ground-pound

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Ground-pound the stage panels to find circles! Be the first to find three to claim victory!''
* '''Advice''': ''Before the game begins, carefully study the stage to figure out which panels are circles.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on "{{w|ozone layer}}."
