'''Max Mushroom''' is a type of health-restoring item which appears in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]], having first appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

== Description ==
Max Mushrooms have the same appearance as a regular [[Mushroom]], much like with [[Super Mushroom]]s and [[Dash Mushroom]]s.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
A Max Mushroom fully restores the [[Heart Point|HP]] of either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]. At an [[Item Shop]], a Max Mushroom costs 180 [[coin]]s to purchase.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''==
The Max Mushroom retains its effects from ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' but is instead valued at a price of 80 coins.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''==
Max Mushrooms only restore up to 160 HP.
