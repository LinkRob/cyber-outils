'''A Speeding Bill''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
A Speeding Bill takes place in the [[Mushroom Stadium]], where it is provided by [[Bullet Bill (character)|Bullet Bill]]. It begins if the player's character agrees to challenge Bullet Bill, who boasts about his skills, claiming to have obtained a hundred medals, and asks the player if they think they can outrace him. By replying yes, Bullet Bill laughs but nonetheless offers a [[Gaddget]] as a reward for winning.

The scene transitions to a horizontal stadium track, where the player has to repeatedly press {{button|GBA|A}} until their character crosses the [[finish line]], which is a very short distance away from the starting point. If the player's character wins, Bullet Bill is stupefied, admits that he had been egoistical, and gives the player the [[Mini Soccer]] Gaddget. If the player loses, Bullet Bill continues boasting about his skill, telling the player's character to run away since they failed to get close to him.
