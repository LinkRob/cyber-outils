A '''Malakoopa''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] variant with a [[Green Shell]] only encountered in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]], including within [[The Trial Course]].

==Battle==
In battle, Malakoopas mainly attack with flying shell attacks, and they do not use any other special moves in battle.

===Strategy===
Malakoopas are weak against [[Thunder (element)|thunder]], so any of [[Mallow]]'s thunder attacks are effective against them. If [[Bowser]] is an active party member, the Malakoopa flees from battle.
