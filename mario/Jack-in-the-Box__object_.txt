==History==

Jack-in-the-Boxes are found throughout [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] in Chapter 4.  When [[Mario]] [[Ground Pound (move)|Spin Jumps]] a Jack-in-the-Box, it will spring out sending Mario into the air allowing him to access higher areas. They can also be hammered with Mario's hammer to set them off.
