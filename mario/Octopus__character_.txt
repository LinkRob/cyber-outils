'''Octopus''' is a giant octopus who is featured as the antagonist in the [[Game & Watch]] game of the [[Octopus (game)|same name]]. It later made its first appearance in a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game with ''[[Game & Watch Gallery]]'', which features both the original ''Octopus'' and a Modern remake starring [[Mario]].

==History==
===''Game & Watch Gallery'' series===
====''Game & Watch Gallery''====
[[File:G&WG Octopus Modern gameplay.png|thumb|left|Octopus's appearance in ''Octopus''{{'}}s Modern remake in ''Game & Watch Gallery'']]
Octopus appears in both the Classic and Modern remakes of ''Octopus'', the former of which is a recreation of the original Game & Watch release. Like the original game, ''Octopus'' involves Octopus guarding sunken treasure from the diver. While the diver is underwater, the Octopus attempts to grab them. If it is successful, the game registers a miss. The Modern version replaces the diver with Mario, who has an added move of throwing a treasure chest at the Octopus, temporarily stunning whichever of its tentacles it hits.

====''Game & Watch Gallery 4''====
[[File:GWG4 Octopus Modern gameplay.png|thumb|Octopus in the Modern remake of ''Octopus'' in ''Game & Watch Gallery 4'']]
In ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 4]]'', Octopus returns in the Classic and Modern versions of ''Octopus''. Aside from being graphically enhanced, Octopus is mostly the same in the Modern remake, but it is now either orange, green, or blue, and changes between one of these colors whenever Mario earns 200 [[point]]s.

In the game's preview animation for ''Octopus'', Mario arrives with a bag of loot in one hand and a [[Game Boy Advance]] in another. Two of Octopus's arms then surround him on the left as well as another two on the right. As Mario escapes with help from [[Lakitu]], each of Octopus's arms grabs one of Mario's dropped items, including the loot bag, its treasures, and the Game Boy Advance.

===''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''===
In ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', the Octopus appears in the [[microgame]] [[Game & Watch Octopus]]. It involves having to raise the [[Wii Remote]] with precise timing for the diver to escape the Octopus.

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
[[File:Octopus SSBB screenshot.png|thumb|200px|A screenshot of Octopus in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
Since the introduction of [[Final Smash]]es in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', [[Mr. Game & Watch]]'s Final Smash in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} involves him transforming into Octopus. He can quickly move around across the ground and jump, but remains at risk of falling off the stage. Octopus damages any opponents who touch it, dealing heavier damage with opponents in the front than the back. Pressing the attack buttons extends Octopus's tentacles, giving it more range to hit others.

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]] / [[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'', the Octopus has a rainbow-colored outline that resembles the glow of a character from collecting a [[Smash Ball]].

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', aside from returning as Mr. Game & Watch's Final Smash, Octopus is also featured in his own [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]].

===''Nintendo Land''===
In ''[[Nintendo Land]]'', he is named '''Large Octopus''', and appears during the [[Octopus Dance]] attraction. Unlike other appearances, the Octopus is depicted as red. The Large Octopus can be seen in the background of the screen. If a player's [[Mii]] loses all of their hearts, the Large Octopus abducts the Mii and takes them offscreen.
