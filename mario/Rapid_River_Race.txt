'''Rapid River Race''' is a Free-for-All minigame in the game ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' and a returning minigame in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

== Gameplay ==
{{Sectstub}}
Rapid River Race is a boat race where you race along a pool try to get first. There are [[Urchin]]s serving as obstacles, and if you hit them, you slow down. The Urchins can be avoided by switching lanes as you approach them.

== Controls ==
*{{button|wii|Padleftright}} - Switch spots
*{{button|wii|2}} - Accelerate
