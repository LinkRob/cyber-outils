'''Mona Pizza''' is a restaurant establishment owned by [[Mona]] in [[Diamond City]] in the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' series. It appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. Its theme song is sung by [[Sal Out]]. Mona Pizza's main competitor is [[Pizza Dinosaur]]. As suggested by the posture of Mona within the restaurant logo, the name "Mona Pizza" is a parody of the {{w|Mona Lisa}} painting by {{w|Leonardo da Vinci}}.

During Mona's stage in ''WarioWare: Twisted!'', while playing through the [[microgame]]s, the Pizza Dinosaur theme intertwines with Mona Pizza at parts.
