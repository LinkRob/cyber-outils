'''Mario's Three-peat''' is a Showdown minigame from [[Super Mario Party Jamboree|''Super Mario Party Jamboree'']]. It is centered around [[Mario (character)|Mario]], and has four players competing against him and each other in three free-for-all minigames from past installments in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'']] franchise. It cannot be played in the Mario Party mode if Mario is an active player character.

== Intro ==
The minigame opens with four characters standing next to a [[Warp Pipe]] in a grassy plain, when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] (or a greyscale clone in his likeness if he is an active playable character, only possible in the [[Minigame Bay]]) pops out and invites the characters to play.

== Gameplay ==
This minigame is a combination of three previous [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'']] minigames.

=== Round 1 ===
This round is based on [[Leaf Leap]] from [[Mario Party 5|''Mario Party 5'']], with identical gameplay. The characters must jump from leaf to leaf to climb high on their beanstalks. If a player tries to jump to a leaf but there is no leaf there, they fall until they land on the highest leaf below. The player who climbs the highest wins this round.

=== Round 2 ===
This round comes from [[Bumper Balls]], which comes from the original [[Mario Party|''Mario Party'']] and returned in [[Mario Party 2|''Mario Party 2'']], albeit set on an airborne platform in the sky. The characters now balance on rubber balls and have to knock each other off the platform. Mario starts in the middle while the other characters start in the corners. Whoever survives the longest wins this round.

=== Round 3 ===
This round comes from [[Shy Guy Says|''Shy Guy Says'']], with the version used coming from [[Mario Party 2|''Mario Party 2'']]. During this phase of the minigame, players must raise the same-colored flag as the [[Shy Guy]] host, who will eliminate any character who raises the incorrect flag or does not raise any flag (The eliminatees get their balloons popped by arrows.).

=== Scoring ===
Following each round, each character gains points with how many points they earn depending on their performance. The first place player gets five points, the second place player four points, the third place player three points, the second-to-last place player two points, and the last place player a single point. The player with the most points wins. Mario has his score displayed like the other participants, but the game will not count it when determining who is first. In Mario Party Mode, the player who encountered Mario first to trigger the minigame gets a three-point bonus as an advantage.

== Ending ==
The first place player celebrates their victory, with their opponents sulking in defeat.

== Controls ==
The right button (A using the two Joy Con scheme) is used to jump during the first round, and is useless during rounds 2 and 3. The control stick is used to move during the first two rounds, with the first round using it to aim the player's jump. The left and right triggers raise the white and red flags, respectively, but only during the third round.
