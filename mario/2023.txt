== Other events ==

* August 21: [[Nintendo]] announced that for over 30 years, [[Charles Martinet]] will finally be retiring as the voice of [[Mario]] (as well as [[Luigi]], [[Wario]], & [[Waluigi]]) and will live out the rest of his career as a "Mario Ambassador".
* October 13: It was announced that [[Kevin Afghani]] will be replacing Charles Martinet as the voice of Mario (as well as Luigi, Wario, & Waluigi).
