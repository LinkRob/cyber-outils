A '''Star Marker''' is an object which appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
A Star Marker is in the shape of a five-pointed star, and it is dark blue and somewhat translucent. A Star Marker is always hovering slightly above the ground in a set location. A Star Marker only appears in courses and secret levels with a set of eight red [[Red Coin]]s, as when [[Mario]] collects every Red Coin, a [[Power Star]] appears over the Star Marker.

The only main course to not have a Star Marker for its Red Coin mission is [[Jolly Roger Bay]], where completing the mission [[Red Coins on the Ship Afloat]] causes the Power Star to appear over the [[Ship (Super Mario 64)|ship]]. All three [[Bowser]] stages—[[Bowser in the Dark World]], [[Bowser in the Fire Sea]], and [[Bowser in the Sky]]—lack a Star Marker even though they each have their own set of eight Red Coins for Mario to collect.
