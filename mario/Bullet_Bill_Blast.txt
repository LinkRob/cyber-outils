'''Bullet Bill Blast''' is the [[Skillshot]] used by [[Shy Guy]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Once fully charged, Shy Guy jumps on a [[Bullet Bill]] and heads toward the goal. It explodes, knocking out every character within blasting range, including Shy Guy and the [[Kritter]]. Shy Guy, however, recovers earlier than all the other characters, including the goalie, and has the opportunity to kick the ball into the goal.
