[[File:Taunt_Ball.gif|thumb]]'''Taunt Balls''' are items that debuted in [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team|''Mario &amp; Luigi: Dream Team'']].

== Description ==
Taunt Balls are yellow balls with faces. Their eyes are black beady eyes while their mouth has white lips and a tongue sticking out to imitate a teasing expression. They are used to bring background enemies to the foreground (also inflicting one point of damage). However [[Nipper Plant|some]] [[Inky Piranha Plant|background enemies]] don't come to foreground after being hit by a Taunt Ball, and some others perform retaliation attacks, such as the [[Shy Guy Airtub]].

The in-game description reads: ''"Enrages an enemy in the background! Whooooosh!"''
