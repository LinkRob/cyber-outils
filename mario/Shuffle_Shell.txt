'''Shuffle Shell''' is one of [[Mario]]'s minigames in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

== Layout ==
This minigame is in the exact same setting as [[Shell Smash (minigame)|Shell Smash]], still featuring the same background music as [[Cool, Cool Mountain]]/[[Chief Chilly Challenge]]/[[Snowman's Land]].

== Gameplay ==
This minigame has similarities to {{w|curling}}, but its scoring system is like {{w|archery}}. The player gets five [[Green Shell]]s, and they need to get them onto the target on the top screen by using the blue paddle to hit the Green Shells. Shells on the outer blue ring will score 100 points, shells on the white ring will score 300 points, shells in the center red ring will score 500 points, the white center of the target will score 1,000 points, and shells outside of the target will not score any points. The final scores on the last rounded are added up for a total score at the end, so the player needs to take note that the shells can still move if bounced against other shells.
