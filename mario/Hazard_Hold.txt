'''Hazard Hold''' is a 1-vs-Rivals mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Opening==
The solo character is shown firing an electricity ring at the team members, who [[jump]] to avoid it. The screen then splits into one side for the solo character and another for the rivals.

==Gameplay==
The solo character has to try and eliminate other characters by using three different buttons, each executing a different attack: the electricity attack button sends a ring of electricity that expands from the center out, with a four-second regeneration time; the fire attack button causes the four [[burner]]s to activate and slowly pivot 90 degrees; and the [[wind]] attack button sends a strong gust of wind from the fan. The electricity and fire attacks take four seconds to regenerate, while the wind attack takes two and a half seconds to regenerate. If a rival touches any of the three types of attacks, they are eliminated. The solo character wins if they eliminate all the rivals before the 30-second [[time limit]] is up. However, if at least one rival is remaining at the end, the rivals win instead.

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Unleash fire, electricity, and wind on your opponents! The solo player must hit all of the other players to win.''
*'''Controls (Solo)''': ''Hold the Wii Remote sideways. Press {{button|wii|Pad}} to face the different switches. Press {{button|wii|2}} to activate the switch you're facing.''
*'''Controls (Team)''': ''Hold the Wii Remote sideways. Press {{button|wii|Pad}} to move and {{button|wii|2}} to jump.''

==Trivia==
*The electricity and fire of the solo player's attacks are similar to those in the minigame [[Revolving Fire]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.
