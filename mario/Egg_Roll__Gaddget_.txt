'''Egg Roll''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is unlocked as a reward from [[Goomba (Mario Party Advance)|Goomba]] for completing the [[Kind Goomba]] quest.

==Description==
Egg Roll features an egg balancing minigame, in which the player has to try and keep the [[Yoshi Egg]] balanced in a short amount of time. If done successfully, either a springy hand, confetti, or balloons hatches from the Yoshi Egg, depending on how fast it was balanced.
