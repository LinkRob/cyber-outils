A '''Wilt Shroom''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
A Wilt Shroom restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] to a single character. Wilt Shrooms are only obtained by being "won" as prizes from [[Grate Guy]] or [[Knife Guy]]'s game. They cannot be used during battle.
