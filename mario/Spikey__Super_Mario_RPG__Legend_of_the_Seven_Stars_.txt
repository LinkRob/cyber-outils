'''Spikeys''' are a variant of [[Spiny]] encountered as an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Spikeys are encountered in four different locations: [[Mushroom Way]], [[Bandit's Way]], [[Pipe Vault]], and [[Booster Pass]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
In a sense, Spikeys are similar to Spinies in that they are small enemies with a spiky red shell. Aside from being bipedal, the difference with Spikeys is that they wear a few clothing items, most noticeably armor covering their face, from which only two glowing yellow eyes are visible. Aside from this, Spikeys wear a pair of gloves and shoes. They also have yellow spikes on their shell.

===Behavior===
During battle, Spikeys attack by either curling up into a spiked ball to then roll into the selected target or by a [[spike (hazard)|spike]] at them.
