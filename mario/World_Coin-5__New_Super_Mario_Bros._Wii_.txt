|time=300 seconds}}'''{{world|coin|5|}}''' is the fifth and final level of [[World Coin]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 

<br />
==Layout==
This level is snow-themed, and characters must climb to the top to reach the pipe that leads to the end. This stage is auto-scrolling, and there are [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s and [[Penguin Suit]]s in it. The primary enemies are [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. 

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In a compartment covered by [[Ice Block]]s and [[Brick Block]]s. Players need to be [[Super Mario]] to break the Brick Blocks.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Under a snowy platform. A running start then a jump is recommended.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Near the end of the level, under a snowy platform.
