'''Bowser Jr.'s Aireship''' is a floating fortress ship pilotted by [[Bowser Jr.]] in ''Super Mario Galaxy''. In the end of the fight against Bowser Jr. in [[Bowser Jr.'s Airship Armada]], the airship was destroyed, but is later, rebuilt.
