'''Fresh off the Grill''' is a boss microgame belonging to [[Ashley]] in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must beat this level by picking up the [[Wii Remote]] at the right time, which is when the bun is on top; but the player must pick it up, before a rats destroy it, or the employee moves the tray aside and takes the burger away, which the player loses. If the player pick up the unfinished burger, the employee gets angry. If the microgame is won, the flag with a smiling face lands on the burger. The burger may have many normal and unusual toppings such as American cheese, asparagus, baby corns, bacon, chocolate bar, chocolate pudding, clovers, cracker, cucumber, fish, fried egg, [[Fronk (species)|Fronks]], ketchup, lettuce, mayonnaise, minced garlic, mustard, onions, patties, popsicle, potato chip, salami, sausage, tentacles, tomato slices, turkey dog, and a [[Wii Remote]].

*'''Level 1:''' The burger is made normally. There are no distractions.
*'''Level 2:''' A distraction to the player; some ingredients would either fly or thrown onto the burger.
*'''Level 3:''' More distractions besides toppings flying, people walk pass the burger.
