'''Out of the Woods''' is the very first [[level]] of ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. It is featured on the northern side of [[Music Box World]]. Out of the Woods takes place within a forest, and [[Anonster]] is fought by [[Wario]] as the boss at the end.
