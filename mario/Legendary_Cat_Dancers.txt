The '''Legendary Cat Dancers''' is a group of [[cat]] dancers in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''===
The Legendary Cat Dancers are featured in the stage [[Feline Fever]]. In the opening, a small cat is found behind a garbage can by [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]] as he walks down a street in the rain. In an act of compassion, Jimmy gives the cat his umbrella and continues walking. However, the cat, along with other cats, keep following Jimmy wherever he goes. He starts to dance with them, and they go to [[Club Sugar]].

While playing through the [[microgame]]s, four cats each represent one of the player's chances. One cat appears inside a mirror ball, and it dances if the player wins a microgame. However, if the player loses at a microgame, the cat falls and becomes dizzy.

In the ending cutscene, during the morning, after the stage is beaten, the Legendary Cat Dancers leave Club Sugar along with Jimmy. The first cat from earlier nudges Jimmy and returns his umbrella to him.

===''[[Game & Wario]]''===
In the opening intro, two of the Legendary Cat Dancers make a minor appearance alongside Jimmy.
