'''Apprentices''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are a specific type of [[Snifit|Spookum]] serving as [[Booster (character)|Booster]]'s minions, and they are encountered at [[Booster Pass]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Apprentices have the same appearance as regular Spookums.

==Battle==
In battle, Apprentices can only use [[Thunder (element)|Thunder]]-based moves such as [[Bolt]] and [[Static E!]]. They tend to fight in groups due to their rather low [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Attack]] power.

===Tips===
The Apprentices are weak to [[Ice (element)|Ice]] moves, so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] will be enough to due them in.
