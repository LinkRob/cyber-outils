'''Ashley Nicole Flannegan Russell''' (born November 22, 1982) is an American actress and voice actress who portrays [[Wendy O. Koopa]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', replacing [[Lani Minella]] from that game onwards (aside from Lani Minella's reused clips for ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'').

== Voices ==

* [[Wendy O. Koopa]] (2014-present)

* ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' - Wendy O. Koopa
* ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' - Wendy O. Koopa (uncredited)
* ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' - Wendy O. Koopa
* ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' - Wendy O. Koopa
* ''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]]'' - Wendy O. Koopa
* [[Super Nintendo World]] - Wendy O. Koopa (Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge)
