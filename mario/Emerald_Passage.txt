'''Emerald Passage''' is the first world of ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
The levels of the Emerald Passage feature botanical elements such as a swampy lake or a thick jungle in the monsoon season. The four levels include [[Palm Tree Paradise]], [[Wildflower Fields]], [[Mystic Lake]], and [[Monsoon Jungle]]. At the end is a boss fight against [[Cractus]], who is a giant monstrous potted plant, adding to the Emerald Passage's botanical theme. The [[jewel piece]]s of Emerald Passage appear as an emerald, and its [[CD]]s are green.
