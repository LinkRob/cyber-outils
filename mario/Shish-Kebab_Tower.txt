==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': On the first elevator, go on the right side of it and go it the first green statue of Bowser Jr. to reveal a hidden area with the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Before going on the second elevator, jump on the right side while avoiding the spiked pillars on the ride side to reveal the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At the right time in Super form, go to the cage where the final spiked pillar will be and then ground-pound the Brick Blocks to get the final Star Coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
In the second elevator, watch out for a giant picture of standard Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' on the right wall.
