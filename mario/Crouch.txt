'''Crouch''' (or '''Duck''') is a move featured in many ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' series installments. The move is first used in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
In order to crouch, the player must press Down on most 2D games or Z/ZL on most 3D games. When crouching, many jumps are achievable, including [[Long Jump]]s, [[Backward Somersault]]s, and [[Roll]]s. The player will be able to dodge attacks or break bricks if this action is performed. In the instruction manual, it is called squat.
