'''Fire and Ice''' is a level located in [[Frosted Glacier]] of ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
== Layout ==
{{sectstub}}
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': While riding Yoshi or after the Venus Fire Trap destroys the Prickly Goombas (unless you're Nabbit), go to the hidden passageway protected by the Prickly Goombas to go to a hidden pipe into a secret area with a Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After passing the ninth Venus Fire Trap, jump to the hidden passageway above it to the coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Right after the second Star Coin, go on the path of two Brick Blocks and one "?" Block and jump on the middle of it to reveal a hidden block under a P Switch to reveal a path to the Star Coin below.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Near the first Star Coin shows an ice sculpture of modern Luigi's torso without the arms.
