'''''Mario &#38; Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020 - Arcade Edition''''' is the second [[arcade]] installment within the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' series, following after ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games Arcade Edition]]'', and the [[arcade]] counterpart to the [[Nintendo Switch]] game ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''. It was first released in Japan in January 2020, then in English-speaking countries a year later, in January 2021.

Like its home counterpart, ''Mario &#38; Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020 - Arcade Edition'' features events featured within the {{w|2020 Summer Olympics}} and {{w|1964 Summer Olympics}}, both of which took place in [[Tokyo]], [[Japan]].
