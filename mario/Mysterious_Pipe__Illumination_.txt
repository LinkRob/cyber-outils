The '''Mysterious Pipe''' is an object that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series.

==Description==
It is a special [[Warp Pipe (Illumination)|Warp Pipe]] that transports [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] to the Mushroom Kingdom and the Dark Lands respectively. The pipe is located underground in [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]] in the utility tunnel, where the brothers end up in after breaking through a brick wall in the sewers. Unlike regular Warp Pipes, the mysterious pipe sucks up things into it and transports them through the Warp Zone "into a magical new world." While in the Warp Zone, the brothers get transported through a transparent tunnel, but get separated and sent into two different kingdoms. Also unlike regular Warp Pipes, the mysterious pipe flashes when things come in or out of it.

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''==
Before the film began, a baby girl [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]] was the first human being to fall down this pipe, having being seperated from her older sister, [[Rosalina (Illumination)|Rosalina]], being sent to safety, and end up lost in the [[Mushroom Forest (Illumination)|Mushroom Forest]], before she was raised by the [[Toad's species (Illumination)|Toads]].

At the end of the movie during the Bomber Bill chasing scene, Mario tricks it into flying inside the mysterious pipe. The Bomber Bill shrinks and blows up while inside the [[Warp Zone]]. This causes a shockwave at first, which damages Mario, but then starts to suck everything into it, including the characters and [[Bowser's Castle (Illumination)|Bowser's Castle ship]].

==In ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''==
Mario and Luigi were sent to the Sand Kingdom to investigate a blocked pipe inside a pyramid. It turns out whoever had a hard time trying to come out of the pipe after the Brookyn attack was [[Yoshi (Illumination)|Yoshi]] who hatched out from his egg.
