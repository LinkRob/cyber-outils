}}'''Flower Finder''' is a [[badge]] which appears in the early [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]] installments.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Flower_finder_badge.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Flower Finder is purchased form [[Merlow]] for twelve [[Star Piece]]s. Similar to the [[Heart Finder]], this badge will make it likelier to drop extra [[Flower Point|Flowers]] after battle and guarantees at least one. Flower Finder costs three [[Badge Point]]s to wear just like Heart Finder.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Flower Finder has the same exact effect as in ''Paper Mario''. It is purchased from [[Dazzle]] but the price has changed to five Star Pieces. This badge, like in ''Paper Mario'', has a Badge Point cost of 3.
