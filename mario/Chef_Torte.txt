'''Chef Torte''' is a character and a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He is a [[Terrapin]] who works as a master chef in [[Marrymore]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[File:Torte SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
Chef Torte is first seen after [[Mario]] and the others sneak into his kitchen trying to stop the wedding of [[Booster (character)|Booster]] and [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]]. Chef Torte and his [[Chef Torte's Apprentice|apprentice]] eventually carry the cake into the wedding room but find out that there is no one to eat the cake. Angry, Chef Torte and his apprentice engage Mario and co. in a battle.

In battle, Chef Torte and his apprentice attack mainly with just charges. However, Chef Torte is a little stronger in his attacks. The main key to this fight is to not attack Chef Torte or his apprentice but their [[Bundt|cake]]. After enough attacks, the cake suddenly starts to move and Chef Torte and his apprentice run away from the battle.

==Trivia==
*"Torte" means "layer cake" in German. This is appropriate as Chef Torte speaks in an thick German accent.
*Chef Torte and his apprentice are the only bosses in the game not to use any special attacks.
**Also, given they run away from the battle at the point their cake starts to move, they are the only bosses in the game to not be defeated in battle.
*Chef Torte has the lowest stats out of all the bosses in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''.
