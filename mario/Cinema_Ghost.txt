'''Cinema Ghosts''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Cinema Ghosts are an invisible variant of [[Grabbing Ghost]]s, although a Cinema Ghost becomes visible while it is stunned by a [[flashlight]]. While visible, Cinema Ghosts are shown to have the same body as a Grabbing Ghost, except Cinema Ghosts are colored dark purple.

===Behavior===
Cinema Ghosts act identically to Grabbing Ghosts in that it tries to sneak behind [[Luigi]] and grab him, preventing Luigi from moving around. Like with [[Red Grabbing Ghost]]s and [[Mirror Ghost]]s, a Cinema Ghost damages Luigi while it chokes him, but regular Grabbing Ghosts cannot damage Luigi. 

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Cinema Ghosts are only encountered in the [[Projection Room]], where there are seven of them, each having 20 [[Heart Point|HP]]. The Cinema Ghosts only begin to appear once Luigi has turned on the projector. Even while a Cinema Ghost is invisible, its silhouette can be seen on the faint light displayed on the projector screen. When Luigi stuns a Cinema Ghost with his flashlight, he can begin vacuuming it into his [[Poltergust 3000]].

Cinema Ghosts are similar to Mirror Ghosts, which are only visible from the [[mirror]] in the [[Mirror Room]] unless Luigi stuns it using his flashlight. Cinema Ghosts and Mirror Ghosts both leave behind a dust cloud upon materializing.
