'''World 4''' is the fourth world of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''.

== Description ==
This world is a grassland that, once again, takes place in daylight. Enemies such as [[Lakitu]]s, [[Spinies]], and [[Buzzy Beetle]]s are introduced. This is also the second and last world to contain an underground course.

On the map in ''Deluxe'', there are quite a bit of trees, and less water is shown. The land elevator is slightly higher than before. 4-1 lies to the far west. The player is then lead east to 4-2, which is next to a pipe that will transport the player to a pipe on the north end. 4-3 sits on a small piece of land in the water, sitting just across from the castle.
