The '''Sling-a-Thing Toad''' (known as the '''Fling-a-Thing Toad''' in PAL regions) is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] in [[Decalburg]] who runs the [[Sling-a-Thing]] (Fling-a-Thing in the British English versions) stand in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star|Paper Mario: Sticker Star.]]''


==Profile==
===Physical description===

==='''Sling-a-Thing Toad''' is a purple-spotted toad that stays in his stand.===

== In [[Paper Mario: Sticker Star|''Paper Mario: Sticker Star'']] ==
His stand will open after Mario has collected the Fan Thing in [[Bouquet Gardens]]. His job is to convert [[Things]] into stickers for free. If Mario is out of Things and talks with the Sling-a-Thing Toad, he will tell Mario to check the wall near him, where the Shady Toad sells Things. The shopkeeper Toad of Decalburg gets jealous of him when he hears that a new shop has just opened. He frequently uses the word "perky", and his motto is "Stay perky", this motto is omitted in the PAL version of the game (where he never uses the word "perky" at all) being replaced with various other phrases.

When tattled, [[Kersti]] will comment: "This operation looks pretty shady/doggy, but I have to admit, the thing stickers are pretty amazing." A Red Toad at the [[Outlook Point]] runs the same business.
