'''Zoooooooo''' is a [[microgame]] found in the [[List of WarioWare: Touched! microgames#Cute Cuts|Cute Cuts]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''

==Gameplay==
The objective of the game is to stretch out a part of an animal before time runs out.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The player must stretch the elephant's trunk to the left.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The player must stretch the giraffe's neck up.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The player must stretch the dog's rear part to the right.
