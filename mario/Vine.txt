'''Vines''', also named '''Ivy''',<ref>Tatsumi Yamashita, ''The Official Nintendo Player's Guide'', Tokuma Shoten, 1987, ISBN 999832369X. Pages 28-29.</ref> '''Magic Vines''',<ref>''Nintendo Power'' Volume 13, page 14.</ref> or '''Fine Vines''',<ref>Hodgson, David S J. ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide. Page 38.</ref> are climbable ladder-like plants in various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. Vines first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', usually inside of [[Question Block]]s or [[Hidden Block]]s. When the vine is released, it will grow vertically. The player can climb up the vine, and these vines usually lead to alternate paths, hidden areas, or secrets. If you ground-pound it the vine will grow down, thus making the secret path or power-up impossible to get to. In more recent games, Vines also have a [[Piranha Plant]]-like mouth at the top of their stalk.

==Mainline appearances==
===''Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:NES Vine.png|thumb|left|A Vine grows into a secret area in ''Super Mario Bros.'']]
Vines are first seen in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. They are found in a couple levels. When [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] hits the block above him, the Vine grows out of it and extends into the skies. He can use the Vine to reach [[Coin Heaven]]s or the [[Warp Zone]] in [[World 4-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 4-2]], both of which allow him to skip a large portion of areas. Due to this, Vines are uncommon and often appear inside [[Hidden Block]]s, but they are sometimes in a [[Brick Block]] or a [[? Block]].

===''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''===
[[File:Vine Hard.png|thumb|right|Mario climbs a Vine to a secret area in ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''.]]
Vines appear again in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''. In this game, they are once again found inside [[Hidden Block]]s and [[Brick Block]]s. The Vines once again lead the plumbers to [[Warp Zone]]s and [[Coin Heaven]]s allowing them to skip large parts of levels but they are also more hidden this time than they were last time. In [[World 8-2 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 8-2]], a vine is required to complete the level.

===''Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:Vine Climbers.png|thumb|left|Mario climbing vines in ''Super Mario Bros. 2''.]]
Vines return in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. This time, they are already grown in the levels where they are found, as neither ? Blocks nor Brick Blocks appear. Some vines are in midair. Just as before, [[Mario]] or another playable character can climb a vine to reach higher areas to progress through the level. Vines function identically to [[Chain (object)|chains]] and [[ladder]]s.

===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:Vine Way.png|thumb|A vine grows in a level of Water Land in ''Super Mario Bros. 3''.]]
Vines are seen again in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. Here, they can be found in several levels of various levels of the game. Just as before, Vines can be revealed by hitting [[Hidden Block]]s or hitting certain [[? Block]]s (typically those close to the ground). Vines can be used to reach [[Coin Heaven]]s as well as higher platforms.

The color of the vines differs between the worlds. Vines are brown in [[Desert Land]], green in [[Water Land]] and [[Giant Land]], blue in [[Sky Land]], and white in [[Ice Land]].

In ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'', ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', and ''[[Super Mario All-Stars 25th Anniversary Edition]]'' (the remakes of the game), Vines were given [[Piranha Plant]]-like heads on the tip of the stalk.

===''Super Mario World''===
[[File:Vine SMW.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] climbing a Vine in ''Super Mario World''.]]
Vines return in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', the first game where a [[Piranha Plant]]-like head grows from the tip of their stalk. Vines once again appear by hitting [[? Block]]s and are necessary to reach higher platforms or secret areas, but they stop growing upon hitting a [[Block]]. The vine can continue growing if the block in its way is broken before the Vine hits it; otherwise, it will permanently stop. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] is carrying an object such as a [[P Switch]], he cannot climb the vine normally, but he can kick the item up, start to climb the vine, and then hold the {{button|snes|Y}} button to catch the object while climbing.

Upon completing the level [[Funky]], the tip of the vine gains a pumpkin head, similar to [[Jumping Pumpkin Plant]]s.

===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:Vine Secret.png|thumb|left|Mario climbs up a vine in ''New Super Mario Bros.'']]
Vines return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', once again being found in either [[Hidden Block]]s or certain [[? Block]]s and [[Brick Block]]s. Just as before, they are used to reach higher platforms or secret areas (which often contain [[Star Coin]]s or [[Red Flagpole|secret exits]]). For the first time, if a [[block]] containing a vine is [[Ground Pound]]ed, the vine grows downward, which can be troublesome as this renders the intended secret inaccessible, forcing the player to redo the level if they want to access it. Vines can be found in a variety of levels, no longer just grass-themed ones.

Unlike ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' and ''Super Mario World'', the top of a vine still looks like a Piranha Plant, but the sprite is not identical to the regular Piranha Plant's sprite. Also, all tips of the Beanstalks are red, to look more like a Piranha Plant. Their stems also have a twisted appearance in the game.

In the game's files, there exists an unused type of vine that appears to be naturally placed and lacks a [[Piranha Plant]]-like head. They would have likely been used in certain levels as platforming mechanics to climb across.

===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[File:Vine Skies.png|thumb|The characters in a secret area with several vines in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.]]
Vines appear again in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. Just as before, they are once again commonly found in [[Hidden Block]]s and rarely [[? Block]]s or [[Brick Block]]s. They take the player's character to higher platforms or [[Coin Heaven]]s, where they can obtain [[Star Coin]]s and other items.

Vines are also seen on the world map of [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7]], leading to [[World 7-6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7-6]] and down to [[World 7-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7-Castle]]. When the player completes World 7-6, another Vine appears, growing out of the top of World 7-Castle. Entering the castle through this method allows the characters to skip the castle, leading them directly to the door where [[Ludwig von Koopa]] awaits.

The tips of the Beanstalk return to their original green appearance. Their stems also revert to their non-twisted appearance, and their lips are yellow.

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:Vine Growth.png|thumb|right|A Vine grew in a secret area.]]
Vines appear yet again in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' and retain their design from the previous game. Vines are once again found by hitting ? Blocks, Brick Blocks, and Hidden Blocks and lead to secret areas housing [[Star Coin]]s and other secrets. In this game, Vines appear much more commonly and mainly lead to higher platforms due to [[Cannon Pipe]]s and [[Flying Question Block]]s making more appearances in this game alongside them.

===''New Super Mario Bros. U'' / ''New Super Luigi U'' / ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[File:Vine Time.png|thumb|left|[[Luigi]] has reached a secret area with [[Jump Block]]s and a [[Star Coin]] thanks to the Vine in ''New Super Luigi U''.]]
Vines also appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. In these games, they retain their role from previous games (being found in [[Hidden Block]]s as well as [[? Block]]s and [[Brick Block]]s) and once again take the heroes to secret areas (which often times contain [[Star Coin]]s or [[Red Flagpole|secret exits]].

Additionally, [[Beanstalk]]s appear in the levels [[Skyward Stalk]] and [[Beanstalk Jungle]].

===''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS''===
[[File:Vine Maker.png|thumb|right|A Vine grows from a Block traveling along a track.]]
Vines are seen yet again in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' as placeable objects. In these games, vines can be placed in any style or theme. A vine can be placed inside a block, from which it continuously grows until going offscreen or hitting the ceiling. If a vine is placed outside a block, players can manually extend its length. The [[Piranha Plant]]-like heads have also been added to the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' variations of the vines.

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
Vines retain their role in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. Additionally, in the story mode hub, a vine sprouts out of a [[Hidden Block]] which allows Mario to talk to [[Soundfrog]]. Also, in [[World Maker]], placing a cloud with a path leading to it causes a vine to appear for [[Mario]] to climb.

===''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''===
[[File:Grapple SMBW.jpg|thumb|left|Elephant Daisy using the Grappling Vine ability in ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''.]]
Vines reappear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', where some of them can be spawned by watering flowers as [[Elephant Mario]]. The Grappling Vine badge also grants the player who equipped it the ability to shoot a Vine that latches onto walls.

==Other appearances==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''===
[[Image:Vine Toy.png|thumb|right|Vines in Jungle Hijinks.]]
Vines are seen again in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]'', this time as more of a roadblock obstacle instead of a climbable object. They are primarily found in levels of [[Jungle Hijinks]] and block the [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Marios]] paths forcing them to take certain detours. The vines can be chewed away by [[Snapjaw]]s. Vines can also be placed in the [[Construction Zone]] in the [[Jungle Hijinks]] Kit as well as [[Special Kit 3]].

===''Mario Party 9''===
[[Image:Waluigi Vine.png|thumb|left|[[Waluigi]]'s Vine grew the highest and so he is the winner!]]
Vines are seen in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' this time appearing in the minigame [[Growing Up]]. Here, players are standing underneath some [[Brick Block]]s and must hit the button displayed on the [[Flying Question Block]] to make the Vine grow from the Question Block, whoever hits the correct button quickest will make their Vine grow further, whoever hits the wrong button will trip up and not hit the [[Brick Block]]. After all 8 rounds are over, the players will climb their Vines and whoever grew their vine the tallest wins. A Vine is also seen in [[Toad Road]], when a player lands on the [[Lucky Space]] closest to the Fort, the captain can climb the Vine to a hidden sky area with heaps of [[Mini Star]]s, the captain can roll a [[Dice Block]] to earn the Mini Stars, up to 12 Mini Stars can be earned from this event.

==In other media==
===''Super Mario World'' television series===
In the ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|Super Mario World]]'' episode "[[Party Line]]", countless vines are used as telephone lines for the [[Cave People]] of [[Dome City]]. After the concept of telephones proves to be unnecessary for the Cave People, [[Yoshi (DiC cartoons)|Yoshi]] is given the job of eating all the vines.

==Trivia==
*The Vine's [[Piranha Plant]]-head in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' strongly resembles the [[Venus Fire Trap]]s in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]'' as they lack the white spots and teeth.
