'''Exp. Booster''' (short for '''Experience Booster''') is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When any user equips the Exp. Booster, the party earns twice the number of [[Experience Point]]s after a battle. The player can buy the Exp. Booster from [[Frogfucius' Student]] in [[Seaside Town]] for 22 [[Frog Coin]]s, and the item can be sold for 11 regular [[coin]]s. The Exp. Booster is still effective if the one who has it equipped is not currently within [[Mario]]'s party.

From its artwork, the Exp. Booster is a medallion necklace reading EXP x2, stating its effect.
