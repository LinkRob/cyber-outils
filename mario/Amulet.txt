'''Amulet''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
The Amulet is a "great item [with a] bad smell". When any user equips the item, the player receives half of the elemental damage. The player can obtain the item by winning Booster's minigame, "Curtain Contest".
