'''Thriller Dunk''' is the [[Special Shot]] of [[Boo]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Boo use Thriller Dunk by tapping out an uppercase "B" on the Touch Screen twice, with the "B" representing his name, just like [[Bowser]] for his [[Bomber Dunk]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] for his [[Graffiti Shot]].

Boo starts Thriller Dunk by dribbling in a "B" shape twice. After this, Boo teleports while throwing the ball up, reappears over the ball, launches it at the basket, teleports over the basket, and slams the ball into the basket when it reaches him. An explosion occurs when the shot is made, and a scream also plays.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The shot's name may be reference to the [[wikipedia:Thriller (genre)|thriller]] genre of films.
