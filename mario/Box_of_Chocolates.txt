The '''Box of Chocolates''' is an item that appears exclusively in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. Although [[Dixie]] or [[Kiddy Kong]] can't eat it, they will need it to complete a trade.

[[Bachelor]] will tell the [[Kong]]s that he needs some chocolates and a [[Purple Flower]] to take on his hot date. The Kongs can purchase the Box of Chocolates from [[Bazzar]] for twenty [[Bear Coin]]s and then give it to Bachelor. Once Bachelor receives both the Box of Chocolates and the Purple Flower, he will reward the Kongs with his [[Banana Bird]].
