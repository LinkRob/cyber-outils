'''Dress for Success''' is a [[Microgame]] that comes from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''. It is not to be confused with the [[Dress for Success|minigame of the same name]] from ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. It uses the Scales form for its gameplay. [[Ashley (WarioWare)|Ashley]] hosts the Microgame, which is featured in the level Caveat Imptor.

== Layout ==
A girl with blue hair fills the role of the protagonist here, with a different outfit in each of her hands (either a swimsuit consisting of a bikini top and skirt or a red jacket and black snow pants) and looking into a mirror in a room that has a window to the outside behind the mirror.

== Gameplay ==
The player has to choose which getup the blue-haired girl should wear by moving their hand in front of their chest. If the getup matches the setting, the girl will be shown outside cheering with the outfit she is made to wear, and the player wins. However, a loss triggers if the player chooses the wrong getup for her to wear; choosing the winter jacket and snow pants for the beach causes the girl to sweat profusely (presumably from heat exhaustion), while forcing her into a swimsuit with a snowy setting causes her to contract hypothermia and shiver.

=== Difficulty Levels ===
As the difficulty rises, the player will be sabotaged with the view to the outdoors being obscured more and more.
*'''Level 1 (Easy)''': The outside view can be seen clearly with next to no obstruction, with the Microgame taking place during the day.
*'''Level 2 (Hard)''': Some obstruction occurs, with the Microgame taking place during sunset or sunrise.
*'''Level 3 (Expert)''': The outside view has been heavily blocked to sabotage the player, with the Microgame taking place at nighttime.
