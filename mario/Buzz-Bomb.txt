'''Buzz-Bomb''' are enemies mixed with a [[Bob-omb]] and a [[Buzzy Beetle]] (or possibly meant to be a [[Para-Beetle]]. They appear in [[Monster Mix-up]].

==Appearance==
It resembles a [[Bob-omb]] with wings and can fly haphazardly. The Buzz-Bomb's seems volatile than the regular Bob-ombs. It was the fact that [[Bowser]] created it in the [[Monster Mixer]]. It was mentioned that it is obliterated with forty-seven creatures.
