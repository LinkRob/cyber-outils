'''Marina Stadium''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]''. It is located near the entrance of [[Kingdom of Bask]]. The court features [[Hard Court|Hard]], [[Glass Court|Glass]], [[Clay Court|Clay]], and [[Night Court|Night]].

==Flavor text==
*'''In-game:'''
** '''Hard, Clay, and Grass Courts:''' ''"Every tennis lover dreams of winning here! A truly magnificent stadium-court experience where you can challenge yourself to an ultimate battle of skill."''
** '''Night Court:''' ''"Every tennis lover dreams of winning here! A dazzling stadium court experience where you can enjoy an exhilarating match at night."''
