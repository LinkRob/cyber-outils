A '''Goomba Tower''' is a stack of [[Goomba]]s that first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. Sometimes a [[Green Power Star]] can be found in the middle of the stack. 

In ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'', they are enemies in their own boss mini-game. In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', these appear as obstacles in the course [[Mario Circuit]]. In Dr. Mario World, they appear as a doctor.

== Game Appearances ==

=== In {{Series|Super Mario}} ===

==== ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' ====
In ''Super Mario Odyssey'', there are many [[Power Moon|Power Moons]] that can only be obtained with a Goomba Tower, usually by going to [[Goombette (Super Mario Odyssey)|Goombette]]. 

=== In {{Series|Super Mario Maker}} ===
By placing [[Goomba|Goombas]] on top of each other in the editor, you can form a Goomba Tower. 

=== In {{series|Mario & Luigi}} ===

==== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' ====
In the [[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]] campaign of the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', Goomba Towers are an obtainable Melee troop, and their special attack is [[Whomping Wallop]]. The in-game description is "They can use Whomping Wallop to attack all foes in front of them. Weak against [[Boomerang Bro|Boomerang Bros]]."
