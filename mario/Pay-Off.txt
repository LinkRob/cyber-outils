'''Pay-Off''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Pay-Off is a unique badge that increases the [[coin]] payout depending on the damage [[Mario]] takes in battle. It is given to [[Mario]] by [[Merlow]] after he trades one [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] for the badge. Pay-Off could be useful to use alongside the [[Money Money]] badge that also increases the amount of coins that Mario earns from battle. Two [[Badge Point|BP]] are required to use Pay-Off.
