'''Target Shot''' is a minigame in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]].

==Description==
The minigame stars [[Baby Mario]] as the playable character. The gameplay involves him having to hit a ball into the target area on the other side of the court, indicated by the flashing corners. The shots are returned to Baby Mario by the [[Ball Bot]]. He earns points depending on the type of shot he makes: three points for either a Flat Shot, Strong Topspin, or Strong Slice and five points for either a Smash, Lob, or a Drop Shot. Baby Mario can earn bonus points by hitting the shot indicated onscreen. The minigame ends once Baby Mario fails to hit the ball into the target area.
