'''Toad Force V''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Toad Force V Gaddget consists of a standard [[Toad Force V Figure]] on a black background with the Toad Force V theme playing in the background. At first, the figure is in a compressed state, but activating it reveals all of the Toad Force V Figure's features, including its eyes, arms, and legs. The figure also has a "V beam," which applies a yellow lighting upon activation, and an "eye beam," which applies a light-green lighting to both of its eyes; neither can be activated while the figure is compressed.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Toad Force V is a hidden Gaddget, so it cannot be accessed from the Gaddgets menu in [[Play Land]]. Instead, it is only available by completing the 100-player Attack in Party Land, after which [[Toadette]] congratulates the player and allows them to see her "treasure," which is the Toad Force V Gaddget. If the Gaddget is exited, it can only be accessed again by completing the mode another time.
