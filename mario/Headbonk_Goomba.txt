'''Headbonk Goombas''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They can only be found in the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. They also have a darker version called the [[Dark Headbonk Goomba]].

== History ==

=== ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ===
Headbonk Goombas attack the player by headbonking them from an angled jump. They appear more commonly near the end of the Flipside Pit of 100 Trials. They are cannot be found in any other place.{{GoombaNav}}
