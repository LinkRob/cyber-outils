The '''Rescue Lakitu''' is a character from the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Rescue Lakitu is indistinguishable from the rest of his species, though he does carry a fishing line.

===Personality traits===

===Powers and abilities===
The Rescue Lakitu is shown to be capable of teleportation in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', teleporting over to where racers have fallen out-of-bounds immediately. As with most Lakitus, his [[Lakitu's Cloud|cloud]] serves as his personal transport vehicle or steed.

==In the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series==
<!-- Replace "game" with the game they appear in, if they appear in more than one game, add headings with "In <game>"-->
The Rescue Lakitu serves as the rescue service for the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' games. Whenever a racer falls into a spot that they cannot escape, the Rescue Lakitu will grab the kart with his fishing line and plop it back down on the track so the fallen racer can continue. In games that use [[Coin]]s, the Rescue Lakitu will take three; if there are less than three, the Rescue Lakitu just takes all the coins.
