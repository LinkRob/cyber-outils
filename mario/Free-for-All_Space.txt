'''Free-for-All Spaces''' are an object in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Description==
Free-for-All Spaces are orange [[space]]s with "VS" printed on it in dark red text, giving them a similar appearance to [[Minigame Space]]s in previous ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' games.

When the Captain lands onto a Free-for-All Space, this a Free-for-All [[minigame]] is initiated. Normally, whoever lands onto a Free-for-All Space gets to choose which minigame to play. When the [[Homestretch!|Almost There!]] event is active, the character in last place gets to choose which minigame to play. Alternatively, if two characters are tied for last place during the Almost There! event, the game selects the character randomly, and the Captain still chooses the minigame if they are in last place. In a rare occurrence, if all characters are tied or if three characters tie for last place, the decision goes back to the Captain.

Once the Free-for-All minigame has finished, the characters are rewarded a number of [[Mini Star]]s accordingly. The character in first place always receives five Mini Stars, and the character in last place always receives only one Mini Star. If there are three players, whoever is in second wins three Mini Stars, and if there are four players, the characters in second-place and third-place receive three and two Mini Stars, respectively. If the minigame ends in a [[draw]], all of the characters receive the same mini stars.
