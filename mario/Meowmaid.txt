A '''Meowmaid''' is a harmless enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Meowmaids serves as the personal servants of [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]] in [[Fort Francis]], where they are encountered in the hallways. They help record new episodes of "The Blubbening." Despite being labeled as enemies (from the [[Catch Card]] notes), Meowmaids are harmless and will never try to harm the player in any way, and they are the only robots who do not attack [[Mario]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Bowser]], and [[Luigi]]. Oddly enough, they can be picked up with [[Thoreau]] but cannot be destroyed.
