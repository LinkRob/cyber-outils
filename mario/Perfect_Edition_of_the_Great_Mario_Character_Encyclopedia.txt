The {{nihongo|'''''Perfect Edition of the Great Mario Character Encyclopedia'''''|パーフェクト{{hover|版|ばん}} マリオキャラクター{{hover|大事典|だいじてん}}」|''Perfect Ban Mario Character Daijiten''}} is a book published by [[Shogakukan]] some time in 1994. It features bios on characters and enemies from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], many of them being from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]], as well as relatively new spinoffs at the time, such as ''[[Mario & Wario]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Cookie]]'', and releases predating ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', including the [[Donkey Kong (series)|''Donkey Kong'' arcade series]], ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]'', and ''[[Wrecking Crew]]''.
