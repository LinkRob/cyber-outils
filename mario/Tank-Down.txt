'''Tank-Down''' is a Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, during the [[Mustached Hero!]] quest, the player's character, who has to be either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], faces off against [[Spear Guy (character)|Spear Guy]] in this minigame at the [[Spear Thicket]].

==Opening==
The player's character is sitting in their tank. Suddenly, their opponent appears directly in front of them. The characters notice each other and move to separate corners.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Tank Down MPA screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario and Luigi facing off in the minigame]]
Both character drive tanks and try to pound each other with punch-like missiles. Each time a character is hit, they lose one heart and are sent flying. The player's character can also charge up a shot that can deal up to three hearts of damage, but this causes them to move slower. The arena is dark, so neither character can see their opponent unless they are in the vicinity of the light surrounding them. Both characters have a health bar consisting of five hearts, and whoever is first to eliminate their opponent is the winner.
