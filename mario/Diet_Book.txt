The '''Diet Book''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
The Diet Book belongs to [[The Three Hags|Hagra]] of [[The Underwhere]]. Hagra lent this book to a [[D-Man]] a while ago and wants it back, but she is too old to retrieve it herself. When the heroes agree to help her out, Hagra warps them to the D-Man with the Diet Book. The D-Man gives up the Diet Book, saying it was just a fad anyway. When the Diet Book is given back to Hagra, she gives the heroes the [[Door Key]].
