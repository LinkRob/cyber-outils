'''Treasure Pest''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

== Profile ==
===Behavior===
A Treasure Pest disguises itself as a normal [[treasure chest]], but [[Wario]] approaches it, the Treasure Pest reveals its big eye and attacks Wario.

== Flavor text ==
*'''In-game description:''' "''A devious mechanical trap made to look like a treasure chest. It yearns to put a hurtin' on all who draw near to steal the sweetness within.''"

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
A Treasure Pest's name is a pun on "treasure chest," but with the latter word replaced with "pest" to indicate that it is an enemy.

==Trivia==
*Treasure Pests are most likely a reference to [[Mimic]]s, a type of treasure chest appearing throughout role-playing games, such as ''Final Fantasy'' and ''Dragon Quest''.
