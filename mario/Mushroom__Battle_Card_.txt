|name=Mushroom (Battle Card)}}'''Mushroom''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Mushroom is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s healing ability. It is a red and white speckled mushroom with an orange/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 20 coins (painted)

Sell Price: 10 coins (painted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Mushroom is first seen in [[Cherry Lake]], but can also found in [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[Château Chanterelle]], [[Fort Cobalt]], and [[Toad Trainworks]].
