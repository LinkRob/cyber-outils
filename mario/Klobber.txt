|attacks = Ram}}'''Klobber''' is a [[Kremling]] male who is one of [[Kremling Krew]] members. He is the most treacherous Kremling who uses a [[Barrel]] as a disguise to attack the [[Kong]]s when they get close to him and uses it as protection to push them away.

When he uses a [[TNT Barrel]], Klobber becomes '''Kaboom''' and is quite dangerous when using this barrel. Because if he pushes anyone, he explodes like a real bomb.
==Characteristics==
Klobber is a green/yellow/red/black [[Kritter]] with big head, yellow belly, bruised right eye, pink bandage on right side, wears golden earrings and wears a wooden barrel/TNT barrel.

==History==
Along with his fellows from Kremling Krew in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'', Klobber patrolled areas of [[Crocodile Isle]] to stop [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] from saving [[Donkey Kong]] who was kidnapped by [[King K. Rool]]. Klobber's attacks are just pushing, but also pushing to steal ([[Banana]]s & [[Extra-Life Balloon]]s) and defeat (as Kaboom in the TNT barrel). His downside is when he hides in the barrel in which he is hit, he is used as a weapon against his colleagues or destroy secret doors. But he is completely defeated when he is hit by the invincible power of the [[Exclamation Barrel]] or steps on something pointy.

In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'', Klobber along with the Kremling Krew is involved in the distraction under Klump's supervision making Donkey Kong recover all the [[Golden Banana]]s and save his [[Kong Family]] friends to buy time to fix [[Blast-O-Matic]]. He appears on [[Angry Aztec]] temples, [[Gloomy Galleon]] and [[Crystal Caves]] hidden on barrel to attack the Kongs. He can be defeated by [[Orange Grenade]]s, shockwaves or musical instrument power. As Kaboom, he appears on [[Jungle Japes]] mine cart challenge, Angry Aztec five-doored temple and Gloomy Galleon in the TNT Barrel to attack them until he self-destructs into soot.

In ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]'', Klobber is the only one from smaller group of the Kremling Krew along with Kritters on [[Ghost Island]]. Here he just climbs the [[Peg]]s trying to push the Kongs due to the fog that makes him invisible, but he can be seen on the map he is climbing on.

In ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'' as '''Kaboom''', Klobber replaced the [[Squeeloid]]s in the [[Planet Core]]. This time the TNT barrel has spikes in the adornments.
