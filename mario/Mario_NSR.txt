'''Mario NSR''' is a [[Nintendo Switch]]-exclusive [[w:c:rocketleague:Vehicles|Battle-Car]] based on [[Mario]] for ''[[w:c:rocketleague:Rocket League|Rocket League]]''. The Battle-Car was released on November 17, 2017, most likely as a promotion for ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. The Mario NSR can only be used by Orange team players.

==Customization==
The Mario NSR is not compatible with any sort of [[w:c:rocketleague:Decal|Decals]] or [[w:c:rocketleague:Paint|Paint]]. It can only equip the "Mario" [[w:c:rocketleague:Topper|Topper]], which is Mario's hat.

==Trivia==
*When the car jumps, the sound Mario makes when he jumps in the original ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' can be heard.
*On other gaming platforms, Mario NSR is seen as an [[w:c:rocketleague:Octane|Octane]] instead.
