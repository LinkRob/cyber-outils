'''Drain Beam''' is a powerful special move used by [[Raspberry]] and [[Valentina]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
Whenever Drain Beam is used, a huge blue light appears on the screen. The move strikes all party members, dealing high damage.
