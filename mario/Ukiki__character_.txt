'''Ukiki''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. He is the namesake character representative of the species of the [[Ukiki|same name]].

==Biography==
Ukiki was formerly the mayor of the [[Jungle Area]]. He is also the uncle of [[Akiki]], who lives with him in [[Ukiki House]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Ukiki wants to play a prank on Akiki, so he writes a letter to say that he has been kidnapped. The contents of the letter are "bIg teAM cAT leaTHEr fLAKE!? blarmpot," with the capital letters giving a clue to where he is located (the capital letters spell out "I AM AT THE LAKE"). After leaving the note at his house, Ukiki goes to hide at [[Loch Dorrie]]. However, this causes Akiki to panic, so she asks the player's character to help her find him.

At Loch Dorrie, Ukiki jumps out, expecting Akiki to be there, but is surprised to see the player's character. After the situation is explained to Ukiki, he apologizes for the confusion and rewards the player's character with the [[Mini Maze]] [[Gaddget]] before running back to Akiki.

The ending credits state that Ukiki resigned as mayor, and that he is still on good terms with Akiki.
