'''Brutius Maximus Grouchimus''' is a one-time character who makes an appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Great Gladiator Gig]]." He serves as the guardian of the [[Linguini Empire Colosseum]] within the [[Linguini Empire]], based on the {{w|Roman Empire}} in the real world.

==In "The Great Gladiator Gig"==
{{spoiler}}
At first, Brutius Maximus Grouchimus is welcoming to [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]], and invites them to a [[spaghetti]] dinner. However, he later betrays them to ally with [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and the [[Koopa Troop]]. By the end, Brutius Maximus Grouchimus had become good again.
{{endspoiler}}
