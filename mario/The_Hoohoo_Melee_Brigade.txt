'''The Hoohoo Melee Brigade''' is the fifth level of [[Hoohoo Mountain]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 4.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle consists of 7 [[Beanie]]s and 1 [[Rex]], who is also the captain.

The final battle consists of 4 [[Bob-omb]]s and 4 Beanies. The enemy captain is a Bob-omb.

After the final battle, the player unlocks a Bob-omb.

The player can replay the multiple times to sometimes unlock another Bob-omb each time.
