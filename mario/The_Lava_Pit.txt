'''The Lava Pit''' is a stadium in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
The main gimmick of The Lava Pit is the balls of lava that shoot out from the [[lava]]. When a ball of lava touches the ground, it sticks onto the ground, becoming a puddle. Whoever touches a lava puddle gets burnt, and if they have the ball in possession, they drop it as well. Like with most stadiums, The Lava Pit features an [[electric rail]]. If the ball touches the lava, it lobs into the air and instantly begins to glow white.

==In ''Mario Strikers Charged''==
The Lava Pit is an unlockable stadium, and it can be unlocked by completing the [[Fire Cup]] at least once as well as winning against [[Bowser Jr.]], which also unlocks him.
