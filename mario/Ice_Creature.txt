The '''Ice Creature''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
The Ice Creature is a blue, frozen [[Tomato]] with yellow eyes and a mouth full of fangs.

== In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' ==
The Ice Creature can be encountered in the igloo of [[Crystal Caves]] in an area that has to be accessed by [[Lanky Kong]] using is [[Trombone Tremor]] to open the gate to the Ice Creature's room. The Ice Creature states that it has lived for so long and requires someone to play with. The Ice Creature's game involves smashing the titles to the color of the one that smashed it. If Lanky loses, the Ice Creature will comment that he had fun and asks Lanky if he would like to play again. However, if Lanky wins, the Ice Creature will get upset. He gives Lanky a [[Golden Banana]] before kicking him out of his home saying Lanky isn't his friend anymore.
