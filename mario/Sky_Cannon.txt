A '''Sky Cannon''' is an object in ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''.

==Description==
A Sky Cannon is a type of [[cannon]] that, upon being entered, allows [[Mario]] to blast off to a different area. To access the Sky Cannon in [[the Fun Fair]], the [[Flipper (Pinball)|flippers]] must hit Mario into it. While Mario is inside of a Sky Cannon, the game switches to a zoomed-in view where the player can select a location for Mario to blast off to.

==In ''Mario Pinball Land''==
At first, a Sky Cannon is only available in the Fun Fair, but obtaining a [[Star Key (Mario Pinball Land)|Star Key]] in a [[level]] results in a Sky Cannon replacing the [[Question Block|? Block]] in its first area.
