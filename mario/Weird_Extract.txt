The '''Weird Extract''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
It takes the appearance of an oddly-shaped bottle with an irregular green liquid. It is made by mixing [[Peach Juice]] with [[Hot Sauce]]. This drink works exactly like the [[Sleepy Sheep]]. They both make enemies [[sleep]].
