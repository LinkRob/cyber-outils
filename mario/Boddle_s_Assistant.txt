'''Boddle's Assistant''' is a supporting character in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Appearance ==
Boddle's Assistant is a Beanish character with bucked teeth. In the Game Boy version of the game, he has yellow hair and wears a blue shirt. In the 3DS remake, the assistant has his palette changed to have a purple shirt and brown hair and shoes; this is incidentally an unused palette for him in the original Game Boy version.

== History ==
Boddle's Assistant seems to be pessimistic by the way he spoke to the Bros., believing that they are going to mess up with [[Boddle]]. However, Boddle ignored him and aimed to hit him for his impudence. Although he did this action, he seems very loyal to Boddle. He even had no belief that [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] will have the ability to get all the [[Neon Egg]]s, meaning he is thinking they'll never get them.

== Trivia ==
* In the game files, Boddle's Assistant is referred to as "tuki."
