'''Bink''' (ビィンキー, ''Binky'') is a Kong-like character that appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' as a crew member of the landlocked ship, the [[S.S. Chuckola]].

==History==
Bink plays an important role on obtaining the first piece of the [[Beanstar]]. This skeleton challenges [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to a game involving matching barrels to earn a [[Membership Card]]. He can also be seen in the [[Little Fungitown]] Arcade, playing the arcade version of his barrel game.

==Trivia==
*His appearance largely resembles [[Donkey Kong]]. 
**However, in the [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]], his design has been changed so he no longer resembles Donkey Kong but rather the rest of the crew members of the ship. This is most likely to avoid people mistaking Bink for an undead Donkey Kong.
