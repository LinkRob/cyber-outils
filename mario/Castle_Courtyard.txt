'''Castle Courtyard''' (also called '''Castle Gardens''') is in the back of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] in the games [[Super Mario 64|''Super Mario 64'']] and [[Super Mario 64 DS|''Super Mario 64 DS'']]. There are 4 signs and a fountain. 

When you have no stars, there's nothing in the courtyard. After one [[Star]], a [[Boo]] will be in the hallway and a group of Boos will appear in the courtyard (in the DS version hitting Boos with 8 red coins gives you a secret star). After 12 stars (15 in the remake) a [[Big Boo]] will be in the hallway and also appear in the courtyard. Killing it will result you get access to the fifth level, [[Big Boo's Haunt]]. Also in the DS version there 3 are brick blocks, which takes you to [[The Secret Of Battle Fort]] course, which is accessible only if you are [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]], as [[Yoshi]] cannot destroy brick blocks. 

One of Luigi's and Wario's rabbits can be found in the courtyard. Luigi's is on the upper alcove (do a backflip spin) and Wario's rabbit is over the fountain, near the two signs.

== Super Mario Odyssey ==
In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]],'' the castle courtyard returns as an Easter egg containing 2 power stars (acting as a [[Power Moon]]). One for opening several chests in a specific order and the other for using [[Cappy]] on the eternal star statue. The courtyard can be accessed by talking to a [[Toad (character)|Toad]] behind [[Princess Peach's Castle|Princess Peach's castle]] while wearing the ''Super Mario 64'' costume. The courtyard retains its original design from ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.
