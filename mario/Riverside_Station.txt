|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}{{Nihongo|'''Riverside Station'''|リバーサイド駅|Ribāsaido Eki}} is an abandoned train station in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It has been taken over by a swarm of [[Poison Pokey]]s, [[Ruff Puff]]s, [[Spiked Parabuzzy|Spiked Parabuzzies]], [[Spike Top]]s and [[Smorg]]s.

==History==
During the events of ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', some-one thinks it's funny to lift up the Riverside Station drawbridge, preventing the [[Excess Express]] from crossing. A station worker informs [[Mario]] of the situation, and forks over the [[Station Key]], which [[Mario]] uses to access the decrepit interior of the train station and can then use his [[Ultra Boots]] to hit the switch which in turn will allow the bridge to fall in place so the [[Excess Express]] can continue on its trip.
