The '''Greaper Flag''' is one of three flags hidden by the [[Three Musty Fears]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The Greaper Flag in particular is hidden by [[Greaper]], and [[Mario]] can find it by examining a sign around the entry to [[Rose Town]].
