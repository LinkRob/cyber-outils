'''Rage''' is one of [[Princess Peach]]'s [[vibe]]s only appeared in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Description==
When Princess Peach uses Rage, she is furious. Additionally, Peach is immune to the fire. It helps her push down giant [[Spring]]s, light up lamps, fuel hot-air balloons, defeat enemies or scare them away.

== In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' ==
Being one of Princess Peach's vibes, [[Perry]] teaches Peach the Rage vibe in [[Ladida Plains 1-3]] if she hits the [[Perry Block]].

{{Spoiler|hide}}
Peach must use the Rage vibe to light up lamps in [[King Boo]]'s battle.

Peach must use it to push down one of the springs to stun [[Wiggler]] in its battle. Wiggler also uses the Rage vibe.

Peach must use the Rage vibe to inflict damage onto [[Blizzaurus]]; it can damage her shield.

[[Army Hammer Bro]] and [[Bowser]] also utilise the Rage vibe in their respective battles.
{{Endspoiler}}
