A '''Stone Doohickey''' is an obstacle in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
Stone Doohickeys are tall, light red pillars with a crest of the [[Black Jewel]] on top. In each level, a Stone Doohickey guards a [[Trapdoor (Wario World)|goal trapdoor]] leading to the boss, and the only way for [[Wario]] to get rid of it is by bringing a certain number of [[Red Diamond]]s to it. In doing so, the Stone Doohickey disappears, allowing Wario to proceed to fight the boss.

==In ''Wario World''==
A Stone Doohickey appears in all eight courses. The first two courses, [[Greenhorn Forest]] and [[Greenhorn Ruins]], both in [[Excitement Central]], require three Red Diamonds for the Stone Doohickey to move. Both courses in each world increases the Red Diamond requirement by one: [[Horror Manor]] and [[Wonky Circus]] of [[Spooktastic World]] each require four; [[Shivering Mountains]] and [[Beanstalk Way]] of [[Thrillsville]] each require five; and [[Mirror Mansion]] and [[Pecan Sands]] of [[Sparkle Land]] each require six Red Diamonds.
