'''Winky the Frog''' is an [[Animal Buddy]] found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' that allies [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong]] throughout their adventure to regain their bananas from [[King K. Rool]] mainly in [[Monkey Mines]]. He can be found in four levels: [[Winky's Walkway]], [[Bouncy Bonanza]], [[Millstone Mayhem]], and [[Rope Bridge Rumble]]. Winky has very similar abilities to [[Rattly the Rattlesnake|Rattly]] due to both bouncing with extreme heights. Winky also is able to defeat enemies that Donkey and Diddy cannot defeat such as [[Zinger]]s.

==Trivia==
*It is known that Winky, along with [[Expresso the Ostrich|Expresso]] are [[Cranky Kong]]'s favorite Animal Buddies.
*Flibbits, characters from ''[[w:c:banjo:Banjo-Kazooie|Banjo-Kazooie]]'' resemble Winky in some ways.
