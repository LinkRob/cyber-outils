'''''Mario Party: The Top 100''''' is a game in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]] for the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. It is a compilation of the "top" 100 minigames taken from the ten home console (at the time) ''Mario Party'' games. The game supports download play for up to 4 players. The game comes with a favorites option to allow access to quick selections of minigames and play them whenever you like.

It was released in North America on November 10, 2017, in Europe & Australia on December 22, 2017, and in [[Japan]], China, Hong Kong, & Taiwan on December 28, 2017.

It is unknown if the game includes boards like the other ''Mario Party'' games, however, the ''Mario Party: The Top 100'' website mentions setting the number of turns for board-game play. The game also supports most amiibo functionality for Minigame Island and for Minigame Packs. Additionally, [[Minigame Match]] is the only one playable board in the game.

==Amiibo==
On [[Minigame Island]], tapping compatible amiibo will result in in-game bonuses. Tapping an amiibo while standing on a circle with an amiibo symbol will result in 10 Coins. If the amiibo circle is occupied by a Goomba or Koopa Troopa, tapping their respective amiibo figure will result in 50 Coins. If the player's life reaches 0, they can tap a compatible amiibo to restore one life. Each of the compatible amiibo can be used once per day.

Tapping the Goomba or Koopa Troopa amiibo at the Minigame Pack selection screen in [[Minigame Match]] or [[Champion Battles]] results in a shortcut to unlocking the Goomba Minigame Pack or Koopa Troopa Pack.

[[Minigame Match]] is the game's Party Mode. In this mode, there is only one playable board. As a result, this is the closest that the player is ever going to get which regards to normal Party Mode.

==Trivia==
*This is the most recent and the last installment in the [[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]] series where [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] is not a playable character.
*This is the last Mario Party game with a male announcer.
*This is the third and final Nintendo 3DS game in the ''Mario Party'' series (the first two being ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' and ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush|Star Rush]]'').
*Overall, this game has the 2nd most minigames in the whole series, tied with ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. However, neither game introduces any minigames of their own.
