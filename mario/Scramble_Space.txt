A '''Scramble Space''', titled '''Switch Star Space''' in PAL releases,  is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Scramble Spaces exclusively appear on the board [[Star-Crossed Skyway]]. If a character lands onto a Scramble Space, they can decide which order to rearrange the characters and their corresponding [[Mini Star]]s or [[Mini Ztar]]s at the [[Star Stage]].

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
Only three Scramble Spaces appear on Star-Crossed Skyway, and all of them are the last spaces before the second, third, and fourth [[Star Stage Space]]s, respectively.
