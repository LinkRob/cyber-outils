'''Mega Goomba's Ladder Leap''' is a Boss Battles minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. It involves characters engaging in a boss fight against [[Mega Goomba]].

==Gameplay==
Players must climb the ladders and stomp on Mega Goomba's head as he rampages through the field. Additionally, [[Waddlewing]]s fly around the arena trying to hit you. A character earns a [[point]] each time they stomp on Mega Goomba's head, and can jump on Mega Goomba up to three times in succession before it forcefully shakes them off. If a character touches Mega Goomba or a Waddlewing, they lose a point.

Halfway through the minigame, the Mega Goomba begins gets really angry and paces around in a rage. Waddlewings also attack more often.

The Mega Goomba has to be jumped on a total of 75 times until it can be defeated, and whoever deals the finishing attack earns an additional three points.

==Ending==
The Mega Goomba falls onto its back and explodes, revealing [[Mini Star]]s.
