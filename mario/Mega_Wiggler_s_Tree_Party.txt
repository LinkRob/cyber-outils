'''Mega Wiggler's Tree Party''' is a board in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Description==
The board takes place in a tree orchard. It is owned by a [[Mega Wiggler]], who is in an opening in the center of the board. There are a few [[Space]]s atop Mega Wiggler's segments that the characters can use to move between the western and eastern sides of the board. The [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] is located at a random spot on the board.

The event "Wiggler's big move" involves Mega Wiggler moving, thereby altering the path, if the player either lands on an [[Event Space]] next to a bell or uses the board-exclusive [[Wiggler Bell]] item.

Around the northeastern side of the board is a tree with three [[beehive]]s. For the "Honey harvesting" event, hosted by a [[Spike]], the player uses a stick to choose between beehives, though one of them contains [[bee]]s. If the player chooses one of the two beehives containing honey, they earn [[coin]]s and can then select from the two remaining beehives. The event ends once the player selects either the beehive with the bees or both beehives with honey.

There is a Piranha Plant on the northern and southern parts of the board. Landing on an Event Space next to a Piranha Plant initiates the event "Piranha Plant's attack," in which it lunges at whoever landed there, stealing five coins from them. Afterward, a water droplet falls on the Piranha Plant, increasing its size slightly, so the next time someone lands on its Event Space, the Piranha Plant takes away one more coin than it had done before.
