The '''Pirate Ship''' is a playable court in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It is the third court of the [[Rainbow Tourney]].

==Description==
[[File:Pirate Ship icon.png|thumb|left|Preview icon]]
The court is on the deck of a pirate ship, which is always in the middle of a downpour. basketball hoops have a backboard shaped like a [[Dry Bones]]'s head.

The court features a few different gimmicks. [[Cannon]]s mounted on the sides of the galleon periodically shoot [[cannonball]]s that roll slowly onto the court. If a character touches a cannonball, it explodes, launching everyone around it, so holding on the ball is more challenging than in other courts. The other obstacle is [[Gooper Blooper]], who tries to hit the basketball players using his tentacles if they are too close to the edge.

The court could be the pirate ship in the background of [[Koopa Troopa Beach (court)|Koopa Beach]].

There is a very similar course in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'' titled [[Ghoulish Galleon]], which similarly takes place on a pirate ship during heavy rainfall.
