}}The '''Piranha Prowler''' is a heavyweight kart in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' that has a [[Piranha Plant]] face on the front of it. It is green with spots (depending on the character) and is extremely heavy.
==Information==
Thre Piranha Prowler is unlocked by winning the [[Engine Class|50cc]] [[Special Cup]] or playing 600 games.

The kart has high weight and speed but low acceleration, off-road, mini-turbo, drift and handling.
==NPC Uses==

The normal and expert staff ghosts for GCN [[Waluigi Stadium]] (Nin*Ryo, Waluigi) and [[Bowser's Castle]] (Nin*YABUKI, Bowser) use the Piranha Prowler.
