'''Snow Globe''' is a [[Gaddget]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is given to the player by the [[Dolphin (character)|Dolphin]] for completing the [[Comedy Bomb]] quest.

==Description==
Contained within the Snow Globe is a Christmas tree and a [[Mr. Blizzard]]. The Snow Globe is intended to act like a regular one, and the player can use the Control Pad to shake it.
