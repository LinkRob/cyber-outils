'''Deck Dry Bones''' is a ​Mid-boss Battle ​mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. In Solo mode, it appears in [[Boo's Horror Castle]] as a boss minigame. A single [[Dry Bones]] shows up as its boss, although his size could indicate he is actually a [[Super Dry Bones|supersized variant of the species]].

==​Gameplay==
[[File:Deck Dry Bones points earned.png|thumb|left|200px|The characters earn their first set of points]]
This is a matching game. To play the game, look at the cards by memory; then when the cards are turned, [[Dry Bones]] lands on a shape. Choose a card with the same shape, if it's the wrong card, then it'll earn you nothing. Using the Dry Bones card can cause you to lose 1 point, which he'll throw a bone at your character.

Upon losing half his health, Dry Bones makes things complicated by us looking at the cards and then when cards are turned around, running over 1 row or column of a card and then some or all of those cards be swapped.

==​Rules==
''"Watch the cards. After they flip over, try to choose a card that matches the image of the platform Dry Bones is standing on."''

==​Controls==
Hold the Wii Remote vertically. (This means pointing it at the screen.)
*Use your '''Wii Remote'''​ to point at the censor.
*Use the {{button|Wii|A}} to select a card.
