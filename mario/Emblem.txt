An '''emblem''' is a symbol used to represent a certain character, usually on an item belonging to them or a scoreboard display. Some games feature them more prominently than others, such as ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', in which the player can design a custom emblem for the characters to use on their [[kart]]s. The star of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], [[Mario]] has a red "M" as his emblem, originating from the front of his [[Mario's Cap|cap]], and is often displayed on a white backdrop.

The {{series|Mario & Sonic}} gives ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' characters each their own emblem, just like each of the ''Mario'' characters have, and the spectators can often be seen waving a flag of a character's emblem in the background.
