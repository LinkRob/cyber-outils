'''Crabbie Grass''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|its remake]]. The item's name is a pun on "crabgrass."

==Description==
It is a small, bitter-tasting herb and the only known cure to [[Bean Fever]]. Its only known location it grows at is the end of the temple of [[Guffawha Ruins]], tucked into the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. In the remake, the Crabbie Grass is red instead of its original green coloration.

==History==
During the adventure, [[Mario]] contracts Bean Fever after eating an [[Invincishroom]] (clarified to be a misidentified [[Poison Mushroom]] in the game's remake), and [[Luigi]] is tasked by the [[doctor]] with retrieving it. Luigi finds only one piece of this herbal medicine, by completing a minigame with the [[Moai]], but it is sufficient enough to cure Mario.

==Flavor text==
*'''In-game description:''' ''Bitter medicine for Bean Fever.''
