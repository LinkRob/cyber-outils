'''Castle of the Big Burt Bros.''' is the fourth level of [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. It is the game's first castle and a [[Yellow Yoshi]] takes control in this level. The level consists of several different enemies, gimmicks, and traps and can turn hard for an unprepared gamer, especially because of the [[lava]] pits, the key is to proceed slowly and take care of any enemies in the way. The bosses in this level are the [[Big Burt Bros.]], two [[Burt]]s enlarged by [[Kamek]]'s magic. A [[Stork Stop]] appears in this level after [[Catch the Breeze]] is completed.

==Walkthrough==
Starting off at the Castle's entrance, advance to the right across the arced bridges above the [[lava]] and hit the [[Yellow Egg Block]]s to snag some [[Yellow Yoshi Egg]]s for use on the [[Bucket]]s above (hitting these Buckets on the sides with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] will cause it to tip and drop some [[Coin]]s, continue onward and jump on the [[Ferris Wheel]]s here, the are [[Coin]]s along them and both Ferris Wheels have two [[Red Coin]]s each. After the Ferris Wheels, hit the [[Winged Cloud]] to reveal a [[! Switch]], stepping on the switch will cause the outlined blocks to become solid, hop across the blocks and the Ferris Wheel) (and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] above the fourth set of switch blocks to get some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and then hop up to the upper platform, pound the [[Stake]] to reach the niche where the [[Smiley Flower]] is and grab it. After that, drop back down and continue onward dealing with the [[Shy Guy]]s along the way, grab the [[Coin]]s and two [[Red Coin]]s above and then exit through the door.

In the next room, continue to the right and hit the [[Egg Block]] for some extra [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s and grab the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]s along the path while avoiding the [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s, on the hedge with the dual-headed [[Ball 'n' Chain]], there is a [[Winged Cloud]] in the blunt right corner, hit it with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to get some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], next grab the [[Coin]] along the path (as one of them is a [[Red Coin]]) whilst avoiding the six spinning [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s, after this, tag the [[Middle Ring]] and grab the [[Coin]] and [[Red Coin]] in the area, hop up the platforms and once at the top, move to the left to reach a platform with some [[Coin]]s and a [[Smiley Flower]], then move all the way to the right to grab the [[Baby Coin]] and to read a [[Hint Block]] (if you want to), after that, drop back down, watch out for the [[lava]] and avoid the [[Woozy Guy]]s and [[Shy Guy]]s in the area, as you get near the [[lava]], [[Co-Blargg]]s will start attacking, avoid them and hop across the [[Paddle Wheel]] collecting the [[Coin]]s as four of the coins at the end of the bottom row are [[Red Coin]]s, next hop down to the lower platform to grab the [[Coin]]s (if you want to) and hop up to the singular platform and toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] in line with the arrows so it ricochets off the wall and grabs the [[Smiley Flower]] in the alcove above, after that, enter the door at the end.

In the next room, advance to the right and hop over the [[lava]] pool grabbing the [[Coin]]s while you do, next grab some [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s from the nearby [[Egg Block]] then hop onto the [[Flatbed Ferry]] and go up and grab the [[Coin]]s and two [[Red Coin]]s here, to the right, there are two [[Yellow Egg Block]]s and a single [[Red Egg Block]], hit these to get two more [[Yellow Yoshi Egg]]s and a [[Red Yoshi Egg]], then drop down to the platform to the left where two [[Bouncing Burt]]s are, either ground pound them or toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them, either way, they will explode and release some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] to collect, after this, grab the [[Smiley Flower]] and then drop back down to the main level, grab the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]s above the moving [[Flatbed Ferry]] and drop down to the lower [[Flatbed Ferry]] (while being careful not to fall into the [[lava]]) and grab the two [[Coin]]s and well as the last [[Red Coin]], next, advance to the right, hop onto the moving [[Flatbed Ferry|Flatbed Ferries]] and hit the [[Winged Cloud]]s above with [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s, the first two give [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and [[Coin]]s respectively while the third one reveals some stairs, enter the door at the top to reach a secret area with some [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[Lantern Ghost]]s, defeat all the enemies in this room to reveal the [[Smiley Flower]] to collect, after this, exit the area and hop back on the [[Flatbed Ferry|Flatbed Ferries]] to reach a higher platform with a [[Box]], ground pound the Box to reveal a [[Key]] and grab the Key to unlock the locked door at the room's end, from here, climb the stairs, tag the [[Middle Ring]], fill your [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] supply via the [[Egg Block]] (if you haven't already) (a [[Stork Stop]] appears in this area after the next level is completed as well), and then enter the Red Door.

===BOSS BATTLE: Big Burt Bros.===
Upon entering the room and advancing to the right, two [[Burt]]s appear, [[Kamek]] then emerges and uses his magic to enlarge the Burt Bros. into the [[Big Burt Bros.]] These two giant Burts attack by hopping around the room (sometimes on eachother) at a very fast speed). If you want to finish this fight with 30 Stars, stay as far away from the center of the room as possible, due to the unpredictability of the Burts, they will crush you if you're out in the open so remain in the small pits on each side and when the Burts come at you, duck to avoid being hit. To beat the Burts, hit them with [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s, each time you do, their pants will come down, if you're running low on [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s grab some more with Yoshi's tongue from the [[Egg Plant]] in the center of the room and continue attacking the Big Burt Bros. from the shelter points, after hitting them six times, their pants fade and they become turn red in embarrassment and explode and the level is completed.

==Baby Coin Location==
You will need [[Baby Mario]] for this [[Baby Coin]] after tagging the first [[Middle Ring]], jump up along the platforms while avoiding the [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s, the [[Baby Coin]] is located atop the second hedge with the dual-headed [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s, grab it.
