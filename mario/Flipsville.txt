'''Flipsville''' is the titular location in the [[Flipsville Galaxy]]. It appears to be based around a doll's house. Flipsville's [[Gravity]] is irregular: it will change direction when [[Mario]] uses his [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] attack on a grate.

There is a house which is found on the galaxy's starting planet (encompassing the whole planet), and a garden area on another planet. There is also a digs region that is visited in the second mission.
