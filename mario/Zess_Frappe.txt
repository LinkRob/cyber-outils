The '''Zess Frappe''' is a [[recipe]] made by [[Zess T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Description ==
A Zess Frappe is made by mixing an [[Ice Storm]] with either [[Maple Syrup]] or [[Jammin' Jelly]]. [[Mario]] can drink Zess Frappe to restore 20 [[Flower Point|FP]], but if Mario uses it in battle, he is at risk of becoming [[frozen]].
