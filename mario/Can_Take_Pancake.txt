'''Can Take Pancake''' is an unlockable Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera shows the players and customers enjoying on the table, then the camera pans above the plate.

== Gameplay ==
The players must grab the pancakes onto their plate. Regular pancakes are worth a point while star pancakes are worth three points. The game ends when nothing is left on the big plate.

== Ending ==
The winners do their victory pose and other players enjoy their time on the table.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move cursor
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – Grab
