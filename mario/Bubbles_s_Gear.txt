'''Bubbles's Gear''' is a [[clothing]] item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They can be worn by either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], and are sometimes dropped by [[Fuzzbush]]es, which is the only means of obtaining Bubbles's Gear from.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', equipping Bubbles's Gear increases the user's [[HP]] by 6 and their [[Defense]] by 40. Its possibility of being dropped by Fuzzbushes only occurs if Mario or Luigi either use the [[Advanced Swing Bros.]] attack or have the [[Game Boy Horror SP]] equipped.

In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] remake, Bubbles's Gear instead increases a user's Defense by 21, nearly half as in the original version, and it no longer increases any HP. Bubbles's Gear still has a possibility of being dropped by Fuzzbushes, but no longer have any prerequisites. Bubbles's Gear now has the [[Poison Dodge]] [[Special Effect]], giving its user invulnerability to the [[Poison (status effect)|Poison]] status effect. It can be sold at a low price of 5 [[coin]]s.
