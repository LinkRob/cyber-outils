'''Arwings''' are small spacecraft vehicles used by [[Fox McCloud]] and the [[Star Fox (team)|Star Fox team]] in every ''Star Fox'' series installment. Arwings have made a few minor appearances in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, and they are recurring stage hazards in most of the ''Star Fox''-themed stages within the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]]. An Arwing makes its first ''Mario''-related appearance in the form of a small cameo in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. A similar spacecraft is the [[Wolfen]], [[w:c:starfox:Star Wolf|Star Wolf]]'s equivalent of the Arwing.

==History==
===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', an Arwing model makes a cameo appearance in the [[Hino Mart]], next to a [[Blue Falcon]] and [[Fire Stingray]].

===''Super Mario Maker''===
[[File:Arwing SMM sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', one of the 8-bit [[Costume Mario|costumes]] [[Mario]] can receive from a [[Mystery Mushroom]] is a Walker from ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox Zero|Star Fox Zero]]''. Upon touching the Mystery Mushroom, the sound effect when collecting a supply ring in ''Star Fox Zero'' will play. Pressing {{button|wii|Padup}} will have it transform into an Arwing and use General Pepper's "Good Luck" voice clip from ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox 64|Star Fox 64]]''. It also turns into an Arwing while running at high speeds or while swimming. The death tune is heard when losing a life in ''Star Fox Zero''. When clearing a level, the "Mission Accomplished" theme from ''Star Fox Zero'' plays.

==''Wario'' franchise==
===''WarioWare'' series===
All of the ''Star Fox''-themed microgames in the {{series|WarioWare}} involve the player controlling an Arwing, reused or derived from its design in ''Star Fox'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]], as this is the game that each of these microgames are based on.

====''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''====
[[File:ROB WWSM screenshot.png|thumb|180px|The Arwing encounters R.O.B. in the Star Fox microgame]]
In ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', the player takes control of an Arwing throughout the boss [[microgame]] [[Star Fox (WarioWare: Smooth Moves)|Star Fox]].

====''WarioWare: D.I.Y.''====
In ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'', an Arwing is playable in another microgame titled [[Star Fox (WarioWare: D.I.Y.)|Star Fox]]. Here, the player has to tap the Touch Screen to have the Arwing shoot a bullet at an enemy onscreen.

====''WarioWare Gold''====
In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', an Arwing is playable in yet another microgame titled [[Star Fox (WarioWare Gold)|Star Fox]]. Here, the Arwing's right wing has taken critical damage, and the object is to protect the Arwing from taking any further damage from enemies and obstacles. The player can get the Arwing to move by tilting the system and, like in previous ''Star Fox''-themed microgames, the Arwing can still shoot at enemies and obstacles alike.

==Crossover appearances==
{{Smashpedia}}
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
[[File:Starfoxarwingssbb.jpg|thumb|Fox landing after jumping out of an Arwing in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
Arwings appear in the background of all Star Fox stages in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], namely [[Sector Z]], [[Corneria]], [[Venom]] and [[Lylat Cruise]]. They fly by, creating another platform, or shooting the players from behind. They are piloted by [[Peppy Hare]] and [[Slippy Toad]], and either [[Fox McCloud]] or [[Falco Lombardi]] in the Adventure Mode. Additionally, at the beginning of every match in every ''Super Smash Bros.'' game except ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', Fox or Falco would enter the stage from jumping off an Arwing. Arwings also appear in all openings of the ''Super Smash Bros.'' games.

====''Super Smash Bros. Melee''====
In Adventure Mode of ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'', Arwings are not present in the first battle against Fox or Falco at Corneria, but charge for a heavy attack in the second battle. A [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] of an Arwing also appears.

====''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''====
In [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]], Fox rides in his Arwing while avoiding the shots fired from the [[Halberd]]. The Halberd is eventually able to hit the Arwing and knock it down. The Arwing flies right past [[Kirby]] and the rescued princess ([[Princess Peach|Peach]] or [[Zelda]]) knocking them both off the ship. The Arwing then crashes down in [[Donkey Kong Island]]. Meanwhile, [[Diddy Kong]] is walking by and notices the destroyed Arwing. However, [[Rayquaza]] suddenly appears and launches a beam at Fox and his Arwing. The beam causes an explosion which sets the Arwing on flames. However, somehow, Fox survives the hit and jumps out of his Arwing and saves Diddy from Rayquaza. Later in the story, Fox rides an Arwing in the dogfight against [[Ganondorf]] and [[Bowser]] in their large [[Subspace Gunship]].

====''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U''====
Arwings appear in the background of the [[Orbital Gate Assault]] stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', where they take their appearance from ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox: Assault|Star Fox: Assault]]''. Eventually during a match, players are able to fight on top of the Arwings as they enter the gate itself.

====''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', for Fox and Falco's [[Final Smash]], instead of piloting the Landmaster as in previous games, they call upon an army of Arwings that swoop into the stage, catching any opponents they hit. Fox and Falco then join in and fire multiple shots at the opponents, inflicting damage and launching them.

==In other media==
===''Nintendo Monopoly''===
An Arwing appears as a station in the 2006 version of ''[[Nintendo Monopoly]]'', taking the place of Short Line Railroad. In the 2010 version, it was replaced by [[w:c:zelda:Epona|Epona]] from the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''Legend of Zelda'' series]].

===''Super Mario'' film series===
====''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''====
In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', a model of an Arwing appears on a TV in Mario's bedroom in Brooklyn as he plays ''Kid Icarus'' and watches the news.

====''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''====
[[File:Mario Galaxy Movie Arwing.jpeg|thumb|Fox McCloud's Arwing in ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'']]
In ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'', an Arwing piloted by [[Fox McCloud (Illumination)|Fox McCloud]] makes an appearance. It combines its designs from ''Star Fox 64'' and ''Star Fox Zero'', but it has two extra seats in the engines, a seat behind the pilot in the cockpit, and pop-out laser cannons in between the engines.

In the film, the Arwing was first seen parked at [[Gateway Galaxy (Illumination)|Gateway Galaxy]], and it later took off with Mario and his friends to [[Space Junk Galaxy (Illumination)|Space Junk Galaxy]]. It fought off against Bowser Jr.'s UFO, but was shot down and crashed on the [[Fossil Falls (Illumination)|Fossil Falls]] planet. In the first post-credits scene on the snow planet, [[Rosalina (Illumination)|Rosalina]] fixed up the Arwing's warp drive before heading back to the Lylat System.
