The '''Wind Crystal''' is one of the four [[Elemental Crystals]] who fights alongside [[Culex]] in [[Monstro Town]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a sentient crystal associated with the element of wind (or electricity, as evidenced from its attacks). The Wind Crystal, like the other three Elemental Crystals, originates from the ''[[Final Fantasy]]'' franchise, but in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'', the Wind Crystal is from the dimension of [[Vanda]], like Culex.

The Wind Crystal has the highest [[Defense]] out of all the Elemental Crystals, and it also has low [[HP]] but decent [[Attack]]. The Wind Crystal uses many thunder-based moves such as [[Static E!]] and [[Electroshock]]. It can also use the dreaded [[Petal Blast]] to turn one of [[Mario]]'s party into [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]]. The Wind Crystal is vulnerable to [[Jump]]ing attacks, and it is the only Elemental Crystal to have a vulnerability that is not a [[Special Attack]].
