'''Poltergust G-00''' is [[Luigi]]'s [[Final Smash]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', being titled '''Poltergust 5000''' in the former game. The move replaces the [[Negative Zone]] from ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

==Effects==
With his [[Poltergust G-00]] (or [[Poltergust 5000 (object)|Poltergust 5000]] in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U''), Luigi can vacuum anyone in range into the device and blows them out with pretty high knockback. The move isn't too damaging itself, but it is mainly used to blow away opponents. This move is KO potential at high damage percentages. The move is also able to suck up any nearby items and objects, which can become useful in further damage if blown at another player, depending on the object.

==Trivia==
*The Poltergust 5000 is the only Final Smash of a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' character to replace a different one from ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''.
*This Final Smash functions similarly to [[Kirby]]'s Final Smash from ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', [[Cook Kirby]].
