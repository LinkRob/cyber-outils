'''Dry Desert Mushrooms''', or '''Layer-Cake Desert-5''', is the fifth level of [[Layer-Cake Desert]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is an athletic mushroom course with [[Stone Spike]]s and moving platforms. This course is unlocked by completing [[Stoneslide Tower]], and completing this level unlocks [[Blooming Lakitus]]. 
==Layout==
The course begins near a [[lift]], which is found below a [[Mushroom Platform]] with two [[Stone Spike]]s on it. A [[? Block]] with a Stone Spike above it, followed by another lift with two Stone Spikes above it. More Mushroom Platforms are found, followed by more lifts and Stone Spikes. A [[Stretch Shroom]] is found with a ? Block above it, followed by three Stretch Shrooms beneath two Stone Spikes. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is then found before another ? Block. More lifts are found below another Stretch Shroom with Stone Spikes above it. More lifts, Stretch Shrooms, and Stone Spikes are found in a section of the level with a [[Green Ring]]. A [[Koopa Troopa]] is found on a Mushroom Platform, which can be used to hit a [[Roulette Block]] beneath it. A lift that travels on a track beneath multiple Stretch Shrooms and Stone Spikes, ending in front of the [[Goal Pole]].

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coins is found between two stationary platforms.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is found between two Stone Spikes on a Stretch Shroom.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In a [[Warp Pipe]] below two Stone Spikes, the player must use the [[POW Block]] to make the third Star Coin drop when the two Stretch Shrooms below are expanded.
