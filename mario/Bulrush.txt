'''Bulrushes''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bulrushes are light-blue, bison-like creatures with blue horns. They appear to be wearing a black jumpsuit with a blue stripe spanning from top to bottom.

===Behavior===
Bulrushes will charge ahead until they bump into an unbreakable surface, and after doing so they will then turn around and rush the other way. They can also be seen in herds, presumed to be one of the wonder effects. Their rush attacks can hurt the player, as well as break certain blocks.

== Trivia ==

* Their name is a pun on "bull" and "rush".
