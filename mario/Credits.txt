'''Credits''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Gaddget allows the player to see the game's credits. During the credits screen, the player can also control [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], and [[Spear Guy (character)|Spear Guy]] with {{button|gba|A}}, {{button|gba|B}}, and the {{button|gba|Pad}}, like in [[Dance Stage]]. The player's name is displayed at the end.

Credits can be obtained by purchasing it in Challenge Mode for 2,000 [[coin]]s.
