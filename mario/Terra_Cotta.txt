'''Terra Cotta''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are stronger variants of [[Terrapin]]s encountered in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] and [[The Trial Course]] within it.

==Battle==
In battle, Terra Cottas attack by using their special move, [[Terrapunch]], against a party member. Terra Cottas also flee from battle if [[Bowser]] is an active party member.
