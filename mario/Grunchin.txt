'''Grunchin''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Grunchin are explosive {{w|sea urchin}} creatures that are either purple or red in color.

===Behavior===
In underwater levels, Grunchin form rings to block paths and to shield themselves against the [[Subwarine]]'s torpedoes. The rings are primarily made from purple Grunchin, with red ones spread out between them.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Grunchin are the only enemy to appear in both underwater Subwarine levels and standard platforming levels. A Red Grunchin can be defeated, which in turns destroy all other Grunchin in a ring. However, purple Grunchin are invincible. A defeated Grunchin produces a silver [[coin]] and a bronze coin for each subsequent Grunchin in its ring. In certain levels, such as [[Savannah Valley]] and [[Its-all Mine]], red Grunchin are produced by spawners as timed hazards that [[Wario]] has to dodge.
