The '''Corkscrew Conk''' is a Special Move in the Nintendo GameCube game ''Wario World''.

==Description==
To execute a Corkscrew Conk, Wario has to begin using a [[Dash Attack]] and jump right in the middle of using the move. In doing so, Wario hurtles into the air, wildly spinning in the process. Corkscrew Conk can be used to cross large gaps, as a way to hit even powerful enemies into the air, or sometimes even to hit a [[treasure button]] that is otherwise out of reach. While using Corkscrew Conk, Wario does not have control until he lands, which is when the move ends.
