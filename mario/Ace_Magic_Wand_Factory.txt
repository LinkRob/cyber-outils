The '''Ace Magic Wand Factory''' is a location in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[A Toadally Magical Adventure]]."

==Description==
The Ace Magic Wand Factory is a factory that produces [[Magic Wand]]s. It is located in the [[Sky World]].
