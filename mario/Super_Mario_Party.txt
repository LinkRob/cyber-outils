'''''Super Mario Party''''' is a game in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]] for the [[Nintendo Switch]]. 

The game supports play for up to 4 players. It was released Internationally on October 5, 2018.

==Features==
*84 new minigames with many ways to play.
*Use of Joy-Con controllers in different ways.
*Like ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'', players can get allies.
*Similar to ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', players team up into partners.
*Super Mario Party features online leaderboards with prizes.
*'''[[Rhythm Minigame|Rhythm]]''', '''[[Co-op Minigame|Co-op]]''', and '''[[Toad's Rec Room]]''' minigames are also minigame categories.
*'''Mario Party:''' The original Mario Party board game has new elements like character dice blocks, a party system, and new boards. This is the real Party Mode. However, this only covers the traditional 4 player battle royale mode (unlike in previous installments from ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' through ''[[Mario Party 8|8]]'' and ''[[Mario Party DS|DS]]''). Team Mode is instead part of another mode (that being, ''Partner Party''). 
*'''Partner Party:''' A team edition of Mario Party featuring free movement and shared dice rolls. 
*'''Online Mario-thon:''' An online mode that features five different, ever-changing minigames. Players can play against friends or people all over the world.
*'''Sound Stage:''' Players play rhythm-based minigames such as Time To Shine, Baton and On, All-Star Swingers, Take a Stab, and one that features a horse. 
*'''Toad's Rec Room:''' A new playstyle that pairs up two Nintendo Switch systems for Tabletop mode.
*'''River Survival:''' Teams are assigned a boat which they have to row downstream.

==Trivia==
*This is the first installment in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series (if counting just the home console games): 
**to have Bowser as a fully playable character (aside from Bowser Party from ''[[Mario Party 10]]'').
***The next installment (after this one ''Super Mario Party'') is ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''. 
**to not introduce a playable protagonist as a newcomer.
**to not have [[Bowser Space]]s
***Bowser Spaces do not appear due to [[Bowser]] being a playable character. This is replaced with [[Bad Luck Space]]s (which are the successor to Bowser Spaces). 
*While this installment in the series has more than one playable board, this has the fewest boards of any installment released for a home console, with only four.
*This installment (along with ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', ''[[Mario Party 6|6]]'', and ''[[Mario Party Superstars|Superstars]]'') are the only three home console games to not have any Bowser-themed boards.
*This is the first installment since ''[[Mario Party DS]]'' to have the game played in the traditional Mario Party style, as ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 10|10]]'' (in the home console games) are both played out in a different way in the rest of the series.
**Additionally, this is the first installment by Nd Cube to play the same as the games by Hudson Soft, as the other games by them had played differently to the rest of the series, such as making the players drive in cars around the board and collecting [[Mini Star]]s rather than regular stars.
*This is the first installment to not be a numbered installment (counting just the home console games). (That is, if ''[[Mario Party]]'' --the first installment in the series-- is known as "Mario Party 1".) 
**This means this installment and the other two home console games (''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'') do not use the numbers 11, 12, and 13. (That is, despite the fact that these 3 home console games are the 11th, 12th, and 13th installments in the main series.) 
*This is the last installment to have playable antagonists until ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'' ([[Goomba]], [[Bowser Jr.]], [[Boo]], [[Hammer Bro]], [[Blooper]], [[Dry Bones]], and [[Pom Pom]]). 
**The next installment --''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''-- is back to having playable protagonists (including [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], [[Wario]], [[Waluigi]], [[Yoshi]], [[Rosalina]], [[Birdo]], and [[Donkey Kong]]). That is, not counting Mario, Wario, Waluigi, Birdo, and Donkey Kong who have been both a protagonist and a villain.
**In the next installment (''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'') [[Goomba]], [[Bowser Jr.]], [[Boo]], [[Hammer Bro]], [[Blooper]], [[Dry Bones]], and [[Pom Pom]] all return. However, the newcomer --also a playable antagonist-- is also introduced as a playable character (that being, [[Ninji]]).
