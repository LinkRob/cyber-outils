'''Compat-I-Com''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is one of the Gaddgets available from the start.

==Description==
Compat-I-Com can be used to play a two-player minigame on the same system. Both players must press the two buttons on their left and right side each. After doing so, the game calculates a random percentage out of a 100; the higher the percentage rating, the more the Gaddget deems the two players to be compatible for one another.
