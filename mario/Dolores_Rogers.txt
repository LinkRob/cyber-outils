'''Dolores Rogers''' (born August 13, 1967) is a voice actress who voiced as [[Bowser Jr.]], [[Baby Bowser]] and [[Wiggler]]. Her roles as Baby Bowser and Bowser Jr were taken over by [[Caety Sagoian]] whilst her role as Wiggler was replaced by [[Eri Inagawa]].
