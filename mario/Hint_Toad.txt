The '''Hint Toad''' (also commonly referred to as simply '''Brigade Blue Toad''') is a Blue-colored Toad character who first appeared in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' were he is part of the [[Toad Brigade]]''.'' Unlike his other other members, Hint Toad is the only Toad that wears goggles, which depicts him being the smartest Toad of the group. 

== [[Super Mario Galaxy]] ==
As a member of the Toad Brigade, Hint Toad along with his members help [[Mario]] in finding [[Power Star|Power Stars]] to retrieve the [[Comet Observatory|comet observatory]].

== [[Super Mario Galaxy 2]] ==
Hint Toad returns his role with the Toad Brigade to help Mario save [[Princess Peach]].

== [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]] ==
In the [[Nintendo Switch|Switch]] version, Hint Toad apears as one of the playable characters in multiplayer mode of: [[The Adventures of Captain Toad]]. In [[Bowser's Fury]], [[Mario]] saves Hint Toad from dangrious obstacles along with [[Bowser Jr.]]. He later regroups with the [[Toad Brigade]] at the campsite.

== [[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]] ==
Hint Toad is a supporting character who helps [[Captain Toad]] on his adventures similarily to the [[Super Mario Galaxy (series)]]. 

== [[Super Mario Odyssey]] ==
In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', he can assist [[Mario]] in finding [[Power Moon]]s for a price of 50 [[coin]]s. He appears after the main conflict in a kingdom is solved, along with [[Uncle amiibo]].

== [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park]] ==
Brigade Blue Toad reappears running Toad Brigade Training Camp in Bellabel Park.
