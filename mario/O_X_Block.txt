'''O/X Blocks''' are [[block]]s that appear in the [[1-Up Room]]s of ''[[Super Mario World]]''. When a [[? Block|Prize Block]] in one of the rows of Prize Blocks in the 1-Up Room is struck, the block turns either into a block with an ''O'' on it or into a [[Empty Block|Brown Block]]. If an O/X Block turns into a Brown Block when struck, all remaining Prize Blocks in the row will do the same. The order of the blocks that must be struck to reveal an O/X Block is randomized, determined when the Prize Block is hit.<ref>[https://youtu.be/EzQq79Rzk_w Super Mario World - O/X Block Randomization Proof of Concept]. YouTube.</ref> Upon the player successfully revealing the first one or two O/X Blocks, each block releases a [[coin]]. If a full row of O/X Blocks is successfully revealed, the last one struck in that row releases a [[1-Up Mushroom]].

While the order is random, a [[glitch]] allows the player to successfully execute a perfect score: If the player [[Spin Jump|spin-jumps]] into the blocks while in [[Cape Mario|Caped form]], the Prize Blocks will always turn into O Blocks. However, the row of blocks must be hit in order from left to right.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8p5cfC87x0k 🍄 Do you know this TRICK in the 1-UP Bonus Room??]. YouTube.</ref> This method does not work in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake, ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.
