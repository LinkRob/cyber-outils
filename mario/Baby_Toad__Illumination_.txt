'''Baby Toad''' is a character who made a cameo in [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'']].

== Profile ==

=== Physical description ===
As a toddler, Baby Toad wears an only wears his diaper pants just like [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]].
