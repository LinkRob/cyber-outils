'''Enigmas''' are pig/bat-hybrid enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Enigmas are mainly fought in [[Moleville Mines]], where they tend to appear alongside weaker enemies such as [[Bob-omb]]s. Enigmas are actually composed of smaller colonies that form the bigger Engima. They have a variant named [[Gorgon]].

==Battle==
In battle, Engimas tend to attack physically which can be troublesome due to their high physical attack power. They can also use [[Echofinder]] which causes the [[Mute]] status on a party member.

===Strategy===
Enigmas are weak to [[Jump]] attacks so one should be easily taken down with [[Mario]]'s [[Super Jump]] technique.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Enigma has the same name as the word "enigma," which is used to describe something unusual. The "ig" suffix could refer to pig, which Enigmas share some common qualities with.
