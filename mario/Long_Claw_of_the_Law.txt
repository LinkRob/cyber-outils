'''Long Claw of the Law''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] from ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame starts with each character controlling an extendable claw. A Wanted! poster is seen with a picture of one of the following: [[Goomba]], [[Bob-omb]], [[Chain Chomp]], [[Boo]], [[Thwomp]], or [[Shy Guy]]. Players must use their claws to grab three of the type of enemy seen on the Wanted! poster, who are hiding behind various doors throughout the setting. The doors open and close, so players need to time their grab. Although the player needs to capture only one type of enemy, four of the six appear in the minigame. If the player attempts to grab the wrong type of enemy, the door will slam shut immediately. The first player to grab three of the right enemy wins. If nobody does so within 60 seconds, the minigame will end in a draw.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|R}} &ndash; Rotate right 
*{{button|gcn|L}} &ndash; Rotate left
*{{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Move up and down
*{{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Catch character

==Flavor text==
*'''Game Rules''' &ndash; ''"Catch the character shown in the wanted poster three times to win."''
*'''Advice''' &ndash; ''"The wanted character is hidden behind a door. When you can't find him, either wait for a door to open or look at another door."''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Its name is a pun on the phrase "long arm of the law."
