'''Slippy Toad''' is a character from the ''[[Star Fox (series)|Star Fox series]]''. Slippy does not excel at piloting, as he is the mechanic of the [[Star Fox (team)|crew]]. Slippy makes his first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''.

==''Mario''-related appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
====''Super Smash Bros. Melee''====
In ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'', Slippy appears in the [[Corneria]] and the [[Venom]] arenas. Slippy and his team will occasionally shoot energy bursts at the players on the arena. He also plays a role in the event match, ''Slippy's Invention''. In this event match, Slippy has "invented" a [[Cloaking Device]] which makes Fox and Falco invisible.

====''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', he returns as a guest character in not only Corneria but also appears the same way in [[Lylat Cruise]]. He also appears in the [[Nintendo Monopoly]]. In [[Shadow Moses Island]], instead of one of Snake's team members giving a call, if he does a Codec Conversation on [[Falco]], Slippy will come in by hacking the system.

Like Snake's codec calls, Fox, Falco and Wolf may call friends while on their series' stages. Slippy is one of the recurring characters who will take part in the conversation.

===''Super Mario Maker''===
Slippy is also one of the [[Costume Mario|costumes]] featured in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. It is unlocked randomly by completing the 100 Mario Challenge on Easy difficulty or higher.

==Other media==
Slippy appears in ''[[How to Draw Nintendo Greatest Heroes & Villains]]''. 

Slippy Toad is a property in the 2006 version of ''[[Nintendo Monopoly]]'', taking the place of Oriental Avenue, and costs $100. In the 2010 version, he was replaced by Rosie the Cat from the ''Animal Crossing'' series.
