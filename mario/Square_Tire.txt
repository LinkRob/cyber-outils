The '''Square Tire''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
Using the item turns an opponent's tires square-shaped, as the name suggests, making it harder for them to control their vehicle. After about twenty seconds, the effect wears off.
