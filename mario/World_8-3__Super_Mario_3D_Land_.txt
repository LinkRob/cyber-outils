'''World 8-3''' is the forty-first level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
World 8-3 is a bubble-filled pass of rotating blocks and [[Rocky Wrench]]es. The level opens with a Rocky Wrench on a large square platform, and from here, [[Mario]] must traverse several rotating blocks in order to reach the second stage of the level. From here, the blocks get bigger. Across the long "T-like" shaped block, Mario reaches the [[Checkpoint Flag|checkpoint]]. A ten-block checkered pattern is made of rotating blocks that must be traversed to reach the next part of the level. Here, Mario can "bowl" over a trio of Rocky Wrenches using pre-placed [[Koopa Shell]]s. The last leg of the level opens with cylindrical platforms that rotate before a rather tall platform. The finally of the level is made up of a large platform made with two separate turns, and across this long, final platform, Mario reaches the [[Flagpole]].

== Enemies ==
Enemies that appeared in World 8-3:
* [[Rocky Wrench]]
* [[Bob-omb]]s
* [[Goomba]]

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': If Mario is equipped with a [[Super Leaf]], he can use a small cog near the first instance of larger, rotating platforms. From here, he can reach a higher platform where the first [[Star Medal]] is found.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is among the several checkered platforms near the middle of the level.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The final Star Medal can be acquired by jumping onto the tall platform beyond the bridge of cylindrical platforms and moving right from there. A teleportation block will take Mario forward into the level as well.
