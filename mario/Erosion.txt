'''Erosion''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', as part of the [[Dance Club Rub]] stage, then in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Young Cricket & Master Mantis]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
Eroison is a double-length microgame in which the objective is to rub the top of the mountains to flatten them so that the helicopter can travel past to the other side. If the helicopter crashes, the player loses at the microgame.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', the player has to flatten the mountains so that a glider can pass through. Because the glider travels faster than the helicopter, the microgame timer was halved.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' Only one mountain has to be flattened.
*'''Level 2:''' Two mountains have to be flattened.
*'''Level 3:''' Same as Level 2, but the mountains are higher. In ''WarioWare Gold'', the difference is instead that mountain is placed below the glider while the other one is placed above the glider.
