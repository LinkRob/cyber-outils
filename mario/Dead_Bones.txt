|games   = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}The {{Nihongo|'''Dead Bones'''|ボーン・ワイルダース|Bōn Wairudāsu}} are a team of [[Dull Bones]] that is fought in the [[Glitz Pit]] of [[Glitzville]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Dead Bones consists of three Dull Bones that are fought very early in the tournament. [[Mario]], as the Great Gonzales, can easily defeat them since Dull Bones have very low HP and attack power. Defeating Dead Bones allows Mario to move up to their ranking at 17.
