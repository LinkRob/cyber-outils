'''Quickshaw''' is a High-End kart in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' that debuted in [[Tokyo Tour]]. Its special skill is Rocket Start.
== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Quickshaw is a High-End kart. Its special skill is [[Rocket Start Plus]], which increases the time of the [[Rocket Start]] and grants points when Rocket Start is used. Quickshaw debuted in Tokyo Tour.
