A '''Sad Paragoomba''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sad Paragoombas are a variant of [[Paragoomba]]s and [[Sad Goomba]]s, and have a blue head, a light blue stem, and purple feet like the latter, plus the addition of wings.

===Behavior===
Sad Paragoombas are always crying because they are imbued with the [[Gloom]] [[vibe]]. Unlike regular Paragoombas, Sad Paragoombas always follow a set pattern while flying.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Sad Paragoombas first appear in the [[level]] [[Ladida Plains 1-6]].
