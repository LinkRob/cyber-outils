'''Drivers''' are characters who appear in two ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' [[Game & Watch]] games, ''[[Mario Bros. (Game & Watch)|Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Mario's Cement Factory]]'', as well as both games' various Classic iterations throughout the [[Game & Watch Gallery (series)|''Game & Watch Gallery'' series]].

==In the games==
===''Mario Bros.'' (Game & Watch)===
In ''Mario Bros.'', the drivers need to have their trucks provided with bottles. The drivers also appear in the Classic version of both ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 3]]'' and ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 4]]''.

===''Mario's Cement Factory''===
In ''Mario's Cement Factory'', [[Mario]] has to guide the cement to fall into the trucks, which are once again used by the drivers.

The drivers make a reappearance in the ''[[Game Boy Gallery]]'' remake ''Cement Factory'', which removed ''Mario''-specific elements. Aside from this, the drivers also appear in the Classic version of ''Mario's Cement Factory'' in ''Game & Watch Gallery 4''.
