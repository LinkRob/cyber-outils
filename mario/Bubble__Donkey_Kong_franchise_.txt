'''Bubbles''' are objects in some games of the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}} as well as an item in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]''.

==History==
===''Diddy Kong Racing'' / ''Diddy Kong Racing DS''===
[[File:GreenDropper 3.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Bubbler's Course.png|thumb|Bubbler dropping behind bubbles in ''Diddy Kong Racing'']]
In ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and the remake ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]'', Bubbles are the third (after oil slick and mine) and most powerful upgrade obtained from green [[Weapon Balloon]]s, which give Droppers, items that players place behind them as hazards. Bubbles trap anyone who drives into them, stop them, lift them slightly, and pop, dropping them.

Bubbles are regular hazards in the [[Bubbler Challenge]] rematch in Adventure Mode, where [[Bubbler]] drops them behind as the racer approaches.

====''Donkey Kong Jungle Beat''====
In ''[[Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]'', bubbles are spawned by [[Bubble Shrub]]s. They can be climbed into, causing them to rise with him inside. In the original release, controlling the bubble is the same as controlling [[Donkey Kong]]'s walk, but in ''[[New Play Control! Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]'', it is instead controlled by tilting the [[Wii Remote]]. Additionally, small bubbles hold [[Banana Bunch]]es in many levels, and can be popped with a [[clap]]. Winged versions of these move around. In the reissue, bubbles in the [[Opening Ceremony]] and [[Banana Banquet]] also contain trapped [[Helper Monkey]]s.

====''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze''====
[[File:DKCTF Air Bubble Prima.jpg|240px|thumb]]
'''Air bubbles'''<ref>von Esmarch, Nick, and Cory van Grier. ''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 23.</ref> are used in ''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]'' to recover air while underwater. Generally, air bubbles sprout discretely from sunken diving helmets, and the [[Kong]]s can run into them to restore air. In some levels, such as [[Deep Keep]], they also encounter clusters of tiny bubbles gushing out of sea sponge. These rising columns of bubbles have the same effect on the characters.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' (Nintendo Switch)===
[[File:MarioVsDKSwitchCasualMode2.jpg|thumb|250px|left|Mario floating back to a [[Checkpoint Flag]] while in a bubble]]
In the [[Nintendo Switch]] version of ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (Nintendo Switch)|Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'', bubbles are only used in the [[Easy Mode|Casual]] play style, and are used to transport the player back to the last active [[Checkpoint Flag]] if they end up in situations where they would be normally defeated in Classic. However, the player has a limited supply of five bubbles, with one being used up if they are bubbled back to a checkpoint. Touching any hazard while having no bubbles left will cause the player to instead lose a life.
