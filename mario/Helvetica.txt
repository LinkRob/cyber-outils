==History==
Helvetica is an struggling author who could never find any good ideas. However, Helvetica sends his [[Flopside]] counterpart, [[Garamond]], ideas through his dream and the two of them eventually switch places. While Garamond continues to be successful, Helvetica still isn't doing well and believes he should be a chef instead.

==Trivia==
*Like Garamond, Helvetica's name is a reference to a font style of the same name.
