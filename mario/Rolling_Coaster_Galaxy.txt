The '''Rolling Coaster Galaxy''' is a galaxy in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is similar to [[Rainbow Road]] from the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]].

[[Mario]] must balance on a ball and proceed through the course. The only enemies that are encountered in the level are [[Bob-omb]]s. At the turning point, the left route is considered easier, and the right for Experts.

The [[Comet Medal]], a small handful of [[coin]]s, and a [[1-Up Mushroom]] are on the right route. The [[Power Star]] is at the end of the course.
