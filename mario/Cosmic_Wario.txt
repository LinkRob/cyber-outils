|latest = }}'''Cosmic Wario''' is a form of [[Wario]] that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. This form can be used after Wario gets the Cosmic [[Guise Gem]] from the [[S.S. Caviar]]. To use Cosmic Wario, the player must draw a helmet (basically an upside down "U") on top of his head.

==Appearance==
Cosmic Wario wears a space helmet and silver space clothes. It should also be noted that Wario wears his traditional "W" cap underneath his helmet making this the only form in the game where Wario is seen with his "W" cap.

==Powers and Abilities==
As Cosmic Wario, Wario is armed with a laser gun that allows him to shoot down enemies with lasers. These lasers often bounce off walls until time has passed or they have hit something. Wario can also shoot down special orbs that are often used to activate hidden objects such as ladders and platforms. In addition, Wario is able to jump very slowly as if he was in space. After getting the [[Mastery Gem]] later in the game, Cosmic Wario can now shoot up to three lasers at once making this Wario's most useful form offensive-wise.

Cosmic Wario's biggest weakness is his slow movement and jumping speed. This makes him not an ideal choice when it comes to maneuvering in general especially as most puzzles and/or bosses in the game require speed to accomplish. Cosmic Wario is also at a disadvantage early on in the game as since he can shoot only one laser at a time and the laser doesn't disappear until it has hit something or after some seconds have passed. During the moment that the laser as fired, Wario cannot fire another one and is defenseless.

Cosmic Wario is used quite commonly throughout the game. He is also one of four transformations that is needed to defeat [[Terrormisu]] at the end of the game. During the second phase of the fight, Cosmic Wario must shoot three faces in a specific order quickly in order to damage her. Wario must also take note of the spikes that will appear on the ground whenever he damages her.
