'''Tuber Tug''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Opening==
Each character is looking toward their basket, then they turn around and each grab a vine.

==Gameplay==
The object for each character is to pick vegetables. The player must hold {{button|wii|A}} and {{button|wii|B}} to pull a vine, then to pick the vegetables, they must shake the [[Wii Remote]] and release the two buttons used for holding the vine. Each time a character picks a vegetable, they earn one [[point]], and one can obtain up to 60 points. Whoever has the most points after ten seconds is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|A}} and {{button|wii|B}} (hold): Pull vine
*[[Wii Remote]] (shake): Pick vegetable

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules:''' ''Pull the vine to pick the vegetables! Move the Wii Remote as fast as you can and fill your basket with the most tubers to win.''
