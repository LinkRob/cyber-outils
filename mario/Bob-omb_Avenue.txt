'''Bob-omb Avenue''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Bob-omb Avenue is located in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It has several silhouetted buildings in the background, each with several flashing lights and colors, and a dark blue sky, suggesting a nighttime setting.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Several members of the [[Bob-omb gang]] operate in the area, including [[Bob-omb (Mario Party Advance)|Bob-omb]], who also starts the [[Find the Password]] quest if the player's character talks to them. Bob-omb asks the player's character to obtain a password from [[Junk]], a nearby item store, so that they can join the gang. Once they obtain and deliver the password, the player's character joins the gang, and they are rewarded with the [[Shroom Slide]] [[Gaddget]].
