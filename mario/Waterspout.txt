'''Waterspouts''' are objects found in [[Oho Ocean]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
Waterspouts are swirling vortexes of water that are underwater counterparts to [[whirlwind]]s. Waterspouts are formed due to underground volcanic activity. A difference from whirlwinds is that if [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] enter one with a [[Spin Jump (Mario & Luigi series)|Spin Jump]], they do not get sent forward but rather straight up before slowly making their way back down. Another difference from whirlwinds is that as soon as Mario or Luigi enter a waterspout, they are immediately launched out, whereas a whirlwind contains the brothers unless they move out of it.
