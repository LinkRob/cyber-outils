A '''Stone Buzzy''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are a variant of a [[Buzzy Beetle]] with a stone shell, and they are encountered in the [[Land of the Cragnons]]'s wilderness.

The easy way to defeat a Stone Buzzy is by stomping it three times. Defeating one is a breeze because instead of a [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]] shell-like attack of spinning endlessly, it dissolves. This also, though, makes it exactly four jumps to defeat. Its slow speed is also gives [[Mario]] an advantage. These enemies have are found in the [[Flipside]] [[Pit of 100 Trials]] as well as the Cragnon lands and also have a [[Dark Stone Buzzy|dark counterpart]] that appears in the [[Flopside]] equivalent to the Pit of 100 Trials.
