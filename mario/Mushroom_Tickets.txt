The '''Mushroom Tickets''' are an item in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Description==
They consist of two to four individual tickets, with the number of tickets being random, allowing the player to use a [[Mushroom]] two, three, or four times before they are all used up. The player can only use the tickets on themself. 

==In ''Super Mario Party Jamboree''==
One set of Mushroom Tickets costs six [[coin]]s. They can be obtained at [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shops]] at [[Roll 'em Raceway]], [[Mario's Rainbow Castle]], [[Goomba Lagoon]] with a Pro Rules ruleset, and [[Western Land]], or they can be received on a [[Lucky Space]] or an [[Item Space]]. On boards where Mushroom Tickets are normally not obtainable, they can also appear in an [[Item Bag]].
