'''Captain Toad goes Forth''' is the first [[Captain Toad]] [[Captain Toad Courses|course]] played in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The level is very basic, featuring only a few [[Biddybud]]s. This is also the first level in 3D World to feature more than three stars (Until Mystery House Mad Dash).

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Go under to platform under the moving platform and reach to the dead end.
*'''Green Star 2''': Take the moving platform to the small platform where the second star is.
*'''Green Star 3''': When finding two holes in the wall, take the one on the left facing the enterance.
*'''Green Star 4''': After going on the platform that can be raised through blowing, get the star while avoiding the biddybuds.
*'''Green Star 5''': After the fourth star, move carefully on the thin bridge and get the star on the other side.
