'''Burst Your Bottle''' is one of the [[microgame]]s under [[Ashley]]'s Food category in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to escape from the bottle by either breaking it open or by popping the cork plugging the top. The player's character can break the bottle more quickly if they hit the cracks on it.

The characters listed as most suitable include [[5-Volt]], who can simply teleport outside the bottle without damaging it, and [[Penny Crygor|Penny]], who can use her [[Jet Tank One]] to rapidly shoot at the bottle and break it quickly. Contrarily, [[Orbulon]] is listed as the least suitable character due to the [[Oinker]]'s tractor beam only going downward.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty setting has a different number of cracks on the bottle and a different requirement for how many times a cork has to be hit before it can open.

* '''Level 1:''' There are three cracks on the bottle, and the cork can pop after a few hits.
* '''Level 2:''' There are two cracks on the bottle, and the cork takes more hits to pop.
* '''Level 3:''' The bottle has only one crack, and even more hits are required to pop the cork.

The difficulty levels are different in two-player mode:

* '''Level 1:''' Both characters are trapped in a bottle, which has a crack on the left and right side each.
* '''Level 2:''' Similar to Level 1 except one character is inside the bottle while the other one is outside.
* '''Level 3:''' Has the same set up as Level 2 but without any cracks on the bottle.

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name is a pun on the phrase "burst your bubble," meaning to to destroy one's sense of illusion or well-being.
