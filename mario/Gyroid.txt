'''Gyroid''' is a recurring species from the ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' franchise. The first time a gyroid appears in a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related game is ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''. One gyroid in particular, [[Lloid]], is used by [[Villager]] and [[Isabelle]] in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Gyroids are based on {{w|haniwa}}, which are terracotta clay figures created in real-world [[Japan]] during the {{w|Kofun period}}.

==In ''WarioWare: Twisted!''==
A Gyroid is featured in the [[microgame]] [[Gyroid Jumble]], in which the objective is for the player to line the gyroid with the same outline in the background.
