A '''world''' is a type of location featuring a collection of [[level]]s.

== Description ==
Worlds appear in many ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' platformers, the first being ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. In many 2D platformers, worlds are titled "World," followed by its number in progression order (e.g. World 1 is the first world). Levels in worlds with such a name typically include the world name, followed by a dash, and the level number in progression order (e.g. World 1-1). In ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', levels accessed via alternate paths or behind a path initially blocked off by a [[Star Coin Sign]] tend to instead have a letter representing the level name (e.g. [[World 1-A (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-A]]).
