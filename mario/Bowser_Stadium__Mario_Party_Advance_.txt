'''Bowser Stadium''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Bowser Stadium is a personal soccer stadium of [[Bowser]]'s. There are solid blue walls lining the stadium, with a blue Bowser [[emblem]] on the left and right sides each, and the ground is smoothly covered in green grass. One side of the field contains a {{w|Association football|soccer}} goal with a net.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Bowser Stadium is located in the [[Desert Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], although it is only accessible through the blue [[Warp Pipe]] in the [[Pipe House (Mario Party Advance)|Pipe House]].

Bowser often uses the net in Bowser Stadium to practice his {{w|Goalkeeper (association football)|goalkeeping}}, to the extent that he boasts about being the world's greatest goalkeeper. Bowser Stadium is associated with the quest [[Goal Tenderizer]], in which the player's character has to score three goals into the net while Bowser is goalkeeping. After the player's character scores three times, Bowser challenges them to the minigame [[Splatterball]]. Upon winning the minigame, the player unlocks both it and the [[Snooze Ewes]] [[Gaddget]].
