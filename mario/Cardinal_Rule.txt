'''Cardinal Rule''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
During a round, each team, consisting of two characters, has to be the first to [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] onto whichever card pair matches the one displayed on the large mirror. The card pairs consist of a [[Goomba]], a [[Koopa Troopa]], and a [[Bob-omb]] as well as variations of each type showing them asleep. Both teams have ten seconds in each round to match a card pair, and the first team to do so earns a [[point]].

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: [[Jump]]
* {{button|gcn|A}} (after jumping): Ground Pound

==Trivia==
*The minigame has the same name as the phrase "cardinal rule," but is a pun because the minigame involves the use of cards.
