}}A '''Sobarribad''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Sobarribads are stronger versions of [[Barribad]]s that are only seen in [[Castle Bleck Interior]]. Sobarribads are found in certain spots where they put up a barrier and attack with a laser blast from a distance. Sobarribads can be defeated by [[flip]]ping into 3-D with [[Mario]] to bypass the barrier and then jumping on it.
