'''Pretty Flowers''' is an item in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
Pretty Flowers allows [[Princess Daisy]] to use her [[Star Skill]], [[Flower Ball]], in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge mode]]. Pretty Flowers is a gold coin depicting an orange flower on an orange background with other orange flowers, as seen from its item. The item can be bought from the shop for 200 [[coin]]s if Daisy is on the player's team or once she has been recruited over.
