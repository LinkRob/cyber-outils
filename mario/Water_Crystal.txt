The '''Water Crystal''' is one of the four [[Elemental Crystals]] who fights alongside [[Culex]] in [[Monstro Town]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a sentient crystal associated with the element of water. The Water Crystal, like the other three Elemental Crystals, originates from the ''[[Final Fantasy]]'' franchise, but in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'', the Water Crystal is from the dimension of [[Vanda]], like Culex.

The Water Crystal almost exclusively uses Ice-based moves, such as [[Blizzard (move)|Blizzard]] and [[Ice Rock]], even though it has "Water" in its name. Ironically, the [[Earth Crystal]] can use [[Water Blast]] but not the Water Crystal. It has medium [[Attack]] status but a rather high [[Defense]].
