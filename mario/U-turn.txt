The '''U-turn''' is a manoeuvre from the ''Mario Kart'' series. These manoeuvres made their debut in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', where they are an exclusive technique to [[Battle Mode]] in both versions.

== Procedure ==
To perform a U-turn, players have to release the accelerator during a drift.  This causes the kart to stall, doing a 180-degree turn much tighter than a standard [[drift]] would allow.
