The '''Jungle Tour''' is a tour in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

==Description==
It involves racers driving through lush, tropical environments filled with vibrant greenery, towering palm trees, and winding tracks that weave through the jungle. This tour introduces players to a variety of classic Mario Kart characters, each equipped with unique abilities and power-ups to help them navigate the challenging terrain. The Jungle Tour's tracks feature hazards such as swinging [[vine]]s, slippery mud patches, and treacherous water crossings.
