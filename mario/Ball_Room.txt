The '''Ball Room''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==History==
The Ball Room is the second room accessible in [[Area Two]]. [[Luigi]] can access the Ball Room once he obtains its [[key]], which is located in the [[Bathroom (1F)|Bathroom]].

Upon entering the Ball Room, Luigi has to vacuum up six [[Ghost Guy]]s, dancing in pairs of two each. After doing so, [[The Floating Whirlindas]] appear, and two circle platforms on the ground begin to spin around. After Luigi vacuums up The Floating Whirlindas, the Ball Room's lights turn on, and a [[treasure chest]] containing the [[key]] to the [[Storage Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Storage Room]] appears.

During the [[blackout]], five Ghost Guys are encountered in the Ball Room. In the PAL releases' and the Nintendo 3DS version's [[Hidden Mansion]], several [[Flying Fish]] also appear in the Ball Room prior to the blackout.

==Ghosts==
*Six [[Ghost Guy]]s (20 HP each)
*The Floating Whirlindas (100 HP)

===PAL Hidden Mansion===
*Six Ghost Guys (20 HP each)
*Several [[Flying Fish]]
*The Floating Whirlindas (100 HP)

===Blackout===
*Five Shy Guys (20 HP each)

==Trivia==
*The Ball Room appears in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' as the second floor in the battle course [[Luigi's Mansion (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Luigi's Mansion]].
