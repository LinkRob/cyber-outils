A '''Cracked Wall''' is a reoccurring object that appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
First appearing in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Cracked Walls can be destroyed by [[Bombette]] once [[Mario]] adds her to his party. Cracked Walls are found throughout the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and destroying one usually leads to an important item or area.
