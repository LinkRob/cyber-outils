The '''Spooky Speedster''' is a character in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. He is an incredibly fast [[Boo]], that dwells in [[Boo's Boneyard Galaxy]], although he first appears in the [[Ghostly Galaxy]]. He wears a bright orange helmet and visor. He will challenge [[Mario]] to race to a specific point. While the Spooky Speedster, due to his nature as a Boo, can float over to the finish line, Mario has use [[Pull Star]]s to slingshot his body towards the goal in the first race and use the [[Boo Mario|Boo suit]] to phase through crystal walls in the second. If Mario wins against the Spooky Speedster, he will obtain a [[Power Star]]. However, if the Spooky Speedster wins, Mario will lose a life.

Speedy Spookster is ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s version of ''[[Super Mario 64]]''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s [[Koopa the Quick]] and ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s [[Il Piantissimo]].
