'''World 3''' is the third [[world]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. It is unlocked upon defeating [[Mummipokey]] without using [[Mini Mario]]/Mini Luigi at {{world-link|2|castlen|World 2-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)}}. The boss of this world is [[Cheepskipper]].

== Layout ==
Similar to [[Water Land]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', World 3 takes place on a costal shore, with underwater and beach levels. There are 3 main levels, 3 bonus levels, a tower, a [[Ghost House]], a Cannon, a castle and 3 [[Toad House]]s. Like with World 2, World 3 also has nine total levels for [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to explore.
