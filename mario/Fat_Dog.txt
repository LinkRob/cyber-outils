|notes = Was going to be combined with [[Hot Sauce]].}}The '''Fat Dog''' is a type of food that is mentioned in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Once [[Mario]] helps the [[Businessman]] find the combination to his [[Briefcase|briefcase]], he mentions that his company and [[Mr. Hoggle]]'s [[Hot Dog Stand]] were going to combine the Fat Dog and the [[Hot Sauce]] together into one product. It is unknown if they ever went through with this.
