'''Reverse Dice Block''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Description==
It is a purple [[Dice Block]] that has the opposite effect from a normal Dice Block, as its name suggests, because when used, a Reverse Dice Block has the character move back 1-6 spaces, depending on what they rolled. It is similar to the [[Reverse Mushroom]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==In ''Mario Party 10''==
The Reverse Dice Block exclusively appears in [[amiibo Party]]. It can appear if a character stops on a [[Special Dice Block Space]].

== In ''Mario Party Superstars'' ==
The Reverse Dice Block exclusively appears in [[Woody Woods]]. If you stop on [[Evil Woody|Warukio]]'s space and choose the dice fruit, you'll get this dice and head back{{MP10Nav}}
