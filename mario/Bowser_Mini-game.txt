A '''Bowser mini-game''' is a special type of 4-player mini-game in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series. When a Bowser mini-game is played, all four players are usually placed into a certain situation where they must survive, usually trying to avoid harm from Bowser. A player that unsuccessfully completes a Bowser mini-game usually ends up losing [[coin]]s or their items. In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', these minigames are referred as [[Reverse Minigame]]s, in which players compete to lose for [[Mini Star]]s. In ''[[Mario Party 10]],'' there are Bowser mini-games in the [[Bowser Party]] mode.
