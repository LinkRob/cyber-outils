'''Beanies''' are green, bean-like enemies that appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. They are only found in [[Big Beanie's Castle|World 1-8: Big Beanie's Castle]]. They approach [[Yoshi]] and [[Baby Mario]] by hopping towards him in a similar manner to [[Burt]]s from the previous [[Yoshi (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' games]]. Beanies can be a handful, especially when they come in large quantities however they are far from invincible and can be defeated with basically any attack.

In the castle's final room, [[Kamek]] "plants" a Beanie with his hammer, which grows into a [[beanstalk]] leading to the sky, where it grows into [[Big Beanie]], the boss of [[World 1 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 1]]. During the boss battle, Big Beanie spits out Beanies at Yoshi, which he can eat to make [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s to throw at Big Beanie, tossing eggs at Big Beanie is the way to defeat him.
