The '''Toad Minister''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Physical description==
The Toad Minister is an elderly [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with dark green spots on his head and a large, white mustache.

==History==
The Toad Minister is first seen in [[Princess Peach's Castle]] during the early moments of the game. He is a high-ranked government official who serves as the right-hand man to [[Princess Peach]]. After [[Bowser]] lifts up Princess Peach's Castle into the sky, the Toad Minister, along with some other Toads, were locked away in a cell in [[Bowser's Castle]]. He is later freed by [[Mario]] and, after Bowser is defeated, he returns to his position as Peach's adviser.

==Trivia==
*The Toad Minister's appearance and role is similar to the [[Chancellor]] from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They both would go on to be replaced by [[Toadsworth]] as Princess Peach's adviser.
