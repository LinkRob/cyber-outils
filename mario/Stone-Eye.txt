The '''Stone-Eye''' is a harmless obstacle that debuted in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. They are featured in the levels [[Stone-Eye Zone]] in the [[Layer-Cake Desert]] world and [[Deepsea Ruins]] in the haunted area of [[Soda Jungle]]

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Stone-Eyes are based on the Moai statues from Easter Island. They have eyes which move to follow the player. in the Stone-Eye Zone, they are brown, while in the Deepsea Ruins, they are grey.{{sectstub}}
=== Behavior ===
Stone-Eyes are either stationary or swing back and forth. Their movement patterns can either assist [[Mario (character)|Mario]] or hinder him.

If Mario performs a spin jump near a Stone-Eye, the Stone-Eye's eyes roll. Additionally, a Stone-Eye near his location will watch his movements.{{sectstub}}

== History ==

=== [[Mario Golf (series)|''Mario Golf'' series]] ===
Mario and company encounter Stone-Eyes that can act as obstacles in the Layer-Cake Desert course in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''. Brown Stone-Eyes can be seen out-of-bounds.

The Balmy Dunes course in ''[[Mario Golf: Super Rush]]'' features Stone-Eyes, and one mission involves one of them carrying a pot into which Mario and his friends have to launch their balls.

=== In ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' ===
The Stone-Eyes make an appearance in the minigame, [[Fruit Scoot Scurry]]. Here, Mario and company have to hop between them to get the fruits that spawn randomly on top of them.
