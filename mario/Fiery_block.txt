'''Fiery blocks''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Description==
Fiery blocks are a type of [[block]] that can only be destroyed using one of [[Fire Mario]]'s [[fireball]]s. Fiery blocks are usually found obstructing either paths or [[Warp Pipe|pipes]]. Certain fiery blocks also release a power-up upon being destroyed.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
Fiery blocks are first found in [[Mushroom Zone]], against the left and right sides of the third [[Piranha Plant]]'s pipe.
