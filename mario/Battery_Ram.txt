'''Battery Ram''' is a 2-vs.-2 minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object for both teams is to navigate a battering ram with a battery on it to the end of the block maze. Each player holds on to one end of the battery, and they must cooperatively move around to maneuver the battery around tight corners. Whoever reaches the end first is the winner, but if neither team reaches the end within five minutes, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Controls==
*Control Stick: Move (both teams)
