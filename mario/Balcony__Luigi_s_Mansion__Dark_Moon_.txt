The '''Balcony''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It can be accessed from the [[Lobby]], and it is one of the smallest areas in the mansion.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
During [[Sticky Situation|A-5: Sticky Situation]], [[Luigi]] must head out to the Balcony to clean up spider webs. Luigi must grab onto a ball of web and then light it on fire to burn away the larger webs.

During previous missions, Luigi can head out to the Balcony anytime but all he will find are Bats.
