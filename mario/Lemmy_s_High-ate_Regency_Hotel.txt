'''Lemmy's High-ate Regency Hotel''' is a location in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''.

==Description==
Lemmy's High-ate Regency Hotel is a [[Koopa hotel]] owned by [[Lemmy Koopa]], and it is found on the clouds. Its name is a pun on the real-world hotel company {{w|Hyatt#Brands|Hyatt Regency}} and High-Rate. Enemies found here include [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]]s, [[Paragoomba|Flying Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Paratroopa|Flying Koopa]]s, and [[Banzai Bill]]s. There are also some evil clouds that follow Mario, going in front of him to try and distract the player.

==In ''Hotel Mario''==
Lemmy's High-ate Regency Hotel is the fourth Koopa hotel explored by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. The [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] travel to here by activating a [[beanstalk]] from a [[! Block]].

After Mario and Luigi beat the area, they activate a giant fan that somehow causes the area to dissipate and blows Lemmy away. Just as Mario and Luigi believe that they have finally rescued [[Princess Peach|Toadstool]], she disappears into thin air, so the Mario Bros. continue their journey to [[Ludwig's Thump Castle Hotel]].
