'''Mario Paint: Fly Swatter''' is a [[microgame]] part of [[9-Volt]]'s [[Nintendo Classics]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'' It is based on the [[minigame]] [[Gnat Attack]] from ''[[Mario Paint]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object is to swat the [[Big Fly]] when it is underneath the fly swatter. If done successfully, the player wins, but if the objective is not met on time, they lose.

The first and second difficulty levels feature one and two Big Flies, respectively, while the third difficulty level features three Big Flies that constantly move around.
