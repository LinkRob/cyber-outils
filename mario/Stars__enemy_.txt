A '''Stars''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Stars SML2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
A Stars is a five-pointed star with a frowning expression. In its artwork, a Stars is shown to have a yellow body, and the artwork itself is a variation of the [[Starman]] artwork from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' with a smiling expression, in contrast with their in-game frowning expressions.
{{br|left}}

===Behavior===
Stars can be found in clusters forming an impassable wall, and they sometimes move back and forth in space.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
[[File:Star stage SML2.png|thumb|left|Mario making his way through a field of stars in ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'']]
Stars are encountered in the [[star stage]] of the [[Space Zone]], and one also appears at the end of the [[Secret Course 2|secret level]] of the Space Zone. Stars cannot be defeated, being the only enemy to have this status.
