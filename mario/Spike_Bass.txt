The '''Spike Bass'''<ref>Loe, Casey. ''New Super Mario Bros.'' Player's Guide. Page 12.</ref> is an enemy in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' It resembles an oversized brown tadpole with a pale underbelly. It also has beaming yellow eyes, sharp teeth, and spikes all over its body. The Spike Bass jumps out of the water to attack [[Mario]], similar to [[Boss Bass]]es from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and [[Porcupuffer]]s in later ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' series installments. Spike Bass can only reach a certain water level, so if Mario is on high grounds, he has a good chance of being safe. 

==History== 
=== ''New Super Mario Bros.'' ===
The Spike Bass appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', in this game, they only appears in [[World 2-A (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-A]] and [[World 3-C (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3-C]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros]].'' Sometimes Mario has to swim to complete the level, and a Spike Bass will be right there to attack the plumber. A [[Starman|star]] is available in the level, and can help Mario during the swimming areas. A Spike Bass can be defeated with the use of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Blue Shell]], [[Starman]], or a [[Mega Mushroom]]. Defeating a Spike Bass earns the player 200 [[point]]s. The victory however only lasts several seconds as a new Spike Bass will arrive to continue the attack. This can still give Mario (or [[Luigi]]) a breather for some time.

==Trivia==
*The Spike Bass uses similar methods of attack to [[Boss Bass]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', but Spike Bass does not attempt to eat Mario or Luigi, unlike Boss Bass.
*The Spike Bass' colors slightly resemble those of the Cheep Cheeps in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.
