The '''Smelter''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The Smelter is a member of the [[Smithy Gang]], and is the source for all of [[Smithy]]'s creations throughout the game. The Smelter is located in [[Smithy Factory]].

==Battle==
The Smelter itself in battle never attacks directly. Instead, it will produce liquid metal for Smithy who will then create [[Shyper]]s to aid them in battle.

===Strategy===
The Smelter is weak to thunder attacks meaning [[Mallow]]'s [[Shocker (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Shocker]] will be effective in taking it out. The Smelter can be rather troublesome in battle as it will continue to produce liquid metal for Smithy to use. As such, the Smelter should be taken out first. A combination of [[Mario]]'s [[Lazy Shell]] and Mallow's Shocker should be able to take it out in a few turns. [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] should focus on keeping them healed.
