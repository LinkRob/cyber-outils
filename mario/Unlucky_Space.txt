An '''Unlucky Space''' is a recurring type of [[space]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Description==
When a character lands on an Unlucky Space, they are penalized with a negative event, the exacts of which differ between the ''Mario Party'' games. They are functionally the opposite of [[Lucky Space]]s, similarly to how [[Mini Ztar Space]]s function the opposite of [[Mini Star Space]]s.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party 9''===
[[File:Unlucky Space MP9.png|thumb|left]]
An Unlucky Space is typically located next to a red [[Warp Pipe]]. If landed on, an Unlucky Space a [[Piranha Plant]] appears from the Red Pipe and grabs the Captain, forcing them down into it. The Captain ends up in a cavern with six [[Mini Ztar Space]]s, where they have to roll a standard [[Dice Block]] and move onto that many number of [[Mini Ztar Space]]s. Rolling higher numbers can lead to a Mini Ztar Space with a higher number printed on it, therefore causing the Captain to lose more [[Mini Star]]s. After that, the Captain exits to the main board, and their turn concludes.

Unlucky Spaces are less common than Lucky Spaces, and they only appear in three boards: [[Toad Road]], [[Bob-omb Factory]] and [[Blooper Beach]]. There is only one Unlucky Space in Toad Road, while Bob-omb Factory and Blooper Beach have their Unlucky Spaces on branching paths that the captain either can or cannot avoid.

Blooper Beach, has two consecutive Unlucky Spaces just before the last fork of the board, and landing on either of them has a different effect of the nearby Blooper statue activating and creating a whirlpool, which draws the vehicle and the characters to a path around it consisting of [[Mini Ztar Space]]s and [[Dash Space]]s. The Captain who landed on the Unlucky Space also loses three Mini Stars. The characters take turns getting out of the path to avoid losing several [[Mini Star]]s while the Blooper statue drags the vehicle two spaces back at the end of each character's turn.

===''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''===
The game only has one Unlucky Space (Event Space (red) in the European release), found right in the path of a sleeping [[Chain Chomp]] in [[Perilous Palace Path]]. If a character lands on the Unlucky Space, the Chain Chomp wakes up and chases any characters in its path up to six spaces back.

===''[[Mario Party 10]]''===
If a character lands on an Unlucky Space, they usually just lose Mini Stars. However, a different negative effect can occur, such as in [[Whimsical Waters]], where a [[Blooper]] may ink a character, who can then not use any [[Special Dice Block]]s the next turn.
