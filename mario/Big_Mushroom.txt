'''Big Mushrooms''' are a type of [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]] power-up item which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and returns in both ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. Using it turns a [[Mario]] or another character into their [[Big Mario|Big]] form.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Maker''===
A Big Mushroom could only be accessed in the editor from using a [[Amiibo#Super Mario Bros. 30th Anniversary amiibo|Super Mario Bros. 30th anniversary amiibo]]. This power-up had a major effect on a level. For starters, Mario grew from his either 1x1 blocks or 1x2 blocks size to 2x2 blocks. His movements slightly slowed down and he gained the ability to smash hard blocks, [[Brick Block]]s and [[? Block]]s just by jumping into or onto them. In addition to all this, enemies began appearing differently when Mario took this mushroom. For instance, a [[Dry Bones]] will now wear a hat and have [[Luigi]] riding on their backs. However, despite these abilities, Mario will revert to his [[Super Mario (form)|Super]] form if he touches anything harmful, and he is still vulnerable to [[lava]], being crushed and falling through gaps. 

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
The Big Mushroom has the same effect, although the appearances of enemies go unchanged, and every character can use it without using amiibo. A Big Mushroom is now automatically accessible, and it can be found when a player selects a mushroom and holds it.
