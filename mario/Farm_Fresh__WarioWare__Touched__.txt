'''Farm Fresh''' is a [[microgame]] found in [[Ashley]] and [[Red (WarioWare series)|Red]]'s [[Total Drag]] stage of ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''

== Gameplay ==
The objective of the game is to drag all of the animals into the fenced-in area. 

*'''1st level difficulty:''' There are two animals to capture.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' There are four animals to capture.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' There are five animals to capture.
