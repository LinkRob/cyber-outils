'''Lighthouses''' are important structures present in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They can be found on all major islands in [[Concordia]]'s seas. These lighthouses have a structure that resembles a flower at their top as well as a plug on their side. To activate the lighthouse, this plug must be pulled out so [[Snoutlet]] can guide it back to [[Shipshape Island]], connecting the island to the [[Uni-Tree]] which powers the lighthouse up with Connectar.

There are only two instances of lighthouses being disconnected without killing the [[Uni-Tree]]: [[Heatfreeze Island]] and [[Twistee Island]]. In both cases, [[Bowser]] severed these connections by damaging the plugs during his sortie on [[Shipshape Island]], forcing these plugs to retract.

== Uses ==
An enormous light bulb powered by Connectar can be found in every lighthouse, illuminating the area around it and causing Connectar Flowers to bloom. When powered, the area around the lighthouse will be bound physically to the socket and pulled along, despite the cord being otherwise intangible. [[Warp Pipe|Warp Pipes]] can be established through the properties of Connector, allowing island residents to traverse through connected areas.

== Great Lighthouses ==
On the [[Great Lighthouse Islands]] are four massive Lighthouses called the Great Lighthouses. Unlike their lesser counterparts, Great Lighthouses are not activated by connecting their plugs to [[Shipshape Island]]'s [[Uni-Tree]]; instead, they must be activated by [[Wattanist|Wattanists]]. During the activation cutscene, [[Connie]] is seen flying on a golden platform and pouring Bond energy over the Lighthouse's flower with her Bonding Can.

== Nomenclature ==
In most languages, the name "lighthouse" is translated just like with real lighthouses (i.e. ''Tōdai'' (written 灯台) in Japanese and ''faro'' in Spanish). The one exception is Italian, which produces a pun on ''fjore'' ("flower") and ''faro'' ("lighthouse") to produce ''Fiorfaro''.
