'''Big Penguin Race''' is the third [[mission]] of [[Cool, Cool Mountain]] in [[Super Mario 64]] and it's [[Reissue|remake]]: [[Super Mario 64 DS]]

== Description ==
To collect the third [[Power Star]] of [[Cool, Cool Mountain]], [[Mario]] races a penguin down a slide for a Power Star. At the start of the level, there is a chimney that Mario can jump into. Once he has jumped down the chimney, he will find a slide and a penguin who challenges him to a race down the slide. Mario must race him down the slide while being careful to not fall off the slide and win the race to collect the Star.
