'''Eggland''' is the home of the [[Eggling]]s in ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''. At the beginning of the game, Eggland is attacked by [[Bowser]]. The Egglings managed to escape to [[Yoshi's Island (Yoshi franchise)|Yoshi Island]], but before they could outrun Bowser, he transformed them into [[Apple (Yoshi franchise)|apples]]. At the end of the game, [[Yoshi]] frees the Egglings from Bowser's curse and they all have a meal. As narrated, they will never forget Yoshi's deeds.
