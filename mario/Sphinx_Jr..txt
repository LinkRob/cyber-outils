A '''Sphinx Jr.''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is a cannon in the shape of the Sphinx that can be found in [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]].

==History==
The Sphinx Jr. mainly attacks by shooting energy balls at [[Wario]] when he is close. This enemy can also be spawned as one of [[Poobah the Pharaoh]]'s attacks. [[Genius Wario]] must be used in order to uncover the Sphinx Jr. and hit it back at Poobah.
