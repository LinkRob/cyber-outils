'''Duck Hunt''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS and Wii U]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. 

==Stage design==
{{sectstub}}
The design of the stage is the same map feature from the NES game of the same name. 

===Features===
{{sectstub}}
Features in this game include the ducks flying around as though you were in the game. You can use your character and move to kill a duck for the dog to nab.

==Origin==
The stage, Duck Hunt, is based off of the same basic stages from the game of the same name.
