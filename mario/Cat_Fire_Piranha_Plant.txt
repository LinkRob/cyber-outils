'''Cat Fire Piranha Plants''' (or '''Fire Cat Piranha Plants''') are a variant of both [[Cat Piranha Plant]]s and [[Fire Piranha Plant]]s in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

Cat Fire Piranha Plants can commonly be seen all around [[Lake Lapcat]] and like their regular counterparts, wait for [[Mario]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] to approach before breathing a fireball at them. When [[Fury Bowser]] emerges from the darkness, the Cat Fire Piranha Plants become tougher, Mario can easily take out a Cat Fire Piranha Plant with stomps or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Bell]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Boomerang Flower]], or a [[Starman]]. Bowser Jr. can destroy one with his magic brush.
