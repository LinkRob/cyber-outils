A '''Ruin Powder''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Using the Ruin Powder causes inflicts all the [[Confused]] status effect on all enemies. It can be made by combining a [[Spite Pouch]] with a [[Point Swap]]. Ruin Powders can be found in certain areas, but they can mainly be purchased at the [[Northwinds Mart]] for 15 [[coin]]s.
