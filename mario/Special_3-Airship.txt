'''{{world|s3|airship|}}''' is the [[Airship]] level of [[Special 3 (Super Mario 3D Land)|Special 3]]. It is based off [[World 2-Airship (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-Airship]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts out on an [[Airship]], and must dodge flames from several [[Burner|jet engine]]s. The player then crosses a [[rope|tightrope]] onto a second Airship, where three [[Bill Blaster]]s are firing [[Bullet Bill]]s. The player then jumps onto the next Airship; it contains a [[? Block]] with a [[Statue Leaf]], a [[Rocky Wrench]] and several Bill Blasters that fire both Bullet Bills and [[Tail Bullet Bill]]s. The player jumps onto a fourth Airship that has more jet engines, before moving onto the final Airship. If the player goes down the steps, they receive several [[coin]]s from [[Tanooki Mario|Tanooki Toads]], and if they go up the stairs, the player find the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Medal]]s==
*'''Star Medal 1:''' The first Star Medal is found floating above the Bill Blasters on the second Airship.
*'''Star Medal 2:''' The second Star Medal is found floating  above a Rocky Wrench, which the player needs to jump off to obtain it.
*'''Star Medal 3:''' The third Star Medal is near a few jet engines underneath the same Airship where the second Star Medal is.
