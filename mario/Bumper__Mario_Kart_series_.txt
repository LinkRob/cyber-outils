'''Bumpers''' are an obstacle in the {{series|Mario Kart}}, specifically in the course [[Waluigi Pinball]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

==Description==
A Bumper automatically bounces off any character or [[Pinball (obstacle)|pinball]] if they move into it, just like bumpers in a regular game of pinball. Some bumpers remain in one position, but there are also a group of others that move around in a circle. In ''Mario Kart DS'' and ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', the stationary bumpers are blue and cyan, while the moving ones are magenta. In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' [[Booster Course Pass]]'s second wave, bumpers light up in various colors. If a racer lands onto a bumper, they perform a [[Jump Boost]].
