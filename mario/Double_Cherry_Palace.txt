'''Double Cherry Palace''' is a level from ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''. It features the [[Double Cherry]], a powerup from [[Super Mario 3D World]].
==Features==
{{Empty section|date=May 2023}}

==Super Gems==
*'''Super Gem 1''': Pluck the mole in the maze.
*'''Super Gem 2''': During the drop section, have the Toad on the right hit the third to final P Switch. The Toad on the left side should drop and walk to a hidden passage in the background leading to the Super Gem.
*'''Super Gem 3''': After the second diamond, have the two clones drop down one more floor and go outside. Go to the stem to reveal a mole digging. Pluck the mole with either clones.

==Optional Mission==
*Collect 70 coins.
