'''Spoonatic''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

== History ==
Spoonatic is a sentient spoon about the size of [[Wario]]. It attacks by aiming at him and charging against him, like the [[Slaughterfork]]s and [[Jack the Knife|Jacks]]. Spoonatics can be only found on the [[S.S. Caviar]].

== Flavor text ==
*'''In-game description:''' ''These spoons, warped and twisted by hate, wish only to scoop out joy and happiness from the lives of others.''

== Trivia ==
* According to its game description, it is a spoon warped and twisted by hate and wishes only to scoop out happiness and joy from the lives of others.
* Its name is a portmanteau of "spoon" and "lunatic".
