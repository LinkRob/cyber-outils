The '''Trash Smasher''' is a vehicle in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Recycled Koopa]]."

==Description==
The Trash Smasher is a combination of a tank and a vacuum cleaner. It is a large vehicle that [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] built using various random parts, such as a [[Note Block]], several [[Warp Pipe]]s and scrap metal. It was designed to clean up all the [[Koopa trash]] dumped onto [[New York City]] by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and the [[Koopalings]]. It can also undo the magical effects of Koopa trash, such as from transforming [[Koopa Zombie]]s back into regular [[human]]s, and convert garbage into appliances.

==In other media==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
===="Recycled Koopa"====
Mario and Luigi build the Trash Smasher to help clean up all the Koopa trash and stop King Koopa from dumping any more. Luigi ends up using the Trash Smasher alone to clean up [[Brooklyn]] and the rest of New York City as Mario, while adding the finishing touches to the Trash Smasher, had finished mutating into a Koopa Zombie after heavy exposure of Koopa's trash. With the Trash Smasher, Luigi manages to clean the entire city and transform every Koopa Zombie back into humans. As a bonus, Luigi uses the Trash Smasher to convert the Koopa trash into various household appliances.

With all of the garbage cleaned up, Mario and Luigi modify the Trash Smasher to make it flight-capable, sprouting whirling helicopter blades, and chase after King Koopa and his [[Doom Ship]], as well as suck up all the garbage onboard before it can reach New York City again. With Mario and Luigi using the Trash Smasher to chase King Koopa, he quickly maneuvers his Doom Ship and tries to escape from the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]].
