'''POW-Down''' is a status effect in the {{series|Paper Mario}} and {{series|Mario & Luigi}}.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
In both ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', POW-Down appears in the form of the [[Tiny (ailment)|Tiny]]. [[Mario]] can dish out this effect by using a [[Shrink Stomp]] while enemies like [[Bzzap!]]s can inflict this on him.
