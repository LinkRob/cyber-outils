The '''Koopa Shellceiver''' is a device used by the [[Koopa Troop]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It is a {{w|walkie-talkie}} designed in the likeness of a [[Koopa Shell]].

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
The Koopa Shellceiver is seen during the intro. Here, a [[Goomba]] walks into [[Peach's Castle]] with the [[Vibe Scepter]]. [[Army Hammer Bro]] waits for the Goomba to give the clear signal before the [[Hammer Bro]]s. begin their attack on the castle.
