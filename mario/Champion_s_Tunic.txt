The '''Champion's Tunic''' is the signature outfit worn by [[Link]] in ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild]]''. In ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]''-related crossovers, it is one of his outfits in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' (since the version 1.6.0 update) and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
