'''Huckit Crabs''' are recurring enemies in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Description==
They are a species of red crab hidden within a shell. They sport two yellow feet and a single claw which is used to hurl projectiles, which are mostly rocks. The Huckit Crab can toss an unlimited number of projectiles, consisting of either rocks or balls of sand, like [[Clawgrip]]. The rocks or sandballs thrown always move in a straight line. Huckit Crabs are very, very similar to [[Sidestepper]]s from the original ''[[Mario Bros.]]'' in terms of behavior. Since their debut in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', Huckit Crabs have essentially filled the role for the common crabs throughout the ''Mario'' franchise.

== In {{series|Super Mario}} ==
=== ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' ===
[[Image:Huckit Crab Beach Fun To Chaos.png|thumb|left|200px|The Huckit Crabs pelt their Sandballs at the succeeding Mario Crew.]]
Huckit Crabs appear in large quantities, but only in one area, the third stage of [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4]], [[World 4-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4-3]]. The crabs attack by tossing sandballs at the heroes which can either be avoided or stomped on to be destroyed (the sandballs cannot be destroyed whilst under the effects of a [[Mini Mushroom]]), upon tossing a sandball, a Huckit Crab will jump a few feet back, this can sometimes lead them to falling into the water behind them. Huckit Crabs can be defeated in multiple ways, including jumping on them, attacking them with [[Ice Flower]]s or [[Fire Flower]]s. Using the [[Penguin Suit]] or [[Starman]] is also effective.

=== ''New Super Mario Bros. U / New Super Luigi U / New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe'' ===
[[Image:Huckit Crab Beachfront.png|thumb|200px|The Luigi Crew near the stone-tossing Huckit Crabs.]]
Huckit Crabs reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. In these games, their behavior is relatively unchanged from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' however this time, they toss small rocks instead of sandballs however just as before, the rocks can be destroyed with stomps and the Huckit Crabs themselves can be defeated with basically any attack, Huckit Crabs can also be lifted into the air by [[Waterspout]]s if they walk backward into one and the rocks they throw can be raised in elevation by the Waterspouts. Huckit Crabs are slightly more common in these games than in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' appearing a few levels of [[Sparkling Waters]] and other water areas, levels they appear in include [[Waterspout Beach]], [[Huckit Beach Resort]], and [[Porcu-Puffer Cavern]].

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
=== ''Mario Party 9'' ===
[[Image:Huckit Crab Pirates.png|thumb|200px|The Huckit Crabs launching their cannons to trigger the Mini Star/Mini Ztar event.]]
Huckit Crabs make a reappearance in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. In this game, they are seen around an ashore Pirate Ship on [[Blooper Beach]], when players land on a [[Happening Space|Event Space]], the Huckit Crabs will fire the cannons on the Pirate Ship turning the [[Mini Star]]s around the board into [[Mini Ztar]]s and vice versa. Several Huckit Crabs can also be seen around the board, the Huckit Crabs themselves have no other effect on the gameplay and only fire the cannons.

===''Mario Party: Island Tour''===
[[Image:Match Faker Choices.png|thumb|left|200px|A Huckit Crab in Match Faker.]]
Huckit Crabs appear again in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' during the minigame [[Match Faker]] as one of the possible selectable choices in the minigame alongside various other enemies and species.

=== ''Mario Party 10'' ===
[[Image:Meanie Match Lineup.jpg|thumb|200px|A Huckit Crab along with several other enemies.]]
Like most enemies from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', Huckit Crabs are seen yet again in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' appearing around [[Whimsical Waters]], the Huckit Crabs however have no effect on the gameplay and are rather just seen as citizens of the underwater world. Huckit Crabs also make an appearance in the minigame [[Meanie Match]] as one of the possible selectable choices.

===''Mario Party: Star Rush''===
[[Image:Mega Blooper Boss Huckit Crabs.png|thumb|200px|left|Huckit Crabs watching the Battle.]]
Huckit Crabs reappear in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'', in this game, they can be seen jumping around to the beat of the music during the boss battle with [[Mega Blooper]] in the minigame, [[Mega Blooper's Bayside Bop]]. They also have a chance of appearing in the [[Pop Quiz]] minigame as well.

=== ''Super Mario Party'' ===
[[Image:SMP Smash and Crab.png|thumb|200px|Huckit Crabs watch the battle between [[Diddy Kong]] and the Robo-Sidestepper.]]
Huckit Crabs are back in ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' retaining a very similar role to past ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' games, this time, they can be seen during the minigame [[Smash and Crab]] where they are seen watching the minigame as the solo player tries to evade the teammates attacks from their Robotic Sidestepper Walker. A Huckit Crab is also seen in the minigame, [[Absent Minded]] as one of the selectable characters during the minigame.

===''Mario Party Superstars''===
[[Image:Huckit Crab Island DX.png|thumb|left|200px|A Huckit Crab enjoying the Island view.]]
Huckit Crabs appear once again in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. In this game, some Huckit Crabs can be seen around the [[Yoshi's Tropical Island]] board walking around or enjoying the view of the beach. The Huckit Crabs however have no effect on the gameplay and like past games in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series, reprise their usual role as background characters.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''==
[[Image:Hermie III Huckit Crab Cameo.png|thumb|200px|A Huckit Crab runs by as Mario and Luigi attempt to reason with Hermie III.]]
Huckit Crabs also appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. In this game, they can be seen during the battle with [[Hermie III]] running across the beach as [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] battle Hermie III however they have no impact on the boss fight and only serve as a background element (possibly as a distraction). The Huckit Crabs replace the [[Sidestepper]]s seen in the original ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', they also show up more frequently than the Sidesteppers did in the original.

==In ''Dr. Mario World''==
Huckit Crabs return in ''[[Dr. Mario World]]'' as assistants to the main doctors.

== Trivia ==
*Huckit Crabs behave very similarly to [[Spike]]s.
*Huckit Crabs are slightly similar to [[Crabber]]s and [[Sidestepper]]s and could be related to them, in more recent games, both Crab species have appeared less in the series in favor of the Huckit Crab.
**''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' however broke this trend by reintroducing the [[Sidestepper]] (alongside the [[Blue Sidestepper]]) in a non-spinoff game after many years.
*[[Clawgrip]], the boss of [[World 5 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', may have been an inspiration for this enemy. Like [[Clawgrip]], it also has a resemblance to the original [[Sidestepper]]s from ''[[Mario Bros.]]''
*Their name is a pun on "hermit crab" (the crab species they resemble), and the term "chuck it", as they throw sand balls.
*The way the Huckit Crabs move is very similar to the [[Snifit]]’s moves. Both jump, walk, and throw small balls.
