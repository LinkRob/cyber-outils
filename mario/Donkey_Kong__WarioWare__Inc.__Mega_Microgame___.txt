'''Donkey Kong''' is one of [[9-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s, present under the [[Nintendo Classics]] folder, in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place in [[25m]] of ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'', where [[Mario]] stands on the [[girder]] just below the top one. Just as in the original layout, [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] is on the left side of the topmost girder with a pile of [[barrel]]s, and to the right of him is [[Pauline]], although she does not serve any role.

==Gameplay==
The object is for Mario to [[jump]] over any barrels that Donkey Kong sends his way.

*'''Level 1:''' The barrels roll down the rightmost [[ladder]]. Mario has to jump over only one barrel.
*'''Level 2:''' The barrels roll down the broken ladder. Mario has to jump over two barrels.
*'''Level 3:''' The barrels roll down both ladders. Mario has to jump over two or three barrels, and sometimes has to jump over two of them at once.
