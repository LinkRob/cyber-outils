The '''T-Rex'''  (''Tyrannosaurus rex)'' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. She can be found in the [[Cascade Kingdom]], in the dark forest of the [[Wooded Kingdom]] and the [[Metro Kingdom]] in a bonus stage where the player gets chased.

==Physical description==
Unlike most dinosaurs in the ''Super Mario'' franchise, T-Rexes have a more realistic appearance, with visible sharp teeth in their mouths. Their bodies are brown with a tan belly.

==In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''==
===Cascade Kingdom===
The T-Rex is found in the [[Cascade Kingdom]]. Once the player enters [[Fossil Falls]] the player will see three [[Chain Chomp]]s. Right next to the three [[Chain Chomp]]s will be a hill, and at the top of the hill will be the T-Rex. She will be asleep. If the player climbs or jumps on her, it will not affect the T-Rex. The player can control the T-Rex by throwing [[Cappy]] on her. Once [[Cappy]] can no longer handle the T-Rex, the player jumps out of the T-Rex watching her go back to sleep or spawn back on the hill still sleeping.

===Wooded Kingdom===
The T-Rex can also be found in the [[Wooded Kingdom]]. The player can find her in a secret forest in a certain spot somewhere off the map. The player has to find what appears to look like very small trees on the edge of the map. The player will notice the Switch's shaking to indicate the T-Rex. Unlike her latter appearance, it is walking in circles. If the player attempts to move close to the T-Rex while she is not asleep, she will attempt to chase Mario. If the player hides close to the T-Rex, she will start sniffing the player out and spot the player. However, the player can leave the place by grabbing the seeds and planting a [[beanstalk]]. The player can grab a seed by pulling the lever.

===Metro Kingdom===
The T-Rex is once again spotted in the [[Metro Kingdom]]. The T-Rex will appear once the player rides on a motorbike. While the T-Rex chases the player, the player will spot a [[Power Moon]] while she is chasing Mario and after she falls into a hole.

==Trivia==
*The T-Rex is somewhat similar to Rexy in the 1993 film, ''{{w|Jurassic Park}}''.
**She also appears in [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|''the'' ''Super Mario Galaxy Movie'']], and uses Rexy's roar from the same franchise.
*According to the official map guide for the [[Cascade Kingdom]], the T-Rex is actually a female.
