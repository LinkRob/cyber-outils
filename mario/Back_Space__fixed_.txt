The '''Back Space''' is a recurring type of [[space]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
A Back Space is red and depicts a negative number. If a character lands on a Back Space, it always moves them back the same number of spaces depicted on it.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party: Island Tour''===
[[File:Back Space -1 MPIT icon.png|thumb|left|A -1 Back Space in ''Mario Party: Island Tour'']]
Back Spaces only appear in [[Kamek's Carpet Ride]] and [[Shy Guy's Shuffle City]]. A Back Space can depict any number between -1 through -4, moving whoever lands on it the corresponding number of spaces.
{{br|left}}
===''[[Mario Party 10]]''===
Back Spaces exclusively appear in [[Bowser Party]] mode.

===''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''===
Back Spaces appear in the [[Balloon Bash]] and [[Mario Shuffle]] game modes.
