The '''Batteroid''' is an enemy from [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']].

== Description ==
Batteroids are chiropteran monsters with white bodies that have circular patterns. They wear grey metallic masks with purple accents and golden eyes. When knocked down, they become golden bat skeletons.

== Behaviour ==
Batteroids usually appear hanging from ceilings. When [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] enters their territory, the creatures will detach from the ceiling and fly around Donkey Kong, attacking by diving at their prey. Donkey Kong can stun a Batteroid by throwing a piece of terrain at them, causing them to plummet and become exposed. To defeat the enemy, Donkey Kong must smash their skeletons with his punch or [[Roll]] attack. However, their bodies will regenerate if their skeletons are not destroyed, at which point they take off and attack again.

Batteroids are featured in the Tempest Layer's first Battle Challenge, making their only appearance in Battle Challenges there. In this challenge, Donkey Kong must kill three of them to win. Additionally, a giant Batteroid called the Sinister Blusterwing appears.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Batteroids are named after the verb "batter" ("to beat with successive blows") and "bat" (the animal they resemble). The name also includes the -oid suffix that most enemies in the Underground World share, referencing how they were created by [[VoidCo]].
