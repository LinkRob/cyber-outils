'''Flashy Line Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. These stickers are more powerful versions of the [[Line Jump]] sticker, a sticker that allows [[Mario]] to attack all enemies with a repeating jump attack.

== Appearance ==
Flashy Line Jump stickers look similar to Shiny Line Jump stickers except for the diagonal rainbow background. It takes up one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].
