'''Android''' is an open-source operating system developed primarily by Google, and is utilized on several smart devices, especially those that do not use [[iOS]], the main competitor of Android. As of the mid-2010s, [[Nintendo]] has started releasing games and software for Android devices, a few of which are ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games.
