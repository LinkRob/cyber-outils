The '''Chimp Banana''' is an item in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
The Chimp Banana is a permanent item in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge mode]] that gives [[Diddy Kong]] the ability to use his [[Star Skill]], the [[Boomerang Ball]]. It can be bought from a [[Yellow Toad]] at the item shop for 200 [[coin]]s, but only if either Diddy is on the player's team or has been recruited to theirs.
