The '''Vacuum Orb''' is an [[orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' that is used by [[Wario]] and [[Waluigi]]. Its effect, when used by a player, is to suck up a number of [[coin]]s (determined by the roulette wheel) from the other opponents. This orb is one of [[Professor Elvin Gadd]]'s inventions, ascertainable by the fact that it sports the company logo. It also bears some resemblance to [[F.L.U.D.D.]]
