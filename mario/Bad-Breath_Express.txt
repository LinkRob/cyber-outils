The '''Bad-Breath Express''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It appears only on the [[board]] [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]].

==Description==
The Bad-Breath Express is a locomotive operated by [[Bowser]]. It bears resemblance to the [[Koopa King]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', similar to how the [[Barrel Train (Mario Party 8)|Barrel Train]] resembles the [[Barrel Train|kart of the same name]].

The Bad-Breath Express appears whenever a player lands on a [[Bowser Space]], replacing the train's current locomotive, which could be either [[Shy Guy]]'s standard one or [[Donkey Kong]]'s Barrel Train. In Battle Royale and Tag Battle, the first player who reaches Bowser's locomotive is forced to give a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] to him. However, if the player does not have any Stars, Bowser gives them ten [[coin]]s. In Duel Battle and Star Battle Arena, Bowser steals half of the player's coins. If they have no coins, Bowser gives them ten coins.

The Bad-Breath Express leaves either after someone reaches it, which replaces the Bad-Breath Express with the normal locomotive, or if somebody lands on a [[DK Space]], in which case the Barrel Train replaces the Bad-Breath Express.
