'''Attack FX A''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Found at [[Shooting Star Summit]], [[Mario]] must trade one [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] with [[Merlow]] for the badge. This badge does not effect Mario's statistics and it does not give him a new technique. Instead, it changes the sound of Mario's attacks.

The Attack FX series of badges do not apply to Mario's party members.
