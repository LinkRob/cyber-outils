'''Star Medals''' are the replacement of [[Star Coin]]s in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. Like in the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' series]], there are three in every level, except the final one, [[Special 8-Crown]]. In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', they were replaced by [[Green Power Star|Green Stars]]. If all of them are collected in a [[world]], a level is unlocked in [[World 9]].

There is one Star Medal in each [[Mystery Box]]. Replaying a completed Mystery Box will not contain a Star Medal, but if said Mystery Box is one received via StreetPass, it will contain a new Star Medal, allowing players to obtain unlimited Mystery Boxes via Streetpass.

==Trivia==
*Star Medals slightly resemble [[Comet Medal]]s from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.
