'''Tally Me Banana''' is a [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Layout==
Tally Me Banana takes place in a tropical area. There is a circle-shaped pool in the center, surrounded by a white, circle-shaped wooden bridge. To the upper-right is a bridge made of brown wood that leads to a different platform with two palm trees. There are also large palm tree leaves from both the bottom and the sides of the screen. Behind the circle-shaped bridge is a flat, white bridge with a [[DK Barrel]] on the left and right sides each as well as a red-and-yellow checkered flag bearing the "DK" [[emblem]]. The [[Blooper]] from [[Blooper Scooper]] can be seen at the top-left corner of the screen, where Donkey Kong can also be seen standing on a wooden raft.

==Opening==
Donkey Kong jumps on a [[barrel]] and collects a [[banana]], which he eats before stowing the peel away behind him. 

==Gameplay==
The characters have to [[jump]] on the barrels scattered throughout the pool to collect as many bananas as possible. If a character misses a barrel and lands in the water, they cannot perform any other action for the remainder of the mini-game. It is possible for them to land back onto the bridge after hopping from some barrels before continuing to jump from barrels. Most barrels contain a single banana, though some contain [[Banana Bunch]]es, which are worth five bananas. Any opponent who has fallen into the water can be bounced on by the others as if they were a barrel.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Jump

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is derived from a line in the song "{{w|Day-O (The Banana Boat Song)}}."
