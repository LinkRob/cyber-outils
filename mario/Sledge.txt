'''Sledge''' is a special move used by [[Smithy]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He only uses this move during the first phase of his boss fight, and this move can be considered his signature move.

==Description==
When Sledge is used, a very large mallet/pillar pounds down on the screen.

==Effects==
Sledge is a very powerful move that is able to damage all of [[Mario]]'s party.
