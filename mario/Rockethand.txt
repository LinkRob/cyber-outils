'''Rockethands''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Rockethands are always found in pairs. They each appear from the edge of the screen, shooting forward to grab [[Princess Peach]]. If the Rockethands miss, they crash into each other and fall off-screen. If the Rockethands are successful, a countdown appears on each, followed by an explosion at the end.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Rockethands are only encountered in [[Bowser's Villa]], and they first appear in the level [[Bowser's Villa 8-5|8-5]]. Even if Rockethands capture Peach, she can escape if she squirms quickly enough. Rockethands are one of the enemies to not have a [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry.
