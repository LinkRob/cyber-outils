The '''Prison Warden Lumalee''' is a minor character from ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'', making her only appearance during the credits. She works as the warden for the prison where [[Bowser]] (who got [[Dry Bowser|skeletonised]]) and [[Bowser Jr.]] were imprisoned after their defeat at the hands of [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] (and being sent there by [https://starfox.fandom.com/wiki/Fox_McCloud Fox]) and after [[Planet Bowser]] was put out of commission at the hands of [[Princess Peach]] and [[Rosalina]].

==Profile==
===Appearance===
The Prison Warden Lumalee resembles the Lumalee from the first film, but she wears a blue prison officer's cap and a golden badge for distinguishment from him.

===Personality and Relationships===
The Prison Warden Lumalee appears to love creeping the Bowsers out, implying she may have a vendetta against Bowser for kidnapping the Lumalee from the first film, implying her to be close to that Lumalee, either as a friend or sibling. Although she has similar speech patterns to that Lumalee, the warden's large-scale personality is unknown, as she hasn't appeared in any other scenes. However, she is implied to have the same cheery-yet-morbid demeanor that the Lumalee from the first film had in his own appearances.

==History==
The Prison Warden Lumalee only appears after [https://starfox.fandom.com/wiki/Fox_McCloud Fox], prior to his departure back to [https://starfox.fandom.com/wiki/Lylat_System Lylat], wishes good luck to a [[Mushroom Retainer]] officer with dealing with [[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]], whom Fox sent to the prison. Inside the prison, Junior attempts to dig his way out of the prison as his father encourages him. The Prison Warden Lumalee then appears, dragging her baton against the bars of the cell that Bowser and Bowser Junior are trapped in. Unamused in seeing them trying to get away, the warden proceeds to say that Bowser and his son will play by ''her'' rules now. As Bowser tells his son to stay close and that she cannot break them, the warden proceeds to intimidate the Bowser duo, telling them that the body goes first, then the bones, with all that is remaining is dust. When Bowser demands that she be quiet, the warden Lumalee retorts that he cannot silence the truth, and any attempt to do so only makes it louder, like the approaching drumbeat of death, at which point Bowser and Bowser Jr. scream in horror.

==Trivia==
*Since she is referred to with feminine pronouns by Bowser, the Prison Warden Lumalee is not likely to be the same [[Lumalee]] from ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', who is male.
