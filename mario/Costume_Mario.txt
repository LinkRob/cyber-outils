'''Costume Mario''' is a feature in the [[Wii U]] version of ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. It involves [[Mario]] taking on the appearance of a different character, from his [[Mario (franchise)|franchise]] and other [[Nintendo]] franchises, alike, by touching a [[Mystery Mushroom]]. The costumes are unlockable, and they can be unlocked by completing the 100 Mario Challenge or by scanning an [[amiibo]] of the character.
