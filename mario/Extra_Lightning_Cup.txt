The '''Extra Lightning Cup''' is the third [[Extra Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. It has tracks from ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. To unlock it, complete the [[Lightning Cup]] with at least 100 coins and first place on the second time and onwards. This cup only appears in ''Mario Kart: Super Circuit''.

*[[Mario Circuit 3]]
*[[Koopa Beach 1]]
*[[Choco Island 2]]
