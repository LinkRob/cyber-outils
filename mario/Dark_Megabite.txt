}}A '''Dark Megabite''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Megabites are very rare enemies that are only encountered in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. They are much stronger versions of [[Megabite]]s and, like them, only take 1 point of damage every time. They have an HP of 8 making them rather troublesome enemies to take out.
