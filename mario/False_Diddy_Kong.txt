'''False Diddy Kong''' is  a minor antagonist in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''<nowiki/>'s Subspace Emissary game mode. He is an evil clone of [[Diddy Kong]] made by [[Bowser]].

== Appearances ==
He has the same appearance as [[Diddy Kong]], but he is darker and taller than his original character.

== Biography ==
Originally, Bowser ambushes Fox and Diddy in the swamp, being able to turn Diddy Kong into a trophy. Using Shadow Bugs, Bowser manages to generate the clone, which seriously endangers the red fox. Before Bowser could shoot Fox, he gets rescued by his close friend, Falco Lombardi. Although his Dark Canon is destroyed by the pilot, Bowser escapes and sends Shadow Bugs to make the clone bigger to kill Fox and Falco. Luckily, Diddy is revived by Fox. Expectedly, False Diddy Kong also acts like his normal version in combat, although with stronger attacks and a larger stature. Founding its defeat, it fades away to Shadow Bugs, allowing Diddy, Fox, and Falco to continue their search for Donkey Kong. His spirt is also brought back in the Great Maze by Tabuu's doing.
