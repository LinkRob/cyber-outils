'''''Nintendo Adventure Books''''' is a series of twelve ''{{w|Choose Your Own Adventure}}''-styled book starring [[Nintendo]] characters. The books were authored and published from 1991 to 1992. A majority of the Nintendo Adventure Books feature ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters, with only the ninth and tenth books starring ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' characters. Each book has a story that progresses in accordance to the reader's decisions, with the addition of puzzles such as mazes.

A decade later, in 2001 and 2002, [[Scholastic]] published a similar series of books titled ''[[You Decide on the Adventure]]'', although a difference is that each of them are based on a particular [[Game Boy Color]] or [[Game Boy Advance]] game released around the time instead of being original stories.
