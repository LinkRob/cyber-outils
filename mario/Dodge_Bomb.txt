'''Dodge Bomb''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. Its name and gameplay come from the real-world sport of {{wp|dodgeball}}.

==Opening==
The first player picks up a [[Bob-omb]] standing in front of them, then throws it into the center of the town square, shocking the other three players.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Luigi Artwork - Mario Party 5.png|thumb|200px|Artwork of [[Luigi]] avoiding Bob-ombs in Dodge Bomb]]
The goal of the minigame is to pick up and throw Bob-ombs at the opposing players while trying to avoid getting hit by Bob-ombs that are thrown by the others. After a while, the Bob-ombs currently in the square flash red, designating that they are about to explode. As time passes, more and more Bob-ombs emerge from the houses surrounding the area. The last player standing wins. However, if two or more players outlast the time limit or if the last two players are eliminated simultaneously, the minigame ends in a draw.

==Ending==
The winning player performs their victory animation where they are, and any Bob-ombs left on the screen explode. If the minigame ends in a draw, the remaining Bob-ombs will stay on the screen, not exploding.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Move
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Grab/throw Bob-ombs

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Pick up and throw Bob-ombs at your opponents while avoiding the ones being thrown at you. The last one standing wins."''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"The Bob-ombs will flash red just before they explode. Stay away from any flashing Bob-ombs!"''
