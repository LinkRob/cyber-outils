}}A '''Dark Magiblot''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Magiblots are the strongest of the Magiblot species. Dark Magiblots are only seen in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]] in a few rooms. Like with the other Magiblot, Dark Magiblots wait in the shadows until their prey approaches and then pop out attempting to hit their prey with a magic blob attack. Dark Magiblots hit very hard with an attack power of ten making fighting them rather difficult.
