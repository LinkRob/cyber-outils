The '''Jetsetter''' (also known as the '''Aero Glider''') is a large [[kart]] in the game ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== Description ==
The Jetsetter resembles a {{w|Le Mans Prototype}} racecar, with blue wheels, a fin located at the back, as well as a singular dual exhaust port. It also has stripes and a windshield. The Jetsetter has a striking resemblance to [[Luigi]]'s [[Streamliner]] from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', as well as the [[Blue Seven]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. The characters' emblem is located on the hood and the sides of the kart.

The Jetsetter is unlocked by achieving at least a 1-star rank in all the cups of the [[Grand Prix|Retro Grand Prix]] on [[Engine Class|150cc]], or by playing 4,500 races.

[[Rosalina]] uses Jetsetter in a [[Time Trial]] course for the Expert Staff Ghost for [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart Wii)|Rainbow Road]] by Nin★Syun1.

Its [[bike]] counterpart is the [[Spear]].

== Statistics ==
The Jetsetter is the [[speed|fastest]] vehicle in the game and has a high [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]] stat tied with the [[Spear]] despite being the lightest large kart in the game, but it also has a low [[acceleration]] stat tied with the [[Wild Wing]] and a low [[drift]] stat. Additionally, the Jetsetter has the worst [[handling]], [[Off-Road|off-road]] and [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] stats in the game. Its speed will increase if [[Funky Kong]] is used in it. If [[Waluigi]] is used in the Jetsetter, its drift stat will tie with the [[Daytripper]] and [[Magikruiser]].
