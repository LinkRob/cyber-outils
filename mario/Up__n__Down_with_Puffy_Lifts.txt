'''Up 'n' Down with Puffy Lifts''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level features many [[Puffy Lift]]s. The level demonstrates how they work by forcing a [[Koopa Troopa]] to fall on a puffy lift. This makes the puffy lift start to shrink to showcase how puffy lifts work.

Later in the level there is a puffy lift that is fully broken when the player enters the area. It is only shown by a orange particles on the ground. Killing the two galoombas on the scene will revive the puffy lift. The puffy lift rising will allow the player to reach the [[Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will transform the player into a puffy lift. At the end of the effect. there is a [[pipe]] releasing [[Spiked Ball]]s. The player must stand in a hole to allow the spiked ball to reach the {{lw|Brick Block}}s, giving access to the [[Wonder Seed]].
==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s can be obtained in the following order:
#Shortly after the start of the level, there is a 10-flower coin under a [[semisolid]] and to the right of a puffy lift. Destroying the brick blocks above the puffy lift and {{lw|ground Pound}}ing on the puffy lift will allow the player to grab the 10-flower coin.
#There is a large puffy lift with a yellow pipe summoning spiked balls. If the large puffy lift breaks, a 10-flower coin spawns.
#Near the end of the level, there is a puffy lift under a pipe summoning spiked balls. The puffy lift is blocking access to a 10-flower coin. Pushing the puffy lift down will allow the player to grab the 10-flower coin.
