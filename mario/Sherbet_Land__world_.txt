'''Sherbert Land''' is a secret world in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. It can only be accessed from the secret exit of [[Mt. Teapot]]. The boss fought at the end of Sherbet Land is named [[Penguin (boss)|Penguin]].
