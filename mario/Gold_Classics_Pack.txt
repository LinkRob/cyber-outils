The '''Gold Classics Pack''' (known in Japan as '''Golden Classic Course Pack''') is a downloadable [[Coin Rush]] pack in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It was released on November 27th, 2012, to celebrate the world coin total reaching up to 300 million [[coin]]s collected in the game. This pack contains levels that have been remade from the original ''Super Mario Bros.'' games, such as ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.
