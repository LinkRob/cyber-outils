'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a course in [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
==Layout==
The level heavily features [[handle]]s and [[Rolla Koopa]]s, the latter of which are the only enemies in the level. The level starts off with ramps above the player with Rolla Koopas skating across many [[Marimba Block|Marimba Blocks]].

Eventually, there is a tree with a handle. Pulling the handle will reveal the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will darken the scene and create invisible slopes that light up where the player is. Five [[Wonder Token|Wonder Tokens]] will spawn that the player has to collect to summon the [[Wonder Seed]] at the top of the level.
==[[10-Flower Coin]] locations==
The first 10-flower coin is on top of a row of colored [[Marimba block|Marimba Blocks]] with Rolla Koopas. To get the 2nd 10-flower coin, hitting a [[? block]] will spawn a [[Wow Bud]] that the player can ride to enter a special area. In the area, there are Rolla Koopas going back and forth above a spring with [[shell]]s. The player must time their jumps to hit the spring and kill the Rolla Koopas, which will spawn the second 10-flower coin. The last 10-flower coin can be grabbed via a ? Block in a wall, which must be hit with a Koopa Shell.
