'''Scratchy the 'Fro Bug''' is an insect who appears in [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s mode in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
In the intro of Jimmy T.'s microgame set, Scratchy flies into Jimmy's afro, causing him to itch like crazy, and the player has to play through a series of [[microgame]]s to get Scratch out of Jimmy T.'s afro. In the ending of Jimmy's microgame set, Scratchy makes his way back into Jimmy's afro as he and his family walk in downtown [[Diamond City]], after leaving [[Club Sugar]], but everyone believes Jimmy to just be dancing again.
