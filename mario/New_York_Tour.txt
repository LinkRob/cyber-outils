'''New York Tour''' is the first tour of ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' that began on September 24, 2019 and ended on October 8, 2019.

It marks the first appearance of [[Pauline]] and [[Mario (Musician)]]. It also marks the first appearance of [[New York Minute]].  The spotlights were held from September 24, 2019 to October 8, 2019.

The menu background featered One Times Square as the tour's landmark.
