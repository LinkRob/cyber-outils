'''Capcom''' is a Japanese video game developer and an electronic game machine manufacturer. Their mascot is [[Mega Man]], who has sometimes appeared alongside [[Mario]] in crossovers, such as later ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' games. Capcom's involvement with the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' series itself are the numerous Japan-exclusive [[arcade]] games that they have developed, the first being ''[[Super Mario Fushigi no Janjan Land]]'', which released in 2003.
