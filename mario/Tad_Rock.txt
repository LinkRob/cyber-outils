'''Tad Rock''' is the tenth boss faced by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in the VS COM mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. Tad Rock is a frog who sports the look of a punk rocker, mainly shown from the yellow mohawk on his head.
