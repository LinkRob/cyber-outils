==Effects==
Ultra Shroom Shakes are items that replace [[Ultra Shroom]]s and restore [[Mario]] of his poisonous infections and fifty points of [[Heart Point]]s. While Ultra Shrooms are no longer items Mario can carry, Mario may find them in [[Question Block]]s. These items are purchasable from the shop in [[Flopside]].
