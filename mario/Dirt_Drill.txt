'''Dirt Drill''' is the [[Skillshot]] used by [[Monty Mole]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Upon fully charging a shot, Monty Mole digs toward the Goalie. Once close enough, Monty Mole emerges and charges into the goalie. If successful, Monty Mole knocks the Goalie aside to throw the ball into the goal. If Monty Mole is too close, it gets caught by the Goalie, who takes the ball and throws Monty Mole at the [[Electric rail|Electric Fence]], but if Monty Mole is too far, it emerges from the ground too early and gets hit by the Goalie.
