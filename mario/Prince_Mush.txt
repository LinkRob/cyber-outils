{{Nihongo|'''Prince Mush'''|プリンス・マッシュ|Purinsu Masshu}} is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He has a sister named [[Jolene]].

==Physical description==
Prince Mush has magenta spots on his mushroom head, yellow hair similar to Jolene's, white shoes, and a blue sports outfit with white outlines. He always has a cheerful smile on.

== Biography ==
He fought in the [[Glitz Pit]] for [[coin]]s. He became the first ever champion, yet [[Grubba]] used his machine that was powered by a [[Crystal Stars|Crystal Star]] to suck out his power.

== In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
When [[Mario]] and company defeated Grubba in his ultra powerful form, [[Macho Grubba]], and took the Crystal Star from the machine, Prince Mush got his power back and hopped out of the machine.  This allows him to reunite with Jolene (who had been aiding Mario as 'X' for a while). 

===In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)===
In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], Prince Mush's role has been significantly expanded upon. Unlike in the original where Prince Mush disappears from the game after he is rescued at the end of Chapter 3, Prince Mush can now be found in the halls of the Glitz Pit. After completing Jolene's "Help wanted" trouble, Prince Mush will then send an email to Mario wanting to face the current champion in an exhibition match. Prince Mush can be challenged anytime after this trouble and proves himself to be a very fierce some opponent. After being defeated, Prince Much rewards Mario with his [[Prince Mush's Belt|personal belt]] as a sign of respect and gratitude.

==Trivia==
*Prince Mush is one of very few characters that is able to steal [[Flower Point|FP]] from Mario. The other being the [[Flower Fuzzy|Flower Fuzzies]].
