'''Mini-Fang Swarm''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mini-Fang Swarms consist of a cloud of tiny [[Fang]]s.

===Behavior===
Mini-Fang Swarms do not move around, and they obstruct paths. If attacked, Mini-Fang Swarms block with their wings.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Mini-Fang Swarms only appear in two levels, [[Hoo's Wood 2-4]] and [[Hoo's Wood 2-5]]. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can only pass them by using the [[Rage]] [[vibe]], causing the swarm to disperse.
