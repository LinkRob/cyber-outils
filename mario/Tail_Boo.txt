{{nihongo|'''Tail Boo'''|しっぽテレサ|Shippo Teresa}} is a variant of a [[Boo]] with a tanooki tail, and it appears as an enemy in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. Tail Boos were originally Boos, but with the granted the power of [[Super Leaf|Super Leaves]]. Unlike most species with a tanooki tail, a Tail Boo has a white and gray tail instead of the original brown and dark brown color.

==In ''Super Mario 3D Land''==
Tail Boo first appears in the level [[World 4-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 4-4]]. It acts like a normal Boo, being able to levitate in the air and covers its eyes when [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] stares at it. It also has the ability to sweep its tail at Mario/Luigi upon contact. If Mario/Luigi stare at it, it first sways its tail and then closes its eyes. Some of these foes may also drop a [[Statue Leaf]] or a [[Super Leaf]] when defeated in the only way possible; a [[Star]].

==In {{Series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===In [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]===
In the [[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]] campaign of the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', Tail Boos are an obtainable Flying troop, and their special attack is [[Spin Cycle]]. The in-game description is "A rare type of Boo with a tail. Getting hit with said tail really smarts!"{{BooNav}}
