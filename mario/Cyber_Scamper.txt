'''Cyber Scamper''' is a 4-Player and Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

== Opening ==
The screen shows a dimension of tiles, and scrolls all down to the bottom where the players all start.

== Gameplay ==
The aim of the game is to get to the exit by traversing through the tiles. All tiles are in different colored lights (pink, green, and yellow), which switch on and off constantly. Players will need to jump across sets of tiles, being careful to not fall off the course. The screen is also constantly scrolling up, which also pushes the characters forwards. Players can win be two methods; either get to the exit or be the last one standing if the other characters have fallen off.

== Ending ==
The characters who made it to the exit jump out of an arcade cabinet and do their victory dances. If all characters have fallen off the course, the game ends in a tie.

==In-game description ==
*'''Rules''' - ''"Race to the finish or just be the last one standing on a course full of holes and chasm crossings."''
*'''Tips''' - ''"When the lights go out, you can't tell where the gaps are. Memorize where the safe footing is when the lights are on!"''
