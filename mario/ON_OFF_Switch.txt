The '''ON/OFF Switch''' is a type of [[block]] which first appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
It can toggle between On and Off options. An ON/OFF Switch does not necessarily involve activating or deactivating a mechanism but instead is generally used to alter the black line path on which the [[Lift]] moves. While it reads "ON," the ON/OFF Switch is colored magenta (red in later appearances), and while it reads "OFF," the block is colored blue. [[Mario]] or another character can toggle between two options by either [[jump]]ing at the ON/OFF Switch from below, whirling [[Cape Mario]]'s [[cape]] next to it, or throwing an object at it, such as a [[Koopa Shell]].

==In ''Super Mario World''==
They are prominently featured in both [[Donut Plains 3 (level)|Donut Plains 3]] and [[Way Cool]].

==In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''==
They can instead change the direction of [[Conveyor Belt]]s and Tracks.
