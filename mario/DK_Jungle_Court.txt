'''DK Jungle Court''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
DK Jungle Court is a tennis court belonging to [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]. It takes place on wooden platform on a river, with a small waterfall in the background. Green, red, and blue [[Kritter]]s are seen spectating the match. Behind the court is a set of tracks on which [[Ukiki]]s are sometimes seen riding a [[Mine Cart]] on.

If the gimmicks are on, green and red [[Klaptrap]]s appear on the net during gameplay. If a character hits a Klaptrap, they can send it to move around on the opponent's side. If a Klaptrap bites a character, that character slows down, making it harder for them to return the ball. There is a panel depicting a thunderbolt on each side of the court, and the one on the opposite side glows if the character has Klaptraps attached to them. If the character hits a glowing panel, a [[Thunder Lakitu]] shoots several thunderbolts at the Klaptraps on their side, causing them to run away burnt; it also does this when the characters switch sides.

DK Jungle Court is also where the Special Game [[Balloon Panic]] takes place.
