'''Spin Blocks''' are a type of platform featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Spin Blocks are found in [[Gwarhar Lagoon]], specifically within midair in certain locations, and [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] use them to travel between raised ground platforms that they cannot reach otherwise. A Spin Block can be moved by performing a [[Spin Jump]], upon which the Spin Block continues traveling in the same direction the brothers are facing until it hits a wall.
