The '''Rockslides''' are minor antagonists in [[Paper Mario: Color Splash|''Paper Mario: Color Splash'']]. They are a duo of [[Thwomp|Thwomps]] that antagonise the [[Sunset Express]]. It is unknown what these Thwomps are really named; their nickname was given to them by the [[Mushroom Retainer|Mushroom Retainers]].

== Description ==
The Rockslides are indistinguishable from most Thwomps.

== History ==
Mario encounters one of the two Rockslides outside of the [[Kiwano Temple]]. This Rockslide smashes the [[Sunset Express]]'s head, forcing it into a repair shop, though the conductor survives the ordeal. Another one of the Rockslides blocks the train, where [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] has to wake him up with the [[Things|Megaphone]].
