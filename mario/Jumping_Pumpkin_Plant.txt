'''Jumping Pumpkin Plants''', also named '''Pumpkin Plants''',<ref>''Nintendo Power'' volume 28, page 11.</ref> are enemies in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. They are the [[Fall]] reskin of [[Jumping Piranha Plant]]s, and have the same behavior as them.

==History==
In ''Super Mario World'', Jumping Pumpkin Plants can be seen after finishing the [[Special World]], much like [[Pidgit Bill]]s and [[Mask Koopa]]s. Jumping Pumpkin Plants can't be defeated by [[Jump|jumping]] because they will harm [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], but they can be hopped on with a [[Spin Jump]], but they can't be defeated by that. To defeat them, the player must hit Jumping Pumpkin Plants with [[Fireball|fireballs]] from [[Fire Mario]], hit with a cape twirl from [[Cape Mario]], or make contact with Mario or Luigi while one of them is [[Invincible Mario|invincible]]. These characteristics are pretty much the same of a Jumping Piranha Plant's.
