'''Bowser's Bullet Bill Brigade''' is a level of [[World 2 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 2]] in the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== Features ==
As the final level of [[World 2 (Super Mario 3D World)]], there is a barrage of enemies waiting for you, including [[Bullet Bill]]s, [[Blockstepper]]s, and [[Fire Piranha Plant]]s. Other obstacles include the cannons and spikes. [[Boom Boom]] waits at the end of the level for you.

== Green Stars ==
*'''Green Star 1''': Use the [[Cannon Box]] to destroy the three Venus Fire Traps guarding the star.
*'''Green Star 2''': In second tank section, destroy the crate on the second tank to get the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': With the cannon box on the last tank, destroy the wall that has Bowser's symbol to get the star.

== Stamp ==
[[File:Bowser's_Bullet_Bill_Brigade_Stamp.png|thumb]]In the second tank section on the third tank, get the Boom Boom stamp by either jumping really high on the crates or destroying them with the cannon box.
