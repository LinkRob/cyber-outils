'''Frame Boos''' are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Frame Boos are a variant of [[Boo]], which it resembles, but has a frame around a glowing core. If [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] looks at a Frame Boo, its glowing core turns red, and while chasing Yoshi, a Frame Boo has a blue glow. If a Frame Boo is colored with black or dark gray yarn, its face is drawn white white. Frame Boos have a variant named [[Big Frame Boo]], which resemble them but are a larger size.

===Behavior===
A Frame Boo goes after Yoshi while his back is turned. If a Frame Boo is hit by a [[Yarn Ball]], it turns into a Boo-like [[Balloon (platform)|balloon]] and rises. Shortly after, the Frame Boo breaks free of its balloon form and returns to normal, but a Frame Boo can also return to normal if Yoshi uses his tongue to untie the ribbon on top of it.

==In ''Yoshi's Woolly World''==
Frame Boos only appear in level 6-5: [[Yoshi, the Terror of All Boos]].
