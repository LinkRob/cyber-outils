'''A Tense Moment''' is a song that is featured in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It usually plays in a pre-boss area. It has the key of E minor, and its duration is around 24 seconds.

== Locations ==
*[[Good Egg Galaxy]] ([[King Kaliente]]'s Battle Fleet, Octo Lava Planet before boss fight)
*[[Bowser Jr.'s Robot Reactor]] (entire level before boss fight)
*[[Space Junk Galaxy]] (Phantom Boss Spider Zone area before boss fight)
*[[Battlerock Galaxy]] ([[Topmaniac]]'s Saucer before boss fight (Topmaniac and the Topman Tribe only))
*[[Bowser Jr.'s Lava Reactor]] (entire level before boss fight)
*[[Dreadnought Galaxy]] (Topmaniac's Saucer before boss fight (Revenge of the Topman Tribe only))

== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' ==
In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', a more unsettling remix plays, with the same chord progression for the first 18 seconds, but the last 6 seconds have a D minor chord instead of an F#7. It plays during Daredevil Comet missions that lead to a boss fight for the entire level prior to that fight and when [[Mario]] approaches [[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]].

== Trivia ==

* The [[Tarantox]] and the [[Megaleg]] are the only bosses that have this song play in the areas they haunt before the boss fight but do not have [[King Kaliente]]'s theme. (The Tarantox shares his theme with [[Dino Piranha]], while the [[Megaleg]] has his own theme.)
