'''Bone Goombas''' a variant of [[Goomba]] with a skull on their head, and they resemble a [[Dry Bones]]. Bone Goombas first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Bone Goombas are the underground versions of a [[Goomba]]. Their dark blue color may have been inspired from the palette utilized for Goombas in underground settings in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

=== Behavior ===
Like the normal [[Goomba]], it can be defeated by any attack such as a stomp.
