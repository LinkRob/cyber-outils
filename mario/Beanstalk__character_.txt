==History==
The Beanstalk appears in [[Fungi Forest]] after [[Tiny Kong]] finds a seed near the Owl's tree and plants it in a dirt patch next to [[Funky's Armory]]. The Beanstalk will then grow and a [[Golden Banana]] will appear at the very top. Tiny must shrink and then play her instrument to summon [[Squawks the Parrot]] to take her up to the Golden Banana.
