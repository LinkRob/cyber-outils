'''Li'l Sparkies''' (also spelt as '''Lil' Sparky''' or '''Lil Sparky''' and also called '''Sparkies''',<ref>''Mario Mania'' Player's Guide, page 146.</ref>) are small electrical creatures featured as enemies in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. They first appear as an enemy in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. Li'l Sparkies behave similarly to [[Spark]]s in that they circle around platforms, although they are not a variant of them. The most notable appearance of a Li'l Sparky is in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', which features a character of the species named [[Watt]], who is also the only Li'l Sparky to have a name.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario World''====
[[Image:Lil Sparky Castle Mania.png|thumb|left|Mario near some Li'l Sparkies.]]
Li'l Sparkies first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', in this game, they can be found in various Castles such as [[Wendy's Castle]] and [[Front Door|Bowser's Castle]]. Li'l Sparkies move along the surfaces of thin platforms and like [[Boo]]s are completely invincible to all attacks and can only be beaten with the effects of a [[Starman]]. Larger variations of Li'l Sparkies called [[Hothead]]s also appear commonly appearing in the same levels as the Li'l Sparkies.
{{br|left}}

====''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''====
[[Image:Lil Sparky GBA.png|thumb|right|A lone Li'l Sparky moving along the platform.]]
Li'l Sparkies appear in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' in the [[e-Reader]] levels [[Swinging Bars of Doom]], [[A Sky-High Adventure]], and [[Puzzling Pipe Maze]]. They act the same as in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', but Li'l Sparkies can now also be defeated by [[Statue Mario]], and by [[Hammer Mario]]'s hammers in addition to the effects of a [[Starman]].
{{br}}

====''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''====
[[File:SMBWColorSwitchDungeon.jpg|thumb|A Li'l Sparky in ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']]
After a 20-year absence from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]], Li'l Sparkies reappear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' alongside [[Hothead]]s. This time, they can be defeated either with bubbles while the player is in [[Bubble Mario|Bubble form]] or by being crushed by a platform.
{{br|left}}

===''Super Mario Bros.'' (1993 film)===
A neon sign reading "Sparky" appears in [[Koopa Square]] in the 1993 [[Super Mario Bros. (film)|''Super Mario Bros.'']] film. 

===''Paper Mario''===
{{Main|Watt}}
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', a young Li'l Sparky named Watt is one of [[Mario]]'s partners in the game. Watt is the sixth partner to join Mario. She joins him in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]].

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
[[Image:Lil Sparky GBA Throwback.png|thumb|right|[[Luigi]] next to a Li'l Sparky.]]
[[Image:Lil Sparky DX.png|thumb|left|A Li'l Sparky above Luigi.]]
Li'l Sparkies appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' this time appearing exclusively in the psuedo-side scrolling areas where [[Presenter]]s can be found. They act as obstacles for [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to avoid as they navigate the area, in addition to moving along platforms, Li'l Sparkies can also float around the air or bounce along the floor in a similar fashion to a [[Superball]] (only slower), if the plumbers are hit by a Li'l Sparky instead of being dragged into a battle, they are sent back to the starting point of the section.

===''Super Princess Peach''===
[[Image:Lil Sparky Zoom.png|thumb|left|Peach navigates the Li'l Sparkies.]]
Li'l Sparkies return in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', in this game, they appear in various levels of [[Shriek Mansion]] and [[Bowser's Villa]] and retain their behavior from before (moving along the surfaces of platforms). In this game, Li'l Sparkies are completely invincible and cannot be destroyed in any way (as a result, they can be earned in the glossary by simply encountering them), the only way [[Princess Peach]] can deal with them is by avoiding them altogether. [[Hothead]]s also return in this game as well.
