'''Gold Bones''' are a recurring item in the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}. They first appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'', hidden in certain rooms in all of the mansions, and return in both ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|Luigi's Mansion]]''{{'}}s [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] and ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Usage==
Gold Bones act like extra lives for [[Luigi]]. Whenever Luigi loses all of his [[HP]] and he has a Gold Bone on him, a [[Polterpup]] will come by and lick his face fully restoring him. It is noted that one Gold Bone is found hidden in each mission during the game. Gold Bones are usually found inside objects that Luigi can open or shake.
