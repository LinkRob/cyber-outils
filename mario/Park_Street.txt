'''Park Street''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Description==
Park Street is a street in [[Diamond City]], and it is located near [[Young Cricket]] and [[Master Mantis]]'s dojo. Several colorful buildings can be seen along the side of the street. At one point, [[Mona]], [[Joe (WarioWare series)|Joe]], and their friends ran a food stand here.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
Park Street is the setting of [[Young Cricket]] and [[Master Mantis]]'s stage [[Enter Young Cricket]]. In the opening cutscene, the two characters travel down Park Street on a sunny day. Young Cricket is hungry, but when the two of them smell a nearby food stand that sells [[dumpling]]s, Young Cricket agrees to get a dumpling for himself and Master Mantis each. Young Cricket runs on the heads of the gathered [[Park Street Residents|crowd]] to skip in front, during which the player plays through the [[Young Cricket and Master Mantis|duo]]'s set of [[microgame]]s.

In the ending cutscene, Young Cricket makes it to the front of the line successfully. After he requests two dumplings, Young Cricket turns to see the angered residents behind him. Young Cricket then apologizes, then proceeds to run on the other residents' heads to return to the back of the line, and he and Master Mantis agree to wait in line for their dumplings.
