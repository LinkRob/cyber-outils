|shows=[[Bound Bowl Grand Prix]]|ruler=Shiverian Elder}}The {{Nihongo|'''Snow Kingdom'''|雪の国|Yuki no Kuni|lit. '''Snow Country'''}} is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It is a vast realm in the form of a snowflake. Its most notable city is [[Shiveria]], which serves as the kingdom's main playable area in the game.

The kingdom is notable for being primarily inhabited by [[Shiverian]]s. The primary currency of the Snow Kingdom is purple [[Regional Coin|Snowflake]]; however, they also accept regular gold coins. This location is based off of Russia, specifically the icy cold areas of Siberia. This is evidenced by the name (Shiveria), the nesting dolls on sale at the local Crazy Cap, and by the local performing the Hopak. The town of Shiveria also seems to draw inspiration from Scandinavia, as the locals here resemble harp seals in knitwear and live in wooden Scandinavian-style housing.

==Paintings==
There are two paintings in this kingdom. One can be accessed by capturing a [[Ty-Foo]] and taking it to where a wood piece is. With the Ty-foo, the wood piece must be blown up until it touches a snow wall. Then, Mario must un-capture it and go to the painting. It takes the player either to the Iron Cage at the [[Wooded Kingdom]], the Island in the Sky at the [[Cascade Kingdom]], or the Viewing Balcony in the [[Lake Kingdom]] depending on the order of which order the kingdom's were selected. If the [[Lake Kingdom]] was selected before the [[Wooded Kingdom]], and the [[Snow Kingdom]] was selected before [[Seaside Kingdom]], then the painting will be in the [[Seaside Kingdom]]. If the [[Lake Kingdom]] was selected before the [[Wooded Kingdom]], and then the [[Seaside Kingdom]] was selected before the [[Snow Kingdom]], then the painting will be in the [[Snow Kingdom]]. If the [[Wooded Kingdom]] was selected before the [[Lake Kingdom]], then the painting will be in the [[Metro Kingdom]]. It can only be accessed after collecting the The Bound Bowl Grand Prix Multi Moon.

The other painting can only be accessed, by normal means, from a painting in either [[Bowser's Kingdom]] or the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] depending on which Kingdom was selected first. If the [[Seaside Kingdom]] was visited before the [[Snow Kingdom]], the painting will be found in [[Bowser's Kingdom]] however if the [[Snow Kingdom]] was visited before the [[Seaside Kingdom]], you can find the portrait in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

==Power Moons==
{{main|Shiveria}}
There are a total of 57 [[Power Moon]]s (54 regular Power Moons and 1 [[Multi Moon]]) in the Snow Kingdom. 32 of the Power Moons and the 1 Multi Moons can be collect during the player's first visit to the kingdom. 1 Power Moon can be collect if the player travel to this kingdom using a painting. After the player defeats Bowser in the [[Moon Kingdom]], 3 more Power Moons become available to collect. The Moon Rock moons will also be available to destroy, causing 18 additional Power Moons to be adding.
