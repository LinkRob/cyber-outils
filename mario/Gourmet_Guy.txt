'''Gourmet Guy''' is a character who only appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is a particularly large [[Fat Guy]] being the only one seen in the game.

== Appearance ==
Gourmet Guy is an extremely large Fat Guy who wears a pale red robe, a black and white belt, and a white cloth on his chest. He is always seen carrying a fork and a knife.

== History ==
Gourmet Guy is first seen in Chapter 4 inside [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]. He is blocking a path that [[Mario]] needs to get past in order to proceed in his quest. Gourmet Guy is hungry and wants Mario to give him food. However, he doesn't accept anything but a [[Cake]] baked by [[Tayce T.]] Mario can get a free cake from Tayce T. by giving her back her [[Frying Pan]] and can then feed it to Gourmet Guy which makes him run around with joy leaving the pathway now accessible (he also drops a [[Cookbook]] which can be given to Tayce T.). 

Gourmet Guy is seen again later in [[Princess Peach's Castle]] during [[Princess Peach]]'s intermission right after Mario defeats [[General Guy]]. He thinks about turning in Peach into [[Bowser]] but he is once again hungry and asks Peach to cook him some food. He gives Peach the Key to downstairs where she can enter the kitchen. After tasting Peach's [[cake]], Gourmet Guy runs around the room super satisfied. He then tells Peach to tell Mario that the next [[Star Spirit]] is on [[Lavalava Island]] where it is being guarded by the [[Lava Piranha]]. He admits that he doesn't really hate Mario (most likely since Mario fed him a cake earlier) and walks off where he isn't seen for the majority of the game.

However, Gourmet Guy is makes one final appearance during the parade at the end of the game where he is walking behind [[Huff N. Puff]] and two [[Ruff Puff]]s in front of the [[Fearsome 5]]. The Fearsome 5 eats Gourmet Guy's fork and knife, and some of Huff n' Puff and the [[Tuff Puff]]s making them shrink in size then runs off. Unfortunately for all of the Puffs, Gourmet Guy is once again hungry, spawns another fork and knife, and then chases the Puffs off.
