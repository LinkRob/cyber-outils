A '''Backflip Block''' is a block that first debuts in [[Super Mario Run]]. These are the same behavior to [[Long Jump Block|Long Jump Blocks]] and the [[Pause Block|Pause Blocks]] (when the player stands on the block), except these blocks are blue with a red-colored arrow. When the player taps the screen while running across them, those blocks will dispatch a [[Backward Somersault|Backflip]], it will be helpful in collecting those [[Color coin|color coins]] if the player misses those blocks. Unlike the Long Jump Block, players can press and hold to do a bigger backflip.
