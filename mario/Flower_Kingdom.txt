The '''Flower Kingdom''' is a kingdom where the main events of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' take place. It is located not too far from the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. It consists of 6 areas surrounding the middle area, The Petal Isles.

== ''Super Mario'' series ==

=== ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ===
[[Prince Florian]] invites [[Mario]] and all his friends in the Flower Kingdom. However, disaster had struck when [[Bowser]] and his army shows up and spreads chaos all over the kingdom, thus prompting the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] and their allies to embark on an adventure to defeat him. The Flower Kingdom features [[Wonder Flower]]s, which allow the [[Wonder Effect]]s to happen. Three newly-introduced power-ups include the [[Elephant Fruit]], the [[Bubble Flower]], and the [[Drill Mushroom]].
