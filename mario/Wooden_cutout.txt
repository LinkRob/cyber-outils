'''Wooden cutouts''', also named '''Boards''', are a recurring object in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
Wooden cutouts are wooden objects that depict a certain object or enemy.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
[[File:Goomba Board SM3DL.png|frame|left]]
Boards, as they are named in the game, are designed after either a [[Goomba]], a [[1-Up Mushroom]], or a [[Warp Pipe]], specifically named [[Goomba Board]]s, [[1-Up Mushroom Board]]s, and [[Pipe Board]]s respectively. Boards are minor obstructions within a path, but it can be jumped on or broken using either [[Tanooki Mario]]'s tail, a [[roll]], or a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]]. A board sometimes releases an item upon breaking. 

In [[World 8-Bowser: Part 1]], a board shaped like [[Princess Peach]] appears at the the exit of the castle after Bowser is defeated a first time, but Bowser shows up and crushes the board. If the player replays the [[level]], only some fragments are there.

===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' / ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''===
A board shaped like [[Bowser]] appears in the level, [[Shadow-Play Alley]], which scares [[Captain Toad]]. The player's character has to touch the Bowser board so that it falls and breaks, and by doing so, they are rewarded a [[Green Star]] from Captain Toad. Another Bowser board appears in [[Trouble in Shadow-Play Alley]], blocking the path of the player's character.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 7]]''===
Several Goomba Boards and bush boards appear in the track [[Piranha Plant Slide]]. A driver can break a board by driving into it, although this slows them in the process. One of the bush boards stands between the fences in an off-road area at the end of the course.

===''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
[[File:Wooden cutouts MK8.png|thumb|A wooden cutout of a Goomba and a bush in {{classic course|3DS|Piranha Plant Slide}} in ''Mario Kart 8'']] 
Goomba Boards and bush boards return on the [[classic course]] {{classic course|3DS|Piranha Plant Slide}}, functioning identically as in the track's original ''Mario Kart 7'' iteration.
