The '''Duel Capsule''' is a type of [[Orb|capsule]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. It can be thrown to a [[space]] on the board, or used on the player for ten [[coin]]s. Whoever lands on a space containing a Duel Capsule then starts a [[Duel Space|duel]] with another player for coins or [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s.

==Nomenclature==
*'''German:''' Duell-Kapsel, meaning "Duel Capsule"
*'''Japanese:''' けっとうカプセル (''Kettō kapuseru''), meaning "Duel Capsule"
