The '''Super Star''' is an object that appeared in the [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|''Super Mario Bros.'' film series]] by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. It is a power-up item that can grant any given user invincibility for an extended period of time and heals all injuries that the user sustained.
== Description ==
Where Brooklyn plumbers Mario and Luigi get sucked into a magical pipe, landing in different worlds, with Mario teaming up with Princess Peach and Toad to rescue Luigi from the villainous Bowser, who seeks to conquer the Mushroom Kingdom using a powerful Super Star.
== History ==

=== ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' ===
[[File:Bowser Wins The Star.jpg|thumb|left|Bowser steals the Super Star.]]
The [[Penguin King]] and his [[Penguin]] subjects guarded the Super Star in the [[Snow Kingdom (Illumination)|Snow Kingdom]] for years. However one day, Bowser and his [[Koopa Troop]] invaded the Snow Kingdom and overpowered the [[Penguin]] citizens, after capturing them, Bowser broke into the castle and stole the Super Star declaring himself unstoppable.

[[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] travelled in his [[Bowser's Castle (Illumination)|castle]] with the Super Star. After [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]] rejects Bowser's wedding vows, she battles him and his army preventing the sacrifice of [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]], [[Lumalee (Illumination)|Lumalee]], the [[Penguin King]] and his [[Penguin]] subjects, and all the [[Kong]]s buying [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] enough time to rescue the captives. After Mario outruns the [[Bomber Bill]] and winds up back in [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]], Bowser and his army invade the city. Bowser tries to harness the Super Star's full power but ends up losing it in the ensuing chaos.

[[File:Heheh, I wish I were them..png|thumb|left|Mario and Luigi possessing the Super Star's power, granting them invincibility.]]
Mario and Luigi eventually grab the Super Star and harness its full power thus making them [[Invincible Mario|invincible]] and allowing them to plow through all of Bowser's cronies without effort and defeat Bowser himself. The Super Star is not seen again after this, implying that once the Star's power is used, it vanishes.

== Trivia ==
*The 2023 ''Super Mario Bros. Movie'' is packed with Easter eggs, like the "Jump Man" arcade machine (Mario's original name), the GameCube jingle as Luigi's ringtone, references to "the princess is in another castle," and a nod to ''Duck Hunt'' with the "Chasse du Canard" restaurant.
