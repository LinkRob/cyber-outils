'''Malboro Garden''' is a court in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The court could be considered a variation of [[Daisy Garden]], but with a [[Malboro]] standing behind each basket, rather than [[Petey Piranha]]s appearing as baskets themselves. Malboro Garden appears to take place at sunset, as indicated from the orange sky. The court is filled with overgrown vegetation ruins, including several trees overgrown with moss alongside a [[Warp Pipe|warp pipe]] in the background.

If a player gets too close to the Malboro, it uses [[w:c:finalfantasy:Bad Breath|Bad Breath]], releasing a toxic purple gas that can knock over any character and slow their movement, as if they collected a [[Poison Mushroom]].

The background music of Malboro Garden is a rearrangement of [[Daisy Garden]]'s theme.

==''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Malboro Garden is an unlockable court. To unlock Malboro Garden, the player must choose the [[Flower Tourney]] on normal difficulty. When playing on Daisy Garden, the player must dunk the basketball three times in one half without letting the opposing team score even once. After that, one of the [[? Panel]]s contains a blue seed, and the player's character must collect it to unlock Malboro Garden.

Malboro Garden is considered a part of the Flower Tourney despite never being played in the tourney.
