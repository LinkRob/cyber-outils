'''Ghost Valley 3''' is the final Ghost Valley course. It is the third course in [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. With almost no walls, players must be careful. [[Oil Slick]]s are spilled along some parts of the track, slowing down players. Like the other two, this one also has [[Boo]]s that move slowly around the layout. 

This track is arguably one of the hardest tracks to drive on in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'']] series.

This course reappears in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', in the [[Extra Special Cup]].
