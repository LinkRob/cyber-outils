'''Smooch''' is a special move that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can be used once Mario defeats the [[Lava Piranha]] at [[Mt. Lavalava]] and frees [[Misstar]]. It requires 3 [[Star Power|SP]].

==Effects==

Using Smooch, Misstar will appear and kiss Mario recovering 20 of his [[Heart Point|HP]]. This move can be very useful as it is considered an improved [[Refresh]] execpt that it only restores HP instead of both HP and [[Flower Point|FP]]. Although Smooch is a useful move, it is recommened to heal by other means such as [[Whacka Bump]]s or [[Jelly Ultra]]s and use the Star Power for something else like [[Star Storm]] multiple enemies in battle.

==Trivia==
*This is the most expensive Star Power to use in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' as it is the only one that requires 3 Star Power to use while all of the others use either 1 or 2.
