The '''Red Kiddie Kart''' is a [[kart]] which appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Kart is a small cubby with a design that is based of [[Mario]]'s shirt and overalls. The driver's emblem is located on the sides of the kart. There is an exhaust pipe on the back which resembles a [[Warp Pipe]], along with a plague that resembles an empty [[Brick Block]]. The "MARIOKART" logo is embroidered at the front of the kart, and comes equipped with the [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

There are two varations of this kart; [[Green Kiddie Kart]], which is based of [[Luigi]]'s design, and [[Gold Kiddie Kart]], which is completely gold.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The kart is classified as a Normal kart, and it debuted during the [[Mario Bros. Tour]] in 2020. This kart increases the duration of [[Drafting|Slipstream]] boosts and the points earned for performing them.
