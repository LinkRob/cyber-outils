'''Egg-enuts''' are an enemy which appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They are a small variant of [[Nep-Enut]]s.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Egg-enuts have a white body shaped like the upper half of an oval. They have a long antenna made up of individual dots and a tip at the end that resembles a [[Yoshi Egg]], but it can be differed from its yellowish color and having unfilled, light green circles as a pattern.

===Behavior===
An Egg-enut is found either in [[Warp Pipe]]s or underwater. It makes use of its antenna to try and lure a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] toward it. If a Yoshi gets too close, the Egg-enut whips back its antenna and starts waving it. If it hits Yoshi, he gets damaged, triggering the [[Countdown Timer]].

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
An Egg-enut is first encountered in the level [[Up the Creek]]. In the [[Island Museum]], three Egg-enuts are found in the aquatic-enemy exhibit, two of which are in their own pipe while one is in the water.
