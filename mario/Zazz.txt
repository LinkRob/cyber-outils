'''Zazz''' is a Zeti and member of the [[w:c:sonic:Deadly Six|Deadly Six]], the main antagonists of ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Lost World|Sonic Lost World]]''. When [[Dr. Eggman]] came to the [[w:c:sonic:Lost Hex|Lost Hex]], Zazz and the other members of the Deadly Six were forced into serving him, due to the effects of his [[w:c:sonic:Cacophonic Conch|Cacophonic Conch]], though after Sonic rashly got rid of the conch, the Deadly Six rebelled and took control of Eggman's forces.

==''Mario''-related appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U''===
Zazz, along with [[Zavok]] and the rest of the Deadly Six, makes a minor appearance in a [[trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''.

===''Mario & Sonic'' series===
====''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games''====
[[File:Zazz Rio 2016.png|thumb|Artwork for ''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games'']]
Zazz makes an appearance as a playable character in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] and [[Wii U]] versions of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]''. Like with Zavok and [[Eggman Nega]], the former marks not only his first playable appearance in the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' series but also his first playable appearance in any game. Zazz is unlockable in both versions.

In the 3DS [[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|version]], Zazz is exclusively playable in [[Table Tennis (Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games for Nintendo 3DS)|Table Tennis]] and [[Table Tennis Plus]], and is unlocked if the player beats him in a Table Tennis Plus match on Day 2 of Sonic's story.

In the Wii U [[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Wii U)|version]], Zazz is exclusively playable in Table Tennis, and he is unlocked after the player beats him in a Table Tennis match following a tournament.

====''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020''====
Zazz appears in  ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]'' as a playable guest character exclusive to Table Tennis. To unlock him, players must beat Zazz in Table Tennis as [[Vector]] in Chapter 7 of Story Mode.
