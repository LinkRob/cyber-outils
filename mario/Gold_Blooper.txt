The '''Gold Blooper''' is a [[kart]] that appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Gold Blooper is a golden variant of the [[Super Blooper (kart)|Super Blooper]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The Gold Blooper is classified as a Super kart. When used, it will increase the duration of [[Mini-Turbo]] boosts and the points earned of performing them. The kart has been not appeared in regular tours nor the Daily Selects.
