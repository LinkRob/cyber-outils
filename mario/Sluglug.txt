'''Sluglugs''' are purple-colored enemies resembling sea slugs that appear in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|Super Mario Bros. Wonder.]]

== Appearance ==
Sluglugs have yellow spikes and two orange and yellow antennae.

== Behavior ==
Sluglugs move their bodies in and out of [[Warp Pipe|Warp Pipes]]. They can be defeated with [[Fire Mario]], [[Elephant Mario]], a [[Super Star]] or [[Drill Mario]].
