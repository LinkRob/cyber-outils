A '''Yellow Apple''' (or '''Orange Apple''' in the European version) is an item in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
The Yellow Apple is an orange polygonal apple with yellow lining on the bottom. It can be found on a tree in the [[Overthere Stair]]. Eating a Yellow Apple restores 5 HP and can cure poison. If this apple is fed to [[Princess Peach]], she will grow a mustache but will go away after a few seconds.
