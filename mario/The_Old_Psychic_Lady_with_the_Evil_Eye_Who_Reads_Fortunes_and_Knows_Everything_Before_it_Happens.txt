'''The Old Psychic Lady with the Evil Eye Who Reads Fortunes and Knows Everything Before it Happens''' is a fortune-teller character who made a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[The Great Hereafter]]," in which she plays a major role. [[Mario (DiC cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC cartoons)|Luigi]] struggle to remember her full name due to how long it is. Despite her name, The Old Psychic Lady with the Evil Eye Who Reads Fortunes and Knows Everything Before it Happens does not appear to be that old.
