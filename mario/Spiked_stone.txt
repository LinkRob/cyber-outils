'''Spiked stones''' are a hazardous object which appear in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Descriptions==
Spiked stones consist of an angry stone face and a protective golden, spiked armor on the top and bottom.

==In ''Yoshi's Story''==
Spiked stones only appear in the first three levels of the [[P. 6-Finale|final page]]: [[Mecha Castle]], [[Lift Castle]], and lastly [[Stone Castle]]. Each of them are carried by a [[Black Shy Guy]]. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] approaches a Black Shy Guy, it releases the spiked stone it is carrying. If a Yoshi touches a spiked stone, he loses some petals from his [[Smile Meter]].
