The '''Start Space''' is a recurring [[space]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
The Start Space is the starting point when playing on a board. Before starting, each character has to roll a [[Dice Block]] to determine who goes first.
