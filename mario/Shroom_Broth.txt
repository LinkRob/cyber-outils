'''Shroom Broth''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
[[File:ShroomBrothTTYD.png|frame|left]]
In this game, eating a Shroom Broth restores 2 [[Heart Point|HP]] over the course of five turns, almost as much as a [[Slow Shroom]]. However, it has an added effect of softening the user meaning it reduces defense making it a rather risky item to use especially in boss fights. A common way to making a Shroom Broth is by mixing a [[Turtley Leaf]] and a Slow Shroom.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Shroom Broths now restore 15 HP, making it now a useful restoring item. Creating it now requires a [[Smelly Herb]] and a [[Shroom Shake]].
