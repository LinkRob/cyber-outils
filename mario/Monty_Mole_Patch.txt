'''Monty Mole Patches''' are a patch variant of [[Monty Mole]] who appear in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] remake ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.

==History==
Monty Mole Patches appear exclusively in in the levels [[Yoshi and Cookies]] and [[Big Montgomery's Bubble Fort]] where they appear in blocks that can be unraveled using [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s tongue. When the Monty Mole Patches are freed, they chase Yoshi in a similar manner to normal Monty Moles in previous 2D games such as ''[[Super Mario World]]''. Monty Mole Patches can be defeated by simply jumping on them, although they are surprisingly immune to getting eaten by Yoshi (unlike normal Monty Moles).

==Trivia==
*Monty Mole Patches are one of the few Monty Mole enemies to be immune to getting eaten by Yoshi.
*Monty Mole Patch heavily resembles the jumping pose for normal Monty Moles seen in several artwork of them.
