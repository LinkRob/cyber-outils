==Description==

Battle Arena Pads are silver in color that have an image of [[King K. Rool]] on them.

==History==

Battle Arena Pads are found throughout all levels on [[DK Isles]] including the final level, [[Hideout Helm]]. To activate a pad, the player must stand on it and press the {{button|n64|z}} button to activate it. The Kong who activated the pad is then taken to an arena to do battle with any three members of the [[Kremling Krew]]. Completing a Battle Arena Pad results in the Kong being rewarded with a [[Battle Arena Crown]].

==Battle Arenas==
*[[DK Isles]]:
**One Battle Arena Pad is found right near the entrance to [[Snide's H.Q.]] However, there is a boulder on top of it requiring [[Chunky Kong]] to lift it off of the pad.
**Another one is found in the [[Fungi Forest]] lobby but requires a few steps to get to it. First, there is a colored mushroom moving around the ceiling in the lobby. The mushroom changes colors that corresponds to the Kong that needs to shoot it (yellow for [[Donkey Kong]], red for [[Diddy Kong]], purple for [[Tiny Kong]], blue for [[Lanky Kong]], and green for Chunky Kong. Once the mushroom has been shot by all five of the Kongs, a Chunky Pad will appear on the ground. Chunky must then turn invisible with [[Gorilla Gone]] to reveal the hidden Battle Arena Pad.
*[[Jungle Japes]]: The Battle Arena Pad is located right in front of [[Funky's Store]].
*[[Angry Aztec]]: Found in the room of the Tiny Temple where Lanky had to shoot the vulture multiple times to get the [[Golden Banana]]. Once it is claimed, the water will drain and the Battle Arena Pad will be usable.
*[[Frantic Factory]]: The pad is found in the R&D room near the blue [[Kasplat]] underneath a grate. Donkey Kong must head into the room that leads to Tiny's car race and use [[Gorilla Grab]] on the lever to uncover the grate leading to the Battle Arena Pad.
*[[Gloomy Galleon]]: The pad is located behind a gate underneath [[Cranky's Lab]]. Chunky must [[Primate Punch]] the gate to knock it down and get to the pad.
*[[Fungi Forest]]: Found on the giant mushroom area near the wooden walkways where a Kong can simply jump down to get to it.
*[[Crystal Caves]]: This one requires Donkey Kong to get to. In the rotating cabin room, the Battle Arena Pad can be found on one of the walls requiring Donkey Kong to rotate the room to get to it. However, he is also timed in trying to get a Golden Banana. It is recommended to collect the Golden Banana first before getting to the Battle Arena Pad.
*[[Creepy Castle]]: This one requires Lanky Kong. In the hedge maze, Lanky must retrieve the Golden Banana before the timer runs out. Once he does, the Battle Arena Pad will reveal itself and Lanky can safely go back to it.
*[[Hideout Helm]]: Found on the very top of the [[Blast-o-Matic]] that can only be reached by Diddy and his [[Rocketbarrel Boost]].
