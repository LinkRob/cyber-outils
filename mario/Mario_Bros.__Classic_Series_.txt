== Overview ==
'''Mario Bros.''', also known as '''Mario Bros. Classic''' is a 1993 European exclusive version of ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]''. 

== Changes ==

* The cutscenes from the [[List of arcade games|arcade]] version are in this game.
* You can move left or right midair after [[Jump|jumping]], this feature was introduced in [[Kaettekita Mario Bros.]].
* The [[Shellcreeper]] sprites are more accurate to the arcade version.
* The invisible floor bonus stage from the arcade version is in this game.
* The [[Icicle]] enemies from the arcade version are this game.
