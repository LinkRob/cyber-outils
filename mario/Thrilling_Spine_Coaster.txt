'''Thrilling Spine Coaster''' is the sixth level of [[Rock-Candy Mines]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

==Overview==
It is unlocked via secret exit in [[Grinding-Stone Tower]], and its own completion opens the path to [[Screwtop Tower]].
==Walkthrough==
The level begins in a dark underground section on a platform that leads to a [[Bonecoaster]]. A [[small pipe]] is found at the beginning of the level, leading to an area with [[coin]]s and a [[Brick Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The Bonecoaster travels along a track above a pit of [[Poisoned Water|poison]]. A [[Swooper]] is found, followed by a [[Red Ring]]. A [[? Switch]] is found, causing blocks to appear under rows of coins. A [[? Block]] containing a [[Starman]] can also be found. The Bonecoaster then travels through a section with a low ceiling and some [[Spike Top]]s. Another ? Block containing a Super Star is found, followed by a section where the Bonecoaster repeatedly dips into the poison. A [[Warp Pipe]] is found, leading above ground. Another Bonecoaster is found, leading to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is found near the beginning, in midair.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is found after a series of [[coin]]s, in midair.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is found near the end, after a series of [[Muncher]]s. The [[Starman]] or [[Nabbit]] is required to reach the Star Coin.
