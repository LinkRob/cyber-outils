[[File:Bowser_Cup.png|thumb|The Bowser Cup as it looks in the Cup Battles menu]]The '''Bowser Cup''' is the fourth and final cup in the Cup Battles in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''. This cup is not in any of the ''Mario Kart'' games.


== In ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'' ==
The Bowser Cup is the fourth and final cup in the Super Mario Strikers Cup Battles. To unlock this cup the player will need to have been in the top three at each of the other cups (Mushroom, Flower, Star). They will need to win this cup to be able to unlock and advance to the Super Cup Battles.
===Rounds===
There are eight rounds in this cup, which means that the player will have to compete with seven other teams. After seven rounds, the top four move on to the semifinals and the two winners of the semifinals move on to the finals. If the player wins the finals, at the end they will have to go against the Super Team. The Super team is the final match of this cup.
==Additional information==
After the player has won all or been in the top three Cup Battles, at the end of the Bowser Cup final match, the player will win the Bowser Cup and unlock the Super Mushroom Cup and the Bowser Stadium. A similar cup is the Super Bowser Cup. It is important to remember that one needs to win this cup to unlock the Super Cup Battles.
