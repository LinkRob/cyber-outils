'''Mechanical Bowser''' (also referred to as '''Mecha-Bowser''' or '''Bowser (Mechanical)''') is a gigantic robotic [[Bowser]] that can be found in [[Bowser's Castle]] in the games ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. It has an obstacle that spits out large [[fireball]]s that can spin<ref name="MKDD">{{cite video game| title = [[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]| developer = Nintendo EAD| publisher = Nintendo| date = 2003-11-17| platform = Nintendo GameCube| level = [[Bowser's Castle]]}}</ref> or knock<ref name="MKW">{{cite video game| title = [[Mario Kart Wii]]| developer = Nintendo EAD| publisher = Nintendo| date = 2008-04-27| platform = Nintendo Wii| level = [[Bowser's Castle]]}}</ref> the racer. The Wii version can be easily avoided by using the Half-pipe in the room.
