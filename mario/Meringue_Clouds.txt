'''Meringue Clouds '''is the sky world of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. The world is east of [[Acorn Plains]], [[Layer-Cake Desert]], [[Sparkling Waters]], and [[Frosted Glacier]], northeast of [[Soda Jungle]], northwest of [[Rock-Candy Mines]], and south of [[Peach's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Peach's Castle]]. The world can be accessed by completing the Castle level of Rock-Candy Mines or completing Soda Jungle's secret level. The boss here is [[Ludwig von Koopa]].

The world contains 10 levels, including six normal stages, one Ghost House, one Tower Level, one Castle level, and one Airship level.
