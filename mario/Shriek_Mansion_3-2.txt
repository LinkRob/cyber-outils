== Toads ==
* '''Toad 1''': Use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe to fly up and reach to the top and eand on the wooden platform.
* '''Toad 2''': Run one the long brown platform below the secret room, jump in and jump on the brown platform and go to the right to save the Toad.
* '''Toad 3''': Get in the middle purple pipe and use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe to make it easier to find the Toad.
