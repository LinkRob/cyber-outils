'''All Drill Power''' is a [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Badge]] from [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]].

{{Infobox_Badge|first game=Super Mario Bros. Wonder}}

== Abilities ==
This Badge makes it so any [[Power-Up]] you collect will turn you into your [[Drill Mario|Drill form]].
