'''Big Bob-omb on the the Summit''' is the first [[mission]] of [[Bob-omb Battlefield]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]],'' its [[Nintendo DS|NDS]] [[Reissue|remake]], ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and its 2021 enhanced port, [[Super Mario 3D All-Stars|''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'']].

== Objective ==
=== ''Super Mario 64'' ===
[[File:King Bob-omb encounter SM64.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario and Big Bob-omb, just before battling, in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'']]
To collect the first Star of the game, [[Mario]] needs to advance throughout the course and scale the mountain until reaching the top of the summit. There are several obstacles and enemies on his way, including [[Goomba]]s, a [[Chain Chomp]], water bombs, and [[Big Steely|Big Steelies]] which roll down the path. Here, he encounters King Bob-omb, and the two start battling one another. In order for Mario to defeat him, he has to go behind him and lift him up to slam him to the ground. King Bob-omb then makes a single bounce and gets back up. Mario must repeat this step two more times in order to fully defeat King Bob-omb to gain his first Power Star, allowing him to gain access to the top-right room with [[The Princess's Secret Slide]] and [[Whomp's Fortress]].

=== ''Super Mario 64 DS'' ===
[[File:King Bob-omb encounter SM64DS.png|thumb|left|200px|Yoshi and Big Bob-omb, just before battling, from ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'']]
The battle is changed as [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is the only character available at the beginning of the game. This makes King Bob-omb not having to be picked up in order to be defeated and rather more must be defeated by Yoshi licking the Bob-ombs he summons and throws it at him three times before they explode in Yoshi's mouth to gain the Power Star. He also bounces more than once after being hit, unlike ''Super Mario 64'' where he only bounces once and then gets up. When playing as Mario, [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]], they can throw Bob-ombs back at King Bob-omb three times in addition to throwing Bob-ombs at him.
