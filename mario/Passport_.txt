'''Passport+''' is a [[Gaddget]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be bought for a thousand [[Coin]]s at [[Professor E. Gadd]]'s Gaddget shop. With the Passport+, the player can hold up to 30 customizable passports aside from the one they created at the start.
