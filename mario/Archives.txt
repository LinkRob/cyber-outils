The '''Archives''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Description==
The Archives is located in the [[Treacherous Mansion]], on the second floor on the west side of the mansion. It can be accessed via the [[West Corridor]], and the room located between the [[Aviation Exhibit]] and the [[Space Exhibit]]. The Archives is a storage area, consisting of table and a ladder to the right, four cardboard boxes to the left, among other decorations.

== In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' ==
In E-3: [[A Train to Catch]], [[Luigi]] can optionally explore the Archives for extra [[treasure]]. If Luigi examines the boxes, a [[Greenie]] laugh is heard, then the boxes close and reopen. If Luigi correctly examines the boxes in the order that they reopened, a [[Gold Greenie]] emerges from them.

In the bonus mission, [[Terrifying Invasion]], the Archives is one of the rooms that may consist of several [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]].
