'''Clothing''' is equipment that plays different roles in the ''Mario'' series. In the ''[[Mario & Luigi series]]'', it is a mechanic that boosts [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s stats.

== Game appearances ==
{{sectstub}}
=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' ===
Clothing in '''Superstar Saga'' raises Mario and Luigi's defensive stats: [[HP]] and [[Defense]]. Most of the clothing can be bought at the clothes shop at the [[Beanbean Castle Town]].
