'''Geckits''' are lizard-like enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Geckits appear in [[Land's End]], where they are frequently encountered alongside other enemies, but they also appear in [[Belome Temple]], [[Bean Valley]], and [[The Trial Course]]. Geckits have a stronger variant named [[Gecko]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Geckits tend to be confused and often attack themselves and even other enemies when they attack. However, if they do attack normally, Geckits tend to use [[Sleep Sauce]] to put one of [[Mario]]'s party members to sleep.

===Strategy===
Geckits are very weak and often attack themselves and other enemies, so it is recommended to use physical attacks to save [[Flower Point]]s. However, they are weak to [[Ice (element)|ice]] moves, so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] will be effective against them.
