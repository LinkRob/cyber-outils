'''Bowser's Piano''' is an object that appears in [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros. film series]].

==History==
Bowser plays piano, at his [[Bowser's Castle (Illumination)|Castle]], and his [[Bowser's Miniature Castle|prison]] singing a [[Peaches|song]], about how he expresses his love of [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]], while he plans to marry her by force.
