'''World Star''' is the ninth world and the first Special World in the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. It can also be numbered '''World 9'''.

== Layout ==
World Star takes place above the [[Sprixie Kingdom]], and can only be accessed by completing [[World Bowser]], and taking the Rocket in [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]] which was built by the [[Sprixie]] Princesses. It contains nine standard courses and a [[Captain Toad]] course. [[Rosalina]] is unlocked as a playable character after completing [[Super Galaxy]]. After completing [[Cosmic Cannon Cluster]] and collecting the necessary amount of [[Green Power Star]]s, [[Stamp]]s, and [[Gold Flag]]s, the [[Rocket]] to [[World Mushroom (Super Mario 3D World)|World Mushroom]] will be available. In the background, the [[Sprixie Homeworld]] can be seen.
