'''Unhappy Trails''' is an 8-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
The goal is shown before panning over to the characters in the middle of the course, each of whom wave, then view pans over to the characters at the start, each of whom are given a key by a [[Goomba]].

==Gameplay==
Both characters on each of the four teams have to work together to get a treasure at the end of a crooked and narrow course. One character brings a [[key]] to their partner, who then runs to the [[treasure chest]] and unlocks it to get a jewel. After this, the character who opened the chest must return the jewel to their teammate, who then has to take it to the start of the path. If a character falls into the swamp, a [[Lakitu]] arrives to fish them out and return them back onto the course. The first team to take the jewel to the start wins.

==Ending==
Both characters on the winning team and the Goomba are on the first island with the diamond they collected, and the characters perform their respective victory animation.
