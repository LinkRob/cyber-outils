A '''Power Precision Card''' is a type of [[card]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
The Power Precision Card is an item exclusively used in the board [[Kamek's Carpet Ride]]. It is a more powerful variant of [[Precision Card]]s and therefore more rare. When a character uses a Power Precision Card, they are moved either one or two [[space]]s forward, depending on which number is on the Power Precision Card, but opponents are moved backward the same number of spaces. A Power Precision Card is usually obtained by pressing the key space on the outside of the board or by winning [[minigame]]s later on in either of the pathways.
