'''Dark Puffs''' are a variant of [[Ruff Puff]]s and an enemy featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
They are found throughout [[Boggly Woods]] and [[The Great Tree]]. These small lighting creatures attack in a variety of ways. They can charge themselves up with lightning. One of the fighting teams in the [[Glitz Pit]], the "Mind Bogglers", has one of these guys on it.
