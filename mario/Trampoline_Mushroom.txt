'''Trampoline Mushrooms''' are common features in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'s'' [[Mushroom Gorge]]. They also appear in two [[Mario Kart Wii Competitions|competitions]] (if you don't count the reverse [[Time Trial]]s on [[Mushroom Gorge]]): July 2009 Competition 2 and April 2010 Competition 1.

==Colors==

There are Red and Green Trampoline Mushrooms: the red ones are bouncy (you can do [[Aerial tricks]] on them, by the way) and the slightly bigger green ones are bouncy as well, but you cannot do Aerial tricks on these. They are on large stalks above a bottomless pit in Mushroom Gorge.
