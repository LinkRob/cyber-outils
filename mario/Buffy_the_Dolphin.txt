'''Buffy the Dolphin''' is an enemy from ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
Buffy the Dolphin is a muscular dolphin that is usually seen flexing his muscles and can only be found in [[Blowhole Castle]]. When he spots [[Wario]], he will flex and release yellow balls of energy that can damage Wario. However, Buffy the Dolphin can only attack Wario and take damage from him at the same time after a [[Spirit switch]] has been pressed. There are only five Buffy the Dolphins that can be found within the level.
