'''Bald Clefts''' are variant of [[Cleft]] featured in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
They first appear in one of the fortresses in front of the castle in [[Petal Meadows]]. Bald Clefts look like slabs of stone shaped sloppily in the shape of a skull. They have little red pinpoint eyes and crooked teeth. They have little blue rubber boots similar to [[Mario]]'s. They are gray and their only attack is a charged headbutt. Due to their defense of 1, the best way to take them out is with either Mario's hammer or [[POW Block]]s.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
Bald Clefs reappear in this game and are mainly found in [[Yold Desert]]. However, they aren't as strong as their counterparts from the previous game. Bald Clefts behave the same in this game disguising themselves as rocks in order to catch Mario off guard.
