'''Eggs''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The egg has the appearance of an ordinary egg with a white eggshell. A yellow duck hatches from an egg.

===Behavior===
A yellow duck hatches from an egg upon hitting the ground. Upon hatching, the duck moves slowly across the bottom of the screen. After a few seconds, the duck starts to flash orange, moves faster, and then explodes, damaging [[Wario]] if he is nearby.

Eggs have a more aggressive variant named [[black egg]], which only appear boss fight against [[Golden Diva]]. Black eggs are more aggressive than eggs and explode quicker than them.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
[[File:Cuckoo Condor battle.png|thumb|left|Cuckoo Condor dropping eggs, two of which already hatched into ducks. Wario is on the left side, holding an egg.]]
Eggs only appear in the battle against [[Cuckoo Condor]] in the [[Ruby Passage]], where they are produced by the Cuckoo Condor while it is in its condor form. Wario can counter its attack by catching a egg before it hits the ground and throw it as a projectile. If an egg has hatched into a duck and is not about to explode, Wario can defeat it by either [[Dash Attack|ramming]] into it or using a [[Ground Pound (move)|Smash Attack]] on it.
