'''Ice Power''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ===
Ice Power is like [[Fire Shield]] in that it gives [[Mario]] protection to [[Fire (element)|fire]] attacks. This badge increases Mario's attack on fire enemies by two points, and Ice Power also allows Mario to [[jump]] on fire-elemental enemies. Mario can wear this badge by using two [[Badge Point|BP]].

This badge is found in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]. Mario finds a door that opens when pressure is put on the opposite sides and pushing opposite directions, and when a [[Shy Guy]] pushes through the door, Mario can access the other side and find this badge. This badge is extremely useful for dealing with the [[Ember]]s and, later, the [[Lava Piranha]] that are found within [[Mt. Lavalava]]. A similar badge called the [[Fire Shield]] badge also appears in this game with the same functions as Ice Power, except that it only grants immunity to jumping on fire enemies rather than doing that and extra damage compared to the Ice Power badge.

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
Ice Power is found in [[Keelhaul Key]] in an area with a small [[coconut]] tree. In addition, Mario can wear this badge at a cost of only one BP rather than the two from ''Paper Mario''.
