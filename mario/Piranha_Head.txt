'''Piranha Heads''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
They are miniature versions of [[Petey Piranha]] that are formed from large blotches of [[Goop]], more commonly around [[Polluted Piranha Plant]]s.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
When [[Mario]] approaches their section or sprays at it with [[F.L.U.D.D]], the Piranha Head will emerge and attempt to bite Mario. They are only a minor annoyance as a Piranha Head can easily be defeated with enough sprays from F.L.U.D.D. Piranha Heads are rather common appearing in [[Bianco Hills]], [[Ricco Harbor]], [[Pianta Village]], and various areas of [[Delfino Plaza]] more specifically amidst Goop masses.

==Trivia==
*The Piranha Heads slightly resemble [[Piranha Bean]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.
