'''Goro Desert''' is a minor location in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Description==
Goro Desert is a desert-themed location based on [[Egypt]], and it features pyramids, dunes, and cat statues based on the [[Great Sphinx of Giza]].

==In ''WarioWare: Get It Together!''==
In the intro to [[Orbulon]]'s stage, [[Culture]], Goro Desert is one of the locations where Orbulon goes to steal artifacts. With his [[Oinker]], Orbulon hovers above a spot behind a sand dune and steals a stone beetle tablet, a vase, a cat statue, and a sarcophagus. After taking them all, Orbulon warps away, and the scene transitions to [[Takechiko City]]. 

==Trivia==
*The desert-themed area in the background art for the [[Mash League Arena]] in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' is possibly Goro Desert, given that the same type of cat statue appears in the background.
