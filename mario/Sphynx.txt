The '''Sphynx''' is a massive stone statue that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. As their name implies, they are semi-sentient sphinx statues that ask questions. Many of them appear across several kingdoms, usually blocking the way to a small area where [[Mario]] can obtain a [[Power Moon]].

== In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' ==
These statues will move once the player answers their riddles, which are often related to the kingdoms in which they are present. After the secret pathway has been opened, the Sphynx will ask additional questions. By answering all of the Sphynx's questions correctly, an achievement will be unlocked from [[Toadette]] in [[Mushroom Kingdom]], which grants Mario a Power Moon.

The Sphynx appears in the [[Sand Kingdom]], [[Wooded Kingdom]], [[Seaside Kingdom]], [[Moon Kingdom]], and the [[Darker Side]].
