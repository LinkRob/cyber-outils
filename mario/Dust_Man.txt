'''Dust Men''' are an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]]. They were eventually succeeded by a similar enemy named [[Bucket Man]] in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Dust Men are [[Bin]]s with a pair of eyes under their lid and round feet.

===Behavior===
At first, a Dust Man has an indistinct appearance from a regular Bin, but if [[Mario]] approaches a Dust Man, it rumbles, grows a pair of feet, and reveals its pair of eyes. A Dust Man wanders around aimlessly.

==In ''Donkey Kong'' (Game Boy)==
Dust Men first appear in [[Stage 1-3]]. If Mario touches a Dust Man, he gets defeated. Mario can stand on a Dust Man but cannot pick it up.
