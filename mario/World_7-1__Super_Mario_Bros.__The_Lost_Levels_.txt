'''World 7-1''' is the first [[level]] of [[World 7 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 7]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the twenty-fifth level overall in said game.

==Layout==
The player begins next to a grounded and hanging set of pipes with [[Piranha Plant]]s in them. Past them is a small flight of stairs, where [[wind]] will begin blowing as the player [[jump]]s up them, with [[Koopa Troopa]]s at the top. The player must then use the wind to jump cross the large [[pit]] and land in an area with four grounded and hanging pipes, with a power-up in a [[Brick Block]] above the third pipe. After the pipes are two pools of water that have [[Cheep Cheep]]s jumping out of them, with a long platform above with a [[Bill Blaster]] and a Brick Block next to a pipe containing a [[Super Star]].

After crossing the waters, the wind will stop blowing. If the player jumps off of the [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, they can reach a [[Warp Pipe]] leading to areas with [[Coin]]s and a row of various enemies. Otherwise, the player will have to fight two [[Hammer Bro]]s., which will begin charging at the player once they are on screen. In the Brick Block above the fourth [[? Block]] from the right is a power-up, and there is a [[Hidden Block]] with a [[1-Up Mushroom]] on the right end of the row of Brick Blocks. Across a pit is the Warp Pipe the player would exit out of the alternate path, followed by a set of stairs with another Hammer Brother that will charge at the player. After the Hammer Brother are narrow platforms, one of which having a Bill Blaster on it, before the [[Goal Pole]].
