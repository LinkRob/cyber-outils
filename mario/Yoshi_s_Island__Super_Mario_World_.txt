The '''Yoshi's Island''' is the first [[world]] of ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
Yoshi's Island is grassland-themed world located at the southwest portion of [[Dinosaur Land]]. It is where [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] meet up with the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] for the first time. [[Yoshi's House]] is located where [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] begin their journey, with the [[Yellow Switch Palace]] at the summit of [[Kappa Mountain]] to the northwest, and [[1 Iggy's Castle|#1 Iggy's Castle]] to the northeast, where Iggy Koopa is fought. After the castle is cleared, a bridge opens to the [[Donut Plains (world)|Donut Plains]].
