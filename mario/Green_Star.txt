'''Green Stars''' are green-colored [[Star]]s that appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' as well as ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. They function similarly to [[Star Medal]]s from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' and [[Star Coin]]s from the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' series however there are some levels (such as the [[Captain Toad]] levels, [[Enemy Blockade]]s and the [[Mystery House]]s) which contain more or less Green Stars. The Green Stars can be found in various locations of each level and using the heroes' abilities is necessary to obtaining all of them.

==History==
===''Super Mario 3D World / Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''===
[[Image:Green Star Collect.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] about to collect a Green Star in a [[Clear Pipe]].]]
Green Stars are first seen in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and later go onto appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. In these games, Green Stars are found scattered around the courses and utilizing each of the heroes' abilities is necessary for obtaining them, sometimes the Green Stars can be hidden in secret areas and the heroes' need to look high and low for them to find them. Collecting a Green Star will also award 4000 points. Green Stars are the replacement for [[Star Medals]], and by extension, [[Star Coin]]s in these games. Collecting the Green Stars is one of the necessary requirements to beating the game 100%, there are 380 Green Stars in all for this installment. How many Green Stars can be found in a course depend on the stage in question:
*Normal & Castle Courses have 3 Green Stars.
*Blockades & Boss Blockades have 1 Green Star.
*[[Captain Toad Courses]] ([[Captain Toad's Fiery Finale|nearly]] exclusive to odd numbered Worlds) have 5 Green Stars.
*[[Mystery House]]s (exclusive to even numbered Worlds) vary, swinging between 5 or 10 Stars, except for [[Mystery House Marathon|the last one in World Crown]], which has 30 Green Stars.

===Locked Levels===
Some levels in the game are locked upon reaching them and require a certain number of Green Stars to unlock before being able to play the level, below is a list of the locked levels and how many Green Stars are required to unlock them.
*[[Bowser's Highway Showdown]] - 10 Green Stars*
*[[Puffprod Peaks]] - 15 Green Stars
*[[Chain-Link Charge]] - 30 Green Stars
*[[Lava Rock Lair]] - 50 Green Stars*
*[[Backstreet Bustle]] - 80 Green Stars
*[[Hands-On Hall]] - 100 Green Stars
*[[Bowser's Lava Lake Keep]] - 130 Green Stars
*[[The Great Tower of Bowser Land]] - 170 Green Stars
*[[Cosmic Cannon Cluster]] - 210 Green Stars
*[[Broken Blue Bully Belt]] - 240 Green Stars
*[[World Crown]] - 342 Green Stars
<nowiki>*</nowiki> means the level can be skipped via the [[Warp Zone]]s found in [[Koopa Troopa Cave]] and [[Piranha Creeper Creek]] respectively.

===''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''===
[[Image:Soaring Green Star.png|thumb|left|A Green Star soaring through the skies.]]
[[Image:Captain Toad Green Star Get.png|thumb|right|Captain Toad gets a Green Star.]]
Green Stars appear again in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' (the [[Wii U]] version only), in this game, one Green Star is seen soaring through the skies as [[Captain Toad]] and [[Toadette]] are riding in their mine cart during the ending, upon sight of the emerald twinkle, [[Captain Toad]] ditches [[Toadette]] in the mine cart and chases after the Green Star comet which proceeds to fall in a [[Clear Pipe]]. [[Captain Toad]] then proceeds to chase the Green Star into the Clear Pipe (after [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Peach]], and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] enter it chasing [[Bowser]] who has kidnapped the [[Sprixie Princess]]) entering the [[Sprixie Kingdom]]. In the bonus levels of the game, a Green Star marks the level's goal instead of a regular [[Power Star]].

==Trivia==
*Green Stars strongly resemble the [[Green Power Star]]s from the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games however those Green Stars are used to power-up the [[Comet Observatory]] and [[Starship Mario]] while these Green Stars function similarly to [[Star Coin]]s and [[Star Medal]]s.
