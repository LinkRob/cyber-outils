When an enemy is airborne, it cannot be hit by ground-based weaponry barring counterattacks. This is a '''flying''' enemy. Enemies of this type can be hit by jump attacks and certain [[Special Attack|Special Attacks]].

== Appearances ==

=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' ===
Flying foes can still be hit with the regular [[Chopper Bros.]] (and the [[Advance Command|advanced]] version in the original version), the [[Knockback Bros.]], and the [[Cyclone Bros.]] [[Bros. Attack|Bros. Attacks]]. despite those attacks using hammers.

* [[Fawful]] is considered a flying enemy during the first phase of his battle, but this can only be seen via hacking, since the Bros. have not gotten their [[Hammer|Hammers]] yet.
* [[Parabeanie|Parabeanies]] can dodge hammer attacks until their wings fall off.
* [[Goomdiver|Goomdivers]] can dodge hammer attacks after their first floating attack.
* [[Blooper]]
* [[Lakipea]]
* [[Paratroopea|Paratroopeas]] and their [[Scaratroopea|elite variant]] can dodge hammer attacks until their wings fall off.
* [[Jojora]]
* [[Boo]]

=== [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time|''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'']] ===

* [[Boo]]
* [[Boo Guy]]
* [[Lakitufo]]
* [[Pidgit]]
* [[Kamek]] rides on his broom for much of his fight, but will drop to the ground when he takes enough damage.
* [[Dark Boo]]
* [[Snifaro|Snifaros]] hover above the ground in their coffins, but become grounded if the player uses a [[Fire Flower]] to destroy the coffin.
* [[Love Bubble]]
* [[Piranha Planet]]
* [[Soul Bubble]]
* [[Ghoul Guy]]
* The younger [[Princess Shroob]] rides in her chair, which hovers above the ground, if the Bros. didn't disable it. If they did, she is considered a grounded enemy due to stepping off it while the chair undergoes repairs.
* The [[Elder Princess Shroob]] has seven targets, which count as flying enemies due to them being suspended off the ground except for her feet.

=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' ===

* [[Bubble Blooper|Bubble Bloopers]] float in the water when [[Bowser]] floods the [[Pump Works]] area of his body; therefore, they are considered airborne enemies. When the water drains, they become [[Dried Blooper|Dried Bloopers]], which are grounded.
* [[Leaf Guy]]
* [[Lakitu King]] ([[Glam Lakitu]])
* [[Fawfulcopter]]
* The [[Kretin]] boss's [[Alpha Kretin|Alpha]] form has six targets, five of which are considered to be airborne due to them not touching the ground, while the sixth one, the bottommost, is considered a grounded enemy.
* [[Napse]] ([[Airnapse]])
* [[Mechawful|Mechawfuls]] ditch their body and continue to fight as a floating head after the Bros. deal enough damage to the body. The head is treated as a flying enemy. Dark Mechawfuls work the same.
* [[Air Cheep]]
* The [[Dark Star]] hovers and can dodge ground-based weaponry until it gets hit by two of its own [[Dark Satellmite]] minions, causing it to drop to the ground and become a grounded target for one turn. Later, as the Dark Star Core, it is always treated as a flying enemy.
* [[Fawful]] is considered a flying enemy with his [[Vacuum Helmet]] attached to his head, but becomes a grounded enemy when they separate. When fighting alongside the Dark Star Core, his eyes are treated as airborne targets.

=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' ===

* [[Smoldergeist]] is considered a flying enemy when on fire, but will drop to the ground to become a grounded target if he cannot score a hit.
* [[Flibbee|Flibbees]] are considered flying enemies when they take flight, but can drop to the ground to become a [[Spiny (Enemy type)|spiny]] enemy if hit by a jump attack. Flibbee R's have the same gimmick.
* [[Propellion]]
* [[Antasma]] is treated as a flying target when supporting [[Bowser]] in [[Dream's Deep]].
* [[Skipdrill]] (Skipdrill R)
* The [[Robo-Drilldigger]] can assume a flying form that can dodge ground-based weaponry when it takes enough damage. In this form, it can shoot drills and ram the player.
* [[Ticksquawk]]
* [[Grumbell]]
* [[Pi'illodactyl]] (Pi'illodactyl R)
* [[Lakitu]] (Lakitu R)
* [[Fly Guy]] (Fly Guy R, [[Fly Guy R Thieves]])
* [[Corporal Paraplonk]]
* [[Dark Block]]
* [[Eyepi'illo]]
* The [[Pi'illodium]] is considered a flying target with at least one wing active, but if the Bros. break both his wings, it drops to the ground and becomes a grounded enemy.
* [[Pelicrump]]
* [[Kamek]] can ride on his broom, becoming a flying target until he falls off. His clones in his third battle ride their brooms all the time.
* [[Antasmunchie|Antasmunchies]] are considered flying targets when [[Antasma]] is in his active phase, but become grounded spiny enemies when he starts resting.
* [[Bowser Jr.]] is treated as a flying target in his [[Junior Clown Car]], but will become grounded when he ditches the car.
