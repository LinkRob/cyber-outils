'''Suus''' are spider-like enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''.

== Description ==
In their artwork, Suus are portrayed as yellow with black striped bodies and brown faces and legs.

==History==
Suus only appear in [[World 3-2 (Super Mario Land)|World 3-2]] of the [[Easton Kingdom]], and are commonly found in higher areas in the level. When a Suu spots [[Mario]], it attempts to drop onto him. Mario can defeat a Suu by waiting for it to drop and then either [[jump]] on the Suu or throw [[Superball]]s at it until it is defeated. Defeating a Suu earns the player 400 [[point]]s.
