'''Bean Fever''' is a disease that affects foreigners and is commonly afflicted in the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. This is seen in the games, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
The sickness can cause the person stricken with the disease to become a bean if the disease is not cured in three days. [[Mario]] caught Bean Fever when he ate an [[Invincishroom]] (which was actually a [[Poison Mushroom]] in the remake) and was put under intensive care. [[Luigi]] was sent to obtain [[Crabbie Grass]], which is the only known cure of Bean Fever, and only found in the [[Guffawha Ruins]].

While infected, Mario has a green face, akin to regular beans.

== Trivia ==
* Bean Fever is one of few aliments in the series that cannot be inflicted during battle. The other being the [[Blorbs]].
** Coincidentally, both of these ailments were caused by a type of mushroom and are only seen in the ''[[Mario & Luigi series]]''.
