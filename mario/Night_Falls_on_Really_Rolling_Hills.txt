'''Night Falls on Really Rolling Hills''' is the first level of [[World Mushroom (Super Mario 3D World)|World Mushroom]] in the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== Layout ==
The level is a remake of [[Really Rolling Hills]] from World 2, but a few changes can be found.
*A good chunk of normal [[Skipsqueak]]s are now [[Spiny Skipsqueak]]s.
*It is night.
*The rolling hills move significantly faster.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': At a green star ring before the first pipe.
*'''Green Star 2''': Above the green star 3 pipe, there is a cloud that leads to the star.
*'''Green Star 3''':In a mild detour pipe, light up the rainbow tiles for the star.
