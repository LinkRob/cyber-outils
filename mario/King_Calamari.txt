{{Nihongo|'''King Calamari'''|タコつぼゲッソー|Takotsubo Gessō}} is an enormous [[Blooper]] who is fought as a boss in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Biography==
According to various logs within the Sunken Ship, King Calamari is the one responsible for sinking it.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
King Calamari is fought in the [[Sunken Ship]] after Mario unlocks the cellar with the password (which is "pearls") that he got after solving all the puzzles. First, Mario and co. fight three of King Calamari's tentacles, followed by another three, then the walk to him. King Calamari is fought along with a group of three tentacles. After Mario and co. defeat him, they will gain access to the cellar, where [[Jonathan Jones]] and the fifth [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]] are found.

===Battle===
In battle, King Calamari has the habit of tossing away one of the party members for a number of turns making it a 2-on-1 battle. He can also use special moves like [[Ink Blast]] and the deadly [[Sand Storm]] to weaken the party. However, King Calamari is weak to fire, so, Mario's flame moves like [[Super Flame]] is helpful in this battle.
