'''Femme Brûlée''' is one of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object is to move the umbrella over the sunbather to protect her from the sun, which can be done by tilting the [[Wii Remote]] to move the umbrella over her. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame. However, if the sunbather is exposed to the sun, she will burn and fly off screen, causing the player to lose at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The sunbather only moves a bit.
*'''Level 2:''' The sunbather rolls around.
*'''Level 3:''' The sunbather pops up from the ground in different places, with the upper part of her body exposed.

An additional difference is that in the first and third difficulty levels, the sunbather wears blue clothing, but in the second difficulty level, she wears red clothing.

==Trivia==
*''Femme brûlée'' means "burned woman" in French, and is also a play on the name of the dessert {{w|crème brûlée}}.
