'''Hex Areas''' are walk-by locations which appear in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Description==
Hex Areas are comparable to the [[Orb Space]] from ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' as well as [[Candy Area]]s from ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' in that they are not a [[Space]] to land on but rather to pick up an item at. Hex Areas are located between two Spaces at a specific point of each game board, and are indicated by a floating [[Hex]], which a character automatically receives upon walking to it. After this occurs, a different Hex appears in its place, awaiting someone to pick it up.
