'''World 7-4''' is the fourth level of [[World 7 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. The player is given a time limit of 400 seconds.

== Layout ==
Similar to [[World 4-4 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 4-4]], this castle features a maze of paths. The player must once again choose the correct path to make it through in time. The only enemies the player encounters here are two [[Podoboo]]s, and just one [[Fire-Bar]]. The [[fake Bowser]] at the end breathes fire towards the player as he approaches. When encountered, the Bowser also hurls hammers at the player, just like the previous boss.

== Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' ==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 16000.

=== Red Coin locations ===
* Red Coin #1 is located underneath the second falling platform.
* Red Coin #2 is located above the entry way into the first puzzle area.
* Red Coin #3 is located on the bottom path of the second puzzle area, next to the Fire-Bar.
* Red Coin #4 is located on the third bottom path of the second puzzle area.
* Red Coin #5 is located next to the fake Bowser, above where the Podoboo appears.

=== Yoshi Egg location ===
* The Yoshi Egg is found inside a [[Hidden Block]] placed right of the first large stone platform.
