'''Komori''' are bat enemies in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''. They spawn from small roost and fly horizontally across the screen. If they collide with [[Mario]] they hang onto him and throttle him, causing him to fall the rope he is climbing on. They must be avoided.
