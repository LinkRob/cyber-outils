'''Megasmilax''' is a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
Megasmilax is a giant [[Piranha Plant]] who also has smaller heads called [[Smilax]]s. In the game, Megasmilax is shown with two Smilaxes, but is shown with three in its artwork.
{{br|left}}

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Megasmilax is encountered in [[Bean Valley]], where it appears in a [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] leading to [[Nimbus Land]] to block access to there. Right as Mario and his team defeat several Piranha Plants, a [[Shy Away]] comes to pour recycled water on another Piranha Plant which grows three heads, forming Megasmilax.

After defeating Megasmilax in a boss fight, Mario and his team heads further into the clouds of Bean Valley and into Nimbus Land.

===Battle===
In battle, Megasmilax itself attacks mainly by biting Mario and his team and can also use the troublesome [[Petal Blast]] move. The smaller Smilaxs can also attack mainly by biting.

===Strategy===
Megasmilax and the Smilaxs are highly vulnerable to Ice, so it is recommended to have [[Mallow]] in his fight and use his [[Snowy]] move. Mallow should also be equipped with a [[Trueform Pin]] to avoid being turned into a mushroom while Mario attacks normally and [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] heals.

==Crossover appearances==
In ''Minecraft''{{'}}s [[Super Mario Mash-up]] pack, the Wither is replaced with Megasmilax with two Smilaxes.

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Megasmilax is mentioned by [[Viridi]] during the [[Palutena's Guidance]] dialogue for [[Piranha Plant]]. Megasmilax is mentioned in plural form, suggesting that it is a species rather than an individual.
