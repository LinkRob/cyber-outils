'''Banana Swing''' is the [[Star Swing]] of [[Diddy Kong]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces his [[Banana Ball]] for batting in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', as he now instead only uses the move while pitching.

==Description==
When used, Diddy stretches his body in the shape of a [[banana]], similarly to when using [[Acrobatic Banana]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''. After doing this a few times, Diddy hits the ball, which then flies in the direction he hit it in, but the ball does a very sharp curve, making it hard for a fielder to catch.
