The '''Cheermellow''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is the signature kart of [[Mario (Santa)]] and [[Yoshi (Reindeer)]].

== Description ==
The Cheermellow is a winter-themed variation of the [[Mushmellow]]. The mushroom appears to have snow on the top half. The whole kart has a [[Wikipedia:clobberstone|clobberstone]]-esque design. The tires are deep red with white spots, greatly resembling the [[Mushroom (tires)|Mushroom]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', as well as the tires used on the [[Pink Mushmellow]], though they are colored pinkish-magenta. The steering wheel and seat are green, and the exhuast pipes are gold.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
[[File:Cheermellow.jpeg|thumb|200px|right]]
The Cheermellow is one of the featured spotlights for the 2019 [[Winter Tour]]. When used in races, it increases the duration of Rocket Boosts and the given points for performing one.
