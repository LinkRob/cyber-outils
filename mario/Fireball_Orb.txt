The '''Fireball Orb''' is an [[orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. It can be only used by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. Whoever uses this orb is surrounded by [[fireball]]s. As they go around the board, they throw fireballs at any opponents in their way, getting 10 [[Coin]]s from each person they hit. The effect of this orb lasts for three turns. 

==Nomenclature==
*'''German:''' Feuerball-Kapsel, meaning "Fireball Capsule"
*'''Japanese:''' ファイアカプセル (''Faia kapuseru''), meaning "Fire Capsule"
