The '''Wario Key''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
The Wario Key is a gold [[key]] with an uppercase "W" in its ring. [[Wario]]'s [[Wario Cap|cap]] can be seen on top of the key.

==In ''Super Mario 64 DS''==
[[Luigi]] can obtain the Wario Key by defeating [[Chief Chilly]] in [[Chief Chilly Challenge]]. With the Wario Key, the door that Wario is locked behind can be unlocked, freeing Wario and unlocking him as a playable character.
