'''Episode 1: Road to the Big Windmill''' is the first episode of [[Bianco Hills]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', let alone the first within the game itself.

==Overview==
[[Shadow Mario]] has escaped into Bianco Hills and has a [[Polluted Piranha Plant]] an area near the windmill. [[Mario]] must head for that area and stop them. Once Mario has made it to the Polluted Piranha, he can spray into its mouth using [[F.L.U.D.D.]] to damage it. Hitting it three times will cause it to disintegrate clearing up all of the pollution in the area and causing the ramp to the windmill to reappear. Also, a [[Shine Sprite]] will appear where Mario can claim as his reward.
