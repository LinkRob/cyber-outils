'''Toadley Clinic''' is a location that appears in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Super Mario'' series]]. It is a hospital clinic located in [[Toad Town]] and is owned by [[Dr. Toadley]]. During the events, several [[Toad (species)|Toads]] afflicted by the [[Blorbs]] disease are taken here as patients.
