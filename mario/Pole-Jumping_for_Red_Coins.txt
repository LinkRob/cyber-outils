'''Pole Jumping For Red Coins''' is the third mission featured in [[Dire, Dire Docks]] in both ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[Nintendo DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. The objective is to collect the eight [[Red Coin]]s found within the submarine dock area.
