'''Munchelangelos''' are enemies in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are mice that attack by jump-charging at [[Wario]] and trying to bite him. Its name is a pun of the real-world artist {{w|Michelangelo}}.

== Profile ==
A Munchelangelo is a big humanoid mouse found in [[S.S. Caviar]]. Here, a Munchelangelo wanders around harmlessly but if Wario stays still for too long, Munchelangelo charges at Wario to bite him. Munchelangelo occasionally lifts his head, peeking out two small eyes, suggesting he is really a costume.
