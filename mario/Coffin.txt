The '''Coffins''' are obstacles appearing only in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its DS version ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. They are Haunted Coffins that appear to be possessed as their lids periodically lift up before slamming back down. 

==In ''Super Mario 64/Super Mario 64 DS''==
[[Image:Coffin DS.png|thumb|left|[[Luigi]] near the Coffins.]]
Coffins are first seen in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. In these games, they are located in the fourth room of the second floor in [[Big Boo's Haunt]]. They rise when [[Mario]] approaches them and after some seconds they close. If [[Mario]] is caught in a coffin, he will take heavy damage so be careful, Coffins usually shake to indicate when they're going to slam back shut. Two of the [[Red Coin]]s are hidden underneath two of the Coffins, in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', this was changed to one as the other Red Coin stashed beneath the Coffin was moved to be above the staircase. 

==In ''[[Mario Party 2]]''==
[[Image:Coffin Congestion Game.jpg|thumb|right|[[Mario]] playing the minigame.]]
Similar coffins appear in the mini-game [[Coffin Congestion]]. The minigame features six Coffins, five of which contain a useful items while one of them possesses [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]]. The player will have to watch the Coffins as they get shuffled around and pick the right one. If a Coffin with an item is selected, the player will win that item however the game is lost if the coffin with Baby Bowser is picked so be careful.
