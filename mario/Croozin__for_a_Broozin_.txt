'''Croozin' for a Broozin''''<ref name="SMPGameplay2">Nintendo. (June 15, 2018) [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EVaIpD8q79Y Super Mario Party Gameplay Pt. 2 - Nintendo Treehouse: Live | E3 2018], ''YouTube''. Retrieved December 18, 2018</ref> is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. "Broozin'" is a pun of bruising and [[Broozer]].

== Opening ==
Players are positioned facing a wall, they are looking around while the [[Broozer]]s are encountering them. Players are shocked when the Broozers break the wall and they run away.

== Gameplay ==
Players can run while the side-scrolling screen is panning right. There are obstacles such as walls and [[Warp Pipe|pipes]] where Broozers come out. Players lose the minigame if they are touched by a Broozer. The last player standing is the winner.

== Ending ==
The winner does his/her victory pose while Broozers are chasing after other players.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move

==In-game description==
*''"Run from the oncoming Broozers!"''
*''"Be careful of the pipes – Broozers might be hiding inside!"''
