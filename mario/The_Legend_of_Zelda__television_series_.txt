'''''The Legend of Zelda''''' is an animated television series produced by [[DiC Entertainment]]. It was part of the same syndication package as ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', even though it is based on the first two ''The Legend of Zelda'' games, ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'' and ''[[w:c:zelda:Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]]''.

Several live-action segments of ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' would feature a preview of a ''The Legend of Zelda'' episode, most of which were even syndicated with a live-action ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' segment, just as with the animated ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' episodes.
