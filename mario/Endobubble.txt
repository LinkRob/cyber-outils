'''Endobubble''' is a special move used by certain enemies and bosses in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Endobubble is used, a large black bubble travels slowly at its target. Endobubble can cause the [[Fear]] ailment, cutting the target's [[Attack]] and [[Defense]] rating in half.
