'''Elden''' is a tennis player in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]. He is partnered with [[Spike (Mario Tennis)|Spike]] in doubles. Spike, who is an Speed type, can be unlocked by winning against him in Mario Tour in Singles or Doubles in [[Island Open]].
