The '''Sandy Grand Hall''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Description==
The Sandy Grand Hall is found in the [[Tomb Suites]] on the tenth floor of [[The Last Resort]]. The Sandy Grand Hall is mostly empty and does not have much contents. It is north of the [[Elevator Hall (Tomb Suites)|Elevator Hall]] and south of the [[Altar Chamber]], which is contained in the pyramid in the center of Sandy Grand Hall. The pyramid has two statues next to it, and the doors to the Altar Chamber can be opened by examining the left statue. The southeast area of the Sandy Grand Hall has a stone snake with two missing segments that can be revealed using the [[Dark-Light Device]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Two of the Tombs Suites's [[gem]]s are located in the Sandy Grand Hall. The purple gem is hidden beneath a pyramid-shaped sand dune left of the pyramid. The yellow gem is located within the snake statue's mouth, which spits it out once Luigi reveals its two missing segments. There is also a magic lamp containing [[treasure]], including several [[coin]]s, [[bill]]s, [[Gold Bar]]s, and a [[Golden Goob]].

If [[Luigi]] enters the cutout island, several [[Mini Goob]]s and a [[Hammer (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Hammer]] appear to attack him, and a [[treasure chest]] is also revealed.

Once Luigi escapes from the bottom of the pyramid, he encounters [[Serpci]] again. After defeating her, Luigi obtains the [[elevator button]] to [[Twisted Suites]].

If Luigi returns to the Sandy Grand Hall later on, there is the possibility of him encountering several [[Goob]]s with brooms.
