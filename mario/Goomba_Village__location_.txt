'''Goomba Village''' is a village inhabited by the [[Goomba Family|Goombas]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Goomba Village is the first major location [[Mario]] visits in this game after being knocked out of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] by [[Bowser]] after he lifted it into the sky. It is only populated by a family of Goombas and a [[Toad]] who runs the [[Toad House]]. Mario can get [[Goomnut]]s here by hitting the tree with a [[Hammer]]. This is also the location where Mario gets his hammer. The village (specifically the underside of it) acts as the tutorial for the game, as Mario learns about battles, items, badges, and various other information.

Mario can also take a blue [[Warp Pipe]] that is located in [[Toad Town Tunnels]] to reach Goomba Village faster and easier.
