'''Target Switches''' are a type of switch in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
Target Switches can be activated with the Kong's respective shooters after buying them from [[Funky's Store]]. They are very common throughout the game.

==Types==
*'''Coconut Switch:''' Must be pressed with [[Donkey Kong]]'s [[Coconut Shooter]].
*'''Peanut Switch:''' Must be pressed with [[Diddy Kong]]'s [[Popgun#Peanut Popguns|Peanut Popguns]].
*'''Grape Switch:''' Must be pressed with [[Lanky Kong]]'s [[Grape Shooter]].
*'''Feather Switch:''' Must be pressed with [[Tiny Kong]]'s [[Feather Bow]].
*'''Pineapple Switch:''' Must be pressed with [[Chunky Kong]]'s [[Pineapple Launcher]].
