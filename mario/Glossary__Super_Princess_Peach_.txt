The '''Glossary''' is a feature in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It keeps track of any enemy that [[Princess Peach]] has defeated and provides a description for them. For invincible enemies, Princess Peach only has to visit a [[level]] with them for the enemy to appear in the Glossary. Several [[vibe]]-infused enemies have their names shortened within the Glossary.
