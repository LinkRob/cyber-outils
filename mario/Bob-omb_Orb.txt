A '''Bob-omb Orb''' is an [[Orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. It is the successor to the [[Bob-omb Capsule]] from ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==History==
===''Mario Party 6''===
In ''Mario Party 6'', players can place a Bob-omb Orb on a Red or Blue Space as a [[Character Space|Roadblock Character Space]]. Opponents that pass a space containing the Orb cause a Bob-omb to explode on them, cutting the remaining number of spaces they can go in half rounded down; if the player is under the effect of a [[Metal Mushroom Orb]], they simply kick away the Bob-omb. If players have a low amount of spaces left, the Bob-omb can cause them to stop their movement, behaving similarly passing a space with a [[Thwomp Orb]] placed.

===''Mario Party 7''===
In ''Mario Party 7'', Bob-omb Orbs can be found only on [[Pagoda Peak]]. Opponents that pass a Roadblock Character Space containing a Bob-omb Orb make a Bob-omb blow up on them, sending them down to a lower part of the board. Like ''Mario Party 6'', players kick away the Bob-omb when under the effect of a Metal Mushroom Orb.
