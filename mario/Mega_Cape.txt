'''Mega Cape''' is a basic armor used by [[Geno]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from [[Moleville]] for 22 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Mega Cape increases Geno's [[Defense]] by 6 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 3.
