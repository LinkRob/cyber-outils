|name=Spike Helmet (Battle Card)}}'''Spike Helmet''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Spike Helmet is a defensive card that gives [[Mario]] an extra line of defense when it's the opponents turn. It is a green helmet with a white trim and sharp white spike on top, accompanied with a green/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 80 coins (painted)

Sell Price: 40 coins (painted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Spike Helmet is first seen in [[Indigo Underground]], but can also found in [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fort Cobalt]], and [[Toad Trainworks]].
