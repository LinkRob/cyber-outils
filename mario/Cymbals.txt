The '''Cymbals''' are one of [[Mallow]]'s weapons in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They can be bought from [[Moleville]] for 42 [[coin]]s, and equipping the Cymbals increases Mallow's [[Attack]] power by 30. Upon using the Cymbals, Mallow holds a cymbal in each hand and slams it on the enemies.
