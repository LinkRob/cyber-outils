The '''Extra Bad Luck Space''' is a variation of the [[Bad Luck Space]] featured in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. This space is worse than Bad Luck Spaces, and same thing will happen when you land on this as on the Bad Luck Space, only now the penalty is more extreme.
