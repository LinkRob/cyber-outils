The '''Zokket Army''' is an antagonistic faction that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. Hence the faction's name, [[Zokket]] is the leader of this group.

==Members==
*[[Zokket]]: This cloaked figure has made the downfall of [[Concordia]] his mission, with his plan involving spreading a toxic substance/energy called "[[Glohm]]." Zokket's outfit incorporates a top hat with red and green cables in it, a mask resembling a skull, and a dark red cloak with a wire cutter-esque object fastening it.
*Higher-ups:
**The [[Extension Corps]]: These beings are the three primary higher-ups in Zokket's forces. 
*** Ecks: This being is a small, pink, legless creature with arms resembling coaxial cables.
***Ten: This purple, lanky being takes his design from a headphone jack merged with a stereotypical Japanese delinquent.
*** Shun: This being is a stout, green creature with a top-heavy build resembling a VGA connector.
**The [[Gobblick]]: This monster resembles a pitcher plant, serving as the guardian of the Lushgreen Sea's Great Lighthouse. He uses his tentacles to strangle [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]], but the latter can use Luigi Logic to throw the Gobblick into fans north of the battlefield to stun him if the fans are active.
**The [[Sharpcask]]: A monster resembling a barrel. He is encountered in the Color-Full Sea's Great Lighthouse. The Sharpcask uses barrels as his main weapon and can retract into his shell, giving his defense a huge boost. Mario and Luigi can stun him by stacking crates on him.
**The [[Pipegunk]]: A purple slimy slug-like creature that can be powered up through fire and ice. It haunts the Brrning Sea's Great Lighthouse.
*Minions:
**[[Mettozokettohei]]tachi: These enemies, resembling USB cables, fill the role of the army's footsoldiers. Their normal variant can wear helmets for protection, but some are armed with jack plug-like swords or fitted with jetpacks.
** [[Snaptor]]s: These avians have beaks resembling crocodile clips. They are a common enemy to face on Merrygo Island. Their neck can stretch for a peck attack. The Extension Corps sometimes uses them for transport.
** [[Krode]]s: Enemies resembling a cross between [[Treevil]]s and corroded electrical conduits. These monsters can throw pipes and metal balls at their enemies.

== Backstory ==
Zokket created the [[Mettozokettohei|Mettozokettoheitach]](Zok Troops in English dubs) out of junk that was forged into [[Zokket Alloy]]. As stated in a log in [[Gulchrock Sea Lighthouse]], they were given speech purely out of convenience, and were predicted to rebel when Reclusa was released, which was treated as no issue. At some point, Zokket then found the Extension Corps, who joined him for unspecified reasons.
