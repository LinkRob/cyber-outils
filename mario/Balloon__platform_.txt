'''Balloons''' are a type of platform that characters can ride on. These types of balloons first appeared in ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'', but different ones with a smiling face on the front were created for the ''[[Yoshi (series)|Yoshi]]'' platformers, in which they debuted in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', and went on to also appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
Balloons appear in red, green, yellow, and purple, although this does not indicate any difference in their behavior. They are featured in the level 4-3: [[Don't Look Back!]].
