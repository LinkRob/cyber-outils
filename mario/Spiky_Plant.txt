'''Thorny Flowers''' are enemies that first appeared in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

When [[Mario]] touches them, he is injured. The Thorny Flower are invulnerable to most attacks, however, if Mario is in his [[Rainbow Mario|Rainbow]] form, Spiky Plants, like most enemies, are defeated instantaneously. Also, in [[Melty Monster Galaxy]] , it is shown that Spiky Plants can be elimated by Rock Mario when he is rolling around in his boulder form. However, the rock mushroom is only available in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', meaning that this is not an option in its prequel.
