'''Spiny Skipsqueaks''' ​are enemies which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Spiny Skipsqueaks are a variant of [[Skipsqueak]]s with purple fur, yellow spikes on their back, and green feet.

===Behavior===
Spiny Skipsqueaks jog in place on rolling platforms and jump, just like regular Skipsqueaks. In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', they can [[Triple Jump]].

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
=== ''​Super Mario 3D World'' ===
Spiny Skipsqueaks are first encountered inside the cave of [[Really Rolling Hills]]. Because they have spikes, Spiny Skipsqueaks cannot be defeated from a [[jump]], although [[Rosalina]]'s [[Star Spin]] or the effects of either a [[Super Bell]], [[Fire Flower]], or [[Tanooki Suit]] can defeat it. Defeating a Spiny Skipsqueak earns the player 200 points and one [[coin]].

=== ''​[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' ===
Spiny Skipsqueaks are enemies exclusive to the ''Super Mario 3D World'' style. They jump whenever [[Mario]] does, like with Skipsqueaks. If an object such as a [[Thwomp]] or [[Track Block]] removes a Spiny Skipsqueak from its starting point, it attempts to return. If a Spiny Skipsqueak cannot pass an obstacle, even from a Triple Jump, it gives up and resets its movement pattern at its current location.
