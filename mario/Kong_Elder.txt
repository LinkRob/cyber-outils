The '''Kong Elder''' is a character that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. He is one of the five Elders that can bestow the ancient power of [[Bananza]] upon [[Donkey Kong]] and he rules over the [[Lagoon Layer]].

==Appearance==
The Kong Elder is a very large and elderly Kong that has black and grey fur and yellow eyes. He also wears large, old bananas as a headpiece and has a bundle of them under his chin acting as a beard. He also wears dark red rings on his fingers and has bracelets in the shape of bananas.

==History==
The Kong Elder is first seen after Donkey Kong and [[Pauline]] (who was then still in her Odd Rock form at the time) visit him upon their journey to the Lagoon Layer. The Kong Elder mentions a [[VoidCo|group of evil monkeys]] have recently caused trouble and are making their way down to the [[Planet Core]]. The Kong Elder then promises to bestow an ancient power known as Bananza that will allow Donkey Kong and Pauline to take out the evil forces by having them retrieve his [[Ancient Record]]. Afther they do so, Donkey Kong and Pauline preform a duet where Donkey Kong finally acquires his first Bananza form, [[Kong Bananza]]. After training Donkey Kong and Pauline in how to work together to make full use of their powers, the Kong Elder informs the two of them that they must meet with the other Elders in the lower layers to learn their Bananza forms and reach the Planet Core.

The Kong Elder is later seen again where [[Void Kong]] was causing a rampage on the Lagoon Layer and the [[Hilltop Layer]] where he is trying to gather enough more bananas in order to restart his [[Pay-Dirt Piledriver|machine]] to make it to the Planet Core. The Kong Elder mentions that Void Kong had stolen the rest of his bananas, and urges Donkey Kong and Pauline to chase after him before he reaches the Planet Core.
