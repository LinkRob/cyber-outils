A '''Bomb Bud''' is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Description==
At first, a Bomb Bud resembles a large, black [[fruit]], but plucking a Bomb Bud shows that it resembles a [[Bob-omb]] with no legs. A Bomb Bud can be grabbed using [[Trio Grab]], but if a Bomb Bud is held for too long, it explodes.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''==
Bomb Buds can be used to destroy the electrical gates, and also to harm [[Kamek]] in the mission [[Outta the Way, Kamek!]].
