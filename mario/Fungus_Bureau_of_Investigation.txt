The '''Fungus Bureau of Investigation''' is an espionage-based organization mentioned briefly in the ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]'' comic "[[Duh Stupid Bomb!]]". Its name is a pun on the real-world American federal agency, the {{w|Federal Bureau of Investigation}}, but with the word "federal" substituted by fungus" to suggest that it is run by [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s.
