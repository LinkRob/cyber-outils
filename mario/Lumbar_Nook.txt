The '''Lumbar Nook''' is a location in [[Bowser's body]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|its]] [[Reissue|remake]]. It is known to be the smallest area in his body.

During gameplay, [[Bowser]] is released from the safe that his three minions, [[Private Goomp]], [[Sergeant Guy]] and [[Corporal Paraplonk]] tricked him into going in after [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] defeat [[Junker]]. He then states that he has a terrible back pain and then faints.

Mario and Luigi must then go inside of this area, drill into the center of the nook after greeting [[Toadsworth]]. Then, Mario and Luigi slowly heals Bowser and [[Toadbert]] and Toadsworth have tea while Mario and Luigi are slowly healing Bowser. Then, the player must wait a few minutes for Bowser to heal before doing anything else as a [[Nintendo DS]] (or [[Nintendo 3DS]] remake version) puts up a message to wait a while until hearing an alarm, meaning Bowser feels better.

When Bowser is healed, he feels that he has become more flexible and then uses his new [[Spike Ball]] attack he had just learned from this flexibility.

==Trivia==
*According to [[Toadbert]] in [[Toad Square]], he states about having a weird dream, showing an event in the Lumbar Nook and states a code going in order from ABXYLRYXBA and the player must press these buttons in order to speed up the healing process and Bowser becomes healed instantly.
