Characters who drop to 0 (or negative) hit points in the ''Super Mario Bros.'' role-playing games '''die''', collapsing to the ground and lying motionless. They cannot attack or use any moves while dead, though they can be revived via certain methods. Usually, enemies who die are gone for good, though some enemies can revive others. If all enemies die and at least one player character survives, the player wins the battle, and survivors gain [[Experience Point|Experience]] and items like [[Coin|Coins]] and other loot, while dead characters miss out on the Experience. Conversely, if all members of the player's party die, the player loses the battle, which triggers a [[Game Over]].

== History ==

=== In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' ===
Deceased party members in [[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars|''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'']] are effectively out of the battle until they come back to life via a Pick Me Up item or [[Princess Peach]]'s Come Back technique. If a character suffers a mortal blow, they can potentially die instantly regardless health.

=== In the ''Paper Mario'' franchise ===
In most games in the [[Paper Mario|''Paper Mario'']] franchise, a Game Over will happen if Mario dies without any [[Life Shroom|Life Shrooms]] in his possession. 

In [[Paper Mario 64|''Paper Mario 64'']], partners have only one hit point and will be [[Injured|dead for a number of turns based on the damage the attack would normally deal]], and will be unable to help out, but they will automatically revive at the end. This is removed in [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'']], where they have health and only die if they drop to a non-positive amount of hit points and must be revived manually. In these games, Mario will immediately revive with ten hit points if a [[Life Shroom]] is in his possession.

In [[Super Paper Mario|''Super Paper Mario'']], [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Bowser]], and [[Princess Peach]] will die upon hitting a non-positive amount of health, but will be revived at five hit points rather than ten.

In [[Paper Mario: The Origami King|''Paper Mario: The Origami King'']], Mario can carry a [[1-Up Mushroom]] in his inventory, meaning if he dies, he can be revived with it.

=== In the ''Mario & Luigi'' franchise ===
When his health reaches a non-positive value in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'']] franchise, [[Mario (character)|Mario]] or [[Luigi]] will die and can no longer help out in battle. If the battle ends and he stays dead, he will not gain [[Experience Point|Experience]], but will be revived at a single hit point. The survivor carries the corpse on his shoulder, making counterattacks harder to perform, and [[Special Attack|Special Attacks]] will be banned until the dead Bro. is revived. If the player decides to [[Flee|run from battle]], the survivor carries the corpse while running, but cannot trip.

In [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time|''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'']], the adult brother's corpse will be carried off-screen by the baby. If the baby dies as well, the remaining adult has to carry the body. When using a 1-Up Mushroom to revive the adult, the baby retrieves the body from the off-screen area he dragged the corpse to.

In [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'']], Mario and Luigi say, "Mamma mia..." when they die, and Luigi falls onto his back instead of his front when killed; this carries into later games in the franchise. Bowser, due to fighting on his own, will trigger a Game Over if his health drops to a non-positive value.

Death in [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team|''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'']] will cause the side of the [[Badge]] Meter that corresponds to the Bro. who just got killed to instantly deplete, akin to what happens in [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'']] if that Bro. got a Great! or worse when [[Luigi]] has his Excellent!! badge equipped. Losing the battle in Hard Mode will force the player back to the title screen; otherwise, the player can restart the battle.

If [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] dies in battle in [[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam|''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'']], he will not be carried by the Bros., unless the player chooses to run away. Additionally, Paper Mario loses access to his [[Trio Attack|Trio Attacks]] if either non-paper Mario or Luigi is dead, as all three must be active to use them. If all three party members die, the player can retry the battle.

Death in [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']] disables combo attacks, just like with [[Bros. Attack|Bros. Attacks]], similar to what happens if a Bro. is poisoned by [[Glohm]]. If a Bro. dies with the other Bro. knocked away by [[Bowser]], captured by the [[Great Conductor]], or [[Zokket]] has brought the still-alive Bro. under his control, the alive Bro. will be considered dead as well, causing an instant Game Over. Luigi also loses access to his [[Luigi Logic]] power if something kills Mario. If the standing brother is poisoned by Glohm, he will not carry the dead brother's corpse when enemies attack. Losing the battle allows retries, with losing enough times allowing the player to access Easy Mode to make the fight easier. There is also a [[Battle Plug]] called the Auto 1-Up, which revives a dead Bro. with a [[1-Up Mushroom]] (or the Deluxe variation with Deluxe Auto if there are any 1-Up Deluxes) instantly.

=== In the ''Mario + Rabbids'' franchise ===
The heroes will fall unconscious in the [[Mario + Rabbids (series)|''Mario + Rabbids'']] franchise when their health drops to 0 or negative values, and will be unable to fight. If all heroes die, the player will lose the battle. Dead heroes in [[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle'']] cannot be revived until the battle is cleared. If the level is completed, their health is restored. In [[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope|''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'']], dead heroes will still gain experience when the battle is cleared, and they can be revived with [[Pulser]]'s revive ability.
