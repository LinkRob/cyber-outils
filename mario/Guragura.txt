'''Guragura''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Guragura are bipedal moles with round feet. They wear a bandana and appear to have sunglasses lens for eyes. In their artwork, Guragura are shown to have a light orange body, a cream belly, and brown feet.

===Behavior===
Guragura always carry a flail with them. They attack [[Wario]] by swinging the spiked ball end of their flail at him.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
Guragura are encountered in as early as [[Course No.01]].
