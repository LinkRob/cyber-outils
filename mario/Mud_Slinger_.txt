'''Mud Slinger!''' is the [[Super Ability]] used by [[Petey Piranha]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Petey pukes lots of mud onto the ground, similar to his [[goop]]-spitting attack from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. Any character who walks into it, aside from Petey, has their speed greatly reduced, unless that character is under the effects of a [[Starman|Star]].
