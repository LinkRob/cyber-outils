'''Princess Daisy''' is the princess of [[Sarasaland]] who came from the [[Game Boy]] video game, ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. She has attributes that are no different to [[Nintendo]]'s [[Princess Daisy]] from ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' such as the long brown hair and her yellow dress.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Princess Daisy resembles Nintendo's Princess Daisy's appearance in ''Super Mario Land''; long brown hair and a yellow dress.
