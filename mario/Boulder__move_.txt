|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Boulder''' is a very strong special move used by certain enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
With Boulder, the foe launches a bunch of boulders that roll across the screen and deals massive damage to all of [[Mario]]'s party. This move is considered even stronger than [[Water Blast]] but a tad weaker than the [[Breaker Beam]]. Boulder is a [[Earth (element)|Earth]] type move.
