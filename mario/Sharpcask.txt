The '''Sharpcask''' is a character from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is a [[Barrel|barrel]]-shaped monster that serves as the guardian of one of the Great Lighthouses, specifically that of the Color-Full Sea. When Mario and Luigi storm the lighthouse, the Sharpcask is called by [[Extension Corps|Ecks]] to deal with the duo. As with the [[Gobblick]] and the [[Pipegunk]], he has a [[Glohm]] doppëlganger that can be fought later in the game.

== Description ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Sharpcask is a monster with silver limbs with the hands being pincers akin to those of a crab or a Mixel from ''Mixels'' and a silver shell with a gap with a black head in the centre with cyan eyes. He can withdraw into his shell to give himself a defense boost, resulting in him being indistinguishable from his namesake.

The creature's [[Glohm]] doppelgänger looks the same, but it has red eyes and its shell is tinted purple, and a Glohm effect surrounds it.

== Combat ==
The Sharpcask begins the fight in his exposed form. In this state, he attacks by throwing barrels or rolling around as one, and both these attacks must be jumped over. He can also perform a similar move to the [[Monolift|Monolifts]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' where he grabs a crate and lifts it high while standing in front of his target. After a while, the Sharpcask will slam his crate onto the victim. The target has to smash the Sharpcask with his hammer when the crate tilts toward the conveyor belt on the monster's side of the arena. Another one of the Sharpcask's attacks has him plant his arms on the ground and withdraw his head and legs, followed by a bomb going off behind his back to launch him onto his prey. Mario and Luigi have to use their hammers to deflect the Sharpcask's headbutt when the bomb goes off.

When his health reaches 75% maximum, the Sharpcask can also withdraw into his shell, giving himself an even bigger defense boost. The first time this happens, [[Snoutlet]], looking at him, wonders how to deal with the creature. However, the conveyor belt activates, and [[Luigi]] can perform his [[Luigi Logic]] skill to move the conveyor belt so that the metal crates that fall onto it land on the Sharpcask instead. The Sharpcask will become dizzy with his defense dropping to lower than normal if Luigi stacks enough crates on top of the creature's head. However, the monster will recover in a few turns, with his defense resetting. On later turns, the Sharpcask can withdraw into his shell at any time, and using Luigi Logic then will have bombs mixed in with the crates; if a bomb hits the stack, the Luigi Logic fails.

Eventually, [[Extension Corps|Ecks]] will butt in and perform a dance. This gives the Sharpcask's [[Power|Attack]] and [[Defense]] stats a boost. The Sharpcask also becomes enraged and fights harder when his health bar is almost empty.

The Sharpcask is a Level 19 monster with 1600 hit points, an attack power of 73, a defense of 88, and a speed of 214. When beaten, he gives 2500 experience, dropping 550 coins and the Sharpcask Hammer, which allows the wielder to get a defense boost every time they defeat an enemy with it.

==Glohm Sharpcask==
The Sharpcask has a Glohm-infused doppelgänger that can be faced later in the game fights just like the Sharpcask himself did, but it is much stronger and its attacks come out more quickly, and when Luigi uses his Luigi Logic ability to incapacitate it, bombs must be avoided. This clone is a Level 39 monster with 7000 hit points, an attack power of 235, a defense of 294, a speed of 585, an experience drop of 4,000, and a loot drop consisting of 1400 coins and the Platinum Medallion, which increases speed by a bit, but also makes the wearer more likely to be targeted; the Platinum Medallion's drawback can be negated by having the opposite Bro. wear a Rainbow Medallion.

== Nomenclature ==
The Sharpcask is called "Tarandoru" (written タルンドル) in Japanese, a pun on ''taru'' (Japanese for "barrel"; written 樽 in Kanji, たる in Hiragana, and タルンドル in Katakana) and ''tarundoru'' ("slacking off"; written 弛んどる). In Chinese, he is called ''Dàiduòsōngxiè Tǒng'' (written 怠惰松懈桶), meaning "Slack Off Barrel." His Spanish name, ''Barrilente'' comes from ''barril'' ("barrel") and ''(s)ente'' ("being"). Portuguese calls the boss Barrílio, from ''barril'' ("barrel") and the given name ''Emílio'' ("Emil"). Italian refers to him as ''Barillor'' (from ''barile'' ("barrel") and ''orrore'' ("horror")). In French, the creature is called "Tonnéphar", from ''tonneau'' ("barrel") and ''phare'' ("lighthouse"). Dutch refers to him as ''Tonront'' (a pun on ''tonrond'' ("rotund") and ''ton'' ("barrel"), and in German, his name is ''Fasstor'' (from ''Fass'' ("barrel") and ''Motor'' ("engine")). He bears the name ''Baereodoreu'' in Korean, from his Japanese name ''Tarundoru'' and a Korean transliteration of "barrel."

== Trivia ==
* If the Sharpcask is killed by [[Luigi Logic]] damage, he will not enter a dizzy state when reaching the bottom, instead remaining hunkered until the boss defeat animation plays. Given how early into his battle the Sharpcask hunkers, this can be best found by leveling Mario and Luigi up a lot. This is harder to find when facing the Glohm doppelgänger.
