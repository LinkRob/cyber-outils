'''Hopping Tap-Taps''' are a variant of [[Tap-Tap]] in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. They are distinguished from regular Tap-Taps from their behavior of constantly hopping up and down. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', and the distinction of Hopping Tap-Taps were only mentioned in the game's [[Nintendo Player's Guide]]. Other sources otherwise suggest that regular Tap-Taps have gone on to receive this behavior. Hopping Tap-Taps are featured in either gray or orange varieties.
