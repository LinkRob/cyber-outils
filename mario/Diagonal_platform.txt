'''Diagonal platforms''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==Description==
They are angled [[lift]]s made of solid dirt and consisting of a tuft of grass on top. A diagonal platform either faces the top-left or top-right. A diagonal platform has a repeated pattern of moving slowly to one side, stopping, and moving back. A diagonal platform is not slipped on like the slopes of a [[Semisolid Platform]]s' slopes, but [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can still [[slide]] on a diagonal platform.

==In ''Super Mario World''==
Diagonal platforms appear in two [[level]]s, [[Chocolate Island 4]] and in [[Valley of Bowser 3]].
