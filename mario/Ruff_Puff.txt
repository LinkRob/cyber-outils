'''Ruff Puff''' is a yellowish-brown, cloud-like enemy. They appear as regular enemies in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Physical description==
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Ruff Puffs are brown in color. They also have pink eyes. In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', they are now tan in color and have orange eyes.

==In the ''Paper Mario'' series==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
[[File:Ruff Puff PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
Ruff Puffs only appear in [[Flower Fields]], where they are common enemies. Ruff Puffs also appear in the battle alongside their leader [[Huff N. Puff]].

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand Year Door]]''===
Ruff Puffs appear in [[Riverside Station]] in this game. They are very common there along with [[Poison Pokey]]s.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
[[File:RuffPuffCard.png|thumb]]
Ruff Puffs appear in the [[Overthere Stair]] and the Flipside [[Pit of 100 Trials]] in this game. They attack mainly by lanching electric balls at Mario's group.
