'''Spray Anything''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
Two of teammates shoot water balls at the solo characters, who reacts in surprise by jumping and moving out of the way, causing the water balls to hit the other teammates.

==Gameplay==
The object for the team members is to try and hit the solo character with a water ball, and meanwhile, the solo character has to avoid being hit until the 30-second [[time limit]] expires. If a team member is hit by a water ball, they are stunned temporarily. The team wins if the solo character gets hit by a water ball, and the solo character wins if they avoid taking hit from any of the water balls up until the timer expires.

==Controls==
===Solo===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move

===Team===
* {{button|gcn|L}} {{button|gcn|R}}: Circle the ring
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Fire water ball

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''Three players fire water balls at the lone player, who must try to avoid being soaked!''
* '''Advice''': ''If you are firing water balls and an ally hits you, you'll be stunned. Check your fire!''
