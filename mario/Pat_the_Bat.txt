'''Pat the Bats''' are a type of enemy encountered in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''. They are first encountered in [[Ludwig's Thump Castle Hotel]], and are similar to [[Swooper]]s in that they attack by swooping down at [[Mario]]. Pat the Bat can open a door upon coming into contact with it. A Pat the Bat can be defeated by either [[jump]]ing on it or shooting a [[fireball]] at it.
