The '''Koopa Bros. Fortress''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is a massive fortress located beyond [[Pleasant Path]] and is the second fortress visited in the game with the first being the [[Goomba King's Fortress]].

==History==
Owned by the [[Koopa Bros.]], this Fortress has one of the [[Star Spirits]], [[Eldstar]], located beyond [[Koopa Village]]. Here is where the [[Star Spirit]] [[Eldstar]] was being guarded by the Koopa Bros.

This Fortress was originally owned by the [[Bob-omb]]s until, under [[Bowser]]'s command, the Koopa Bros. seized it.

After [[Mario]] defeats the Koopa Bros., they are trapped in their own jail cell with their [[Bowser???|replica]] of Bowser. Having been freed, or having had escaped, the Koopa Bros. send Mario a letter saying they plan for revenge.
