A '''Muku Cookie''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Muku Cookie is a rather unique item that restores an odd amount of 69 [[Heart Point|HP]] to all party members. It can also be purchased for 69 [[coin]]s as well. However, Muku Cookie's can only be purchased in [[Seaside Town]] during [[Yaridovich]]'s takeover.
