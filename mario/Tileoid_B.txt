}}A '''Tileoid B''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
TIleoid Bs are members of the [[Tileoid]] family that are found in the [[Dotwood Tree]] and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Like with other Tileoids, Tileoid Bs move around on the floors, walls, and ceilings. However, they move very slowly as they are the slowest of the Tileoids.
