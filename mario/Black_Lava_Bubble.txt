'''Black Lava Bubbles''' are an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Black Lava Bubbles are a variant of [[Podoboo|Lava Bubble]]s made of [[black paint]].

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
They only appear in [[Black Bowser's Castle]], during the battle with [[Black Bowser]]. Here, Black Lava Bubbles are formed from the black paint dropped by Black Bowser to assist him in battling [[Mario]] and [[Huey]]. Black Lava Bubbles can vary in size when summoned, depending on how much damage Black Bowser takes. Only three Black Lava Bubbles can appear at the same time before returning to Black Bowser.
