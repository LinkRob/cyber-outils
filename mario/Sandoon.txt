'''Sandoons''' are creatures that can be found in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' as enemies to fight. They can be found in the [[Dozing Sands]] area.

==Description==
Sandoons are slug-like enemies that resemble sand dunes. Their body is tan and gets darker close to a reddish-pink "foot" at the bottom with a single wavy pink stripe close to the bottom of their body. Bulbous eyes with brown eyelids and black pupils and three purple "antennae" are on the creature's "head."

==Behaviour==
Sandoons wait for their prey to pass by them with only their eyes ticking out of the ground. If they spot someone, they will charge as fast as possible, and if they miss, they will run around wildly for a bit.

==Combat==
Sandoons attack either by charging through their target or attempting to swallow him from below (which can negate the [[Boo Biscuit]]'s invincibility). The target can avoid the former by jumping and the latter by smashing the monster's eyes with his hammer when it pops up in front of his feet. A brother struck by the second move will fall into the Sandoon's mouth as it pops up, losing all his next turns until he is freed by the brother did not get hit, although the victim will get a quick second to retaliate by jabbing the monster's insides. A Sandoon does not use this attack if another has already scored a hit with it until the latter dies, and a Bro. takes half the damage that the Sandoon whose guts he's trapped takes. When a Sandoon dies, it will spit out its victim before popping. 

A Sandoon is a Level 10 enemy that has 52 HP, 50 Power, 53 Defense, and a Speed of 32 that gives 30 experience and 12 coins upon defeat in addition to a Super Mushroom in 1 out of 25 Sandoon kills.

Two Sandoons appear as a miniboss harassing two [[Shelltop]] [[Britta's Work Force|construction workers]] who proceed to teach the Bros. how to use the [[Mini Mario]] and [[Mole Mario]] techniques as a reward for defeating the Sandoons.

==Variations==
===Dreamy Sandoon===
{{main|Dreamy Sandoon}}
Dreamy versions of Sandoons appear in the [[Dream World|Dreamy version]] of Dozing Sands as common enemies. 

===In [[Torkscrew]]'s battle===
For one of his attacks, the [[Torkscrew]] will taunt a Mario brother to follow his trail into the background, forcing that brother to contend with him and dodge his charges repeatedly. The oppose Bro. has to deal with small Sandoon variations in addition to [[Purple Flounderflage]]s that charge. All three can be dodged by jumping. These short Sandoons give nothing upon defeat.

==Behind the Scenes==
Sandoons are based on sand dunes, with their name coming from an intentional misspelling of that as well as "goon."

==Nomenclature==
In the Japanese dub, Sandoons are referred to as ''Dyūn'' (written デューン), from a transliteration of "dune." Their Spanish name, ''Dunante'', comes from ''duna'' ("dune") and ''tunante'' ("thug"). Portuguese refers to them as ''Dunaréia'', from ''duna'' ("dune") and ''aréia'' ("sand"). In Italian, the creature is called ''Duno'', from a gender flip of ''duna'' ("dune"). French gives them the name ''Sablon'', from ''sable'' ("sand") and the augmentative suffix ''-on''. German translates their name as ''Wunderdüne'', literally "wonder dune." In the Russian dub, they are called ''Sypun'' (written Сыпун), from ''sypat'' ("to pour"; written сыпать) and the Russian masculine noun suffix ''-un'' (written -ун).

==Trivia==
*If a Sandoon gulps a Bro. while a [[Boo Biscuit]] is active, it will automatically deactivate for Mario and Luigi and the eaten Bro. will be liable to taking damage while in the Sandoon. This is a rare case of this happening.
