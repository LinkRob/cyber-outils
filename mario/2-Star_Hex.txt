The '''2-Star Hex''' is a type of [[Hex]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Description==
A player possessing a 2-Star Hex can choose to place it on any [[Blue Space]] or [[Red Space]] within five spaces from their current location. When a player lands on it, [[Star (Mario Party series)|star]]s are distributed and the hex is destroyed. If an opponent lands on it, two stars are taken from them and given to the player who placed the hex. If the player does not have any stars, the hex does not have any special effect; it is just destroyed. If the player/team who threw it lands on it, they receive five coins. This hex can be received in the [[Hex Area]] or by stealing it with a [[Snag Bag]].
