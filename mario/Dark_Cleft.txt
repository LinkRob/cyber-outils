}}A '''Dark Cleft''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Clefts are stronger versions of [[Cleft]]s and they can only be found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]].  They have tons of defense (10 to be exact) and attack with a ram like regular Clefts. It is recommended to flip them over with [[Boomer]] and then defeat them.
