The '''Extra Room''' is both a location and a mode in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Description==
The location is hosted by a [[Thwomp]] and a [[Whomp]]. The player's character can play bonus mini-games exclusive to this mode in either one of two exclusive boards, [[Thwomp's Backroom Ball]] or [[Whomp's Basement Brouhaha]].
