'''Baddie Boxes''' (also called '''Bowser Dispensers''') are objects from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]],'' ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]].'' They throw out enemies, typically [[Goomba]]s, although in [[Special 5-Castle]] some appear and throw out [[Dry Bones]], and in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' they have on occasion thrown out [[Bob-omb]]s as well. Baddie Boxes have a [[Bowser]] symbol on them, suggesting that they were designed by [[Bowser]]. They can be destroyed with the effects of the [[Lucky Bell]], [[Statue Leaf]], [[Starman]] [[Invincibility Leaf]], and the [[Invincibility Bell]]. Baddie Boxes are typically found above hazardous places such as [[Bottomless pit|Abyss]] or [[Lava]] so destroying one can be fatal, the key is to approach them with extreme caution before proceeding to destroy them. 

==Trivia==
* In [[World 2-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-4]] of [[Super Mario 3D Land|''Super Mario 3D Land'']]'','' there is a glitch you can do with Baddie Boxes.  If you die five times and obtain a [[Invincibility Leaf]], you can cause major lag by [[Ground Pound (move)|ground pounding]] on a Baddie Box.
