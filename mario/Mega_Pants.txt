'''Mega Pants''' is an armor item used exclusively by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from [[Moleville]] for 22 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Mega Pants increases Mallow's [[Defense]] by 18 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 9.
