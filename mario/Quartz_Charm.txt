'''Quartz Charm''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When the user equips the Quartz Charm, the item increases the player's offense and defense by 50%, and prevents the player from instant death. The player can obtain the item by defeating [[Culex]].
