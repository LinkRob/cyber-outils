|race = [[Pig]]}}'''Troff''' is a character that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==Appearance==
Troff is a large, pink pig with yellow eyes.

==History==
Troff is always seen with [[Scoff]] behind their portal as both hold the key to getting into a boss for a certain level. The [[Kong Family|Kongs]] must feed smaller bananas to Scoff who will then slam down on his platform causing the one Troff is standing on to rise. Troff is always tasked to unlock the door leading to the boss when the right amount of bananas is fed to Scoff.

During the end credits, Troff is seen dancing alongside Scoff.
