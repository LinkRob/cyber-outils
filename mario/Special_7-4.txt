'''Special 7-4''' is the fourth level of [[Special 7]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is based off of [[World 2-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-4]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts on a small wooden platform that is next to a [[Flip-Swap Panel]]. Next to this are some more platforms and Flip-Swap Panels. After are some Flip-Swap Panels which have [[Spike Trap|spikes]] on them, the platforms lead to another platform with a [[Spike Roller]], a [[Flying ? Block]] and several [[Brick Block]]s on it. The next section contains some more Flip-Swap Panels that have Spike Rollers on them. Finally, there is a platform that leads to more Flip-Swap Panels that lead to the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Medal]]s==
*'''Star Medal 1:''' The first Star Medal is on a blue [[Flip-Swap Panel]] next to some [[Spike Trap|spikes]].
*'''Star Medal 2:''' The second Star Medal is on the platform with the [[Flying ? Block]].
*'''Star Medal 3:''' The third Star Medal is on a blue Flip-Swap Panel near the Spike Roller.
