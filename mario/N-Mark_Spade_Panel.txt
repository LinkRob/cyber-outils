'''N-Mark Spade Panels''' are a type of [[Spade Panel]] and object in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and its [[reissue|remakes]].

==Description==
An N-Mark Spade Panel appear on the world map for every 80,000 [[point]]s gained by the player. When [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] walk over to an N-Mark Spade Panel, they automatically enter the area, where they can play the [[Matching Game]]. The cards are all flipped over, but all of them depict a power-up. If Mario or Luigi pick up two cards depicting a power-up, they earn that item for their inventory. If Mario or Luigi make two mistakes, the minigame ends, and they are forced out of the N-Mark Spade Panel back onto the world map. The cards do not reset until the game has been completed, regardless of how many card pairs have been flipped over.
