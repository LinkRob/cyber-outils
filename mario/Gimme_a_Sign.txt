'''Gimme a Sign''' is a Duel [[minigame]] played in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place on a river. There is a wooden dock in the center, and the left and right sides of the dock each have four wooden boards for both characters.

==Gameplay==
A Shy Guy holds up one or two signs, each depicting the same image as on some of the boards. When Shy Guy does this, any of the boards that depict the same image sink into the water for a second, and both characters have to avoid falling into the water. The signs change more rapidly as the minigame progresses. Whoever falls into the water loses, making the other character the winner. If both characters remain in the minigame for 30 seconds or if they both fall into the water simultaneously, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Ending==
Both characters are on the dock, where they perform their winning and losing animation respectively, and both the Shy Guy and [[Goomba]]s can be seen cheering. If the minigame ends in a tie, both characters perform their respective losing animations, and the Shy Guy and Goombas do not cheer.
