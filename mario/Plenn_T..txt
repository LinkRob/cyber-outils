'''Plenn T.''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a green [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who lives in [[Rogueport]].

== History ==
Plenn T. is the owner of the [[Toad Bros. Bazaar]] in [[Rogueport]]. He is the oldest of the Toad Bros. His younger brother is [[Niff T.]] He worked with his brother [[Thriff T.]] until his brother left to [[Keelhaul Key]].

== Trivia ==
*His name is a pun on the word "plenty" which references his occupation as a store clerk that sells a variety of items.
