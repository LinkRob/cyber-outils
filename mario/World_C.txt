'''World C''' is the fourth secret world that was first featured in the game ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'', following [[World B (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World B]]. After clearing this world, the player will gain access to the final world in the game, which is [[World D (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World D]].

==Level Information==
===World C-1===
This nighttime overworld level (which is snowy in ''All-Stars'') mostly has [[Piranha Plant]]s and Green [[Paratroopa]]s. There's a warp pipe that leads to a staircase made of blocks with coins. There are also two [[Poison Mushroom]]s on this stage so [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] need to watch out.

===World C-2===
This stage mostly contains bridges and mushrooms-like platforms. There are dangers from above and below with jumping [[Cheep Cheep]]s, [[Blooper]]s, and off-screen [[Bullet Bill]]s. This stage has only one [[? Block]] and no [[Brick Block]]s or [[Warp Pipe]]s.

===World C-3===
This stage has treetop-like platforms and springs, and is identical to World 7-3, aside from the fact that this level has [[Lakitu]] in it and 7-3 doesn't. The only enemies here are [[Lakitu]] (with [[Spiny|Spinies]]) and both red and green [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. There is also a strong wind in this stage that blows against the player. This wind can blow the player right off the stage. The player must advance through this level by jumping on green springs that can bounce [[Mario]] over pits that are impossible to jump over.

Glitch: The player MUST kill Lakitu before jumping on the fourth spring of the level because if she/he does not do this, the next spring will not spawn, making the level impossible to complete.

===World C-4===
This level is exactly the same as World 7-4, except for that a few more [[Fire Bar]]s have been added. The Koopa Troopas and [[Buzzy Beetle]]s also move at a faster speed in ''All-Stars''. Another change is the addition of some blocks that appear near two sets of clockwise-moving Fire Bars. The blocks are obviously there to make it harder for Mario or Luigi to get past the Fire Bars unscathed. In fact, there's one area where it's impossible to do so, although there's a hidden Mushroom before it. The fireball-spitting [[Bowser Impostor]] is really a Buzzy Beetle in the original FCD version, but in ''All-Stars'', it's a [[Bullet Bill]] who throws hammers. After clearing this level, the player must head to the next (and [[World D (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|final]]) secret world.
