'''Zero to Hero''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', featuring it under [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[Sports]] folder.

==Layout==
A character is standing in the middle of an entirely black background. To the right is a power meter consisting of twelve colored blocks, which continually appear and disappear in the order of up to down. The lowest of the blocks are light green, the middle ones are yellow, and the topmost blocks are red.

==Gameplay==
The object is for the player to turn the character into a superhero. To accomplish this, they must press {{button|gba|A}} when the meter is in the red zone. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame. However, if a button is pressed while the meter is in the light green or yellow zone, the character turns into a pig instead and loses at the microgame.

The power meter has one less red block with each increased difficulty level: the first level has three red blocks; the second level has two red blocks;, and the third level has only the topmost blocks red.
