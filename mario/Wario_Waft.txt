'''Wario Waft''' is the down special move of [[Wario]] in all of his playable {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} appearances, the first time being in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

==Description==
Wario lets out a big fart, represented by toxic yellow gas, that can damage and knockback opponents within range as well as cause Wario to charge upward.

==In ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' ([[Nintendo DS]])==
Wario can perform Wario Waft during the minigame [[Blow Away Chain Chomp!]] in [[Adventure Tours]]. He must use Wario Waft to push back a [[Chain Chomp]] on a platform until it falls off, upon which the minigame is cleared.
