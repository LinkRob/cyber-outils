'''Tip Networks''' are objects in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Description==
Tip Networks are signboards that are yellow and green in color and with a screen. They provide hints about upcoming obstacles, essentially giving them the role of [[Hint Movie]]s from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', but watching a Tip Network does not cost anything. There are only a few Tip Networks, and they appear only while playing as [[Mario]], not as [[Luigi]]. Mario can activate a Tip Network by standing in front of it. After watching a video on a Tip Network, all enemies respawn.

==Trivia==
*If the player looks through first-person view while standing in front of a Tip Network, they can see an infinite mirror effect on the screen.
