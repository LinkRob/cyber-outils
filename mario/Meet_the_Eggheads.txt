'''Meet the Eggheads''' is [[Young Cricket]]'s microgame in the game ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

== Gameplay ==

The player must direct the [[Wii Remote]] to the lost Egghead. The player must bring the Egghead back to its family. If it touches the spike, he/she loses the microgame. The colour of the screen background changes which hints that they are about to hit the spike.

*'''Level 1:''' A linear maze.
*'''Level 2:''' A curved maze.
*'''Level 3:''' A U-turn maze; or a maze that moves.
