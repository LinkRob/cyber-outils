==History==
The Black chest contain the souls of the Four Heroes who helped defeat the [[Shadow Queen]] 1,000 years ago. However, they have become [[Black Chest Demon]]s who do nothing but "curse" [[Mario]] whenever he opens one (though this always give him a helpful ability). A [[Black Key]] is always needed to open a Black chest but they are usually found nearby. These are the locations of the four Black chests in the game:

*[[Rogueport Sewers]] on a lower floor in a room.
*[[Hooktail Castle]] past the jailing room before the spiked ceiling room.
*[[Twilight Town]] in the storeroom of the item shop.
*[[Pirate's Grotto]] inside an old ship.

After a Black chest is opened and the Black Chest Demon goes away, the Black chest will stay there opened for the rest of the game.
