The '''Blue Falcon''' is an unlockable [[kart]] in the game ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', as well as [[Captain Falcon]]'s personal race vehicle in the ''F-Zero'' franchise.

==Information==
The Blue Falcon is the fastest small kart in the game, and it can be either unlocked by winning the [[Lightning Cup]] in [[Mirror Mode]] or by playing 4,200 games. Its bike counterpart is the [[Jet Bubble]].

==NPC Use==

Toad (Nin☆GQO) drives a Blue Falcon in Time Trials for [[Toad's Factory]] and FJ (Nin☆FJ) [[Sherbet Land (Mario Kart 64)|Sherbet Land]].

==Mario Kart Wii==
The Blue Falcon is the fastest small vehicle in the game and has above average drift. Its acceleration is average. However, it is rather light and has the worst handling, off-road, and mini-turbo among all small vehicles in the game.{{MKW Kart Stats/Blue Falcon}}

==''Mario Kart 8''==
The Blue Falcon returns in Mario Kart 8 as a DLC kart body. This time, it has its SNES appearance. Unlike its appearance in Mario Kart Wii, the Blue Falcon is available to every weight class and in only one color rather than color based on the character chosen. 

==''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)]]''==
The Blue Faclon first appears in the first Super Smash Bros. game for [[Captain Falcon]] to appear in the start of a battle match. It later appears in the background Melee stages, [[Big Blue (stage)|Big Blue]], and [[Mute City]]. It officially appears throughout the other three games as Captain Falcon's [[Final Smash]].

==Trivia==
*Although this kart is called the "Blue Falcon", only when Toad and Baby Mario drive a Blue Falcon that it is blue.
*The Blue Falcon is a replica of the ''[[w:c:f-zero:F-Zero (series)|F-Zero]]'' supercar of the same name (hence its speed), which is piloted by [[w:c:f-zero:Captain Falcon|Captain Falcon]] in the ''F-Zero'' games. In those games, it is the most balanced machine and always available from the start.
