'''Dream Snowball Fight''' is a [[Dream Event]] in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==Layout==
The event takes place in [[Christmas Village]].

==Gameplay==
The objective of the event is to score more points than the opposing team by hitting them with [[snowball]]s.

This event is played with a tournament structure, where the player competes in a qualifying match, and goes on to compete in the final if they win or ends the event if they lose. In the event, the player can move around by pressing the direction they want to travel in on {{button|wii|pad}}, and can double tap the direction quickly to perform a faster dodge in the direction. The player throws snowballs by swinging the Wii Remote, and can restock their snowballs by pressing {{button|wii|A}}, being able to hold a maximum of between four and ten (depending on the character's Power stat) at any one time. The player can lock onto standing players on the other team by holding {{button|wii|B}}, and can change targets by quickly pressing it. The player scores 1 point for hitting a character, and three points for knocking them down entirely by hitting them three times. If the player is hit three times, they must shake the Wii Remote to get back up, but points cannot be scored by hitting characters while they are still down. Bonus points can also be scored by entirely knocking down the opponent's snowman. [[Bullet Bill]]s will also occasionally be shot into the area, and can be hit by snowballs to turn them around and target the opposition. If hit by a Bullet Bill, the character will be immediately knocked down regardless of how many hits they have taken so far, but can defend against them using a Super Star. The area also features [[Brick Block]]s that the players can hide behind, though do not provide full protection from snowballs, and [[Item Box]]es that can be collected to obtain items. Six items can be obtained and are used automatically when obtained, each with different effects:

*[[Super Star]]: Gives the player invincibility and a speed boost for a short amount of time.
*[[POW Block]]: Briefly stuns all of the other team. Can be defended against using a Super Star.
*[[Mega Mushroom]]: Increases the character's size, making them require two hits to take hit point and each of their snowballs to cause two hits.
*[[Power Sneakers|High Speed Shoes]]: Increases the character's speed for a short amount of time.
*[[Power Up (item)|Power Up]]: Allows the player to perform a more powerful special shot that can immediately knock down characters it hits.
*[[Ammo Boost]]: Gives the player unlimited snowballs for a short amount of time.
At the end of each match, the team with the most points wins.

==Instructions==
===Rules===
*In the '''Dream Snowball Fight''', teams of four face off to see who can score the most points by hitting the opposing team with snowballs.
*Dodge incoming snowballs as you take aim at the opposition and try for an all-time high score!

===Controls===
*Press up on the +Control Pad to move forward, or down to move back.
*Press left or right on the +Control Pad to move in the direction entered.
*Quickly tap a direction on the +Control Pad to '''dodge''' in that direction.
*Hold down {{button|wii|B}} to '''lock on''' to a rival.
*Release {{button|wii|B}} to remove the lock-on.
*Swing the Wii Remote to throw a snowball.
*When you run out of snowballs, press {{button|wii|A}} to restock.

===Tips===
*Players are wide open to enemy snowballs when restocking, so watch for the chance to catch the opposition unarmed!
*Quickly tap {{button|wii|B}} when '''locked on''' to change which character you're locked onto.
*Targeting nearby, easy-to-hit characters is the key to a killer score!
*Take down a '''snowman''' for a big point bonus! It takes a good deal to break a snowman, so try taking them on as a team.
*'''Bullet Bill''' can be turned around by hitting him with snowballs. If you see him coming, send him back to your opponent's camp!

===Items===
*Collect a '''Super Star''' to become invincible and move faster than normal for a set time.
*'''POW Blocks''' make the entire opposing team dizzy.
*Collecting a '''Mega Mushroom''' will make the character grow in size, enhancing both their attack and defense.
*Using '''High Speed Shoes''' will boost your speed for a set time, letting you go faster.
*Collect a '''Power Up''' item to supercharge your character, enabling them to perform a '''special shot'''.
*Collecting an '''Ammo Boost''' item will enable your character to throw a large number of snowballs.
