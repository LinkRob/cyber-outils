'''Starting blocks''' are objects which first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

==Description==
Starting blocks are set of three pink [[block]]s in certain games of the {{series|Super Mario}}. The starting blocks disappear whenever the player's character either [[jump]]s or moves off of them. 

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' / ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
Starting blocks are featured in challenges where the player cannot touch the ground.

===In {{series|Super Mario Maker}}===
In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', starting blocks are in [[Course Maker]] only, appearing while testing a level. Starting blocks disappear once the player either jumps or walks off of them.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
Starting blocks spawn underneath each player during the starting countdown of a match in [[Super Mario Maker (stage)|Super Mario Maker]], a stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. The starting blocks appear in place of the usual respawn platforms to match the aesthetic of ''Super Mario Maker'', and they fade out when the match starts.
