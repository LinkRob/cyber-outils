'''Cannon Kongs''' are an enemy in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars]]'' and ''[[Mini Mario & Friends: amiibo Challenge]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Cannon Kongs are a type of [[monkey robot]] with a large, blue [[cannon]] for a torso.

===Behavior===
A Cannon Kong cannot hurt the [[Mini]] toys directly. Instead, it vacuums them into their cannon-shaped torso, which it then uses to blast the Minis forward in the direction that the Cannon Kong is facing.

==In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars''==
Cannon Kongs are first encountered in [[Runaway Warehouse]]. While in the air after being fired from a Cannon Kong, Minis can destroy enemies and crumbling blocks or stun [[Circus Kong]]s. A Cannon Kong can be stunned with a [[hammer]], like with Circus Kongs. Players can also stop the Minis after they are launched by putting up objects in front of the toys, such as [[Red Girder]]s.
