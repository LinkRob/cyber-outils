The '''Mario Brothers Rap''', originally '''The Mario Rap''' or simply '''Mario Rap''' (with the first part also being known as the '''Plumber Rap''' and the second part also being known as the '''Super Mario Bros. Super Show Animated Opening''') is the theme song to ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. Most of the raps were performed by Lou Albano and Danny Wells in the TV version and Italian rappers in the video game version, through the database they were translated with any language. The intro was filmed in greenscreen in addition to animation by [[DIC Entertainment|Andy Heyward's company]].

An altered cover of the song is featured in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. This song was changed in the film to remove any mention of other characters such as the [[Bowser (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|king of the Koopas]], his army or even [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]] since they didn't meet or interact each other yet.

== Intro (TV Version) ==
The intro begins with Mario's head, reading the title card. The real-life Mario and Luigi are dancing and rapping. The characters are in poles, coming down and the only one who breaks in, rushing, but King Koopa is unimpressed. Again, Mario and Luigi (real-life) are rapping in a cartoonish setting (with birds flying) while the characters open the door, but the brothers (real-life) step inside into a cartoonish setting and the characters in a carpet, flying above the villainous man-eating plants. The brothers (real-life) are chasing below the fish and Mario reaches the star, but turned into color. Again, the characters are in a carpet, flying into the cannon. The owl creeps out and the characters are in a carpet after exiting the castle. At the end, the show's logo appears alongside the characters always flying, zooming out.
