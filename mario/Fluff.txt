'''Fluff''', also named '''Floaty Fluff''', is a type of plant that appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and its sequel, ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
=== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' ===
Fluff first appears in [[Gusty Garden Galaxy]] as an item that must be used to reach other planetoids. In order to use them, the player must spin around them, then jump into them. The player can shake the Wii Remote up to 3 times to boost up higher. Jumping while hanging onto one will result in falling.

=== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' ===
Fluff returns in [[Honeyhop Galaxy]] and [[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]]. They are used the same way as the first game.
