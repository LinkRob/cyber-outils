'''Oozer''' is an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
Oozers are [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] with an orange, slim body with white eyes and a mouth.

===Behavior===
Oozers are more aggressive than their ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' counterpart, [[Hider]]s, as Oozers move and hide more quickly and attack more frequently. Oozers attack by throwing objects, sometimes in a set of three instead of one. Unlike [[Goob]]s, Oozers are not protected.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3''==
Oozers first appear in the [[Entertainment Room]] of the [[Mezzanine]] after defeating [[Chef Soulfflé]]. Here, Oozers attack by throwing balls at [[Luigi]].
