'''Icy''' is a [[Snow spirits|snow spirit]] in [[Adventure Tours]] of the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
Icy is the snow spirit of [[Icepeak]] and the fifth snow spirit encountered by [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]]. Mario and Sonic first find Icy held hostage after defeating [[Big Bullet Bill]] at the end of [[Cubyrinth]] 2. They then free Icy and it joins the team after [[Cuby]] returns to Cubyrinth. If the player has four green crystals, Icy can break the ice walls in Icepeak.
