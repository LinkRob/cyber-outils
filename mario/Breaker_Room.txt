The '''Breaker Room''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Breaker Room is on the basement floor of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]]. It consists of two oil drums, several [[barrel]]s, a generator, and a cloth-covered table. There is a [[mirror]] on the opposite side of the generator, and the mirror can only be seen using the [[Game Boy Horror]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Breaker Room is accessible from as early as the start of [[Area Two]], but it does not have a role until the [[blackout]], which begins at the start of [[Area Four]]. During the blackout, the Breaker Room is locked, and the [[key]] to the room is held by [[Uncle Grimmly]], whom Luigi has to defeat to obtain the key to the Breaker Room.

During the blackout, a difference in the Breaker Room is that the handle of the generator is down, but if Luigi pulls it back up, power is restored to all previously completed areas as well as the Breaker Room itself.

The only furniture in the Breaker Room that has [[treasure]] is the small lamp on the table, which contains five [[Coin]]s. While the room is dark, there is a [[Speedy Spirit]] hidden under the cloth-covered table, and capturing the Speedy Spirit causes it to drop 20 Coins, 5 [[Bill]]s, and a [[Blue Stone]].
