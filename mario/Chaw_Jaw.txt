The '''Chaw Jaw''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Chaw Jaw.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Chaw Jaw resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has medium cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Chaw Jaw deals around 65 to 75 damage. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Honey (status effect)|Bounce]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Chaw Jaw as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it when the player defeats [[Blizzy and Sandy]].
