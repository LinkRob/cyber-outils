They were later replaced by living [[Bat (Luigi's Mansion series)|bats]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bats are either purple or yellow in color. Aside from this, they do not bear much physical difference to generic bats.

===Behavior===
Like [[Mouse|Mice]], Bats are generally only found in hallways and around staircases and lack any [[Health Meter]], so [[Luigi]] can instantly vacuum one into his [[Poltergust 3000]]. Bats can also be instantly defeated from any [[Element Medal|elemental attack]] as well. If a Bat hits Luigi, he takes five [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage, also defeating the Bat, which disappears in a puff of smoke.

Purple and yellow Bats behave differently. Purple Bats swoop down at Luigi whenever he approaches. Yellow Bats hang from the ceiling and only start trying to attack Luigi if he vacuums nearby with his Poltergust 3000 in the same room or corridor. Yellow Bats do not react if Luigi is busy unleashing [[Fire (element)|fire]], [[Water (element)|water]], or [[Ice (element)|ice]] from his Poltergust 3000. Due to a [[glitch]], yellow Bats are also awoken if the player presses {{button|GCN|R}} whenever the Poltergust 3000 is unusable or does not activate (such as while in first-person mode with the [[Game Boy Horror]]).
