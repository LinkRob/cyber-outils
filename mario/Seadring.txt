'''Seadrings''' are special creatures that appear in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They are only seen and found in [[Dreamy Driftwood Shore]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical appearance ===
Seadrings are seahorse-like creatures that have springy bodies. They also come in different colors.
=== Powers and abilities ===
Seadrings have the ability to produce [[Dream Egg]]s from their mouths. Due to them having springy bodies, they can also leap very high. Also, though this was only shown by Seadric, Seadrings can also eat the Dream Eggs to expand the dream even more.
