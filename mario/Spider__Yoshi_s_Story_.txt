'''Spiders''' are a type of enemy encountered in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. They are encountered in two levels, [[Surprise!!]] and [[Jungle Hut]].

Similar to real-world spiders, Spiders dangle from a silk thread to try and damage a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. Unlike generic spiders, Spiders have seven spiky, light green legs on its body and tries to injure a Yoshi with them. Spiders cannot be defeated and therefore have to be avoided.
