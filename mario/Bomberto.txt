|race = [[Bob-omb]]}}'''Bomberto''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a male [[Bob-omb]] that is a sailor.

==History==
Bomberto plays a minor role in the game only having a trouble done at the [[Trouble Center]]. His trouble involves bringing him any type of food. After giving Bomberto a food item (which can even be as something as simple as a [[Mushroom]]), he will reward Mario with eleven [[coin]]s.

==Trivia==
*Bomberto's name is a pun of the words ''bomb'' and the Italian/Spanish name ''Roberto''.
