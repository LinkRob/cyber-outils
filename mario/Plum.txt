'''Plum''' is an amateur golfer who debuts in the [[Nintendo 64]] game ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' as one of the playable golfers.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Plum has black hair with double side ponytails and wears a blue shirt with a red neckline, black shorts, white socks. In artwork, Plum's shoes are white, but in the Nintendo 64 game ''Mario Golf'' and her ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]], Plum has brown shoes.

In the [[Game Boy Color]] title ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'', Plum has red hair and her shoes are red.

==History==
===''Mario Golf'' series===
====''Mario Golf'' (Nintendo 64)====
Plum makes her debut in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' as one of the playable characters available from the start. She is generally a well-balanced golfer, though she lacks strength; as such, she cannot drive the ball very far. However, Plum makes up for her lack of power with technique and control. She also has a straight shot trajectory.

====''Mario Golf'' (Game Boy Color)====
Plum makes a small cameo appearance in the dictionary menu of the [[Game Boy Color]] ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]''. Here, she serves as the person explaining and defining golf terms.

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
====''Super Smash Bros. Melee''====
Plum received her own [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and is the only original ''Mario Golf'' character to be depicted as a trophy in the game. It can be obtained when it appears at random in one of the game's single-player modes.

====''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''====
Plum appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' as a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]]. In [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]], the sticker can be used by any character, raising their attack power by five points.
