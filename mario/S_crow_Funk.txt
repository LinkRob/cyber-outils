'''S'crow Funk''' is a special move used only by [[Belome]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When S'crow Funk is used, Belome will recite a rhyme before using it that goes as followed:

''Stick for a body,''

''Head full of straw,''

''Give me a Scarecrow,''

''Rah, Rah RAH!''

Afterwards, Belome then sprays a dust of red, blue, and yellow particles at the target.

== Effects ==
With S'crow Funk, Belome can turn any target into a [[Scarecrow]]. Belome tends to only use this attack during the first battle with him at [[Kero Sewers]]. Wearing a Trueform Pin will cancel this attack out.
