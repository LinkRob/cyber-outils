'''''The Wizard''''' is a 1989 adventure film starring Fred Savage, Luke Edwards and Jenny Lewis.

== Plot ==
The film follows three children as they travel to California so the youngest, who has a condition that makes him emotionally withdrawn, can compete to win $50,000 in Video Armaggedon at Universal Studios Hollywood. The movie was North America's introduction to what would become one of the best-selling video games of all time, ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]],'' since Mario is seen on the poster.

== References to other media ==
* ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' made its appearance in the final event of the Video Armageddon competition.
* A variation of the [[Raccoon Mario]] artwork from the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' game cover, without the Raccoon features, is shown on the film poster.
