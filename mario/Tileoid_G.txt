}}A '''Tileoid G''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Tileoid Gs are members of the [[Tileoid]] family that are found throughout the [[Dotwood Tree]]. Tileoid Gs are the weakest of the Tileoids but move faster than most of the other Tileoids. Tileoid Gs commonly move around on the floors, walls, and ceilings. Tileoid Gs can also be found in the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]].
