A '''Slinker''' is a kind of ghost in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. appears in both [[The Last Resort]] and [[ScareScraper]]. Slinker is the sixth kind of ghost fought in the game which first appears in the [[Garden Suites]]. Slinkers are similar to [[Ceiling Surprise]]s in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' and [[Sneaker]]s in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They have 120 HP.

== Profile ==
Slinkers are purple ghosts with long hands, white eyes and mouth and two tails like [[Hammer (enemy)|Hammers]].

== In ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' ==
[[File:Slinker on B2.jpg|thumb|220px|right|A Slinker on B2.]]
Slinkers attack [[Luigi]] by grabbing him from behind, dealing damage until Luigi uses a [[Burst]]. They will also become invisible and get behind Luigi to scare him like Sneakers. Luigi can find them with the [[Dark-Light Device]], in ScareScraper, Luigi can still see them while they are invisible if he has a pair of glasses. When a [[Toad]] is also in the room, Slinkers can use the Toad [File:as a shield and drag it into another room.

== Trivia ==
*Like Hammers which also have two tails, Slinkers can also be vacuumed by Luigi and [[Gooigi]] at the same time.
