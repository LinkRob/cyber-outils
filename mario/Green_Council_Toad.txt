The '''Green Council Toad''' is a minor character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. He is a tired Green Toad who eventually supports the [[Toad Council]], lead by their [[Toad General|general]]. He was forced to evacuate with the others along with their general, since Bowser is on his way.
