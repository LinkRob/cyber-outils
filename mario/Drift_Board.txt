The '''Drift Board''' is an item featured in [[Adventure Tours]] mode of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for [[Nintendo DS]].

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''==
At some point, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] had lost his Drift Board. Without it, neither Yoshi nor the other characters cannot participate in snowboarding missions.

At [[Cubyrinth]], [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] encounter [[Birdo]], who tells them that she found the Drift Board. Yoshi realizes that it is his exact Drift Board, so he asks Birdo to return the board to him. Birdo then goes on to say that only top athletes are worthy of it. To regain his snowboard, Yoshi has to compete in a [[Ski Cross Racing]] mission and win. Upon doing so, Yoshi retrieves his Drift Board.
