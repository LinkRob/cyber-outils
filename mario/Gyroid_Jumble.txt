'''Gyroid Jumble''' is one of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The microgame stars a [[gyroid]], originally from the ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' games. The player has to tilt the system until the gyroid lines up with the outline that has the same facial expression and arm placement, then press {{button|gba|A}} to join them. If the correct one is chosen, the gyroid is seen waving its arms up and down, but if the incorrect outline is chosen, the gyroid hits it and falls over, causing the player to lose at the microgame.

Level 1 features two outlines to choose from, Level 2 has three, and Level 3 has four.
