'''Kobatto''' are vampire bat enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. They are always encountered in groups of four.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Kobatto always fly around in a circular formation. If [[Wario]] touches a Kobatto, he becomes [[Vampire Wario (form)|Vampire Wario]]. Kobatto cannot be defeated.

==Trivia==
*While Kobatto does not reappear, a similar enemy appears in ''[[Wario Land 4]]'', [[Minicula]], but it instead turns Wario into [[Bat Wario]] upon contact.
