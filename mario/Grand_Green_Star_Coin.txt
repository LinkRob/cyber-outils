'''Grand Green Star Coin''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
A Grand Green Star Coin raises the Skill level of a teammate whose Skill level had been increased to level 4 using [[Green Star Coin]]s. This can apply to several teammates, including several of the [[Luigi]] forms, [[Spiny|Spinies]], and [[Bullet Bill]]s, [[Wiggler]]s, [[Chain Chomp]]s and [[Flame Chomp]]s as well as their respective variations.

A Grand Gold Mushroom can be obtained either from opening a [[? Block]] at random, winning it from the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] game in [[Toad House]]s, or from defeating either a "[[Flutter]] & Flame Chomp", "Chain Chomp & Flame Chomp," or a [[King Bill]] with a 10+ Combo attack.
