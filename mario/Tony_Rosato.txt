'''Tony Rosato''' was an Italian-Canadian person who provided the voice work of [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] in two [[DIC Entertainment]] cartoons, ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|Super Mario World]]'', following after [[Danny Wells]], who previously voiced Luigi in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''.

Aside from voicing Luigi, Rosato was also known for his involvement in ''{{w|Saturday Night Live}}''. Tony Rosato died from a heart attack on January 10, 2017.
