'''Koopa Bank Capsules''' are a type of [[item]] in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
Koopa Bank Capsules are yellow [[Orb|Capsule]]s represented with the appearance of a [[coin bag]]. A character can throw it onto a [[space]] to change it into a [[Bank Space]], where any opponent passing by has to pay five [[coin]]s to the [[Koopa Bank]], or all of their coins if they have only fewer than five coins. If either a character uses a Koopa Bank Capsule on themselves, or if a character's turn ends on the Koopa Bank Space, they obtain all of the coins from the Koopa Bank.
