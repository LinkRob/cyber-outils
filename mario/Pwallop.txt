A '''Pwallop''' is the blue variant of [[Protobatter]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. They are located in the [[Energy Hold]].

Like Protobatters, Pwallops attack by spitting out balls of batter, and hitting them with their club toward the bros., and creating a shockwave by slamming their clubs on the ground. The only difference is that Pwallops have a faster speed than its weaker counterpart, they use the shockwave attack a lot more often, and have more [[Heart Point|HP]].
