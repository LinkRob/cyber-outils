}}'''Prism Island''' is the main location of ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. This map is used to select each level, similar to the level selection in [[Super Mario Bros. 3|Super Mario Bros 3]]. This map is also where the [[Shy Bandit]] is fought, seen attempting to drain levels of their color.
[[File:PMCS Prism Island colorless.png|border|center|364x364px]]

== Regions ==
The island is first found drained of half its colors. As each [[Big Paint Star]] is collected, a new area is unlocked to find the next colored [[Big Paint Star]]. Prism Island has six unique regions in each of the colors of the rainbow. The following regions have these levels in them:
* Red region: [[Ruddy Road]], [[Cherry Lake]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Redpepper Volcano]], [[Redpepper Crater]]
* Yellow region: [[Daffodil Peak]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Château Chanterelle]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Mustard Café]]
* Blue region: [[Indigo Underground]], [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Dark Bloo Inn]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fort Cobalt]]
* Purple/violet region: [[Plum Park]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Lighthouse Island]], [[Fortune Island]]
* Orange region: [[Marmalade Valley]], [[Kiwano Temple]], [[Toad Trainworks]], [[Tangerino Grill]], [[The Sunset Express|Sunset Express]]
* Green region: [[Mondo Woods]], [[Sacred Forest]], [[Green Energy Plant]], [[Mossrock Theater]], [[The Emerald Circus|Emerald Circus]]
The only locations without specific regions are [[Port Prisma]] and [[Black Bowser's Castle]].
