'''Small pipes''' are a recurring object in the {{franchise|Mario}}, especially the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
Small pipes are a variation of [[Warp Pipe]]. Like their regular counterpart, small pipes have green as their default color. In ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', some of the small pipes are purple, and in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', aside from green small pipes, there are also red, blue, and yellow small pipes respectively.

In several of their appearances, small pipes only act as solid objects, and [[Mario]] and other characters normally cannot enter a small pipe because they are too large for it. In ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', with the first appearance of the [[Mini Mario]] form in a ''Super Mario'' platforming title, Mario or Luigi can enter most small pipes while in their Mini form.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
Small pipes first appear in the level [[World 2-1 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 2-1]], where they have a purple coloration and only act as decorative structures. In [[Pipe Land]], there are green small pipes, some of which have [[Muncher]]s appear from them.

===''[[Super Mario World]]''===
[[File:SMW Yoshi's Island 1 Pipe Cannon.png|thumb|200px|''Super Mario World'' screenshot of two small pipes supporting a Pipe Cannon]]
Small pipes act the same as in ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', although Munchers no longer spawn from them. Small pipes are occasionally seen supporting [[Pipe Cannon]]s.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''===
[[File:Small pipe NSMB sprite.png|frame|left]]
This is the first game in which small pipes are functional. [[Star Coin]]s are often hidden inside rooms that are only accessible from small pipes. Many small pipes contain shortcut rooms with coins that can be used to skip portions of the level.
{{br|left}}
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''===
[[File:Small pipe NSMBW sprite.png|frame|left]]
Small pipes function identically as in ''New Super Mario Bros.''
{{br|left}}
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''===
Small pipes retain their functionality from ''New Super Mario Bros.'' and ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''===
Small pipes retain their usual role from previous ''New Super Mario Bros.'' titles. One small pipe in particular is required to reach the secret exit in [[Grinding-Stone Tower]].

===''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
Small pipes only appear in the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' game style, as ground tiles in the forest and desert themes, in which they are colored green and purple respectively.

==In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''==
Small pipes appear as functional objects, but only if [[Dottie]] is used to shrink the character.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' / ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''==
A single small pipe appears along [[Bowser Path]]. It can only be entered by Mario using the [[Mini-Mario]] technique, and doing so is necessary to reach the [[? Block]] containing the [[POW Mush Jam]].

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
In the [[Yoshi's Island (Super Smash Bros. Melee)|Yoshi's Island]] stage introduced in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and later returning ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', there are small pipes supporting a larger diagonal Warp Pipe, similarly as in ''Super Mario World''.
