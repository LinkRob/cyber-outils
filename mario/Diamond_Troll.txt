The '''Diamond Troll''' is a one-time character who appears during [[Kat]] and [[Ana]]'s story in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Twisted!''==
During a field trip at [[Diamond Knoll]], when Kat and Ana try to flee from a swarm of bees, they run into a nearby hut. Unbeknownst to them, the hut is inhabited by the Diamond Troll, a monster with buff human arms and a Japanese-style mask. The player has to play through and complete Kat and Ana's [[microgame]] set for an end cutscene to play showing Kat and Ana's pets, [[Shadow the Dog|Shadow]] and [[Shuriken the Falcon|Shuriken]], arrive to help them defeat the Diamond Troll.
