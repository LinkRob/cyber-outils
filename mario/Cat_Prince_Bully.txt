'''Cat Prince Bully''' is a boss in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==In ''Bowser's Fury''==
Cat Prince Bully is fought at [[Mount Magmeow]], during the first mission.

Cat Prince Bully is fought the same as regular Prince Bully, and he is fought in a big box containing several [[Clear Pipe]]s. [[Mario]] has to push the Cat Prince Bully into a Clear Pipe, immobilizing him, and then has to kick him to damage Cat Prince Bully. After doing this three times, the Cat Prince Bully is defeated.
