A '''Beanstone''' is a collectible in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Beanstones only appear during the sidequest, so after [[Bowletta]] invades the [[Beanbean Kingdom]], the sidequest becomes unavailable.

== Description ==
It is a blue gemstone that flashes different colors. In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', it appears to flash in different colors, although the Beanstone is green in the inventory.

At the [[Beanbean Castle Town]], [[Fava]]'s brother, the town's oldest intellect, has a sidequest for [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to find him all of the ten Beanstones, which are buried underground around the town. In doing so, he rewards them the [[Bros. Rock Badge]].
