'''Subrella''' is a move in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Description==
Subrella can only be used while underwater, since it involves [[Perry]] transforming into a submarine and [[Princess Peach]] sitting inside of Perry. The player can use the {{button|ds|Pad}} to have Perry shoot [[bubble]]s that can defeat certain enemies by blowing into the Mic.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Subrella first appears in [[Wavy Beach 5-2]] and appears in a few other [[level]]s. There are two Subrella sections in [[Wavy Beach 5-5]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name "Subrella" is a portmanteau of "sub" or "submarine" and "umbrella."
