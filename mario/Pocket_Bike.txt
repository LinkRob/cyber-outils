The '''Pocket Bike''' is an object in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', where [[Penny Crygor]] shows it in the titular contest of "The Invent-Off."

== Description ==
The Pocket Bike is a small, pink motorbike. [[Wario]]'s face can be seen on the front, and a red zig-zag can be seen on the side.

== In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'' ==
During the events of the level [[The Invent-Off]], Penny Crygor and her [[Doctor Crygor|grandfather]] go head-to-head during the titular competition to see who is better. She pulls out the Pocket Bike which leads her to victory.

[[Wario]] later gets the Pocket Bike during his second stage, [[Forever Form Baton]], getting sucked into it and [[Tiny Wario|shrunken down]], though he reverts back to normal when the player completes the level. The bike later appears after Wario returns the Form Baton to the [[Temple of Form]], riding on for a bit before collapsing.
