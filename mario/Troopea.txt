The {{Nihongo|'''Troopea'''|マメノコ|mamenoko}} is an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and the ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|3DS remake]]''. They are the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] equivalent of a [[Koopa Troopa]].

== Profile ==
The Troopea's appearance is similar to the Koopa Troopa, but a green sprout sits on their head and they have blue shells. In the original game, they are quadruple, but in the remake, they are bipedal.

They also have stronger variants called [[Elite Troopea]], [[Paratroopea]], and [[Scaratroopea]]. Their undead variant may be the beanish [[Dry Bones]], although this is unlikely since those foes are quadrupeds, even in the remake.

=== Etymology ===
''Troopea'' is a combination of the words ''Troopa'' and ''pea'', hence being the Beanbean Kingdom equivalent of a Koopa Troopa.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ==
Troopeas are found in the [[Beanbean Outskirts]].

=== Battle ===
In battle, Troopeas only have two attacks, which is trying to ram into one of the brothers. However, Troopeas can also heal their allies in combat by launching a water droplet onto them. Troopeas recover their allies or themselves with a water droplet. They can dive under the Bros.. If the target does not jump, the target will deal damage.

== In ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'' ==
Troopeas appear as enemies in the side story of the remake, ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]''. They first appear in [[Turnip Tantrum]]. They fight as melee troopers, and their [[Special Skill]] is Rock Solid.
