'''World 3-Airship''' is the third [[airship]] level in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

== Layout ==
The player starts on the left edge of the [[Airship]], shortly afterwards there are some [[Rocky Wrench]]es and a [[Bill Blaster|Turtle Cannon]], followed by three [[cannon]]s and a [[? Block]] with a power-up. Next is another Turtle Cannon and more cannons, along with a [[Bolt Lift]] that the player can use to avoid two [[Burner|Rocket Engine]]s below. After this, there are more Rocky Wrenches and another Rocket Engine, followed by another ? Block containing a power-up and a [[Warp Pipe]] leading to Wendy's room.

The battle against Wendy takes place in a flat room, and she attacks by shooting magic blasts that bounce off the walls, the floor and the ceiling. After a single hit, Wendy will shoot another magic blast. After three hits, she is defeated, and will drop the [[Magic Wand]].
== Boss Battle ==
The battle against Wendy O. Koopa takes place in a flat room. She will first attack by shooting a magic blast, which will then bounce off the corners of the room and won't disappear until the player defeats Wendy. Wendy will then move around the room and try to hit the player so that they can be damaged (or lose a life if the player is [[Small Mario]]/Luigi). To defeat her, Mario/Luigi must jump on her three times. Every time she is jumped on, Wendy will summon another magic blast. Meaning that after two stomps on her, she would have summoned three magic blasts which are permanently there until the player finally stomps on her the third time. After she is defeated, the player must grab the wand and give it to the king. The player will then receive a letter from Princess Toadstool and can then move on to [[World 4-1 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 4-1]].
