'''Attack FX G''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Attack FX G is new attack FX badge so it does not cost any of [[Mario]]'s [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear. This attack FX badge changes Mario's attack's sounds into a slight giggle.

This badge is obtained by Mario when he purchases this badge from a shopkeeper named [[Charlieton]] in central [[Rogueport]]. This badge is on sale for a total of 120 [[coin]]s. After Mario has purchased this badge, he can wear it any time. Mario's partners, like in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', do not have their attacks have the sounds too.

In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], this Badge now changes the sound effects to sound like frogs and can be found in [[Hooktail Castle]] replacing the [[Attack FX R]] badge due to [[Hooktail]]'s weakness now being frogs rather than crickets like in the original. The giggle sound effect this badge provides was instead given to [[Attack FX P]] in the remake.
