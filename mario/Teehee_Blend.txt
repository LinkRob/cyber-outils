The '''Teehee Blend''' is a type of blend in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Usage==
The blend can be obtained in the [[Starbeans Cafe]]. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must have at least 25 [[Hee Bean]]s for the [[Starbeans Employee]] to create it. The blend boosts 4 [['Stache]] points, or 2 in the remake.
