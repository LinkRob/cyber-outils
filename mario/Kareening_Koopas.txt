'''Kareening Koopas''' is a recurring [[minigame]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' as a Story minigame and returns in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' as a Duel minigame.

==Gameplay==
The characters must tilt their panels to get the four [[Koopa Shell]]s into each of the four holes. The players must take their time to get the Koopa Shells into the holes fast enough. There are also small trees on the panels that serve as obstacles. Whoever gets in all of the Koopa Shells three times wins.
