The '''Elevator Key''' is a key item of a [[key]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
There are two different types of Elevator Keys; the first one is orange and resembles an actual key, while the second one is blue and resembles a [[Card Key]].

==History==
The first type of Elevator Key is found in a room of [[Riverside Station]] where [[Mario]] needs to use his newly required Spring Jump to get to it. The Elevator Key can then be used on the elevator back near the entrance where it leads him down into a room to activate the bridge. However, a bunch of [[Smorg|strange creatures]] were first seen down there.

The second Elevator Key can be found in a room of the [[X-Naut Fortress]]. It can be used to access the elevator of the fortress.
