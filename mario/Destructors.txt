The '''Destructors''' (also known as the '''Flying Bullets''') are a team that are members of the [[Glitz Pit]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. The team consists of two [[Spinia]]s and two [[Spania]]s.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
The Destructors are only fought when they replace [[Bandy Andy]]'s team after he "[[Power-draining machine|retires]]." The Destructors take the [[Hand-It-Overs]]' place of 14 should [[Mario]]'s rank drop down to that if he hasn't defeated [[Rawk Hawk]] for the first time yet. After Chapter 3 has been completed, Bandy Andy and his team take their place back and the Destructors are never seen again.

In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], Mario can request an Exhibition Match with the Destructors during Chapter 3 regardless of his current rank. This is due to the fact that it is no longer possible to move down a rank after losing to a lower ranked opponent which would've made fighting the Destructors impossible.

==Trivia==
*The Destructors are one of only two special teams that temporary replace another team due to the events of the chapter. The other being the [[Wings of Night]] who replace [[KP Pete|King K]]'s team.
