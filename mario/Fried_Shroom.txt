A '''Fried Shroom''' is an item featured in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Fried Shrooms are basic items that can heal a total of 6 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 2 [[Flower Point|FP]]. This item can be useful early in the game, but loses is vitality as Mario progress through his adventure. Mario can get this item via [[Tayce T.]] Tayce will give this item to [[Mario]] if he brings the right ingredients, namely a [[Mushroom]] or a [[Super Shroom]]. When cooked, a shroom can become a Fried Shroom. However, cooking a Super Shroom into a Fried Shroom will diminish it's value and effects.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Fried Shrooms are replaced by [[Shroom Fry]]. Shroom Fry restores the same amount of HP and FP as Fried Shrooms.
