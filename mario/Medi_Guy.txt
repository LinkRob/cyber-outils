'''Medi Guys''' are an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Medi Guys are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] that ride in small hovering vehicles with a symbol of a heart on them.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
Medi Guys appear only in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]. However, they cannot be found in the overworld of Shy Guy's Toy Box and, instead, can only be encountered in battles alongside other enemies. Medi Guys serve as the medic of the team, mainly healing itself or it's allies. However, Medi Guys can still attack by ramming their hovercraft into Mario. Of course, being air enemies, they can only be taken out with air attacks such as jumps, headbonks, or flying kicks.
