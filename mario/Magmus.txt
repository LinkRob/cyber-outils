A '''Magmus''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Magmuses are a variant of [[Buzzy Beetle]]s with a rock as its shell, and are mainly encountered in [[Barrel Volcano]]. Magmuses are a stronger variant of [[Magmite]]s.

==Battle==
Like with Magmites in battle, Magmuses mainly attack with charges. However, they can also use [[Valor Up]] to increase their high defense even more.

===Strategy===
Magmuses are weak against [[Ice (element)|ice]] attacks, so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] is very effective against them. This could prove useful against fighting a group of Magmuses.
