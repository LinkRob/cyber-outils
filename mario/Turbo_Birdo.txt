'''Turbo Birdo''' is an unlockable Medium weight [[kart]] which appears in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is [[Birdo]]'s signature [[kart]], and it has balanced stats. One advantage of Turbo Birdo is that it has the highest [[weight]] of all the medium weight karts, without decreasing its [[acceleration]]. Because of this, the Turbo Birdo is one of the most recommended medium weight karts to use. The Turbo Birdo can be unlocked by clearing the [[Engine Class|150cc]] [[Flower Cup]] in first place.

== Stats ==
* Class: Medium
* Speed: 3/5
* Accel: 3/5
* Weight: 4/5
