A '''Shell Block''' is an object which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. Jumping at one allows [[Mario]] to wear a [[Blue Shell]] up until he jumps out of it.

==In the games==
In the clouds high above [[Toad Town]] is a giant Shell Block that, upon being struck, releases and spreads several regular Shell Blocks all over the overworld (similar to when activating a [[! Switch]] at the end of a [[Switch Palace]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'').

When this occurs, Mario and [[Luigi]] are confronted by the [[Blue Paratroopas]]. The brothers come up with a purpose for the Shell Block in that while Mario is in a Blue Shell, Luigi can use his [[hammer]] to whack Mario forward in his Blue Shell, allowing to hit certain otherwise unreachable objects, followed by Mario spinning back in the Blue Shell like a boomerang.
