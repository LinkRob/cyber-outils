A '''Dark Headbonk Goomba''' is a dark variant of a [[Headbonk Goomba]] that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They have 10 HP and 4 Attack. They only appear in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]] in room 95. Just like their standard counterpart, they attack by jumping in the air and doing a [[headbonk]].
