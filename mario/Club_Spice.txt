'''Club Spice''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Description==
Club Spice is a dance club located in [[Diamond City]]. It is where [[Jimmy P.]] frequents to go dancing, unlike [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]], who instead dances at [[Club Sugar]]. The two clubs are very similar, owing to how Jimmy P. and Jimmy T. are doppelgangers of each other, but unlike Club Sugar, which is a nightclub, Club Spice is open during the daytime.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
Club Spice has a large role in Jimmy P.'s stage, [[Canine Crazed]]. In the opening cutscene, the [[Legendary Dog Dancers]] follow Jimmy P. into Club Spice, where they dance with him. The intermission segments while the player plays through the microgames take place on the dance floor in Club Spice. In the ending cutscene, Jimmy P. and the Legendary Dog Dancers all leave Club Spice together.

==Trivia==
*Club Spice, in conjunction with Club Sugar, is derived from "sugar and spice and everything nice," a phrase originating from the nursery rhyme "{{w|What Are Little Boys Made Of?}}."
