'''Lethal Bob-ombs''' are variants of [[Bob-omb]]s that appear in the cellar of [[Shroob Castle]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Lethal Bob-ombs resemble their weaker counterparts, but their fuses are always lit. Additionally, they are constantly flashing, wear purple shoes like [[Rob-omb]]s, and are faster than Bob-ombs. If they touch a playable character, they explode. They can only be fought by [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]]. They are the last regular enemy introduced in the game.

In battle, Lethal Bob-ombs attack by approaching the Baby Mario Bros., and then run back and forth in front of them. It then rams into one of the Baby Bros., self-destructing, causing damage and possibly [[Dizzy|dizziness]]. The attack can be countered using [[Hammer]]s. As with their weaker counterparts, Lethal Bob-ombs resist fire and cold damage.
