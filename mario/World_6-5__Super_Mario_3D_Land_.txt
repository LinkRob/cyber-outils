'''World 6-5 '''is the thirty-second level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
World 6-5 is an ice-based level. The beginning of the level features a group of mushroom-like platforms that bounce [[Mario]] high up the mountainside. The second portion of the level contains two mushroom platforms along a huge icy brick wall. A top-down view is given throughout this portion of the level and is needed to see Mario's positioning with the moving mushroom platforms. Past these platforms, Mario finds the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]]. Along this path, another section just like the one before lies ahead, but this time features more wall-kick jumps.

== Enemies ==
Enemies that appeared in World 6-5:
* [[Paragoomba]]
* [[Goomba]]
* [[Goomba Tower]]

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] is under a stone platform found near the first the moving mushroom platform.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is found at the top of a pair of wooden walls. Mario must wall-kick to rise to the top.
*'''Star Medal 3''': Right after the second Star Medal, Mario can climb upward to find the third and final Star Medal.
