'''World 6-A''' is the first special course of [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. This course is unlocked when getting to the secret exit in {{world-link|6|ghost|World 6-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}}. 
==Layout==
The player starts on a platform next to a [[Podoboo]], and must enter a red pipe to access the level; it takes them to an area with five more Podoboos followed by several groups of coins further down. In the next section, the [[lava]] will begin to rise as soon as the player begins moving to the right. The area contains several coins along with [[Dry Bones]], Podoboos and [[Super Dry Bones]]. Near the end, there is a [[? Block]] that contains a [[Starman]] inside, followed by a red pipe that leads to the exit and the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' The Star Coin is in plain sight in the air. The player must jump on the Dry Bones or off the platform just ahead to reach it.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' The second Star Coin is found shortly after the first above a gap in the player's path.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' After the second, there is a platform below that moves up when stepped on. Past it is a Super Dry Bones that can be used to reach the third Star Coin. [[Raccoon Mario]] or Raccoon Luigi can also fly up to the platform.
