The '''Great Fairy of Victory''' is a non-playable character who appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]] and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. She is a reference to the [[w:c:zelda:Great Fairy|Great Fairies]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' games. In both of her appearances, the Great Fairy of Victory is found at the pond in the [[Training Grounds]] of the [[Royal Tennis Academy]].
