'''Dream Depot''' is the hub location of ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. It is where all dreams in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] go to.

== In ''Mario Party 5'' ==
The [[Star Spirits]] guard the Dream Depot. During the game's story, [[Bowser]] attempts to take over the depot and make the inhabitants of the Mushroom Kingdom have the inability to dream. The Star Spirits request [[Mario]] and his friends to prevent this from happening. Mario and his friends chase Bowser across six dreams, which is the [[Toy Dream]], [[Rainbow Dream]], [[Pirate Dream]], [[Undersea Dream]], [[Future Dream]] and [[Sweet Dream]], before reaching Bowser's dream, [[Bowser Nightmare]].
