'''Game Boy Advance - Nintendo Classics''', formerly referred to as '''Game Boy Advance - Nintendo Switch Online''', is an application for the [[Nintendo Switch]] and [[Nintendo Switch 2]]. For the former, it was released alongside [[Game Boy - Nintendo Classics|Game Boy - Nintendo Switch Online]] on February 8, 2023 in the Americas and February 9, 2023 in most other regions, shortly following its announcement in the February 2023 Nintendo Direct; it was later released on September 9, 2025 in Taiwan.<!-- The app may officially launch in Malaysia, the Philippines, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, and Thailand in the near future as Nintendo Switch Online is confirmed for these markets. --> It features a library of [[Game Boy Advance]] games and is exclusive to the [[Nintendo Switch Online|Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack]] subscription. Multiplayer-compatible games such as ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' can be played with other Nintendo Switch devices locally or online.<ref>Nintendo of America (February 8, 2023). [https://youtu.be/_-KAU3bK1Y8 Nintendo Switch Online - Game Boy & Game Boy Advance Announcement - Nintendo Direct 2.8.23]. ''YouTube''. Retrieved February 8, 2023.</ref>

Games that had [[Game Boy Player]]-specific features such as rumble support, including ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', retain those features in this application, unlike on the [[Virtual Console]] for [[Nintendo 3DS]] and [[Wii U]].

The app's version 3.0.0 update changed the app's subtitle from "Nintendo Switch Online" to "Nintendo Classics". It also added button remapping, updated the "Reproduce classic feel" screen filter, and changed the app's startup splash screen to use the sound effect of a [[coin]] from ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'', rather than the Game Boy Advance's startup logo, to match with the other Nintendo Classics apps. Like the [[Nintendo GameCube - Nintendo Classics]] app, the Game Boy Advance's original startup screen can be seen by holding any direction on {{button|ns|leftstick}} upon startup. On the Nintendo Switch 2, the application now runs at a resolution of 1080p in handheld mode. There were no changes for resolution in TV mode and the controls page still shows a Joy-Con or a Nintendo Switch Pro Controller even when a Joy-Con 2 or a Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller is connected, unlike [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Classics]] onward.

==Default button mapping==
Despite the fact that only one controller can be connected to the app at a time and multiplayer requires each player to own a console, the app allows players to use a single [[Joy-Con]].
