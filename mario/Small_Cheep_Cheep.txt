'''Small Cheep Cheeps''' are a small variant of [[Cheep Cheep]] introduced in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Small Cheep Cheeps have the same appearance as regular Cheep Cheeps, except they have been shrunk by [[Kamek]]'s magic.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario: Color Splash''===
Small Cheep Cheeps are found in the [[Sacred Forest]], where they hop out of the rivers across the bridges, and can engage [[Mario]] in battle through this method. One of the Small Cheep Cheeps is also holding one of the five [[Paint Star Piece|pieces]] of the [[Mini Paint Star]]. A Small Cheep Cheep has a [[Battle Card]], numbered 3.

In battle, Small Cheep Cheeps are not that strong and only attack by leaping at Mario, any attack is strong enough to bring one down as they have 10 HP and 1 Attack Power.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''===
Small Cheep Cheeps are commonly found in all three fishing holes, [[Overlook Mountain]], [[Shogun Studios]], and [[Full Moon Island]], but they are not fought as enemies.

==Trivia==
*With their size, Small Cheep Cheeps somewhat resemble [[Baby Cheep]]s. However, unlike Baby Cheeps, Small Cheep Cheeps can take leaps out of the water and are not protected by [[Big Bertha (fish)|Big Berthas]].
