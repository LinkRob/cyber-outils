'''The Glohm Dodging Slippenglider''' is a minor character from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']], making his home in [[Slippenglide Island]]'s village. He can be encountered on the north side of the village.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Glohm Dodging Slippenglider is a generic Slippenglider Concordian with a cyan face and limbs. He has an ice crystal protruding from the top of his head, where regular Concordians have short cables.

=== Personality ===
The [[Glohm]] infection that plagued the village of [[Slippenglide Island]] caused the Glohm Dodging Slippenglider to become fearful. This in turn made him partially hostile towards [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] when they encountered him. When they explained that they are friendly, he found out that he was mistaken, driving him to apologise. The Slippenglider maintains his people's pun-making habit, but drops it in serious situations. He is also friendly and approachable when not scared. He also has a brave side, spying on the enemy's hideout after the Glohm rampage that plagued Slippenglide Island.

== History ==
The Glohm Dodging Slippenglider is first seen cowering at the north end of his village. When Mario and Luigi approach him, the Slippenglider freaks out and begs them not to attack him with a [[Glohm]] injector (referred to by the Slippenglider as a "zappy thing"). Mario and Luigi explain themselves, and the Slippenglider apologises for his hostility, explaining that it was due to someone carrying the device infecting others. He got scared and hid so he would not get caught and infected. The Slippenglider then spied on the mysterious man's hideout, which turns out to be a research laboratory separated from Slippenglide village by a lake.
