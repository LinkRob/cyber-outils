'''Blooming Lakitus''', or '''Layer-Cake Desert-6''', is the sixth course of [[Layer-Cake Desert]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked by passing either [[Spike's Spouting Sands]] or [[Dry Desert Mushrooms]]. Passing this level unlocks [[Morton's Compactor Castle]]. This is also the location of the second Nabbit chase, triggered by completing [[Waterspout Beach|Waterspout Beach]]. 

==Layout==
The level begins near a group of palm trees with two [[? Block]]s above them. The first one contains either a [[Super Mushroom]] or an [[Ice Flower]], depending on the player's form. A group of rolling hills is found, and two [[Piranha Plant]]s are on them. A [[Lakitu]] that tosses [[Piranha Plant Egg]]s that spawn Piranha Plants when grounded also appears. Two [[Jump Block]]s appear, along with a [[Koopa Troopa]]. Another Koopa Troopa is found on a [[Brick Block]] structure, which is followed by a group of Note Blocks, along with a Piranha Plant in front of a hidden area containing [[coin]]s. Another group of three Note Blocks is found, with the third one containing a [[Starman]]. More rolling hills and Piranha Plants are found, along with a structure made of Brick Blocks and ? Blocks. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is found in a group of palm trees. More rolling hills and Piranha Plants are found, along with Note Blocks used to reach a [[Red Ring]]. A [[Big Piranha Plant]] is found at the end of the hills. More rolling hills are found, and a [[POW Block]] on a platform can be used to cause a group of coins to fall. After a group of Piranha Plants, a Koopa Troopa is found on a platform. A hidden [[Roulette Block]] can also be found. After a group of Piranha Plants, Big Piranha Plants, and rolling hills, the [[Goal Pole]] is found.

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is found in mid-air, above a structure of [[Brick Block]]s.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Above the section of the course with the POW Block is a [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the second Star Coin, which can be reached using a [[Lakitu's Cloud]].
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is found in the middle of a series of Big Piranha Plants on rolling hills at the end of the level.
