'''Vanilla Lake 2''' is the fourth course in [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. Similar to the [[Vanilla Lake 1|previous course]], it is an icy and winter-themed track that loops around an icy lake. Like the Vanilla Lake 1, there is much ice that can make the kart  slippery like thick snow, pipes, water, and ice blocks.

The course returns in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' in the Extra Special Cup.
