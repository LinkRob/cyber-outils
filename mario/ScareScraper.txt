The '''ScareScraper''' (known as the '''Thrill Tower''' in the British English version of ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'') is the multiplayer mode of ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' and ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]'' that is unlocked by completing the fourth mission, Visual Tricks, in the main game. The mode returns in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. It can be played online, locally, or alone. Its name is a portmanteau of "scare" and "skyscraper".

In ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' and ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]'' the ScareScraper is located in the [[Evershade Valley]] and in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' [[Professor E. Gadd]] tells Luigi that it's located near [[The Last Resort]]. They also look completely different externally and internally and host different ghost types, one native to the Evershade Valley and one to the Last Resort. It is unknown if they're same building or if they just share the same name.

== [[Luigi's Mansion 3]] ==
=== Description ===
[[Professor Elvin Gadd]] told [[Luigi]] that there is a tower near [[The Last Resort]] filled with ghosts which is ScareScraper. Luigi can play with three other plaers to explore ScareScraper when using Nintendo Switch Online, you can choose if you want to play with twenty floors, ten floors mod or five floors mod. The boss is on the roof. There will be different missions on each floor.
