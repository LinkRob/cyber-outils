'''To the Top of the Fortress''' is the second mission of [[Whomp's Fortress]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', its [[Nintendo DS|NDS]] [[Reissue|remake]], ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and its 2021 enhanced port, ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]''. The objective is for Mario to climb up a tall tower on the peak of Whomp's Fortress and collect a [[Power Star]] at the top of it.

== Description ==
This level is unchanged between ''Super Mario 64'' and its remake. The second Power Star is located on top of a tall tower which has appeared after the Whomp King's defeat. When Mario has climbed up to the flat platform as before, he will find a [[Bill Blaster]] shooting [[Banzai Bill]]s at him ([[Bullet Bill]]s in the remake), making it the only appearance of Banzai Bills in the entire game (in the remake the Bullet Bills have more appearances). Mario has to use ledges around the tower to get to the top and to get the Power Star. Under certain conditions, [[Hoot the Owl|Hoot]] can be used to easily fly to the top get this Power Star. He can also use the cannon to fly up to the platform which rises to the top of the tower, but only if he's completed the mission after this one. Additionally, Yoshi and Luigi’s high jumping skills can be used to reach the top of the tower faster.
