A '''Star Stage Space''' is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Star Stage Spaces exclusively appear on the board [[Star-Crossed Skyway]], somewhat having the role of the [[Special Event Space|Dead-End Space]]s in other boards. If a character lands on one, they receive a number of [[Mini Star]]s or [[Mini Ztar]]s from whoever is at front of the Star Stage. Whenever all but one character has landed on a Star Stage Space, the last character is carried to the Star Stage Space by a [[Fishin' Lakitu]].
