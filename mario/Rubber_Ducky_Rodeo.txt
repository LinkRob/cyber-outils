'''Rubber Ducky Rodeo''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. It takes place in a giant pool of water.

==Gameplay==
One player controls a large rubber ducky, while the other three contestants try to maintain their balance on smaller duckies attached to the larger duck in a row. The solo player wins if all three team players fall off their ducks, while the team players win if any of them sustain their balance until time is up.

==Ending==
The winning side is shown celebrating while standing on a rubber ring with a large rubber duck with a crown and two smaller ducks near the ring.

==Controls==
===Solo (1 player)===
*{{button|Padleftright}} &ndash; Steer and veer

===Group (3 players)===
*{{button|Padleftright}} &ndash; Balance on your ducky

==In-game text==
===Solo (1 player)===

===Group (3 players)===

*'''Tips''' &ndash; ''"Keep an eye on (opponent)'s ducky. When the driver turns, change your seating to stay balanced on your ducky."''

==Trivia==
*Despite how fast the large rubber duck goes, reaching the pool's edge is impossible.
