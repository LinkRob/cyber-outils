'''Slime Drops''' (originally known as '''Lemon Drops''')<ref name=guide>''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Nintendo Player's Guide. Page 127.</ref> are enemies introduced in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|its reissue]]. They resemble gooey lemon drops.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
[[File:Lemon Drop.png|frame|left]]
In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', Lemon Drops emerge from the ceiling. Upon hitting the ground, a Lemon Drop starts to slowly move toward a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. A Lemon Drop can be defeated either by being swallowed, [[stomp]]ed, or being hit by an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]. Lemon Drops are invulnerable to [[spike]]s, which they can land on without taking any damage.

Lemon Drops are found in only a few [[level]]s. They are especially prominent in [[Salvo The Slime's Castle]], where they break off [[Salvo the Slime]] when he is hit. They also appear in [[Prince Froggy's Fort]].

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', Slime Drops appear in the level [[Slime Drop Drama]]. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can defeat Slime Drops using the same tactics as before. Slime Drops can now move around in [[water]].

===''Mario Party 4''===
Lemon Drops seem to appear in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' in [[Slime Time]]. They appear as small blue drops instead of yellow.

===''Nintendo Puzzle Collection''===
In ''[[Nintendo Puzzle Collection]]'', yellow and blue Slime Drops cascade down the screen when a stage of Round 3 is cleared in the Action Mode of ''Yoshi no Cookie''.

==Profiles==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
*'''Shogakukan guide:''' 天井らポタッと落ちてくることがある。頭上に注意。<ref name=Sho>「スーパーマリオヨッシーアイランド任天堂公式ガイドブック」 (''Super Mario: Yossy Island Nintendo Kōshiki Guidebook''), page 8.</ref> (''Sometimes they fall from the ceiling. Be careful overhead.'')
*'''[[Nintendo Power|Player's Guide]]:''' ''Tasty morsels that drop from the ceilings, Lemon Drops help Yoshi replenish his egg supply.''<ref name=guide/>

====''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
*'''Shogakukan guide:''' 天井からボタッと落ちてくることがある。プチプチくんと同じように、ふみつけると周囲の敵を少しだけはじき飛ばせる。<ref name=Advance>「スーパーマリオアドバンス3任天堂公式ガイドブック」 (''Super Mario Advance 3 Nintendo Kōshiki Guidebook''), page 17.</ref> (''Sometimes they fall from the ceiling. Like [[Milde]]s, they can send surrounding enemies flying a little when you step on them.'')

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
*'''Shogakukan guide:''' 天井から水滴のように落ちてきて、地面に着地するとこちらに近づいてくる。決まった場所に落ちてくるので、出現場所には長居しないようにすること。<ref name=YNI>「ヨッシー New アイランド 任天堂公式ガイドブック」 (''Yoshi's New Island Nintendo Kōshiki Guidebook''), page 20.</ref> (''They fall from the ceiling like water drops and approach you when they land on the ground. They fall at a fixed location, so do not stay at the location where they appear for a long time.'')

==Trivia==
*The Slime Drop's original name, "Lemon Drop" references the Slime Drops' bright yellow color and their behavior of dropping from ceilings (as well as being simply small blobs of liquid), creating an additional reference to the candy of the same name.
