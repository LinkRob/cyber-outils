'''Scratch 'Em''' is a minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:MPA Scratch Em Values.png|thumb|left|200px|The picture match values]]
The player's character has to bet [[coin]]s on the possibility of getting a match in a scratch-off game. After placing a bet, the player's character has to scratch off five spaces on the card, and the player's character wins coins equal to the number given for each set. However, there is a possibility of scratching a blank space, which reveals nothing, and a [[Bob-omb]], which explodes, causing all adjacent spaces to become blank. In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], the player wins if they obtain one more coin than what they began with, which is 30. The game also ends whenever the player runs out of coins to wager.

The picture match values are as follows:
*Three [[Green Shell]]s: x1
*Four Green Shells: x5
*Five Green Shells: x20
*Three [[Mushroom]]s: x5
*Four Mushrooms: x10
*Five Mushrooms: x50
*Three [[Star]]s: x10
*Four Stars: x30
*Five Stars: x100

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|Pad}}: Change coins
*{{button|gba|A}}: Start game
*{{button|gba|A}}: Scratch card
*{{button|gba|L}} / {{button|gba|R}}: Next screen

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Scratch 'Em is played at the [[Town Game Room A]] in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] for the [[Winners Keepers]] quest. Here [[Monty Mole (character)|Monty Mole]] is on a losing streak, and he wishes for the player to win the minigame on his behalf. If the player wins, they unlock the minigame.
