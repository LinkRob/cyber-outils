'''Rich with Ropes''' is a [[level]] of [[World-e]] in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
A lot of the level involves climbing up [[rope]]s. A few of the enemies are from ''[[Super Mario World]]'', including [[Wiggler]]s, [[Galoomba]]s, and [[Para-Goomba (Super Mario World)|Para-Goombas]], and one of them is from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', [[Hoopster]]s.
