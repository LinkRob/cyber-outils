'''Punkey the Pokey Prince''' is a boss in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Punkey attacks by shooting [[Needlenose]]s out of the plant on his head. He can also ram into [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].

==In ''Yoshi's New Island''==
Punkey is fought as the boss of [[World 5 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 5]], in [[Punkey the Pokey Prince's Castle]]. Here, [[Kamek]] is seen changing Punkey from a normal [[Pokey]] into his enlarged form.

During the boss fight against Punkey, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can hit his segments with [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] when spikes are not protracting from them. Punkey's vulnerable spot is its head, and hitting it angers Punkey, who then starts to drill into Yoshi. Once he is done, Punkey adds another segment to his body. After taking another hit in the head, Punkey adds another segment to his body. After a third and final hit, Punkey is defeated.
