A '''Pounder''' is a hammer-like enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They work for the [[Smithy Gang]] in [[The Factory]]. Pounders also assist the [[Manager]] in battle. Pounders have a stronger variant named [[Poundette]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Pounders mainly attack by pounding at one of [[Mario]]'s party members. They can also deal high damage by tossing hammers at a party member.

===Strategy===
Pounders are weak to [[Thunder (element)|thunder]] attacks, so any of [[Mallow]]'s thunder techniques are effective against them. Pounders are often found in groups, so using [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] is a better strategy.
