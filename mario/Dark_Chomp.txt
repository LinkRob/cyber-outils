'''Dark Chomps''' are pitch black [[Chain Chomp]]s that appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]].

==History==
Dark Chomps have their HP and Defense stats matching with them both being 10 total. They attempt to bite [[Mario]] and co. when they approach them, but get pulled back if they aren't close by. They can be found in the Flopside Pit of 100 Trials rooms 52, 61 and 65.
