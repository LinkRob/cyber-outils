A '''Whirlpool''' is an object which appears in a handful of ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, especially within the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. A Whirlpool first appears in ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]'', but only in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version. While whirlpools are not a hazardous obstacle in ''Mario's Time Machine'', many later appearances feature whirlpools as such; in many later appearances, if a Whirlpools sucks in [[Mario]] or another character, they lose a life.

==In ''Mario's Time Machine''==
A whirlpool appears during the surfing minigame, after Mario has collected ten [[Mushroom]]s. Mario has to enter the whirlpool to travel to the time period that he selected on the [[Timulator]] beforehand. The whirlpool only appears in the Super Nintendo version, as the PC and MS-DOS versions instead transport Mario automatically after he has collected four Mushrooms.

==In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''==
[[File:Whirlpool SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
By touching a whirlpool, Mario can go to the bottom of the water.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario 64]]''/''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''===
A whirlpool is featured in the center of the first area of [[Dire, Dire Docks]], around the bottom of the water. If Mario (or [[Luigi]], [[Wario]], or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in the remake) are sucked into the whirlpool, they lose a life.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''===
Whirlpools only appear in [[World 6-5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-5]]. Here, whirlpools have a pattern of repeatedly appearing and then disappearing. If Mario or Luigi is caught in a whirlpool, the player must press the A button repeatedly to prevent them from being sucked in, otherwise they lose a life. An exception to this is one particular whirlpool that takes Mario or Luigi down to a hidden area with the third [[Star Coin]].

===''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''===
Whirlpools appear in both the [[Sea Slide Galaxy]] and the [[Bigmouth Galaxy]]. They are not a stage hazard, and instead have the opposite effect of pushing Mario or Luigi forward.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''===
Like in ''New Super Mario Bros.'', whirlpools appear in only one level, specifically [[World 3-2 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3-2]]. They function the same as in ''New Super Mario Bros.''.
