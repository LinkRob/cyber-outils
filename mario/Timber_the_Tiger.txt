'''Timber the Tiger''' is a playable character in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]'' who has orange fur, is a tiger as its name states, and has a blue cap with the [[Rare]] symbol on it which later turned into a [[Nintendo]] symbol due to Rare's move to Microsoft. Timber's parents left him in charge of their hometown, [[Timber's Island]] when [[Wizpig]] invaded the island and Timber and his friends had to stop Wizpig and kick him off Timber's Island. His theme instrument is the harmonica according to the opening of ''Diddy Kong Racing''.
