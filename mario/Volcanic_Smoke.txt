The '''Volcanic Smoke '''is an obstacle first introduced in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]] . ''back in 2009. It appeared in [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]] as a reminder to get going.

==Objective==
The point of the''' Volcanic Smoke '''is to add difficulty to the [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]] levels of [[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]] . It chases the players. If they players aren't fast enough. The toxic ash consumes them. Killing them instantly or making them lose their power-up.

==In Other Games==
Volcanic Smoke later appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. When the player collects the five Key Coins in [[Trick Trap Tower]] in [[World 7 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 7]], the tower will start to collapse, leaking volcanic smog, forcing the player to evacuate using the [[Warp Block]] that is activated by the Key Coins and lies at the bottom.
