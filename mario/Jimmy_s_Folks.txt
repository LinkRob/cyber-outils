'''Jimmy Folks''' are a group of characters in the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' series, in which they first appear in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. They are [[Jimmy T.]]'s family members, and like Jimmy, they share a common love for dancing. All of them also sport afro hair, with each one having theirs a different color.
