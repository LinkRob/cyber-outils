{{DISPLAYTITLE:List of glitches in ''Luigi's Mansion 3''}}
This article shows a list of glitches in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' for the [[Nintendo Switch]].

==Glitches==
==={{Conjecturaltext|E. Gadd Circling}}===
After [[Luigi]] rescued [[Professor Elvin Gadd]] with the [[Dark-Light Device]], E. Gadd might start circling in a specific spot, which causes him not following Luigi. The glitch will be fixed if Luigi waits for a while or leave the room. The cause of the glitch is still unknown.<ref name="soag">https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GBPXcuXtCEQ</ref>

==={{Conjecturaltext|E. Gadd Flips Out of The Room}}===
Luigi is possible to stuck E. Gadd by a light in front of the painting after rescuing E. Gadd, this causes E. Gadd can't follow Luigi for a moment. If Luigi gets out of the room, E. Gadd will flip out of the room instantly to follow Luigi.<ref name="soag" />

==={{Conjecturaltext|Polterpup stuck on Luigi}} (verson 1.1.0 only) ===
This glitch only happens in version 1.1.0, it occurs after Luigi beating the first [[Goob]]s in the Grand Lobby. When the player sucks up the last Goob and Polterpup appears at the last moment teaching Luigi how to capture ghosts, he will get stuck on Luigi. This glitch is patched.<ref name="soag" />

==={{Conjecturaltext|Immortal Boolossus}}===
On some occasions in [[ScareScraper]] during the [[Boolossus]] battle, it is possible to have a Boo which is unable to be slammed and freeze in its place after Boolossus explodes. The glitch causes the battle to be unbeatable.<ref name="soag" />

==={{Conjecturaltext|Serpci's Cobra glitch}}===
During [[Serpci]]'s battle, the glitch occurs when Serpci appears with six sand cobras. If Luigi touches the cobra in front of Serpci, the sand cobra will remain in front of Serpci. It will stay during the boss battle and after Serpci's defeat.<ref name="soag" />

==={{Conjecturaltext|Clem fighting Luigi on The Ground}}===
While fighting Clem, this glitch might occur if Luigi sucks Clem's ducky tube after making him dizzy and let the bomb blow Luigi to the ground. Luigi then will fly to the ground grabbing Clem, soon as Clem hits Luigi again, they will both sit on their ducky tube on the ground.<ref name="soag" />

==={{Conjecturaltext|No Boo Music}}===
For some unknown reasons, there might be no [[Boo]] music even when Luigi gets into the room the Boo is hiding in. This causes it harder to find the Boo and Luigi might have to buy a [[Boo Finder]] from E. Gadd to find the Boo.

==={{Conjecturaltext|E. Gadd T-Pose}}===
Sometimes when E. Gadd calls Luigi, E. Gadd will be in a T-pose for a short moment.[[File:E. Gadd T-pose.jpg|thumb|E. Gadd T-posing.]]

==={{Conjecturaltext|Reactivate Red Toad after Amadeus Wolfgeist}}===
In The Great Stage, it is possible for the [[Toad]] to appear again in the painting and also safe in E. Gadd's lab long after [[Amadeus Wolfgeist]] has been defeated. If you use the Dark-Light Device to flash the painting again, the Toad will vanish from the painting.

==={{Conjecturaltext|Musicless Chef Soulfflé battle}}===
For some unknown reasons, there might be no music when Luigi is fighting [[Chef Soulfflé]]. This glitch is common which occurred in many videos.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NrfU1AiC6GQ</ref>

==={{Conjecturaltext|King MacFrights' invisible audience}}===
This glitch seldom appears, but it still appears sometimes. When Luigi reaches the battle arena of [[King MacFrights]] and triggers the cutscene, there might be only cheering sounds but no audience watching King MacFrights and Luigi. There will also be booing and cheering sounds without audience during the battle.

[[File:Luigi's Mansion 3 King MacFrights Boss Fight-0|thumb|right|335 px|The glitch occurred in this video.]]

==={{Conjecturaltext|Hellen Gravely's broken televisions}}===
If you already fought the boss but you load back to refight the boss, you will notice that you are able to skip the cutscenes. If you skip [[Hellen Gravely]]'s cutscene, you will find out that her screens won't turn over and she won't appear on the screen either. But you can still hear her laughing at Luigi.

==={{Conjecturaltext|Twisted Suites glitch}}===
In Twisted Suites, for some unknown reasons, the game will crash if Luigi shoots a fish into the rabbit cage.

==={{Conjecturaltext|Ghosts' stay when their HP are zero.}}===
In ScareScraper, sometimes when Luigi slams a ghost causing its HP is zero, the ghost might stay lying on the floor screaming and panicking. Luigi can leave the ghost and stand beside, the ghost will then vanish as if it's sucked up by the air.
==={{Conjecturaltext|Toad stuck beside the television}}===
In ScareScraper during the saving Toad mission, for some unknown reasons, the Toad might stuck beside the television which causes the players can't pass the level. The glitch is patched in the update during 2020.
==={{Conjecturaltext|Dr. Potter's camera glitch}}===
For some reasons, the camera might show an out of bounds area during the battle with [[Dr. Potter]], the glitch is patched in the update in 2020.

==={{Conjecturaltext|Skipping Amadeus Wolfgeist}}===
There is a glitch that's discovered after the update, if Luigi is fighting a boss, normally all the doors in the room are blocked. However, the little door on the stage where [[Amadeus Wolfgeist]] is playing his piano isn't blocked, this causes the player to be able to get into the room. In order to get into the room, Luigi has to stand right beside the stage and [[Gooigi]] has to flash him, Luigi will then walk backwards and will be glitched onto the stage. He can now enter the room and Gooigi has to defeat the Goobs, and Luigi will fall into the small room when the Amadeus getting mad cutscene starts, Luigi can now exit the room and to the main menu after exiting and saving, then load back again, the system will be now fooled and think that you've defeated Amadeus and the button will pop out. But the player will not able to get an "A" in the ending because Amadeus won't appear in the gallery, unlike [[Director Morty]], he can never be captured then. When Luigi is glitched onto the stage, it is unknown that if Luigi can flash Amadeus when he is still playing the piano.

==={{Conjecturaltext|Skipping the entire Castle MacFrights}}===
In the Castle MacFrights, it is possible to get to King MacFrights without triggering any cutscene. Just do the flashing Luigi glitch and Luigi will get through the door and be able to walk down the stairs to the arena.

==={{Conjecturaltext|Entering the kitchen early}}===
After getting the [[Suction Shot]], it is possible to enter the restaurant on the second floor before beating Kruller, Luigi is also able to enter the kitchen, you can see that the kitchen is already filled with smoke by looking from outside. But then you will find out that the kitchen actually isn't filled with smoke at all after getting in, Chef Soulfflé is not there either.
