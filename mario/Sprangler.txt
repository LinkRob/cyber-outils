'''Spranglers''' are a type of spider enemy featured in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Profile==
=== Behavior ===
Spranglers are the web-hanging equivalents of [[Spoing]]s and they attack by trying to swing themselves into [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]. They can be defeated from either being [[jump]]ed onto or being [[Star Spin|spun]] at twice.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
They are commonly found in the [[Space Junk Galaxy]] (where their leader [[Tarantox]] lives).
{{sectstub}}

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
Spranglers are now encountered in [[Yoshi Star Galaxy]] and [[Tall Trunk Galaxy]].
