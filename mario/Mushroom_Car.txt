'''Mushroom Cars''' are a type of [[car]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.

==Description==
Mushroom Cars are three-wheeled pick-up trucks shaped like a [[Mushroom]]. Unlike other cars, Mushroom Cars do not spin out the racer upon being bumped into but rather briefly halts their movement. A Mushroom Car serves as helpful, unlike most [[traffic]], because bumping into one causes a [[Dash Mushroom|Mushroom]] to launch from it and onto the road. Bumping into a Mushroom Car plays the same sound as when a power-up item emerges from a [[? Block]], specifically referencing when a [[Super Mushroom]] emerges from one.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''===
[[File:Mushroom Car.png|thumb|left|200px|''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'' screenshot]]
on the main streets of the [[Mushroom Bridge]] and the [[Mushroom City]] courses from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. In Time Trial mode, Mushroom Cars appear in neither course, likely to prevent the characters from obtaining extra Mushrooms.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
Mushroom Cars make a reappearance in {{classic course|GCN|Mushroom Bridge}}, following an absence from said course in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.
