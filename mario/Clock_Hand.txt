'''Clock Hands''' are an obstacle featured in the race track [[Tick-Tock Clock (Mario Kart DS)|Tick-Tock Clock]] in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and reappears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', along with the course itself.

==Description==
The Clock Hands are found near the end of Tick-Tock Clock. If a racer drives into a Clock Hand, they get spun out.
