'''Bomubomu''' (ボムボム, ''Bomubomu'') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. Strange enemies resembling metallic pigs, these baddies have a cannon like snout from which they attack by shooting cannonballs in Mario's direction either straight or diagonal. However these enemies are common and can be defeated with any attack.
