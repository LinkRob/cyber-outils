The '''Castle Grounds''' are the area in front of the [[Mushroom Castle (Super Mario 64)|Mushroom Castle]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. They are a hilly, grassy area with several small [[tree]]s and a waterfall on the far left that pours into the castle's moat. There are two bridges over the moat: a wooden one that leads to the right side of the Castle Grounds, and a stone one that leads to the front doors of the castle.

In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Super Mario Odyssey)|Mushroom Kingdom]] and [[Peach's Castle]] in specific are heavily based on ''Super Mario 64'' and ''Super Mario 64 DS'', so the Mushroom Kingdom's layout has a drain-able moat, a hedge maze, a pond, and [[Scarecrow (object)|a device]] that allows the player to reach the roof of the castle like the Castle Grounds.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario 64''====
In ''Super Mario 64'', [[Mario]] arrives at the Castle Grounds via a [[Warp Pipe]] upon receiving a [[letter]] from [[Princess Peach]]. When he approaches the castle, he is greeted by the [[Lakitu Bros.]] There are multiple [[sign]]s in the Castle Grounds that explain the game's controls. After obtaining the [[key]] from [[Bowser in the Dark World]], Mario can drain the moat by [[Ground Pound|ground-pound]]ing two [[brick pillar]]s in the castle's basement, which allows him to access an area [[The Secret Under the Moat|underneath the moat]] where he can activate the [[Vanish Cap]]. After obtaining all 120 [[Power Star]]s, Mario can use the [[cannon]] next to the lake to launch himself on top of the castle, where he finds three [[1-Up Mushroom|1UP Mushroom]]s, a [[Cap Block|red block]], and [[Yoshi]], who maxes out Mario's lives and gives him a special [[Triple Jump]].

====''Super Mario 64 DS''====
[[File:SM64DS Outside Cannon.png|thumb|Mario jumping into the cannon of Castle Grounds in ''Super Mario 64 DS''.]]
In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', Mario, [[Luigi]], and [[Wario]] all arrive at the Castle Grounds after Peach sends her letter. They then enter the castle to search for Peach while Yoshi is seen sleeping on the roof. When they fail to return, one of the Lakitu Bros. wakes Yoshi up, prompting him to search for them. When he tries to enter the castle, it turns out to be locked. To obtain the key, Yoshi has to chase a [[rabbit]] through a small hedge maze, which was added to the Castle Grounds in this version of the game. Later, after speaking to the [[Toad (species)|Toad]] in the [[Rec Room (Super Mario 64 DS)|Rec Room]], Yoshi can find six more rabbits in the Castle Grounds that give him keys when caught. A [[Block (Super Mario 64)|Black Brick]] was placed on top of the entrance to The Secret Under the Moat, requiring Wario to access it. Luigi's final rabbit can be found on the castle roof, and the red block now gives a [[Power Flower (Super Mario 64 DS)|Power Flower]] ([[Wings (Super Mario 64 DS)|Wings]] if the player plays as Mario). The roof also features a small fence around the area where the three [[1-Up Mushroom|1-UP Mushroom]]s are found.

The [[Super Mario 64 DS#VS mode|VS mode]] also features the Castle Grounds as a playable course. In this mode, the doors are blocked, preventing access to the castle; a moving [[platform]] above the pond is added; and a [[Chain Chomp|Chain-Chomp]] is hopping around in front of the castle with one of the Stars located at the end of its chain.

===''Mario Kart 64''===
The Castle Grounds return in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. The area can be accessed by driving off the road to the right on [[Royal Raceway]] after the big jump, though doing so has no benefit during a race. The layout has been slightly simplified; some of the trees, as well as the small beach with the cannon on it and the waterfall, are missing. The Award Ceremony takes place here.

Though Royal Raceway returns in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', the area in front of Peach's Castle (as well as the castle itself) has been redesigned, and it is no longer possible to explore it.

===''Mario Party'' series===
====''Mario Party 3''====
[[File:MP3 Story Cutscene.png|thumb|The characters standing outside Castle Grounds in ''Mario Party 3''.]]
The Castle Grounds can be briefly seen in the introductory cutscene to [[Story Mode]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', and it is also where the [[Millennium Star]] first crashed down there. A 2D popup-like replica of the Castle Grounds (and Peach's Castle by extension) can also be seen inside the toy box, which also doubles as the background of the main menu.

====''Mario Party 4''====
The introductory cutscene of ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' takes place in the Castle Grounds, which appear to be a very vast and flat grassy plain with realistic-looking trees bordering it. The castle moat does not appear to be visible in this depiction.
