'''Flashy Clone Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in the game ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Description==
Flasy Clone Jump stickers look very similar to [[Super Boot]] stickers except with the turning key in the back of it. They are mainly green in color with a diagonal, rainbow background. They only take one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].

==Notes==
When Mario uses a Flashy Clone Jump, he will split into more Marios who then start attacking enemies. Flashy Clone Jumps may even leave enemies crumbled.

==Trivia==
*When a player [[Paperize]]s the Flashy Clone Jump into a box, there is a chance that an [[Infinijump]] might pop out.
