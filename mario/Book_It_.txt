'''Book It!''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object for both teams is to reach the top of a large stack of books. Both teams each have their teammate holding a card. To lift their teammate up to the next book, the player must press a few buttons in the exact order of {{button|ds|A}}, {{button|ds|B}}, {{button|ds|Y}}, and {{button|ds|X}} twice. After this, their teammate then has to do the same, after which the other character has to do the same again, repeating the cycle. The four buttons are shown on the bottom-left corner, and whichever button that has to be pressed is highlighted white while the others are dark gray. The icon of whoever is shown is the activate character who must press the buttons. Whoever manages to get onto the top of the books first is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|ds|A}}, {{button|ds|B}}, {{button|ds|Y}}, {{button|ds|X}} (press in exact order twice): Move teammate around to the next book
