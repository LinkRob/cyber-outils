'''Binoculars''' are a recurring object in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] steps on a green platform in front of Binoculars, he can look through the lens and get a view of the area. The player can control the Binoculars by moving the [[Nintendo 3DS]] around, utilizing its gyro-sensor control. The L and R buttons are used to zoom out and zoom in, respectively. Sometimes, there is a hidden [[Toad (species)|Toad]], and if sighted through the Binoculars, he throws out an item to assist Mario, such as a [[Star Medal]] or a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The image within the binoculars appears as 2D even if the system has 3D mode turned on.

===''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''===
Unlike before, Mario can instead use Binoculars by [[capture|capturing]] them with [[Cappy]]. In doing so, the Binoculars launch up a set distance before usage. This allows Mario to see certain [[Power Moon|Power Moons]] and even get some, like [[Taxi Flying Through Bonneton]].
