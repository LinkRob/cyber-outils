The '''Fling Spring''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Description==
Like the [[Coinado]], a Fling Spring can be added somewhere on the [[board]], and whoever lands on the Fling Spring gets sent to another area of the board. After a characters lands onto the Fling Spring, the item disappears.
