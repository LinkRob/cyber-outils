The '''Biplane Barrel'''<ref>''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' instruction booklet, pages 18-19.</ref> is an object in the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}. It first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''.

==Description==
It is a propeller plane shaped like a [[barrel]] that is owned by [[Funky Kong]]. It is the successor to the [[Jumbo Barrel]] from ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', which looks more like a conventional airplane, unlike the Jumbo Barrel.

==In ''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' / ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''==
If [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] or [[Dixie Kong]] pay a visit to one of the [[Funky's Flights II]] on [[Crocodile Isle]], Funky will let them ride around to another area of Crocodile Isle, provided that they've already been there, as the only other method of returning to the main Crocodile Isle map is by defeating a boss. This is done as a means to prevent level-skipping, but serves as a safe alternative to having to fight a boss.

While usage of the Biplane Barrel will cost two [[Banana Coin]]s for the first ride from every Funky's Flights II location on Crocodile Isle, all consecutive uses from any location will be free.

The Biplane Barrel is replaced by a yellow [[Gyrocopter]] in the [[Game Boy Advance]] [[remake]] of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2 (Game Boy Advance)|Donkey Kong Country 2]]''. The player can use the Gyrocopter from the world map whenever they desire after they first complete the first [[Funky's Flights (Bonus Game)|Funky's Flights]] challenge, [[Aerial Loop]], without any Banana Coin fee.

==In ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]''==
[[File:Biplane Barrel JC.png|thumb|The Biplane Barrel in ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]'']]
The plane used to travel to the different islands largely resembles the Biplane Barrel. [[Donkey Kong]] is required to have [[Oil Barrel]]s to use the plane.
