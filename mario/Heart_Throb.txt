"'''Heart Throb'''" is the thirty-third live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the animated episode "[[Mighty McMario and the Pot of Gold]]."

==Plot==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] is busily working on another project and a ruckus starts up outside.  Suddenly, [[Rob Stone]] races into the basement pleading to be allowed to hide.  Mario agrees and stands in front of Rob to block him from view. [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] then enters, speechless when seeing their guest.

The Mario Bros. and Rob all have pizza together.  Mario doesn't seem too trilled about Rob being there and treats him normally.  Luigi on the other hand, tends to insist that Rob be treated specially.  Rob decides he has to leave but isn't trilled about going out to face the crowd of fans.

In order to get out safely, Rob switches clothes with Luigi and heads out. Luigi discovers that Rob forgot his guitar and goes out after him with it.  Luigi is then mistaken for Rob and is chased by the crowd.
