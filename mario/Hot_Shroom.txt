The '''Hot Shroom''' is an item and [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
A Hot Shroom can be made in numerous of ways, but the quickest is by cooking a single [[Volt Shroom]] (with them being purchased from [[Harry's Shop]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]). The Volt Shroom is also the most common ingredient used for a Hot Shroom. A Hot Shroom can restore 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 5 [[Flower Point|FP]], which is useful for early in the game.
