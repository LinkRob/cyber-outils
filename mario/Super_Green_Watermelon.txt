A '''Super Green Watermelon''' is a [[Special Item]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. It is one of the items earned from clearing minigames. A Super Green Watermelon is a more powerful variation of the [[Watermelon]] used from the pause screen, and using the Super Green Watermelon puts a Watermelon in the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s mouth capable of spitting up to sixty seeds.
