==Effects==
A Speed Flower is an uncommon item that can only be found in blocks. Whenever a Speed Flower is touched, time quickly speeds up but triples coins and points during the time. However, it can be rather difficult to move around during this time and even [[Mario]]'s [[Flip]] meter decreases much faster. The Speed Flower is the opposite of the [[Slow Flower]].
