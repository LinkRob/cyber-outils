The '''Melody''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Melody can only be obtained once [[Mario]] gets the [[Lyrics]] from the [[Master Poet]]. Mario must take the lyrics to [[Toad in the Desert]] at [[Dry Dry Outpost]] who will then rewrite them into the Melody. Mario must then take the Melody back to the Master Poet in [[Club 64]] who will let [[Chanterelle]] perform them. After the song, Mario is rewarded with the [[Attack FX D]] badge.
