'''Behind Chain Chomp's Gate''', which spells it '''Behind Chain-Chomp's Gate''', is the final mission of [[Bob-omb Battlefield]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', its [[Nintendo DS|NDS]] [[Reissue|remake]], ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and its 2021 enhanced port, ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]''. In ''Super Mario 64'', Behind Chain Chomp's Gate is the sixth mission, while in ''Super Mario 64 DS'', it is the seventh mission.

==Layout==
The mission retains the layout of the course in general. As such, the mission can be completed out-of-order if playing another mission in Bob-omb Battlefield. The [[Power Star]] is behind a set of iron bars next to a [[Chain Chomp]], but is positioned slightly lower in ''Super Mario 64 DS''.

==Objective==
[[Mario]] has to obtain a Power Star located behind a set of iron bars guarded by a Chain Chomp. To do so, he must [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] the [[stump]] that the Chain Chomp's chain is attached to three times. This frees the Chain Chomp, who bounces around briefly before ramming into the bars, breaking them and allowing Mario to go to the Power Star.

In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', a few more methods can be used to obtain the Power Star. There are two additional ways to instantly flatten the stump, including from [[Wario]] performing a single Ground Pound or by breaking a certain [[Block (Super Mario 64)|Brick]] to obtain a [[Super Mushroom|mushroom]] and having a character touch the stump while in their [[Mega Mario|Mega]] form. If all the red [[Question Block|? Blocks]] have been activated, the player can have [[Luigi]] turn into his [[Vanish Mario|Vanish]] form to pass through the iron bars and skip Ground Pounding the stump altogether. If aiming to obtain Behind Chain Chomp's Gate's Power Star during the [[5 Silver Stars!]] mission, this method is required because the Chain Chomp is roaming the field and cannot be lured into breaking the iron bars.
