'''{{world|6|rainbow}}''' is the sixth of the [[Rainbow Course]]s that appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. To access this level, the timer must end with the two digits '66' (that is, 'x66', where 'x' can be any number) when the player reaches the flagpole of any World 6 level (excluding Towers or Castles). Once the level is completed, it can be accessed again by satisfying the above condition once more.  
==Layout==
The level begins in a volcanic area on the ground. When Mario heads to the corner of the stage, coins begin to rain down from above. Entering the [[Pipe Cannon]] in the center of the stage takes him to the Rainbow Course itself.

The upper level begins with a normal platform. This is followed by several bouncy mushroom platforms and coins that rotate in circular formations. Near the first platform after beginning, a [[100-Coin|giant coin]] descends. Afterward is a [[Red Ring]], which spawns the eight [[Red Coin]]s amongst the rotating coins following. The level ends with more platforms and several rotating coin formations above the pit that must be used to exit the level.
