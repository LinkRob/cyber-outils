'''Kamek's Card Tricks''' is a boss minigame from ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. It becomes available in free play upon its initial completion. As implied by the name, [[Kamek]] is fought here.

== Introduction ==
[[Kamek]] appears as four beams of magic light converge on his position, then enlarges his body as he laughs.

== Gameplay ==
Players are shown cards, and are told to pick a card with arrows facing down. Cards with up to three arrows can be shown, and more arrows pointing downward mean more damage, which leads to more points from striking Kamek. However, Kamek can flip the cards; picking that card means no damage dealt which leads to no points being awareded. If a character picks a card with Kamek's icon, Kamek uses his magic to attack the character with a thunderbolt, damaging them.

Upon losing half his health, Kamek gets angry, adding more Kamek cards and reversing more cards. He can also swap some cards. Whoever deals the final blow earns a bonus three points.

== Ending ==
Upon taking the last blow, Kamek shrinks back down to his normal size and disappears. In Toad Scramble, he reveals a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]].

== Controls ==
Players use the circle pad to pick between cards and press A to confirm their choice.

== In-game ==

* When the minigame begins, the text ''"Beat Kamek!"'' appears.
* Just before the minigame begins, the message ''"Pick a card with lots of"'' followed by a triangle standing on a vertex followed by the letter S and an exclamation mark appears. The triangle presumably refers to the downward-pointing arrows, meaning the proper message is ''"Pick a card with lots of downward-pointing arrows!"''
