The '''Royal Tennis Academy''' is one of the main locations in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]] and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. It is where the player's character trains at in each game's story mode.

In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour'', the Royal Tennis Academy features three classes — the Junior Class, Senior Class, and the Varsity Class — coached by [[Mark (Mario Tennis series)|Mark]], [[Emily]], and [[Kevin]], respectively.
