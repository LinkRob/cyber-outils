'''Forest of Illusion 2''' is an underwater level in [[Forest of Illusion]]. It appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Advance 2: Super Mario World]]''. [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Urchin]]s are predominant in this level.

==Layout==
here are many [[Blurp]]s that swim towards the player and go off the screen. Near the end of the level, there is a whistle-blowing [[Chargin' Chuck]] that wakes up all [[Rip Van Fish]] in the area.

On the left near the end of the level, there is a [[Keyhole]] hidden behind a false wall near the yellow [[! Block]] that brings [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to the [[Blue Switch Palace]]; it is also highly populated with Rip Van Fish.
