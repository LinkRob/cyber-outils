"'''Adee Don't'''" is the fifty-first live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the animated episode "[[Karate Koopa]]."

==Plot==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] practice their newest ad jingle, which will be used in their first live television commercial.  While Luigi is worried, Mario is calm because he called in [[Tawny Tyler]] for help.  Luigi is skeptical but Tawny does show up to help them with the commercial.  The camera starts rolling but as soon as it's turned on Mario freezes.

Desperate to get Mario back to normal Luigi and Tawny start to display items around the basement as if they were working a game show.  Mario is then prompted to spin a wheel, which was a prop for their commercial.  He is hit on the head and is restored.  Unfortunately, once the camera comes back on he freezes once again.  Things are even worse since they only have fifteen seconds left before the commercial comes on.  Tawny decides to stand behind Mario and does his part for him.  Unfortunately, when Mario and Luigi take a bow, Tawny is revealed.

== Trivia ==
* This is the second time Luigi uses his game show host voice, the first time being in "[[Game Show Host]]."
