}}A '''Sentry Beam''' (also called a '''Laser Robot''' or simply a '''Robot''') is an enemy that first appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They are gray robots that have propellors on their undersides and attack by emitting powerful lasers at [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]].
==History==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Sentry Beams first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', in this game, they are first seen in the [[Battlerock Galaxy]]. They chase [[Mario]] to shoot a laser at him. If Mario jumps on top of them, they will stop to shoot a laser until Mario gets off. They also appear in [[Bowser Jr.'s Airship Armada]] and [[Toy Time Galaxy]].
===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
Sentry Beams return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', remaining unchanged from their first appearance. They appear in [[Mario Squared Galaxy]] and [[Grandmaster Galaxy]].
