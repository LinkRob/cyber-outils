A '''Zombie Shroom''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is an evil mushroom that will attack whoever unleashes it.

== In ''Super Paper Mario'' ==
Zombie Shrooms are malicious mushrooms that are found in [[Question Block|? Block]]s. When a Zombie Shroom is unleashed, it comes out like a regular [[Mushroom]], then unveils its cover. It will move around quickly on the ground attempting to damage anything that gets too close to it. Zombie Shrooms are very weak and can be taken out in a single hit.
