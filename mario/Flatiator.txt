'''Flatiator''' is a 1 vs. 3 mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Gameplay==
The solo character is on a large ball, which they must try to roll over the three characters, who have to avoid being crushed by the ball. The solo player must try to run over the three players but the solo player's large ball has rather poor turning meaning he or she must get the three players in a group. The three players must be careful as [[Monty Mole]]s will appear on the field as running over one will cause them to trip. The solo player can also use that to her or his advantage as the Monty Moles won't trip them up. Whoever achieves their goal wins this minigame.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|stick}} - Move

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a portmanteau of "flat" and "gladiator."
