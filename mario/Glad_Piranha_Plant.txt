'''Glad Piranha Plants''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are a variant of either a [[Piranha Plant]] or a [[Venus Fire Trap]] with a yellowish-orange head and a consistent smiling face, given how Glad Piranha Plants are imbued with the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
A Glad Piranha Plant is initially seen sleeping until [[Princess Peach|Peach]] approaches, at which point the Glad Piranha Plant starts to fly and spit [[fireball]]s at her, which is what suggests a Glad Piranha Plant's relation to Venus Fire Traps. This behavior is similar to how [[Petey Piranha]] spits out [[goop]] puddles. In their [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry, Glad Piranha Plants are said to dance around happily, but they have not been shown to do so.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Glad Piranha Plants are first encountered in [[Ladida Plains 1-9]], and go on to appear in later worlds, such as [[Hoo's Wood]]. Peach can quickly defeat a Glad Piranha Plant by hitting it with [[Perry]]. In their Glossary entry, their name is formatted '''Glad P. Plant'''.

==Flavor text==
*'''Glossary:''' ''Dances around happily and spits fireballs.''
