'''Coal''' is a key item in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
At the [[Train Station]], [[Shy Guy (Mario Party Advance)|Shy Guy]] asks the player's character to retrieve some Coal for him so that the trains can start moving again. Shy Guy says that the Coal can be bought in the [[Town Area]], and he gives a clue by stating that they are not found in [[Junk]].

During Shy Guy's quest, [[Locomotionless]], the player's character can obtain Coal from the [[Vending Machines]]. Here, Coal is the only product sold, possibly making them based on {{w|cola}}. After obtaining some Coal, the player's character must return with it to Shy Guy. When he is given the Coal, Shy Guy happily rewards the player with the [[Cake Maker]] [[Gaddget]].

==Trivia==
*If the Vending Machines are visited outside of the Locomotionless quest, the player's character remarks that they do not need coal and leaves afterward.
