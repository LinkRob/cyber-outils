==History==
The Seal can only be encountered in [[Gloomy Galleon]] where he was first seen locked inside of a prison in the lighthouse area. Only [[Donkey Kong]] can free the Seal after going through a [[Baboon Blast]] course. The Seal thanks Donkey Kong and gives him a [[Golden Banana]] as reward but offers him to race him for another one.
