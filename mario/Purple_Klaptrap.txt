==History==
Purple Klaptraps are stronger versions of [[Klaptrap]]s that are found in levels of [[DK Isles]]. Purple Klaptraps, unlike the regular Klaptraps, cannot be defeated with normal attacks. Instead, they must be taken out with [[Orange (Donkey Kong 64)|Oranges]]. When a Purple Klaptrap is defeated, they release more Oranges and their teeth do not chase the Kongs unlike with regular Klaptraps.
