|first = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}The '''Patio''' is an area in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It can be accessed from the [[Common Hall]] and it leads out to [[Under the Stairs]] and the [[Kitchen (Gloomy Manor)|Kitchen]].
==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-4: Visual Tracks|A-4: Visual Tricks]], Luigi heads out to the Patio to get a Key from a statue of Professor E. Gadd that must be revealed with the [[Dark-Light Device]]. That key leads into the Kitchen.

Luigi can return to the Patio during [[Gloomy Manor#A-5: Sticky Situation|A-5: Sticky Situation]] where he can use the [[Dark-Light Device]] on the hidden Cheep-Cheep statue to earn himself a [[Gem]]. This Gem can also be found in A-4: Visual Tricks.
