'''Slides''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==In ''Super Mario 64'' / ''Super Mario 64 DS''==
There are a few areas centered around a slide, including [[The Princess's Secret Slide]], an area within the chimney of [[Cool, Cool Mountain]], and an area in [[Tall, Tall Mountain]]. When [[Mario]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in the [[reissue|remake]]) approaches a slide, he automatically begins to [[slide]] downward. A character can either speed up or slow down while traveling down a slide. All three slides take place above a black void, and a large portion of them do not have any protective walls on either side. Because of this, if a character falls off the edge of a slide, they lose a life. Slides consist of [[coin]]s and at least one [[1-Up Mushroom]] down the path. When the player's character reaches the end of a slide, they earn a [[Power Star]]. In the case of Cool, Cool Mountain's [[Big Penguin Race]] [[mission]], the objective is to race the [[Penguin Champ]] to the bottom for a Power Star.

==In ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''==
All three slide areas are featured in the microgame [[Super Mario 64 DS (microgame)|Super Mario 64 DS]], each in a difficulty level: The Princess's Secret Slide in level 1; the Cool, Cool Mountain slide in level 2; and lastly the Tall, Tall Mountain slide in level 3. If Mario makes it down the slide, the player wins the microgame, but if he falls off, they lose.
