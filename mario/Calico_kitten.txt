The '''calico kittens''' are [[kitten]]s in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. They are located in various areas, and calico kittens are sought by the [[Calico Cat Parent]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The calico kitten is a cat that looks just like the [[kitten]]s from [[Bowser's Fury|Bowsers Fury]] except it is a calico kitten instead of plain colors. Their fur is light yellow and has orange and dark brown. Their mother resembles a larger version of them.

==In ''Bowser's Fury''==
The calico kitten is hidden all around the place near the [[Giga Bell]]s. Sometimes there are multiple calico kittens around the stage. Their mother is at a Giga Bell area crying until you find her kids. Also whenever [[Fury Bowser]] gets up the calico kitten becomes a [[Fury Cat]] along with his siblings. calico kittens cannot be carried through pipes or water, otherwise they respawn at their original location. The calico kitten is at first on it's own, but the second time, it has two siblings, and the third time, it has four.

==Trivia==
* The calico kittens are also known as '''Daisy Cats''', which originally started a rumor that Daisy was playable in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.{{Citation needed}}
