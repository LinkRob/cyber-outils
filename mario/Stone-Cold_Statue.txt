'''Stone-Cold Statues''' are a mini-boss in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Stone-Cold Statues are made up of four sides consisting of three tiers, each consisting of either pair of eyes, a nose, a mouth, or a green orb. Stone-Cold Statues also have a bird-like antenna growing out of their head. They are supported by a pair of stone feet, even though it does not attempt to move around. Gradually dismantling a Stone-Cold Statue reveals a purple rib-cage inside of it. 

===Behavior===
Stone-Cold Statues have three attacks, each coming from one of their facial features: the eyes fire homing lasers at [[Wario]]; the mouth [[Fire Breath|breathes fire]]; and the nose sucks in Wario to be attacked by a different tier.

==In ''Wario World''==
Two Stone-Cold Statues are encountered in [[Pecan Sands]]. The first one is encountered at around the middle of the course, and the second one is toward the end, near the [[Stone Doohickey]]. To defeat a Stone-Cold Statue, Wario has to destroying the green orbs on every tier.
