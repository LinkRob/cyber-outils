'''Witches''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Witches are magical beings that wear a purple robe and a mask that is half-white, half-purple. They are invisible and can only be seen on a mirror's reflection.

===Behavior===
A Witch attacks by firing bolts of electricity at [[Wario]]. They can also appear and disappear at will.

==In ''Wario World''==
Witches are only encountered in [[Mirror Mansion]].
