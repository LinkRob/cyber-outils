'''Eric Newsome (real name Eric Kurtis Stroy) '''(born December 8, 1963) is an American voice actor who voiced [[Bowser]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. He also provided the voice of [[Meta Knight]] in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.
