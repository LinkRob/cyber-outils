The '''Planet of Trials''' is a location in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
The Planet of Trials is a small planet orbiting the [[Comet Observatory]]. It is located on a small hill next to the [[Garage (Super Mario Galaxy)|Garage]]. There is an orange [[Warp Pipe]] on the Planet of Trials that leads back to the Comet Observatory.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy''==
The Planet of Trials can be accessed only after collecting all three [[Green Star]]s so that the three nearby Green [[Luma]]s can activate a green [[Launch Star]] leading to the Planet of Trials. From this point on, the planet serves as a hub for the three [[Trial Galaxies]]: the [[Rolling Gizmo Galaxy]] on the yellow arm, the [[Bubble Blast Galaxy]] on the green arm, and the [[Loopdeeswoop Galaxy]] on the blue arm.

If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] attempts to access the Planet of Trials using the [[Red Star]] instead of the green Launch Star, they hit an invisible barrier that prevents them from doing so and get pulled back to the Comet Observatory, even if all three Green Stars have been collected.

A fourth Green Luma appears on the back of the planet once Mario and Luigi have recovered all 120 Power Stars. It grants access to the [[Grand Finale Galaxy]].
