'''Shortcut Circuit''' is a 4-player and a Duel minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Gameplay==
Shortcut Circuit is a minigame which involves collecting [[Coin]]s, so there is no losing or winning criteria. To collect the Coins, the characters have to walk between straightforward paths made from computer chips by placing bridge segments, with a few arrows pointed either left or right, to where the Coins are. Any character who walks up to a bridge segment automatically moves across in the specified direction to cross over to another path. A few [[Red Coin]]s, worth five individual Coins, are also placed between the paths. The minigame ends once every character reaches the end.

==Controls==
*[[Stylus]]: Place bridge segment
