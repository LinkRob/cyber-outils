'''Hearts''' (often spelled ♥) are an item in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Description==
Hearts are collectible items that are symbols of happiness. Hearts are essential to improving a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s mood, and they can recover petals on the [[Smile Meter]]. Hearts are earned by eating [[fruit]], defeating enemies, and collecting [[Coin]]s. Eating a [[Lucky Fruit]] earns the Yoshi eight Hearts, while eating a [[Favorite Fruit]] earns them three Hearts.

At the end of a [[level]], 100 extra Hearts apiece are awarded for each [[melon]] eaten, for each [[Special Heart]] collected, and for each Yoshi who survived the stage.
