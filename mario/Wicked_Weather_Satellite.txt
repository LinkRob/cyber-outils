The '''Wicked Weather Satellite''' is an object in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Mario and the Red Baron Koopa]]."

==Description==
The Wicked Weather Satellite is capable of drastically altering the weather patterns.

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
The Wicked Weather Satellite is an invention of [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and [[Lakitu]], who used the machine to hold [[Pastaland]] at ransom, as if Pastaland didn't surrender to King Koopa's rule, the Wicked Weather Satellite would freeze the kingdom over with an endless snowstorm. However. [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] managed to destroy the machine using a [[bomb]] from an airplane powered by a magic carpet.
