'''Pink Coins''' are a type of [[Coin]] which appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' (as of version 1.40), ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

== Description==
Pink Coins resemble [[Key Coin]]s, and their functionality combines the traits of both a Key Coin and a [[Red Coin]]. Collecting every Pink Coin within a [[level]] unlocks a [[Key Door]].
