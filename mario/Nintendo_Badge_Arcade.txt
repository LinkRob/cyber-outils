'''''Nintendo Badge Arcade''''' is a free-to-start [[Nintendo 3DS]] application. It was first released in Japan in 2014, followed by North America, Europe, and Australia in 2015. It involves the player collecting badges representing various [[Nintendo]]-related franchises, including ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', in a series of crane machine minigames. The badges serve as extra customization options for the Nintendo 3DS Home Menu.
