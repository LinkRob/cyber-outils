'''Iron Clefts''' are a variant of [[Cleft]] featured as an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
The dreaded Iron Clefts make up the first Major League team in the [[Glitz Pit]]. The Iron Clefts are so powerful that they cannot take damage, nor do they have any apparent [[Defense]] rating, which is represented only by three question marks. As such, Iron Clefts are completely impenetrable to all attacks, and the only way for [[Mario]] to attack and therefore defeat Iron Clefts is by using their power against them (this can typically be done by using the [[Yoshi Kid]]'s [[Gulp]] ability). The only named Iron Clefts are the [[Armored Harriers]].
