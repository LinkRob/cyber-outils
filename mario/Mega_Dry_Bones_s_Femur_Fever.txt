'''Mega Dry Bones&#39;s Femur Fever''' is a boss minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'' that involves players fighting a [[Super Dry Bones|Mega Dry Bones]], who can be attacked by firing [[Cannonball]]s at him.

== Gameplay ==
You have to do [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounds]] on a cannon to shoot Cannonballs at Mega Dry Bones; when you Ground Pound near the peak of your jump it fills up two sections of the three section-long bar, of which shows when the Cannonballs will be fired, otherwise it will only fill up one section of the bar. During the fight Mega Dry Bones will throw bones in a boomerang-like manner, and they act as such; if you hit the bone you lose a point and the bar will reset. In the second section of the fight, it will throw bones shrouded in blue fire which now move much faster and make you lose two points.
