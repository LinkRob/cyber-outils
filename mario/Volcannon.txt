'''Volcannons''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Volcannons are small, living, red (gray in their artwork) volcanoes. Their bodily features include a pair of feet and a crack around their left and right eye each. A small stream of [[lava]] flows down each crack, making it seem as if the Volcannon is crying.

===Behavior===
If a Volcannon is near [[Wario]], it erupts, releasing two fiery rocks from the opening on top of its body.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Volcannons are encountered in [[Mt. Lava Lava]] and [[Sneak Peak]]. If Wario touches any of a Volcannon's rocks, he takes damage.
