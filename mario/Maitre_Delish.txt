|race = Unknown}}'''Maitre Delish''' is a character that is mentioned in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Maitre Delish was well regarded as a famous chef. He made numerous of [[cookbook]]s where it is said to make anyone into a master chef upon reading them. [[Zess T.]], the chef of [[Rogueport]], posted a trouble at the [[Trouble Center]] where she wanted the cookbook of Maitre Delish. She states that the only known other copy is in the [[Creepy Steeple]]. [[Mario]] can find Maitre Delish's cookbook in a secret room of the Creepy Steeple and hands it to Zess T. which allows her to cook two ingredients together for a better dish.

==Trivia==
*It is very likely that the [[Cookbook]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' was also written by Maitre Delish as it also allows the chef in the game, [[Tayce T.]], to cook dishes using two ingredients as well.
