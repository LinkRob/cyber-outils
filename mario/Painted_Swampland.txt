}}'''Painted Swampland''', or '''Soda Jungle-4''', is the fourth level of [[Soda Jungle]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is located in the haunted sub-area of Soda Jungle. Most notably, this course is an outside ghost course with the design and art style inspired by [[wikipedia:Vincent van Gogh|Vincent van Gogh]]'s ''[[wikipedia:The Starry Night|The Starry Night]]''.

Completing the course normally makes a path that leads back to the first point after [[Which-Way Labyrinth]], but completing the level with its secret exit reveals the true path through Soda Jungle. 
==Layout==
The level begins on a platform with a [[Boo]] nearby, with [[Poisoned Water]] filling the bottom of the area. A [[? Block]] containing a power-up is found, along with [[Warp Pipe]]s that act as platforms in the poison. The third Warp Pipe sinks when it is stepped on. Some more Boos are found, along with Warp Pipes of various colors, some of which sink when stepped on. A section of the ground with a ? Block containing a power-up is found, and is followed by a section with more Warp Pipes acting as platforms. [[Piranha Plant]]s can be found in yellow Warp Pipes. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is then found, with a ? Block containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] hidden in a wall. A section of blue Warp Pipes with [[Boo Buddies|Circling Boo Buddies]] is then found. A [[Red Ring]] is found above some red Warp Pipes, and a few more Warp Pipes are found leading to the [[Goal Pole]]. In the background around the end of the level, an image of [[Bowser]] with no pupils is present.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In the course's second yellow [[Warp Pipe]], the player can find the first Star Coin surrounded by Circling Boo Buddies.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Before the [[Checkpoint Flag]], the player must get a running start off of the closest slanted pipe to reach the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': After the Checkpoint Flag, the third Star Coin is found among a group of Circling Boo Buddies.
==Secret exit==
Unlike most secret exits in the game, this course's secret exit is required to continue through [[Soda Jungle]]. After a series of red pipes, there's a green slanted one leading to a wall. The wall contains a Warp Pipe that leads to the course's secret exit.
