'''''Mario &#38; Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games Arcade Edition''''' is the [[arcade]] variation of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' and the first arcade title in the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' series. It was first released in Japan in February 2016 then in North America in June 2016. It is set during the {{w|2016 Summer Olympics}} in [[Rio de Janeiro]], [[Brazil]]. The game only supports 2-4 players multiplayer.

In 2020, another ''Mario & Sonic'' arcade game was released, ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020 - Arcade Edition]]''.
