}}'''Pendulum Castle''' is the eighth level of [[Superstar Road]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked when the player collects every [[Star Coin]] in [[Peach's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Peach's Castle]]. This is one of the two castle levels in the game with no boss, the other being [[Red-Hot Elevator Ride]].

Pendulum Castle is used for two challenges in the game. The first is [[Castle Crawl]], which challenges the player to complete the level without being able to run. The second is [[Cashless Castle Crawl]], which is the same challenge, but also requiring the player to beat the level collecting less than six coins. 
==Layout==
The course is based off of the castle levels throughout the game, specifically [[The Final Battle (New Super Mario Bros. U)|The Final Battle]]. The level begins next to a large [[Ball 'n' Chain]] above a pit and platforms made of [[Donut Block]]s. A platform with two [[Bony Beetle]]s and a [[? Block]] is found, along with a smaller Ball 'n' Chain above Donut Blocks. A [[Brick Block]] and another small Ball 'n' Chain are found. The player must cross more Donut Blocks, where they encounter Bony Beetles and a Ball 'n' Chain. A ? Block above a Bony Beetle is encountered before an area with Donut Blocks under multiple Ball 'n' Chains. More Bony Beetles are found in the area. Another Ball 'n' Chain appears before a ? Block and a Brick Block. Some smaller Donut Block platforms must be crossed while avoiding Ball 'n' Chains. A Bony Beetle is found, followed by a large Ball 'n' Chain above some platforms consisting of a single Donut Block. Another large Ball 'n' Chain is found next to a [[? Switch]], which causes red blocks to appear above the pit below. A [[Warp Pipe]] is then found. Entering the pipe leads to a path of Donut Blocks and the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is located below a row of Donut Lifts and a pair of Ball 'n' Chains.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Directly after the first Star Coin, the player should wall jump into a hidden area with a [[Warp Pipe]]. In there, the star coin is blocked off by three giant Ball 'n' Chain. A [[Starman]], which can be found in the [[? Block]] on the other side of the area, is required in order to safely obtain the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At the end of the stage, the player needs to hit an orange ? Switch to make red blocks appear. These red blocks will allow the player to cross the bottomless pit and reach the final Star Coin.
