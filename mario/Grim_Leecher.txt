'''Grim Leechers''' are an enemy featured in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''

==Profiles==
===Physical description===
Grim Leechers have the appearance of a small, dark purple ghost with a skull on their face.

===Behavior===
If a Grim Leecher touches a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], he grabs on to his back, causing [[Baby Mario]] to fall off and triggering the [[Countdown Timer]]. This also inverts the directional pad, but a Yoshi can shake off a Grim Leecher by [[Ground Pound]]ing.

===Etymology===
The name Grim Leecher is a pun on {{w|Death (personification)|Grim Reaper}} and {{w|Leech}}.
