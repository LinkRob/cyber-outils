'''Dennis Lee Hopper''' (May 17, 1936 - May 29, 2010) was an an American actor. He played [[King Koopa]] in the ''[[Super Mario Bros. (film)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' movie. He is also known for his various other movie roles.

Hopper died on May 29, 2010 due to health complications that arose in late 2009.

==Trivia==
*Hopper was also a painter and sculpturer. His works are exhibited worldwide.<ref>https://archive.li/sLY4t</ref>
