'''Rumble Ready''' is a 2-vs.-2 minigame in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Gameplay==
There are two teams, each consisting of two characters, although only two characters face one another at a time. The objective is to react to the rumble faster than the other character to receive a capsule, which falls from a machine. If the Rumble Feature is turned off, the rumble indication is represented by an exclamation point instead. If a character attempts to catch the capsule before the rumble, the capsule bounces off of their hands and goes to the opposing team. The first team to get three capsules is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|A}}: Catch

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Be the first player to react to the rumble and catch the capsule. Catch three capsules first or end with the most capsules to win.''
*'''Advice 1''': ''Press {{button|gcn|A}} the instant you feel the rumble. If you press it early, the capsule goes to the other team!''
*'''Advice 2''': ''If you've turned the Rumble Feature off, an exclamation point will appear instead.''

==Trivia==
*Rumble Ready is the only 2-vs.-2 minigame in ''Mario Party 5'' that uses the Rumble Feature.
