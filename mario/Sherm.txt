'''Sherms''' are tank-like enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Odyssey''==
Sherms can be found at [[New Donk City]] at nighttime, and they are used in the fight against the [[Mecha Wiggler]]. Sherms are also found in [[Steam Gardens]] and [[Honeylune Ridge]]. Sherms attack by shooting at their target. To defeat them, Mario must destroy them with a [[Ground Pound]] attack or capture another Sherm with his hat and shoot the first one. When players capture a Sherm, they will enter first person mode and can move around by tilting the left analog stick, aim by tilting the right analog stick, Joy Cons, or pro controller, and shoot by pressing the Y button. However, the player's movement will be slowed down while as a Sherm.<gallery>
