==History==
The Hand-It-Overs are a team of [[Bandit]]s and [[Big Bandit]]s lead by [[Bandy Andy]]. They place 14th of the Glitz Pit making them a somewhat strong team. Despite being a Bandit and enjoy stealing from opponents, Bandy Andy is overall friendly towards [[Mario]] but says he will not hold back in battle. At some point, Bandy Andy goes missing and the entire Hand-It-Overs team is replaced by another team, the [[Destructors]]. It was later revealed that Bandy Andy was nearly [[Power-draining machine|drained of all of his energy]] after encountering [[Grubba]]. However, after Grubba is defeated, Bandy Andy regains his strength and goes back into the fighting ring with the Hand-It-Overs retaking their #14 spot.
