'''''Amiibo tap: Nintendo&#39;s Greatest Bits''''', titled '''''amiibo Touch & Play: Nintendo Classics Highlights''''' in Europe, is an application for the [[Wii U]] released in 2015.

==Gameplay==
By scanning an [[amiibo]], the player can play a limited trial of a certain [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] or [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] game, including certain ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. The gameplay is divided into "scenes," which take place within different highlight points of the game. One scene occurs only briefly before transitioning to the next one, in a somewhat similar manner to the {{series|NES Remix}} challenges.
