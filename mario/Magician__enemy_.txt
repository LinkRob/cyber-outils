'''Magicians''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
At first, a Magician appears as a human tied up by a rope with a large, yellow lock in the front. It is actually a vampire, and a Magician's wings can be seen if it breaks free from its rope.

===Behavior===
A Magician performs a fast-spinning attack upon noticing [[Wario]]. A Magician breaks free of its chains if Wario performs a [[Piledrive]] onto it, and it throws its lock at Wario.

==In ''Wario World''==
Magicians are only encountered in [[Mirror Mansion]], during the battle against [[Mean Emcee]]. Wario can stun a Magician by hitting it three times. If Wario is hit by a Magician's lock, he loses a heart and a half of his health. Wario has to hit the Magician another three times to stun it, then he can grab it and perform a [[Mad Move]] to defeat it.
