All of Daisy's quotes were reused later in <i>[[Mario Party 7]]</i> and <i>[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]].</i>

* "Let's party!" (when selected)
* "Yahoo!" (when receiving three or more coins)
* "Yes!" (when receiving something)
* "Oh..." (when losing coins)
* "Aaaah!" (when blasted away or falling)
* "Aw, man!" (when losing a minigame)
* "Oh, yeah! I won!" (when winning a minigame)
