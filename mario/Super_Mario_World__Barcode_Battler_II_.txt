'''''Super Mario World''''' is a set of [[w:c:nintendo:Barcode Battler II|Barcode Battler II]] cards created by [[Epoch]] in 1992. It was the first set of Barcode Battler II cards themed around a game in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] to be released. The cards are based on the [[Super Mario World|eponymous SNES game]].

==Story==
<blockquote>お～っとしたいへん！？

ひめだいまおうピーチ 姫が「大魔王クッパにさらわれてしまった！不思議な 島国「恐竜ランド」に住みついた、天 魔王クッパとその手でたち。

きょうりょくみかたマリオ とルイージは、強力な味方ヨッシーといっしょもどに「恐竜ランド」 の 平和を取り戻して、あらぼうけんたびしゅっぱつ 新た なる冒険の 旅へと 出発します。

かずかずきょうてき 数々の強敵をクリアして、

「笑魔クッパの答え」に閉じこめられたピーチ 姫をふじきゅうしゅつ 無事救出できるでしょうか！？

ぼうけん 不思議なナゾがいっぱいの冒険です･･･。</blockquote>
<blockquote>OH NO!? [[Princess Peach]] has been kidnapped by [[Bowser|Great Demon King Koopa]]! Great Demon King Koopa and  [[Bowser's Minions|his minions]] have settled in the mysterious island nation of [[Dinosaur Land]]. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] embark on a new adventurous journey to restore peace to Dinosaur Land. With their powerful new ally, [[Yoshi]]. Will you be able to clear a number of formidable enemies and safely rescue Princess Peach, who is trapped in the [[Valley of Bowser|valley of the Great Demon King Koopa]]!? It's an adventure full of mysterious mysteries...</blockquote>

The plot of the game is the same as that of the game on which it is based. Bowser kidnaps Princess Peach and takes her to the Valley of Bowser in Dinosaur Land. Mario, Luigi, and Yoshi then save her.
