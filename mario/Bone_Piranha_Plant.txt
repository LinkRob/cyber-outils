'''Bone Piranha Plants''', or '''Dry Piranha Plants''', are enemies which first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. They are a skeleton variant of [[Piranha Plant]] and are found in various [[fortress]] and [[castle]] [[level]]s. Bone Piranha Plants have a larger variant named [[Big Bone Piranha Plant]].

== In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'' ==
Bone Piranha Plants first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' where there are less common first appearing in [[World 4-Tower (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 4-Tower]] then in [[World 5-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5-Castle]].

Unlike a standard [[Piranha Plant]], Bone Piranha Plants are invulnerable to [[fireball]]s but are vulnerable to [[Gold Fireball]]s. They can also be killed using [[Raccoon Mario]]'s tail or from being touched by Mario or [[Luigi]] while they are [[Invincible Mario|invincible]].

== In ''Mario Party: Island Tour'' ==
Bone Piranha Plants Plants also appear in [[Mario Party: Island Tour]].

== In ''Mario Kart 8''/''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' ==
Bone Piranha Plants also appear in [[Mario Kart 8]] and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', as an obstacle in [[Bone-Dry Dunes]].
