'''Bonus Blocks''' are a rare type of [[block]] featured in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario Advance 2: Super Mario World]]''. It usually contains a [[coin]], like some [[? Block]]s, although if [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have collected at least 30 coins in a level with a Bonus Block, hitting it instead releases a [[1-Up Mushroom]].
