'''Coin Piles''', alternatively named '''Coin Stacks''' are a recurring item in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', although in the background of the title screen, and are first featured as items in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Description==
Coin Piles consist of [[Coin]]s stacked upon one another. The number of Coins that a Coin Pile is worth matches how many are stacked upon one another.
