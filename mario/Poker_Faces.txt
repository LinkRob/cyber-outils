|games   = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}The {{Nihongo|'''Poker Faces'''|ポーキングストーンズ|Pōkingu Sutōnzu|Poking Stones}} (also known as {{Nihongo|'''The Ultimate Weapons'''|ざんこくなぜんしんキョウキ|Zankoku na Zenshin Kyōki}}) is a team of the [[Glitz Pit]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Poker Faces consist of two [[Bristle]]s, rather troublesome enemies to deal with if [[Mario]] doesn't come prepared. This is certain if [[Grubba]] makes it a rule not to use special moves, meaning [[Mario]] cannot use [[Earth Tremor]] to take them out (unless he has a [[POW Block]]). The Poker Faces place 8th, making them one of the top ten teams of the Glitz Pit.
