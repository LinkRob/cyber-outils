'''Coco Candy''' is a [[recipe]] made by [[Zess T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Eating a Coco Candy restores 3 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 15 [[Flower Point|FP]] making it an effective recovery item. In order to make a Coco Candy, [[Mario]] must bring Zess T. a [[Cake Mix]] (which can be bought at the [[Pianta Parlor]]) and a [[Coconut]] (which can be found on [[Keelhaul Key]]).
