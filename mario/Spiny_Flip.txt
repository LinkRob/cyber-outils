|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Spiny Flip''' is a move used by [[Lakilester]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It serves has his main basic move that requires no [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.
==Usage==
To use Spiny Flip, a cursor will appear on the screen and the player must aim it in the circle to inflict damage. Once the cursor is lined up in the circle, the player can press the {{button|n64|a}} button to have Lakilester throw a Spiny egg at the enemy. This move is very useful as it can even attack enemies on the ceiling.
==Trivia==
*The way to use Spiny Flip is reused for [[Madame Flurrie]]'s [[Body Slam]] technique in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
