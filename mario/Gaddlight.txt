The '''Gaddlight''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. It is an invention created by [[Professor E. Gadd]]. In ''Mario Party 4'', this item replaces the ''[[Boo Repellent]]'' (from ''[[Mario Party 3]]'') and was succeeded by the [[Bone Capsule]] (in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'') and the ''[[Boo-Away Orb]]'' (in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'').

==Description==
The Gaddlight is a small, green flashlight with an image of a [[Boo]] crossed out on it. If someone uses a Boo (from a [[Boo's Crystal Ball]] or by summoning one from a Boo House), the victim can tap "A" to lose less coins. However, they cannot fight back if the Boo steals a star. If the victim has a Gaddlight, they can flash the light at the Boo to scare it away when it runs after their stars or coins. In this case, the Boo does not steal anything from the victim, and the player --who summoned the Boo-- they end up wasting 5 coins (to steal coins) or 50 coins (to steal a star) for nothing.

This item --to scare away a [[Boo]] (when he runs after a player's stars or coins)-- it does not work against the [[Big Boo]] on [[Boo's Haunted Bash]] (via the [[Happening Space]]s). That is, even if all three victim players have a Gaddlight. Since this item does not protect against the Big Boo, the targeted players (or opposed team in Team Mode) are forced to watch their stars and/or coins get stolen. (As for the [[Big Boo]], the targeted players --opposed team members in Team Mode-- can lose less coins by tapping "A". But they can't fight back if the Big Boo is stealing a star.) 

The item was supposedly invented by [[Professor E. Gadd]], with "Gadd" being in the title of the item. However, it lacks his emblem seen on other [[Gadd Science, Incorporated]], and the Japanese version has no such connection to the item.
