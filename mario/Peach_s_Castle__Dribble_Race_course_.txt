'''Peach's Castle''' is a location in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
Peach's Castle takes place right in front of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] and far behind of [[Peach Field]]. Two small trenches of water are to the left and right sides of the court each. The main obstacle is the [[Green Shell]]s shot out from three [[Warp Pipe]]s at the [[finish line]], one at a time in quick succession. There are always three [[? Panel]]s present at a time, containing ten [[coin]]s each, and they either move back and forth slowly in a vertical or horizontal direction or not at all.

==In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Peach's Castle is the first course of Dribble Race. It is available from the start, and its default time is 45 seconds. If the player's character manages to beat both Peach's Castle's and [[Sunshine Road]]'s time, they unlock [[Rainbow Road (Dribble Race course)|Rainbow Road]].
