'''Final Destination''' is a stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' This stage returns in every other ''Super Smash Bros.'' title.

== Description==
One of the most basic courses in the game, Final Destination is a small, blue metal platform floating in space and other scenes. It has a lot of mechanisms on the bottom, and a purple crystal power generator is in the center.

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Ultimate]]'', the stage takes place in [[World of Light]], where the player fights [[Master Hand]], [[Crazy Hand]] or [[Giga Bowser]]. Most characters in [[Classic Mode]] fight against the bosses in the final round taking place on Final Destination.
