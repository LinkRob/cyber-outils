'''Lurchins''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
Lurchins are a species of sea urchins in the series that can only be found in the underwater sections of the [[Smithsnorian Museum]]. They cannot move at all and can only damage [[Wario]] if he comes into contact with one. According to Wario, the Lurchins are in a constant state of loneliness due to being unable to safely touch anyone.
