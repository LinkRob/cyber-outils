'''Paradise''' is a location in the [[MS-DOS]] and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] releases of ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''.

==Description==
Paradise is a typical beach island with palm trees.

==In ''Mario's Time Machine''==
Paradise only appears if [[Mario]] does not return the artifacts quickly enough. In this event, when Mario returns every artifact and leaves the [[Bowser's Museum|museum]], [[Bowser]] quickly snatches the [[Timulator]], sets the destination to "Paradise," and escapes to there.
