'''King Green Coin Coffers''' are the standard color type of [[King Coin Coffer]], and only appear in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. They are Wood/Wood attribute enemies and are found late in the game, starting from World 8-2, and can also be found in World 8-5 and World 8-6. A King Green Coin Coffer may also randomly appear in the boss battles of Special World courses ★World 5-4 and ★World 7-8. As teammates, their Awoken Skill is '''Wood Boost''', and may provide HP+ bonuses when depowered.
