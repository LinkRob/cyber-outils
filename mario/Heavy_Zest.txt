==Appearance==
Heavy Zest is a [[Hooski]] that wears orange clothing. He also has a smiling face. His idle animation in combat has him raise his right arm.
==Battle==
In battle, Heavy Zest summons [[Hooraw]] minions to his side, and must be defeated alongside his minions within three turns, if the Bros. fail, the battle will be "reset," with the referee giving the Bros. and Heavy Zest a [[Star Candy|Max Candy]]. If the Bros. knocked Heavy Zest out without defeating any other Hooraws, the [[Beef-Off Referee]] will give him a [[1-Up Super]] instead.
===Attacks===
*'''Charge/Jump''': Heavy Zest will charge at Mario. The player must watch the background where the crowd of Hooraws are at. It the Hooraws jump low, Heavy Zest will charge right through Mario requiring him to jump to avoid damage. If the Hooraws jump high, Heavy Zest will jump over Mario not requiring him to jump.
*'''Charge''': Heavy Zest charges, which can be jumped over. Due to his slow speed, Heavy Zest cannot be avoided entirely; however, he will take damage if jumped on.
===Strategy===
[[Luiginary Attack]]s are the key to winning this fight. Mario should avoid taking any damage to not waste a turn healing. Keep in mind that Heavy Zest will only faint if you drain his health before defeating his minions, take them out as well.

=== Stats ===
The Heavy Zest is the most balanced [[Hooraw]], with his health and defense being the second highest out of all of them (282 maximum and 80, respectively), but his speed and power being the second lowest (60 and 113, respectively).
