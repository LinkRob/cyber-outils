The '''Badwagon''' is a recurring [[kart]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
=== ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''/''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ===
It appears as a default [[kart]] body. In ''Mario Kart 8'', it possesses high weight and traction. In its remake, it gains high speed. In both games, they possess acceleration and handling.

=== ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
Badwagon returns as a High-End kart.

==Crossover appearances==
=== ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' ===
Badwagon is a primary Ace-class [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]].
