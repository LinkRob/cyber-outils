'''Shroom Shakes''' are a type of item which only appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can use these items to restore ten [[Heart Point]]s and cure poison if he affected. These items replace [[Mushroom (Paper Mario series)|Mushrooms]] as the basic recovery item, although Mushrooms may still appear via [[Question Block]]s. This item is purchasable from both the [[Flipside]] shop and the shop on [[Mount Lineland]].
