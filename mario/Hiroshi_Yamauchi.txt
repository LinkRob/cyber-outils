'''Hiroshi Yamauchi''' (November 7, 1927 - September 19, 2013) was the third president of [[Nintendo]]. He was president for over 50 years and the third and final member of his family to be president of Nintendo; after his retirement in 2002, he was succeeded by [[Satoru Iwata]] as president. Yamauchi was a key figure in transforming Nintendo from a small card company into a prolific video game developer and publisher.

He passed away on September 19, 2013 from pneumonia.
