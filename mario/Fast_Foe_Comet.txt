'''Fast Foe Comets''' are rare types of Prankster Comets in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' that make the enemies on a galaxy move faster (normally [[Tox Box]]es). These comets do not appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', but have a similar comet in ''Galaxy 2'' called the [[Double Time Comet]], which makes moving parts move twice as fast.

Fast Foe Comets share their theme with the Cosmic Comet.

==Prankster Comet Locations==
*'''[[Beach Bowl Galaxy]]''' - Fast Foes On The Cyclone Stone: [[Tox Box]]es move [[Fast Tox Box|faster]] on the "Cyclone Stone". The player must evade their rampage to get the [[Power Star]]. This is based off Mission 3: The Secret Undersea Cavern.

*'''[[Toy Time Galaxy]]''' - Fast Foes Of Toy Time: Tox Boxes and hazards moves faster on the Chain Planet. Mario must activate all [[Flipswitch Panel]]s before claiming the Power Star. This is based off the Secret Star: The Flipswitch Chain.
