'''Dairas''' are an enemy from the {{series|The Legend of Zelda}}, and they first appear in ''[[w:c:zelda:Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]]''. Its only appearance in a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game is ''[[Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up]]'', in the form of a cameo.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Dairas are humanoid alligators bearing a mohawk-like crest on their head. They have been featured in various colors, and their sprites in ''Zelda II: The Adventure of Link'' depict them as red or orange.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up''==
A Daira makes a cameo on the Teacher page, where [[Luigi]] is teaching it and a [[Moblin]] some basic addition and subtraction. The Daira on the right is seen raising his hand as Luigi points to the clock, possibly meaning that the Daira wants to excuse itself from class.

==In ''[[StreetPass Mii Plaza]]''==
[[File:Puzzle Swap Ultimate NES Remix screenshot.png|thumb|A Daira's appearance in the ''Ultimate NES Remix'' puzzle]]
In [[Puzzle Swap]], a red Daira also appears alongside several Nintendo characters in the ''[[Ultimate NES Remix]]'' puzzle, first introduced in November 2014. The Daira can be seen at the very end, around the bottom-right, where it is left of a [[Fireball (Donkey Kong)|Fireball]] from ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' and a [[Piranha Plant]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.
