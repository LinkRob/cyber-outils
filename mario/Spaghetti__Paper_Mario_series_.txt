'''Spaghetti Plate''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', being simply named '''Spaghetti''', and made a reappearance in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', retaining its original name, and then in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', which renames it to Spaghetti Plate. 

==Effects==
In its first two appearances, Spaghetti restores 6 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 4 [[Flower Point|FP]]. [[Tayce T.]] makes it with [[Dried Pasta]] while [[Zess T.]] does so with [[Fresh Pasta Bunch|Fresh Pasta]].

In ''Super Paper Mario'', the Spaghetti Plate restores a higher number of 15 HP, but no longer restores any FP. The Spaghetti Plate was also given the ability to cure [[Poison (status effect)|poison]]. The Spaghetti Plate is created by [[Saffron]], who makes it from a Fresh Pasta Bunch, like Zess T. in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''.
