'''Katakata Yariho '''(カタカタヤリホー) is an enemy that appears in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''. 
[[File:MvsDK SpearGuy.png|thumb|17x17px]]
They are pink [[Shy Guy (toy)|Shy Guy]] toys that sleep on a platform if [[Mario]] jumps on the same platform, they will wake up and run towards him. If they touch Mario, they will harm him and if they reach the end of the platform they will fall back asleep.
