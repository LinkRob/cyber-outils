'''Samantha Kelly Courtney''' (born June 14, 1967 in Kenya) is an American voice actress who was the voice for [[Princess Peach]], [[Toad (character)|Toad]], [[Toadette]], and [[Baby Peach]] in the [[Mario series|Mario Universe]] from 2007 to 2024. Samantha started her role as these characters starting at ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. From then on, she had been the voice actress for these four characters.

On June 6, 2025, she announced that she would no longer be voicing Princess Peach and Toad, as Nintendo recast the role for both characters.<ref>samanthakellypeach (June 6, 2025). [https://www.instagram.com/samanthakellypeach/p/DKj0BYwxhgj/ "''Thank you for so many years of friendship and joy. I’m sad that it’s over, I truly would have wanted to voice Peach and Toad forever. Nintendo let me know yesterday that they decided to recast these roles. I’m grateful that I got to do these voices for so many years. Peach and Toad are such strong and beautiful characters that I pray they live forever no matter who voices them. So much love🩷#princesspeach #toad''" ]''Instagram''. Retrieved June 8, 2025.</ref> [[Courtney Lin]], [[Laila Berzins]], and [[Laura Stahl]] would take over the role of Peach, Toad, and Toadette respectively beginning with ''[[Mario Kart World|World]]''.
