'''Artichokers''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are strange, plant-like enemies resembling artichokes, are are only encountered at [[Booster Pass]]. Outside of battle, Artichokers resemble shrubs that may hold items, but if [[Mario]] checks a wrong shrub, an Artichoker attacks.

==Battle==
In battle, Artichokers can attack physically but can use strong spells such as [[Static E!]]. They can even use a [[Stench|spraying move]] that may [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] someone on Mario's team. Artichokers have the ability to [[Recover|heal]] themselves, possibly due to being plants.

===Strategy===
Artichokers are weak to both Jump and Fire moves so Mario is by far the best choice against the Artichokers. Wearing an Antidote Pin or bring [[Able Juice]]s to the battle will help against any poisoning.
