The '''Water Element Medal''' is an item in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Water Element Medal is one of the three [[Element Medal]]s, the other two being the [[Fire Element Medal]] and the [[Ice Element Medal]]. The Water Element Medal allows the [[Poltergust 3000]] to vacuum in a [[Water Elemental Ghost]], giving it a limited supply of water, which can either water plants (most revealing [[treasure]] the first time around), extinguish [[Fire (element)|fire]] blocking a doorway, defeat a [[Spark (ghost)|Spark]] without it exploding, and expose a [[Temper Terror]]'s [[Ghost's heart|heart]], as it is protected by the element of fire.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Water Element Medal is the second Element Medal obtained by [[Luigi]]. It is contained within a large [[treasure chest]] in the [[Kitchen (Luigi's Mansion)|Kitchen]], where it is revealed after vacuuming up every [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]]. Like with the other two Element Medals, the effects of the Water Element Medal are not shown in a [[Mirror (object)|mirror]].
