'''Ridiculous Relay''' is a 1-vs-3 [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
The solo player and team players must outrace one another in four different vehicles. The solo player is hang-gliding and must dodge [[Bullet Bill]]s and [[Paragaloomba|Flying Goomba]]s on the way. Hitting any enemy slows the player down. The team players each partake in a three-legged course on water. The first player rows a [[Red Shell]] with an oar. The second player operates a [[Skeeter]] vehicle by positioning the legs to glide across the water. The third player drives a Bullet Bill motorboat. When the second player starts going, a [[Thwomp]] rises up, letting them continue. This also happens with the third player. The first side to cross the finish line wins.

==Controls==
===Solo (1 player)===
*{{button|n64|Stickup}} '''/''' {{button|n64|Stickdown}} &ndash; Move
*{{button|n64|Stickright}} &ndash; Speed up
*{{button|n64|Stickleft}} &ndash; Slow down

===Group (3 players)===
====Shell====
*{{button|n64|A}} &ndash; Paddle right oar
*{{button|n64|B}} &ndash; Paddle left oar

====Water bug vehicle====
*{{button|n64|Stickup}} '''+''' {{button|n64|A}} '''→''' {{button|n64|Stickdown}} '''+''' {{button|n64|A}} '''→''' {{button|n64|Stickup}} '''+''' {{button|n64|B}} '''→''' {{button|n64|Stickdown}} '''+''' {{button|n64|B}} &ndash; Repeat to proceed

====Motorboat====
*{{button|n64|A}} (Repeatedly) &ndash; Proceed
