'''Shroobified''' is a term used in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' for enemies that have shroob-like features, such as purple skin, red eyes etc. [[Shroobs]] have the ability to shoot purple lasers that turn anything they touch into [[Shroob Mushroom|shroob mushrooms]].
