A '''Dark Muth''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
Dark Muths are the strongest versions of [[Muth]]s that can be found in rooms 51 and 98 of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Dark Muths are extremely powerful enemies having a total HP of 255 and an attack of 10 (with the HP being the same as the first [[Brobot]] fight). Dark Muths are often the most feared enemies in the game despite only appearing a few rooms. Dark Muths are also the most powerful regular enemy in the entire game and defeating one will result in tons of points. If one wants to take out a Dark Muth, [[Bowser]] is highly recommenced especially with power-up items such as [[Bone-In Cut]]s.
