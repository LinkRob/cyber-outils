'''Copy Flowers''' are a type of [[Bros. Item]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is a two-headed purple flower with a yellow face. A Copy Flower costs 160 [[coin]]s to purchase. During battle, when either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] use a Copy Flower, several clones of both of them, [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]] are created that proceed to attack the selected enemy.
