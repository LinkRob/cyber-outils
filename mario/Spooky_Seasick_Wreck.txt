{{Nihongo|'''Spooky Seasick Wreck'''|ゆらゆら幽霊船|Yurayura yūreisen|Swaying Ghost Ship}} is the second level of World 6 in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The coastal-themed level takes place on haunted ships at the rainy area. The dark fogs sharrow the water under the ship implied by the ships' tilt. [[Bully|Bullies]] are predominant to the level. Ships in this level have broken and tilted platforms. Inside the ship are ghosts including [[Peepa]]s and [[Boo]]s, there is water flooding under the ship.

==[[Green Star]]s==
*'''Green Star 1:''' Taking place on the second ship encountered, the player must barge the two Bullies of the ship. After completing it, the Green Star will appear.
*'''Green Star 2:''' Inside the ship, on the section where there are two [[Question Block|? Blocks]]. The Green Star can be found inside the platform hole, on the bottom where the circling [[Peepa]]s forming a small circle.
*'''Green Star 3:''' Taking place on a huge crooked wooden platform, the Green Star can be found right at its spot.

==Stamp==
[[File:Spooky_Seasick_Wreck_Stamp.png|thumb]]
After transporting from the [[Warp Box]], the stamp is found above a wooden platform where the Peepas are circling.
