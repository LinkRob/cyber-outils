'''Twirlips''' are an enemy featured in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. At first, a Twirlip appears to be in the shape of an onion, but upon being approached the Twirlip opens up, revealing a flower with four pink petals and a large yellow center. It spins around the area, attempting to attack [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]. In either form, a Twirlip can be defeated from being [[jump]]ed on, but while in its onion-shaped form, a Twirlip can be defeated from any other attack.
