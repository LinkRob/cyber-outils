|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Infatuate''' is a special move used by [[Vivian]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Infatuate can be used once Vivian reaches the [[Ultra Rank]] and requires 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Effects==
To use Infatuate, the player must press {{button|gcn|a}} when the heart appears over an enemy's head. When successful, this move can [[Confused|Confuse]] all enemies making their attacks miss sometimes.
