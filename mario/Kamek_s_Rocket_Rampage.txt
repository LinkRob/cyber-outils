'''Kamek's Rocket Rampage''' is a boss minigame featured in [[Airship Central]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. It features the characters fighting [[Kamek]], and features a daytime to nighttime transition as players damage him. Like other boss minigames, whoever scores the most points is the winner.

== Introduction ==
[[Kamek]] appears flying on his broom, casting a spell to grow bigger as he laughs.

== Gameplay ==
The characters, flying airplanes, have to grab missiles that they can find and fire them. If Kamek collides with a missile, he takes damage. Kamek can retaliate by summoning [[Bullet Bill|Bullet Bills]] in various formations. The characters will drop their missiles should Kamek hit them with a Bullet Bill, which takes away a point. The missiles earn points based on their size: if small, they give two points, while big ones give five. At half health or lower, Kamek will summon [[Banzai Bill|Banzai Bills]] and [[Big Amp|Big Amps]].

Kamek has 147 hit points in total, across both phases. The player who lands the finishing blow gets 5 extra points before the result screen is shown. The player with the most points overall wins the minigame. If Kamek is not defeated before five minutes pass, the minigame ends in a tie.

== Ending ==
Kamek gets launched into the sky, bursting into fireworks.

== Controls ==
*D-Pad: Move
*2 Button: Fire missile
