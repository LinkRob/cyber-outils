'''Clear Pipe Cruise''' is the first course in [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] port ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It is unlocked via completing [[King Ka-thunk's Castle]], and completing Clear Pipe Cruise unlocks [[Spooky Seasick Wreck]]. Clear Pipe Cruise is also the fourth course of the [[Bonus Episode]] in the [[Wii U]] version of ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

==Layout==
The level starts on a set of platforms with some [[Fire Piranha Plant]]s, [[Floating Mine|Mine]]s, and [[Clear Pipe]]s nearby, followed by a [[Warp Box]] which transports the player to the next area. After avoiding some enemies, the player reaches the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. A nearby Clear Pipe takes the player to an upwards slope filled with Clear Pipes with [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] in them. A Warp Box and a [[Piranha Plant]]-infested area follows, allowing the player to reach an area where five [[Key Coin]]s need to be collected in order to progress. The last Warp Box of the level transports the player to an area with [[Captain Toad]] and the [[Goal Pole]].

==Collectibles==
The following only apply to ''Super Mario 3D World'' and its remake, not ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''.

===Green Stars===
#In the second section of Clear Pipes, there is a [[Starman]]. Use it to break through two Spike Balls in the pipe to obtain the [[Green Star]] between them.
#In the second Clear Pipe loop, there is a Green Star.
#Be in [[Fire Mario|Fire]] form to throw [[fireball]]s that can break through two Spike Balls to reveal a pipe that leads to a [[Cloud Cannon]], leading up to a [[Coin Heaven]] where the Green Star is at the end.

===Stamps===
[[File:Clear Pipe Cruise Stamp.png|frame|left]]
After the checkpoint, there is a long clear pipe. Rather than launching yourself through the pipe, jump on top of it. You may need a cat suit, seeing as the pipe curves upward, but if you want to you could also try to jump up. If you succeed, the stamp will be right there.
{{br|left}}
==Optional mission==
* An 8-bit Luigi can be found on the clear pipe on the curve, under the stamp.
* In ''Captain Toad: Treasure Trackers'', another 8-bit Luigi is at the bottom of a pillar near the first Fuzzies.
