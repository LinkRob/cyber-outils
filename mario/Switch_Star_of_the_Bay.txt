'''Switch Star of the Bay''' is the sixth [[mission]] of [[Jolly Roger Bay]] in [[Super Mario 64 DS]] only.

== Description ==
To collect the sixth [[Power Star]] from [[Jolly Roger Bay]] in [[Super Mario 64 DS]], [[Luigi]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] using a Luigi cap) must use the [[Vanish Cap]] to find a switch for the Star. At the start of the level Luigi needs to swim down in the water to find the hole in the level's wall. Once he has gone through the hole, there will be an area that contains a switch. Once he has pressed the switch, he must use the Vanish Cap to go through the gate in the same area and collect the Star within a time limit. This is the second Switch Star mission.
