== Profile ==
A Toopa Striker is a Koopa Troopa, a [[Koopa Troop]] member. The Toopa Striker strongly resemble the [[Koopa Striker]]. However, it wears a green-and-yellow striped T shirt, green-and-white striped shoes, and yellow shorts. Its shell is reminiscent to the [[Wikipedia:Football (association)|soccer ball]].

In gameplay, Toopa Strikers are found in [[The Bitlands]]. Two Toopa Strikers ambushes the player. Toopa Strikers cooperate to target the player.<ref name="SPM tattle"/><ref name="SPM Catch Card">{{Cite video game|title=[[Super Paper Mario]] |developer=[[Intelligent Systems]], Nintendo SPD Group No.3|publisher=[[Nintendo]]|date=2007-04-09|quotesource=''Toopa Striker's'' Catch Card description|quote=Max HP: 8, Attack: 2, Defense: 1. Toopa Strikers are true team players. Watch out for the no-look shell-to-face assists!}}</ref> The Toopa Striker performs tricky moves.<ref group="note" name="Tricky moves">The Toopa Striker will do the following moves:
*The enemy kicks the shell, sliding on the ground.
*The enemy kicks the shell upwards.
**It may be the same height as the enemy or higher.
*Their eyes flash before kicking the shell. The shell will slide on the ground with flames. It has a short range.
</ref> The player can counter the shell towards the Toopa Striker. The player can stomp the Toopa Striker; it will hide in its shell, the player or another Toopa Striker can kick the shell. Toopa Strikers can [[flip]] dimensions.

==Behind the scenes==
=== Etymology ===
Toopa Striker derives from [[Koopa Striker]]. The Japanese name, ''Tsuinshūtā'' translates to ''twin shooter''. ''Twin'' derives from ''twi'' meaning ''two''. A ''twin'' is one of two children born to the same mother at almost the same time. This implies two Toopa Strikers as twins.
