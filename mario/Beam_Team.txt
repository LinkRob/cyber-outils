'''Beam Team''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] from ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. Its name is a pun on the term "dream team."

==Layout==
The setting of the minigame is based on real-life {{wp|Stonehenge}}.

==Opening==
The solo player is first seen. The other three players in [[UFO]]s appear and try to capture the solo player, while the solo player escapes.

==Gameplay==
The three players in UFOs must move around and capture the solo player with their tractor beams, while the solo player has to avoid them. The players controlling the UFOs have to avoid knocking into each other, or they stop moving for a moment. The team players win if they capture the solo player. The solo player wins if they survive until 30 seconds are up.

==Ending==
[[File:Beam Team ending.png|thumb|right|The ending to Beam Team if the team players win]]
If the team wins, the UFO that caught the solo player goes to the center of the trio, who cheers. If the solo player wins, the team players' UFOs collapse while the solo player celebrates.
