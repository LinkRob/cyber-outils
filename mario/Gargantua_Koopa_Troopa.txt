'''Gargantua Koopa Troopas''',<ref>Hodgson David, ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3'' Prima Official Game Guide</ref> also named '''Big Koopa Troopas''',<ref name=big>''New Super Mario Bros. U'', [[Big Goomba Bounce Bash]] [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xpWVbi00KOU&t=9m52s Bonus Video description]</ref> '''Big Koopas''',<ref name=big/> or '''Giant Koopa Troopas''',<ref>''Nintendo Power'' Volume 10, page 21.</ref> are enlarged versions of normal [[Koopa Troopa]]s that appear throughout the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]].

==History==
===''Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!''===
In the 1986 anime ''[[Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]'', an unusually large green Koopa Troopa appears during a walking montage scene. It looks curiously at a passing [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Haru-ōji|Kibidango]] before jumping on them. However, Mario uses the power of the [[Super Mushroom|mushroom]] he had recently acquired to push it off of them by growing even larger than it. The next shot of it shows it apparently regressed to normal size, staring at the super-sized Mario next to a similarly shocked [[Buzzy Beetle]].

===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Bros. 3''====
[[File:Big Koopa Troopa.png|frame|left]]
Gargantua Koopa Troopas, make their first game appearance in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and reside only on [[Giant Land]] (along with other gigantic creatures like [[Grand Goomba]]s and [[Piranhacus Giganticus]]). [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have a harder time avoiding these Koopas because of their increased size. Despite this, if the plumbers [[stomp]] on a Gargantua Koopa Troopa, it hides inside its [[Giant Green Shell|green]] or [[Giant Red Shell|red]] shell, just like an average-sized Koopa Troopa does when it feels threatened. Once inside, the plumbers can kick their shells away. Despite it's larger size, a Gargantua Koopa Troopa isn't any more durable than a basic Koopa Troopa and can be defeated in the same ways, with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Tanooki Suit]], [[Hammer Suit]], or a [[Starman]]. 

In the [[Nintendo Power|Nintendo Power Strategy Guide]], they are split between '''Green Gargantua Koopa Troopas'''<ref name=redgreen>''Nintendo Power'' Volume 13, page 8.</ref> and '''Red Giant Koopa Troopas'''.<ref name=redgreen/>
{{br}}

====''Super Mario 64'' / ''Super Mario 64 DS''====
In ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[Super Mario 64 DS|Nintendo DS remake]], a single Gargantua Koopa Troopa known as [[Koopa the Quick]] appears on the [[Bob-omb Battlefield]] and [[Tiny-Huge Island]] as a friendly racing rival to Mario.

====''Super Mario Galaxy 2''====
[[File:GargantuaKoopaSMG2.png|thumb|Three Gargantua Koopa Troopas in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.]]
Gargantua Koopa Troopas reappear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' in the [[Supermassive Galaxy]]. Only three are found patrolling a solitary planetoid where they attempt to flatten Mario if he gets in their way. They can only be defeated with a [[Spin Drill]] by drilling into their belly from the opposite side of the planet. If [[Mario]] performs a [[Long Jump]] on a Gargantua Koopa Troopa so that he catches up with it each time, he can get [[Extra life|1-UP]]s for every consecutive time he stomps onto it after seven initial consecutive jumps, allowing maximum lives to be gained. Once a Gargantua Koopa Troopa is defeated, it drops one [[coin]].

====''New Super Mario Bros. U'' / ''New Super Luigi U''====
[[File:NSMBU Jungle of the Giants Screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Gargantua Koopa Troopa in ''New Super Mario Bros. U''.]]
Gargantua Koopa Troopas also appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. They are very rare, and only appear in the levels [[Jungle of the Giants]] and [[Giant Swing-Along]]. They behave similarly to regular-sized Koopa Troopas except they walk slower and they have a slightly different dance. A Gargantua Koopa Troopa can be forced in it's shell by stomping on it and then kicked around, the Gargantua Koopa Troopa's shell is also strong enough to shred through masses of Brick Blocks without ricochetting. Unlike ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', carrying their Shells is now impossible. Gargantua Koopa Troopas can be defeated with ground pounds or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]], the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]] can freeze them but they break free instantly due to their size.

This is the first game where the Gargantua Koopa Troopa as a species is bipedal and wears shoes. 

====''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Super Mario Maker 2''====
[[Image:Gargantua Koopa Troopa Maker.png|thumb|right|A Gargantua Koopa Troopa in ''Super Mario Maker''.]]
Gargantua Koopa Troopas reappear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as it's [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|two]] [[Super Mario Maker 2|sequels]]. In these games, a Gargantua Koopa Troopa can be made by dragging a [[Super Mushroom]] to a Koopa Troopa. They act the same like in ''New Super Mario Bros. U'', in all themes and can be defeated with ground pounds or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]]. A Gargantua Koopa Troopa can also be given wings to become a [[Colossal Paratroopa]]. Gargantua Koopa Troopas now alsohave the ability to break [[Hard Block]]s.

===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
A single Gargantua Koopa Troopa named [[Hookbill the Koopa]] appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' alongside ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. Hookbill the Koopa was a regular Koopa Troopa that was turned into a Gargantua Koopa Troopa by [[Kamek]]'s magic. Hookbill the Koopa acts as the boss of [[World 4 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 4]] and can be defeated by tossing [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] at his face and ground pounding him.

===''Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', a greedy and nearsighted Gargantua Koopa Troopa named [[Kent C. Koopa]] appears as a boss in the game. He is known as a bully and refuses to let anyone pass by him unless they pay his fee of 100 [[coin]]s. If anyone refuses his fee he will not let them pass and if they disobey him he will proceed to beat anyone up unmercifully. Mario and his partners had to fight him in order to return to [[Koopa Village]]. He also taught the [[Koopa Bros.]] how to fight. 

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
[[Image:Gargantua Koopa Troopa Blocker.png|thumb|left|A Gargantua Koopa Troopa blocking a path.]]
Gargantua Koopa Troopas reappear in the remake of [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga|''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'']], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' replacing the [[Ankoopa]]s found throughout the game. They have the same dialogue and purpose as the Ankoopas from the original game. They block various paths which lead to latter areas of the game and can be knock over once the [[Fire Tickle]] is mastered. A non-obstructive one also appears in a house in [[Beanbean Castle Town]].
