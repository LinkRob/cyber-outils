|games = ''[[Super Smash Bros. 3DS/Wii U]]''}}'''Shadow Mario Paint''' is the Final Smash used by [[Bowser Jr.]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. 3DS/Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. Bowser Jr. transforms into [[Shadow Mario]] from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' and creates a big X across the screen. 

==Description==
When Shadow Mario Paint is used, a giant, orange X is put on the screen it will deal constant damage until, after about 10 seconds, explodes sending everyone in range away with rather high knockback. Due to the range and nature of this attack, Shadow Mario Paint is one of the most difficult Final Smashes to avoid taking any damage from. This Final Smash also [[Breaking the Fourth Wall|breaks the fourth wall]].

==Trivia==
*Shadow Mario Paint is also shared by the [[Koopalings]].
*Although the user transforms into Shadow Mario to use this move, Shadow Mario Paint is a stage-wide Final Smash rather than a transforming one.
*Shadow Mario Paint's name is a reference to ''[[Mario Paint]].''
