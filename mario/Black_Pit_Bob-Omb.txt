The '''Black Pit Bob-Omb''' is a large [[Bob-omb]] capable of creating explosions. It only appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Rolling Down the River]]."

== History ==
In [[Rolling Down the River]], [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] ordered [[Mouser]] to bring him a Black Pit Bob-Omb. King Koopa lit the Bob-omb's fuse and threw it into the river near [[Ding Bell]]. The Black Pit Bob-Omb's explosion created a black pit.<ref>{{cite episode| title = Rolling Down the River| series = The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!| airdate = 1989-09-11| season = 1| number  = 5}}</ref>
