'''Icy Donut Lifts''' (also called '''Ice Donut Lifts''') are a light-blue, [[ice]]-covered variation of [[Donut Lift]]s in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. They function similarly to Donut Lifts but are slippery from being covered in ice.

Icy Donut Lifts are primarily found in snowy or icy areas as well as frozen caverns. They appear in various levels of [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 4]] as well as [[World Mushroom-A]] and [[World Flower-A]]. Icy Donut Lifts work almost identically to regular Donut Lifts, but because of their icy properties, if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] walks or runs along one, their traction is greatly reduced (like when walking on ice), and they slide along them. Like regular Donut Lifts, when stood on, they turn red and then descend if stood on for too long. Sometimes the use of Icy Donut Lifts is required for reaching lower platforms.

Some levels feature longer Icy Donut Lifts, which are sometimes used to obtain certain [[Star Coin]]s.
