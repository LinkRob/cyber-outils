A '''Spear-Mask''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Spear-Mask wears either a red, a yellow, or a dark blue robe with a light blue mask showing only a pair of large eyes. A Spear-Mask also holds out a short spear in front of it horizontally.

===Behavior===
If [[Wario]] touches a Spear-Mask's spear, he takes damage. Wario can change both the color and behavior of a Spear-Mask by [[Ground Pound]]ing near it. The cycle of a Spear-Mask's color goes from yellow to blue, then from blue to red, and then back to yellow. All Spear-Masks patrol a given area, although blue Spear-Masks also spin their spear around before changing direction whereas red Spear-Masks charge at Wario upon noticing him.

Each type of Spear-Mask drops a different [[coin]] upon being defeated; yellow ones drop a [[Small Coin]], blue ones drop a [[Bronze Coin]], and red ones drop a [[Silver Coin (Wario Land 4)|Silver Coin]].
