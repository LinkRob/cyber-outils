The '''Twinsy Tropics Dungeon''' is a dungeon area located under [[Twinsy Tropics]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. It is used as a prison and mining facility by [[Paper Bowser]]'s and [[Bowser]]'s respective [[Koopa Troop|forces]].

==History==
===First visit===
The Mario Bros are first taken to the dungeon after fighting [[Big Pokey]] and [[Tower Power Pokey]]. After being knocked out by a cannonball shot from [[Bowser's Castle]], the trio are captured by the [[Koopalings]] and [[Chargin' Chuck|Chargin' Chuck Corps]] and imprisoned in a cell in the dungeon.

After a quick escape courtesy of [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]], the trio find themselves with no [[Command Block]]s, with them all having been taken earlier by the [[Hammer Bro]]s. standing guard. At this point, they must hunt down the Hammer Bros. and use a mixture of [[First Strike]]s and counter attacks to defeat them, gaining back one Command Block or [[Bros. Attack]] after each battle.

After getting their abilities back, the trio come across a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who explains how the Toads are being forced to mine [[Fire Ore]] for an unexplained purpose (which was later revealed to be a fuel source for [[Neo Bowser Castle (Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam)|Neo Bowser Castle]]). They also free the [[Crinkle Goomba|Paper Goomba]] they came across earlier in exchange for a deck of [[Battle Card (Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam)|Battle Card]]s. Before they can escape, they battle [[Roy Koopa]] and [[Wendy O. Koopa]], and notice a gate that can be opened with a [[Red medallion]].

===Second visit===
Later in their adventure, the trio revisits the dungeon after facing off with [[Papercraft Bowser Jr.]] on [[Mount Brrr]] and getting the Red Medallion they needed earlier. They come across the Paper Goomba from earlier, who betrays them and hands the medallion over to the new [[Fire Bro]] wardens.

After a quick hunt for the medallion via fighting the Fire Bros. littered around the dungeon, the party opens the mysterious gate seen earlier, which leads to a new part of the dungeon filled with conveyor belt and [[Horned Ant Trooper]]s. They then follow this path out to a new area of Twinsy Tropics, which leads to [[Bowser's Castle]].
