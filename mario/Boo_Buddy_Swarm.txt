'''Boo Buddy Swarms''' are small [[Boo]]s in masses of attack. They make their debut appearance in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==In ''Super Mario World''==
[[File:Donut GhostHouse.PNG|thumb|left|200px|Fire Mario looking up at a Boo Buddy Swarm in ''Super Mario World'']]
Boo Buddy Swarms are encountered in the [[Donut Ghost House]] and the [[Forest Ghost House]]. They are always found at the top of the screen. Boo Buddy Swarms often move around quickly phasing in and out. The Boos that are visible will often swoop down at [[Mario]] attempting to damage him. Boo Buddy Swarms cannot take damage from any attack, and must only be avoided.
{{br|left}}
==In ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''==
Here, they are named '''Boo Crew''', and are encountered as enemies.
