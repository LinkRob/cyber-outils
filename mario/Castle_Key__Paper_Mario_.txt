'''Castle Keys''' are a set of three key items in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Castle Key, as the name suggests, is a key that opens a key within a castle. There are three different types of Castle Keys in ''Paper Mario'', and each of these types of keys open doors in [[Tubba Blubba's Castle]], [[Bowser's Castle]], and [[Princess Peach's Castle]] respectively. All three of these are keys with a '♣' shape at the handle, in green, grey, and pink colors respectively.

The Castle Key in Princess Peach's Castle is not obtained by [[Mario]] but rather [[Princess Peach]] herself. She is given a key by [[Gourmet Guy]] after Mario completes Chapter 4, and this key leads Peach to the first floor of the castle (as Gourmet Guy requested Peach to bake him a [[cake]] in the kitchen). After Mario has completed Chapter 6, a [[Koopatrol]] gives another key to Peach if she is using [[Sneaky Parasol]] to disguise herself as another solider, and this key grants the princess access to the front door of the castle. While outside of the castle, she cannot access Bowser's Castle, however.
