|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Super Flame '''is a fire magic move used by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It resembles [[Fire Orb]], but is more powerful.

==Usage==
When the move is used, Mario will release a large amount of [[fireball]]s that will deal serious damage to a single enemy.

==Trivia==
*The move is learned at Level 10.
**It costs 9 [[Flower Point]]s to use the move.
