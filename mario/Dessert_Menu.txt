The '''Desert Menu''' is a type of [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be unlocked via completing the quest [[Accessorize!]].

==Summary==
The place itself seems to be in the clouds. It is also in relation to the related [[Cake Maker]] Gaddget.

First, when playing the Gaddget, the player has to first choose between a strawberry, banana or watermelon. Then, red, cyan or pink ice-cream, following up with choosing a cup, an ice-cream holder or a plate. Then, these things all become bundled together to make a type of fancy sundae, cake, etc. for the player to see.
