'''Switch Star of the Fortress''' is the seventh [[mission]] of [[Whomp's Fortress]] in [[Super Mario 64 DS]] only.

== Description ==
This is the seventh mission in Whomp's Fortress. It is a Switch Star mission, where Mario, Luigi, or Wario had to find the switch on the new area of the fortress not present in the original game. The switch was obscured by an orange block, so Yoshi cannot do this level without the use of caps. After hitting the switch, the player must run to the other side of the fortress along the new grassy area to nab it before the timer runs out, or else the Star will disappear and he has to do it all over again. At this point, if the player was using a cap on Yoshi (Mario Caps appear by default in the first two missions of the course making it possible for Yoshi to get this Star before unlocking Mario), they may do the mission as Yoshi. This mission is the first [[Switch Star]] mission.
