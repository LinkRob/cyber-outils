The '''Fearless Pin''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When any user equips the Fearless Pin, the item makes them invulnerable to [[Fear]] attacks. The item can be obtained after defeating [[Yaridovich]] and arriving at [[Rose Town]].
