'''Droppers''' are one of the enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. They are mainly found in [[Rice Beach]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Droppers bear a somewhat similar appearance to [[Spiny|Spinies]], especially given their spiky shell, so it is possible that Droppers are based on Spinies. The biggest difference with a Dropper is that it is a gastropod, not a [[Koopa]], and its sticky belly allows it to stick to surfaces.

===Behavior===
A Dropper is attached to the ceiling, and if [[Wario]] goes near a Dropper, it tries to land on him, dealing damage.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
Droppers are mainly encountered in [[Rice Beach]]. While a Dropper is stunned, Wario can pick it up and throw the Dropper to defeat it. In doing so, the Dropper releases a coin.
