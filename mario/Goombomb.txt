'''Goombombs''' are explosives that appear in the ''[[Nintendo Adventure Books]]'' story ''[[Monster Mix-Up]]''. They were created by [[Foomby]] when she and her brother [[Goomby]] lead the assault.

== Description ==
Goombombs resemble [[Amalgamates|Goomba Troopae]]. However, they can blow up to deal damage. It is not known how their detonation works, but it does spare its user, in this case Foomby or Goomby.

== Appearances ==
After they get captured when [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] attack the Goomba Troopa pack, Goomby and Foomby suddenly deploy some Goombombs for their defense. Mario and his friends have to find which Goomba Troopae are actually Goombombs. Getting the puzzle right means Mario knocks out the Goombada twins, and the bombs do not explode. Failure means that the Goombombs explode.

== Trivia ==
*The Goombombs are located at slots B and D.
