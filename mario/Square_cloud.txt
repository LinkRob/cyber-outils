A '''square cloud''' is an object in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Description==
A square cloud is a white [[block]] with a smiling face, resembling a [[Lakitu's Cloud]]. Whenever a square cloud is hit, it turns into a [[Lakitu]], which appears onscreen temporarily to throw down [[coin]]s. Aside from this, these Lakitus are no different than regular Lakitus, which also appear in the level.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''==
A square cloud only appears in [[World 4-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4-5]].
