'''Bounty Beach''' is the first racetrack that appears in the 2001 build of ''[[Diddy Kong Pilot (2001 build)|Diddy Kong Pilot]]''. It is the first racetrack of the world Sleepy Shores, according to the [[Clock Race]] menu screen.

This track shares its name with a [[Banana Bird Cave]] in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!|Donkey Kong Country 3]]'', also named Bounty Beach.
