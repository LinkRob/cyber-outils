'''Box Boys''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are haunted treasure chest enemies found only in [[Bean Valley]].

== Description ==
Box Boys resemble [[Pandorite|Pandorites]] in their treasure chest form, though grey. Exposing them reveals a [[Peepa]]-esque ghost.

==Battle==
In battle, Box Boys often attack physically but can use the deadly [[Carni-Kiss]] move. Box Boys can also summon genie-like enemies called [[Fautso]]s to aid them in battle. Box Boys also have very high stats which is some of the highest stats of all of the enemies in the game.

===Strategy===
Box Boys are weak against Jump attack so it is recommended to use [[Mario]]'s strongest jump move [[Ultra Jump]]. Having [[Geno]] use his [[Geno Boost]] onto Mario to further increase the strength. Defeating a Box Boy results in 100 [[Experience Point]]s and 150 [[coin]]s.
