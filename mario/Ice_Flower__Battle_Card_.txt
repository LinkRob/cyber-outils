|name=Ice Flower (Battle Card)}}'''Ice Flower''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Ice Flower is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s ice [[Flower attack.]] It is a sentient blue flower with a green stem, accompanied with a blue/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 40/200 coins

Sell Price: 20/100 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Ice Flower is first seen in [[Ruddy Road]], but can also found in [[Marmalade Valley]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Plum Park]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Redpepper Volcano]], [[Redpepper Crater]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
