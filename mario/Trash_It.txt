'''Trash It''' is a minigame that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player must drop a certain object into the appropriate disposal. This is done using a horizontal grid and moving around while pressing the {{button|ds|x}} and {{button|ds|b}} buttons. As the difficulty increases, so does the rate of which the items drop. The player must drop a certain number of items within the time limit in order to complete this minigame.

===List of items that go with their disposal===
*Garlic → Mouth
*Coin → Money bag
*Poop → Toilet
*Gem → Chest
*Baseball → Baseball glove
