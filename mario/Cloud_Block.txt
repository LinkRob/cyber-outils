A '''Cloud Block''' is a type of platform which appears in a few games of the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', where they resemble a [[Lakitu's Cloud]]. They are generally used as platforms in a [[Coin Heaven]] or areas hidden above the ground. Cloud Blocks generally can be passed through below, and stood on above, akin to Semisolid platforms, although they are completely solid in ''Super Mario Bros.,'' and even act as walls that must be jumped over.
