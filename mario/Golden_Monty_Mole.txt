'''Golden Monty Moles'''<ref>''"Monty Moles are worth one point. Golden Monty Moles are worth three points. Piranha Plants will temporarily stun you."'' - Mole-it! minigame advice, ''[[Mario Party 6]]''</ref> are a golden variant of [[Monty Mole]] that appear exclusively in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Profile==
Golden Monty Moles resemble normal Monty Moles, except with golden fur and a red nose.

==History==
Golden Monty Moles appeared exclusively in the minigame [[Mole-it!]]. In the minigame, Golden Monty Moles would function identical to the normal Monty Moles in the minigame by popping their heads out of the [[Warp Pipe|pipe]]s. They appear less frequently than the normal Monty Moles, but they award three points when getting hit instead of the normal Monty Moles who just give one point.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Super Mario Party]]'', the playable Monty Mole can drink the [[Golden Drink]] item to become a different separate golden form.
*Golden Monty Moles are one of the few golden enemies to not be affiliated with ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.
