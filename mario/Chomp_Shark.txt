'''Chomp Sharks''', also named '''Shark Chomps''',<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 128.</ref><ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'' Player's Guide. Pages 73 and 112.</ref> or '''Giant Chomp-Chomps''',<ref>''Nintendo Magazine System'' (UK) issue 43, page 66. "'''''MARCHING MILDE'S FORT''' The first four coins and flower are all out in the open but Yoshi has to find them quickly before he is caught in the razor-toothed jaws of the Giant Chomp-Chomp.''"</ref> are giant sized chainless [[Chain Chomp]]s who constantly move forward while gnashing their jaws. Chomp Sharks primarily appear in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' franchise. They cannot be attacked, so [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s are forced to either run from them or avoid them entirely.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
[[File:SMA3_Chomp_Shark_falling_in_Marching_Milde's_Fort.png|thumb|left|A Chomp Shark is defeated in Marching Milde's Fort in ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'']]
Shark Chomps (named '''Bow Wow''' on one occasion<ref>''Nintendo Magazine System'' (Australia) issue 30, page 12.</ref>) are introduced as one of the three varieties of "Chomps" in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', where they appear in [[Marching Milde's Fort]] and [[KEEP MOVING!!!!]] In the former level, the Shark Chomp appears right after a group of [[Incoming Chomp]]s. Chomp Sharks appear as soon as [[Yoshi]] crosses a threshold at which point they will chase after him chomping away at anything in their path, Chomp Sharks are incredibly dangerous as they can knock [[Baby Mario]] off Yoshi's back and send Yoshi falling to his doom if he’s not careful, if Yoshi successfully makes it to the end, the Chomp Shark will break its teeth on the stone, start crying, before falling down the edge, Chomp Sharks first appear in [[Marching Milde's Fort]] and also appear in [[KEEP MOVING!!!!]] which is named as such  for good reason too. Several Chomp Sharks appear in this level and make backtracking impossible. Chomp Sharks are completely invincible and there is no attack that can destroy them.

====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
[[Image:Chomp Shark DS.jpg|thumb|right|A Chomp Shark chasing Yoshi across the sky.]]
Chomp Sharks reappear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. In this game, they’re much more rare with only one appearing in the whole game. It only appears near the end of [[Rompin' Stompin' Chomps]]. Chomp Sharks behave the same as they did before. Chasing after Yoshi and destroying anything in their way however unlike the [[Super Big Tap-Tap]], a Chomp Shark will not disappear if it goes offscreen for too long, instead it keeps going until it finishes its course so [[Yoshi]] is unable to wait them out. Chomp Sharks are once again invincible and only stop once they damage their teeth on the rock wall after which they will cry and fall down the pit, if Yoshi manages to hit a Chomp Shark with an egg, the Chomp Shark will appear in the "Giant Enemy" section of the [[Island Museum]]. In the exhibit, an infinite supply of Chomp Sharks will periodically race towards the wall, damaging their teeth and falling down the pit.

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[Image:Chomp Shark 3DS.jpg|thumb|left|Yoshi hops to avoid a Chomp Shark.]]
Chomp Sharks  return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', in this game, Chomp Sharks retain their behavior from previous games, chasing after Yoshi and destroying objects in their path, they’re invincible just as before and can only be beaten once they hit their teeth on the wall at the end, unlike previous games however, a Chomp Shark's teeth is not damaged upon hitting the wall but it still cries upon impact and falls into the pit. A Chomp Shark is the main and titular feature of [[Chomp Shark Chase]].

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', a Chomp Shark appears on either side of the [[Yoshi's Island (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Yoshi's Island]] stage as background elements, where they change their behavior depending on the season of the stage, though they do not affect gameplay. In the summer and spring, their mouths are open, while in the winter and fall, they are closed.

==Trivia==
[[File:Yoshi's Story Chomp.gif|frame]]
*Chomp Sharks are one of the only species of [[Chomp]] that possesses the ability to fly given that they use unsupported flight while chasing after [[Yoshi]], something most [[Chomp]]s and [[Chain Chomp]]s lack.
*The Chomps in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', which also constantly move forward while perpetually chomping, are quite similar to and may have been based on the Shark Chomp. The [[Nintendo Power|Nintendo Player's Guide]] also takes note of their in-game size.<ref>Leung, Jason, Terry Munson, and Scott Pelland. ''Yoshi's Story'' Player's Guide. Page 79. "''Three mammoth Chomps guard four melons, and, to borrow a line from another story, “My what big teeth they have!”''"</ref>
