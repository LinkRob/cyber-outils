'''Metal Luigi''' is the metal counterpart to [[Luigi]], specifically encountered as one of the mid-bosses in the Adventure mode of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' encountered before the final boss, [[Bowser]]. He is fought alongside [[Metal Mario]]. Once the player has unlocked Luigi, Metal Luigi will appear in the battle, teaming up with Metal Mario against the player. Like [[Mario]]'s metal counterpart, Metal Luigi is heavier than the regular Luigi and uses the same moves. Additionally, players can pick up the [[Metal Box]] item, turning themselves into metal forms, boasting the same attributes as Metal Luigi and Metal Mario. KOing both the metal brothers in Adventure Mode awards the player 8000 points.

While the character of Metal Luigi does not appear in later ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' games, Luigi can become a Metal form if he picks up a Metal Box or is playing using the option in Special Smash. A metal form of Luigi can also appear as an opponent in Classic Modes.
