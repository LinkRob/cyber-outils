'''Close Call P''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Close Call P, like [[Close Call]], gives the wearer the ability to be "lucky" during combat so long at they are in danger. This, like all other partner badges, is equipped in the same fashion as any other regular badge, but only works for [[Mario]]'s partners. All of Mario's partners are affected by this badge's effect. There is a way to obtain multiples of this badge. Inside of the beginning of the [[Riverside Station]], one of the two Close Call P badges can be found on a pillar. Using special tactics, Mario can obtain the badge. The other Close Call P badge is found inside of the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] in [[Rogueport]]. It is on sale for one hundred [[coin]]s, fifty if it is on sale.
