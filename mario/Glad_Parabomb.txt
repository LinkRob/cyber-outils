'''Glad Parabombs''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Glad Parabombs are a variant of [[Para-bomb]]s and [[Glad Bob-omb]]s, resembling the former, except Glad Parabombs are purple and wear a yellow parachute.

===Behavior===
Like with regular Parabombs, a Glad Parabomb descends using a parachute, except Glad Parabombs do so more dramatically. It acts identically to a regular Parabomb, except when a Glad Parabomb lands, it becomes a Glad Bob-omb, inheriting its behavior as well. Upon exploding, a Glad Parabomb leaves behind strings of confetti.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Glad Parabombs appear in [[Fury Volcano]], [[Wavy Beach]], [[Giddy Sky]], and [[Bowser's Villa]]. They are first encountered in the level [[Fury Volcano 4-2]]. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can knock a Glad Parabomb off of its parachute using any attack, and from there, Peach can defeat it using any attack.
