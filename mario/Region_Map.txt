'''Region maps''' are objects in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They do not appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', since they are made obsolete by the mini-map.

==Description==
A region map is always found on a wall. In their overworld appearance, region maps have only five rooms shown. When [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] consult the map, a basic diagram of the area they are currently in is shown. An animated sprite shows the area of the region the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] are currently in, while the blue sprites on the map show [[Save Album]]s and their locations in the region.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
Region maps are first seen in [[Chucklehuck Woods]] (The Chasteau de Chucklehuck section is not shown.), and are later also found in [[Woohoo Hooniversity]], [[Oho Ocean]], [[Teehee Valley]], [[Gwarhar Lagoon]], and [[Joke's End]].

==Nomenclature==
In French, Region Maps are called Carte du Donjon (which literally means "Map about the Dungeon").
