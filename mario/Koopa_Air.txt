'''Koopa Air''' is a sponsor in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'' ==
Koopa Air appeared in a few courses as trackside banners and signs.
=== List of appearances ===
* Trackside banners in [[Luigi Raceway]]
* Signs in [[Kalimari Desert]]
* Signs in [[Mario Raceway]]
* Signs in [[Royal Raceway]]
