'''Big Bees'''<ref>Hamm & Rudolf GmbH, Frankfurt. ''Super Game Boy'' Player's Guide. Page 22. "''When Mario makes his way into the honeycomb, he should be careful to avoid the big bees that fall down from the ceiling onto Mario. With a little patience, Mario can get around this annoying problem.''"</ref>) is a [[Beebee]] enemy from the [[Tree Zone]] level [[Honeybees (level)|Honeybees]] in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. If [[Mario]] goes near to the Bigbee, it will drop down and then slowly fly back up to its position upon hitting the floor, very similar to [[Suu]] from ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. Unlike Suu, they cannot be [[stomp]]ed, similar to [[Karamenbo]] and [[Thwomp]]s. With the exception of a [[Star]], nothing can defeat them.
