'''World 1-1''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the first level of World 1, [[Birabuto Kingdom]]. This is where [[Mario]] begins his journey to rescue [[Princess Daisy]].

==Overview==
World 1-1 is a desert level with Mario beginning near a [[Warp Pipe]] and a [[Question Block]]. There is also a block containing a [[Super Mushroom]] that Mario can grab. There is another Warp Pipe in this level that takes Mario to an area with many [[coin]]s in it. The main enemies of this level include [[Goombo]]s, [[Fighter Fly|Flies]], and [[Bombshell Koopa]]s.
