|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}The '''Fresh Juice Bar''' (also known as the '''Fresh Juice Shop''' and '''The Watering Hole''') is a location in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and is ran by [[Podler]] and is located in [[Glitzville]].

==History==
During the events of Chapter 3, the mysterious [[Jolene|X]] instructs [[Mario]] to head to "The Watering Hole" meaning the Fresh Juice Bar. After speaking with Polder, Mario will receive the [[Super Hammer]]. Mario and company must head back here during the events of Chapter 7 to look for [[General White]]. [[Podler]] tells them that General White just left to see a "giant tree" meaning Mario and company must head for [[The Great Tree]] next.
