'''Dodge Fuzzy, Get Dizzy''' is a Free-for-All minigame featured in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. Unlike other minigames, Dodge Fuzzy, Get Dizzy's name originates from a ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' level, [[Touch Fuzzy, Get Dizzy]], but the minigame concerns the [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] rather than the [[Fuzzy (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|Fuzzies]] from ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''.

==Gameplay==
Each character stands on a screw-based platform, which functions similar to a [[Screwtop Shroom]] in that it can go upward by spinning while standing on it. The goal for each character is to be the first to reach the top. Fuzzies are the only obstacle they need to avoid, as touching one causes them to be stunned briefly.
