'''Squiglets''' are short dainty creatures that are the low class of its kind in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. When you come close to them, they stop for a moment. Other than that, they walk like [[Goomba]]s. Just stomping on them can bring tragedy. A Squiglet [[Catch Card|card]] is on top of [[Bestovius]]'s house.
