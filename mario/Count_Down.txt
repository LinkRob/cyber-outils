'''Count Down''' is a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a giant clock that is accompanied by two [[Ding-A-Ling]]s, which are two bells mounted on it. Count Down appears to be controlled by the Ding-A-Lings, and all of them work for [[Smithy]] of his [[Smithy Gang|gang]]. Count Down and the Ding-A-Lings are also fought in [[The Factory]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
===Battle===
Count Down is a very unique boss at it uses magical attacks depending on what the time the clock hands land on.

====Attacks====
*At 1:00, it will use either [[Ice Rock]] or [[Crystal]].
*At 3:00, it will use [[Recover]].
*At 5:00, it will use [[Aurora Flash]].
*At 6:00, it will use [[Mega Recover]].
*At 7:00, it will use [[Water Blast]].
*At 9:00, it does nothing.
*At 10:00, it will use [[Petal Blast]].
*At 12:00, it will use [[Corona]].

====Strategy====
The key to defeating Count Down is to defeat the two Ding-A-Lings on its head so that they will not all attack at once. Count Down is also weak to Jump attacks, thunder attacks, and Snowy so Mario and Mallow are highly recommended for this fight.

==Trivia==
*Count Down is the only boss in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' to not utilize any physical attacks, which is due to having an attack power of 0 and only using magical attacks.
*Count Down has more in-battle quotes than any other boss in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''.
