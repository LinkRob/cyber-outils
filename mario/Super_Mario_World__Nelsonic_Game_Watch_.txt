'''''Super Mario World''''' is a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game within the [[Nelsonic Game Watch]] line of wristwatches. It was released in 1991 as a tie-in product to the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[Super Mario World|same name]]. ''Super Mario World'' came bundled with a pair of headphones.
