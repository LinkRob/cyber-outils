'''Bouncing Bullet Bills''' are a gold subspecies of [[Bullet Bill]]s. They appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They have the ability to bounce off any surface after they touch it. They are often found in Raphael the Raven's castle. They can be defeated by being jumped on, swallowed, or spat out by [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]].
