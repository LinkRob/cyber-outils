A '''Megalixir''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Drinking a Megalixir restores 150 [[Heart Point|HP]] to all party members, making the Megalixir more effective than the [[Froggie Drink]] and [[Elixir]]. Megalixirs can be purchased at the item shop on [[Tadpole Pond]] after getting the [[Tenor Card]]. It can be then purchased with that card for 90 [[coin]]s. However, if Mario has a [[Soprano Card]], he can purchase Megalixirs for 60 coins.
