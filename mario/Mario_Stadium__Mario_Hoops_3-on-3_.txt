'''Mario Stadium''' is a basketball court owned by [[Mario]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It was later replaced by a similar indoor sports arena of the [[Mario Stadium (Mario Sports Mix)|same name]] in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''.

==Description==
[[File:MH3o3 Mario Stadium.png|thumb|left|Preview]]
As is typical of sports arenas owned by Mario in spinoff ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', Mario Stadium is a basic court with no hazards or gimmicks, aside from [[? Panel]]s like the other basketball courts. Mario Stadium takes place outside, and has the features of a regular basketball stadium, complete with stands, a big screen, some banners on the sides, and a smooth court where both teams play on.

Mario Stadium still has some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' branding. The audience of [[Goomba]]s, [[Shy Guy]]s, [[Pianta]]s, and [[Toad (species)|Toads]] are cheering on the teams while they play. There is a display in the background that shows a ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''-themed animation of [[Small Mario]] chasing two Goombas. Furthermore, some of the banners have the [[Square Enix]] logo and the [[Nintendo]] logo.

The music used in the Mario Stadium is also used in [[Glare Desert]]. Mario Stadium is where all the tutorials are held, but it has different background music.
