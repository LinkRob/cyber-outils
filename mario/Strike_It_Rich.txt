'''Strike It Rich''' is a [[Rhythm Minigame|Rhythm]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The  second player pose with his/her right arm in the sky. The metronome ticks and the red arrow points upwards.

== Gameplay ==
The minigame plays a ''[[Super Mario|Super Mario Bros.]]'' overworld theme cover that helps players time their hit. The player must thrust the left Joy Con to hit the Block beneath them. If the player times the hit correctly, the [[Red Coin]] will jump out of the [[? Block]] and the player will gain three points; the "Nice!" message indicates the player timed it. If the player hits the block late or early, the [[Coin]] will jump out of the ? Block and the "Early"/"Late" text will appear above the player. At the end of the track, players will encounter [[Brick Block]]s; they must hit the blocks rapidly to gain more coins.

== Ending ==
The winner(s) do(es) their victory pose while other players sulk.

==Controls==
* [[Jump]] - Hit ⬆️ or the jump button
