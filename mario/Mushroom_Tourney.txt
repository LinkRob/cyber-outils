'''Mushroom Tourney''' is the first tournament of ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It consists of four courts: [[Mario Stadium (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Mario Stadium]], [[Koopa Troopa Beach (court)|Koopa Beach]], [[Peach Field]], and the unlockable [[Sunset Beach]].

==Unlockables==
Various content can be unlocked by completing Mushroom Tourney on a certain difficulty or by obtaining a bronze, silver, or gold prize at minimum, depending on the item. 

*[[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]]: Complete on normal with at least a bronze prize.
*[[Princess Peach|Peach]] alternate costume: Complete on normal with at least a silver prize.
*[[Yoshi Egg]]: Complete on normal with a gold prize.
*[[Birdo]]: Complete on hard with at least a bronze prize.
*[[Soccer Ball]] alternate costume: Complete on hard with at least a silver prize.
*[[Cheep Cheep|Cheep-Cheep]] Ball: Complete on hard with a gold prize.
