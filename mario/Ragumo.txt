'''Ragumo''' (ラグモ) is a mole enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' and its various manga adaptations. They are moles with a big drill on their faces and have also been confirmed to be a relative to the [[Monty Mole]] species.<ref>https://www.nintendo.co.jp/n02/dmg/l6j/enemy/index.html</ref>

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'' Ragumo appears exclusively in the lower sections of the first level in [[Tree Zone]], attacking by emerging out of the ground similar to their relatives. However, they are noticeably slow and do not chase [[Mario]] unlike Monty Moles. Ragumo are not particularly strong as they can be defeated by most attacks such as [[jump]]ing onto them or having [[Fire Mario]] throw a [[fireball]] at it.
