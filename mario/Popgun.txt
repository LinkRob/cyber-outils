The '''Popgun''' is a weapon that appears mainly in the [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]].

==In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''==
The Popguns are first seen in ''Donkey Kong 64'' under the name the '''Peanut Popguns'''. They are the personal weapon for [[Diddy Kong]]. They can be bought at [[Funky's Store]] for three [[Banana Coin]]s. Diddy always wield two Peanut Popguns and can fire peanuts fast at his foes. Diddy most likely wields two of them due to the peanuts being the weakest ammunition in the game. The Peanut Popguns are also used alongside Diddy's [[Rocketbarrel Boost]] allowing him to shoot switches or others things in the skies.
