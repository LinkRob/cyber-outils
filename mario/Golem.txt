'''Golem''' (ゴーレム ''Gōremu'') is an enemy from ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for the [[Game Boy]]. Only one makes an appearance, and it walks back and forth. If [[Mario]] touches a Golem, he loses a life.
