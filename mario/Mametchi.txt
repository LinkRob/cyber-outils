'''Mametchi''' is a character from ''{{w|Tamagotchi}}'', a line of egg-shaped digital pet toys, as well as a mascot of the franchise. Mametchi made his first and only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearance in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', as a playable guest driver.

Mametchi is categorized as an Easy Control driver whose stats are the same as [[Ms. Pac-Man|Ms. PAC-MAN]]'s and [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s. Mametchi's special items include [[Bagubagutchi]], [[Mame Block]], [[Chibi Syrup]], [[Mame Drink]], and [[Giant Pencil]]. His voice clips imitate the beeping sound effect heard in Tamagotchi virtual pets. As with the other drivers, aside from a standard [[kart]], Mametchi gets a unique vehicle with slightly reduced [[acceleration]] in favor of increased [[speed]]. Mametchi's unique vehicle loosely resembles a Tamagotchi virtual pet pulled by a wheeled mechanical Tamagotchi wearing a Kasa.

Mametchi makes cameo appearances in adverts of the [[Stadium Arena]] and [[Waluigi Stadium]] courses in the [[Waluigi Cup]].
