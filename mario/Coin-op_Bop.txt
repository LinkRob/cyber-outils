'''Coin-op Bop''' is a 1-vs-3 [[coin]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
Both the solo character and team each stand in front of a slot machine-like device. The solo player is standing at the slot machine on the left while the team is standing in front of the slot machine on the right.

==Gameplay==
Each side has to input the correct sequence of buttons shown on their respective slot machine, and doing so causes them to win three Coins. The solo character has to input every button on their own while the teammates each take turns inputting a button. Pressing the wrong button causes a [[Bob-omb]] to fall on that character and explode, stunning them temporarily. The minigame continues until the 30-second [[Time Limit]] runs out.

==Controls==
*A/B/X/Y/L/R: Press in the order shown on the machine
