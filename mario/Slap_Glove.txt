The '''Slap Glove''' is a weapon used by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. She has it equipped from the start when joining the party. Equipping the Slap Glove increases Princess Toadstool's [[Attack]] rating by 40. At [[Seaside Town]], once [[Yaridovich]] has been defeated, additional Slap Gloves can be purchased for 100 [[coin]]s each.
