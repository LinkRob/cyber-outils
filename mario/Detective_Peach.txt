'''Detective Peach''' is a power-up in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''

==Overview==
Detective Peach involves [[Princess Peach]] donning an outfit reminiscent of {{w|Sherlock Holmes}}' and using a magnifying glass for investigating clues.

===Physical appearance===
In her Detective form, Peach wears a brown woolen detective uniform consisting of a short coat and beige shorts, and a Sherlock Holmes-like hat. A lighter plaid pattern is present on the shoulder area of the coat, taking the form of a short shawl, and on the sides of the hat; under the coat is a white shirt with her usual brooch on the front, peeking out of the collar. Peach's hair is tied back, although the ponytail seems to be obscured within the hat, and her earrings are aqua instead of the usual blue. She also wears white gloves and leggings, and brown knee-high leather boots with lighter soles and pink laces.

==Abilities==
Peach gains the Strike of Intuition power, which she performs by investigating an object with a magnifying glass. She can also use this on [[Theet|Theets]] to expose disguised [[Sour Bunch]] goons.

== Appearances ==
As with the other transformations, Peach uses this power thrice in the game. The first time, she uses the Detective transformation to find who stole a mural from a museum in The Case of the Missing Mural. She later gets the transformation in The Case of the Rainy-Day Plot, and later has to use it again in The Dark Museum & the Purple Mystery.
