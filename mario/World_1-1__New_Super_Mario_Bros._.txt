'''World 1-1''' is the first level of [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. This level is an overworld level which is also the first level in the game.

== Layout ==
This level itself is inhabited by ten [[Goomba]]s overall, and four [[Koopa Troopa]]s, two red and two green. As it is the first level in the game, these enemies are introduced to this level. As a background element, [[Princess Peach's Castle]] can be seen from the beginning section of the level before the first ledge. The terrain of this level is grass-themed with several slopes and [[Warp Pipe]]s. You're also able to see yellow hills in the background.

[[File:NSMB 1-1.png|thumb|left|[[Mega Mario]] rampaging through World 1-1.]]
When [[Mario]] starts the level, he must walk towards three [[Question Block]]s, one with the other two not connected to it and the other two connected to one another. The second block contains a [[Mushroom]] ([[Fire Flower]], if [[Super Mushroom]] is powered already). Also, the very first obstacle in the game is underneath these [[Question Block]]s; a lone [[Goomba]] on which [[Mario]] must avoid. Then, there is a group of three [[coin]]s which can be collected, and another three, making six total. Then, there is a lone [[Brick Block|brick]] on the top of the ledge which contains a single [[coin]] inside of it. Down the ledge is a group of three connected [[Brick Block|bricks]], the center one with a lone [[coin]] and a lone [[Brick Block|brick]] atop the three in midair (containing multiple [[coin]]s, maximum of fourteen total). Then, there are four more [[coin]]s for [[Mario]] to collect along with another [[Goomba]] with [[Warp Pipe]]s to his left and right. Then, the first ledge in the game can be seen. This ledge must be jumped across and can be considered simple, due to it being small. There is another group of four [[coin]]s above it.

On the next section, there is a [[Mega Mushroom]] inside a lone [[Question Block]], which can be used to scavenge through the level easily. Though it is an optional element to the course but makes the course easier. There is also another pair of [[Brick Block|bricks]] in three, the center having a [[Question Block]], along with the third [[Goomba]] in the level underneath the pair of [[Brick Block|bricks]]. Two more [[Brick Block|bricks]] connected together can be seen, both contain a single [[coin]] and a [[coin]] group of four can be seen above the second ledge after the two connected [[Brick Block|bricks]]. There is also a [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] next to the ledge. Then, there are three [[coin]]s that can be seen next to the group of five [[Brick Block|bricks]] in the air, after the [[Warp Pipe]] and the first [[Star Coin]] even higher and next to it. The first and third [[Brick Block|bricks]] contain a [[coin]], while the second contains a [[Super Mushroom|Mushroom]] or a [[Fire Flower]]. Beneath the pair of five [[Brick Block|bricks]] is the first green [[Koopa Troopa]] in the game. Then, there is a red [[Koopa Troopa]] on a pair of nine [[Brick Block|bricks]], the third containing a hidden [[star]] above it. This pair is divided into three and split into sections, one section on the top. There are also three hidden [[Brick Block|blocks]] that give out a single [[coin]] at the bottom. There is also a [[coin]] in the third and seventh visible bottom [[Brick Block|bricks]]. The center-top has a [[beanstalk]] leading to an option secret cloud-themed area with a [[Red Ring|Red Coin Ring]] in it where eight [[Red Coin]]s can be collected for a [[Fire Flower]] or a [[1-up Mushroom|1-Up Mushroom]]. Also downwards, there are four [[coin]]s leading to the bottom.

If not, [[Mario]] must continue a few steps to the [[Midway Point|midway point]] directly after the [[Warp Pipe]] leading to an optional [[Underground theme|underground]] area. In this area, there is a lone [[Goomba]] at first with five [[coin]]s above five [[Brick Block|bricks]]. There is also a hole with four [[coin]]s above it, like always. Then, two [[Question Block]]s on the sides with three [[Brick Block|bricks]] used to connect the [[Question Block]]s together with the [[Brick Block|bricks]] used. The fifth [[Brick Block|brick]] contains a [[Super Mushroom|Mushroom]] or a [[Fire Flower]] and the first and third have a [[coin]]. There is also a [[Warp Pipe]] leading out of the area with the third [[Star Coin]] above it and a formation of four [[coin]]s on the left of the [[Warp Pipe|pipe]].

If this area isn't chosen, [[Mario]] must continue along where there are three lone [[Brick Block|bricks]] not connected that all contain a [[coin]]. Another [[Goomba]] can be found beneath these [[Brick Block|bricks]]. Then, there is a ledge which [[Mario]] needs to jump over and is rather smaller with another four [[coin]]s above it and a [[Question Block]] after it contains a [[Super Mushroom|Mushroom]] or a [[Fire Flower]] (depending on the form) and another ledge, step-formation style with another [[Goomba]] walking down it. Then, there is a small hole with three bricks enclosing the second [[Star Coin]] below it. [[Super Mario]] or [[Fire Mario]] forms are required to get it as small [[Mario]] cannot destroy [[Brick Block|bricks]]. Another [[Goomba]] can be seen next to this [[Brick Block|brick]]-enclosed hole, along with a [[Question Block]] containing a [[coin]] aside another ledge with a hole next to it. A [[Goomba]] can be seen next to a hole with a [[Warp Pipe]] dividing the two nearby [[Goomba]]s. There is also a group-of-four connected [[Brick Block|bricks]], the fourth containing a [[coin]]. Next to these [[Brick Block|bricks]] is a single [[Goomba]] and green [[Koopa Troopa]], along with a nearby ledge with the only formation of three [[coin]]s in the level itself. Then, the final [[Brick Block|brick]] formation in the level is encountered with a red [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]] on top of the [[Brick Block|bricks]], the third [[Brick Block|brick]] having a lone [[coin]] and a hidden [[star]] above the [[Brick Block|bricks]]. Then, [[Mario]] must go up a stair of blocks, like in the original ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' to reach the [[flagpole]] in order to complete the level and move on to [[World 1-2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-2]].

==[[Star Coins]]==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In plain sight after you get the [[Mega Mushroom]]
*'''Star Coin 2''': In an [[Underground theme|underground]] area which can be accessed via a [[Warp Pipe]] next to the [[Midway Point|midway point]] of the level. The [[Star Coin|coin]] is in the top-right corner of this room.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In a gap after the [[Midway Point|midway]] section with a hole splitting the two areas, blocked by three [[Brick Block|bricks]] on a ledge. Small [[Mario]] cannot obtain this [[Star Coin|coin]].
