'''Ribboner''' is a character in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]'' He is encountered multiple times in different plays, and always needs help. When rescued, he gives her a ribbon, which can either be a dress ribbon or a Stella ribbon.

== Description ==
Ribboner is a yellow humanoid creature with a bulbous nose nose that glows different colours depending on his mood, beady eyes, and no mouth, just like his fellow [[Theet]]s. His outfit is a suit that has a top hat and a red bowtie, and a moustache appears where the mouth of a human should go. A small bag with green and blue bowties appears beside him.
