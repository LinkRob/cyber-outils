'''Cheeky Monkey''', not to be confused with the [[Cheeky Monkey (WarioWare: Mega Microgame$!)|version]] from [[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!|first game]], is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''. It returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', specifically within the Twist league to show its roots from ''WarioWare: Twisted!''.

In ''WarioWare: Twisted!'', Cheeky Monkey becomes available by clearing the Smorgasbord Sampler stage, and in ''WarioWare Gold'', it becomes available by clearing the final stage of the Twist league.

== Gameplay ==
There is a little girl and her mouth is stuffed with food. Players have to poke her cheeks to make sure she spits it out. ''WarioWare Gold''{{'}}s version instead features a boy and a four-second time limit, though the gameplay is the same.

On the first difficulty level, the characters who are seen each have one food item in their cheeks. At level 2, you have to poke the girl's cheeks a bit faster, while the boy has more candy in his mouth. At level 3, you have to poke the girl's cheeks constantly until she spits out more candies and at least two bananas, while the boy tries to move the food in his mouth, but there is less than in the second level.

==Trivia==
*The game's objective is the opposite to [[Force Feeding]]. In the latter, players have to help the character (who is an alien) get food in their mouth.
