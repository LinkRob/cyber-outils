'''Dash Yoshi''' is one of the power-ups [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can utilize in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is obtained when Yoshi eats a [[Dash Pepper]].

Dash Yoshi appears as a flaming red-orangeish in color, and runs at super-fast speed with fire trailing behind him with an alarm going off as he is dashing. The extra speed allows him to sprint across water and climb up walls with incredibly steep slopes. It does, however, expire after a short time. Dash Yoshi is prominently featured in [[Hightail Falls Galaxy]].
