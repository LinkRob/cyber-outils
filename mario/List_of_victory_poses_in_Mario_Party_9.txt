== Trivia ==
* These victory poses loop endlessly:
** Luigi's 3rd place animation
** Peach's 4th place animation
** Wario's 1st, 3rd and 4th place animations
** Waluigi's 1st, 3rd and 4th place animations
** Toad's 3rd and 4th place animations
** Shy Guy's 3rd place animation
** Kamek's 2nd and 3rd place animation
*Mario's 1st and 2nd place animations have him strike the same pose at the end, with the only difference being that his 2nd place animation has his right foot completely on the ground.
