The '''Bonus Board''' is a board game bundled with ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
It is a multiplayer game that supports up to four players, each of whom have to try and obtain the [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]. Whoever obtains the Star is the winner.
