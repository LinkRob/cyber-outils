A '''Mad Spike''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Mad Spike is a variant of [[Spike]], which it resembles, except Mad Spikes have red hair instead of blue, an angry face, and smoke coming out of them.

===Behavior===
Mad Spikes are imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]]. They stamp their feet around angrily as they walk while appearing to shout. Whenever a Mad Spike sees [[Princess Peach|Peach]], it gets furious and throws [[Spiked Ball]]s at her. Mad Spikes have the ability to [[jump]], unlike regular Spikes.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
[[File:Peach VS Mad Spike.png|thumb|left|200px|Peach encountering a Mad Spike in ''Super Princess Peach'']]
Mad Spikes are first encountered in the level [[Ladida Plains 1-7]]. They are also encountered in [[Fury Volcano]], [[Gleam Glacier]], and [[Bowser's Villa]]. Peach can defeat a Mad Spike either from the effects of Rage or by hitting it with [[Perry]]. When Peach stomps onto a Mad Spike, she can pick it up to throw at other enemies or objects.
