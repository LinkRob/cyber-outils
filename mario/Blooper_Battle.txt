'''Blooper Battle''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The quest takes place at [[Mushroom Beacon]], where it begins when the player accepts to duel [[Blooper (character)|Blooper]]. At the start, Blooper mentions that he is nervous about the match, but he offers a reward to the player's character if they win nonetheless. The duel takes place in the minigame [[Hammergeddon]]. If Blooper wins and the player does not restart using a [[Mushroom]], Blooper becomes very happy about his first victory and offers to let the player try again, therefore restarting the quest. If the player's character wins, Blooper compliments their dueling skill and rewards them the Hammergeddon minigame.
