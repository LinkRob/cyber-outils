A '''Pink Boo Orb''' is an [[Orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Descriptipn==
This orb can be thrown onto the board to create a [[Character Space]]. Any opponents who land on it will cause [[Red Boo|Pink Boo]] to appear and relieve them of a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]], or 20 [[coin]]s if the opponent has none, and give whichever of them he got to whoever planted the trap. Most [[Orb Hut|shop]]s sell Pink Boo Orbs for 30 coins, or 15 if there is a discount. The Pink Boo Orb can be found on [[Grand Canal]], [[Pyramid Park]], [[Neon Heights]], and [[Bowser's Enchanted Inferno!]].

==Names in other languages==

*'''Italian:''' Pallina Boo rosa, meaning "Pink Boo Orb"
