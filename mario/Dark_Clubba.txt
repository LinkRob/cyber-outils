}}A '''Dark Clubba''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Clubbas are stronger, darker versions of [[Clubba]]s that can only be found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Dark Clubbas are very strong enemies having a lot of HP (60 in total). They attack with a powerful club attack that makes them rather difficult to get in close to attack. Using [[Bowser]]'s fire breath from a safe distance is the best way to take out Dark Clubbas.
