'''Longators '''are white, rectangular enemies from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They attack by elongating their stretchy heads towards the player. Longators are only encountered twice, the first time being in [[Planet Blobule]] (4-2) and the second being the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]] (Rooms 32 and 67). Longators can be defeated by any attack, but they are the most vulnerable to [[Jump]] attacks.
