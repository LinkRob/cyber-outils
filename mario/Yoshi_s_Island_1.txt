{{Nihongo|'''Yoshi's Island 1'''|ヨースターとう　コース１|Yōsutā Tō Kōsu 1|Yoster Island Course 1}} is one of the first levels of [[Yoshi's Island (Super Mario World)|Yoshi's Island]] from ''[[Super Mario World]]'' (along with [[Yoshi's Island 2]]). It is a plains-themed level featuring a few [[Banzai Bill]]s.

== Layout ==
[[File:Yoshi's Island 1 SMA2.png|thumb|left|200px|''Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2'' screenshot of Mario ducking as a Banzai Bill approaches]]
The player starts behind a slope. On the slope, there is a blue [[Beach Koopa]] that will slide down the slope. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] has avoid it and climb up the slope, then need to jump and get the first [[Dragon Coin]]. Then a Banzai Bill will come and Mario has to avoid it. He can then hit the [[Question Box]] to get a [[Super Mushroom]] (or a [[Fire Flower]] if Mario is already [[Super Mario (form)|Super Mario]]). Mario then has to go forward and avoid the Rexes. He needs to climb the set of slopes and get the second Dragon Coin. Mario needs to go forward and spin jump on the [[Rotating Block]]s (if he is Super Mario). He then needs to go down the [[Warp Pipe]]. There will be another Dragon Coin under three Rotating Blocks. Mario needs to spin jump on them and get the third Dragon Coin. He then needs to go to the Warp Pipe and exit the room. This will make him end up in the [[Midway Gate]].

The player needs to continue and get the fourth Dragon Coin and needs to avoid the second Banzai Bill. They then need to hit the rotating block. A [[1-Up Mushroom]] will come out. Mario needs to avoid the third Banzai Bill and either get to the [[pit]] where the 1-Up Mushroom will fall, or jump on the Banzai Bill and get the 1-Up Mushroom on the clouds. He then needs to continue and hit the next [[Question Box]] which will give him a power-up. He then needs to avoid the last Banzai Bill, get the last Dragon Coin and get past the Chargin' Chuck to reach the [[Giant Gate]] where he will then unlock the [[Yellow Switch Palace]].

== [[Dragon Coin]]s ==
<tabber>
Dragon Coin 1 =
At the start of the level, in plain sight. The player has to jump from the slope that the [[Beach Koopa]] slid off of.

|-| Dragon Coin 2 =
In plain sight near some [[Rex]]es. The player has to jump from the slopes above.

|-| Dragon Coin 3 =
In a blue [[Warp Pipe]] blocked off by some [[Flip Block]]s. The player has to [[spin jump]] to break them. This will then bring them into a room with some [[Coin]]s. The [[Dragon Coin]] will also be there, being blocked off by some [[Flip Block]]s again. Once again, the player has to [[spin jump]] to break them.

|-| Dragon Coin 4 =
After the [[Midway Gate]], to the top of a hill filled with [[Rex]]es. The player has to dodge the [[Rex]]es to obtain it.

|-| Dragon Coin 5 =
In plain sight right before the [[Goal Pole]]. The player has to jump to get it.
</tabber>
