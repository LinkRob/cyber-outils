The '''Max-Mix Vortex''' is the final [[Special Attack]] that [[Luigi]] can use in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It costs 14 [[Special Point]]s to use. The attack is gained when the Bros. get their hands on the Bond that [[Prince Chilliam]] and [[Princess Burnadette]] made with their romance.

==Description==
In the menu, the Max-Mix Vortex resembles a blue top with a golden rim and axis. Its offensive form in official artwork portrays a purple tornado with streaks of red and blue. However, the actual game allows it to be scarlet, cyan, or purple depending on the ratio of [[Fireball|fireballs]] to [[Ice Ball|magic snowballs]] inserted in the ring in the first phase.

===Procedure===
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have to set [[fireball]]s and [[Ice Ball]]s in a ring around a small whirlwind in the centre. After they fill up the slots, they [[Spin Jump (Mario & Luigi series)|spin]] to generate a damaging tornado which they have to move into the enemies to deal damage. There are 18 slots in the ring.

Putting mostly fireballs into the tornado gives it a scarlet colour and fire attributes, meaning enemies might contract the [[Burn]] status effect. If the Bros. put mostly snowballs in the tornado, the resultant tornado will be cyan, gaining ice attributes and becoming able to [[Frozen|freeze the opposition]]. Putting an equal amount causes the tornado to gain neither attribute, but boosts attack power.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, the Max-Mix Vortex is called "''Mikkusu Torunēdo''" with the name coming from "Mix Tornado" transliterated into Japanese, though with an extraneous "-''ru''-" syllable and no lengthening of the O in the first syllable of "Tornado." It has its name translated literally in Chinese, resulting in ''Hùnhé Lóngjuǎnfēng'', written 混合龙卷风. In Spanish, the attack is called "Dual Tornado" (''Tornado Dual''). Portuguese refers to it as ''Vórtex Mix'', with ''Vórtex'' meaning "vortex" and "mix" being an English word. (The actual translation of "mix" would be "''mistura''" or "''mescla''.") In Italian, the attack is called ''Tornado Misto'', which literally means "mixed tornado" (''Misto'' can be used as a past participle of ''mischiare'' ("to mix").). Dutch refers to it as "''Tornadomix''," combining the English words "tornado" and "mix."
