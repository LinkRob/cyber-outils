The '''Battle Ring''' is an area in [[Pi'illo Castle]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. Here, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] fight the ultimate or "X" versions of every boss they encountered in the main mode.

Mario and Luigi can fight both normal and giant bosses, instead of just normal ones like in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

After winning their first battle in the Battle Ring, Mario and Luigi will receive a part of the [[Ultibed]], which is crucial to continue the game.
