'''Farm Hands''' is one of [[Ashley]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place on a farm, and it shows a close-up view of a pair of hands clutching a cow's udder

==Gameplay==
The object is to milk the cow three times, which the player can do by tapping the hands. The specific hand that has to tapped is outlined in red. If the player taps the correct hand three times, they win at the microgame, but if the player taps the wrong hand or fails to meet the objective on time, a speech bubble reading "Moo! (Get with it!)" appears, and they lose the microgame.
