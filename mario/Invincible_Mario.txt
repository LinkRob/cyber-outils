'''Invincible Mario''' (known as '''Rainbow Mario''' in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'') is the transformation that [[Mario]] (or another character) takes when they grab an [[Super Star|Invincibility Star]]. When a character is in this form, they are faster and invincible for a short time. The power-up first debuted in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

In certain games, like ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', Mario will change his jump animation to a somersault. In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', he is also rainbow-colored, and leaves afterimages. A similar form is seen in the Smash Bros. series.

==Powers and abilities==
As Invincible Mario, Mario is invincible and no other enemies can damage him, making the power-up one of the strongest of Mario's power-ups. Mario also gains increased speed and is easily able to outrun some enemies. Mario is also able to defeat any enemy that is usually rendered invincible themselves such as [[Thwomp]]s, [[Chain Chomp]]s, and [[Dry Bones]], however, the invincibility effect lasts for a short time and the player can still lose a life by touching [[lava]], poison water and pits, or getting crushed to death by something like a descending ceiling or an advancing wall. 

==History==
===Super Mario series===
====''Super Mario Bros.'' / ''Super Mario Bros. The Lost Levels''====
In ''Super Mario Bros.'' / ''Super Mario Bros. The Lost Levels'', when Mario or Luigi grabs a Starman, they become invincible and faster, killing every enemy in their way.
