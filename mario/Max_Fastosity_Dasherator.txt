'''Max Fastosity Dasherator''' is an object in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Description==
To use a Max Fastosity Dasherator, [[Wario]] must stand on the red pipe in the manner of a [[Warp Pipe]]. After an alarm sounds, Wario is propelled out of the lower red pipe, causing him to dash rapidly in the direction it is pointed and allowing him to run on water and break through metal blocks.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
At least one Max Fastosity Dasherator appears in every ground level except [[Launchpad Labyrinth]].
