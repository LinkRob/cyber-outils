An '''Egg Missile''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Since ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', it has been replaced with the [[Egg Bomb]] item.

==Effects==
This item is not a healing item, but an offensive one. The Egg Missile is created by [[Tayce T.]] when Mario gives her an [[Egg]] and a [[Fire Flower]] . This item is used in battle to damage a single enemy. Since this item only deals 6 damage to a single enemy and is a recipe that requires two items to be cooked together, it is not very effective during battle especially compared to the [[Shooting Star (item)|Shooting Star]] which deals 6 damage to all foes and can be purchased in certain item shops.
