'''Red-Hot Elevator Ride''' is the first castle and the fifth level in [[Peach's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. U & New Super Luigi U)|Peach's Castle]] in New Super Mario Bros U.

==Overview==
Despite being labeled as a castle, it is completely vertical and takes place on an elevator, similar to [[World 7-2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7-2]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' (the player needs to step on the sides to go left or right and the middle to go up) and [[World 7-Tower (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7-Tower]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. The level also has no boss, and the end-of-level [[fortress]] seen behind the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]] is missing, making it the only level in the game with this distinction.

This course is used for the [[Challenge Mode (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Challenge Mode]] courses [[Elevator Out of Service]] and [[Express Elevator]].
==Walkthrough==
The level begins in a area with a [[Goomba]], a [[? Block]] containing a power-up, and rising [[lava]]. A [[Warp Pipe]] is found, leading to the next area. The second area is a vertical level, similar to [[Tower]]s, with an elevator platform above lava that rises. The elevator can only hold one other enemy or object, so if a [[coin]], Goomba, or [[Bob-omb]] lands on it, the elevator stops. Coins on parachutes are found, along with [[Bowser Amp]]s. Some yellow Warp Pipes that generate Goombas are also found. More Bowser Amps are found, followed by a rows of [[Brick Block]]s blocking the elevator's path, along with a [[P Switch]] that turns them into coins. More coins on parachutes and [[Parabomb]]s are found. More Bowser Amps are found, along with a [[Red Ring]]. A Warp Door is located at the top of the area, leading to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is found after the first Goomba-generating [[Warp Pipe]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is behind a wall of [[Brick Block]]s. It can be obtained by using a [[P Switch]] or a Bob-omb.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is found between a group of Bowser Amps, right before the end of the course, avoid them and grab the Star Coin.
