'''Ice Mario''' is one of [[Mario]]'s transformations, and it first appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Ice Mario is the opposite of the [[Fire Mario]] form, which first appeared much earlier on in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. Mario requires an [[Ice Flower]] for this transformation.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[File:Ice Mario Artwork - Super Mario Galaxy.png|thumb|left|200px|Artwork for ''Super Mario Galaxy'']]
See Page, [[Ice Mario (Super Mario Galaxy)]]

Mario first becomes Ice Mario in the [[Freezeflame Galaxy]]. He needs an Ice Flower to transform. The effect of the power is the ability to walk and skate on water and lava by freezing it, including vertical jets of water, and can wall-jump off of waterfalls. The person controlling Mario is required to shake to activate the skating power, but ice is always being generated. If you jump while skating, you will cover more distance than if you aren’t skating. It wears off after a set period of time, after which the flower reappears for collection again. The music gets faster and faster until it wears off. Ice Mario is needed to defeat [[Baron Brr]], the boss of this level.
{{br|left}}
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''===
[[File:Ice Mario 4.png|thumb|200px|''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' artwork]]
Ice Mario makes his second appearance. Now, Ice Mario's design has changed; instead of being completely made of ice, the color of Mario's clothes simply changes to a light blue color with red overalls. Mario can launch ice balls that not only freeze the enemies, but allow Mario to pick up the enemy and throw it at others to defeat them. Mario can also freeze some tall enemies and use them as an extra platform to reach higher places. In some cases, this technique is needed to get some Star Coins.

===''New Super Mario Bros. U''/''New Super Luigi U''/''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
Ice Mario returns in this installment and plays the same role as he did in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''===
Ice Mario only makes a cameo in this game whenever Mario uses an Ice Flower Sticker. Ice Mario can freeze enemies and, if they are defeated, they will crumble away in flakes of snow.

===''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''===
Ice Mario returns in'' Paper Mario: Color Splash'', playing the same role as he did in ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''.

==In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''==
Ice Mario was an added character in an update, with his special item being the Ice Flower. Ice Mario uses his'' Super Mario Galaxy'' design for this game, along with a voice filter.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'' ==
While the Ice Flower is not used by Mario, Luigi uses the Ice Flower to become Ice Luigi in a move the Bros. can perform, while Mario instead uses the [[Fire Flower]] to become Fire Mario.

==In other media==
===''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''===
While it is not Mario specifically that gains ice powers, [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Peach]] has an Ice Flower smuggled into her bouquet by [[Toad]] just before the wedding begins. This marks Peach's first use of the Ice Flower as a power-up.

==Trivia==
*Ice Mario is one of few transformations that can effectively destroy [[Dry Bones]], aside from [[Invincible Mario]].
*In [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|The Super Mario Bros. Movie film series]], Peach (except for [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]])  transformed into an ice form.
