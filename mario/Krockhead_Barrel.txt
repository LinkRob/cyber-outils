A {{nihongo|'''Krockhead Barrel''',<ref>''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest Nintendo Player's Guide'', page 54.</ref>|クロコバレル|Kuroko Bareru|Croco Barrel}} also formatted as '''Krockhead barrel''',<ref>''Nintendo Magazine System'' (Australia) issue 47, page 48.</ref> is a type of [[barrel]] which only appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''. These barrels depict a Krockhead, specifically a green one in the former game. In both games, Krockhead Barrels only appear in their respective iteration of the level [[Krockhead Klamber]].

Krockhead Barrels are usually suspended in midair. If [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] or [[Dixie Kong]] touch a Krockhead Barrel, several [[Krockhead]]s emerge from the swamp, and the [[Kong]]s can jump from them to travel across. After a short while, the Krockheads retreat back into the swamp water, so the Kongs must hop from the Krockheads quickly to get across on time or risk falling into the swamp. While a Krockhead Barrel is active, the Krockhead icon depicted on it blinks repeatedly. If the Kongs touch a Krockhead Barrel again, even while it is still active, the duration of its effect is reset.
