'''Blizza''' is one of the [[snow spirit]]s featured in the [[Adventure Tours]] mode of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for the [[Nintendo DS]].

==Physical description==
Blizza has the appearance of a large, levitating purple snowflake with black eyes.

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
Blizza is the snow spirit of [[Blizland]]. It is the sixth and final snow spirit found by both [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] at [[Icepeak]], when they meet up with [[Rouge the Bat|Rouge]], [[Dr. Eggman]], and [[Bowser]] at the end. The two villains bribe Rogue with several [[Whitestone]]s, and Dr. Eggman even strikes a deal that if Mario and Sonic's team wins against her in [[Figure Skating (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games for Nintendo DS)|Figure Skating]], he will release Blizza, whom he vaguely refers to as a snow spirit at first.

Even when the player's character wins against Rogue, Dr. Eggman and Bowser get frustrated and reluctantly free Blizza, who properly introduces himself to both Mario and Sonic. Releasing Blizza was a trick set up by both Dr. Eggman and Bowser, as they then decide to capture [[Frosty]]. Blizza joins the heroes, and they continue onward to Blizland. Here, Blizza can melt an [[ice wall]] if Mario and Sonic's team have four [[Crystal (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games)|crystals]].
