'''Captain Koopa Troopa Trial''' is the tenth level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 35.

==Layout==

Is this level, the only ally on your team is [[Captain Koopa Troopa]].

The 1st battle contains 2 [[Clumph]]s, and 1 [[Elite Limbo Bro]]. The enemy captain is the Elite Limbo Bro.

The final battle contains 3 Clumph, 2 [[Tanoomba]]s, a 1 Elite Limbo Bro. The enemy captain is the Elite Limbo Bro.
