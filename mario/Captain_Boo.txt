'''Captain Boo''' is a character appearing in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' and later appearing again in ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser''===
Captain Boo first appeared in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' as a ordinary  [[Boo]]. He is first encountered by [[Captain Goomba (Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser)|Captain Goomba]] and [[Captain Shy Guy (Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser)|Captain Shy Guy]] in [[Chucklehuck Woods]] whilst working for a brainwashed [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]]. The two captains try to recruit him, but he tells him that he is busy working for Iggy. Then they try explaining to him what actually happened to Iggy, but he doesn't understand any of this. Boo then tells them that he wants to go home, but he doesn't want to disobey Iggy. Captain Shy Guy then tells him that he is actually disobeying [[Bowser]]. Realising this, the Boo joins the two captains and becomes Captain Boo.
{{Sectstub}}

===Bowser Jr.'s Journey===
Captain Boo returns in Bowser Jr.'s Journey as a enemy and a recruitable ally. Just like ''Minion Quest'', he is a Flying-type enemy/ally.
