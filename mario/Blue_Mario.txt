'''Blue Mario''' refers to various unidentifiable counterparts of [[Mario]] who wear blue clothing. One such character first made a minor cameo in ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]''. Just like [[Yellow Mario]], it is unknown whether he is just a colored copy of [[Mario]] or if he is a relative. Both he and the mysterious Yellow Mario also appear in the 4-player mode of the ''[[Mario Bros. (game)|Mario Bros.]]'' remake for the [[Game Boy Advance]]. He also appears as an alternate costume in all ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' games. it is unknown whether he is just a colored copy of [[Mario]], or if he is a relative.

While he wears a blue cap and shirt in all his appearances, his other clothes have been different; in the ''Mario Bros.'' Remake, it is white, in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]], [[Super Smash Bros. Melee]],'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' it is red, in [[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS|all subsequent]] [[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Super Smash Bros.]] [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|games]], it is pink, and in ''Mario Tennis,'' he has a variety of different outfits.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
"Blue Mario", as his "name" suggests, bears a strong resemblance to [[Mario]], with most of Mario's red features being replaced by Blue. he also wears white overalls, howewer, in Mario Tennis he has many different referee outfits, with all of them including a red bowtie.

=== Personality traits ===
While not much is known, he appears to be a man of business not appearing to do anything else in any game than working at his job, including his [[Mario Bros. Plumbing|former job]].
