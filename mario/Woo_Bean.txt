A '''Woo Bean''' is a type of [[bean]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
Woo Beans, like other beans, have an entirely optional purpose of being items that can be blended at the [[Starbeans Cafe]] to create stat-boosting drinks known as [[Bean Juice]]. The only visual difference that Woo Beans have from other beans is their light green color. Woo Beans are sometimes obtained as dropped items from certain enemies, such as [[Beanie]]s and [[Sharpea]]s.

[[Woohoo Blend]] is made entirely from Woo Beans, 25 of which are required to brew it. [[Hoolumbian]], [[Chuckoccino]], and [[Teeheespresso]] each have 15 Woo Beans as one of the ingredients required to brew it. In total, Woo Beans are an ingredient for four Bean Juices, more than the other beans, which are each an ingredient for only two different Bean Juices.
