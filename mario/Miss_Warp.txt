'''Miss Warps''' are a species of allies who assist the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] during their journey in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Miss Warps are egg-shaped creatures with a number painted on their belly, either 1 through 4. The color of a Miss Warp's body differs depending on which number is on its belly; Miss Warps with a 1 are pink; those with a 2 are cyan; those with a 3 are yellow, and lastly Miss Warps with a 4 are light green.

===Behavior===
Miss Warps are lazy, as they are always found sleeping at first, but a Yoshi can wake up the Miss Warp by jumping onto it. In doing so, a Miss Warp jumps up and down and shouts.

==In ''Yoshi's Story''==
[[File:Miss Warp 1 YS screenshot.jpg|thumb|left|200px|A screenshot of a Miss Warp sleeping in [[Lots O'Fish]]]]
Each [[level]] features four Miss Warps, numbered 1 through 4 respectively, with the number depending on their distance from the start of the level. When at least two Miss Warps are awoken, a Yoshi can jump onto a Miss Warp to be thrown to the next available one in sequence. Miss Warps also act as [[checkpoint]]s, so if a Yoshi loses all its health and gets kidnapped by [[Baby Bowser]]'s [[Toady|Toadies]], the next Yoshi continues the level starting from the highest numbered Miss Warp that they have reached.
