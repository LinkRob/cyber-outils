==Features==
The first half of the level takes place in a beach-like area, earning the level its name. This level is known for the Immense amount of [[Koopa Troopa]] and the ability to ride around in their shell, making part 1 of this level much easier to bypass. (Althugh, the [[Key Coin]]s will be skipped in that case.) This level has a [[Boomerang]] Suit for the playerand Has a small section with 4 [[Chargin' Chuck]] and a couple Sand statues. After 5 key coins are collected, you will be granted access to the Checkpoint. In part 2 of the level, you simply ride [[Plessie]] in a dark cave and jump on some [[Splounder]] here and there.  After this section, you go throguh A pipe where you complete the level.

==[[Green Star]]s==
*'''Green Star 1''': Destroy the third Sandgooba by ground-pounding it or destroying it with a Koopa Shell to get the star.
*'''Green Star 2''': Go in the back of the water near the [[Warp Box]] in a dark region to a hidden pipe to a secret area. In the area, light up all the tiles to reveal a star.
*'''Green Star 3''': On the right wall before entering the Warp Box, backflip in a hole to a hidden alcove. In there, destroy all the Biddybuds to have Captain Toad reveal the star.

==[[Stamp]]==
[[File:Sunshine_Seaside_Stamp.png|thumb]]
*After passing the third moving bridge while riding on [[Plessie]], jump on the three small bridges on the right to get the Koopa Troopa Stamp.

==Trivia==

* Just like in Koopa Troopa Cave, there is an exploit in this stage where the player can get unlimited lives by bouncing on a Koopa shell repeatedly.
