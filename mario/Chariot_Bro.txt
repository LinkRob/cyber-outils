'''Chariot Bro''' is an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Chariot Bro is a variant of [[Hammer Bro]], which it has an identical appearance to.

===Behavior===
A Chariot Bro rides around on a chariot pulled by a [[Chain Chomp]], but like Hammer Bros., a Chariot Bro attacks by throwing [[hammer]]s. A Chariot Bro can evade any of [[Mario]]'s attacks except the [[Bone (Thing)|Bone Thing]], which is required to defeat it.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
There is only one Chariot Bro encountered, specifically at [[The Golden Coliseum]], where it assists [[Iggy Koopa]] and a [[Chariot Snifit]] in battle.
