'''Deep Sea Divers''' is a 2 vs. 2 mini-game in the game ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Gameplay==
Two players will swim under water, while two players will control the teams boats. The two players who swim under the sea will collect treasure chest and bring them to the surface. The chests contain coins, and the players with the most coins win.

==Controls==
===Players in Boat===
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Rotate to reel in
*{{button|n64|stick}} ({{button|n64|stickleft}}/{{button|n64|stickright}}) - Move Boat Left or Right

===Players Underwater===
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Move
*{{button|n64|a}} - Swim
