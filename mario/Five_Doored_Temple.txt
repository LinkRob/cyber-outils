==History==
Each of the five [[Kong Family|Kongs]] can access their own area of the Five Doored Temple. However, in order to unlock the way into the temple, [[Diddy Kong]] must first feed the tiki using his Peanut Popguns three times to make the switches appear under each of the doors with the Kong's respective weapon under them. Inside, each of the Kongs can acquire a [[Golden Banana]] but, after getting the Golden Banana, they must escape before [[Kroc]] shoots them down.
