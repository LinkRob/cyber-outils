'''DJ Phantasmagloria''' is a character making her debut as a boss in [[Luigi's Mansion 3]]. She is the Disc Jockey of [[The Last Resort]], encountered in the [[The Last Resort#The Dance Hall .2814F.29|Dance Hall]]. [[Luigi]] battles her for the fifteenth floor's elevator button, and she has 450HP.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Phantasmagloria appears to be the music DJ of The Last Resort, she has light hot pink skin and wears a red afro containing her short black hair underneath which is used to protect her glowing monochromatic eyes from the Strobulb, as well as red lipstick and a purple skirt, plus purple earings, blue bracelets and blue headphones on her neck. She also has a red sleeveless tank top that lack straps in the vein of a tube top.

==Battle==
===Battle Phase One===
Luigi found the fifteenth floor's elevator button unguarded upon his arrival, so he picks it up, but DJ Phantasmagloria then startles Luigi with a loud noise which sent Luigi flying and causes him to be knocked into the door and drop the button. During phase one, eight dancing [[Disco Goob]]s appear and one of them picks up the button to guard it. The Goobs wore hoods to prevent Luigi from flashing them, then they start to do spinning attack, Luigi has to find the right Goob and use a [[Burst]] or else the Goob throws the button to another Goob. After the right Goob is found, it will throw the button to another Goob, Luigi has to find the Goob and give it a burst. If Luigi finds the right Goob, he has to capture it and the battle will get into phase two.

===Battle Phase Two===
During phase two, the Goobs will disappear and Phantasmagloria will battle Luigi by herself. She throws vinyl records which can be dodged by doing a burst, she can't be attacked when doing a spinning attack. When she stops spinning, Luigi can give her a burst to knock her afro off to flash her. When she is vacummed, she will do some more disco movements before getting sucked into the [[Poltergust G-00]]. Luigi then receives the fifteen floor's elevator button upon her defeat.

== Trivia ==
* Hence the name, Phantasmagloria's hairstyle is remniscent of the American singer, [[wikipedia:Gloria Gaynor|Gloria Gaynor]] from "I Will Survive".
===Etymology===
DJ Phantasmagloria earned her name from the Latin terms Phantasma (a masculine first declension noun (Genitive Phantasmae) that means "ghost" since she is one) and Gloria (which is a feminine first declension noun (Genitive Gloriae) that means "glory")).
