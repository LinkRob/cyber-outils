'''Sandwiched''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Opening==
The characters look at each other inside of a giant picnic basket. They are then startled as a giant ham sandwich falls in the center.

==Gameplay==
Each character has to try and avoid being squished by the giant sandwiches, which appear in egg, ham, caprese, and cucumber varieties. The ham and caprese sandwiches are the tallest, but all of them pose an equal amount of threat. A character can be slowed if jumped on. The winning condition is either surviving within a [[time limit]] of 60 seconds or being the last character remaining.

==Ending==
The characters perform their winning and losing animations, depending on their performance, next to a massive ham sandwich
