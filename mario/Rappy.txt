'''Rappy''' is a rat-based enemy which only appears in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for the [[Game Boy]].

==Behavior==
Rappies circle around any platform that they are on, including the bottom side. In some levels, Rappies walk around the entire border of the level, including over any [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]]. Rappies cannot damage [[Mario]] upon contact, but they can push him off a platform. Mario has the ability to stand on a Rappy, but he cannot pick it up.
