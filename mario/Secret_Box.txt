'''Secret Boxes''' are an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==Description==
Secret Boxes are a purple box with a white ? on its cover. When a Secret Box is used, it inflicts a random badge effect on [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], enemies, or everyone in the battlefield.
