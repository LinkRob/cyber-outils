The '''Odd Key''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
The Odd Key can only be found deep within [[Toad Town Tunnels]] near the area where [[Rip Cheato]] is located. The Odd Key can then be found by taking a [[Warp Pipe]] to the locked house in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] found next door to [[Tayce T.]]'s house. With the locked house now unlocked, Mario can get to Rip Cheato and the deeper parts of Toad Town Tunnels much more quickly.
