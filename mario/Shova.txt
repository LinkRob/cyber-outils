The '''Shova''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They are [[Sledge Bro]]-esque [[Koopa]]s that can push objects.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Shovas have jaws akin to Guts Man from the ''Rockman'' franchise and black lines slanted intwards akin to the [[Boss Sumo Bro]] from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. They have black shells and wear black helmets and red gloves.

=== Behavior ===
Shovas are able to push things, though their pushing can be countered by pushing onto an object that is being pushed by them from the other side.

== In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ==
Shovas are first seen in the level Swamp Pipe Crawl and later appear in Secrets of Shova Mansion. They can be destroyed by any means, including of [[Mario (character)|Mario]] pushes on the object that they are pushing on from the other side and overpowers them with his own strength.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name "shova" comes from the verb "to shove," which means "to push."
