'''Sachi Matsumoto''' (born March 29, 1973) is a prominent voice actor at [[Kenyu Office]]. Acting primarily in the ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' series. Sachi Matsumoto has voiced [[Link]] and [[w:c:zelda:Aryll|Aryll]] from ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker]]'', Link in ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures]]'' and ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass]]''. She also voices [[Toon Link]] from ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and ''[[w:c:zelda:Hyrule Warriors|Hyrule Warriors]]''. Her voice clips for Toon Link are reused in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
