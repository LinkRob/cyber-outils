The '''Big Cookie''' is an item and [[recipe]] made by [[Tayce T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
The Big Cookie made by mixing a [[Cake Mix]] (found only in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]) and an [[Egg]], [[Goomnut]], [[Blue Berry]], [[Red Berry]], or [[Yellow Berry]]. This dessert restores twenty [[Flower Point|FP]] and sells for ten [[coin]]s at any shop.

Big Cookies are fairly useful items in battle in case [[Mario]] needs to restore a fair amount of his FP. Big Cookies strongly resemble [[Yoshi Cookie]]s and the [[Sweet Cookie Snack]].
