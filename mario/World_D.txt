'''World D''' is the fifth and final secret world that was featured in the game ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'', following [[World C (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World C]]. Like the other secret worlds, it was left unused in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. When [[Bowser]] is defeated at the end of World D-4, [[Princess Peach]] is saved, and, as such, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] beat the game.

== Level Information ==
=== World D-1 ===
[[File:World_D-1.png|thumb|right|World D-1.]]
This level is a similar level to [[World 8 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 8-1]]. In the original FCD version, it is a snowy level, but in the SNES remake, its a grassy level.

=== World D-2 ===
[[File:World_D-2.png|thumb|right|World D-2.]]
This level includes several upside down [[Piranha Plant]]s.

=== World D-3 ===
[[File:World_D-3.png|thumb|right|World D-3.]]
This level has a brick wall in the background similar to World 8-3. In ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' the wall is gone so the brick blocks are replaced with hidden blocks.

=== [[World D-4]] ===
[[File:World_8-4Prev.png|thumb|World D-4 (Note that this preview is reused from [[World 8-4 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 8-4]]).]]
This castle level is a castle which starts out like World 8-4, but there are only 5 Fire Bars that Mario or Luigi need to avoid.
