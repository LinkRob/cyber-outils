'''Jungle Fuzzy''' is an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Jungle Fuzzies are yellow-colored [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] and incidentally resemble [[Gold Fuzzy|Gold Fuzzies]] in the sequel, ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

===Behavior===
Jungle Fuzzies attack by sucking [[Health Point|HP]] from [[Mario]]. Unlike other Fuzzies, they continue to do so until he fills a gauge, with the help of the [[Action Command]]. Jungle Fuzzies can also divide into two, an ability shared with [[Forest Fuzzy|Forest Fuzzies]].

===Habitat===

==In ''Paper Mario''==
In ''Paper Mario'', a Jungle Fuzzy attacks [[Kolorado]] when Mario first arrives on Lavalava Island. Mario is not required to defeat the Jungle Fuzzy.
