'''Juliet Jelenic''' is the daughter of [[Michael Jelenic]], co-director of The ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' and The ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. She portrays [[Lumalee (Illumination)|Lumalee]] in the English-language versions of both films.<ref name=ScreenRant>https://screenrant.com/who-voices-lumalee-super-mario-bros-movie-juliet-jelenic/</ref>. She was originally casted as a stand-in voice that was going to be replaced, however, due to Lumalee's popularity, the staff behind the movie decided to keep her as the voice actress.
