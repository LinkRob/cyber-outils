'''Toadofsky''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
Toadofsky is a rather sharply-dressed [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who has curly hair and red diamonds on his head.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Toadofsky is a famous Toad composer who is trying to perfect the next melody. However, he suffers from "composers block," and asks [[Mario]] to help him gather some melodies. In exchange for this, Toadofsky gives Mario various membership cards for the [[Juice Bar]].
{{sectstub}}

== Trivia ==
* His name is a pun of the famous real-world composer {{w|Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky}}.
