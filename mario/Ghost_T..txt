'''Ghost T.''' is a deceased [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]] aboard the [[Excess Express]]. He resides in Cabin 004 and lives between this life and beyond. He died on the train many years before the game took place.

==History==
The [[Excess Express Conductor|conductor]] of the Excess Express is missing a blanket, so he asks [[Mario]] to find one. To complete this, Mario must go to Cabin 004 and use [[Vivian]]'s [[Veil]] for a few seconds to make Ghost T. appear. Ghost T. asks Mario to find his [[Ragged Diary|Diary]] and to NOT read it, or he will have a horrible fate. Mario finds the Diary in the baggage area and returns it to Ghost T. (Note: If Mario reads his Diary, he is repeatedly asked if he really wants to.
If he answers "yes" on all three questions, he will read the Diary, Ghost T. will appear in the baggage area, curse Mario by spinning the game screen (or turning the screen monochrome with static effects in the Nintendo Switch version), and there will be a [[Game Over]].
Ghost T. will grant Mario with a blanket to give to the train conductor. He will attempt to move on to the sky for the next life, but will decide to stay for a while longer on the train.

If Mario comes back to the Excess Express after this encounter, Ghost T. will appear again when Vivian does her Veil move. Later on in the game, Ghost T. sends an e-mail to Mario. He does so by possessing the train conductor, since he cannot hold physical objects. He thanks Mario again for retrieving his diary and hopes to see him again.

In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], Ghost T.'s role remains mostly the same. However, one notable difference is that after Ghost T. asks Mario to get his diary for him without reading it, Ghost T. now has Mario put it inside of his drawer for him (as he cannot pick up physical objects on his own). Ghost T. also has other lines of dialogue when spoken to afterwards (as, in the original, he only says "How goes things?" when spoken to after the events of Chapter 6). Ghost T. also has a new animation of when he is punishing Mario for reading his diary.

==Trivia==
*He never leaves the train once Mario encounters him.
*His name is a pun on the word "ghostly."
*Even though Ghost T. tells Mario that he cannot hold physical objects, it is unknown how he could hold his diary. However, in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], this issue was fixed as Ghost T. now has Mario put his diary in the drawer instead of physically taking it himself.
*It is unknown how he died but it has been hinted that he may have eaten a [[Poison Mushroom]] during his time alive.
