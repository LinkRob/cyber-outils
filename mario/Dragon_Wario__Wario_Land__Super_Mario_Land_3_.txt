'''Dragon Wario''' is a form used by [[Wario]] in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. Successors to it have been featured in later ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' platforming games, although a common trait they all share is the ability to breathe fire, something that dragons can do in general.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
Upon using the [[Dragon Pot]], Wario transforms into Dragon Wario. Dragon Wario's hat can breathe a stream of fire anywhere, even underwater.

==Related forms==
In ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]'', Wario instead has two other dragon forms: one of them is [[King Dragon Wario|King Dragon]], a hat that resembles a dragon with wings, and another is [[Sea Dragon Wario|Sea Dragon]], a hat resembling a purple dragon.

In ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]'', Wario has a different [[Dragon Wario (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Dragon]] form that involves him wearing a carnivorous Dragon suit.
