}}'''Bowser Jr.'s Boom Bunker''' is a galaxy and the final galaxy of [[World 5 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 5]] in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' starring [[Bowser Jr.]] as the boss for the final time in the game. It has a darker appearance compared to its predecessors. Enemies found here inclue [[Fizzlit]]s, [[Bullet Bill]]s, [[Boomerang Bros.]], and [[Dry Bones]]. [[Mario]] can only visit this galaxy once he grabs 55 [[Power Star]]s.
==Missions==
===Bowser Jr.'s Boomsday Machine===
[[Mario]] must make his way to the centre of the Galaxy, where [[Bowser Jr.]] awaits in his [[Bowser Jr.'s Boomsday Machine|Boomsday Machine]].
===Prankster Comets===
====Boomsday Machine Daredevil Run====
Can [[Mario]] complete Bowser Jr.'s Boomsday Machine without taking damage?
===Green Stars===
====Green Star 1====
Climb on one of the flagpoles that appear on the second planet.
====Green Star 2====
Aim to the west of the Spinning Platforms Planet.

==Planets==
===Starting Planet (Megahammer's Junkyard)===
A destroyed [[Megahammer]] appears here. [[Mario]] must lead a [[Bullet Bills|Bullet Bill]] to the glass in order to progress

===[[Boomerang Bro]] fort planet===
Beat the [[Boomerang Bro]]s that lair here to find the [[Launch Star]].
===Cannon Planet===
Use the [[Cannon]] to make your way forward. This planet's equator is straddled with an electric barrier.
===Spinning Platforms Planet===
[[Mario]] must make his way to the top to find the [[Launch Star]], which leads him to the boss area.
===Boss Planet===
[[Bowser Jr.]] awaits... in his [[Bowser Jr.'s Boomsday Machine|Boomsday Machine]] tank!
===[[Dark Matter]] Area===
[[Dark Matter]] lurk here, and their patch covers the entire planet, save for a few landmasses. A lone bipedal [[Koopa Troopa]] appears here, right after the target, though his shell is too large to carry.

==Trivia==
*This is the only boss galaxy whose background lacks an orbiting blue planet in the background in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. This is similar to [[Bowser Jr.'s Lava Reactor]] in [[Super Mario Galaxy|the first game]] in the [[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|franchise]].
*The daredevil run in this galaxy is one of the two mario galaxy stages in the game to contain the dark matter obstical. (the other one being [[Bowser's Dark Matter Plant|Bowser's dark matter plant]].
*This is the first, and currently the only, galaxy to feature a normal Koopa Troopa walking on two legs. Most Koopa Troopas in the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' games walk on all fours.
