'''Busted!''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', in which it was originally named '''I Spy''', and is a part of [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] set. A remake of the microgame went on to return in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', under its current name, as one of Wario's microgames in the [[Mash League]].

==Gameplay==
The object is for the player to keep the spotlight pointed on Wario for the entire microgame, or at least when the microgame ends. If done successfully, the player wins the microgame. If Wario is not present under the spotlight when the microgame ends, the player loses.

The spotlight is made smaller with increased difficulty level. In ''WarioWare Gold'', Wario's movements are affected, as he moves faster in level 2 and consciously attempts to evade the spotlight in level 3.
