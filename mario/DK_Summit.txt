{{Nihongo|'''DK Summit'''|DKスノーボードクロス|DK Sunōbōdo Kurosu|DK Snowboard Cross}} (called '''DK's Snowboard Cross''' in PAL regions), is a track in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]].

== Layout ==
The course's start line leads to a right turn of the course. Ahead, the racers encounter a cannon that blasts the player to the top of the mountain. Landing on the top of the mountain, there are half-pipe ramps. Making a left turn, there are bumpy ramps and a boost ramp. Two piles of snow can slow down the racer. The racers will approach small bumpy hills and the boost ramps. The player will make the first right U-turn that has a half-pipe ramp and a left U-turn where the player will encounter a half-pipe ramp. The racers will race on a straight track with a boost ramp, half-pipe ramps and off-road snow. The snowboarding [[Shy Guy]]s can disrupt the driver if the driver hits them. The racers make their right turn to return to the start.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
In ''Mario Kart Tour'', the course remains the same in terms of layout with some few aesthetic changes. The starting line is metallic instead of being wooden. The gap from the launch cannon to the summit now lacks the fog. The Mario statue near the multiple bumps is replaced by a stone statue of Donkey Kong. 

==In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''==
DK Summit appeared in [[Booster Course Pass#Wave 4|Wave 4]] of the [[Booster Course Pass|Booster Course pass]], being the third track in the [[Fruit Cup]]. The track uses many of the aesthetic changes from ''Mario Kart Tour'', with a few differences, such as the DK statue returning to wood.
