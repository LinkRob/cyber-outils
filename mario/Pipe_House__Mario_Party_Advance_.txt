The '''Pipe House''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Pipe House is located in the [[Desert Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It acts as a hub for the various locations owned by [[Bowser]], each accessed from one of eight color-coded [[Warp Pipe]]s, which are located at the Pipe House's entrance. The color-coded Warp Pipes, their destination, and the criteria for unlocking them include:

*Blue: leads to [[Bowser Stadium (Mario Party Advance)|Bowser Stadium]] and unlocked after completing three quests
*Cyan: leads to [[Mt. Frostbite]] and unlocked by completing ten quests
*Red: leads to [[Bowser Mansion]] and unlocked by completing fifteen quests
*Pink: leads to [[Bowser Pad]] and unlocked by completing twenty quests
*Black: leads to [[Bowser Hideout]] and unlocked by completing twenty-five quests
*Yellow: leads to [[Bowser Toy Shop]] and unlocked by completing thirty quests
*Orange: leads to [[Bowser Game Hall]] and unlocked by completing thirty-five quests
*Purple: leads to [[Bowser Lab]] and unlocked by completing forty quests

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
At first, all of the pipes are blocked by statues of Bowser's head, but Bowser destroys these as more quests are completed. [[Koopa Kid]] oversees the Pipe House's entrance and informs the player's character as to how many more quests need to be completed to unlock each pipe.

Once the player's character has completed 49 quests, Bowser opens the large door in the background and directs them to his throne room, where the [[Final Showdown]] takes place. The quest involves Bowser challenging the player a final time at a Bowser minigame, [[Trap Floor]], and the reward for winning is the [[Bowser Print]] [[Gaddget]].
