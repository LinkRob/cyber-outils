The '''Thick Pants''' are a type of armor only usable by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They can be bought at a shop in [[Rose Town]] for 14 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Thick Pants increases Mallow's [[Defense]] by 12 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 6.
