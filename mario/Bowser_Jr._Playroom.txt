'''Bowser Jr. Playroom''' is an unlockable baseball stadium in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It is also where the minigame [[Graffiti Runner]] is hosted.

==Layout==
The layout is similar to the [[Bowser Castle (baseball stadium)|Bowser Castle]] stadium of ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. The walls of the field are very high, which makes it difficult to get home runs, even for power hitters like [[Donkey Kong]]. There are also toys like [[Thwomp]]s (which can break when hit) sitting on them. A giant Bowser Jr. statue also hangs over the middle of the wall and the whole field is surrounded in a train track and building blocks. In the outfield, there are many floor images of [[Chain Chomp]]s and [[Bullet Bill]]s. A Chain Chomp or [[Bullet Bill]] will pop up if its image was hit by the ball. The Chain Chomps and Bullet Bills will chase the player; the Chain Chomps run on the ground and the Bullet Bills fly. No matter what hit the player, they are stunned and drop or miss the ball. However, the enemies can be avoided if the player runs away fast enough by shaking the [[Wii Remote]].

==In ''Mario Super Sluggers''==
During Challenge Mode, the stadium initially serves as a cover-up for [[Bowser's Castle]] during the day, into which the stadium transforms after a certain point in the story and whenever the player switches to nighttime mode after beating Bowser Jr's team composed of [[Dry Bones]] and [[Magikoopa]]s.
