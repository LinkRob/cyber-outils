'''Mecha-Fawful Zs''' are enemies which appear exclusively in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of the ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' [[Reissue|remake]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mecha-Fawful Zs are a larger, robotic version of [[Fawful]]. They have a large hole in their chest that they shoot beams through, and they can also fire lasers through their claw hands.

==Battle==
Mecha-Fawful Zs are a Ranged troop, and use [[Piercing Projectile]] as their [[Special Skill]]. 

==Flavor text==
*'''In-game description:''' "Mass-produced robots that serve Fawful. They fire powerful beams from their chests."
