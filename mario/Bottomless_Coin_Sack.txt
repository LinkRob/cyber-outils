The '''Bottomless Coin Sack''' is an item in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

== Description ==
It is a magical bag that holds an infinite number of [[coin]]s. Shaking the Bottomless Coin Sack causes it to spit out coins.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
The Bottomless Coin Sack is a legendary treasure located in the [[Shake Dimension]]. The game's plot revolves around the [[Shake King]] stealing the Bottomless Coin Sack and [[Wario]] wanting to retrieve it for self-centered reasons. By the ending, Wario manages to acquire the Bottomless Coin Sack, only for it to be stolen by [[Captain Syrup]].
