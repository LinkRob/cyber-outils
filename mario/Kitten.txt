'''Kittens''' are a friendly species in ''[[Bowser's Fury]]''. They are normal cats until [[Fury Bowser]] attacks [[Lake Lapcat]], turning them into antagonistic [[Fury Cat]]s.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Kittens appear in one of five colors: blue, magenta, calico, green, or black. These are all colors of the [[wikipedia:Maneki-neko|Maneki-neko]], which is also what the [[Lucky Cat Mario]] is based on. There is a rumor that says the kittens represent Mario and the others. Green for [[Luigi]], pink for [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[blue]] for [[Toad (character)|Toad]] and black for [[Rosalina]] (in her [[Super Bell|cat]] form). The kittens are usually friendly and caress [[Cat Mario]], relax or groom themselves. [[Mario]] can pick up an individual kitten and it will scratch things in front of it like enemies and other things. When Mario is near a kitten and wearing a cat suit, it will dash towards Mario and rub against him. But if he's not, the kitten will run away from him in fear. If they are thrown into water, the kittens will respawn shortly after.

== In ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' ==
Kittens are implemented as a mechanic for obtaining three different [[Cat Shine]]s. Mario can find a [[Calico Cat Parent|parent cat]] near each of the three [[Giga Bell]]s crying. Once approached, a bubble will appear over the kittens's head requesting Mario to find their lost kittens, who are also [[calico kitten]]s. When Mario brings back all of the kittens, he will obtain a [[Cat Shine]]. Each Cat Shine using this format can only be obtained if Mario finds the previous Cat Shine. The more [[islands]] you unlock, the harder the kittens are hidden.

== In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' ==
Kittens are a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]] obtainable from the Spirit Board in version 11.0.1 and later updates.
