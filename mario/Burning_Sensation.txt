'''Burning Sensation''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'' as of [[Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s, and it reappears in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', now as one of [[Kat and Ana]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player has to position a magnifying glass so that a ray of light can appear on the germ to kill it.

===Difficulty levels===
In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'', the first level features only one germ, the second level features two germs, and the third level is identical to the second level, except it is vertical.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', the three difficulty levels are significantly different from one another:

*'''Level 1:''' The player has to light a match. After doing so, a dog named [[Clever Pup]] eats the match.
*'''Level 2:''' The player has to light a match dragged by the Clever Pup. When done, the flame bursts and propels the dog backward.
*'''Level 3:''' The player has to heat up three popcorn kernels, but one of them is a dog. Sometimes, every popcorn kernel is a dog.
