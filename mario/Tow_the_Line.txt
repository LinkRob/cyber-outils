'''Tow the Line''' is an unlockable [[2 vs. 2 Minigame|2-vs-2]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The minigame sets in a sewing box. The camera zooms closer to the sewing box in bird's eye view.

== Gameplay ==
There are five rounds in this minigame. The teams must work together to copy the nine dot grid diagram on the center. Teams must copy it by navigating the string. The team that copies it correctly wins the first round. The team winning the most rounds wins the minigame.

== Ending ==
The camera zooms close to the winning team that does their victory animation.
