'''Peach Dome''' is a tennis court owned by [[Princess Peach]] in the [[Mario Tennis (series)|''Mario Tennis'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', and would reappear in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. The minigame [[Artist on the Court]] takes place on Peach Dome.

==Description==
Peach Dome has little difference from real-world tennis stadiums, especially from lacking any gimmicks or hazards. However, it is located on a floating stadium, unlike real-world stadiums, and is connected behind [[Princess Peach's Castle]] by a brick-shaped bridge. The stadium has numerous seats where all the spectators sit, with audience members including [[Toad (species)|Toads]], [[Noki]]s, and [[Pianta]]s. There is a jumbotron held up by both red and dark purple [[girder]]s, and it has the Peach Dome logo and four large, yellow speakers sticking out from the side. Toads and a [[Fishin' Lakitu]] are more nearby, acting as line judges.

In ''Mario Power Tennis'', Peach Dome consists of three courts:
*[[Hard Court]]: The court consists of a blue floor. Its Ball Speed is set to Normal while its Bounce is set to Strong.
*[[Clay Court]]: The court consists of a floor with the color of light soil. Its Ball Speed is set to Slow while its Bounce is set to Weak.
*[[Grass Court]]: The court is light green and covered in mowed grass. The Ball Speed is set to Fast and, like the Clay Court, has a Bounce set to Weak. 

The different game menus depict pre-rendered backgrounds showing different views of Peach Dome.

In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour'', Peach Dome does return as a playable area, being the only court to return from ''Mario Power Tennis''. It is also featured in the game's start-up sequence. Peach Dome does not consist of three courts like it once had, as they were all made into separate courts. While Peach Dome still has a ground covered in mowed grass, the playing area in the center is colored magenta and depicts a large heart with diamond-shaped sparkles below it.
