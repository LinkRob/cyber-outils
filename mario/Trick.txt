'''Tricks''' are features in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' , ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It allows players to do tricks and moves in the air. When the player lands on the ground, he or she gets a boost. This makes doing a trick a way of going faster. Tricks can be done on any thing that launches the player into the air (like a ramp or a wavy track).

In ''Mario Kart Wii'', tricks can be done when flicking the [[Wii Wheel]] or [[Wii]] Remote up when going off a ramp or something that will launch the racer into the air. It also can be done by pressing the top arrow button on the [[Nintendo GameCube]] controller.

In ''Mario Kart 7'' and ''Mario Kart 8'', tricks can be done when pressing the R button when going off a ramp or something that will launch the racer into the air.

In ''Mario Kart Tour'', tricks are performed automatically when going off any ramps or anything that will launch the racer into the air.
