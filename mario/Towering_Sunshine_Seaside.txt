|game      = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Towering Sunshine Seaside''' is the ninth level of [[World Flower (Super Mario 3D World)|World Flower]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
Towering Sunshine Seaside is an alternate version of [[Sunshine Seaside]], taking place during a sunset and includes only the beach and the Goal Pole. The main objective is to defeat all [[Goomba Tower]]s and [[Fire Bro]]s. to spawn the Warp Box leading to the Goal Pole. The level has a 100 second time limit and provides blue clock items that each add 10 seconds to the timer.

==[[Green Star]]s==
*'''Green Star 1''': Inside one of the [[Question Block]]s is a [[Fire Flower]]. Use the Fire Flower to light all four torches on the far left of the level. Upon completing this task, the first Green Star will appear.
*'''Green Star 2''': To the left of the level, there is a Clear Pipe leading to an invisible cloud platform with the second Green Star.
*'''Green Star 3''': On the far right of the level, the third Green Star is sitting atop a small Goomba Tower. Defeat the Goomba Tower and collect the Star.

==Trivia==
*The alcove where [[Captain Toad]] was being chased by [[Biddybud]]s in Sunshine Seaside is present in Towering Sunshine Seaside and can only be accessed by [[Spin Jump]]ing off of the Question Block closest to its wall, climbing up the wall in [[Cat_Mario|cat form]] and falling in through the roof. Once inside, the player cannot get out, and can only see inside it when he/she stands in its center. The only way to escape is to quit the level or let the timer run out.
