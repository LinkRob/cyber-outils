'''Fishin' Lakitu''' is a variant of [[Lakitu]] which first appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. Fishin' Lakitus look just like their regular counterparts, although (hence their name) they carry around a fishing rod with them, although regular Lakitus have since also done the same.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario World''===
[[File:Fishin Lakitu SMW sprite.png|frame|left]]
Only one Fishin' Lakitu makes an appearance, and it is encountered at [[Forest of Illusion 4]], where it carries a [[1-Up Mushroom]] on the hook of its fishing rod. The 1-Up Mushroom serves as bait for [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] because if they collect it, the Fishin' Lakitu begins starts to act like a regular Lakitu and by throwing [[Spiny Egg]]s at them. Like regular Lakitus, a Fishin' Lakitu can be defeated by either [[jump]]ing on it or by attacking it using a [[fireball]].

Fishin' Lakitus have an undead variant found in [[Ghost House]]s named [[Fishin' Boo]]s, which carry blue fire with them.

===''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''===
Fishin' Lakitu is seen fishing for [[Cheep Cheep]]s. He can be [[capture]]d by [[Cappy]] and used to fish. If a fish is caught, it will reward players with a heart or, if they catch the right fish, a [[Power Moon]]. He appears in multiple kingdoms and is not hostile.

==In ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''==
Fishin' Lakitu also appears as an enemy, exclusively within [[Hookbill the Koopa's Castle]]. Here, Fishin' Lakitu tries to steal [[Baby Mario]] using his fishing rod.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
{{main|Lakitu (referee)}}
A Fishin' Lakitu named Lakitu appears throughout the ''Mario Kart'' series. He acts as the referee, holding the [[Start Signal|starting lights]] and saving racers who fall out of bounds.
{{sectstub}}

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
A Fishin' Lakitu makes a cameo appearance flying in the background of the [[Yoshi's Island (Super Smash Bros. Melee)|Yoshi's Island]] stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and its subsequent appearances. It reuses its sprites from ''Super Mario World''.
