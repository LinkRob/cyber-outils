|race = [[Shelltop]]}}The '''Mole Hunt Host''' is a character that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He hosts the [[Mole Hunt]] game and is found on [[Driftwood Shore]].

==Appearance==
The Mole Hunt Host is an elderly [[Shelltop]] that is brown in color. He also has a mustache.

==Personality==
The Mole Hunt Host is incredibly greedy as he would only give important information if people play his Mole Hunt game by giving him 10 coins each time.
==History==
The Mole Hunt Host is first seen when Mario and Luigi are looking for a [[Dreampoint]] to rest on. However, the Mole Hunt Host wouldn't let them go by until they beat his Mole Hunt game. After they have done so, the Mole Hunt Host lets them go by.

The Mole Hunt Host is seen again after the [[Fly Guy R Thieves]] have made off with the [[Driftwood Jellyfish Sheets]]. Mario and Luigi asks the Mole Hunt Host where they went to but he wouldn't tell them until they beat his Mole Hunt game again (while thinking to himself as he can get a few extra coins out of them). After Mario and Luigi beat the game again, the Mole Hunt Host tell them that they were heading for the [[Pi'illo Blimport|blimp]].
