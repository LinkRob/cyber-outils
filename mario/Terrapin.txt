'''Terrapins''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Terrapins are a variant of [[Koopa Troopa]]s that wear armor alongside a [[Green Shell]].

===Behavior===
Terrapins fight alone or in groups of two to four. They attack by punching, dealing only one damage. Despite being armored, Terrapins are the weakest members of the [[Koopa Troop]], having only an [[Heart Point|HP]] of ten.

==History==
===In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
Terrapins are encountered patrolling the halls of [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] during the first visit.
