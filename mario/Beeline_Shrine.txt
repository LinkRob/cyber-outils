'''Beeline Shrine''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The minigame's objective is similar to [[All Fired Up]] and [[Horror Harriers]]: the characters have to run around a big field with [[bee]]s everywhere. The bees fly around in patterns to try and sting a character. Each character has three [[heart]]s in their health meter, and if a character gets stung, they lose one. If a character gets stung three times, they sit down and become dizzy; eliminated from the minigame. The last player standing or with the most hearts within the 30-second time limit is the winner.

== Controls ==
*{{button|wii|Pad}} - Move
*{{button|wii|2}} - Jump
