'''Cliffhangers''' is a 2 vs. 2 mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Gameplay==
Both characters on each team cooperatively tries to climb up a snowy mountain, aiming to reach the top before their rival. There will be a strong gust of wind at moments that can blow the characters farther down the mountain, interfering with their progress. The players must hold onto the cliff to prevent themselves from being blown off. Human players can tell with the [[Nintendo GameCube Controller]] rumbling before it happens. The first team to arrive at the top is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|stick}} - Move
*{{button|gcn|a}} - Hold on
