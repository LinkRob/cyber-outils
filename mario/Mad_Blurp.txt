A '''Mad Blurp''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Mad Blurp is a variant of [[Blurp]], which they resemble, except they wear red goggles instead of blue like with regular Blurps.

===Behavior===
Mad Blurps are imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]], so they are more aggressive than a regular Blurp. Upon noticing [[Princess Peach|Peach]], a Blurp starts moving in a direct line to her location, a behavior comparable to [[Deep Cheep]]s in the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' sub-series. Mad Blurps do not adjust their path if Peach moves.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Mad Blurps first appear in the level [[Ladida Plains 1-9]]. They only appear in underwater areas that utilize [[Subrella]], starting with [[Wavy Beach]] and later returning in [[Gleam Glacier]] and [[Bowser's Villa]]. Mad Blurps most commonly appear in tight spaces or areas with scrolling screens, making it a challenge to avoid them at times. Despite their aggressive nature, Mad Blurps are no stronger than standard Blurps, and Peach can quickly defeat one by blowing Air Bubbles at it.
