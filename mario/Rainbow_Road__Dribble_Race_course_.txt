'''Rainbow Road''' is a location in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Rainbow Road MH3o3.png|thumb|left|200px|''Mario Hoops 3-on-3'' screenshot of Mario dribbling on a [[? Panel]] as a green and a blue Bomb approach from behind]]
Rainbow Road takes place in a tower-like setting above the clouds, and is named for the [[Rainbow Road]]-esque course that loops around the court as an aesthetic addition. The main gimmick of Rainbow Road is [[Lava Bubble]]s and colorful [[Bomb (species)|Bombs]] that come out of holes to attack the player, like with [[Bowser's Castle (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Bowser's Castle]], although only orange Bombs appear there.

==In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Rainbow Road is the unlockable third course of Dribble Race mode.
