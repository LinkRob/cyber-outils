'''Star Cape''' is the most powerful type of armor usable by [[Geno]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] for 100 [[coin]]s, and equipping the Star Cape increases Geno's [[Defense]] by 36 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 18.
