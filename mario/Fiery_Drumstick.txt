'''Fiery Drumsticks''' are items that they are expendable by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]. ''Fiery Drumsticks are better than the [[Hot Drumstick]]s but they are weaker than the [[TNT Drumstick]]s. If the Drumstick is used, Bowser mends 150 HP instantly. It can be lead from [[Broque Monsieur]]'s item shop after the boss fight at [[Bowser's Castle]] for 30 [[Coin|coins]]. Some enemies  possess a chance of dropping one of them fighting themselves among them, [[Midbus]] has a 100% chance during the next battle, while [[Naplock|Naplocks]] only have a 5% chance.
