This game includes graphics and sounds that are downgraded to fit the handheld system's more limited capabilities; several new elements are also added. This game acts as a spiritual successor to the ''[[Donkey Kong Land (series)|Donkey Kong Land]]'' series of [[Game Boy]] games. This game would later be succeeded by [[Donkey Kong Country (Game Boy Advance)|another remake]] for the [[Game Boy Advance]] few years later.

== Story ==
The game's manual includes an abridged version of the story from the original game's manual. During a stormy night, "hero-in-training" [[Diddy Kong]] is overpowered by the [[Kremling|Kremlings]], who proceed to trap him in a [[barrel]], toss him into the jungle, and steal [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s [[Banana Hoard|banana hoard]]. In the morning, Donkey Kong is woken up by a smug [[Cranky Kong]], and vows to find Diddy, beat the Kremlings, and reclaim his bananas.
