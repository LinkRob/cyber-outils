An '''Airnapse''' is a more powerful variant of a [[Napse]] encountered in the [[Airway]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

==Physical description==
Airnapses have blue bodies surrounded by yellow tendrils for limbs around their bodies.

== Battle ==
Airnapses possess the same attacks as its weaker counterpart, but it attacks more faster and multiplies more frequently than Napse.

== Stats ==
Airnapses are of Level 29 with 150 hit points, an attack power of 150, a defense of 72, and a speed of 96, giving 610 experience points and dropping 70 coins as loot along with 5% chance of a Supersyrup Jar and a 20% chance of the Doctor Socks gear upon their defeat.
