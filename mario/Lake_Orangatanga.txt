'''Lake Orangatanga''' is the first world from ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'', set in the [[Northern Kremisphere]]. It's levels are primarily located around the large lake in the center of the map. To access Lake Orangatanga, the player must visit [[Funky's Rentals]] and use a boat to reach the path leading to this world. The world is followed by [[Kremwood Forest]].

==Design==
This world is bright and filled with vegetation. Lake Orangatanga is in the center of the map. The levels consist of boardwalks, mills and a snow level. 

The Brother Bear inhabiting this world is named [[Barnacle]]. He has a passion for diving and he keeps a [[Banana Bird]] in his shack. He lives on a small island in the center of the lake.

== Levels==

==='''[[Lakeside Limbo]]'''===
This level features many [[Kobble|Kobbles]] and [[Ellie the Elephant]]. It is a rather simple level designed to make the player comfortable with the controls & making them familiar with an [[Animal Crate|Animal Buddy]]. It takes place on a long boardwalk.

==='''[[Doorstop Dash]]'''===
The concept of levers and closed doors is introduced. To proceed in the level, the player must pull down a lever and then rush through the door, before it drops down and closes the path again. It is set inside a large mill.

==='''[[Tidal Trouble]]'''===
A level similar to Lakeside Limbo, but more athletic and with more enemies in your way.

==='''[[Skidda's Row]]'''===
Skidda's Row is the first snow level in the game. [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] and [[Kiddy Kong]] slip and slide through a small frosty village covered in snow. Here, the player first encounters [[Skidda|Skiddas]], purple variants of [[Kobble|Kobbles]].

==='''[[Murky Mill]]'''===
Once again located inside a mill, this dark place must be explored by transforming into [[Ellie the Elephant]]. The main obstacles in this level are [[Sneek|Sneeks]], which will scare Ellie upon sight.

=== Boss level: '''[[Belcha's Barn]]'''===
The first boss level is located in the barn southwest of Lake Orangatanga. The boss here is [[Belcha]], an oversized burping barrel.<br />
