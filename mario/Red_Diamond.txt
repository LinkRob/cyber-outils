'''Red Diamonds''',<ref>Sinfield, George. ''Luigi's Mansion'' Player's Guide. Page 95.</ref> also known as '''Big Red Jewels''',<ref>Bogenn, Tim. BradyGAMES ''Luigi's Mansion'' Official Strategy Guide. Page 11.</ref> are treasures in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]''. Unlike the [[Blue Diamond]]s and [[Gold Diamond]]s, Red Diamonds are extremely low in value, with each Red Diamond worth only 5,000G, the same amount as a [[coin]]. There are only two: one of them is [[King Boo]]'s [[crown (King Boo)|crown]], which is automatically given to [[Luigi]] when he sucks up King Boo after his battle on the roof. The other is found by watering a plant in the northeastern corner of the [[Balcony (3F)|Balcony]].
