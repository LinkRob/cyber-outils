'''Penny Crygor''' is the granddaughter of [[Dr. Crygor]]. She first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Profile==
===Personality traits===
Penny admires her grandfather and wants to grow up and be a great scientist, though she also desires to be a singer. She attends [[Diamond Academy]] in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

== In {{series|WarioWare}}==
=== ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'' ===
Penny is unlocked when completing [[Jimmy T.]]'s level along with [[Ashley and Red]], and [[Dribble and Spitz]]. Her level is called "The Invent-Off". Penny's story starts with her attending the [[Diamond Academy]], she faces off with [[Dr. Crygor]] to who is the best inventor. Penny pulled out her invention, [[Pocket Bike]]. However, Dr. Crygor was knocked down with whatever reason. Penny took [[Mike]]'s microphones off and sang a song.

Later, Penny lends her Pocket Bike to [[Wario]] and wonders if he likes her invention.

Her level features three forms, "The Finger Food", "The Mortar and Pestle" and "The Boxer".

=== ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' ===
Penny is in the last stage of the "Twist League". She is seen in the opening cutscene receiving a call from [[Wario]], holding a large phone. Penny made "the best potion" called the [[Tonally Transformative Elixir]], a way to become a famous pop-star singer. She tests it on Dr. Crygor to be her "Guinea Pig". The effects resulted the voice become slightly pitched higher, but even an intense stomachache; surprised, Penny screamed "GRANDPAAA!!". Fortunately, Penny has the [[Super-Special Tummy Tonic]] that will heal the stomachache. As Dr. Crygor sips it, the microgame gameplay starts.

Penny has "Twist" microgames and its microgames are "That's Life". After the microgame gameplay, Dr. Crygor confirms to Penny that he feels better, and comes to conclusion to work n removing the stomach pains. He asks Penny when the effects will wear off, she does not answer but chuckles and disappears from the screen. It appears that the Tonally Transformative Elixir will not wear off soon anytime.

=== ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''===
Penny is one of the playable characters.
