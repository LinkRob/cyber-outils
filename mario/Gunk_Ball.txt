|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Gunk Ball''' is a special move used by a few enemies in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
Gunk Ball appears as a small, black ball that travels slowly at its target.

==Effects==
With Gunk Ball, the target will be unable to use any special moves for the rest of the battle. This can be countered by using [[Able Juice]]s or [[Princess Peach|Princess Toastool]]'s healing moves.
