'''Chain Chomp Fever''' is a Battle [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Opening==
The camera shows a pool of [[lava]], and a [[Chain Chomp]] jumps out. He lands on a rocky platform, and the camera zooms out as the four players jump into the scene. The game then begins.

==Gameplay==
The goal of the game is to try to avoid the Chain Chomp that continuously charges at the players. Other things to avoid are falling off the course through the breaks in the wall the Chain Chomp makes and the fire that comes up through the ground. When the Chain Chomp hits a wall, he becomes stunned temporarily. Any player who gets rammed by the Chain Chomp, touches the fire, or falls off the course is eliminated. The last player standing, or any players remaining after time runs out, wins.

==Ending==
The Chain Chomp stops rampaging, and any remaining players do their winning poses.
