'''World 3-2''' is the twelfth level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is the first water-themed level of the game.

== Layout ==
The level begins with [[Mario]] swimming into a small pool surrounded by rocks. Inside of the pool is a [[Warp Pipe]] that takes Mario to the actual level. Inside, the level plays out like a regular water-based level from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' but contains some parts that are above ground. After a small stream with cheep cheeps, Mario finds the [[Checkpoint Flag|checkpoint]] of the level, and past that, finds some [[Porcupuffer]]. There is also a [[Fire Flower]] Mario can use to defeat the enemies, even though he is underwater. Past the Porcu-Puffers are the debut of the [[Spike Eel]]. One of these blocks the way to the Warp Pipe that leads to the [[Flagpole]].

After reaching the Warp Pipe, Mario can take a different path, also blocked by a Spike Eel, that leads to the final [[Star Coin]] of the level, and a special Warp Pipe that leads him to the top of the Flag Pole.

== Star Coins ==
#The first Star Coin is behind a cage. To go inside the cage, there is a hole in the wall which leads to the Star Coin.
#While avoiding the Spike Eel, go down the small rectangular manhole. There is nothing down there but a pipe. Inside the pipe is a small room, holding the Second Star Coin.
#To collect the third Star Coin, the procedure is similar to collecting Star Coin 2, but instead Mario/Luigi has to swim upward, avoiding yet another Spike Eel. The Third Star Coin is above the water.
