'''Casual Coral''' is a [[clothing]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is a one-time reward for clearing the [[surfing minigame]] within 38 seconds. It can be equipped by either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]].

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', equipping Casual Coral increases Mario or Luigi's Defense by 80. It also has the special effect [[60% Poison Dodge]], which gives the user a 60 percent chance of evading the [[Poison (status effect)|Poison]] status effect from attacks that inflict it.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', Casual Coral grants a significantly lower Defense of 22. Despite its rarity, the Casual Coral can only be sold for three [[coin]]s, and it instead has [[Lucky Critical]] as its special effect.
