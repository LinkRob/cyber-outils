'''Fizzlits''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. They take on an electric puddle form that electrocutes whoever tries to jump on it. Therefore, Fizzlits can only be defeated while in their purple form.

==Physical description==
In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', Fizzlits have a tooth-like appearance, and in their electric puddle form, they have a simple flat, circle shape. However, in ''Super Mario 3D World'', they have a more cube-like appearance, as well as having no stubby legs. In their electric puddle form, they have a less flat, more square-like appearance.
