'''Dieter''' is the leader of the [[Best Fitness Friends]], and a major antagonist in ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. He is a Ranged-type enemy in battle. His name is a pun on the German name "Dieter" and the term "diet".

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Dieter is a tall, slim figure with pink skin, black sunglasses with a white trim, a black hat with yellow hair, black clothing complete with a yellow tie on his shirt, white gloves and boots. He is also almost seen carrying red, yellow and blue cards.

=== Powers and abilities ===
*'''Rain of Pain''': As a Ranged-type unit, Dieter's special skill is throwing multiple sets of cards at many targets to deal damage.
*'''[[Stop and Drop]]''': Dieter can throw a set of cards to prevent one of [[Bowser Jr.]]'s troops from using their special skill.
*'''[[Rally]]''': Dieter can use Rally to make his troops' special skills easier to execute.
*'''[[Call for Backup]]''': Dieter can summon enemy troopers. This is only used in [[Like Father, Like Son]], where he summons two [[Malatone Formula:X|Malatone-infected]] [[Borp]]s.
*'''[[Chance|Mystery Box]]''': Dieter can use Mystery Box to choose an option to benefit his troops. This is only used in [[The Trio Clash!]]. 
*'''[[Malatone Formula:X]]''': Dieter can give his troops Malatone Formula:X to increase their stat attributes.
*'''Fusion''': With the other BFF members, [[Beef]] and [[Kaley]], they can fuse into [[Best Fitness Friends (merged form)|a gigantic monstrosity]] and take Malatone Forumla:X.

== Trivia ==
*Dieter is known for using various German terms, such as saying to Bowser Jr. "Auf wiedersehen" (''goodbye'' in German) before attempting to kill him.
*In the English localization, during "The Trio Clash!", Dieter mentions that he is allergic to Turnips. In the Japanese script, however, there is no mention of any allergy as he simply mentions "What's that!?".
