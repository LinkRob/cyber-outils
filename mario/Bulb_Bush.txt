A '''Bulb Bush''' is a plant that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
This species of a plant is found only in [[Jade Jungle]] of [[Lavalava Island]]. Whenever [[Mario]] interacts with a Bulb Bush, it will light up and make a hollow-like sound.
