'''World 1-4''' is the fourth level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
World 1-4 takes the switch platform from [[World 1-3 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 1-3]] and expands upon it, containing the platforms throughout the level. Mario proceeds on one of these panels for half the level, dodging [[Paragoomba]]s and grabbing [[coin]]s along the way to the [[Checkpoint Flag|checkpoint]]. After the checkpoint, is another platform, but with more complex puzzles to obtain collectibles. [[Waterspout]]s also appear in the level, rising the switch platforms onto higher ground occasionally. A [[Warp Pipe]] at the end of the level will take Mario to the [[Flagpole]].

== Star Coins ==
'''Star Coin 1 - '''Let the platform ride the first Waterspout, and it'll reach another set of tracks. There is a Star Coin above the other set of tracks.

'''Star Coin 2 - '''After the checkpoint, when the platform reaches the first drop, let it go in reverse, and there is a Star Coin in the track behind the drop.

'''Star Coin 3 - '''After the small area with the purple warp box, take the left geyser onto the other set of tracks and put it in reverse to grab the last Star Coin.
