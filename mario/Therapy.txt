'''Therapy''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] and only requires 2 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

== Effects ==
When Therapy is used, a bunch of light appears on the character that is chosen. Therapy heals the [[Heart Point|HP]] of the character and, if timed right, can heal even more HP than usual. Therapy restores more HP than [[HP Rain]] and [[Group Hug]]. Seeing as it only requires 2 FP to use, Therapy is extremely useful in battle and may just replace the single healing items such as Super Mushrooms.
