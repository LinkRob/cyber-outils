'''World 5-2''' is the seccond [[level]] of [[World 5 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 5]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the eighteenth level overall in said game, taking place underground.

==Layout==
After a short transition showing the player entering a [[Warp Pipe]], the player will drop in to an area with hanging [[Piranha Plant]]s and a [[Koopa Troopa]], with a hidden power-up against the wall to the left. Above the next platform is a row of [[? Block]]s with the second from the right containing a [[Poison Mushroom]], and within the next [[Brick Block]] square formation is a power-up in the top third block from the left. After a Piranha Plant is another power-up in the first single Brick Block after the square formation. At the end of this row of Brick Blocks, before the next Piranha Plant, is a [[Super Star]], with a Poison Mushroom being at the end of the next row of Brick Blocks. Above the next tall pipe to the far right is a block containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] that will slide along the top, above pipes containing Piranha Plants, and into a [[pit]] if the player does not collect it in time. Across this pit is a block formation, with a [[Coin Block]] in the first block on the left and a [[Beanstalk]] leading to [[World 8 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 8]] on the right. After this block formation is a trio of [[lift]]s, with the first moving upward and the next two moving downwards. Next is a row of [[Coin]]s, followed by some Piranha Plants and narrow platforms before the Warp Pipe to the [[Goal Pole]], though if the player goes above the pipe, they will reach a [[Warp Zone]] to [[World 7 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 7]].
