'''Starview Peak''' is the second race course of the [[Star Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. It is the only new course in the [[cup]], and is the first checkpoint of both the [[Ice Rally]] and [[Spiny Rally]]. Starview Peak is an ice-themed course that resembles [[Rosalina's Ice World]] from ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' and [[Ice Ice Outpost]] from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. It takes place in and around a building that resembles the [[Comet Observatory]], located on top of an icy mountain in the arctic region of the game's map.
