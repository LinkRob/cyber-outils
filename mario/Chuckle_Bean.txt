A '''Chuckle Bean''' is a type of [[bean]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The only visual difference that Chuckle Beans have from other beans is their red color.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
They can be uncovered from certain underground spots on the overworld by using the [[Dunk Hammer]] technique. Chuckle Beans, like other beans, have an optional purpose of being used at the [[Starbeans Cafe]], where they are used to brew stat-boosting drinks named [[Bean Juice]]. In particular, Chuckle Beans are used as an ingredient for two Bean Juices: [[Chuckle Blend]], which takes only 25 Chuckle Beans, and [[Chuckoccino]], which takes ten Chuckle Beans alongside 15 [[Woo Bean]]s.
