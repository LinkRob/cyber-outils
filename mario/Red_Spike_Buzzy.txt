'''Red Spike Buzzies''', sometimes called '''R. S. Buzzies''' and also known as '''Red Spike Tops''', are enemies which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Red Spike Buzzies are more powerful versions of [[Spike Top]]s, and sport a red shell instead of a blue one. This plays well with the original Spike Top design from ''[[Super Mario World]]'' as well as the one from ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Red Spike Buzzies are one of the many teams in the [[Glitz Pit]], [[Spiky Joe]] and another Red Spike Buzzy make up the team, the [[Tiny Spinies]]. In being so, it can be difficult to obtain the [[tattle]] information if Mario fails to do so earlier. In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], Exhibition Matches have been added to the Glitz Pit which allow players to fight opponents they’d previously faced at any time they choose, this makes getting the tattle information for the Red Spike Buzzy marginally easier in the remake compared to the original.

==Trivia==
*The American version of ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' mistakenly lists the Defense stat for the Red Spike Tops as 5 instead of 4. This mistake was not present in the Japanese version and was corrected in the European version.
**In all versions of the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], the Red Spike Buzzies have their Defense stat correctly listed as 4.
*The Prima guide for ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' refers to Spike Tops as Red Spike Buzzies.
