She lives on [[Cool, Cool Mountain]] with her mother, [[Mother Penguin]].

==History==
At Cool, Cool Mountain, Mother Penguin is worried about Tuxie as she went missing and can't remember where she left her. She requests [[Mario]] to find her. Tuxie can be found on the top of the mountain, near the exact area where Mario starts. Mario must then take Tuxie to the bottom of the mountain where Mother Penguin is. In doing so, Mario earns a [[Power Star]] from her.

A similar-looking penguin can be found on the chimney of a house. When Mario brings that penguin in specific to Tuxie's mother, she says it isn't her child, because her child has her beak.

If Tuxie is reunited with Mother Penguin and Mario then steals Tuxie, Mother Penguin gets angry and waddles to Mario with an angry face. Despite this, Mother Penguin is not angered if Mario throws Tuxie off the mountain. This is most likely an error in the programing.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Her name originates from "tux," the shortened form of "{{wp|Black tie|tuxedo}}," a type of formal suit resembling a penguin's plumage.
