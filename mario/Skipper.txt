'''Skipper''' is one of the [[Royal Tennis Academy]] students featured in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. He is initially partnered with [[Flit]], but later switches over to [[Roy (Mario Tennis: Power Tour)|Roy]]. Skipper has the [[Tackle Shot]] as his [[Offensive Power Shot]] and the [[Lunging Save]] as his [[Defensive Power Shot]], both shared with [[Paula]].
