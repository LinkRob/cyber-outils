An '''Arrow Switch''' is an object which first appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''.

==Description==
Arrow Switches are placed on both the floor and ceiling alike. When [[Luigi]] steps onto an Arrow Switch on the floor, he travels up to the ceiling, where Luigi can move around on onto until he walks onto an Arrow Switch placed on the ceiling, sending him back onto the ground bellow.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Arrow Switches first appear in the [[Tea Room]] and later also appear in the [[Cellar (Luigi's Mansion)|Cellar]]. In the Tea Room, Luigi has to use the Arrow Switches in order to reach the [[treasure chest]] with the [[Ice Element Medal]], which is on the left table. In the Cellar, using the Arrow Switches are optional, but Luigi can use them to quickly move past several sand piles without having to vacuum them up into his [[Poltergust 3000]].
