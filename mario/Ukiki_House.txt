==Description==
Ukiki House is the home of the character [[Ukiki (character)|Ukiki]] and his niece, [[Akiki]]. Ukiki House is located inside of a leafy green tree, and it is surrounded by other trees. There is a wooden platform with a bundle of bananas and a net hammock located above the house.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Ukiki House is located in the [[Jungle Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. Akiki is panicking because of Ukiki's sudden disappearance. When the player's character walks in, Akiki asks for their him to find Ukiki, and agreeing to this initiates the [[Monkeynapping!?]] quest.

At the start of the Monkeynapping!? quest, [[Shroomlock]] enters Ukiki House in response to Akiki crying for help. Akiki gives Shroomlock a kidnapping note ("bIg teAM cAT leaTHEr fLAKE?! blarmpot") found inside the house, and Shroomlock tells the player to decipher the latter to search for Ukiki. A clue is that all of the capital letters spell out a sentence ("I AM AT THE LAKE"), meant to point out that Ukiki is hiding at [[Loch Dorrie]]. When the player's character visits there, they find Ukiki, who explains that his disappearance was intended to prank Akiki, then Ukiki returns back to Ukiki House.
