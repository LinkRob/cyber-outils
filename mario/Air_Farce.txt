'''Air Farce''' is a Battle [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place over an ocean.

==Opening==
The players are shown on a large platform with [[hang glider]]s depicting their [[emblem]]s from a bird's eye view, which transitions to a side view.

==Gameplay==
All players start on the platform with hang gliders, and they need to glide over a long area of water, passing measuring points. Players who fall into the water are out of the minigame. The player who can get the farthest before falling into the water wins the minigame. If everyone flies the same distance, the minigame ends in a tie.

This is one of the minigames that can appear in Decathlon Castle. Here, the player's objective is to glide as far as possible to earn as many points as possible, up to 1,000.

==Ending==
[[File:Air Farce Finish.png|thumb|right|The ending to Air Farce]]
The players do their winning and losing animations on the starting platform. The minigame can end without a loser.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Adjust angle

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Strap yourself into the hang glider and fly as far as possible."''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"Dive first to pick up speed, then fly parallel to the water to cover the greatest distance."''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The title of the minigame is a pun on "Air Force."
