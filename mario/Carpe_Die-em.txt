The '''Carpe Die-em''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Carpe Die-em.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Carpe Die-em resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has medium cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Carpe Die-em deals around 165 to 175 damage.

It has a 60% chance of triggering the Super Effect. However, aiming it at the [[Valkyrie]] increases the weapon's chances of triggering the effect by 90%. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Bounce (status effect)|Bounce]] dealing 210 damage.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Carpe Die-em as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it after the player defeats [[Mecha Jr.]]

== Etymology ==
Carpe Die-em comes from the phrase, ''carpe diem''. It came from the Roman poet Horace, which is translated as "seize the day".
