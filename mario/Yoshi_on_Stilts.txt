'''Yoshi on Stilts''' is the seventh level of [[World 2 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 2]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', a [[Red Yoshi]] takes control in this level. Hence the level's name, there are plenty of [[Stilt Guy]]s found throughout the level however at one point of the level, Yoshi gets his own pair of [[Stilts]] which are used to cross a dangerous sea of [[lava]]. The level itself is divided into two sections, the first section is aboveground which takes place in a jungle with a large lake, the second section takes place in an underground cave with a straight path consisting of many split paths (upper and lower), the cave goes very deep underground where molten earth starts filling the floor. The level's end consists of a small jungle lake where [[Cheep Cheep]]s are seen hopping out of.

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the left, this doesn't take your forward through the level but there is a [[Winged Cloud]] here which contains [[Coin]]s, [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]] as well as a [[Stork Stop]] can also be found here, be quick to swallow the [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]] for [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] and then switch to [[Baby DK]] as you will be needing him for the level's next section. Advance to the right and past the starting point, there is an [[Egg Block]], hit it to fill your egg supply, advance to the right and hop across the lakes but watch out as a [[Lunge Fish]] is seen lurking in one of them (if you get too close, the Lunge Fish will eat you). Climb along the vines as amidst the [[Coin]]s here, two of them are [[Red Coin]]s, [[Snap Jaw]]s can be seen climbing down the vines, watch out for these as they can knock [[Baby DK]] off your back and it can be challenging to retrieve him to the vine-filled areas. Once you've gotten all the [[Coin]]s, advance to the left and return to the starting point, hit the soft stone with an egg, this will cause the slab it was holding to descend revealing a vine. Use the nearby tree to hop at the vine, jump from vine to vine and use the ropes to swing farther, there is a [[Smiley Flower]] at the end of this track, grab it and jump to the ledge at the end and move to the right to enter the cave (you can also take the lower entrance below but you will miss some key collectibles if you do).

Upon entering the cave, you will come across a several soft stones, smash them, drop down and smash the craked stone to the right with a egg from [[Baby DK]] (the cracked stones won't crack if another baby's egg is to hit it) to get a [[Smiley Flower]]. Once done, advance to the left and hit the soft stone to the left and jump up to the empty gap in the niche to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]], hit the [[Winged Cloud]] to get a [[Key]], from here, use the [[Spring Ball]] to reach the higher ledge, smash the soft stones with eggs and ground pound the cracked stone to reach a small nook with dozens of [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] of different colors. Grab all the eggs and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] sealed off by the flooring (only the explosion radius from [[Baby DK]]'s egg throws can hit this winged cloud) as hitting it will activate the wind allowing [[Baby Peach]] to glide up to the upper ledge, go back to the [[Stork Stop]] and switch to [[Baby Peach]] and from here, glide up to the ledge and enter the locked door, in here, you will find a secret area with many [[Donut Lift]]s, hop across the [[Donut Lift]]s  and use the wind to glide to the upper platform, from here, grab all the [[Coin]]s as two of the [[Coin]]s here are [[Red Coin]]s and use the [[Donut Lift]]s to descend to the [[Baby Coin]] below, you can either jump back up and descend through the side gap or use the next set of [[Donut Lift]]s to descend back down, after collecting all the goodies, exit through the door you entered from.

Upon exiting the area, progress downward and drop down, advance to the right and drop down through the drop, tag the [[Middle Ring]] on your way down, and beat the [[Stilt Guy]]s and [[Nipper Plant]]s in the area, in the corner of the niche to the right of the mushroom below the [[Middle Ring]]'s location is a [[Winged Cloud]], hit it with an egg for some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and hop back up and advance to the right (collecting the [[Coin]]s in your path), there is a [[Stork Stop]] here, switch to whichever baby you want and continue through the [[Coin]]-filled tunnel collecting the [[Coin]]s as you go. Four of the many [[Coin]]s are [[Red Coin]]s, keep going collecting [[Coin]]s as you go but watch out for [[Bungee Piranha]]s above you, (these guys will extend their necks down to try and swallow you, if they succeed, you will be swallowed and they will pull you up to their level, this can be troublesome if one of the last two get you because the [[Flipper]]s prevent you from going back and getting the collectibles you missed). Kill the [[Bungee Piranha]]s as they attempt to swallow you and progress to the right and make sure you collect the [[Smiley Flower]] here amidst the [[Coin]] mass, exit through the [[Flipper]]s and enter the next section of the cave.

In the next section, continue progressing to the right but watch out for the [[Stilt Guy]]s coming your way, at the [[Stork Stop]], switch to [[Baby DK]] and swallow any [[Shy Guy]]s that emerge from the [[Pipe]], jump to the swinging ropes and snag the [[Key]] from the [[Baron Von Zeppelin]]. Next, hop to the ledge and fill your egg supply with the [[Egg Block]] and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] above to create a rope, use the rope to swing to the vines and climb to the hut to play a minigame ([[Egg Toss]]). From here, return to the ledge and shoot an egg at the extremely tall [[Stilt Guy]] in the area (beating the Stilt Guy from the lower level is possible but it will take multiple hits due to the [[Shy Guy]] being higher ([[Baby DK]]'s powerful egg tosses can lower the Stilt Guy much farther)). Once the [[Stilt Guy]] is beaten hop to the opposite ledge it was blocking and grab the four [[Red Coin]]s here and drop down to grab the rest of the [[Coin]]s as you descend to the [[Flipper]]s and even the two [[Red Coin]]s below the [[Flipper]]s, tag the [[Middle Ring]] to your left and advance to the right and enter the [[Pipe]] to the right (if need be, return to the [[Stork Stop]] to switch out your baby before entering).

Upon entering the cave's mantle, you will immediately notice a sea of [[lava]], you will need to use the [[Stilts]] to cross this dangerous [[lava]] cave. Hop onto the stilts and walk across (the torches around the area can be swallowed and used as projectiles for the enemies up ahead). Continue moving and hit the [[Yellow Egg Block]]s and beat or avoid the [[Piranha Plant]] above you, continue moving and as you move, make sure you collect the two [[Red Coin]]s above near the track's end. From here, use the [[Spring Ball]] to reach the upper platform and hop to the longer stilts. As you start walking on these stilts, collect the [[Coin]]s in your path, [[Podoboo]]s can be seen below taking high leaps out of the [[lava]] (sometimes jumping in arcs), as the [[Podoboo]]s don't jump high enough, avoiding them shouldn't be too much of a problem (unless you slip off the stilts in which case you will just end up falling into the [[lava]] anyway). Continue along the track and hit the [[Red Egg Block]]s and kill the [[Piranha Plant]] in your way, there is also an invisible [[Winged Cloud]] containing [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] so hit this too, continue along the path and watch out for the [[Stilt Guy]] heading in your direction. Upon reaching the end ledge, enter the [[Pipe]] to exit the lava cave.

Upon returning to the jungle, hop upward to reach the [[Smiley Flower]], from here, hop from tree to tree collecting [[Coin]]s as you avoid the [[Cheep Cheep]]s, at the end, collect the [[Coin]] and the last two [[Red Coin]]s and jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
You will need [[Baby Peach]] for this [[Baby Coin]]. Upon taking the cave's upper entrance by using the vines above with [[Baby DK]], drop down and use the [[Spring Ball]] to reach the upper ledge above and toss an egg with [[Baby DK]] at the ground above where the [[Winged Cloud]] is sealed off. The blast radius will hit the [[Winged Cloud]] activating the wind, destroy the soft stone above and switch to [[Baby Peach]] at the nearby [[Stork Stop]], use the wind to glide to the ledge and enter the door. From here, hop across the [[Donut Lift]]s to reach the ledge at the end (if you accidently use them all you can still make the jump to the end ledge but it is tricky, alternatively, you can exit and reenter the are and the [[Donut Lift]]s will respawn) from here, glide to the upper ledge and collect the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]s and use the top [[Donut Lift]]s to descend to the [[Baby Coin]] and obtain it, to exit, either hop to the upper ledge and drop through the sides or use the [[Donut Lift]]s below the [[Baby Coin]].
