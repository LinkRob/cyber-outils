'''Grow Wario Grow''' is a [[microgame]] in the [[Anything Goes]] folder of ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''

==Layout==
The microgame is based on ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' and even takes place in [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Land)|World 1-1]]. Grow Wario Grow is one of the few microgames based on an earlier [[Nintendo]] game to not be part of [[9-Volt]]'s microgame set, [[Nintendo Classics]]. Although this is the case, Wario's sprites are from ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and the grayscaled [[Super Mushroom]] is from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Gameplay==
The player has to get [[Tiny Wario]] to hit the [[? Block]] to acquire the [[Super Mushroom]] before [[Mario]] does. Unlike other appearances, the ? Block does not turn into an [[Empty Block]] after being hit. If done successfully, Tiny Wario becomes regular [[Wario]], who laughs while Mario pouts, and the player wins the microgame. If unsuccessful, Mario appears happy while Tiny Wario appears shocked, and the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
* '''Level 1:''' Tiny Wario must step to the right side of the ? Block, where the Mushroom will fall onto him. 
* '''Level 2:''' The Super Mushroom moves along two [[Empty Block]]s before landing further to the right. Tiny Wario must jump onto the platform to catch the Super Mushroom before it falls onto Mario.
* '''Level 3:''' The Super Mushroom moves three blocks to the right. The player must jump on Mario's head to obtain the Mushroom before it falls onto him.
