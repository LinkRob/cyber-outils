An '''Inflatable Thwomp''' is an object which first appears in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', exclusively in the [[2 vs. 2 minigame]] [[The Great Deflate]], and retain this role in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.

==Description==
Inflatable Thwomps are blue inflatable objects resembling [[Thwomp]]s. They appear to be made of complete rubber, as shown in official artwork of [[Wario]] and [[Waluigi]] pounding one, but in the games, they are also shown to also be transparent. When an Inflatable Thwomp is [[Ground Pound]]ed, it loses some air. Inflatable Thwomps are quite sturdy because it takes several Ground Pounds to flatten an Inflatable Thwomp completely.

It is unknown if Inflatable Thwomps are organic, conscious beings or simply inflatable toys. However, they do make a grunting noise similar to a Thwomp's upon hitting the ground at the start of The Great Deflate. They also have a face on the front, later changed in ''Mario Party: The Top 100'' to look angrier and to resemble a Thwomp's face more closely. Inflatable Thwomps have red pupils in both designs.

==In ''Mario Party'' series==
Inflatable Thwomps have a central role in The Great Deflate, where the goal is for both characters on each team to flatten their Inflatable Thwomp first. The characters can deflate an Inflatable Thwomp by Ground Pounding it repeatedly; the higher one Ground Pounds, the more their Inflatable Thwomp deflates. The first team to completely deflate their Inflatable Thwomp wins.
