'''Lumalee'''<ref>https://i.ytimg.com/vi/P4uPcjdQar4/maxresdefault.jpg</ref> is a supporting character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. He is an unfortunate [[Luma]] who was captured by [[Bowser (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Bowser]] and imprisoned in the latter's castle. Lumalee was seen in the film, where [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]] wonders how to escape his cage.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Lumalee resembles his [[Lumalee|video game counterpart]], with a few differences. His eyes match the position of a normal [[Luma]]’s instead of being close together in the center. Also, to match Illumination’s choice of character design, he is given teeth.

=== Personality traits ===
As shown during his appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', Lumalee is insane and pessimistic, constantly making creepy and dark comments. He speaks in a child's voice.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Like most [[Luma]]s, Lumalee can fly. He apparently also has telekinesis, as shown when he used his cage as a swing without touching it.

== Biography ==
Lumalee, Luigi, and the [[Penguin]]s are imprisoned in cages above the lava. Lumalee stated in his mad rambling that there is no escape except death. The Penguins' [[Penguin King|king]] tells Luigi to pay Lumalee no heed, describing him as cute but insane.

At one point, Lumalee was supposed to be burnt in the lava publicly in the wedding reception, and was excited to meet his end. However, [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and [[Donkey Kong (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Donkey Kong]] saved Lumalee and the other prisoners, much to his dismay.

After the events of the second movie, Bowser (now in his Dry Bowser form and reduced to a hopping skull), along with his son, Bowser Jr., are trying to escape a [[Far-Away High Security Prison Facility|high security prison]], where the prison cells are guarded by Lumalee. He tells the terrified prisoners that they should look forward to "misery and death" during their stay, earning a terrified scream from them both.
