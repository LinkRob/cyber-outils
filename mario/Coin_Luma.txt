'''Coin Lumas''' are a behavioral variant of [[Hungry Luma]]s in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
They are found in certain [[galaxy|galaxies]], and must be fed a certain number of [[coin]]s to transform into a planet. This is somewhat different from regular Hungry Lumas, which instead eat [[Star Bit]]s to transform into a galaxy.
