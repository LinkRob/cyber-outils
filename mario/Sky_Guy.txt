'''Sky Guys''' are a variant of [[Shy Guy]]s that float around while strapped to a few balloons. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', in which they are enemies encountered in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]. It is possible for [[Mario]] to pop a Sky Guy's balloons, causing it to fall down and become a regular Shy Guy.
