'''Airpain''' is an [[Anything Goes]] [[microgame]] which appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
[[Wario]] is flying an orange biplane above the sea, which has dark blue water, during a cloudless day.

==Gameplay==
Wario has to shoot down any plane headed his way to avoid crashing into them. Levels 1, 2, and 3 feature one, two, and three planes to shoot down, respectively.

==Etymology==
The minigame's name is a portmanteau of "airplane" and "pain."
