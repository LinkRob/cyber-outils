A '''Jelly Candy''' is an item found in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Effects ==
It is a recipe made by giving [[Zess T.]] a [[Cake Mix]] and a [[Jammin' Jelly]]. Eating a Jelly Candy restores 64 [[Flower Point|FP]], and the Jelly Candy restores the most FP compared to any other item. Jelly Candies serve as the replacement of [[Jelly Pop]]s from ''[[Paper Mario]]'', which they are functionally identical to.
