'''Petey Piranha's Shell Smackdown''' is a boss minigame featuring [[Petey Piranha]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. The characters can damage Petey by throwing [[Green Shell]]s at him, which earns them points. However, Petey can reflect shells back to the sender; the second phase features bigger shells that grants the thrower two points. Whoever has the most points at the end is the winner.
