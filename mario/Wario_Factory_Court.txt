'''Wario Factory Court''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
In normal mode, Wario Factory Court takes place on a chain-like platform with visible gears below. A caution tape is used as the net, owing to the factory theme of the court. There is a graphic of Wario from ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' in the background, and two metal structures depict the ''[[WarioWare]]'' [[bomb]] symbol. [[Bob-omb]]s are the spectators, and they explode whenever a side gets a [[point]].

In the gimmick version, the floor consists of three [[conveyor belt]]s, which are divided into segments by blue lines on one side and red on the other. There are both red and light blue arrows lined above the net horizontally. If a character hits the ball into an arrow, the conveyor belt of the corresponding color and direction starts to move in the direction indicated. When the characters switch sides, the conveyor belts stop moving.

The background music is an arrangement of the [[Greenhorn Forest]] theme from ''[[Wario World]]''.

==In ''Mario Power Tennis''==
Wario Factory Court is where the [[Minigame|Special Game]] [[Chain-Chomp Challenge]] takes place. The Special Game similarly involves the conveyor belts as a gimmick.

==Trivia==
*[[Wario Factory (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Wario Factory]] from ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]'' is likely based on Wario Factory Court. It also features Bob-ombs, although these ones walk on to the court itself from a conveyor belt.
