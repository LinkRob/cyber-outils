'''Bungee Buds''' are young [[Bungee Piranha]]s appearing in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', they are seen during the battle with the [[Big Bungee Piranha]] and aid him by attempting to pester [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] as he dodges the colossal [[Piranha Plant]]'s attacks. Bungee Buds resemble small [[Bungee Piranha]]s with prickly stems.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
Bungee Buds appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. In this game, two appear in [[Big Bungee Piranha's Lair]] during the boss fight with [[Big Bungee Piranha]]. After [[Kamek]] has used his magic to enlarge the [[Big Bungee Piranha]], the Bungee Buds will also bloom. During the battle, the Bungee Buds will occasionally pull themselves down attempting to hit Yoshi as he dodges the [[Big Bungee Piranha]]'s attacks. Every time the [[Big Bungee Piranha]] lunges down to swallow [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], the Bungee Buds follow as well. If the [[Big Bungee Piranha]] successfully swallows Yoshi, one of the Bungee Buds will grab hold of the baby making it rather challenging to retrieve the baby from the Bungee Buds, they will constantly move up and down at this time as well. If Yoshi successfully tricks the [[Big Bungee Piranha]] into biting down on the [[Chomp Rock]], they will be stunned with their leader, with every hit the [[Big Bungee Piranha]] receives, the more aggressive the Bungee Buds get. The Bungee Buds can't be destroyed directly but after the [[Big Bungee Piranha]] is destroyed, they also die with it.

==Trivia==
*The Bungee Buds are similar to the [[Naval Bud]]s which appeared alongside [[Naval Piranha]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.
