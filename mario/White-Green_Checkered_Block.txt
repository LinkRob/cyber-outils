'''White-Green Checkered Blocks''' (also called '''Spring Blocks''', '''Bounce Blocks''', or '''Bouncy Blocks''') are a type of springy [[Block]] that propel [[Mario]] and friends higher when jumped on similar to the [[Jump Block]]s. White-Green Checkered Blocks require timing to spring higher into the air by pressing {{button|wii|2}} at the right moment.

==History==
===''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''===
[[Image:WGC Block Wii.png|thumb|left|White-Green Checkered Blocks from ''NSMBW''.]]
White-Green Checkered Blocks are first seen in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. They are first seen in [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5]] being semi-common appearing more towards the end of World 5, they also appear slightly more commonly in [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]] and [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7]]. They can be used for reaching higher ledges and obtaining secrets. Players need to press {{button|wii|2}} at the right time when they jump on a White-Green Checkered Block.
===''[[Mario Party 9]]''===
[[Image:WGC Block Minigame.png|thumb|right|WGC Blocks in Upward Mobility.]]
White-Green Checkered Blocks reappear in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' in the minigame [[Upward Mobility]]. They function the same way they did in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' and players need to make high jumps to the higher sets of blocks to progress, once again players need to press {{button|wii|2}} at the right moment to make high jumps however, the timing is slightly stricter. The player that reaches the top first is the winner.
==Trivia==
*As of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', it seems the [[Jump Block]]s have replaced the White-Green Checkered Blocks as the springy blocks again.
