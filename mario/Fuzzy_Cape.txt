'''Fuzzy Cape''' is one of [[Geno]]'s personal armor in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought at a shop in [[Nimbus Land]] for 70 [[coin]]s. Fuzzy Cape increases Geno's [[Defense]] by 24 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 12.
