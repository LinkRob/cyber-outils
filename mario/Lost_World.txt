== '''Introduction''' ==
'''Lost World''' is the name given to a hidden location in the Donkey Kong Country 2 and Donkey Kong Land 2 and 3 games. 

== '''Trivia''' ==
* Donkey Kong Country and Donkey Kong Land are the only games in the Donkey Kong Country series that don't feature a hidden world.
* In [[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]] and [[Donkey Kong Country 3]], it is named '''Krematoa'''.
* The Game Boy Advance version of Donkey Kong Country 3 features one additional hidden world named '''Pacifica'''.
* In Donkey Kong Country Returns, it is named '''Golden Temple.'''
* In Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze, it is named '''Secret Seclusion'''.

The '''Lost World''' could mean;

*[[Lost World (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)]] - The Lost World guarded by [[Klubba]] in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and the semi-sequel ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''.
*[[Lost World (Donkey Kong Land III)]] - The Lost World in ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'' where it's the primary setting of the game.
*[[Krematoa]] - The Lost World of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' called the Lost World by some.
