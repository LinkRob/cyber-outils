'''Captain Shy Guy''' is a [[Shy Guy]] character who appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. He is encountered when the player lands on a [[Lucky Space]] on [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]]. Whenever this occurs, Captain Shy Guy leads whoever landed on the space to an outer space diner (called "Shy Guy's Orbital Buffet"). The Lucky Space area does not lead to a free star. Instead, it takes the player to a path of [[Blue Space|Blue Spaces]] --with 3 coins-- and their inventory (by passing Candy Spaces) gets filled with [[Bullet Candy|Bullet]] and/or [[Bowser Candy]] at the end of it. Unlike other Shy Guys, he does not have a belt or a brown ribbon for his mask. In the buffet restaurant, some other [[Shy Guy]]s of his kind can be seen.

== Trivia ==
* If the player reaches 10,000 [[point]]s in [[Test for the Best]], other Shy Guys of his kind can be found playing around on a moon.
* There is a figure of Captain Shy Guy and his spaceship that can be purchased for 50 party cards in Koopas item stand.
