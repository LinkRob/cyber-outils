The '''Custom Club Shop''' is a location in ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''.

==Description==
The Custom Club Shop is a golf club smithy where [[Custom Ticket]]s can be exchanged to produce up to fifteen [[Custom Club]]s. As such, it is an updated version of the [[Club Maker's Hut]] from ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for [[Game Boy Color]]. The Custom Club Shop is always open, and is where Golf Clubs can be picked up in storage.  A Custom Ticket A can be obtained in the cabinet of the shop.
