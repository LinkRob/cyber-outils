'''Blue Jeans''' are a [[clothing]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They can only be equipped by [[Luigi]].

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Blue Jeans can be bought from the clothes store in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 220 [[coin]]s. If Luigi is equipped with them, his [[HP]] increases by 5 and his [[Defense|DEF]] goes up by 16.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Blue Jeans are instead obtained at [[Château de Chucklehuck]], and are no longer sold at Beanbean Castle Town. Blue Jeans increase DEF by 14, slightly lower than in the original version, and Blue Jeans no longer increase HP in favor of the [[HP-Auto Gain]] ability. Blue Jeans can be sold for only 3 coins.
