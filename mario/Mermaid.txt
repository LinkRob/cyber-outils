==History==
The Mermaid is only seen in [[Gloomy Galleon]] where she lives inside of a large shell that only [[Tiny Kong]] can get into. The Mermaid is upset that her pearls have gone missing and Tiny agrees to help her. The Mermaid's pearls can be found inside of a large treasure chest inside a bunch of [[Oyster]]s. After retrieving all of the pearls, Tiny heads back to the Mermaid who is pleased to have her pearls back. The Mermaid then gives Tiny a [[Golden Banana]].
