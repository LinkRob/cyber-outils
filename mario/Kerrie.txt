==History==
Kerrie lives in [[Flopside]] where she was a well-known singer in her days. However, she never caught on with anyone else which lead to her singing less. However, she would be happy to sing for [[Mario]] and the others. She mentions her boyfriend is at the arcade which is either [[Mort]] or [[Hort]].
