==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': After sliding down the fifth wheel, jump up the hovering platform leading to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': In Penguin form, get on the platform in between the fifth and sixth red coins and then destroy the Brick Block surrounding the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In Penguin form, slide on the last floating platform and destroy the Brick Blocks surrounding the top warp pipe. Before going in the pipe, jump up to reveal a hidden hole and then wall jump to get the coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Under the third Star Coin shows a picture of Super Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' sliding.
