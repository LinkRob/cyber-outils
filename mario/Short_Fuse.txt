'''Short Fuses''' are a behavioral variant of [[Grinder (species)|Grinder]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They attack by dropping [[Needlenose]]s or [[Grenade]]s on a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] while holding on to a vine. A Short Fuse is generally found high up, but it can be defeated by either swallowing it or throwing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at it.
