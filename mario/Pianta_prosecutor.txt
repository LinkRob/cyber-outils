'''Pianta prosecutor''' is a [[Pianta]] character who appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. He appears only in the cutscene where the [[Pianta judge]] presides over a trial about whether [[Mario]] is guilty of polluting [[Isle Delfino]].

In the courthouse cutscene, the Pianta prosecutor starts testifying against Mario after the judge declares that court in session. The Pianta judge ultimately sides with the Pianta prosecutor, immediately overruling [[Princess Peach]] and [[Toadsworth]]'s object, and forces Mario to clean the entirety of Isle Delfino. It is possible he was seen at the ends of the game celebrating after [[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]]'s defeat.
