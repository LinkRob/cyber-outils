'''Dotted-Line Blocks''' are a type of [[block]] which appear in several ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' series and ''[[Yoshi's Island (series)|Yoshi's Island]]'' series, with its debut appearance being in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. Dotted-Line Block is the outline of a block, and a switch usually has to be activated for them to soldify, either temporarily or permanently.
