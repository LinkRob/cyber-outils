'''''Super Mario Race''''' is a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game produced by [[Mani Industries]] as part of the [[Gamewatch Boy]] line in 1992. It is a racing game, released around the same time as ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'', the first game of the {{series|Mario Kart}}. ''Super Mario Race'' is the first game within the Gamewatch Boy line, with the second and final one being ''[[Dr. Mario (Gamewatch Boy)|Dr. Mario]]''.
