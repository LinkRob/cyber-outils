'''Tobī''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tobī are aquatic creatures with gazing eyeballs, an open mouth, and incisors that are very large in proportion to their body. In the [[Game Boy Color]] version, Tobī have a brown body, and the palette also gives them light yellow eyes and teeth.

===Behavior===
Tobī jump out of the water, similar to [[Cheep Cheep]]s, except they only bounce [[Wario]] slightly back rather than damage him. This behavior was later given to the [[Tadpole (enemy)|Tadpole]] enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]'', with the added effect of causing Wario to roll back until he hits a wall.

==In ''Wario Land II''==
Wario first encounters Tobī in the story [[Turn off the giant faucet!]], where they appear after his [[Wario Castle|castle]] had been flooded by the [[Black Sugar Gang]].
