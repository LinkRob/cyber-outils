A '''Dark Spiked Goomba''' is a dark variant of a [[Spiked Goomba]] that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They have 10 HP and 4 Attack. They only appear in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]] in rooms 5, 19, and 48. Similar to their Spiked Goomba counterpart, Dark Spiked Goombas are not able to be jumped on.
