A '''Fright Bomb''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
[[File:Fright Bomb SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
When a Fright Bomb is used, a [[Boo]] appears over the selected enemy and deals 100 [[Heart Point|HP]] to it, with the possibility of also inflicting the [[Fear]] status. Fright Bombs can be purchased in the [[Item Shop]] in [[Seaside Town]] for 100 [[coin]]s and can also be received from trading items in [[Moleville]].
