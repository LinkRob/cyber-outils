'''Fish & Cheeps''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
The two characters have to swim in a river filled with [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Mega Cheep Cheep]]s, which they must avoid while swimming. After a short time, some Cheep Cheeps start to go in zigzags and even chase a character if they are too close. The game ends once either character bumps into a Cheep Cheep. If both characters are still remaining when the 30 second [[time limit]] runs out, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
*{{button|gcn|A}}: Tap to swim

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Swim and dodge the school of angry Cheep Cheeps. If you touch one, it's game over!''
*'''Advice''': ''Watch for vengeful Cheep Cheeps! Some of them will chase you!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a pun on the British dish {{w|fish and chips}}.
