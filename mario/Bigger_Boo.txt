'''Bigger Boo''' is the third boss of ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. He was a regular [[Big Boo]] until [[Kamek]] used magic to increase his size. Bigger Boo is encountered in his fortress, aptly named [[Bigger Boo's Fort]], in [[World 2 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 2]].

== Battle ==
The player must defeat him by throwing [[egg]]s at him. If Yoshi looks at him, he becomes transparent and invulnerable. Yoshi must throw eggs by bouncing off the walls, while facing away from Bigger Boo. With every hit, he becomes bigger until he explodes. It takes five hits to defeat Bigger Boo.
