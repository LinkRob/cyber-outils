'''Spikes''' are a hazardous object which appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==Description==
[[File:BeakPillar.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Mario near some Spikes]]
Spikes are wooden stakes with a pointed edge giving it the shape of a pencil. They only appear in [[castle]] and [[fortress]] levels, specifically only [[Chocolate Fortress]] and [[Larry's Castle]]. Spikes come out from either the ceiling or floor, similar to a [[Spike Pillar|Skewer]], but unlike them, most Spikes do not completely push their way out from one side to another, leaving them vulnerable to attack. if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] touch a Spike from the top, they take damage, due to its pointed edge, but if either brother touches the side, they are unaffected.
