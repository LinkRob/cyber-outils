A '''Safe Space''' is a [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
A Safe Space is a variation of the [[Blue Space]] and the only appearance of such a space in ''Mario Party: Island Tour''. Safe Spaces only appear in [[Banzai Bill's Mad Mountain]], specifically nine in total, and each of them are in an alcove on both halves of the mountain. A Safe Space can only be occupied by one character at a time.

While a Safe Space does not cause a character to go closer to the goal, most [[Danger Space]]s have a Safe Space just north of it. A character can decide to hide in a Safe Space, even if they still have remaining spaces to move the current turn, in which case going to a Safe Space ends the character's turn prematurely. The purpose of a Safe Space is that if a character hides in one, they are not at risk of being hit by a [[Banzai Bill]] if it were to launch out of a [[Banzai Bill Cannon]], although this only applies up until their next turn.
