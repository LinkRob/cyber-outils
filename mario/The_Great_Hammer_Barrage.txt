'''The Great Hammer Barrage''' is the sixth level of [[Hoohoo Mountain]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 4.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 8 [[Hammer Bro|Hammer Bros]], with the enemy captain being a Hammer Bro.

The final battle contains 2 [[Rex|Rexes]] and 6 Hammer Bros.

After the final battle, the player unlocks a Hammer Bro.

If the player replays the battle, they can unlock up to 2 more Hammer Bros each time.
