The '''Seed''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is dropped by a [[Beezo|Shy Away]] after defeating [[Megasmilax]].

==History==
The Seed is highly coveted by the [[Gardener]], who spent his entire life looking for it. When [[Mario]] goes to [[Rose Town]] to show the Seed to the Gardener, he gets very excited, and asks Mario if he can have it. When the Gardener receives both the Seed and the [[Fertilizer]] from Mario, he puts both of them into a nearby pot; a [[beanstalk]] then grows out from it, allowing Mario to obtain both the [[Lazy Shell]] weapon and armor.
