'''Mr. E''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Physical description==
Mr. E is a [[Dryite]] that wears a blue turban. He is one of two Dryites that wears this color of clothing.

==History==
Mr. E lives in [[Dry Dry Outpost]] who is known for not speaking too much. However, during the chain [[letter]] sidequest, Mr. E reveals that he ships out items from [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]] to various other locations. Mr. E then gives a letter to [[Parakarry]] to give to  [[Three Beautiful Sisters|Miss T.]] of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]].

*Mr. E's name is a pun of the word "mystery" which describes his overall mysterious and quiet nature.
