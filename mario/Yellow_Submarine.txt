The '''Yellow Submarine''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Yellow Submarine.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Yellow Submarine resembles a hand cannon. Its design is a strong resemblance to a submarine. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack with a medium range. The Yellow Submarine deals around 55 to 65 damage. When dealing damage to foes, it can inflict [[Bounce (status effect)|Bounce]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Yellow Submarine as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it after the player defeats [[Rabbid Kong]].
