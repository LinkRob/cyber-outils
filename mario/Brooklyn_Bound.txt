"'''Brooklyn Bound'''" is the twelfth animated episode of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''.

== Synopsis ==
[[Koopa Khan]] (the alias taken on by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] during the episode) has been chasing the Mario Bros. and their companions for days. After the group gets caught up in a windy storm in the [[Snow World]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] complains that he is fed up with the [[Mushroom World]] and wishes to return to [[Brooklyn]], and [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] agrees with him. At this point, Koopa Khan catches up with them and chases them into a cave. Inside, Luigi stupidly pulls a switch that causes a pit to open up beneath them. Koopa Khan walks off, confident that his adversaries have been finished off.

Fortunately, the good guys land in a cavern filled with well-constructed pipes. There, they meet [[Salvador Drainado]], a master plumber from Brooklyn who has also been trapped in the Mushroom World (for thirteen years, according to him). Drainado tells them that he has found a drainpipe that will bring him back to Brooklyn, and the Marios jump at the chance to help him find it. After helping Drainado clear away a wall blocking their path, the group crosses over a chasm where molten lava flows at the bottom. However, Koopa Khan, having learned the Mario group survived, shows up and blows up their bridge using a [[Bob-omb]]. However, the good guys grab onto a ledge while falling down, and in an example of [[Deus Ex Machina]], Mario airlifts them back up by powering up with a [[Fire Flower]].

Just as they reach the [[Last Drainpipe to Brooklyn]], the Mario Bros. reconsider returning to Brooklyn, but [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] insists they go. Just when they're about ready, Koopa Khan reappears and captures Princess Toadstool and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]]. Mario immediately bolts out of the drainpipe, and Luigi goes with him, so only Drainado returns to Brooklyn. In the meantime, the Marios rescue their friends from Bowser once again.

== Trivia/Goofs ==
*In a rare instance of inter-episode continuity on ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show'', "[[Plumbers Academy]]" reveals that Drainado had founded a plumbing academy attended by Mario and Luigi.
*In this episode, Mario gains the ability to fly after grabbing the [[Fire Flower]], something that never occurs in any ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game.
*Although Drainado claims that the drainpipe will be the Mario Bros.' only chance to return to Brooklyn, this is later contradicted by the episode "[[Flatbush Koopa]]", where the Marios do indeed return to Brooklyn, as well as several episodes of ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' where the characters frequently made visits to [[Earth]].
*In a segment in the episode, Mario's mouth is moving when Drainado talks.
