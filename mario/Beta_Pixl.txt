The '''Beta Pixl''' is one of the many beta elements of ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It, as the given name suggests, is a [[pixl]]. It looks like a ladder and was cut from the game for unknown reasons. Some people say this is an early version of [[Carrie]], when ''Super Paper Mario'' was still going to be on the [[Nintendo GameCube|GameCube]]. They think this due to the odd wing position of this pixl which is similar to that of Carrie's.
