'''Bowser Block Space''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Description==
Bowser Block Spaces are a type of [[space]] which exclusively appear in [[Bowser Party]] mode. If a character lands onto a Bowser Block Space, they either summon [[Bowser Jr.]] to add one [[Bowser Dice Block]] to [[Bowser]]'s collection or [[Nabbit]] to remove one from his collection, depending on whether the space has a +1 or -1 depicted on it.
