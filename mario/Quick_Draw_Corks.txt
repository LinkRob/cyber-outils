'''Quick Draw Corks''' is a Duel [[minigame]] found in [[Western Land]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
When the minigame starts, players have to wait until the [[Goomba]] says, "GO!" If a player acts before the right time, the Goomba gives that player a warning; if the player does it again, they lose. When the Goomba says "GO!" players have to quickly shoot the other player by pressing {{button|n64|A}}. Whichever player shoots the other player wins [[Coin]]s from the other player.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|A}} &ndash; Draw firearm
