The {{Nihongo|'''Power Moon'''|パワームーン|Pawā Mūn|Power Moon}}, also simply known as {{Nihongo|'''Moon'''|ムーン|Mūn|Moon}} is a main collectable found in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. Power Moons are the equivalent of [[Power Star]]s and [[Shine Sprite]]s from previous Mario games. There are more Moons in ''Super Mario Odyssey'' than Stars and Shines in previous games. Capping at a whopping 999 moons in total. Moons will also not force [[Mario]] to leave the level, unlike previous games.

When Mario collects a [[Grand Moon|Multi Moon]], it is worth as much as three normal Moons.

==Appearance==
Each Moon is in the shape of a crescent moon or star with eyes, similar to that of Power Stars and Shine Sprites. Their color depends on which Kingdom the Moon is found. Each Kingdom's set of Moons have a different color than those of other Kingdoms.

*[[Mushroom Kingdom]] - Yellow Power Star Moons
*[[Cap Kingdom]] - Yellow Moons
*[[Cascade Kingdom]] - Yellow Moons
*[[Sand Kingdom]] - Green Moons
*[[Lake Kingdom]] - Pink Moons
*[[Wooded Kingdom]] - Blue Moons
*[[Cloud Kingdom]] - Yellow Moons
*[[Lost Kingdom]] - Yellow Moons
*[[Metro Kingdom]] - Bronze Moons
*[[Snow Kingdom]] - Orange Moons
*[[Seaside Kingdom]] - Purple Moons
*[[Luncheon Kingdom]] - Cyan Moons
*[[Ruined Kingdom]] - Yellow Moons
*[[Bowser's Kingdom]] - Ruby Red Moons
*[[Moon Kingdom]] - Milky White Moons
*[[Dark Side]] - Yellow Moons
*[[Darker Side]] - Yellow Moons
