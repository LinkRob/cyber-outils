'''Grabbing Ghosts''', also named '''white grabbers''',<ref>Loe, Casey. Versus Books ''Luigi's Mansion'' Perfect Guide. Page 24.</ref> are enemies in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Grabbing Ghosts are relatively long lavender [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] with a horizontally-shaped body.

===Behavior===
Grabbing Ghosts slowly fly behind [[Luigi]], whom they try to choke by grabbing him, and Luigi cannot move while he is grabbed. A Grabbing Ghost has three variants—[[Cinema Ghost]]s, [[Mirror Ghosts]], and [[Red Grabbing Ghost]]s—all of which behave identically, but unlike standard Grabbing Ghosts, the three variations gradually damage Luigi while choking him. Grabbing Ghosts make constant gasping or wheezing noises while moving around.

[[Sneaker]]s from ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' and [[Slinker]]s from ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' behave similarly to Grabbing Ghosts.

==History==
If a Grabbing Ghost grabs Luigi, the player has to shake the Control Stick so that Luigi can throw off the Grabbing Ghost. A Grabbing Ghost has only ten [[Heart Point|HP]], and Luigi can defeat one by vacuuming away all of its HP using the [[Poltergust 3000]].

Grabbing Ghosts are first encountered in the [[Bathroom (1F)|Bathroom]]. After Luigi has vacuumed in both Grabbing Ghosts, the Bathroom lights turn on, and Luigi receives a call on his [[Game Boy Horror]] from [[Professor E. Gadd]], who provides him with advice on how to fight the Grabbing Ghosts. E. Gadd notes that Luigi struggled with the Grabbing Ghosts, regardless if Luigi had even been grabbed by one.
