'''Gold Ghosts''', also known as '''jabbers'''<ref>Loe, Casey. Versus Books ''Luigi's Mansion'' Perfect Guide. Page 13.</ref> or '''orange ghosts''',<ref>Bogenn, Tim. BradyGAMES ''Luigi's Mansion'' Official Strategy Guide. Page 18.</ref><ref>''Nintendo GameCube Collection: 4 Guides for the Price of 1'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide. Page 7.</ref> are a type of [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Gold Ghost, despite its name, has an orange body with yellow eyes and two small teeth on its upper jaw. Gold Ghosts are generally shown to be smiling.

===Behavior===
In ''Luigi's Mansion'', like other regular ghost enemies, Gold Ghosts travel after [[Luigi]]. If it gets close enough, the Gold Ghost tries to punch him. If the Gold Ghost is successful, it laughs before vanishing, but later reappears again. Gold Ghosts make various minor appearances in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' spinoffs, but act the same as the other ghosts that appear alongside them.

==History==
===''Luigi's Mansion''===
[[File:Gold Ghosts approaching LM.png|thumb|Three Gold Ghosts approaching Luigi and E. Gadd during their first meeting in ''Luigi's Mansion'']]
In ''Luigi's Mansion'' and its [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|remake]], Gold Ghost is the first type of ghost encountered by [[Luigi]] in [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Luigi's Mansion]]. Gold Ghosts are one of the most common ghosts found within the mansion. Gold Ghosts usually appear in groups of two or more, and they can be damaged from any element. A Gold Ghost has 10 [[Heart Point|HP]].

A Gold Ghost is first seen in the [[Foyer (Luigi's Mansion)|Foyer]] as a foggy cloud carrying a [[key]], which then passes through a door into the [[Parlor (Luigi's Mansion)|Parlor]]. When Luigi first enters the Parlor after unlocking it, he is ambushed by a Gold Ghost while nervously walking through the room, but he is saved by [[Professor E. Gadd]] when he uses the [[Poltergust 3000]] to try and vacuum up the Gold Ghost. However, the Gold Ghost ends up punching E. Gadd down, laughs, and then disappears. Shortly after E. Gadd introduces himself to Luigi, three Gold Ghosts appear together, prompting the two to quickly leave the room and to [[Professor E. Gadd's Lab]].

Gold Ghosts are the only enemies in the [[Training Room]], and while they travel after Luigi, these Gold Ghosts do not try to punch him, unlike the ones in the mansion. The intent of these Gold Ghosts is for Luigi to vacuum them up to practice using the Poltergust 3000, which E. Gadd gave to him.

In [[The Artist's Studio]], three Gold Ghosts are the first wave of ghosts that [[Vincent Van Gore]] brings to life via a Gold Ghost painting. In the [[Hidden Mansion]], Vincent Van Gore instead paints five of the Gold Ghosts to life.

Even though its one of the easiest Ghost to beat in the game, it relies on using sneak attacks to scare [[Luigi]] in order to attack him. As with most ghosts, to capture it, Luigi must first have to use his flashlight on it and use the [[Poltergust 3000]] to vacuum it up.

[[Greenie]]s, found in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'', and [[Goob]]s, found in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', are the equivalent of the Gold Ghosts.

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
[[File:Gold Ghost MLSS.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|its remake]], a Gold Ghost briefly appears in [[Starbeans Cafe]] after the player brews the first drink where it scares Luigi, only to be sucked up by Professor E. Gadd upon entering.

===''Mario Power Tennis''===
In ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', Gold Ghosts, as well as several other generic ghost species from ''Luigi's Mansion'', appear on the [[Luigi's Mansion Court]] and in the [[minigame]] [[Terror Tennis]].

===''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''===
[[File:MKAG2 GhostGold YoshiPark.png|thumb|The Gold Ghost drawing in ''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2'']]
In ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', Gold Ghost is one of the crayon-like sketches in the Horror House section of [[Yoshi Park 2]]. 

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
In ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'', Gold Ghosts are among the ghosts that appear in the [[Luigi's Mansion (baseball stadium)|Luigi's Mansion]] stadium.

===''Mario Party 8''===
In ''[[Mario Party 8]]'', a Gold Ghost is among the enemy species that can be uncovered by examining the furniture in [[Specter Inspector]].

===''Mario Sports Mix''===
A Gold Ghost makes an appearance in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'' on the Luigi's Mansion volleyball court, where it can assist either the player's team or the opposing team by either striking the ball over the net or blocking spikes from the team serving.

==Crossover appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
The Gold Ghost also appears as a part of a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]], alongside the [[Blue Twirler]] and the [[Purple Puncher]], in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''. In each one of them, the trophy can be unlocked at random.

===''Nintendo Land''===
In the ''[[Nintendo Land]]'' minigame [[Luigi's Ghost Mansion]], the Mii attacking the other four Mii players use a Gold Ghost costume.
