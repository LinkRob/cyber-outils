'''Pianta Pool''' is a Free-for-All mini-game which appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to gain the most points by grabbing the rings. Pink rings are worth three points. Gold rings are worth five points. Whoever has the most points by the end is the winner.

== Controls ==
Hold the {{button|Wii|Wiimote}} sideways
* {{button|Wii|Wiimote}} - Move
