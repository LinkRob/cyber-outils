'''Fire Snake Cavern''', or '''Layer-Cake Desert-3''', is the third course of [[Layer-Cake Desert]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is a dark cave featuring the first appearance of [[Glowing Baby Yoshi]]. The level is unlocked by completing [[Stone-Eye Zone]], and completion of this level unlocks [[Stoneslide Tower]]. 
==Layout==
The level begins above ground, where a [[? Block]] containing a power-up is found, as well as a [[Warp Pipe]] leading to a dark underground area. Metallic  platforms that have lanterns at both ends and tilt frequently are found, as well as a [[Fire Snake]]. A portion of ground with Warp Pipes containing [[Venus Fire Trap]]s follows, with a ? Block containing a [[Glowing Baby Yoshi]]. Two [[Fire Bro]]s are found among a group of metallic tilting platforms. The top left one has a [[Brick Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. A passage containing [[coin]]s is found behind the Fire Bro at the bottom. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is then found. A Warp Pipe leading to an area with [[Piranha Plant]]s and coins is located beneath the Checkpoint Flag. In the center is a [[Hidden Block]] with a 1-Up Mushroom. Another ? Block containing a Glowing Baby Yoshi is found, along with more tilting platforms with a Fire Snake and a [[Koopa Troopa]] on them. A [[Red Ring]] is then found, along with two Fire Snakes. More tilting platforms are found, as well as two Fire Bros. A Warp Pipe leads above ground to an area with tilting platforms and the [[Goal Pole]].

The player can skip the underground sections by using the [[Flying Squirrel Mario|Flying Squirrel Suit]] to fly over the wall at the beginning of the level. In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', [[Peachette]] can be used as well.
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': At the beginning, there are two metallic platforms on top of each other. The player  must jump from the higher one when the right side is up to reach a ledge. Then he must run through the path to get the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''':  After the Checkpoint Flag, there are metallic platforms with bricks in between. The player must break the bricks in the middle to reach a pipe below and enter it. Next, the player must avoid the Fire Snakes and press the [[P Switch]], then run right and jump onto the ledge to grab the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At the end, the player encounters two Fire Bros on standing metallic platforms. The player must jump to the highest platform, where there is a pipe. Inside the pipe is the third Star Coin.
