'''Tart Top''' is one of the four new [[Battle Stage]]s in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. The course itself is very much related to [[Cookie Land]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''; the theme is pastries and candy (such as heart-shaped jellies and cream puffs).

In the center of the stage, there is a giant cream mountain. The only [[Item Box]]es are in the air, making it where they are only accessible after going off a [[Dash Panel]] that goes up the cream mountain. One obstacle in this course is related to the [[Blooper]], but not as powerful. When hitting a cream puff, icing will splat on the screen.
