== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
An '''AirMeow''' is a robot created by [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]].<ref group="note" name="Tippi">Tippi thinks Francis bought robots on the internet and customized them himself.</ref> It resembles a cat that has wings. The AirMeow runs on volatile mixture of catnip and cooking oil.<ref name="SPM Catch Card"/>

In gameplay, the AirMeow is found at Fort Francis. It patrols in the skies.<ref name="SPM Catch Card">{{Cite video game|title=[[Super Paper Mario]] |developer=[[Intelligent Systems]], Nintendo SPD Group No.3|publisher=[[Nintendo]]|date=2007-04-09|quotesource=''AirMeow's'' Catch Card description|quote=Max HP: 5, Attack: 2, Defense: 1. This AirMeow guards the skies over Fort Francis. It runs on a volatile mixture of catnip and cooking oil.}}</ref> The AirMeow attacks the player when it sees him/her. The AirMeow will dive towards the player. However, the player can stomp the AirMeow. Additionally, [[Pixl]]s can defeat the enemy.<ref group="note" name="switch">One of the AirMeows are mandatory to press the blue [[! Switch]]. [[Thoreau]] must be present for the player to pick up the AirMeow.</ref>

=== Catch Card ===
{{Main|Catch Card}}
The player can summon the [[Catch Card]]/[[Catch Card SP|SP]] to defeat the '''AirMeow'''; the AirMeow becomes a [[Caught Card]]. Additionally, the player can buy the AirMeow's Catch Card in the [[card shop]].
== History ==
[[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]] created cat robots to guard Fort Francis; Francis created the '''AirMeow'''. The AirMeow patrols the skies and attacks intruders.

== Etymology ==
''AirMeow'' derives from ''air'' and ''meow''. ''Air'' is an invisible mixture of gases around the person that he/she needs to breathe. ''Meow'' is also known as ''miaow''. ''Miaow'' is the characteristic crying sound of a cat. Its Japanese name, ''Furainyan'' translates to ''FlyMeow''. ''FlyMeow'' derives from ''fly'' and ''meow''. ''Fly'' means to travel through the air. This means AirMeows travel through air, they also have the cat's characteristic crying sound.
