'''World 5-Tower''' is the tower level of [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]].''

== Layout ==
This tower appears to be a combination of [[Ludwig's Castle]] from ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and [[World 4-Fortress (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 4-Tower]] from ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' in terms of layout, as this tower features [[Fence]]s like both forts, though includes rising lava and even begins similarly to the latter, while this fort includes [[Climbing Koopa]]s and the last segment is similar to the former.

In this level, Mario must climb the Fences and avoid touching the rising lava, whilst avoiding enemies like Climbing Koopas and [[Dry Bones]], and obstacles like [[Burner]]s and spikes. There are also [[Jump Block|Note Blocks]] which Mario can use to progress further into the level. At the end, Mario must face four [[Reznor]] to beat the level.

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': After the fence rotates for the first time at a 90-degree angle, the first Star Coin is located to the right, above a fence with a [[Climbing Koopa]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': In the area after the checkpoint flag, when the moving fences starts to split, the second Star Coin is to the right.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Immediately after the second Star Coin, the third Star Coin is on a [[Burner]] on the left side of the area.
