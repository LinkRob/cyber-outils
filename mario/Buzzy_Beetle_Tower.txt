'''Buzzy Beetle Towers''' are an enemy with a few appearances in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', a Buzzy Beetle Tower consists of a stack of [[Buzzy Beetle]]s. In ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'', it instead consists of a [[Big Buzzy Beetle]], a regular Buzzy Beetle, a [[Small Buzzy Beetle]], and also a green [[Spike Guy]] ironically.

===Behavior===
In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'', its attack involves the Buzzy Beetles spinning as the Spike Guy runs on top, then the Big Buzzy Beetle launches itself into Mario. A Spike Guy can fall off the stack from [[Mario]]'s [[hammer]] attack, upon which it attacks by landing on Mario headfirst before climbing back up the tower. If a [[KO Hammer]] is used, the tower spins in place, and the Spike Guy strikes a pose as the audience cheers. The [[POW Block]] causes the tower to collapse, flipping the Buzzy Beetles onto their back as the Spike Guy [[flee]]s from battle. From this point, the Buzzy Beetles act as regular enemies.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
In ''Super Mario Maker'' and its follow-ups, Buzzy Beetle Towers can be created by stacking Buzzy Beetles onto one another.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
A single Buzzy Beetle Tower is encountered in [[The Emerald Circus]]. During the battle, if Mario defeats the Spike Guy, a different one drops from a trapeze at the start of the next turn to take its place. The Buzzy Beetle Tower is unaffected by Mario's [[jump]] attack, but Mario's hammer attack can damage the Buzzy Beetles take damage, although not the Spike Guy. The Buzzy Beetle Tower is vulnerable to [[Ice Flower]]s, [[Eekhammer]]s, [[Hurlhammer]]s, and [[Things]].
