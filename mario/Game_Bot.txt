'''Game Bots''' are arcade machine robots who run the [[Mini-Game Shop]] in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. There are three Game Bots, and they are colored red, blue, and yellow respectively. Each Game Bot hosts a different [[minigame]] centered around [[Wario]]; the blue one hosts [[Wario's Homerun Derby]], the red one hosts [[The Wario Hop]], and the yellow one hosts [[Wario's Roulette]]. It usually costs 5,000 [[point]]s to play one of the minigames, but it is lowered to 2,000 once Wario completes the [[Entry Passage]]. If Wario reaches at least a certain number of points in one of the minigames, he earns [[Medal (Wario Land 4)|medals]].
