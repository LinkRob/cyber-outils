'''World 2''' is the second world of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. 

== Description ==
This world is also a grassland, primarily inhabited by [[Little Goomba]]s, [[Piranha Plant]]s, and [[Koopa Troopa]]s. Other than the castle, it consists of an overworld course, an underwater course, and a bridge course.

On the map in ''Deluxe'', the world features fewer trees (seen on the far east side) and more water than [[World 1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1]]. 2-1 is located on the northwest corner. The player is then lead east to 2-2, following a long, straight path. A [[pipe]] sits here, and it leads the player to another pipe towards the southwest. West of the pipe is 2-3, which is located on a small island that connects to the two others via two bridges. The castle lies just ahead.
