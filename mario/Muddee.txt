'''Muddee''' is a large salamander (also described as a beaver) boss encountered by [[Wario]] in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. Its name is a pun on "muddy."

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Muddee is a brown salamander-like creature described as a beaver. It has a spike on its tail.

== In ''Wario Land 3'' ==
[[File:Muddee.png|frame|left|Boss fight]]
Muddee is the fifth boss, and Wario encounters it in the [[The Stagnant Swamp]], where it scares the [[Turtle (Wario Land series)|turtle]] away after [[Wario]] uses it to get past the [[current]]s. Muddee's main attack is trying to attack Wario with his spiky tail. Wario must perform a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] on Muddee's body. Upon taking enough hits, Muddee falls down and the turtle returns, allowing Wario to progress and open the red chest.
