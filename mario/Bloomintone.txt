'''Bloomintone''' is a [[Fractone]] that appears in [[Donkey Kong Bananza]].

== Appearance ==
Bloomintone resembles a wooden pot with a small blue tinted flower bud inside.

Once bloomed, it is revealed that the flower is another Fractone entirely that lives on top of Bloomintone.  

== Behavior ==
Once [[Donkey Kong|DK]] puts Bloomintone into light, Bloomintone's flower will bloom. Afterwards, they will give DK a [[Banandium Gem|banana]]. 

== Trivia ==

* Bloomintone is one of two Fractones that is two in one, the other being [[Collectone]].
