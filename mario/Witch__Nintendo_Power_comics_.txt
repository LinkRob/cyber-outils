The '''Witch''' is a character in ''[[Mario vs. Wario]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Her appearance is the same as her Nintendo [[Witch (boss)|counterpart]].

=== Personality traits ===
While determined to defeat Mario, she expressed frustration when she cannot defeat him.

=== Powers and abilities ===
==== Powers ====
* '''Pyrokinesis'''
** '''Fireballs''': Witch can shoot fireballs.
* '''Leaping Lids''': She can control cauldrons to make the lids leap.

== Biography ==
[[File:Witch in Mario vs Wario.jpg|thumb|250px|The Witch's appearance in ''Mario vs. Wario'']]
The Witch is the third boss fought by [[Mario (Nintendo Power comics)|Mario]]. At first, she shoot fireballs at Mario, who trips over a small rock. As a result, the fireball ends up hitting [[Big Bird]] in the nearby bush. The Witch becomes angry, stating that she needs to work on her aim. The Witch then boils her cauldron to launch the lids at Mario, but as he jumps, both of Mario's shoes each become stuck within one of the handles. The lids somehow enable Mario to walk across the water, and he thanks the Witch for involuntarily helping him as she yells, asking Mario how he managed to do it.
