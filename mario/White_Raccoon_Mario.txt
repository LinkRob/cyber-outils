'''White Raccoon Mario''' is the more powerful version of [[Raccoon Mario]]. The form made its first appearance in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'' ==
[[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can transform into their White Racoon form by obtaining a [[Invincibility Leaf]].

The White Raccoon form grants permanent invincibility to the player. The [[P-Meter]] charges fast. The player in this form can run on water.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' ==
White Raccoon Mario made an appearance as a [[Battle Card]].
