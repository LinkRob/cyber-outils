'''Money Money''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
[[File:Money money badge.png|thumb|left]]

In ''Paper Mario'', Money Money is found at [[Shooting Star Summit]]. It is one of the many badges that [[Merlow]], a badge proprietor, sells for twenty [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]]. While equipped, Money Money gives [[Mario]] twice the amount of [[coin]]s upon winning a battle. With Money Money, Mario obtains about 5 to 15 coins each time on average. Money Money requires 7 [[Badge Point|BP]] to be equipped, and it can be useful if the player wants to earn more coins from fights.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', Money Money can be obtained an infinite number of times. Money Money is found inside of the [[Pianta Parlor]]. Mario, can purchase this badge for a large sum of 234 [[Pianta]]s. There is also a very slight chance that this badge can be dropped from [[Dark Boo]]s which are found in [[Poshley Sanctum]] and in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. Equipping multiple Money Money badges will cause Mario to gain even more coins after battle. This caps out at 64 coins in the original [[Nintendo GameCube]] version, and caps at 99 coins in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|Nintendo Switch remake]].
