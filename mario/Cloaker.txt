'''Cloaker''' is a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He is a shield and sword wielding jester and a member of the [[Smithy Gang]]. He is fought alongside [[Domino]] in [[Smithy Factory|The Factory]].

==Battle==
In battle, Cloaker mainly uses contact moves and doesn't possess any special attacks unlike his partner Domino. If Cloaker is defeated first, then Domino will hop onto his snake [[Mad Adder]]. However, if Domino is defeated first, Cloaker will hop on his snake [[Earth Link]].

===Strategy===
Cloaker has high attack and defense but low magic defense while Domino lacks in defense all together. It is recommend to take down Cloaker since Domino's snake, Mad Adder, has much less HP (Earth Link has 2500 HP but Mad Adder only has 1500 HP).
