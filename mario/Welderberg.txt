'''Welderberg''' is an odd hairy creature traveling between [[Flipside]] and [[Flopside]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. 



==History==
He seems to be able to flip dimensions, and make gateways through them, and seems to be skilled at building [[Warp Pipe]]s in little time. Welderberg can make a pipe from Flopside to Flipside for 300 [[coin]]s, a pipe from Flopside Tower to the [[Pit of 100 Trials#Flopside|Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]] for 100 coins, and a pipe from [[Flipside Tower]] to the [[Pit of 100 Trials#Flipside|Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]] for 100 coins.
