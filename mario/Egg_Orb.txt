The '''Egg Orb''' is an [[orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
The Egg Orb can only be used by [[Yoshi]] and [[Birdo]]. When the player takes a turn, the player reverts any [[Character Space]]s crossed back to their original spaces by turning them into [[Yoshi's Egg]]s (for Yoshi) or [[Birdo's Egg]]s (for Birdo). When the turn is done, the eggs are turned into orbs that Yoshi and Birdo can use. The Egg Orb can also eliminate [[Koopa Kid Space]]s. CPU players will never use it.
