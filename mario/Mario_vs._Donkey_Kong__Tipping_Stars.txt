'''''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars''''' is a game for the [[Wii U]] and [[Nintendo 3DS]] and an installment of the {{series|Mario vs. Donkey Kong}}. It is the same game as ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!]]'', except it allows levels to be rated. The game was released in 2015.

== Story ==
[[Pauline]] is once again kidnapped by [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], and [[Mario]] chases after Donkey Kong with his [[Mini Mario]] toys to go on a journey to rescue her.
