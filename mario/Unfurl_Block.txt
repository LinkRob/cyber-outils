An Unfurl Block is an item that appears in Paper Mario: Color Splash.
==History==
If Mario hits an Unfurl Block, an orb of silver light will come out and turn Mario a shiny silver color. Mario then has 20 seconds to find an object with an Unfurl mark (which is sometimes hidden) and hit it with his hammer. Those objects will usually make a path for Mario to access new areas.
