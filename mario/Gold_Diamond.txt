'''Gold Diamonds''' are items in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
Gold Diamonds are worth 20,000,000G each, a higher value than any other [[treasure]] in ''Luigi's Mansion''. They are about twice as large as [[Blue Diamond]]s and [[Red Diamond]]s.

==History==
Gold Diamonds are found in two spots. For the first one, [[Luigi]] must water a plant three times in the [[Boneyard]]: once when he first sees it, once after defeating [[Bogmire]], and once after defeating [[Boolossus]] to make the now-flower bloom, revealing the diamond and several other treasures. The other can be found if Luigi captures all [[List of Boos in Luigi's Mansion|50 Boos]] (not counting [[King Boo]]); the last Boo drops the Gold Diamond.

If Luigi captures the final Boo and saves, the Gold Diamond's location is saved as being on the ground. Therefore, if players do not save using a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] after obtaining the Gold Diamond, it will be lost if the game is turned off or Luigi dies, since as the game is saved automatically after the final Boo is captured but before the Gold Diamond is obtained, the Gold Diamond will disappear and cannot be obtained again.
