'''Luigi''' is a plumber who is the deuteragonist in the ''[[Super Mario Adventures]]'' comics published in ''[[Nintendo Power]]''. Luigi is based off the [[Nintendo]] [[Luigi|character]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Luigi strongly resembles his [[Luigi|video game counterpart]]. He wears a long-sleeved green shirt with blue overalls and a green "L" hat.

== Biography ==
Mario and Luigi lived in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] with their occupation as plumbers. As the brothers entered [[Princess Peach's Castle]], a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] informed them about the unconnected pipes leaking. He also informed that [[Princess Toadstool (Nintendo Power comics)|Princess Toadstool]] was "expecting plumbing by tonight". Mario and Luigi connected the pipes when fixing but Mario found a [[Warp Pipe|green pipe]] that had a [[Jumping Piranha Plant]] jump out of the pipe. This followed up with more pipes summoned from the ground and Mario dragged Luigi to a garden full of pipes. Various creatures came out of the pipes surrounding the Mario Bros. They fought the creatures until [[Bowser (Nintendo Power comics)|Bowser]] came out of a pipe with his [[Koopa Clown Car]].<ref>''Super Mario Adventures Issue 32''. Retrieved 2023-04-16.</ref>
