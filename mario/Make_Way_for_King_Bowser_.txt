'''Make Way for King Bowser!''' is the final level of the [[World 6 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 6]] and the game itself. 
==Layout==
[[Yoshi (species)|Green Yoshi]] begins the level from a part on the lava, near [[Bowser]]. He routinely commands [[Metal Guy]]s that Yoshi has to eat and dash at Bowser. Duplicate this task three times will win against Bowser, but immediately, [[Kamek]] become visible, spread Bowser to mega size. The player needs then strive against [[Giant Bowser]] in a battle same to the one with Big Baby Bowser from [[Bowser's Castle Break-In|the previous level]], except it is more tough. Once Yoshi directs [[Mega Eggdozer]]s to his mouth three times, he will finally be conquer. Yoshi then can be met with [[Baby Luigi]] by walking to the right, and the credits rolls.
