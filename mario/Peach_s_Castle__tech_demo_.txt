'''''Peach's Castle''''' is a [[tech demo]] for the [[Nintendo GameCube]]. It was never released as a game but rather was included within the Nintendo GameCube Software Development Kit to showcase the system's graphical capabilities.

The player takes control of a red arrow in [[Princess Peach's Castle|Princess Peach’s Castle]] (as it is spelled in-game). The red arrow can travel to various rooms, each showcasing a different graphical effect, such as anti-aliasing.
