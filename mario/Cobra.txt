'''Cobras''' are snakes and an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are large, pink cobra snakes.

===Behavior===
Cobras inhabit small, red jars. If [[Wario]] approaches a Cobra, it emerges from its jar. A [[snake charmer]] can be found sitting atop the Cobra. If the Cobra has been punched a few times, it charges up and then quickly lashes at Wario.

==In ''Wario World''==
Cobras are only encountered in [[Wonky Circus]]. They are one of two enemies that a snake charmer is seen riding, the other one being an [[Elephant]]. Wario can stun a Cobra by hitting it six times. If Wario picks up a Cobra, the snake charmer on top falls off.
