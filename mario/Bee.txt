'''Bees''' are real-world insects which have made appearances in the {{franchise|Mario}} and its spinoffs. Bees make their first ''Mario''-related appearance in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Bees are often located around their [[beehive]]s. They are generally peaceful if undisturbed, but if someone gets in the way of the bees, they try to attack that character.

==In ''Yoshi's Story''==
Bees appear as enemies, and they fly around their beehive in swarms, forming a large hand shape to prevent a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] from passing them. If a Yoshi walks slowly, the Bees do not bother him, but if Yoshi throws an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at their beehive, the bees comes to attack him.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''[[Mario Party 2]]''===
In the minigame [[Honeycomb Havoc]], whenever a character ends receiving a beehive, a group of bees come out to attack them, eliminating that character from the minigame.

===''[[Mario Party 7]]''===
Bees appear in the minigame [[Big Dripper]], where the characters must duck down to avoid the swarm while collecting their dripping honey. In addition, the minigame [[Buzzstormer]] involves the characters piloting bee-like aircraft.

===''[[Mario Party DS]]''===
At [[DK's Stone Statue]], there is an [[Event Space]] where bees emerge from their hive with [[coin]]s that can be collected.

During the minigame [[Hedge Honcho]], the characters encounter a few bees along their path as they push away the leaves. Unlike with [[ladybug]]s, a character must avoid touching a bee, otherwise it stings the character, briefly stunning them. Instead, the character has to wait for the bee to fly off before they can continue pushing away the leaves.

===''[[Mario Party 10]]''===
[[File:Beeline Shrine.png|thumb|Bees in ''Mario Party 10'']]
Bees appear in the minigame [[Beeline Shrine]], where they serve as the main obstacles.
{{sectstub}}

===''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''===
Bees return in the ''Mario Party 2'' minigame Honeycomb Havoc, acting identically as in the minigame's original iteration.

==In ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' ==
Bees are found in [[Beehive]]s, and if [[Mario]] was to get too close to them, they would swarm around him and possibly sting him, causing Mario to lose health. Although dangerous to Mario alone, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can eat them and get a [[coin]] out of them. Once eating all of them, a [[Blue Coin]] will appear.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''===
[[File:Bee M&LBIS sprite.png|frame|left]]
Bees are encountered as enemies exclusively amongst [[Beehoss]], which are enemies themselves. A Beehoss can summon Bees from its honeycomb during battle.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''===
Bees return as enemies alongside Beehoss. A difference is that if any bee is counterattacked and defeated on the Beehoss's turn, the remaining bees return to attack either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], depending on who counterattacked them, and this attack cannot be avoided.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''===
Bees now use their design from ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''. They retain their role in the main game as well as the ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' campaign, which also features them as obstacles.
