The '''Standard MR''' is [[Mario]]'s standard kart in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. This is one of three karts Mario has in the game, the others being the [[B Dasher]] and the [[Shooting Star]]. 

== Description ==
[[File:StandardKartMR.png|thumb|150px|Data-rendered model of the Standard MR.]]
It is an all-around kart with average stats on [[Speed]], [[Acceleration]], [[Weight]], [[Handling]], [[Drift]], and & [[Items (stat)|Items]] stat. Oddly enough, if you wait long enough on the Mario Kart DS title screen when it shows a race, Mario will always be racing with this kart. He is pictured with this kart on the ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' title screen.

It shares its speed with the B Dasher, [[Luigi]]'s [[Standard LG]], and [[Streamliner]], as well as [[Waluigi]]'s [[Standard WL]]. It shares its acceleration with the B Dasher, Luigi's Streamliner, [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Royale]], and [[Standard PC]], as well as [[Princess Daisy]]'s [[Light Dancer]]. Its weight is equivalent to Luigi's Standard LG, Princess Peach's Royale, [[Donkey Kong]]'s [[Wildlife]], [[Wario]]'s [[Brute]] and Waluigi's [[Gold Mantis]]. Its handling is shared with Waluigi's Standard WL, and its drift is shared with the Shooting Star and Waluigi's Standard WL.

It is colored red. The [[Standard Kart M]] replaces this kart in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', which is also red.

Once all cups in Mirror Class have been completed, all characters can use this kart.
