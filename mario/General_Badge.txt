The '''General Badge''' is one of the [[badge]]s featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the General Badge can be bought from the clothes shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 350 coins. Equipping it increases [[Power|POW]] by 45 and [[Bros. Point|BP]]s by 15. In the Japanese version, the General Badge is sold at a much higher price of 900 coins. It instead increases POW at a higher number of 55, although its BP was lessened to 12.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the General Badge is instead obtained from [[Bowser's Castle]]. It can be sold at a low value of three coins. The General Badge now increases POW at 64 and BP by 10, and it was given an additional special effect, the Lucky Speedster.
