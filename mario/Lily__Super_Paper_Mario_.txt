==Appearance==
Lily is a very young [[Flip-Flopian]] that is blue in color.

==History==
Lily can be found in [[Flopside]] where she lives with her father, [[Bleu]]. There are hints that Lily's parents are divorced and she has been left in the care of her father. It's stated by [[Tippi]] (or [[Tiptron]]) that Lily hopes to become an actress one day and even spends nights practicing.

==Trivia==
*Her [[Flipside]] counterpart is [[Ellie (Super Paper Mario)|Ellie]].
