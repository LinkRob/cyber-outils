'''Gamer''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It originates from the game [[Game & Wario]].

==Origin==
Gamer is based on the minigame of the same name from ''[[Game & Wario]]''. The player controls [[9-Volt]] and must play microgames past bedtime and avoid his mother, [[5-Volt]]. The player uses the Wii U Gamepad to play the microgames while they must also pay attention to the top screen to avoid being caught by 5-Volt.
