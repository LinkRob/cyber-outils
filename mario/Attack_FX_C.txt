'''Attack FX C''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==

Attack FX C costs 0 [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear. This badge, like all other attack FX badges, makes a sound when Mario attacks and has a BP cost of 0. Attack FX C is hidden in an invisible [[Question Block]] found in southern [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]]. By moving directly south of where [[Mario]] enters the desert via [[Mt. Rugged]], Mario can find many different sections of the desert. At the south most area, Mario will encounter a lone rock, and jumping on this rock and jumping subsequently while one it will hit an invisible Question Block with this badge inside.
