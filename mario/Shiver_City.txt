'''Shiver City''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is featured in "Chapter 7: A Star Spirit On Ice".

==In ''Paper Mario''==
When Mario arrives in Shiver City, he is prohibited from leaving the city due east, due to a new regulation put up by the mayor, being that no-one can leave without the mayor's permission, due to rumors of a [[Monstar|monster]] and a [[Jr. Troopa|troublemaker]]. When Mario goes to get permission, he discovers the Mayor is unconscious. However, [[Mayor Penguin]] is mistaken for being dead, and Mario is seen as the killer. Mario then locates [[Herringway]] (prompted by a clue from the "dead" mayor) who clears his name (as Mayor Penguin was simply knocked out when trying to retrieve a present for Herringway). Mario later returns to Shiver City so that the mayor can provide him with a [[Scarf]] which helps him open [[Crystal Palace]].

If Mario stays in the [[Toad House]] after opening Crystal Palace, the snowmen who he gave a hat and scarf to will leave him four [[Mushroom]]s and an [[Ultra Shroom]]. Also, one of the penguins will comment on whichever award Mario has received from [[The Master]] in [[The Dojo]], and also, The [[Attack FX E]] Badge is over there in the balcony.

==Trivia==
*It should be noted that during the investigative time when Mario is the prime suspect of Mayor Penguin's "murder," Mario cannot leave Shiver City nor he can purchase any items from the Item Shop. This marks the second time in the game where Mario is banned from purchasing items with the first time being at [[Boo's Shop]] in [[Boo's Mansion]] due to orders from [[Lady Bow]].
