'''Kent C. Koopa''' is a large [[Koopa Troopa]] who wears a [[Red Shell]] and an optional boss in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Kent makes an appearance after the completion of Chapter 5. He is found blocking the way to [[Pleasant Path]], which isand is a necessary to get to [[Koopa Village]], the former hometown of Kent C. Koopa. [[Mario]] has to either pay 100 [[coin]]s to pass or defeat him in battle to be able to access the village or Toad Town, as Kent charges characters coming from either direction. After the completion of chapter 7, [[Kolorado]], who stays in Toad Town because of Kent C. Koopa, makes his way to Koopa Village, and is assumed to have summed up the 100 coins to pay the toll.

==Battle==
Kent is one of the most difficult bosses in the game. He has an astonishing seventy [[Heart Point|HP]] and one of the highest [[Defense]] values out of any other foe fought. He has a small weakness of getting flipped onto his shell like a regular Koopa, but it takes multiple hits to his tail for that to happen. The battle with Kent is a long, tedious, and an annoying one, but is completely optional.

===Strategy===
Even with his high stats, Kent C. Koopa has a major weakness that will make the battle with him extremely easy: [[Sleep]]. That means using either the [[Lullaby]] move, a [[Sleepy Sheep]], or the [[Sleep Stomp]] every turn keeps Kent asleep for the remainder of the battle. Having [[Watt]] at full power is also extremely helpful, as her move can pierce defenses.
