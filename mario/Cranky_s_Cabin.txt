'''Cranky's Cabin''' is a location in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==Description==
It is where [[Cranky Kong]] can be found, and both [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] can some here to hear him ramble and provide tips in between.
