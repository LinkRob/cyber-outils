'''The Anglefish Trial: Ready, Aim, Fly!''' is a course of [[Shining Falls]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level features many [[anglefish]]. Water is at the bottom of the level at all times<ref group="note">Water is not present at the bottom of the screen during the Wonder Effect.</ref>, where the anglefish reside. 
[[de:Rochella-Test: In Visier geraten]]
[[Category:Levels]]
[[Category:Levels in Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]
Eventually there is a [[Wonder Flower]] in the air just out of reach. The player must reach the Wonder Flower by jumping on anglefish jumping out of the water. The [[Wonder Effect]] will spawn long [[Cloud Block|Cloud Blocks]]. Anglefish will come from the top and sides of the screen. The player is required to jump on these to reach the top, where the [[Wonder Seed]] is located. [[Fireworks]] will pop when the player reaches the top.

== [[10-Flower Coin]] locations ==

# A dry [[Wow Bud]] will spawn a [[vine]] when watered. The vine will allow the player to reach a platform with a 10-flower coin.
# After entering a [[pipe]] in a [[semisolid]], there is a secret area with a [[handle]] that will spawn a 10-flower coin.
# There is a pipe floating in the air with [[Hard Block|Hard Blocks]] blocking the enterance. Breaking the blocks with the shell of a [[Big Swirlypod]] will allow the player to enter a secret area. In this area, throwing a shell at the left Wow Bud will drop a 10-flower coin.
