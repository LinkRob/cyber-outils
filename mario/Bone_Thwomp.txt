A '''Bone Thwomp''' is a species in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are bones bearing a [[Thwomp]]-like face. They appear in either a square or a vertical rectangular shape.

===Behavior===
Bone Thwomps block [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s path, and serve as more of an obstacle than an enemy. However, the brothers can often use Bone Thwomps as platforms to reach certain higher areas.

===Habitats===
Bone Thwomps are only found in the [[Pump Works]] and [[Airway (Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story)|Airway]] of [[Bowser's body]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
At [[Plack Beach]], if [[Bowser]] drinks water from the [[Sea Pipe Statue]], the Bone Thwomps in the Pump Works float upward, allowing Mario and Luigi to progress further. The Bone Thwomps in the Pump Works are all shaped like a vertical rectangle.

Later on, square-shaped Bone Thwomps can be found in the Airway.
