'''Peewee Piranha''' is a boss in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Peewee Piranha resembles a more toddler version of [[Dino Piranha]]. He has green lips, a purple head and green petals. He has a his egg on his back with yellowish-brown spots. His exposed back is red with a small tail. Like with Dino Piranha, he turns red when angered.

== In ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ==
[[File:Pewee Piranha battle.png|thumb|left|The boss fight against Peewee Piranha]]
Peewee Piranha is the first boss fought by [[Mario]]. He is encountered in the [[Sky Station Galaxy]], on the Geo planet, where he is fought during the [[mission]] [[Peewee Piranha's Temper Tantrum]].

After making his way to the Geo Planet where the boss makes his home, [[Mario]] inadvertently breaks Peewee Piranha's egg. This angers Peewee and causes him to break out of the egg. Peewee Piranha chases Mario and jumps at him with his mouth. Mario must use his [[Star Spin]] to strike the back of the boss three times in to expose his tail. After Mario damages him there again, Peewee Piranha will have an even larger tantrum, turning red with rage.

During the second phase of the boss fight, Peewee will chase Mario more aggressively, with longer and more numerous strides before resting for a few seconds. When Peewee Piranha is hit for the fifth time, he will run away faster than Mario can run. Peewee Piranha must be intercepted, where he will stop for a quick moment to turn around, so the final hit can be dealt on his "tail". After this Peewee will collapse without any petals on his head, which turns blue to show Mario defeated him, and he will finally explode to reveal the [[Power Star]].

Peewee is fought again during the [[Speedy Comet]] mission, [[Peewee Piranha's Speed Run]]. Peewee utilizes the same strategies, and since the mission has short time, [[clock]]s appear around the planet for Mario to replenish the timer as he fights Peewee Piranha.

==Trivia==
*Like [[Dino Piranha]] from the first Super Mario Galaxy, Peewee Piranha resembles [[Petey Piranha]], with one particular feature that he doesn't have (in his case, the eggshell) that must be attacked. Also, all three are the first boss of their respective games and two of the three are first encountered enclosed in an egg. They are also battled as the first boss of their games and two of them once again under Speedy Comet conditions. They are also both in the first levels of the first galaxy in their games (not counting [[Gateway Galaxy]]).
*The non-English names suggest that Peewee Piranha is seemingly the child of Dino Piranha. Additionally, some fans claim that Peewee Piranha is Dino Piranha's cousin.
*Peewee's name derives from the word "pee-wee", which is a word that can be used to describe something small or immature, which Peewee Piranha obviously is, and may have also derived from Petey Piranha.
*Every time Peewee Piranha generally does anything, a squeaking noise can be heard.
*Peewee Piranha shares his boss theme with [[Sorbetti]], [[Rollodillo]], and [[Prince Pikante]]. This implies that that may be the main boss theme for the game.
*Peewee Piranha's boss theme was also placeholder music for [[Boom Boom]]'s boss battle in the ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' demo.
*Peewee Piranha's similarity to [[Dino Piranha]] is somewhat acknowledged in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'', in which Dino Piranha appears on Peewee Piranha's planet during [[Dream Spacewalk]].
