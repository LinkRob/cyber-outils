==Glossary==
*Own, owns, owned, owning, ownage = To be better than someone. The O can also be replaced by a P (e.g. ownage → pwnage)
*Leet, Leeter, Leetest = Cool; awesome; elite
*ROFL = "Rolling on (the) floor, laughing"
*WOOT = Exclamation of excitement, similar to "Yeah!", or "Hi!"
*N00B = Someone who is not only bad at a game or concept, but also annoying and disliked; this is a common leet-speak insult. Derived from the term newbie.
