A '''Control Coin''' are a type of [[coin]] and an object which first appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. A Control Coin is uncommon, and upon being released from its [[Question Block|? Block]], a Control Coin can have its movement controlled by the player to create a trail of [[coin]]s, which [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can then collect. This ends once the the Control Coin touches a wall or an object. Aside from returning in the [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]'', Control Coins also appear in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', within two [[World-e]] levels, [[Doors o' Plenty]] and [[Vexing Doors]].
