'''Podoboos''',<ref name=manual>''Super Mario Bros.'' English instruction booklet. Page 14.</ref><ref>''Nintendo Power'' Volume 13. Page 9.</ref><ref>Hodgson, David. ''Mario Kart Wii'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 34.</ref> also called '''Lava Bubbles''',<ref>M. Arakawa. Nintendo ''Mario Mania'' Player's Guide. Page 52.</ref><ref name=SMW2>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 127.</ref> '''flames''',<ref>Walsh, Doug. BradyGAMES (2001). ''Mario Kart: Super Circuit'' Official Racing Guide. Page 35.</ref> or simply '''Bubbles''',<ref>Bubble Capsule, ''Mario Party 5''</ref> are living [[fireball]]s that emerge from [[lava]]. Podoboos dwell within lava and have a frequent pattern of jumping in and out of it. They are commonly depicted as a large fireball with two black eyes or also as only a large fireball. Podoboos mainly appear in the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] platformers, but occasionally appear in several other series of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], such as the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]], the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]], and the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. In the main series, Podoboos can be destroyed by using a [[Star]], a [[Gold Flower]], a [[Hammer Suit]], a [[Penguin Suit]], or an [[Ice Flower]].

Podoboos initially just had the appearance of a fireball, but in their more recent appearances, Podoboos resemble a roundish jellyfish and do a flip before landing back in the lava.

Related species to Podoboos include [[Fire Snake]]s, [[Hot Foot]]s, [[Lava Bubble (Paper Mario)|Lava Bubbles]], and [[Li'l Cinder]]s.

==Main appearances==
===''Super Mario Bros.''===
[[Image:Podoboo SMB Booklet.png|thumb|left|Artwork of a Podoboo from the ''Super Mario Bros.'' instruction booklet]]

Podoboos first appeared in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', as fireballs that would jump out of the lava then descend back in the lava in simple patterns in an attempt to burn Mario whenever the plumber tried to jump over them. In this game, they only appear in [[castle]]s and are completely invincible, therefore they cannot be defeated. They debut in level [[World 2-4 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-4]]. Most Podoboos do a single leap out of the lava, although some may do double and even triple conclusive leaps as well. The game's instruction booklet describes them as Bowser's bodyguards.

===''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''===
In ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'', Podoboos reappear and act as they did in ''Super Mario Bros.'', and are still primarily found in castles. Some of them now hop out of lava from the roof. However, a single Podoboo can also be found in [[World 9-4 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 9-4]] between the katakana characters "ト" and "ウ" in the word "アリガトウ" (''arigatō'', meaning "thank you" in Japanese) that is formed by blocks. It is possible to defeat Podoboos in [[World A-4]] by kicking a [[Red Shell]] at them. It is not possible to defeat a Podoboo using a [[Starman]], which merely causes Mario to phase through a Podoboo harmlessly.

===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[Image:The Magma Fortress.png|thumb|left|Podoboos in [[World 5-Fortress 2]].]]

Like many other enemies from the previous games, Podoboos appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. In [[World 5-Fortress 2]], there are also some Podoboos that jump out of lava on the ceiling. Podoboos can be defeated with [[Statue Mario]], [[Hammer Mario]]'s [[hammer]], or a [[Super Star|Starman]]. Defeating a Podoboo earns the player 100 [[point]]s.

===''Super Mario World''===
[[File:PodobooSMW.png|frame|left]]
[[Image:Podoboo Super Mario World.png|thumb|right|A Podoboo hopping out of the lava in ''Super Mario World''.]]
In ''[[Super Mario World]]'', Podoboos, referred to as '''Lava Bubbles''' in the Player's Guide, return as enemies. It is the first game in which they have eyes, which would become standard for a majority of subsequent ''Mario'' titles. Fire Bubbles appear in castle levels again, where they act the same as in their previous appearances &mdash; they simply jump out of the lava in an attempt to burn Mario. They are also encountered during the boss fight against [[Larry Koopa]].

The game introduces a behavioral variety, the [[Zigzag Podoboo]], which is always found outside the lava, bouncing around the rooms of castle.

In the original ''[[Super Mario World]]'', Podoboos were referred to as '''Lava Bubbles''' in the Player’s Guide with a couple pages using the name '''Fire Bubble''', in the Player’s Guide for the [[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2|remake]], their traditional name is reinstated.
{{br|left}}

===''Super Mario 64''===
[[File:LavaBubbleSM64.png|thumb|left|A Podoboo in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'']]
Podoboos appear again in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', where they are rare and primarily appear in [[Lethal Lava Land]], which is themed after them according to the course's Japanese name. They jump out of the lava horizontally on one side of a [[tapering platform]] or some other platform and bounce their way into lava on the other side. Podoboos are indestructible, and if Mario touches one, he runs uncontrollably and loses three wedges of health from his [[Power Meter]], like all other fiery enemies or obstacles. In the battle with [[Bowser]] in [[Bowser in the Sky]], he spits some blue (green in the remake) fireballs that split into three like-colored Podoboos which immediately bounce across the stage. Both types disappear after a few seconds.

===''Super Mario 64 DS''===
[[File:Podoboo Land.png|thumb|A Podoboo as seen in ''Super Mario 64 DS''.]]
Podoboos retain their role in the remake ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. Additionally, Yoshi can eat a Podoboo, and then [[Fire Power (Yoshi)|spit out fire]], similar to his ability in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:PodobooNSMB.png|frame|left]]
[[Image:NSMB World 1-Castle.png|thumb|right|Podoboos in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.]]
Podoboos return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. They are depicted without eyes, with their design being heavily based on their appearance in both ''Super Mario Bros.'' and ''Super Mario 64 DS''. Podoboos can only be defeated with the effects of the [[Mega Mushroom]] or the [[Starman]].

Podoboos are found in [[World 1-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-Castle]], [[World 4-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 4-Castle]], [[World 6-Fortress 2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-Fortress 2]], [[World 6-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-Castle]], [[World 8-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8-Castle]], [[World 8-5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8-5]], [[World 8-Fortress 2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8-Fortress 2]], and [[World 8-Bowser's Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|Bowser's Castle]].

In the game's files, there is an unused Invisible Podoboo sprite which was never used in any levels, this Podoboo isn't shown to take leaps out of lava but rather move along a set path back and forth horizontally, if [[Mario]] were to be hit by an invisible Podoboo, it would briefly reveal itself before disappearing again. The [[Phantom Hand]]s were intended to point at the locations of invisible Podoboos, much like they already do with other invisible objects.
{{br|left}}

===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
{{main|Lava Bouncer}}
In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', while regular Podoboos do not appear, the game introduces a cyan variant of them named [[Lava Bouncer]]. Besides their color, a key difference is that they hop out of lava and bounce along its surface and the ground, following Mario, rather than simply going in and out of the lava. They can only be defeated by shooting a [[Star Bit]] at them, which releases one [[coin]].
{{br|left}}

===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[File:NSMBW Lava Bubble Render.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Podoboo Trio.png|thumb|170px|Three Podoboos from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.]]
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', Podoboos appear in many [[boss]] castles, acting like they did in ''New Super Mario Bros.''. A difference is that for their design, Podoboos have a pair of eyes, as in ''Super Mario World''. Podoboos can also be defeated by either throwing an [[Ice Ball]] at it from the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]] or by touching one while using a [[Super Star|Star]].

They first appear in [[Roy Koopa|Roy]]'s Castle in [[World 2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2]] right at the beginning and continue to appear in the castles of [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5]], [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]], and [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]]. Podoboos are common in World 8 in general and also appear as enemies in the World 8 [[Enemy Course]], where they leap out of the lava on the bridges periodically, requiring players to avoid them as they grab the [[Toad Balloon]]s. Podoboos lastly appear in [[World 9-6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-6]].

In the beta version of ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', it was shown that Podoboos were going to appear in the boss fight with [[Morton Koopa Jr.]] instead of the [[Spike Pillar]]s hopping out of the lava on the sides of the arena.

Through exploits, it is revealed that [[Yoshi]] can eat a Podoboo, but like [[Green Shell|Green]] and [[Red Shell]]s, cannot swallow them, spitting it out as a large fireball akin to the type that he would swallow from [[Venus Fire Trap]]s.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnUMLLINtX0&t=1025s]</ref>
{{br|left}}

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[File:PodobooSMG2.png|thumb|left]]
Podoboos appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' as obstacles in [[Galaxy|galaxies]] with lava, as well as in Bowser's galaxies. Just as in previous installments, Podoboos jump out of the lava trying to hit Mario or [[Luigi]]. For the first time in the ''Super Mario'' series, Podoboos have received a new ability: In [[Bowser's Gravity Gauntlet]], there are Podoboos that jump from one lava pit into another nearby lava pit. Podoboos are completely invincible, although the [[Co-Star Luma]] can stun them temporarily. Podoboos can be found in [[Bowser's Lava Lair]], Bowser's Gravity Gauntlet, the [[Melty Monster Galaxy]], and [[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]].

The [[Lava Bouncer]]s from the first ''Super Mario Galaxy'' also appear, solely in the battle against King Kaliente in the [[Boss Blitz Galaxy]].

===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
[[File:Mario 3D Land Bowser's Castle.png|thumb|170px|Three Podoboos appearing in ''Super Mario 3D Land''.]]
Podoboos return in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. They mainly appear in castle stages, often in groups. Like previous games, Podoboos act as obstacles that jump out of the lava, attempting to hit Mario or Luigi. Podoboos can also leap through mesh platforms, requiring some precision to avoid being scorched. They can also be seen hopping out of gaps between bridges above the lava. Podoboos can only be defeated while Mario or Luigi are under the effects of a [[Star]] or an [[Invincibility Leaf]]. 

Podoboos regained their dotted eyes, marking the first time they have sported eyes in a 3D title of the ''Super Mario'' series.
{{br|left}}

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:NewPodoboo.png|frame|left]]
[[Image:Podoboo High Jumper.png|thumb|A Podoboo doing an extraordinarily high jump as seen in ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''.]]
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', Podoboos return as obstacles, behaving similarly as before. They are commonly found in Castles and various levels of [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]]. Some Podoboos jump much higher, and for the first time, some jump out of lava in a diagonal pattern. Podoboos can only be defeated with the effects of the [[Starman]], [[Gold Flower]], [[Invincibility Leaf]], or the [[Mega Mushroom]]. Unlike most enemies, Podoboos are not affected by a [[Gold Ring (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|Gold Ring]].
{{br|left}}

===''New Super Mario Bros. U'' / ''New Super Luigi U'' / ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[Image:Podoboo Lava Hill.png|thumb|left|A Podoboo doing a jump from a Lava Mound (''New Super Mario Bros. U'')]]
Podoboos reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]] as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', where they behave exactly the same as in other installments. They can only be defeated with the effects of the [[Ice Flower]], [[Penguin Suit]], or the [[Starman]], as in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''. Podoboos are commonly found in Castles such as [[Lemmy's Swingback Castle]], [[Iggy's Volcanic Castle]], and various others. Podoboos are also seen in various levels of [[Peach's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Peach's Castle]] as well.

In ''New Super Luigi U'', the level [[Hot Cogs]] features some Podoboos that leap sideways and at a much quicker pace. 

===''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''===
[[File:SM3DWLavaBubble.jpg|thumb|150px|left|Podoboos in ''Super Mario 3D World'']]
In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', Podoboos reappear and behave the same way as in ''Super Mario 3D Land''. Podoboos can only be defeated with the effects of the [[Starman]], [[Invincibility Leaf]], or [[Invincibility Bell]]. They are commonly found in fiery levels such as [[Lava Rock Lair]] and various levels of [[World Castle]]. The [[Blue Podoboo]] from ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' also makes a reappearance and often appears in levels with blue lava such as [[Fort Fire Bros.]]. Podoboos do not appear in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign.

===''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS''===
[[Image:Boo Podoboo Thwomp SMM.png|thumb|right|A Podoboo near several [[Boo]]s and [[Thwomp]]s in ''Super Mario Maker''.]]
Podoboos reappear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', where they are referred to as '''Lava Bubbles'''. In all the games' themes, Podoboos behave as they do in the 2D ''Super Mario'' titles, by hopping vertically from the lava. Podoboos can now be set to come out of more objects, including [[pit]]s, [[Warp Pipe]]s and [[? Block]]s, or be produced by [[Bill Blaster]]s and [[Lakitu]]s. Like all enemies, Podoboos can be given a [[Super Mushroom]] or [[wings]], turning it into [[Big Podoboo]] and [[Para-Podoboo]], respectively. Additionally, while under the effects of a [[Big Mushroom]], Lava Bubbles gain eyes and mustaches.

===''Super Mario Run''===
[[Image:Podoboo Fire Castle.png|100px|thumb|left|Mario evades several Lava Bubbles in ''Super Mario Run''.]]
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'', vertically leaping Lava Bubbles appear as enemies castle stages, where they are one of the most common types of enemies, besides [[Dry Bones]]. They can only be defeated when the player is under the effect of a [[Super Star]]. Each supplies players with a +3 coin bonus when in [[Toad Rally]]. Lava Bubbles are usually found near [[Pause Block]]s, allowing Mario to time the movements of the Lava Bubbles before passing them.

A derived species called [[Poison Bubble]] also appears. Instead of leaping from lava, this creature leaps out of [[Poisoned Water]].

===''Super Mario Odyssey''===
[[File:Lava Bubble Mario.png|thumb|100px|left|A Podoboo being controlled by Mario.]]
[[Image:Podoboo SMO.png|thumb|right|A Podoboo in the Luncheon Kingdom of ''Super Mario Odyssey''.]]
Podoboos reappear in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', where they are found in the [[Luncheon Kingdom]] and the [[Darker Side]], along with a secret area in the [[Lost Kingdom]], the [[Cookatiel]] rematch in the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Super Mario Odyssey)|Mushroom Kingdom]], and the [[Cloud Kingdom]]. Podoboos are frequently found hopping out of the lava near edges of the mainland, always next to lone islands surrounded by lava. Some Podoboos even swim around the islands.

Podoboos are among the enemies that can be [[capture]]d by Mario, giving them his nose, eyebrows, and mustache while retaining their eye color. As a Podoboo, Mario can only travel in lava. Podoboo Mario can jump by pressing {{button|ns|B}} and dash by pressing {{button|ns|Leftstick}} and {{button|ns|Y}}. If Podoboo Mario jumps on a [[Magmato]], a puddle of lava is formed, allowing him to traverse on the ground. If Mario touches a Podoboo while controlling one, he does not take damage and is only bumped aside lightly. Podoboo Mario can jump through mesh flooring and ride [[Lava Plume]]s to gain some extra height. There are also [[Lava Cannon]]s in the Luncheon Kingdom and the Darker Side that Podoboo Mario can jump into to launch to another area of the kingdom. If Mario touches land as a Podoboo, the Podoboo dissipates, and Mario returns to normal automatically.

If Podoboo Mario jumps into a pot, it heats up, which is required to obtain one of the [[Power Moon]]s.

Various 2D sections also have 8-bit Podoboos that behave as they do in ''Super Mario Bros.'' and inflict damage if touched.

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', Lava Bubbles return with their properties from ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. They additionally appear in the new ''Super Mario 3D World'' style, where they do not leap if the player is wearing a [[Goomba Mask]]. Moreover, they can now be attached to [[parachute]]s. 
{{br}}

===''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''===
Podoboos return in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', in this game, they behave as they do in other games, hopping out of lava on either the ground or ceiling, once again touching a Podoboo will cause damage but they can be defeated with either the [[Bubble Flower]] or [[Star]], in this game, they appear exclusively in the level [[Pull, Turn, Burn]]. Despite only appearing in one level, they can be seen around the World Map of [[Deep Magma Bog]].

==Trivia==
*One of the Japanese ''Super Mario'' character encyclopedias postulates that Podoboos may be the manifestation of the resentment of the Koopa Troop.<ref>[https://imgur.com/a/S1rzhUx Translated page]</ref>
*Their name, "Podoboo" might suggest that they’re related to the [[Boo]]s, this is probable due to them being grouped in the same section as the [[Boo]]-enemies in <i>[[Super Princess Peach]]</i> but also unlikely since Podoboos made their debut before the Boos.
*Only receiving an official artwork in <i>[[Super Mario Run]]</i> in 2016, this makes the Podoboo the last enemy that debuted in <i>[[Super Mario Bros.]]</i> to receive an official artwork.
