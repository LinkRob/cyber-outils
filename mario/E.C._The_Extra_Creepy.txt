"'''E.C. The Extra Creepy'''" is the sixteenth live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the animated episode "[[Toad Warriors]]."

==Plot==
At [[Mario Bros. Plumbing]], [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] gets dressed up so that he can go out with his new computer date, [[E.C.]]. [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] finds that the woman's profile is a little odd.  Soon after, the entire room shakes and the lights start to go out.  Mario opens the door to greet his computer date and finds her to his liking.  However, when he asks for her hand, she speaks in a metallic voice and when asked for her hand she literally removes it and gives it to Mario.

In spite of the initial problem, only Luigi sees that there's something wrong with E.C. and that has more to do with the fact that her vehicle has no license plates.  Mario ignores Luigi's warnings and claims that he may be in love.  Mario and E.C. go out for a drive.  Luigi looks through a periscope and sees a ship flying.

Unfortunately for Mario, E.C. gets called back to her ship after their drive and cannot stay with him. E.C. proceeds to say goodbye to Mario, who asks if she can give him something to remember her by. In response to this, E.C. gives him her mechanical heart and leaves Mario Bros. Plumbing.
