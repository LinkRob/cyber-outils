|name=Big Jump x2 (Battle Card)}}'''Big Jump x2''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Jump x2 is the strong version of [[Mario]]'s "Jump x2" attack.  It is two of the [[Big Jump]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied by an orange/tan checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 40 coins (unpainted)

Sell Price: 20 coins (unpainted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Jump x2 is only obtainable in [[Redpepper Volcano]].
