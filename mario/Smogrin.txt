'''Smogrins''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical Description===
Smogrins are purple clouds of varying sizes. They have sinister grins and sharp teeth.

=== Behaviour ===
Smogrins move back and forth, with smaller clouds in their wake. Contact with a Smogrin damages the player.

== In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' ==
The first level to feature the Smogrin is Bulrush Coming Through!, with some levels afterwards featuring them as an uncommon enemy. The latest level to feature the Smogrin is Spring Feet II.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The name "Smogrin" comes from the words "smog" and "grin."{{stub}}
