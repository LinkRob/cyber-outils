'''Fruit Cahoots''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. It has a similar fashion to the [[Whomp King]] battle from ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It is a part luck, part skill game where what you have to do is hit up on the D-pad to say 1 and down to say 0.
