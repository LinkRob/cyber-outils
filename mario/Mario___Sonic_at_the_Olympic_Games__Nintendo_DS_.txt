'''''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games''''' is a game for the [[Nintendo DS]], developed by [[Sega]] and produced by [[Nintendo]]. It was released shortly after its [[Wii]] [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|counterpart]], and is the first handheld installment of the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' series. It is an official game for the [[wikipedia:2008 Summer Olympics|Beijing 2008 Olympics]], and it features venues similar to real ones in Beijing. Like its Wii counterpart, ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'' for the Nintendo DS featured support for [[Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection]] prior to its closure in May 2014.

The game is a collection of Olympic sports events at which characters from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] and characters from the ''[[Sonic the Hedgehog (franchise)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' franchise, relying heavily on the motion-sensing capabilities of the [[Wii Remote]]. This is the first handheld game to feature both ''Mario'' and ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' characters as playable.

*[[Doctor Eggman]] vs. [[Waluigi]]
*[[Blaze the Cat]] vs. [[Princess Daisy]]

===Characters Specialties===
====Team Mario====
*[[Mario]]- high jump, hurdles
*[[Luigi]]- adequate in all events
*[[Princess Peach]]- archery, skeet
*[[Princess Daisy]]- aquatics, gymnastics
*[[Bowser]]- hammer throw, javelin throw
*[[Waluigi]]- archery, vault
*[[Wario]]- Accuracy in Stamina, can hurl objects at long distances
*[[Yoshi]]- table tennis, all racing events

====Team Sonic====
*[[Sonic the Hedgehog]]- no one can easily catch him, all racing events
*[[Miles "Tails" Prower]]- catch ups speed quickly
*[[Knuckles the Echidna]]- all strength events, hurl objects at long distances
*[[Amy Rose]]- successive in all events
*[[Vector the Crocodile]]- stroke swimmer
*[[Doctor Eggman]]- trampoline, vault
*[[Blaze the Cat]]- vault, all racing events
*[[Shadow the Hedgehog]]- archery, fencing
