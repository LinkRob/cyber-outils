'''Boo Blast''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] used by [[Boo (character)|Boo]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''. Upon using it, Boo spins, and Boos gather to form up a Hammer. Upon hitting the tennis ball, the Boos follow it to go and harass the receiver. The [[Power Shot]] can stun the player from controlling their time.
