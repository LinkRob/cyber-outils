==''Donkey Kong'' franchise==
===''Donkey Konga'' series===
====''Donkey Konga 2''====
In ''[[Donkey Konga 2]]'', Pikmin make a cameo appearance in the form of three badges depicting a Red Pikmin, a Blue Pikmin, and a Yellow Pikmin respectively. Each badge depicts that Pikmin's artwork from ''Pikmin 2''.

====''Donkey Konga 3 JP''====
In ''[[Donkey Konga 3 JP]]'', the same badges make a reappearance from ''Donkey Konga 2''.

==''Wario'' franchise==
===''WarioWare'' series===
====''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''====
[[File:WWSM Pikmin 2.png|200px|thumb|The Pikmin microgame in ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'']]
In ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', Pikmin appear in the [[microgame]] [[Pikmin 2 (WarioWare: Smooth Moves)|Pikmin 2]], although the object is to roll [[Empress Bulblax]] over all of the Pikmin to squish them within the time limit. In Level 1, Empress Bulblax has to only roll over sixteen Pikmin. In Level 2, there is only one large Pikmin having to be rolled over. In Level 3, there are sixteen Pikmin, but with eight on each side of Empress Bulblax.

====''WarioWare: D.I.Y.''====
In ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'', Pikmin are featured in the microgame [[Pikmin (microgame)|Pikmin]], absent from the Japanese version. The object is for Captain Olimar to throw a Red Pikmin onto a rock with nectar. If time runs out or if the Pikmin is accidentally thrown into the water, the game ends in a loss.

====''WarioWare Gold''====
In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', Pikmin return in the Pikmin 2 microgame, which has the same objective, although the Pikmin quantities differ in Level 1 and 2, instead featuring six Pikmin and twelve Pikmin, respectively.

====''WarioWare: Get It Together!''====
In ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'', Pikmin appear in a different [[Pikmin 2 (WarioWare: Get It Together!)|Pikmin 2]] microgame. They do not have any effect on gameplay.

==Crossover appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
====''Super Smash Bros. Melee''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', a group of Red, Blue, and Yellow Pikmin make a cameo appearance as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]].

====''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''====
[[File:BrawlOlimar.jpg|thumb|left|200px|''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' artwork of a Red, a Blue, and a Yellow Pikmin with Captain Olimar]]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', Pikmin assist [[Captain Olimar]] during battle. They are not playable, although Olimar uses them in a majority of his moves. Olimar can pluck Pikmin from the ground to assist him. Aside from Red, Blue, and Yellow Pikmin, Olimar is also assisted by Purple and White Pikmin, both of which first appear in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Pikmin 2|Pikmin 2]]''. Olimar can have up to six Pikmin with him at a time.

In a cutscene of [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]], several Pikmin are seen pecking on [[R.O.B.]], who then shakes off the Pikmin, killing them.

====''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', Olimar, and now also [[Alph]], are assisted by Pikmin once more. Aside from the five types which return from ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', there is also the Winged Pikmin, originally introduced in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Pikmin 3|Pikmin 3]]''. Unlike in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', only up to three Pikmin can be out at a time. 

====''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Pikmin retain the same role from ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U''.

===AR Games===
In [[AR Games]], a group of Pikmin, depicted in their artwork for ''[[w:c:pikmin:Pikmin 2|Pikmin 2]]'' are displayed on one of five Character Cards packaged with the [[Nintendo 3DS]] and used with the [[AR Games]] software.

==Shared appearances==
===''Pikmin'' Short Movies===
In the short "Occupational Hazards", there is a ''Mario'' reference where a red Pikmin is seen going through a [[Warp Pipe]] while collecting some [[Coin]]s, then emerging with dust forming Mario's hat and mustache on its face. Sound effects from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' as well as the first seven notes of "[[Ground BGM (Super Mario Bros.)|Ground BGM]]" are also played.

===Super Nintendo World===
Several Pikmin appear on the artwork used for the bags in the [[Super Nintendo World]] theme park land. They can be found hidden on ledges throughout and as an hidden animation viewed through the augmented reality [[Binoculars]]. Some of these hidden Pikmin are seen working together carrying items such as a [[Coin]] and a [[Fruit]].
