'''Crazy Crosshairs''' is a 2-vs.-2 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Layout==
Teams play in a garden, standing on toy tanks that aim at a web infested with [[Scuttlebug]]s.

==Gameplay==
The characters must aim and shoot at Scuttlebugs to gain [[point]]s. To do so, one player on each team must aim horizontally, and the other player must aim vertically. Normal Scuttlebugs are worth one point, while golden ones are worth three. The team who shoots more Scuttlebugs wins; if both teams shoot the same number of Scuttlebugs, the minigame ends in a tie. 

==In ''Mario Party DS''==
Crazy Crosshairs is one of the minigames to not appear in Simple Set.

==Controls==
*{{button|ds|A}} &ndash; Control the crosshair
