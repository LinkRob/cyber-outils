'''Bob-omb gang''' is a group of Bob-ombs lead by [[King Bob-omb|Big Bob-omb]]. The group only appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'', even though the leader and the Bob-ombs make several independent appearances in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games.

== In ''Mario Party Advance'' ==
[[Bob-omb (character)|Bob-omb]] or [[Bob-omba]] told about where their boss is hiding. The player came to the [[Mushroom Condo]], he hid under a rare floor, the basement. Until the player entered the floor, Big Bob-omb was shocked asking how did he find them. He asks for a dice game, the player agrees as the leader was pleased; then they competed each other. In the game, Big Bob-omb rolls the dice and then the player has to roll the highest number to defeat him with three rounds. As a reward, he grants him a [[Gaddget]].
