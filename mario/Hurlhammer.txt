'''Hurlhammer''' is a type of [[hammer]] used as a weapon in a few of the later {{series|Paper Mario}} games. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' and later returns in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''.

==Description==
Whenever Mario uses the Hurlhammer, he throws it into the air so that it can land on enemies, including airborne ones. When it lands, the Hurlhammer bounces, possibly hurting enemies within its path.
