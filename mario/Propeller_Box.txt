A '''Propeller Box''' is a power-up which appears in a few ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' series installments. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
A Propeller Box is an orange block with eyes and a yellow propeller that can be worn by [[Mario]] and other playable characters. The blades of the propeller allow for it to be launched in the air, and then slowly propel back down to the ground.

The [[Propeller Block]], which debuted in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', could possibly be the predecessor to the Propeller Box, as it functions very similarly but is instead held by a playable character.
