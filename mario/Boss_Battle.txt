A '''Boss Battle''' is a term used in many ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' games where it usually means someone is fighting a much tougher enemy. The first sign of Boss Battles are usually indicated by the change of scenery or, more commonly, the change of theme music.

==Other Types==

===Mini Boss Battle===
The Mini Boss Battle is described as a stronger fight with an enemy but not strong enough to be a boss. The ''[[Paper Mario series]]'' is known for it's Mini Boss fights before the Chapter's boss.

===Final Boss Battle===
The Final Boss Battle, as the name says, is usually a fight with the final boss of the game. The Final Boss is stronger than any other of the game's bosses and it usually involves Mario or some other character using all of their skills to defeat that opponent. [[Bowser]] currently holds the record for being the most fought Final Boss in the series (including as different forms such as [[Shrowser]] and [[Dreamy Bowser]]).

==Appearances==

===''[[Donkey Kong (game)|Donkey Kong]]''===
The only Boss Battle in this game is with the final boss [[Donkey Kong]]. Mario must run all over the pegs to defeat him. The stage where Donkey Kong is fought on is [[100m (Donkey Kong)|100m]].

===''[[Super Mario Bros. series]]''===
In ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', the bosses in this game are mainly [[False Bowser]]s while Bowser, himself, is the final boss.

In ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', the mini bosses are [[Birdo]]s and the bosses are [[Wart]]'s gang. Wart, himself, serves as the final boss.

In ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', the mini-bosses are [[Boom Boom]]s and the bosses are the [[Koopalings]]. Bowser is the final boss of the game and he is fought on World 8 also known as Dark World.
===''[[Super Mario World]]''===
In this game, the [[Reznor]]s and [[Big Boo]]s serve as mini-bosses while, once again, the Koopalings serve as the bosses. Bowser is once again the game's final boss.
