}}The '''Beetle''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Beetles are considered items in this game despite being insects. After buying a Beetle Box for 150 coins, [[Mario]] can head back to [[Booster Hill]] to play the Beetle Catching minigame. Mario will have a certain amount of time to catch as many beetles as he can.  These Beetles can then be traded for coins at Beetles Are Us in [[Seaside Town]].
