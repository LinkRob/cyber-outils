'''Gold Mario''' debuted as a playable character from ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'' whom is a form of [[Mario]].

==Appearances==
===''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''===



===''Mario Kart Tour''===

In this game, he is in Gold Rush because in New Super Mario Bros. 2, Mario or Luigi will get a block on their head which is gold. They get coins when running.

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===

Mario will turn gold when getting a Gold Flower. Like the gold block (brick), he will get coins when runs.

==Non-Canonical appearances==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Gold Mario appears in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' as a support spirit. Gold Mario's spirit can be summoned by [[Metal Mario]], [[Super Mushroom]] and [[Fire Flower]].
