'''Windmill Village''' is a small village that is found in [[Bianco Hills]] of the game ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
Windmill Village is a fairly small town with houses around. There are also clotheslines that can be used as tightropes and a river that is located not too far away.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Only three episodes require Windmill Village to be accessed: [[Red Coins of Windmill Village]], [[Petey Piranha Strikes Back]], and [[Shadow Mario on the Loose]]. During the time, [[Mario]] must mainly use the tightropes to reach higher areas. [[Blue Coin]]s could also be found in areas such as the river far west or up near a pole that Mario can only get up their either being thrown by a [[Chuckster]] or by using the [[Rocket Nozzle]].
