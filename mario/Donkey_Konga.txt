'''''Donkey Konga''''' is a ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' rhythm game for the [[Nintendo GameCube]]. It is the first game of the ''[[Donkey Konga (series)|Donkey Konga]]'' series, and it went on to receive two sequels, ''[[Donkey Konga 2]]'' and ''[[Donkey Konga 3 JP]]'', the latter only being released in Japan. ''Donkey Konga'' was first released in Japan, on December 12, 2003 (the same day as the [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] of ''[[Donkey Kong Country (Game Boy Advance)|Super Donkey Kong]]'' was released there), then in late 2004 in other countries.

Four ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters—[[Mario]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], and [[Wario]]—make a cameo as slots during Battle mode in multiplayer. Each of the four slots depict a crop of a ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' artwork to show only the character's head.
