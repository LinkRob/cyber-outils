'''Valley of Bowser 2''' is a [[level]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', more specifically the second level of the [[Valley of Bowser]]. It has two exits, with the main one unlocking [[Valley Ghost House]] while the secret exit unlocks the [[Valley Fortress]].
