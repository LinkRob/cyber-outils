The '''Star Cup''' is the third cup in every [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'']] series game to date (except for ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', in which it is the fourth), but the tracks vary in each game.

{{Infobox_Cup|name=Star Cup|image=StarCupMK8.png|caption=The Star Cup's Icon as it appears in [[Mario Kart 8]]|first=[[Super Mario Kart]], (1992)|latest=[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]], (2017)}}

Up until ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', Star Cup was a standard cup. Since then, it has been unlockable. Star Cup can be unlocked by placing in [[Grand Prix]] in [[Flower Cup]]. Star Cup has always been needed to unlock [[Special Cup]].
