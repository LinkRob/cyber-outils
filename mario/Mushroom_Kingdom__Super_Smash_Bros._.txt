'''Mushroom Kingdom''' is a plains-themed stage ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]''. It is the only unlockable stage in the game, and it can be unlocked by first clearing 1P Game with the eight starter characters ([[Mario]], [[Link]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], [[Samus Aran|Samus]], [[Pikachu]], [[Fox McCloud|Fox]], and [[Kirby]]) and then playing on every other stage in Vs. mode. Mushroom Kingdom also returns as a stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Features==
One the main features of Mushroom Kingdom are the [[Warp Pipe]]s, where if one player goes down one, they will come out the other. Also, sometimes, Piranha Plants may come out of the warp pipes dealing moderate damage with rather high knockback. Another feature of this stage are the two platforms directly in the center of the stage. If one player stands on one of the platforms, that platform will sink while the other one goes into the air resulting in a self-destruct if the player stays on longer.

Another thing that makes this stage very unique from the others is when a player is playing a time-themed battle and it reaches 30 seconds, the music will pick up making this stage the only stage in ''Super Smash Bros.'' to have two different themes playing on it.

==Trivia==
*This stage is directly based on World 1-1 from the original ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', with even the original [[Ground Theme]] playing as the background music.
