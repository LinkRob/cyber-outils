'''Green Fuzzies''' are a variation of [[Fuzzy]] which made their first appearance in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
Green Fuzzies have the same appearance as Fuzzies except they are green. They have a little more health than regular Fuzzies and suck more [[HP]] from [[Mario]] or his partner. A Green Fuzzy can also divide into another one. Green Fuzzies are only encountered in [[Keelhaul Key]], although one Green Fuzzy is fought beforehand at the [[Glitz Pit]]. Unlike [[Flower Fuzzy|Flower Fuzzies]], Green Fuzzies chase their target instead of bouncing around randomly. There is a level up strategy used with them.
