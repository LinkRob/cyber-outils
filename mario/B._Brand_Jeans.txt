'''B. Brand Jeans''' is a [[clothing]] item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It can only be equipped by [[Luigi]]. In both versions, the B. Brand Jeans can only be bought from the clothes store in [[Beanbean Castle Town]].

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', equipping the B. Brand Jeans increases Luigi's [[Defense]] by 55. It can be bought for 600 [[coin]]s. In the Japanese version, it instead costs 1,050 coins and raises Defense by 65, likely owing to its higher price.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the B. Brand Jeans grant an even higher defense of 74, and are priced at 1,200 coins. B. Brand Jeans can be sold for 300 coins.
