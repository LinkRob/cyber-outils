'''Rock Mushrooms''' are power-up items in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They transform [[Mario]] into [[Rock Mario]], ([[Luigi]] into Rock Luigi) and when collected, Mario's hat and overalls turn into stone, and it gives Mario the ability to encase himself in a boulder upon shaking the [[Wii#Wii Remote|Wii Remote]] once.

In this form, Mario can roll around, jump, damage enemies, and destroy red crystals with items in them. However, when he comes into contact with a crystal or a certain type of enemy, he will revert to his rock form. However, players can almost always return to his boulder form by re-shaking the Wii Remote or pressing Z on the nunchuck. In [[Melty Monster Galaxy]]'s [[A Stroll Down Rolling Lane]], there is a special Rock Mushroom, and with it, Mario will do a spin if Z is pressed or if the Wii Remote is shook instead of reverting to normal. In either scenario, Mario will revert to his normal form if he takes damage or touches a [[Launch Star]].

The Rock Mushroom is of paramount importance in the [[Boulder Bowl Galaxy]], and in the second mission of [[Melty Monster Galaxy]].
