The '''Magical Seeds''' are a series of four items in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
There are only four [[Bub-ulb]]s in this game, and each of them has one of the Magical Seeds. Mario can give the four magical seeds to [[Minh T.]], the flower planter in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. Minh T. plants the four magical seeds to grow four different flowers, which magically creates the door to [[Flower Fields]].

===Locations===
The four locations of the Bub-ulbs and Magical Seeds are:

*'''Toad Town''': There is a Bub-ulb near [[Tayce T.]]'s house that will give Mario a Magical Seed.
*'''[[Mt. Rugged]]''': There is a gap with a Bub-ulb in Mt. Rugged in the area with the three levels (that has a Cleft in the middle section). Mario needs [[Parakarry]] to carry him to the space with the Bub-ulb in it, who will give you a Magical Seed.
*'''[[Forever Forest]]''': In the second main area of Forever Forest, there is a Bub-ulb that will give Mario a Magical Seed.
*'''[[Jade Jungle]]''': There is a Bub-ulb on a small island that Mario needs [[Sushie]] to reach. The Magical Seed is not ready yet, so the Bub-ulb will promise you the Magical Seed when it is ready. However, after Mario defeats the [[Lava Piranha]], Mario will find out the Bub-ulb gave the Magical Seed to [[Kolorado]]. A treasure chest with the [[Volcano Vase]] inside flies out when the volcano erupts, and the only way Kolorado will give Mario the seed is if he gives him the Volcano Vase. Once Mario has the vase, he can trade for the Magical Seed.
