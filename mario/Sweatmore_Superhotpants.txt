The '''Sweatmore Superhotpants''' are key items that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are an upgraded version of the [[Sweatmore Hotpants]].

==Description==
The Sweatmore Superhotpants resemble the Sweatmore Hotpants except being red in color instead of white/gray.

==History==
The Sweatmore Hotpants, as stated, are stronger versions of the Sweatmore Hotpants and can be found within the magma lake of [[Sweatmore Peak]]. These clothing allow [[Wario]] to dive deeper into the magma lake without taking damage (as even the basic Sweatmore Hotpants won't protect him from the more intense heat of the lower levels). Wario must avoid the [[Snackeral]]s as they can steal the Sweatmore Superhotpants. However, if he still has the regular Sweatmore Hotpants, they will steal them instead.
