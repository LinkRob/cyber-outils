'''Wind-Up Tower''' is the tower of [[Layer-Cake Desert]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

== Features ==
This level is mainly gears that you spin to go up the tower.

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': After winding the first spinning switch a lot, climb up to the Star Coin before the block above it comes to it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After winding the second spinning switch a lot, climb to the third lowest conveyor and get the Star Coin on the right of the block before it hides in the wall.
*'''Star Coin 3''': After winding the third spinning switch a lot, climb up to the second highest layer to the left where the final coin is before the coin hides in the block.

== Hidden Luigi image ==
To the left on the last crushing blocks group in the fourth lowest layer shows a picture of Super Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' You just see it before the crushing blocks hide it.
