Holiday Tour was the eighty-fifth Tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']], that began on December 13, 2022 and ended on december 27, 2022. This Tour was rerun on December 12, 2023 to December 26, 2023 and December 10, 2024 to december 24, 2024. 

This Tour like [[Holiday Tour (2019)]] and [[Holiday Tour (2021)]] featered the events of Christmass in which [[Merry Mountain]] is featured along with [[mario (Santa)]], [[Daisy (Santa)|Daisy (Santa),]] [[Bowser (santa)]] and [[Yoshi (Reindeer)]]. It also gave [[Larry Koopa]] a winter variant making it the only [[Koopalings|Koopaling]] that gets a new outfit. [[Snow Land]] also returns from [[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]].
