'''Jungle Falls''' is a battle arena in the Vs. Mode of [[Super Duel Mode]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
Jungle Falls takes place on a rectangular suspension bridge near a waterfall in the middle of a jungle filled with bamboo.
