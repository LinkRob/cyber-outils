A '''Goodie Bag''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
The Goodie Bag can only be used after a battle where it adds a certain amount of [[coin]]s. This rare item can be found in [[Booster Tower]] after jumping on top of a certain chest to reveal another hidden chest. The Goodie Bag can also be sold for 555 coins.
