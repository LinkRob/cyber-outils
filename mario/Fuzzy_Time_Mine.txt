'''Fuzzy Time Mine''' (known as '''Fuzzy Flood Mine''' in European releases) is a [[level]] of [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Layout==
This level is mainly set inside a mineshaft that auto-scrolls both vertically and horizontally. While they are in it, [[Mario]] and his friends must run from a [[Fuzzy Horde]] that chases them throughout. To help the characters scaling the area, there are many cloud platforms, [[seesaw]]s, [[Jump Panel]]s, and compactors that periodically shift.

At the top of the tower, there is a door to a deck, where the gang finds the [[Goal Pole]].

==Green Stars==
*'''[[Green Power Star|Green Star]] 1''': The first star is visible below a cloud platform to the right side of the mine at the start of the level, after the stamp. Ground pound through the cloud platform and collect the stamp.
*'''Green Star 2''': Farther up the mine, to the right, the second star is one a tilting platform. Shift weight to one side then rush up to snatch the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': At the point where the mine heads straight up, there are four shifting blocks. When they pull apart, there is a hollowed out area containing the third star.

==Stamp==
[[File:Fuzzy Time Mine Stamp.png|frame|left]]
Near the beginning of the stage, while climbing up the mine, look for the first flying Dry Bones, and duck into an alcove below him when a block moves out of the way to grab the Stamp.
