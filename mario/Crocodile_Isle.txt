'''Crocodile Isle''' (also known as '''Kremling Island''' and '''Krem Island''')<ref>"'''Swanky Kong''': ''On the Kremling island map, how many small wasps hover around the wasp hives?''" - ''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''</ref><ref>"'''Swanky Kong''': ''What is the name of the Kremling island that you are on?''" - ''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''</ref><ref>''Nintendo Power'', Vol. 76, September, 1995, page 23 - "'''''CAPTURED:''' Dixie and Diddy won't go it alone when they reach Krem Island on their search for Donkey Kong.''"</ref> was a decadent island located on the southeastern side of [[Donkey Kong Island]]. Here was homeworld of [[Kremling]] species, the [[Kremling Krew]]'s capital base of operations and [[King K. Rool]]'s regin. Here was the main world in the events of  ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''. ==Geography==
The island visibly was a highest rock peak with double cliffs with grass and a crocodile skull carved below. The geography consists of natural settings such as swamps, underground volcanos, brambles, pines and a very high glacier. Under [[Kremling Krew]] in power, a [[K. Rool's Keep|castle]] was established at the top with K. Rool's head carvings below, an [[Krazy Kremland|amusement park]] in the middle and hives were build.<ref>" '''Cranky Kong:''' ''Where does K. Rool keep getting these giant Zingers from?'' - ''Donkey Kong Country 2 (GBA)''</ref>

Completely opposite of the tropical and preserved Donkey Kong Island, Crocodile Isle's places become a real horror during the K. Rool's dictatorship.<ref name=K.Rool>''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy Kong's Quest'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1995, p. 46 - "'''''JUNGLE HINX:''' It looks like K. Rool hasn’t heard about preserving the rain forests.''"</ref> The cause was the pollution and waste that involved a lot of garbage, mining and toxic waste. As a result, all flora became dead caused by sterility.

==History==
===Background information===
This island contains a very large historic. Here has been the birthplace of the Kremling species since ancient times where society was a tribe at the time.<ref name=Krem-Isle>[https://web.archive.org/web/20210531044052/https://twitter.com/Ghoulyboy/status/999927794892181504 Gregg Mayles' Twitter] (retrieved on May, 4, 2018)</ref> The only place that all history was kept was the [[Kremling's Lost World|Lost World]] where it was kept secret for a long time.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20210531043854/https://twitter.com/Ghoulyboy/status/1080388725320884226 Gregg Mayles' Twitter] (January 2, 2019)</ref>

In ancient times, the island was invaded and taken over by pirate [[Snap]]s forcing K. Rool's ancestors to leave their home island and establish [[Kremlantis]].<ref name=Kremlantis>[https://mario.fandom.com/wiki/File:Like_of_Adrian_Smith_in_Twitter_(12,_25,_2023).png ''Adrian Smith'''s like on Twitter] (December 25, 2023)</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Land'' Nintendo Magazine System, page 13. - "'''''KREMLANTIS''' Rock hewn and rock solid is Kremlantis, ancient home of K. Rool's masterful predecessors.''"</ref> The trail of Snaps that remained on the island was an [[Lava Lagoon|abandoned galleon]] in [[Crocodile Cauldron]].<ref name=Kremlantis/>

With Kremling society corrupted by the pirate Snaps' greed, the island has become a pirate lair by [[Kremling Kuthroats]] where a [[Gloomy Gulch|town]] was established upon the second cliff, a quay and hidden caves stored all the valuables that were stolen.<ref name=Krem-Isle/><ref name=GloomyGulch>''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1995, p. 20 - "'''''GLOOMY GULCH:''' Don't count on meeting another living soul in this dilapidated ghost town, where even the ropes fade in and out of existence like phantoms.''"</ref><ref>''Nintendo Power'', Vol. 79, December, 1995, page 26 - "'''''GLOOMY GULCH:''' The last stretch of road leading to K. Rool's Keep passes though the ghost town of Gloomy Gulch, and you'll be up to your hips in ghosties, beasties and bogles.''"</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 1995, p. 21 - "'''''KREM QUAY:''' Once the port of Crocodile Isle (as if anyone would really want to visit), the place is now a swamp, crawling with rats and other vile vermin.''"</ref> At the end of the pirate era during the [[Kremean War]], the island consisted of a shipwreck in [[Krem Quay]].<ref>[https://mario.fandom.com/wiki/File:Like_of_Adrian_Smith_in_Twitter_(2,_9,_2024).png ''Adrian Smith'''s like on Twitter] (February 9, 2024)</ref>  

After K. Rool's accession to power, the island undergoes several changes where a castle was established at the top to be the seat of government and a funfair in middle for the Kremling Krew to have fun.<ref name=Krem-Isle/><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1995, p. 20 - "'''''K. ROOL'S KEEP:''' The Kaptain's home is on the highest peak Crocodile Isle.''"</ref> <ref>''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 1995, p. 21 - "'''''KRAZY KREMLAND:''' What do the Kremlings do for fun when they aren't making the Kong Family's life miserable? They go to Krazy Kremland, the island amusement park!''"</ref> The landscapes of island also had a completely horrible change because of pollution & waste, where all flora died and town was dilapidated leaving only [[Kackle]]'s house.<ref name=K.Rool/><ref name=GloomyGulch/>

==Locations in Crocodile Isle==
*[[Gangplank Galleon]]: A peaceful area in Crocodile Isle taking place on a ship and has planks, ship masts, etc.
*[[Crocodile Cauldron]]: A volcanic-like area filled with lava infested levels and a ship that has crashed into this area as an Easter Egg.
*[[Krem Quay]]: A swampy-like area with swamps, polluted water, and large swamp gaps needed to be crossed via [[Krockhead]]s.
*[[Krem Cauldron]]: A mix between Crocodile Cauldron and Krem Quay. Happened once Crocodile Isle resurrected and is exclusive to ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''.
*[[Krazy Kremland]]: An amusement park for the [[Kremling]]s and also has some wasp hives in the area.
*[[Gloomy Gulch]]: A spooky area filled with ghosts and a wasp hive with [[Zinger]]s.
*[[K. Rool's Keep]]: The storage area for K. Rool. It has icy level terrains and is in a castle.
*[[The Flying Krock]]: The area where Donkey Kong was taken at and where the race with [[Screech]] happens.
*[[Lost World (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Lost World]]: The area with a mixture of jungle, volcanic and ice areas. K. Rool is battled here again.
