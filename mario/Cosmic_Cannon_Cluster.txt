'''Cosmic Cannon Cluster''' is a level of [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Layout==
The level contains many various challenges, ranging from using a [[Cannon Box]] to hit a switch on a moving [[Switchboard]] to dodging [[cannonball]]s underwater. The characters start by going on a switchboard around several [[Parabones]]. Then they climb a wall by jumping on the cannonballs, and go through an underwater tunnel. After the [[Checkpoint Flag]], there is a network full of Switchboards, Parabones, and [[Switch Block (Super Mario 3D World)|Switch Block]]. All that remains is to get to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Green Stars==
*'''[[Green Power Star|Green Star]] 1''': Before entering the first pipe, go right with a cannon box to blow up the wall.
*'''Green Star 2''': In the underwater section, Use cat abilities to break under a trapezoid and get a star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Really difficult. Use the touchscreen to slow down the switchboard, then take a charged shot at the star.
