The '''Koopa Clown''' is a recurring [[kart]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}, first appearing in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. It is based on the [[Koopa Clown Car]]. The character's emblem is located on the back.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 7]]''===
[[File:MK7 Koopa Clown.png|thumb|150px|The Koopa Clown as it appears in ''Mario Kart 7''.]]
The Koopa Clown is a unlockable kart body part. The Koopa Clown offers a boost in [[handling]], but lowers [[acceleration]] and [[Off-Road|off-road]]. In terms of hidden stats, the Koopa Clown offers a boost in sea and air handling, as well as [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]].

The Koopa Clown body is particularly favoured by the CPU-controlled [[Lakitu]].

It is also used for the Expert Staff Ghost for {{classic course|GBA|[[Bowser Castle 1 (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|Bowser Castle 1]]}}, using Bowser with the Roller tires and the Super Glider. It is also used for the Expert Staff Ghost for {{classic course|Wii|[[Coconut Mall]]}} using [[Honey Queen]], equipped with the [[Sponge]] tires and the [[Paraglider]].

=== ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ===
[[File:MK8D_Koopalings_and_Bowser_Jr_Screenshot.png|thumb|200px|left|[[Bowser Jr.]] and the [[Koopalings]] driving their Koopa Clown karts on {{classic course|SNES|[[Donut Plains 3 (track)|Donut Plains 3]]}} in ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''.]]
The Koopa Clown is an unlockable kart body part. Unlike in ''7'', the vehicle itself now has shorter exhaust pipe in the rear. A side panel on both sides pop open to reveal small propellers when the Koopa Clown is in anti-gravity and gliding sections. A larger propeller also comes out from the bottom when the vehicle is in said sections. In terms of stats, the Koopa Clown has a huge reduction in acceleration and a slight reduction in speed.

The Koopa Clown kart is one of the many karts used by the CPU-controlled [[Bowser]], [[Bowser Jr.]] and all seven [[Koopalings]].
{{br|left}}
=== ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
[[File:MKT_Bowser_Jr_and_Koopalings.jpg|thumb|180px|Bowser Jr. and the Koopalings driving their Koopa Clown karts on {{classic course|N64|[[Kalimari Desert]]}} in ''Mario Kart Tour''.]]
The Koopa Clown is classified as a Super kart. Performing Mini-Turbos with the Koopa Clown will earn more points. It retains its design from ''Deluxe'', equipped with the [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires from said game. However, unlike in ''Deluxe'', it's rim does not change color for the Koopalings, sticking with the default green rim. There is also a dark variant, simply named the [[Dark Clown]], which is also classified as a Super kart.
