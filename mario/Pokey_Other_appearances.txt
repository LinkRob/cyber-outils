==''Yoshi'' appearances==
===''Yoshi's New Island''===
[[File:Underground Pokey Patrol.png|thumb|Yoshi eating the head of a Pokey]]
While only [[Pokey (projectile)|Pokey]] [[Pokey (heading)|variant]]s appeared in previous [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' games]], regular Pokeys appear for the first time in the ''Yoshi'' series in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. In this game, Yoshi can swallow individual segments to reduce the Pokey's height, or eat its head to fully defeat it, as in ''Super Mario World''. Upon eating a segment, Yoshi automatically ingests it and produces an egg without the player's input. A white variant of Pokeys also appears in [[Punkey the Pokey Prince's Castle]], which move along the ceiling rather than on the ground. As the stage name implies, a large white Pokey, [[Punkey the Pokey Prince]], is the boss of the stage.

==Crossover appearances==
===''The Legend of Zelda'' series===
{{multiframe|[[File:Pokey LA sprite.png]] [[File:Pokey LADX sprite.png]]|align=left}}
[[File:TLOZ LA remake Pokey.jpg|thumb|80px|A Pokey in the ''Link's Awakening'' remake]]
Pokeys have even appeared in various ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' games, such as ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening]]'' and its [[reissue|rerelease]]s, and ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons]]''. 

In ''The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening'', Pokeys are found in [[w:c:zelda:Yarna Desert|Yarna Desert]]. If [[Link]] strikes them with his sword, sections of their body will fly off while the remaining segments get faster, similar to the [[Hokkubokku|Bouncer]]s from ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past]]''. In the remake, a Pokey figure can be won in the [[w:c:zelda:Trendy Game|Trendy Game]] after clearing [[w:c:zelda:Turtle Rock (Link's Awakening)|Turtle Rock]] and placed in the [[w:c:zelda:Quadruplet|Quadruplet]]s' house. The text that appears upon acquiring the figure reads, "''You got a Pokey figure! The memory of meeting these still stings...''"

In ''The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons'', Pokeys are found in [[w:c:zelda:Samasa Desert|Samasa Desert]]. Unlike in ''Link's Awakening'', attacking a Pokey with a sword does not cause sections of its body to fly off and is instead defeated outright. Hitting one with a [[w:c:zelda:Mystery Seed|Mystery Seed]] causes it to grow a segment, with up to five total segments being growable from the default three.

==Guest appearances==
===''Minecraft''===
In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''[[Minecraft]]'', [[w:c:minecraft:Creeper|Creepers]] are replaced by ''Super Mario Bros. 2''-style Pokeys. Cacti are replaced by yellow Pokeys.

===''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope''===
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'', a Pokey can be seen as part of a desert-themed "snow globe-house" at [[Pristine Peaks]].
