The '''Casino Box''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
The Casino Box stores [[coin]]s and [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s like the [[Coin Box (Mario Party series)|Coin Box]], but after every game, it gives the player a chance to win more or lose some coins by roulette. If the player gets "x2", the coin amount deposited is doubled. If the player gets "−1/2", half the deposited coins are spat out. Getting "0" does nothing. The Casino Box can hold a maximum of 99,999 coins and 100 Stars.

It can be bought in the [[Toad's Shop|Mushroom Shop]] for 300 coins.
