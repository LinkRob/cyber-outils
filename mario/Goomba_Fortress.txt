'''Goomba Fortress''' is the sixth and final level of World 1 in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Layout==
Goomba Fortress is very large in size. It has many rooms, traps, and puzzles. It is also mainly brown in color. 

==History==
Mario and [[Kersti]] finally reach Goomba Fortress where they know where one of the [[Royal Sticker]]s is found. Ready to meet them at the entry are two [[Goomba]]s, though they are quickly vanquished. After battling through many Goombas and Bomb-ombs, they finally encounter the first boss in the entire game: the [[Megasparkle Goomba]]. The Megasparkle Goomba gains it's strength with the Royal Sticker on its head. Mario manages to defeat this very tough boss and acquire the first Royal Sticker in the game. However, his journey is only just beginning.

==Boss Battle==
[[Megasparkle Goomba]] has a multitude of attacks. He can fold into a cylinder and attempt to roll over Mario. After performing that attack, he rolls himself into a cone. This hurts Mario if he jumps on him. He will also send the individual Goombas who make him up at Mario. 

Eventually, Mario damages the Megasparkle Goomba to a point that it becomes visibly damaged. If Mario uses the [[Scissors]] then the boss fight ends there (almost). Otherwise Mario is forced to do battle longer. Eventually, the individual Goombas abandon the Megasparkle Goomba and leave. The boss attempts to continue the battle despite being on 1 life, but is inevitably vanquished.

==Luigi Sighting==
[[Luigi]] can be found in this level and he is located in the background of the bridge leading to the Megasparkle Goomba. Mario can [[Paperize]] Luigi out of the background.
