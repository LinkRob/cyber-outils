}}The '''Sky Station Galaxy''' is a galaxy in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is the very first galaxy in the game. [[Mario]] is launched there before going to [[Starship Mario]] and meeting [[Lubba]]. [[Yoshi's House]] is there, but [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is not home. The first [[Luma]] says that a [[Bowser|monster]] stole all the [[Power Star]]s.


==Missions==
===Peewee Piranha's Temper Tantrum===
In this level, Mario traverses through several planets to get to a planet with a giant egg containing [[Peewee Piranha]]. Once Mario defeats Peewee Piranha, he gets a Power Star.

===Storming the Sky Fleet===
This takes Mario on a journey across skyships riddled with [[Banzai Bill]]s. Mario must then defeat a [[Spiky Piranha Plant]] in order to collect the star.

===Peewee Piranha's Speed Run===
This is the same as Peewee Piranha's Temper Tantrum, except there is now a timer, and Mario or [[Luigi]] must collect clocks to increase the amount of time they have left. The [[Comet Medal]] in Peewee Piranha's Temper Tantrum must be collected before this level can be accessed.

===Green Star Missions===
====Green Star 1====
The first [[Green Power Star|Green Star]] is floating near the roof of Yoshi's House. It is accessible from Peewee Piranha's Temper Tantrum.

====Green Star 2====
The second Green Star is also in the first mission. It is found on the second planet, behind the Launch Star.

====Green Star 3====
The third Green Star is located in the second planet in Storming The Sky Fleet, just above the teleporter.

==Trivia==

*In 2010's Official Nintendo Magazine, the [[Yoshi Star Galaxy]] is mistaken as the Sky Station Galaxy.
*The Yoshi House Planet is similar to the first planet in the [[Good Egg Galaxy]], as both are the first planets encountered in their respective galaxies. In addition, each planet has a bright and dark side, houses are located on the top halves of both planets, and both planets seem to have their top and bottom halves divided exactly in half, access between which is made possible by green [[Warp Pipe]]s on both planets.
*The sign that tells Mario to go around the back in the Peewee Piranha's Speed Run mission on the Yoshi House Planet calls Yoshi a space dragon.
*When Mario flies to the galaxy the first time the first mission is played, he is flying uncontrollably. If replayed, Mario is flying smoothly.
*A portion of Sky Station Galaxy's music is remixed and used in [[Cloudtop Cruise]]'s music in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. Additionally, the storm cloud section of the course has a blend of the World 6 Map's theme from ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
