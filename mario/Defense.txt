'''Defense''' is a common stat that appears in the series mainly the RPGs. As such, it first appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
{{sectstub}}

===''Mario and Luigi'' series===
{{sectstub}}
In the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]], defense decreases the amount of damage that a character takes.

===''Paper Mario'' series===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', defense decreases the amount of damage taken from most attacks by the number of points in defense.

In this series, Mario's default defense is zero. Leveling up Mario does not increase his defense in any way. Mario can increase his defense by wearing a [[Defend Plus]] badge that raises his defense by one point. 

Mario's partners have 0 defense except for [[Kooper]], [[Parakarry]], and [[Koops]] as they are all [[Koopa Troopa]]s that always have a point of defense. 

Using certain items such as [[Courage Shell]]s also raise defense but only for a few or so turns. Certain abilities such as [[Sushie]]'s [[Water Block]]] and Koops' [[Shell Shield]] also raises defense for Mario only.
