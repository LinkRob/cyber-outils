'''Arantulas''' are enemies featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They possess a target-like symbol on their back.

==History==
Arantulas are significantly more power variants of [[Pider]]s, and are located in some of the lowest levels of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]], levels 81 through 89. Arantulas are capable of dealing massive amounts of damage a turn that can add up to 21 from a single attack turn. These creatures also have much more health points, 16, putting Arantulas in the top five most powerful non-boss enemies in the game.

Aside from their higher stats, Arantulas are overall very similar to Piders during battle.
