The '''Mushroom Derby''' is a race that takes place on [[Yo'ster Isle]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Mostly, only [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] participate in the Mushroom Derby (however, the Yoshis may have riders accompany them during the race). A Yoshi can boost its speed with a [[Yoshi Cookie]]. The winner also gets cookies rewarded.

During the race, [[Mario]] rides on [[Yoshi]]'s back, helping him defeat [[Boshi]]. To beat Boshi to the finish line, Yoshi has to move to the beat of the derby's music. As the music plays, the player must alternate between {{button|snes|A}} and {{button|snes|B}} with the music's tempo. If the player presses the buttons too fast, Yoshi does not move. As the song plays, the beat may stop or change. The player must continue pushing the buttons to the original beat, not the new beat, however. Additionally, if Mario has any additional Yoshi Cookies, he can feed them to Yoshi, giving him a boost of speed.

In the ending sequence of the game, [[Croco]] can be seen racing with Yoshi in the Mushroom Derby.
