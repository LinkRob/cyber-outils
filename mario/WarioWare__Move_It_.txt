'''''WarioWare: Move It!''''', titled {{Nihongo|'''''Super Dancing Made In Wario'''''|超おどる メイド イン ワリオ|Chō odoru meido in wario}} in Japan, is a {{series|WarioWare}} game for the [[Nintendo Switch]]. It was the second and final ''WarioWare'' game released for the platform, following after ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''. It was first revealed in a [[Nintendo Direct]] on June 21, 2023, and the game was released worldwide on November 3, 2023.

==Gameplay==
Playing the microgames each require the use of a specific "form" making use of the [[Joy-Con]] motion controls, similarly to the [[Form Baton]]'s functionality in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

== Trivia ==
*On August 21, 2023, It was announced that [[Charles Martinet]] would retire from doing voice work in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, so this is the first time in the {{series|WarioWare}} that [[Kevin Afghani]] voices [[Wario]]. Afghani also voices Mario in the [[Super Mario 64 DS (microgame)|Super Mario 64 DS]] [[microgame]].
