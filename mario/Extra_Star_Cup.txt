The '''Extra Star Cup''' is the fourth [[Extra Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. It consists of tracks in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. It is unlocked by completing the [[Star Cup]] with 100 coins and a 1st place win on the second try and onwards.

*[[Bowser Castle 3]]
*[[Mario Circuit 4]]
