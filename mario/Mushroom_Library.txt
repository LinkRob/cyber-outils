The '''Mushroom Library''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Mushroom Library appears in the [[Seaside Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. There are two wooden bookshelves in the background, although the one on the left is only partly onscreen. There are several books, with covers of varying colors, and they line up not just in the bookshelves but also against the wooden desk. Aside from the prominence of books, there is a chalkboard hanging on the yellow, diamond-patterned wall in the background.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
[[Mechakoopa (character)|Mechakoopa]], the only known employee of the Mushroom Library, spends a lot of his time filling the blackboard with his mathematical theories.

When the player's character enters the Mushroom Library, they find Mechakoopa reading through one of the library books. Talking to Mechakoopa initiates the [[Mathemagician!]] quest, which involves him quizzing the player with a few arithmetic questions, and the reward for answering every one of them correctly is the [[Castle Night]] [[Gaddget]].
