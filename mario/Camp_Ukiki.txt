'''Camp Ukiki''' is a [[Duel Minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
Camp Ukiki has a split-screen view, and the goal for both characters is to be the first to reach the end of an obstacle course. The first set of obstacles are several tall stakes forming a maze that has to be navigated through. The second section is a series of platforms that must be jumped from for the character to reach a zip-line that takes them to the next area. The third obstacle is a pair of paths, one of which is a walkway with a short ramp and the other is a small group of platforms. If a character falls off, a [[Lakitu]] comes to their rescue and returns them back to the last gate they passed through. Whoever reaches the end first is the winner. If neither character makes it to the end within five minutes, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Controls==
*Control Stick: Move
*A button: Jump
