'''Kate Fleming''' (born October 6, 1965 - December 14, 2006) was an American actress, artist, singer, and award-winning audiobook narrator and producer. She voiced [[Princess Daisy]] in [[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]] for the [[Nintendo 64]]. She died on December 14, 2006 when a flash flood trapped her in her basement studio.
