The '''portrait ghosts''' (sometimes capitalized<ref>''Luigi's Mansion'' instruction manual, page 25.</ref>) or '''gallery ghosts'''<ref>''Luigi's Mansion Nintendo Power Player's Guide'', various pages</ref> are ghosts that were once portraits in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]''. Outside of this game and [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|its remake]], some of the portrait ghosts reappear in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series in {{classic course|GCN|[[Luigi's Mansion (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Luigi's Mansion]]}} and {{classic-link|DS|[[Luigi's Mansion (Mario Kart DS)|Luigi's Mansion]]}}, which were replaced with generic [[Boo]] portraits in later appearances.

==History==
According to [[Professor E. Gadd]], the first twenty one portrait ghosts and [[Boolossus]] (No. 22)<ref>"''The last ghost I caught was No. 22, {{color|Boolossus|peru}}, but there are lots of Boos, and {{color|King Boo|peru}} rallied them all to take revenge upon me!''" — Professor E. Gadd, ''Luigi's Mansion''</ref> were released from their paintings by [[Boo]]s under the command of [[King Boo]]. [[Luigi]] has to vacuum up all of the portrait ghosts (except for a few "optional ghosts" like [[Mr. Luggs]]) using the [[Poltergust 3000]]. Captured portrait ghosts are returned to their paintings via the [[Ghost Portrificationizer]]. The frame and the style of the portraits change depending on how Luigi fares against the ghost.

Sepia tone pictures can be found in many rooms of the mansion. These pictures look similar to each ghost that inhabits the room they are in, suggesting these pictures may be depicting these ghosts when they were alive. Pictures resembling [[Neville]], [[Lydia]], [[Nana]], [[Vincent Van Gore]], [[Spooky (character)|Spooky]], [[Chauncey]], [[The Floating Whirlindas|Mr. Whirlinda]], [[Slim Bankshot]], [[Henry and Orville]], and [[Sue Pea]] can be seen in the [[Parlor (Luigi's Mansion)|Parlor]], [[Study (Luigi's Mansion)|Study]], [[Nursery]], [[Ball Room]], [[Billiards Room]], [[The Twins' Room|Twins' Room]], and [[Guest Room]].

All of the portraits were given new designs in the [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|Nintendo 3DS remake]].
