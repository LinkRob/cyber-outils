The '''''Game & Watch Gallery'' series''' is a series of handheld games by [[Nintendo]] each consisting of a few [[Game & Watch]] games. Its first installment is ''[[Game Boy Gallery]]'', released in 1995 exclusively in Europe and Australia, but the second one and first one to feature ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters is ''[[Game & Watch Gallery]]'', released in 1997. Since then, all subsequent ''Game & Watch Gallery'' installments also have ''Mario'' characters.
