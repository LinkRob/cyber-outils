The '''Deluxe Feast''' is a [[recipe]] item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
The Deluxe Feast can be created by using either a combination of a [[Potato Salad]] and a [[Shroom Steak]] or a [[Whacka Bump]] and a [[Strange Leaf]]. This item can heal a massive 40 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 40 [[Flower Point|FP]] making it one of the best healing items in the game second only to the [[Jelly Ultra]].
