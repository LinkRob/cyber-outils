The '''Lucky House Host''' is a character making her debut appearance in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. She is the owner of the [[Lucky House]]s that can be found. More honorable than your average Casino manager, this character can be visited by those who wish to play in the Lucky House. Whenever the character gets matches, the host will give out coins based on the results:
*No matches: 10 coins
*1 pair: 100 coins
*2 pairs: 200 coins
*3-of-a-kind set: 300 coins
*4-of-a-kind set: 777 coins
