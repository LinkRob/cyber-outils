'''Mona's Pig''', also named '''Mona's Piggy''', is a character who first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. It is one of the [[Yum Yums]], being a pet of [[Mona]]'s.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mona's Pig is a small pig who wears a light yellow shirt with a red "2" on it.

===Behavior===
Like the other Yum-Yums, Mona's Pig assists Mona whenever she gets caught within roadside conflict. Mona's Pig kicks soccer balls as his method of attack.
