'''Rolls for Moles''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Gameplay==
There are three rounds, and for each one, the characters have to time their shots to hit the most [[Monty Mole]]s in a row with one ball. The Monty Moles have a pattern of jumping straight up before landing back into their hole. A character earns a [[point]] for each Monty Mole hit. If a player scores a perfect, a Monty Mole hits the screen. Whoever has the most points by the end is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|3DS|A}}: Roll
