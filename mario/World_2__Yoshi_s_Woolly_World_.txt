''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''|levels=9|prewor=[[World 1 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 1]]|nxtwor=[[World 3 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 3]]}}'''World 2''' is the second world of ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. It is a desert-themed world. It has nine levels: eight are compulsory to complete, but there is one special, optional level (called [[Perils of the Perplexing Pyramid]]) after collecting all 40 [[Smiley Flower|Smiley Flowers]] for the world. Completing the world unlocks [[World 3 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 3]].
