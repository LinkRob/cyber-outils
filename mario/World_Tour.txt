The '''World Tour''' is the main game mode in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. 

== Description ==
Similar to ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', the goal is to rescue Princess Peach. 

On each level, special coins can be collected, there are five special coin per level, the coins starts as pink, then after collecting all the five coins of the level in one go, the coins change colors (purple) and places, and then again in black.

After defeating Bowser at [[Bowser's Bob-ombing Run|World 6-4]], Princess Peach becomes a playable character and the World ★ mode can be unlocked after Princess Peach saved.

== Worlds ==
There are 6 worlds and 4 levels in each world, the last levels of each worlds are the airships, dungeons, and castles. The first three levels are free, the fourth level requires either to buy the full game or to complete a certain challenge while the rest of the worlds and levels must be unlocked for $9.99.
