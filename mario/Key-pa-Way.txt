'''Key-pa-Way''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party]]''. Its name and gameplay are based on the real-life game {{wp|keep away|Keep Away}} (a [[key]] is used in the minigame, rather than a ball). This is one of the two minigames in ''Mario Party'' that require four-way cooperation (the other being [[Running of the Bulb]]) and the only minigame where all four players win or lose together.

==Gameplay==
All the players must team up to win this minigame; one player gets a key, and the rest of the players have to intercept the key like a team. If the player with the key makes it to the key lock, all the players win, earning ten [[Coin]]s. If one of the five [[Spike Koopa]]s gets the key or the timer runs out, the minigame ends and all players lose five Coins.

The minigame can be purchased from the [[Mini-Game House]] for 400 Coins when it is available for purchase.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|Stick}} &ndash; Move
* {{button|n64|A}} &ndash; [[Jump]]
* {{button|n64|B}} &ndash; Throw key
