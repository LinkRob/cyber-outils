A '''Screwtop Lift''', also named '''Screwtop Platform''', is a type of [[lift]] which first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''

==Description==
A Screwtop Lift has a screw on it that needs to be spun to activate it so that it moves. A Screwtop Lift moves in a set direction when spun on, and the direction that it spins is represented by a [[track]] symbolizing its movement path. If a character is no longer on a Screwtop Lift, it slowly spins back to its original position.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
Screwtop Lifts only appear in {{world-link|8|airship|World 8-Airship (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)}}, although they appear throughout the level.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' / ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' / ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
Screwtop Lifts appear in all three games, retaining their functionality from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''. In ''New Super Mario Bros. U'', they appear in [[Screwtop Tower]], and are used in the challenge [[Screwtop It Up]], taking place in the same level. In ''New Super Luigi U'', Screwtop Lifts appear in [[Sumo Bro's Spinning Tower]].
