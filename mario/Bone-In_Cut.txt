==Effects==
Eating a Bone-In Cut doubles Attack for a short time. They only way to obtain Bone-In Cuts are to defeat [[Muth]]s in the [[Land of the Cragnons]] which may drop the items. Bone-In Cuts can be cooked by itself for the [[Spit Roast]] which doubles attack for a longer period of time or be combined with the [[Power Steak]] for a [[Gorgeous Steak]].
