'''World 3-5''' is a level from [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]].''

== Overview ==
It is unlocked the first time that [[World 3-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3-4]] is completed. Taking place in a storm, this is a vertical level which auto-scrolls when all players step on the rotating pink block. Completing this level unlocks the [[! Switch]] on the World 3 Map. There are hidden blocks in Worlds 3-4 and 3-5 which turn into red blocks when this switch is activated.

This course only needs to be completed once to progress in the game, but must be completed twice in order to collect all of the [[Star Coin]]s and access the level's secret exit (except in multiplayer - see "''[[#Secret Exit|§ Secret Exit]]''").
== Walkthrough ==
The normal way to beat this auto-scrolling level is standing on the rotating pink block, while [[jump]]ing when needed and avoiding [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Venus Fire Trap]]s. There are [[Propeller Mushroom]]s, [[Propeller Block]]s, and [[Ice Block]]s available to assist the players, along with Red Blocks which only appear after activating the [[! Switch]] in the World 3 Map. At the pink block elevator is a green [[Warp Pipe]] leading to another rotating pink block and the [[Flagpole]]. By turning on the ! Switch on the World 3 map and using a Propeller Mushroom, it is possible to beat this level without using the rotating pink block. However, the Red Flagpole cannot be reached that way.

== [[Star Coin]]s ==
The first Star Coin is always available, while the second and third ones are only available if the ! Switch is activated.
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is floating to the far right of the first Venus Fire Trap. Mario and co. can obtain it with a [[Propeller Mushroom]] or [[Propeller Block]], or by jumping at just the right time so as to avoid falling.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Instead of taking the green exit pipe at the end of the level, Mario and co. should jump up from the pink block to the red block platform, then up to the red pipe. In the next section, the second Star Coin is on top of many [[Ice Block]]s, guarded by a [[Red Koopa Troopa]].
*'''Star Coin 3''': Not far from the second Star Coin, the third Star Coin is floating in midair, guarded by four Koopa Paratroopas. Mario and co. should jump to the Star Coin while avoiding the Paratroopas.

==Secret Exit==
World 3-5 has a secret exit which is only accessible after the ! Switch has been activated, meaning the level must be played through twice to access it (except in multiplayer-see below). This hidden path contains the level's second and third [[Star Coin]]s. When the players reach the top of the first pink block elevator, they can jump onto a platform of red blocks to access a red Warp Pipe. The players must then ascend another pink block elevator while avoiding more Koopa Troopas and Paratroopas and collecting the remaining two Star Coins. Once the block reaches the top of the level, the red flag is located immediately to the right. This exit unlocks a yellow [[Toad House]].
