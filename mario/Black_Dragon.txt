The '''Black Dragon''' is an item which appears in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
The Black Dragon is one of nine items that [[Wario]] can purchase from the [[shop owner]] at the [[Item Shop (Wario Land 4)|Item Shop]] before facing a boss. The Black Dragon is a one-time item, and it bears a similar design to the [[Black Dog]].

When Wario starts a boss battle, the shop owner himself transforms into the Black Dragon to [[Fire Breath|breathe fire]] at the boss, damaging it. After doing so, the Black Dragon then leaves, although Wario can defeat the boss more easily because of the damage the Black Dragon dealt to it.

The Black Dog costs ten [[Medal (Wario Land 4)|Medals]] in Normal difficulty, fifteen in Hard difficulty, and twenty in S-Hard difficulty.
