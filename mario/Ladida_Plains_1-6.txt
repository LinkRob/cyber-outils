'''Ladida Plains 1-6 '''is the sixth level of [[Ladida Plains]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. This doesn't need much skills but shoot her up without hitting any enemies. Use the {{button|ds|Stylus}} to pull the vine down and shoot her up a different.

== Enemies ==
* [[Sad Paragoomba]]
* [[Nipper Plant]]
* [[Petey Piranha]] (boss)

== Battle ==
{{Main|Petey Piranha}}
Petey Piranha first appears to rise up and eat the kidnapped Toad in the bubble, then he starts to howl at Peach. According to Perry, its weakness is to gloom tears on him to fill its tummy with water; while weaken pound its weak spot, belly button.

He howls at Peach first, then he makes fast whips with his two arms. He starts to open his mouth, you still have time to pour water on it! When you are too slow enough to pour tears on Petey Piranha, he hurls out [[Nipper Plant]]s.

When enraged, he turns hue, then red. He starts to jump up and down. He then opens his mouth the opposite way where Peach faces him.

After he is finished off, he starts to throw up the Toad inside the bubble, turns back to his normal colors, turns into a white silhouette and explodes.{{SPPNav}}
