The '''Ramenses II''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
The Ramenses II is a living statue enemy that can be only found in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]]. When it sees [[Wario]] approaching, it levitates in the air and attempts to slam down on him as it's means of attacking. Wario can defeat the Ramenses II by stomping on it's head several times.
