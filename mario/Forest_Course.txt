The '''Forest Course''' is a golf course in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''. It is the first [[Castle Club]] golf course. It is located in a forest, with many multi-colored hills in the background. Most of the holes have little to no hazards. The main hazards are bunkers, trees, and in some holes, water. Collecting all the Star Coins for this course's challenges awards the player with a [[Daisy]] costume.

== Challenges ==
=== Star Coin challenges ===
'''Star Coin Collector 1'''

Hole: 6

Location: The star Coin is located between 2 trees.

Procedure: Collect the Fire Flower item and use it to let the golf ball go through the 2 trees, so the ball can continue moving toward the green.

'''Star Coin Collector 2'''

Hole: 10

Location: The star Coin is located above one of the Bunkers.

Procedure: Once collecting the Tornado Item from the first stroke, it can be used to collect the Star Coin without having the ball landing in the bunker.

'''Ring Master 1'''

Hole: 9

Number of Rings: 3

Procedure: If 2 of the rings are inside the Green, and 1 ring is outside the Green, the ball must collect the ring outside the Green first. If the ball stays outside the Green, an Approach shot can be done to collect the last 2 rings.

'''Ring Master 2'''

Hole: 13

Number of Rings: 4

Procedure: The Ball must be shot in a straight direction about 2 times. Characters with a Draw or Fade ball direction is a little tricky to do, but moving the cursor while the character is about to swing at the ball, reforming the direction into a straight shot. They shouldn't be too hard to attempts as both of the rings are located half underground, half visible.

'''Ring Master 3'''

Hole: 5

Number of Rings: 2

Procedure: The first stroke can go through the first ring, and the second stroke can go through the second ring if the ball goes far enough.

'''100 Coins'''

Hole(s): 4 (Par 5/3 Strokes), 7 (Par 4/2 Strokes), 17 (Par 3/ 1 Stroke)

'''Score Attack'''

Hole(s)': 1-9 (First half of Forest Course).

Quota Reach: Complete nine holes with an overall score of -3 or better.

'''Character Match'''

Challenge '''Daisy''' to a round of match play.

'''Time Attack'''

Hole(s): 3, 11, 16

Procedure: Complete 3 given holes within 130 seconds (2 Minuets and 10 Seconds)

'''Point Challenge'''

Hole(s): 6, 14, 18

Procedure: Play 3 given holes using the Club Slots. To clear the challenge, the player must score 6 points.
