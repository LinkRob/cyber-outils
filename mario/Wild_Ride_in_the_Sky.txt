'''Wild Ride in the Sky''' is one of the [[World-e]] levels in both the Japanese and North American versions of ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. In direct [[reissue|ports]] of the game for the [[Wii U]] [[Virtual Console]] and [[Nintendo Switch Online]], the level is included in the base game in all regions, along with other e-Reader content.

Wild Ride in the Sky is an auto-scrolling level taking place in the sky, and [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] has to use various types of platforms along the way, including a series of clouds, [[Lift]]s automatically moving on [[track]]s, and some [[Donut Block]]s. The only enemies are a few [[Red Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Bullet Bill]]s that fire diagonally in indefinite numbers.
