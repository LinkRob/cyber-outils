'''John Alberto Leguizamo Peláez''' is an American actor, comedian, and film producer who has done voice work for the ''[[Super Mario Bros. (film)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' produced by Hollywood Pictures. He portrayed [[Luigi (film)|Luigi]] in the 1993 live-action film ''[[Super Mario Bros. (film)|Super Mario Bros.]]''.

== History ==
John Leguizamo is best known for portraying character roles, such as Sid the Sloth, from ''Ice Age'' and Bruno Madrigal, from ''Encanto''.
