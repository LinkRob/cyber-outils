'''Babs''' is a character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Babs is a female [[Flip-Flopian]] that is lime green in color.

==Biography==
Babs lives in [[Yold Town]] of [[Lineland]]. Babs has a fondness for shopping and is known to be quite helpful in town.
