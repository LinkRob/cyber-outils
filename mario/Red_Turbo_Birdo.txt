The '''Red Turbo Birdo''' is a red [[Turbo Birdo]] that made its debut in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' in the [[Princess Tour (2023)|Princess Tour]].

== Description ==
The Red Turbo Birdo is a red color scheme of a [[Turbo Birdo]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
[[File:MKT Princess Tour Peach Circuit.png|thumb|left|[[Birdo (Red)]] driving in GBA [[Peach Circuit]].]]
The Red Turbo Birdo made its debut in the Princess Tour.
