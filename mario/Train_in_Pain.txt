'''Train in Pain''' is a [[Team Minigame|team]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
Team players are on vehicles that are standing on two trains. The teams arrive at the square arena and moves forward.

== Gameplay ==
The vehicle has a punching glove that springs out. The team players must hit their rival's allies. Allies cheer while the rival flies off the screen. Hitting the player or other rivals not on the rear gets stunned. The team that remains standing wins the minigame.

== Ending ==
The team pose their victory while the other team poses their loss.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – Punch
