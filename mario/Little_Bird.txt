'''Little Birds''' are a species which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. Since then, they have frequently made background appearances in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, although sometimes serve a gameplay purpose. Little Birds are small, white birds which are very similar to birds in real life.
