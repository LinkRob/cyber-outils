'''Dart Attack''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is available from the start. The Gaddget's name is a pun on the phrase "heart attack."

==Description==
Upon starting the Gaddget, the player starts with four darts, each having to be thrown at the target the instant it appears.

Dart Attack is also in [[Bonus Board]] mode, where each player has to control one of the four darts.
