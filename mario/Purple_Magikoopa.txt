A''' Purple Magikoopa '''is an enemy that only appears in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. His main attribute is Dark and has no sub attribute.

==In ''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''==
Purple Magikoopas appear only in World 8, and in only 4 levels total. They may show up in 8-Tower 1, 8-Tower 2, 8-Tower 3, and 8-Bowser's Castle. In battle, they normally attack every turn for a fairly low amount of damage, or they may use "Dark Trick" to turn all Light and Heart [[Orb (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|Orbs]] to Dark Orbs. When defeated, they may drop a Grand Dark Block containing themselves, 100 [[Coin]]s, or if defeated with 10 or more combos, will drop a Spiky Shell. 

As an ally, the Purple Magikoopa has good stats but no sub-attribute, but has the skill Dark Trick, which does the same thing it did as an enemy skill; turning Light and Heart Orbs into Dark Orbs. His awakening is Skill Boost, giving 1 turn of skill change at the start of a stage, per level of the awakening.

After the 2.0 Update, he gained 3 separate evolutions that involve him teaming up with another Magikoopa. In this case, he can now evolve into Purple & Red Magikoopa, Purple & Yellow Magikoopa, or Purple Magikoopas. These evolutions require 6 [[Fire Flower]]s, 6 [[Super Mushroom]]s, and 6 [[Boo Mushroom]]s respectively, and they increase stats a bit, gain a sub attribute according to their partner, get a different awakening than Skill Boost, depending on which evolution is chosen, Twin Target for Red and Yellow and Dark Boost for dual Purple. Their skill does not change when evolved, and they require Spiky Shells to skill up.
