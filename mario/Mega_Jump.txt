'''Mega Jump''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
At the cost of three [[Badge Point|BP]], Mario can wear the Mega Jump to perform a more powerful version of [[Power Jump]] in battle for six [[Flower Point|FP]].

Mario can obtain this badge on his way through [[Shiver Mountain]]. It is found in a red [[Question Block]] found on Mario's way up several flights of crystal stairs.
