'''Luigi Raceway''' (also called '''Luigi Circuit ルイージ回路 '''in Japan) is the first track in the [[Mushroom Cup]] in the game, ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. It is the most basic course as it has no hazards whatsoever, being a simple course featuring a straightaway, two wide turns and a curve through a tunnel. A staff ghost can be unlocked in [[Time Trial]]s. In the Grand Prix, a [[Spiny Shell]] can be obtained by jumping at the [[Item Box]] on the balloon. It is the prequel to [[Mario Raceway]] and [[Royal Raceway]], and has the same music.

This course reappears as a retro course in ''[[Mario Kart 7]] ''as it is the first course in the [[Shell Cup]].
