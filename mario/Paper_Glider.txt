The '''Paper Glider''' is a type of [[glider]] featured in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. In the former, it was associated with the ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' series, but the latter removes it in favor of making it a generic red glider.
