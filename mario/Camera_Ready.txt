'''Camera Ready''' is a [[Microgame]] that hails from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' It uses the Fashionista form for its gameplay, and [[Wario]] has it in his second set, involved in Lava at First Sight.

== Layout ==
A [[Fashion Model (WarioWare: Move It!)|girl who wears a dress, fancy heels, and a Wario nose-and-moustache mask]] is posing for the camera on a stage, and some folks of random sexes and wearing random outfits are cheering from in front of the stage.

== Gameplay ==
The player has to turn their body so the girl can face the camera to get her picture taken. If the girl is photographed, the player wins, but if she is not photographed, they lose.

=== Difficulty Levels ===
The number of persons in the crowd increases with the difficulty, and the girl has a different look depending on the difficulty level:

* '''Level 1 (Easy)''': The girl is wearing a white dress and has brown hair in a ponytail. There are few persons in the crowd, and none of them are moving around a lot.
* '''Level 2 (Hard)''': More persons are in the crowd, with some jumping and/or waving around (The cameraman always stands still.), and the girl is an African American who has grey hair in a ponytail in a dark blue dress.
* '''Level 3 (Expert)''': The crowd has many persons, with most jumping and/or waving around (The cameraman always stands still.), and some have gift boxes to throw the player off. This level portrays the girl as white-haired with twin pigtails and wearing a black dress with a golden hem.
