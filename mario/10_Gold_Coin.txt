The '''10 Gold Coin''' is an item which appears in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and ''[[Wario Land II]]''. It is worth ten individual [[coin]]s.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
A 10 Gold Coin can be created from [[Wario]]'s inventory from ten regular coins and thrown at enemies to attack them.

==In ''Wario Land II''==
10 Gold Coins now depict Wario's face from the side, and is larger than other collectible coins. The 10 Gold Coin can no longer be used as a projectile. Given that it is more valuable, a 10 Gold Coin is often hidden in hard-to-reach areas.
