'''Key Doors''' are [[door]]s that cannot be unlocked without the use of a [[key]]. Locked Doors usually have the appearance of a regular [[Warp Door]] with a hole.

== In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Bros. 2''===
Key Doors make their first appearance in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', first debut in [[World 1-2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1-2]]. The Key Door can only be opened by [[Key]]s. If the player collects an key for a Key Door, the [[Phanto]] will chase the player.

===''Super Mario 64'' / ''Super Mario 64 DS''===
The keys in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' are used to open the doors that lead to the courses of the game, and in the [[Super Mario 64 DS|DS remake]], Key Doors that hold [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Wario]], and an secret [[Power Star]].

===''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''===
While Key Doors did not appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', but they appeared in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', in the World-e-exclusive level [[Doors o' Plenty]].

===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Key Doors did appear in the [[Ghostly Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', where they automatically open as soon as Mario or Luigi obtains a key. In the case of the "[[Matter Splatter Mansion]]" mission, multiple doors can be not locked at one time with single key.

===''Super Mario Maker'' series===
Key Doors were introduced in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' in the version 1.40 update, and available at the start in ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]''. The key doors can be shaking a [[P Warp Door]] which shaking a [[Warp Door]]). The key doors also appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', behaving as in the previous game. As of version 3.0.0 of ''Super Mario Maker 2'', [[Cursed Key]]s can also be used to unlock the doors.

===''Super Mario Run''===
Key Doors reappear in [[Boohind Lock and Key]], where the doors can only be unlocked with keys. Once it is opened, Key Doors did not open again. They take after the doors of the Ghost House from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', but with a peephole in the base. 

==In ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''==
Key Doors reappear again in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'', similar in previous ''Super Mario'' games.
