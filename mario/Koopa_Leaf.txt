The '''Koopa Leaf''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Found on [[Pleasant Path]] and in some bushes and trees in [[Koopa Village]], the Koopa Leaf is a leaf that restores three of [[Mario]]'s [[Flower Point|FP]] when used. This item, by itself, isn't as effective as a healing item, but can be cooked by itself by [[Tayce T.]] to make [[Koopa Tea]]. The Koopa Leaf is also  used in other recipes mainly as other Koopa-related dishes. In both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Koopa Leaves do not appear, and are instead replaced with the similar [[Turtley Leaf|Turtley Leaves]].
