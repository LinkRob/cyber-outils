'''Fit for a Frame''' is [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player must take a photo of the people at the right moment.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The player must take a photo of a boy at a graduation. The photo must be taken while the boy does not have mucus hanging from his nose.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The player must take a photo of the married couple at the wedding. The photo must be taken while the bride is not sneezing.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The player must take a photo of two men holding a trophy. The photo must be taken while the man on the left is not sleeping and while the man on the right is not having chills.
