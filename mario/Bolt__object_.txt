'''Bolts''' are an object which first appear in the [[arcade]] game ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]''.

==Description==
Bolts are objects that keep the platforms of [[100m]] and similar locations intact.

==In ''Donkey Kong''==
Bolts are objects that [[Mario]] has to destroy in 100m. There are eight orange bolts (six in the [[Game Boy]] [[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|version]]) that are worth 100 [[point]]s each. Removing a bolt only requires Mario to either walk or [[jump]] across it. Removing a bolt also creates a gap that [[Fire (enemy)|Fire]]s cannot cross, and if a bolt is removed while a Fire is on top of it, the Fire becomes stuck over the gap. This allows Mario to safely jump over the Fire repeatedly to gain points.

Once Mario has walked over every bolt, the platforms supporting [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] collapse, ending the game.

==In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''==
[[File:MvsDK2 Bolt.png|frame|left]]
On level B1 of [[DK's Hideout]], [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Mario]]s have to remove bolts around the stage to defeat Donkey Kong, similarly to Mario in 100m. They do so by walking over, jumping across, or standing on top of a bolt, which then melts away and disappears.
