|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}'''Tubba Blubba's Castle''' is a very large castle that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is home to the "invincible" [[Tubba Blubba]]. This level also contains many enemies called [[Clubba]]s.

==Description==
===Exterior===
The outside of Tubba Blubba's Castle is very large in size. It has a very large door that is even bigger than Tubba Blubba himself.

===Interior===
The inside of Tubba Blubba's Castle appears to be in very bad shape as everything seems to be cracked. Tubba Blubba's Castle has many rooms which includes:
*'''Foyer''': The first room in Tubba Blubba's Castle. There is many enemies in this room called [[Sentinel]]s that will pick up Mario and carry him out the castle. Mario must use Bow to hide from them when they get close.
*'''Hallways''': There are many hallways in Tubba Blubba's Castle that usually lead to many other rooms. At one point, Tubba Blubba appears in the hallway and Mario must use [[Bow]]'s ability to hide from him. Mario must also stay alert of the Sentinels that patrol certain hallways.
*'''Basement''': The basement is located directly below the Castle. Mario heads down there to get the key in order to proceed further into the castle. There is also a [[Boo]] hiding down there telling Mario that he is hiding from Tubba Blubba. He also tells Mario that the secret to Tubba Blubba's weakness is somewhere in his room.
*'''Tubba Blubba's Room''': This is where Tubba Blubba sleeps. This room has very large objects including a bed. Mario comes here to get the [[Yakkey|Key]] which opens the [[Windy Mill]] and leads to [[Tubba Blubba's Heart]]; the secret of Tubba Blubba's weakness.
