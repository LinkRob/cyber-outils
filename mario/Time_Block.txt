A '''Time Block''' is an object which only appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

==Description==
{{Infobox_Object|image=Time Block.png|caption=The Time Block|first=[[Super Mario Run]] (2016)}}
A Time Block is a purple [[block]] with a go around dial on the front. If [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or another character hits a Time Block, more time is added to the [[time limit]], and the Time Block becomes an [[Empty Block]].

==In ''Super Mario Run''==
A Time Block is rare, and it only appears in the levels [[Sky-High Lifts and Leaps!]], [[Cutting-Edge Spire]], [[Boohind Lock and Key]], [[Switch Ghost House]], [[Scuttlebug Forest]], and [[Scaredy Rat Race]].
