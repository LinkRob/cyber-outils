}}''{{world|6|ghosthousen2}}'' is the second level of World 6 in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. 
==Layout==
This Ghost House is a difficult maze, similar to the Ghost House found in [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. Mario must press the ? Switch at the end of the room and go in the right door. Then, the player must head right until they see a door. Mario should go inside. What looks like the same room is actually different, as the [[Big Boo]] has 'turned into' a [[Boo]]. Mario must then press the ? Switch and head left. When Mario gets to the first part of the room, he should lure away the Boo from its position, and press the ? Switch that it was hiding. Some stairs will then be visible, and Mario must climb them. At the top is a door that Mario must enter. Then the player will be in a room with the second Star Coin. Mario should not enter the door located on the bottom of the room. He should instead wait and head back up for a block that has a ? Switch on it, on the left side. When it is pressed, a path is made to the exit door. 
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' At the end of the first room, the player must hit the ? Switch. They should then fall down, head right, hit the Brick Block and press another ? Switch. Up ahead is the first coin.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' In the third area, The player must continue left and find a ? switch to reveal the path to the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' In the second area (after leaving the third area), the player should continue to the right and find a ? switch to reveal a hidden door that leads to the third Star Coin.
==Secret exit==
After the player hits the ? Switch in the room that slides opens the ceiling leading to the third [[Star Coin]], they must wall jump up to the star coin, but instead of heading back down, keep wall jumping for about 3 screens and they will come to a door leading to the secret exit. If the walls are closed after the ? Switch wears off, he will be forced to go to the secret exit.

The Secret Exit unlocks [[World 6-A (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6-A]].
