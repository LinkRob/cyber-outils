The '''Vacuum Shroom''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
The Vacuum Shroom is a plot-essential item given to [[Bowser]] by [[Fawful]] shortly before Bowser's journey begins. When Bowser obtains the Vacuum Shroom, the game text calls it '''Lucky(?) Mushroom'''. It causes him to vacuum things into his mouth uncontrollably, including [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Starlow]], setting the premise for the game.
