|games = ''Wario: Master of Disguise''}}'''Sneezemore Cave''' is a location that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Layout==
Sneezemore Cave is a very large, icy cave with many brightly covered crystals around. There are also plenty of red blocks and other objects here that require [[Dragon Wario]] to take them out with.

==History==
===Episode 3: A Third Thief Causes Grief===
[[Wario]], along with [[Goodstyle]], make it to Sneezemore Cave for the next [[Wishstone]] piece after the tip from the [[Sphinx (character)|Sphinx]]. However, Wario soon runs into a third thief, [[Carpaccio]]. The sly thief blocks off Wario's way to the Wishstone piece but the greedy hero finds his way around it and defeats Carpaccio in his [[Head Honcho Carpaccio|transformed state]] for the Wishstone piece.

===Special Episode 2===
[[Count Cannoli]] returns to Sneezemore Cave in order to get ingredients for ramen noodles. However, Wario decides to get them before Cannoli much to his annoyance.
