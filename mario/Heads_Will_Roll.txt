'''Heads Will Roll''' is a [[microgame]] in [[Dr. Crygor]]'s [[Slightly Unscrewed]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Gameplay==
Heads Will Roll is a double-length microgame in which the player has to roll the snowball toward the snowman's body, making the snowball increasingly larger in the process. When they meet, the snowball becomes the snowman's head, and the player wins the microgame. If the objective is not met on time, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The player must roll a big snowball.
*'''Level 2:''' The player must roll a medium-sized snowball.
*'''Level 3:''' The player must roll a small snowball.
