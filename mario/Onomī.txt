'''Onomī''', (斧ミイ, also called '''Ax Mummy''' or '''Axe Mummy''' in American and British translations), is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. It is a mummy that carries an axe and like a [[men'ono|Men'ono]], will attack by trying to slice [[Wario]] with it's axes.
