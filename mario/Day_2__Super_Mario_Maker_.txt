{{DISPLAYTITLE: Day 2 (''Super Mario Maker'')}}
[[File:Super_mario_maker.jpg|thumb|[[Mario]] in ''Super Mario Maker''.]]'''Day 2''' is the second day in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' when you are about to create a course. A few moments later, you will hear a truck coming. A message will appear and ask, "Do you want to play a sample course?". You could either say "Create" or "Play." If you press Create, you will get the new items. If you press Play, then you will be taken to the sample course: [[Ground]]. Listed below are the new objects.
