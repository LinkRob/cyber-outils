'''Blow Hards'''<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 126.</ref> are rooted, immobile variants of [[Pokey]]s in the [[Yoshi (series)|''Yoshi'' series]] that fire [[Needlenose]]s. They always face [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] and have a blinking crosshair to tell where they are firing. Any solid objects and platforms can stop their projectiles. Blow Hards are nearly invincible, but a Yoshi can [[Ground Pound|pound the ground]] nearby or throw an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them to temporarily knock them out; however, they wake up a few seconds later. Due to this, Blow Hards can become very tough customers which can become a handful especially when there’s a lot of them so evasion is very important. Typically, the area where a Blow Hard is aiming is shown onscreen which makes it easy for players to gauge where their projectiles will fly.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
[[Image:Blow Hard SNES.jpg|thumb|left|[[Pink Yoshi]] near a Blow Hard.]]
Blow Hards make their first appearance in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' as semi-common enemies. They appear on floors and ceilings, and upon alerting a Yoshi, they will begin to shoot Needlenoses at him. They can be defeated entirely with a [[Ice Watermelon|Blue Watermelon]], [[Fire Watermelon|Red Watermelon]], [[Winged Cloud]] item, or an [[Anywhere POW]]. 

Blow Hards first appear in the level [[The Baseball Boys]] and return in [[Marching Milde's Fort]], [[Raphael The Raven's Castle]], [[Scary Skeleton Goonies!]], [[The Cave Of The Bandits]], and [[KEEP MOVING!!!!]]. In the remake ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', Blow Hards also appear in [[Exercise in the Skies]].

====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
[[Image:Blow Hard Dozens DS.jpg|thumb|right|[[Yellow Yoshi]] near many Blow Hards, a perfect example of how many appear in World 5.]]
Blow Hards reappear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', maintaining their appearance and behavior from the previous game, although they are now more common. Due to the removal of items in this game, Blow Hards are completely invincible and cannot be destroyed in any way. They can still be stunned with ground pounds however when they wake up, they will immediately retaliate by spitting a [[Needlenose]]. Blow Hards can be made to appear in the [[Museum]] by tossing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them. They appear in the Botanical Enemy Exhibit.

Blow Hards first appear in the level [[Return of the Moving Chomp Rock!]] and reappear in [[Yikes! Boiling Hot!]], [[Moving Statues, Standing Statues]], [[In the Clouds]], [[The Fort of Moltz the Very Goonie]], [[The Cave That Never Ends]], [[Find the Number Ball!]], [[Superhard Acrobatics!]], [[At Last, Bowser's Castle!]], and [[Yoshi's Island Easter Eggs]]. Blow Hards are very common towards the end of the game appearing in almost every level of [[World 5 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 5]].

Two new variants of the Blow Hard also appear in this game, called the [[Drool Hard]] and the [[Hanging Blow Hard]].

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[Image:Blow Hard 3D.jpg|thumb|left|[[Green Yoshi]] above a Blow Hard.]]
Blow Hards reappear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', maintaining their behavior but with a slight difference in appearance, now gaining a white protrusion on their lower right side. They once again attack by spitting [[Needlenose]]s and can still be temporarily stunned with ground pounds but they’re once again invincible foes. Blow Hards now appear as rather rare enemies, appearing only in two levels of the entire game: [[Diagonal Valley Rally]] and [[Underground Pokey Patrol]].

===''Super Mario-kun''===
[[File:Blow_Hard_SMKun.png|thumb|Blow Hards in ''Super Mario-kun'']]
In stage 6 of the [[Super Mario-kun Volume 14|volume 14]] of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', a group of Blow Hards along with a group of [[Dizzy Dandy|Dizzy Dandies]] is seen attacking the heroes following [[Kamek]]'s orders, with the objective of stealing a money bag and [[Baby Mario]]. After [[Mario]] accidentally angers a [[Flutter]] by ripping a flower that belonged to it, the Flutter, in its rage, attacks both the heroes and [[Bowser's Minions]] present, including the group of Blow Hards. Additionally, a Blow Hard makes a cameo appearance in stage 6 of [[Super Mario-kun Volume 15|volume 15]]. It appears alongside two [[Piranha Plant]]s, a [[Hootie the Blue Fish]], and a Needlenose in the tunnel that [[Yoshi]] had made while transformed into the [[Mole Tank]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Blow Hards are named after the slang term "blowhard," meaning "someone who exaggerates about themselves," and referencing their method of attack of spitting Pokeys.
