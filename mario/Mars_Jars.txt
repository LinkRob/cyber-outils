'''Mars Jars''' is [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' in the Sci-Fi folder. It returns as their microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player controls a character on Mars and the little men move while following the character. The cup is upside-down while that cup is attempting to follow the playable character. In awhile, the cup will land to capture the player and the characters, the player wins if none of them fall into the trap. The player loses if the player or the little men gets captured.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' One small man follows the player.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' Two small men follows the player.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' Three small men follows the player.
