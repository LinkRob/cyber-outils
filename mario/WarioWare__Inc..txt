'''WarioWare, Inc.''' is a company featured in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]], and first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. The company is run by [[Wario]], as the name suggests, and they specialize in creating [[microgame]]s, with each employee having their own style of designing their own microgames.
