The '''shop owner''' is a supporting character in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. He is the proprietor of the [[Item Shop (Wario Land 4)|Item Shop]], and bears a similar appearance to [[Mr. Game & Watch]]. The shop owner specifically sells items that damage a boss before the battle commences, giving [[Wario]] a headstart.
