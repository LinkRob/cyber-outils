|cup       = [[Star Cup]]}}'''Electrodrome''' is a course in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. The course is located in the [[Star Cup]].
==Layout==
Electrodrome is a neon night dance course that is the third race in the Star Cup. After activating a gliding ramp, you have to take a sudden left turn before entering the course's anti-gravity segment. You then take a right turn as you create a path of geometric figures behind you. After a long straight path, you come across a green and pink/red path. You have to choose quickly before a barrier blocks you from merging with another path.

On these paths, there are [[Item Box|item boxes]] to collect before making two right turns. After a left turn, there are more item boxes. You then activate yet another gliding ramp, ending the anti-gravity segment. Upon landing, you can perform a trick and take a left turn or go through water as a shortcut. If you don't take the shortcut, there will be a short straight path before the course repeats.
