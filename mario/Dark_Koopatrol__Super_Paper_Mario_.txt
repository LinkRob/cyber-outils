A '''Dark Koopatrol''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Koopatrols are only found in a few rooms of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]] and are much stronger versions of [[Koopatrol]]s. Dark Koopatrols attack by ramming into one of the heroes upon sight. It is recommended to take them out with [[Boomer]].
