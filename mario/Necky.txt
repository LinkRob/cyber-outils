'''Neckies''' are vulture-like enemies found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'' that roam throughout the skies of [[Donkey Kong Island]]. They appear to have a pink neck and pink talons along with a brown beak and brown feathers. They shoot coconuts as an attack or by pecking Donkey and Diddy in the skies. They have a sub-species called [[Mini-Necky|Mini-Neckies]] which appear in ''Donkey Kong Country'' and ''Donkey Kong Land'' along with their sequels, ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'' along with a boss Mini-Necky, [[Krow]] and his form [[Krow|Kreepy Krow]]. Normal Neckies don't reappear in ''Donkey Kong Country 2/Land 2'' and no species of Necky or himself appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]''.

Neckies later reappear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' in the [[Angry Aztec]] world. Two Neckies appear in the level. One of the Neckies is an enemy and is located near [[Tiny Kong]]'s cage. They can be defeated by being shot multiple times by [[Lanky Kong|Lanky]] using his [[Grape Shooter]] to gain a [[Golden Banana]]. Another Necky can be found in Angry Aztec on whom [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] must release to make the Necky challenge him to a minigame.

Neckies were going to make an appearance in ''[[Donkey Kong Racing]]'', presumably as rideable creatures like [[Zinger]]s were. They were seen in the background on when [[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] was riding [[Ellie the Elephant|Ellie]].

Neckies also appear in ''[[DK: King of Swing]]'' and its sequel, ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]'' as enemies that either swoop down at the Kongs or shoot coconuts at them. A boss called [[Fire Necky]] appears in ''DK: King of Swing''. Neckies also appear as obstacles in ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]''.

==Trivia==
