A '''Heavy Meal''' is an item and recipe that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
A Heavy Meal can be made by [[Dyllis]] by giving her a [[Honey Jar]] and a [[Honey Candy]]. Eating a Heavy Meal only makes the user sluggish and move very slowly. This item is not recommended to use (especially when playing [[Bowser]] who moves slowly).
