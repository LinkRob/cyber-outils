'''Walking Trees''' are a moving obstacle featured in the [[Luigi's Mansion (track)|Luigi's Mansion]] course in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. Despite their exclusive association with this course, Walking Trees have not appeared in the ''[[Luigi's Mansion (series)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' series.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Walking Trees are leafless trees with four curved branch-like appendages that they use for legs and three branches sticking out the top. In ''Mario Kart DS'' and ''Mario Kart 7'', they have a dark green body, although in ''Mario Kart Tour'', they are colored light brown, giving them a less aged appearance.

===Behavior===
Walking Trees move back and forth in a set pattern. If a racer drives into a Walking Tree, they are briefly slowed down.
