A '''Long Jump Block''' is a type of [[block]] that first debuts in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. They have a same behavior to [[Backflip Block]]s and [[Pause Block]]s (when the player is standing on them), but they are instead yellow with a red right arrow. When the player taps the screen while dashing across the blocks, they will do a [[Long Jump]]. Additionally, if the player taps and holds it, the jump will be enough for more [[Ground (course)|ground]].
