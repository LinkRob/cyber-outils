==History==
The Stone Tablet is one of the three elemental tablets needed to be collected in the [[Gap of Crag]]. The Stone Tablet can be found after running around a stone statue of a [[Yoshi]]. The Stone Tablet, along with the [[Fire Tablet]] and [[Water Tablet]], are needed to be placed in their pedestals in order to get the [[Pixl]], [[Cudge]].
