'''Hanging Piranha Plants''' are a variety of [[Piranha Plant]] first appearing in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', these [[Piranha Plant]]s are seen hanging upside down from [[Pipe]]s behaving similarly to a regular [[Piranha Plant]]. Hanging Piranha Plants can be a nuisance as they can block the paths of [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] however they aren't that durable and can be beaten in the same ways.

==History==
===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[Image:Hanging Piranha Plant SMB3.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] sees a Hanging Piranha Plant.]]
Hanging Piranha Plants appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', in this game, they are commonly seen in levels featuring tunnels and behave the same as a regular [[Piranha Plant]] (only hanging upside down hence their name), Hanging Piranha Plants emerge from roofed [[Pipe]]s and can briefly block [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]]'s way forcing the plumbers to wait for the Hanging Piranha Plants to return to their [[Pipe]]s before crossing, a Hanging Piranha Plant can't be beaten with regular attacks but can be beaten with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Tanooki Suit]], [[Hammer Suit]], or a [[Starman]], they can also be beaten by tossing objects such as [[Shell]]s at them. They are commonly found in [[Pipe Land]] but they are first seen in the [[Grass Land]] level [[World 1-5 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 1-5]].

==Trivia==
*Piranha Plants that hang from [[Pipe]]s can also be found in several subsequent ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' games such as those in the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' however it's unclear if those are considered Hanging Piranha Plants as well as they aren't named as such in-game.
*The [[Bungee Piranha]]s from ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' could be a relative of the Hanging Piranha Plants due to their hanging nature but it is unknown.
