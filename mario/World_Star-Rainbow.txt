'''{{world|star|rainbow}}''' is the final [[Rainbow Course]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', and is the Rainbow course featured in [[World Star (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Star]]. To finish this [[level]], the timer finishes with the few digits '99' (that is, 'x99', where 'x' can be any number) when the player touches the [[Goal Pole]] of any World Star level. Once the level is done, it can be completed again by revealing once more.

==Layout==
The stage starts on a flat, green area with a pattern in the setting, a [[Cannon|Pipe Cannon]] in the midpoint of the level. Launching the Pipe Cannon causes couple lines of [[coin]]s to appears before the player goes in the sky.

Mario is launched onto a platform. He will be forced to contend with three coin formations, then many [[Coin|10 Coins]] and a huge coin plummets Mario. He goes into a large [[Roulette Coin Block]] and a [[Red Ring]], forming star-shaped. Collecting all [[Red Coin]]s return to the player's record with a [[Gold Flower]]. The level then finished with few more coins.
