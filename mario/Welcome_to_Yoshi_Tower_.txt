'''Welcome to Yoshi Tower!''' is the secret stage of [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', a [[Black Yoshi]] takes control in this level. This level is unlocked after [[At Last, Bowser's Castle!]] is completed. The level consists of a tower filled with many traps and enemies alongside rising [[lava]] below. The level's background is the same as the one seen in [[Castle of Big Guy the Stilted]] consisting of many [[Shy Guy]]s living in a metropolis of shelves. Despite the level's name, [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] do not populate the place and actually, enemies of Yoshi seem to populate the place instead.

==Walkthrough==
'''Note:''' before starting this level, it is highly recommended to stock up on [[Red Yoshi Egg]]s from another level (these can be earned by ricocheting a [[Yoshi Egg]] of a wall twice and then grabbing the red egg with Yoshi's tongue or by hitting a [[Red Egg Block]]) as the level contains only four [[Winged Cloud]]s with [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] (which totals the count to exactly 30) meaning you will have to make it through the level without getting hit once, on top of all that, there are no [[Middle Ring]]s in this level so if you die anywhere in this level, you are going all the way back to the beginning.

Starting off the level, advance to the right and hop up to the upper platforms, (throughout the entire level, you are required to keep moving as the [[lava]] below is rising and slowly consuming the entire level) and use your [[Red Yoshi Egg|red egg]]s on the [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Crazee Dayzee]]s along the path (you get two [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] for every enemy hit (this can be multiplied if two enemies are amongst eachother and are hit simultaneously) once done, you should have at least 22 [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]]), anyway, get the [[Coin]]s and use the [[Stork Stop]] to switch out your baby (if need be) and then continue along the path, hit one of the [[Winged Cloud]]s for some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], next hit the [[Expanding Block]] to reach the upper platforms and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] for some [[Coin]]s, climb the stairs, ground pound the dirt to reach some [[Coin]]s, two [[Red Coin]]s are stashed here, toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the [[Smiley Flower]] here and quickly return to the main path, hop up to the upper path and destroy the [[Bandit]] that comes after you as you collect the [[Red Coin]]s and [[Coin]]s here, hit the green [[Arrow Block]] at the end to flip the [[Flipping Platform]]s around and the hit the red [[Arrow Block]] to flip the red [[Flipping Platform]], hit the green [[Arrow Block]] to flip the next green [[Flipping Platform]] and continue climbing, break away the [[Thorn]]s blocking the path up ahead and switch out to [[Baby Peach]] with the next [[Stork Stop]] and use the wind to glide up and get the [[Coin]]s, two [[Red Coin]]s, and a [[Smiley Flower]] at the top (the left path is infested with [[Pike Guy]]s and the right path has some fast vertical-moving [[Flatbed Ferry|Flatbed Ferries]]), after gliding, use the green [[Flatbed Ferry]] from the right side to leap up to the next platform, there are several [[Bill Blaster]]s that fire [[Bullet Bill]]s here, avoid the [[Bullet Bill]]s and move to the left ledge (with the [[Bill Blaster]] at the end) and launch an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the two [[Red Coin]]s off the edge, after this, climb up and grab two [[Red Coin]]s from the [[Coin]] line here, then hop on a [[Bullet Bill]] and use it to propel to the higher platforms, from here, toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the [[Baron Von Zeppelin]] above carrying a [[Spring Ball]] (if you're short on eggs, just swallow some [[Shy Guy]]s that endlessly spawn from the [[Pipe]], after this, use the Spring Ball to spring to the upper platforms whilst avoiding the spinning [[Podoboo Cog]]s.

Upon reaching the higher platform, advance to the left to obtain the [[Smiley Flower]] on the outside and return to the main path, the right side also has a [[Winged Cloud]] containing a 1-Up but getting this one is risky, if you start to see the [[lava]], just keep moving, from here, jump on the [[One-Jump Platforms]] and move across them (be sure not to use them all up as they don't repspawn upon disappearing and if you suddenly slip off, there's no way back up so play it safe when crossing them), the two lowest, singular [[One-Jump Platforms]] have two [[Red Coin]]s on them so you can net a total of four [[Red Coin]]s here. Next, you will come across two paths divided by walls, the first path is filled with [[Tap-Tap]]s and a [[Wild Ptooie Piranha]] while the second path is filled with [[Spear Guy]]s and [[Piranha Plant]]s, you're going to want to take the right path (with the [[Spear Guy]]s) as there are two [[Red Coin]]s in a niche which can be obtained with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] toss but you can still cross over from the left path to the right by destroying the dirt wall blockage halfway. Upon reaching the upper platforms, you will see [[Shy Guy]]s hopping out of the [[Pipe]], destroy the [[Thorn]]s blocking the path forward with [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s and continue upward, cross to the left collecting the [[Coin]]s, you will see another [[Smiley Flower]] here but watch out for the two [[Dizzy Dandy|Dizzy Dandies]] which cleverly disguise themselves as one, grab the Flower and flutter across to the platform (collecting the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]]), if you can't make the jump, bounce off one of the respawning [[Dizzy Dandy|Dizzy Dandies]] for some extra height. Once done, jump to the higher platforms.

In the next area, you will see a massively long [[Ball 'n' Chain]] spinning around along the small platforms but as if that's not enough, there is also an onslaught of [[Gusty|Gusties]] coming from each direction as well as [[Flightless Goonie]]s on the floor, amongst all the [[Coin]]s by each small platform here, two of them are [[Red Coin]]s and both are on the right side (it's easier to toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the Coins from below to obtain them but be quick about it), avoid the hazards as you reach the highest platforms, [[Goonie]]s will start flying into view, you can use them to reach the two [[Winged Cloud]]s on either side of the top here, (both contain [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]]) and after collecting these, either bounce off the [[Shy Guy]] on the highest platform or hitch a ride from a [[Goonie]] to reach the upper platform. Here, you will see a [[Winged Cloud]], grab the [[Yoshi Egg]] here and hit the Winged Cloud to make a [[! Switch]] appear, hit the switch to form some stairs to reach the higher platform, jump on the [[Spring Ball]] to reach the top, move to the left ledge and hop up to reveal an invisible [[Winged Cloud]], hit it to get some more [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], after this, jump on the ferris wheels (while avoiding the [[Spike (hazard)|Spike]]s) grab the [[Smiley Flower]] and jump across the two ferris wheels bounce off the [[Zeus Guy (Snifit)|Zeus Guys]] heads to reach the door at the end to escape the gauntlet.

Upon exiting the dangerous tower, you will be at the level's end, wait for the [[Fly Guy]] to come into view as it is carrying a [[Red Coin]], beat the [[Fly Guy]] to obtain it's [[Red Coin]] and then jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to finish the level.

==Trivia==
*This level is often cited as one of the examples of so-called "Platform Hell", which is essentially an even harder version of "[[Nintendo]] Hard", meant to punish and frustrate the player.
*The [[Shy Guy]]s in the background of this level seem as though they are doing the following:
**Learning how to use the Stilts.
**Talking to each other.
**Climbing ladders (it appears that this is the way they travel around the castle).
**Washing their wet clothes.
**Studying on how [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] attacks and who he is.
**Growing [[Piranha Plant]]s.
**Sleeping
