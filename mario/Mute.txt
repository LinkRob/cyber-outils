|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Mute''' is a special move used only by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Toadstool can learn this move through leveling up and requires 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use. To use Mute, the player must rotate the control pad quickly.

==Description==
When Mute is used, a blue balloon with a white "X" appears over the target.

==Effects==
With Mute, Toadstool may be able to [[Silence]] an enemy. Note that this move doesn't tend to work on bosses and should only be used on tougher enemies such as [[Bahamutt]]s.
