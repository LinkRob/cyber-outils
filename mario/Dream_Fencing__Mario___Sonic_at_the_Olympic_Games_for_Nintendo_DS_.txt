'''Dream Fencing''' is an unlockable [[Dream Event]] in the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]''. It is unlocked by clearing Satellite Circuit.

== Layout ==
Dream Fencing takes place in the Blue Sky Field.

== Gameplay ==
Much like regular fencing, the player slides the [[stylus]] at the opponent to hit them. They can move using the D-Pad. The player can slide up to block a strike from the opponent, but if it is vice versa, the player has to slide the stylus back and forth across the screen quickly to recover. The player can charge a special move and slide the stylus down to use it.
