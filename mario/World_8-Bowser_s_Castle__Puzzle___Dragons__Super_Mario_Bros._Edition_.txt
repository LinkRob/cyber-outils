'''{{world|8|bowser4}}''' is the final main [[level]] of ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. It is very long and difficult, consisting of many difficult battles in a row without any breaks. Once the player beats this level, they are transported to the true final battle against Bowser and his Minions along with [[Bowser Jr.]] (Warning: Skill cooldowns will be reset as it is counted as a separate level.) After winning the final battle, the credits will play and you will unlock [[Bowser]] and [[Princess Peach]] as helpers, along with ★World 1, and the Lucky ? Block's prizes will be enhanced to contain Star Gems and Grand/Rare Skill Up Items.

The level has a harder variation titled [[Special World 8-Bowser's Castle (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|★World 8-Bowser's Castle]].
