'''Secret of the Village Underside''' is the fifth [[mission|episode]] of [[Pianta Village]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It prominently features [[Chuckster]]s more than any other episode.

==Layout==
The main part of the episode takes place in a secret course accessed under Pianta Village. After completing the episode once, [[Mario]] can return to this episode to collect the [[Shine Sprite]] of the bonus ninth episode [[Red Coin Chucksters]].
