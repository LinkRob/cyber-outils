'''Wiggler''' is a young [[Wiggler]] (eventually turning into a [[Flutter]]) and a major character in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. In 3-1, [[Leaflitter Path]], he is sleeping and blocking the pathway to the rest of the Forest. Upon being woken up by [[Paper Mario (character)|Mario]] via the [[Trumpet]] thing, he explains why he was sleeping there, the Forest had been covered in [[Poisoned Water|Poison]] lately and this is why Wiggler was sleeping outside of his home because he could not find any food. Since the leaves were polluted, he set out to find food. Mario and Kersti offer to help him, Wiggler then invites them to come over to his house to hear the details. Mario and [[Kersti]] follow him deeper into the woods, as it gets deeper, Wiggler explains that the Poison has made the forest darker. He then exclaims how he would love for Mario to "look at Wiggler's [[Wiggler's Diary|picture diary]]" and "listen to Wiggler reminiscent music". However, while doing this, he is captured by [[Poison Blooper|a huge tentacle]] leaving Mario and Kersti to wonder where he went, they decide to try and find [[Wiggler's Tree House]], meanwhile, Wiggler is brought to his Tree House by the Poison Bloopers and subsequently split into [[Wiggler Segment|pieces]] by [[Kamek]] (or, as he calls, his "children"). Mario and Kersti manage to explore the Forest and find all four Wiggler Segments. Once all four are found and Wiggler’s Segments are stuck back together, it is found that Kamek ripped Wiggler’s as a means to dissuade Mario from attempting to get the purple Royal sticker, as Wiggler is the only entity besides a boat that is capable of travelling to the island where the royal sticker was located. With all Wigglers segments brought back to Wiggler, Wiggler storms off to [[Whitecap Beach]] and waits for Mario and Kersti there, once they arrive, Wiggler helps them to get to the island where the source of the pollution is located, which is revealed to be the purple Royal Sticker on [[Gooper Blooper]]. Once Gooper Blooper is defeated, the Forest is cleared of the Poison and Wiggler can be seen in his Tree House eating delicious leaves, he thanks Mario and Kersti for their efforts. After all five Royal Stickers are collected and [[Bowser's Sky Castle]] is revealed, Mario and Kersti return to Wiggler’s Tree House for help where they find that Wiggler has aged into a Flutter and agrees to help Mario and Kersti get to Bowser's Sky Castle.

==Trivia==
[[Image:Beta Wiggler.webp|thumb|right|Mario near the Wiggler in the beta version of the game.]]
*Wiggler always refers to himself in the third person.
*Wiggler has a notebook where he keeps his memories with Mario. Certain scenes such as him in the hot spring will be recorded and drawn out by Wiggler.
*In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', there is a [[Wiggler (Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam)|Wiggler]] who plays a very similar role to this Wiggler, helping [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] traverse the Forest, eating fruits, before growing into a Flutter.
*In certain beta screenshots of ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star'', a multi-segmented Wiggler could be seen moving through the forest blocking Mario's path, its likely at this point, the Wiggler Segment subplot was not going to be in the game and that the Wiggler would’ve had a much smaller role than in the final game.
