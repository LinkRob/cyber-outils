[[File:Double_Time_Comet.png|thumb]]A '''Double Time Comet''' is a [[Prankster Comet]] that only appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It replaces ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'s'' Fast Foe Comet. The comet causes the objects in a level to move faster.
==Locations==
===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
*[[Beat Block Galaxy]] (Silver Stars in Double Time; [[Mario]] must complete Step To The Beat as the [[Beat Block]]s have their speed quadrupled, making traversing them harder. Based off Mission 1: Step To The Beat.)
*[[Twisty Trials Galaxy]] (Turning Turning Double Time; Mario must complete Spinning and Spinning and Spinning as the blocks forming his path have their speed doubled, making travel harder. Based off Mission 1: Spinning and Spinning and Spinning.)
==Trivia==
*The Double Time Comet appears the least amount of times than any other Prankster Comet, only striking two galaxies with their influence.
*Despite being called the Double Time Comet, the objects actually move at four times the normal speed.
