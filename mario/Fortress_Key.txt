The '''Fortress Key''' is a key item found in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Fortress Keys are located in a few areas within the [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]], where it can open locked doors. There are four Fortress Keys in total, each of which are collected through varying means (mainly by defeating [[Koopa Troopa]]s that hold a key).
