The '''Bowser Toy Shop''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Bowser Toy Shop is located in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], but it is only accessible from using the yellow pipe in the [[Pipe House (Mario Party Advance)|Pipe House]]. The interior of Bowser Toy Shop features ''[[Toad Force V]]'' memorabilia, including several [[Toad Force V Figure]]s on the back wall's shelves and a blue poster on the left side. Everything in the shop is a part of [[Bowser]]'s collection, and Bowser uses it as proof to show how much he likes the show.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In Shroom City mode, one of Bowser's quests takes place here, [[Bowser's Toys]], which involves Bowser providing five trivia questions about ''Toad Force V''. After the player's character answers all five questions correctly, Bowser challenges them to the Duel minigame, [[Slammer (minigame)|Slammer]], and winning it unlocks the [[Screen Clean]] [[Gaddget]].
