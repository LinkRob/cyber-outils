A '''Sproutle Vine''' is a type of twisted [[vine]] featured in the [[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|''Super Mario Galaxy'' sub-series]].

In both ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] use a Sproutle Vine by grabbing on it, and the player can shake the [[Wii Remote]] to have them climb upward, often to continue on to the next planetoid.
