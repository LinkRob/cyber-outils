'''Shroob Castle'''<ref>"''Our once beautiful castle...no...look at it now.  No doubt those fiends call it Shroob Castle.''" -Toadsworth the Younger, ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''</ref> is the [[Shroobified]] version of [[Peach's Castle]] set in the past after it was taken over by the [[Shroob]]s. . This dark, purple castle is the setting of final chapter in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Interestingly, the [[Save Album]] recognizes it as '''Peach's Castle of the Past''', despite the fact that [[Princess Shroob]] has complete control over it.

==Appearance==
[[File:ShroobCellar.png|thumb|150px|left|The cellar]]
The castle towers are changed into tall [[Shroob Mushroom|purple mushroom]]s, along with all the pillars and bushes. The castle itself glows purple and is completely [[Shroobified]]. Inside, a lot of the castle seems the same as the original, only darker, with the Shroob emblem and more violet in coloration. The map resembles the one of the present-day castle. Glowing purple mushrooms grow all over and a giant statue of the castle's ruler, [[Princess Shroob]], can also be found here. [[Shroob UFO]]s fly around the exterior of the castle. Beneath the castle is an underground cellar, and unlike the Shroob castle, it has the same music as the cellars in the present-day castle. It has a few enemies, most of them being [[Shroobsworth]], [[Shroob Rex]] and [[Soul Bubble]]s. A [[Chomp]] pesters [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] here as well.

== History ==

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'' ===
In the past time period, moments before the Shroob takeover, the castle was once [[Princess Peach's Castle|home]] of [[Baby Peach|Baby Princess Peach]]. [[Toadsworth the Younger|Young Toadsworth]] invited [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] to play with Baby Peach until [[Baby Bowser]] kidnaps her. After they defeat him, their victory was cut short when the castle starts to crumble from the Shroobs invasion with several [[Toad (species)|toads]] are taken by [[Shroob UFO|UFO]]. With hope lost, Baby Bowser contact the [[Koopa Cruiser]] to evacuate others from the castle in which they successfully did. Sometime later, a [[E. Gadd's time machine|time machine]] appears with the arrival of the [[Princess Peach|future Princess Peach]], [[Toadbert]] and [[Toadiko]]. Much to their shock and confusion, they're surrounded by Shroobs and [[Elder Princess Shroob|two]] [[Princess Shroob|princess]] beginning to capture her, however Peach uses the [[Cobalt Star]] that powered the time machine to imprison the Elder Princess Shroob and it shatters, and the shards are scattered across the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. The younger Princess Shroob then ambushes and captures Peach locked inside on top of the statue tower, while Toadiko and Toadbert escape the castle only to get separated.

The castle now newly converted called Shroob Castle, Princess Shroob notices the Koopa Cruiser far distance. She sends a transmission and fires a Shroob Missile heading straight to the Koopa Cruiser leading to crash land [[Bowser's Castle|its course]].

After the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] assemble the rest of the scattered shards of the Cobalt Star, they eventually go through infiltrate Shroob Castle, inside they find warp pipe passage to enter where Peach is being locked, only for a [[Chain Chomp]] bash the pipe sewer, redirecting it destination through the cellar's underground basement.

The Bros. then leave the basement and finally arrived inside to rescue Peach as the Shroob Castle's Tower is being attacked by Princess Shroob. At the top of the highest tower, they hijack a Shroob Battleship and engage Princess Shroob aboard the [[Shroob Mother Ship]], eventually taking it down. As they return downstairs to investigate the wreckage alongside Princess Peach, a furious Princess Shroob descends from the wreckage on a floating throne, declares Peach useless and moving to finish the group off and take over the kingdom, shouting "Destroy!" as the Bros. prepare to battle her.

As the Mario Bros. defeated her, they were soon able to leave the tower only to spot the almost-whole Cobalt Star floating entering the room. Princess Peach reveals that she has the final shard, and that she refuses to let it be reassembled, confusing the [[Mario|adult Mario]] [[Luigi|Bros.]]; Baby Bowser suddenly ambushes the group and snatches the shard, assembling the Cobalt Star, much to Peach horror shout him no to do it. Princess Shroob suddenly crawls into view, shouting that it is her sister's turn to defeat them now before her eyes go dim and she explodes in a burst of sparks; the Cobalt Star then begins darting around, storming the room and turns Baby Bowser into a [[Shroob Mushroom]]. Princess Peach tells Mario and Luigi the truth about the Cobalt Star just before the Elder Princess Shroob regains her true form.

As her form is restored, Elder Princess Shroob promises to kill the Bros. and Peach as revenge for her sister's death and then activates the platform they were standing on, engaging them in battle high skies above Shroob Castle. The heroes finally defeat the Elder Princess Shroob as she becomes a Shroob mushroom, although not before knowing that even with her defeat, the Shroobs would still destroy the Mushroom Kingdom regardless. The heroes soon learn about Shroobs true weakness: Baby Tears.

Soon after, [[Professor E. Gadd]] sends Mario, Luigi, Baby Mario, Baby Luigi, [[Stuffwell]] and Princess Peach back to their own present.

==Music==
The music in Shroob Castle is a key-shifted arrangement of the Shroob theme, which plays in the opening scene as well as while the four Bros. are aboard the [[Shroob Mother Ship]]. No music plays in the room of the time hole; the music starts only after the player enters through the gate, which is blocked off until the player defeats the [[Elder Shrooboid]] at the [[Star Shrine]].
