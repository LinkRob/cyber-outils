'''Herb T.''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. He is a green [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who runs a [[Herb T.'s Place|bar]] in [[Rogueport Sewers]].

==History==
Herb T.'s role is minimal as he only tends to his customers. Herb T. himself describes his bar as a special place where people can cultivate information (most likely referring to one of his customers being [[Wonky]] who is a known information dealer). According to [[Goombella]], Herb T. is middle-aged and also says that he is an old-school cola bartender rather than just a simple glass polisher. The [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]] also gives Herb T. a very distinct deep voice.

==Trivia==
*Herb T. is the only Toad in the game to run a bar.
*He earned his name from the drink "herb tea", although, his given name is real.
