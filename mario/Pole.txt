'''Poles''' are a type of object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Description==
[[Mario]] or another character can grab on to a Pole and then move up and down it before [[jump]]ing off. They can also perform a [[handstand]] on certain poles, especially those that are not attached to a ceiling.

==In ''Super Mario'' series==
===''Super Mario 64'' / ''Super Mario 64 DS''===
There are yellow and black-striped poles found in the wide room of [[Dire, Dire Docks]]. They are attached to the ceiling, where they move back and forth in a set pattern. Poles are the main focus of the mission [[Pole-Jumping for Red Coins]], in which Mario (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in ''Super Mario 64 DS'') has to jump from one Pole to the next and move up and down to collect the eight [[Red Coin]]s, causing a [[Power Star]] to appear over a [[Star Marker]].
