{{Nihongo|'''Hands-On Hall'''|タッチ！謎のからくり城|Tacchi! Nazo no karakuri jō|Touch! Mysterious Mechanism Castle}} is a urban-themed level in World 6 in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The urban-themed level takes place in a traditional Japanese dojo combining Chinese and Japanese elements. Inside the pagoda, are traditional designs of red and yellow. The painting on the wall depicts the plains in the game. There is a large gong engraved with [[Bowser]]'s symbol. Outside the pagoda, it takes place at night.


There is also a propeller box exploit that makes you be able to skip the warp box with the key coins. Just propeller on the ledges above the warp box.

==[[Green Star]]s==
*'''Green Star 1''': On the visited second section taking place on the second floor. There are doors that can be opened using the GamePad. The Green Star is found where the two Galoombas are hiding.
*'''Green Star 2''': Use the Propeller Block to claim the Green Star infront of the two [[Thwomp]]s.
*'''Green Star 3''': In the fourth section taking place inside the pagoda after transporting from the [[Warp Box]]. The player must reach the furthest to the right to hit the gong. The gong will summon the [[Mystery Box]]. Entering the Mystery Block, the player must defeat the Spike to claim in ten seconds and immediately claim the Green Star.

==Stamp==
[[File:Hands-On_Hall_Stamp.png|thumb]]
Outside the pagoda where there are [[Spike (enemy)|Spikes]], the player must use the [[Propeller Block]] to reach the stamp found on a white platform.
