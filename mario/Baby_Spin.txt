The '''Baby Spin''' is a move in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==Description==
Baby Spin is a height-gaining move used by any of the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]], which include [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]]. To execute the move, Mario and Luigi have to move into their baby counterparts during a [[Spin Jump (Mario & Luigi series)|Spin Jump]]. In doing so, the babies are forced into the air, spinning as they descend. Baby Spin can be used to hit blocks and switches on certain ledges that not even [[Baby Toss]] can reach. If the Mario and Luigi try to spin around Baby Mario and Baby Luigi before they learn the Baby Spin, the babies only do a low spin before dropping instantly.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''==
Both Baby Spin and [[Baby Cakes]] are taught by [[Toadsworth]] and [[Toadsworth the Younger]] after the Mario Bros. complete [[Gritzy Caves]], but before they head for [[Thwomp Volcano]]. Once both moves have been taught, the Mario Bros. have to rescue [[Baby Peach]] using the two moves.
