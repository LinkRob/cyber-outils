'''Andrew Sabiston''' (born February 8, 1965) is a Canadian voice actor. He provided the voice work for [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in the [[Super Mario World (television series)|''Super Mario World'' television series]] and later went on to also voice [[Diddy Kong]] in the [[Donkey Kong Country (television series)|''Donkey Kong Country'' television series]].
