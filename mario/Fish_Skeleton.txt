A '''Fish Skeleton''', alternatively named '''Fish Bone''', is an item in ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros. Classic]]'' for [[Game Boy Advance]].

==Description==
A Fish Skeleton resembles an eaten fish with the head and tail intact. They are a gag item only found in the Mario Bros. Battle multiplayer mode, in which it can be obtained from a [[garbage can]] at random. Fish Skeletons have no function, and can simply be thrown away.
