'''Eye Spy''' is a [[List of microgames in WarioWare Gold#Wario|Mash League introduction]] microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It is not to be confused with [[I Spy (WarioWare|I Spy]] from ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgames!]]''.
==Gameplay==
The player has to memorize the amount of pairs of eyes there are. The player is then asked how many pairs of eyes there were. If they answer correctly, the game is won.
*'''1st level difficulty:''' There are 2-4 pairs of eyes. The pairs of eyes do not move.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' There are 3-5 pairs of eyes. The pairs of eyes sometimes move, and some may hide behind objects.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' There are 4-6 pairs of eyes. The pairs of eyes move constantly, and some may or may not hide behind objects.
