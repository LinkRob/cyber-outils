The '''Brilliant Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its remake ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Brilliant Badge costs 350 [[coin]]s to buy, and equipping it increases [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s [[Power]] by 45. The Brilliant Badge can be bought from the [[Clothes Shop]] in [[Beanbean Castle Town]].

In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] version, the price is slightly reduced to 330 coins, and it can be sold for 83 coins. It increases [[POW]] by 56 and [[Bros. Point]]s by 10.
