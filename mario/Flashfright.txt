'''Flashfright''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] from ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place at night in a symmetrical castle courtyard with four large [[Shy Guy]] statues, two on each side. There is also a small, rectangular pool of water in the center, and the water enters it from the mouth of a Shy Guy mask-like statue on the left and right sides each. Parts of the pool are covered by short bridge segments.

==Opening==
The team reaches a courtyard, being chased by the solo character as the gate closes.

==Gameplay==
With their [[flashlight]], the solo character has to try to catch the team players, who must move around to try and avoid the flashlight. If a character is caught by a flashlight, they are instantly eliminated, and a [[Fly Guy]] arrives to carry them away. If the solo character catches all three team members, they win, but if at least one team member avoids being caught for the entire 30-second [[time limit]], they win.

==Ending==
If the solo character wins, they do their victory animation while Fly Guys arrive to carry away each of the team members. If the team wins, the solo character looks around and walks out of the courtyard while the team members perform their respective victory animation.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''One player tries to shine a flashlight on the other 3 while they attempt to run and hide.''
* '''Advice''': ''If you're being chased, run around the Shy Guy statues to keep your opponent guessing!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a portmanteau of "flashlight" and "fright."
