The '''Block Meal''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Effects==
It is made by [[Saffron]] in [[Flopside]] out of a [[Block Block]]. It simply looks like a Block Block with an orange slice next to it. The Block Meal makes the player invincible to everything for a short amount of time. The player does not have to do a [[Minigame|mini-game]] with the [[Wii#Wii Remote|Wii Remote]].
