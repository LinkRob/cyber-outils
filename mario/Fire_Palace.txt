The '''Fire Palace''' is located in the [[Oho Oasis]].

==History==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] enter the Fire Palace during their journey back to [[Beanbean Castle]], however, Luigi has to form Mario into a [[Mini-Mario]] because the hole was small. Mario meets the Fire God. The Fire God tends to get confused whether he forgot Mario is or has not remembered what he is doing. After he disappears, Mario touches the red orb and as a result, his hands heated up, but it fused his hands.

When the Fire God came back, he remembered. He taught Mario bow to master the [[Firebrand]].
