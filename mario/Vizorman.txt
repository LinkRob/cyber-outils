'''Vizorman''' is a one-time use item in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. [[Wario]] can purchase it from the [[shop owner]] for a certain number of [[Medal (Wario Land 4)|medals]], depending on the mode (4 in Normal, 6 in Hard, and 8 in S-Hard). Then, when Wario enters a boss room, the shop owner drags Vizorman into there, and Vizorman shoots a laser at the boss's weak spot, dealing damage and giving Wario a headstart during the battle, which he then has to continue in on his own.
