'''Paintball Battle''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. The minigame takes place in an arena with six elevated platforms.

== Opening ==
Each player shoots a paintball.

==Gameplay==
The player's objective is to survive the minigame in thirty seconds. However, the player's opponents will attempt shooting paintballs at the player. Every player starts with three lives. The player to lose their lives are out of the minigame and their place is determined. The minigame ends after one player stands or the time runs out.

==Controls==
* {{button|wii|pad}} − Move
* {{button|wii|2}} − Shoot
