}}'''Wing and a Scare''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Introduction==
The minigame starts as the player-piloted planes take off from a stone platform.

==Gameplay==
The goal of the minigame to to reach the finish first. This is achieved by tilting the plane with the Wii Remote through obstacles like openings, columns, and rotating holes.

==Ending==
Unless a tie occurred, the plane that crossed the finish line first gets showered in confetti while the other plane is unseen.

==In-game text==
'''Rules''' - ''"Tilt the wings of your plane as you try to clear the obstacles and reach the finish line first."''

'''Hint''' - ''"If you hit an obstacle or trap, your aircraft will rebound and you'll lose time!"''

==Trivia==
The name of the minigame is a pun on the phrase "on a wing and a prayer", which is when someone has a small chance of succeeding at their task.
