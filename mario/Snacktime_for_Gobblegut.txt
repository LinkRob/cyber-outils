'''Snacktime for Gobblegut''' is the hidden third [[mission]] of [[Battle Belt Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Layout==
Accessing the mission requires the player to re-enter the [[Mini-Planet Mega-Run]] mission. This time, [[Mario]] has to defeat every enemy and collect their [[Coin]]s until having at least twenty Coins to feed to the [[Hungry Luma]] on the Brrr Bit Planet, allowing it to transform into the Gobblegut Planet. Here, Mario has to fight [[Fiery Gobblegut]], and once Mario defeats it, he can obtain the [[Power Star]] and clear the mission.
