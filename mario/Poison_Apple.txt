'''Poison Apple''' is a [[Microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''. Acting as a reference to the ''Snow White'' fable, it acts as the boss Microgame of [[Doctor Crygor]]'s stage, A Curious Case of Cave Art, and uses the Big Cheese form for its gameplay.

== Layout ==
The princess who is controlled by the player is shown in black, with her face matching the background. When the microgame starts, the princess is shown being fed a poisoned apple by a witch, and her digestive system is shown as a pink tube going down her torso, with the layout becoming more complex on harder levels.

== Gameplay ==
The player must tilt their body so the princess can pass the poisoned apple out of her system. The player is given 1 minute and 39.99 seconds to do so at normal speed. If the player is successful, the princess celebrates along with several dwarves. If the poisoned apple touches a hazard or time runs out, she dies (or at least gets paralysed) and the dwarves grieve for her as the apple is digested and the poison takes effect. The Easy level has a simple maze, the Hard level contains more hazards with a more complex maze, and the maze in the Expert level is very complex with a split path in the first section with one path having spikes and both paths having acid, the second section having three sets of spikes (two in multiplayer), and the third section having two sets of moving platforms surrounded by spikes.

In multiplayer, the second player's princess has a tiara with a star. Failure on one player's part [[Softlock|sabotages the other player]].
