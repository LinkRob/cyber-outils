'''World 1''' is the very first world in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. It is themed as a plain near [[Princess Peach's Castle|Peach's Castle]]. The boss of this world is [[Bowser/Appearances/New Super Mario Bros.|Bowser]] himself.

== Layout ==
The appearance of World 1 is very simple. It takes a grassy plain filled with bushes, [[Mushroom Platform|mushrooms]], fences, and flowers. There are also some [[Brick Block|brick blocks]] lying around the map. Behind [[World 1-Fortress (New Super Mario Bros.)|the tower]], many rocky cliffs can be seen with dark green grass on them. [[Princess Peach's Castle|Peach's castle]] can be seen in the background right before [[World 1-1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]]. World 1 is also surrounded by bright blue water, which also connects to [[World 1-A (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-A]].

== References in later games ==
* The background for World 1 can be seen in a few ''Mario'' [[Wii]] games, such as ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' and ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.
** In ''Mario Kart Wii'', the yellow mountains found in levels such as the outside portions of World 1-2, {{world|1|towern}}, World 1-4 and World 1-A can be seen in the backgrounds for [[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart Wii)|Luigi Circuit]] and [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart Wii)|Mario Circuit]].
** In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'', a background based on World 1-1 appears in during the first segment of the Mario World Routine of [[Dream Figure Skating]].
