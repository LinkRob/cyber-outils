Wart's Casino is a location in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'' it is under [[Gateway Galaxy (Illumination)|Gateway Galaxy]] and within the [[Gateway Underground]] the Casino itself is a reference to the slot machine that is present at the end of every level in [[Super Mario Bros. 2|Super Mario Bros 2]]. [[Wart (Illumination)|Wart]] is the owner of the casino and presents himself as a tough mob boss who seems to have ties to [[Bowser Jr. (Illumination)|Bowser Jr.]] and presents himself as a force to be reckoned with after he summons [[Ninji|Ninjis]] to fight Peach and Toad
