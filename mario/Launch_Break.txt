'''Launch Break''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. Its name is a pun on the phrase "lunch break," referring to when workers take a break from their shift to have lunch.

==Gameplay==
* Use the controls on-screen to build up energy in your three-stage rocket. The player who's rocket goes highest wins.
* Repeatedly press the buttons as fast as you can. The controls are simple, but the COM's Very Hard and Master difficulty are quite difficult to beat.

==Controls==
Hold the [[Wii Remote]] vertically. Rapidly press 1, then 2, then shake your Wii Remote.
