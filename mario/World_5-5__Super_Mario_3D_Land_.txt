'''World 5-5''' is the twenty-seventh level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
World 5-5 begins with a Giant Goomba jumping around, attempting to squish [[Mario]]. The level focuses on the new item, the [[Propeller Block]]. Mario, throughout the level, uses clouds and the Propeller Block to fly up into the clouds. After the third piece of actual land, Mario finds the level's [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]]. From here, Mario must climb up the final land using muchroom-like bouncing panels. Afterward, Mario must float across a vast, empty chasm. At the far end is the level's [[Flagpole]].

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': Near the second Propeller block, Mario will find the first [[Star Medal]] in the area between the two large pieces of land.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is found by floating in-between two parallel bouncy platforms. Mario must use the Propeller block to regain altitude and land on the other bouncy platform.
*'''Star Medal 3''': On the way down into the Flagpole, Mario can obtain the final Star Medal, floating above a block.
