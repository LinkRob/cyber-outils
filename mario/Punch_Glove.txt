The '''Punch Glove''' is one of the weapons only usable by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The Punch Glove can bought from the shop in [[Moleville]] for 36 [[coin]]s, and equipping it increases Mario's [[Attack]] rating by 30.
