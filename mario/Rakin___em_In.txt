'''Rakin' 'em In''' is a Battle [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
When the minigame starts, the round table starts spinning slowly as there are types of [[Mushroom]]s on it. Players have to grab the Mushrooms by using a mechanic claw to rake them into their baskets. A regular Mushroom is worth one point, a [[Gold Mushroom]] is worth three points, and a [[Poison Mushroom|Poison Purple Mushroom]] takes away three points. If a player rakes a Mushroom into their basket after time runs out, they still receive the number of points that Mushroom is worth. Anyone who ends with the most points wins.

The minigame can be purchased from [[Woody]] for 800 [[Coin]]s.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|A}} &ndash; Move crane arm
