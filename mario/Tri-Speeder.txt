'''Tri-Speeder''' is an unlockable body for ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. Due to its name, it is a three-wheeled kart. It shares the same statistics with the [[Badwagon]], [[Steel Driver]], [[Standard ATV]], and the [[GLA]].
