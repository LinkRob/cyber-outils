'''Wall-Climb Jump''' is a [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|badge]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It is like the [[Wall Jump]] move, except the user does not bounce off of a wall but rather goes up, something that can only be done once.
