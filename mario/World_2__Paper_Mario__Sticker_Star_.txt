'''World 2''' is a world in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==History==
World 2 can be accessed immediately after Mario exits [[Decalburg]]. This world is full of many secrets. To complete the world, Mario must locate three Ruins Pieces throughout the world which will allow him to access the final level.

==Levels==
===2-1 [[Drybake Desert]]===
Full of quicksand and [[Pokey]]s, Mario must venture through many hills of quicksand and climb a pyramid. Mario can also find multiple [[Things]] throughout the level, in addition to the rare [[Big 1-UP]] and a piece of the Ruins.

===2-2 [[Yoshi Sphinx]]===
A mysterious level with many secret areas, Mario rescues the [[Travelling Toad]] and enters this ancient structure. The second piece of ruins can be found here, but is guarded by [[Kamek]]

===2-3 [[Sandshifter Ruins]]===
These ruins are a maze, which lead to two secret exits and a vital Thing for the boss battle. It's easy to get lost in this level, so Mario must remember his path. Despite the difficult layout, Mario will find many riches in this level and even a [[HP-Up Heart]].

===2-4 [[Damp Oasis]]===
Mario takes a break from the usual levels filled with enemies and puzzles, at least, after taking care of a sandstorm with a Vacuum Cleaner. A toad lives in the center of the oasis, although he is distraught because his lake ran dry. Mario locates the final piece to make the Ruins for the last level of World 2.

===2-5 [[Drybake Stadium]]===
After [[Paperize|Paperizing]] the three pieces of the Ruins found throughout World 2, Mario gains access to an enormous temple. Mario must make his way through many levels, fighting enemies and solving puzzles. Eventually, Mario will reach to top level and find himself in a stadium. He must do battle with the [[Tower Power Pokey]] to gain the second [[Royal Sticker]].
