'''Innertube Goomba''', also named '''Swim Ring Goomba''', is an enemy which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. It is depicted as a regular [[Goomba]]  with a red and white spiked inner tube.

==History==
===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]/[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''===
Innertube Goombas make their debut in the level [[Pipeline Lagoon]] at first it is normally encountered asleep on the surface of the water however if the player approaches them it will immediately wake up and paddle after them damaging the player on contact it can be defeated with any form of attack.

In the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign, Innertube Goombas have a cat variation, [[Cat Innertube Goomba]]s.

===''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''===
Innertube Goombas reappear in ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker'' they are found in a few water levels such as [[Floaty Fun Water Park]] they once again reprise their same behavior from ''Super Mario 3D World'' they only be defeated by landing on top of one or the use of the [[Super Pickax]].
