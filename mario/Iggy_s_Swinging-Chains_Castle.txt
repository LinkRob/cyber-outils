|game = ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''}}'''Iggy's Swinging-Chains Castle''', is the [[Castle]] level of [[Soda Jungle]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by completing [[Sumo Bro Bridge]] or [[Wiggler Floodlands]], and its own completion will unlock [[Mount Fuzzy]].
==Layout==
At the beginning of the level, [[Luigi]] must swing on a chain above [[Lava Wave|lava waves]]. A moving platform with two [[? Block]]s above it is found, with a [[Hidden Block]] in between them containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. This is followed by three more chains. Three more moving platforms are found, two of which have [[Dry Bones]] on them. Three [[Brick Block]]s float above the middle platform. Above these Brick Blocks is another Hidden Block with a 1-Up Mushroom. More chains are found, along with a [[Green Ring]]. Some more chains are found, along with the boss door and a Dry Bones. Luigi must battle [[Iggy Koopa]], who fights as he did in [[New Super Mario Bros. U|the game's predecessor]]; the only difference is that [[Lava Wave|lava wave]]s are now included in the battle.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Jump on the third chain while avoiding the lava.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Go on the platform after the seventh chain and go down to the far right side of the platform while avoiding the lava and turn to left to a hidden alcove with platforms. Jump over those platforms and get the coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Get the Star Coin while avoiding the second Super Dry Bones and lava.
