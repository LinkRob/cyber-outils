'''Billistics''' is a Free-for-All mini-game found in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place on top of a tall tower in a desert-like environment.

==Opening==
A part of a high tower is shown, then the view goes up, showing [[Bullet Bill]]s being fired near it, until reaching the top, where the characters are.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to avoid touching the [[Bullet Bill]]s as they charge at them from multiple directions. Aside from Bullet Bills, which travel in a straight line, there are also [[Missile Bill]]s, which home in on a character. Over time, an increasing number of Bullet Bills appear at once, and any Bullet Bills that crash into each other explode. If a character touches a Bullet Bill, they are eliminated. Whoever is the last character remaining or any characters remaining by the end of the 60-second [[time limit]] is the winner.

Billistics also has a [[Reverse Minigame]] variation, which has an opposite objective of trying to be taken out by a Bullet Bill first.
[[File:Billistics Perspective Mode.png|thumb|250px|Billistics in Perspective Mode]]
In [[Perspective Mode]], Billistics is the penultimate minigame. In it, there is the disadvantage of not being able to pay full attention to any Bullet Bills approaching from behind.

==Ending==
If the minigame ends with only one character remaining, they perform their victory animation. In the Reverse Minigame variation, after a Bullet Bill hits a character, the remaining characters stand idle.

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''' (Normal): ''Dodge the incoming Bullet Bills until you are the last player standing. Be careful: flashing Bullet Bills will home in on you!''
* '''Rules''' (Reverse): ''Be the first player to get hit by an incoming Bullet Bill!''
* '''Controls''': ''Hold the Wii Remote sideways. Press {{button|wii|Pad}} to move.''

== Etymology ==
Billistics is a pun of "ballistics," a word meaning the flight characteristics of a projectile.
