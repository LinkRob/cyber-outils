{{Nihongo|'''Minotaur'''|ビーフン|Bīfun}} is the second boss in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' who appears to be a red bull with blue overalls. He is a member of the [[Black Sugar Gang]]. He is located at the end of [[Mt. Teapot]]. He is immune to ground pounding and Wario jumping on him, due to his muscular appearance and sharp horns on his head. Minotaur tries to ram into Wario as an attack and also tries to attack Wario by attempting to throw him into the lava. Wario must throw him in the lava instead to defeat him.
