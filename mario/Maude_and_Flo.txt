|race = Crow}}'''Maude and Flo''' are a pair of crows that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Biography ==
Maude and Flo spend their time in [[Twilight Town]] sitting on a tree gossiping with each other.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
[[Mario]] and [[Vivian]] can eavesdrop on their conversations by using [[Veil]] on the ground near them. Maude mentions being married to a crow named [[Bob (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|Bob]] with a son named [[Billy]]. Flo, on the other hand, has many children and mentions [[Ronnie]] as her oldest child.

== Trivia ==
* Unlike the other crows of Twilight Town, these two are the only ones with confirmed names.
