The '''Suitcase''' is a is an object item that appears in [[Mario (franchise)|''Super Mario'' series]]. It is used by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], given by [[Toadsworth]] as they can use it to find or acquire [[List of items|items]], [[Badge|badges]], and [[Clothing|clothes]], and keep them inside the suitcase, as well as their [[Beanbean Map|map]] and passports during their travel on the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. It has a major role on ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|its]] [[Reissue|remake]] on the title screen.

The Suitcase would be replaced by [[Stuffwell]] in the sequel, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. By the time of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and later [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]], the suitcase concept is replaced by a simple menu from which the player can access the features of the suitcase.

== History ==

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''/''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ''+'' ''Bowser's Minions'' ===
[[Mario]] was about to walk to the [[Koopa Cruiser]] until [[Toadsworth]] caught up to him and brought the Suitcase too him. Meanwhile, [[Luigi]] was mistaken to be part of the [[Koopa Cruiser]]. Bowser instructed Mario and Luigi to press {{Button|gba|select}} to open their suitcase and arrange their stuff.

Mario and Luigi went pass by Bowser's minions having passports, the photographer halted Mario and Luigi. He instructed them to open their Suitcase and he checked through the Suitcase and realized that they do not have passports and allows them to cut in line.

The Suitcase was used for different items and the [[Beanbean Map]].{{M&LSSNav}}
