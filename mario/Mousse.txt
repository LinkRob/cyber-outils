}}A '''Mousse''' is a recipe made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==

It is made by mixing a [[Cake Mix]] with a [[Big Egg]]. If the player combine a Mousse with a [[Chocolate Cake]], it will result with a [[Gingerbread House]]. When used it gives 15 HP and cures poison.
