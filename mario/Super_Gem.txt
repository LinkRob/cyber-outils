'''Super Gems''' are collectibles that appear in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' and are a [[Captain Toad]] equivalent to [[Mario]]'s [[Green Power Star]]s. They are supposed to represent diamonds and also have eyes, like for example the [[Power Star]].
