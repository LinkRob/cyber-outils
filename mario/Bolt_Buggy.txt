The '''Bolt Buggy''' is a kart appearing in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'']] series.

== Description ==
The Bolt Buggy has a chassis that resembles the [[Pipe Frame]]. It is light yellow, with headlights on the front. The emblem is located on the back, on a navy blue block.

== ''Mario Kart'' series ==
=== In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' ===
The Bolt Buggy debuted in ''Mario Kart 7'', as a default vehicle part. It appears to be [[Donkey Kong]]'s signature kart in the game, along with the [[Barrel Train]]. It gives a boost in speed, weight and off-road, but hinders acceleration and handling. However, the Bolt Buggy has the lowest calculated stats total, similar to the [[Gold Tires]], [[Peach Parasol (glider)|Peach Parasol]] and [[Flower Glider]].

During races, a computer controlled [[Bowser]], Donkey Kong and [[Rosalina]] may use the Bolt Buggy frequently.

The Bolt Buggy is used in [[Time Trial]]s, for the normal [[Staff Ghost]] on [[DK Jungle]], and the Expert Staff Ghost for [[Kalimari Desert|<small>N64</small> Kalimari Desert]] and [[DK Pass|<small>DS</small> DK Pass]].
