}}A '''Spiky Skellobait''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Spiky Skellobaits are versions of [[Skellobait]]s with spikes on their heads. Spiky Skellobaits are only found in [[The Overthere]] and [[Castle Bleck Interior]]. Like with Skellobaits, Spiky Skellobaits attack with an icy breath which can [[Freeze]] on contact. Since they have spikes on their heads, Spiky Skellobaits cannot be jumped on unless the player is already riding on the [[Pixl]] [[Carrie]].
