A '''Huge Red Electrokoopa''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Huge Red Electrokoopa is a large variation of a [[Red Electrokoopa]] that, despite its name, wears a dark green shell instead of a red one.

===Behavior===
The Huge Red Electrokoopa can be found sleeping, a trait mainly associated with [[Snooza Koopa]]s. It does not attack [[Mario]].

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Like the other [[Koopa Troopa]] variants, the Huge Red Electrokoopa is encountered in [[Pinna Park]], specifically during Episode 5: [[The Runaway Ferris Wheel]]. Although the Huge Red Electrokoopa is classified as an enemy, only one ever makes an appearance.

In The Runaway Ferris Wheel, the Huge Red Electrokoopa is sleeping on a [[Chain-Link]] behind the [[Ferris Wheel]]. The electrical discharge emitted by Huge Red Electrokoopa causes the ride to spin uncontrollably. To defeat the Huge Red Electrokoopa, [[Mario]] must hit the [[Flip Panel (Chain-Link)|Flip Panel]] beneath it. In doing so, the Huge Red Electrokoopa falls into the water, the Ferris Wheel goes returns to moving at normal speed, and a [[Shine Sprite]] appears for Mario to collect.
