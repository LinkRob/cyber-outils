*[[Tiny Kong]]}}'''Banana Fairy Island''' (also known as '''Banana Fairy Kingdom''') is a location that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==Description==
Banana Fairy Island is a small island with the main area resembling a giant fairy head.

==History==
Banana Fairy Island is located beside [[DK Isles]] and can only be accessed by [[Tiny Kong]] with her [[Mini-Monkey]] ability. Upon entering, Tiny can then talk with the [[Banana Fairy Princess]] who is crying about the [[Banana Fairy|Banana Fairies]] leaving due to [[King K. Rool]]'s appearance. After Tiny agrees to help, the Banana Fairy Princess gives her a [[Banana Fairy's Camera|camera]] and [[Banana Camera Film|film]] to capture the Banana Fairies in and a useful shockwave attack that can take out most enemies in a single hit (and unearth the dirt piles that have the rainbow coins). After retrieving all of the Banana Fairies, Tiny can come back here to a pleased Banana Fairy Princess who gives a [[Golden Banana]] as a reward.

There is also another Golden Banana behind the head locked behind a gate. Tiny must use her [[Feather Bow]] to unlock it.

==Trivia==
*There is a glitch to allow the other Kongs ([[Donkey Kong]], [[Diddy Kong]], [[Lanky Kong]], and [[Chunky Kong]]) to gain access into Banana Fairy's Island. It involves climbing on the vines underneath the eyes.
