'''Hard Court''' is a recurring tennis court in the {{series|Mario Tennis}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]]. Aside from the ''Mario Tennis'' games, the Hard Court also appears in ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]''.

==Description==
In its appearances, the Hard Court has a blue surface that the characters play on. The Hard Court is a regular tennis court and essentially the same as the average tennis court from the real world. As such, the Hard Court does not feature any gimmicks, similar to the [[Clay Court]] and [[Grass Court]], and the main difference with the Hard Court is that its surface allows for a ball speed of Normal and a bounce strength of Strong.
