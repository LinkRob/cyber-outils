'''Daisy's father''' has been mentioned by [[Princess Daisy]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

== Profile ==
Daisy's father does not appear in the game. However, Daisy mentioned him after wailing her loss against her opponent. She said she had never lost to her father.<ref name="Daisy's father in Mario Party 3">''''"Augh! I don't believe this! I've never lost - not even to my dad!"'' Daisy, ''[[Mario Party 3]]''</ref> Daisy's royal status hints that her father is a ruler too.
