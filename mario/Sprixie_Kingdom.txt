The '''Sprixie Kingdom''' is the setting of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It is located on an [[Sprixie Homeworld|Sprixie Planet]], whose environment is similar to that of the [[Mushroom World]]. The main inhabitants of the Sprixie Kingdom are the magical [[Sprixie]]s, whose [[Sprixie Princesses|leaders]] are kidnapped by [[Bowser]], presumably for their magical [[Green Power Star|Green Star]] power. It is assumed to be a neighboring kingdom to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], [[Beanbean Kingdom]], and [[Pi'illo Kingdom]].
