'''Witch's Brew''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' It uses the Gift Giver form for its gameplay, and [[Orbulon]] acts as its host, hence its potential to appear in his level, Sweet Dreams, Sweeter Fruit.

== Layout ==
The protagonist is a purple-skinned man standing near a cauldron. His master, a witch with grey skin and black clothing, stands on the opposite side of the cauldron with a parchment in her hands.

== Gameplay ==
The player has to compare the silhouette on the parchment that the witch holds to the objects in the protagonist's hands, then drop the object that matches the parchment into the cauldron. To drop the object, players must turn the matching hand. If the object matches the silhouette, the player wins and the witch chuckles as the cauldron's brew lights up while the protagonist gives a thumb's up. However, if he drops in the wrong ingredient, she gets angry and the player loses.

=== Difficulty Levels ===

* '''Level 1 (Easy)''': The two objects have distinctly different shapes. The witch holds the parchment still.
* '''Level 2 (Hard)''': The two objects have similar shapes. The witch waves the parchment around slightly.
* '''Level 3 (Expert)''': The two objects have similar shapes just like in Level 2. The witch waves the parchment around more.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Mixing together dangerous chemicals in real life results in a real "witch's brew," but this version of the "witch's brew" is a literal one, as a witch has someone brew potions.
