'''Rail Riders''' is a 4-player and duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. This minigame is also played in [[Score Scuffle]], where it is the first one, with its successor being [[Domino Effect]].

==Opening==
The camera moves up the rail of the stairs, and zooms onto the players on leafs.

==Gameplay==
The player will need to repeatedly slide the stylus up, creating speed on the railways. The players build up enough speed to make a high jump off the rail, and mark their distance on the carpet.

The faster players slide the stylus whilst using the full length of the screen, the more speed they gain.

==Ending==
The player(s) with the highest score does their/his/her victory pose, while the other players do their/his/her losing poses.
