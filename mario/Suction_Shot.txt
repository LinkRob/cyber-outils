The '''Suction Shot''' is a move in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Description==
[[Luigi]] can use the move while equipped with the [[Poltergust G-00]]. Using it involves Luigi shooting out a suction cup with a long string attached, and the suction cup can attach to certain objects. If the suction cup sticks, Luigi can use the Poltergust G-00 to yank the string and then smash the object onto the ground.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3''==
To have Luigi shoot out the suction cup, the player must press {{button|ns|y}}. If the suction cup sticks to an object, the player can have Luigi pull back and then press {{button|ns|a}} for him to smash the object onto the ground, similarly to whenever Luigi smashes a [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]]. This move is especially useful for destroying furniture, pulling weapons away from ghosts, and uncovering hidden [[gem]]s.

The Suction Shot has an unlockable variation named Suction Shot Type B, turning the plunger into a diamond plunger. Suction Shot Type B can also be used against ghosts, which causes them to get their face stuck in the suction cup. Afterwards, by yanking the string and pressing {{button|ns|a}}, the ghost becomes dizzy, making it easier for Luigi to vacuum it. The Suction Shot can stick to [[Goob]]s, [[Oozer]]s, and [[Slinker]]s.

The Suction Shot has an unlockable add-on called the [[Suction Shot Type-C]], which can be unlocked by collecting every [[Gem]].
