'''Konga Coliseum''' is a recurring stadium in the {{series|Mario Strikers}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Konga Coliseum takes place on [[Donkey Kong Island]], and the stadium itself is made out of wood. It is the special field of the [[Super Flower Cup]].
