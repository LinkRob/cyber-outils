The '''Speedometer''' is a feature that shows you how fast your kart is accelerating. It appears on the bottom right-hand corner of your game screen in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It can be toggled on and off in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

==Trivia==
*A speedometer would have returned in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', as shown in some early screenshots, but was removed at some point during development.
