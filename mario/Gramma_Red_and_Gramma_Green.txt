'''Gramma Red and Gramma Green''' are two elderly [[Toad (species)|Toads]] who run an item shop named [[Gramma's Place]] together in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. The spots on their mushroom cap are colored red and green respectively, comparable to the colors of [[Mario]]'s and [[Luigi]]'s sets.

The old ladies appeares hiding in their shop after Shroob Invasion, they confirm they are the only survivors and use this as a way to gain Mario and Luigi's sympathy into bying items, recklessy ignoring the current situation and fall of the [[Toad Town]]. 

They are seen after [[Shroob|Shroobs]] were destroyed for good, celebrating.

They are never met in present, as the game heavily implies, they survived the original invasion (the one where [[E. Gadd's time machine|Time Machine]] wasn't used). Due to their age, it is safe to say they died out of neutral causes.
