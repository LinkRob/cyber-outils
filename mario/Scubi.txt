}}A '''Scubi''' (also called a '''Whip Shuri'''<ref>The official guide to ''Donkey Kong 64'' refers to them as Whip Shuris.</ref>) is an enemy that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
Scubis are a species of [[Shuri]] that can be only found in the sunken ship areas of [[Gloomy Galleon]] and during the [[Splish Splash Salvage]] minigames. These malicious starfish swim around in the waters until they spot a Kong and attempt to damage them with their whips. Since Scubis are underwater, they cannot be defeated and must be avoided.

==Trivia==
*The Scubi's name is a combination of the words "Shrui" and "scuba" being the name of it's species and also referencing "scuba divers."
