'''Veg Out''' is a [[microgame]] featured within [[Dr. Crygor]]'s [[Reality]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to stop the rotating slot so that the photograph in the middle properly depicts the same vegetable as the icon to the upper-left; the same vegetable's icon is stated in the microgame's opening. The possible vegetables can be a cucumber, a potato, a tomato, a pepper, broccoli, or a carrot.

Levels 1, 2, and 3 feature four, five, and six different vegetable slots respectively.

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name refers to the phrase "veg out," meaning to do nothing for a long period of time.
