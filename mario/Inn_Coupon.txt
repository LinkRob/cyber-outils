An '''Inn Coupon''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
The Inn Coupon resembles a small, golden card. The Inn Coupon allows [[Mario]] to stay in any [[Inn]] or hotel in the game for free. These items are hidden, although a few can still be found in plain sight. Because of their appearance and their ability to hide well, Inn Coupons are mostly mistaken for [[Star Piece]]s.
