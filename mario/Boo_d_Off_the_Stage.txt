'''Boo'd Off the Stage''' is a [[Duel Minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame involves the characters having to run around while avoiding [[Pink Boo]]s within a 30-second time limit. Some walls are placed around the path as obstacles to slow the characters. The Pink Boos come in small and large sizes. Whoever gets caught by a Pink Boo first loses. If both characters manage to avoid the Boos to the end or are simultaneously caught by one, the minigame ends in a tie. 

==Controls==
*Control Stick: Move
