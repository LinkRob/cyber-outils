{{plagiarism}}
In every course of ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' a picture of [[Luigi]] is hidden, whether it be a sprite, an image in a wall, or something else. There are a total of 83 Luigi sightings (two in [[Flying Squirrel Ovation]]), a reference to [[Super Mario Bros.|Luigi's debut year]].
