'''Toad Park''' is [[Toad]]'s home stadium in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''. There is a track circling the center and a scoreboard the to the north of it.

==Sports==
=== Dodgeball ===
In dodgeball, instead of one large field, they are divided into three courts, red, yellow and blue. It randomly picks which one you play at. Cones are on the courts and if the ball hits one, the cone will blow up.

=== Hockey ===
Instead of three courts, one long field is played. Using your inline skates, teams must dodge cones and shoot the puck into the goal. On top of the goal, there is a small timer. On the floor of the field, are [[Mushroom]] etched upon the blue flooring.

== Trivia ==
*Interestingly enough, instead of [[Shy Guy]] blocking the goals, cones are replacing them. They, however, prove to be much less effective.
