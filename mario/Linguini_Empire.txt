The '''Linguini Empire''' is a one-time location that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Great Gladiator Gig]]." It is a kingdom similar to ancient Rome or Greece even features its own [[Linguini Empire Colosseum|colosseum]]. The ruler is not known, but [[Brutius Maximus Grouchimus]] is of some noble rank there, due to serving as the guardian of the Linguini Empire Colosseum.

The Linguini Empire is named after {{w|linguine}}, a type of pasta.
