'''Wonder Effect''' is a gimmick that occurs upon collecting a [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. While their effects vary, they are the core mechanic of the game. They also appear in [[Super Mario Run]]. 

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
====''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''====
One of the effects that happen when you collect one is making objects alive, like pipes going up and down, or pipes moving like worms. The effect is terminated with a [[Wonder Seed]]. Almost every Wonder Effect happens in the level, "The Final Battle! Bowser's Rage Stage"
