'''Slaughterfork''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is a living fork that acts similar to a [[Spoonatic]].

== History ==
It is a sentient fork about the size of [[Wario]] that attacks by aiming at him and charging against him, like the [[Spoonatic]]s and [[Jack the Knife|Jacks]]. Slaughterforks can only be found on the [[S.S. Caviar]] and they can be taken out with any attacks.

== In-game Description ==
{{Quote|Forks dropped under the table and forgotten are twisted into these monstrosities by their own bitter resentment.}}

== Trivia ==
*According to its game description, it is the result of a fork that was forgotten and dropped under the table.
