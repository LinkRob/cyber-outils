'''''Undake30 Same Game''''' is a tile matching ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game released the [[Super Famicom]] in 1995, exclusively in Japan. ''Undake30 Same Game'' was first released on a cartridge medium, although only through promotional means, and then broadcast for the [[Satellaview]] shortly afterward.
