The '''Magic Treasure Box''' is an object which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The Magic Treasure Box is a golden [[Treasure chest|treasure box]] created by [[Kamek|Magikoopa]], and it contains an unlimited amount of [[coin]]s.
