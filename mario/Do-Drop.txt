'''Do-Drops''' are a large raindrop enemies featured in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Do-Drops have the appearance of a large raindrop with googly eyes.

===Behavior===
A Do-Drop damages a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] upon hitting it, which also causes the Do-Drop to dissolve. Given they can damage Yoshi, it is possible that a Do-Drop is acid rain.
