The '''Ice-blue Poltergust''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Ice-blue Poltergust is an icy-blue and silver variation of the [[Poltergust 4000]]. It uses a larger version of the [[Pipe Buggy]]'s tires.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
This kart is the signature kart for [[Penguin Luigi]]. It is a High-End kart. This kart increases the duration of [[Dash Panel]] speeds boosts and the points given of performing them. This kart was first available in the [[Ice Tour]].
