'''Gas Mask!''' is a [[Super Ability]] used by [[Wario]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. He later received a similar move named [[Wario Waft]], first appearing in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' 

==Description==
By using Gas Mask!, Wario lets out a big fart, leaving behind a trail of toxic dark green gas behind him as he moves around. If Wario decides to run while the move is active, he can run slightly faster than normal. Whenever someone is inside of the gas, their directional controls are inverted, except for Wario himself or a character under the [[Invincible Mario|invincibility]] effects of a [[Starman]].
