The '''Tower Lobby''' is a room in [[Haunted Towers]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is accessed west of the [[Courtyard (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Courtyard]] and the Tower Lobby leads out to multiple rooms with the [[Maintenance]] and the [[West Garden]] downstairs and the [[Plant Nursery]] upstairs.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
During [[A Job for a Plumber|B-1: A Job for a Plumber]], Luigi heads through the Tower Lobby in order to access through rooms to get to the [[Water Supply]].

Regularly, Luigi can head into the Tower Lobby in later games to find [[coin]]s, [[Gold Bar]]s, and sometimes [[Golden Bone]]s.
