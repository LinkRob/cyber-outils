'''King Nimbus''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He is the real father of [[Mallow]] and the husband of [[Queen Nimbus]]. King Nimbus is the king ruling over [[Nimbus Land]].

==Physical description==
[[File:King Nimbus SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
King Nimbus is a [[Nimbus]] with brown hair and a brown mustache. He wears fancy blue clothes to symbolize his status as royalty.

==History==
During the events of the game, King Nimbus with his wife were kicked off their throne and locked into a room by [[Valentina]]. Using [[Dodo]] to disguise himself as [[Mallow|Prince Mallow]], Valentina attempted to take over [[Nimbus Land]] as its new queen. Fortunately, the real Mallow shows up and he along with [[Mario]] and the others defeat Valentina and Dodo and free King Nimbus and Queen Nimbus. King Nimbus then reunites with his son and shows him and the others the way to [[Barrel Volcano]] as thanks for freeing him and his wife.

During the end credits, King Nimbus can be seen with Mallow, who is happy to be appointed as prince.
