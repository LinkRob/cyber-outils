'''Star Storm''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can be used once [[Mario]] defeats [[Tubba Blubba]] and frees [[Skolar]]. Star Storm requires two [[Star Power|SP]] to use, and it is the only direct offensive ability that Mario can use.

==Effects==
With Star Storm, Skolar makes an appearance to attack all enemies on this screen and deal 7 points of damage. This is considered very useful due to the attack power and range. Star Storm is also similar to the [[Shooting Star (item)|Shooting Star]] item.

==Trivia==
*Star Storm is somewhat similar to [[Earth Tremor]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' as they are both the third special moves obtained in the game and can attack all enemies on the screen for high damage.
