'''Needlenose Plants''' are an enemy variant of [[Egg Plant]] which appear in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and went on to reappear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' and then in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

A Needlenose Plant has the exact appearance of an Egg Plant, but behave differently in that they spit out green [[Needlenose]]s instead of [[Yoshi Egg]]s like an Egg Plant does. Needlenose Plants can spit out several Needlenoses at once unlike Egg Plants, which spit out Yoshi Eggs individually. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has a full set of six eggs, a Needlenose Plant does not spit out any Needlenoses.

In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', Needlenose Plants behave differently in that they spit out yellow Needlenoses, even if a Yoshi has a full set of six eggs.
