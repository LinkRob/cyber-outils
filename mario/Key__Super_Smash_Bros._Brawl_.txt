'''Keys''' are items featured in [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]] of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. They are found somewhere near locked gates. If the player's fighter finds a key and has it in hand while approaching a [[Gate (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|gate]], they can unlock it.
