'''Space Tour''' was the eighty-seventh tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] which began on January 10, 2023 and ended on January 24, 2023. This tour is also rerun on January 9, 2024 to January 23, 2024 and January 7, 2025 to January 21, 2025.

This tour features space themes in which [[Mario (Satttelaview)]] returns from [[Space Tour (2021)]] along with Astronaut suits of a [[Blue Toad]] and [[Toadette]] who become new drivers which are based on atronaut [[Toad (species)|Toads]] that are seen since [[Mario Kart 8]] in online mode. This tour also added [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart Wii)|Rainbow Road]] from [[Mario Kart Wii|''Mario Kart Wii'']].

In the first half of the tour, players could recieve [[Toadette (Astronaut)]] and [[Pink Toad (Pit Crew)]] can be recieved as rewards for placing high in the Ranked Cups of [[Kamek Cup (Space Tour) (2023)|Kamek Cup]].
