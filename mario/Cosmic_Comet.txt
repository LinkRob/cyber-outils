
[[File:Cosmic_Comet.png|thumb]]A '''Cosmic Comet''' is a type of [[Prankster Comet]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must race against [[Cosmic Mario|their cosmic counterparts]]. At the end of the race, there is a [[Power Star]]. If the Cosmic version of Mario or Luigi gets to the Power Star first, the player loses a life, and has the option to start again, or return to the [[Comet Observatory]]. The Cosmic Comet doesn't reappear in the sequel but a similar one called the Clone Comet appears. This comet basically spawns multiple [[Cosmic Clone]]s.

Cosmic Comet share their theme with the Fast Foe Comet.

==Locations==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
*[[Honeyhive Galaxy]] (Honeyhive Cosmic Mario Race; Mario must race [[Cosmic Mario]] through the Starting Planet.)
*[[Freezeflame Galaxy]] (Frosty Cosmic Mario Race; Mario must race [[Cosmic Mario]] to the end of the Ice And Lava Platforms Area, though the lava tiles are now frozen solid and can be Skated upon. This is the only Cosmic Mario Race whose [[Gravity]] changes.)
*[[Gold Leaf Galaxy]] (Cosmic Mario Forest Race; Mario must race [[Cosmic Mario]] through the forest to reach the [[Power Star]].)
*[[Sea Slide Galaxy]] (Underwater Cosmic Mario Race; Mario must race [[Cosmic Mario]] through the Sea Slide and to the light house area to get the [[Power Star]].)
