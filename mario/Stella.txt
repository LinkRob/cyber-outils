'''Stella''' is the guardian of [[Sparkle Theater|Sparkle Theatre]] in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]].'' She is a fairy who teams up with [[Princess Peach]] to save the theatre after [[Grape (character)|Grape]] and her [[Sour Bunch|minions]] ruin it. Stella can extend her ribbon to help [[Princess Peach]] interact with enemies and elements of scenery, and Stella herself can magically grant Peach new transformations, including Swordfighter Peach, Detective Peach, Pâtissier Peach, and Kung Fu Peach.

== Physical Appearance ==
Stella is a fairy with a yellow, star-shaped head, blue eyes, and a deep turquoise outfit consisting of a dress and a ribbon that seems to function as a pair of arms.
