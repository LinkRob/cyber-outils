'''Tricky the Triceratops''' is the first boss in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]'' in the boss level of [[Dino Domain]]. Tricky was brainwashed by [[Wizpig]] along with [[Bluey the Walrus|Bluey]], [[Bubbler the Octopus|Bubbler]], [[Smokey the Dragon|Smokey]] and given a piece of a [[Wizpig Amulet]] afterwards. 

When Tricky is raced, he can squish the player with his mighty legs to attempt to beat them and make it more challenging for the player to win. There are also items not required much to be in front of Tricky, but are still useful however. There are also boulders that can squish the player if making contact with one like Tricky can. There is also a shortcut with a [[zipper]] in it. After the player completes the race by winning the second race, Tricky becomes free of being brainwashed and gives the player one of the Amulets and opens the Silver balloon challenges.

==Trivia==
*Tricky is related to a styracosaurus enemy, Tricky in the Star Fox series as both are dinosaurs and are related to each other, due to their appearance, name, and personalities.
