'''Trueform Pin''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When the user equips the Trueform Pin, enemy attacks that would normally inflict the [[Scarecrow]] or [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]] ailment does not affect the user. The Trueform Pin can bought for 60 [[coin]]s at a shop either in [[Rose Town]] or from [[Seaside Town]] after defeating [[Yaridovich]]. A Trueform Pin can also be obtained by defeating [[Pandorite]] in [[Kero Sewers]].
