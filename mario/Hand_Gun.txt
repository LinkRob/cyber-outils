The '''Hand Gun''' is one of [[Geno]]'s personal weapons in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought for 75 [[coin]]s either at the shop in [[Marrymore]]'s hotel or from the [[Shaman]] merchant at the [[Sea]] or [[Sunken Ship]]. Equipping the Hand Gun on Geno increases his [[Attack]] power by 24. If Geno uses the Hand Gun in battle, six bullets are fired from his right arm.
