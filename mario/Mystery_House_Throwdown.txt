'''Mystery House Throwdown''' is the mystery house located in World 6 of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The mystery house mainly encompasses throwing baseballs towards specific targets to earn [[Green Star]]s. It contains five courses. It can be accessed through a warp pipe after completing [[Fuzzy Time Mine|World 6-7]].

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': In Section 1, throw a baseball towards the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 2''': In Section 2, jump once and then jump again while holding the baseball and throwing it in midair to get the star over the [[Flipswitch]] within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 3''': In Section 3, jump on the trampoline with a baseball and throw the ball to the P Switch to get the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 4''': In Section 4, jump with a baseball and throw it to the top [[POW Block]] to have the other balls land on the [[Para-Biddybud]] and other POW Block to get the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 5''': In Section 5, throw the baseballs to the switches behind the Thwomps to get the star.
