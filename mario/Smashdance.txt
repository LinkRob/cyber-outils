}}'''Smashdance''' is a 4-player mini-game found in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
The players must ground pound the glowing panels in order to score points. Panels will glow for just a short time and turn blue just as they are about to fade. The player who ground pounds the most glowing panels wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|stick}} - Move
*{{button|gcn|A}} - Jump
*{{button|gcn|A}} → {{button|gcn|A}} - Ground-pound

== Trivia ==
* The name is a pun of ''Flashdance'', the movie.
