A '''Silver Dice Block''' is a [[Dice Block]] which first appears in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'', having a different role between both games.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party: Island Tour''===
A Silver Dice Block is a type of Bonus Dice Block obtained by whoever finishes a minigame for Bonus Dice Blocks in second place in [[Perilous Palace Path]], or third in [[Bowser's Peculiar Peak]]. Upon using a Silver Dice Block, the character can roll it alongside the regular Dice Block, and the Silver Dice Block adds 1-3 to the total roll, depending on the number it lands on.

===''Mario Party: Star Rush''===
The Silver Dice Block only appears when [[amiibo]] are used. If an amiibo has save data from ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' on it and a [[Silver Token]] is detected before starting [[Toad Scramble]], [[Balloon Bash]], or [[Mario Shuffle]], a Silver Dice Block is added as an item at the start of the game. The Silver Dice Block can roll either a 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, or an 8.
