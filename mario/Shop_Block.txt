'''Shop Blocks''' are an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Description==
Shop Blocks are a type of [[block]] owned by [[Broque Monsieur]] that appear in various locations. A Shop Block can only be used by [[Bowser]], as activating one requires him to punch it. Upon being punched, the Shop Block plays a small jingle before Broque Monsieur appears on the monitor.

The Shop Block's main purpose is to sell items and equipment, the inventory of which gradually expands over the course of Bowser's journey. Additionally, a Shop Block can also fully restore Bowser's [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Special Point|SP]] from [[Negative Ions]] if he pays ten [[coin]]s and provide information on the fifteen [[Blitty|Blitties]].
