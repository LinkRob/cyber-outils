'''''[[The Legend of Zelda (franchise)|The Legend of Zelda]]''''' is a long-running major video game franchise by [[Nintendo]] that is closely related to the {{franchise|Mario}}. It is also the name for a few different [[microgame]]s in the {{series|WarioWare}}:

*[[The Legend of Zelda (WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!)|The Legend of Zelda]], from ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''
*[[The Legend of Zelda (WarioWare: Twisted!)|The Legend of Zelda]], from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''
*[[The Legend of Zelda (WarioWare: Touched!)|The Legend of Zelda]], from ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''

A few other subjects also have "''The Legend of Zelda''" within its name:
*[[The Legend of Zelda x Mario Kart 8|''The Legend of Zelda'' × ''Mario Kart 8'']], a DLC pack for ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.
*[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild]], a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'', named after the [[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|game of the same name]]
