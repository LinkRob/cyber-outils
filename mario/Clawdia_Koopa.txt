'''Clawdia Koopa''' is a character mentioned in a popular rumor that is unspecified by ''Nintendo Power UK'' magazine article. Her brief appearance sparked many fan theories, such as her being the wife of [[Bowser]]. However, the writers themselves confirmed that Clawdia was an unofficial character, and she was never mentioned in the franchise ever again.
