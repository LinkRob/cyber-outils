}}A '''Shrui''' (also called '''Starfish'''<ref>''They are referred to as Starfish in [[Donkey Kong 64]] during the [[Splish Splash Salvage]] minigames</ref>) is an enemy that appears in the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong series]]''.

{{sectstub}}

===''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''===
Shruis return in this game as uncommon enemies. Shruis can be first encountered in [[Angry Aztec]] in the Monkey Temple and very commonly later in [[Gloomy Galleon]]. Shruis swim around in the water until they spot a Kong. Shruis will then swim toward the Kong and do a spinning cartwheel at them. Another species of Shruis known as [[Scubi]]s also appear in this game. They attack with a whip instead of a spinning cartwheel. They also appear as the main enemies of the minigame [[Splish Splash Salvage]].
