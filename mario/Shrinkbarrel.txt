'''Shrinkbarrels''' are an object which appear in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''. They only appear in two [[level]]s, [[Bad Manor]] and [[Boogie Mansion]].

==Description==
A shrinkbarrel has the appearance of a regular brown [[barrel]] with yellow left and right directional arrows and two openings, one of which is larger than the other. [[Wario]] can enter the large opening to emerge as [[Tiny Wario|Mini Wario]] from the smaller opening. Conversely, Mini Wario can enter the smaller opening to transform back into regular Wario, but entering the large opening as Mini Wario does not have any effect.
