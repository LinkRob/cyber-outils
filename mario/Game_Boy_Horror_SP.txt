The '''Game Boy Horror SP''' is an item invented by [[Professor E. Gadd]] that appears as an equippable item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is designed after [[Game Boy Advance SP]].

==Effects==
With the Game Boy Horror SP, regular enemies that normally have a chance of dropping rare items when defeated always do so. This is helpful when trying to get a good item along the quest or when grinding [[Woo Bean]]s for stats.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
The Game Boy Horror SP can only be obtained by creating a different blend of [[Starbeans Espresso|espresso]] at [[Starbeans Cafe]] for the seventh and final time. When [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] do this, the Game Boy Horror SP is given to them as a gift from E. Gadd.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name combines "Game Boy Advance SP" with "[[Game Boy Horror]]," another invention of E. Gadd's from ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''.
