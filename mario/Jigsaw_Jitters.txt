'''Jigsaw Jitters''' is an [[Extra Room]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. It can be unlocked by playing [[Photo Finish]] at least once.

==Gameplay==
Jigsaw Jitters is similar to Photo Finish, except the puzzle has a lot more pieces—either 20, 30, or 40—and the player has to complete the puzzle in the quickest possible time. Each of the puzzles depict a scenic artwork featuring the eight playable characters. The default completion record for each of the puzzles is five minutes. If the puzzle is not finished within ten minutes, the minigame ends.

A few of [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s [[Present Room]] items can be unlocked by meeting certain criteria in Jigsaw Jitters. If a 20-piece puzzle is completed within a minute, Toad's teacups are placed in the Present Room's minigame room. If a 30-piece puzzle is completed within two minutes, Toad's teapot is unlocked. If a 40-piece puzzle is completed within three minutes, the Toad plate is unlocked.
