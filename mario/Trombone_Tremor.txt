|games = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}'''Trombone Tremor''' is [[Lanky Kong]]'s musical instrument attack from ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. This technique can be purchased from [[Candy's Music Shop]] in [[Angry Aztec]]. Lanky preforms this technique on his trombone.

==Usage==
Like with all of the other musical instrument attacks, Trombone Tremor can be used on music pads to trigger events or to defeat all enemies in the area.
