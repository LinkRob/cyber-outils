A '''Stomp''' is a second part of a [[Jump]] when landing on an enemy. The move first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. Not all stomps occur from jumps, like when [[Mario]] falls off a platform. Stomps are generally Mario’s primary attack, as it requires a type of power up such as [[Fire Flower]] to attack enemies in another way. Some enemies are invulnerable to being stomped on, including spiked, ghostly, underwater (prior to 2023), or fiery enemies. As a result, Mario will have to defeat the specified enemy a different way. If Mario attempts to stomp on enemies that are invulnerable to stomps, Mario himself can take damage.

Enemies that are vulnerable to stomps are often flattened, but some, such as [[Koopa Troopa]]s, retreat into their shell(s) when stomped on. In some games such as Super Mario World, Koopa Troopas fly out of their shells when stomped on. One stomp will defeat a shell-less Koopa Troopa.

Enemies that are vulnerable to stomps in the underwater as of June 2023, such as [[Cheep Cheep]]s, [[Blooper]]s, and more.
