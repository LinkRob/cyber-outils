'''[[Fox McCloud]]''' is a major supporting character in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. Whilst piloting his Arwing, McCloud's warp drive malfunctions sending him through a dimensional rift that leaves him stranded in a new [[Universe (Illumination)|universe]].

===History===
Fox was a space pilot who replaced his father James as leader of the Star Fox team, protecting the Lylat system from villains like Andross and other aliens, as well as events in Starlink: Battle for Atlas and those who threaten the peace, alongside his teammates Falco Lombardi, Peppy Hare, and Slippy Toad, but one day a rift in spacetime opened, and he ended up in the Gateway Galaxy. He then tries to return to his universe.

=== ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'' ===
While Fox was searching for a way to return to his universe, he eventually arrived at the "Gateway Galaxy." While repairing his Arwing, he encountered Peach and Toad, who were trying to hire a pilot to take them to Bowser's planet. Fox agreed after Peach and Toad offered to pay him for the ride. Eventually, Mario, Luigi, and Yoshi arrived at the Gateway Galaxy and meet up with them before boarding Fox's Arwing and flying off to the Space Junk Galaxy.
