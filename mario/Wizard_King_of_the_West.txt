The '''Wizard King of the West''' is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[A Toadally Magical Adventure]]." His name is a pun on the {{w|Wicked Witch of the West}}, the main antagonist of ''{{w|The Wizard of Oz}}'', although the Wizard King of the West is not a villain.
