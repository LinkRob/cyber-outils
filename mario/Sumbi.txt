==History==
Sumbi is a [[Nimbi]] that is found in [[The Overthere]]. Sumbi was found in a block of ice and was eventually freed by [[Bowser]].
==Trivia==
*Like many of the other Nimbis, Sumbi is named after a season or month. In this game, she is named after Summer.
