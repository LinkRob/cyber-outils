'''Black dozer''' is a [[Kart]] from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]].''
== In [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] ==
The Kart made it's first debut in [[Sunset Tour]] as a High-End kart. It is a black and silver variant from the [[Dozer Dasher]] version. This kart type is comonly used for [[Builder Mario|Builder Toad]] and [[Builder Mario|Builder Luigi]]. Unlike the Dozer Dasher, this version provides longer Rocket Boosts. It uses the Tires of [[Brown Offroader]] which are the oposite those used by [[Dozer Dasher]].
