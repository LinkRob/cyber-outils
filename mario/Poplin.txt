'''Poplins''' are a species native to the [[Flower Kingdom]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They are voiced by [[Christine Marie Cabanos]].

== Appearance ==
A Poplin is a humanoid being that resembles a [[Toad (species)|Toad]]. They are about the same size (albeit slightly taller), and have a headdress with a flower head, white poofy trousers, and shoes matching the color of their headdress and flower.

==Games==
=== ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ===
They seem to replace the regular Toad species in this game, as the only Toads in the game are [[Blue Toad]], [[Yellow Toad]], [[Toadette]], and [[Captain Toad]]. They are ruled by [[Prince Florian]].

=== ''Mario Tennis Fever'' ===
The appear as audience members in the Flower Kingdom court, alongside [[Prince Florian]] as the referee. 

==Trivia==
*If the player finished a level while being an [[Elephant Fruit|Elephant]] and having water, they will throw the water at a Poplin and the bud on the Poplin's head will sprout into a flower.
*They are the [[Flower Kingdom]]'s equivalent of the Toads.
