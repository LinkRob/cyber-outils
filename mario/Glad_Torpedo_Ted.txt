'''Glad Torpedo Teds''' (spelled '''G. Torpedo Ted''' in the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]]) is a variant of [[Torpedo Ted]] under the effect of the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] [[vibe]]. It appears as an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Glad Torpedo Ted resembles a Torpedo Ted, but has a golden body and a wide smile on its face.

===Behavior===
Glad Torpedo Teds move in a zigzag pattern instead of straightforward like a regular Torpedo Ted. Like their regular counterpart, Glad Torpedo Teds cannot be defeated.
