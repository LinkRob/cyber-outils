'''Fudjie''' is an item in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Up, Up, and a Koopa]]."

==Description==
Fudjie is essentially an ordinary chocolate bar.

==In ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''==
When [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] are held captive in a cage on [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s [[Doom Ship]], Mario attempts to bribe a [[Rocky Wrench]] guard with a Fudjie, hoping to convince the Rocky Wrench to open the cage. The Rocky Wrench growls at Mario, who then returns the Fudjie back into his pocket.
