'''Ranged EXP Beans''' are an obtainable item in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Locations==
Ranged EXP Beans can be gained by completing levels, or by dismissing troops. Better Ranged EXP Beans are gained in harder levels, or by dismissing troops with a higher level.

*Ranged EXP Bean S can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 11 or higher, or by dismissing Ranged troops that are level 1-13.
*Ranged EXP Bean M can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 16 or higher, or by dismissing Ranged troops that are level 14-24.
*Ranged EXP Bean L can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 25 or higher, or by dismissing Ranged troops that are level 25-29.
*Ranged EXP Bean DX can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 30 or higher, or by dismissing Ranged troops that are level 30 or higher.

==Types==
===Ranged EXP Bean S===
The Ranged EXP Bean S gives one recruited Ranged troop 10 EXP, or it can be used on a Flying or Melee troop to give them 5 EXP.
===Ranged EXP Bean M===
The Ranged EXP Bean M gives one recruited Ranged troop 100 EXP, or it can be used on a Flying or Melee troop to give them 50 EXP.
===Ranged EXP Bean L===
The Ranged EXP Bean L gives one recruited Ranged troop 1000 EXP, or it can be used on a Flying or Melee troop to give them 500 EXP.
===Ranged EXP Bean DX===
The Ranged EXP Bean DX gives one recruited Ranged troop 3000 EXP, or it can be used on a Flying or Melee troop to give them 1500 EXP.

==Flavor text==
*The Ranged EXP Bean S in-game description is: "A Bean that gives 10 EXP to Ranged troopers. Effect is halved if eaten by other unit types."
*The Ranged EXP Bean M in-game description is: "A Bean that gives 100 EXP to Ranged troopers. Effect is halved if eaten by other unit types."
