|type = 4-player mini-game}}'''Bob-omb Breakers''' is a 4-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' and ''Mario Party DS''.

==Gameplay==
In this game, players must match at least three pictures to score points. Players can use blocks to box in [[Bob-omb]]s to destroy nearby blocks. Players can even place blocks into other player's spots.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|stick}} - Move blocks.
*{{button|gcn|a}} - Rotate clockwise.
*{{button|gcn|b}} - Rotate counterclockwise.
*{{button|gcn|x}}/{{button|gcn|y}} - Flip
