'''Rotary Lifts''' are a type of object featured in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and its [[reissue|remakes]]. Rotary Lifts are unstable to use as platforms as they spin around, putting [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] at risk of being thrown into an enemy or a hazard, including a pit.
