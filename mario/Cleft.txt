'''Clefts''' are enemies in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and return in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Originally, in ''Paper Mario'', Clefts had a brown appearance and had red boots. Instead of the brownish color and red boots they had in this first game, they now are gray and have black boots. In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Clefts didn't make an appearance as regular "Clefts," but "[[Moon Cleft]]s" did. Moon Clefts were Clefts from ''Thousand-Year Door'' that had a very similar color scheme to that of the original. Oddly enough, the Moon Clefts in ''Super Paper Mario'' do not appear on a moon at all, as they appear in the Crag land.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Clefts are powerful rock enemies with spikes on them, and can be tough to beat. These guys are found on the steep ranges of [[Mt. Rugged]]. These creatures, in the overworld anyway, appear like a normal gray-colored rock. However, they reveal their true color and appearance when approached.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
Clefts return as enemies, and they are just as hard to defeat, but still have the weakness of a quake attack. These guys appear in the [[Glitz Pit]] as well, against the "Mind Bogglers."
