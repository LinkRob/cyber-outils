The '''Beef Cloud''' is a boss that appears in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He is one of the [[Beef-Off#Hooraws|Hooraws]] that must be fought in order to obtain a [[Hooraw Medal]] in order to see [[Big Massif]].

==Appearance==
Beef Cloud is a large, round Hooraw that wears white clothing. His eyes and mouth are triangular.

==Battle==
Beef Cloud is most notable for not attacking at all during his battle but, instead, sleeps and heals himself. For that, [[Mario]] must keep attacking quickly with [[Jump]] moves in order to take him down. Beef Cloud restores his health in real time as well. after enough damage, Beef Cloud will wake up and cannot heal, after which he can be hit without Luiginary Attacks without healing too much.

Beef Cloud has the highest defense (105), the second-highest stamina (357), and the lowest power and speed (1).
