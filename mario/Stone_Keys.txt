==History==
The Stone Keys consist of the [[Sun Stone]] and the [[Moon Stone]]. Both of these items are required to access [[Hooktail Castle]] so that [[Mario]] can get the first [[Crystal Star]] from [[Hooktail]]. However, Mario must venture into [[Shhwonk Fortress]] in order to get the Stone Keys. They are located opposite from each other and both are being guarded by [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]]. As Mario and [[Goombella]] try to leave, the [[Gold Fuzzy]] stops them not wanting them to escape with the Stone Keys. However, the Gold Fuzzy is soon defeated allowing Mario and Goombella to leave with the Stone Keys.
