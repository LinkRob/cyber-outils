'''Super Jump Panels''' are an object exclusively appearing in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
Super Jump Panels are a large, red variation of [[Jump Panel]]s. Like their regular counterpart, Super Jump Panels launch [[Mario]] upward if he [[jump]]s while standing on it, except that they launch him a lot higher. If [[Cat Mario]] immediately becomes [[Lucky Cat Mario]] after using a Super Jump Panel, he can collect a lot of [[coin]]s at once.

==In ''Bowser's Fury''==
Three Super Jump Panels appear at the start, middle, and end of [[Pounce Bounce Isle]]. The first two of these are hidden under [[crate]]s, and if any crates are remaining when Mario uses the Super Jump Panel, they are all destroyed. There is also a Super Jump Panel at the entrance to the Wasteland region, in front of its [[Giga Bell]].
