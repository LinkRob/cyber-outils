The '''Eagle Statue''' is a power-up item which exclusively appears in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. Touching it turns [[Wario]] into his [[Eagle Wario]] form. The Eagle Statue has the appearance of a sparkling statue of an eagle (hence its name) with a handle on the back.
