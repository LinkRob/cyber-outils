'''Sign, Steal, Deliver'''   is a [[1 vs. 3 Minigame|1-vs-3]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
A solo player is controlling a drone and the team players are standing. The solo player and team are waiting for the delivery trucks to arrive and they stand back.

== Gameplay ==
The aim of the minigame is to deliver the most packages to the [[Spike]] with the truck by color. The solo player can use the drone to grab packages and steal packages from the team, but the player can carry one package at a time with the drone. The team can climb up the three-story building and can grab two packages at a time. However, the team players may not grab the package before the drone as they use the stairs. Either the solo player or the team player wins if they have the most packages.

== Ending ==
If the solo player wins, the player does his/her victory animation with the drone clapping, and the players on the team do their losing animation. If the team wins, they do their winning animation while the solo player does his/her losing animation and the drone flops in disappointment. If it is a tie, the players do their neutral animation while the drone does nothing. The delivery truck drives off in both versions.
