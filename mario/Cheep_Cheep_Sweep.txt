'''Cheep Cheep Sweep''' is a 2-vs.-2 mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

== Gameplay ==
In this minigame, the two teams must work together to scoop up as many [[Cheep Cheep]]s into their nets as they can and empty them out to their respective tanks. There are two types of Cheep Cheeps: the red Cheep Cheeps are worth 1 point, while the yellow Cheep Cheeps are worth 2 points. The team that collects the most Cheep Cheeps wins.

== Controls ==
*{{button|gcn|stick}} - Move
*{{button|gcn|a}} - Catch
*{{button|gcn|b}} - Empty net
