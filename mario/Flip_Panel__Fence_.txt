'''Flip Panels''' are an object featured in a few {{series|Super Mario}} and {{series|Mario Party}} games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
A Flip Panel is a part attached to a [[Chain-Link]]. [[Mario]] or another playable character can [[punch]] it to have it rotate them over to the opposite side of a Chain-Link. A Flip Panel can be used from either the front or the back. In the 2D ''Super Mario'' games, whenever Mario or another playable character uses a Flip Panel to go behind a Chain-Link, they can only grab on to Chain-Links from the back unless they use a Flip Panel to flip back to the front.
