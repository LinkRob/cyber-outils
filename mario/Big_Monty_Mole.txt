'''Big Monty Moles'''<ref>"''Try searching while '''picking off a big Monty Mole.''' Surely you're up to the challenge!''" - [[Blockafeller]], ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''</ref> (大きなチョロプー ''Ōkina Choropū'', Large Monty Mole) are enlarged [[Monty Mole]]s first seen in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''.

==History==
===''Super Mario Maker / Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS / Super Mario Maker 2''===
[[Image:Mega Mole Maker.png|thumb|left|A big Monty Mole above [[Mario]].]]
Big Monty Moles first appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as it's [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|two]] [[Super Mario Maker 2|sequels]] as placeable enemies. In these games, they can be made by giving a [[Super Mushroom]] to a Monty Mole. In all 4 styles, big Monty Moles behave identically to the standard Monty Moles and also look identically to them only larger.

===''Yoshi's Crafted World''===
[[Image:Mega Mole Background Yoshi.png|thumb|left|A big Monty Mole in the background.]]
Big Monty Moles also appear in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''. In this game, they appear in [[Monty-Mole-B-Gone]] as semi-common enemies and are always found in the background of each level they're in, and unlike the smaller Monty Moles, must be hit with two [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] to be defeated. It becomes red and enraged when hit once (with a similar look to that of [[Morty Mole]]) but is brought down upon a second hit. Big Monty Moles often appear when a red [[Winged Timer Cloud]] is hit, and must be defeated to make a [[Flower (Yoshi's Island)|Flower]] appear.

==Trivia==
*Two enlarged Monty Moles appear in hole 7 of [[Wiggler Park]] in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''. However, it is unknown if they are big Monty Moles or not.
*Although no normal big Monty Moles appear in ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', two notable members to them do. These members being [[Super Choropū]] from volume 1 and [[Choropūko]] from volume 23.
