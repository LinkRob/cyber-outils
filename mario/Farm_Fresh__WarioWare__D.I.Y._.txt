'''Farm Fresh''' is one of the [[Job Center]] [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''.

==Gameplay==
Prior to starting the microgame, the player has to draw a vegetable. During gameplay, they have to use their [[Stylus]] to tap the grass on the ground to pick the vegetables before they become rotten. If done successfully, the player wins the microgame. If the vegetables rot or at least one is not plucked before the timer expires, the player loses at the microgame.
