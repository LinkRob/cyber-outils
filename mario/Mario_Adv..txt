'''Mario Adv.''' is one of [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s which appears in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''.

==Layout==
Mario Adv. is directly based on ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'', although the microgame uses slightly different assets than that of the origin game. The sprites are less colored than in ''Super Mario Advance'', which incidentally causes some, such as the [[Tweeter]]'s, to more closely resemble its sprites from the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' version of ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. The background music that plays is a higher pitched variation of the overworld theme from ''Super Mario Advance''.

Given the grassland setting, Mario Adv. appears to take place in a [[World 1 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1]] level, although the HUD reads World 0-0, and the microgame takes place in an area that does not appear in the original game. The HUD in general is like that of ''Super Mario Advance''{{'}}s: the [[Health Meter]] on the left shows Mario with two [[small heart]]s, meaning he is in his [[Super Mario (form)|Super form]] during the microgame; one [[Ace Coin]] is shown to have been collected; and the score is at 1,983. There are two grass platforms, and a small gap is placed between them. [[Mario]] is on the left platform, which is slightly wider than the one on the right, which has an enemy on it.

==Gameplay==
The object is for Mario to throw a [[vegetable]] at an enemy to defeat it. The enemy is either a [[Shy Guy]], a [[Mega Guy|Big Shy Guy]], a [[Tweeter]], or a [[Ninji]], selected by the game at random. If Mario misses the enemy or does not throw a vegetable within the time limit, the player loses at the microgame.
