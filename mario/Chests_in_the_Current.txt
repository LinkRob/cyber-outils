'''Chests in the Current''' is the second mission of [[Dire, Dire Docks]] featured in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Layout==
[[Mario]] has to swim underwater to where a giant whirlpool is and open [[Treasure Chest|Chests]] surrounding it in a specific order, similar to [[Jolly Roger Bay]]'s [[Treasure in the Ocean Cave]]. In doing so, a [[Power Star]] is rewarded.
