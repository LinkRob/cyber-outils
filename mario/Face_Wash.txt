'''Face Wash''' is one of the [[microgame]]s under [[Mona]]'s [[That's Life]] category in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to splash water onto a man's face to rinse the soap off the lower half. Upon completing this objective, the man can be seen smiling. If they do not rinse the soap off, the player loses at the microgame.

[[9-Volt]], [[Orbulon]], and [[Penny Crygor|Penny]] are listed as the three most suitable characters. For 9-Volt, his ground movement and use of his yo-yo can cause continuous large splashes of water. Similarly, Orbulon can use his [[Oinker]]'s tractor beam to make large and high splashes of water. Penny can easily use her [[Jet Tank One]] to rinse the soap off the man's face. [[5-Volt]] is listed as the least suitable because her teleportation ability is ineffective at creating large splash of water. However, if 5-Volt splashes near the middle, she can cause a huge splash that rinses all of the soap off the man's face.

In two-player mode, Face Wash received a few differences so that it is better suited for two players. The container holding water is a lot longer, and the man moves left and right. His entire face is covered with soap rather than just the lower half. If the characters win at the microgame, whoever washed off more soap receives an effort point. In [[Rising Star]] mode, they receive a star instead of an effort point.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The man's head does not move.
*'''Level 2:''' The man's head moves up and down.
*'''Level 3:''' The man's head moves in a circular motion.

==Mission==
*'''Lather, Rinse, DO NOT Repeat''': As [[Jimmy T]], the player must complete Level 3 using only one move. This mission is only doable in single-player mode.
