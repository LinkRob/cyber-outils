'''Roy's Ironclad Castle''' is the castle in [[Rock-Candy Mines]] in the game ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. 

==Layout==
The castle begins on [[Iron Block]]s, with a [[? Switch]] found nearby. Pressing the switch causes the Iron Blocks to begin moving. A [[Dry Bones]] is found, along with with a [[? Block]] and a [[Conveyor Belt]]. A Conveyor Belt with Iron Blocks dropping from the ceiling is found, followed by a Dry Bones on a Conveyor Belt. More Iron Blocks are dropped onto Conveyor Belts, followed by a platform. A ? Block is found, along with another ? Switch that causes more Iron Blocks to begin moving. Two Iron Blocks with [[Bony Beetle]]s are found moving along a Conveyor Belt. Another ? Switch is found, along with stacks of Iron Blocks in the lava. After the switch is pressed, the Iron Blocks begin moving and lowering into the lava. Bony Beetles and a [[P Switch]] are on the Iron Blocks. A Bony Beetle is found at the end of the area, followed by a platform leading to the boss door. Like in ''New Super Mario Bros. U'', Roy Koopa is the boss.

It is notable that this castle has a shortcut, which can be accessed by taking the alternate route from [[Sumo Bro's Spinning Tower]]. The third Star Coin, which is otherwise unaccessible, can also be obtained, along with a ? Block.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': At the start of the level, the player must press the [[? Switch]] to make the Iron Blocks fall, along with the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': In a similar way to the first Star Coin, the player must press another ? Switch to make some Iron Blocks move, and get the Star Coin below quickly, before it becomes impossible to get.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is only accessible from the shortcut from [[Sumo Bro's Spinning Tower]]. The player must avoid the Iron Blocks on the Conveyor Belt, then grab the final Star Coin in the level.
