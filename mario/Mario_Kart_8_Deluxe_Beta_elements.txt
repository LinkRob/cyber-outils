== Unused Wii Coconut Mall Car Routes & Settings ==
The cars in ''Wii Coconut Mall'' have a set route to follow, along with a set speed and set settings which would be related to the car behavior when following the routes. The cars ignore the route and does not attach to it at all, since they don't move and their rotation is not facing the route. By looking at the values of the settings, it shows that one of them could be the amount of time it stays stopped until following the route again.

By setting an object that follow routes (such as ''N64 Toad's Turnpike'' for example) to follow the route the cars ''in Wii Coconut Mall'' are linked to, and set the same speed, it shows that they follow the routes and would've looked like something the player would see in ''Mario Kart Wii'', which shows the routes were indeed intended to be for the cars. Because of the cars in the other tracks (like ''N64 Toad's Turnpike'') being coded differently, they don't behave as intended in the routes and are then teleporting back to the start point of that route.<gallery>

== Leftover Wii U Battle Tracks ==
All tracks that were used as battle arenas in the Wii U version still have the '''battle_muunt.byaml''' in their files, meaning that they can be accessed and battled on like the original game (with some hacks).

== Removed content ==
=== Removed test track ===
There are leftovers for a test track ('''test_DanceFloor'''). All that remains of this is some lighting data.

=== Removed Reverse Mode ===
At one point of development, there were originally going to be plans for a racing mode where the racers go around the track backwards.  In '''ObjFlow.byaml files''', there are six references to "'''Gyakusou'''" (which translates to "'''Reverse Run'''" in Japanese).  This concept was later resurfaced in ''Mario Kart Tour'' as the R and R/T variants of tracks.

* TEST_Gyakusou_Barrier
* TEST_Gyakusou_Dash
* TEST_Gyakusou_Glide
* TEST_Gyakusou_Guide
* TEST_Gyakusou_Step
* TEST_Gyakusou_StepJA

=== Removed difficulty ===
In most of the AI tables ('''RaceCommon/AI'''), there's some AI settings for an Expert difficulty. There are no other references for this difficulty, so it may have been cut from the game in early development. Though why it was removed is unknown, it's possible that may have simply been too hard for the players than at 200cc.
