}}'''Shy Guy Shuffle''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Gameplay==
There will be five [[Shy Guy]]s. One contains five donuts, one contains four donuts, and so on. Then the [[Shy Guy]]s will start to move. The player can use the Wii Remote to keep track of the [[Shy Guy]] with five donuts. After they shuffled, the player can choose. There are only ten seconds for you to choose each round, so you must be fast. There are a total of three rounds. At the end, it shows all the characters eating donuts.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|A}} − Choose
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} − Select
