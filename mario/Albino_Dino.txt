'''Albino Dinos''' are beta enemies and regular characters found in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Biography==
Three do appear as stable statues in the [[Crystal Palace]] and as creatures who can be talked to and flip in the palace when their corresponding statues are moved into a different position when [[Mario]] has to solve a puzzle involving them. When completed, the allow Mario to move on. They are also white variations of [[Dino Rhino]]s. They are found in the room right next to the [[Crystal King]]'s lair.
