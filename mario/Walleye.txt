The '''Walleye''' is a variant of [[Wallop]] which debuted in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. It has hands, feet, and a hat with spikes on the side. Walleyes make their debut appearance in the stage [[Ty-Foo Flurries]] from [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]] and can later be found in [[The Great Goal Pole]] of [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]] and round 11 of [[Mystery House Marathon]]. A Walleye needs to be passed while it is walking left to right blocking the way.

Walleyes also appear in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'', behaving the same as in ''Super Mario 3D World''. A Walleye can only be defeated while under the effects of an [[Invincibility Mushroom]]. 

In ''[[Super Mario Party]]'', Walleyes are one of the obstacles in the platforming minigame [[Follow the Money]]. Walleye is among one of the possible images shown in the minigame [[Absent Minded]].
