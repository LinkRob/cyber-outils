'''For the Galaxy where this gimmick serves as the core element, see [[Matter Splatter Galaxy]].'''

'''Matter Splatter''' is a gimmick in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. The gimmick made its debut in the [[Ghostly Galaxy]], where it is the focus of the secret star Matter Splatter Mansion, and it appears later with a greater role in the [[Matter Splatter Galaxy]].

== Description ==
The Matter Splatter Gimmick involves some terrain being comprised of invisible matter and bubbles of solidity engulfing sections of it, creating solid, visible sections that can be stood on normally. If [[Mario (character)|Mario]] steps on an invisible section, he will fall through as though he were standing on nothing. The [[Matter Splatter Galaxy]] introduces bubbles that can shrink and grow, with an arc of light bouncing on them to make them grow; without this arc, the bubbles shrink back down until the light returns to augment their size.

== Appearances ==

=== [[Ghostly Galaxy]] ===
In the secret star Matter Splatter Mansion, the secret mansion accessed will have some solid platforms and bubbles of solidity.

=== [[Matter Splatter Galaxy]] ===
The mission Watch Your Step has this gimmick strewn throughout the galaxy. The setting is Greco-Roman ruins.
