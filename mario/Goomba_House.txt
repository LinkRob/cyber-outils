'''Goomba House''' is owned by [[Goomba (Mario Party Advance)|Goomba]] in the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It appeared in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Description ==
Goomba House is located in the [[Town Area]]. It is on the right of the [[Train Station]].

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
Found in the Town Area, the player can accept Goomba's quest for the [[Kind Goomba]] quest.
