'''''Donkey Kong Country''''' is a 2003 remake of the [[Donkey Kong Country|SNES game of the same name]] for the [[Game Boy Advance]]. The difference is, this version is less downgrade than the [[Donkey Kong Country (Game Boy Color)|2000 GBC remake]], though some graphical and sound clarity are still lost due to the smaller screen and lack of a backlight. The GBA version includes new content, such as greater boss diversity and in-game cutscenes.
