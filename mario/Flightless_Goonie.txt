'''Flightless Goonies''' are a recurring wingless variant of [[Goonie]]s featured in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming titles. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. Flightless Goonies have their own skeletal variant named [[Flightless Skeleton Goonie]]s.

A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can turn a regular Goonie into a Flightless Goonie by either eating then spitting it back out or by throwing a [[Yoshi Egg]] or an enemy at it. Some Flightless Goonies already do not have wings, and presumably did not start out as a Goonie.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Flightless Goonies have the same appearance as Goonies but without a pair of wings. Specifically, Flightless Goonies have a white head, two stubby fork-shaped feet, a yellow beak, and three strands of black hair on its head.

===Behavior===
Upon noticing a Yoshi, a Flightless Goonie tries to run into him. They generally stick together in a pack, just like Goonies.

==List of appearances==
Flightless Goonies have a consistent role of being an enemy in all of their appearances.

*''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' ([[Game Boy Advance]]) - 2002
*''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'' ([[Nintendo DS]]) - 2005
*''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' (Nintendo DS) - 2006
