It is very rare and like the [[Megaflash Infinijump]], it is found in the level [[Long Fall Falls]]. With this, Mario can stomp on foes up to 100 times.
==Description==
The Big Shiny Infinijump sticker looks identical to the Megaflash Infinijump sticker except for the size and silver background. It takes up one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].
