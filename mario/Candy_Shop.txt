'''Candy Shops''' are locations in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
They are stores where players can purchase [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candies]]. Provided a player has at least five [[coin]]s, the player is allowed to enter a Candy Shop. Candy Shops are always run by the local residents of the board, which are [[Spear Guy]]s in [[DK's Treetop Temple]], [[Pianta]]s on [[Goomba's Booty Boardwalk]], [[Red Boo]]s in [[King Boo's Haunted Hideaway]], [[Shy Guy]]s on [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]], [[Koopa Troopa]]s in [[Koopa's Tycoon Town]], and [[Toady|Toadies]] in [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]].

Inside a Candy Shop, the player is presented with three Candies to choose from (if the player has fewer than 20 coins, two or even all three of the Candies may be identical). Candies usually cost between five to 20 coins, and they are sold at fixed prices. However, if a player's ranking is low, the Candy Shop they are visiting offers its Candies at a discounted price. [[Twice Candy]] is commonly sold in Candy Shops, but on occasions, a Candy Shop sells a variety of Candy such as [[Thrice Candy]], [[Duelo Candy]], and [[Bowlo Candy]]. However, the Candy Shop does not include some types of Candy, depending on the board.{{citation needed}} When the game reaches the last turn, the Candy Shops are closed.
