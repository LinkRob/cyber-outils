|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Deathsickle''' is a move used by [[Greaper]]s and [[Glum Reaper]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When using Deathsickle, the enemy has other enemies form around a single character in [[Mario]]'s party and attacks them.

==Effects==
Deathsickle has the chance to inflict the [[Fear]] aliment at the same time causing damage.
