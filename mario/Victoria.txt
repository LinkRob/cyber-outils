'''Victoria''' is a location in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show]]'' episode "[[The Adventures of Sherlock Mario]]."

==Description==
Victoria is based on [[England]] during the Victorian era, hence its name. It is described by [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] as being the land of mystery, cloaks and daggers and screams in the night and is somewhat dark and gloomy in appearance at night, being surrounded by extremely thick fog. A [[horse]] pulled carriage appears to be the main mode of transportation. Some notable landmarks of Victoria include the warped [[Tower of Victoria]], Herlock Solmes' home street, [[Bonkers Street]], and a [[Big Ben|Clock Tower]].

==''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] and [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] venture to Victoria in search of the famous detective [[Herlock Solmes]] and wind-up saving the city from [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Professor Kooparity]], [[Mouser (DiC Cartoons)|Mouser]], [[Tryclyde (DiC Cartoons)|Tryclyde]] and [[Koopa Troopa (DiC Cartoons)|Koopa Troopa]], who attempted to flood it.
