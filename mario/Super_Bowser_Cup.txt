'''Super Bowser Cup''' is a [[cup]] in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Strikers''==
Super Bowser Cup is the final cup, and it can be unlocked winning the [[Super Star Cup]] in the top three positions. Super Bowser Cup is essentially the same as [[Bowser Cup (Super Mario Strikers)|Bowser Cup]] but also has return matches. The Super Bowser Cup features eight teams competing. If the player wins the Super Bowser Cup in first place, they unlock the "Infinite Powerups" cheat.
