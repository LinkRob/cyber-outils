[[File:BeetlesLMDM.png|thumb]]'''Beetles''' are enemies in'' [[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They look like small green domes with wings. They follow [[Luigi]] and can cause five hearts of damage if Luigi comes in contact with them. First encountered in [[Old Clockworks]], they appear in [[Secret Mine]] in a blueish form as well as [[Treacherous Mansion]] in their normal green form.
