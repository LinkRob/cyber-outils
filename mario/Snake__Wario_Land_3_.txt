'''Snakes''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
A snake raises its body from a pot at the sound of a certain tune, referencing the act of {{w|snake charming}}. When its head reaches as high as possible, the Snake breathes fire onto the ground.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Snakes emerge from pots bearing the image of a snake's head only after Wario has acquired the Flute from [[The Stagnant Swamp]]'s Gray Chest, as the Flute plays a tune that causes the snake to emerge. One Snake appears in [[The Peaceful Village]], [[The Volcano's Base]], and [[The Grasslands]], eight appear in [[The Colossal Hole]], and lastly two appear in [[The East Crater]].

If Wario touches a snake's flames, he briefly becomes [[Flaming Wario|Hot Wario]]. Wario can [[jump]] onto a snake's head to use it as a platform for reaching certain higher areas. He can also perform a [[Ground Pound (move)|Smash Attack]] to retract the snake's head sooner. Snakes cannot be defeated.
