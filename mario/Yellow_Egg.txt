'''Yellow Eggs''' are a type of [[Yoshi Egg]] which appear in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Description==
The only visual difference that Yellow Eggs have from regular Yoshi Eggs is that they have yellow spots. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] throws a Yellow Egg at an enemy, it releases a [[coin]].

One way a Yoshi can obtain a Yellow Egg is by throwing a regular Yoshi Egg against a surface once, turning it into a Yellow Egg, and then catching the egg. If a Yellow Egg bounces against a surface, it turns into a [[Red Egg]]. There is a yellow [[Egg Block]] that transforms into a Yellow Egg if [[jump]]ed at from below, in the same manner as how red Egg Blocks turn into a Red Egg if jumped at.
