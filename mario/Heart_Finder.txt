'''Heart Finder''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. In both of its appearances, the Heart Finder costs three [[Badge Point|BP]] to equip.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Heart_finder.png|thumb|left]]
In ''Paper Mario'', the badge is found at [[Shooting Star Summit]]. It is one of the many badges that [[Merlow]] trades to [[Mario]]. It has a cost for twelve [[Star Piece]]s. When equipped, more hearts appear upon defeating enemies.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', this badge has the same effect as its ''Paper Mario'' counterpart, giving Mario more hearts upon defeating enemies. The badge is bought from [[Dazzle]], and she sells it at half the price of six Star Pieces.
