'''Bulrush Express''' is a course of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level opens with a [[Bulrush]] rushing forward and hitting another [[Bulrush]], allowing the player to enter a [[warp Pipe|warp pipe]].

After grabbing the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]] in the area where the pipe leads, the [[Wonder Effect]] will summon a herd of bulrushes. The player must ride the "Bulrush Express"  to the end of the level, as a [[Poison tide|Poison Tide]] rises. There are many [[P Switch]]es throughout the course. Once the herd exits, the poison tide continues rising, and the player must jump on some [[Semisolid Platform]]s with Bulrushes to escape it.

Before entering the pipe to the end of the level, there is a wall of [[Brick Block]]s to the right. Breaking these brick blocks with an [[Elephant Fruit]] will expose the path to the secret exit with a [[Red Flagpole]]. If the player does so, the next course, [[Sproings in the Twilight Forest]] will be unlocked.

== [[10-Flower Coin|10-flower Coin]] locations ==
All three 10-flower coins are easily accessible during the level. The first coin is found above a group of six [[Swirlypod|Swirlypods]], the second is found below a platform with a [[Warp Pipe]] directly above, and the third is above a big [[Swirlypod]].{{SMBWNav}}
