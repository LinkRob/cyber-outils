'''End of the Line''' is a Duel minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, two players are knocked onto a train and must guide their way to the correct path. However, only luck will give the player the right path each time. If the wrong path is chosen, the player will fall off and must start back over, though, the correct paths remain the same. The player that reaches the end first wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Select Tunnel

==Trivia==
*'''End of the Line''' comes from a term usually when a person is cornered and about to be captured.
*The train and location of this mini-game resembles [[Kalimari Desert]] from ''[[Mario Kart 64]].''
