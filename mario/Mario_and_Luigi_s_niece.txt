==Biography==

=== ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' ===
The niece is shown at the dinner table during the family gathering. She wears a pink cap backwards with a flower mark shown and a teal shirt. She appears focused on her phone during the scene, not paying attention to the conversation about her uncles’ Mario and Luigi's [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing (Illumination)|Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]] commercial. At the end of the movie, when the crowd is cheering for Mario and Luigi's saving [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]] from [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]], she is seen enthusiastically recording footage with her phone.{{TSMBMNav}}
