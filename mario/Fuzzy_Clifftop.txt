'''Fuzzy Clifftop''' is the first level of [[Rock-Candy Mines]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]. ''It is revealed when Skyward Stalk, Fliprus Lake, or Iggy's Volcanic Castle is completed, and this course itself unlocks a red Exclamation Mark Switch and Porcupuffer Falls.

==Layout==
The level begins on a platform, with a [[Fuzzy]] on a [[track (object)|track]] above. Two [[Stretch Block]]s are found, and the Fuzzy lands on a circular track located between them. A [[? Block]] containing a power-up is found, followed by one containing a [[Yoshi's Egg]]. More Stretch Blocks are found, along with Fuzzies on tracks. Some [[berry|berries]] on bushes are found, along with an [[Venus Ice Trap]] in a [[Warp Pipe]]. A [[Piranha Plant]] is found, along with more Stretch Blocks and Fuzzies. A bush with two berries on it is found, along with the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. A [[Paddle Platform]] on a track follows, and a Fuzzy is found above. Another Yoshi's Egg is located in a ? Block before another Venus Ice Trap. More Stretch Blocks and a Fuzzy are then found. A third Venus Ice Trap is found on upside down on a platform. A section with more Fuzzies and Stretch Blocks is found, leading to a Warp Pipe. The Warp Pipe leads to another Paddle Platform and the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first [[Star Coin]] is above a Fuzzy in the beginning of the level.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Before the section with a spinning four-way platform is a green [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the second Star Coin. The player needs [[Yoshi]]'s [[Flutter Jump]] in order to reach the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is in plain sight. It can be reached after a series of [[Piranha Plant]]s.
