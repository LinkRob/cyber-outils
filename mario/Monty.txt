{{Nihongo|'''Montys'''|モグ|Mogu}} are a species of brown moles. They appear as enemies in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They are seen residing in closable holes and pop out to throw wrenches at their enemies.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
A brown Monty wears a striped blue-and-white bandana. 

=== Behavior ===
[[Image:Montys Attack.png|thumb|left|Mario near two attacking Montys.]]
A Monty starts out inside it's hole, as [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] approach the hole, the Monty pops out. If the Monty see the plumbers, it will throw wrenches that will harm him. Montys are also very agile as they quickly duck back in their holes if the plumbers try to attack them, however, they can still be stopped as shooting a [[Star Bit]] at a Monty or ground pounding near a Monty can stun them. When stunned, stomping or performing a [[Star Spin]] on them defeats the Monty.

== In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' ==
Montys make their first appearance in [[Battlerock Galaxy]] as enemies. They make later appearances in [[Gusty Garden Galaxy]] and [[Dreadnought Galaxy]].
