'''Walking Piranha Plants!''' is the fifth level of [[Rock-Candy Mines]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Waddlewing's Nest]] or [[Light Blocks, Dark Tower]]. Its own completion opens the path to a blue Exclamation Mark Switch and the course [[Screwtop Tower]], while the secret exit opens the path to a red Exclamation Mark Switch and Screwtop Tower.
==Overview==
As the name implies, the main enemies of the course are Stalking Piranha Plants, while enemies like [[Goomba]]s and [[Koopa Troopa]]s are also present. Another gimmick of the course are chains which the player can use to swing across gaps with.

==Walkthrough==
The level begins with a chain that is used to reach a row of [[Wobble Rock]]s above a pit. A [[? Block]] containing a power-up is found, along with a [[Goomba]] and a [[Koopa Troopa]]. Another Wobble Rock and a chain are found, followed by a group of [[Stalking Piranha Plant]]s. More chains are found above Stalking Piranha Plants. Another section of chains over Stalking Piranha Plants follows, with the Stalking Piranha Plants on horizontally-moving [[lift]]s. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is then found. A [[Roulette Block]] is found near a Goomba and some Koopa Troopas. Another row of Stalking Piranha Plants follows, along with more Stalking Piranha Plants on lifts. A [[Red Ring]] is found above one of the lifts. Another chain is found, along with the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
* '''Star Coin 1''': After a series of Stalking Piranha Plants, hidden inside a wall.
* '''Star Coin 2''': Shortly after the first Star Coin in a gap in the sky which leads to the area with the second Star Coin.
* '''Star Coin 3''': Beneath an orange bridge, Flying Squirrel Mario is required to reach it.

== Secret Exit ==
Before the final chain is a hidden wall with a [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the secret exit. It unlocks a path to a red Exclamation Mark Switch and Screwtop Tower. This switch serve as an access switch to the second entrance of [[Roy's Conveyor Castle]] in case [[Thrilling Spine Coaster]] is not yet unlocked.
