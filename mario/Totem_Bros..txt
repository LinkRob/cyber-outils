The '''Totem Bros.'''<ref>''"It's a race to see who'll be the first to use Hip Drops to pound the Totem Bros. into the ground."'' &ndash; ''Mario Party 2'' Totem Pole Pound game rules</ref><ref>''"You must Hip Drop to pound the Totem Bros. into the ground. Finish first to {{color|clear|cyan}} this Mini-Game."'' &ndash; ''Mario Party 2'' [[Mini-Game Coaster]]</ref> are a type of living totem pole from ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. Totem Bros. vary in color, and each has two circular eyes and a mouth, similar in appearance to [[Grindel]]s.

In the [[minigame]] [[Totem Pole Pound]], characters must perform [[Ground Pound|Hip Drop]]s on the colored Totem Bros., forcing them into the ground. The higher the Hip Drop, the more a Totem Bro gets pounded into the ground. The first player to completely force their Totem Bro into the ground wins.
