'''Gorgons''' are bat and pig-like enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are stronger versions of [[Enigma]]s, and are only encountered on the [[Sunken Ship]].

==Battle==
Gorgons only appear in battle alongside other enemies and can be troublesome using many strong magical attacks such as [[Diamond Saw]].

Gorgons are weak to [[Thunder (element)|Thunder]] moves so [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] is effective against them.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Gorgons were three snake-haired sisters in Greek mythology. The victims who looked at them in the eye turned to stone.
