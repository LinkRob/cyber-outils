'''Neo Bowser Castle''' is the final area explored in the real world in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It makes a reappearance in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' as the final location.

==Layout==
Neo Bowser Castle is mainly a large, dark castle that has many of [[Bowser]]'s features including statues with his face on them, fire, and [[lava]]. It also shares [[Antasma]]'s features including many dark-vain like objects that is later revealed to help power the castle. Also, enemies including Flaming Antasmatons, [[Fire Bro]]s., and Walker Guys with [[Chain Chomp]]s are the main enemies encountered in the castle.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'', Neo Bowser Castlenow has giant rockets at the bottom that allows it to maintain flight thanks to the ore harvested from [[Twinsy Tropics]]. The interior is also very similar to the one from ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''.

==History==

=== ''Mario & Luigi'' series ===

==== ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' ====
Neo Bowser Castle is first created after Antasma used the [[Dream Stone]] to create it for Bowser as part of their agreement. The castle is then lifted into the sky where it is surrounded by a barrier forcing [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Starlow]] and [[Prince Dreambert]] to find a way to shatter the barrier. They eventually enlist the help of [[The Zeekeeper]] where he uses his Wake Beam to destroy the barrier. Mario, Luigi, and the rest all enter in but [[Kamek]] halts their process by mixing up the paths. Antasma then shows up and opens a portal to the [[Dream World|dream world]] where Kamek makes his escape into. [[Prince Dreambert]] suggests heading into there after him in order to fix the paths. Mario has to enter [[Dreamy Neo Bowser Castle]] two more times in order to finally get somewhere. After passing more puzzles, they eventually reach a room where Bowser is sleeping on a [[pillow]] of Antasma. However, there is another barrier that prevents Mario and Luigi from interfering but Prince Dreambert remembers the idea of entering another one's dreams since [[Big Massif]] at [[Wakeport]]. Luigi then falls asleep where Mario enters back into the dream world to confront Bowser.

{{Stub}}

===''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''===
Neo Bowser Castle was first made after the Bowsers (Bowser and [[Paper Bowser]]) harvested enough ore to power it up. They decided to take flight after Mario, Luigi, and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] rescued Princess Peach and [[Paper Princess Peach]] and defeated their respective sons ([[Bowser Jr.]] and [[Paper Bowser Jr.]]). The Bowsers then assaulted the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] wrecking [[Princess Peach's Castle]] in the progress. The Bowsers then decide to station over [[Mt. Brrr]] planning their next form of attack. Eventually, the Marios, Luigi, and Starlow managed to make it onto Neo Bowser Castle a second and final time to confront the Koopa Kings in a showdown. However, their process was hindered by the Kameks and the [[Koopalings]] whom the heroes all defeat. The Bowsers finally engage the heroes in a final battle where, even after [[Shiny RoboBowser|transforming]], the Bowsers were defeated. In the end credits, its implied Neo Bowser Castle lands back, reversed through its [[Bowser's Castle|original state]] as Mario and others, during the Papercraft Parade passes through it.
