A '''Flower''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Collecting a Flower increases [[Mario]]'s maximum [[Flower Point|FP]] by one. There are other variations of Flowers that also increase FP, at varying degrees, including the [[Flower Tab]], the [[Flower Jar]], and the [[Flower Box]].
