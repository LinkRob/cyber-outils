'''World 8-3''' is the third level of [[World 8 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 8]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''. 

==Layout==
The player begins before a platform that will plummet once the player hops onto it, come behind shortly by a [[Lakitu]]. Hoping onto the elevated platform, the player then needs to hops down onto a [[Springboard]] under to cross the next intermission, landing on another falling platform. On the next stretch of clouds, the player will encounter [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Hammer Bro]]s., and blocks containing [[Poison Mushroom]]s. Above the second Hammer Bro. is a block including a power-up and another block to the right including a [[Vine|Magic Vine]] conduct to [[Coin Heaven]], which will land the player at the end to just after a pipe with a [[Piranha Plant]] shortly after the second Hammer Bro. After the pipe is a block including a [[Starman]], and the player has to use the Springboard later on to cross the next intermission, which has [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s flying over it. The player will then come into contact with two Hammer Bros. before extend a [[Scale Lift]], which the player must use a block and a running jump to extend. Afterward is a small platform followed by a Koopa Paratroopa and the [[Goal Pole|Flagpole]].
