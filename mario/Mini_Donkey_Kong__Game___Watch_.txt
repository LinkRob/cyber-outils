'''Mini Donkey Kong'''<ref>''Game & Watch Multi Screen Donkey Kong'' English instruction, pages 1 & 3</ref> is a small [[Kong]] who makes an appearance in the [[Game & Watch]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong (Game & Watch)|Donkey Kong]]''.

While Mini Donkey Kong is not directly involved in gameplay, he appears when the system's alarm goes off, jumping and ringing a bell (he is located on the lower screen, under the difficulty indicators). Mini Donkey Kong can be seen as a predecessor to [[Donkey Kong Jr.]], who would make his debut later that year in [[Donkey Kong Jr. (game)|his own arcade game]]. Because the [[Game & Watch Gallery (series)|''Game & Watch Gallery'' series]] does not recreate the alarm feature of the Game & Watch games, Mini Donkey Kong does not appear in ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 2]]'', but in ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 4]]'', he appears in the starting sequence of the credits. However, the feature was reintroduced in ''[[Game & Watch Collection]]'', so Mini Donkey Kong appears in that game's remake of ''Donkey Kong''.
