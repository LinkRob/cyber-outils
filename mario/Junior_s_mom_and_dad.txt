'''Junior's mom and dad''' are a pair of two one-time characters who appear in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', in the episode "[[Misadventures in Babysitting]]." They have a misbehaved son named [[Junior]], whom they desire to get away from, and a pet cat named [[Kitty]].

== Trivia ==
They were drawn to look like their voice actors. Mom was voiced by Tabitha St. Germain, who often voiced Kootie Pie Koopa and Dad was voiced by Harvey Atkin, best known for Bowser.{{stub}}
