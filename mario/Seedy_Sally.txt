'''Seedy Sally''' is a type of [[Grinder (species)|Grinder]] found in the {{Series|Yoshi}}. They hold a [[Watermelon]] and spit its seeds at a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to attack. They can also run away from Yoshi just like a standard Grinder. Yoshi can also swallow a Seedy Sally's Melon as well, and its effects lasts a bit longer than a normal melon.
