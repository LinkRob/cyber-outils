== Overview ==
Donkey Kong Land is a sub-series of the [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong franchise]] which features 3 games, all for the [[Game Boy]]. All of these games are based on the [[Donkey Kong Country (series)|Donkey Kong Country series]].

== Games ==
* ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'' ([[Game Boy]], 1995)
* ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'' ([[Game Boy]], 1996)
* ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'' ([[Game Boy]], 1997)
* ''[[Donkey Kong Land III|Donkey Kong GB: Dinky Kong & Dixie Kong]]'' ([[Game Boy Color]], 2000) (Japan only)
