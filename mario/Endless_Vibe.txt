The '''Endless Vibe''' is an item in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Description==
The Endless Vibe allows for [[Princess Peach]]'s [[vibe]]s to be used without the Vibe Gauge and therefore indefinitely.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
The Endless Vibe is the last obtainable item from the shop. Peach can obtain it once she has cleared every level (including minigames), collected every musical score, and have a recorded time for finishing each Puzzle. Once the Endless Vibe has been purchased, the player can toggle its effect on or off via the "Bonus" section of the menu.
