'''World 3-2''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the second level of World 3, the [[Easton Kingdom]].

==Overview==
World 3-2 is mainly a forest-like level with some waterfalls around. The main enemies of this level are [[Kumo]]s and [[Suu]]s. Both of them are spider-like enemies that try to attack from the ground (Kumos) or the air (Suus). [[Ganchan]]s are also new enemies to this level being rock enemies that try to roll into Mario. There are also other hazards, such as falling spikes that can damage [[Mario]]. Mario must also carefully jump over gaps while avoiding the enemies of this level to make it to the end.
