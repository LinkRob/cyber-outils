'''Mallet-Go-Round''' is the Item [[minigame]] of [[Mystery Land]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. Its name is a pun on "{{wp|carousel|merry-go-round}}," a type of carnival or amusement park ride.

==Gameplay==
The player attempts to use a [[hammer]] to knock two blocks down on a roulette to get the item they desire. Each time the player knocks down a block, the remaining blocks on the roulette change direction and spin faster. If the player knocks down [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]]'s block (which is a single one, rather than two like the items), they get nothing. If the player loses by running out of time, the roulette simply stops.

The minigame can be purchased from [[Woody]] for 500 [[Coin]]s.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|A}} &ndash; Swing hammer
