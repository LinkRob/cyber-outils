'''Flower Grapples''' are objects found in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They resemble dual-plastic flowers with hooks in between and are commonly found in Galaxies where [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is found in. Touching a Flower Grapple directly does nothing but while [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] is riding [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], if the green dino is to use his tongue on one with the help of the [[Star Cursor]], he will extend his tongue to grab onto the Flower Grapple and flip upward gaining some extra height which allows him to reach higher platforms or effectively reach another Flower Grapple to go even higher, sometimes, Flower Grapples are used as a means of crossing high chasms, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can constantly swing from Flower Grapple to Flower Grapple to reach the other side or to simply reach items that are too high to reach. Flower Grapples are seen in several sections and planets of the [[Yoshi Star Galaxy]], the [[Hightail Falls Galaxy]], the [[Tall Trunk Galaxy]], the [[Haunty Halls Galaxy]], [[Bowser Jr.'s Fearsome Fleet]], the [[Sweet Mystery Galaxy]], the [[Flash Black Galaxy]], [[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]], and the [[Grandmaster Galaxy]]. Flower Grapples are also seen on [[Starship Mario]] after [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] joins the ship crew, they can be found above the nose (where the [[Yoshi Egg]] is located) and lead to an small island with 10 [[Berry|Berries]] which grant an extra life upon being swallowed by [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].
