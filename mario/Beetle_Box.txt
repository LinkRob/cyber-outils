The {{nihongo|'''Beetle Box'''|むしかご|Mushi Kago|Insect Cage}} is a special item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is purchased at [[Beetles Are Us]] from the [[Snifit (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Snifit]] who runs the store. A Beetle Box costs 50 [[coin]]s, or an additional 100 coins if it is [[Mario]]'s first time buying one. With a Beetle Box, Mario can store any [[Beetle (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Beetles]] that he caught in the [[Beetle Race]] on [[Booster Hill]] before turning them back in to Beetles Are Us to be valued. Mario has to purchase a new Beetle Box whenever he wishes to replay Beetle Race.
