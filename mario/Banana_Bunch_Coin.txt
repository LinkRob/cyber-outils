The '''Banana Bunch Coins''' (also simply as '''Banana Coins''') are items that appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. They are common items that can be found throughout levels (except for [[DK Isles]] and [[Hideout Helm]]).

==Summary==
The Banana Bunch Coins are common items in the game and are usually found near [[Bananas (Donkey Kong 64)|bananas]], the main purpose of the Banana Coins is that they are needed to pay [[Cranky Kong]] for various potions for abilities. The Banana Bunch Coins can also be used for other things such as playing the ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' arcade cabinet a second time for two Banana Bunch Coins, buying instruments from [[Candy Kong]] and buying weapons from [[Funky Kong]]. There are also special coins called [[5-Banana Coin]]s that can be unearthed from [[DK Dirt Pile]]s.
