'''Shroom Drop''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is one of the Gaddgets available from the start.

==Description==
The object is to take a [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]] to the goal. To accomplish this, the player has to move the rows, made up of [[Brick Block]]s, to make room for the selected mushroom to fall. However, a mushroom can fall through gaps and [[? Block]]s. There is not an option to reset the Gaddget, so when a Mushroom has made it to the goal, the player has to manually reopen the Gaddget to replay it.
