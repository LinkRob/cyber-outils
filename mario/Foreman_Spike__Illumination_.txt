'''Foreman Spike''' is the major [[Punch-Out!! Pizzeria (Illumination)|opening]] antagonist in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', who reforms in the end of the film. He is voiced by [[Sebastian Maniscalco]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Spike has a slightly different appearance in ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'', wearing an all blue workman's uniform with no overalls and a cap instead of a hardhat, with his nose being noticeably smaller than in previous design. His outfit has the logo "Wrecking Crew" on his hat and left sleeve. Despite his vastly different appearance, Foreman Spike's emblem still depicts his original sprite's design.

Foreman Spike's attire bears a high resemblance to Fix-It Felix, Jr. from ''Wreck-It-Ralph''. Coincidentally, [[Bowser]] and a [[Super Mushroom]] appear in the film, and Mario was planned to appear before being scrapped.

== Biography ==
[[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] resigned from their old job under Spike, and while the reason isn't explicitly explained, the SMBPlumbing.com website mentions that they left because they had enough of his unlikable attitude.

Spike first appears in the film at [[Punch-Out!! Pizzeria]], where he mocks the brothers (calling them the "Stupid Mario Brothers") after the airing of their first commercial, scoffing at the idea that the Mario Bros. will succeed on their own and telling them that their plumbing business will fail; when Mario stands up to him, Spike threatens him physically before leaving. When Mario gets flung back into Brooklyn due to the [[Bomber Bill (Illumination)|Bomber Bill]] exploding in the [[Mysterious Pipe (Illumination)|Warp Pipe]], Spike nearly runs over Mario and is shocked to see him land right in front of his van before he starts to yell at him for nearly damaging his vehicle, only to run away when other objects from the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] begin appearing. He is later seen among the panicking civilians when [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]'s army invades New York and is saved by Luigi while being attacked by Bowser's minions, much to his shock. After the final battle between the brothers and Bowser in Brooklyn, Spike is the one who starts up the cheers for Mario and Luigi, finally calling them "the Super Mario Brothers".

==Behind the scenes==
A fictional testimonial on the [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing (Illumination)|SMBPlumbing.com]] website is attributed to Spike, who goes by the username '''SpikeIsCool'''. Spike does not directly address the [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing (Illumination)|Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]] company itself, to which the website pertains, instead directing the testimonial towards the Mario brothers, who operate the company; in it, he is bitter and critical, stating, with many spelling and grammatical errors, that the brothers used to work for him until they left to start up their own business. He ends the testimonial by threatening that they will "learn their lesson someday."<ref>[https://www.smbplumbing.com/testimonials/ SMB Plumbing | Testimonials]</ref>

Since the release of the film, the testimonial has changed with Spike recognizing the brothers' heroism and increasing his rating of the company from one star to one and a half.
