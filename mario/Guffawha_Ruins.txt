|games       = ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''}}'''Guffawha Ruins''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It is located to the east of [[Teehee Valley]] and at the bottom of [[Hoohoo Mountain]] and, through the first time, can be only accessed by one person.

==History==
After [[Mario]] has been infected with [[Bean Fever]] from eating a [[Invincishroom|foreign mushroom]], [[Luigi]] has been sent to Guffawha Ruins to get the [[Crabbie Grass]]. However, Luigi first had to be hypnotized by [[Psycho Kamek]] into thinking he's Mario because of a horrible [[Guffawha Monster|monster]] that lives there. Luigi goes through many trials until he meets up with the "monster". The monster is angry that many people tell lies about it and requests Luigi to help him relieve his anger. Luigi does so leaving the monster pleased and allows Luigi to get the Crabbie Grass.
