'''Classic World 1-1''' is the first level of [[World-e]] in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It is a recreation of [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]] of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. Classic World 1-1 was available as an [[e-Reader]] card in both Japan and North America, but was later included in the base game for ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3'', along with every other e-Reader level.
