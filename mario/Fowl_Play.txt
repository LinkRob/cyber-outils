'''Fowl Play''' is a Duel Mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame involves both characters chasing a [[Chicken (real world)|chicken]] around the field, attempting to catch it. The chicken runs around in mostly zig-zags, making it tricky to catch at times. If a character gets too close to the chicken, it runs away. A character can jump over fences to try and cut it off and catch the chicken before their opponent. Whoever catches the chicken on time wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Move
*{{button|n64|a}} - Jump
*{{button|n64|b}} - Catch

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
*This mini-game's name is a play on "foul play," a term referring to bad practices.
