'''Mario in Real Time''' (also known as '''MIRT''') is a interactive device, used by [[Nintendo]] in different kinds of trade fairs. [[Minoru Arakawa]] made the device, and [[Charles Martinet]] takes the role of being [[Mario]]'s voice actor as usual. MIRT shows Mario on a screen being voiced by Charles Martinet (previously [[Stevie Coyle]]). Martinet can also move Mario with sensors. [[Luigi]] can also be shown on or off screen. There are different types of MIRT, such as '''[[Wario]] in Real Time''' ('''WIRT'''), and '''[[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] in Real Time''' ('''DKIRT'''). This device was also used for the [[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon|Dark Moon]] launch event, where Luigi himself greets guests to the event.
