The '''Tomb''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Description==
The Tomb is a room in the [[Old Clockworks]], and it can be accessed by going to the end of the hallway of the [[Antechamber (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Antechamber]]. The Tomb is covered in sand, and it has two torches on the north wall and a stone tomb in the middle of the room.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
During C-2: [[Underground Expedition]], [[Luigi]] must travel to the Tomb because [[Professor E. Gadd]] has pinpointed it as the location of the minute hand for the [[Clock Tower Gate]]. When Luigi inspects the stone tomb, an [[Ancient Poltergeist]] appears holding the minute hand. However, the Ancient Poltergeist flees with the object, and three [[Mummy|mummies]] for Luigi to fight. After the Mummies are defeated, the mission ends.
