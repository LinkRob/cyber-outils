'''World 7-3''' is the third level of [[World 7 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. The player is given a time limit of 300 seconds.

== Layout ==
This level features a similar layout to [[World 2-3 (Super Mario Bros.)|2-3]], except the player will not only encounter [[Cheep-Cheep]]s, but also [[Koopa Troopa]]s. The player will again be crossing a bridge, where they must dodge the fish. There are several coins to collect and only one [[? Block]], which contains a power-up. Like before, the Cheep-Cheeps will stop appearing once the player reaches the end of the level.

== Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' ==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 28000.

== Red Coins ==
* Red Coin #1 is located at the beginning, above the small set of stone stairs.
* Red Coin #2 is located high above the center of the third bridge, with a new [[Hidden Block]] directly underneath.
* Red Coins 3 and #4 are located between the floating platforms. The third coin is above the first platform, while the fourth is found low between the second and third platforms.
* Red Coin #5 is located near the end, above the final grass platform.

== Yoshi Egg ==
* The Yoshi Egg is found directly above the second support column of the first bridge, inside a Hidden Block.
