'''Cheep Diamonds''' ('''Cheepers Keepers''' in Europe/Australia releases) is a General minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to gain the most points by guessing the [[Cheep Chomp]] with the most diamonds. The Cheep Chomps will swallow the diamonds, then swim around the water.
* '''1st round''': Four Cheep Chomps appear in the water and swim normally. Three Cheep Chomps carry diamonds.
* '''2nd round''': Five Cheep Chomps appear in the water and the shuffling is complex. Four Cheep Chomps carry diamonds.
* '''3rd round''': Six Cheep Chomps appear in the water and the shuffling is more complex than the last round. Four Cheep Chomps carry diamonds.
