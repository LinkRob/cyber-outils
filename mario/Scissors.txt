'''Scissors''' are [[Stickers#Thing Stickers|Thing Stickers]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Appearance==
The Scissors sticker appears to look like regular scissors. Their handle is green in color.

==Usage==
The Scissors are used to cut up enemies and certain objects. The Scissors are first found after a [[Toad (character)|Toad]] gives [[Mario]] the Thing Sticker when he was running from [[Bowser Jr.]]. The young prince wants Mario's sticker, so they get into a fight. Mario instantly defeats Bowser Jr. using the Scissors sticker and he flies away. The Scissors could also be used on [[Megasparkle Goomba]] when he splits into smaller [[Goomba]]s in a giant square (though the Fan thing sticker is better for the job) but it also comes in handy after the use of the [[Fan]]. It is also used to cut up the Boat so Mario can reach later Worlds.
