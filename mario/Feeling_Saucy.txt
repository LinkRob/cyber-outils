'''Feeling Saucy''' is one of the [[microgame]]s in [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[Ninja Scribble]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Gameplay==
Using their [[Stylus]], the player must squirt ketchup all over a food, which differs between difficulty level. If done successfully, the player wins the microgame, but if they are unsuccessful, the player loses.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level not only has a different type of food but also increases the total number of food items by one. As such, this means more of the screen has to be covered in ketchup within the [[time limit]].

*'''Level 1:''' Ketchup must be squirted all over over a box of french fries appearing to read "Wario Brand."
*'''Level 2:''' Ketchup must be squirted all over two hot dogs.
*'''Level 3:''' Ketchup must be squirted all over three corn dogs.
