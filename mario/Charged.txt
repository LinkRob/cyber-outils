==History==
Charged indicates when a user is charging for a next attack. This results in the user's attack power being raised by a certain amount for the next turn. Once the attack has been made, the user goes back to normal. There are a number of ways to have the Charged status mainly with the [[Charge (badge)|Charge]] and [[Charge P]] badges. Using [[Hot Sauce]] also gives the user the Charged status.

Enemies can also give themselves the Charged status mainly the [[Hyper Goomba]] family. Certain bosses such as the [[Shadow Queen]] and [[Bonetail]] can charged up their attacks as well.
