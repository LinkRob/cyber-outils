The '''Flashy Slaphammer''' is a type of [[Slaphammer]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It can be found in the [[Drybake Desert]], [[Ice Flow]], and [[Shy Guy Jungle]].

== Effect ==
* Its damage is pretty strong, making it stronger than a [[Slaphammer]].
