The '''Fury Block''' is a type of block which appears in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It can be activated by [[Bowser]] using his breath on it. Breaking a Fury Block often yields a [[Cat Shine]].
