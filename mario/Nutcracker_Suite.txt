Now, the one composition of Tchaikovsky's that he really detested was his Nutcracker Suite, which is probably the most popular thing he ever wrote. It's a series of dances taken out of a full-length ballet called The Nutcracker, that he once composed for the St. Petersburg Opera House. It wasn't much of a success, and nobody performs it nowadays, but I'm pretty sure you'll recognize the music of the suite when you hear it. The '''Nutcracker Suite''' composed by Tchaikovsky is a series of dances taken out of the Nutcracker Ballet, and was featured in the Wii and DS versions of [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]].
