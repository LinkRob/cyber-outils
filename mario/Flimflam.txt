'''Flimflams''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are small, fiery creatures with the appearance of a flame with angry eyes.

===Behavior===
Some Flimflams burn out after a while, although some remain in a fiery state continuously.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Flimflams are encountered in various [[level]]s. If [[Wario]] touches one, his entire body is set on fire, turning him into [[Flaming Wario]]. Flimflams typically appear alongside [[Bonfire Block]]s.

Flimflams are most often produced from spawners, but they can also be dropped from [[Slot machine (Wario Land: Shake It!)|slot machines]]. In [[Stonetooth Cave]], Flimflams cannot ignite on [[Conveyor Belt]]s, and they dissolve instantly upon contact with water.

In [[Slipshod Slopes]] and [[Freezing Fields]], there is a blue Flimflam relative that instead burns with ice. If Wario touches it, he becomes [[Frozen Wario]].
