The '''Thorn Plant''' is a boss from ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''. He is encountered in the game's first level, [[The Castle of Thorns]].

== Appearance ==
The Thorn Plant is a monster that resembles a Piranha Plant. His head is purple, and his stem is dark purple with thorns protruding out of it.

== Battle ==
In battle, the Thorn Plant can summon thorny branches across his arena, though [[Princess Peach]] can destroy them by butchering them with her sword, having [[Swordfighter Peach|transformed into a swordsman]]. Another attack of his involves slamming his head on the ground. Peach has to jump over the shockwave, stomp on his head to expose his weak spot, then slash his head with her sword to deal damage. Dealing enough damage to him causes the creature to literally fall to pieces, revealing a Sparkle Gem and allowing the level to be completed.
