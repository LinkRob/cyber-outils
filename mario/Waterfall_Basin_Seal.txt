The '''Waterfall Basin Seal''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Waterfall Basin Seal is a green key with a big tip at the end of it.

==History==
The Waterfall Basin Seal is one of four keys that can be found in [[Ancient Waterworks]]. The Waterfall Basin Seal can be found in the bottom-left corner of the area inside of a purple treasure chest. However, [[Wario]] must become [[Cosmic Wario]] in order to shoot some orbs to make platforms appear in order to get to it. This key can be used on the door with the same symbol as it and it happens to lead into the room before the boss room.

==Trivia==
*The Waterfall Basin Seal is the only key in Ancient Waterworks that can't be obtained in [[Special Episode Part 3]].
