}}A '''Dark Spiny''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Spinies are stronger and darker versions of a [[Spiny]] that are only found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Being a species of Spinies, Dark Spinies cannot be jumped on unless [[Carrie]] is being rode on. Using [[Boomer]] is the best method to take them out.
