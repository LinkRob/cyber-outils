'''Fire Bar Castle! Youch!''' (or '''World 3-4''') is the fourth and final level of World 3 in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. 
==Layouts==
<tabber>
|-|Pink Coins = The level begins with a drop down to an area with a . The first Pink Coin is immediately seen, and the player must jump and grab onto the platform ledge to get it. After dropping down, the player will need to follow a trail of coins to the second Pink Coin. The player must then use some s to get past some s. When the path splits, the player will need to take the top path, which leads directly to the third Pink Coin, although the player will need to avoid the Fire Bars to get it. After dropping down, the player must follow the Pause Blocks across the [[Podoboo]]s to the fourth Pink Coin. An obstacle course of Fire Bars follows, as well as a simple drop to the fifth and final Pink Coin. After dodging more Podoboos and Fire Bars, Fake Bowser appears and, just like in , the player will need to wait at the Pause Block until Fake Bowser jumps, then move past and hit the ax at the end to complete the level.
|-|Purple Coins = The level begins with a drop down to an area with a Podoboo. After a few jumps, the first Purple Coin is seen, and the player must simply jump at a ledge to get it. After following a trail of coins, the second Purple Coin can be seen, although the player must wall jump to get it. The player will then cross two Fire Bars, and must then take the top path and jump over the Pause Block to get the third Purple Coin. After moving across the top path and dropping down, the player will need to use the yellow arrow blocks to get to the stone blocks with the fourth Purple Coin. The player must then follow either path, then after the segment ends, jump over the Fire Bars to get the fifth and final Purple Coin. After dodging more Lava Bubbles and Fire Bars, Fake Bowser appears and, just like in the previous castle level, the player will need to wait at the Pause Block until Fake Bowser jumps, then move past and hit the ax at the end to complete the level.
|-|Black Coins = The level begins with a drop down to an area with a Podoboo. The first Black Coin immediately appears to the right of a Pause Block, and the player must be careful to jump immediately after collecting it. After dropping down, the player will need to follow a trail of coins to a Pause Block, then jump onto a Fire Bar to get the second Black Coin. The player will then cross two Fire Bars, and must then take the bottom path and follow the Fire Bars to a jump with the third Black Coin. After moving across the bottom path and dropping down, the player will need to use the yellow arrow blocks to get to some stone blocks, then jump over to more stone blocks to get the fourth Black Coin. After dropping down, the player must jump onto one stone block, then immediately jump again to get to the fifth and final Black Coin. After dodging more Podoboos and Fire Bars, Fake Bowser appears and, just like in the previous castle level, the player will need to wait at the Pause Block until Fake Bowser jumps, then move past and hit the ax at the end to complete the level.
