'''TOSE Software Co.''' is a Japanese video game developer best known for creating [[Starfy]] and ''The Legendary Starfy'' series. They also produced a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related games, the first being ''[[Mario's Tennis]]'', and they also co-developed the {{series|Game & Watch Gallery}} and ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. They also provided additional development support for ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
