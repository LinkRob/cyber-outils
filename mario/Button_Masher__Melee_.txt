'''Button Masher''' is a [[Melee (Secondary weapon)|Melee]] weapon in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Button Masher.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
The Button Masher is a Melee weapon resembling a hammer. Like most Melee weapons, it lacks cover damage and has a cooldown of 1. However, it can target foes in any cell that is adjacent to the user dealing 55 to 65 damage. The weapon lacks a [[Super Effect]].
