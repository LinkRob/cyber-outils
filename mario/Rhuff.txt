'''Rhuff''' is a character in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Found at [[Rowf's Badge Shop]], Rhuff is a [[Doogan]] who helps his father, [[Rowf]], run his badge shop. Rhuff appears with his father, following him wherever he goes. During the parade at the end of the game, Rhuff and Rowf were seen walking in the opposite direction of the parade. They were stopped by [[Moustafa]] and a [[Nomadimouse]] where the two groups were discussing something. It was unknown what Rhuff and his father were talking about, but all of the characters soon left afterwards with Rhuff and Rowf still heading in the opposite direction.
