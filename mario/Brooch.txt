The '''brooch''' is a clothing item worn by [[Princess Peach]] in several of her appearances. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]'', and is an item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.
