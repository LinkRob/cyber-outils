'''MAx''' is a downloadable [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It is based on the [[WiiWare]] game called ''[[w:c:nintendo:Max & the Magic Marker|Max & the Magic Marker]]'' made by Bo Strandby, who also created this microgame.

==Gameplay==
Using Max's Magic Marker, the player has to drop ink onto the monster to destroy it. If done successfully, the player wins, but if the monster is not destroyed on time, they lose at the microgame.
