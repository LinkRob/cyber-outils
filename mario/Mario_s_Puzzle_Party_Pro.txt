'''Mario's Puzzle Party Pro''' is a Rare [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. It is essentially a single-player version of [[Mario's Puzzle Party]].

==Gameplay==
Mario's Puzzle Party Pro is slightly modified from Mario's Puzzle Party. The catch is that the player has three minutes to earn the most [[point]]s possible to set a new record. [[Garbage block]]s fall for every 100 [[point]]s the player scores. The player must last three minutes to register their score.

==In ''Mario Party'' series==
===''Mario Party 3''===
Like other Rare minigames, Mario's Puzzle Party Pro has to first be unlocked. To do so, the player must to the [[Game Guy Room]] after beating the [[Millennium Star]] in [[Stardust Battle]] and beating the [[Game Guy]] Room. Therefore, Mario's Puzzle Party Pro can be unlocked only after [[Completion|completing]] the game.

===''Mario Party Superstars''===
While Mario's Puzzle Party Pro does not return in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', the game does have a single-player version of Mario's Puzzle Party in the Score Attack mode, technically merging this minigame into Mario's Puzzle Party.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|Stick}} &ndash;  Move cursor
* {{button|n64|A}} '''or''' {{button|n64|B}} &ndash; Rotate blocks
* {{button|n64|Z}} &ndash; Drop blocks
