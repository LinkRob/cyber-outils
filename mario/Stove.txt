'''Stoves''' are an object in the {{series|Wario Land}}. They first appear in ''[[Wario Land II]]'' and return in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Description==
Stoves are chunky, cylindrical furnaces with a visible grid on their front side. Their main purpose is to help [[Wario]] reach higher areas, particularly those that are inaccessible to him otherwise. Wario can move a stove by performing a [[Dash Attack]] on it. When a stove is moved over a ledge, it descends rapidly and creates percussion upon contact with the ground, indicating their massive weight.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Stoves are slightly taller than in ''Wario Land II''. They appear in three levels: [[A Town in Chaos]], [[The Grasslands]], and [[Castle of Illusions]].
