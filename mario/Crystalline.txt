'''Crystalline''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
[[File:Crystalline SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
Crystalline, when used, increases the defense of all party members by 100%. This is extremely useful in tough boss fights. However, Crystalline can only be purchased at the Frog Coin Emporium. Crystalline is considered an improved version of a [[Bracer]].
