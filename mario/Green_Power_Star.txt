'''Green Power Stars''', or '''Green Stars''', are items which appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Description==
Green Power Stars are a special type of [[Power Star]] first found in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. These power stars are very rare and typically appear under the shadow of the [[Green Comet]], they also appear hidden in certain levels and must be found as a normal [[Power Star]]. Typically, discovering all of the Green Power Stars reveals another [[galaxy]].

=={{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[File:Green Power Star get SMG.png|thumb|Mario obtaining a Green Power Star in ''Super Mario Galaxy'']]
There are only three Green Power Stars. When a Green Power Star is found, a [[Green Luma]] appears in the [[Comet Observatory]] around a green transparent [[Launch Star]] located near the [[Terrace]] and the [[Garage]]. Once all three Green Power Stars are found, the green Launch Star is restored and becomes usable, it launches [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] to the otherwise unapproachable [[Planet of Trials]], where three more Green Lumas await to transform into Launch Stars which can send Mario/Luigi to [[Rolling Gizmo Galaxy]], [[Bubble Blast Galaxy]], and [[Loopdeeswoop Galaxy]], respectively. Once the game is beaten with both [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], a fourth Green Luma appears and takes him to the [[Grand Finale Galaxy]].

The three galaxies and the missions where the Green Power Stars can be obtained are listed as such:

*[[Battlerock Galaxy]] - [[Luigi under the Saucer]]: Rescuing [[Luigi]] from a cage at the bottom of the first [[UFO]].
*[[Dusty Dune Galaxy]] - [[Treasure of the Pyramid]]: Collecting 5 [[Silver Star]]s in the Pyramid Planet.
*[[Buoy Base Galaxy]] - [[The Secret of Buoy Base]]: Under the Buoy Base sealed in a cage that needs to be destroyed by a [[Torpedo Ted]].

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[File:Green Star Get.png|thumb|[[Mario]] lays claim to yet another Green Power Star.]]
In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', the Green Power Stars are first hinted by the [[Green Luma]]s which show up on [[Starship Mario]] late in the game, often referring to them as '''Cosmic Jewels'''. The first Green Power Star shows up once 117 [[Power Star]]s are collected and the second one shows up once all 120 Power Stars are collected.

Every Green Power Star is summoned by [[Green Comet]]s, which orbit every galaxy after collecting all 120 Power Stars. A Green Power Star can be collected in each of the galaxies once [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] collects all 120 Power Stars, beats the first mission of [[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]] a second time, and after the player sees the secret ending. Every galaxy has the same number of Green Power Stars as Power Stars, but unlike them, Green Power Stars do not have to be collected in any particular order.

Green Power Stars are usually well hidden and are often off edges, in dangerous locations, or require some tactical maneuvers to obtain, similar to [[Comet Medal]]s. A distinct twinkling sound now plays when Mario/Luigi is close to a Green Power Star. Once every Green Power Star is collected in a galaxy, the silver crown denoting that every Power Star in a galaxy has been collected changes to gold on the world map.

All 120 Green Stars are required to unlock the final galaxy, [[Grandmaster Galaxy]], so it is therefore the only galaxy that without any Green Stars.

===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''===
Green Power Stars do not appear, although there is the similar [[Green Star]]s, which are effectively the replacement for [[Star Medal]]s, and by extension, [[Star Coin]]s, as there are three Green Stars to collect in every level.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', even though only three Green Power Stars are able to be recovered in the game, a total of seven Green Lumas can be found on the [[Comet Observatory]] and the [[Planet of Trials]] (counting the one that takes [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] to the [[Grand Finale Galaxy]]).
*Green Lumas grow up to become Green Power Stars, according to [[Polari]].
*When the Green Comets are in orbit in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', a remix of the [[Good Egg Galaxy]]'s music from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' is played in [[World 1 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 1]]'s map.
*When a Green Star mission is chosen in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', the borders of the screen are green instead of black.
