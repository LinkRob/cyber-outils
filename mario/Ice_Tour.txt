'''Ice Tour''' was the ninth Tour added in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] that began on January 14, 2020 and ended on January 28, 2020. This tour featured [[Penguin Mario|Penguin Luigi]] from [[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]] and [[Peach (Wintertime)]] from [[Super Mario Odyssey]]. This tour also added [[Ice Mario]] from [[Super Mario Galaxy]] as a new driver.

The tracks featured in this tour are themed after ice courses that introduced [[Frappe Snowland]] from [[Mario Kart 64]] and [[Vanilla Lake 1]] that returns as a retro track since [[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]. It also introduced R/T variants of [[Choco Island 2|SNES Choco Island 2]], [[DK Pass|DS DK Pass]] and [[Shy Guy Bazaar|3DS Shy Guy Bazaar]].
