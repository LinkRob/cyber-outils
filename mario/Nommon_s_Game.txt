The '''Nommon's Game''' is a mini-game in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It is played mainly in [[Somnom Woods]] by the [[Nommon]]s in order to get them to hand over their "[[Pi'illo|Nom Pi'illos]]". Nommon's Game is most notable for being one of the most difficult and frustrating mini-games in that game (next to [[Mole Hunt]]). It is similar in its gameplay to the [[Ball Hop]] puzzles, except players must not cross their path.

==Gameplay==
The Nommon's Game is mainly played using the [[Ball Hop]] technique. In this game, Mario and Luigi must bounce over all of the rings in a certain amount of steps. They have a certain amount of lives (three on the first game and six on the second) to get through all of the rings in all three rounds. However, there is a number of hazards. Mario and Luigi must avoid bouncing on the same tile twice or they will fall through and lose a life. They must also avoid landing on the Nommons that sometimes appear on the board.

The first game of the Nommon's Game is required to beat since they have one of the Pi'illo Master's pillows. The second time is optional but required if Mario and Luigi want the [[Zee Egg]] Bros. Attack from [[Eldream]].
