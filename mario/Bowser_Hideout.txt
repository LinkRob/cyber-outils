The '''Bowser Hideout''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Bowser Hideout is one of [[Bowser]]'s locations, serving as a base of his. It is located in the [[Jungle Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], but it can only be accessed through Bowser's [[Pipe House (Mario Party Advance)|Pipe House]]. The interior of Bowser Hideout has light gray, cracked walls, a dark purple trim, and a large, purple door depicting an emblem of Bowser's face. Bowser's throne is also light purple with light-blue accents and a red cushion. The center of Bowser Hideout has a light gray table with a green surface, and there is a deck of cards on it.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
One of Bowser's quests, [[Boss Bowser]], starts in Bowser Hideout once Bowser challenges the player to a card game. The card game is played on the table, and it involves the player guessing whether the next card is either higher or lower than the one currently drawn. If the player's character wins the card game, Bowser challenges them to the Bowser minigame [[Koopa Kappa]], and winning it not only unlocks the minigame but also two [[Gaddget]]s, [[Faux Flame]] and [[Hourglass]].
