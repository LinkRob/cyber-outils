'''Tickle''', also named '''Thunder Tickle''', is an overworld technique in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
[[Luigi]] can use this move while he is behind [[Mario]]. The player can hold {{button|GBA|B}} to charge up the [[Thunderhand]], then release to have Luigi paralyze Mario, allowing him to move Mario anywhere he wants. This technique is used to push [[Boo Statue]]s into place while the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] face away from one.
