'''Rabbid Peach''' is a Rabbid dressed as [[Princess Peach]], and one of the eight main heroes in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. She was created when [[Spawny]] used the SupaMerge to fuse a Rabbid with a Princess Peach wig. In ''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle'', Rabbid Peach teams up with [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], Princess Peach, [[Yoshi]], [[Rabbid Mario]], [[Rabbid Luigi]], and [[Rabbid Yoshi]] to save the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] from Rabbid-fused enemies and [[Bowser Jr.]]'s attempted takeover. 

== Profile ==
===Physical appearance===
Rabbid Peach has white fur everywhere except inside her rabbit-like ears, around her mouth, on her tummy, paws, and under her feet, showing pale skin. She usually has blue eyes, but when in an adrenaline rush, her eyes turn red, which is her most notable feature. She has paws with one opposable thumb on each paw and no fingers. She also has buck teeth and feet with no toes.

==== Peach form ====
Rabbid Peach still retains her Rabbid appearance, but now has an outfit. She has blue eyes and long blonde hair, her primary outfit consists of a pink dress with the bottom cut off and a ring at the bottom, pink panniers, short puffy sleeves, a raised collar, and a sapphire brooch set in gold. Accompanying it is a golden crown with four jewels on it, she has paws with one opposable thumb on each paw and no fingers. She also has buck teeth and feet with no toes.

=== Personality traits ===
When she was still a normal Rabbid, Rabbid Peach exhibited traits of a normal Rabbid; curious, wild, chaotic, and mischievous.

Unlike Rabbid Mario, Rabbid Luigi, and Rabbid Yoshi, Rabbid Peach is playful, curious, and sometimes bossy. She is also sassy, easily bored, and fond of taking [[wikipedia:Selfie|selfies]]. She is not scared of ghosts when she goes ghost hunting with the other seven heroes. Rabbid Peach seems to have a vain side, flaunting her looks with every chance she gets.

Rabbid Peach does have a crush on Mario as she always tries to get his attention, and even showed jealousy towards his and Peach's relationship. Some of her hostility towards Peach steams not only from jealousy but taking her cosplay role seriously. Despite these traits, Rabbid Peach still has a good heart, and aids her allies when they need her.

===Powers and abilities===
In ''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle'', she has poor offense, exceptional defense, and average mobility. Her primary weapon is the Blaster, a mid-range weapon that does a great amount of damage. Her secondary weapon is a Sentry, a deployable unit that explodes on any foe that is in range of its radius and absorbing damage from Line-of-Sight attacks. She can also use the Stylish Dash up to four times. She also has two abilities, the Shield which projects a barrier around her to reduce damage by a determined percentage and the Heal replenishes a percentage of her own health and any ally that is within her range. 

In ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'', Rabbid Peach now uses a new blaster-like weapon called the [[Triple-Troll]], which can fire three missiles at a time at foes. they can easily bypass partial cover and can even destroy full destructible cover. It also has a mount for her smartphone that she uses for selfies or go live-streaming. She retains the Heal ability from the first game and has the Healing Jump that Princess Peach originally had.

==In {{series|Mario + Rabbids}}==
===''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''===
====Earth====
Rabbid Peach started as an ordinary Rabbid who travels with her Rabbid comrades through the Time Washing Machine up until they arrive at the basement of the Genius Girl and her robot assistant [[Beep-0]], where the Rabbids cause chaos, while she tries on a Peach wig and the Rabbid with the SupaMerge named [[Spawny]] made her the first living subject, turning her into Rabbid Peach making one of the Rabbids laugh at her as Spawny merges the Rabbids with other objects and caused the Time Washing Machine to pull them into it along with a Super Mario poster, teleporting them to the Mushroom Kingdom. 

====Ancient Gardens====
After a long fall, Rabbid Peach lands in a hedge and tries to catch Rabbid Luigi, who lands face first on the ground where they meet Mario, who is surprised to see new faces, but they end up having to face the Rabbids, who are now hostile due to the SupaMerge's corruption while she and Rabbid Luigi were unaffected as Beep-0 made the mission to find Spawny, who merged with the device during the fall. They get a view of the vortex which Beep-0 referred to it as the [[Megabug]]. They meet Princess Peach, who was revealed to be fine since the chaos and Rabbid Peach is critical of her outfit. They find Luigi dealing with the Pirrabbid Plant and help him defeat it as Rabbid Peach tries to take a selfie with it but is snagged by the Pirrabbid Plant and she takes a selfie before the heroes free her and defeat it and Luigi joins the team. Heading along further, they find Bowser Jr. befriending Spawny and giving him his name and summons [[Rabbid Kong]] to deal with them, but he is defeated when Rabbid Peach, despite Mario's objections, knocks down the tower he was on and takes a couple of selfies of him during his fall and she shows them to Rabbid Luigi, who laugh at them while refusing to let Luigi himself see them, resulting in Luigi trying to take a peek without them knowing while turning away when they notice. 

====Donkey Kong Adventure====
Taking place in between the first and second chapter, when Rabbid Kong and the Rabbids accidentally cause the Time Washing Machine to malfunction, Rabbid Peach tries to plug in her smartphone with Beep-0 trying to pull her away only for them to get transported to Donkey Kong Island where they meet Donkey Kong himself and Rabbid Cranky. She will sometimes use Rabbid Cranky's cane to take selfies without his permission, much to his annoyance. Rabbid Cranky tells them that Rabbid Kong has been gathering the SupaMerge corrupted bananas called the Bad Bananas to garner more power and is infecting the inhabitants there. After a long journey of confronting the cronies of Rabbid Kong, they eventually confront Rabbid Kong himself and managed to destroy the energy and defeated him again. The Bad Banana he coughed up reverts to normal and Rabbid Peach simply hands it to him rather than hurting him, marking their friendship. As she and Beep-0 prepare to return to the Mushroom Kingdom, Rabbid Kong is despondent over this while Rabbid Peach cheers him up and they take a group photo to remember their adventures together. 

====Sherbet Desert====
They head to Sherbet Desert where they solve many puzzles and fighting the Rabbids. They would find [[Rabbid Mario]] trapped in a block of ice and free him, and Rabbid Mario joins them while befriending Mario. Bowser Jr. scares Spawny into creating Blizzy and Sandy to attack the heroes. As a result, all of the members are imprisoned in ice by Blizzy, only for Princess Peach to appear and free both Mario and the other partner while the third member remains trapped and Rabbid Peach refuses to let the princess any closer to Mario. Blizzy and Sandy are defeated, and the third member is also set free. 

====Spooky Trails====
They head to Spooky Trails where they find the Moon Gate locked in place and they must find the Relics of Goodness, a gramophone and a Boo-shaped balloon, under the instruction of a mysterious third party who wants them to wake up a being named "Tom Phan" who they claim has the power to defeat Bowser Jr. Once they find the relics but did not open the gate, they learn that they need a full moon to open the gate. They encounter [[Rabbid Yoshi]], who was held hostage by Calavera, and the heroes managed to save him as Rabbid Yoshi gets his revenge. They reach the clock tower and reversed time to get the full moon back into position, resulting in the Moon Gate finally opening. Once they pass the gate, they reach a theater where Bowser Jr. was waiting and forced Spawny to fuse a Rabbid with the Relics of Goodness to create the Phantom, who then sings an insulting song about Mario and his series, which they then defeat him.

====Lava Pit====
They head to the Lava Pit where they find that Bowser Jr. had turned the volcano into his base of operations with significantly tougher foes. They eventually run into Bowser Jr. who now pilots the Mecha Jr. and they get into a battle. After the Mecha Jr. is defeated, Spawny falls off and towards his impending doom, much to Jr.'s dismay as he leaves. Fortunately, Spawny was rescued by Yoshi, who joins the team to set things right. But as they attempt to leave the crystal caves, Spawny was hypnotized by the MegaBug's energy and the MegaBug, who is now in its phoenix form and absorbs Spawny and flies towards Bowser's Castle. Bowser Jr. chastises the heroes for letting it happen and is relieved that his father Bowser is still on vacation only to get a text from him saying that he has returned home early, much to Jr.'s shock, realizing that Bowser is in danger. The heroes follow but have to deal with [[Bwario and Bwaluigi]] and the [[Lava Queen]]. Upon reaching the castle, Jr. begs the heroes to save his father, who was fused with the MegaBug to become MegaDragonBowser and after a tough fight, they managed to free Bowser from the MegaBug, and the latter charges at them, but the heroes combine their efforts to destroy the MegaBug, closing the vortex and freeing Spawny as well. Bowser Jr. is grateful for their help and asks on his behalf to sort things out with Bowser, but they soon head back to [[Princess Peach's Castle]] where Rabbid Peach's statue is inaugurated for her efforts. 

===''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''===
====[[Mushroom Kingdom]] (Prologue)====
Princess Peach calls over Mario and her Rabbid counterpart and tasks them to find Rabbid Mario's missing overalls and he refuses to leave the bush until he has them back. Once Mario and Rabbid Peach find them, Rabbid Luigi confesses to his counterpart that he was the culprit. After returning them, Rabbid Mario puts them back on and is finally able to emerge from his hiding spot. However, just as everyone was about to have a relaxing day, a Spark named Starburst runs into Mario and a Darkmess Manta arrives and begins infecting the kingdom with Darkmess and Rabbid Peach is distracted by taking selfies with it. Mario tries to warn her, though she gets mad at him for interrupting her, but she ends up getting abducted by the Darkmess Manta. Princess Peach helps Mario and Beep-0 board the Manta to save her Rabbid counterpart. 

Rabbid Peach is hanging on for dear life on the Manta's tail and as soon as Mario and Beep-0 catch up, she was able to safely let go and immediately goes into action with Mario, but their weapons are disabled in the Darkmess Dimension, to her frustration. Eventually, Starburst helps to restore them with its power and Cursa arrives and begins brainwashing Bowser's minions. They fight through many of Cursa's minions and confront it. Before Cursa could act, the WM ARC driven by their friends arrives and rescues all of them before Cursa gets a hold of them. They meet Beep-0's creation [[JEANIE]], who is glad that they are safe and brings up a topic about the Luma-Rabbid creatures which were fused from Lumas and Rabbids. Once Princess Peach states that Rosalina would not allow the fusion to happen, Starburst reveals that its mama is in danger because of Cursa. Beep-0 wanted to call the creatures Rabbid Lumas, but everyone else prefers the name Sparks. With Cursa's Stronghold being Beep-0 believes that with the Sparks at their side, Cursa won't bother them anymore. 

====Beacon Beach====
Rabbid Peach and her friends arrive at Beacon Beach, which is suffering a heavy rainfall and Rabbid Peach gets annoyed with Rabbid Luigi splashing in a puddle near her. They are almost ambushed by [[Augie]], who then stops himself short upon seeing that they are not enemies and tells them to leave. Beep-0 explains that they are heroes and wish to help out, and Augie explains that he that the lighthouse is infected with the Darkmess Tentacle that is subjecting Beacon Beach to darkness and rain and once he hears of the Sparks, he says he has seen one in town and instructs them to head there. Once they take care of the [[Bob-omb]]s that are invading it. After meeting the Rabbid that had the Spark, who is named Twinkle, the Spark then flees to the sun beacon where it causes the Sunrise Temple to emerge from the water. The heroes enter the temple, completely oblivious to a [[Edge|mysterious Rabbid]] following them, but not without intimidating Augie first. During their exploration, they catch up with Twinkle, who was scared of them, but Mario gently approaches it to show that he wants to help it and Twinkle relaxes, but Twinkle is captured by the Darkmess Tentacle. They head through to defeat the enemies within the Darkmess Tentacle. Twinkle was fine but is soon pursued by the Stooges that seek to capture it. Rabbid Mario defeats the ones blocking the way and the heroes go after them. Just as Twinkle was about to face capture, the [[Flying Blade]] comes out of the waterfall and slays it and it returns to where it emerged where the mysterious Rabbid that followed them catches it. The Rabbid in question walks out confidently and gestures to the Stooges disapprovingly, which they retaliate, but she immediately dispatched all of them in quick succession, much to Rabbid Peach's shock and becoming jealous. She looks back at Twinkle, who is scared of her, but she puts her sword down and reaches out with a smile, as Rabbid Peach glares at her. They are attacked by the Giant Wildclaw, which they quickly defeated. Beep-0 informs the strange Rabbid that Twinkle accompanies them to the lighthouse to deal with the problem. Augie arrives to congratulate them for saving the Spark, but becomes scared of the Rabbid who intimidated him, calling her a Spark Hunter. The Rabbid introduces herself as Edge and insists on being called as such and offers to join them. They head to the lighthouse where they destroyed the Darkmess Tentacle, restoring sunlight to Beacon Beach and Augie warns them about Cursa's ringleaders known as the Spark Hunters, who came earlier and threatened the inhabitants if he did not hand over the Sparks. They promise to deal with them if they come across any of them.

====Pristine Peaks====
They head to [[Pristine Peaks]] where it was discovered that it was suffering under a blizzard and extremely low temperatures and if left unchecked, it will become a wasteland and cause the planet to become uninhabitable to all life. They land and make their way up the mountain while dealing with hostile life along the way. Once they destroy the Darkmess Tentacle, the blizzard ends and the team build snowmen, with Rabbid Mario creating a huge muscular snowman with himself as the head, but neither Edge or Rabbid Peach were impressed, and they notice that the path opened. With the weather back to normal, they head to the Winter Palace where they free the warden named Captain Orion, who is anxious for the safety of the girl he bonded with, and they head inside. Then they encounter a ghostly girl named Midnite who makes her presence known before floating away through the halls and Edge notes that she is one of Cursa's Spark Hunters. As they encounter her more often, they confront her at the Darkmess Tentacle where they first learn that Edge were once good friends, but Edge refuses to disclose further information, before Midnite disappears through the Darkmess Tentacle. Captain Orion is soon captured by her and the girl, [[Rabbid Rosalina]] goes into action and teams up with the heroes to defeat Midnite. Once Midnite is subdued, Rabbid Peach looks on as Rabbid Rosalina is reunited with Orion while Edge spares Midnite, who flees in sorrow. Rabbid Rosalina was reluctant about going with the heroes, since she wants to meet her [[Rosalina|human counterpart]], until they confirm that they are searching for the missing Rosalina which she then agrees and promises that she will make Orion proud. 

====Palette Prime====
Rabbid Peach partakes in the meeting where they are headed to Palette Prime, where they learn that the planet is losing its color and valor due to the Darkmess Tentacle affecting the forest with T.S. Woodrow lamenting over the state of the forest and the town where it was being attacked. Once they destroy the Darkmess Tentacle, they use the shortcut through Woodrow's basement due to the bridge being broken. Upon reaching Paletteville, they find Bowser and the Spark Hunter named Bedrock in a huge scuffle and they each end up in the Darkmess Tentacle. Edge confirms that Bedrock is also a Spark Hunter and denies any relationship with her and Beep-0 explains that Bowser has a long-standing feud with Mario, often kidnapping Peach. The team grants Bowser a new weapon called the [[Bowzooka]]. After Bedrock is defeated, a ship came to help her escape from the heroes. Bowser explains that he is searching for Cursa, who mind controlled his minions and he needs them back and forms a temporary alliance. 

====Terra Flora====
As the team look into their next destination, Terra Flora, JEANIE reveals that they are now in range of Cursa's Stronghold, but it was heavily protected by a barrier that deters intruders. Rabbid Peach can be seen narrowing her eyes at Beep-0 when he cornered an agitated Edge about her connection to the Spark Hunters, who doesn't want anyone getting into her personal business. They soon land at Terra Flora, which is revealed to be on the verge of a drought that threatens floral life, due to the Darkmess Tentacle that was infecting the water volcano, Mt. Spout. They inquire the train engineer, Sullivan's help in reaching Mt. Spout and he takes them there, but they were being pursued by a Darkmess infected Wiggler who then attacks the train, forcing them to fight and managed to stop its rampage. After their arrival, they managed to destroy the Darkmess Tentacle, allowing Mt. Spout to erupt which encases most of the heroes in bubbles before they fall. After gaining information from Bea about a mechanic who might be able to help them, they set a course for Barrendale Mesa.  

====Barrendale Mesa====
The heroes make their way to Barrendale Mesa where it was once full of abundant life until the Rabbids arrived and used up all the resources before departing, while JEANIE points out that Momma is still living on the planet, and she might create a Shield Breaker to deal with the barrier around Cursa's Stronghold, which happens to be close by. Edge becomes concerned about the Spark Hunter that might be awaiting them but brushes off all worries. Due to the electric storm, the [[WM ARC]] is unable to properly land meaning that they would have to jump from high ground. Rabbid Peach, Luigi, Rabbid Mario, Rabbid Luigi, and Rabbid Rosalina land on a giant balloon, but Bowser's sharp claws end up destroying it. Their entrance has annoyed Momma, whose garage is closed due to harsh weather conditions and instructs them to head to the malfunctioning windmill if they were that serious about getting her help. Once the windmill is repaired, they receive a transition from "Momma" who tells them to return to the garage, but once they do, they find out that it was a trap and "Momma" was really [[Daphne]] in disguise and traps them in the Darkmess Dimension, but they each managed to beat her. Cursa then arrives to confront the heroes but ends up immobilized and with an explosion of light, Rosalina herself emerges, to Rabbid Rosalina's joy as Rosalina uses her wand to open a portal for them to escape before she is recorrupted by Cursa and Edge leads everyone out of the Dimension. With everyone disoriented with Rosalina's true fate, with how the events through a Purified Darkmess Crystal revealing what kick started the events, Momma assures them that Rosalina is counting on them and they must retain hope. 

====Melodic Gardens==== 
In ''The Last Spark Hunter'', as they make their way to Cursa's stronghold, they find that Melodic Gardens have gone silent, and they change course to investigate what was happening there. After saving the members, they meet with Allegra who is the warden of the planet, who explains that the silence is threatening Melodic Garden's existence and one of the Spark Hunters is responsible for its state, which left Edge confused as she states that they have defeated all of them. Upon meeting Kanya, Edge states that she does not know who she is at all. Sometime later, after getting past all the obstacles, Rabbid Peach and the heroes look on in sympathy as Allegra is comforting a Dorrie who is trapped in a cage, who is a special friend. They help Allegra free Dorrie and work with her to defeat Kanya once and for all. Allegra is grateful for their work and restored harmony to the planet. They leave to Cursa's stronghold.

====Cursa's Stronghold====
The team used the Shield Breakers to destroy the barrier around Cursa's Stronghold which reveals that it was the Comet Observatory, to everyone's horror over how low Cursa went to attack the symbol of hope in the galaxy. They land on the debris of the shields where they find Darkmess Puddles, one containing Darkmess Bowser and the other containing Darkmess Edge. After defeating all of them, Rabbid Peach is shocked to learn that Edge was created by Cursa and the former leader of the Spark Hunters, but due to Edge's heroism, Rabbid Peach comforts her that they still love and trust her. During the battle with Cursa, they destroy its hands and attack the broken star until it couldn't contain the drained Sparks, releasing them as well as Rosalina, who then partakes in the battle. With Cursa being weakened further, Rabbid Rosalina stands in-between her friends and Cursa, but the drained Sparks do their part and turn themselves into a laser to severely wound Cursa and the heroes all combine their strength along with Rosalina merging them into a single golden arrow to completely annihilate the entity once and for all.

====Mushroom Kingdom (Epilogue)====
Rosalina is completely grateful to the heroes for their help in saving her and the galaxy and she even restored the lost energy to the Sparks who fell victim to Cursa, proving that energy cannot be destroyed, but altered. Both Rabbid Rosalina and Edge choose to stay with the heroes when offered to join her on the Comet Observatory. Rabbid Peach, Rabbid Mario, and Rabbid Luigi are toying around with a grumpy Bowser, which Mario tries to stop them, but was too late when Bowser unleashes a fire ball to throw them off.

====The Tower of Doooom====
When it was revealed that [[Spawny]], one of the heroes' old friends was imprisoned in a place that belongs to Madame Bwahstrella, called The Tower of Doooom, Rabbid Peach and her friends head through ten floors and after defeating the giant enemy on the tenth floor Spawny is freed and has gained the confidence to go on his own, knowing that his friends will have his back in his time of need.

====Space Opera Network====
Rabbid Peach, Rabbid Mario, and Beep-0 enter the Space Opera Network after they gained gold tickets to take part on stage. It was there where they meet Rayman, who arrived on a rocket, but Rayman was wary of them, due to his bad experience with their kind, but Beep-0 assures them that the two of them are docile and after dealing with some of the remnants of Cursa's minions, Rayman became somewhat more comfortable around them. They end up meeting the Phantom of the Bwahpera again, whom they haven't seen since their encounter in Spooky Trails, and he explains that he was under the control of the Megabug and has turned to the side of good and wanted to help the Space Opera Network become a popular show again after years of disaster and the recent Darkmess infestation was not helping, and they can help get rid of the Darkmess in all three sets. After they managed to clear the Darkmess in the Pirate, Western, and Medieval sets, the Phantom congratulates them and invites them to the floor below them, but Rayman believes that something was wrong. When they arrive at the basement, the Phantom reveals that he truly was setting them up secretly, so he can get back at them for his previous defeat and to draw power from viewership. During the last battle, the Phantom sings a degrading song about Rayman losing his fame to the Rabbids and later gives the same treatment to Rabbid Peach, Rabbid Mario, and Beep-0, but with them turning off the lights, they defeated the Phantom once again. Rayman is happy to actually be fighting alongside the Rabbids for once instead of against them like he always did. Due to them becoming civilized, Rayman decides to stay at the Space Opera Network so he can help keep the Rabbids in line and keep their moral compasses straight, while Beep-0, Rabbid Peach, and Rabbid Mario return to the Mushroom Kingdom. Before they leave, Rabbid Peach gives Rayman a Mario Cap while Rayman gives the Rabbids a copy of his hoodie.
