'''Larry's Torpedo Castle''', or '''{{world|Waters|castle}}''', is the castle of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. This course is unlocked via completion of [[Dragoneel's Undersea Grotto]] and its own completion unlocks the path to [[The Mighty Cannonship]].

== Layout ==
This castle contains moving Grates, [[Burner]]s, and [[Torpedo Ted]]s. In the first room of the stage, the player has to climb moving fences and dodge the fire of the Burners. Next, the player goes in to a [[Warp Pipe]] which leads to a room filled with water and [[Torpedo Ted|Torpedo Teds]]. After dodging some [[Torpedo Ted]]s, the player reaches the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]]. This is followed by a [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to an area with [[Coin|Coins]] and Burners. As the player goes back to the underwater area, there is a [[Red Ring]]. Next, the player enters a Warp Pipe which leads to a short room with Burners and moving fences. After avoiding the fire of the Burners, the boss door is found. A cannon is found through the boss door, and leads to [[Larry Koopa]]. [[Waterspouts]]s are found during the battle with Larry, and he is defeated after being hit three times.

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first [[Star Coin]] can be accessed by jumping on top of the [[? Block]]s and wall jumping to the Star Coin while avoiding the Burners.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The player must swim under a platform where [[Torpedo Ted]]s are shot without getting hit in order to collect the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is hidden at the top of the screen. The player must climb the moving fences.
