The '''Mushroom Genie''' is a character in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]] first appearing in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. He is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]]-like genie. A similar character appears in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' under the name [[Genie of the Lamp]].

==Appearances==
===''Mario Party 2''===
Making his debut in the game, the Mushroom Genie appears after a [[Magic Lamp]] is used. He then takes the user around the world to the [[Star Space]].

===''Mario Party 3''===
In ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', the Mushroom Genie returns with his role being the same. However, instead of taking the user of the Magic Lamp around the world, the Mushroom Genie takes them through a dimension to the Star Space.

The game features a similar character named [[Mushroom Jeanie]], a female genie who appears from the [[Lucky Lamp]] and changes the location of the [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]].
