'''Michelle Marie Hippe (née Drollman)''' (born May 7, 1976) is an American voice actress who portrays [[Larry Koopa]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', replacing [[Lani Minella]] from that game onwards (aside from Lani Minella's reused clips for ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'').

Michelle Hippe provides Larry with a higher-pitched and cuter voice compared to Lani Minella's portrayal.
