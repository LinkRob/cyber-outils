==History==
The Deepdown Depot is located within [[Rogueport Sewers]] that can be accessed after Chapter 2. The Deepdown Depot is well known for selling items unique to it such as [[Slow Shroom]]s and [[Gold Bar]]s. It is even known for selling [[Ultra Shroom]]s, although they are quite expensive and do not benefit Mario during the early parts of the game where his HP is well under 50. The shop owner is a female [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who doesn't speak too much.
