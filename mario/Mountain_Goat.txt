'''Mountain Goats''', also named '''Goats''', are white ungulates which appear in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. They are based on real-world {{w|goat}}s. Mountain Goats first appear in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', in which they appear on the [[Daisy Hills]] course.

== Description ==
In ''Mario Kart 7'', Mountain Goats have light turquoise fur/wool that covers their bodies barring their orange horns and hooves and their face, which has black beady eyes and an orange nose. ''Mario Kart Tour'' gives them a smoother model.

==Behavior==
Mountain Goats act similar to [[Moo Moo]]s as they move slowly around the track, serving as obstacles that must be dodged. Mountain Goats make bleating sounds whenever a racer enters their vicinity, perhaps as a warning sign not to touch them.

==Game appearances==
===''Mario Kart 7''===
[[File:Mountain Goat MK7.png|thumb|left|250px|A Mii drives toward a Mountain Goat in ''Mario Kart 7'']]
In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', only three Mountain Goats appear in [[Daisy Hills]], after the first bridge. Whenever a racer drives into a Mountain Goat, they lose three [[coin]]s. A Mountain Goat can be launched upward by throwing either a [[Green Shell]], a [[Red Shell]], or a [[Bob-omb]] at it.
{{br|left}}
===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
Mountain Goats, along with the Daisy Hills course, make a reappearance. In the [[Peach vs. Bowser Tour (November 2022)|Peach vs. Bowser Tour]] of late 2022, Mountain Goats were also added into [[Amsterdam Drift 3]], replacing the [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plants]] in the reverse layout.

=== Outside the ''Mario Kart'' franchise ===
Goats are referenced in ''Minecraft'' where the player can change the texture of the goat to a light turquoise colour.{{sectstub}}
