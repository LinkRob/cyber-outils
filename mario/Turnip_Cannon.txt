'''Turnip Cannons''' are an object in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

==Description==
They are mainly used to shoot [[turnip]]s at enemies. Before a Turnip Cannon can be used, the player must first pull a blue handle to spawn the Turnip Cannon.

==In ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''==
[[File:Turnip Cannon screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|A Turnip Cannon shooting out a turnip]]
Turnip Cannon appear in certain courses, and are even the main feature of their own one, [[Turnip Cannon Jungle]]. After spawning a Turnip Cannon from pulling its handle, [[Captain Toad]] or [[Toadette]] enter inside the Turnip Cannon.

The controls for using the Turnip Cannon differ between versions. In the original [[Wii U]] version, the [[Wii U GamePad]] gyroscope can is used to aim the Turnip Cannon, and pressing {{button|wiiu|A}} to fire Turnips at enemies, [[coin]]s, [[Super Gem]]s, and [[Rock Block]]s. In the [[Nintendo Switch]] version, the player has to press {{button|ns|r}} if the system is in docked mode or use touch controls if it is in handheld mode. In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] version, the player uses touch controls.
