The '''Mario Kart 2017 Calendar''' is a form of merchandise relating to the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario series]]''. The calendar was based off characters and vehicles from the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart series]]'' and uses artworks and renders created by [[Nintendo]]. The calendar was designed by Celina Carvalho, made in China and published by ABRAMS on 2016 licensed by Nintendo.

==Description==
[[File:MarioKart2017CalendarBack.jpg|thumb|left|The back of the calendar.]]
The calendar uses artworks from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. For each month,  the artwork is seen on top of the page with the game logo on the bottom-right corner. The name of the month is above the artwork. On the bottom of the calendar page, has items from ''Mario Kart''.
