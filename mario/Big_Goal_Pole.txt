'''Big Goal Poles''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
Big Goal Poles are a variation of [[Goal Pole]]s, which they differ in appearance from. The main difference of a Big Goal Pole is its larger size, as its name would suggest. Instead of having a triangular flag like their regular counterparts, Big Goal Poles instead have a larger, rectangular flag depicting a red [[Bowser]] [[emblem]] and a yellow border on the top and bottom each. In ''Super Mario Maker 2'', a Big Goal Pole's pole was changed to yellow, and the pole is on a short, brown structure instead of a gray one.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
Big Goal Poles can be found at the end of [[castle]] and [[airship]] levels as well as {{world-link|s8|crown|Special 8-Crown}}.

===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' / ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''===
A Big Goal Pole is featured at the end of castle levels, and they function the same as in ''Super Mario 3D Land''.

===''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
In [[castle]] levels of the ''Super Mario 3D World'' style, a Goal Pole is replaced with a Big Goal Pole, which function the same as Goal Poles.
