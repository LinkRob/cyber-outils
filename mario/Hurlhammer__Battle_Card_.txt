|name=Hurlhammer (Battle Card)}}'''Hurlhammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Hurlhammer is the line version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a blue-gray hammer with a blue/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 15/65 coins

Sell Price: 8/33 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Hurlhammer is first seen in [[Marmalade Valley]], but can also found in [[Plum Park]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Lighthouse Island]], [[Mustard Café]], and [[Sunset Express]].
