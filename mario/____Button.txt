'''"!" Buttons'''<ref>''Wario Land 4'' English instruction booklet, page 26.</ref> (or '''Exclamation Switches'''<ref>''Nintendo Power Advance V.3, page 53.</ref>) are [[block]]s in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
There are three colored "!" Buttons, and [[Wario]] can hit them to solidify transparent blocks, much like the effects of the [[! Switch]]es in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', except Wario can also hit a "!" Button again to return the blocks to their transparent states. "!" Buttons are usually featured in puzzles within [[pink area]]s.
