'''Chibi Syrup''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
The Chibi Syrup is one of [[Mametchi]]'s five special items, referencing his association to science in the ''Tamagotchi'' franchise. It is a scientific potion that has the appearance of a green liquid in a beaker and a face consisting of black dotted eyes and prominent lips.

Upon being used, the Chibi Syrup locks on to a racer, in the manner of a [[Basin]]. When the racer is hit by a Chibi Syrup, they lose speed and shrink temporarily, as if they had been hit by [[Lightning]].
