|game = ''[[Super Mario World]]''}}'''Tubular''' is the second level of the [[Special World]] in the game ''[[Super Mario World]]''. This world is most notable for containing six [[Dragon Coin]]s and is fairly linear. Many fans also acclaim this level to be the hardest in the game.

==Overview==
Tubular consists of a vast pit where the player must guide [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] over it by grabbing numerous [[P-Balloon]]s. This can be difficult to do as multiple enemies are blocking the paths, and moving around with the P-Balloon can prove a challenge. This stage can also be done with a [[Cape Feather]], but getting the gliding started up is even more difficult than completing the level regularly. After reaching the [[Giant Gate]], the player can access [[Way Cool]].

A more straightforward method to complete this level is to use a Blue Yoshi and swallow a shell, then fly to the exit while holding down {{Button|snes|b}}/{{Button|gba|b}} (to fly faster).
