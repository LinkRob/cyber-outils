'''Sharpeas''' are [[list of enemies|enemies]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] versions of [[Spiny|Spinies]]. Sharpies have yellow bean-shaped bodies with green spikes on their back as protection, brown feet, and a black face with white eyes with black dots (horizontal lines in the remake) for pupils. Sharpeas are found in [[Beanbean Fields|areas]] around [[Beanbean Castle]], as well as a few at the very bottom of [[Hoohoo Mountain]].

==Battle==
Due to the spikes on their back, Sharpeas cannot usually be jumped on without causing harm to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], though the Bros.' [[hammer]]s can damage them, as well as Luigi's [[Thunderhand]] is also effective against them as it deals critical damage. They attack Mario and Luigi by charging at them, which can be countered by hammering them. They will always jump before they charge towards the Mario Bros. They frequently show up in battles alongside [[Sworm]]s, and are thrown by [[Lakipea]]s, both in-battle and in the overworld.

In ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'', Sharpeas appear as enemies. They are melee troopers, and their spikes cause damage to minions who attack from above. Their special skill, Spin to Win, allows them to roll into enemies three times. Sharpeas are weak against [[Lakitu]]s.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name "Sharpea" comes from the word "sharp" (due to the creature's spiky back) merged with "pea" (referencing the "bean" theme of the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]).
