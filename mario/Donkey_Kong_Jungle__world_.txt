'''Donkey Kong Jungle''' is the second [[world]] in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'' and [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (Nintendo Switch)|its]] [[Reissue|remake]]. This world takes place in a nature setting area, possibly set in [[Kongo Jungle]] from [[Donkey Kong Island]]. This world introduces new gimmicks: [[Rope|ropes]] and [[fruit]]. It also has new enemies, such as [[RamRam|RamRams]], [[Tane Pakkuns]], and [[Bird (Mario vs. Donkey Kong)|birds]].
