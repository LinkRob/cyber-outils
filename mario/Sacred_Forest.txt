The '''Sacred Forest''' is a green level in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. It is a forest that has been tampered with by Kamek's magic, causing some areas to be overly large or small. Three Color Stars can be found here. The Yellow Rescue Squad's leader can also be found here, and he needs his 11 squaddies to help him pull the Turnip thing out of a giant pipe leading further into the level.
