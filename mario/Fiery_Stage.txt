The '''Fiery Stage''' is the fifth and final world in ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''. It takes place within [[Bowser's Castle]], and [[Mario]] can only access it if he has all four [[Star Key (Mario Pinball Land)|Star Keys]] to unlock each of the four [[boss statue]]s. [[Bowser]] is fought as the boss here, and the first four bosses—[[Petey Piranha]], [[Egyptian Koopa]], [[Porcupuffer (boss)|Porcupuffer]], and [[Big Boo]]—can be fought here again, each rewarding a [[Power Star|Star]] upon being defeated.
