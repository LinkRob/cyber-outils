'''Firefall Cliffs''' is the fourth [[level]] of [[Peach's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. U & New Super Luigi U)|Peach's Castle]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

==Overview==
This course is an autoscrolling course that involves climbing walls and dodging meteors. 

==Walkthrough==
The level begins near a [[Koopa Troopa]], as well as a climbing wall with Meteors that fall above it. Some pits of [[lava]] are found, along with more Meteors. Hard Blocks that can be destroyed by the Meteors are found above sections of the lava, with a [[POW Block]] on one of them. Another climbing wall is found, along with a [[Question Block|? Block]] containing a [[Starman]]. A section of Meteors is found leading up to the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. The section of the level following the Checkpoint Flag autoscrolls upwards, and the player climbs up using platforms and climbing walls. More Meteors act as obstacles throughout the section. More lava pits are found, with a Green Ring located above one of them. More Hard Blocks are found, along with a [[Brick Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. A vertical section containing climbing walls and Meteors is found leading to a [[Warp Pipe]]. The [[Goal Pole]] is then found.

==Enemies==

*[[Koopa Troopa]]s
*Meteors

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The player must use the POW Block to make the first Star Coin drop before fireballs destroy the platform below.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The player must stay to the left side of the first climbing section in order to collect the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The player must stay to the right side of the second climbing section in order to collect the third Star Coin.
