'''Dream Island''' is the main setting of [[Adventure Tours]] mode in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for the [[Nintendo DS]]. The location consists of three towns, accessed in the order of [[Sparkleton]], [[Cubyrinth]], and [[Blizland]]. The other island is [[Winter Island]], which has [[Frostown]], [[Polastraits]], and [[Icepeak]].
