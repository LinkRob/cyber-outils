The '''hidden smasher'''<ref>''Super Mario 64'' Player's Guide (page 67)</ref> is an obstacle in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
It is giant slab of steel hidden in a wall of the volcano in [[Lethal Lava Land]]. The hidden smasher is disguised by its front, which has the same wooden texture as the wall. The hidden smasher is not seen until [[Mario]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in ''Super Mario 64 DS'') approaches it, upon which the hidden smasher falls out of the wall and crashes onto the ground, then retracts into the wall a few seconds later. If Mario gets crushed by the hidden smasher, he loses three wedges of his [[Power Meter]].
