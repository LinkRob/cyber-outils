The '''10-Coin''' is a [[coin]] variation featured in some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. It has the number 10 engraved on it to indicate its value. The 10 Gold Coin first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', although individual coins worth only ten coins first appear in ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]'', which features [[Big Coin]]s in particular.

In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', the 10-Coin appears alongside the [[30-Coin]] and the [[50-Coin]].
