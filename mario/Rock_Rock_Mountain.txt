'''Rock Rock Mountain''' (known as '''Alpine Pass''' in Europe and Oceania) is the final course in the [[Flower Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. It takes place on a high mountain, consisting of pipes, pine trees, and clouds, hence the name.

Although the majority of the course takes place outside, one part is in a cave, where several [[Swooper]]s can be found. Near the end, the player encounters a steep hill where boulders will roll down randomly as hazardous objects. [[Glide|Gliding]] areas can be found all over this course.
