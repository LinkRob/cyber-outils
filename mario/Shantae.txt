'''Shantae''' is the titular character and main protagonist of the {{wp|Shantae|''Shantae'' series}}. She is a half-genie, the daughter of a human father and a mother who was a Guardian Genie.

==In ''WarioWare: D.I.Y.'' / ''WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase''==
In ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]'', Shantae appears in the downloadable [[microgame]] [[Shantae NAB!]], based on the original ''[[w:c:shantae:Shantae (video game)|Shantae]]'' game for [[Game Boy Color]]. It was created by {{wp|Matt Bozon}}, and it released in America on April 19, 2010 and Europe on June 4, 2010. In the microgame, the player must help Shantae in her Monkey form obtain a gem. If successful, Shantae will revert back to her human form.

==In ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''==
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Shantae appears as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]]. In the spirit battle, the player must defeat a [[Killing Edge]]-wielding [[Samus|Zero Suit Samus]] on the [[Pirate Ship (stage)|Pirate Ship]] stage. A Shantae [[List of Mii Fighter Outfits|Mii Brawler outfit]] was also added to the game as DLC on June 29, 2021.
