The '''Smithsnorian Museum''' is a location that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is the second location that [[Wario]] explores in the game.

==Layout==
As the name states, the Smithsnorian Museum is mainly a very large museum with many different kinds of exhibits around. The main area has a very large staircase that eventually leads up to the room that the [[Sphinx (character)|Sphinx]] resides in.

==History==
===Episode 3: The Sphinx Sits on a Secret!===
Wario and [[Goodstyle]] make it to the Smithsnorian Museum in order to get more clues about the [[Wishstone]] pieces. After going through many rooms and past security guards, the two eventually make it to the room with the Sphinx who will only be willing to tell them where the Wishstone pieces are if they bring him three of the pharaoh's treasures. After Wario does so, the Sphinx mentions a [[Sneezemore Cave|chilly mountain]].
