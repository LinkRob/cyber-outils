The '''Toad Trail''' is a [[Bros. Attack]] which first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. It is obtained after defeating [[King Bob-omb]]. It costs 11 [[Bros. Point|BP]] to use Toad Trail.

==Procedure==
[[Mario]] starts this attack. To start, Mario and [[Luigi]] run south of their opponent. Mario then takes the Paper Toads out. The Paper Toads then form a bridge with their bodies. Mario and Luigi ride along this bridge on their way to the enemy, then crash into him.

==Controls==
===Phase 1===
Hold forward on the control stick to speed up. Press left and right to steer. Press A to jump over the gaps that might form.

===Phase 2===
Move the control stick left or right to steer. Press B to strike.

==Ratings==
Falling down causes you to miss the target. travelling slowly or at decent speeds at the wrong target causes an Okay rating, travelling at a decent speed or quickly at the wrong target will give a Good rating, travelling quickly or travelling at near-maximum speed at the wrong target causes a Great rating, and travelling at near-max speeds and hitting the correct target gives an Excellent rating.
