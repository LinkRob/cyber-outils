The '''sunglasses vendor''' is a [[Pianta]] character who appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
[[File:Sunshine_Sunglasses_Vendor_2.png|thumb|180px|left|The sunglasses vendor complimenting Mario's appearance wearing the shirt and shades]]
The sunglasses vendor's main location is under an umbrella on the beach area of [[Delfino Plaza]]. He also appears in most of the courses, except [[Noki Bay]] and [[Sirena Beach]]. In the first course, [[Bianco Hills]], the sunglasses vendor is found at the start.

If [[Mario]] has retrieved at least 30 [[Shine Sprite]]s, he can talk to the sunglasses vendor for the [[Sunshine Shades]], which slightly darken the display. Later, after Mario has defeated [[Bowser]] in [[Corona Mountain]], he can talk to the sunglasses vendor to wear the [[Sunshine Outfit]], a light blue shirt with [[Shine Sprite]] patterns. Both items serve only a cosmetic purpose, so Sunglasses vendor does not have an essential role in the game.

If Mario talks to the sunglasses vendor a second time after receiving the Sunshine Outfit and/or Sunshine Shades, he can return them to the sunglasses vendor.

==Physical description==
The sunglasses vendor wears a green Hawaiian-like shirt with white patterns on them, a pair of sunglasses, and a hat. The sunglasses vendor himself has yellow skin and a black mustache.
