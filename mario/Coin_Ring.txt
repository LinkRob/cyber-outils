'''Coin Rings''', alternatively named '''Gold Rings''', are a recurring object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
Coin Rings are yellow rings that are based on [[Red Ring]]s, although they have an entirely different effect of instantly giving [[coin]]s to [[Mario]] or another character if passed through. In ''Super Mario 3D Land'', passing through a Coin Ring grants five coins to Mario or [[Luigi]], but in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', passing through a Coin Ring instead grants three coins. Several Coin Rings can be found in underwater courses, as well as courses involving [[Plessie]] in ''Super Mario 3D World''.
