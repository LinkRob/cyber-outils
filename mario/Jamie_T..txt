'''Jamie T.''' is the younger sister of [[Jimmy T.]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. She first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' along with her other brother, [[James T.]], although neither of them have made any major appearances. Her mother is [[Mama T.]] and her father is [[Papa T.]].

==Physical description==
Jamie T. has a dark pink afro like her mother, Mama T. although Jamie wears hers in pigtails. Jamie wears a pale yellow tank top bearing a shooting star and a rainbow pattern, a red skirt, and white boots.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
Jamie is first seen in Jimmy T.'s stage [[Dance Club Rub]] along with her other family members, dancing at [[Club Sugar]]. Jamie hosts her own [[microgame]] set, Jamie's Mix, featuring [[Wario]]'s, [[Mona]]'s, and Jimmy T's microgames.
