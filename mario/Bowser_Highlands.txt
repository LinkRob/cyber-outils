'''Bowser Highlands''' is a golf course in ''[[Mario Golf: Super Rush]]''.

==Description==
Bowser Highlands is a lava-based course themed after [[Bowser's Castle]]. There are several [[Bob-omb]]s dotting the holes and some other enemy species, including [[Whomp]]s, [[Thwomp]]s, [[Podoboo]]s, and [[Magmaargh]]s. In Story mode, some of Bowser Highlands is covered in snow, and the affected holes are dotted with [[Mr. Blizzard]]s, [[Freezie]]s, and [[Snow Pokey]]s.

Bowser Highlands has 18 holes and a par of 72, like the other courses.
