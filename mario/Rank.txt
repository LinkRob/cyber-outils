'''Ranks''' are a game mechanic in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''[[Mario and Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''===
Ranks appear alongside levels, and are given to the Bros or Bowser when they reach a certain level. The Bros have six ranks: [[Mushroom]], [[Shell]], [[Fire Flower|Flower]], [[Shine Sprite|Shine]], [[Star]], and Rainbow. Bowser has four ranks: First Boss, Second Boss, Third Boss, and Final Boss.
