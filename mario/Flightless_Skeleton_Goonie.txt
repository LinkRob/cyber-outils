'''Flightless Skeleton Goonies''' are a variant of both [[Flightless Goonie]]s and [[Skeleton Goonie]]s in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Flightless Skeleton Goonies have properties of both Skeleton Goonies and Flightless Goonies, namely the latter in that they have a head and two stubby, fork-shaped legs. Unlike Flightless Goonies, Flightless Skeleton Goonies have only a single hair on their head instead of three. Their eye sockets are entirely black.

===Behavior===
Flightless Skeleton Goonies, like Flightless Goonies, often stick together in groups. They run very fast, trying to hit a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], who can defeat a Flightless Skeleton Goonie by jumping on it.
