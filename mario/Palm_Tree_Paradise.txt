'''Palm Tree Paradise''' is the first level of the [[Emerald Passage]] in ''[[Wario Land 4]]'' and the second regular one overall.

==Description==
It is set in a quiet paradise near the sea, with many palm trees (hence the name) and wildlife in the area. A few parts of this level are cavernous, where the music changes to a darker tone. The level is straightforward as it is only a simple path, with no floors or separate routes to take, so it is often considered one of the easiest. In [[Wario Land 4#Game mode|S-Hard mode]], [[Wario]] is forced to hit the [[switch (Wario Land 4)|switch]] upon entering the level (just like the [[Golden Passage]]). This level's [[CD]] is called [[About That Shepherd]] and once collected, it can be played in the [[Sound Room]].

==Enemies==
*9 [[Marumen]] (3 purple, 6 red)
*3 [[Spear-Mask]]s (yellow)
*1 [[Spiky]]
*1 [[Ringosukī]]

==Time given in different modes==
The time given to escape the level after hitting the [[switch (Wario Land 4)|switch]] varies between [[Wario Land 4#Game mode|game modes]].

*[[File:WL4-Normal Mode Sprite.png]]: the time given is 1:30.
*[[File:WL4-Hard Mode Sprite.png]]: the time given is 0:45.
*[[File:WL4-S-Hard Mode Sprite.png]] (unlockable): the time given is 4:30, but the switch is located at the beginning of the level.
