'''Mummy-Me''' are shadow versions of [[Toad (species)|Toads]] that appear in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''. They resemble Toads in body structure but they have a dark purple skin covered in white bandages like a mummy and have lavender eyes. They behave like [[Cosmic Clone]]s in that they'll chase the player by mimicking their movements until they reach them.
