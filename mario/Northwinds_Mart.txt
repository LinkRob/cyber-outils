'''Northwinds Mart''' is an [[Item Shop]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== History ==
Northwinds Mart is located in [[Fahr Outpost]] and can be visited once [[Mario]] reaches Chapter 7. This Item Shop sells some of the best items in the game such as the strong, multi-hit item, the [[Shooting Star (item)|Shooting Star]].
