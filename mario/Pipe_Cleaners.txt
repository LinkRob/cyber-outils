==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the four players must try to hit the numerous of [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]]s that emerge from the four pipes. They must have fast reflexes to hit all of the Koopa Kids to earn points. The perfect score is 30 points and the player that has the most points at the end of the mini-game wins. This mini-game could also have multiple winners.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Aim at direction
*{{button|n64|a}} - Swing hammer

==Trivia==
*This mini-game's title is a pun of the arts and crafts object. They could also be used to help remove moisture from smoking pipes.
