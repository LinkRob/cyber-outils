'''Jelly Fish''' is an aquatic enemy featured in ''[[Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]'' and its [[Wii]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[New Play Control! Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]''. They are purple creatures based on real-world [[jellyfish]]. If [[Donkey Kong]] touches a Jelly Fish, he gets electrocuted, but Jelly Fish do not otherwise try to actively attack him. The only way to defeat a Jelly Fish is by using the [[Sound Wave Attack]] while on [[Orco]], because if Donkey Kong uses it by himself, the Jelly Fish only produce more electricity.
