'''Jaxis''' make a fast way to travel in the [[Sand Kingdom]] in the game ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. They can be found at [[Jaxi Stand]]s. They cost 30 coins to be used by [[Mario]].

==Trivia==
*Their name is a portmanteau, ''jaguar'', and ''taxi'', explaining why they are used for transport.
