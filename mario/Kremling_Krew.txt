'''Kremling Krew'''  (also referred as '''Kremling Army''' or '''Kremling Gang''')<ref name=Months>''Donkey Kong Country 3'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 2005, 
[https://dkpedia.com/w/index.php?title=File:Donkey_Kong_Country_3_(GBA)_-_Manual.pdf&page=4 p. 6] - '''''TROUBLE AGAIN:''' Months after their most recent victory against King K. Rool and his Kremling army, the Kongs were still celebrating.''"</ref><ref name=General>''Donkey Kong 64'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 1999, p. 5 - '''''STORY:''' Deep within the gloomy bowels of his latest creation, a furious King K. Rool sat on his throne, glaring down at the quaking generals of his vast Kremling army.''"</ref> is a evil organization led by [[King K. Rool]] which serves as the main antagonistic group in the [[Donkey Kong]] series.

Within months from K. Rool's disappearance, [[Kremling]] remnants were referred to as [[Kremling Kreeps]] until beging formed again with return of same.

==Emblem==
The emblem of the Kremling Krew is a variant of the Jolly Roger, but with a Kremling head in place of a human skull. This is shown on the [[Gangplank Galleon]] sail rig, flags, pirate hats, carpet, bedspreads and behind [[The Flying Krock]].

===''Donkey Kong'' franchise===
Upon discovering that there is a neighboring island, half of Kremling Krew forces under the supervision of Klump were sent to [[Donkey Kong Island]] in search of resources and export to their homeland. The group establishes an industrial zone and a network of mines across the mountain.<ref>''Donkey Kong Country'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1994, p. 13 - "'''''KREMKROC INDUSTRIES INC.:''' Welcome to the wastelands. This area of the island, once lush and overgrown with island greenery, has been stripped of all its natural beauty. Instead, smog clouds the air and there is a general lack of greenery. King K. Rool and his factories are to be held responsible for polluting the place.''"</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 1994, p. 20 - '''''DONKEY KONG COUNTRY ATLAS:''' The mountain are riddled with mines, tunnels and caves.''"</ref><ref name=Mines>''Donkey Kong Country'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 1994, p. 23 - '''''MINES:''' The vast mines of Donkey Kong Country have been stripped of any ore they may have once contained. All the tools of the miners remain, however, from mine carts to rickety scaffolding.''"</ref><ref>[https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/mario/images/c/c5/Kevin_Bayliss_in_Twitter_%2810%2C_19%2C_2019%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20240102170632&format=original ''Kevin Bayliss'' on Twitter (retrieved on October, 19, 2019)]</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1994, p. 92 - "'''''TRICK TRACK TREK:''' The burned-out mines seem to be located all over the island.''"</ref> The first resource they found was [[wikipedia:Ore|ores]], but they leave it all behind and some Kritters could not get out of the mine where they were petrified.<ref name=Mines/><ref>''Donkey Kong Country'' (GBA) Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1994, p. 14 - "'''''ROCKKROC:''' Rock Krocs are rare subterranean Krocs that have glowing red eyes and rocklike skin.''"</ref><ref>[https://twitter.com/Ghoulyboy/status/643498510348877824/photo/1 ''Gregg Mayles'' on Twitter (retrieved September 14, 2015)]</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country'' (GBA) Prima Official Game Guide, Nintendo, 2003, p. 43 - "'''''MINE CART CARNAGE:''' Aside from the hidden Warp Barrel that rests invisibly underneath a Camera Piece at the start, many other areas of fearful jumping are to be had during this race through an abandoned mine.''"</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country'' German Player's Guide.  p. 44 - "'''''STOP & GO STATION:''' {{hover|Nachdem Diddy und Donkey die Ruckzuck-Station betreten haben, meinen sie, in einem billigen Gruselfilm gelandet zu sein: Zombies lauern!|After entering the Stop & Go Station, Diddy and Donkey think they've landed in a cheap scary movie: zombies lurk!}}''"</ref>

In ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', the Kremling Krew discovered a [[Banana Hoard]] who was under the [[Donkey Kong]]'s protection and also find its opposition which is the [[Kong Family]].<ref>''Donkey Kong Country'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 1994, p. 4 - '''''IT WAS A DARK AND STORMY NITE...:''' The Kremlings were out there, this much was certain. They coveted Donkey Kong's banana stock-pile, the largest on the island, and probably in the world. A treasure in potassium and vitamin A. The perfect food.''"</ref> The advantage from having managed to steal the entire hoard was defeat [[Diddy Kong]] and ordered by K. Rool to for wait for [[Gangplank Galleon]].<ref>''Donkey Kong Country'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1994, p. 119 - '''''KING K. ROOL:''' The lumbering lard bucket just sits out there on his ship, barking orders at his minions.''"</ref> But the pack had its defeat when Klump with his squad were driven off and K. Rool defeated.

The Kremling Krew began to have its big fall in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''/''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''.<ref>''Donkey Kong Land 2'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 1996, p. 2 - '''''INTRODUCTION:''' King K. Rool and his band of Kremlings apenapped Donkey Kong. They're demanding the Kongs to hand over the banana hoard for Donkey's safe return. Donkey Kong would have a fit if they lost the banana hoard again. So it's up to Diddy and Dixie Kong to save him. Help the two monkeys make their way through all the Kremlings and all the traps to find Donkey Kong.''"</ref> But before this event, the pack returns to its origins as a pirate group and was assigned to kidnap DK to extort the Banana Hoard.<ref>''Nintendo Power'', Vol. 76, September, 1995, p. 26 "'''''CAPTURED:''' They're the same, but not the same. They're deranged, estranged, and subtly changed. K. Rool's Kremling crew show its true colors in DKC2. No longer are they merely repulsive reptiles.''"</ref> During the adventure, Kong Family and Kremling Krew fought across the Crocodile Isle in a conflict that culminated on DK rescue and Crocodile Isle destruction.

Because this, various Kremlings that serves the Kremling Krew were forced to withdraw to the [[Northern Kremisphere]] for months.<ref name=Months/><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 1 - "'''''DONKEY KONG, I PRESUME?:''' Intrepid video game explorers Donkey and Diddy Kong appear to have been swallowed up by the trackless terrain of a whole new continent crawling with Kremlings.''"</ref> Two days earlier when [[KAOS]] appears, the Kremlings grouped in the [[Mekanos]] formed a temporary group called Kremling Kreeps.<ref>''Nintendo Power Magazine'' Vol. 89, 1996, p. 96  - '''''THE ARMY OF KAOS:''' No diabolical plan would be complete without a bevy of baddies. KAOS has an army of Kremling kreeps. These fanatical followers will obey any order given by their mechanized master of mayhem.''"</ref> With the KAOS defeated, is revealed that the Kremling Kreeps was overseen by none other than K. Rool. The Kremling Krew was started to be restored in [[Krematoa]] and [[Kuff 'n' Klout]] are K. Rool's remaining minions.<ref>''Nintendo Power'', Vol. 96, May, 1997, p. 50 - "'''''FREE ALL FIFTEEN BANANAS BIRDS AND UNLEASH THE WRATH OF THE QUEEN BANANA!:''' DKC3 isn't just about rolling around levels and stomping on K. Rool's Kremlings.''"</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 112 - '''''THE KUFF 'N' KLOUT BOUT:''' The Kremling Twins, Kuff 'n' Klout, make their home in Tyrant Twin Tussle.''"</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 116 - "'''''ROCKET RUSH:''' Long ago a rocket fell off Baron K. Roolenstein's junk pile and bounced down a canyon.''"</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 118 - "'''''THE BIGGEST, BADDEST, BATTLE IN THE SEVEN SEAS:''' The gears in Boomer's Bomb Shelter yanked the anchor loose on Baron K. Roolenstein's secret submarine, the Knautilus.''"</ref> The Kremling Krew was in the Northern Kremisphere in ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'' where they lived in the factories.<ref>''Donkey Kong Land III'' Instruction Booklet, Nintendo, 1997, p. 6 "'''''LEVEL MAPS:''' During your adventure, you'll explore many Kremling-infested terrains.''"</ref><ref>''Nintendo Power'', Vol. 103, December, 1997, p. 85 "'''''KUCHUCKA KARNAGE:''' This factory is a marvel of advanced engineering, but you'll be too busy dodging the Kremling creeps to appreciate it.''"</ref>

Before ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' when K. Rool regrouped the Kremling remnants, this time the Kremling Krew is reinforced with an [[K. Rool's Mobile Island Fortress|artificial island]] that fits several members. The pack began to be a threat through a powerful technological weapon called [[Blast-O-Matic]] that intended to destroy Kong Isle. But ex-members as [[K. Lumsy]] and [[Snide]] betrayed the pack by helping the Kong Family deactivate the weapon.<ref>"'''K. Lumsy''': ''Hey, little monkey. Perphaps you could help poor old K. Lumsy escape?  Go on, I'll be your bestest friend...''" - ''Donkey Kong 64''</ref><ref>"'''Snide''': ''I was the chief technician on the project, but that double-crossing lizard kicked me off his team because he didn't trust me.''" - ''Donkey Kong 64''</ref>

In ''[[DK: King of Swing]]'' and ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]'', Kremling Krew had few resources and members. The pack is always backed up by the common Kritters as in previous games involving Kremlings as antagonists.
