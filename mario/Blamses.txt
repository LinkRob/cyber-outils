'''Blamses''' is an enemy from ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is encountered only in [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]].

==Profile==
It is a cannon made of gold. It looks precious but when [[Wario]] approaches it, it will shoot a projectile at him. According to Wario, many thieves before him tried to steal the Blamses thinking it's a real gold statue only to be met with projectiles as a result.

== Flavor text ==
{{Quote|A cannon made of solid gold. Many a robber has tried to steal it, only to end up blown flat on his duff by the very treasure he sought.}}

==Trivia==
*The Blamses' name is a reference to King Ramses who was a famous pharaoh from the ancient Egyptian times.
