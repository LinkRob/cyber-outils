The '''Nintendo 64DD''' (also called '''N64 DD''' or '''N64 Disk Drive''') is a console for the [[Nintendo 64]]. It has 5 released games, and 6 cancelled games. It has the [[Mario Artist (series)|''Mario Artist'' series]], a ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' game and a ''[[Mario Paint]]'' game. It was released exclusively to Japan in December 1999. It was a commercial failure and was discontinued in February 2001 after only 14 months.

==Released Games==
*''Mario Artist: Paint Studio'' (1999)
*''Mario Artist: Talent Studio'' (2000)
*''Mario Artist: Communication Kit'' (2000)
*''Mario Artist: Polygon Studio'' (2000)

==Cancelled Games==
*''Mario Artist: Game Maker'' (1999/2000)
*''Mario Artist: Graphical Message Maker'' (1999/2000)
*''Mario Artist: Sound Maker'' (1999/2000)
*''Mario Artist: Video Jockey Maker'' (1999/2000)
*''[[Mario Paint 64]]'' (working title(?) of ''Mario Artist: Paint Studio'') (1999)
*''Super Mario 64 2'' (working title(?) of ''Super Mario 64DD'') (1999)
