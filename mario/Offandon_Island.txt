'''Offandon Island''' is a major location appearing in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is the thirteenth island of to be found and linked to [[Shipshape Island]] following the [[Uni-Tree]]'s destruction. The island is the first to be visited in the [[Gulchrock Sea]], and the only major island native to the area, though its linking to Shipshape Island is followed by having to reconnect [[Twistee Island]] and [[Heatfreeze Island]], which [[Bowser]] accidentally disconnected during his assault on Shipshape Island.

== History ==
When the [[Great Conductor]] created a vortex to summon [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to save [[Concordia]] after [[Zokket]] destroyed the Uni-Tree in his efforts to sunder the land, [[Bowser]] and the [[Koopa Troop]] were dragged through the portal. Upon arrival, Bowser brought Offandon Island under his control, spreading propaganda claiming that Mario and Luigi are villains. Bowser also gained the loyalty of the Concordian persons there by giving them nourishment.

When the Bros. and their allies reach the Gulchrock Sea, Bowser, who was fighting Zokket's forces, turns his attention and weaponry towards [[Shipshape Island]]. Mario and Luigi are thrown off the ship but manage to reach Offandon Island. Mario and Luigi find that they have been demonised by propaganda, although they rescue someone named [[Furen]], who provides a shortcut to the [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Lighthouse]] to link the island and would later befriend [[Bowser Jr.]]

After the Bros. reconnect [[Twistee Island]] and [[Heatfreeze Island]] (which were disconnected during Bowser's attack), they return to Offandon Island to get clues on how to get to [[Wayaway Island]], wearing [[Disguise Kit|masks to avoid getting caught]].

After [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|takes over Concordia]], the Offandonians become friendly toward the Bros., who therefore can enter the plaza without masks. Furen also came here to save his friend Bowser Jr. after some [[Glohm]]-infected Koopa Troop soldiers attack him. Saving Bowser Jr. leads to the [[Bond (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Bond]] between them being obtained.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Offandon Island's name comes from a spoonerism of the phrase "on and off," which means "intermittently" or "sometimes."
