'''Kōji Takeda''' (born April 10, 1982) is a Japanese voice actor. He did the Japanese voice for [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] in [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie|''The Super Mario Bros, Movie'']]. He would eventually voice [[Donkey Kong]] in the games starting with [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']] and [[Donkey Kong Bananza/Gallery|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']], replacing [[Takashi Nagasako]].
