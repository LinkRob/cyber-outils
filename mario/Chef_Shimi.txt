'''Chef Shimi''' is a chef and a [[Cheep Cheep]] found on the [[Excess Express]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He makes the meals on the train and often speaks incorrect English.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
During the events, Chef Shimi can be seen in the kitchen on the Excess Express. At some point, Chef Shimi's [[Galley Pot|cooking pot]] gets stolen and a crime scene goes underway. Chef Shimi asks for [[Pennington]]'s help and the detective pengiun manages to get Mario on the case. [[Mario]] manages to track down the culprit as [[Heff T.]] and after Mario gives back the pot to Chef Shimi, the chef will reward Mario with a [[Star Piece]].

Chef Shimi has also put up a trouble at the [[Trouble Center]]. His trouble involves bringing him three specific items: a [[Mystic Egg]], a [[Golden Leaf]], and a [[Keel Mango]]. A Mystic Egg can be received from [[Petuni]] after playing "Stump Petuni" with her at [[The Great Tree]]. A Golden Leaf can be found hidden in the [[Creepy Steeple]] on a golden tree, and a Keel Mango can be found on [[Keelhaul Key]]. After bringing Chef Shimi these three items, he will reward Mario with 40 coins.

==Trivia==
*Chef Shimi's name is a reference to "sashimi" which is a Japanese food dish that uses fish.
