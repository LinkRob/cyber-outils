'''Chikunto''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Chikunto are a variant of [[ant]]s, which they resemble, but have retractable spikes on their head and abdomen.

===Behavior===
A Chikunto usually walks with its spikes retracted, making it resemble a regular ant, but when [[Mario]] approaches, a Chikunto sticks out its spikes before retracting them again.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
[[File:Chikunto SML2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Chinkunto are encountered in the [[level]] [[The Ant Monsters]]. If Mario [[jump]] on a Chikunto while its spikes are out, he takes damage. Mario can only safely jump on a Chikunto once it has retracted its spikes. Regardless of their state, a Chinkunto can be defeated from one of [[Fire Mario]]'s [[fireball]]s.
