'''Waluigi's Cap''' is a signature clothing item worn by [[Waluigi]] in all of his appearances since [[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|''Mario Tennis'']].

==Description==
Waluigi's Cap is a purple cap that is Waluigi's equivalent to Luigi's Cap. Waluigi's Cap has a yellow uppercase, a upside down "L" emblem on the front.
