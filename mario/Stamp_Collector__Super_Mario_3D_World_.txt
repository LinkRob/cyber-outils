[[Koopa Troop]]|alignment = Neutral/Good}}The '''Stamp Collector''' is a minor character making her debut appearance in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. She is a [[Sprixie]] who can be found in the [[Sprixie House]], and meeting her allows the player to grab the [[Sprixie Princesses|Sprixie Princess]]-laden stamps that she has.

The Stamp Collector's design is akin to a normal Sprixie: she has a pink outfit and hoodie with a white collar. Just like her species, her outfit seemingly contains an extradimensional space that hides her lower body.
