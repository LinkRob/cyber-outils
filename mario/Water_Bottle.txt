{{Infobox Item|name = Water Bottle|image = [[File:SMS Water Bottles.png|thumb]]|first = ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' (2002)|games = ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''|owner = [[Mario]]|effects = Fill up the [[F.L.U.D.D.]]|notes = The player must quickly grab the Water Bottle since it nearly disappear after it drops.}}The '''Water Bottle''' is an item that is used to fill up [[F.L.U.D.D.]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

== Usage ==
The Water Bottle can come into two sizes. They are found on [[Isle Delfino]] and dropped by some enemies after they are defeated. The player must quickly grab the Water Bottle since they almost disappear after being dropped.{{SMSNav}}
