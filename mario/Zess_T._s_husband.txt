'''Zess T.'s husband''' is a character who is mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is the late husband of [[Zess T.]]

==History==
Zess T.'s husband was only mentioned by [[Wonky]] during one of his tales. According to him, Zess T. and her husband used to share [[Couple's Cake]] together. However, at some point, Zess T.'s husband died and this may be the reason why Zess T. is always in a foul mood.
