'''Flashy Jump''' is [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It is a more-powerful variant of the [[Jump]] sticker.

== Appearance ==
Flashy Jump is the same in appearance to the [[Shiny Jump]] except for a diagonal rainbow background.
