'''Swamp Pipe Crawl''' is a course of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level features many [[Swirlypod]]s and [[Shova]]s. The level opens with many Swirlypods on [[Semisolid Platform]]s and [[pipe]]s. After this, the level immediately showcases how Shovas work, forcing one to move a pipe before the player can stop it. The rest of the level is filled with pushable pipes.

Later in the level is are two pushable pipes, one on top of a [[Semisolid Block]]. Pushing the bottom pipe to the right will allow the top pipe to fall into a slot. After this, a [[Wow Bud]] will spawn on top of the pipe. Touching this Wow Bud will summon the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will cause some pipes to because alive and become [[Inchworm Pipe|Inchworm Pipes]]. 

==[[10-Flower Coin]] locations==
Pushing the first pushable pipe into a slot will make the pipe able to enter. The pipe will bring the player to the background where touching a [[Wow Bud]] will summon the first 10-flower coin.

Shortly after the first 10-flower coin is a pushable pipe connecting to another pipe that can be entered. In the room after entering the pipe, is the second 10-flower coin in the center.

During the wonder effect, there is a [[Wow Bud]] that will summon a pipe when touched. Entering the pipe will send you to an area in the background, where the final 10-flower coin is obtainable.
