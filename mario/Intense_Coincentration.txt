'''Intense Coincentration''' is one of [[Wario]]'s [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It is a variation of [[Coincentration]], which went on to appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', unlike Intense Coincentration.

==Gameplay==
Intense Coincentration plays almost the same as Coincentration, except the [[Moneybag]] drops fewer [[Coin]]s after Wario punches it upward, precisely 16 instead of 40 like in Coincentration.

After the Coins fall into the [[Brick Block]]s on the lower screen, the player must use their [[Stylus]] to tap whichever Brick Blocks contain a coin. If they tap a Block without any Coins, the minigame ends, and any coins found up to that point are added to the player's score.
