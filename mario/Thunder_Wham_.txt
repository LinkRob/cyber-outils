'''Thunder Wham!''' is [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s [[Super Ability]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

== Description ==
Donkey Kong can pound his fists on the ground and hit nearby foes, and can even tilt the screen whether which side he does it.
