'''Blue's Beach Hut''' is the residence of [[Blue]] during the events of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. This area is located directly next to the interior and exterior of [[Cotton-Top Cove]].

In the events of the game, Blue is sad because his [[Brothers Bear|brothers]] failed to remember his birthday (January 16). Though, [[Blizzard]] who resides in his [[Blizzard's Basecamp|basecamp]] managed to have remembered his birthday and requests [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] and [[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] to send it to Blue as he lives too far away. Once they reach the Beach Hut, Blue finds out the present is a [[Bowling Ball]], and he finds no use for it, allowing the monkeys to keep it.
