'''Flame Gear Tower''' is the first [[castle]] level which appears in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Features==
The level features rotating [[cog]]s with [[Burner]]s attached to them. It is a little difficult to pass them, as the flames are mostly on the gears, which will rotate the characters towards the flames if the characters do not jump.

==Star Coins==
*'''[[Star Coin]] 1''': Ride the first gear to the right side of it and avoid flames from shooting out to get the Star Coin. When collecting the coin, make a wall jump to avoid death.
*'''Star Coin 2''': On the right of the second gear, the Star Coin is revealed. Just get it and jump off immediately to avoid getting hit by the flames.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Over the platform where you'll meet the first [[Dry Bones]], jump over the middle of the platform to reveal invisible blocks leading up to the third Star Coin in between two flames.
