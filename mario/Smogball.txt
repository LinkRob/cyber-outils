'''Smogballs''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Smogballs are dense, stationary, yellow clouds.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Smogballs are encountered in [[Ladida Plains]] and [[Giddy Sky]]. They first appear in the level [[Ladida Plains 1-3]]. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can only destroy a Smogball by twirling it repeatedly from the effects of the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] [[vibe]]. Smogballs are one of the enemies to not have a [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry.
