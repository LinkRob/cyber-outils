'''P Panels''' are an object which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Description==
A P Panel is an orange floor switch depicting a "P," and it is a variation of the [[P Switch]]. If a group of P Panels are pressed simultaneously, with at least one usually having to be held down by an object, a certain event occurs.
