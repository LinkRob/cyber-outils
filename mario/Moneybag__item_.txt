A '''Moneybag''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Description==
A Moneybag awards 50 [[coin]]s when collected and predates the first appearance of [[50-Coin]]s by several years. It can be found in certain [[? Block]]s.
