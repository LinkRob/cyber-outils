The '''Space Warrior Mask''' is a [[Useful Item]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''.

==Description==
It is a [[wikipedia:papier-mâché|papier-mâché]] mask in the shape of [[w:c:metroid:Samus's Helmet|Samus's Helmet]]. When equipped, sound effects and music from the ''[[w:c:metroid:Metroid (game)|Metroid]]'' game play as Mario imitates [[Samus Aran|Samus]]'s [[w:c:metroid:Arm Cannon|Arm Cannon]].

==In ''Paper Mario: The Origami King''==
The Space Warrior Mask is found in the staff room of [[Shogun Studios]] alongside the [[Jungle King Mask]] and [[Goomba Mask]]. In order to sneak into [[Big Sho' Theater]], Mario is prompted to use one of the masks and disguise as a [[Paper Macho Soldier]]. If the Space Warrior Mask is chosen, he is quickly chased out and [[Olivia]] and [[Bob-omb (Paper Mario: The Origami King)|Bob-omb]] suggest he use a different item. When Olivia is sad over Bob-omb's death in [[Breezy Tunnel]], Mario can use the Space Warrior Mask and imitate Samus's Arm Cannon again, although she remains silent. In both cases, the Goomba Mask must be used instead.
