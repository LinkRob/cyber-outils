A '''Squoinker''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are light pink colored variants of [[Squiglet]]s. They are also the strongest variant of them.



==History==
Squoinkers are the strongest versions of [[Squiglet]]s that can be only found in later rooms of the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]] making them rather rare enemies to encounter in the game. Like all of the Squiglet species (except for Squiglets themselves), Squoinkers attack with rocks. Due to being a stronger species, the Squionker's rocks travel much faster.
