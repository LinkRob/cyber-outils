A '''Flying Snifit''' is a secondary enemy which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. They are blue [[Snifit]]s with a pair of butterfly wings.

Flying Snifits are only seen aiding the [[Flifit]]s in [[Bumpsy Plains]]. Bowser can inhale several of a Flying Snifit's petals to leave it wilted. During a wilted Flifit's turn, the Flying Snifit then comes to water it, instantly restoring the Flifit to its original state, and then flies away afterward. This role is somewhat similar to those of [[Beezo|Shy Aways]] from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.
