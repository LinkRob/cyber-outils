'''Toad's Quick Draw''' is a 4-player mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in front of a long [[train]] within a desert. There is an oil reserve in the background. Each car in the train carries up to nine [[balloon]]s.

==Opening==
A train comes out of a tunnel, then stops in front of the four players, who are each holding their own [[pop gun]].

==Gameplay==
Each round begins with a [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]] releasing the balloons from the train car. When [[Toad (character)|Toad]] holds up a flag of a certain color, a few seconds after the balloons are released, the characters must try to shoot a balloon of the same color, so the mini-game requires quick reflexes. The first character to shoot a balloon with the same color as Toad's flag wins a [[point]], then the next round begins. The first player to shoot down the correct balloons three times wins. There is no penalty for shooting wrong balloons.

==Ending==
The players' pop guns disappear. The winner does their victory animation whilst the others do their respective losing animations.
