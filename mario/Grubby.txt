'''Grubby''' (トゲラ) is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It is the matured form of a [[Unera]]. Grubbies have spikes protruding from their body, protecting them from [[jump]] attacks. [[Mario]] can defeat a Grubby by either hitting a block they are on top from below or by touching one while under the effect of a [[Starman]].
