The '''Hammer Throw''' is [[Hammer Bro]]'s [[Skillshot]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Once fully charged, Hammer Bro jumps up and tosses three [[hammer]]s at the [[Kritter|Goalie]], immobilizing the Goalie, then Hammer Bro shoots the ball with his second hammer. Once the shot is initiated, opponents usually cannot counter it. Though Hammer Bro is good at kicking balls, there is a small chance the shot misses and hits the goalpost instead, especially if he is at the sides of the goal. Hammer Throw must be done at close range, otherwise the hammers are too far off and will be most likely blocked by the Goalie. Even if the player can quickly recover the ball using another character, the Goalie remains immobilized, and a rebound shot can be kicked in for a goal.
