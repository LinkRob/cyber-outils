'''DK Spaces''' (formatted as '''DK spaces''' in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'') are a type of [[space]] in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]]. They are where [[Donkey Kong]] shows up if a [[List of characters|character]] lands on one, generally resulting in a positive effect. DK Spaces mainly serve as a counter to [[Bowser Space]]s. Many show either Donkey Kong's mugshot (facing sideways or directly forwards) or the DK symbol.

==History==
===''Mario Party 5''===
The space first appears in ''Mario Party 5'', which is also the first ''Mario Party'' game where Donkey Kong leaves the playable roster until his appearance in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' where he returns as a fully playable character. Only one DK space appears on each board, and it is placed on any existing blue space at the start of the board. Landing on a DK space triggers one of three events:
*'''DK Bonus''': Donkey Kong lets the player roll a [[Dice Block|dice block]] to get coins (5, 10, 15, 20, or 25 coins) equal to the number shown. 
*'''DK Minigame''': All players participate in a [[minigame]] where they collect [[banana]]s. At the end, Donkey Kong gives everyone [[coin]]s equal to the number of bananas that they got by once, twice, or even thrice (as he converts them into coins).
*'''DK Roulette''': Donkey Kong rolls a number on the dice block, and the player must roll a number higher than Donkey Kong's to get a [[Star (Mario Party series)|star]]. This is similar to the dice game on [[Goomba's Greedy Gala]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. If the player rolls a higher number or if Donkey Kong rolls a "1" (as the player instantly wins because they do not roll higher than "1"), they get a free star. If the player rolls lower or if Donkey Kong rolls a "10" (as the player instantly loses because they can't roll higher than "10"), they lose and get nothing.

In Story Mode, if a [[Koopa Kid]] lands on a DK space, Donkey Kong punches the Koopa Kid, and make them lose half of their coins. If the player lands on a DK space, they earn 10 coins from Donkey Kong.

===''Mario Party 6''===
In ''[[Mario Party 6]]'', more DK Spaces are placed on the board, but they are only around during the day, as they are replaced with Bowser Spaces at night. The DK Roulette event from ''Mario Party 5'' has been removed. The DK Bonus event now allows the player to possibly win a Star or up to 25 coins (same as in ''Mario Party 5''). The DK Bonus now involves a Dice Block shaped like a barrel called a "Dice Barrel". DK Minigames work the same as in ''Mario Party 5''.

There are no DK Spaces or [[Bowser Space]]s in [[Castaway Bay]] or [[Clockwork Castle]] (the final two boards) due to Donkey Kong and Bowser appearing on the board. (And the player follows Donkey Kong at day, but Bowser at night.) However, the "DK Space" music will play if the player stops at a [[DK's Riverboat]] in [[Castaway Bay]] or runs into [[Donkey Kong]] in [[Clockwork Castle]].

Additionally, this is the only game where DK Spaces are yellow-orange instead of blue and the last one to use DK's face as the space's emblem.

===''Mario Party 7''===
In ''[[Mario Party 7]]'', the DK Roulette and DK Bonus events are gone. Players can only earn their prizes from the DK Spaces by minigames alone. In this game, there are two types of Minigames &mdash; single-player and multiplayer. In single-player, players have to beat Donkey Kong to the goal in order to earn coins --10, 20, or 30 coins-- or even a Star (though Solo Cruise only shows the coin prizes --10, 20, or 30 coins). If Donkey Kong wins, they receive nothing. However, the exception is [[Windmillville]] --where it can only be 10, 20, or 30 coins-- for single-player (due to the unorthodox method of stealing stars there). Multiplayer works the same as it did in the last two installments.

===''Mario Party 8''===
[[File:MP8 DK Space Bowsers Warped Orbit.png|thumb|200px|[[Luigi]] landing on the DK Space in Bowser's Warped Orbit, where Donkey Kong will give him a free star.]]
In ''[[Mario Party 8]]'', whenever someone lands on the space, Donkey Kong appears to do something helpful, with a different method for each board, usually the opposite of whatever that board's Bowser Space will do. (The DK Roulette, Bonus, and Minigame events are all gone. That is, since there are no DK minigames.) In this game, the DK Space alternates with the Bowser Space, meaning that when one lands on the DK Space, after the event is over, it will be replaced with a Bowser Space and vice-versa. Also, the shape of the space is different with each board, including the tutorial. This time around, all three of the DK events from the previous three installments are gone (the DK Roulette, DK Bonus, and DK Minigame).

The following events occur on each board:

*'''[[DK's Treetop Temple]]''': Donkey Kong tosses the player directly to the Star. However, the player still has to trade 20 coins for the star. This is similar to the [[Genie Lamp|Magic Lamp]] and [[Flutter Orb]] from past installments.
*'''[[Goomba's Booty Boardwalk]]''': Donkey Kong tells the player to deposit 5 coins into a [[DK Barrel]]. The player then shakes the barrel as much as they can within a few seconds to earn back more coins (the player can earn back 11 to 25 coins). If the player has no coins, Donkey Kong gives them 10 coins as a gift.
*'''[[King Boo's Haunted Hideaway]]''': Donkey Kong caps all of the pitfalls with special platforms. The first player to reach a pitfall gets a free Star from Donkey Kong before being sent back to the start (via a [[Warp Pipe]] at the back). The platforms also disappear when [[King Boo]] shuffles the house or whenever someone lands on a Bowser Space.
*'''[[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]]''': Donkey Kong's [[Barrel Train (Mario Party 8)|Barrel Train]] will overtake the current locomotive. Donkey Kong will then give a free Star to the first player that reaches him. He doubles the player's coin total in [[Mario_Party_8#Star_Battle_Arena|Star Battle Arena]] (dependent on the number of coins that the player has when they reach him) and then leaves. However, if the player has zero coins, Donkey Kong gives them 10 coins. The Barrel Train will also leave if someone lands on a Bowser Space.
*'''[[Koopa's Tycoon Town]]''': Donkey Kong deposits 20 coins into one of the five main hotels (three on Star Battle Arena) on the player's (or team's) behalf. In Battle Royale and Tag Battle, if the player (or team) has invested 100 coins in all five main hotels, Donkey Kong will instead deposit 20 coins to a hotel in a Lucky Space area. If the player (or team) has invested 100 coins in all seven hotels, Donkey Kong will do nothing.
*'''[[Bowser's Warped Orbit]]''': Donkey Kong gives the player a free star (a free piece of candy in Star Battle Arena).

===''Mario Party 9''===
In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', DK Spaces appear only on the board [[DK's Jungle Ruins]]. Unlike the previous four installments, the event is used as a [[Event Space|Green Space]] event. Because when landed upon, the [[Banana]]s and [[Z-Banana]]s (which replace [[Mini Star]]s and [[Mini Ztar]]s from the previous six boards but have a similar function), rotate around the stage in an event called Banana Spin Wheel. The bananas rotate until the captain presses {{button|wii|A}}. When this happens, the captain can keep any bananas that flies past after the rotation is stopped.
