A '''Midair Spin''' is a move which appears in some {{series|Super Mario}} games. It first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

== Description ==
In most of its appearances, a Midair Spin is generally performed by shaking the controller. It involves [[Mario]] or another playable character doing a 180-degree spin in midair that delays their fall for a split second. Using a Midair Spin is not required, and it serves as just a small additional benefit.
