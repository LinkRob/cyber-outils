=Reptile Rumble=
{{Donkey_Kong_Level|game=[[Donkey Kong Country]]|world=[[Kongo Jungle]]|terrain=Cave|animalbuddies=None|bonuslevels=Three|enemies=[[Slippa]], [[Kritter|Bouncing Kritter]], [[Zinger]]|prev=[[Ropey Rampage]]|next=[[Coral Capers]]|image=Reptile Rumble.webp}}'''Reptile Rumble''', (クレムリンのどうくつ ''Kremling's Den'' in Japan), is the third level in [[Kongo Jungle]] and ''[[Donkey Kong Country]].'' It's previous level is [[Ropey Rampage]] and the next is [[Coral Capers]].

==Overview==
Reptile Rumble is somewhat flat at the first half of the level, but it gets bumpy as you progress the course. It is a cave theme level like [[Bouncy Bonanza]], [[Torchlight Trouble]], [[Elevator Antics]] and [[Manic Mincers]]. The level overall has minor peril, but it is more wild than previous levels in [[Kongo Jungle]]. It is unknown if it is day or night during this level. There is a minor count of abysses in this level, as if it is almost all just land.

The enemies in this level are; seven [[Slippa|Slippas]], thirteen [[Kritter|Bouncing Kritters]], and four [[Zinger|Zingers]].  

==Walkthrough==
When the level starts, [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey]] enters through a cave entrance. The starting view has a barrel on the first floor. Start walking and pass the [[Slippa]] on the deeper floor. Then grab [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] from the [[DK Barrel]] and pass the two [[Kritter|Kritters]] while walking down the hill and grab the letter "K". Jump up the hill and pass 3 [[Kritter|Kritters]] and pass 1 more [[Kritter]] after jumping on a hill. Jump on the tire to bounce up on the hill. There is another [[DK Barrel]] on a hill where you use a tire to jump on. Jump down the hill and pass 3 [[Kritter|Kritters]] on the ground and jump on the tire on the higher floor and pass a [[Zinger]], get the letter "O", and pass the next [[Zinger]].

Next, pass the 2 [[Slippa|Slippas]] on the higher ground and bounce on the tires to pass the [[Zinger]] and get the letter "N" and bounce on the next tires to get to the next floor. There is another higher floor with a barrel and a [[DK Barrel]] which is accessed with a tire. Pass the 2 [[Slippa|Slippas]] on the lower floor and bounce on the tire to get to the higher floor, while passing a [[Zinger]]. Pass the [[Kritter]] and get the [[Enguarde the Swordfish|Enguarde]] [[Animal Token]], then pass another [[Kritter]]. Then, bounce on the tire to pass 4 [[Slippa|Slippas]]. Pass a [[Kritter]] and bounce on the tires to get the letter "G", pass another [[Kritter]] and keep bouncing up the tires to reach the exit of the level.

==Collectibles and Secrets==

===K-O-N-G Letters===

*K: After passing 2 [[Kritter|Kritters]] right before a hill.
*O: After jumping up a hill, jump down to pass a [[Zinger]] and get the letter before another [[Zinger]].
*N: After passing 2 [[Slippa|Slippas]] and bouncing on tires to pass a [[Zinger]] and get the letter.
*G: After passing a [[Kritter]], the letter is accessible by bouncing on the tires to get it.

===Bonus Levels===

*The first bonus level is located after grabbing a barrel either at the start or grabbing the one after the first [[DK Barrel]] and carrying it until you reach the hill right after the letter "K", and then smash the barrel into the hill's left wall. The bonus level is a darker area than the normal level and has an [[Extra Life Balloon]] on the only hill.
*The second bonus level is located after using a tire to bounce up to the highest hill in the level which has the second [[DK Barrel]] and jumping up to the [[Blast barrel|Blast Barrel]] on the left of the hill. The bonus level consists of a purplish color. After getting into the bonus level, get into the [[Blast Barrel]] at the start of the bonus level and it will blast you into numerous [[Blast Barrel|Blast Barrels]] and will get you blasted into bananas.
*The third and final bonus level is located when you reach the three hills where there are 2 [[Slippa|Slippas]]. Once you defeat those, enter the hole on the hill on the right of the [[Slippa|Slippas]]. It is also purplish like the previous bonus level. Once entered, hop on the moving tire platform and and bounce up and down to get the bananas in the air.

===Warp Barrel===
This [[Warp Barrel]] is exclusive to the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]].''

[[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey]] has to jump on the hill which has the first bonus level's entrance on it's left and jump high in the to find the [[Warp Barrel]]. It will lead them to letter "G" and the exit.

===Sticker Pack===
This [[DK Sticker Pack|Sticker Pack]] is exclusive to the [[Game Boy Color]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]].''

This Sticker Pack is found in a hump below right below one of the [[Zinger|Zingers]] and the letter "N"

===Photographs===
The [[Photographs]] are exclusive to the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]].''

The first photograph is obtained by stomping on 3 [[Kritter|Kritters]] in a row without touching the ground. This is possible by stomping on the 3 [[Kritter|Kritters]] before the letter "O".

The second photograph is obtained by jumping on 3 [[Slippa|Slippas]] in a row without touching the ground. This is possible by stomping on 3 of the 4 [[Slippa|Slippas]] after the [[Enguarde the Swordfish|Enguarde]] [[Animal Token]].
