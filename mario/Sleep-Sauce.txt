'''Sleep-Sauce''' is a special attack used by certain enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Sleep-Sauce is used, an cyan-colored orb bounces toward the target. With Sleep-Sauce, the foe may be able to inflict the [[Sleep]] status ailment on one of [[Mario]]'s party. Wearing a [[Wake Up Pin]] or using a healing technique like [[Therapy]] can nullify this.
