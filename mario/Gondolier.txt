'''Gondolier''' is a pizza-delivery man from the in-universe [[Venice]], [[Italy]] and a character who makes a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[The Venice Menace]]." He seeks [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]]'s assistance in dealing with a sea monster attacking Venice, later revealed to be the [[Doom Sub]].
