==Effects==
Slow Flowers can only be found in blocks around in levels. When someone touches Slow Flower, time itself slows down drastically but the player is able to earn a lot of points during the time. This is very useful in leveling up especially in an area with a lot of enemies. The opposite of the Slow Flower is the [[Speed Flower]].
