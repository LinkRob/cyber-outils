The '''Pipe Master''' (also referred to as '''Piper''' or the '''Pipeman''') is a character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. He can be found inside the [[Pipe House (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Pipe House]].

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
The Pipe Master is a pipe-inhabiting creature who resembles a [[Bullet Bill]], but it is unknown how long he actually is. The Pipe Master has a long, black body, with two white arms at his sides. His face has a wide grin, but no eyes, but this does not prevent him from seeing.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ==
While the [[Beanbean Castle Town]] is peaceful, Mario and Luigi went into the [[Pipe House (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Pipe House]], they were about to go through the [[Red Pipe]] until the Pipe Master popped out looking pleased to see the Bros.. He asks them about what pipe do they want to ask about. When asking about [[Warp Pipe|Green Pipes]], he explained to jump in one to another place to "elsewhere land". When asking about [[Yellow Pipe]]s, it explained that they are traveled below the "seafloor and the surface or between above and belowground". If the Bros. allowed him to explain more about pipes, he greets them a goodbye. He told them to "study a little on their own".
