{{Image}}The '''Infinijump''' a sticker in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It takes 100 jumps to finish it, making it for the player to defeat a battle extremely easy. It only has one problem. In some battles (like the [[Big Cheep-Cheep]] battle) were it is necessary have a specific item, even if the boss has 100 HP, if the Infinijump lands 100 jumps, only a certain amount will damage them. Apart from this one problem, it is an extremely good item and will be useful in almost any situation.
