'''Charge''' is a special move used by [[Goombario]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Usage==

This move uses only 1 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use and Goombario must be in the [[Super Block#Super Rank|Super Rank]] in order to use the attack. When Goombario uses Charge, it will raise the power of his next attack by 2. This can be helpful when followed by his [[Multibonk]] move.

The Charge technique is also used by the Hyper-based [[Goomba]]s where it actually increases their Attack power by so much more than 2.
