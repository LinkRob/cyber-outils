The '''Cannonball Chuck''' is a new [[Bros. Attack]] for ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', replacing [[Jet-Board Bash]]. Being a [[Special Attack]], it costs 9 [[Special Point]]s to perform. Its location is [[Doop Doop Dunes]], with the [[Nabbit]] dropping the move's [[Attack Piece]]s upon being captured in Nabbit Multiplication.

== Description ==
===Procedure===
[[Mario]] starts the move by summoning the cannonball. [[Luigi]] then grabs on and Mario starts spinning. Press the A button when Mario has the cannonball pointing north. If he throws the cannonball at this point, Mario will send the ball flying northward. Luigi then has to steer the cannonball into his target.

===Controls===
====Phase 1====
*Throw Cannonball - {{button|3ds|A}}

====Phase 2====
*Steer - {{button|3ds|Stick}}
*Strike - {{button|3ds|B}}

=== Ratings ===
Striking the ground just releases the shockwave to hurt all grounded enemies and results in an Okay! rating. Striking an enemy from the side results in a Good! rating. Failure to press B or striking the wrong target causes a Great! rating. Striking the proper target will net the Excellent! rating.

=== Usage ===
Cannonball Chuck is Mario's single-strongest attack in the game if you successfully land an Excellent! rating, even outperforming Toad Trail against Paper enemies. It's exceedingly powerful when used against single targets, or multiple strong targets that are close together (such as the Bowser Jr. Duo), while it suffers against large crowds and/or stacked Paper enemies. The attack also scales off of Mario & Luigi's raw POW stat, rather than their equipped Boots and Hammers, meaning it'll do consistently high damage no matter what.
