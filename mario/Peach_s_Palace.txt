'''Peach's Palace''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''.

==Description==
Peach's Palace is one of [[Princess Peach]]'s personal tennis courts. In the back is a large stained-glass window of Peach, identical to the one in front of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] in many of the castle's appearances since ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. A pink border and two crystal-like hearts are near the stained-glass window. On the tennis court itself, one side is blue ground, while other side is purple. Multiple [[Toad (species)|Toads]] appear in the background as spectators. Peach's Palace has the ball speed set to fastest, but the bounce strength of the ball is at normal. 

==In ''Mario Tennis Open''==
The [[1-Up Mushroom Cup]] takes place in Peach's Palace. The tennis court's background music is three different arrangements of [[Inside the Castle Walls]].
