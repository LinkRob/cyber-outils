'''Poundbrella''' is a move in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It can be learned from the [[Shop (Super Princess Peach)|Shop]] for 500 [[coin]]s.

==Description==
Poundbrella is essentially a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] in that [[Princess Peach]] can use [[Perry]] to drop down and break [[block]]s down their path. The area also shakes, stunning any enemy on the ground onscreen and also moving certain objects toward Princess Peach, such as a [[Springboard]].
