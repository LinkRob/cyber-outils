'''Bunson the Hot Dog''' is a boss in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. He is fought in [[Bunson the Hot Dog's Castle]].

==Battle==
Once entering Bunson's lair, you will see Bunson as a puppy. [[Kamek]] will then use magic on Bunson, turning him into Bunson the Hot Dog. First, Bunson will breathe 3 balls of fire. He will then jump to the other side of the room. Throw a yarn ball at Bunson's gray head. He will fire up and then you must ground pound his tongue. He will then jump to a platform in the background, and breathe a long burst of fire. Bunson will then jump back into the battle area. He will then jump to the other side, then jumping onto the wall. Throw another yarn ball at his gray head, and once again, Ground Pound his tongue. He will then leap to the same background platform, but this time, breathe a faster long burst of fire. He will jump back into the battle area, and then get on a wall. Instead of starting to breath fire, he will leap to the other side, dropping 3 blue balls in the process. Throw a yarn ball at Bunson's gray head, then Ground Pound his tongue, to win the fight.
