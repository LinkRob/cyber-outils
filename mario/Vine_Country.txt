'''Vine Country''' is a single-player [[Donkey Kong]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' and a Special [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''. Its name is a pun on the term "wine country."

==Gameplay==
[[File:MP7 Vine Country.png|thumb|The ending in ''Mario Party 7'']]
The minigame consists of climbing [[vine]]s in a race to the top with Donkey Kong, but the player also has to avoid falling spiders and [[beehive]]s. If the player is hit, the player gets stunned, giving Donkey Kong the advantage, but he can also be stunned.

==Controls==
===''Mario Party 7''===
*{{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Tap to climb
*{{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Choose a side

===''Mario Party: The Top 100''===
*Mash {{button|3ds|A}}: Climb
*{{button|3ds|stick}} Left/Right: Dodge
