'''Baby Bowser Broadside''' is a 2 vs. 2 mini-game which appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the two teams must shoot at the picture of the [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]] to earn points. This could be tricky as the picture moves around and the platform it is on is spinning which prevents shots from certain angles. During halfway point, all of the players will start moving making this mini-game even more difficult. The team that has the most points by the end of the mini-game wins.
