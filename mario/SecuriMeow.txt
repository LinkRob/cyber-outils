A '''SecuriMeow''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It secures [[Fort Francis]] from intruders.



==History==
SecuriMeows are robots created by [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]] that are seen in a couple of room of [[Fort Francis]]. The SecuriMeows serve as security guards to Francis' private rooms. The heroes must tell the SecuriMeows the correct code in order to advance into the rooms.

Answering it incorrectly will cause the SecuriMeow to drop the intrudes down a pit. Answering one of the questions wrong is actually required as the room, the pit, leads into the room where the [[Pixl]] [[Carrie]] is found in a cell.
