'''Balloon Baby Yoshi''' is a magenta-colored Baby Yoshi appearing in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It allows the player to reach higher places by inflating like a balloon when the player shakes the  or presses the  button on the . Balloon Baby Yoshis can inflate to a maximum of seven times, and will deflate afterwards or when the player lands. Other players can grab on to the player with the Baby Yoshi in the same way players can lift one another and can also be carried into the air. Balloon Baby Yoshi also adds a chorus to the current music, one he himself creates.

Only one Balloon Baby Yoshi appears in ''Super Mario-kun'', where the Baby Yoshi bites Mario with sharp/properly brushed fangs, causing blood.
