{{Nihongo|'''The Fuzz'''|チョロボン３|Chorobon Surī}} are a team of [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] in the [[Glitz Pit]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
In battle, The Fuzz battle much like regular Fuzzies using draining attacks. However, it is recommended to take down the [[Green Fuzzy]] first as it can create clones of itself (though many find this useful as more [[Star Point]]s can be earned this way). The [[Flower Fuzzy]] should be taken out next since it can absorb Mario's [[Flower Point]]s and use a lightning attack that can deal damage to both Mario and his partners.
