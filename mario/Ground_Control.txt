'''Ground Control''' is a microgame from [[WarioWare Gold|''WarioWare Gold'']]. It is set in the Fantasy set, hosted by [[Dribble]] and [[Spitz]], and uses the Mash control scheme for its gameplay.

== Layout and Gameplay ==
Ground Control's layout is identical to [[Paper Plane]] from [[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!|''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'']], but with an aesthetic overhaul. The protagonist is a small white humanoid being with black beady eyes, riding a leaf. The obstacles are green bars with flowers, with their thickness increasing with the difficulty level. If the character crashes into a wall, they will be absorbed into the wall with a sapling growing where they made impact as a winged halo flies away, and the player will be defeated.
