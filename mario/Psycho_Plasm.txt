'''Psycho Plasm''' is a special move used by [[Ameboid]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Psycho Plasm is used, a cyan ball bounces toward an opponent.

==Effects==
With Psycho Plasm, the Ameboid may be able to cause the [[Fear]] status to one of [[Mario]]'s party. Wearing a specific item such as a Safety Pin will cancel out this effect.
