'''Cosmic Mario''' is a minor antagonist from the first ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. He appears as a boss in Galaxies that have been infected by a [[Prankster Comet|Cosmic Comet]]. As ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' lacks Cosmic Comets, replacing them with Clone Comets, Cosmic Mario doesn't appear in that game.

Cosmic Mario appears as a [[Star Bit]]-filled version of [[Mario]] (or Luigi) in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. His voice is unusually high-pitched and squeaky. His primary role in the game is similar to [[Koopa the Quick]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', and [[Il Piantissimo]] from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', in which the player races him to get an award; in this case, a [[Power Star]]/[[Flagpole|Flag Pole]]. Holding Z when in the ready position causes Mario or Luigi to get a boost. The races are slightly more difficult against [[Cosmic Luigi]].

Cosmic Mario resembles [[Shadow Mario]]'s appearance and [[Cosmic Clone]]s. It was given to him to add a space effect to the character.

Cosmic Mario/Luigi is only in a galaxy if there is a [[Cosmic Comet]] orbiting the galaxy. Cosmic Comets appear in the following galaxies:
*[[Honeyhive Galaxy]]: Honeyhive Cosmic Mario Race: Mario and Cosmic Mario race from the starting area, through the archway, and through the honey hall that is visited in the first mission.
*[[Freezeflame Galaxy]]: Frosty Cosmic Mario Race: Mario and Cosmic Mario race in the ice and lava platforms area. The lava platforms have frozen to make the race easier.
*[[Gold Leaf Galaxy]]: Cosmic Mario Forest Race: Mario and Cosmic Mario race from the starting area to the rafters of the main planet, taking a route akin to the mission When It Rains, It Pours.
*[[Sea Slide Galaxy]]: Underwater Cosmic Mario Race: Mario and Cosmic Mario race from the starting area, past the Faster Than A Speeding Penguin zone, to the lighthouse. Strangely, Cosmic Mario exits the water, and because of that, he could slow down.

==Trivia==
*Cosmic Mario shares his voice with the [[Cosmic Clone]]s in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.
*Cosmic Mario/Luigi's theme, whenever Mario or Luigi is racing him, is a sped up, high-pitched and distorted version of the original ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' theme with a bassline of a sped-up and distorted version of the Underground Theme.
*The [[Gold Leaf Galaxy]] is the only Galaxy whose Cosmic Comet mission has Cosmic Mario's name at the start of the name. The rest have his name in the middle.
*Cosmic Mario is one of the few bosses that can cause instant death. He can do this by grabbing the [[Power Star]] before [[Mario]] himself can.
*Cosmic Mario is the only boss that does not have a defeat animation. Like the [[Spooky Speedster]], however, he does have a victory pose, where he poses tauntingly on the [[Power Star]] while Mario watches the scene.
*Cosmic Mario was originally going to be fought in the [[Space Junk Galaxy]], but that fight was scrapped and replaced with a Speedy Comet in the final game due to the [[Pull Star]] paths.
