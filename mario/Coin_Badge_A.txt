'''Coin Badge A''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Here, it can be bought from [[Fawful's Bean 'n' Badge]] at a price of 50 [[bean]]s. Coin Badge A functions similar to [[Coin Badge]], except it grants triple [[coin]]s to the bros. for any enemy they defeat using a [[Bros. Item]].
