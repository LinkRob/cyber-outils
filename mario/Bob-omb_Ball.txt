'''Bob-omb Ball''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
The Bob-omb Ball exclusively appears in the Bonus minigame [[Beach Volleyball (Mario Party 5)|Beach Volleyball]]. Here, it is one of three balls that can be used. A Bob-omb Ball is black and has a red LED display of a number that counts down from ten whenever it is hit, including during serves. When the Bob-omb Ball shows a zero, it explodes before the team can hit it, and the opposing team gains a [[point]]. Like with a regular volleyball, a point can also be scored if the Bob-omb Ball hits the sand.
