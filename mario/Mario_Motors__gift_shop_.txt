'''Mario Motors''' is a gift shop located at [[Super Nintendo World]] in Universal Studios Japan, based on the ''Mario Kart'' sponsor of the same name, It mostly sells merchandise themed after the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. The music played in the store includes arrangements of the circuit themes from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. and [[Moo Moo Meadows]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.
