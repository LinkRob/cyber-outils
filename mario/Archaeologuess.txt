'''Archaeologuess''' is a story mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. It can only be played and unlocked upon completing [[Shy Guy's Jungle Jam]].

==Gameplay==
The two characters must guess which of the spinning shapes is different from the others. Some shapes are easy to tell from the beginning while others are not. If players are taking a little long to find the odd-one-out, the shapes will slow down making identifying the one that is different easier. Selecting the incorrect choice results in the shape crushing that player stunning them for a few seconds. The player that gets the answers right three times wins.
