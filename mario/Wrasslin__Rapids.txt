'''Wrasslin' Rapids''' is a Battle [[minigame|mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
There are four cranes, each lowering a character onto the wooden raft.

==Gameplay==
Each character has to try and knock their opponents off of the wooden raft and into the river it is headed down. Aside from moving around, the characters can [[jump]], [[punch]], or even [[kick]] while they are in the air. If a character is jumped onto, they become squished temporarily, during which they cannot jump as high. If a character is knocked off the raft, they are eliminated from the mini-game.

After the raft passes through a gate, a few obstacles begin to appear, including falling nuts and [[Shy Guy]]s on a train who each use a [[cannon]] to shoot onto the raft. If the character is hit by an obstacle, they become stunned temporarily.

The winner is determined either by who is last character remaining, or whoever is remaining when the raft comes to a standstill.

==Ending==
At the start of the river, the winners and losers perform their victory and losing animations, respectively.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Jump
* {{button|gcn|B}}: Punch
* {{button|gcn|B}} (in midair): [[Kick]]

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Push your opponents off the raft as it floats down the river. The last player standing wins!''
* '''Advice''': ''Beware of falling nuts and incoming cannon fire!''
