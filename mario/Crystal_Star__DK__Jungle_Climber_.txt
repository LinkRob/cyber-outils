'''Crystal Stars''' are a type of Power-Up found in ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]''. They can be obtained every time [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] obtain 100 [[Gem (DK: Jungle Climber)|Gems]]. When activated, Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong become a glowing rainbow (similar to [[Invincible Mario]]) and can also fly in any direction (up, down, left, or right) for up to 10 seconds. This can be useful for crossing dangerous chasms, reaching higher ledges, or for defeating bosses or larger foes. While under the effects of a Crystal Star, enemies and other dangerous hazards are unable to hurt you and you are able to open doors and solve puzzles much quicker. Additional Crystal Stars can be earned by obtaining another 100 Gems. Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong can possess up to 3 Crystal Stars at a time, if they max out the Gem counter after that, it will remain at 99 until they use one of their Crystal Stars. Once 25 [[DK Coin]]s are obtained, the maximum amount of Crystal Stars that can be held can be increased to 6.
