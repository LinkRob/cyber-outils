|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''The Great Goal Pole''' is the fourth level in [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]] of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
You will start with 100 seconds of time. Near the starting area, you will see a flag pole with no enemies to accompany it. But when you try to jump towards it, it will gain wings, and fly away towards an obstacle course of various types of obstacles blocking the way. If the flag is not reached in time, it will go onto an endless trail of two floors of donut blocks, where you should be able to catch it, as it's speed decreases.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': The first Green Star appears after the first boost pad on the right. Make sure to turn in time, or you'll fall off the clouds.
*'''Green Star 2''': After you grab the stamp, go along the level near where the dropping platforms move away from the clouds. On the end is the second Star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Where the level ends, or where the Goal Pole slows down a bit, go to the right hand side, and find the dropping platforms and climb up them. You should find the Green Star on top of these.

==Stamp==
[[File:The_Great_Goal_Pole_Stamp.png|thumb]]After the second boost pad, or the spot with four dropping platforms, find the Walleye and go behind him. The stamp should be there.
