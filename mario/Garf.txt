'''Garf''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]''.

==History==
Garf is a [[Craw]] who is a member of [[Ishnail]]'s [[Robbo Gang]] and is the brother of [[Gus]]. At the beginning of ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', both were beaten up by two of [[Don Pianta]]'s henchmen. After the first chapter, Garf puts up a trouble in the [[Trouble Center]], asking for someone to find the [[House Key (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|key]] to his house. If [[Mario]] takes the trouble and completes it, Garf will allow Mario to go into his house, but Garf will return to Ishnail's hideout.
