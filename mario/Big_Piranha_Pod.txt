'''Big Piranha Pods''' are larger variations of [[Piranha Pod]]s. They are similarly thrown down by [[Lakitu]]s, hence their name, and bloom into [[Big Piranha Plant]]s instead of regular [[Piranha Plant]]s upon landing on the ground. They first appear in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Superstars''==
Big Piranha Pods appear on the board [[Peach's Birthday Cake]] whenever players land on the [[Happening Space]]s, [[Lakitu]] will show up and give the player two options, if they pick the larger option for 30 [[Coin]]s, Lakitu will toss a Big Piranha Pod in the icing which will bloom into a [[Super Piranha Plant]], these colossal [[Piranha Plant]]s will steal a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] from any characters who land on the Happening Spaces by them. If a player is to land on their own Happening Space, after deploying a [[Piranha Plant]], he/she will be rewarded 10 [[Coin]]s and be given the option to upgrade to a [[Super Piranha Plant]] for 30 [[Coin]]s.

==Trivia==
*Despite the Big Piranha Pods having spikes, the Big Piranha Plants that bloom from them lack any spikes.
