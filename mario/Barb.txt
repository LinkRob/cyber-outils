'''Barb''' is a [[human]] tennis player featured in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. She is the first opponent faced by [[Clay]] or [[Ace]] in the [[Island Open]] tournament. Barb's partner in doubles is [[Oscar]]. Her [[Offensive Power Shot]] is the [[High-Power Shot]] while her [[Defensive Power Shot]] is the [[Bubble Save]].
