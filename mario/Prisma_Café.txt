{{Infobox Location|name=Prisma Café|games=''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' (2016)|owner=Ruddy Road Café [[Toad]]|image=Prisma Café Logo.png|inhabitants=|caption=The logo seen above the Prisma Café in Fort Cobalt|located=Blue District in [[Port Prisma]], [[Ruddy Road]], [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Fort Cobalt]], and the [[Roshambo Temple]]s.|visitors=Red [[Toads]], [[Shy Guys]], and [[Slurp Guy]]s}}'''Prisma Café''' is a location seen in various levels in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. Other individual locations are seen at [[Ruddy Road]], [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Fort Cobalt]], and the [[Roshambo Temple|Roshambo Temples]]. The owners of these establishments are mostly Toads of red and yellow color. [[Toad (character)|Toads]], [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]], and [[Slurp Guy|Slurp Guys]] appear as customers of these establishments. All of these cafés offer two items, [[Hearty Latte]] and [[Prisma Juice]]. Some locations, however, offer different items unique to their location.

== Description ==
Prisma Café's have a sign depicting a [[Toad (character)|Toad]], a coffee cup, a fork, and a knife in the [[Ruddy Road]] and [[Fort Cobalt]] locations. Other locations may include other items such as a toad with sunglasses with a coconut drink at [[Bloo Bay Beach]], or a simple awning at [[Port Prisma]] and the [[Roshambo Temple|Roshambo Temples]].

== In ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' ==

=== Port Prisma ===
The Prisma Café in [[Port Prisma]] is a well established and popular café that is held in the blue district of [[Port Prisma]]. After talking to the bridge builder in [[The Crimson Tower]] and returning to the ''Blue District'' in Port Prisma, a Prisma Café will be available when you uncolor the business owner. The business owner, unsatisfied by the mess of his café, wishes for a simply life where it isn't too busy. Returning to the Prisma Café in [[Ruddy Road]] and speaking with the owner will unlock a cutscene. This shows the two owners agreeing to switch locations in favor of each others stance in life. After this cutscene is complete, returning back to Port Prisma will make the café accessable, selling you a [[Hearty Latte]] and [[Prisma Juice]].

[[File:PMCS Prisma Cafe menu.png|frameless|420x420px]]

=== Ruddy Road ===
The Prisma Café in [[Ruddy Road]] is a more ruined and messed up café. This is because a bunch of [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] rolled up the road. After the cutscene with the Port Prisma and Ruddy Road café owners switching locations, the Ruddy Road café becomes accessable. This café sells a [[Hearty Latte]] and [[Prisma Juice]].

[[File:PMCS Ruddy Road Prisma Cafe Menu.png|frameless|420x420px]]

=== Bloo Bay Beach ===
The Prisma Café in [[Bloo Bay Beach]] is an unaccessable Prisma Café located in the Oceanfest area. This café is meant to hide one of the five fun guys during the Toad and Go Seek event in the Oceanfest, and doesn't sell anything to Mario. He claims to have made a lot of money after the Oceanfest, implying he sells items to other visitors on the island.

[[File:PMCS Bloo Bay Beach Prisma Cafe Menu.png|frameless|420x420px]]

=== Fort Cobalt ===
The Prisma Café in [[Fort Cobalt]] is an accessable prisma café just outside the [[Shy Guy]] training area. This café sells a special assortment of items. These items are a [[Hearty Latte]], [[Prisma Juice]], the [[Magma Burger]] special, and a unknown Special item worth 100,000 coins. The special item, not actually costing anything, contains dialogue with a hint at the item needed for the [[Ludwig von Koopa|Ludwig van Koopa]] fight. This item is revealed to be the [[Tail]] battle card, which helps deflect the fireballs fired from Ludwig's battleship.

[[File:PMCS Fort Cobalt Prisma Cafe Menu.png|frameless|420x420px]]

=== Roshambo Temples ===
The Prisma Cafés in the [[Roshambo Temple|Roshambo Temples]] are similar to the Prisma Café in Port Prisma, only selling a [[Hearty Latte]] and [[Prisma Juice]]. These 8 café owners, however, are revealed to be the 8 Rock Paper Wizards in their respective location. After defeating all of them and receiving the letter from the Rock Paper Wizards, a hat is seen inside each Prisma Café in all 8 locations, with them now asking who the Rock Paper Wizard could possibly be.

[[File:PMCS Roshambo Temple Prisma Cafe.png|frameless|420x420px]]
