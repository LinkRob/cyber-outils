'''Banana Bunches''' are collectible items in a few games of the {{franchise|Yoshi}}.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Story''===
Bananas appear as a bunch in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', but are only worth one fruit.

===''Yoshi Touch & Go''===
Banana Bunches also appear in ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]''. If a Yoshi eats one, it gets three more [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s.
