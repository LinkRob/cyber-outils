'''Goomdivers''' are a variant of [[Goomba]] which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They resemble a normal Goomba, but have a scuba diving helmet on their head, hence their name.

==''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' / ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''==
Goomdivers are found in the [[Oho Oasis]] and [[Gwarhar Lagoon]]. In the overworld, they drop down, stomping to target the Bros. In battle, they float around and tackle the Bros. who must counterattack with the hammer if the tackle is high, or by jumping if it is low. If the former is used, the Goomdivers keep floating and cannot be hit by ground-based moves except [[Chopper Bros.]] and the [[Fire Bros. (move)|element]]-[[Thunder Bros.|based]] [[Bros. Attack]]s.

It is not known if the Goomdivers are part of the Koopa Troop, or if they are "rogue Goombas".
