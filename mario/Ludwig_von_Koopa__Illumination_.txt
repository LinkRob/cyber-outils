'''Ludwig von Koopa''' is a character referenced in Illumination's ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' as a cameo. His name appears on [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]'s piano while singing Peaches.

== Trivia ==
* It is unknown if Ludwig formed a group organization, the [[Koopalings]] like his [[Ludwig von Koopa|game counterpart]] does.
* Though, Ludwig does not appear in the first movie physically, he’s the first character to debut by name in a [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|film series]] before the other six koopalings.
