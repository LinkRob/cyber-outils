'''Sloppy Salon''' is one of [[microgame]]s under [[Mona]]'s [[Strange]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''. It later returns as her microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player must repeatedly press {{button|gba|A}} to have the barber cut the hair off the customer. In doing so, the player wins at the microgame. The barber changes position every five presses. Following this, before the timer expires, a comical scene is shown in which the barber is laughing on the floor, while the customer has an angry face and two exclamation marks to the upper-left of his head. If the barber does not cut off all of the customer's hair on time, the player loses at the microgame.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The player must press 6 times to cut the customer's hair off.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The player must press 9 times to cut the customer's hair off.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The player must press 12 times to cut the customer's hair off.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
The microgame's objective is to match the hairstyle as the character expects. The player controls a mouse that is a barber.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The man's expected hairstyle is the same which requires the player to press 8-11 times.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' A woman's expected hairstyle is varied which requires the player to press 12-15 or 16-19 times.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' A gorilla's expected hairstyle is varied which requires the player to press 16-19 or 20-23 times.
