'''Enguarde the Swordfish''' is a swordfish being the most recurring of the [[Animal Friends]] on [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]] in underwater levels. Like [[Rambi]], he is capable of defeating enemies or even destroying secret doors with his sword.

Enguarde is a blue swordfish with a green/beige belly and black/blue iris.
==History==
===''Donkey Kong'' franchise===
====Canon====
In ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', Enguarde is first encountered with [[Donkey Kong|DK]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] in the [[Banana Hoard]] quest that was stolen by the [[Kremling Krew]]. He appears in all underwater levels to attack aquatic enemies. He can be defeated from behind, by [[Croctopus]]es, [[Clambo]]s and [[Mincer]]s.

In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''/''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'', Enguarde who took Diddy and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] to [[Crocodile Isle]] to save DK who was kidnapped by [[King K. Rool|Kaptain K. Rool]].<ref>"'''Diddy Kong''': ''No, we must go now. Enguarde will take us.''" - ''Donkey Kong Country 2'' (GBA)</ref> The attacks to defeat enemies are the same, but he has a way to destroy the secret doors which is the Superstab. He can be easily defeated by hot water, [[Snapjaw]] and [[Puftup]]'s spikes.

In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'', Enguarde is encountered by Dixie and [[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] on their journey to look for DK & Diddy. He is defeated by the [[Lurchin]]s when they close in and even fought a Lurchin named [[Barbos]].

In ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'', he's only found in the five stages regarding water.

In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'', Enguarde is only accessed by [[Lanky Kong|Lanky]] when he touches his [[Animal Crate|crate]]. He appears in the [[Gloomy Galleon]] where he has the ability to destroy [[Treasure Chest]]s.

In ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'', Enguarde's aspect appears on the billboards in [[New Donk City]] written '''Enguarde Eatery'''. There is an extra-rare fossil found on [[Tempest Layer]] of Enguarde and a statue that costs 80.000 [[Emerald ore|emerald ore]] sold by [[Void Kong]] from ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza|Donkey Kong Bananza:]] [[DK Island & Emerald Rush]]'' during the Chip Fever event. 

====Spin-off====
In ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]'', Enguarde appears in the circuits called [[Salty Sea]] & [[Open Ocean]] where his crate is seen. When a player break his crate, Enguarde is mounted and has the ability to take down aquatic enemies in front.

===Other games===
In [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], Enguarde appears in [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophies]] (''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl|Brawl]]'' & ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]''), [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|stickers]] (Brawl) and [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirits]] (''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Ultimate]]'').

In the [[Donkey Kong Adventure]] story of ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'', Enguarde's aspect appears on one of [[Rabbid Peach]]'s sentry.
