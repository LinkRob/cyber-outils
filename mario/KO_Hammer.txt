|name=KO Hammer (Battle Card)}}'''KO Hammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The KO Hammer is the sideways version of [[Mario]]'s Hammer attack. It is a yellow and blue hammer with a star in the center, accompanied with a yellow/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 7/28 coins

Sell Price: 4/14 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The KO Hammer is first seen in [[Cherry Lake]], but can also found in [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Marmalade Valley]], [[Kiwano Temple]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Mustard Café]], and [[Toad Trainworks]].
