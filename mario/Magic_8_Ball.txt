A '''Magic 8 Ball''' with a ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' theme was produced by [[Mattel]] in 2017.

The ball, shaped like a question mark block with an "8" at the top, gives answers in character phrases:

* Wowzer, outlook good
* Oh yeah
* No problem!
* Okie dokie!
* Signs say ya-haaa!
* It's-a certain
* Without a doubt!
* Signs point to yes
* As I see it yes
* Yeah! Yeah! Of course!
* Concentrate let's-a go
* Ask a bro again
* Sorry, I'm-a tired
* All right, let's-a see
* Ask again later
* No way, amateur!
* As I see it, tough luck!
* Gah! Give it up already!
* Don't count on it
* My sources say no
