'''Bowser's Ghost Cologne''' is an object which appears in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''.

==Description==
Bowser's Ghost Cologne is contained in a light-pink spray can, and the lid is a yellow spray. Both the brand name and its tagline are shown in hot pink text on front of the bottle. The tagline in particular is "Keeps magic spells away!". Similar to [[Bowser's Sourpuss Bread]], the name suggests that Bowser's Ghost Cologne was made by [[Bowser]] or the [[Koopa Troop]].

Bowser's Ghost Cologne is not used like regular cologne, because at one point, when [[Mario]] is in [[Ludwig's Thump Castle Hotel]], he sprays himself with Bowser's Ghost Cologne to have the doors stop vanishing.
