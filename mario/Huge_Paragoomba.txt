'''Huge Paragoombas''' (also known as '''Grand Paragoombas'''  or '''Giant Land Paragoombas''' in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''<ref>"''Check 'em out, Cheatsy! These Giant Land Para-Goombas will mangle those crops, and when Princess Toadstool's people are starving, I won't sell her any food unless she gives me her kingdom. *throaty chuckle*''" - King Koopa, ''Princess Toadstool for President''</ref>) are enormous [[Paragoomba]]s that are essentially [[Grand Goomba]]s with wings.

==History==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:GrandParagoombas.jpg|thumb|left|Para-Goombas in ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3'' episode "[[Princess Toadstool for President]]".]]
Large-sized Para-Goombas are shown once in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Princess Toadstool for President]]". [[Bowser|King Koopa]] and [[Larry|Cheatsy]] bring a cage full of Giant Land Paragoombas to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], whom they unleash on a field of crops. However, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] show up and defeat the Giant Land Paragoombas, stomping them as if they were standard Paragoombas. They are named after [[Giant Land]], and due to this and being imported, can be inferred to have come from there. Despite [[Grand Goomba]]s and [[Colossal Koopa Paratroopas]] appearing in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', large Paragoombas do not appear, leaving Giant Land Paragoombas to be an invention of the show until their official debut in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.

===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Galaxy 2''====
[[File:SMG2 Supermassive Giant Star Chips.png|thumb|Luigi near a [[Giant Luma]] and a Huge Paragoomba in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.]]
Huge Paragoombas make their game debut in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Super Mario Galaxy 2 (Nintendo Switch)|port]], in this game, they appear exclusively in the [[Supermassive Galaxy]] primarily appearing on the Galaxy’s third planet. Huge Paragoombas are unable to be stomped by standard means due to their size but [[Star Spin]]ning a Huge Paragoomba will knock loose it’s wings turning it into a [[Grand Goomba]], then [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] can kick the vulnerable [[Grand Goomba]] to defeat it. Upon defeat, a Huge Paragoomba will leave behind several [[Star Bit]]s.

====''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Super Mario Maker 2''====
Huge Paragoombas return in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', in this game, they can be made by either enlarging a [[Paragoomba]]s with a [[Super Mushroom]], or by applying wings to a [[Grand Goomba]]. A Huge Paragoomba behaves similarly to a regular Paragoomba but it’s larger and therefore harder to avoid, a Huge Paragoomba can be beaten with a stomp or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Star]]. ''Super Mario Maker'' marks the debut of large-sized Paragoombas in a 2D ''Super Mario'' platformer.

===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
[[File:PDSMBE-BigParagoomba-TeamImage.png|thumb|Team image in ''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition'']]
Huge Paragoombas appear in the RPG/puzzle hybrid ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]'' as stronger variants of Paragoomba. They are of [[Super Leaf|Wood]] and [[Star|Light]] types. A Huge Paragoomba's maximum level is 99, and its HP and RCV ratings are higher than those of a normal Paragoomba, although it has the same Attack stat. Its skill, "Rush", deals 50 Wood damage to all foes, and its helper ability is "Attack Boost", which raises the attack of the player's team members.
