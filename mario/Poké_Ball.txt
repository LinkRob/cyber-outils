'''Poké Ball''' is an item in the ''Pokémon'' franchise. Its only connection to the {{franchise|Mario}} is its appearance in every installment of the crossover {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. 

Poké Balls are used to store a Pokémon, although each Poké Ball is only capable of storing one at a time. Various types of [[Pokémon]] can appear from Poké Balls, including [[Pikachu]], [[Jigglypuff]], [[Pichu]], [[Mewtwo]], [[Squirtle]], [[Ivysaur]], [[Charizard]], [[Lucario]], [[Greninja]], and [[Incineroar]].
