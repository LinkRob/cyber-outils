|[[File:MKWiiChainchomp.png|center|thumb]]
|Chain Chomp
|N/A
|While [[Chain Chomp|Chain Chomps]] do appear in the game as obstacles (such as Mario Circuit or [[Chain Chomp Wheel]]), there is an icon for the Chain Chomp item as seen in ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!''. Next to the mouth is the '''kanji''' ('''仮'''), which translates to "'''temporary'''". This Chain Chomp icon can be seen in an early prototype of ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' without the '''仮''' symbol, but it was replaced by the Bullet Bill in the final game.
|-
|[[File:MKW Boo NintendoPower.png|center|thumb]]
|Boo
|N/A
|In Volume 227 of the Nintendo Power Magazine, Page 42 mentions that a Boo item was among the items to be included in the game. The only trace of anything resembling it in the game files is the lack of any behavior for item '''0x13'''. Normally, if the game tries to execute a behavior of an item that doesn't exist, it just gives a mushroom. But the fact that it does nothing implies that an item was present at slot '''0x13''' at some point during development to then subsequently be stripped from the game altogether.

|[[File:MKWii-BodyHoneycoupe.png|center|thumb|Vehicle's body (Texture)]][[File:MKWii Wario Honeycoupe Body Texture Mapping.png|center|thumb|Vehicle's body (Texture Mapping)]]
|N/A
|The bottom right area of the texture for the [[Honeycoupe]] has a [[Chain Chomp]] with a Prohibition symbol on top of it, along with half of a Chain Chomp eyeball. Southwest of the "W" emblem seems to be a Chain Chomp mouth, but with the red part of the mouth removed, and to the right of that are Chain Chomps teeth.


These are never used in game because of the texture mapping and these oddities are in every character's version of the Honeycoupe. It's could be possible that it can either relate to the scrapped Chain Chomp item or that the Honeycoupe was originally going to be a Chain Chomp version of the Piranha Prowler.

|[[File:MKWii Unused A Graphic.png|center|thumb]]
|tt_a_body.tpl
tt_a_light.tpl
|An image of the letter "A" ('''tt_a_body.tpl'''). It's likely the purpose for it was meant to alert players to press the A button to register their controller. This graphic is actually used in the Controller Registration screen in the Local Multiplayer mode where it appears in the squared frame when a controller is not assigned to a given player. However, it is invisible in-game since it uses the '''RGB5A3''' image format (the image here that goes unused was converted to the '''RGB565''' format so that it could be seen for that specific player). This texture also has a white outline variant, just like the other controllers ('''tt_a_light.tpl''') and is also invisible. However, even so, this texture seems to be completely blank.

|[[File:MKWii Question Mark Controller Icon.png|center|thumb]][[File:MKWii Question Mark Controller Icon White.png|center|thumb]]
|tt_hatena_body.tpl
tt_hatena_light.tpl
|An image with a question mark on it ('''tt_hatena_body.tpl'''). While the purpose of this graphic is exactly unknown, it's likely that it could have been used when the player tried to register an unsupported controller. In the final game, if this happens, the game shows the GameCube controller image instead. Interestingly, a similarly named animation file ('''common_w055_message_connect_hatena.brlan''') contains references to the invisible A button graphic, which could mean it was originally meant to appear in the Controller Registration screen in the Local Multiplayer mode. This texture has a white outline variant, just like the other controllers ('''tt_hatena_light.tpl''').
