}}A '''Big Sammer Guy''' is a type of [[Sammer Guy]] that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Big Sammer Guys are only found in [[Sammer's Kingdom]] and they are often the hardest to take out of the Sammer Guys. They usually have more HP, attack, and defense than the smaller Sammer Guys. Big Sammer Guys mainly attack by swinging their clubs but may also use a rolling attack for speed. End Boss is the final Big Sammer Guy (and Sammer Guy overall) that is fought in the game.
