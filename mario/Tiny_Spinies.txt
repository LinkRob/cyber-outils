The {{Nihongo|'''Tiny Spinies'''|ザ・トゲトゲン|Za Togetogen}} is one of the many teams in the [[Glitz Pit]]. They appear only in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== History ==
The Tiny Spinies are a group of [[Red Spike Buzzy|Red Spike Buzzies]], a stronger variant of [[Spike Top]] that are obviously colored red. As the name might suggest, these enemies are '''not''' [[Spiny|Spinies]]. The group is made up of [[Spiky Joe]] and another Red Spike Buzzy. This team is ranked 8th in the tournament, in being so, are in the major league making them one of the stronger teams of the Glitz Pit. Despite their high defense, they can be taken out rather easily with an [[Earth Tremor]] or [[Yoshi Kid]]’s [[Gulp]] ability.
