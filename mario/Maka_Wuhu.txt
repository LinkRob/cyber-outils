'''Maka Wuhu''' (known as '''Wuhu Mountain Loop''' in Europe and Oceania) is the last course in the [[Star Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. It takes place in [[Wuhu Island]], where drivers have to race around Maka Wuhu (the volcano in the middle of the island) and even inside it.

Like [[Wuhu Loop]] and [[Rainbow Road]] in this game, this track does not go by the lap rules, but rather is split into three parts. The first one to reach the finish line (at the end-point, considering this course doesn't have laps) wins first.

A glitch was discovered in this course - immediately after entering Section 2 and before the cave, use a Mushroom or another way to boost onto a nearby rock. If done correctly, you will respawn right before the start of the third section. The glitch was patched for online mode on May 15, but the glitch still exists during Solo play, including Time Trials. The patch also fixed glitches for [[Wuhu Loop]] and [[GBA Bowser Castle 1]] for online play.
