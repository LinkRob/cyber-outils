'''Tokyo Blur 1''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. Hence the prefix, it is the first layout of [[Tokyo Blur]]. Tokyo Blur 1 made its debut during the Tokyo Tour, which took place in October 2019 from the ninth to the twenty-second.

== Layout ==
The racers on Tokyo Blur 1 start on the Tōkyō Kō Renkakukyō, which is placed on the south side of the city, facing west. After the [[Lakitu (referee)|referee]] says "Go!", they continue west and turn right before hitting the southwest corner of the map, heading north a short distance before meeting a three-way intersection, at which point they turn left slightly, heading northwest for a bit until they hit the northwest corner of the map; the road makes a 180-degree right turn so the racers head back southeast to the city, passing under another three-way intersection (which is used in Tokyo Blurs [[Tokyo Blur 2|2]] and [[Tokyo Blur 3|3]]). After the racers pass this area, they make a slight left turn, heading due east and going straight at a third intersection, then make a right turn and head due south. They then make another right turn and head west to get back to the starting line on the Tōkyō Kō Renkakukyō.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', this route is featured as the first lap of [[Tokyo Blur]]; on the second lap, the racers switch to [[Tokyo Blur 2]].
