This article has a list of glitches in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Freezes and Softlocks ==

* Bridge Tumble Softlock: If [[Luigi]] falls off the bridge just before his encounter with the [[Piranha Bean]], the plant will eat the bean, but the game will enter a [[Softlock]] until the player resets.
* Burning Boss Freeze:
* Disappearing Sprites Glitch: This Glitch will cause all sprites to vanish from the screen.  The player must have access to the advanced [[Cyclone Bros.]] When the Bros. find an enemy, they must fight it. They should use Cyclone Bros. to make one of the enemies dizzy, then defeat the enemy right after. This causes all sprites to disappear. The music will still play, but the player cannot do anything other than resetting the game.
* Slow Cackletta Music Fails To Stop: [[Cackletta]] has to be at low health during her first phase in the final battle for this glitch to work. When she goes dark and summons stars to crush the Bros. and the player throws enough back to defeat her, but is also killed, they will see a mixed scene of Game Over and Cackletta eating the Bros. This segues into a black screen with Cackletta's slow music being looped, causing a [[Softlock]] that can be stopped by resetting the game.
* Thunderhand Freeze: After defeating Bowletta, walking off of the stairs normally will trigger a cutscene with Prince Peasely. However, if the player uses the Thunderhand to stun Mario while walking off of the last step, the cutscene will not trigger and the game will be softlocked.

== Graphical Glitches ==
These are aesthetic glitches that have no definite impact on gameplay.

* Dizzy Bowletta Glitch: [[Cackletta]] will be stuck in her dizzy animation if Mario and Luigi drain the rest of her health with their Cyclone Bros. move. However, she will still inhale the Mario Bros. to progress the battle into the next phase. The Japanese version does not have this Glitch compatible for when Cackletta is in a shadowy form.
* Discolored Ultra Hammer: If [[Popple]] strikes his target with his steal attack in Teehee Valley and takes the victim's Ultra Hammer, the hammer will have a silver colour. Ultra Hammers are usually gold.
* The Invisible Jojora: [[Jojora]] will still have her dialogue box calling the Bros. out for hurting her friend if they both get hit by a Bounce Bros. attack (or any other move that strikes multiple targets) and Jojora dies as a result.
* Luigi Speaks Through Mario's Mouth: This happens when Luigi pulls on the [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plants]] on the [[Beanbean International Airport]]'s runway as his brother explains something to [[Lady Lima]]. Mario has his voice swapped for Luigi's.
* Mario Flying Glitch: Right after he and Luigi get their hammers, Mario should be made the leader. Mario should ingest water and get his head hammered until his water supply runs out. This causes him to fly into the air during his angry animation.
* Shadowless Mario: In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] version, Mario can sometimes lose his shadow during [[Harhall]]'s Splart minigame.
* Wrong Defense Action Icons:  After Cackletta finishes throwing fireballs and spawning [[Flaret]]s, hammer defense icons will appear briefly.

== Physics-related glitches==
* Bottomless Golden Mushroom Supply
* Control Input Display Fail In Seabed Pipes: The control input required to climb the Yellow Warp Pipes from the seabed may not properly register when the player uses an emulator to play.
* Double Glurp: This only works in the Nintendo 3DS version. In battle with two [[Glurp|Glurps]] and a single [[Scaratroopea]], if the Scaratroopea loses their wings and both Glurps attempt to clone, one Glurp will be forced to share the same space as the clone that it created.
* Endless [[POW-Up|Attack]]/[[DEF-Up|Defenses]] Boost: If the Bros. eat a red or green pepper and then die and then are revived, they keep the boosts offered by the peppers for the rest of the fight.
* Fast Luigi Dunk Movement: Mario and Luigi usually go slow with the [[Dunk Hammer]], but their movement speed can be increased to normal speed when talking to the Starbeans Café's owner and making different drinks. Should one trigger a cutscene where Luigi is involved by triggered, he will run back and forth with various side effects; sometimes he appears above ground, and sometimes he stays below the ground. After the cutscene, Luigi will return to his position in the ground, and the player can walk around at normal speed with him that way. The glitch stops when Luigi exits the ground.
* Firebrand Run Off: Trying to use the [[Firebrand]] Bros. move pointing towards the statue at the north of said area before meeting [[Popple]] while at the [[Winkle]] segment of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] may cause [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to run further instead of walking to Popple and said statue.

== Scripting Glitches ==

* 5000 Damage Glitch: Bosses actually have 5000 plus their given health, to prevent defeat before their cutscene plays out. If the foe is dealt more than 5000 damage, this can result in a glitched victory screen when the enemy finally dies. This may lead to freezing, invisible monsters, or simply a glitched cutscene depending on the defeated enemy.

== Sound Glitches ==

* Wrong Music: Mario has to get hit on the head by Luigi's hammer to access his small form and walk into an area with a different song. This can cause the music transition to fail.
