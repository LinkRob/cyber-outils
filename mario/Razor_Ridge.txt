== '''World Overview''' ==
'''Razor Ridge''', (named カミソリ渓谷 '''Kamisori Keikoku''' in Japan), is the sixth world in [[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]] on which [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] and [[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] venture throughout. This area is located in [[Northern Kremisphere]]

This area appears to take place at a lake with high cliffs found around the area. There also appears to be large trees and deep caves around Razor Ridge. The water in the area also appear to be a pitch black, possibly because of shadows or an oil spill caused by the [[Kremling]]s. There is also a [[Banana Bird Cave]] that can be accessed via [[Björn]]'s chairlifts. However, he needs a [[No. 6 Wrench]] as his chairlifts are not working properly.

Two [[Brothers Bear]]<span>s,</span> [[Benny]] and [[Björn]], run chairlifts to allow Dixie and Kiddy to transport from one area to another. [[Barbos]] is this world's boss. He was later relocated to [[Pacifica]] and replaced by [[Kroctopus]] in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake.
