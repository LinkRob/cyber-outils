The '''Blue Seven''' is a [[kart]] that appears in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart series]]''.

== Description ==
It resembles a 1960's prototype racecar, with a "7" printed on the gold-yellow spoiler. Its design is near-identical to [[Luigi]]'s [[Streamliner]] and the [[Jetsetter]] from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' respectively. It also shares similarities to the [[Mach 8]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
=== In ''Mario Kart 7'' ===
[[File:MK7 Blue Seven.png|thumb|180px|right|The Blue Seven as it appears in ''Mario Kart 7''.]]
The Blue Seven debuted in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', as a unlockable kart body. It offers a boost in [[speed]] and [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]], whilst lowering [[acceleration]] and [[handling]]. In terms of hidden stats, it also lowers air speed, [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] and [[drift]].

The Blue Seven kart body is particularly favoured by the CPU-controlled [[Mario]], Luigi, and [[Princess Peach]] during races.

In [[Time Trial]]s, the Expert [[Staff Ghost]] in [[Neo Bowser City]] uses [[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]] in the Blue Seven, equipped with Monster tires and the Paraglider.

=== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
[[File:MKT Blue Seven.jpg|thumb|150px|[[Toad (Pit Crew)]] using the Blue Seven in ''Mario Kart Tour''.]]
The Blue Seven appears as a kart in ''Mario Kart Tour'', where it is classified as Super kart. [[Rocket Start]]s will last longer and earn more points for the player if it is used. It is equipped with the [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires from ''Mario Kart 8'' and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''. The game also introduces a red-orange variant, which is called the [[Super 1]], which just like the Blue Seven, is classified as a Super kart. However, it is exclusive to the [[Mario Bros. Tour]].
