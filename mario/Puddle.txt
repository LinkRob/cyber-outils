'''Puddles''' are an obstacle in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''.

==Description==
They are small water puddles placed along the track in {{classic course|GBA|[[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|Luigi Circuit]]}} and [[Neo Bowser City]], although only in said course's ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' iterations. If a racer drives onto a puddle, they spin out.
