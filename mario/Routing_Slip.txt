The '''Routing Slip''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Routing Slip can only be obtained by taking on [[Mayor Dour]]'s [[Trouble Center|trouble]], "Newsletter...". As stated, [[Mario]] must first get the Routing Slip from Mayor Dour in [[Twilight Town]] and then take it to the [[Puni Elder]] at [[The Great Tree]]. Mario must then take the Routing Slip to [[Kroop]] in [[Petalburg]] and finally report back to Mayor Dour for 30 [[coin]]s as the reward.
