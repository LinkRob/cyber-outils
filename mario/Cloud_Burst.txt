'''Cloud Burst''' is a comic within the ''[[Super Mario Bros. (comic series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' series of comics within [[Valiant Comics|Valiant]]'s ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]''. It is specifically the fifth issue within ''[[Super Mario Bros. Vol 1]]''.

== Plot synopsis ==
[[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] interrogating [[Wooster]] about the whereabouts of her crown. After learning that Wooster does not know where the crown is, Princess Toadstool begins to panic, if she does not have that crown in time for the "re-coronation" ceremony, she will not be able to be princess for an entire year; meaning [[Mushroom Kingdom|Mushroomland]] will be completely under the control of the idiotic [[Mushroom King|King Toadstool]].

==Trivia==
* [[Bowser]] also loses his crown.
* In this comic, [[Lakitu]] is the main villain.
* We get a brief appearance of the Princess's dad, King Toadstool.
* Lakitu hoards [[coin]]s and treasure in his [[Lakitu's Cloud|cloud]].
* Lakitu steals [[Luigi]]'s clothes.
* Bowser accuses [[Mario]] of stealing his crown.
