'''Axem Green''' is a member of the [[Smithy Gang]]'s [[Axem Rangers]], a major antagonist and boss in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its upcoming [[Super Mario RPG|2023 remake]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Axem Green, along with the other Axem Rangers, managed to steal the [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]] from [[Mario]] at [[Barrel Volcano]]. Axem Green and the others tried to escape on their battleship, the [[Blade]], but Mario manages to catch up and the Axem Rangers engage Mario's team in a battle. Untimely, Axem Green and the other Axem Rangers are defeated and destroyed when the Blade crashes into Barrel Volcano.

===Battle===
In battle, Axem Green is capable of using more magical moves than anyone on the Axem Rangers. This also makes his magical attack and defense much higher than the others. However, Axem Green is one of the slowest members in the Axem Rangers with [[Axem Yellow]] being the slowest.

====Strategy====
Axem Green has a weakness to [[Ice (element)|Ice]] but [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] technique may not be useful as it attacks all targets on the screen. It is recommended to take down Axem Green with physical attacks; although, Mario's [[Super Jump]] is effective as well.
