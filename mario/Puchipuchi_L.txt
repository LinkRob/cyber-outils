A '''Puchipuchi L''' is a large variant of [[Milde]] encountered in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Behavior==
Aside from being larger, Puchipuchi Ls have little difference from regular Mildes, and even have the same pattern of simply marching straightforward. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can [[Ground Pound]] a Puchipuchi L to divide it into four Mildes.

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
They are encountered in a few levels, including the battle against [[Marching Milde]], who splits into two Puchipuchi Ls when Ground Pounded by [[Blue Yoshi]]. Like other Puchipuchi Ls, the ones created by splitting Marching Milde also divide into four Mildes upon being Ground Pounded.

==In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''==
Puchipuchi Ls make a reappearance, acting the same as before.
