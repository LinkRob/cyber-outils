The '''Sliding Punch''' is a move used by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==In the games==
Bowser learns the Sliding Punch shortly after [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] defeat the [[Durmite]] in his body. With the Sliding Punch, Bowser can slide forward, performing a charge with his right arm in front. With the Sliding Punch, Bowser can move across short gaps that he cannot break otherwise, and it also enables him to break certain large boulders.
