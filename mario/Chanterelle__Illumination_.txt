'''Chanterelle''' is a purple [[Toad (species)|Toad]]. She appeared in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Like other [[Toad's species (Illumination)|Toad species]], Chanterelle is a purple Toad.

== Biography ==
Chanterelle is a resident in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and is in good terms with [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]], who's taking [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] to see [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]].

== Etymology ==
Chanterelle is a name of a yellow fungi. They are known to be one of the most edible mushrooms.
