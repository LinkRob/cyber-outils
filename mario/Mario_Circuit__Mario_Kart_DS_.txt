'''Mario Circuit''' is the third track of the [[Star Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

== Layout ==
Racers start by a lake near where [[Princess Peach's Castle]] lies. Racers will then take a right turn, and race down a lane piled with [[Goomba]]s who will try to block the racers and will make racers spin out if they come in contact. Note that there is not any fences around the lake, so racers can easily fall off. After taking a couple more turns, racers will run into two [[Venus Fire Trap]]s on a S bend turn. After, racers will go through a a bridge modeled after a green [[Warp Pipe]], take a tight turn and then enter the dirt road after taking another tight turn. Then, racers will encounter [[Piranha Plant]]s on a curly turn. The racers will also be able to see Princess Peach's Castle, unfortunately, racers cannot drive in the castle. After exiting the dirt road, racers will take one final S turn, and finish a lap, or the race.

== Staff Ghost ==
The staff ghost for this course uses [[Mario]] in the [[Shooting Star (Mario Kart DS)|Shooting Star]], with a time of 1:56:533.

==Missions==
In [[Mission Mode]], missions 5-8 and 7-4 take place on Mario Circuit. In Mission 5-8, [[Luigi]] must win one lap against Mario. In Mission 7-4, [[Toad (character)|Toad]] must defeat 30 [[Goomba]]s with [[Green Shell]]s within the [[time limit]].

== Trivia ==
*This course is one of the two Mario Circuit tracks in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]] that is in the Star Cup, the first being [[Mario Circuit 4]] from ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''.
