'''Cat Fuzzies''' are the [[Lake Lapcat]] variant of [[Fuzzy]] included in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. Like several other cat variants of enemies, Cat Fuzzies resemble their base counterpart, except with added ears and a tail.

==History==
Cat Fuzzies appear on [[Risky Whisker Island]]. They are the focus of the [[mission]] "Feasting on Fuzzies", in which [[Mario]] has to pick up a [[Cat Potted Piranha Plant]], carry it around the [[lighthouse]], and have it eat every Cat Fuzzy along the way. Upon completing the task, Mario is awarded a [[Cat Shine]].
