A '''Mini Battle''' is a type of minigame featured in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.

==Description==
Mini Battles are fought in certain [[level]]s after a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] gets a [[key]] to the [[Bonus Game Shack]] where it takes place. A Mini Battle involves a Yoshi competing against a [[Bandit]] in either one of four battles:

*[[Gather Coins]], in which Yoshi and Bandit compete to collect the most [[coin]]s.
*[[Popping Balloons]], in which Yoshi and Bandit compete to pop the [[Balloon (Mini Battle)|balloon]] containing either a [[10-Point Star]] or a [[20-Point Star]].
*[[Throwing Balloons]], in which Yoshi and Bandit input a series of buttons to toss a balloon back and forth, and they must avoid having the balloon pop on them.
*[[Watermelon Seed Spitting Contest]], in which Yoshi and Bandit each spit [[watermelon]] seeds at one another until one of them loses all of their health.

The Level Selection screen features a hidden Mini Battles menu from which the player can select any Mini Battle to replay. To access it, they must hold {{button|snes|select}} and then input {{button|snes|X}}, {{button|snes|X}}, {{button|snes|Y}}, {{button|snes|B}} and lastly {{button|snes|A}}. In ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'', the input combination was changed due to a [[Game Boy Advance]] featuring different controls than a [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]; just as before, {{button|gba|Select}} has to be held, but the player now has to input {{button|gba|L}}, {{button|gba|L}}, {{button|gba|B}}, {{button|gba|A}} and lastly {{button|gba|R}} for the Mini Battles menu to show up. This code is revealed by a certain [[Message Block]] in [[Sluggy The Unshaven's Fort]].

The Mini Battles menu allows any of the four Mini Battles to be replayed. Aside from this, there is a two-player version of Watermelon Seed Spitting Contest as well as a four-button variation of Throwing Balloons mode (playable in either single-player or multiplayer) alongside the usual six-button variation. The two-player Watermelon Seed Spitting Contest was later removed from ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'', although it adds a five-button variation of Throwing Balloons.
