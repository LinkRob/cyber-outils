'''Dive''' is a move performed by many characters. It was first used in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. It also appeared in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

== In ''Super Mario 64'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' ==
Dives can be preformed in Super Mario 64 by pressing B while jumping. In Super Mario 64 DS, it can also be preformed by pressing B, although you cannot dive as [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].{{stub}}
