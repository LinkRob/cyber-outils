'''Picture This''' is a 4-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.
==Opening==
The camera focuses on a character who is standing behind a table with a notepad. The camera switches its focus to a bird-eye view as the notepad opens itself.

==Gameplay==
The player's objective is to be the first player to clear three rounds. This can be done by placing the bookmark on the correct page as shown on the centre of the screen. The first player to place the bookmark on the said page gains a star.

==Ending==
The screen black outs to transition to the winning player posing her/his victory.

== In-game description ==
* ''"Turn the picture book pages to find the matching image. The first player to 3 images wins!"''
=== Controls ===
* {{button|gcn|l}} - Turn pages forward
* {{button|gcn|r}} - Turn pages backwards
* {{button|gcn|a}} - Select a page
