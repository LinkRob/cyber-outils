'''The Secret of Battle Fort''' is the sole mission of the secret course [[Battle Fort]] in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. The object is to collect five [[Silver Star]]s.

==Silver Star locations==
#Atop the pole near the Chuckya.
#At the bottom of the pole inside the Goomba, destroy the Goomba to get the Silver Star.
#Near the two bricks in the corner is a Goomba, defeat it and obtain the Silver Star it holds.
#Near the corner east of where the Star appears, down the slope by it is two bricks. The brick on the right contains the Silver Star. Break it to obtain your prize.
#In the east corner is a cluster of brick blocks, the one wedged in the corner contains the 5th Silver Star. Break it open to obtain the Silver Star.

Once all five Silver Stars have been collected, the Power Star appears above the [[Star Marker]] on the highest platform which can be reached by running up the ramps.
