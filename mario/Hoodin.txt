'''Hoodin''' is a minor character in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Hoodin is the owner of his shop, [[Catch a Dream]], which sells [[Catch Card]]s in [[Flipside]]. Hoodin is known for being calm as not even children touching and grabbing at his cards bothers him.

==Trivia==
*Hoodin has a [[Flopside]] counterpart in [[Boodin]] who also owns a Catch Card shop, [[Fondest Hopes]].
