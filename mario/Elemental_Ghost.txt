'''Elemental Ghosts''' are a species of [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
There are three types of Elemental Ghosts, named [[Fire Elemental Ghost]]s, [[Water Elemental Ghost]]s, and [[Ice Elemental Ghost]]s respectively, and each type is imbued with the element in its name. The Elemental Ghosts' appearance respectively matches the elemental powers used by them: a Fire Elemental Ghost resembles a small flame; a Water Elemental Ghost resembles a bright, blue water droplet; and an Ice Elemental Ghost resembles a piece of ice. All three types of Elemental Ghosts have a pair of yellow eyes.

===Behavior===
An Elemental Ghost can be summoned certain spaces once [[Luigi]] has acquired the corresponding [[Element Medal]] (e.g. Fire Elemental Ghosts require the [[Fire Element Medal]] to be obtained) and uses his [[Poltergust 3000]] to vacuum at the hiding spot of the Elemental Ghost. Upon being summoned, an Elemental Ghost slowly floats around in the air, and can damage [[Luigi]] upon contact with him.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Luigi can use his Poltergust 3000 to vacuum in an Elemental Ghost, converting it into a limited supply of its element for the Poltergust 3000, which can then discharge the element. For instance, if an Ice Elemental Ghost is vacuumed in, the Poltergust 3000 receives a limited supply of [[Ice (element)|ice]].

==Trivia==
*The Elemental Ghosts share resemblances to elemental creatures found throughout the main ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise such as the [[Podoboo]]s, [[Lava Bouncer]]s, [[Li'l Brrr]]s, and [[Li'l Cinder]]s with the only difference being the eye colors.
