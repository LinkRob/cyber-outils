The '''Golden Drink'''<ref name="SMPGameplay1">Nintendo. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tjpKtIGIqKc Super Mario Party Gameplay Pt. 1 - Nintendo Treehouse: Live | E3 2018] June 15, 2018. Retrieved September 8 2018.</ref> is an item in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Description ==
The Golden Drink is an aluminum can with a [[Coin]] icon engraved on it.

Its effects is similar to the [[Gold Flower]] which it changes the appearance of the character into her/his [[Gold Mario|gold version]]. When the player uses the item, he/she drinks the Golden Drink and their appearance becomes gold. The player receives the amount of coins for each space they move. They can't use it when Peppa is in the way.

== In-game description ==
* ''"Gain 1 Coin, each time you move. Can't use this while Peepa is in the way."''

==Trivia==
*[[Princess Peach|Peach]]'s alternate costume in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' references Peach's transformation with the Golden Drink.
