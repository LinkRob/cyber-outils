'''Jet Wario''' is a transformation of [[Wario]]'s which only appears in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. It essentially allows Wario to do a straightforward [[Dash Attack|Barge]] while in midair. A similar transformation, [[Eagle Wario]], makes an appearance in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.
