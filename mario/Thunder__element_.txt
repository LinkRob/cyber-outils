[[File:MallowThunderbolt.png|thumb|256px|Mallow's Thunderbolt. A known Thunder move.]]'''Thunder''' is a reoccurring element in the Mario RPG games. It is first shown in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Thunder can be used by certain characters and enemies and it will tend to paralyze [[Mario]] and others on contact.
== In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''==
In this game, [[Mallow]] is the only character in Mario's party to use Thunder-based moves. Other enemies in the game could also use Thunder-based moves which are mostly unblockable.
