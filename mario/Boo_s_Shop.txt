|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}'''Boo's Shop''', also known as '''Igor's Shop''', is a location that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
It is an [[Item Shop]] run by a [[Boo]] named [[Igor]] in the basement of [[Boo's Mansion]]. This shop is known for selling [[Life Shroom]]s and other expensive, but rare items such as the [[Stop Watch]]. It is also the only shop to sell the [[Mystery]] item. During the first arrival at Boo's Shop, Igor will not sell any items to [[Mario]] by orders of [[Lady Bow]]. However, once Lady Bow joins Mario's party, Igor allows him to purchase items at any time. Visiting this shop may also be required in order to complete one of [[Koopa Koot]]'s favors that requires the player to bring a Life Shroom to him.
