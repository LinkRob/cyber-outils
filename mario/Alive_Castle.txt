The '''Alive Castle''' is a villain in ''[[Mario Teaches Typing 2]]''.

== History ==
[[Mario]] finds a [[Magical Typewriter]] that is supposed to kill the castle witch is owned by [[Bowser]] although you have to not type badly on the typewriter for the spell to work. Mario then types badly on the typewriter causing it to explode into pieces. Mario than goes on a grand adventure to find the pieces of the Typewriter including even going inside the Castle. Mario ends up finding all the pieces and kills the Castle ending the game.
