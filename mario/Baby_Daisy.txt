'''Baby Daisy''' is a character who appears in some of the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' spinoff games. She is the infant counterpart of [[Princess Daisy]] who first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. Baby Daisy is one of the eight baby characters, the others being [[Baby Mario]], [[Baby Luigi]], [[Baby Peach]], [[Baby Rosalina]], [[Baby Wario]], [[Baby Waluigi]], and [[Baby Donkey Kong]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Baby Daisy dresses in her adult counterpart's traditional orange and yellow color scheme, but instead of wearing a long, flowing dress, she wears a poofy-sleeved shirt adorned with white petal-like frills at the neckline, a green flower brooch at the front lined with white petals, and orange layered frills lining the bottom. On her lower body, she wears light yellow bloomers, white tights, and orange [[Wikipedia:Mary Jane (shoe)|Mary Jane]] flatwear with light brown soles. She wears the same crown as Daisy, and in addition, she is equipped with a golden pacifier.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart Wii''===
Baby Daisy first appeared as a playable character in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' as an unlockable character. She can be unlocked by obtaining at least one Star Rank or better for all 50cc Wii Cups. She is used as the [[Staff Ghost]] character on [[Moo Moo Meadows]] by both a Normal Staff Ghost, '''Nin★YuNya,''' and Expert Staff, '''Nin★Tkym'''. Baby Daisy is also one of the fastest lightweights in the game along with [[Baby Luigi]].

===''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''===
Baby Daisy reappears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' as a default character in the roster.

=== ''Mario Kart World'' ===
Baby Daisy returns as a playable character in [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']]. She has 4 alternative outfits such as Touring, Pro Racer, Sailor, and Explorer.

==In ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''==
Baby Daisy makes her second playable appearance, being an unlockable character once more. Baby Daisy can be unlocked by having her join [[Mario]]'s team in Challenge mode at [[Peach Ice Gardens]].
