They first steal the [[Driftwood Jellyfish Sheets]] from Mario and the rest but Mario manages to come up with a plan to steal them back. Angry, the Fly Guy R Thieves then fight Mario and Luigi.

==Battle==
In battle, the Fly Guy R Thieves basically attack like regular Fly Guy Rs mainly by bringing in Goomba Rs to attack then hovering over Mario and Luigi.

==Trivia==
*Oddly, even though they are just Fly Guy Rs, they share the Boss theme song (Never Let Up!) for this game.
