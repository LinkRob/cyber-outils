'''Cleanser''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Cleanser is only available when [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] have to bake a [[Special Strawberry Cake|cake]] for [[Gourmet Guy]] after the events of Chapter 4. However, the Cleanser serves no purpose other than to ruin the cake, forcing Princess Peach and Twink to start over. It can be assumed that the Cleanser is used by the kitchen staff for cleaning. The Cleanser is also one of few items that can ruin the cake alongside [[salt]].

==In-game description==
''Detergent to wash utensils.''
