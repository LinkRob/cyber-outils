'''Bowser's Castle''' (otherwise known as his '''fortress''' or '''airship''') is the main base of operations for [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] and his army in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

==History==
The floating castle first appears descending upon the [[Snow Kingdom (Illumination)|Snow Kingdom]], carrying hundreds of troops ready to storm the kingdom's palace. The penguins tried to defend their palace, but Kamek was able to dispatch of them, and Bowser destroyed it with his fire breath and retrieved the Super Star.

It later appeared flying through the sky when Bowser housed a large rally, announcing that he is going to propose to his love, Princess Peach, much to the others' confusion, before adding it up with him destroying the Mushroom Kingdom if this proposal fails, now leading to the troop cheering on the king.

It is later seen, when Bowser is singing a song about his love for the princess, and when Shy Guys captured Luigi and brought him to his castle, having Bowser soon interrogate him then imprison him in a cage above lava.

The castle then flies over to the Mushroom Kingdom, where Bowser parks it near Peach's Castle, in order to confess his love for her. After getting Peach to accept his marriage through torture when she refused, the fortress was then decorated as an altar for the special occasion. Meanwhile, Mario and Donkey Kong fight through the army to make it towards the lair, where Peach can be seen battling more of the troops when she calls off the wedding.

The fortress then emerges into Brooklyn, when Mario redirects a Bomber Bill into the warp pipe he exited out of. Bowser is then seen furiously exiting it in order to battle the plumber, soon as more troops and Kamek exit as well to watch. After Mario and Luigi get the star, they use the invincibility to plummet Bowser and destroy his castle.
