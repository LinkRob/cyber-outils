'''Snifit''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'', and he is a representative of his species of the [[Snifit|same name]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Snifit is found on the second floor of the [[Horror Condo]]. He is a fan of being scared, and he wishes for the ghosts to haunt his room. If the player's character can make this happen, he rewards them the [[Porta-Gust]] [[Gaddget]].
