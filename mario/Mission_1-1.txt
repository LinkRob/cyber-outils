'''Mission 1-1''' is the very first mission which appears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

In this mission, [[Mario]] is the playable character. Mario has to go through a portion of [[Figure-8 Circuit]], the course it takes place in, and drive through the five numbered gates. It is required for Mario to do it all in order. There are sometimes a couple of mushrooms around the gates to help Mario gain a boost, so a three-star ranking can be obtained easier. If Mario doesn't complete the mission within thirty-five seconds, he loses the mission.
