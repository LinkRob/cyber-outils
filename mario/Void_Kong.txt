'''Void Kong''' is the primary antagonist of the game ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'' (with [[King K. Rool]] taking the role of the endgame). He is a Kong who serves the leader of [[VoidCo]] and works alongside his two associates, [[Grumpy Kong]] and [[Poppy Kong]]. Void Kong and his crew are responsible for stealing all of the bananas of [[Ingot Isle]] with his goal being to reach the [[Planet Core]] to receive a wish for riches. By the time Void Kong reaches his goal, Grumpy and Poppy quit to get their lives in order, and Void is left on his own. Void ends up being stuck in his own machine when [[King K. Rool]] punches him into it upon being woken from his slumber by [[Pauline]]. After said events and K. Rool is defeated, it's learned from Poppy that Void left the planet after Grumpy saved him from his machine. The Emerald Rush DLC later revealed he fled to [[DK Isles]] to collect emeralds as a new way to be rich, and to build back up VoidCo.

==Description==
=== Appearance ===
Void Kong is a very short primate who has very sharp teeth that are gold in color. He also wears a purple fur coat and gold earrings that resemble banana bunches. Void has yellow eyes with red pupils. His hands and feet have purple nails.

=== Personality ===
Prior to the events of the game, Void Kong was a struggling prospector. However, his discovery of a [[Banandium Root]] led him down the path of greed and avarice when the root ended up turning his fortunes in his favour.  Void is portrayed as even worse than his associates, who eventually redeem themselves while Void continues his pursuit of fortune. His logo, bearing Void Kong's face, highlights his narcissistic personality. He is also shown to be very impatient, constantly undermining and micromanaging the VoidCo employees.

=== Bios ===
In [[Nintendo Today!]], Void Kong is described in his character intro as: ''"The President of Void Company, the mightiest mining operation on the surface. He's been stealing every Banandium Gem he can find as he makes his way toward the [[Planet Core]]."''

In the loading screen tips for the game, Void Kong has the following descriptions:

* ''"President of [[VoidCo|Void Company]], the surface world's mightiest mining operation."''
* ''"Impatient to a fault, he constantly undermines and micromanages his employees."''
* ''"To replace his struggling staff, he's brainwashing the Underground World's monstrous creatures."''
* ''"His mining company struggled until he found and exploited the Banandium Root's golden tendrils."''

== History ==
Void Kong makes his debut at the start of the game, where he introduces himself and his company, demanding [[Pauline]] to come back to him. Pauline refuses, declaring that she will be accompanying [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], which leads to Void sending [[Grumpy Kong]] to fight Donkey Kong as Void and Poppy leave.

It is then revealed that Void Kong had brought the [[Great Harmoneel]] under his control, forcing Donkey Kong to fight the giant eel, which leads to him being arrested by the [[Iron Jailer]].

Void Kong is later seen at the [[Forbidden Layer]], preparing to get his hands on the [[Banandium Root]]. Donkey Kong defeats him, but Void, salty about his defeat, turns Donkey Kong to stone with his remote, so Pauline is forced to sing to free Donkey Kong. However, in doing so, she ends up awakening [[King K. Rool]]. Void, as he tries to get the Banandium Root, is thrown into his own mining machine by the Kremling King, who prepares to seize the Banandium Root for himself. Despite this, both Poppy Kong and Grumpy Kong confirmed that Void Kong survived this and actually help free him from his Pay-Dirt Piledriver.

===Emerald Rush===
In the Emerald Rush DLC for this game, Void Kong is now found on [[DK Isles]] where he recruits Donkey Kong into helping him mine for emerald to help build back up his company.

== Combat ==
In battle, Void Kong attacks by shooting drills from his chair, summoning [[Crockoid|Crockoids]] to his side. He summons purple rotating loops that must be avoided. Since he floats around in his chair, he can only be damaged by hitting his chair with terrain.

Upon losing half his health, Void Kong summons a shield around his body from three shield generators. Donkey Kong must make his way to the generators and destroy them, causing Void to drop to the ground in shock, where Donkey Kong can attack him. If Donkey Kong is too slow, Void will get back on his chair. During this phase, part of the ground is scalding hot, so Donkey Kong must avoid those areas. [[Rolluskoid|Rolluskoids]] will be summoned during this phase. During both phases, [[Pauline]] cannot use her singing powers to give Donkey Kong Bananza transformations to avoid accidentally awakening the Banandium Root (thus making sure Void Kong cannot get his wish prematurely).

When Void is defeated, the chair he was riding on blows up and Void plummets to the ground.

===Trivia===
*The way K Rool knocks Void Kong out is similar to when K Rool knocked out [[King Dedede]] in his [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]] revael trailer.
* Void Kong's name says a lot about his character.
** "Void" references his bottomless greed for Banandium Gems. He is never satisfied and will do anything to get them. Donkey Kong is also obsessed with Banandium. But unlike Void, Donkey Kong tempers his wants with showing genuine concern for others.
** It totally ignores the naming scheme of the Kongs while Grumpy and Poppy continue it, foreshadowing how the latter two are Punch Clock Villains who eventually rejoin the Kong Clan while Void is The Irredeemable Exception who remains an egocentric fiend to the bitter end.
** Void means "nothing," and as we learn, Void Kong used to be a nobody prospector who got rich out of luck and used his new wealth to get richer, but by the end of the game, he goes back to having nothing.
** His large eyes and ears give him some resemblance to certain prosimians, a primate group consisting of lemurs, galagos, tarsiers, pottos, and lorises, which are separate from simians (monkeys and apes). In this case, he could be considered an invalid, false, or ''void'' Kong.
** With his Crown-Shaped Head, large cape, sharp teeth, one bloodshot eye, and high and mighty attitude, one could say he was created to fill the ''void'' left by King K. Rool's absence. In the end, however, he's unceremoniously punched out of the story by King K. Rool himself. His role as the Big Bad has been ''voided''.
** His wish for infinite gold would most likely backfire; by increasing the amount of gold he would diminish the material's value, thus making it worthless. His wish would ultimately render itself null and void.
