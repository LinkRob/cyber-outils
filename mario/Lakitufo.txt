'''Lakitufos''' are a type of [[Lakitu]]s which are found in the [[Vim Factory]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', seemingly being an ally to the invading [[Shroob]] army. A Lakitufo resembles a Lakitu, but wearing a red shell and having yellow antennae coming out of its head. Additionally, instead of riding a cloud, they hang onto the side of a UFO. Using this flying saucer, Lakitufos beam down [[Spiny Shroopa]]s, the Shroob versions of [[Spiny|Spinies]].

In battle, Lakitufos can launch Spiny Shroopa Eggs to attack and beam down more Spiny Shroopas. A Lakitufo can also jump off of its UFO and perform an aerial spin attack, which is dodged by jumping.
