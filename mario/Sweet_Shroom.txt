A '''Sweet Shroom''' is an item and a [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Sweet Shrooms can be created by [[Tayce T.]] by having her cook together a [[Cake Mix]] with either a [[Life Shroom]] or a [[Ultra Shroom]]. Using a Sweet Shroom restores both 30 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 20 [[Flower Point|FP]], which is very useful in the game. It is recommended to create this item using the Cake Mix and Life Shroom as Life Shrooms can be purchased in [[Boo's Shop]] in [[Boo's Mansion]] while Ultra Shrooms are rare items to find.
