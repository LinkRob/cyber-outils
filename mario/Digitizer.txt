'''Digitizer''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained by completing the quest [[Sploosh!]].

==Description==
Digitizer can be used to activate a finger X-ray. The instructions ask the player to put their finger on the screen, although it does not make a difference whether they do or not because the [[Game Boy Advance]] does not have built-in finger recognition and Gaddgets are used as toys. The player can press {{button|gba|A}} to reveal objects inside the finger, such as fish bones, an arrow, a fork, a drill, or a key. An X-ray can be saved as a photo, but another X-ray cannot be taken unless the existing photo is deleted by pressing {{button|gba|B}}.
