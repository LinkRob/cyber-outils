'''Wii Balance Board''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as a [[Mystery Mushroom]] outfit. It is also a real-world accessory for the [[Wii]], and is compatible with ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for said console.
