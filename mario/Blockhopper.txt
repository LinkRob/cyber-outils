'''Blockhoppers'''<ref>Loe, Casey. ''New Super Mario Bros.'' Player's Guide. Page 13.</ref> are enemies in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Profile==
===Physical appearance===
Their upper half is composed of two [[Brick Block]]s whereas their body is a [[? Block]]. Blockhoppers also wear light blue boots. They have green eyes a black body obscured by the [[block]]s, although it is possible that the blocks may be shadowing their skin.

Blockhoppers are similar to the [[Pile Driver Micro-Goomba]]s that disguise themselves as blocks from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. Blockhoppers are also similar to the hopping [[Fake Block]]s and segmented [[Blokkablok]]s from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

===Behavior===
At first, a Blockhopper appears no different from a ? Block with [[Brick Block|bricks]] on top of it. Blockhoppers can be told apart from the question mark on their ? Block being motionless. Once [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] approaches a Blockhopper, it begins to move. Blockhoppers hop at every vocal "[[paah]]" in the background music. If Mario or Luigi is standing on a Blockhopper, it moves in the direction he is faced.

==History==
[[File:NSMB_Riding_Blockhopper.png|thumb|Mario riding a Blockhopper in ''New Super Mario Bros.'']]
In ''New Super Mario Bros.'', Blockhoppers appear only in [[World 2-5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-5]]. They are harmless when touched from the side because they are disguised as blocks, but they damage Mario or Luigi by landing on him. Blockhoppers can be defeated with a thrown [[Koopa Shell]] or a [[Ground Pound]] or by [[dash|running]] into them using the [[shell dash]] ability as [[Shell Mario]]. [[Mini Mario]] is too small to be able to defeat Blockhoppers.

If Mario or Luigi ground-pounds the ? Block on a Blockhopper, they receive a [[coin]] or a power-up item.
