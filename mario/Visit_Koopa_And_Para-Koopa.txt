'''Visit Koopa And Para-Koopa''' is the first [[level]] of [[World 2 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 2]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.

==Description==
This level introduces several things. It is the first appearance of [[Koopa Troopa]]s (specifically the [[Red Koopa Troopa|red]] type), [[Koopa Paratroopa|Para-Koopas]], and [[Baron von Zeppelin]]s that drop harmful objects. It introduces [[Cascading Stone|falling stone blocks]] as an obstacle. It is also the first level to have a [[Super Star]], which enables [[Baby Mario]] to temporarily become [[Superstar Mario]], and the first level to include [[Poochy]] (not counting [[World 1 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 1]]'s [[Poochy Ain't Stupid]]). 

==Trivia==
*The level icon depicts a green Koopa Troopa even though their first level appearance only occurs later on in [[GO! GO! MARIO!!]].
