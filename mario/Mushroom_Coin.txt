'''Mushroom Coin''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. Once the player has completed the game, a Mushroom Coin appears at the start of each [[level]] where a [[White Mushroom House]] can be unlocked, and hitting the Mushroom Coin shows how many [[coin]]s are necessary to reveal it.
