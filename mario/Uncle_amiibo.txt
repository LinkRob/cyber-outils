'''Uncle amiibo''' is a robotic character found in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. After talking to Uncle amiibo, tapping any amiibo or amiibo card will reveal the location of a [[Power Moon]] on the map. In 2-player mode, Uncle amiibo will refuse to let you scan an amiibo, simply stating, “them’s the rules.”

Mario (SSB, Super Mario, 30th Anniv), Luigi (SSB, Super Mario), Wario (SSB, Super Mario), Waluigi (Super Mario), Diddiy Kong (SSB, Super Mario), Gold/Silver Mario, Mario (Wedding), Peach (Wedding), and Bowser (Wedding) will give you outfits that can be purchased later on right away.
