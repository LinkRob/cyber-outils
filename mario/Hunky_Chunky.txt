|games = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}'''Hunky Chunky''' ('''''Kremlinous Churshum''''') is a special ability used by [[Chunky Kong]] in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. Chunky can purchase the potion containing this move from [[Cranky's Lab]] around the same time he can purchase the [[Primate Punch]].

==History==
To use Hunky Chunky, Chunky simply has to locate a barrel with his face on it and jump into it in ordered to be transformed into Hunky Chunky. As Hunky Chunky, Chunky can pick up large boulders and [[Simian Slam|slam]] large switches. Most notably, Hunky Chunky is used in a good number of boss fights including against [[Dogadon]] and [[King Krusha K. Rool]]. Chunky can maintain his Hunky Chunky form as long he has [[Crystal Coconut]]s on his person.

==Trivia==
*Hunky Chunky is considered an inverse of [[Tiny Kong]]'s [[Mini-Monkey]] ability.
