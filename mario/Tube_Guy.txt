[[Image:Tube Guys.png|thumb|right]]
'''Tube Guys''' (also called '''Toober Guys''') are a type of [[Shy Guy]]  found in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They are Shy Guys that swim in water with a floatie. They attack by swimming towards [[Yoshi]]. A Tube Guy can easily be defeated by swallowing one, spitting a Tube Guy out turns it into a standard Shy Guy.
