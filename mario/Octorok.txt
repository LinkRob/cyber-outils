'''Octoroks''' are an enemy from the {{series|The Legend of Zelda}}. They make their first overall appearance in ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'' and their first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in [[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]

Some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' enemies are based on Octoroks or even variants of them, a notable example of which is [[Octoomba]], a variant of both an Octorok and a [[Goomba]].

==In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''==
Octoroks appear as enemies in the Underground Maze. There is also an obtainable [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|Trophy]] of one.

==In ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''==
Octoroks are featured in the [[The Legend of Zelda (WarioWare: Twisted!)|The Legend of Zelda]] [[microgame]], in which they spit rocks at Link, who must use his shield to guard himself from the Octoroks.

==In ''[[Tetris DS]]''==
An enlarged sprite of an Octorok sometimes appears on the touch screen during challenges based on the original ''The Legend of Zelda''.
