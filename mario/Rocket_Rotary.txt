'''Rocket Rotary''' is one of [[Orbulon]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
There are a few exits, and the player has to rotate the path so that the rocket can be guided down the exit marked by a yellow arrow. In doing so, they win the microgame, but if either the rocket takes the wrong exit or hits a wall, the player loses at the microgame.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels feature three, four, and six potential paths respectively.
