'''Whirly Gates''' are an object in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

== Description ==
Whirly Gates replace [[Morph Bubble]]s from ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. Upon entering a Whirly Gate, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] goes into a mini course separate from the main level and transforms into one of six vehicles indicated by the icon on the Whirly Gate, either a [[Mine-Cart (form)|Mine-Cart]], a [[Hot-Air Balloon (form)|Hot-Air Balloon]], a [[Jackhammer Yoshi|Jackhammer]], a [[Helicopter]], a [[Submarine (form)|Submarine]], or a [[Bobsled]]. All vehicle transformations are confined to an area within a Whirly Gate, not the main main level. Sometimes these areas are uses to progress through the level while others take a Yoshi back to the same room.

In a Whirly Gate's course, the Yoshi has to navigate through enemies and obstacles within a [[Time Limit|timer]], although [[+ Clock|clocks]] can be collected to replenish time. At the end of the section are rainbow rings that must be reached, turning the Yoshi to his normal form, similarly to [[Yoshi Block]]s from previous ''Yoshi's Island'' games, and returning him to the main level. These rings replace the [[Yoshi Block]]s from prior entries. If a Yoshi does not reach the goal on time, he is forced back to the entrance of the Whirly Gate, although he can keep every item collected in these segments.
