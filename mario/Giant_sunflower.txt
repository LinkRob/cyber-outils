==Description==
Certain [[Winged Cloud]]s contain a Giant Sunflower, which starts out in the form of a seed. Whenever a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] breaks open a Winged Cloud, a Giant Sunflower seed falls directly below, and the instant the seed falls into the ground, the Giant Sunflower starts to grow from it. As it grows, the Giant Sunflower produces a number of leaf platforms along the way and concludes its growth with a sunflower head with a smiling face. Giant Sunflowers are always tall, but they can appear in varying heights.
