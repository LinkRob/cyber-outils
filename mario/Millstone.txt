'''Millstones''' are a type of object which appear in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related games. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==Description==
Millstones are large stone wheels mainly found in ancient ruins. In most of their appearances, Millstones serve as an obstacle. In ''Donkey Kong Country'', a brown [[Gnawty]] rides one after [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]].

==In ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''==
A large Millstone based on its appearance from ''Donkey Kong Country'' makes an appearance in the background of the [[Donkey Kong Jungle (baseball stadium)|Donkey Kong Jungle]] stadium.
