'''DK Kongs''' is the name of [[Donkey Kong]]'s default baseball team in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', particularly in Challenge Mode. Here, they own their own stadium named [[Donkey Kong Jungle (baseball stadium)|Donkey Kong Jungle]].

Aside from [[Donkey Kong]], the team always consists of [[Diddy Kong]], [[Dixie Kong]], [[Goomba]], [[Paragoomba]], at least one [[Koopa Troopa]], and at least one [[Koopa Paratroopa]]. In Challenge Mode, both the red and green Koopa Troopas as well as the red and green Paratroopas are members of the DK Kongs.

Despite the team's name, DK Kongs only consists of three Kongs whereas the others are all enemies from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]].

In ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'', DK Kongs and Donkey Kong's other teams were replaced with the [[DK Wilds]], which only consists of ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' characters (including [[Baby Donkey Kong|Baby DK]], who debuted in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''). Because of this, Goomba, Paragoomba, both Koopa Troopas, and both Koopa Paratroopas were relocated to being members of [[Wario]]'s team, [[Wario Muscles]].
