==Features==
The Bowser Express is a level looking similar to a traveling Circus. It is home too many enemies, and is the only level in the game that is World-Train.

==Layout==
The level starts on a platform near a train with some [[Skipsqueak]]s nearby. The player needs to hop on the train and avoid a nearby Spike Bar in order to reach an area infested with [[Conkdor]]s and [[Bully|Bullies]]. A pool of water comes soon after that where the player must navigate past some [[Madpole]]s in order to get back on the train. The player must now avoid a Spike Bar and enter a room filled with [[Mini Goomba]]s. A [[Hammer Bro]] guards the nearby [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] which brings the player to [[Pom Pom]]. After defeating her, a [[Warp Box]] brings the player to the area with the [[Goal Pole]]. As with the [[Bullet Bill Express]], the train starts off at a platform before speeding up and leaving, and if the player chooses not to board the train or fails to in time, they will lose a life due to being left behind by the level's auto-scrolling camera and the train itself.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': The first star is on the train near the beginning of the level. Be quick to jump up and grab it.
*'''Green Star 2''': The second star is a coin catch. Jump through the green star and collect the green coins for the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': The last star is just before the green pipe leading out. Jump down to the little platform and kill the fire guy and nab the star.
