'''Furafurawa''' (フラフラワー ''Furafurawā'') is an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for the [[Game Boy]]. It is a flower that first appears hiding underground. When approached, it pops out of the ground and walks back and forth. In later stages, a Furafurawa also shoots out projectiles.
