'''Mega Board Mayhem''' is a board in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==In ''Mario Party 4''==
Mega Board Mayhem is exclusively accessed via the [[Extra Room]], like with [[Mini Board Mad-Dash]]. Mega Board Mayhem was constructed by [[Thwomp]], who claims that it took him six months to construct. Unlike normal boards in Party Mode, there are no [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] and [[Star Space]]s found on the board, no special events, and no [[minigame]]s each turn.

The characters start with 100 [[coin]]s, and the object of Mega Board Mayhem is to have the most coins by the end. There are many [[Mushroom Space]]s and [[Item Shop]]s around, where the characters can get [[Mega Mushroom]]s, [[Super Mega Mushroom]]s, and other [[item]]s to steal coins from opponents. Characters can also use [[Mini Mushroom]]s and [[Super Mini Mushroom]]s to go through the board's two [[Gate (Mario Party 4)|Gates]], to access the inner part of the board, where there is a [[Lottery Shop]].

Like in Mini Board Mad-Dash, [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shops]] on Mega Board Mayhem function differently from other boards. When a character visits an Item Shop, it always has five of the same item for sale, which can be [[Warp Pipe]]s, [[Sparky Sticker]]s, Super Mini Mushrooms, or Super Mega Mushrooms.
