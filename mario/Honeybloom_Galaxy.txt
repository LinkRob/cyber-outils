}}'''Honeybloom Galaxy''' is a galaxy in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is the first Galaxy in the game where the Bee Mario power up becomes accessible.
==Missions==
===Bumble Beginnings===
Upon entering the galaxy, [[Mario]] finds some Bees searching for their [[Queen Bee]]'s [[Power Star]]. Once [[Mario]] progresses a bit more, he finds a [[Bee Mushroom]]. To complete this mission, he must make his way to the end of the Honeybloom Planet using his [[Bee Mario|Bee form]] to stand on clouds, cross gaps, and bounce off grapes. He then must use a Sproutle Vine to reach the Log Wood Planet, where he must dodge [https://www.mariowiki.com/Bee_Eater Bee Eaters], [[Choppah|Choppahs]] and [[Flower Mimic|Flower Mimics]]. When he reaches the end of this planet's sidescrolling section, Mario finds the Power Star above a cloud guarded by a [[Choppah]]. After getting the Power Star, a Bee sends [[Mario]] a letter containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] congratulating him for finding the Power Star.

===The Secret Wall Jump===
To complete this mission, [[Mario]] must use the first swing he finds. When swinging on it, he must perform a [[Star Spin]] to reach the two walls, then use the [[Wall Jump]] on them until he reaches the top, where the [[Power Star]] is located. After completing the mission, a Honeybee appears on [[Starship Mario]].

==Trivia==
*The Italian name of the mission "Bumble Beginnings", "Vola, vola, vola l'Ape Mario" is a reference to the Italian opening of the anime "Maya the Bee".
*When the first mission is completed, you will receive a letter from a [[Bee]] saying thank you for finding a [[Star]], and that they had trouble doing it.
