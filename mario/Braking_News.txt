'''Braking News''' is a downloadable [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It was made available in Japan on June 10, 2009, then in North America on May 10, 2010, and lastly in Europe on June 11, 2010.

==Layout==
[[Wario]] is driving the [[Wario Car]] on a straight road toward a cliff, leading down into water. There is a yellow 10m marker and a red 5m marker, and starting from the former is an orange line leading over to an edge.

==Gameplay==
The player must tap the Touch Screen to stop the Wario Car at least 10m from the cliff. In doing so, the player wins the microgame, but if Wario stops too far away or falls into the water, they lose at the microgame.
