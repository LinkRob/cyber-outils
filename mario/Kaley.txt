'''Kaley''' is one of the members of the  [[Best Fitness Friends]], and a major antagonist in ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. She is the only female member of the Best Fitness Friends, and fights as a Flying-type enemy in battle.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Kaley is a small, flying bird-like creature with pink skin. She has blue hair and her bun is tied with a black headband. She wears black clothing, a blue bowtie with a ribbons on it, white gloves and shoes.

=== Personality traits ===
At first, Kaley was seen as bubbly and had an upbeat personality. Much later on in the game, though, it was revealed that she is hyperactive and a drug addict, relying on the supplements by the BFFs. Halfway into the game, it is revealed that she is quick to anger and is not really into fitness, which were exposed by Ludwig, who had been disguising himself as [[Beef]]. Also, at [[Cavi Cape]] and [[Dimble Wood]], Kaley acted hostile yet worried for "Beef" when he wasn't acting like himself. She does, however, tend to think before she acts, as shown when she was somewhat reluctant to [[Malatone Formula:X]] in the final battle.

== In ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'' ==
When [[Fawful]] sees [[Bowser]] leave his [[Bowser's Castle|castle]], he summons the Best Fitness Friends to participate in his plan two take over both Bowser and [[Princess Peach's Castle]]s. The BFFs introduce themselves to both [[Roy Koopa|Roy]] and [[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]] about "personal fitness". Roy forces them to leave, but quickly changes his mind when Wendy suggests that their products might have an effect on the blorbs. Kaley gives [[Skeletone Formula:D]] to a blorbed [[Goomba]], turning it back to normal. As Roy asks them to give supplements of the Skeletone, but gets mad when Kaley says she only brought the one portion with her. Kaley does, however, give the [[Koopalings]] the ingredients for the formula.

In Dimble Wood, Ludwig suspects that someone is following the Koopalings and [[Bowser Jr.]] and returns to Bowser's Castle. Eventually, Kaley reveals herself to the remaining four Koopalings ([[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]], Wendy, [[Larry Koopa|Larry]] and [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton]]) and Bowser Jr., revealing that Bowser's Castle was under attack, so Wendy and Larry leave Bowser Jr. to check the castle. As they leave, Kaley and [[Dieter]] taunt how the Koopa Troop will be defeated by their own allies and mock how they left the castle basically defenseless (this is the part where it is revealed Kaley is a drug addict).

== Trivia ==
* Kaley is the only BFF member that is fought without the other members involved in a battle.
* Kaley is similar to Iggy, as both are hyperactive, they have the same laugh ("''Bahaha!''"), they fight as Flying-type troops, and both can use Super Counter, Lightning, and Call for Backup as commands. They only major difference is that Kaley is hyperactive due to being a drug addict, while Iggy is just generally hyper.
