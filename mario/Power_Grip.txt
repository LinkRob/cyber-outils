The '''Power Grip''' is an item that appears in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It can only be obtained by making a different kind of [[Starbeans Espresso|espresso]] at [[Starbeans Café]] for the fifth time. [[Professor E. Gadd]] will then appear and gives the Power Grip to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

==Appearance==
The Power Grip appears to resemble Mario and Luigi's gloves except it is gray in color.

==Usage==
With the Power Grip equipped, Mario or Luigi's [[hammer]] will not lose its head when defending. This is very useful against enemies or bosses that take awhile to attack and to hold up the hammer longer for safety.

The Power Grip does not return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', which replaces it with the [[Secret Specs]] because [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can hold out their hammer for as long as they desire without its head falling off.

==Trivia==
*In pre-release versions, Mario and Luigi originally would have received an Energy Tank from [[Samus Aran]], but when this cameo was removed, the Energy Tank was replaced with the Power Grip.
