'''Banktoad''', a member of the [[Toad Brigade]], is a [[Toad (species)|Green Toad]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', and can be found aboard the [[Starship Mario]]. He keeps [[Mario]]'s [[Star Bit]]s and will share them with other players, playing on the same Wii. If there are no other players, Banktoad will just store Mario's star bits for him and occasionally add a few to the total. At various intervals, when Banktoad has enough star bits, he will change costumes. When you give him 9999 star bits, the last galaxy level opens up. In [[Super Mario Odyssey]] banktoad can be seen as a minor character on the top of the [[Lost Kingdom]] and in some hills of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. In [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]], you can play as banktoad in multiplayer mode of the level series: [[The Adventures of Captain Toad]]. In [[Bowser's Fury]] story, [[Mario]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] save him as he regroups with his Toad Brigade members.
