A '''Dark Squiglet''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is the dark version of [[Squiglet]]s.



==History==
Dark Squiglets are stronger versions of [[Squiglet]]s that are found commonly in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]].  Dark Squiglets walk around on the ground until they spot one of the player and shoot rocks at them.
