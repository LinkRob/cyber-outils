A '''DaVincheese''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are related to [[Munchelangelo]]s but are golden colored. They can only be found onboard the [[S.S. Caviar]] and attack [[Wario]] by biting him. They can be defeated by any attacks.

==Trivia==
*Their name is a reference to Leonardo da Vinci, a famous Renaissance artist (similar to the Munchelangelo's name being based off of Michelangelo). The "cheese" part of their name is also a reference to them being rats.
