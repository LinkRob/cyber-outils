==Effects==
Like [[Shroom Shake]]s, these items replace [[Super Shroom]]s from previous [[Paper Mario series|''Paper Mario'' installments]]. These items restore twenty [[Heart Point]]s and restore [[Mario]] of poison. Although this item replaces Super Shrooms as an item Mario can carry, some may appear in [[Question Block]]s. Mario can also purchase these items from some shops such as the one in [[Flopside]] for eighty [[coin]]s.
