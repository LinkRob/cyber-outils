There are 16 [[Boo]]s hiding in the [[The Last Resort]]. One Boo appears on each floor of the hotel, barring the [[Master Suite]]. They appear after obtaining the floor's [[elevator button]], and leaving and returning.

The precise location of Boos is randomized, but they have fixed rooms in which they can appear; each time the player loads the game, the room in which a Boo appears switches between the fixed rooms. If a Boo is in the room ominous organ music starts playing. The Boos hide in one of the interactable objects in the room, the best method to detect the right one is to use [[Gooigi]] because he begins vibrating if a Boo is in the same room as him and the vibrating gets stronger the closer he gets to the Boo hiding spot. 

Some Boos will spawn new objects in the rooms to hide in as well. After their capture, some rooms will go back to how they were before, while others will retain these new objects. No other ghosts will appear in the room where the Boo is, either.

==Tracking==
If a Boo is in the room, ominous organ music starts playing. The Boo hides in one of the interactable objects in the room, which can be detected with [[Gooigi]], who vibrates the closer he is to it. If Gooigi is still inside the Poltergust G-00, these vibrations will be felt in the player's controller, but can also be seen on-screen as the green goo in the Poltergust G-00 shakes. If a second player is playing as Gooigi, only their controller will give these signals.

If the correct hiding spot is found the Boo comes out with a quote and the fight begins. If the wrong object is interacted with, the Boo comes out of his real hiding spot, says a different quote, spawns a group of [[bat]]s and disappears into the next room.

Interacting with objects that are not part of the Boo's possible hiding places or the same area will not trigger the Boo to come out of its real hiding spot.
