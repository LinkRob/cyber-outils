The '''Drillby''' is an enemy from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They are common in [[Desolatt Island]].

== Description ==
Drillbies are orange wormlike enemies with spiked backs and bulging eyes. Their head has a drill on top.

== Behaviour ==
In combat, Drillbies cannot be [[Spiny (Enemy type)|stomped due to the drill on their head]]. Whenever a Drillby attacks, it can charge into its opponent drill-first or burrow underground and then pop out in front front of its target to strike. Both attacks can be blocked via the [[Hammer]]. The Drillby is of Level 16 with a health maximum of 87, an attack power of 45, a defense of 46, and a speed of 3. When slain, it gives 40 experience, with its loot drop consisting of 19 coins along with a [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]] (3% of cases), a Knockout Hammer (1% of cases), and/or a Desolatt Scrap (1 in 4 cases).

== Variations ==

=== Drillby R ===
A stronger, pink variation can be found on [[Slippenglide Island]], known as a Drillby R. Drillby R's have three attacks, with two being shared with their Desolatt Island counterpart. However, the Drillby R's third technique is a move which its Desolatt Island counterpart lacks, in which it burrows slowly and approaches a Bro. before popping up and spinning its drill. This attack can be dodged by jumping, but can [[Dizzy|stun the victim]] if not countered. The Drillby R is of Level 25, with 262 hit points, an Attack of 104, a Defense of 89, and a Speed of 109, giving 202 experience and 46 coins (along with possibly a Super or Ultra [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]] (3% and 1%) or a sample of [[Crafting Materials (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Slippenglide Snow]] (21%)) when slain.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gxj0d05e4e8</ref>

=== Glohm Drillby and Glohm Drillby R ===
Both versions of the Drillby have stronger versions that are infected with [[Glohm]]. These infected Drillbies fight like their regular counterparts, but much faster. The Glohm Drillby R is the first to be encountered, being Level 25 with 304 hit points, an Attack of 107, a Defense of 111, and a Speed of 126, and it yields 253 experience and 58 coins when destroyed<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gxj0d05e4e8</ref> (along with the same loot drops as a regular Drillby R aside from also being able to drop a Glohm Fragment (3%) or a sample of Glohm Dust (7%)). It can be fought alongside clean Drillby R's at Slippenglide Island.

When [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|takes over Concordia]], both versions become infected by the bond-destroying substance.  Glohm Drillbies are of Level 39 with 612 hit points, with their destruction netting 794 experience and 120 coins, although on Mykroh Islet, their level is increased to 40, with 649 hit points and giving 848 experience and 126 coins when destroyed. Glohm Drillby R's have their level bumped up to 40, with 789 hit points and giving 848 experience and 126 coins when destroyed.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Drillbies seem to be named after drill bits due to their drill horn.

Drillby R's seem to take their design from strawberry ice cream.

== Nomenclature ==
Drillbies are called ''Dorimaru'' in the Japanese dub (written ドリマル), with their name coming from a Japanese transliteration of "drill" and the antiquated Japanese masculine suffix -''maru'' (written 丸). The Chinese translation gives them a calque of this name: ''Zuànwán'' (written , )which comes from ''zuāntóu'' ("drill"; written 钻头) and ''wán'' ("round"; written 丸). Their Spanish name, ''Talavo'', comes from ''taladro'' ("drill") and ''chavo'' (Mexican for "boy"). Portuguese translates their name as ''brocássio'', from ''broca'' (a translation of "drill") and the given name ''Cássio'' ("Cassius"). In Italian, their name is ''Trivellik'', from ''trivella'' (a translation of "drill") and the Russian dimunitive suffix ''-ik'' (written -''ик''). In French, their name is ''Asticreuse'', from ''asticot'' ("maggot") and ''creuser'' ("to dig"). German translates their name as ''Bohris'', from ''Bohrer'' ("drill") and the given name "Boris." They have the name ''Aebeoldeuril'' (written 애벌드릴) in Korean, derived from ''aebeolle'' ("caterpillar"; written 애벌레) and a Korean transliteration of "drill."
