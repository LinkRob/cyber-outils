'''Treasures''' are collectible items which appear in the ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' platformers as well as the [[Luigi's Mansion (series)|''Luigi's Mansion'' series]]. Treasures are valuable items that often have an effect on the ending, depending on much has been collected.
