'''Octoombas''' (also known as '''Electrogoombas''' or '''Octogoombas'''), are alien enemies which first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They are a variant of both a [[Goomba]] and an [[Octorok]], the latter of which is an enemy from the [[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The Legend of Zelda]] series. It is fairly common to find Octoombas in a majority [[Galaxy]]s. Their main attack is by headbutting [[Mario]]. In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', they were also given the ability to spit rocks like Octoroks. They appear frequently in the two ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games.

==Etymology==
Octoomba is a portmanteau of Octorok and Goomba, its two parent species.

==History==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[Image:Electrogoombas.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] near the first Electrogoombas.]]
Octoombas (under the name '''Electrogoomba''' in this game) make their debut in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', in this game, they can be found in several Galaxies. They are typically seen bouncing about but as soon as [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] gets close, they pull their heads back and attempt to headbutt the plumbers, the plumbers will take damage if they're hit by this attack. An Octoomba can easily be beaten with either a stomp or a [[Star Spin]], Octoombas are often found before [[boss]]es. They can be found in several galaxies such as the [[Good Egg Galaxy]], the [[Honeyhive Galaxy]], the [[Beach Bowl Galaxy]], the [[Gusty Garden Galaxy]], the [[Freezeflame Galaxy]], the [[Dusty Dune Galaxy]], the [[Gold Leaf Galaxy]], the [[Toy Time Galaxy]], and the [[Rolling Gizmo Galaxy]], an Octoomba can also be seen in the ending on [[Bowser Jr.'s Airship]] alongside an [[Octoguy]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] himself dancing. [[Octoguy]]s also appear in this game, they are a more advanced relative of the Octoomba that can move in more directions and can actually spit rocks at the plumbers. [[Octopus (Super Mario Galaxy)|Octopuses]] also appear, these guys act as road blocks which commonly shoot [[Fireball]]s or [[Coconut]]s/[[Watermelon]]s at the plumbers.

{{br}}
===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[Image:Octoombas.png|thumb|right|Mario near some Octoombas.]]
Octoombas return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' retaining the same overall behaviors as the previous game, in this game however, they have been given the ability to spit rocks at [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] which they must avoid, additionally, their face turns orange as they spit their rocks. Just as before however, an Octoomba can easily be beaten with a stomp or a [[Star Spin]], they can also be swallowed by [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. Octoombas can also appear in several planets which can be accessed via [[Teleporter (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|Teleporters]]. They are also the primary enemies found during [[The Chimp]]'s various challenges as well as the [[Romp Comet]]s, in addition to the returning [[Octoguy]]s and [[Octopus (Super Mario Galaxy)|Octopuses]], [[Elite Octoomba]]s and [[Octoboo]]s. The former is a more advanced version of the Octoomba that is faster and spits two rocks and the later is a ghostly version that is invincible to all attacks. Octoombas can be found in the [[Sky Station Galaxy]], the [[Yoshi Star Galaxy]], the [[Spin-Dig Galaxy]], the [[Fluffy Bluff Galaxy]], the [[Flip-Swap Galaxy]], the [[Rightside Down Galaxy]], [[Bowser Jr.'s Fiery Flotilla]], the [[Puzzle Plank Galaxy]], the [[Cloudy Court Galaxy]], the [[Rolling Masterpiece Galaxy]], the [[Beat Block Galaxy]], the [[Flipsville Galaxy]], the [[Chompworks Galaxy]], the [[Honeyhop Galaxy]], the [[Shiverburn Galaxy]], the [[Melty Monster Galaxy]], the [[Clockwork Ruins Galaxy]], the [[Flash Black Galaxy]], [[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]], the [[Stone Cyclone Galaxy]], and the [[Grandmaster Galaxy]].
{{br}}

===''Super Mario 3D World / Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''===
[[Image:New Octoombas.png|thumb|left|[[White Tanooki Mario]] near some Octoombas.]]
Octogoombas return in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'' predominantly on the [[Super Galaxy]] level of [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|Super Mario 3D World]]. Unlike the past games, Octogoombas can not be stomped, instead players must ground pound them to defeat them. In this game, instead of spitting out rocks, they can now spit out three iron balls that explode on contact. They are invincible to regular attacks and projectiles but can be taken out instantly with a [[Starman]], [[Invincibility Leaf]], [[Invincibility Bell]], or a [[Mega Mushroom]]. In addition to [[Super Galaxy]], Octoombas can also be found in [[Champion's Road]].
{{br}}
==Trivia==
*In the beta stages of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', Octoombas were referred to as '''Cosmic Bloopers''' which seemed to suggest that they had some relation to [[Blooper]]s, this was later retconned.
