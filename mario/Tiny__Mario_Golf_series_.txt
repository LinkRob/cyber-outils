'''Tiny''' is a human golfer from the ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' series who, despite his name, has a large muscular build. He first appears in ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]], and he also went on to appear in ''[[Mobile Golf]]'' and ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''.
