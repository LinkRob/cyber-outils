|race = Unknown (possibly [[Dry Bones]])}}The '''S.S. ''Chuckola'' Captain''' is a minor character that appeared in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. He is the leader of the [[S.S. Chuckola Crew|S.S. ''Chuckola'' crew]] and is the captain of the [[S.S. Chuckola|S.S. ''Chuckola'']].
==History==
The S.S. ''Chuckola'' Captain's role is pretty small in this game. Mario and Luigi can visit in his room, where he happily greets them on his ship. The S.S. ''Chuckola'' Captain is seen again where he is excited that the S.S. ''Chuckola'' is on the seas again, but it soon crashes into a giant rock and sinks to the bottom of the ocean. It is unknown what happened to him and the rest of his crewmates.
