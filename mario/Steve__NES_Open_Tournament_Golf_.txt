'''Steve''' is a character in ''[[NES Open Tournament Golf]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Steve wears black pants, a black vest, and a brown hat, which is tilted down to cover his eyes.

In his artwork, Steve's shirt and pants are light shades of brown, and he also wears a dark brown vest. He also is given a black bandana and dark brown boots. The artwork makes it evident that Steve is wearing a fedora, which covers most of his brown hair.

==In ''NES Open Tournament Golf''==
Steve, like with [[Mark (NES Open Tournament Golf)|Mark]], exclusively appears in the North American version. Steve is considered to be a beginner-ranked character. If the player has $500, they can play against Steve in the bet on 1 hole mode. Steve can also be challenged in match play.
