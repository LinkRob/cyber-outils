'''Squigs''' are common enemies and a stronger version of [[Squiglet]]s. They appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]], ''and have a rock spitting attack unlike Squiglets. Squigs can be easily defeated by jumping on them. Their [[Catch Card]] can be found in Chapter 1-3 near the red palm tree after flipping to 3D.
