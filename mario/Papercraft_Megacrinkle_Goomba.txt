It serves as the first [[Papercraft]] boss in the game and is fought in [[Sunbeam Plains]]. It is piloted by a [[Crinkle Goomba|Paper Goomba]] who was crumpled earlier in the game.

==Appearance==

The Papercraft Megacrinkle Goomba is a large Papercraft Goomba with a crown on its head.

==Battle==

Despite being a boss, the Papercraft Megacrinkle Goomba acts just like the smaller Papercraft Goombas except with more HP. It's main attack includes a tackle which deals moderate damage. The papercraft used in this fight is Papercraft Mario.

===Strategy===

It's very simple to dodge the Papercraft Megacrinkle Goomba's dash then hitting it with a pound attack. After it loses half of its HP, it speeds up its attacks and moves in an odd pattern but it is still easy to dodge its tacking attack then either knocking it over with a ram or crushing it with a stomp.

==Trivia==
*The Papercraft Megacrinkle Goomba's name and appearance is a reference to the [[Megasparkle Goomba]] from ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
