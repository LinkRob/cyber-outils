'''Bingo''' is a character who makes a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Too Hot to Handle]]." Here, he is featured as one of the [[Toad (species)|Mushroom]] inhabitants of [[Waki-Waki]]. At one point, Bingo assists the island's leader, [[Scooter]], to try and throw [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] into the volcano as a sacrificial offering to the [[Fire God]]. This order was not given by the real Fire God but rather [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] impersonating him.
