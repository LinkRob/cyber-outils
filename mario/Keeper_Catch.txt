'''Keeper Catch''' is a move in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

== Description ==
Keeper Catch is an outfield special move usable only by [[Kritter]]s, in which it dives for the ball if it is out of reach. The player can have Kritter do a Keeper Catch by pressing the A button.
