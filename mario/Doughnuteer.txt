'''Doughnuteer '''(コックン ''Kokkun'') is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. He is a small grouchy creature that holds a fork with a doughnut. He will attack on sight and throw the doughnut at [[Wario]] causing him to eat it and become fat.
