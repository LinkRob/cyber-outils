'''Super Bouncer '''is an [[Special Attack]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

==Effect==
When this attack is used, either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] will throw a ball, then Luigi will jump on top of it. Then press {{button|ds|a}} to start the attack. Use {{button|ds|Pad}} to rotate the ball, and press {{button|ds|b}} to damage the enemy when the ball is on top of it. If Luigi is too close to the bottom of the ball and hits the enemy, it will deal less damage than before plus stopping the attack. If the player presses {{button|ds|a}} too late if the ball is above Mario after hitting the enemy at least once correctly, he will miss the ball and the attack is canceled.

The attack can be unlocked by obtaining all ten [[Attack Piece]]s in the [[Energy Hold]].
