'''Flying Spades''' are flying shovel enemies which appear in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Behavior==
Flying Spades are like [[Monstrous Magnet]]s in that they use small insect wings to fly around. Flying Spades also shoot bolts of electricity. Upon being knocked down, a Flying Spade makes the same echoing metal sound as a Monstrous Magnet.

==In ''Wario World''==
Flying Spades are only encountered in [[Pecan Sands]]. [[Wario]] can stun a Flying Spade by hitting it three times, causing the Flying Spade to drop to the ground. Wario can then pick up the Flying Spade and throw it into a soft section of dirt on a wall. A Flying Spade sticks on the dirt for a short while, allowing Wario to reach areas that he could not access from a single jump.
