==Appearance==
Green is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that resembles [[Luigi]].

==History==
Green lives in [[Yold Town]] working as the bridgemaster for the east side. Green is at odds with his older brother, [[Red (Super Paper Mario)|Red]], as Green hates the color red. After [[Old Man Watchitt]] allows him to lower the bridge, Green asks [[Mario]] which color is better. Even if Mario answers "green," Green will kick him out of his house calling him a liar (due to Mario wearing red). Green will only speak peacefully with [[Luigi]] and [[Bowser]] (most likely not [[Princess Peach|Peach]] because she wears pink and pink is a mix of red and white).
