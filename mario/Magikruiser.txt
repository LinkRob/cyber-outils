The '''Magikruiser''' is a [[bike]] used by small-sized characters in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:Rocket_Start_(Mario_Kart_Wii).png|thumb|190px|right|[[Koopa Troopa]]'s Magikruiser in action on [[Koopa Cape]].]]
The bike is based of a [[Magikoopa]] enemy (possibly [[Kamek]]) lying with wheels on his legs and hands. The bike's engine and exhaust is based of Kamek's broom.

The Magikruiser can be unlocked by completing eight different [[Time Trial]] course, or lay 900 races.

The Magikruiser is used for the Normal Staff Ghost for [[Peach Gardens|<small>DS</small> Peach Gardens]] by Nin★Ito.y, using [[Baby Peach]], with the allotted time of 02:34.894, and is used for the Expert Staff Ghost on [[Shy Guy Beach|<small>GBA</small> Shy Guy Beach]], by Nin★Matt using [[Koopa Troopa]], with the allotted time of 01:32.867. This bike with [[Toadette]] is used for the World Record in [[Sherbet Land (Mario Kart 64)|<small>N64</small> Sherbet Land]].

Its [[kart]] counterpart is the [[Tiny Titan]].

== Statistics ==
The Magikruiser offers above average [[speed]], [[acceleration]], [[handling]] and [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]], while it also offers low [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]]. Additionally, this bike has the best [[Off-Road|off-road]] stat in the game, and if paired with Toadette, it will have the best off-road of any kart or bike in the game. The player can take any off-road shortcuts without hardly losing any speed. Its speed is tied with the [[Phantom]], and its acceleration is tied with the [[Zip Zip]].

== Color schemes ==
*[[Baby Mario]]: Blue cloak, red windshield, yellow with white lines, red shoes and tires.
*[[Baby Luigi]]: Dark green cloak, blue windshield, orange with white lines, brown shoes and dark blue tires.
*[[Baby Peach]]: Hot pink cloak, sky blue windshield, pink with white lines, deep pink shoes and dark pink tires.
*[[Baby Daisy]]: Orange cloak, sky blue windshield, dark orange with white lines, red shoes and tires.
*[[Toad (character)|Toad]]: Red cloak, deep sky blue windshield, white with yellow lines, brown shoes and tires.
*[[Toadette]]: Magenta cloak, sky blue windshield, gold yellow with white lines, brown red shoes and purple tires.
*[[Koopa Troopa]]: Lime green yellow cloak, yellow windshield, white with light blue lines, green shoes and tires.
*[[Dry Bones]]: Black cloak, silver windshield, dark red with white lines, pale blue shoes and black tires.
*Male Miis: Azure blue cloak, sky blue windshield, deep sky blue windshield, brown shoes and blue tires.
*Female Miis: Light pink cloak, sky blue windshield, deep pink with white lines, light brown shoes and dark pink tires.
