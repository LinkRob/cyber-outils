'''Guardian Shroobs''' are white [[Shroob]]s with blue spots. They are found in the [[Shroob Mother Ship]] during the events of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. They are stronger and have faster attacks than normal Shroobs, and often attack in groups. However, there are only two Guardian Shroobs encountered in the overworld and only a total of nine appearing in the whole game. After the four Mario Bros. fall out of the airship, Guardian Shroobs cannot be battled again. 

In battle, a Guardian Shroob can call another Guardian Shroob to approach the Mario brothers and fire two quick energy blasts from their ray guns, one for each [[Mario]] brother. To dodge this attack, the Mario brothers can simply [[jump]] over the energy blasts. They can also attack by themselves, just like an ordinary Shroob, though the energy blast is way faster than a normal Shroob's. 

The Shroobs fought in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' share a similar attack pattern to the Guardian Shroobs.
