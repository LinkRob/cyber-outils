'''Midway Gates''' are an object which appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==Description==
Midway Gates are [[checkpoint]]s found in nearly all [[level]]s except auto-scrolling, underwater, and those that take place in the [[Star World]] or [[Special Zone]]. Midway Gates resemble [[Giant Gate]]s in that they have a blue and white-striped pole with a white piece of tape in the center, but a Midway Gate's tape does not move up and down. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can activate a Midway Gate by going through the piece of tape, which then disappears. If either Mario or Luigi goes through a Midway Gate while in their [[Small Mario|Small]] form, they turn into their [[Super Mario (form)|Super]] form.

As a checkpoint, a Midway Gate is where Mario or Luigi restart the level from if they lose a life. The Midway Gate's effects wear off if either the player gets a [[Game Over]] or if the player activates the Giant Gate at the end of the same level. As such, the player can freely exit that level and clear another level and when they go back on it, they will be back at its respective checkpoint.

Midway Gates appear to have been replaced by [[Checkpoint Flag]]s in later [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] titles, including ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''{{'}}s ''Super Mario World'' style.
