'''Mawful Moles''' are a [[Monty Mole]] variant with a resemblance to [[Fawful]]. They are encountered in [[Blubble Lake]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]. [[Bowser]] can defeat these in the overworld by simply touching them, but [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can fight Mawful Moles themselves. Mawful Moles have a metal variant named [[Metal Mawful Mole]].

They attack by throwing boulders at Mario and Luigi. This attack can be countered by a jump. If they get dirt on their faces, they miss and throw it above the bros., which can hurt Mario and Luigi if they jump. The moles may also "wink," showing which bro. they are attacking. This attack can be countered with the [[hammer]], which can be done several times until the mole jumps over the selected bro. to fall on him.
