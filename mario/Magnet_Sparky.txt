'''Magnet Sparkies'''<ref name=Mini-Land>''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!'' Help Mode & Construction Zone</ref> (originally known as '''Sparkies'''<ref>''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis'' object graphics filename (<tt>data/data/anims/sparky_anim.bin</tt>)</ref>) are a type of [[Spark (Donkey Kong franchise)|Spark]] in the ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (series)|Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'' series that move along the edges of magnetized surfaces.

==History==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''===
[[File:MvDK2 Room 4-5.png|thumb|left]]
Sparkies first appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''. They only appear in [[Room 4-5 (Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis)|Room 4-5]] and [[Room 4-7 (Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis)|Room 4-7]] of [[Magnet Mania (Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis)|Magnet Mania]]. They spawn from the red side of metal [[block]]s with an "S" engraving, and slowly move clockwise. If a [[Magnetic Block]] is disabled from underneath it, the Sparky will vanish with it, and another Sparky will soon generate from its metal "S" block. They resemble Sparks from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', as they quickly flash between a white color with a light blue outline and a black color with a yellow outline.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!''===
[[File:MvsDK6-2.png|thumb]]
Magnet Sparkies reappear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!]]'' In this game, they retain a solid white color and blue outline rather than flashing between colors. They act much as they do in their previous appearance, except they move at a slightly faster speed. Upon approaching the type of block it spawns from (now known as [[Magnet Sparky Generator]]s), it will briefly retreat inside the block rather than passing it by. They are introduced in [[Cosmic Adventure]].
