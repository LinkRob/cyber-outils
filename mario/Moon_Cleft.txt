'''Moon Clefts''' are a variant of [[Cleft]] which appear in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Moon Clefts are, as the name suggests, Clefts that live on [[The Moon]]. These are the most powerful versions of Clefts, and can also be found in some of the later areas of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. With a very high defense value as well as high attack, these guys are better off avoided.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
[[File:MoonCleftCard.png|thumb]]
Here, Moon Clefts are found throughout Chapter 5. While they retain their name, Moon Clefts do not appear on any moon. As another point of interest, regular Clefts don't appear in the game either.
