'''Big Bertha''' is the name used for a species as well as a character:

*[[Big Bertha (film)]] the large woman from the 1993 film ''[[Super Mario Bros. (film)|Super Mario Bros.]]''.
*[[Big Bertha (fish)]] the female variant of the [[Boss Bass]] which debuted in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.
