|race = [[Little Mouser|Squeek]]}}'''Pierre''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a [[Little Mouser|Squeek]] that lives in [[Rogueport]].

==History==
Pierre is a thief that is always seen just outside of the [[Robbo Gang]]'s lair. Pierre appears to be a fan of [[Ishnail]] and has been thieving in order to join. Eventually, he does join the Robbo Gang after giving up his dream of being an archaeologist. Pierre's partner can be seen in [[Rogueport Sewers]] but after the game has been beaten, his partner claims that Pierre became an archaeologist again. However, Pierre still says he is part of the Robbo Gang.
