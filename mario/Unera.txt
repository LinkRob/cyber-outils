'''Unera''' (ウネラ) is a bee larva enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Behavior==
A Unera grovels along the ground, moving back and forth like a [[Goomba]]. It is one of the most basic enemies, and [[Mario]] can defeat a Unera by either [[jump]]ing onto it, shooting a [[fireball]] at it as [[Fire Mario]], or touching it while [[Invincible Mario|invincible]].
