The '''Iron Jailer''' is a character from [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']] who serves as a guard in the Groove Layer's prison.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Iron Jailer is first seen made of gray Slippery Iron, though he has the same pink/blue eyes of other [[Fractone|Fractones]]. He holds what is presumably a spear or staff with a brown handle and a blue tip. Like other Fractones, he can shapeshift, transforming into an exclamation mark when [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] breaks out and the Iron Jailer takes notice. During the postgame, the Iron Jailer changes to blue Fractonium.

=== Personality ===
The Iron Jailer is the only [[Fractone]] not from the [[Forbidden Layer]] to be hostile towards [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], blaming him for ruining the Groove Layer's disco. During the postgame after [[King K. Rool]]'s defeat, the Iron Jailer softens up and thanks Donkey Kong for fixing the disco.

== History ==
The Iron Jailer makes his debut when Donkey Kong wakes up after his arrival in the Groove Layer's prison. Upon meeting the Iron Jailer, Donkey Kong can ask him a question, but is held under suspicion for wrecking the Groove Layer. (In reality, [[Void Kong]] had brainwashed the Great Harmoneel, as [[Poppy Kong]], who had been jailed too, reveals when she is spoken to.) Donkey Kong must then escape without [[Pauline]] (who had been eaten by the Great Harmoneel), at which point the Iron Jailer takes notice.

After the player beats the game, Donkey Kong can once again talk with the Iron Jailer, who apologises for assuming Donkey Kong destroyed the disco. The Iron Jailer also claims to have softened up since the disco's reparations during this conversation.
