The '''Swap Shop''' is a small shop run by [[Mole]]s in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It uses a system of points for currency, which [[Mario]] can use to purchase either a [[Fire Bomb]], an [[Ice Bomb]], or a [[Fright Bomb]], each priced at 100 points.
