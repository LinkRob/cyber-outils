'''Propeller Platforms''' are objects that appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''. 

==Description==
Propeller Platforms are grated platforms with a propeller on the underside of each. By blowing into the [[Wii U GamePad]] or Nintendo 3DS's microphone, the platforms either rise into the air or move to the end of a set path. After some time, they move back to their origin points. In the [[Nintendo Switch]] versions of ''Super Mario 3D World'' and ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker'', they are replaced by [[Lift]]s, invisible [[Cloud Lift]]s, and [[Touchstone]]s due to the console's lack of a microphone.{{SM3DWNav}}
