==Layout==
Mt. Rugged is mainly a large mountain with many rocks and uneven platforms around. The main enemies in this area include [[Monty Mole]]s and [[Cleft]]s.

==History==
This is where [[Mario]] meets his mailman, [[Parakarry]], and asks him if he wants to join his party, and when he agrees, Parakarry joins Mario's party. You get there by taking by taking the [[K64]] train engine in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. It is also home to a [[Whacka]] and [[Buzzar]], a giant vulture that will fight [[Mario]] unless he answers his question by saying "[[Luigi]]."
