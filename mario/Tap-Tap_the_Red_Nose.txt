'''Tap-Tap the Red Nose''' is a large [[Tap-Tap]] who appears as a boss in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

Tap-Tap the Red Nose has a golden relative named [[Tap-Tap the Golden]], who is around the same size, but is classified regular enemy instead of a boss.

==History==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
[[File:Tap-Tap the Red Nose battle SMW2.png|thumb|Yellow Yoshi, about to defeat Tap-Tap the Red Nose by knocking him into the lava]]
In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', Tap-Tap the Red Nose is the mid-boss of [[World 6 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 6]], where he is encountered and fought by [[Yellow Yoshi]] in his own fort named [[Tap-Tap The Red Nose's Fort]].

Tap-Tap the Red Nose was originally a regular Tap-Tap until he was enlarged by [[Kamek]]. During his battle, Tap-Tap the Red Nose hops around on a bridge made of [[breakable block]]s over a pit of [[lava]]. He tries to attack Yellow Yoshi by landing on him. Like regular Tap-Taps, Yellow Yoshi can throw [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] at Tap-Tap the Red Nose to knock him back. Tap-Tap the Red Nose is invulnerable to all of Yellow Yoshi's attacks, and must be defeated from falling into the lava. To accomplish this, Yellow Yoshi can use his eggs to break the breakable blocks, opening a gap for Tap-Tap the Red Nose to fall into.

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', Tap-Tap the Red Nose, makes a cameo during the opening cutscene, as with the other world bosses from ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Tap-Tap the Red Nose's name is likely derived from {{w|Rudolph the Red-Nose}}.

==Trivia==
[[File:SMW2 Tap-Tap the Vibrant.png|frame]]
*Tap-Tap the Red Nose may be a pun on {{wp|Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer}}.
*While the [[Nintendo Power|Player's Guide]] for the remake ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' implies that [[Tap-Tap the Golden]] is a resurrected Tap-Tap the Red Nose,<ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'' Player's Guide. Page 115. "'''''Golden Opportunity''' Tap-Tap the Golden is back to torment you. You can run only as fast as the scrolling screen allows. If you run too far to the left, you’ll fall into lava pits.''"</ref> the Player's Guide for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] version suggests otherwise,<ref>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 128. "'''''TAP-TAP THE GOLDEN''' This mega Tap-Tap pursues Yoshi through the lava caves beneath Red Nose's Fort.''"</ref> albeit by erroneously stating that Tap-Tap the Golden is located "beneath Red Nose's Fort."
*Tap-Tap the Red Nose is the only boss that Yoshi does not meet by entering a boss door. Instead, he goes through a side pipe and falls from the other end.
*Tap-Tap's battle arena is similarly structured to {{wp|Breakout (video game)|''Breakout''}}, seeing as Yoshi has to break certain parts of the floor with eggs to knock him into the lava.
*After finishing being imbued with Kamek's magic and before he starts to enlarge, Tap-Tap the Red Nose is briefly shown as a normal Tap-Tap with a unique blueish palette. Due to the short amount of time between these and how he is constantly moving during it, it can be difficult to see.
