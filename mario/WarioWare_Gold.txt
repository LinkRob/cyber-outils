'''''WarioWare Gold''''' (also known as '''''Made in Wario Gorgeous, '''''メイド イン ワリオ ゴージャス ''Meido in Wario Gōjasu'') is a ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' game in the ''WarioWare'' series for the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. It features 325 microgames and 16 boss stages, with many of the microgames returning from previous games, making this game the biggest in terms of microgames in the series, surpassing the previous record from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.
