'''''Art Style: PiCTOBiTS''''' is a [[DSiWare]] puzzle game released in 2009. It involves the player having to assemble 8-bit sprites from a few different [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games, with most of the sprites being from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.
