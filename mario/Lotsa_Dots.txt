'''Lotsa Dots''' is a minigame that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Gameplay==
In Lotsa Dots, the player must use the stylus to connect the dots on the picture to eventually form a picture. The dots are numbered starting with "1" and goes on until the final number is reached. The player must take care not to incorrectly draw to the wrong number next or they will risk losing the minigame. The three images the player could end up drawing is a "W", [[Wario]]'s mustache, or poop. Completing Lotsa Dots results in Wario receiving treasure.
