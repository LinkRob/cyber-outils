A '''Horsetail''' is an item introduced in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Horsetail.png|frame|left]]
Horsetails can be found in [[Petal Meadows]], where [[Mario]] must [[hammer]] a [[Candy Cane]] ten times to make a Horsetail pop out. Horsetails only restore three [[Heart Point|HP]], making them mostly ineffective. However, Horsetails can be cooked with a [[Turtley Leaf]] for a [[Healthy Salad]] which are both much better recovery items.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Horsetails can now be purchased in the [[Itty Bits]] shop in the [[Dotwood Tree]] for 18 [[coin]]s. [[Putrid Piranha]]s may also drop Horsetails when they are defeated. Horsetails now replenish 7 HP and cures [[Poison (status effect)|Poison]] which is a minor improvement from the previous game. However, they can be still used in recipes that make for better healing items such as cooking a Horsetail by itself for a [[Roast Horsetail]].
