'''Hooraw Medals''' are an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==Description==
They are gold coins depicting an icon of [[Big Massif]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
[[Mario]] and [[Dreamy Luigi]] are given one Hooraw Medal each time they defeat a [[Hooraw]], including [[Sorrow Fist]], [[Beef Cloud]], [[Thunder Sass]], and [[Heavy Zest]]. Once all four Hooraw Medals are obtained, the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] must present them to the announcer so that they can battle Dreamy Big Massif in battle.
