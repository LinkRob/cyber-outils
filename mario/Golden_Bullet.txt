The '''Golden Bullet''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Golden Bullet.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Golden Bullet resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Golden Bullet deals around 55 to 65 damage. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Honey (status effect)|Honey]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Golden Bullet as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it when the player completes [[The Hidden Bridge]], the secret level in [[Ancient Gardens]].
