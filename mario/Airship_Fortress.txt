'''Airship Fortress''' is the final track of the [[Star Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. It later returns as a retro course in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', as part of the [[Lightning Cup]], and in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
=== ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' ===
[[File:MKDS Airship Fortress Map.png|150px|thumb|A map of Airship Fortress.]]
This is the only track in ''Mario Kart DS'' that features [[Bullet Bill]]s as Hazards. These Bullet Bills are fired toward the racers, and upon impact, makes the character flip over and drop their item. The course only features one dash panel. Some wooden boxes can be hit and drop a [[Mushroom]] or a [[Banana]], similar to those of [[Delfino Square]]. The crates cannot be destroyed on [[Time Trial]]s or online races.

There are also [[Rocky Wrench]]es that pop up, and some flames to also serve as obstacles. There is also a cannon which launches the racers in a spiral tower. At the end, there is one side where it is open, and racers can be knocked off the edge and fall back a few places. This course belongs to [[Bowser]], and he is even used in the [[Hurricane]] as the [[Staff Ghost]].

This track is inspired by the Airship levels from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

[[Mario Kart DS#Missions|Missions]] 6-2 and 7-7 take place in this course. In Mission 6-2, [[Wario]] has to drive into fifteen [[Rocky Wrench]]es using a [[Super Star]]. In Mission 7-7, [[Bowser]] must collect twenty coins, all while driving backwards and not hitting a [[Burner]].

===''[[Mario Kart 7]]''===
This track appears as a retro track in ''Mario Kart 7'', where it is the third course of the [[Lightning Cup]]. Although there are hardly any major differences apart from the 3DS graphic enhancement, it appears that the Airship cannons now fire [[Banzai Bill]]s instead of Bullet Bills. Also, at the bottom of the track, it is now a reddish-orange color, possibly the sky or lava. In addition, edges of the tower at the beginning of the track are modelled in 3D, allowing players to race on top of them to avoid incoming items and Banzai Bills, at the potential risk of falling off the course, as well as the fact that the cannon which launches the racers is now a glider segment. There is also a glitch that only appeared in ''Mario Kart 7'' is at the part where in the circular tower if the player drives backwards after the player was shot in the tower, the beginning of the track will not be seen. The sky is slightly a different color.

[[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]] is used for the normal and expert Staff Ghosts on this course. The normal staff ghost uses him with the [[Zucchini]] with [[Slick]] tires and the [[Super Glider]], whilst the expert staff ghost uses his [[Standard Kart]] with [[Monster (tires)|Monster]] tires and the Super Glider.

For an unknown reason, {{Classic course|DS|{{PAGENAME}}}} is the only track of the entire game to not appear during the game's credits.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
Airship Fortress appears in ''Mario Kart Tour'', in the [[Pirate Tour]] update.

=== ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' ===
Airship Fortress returns in ''Mario Kart World'' as a retro track, now making its home console debut. It is placed in the Flower Cup as the last course. The race course has gone through several changes compared to its previous appearances; grind rails are placed throughout the track and the interior of the main airship is now converted into a Bomber Bill factory.
