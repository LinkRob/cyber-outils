'''''Too Hot To Handle''''' is a DVD release of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', released exclusively in Britain in 2008. It is specifically named after the "[[Too Hot to Handle]]," but it also includes four other episodes: "[[Mario of the Deep]]," "[[The Fire of Hercufleas]]," "[[Mario Meets Koop-zilla]]," and "[[Mario and Joliet]]." The cover to the DVD features artwork of [[Raccoon Mario]] from ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', though without the raccoon ears or tail.
