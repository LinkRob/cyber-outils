==Features==
Unlike most other levels, The Bullet Bill Express features two trains that can both be accessed.

==The Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': From the very start, move fast towards the star before it goes offscreen. You can use the [[? Block]]s to help.
*'''Green Star 2''': Destroy the Goomba Tower with Cat Bullet Bills to get the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': By the [[Warp Pipe]] in between crates, go in it to go on the other train. On that train, destroy the crate with [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in it whom reward you with a star.
