'''Sven''' is the fifth boss fought by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in the VS. COM mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]].

==Physical description==
Sven is a brown furball with arms and legs, wearing a yellow viking helmet, a pair of red gloves, and shoes.
