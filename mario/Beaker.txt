'''Beakers''' are a rare type of enemy in both the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] versions of ''[[Wario's Woods]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Beaker is a small creature with two faces on both sides of its body. One face is heart-shaped and features two large, blank eyes, while the other face is a saddened face with a different shape. In ''Wario's Woods'' for [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]], Beakers are shown to appear in a variety of colors, including cyan, gray, yellow, blue, red, pink, and green.

A Beaker's primary color is unclear, although their artwork depicts them with a dark green body, a light green, heart-shaped face, and light blue hands and feet. This color is used in the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version of ''[[Wario's Woods (Nintendo Entertainment System)|Wario's Woods]]'', but with a brown-colored face.

===Behavior===
Beakers constantly rotate to show off their faces.

==In ''Wario's Woods''==
Beakers are the rarest enemies, along with [[Scram]]s. Like Scrams, Beakers can only be destroyed from [[bomb]] chains.
