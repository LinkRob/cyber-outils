'''Orb Spaces''' are [[space]]s that appear in the games ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
Orb Spaces serve a similar function to [[capsule machine]]s from ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', as a character passes over one, they receive a random [[Orb]] for free. Like [[Star Space]]s, Orb Spaces do not count toward players' movement and cannot be landed on. On the last turn, players are unable to receive Orbs.

In ''[[Mario Party 8]]'', Orb Spaces are replaced by [[? Candy|? Candies]], which work in the same manner, except that they give [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candies]].
