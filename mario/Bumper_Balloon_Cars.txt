'''Bumper Balloon Cars''' is a Battle mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this [[mini-game]], all players must try to pop their opponents' balloons with their cars' sharp fronts. The player or players whose balloons have not been popped when the time limit expires are the winners.

==Controls==
* {{Button|n64|stickup}} – Move Forward
* {{Button|n64|stickdown}} – Move Backward
* {{Button|n64|stickleft}}/{{Button|n64|stickright}} – Change Direction

==In-game text==
* '''Game Rules''' – ''"Use your car's spiked front to burst opponents' balloons! Tilt your Control Stick up to move forward and down to move backward."''
* '''Advice''' - ''Tilt your {{button|n64|stick}} to the side to change direction. Once you master the controls, bursting balloons is a breeze!''
