'''Super Nuts''' are an [[Heart Point|HP]]-restoring item featured in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', and were replaced by [[Super Drop]]s in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' before appearing in every other ''Mario & Luigi'' installment.

==In the ''Mario & Luigi'' series==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
[[File:Nut MLSS sprite.png|frame|left]]
Using a Super Nut restores 40 HP for both [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], or 45 HP in the Japanese version. It can be bought for 80 [[coin]]s at either the [[Castle Town Item Shop]], but only if [[Mom Piranha]] has been defeated, or in the [[Little Fungitown Item Shop]]. Super Nuts can also be obtained in certain areas of the overworld, and are also dropped by certain enemies.

===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''===
Super Nuts function the same as in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''.
