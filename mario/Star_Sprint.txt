'''Star Sprint''' is a mode in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player uses the [[Nintendo GameCube Microphone]] to have their character sprint to the [[finish line]] at the end in the quickest possible time. The player can say "Run" to make their character run, and if they say it again, their character runs faster. Saying "Left" or "Right" causes the character to move in that direction. Saying "Jump" to makes the character [[jump]]. If the player needs help, they can say "Mario Party" to summon allies for assistance.

There are three courses: [[Meadow Road]], [[Dark Path]], and [[Magma Flow]], all of which have [[Dash Panel]]s and [[Metal Mushroom Orb|Metal Mushrooms]] placed along the way to help the player's character progress faster.
