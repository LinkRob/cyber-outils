'''Cat-O-9-Tails''' is a blue cat enemy appearing in the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' series.

==Games==
===''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''===
Cat-O-9-Tails make their first appearance in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' in the level [[Mudhole Marsh]] and final in [[Fiery Furnace]].

Cat-O-9-Tails appears uncommonly in-game and only appears in three levels in the entire game itself. It is also one of the few [[Kremling Krew]] members to not be a [[Kremling]] itself.

Its attacks include spinning around and chasing [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] while spinning them around, trying to fling them into some area such as an abyss, [[Zinger]] or another harmful area. They constantly spin until becoming tired out temporarily, allowing the two monkeys to have their chance to jump or spin/cartwheel into it. Cartwheels do not work when Cat-O-9-Tails is spinning.

==Trivia==
*Cat-O-9-Tails is one of the few enemies not to reappear in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''.
*Despite being called Cat-O-9-Tails, it only has seven. Its name is also based on the saying that cats have nine lives.
