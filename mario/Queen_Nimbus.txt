'''Queen Nimbus''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. She and her husband [[King Nimbus]] are the rulers of [[Nimbus Land]], and both of them are [[Mallow]]'s real parents.

==History==
During the events of the game, Queen Nimbus and King Nimbus were captured by [[Valentina]] and her forces and are locked away in a room in their own castle. Valentina then planned to have [[Dodo]] pose as Prince Mallow and marry her, allowing Valentina to become the new ruler of Nimbus Land. Fortunately, Valentina's plans were thwarted by the real Prince Mallow and his friends. Queen Nimbus and King Nimbus then congratulate [[Mario]], Mallow, and the others, and they open up access to [[Barrel Volcano]] so they can continue onward to retrieve the final [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]].

In the ending, Queen Nimbus is seen with her husband welcoming Mallow back into their family as the prince of Nimbus Land.
