A '''Golden Egg''' is a battle enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', dropped by a background [[Bandit]] in [[Somnom Woods]] if thrown with a [[Taunt Ball]]. Gold Eggs appear exactly as their name suggests, an egg made of gold.

==Battle==
Golden Eggs are never encountered on the overworld, and a battle of one is instead initiated by throwing a [[Taunt Ball]] at a [[Bandit]] if it is in the background. When the Bandit drops the Golden Egg, it begins to move and clink everywhere. To battle it, the player must hit the egg with their hammer when it is moving around, then it goes to the opposite side of [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] (unless it escapes). It has very high [[Defense]] stats but low [[Heart Point|HP]].

==Names in other languages==
Gold Eggs have their name literally translated for translations, with their Spanish name being "El Huevo Dorado", their Portuguese name being "O Ovo Dorado", their Italian name being "L'Uovo Dorado", their French name being "L'Oeuf D'Or", their Japanese name being "Kin No Tamago", and so on.
