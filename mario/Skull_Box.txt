'''Skull Boxes''' (also known as '''Torpedo Bases''') are a semi-recurring object in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
A Skull Box is a black artillery and variant of a [[Bill Blaster]] containing a gloved hand that individually sends out [[Torpedo Ted]]s at [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or another character. In ''Super Mario World'', a Skull Box's hand is four-fingered, but in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', it is five-fingered.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario World''===
[[File:Skull Box SMW sprite.png|frame|left]]
Skull Boxes only appear in one level, [[Soda Lake]], along with Torpedo Teds themselves. Like other obstacles, Skull Boxes cannot be destroyed.
{{br|left}}
===''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''===
Here, they are named '''torpedo tubes''' and were given a redesign. Torpedo tubes exclusively appear in [[Buoy Base Galaxy]], mostly underwater. Unlike before, a torpedo tube can now be destroyed by luring a Torpedo Ted right back into it.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' / ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
[[File:Skull Boxes and Torpedo Teds NSMBU.png|thumb|Mario swimming through several Torpedo Teds, which are launched by the Skull Boxes, in ''New Super Mario Bros. U'']]
Skull Boxes, as well as Torpedo Teds, make a reappearance, functioning the same as in ''Super Mario World''. Both Skull Boxes and Torpedo Teds appear in [[Larry's Torpedo Castle]]. Skull Boxes are absent from ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', although Torpedo Teds are not.

Skull Boxes were given a variation named [[Red Skull Box]]es, which launch out [[Targeting Ted]]s, a homing variation of Torpedo Teds.

==In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''==
[[File:Skull Box SPP sprite.png|frame|left]]
Skull Boxes make a reappearance, functioning identically as in ''Super Mario World'', but can now also launch [[Glad Torpedo Ted]]s. Skull Boxes appear in [[Subrella]] segments of levels, and both they and Torpedo Teds first appear in the [[level]] [[Wavy Beach 5-5]].
{{br|left}}
==In ''[[Mario Party 10]]''==
Skull Boxes appear in the minigame [[Blooper Blastoff]], where they launch Torpedo Teds.
