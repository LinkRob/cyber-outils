'''Para Mask Koopa''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. They are an alias of [[Koopa Paratroopa]] which wear a mask resembling [[Mario]]'s face and cap. They replace all Paratroopas during [[Fall]], and are the winged variant of [[Mask Koopa]], the Fall alias of [[Koopa Troopa]]s.
