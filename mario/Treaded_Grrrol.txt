'''Treaded Grrrols''' are a type of [[Grrrol]] that appear in ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. They can be found on certain routes. 

== Appearance ==
Treaded Grrrols largely resemble their "normal" counterparts, but the main difference between them and normal Grrrols is the tires on the edges replacing the usual spikes. Their faces also appear more metallic. 

== Behaviour ==
Treaded Grrrols have similar behaviour to regular Grrrols, rolling from side to side on the road. If they collide with a racer, they spin that racer out.

== Mission Appearances ==
In Free Roam mode, Treaded Grrrols appear in [[Mario Kart 64|N64]] [[Wario Stadium (Mario Kart 64)|Wario Stadium]], and appear in two missions. In both missions, they roll across the road and must be avoided as the player character races for the goal ring. In one mission, the Treaded Grrrols are accompanied by tire stacks.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Treaded Grrrols take their design from tyres, with their name referring to their treaded edges.
