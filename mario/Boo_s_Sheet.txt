'''Boo's Sheet''' is an item found exclusively in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', and it bears a resemblance to a [[Boo]].

==Effects==
Boo's Sheets make [[Mario]] or a partner [[Invisible]] for two turns, which causes all enemy attacks to miss. Boo's Sheets can be purchased at the [[Excess Express]] shop for twenty [[Coin|coins]] or bought from [[Charlieton]] for eighteen coins. Boo's Sheets can also be [[Kiss Thief|stolen]] from [[Hyper Goomba]]s, [[Hyper Paragoomba]]s, and [[Hyper Spiky Goomba]]s if they have them present during battle.

Boo's Sheets can also be made by [[Zess T.]] by cooking together a [[Point Swap]] and [[Repel Cape]]. It can be used to protect someone in danger, or better yet, when a person is in peril. This allows the one in peril to perform a Mega Rush attack, which causes massive damage to enemies without having to [[Superguard]].
