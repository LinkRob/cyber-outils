The '''Common Hall''' (called the '''Downstairs Corridor''' in the PAL versions) is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is one of the longest rooms in the game and it is accessed from the [[Foyer]] and leads to the [[Lab]], [[Secret Pocket]], and the [[Parlor]].
==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-2: Gear Up!|A-2: Gear Up!]], Luigi has to go through the Common Hall to get into the Lab in order to retrieve a gear that was stolen from one of the [[Slammer]]s.

During [[Gloomy Manor#A-4: Visual Tracks|A-4: Visual Tricks]] Luigi has to go through the Common Hall once more to get into the Lab to get the [[Dark-Light Device]]. However, the door to the Lab disappears due to the [[Spirit Ball]]s making it invisible forcing him to find another way around. After getting the Dark-Light Device and revealing a Key in a picture frame, Luigi has access to the [[Patio]] to continue his mission.

During [[Gloomy Manor#A-5: Sticky Situation|A-5: Sticky Situation]], Luigi has to visit the Common Hall once more this time to get rid of the spider webs that overflow the hall.
