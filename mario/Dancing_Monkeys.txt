The '''Dancing Monkeys''' are a group of minor characters in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
The Dancing Monkeys are a pair of monkeys who appear in [[Young Cricket and Master Mantis]]'s stage, [[Enter Young Cricket]]. They help [[Joe (WarioWare series)|Joe]] and [[Mona]] run the dumpling stand.

In [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s stage, [[The Multiplayer Test]], a doll version of a Dancing Monkey makes a cameo on a shelf in [[Toy Express]].
