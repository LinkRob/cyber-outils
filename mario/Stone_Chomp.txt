'''Stone Chomps''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Stone Chomps are a variant of [[Chain Chomp]]s composed of rock.

===Behavior===
A Stone Chomp only attacks by biting, which can be guarded against.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
They guard national artifacts in the temple of Dry Dry Ruins (the [[Diamond Stone]], the [[Lunar Stone]], and the [[Pyramid Stone]]) and are able to stun [[Mario]] and co. by falling from the ceiling or a wall design. Despite their appearance, they are said to be more of [[Chomp Rock]]s to honor the Chain Chomp species.

Despite only one Stone Chomp appearing at a time, two are always encountered in battle.
