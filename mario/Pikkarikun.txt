'''Pikkarikun''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Pikkarikun are small thunderclouds with an impish face, and they appear to have a lamp cord on the side.

===Behavior===
Pikkarikun have the ability to float and drop lightning bolts while flashing. A Pikkarikun follows after [[Wario]] if he is nearby. Pikkarikun cannot travel through walls.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
They first appear in [[Course No.17]]. Wario cannot defeat a Pikkarikun, and has to avoid them. If a Pikkarikun's lightning bolt hits an enemy, they turn into [[10-Coin|10 Gold Coin]]s.
