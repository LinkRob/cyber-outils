The '''Dynamite''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Dynamite simply appears as a single stick of dynamite that is red in color.

==History==
The Dynamite can be found in a purple chest in [[Carpaccio's Lab]] where [[Wario]] can find it in the uppermost room of the lab. The Dynamite is used to help blow away a wall leading to the fourth [[Wishstone]] piece after [[Count Cannoli]] sealed it up earlier.
