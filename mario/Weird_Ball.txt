'''Weird Ball''' is the [[Star Skill]] used by [[Birdo]] in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. It does not return in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'', in which Birdo instead has [[Suction Ball]] as her [[Star Pitch]] and [[Cannon Swing]] as her [[Star Swing]].

==Description==
Whenever Birdo uses Weird Ball while either pitching or batting, the baseball turns into a pink-spotted [[Birdo Egg|egg]] as it bounces from one side to the next, leaving behind a rainbow trail as it moves. If Birdo uses the move while pitching, it can trick the batter and mess up their timing due to the movement of the egg. Overall, Weird Ball functions very similar to [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Egg Ball]].

In Challenge mode, the [[Pink Egg]] item must be bought before Birdo can start using Weird Ball.
