'''Dawn Michelle Bennett''' (born December 13, 1992) is an American voice actress. She is the current voice of [[Nabbit]] since ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' replacing [[Natsuko Yokoyama]].

== Voices ==

* Nabbit (2023-present)

== Roles ==

* ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' – Nabbit
* ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' – Nabbit
* ''[[Mario Tennis Fever]]'' - Nabbit
