'''Shifting Sands''' is a golf course featured in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''.

==Description==
Shifting Sands is a desert course featuring rocky valleys, sandy seas, and ruins, including one that resembles the [[Stonehenge]]. Shifting Sands only has one water hazard—an oasis in Hole 2—and has the fewest water hazards out of any other golf course. Cacti can also be seen growing throughout Shifting Sands. 

Shifting Sands is possibly based on ''[[Super Mario 64]]''{{'}}s [[Shifting Sand Land]], given that both are desert-themed locations with a [[Klepto]] and a similar English name.

==In ''Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour''==
Shifting Sands is the second unlockable course and the third golf course overall. It can be unlocked by winning the tournament at [[Cheep Cheep Falls]].
