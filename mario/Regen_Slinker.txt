{{Infobox_Enemy|name = Regen Slinker|image = Regen Slinker.png|games = [[Luigi's Mansion 3]]|located = [[ScareScraper]]|species = ghost|subspecies = [[Slinker]]|leader = [[Hellen Gravely]]|attacks = grabbing}}Regen Slinker is a kind of rare ghost in [[Luigi's Mansion 3]], it is a form of [[Slinker]]. Slinkers appear in both [[The Last Resort]] and [[ScareScraper]], but Regen slinkers only appear in the ScareScraper's ten floor mod. Regen Slinkers have 250HP, more than the double life of a Slinker, they can regain HP when they are released.

==Appearance==

Regen Slinker is a form of Slinker. Although the two kinds of ghosts are similar, Regen Slinker has glowing pink body with yellow eyes and mouth, a regular Slinker has purple body, eyes and mouth. Regen Slinkers are all defeated and attacking in the same ways that Slinkers.

==Battle==

When [[Luigi]] reaches the fifth floor of the [[ScareScraper]] in the ten floor mod, the game will select a kind of rare ghost to appear. If Regen Slinkers appear, they will try to sneak and grab Luigi. When they get Luigi, they will hound him and deal damage to Luigi until they are blown away. To escape the grab, Luigi has to press ZL and ZR at the same time to blow them away with a [[Burst]]. The Regen Slinkers will restore HP when they are released or finished slamming before being defeated, so Luigi shouldn't slam them until they have 80HP or lower. They are defeated in the same way as Slinkers.
