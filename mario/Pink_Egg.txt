'''Pink Egg''' is an item in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
Pink Egg gives [[Birdo]] the ability to use her [[Star Skill]], [[Weird Ball]], in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]]. The Pink Egg is a pink-spotted [[Birdo Egg]] with a golden outline. It can be bought from the shop for 200 [[coin]]s if Birdo is on the player's team.
