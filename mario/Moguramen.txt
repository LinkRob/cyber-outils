'''Moguramen''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Moguramen are moles that wear a green and red mask, which a pink drill is attached to.

===Behavior===
Most Moguramen move back and forth on a platform, but some are within parts of the soil unreachable by [[Wario]]. A Moguramen leaves the soil if Wario performs a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] nearby. A Moguramen can use its drill to damage Wario.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
A Moguramen only appears in the underground areas of [[Wildflower Fields]]. Wario can attack any of the unprotected parts of their body to defeat it. If a Moguramen is stunned, their drill cannot affect Wario, who can also grab and throw a Moguramen. Upon being defeated, a Moguramen drops behind a [[Bronze Coin]].
