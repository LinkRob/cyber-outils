'''Hot-Air Hijinks''' is a game mode in [[Mario Party: Island Tour]]. It is one of the modes when you select the "Minigames" option.

=== Gameplay ===
Hot-Air Hijinks is played like this: four players race in hot-air balloons and compete in minigames. For each minigame that is won by a certain player, they move up with their respective hot-air balloon. You can choose if you want to play minigames until one player wins 3, 5, or 7 minigames total.
