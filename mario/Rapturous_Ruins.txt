|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Rapturous Ruins''' is a location mentioned in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Rapturous Ruins is a location of the [[Waffle Kingdom]] that [[Luigi]] has visited during his journey. In these old ruins, Luigi meets [[Cranberry]], the last of an ancient species known as the [[Luff]]s. He also encounters his newest ally [[Screamy]].
