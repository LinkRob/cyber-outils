|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Gale Force''' is a default special move [[Madame Flurrie]] can use in combat in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
If successfully timed, Flurrie will exhale a gust of wind with extreme force, which could possibly blow away weaker enemies. This is especially useful for ending enemy fights quickly without hassle. Most notably, this technique is useful for permanently blowing away [[Cortez]]'s weapons in the third phase of his fight.
