|race = [[Lakitu]]}}'''Flight''' is a character that is only mentioned in the game, ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Flight is a [[Lakitu]] who is friends with [[Lakilester]]. Flight also calls Lakilester by his nickname "Spike" and sends him a letter after the events of Chapter 6. Flight has heard that Lakilester turned on [[Bowser]] and is quite upset with him. Flight says he will not forgive Lakilester unless he and Mario can defeat Bowser. As Bowser is defeated at the end of the game, Flight most likely forgives Lakilester and the two may have become friends again.
