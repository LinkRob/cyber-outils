'''Minister Crepe''' is a character who is only mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Minister Crepe is the true villain of [[Luigi]]'s adventure through the [[Waffle Kingdom]]. Crepe is the one responsible for luring Luigi into the Waffle Kingdom by sending him a letter and even transformed the [[Chestnut King]] into a monster. Crepe did this knowing Luigi would collect the pieces of the [[Marvelous Compass]] hoping to rule the world with them. However, Minister Crepe was defeated by Luigi who was able to read his every move thanks to [[Princess Eclair]]'s compass piece showing Crepe's every move.

==Trivia==
*Luigi doesn't directly mention his battle with Minister Crepe as he casually brushes it aside as he told Mario he fought another villain but was quickly defeated. This was because, after Minister Crepe was defeated, the Chestnut King returned back to normal and would marry Princess Eclair, something Luigi was upset about.
