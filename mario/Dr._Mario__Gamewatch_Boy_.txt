'''''Dr. Mario''''' is a puzzle game for the [[Gamewatch Boy]] released in 1993. It is based on the original ''[[Dr. Mario (video game)|Dr. Mario]]'' game. The Gamewatch Boy version of ''Dr. Mario'' is the second and final game within the Gamewatch Boy line, following after ''[[Super Mario Race]]'', which released in 1992. Both ''Dr. Mario'' and ''Super Mario Race'' were created by [[Mani Industries]].
