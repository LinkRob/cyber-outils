==Appearance==
Chaz is a male [[Flip-Flopian]] that has a unique shape compared to most other Flip-Flopians (especially given he's one of few minor Flip-Flopians to have arms).

==History==
Chaz lives in [[Yold Town]] where it's said he hangs out with [[Red (Super Paper Mario)|Red]] even playing cards with him. Chaz can also give out information on how to use each of the hero's abilities.
