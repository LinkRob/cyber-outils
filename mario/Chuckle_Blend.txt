}}The '''Chuckle Blend''' is a type of blend in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Usage==
The blend can be obtained in the [[Starbeans Café]]. The player must have 25 [[Chuckle Bean]]s for the [[Starbeans Employee]] to create it. The blend boosts 4 (2 in the remake) [[Speed]] points.
