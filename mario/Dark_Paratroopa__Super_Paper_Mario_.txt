}}A '''Dark Paratroopa''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Paratroopas are stronger versions of [[Paratroopa]]s that are only found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. They attack by flying their shells into one of the heroes for damage. Dark Paratroopas are only found in room 13 of the Flopside Pit of 100 Trials.
