[[File:MonkeyRap.jpg|thumb|The Kongs in the DK Rap.]]The '''DK Rap''', also known as '''Da Banana Bunch''' is a rap song about [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]], [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]], [[Tiny Kong|Tiny]], [[Chunky Kong|Chunky]], and [[Lanky Kong|Lanky]] during the intro of ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' before the title screen shows up.

The DK Rap is very infamous for its considerably corny lyrics, particularly Lanky Kong's verses, and the fact that it contains the word "hell" in Chunky Kong's verses, It was later replaced by ''heck'' in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. This song also appears in ''[[Donkey Konga]] and later in [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]],'' The Latter which removed Lanky and Chunky Kong's Verses. A Short Version of the Rap is played in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. This short version also appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'', albeit the song is renamed "Getaway". [[Lanky Kong|Lanky]]'s verse is referenced in [[New Donk City]], where there is a company called "Lanky! No style. No Grace. Funny face." 

[[Funky Kong|Funky]] also mention the DK Rap in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''.
