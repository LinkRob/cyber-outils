'''Bundt''' is a giant, living wedding cake and a boss in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Bundt was made by [[Chef Torte]] for [[Booster (character)|Booster]] and [[Princess Peach|Peach]] at their wedding. Chef Torte goes to the wedding with the cake and sees Mario. He goes over to attack, but the cake begins to move. This cake is actually a monster and attacks [[Mario]].

===Battle===
In battle, Bundt mainly attacks by ramming at [[Mario]] and company. Bundt can only be defeated by blowing out all five candles off its head. In doing so, Bundt weakens down to the even stronger [[Raspberry]].
