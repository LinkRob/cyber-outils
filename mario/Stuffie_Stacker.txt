'''Stuffie Stacker''' is a mouse [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree: Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV]]''. It is playable only in [[Jamboree TV]] mode.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a playroom inside a house. Objects like a lamp and bookshelf are visible in the background.

==Gameplay==
The object is to build the tallest tower using plush toys. In 2 vs. 2 mode the goal is to create a tower taller than the opposing team.

To build a tower, the player move the cursor over one of the five types of toys available at the top, hold down the button to pick it up, drag it and, if necessary, rotate it, and finally place it on the table below. The usable plush toys include colored blocks arranged in various shapes, a [[Green Shell]], a [[Super Mushroom]], a [[Super Star]], and a large [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. To build the tower, the player has to arrange the plush toys correctly, taking into account their shape and size. Furthermore, the player cannot use other plush toys until after using the first five.

Once the 45 seconds are up, the mini-game ends. The tower that has been built must remain stable for three seconds. After this time has passed, the tower will be measured. Whoever has the tallest tower at the end wins.

In Co-op mode, playable on Carnival Coaster, players must work together to create the tallest tower possible. At the end of the minigame, players are ranked based on how tall their tower is.
