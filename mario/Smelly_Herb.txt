}}A '''Smelly Herb''' is an item found in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
When used, Smelly Herbs gives 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures poison. Smelly Herbs can be found in the [[Itty Bits]] shop in [[Flopside]] and can be used in a few recipes.
