'''Castle of Illusions''' is the third level on the eastern size of [[Music Box World]] in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. It takes place in a castle. Castle of Illusions becomes accessible once the player has collected the Lantern from [[The Big Bridge]] and the Magical Flame from [[The Frigid Sea]].
