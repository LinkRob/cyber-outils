'''Fiddler on the Hoof''' is an unlockable [[Rhythm Minigame|Rhythm]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The players sit on their horses. The metronome ticks and the red arrow points opposite where the player is facing.

{{clr}}
== Gameplay ==
[[File:SMP Fiddler on the Hoof.gif|thumb|left|200px|Gameplay of Fiddler on the Hoof.]]
The player's aim is to earn many points by pulling the reins at the right time. The pink rings indicate when the player must pull the reins. If timed correctly, the player earns three points. If the player pulls the reins late or early, they earn one point. If the player pulls the reins when the horse is not running on a pink ring or does nothing, the player earns no points.

{{clr}}
== Ending ==
The winner(s) do(es) their victory pose while the other players sulk defeated by their horses.
