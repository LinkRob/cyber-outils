'''Masahiro Sakurai''' is a game developer known for creating the crossover {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. He had previously created ''Kirby'' and ''Kid Icarus'', which are among the franchises represented within the game.

==Role==
He is known for deciding which fighters appear on the roster in various ''Super Smash Bros.'' games, a notable example of which is [[Piranha Plant]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. Likewise, he also has decided which characters do not become playable fighters, such as [[Waluigi]], despite fan demand. In a [[Miiverse]] post on February 27, 2014, when Sakurai revealed that Waluigi would be an [[Assist Trophy]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]'', he also added that trying hard does not mean a character will make it into battle.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20151207023153/https://miiverse.nintendo.net/posts/AYMHAAACAAAYUKlLqsgn_Q</ref>
