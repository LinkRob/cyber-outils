*''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''|time=100 seconds|located=[[Mushroom Kingdom]]}}'''Crooked Cavern''' is the second level in the [[Acorn Plains]] area in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
==Features==
The level features crystal floors that will rise and fall, similer to the platforms in [[Waddlewing Warning!]], except, some of the platforms can crush you if you're under or on top of them. It also has [[Warp Pipe|lots of pipes]], with [[Piranha Plant|Piranha plants]] that will sit on top, or pop in and out of the pipes and spit fire at you.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Before going underground, go into the secret passageway near the left side of the left pipe to get the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Go to the second [[Koopa Troopa]] and jump on it. Kick it on the left side to clear off a path of [[Brick Block]]s on the right and make a hole having the shell reach for the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': If you have the [[Flying Squirrel Mario|Flying Squirrel ability]], ignore the first exit leading overground and glide by following the trail of the coins over the large gap. After landing, jump to the coin.

==Secret Exit==
Once you collected the third Star Coin, ground pound the part of the floor left to it and revealing a hidden passageway leading to the yellow wrap pipe revealing the secret exit. The exit takes you to [[Cheep Chomp Chase|Acorn Plains-S:Cheep Chomp Chase]].
==Hidden Luigi image==
Ground pound the steps made of brick blocks near the original flagpole revealing a standard [[Luigi]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''
