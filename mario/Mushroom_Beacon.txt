'''Mushroom Beacon''' is a lighthouse located in the [[Seaside Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is where the [[Blooper (character)|Blooper]] trains to become a duelist and where the player can play the [[Hammergeddon]] minigame, and the reward for completing it is unlocking the minigame itself.
