The '''Storehouse''' is an [[Item Shop]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
The Storehouse is located deep within [[Bowser's Castle]] and is run by a [[Spiked Goomba]]. However, the Spiked Goomba got bored from watching over the Storehouse for a while. The Spiked Goomba, at first, doesn't sell any items to [[Mario]], but will once Mario shows him that he has coins to spend. The Storehouse sells some fairly useful items but at higher prices. Considering this is the final Item Shop that can be visited in the game, it is recommended to purchase as much items as needed.
