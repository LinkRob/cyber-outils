'''Ball Dash''' is a special move in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Ball Dash is an ability only usable by [[Goomba]] or [[Monty Mole]] in ''Mario Superstar Baseball'' or Goomba and [[Noki]] in ''Mario Super Sluggers'', with Monty Mole instead receiving [[Burrow]] as their ability in the latter game. The ability becomes active for either character while they are holding the baseball, causing them to run around the field at fast speed. This can be used in the outfield to shorten their throwing distance quick, or to make easy tag-outs in infield.
