A '''Giant Spiny Shell''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'' and ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
A Giant Spiny Shell is a large, red, spiky shell that is the larger counterpart of a [[Spiny Shell]]. A Giant Spiny Shell functions like a [[Giant Green Shell]], but has more durability. A Giant Spiny Shell continues traveling around the field temporarily, but for a longer period of time than a Giant Green Shell. Sometimes, after traveling a while, a Giant Spiny Shell does a sudden explosion, knocking out more characters in a larger radius. Unlike Giant Green Shells, a Giant Spiny Shell can turn against whichever team used it.
