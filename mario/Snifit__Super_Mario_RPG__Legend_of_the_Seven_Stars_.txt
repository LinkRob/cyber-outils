{{nihongo|'''Snifits'''|ブッカー|Bukkā}} are a type of enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are encountered in [[Booster Tower]], and serve as [[Booster]]'s henchmen. Snifits have the same name as their [[Snifit|parent species]], with which they essentially have the same appearance aside from wearing a black robe. Snifits also have a different Japanese name than their parent species.
