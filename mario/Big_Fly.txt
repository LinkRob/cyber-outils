'''Big Flies''' are an enemy in the [[Gnat Attack]] minigame of ''[[Mario Paint]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Big Flies are a larger variant of [[Fly (Mario Paint)|flies]]. They have a light blue body.

===Behavior===
Like the other flies, Big Flies buzz around. If the player takes too long to swat a Big Fly, it flashes red and attacks by launching several projectiles at once.

==In ''Mario Paint''==
During gameplay, Big Flies appear after some time of defeating regular flies. They are the first enemies to attack.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''===
Big Flies appear in the [[Fly Swatter]] minigame, and one is even depicted on the minigame icon.

===''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''===
