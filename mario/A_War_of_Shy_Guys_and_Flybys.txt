'''A War of Shy Guys and Flybys''' is the fourth level of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 6.

== Layout ==
The first battle contains 6 [[Paratroopea]]s and 2 [[Shy Guy]]s. The enemy captain is a Paratroopea.

The final battle contains 2 Paratroopeas, and 6 Shy Guys. The enemy captain is a Shy Guy.

After the final battle, the player unlocks another Shy Guy.

The player can replay the battle to unlock up to one Shy Guy each time.
