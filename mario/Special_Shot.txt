A '''Special Shot''' is a type of move that can be used by characters in various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' sports titles. It first appears in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
A Special Shot can usually be activated if the player taps out a letter or shape on the Touch Screen, depending on the Special Shot. The shot itself usually has the ball being thrown straight to the rival team's basket, but if it is performed outside the three-point line, another character dunks the Special Shot.
