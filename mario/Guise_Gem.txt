'''Guise Gems''' are collectible items found only in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
They can only be found in green treasure chests. Once a Guise Gem is obtained, [[Goodstyle]] will eat it, giving [[Wario]] to ability to wear and learn the abilities of a new disguise. To obtain a Guise Gem, the play will need to play a minigame of [[Color Me Happy]]. [[Mastery Gem|Mastery Gems]] are also similar to Guise Gems, but they give new and advanced powers to a disguise that Wario already has. There are seven Guise Gems in total.
