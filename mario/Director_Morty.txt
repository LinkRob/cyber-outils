'''Director Morty''' is a [[ghost]] who appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Morty is a blue ghost. He has dark brown eyebrows. His outfit consists of a blue movie director's uniform.

=== Personality traits ===
Morty has a penchant for making films. He feels that his [[Director Morty's Megaphone|megaphone]] is very important to him. When he sees [[Luigi]], he does not attack him. Rather, he sees potential as a movie star. Luigi, against his will, is then directed to act as a titanic monster fighting [[Godzilla (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Godzilla]]. Morty even promises one of the hotel's elevator's buttons that he found and is now keeping in his possession. Once Luigi wins, Morty remains true to his word.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Being a ghost, Morty can fly. However, he does not seem to be strong, as he had to struggle to get his movie out of the camera.

== Biography ==
Morty was a movie director prior to his death. When he died, he decided that he should inhabit his studio, which is in the [[The Last Resort]]. Eventually, he and [[Luigi]] met up.

== Appearances ==
===''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''===
Morty first appears bemoaning about how he lost his megaphone. However, [[Luigi]] gives the megaphone back, at which point Morty directs him to take part in a movie he is making. Luigi must then defeat [[Godzilla (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Godzilla]]. Once Godzilla meets his defeat, Morty gives an elevator button he found.

== Trivia ==
*Morty is the third ghost to be Luigi's ally. [[Madame Clairvoya]] is the first one since she gives Luigi the means to save [[Mario (character)|Mario]], and the [[Polterpup]] miniboss from ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' is the second one, having been reformed after the game ended.
