'''World 2-1''' is the first [[level]] of [[Desert Land]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It is also the first level to feature [[Pile Driver Micro-Goomba]]s. World 2-1 becomes accessible once [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] have completed [[World 1-Airship (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 1-Airship]] in [[Grass Land]].
