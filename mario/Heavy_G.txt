'''Heavy G''' is a status effect in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''/''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' ==
Heavy G is a status effect that affects dodging. If Mario or Luigi is afflicted with Heavy G, their jump is faster than normal.
