'''Paratrooper Plunge''' is a 4-Player [[coin]]-collecting [[minigame]] from ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Opening==
A yellow plane is seen flying through the sky, with the four players inside it. After it does a loop before the sun, the players jump out, each one with a [[balloon]]s on their back as they float swiftly downward. They pass through the cloud, and the game starts.

==Gameplay==
The goal of the minigame is to move around and collect as many coins as possible. Players skydive from an airplane with customized balloons strapped to their backs. There are individual coins and [[coin bag|bags of coins]] worth five coins each. Along the way, the characters encounter [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s and [[Fly Guy]]s, and hitting either type of enemy causes that player's balloon deflating a little. Hitting four enemies eliminates a player from the minigame. However, the player still keeps any coins they collected up to that point.

==Ending==
[[File:Paratrooper Plunge Ending.png|thumb|240px|The ending to Paratrooper Plunge]]
All remaining players float slowly onto an island, release their balloons, and do winning animations (or do losing animations if no coins were collected). Eliminated characters are not seen. If all characters were eliminated, they will be seen on the island, dizzy.
