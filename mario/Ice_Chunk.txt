'''Ice Chunks''' are objects in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|enhanced port]], ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
Ice Chunks are giant blocks of ice capable of pushing characters across the course and farther back the level. 

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. U''==
Ice Chunks appear only in [[Piranha Plants on Ice]], where they appear throughout.
