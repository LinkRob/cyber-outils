'''Goal Space''' is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
A Goal Space the final space featured on four of the boards, including [[Perilous Palace Path]], [[Rocket Road]], [[Banzai Bill's Mad Mountain]], and [[Shy Guy's Shuffle City]]. It is described as being the [[finish line]]. Other boards replace the Goal Space with a different space or requirement for winning, such as how [[Kamek's Carpet Ride]] requires having to land onto the [[Just-Right Space]].
