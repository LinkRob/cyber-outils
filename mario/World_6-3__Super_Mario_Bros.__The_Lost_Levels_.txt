'''World 6-3''' is the third [[level]] of [[World 6 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 6]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the twenty-third level overall in said game. The level was originally part of ''[[VS. Super Mario Bros.]]'' as World 7-3 and was imported into ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''.  This level reappears in ''[[All Night Nippon: Super Mario Bros.]]'' as World 7-3.

==Layout==
World 6-3 consists of the player jumping across small bridges while avoiding [[Cheep Cheep]]s that are jumping up from below. [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s are also placed above or on several bridge pieces. The two blocks in the level contain power-ups. Near the end of the level, at the second set of tree platforms, the player will have to bounce off of a Koopa Paratroopa to reach the top of said platforms, which are followed by the [[Goal Pole]].
