'''Coach''' is a yellow [[Cheep Cheep]] character featured in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
He works at the [[Mushroom Pool]] in [[Seaside Area]], where he provides swimming lessons. One of his students is named [[Cheep Cheep (character)|Cheep Cheep]], who is not confident in his swimming skills. In the quest [[Swimmin' Wimp]], player's character is put in a swim race against the Cheep Cheep, with the goal being to allow the Cheep Cheep to win while still putting in effort in on their end. Once this is done, the Coach proudly gives the player the [[Desktop Golf]] [[Gaddget]] as a reward.
