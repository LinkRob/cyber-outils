'''Funky''' is a special level found in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_remake remake] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. It is the last level in the [[Special Zone]], as well as the final level of the entire game, and as such, it is extremely difficult.

At the end of the level spelled out in coins it says, "You are a super player!" When you beat this level in its secret exit, the worlds outside the Special Zone change color.

== Layout ==
This level takes places in a grassy field at night, featuring various enemies such as all four colours of [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, [[Piranha Plant]]s, [[Paragoomba]]s, [[Sumo Bro]]s, and multiple varieties of [[Chargin' Chuck]]s.

This level starts at 200 seconds, and is the longest single-room level of the entire game. However, to make things easier, there are green berries found within the level, that if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] grabs a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to eat them, it adds 20 units to the time. The amount of green berries different from the international and Japanese version, in the former, there are nine, whilst in the latter, there are only three. So in total, Mario or Luigi can have up to 380 seconds to complete the level, or 260 in the Japanese version. This level also includes five [[Dragon Coin]]s.

This level can be made a lot easier if Mario or Luigi turn into their [[Cape Mario|caped]] form, have a Yoshi along, or both methods if necessary, in order to avoid most enemies and clear most of the gaps within the level, since there is a very high frequency in the amount of enemies.
