'''Green Hill Zone''' (also known as '''Green Hill''') is one of the main locations in the {{franchise|Sonic the Hedgehog}}.

==Description==
Green Hill Zone is located on [[w:c:sonic:South Island|South Island]]. It is the first level in the 16-bit game ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic the Hedgehog (16-bit)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]''. Throughout the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' series it has made numerous appearances in many games, including ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Adventure 2|Sonic Adventure 2]]'', ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Battle|Sonic Battle]]'', ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Chronicles: The Dark Brotherhood|Sonic Chronicles: The Dark Brotherhood]]'' and ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Generations|Sonic Generations]]''. In other games in the series, various levels including [[w:c:sonic:Emerald Hill Zone|Emerald Hill Zone]] from ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic the Hedgehog 2|Sonic the Hedgehog 2]]'' and [[Seaside Hill]] from ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Heroes|Sonic Heroes]]'' are based off Green Hill Zone, having the same kind of green grass and checkered soil.

==History==
===''Mario & Sonic'' series===
In the "Gallery" section in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'', one of the two backgrounds for the Count Master [[mini-game]] is Green Hill Zone; it appears in the second, fourth and final levels. The music of the Green Hill Zone can optionally be heard in the game, once it has been unlocked.

A background based on the Green Hill Zone appears in [[Dream Figure Skating]] in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for the first segment of the Sonic's World routine.

Green Hill Zone reappears as the setting for [[Hole-In-One Curling]] in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'', more closely resembling its appearance in ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Generations|Sonic Generations]]'', though the default music track is [[w:c:sonic:Splash Hill Zone|Splash Hill Zone]] from ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic the Hedgehog 4 Episode I|Sonic the Hedgehog 4: Episode I]]''.

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
{{main|Green Hill Zone (stage)}}
Green Hill Zone also appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' as an unlockable stage. It is automatically unlocked when the player unlocks [[Sonic the Hedgehog]]. In the background, [[Miles "Tails" Prower]], [[Knuckles the Echidna]], and [[Silver the Hedgehog]] often appear running through the loop. If the terrain is attacked too often, a section of it will collapse, but it can eventually repair itself. A [[checkpoint]] from the ''Sonic'' games occasionally appears on the stage, which temporarily spins when attacked and deals damage to opponents. The stage is also featured as a returning stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', as a default stage. The [[Final Destination]] form of Green Hill Zone is set on a flat platform; the checkpoints also do not appear. In ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', the unlock match for Sonic takes place here.
