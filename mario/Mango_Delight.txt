A '''Mango Delight''' is a [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Effects ==
A Mango Delight is made by having [[Zess T.]] cook a [[Keel Mango]] and a [[Cake Mix]]. It restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 3 [[Flower Point|FP]]. The [[Cookbook]] is first required to cook it. If she mixes a Mango Delight with a [[Mystic Egg]], this makes a [[Love Pudding]].
