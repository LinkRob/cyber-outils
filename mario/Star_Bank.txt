The '''Star Bank''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Star Bank Logo MP6.png|thumb|left|Logo]]
[[File:Star Bank MP6 close-up.png|thumb|left|The Star Bank from the outside]]
It is a [[windmill]] where players trade [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] for prizes and other valuables, such as new difficulty levels, Rare [[minigame]]s, and the unlockable playable character, [[Toadette]]. It is not a part of any [[board]] in the game, but it is accessed in the area where single-player or multiplayer mode is located. It is always open, but [[Brighton]] and [[Twila]] will immediately tell players that they do not have enough Stars to buy anything if the players do not have any Stars. There are 55 articles in total; 26 of them are purchasable with 10 Stars, 5 of them are purchasable with 20 Stars, 14 of them are purchasable with 30 Stars, 4 of them are purchasable with 40 Stars, 4 of them are purchasable with 50 Stars and 2 of them are purchasable with 100 Stars. To buy all of the items in the Star Bank, the player must have 1340 Stars in total.
