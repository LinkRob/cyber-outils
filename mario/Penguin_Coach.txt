The '''Penguin Coach''' is a large [[penguin]] who teaches smaller penguins how to swim during the events of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
In the [[Loopdeeloop Galaxy]] and the [[Loopdeeswoop Galaxy]], the Penguin Coach teaches Mario how to play Ray Surfing. He makes a reappearance in the [[Beach Bowl Galaxy]], where he teaches swimming classes to penguins. Mario is assigned the task of retrieving the [[Gold Shell]] from some mischievous penguins in the water. He will then compliment Mario's swiming.
