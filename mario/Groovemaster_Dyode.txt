'''Groovemaster Dyode''' is a supporting character from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. He is the owner of Club Dyode, which can be found on Jellyfish Island. Dyode also has a pupil by the name of [[Emmit]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Groovemaster Dyode is a red-skinned Concordian. He wears a white and blue disco suit. On top of his head is a speaker representing a pompadour. His glasses have white frames, and their lenses are opaque, obscuring his eyes.

=== Personality ===
Dyode loves attention, spending his time doing disco. However, when things do not go his way, this can cause him to become dramatic.

== History ==
Dyode makes his first appearance on [[Twistee Island]], moping over the absence of hair wax which, according to him, is necessary for him to get funky, which costs him the attention of a crowd. However, the Bros. can find a merchant named Spiralia who can hand over an item called MaxGlam Wax which can get the Groovemaster out of his blue funk. After he receives the MaxGlam Wax, a cutscene plays where Dyode dances impressively in a disco party setting, much to the amusement of his student Emmit and the crowd. This also triggers a minigame where the Groovemaster, his pupil, and the Bros. can play a dancing minigame. Upon completing this minigame, the Twisten Sprout blooms, granting access to the [[Lighthouse]] on the platform that [[Maykit and Billdit|Maykit]] is trapped on (whose linking grants access to [[Raynforst Island]]).

The dancing Concordian duo later crosses paths with the Bros. after [[Bowser]] disconnects Twistee from [[Shipshape Island]] during his assault. Dyode intents to perform his latest work to cheer up the island inhabitants, only to be ambushed by enemies. He is freed and the four dance again, allowing the Twisten Sprout to recover which reopens the path to the Twistee Lighthouse.

Dyode makes another appearance when the Bros. attempt to enter [[Club Dyode]] on [[Jellyfish Island]], where he invites the Bros. to another one of his and Emmit's dance-offs in exchange for the [[Club Dyode Ticket|tickets]]. If the Bros. succeed, Dyode reveals the club to be his property as Emmit gives the Bros. the tickets.

After [[Reclusa]] goes on a rampage throughout Concordia, coating it in [[Glohm]] and sealing many residents in lonely delusion, Dyode sets off on a tour to inspire more Concordians, stopping at Twistee Island and [[Merrygo Island]] and rallying the Bros. to co-perform. He ends the tour at [[Jellyfish Island]] where he challenges Mario and Luigi to a dance battle. If the Bros. succeed, the Groovemaster has a heartfelt discussion with his student on the meaning of being a "Groovemaster." This forms a [[Bond (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Bond]] strong enough to partially fill the Bonding Can.
