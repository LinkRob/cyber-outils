'''World 1-5''' of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (DS)|New Super Mario Bros]]'' is set in the sky, similarly to [[World 1-3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-3]].

==Walkthrough==
[[File:NSMB 1-5.png|thumb|160px|right|[[Mega Mario]] in World 1-5.]]
At the beginning of the level, there are several bouncy pink mushrooms, which [[Mario]] can use to grab the [[coin]]s above him. Then, there are some [[Question Block]]s, which contain [[coin]]s and a power-up. The first [[Star Coin]] of the level is just after this. It can be reached by jumping on the pink mushrooms. [[Mario]] will then jump off the pink mushrooms onto a green one, where he will encounter a red [[Koopa Troopa]]. After that, even more pink mushrooms will appear, but this time [[Koopa Troopa]]s are walking across, meaning that [[Mario]] has to be careful. Just past this, there are two pits and a red [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]]. Then, there are several dropping platforms and the second [[Star Coin]]. There is also a [[Warp Pipe]] to [[Coin Heaven]], which can be entered by using the dropping platforms. Inside, there is a big red mushroom which [[Mario]] can ride. The mushroom will move through many [[coin]]s, and in a mushroom formation of [[coin]]s, there will be the third and final [[Star Coin]] of the level. If [[Mario]] chooses not to do this, he will encounter two green [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, and then more pink bouncy mushrooms. Here, there are six [[Question Block]]s, and one of them contains a power-up. After two pits, [[Mario]] will find a [[Red Ring]], and he has to bounce on the mushrooms to get the [[Red Coin]]s. There is also a [[Goomba]] and a [[Koopa Troopa]]. After another [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]] and some more bouncy mushrooms, [[Mario]] will find four [[Brick Block]]s, and he can use the mushrooms to get a large number of [[coin]]s from these [[Brick Block|blocks]]. Then, there will be a [[Goomba]] and some more bouncy mushrooms, and then [[Mario]] will slide down the [[flagpole]] and exit the level.

==[[Star Coins]]==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' Near the beginning, the first [[Star Coin]] will be in plain sight. Use the pink mushrooms to obtain it.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' Use the dropping platforms to jump onto a smaller orange mushroom where the [[Star Coin]] will be.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' Use some dropping platforms to go up a [[Warp Pipe]] to enter [[Coin Heaven]]. You find an area with a moving orange mushroom and coins in the sky that you can collect. Near the end, the last [[Star Coin]] will be surrounded by other [[Coin]]s. Use the mushroom to get it.
