The '''1-UP Factory''' serves as the main souvenir shop of [[Super Nintendo World]]. The shop sells various exclusive ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-themed merchandise, including clothing, figurines, plush dolls, candies, and pins. Music played in the store includes Puzzle Plank Galaxy's theme from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', the title theme from ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', and the overworld themes from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''
