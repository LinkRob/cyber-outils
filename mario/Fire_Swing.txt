'''Fire Swing''' is the [[Star Swing]] of [[Mario]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces his [[Fireball]] [[Star Skill]] for batting in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', but Mario still uses the move while pitching.

==Description==
Upon activating Fire Swing, Mario swings his bat back and forth until the end catches fire. Mario then hits the ball, causing it to fly across the field while cloaked in fire. If a fielder tries to catch the baseball, they get burnt for a few seconds. Even if this occurs, the ball continues to travel. Regardless, a few seconds after the ball is hit, the fire engulfing it wears off, then the ball lands onto the ground.

Fire Swing is usually effective when attempting to make a homerun. If two fielders are close to each other, even if one gets burnt, the other one can catch the ball and therefore get Mario out. If the ball is surrounded by flames and hits an object, such as a [[Freezie]] in [[Peach Ice Garden]], the flames wear off earlier.
