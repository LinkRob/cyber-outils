A '''Pink Berry''' is a type of [[berry]] featured in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. If [[Yoshi]] eats two Pink Berries, he lays a [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] from which a [[Yoshi Cloud]] hatches, and the Yoshi Cloud drops ten [[Bonus Coin]]s that can be collected for a [[1-Up Mushroom]].
