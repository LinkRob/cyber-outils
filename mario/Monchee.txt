'''Monchee''' is an enemy in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''.

== Profile ==
Monchees are monkey toys with tails that [[Mario]] can use to get over dangerous obstacles. Mario can find Mochees in Mystical Forest. Monchees can harm Mario by touching him.
