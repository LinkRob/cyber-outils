A '''Mouse''' (サイレス) is an enemy which appears in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for the [[Game Boy]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Its name refers to the real-life animal, but the Mouse appears a lot different. It has giant ears and a spike on the top of its head.

=== Behavior ===
The mouse is usually found asleep, but it wakes up if it hears a character and starts to charge at them.

== In ''Donkey Kong'' (Game Boy) ==
Mice appear only in a few [[level]]s, where they are usually found asleep. Whenever it hears [[Mario]], the Mouse wakes up and charges at him, causing him to lose a [[1-Up|life]] if hit. Mice cannot be defeated because if Mario jumps on a Mouse, he hits the spike on the top of its head and loses a life.
