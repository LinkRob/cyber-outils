'''Amphibigun''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are green enemies that are attached on walls and attack by shooting bullets in a direction.

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Amphibiguns are only found in [[Allergia Gardens]] and do not pose too big of a threat as their bullets travel slowly. Any attack from [[Wario]] is enough to take them out.
