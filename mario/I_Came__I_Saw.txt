'''I Came, I Saw''' is [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' in the Strange folder and reappears as her microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to saw the log.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The player must press four times to saw the small log where a cuckoo is.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The player must press six times to saw the medium log where a big white afro is.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The player must press eight times to saw the large log where a man is doing sit-ups.
=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===

* '''1st level difficulty:''' A worm is sitting on a chair or toilet.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The woodpecker tries to catch the worm or the worm is on a date or sleeping.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The worm would be cooking, working out or in a pool which the water drains.
