'''Tiny Wario''', also named '''Small Wario''', is a recurring form of [[Wario]]'s. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'', and has gone on to appear in various ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' games.

==Description==
Tiny Wario looks like his regular form, but smaller without a hat, revealing some brown hair starting from ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''; in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'', he was bald. In ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', Tiny Wario wears a yellow singlet with a cyan W, blue elf shoes, and yellow gloves with cyan W's.

== In ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''==
After [[Wario]] falls in the final battle, he collapses into this form before fleeing the castle.

== In {{series|Wario Land}}==
===''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''===
Wario collapses into this state when damaged by an enemy. In this state, he can no longer perform a body-slam, and dies when he takes any further damage, but he can still throw coins and pick up enemies. Wario can revert back to his regular self with any type of pot.

[[Wizard]]s can turn Wario into Tiny Wario with their spells. This form prevents him from attacking, but allows him to jump higher. If he falls into water or gets hit by an enemy, Wario reverts back to normal.

=== ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''===
There is an object called a [[Shrinkbarrel]] that Wario can enter. Entering from the large end turns him into Tiny Wario, but this does not penalise him in any way. Wario can revert back to his regular form by entering through the smaller end.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''===
Tiny Wario makes his debut in this game. During the introduction cutscene for the level "Forever Form Baton," [[Wario]] gets his hands on [[Penny Crygor]]'s [[Pocket Bike]] after she mails it to his house. While riding it, he gets sucked into the machine and turns into Tiny Wario and gains hundreds if not thousands of clones. Tiny Wario and his duplicates raid the nearby strawberry farm. Wario assimilates his clones to get back to his regular form.

Tiny Wario and his clones can be seen partying on the intermission screen. Whenever a microgame is about to pop up, the clones in the centre move to the edges to reveal the form. If the player wins, some of the Tiny Warios will make a W formation. If the player loses, the Tiny Warios cry and flip upside-down relative to their original orientations.

===''[[WarioWare Gold]]''===
Multiple Tiny Warios appear in the [[Balloon Brigade]] microgame in [[Wario]]'s Touch League stage. The player has to tap their balloons. In the Wario Interrupts game, [[Wario Deluxe]] can call down balloons with Tiny Wario's face on them.

==Crossover appearances==
Tiny Wario appears in the Wario... Where? challenge set of ''Rhythm Heaven Megamix'', where he is depicted with his design from ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'', and he can be seen in Freeze Frame. He drives a yellow nondescript car.
