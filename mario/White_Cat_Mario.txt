'''White Cat Mario''' is a power-up exclusive in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It is a variation of [[Cat Mario]] that also grants invincibility. The player can access this form by scanning the [[Amiibo/Gallery|Cat Mario Amiibo]]. Doing this will grant the Player access to the [[Invincibility Bell]], which will give the player the white cat suit. The White Cat Suit has different designs depending on who wears the suit.

== Design ==

=== Mario ===
[[Mario|Mario's]] suit is primarily white, has yellow stripes on both sides of the face area, a yellow cap in front of the face area, and a bit of yellow on the end of the tail.

=== Luigi ===
[[Luigi|Luigi's]] suit is primarily white with yellow dots on both of the arms.

=== Peach/Blue Toad/Rosalina ===
[[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Blue Toad]], and [[Rosalina]] all have completely white suits.
