'''Corona''' is a [[Fire (element)|Fire]]-elemental move used by a few different bosses in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
By using Corona, a yellow orb appears on the screen and emits a ray of light before flying away. Corona can attack all members in [[Mario]]'s party, dealing rather high damage.
