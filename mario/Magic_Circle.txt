The '''Magic Circle''' is an object in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. [[Mario]] can use it to use his [[1,000-Fold Arms]]. Mario can not use the Magic Circle without [[Olivia]]'s help. The first Magic Circle appears in Peach's Castle, where Mario must use it to free Olivia from inside the castle walls. Oddly enough, even though Mario needs Olivia to use the Magic Circle, he still is able to use it to free her. A Magic Circle can only be used once, and often times you must find by hitting something with Mario's hammer, defeating an enemy, or by defeating a boss. Each of the [[Legion of Stationary|Legion of Stationaries]] drop a Magic Circle upon defeat. You must use their Magic Circle to destroy the Streamer that they were protecting. Magic Circle can also appear in Boss battles, and Mini Boss battles.
