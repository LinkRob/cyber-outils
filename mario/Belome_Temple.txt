|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Belome Temple''' is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a temple built in the honor of [[Belome]].

==Description==
Belome Temple is a large temple that is mostly golden in color. Many statues in Belome Temple also resemble Belome himself.

==History==
After passing through [[Land's End]], [[Mario]] and company head into Belome Temple. Mario must solve puzzles and get the correct fortune until he meets with Belome again. Mario and his team defeats Belome and they pass onto [[Monstro Town]].
