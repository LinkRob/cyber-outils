'''Rising Piranhas '''is the fifth level in [[Rock-Candy Mines]] in the game ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Features==
It is unlocked by completing [[Spike's Seesaws]] or [[Light-Up-Lift Tower]], and its completion unlocks [[Sumo Bro's Spinning Tower]].

==Layout==
The level starts in a mountainous area with two [[Stalking Piranha Plant]]s, two [[? Block]]s, and a [[Koopa Troopa]]. Two [[lift]]s on tracks above a pit are found, circling around platforms with Stalking Piranha Plants on them. A rocky area with three Stalking Piranha Plants and a Koopa Troopa is found, followed by a [[Big Piranha Plant]] on a lift. A group of three ? Blocks is found, with one containing an [[Ice Flower]] and the top one containing a [[Super Acorn]]. Another Big Piranha Plant on a lift is found, followed by two lifts with a Stalking Piranha Plant on them. Two more lifts are found, along with two Stalking Piranha Plants and a [[Big Venus Fire Trap]]. After three more groups of lifts with a Stalking Piranha Plant on them, the [[Goal Pole]] is found.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first [[Star Coin]] is found near the beginning of the level, under the first lift.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is hidden inside of a rock, surrounded by two Stalking Piranha Plants.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is found behind a Big Venus Fire Trap, which must be defeated in order to obtain the Star Coin.
==Secret Exit==
After the second Star Coin, the player must use a lift with a Big Piranha Plant on it to reach a rocky platform above. Luigi then enters an area with a Stalking Piranha Plant and the secret Goal Pole.
==Hidden Luigi image==
There is a large Luigi-shaped hole in the rock wall just before the [[Big Piranha Plant]] next to the secret exit in the ceiling.
