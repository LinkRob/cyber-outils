'''Fungi Frenzy''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Opening==
The players run into the first field of nine [[? Panel|panels]] arranged in a 3×3 grid.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to earn [[point]]s by [[Ground Pound (move)|ground-pounding]] panels containing either one, two, or three [[Mushroom (item)|Mushrooms]]. Before each round, the contents of each panel are shown for only about one second. The scoring solely depends on how many Mushrooms a character has collected, with each Mushroom being worth a point. The characters generally have to try and aim for the panels containing the most mushrooms first. It is possible for a character to ground-pound an opponent, temporarily stunning them. Once a round has concluded, one either all nine panels have been ground-pounded or after thirty seconds, the characters run through a gate to the next field. After three rounds, whoever has the most points is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Pad}}: Move
*{{button|wii|2}}: Jump
*{{button|wii|2}} (in midair): Ground-pound

==Flavor text==
