{{Nihongo|'''Slaparazzi'''|センターで写れ|Sentā de utsure|Be photographed in the center}} is a [[4-player minigame|Free-For-All minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The minigame is similar to [[Flash Forward]] from ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

== Opening ==
A red [[Koopa|Koopa Troopa]] stands on a stool while the players move around. The opponent hits their opponent and itself and the other two opponents move. The red Koopa shoots a photo.

== Gameplay ==
The objective of the minigame is to get the most points. The player may get three, two, one or zero points depending on their pose and position. The player can move around and they can punch or push their opponent. The player with most points wins.

== Ending ==
The winner(s) do(es) their pose and the three red Koopa Troopas shoot a photo of the winner(s) with their cameras.
