'''Charlie Day''' is an American actor who is known for his role as [[w:c:itsalwayssunny:Charlie Kelly|Charlie Kelly]] on [[w:c:itsalwayssunny:It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia|It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia]], [[w:c:thelegomovie:Benny|Benny]], a 1980-something spaceman in the [[w:c:thelegomovie:The Lego Movie|Lego Movie]] franchise and [https://pacificrim.fandom.com/wiki/Newton_Geiszler Newton Geiszler] in the ''[https://pacificrim.fandom.com/wiki/Pacific_Rim_(film) Pacific Rim]'' franchise. He voiced [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' and ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. 

==''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''==
{{Quote|Thank ya for callin' Super Mario Brothas Plumbin'|Luigi's New York Accent before it was axed by directors}}<br>[[File:Smbplumbing.ogg]]

Charlie Day originally voiced Luigi with a New York accent during production. However, the accent was subsequently axed when directors told him was too much like a “''Goodfellas''” gangster. In response to the change, Day said “Alright! I think you’re wrong, but fine!”<ref name="accent">Standford, Alesia (April 8, 2023) [https://www.dailymail.co.uk/tvshowbiz/article-11952811/Chris-Pratts-Super-Mario-Bros-Movie-accent-rejected-Tony-Soprano-similarities.html Chris Pratt says his first accent was rejected by The Super Mario Bros. Movie filmmakers because it sounded like 'Tony Soprano']. ''Daily Mail''</ref>

Remnants of takes with the New York accent can be found from the movie's promotional website ''SMBPlumbing'', where Luigi speaks in a voicemail.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/shorts/IlixjaAGAuk Calling The Super Mario Bros Movie Phone Number (Mario Bros Plumbing Commercial)]</ref> Day also spoke in a higher pitched New York accent (similar to Joe Pesci) regarding the movie in an interview with Jimmy Kimmel.<ref>[https://youtu.be/SnvYxE2Dh18?t=149 Charlie Day on Always Sunny Podcast, Playing Luigi in Mario Bros Movie & His First Commercial]</ref>
