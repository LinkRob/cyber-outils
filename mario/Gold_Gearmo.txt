The '''Gold Gearmo''' is a specific [[Gearmo]] who appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. He is located in [[Boulder Bowl Galaxy]], on the Boulder Bowl Planet, during the hidden mission [[C'mere, Goomba]]. As suggested by the title, Gold Gearmo wants to capture a [[Goomba]], and [[Mario]] must help him acquire one. In doing so, Mario receives a [[Power Star]] from the Gold Gearmo, and it goes aboard [[Starship Mario]].

The [[Space Storm Galaxy]] has a similar character named the [[Silver Gearmo]]. Like the Gold Gearmo, it appears on Starship Mario after Mario has completed its mission.

==Trivia==
*When the Gold Gearmo acquires a Goomba, he holds it up in the air in a similar fashion to [[Link]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' series whenever he obtains an item.
