A '''Maple Shroom''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:Maple Shroom.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', [[Mario]] can obtain a Maple Shroom by giving [[Tayce T.]] a [[Mushroom (Paper Mario series)|Mushroom]] and a [[Maple Syrup]]. Using a Maple Shroom restores five [[Heart Point|HP]] and ten [[Flower Point|FP]].

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Maple Shrooms reappear with the same effects. They are made by [[Zess T.]] in the same manner as their ''Paper Mario'' counterparts.
