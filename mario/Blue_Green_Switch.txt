The '''Blue Green Switch''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
A Blue Green Switch resembles a [[P Switch]], but is a darker shade of blue and depicts a lowercase "e," as it can only be brought into the game by scanning an [[e-Reader]] card of one. Once the Blue Green Switch e-Reader card is scanned, patches of [[grass]] with a [[turnip]] below appear throughout the main game. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can pluck the turnips from the ground and throw them at enemies, just like in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. A Blue Green Switch's effects do not apply to [[World-e]], although it features certain [[level]]s that already have turnips.
