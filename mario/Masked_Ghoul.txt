'''Masked Ghouls'''<ref>[https://www.nintendo.co.jp/character/mario/archives/land2/?lang=en English ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'' entry on the official Mario Portal]. ''nintendo.co.jp''. Retrieved August 13, 2022. ([https://archive.ph/xw7hA Archived] August 13, 2022, 13:51:45 UTC via archive.today.)</ref> (also parsed '''masked ghouls''')<ref>"''You can find Coins and masked ghouls between the potion bottles.''" - ''Nintendo Power'' Volume 43, page 50.</ref> are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. They are walking enemies wearing hockey masks with daggers stuck in them, a reference to {{wp|Jason Voorhees}}, the main antagonist of the ''{{wp|Friday the 13th (franchise)|Friday the 13th}}'' film series. Masked Ghouls appear in the [[Pumpkin Zone]]'s [[Bat Course|first]] and [[Witch's Mansion Course|final]] stages.

Functionally, they act the same as [[Goomba]]s, and are directly depicted as such in the [[Super Mario (Kodansha manga)|Kodansha adaptation]].<ref>Kazuki, Motoyama. [[Super Mario (Kodansha manga)|KC Deluxe]] vol. 19 - ''Super Mario Land 2: 6-tsu no Kinka 1''. Page 114.</ref>
