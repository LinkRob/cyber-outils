|name=Jump x2 Battle Card}}'''Jump x2''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Jump x2 is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s "Jump x2" attack. It is two of the [[Jump (Battle Card)|Jump]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied by an orange/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 15 coins (unpainted)

Sell Price: 8 coins (unpainted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Jump x2 is first seen in [[The Crimson Tower]], but can also found in [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Marmalade Valley]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Plum Park]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Lighthouse Island]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Toad Trainworks]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
