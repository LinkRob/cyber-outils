'''Papa''' is a character who appears in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'', released overseas as ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. His wife is [[Mama (Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic)|Mama]], his eldest son is [[Imajin]], and Papa has toddler twins named [[Poki and Piki]] respectively.

==Physical description==
Papa is a plump man with tan-colored skin. He wears a white turban, green clothing, and yellow pointy shoes.

==In ''Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic''==
Papa is one of four playable characters, the others being Imajin, Mama, and [[Lina]]. With his family, Papa has to rescue Poki and Piki from [[Wart|King Mamu]], who dragged them into a storybook. Papa is the strongest of the four, being able throw enemies and items a far distance, although his running and [[jump]]ing skills are subpar.

Papa is replaced with [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in ''Super Mario Bros. 2''.
