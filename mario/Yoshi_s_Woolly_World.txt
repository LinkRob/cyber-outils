'''''Yoshi's Woolly World''''' is a 2.5D side-scrolling game for the [[Wii U]]. It was developed by [[Good-Feel]], a game company that developed a similar game: ''[[w:c:kirby:Kirby's Epic Yarn|Kirby's Epic Yarn]]''. It is the next ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming game to not be in the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}, after ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. ''Yoshi's Woolly World'' also supports the use of [[amiibo]]s.

In 2017, the game received a [[reissue|port]] for the [[Nintendo 3DS]] titled ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.

==Story==
The story takes place on [[Craft Island]], where all the yarn Yoshis live in harmony. Kamek arrives with a sack and uses her magic to turn all the Yoshis into Wonder Wool but misses one (two in 2-player mode), who has to save the other Yoshis by completing levels and obtaining wool. After Yoshi defeats [[Baby Bowser]] on the first round, Kamek upgrades him to grow large by him attacking with huge yarns. After defeating Baby Bowser, the "''yarns unravel''" from him and it put backs the Yoshis together. Kamek was so mad with the Yoshis, so he carried Baby Bowser on his broom and protested against the Yoshis. The Yoshis cheered happily ever after. During the credits, he realized that Baby Bowser was awake, he tried to carry him, but Baby Bowser was going crazy. Kamek was shocked with Poochy appearing on the moon, dropped Baby Bowser and fled down to get him.

==Amiibo==
[[File:Maxresdefault-5.jpg|thumb|Pic of 1 of Many Yoshi patterns]]
Amiibo can be played after an amiibo is tapped onto the gamepad. The Yoshi will turn into the character version of the Yoshi, unless it's one of the few amiibos that will just turn into an amiibo Yoshi.
