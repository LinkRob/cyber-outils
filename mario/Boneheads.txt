'''Boneheads''' is a character in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. He is a skeleton who bears the appearance of a Japanese shogun. 

==In ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''==
Boneheads is featured in the plot for [[Kat & Ana]]'s stage. He possesses the prince or princess of the [[Diamond City Castle]]. Ana and a team of six ninjas attempt to stop Boneheads, but all of them fail, so Ana sends a letter to Kat asking for her help. Immediately upon receiving the latter, Kat and her dog, [[Paw the Dog|Paw]], rush to the Castle, where Boneheads challenges them to come to him on the top floor. The player must clear a series of [[microgame]]s as Kat and Paw progress along.

At the top, Kat also fails to take out Boneheads, although Paw turns into a sword that Kat uses to rip Boneheads to shreds.
