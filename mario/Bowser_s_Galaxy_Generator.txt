'''Bowser's Galaxy Generator''' is the final level of both [[World 6 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 6]] as well as the main story of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is not the last overall level because [[World S]] becomes available upon clearing the level.

==Stars==
===Bowser's Fortified Fortress===
Mario crosses the entire fortress, dodging lava pits, fire balls, [[Banzai Bill]]s, crushing walls, and many different types enemies in hopes are confronting [[Bowser]] for the final time. Upon reaching Bowser, the fight is played the same as the previous ones, only after his defeat, there is a part two in which Mario mush fling meteorites at Bowser while flying through space at what seems to be a very fast pace.

===Bowser's Big Bad Speed Run===
Mario has to complete the galaxy within the time limit. Bowser is not fought at the end.

===Green Star 1===
It is hovering above lava near the start, and it can only be safely reached using a [[Floaty Fluff]].

===Green Star 2===
It is found near the tilting platform with [[Yoshi]], somewhat out of bounds. It can only be safely reached using the [[Cloud Flower]] power-up or with the assistance of Yoshi.

==Other appearances==
While the location itself has not returned, the theme for Bowser's Galaxy Generator has also appeared in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]''. It is also one of the playable songs in the [[Mario Galaxy (stage)|Mario Galaxy]] stage of both ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
