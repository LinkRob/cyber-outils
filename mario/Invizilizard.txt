The '''Invizilizard''' is an enemy from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They inhabit [[Raynforst Island]].

== Description ==
Invizilizards are green chamaeleon enemies with tails curled into spirals.

== Behaviour ==
In the field, Invizilizards wander aimlessly until they spot an intruder, whereupon they charge while becoming camouflaged.

=== Combat ===
During combat, Invizilizards can attack by lashing out at their target with their tongue or charging, turning invisible during their charge attack. Both attacks can be dodged by jumping, and landing on the Invizilizard will damage the creature. However, getting hit by the Invizilizards during the charge attack causes them to remain invisible until something damages them.

[[Bumbleshoot#Variants|Parashoots]] may accompany Invizilizards in combat.

The Invizilizard is a Level 4 monster with 30 hit points, an [[Attack]] of 16, a [[Defense]] of 9, and a [[Speed (Initiative)|Speed]] of 16. Defeating one nets five [[Experience Point|Experience Points]] and 4 [[Coin|Coins]] along with possibly a [[Crafting Materials (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Raynforst Vine]], [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]] or [[1-Up Mushroom]].

== Variants ==

=== Invizilizard R ===
A desert variant of Invizilizard with white skin and yellow markings called "Invizilizard R" can be found in Allsand Island. These stronger Invizilizards attack twice during their tongue attack and turn invisible sooner during their charge attack. This version of the Invizilizard cannot be found on the field, but [[Shower Fish|Shower Fishes]] can be aided by the creature.

An Invizilizard R is of Level 16 with 90 hit points, an attack power of 57, a defense of 46, and a speed of 50, giving 66 experience and 25 coins when slain.

=== Glohm-infused Invizilizards ===
After [[Reclusa]] hatches and [[Reclusa World|wreaks havoc]], certain Invizilizards of both types contract [[Glohm]] and become stronger. These "Glohm Invizilizards" can transfer the Glohm virus to their victims if their target fails to counter their attacks, and will explode in a large shockwave of fire that must be jumped over when destroyed.

A Glohm Invizilizard is of Level 39 with 577 hit points, giving 794 experience and 120 coins when destroyed. A Glohm Invizilizard R is of Level 40 with 612 hit points, giving 848 experience and 126 coins when destroyed.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Invizilizards are based on chamaeleons, and their name comes from "invisible" mixed with "lizard."

== Nomenclatures ==
The name that Invizilizards get for the Japanese dub is ''Suketerusu'' (written スケテルス), from ''suketeru'' ("transluscent"; written 透けてる), which refers to their ability to camouflage themselves and turn invisible, and a transliteration of the Latin suffix -''saurus'', which refers to names of dinosaurs and extinct reptiles. Chinese calls them ''Tòuyǐn Guài'' (written 透隐怪), from ''tòuyǐn'' ("invisible") and ''guài'' ("monster"; written 怪). Spanish translates their name as ''Ninguana'' (a combination of ''ningún'' ("none") and ''iguana'' ("iguana")). In Portuguese, the creature is called ''Invisirréptil'', from "invisible" (or its Portuguese translation, ''invisível'') and ''réptil'' ("reptile"). In Italian, their name is ''Trasparonte'', a portmanteau of ''transparente'' ("transparent," "see-through") and ''camaleonte'' ("chamaeleon"). Their French name, ''Cachéléon'', comes from ''cacher'' ("to hide") and ''caméléon'' ("chamaeleon"). In Korean, they are called ''Seutelle-on'' (written 스텔레온), a portmanteau of Korean transliterations of "stealth" (''seutelseu''; written 스텔스) and "chamaeleon" (''kamelleon''; written 카멜레온). In German, Invizilizards are called ''Vaschmäleon'', from ''Chamäleon'' ("chamaeleon").
