''Not to be confused with [[Luigi Bros.]].''

'''''Super Luigi Bros'''.'' is a version of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' included in [[NES Remix 2]] where you have to traverse levels from right to left instead of left to right. You can only play as [[Luigi]] in this version. [[Mario]] can only be seen at the end of the game, next to [[Princess Peach]].
