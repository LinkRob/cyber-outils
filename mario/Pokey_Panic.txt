'''Pokey Panic''' is the fifth level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 29. 

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains 6 [[Spiny|Spinies]] and 2 [[Goomba Tower]]s. The enemy captain is a Spiny, and he knows [[Chance]] and [[Call for Backup]]. Call for Backup calls a [[Para-Beetle]].

The 2nd battle contains 2 Spinies and 6 [[Pokey]]s. The enemy captain is a Pokey, and he knows Chance, [[Deny]], and Call for Backup. Call for Backup call a Para-Beetle or a [[Lakitu]].

The final battle contains 3 Spinies, 2 Pokeys, and 3 Goomba Towers. The enemy captain is a Goomba Tower and it knows [[Rally]] and Chance and Call for Backup. Call for Backup calls a Lakitu or a Para-Beetle.
