'''Swordfighter Peach''' is a power-up in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''

==Overview==
Swordfighter Peach involves [[Princess Peach]] dressing in a uniform reminiscent of an English swordfighter and using a rapier for swordfighting combat.

===Physical appearance===
In her Swordfighter form, Peach wears a gold-lined, blue redingote with gold epaulettes and burgundy interiors, a matching musketeer hat and knee-length cuffed boots with golden tips. Under the jacket are a burgundy gilet and tight white pants; hanging on her chest is a drape of white fabric with a smaller version of her usual brooch pinned on, and on her hat are a red rose and two feathers, the largest one being pink with pale yellow barbs and the smallest being white. She wears her hair in a low ponytail and has red earrings instead of her usual blue ones.
