'''Glad Bob-omb''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
Glad Bob-ombs are a variant of Bob-omb imbued with the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] [[vibe]]. Glad Bob-ombs have a similar appearance, except they are seen with a smiling expression.

===Behavior===
Glad Bob-ombs have the same abilities as Bob-ombs, except they can also hop. Glad Bob-ombs can be seen singing while hopping. A Glad Bob-omb can explode when [[Princess Peach|Peach]] comes near one, releasing harmless confetti out of itself. A Glad Bob-omb is also the result of when a [[Glad Parabomb]] lands onto the ground.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Glad Bob-ombs are first encountered in [[Fury Volcano 4-2]].
