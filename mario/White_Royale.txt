The '''White Royale''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

==Description==
The White Royale is a white variant of the [[Royale]], with a maroon-colored seat. The Kart is equipped with the [[Quickshaw]]'s tires.

==In ''Mario Kart Tour''==
[[File:MKT Peach Vacation White Royale.jpg|thumb|200px|right|Peach (Vacation) in the White Royale.]]
The White Royale was introduced in the [[Paris Tour (2019)|2019 Paris Tour]]. This kart increases the duration of Rocket Boosts and the points earned for performing them. This is the signature kart for [[Peach (Vacation)]].
