}}The '''Thunder Palace''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It is a temple where Luigi learnt the [[Thunderhand]] technique.

==History==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] entered the this temple while they were on Oho Oasis. However, both of them can go pass the obstacle, so he did a [[Luigi Dunk]] to go under. A Thunder God appeared and warned him to leave because they have no business to each other. After he left, Luigi rouched the blue orb, he was electrocuted which resulted his hand to be fused with thunder. He angered but realized Luigi survived the spark, the Thunder God decides to help him with his [[Thunderhand]] technique. After mastering it, he left and Luigi continued his adventure with Mario.
