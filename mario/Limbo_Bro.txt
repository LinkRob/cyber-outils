'''Limbo Bro''' is a [[Hammer Bro]] variant featured as an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. These enemies can be encountered in [[Guffawha Ruins]].

Limbo Bros. have a stronger variant named [[Elite Limbo Bro]]s., which appear in [[Teehee Valley]]. They were originally named Limbo Bros. in the original version but were renamed to Elite Limbo Bros. in the remake for differentiation.

==Physical description==
A Limbo Bro has a body resembling that of a [[Sledge Bro]]'s. They wear a blue helmet and face paint, and their idle animation has them do a dancing hip movement. Elite Limbo Bros. themselves resemble a Limbo Bro, but can be distinguished from their green skin, black helmet, and their torch having a purple flame instead of an orange one.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ==
Limbo Bros. do not appear on the map but accompany [[Elite Chuck Guy]]s. They have two methods of attacks. The first method of attack is doing a limbo under the torch and fly to the target. The second method of attack is doing a limbo under a torch and striking a fire with its torch.

The first method of attack can be blocked if the target uses his [[Hammer]] to strike the Limbo Bro's stomach as it flies at him. The second method of attack can be dodged with a jump when it strikes a fire on [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]. The Limbo Bro is healed by Mario's [[Firebrand]], but they are vulnerable to Luigi's [[Thunderhand]].

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'' ==
Limbo Bros. have their skin changed to a more orange tone.
