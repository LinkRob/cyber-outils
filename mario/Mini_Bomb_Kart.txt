'''Mini Bomb Kart''' is a hazardous object in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. They can appear Battle Mode if there are either three or four players. Here, if a driver loses all of their [[Balloon (Mario Kart series)|balloons]], they turn into a Mini Bomb Kart. While they remain disqualified, the Mini Bomb Kart can be controlled to crash into another driver, creating an explosion and causing that driver to lose a balloon. If a player during Grand Prix mode gets 4th or less place, this object will send the losing player up in the air and they'll give a disappointed reaction to their failure to get a trophy.
