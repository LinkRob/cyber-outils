'''Meteor Shot''' is the [[Special Shot]] of [[Black Mage]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Black Mage initiate Meteor Shot by tapping out a star shape on the Touch Screen twice. Meteor Shot is Black Mage's counterpart to [[White Mage]]'s [[Holy Shot]].

Meteor Shot begins with Black Mage creating a large purple rune on the ground. He levitates the ball, which starts glowing purple, in each of the four cardinal directions, then he spins and casts the ball straight up into the air. The screen gets darker and flaming meteors streak down from the sky, including a big meteor that contains the ball. The big meteor slams into the basket, creating a large plume of fire that can knock over nearby characters.

If Black Mage uses Meteor Shot from outside the three-point line, he uses his wand to shoot the ball. The ball takes on a red glow, with a tangled red ribbon of energy following it. Like other Special Shots, using Meteor Shot from outside the three-point line requires a teammate to grab the ball and dunk it. The plume of fire still forms if a teammate dunks the ball.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The "Meteor" of Meteor Shot refers to the most powerful [[w:c:finalfantasy:Black Magic|Black Magic]] spell from the ''[[Final Fantasy]]'' franchise, [[w:c:finalfantasy:Meteor (ability)|Meteor]], which first appears in ''[[w:c:finalfantasy:Final Fantasy III|Final Fantasy III]]''.
