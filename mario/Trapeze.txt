'''Trapezes''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==In ''Super Mario 3D World''==
A playable character can swing on a trapezium by jumping on to it and then holding the jump button. Letting go of the trapezium is self-explanatory, as you just need to let go of the jump button.
