'''Basic Space''' is a common space found on duel boards in the game ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

== Description ==
Basic Spaces are found throughout all of the Duel Mode boards. When a player lands on a Basic Space, it will transform into the symbol of the player and becomes the owner of it. Landing on a Basic Space after becoming the owner of it will result in the owner receiving coins. The amount of coins earned depends on the turns that passed by (it starts with 3 coins but can go as high as 10 coins). When a different player lands on a Basic Space owned by another player, then they will lose coins and those coins will be given to the owner of that space. The only way to avoid losing coins on a Basic Space to another player is to have a [[Toad]] in your party.
