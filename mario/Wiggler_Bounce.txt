(Rage)}}'''Wiggler Bounce '''is a '''​Stage Boss battle mini-game''' in ''​[[Mario Party 9]]''​. In 'Solo' mode, Wiggler appears in [[Toad Road]] as the boss.
==​Gameplay==
In this game, you should pound on [[Wiggler|Wiggler's]] body marks. Two marks are worth differently:
*Purple Marks are worth +1 point.
*Red Marks are worth +2 points.
Beware of those [[Piranha Plant]]s; they take away 1 point from you.
===​''​Rage''===
​When Wiggler rages, it runs very fast from letting you aim at your spot and more Piranha Plants pop out.

==​Controls==
'''Wii Remote '''​should be held horizontally.
*{{System Buttons|Wii|Pad}}-Move
*{{System Buttons|Wii|2}}-Jump and Pound (while on mid-air)
