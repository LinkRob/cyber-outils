'''False Link''' is a [[False Character]] and counterpart of [[Link]] who appears in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]]. He first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', as one of the enemies fought in [[The Great Maze]] of [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]].

== Physical description ==
False Link is the same as [[Link]] but he is dark and colored green all over.

== Biography ==
The Great Maze is populated by dark clones of the fighters. There are clones for almost every character in the game. They must all be defeated including False Link, along with every boss of Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary, to open the door leading to [[Tabuu]] in order to save the Nintendo world.
