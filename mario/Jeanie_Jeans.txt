The '''Jeanie Jeans''' is an unused clothing item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It would only been usable by [[Luigi]], and equipping it would have increased Luigi's [[Defense]] rating by 60 (or at 65 in the Japanese version) as well as provide the [[DEF-Up]] special effect.
