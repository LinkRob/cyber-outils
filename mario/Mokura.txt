'''Mokura''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is also known as '''Formless''' in its original form when starting the battle, or as '''Mokuka''' in its line of dialogue on-screen.

Mokura is a strange, gaseous enemy that can be found only in [[Land's End]] and the [[Belome Temple]]. It is seen as a white puff of smoke on the overworld that appears in different areas of Land's End.

==Battle==
In battle, Mokura takes an invisible form known as Formless making it immune to physical attacks. The Mokura is also capable of using mainly electric spells such as [[Static E!]] and [[Bolt]].

===Strategy===
Even though Mokuras are immune to physical attacks, they are not immune to special attacks and can be easily taken out by almost any one (except for any of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump]] techniques).
