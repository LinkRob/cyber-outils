The '''Standard DB''' is [[Dry Bones]]' standard [[kart]] which appears to have a tan color in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. Dry Bones has two other karts in this game, being the [[Dry Bomber]] and the [[Banisher]].

It has very high acceleration and handling stats, but a low top speed When Dry Bones is the driver, the acceleration and handling stats are high. Also, because of this high acceleration, after getting hit and getting back under control, it quickly reaches its low top speed again. This kart is often used of snaking, because of its high [[Acceleration]], high [[Handling]], low [[Drift]] cornering, long [[Mini-Turbo]]s, and good [[Off-Road]].

It shares its drift with [[Wario]]'s [[Standard WR]] and shares its items stat with all other characters' standard karts.

He has a new kart called [[Standard Kart S]] replace this kart in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' which is gray color instead of tan.
