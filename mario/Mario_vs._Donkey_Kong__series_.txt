The '''''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series''' is a continuation of the {{series|Donkey Kong}} and a part of the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}} The series brings back [[Pauline]], along with the rivalry between [[Mario]] and [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]. The first installment, ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'', follows the same format as ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]], but all subsequent ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' installments focused on controlling the [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Marios]] themselves.
