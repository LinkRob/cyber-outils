'''Princess Peach Toadstool''' is one of the main characters of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]], serving as the deuteragonist of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' and the tritagonist of ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. She brings herself along for the adventure and assists Mario in an attempt to stop Bowser. She plays a bigger role in the film than just being a typical damsel-in-distress, which is depicted in some of her appearances in the Mario franchise. [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] claims that Peach is his "true love" and wants to marry her by force, and make her his [[wikipedia:Queen consort|queen]], but only her own true love was the [[Mario (Illumination)|mustachioed human himself]].

Peach is depicted as a confident leader who is willing to protect her kingdom from Bowser.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
[[File:TSMBM Size Chart.png|thumb|left|Size chart of Peach compared with other characters.]]
Princess Peach is based on her [[Princess Peach|video game counterpart]]. Her dress attire, crown and jewellery are realistic. Also, her eyebrows are distinctly longer than her video game counterpart and she wears leggings that extend to the top of her feet which change color depending on what color of dress she is wearing.

One of Peach's outfits is based on her biker outfit from the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. Her hair is made into a ponytail while in the outfit and a matching [[helmet]] is worn.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TnGl01FkMMo|title=The Super Mario Bros. Movie|}}</ref>

Her form as [[Fire Mario|Fire Peach]] retains her outfit and hairstyle, however, the pink dress is replaced with white and red colors.

Peach wore a wedding gown, based on the same outfit from ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. However, it has a hint of pink on it.

Her ice form retains her wedding outfit and hairstyle, however, the coloring of the dress is changed to blue with a hint of light blue on it.

Peach is taller than [[Koopa Troopa]], [[Koopa Paratroopa]], Luigi, Mario, [[Kamek (Illumination)|Kamek]] but shorter than Bowser.

=== Personality traits ===
{{Q|I will not let [[Bowser (Illumination)|him]] hurt you. We are going to stop Bowser.|Princess Peach to [[Toad (species)|Toads]], ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]}}
Peach is a charismatic, capable and a confident leader. When a threat to her kingdom such as Bowser arrives, she will stop at nothing to defend it. Despite her qualities of a leader, she relies and helps her allies such as giving [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] the access to [[Super Bell]] to beat [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]]. In return, her allies help Peach when needed such as Toad giving Peach the [[Ice Flower]] to freeze [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]. Unlike in the games, Peach didn't seem to have much faith in Mario, most likely due to being a foreigner from another world, until she sees his endless determination, and desire to never give up against the odds. This is shown in when they first met, to the battle against Donkey Kong. Her respect and admiration for the plumber grows over the course of the film. In addition, she is not submissive when Bowser forces her to marry him, instead fighting back the first chance she gets.

== Biography ==
Peach was first born from an unknown planet and lived with her sister, [[Rosalina (Illumination)|Rosalina]] by magical stardust. When [[Evil Forces|evil forces]] tried to capture them, trying to get power for themselves, Rosalina had to send Peach to safety forcing them to be seperated from each other, and Peach fell down a [[Mysterious Pipe|Mysterious Warp Pipe]], and was transported to the [[Mushroom Forest (Illumination)|Mushroom Forest]] at the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] in the [[Mushroom World (Illumination)|Mushroom World]] continent in a strange new world; the [[Mushroom Earth (Illumination)|Mushroom Earth]], where Mushroom people, known as [[Toad's species (Illumination)|Toads]] would find her. And they would adopt the young girl, and raise her in the Mushroom Kingdom, like one of their own. When she grew up in her adulthood, she thought she was not good at the [[Training Course (Illumination)|Training course]] at a Castle; worse than Mario before she met up with him, but she was able to complete it after so many tries. Long after completing the course, the [[Toad General]] crowned her as the Mushroom Kingdom's princess. At some point, Peach has a serious hatred on a monster; the Koopa King, [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]], because of his depraved actions, but this still makes him love her all the more, and he will try and get her to marry him by force, once and for all.

== Behind the scenes ==
=== Creation and development ===
In ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', Princess Peach was supposed to be a playable character and a "more powerful princess". This was changed for Princess Peach to be the damsel-in-distress rescued by Mario. One of the conversations with Peach involved was to make her have her own story and to be a more powerful princess which the developers tried to push further into the movie.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://youtu.be/-q62SLpZgTc|title=Shigeru Miyamoto Explains Why This 'Super Mario Bros.' Movie Lives Up to Fan Expectations|work=TheGamer|author=Variety|date=2023-04-04|accessdate=2023-05-01}}</ref>

=== Voice ===
[[Anya Taylor-Joy]] portrayed Princess Peach. In an interview, Taylor-Joy praised how cool Peach is and the fact she is going to be a "role-model that kids can have" and she left Taylor-Joy inspired. She was impressed and excited by the fact the filmmakers, Nintendo, Illumination "were all on the same page about where [they] wanted Princess Peach's direction to go".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gamingbible.com/features/anya-taylorjoy-interview-powerful-peach-makes-more-sense-894271-20230403|title=Anya Taylor-Joy interview: a powerful Princess Peach just “makes more sense”|date=2023-04-03|accessdate=2023-04-14}}</ref>

Peach and Bowser had a one-sided relationship, Bowser loves Peach as he expresses his love for her in "Peaches" but she does not love him back.

Peach had a goal to stop Bowser as she also has a cardboard cut-out that looks like Bowser in her obstacle course as a target.

Despite Peach not loving him back, Bowser intervened the Mushroom Kingdom forcing a proposal to Peach. Knowing Toad was tortured, Peach reluctantly agreed to be his "wife" in his wedding. Aware that he was planning to publicly burn the prisoners in the lava, she confronted Bowser telling him that she will not marry a "monster" like him.

Before Peach shrank Bowser with a Mini Mushroom, Bowser begged Peach to give him a chance which she refused. Toad then followed up with putting Bowser inside the jar.

===Mario===
{{Quote|We have a long journey ahead of us, mustache.|Peach to Mario}}
First meeting each other in Princess Peach's Castle, Peach was amazed to see another human. Knowing he is new to the Mushroom Earth, she acts as a mentor and romantic friend of Mario being supportive of him in his struggles.

Struggling to complete her obstacle course. Despite Mario nearly reaching the end after many attempts, Peach focused on his improvements and rewarded him. In the Jungle Kingdom, Mario struggled to fight Donkey; Peach helped him by giving him a Super Bell to transform into Cat Mario helping him defeat Donkey Kong.

While Peach was forced in a wedding with Bowser, Mario came to her aid crashing the wedding.

Also, before Mario finished the reconstruction of the Mushroom Castle by placing the flag on the top, Peach gave Mario a kiss in his cheek.

===Toad===
{{Quote|I said I wouldn't let anyone hurt you.|Toad}}
Peach has known Toad since she was a princess of the Mushroom Kingdom. She took him to their travels to the Jungle Kingdom. When Bowser forced a marriage on Peach while torturing Toad, Peach reluctantly agreed to Bowser's proposal for Kamek to stop torturing Toad. Before Bowser's wedding started, Toad helped Peach smuggle an Ice Flower in a boquet to surprise Bowser when she transforms into her ice form.

===Luigi===

Peach cared for Mario's brother, Luigi, because Mario needed Peach's help on rescueing him. Luigi told the princess that bravery is his thing, after defeating Bowser and saving Broklyn with the help of his brother together.

== Trivia ==
[[File:Fire Princess Peach Artwork - Super Mario 3D World.png|thumb|right|Fire Peach in ''Super Mario 3D World''.]]
* In video games, Princess Peach's fire form has her hair tied up into a ponytail as depicted in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. However, in this movie and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', Peach retains her normal hairstyle when she transforms into her fire version.
* Princess Peach has not yet used the Ice Flower in her playable appearances, making this movie her first time using it.
** [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] also didn't use an Ice Flower once in the film, unlike Peach.
* Peach's wedding gown is based on the same wedding gown she wore in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''; however, rather than wearing [[Tiara]] on her head, Peach wears her golden crown. Also, she wears her hair down and instead of wearing her hair up.
* Peach's biker outfit is based on the same biker outfit in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''Mario Kart 8: Deluxe''. The only difference is that she doesn't wear her earrings.
*Unlike her game counterpart, Peach was not a princess by birth, instead taking on the title after being found and adopted by the Toads and does not have a damsel in-distress role as that role is taken by Luigi in the movie.
* Peach along with [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]], [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]], [[Kamek (Illumination)|Kamek]] and [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] are the only five characters in the first film to ever visit [[Brooklyn]].
* Peach's voice actor has also voiced Marla Brenner in ''Playmobil: The Movie''.

=== References to video games and other media ===
* Her appearance as a child strongly resembles [[Baby Peach]].
* Similarly to ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', Peach has the ability to float.
* Peach rides a bike at one point which strongly resembles her [[Mach Bike]].
**She even wears her biker outfit from that game.
* Bowser wanting Peach to marry her while she is not interested also occurs in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.
* Although it is not confirmed, she seems to enjoy using her feet, especially in battle. This is evident by how often she does so.
**After she walks down from her throne during her introduction, the first parts of her body that was shown were her feet.
**As she throws Mario to the floor when they first met, she can be shown lifting her foot.
**As she runs across the obstacle course, she kicks the fake Bowser to pieces.
**During her conversation with Mario after he failed to complete the obstacle course, she can be shown swinging her feet.
**While not in the final cut, she pushes Mario with her foot as she tries to pull a Cheep Cheep off of him and given that a Cheep Cheep was shown sucking Mario's face in the final cut, it possibly proves she did it offscreen. Additionally, after she does pull it off of him, the Cheep Cheep then sucks Toad's face, so it's possible Peach stepped on Toad to push him down and pull the Cheep Cheep off of him too.
**During one of her flashbacks, she can be shown being fully barefoot.
**During Bowser's ambush on Rainbow Road, she can be shown kicking one of Bowser's troops off their kart.
**When preparing to fight Bowser, she picks up a halberd by stepping on the edge of it.
**After walking up to the balcony as soon as Bowser arrived, the camera starts at her feet before scanning up to the rest of her body.
**After catching the spear that one of Bowser's troops threw, she can shown stepping on the wall just before she lights the spear on fire with lava to explode King Bob-Omb.
**During the final battle in Brooklyn, she kicks Bowser to protect Mario, a Koopa Troopa to break free and a shell to knock the Super Star closer to Mario.
**Overall, if someone asked her to use her feet on them, it might be more than likely she'd do it. If this is true, it would prove her potential love for footwork even further.
