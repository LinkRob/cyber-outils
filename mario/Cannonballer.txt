'''Cannonballer''' is a [[Bros. Item]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==Description==
Cannonballers have the appearance of a regular black [[cannon]] attached to a wooden carriage. They are sold at item stores, and it costs 40 [[coin]]s to buy a Cannonballer.

==History==

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'' ===
A Cannonballer can only target a single enemy. Upon using it, [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]] hop into the cannon, from which they are shot out of in a random order, at the selected target. The player must press a certain button the instant each of them hit the enemy, otherwise that brother's attack is unsuccessful and the targeted enemy does not take as much damage. Spiked enemies are unaffected by the Cannonballer, and firing at one results in the brother taking damage themselves.
