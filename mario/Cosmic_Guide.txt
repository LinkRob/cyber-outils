'''Cosmic Guide''' is a feature in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Description==
Cosmic Guide is characterized as a cosmic version of [[Rosalina]]. It can appear in any [[galaxy]] if the player has lost too many lives there. The player can ask the Cosmic Guide to complete the level on their behalf, but if a Cosmic Guide does, they do not receive a [[Power Star]], but a [[Bronze Star]] that does not help them with unlocking the [[Grandmaster Galaxy]].

It is the ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' version of the [[Super Guide]] used in other [[Wii]] games.
