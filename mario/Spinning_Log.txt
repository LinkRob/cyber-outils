'''Spinning Logs''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue]], ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.

==Description==
Spinning Logs are pink log [[platform]]s that have a function of remaining still for a while and then start spinning wildly, similarly to some of the [[Rotary Lift]]s in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. While spinning, a Spinning Log can throw a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] into a certain direction, which can either provide the benefit of giving him a boost to his [[Flutter Jump]] or send him falling into a [[pit]].
==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
Spinning Logs are prominently featured in the level [[Beware The Spinning Logs]].
