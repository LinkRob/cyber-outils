'''Queen Rotunda''' is the gluttonous ruler of [[Rotundaland]] in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Love 'Em and Leave 'Em]]." She is the main antagonist of the episode, filling in for [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] throughout, as he does not appear in it aside from a brief mention. Queen Rotunda's name is presumably derived from the word {{w|rotund}}, given her physical appearance.
