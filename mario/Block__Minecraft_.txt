'''Blocks''' are the main type of object featured in ''[[Minecraft]]''. They made their first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in ''Minecraft''{{'}}s ''[[Super Mario Mash-up]]'' pack, first released for the [[Wii U]] in 2016, and went on to appear in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', as of Challenger Pack 7, with the introduction of ''Minecraft'' protagonist [[Steve]] and the [[Minecraft World]] stage. Grass Blocks are the most common type of Block, to the point of being used as a symbol for the ''Minecraft'' series.

==In ''Minecraft'' (''Super Mario Mash-up'')==
The ''Super Mario Mash-up'' replaces the textures of some of the ''Minecraft'' Blocks with ones from the ''Mario'' franchise, including [[Brick Block]]s over the ''Minecraft'' object of the [[w:c:minecraft:Bricks|same name]], [[Light Block]]s over [[w:c:minecraft:Redstone Lamp|Redstone Lamps]], red [[POW Block]]s over [[w:c:minecraft:TNT|TNTs]], [[Gold Brick]]s over [[w:c:minecraft:Block of Gold|Blocks of Gold]], and [[Note Block]]s over the ''Minecraft'' object of the [[w:c:minecraft:Note Block|Note Block]]. In addition, the [[w:c:minecraft:Crafting Table|Crafting Table]] is replaced with a [[Question Block|? Block]].

==In ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''==
Steve's moveset enables him to craft Blocks. The entirety of the Minecraft World stage is made up of Blocks, like the world within ''Minecraft'' itself.
