'''Mushroom''' is a status ailment that only appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''

==Description==
The Mushroom effect makes characters turn into a mushroom. The color of the mushroom depends on who is transformed (Mario is red, Peach is pink, Mallow is white/grey, Geno is blue, and Bowser is green).

==Effect==
Whenever someone is turned into a mushroom, they cannot act. Instead, they recover a certain amount of health each turn until they transform back. Many moves such as [[Petal Blast]] can inflict this ailment. Using the Trueform Pin and Safety Badge will protect from this ailment.
