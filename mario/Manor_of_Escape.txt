'''Manor of Escape''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place at a haunted manor. There are three floors above the entrance and a rooftop area, where there is only one door. The three floors each have a balcony with six doors.

==Opening==
The four characters are on the roof of the haunted manor. All of them enter the door to go down to the highest balcony.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to enter doors to make their way down the manor until they finally escape from the front entrance. Each level has only one door that leads to the door of the level beneath it, so the other five doors on a level only lead a character out of a different door on the same level. The correct door to take on each floor is randomized each time the minigame is played. Once every character has taken a door to the floor below them, the door that they took to get to the level below them disappears and gets blocked off by wooden planks. Whoever escapes the manor first is the winner. If none of the characters escape the manor after several minutes, the minigame ends in a [[Draw|tie]].
