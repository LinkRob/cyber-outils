'''Boss Brolder''' is a boss that appears in [[Super Mario 3D World|''Super Mario 3D World'']] and [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]. It serves as the primary boss of World 4, later on serves as one of the two first bosses of [[World Castle]] and is overall the fifth and later either the tenth or eleventh boss of the game. 

== Appearance ==
As his name suggests, Boss Brolder resembles a regular [[Brolder]] but with some key differences. Boss Brolder's arm and body are constantly flowing with [[lava]]. His eyes now have a golden-orange glow and an angry scowl. Shards of rock and crystal now protrude from his body, and a large chunk of crystal is placed below Boss Brolder's eyes, making it look like a nose. Boss Brolder is the supposed leader of the Brolders. 

== Overview ==
He is fought at the end castle of [[World 4 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 4]], at a blockade in [[World Bowser]], and at the [[Boss Rush]] level in [[World Flower (Super Mario 3D World)|World Flower]]. 
Like most [[List of bosses|bosses]] in the game, Boss Brolder will explode after being defeated by the player. 

==Attacks==
*'''Brolder Call:''' Boss Brolder will jump and stomp on the ground bringing three [[Brolder|Brolders]] to assist him.

*'''Splorches Call:''' Similar to Brolder call, although this attack summons two [[Splorch|Splorches]] instead. He only uses this attack in his blockade fight.

*'''Rollout:''' Like many other Brolders, Boss Brolder will roll at the player chasing it and after being hit 2 times it will begin to roll faster in order to difficult the player.

*'''Flame Spin:''' After taking a hit from the tossed Brolder, Boss Brolder will start rapidly spinning while launching multiple fire rocks at the player(s). He will also slowly move towards the player while spinning.

==Level Appearances==
*[[Lava Rock Lair]]: He is first seen in this World 4 level as the boss. Players will have to hit him with [[Brolder]]s to defeat him.
*[[Brolder Blockade is Back]]: The same battle in World 4, except now Players have to dodge the rolling [[Splorch]]es.
*[[Boss Blitz]]: He is one of the bosses in the level. Defeat him normally.

==Trivia==
*Boss Brolder shares his boss theme with [[King Ka-Thunk|King Ka-thunk]] and [[Motley Bossblob]]. 
*Boss Brolder is the first of the only Main World Bosses of ''Super Mario 3D World'' that later on in the game become a Blockade Boss, the other being [[Pom Pom|Pom-Pom]].
*Boss Brolder has many similarities to [https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Scaldera#Skyward_Sword Scaldera], a boss from the Nintendo Wii game, ''The Legend of Zelda: Skyword Sword'', as they are both large rock ball-like creatures that resides in fiery areas, they both have fire appendages and as they get damage, and they lose their rock armor when taking damage, revealing lava skin. Additionally, they both have similar attacks, such as rolling at the Player and launching fireballs.
*Like his Brolder counter-parts, Boss Brolder has a slight resemblance to Geodudes, a race of rock-like creature from the ''Pokemon'' franchise.
