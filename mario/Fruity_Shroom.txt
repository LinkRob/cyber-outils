The '''Fruity Shroom''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. The item can be sold for 60 coins.

==Effects==

It is made by mixing a [[Peachy Peach]] with a [[Shroom Shake]]. This meal restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]. The Fruity Shroom takes the appearance of three [[Fried Shroom]]s accompanied by an orange and two cherries.
