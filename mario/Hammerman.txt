'''Hammerman''' is a [[badge]] which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear this badge by using two [[Badge Point|BP]], and while wearing this badge, Mario cannot use his [[boots|jump commands]] during battle, but gains one point of attack power. Mario, however, can still deal first strikes with his jump attacks since he can jump outside of battle.

[[Charlieton]], a salesman in [[Rogueport]], sells this badge to Mario. The Hammerman badge is at an expensive price of 180 [[coin]]s.

==Etymology==
In contrast to the badge [[Jumpman (badge)|Jumpman]], this badge is named Hammerman as Mario uses his hammer instead of jumping and vice-versa. Jumpman is a reference to Mario's original name "[[Mario|Jumpman]]."
