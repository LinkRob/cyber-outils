'''Fuchsia Fury''' is [[Rabbid Peach]]'s starting [[Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Fuchsia Fury is a [[Blaster]] in ''Kingdom Battle''. It has medium range and low cover damage. Its hit damage deals 25 to 35 damage. This weapon has no [[Super Effect]] however.
