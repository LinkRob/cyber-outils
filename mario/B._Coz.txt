'''B. Coz''' is a tennis player in the [[Mario Tennis (series)|''Mario Tennis'' series]]. He is the younger brother of [[A. Coz]] and the older brother of [[Willy]]. B. Coz first appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]].

==In the ''Mario Tennis'' series==
In ''Mario Tennis'' for the Game Boy Color, B. Coz is an unlockable playable character of the Power type. He can be unlocked by winning against him in Mario Tour in Doubles in [[Island Open]].

In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour'', both B. Coz serves as a coach of the [[Factory (tennis school)|Factory]] tennis school alongside A. Coz. However, neither of them return as a playable character.
