'''Chows''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are stronger versions of [[K-9]]s, and are only encountered in [[Land's End]].

==Battle==
In battle, Chows can attack by biting for damage. They can also use special attacks such as [[Poison (move)|Poison]] to build up on damage on someone over time.

===Strategy===
Despite appearing very late in the game, the Chow's stats are rather low, so any physical move should take them out.

==Trivia==
*There is another Chow that appears in [[Monstro Town]]. However, this Chow is friendly and records how many [[Super Jump]]s that [[Mario]] can do in succession. If Mario has thirty jumps, then he is awarded with an [[Attack Scarf]]. If he has reached over one hundred, he is awarded with the [[Super Suit]].
*Their [[Psychopath]] message indicates they fought Mario already. This may indicate that these are the same K-9s from [[Mushroom Way]].
