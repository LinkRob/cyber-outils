The '''Mystery Note''' is a key item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
This item is found after defeating a [[Shy Guy#Paper Mario|Yellow Shy Guy]] in one of the sub-rooms of the Green Station in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]. This note is written in the language of the Shy Guys and cannot be read normally. [[Mario]] has to take it to [[Russ T.]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] to have him decipher it. In doing so, Russ T. reveals the note's contents as "''Hit yellow, green, red, then blue.''" indicating the order of which the boxes should be hit in the Green Station.

Once the boxes are hit in the correct order, train tracks fall from the wall, opening up the way to the Red Station. Once the train tracks appear, the boxes disappear into confetti.

==Trivia==
* If Mario hasn't returned Russ T.'s stolen [[dictionary]], he won't be able to read the Mystery Note.
