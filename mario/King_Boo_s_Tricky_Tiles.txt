'''King Boo's Tricky Tiles''' is a boss minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', set in [[Haunted Trail]]. It involves the characters fighting [[King Boo]], whose health bar they can decrease by hopping onto a certain switch to shine a light on him and therefore receive [[point]]s. Like the other boss minigames, whoever has the most points by the end is the winner. Whoever does the last attack gains an additional five points.
