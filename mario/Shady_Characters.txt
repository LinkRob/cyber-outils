'''Shady Characters''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. The microgame takes twice as much time as other microgames.

== Gameplay ==
There is a silhouette of an object, the player has time to turn it with its [[Wii Remote]] to identify its features. Then the player has to pick between shapes and objects by pressing {{Button|wii|A}} or {{Button|Wii|B}}.
*'''Level 1:''' There are three shapes to choose from.
*'''Level 2:''' There are three objects to choose from.
*'''Level 3:''' There are six shapes and objects to choose from.
