'''64th Trivia Quiz-Off''' is a quiz show featured in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Description==
The 64th Trivia Quiz-Off is hosted by [[Mr. Hammer]]. [[Princess Peach]] is required to participate in the quiz show once [[Misstar]] has been rescued. The 64th Trivia Quiz-Off features ten questions relating to the game, and most of the questions are about the locations themselves. Aside from Peach, the other two contestants are [[Spiky Tom]] and [[Spiky John]].

The "64th" part of the quiz title is a reference to the [[Nintendo 64]], the console that ''Paper Mario'' had originally been released for.

''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' features two similar successor quizzes, [[65th Super Fun Quirk Quiz]] and [[66th Annual Quirk Quiz]].
