The '''10-Point Star''' is a type of [[Special Item]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. It is worth 10 [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]], and fills the [[Countdown Timer]] by 10 seconds. 10-Point Stars have a more effective variation named the [[20-Point Star]].
