'''World 4-1''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the first level of World 4, the [[Chai Kingdom]].

==Overview==
World 4-1 is a plains-like level with a feel of China as a theme. This level introduces [[Pionpi]]s who are zombie-like enemies that cannot be killed by normal means. Other than that, this level contains mainly [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[Bombshell Koopa]]s as regular enemies along with bottomless pits to jump over and avoid.
