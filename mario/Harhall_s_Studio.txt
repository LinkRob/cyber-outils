*[[Mario]]}}'''Harhall's Studio''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It is home to the famous clothes stylist of the [[Beanbean Kingdom]], [[Harhall]], and it's where a [[Beanstar]] piece is located.
==History==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] continue their search for the next Beanstar piece. They notice the duo leaving complaining about the work habits and decide to see what's happening for themselves. Then they notice Harhall complaining about his employees leaving and notices Mario and Luigi. Harhall agrees to give over the Beanstar piece only if they can style three sets of clothes for him. Mario and Luigi done so and an excited Harhall hands over the Beanstar piece.
