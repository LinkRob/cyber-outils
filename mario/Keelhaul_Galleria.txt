|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Keelhaul Galleria''' is an [[Item Shop]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Keelhaul Galleria is located on [[Keelhaul Key]] where it's being run by [[Thriff T.]], the brother of [[Plenn T.]] who runs [[Toad Bros. Bazaar]] in [[Rogueport]]. Thriff T. mentioned before Chapter 5 that it was always his dream to open his own Item Shop. Despite being shipwrecked, Thriff T. was able to make his dream come true and opens up his Item Shop near the shore that he calls it "Keehaul Galleria." This Item Shop sells rather useful items such as [[Ice Storm]]s and [[Super Shroom]]s.
