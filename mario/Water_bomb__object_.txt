'''Water bombs''' are hazardous objects which appear in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
Water bombs are reflective [[cannonball]]-like objects shot from [[cannon]]s. They appear in [[Bob-omb Battlefield]], where they are shot from a cannon on the mountainside. Water bombs most prominently during the mission [[Big Bob-omb on the Summit]], in which the [[Bob-omb Buddy|Bob-omb Buddies]] and regular [[Bob-omb]]s are at war with one another, and two Bob-omb Buddies at the start are using the cannon to shoot out water bombs at the Bob-ombs. A water bomb usually lands in a wide open area, where if [[Mario]] or another character gets hit, he takes damage.

In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', water bombs also appear in [[Battle Fort]], although here they rain down from the sky at random. In VS mode, water bombs and other obstacles (except [[Big Steely|iron balls]]) were removed from here.
