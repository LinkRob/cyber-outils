'''Petit Piranhas''' are a small variant of a [[Piranha Plant]] and an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
These small little baby Piranha Plants are only fought once, during the boss battle with [[Lava Piranha]]. Petit Piranhas are budding off of him. Once Lava Piranha is defeated the first time, these two will also catch on fire, making them more of a threat as well. However, this makes them vulnerable to water and ice attacks like Lava Piranha, himself.

These guys aren't the type to battle as they only have 1 Hit point. Simply attacking them and lowering that number to zero prevents them from attacking for a couple of turns.
