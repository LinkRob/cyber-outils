'''Outta My Way''' is a single-player [[minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Opening==
The end of the course is shown before the game screen moves over to where the player's character—either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is at.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to reach the end of an obstacle course as quickly as possible. A few enemy species serve as obstacles along the way, including [[Thwomp]]s, [[Whomp]]s, and [[Pokey]]s. Pokeys roam a certain area, and the player's character can defeat them using several punches. Thwomps only obstruct the path, and the player's character can push one by rapidly pressing {{button|gba|A}} and {{button|gba|B}} alternately. A Whomp only attacks when the player's character is nearby. The Whomp performs its signature ability of slamming onto the ground before returning back up shortly afterward. The player's character can quickly defeat a Whomp by [[jump]]ing onto it while the Whomp is lying flat on the ground.

In the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] and Mini-Game Attack modes, the player's character reach the end within a [[time limit]], set to 60 seconds in the former and 99 seconds in the latter. In Play Land, the player's character has to reach the end in the fastest time. The minigame's Shroom City variation uses a smaller course than the Mini-Game Attack and Play Land modes.

==Controls==
*{{Button|gba|Pad}}: Move left or right
*{{button|gba|A}}: Jump
*{{button|gba|B}}: Punch
*{{button|gba|A}} and {{button|gba|B}} alternately: Shove a Thwomp

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules:''' ''Shove Thwomps out of your way while fighting off Whomps and Pokeys.''
