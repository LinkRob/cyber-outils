The '''Castle Gardens''' is a location within [[Toad Town]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
The Castle Gardens are in the front of [[Princess Peach's Castle]], located within [[Toad Town]], replacing the [[Castle Grounds]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and various other appearances. The area is inhabited by [[Toad (species)|Toads]], and it is lined with various buildings, lampposts with star-shaped lights, and a fountain. The Castle Gardens are also decorated with blue flags depicting stars, which are hung from lampposts, and multicolor banners depicting [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]]s, strung from the top of buildings to lampposts to treetops.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy''==
[[File:SMG Castle Grounds Invaded.png|thumb|left|200px|The Castle Gardens are invaded by Bowser's army.]]
The Castle Gardens are the first playable section, and it is the site of the [[Star Festival]]. Here, [[Mario]] can collect some of the [[Star Bit]]s. Not long after Mario makes his way through the Castle Gardens and enters Toad Town, the area is attacked by [[Bowser]] and his [[airship]] fleet. In response to this, some of the Toads flee, while others end up trapped in [[Crystal (Super Mario Galaxy)|Crystals]]. Mario attempts to enter Princess Peach's Castle to rescue [[Princess Peach]], but she is abducted by Bowser, and [[Kamek]] blasts Mario to [[Gateway Galaxy]]. Following this, the Castle Gardens are inaccessible for the remainder of the main storyline.

In the ending, Mario awakes in the Castle Gardens, along with Bowser, Princess Peach, and various other characters Mario met in his journey. The last [[galaxy]], the [[Grand Finale Galaxy]], takes place in the Castle Gardens, and there is only one [[Power Star]] to collect, and this is by obtaining all 100 [[Purple Coin]]s.
