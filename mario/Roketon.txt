'''Roketons''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. Roketons are airborne enemies that serve directly under [[Tatanga]]. They only appear in the last level, [[World 4-3 (Super Mario Land)|World 4-3]] of the [[Chai Kingdom]].

== History ==
Roketons patrol the skies in their planes until they spot [[Mario]] riding in his [[Sky Pop]], upon which Roketons try to attack him. Using the Sky Pop, Mario must shoot down the Roketons to defeat them. If a Roketon passes by Mario, it fires a [[Bullet Biff]] backward at him. Defeating a Roketon earns the player 400 [[point]]s.

==Trivia==
* Although artwork for a Rocketon depicts it shooting a cannonball from its plane, Bullet Biffs are the only object shot by Roketons in the game.
