The '''Happy Pants''' is a type of armor only usable by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the [[Marrymore]] hotel for 38 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Happy Pants on Mallow increases his [[Defense]] by 24 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 12, both of which are the same for [[Mario]]'s [[Happy Shirt]].
