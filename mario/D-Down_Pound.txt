'''D-Down Pound''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] must pay two [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear this badge and by paying two [[Flower Point|FP]], he can use this badge's attack. This attack is a [[hammer]] attack that pierces through an enemy's defensive points.

Mario finds this badge in [[Rowf's Badge Shop]]. Rowf prices this badge for seventy-five [[coin]]s. [[Piercing Blow]], a badge in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', has the same effect as this badge, although the [[D-Down Jump]] has no replacement in ''The Thousand-Year Door''.
