'''Spunias''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Physical description==
Spunias resemble Spanias, with both enemies having two spikes on their head, although Spunias have a pinkish color.

==History==
These enemies pack quite the punch and are primarily found only in some of the deepest levels of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. [[Mario]] can fight one of these along with four Spanias in a very secret room in [[Rogueport Sewers]]. It is standing on the tall pillar and almost must be fought in order to obtain a [[Defend Plus]] badge. The Spunia in the Rogueport Sewers secret room doesn't spin to Mario and only acts as a mini-boss.
