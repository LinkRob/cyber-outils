|game = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}'''Mad Maze Maul''' is a [[Bonus Area|Bonus Stage]] played by the Kongs in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player must defeat all of the [[Kremling]]s in the area and cross the finish line before the 60 seconds is up. The only two Kremlings in the area are [[Kritter]]s and the harder to defeat [[Kasplat]]s. Mad Maze Maul is only played by [[Diddy Kong]] and [[Lanky Kong]]. Like with a few other minigames, this is one of few minigames that cannot be played at [[Snide's H.Q.]] after collecting all of the [[Blueprint]]s.
