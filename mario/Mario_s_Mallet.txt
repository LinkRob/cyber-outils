'''Mario's Mallet''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party-e]]'', accessible by swiping the "Graceful Princess Peach" [[e-Reader]] card.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of [[Mario]], who must use his [[hammer]] to smash [[Goomba]]s that emerge from the [[Warp Pipe]] to both protect [[Princess Peach]] from them and impress her. The player can decide on the number of Goombas at the start. During gameplay, [[Luigi]] may sometimes pop out of the pipe, and if the player hits him, they lose. Another way of losing is by taking too long to smash a Goomba, as it goes after Peach. Once the minigame has been completed, a heart appears above Peach.
