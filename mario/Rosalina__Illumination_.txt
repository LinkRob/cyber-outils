'''Princess Rosalina''' is the overarching protagonist of the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series who appears in the sequel, ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'' as the overarching protagonist. She is voiced by [[Brie Larson]]. She is  [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]]'s sister.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
While her appearance in the present day is nearly the same as in the games, Rosalina originally wore a sleeveless dress in her youth.

===Powers and Abilities===
Rosalina retains many of her abilities from the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' games, but is also able to use her wand as a grappling hook to redirect a [[Bullet Bill]]. She is also able to summon and reconstruct her wand into her hands, although it disintegrates when it slips from her grasp.

== Biography ==

=== Background ===
Prior to the events of the films, Rosalina lived happily alongside her baby sister, Peach. The two had magic abilities, which they could use together. However, one day, according to Rosalina, Evil Forces found the siblings and attempted to steal their powers for themselves. Rosalina and Peach ran away in the woods away from the Evil Forces as they were chased, leading to Rosalina noticing a warp pipe. Seeing no other option, Rosalina takes Peach, and brings her to the warp pipe, taking her through it, separating the two in the process. This ended up leading into Peach landing in the Mushroom Kingdom and being taken in by the Toads, although forgetting her early life with Rosalina.

After, Rosalina likely managed to evade the Evil Forces, seeing as Peach was in safety. Through an unknown chain of events, she eventually ended up finding the Lumas, and decided to become their adoptive mother, reading them bedtime stories and tucking them in at night.

=== ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'' ===
Rosalina called to the Lumas, telling them that it was bedtime, to which most of them were excited, except for one. Rosalina stops one of the Lumas to ask if they washed their face, which they at first try to lie about, but eventually give in and tell the truth. Rosalina sits down with the Lumas and reads a book about Princess Peach to them, before a red Luma interrupts her and asks if they can read the book about the plumbers, Mario and Luigi. Rosalina is reluctant at first, due to the fact that they have read that book for many nights, but eventually gives in after convincing from the Lumas. Rosalina begins reading the book, but is interrupted once again by a Luma asking if they could skip to the part where Donkey Kong appears and fights Mario, leading to all of them chanting his name, before they hear a loud bang. Rosalina decides to close the book and tells them they'll finish the book tomorrow, before telling them to get tucked in and go to sleep.

She heads out to investigate and is shocked to see Megaleg invading. Rosalina tells the Lumas she'll return in a moment, before the Megaleg tells Rosalina that she will be coming with him. Rosalina takes out her wand, and attacks Megaleg, engaging in a battle with him. She ends up easily defeating Megaleg, however, just as she is about to deal the final blow, a Luma appears, telling Rosalina about a bad dream, before Megaleg notices, realizing he immediately fires at the Lumas, causing Rosalina to quickly take action to defend them, leading to her getting overpowered by Megaleg due to being distracted. Megaleg's head opens to have Bowser Jr. standing proud inside of it, telling Rosalina that a new conqueror shall arise after Bowser's defeat, and that the Bowser name will be feared again, before Kamek interrupts him. A luma rushes to Rosalina's rescue, but is easily overpowered by Bowser Jr., being taken with Rosalina and captured. Bowser Jr. then takes both Rosalina and the Luma and imprisons them.

They are taken across the galaxy, inside of Bowser Jr.'s ship. Rosalina asks where he is taking the two, to which he taunts her. They arrive at Planet Bowser, where Bowser Jr. proudly reveals that he has captured a princess, and announces he will use her power to power up their weapon and destroy the universe, giving them eternal glory. The Koopas open the cage and roughly drag the Yellow Luma out, enraging Rosalina, making her power up and furiously scream at the Koopas, before attacking them and telling the Yellow Luma to leave. Rosalina is overpowered by Bowser Jr. and forced onto a chair, where Bowser Jr.'s paint covers Rosalina and traps her.

Eventually, Rosalina is put in a cage by Bowser Jr. and used to power up his weapon, while her sister Peach, Mario, Luigi, and others try to save her. Peach eventually finds her in a cage, and quickly jumps up, putting her hand over to the cage in an attempt to use their magic. Rosalina notices Peach and attempts to use her hand, but is too weak and fails. Peach begs Rosalina to try, and she uses her remaining strength and is able to connect hands with Peach through the glass, creating an explosion that destroys the Boomsday Weapon, and freeing Rosalina, ending Bowser Jr. and Bowser's reign of terror and plans to destroy the universe. Rosalina reunites with her Lumas in the Mushroom Kingdom, and accepts their requests to see Peach's castle, only to find it gone, with Luigi stating it died. Mario steps in and declares that they will build a better castle instead. Rosalina assists them. Afterwards, she and the Lumas head home, and Rosalina fixes Fox's starcraft to allow him to go home as well.

==Trivia==
*This is not the first time that Brie Larson has played a cosmically empowered, blonde haired woman from space who also happens to be a princess. She played [[community:c:marvel:Carol Danvers (Earth-199999)|Captain Marvel]] in the ''Marvel Cinematic Universe'' who was also a princess due to a political marriage to a prince in the movie, [[w:c:marvel:The Marvels|''The Marvels'']].
*Unlike her video game counterpart, Rosalina is referred to as a princess and is related to Peach. Neither the games nor official material had ever called her a princess, nor did it confirm or deny that she and Peach were related. Not even [[Shigeru Miyamoto]] himself has been asked if they are related or not.
*The concept of Peach and Rosalina being related had been devised early in the development of ''Super Mario Galaxy'', though it was ultimately cut for unknown reasons. However, the characters both have similar appearances.
