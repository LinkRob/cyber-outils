'''Winter Tour''' is the fifth Tour from [[Mario Kart Tour]] which began on november 20, 2019 and ended on december 3, 2019. This Tour features Mario (Santa) outfit from [[Super Mario Odyssey]] and [[DK Pass|DS DK Pass]] returns from [[Mario Kart DS]].

This tour also introduced [[Pink Gold Peach]] and a [[Black Shy Guy]] as new drivers. This is also the first time R/T variants are introduced to classic tracks (those bieng [[Rainbow Road (Super Mario Kart)|SNES Rainbow Road]], [[Daisy Hills|3DS Daisy Hills]] and [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart 7)|3DS Mario circuit]]).
