The '''sewer rat'''<ref>''Nintendo Magazine System'' (AU) Issue #5, page 54.</ref> is a boss in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
It is a large rat with a spiked nose.

===Powers and abilities===
During its boss fight, the sewer rat can run up walls and into pipes. Eventually, the sewer rat crawls to the top of the ceiling and falls straight onto [[Mario]], comparable to [[Morton Koopa Jr.]] and [[Roy Koopa]]'s respective boss fights in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
The sewer rat is the boss of [[Macro Zone]], where it is fought in the level [[Final Boss: One Mighty Mouse!]]. Mario must defeat the sewer rat by either [[jump]]ing on it three times or throwing six [[fireball]]s at it. Upon being defeated, the sewer rat falls off the screen, and Mario will recover one of his six [[Golden Coin|Golden Coins]].

==In other media==
===''Mario vs. Wario''===
[[File:SewerRatMarioAdventures.jpg|thumb|200px|The sewer rat in the comic ''Mario vs. Wario'']]
The sewer rat also appears in the first ''[[Mario vs. Wario]]'' comic. It is the the first enemy whom Mario encountered on his way to visit Wario. Mario mistakenly assumes that the sewer rat just wants to have a party, so he pulled its needle-point nose off to use as a party-popper, causing the sewer rat to faint.
