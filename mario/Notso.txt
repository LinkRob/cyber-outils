==History==
Notso is a shopkeeper that runs his shop in [[Flopside]]. Unlike his [[Flipside]] counterpart, [[Howzit]], who has shops throughout the game, Notso only has his one shop in Flopside. He also has a more glum expression compared to the always energetic Howzit (most shown with the constant frown on Notso's face).

== Trivia ==
* Despite his expression, Tippi says that he is happy for some reason.
