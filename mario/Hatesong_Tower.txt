|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Hatesong Tower''' is a location mentioned in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Hatesong Tower is mentioned by [[Luigi]] and it's the final area that he and his friends went to during their journey. This is where the [[Chestnut King]] was keeping [[Princess Eclair]] captive. Luigi and co. eventually defeat the Chestnut King (and later [[Minister Crepe]] who was the true villain) and saved Princess Eclair.
