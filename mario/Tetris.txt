'''''Tetris''''' is a puzzle game for the [[Game Boy]]. It is a launch title, having been released in 1989 in Japan and North America and in 1990 in Europe and Australia. ''Tetris'' was a pack-in game with several original Game Boy units. It was later ported to the [[Nintendo 3DS]]'s [[Virtual Console]] in 2011 and then to the [[Nintendo Switch]] on February 9, 2023, as one of the first Game Boy games released for the [[Nintendo Switch Online]] service.

While it is not a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game, the multiplayer mode features [[Mario]] (player one) and [[Luigi]] (player two) competing against one another. In addition, the game features artwork of Mario leaning against a [[Tetrimino]], another of him playing the Game Boy, and lastly [[Luigi]] playing the Game Boy.
