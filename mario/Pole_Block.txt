'''Pole Blocks''' are objects in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Description==
Pole Blocks are a type of [[Block]] that can be hit to cause a long purple striped pole to extend from the block, and after some time, the pole retracts back into the block.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''==
Pole Blocks first appear in the [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] level [[Pole Block Passage]].
