'''Snake Block Party''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player have to avoid falling off by using [[Snake Block|Snake Blocks]] to travel over otherwise unclearable pits. If you do fall off, you float back to land using a [[Bubble]] as well as losing a health point. If they run out of health points, the player's place is determined. The player who survives the longest wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Pad}} – Move
*{{button|wii|2}} – Jump
