'''''Koopa Capers''''' is the fourth book in the ''[[Nintendo Adventure Books]]'' series, first published in August 1991.<ref>[http://www.smbhq.com/users/comics/kcapers.html SMBHQ] (Retrieved June 23, 2011)</ref> It was written by {{wp|Bill McCay}}. It is also notable in that [[Mario]] does not make an appearance in it, with Luigi mentioning he is busy "fixing [a] drip" at Niagara Falls.

==Plot==
The story begins with Luigi by himself at the Bros.' shop in Brooklyn, receiving a package that turns out to be a flying carpet. It wraps itself around him and whisks the plumber off to Bowser's castle, dropping him at the big guy's feet. Bowser, visibly embarrassed, explains to Luigi that Wendy O. Koopa is missing and possibly kidnapped. He asks Luigi to find her for him. Luigi reluctantly agrees, thinking it might be fun.

Luigi decides to check Wendy O.'s room, where he finds two pieces of chewed-up paper reading "fort" and "magma." He then checks out the familiar fortress section of the castle, where there are plenty of Boos, Thwomps, and Dry Bones, but no sign of the Koopa princess. He exits into the magma pits, where he finds a recipe containing a strange stanza he doesn't understand: ". . . add just one of Boom Boom's socks. Too many socks and the turtles will doze, until the fragrance leaves their nose. Add seven wands and then you are done. Now the seven will act as one." He pockets the recipe and continues through the pits.

Along the way, he encounters a Grand Goomba with six missing leg hairs and the Fire Bros., whom he defeats with the help of a Fire Flower, before crossing a catwalk full of Koopa Troopas. Luigi then finds a key in the shape of Bowser's head, which proves useful when it fits the lock of a nearby door. Inside, he discovers a pair of sweat socks marked "BOOM-BOOM" and carefully pockets them, gasping for air from the smell. He then ducks through a small door on the opposite wall.

Inside, he finds Wendy stirring a mixture in a cauldron over a bubbling pit of lava, yelling at some Koopa creatures about losing the sweat socks. Remembering the recipe and the socks, he is glad he picked them up and hides behind some old sacks. Just then, Bowser and the other six Koopalings enter the room. Bowser bellows at Wendy for stealing his recipe and his sons' wands in order to create a Super Wand and seize the throne from him. Wendy, of course, denies it, and Luigi soon becomes the topic of discussion. They all vow to make him pay when they find him.

Luigi leaps out from behind the sacks, chants "Too many socks and turtles will doze," and throws the socks into the pot. Bowser shrieks, and soon every Koopa in the Mushroom World is fast asleep. Luigi pockets all of Bowser's stolen coins and decides to enjoy himself in Koopaland while the turtles sleep.
