'''Dragon Crystal''' is a power-up item which only appears in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

==Description==
A Dragon Crystal has the appearance of a dragon claw holding out a flame. If [[Wario]] obtains a Dragon Crystal, he becomes [[Sea Dragon Wario]]. By collecting both a Dragon Crystal and an [[Eagle Statue]], Wario can become [[King Dragon Wario]].
