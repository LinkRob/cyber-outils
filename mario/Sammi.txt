'''Sammi''' is a tennis player who appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]. She is partnered with [[Sean]] in doubles. Sammi is a unlockable playable character of the Technique type, and she can be unlocked by winning against her in the Mario Tour by Singles or Doubles in [[Island Open]].
