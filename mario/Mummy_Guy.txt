'''Mummy Guys''' are a variant of [[Mini Shy Guy]]s which appear as an enemy in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mummy Guys resemble Mini Shy Guys but have a grayish color to them.

===Behavior===
Like several other enemies, Mummy Guys have a pattern of moving from left to right. If a [[Mini]] stands on a Mummy Guy, it breaks apart and reforms shortly after, similar to a [[Dry Bones]].

==In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''==
Mummy Guys only appear in three rooms of [[Spooky Attic]]: [[Room 7-1]], [[Room 7-3]], and in [[Room 7-7]]. In the [[Construction Zone]], Mummy Guys can be used in both the Spooky Attic construction kit and [[Special Kit 3]].
