'''Morty Moles''' are large variations of [[Monty Mole]]s that are found in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. They walk slowly through tunnels and are slightly more durable.

== Profile ==
Morty Moles have a similar appearance to regular Monty Moles, being larger than them and having a dark red color.

==History==
===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
First appearing in [[World 5-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|5-4]], Morty Moles commonly appear alongside Monty Moles. When attacked they will reduce in size and become faster. It takes two hits to defeat them. The use of the [[Super Leaf]], [[Invincibility Leaf]], [[Statue Leaf]], or a [[Starman]] can instantly defeat a Morty Mole, aditionally, 3 fireballs or boomerangs can also be used. They appear in [[World 5-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 5-4]] and [[World 7-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 7-4]].

==Trivia==
*Morty Moles behave similar to [[Rex]]es, in the sense that they also require two hits and shrink and speed up when stomped on.
