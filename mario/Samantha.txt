'''Samantha''' is a non-playable character in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Samantha is a blonde woman who wears glasses, a white blouse with a black jacket, and a skirt. She is frequently adjusting her glasses.

==In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour''==
Samantha is the secretary of the [[Royal Tennis Academy]]'s [[Headmaster]] during the Power Tour mode. She often follows the Headmaster around. Samantha can also be found in the Glossary.

Samantha attends the [[Island Open]] with the Headmaster when he brings [[Clay]] and [[Ace]] there. Shortly after, when the Headmaster when he gets caught on fire upon entering the gates of the Island Open, Samantha pours a bucket of water on him to extinguish the flames. Later on, after Clay and Ace win the championship and unlock access to [[Mario World]], Samantha can be found in the Headmaster's Office with the Headmaster as well as [[Alex]].
