'''Jelectros''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Jelectros are glowing, electric [[jellyfish]] enemies. Jelectros are usually colored black. In the television show ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', Jelectros have glowing red eyes and a more squared shape than in the games.

Jelectros have had their design changed in both [[reissue]]s of ''Super Mario Bros. 3''. In the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' remaster of ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', Jelectros have been recolored to light purple. In ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3'', their color is changed once more to blue.

===Behavior===
Jelectros bob slightly in place underwater and harm [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] upon contact.

==Appearances==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Bros. 3'' / ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''====
[[File:Jelectro.gif|thumb|left]]
[[File:SMAS SMB3 World 3-5 Screenshot.png|thumb|200px|Jelectros in World 3-5 of the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' remaster in ''Super Mario All-Stars'']]
Jelectros appear in [[World 3-5 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 3-5]] and [[World 7-4 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 7-4]] of ''Super Mario Bros. 3''. In World 3-5, they are spread throughout the level, while in World 7-4, a large swarm of them can be found in the middle of the autoscrolling level. Much like [[Muncher]]s, Jelectros are damaging, animated scenery tiles, making them an indestructible solid surface, so not even [[Invincible Mario]] or [[hammer]]s thrown by [[Hammer Mario]] can damage them.

In the remake ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', Jelectros bob up and down in place, rather than staying completely still. They also appear in one [[World-e]] level: [[Sea to Sky]].
{{br|left}}
====''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Super Mario Maker 2''====
Jelectros appear as one of the course elements that can be placed in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. They replace the [[Spike Trap]]s when creating a course in the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' underwater theme, keeping their properties from ''Super Mario Bros. 3''. In ''Super Mario Maker'', they are only identified as Spike Traps, but ''Super Mario Maker 2'' calls them by their actual name.

===''Super Mario Bros. Print World''===
[[File:SMBPW Swimming Mario.png|thumb|200px|left|A Jelectro in the "Swimming Mario" artwork in ''Super Mario Bros. Print World'']]
A Jelectro appears in the printing software, ''[[Super Mario Bros. Print World]]'', in the artwork entitled "Swimming Mario" alongside [[Mario]].

===''Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up''===
[[File:MarineBiologist.png|thumb|A Jelectro in the Marine Biologist page of ''Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up'']]
In the coloring book game, ''[[Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up]]'', the coloring page for Marine Biologist has a Jelectro swimming with other underwater enemies and [[Frog Mario]]. The description for the job is as follows:

''Mario the marine biologist has just discovered some new life forms living at the bottom of the sea: Cheep-Cheeps, Jelectros, Bloobers, and Trouters, to name a few.''
{{br}}
==In other media==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:CartoonJelectros.png|thumb|Several Jelectros as they appear in ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3'' episode "The Ugly Mermaid"]]
Jelectros also make appearances in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. They appear in episodes such as "[[Reptiles in the Rose Garden]]", where they can actually move, and "[[The Ugly Mermaid]]", where they stand still like they originally do in ''Super Mario Bros. 3''.

===Nintendo Adventure Book===
In the [[Nintendo Adventure Book]] ''[[Leaping Lizards]]'', [[Luigi]] spots some Jelectros through a window while exploring [[Iggy Koopa]]'s secret underwater lab in [[Water Land]]. Three weeks later, some Jelectros appear in the swimming portion of the International Mushroom Games obstacle course. If the reader chooses Luigi to compete in the course, one of the Jelectros will shock and briefly stun him as soon as he enters the water.
