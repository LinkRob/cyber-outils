'''Cat Bob-ombs''' are a variant of [[Bob-omb]] and an enemy which appears in the [[Bowser's Fury]] campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Cat Bob-omb is [[Bob-omb]] with cat ears.

===Behavior===
Cat Bob-ombs have the same behavior as regular Bob-ombs, as they try to explode on to [[Mario]].
