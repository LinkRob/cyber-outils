'''Three-Headed Snake Block''', or '''Meringue Clouds-6''', is the sixth level of [[Meringue Clouds]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by completing [[Vanishing Ghost House]] and its own completion unlocks [[Ludwig's Block-Press Castle]]. 
==Layout==
The level begins on a platform a cloudy area, with a [[Snake Block]] block above a pit. As the Snake Blocks travels, it splits into a set of three Snake Blocks, and [[Foo]]s can be found. The Snake Blocks continue traveling to the right, occasionally overlapping with each other, as well as coming together and splitting. A set of [[Goombrat]]s appears on [[lift]]s, along with a Foo. A [[Red Ring]] is found, along with more Goombrat on platforms. More Foos are found, followed by another section of Goombrats on lifts. The [[Goal Pole]] is then found.
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' The first Star Coin is floating above the path of the bottom Snake Block formation.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' The second Star Coin can be seen hidden behind a cloud.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' The third Star Coin sits in the open, and the player can glide into it to collect it.
==[[List of Luigi sightings in New Super Luigi U|Luigi Sighting]]==
