The Nightmare Chunk traps Pi'llo's inside, and they cannot be freed until Mario breaks it.

== History ==
Before [[Antasma]] was banished into the [[Dream world|Dream World]] by the [[Pi'illo]]'s, [[Antasma]] shattered the [[Dark Stone]], and broken pieces became [[Nightmare Chunk|Nightmare Chunks]], and if fallen onto a [[Pi'illo]], where it would trap them inside. The [[Pi'illo]]'s would be trapped for many years until discovered by [[Mario]].
