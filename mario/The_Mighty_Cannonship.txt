}}'''The Mighty Cannonship''', or '''{{world|Soda|airship}}''', is the first airship level as well as the first level of [[Soda Jungle]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. This course is unlocked by completing [[Larry's Torpedo Castle]] or [[Wendy's Shifting Castle]] and its own completion unlocks [[Jungle of the Giants]] and [[Soda Jungle]] However, completing [[Blooper's Secret Lair]] or [[Piranha Plants on Ice]] involves skipping The Mighty Cannonship entirely. 

== Layout==
[[Bowser Jr.]] comes down with the [[Airship]] and snatches up the player at the beginning of the level. 

The level begins near a [[Mechakoopa]], a [[Cannon]] firing [[Cannonball]]s, and a [[? Block]] containing a power-up. A [[Bolt Lift]] is found above a Mechakoopa, and is followed by a series of Cannons. Rope ladders and [[crate]]s follow, along with a [[Red Ring]] and some platforms. Three large Cannons firing [[giant cannonball]]s are encountered. A Bolt Lift and another large Cannon are found before the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. A red [[Warp Pipe]] leads to an underwater section of the airship. The sections begins with a red [[Red Skull Box]] releasing [[Targeting Ted]]s, a [[Mecha Cheep]], and some ? Block containing [[coin]]s. A ? Block containing a power-up follows, along with more Mecha Cheeps. Another Red Skull Box is found before a passage with Mecha Cheeps in it. A red Warp Pipe is found at the end, with a Red Skull Box above it.

The Warp Pipe leads to Bowser Jr., whose [[Junior Clown Car]] is in submarine mode. He launches Targeting Teds at Mario while the pipes from the ceiling activate periodically and push Mario down. The player must guide the Targeting Teds to hit Bowser Jr.'s submarine. After three hits, Bowser Jr. falls off stage, and the room drains itself. However, Bowser Jr. comes back and tries to use the Mecha Hand to smash Mario, but Mario jumps out of the way and continues into [[Soda Jungle]].
==Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In the first section, the player must climb down a ladder above the first [[Star Coin]] to collect it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The player must jump off a Cannonball to collect the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The player must use the Targeting Teds (or be [[Super Mario]]) to break the [[Brick Block]]s blocking the final Star Coin.
