'''Raphael the Raven''' (also known by the shortened name '''Raphael Raven''') is the leader of the [[Raven]]s and first appears as the boss of the [[World 5 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|fifth world]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. Initially, he is an ordinary Raven but is magically enlarged by [[Kamek]] to fight the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]. Even after his defeat, he remains huge.

==History==
===''Yoshi'' franchise===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
[[File:Raphael the Raven SMW2 battle.png|thumb|left|Blue Yoshi fighting Raphael]]
When [[Blue Yoshi]] first meets Raphael, he is an ordinary Raven. However, [[Kamek]] appears and magically enhances him, increasing his size and strength. Then, Raphael slams into Blue Yoshi, sending it and [[Baby Mario]] flying to a small [[moon]]. Additionally, the force of the attack makes the Yoshi lose all its [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]]. Raphael then launches himself to the moon and prepares for battle.

Raphael fights by chasing and ramming into Blue Yoshi around the moon to send [[Baby Mario]] flying. He also attacks by jumping into the air and slamming the ground, creating shockwaves that circle the moon. The moon's interior is quartered by two [[Stump]]s extending its diameter, the ends of which jut out from two points on the surface. Yoshi must [[Ground Pound]] one of these while Raphael is on the opposite end to damage him. With each hit, Raphael becomes redder, angrier, and faster. After three successful hits, Blue Yoshi defeats Raphael and Raphael becomes a constellation in the sky. This boss battle is the only instance in which [[Baby Mario]] does not cry when he falls off Yoshi's back, but he can still be taken away by the [[Toady|Toadies]], possibly referencing the fact that there is no sound in space.

Along with [[Naval Piranha]], they are the only world bosses to use the extended variation of the boss theme in the original game, in the [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|remake]], all bosses use the extended theme. 

Raphael's sprite has a star on his belly. However, since the star is always the same palette as his skin, it is never seen in-game.

====''Tetris Attack''====
[[File:Raphael The Raven TA.png|thumb|Raphael The Raven on the left of the screen]]
Raphael The Raven returns in the game ''[[Tetris Attack]]'', allied with the [[Shy Guy|Shy-Guy]]s. In this game, Raphael is not considered an enemy. He is also a playable character, replacing the moon fairy Seren from ''[[Panel de Pon]]''. He is in the eighth level and is the last character to be saved from [[Bowser]]'s and Kamek's brainwashing.

====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
Although Raphael the Raven does not appear as a boss in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', he still makes a cameo. In the intro, one part shows a group of bosses from ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', including Raphael and the others.

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[File:YNI Giant Raven.png|thumb|left|The Large Raven in ''Yoshi's New Island''.]]
Though Raphael the Raven himself doesn’t appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', a [[Large Raven]] resembling him does, it can be defeated in the same way as a regular [[Raven]]. It is found in [[Eggstraordinary Terrain]] in the area above the beanstalk. 

In [[World 2 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 2]], the [[Kamek]] mini-boss battle is similar to Raphael's, in that Yoshi has to pound stumps in order to damage Kamek.

===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Pm chap5 raphaelultra.png|thumb|Raphael and his Ravens in ''Paper Mario'']]
[[File:PMRaphael.png|frame|left]]
Raphael the Raven reappears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. In this game, Raphael is the leader of a tribe of reformed Ravens that live in peace with the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s of [[Lavalava Island]]. He lives in nest in a tree in [[Jade Jungle]] and has become a legend to the Yoshis (as no one except the [[Village Leader]] can remember actually seeing him). He has the ability to communicate with both [[Mario]] and his Ravens and is depicted with a military disposition, commonly ending sentences with "over." Raphael helps Mario by ordering his Ravens to build a pulley contraption to take Mario and his allies to [[Mt. Lavalava]], site of the [[Misstar|fifth]] [[Star Spirits|Star Spirit]] and the [[Lava Piranha]]. He also gives Mario the [[Ultra Stone]], an item that allows Mario's party members to become even more powerful.

Later in the game, he writes a [[List of letters in Paper Mario#Raphael the Raven|letter]] to [[Sushie]] which the player can read at the [[Toad Town Post Office]]. In the letter, he asks whether he can provide help for Sushie, such as helping her climb the tree or cooking things she wants to eat. This letter will only be available during Chapter 8 if Mario wishes to go back to [[Toad Town]] from [[Bowser's Castle]].

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
Raphael the Raven makes a cameo in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' as a trophy. The information states that he is the biggest Raven, although it is Kamek who enlarged him. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', he appears as a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Sticker]]. The Sticker's information states that the artwork is from ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''; however, it actually uses the retouched ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' artwork. Raphael also makes an appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]] which makes the fighter immune to any tripping effect like the earthquake hazard and the Banana peels.

Although he does not physically appear, the moon where Raphael is fought on can be seen in the [[Yoshi's Island (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Yoshi's Island]] stage from ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.
