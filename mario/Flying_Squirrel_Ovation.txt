'''Flying Squirrel Ovation''' is the final [[level]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is also the ninth and final level in [[Superstar Road]].

==Layout==
The character hits four [[? Block]]s, eventually finding a [[Super Acorn]] to become their [[Flying Squirrel Mario|Flying Squirrel]] form. The platforms at the beginning eventually disappear and the player begins to slowly descend, collecting moving [[Coin]]s and encountering some [[Waddlewing]]s. After a long descent, the character lands on some bouncy clouds and go through a pipe. The final area shows a "Thank you," and the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].
