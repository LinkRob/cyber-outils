==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player must help guide a number of [[Banana Fairy|Banana Fairies]] to the end safely without getting eaten by [[Klaptrap]]s. The player must shoot the Klaptraps with melons to temporary knock them out. They wake up after a few moments and the player must keep their mouths closed. Reloading between shots is recommended. Completing this game results in a [[Golden Banana]].

==Trivia==
*[[Diddy Kong]] plays this minigame more times than any other Kong in the game.
