'''Silky''' (いも虫 ''Imomushi'', or マユピー ''Mayupī'' in Japanese) is a green worm enemy featured in ''[[Wario Land 3]]'' and a character in ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]''.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
[[File:Silky.png|frame|left]]
Here, Silky attacks by shooting out a certain type of string that transforms [[Wario]] into [[Ball o' String Wario]] upon touching it. Wario can quickly defeat a Silky using any attack.

==In ''Dr. Mario 64''==
Silky is a playable character in several modes and an opponent fought in [[Dr. Mario]]'s storyline.
