}}A '''Gigabite''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Gigabites can only be found in [[The Underwhere]] (specifically Chapter 7-1) and [[The Overthere]] of Chapter 7-4. Gigabites are rather rare enemies to encounter in the game and are stronger versions of [[Megabite]]s. They have a rather low HP of 6 but they only take 1 point of damage from any attack. Even [[Bowser]]'s powerful fire breath or [[Luigi]]'s super jump.
