}}The '''Wallop''' is a stone-based enemy first appearing in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is a sentient, rectangular block of masonry that mimics the Mario or Luigi's movements.

== Appearance ==
A Wallop looks like a section of a castle wall come to life. It has a heavy, grey stone body with brownish-pink bricks reinforcing its corners and forming its feet. Its face features a large, dark figure-eight-shaped cavity containing two glowing yellow eyes and a wide smile (when deactived he is sad)

=== Behavior ===
The Wallop acts as a mimic, responding directly to Mario or Luigi inputs:

* '''Movement:''' It slides left or right to mirror to one of the brothers lateral movement.
* '''Jumping:''' It leaps into the air whenever Mario or Luigi jumps, landing with a heavy thud that momentarily keeps it in place.
* '''Activation:''' It will activate if Mario or Luigi walks too close to it.

=== Strategies ===
There are three primary ways to bypass a Wallop, all of which involve jumping to trigger its mimicry:

* '''Jumping on them:''' Use the Wallop's flat top as a platform after it lands.
* '''Running under them:''' Jump to bait the Wallop into the air, then walk under it.
* '''Walking around them:''' Trigger its jump and then simply walk past it while it is still in the air or recovering from its landing.
* '''Killing''': he is immune to [[Tanooki Mario (power-up)|Tanooki]], [[Fire Flower]] and [[Boomerang Flower]], but he can die to the [[Statue Leaf]], only if you ground pound on top of him, and of course also with the [[Star]] and [[Invincibility Leaf|Invicibility Leaf]].
