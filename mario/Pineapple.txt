A '''Pineapple''' is a recurring type of [[fruit]] in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' and related media. It first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Circus]]''.

==In {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}==
===''Donkey Kong Circus''===
The gameplay involves [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] having to juggle pineapples while balancing on a [[barrel]].

===''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''===
Pineapples are featured as projectiles shot from [[Chunky Kong]]'s [[Pineapple Launcher]]. A [[Target Switch|Pineapple Switch]] can only be activated by shooting a pineapple at it.

===''[[Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]''===
A giant pineapple appears at the end of every [[level]] of its titular kingdom, [[Pineapple Kingdom]]. Just as with any fruit within their respective kingdom, Donkey Kong must bite into the Pineapple to end the level.

Pineapples have an explosive variation named [[Blood Pine]], which have the same body as a regular pineapple but with an added laughing expression.

==In ''[[Yoshi (video game)|Yoshi]]''==
Pineapples appear after levels 16 through 18 of the B-Type are cleared. A Pineapple rewards the player 800 [[point]]s.

==In {{franchise|Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''===
Pineapples appear as a fruit for [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] to eat. Upon eating one, Yoshi becomes [[Orange Yoshi|orange]], and his [[Juice]] meter is filled, allowing it to turn enemies into orange stationary [[Yoshi Platform|platforms]].

There is a large pineapple on a rooftop in [[Delfino Plaza]], blocking the red [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to [[Sirena Beach]]. The course cannot be accessed unless Yoshi eats the large pineapple to unblock the Warp Pipe before they can progress. Unlike regular pineapples, this pineapple does not change the Yoshi's color or fill its juice meter.

===''[[Mario Party 4]]''===
Pineapples appear in the [[Bowser]] [[minigame]] [[Fruits of Doom]], as one of the potential fruits on Bowser's list.

==In other media==
===''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''===
In the episode "[[Too Hot to Handle]]," a pineapple patch grows on [[Waki-Waki]]. [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] throw the pineapples at the [[Shy Guy]]s in the same manner as [[vegetable]]s are thrown in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

====''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''====
Pineapples, along with [[watermelon]]s and [[banana]]s, can be seen in a fruit cart while [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]] is escorting [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] through the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].
