'''Pink Gold Peach''' is a character in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. She first appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Like [[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]], Pink Gold Peach is a metallic-textured version of [[Princess Peach]]'s model. Her body frame is composed in a metallic film with rose gold color. The collar, panniers, and frills are in a darker shade of old rose color. The crown jewels, brooch, and earrings are platinum gray, though they are more hexagonal rather than rotund. She wears a biker suit while riding on motorbikes and ATVs, where the gloves and boots are in am old rose color. In ''Mario Sports Superstars'', she wears the same sports attire as Peach does. In golf and tennis, she wears a sleeveless minidress. In baseball, soccer, and horseback riding, Pink Gold Peach wears a uniform.

== Info ==
===Personality traits===
Pink Gold Peach's personality is equivalent to that of Princess Peach.

===Powers and abilities===
====Abilities====
*'''[[w:c:powerlisting:Vehicular Combat|Vehicular Combat]]''': Like every ''Mario Kart'' contestant, Pink Gold Peach can use items or bump into racers to her advantage.

===Speech===
Similar to [[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]], Pink Gold Peach's voice is equivalent to Peach, although it is filtered.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart 8'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
Pink Gold Peach appears as a playable character. Her weight class is Heavy.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
Pink Gold Peach is a highlight character in the [[Winter Tour (2019)|Winter Tour]]. Her special item is the coin box and her favored courses are:

*'''Three items per Item Box''': DK Pass T, Rainbow Road T, Rainbow Road R/T
*'''Two items per Item Box''': Mario Circuit 1T, Waluigi Pinball T, Mario Circuit 3T, DK Pass, Mario Circuit, Neo Bowser City R
{{Sectstub}}

==In ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]''==
Pink Gold Peach is one of the two unlockable characters alongside Metal Mario. She wears the same attire as Peach in order to meet the requirements for the sports criteria. This is the only game where Pink Gold Peach is seen with her hair down due to her having the same golf animations as Peach.
