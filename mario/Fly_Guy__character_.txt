'''Fly Guy''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Fly Guy is the namesake character representative of his [[Fly Guy|species]], and he has the same appearance as a red-robed Fly Guy.

===Behavior===
In his description, Fly Guy is said to be a recluse who does not see others normally.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Fly Guy is located on the rooftop of [[Mushroom Condo]]. When approached, [[Mr. E (Mario Party Advance)|Mr. E]] shows up, wanting to question Fly Guy about whether a UFO can be seen from the rooftop. Fly Guy responds that he can summon a UFO instantly by using his pair of colored paddles. Fly Guy then offers the player to help with calling the UFO. If the player's character accepts, this starts the [[Hey, UFO!]] quest.

In the quest, Fly Guy asks the player's character to simply follow his lead. Once the signalling is done properly, the UFO flies in and hovers right over their heads, causing Mr. E to panic. Afterward, Fly Guy gives the player the [[Morse Maker]] [[Gaddget]] as a reward for helping him summon the UFO.

In the epilogue, it is stated that Fly Guy is still waiting on the roof, presumably for the UFO.
