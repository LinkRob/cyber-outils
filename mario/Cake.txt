A '''cake''' is a real-world dessert which has made several appearances in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], in which they first appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is often associated with [[Princess Peach]].

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario 64]]'' / ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''===
In the intro, [[Princess Peach]] invites [[Mario]] to her [[Princess Peach's Castle|castle]] for some cake that she had baked. Because she has been kidnapped by [[Bowser]], Mario does not have it until the ending, when Peach is rescued. Mario is never seen eating the cake, although the "The End" has a graphic of cake with a wooden sign reading "Thank you" and has a small figure of both Mario and Peach on top. In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', there is also a figure of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], and [[Wario]] on the cake to account for them also being playable characters and are therefore also invited by Peach to have cake during the ending.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''[[Mario Party]]''===
One of the boards, [[Peach's Birthday Cake]], takes place on a giant cake supposedly made by Princess Peach, even though the characters are a lot smaller. This is the first time since ''Super Mario 64'' to associate a cake with Princess Peach.

===''[[Mario Party 3]]''===
Two cakes, each with three layers, appear in [[Game Guy's Sweet Surprise]]. The minigame features [[Big Chomp]] and [[Little Chomp]] racing to see who can finish their cake first, and the player has to predict the winner.

===''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''===
The Peach's Birthday Cake board returns in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==In ''[[Wario Land II]]''==
Cake is an object that [[Cook]]s carry with them on a fork. Cooks try to throw a Cake at [[Wario]], and if he eats one, he becomes [[Fat Wario]].

===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
A Cake can be made by cooking a [[Cake Mix]]. If Mario gets back [[Tayce T.]]'s stolen [[Frying Pan]], she gives him a Cake. The Cake is actually then needed to get [[Gourmet Guy]] away from blocking the spot who then drops the [[Cookbook]]. Later, [[Princess Peach]] must make a Cake for Gourmet Guy who has a craving for one (with this Cake being specifically called the [[Special Strawberry Cake]]). The Cake, altogether, restores 15 [[Flower Point|FP]] making it a rather useful item.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
Like in the previous game, a Cake can be made by only cooking a Cake Mix. Furthermore, Cakes can be bought at the [[Pianta Parlor]].

Later at the [[Glitz Pit]], Mario receives a Cake from one of his fans, and it fully restores him. There is also a second Cake but it is poisoned, so if Mario takes it, his partners cannot assist him in the battle, specifically against [[Chomp Country]]. It turns out that [[Rawk Hawk]] poisoned the Cake.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' / ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''==
Cake is only featured during the battle against [[Midbus]] in [[Fawful Theater]], where the audience sometimes supports Midbus by throwing a cake at him, although Bowser can either [[punch]] the cake or use his [[Vacuum Block]] on one to eat it instead. In either character's case, eating the cake heals 20 of their [[HP]].
