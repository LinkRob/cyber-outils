'''Vacuum Mushroom''' is a special [[mushroom]] which was created by [[Fawful]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. In the game, they often has their name shortened to "Vacuum Shroom," and the text that appears when obtaining one of them at the start of the game refers to the vacuum mushroom as a "Lucky (?) Mushroom." [[Bowser]] gets one after he is beaten by [[Mario (character)|Mario]] at the start of the game, facilitating his crash-landing in a secluded area in the [[Dimble Wood]] forest, which led him to cross paths with a merchant (who reveals himself to be [[Fawful]] in disguise).

== Description ==
A Vacuum Mushroom looks like every other Mushroom item, although it has black horizontal lines for eyes, and its "stalk" is green, with its cap being a brown-ish yellow-green with purple spots. The remake gives it a yellow cap.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
The Vacuum Shroom was given to [[Bowser]] by [[Fawful]] in [[Dimble Wood]] under the name "The Lucky Shroom," to trick Bowser into eating it. Bowser is told by his [[Magikoopa]] saviour to not eat the so-called "Lucky Mushroom," but eats it as he wants to beat [[Mario (character)|Mario]] just that badly. This causes Bowser to inhale everything in sight. His Magikoopa saviour manages to escape just before they get inhaled as well. He proceeded to [[Princess Peach's Castle]], where he inhaled [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Starlow]], [[Mario]], and [[Luigi]], [[Toadsworth]], [[Toadbert]], and the [[Toad (species)|Toads]]. The Vacuum Mushroom gives the ability to inhale objects and people on whoever eats it. When ever an object gets inhaled, it shrinks down to microscopic size.

A more controllable ability is gained from the [[Vacuum Block]].

==Trivia==
*The Vacuum Mushroom is one of the only two mushrooms (the other being the [[Blorb Mushroom]]) to be created by [[Fawful]].
*Bowser retains the Vacuum Mushroom's powers in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' as he used it to suck in the shattered [[Dream Stone]] pieces.
