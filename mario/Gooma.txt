'''Gooma''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
Gooma is an elderly [[Goomba]] woman who is the mate of [[Goompa]], the mother of [[Goomama]], the mother-in-law of [[Goompapa]] and the maternal grandmother of [[Goombario]] and [[Goombaria]].

===Etymology===
In a similar manner to how Goombario's name is a portmanteau of "[[Goomba]]" and "Mario," Gooma's name would appear to be a combination of "Goomba" and "ma," another word for "grandmother."

==History==
She is the grandmother of the [[Goomba Family]]. She is located in the larger house in [[Goomba Village]] and informs [[Mario]] on where to find [[Goompa]], her husband. Her grandson, [[Goombario]], states that she loves to tell him stories about her youth saying she was particularly popular with the boys.
