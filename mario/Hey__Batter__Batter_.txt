'''Hey, Batter, Batter!''' is an Item [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. The minigame's name is derived from a taunt in baseball, used when someone swings before the ball is pitched.

==Gameplay==
When [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]] throws the ball, the player has to hit it into one of the item signs the player desires. Timing is also important. If Baby Bowser catches the ball or it is missed, the player gets nothing.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|A}} &ndash; Swing bat

==In-game text==
* '''Game Rules''' &ndash; ''"Hit Baby Bowser's pitch. Hit an item with the ball to get that item. You can swing the bat only once."''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"The item you hit will change with the timing of your swing. Watch the ball and time your swing well."''
