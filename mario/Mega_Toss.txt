'''Mega Toss''' is one of [[Wario]]'s three [[Mad Move]]s in ''[[Wario World]]'', the other two being [[Piledriver]] and [[Wild Swing-Ding]].

==Description==
The Mega Toss is a move that involves Wario performing a powerful throw capable of defeating most enemies, including stronger ones. If the player holds the {{button|gcn|B}} button, Wario charges up and begins to flash a white-blue color. When the player releases the button, Wario throws the enemy, which is then usually defeated upon hitting the ground or a wall.
