The '''Resident Demon''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

== Profile ==
The Resident Demon is a large, skeletal [[Kremling]] with green eyes and two large claws.

== History ==
The Resident Demon is only found during [[Donkey Kong]]'s Ghost Train Ride in [[Creepy Castle]]. The Resident Demon will try to attack Donkey Kong with its large claws. It can also fire [[Skull Cart]]s at Donkey Kong as well. The Resident Demon is invincible and cannot be defeated in any way forcing Donkey Kong to avoid it's attacks at all costs.
