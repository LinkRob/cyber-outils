'''Hammers''' are an enemy featured in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. They only appear during the final boss battle against the [[Golden Diva]]. Hammers have an eye with spikes on their side, which is how they first appear when thrown by the Golden Diva, but upon hitting the ground, the Hammer retracts and instead takes on the appearance of a regular [[hammer]].
