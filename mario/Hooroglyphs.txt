'''Hooroglyphs''' are ancient letters which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They appear in [[Hoohoo Village]], [[Hoohoo Mountain]], [[Guffawha Ruins]], and [[Teehee Valley]]'s underground ruins. Whether their pictures are still used or not, although an [[Hoohooligan]] raise and used by the civilization and enter the jokes come into being by them. They mostly seem a mixture of lines and dots that create the picture of a person who is laughing. [[Hoohooros]], guardian of Hoohoo Mountain will hides behind the pillars carved with Hooroglyphs during the battle. 

==Trivia==
The name of the Hooroglyphs is taken from hieroglyphs, with the "hoo" prefix being used as a prefix, among other subjects in the game.
