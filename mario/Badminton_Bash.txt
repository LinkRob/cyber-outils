'''Badminton Bash''' is a Bonus [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' and a Special minigame in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.

==Gameplay==
Badminton Bash is a variation of {{w|badminton}}. Both characters can do either a smash or a drop shot. If a character manages to subdue the birdie on the opponent's side inside the lines, they earn a [[point]]. However, if a character subdues the birdie outside the lines, their opponent gets a point. Whoever gets 21 points before their opponent is the winner.

There is also a doubles mode that supports up to four players, with two characters on each team. In the minigame's original ''Mario Party 10'' iteration, the configuration is restricted to the first and second players against the third and fourth players.

''Mario Party 10'' has an associated mission named Beginning Badminton, which is simply accomplished by playing the minigame for the first time.
