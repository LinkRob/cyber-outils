'''Jelly Ultra''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:JellyUltraPM.png|frame|left]]
Jelly Ultra is a recipe [[Mario]] can make by giving a [[Jammin' Jelly]] and an [[Ultra Shroom]] to [[Tayce T.]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. Jelly Ultra combines the effects of its two respective ingredients, and it restores [[Flower Point|FP]] and [[Heart Point|HP]], putting the Jelly Ultra amongst the most effective healing items in the game.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
Jelly Ultra is created by [[Zess T.]] with the same ingredients required in ''Paper Mario''. It retains the same effects as in ''Paper Mario''.
