A '''Super Jump Block''', originally named '''Magic Note Block''', is a variant of a [[Jump Block]] which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It takes [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] up to [[Coin Heaven]] if the player presses the [[jump]] button just as Mario or Luigi are about to bounce off the block.

In ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', Super Jump Blocks are salmon-colored with a black music note, but in ''Super Mario 3D Land'' they are rainbow-colored with a white music note instead.
