|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Ice Rock''' is a special move used by a few bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Ice Rock is used, a large block of ice is dropped on a target.

==Effects==
With Ice Rock, it can deal moderate damage to one of Mario's party.

==Trivia==
*This move is very similar in appearance to [[Crystal]].
