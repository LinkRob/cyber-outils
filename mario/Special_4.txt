'''Special 4''' is the fourth special world in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' and the twelfth world overall.

As shown from the overworld, Special 4 takes place in a dark green forest at night, with small yellow mushrooms in the background. Special 4 consists of 6 levels, with 5 normal levels and an [[Airship]] level, and a [[Toad House]]. The bosses of this world are both [[Boom Boom]] and [[Pom Pom]], both of whom are fought at the end of [[Special 4-Airship]].
