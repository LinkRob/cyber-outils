'''Wario Shipyard''' (known as '''Wario's Galleon''' in Europe and Oceania) is the second course in [[Star Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. The course is in a sunken ship while in bad weather, and is, for the most part, underwater. This track returns in [[Mario Kart World]] were you drive mostly above the surface of the water as under water sections do not apear in this game anymore.

The course is covered in ramps, harmful fish, anchors, and pipes that push out a stronger current of air underwater, increasing the elevation in the player's position. There are also areas which are eligible for [[Glide|gliding]], like every other track in the game. Even Minecarts (holding gold) similar to those in [[Wario's Gold Mine]] can be seen in here.

This course may possibly be based on ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.
