The '''Shiny Mushroom''' is an item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. It is a stronger version of the [[Mushroom]].

==History==
==={{series|Paper Mario}}===
====''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''====
In this game, the Shiny Mushroom appears as one of many [[Stickers]] throughout the game. Using a Shiny Mushroom in battle or in the overworld restores 40 HP to [[Mario]]. In battle, if the {{button|3ds|a}} button is pressed right before the Shiny Mushroom is used up, it will restore 60 HP instead. Shiny Mushrooms are fairly common in the game as they can be purchased in the [[Item Shop]]s in [[Decalburg]], [[Outlook Point]], and [[Shaved-Ice Cave]] for 30 coins. They can also be peeled off of walls in certain spots of [[Whammino Mountain]] and [[Rumble Volcano]]. Additionally, the [[Traveling Toad]] can also give Mario Shiny Mushrooms for helping him out in [[Drybake Desert]] and [[Holey Thicket]].

====''Paper Mario: The Origami King''====
Shiny Mushrooms are now regular items in this game rather than stickers. Like with before, they are simply stronger versions of the basic Mushrooms where Mario can how heal up to 100 HP when used in battle or in the overworld. Shiny Mushrooms can be purchased for 300 coins in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario: The Origami King)|Toad Town]], [[Shogun Studios]], and [[Diamond Island]]. The [[Shiny Mushroom 3-Pack]] is also an item that involves purchasing Shiny Mushrooms in bulk that can only be purchased at [[Shroom City]] for 840 coins (meaning Mario will save 60 coins if he purchases this).
