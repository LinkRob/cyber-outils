'''Mercedes Rose Cochran''' (born March 22, 1972) is the voice actor for [[Rosalina]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. She was replaced by [[Kerri Kane|''Kerri Kane'']] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. She currently lives in Salem, Oregon and did voice acting for all three of Rosalina's appearances on the same day.
