'''Piranha Swingback''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] of [[Petey Piranha]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Petey Piranha spits out goop, forming a [[Polluted Piranha Plant]] near the tennis ball. The Polluted Piranha Plant then hits a drop shot with its muzzle back at the receiver.
