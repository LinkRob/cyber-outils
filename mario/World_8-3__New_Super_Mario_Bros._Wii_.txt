'''World 8-3''' is a level found in the game, ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

== Overview ==
This level takes place around a volcano with lots of lava. There are [[Lava Wave]]s at the bottom of the screen throughout the level that instantly kill.

== Walkthrough ==
The level starts out in a cavern full of [[lava]]. Throughout the level, Mario and company must contend with giant waves of lava that cause them to instantly lose a life upon contact. Hanging over the lava are chained platforms that tilt and sink when one of the characters step on them; these platforms can be found throughout most of the level's first half, with [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s placed on or around them. Shortly after the [[Checkpoint Flag]] is a series of tunnels that require the player to weave through incoming lava waves to pass safely. Following the tunnel is a series of weighted platforms that also sink if stepped on. [[Flame Chomp]]s are found in this area. At the end is a [[Warp Pipe]] that takes players to the level's [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].
== Enemies ==

== Star Coins ==
* '''Star Coin 1''': Shortly after the beginning of the level, the first Star Coin is floating under the second chain-hanging platform. Mario and co. must stand on the left side of the platform at the right time to descend to the Star Coin without touching the Lava.
* '''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is located in the narrow area shortly after the Checkpoint Flag, floating under a platform with a few other Coins. Mario and co. must wait for any Lava waves to pass, then walk to it.
* '''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is right near the level's second Flame Chomp. Mario and co. must again wait for any Lava waves to pass, then jump to it.
