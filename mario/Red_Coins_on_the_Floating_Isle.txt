'''Red Coins on the Floating Isle''' is the fourth [[mission]] of [[Whomp's Fortress]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', its [[Nintendo DS|NDS]] [[Reissue|remake]], ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and its 2021 enhanced port, ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:Floating Isle Coins.png|thumb|left|200px|Location of the power star.]]
This is the [[Red Coin]] mission of Whomp's Fortress. Despite the name of the mission, only two of the eight red coins are located on the floating islands in the level - unless, the "Floating Isle" in the title refers to Whomp's Fortress as a whole, which is indeed floating in the air. Several other Red Coins are found on top of a Thwomp and on a 'slide'. To get to the floating, spinning islands, Mario first has to kick down a giant plank of wood to build himself a bridge (Yoshi cannot do this in the remake so if you’re doing this before having unlocked Mario, it’s highly advised to select [[To the Top of the Fortress]] as that mission spawns a Mario Cap). However, it is easier to just get [[Hoot]] out of the tree at the starting point and simply fly and drop to the floating islands. The Power Star itself is located near the start of the level, next to two logs of wood. The first two are on the floating islands. Another is above the second [[Sliding Stone|Pushy Wall]]. Three more are near the Piranha Plants: one behind the first, one on the skinny ledge, and one obtained while riding the spinny thing. However, in ''Super Mario 64 DS'', the previous Red Coin is now on the new area of land that connects the whole course together, between three Piranha Plants in a flower patch.
