'''Kyotonbo''' (キョトンボ, ''Kyotonbo'') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. Resembling a dragonfly, Kyotonbo flies in a direction towards [[Mario]]. It attacks by homing in on him, trying to touch him with its harmful body. It can be defeated with any attack.
