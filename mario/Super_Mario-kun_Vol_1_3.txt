== Plot for "Koopaling Iggy! Becoming A Super Cool Battle Of Intelligence?!" ==
Mario and Luigi set their adventure into [[Iggy's Castle]] leaving Yoshi behind who cheers for them outside the castle; Mario assumes Yoshi stayed behind because he is scared. After a [[Pillar]] landed on the ground, Mario presumed Luigi to be dead; Luigi confirmed to Mario that he is alive. Mario looked at the bones that turned out to be [[Dry Bones]]. The squad of Dry Bones formed to their octopus formation attacking Mario and Luigi. The brother defeated them and Iggy showed up telling them it was their first act. Before Iggy prepared his first battle above the lava, Luigi threw Mario onto the platform. Iggy tricked Mario into thinking that his head popped off from his body. To Mario's surprise, Iggy rams Mario with his shell and Mario threw fireballs at Iggy which were harmless to him. Mario used his "ultrasonic" mirror and scratched it to irritate Iggy. In response, Iggy hides his head then Mario glued Iggy's shell with [[Super Fast-Drying Glue]]. As a result, Iggy cannot move out his head. As he tried to wriggle out of his shell, he falls onto the glue which splashed onto Mario and Luigi; they are unable to move. While calling for Yoshi's help, Yoshi assumes that Mario and Luigi are late.
