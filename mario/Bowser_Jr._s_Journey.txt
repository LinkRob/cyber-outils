'''''Bowser Jr.'s Journey''''' (Japanese name '''''Koopa Jr. RPG''''') is a side story of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' and a sequel to [[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser|''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'']]. Like its [[Reissue|remake]] [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|predescesor]], the plot runs parallel to that of [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'']] and centers on [[Bowser Jr.]], who goes on an journey adventure to cure [[Blorbs|the blorbs]] himself after they cause [[Goombas|numerous minions]] to balloon up to an immobile state, while also dealing with a [[Best Fitness Friends|mysterious group]], whose secretly plan to take over [[Bowser's Castle]].

Its gameplay is nearly identical to that of it predecessor.

==Story==
{{Spoiler}}
In [[Bowser's Castle]], [[Kamek]], [[Bowser]], and [[Bowser Jr.]] found out that all the [[Goomba|Goombas ]]<nowiki/>in Bowser's Castle have gotten the [[blorbs]]. A small [[Birdley|messenger bird]] shows up in Bowser's throne room and accidentally reports him of a invitation meeting against the blorbs being held at [[Princess Peach's Castle|Peach's castle]]. Angry that he wasn't invited, Bowser takes off for the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] with Kamek in [[Koopa Clown Car|his Clown Car]]. Meanwhile in the outskirts, [[Fawful]] sends a trio of his own minions, the [[Best Fitness Friends]], [[Kaley]], [[Dieter]], and [[Beef]], to infiltrate the castle, while he goes to capture Bowser at Peach's Castle.

On throne room, Bowser Jr. is seen fighting [[Captain Goomba (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Goomba]] and his Goomba squad multiple times for sparring practice. Nearby, [[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]] and [[Roy Koopa|Roy]] discuss what to do about the blorbs, only to interrupt when the Best Fitness Friends appear to sell them their products. At first, Roy shoos them away initially, they soon realize that their formula, [[Skeletone Formula:D]], is able to cure the blorbs. Roy receives the recipe to make more Skeletone, which requires a [[Giga-carrot]], an Omega Onion, and Ever Ice to create. [[Ludwig von Koopa|Ludwig]] discusses a plan with the rest of the [[Koopalings]] to find the ingredients and cure the blorbs, while Bowser Jr., overhearing, sets off to find the cure himself, thinking it is what his dad would want of him. Ludwig decides to train Junior by teaching him the ropes of battle, using Captain Goomba, [[Captain Boo|Boo]], [[Captain Shy Guy (Mario & Luigi series)|Shy Guy]], and [[Captain Koopa Troopa|Koopa Troopa]] as examples. Bowser Jr. insists that he already knows how to battle as he does not like Ludwig patronizing him. Bowser Jr. goes on to prove himself in subsequent battles. Roy joins Junior's team, followed by Ludwig and [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton]]. [[Larry Koopa|Larry]] challenges Junior, followed by [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]] and [[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]] together, and finally the four minion captains again with [[King Bob-omb]]. Junior and the Koopalings (except [[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]], who stays behind to guard Bowser's Castle) set off to [[Dimble Wood]] to obtain the Giga-Carrot. In the distance, the Best Fitness Friends begin their plan to take over the castle.

==Gameplay==
Just like ''Minion Quest'', ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'' gameplay is dependent on building a squad capable of defeating an enemy troop. However, several noteable changes have been made.
*The maximum amount of troopers in a squad has increased from 8 to 9.
*A First Officer can be added to the squad. It must be Kamek or one of the Koopalings. Each First Officer has their own First Officer Skills that are similar to [[Captain Commands]] from the previous game.
*Depending on how you place your troopers, a formation buff can be granted to the squad.
