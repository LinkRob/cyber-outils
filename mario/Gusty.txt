'''Gusties''' are long, happy, wind ghost-like creatures that appear as enemies in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. They fly across the screen at rapid speed to try and attack a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. Gusties are often encountered in large groups, and can be eaten to be converted into a [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]. Gusties are mainly found in sky-themed levels, and they behave similarly to [[Bullet Bill]]s.

== In ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' ==
[[File:Gusty SMW2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Gusties are first seen in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' where they are commonly found in sky levels such as [[What's Gusty Taste like?]] and [[Don't Look Back!]]. They appear out of the air and fly towards Yoshi, they can be defeated with any attack.

== In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' ==
Gusties appear again in ''Yoshi's Island DS'' commonly found in levels such as [[Windblown Wilderness]] and [[Superhard Acrobatics]]. Unlike the last game, the usually fly offscreen rather than appearing in thin air. Once again, any attack can beat them, tossing an Egg at one will make the Gusties appear in the [[Museum]]. 

== In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' ==
Gusties return in ''Yoshi's New Island'' with the same behavior as the previous games. Once again, any attack can defeat them.
