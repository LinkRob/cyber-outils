'''X Bosses''' are special type of bosses that first appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and reappear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They are stronger versions of the regular bosses that were fought throughout the game. The X Bosses can be fought in the [[Challenge Node]] of [[Bowser's Body]] in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' and they are fought in the [[Battle Ring]] which is located in [[Pi'illo Castle]] in ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''. It should be noted that the X Bosses are stronger versions of the bosses that were encountered throughout the game, and also have a different color palette. ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' was also the first game to feature Giant X Bosses.
