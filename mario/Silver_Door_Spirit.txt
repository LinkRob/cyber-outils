'''Silver Door Spirits''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Silver Door Spirits are living, horned, silver-colored door knockers serving as a variant of [[Door Spirit]]s.

===Behavior===
Like their parent species, Silver Door Spirits shoot bolts of electricity at [[Wario]]. A difference with a Silver Door Spirit is that for every three hits or [[Mad Move]] it takes, the Silver Door Spirit charges up to shoot a much larger and more powerful ray of electricity.

==In ''Wario World''==
Silver Door Spirits are only encountered in [[Horror Manor]].
