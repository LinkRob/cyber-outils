*''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)]]''<br>}}The '''X-Naut Fortress''' is [[Sir Grodus|Grodus]]' main base of operations in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
===Chapter 7: Mario Shoots the Moon===
This location is secretly located on the [[Mushroom Moon]]. The fortress also serves as the home to the X-Naut Legion and the [[Shadow Sirens]]. This is the place where [[Princess Peach]] was taken to. The last Crystal Star, the [[Crystal Star]], is held here and [[Mario]] and his friends had to use the cannon in [[Fahr Outpost]] in order to reach this place and fight [[Lord Crump]] to get the Star. Grodus traveled between the [[Mushroom World]] and his fortress using a Teleportation Machine, which has one end located in his laboratories and the other end located in [[Rogueport Sewers]]. The base is also the home of [[TEC-XX]], which is the main computer of the whole fortress. Also, after Mario gets the 6th Crystal Star, [[Sir Grodus|Grodus]] takes Princess Peach and leaves the Fortress (possibly giving it to Lord Crump) so he can go to the [[Palace of Shadow]]. After Mario successfully destroys Magnus von Grapple 2.0, he gets the seventh [[Crystal Stars|Crystal Star]]. After that, the fortress explodes, and Mario is unable to go back until the [[Shadow Queen]] is defeated.
