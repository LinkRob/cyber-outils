The '''Standard YS''' is a kart for [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. Yoshi's other karts are the [[Egg 1]] and the [[Cucumber]]. 

== Description ==
[[File:StandardKartYS.png|thumb|150px|right|Data-rendered model of the Standard YS.]]
With great [[speed]], great [[acceleration]], extremely good [[handling]], yet fairly low [[drift]], this kart is considered the best standard kart in the game. The only flaw is the [[weight]]. The YS stands for Yoshi. It is green and has a Yoshi egg on the front. The Standard YS shares all of its stats with [[Shy Guy]]'s [[Standard SG]].

Once all cups in the Mirror Class have been completed, any racer can use this kart. Yoshi later got a [[Standard Kart M]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

During races, a CPU controlled Yoshi may choose this kart more frequently than his other karts.
