'''Triple Green Shells''' are a triumvirate of [[Green Shell]]s in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. By using the item, three Green Shells circle around the user's [[kart]], acting as a shield. The user can then throw the Green Shells individually. There is a [[Red Shell]] counterpart to the item named [[Triple Red Shells]].

In ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', Triple Green Shells are the [[Special Item (Mario Kart series)|Special Item]] usable by only [[Koopa Troopa]] and [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]], as well as [[Petey Piranha]] and [[King Boo]], who can use nearly any Special Item.
