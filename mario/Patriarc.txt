'''Patriarc''' is a character appearing in [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']].

== Description ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Patriarc is a rotund Concordian man with teal skin. He wears a sailor outfit, and his hat has a compass on it, giving him a resemblance to his son. His mouth is covered by a moustache.

=== Personality ===
Patriarc cares a lot for his son [[Arc]], putting him in a capsule to protect him from the villains when they get captured.

== History ==
When [[Zokket]] desecreated the [[Uni-Tree]] to further his plans on reviving his [[Reclusa|dark god]], which led to [[Concordia]] being sundered, Patriarc and his son were kidnapped by [[Snaptor|Snaptors]] and taken to one of the [[Great Lighthouse Islands]], presumably the one pertaining to the [[Lushgreen Sea]]. Patriarc locked his son in a capsule and threw it in the sea to give [[Arc]] an escape route. Meanwhile, he stayed behind to monitor Zokket's [[Zokket Army|forces]], leaving behind notes detailing the enemy's plans in each Great Lighthouse.

== Messages ==
Patriarc leaves behind notes with messages regarding the [[Zokket Army]]'s plans in each Great Lighthouse in his efforts to provide information for any heroes that enter the Great Lighthouses.

=== First Note ===
The first note that Patriarc left behind that [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] find is in the same room where they and the [[Gobblick]] squared off in the [[Lushgreen Sea]]'s [[Great Lighthouse Islands|Great Lighthouse Island]], just before the elevator leading to the top. In this note, Patriarc questions why the [[Extension Corps]] was bringing Sprite Bulbs, a hostile variation of [[Sprite Bulb]] that got cut off from Connectar.<blockquote>There's a fishy trio workin' like crazy trawling up a whole mess of Spite Bulbs. What in crustaceans are they planning to do with so many of the rotten things? "How many Spite Bulbs do we need for this?" "Who knows? Just get all you can." I'm logging all this ominous stuff, hoping this info gets to a hero who can help.

From history's front lines, Patriarc.</blockquote>A potential reason is to power up the guardians that [[Zokket]] set up in each Great Lighthouse to prevent any Wattanists from disrupting his plans.

=== Second Note ===
Patriarc's second note appears in the Great Lighthouse Island of the [[Color-Full Sea]], more specifically in a room with nicer Concordian persons following a patrolling Concordian guard area. Here, [[Zokket]] has his name mentioned by his goons, who praise him and wonder what happens in the next stage of his plans. When read, it contains the following information:<blockquote>As the people are forced to make Glohm all day and night, I've heard this: "We're so lucky to have our great Lord Zokket." "I got goosebumps when the land split apart!" "I bet that the next stage of his plan will be even more incredible. I can't wait to see it!" I am fit to burst with anger, but I'll keep recording, for the sake of the world's hero.

From history's front lines, Patriarc.</blockquote>

=== Third Note ===
Patriarc's third note appears in a room with a [[Save Block]] and tanks containing Spite Bulbs following a Concordian guard section in in the Great Lighthouse Island of the [[Brrrning Sea]]. It is revealed here that Zokket is talking to an egg, which appears in his [[Fortress]]. When read, it contains the following information:<blockquote>Zokket's here, the big fish. He's always creepy, but today, he's even more off-kilter. He was talking to an egg. A weird egg. "Only 67 more... Almost done... Heheheh..." "Just wait a little longer. Very soon now..." I was shaking in my boots but kept recording. It's my duty to the hero who will save us.

From history's front lines, Patriarc.</blockquote>The [[Stormstar Sea]] chapter's ending reveals that the egg contains a monster called [[Reclusa]] who seeks to destroy communication and connection, drowning all beings in solitude.

=== Final Note ===
Patriarc left his last note in the [[Gulchrock Sea]]'s Great Lighthouse Island. It is found on the fourth floor, northeast of the main elevator. Its message contains information on a [[Glohm]] ray, and implies that Patriarc is worried about not caring about his son if he got zapped by it. Patriarc also shares more of his thoughts on the relationship with the egg that Zokket keeps in his fortress:<blockquote>If I got hit by this Glohm ray, would I stop caring about my son? I get that Zokket wants to make the world lonely, but I don't understand why. He sure likes talking to his egg though. Maybe even he enjoys "parenting" it.

...Wait. Who is that?

No! It can't be!</blockquote>The text afterwards is too messy to read as [[Snoutlet]] points out (though the game treats it as the note merely ending there), implying that Patriarc lost control of his handwriting upon discovering someone there. This also obscures Patriarc's discovery from the player.
