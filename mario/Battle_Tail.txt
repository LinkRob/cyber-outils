'''Battle Pains''' is the fifth level of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.  

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 3 [[Tanoomba]]s and 5 [[Lakipea]]s. The enemy captain is a Lakipea.

The 2nd battle contains 4 Tanoombas and 4 Lakipeas. The enemy captain is a [[Paratroopea]].

The final battle contains 5 Tanoombas and 3 Lakipeas. The enemy captain is a Tanoomba.

No allies can be gained from this level.
