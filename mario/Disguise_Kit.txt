The '''Disguise Kit''' is an item from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It can be used when the Bros. visit [[Offandon Island]].

== Description ==
The disguise kit is composed of wearable masks that resemble the heads of [[Concordia]]'s inhabitants. The masks have slightly mismatched eyes, with their faces bearing permanent smiles.

== Role in ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] use the Disguise Kit to sneak through Offandon Island's plaza, which the [[Koopa Troop]] inhabits. They cannot use the [[Return Pipe]] or Bros. Moves with the disguises on, and are voiceless while jumping and/or using hammers. The Bros. can remove their disguises during departure from the plaza.

Luigi removes his disguise at one point when the Wayaway Soda Vendor sells his soda to the Bros., resulting in them being found out and jailed as [[Bowser]] turned the inhabitants against the Bros. during his takeover.
