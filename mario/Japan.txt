'''Japan''' is a real-world country located on [[Earth]]. It is an archipelago located in the eastern part of [[Asia]]. Japan has made some appearances in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media, the first instance of which is ''[[Family Computer Golf: Japan Course]]''. Japan's capital city is [[Tokyo]], which is sometimes featured as a setting itself.
