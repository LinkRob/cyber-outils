The '''Gritzy Desert''' is a large, barren desert in the northeastern-most part of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] appearing in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. There is also evidence of remains of train tracks or mine-carts tracks.

In the game, the Mario Bros. arrive here during their quest to find the [[Cobalt Star]] pieces. The [[Koopaseum]] is located in the middle of the desert and [[Gritzy Caves]] is found below the desert. When [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]] attempt to enter the Koopaseum through the front door, they run away when a hoard of Shroobs from inside chases after them. After the group manages to escape all but one Shroob, Mario meets [[Kylie Koopa]], who informs him about a secret entrance to the Koopaseum that is unlocked by activating four [[Dry Bones]] statues scattered around the desert, while Luigi, Baby Mario, and Baby Luigi are chased by the Shroob, eventually defeating it.

==Trivia==
*If the Mario bros. try to enter the main part of the Gritzy Desert before opening the Koopaseum front door, they will see [[Shroob]]s blocking the way.
*In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', the stage [[Mushroomy Kingdom]] features a remix of Gritzy Desert's music from ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'' among other tracks. This arrangement also has an extended part that was not in the original track.
*A train that resembles to the [[Barrel Train]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' can be found buried in the desert.
*Gritzy Desert bears a storyline of the novel ''[[wikipedia:The Time Machine|The Time Machine]]''. Despite the [[Koopaseum]] being a colosseum, the time traveler visits a place via his time machine, in a desert, and the building is also dome-shaped. He then later has to enter an underground set of caves later on in the same place. This is possibly a mere coincidence, however the plot line in the game is similar, as the heroes visit a desert via time travel, enter a dome like building, and visit a underground set of caves.
