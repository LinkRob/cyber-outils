'''Dragoneel's Undersea Grotto''', or '''Sparkling Waters-5''', is the fifth level of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Above the Cheep Cheep Sea]] or [[Urchin Shoals]], and its own completion unlocks [[Larry's Torpedo Castle]]. 
== Layout==
The player starts in a small area with a [[Warp Pipe]] sticking out of the water, which takes them underwater.

The ground underwater is made entirely of [[quicksand]]. After the player exits the Warp Pipe, there is a [[? Block]] with a power-up, followed by a school of [[Eep Cheep]]s. At the second ? Block, a [[Dragoneel]] appears and circle around it, following after the player when they continue onward. The rest of the stage continues with Eep Cheeps, ? Blocks, and various [[current]]-producing Warp Pipes to help the player move faster. Near the end of the level, there is a large school of Eep Cheeps that may hinder the player's progression. Another Warp Pipe then takes the player directly to the [[Goal Pole]].

The player can skip the underwater sections by using the [[P-Acorn]] to fly over the wall at the beginning of the level.
==Star Coins==
* '''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is located in plain sight, with [[coin]]s circling it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is located between two rows of [[Brick Block]]s following the Checkpoint Flag, requiring the use of [[Super Mario (form)|Super Mario]] to obtain it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Almost submerged in quicksand is a [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the area with the final Star Coin. Two [[Baby Dragoneel]] appear in this small area.
