'''Melody Bay''' is an area within [[Tadpole Pond]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is where [[Mario]] can play music by jumping across [[tadpole]]s, who are arranged across a pond, serving as musical notes within the lines, effectively forming a small a musical score.
