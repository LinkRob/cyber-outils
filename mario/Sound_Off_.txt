'''Sound Off?''' is a [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Badge]] that can be obtained from achieving a 100% [[completion]] rating on ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. The badge has the team behind the game mimicking the sound effects in the game.
