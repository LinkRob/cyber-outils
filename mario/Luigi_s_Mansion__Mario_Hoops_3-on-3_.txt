'''Luigi's Mansion''' is a basketball court which appears in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. In Tourney mode, Luigi's Mansion is the second court of the [[Flower Tourney]].

The location was succeeded by a different [[Luigi's Mansion (Mario Sports Mix)|Luigi's Mansion]] in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''.

==Description==
The court takes place within the mansion of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|same name]], specifically within the [[Foyer (Luigi's Mansion)|Foyer]]. The main gimmick of the court is the [[Ghost]]s which float around the court and grab the ball if it comes within their reach. These Ghosts appear to consist of [[Gold Ghost]]s, light-green [[Purple Puncher]]s, and purple [[Grabbing Ghost]]s, although they act no differently from one another. A character can easily retrieve the ball by [[jump]]ing up at the ghost.
