'''Winter Tour''' was the eighty-eight tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] that began on January 24, 2023 and ended on February 7, 2023. This tour was also a rerun that began on January 23, 2023 to February 6, 2023 and January 21, 2023 to February 4, 2023.

The Tour featered [[Singapore Speedway 3]], [[Bangkok Rush 2]] and [[Bangkok Rush 3]] who are variants of [[Singapore Speedway]] and [[Bangkok Rush]]. This tour also featered R/T variants of [[Tokyo Blur]] that were added in [[Tokyo Blur 2]], [[Tokyo Blur 3]] and [[Toykyo Blur 4]].
