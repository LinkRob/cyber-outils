'''Moneybags'''<ref>Loe, Casey. ''New Super Mario Bros.'' Player's Guide. [https://i.imgur.com/W3ehp2b.jpg Page 13.]</ref> (originally spelled '''Money Bags'''<ref>M. Arakawa. ''Super Mario 64'' Player's Guide. Page 11.</ref> and also known in singular as '''Moneybag'''<ref>Michael Knight, Prima Games Nintendo DS Pocket Guide</ref>) are purse-like enemies with coins in them, appearing in games such as ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', and ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. They are living money purses that have their zippers undone, with two glowing yellow eyes inside, and have yellow-orange stubby feet. These cowardly coin purses are seen disguising themselves as [[Coin]]s from afar but as soon as one approaches, the Moneybag reveals itself and proceeds to hop away. Being a bag of money, Moneybags, yield a lot of [[Coin]]s (and in rare cases, a [[1-Up Mushroom]] too) when defeated. Of course, Moneybags are rather rare enemies and appear very uncommonly in the games they're in. Since ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', Moneybags have been replaced by [[Coin Coffer]]s.


==History==
===''Super Mario 64'' / ''Super Mario 64 DS''===
[[Image:Moneybag Discovery.jpg|thumb|left|Mario near a Moneybag in ''Super Mario 64''.]]
[[Image:Moneybag Snow.png|thumb|right|A Moneybag in ''Super Mario 64 DS''.]]
Moneybags make their debut in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and later appear in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' They appear in [[Snowman's Land]], and are defeated by jumping on them or tackling them. They are disguised as a coin until [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Wario]], or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] comes near, and then they reveal themselves and flee by hopping away. They release five coins when defeated. In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can wrack up heaps of coins from a Moneybag by swallowing a Moneybag, making an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], and tossing the Moneybag egg at some [[Spindrift]] lots, as the egg ricochets of each nearby enemy within the radius before hitting a wall you will get 5 coins for each Spindrift killed and each enemy the Moneybag egg hit wracking up over 10 coins for each enemy, this is really useful when going for the 100-Coin [[Power Star|Star]].

===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
[[Image:Moneybag Painting.jpg|thumb|right|A Moneybag hopping in ''New Super Mario Bros.''.]]
Moneybags return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', in this game, they appear in [[World 5-2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5-2]] and [[World 6-3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-3]]. In this game, they disguise themselves as a Coin until the plumbers approach at which point, they hop away from [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] in the pit-filled areas they're found in, hoping that the plumbers will fall to their death. [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] can jump on them or hit with fireballs five times and will spit out a coin each time they are hit. If defeated, they will release a [[1-Up Mushroom]], additionally, a Moneybag can be instantly defeated with a ground pound to get the coin lot in one shot. If a Moneybag is destroyed with a [[Mega Mushroom]], it will not drop any Coins.

===Mini Games===
Moneybags also appear in [[Coincentration]] and [[Intense Coincentration]], two of the puzzle mini-games in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. The former reappears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. During both the minigames, [[Wario]] punches a Moneybag in the air and as it bounces down into the pit below, several coins are also seen descending and land in the various brick blocks, Wario must select blocks that have coins in them and up to three coins may fall in one block, the game ends if Wario picks a block with no coins in it. The Moneybag drops 40 Coins in Coincentration and 16 Coins in Intense Coincentration.

==Trivia==
*The Moneybag is very similar to both the [[Coin Coffer]] and the [[Starbag]] and could be the inspiration for both enemies.
*Although they look like clams, Moneybags are actually money purses with their zipper undone along with eyes and feet. Due to the lack of a known name at the time when a FAQ was being made, the creator just simply called Moneybags '''Fake Coins''', causing some people to confuse their names.
