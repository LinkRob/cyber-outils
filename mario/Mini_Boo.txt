'''Mini Boos''', also named '''Little Boos''', are a small variation of [[Boo]]s which first appear in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==In ''Yoshi's Story''==
Little Boos, as they are named for the game, are enemies encountered in [[Ghost Castle]]. They can be found in groups. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] throws an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at one of them, the Little Boos stop moving and swoop at Yoshi. It is impossible to defeat Little Boos entirely, as more just appear from off-screen. If Yoshi eats a [[Heart Fruit]] and does a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] near Little Boos, they turn into [[Lucky Fruit]], much like [[Shy Guy]]s do.

==In ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''==
Boo's [[Offensive Power Shot]], [[Boo Blast (Power Shot)|Boo Blast]], has him summon several Little Boos to form into a large tennis racket, hitting the ball with it, then allowing them to cloak the ball, although it is still possible for the receiver to return the ball.

==In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''==
Transparent Little Boos can be found at [[Ghostly Galaxy]], on the Boo Race Goal Planet during the mission [[A Very Spooky Sprint]]. They are not enemies, and only come a short distance from the planet's surface before disappearing.

==In ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''==
Mini Boos are featured as an item. Using a Mini Boo makes the ball invisible for about six seconds. The ball is still tangible and can be caught. In Challenge mode, Mini Boos are found in the map for [[Wario City]], and are required for [[King Boo]] to join the player's team.
