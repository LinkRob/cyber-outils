'''Chortlebot''' is the third boss of [[Rocky Range]] in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Battle==
While [[Wario]] is standing, the lights turn on and the music starts to play. It appears Chortlebot going down laughing.

To beat Chortlebot, Wario should grab to the poles, spin faster and hit its head until its weak spot is exposed. Hit him above by spinning on the poles and hit it three times. Chortlebot recovers itself with some colored balls turning into spiky balls as it throws them around.
{{Sectstub}}

==Trivia==
*His design could be a reference from [[Rudy the Clown]].
*He is the only boss to have the music to speed up fast when enraged.
*The boss music is a remix version of [[Wonky Circus]].
