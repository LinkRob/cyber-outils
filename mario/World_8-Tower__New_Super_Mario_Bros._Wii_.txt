'''World 8-Tower''' is a level in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. It is the first tower of [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]].

== Overview ==
This was the last tower of [[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]. Upon completion of this level, the player unlocks World 8-4.

== Walkthrough ==
This tower is the first level to feature [[Fire Bar]]s. The level begins at the bottom of the tower, above a pool of [[lava]]. The first Fire Bar is found next to two [[? Block]]s, one of which contains a power-up. Moving brown rock platforms that allow the player to progress are found, along with two more Fire Bars. Another Fire Bar is found along with a ? Block, where the player can climb up platforms on either the left or the right of a wall in the center. A double Fire Bar appears at the top of the section. On the right side, a [[Warp Door]] can be found in the wall, leading to a room with a [[Roulette Block]]. Above the door is a [[Hidden Block]] with a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. Another section containing moving platforms and ? Blocks follows. The player then encounters a section of climbing semi-solid platforms while avoiding Fire Bars, along with a moving platform. A section with a large amount of moving platforms is found, with [[coin]]s indicating a safe area that the player can stand without getting crushed. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is then found, followed by another moving platform, along with a Fire Bar. A [[Brick Block]] containing a 1-Up Mushroom is next to the Fire Bar. A final Fire Bar is found, along with two moving platforms. The boss door is found at the top of the area, with two ? Blocks above moving platforms, one of which contains a power-up.

[[Kamek]] is fought at the end, where he uses his magic to make the battle room's platforms move to the left. Kamek teleports often while the platforms move. His magic transforms one platform into one or more enemies. Enemies can vary between [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, [[Goomba]]s, [[Bob-omb]]s, [[Monty Mole]]s, [[Thwomp]]s, [[Buzzy Beetle]]s, and [[Spiny|Spinies]]. An occasional [[Super Mushroom]] may also pop up, as well as [[Coin]]s.

== Star Coins ==
* '''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is near the beginning of the level atop a Firebar.
* '''Star Coin 2''': Directly below the Checkpoint Flag is a hidden area with a door that can only be accessed by the moving platforms directly below. Once inside, Mario and co. have to avoid the faster than normal Firebars, make it to the highest platform, then jump up to get the second Star Coin.
* '''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is to the immediate left of the Checkpoint Flag, under a platform with a Firebar. Mario and co. must duck-slide through the gap to get into the area (unless not Super) while avoiding the Firebar to get the Star Coin.
