'''Safety Badge''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When the user equips the Safety Badge, they become immune to [[Mute]] and [[Poison (status effect)|Poison]] attacks, and they are also protected from any status ailment except instant death. The item can be obtained by defeating [[Hidon]] in [[Sunken Ship]].
