'''Nolrem''' is the [[Flopside]] counterpart of [[Merlon]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Nolrem does not help much during the game except for just to greet the player and wish them farewell. He does not hold anything in his home, unlike Merlon, and does not have the [[Light Prognosticus]]. His color scheme is different to that of Merlon but he has the same figure. His name is Merlon's spelled backwards.
