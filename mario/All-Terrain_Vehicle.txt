'''All-Terrain Vehicles''' (abbreviated as '''ATVs'''), known as '''Quad Bikes''' or just '''Quads''' in the British English version, are vehicles first appearing in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', alongside [[kart]]s and [[bike]]s.

The basic model of the ATV for all characters is the [[Standard ATV]], which varies in color and paint design depending on the character, similar to the other Standard vehicles. The game considers all the ATV bodies "karts," as CPU players can be found using ATVs when the CPU vehicle body options are set to "Karts Only." As with karts, ATVs can perform the [[Mini-Turbo#Firehopping, sliding and firesliding|fire-hopping and demon-sliding]] techniques in ''Mario Kart 8''.

When riding ATVs, [[Princess Peach]], [[Pink Gold Peach|her metal form]], [[Daisy|Princess Daisy]], [[Rosalina]], [[Pauline]], and [[Peachette]] wear their biker outfits. Characters riding ATVs share most animations with those in bikes yet have slightly different idling and [[drift]]ing animations.

The [[Bone Rattler]] becomes available through purchase of the [[Animal Crossing x Mario Kart 8|''Animal Crossing'' × ''Mario Kart 8'']] DLC pack.

Two new ATVs, [[Splat Buggy]] and [[Inkstriker]], appear in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' for the [[Nintendo Switch]]. Both vehicles are based on ''{{wp|Splatoon}}'' and appear alongside the new [[Inkling]]s introduced in this game.

ATVs do not reappear in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''; however, ATVs do reappear in ''[[Mario Kart World]]'', though none of the ones from ''Mario Kart 8'' and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' reappear. Unlike in the aforementioned games, Peach, Daisy, Rosalina, and Pauline do not wear jumpsuits when driving ATVs by default; instead, they are costumes for them.
