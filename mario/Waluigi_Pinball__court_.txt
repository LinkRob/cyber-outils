'''Waluigi Pinball''' is a court in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''. It features a giant robotic [[Waluigi]] that spits pinballs, and a fake space theme. The court is based on the ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' course of the [[Waluigi Pinball|same name]].

The primary feature of this board is the giant, robotic Waluigi to the right of this court. This robot will occasionally spit out a giant metal pinball on either side of the court, no matter which game is being played. This ball moves slowly around the court. If the pinball hits a character or if it touches a bumper located on the outside of the court, it will speed up and change direction. Any character who is hit by the ball will be momentarily stunned. In dodgeball and volleyball, there can be no more than one pinball on the court at a time. However, while playing hockey, there can be as many as three pinballs on the court at the same time. The ball or puck, depending on which sport is being played, will bounce off the pinball if it comes in contact with it and send it moving in another direction. At the end of both sides of the court, there is a small exit which allows the balls to leave the arena.

Waluigi Pinball can be unlocked by either winning 35 matches in a specific sport (except for Basketball, as it is unavailable for that sport, and Hockey, as it can be played in the Star Cup), or by winning a match while playing on this court in tournament mode.
