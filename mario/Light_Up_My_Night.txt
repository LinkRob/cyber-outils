'''Light Up My Night''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
Both characters light their torches, focusing on the first character in particular before zooming out.

==Gameplay==
During the minigame, each character is holding a lit torch. There are unlit candles scattered along the dark maze of the board, and the player must press {{button|gcn|A}} when their character is front of a candle to light it. Whoever lights five candles first is the winner.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Light a candle

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Scramble through the dark maze to find candles and set them ablaze! Whoever lights five candles first wins!''
* '''Advice''': ''The candle locations and stage layouts change each time you play.''
