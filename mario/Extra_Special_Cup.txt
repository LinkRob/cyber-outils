The '''Extra Special Cup''' is the final [[Extra Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart Super Circuit]]''. It has tracks that come from ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. Completing the [[Special Cup]] with at least one hundred [[coin]]s and winning first place in the cup unlocks this cup.

==Tracks==
*[[Koopa Beach 2]]
*[[Ghost Valley 3]]
*[[Vanilla Lake 2]]
*[[Rainbow Road]]
