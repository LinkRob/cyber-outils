'''Flame Chomp Coin''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
Flame Chomp Coins are one of the [[coin]] items that allow for certain enemies to be transformed into stronger forms. They can be obtained by either defeating [[Flame Chomp]]-type enemies or by winning them in the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] game in [[Toad House]]s. Using three Flame Chomp Coins can transform a [[Chain Chomp]] into "Chain Chomp & Flame Chomp," which can be further upgraded to "Chain Chomp & Flame Chomps" by using six more. A [[Wiggler]]s can transform into "Wiggler & Flame Chomp" by using three Flame Chomp Coins.
