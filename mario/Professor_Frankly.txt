'''Professor Frankly''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is an elderly [[Goomba]] who helps [[Mario]] throughout his journey.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Professor Frankly is a former teacher at the [[University of Goom]], and [[Goombella]] was one of his students. Professor Frankly lives in [[Rogueport]]. He is knowledgeable, something that is shown throughout the game.

Frankly is in Rogueport to study the legendary treasure underneath the town, which most of the game centers around. After collecting a [[Crystal Star]] in the game and visiting the Thousand-Year Door, Mario will go to him for guidance on what to do next.

Frankly does not accompany Mario on his journey because he is not fit out for travel on the road, something that he tells Mario while training him.

==Trivia==
*Professor Frankly is infamous of being one of few characters in the game to [[Breaking the Fourth Wall|break the fourth wall]].
*[[Ishnail]]'s house is located behind Professor Frankly's house.
