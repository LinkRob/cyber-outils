'''''Mario is Missing! CD-ROM Deluxe''''' is an [[Reissue#Enhanced ports|enhanced port]] of ''[[Mario is Missing! (PC)|Mario is Missing!]]'' Due to the differences in formatting, the CD-ROM is able to hold more data than the floppy disk version, allowing for enhancements to be made.

==Differences==
''Mario is Missing! CD-ROM Deluxe'' included voice acting for its characters, in addition to providing live-action footage for the backgrounds of some of the different cities that the player can visit. The CD-ROM includes 127 different video clips. In addition, characters from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] replace some of the non-playable characters during phone calls that appeared in the previous release; however, none of the written dialogue was modified to reflect this change. In addition, the game also adds loading screens, which show during short moments of darkness.
