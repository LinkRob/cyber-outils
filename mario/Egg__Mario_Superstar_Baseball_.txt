The '''Egg''' is a permanent item in the [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]] of ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. It allows [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] to use [[Egg Ball]] in the mode. Like any other permanent item, the Egg can be purchased for 200 [[coin]]s, although it can only be bought if Yoshi is either the team captain or if he has been recruited to the player's team.

As shown from its icon, the Egg has the appearance of a regular [[Yoshi Egg]], but with an added golden outline.
