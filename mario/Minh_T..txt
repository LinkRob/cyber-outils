'''Minh T. ''' is a cheerful [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who lives in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Physical description==
Mihn T. is a Toad with light pink spots on her head. She also wears gardener's clothes.

==History==
Minh T. is a gardener who cares about flowers and the [[Bub-ulb]]s. Minh T. learns about what happened to [[Flower Fields]] with the clouds blocking out the [[Sun]], and so Minh T. tries to help the Bub-ulbs as much as she can by planting flowers outside Flower Fields. When Mario gets the [[Magical Seed]]s, he can give them to Minh T. so that she can plant them. When Minh T. plants all four Magical Seeds, the door to Flower Fields suddenly appears.

Mario and [[Parakarry]] can deliver a letter to her that's from [[Wise Wisterwood]] who thanks her for taking care of the flowers. She wonders how Wise Wisterwood knows about her, but is otherwise grateful for the letter and gives Mario a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] in return.
