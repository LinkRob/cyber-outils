'''Sorbetti''' is the snow-covered spiked boss of [[Freezy Flake Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
He has  two crystals for eyes, a bulbous red nose for a weak point, an evil grin and an overall resemblance to a snow-covered [[Super Big Tap-Tap]] of ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

=== Personality traits ===
The character laughs in a cheeky way.

== In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' ==
He appears in the mission "Sorbetti's Chilly Reception" at Freezy Flake Galaxy.

== Boss battle ==
Sorbetti is encountered after [[Mario]] makes his way out of the Snowstorm Maze. At first, Sorbetti is frozen inside his homeworld's moon. However, Mario agitates him by launching to Sorbetti's planet itself. An angry Sorbetti wakes up and dislodges himself from his prison, which disintegrates, and the battle begins.

Sorbetti attacks by rolling around his homeworld. Mario must smash him in the nose with his [[Star Spin]] in order to damage the boss. Sorbetti will deal decent damage if his body crushes Mario, causing the boss to laugh. Eventually, Mario is able to defeat Sorbetti with enough damage. Once his stamina is drained, Sorbetti disintegrates, and a [[Power Star]] appears in his place.

==Trivia==
*Sorbetti's name is a pun on the word ''sorbet'', an ice cream with fruit juice and no dairy product.
*When combined with his homeworld, Sorbetti slightly resembles a snowman.
