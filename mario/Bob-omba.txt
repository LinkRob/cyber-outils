'''Bob-omba''' is a female [[Bob-omb]] who appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. She is encountered in the [[Town Area]] in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]].

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
During her quest, [[Flowers Are a Blast!]], Bob-omba wants the player to get her a "pretty and healthy" plant for herself. Fortunately, she explains that it is found in the [[Desert Area]]. He must go the that area and stop by the [[Piranha Plant]] icon and take the Piranha Plant. After returning with the plant, she then rewards the player the [[Gaddget]], [[Rochambeau]]. At the end, she states her plant grows a little too big, still the Piranha Plant is healthy.
