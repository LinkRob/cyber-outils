'''Lava or Leave 'Em''' is a 4-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
The players are located in a field and notice that a volcano nearby has erupted. The players run away from the [[lava]] and jump on a rock. The players must then avoid the [[Podoboo]]s that are found in the minigame. During the minigame, the Podoboos split into two and then split again later. The player will win if he/she can avoid the Podoboos or falling off the rock, either if time runs out, or all the players have been eliminated.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Pad}} - Move
*{{button|wii|2}} - Jump
