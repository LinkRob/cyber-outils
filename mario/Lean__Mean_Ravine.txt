}}'''Lean, Mean Ravine''' is a 2 vs. 2 minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the two teams are in hovercrafts and must race on an obstacle course to get to the goal at the end. There are ramps that can be used to get a boost in speed but the teams must be careful not to turn over a [[Waterspout]] or they will lose a lot of speed. The team that gets to the goal first wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} (Sideways) - Move

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on the saying "lean, mean machine."
