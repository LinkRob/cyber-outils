'''Flower Fields''' is one of the many areas in nearby [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and is the main setting for Chapter 6 in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. 

== Layout ==
The only doorway to Flower Fields can be found in the center of Toad Town disguised as a wooden door. In order to get inside [[Mario]] has to obtain [[Magical Seed|seeds]] from four bub creatures, known as [[Bub-ulb]]s, hidden throughout the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. When Mario arrives there, he must defend the [[Wise Wisterwood]] and the other plant life from [[Huff N. Puff]] as well as rescue [[Klevar]].

The entire area is covered in fields of flowers as well as crumbling ruins that Mario must navigate to get to the individual plants that guide Mario along his quest. It is while searching for the Bowser's cloud machines, that Mario fights and later befriends [[Lakilester]], a Lakitu that defects from [[Koopa Troop|Bowser's army]].
