A '''Mezzo Bomb''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are [[Bob-omb]]s that only appear in the [[Moleville Mines|Coal Mines]].

==Battle==
Mezzo Bombs are summoned into battle by [[Punchinello]]. Mezzo Bombs attack by exploding at one of [[Mario]]'s party members, dealing rather high damage.

===Strategy===
Mezzo Bombs are weak to both [[Jump]] and [[Fire (element)|Fire]] attacks, so any of Mario's special moves are effective against them.
