The '''Sour Bunch''' are the main antagonists of ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''

==Profile==
The Sour Bunch are a group of anthropomorphic purple grapes who take over the [[Sparkle Theater]] alongside their leader, [[Grape (character)|Grape]]. Their name, alongside Grape's, plays on the phrase "sour grapes." Unlike the [[Theet|Theets]], the Sour Bunch do not have noses.

== Agents ==

=== Underling Agents ===

* Dūkes: Regular goons that can be destroyed very easily.
* [[Sour Bunch Knight|Sour Bunch Knights]]: Goons dressed as knights, wielding swords. Known as "Knight Dūke" in Japanese.
* Sour Bunch Kung Fu Fighter: Goons dressed like Kung Fu students, wielding Kung Fu techniques. Called "Kung Fu Dūke" in Japanese.
* Aerial Dūkes: Bird-like goons. Called "Aerial Dūke" in Japanese.

=== Chief Lieutenants ===

* [[Thorn Plant (Princess Peach: Showtime!)|Thorn Plant]]: A flower monster who resembles a [[Piranha Plant]]. Having caputred a castle, he is the first boss in the game, and summons thorny branches that [[Princess Peach]] has to cut down with a rapier.
* [[Sour Bunch Cowboy]]: An outlawed cowboy who fights with a gun. Via her cowgirl transformation, Princess Peach has to use her lasso to capture the barrels that the outlaw summons, then smash his face with them.
* [[Disco Wing]]: A bird who resembles a disco ball. He summons disco balls and has gravity powers, and Princess Peach must turn the balls into birds that she can use to tear his armour off, then strike him directly.
* [[Phantom Knight]]: A knight who can phase through walls, attempting to stab Princess Peach from all directions.
* [[Light Fang]]: A serpent with light beams. If the light beams catch her, Princess Peach will be sent backward.
* [[Purrjector Cat]]: A cat with projectors for eyes. He summons mice and attacks with paw swipes. Princess Peach has to make bombs from his mice, force them to the centre of his room, and then trick him into blowing himself up.
* [[Sour Bunch Figure Skater]]: A figure skater who captures some of the ice skating [[Theet|Theets]]. Princess Peach has to free the Theets that the figure skater captured as she disrupts his routine.
* [[Sour Bunch Kung Fu Master]]: A dark figure that fights with Kung Fu moves that Princess Peach must counterattack with her own.
* [[Spotlion]]: A lion with a spotlight head. He shoots energy balls from his mouth. Princess Peach has to use her Sparkle power to redirect them at him.
* The Dark Baker: An obese chef that challenges Princess Peach to a bake-off.
