
A '''Retro Cup''' is a cup in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]] that contains tracks from previous games in the ''Mario Kart'' series. They have been featured so far in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. Retro cups are first featured in [[Mario Kart DS]].
