{{Infobox Move|backcolor = 1E7F2B|textcolor = FFFFFF|name = Luiginary Hammer|image = [[File:Luiginaryhammer2.png|220px]]|caption = Mario using the ''Luiginary Hammer''.|user = Mario|element = [[Luiginary Attack]]|games = ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' (2013)}}The '''Luiginary Hammer''' is an [[Luiginary Attack]] that is used in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==Usage==
When the attack begins, Mario will back up, spawn Luiginoids out of Dreamy Luigi (who poses like he's holding a hammer), and hold his hammer above him. The player has to keep pressing the [[file:3DS B.svg.png]] button to make the Luiginoids jump on to Mario's hammer while the player moves the [[file:3DS Stick.svg.png]] to the left and right to move Mario in order to keep him and the hammer balanced while trying to stay in between the two white boundaries.

After all Luiginoids are picked up or after a certain period of time if the sequence was successful enough, Mario will advance towards the enemies with his Luiginary Hammer, and a cross-hair will appear in front of him, indicating which enemies are in the main target range. The player can move northwards and southwards with the [[file:3DS Stick.svg.png]] while Mario is advancing to move the target range, and must press the [[file:3DS A.svg.png]] button to unleash the attack on the target area, a shockwave damaging surrounding enemies. The more Luiginoids collected, the more damage dealt and the better rating the player will receive ranging from "OK!" to "Excellent!" ratings.

==Trivia==
*The Luiginary Hammer is one of two Luiginary Attacks that was featured in the commercial for ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' with the other being the [[Luiginary Ball]].
