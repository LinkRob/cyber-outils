'''{{world-link|star|3|World Star-3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}}''' is the third level in [[World Star (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Star]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== Layout ==
This level combines elements from the beach and [[Ghost House]] themes, and features tall, grassy platforms, [[Whomp]]s, [[Peepa]]s, a large body of water with a powerful current and has [[Fish Bone]]s swimming in the water. As Whomps slam their bodies down, they can make [[coin]]s and [[Brick Block]] platforms appear, which can be used to reach higher platforms to collect power-ups or [[Moon Coin]]s.
