'''Jump Charge''' is a [[badge]] which exclusively appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
In the game, Jump Charge is purchased as an upgrade from [[Rowf's Badge Shop]] for fifty [[coin]]s. This badge costs one [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear. Wearing the badge allows [[Mario]] to use a new attack in battle that charges his [[Boots|jump]] attacks, by 2. Wearing this badge could prove beneficial for tough fights, specifically ones against bosses.
