'''[[Goomba]]s''' are among [[King Koopa (DiC cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s many footsoldiers in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' and ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Compared to other enemies in both shows, Goombas accurately resemble their physical depiction from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

===Behavior===
Goombas attack by running toward the protagonist, sometimes while holding an object in their mouth. Incidentally, the action of a Goomba charging toward a character would not occur in a video game until ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', in which a Goomba charges at [[Mario]] upon sight, and even then without holding any object.

==History==
===''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''===
[[File:GoombaSMBSS.png|200px|thumb|left|A Goomba in its regular appearance from the ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' episode "[[On Her Majesty's Sewer Service]]"]]
In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', Goombas are one of the weakest enemies serving the [[Koopa Troop]], the other being [[Koopa Troopa (DiC cartoons)|Koopa Troopas]]. Goombas change aliases depending on the plot, and have been featured as bikers, pirates, and even zombies.

===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
In ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', Goombas retain their role, though they do not appear as frequently as [[Paragoomba (DiC cartoons)|Paragoombas]].
