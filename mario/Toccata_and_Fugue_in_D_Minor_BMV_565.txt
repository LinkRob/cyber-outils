The '''Toccata and Fugue in D Minor BMV 565''' composed by Johan Sebastian Bach is a piece of music featured in the game start of the [[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]] game. A brief sample can be heard when entering the museum of [[Creepy Castle]] in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.
