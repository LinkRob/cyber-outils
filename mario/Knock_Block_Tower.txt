'''Knock Block Tower''' is a single-player mini-game in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Gameplay==
The point of the game is to reach to the treasure. This can be done by destroying the crates first in order to make it possible to get to the treasure. Attacking the thwomps will bump the player character backwards far enough to fall, failing the minigame in the process. A fly guy will loot the treasure if the player runs out of time.{{sectstub}}
