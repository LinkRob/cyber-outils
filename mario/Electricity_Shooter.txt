An '''Electricity Shooter''' (also called an '''Electric Pressure''' or an '''Electro-Blaster''') is an obstacle that only appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. Unlike the [[Water Shooter]] and the [[Fire Shooter]] which have a "neck", Electric Pressures lack one, and are constantly found floating. Electricity Shooters shoot balls of electricity at [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]], these balls will home in on the plumbers for some time until they either hit them or disappear over time.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
[[Image:Electrity Shooters.png|thumb|left|Luigi gliding across space avoiding several Electricity Shooters.]]
Electricity Shooters appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. In this game, they appear in [[Space Storm Galaxy]], in the mission [[Follow Me, Bob-omb]], in the Electric Field Area, where the plumbers use the [[Pull Star]]s. They will shot electric balls at [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] which they must avoid, if they're hit, they lose a health point and can fall into the [[Black Hole]] below if the plumbers aren't quick to grab hold of another [[Pull Star]] upon being hit. There is no attack that can destroy the Electricity Shooters and their projectiles must be avoided. Prior to shooting, Electricity Shooters tend to charge up and glow, thus making it easy to predict their firing pattern. 

==Trivia==
*The Electricity Shooter's nozzle strongly resembles that of [[F.L.U.D.D.]]'s.
*The [[Boomsday Machine]] as well as the [[Undergrunt Gunner]]'s [[Cannon (boss)|Cannon]] (from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'') also shoot homing balls of electricity in a similar fashion to that of the Electricity Shooter.
