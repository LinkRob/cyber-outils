'''Balloon Fight''' is one of the [[microgame]]s within [[9-Volt]]'s [[Nintendo Classics]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''. As its name implies, Balloon Fight takes place in the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[w:c:nintendo:Balloon Fight|same name]], more specifically the Balloon Trip mode.

== Layout ==
The stage featuring the microgame features no land. There is a sea at the bottom, and the Balloon Fighter will get caught by a fish with the sea immediately below his feet. The starting platform is absent.

== Gameplay ==
For the player to win, the Balloon Fighter must avoid the flames, lest he gets knocked into the water below. He must also avoid getting too close to the water lest he get caught by a fish. Rising requires mashing or holding down {{button|gba|A}}, while the player can input left and right so the Balloon Fighter can move horizontally.
