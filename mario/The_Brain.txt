== Description ==
'''The Brain''' is a large and more developed version of the [[Poltergeist]]. He appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' and ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]'' as the final boss of the [[ScareScraper]] mode, where he always appears on the top floor. He also appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion Arcade]]'' as the boss of the Gloomy Manor. He acts and looks like other normal [[Poltergeist]], and primarily uses telekinesis to attack Luigi. He is the most powerful and most developed Poltergeist of them all, being even stronger than the [[Ancient Poltergeist]] and [[Strong Poltergeist]].

==History==
=== [[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD|''Luigi's Mansion Dark Moon'' / ''Luigi's Mansion 2 HD'']] ===

The Brain appears as a [[ScareScraper]] boss and always appears on the top floor as the final boss. His HP changes depending on the difficulty of the ScareScraper. On normal his HP is 300, on hard his HP is 500, and on expert his HP is 700. He's fought on the rooftop and will remain invisible and roam around the roof to various stacks of bricks. He'll use his telekinesis to throw a brick at the player(s). The player(s) can find him by either using the [[Dark-Light Device|dark light device]], or spotting when a stack of bricks shake, as that means he is near that stack. If the player(s) manage to stun him and begin capturing him, he will use telekinesis to throw three bricks at a time in an attempt to release himself. He can also create a purple barrier that can knock back the player(s), causing him to break free, though this attack can be avoided by jumping at the right time. The Brain will also be accompanied by one to six ghosts depending on how many players there are. These ghosts can be [[Greenie|greenies]], [[Strong Greenie|strong greenies]], [[Slammer (enemy)|slammers]], or [[Strong Slammer|strong slammers]]. Once the player(s) get The Brain down to 0 HP, he will be captured and defeated, resulting in the player(s) victory over the ScareScraper.
[[File:The Brain (Luigi's Mansion Arcade).png|thumb|The Brain spotting Luigi in the Library|358x358px]]

=== ''[[Luigi's Mansion Arcade]]'' ===
The Brain appears as the boss of Gloomy Manor and is fought in the library. He resembles a normal Poltergeist, even down to the size. The Brain has 120 HP. During the fight, The Brain will surround himself with an orange force field. This force field will only go away when he stops using telekinesis. The Brain can throw three books at Luigi, summon bats and greenies to attack Luigi, or turn invisible to hide from Luigi, requiring Luigi to use the dark light to reveal him. Once The Brain releases his force field, Luigi can begin to capture him, but The Brain will break out a few times before Luigi can fully capture him.

== Trivia ==

* The Brain is the only ScareScraper ghost that does not have a texture difference between it and its original counterpart ([[Poltergeist]].)

* The Brain is only one of two ScareScraper ghost that has official artwork, the other being [[Boolossus]].
* Another similarity between The Brain and [[Boolossus]] is that they are the only two ScareScraper ghost to appear in one other Luigi's Mansion game as a non-ScareScraper boss, with The Brain appearing in [[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD|Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon/Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]] as a ScareScraper boss and [[Luigi's Mansion Arcade|Luigi's Mansion arcade]] as a non-Scarescraper boss, and Boolossus appearing in [[Luigi's Mansion]] as a non-ScareScraper boss and [[Luigi's Mansion 3]] as a ScareScraper boss. They never appear in the same game, however.

* At expert difficult, The Brain has 700 HP, giving him the highest HP out of any ghost in the series (excluding [[Creeper|Creepers]] and [[Possessor|Possessors]], who have infinite HP.)
* The Brain is the only ScareScraper boss whose original counterpart (Poltergeist) is also a boss, or more specifically a mid-boss.
* The Brain is the only [[Poltergeist]] variant who doesn't have the word Poltergeist in its name. (For Example, <u>Poltergeist</u>, [[Ancient Poltergeist|Ancient <u>Poltergeist</u>]], [[Strong Poltergeist|Strong <u>Poltergeist</u>]].)
