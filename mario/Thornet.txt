|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Thornet''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by a few enemies and bosses.

==Effects==
When Thornet is used, a single stinger is sent toward one of [[Mario]]'s party. If it hits, it deals moderate damage and has the chance to inflict [[Poison (ailment)|Poison]] on them. Guarding against this attack can nullify the poison.
