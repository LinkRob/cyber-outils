A '''Lamb's Lure''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
When a Lamb's Lure is used, a sheep appears on the screen to turn an enemy into a sheep as well, then they both fly away from the screen. However, using a Lamb's Lure does not allow [[Mario]] or his party members to gain [[coin]]s or [[Experience Point]]s from whichever enemies were affected by Lamb's Lure. Larger enemies and bosses are unaffected by Lamb's Lure.

To get a Lamb's Lure, the [[Mystery Egg]] has to be used a total of ten times. When the Lamb's Lure itself is used 48 times, it becomes the [[Sheep Attack]].
