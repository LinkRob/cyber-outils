'''Dart Attack''' is a 4-Player Mic [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' and ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.

==Opening==
A [[Shy Guy]] walks through a gate into the playing area, followed by the four players. The first player steps up to the central pedestal.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Dart Attack MP7.png|thumb|Gameplay screenshot from ''Mario Party 7'']]
The players must gain as many [[point]]s as possible by shooting darts at the targets worth 10, 20, 30, and 50 points. The targets move at different speeds. If a dart goes past all four targets, no points are awarded. Each player has five darts to shoot. The player(s) with the most points at the end of the minigame win(s). The player can get up to 250 points.

==Ending==
In ''Mario Party 7'', winning players do their victory animations while losing players do their losing animations. The Shy Guy also does a small animation, the same one he does in [[Beach Volley Folly]] from ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. If everyone has the same score that is not zero, everyone does their winning animation. If nobody has points, they do their losing animation, including the Shy Guy, doing an angry animation.
