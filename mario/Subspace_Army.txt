The '''Subspace Army''' is an antagonistic group featured in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''{{'}}s [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]].

==Description==
Led by the [[Ancient Minister]], the goal of the Subspace Army is to engulf the entire [[World of Trophies]] into [[Subspace (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Subspace]] through the use of [[Subspace Bomb]]s. The main footsoldiers of the Subspace Army are [[Primid]]s, which exist in many varieties. Some of the enemy species within the Subspace Army are from the {{franchise|Mario}}, including [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, and [[Bullet Bill]]s, but aside from them, all of basic enemies within the Subspace Army are original to the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. [[Wario]], [[Ganondorf]], and [[Bowser]] are some of the higher tier commanders of the Subspace Army.
