'''Calm Volcano Plant''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Calm Volcano Plant is a variant of [[Volcano Lotus|Volcano Plant]] imbued with the [[Calm]] [[vibe]], and unlike their regular counterpart, Calm Volcano Plants have a yellow body.

===Behavior===
At first, a Calm Volcano Plant is found asleep, with its leaves covering its bud, but if [[Princess Peach]] moves by too quickly, the Calm Volcano Plant wakes up and tries to attack her. Like with regular Volcano Plants, Calm Volcano Plants have the same attack of spitting [[fireball]]s.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Calm Volcano Plants are first encountered in [[Hoo's Wood 2-7]]. In the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]], their name is spelled '''C. V. Plant'''.
