'''Fury Shadows''' are a new type of enemy that make their debut exclusively in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. They are black, goop-covered creatures with bioluminescent red eyes, resembling [[Luigi]]. A yellowish-orange health-bar rests above their heads, showing how much damage is required to destroy them.. They seem to possess many of [[Mario]]'s abilities, such as the [[Wall Jump]]. They taunt the player if they are not pursued.

They show up in a spiky black ball of goop (similar to what [[Fury Bowser]] is covered in), which they quickly explode out of upon arrival. They also have the ability to revert to this form whenever they are hit, allowing them to move faster. Mario must then chase after them and land three hits in order to make them disintegrate, rewarding him with a [[Cat Shine]].

== Trivia ==

* Despite their model being based on Luigi, the file for how Fury Shadows can move in the game is named ''ShadowMarioData.szs''. The files that store the model, however, have ''ShadlowLuigi'' in their file names.
