An '''Event Block''' is an item that appears in ''[[Mario Party]]''. It is bought at the [[Mushroom Shop]] for 200 [[Coin]]s, and upon buying it, it can be turned on and off at the [[Mushroom Bank|bank]]. It is hidden in one of the spaces on the board, and when a player lands on that space, the Event Block appears. The player would then get one of three events: [[Boo]], [[Koopa Troopa]], or [[Bowser]]. Boo steals Coins or a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] like normal, Koopa gives the player 20 Coins, and Bowser takes away 20 Coins or however much the player has if they have less. If they have none, Bowser gives them a coin, though this is canceled out if they are on a [[Red Space]].

In later ''Mario Party'' games, the Event Block is replaced with a [[Hidden Block (Mario Party series)|Hidden Block]], which has a similar function. However, up until ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'', Hidden Blocks could not be activated when landing on a [[Red Space]] unlike an Event Block.
