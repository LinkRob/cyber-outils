The '''Monkey Temple''' is a location in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==Description==
The Monkey Temple is located in [[Angry Aztec]]. It is one of three temples in the area that can be explored by the [[Kong]]s, the other two being the [[Llama's Temple]] and the [[Five Doored Temple]].

== In ''Donkey Kong 64'' ==
Any of the Kongs except [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] can enter the Monkey Temple. It is also the temple where [[Tiny Kong]] is trapped and [[Diddy Kong]] must free her.
