'''Battle Beach''' is the sixth [[level]] of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 17.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 4 [[Spiny|Spinies]] and 4 [[Starkiss]]es. The enemy captain is a Spiny.

The 2nd battle contains 3 [[Elite Pestnut]]s, 4 Starkisses, and a [[Chargin' Chuck]]. The enemy captain is the Chargin' Chuck.

The final battle contains 3 Elite Pestnuts, 1 Spiny, 1 Chargin' Chuck, and 3 Starkisses. The enemy captain is a Elite Pestnut, and it knows [[Rally]].

The level can be replayed to recruit up to 1 Spiny and/or Chargin' Chuck.
