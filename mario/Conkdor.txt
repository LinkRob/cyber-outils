'''Conkdors''' are enemies which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Conkdors are large, bird-like creatures resembling ostriches except that they are white in color. They also wear sunglasses and have light purple spiked shells on their back.

===Behavior===
These enemies stand still, waiting for the player to walk by. When the player gets close enough, it "conks" its beak on the ground, which will damage the player. However, doing this leaves their neck and head exposed, and both can be jumped on to defeat the enemy. Conkdors can also rotate, following the player. Their beaks can also be used to break stone blocks.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Conkdor's name is a portmanteau of the words "conk" and "condor." 

==Trivia==
*A Conkdor's shades can be removed by tapping them with the [[Wii U GamePad]].
