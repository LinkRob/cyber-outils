'''Brickman''' (ブロックン) is an enemy which appears in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''. It is comprised of several bricks. A Brickman is found on a platform, where it attacks by dropping three bricks at a time. [[Mario]] can safely move beneath a Brickman by doing a [[handstand]], enabling him to kick off the bricks that land onto his shoes. Otherwise, if a Brick hits Mario, he loses a life.
