'''''Donkey Kong 3''''' is an arcade video game starring [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Stanley the Bugman]]. It was later re-released for [[Wii]] [[Virtual Console]] in 2008 and on ''[[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]]'' and [[Wii U]] Virtual Console in 2013.

==Gameplay==
Help Stanley get rid of Donkey Kong, and the bugs by using his bug spray.

==Trivia==
*This is the first main series ''Donkey Kong'' game not to follow the traditional of reaching the goal as it mainly follows exterminating insects.
*This is the first ''Donkey Kong'' game to not star [[Mario]].
