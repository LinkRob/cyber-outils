'''Bells''' are an item from the ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' franchise. They were first introduced in ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Dōbutsu no Mori|Dōbutsu no Mori]]'', released as ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing|Animal Crossing]]'' outside of [[Japan]], but made their first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearance in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

==Description==
Bells are the main currency of the ''Animal Crossing'' games. Despite their name, bells are shaped more more closely to [[coin]]s. In ''Animal Crossing'', if the player has fewer than one hundred Bells, it is a coin with a star on it, the most common design of Bells.

In the ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing: New Horizons - Happy Home Paradise|Happy Home Paradise]]'' downloadable pack of ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing: New Horizons|Animal Crossing: New Horizons]]'', there is a similar currency called [[w:c:animalcrossing:Poki|Poki]], which are just like Bells, except they are pink with a yellow star and do not work on the current island.

[[Isabelle]]'s name after how her head is shaped like a bell, because her name is said like “is-a-bell”.

==In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''==
[[File:Animal Crossing Autumn Forest.png|thumb|left|250px|Bells seen in the distance on Animal Crossing in autumn, with the changed icon on the bottom left, in ''Mario Kart 8''.]]
Bells appear in the downloadable pack [[Animal Crossing x Mario Kart 8|''Animal Crossing'' × ''Mario Kart 8'']] and later the base game of ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' as the cosmetic replacement of [[coin]]s specifically on the [[Animal Crossing (track)|Animal Crossing]] track to suit the ''Animal Crossing'' theme. They are both scattered throughout the course and obtainable from [[Item Box]]es. Bells function identically to coins, giving a greater speed boost to players the more of them they have, although Bells play a unique sound effect upon being collected.

The bottom left part of the HUD which normally shows a coin next to the counter displays a round bag with a star on it instead.
