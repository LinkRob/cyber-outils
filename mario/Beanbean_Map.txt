The '''Beanbean Map''' is a key item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The Beanbean Map provides a map of the [[Beanbean Kingdom]], showing [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s current location, and it can be accessed from the [[Suitcase]].

==History==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are challenged to a minigame called [[Border Jump]] by the [[Border Bro]]s. Upon winning, Mario and Luigi are allowed to go pass the Beanbean Side. They duo are about to exit the bridge until a Border Bro caught up to them, gave them the Beanbean Map, and told Mario and Luigi they are the first to cross the line.
