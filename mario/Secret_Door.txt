|effect = Opens secret rooms<br>Allows [[Mario]] to run from battle without fail.}}'''Secret Door''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] that is seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. These special types of Stickers are only used to open secret passageways in certain levels which usually leads to coins, [[Hp-Up Heart]]s, and [[Stickers#Thing Stickers|Thing Stickers]]. In some levels, there are outlines of a door that the Secret Door sticker can be placed on. Secret Door stickers can only be bought at [[Outlook Point]], though, Mario can get a free one from defeating the [[Shy Guy]]s that were harassing a Toad in [[Bowser's Snow Fort]]. Also note, Mario can use Secret Door stickers to run from any battle without fail.
==Description==
Secret Door stickers resemble blue doors with a checkered-like pattern on it. They also take up four regular stickers in the [[Sticker Album]].
