'''Snakes''' are a species in the ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|franchise]]. They appear in the [[Donkey Kong Country (series)|''Donkey Kong Country'' series]] and ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. 

== Abilities ==
Snakes have the ability to curl up and spring high, allowing for bigger jumps. In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest|Donkey Kong Country 2]]'', they must be ridden to use, but in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'', [[Donkey Kong|DK]] can use [[Snake Bananza]] to spring himself by turning into a snake.
