{{Q|Mirror, mirror on the wall, I've got a friend who's the ghastliest of them all! Are you frightened yet, little Yoshi?|Kamek, [[Yoshi's Island DS]]}}
'''Hector the Reflector''' is a large [[Invisighoul]]. He is the third boss who first appeared in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. He is an Invisighoul who was fused by [[Kamek]] to turn into invisible but reflected to the mirror.

== In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' ==
=== Battle ===
Hector is invisible, but the player can see Hector on the mirror. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s egg must aim towards Hector while looking at the mirror on the bottom of the dual screen. After hitting him, he laughs with a result of him spitting out table, hitting the mirror and its piece shatters. This has to be done repeatedly, during the battle, Invisighouls will show up carrying furnitures to launch at Yoshi. This is more of a help than a hinderance as Yoshi can easily swallow the furniture and make an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to toss at Hector. Hector himself will continue trying to run into Yoshi until six hits. After six hits, he shrinks and vanishes. 

== Trivia ==
* Kamek's line introduction for Hector the Reflector is a reference to ''Snow White''<nowiki>'s</nowiki> "mirror mirror on the wall, who's the fairest of them all" line.
