'''Oyazi''' is a character who appears in ''[[Wrecking Crew '98]]''.

==Profile==
===Physcial description===
Oyazi is a man with tan skin, a black beard, and curly hair.

==In ''Wrecking Crew '98''==
Oyazi is fought by [[Mario]] in Stage C, which is unlockable by defeating [[Foreman Spike]] within two minutes and unlocking said stage.

Oyazi is first seen disappointed that he built a cold facility, though he still challenges Mario to defend it. after being defeated, Oyazi changes course, appreciating that the building had been destroyed before expressing a desire to build warm, cozy houses from now on.

Oyazi can be unlocked as a playable character in both VS. Mode and Tournament Mode once defeated.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
His name is derived from「親父」(''oyaji''), a Japanese word meaning either "father" or, informally, a middle-aged man.
