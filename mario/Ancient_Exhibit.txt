The '''Ancient Exhibit''' is a room in [[Treacherous Mansion]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Description==
The Ancient Exhibit is a two-story room based on Ancient Egypt.

The top level has a giant Egyptian-style statue with two [[Strobulb]] sensors for eyes, a palm tree and some other plants in sand, multiple ledges and [[bulb]]s (including a [[gold bulb]]), and a ramp that leads down to the basement level.

The basement level consists of multiple sarcophagi as well as an [[E-Gate]] located behind the breakable stone mouth of the huge statue on the north side.

==In ''Luigi's mansion: Dark Moon''==
In [[Double Trouble (mission)|E-2: Double Trouble]], the Ancient Exhibit is one of the rooms explored, and [[Luigi]] opens an E-Gate by breaking the wall in the basement. Luigi also fights several Mummies on the lower level, where they emerge from their sarcophagi. The Ancient Exhibit has torches that are necessary for lighting a log required to melt the ice in the [[Ice Age Exhibit]].

In missions starting with [[A Train to Catch|E-3: A Train to Catch]], Luigi can bring a bulb through the E-Gate to the Ancient Exhibit and use it to go to the upper level and collect [[treasure]].

In [[Paranormal Chaos|E-5: Paranormal Chaos]], the Ancient Exhibit is filled with [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]], including more Mummies.

In [[Terrifying Invasion|Bonus: Terrifying Invasion]], the Ancient Exhibit has the possibility of being one of the rooms filled with ghosts.
