A '''Sling Shy''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are a variant of [[Shy Guy]]s that work for [[Valentina]]. Sling Shys mainly appear in [[Nimbus Land]], but can also be found in [[Bowser's Keep]].

==Battle==
In battle, Sling Shys attack physically with their slingshots. However, Sling Shys are known for using different status-inducing moves such as [[S'crow Bell]] to turn someone into a [[Scarecrow (status effect)|scarecrow]], [[Spore Chimes]] to turn someone into a [[Mushroom (ailment)|mushroom]], or [[Doom Reverb]] to inflict the [[Fear]] status on a character.
