'''Goomba Capsules''' are [[capsule]]s in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
A Goomba Capsule can be thrown to a [[space]] or used on the player for five [[coin]]s. The item summons a [[Goomba]], who makes the player switch [[coin]]s amounts with an opponent via a [[Roulette (Mario Party series)|roulette]].
