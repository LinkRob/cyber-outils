The '''Battle Game''' is an extra game mode which appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and its ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' [[reissue|remake]]. It serves as a [[reissue|remake]] of ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]'' in its own regard, but involves [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] fighting over [[card]]s. The ''Super Mario All-Stars'' remake has another of its own Battle Game featuring different gameplay that can be more easily played from the mode select screen.

Similarly, a ''Mario Bros.'' remake was planned to be added into the canceled ''[[VB Mario Land]]''. All four installments of the ''[[Super Mario Advance (series)|Super Mario Advance]]'' series and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' include a [[Game Boy Advance]] remake of ''Mario Bros.'' as an extra game, titled ''[[Classic Mario Bros.]]'', which is particularly based on the Battle Game mode from ''Super Mario All-Stars''.
