'''World 1-2''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the second level of World 1, the [[Birabuto Kingdom]].

==Overview==
World 1-2 is a level that is set in area with many trees to jump off of. [[Mario]] will have to jump from tree to tree without falling off. [[Goombo]]s and [[Bombshell Koopa]]s are returning enemies in this level but it also introduces [[Bunbun]]s. Bunbuns are bee-like enemies that fly by and attempt to drop arrows onto Mario. This level also contains the game's first [[1-Up Heart]] and a bonus door can be found in the upper part at the end of the level.
