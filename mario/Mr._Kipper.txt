'''Mr. Kipper''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Mr. Kippers are a green variant of [[Cheep Cheep]]s mainly found in the [[Sea]] and the [[Sunken Ship]], but they also appear in [[The Trial Course]].

==Battle==
In battle, Mr. Kippers only attack by swimming at one of [[Mario]]'s party members. They are often found in groups.

===Strategy===
Mr. Kippers are vulnerable to both [[Fire (element)|fire]] and [[Thunder (element)|thunder]] attacks, so Mario's fire techniques or [[Mallow]]'s thunder techniques are effective against them.
