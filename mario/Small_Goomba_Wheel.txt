'''Small Goomba Wheels''' are the small counterpart of [[Goomba Wheel]]s and an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
Small Goomba Wheels inhabit the [[Sacred Forest]], where they were originally regular Goomba Wheels prior to them and the entire forest shrinking as a result of [[Kamek]]'s magic. At one point, when [[Mario]] is about to obtain the second [[Paint Star Piece]], five Small Goomba Wheels suddenly appear, and Mario has to defeat all of them in battle.
