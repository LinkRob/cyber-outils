'''Rock Paper Scissors''' is a [[microgame]] featured under [[Wario]]'s [[Anything Goes]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player plays as [[Wario]], who is playing {{w|rock paper scissors}} against [[Mario]]. Wario's hand signal automatically alternatively between the rock, paper, and scissors signals. The player has to select the correct hand signal for Wario depending on what Mario's is, with rock beating scissors, paper beating rock, and scissors beating paper. The in-game description advises the player to have Wario make his decision late, which is especially applicable in later difficulty settings, in which Mario changes his hand signal.

The player loses at the microgame if either Wario and Mario select the same hand signal or if Wario does not select a hand signal that beats Mario's by the time the microgame ends.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' Mario does not change his hand signal.
*'''Level 2:''' Mario changes his hand signal once.
*'''Level 3:''' Mario constantly switches between hand signals before Wario picks one.
