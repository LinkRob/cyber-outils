A '''Bowling Goonie''' is a fat, round variant of [[Goonie]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
They are named for having a bowling ball-like shape. Bowling Goonies have scrawny legs, which does not protect them from moving aimlessly on the ground.

=== Behavior ===
They roll on the ground as a means of traveling around.
