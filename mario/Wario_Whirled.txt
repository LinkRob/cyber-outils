'''Wario Whirled''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', as a microgame under [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] folder. Wario Whirled later returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', as a microgame part of Wario's [[Mash League]]. 

==Gameplay==
The player has to stop a spinning wedge that [[Wario]] is attached to. With good timing, the player must press {{button|gba|A}} to extend a machine's arrow right against the wedge so that it stops moving. In doing so, the player wins at the microgame. If {{button|gba|A}} is hit at the wrong time, Wario falls off the wedge, and the player loses at the microgame.

Each increased difficulty level makes the spinning wedge smaller, making it harder to stop the arrow with precise timing: in level 1, the wedge is a quarter piece; in level 2, it is an eighth piece; and in level 3, it is a sixteenth piece.

==Trivia==

*The microgame's name rhymes with ''[[Wario World]]'', but it is unclear whether it is intended to be a pun on said title.
