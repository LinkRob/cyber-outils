'''Thorn-Balls''' are a type of enemy which appear in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. Thorn-Balls have the appearance of a bumblebee, but with their body in the shape of a vase. A Thorn-Ball spits out a spiked ball from the opening on top of its body, and if the spiked ball hits [[Wario]], he takes damage. Thorn-Balls are in both the background and foreground of the level, meaning that Wario cannot attack a Thorn-Ball.
