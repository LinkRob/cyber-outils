The '''switching rooms'''<ref>''Super Mario 64 DS'' instruction booklet, page 29.</ref> are a set of three locations appearing only in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
They are [[door]]s located in the [[Princess Peach's room|Princess's Room]] of the [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. The doors are each marked with the letter of the corresponding playable character (except [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]), and opening them allows the player to switch characters. If the player enters the room of the character they are playing as, they switch to Yoshi, the only playable character without a corresponding door.

There is also a fourth, white-bordered set of locked doors that leads to an '''empty room'''.

==In ''Super Mario 64 DS''==
The switching rooms cannot be opened without the proper [[key]]s necessary to unlock them. First, there is the [[Mario Key]], found after defeating [[Goomboss]] in [[Goomboss Battle]], and it unlocks the "M" door to [[Mario]]. Second is the [[Luigi Key]], found after defeating [[King Boo|Big Boo]] in [[Big Boo Battle]], and it unlocks the "L" door to [[Luigi]]. Last is the [[Wario Key]], found after defeating [[Chief Chilly]] in [[Chief Chilly Challenge]], and it unlocks the "W" door to [[Wario]].

The key to the empty room is obtained by catching all eight [[glowing rabbit]]s. When the player's character opens the door to the empty room for the first time, they earn a secret [[Power Star]]. If the empty room is entered again by any character, [[Boo]] laughs are heard, and the character exits the room and groans.
