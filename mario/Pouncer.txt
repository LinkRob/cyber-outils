'''Pouncer''' is a [[Thwomp]]-like enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Pouncer is a gray, stone-shaped creature. It has spikes protruding from all sides, including around its face. Unlike regular Thwomps, Pouncers do not have any spikes on top, so [[Wario]] can stand on one without taking damage.

===Behavior===
Pouncers tend to act like Thwomps, but some Pouncers follow a set path, and they can be used as a platform for Wario to safely move across gaps. If Wario touches the side of a Pouncer, he takes damage.
