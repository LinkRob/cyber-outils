'''Vanessa''' is a pop singer from [[Diamond City]] and the antagonist of [[Mona]]'s story in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. She has green hair and wears a dark purple outfit.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
[[File:Vanessa and The Dinosaurs WWTouched.png|thumb|left|200px|Vanessa with The Dinosaurs in ''WarioWare: Touched!'']]
In the intro for Mona's story, Vanessa is watching [[Ear Candy]] while drinking a cup of tea. When [[Ken the Reporter|Ken the VJ]] reveals that Vanessa has been displaced from her number one slot because of [[Mona]] and the [[Hot Slices]], Vanessa drops her tea, feeling both shocked and angered.

Vanessa and two of [[the Dinosaurs]] from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' chase Mona down the highway, hoping to stop her from reaching the [[Hawt House]]. Vanessa and the Dinosaurs surround Mona and the van with Mona's bandmates [[Art and Deco]] as they drive to their show. Vanessa stays in front, but the Dinosaurs use an eagle-like ship to capture Art with a claw. [[Joe (WarioWare series)|Pizza Joe]] and the [[Yum Yums]] try to help, but all end up being knocked away. Vanessa's plan is foiled by Deco when he ejects himself from the van and into the Dinosaurs' ship, freeing Art.

Later on, at the Hawt House, Mona remembers that she has pizzas to deliver. Vanessa sees an opportunity to perform on stage by styling herself after Mona, wearing similar clothes and having a similar hairstyle but still retaining the color of her own hair and outfit. The audience boos Vanessa and throws objects at her.

Once Mona's microgame set has been completed, the scene for whenever another character joins the Hawt House shows Vanessa happily singing onstage with Mona and the Hot Slices, suggesting that they have made amends.
