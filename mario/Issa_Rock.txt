'''Issa Rock''' is a minor character in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. Issa Rock serves as a record keeper for rare reefs discovered. Her gender is implied to be female due to her names in other languages being feminine.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Issa Rock is a yellow-skinned green-eyed Concordian who wears a brown rocky hat with a grey brim, a white top and orange apron, and dark brown trousers.

== History ==
Issa Rock makes her debut on [[Rumbla Island]], where she complains about being able to only see rocks in the see. She can later be found on [[Shipshape Island]]. If spoken to, she records how many rare reefs the Bros. encountered during their adventure, and gives out gear when they discover a certain amount. She also builds statues based on the reefs and writes poetry about them.

Issa Rock hands out rewards at the following thresholds:
*3 Reefs -- First Contact Gloves (The wearer has their Attack and Defense by +15% on the first turn in combat, with the boost going away afterward.)
*7 Reefs -- Ready-Already Gloves (Boosts the wearer's Stache by +50% provided the wearer has no other clothing items.)
*11 Reefs -- Stash SPEED Gloves (Boosts the wearer's Speed by 1% per [[Battle Plug]] crafted, up to a maximum of +30%.)
*15 Reefs -- [[Nimble Crown]] DX (Gives the wearer the first turn during combat.)
*20 Reefs -- HP+BP Bangle (Heals the wearer by 3% their health maximum and restores 3% of their Special Points. Activates at the start of every turn.)

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The name Issa Rock comes from the phrase "is a rock."

== Nomenclature ==
In the Japanese dub, Issa Rock is known as ''Iwa Gāru'' (written イワガール), with Iwa coming from Japanese ''iwa'' (written イワ) meaning "rock" and Gāru being a transliteration of "Girl." In Chinese, she is literally called a "Rock Girl" (岩石女孩) Issa Rock's Spanish name, ''Doña Peña'', means "Mrs. Peña," with Peña, while a common Spanish surname, being a pun on ''peña'' ("rock/cliff"). Portuguese and Dutch give her a name that forms a pun on "Roxanne" and the appropriate translation of "rock"/"stone": ''Rochane'' (from Portuguese ''rocha'' ("rock," "stone") and ''Rotxanne'' (from Dutch ''rots''). In Italian, she is merely known as ''Raccogli-scogli'', from a pun on the translation of "you collect rocks." (''Raccoglíere'' means to collect.) In French, her name is ''Roxanne'', a common female given name; this also forms a pun as the first syllable sounds like the English word "rocks."
