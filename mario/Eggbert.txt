'''Eggberts''' are a supporting enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They only appear in the battle with [[Birdo]] in [[Nimbus Castle]] of [[Nimbus Land]] after she spits them out.

==Battle==
In battle, Eggberts mainly attack by rolling into a character. They often explode on contact and they can be deflected. They also have very high attack.

===Strategy===
By defeating an Eggbert, it will explode and cause high damage to Birdo. This is very helpful due to Birdo's high defense. They also have very low HP (one of the lowest in the entire game) making them very easy to take out.
