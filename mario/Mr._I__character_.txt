'''Mr. I''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mr. I is the namesake character representative of his [[Mr. I|species]]. He has the same appearance as a regular Mr. I, being an eyeball with a blue eye color.

===Behavior===
Mr. I has a huge crush on [[Princess Peach]], and he is shown to bestow gifts upon her just for visiting him. While Peach does not have a crush on Mr. I, he has someone who is in love with him, [[Flutter (character)|Flutter]], whom he is not in love with.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
[[File:MPA Mr I Screenshot.png|thumb|left|A screenshot of Mr. I in ''Mario Party Advance'']]
Mr. I lives in the [[Horror Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. His associated quest, [[Love That Princess!]], requires playing as Peach, who simply has to visit Mr. I at his residence. When he sees Peach, Mr. I shows his love for her by giving her the [[Gaddget]], [[Hope Chest]].

The [[Hearts A-Flutter]] quest focuses on Flutter's crush on Mr. I. During the quest, the player's character has to relay this information to Mr. I. However, Mr. I reveals that he does not have a crush on Flutter because he hardly knows her and because he already has a crush on Peach. Mr. I requests that the player's character apologize to Flutter on his behalf.

In the [[Probably a Robbery?]] quest, Mr. I is one the three suspects of robbing the [[Koopa Bank]]. He states that he is not guilty, but he remembered hearing [[coin]]s jingle.

In the epilogue, Mr. I is said to be learning to wink for Peach.
