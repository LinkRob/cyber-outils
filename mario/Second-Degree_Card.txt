The '''Second-Degree Card''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Second-Degree Card can only be obtained after defeating [[Lee]] at [[The Dojo]]. Its only function is to serve as proof that [[Mario]] defeated him so that he may continue on to fight [[The Master]].
