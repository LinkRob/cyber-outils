'''Harry Hedgehogs''' are small, quick-moving blue hedgehogs that appear as enemies in the ''[[Yoshi's Island (series)|Yoshi's Island]]'' series.

==History==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''===
[[File:HarryHedgehog SMW2.png|frame|left]]
Harry Hedgehogs make their debut in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|its reissue]]. They continuously and quickly move between two sides of a platform. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] gets too close to one, the hedgehog stops in its tracks briefly and swells up, its spines sticking out. Harry Hedgehogs cannot be eaten while in their defensive position, but they can be defeated using a [[fire watermelon]], and they are otherwise edible and vulnerable to any attacks in their normal state. They usually appear in cavern [[level]]s; the sixth level of [[World 3 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 3]], [[The Cave Of Harry Hedgehog]], is named after this enemy and prominently features the hedgehogs. They appear in another level in ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'': [[Go! Go! Morphing!]]

===''Yoshi's New Island''===
Harry Hedgehogs reappear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', where their spines are now purple instead of blue. They prominently appear in [[Harry Hedgehog's Labyrinth]] and act the same as previously.

==Trivia==
*Harry Hedgehogs are possibly a reference to [[Sonic the Hedgehog]], since they are blue and move very fast. During the 16-bit era (which happened to be the time that ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' was released), Sonic's company of origin, [[Sega]], often mocked the works of [[Nintendo]] in various ways (as Nintendo and Sega were business rivals). The Harry Hedgehog may be a retaliation to these mockeries, which may also explain its change in color for its latest appearance. However, in official artwork, the Harry Hedgehog has always been purple.
