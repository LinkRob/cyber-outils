'''Blimp Fruit''' is a power-up item which only appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They are similar to the [[Bulb Berry]] and the [[Dash Pepper]] as all three transform Yoshi into a specific form. Blimp Fruits appear to be small light blue items with short stems. This item transforms Yoshi into [[Blimp Yoshi]], making him light blue and inflated. Yoshi can then float high in the air until running out of air, making him fall back to the ground. This form is similar to [[Balloon Mario]]. Like the [[Bee Mario]] form, there is a meter showing a limited amount of time Yoshi can float into the air.

== Level appearances ==
[[File:Blimp Yoshi..png|left|thumb|[[Mario]] riding a flying [[Blimp Yoshi]]]]
The Blimp Fruit only appears in [[Tall Trunk Galaxy]] in the mission "[[The Flotacious Blimp Fruit]]," where the power-up is utilized throughout the entire mission. Mario must use Blimp Yoshi to reach higher places without running out of air and falling into the void.{{SMG2Nav}}
