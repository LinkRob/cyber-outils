'''World 2-2''' is a level in [[World 2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros.|New Super Mario Bros]].'' It is a grassy level, although there are still pyramids in the background.

==Walkthrough==
[[File:NSMB W2-2.png|thumb|160px|right|Gameplay of World 2-2.]]
At the start of the level, there are a few red [[Koopa Troopa]]s which are walking on top of platforms. Just after this, a [[Lakitu]] will appear and begin throwing [[Spiny|Spinies]] at [[Mario]]. The [[Lakitu]] first chases [[Mario]] through an area with several [[Brick Block]]s and [[Question Block]]s. [[Mario]] has to be careful, as there are a couple pits here. Then there are more platforms with red [[Koopa Troopa|Koopas]] on them, a [[Piranha Plant]], and the second [[Star Coin]] in the level. In this area, if [[Mario]] gets a [[Lakitu's Cloud|Lakitu Cloud]] he can get the first [[Star Coin]]. The second [[Star Coin]] can be reached by [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] through the [[Brick Block|blocks]]. Then there is another [[Piranha Plant]], numerous blocks, and the [[Midway Point]]. There are even more blocks after this, and two [[Warp Pipe]]s. The green [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] will shoot [[Mario]] up into the sky, where he will enter a yellow [[Warp Pipe|pipe]], which will take him to a cloud area. In this cloud area, there are two [[Warp Pipe|pipes]], which can shoot [[Mario]] further up into the sky so he can grab the third [[Star Coin]]. [[Mario]] can leave by entering the red [[Warp Pipe|pipe]]. If [[Mario]] chooses not to do that, he will go to an area with a few swaying orange mushrooms platforms, and then he will slide down the [[flagpole]].

==[[Star Coins]]==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Before the second [[Star Coin]], get a [[Lakitu's Cloud|Lakitu Cloud]] and go up high. Follow some [[coin]]s, then there should be the first [[Star Coin]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': Right after the first [[Piranha Plant]] of this level, if you're not small [[Mario]], you can [[Ground Pound]] through some [[Brick Block|blocks]] to find this [[Star Coin]].
*'''Star Coin 3''': Right before the first mushroom platform, go into the green [[Warp Pipe]]. It'll launch you up into a yellow [[Warp Pipe]], which will lead you to an area with multiple [[Warp Pipe]]s. Go in one of the middle [[Warp Pipe]]s, then you'll get the [[Star Coin]].
