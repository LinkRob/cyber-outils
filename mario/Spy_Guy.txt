'''Spy Guys''' are an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Spy Guys are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] disguised in camouflage gear, and wear some leaves with flowers that allows them to hide in the ground.

==Battle==
In battle, a Spy Guy attacks with either a slingshot or a [[hammer]]. A Spy Guy changes which weapon it uses after being attacked. It is recommended to hit Spy Guy until he changes his weapon of choice to a slingshot to ensure that [[Mario]] does not lose any commands, because if Mario gets hit with a hammer and does not guard against it properly, he loses one of his [[action command]]s for several turns.
