'''Professor E. Gadd's Lab''' is a wooden shack inhabited by [[Professor E. Gadd]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] version. It also where he does research on the [[ghost]]s, particularly those at the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|location]]. Professor E. Gadd's Lab consists of a few rooms, with the main one being [[The Lab]], as well as the [[Portrificationizer Chamber]], [[Gallery (Luigi's Mansion)|Gallery]], and the [[Training Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Training Room]], all of which are larger than The Lab itself.

In ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'', Professor E. Gadd's Lab is replaced with the [[Bunker]], which particularly has the role of the The Lab room.
