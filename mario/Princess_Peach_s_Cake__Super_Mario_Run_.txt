'''Princess Peach's Cake''' is a Special building that appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
Princess Peach's Cake is a giant cake in with white frosting and edible [[Star]] ornaments. The sprinkle appears on the white frosting.

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' the Princess Peach's Cake is a Special building with no rarity. The game requires a high amount of Toads to unlock the building. Once the player buys the building, it will be placed in front of [[Princess Peach's castle]]. The player can tap the cake, then the cake will bounce and stomp.
