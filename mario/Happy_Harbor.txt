'''Happy Harbor''' is a one-time location featured in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[20,000 Koopas Under the Sea]]."

==Description==
Happy Harbor is a port town located in [[Oceanworld]]. It is one of the locations inhabited by [[Toad (species)|Mushroom People]], as well as [[Captain Abidab]].
