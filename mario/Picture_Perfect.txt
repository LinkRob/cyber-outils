}}'''Picture Perfect''' is a 1 vs. 3 minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It is most likely a reference to past ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' games such as [[Picture Imperfect]] from ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' and [[Pixel Perfect]] from ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, both the single player and the three players must try to match the picture that appears on the projection. The projection will show an image of either a [[Super Mushroom]], a [[Fire Flower]], a Cloud, a [[Cheep Cheep]], a Flying Shell, or a [[Yoshi Egg]]. The single player has only four squares to work with while the three players have nine squares requiring teamwork for success. The one(s) who matches the correct pictures three times wins.
