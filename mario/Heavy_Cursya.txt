A '''Heavy Cursya''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Heavy Cursyas are one of the many [[Cursya]]s encountered in the game. These troublesome enemies are first encountered in the [[Floro Caverns]]. Touching a Heavy Cursya directly will cause the victim to be inflicted with the Heavy status, making it harder for them to [[jump]]. It is recommended to take out Heavy Cursyas from a distance.
