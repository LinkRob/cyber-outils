'''Salt''' is a food and key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Salt is one of many food items that is present in the kitchen when [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] have to bake a [[Special Strawberry Cake|cake]] for [[Gourmet Guy]] after the events of Chapter 4. However, as a cake must be sweet, Salt should not be added, otherwise the cake gets ruined. The Salt is one of few items that can ruin the cake alongside the [[Cleanser]].
