==Appearance==
Monzo is a rather unique [[Cragnon]] in appearance being yellow in color and having a unique-shaped head. He also wears glasses.

==History==
Monzo is one of the members of [[Flint Cragley]]'s crew alongside [[Hornfels]] serving as the cameraman. During the events of Chapter 5, Monzo can be found in a room where Flint Cragley believes either him or Hornfels has the key needed to unlock the door to get further into [[Floro Caverns]]. However, it turns out Flint Cragley had the key in his pocket the entire time and the group head further into Floro Caverns.
