'''Delfino Plaza Court''' is a tennis court which appears in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and its ''[[New Play Control!]]'' port. It is also where the minigame [[Tic-Tac-Glow]] takes places in.

==Description==
Delfino Plaza Court is located in [[Bianco Square]] of [[Delfino Plaza]]. The tennis court is where the [[Grand Pianta Statue]] would normally be, and it is positioned slightly back to make room. The match is spectated by [[Pianta]]s.

When the gimmicks are on, there are [[Piranha Plant]]s that sit on the sidelines hiding in puddles of brown [[goop]]. When the ball lands on one of the [[Petey Piranha]] panels, a Piranha Plant emerges from its puddle to spit goop onto the court, slowing down any player who walks into it. To counteract this, players must run over a [[F.L.U.D.D.]] panel to get a F.L.U.D.D.-like nozzle to spray the goop; after four hits, the goop disappears. When the characters switch sides, the goop disappears.

In the game, only the part of Delfino Plaza comprising Bianco Square is shown. By using a cheating device to modify the camera angle, it becomes apparent that the building with the [[Warp Pipe]] leading to [[Sirena Beach]] is directly in front of [[Corona Mountain]], and that all of the buildings behind it, along with the [[Shine Gate]], are absent.
