'''502 Bathroom''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Description==
502 Bathroom is found in the [[RIP Suites]], the fifth floor of [[The Last Resort]]. It is a bathroom entered from [[Mario]]'s hotel room, [[Room 502]]. The 502 Bathroom has the usual bathroom features, including a bathtub, a sink, and a toilet. In addition, there is a drain in the floor.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3''==
The 502 Bathroom's entrance is initially blocked by a cart is blocking the door, preventing [[Luigi]] from exploring the bathroom. The next time he explores the RIP Suites, Luigi can use the [[Suction Shot]] on the cart to clear the door and explore the 502 Bathroom.

[[Gooigi]] can fall through the drain in the floor. In the area below the drain, Gooigi can use a Suction Shot to pull off the wood barring the valve, then turn the valve all the way so that the flowing water is redirected to the bathtub, unblocking the path to the [[treasure chest]].

Luigi can turn the bathtub faucet all the way to fill the tub with water, allowing Luigi to lift out a suitcase, which he can use the Suction Shot on to obtain a yellow [[Gem]].
