'''Make Eggs, Throw Eggs''' is the first level of ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|its]] [[Reissue|remake]]. It can be considered a tutorial level on one of the main feature of the game: [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s ability to swallow enemies, such as [[Shy Guy]]s and converting them into [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]].

== Layout ==
The player starts in an overworld location. They need to continue until they get to the first flower. After that they need to hit the question cloud with an egg, which will make the empty blocks into stairs, so Yoshi can climb back up and get some [[Red Coin]]s, the second flower and a Shy Guy carrying a question cloud which will give Yoshi 5 stars if he hits the Shy Guy will an egg. After going down the stairs again, Yoshi needs to hit one of the Piranha Plants in order to get to the [[Warp Pipe|Green Warp Pipe]], then he needs to go down it. After going down the Warp Pipe, Yoshi has a chance to get some 1-UPs, some red coins and some more Yoshi Eggs. To exit, Yoshi needs to push the [[Chomp Rock]] to the end of the room and jump on it to reach the Warp Pipe.

When Yoshi exits the Warp Pipe, there will be the third flower. Then a little further (where the lift is), there will be four red coins and the fourth flower, as well as two Piranha Plants which Yoshi needs to avoid. A little further on, there will be a Green Warp Pipe with Shy Guys coming out if Yoshi doesn't have his maximum egg limit. There will also be a question cloud over the Warp Pipe which Yoshi needs to hit to get 5 more stars. Yoshi can go down the Warp Pipe if he wants, but there will be nothing down there. If Yoshi continues (when he's out of the Warp Pipe) he will get to the next question cloud, Yoshi needs to hit it with an egg and a Beanstalk will grow. Yoshi needs to climb it, and go left. He then needs to hit the next question cloud (which will give him the final flower, resulting in a 1-UP if he has collected all the other flowers). There will be a yellow balloon that Yoshi can hit, if he does, he will get 5 [[Coin]]s and another question cloud will appear. If Yoshi hits the question cloud, 5 stars will come out for him to collect. After going back to the Beanstalk again (the top of it), he will need to get the 2 red coins and move on to the next Chomp Rock. Yoshi needs to push it, and it will destroy a bunch of enemies. Yoshi then needs to follow it, and hit the next question cloud with an egg. Then 5 stars will come out for him to collect. Yoshi then needs to move on, avoid the Shy Guys and hit the [[Goal Pole|Goal]]. Then he will give [[Baby Mario]] and his eggs (if he has any) to Pink Yoshi. Pink Yoshi and Baby Mario will then move onto the next level, [[Watch Out Below!]].
