'''Banking Coins''' is a 2 vs. 2 [[coin]] mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Layout==
Each team is on their own round, spinner-like platform, and a vase floats above the middle of each one. The red team plays on a red platform, while the blue team plays on a light blue platform.

==Opening==
The red team runs on a collapsing bridge and jump onto their platform at the end. The screen then splits, showing the blue team on the right side, and the minigame starts.

==Gameplay==
Each team has to hit the vase and collect the [[coin]]s and sometimes [[Coin Bag]]s that bounce out, the latter of which is worth five coins. A team's platform tilts depending on where each one is standing, and both teammates have to avoid tilting the platform too much, otherwise they are at risk of falling off. If a character falls off the platform, [[Lakitu]] arrives to carry them back onto the platform a few seconds later. The minigame has a [[time limit]] of 30 seconds, and like other coin minigames, each character can keep whichever coins they have collected.

*{{button|gcn|a}} - Jump
