'''Feelin' Groovy Fitness''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Description==
Feelin' Groovy Fitness is a nightclub.

==In ''WarioWare: Get It Together!''==
In Story mode, Feelin' Groovy Fitness is the setting for [[Jimmy T]]'s level. In the opening cutscene, [[Joe (WarioWare series)|Joe]] performs a routine in front of the [[Club Goers]], although they are not entertained. Jimmy T is then prompted to start his own routine, which the Club Goers like a lot more. As the player plays through Jimmy T's microgames, they must complete the microgames so that the Club Goers continue to dance.

In the ending's cast credits, Feelin' Groovy Fitness is a shown as a setting in the background when Jimmy T, [[Young Cricket]], [[Master Mantis]], [[Lulu]], and Joe are credited.
