The '''Barfatronic Lavachomper''' is a boss that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is a large robot monster created by [[Carpaccio]] to regulate the lava flow in [[Sweatmore Peak]]. It also serves as the boss of the area.

==Battle==
During the fight, Barfatronic Lavachomper mainly attacks by shooting small energy balls at [[Wario]] or dropping bombs on him. In order to start damaging the Barfatronic Lavachomper, Wario must first transform into [[Captain Wario (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Captain Wario]] and destroy the three pillars at the bottom of the area with his torpedoes. After doing so, the Barfatronic Lavachomper will then use a small hand to drop bombs down onto Wario. Wario must then transform into [[Genius Wario]] and punch the bombs into the Barfatronic Lavachomper's eye. The creature will be hurt by this and a toilet will spawn from his mouth. Wario must then transform into [[Arty Wario]] and draw a piece of dung into the toliet which will cause the Barfatronic Lavachomper to make a device with a heart on it to appear from it's mouth. Finally, Wario as [[Thief Wario]] can tackle it to finally deal damage to the monster. This must be done two more times to destroy the Barfatronic Lavachomper and proceed into [[Blowhole Castle]].
