'''Bait 'n Wait''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is obtained from [[Lakitu (character)|Lakitu]] upon completing the [[What's That Line?]] quest. In its description, Bait 'n Wait is said to be a way to enjoy fishing while at home.

==Gameplay==
Bait 'n Wait simply features catching regular [[fish]] swimming around in a miniature lake. Once the bait has been thrown into the lake, the fishing pole remains still. If the bobber sinks, this indicates that a fish has taken the bait, and the player can capture the fish by reeling it in using the fishing pole.
