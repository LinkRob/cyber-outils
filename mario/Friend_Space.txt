The '''Friend Space''' is a [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Description==
When any player stops on it, the player can choose another character as their friend. They may also let the game decide randomly, which always causes the player in last place to be selected. When chosen, the character and the chosen character both get five [[coin]]s. In Tag Team mode, five coins are instead awarded to both teams, while in Duel Mode, it gives five coins to both players. After the [[Last Five Turns Event#Mario Party DS|Final 5 Frenzy]] event, all Friend Spaces are changed into [[Duel Space]]s. If a CPU lands on this space, they will always befriend the player (other than themselves) who is in last place. If two or more opponents are tied for last place, the CPU prioritizes the highest player number (as with the [[Snag Bag]]).
