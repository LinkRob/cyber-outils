The '''Surf Cabana''', also called the '''Watermelon Shack''',<ref>Loe, Casey. ''Super Mario Sunshine'' Perfect Guide. Pages 60 and 61.</ref> is a location in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
It is a small seaside bar located on the west side of [[Gelato Beach]]. Surf Cabana is uniquely built over the water parallel to the shoreline, with a small yellow zigzag dock leading the way up to it from the shore. Its main function appears to be making juice smoothies for the denizens of Gelato Beach. It features two tables with chairs and umbrellas behind the main hut, a circular counter with fresh [[coconut]] smoothies and the [[jumbo juicer]] on the roof that blocks the entrance into a glass container.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
While the Surf Cabana appears in every episode of Gelato Beach, it plays a major role only in the eighth and final [[Mission|episode]], [[The Watermelon Festival]]. Here, [[Mario]] must roll the beach's largest [[watermelon]] to the Surf Cabana, all while avoiding [[Cataquack]]s. After Mario guides the giant watermelon over to the Surf Cabana, the watermelon enters the jumbo juicer, which in turn releases the [[Shine Sprite]] that had been stuck within the glass container.
