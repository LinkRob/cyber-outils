'''Bowser Banks''' are a type of location in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

They are special banks found only on the board [[Bowser Land]], serving as a reverse variant of the [[Koopa Bank]]s found on other boards. The Bowser Banks are run by a [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]] named the Baby Bowser Banker. Characters who pass one of Bowser Land's [[Bank Space]]s are loaned five [[Coin]]s instead of depositing five. The Bowser Bank keeps a single tab of the number of Coins that have been loaned out to the four players. If a player passes a Bowser Bank while in a [[Bowser Suit]], the Baby Bowser Banker mistakes them for [[Bowser]] and gives them 20 Coins.

If a player lands on the Bank Space, they must pay the total number of Coins that the bank has loaned. If the player does not have enough Coins, the Baby Bowser Banker takes all of the player's Coins; if they have no Coins but at least one [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]], the Baby Bowser Banker steals the Star. In the unlikely event that someone lands on the Bank Space when Coins have been loaned out and they have no Coins or Stars at all, the Baby Bowser Banker complains that the player signed a contract for the loan and he will threaten to get a lawyer to sue the player, but he never acts on his threat. After any of these scenarios, the tab is reset.
