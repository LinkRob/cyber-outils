'''Wario's Cap''' is a signature clothing item worn by [[Wario]] in most of his appearances since his debut in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Description==
Wario's Cap is a yellow [[cap]] that is Wario's equivalent to [[Mario's Cap]] and [[Luigi's Cap]]. Wario's Cap has a purple, or sometimes blue, uppercase "W" emblem on the front.
