'''The Minions Are...Famout?''' is the second level of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 14.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 5 [[Spiny|Spinies]] and 3 [[Elite Pestnut]]s. The enemy captain is a Elite Pestnut.

The 2nd battle contains 3 Elite Pestnuts and 5 Spinies. The enemy captain is a Spiny and knows [[Chance]] and [[Deny]].

The final battle contains 4 [[Koopa Troopa]]s, 2 Spinies, and 2 Elite Pestnuts. The enemy captain is a Koopa Troopa and knows [[Rally]] and Deny.

After the final battle, the player recruits a Koopa Troopa.

The level can be replayed to recruit up to 1 Koopa Troopa, and or Spiny.
