'''Wobbling Rocks''' are a type of platform which first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', and they return in a few games within the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' sub-series.

==Description==
A Wobble Rock wobbles from left to right when the player stands towards one of its edges. Wobble Rocks are found in various environments. In certain games, Wobble Rocks only appear in volcanic areas whereas others feature them in cliff areas.
