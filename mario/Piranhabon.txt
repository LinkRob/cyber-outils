'''Piranhabons''' are boulder-like [[Piranha Plant]] heads made entirely of [[Goop]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
They are commonly found in areas where there is heaps of brown Goop masses such as [[Bianco Hills]]. Throughout the stage on you way to the [[Polluted Piranha Plant]] near the path to the [[Big Windmill]] and during your ascent up there on the way to [[Petey Piranha]], Piranhabons come bouncing or rolling down the pathway, creating slippery goop puddles along the way. When a Piranhabon hits a wall, it splats, creating a larger goop puddle. The only way to defeat a Piranhabon is by cleaning the goop off of it using [[F.L.U.D.D.]], and the process of doing so shows that they bear a resemblance to Petey Piranha's head, notably without the petals.

After Petey Piranha's initial defeat in [[Down with Petey Piranha!|Episode 2: Down with Petey Piranha!]], the Piranhabons do not appear in any of the later episodes.

==Trivia==
*Piranhabons are somewhat similar to the [[Big Steely|Big Steelies]] in [[Bob-omb Battlefield]] as seen in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' as they are both large boulders that roll down a winding mountain during the ascent and both are being rolled by the boss on the apex ([[King Bob-omb]] for the Big Steelies, [[Petey Piranha]] for the Piranhabons).  
*In the beta stages of ''Super Mario Sunshine'', Piranhabons were going to fully resemble Petey Piranha's head, with petals added, but were later changed to be covered in goop.
*Piranhabons receive a brief passing mention by [[Viridi]] during the [[Palutena's Guidance]] dialogue for [[Piranha Plant]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
