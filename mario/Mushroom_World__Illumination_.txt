'''Mushroom World''' is a location that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. Its a continent located on an [[Mushroom Earth (Illumination)|alternate planet]] from [[Earth (Illumination)|Earth]], it is unknown if other continents exist on the planet. The continent compasses other [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|kingdoms]], [[Dark Lands|lands]] and [[Yoshi's Island (Illumination)|islands]]. It was here [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] took residence after they had to defeat [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]].
