The '''Lucky Isle''' is a golden, floating island appearing above [[Lake Lapcat]] in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==In ''Bowser's Fury''==
Lucky Isle appears only once the main story has been completed. It only appears while [[Fury Bowser]] is present, disappearing as soon as he goes back into slumber. It contains five [[Cat Shine]]s, and Mario can only collect one of them per instance of Fury Bowser appearing. After all five Cat Shines have been collected, [[Coin Pile]]s appear on the island instead.
