'''Baby Donkey Kong''', usually referred to as '''Baby DK''', is the infant self of [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] who debuted in the [[Nintendo DS]] game, ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. He is one of the seven [[star children]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:MSS Baby Donkey Kong Character Select Mugshot.png|frame]]
Baby DK is a brown ape. He wears a monogrammed red bib, with his yellow-printed initials "DK" in the center. The bib goes around his neck, is knotted in the back, and is even yellow-bordered. This bib is similar to adult Donkey Kong's tie. Baby DK also has brown eyes with small black pupils. On his head, he has a small piece of fur sticking up in a wavy formation. This is also similar to Donkey Kong's hair. He also has one big ear on each side of his head. He has a big mouth, with two small nostrils north of it. Like a typical primate, his hands, feet, ears, and mouth are all peach. Baby DK also has five fingers on each hand and five toes on each foot. Like [[Baby Mario]], [[Baby Luigi]], and [[Baby Peach]], his appearance in ''Yoshi's Island DS'' changes in future games. Baby DK's change is very minor. The only change for him is that he has a small, furry tail.

===Personality===
Much like his older self, Baby DK is very strong and powerful and enjoys bananas. He is also very sensitive, since he is a baby. However, his cry is much softer than the other babies, indicating that he is also tough and somewhat resilient. He is usually found smiling and happy. He is also somewhat cocky, as in ''Mario Super Sluggers'', when he hits a home run he shows off by jumping around the bases and doing a flip into home plate.

===Powers and abilities===
[[File:DK Dash Attack.png|thumb|The [[DK Dash Attack]]]]
Baby DK is the most powerful out of all the babies. He is capable of bashing large clumps of rocks in ''Yoshi's Island DS''. This maneuver is called the [[DK Dash Attack]]. While on Yoshi's back, he can grab on to vines and help Yoshi climb up them. There is also swinging vines that Baby DK can grab that swings Yoshi. His eggs explode on contact with all walls, collecting or destroying anything in the blast radius. Though he is the most powerful, Baby DK is also the heaviest baby. While Yoshi runs with him on his back, they move much slower. In ''Mario Super Sluggers'', he is the best batter out of all the babies, as his batting ratings are a 6 out of 10. He is also the best fielder out of all the babies, as his fielding ratings stay at an 8 out of 10. Along with the [[Kong]]s, his fielding specialty is the Clamber, which allows him to climb up a wall in order to prevent a possible home run.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
[[File:Baby DK on Yoshi YIDS artwork.jpg|thumb|left|150px|Artwork of Baby DK swinging on a vine in ''Yoshi's Island DS'']]
[[File:YIDS-Baby DK Screenshot.png|thumb|Baby DK riding [[Green Yoshi]] in the level [[Baby DK, the Jungle King!]]]]
In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', [[Bowser]] and [[Kamek]] are looking for the seven [[Star Children]], as a result Kamek and his [[Kamek's Toadies|Toadies]] go back in time kidnapping babies from all over the [[Mushroom World]], including Baby DK. However, Baby DK was not brought to [[Bowser's Castle]], although unlike the other star children, how he escaped capture was left unexplained, the Toady that was meant to capture him was seen carrying an empty blanket. He is also unique from the other babies in that the blanket he is carried in is brown, rather than blue.

[[Yoshi]] finds Baby DK in [[Baby DK, the Jungle King!]] where he helps Yoshi in saving [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Peach]] from the [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]]. After defeating the Grinders and saving the babies, Baby DK decides to join Yoshi and the babies in their quest to rescue [[Baby Luigi]] and all the other captured babies. After this level, Baby DK is available to play as for the rest of the game. He has thirteen [[Baby Coin]]s throughout the game. One is found in [[Baby Mario and Baby Peach: Dynamic Duo]], a level in the first world, even though Baby DK is not initially available in the first world, forcing the player to replay the level as Baby DK in order to obtain the coin.

After a long journey, Yoshi and the babies arrive at Bowser's Castle. When they arrive at the top of the castle, they fight [[Baby Bowser]] and then Bowser. After defeating both of them, [[Baby Luigi]] and the other babies are rescued. Baby DK is revealed to be one of the seven star children.

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
[[File:Baby DK discovered MSSluggers.png|thumb|left|200px|[[Yoshi]] finding Baby DK]]
Baby DK is an unlockable playable character in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. This is currently his only appearance in a ''Mario'' sports game. In [[Challenge Mode]], Baby DK helps [[Mario]] and the others beat Bowser and his son, [[Bowser Jr.]]. In this mode, Baby DK is part of the [[DK Kongs#Mario Super Sluggers|DK Wilds]] team.

Baby DK's bat is a banana. His batting entrance is him rolling into the batter's box. Baby DK has good [[chemistry]] with Yoshi, Baby Mario, Baby Luigi, Baby Peach, [[Baby Daisy]], [[Dixie Kong]], [[Funky Kong]], and [[Tiny Kong]]; his chemistry is neutral with [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]], however; he has bad chemistry with [[King K. Rool]], [[Kritter]]s, [[Shy Guy]], and [[Magikoopa]]. Effectively, he has the most chemistry out of any character in the game behind only Baby Peach. Baby DK's fielding ability is [[Clamber]], meaning he can climb walls to catch a ball, therefore preventing a potential home run from the opposing team. He also has the strongest throwing arm out of all the babies.

[[File:MSS Baby Donkey Kong Character Select Sprite 1.png|thumb]]
Baby DK has a very high fielding stat, along with a decent batting stat, but he is bad at running and pitching. Baby DK bats with his left hand, while fielding with his right hand.

Baby DK is available in the ''Mario Super Sluggers'' online card collection. He is from the first characters to be released, but is then removed and made available later. In Double Play, Baby DK can be combined with [[Donkey Kong]] to get the level 2 card, '''Donkey Kong Star Swing'''. His rookie year is 2006, which refers to the year he debuted in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]].
