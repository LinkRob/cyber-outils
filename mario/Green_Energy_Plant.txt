}}The '''Green Energy Plant''' is a green level in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. One green Mini Paint Star can be found here. One green Toad House can also be found here. Using the Battery thing will turn the power in the house back on and open the main part of the level. A TV leads to a collection of areas inspired by ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

== History ==
The level starts off outside the house, where one Luigi Cutout can be seen. When you try to open the door, nothing really happens, assuming it is locked. Huey will then try to knock on the door and then get grabbed forcefully by a claw machine. You can go inside because of that, when entering the house; its a house full of technology. The problem is that the Toad living in the house seems to have no power since a Green Mini Paint Star entered through the TV, and cant reset it like usual. So you go back outside and then enter the pipe to do a Cutout. This Cutout is a card cutout, in which you have to use the Battery Thing Card to bring back the electricity. Huey also gets electrocuted because it gets shocked from the lightning. The electricity is generated, and Huey is still alive. So going back to the house, there seems to be a Super Mario Bros. 3 level inside the TV, You can perform a Cutout to enter the level.

==Trivia==
*When inside the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' area, Mario can turn the environment 3D for 10 seconds, which may be a reference to the 3D-flip ability in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
*Just like Cobalt Base, this is the only level with 1 colorless spot; the spot is found in one of the clouds eyes near a white platform.
