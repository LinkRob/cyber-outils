'''Worn-Out Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Worn-Out Jump is the weakest version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a brown boot with a patch on the toe part with the base detached from the shoe. There is an orange/white striped background.

==Price==
Buy Price: N/A

Sell Price: 1 coin

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Worn-Out Jump is first seen in [[Port Prisma]], but can also found in [[Cherry Lake]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Sacred Forest]], [[Cobalt Base]] and [[Mustard Café]].
