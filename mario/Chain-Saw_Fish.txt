'''Chain-Saw Fish''' are a type of fish enemy encountered in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]'' They have a dark blue shark-like body, but their face is covered by a hockey mask with a chainsaw protruding from it, a reference to {{w|Jason Voorhees}} in ''{{w|Friday the 13th}}''. Chain-Saw Fish move quickly underwater, essentially activating their chainsaw in the process, and try to lunge into [[Wario]] to damage him. Wario can defeat a Chain-Saw Fish by breathing flames in either his [[Sea Dragon Wario|Sea Dragon]] or [[King Dragon Wario|King Dragon]] form.
