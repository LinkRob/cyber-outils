'''Poses''' are additional forms featured after clearing a boss game in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Description==
Poses act as an intermission between levels of a stage, offering a single position that can be performed with the [[Wii Remote]] and [[Nunchuk]] in a short amount of time. After encountering a pose, the player unlocks its corresponding [[Pose Card]] in the [[Temple of Form]]. In the multiplayer mode [[Bungee Buddies]], there are also Commemorative Poses, which feature two characters as opposed to one, and do not have names or pose cards.

In single-player, there are 81 different poses to collect. Once the player has collected all of them, a new message on the [[Town Bulletin Board]] is unlocked.
