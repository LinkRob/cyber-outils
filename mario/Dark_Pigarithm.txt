}}A '''Dark Pigarithm''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Dark Pigarithm can only be found in rooms 62 and 75 of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. The Dark Pigarithm is a stronger version of [[Pigarithm]]s, but they can still be defeated in three hits. They do not release any points, however.
