'''Buddy Bounce''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.
==Gameplay==
* Bounce on the clouds to collect as many balloons as you can. Bounce on the head of another player to reach the higher, more valuable balloons.
* There are two +10 balloons, five +3 balloons and six +1 balloons at the start. The +10 balloons can't be collected without jumping on other players. If there are only two players, there are two walls on two sides to limit the screen.

==Controls==
Hold the Wii Remote sideways. Use the Control Pad to move.
