'''Showdown Time!!''' is the ninth level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 34.

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains 1 [[Elite Limbo Bro]], 4 [[Parabeanie]]s, and 3 [[Beanerang Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Parabeanie.

The 2nd battle contains 2 [[Rex]]es, 2 [[Troopea]]s, 2 [[Scaratroopea]]s, and 2 [[Piranha Bean]]s. The enemy captain is a Rex, and he knows [[Chance]].

The 3rd battle contains 1 Elite Limbo Bro, 3 [[Lakipea]]s, and 4 [[Fuzzbush]]s. The enemy captain is a Fuzzbush.

The 4th battle contains 1 Rex, 3 Troopeas, 2 Scaratroopeas, 1 Piranha Bean, and 1 Elite Limbo Bro. The enemy captain is the Elite Limbo Bro, and he knows Chance and [[Deny]].

The 5th battle contains 1 Troopea, 5 Lakipeas, and 2 Beanerang Bros. The enemy captain is a Lakipea.

The final battle contains 1 [[Clumph]], 1 Troopea, 2 Parabeanies, 1 Fuzzbush, 2 Beanerang Bros, and [[Fawful]]. The enemy captain is Fawful, and he knows Chance, Deny, and [[Lightning (move)|Lightning]].

After the final battle, the player recruits [[Starlow]] (Lv. 34), and [[Prince Peasley]] (Lv. 34).
