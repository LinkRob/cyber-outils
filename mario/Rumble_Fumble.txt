'''Rumble Fumble''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Opening==
The characters are looking at a bucket containing a [[Mecha Fly Guy|Fly Guy]]. The first character lifts the bucket and releases the Fly Guy, starting the minigame.

==Gameplay==
The players attempt to find three Fly Guys by looking under buckets. At the beginning of the minigame, a rumble pattern shakes the players' controllers. If the Rumble Feature is turned off, an exclamation point pulses in a pattern. Players must then search the many colorful buckets on the stage to find the same rumble pattern. A pattern shakes the controller (or appears onscreen) when a player approaches a bucket. If this pattern matches the first one, a Fly Guy is under the bucket, and the player should lift the bucket.

If the player lifts a bucket that does not match the rumble pattern, a [[Bob-omb]] or [[Chain Chomp]] attacks the player, leaving them unable to move for a moment. Bob-ombs destroy the bucket they are hiding under once uncovered, but Chain Chomps go back into hiding, attacking other players if they happen to choose that bucket again.

The first player to get three Fly Guys is the winner. Sometimes, buckets hold two Fly Guys, meaning it is possible to win with four Fly Guys. If nobody finds three Fly Guys within 60 seconds, the minigame ends in a draw.

==Ending==
The three or four Fly Guys take the winner out of the arena as the winner waves at the screen. The losers stay in the arena and do their losing poses.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Move (check rumble when approaching buckets)
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Lift bucket 

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Find the Shy Guys hiding under the buckets. The first player to find three Shy Guys wins."''
* '''Advice 1''' &ndash; ''"Remember the rumble pattern of the buckets that have Shy Guys hiding under them. Choose wrong, and you'll get attacked!"''
* '''Advice 2''' &ndash; ''"If you have the Rumble Feature turned off, an exclamation point will appear over the buckets instead."''

==Trivia==
* The Rules and Advice sections of the minigame incorrectly state that the players must find '''[[Shy Guy]]s''', rather than Fly Guys.
