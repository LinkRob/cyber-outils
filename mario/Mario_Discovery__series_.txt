The '''''Mario Discovery''''' series consists of a series of edutainment ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games released in the 1990s, such as ''[[Mario is Missing!]]'' and ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''. None of the ''Mario Discovery'' games had ever been released in Japan. Other reasons why the games are grouped together is from being developed by [[The Software Toolworks]] ({{w|Mindscape}}) and sharing the same edutainment genre.
