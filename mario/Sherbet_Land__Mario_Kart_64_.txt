'''Sherbet Land''' is the second course in the [[Star Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. It comes after [[Wario Stadium (Mario Kart 64)|Wario Stadium]], but before [[Royal Raceway]]. It later returns in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' as the first course in the [[Banana Cup]].

== Layout ==
Sherbet Land shares much of its layout with the Vanilla Lake courses, being a loop around a lake that serves as an abyss, meaning if players fall in, they will have to be [[Rescue Lakitu|rescued]], and will freeze until they land back on the course. The first part of it has ice everywhere, and makes it a slight bit harder for the player to control their character. To make it trickier, all throughout the whole entire course, penguins walk or slide around. If these penguins are hit, the character will slip out and will slow them down. A little more than half of the course is in the open and icy, while the rest of it is in a cave, being a little more snowy.

== Trivia ==
* This is the only race course from ''Mario Kart 64'' to not use the standard checkerboard pattern for the [[Finish line|starting line]], instead using a simple yellow strip.
