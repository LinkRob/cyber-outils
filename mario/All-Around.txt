'''All-Around''' is a play style since ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]]. This style classifies characters that are above average on speed, power, technique and defense. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are the most common characters.
