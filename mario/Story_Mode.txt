'''Story Mode''' is a mode in various games of the {{franchise|Mario}}. They are usually single-player and involve a character trying to reach a certain goal.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''[[Mario Party 3]]''===
In this game, the player must try and collect the Stamp Stars for the [[Fake Millennium Star|Millennium Star]] by playing the board games in order. However, after every time they complete a board, another player comes back and says how much he/she deserves it and is forced to compete in boards of [[Duel Mode]]. [[Bowser]] and his underlings usually stop by and cause trouble but it doesn't hinder the player's quest that much.  It is not until [[Waluigi]] steals the final Stamp Star and forces the player to try and earn it back at [[Waluigi's Island]]. After that, the Millennium Star announces that the player has one [[Stardust Battle|final challenge]] before truly earning the title of "[[Superstar]]" and the player defeats him. However, the Millennium Star then reveals he isn't the real one and flies off into the distance. The player is disappointed, but soon, the real [[Millennium Star]] appears and gives the title of "Superstar" to the player and then the credits roll.

By completing Story Mode for the first time, [[Backtrack]] and Waluigi's Island are now unlocked for Duel Mode and Party Mode respectfully and the player can also access [[Game Guy]]'s room in Minigame Mode. Stardust Battle is also unlocked as a Special Minigame. 
===''[[Mario Party 4]]''===
[[File:Story_Mode_MP4.png|thumb]]In this game, the player must battle on every Party Mode board then play against that host in their mini-game. The player will first play at [[Toad's Midway Madness]] and must beat [[Toad]] in his mini-game [[Bowser Bop]]. After defeating Toad, the player can access [[Shy Guy's Jungle Jam]] where, after winning on the board, they must beat [[Shy Guy]] in his mini-game [[Archaeologuess]]. After that, the player can play at [[Goomba's Greedy Gala]] where they, once again, must win on the board. After beating the board, the player must battle [[Goomba]] in a game of chance in [[Goomba's Chip Flip]]. After winning at that mini-game, the player can go onto [[Boo's Haunted Bash]] where they must defeat [[Boo]] in his mini-game [[Mystic Match 'Em]] in the end. Then, the player can go onto [[Koopa's Seaside Soiree]] where the must defeat Koopa in a game of [[Kareening Koopas]]. Before the player can celebrate their wins, Bowser suddenly shows up and the player must battle on [[Bowser's Gnarly Party]] where, after completing the board, they must beat Bowser and his gang of [[Baby Bowser]]s in [[The Final Battle]]. After beating Bowser, the player is awarded the title of Superstar and the credits start to roll.

By completing Story Mode, all of the Story Mode Minigames are unlocked and Bowser's Gnarly Party is now unlocked for Party Mode. 

===''[[Mario Party 5]]''===
In this game, Bowser and [[Koopa Kid]] are scheming to ruin all the dreams and it's up to the player to stop them. For Story Mode, the player must do battle with Koopa Kid who spits up into Red, Green, and Blue K. Kid. Each character will start out with 20 coins and the player must deplete all the Koopa Kids' coins before the 15 turn limit is up. In case of 2 vs. 2 Minigames, [[Toad (character)|Toad]] will be assisting the player every time and the player must battle it out on all of the main boards (though they have been shortened down). [[Misstar]], one of the seven [[Star Spirits]], is the main host of Story Mode.  

By completing Story Mode, [[Bowser Nightmare]] is now unlocked for Party Mode and Frightmare is also now playable in Minigame Mode.
