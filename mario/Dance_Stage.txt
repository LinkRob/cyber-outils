The '''Dance Stage''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is found in the [[Jungle Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]].

==Description==
The Dance Stage has green foliage lining the background and poles strung together with a few yellow banners hanging from each one. There is an elevated ground platform, and in front of it is short grass and a large torch placed to the left and right each.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
By visiting the Dance Stage, the player's character is greeted by [[Hulu]] during one of her training sessions. If they agree to dance with her on the stage, the quest [[Jungle Jive]] starts. While dancing on the stage, Hulu says a button that the player must press. If they keep up for long enough, Hulu compliments the character's talent and rewards the player the [[Mini Bowling]] [[Gaddget]].
