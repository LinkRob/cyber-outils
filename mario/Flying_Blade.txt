'''Flying Blade''' is [[Edge]]'s default weapon in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
The Flying Blade appears in ''Sparks of Hope'' as Edge's weapon. The player can change the skins of the Flying Blade. The player must first unlock Edge in the ''Driven to the Edge'' story mission, in order to unlock the Flying Blade since Edge owns this weapon.

== Statistics ==
The Flying Blade can attack multiple opponents in a straight line and does more damage on the way back.
