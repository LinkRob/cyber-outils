'''Magical Catch''' is a move in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Magical Catch can only be used by [[Magikoopa]]s while on the field. Upon using Magical Catch, a Magikoopa uses his [[Magic Wand]] to retrieve a ball a short distance away, similar to [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Tongue Catch]] and [[Birdo]]'s [[Suction]], but with less range and slower execution. A Magical Catch can almost always be used successfully.
