'''Mona's Scooter''' is an object in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Description==
Mona's Scooter is a red motor scooter that [[Dr. Crygor]] invented for [[Mona]] to use. It is Mona's primary vehicle of traveling around [[Diamond City]], and her pet [[Mona's Elephant|elephant]], [[Mona's Pig|pig]], and [[Mona's Monkey|monkey]] are often stored in a compartment within the scooter to help her during roadside conflict, such as against [[The Dinosaurs]].
