|game = ''[[Super Mario World]]''}}'''Gnarly''' is the first level of the [[Special World]] from ''[[Super Mario World]]''. Because of its layout, it is considered the easiest level of the Special World.

==Overview==
Gnarly starts with a layout where the player must keep on trying to go up before heading down onto the other side. There are numerous of [[Jump Block]]s in this level that allows for easy jumping. However, there are [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s placed in spots where the player may jump into one. After making it over and down the first area, the player can take the [[Warp Pipe]] to the second area where it's more linear. However, there is a [[Lakitu]] lurking in the area and an [[Amazing Flyin' Hammer Bro.]]. Making it to the [[Giant Gate]] completes this level and unlocks [[Tubular]].
