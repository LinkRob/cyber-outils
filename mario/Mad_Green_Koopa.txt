A '''Mad Green Koopa'''  is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Mad Green Koopa is a variant of a [[Koopa Troopa]], the green type in particular, and Mad Green Koopas resemble them except Mad Green Koopas have a red body and an angry expression due to being imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
Mad Green Koopas angrily stomp around, and whenever one notices [[Princess Peach|Peach]], it becomes infuriated and charges at her at an alarmingly fast pace. They stop once they either hit Peach, run into a wall, or Peach is out of their sight. A Mad Green Koopa's [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry describes it as attacking like a spoiled child.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
[[File:Mad Green Koopa.png|thumb|left|200px|''Super Princess Peach'' screenshot of Peach encountering a Mad Green Koopa]]
Mad Green Koopas first appear in [[Ladida Plains 1-4]], and they appear in at least one [[level]] in all eight of the [[world]]s. If Peach [[stomp]]s a Mad Green Koopa, it is ejected from its shell, turning it into a regular [[Princess Peach]]. To defeat a Mad Green Koopa, Peach has to whack it with [[Perry]] twice, with the first hit knocking over the Mad Green Koopa.

Mad Green Koopas were introduced alongside a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] variant named [[Mad Green Paratroopa]].
