'''Thudley''' is a [[pixl]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==History==

He can be found in Chapter 3-2 and go down a warp pipe. Once inside, defeat all the enemies and a box will appear. Note that he will only follow you if you are playing [[Bowser]]. He can be used to ground-pound. He can be used to knock fruit off trees in the Overthere. He can also be used to push posts into the ground. By using Thudley to Pound Pound in mid-air, immediately cancelling the move, flipping to 3D (or 2D if already in 3D) and repeating the process, one can glitch the game to let Mario infinitely jump.

Unlike Thoreau, Bowser, and Cudge, Thudley’s ability cannot trigger [[Coin Block|Coin Blocks]], [[Flip Block|Flip Blocks]], [[! Block|! Blocks]], or [[Ladder|Ladder Blocks]], as his ability only triggers [[Brick Block|Brick Blocks]], [[? Block|? Blocks]], [[! Switch|! Switches]], and posts, however in the [[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario game series]], Coin Blocks and Ladder Blocks can be triggered by a ground pound.
