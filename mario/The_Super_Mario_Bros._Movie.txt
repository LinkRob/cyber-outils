{{Nihongo|'''''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'''''|ザ・スーパーマリオブラザーズ・ムービー|Za Sūpā Mario Burazāzu Mūbī|The Super Mario Bros. Movie}} is a 2023 American-Japanese animated film directed by Aaron Horvath and Michael Jelenic, co-directed by Pierre Leduc and Fabien Polack from a screenplay by Matthew Fogel, based on the [[Mario (franchise)|''Super Mario'' game series]], and marks [[Illumination]]'s first movie entry in the [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|''Super Mario Bros.'' film series]] to be based on a video game. The film was produced by Illumination Entertainment and [[Nintendo]] and released on April 5, 2023.

A direct sequel, ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'', was announced on [[Mario Day|March 10]], 2024, and was set to be released on April 3, 2026,<ref name="MAR10 Day 2024">{{Cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vfjmQlWRHgQ|title=MAR10 Day 2024|author=[[Nintendo of America]]|date=10 March 2024|accessdate=10 March 2024}}</ref> but this date has been moved forward to April 1, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_2YywtBYAaw|title=The Super Mario Galaxy Direct}}</ref>

==Synopsis==
While working underground to fix a water main, [[Brooklyn]] plumbers [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and brother [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]] are transported down a mysterious pipe and wander into a [[Mushroom Earth|magical new world]]. But when the brothers are separated, Mario embarks on an epic quest to find Luigi. With the assistance of a [[Mushroom Kingdom]] resident [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]] and some training from the strong-willed ruler of the Mushroom Kingdom, [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]], Mario taps into his own power.<ref name="trailer3"/>

==Plot==
===Prologue===
[[File:Bowser_stealing_the_star_TSMBM.png|left|thumb|250x250px|[[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] stealing the Super Star]]
The evil [[Koopa]] king [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]], accompanied by his assistant [[Kamek (Illumination)|Kamek]] and the rest of his [[Koopa Troop (Illumination)|army]], invades the [[Snow Kingdom (Illumination)|Snow Kingdom]] in their [[Bowser's Castle|floating volcano castle]]. The [[penguin]] inhabitants fight to defend their home by throwing [[snowball]]s, but their attacks are ineffective against Bowser, leading to them being easily defeated and captured. Bowser then burns their palace down with his [[Fire Breath|fire breath]] and steals their most prized possession, the powerful [[Starman|Super Star]].
===Act 1===
[[File:Mario Movie Screenshot 9.jpg|left|thumb|250x250px|[[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] pound fist as they get ready to work.]]
[[File:TSMBM_warp_zone.png|thumb|250x250px|Mario and Luigi in the Warp Zone.]]
In [[New York City]], the Italian-American brothers [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]] have started [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]], a small plumbing business servicing [[Brooklyn]] and Queens. At [[Punch-Out!! Pizzeria]], the brothers proudly watch the commercial for the business that they spent their life savings to produce, but then encounter their antagonistic former boss [[Foreman Spike (Illumination)|Spike]], who derides them for leaving him to start their own business. Undeterred, Mario and Luigi go to do their first plumbing job at a Brooklyn couple's house, but due to the interference of the couple's dog [[Francis (Illumination)|Francis]], this ends in the destruction of their bathroom. The brothers return home for dinner with their family, most of whom mock their plumbing business and commercial, with only their mother being supportive of them. Mario becomes disheartened after being criticized by [[Mario and Luigi's father|his father]], but after he and Luigi see a newscast reporting about a significant leak in manholes throughout the Brooklyn streets due to a water main bursting, Mario promptly sees this as an opportunity for them to prove themselves and they go down the sewers in an attempt to fix the broken water main, only to fail again.
[[File:Mario Movie Screenshot 1.jpg|left|thumb|250x250px|[[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] looks around seeing all Mushrooms.]]
While exploring the sewers, they arrive at a large room filled with pipes and investigate a large green one, which sucks them into the [[Warp Zone]]. There, the two brothers are separated, with each one going into a different pipe. Mario ends up in a forest filled with large mushrooms, where he meets the adventurous [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]], who informs him that he has arrived in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and that his brother has landed in the [[Dark Lands]], which are under the control of the evil Bowser. Toad then decides to bring Mario to the kingdom's ruler, [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]], who he believes can help Mario save Luigi. Meanwhile, Luigi briefly searches for Mario in the Dark Lands, where he is chased by a swarm of [[Dry Bones]] and tries to escape by hiding in an abandoned [[castle]], only to end up being captured by a group of [[Shy Guy]]s led by a [[Snifit]].
[[File:Mario Movie Screenshot 21.jpg|right|thumb|250x250px|[[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]] meets Mario upon her reaction.]]
Toad and Mario traverse the kingdom's town and reach [[Peach's Castle]]. When the [[Toad (species)|Toad]] guards outside the door refuse to let them in, Toad distracts them to let Mario sneak in, but he is then chased by the other guards. In the throne room, Princess Peach holds a council with many Toads, where the [[Toad General]] describes Bowser's recent actions and that he is headed towards the Mushroom Kingdom, claiming that his possession of the Super Star makes their destruction inevitable. The princess, however, refuses to give up and reveals her plan to defeat Bowser by traveling to the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]] to form an alliance with the [[Kong]]s, despite the fact that their king usually declines alliances. The princess and Mario meet shortly afterward, with the former being surprised to encounter another human. After hearing Mario's plight and seeing how determined he is to save his brother, Peach tests his skill with a [[Training Course (Illumination)|training course]] full of obstacles and agrees to take him with her if he can finish it. After demonstrating her own skills, she then explains that he can clear the course using [[List of power-ups|power-ups]] such as [[Super Mushroom (Illumination)|Super Mushrooms]], which make Mario [[Super Mario (form)|bigger and stronger]]. Mario fails to complete the course on many attempts, but eventually comes close enough that Peach takes him along anyway.

Meanwhile, Bowser holds a party for his troops in his throne room in celebration of their recent conquest and seizing of the Super Star. During this, Bowser reveals to his army that he is deeply infatuated with Princess Peach, and that his motive for acquiring the Star was to impress her and ask for her hand in marriage, stating that if she refuses, he will use the Super Star to demolish her kingdom, to the elation of his army. During this time, Luigi is taken into a balloon by the [[Shy Guy]]s that captured him, where he recalls a moment in [[Baby Luigi|his]] and [[Baby Mario|Mario's infancy]] when Mario stood up to protect his brother from a bully who was abusing him. 
===Act 2===
[[File:Mario Movie Screenshot 13.jpg|thumb|250x250px|left|[[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]], [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]] and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] traveling the world]]
In the morning, Mario and Peach begin their journey to the Jungle Kingdom, with Toad joining as well due to his desire to protect the princess. Together, they travel through several locales, such as the [[Waterfall Area]], the [[Sand Kingdom (Illumination)|Desert Area]], [[Yoshi's Island (Illumination)|Yoshi's Island]], and the [[High Cliffs]], on their way to the Jungle Kingdom. At one point in their trek, the trio set up a camp in the [[Fire Flower Fields]], where Peach shares her backstory: she first arrived at the Mushroom Kingdom by walking through a [[Warp Pipe]] as a [[Baby Peach|baby]], then was taken in by the Toads and eventually crowned as their princess once she was ready. However, she has no memory of anything before her arrival in the Mushroom Kingdom, including where she was originally from. This leads Mario to speculate that Peach may be from the same world as him, to which Peach remarks that the universe is large and contains many galaxies.
[[File:Mario Movie Screenshot 8.jpg|thumb|250x250px|[[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] interrogating [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]]]]
During the heroes' quest, Bowser, while playing on his piano and singing a [[Peaches|song expressing his obsessive love for Peach]], is informed by Kamek about Mario and his association with Peach, making him see the plumber as a potential rival for the princess's affections. When Luigi is brought before Bowser, the Koopa King interrogates him about Mario. Luigi resists at first, but eventually confesses after Bowser loses his patience. Luigi is then placed in a cage above a pit of [[Lava]], where others, such as the penguins and [[Lumalee (Illumination)|Lumalee]], have also been caged.
[[File:DonkeyKongMovieStadium2.png|left|thumb|250x250px|[[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] being easily overpowered by [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]].]]
Upon arriving at the Jungle Kingdom, the heroes are transported to the palace by a Kong driving a [[kart]]. At the palace, Peach consults the Kongs' king, [[Cranky Kong (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Cranky Kong]], and asks for his army to support them in fighting Bowser. He initially refuses to join Peach's cause, but when Mario stands up to him, he agrees to help on the condition that Mario defeats his son [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] in the Great Ring of Kong, which Mario accepts. During the fight, Mario initially struggles against DK's immense strength, so he attempts to use power-ups from the several [[Question Block|boxes]] set around the arena to gain an advantage. However, he accidentally eats a [[Mini Mushroom (Illumination)|Blue Mushroom]] thinking it was a Super Mushroom and gets [[Mini Mario (Illumination)|shrunken down]] and flicked away by DK. He then tries to use a [[Fire Flower (Illumination)|Fire Flower]], but it gets blown away by DK. Mario continues to get pummeled, but refuses to give up. Peach then encourages him to hit another block, which contains a [[Super Bell (Illumination)|Super Bell]] that turns him into [[Cat Mario (Illumination)|Cat Mario]]. With his new speed, agility, and claws, Mario is able to dodge all of DK's attacks and scratch him until he is finally defeated, at which Cranky Kong reluctantly agrees to help Peach's cause.
[[File:TSMBM_Rainbow_Road.png|right|thumb|250x250px|[[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] driving on Rainbow Road.]]
At Donkey Kong's home, Peach congratulates Mario for his tenacity, while DK is frustrated with Mario and claims to have let him win. Cranky suggests that they use karts to quickly return to the Mushroom Kingdom via a shortcut so that they can ambush Bowser and his forces during their attack. In a large garage, Mario, Peach, and Toad each create their own karts, and they drive alongside the Kongs back to the Mushroom Kingdom through a secret passage that leads to [[Rainbow Road]]. On the way back, Peach begins questioning Mario about his world and decides to visit Brooklyn sometime.

Meanwhile, Bowser is practicing his marriage proposal to Peach, with Kamek taking her place. However, they are interrupted by the [[Koopa General]], who has discovered the heroes' plan and reports it to Bowser. At this, Bowser decides to have his army use karts of their own to ambush the heroes as they traverse Rainbow Road, with the Koopa General himself chasing Mario with a bulldozer. During the chase, Mario's kart is destroyed, but he manages to hijack another while Peach saves Toad from falling off the road. After losing his second kart, Mario hitches a ride with Donkey Kong, who fires one of his kart's Rocket Barrels to explode on the bulldozer. Infuriated, the Koopa General locks himself in his winged, spiky [[Blue Spiny Shell|Blue Shell]] and crashes into Mario and Donkey Kong, destroying the latter's kart and a part of the road. The two plummet to the ocean below, much to Peach, Toad, and Cranky's despair, and all of the Kongs besides DK are captured and sent to Bowser's fortress dungeons. After landing in the ocean, Mario saves DK from drowning, but both are then devoured whole by a large [[Maw-Ray (Illumination)|Maw-Ray]].

===Act 3===
[[File:Mario Movie Screenshot 11.jpg|left|thumb|250x250px|[[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] and [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]] prepare to fight.]]
Peach and Toad manage to make it back to the Mushroom Kingdom, where Peach warns the citizens of Bowser's imminent arrival. She orders the Toads in the town and the castle to evacuate while she holds off Bowser, but Toad decides to stay at the princess's side instead of leaving with the rest of his kind, wanting to stay true to his desire to protect her. Bowser then arrives in the Mushroom Kingdom aboard his castle and finally proposes to marry Peach. She initially rejects him, but after he has Kamek torture Toad, she reluctantly agrees to the marriage on the condition that Bowser does not harm her subjects. Kamek then informs all of Bowser's prisoners that they are "invited" to Bowser and Peach's royal wedding to be ritualistically sacrificed, much to their horror (and Lumalee's delight). Meanwhile, Mario and DK, trapped inside the Maw-Ray's stomach, despair over their situation. After attempting to bond over their issues with their respective fathers, they enter an argument as DK begins smashing around the stomach, causing the Maw-Ray to burp and allowing them to find DK's ruined kart. They then use the kart’s last Rocket Barrel to escape the Maw-Ray's stomach and fly back to the Mushroom Kingdom, hurrying to Peach and Bowser's wedding.
[[File:Tanooki_Mario_and_Luigi_reunite_-_TSMBM.png|left|thumb|250x250px|Mario and Luigi reunite.]]
At the wedding, Bowser reveals that he intends to execute all of his prisoners by burning them in lava in Peach's honor. However, Peach punches out Kamek and reveals an [[Ice Flower (Illumination)|Ice Flower]] that Toad smuggled into her bouquet, with which she freezes Bowser and the winch lowering the prisoners before holding down his army. During this, Mario and Donkey Kong arrive, but they are then shot down by a [[Bullet Bill]], forcing them to fight through Bowser's hordes in town. Mario uses a Super Mushroom while DK uses a Fire Flower, but both lose their abilities in the fight. Mario then finds a [[Super Leaf (Illumination)|Super Leaf]], allowing him to fly up to Bowser's flying fortress. During the fight, Peach uses a spear to light [[King Bob-omb (Illumination)|King Bob-omb]]'s fuse, but is then blown back by the explosion, causing her to lose the power before she can re-freeze the winch. Before the prisoners are burned, DK arrives and manages to stop the winch with his own hands, making Cranky proud. Luigi nearly falls into the lava while trying to escape, but Mario saves him, and the two rejoice at being reunited.
[[File:TSMBM_Banzai_Bill.png|thumb|250x250px|An angry Bomber Bill after Tanooki Mario smacks its eye with his tail]]
Enraged, Bowser frees himself from the ice and orders for the [[Banzai Bill|Bomber Bill]] to launch and destroy the Mushroom Kingdom. Before the Bomber Bill can hit Peach's Castle, Mario manages to stop it by hitting one of its eyes with his suit's tail. Angered by this, the Bomber Bill chases Mario instead, and the plumber leads it into the forest and into the pipe that he first entered the Mushroom Kingdom through. However, the resulting explosion in the Warp Zone causes everyone and everything in the surrounding area to be sucked into the pipe and transported to Brooklyn, with Bowser's flying fortress bursting out and landing in the middle of the street, much to the shock of Spike and other citizens.
[[File:TSMBM_Invincible_Mario_and_Luigi.jpg|thumb|250x250px|left|[[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] become [[Invincible Mario]] and [[Invincible Mario|Invincible Luigi]]]]
The Super Star lands in front of Mario, but a furious Bowser stops him from getting it and pummels him while blaming him for his wedding being ruined. A discouraged Mario ends up hiding in Punch-Out!! Pizzeria as Bowser taunts him, and despite their best efforts, Bowser defeats and captures both Peach and Donkey Kong along with his army. However, after seeing a damaged television play the Super Mario Bros. Plumbing commercial, Mario regains his resolve and stands up to Bowser, stopping him from killing DK. Peach then manages to kick the Super Star away from Bowser, allowing Mario to go after it. Bowser tries to incinerate him with his fire breath, but Luigi defends him with a manhole cover, allowing them both to obtain the Star and become [[Invincible Mario (Illumination)|invincible]] just in time. Using the Star's powers, the Mario brothers work together to defeat Bowser and his army. Upon his defeat, Bowser is force-fed a Blue Mushroom by Peach and shrunk to miniature size, allowing Toad to imprison him in a jar. In the end, the Mario brothers and their new friends in the Mushroom Kingdom are celebrated and hailed as heroes by Brooklyn's inhabitants, including Spike and the brothers' parents. Afterwards, the brothers start off their new day of plumbing work, having set up home in the Mushroom Kingdom.

===End-Credits Scenes===
In a mid-credits scene, Bowser is shown singing another verse of his love song while imprisoned in a cage in Peach's Castle vowing revenge on the Mario Bros, but a Toad guard interrupts and silences him, closing the door on him mid-sentence as he rants. In a post-credits scene, a [[Yoshi's Egg]] that was originally given as a wedding gift for Bowser and Peach is seen in the Brooklyn sewers. The egg suddenly begins to hatch as the film cuts to black, followed by the [[Yoshi (Illumination)|creature]] inside the egg shouting "[[Yoshi]]!"

==Development==
In December 2014, information leaked that Sony Pictures had made a deal with [[Nintendo]] to create an animated film based on the [[Mario (series)|''Mario'' franchise]], but it never materialized.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://kotaku.com/report-sony-making-super-mario-bros-cartoon-1670192558 |title=Report: Sony Making Super Mario Bros. Movie |accessdate=2022-10-12 |last=Plunkett |first=Luke |date=2014-12-12 |work=Kotaku }}</ref>

In November 2017, it was reported that Nintendo would be teaming up with Universal Pictures and [[Illumination Entertainment|Illumination]] to make an animated Mario film. However, in January 2018, former Nintendo President [[Tatsumi Kimishima]] stated that a deal had not been finalized, but that an announcement would come soon. Kimishima hopes that if the deal is successful, a 2020 release date would be possible. By the end of January 2018, Nintendo announced that the movie will be made with [[Shigeru Miyamoto]] and Chris Meledandri, the co-producer. According to Miyamoto, he knew that the process of making a film was far different from that of making a video game, and wanted a film expert to lead the effort. In Nintendo's work with Universal Parks & Resorts to create [[Super Nintendo World|Mario-based attractions]], Miyamoto had met Meledandri, and they had several discussions. Miyamoto found Chris Meledandri's creative process similar to his own, and felt he would be the proper lead for a Mario film. They had started more earnest discussions of the film as early as 2016, knowing that if they felt it wasn't working that they could easily walk away. However, by the time of the official announcement, Miyamoto said that they have good discussions over the screenplay and feels good progress to a proper film. Meledandri stated that the film was a "priority" for Illumination and that it will most likely come out in 2022. He added that Miyamoto will be "front and center" with creating the movie.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/the-super-mario-movie-could-revive-a-36-year-old-nintendo-character/ |title=The Super Mario movie could revive a 36-year-old Nintendo character |accessdate=2022-10-12 |date=2021-08-09 |work=Video Games Chronicle}}</ref> The movie's release date was December 21, 2022, was postponed to April 7, 2023, and was then pushed forward to April 5, 2023.

In January 2020, Nintendo's president Shuntaro Furukawa stated that the film is "moving along smoothly" with the film aiming for release in 2022. Furukawa stated that Nintendo would own the rights to the film, and both Nintendo and Universal would fund the production of the film.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2020/01/illuminations_mario_movie_is_moving_along_smoothly_aiming_for_2022_release |title=Illumination's Mario Movie Is "Moving Along Smoothly", Aiming For 2022 Release |accessdate=2022-10-12 |date=2020-01-31 |work=NintendoLife}}</ref>

''GameRant''</ref> However, the accent was axed during production for sounding too similar to the character Tony Soprano.<ref name="accent">Standford, Alesia (April 8, 2023) [https://www.dailymail.co.uk/tvshowbiz/article-11952811/Chris-Pratts-Super-Mario-Bros-Movie-accent-rejected-Tony-Soprano-similarities.html Chris Pratt says his first accent was rejected by The Super Mario Bros. ===Power-ups===
Power-ups allow characters to gain new abilities. They are often, but not always, found within [[? Block|Question Block]]s. The majority of power-ups can be used by touching the power-up. In most cases, using a power-up makes it vanish or otherwise become unusable. When characters get hurt, they lose the effect of any power-up they have.

====Box office====
The film had a budget of $100 million.<ref name="variety">{{cite web|url=https://variety.com/2023/film/news/super-mario-bros-movie-box-office-biggest-video-game-adaptation-ever-1235583559/|title=‘Super Mario Bros. Movie'' was released alongside [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_(2023_film) Air], and was projected to gross around $125 million from 4,000 theaters during its five-day opening weekend. It was also expected to gross around $100 million internationally. The movie made $204 million domestically and $173 million worldwide, with a grand total of $377 million. This does not account for Japan as the movie was not released there yet.

The movie broke some records, such as the biggest opening day for an animated movie in eleven markets, the 2nd biggest opening day for a movie in Central America, behind ''[[w:c:marvel:Avengers: Endgame|Avengers: Endgame]]'', the biggest box office opening for a video game movie worldwide, and biggest opening for an animated movie worldwide. As of April 9, 2023, the movie beat ''[[w:c:disney:Frozen 2|Frozen 2]]'' for being the highest grossing animated movie of all time.

As of April 14, 2023, the box office surpassed $500 million globally making the movie the biggest video game adaptation topping grosses of ''[[w:c:wowpedia:Warcraft (film)|Warcraft]]'' and ''[[w:c:pokemon:Pokémon Detective Pikachu|Pokémon Detective Pikachu]]''.<ref name="variety" />

In April 22, 2023, the film was announced to earn the highest third weekend total ever recorded by an animated film with $58 million in a weekend.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://twitter.com/TheWrap/status/1649796184679014400|title="The Super Mario Bros" movie has earned the highest third weekend total ever recorded by an animated film|author=TheWrap|work=Twitter|date=2023-04-22|accessdate=2023-04-24}}</ref>

As of April 24, 2023, it hit $878 million globally and is expected to hit $1 billion.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2023/04/mario-movie-expected-to-surpass-usd1-billion-at-the-global-box-office|title=Mario Movie Expected To Surpass $1 Billion At The Global Box Office|work=Nintendo Life|author=Liam Doolan|date=2023-04-24|accessdate=2023-04-24}}</ref>

====Critical response====
''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' has received mixed reviews from film critics. It has been praised for its visuals, score, and faithfulness to the video games, while the screenplay and plot have received criticism. Some critics have found the plot to be lacking in substance and sidelined in favor of fan service. Jack Black's performance was additionally a subject of praise. Audience reviews were strikingly more positive, acclaiming the film for the same reasons as the critics, but some have criticized the pacing and the film's length. 

On the review aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes, 59% of 141 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 5.5/10. The website's consensus reads, "While it's nowhere near as thrilling as turtle tipping your way to 128 lives, ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' is a colorful -- albeit thinly plotted -- animated adventure that has about as many Nintendos as Nintendont's." Metacritic, which uses a weighted average, assigned the film a score of 47 out of 100, based on 41 critics, indicating "mixed or average reviews".

Ross Bonaime from ''Collider'' gave a positive review, writing "Obviously, ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' is in many ways a feature-length advertisement for Nintendo as a brand. However, Jelenic, Horvath, and [F]ogel make this feel more like a labor of love, as they’ve been given the keys to the (Mushroom) kingdom, and are allowed to go all-in with what any fan would want to see in a film like this. In many ways, ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' reminds of ''Wreck-It Ralph'' and the glee that came from seeing these characters on screen."

Writing for the ''Datebook'' section of ''The San Francisco Chronicle'', Zaki Hasan said he enjoyed the visuals but felt that the film's strong adherence to and copious references to the games left it lacking in narrative, and that it tried too hard to avoid the failures of the 1993 film, calling it "a blandly efficient piece of brand management".

''IGN''<nowiki/>'s Tom Jorgensen gave a mostly positive review. He praised the film's energy, its musical score despite the inclusion of "shoehorned" pop songs, and its visuals for setting a "very high bar". He commended the use of callbacks but criticized some character arcs for being underdeveloped. Jorgensen had slight criticism towards Pratt and Day's attempt at New York accents for Mario and Luigi, respectively, as "certainly not going to be taking home any commendations from the good people of Brooklyn" and stated "There’s definitely been a flattening of the more cartoonish qualities". However, he applauded the actors for embodying each character's heroism and for a grounded delivery that "balances well with the fantastical trappings of the Mushroom Kingdom". Jorgensen commended Black’s performance as Bowser for being "standout" and fitting to the character.

Kyle Anderson of ''Nerdist'' celebrated the animation for its game accuracy and called Illumination "...the perfect choice to make Mario movies" but opined that "those expecting something akin to ''The LEGO Movie'' might be left a bit cold" due to the lack of a plot outside of the main beats, given the film's short run time.

Soren Andersen of ''The Seattle Times'' commented that, while the film succeeded in appealing to the ''Mario'' fanbase by creating the feeling of being inside a video game, he found the characters to lack personality and criticized their voice performances, remarking: "...everyone expresses themselves at the tops of their voices, sound and fury signifying not a whole lot".

Brian Tallerico of ''Roger Ebert'', a self-described lifelong fan, wrote that the vocal performances were "uniformly mediocre," and that the film does not "reflect the franchise's creativity in the slightest."

The ''Los Angeles Times'''s Katie Walsh applauded the film for its humor and "eye-popping" animation. While she praised Black's performance as Bowser, she criticized both Pratt and Day's lead performances of Mario and Luigi as being "so unremarkable that it could have been anyone at all".

Calum Marsh wrote for the ''New York Times'' criticizing Pratt's performance as "grating" and "unctuous" while describing the overall film as "bland, witless and flagrantly pandering".

Kristy Puchko from ''Mashable'' commended the fan service and music, but was critical of the characters and noted Pratt's lack of accent, commenting "whatever voice Pratt is doing isn't Brooklyn, and it isn't anything particularly specific, consistent, or exciting".

''Screenrant'''s Molly Freeman praised the film for being a "love letter" to fans but criticized it for offering little else. She described most of the voice cast as "fun" with Black's Bowser being a standout, while noting that Pratt's performance was "not so bad as to be distracting, but not strong enough to be at all interesting either", to which she said was an analogy for the film as a whole.

Josh Spiegel from ''SlashFilm'' considered the film's portrayal of Princess Peach not in need of rescuing "unquestionably a good thing". He criticized the film for an "exceedingly straightforward narrative", characters lacking complexity, and Pratt's performance as Mario as being "blandly heroic".

David Kaplan from ''Kaplan vs. Kaplan'' described the movie animation to be excellent depicting the characters' expressions including the canine's expressions which he found its mean grimace hilarious. However, he said the storyline is another "the world is peril and must be saved from a villain". He stated Fogel created a delightful new character in Princess Peach. He also mentioned Fogel borrowed heavily from Alice in Wonderland with edibles that "make one big or small", but was aware that young ones would not care. According to Kaplan, he stated the film is primarily for older children and tidbits may appeal to adults.<ref name="Kaplan vs Kaplan">{{cite web|url=https://www.kaplanvskaplan.com/new-releases/super-mario-bros-movie-the/|title=SUPER MARIO BROS.MOVIE, THE|work=Kaplan vs. Kaplan|author=David Kaplan, Jeanne Kaplan|accessdate=2023-04-05|date=2023-04-05}}</ref>

Jeanne Kaplan said the movie should have turned out better, since the animation is colourful and flashy. While the movie is rated PG, she believes a few scenes can be frightening for children, including the scenes where it took place in Dark Lands. She finds the animation incredible, but it does not make it an enjoyable film. She stated Pratt being chosen to cast as Mario over Charles Martinet is a mistake, describing his portrayal to be flat almost as he sounds like phoned in his performance. Overall for her, she describes the film as one of the films that lands in the middle—not great, but not awful either.<ref name="Kaplan vs Kaplan" />

[[John Leguizamo]], who portrayed Luigi in the [[Super Mario Bros. (film)|1993 live-action film]], criticized ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' for a lack of Latin representation in its cast (despite Anya Taylor-Joy and Fred Armisen having Latin heritage), and stated that he did not intend to watch the animated film.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2021/09/former_mario_movie_star_john_leguizamo_isnt_happy_with_the_casting_on_the_new_film|title=Former Mario Movie Star John Leguizamo Isnt Happy With The Casting On The New Film|accessdate=2021-09-29 |date=2021-09-29}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.gamespot.com/articles/former-luigi-actor-john-leguizamo-is-boycotting-the-super-mario-bros-movie/1100-6513051/|title=Former Luigi Actor John Leguizamo Is Boycotting The Super Mario Bros. ==Differences between the movie and the games==
This movie has several differences with what was established in the video games, and those differences will be described below.

*The film uses a different origin story for Mario and Luigi from the one depicted in the Yoshi's Island series onwards, being Brooklyn natives like in early media predating the series, including the original ''Mario Bros.'' arcade game, the [[DIC Entertainment|DIC]] cartoons and the 1993 film.
*Peach is shown to have come to the Mushroom Kingdom as a baby, when the Toads found and raised her and later crowned her their princess, instead of being born into her position as implied by ''Super Mario Bros.'', ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'', and ''Yoshi's Island DS.''
*When Mini Mario takes damage, he reverts to his normal form rather than being defeated.
*Koopa Troopas and Koopa Paratroopas wear helmets and carry weapons.
*The personalities of the characters are changed (most notably Peach).
*Luigi plays the damsel in distress role instead of Peach, who joins Mario on his quest to save him.
*Pauline acts as the mayor of New York City instead of New Donk City.
*The blue shell in ''Mario Kart'' fires at the leader of the race, whereas in the film, it was fired at Mario and Donkey Kong.
