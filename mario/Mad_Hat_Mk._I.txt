'''Mad Hat Mk. I''' is the first model of the Mad Hat line made by [[Count Cannoli]], and serves as the first boss in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is fought on the [[S.S. Caviar]].

== Battle ==
In the battle, the Mad Hat Mk. I throws bombs at [[Thief Wario]]. To damage it, the player should switch to [[Cosmic Wario]] and shoot many lasers at the blue ball above the Mat Hat. It will then lower the shield, making Count Cannoli vulnerable and dizzy, and Thief Wario can jump on him or [[Shoulder Bash]] him. Then, repeat this method two times but before repeating, duck when he extends his drill and flies, attempting to damage Thief Wario.

It gets an upgrade in Episode 2, named [[Mad Hat Mk. II]], and a second upgrade, named [[Mad Hat Mk. III]], which is fought in Episode 9.
