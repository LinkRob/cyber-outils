== Description ==
Rabbids are white-rabbit type creatures from the video game Rayman Raving Rabbids, and will do, take, and break anything they want. Rabbids are White with two buck teeth, each tooth having a sizable gap between them and the other. They have tan coloring on their belly, hand and foot pads, in their ears, and around their mouths. Rabbids all have Blue eye's (with a few special exceptions) All Rabbids (With a few special exceptions) Communicate using a series of sounds, mostly consisting of screaming, the word Bwa, and a few other sounds.

==[[Donkey Kong Adventure DLC|''Donkey Kong Adventure'']]==
In June 2018, a Donkey Kong DLC was released for ''Kingdom Battle'', featuring new characters such as [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Rabbid Cranky]]. In the DLC, [[Rabbid Kong]] and [[Rabbid Peach]] are taken to a new land in which bananas exist causing Rabbid Kong to turn evil upon eating them.
