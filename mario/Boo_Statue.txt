'''Boo Statues''' are objects in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. As their name implies, they are shaped like [[Boo]]s and rest on a purple slot. Pushing a Boo Statue always results in something happening, such as a bridge materializing out of nowhere. The only problem is that Boo Statues, true to their animate counterparts, disappear should one look directly at them. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] had to utilize Luigi's [[Thunderhand]] power to push them while facing backward.
