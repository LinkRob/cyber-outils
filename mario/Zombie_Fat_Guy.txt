'''Zombie Fat Guys''' are a subspecies of [[Fat Guy]] and [[Zombie Guy]] first appearing in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''. Zombie Fat Guys are commonly seen sitting on couches but begin moving once a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] comes near. Like normal Fat Guys, Zombie Fat Guys are immune to stomps, but can be defeated by eating or ground pounding them. Zombie Fat Guys are rather rare enemies only appearing in one level of the game, [[Haunted Maker Mansion]].

==Trivia==
*Zombie Fat Guys are among one of the few Shy Guy variants to only be exclusive to one level.
