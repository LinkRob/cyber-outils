'''Barrel Cannon Blast''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] of [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Donkey Kong first [[Hand Slap|slams his hands]] onto the ground, causing a [[Barrel Cannon]] to fall behind him. Donkey Kong then backflips into the cannon. He charges for a few seconds before launching at the ball with enough force to send the receiver skidding backwards. The receiver can return the ball more easily if they are a distance away from Donkey Kong.
