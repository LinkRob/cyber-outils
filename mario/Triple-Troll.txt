'''Triple-Troll''' is [[Rabbid Peach]]'s default weapon in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
The Triple-Troll appears in ''Sparks of Hope'' as Rabbid Peach's weapon. The player can change the skins of The Dukes. Rabbid Peach will be unlocked once she is saved from the Darkmess Manta, in order to unlock the Triple-Troll since Rabbid Peach owns this weapon.

== Statistics ==
Triple-Troll can attack enemies with three missiles while bypassing partial cover.
