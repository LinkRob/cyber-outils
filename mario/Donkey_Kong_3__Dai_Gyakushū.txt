'''''Donkey Kong 3: Dai Gyakushū''''' is a sequel to ''[[Donkey Kong 3]]'' exclusively released in Japan in August 1984 for select Japanese computers, including NEC PC-88, Sharp X1, and PC-6601. The game was both developed and published by [[Hudson Soft]]. [[Stanley the Bugman]] once again stars as the main protagonist.
