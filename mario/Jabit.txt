A '''Jabit''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Jabits are small, nail-like creatures that serve as security guards for [[Smithy]] in [[The Factory]]. Jabits are often made as they usually self-destruct in battle.

==Battle==
Jabits usually accompany other enemies such as [[Mad Mallet]]s in battle. As stated above, Jabits often use a move called [[Last Shot!]] which involves them self-destructing at a party member dealing heaving damage.

===Strategy===
Jabits are weak against [[Thunder (element)|thunder]]-based attacks, so [[Mallow]]'s [[Shocker (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Shocker]] is very effective against them. It is recommended to take the Jabits out as soon as possible because their self-destructing attack is very powerful.
