'''Ganchans''' are boulder-like enemies featured in the [[Easton Kingdom]] in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''.

{{Clear}}

== Description ==
Being based on boulders, Ganchans resemble those enemies, and their colour in artwork is yellow-orange. There are also two black beads that are missing in their sprite, presumably their eyes.

==History==
Ganchans attempt to roll into [[Mario]] to damage him, and they are invincible to Mario's attacks. Ganchans are usually found near [[Spike (hazard)|spike]] pits, and although Ganchans are enemies, Mario can safely ride on one, especially to move across spiked terrain.

In [[World 3-3 (Super Mario Land)|World 3-3]], the boss of Easton Kingdom, [[Hiyoihoi]], attacks by throwing Ganchans at Mario.

In the Manga adaptation of the game, Hiyoihoi has his Ganchan projectile attacks replaced by generic boulders, while the sentient Ganchans appear as allies after [[Princess Peach]], in her Mecha Kuribō disguise, stretches her costume's face to mimic either a [[Batadon]] or a [[Tokotoko]].
