'''Bomb Blocks''' are an object in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

A '''Bomb Block'''<ref>''Wario Land: Shake It!'' instruction booklet (page 8)</ref> is a type of [[block]] featured in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''. They also serve as platforms, if temporarily.

==Description==
Bomb Blocks are an explosive type of [[block]], but [[Wario]] can also use them as temporary platforms. Each Bomb Block displays a certain number, and activating a Bomb Block causes the number to quickly count down. Upon reaching zero, the Bomb Block explodes. Wario can take damage if he is within the radius of the explosion, but enemies caught within the radius can be defeated from it.

Wario can activate a Bomb Block in a few different ways, including by walking across it, touching the Bomb Block, shaking the ground, or throwing an enemy at a Bomb Block.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Bomb Blocks are often found in desert levels, such as [[Whoopsy Desert]], and volcanic-themed levels, such as [[Mt. Lava Lava]]. Both levels feature a mission in which Wario has to detonate every Bomb Block.
