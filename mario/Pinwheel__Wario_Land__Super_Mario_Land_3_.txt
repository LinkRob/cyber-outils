'''Pinwheels''' are seahorse-based enemies which inhabit the waters of [[Kitchen Island]] in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical appearance===
Pinwheels have a red body with a while belly and four spikes lined on their back. They are shown to be red only in their artwork but not in-game due to the [[Game Boy]]'s monochromatic palette.

===Behavior===
Upon noticing [[Wario]], a Pinwheel curls into a spiked ball and tries to launch at him.
