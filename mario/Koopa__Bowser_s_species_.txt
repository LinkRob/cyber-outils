'''Bowser's species/race''', named '''royal reptiles''' in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' cartoon,<ref> ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]:'' ''[[Kootie Pie Rocks]]'' "I'll have you know I'm a royal reptile and I command you to play a private concert for me this instant!"</ref> '''royal Koopas''' in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'',<ref>''[[Super Mario Sunshine]] "The royal Koopa line has never been stronger!"</ref> and {{nihongo|'''king tribe'''|「王族」|ō-zoku}} in the Japanese ''[[Super Mario Complete Encyclopedia]]'',<ref>''1991 [[Super Mario Complete Encyclopedia]]'' page 89</ref> are a race of the [[Koopa]] species, the same as [[Bowser]]. They first appeared in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and made many apperances helping Bowser kidnap [[Princess Peach]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bowser's species are a large tortoise-like species and the leaders of the [[Koopa]] species. They generally have a yellow body and wear a [[Koopa Shell]] varying in color, they also often wear spiked metal cuffs around their arms.
