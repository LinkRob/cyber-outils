'''Yikes! Boiling Hot!''' is the secret stage of [[World 2 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 2]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', a [[Black Yoshi]] takes control in this level. This level is unlocked after [[At Last, Bowser's Castle!]] is completed. This level takes place in a deep-underground lava cavern at the island's mantle filled with lava oceans, lava falls, countless enemies, and dangerous volcanic activity. The level itself is divided into three major sections, the first taking place very deep underground with only red, plate-like, underground, square rocks being visible in the background, the second area takes place in an area with several underground volcanoes which are constantly erupting, and the third area takes place in a simple lava cave with several lava rivers in the background. The level also comes with a fair share of traps and proceeding slowly is the best call when dealing with these. 

==Walkthrough==
'''Note:''' It is recommended to start this level as either [[Baby Mario]] or [[Baby Peach]], the former's fast speed allows him to clear the [[Countdown Platform]]s quickly while the latter can use the wind to reach the upper ledges in the second area easier.

Starting off the level, advance to the right and hop on the first [[Teeter Totter]], each of the three [[Teeter Totter]]s in this area have [[Red Coin]]s below them so tip them low enough to be able to grab the [[Red Coin]] and then hop to the next [[Teeter Totter]] before the one you're standing on falls into the [[lava]] below, after you've grabbed the [[Red Coin]]s, hop onto the [[Arrow Wheel]] and guide it upward (once you've hopped on the [[Arrow Wheel]], the [[lava]] below starts rising, the [[Arrow Wheel]] you're using is also a blue one meaning it will disappear after some time so guide it quickly). Guide the [[Arrow Wheel]] straight avoiding the [[Fang]]s that come at you and steer clear of the cascading lava. Upon reaching the top, hop across the [[Teeter Totter]]s and watch out for the [[Blow Hard]]s above which spit [[Needlenose]]s at you. Continue to the right hopping across the descending stone slabs while watching out for the [[Bungee Piranha]]s above, from here, run across the [[One-Jump Platform]]s and grab the [[Coin]]s as you go, drop down to the lower [[Countdown Platform]] to grab the [[Smiley Flower]] and then quickly return to the track above hopping across the remaining [[One-Jump Platform]]s and [[Teeter Totter]]s and then exit the area before the rising [[lava]] below catches up to you.

Upon reaching the next area, use [[Baby Peach]]'s wind abilities to reach the ledge above and tag the [[Middle Ring]] (if you're not using [[Baby Peach]], bounce off one of the incoming [[Fang]]s to reach the ledge instead). Tag the [[Stork Stop]] on the next ledge to switch to [[Baby Peach]] (if you're not using her) and use her abilities to glide the winds in this area, there are several coin lines with rows of two [[Coin]]s here, both of these contain a [[Red Coin]] each, there are also dual [[Coin]]s  in this area, one is on the top left ledge and the other is below the [[Blow Hard]] on the underside of the ledge (these contain a [[Red Coin]] each) (which totals to four [[Red Coin]]s, be careful of the cascading lava as you grab the [[Red Coin]]s, once done, fly to the right but watch out for the occasional lava falls, wait for them to recede before progressing, [[Fang]]s will continue coming at you periodically so avoid these too. Once you've cleared the first lava fall, drop down to the ledge below and grab all the [[Coin]]s in the dual-[[Coin]] line, one of these is a [[Red Coin]], next drop down and use the wind to flutter the the ledge with the [[! Switch]], hit the switch to activate the switch blocks to reach the ledge with the [[Smiley Flower]] (the [[Smiley Flower]] is blocked by cascading lava preventing you from getting it without hitting the switch, egg tosses won't work either as they dissolve after hitting the cascading lava). Once gotten, glide to the lower ledge to the right and grab all the [[Coin]]s in the dual-[[Coin]] lot as one of them is a [[Red Coin]] (but watch out for the [[Needlenose Plant]] that spits [[Needlenose]]s), as you glide back up to the main path, be careful of the cascading lava, from here, watch out for the next lava fall, wait for it to recede, advance to the right, and drop down and grab the [[Coin]]s in the dual-Coin]] lot as once again, one of them is a [[Red Coin]]. Next, you will find some safe ground, switch to [[Baby DK]] at the [[Stork Stop]] here and swing across the large rope to the upper ledge, hop to the rope here and grab any [[Coin]]s here as well as the [[Smiley Flower]] on the ledge (be careful not to swing the rope too much as you may swing it into the cascading lava if you do). Once you've gotten the goodies here, drop down to the lower ledge and advance to the right, there's a [[Stork Stop]] here too, use it to switch to [[Baby DK]] (but chance are you're already using him from the last [[Stork Stop]] but it's there in case you fluttered all the way here instead). Climb the vines above and grab the [[Coin]]s along them, jump to the higher left vine upon scaling the initial vine and use it to grab the upper [[Coin]]s and even a [[Red Coin]], once done, jump back to the initial vine and exit the area through the right ledge.

In the next area, you will enter a new lava cavern (slightly closer to the surface), advance to the right and watch out for the [[Hot Lips]] in the lava that spews lava at you, you will also need to watch out for the [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s and wait for the spikes to be out of the way before hopping to the platforms (if it helps, stun the [[Hot Lips]] with an egg), grab the [[Smiley Flower]] by the upper [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s and then hop to the [[Donut Lift]]s and then the platform (be careful of the second [[Hot Lips]] here as it shots it's lava in an arc rather than straight up (but you should still be safe on the platform), use the [[Egg Block]] here to fill your [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] supply and use your eggs to hit the soft stones holding up the stone slabs, as the stone slabs descend, use them to hop across the [[lava]] safely, the last two stone slabs are thinner than the first two and upon dropping the fourth stone slab, a lava fall immediately descends so be sure to wait on the third one for the lava fall to die down before hopping to the fourth slab. Once you've cleared the slabs, hop to the rope connected to the zip line (remain as high as possible on the rope as the rope zooms fast and dips into the lava below), there are six [[Coin]]s along this track with the two at the end being [[Red Coin]]s, once you've cleared this, immediately hop to the vine above (if you missed one of the [[Red Coin]]s, use an egg to obtain them instead). Once done, exit the area through the right.

Upon entering the next area, tag the [[Middle Ring]] above the logs sticking out of the lava, from here, jump to the next set of logs and stone slabs (there is a [[Red Coin]] above the slab), from here, jump to the partially-submerged [[Ferris Wheel]] and quickly grab the [[Smiley Flower]] and then hop off the paddle before it sinks (you can also toss an egg from one of the upper paddles as well), from here, hop across the slab and the logs (whilst watching out for the [[Co-Blargg]]s and kill the [[Wild Ptooie Piranha]] on the stone block (this stone block is between two logs which both have [[Red Coin]]s above them), from here, continue hopping across the logs and slabs (and watch out for the [[Hot Lips]] as he spews his lava in an arc), from here, grab the [[Red Coin]] on the end log and then jump to the dense stone platforms, tag the [[Stork Stop]] and immediately switch to [[Baby Mario]] with all speed as there is a [[Crusher Blargg]] below the rocks that will destroy them (knocking the [[Stork Stop]] into the lava (without [[Baby Mario]], this level is impossible to finish due to the required use of the [[M Block]]s so make sure to switch to him as quick as possible)). Run to the right and grab the two [[Red Coin]]s here (toss an egg at the [[Crusher Blargg]] to sink it back in the lava as it will move in your direction and smash the rocks you're standing on), from here, leap to the remaining rock platforms (or flutter to the [[M Block]]s if the [[Crusher Blargg]] destroyed them all), and hop across the [[M Block]]s (the [[Crusher Blargg]] cannot destroy the [[M Block]]s but it is still tall enough to reach you so watch out, additionally, [[Fang]]s are see whizzing about too so be careful of these)), at the end of the [[M Block]] track, hit the lone [[M Block]] above to sprout a [[Flower Plant]] to climb, toss an egg at the [[Red Coin]]s in the lava niche halfway up, from here, climb the rest of the stalk to reach the top and jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to finish the level.
