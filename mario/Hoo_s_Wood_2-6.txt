}}'''Hoo's Wood 2-6''' is the sixth level of [[Hoo's Wood]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]].'' The game starts with Peach on the round log.

== Layout ==
The player must spin the platform with the {{button|ds|stylus}} to reach the top. If the player touches the thorns, the player will return to the start of the level.

=== Battle ===
{{Main|Hoo}}
[[Hoo]] swoops and snatches the Toad inside the bubble before Peach runs towards him. According to Perry, when Hoo swoops, Peach should aim its head and stomp it.

Hoo hovers on Peach and throws three eggs. The birds spawn from the eggs, Peach can defeat the birds. When you hit the egg, sometimes it is rarely bonus.

After Hoo has two lives left, it will break the platform. It spins around in a whirlwind and spawn eggs. When Hoo crosses its wings, it signals that it is invincible. Peach can use the Joy vibe to accurately aim at Hoo's head.
