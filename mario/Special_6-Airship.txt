'''{{world|s6|airship}}''' is the [[Airship]] level of [[Special 6]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is based off of [[World 4-Airship (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 4-Airship]]. 
==Layout==
The level starts with the player traversing [[Flip-Swap Panel]]s, with [[Brick Block]]s functioning as stable platforms. Near the end of the section, there is a single [[Stingby]]. Following this section, the player must navigate the lower level of the airship, with the entrance containing another Stingby and a [[Blokkablok]] that contains a [[Statue Leaf]]. Once the player drops into the lower level, they can either take a [[Warp Pipe]], which leads to a battle against [[Boom Boom]], or they can traverse a narrow path made of Brick Blocks to skip this fight. After this section, two [[Fake Block]]s attack the player on the path out of the lower level of the airship. After that, the player must traverse more Red-Blue Panels, with multiple Stingbies attacking them. Following this section, the player reaches the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Medal]]s==
*'''Star Medal 1:''' Behind some [[Brick Block]]s next to the [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the [[Boom Boom]] fight.
*'''Star Medal 2:''' On some [[Red-Blue Panel]]s right after the Boom Boom fight. A single [[Stingby]] is guarding it.
*'''Star Medal 3:''' Floating above a [[Spike Trap|spike]] on the final section of Red-Blue Panels.
