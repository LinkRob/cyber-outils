The '''Slimy Mushroom''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]''.

==Description==
When used, the Slimy Mushroom turns the racer's [[kart]] into yellow slime, allowing them to pass through opponents. [[Blinky]]'s [[Special Item (Mario Kart series)|Special Item]], [[Ghost Morph]], functions identically to the Slimy Mushroom.
