'''Don't Fear Flying Foes''' is the seventh level of [[Hoohoo Mountain]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 3.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 3 [[Beanie]]s and 5 [[Paratroopea]]. The enemy captain is a Paratroopea.

The final battle contains 2 Beanies and 6 [[Paragoomba]]s. The enemy captain is a Paragoomba.

After the final battle, the player unlocks a Paragoomba.

The player may replay the level to gain up to one Paragoomba each time.
