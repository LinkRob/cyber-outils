'''Roller Coasters''' is a 4-player and Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place within a vertical, straightforward sandbox. At the end is a sand castle designed in the likeness of the [[castle]]s in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Gameplay==
Each character is riding their own uniquely colored [[Ball (object)|ball]], and their goal is to reach the end first. There are a few obstacles along the way, including buckets, moving toy trucks, and houses made from blocks. If a character hits an obstacle, they are bounced back briefly.

==Ending==
If the player sets a record for completing the minigame, it is shown. The winning character is on top of the sand castle, doing their victory animation, while the losing characters are in front of the castle, where they each perform their losing animations. If every character reaches the end simultaneously or no one has crossed the [[finish line]] within five minutes, the minigame ends in a tie, during which every character is shown standing in front of the sand castle.

In the Duel variation, if both characters break the current record but get a tie, the new record is still counted, but the minigame display the message "Tie" after the new record fanfare is played.

==Controls==
*[[Stylus]]: Roll the ball

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Race to the finish by pushing your ball with the stylus. Avoid the obstacles along the way."''
*'''Tips''' &ndash; ''"You'll rebound from any obstacle you hit, and it will take time to recover your momentum. Move carefully."''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
*The minigame's name is a reference to "roller coaster," a type of amusement park ride, even though none appear within the minigame.
