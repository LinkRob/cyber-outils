The '''Splat Buggy''' is a kart body for an [[All-Terrain Vehicle]] which appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is one of the [[Inkling]]'s two signature vehicles, the other one being the [[Inkstriker]]. The Splat Buggy does not appear in the original ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.
