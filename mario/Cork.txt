'''Corks''' are objects which first appear in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' equivalent, ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== Description ==
Corks are objects that block openings, usually of a [[Warp Pipe]], usually to prevent the player's character from accessing those locations. The player is required to use some sort of method to remove the cork.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''===
[[File:SMB2 Cork sprite.png|frame|left]]
There is only one cork, and it is closing the opening of a vase, imprisoning the [[Subcon (species)|Subcon]]s within. After [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] or [[Toad (character)|Toad]] defeat [[Wart]], they discover the vase and pull the cork out, freeing the Subcons.

===''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''===
[[File:Cork Sunshine screenshot.png|thumb|The giant cork in ''Super Mario Sunshine'']]
A giant cork blocks the waterfall during the first [[mission|episode]] of [[Noki Bay]], [[Uncork the Waterfall]]. The [[Noki]] fisherman believes that the blockage is polluting the bay. On top of the cork, Mario encounters a [[Monty Mole]] manning a [[Cannon (Super Mario Sunshine)|cannon]]. Mario proceeds to defeat the Monty Mole, causing the cannon to explode. The force of the waterfall throws the cork and the cannon with the Monty Mole into the sky. The waterfall is now uncorked, but this is not the reason for the pollution.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''===
Near the end of [[World 2-6 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-6]], there is a cork with a [[Piranha Plant]] on top of it that is blocking the [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] to the exit. To make the pipe accessible, Mario or Luigi must jump on the nearby pump device three times to send the cork flying, taking the Piranha Plant with it.

===''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''===
Corks are found in [[KO Arena Pipe-Rock Rumble]] and [[KO Arena Fluff-Puff Kerfuff]], where they block the pipes that dispense power-ups in these levels' starting rooms; because of the corks, the [[Bubble Flower]] cannot be obtained in Pipe-Rock Rumble, and the [[Drill Mushroom]] cannot be obtained in either level.

==In ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''==
There is a cork with a keyhole that plugs a pipe in [[Tap-Tap The Red Nose's Fort]]. It can only be removed with a [[key]] from a [[Slime]]. Without the key, [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can push down the cork by performing a [[Ground Pound|Pound The Ground]]

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
[[File:PMCS Cork Pipe.png|thumb|A Cork in ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'']]
{{main|Cork (Thing)}}
A cork is a [[Things|Thing]] found blocking a pipe entrance in [[Vortex Island]], and Mario has to remove it before he can access the parallel universe. The cork is later used against [[Larry]] on the [[Sunset Express]], where it blocks the train's chimney, preventing Larry from healing himself with [[heart (item)|heart]] smoke coming out of it.
