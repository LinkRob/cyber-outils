The {{nihongo|'''8 bits'''|魔夢族|Mamū-zoku|Mamu Family}} is the name of an army that [[Wart]] commanded to take over [[Subcon]]. They make their first and main appearance in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its overseas conversion ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. In the former game, it is revealed that the 8-bits are created from the [[Dream Machine]]. The 8 bits are spread and encountered throughout almost every area of Subcon.

==''BS Super Mario USA''==
In the [[Satellaview]]-broadcast sequel to ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', the 8 bits return to Subcon sometime after taking refuge in another dream when they were defeated by [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Toad (character)|Toad]]. Their return causes Subcon's ruler, [[Ōsama]], to go into hiding and call upon the heroes' assistance in defeating the 8 bits once more.

==In other media==
===''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''===
Most of the 8 bits make an appearance, with the notable exception of Wart. The 8 bits organization is not featured because they instead serve [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]], as part of the [[Koopa Troop]]. [[Panser]] is the only regular enemy of the 8 bits to not appear anywhere within the show.
