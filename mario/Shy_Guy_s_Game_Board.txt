'''Shy Guy's Game Board''' is a [[board]] hosted by [[Shy Guy]] in the [[Mario Party 4 (Flash game)|''Mario Party 4'' Flash game]]. The only junction in the board is the cave in the center, which will randomly send the player to one of three paths. [[Baby Bowser]] occasionally appears, replacing all [[Blue Space]]s with [[Red Space]]s and vice-versa for 3 turns.
