The '''Triple Dice''' is an item that appears in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series. When used, it allows the player to roll three [[Dice Block]]s (3-30 spaces) for the turn, adding the results together to determine their roll, similarly to the effect of the [[Golden Mushroom]] from ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==History==
===''Mario Party DS''===
[[File:Item-Triple.png|frame]]
The Triple Dice first appeared in ''[[Mario Party DS]]'' under the name '''Triple Dice Set'''. It can be purchased from [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shops]] and usually starts appearing in shops around the fifth turn. In this game, rolling three matching numbers with this item grants the player a bonus 20 coins (or 50 coins for three "7's").

===''Mario Party Superstars''===
The Triple Dice reappears in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', where it can be purchased from Item Shops on most boards coins usually starting from midway through the game. Its effects are largely unchanged from ''Mario Party DS''. However rolling three "7's" now awards a bonus 20 coins instead of 50 (unlike previous installments or 100 in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''). (Like the [[Double Dice]], no special bonus is awarded for matching the Dice Blocks with "7's" unlike previous installments.)

===''Super Mario Party Jamboree''===
The Triple Dice returns in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'' with the same function as it had in ''Mario Party Superstars''. A stronger variant, known as [[Payday Triple Dice]], also exists, and works just like Triple Dice, except that the player also receives the number of coins equivalent to the total roll shown.

If the player rolls the same number on all three dice, they earn the "Luck's Favorite Pupil" achievement.
