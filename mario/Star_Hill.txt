'''Star Hill''' is a location in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and [[Reissue|its]] [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. It is a large hill where all wishes are granted; it is similar to that of [[Star World]]. There is also a race of ethereal beings who live in the [[Star Road (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Road]], with [[Geno]] being one of them.

==History==

=== ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' ===
When wishes are made, they appear on Star Hill in the form of [[Wish Star]]s. These stars are scattered along the dark blue ground. In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'', [[Mario]], [[Mallow]] and [[Geno]] travel here for a [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]]. This Star Piece is the only one that can be taken without a fight; it rests on the ground at the end of Star Hill, unguarded by anyone (most likely because the player fought in [[Booster Tower]] and [[Marrymore]]). The Wish Stars can be examined to hear the wishes of the people who made them. Two of these wishes will come true over the course of the game, and if the wishes are examined after it comes true, the wish will be changed.

=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Star Hill was located just north of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]], and it had a [[Star Spirit|similar purpose]]. It was noticeably less dangerous to travel in this time around. In the English version, it was renamed [[Shooting Star Summit]].

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'' ===
[[File:M&L-PIT Star Hill Map.png|thumb|left|Overview of Star Hill, from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.]]
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', in the past, Star Hill was invaded and taken over by [[Shroob|Shroobs]].  Mario, [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] arrive here in via [[Warp Block (Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time)|warp block]] from [[Toad Town]] ruins. They head on reach on top of the hill and fought [[Commander Shroob]]. Arriving on top, [[Stuffwell]] opens his suitcase as the half remaining [[Cobalt Star]] pieces turns into a "spirit". The Cobalt Star spirit tells the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] to quickly find the last three shards and collect two of them to pierce the Shroob barrier of [[Shroob Castle]].  The Cobalt Star spirit makes a large bridge for them to walk through a warp block that connects [[Star Shrine]].

== Wishes ==
There are many wishes added in Star Hill. When [[Mario]] touches certain stars, they talk and say one of various wishes. Many of them are for comedic purposes.
* ''"Wish I had some Cricket Jam."''/''"I wish for everyone to be happy."'' ([[Frogfucius]]'s wishes)
* ''"Can't wait to start a family."'' ([[Raz]]'s wish)
* ''"I hope my baby's cute."'' ([[Raini]]'s wish)
* ''"I wish I weren't such a crybaby."'' ([[Mallow]]'s wish)
* ''"If I could JUST get that melody!"'' ([[Toadofsky]]'s wish)
* ''"The hunger! Oh, for some food!"'' ([[Belome]]'s wish)
* ''"I vant to be a vald class baker."'' ([[Chef Torte]]'s wish)
* ''"Please let Mallow find his way home."''/''"May Mallow fix the Road!"'' (King Nimbus and/or Queen Nimbus's wishes)
* ''"I wanna be the best treasure hunter in the world."'' (Treasure Hunting Toad's wish)
* ''"(Wish I could run faster.)"'' ([[Yoshi]]'s wish)
* ''"I hope I become famous."'' ([[Punchinello]]'s wish)
* ''"I wanna be a great plumber like my brother Mario."'' ([[Luigi]]'s wish)
