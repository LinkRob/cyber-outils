The '''Shell Earrings''' are key items that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
The Shell Earrings are earrings that resemble seashells that are pink in color.

==History==
The Shell Earrings belong to the [[Toad waitress]], and they remind her of her [[Toad waitress's ex|ex-boyfriend]]. During the events of Chapter 6, her Shell Earrings were stolen during Day 2 on the [[Excess Express]]. It is later revealed that [[Doopliss]] disguised as [[Zip Toad]] stole the Shell Earrings along with [[Toodles]]' [[Gold Ring (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|Gold Ring]] and the [[Businessman]]'s [[Briefcase]]. Doopliss planned to get rid of [[Mario]] once and for all by using the [[Nitro Honey Syrup]]. However, his plan was foiled and the Shell Earrings can later be returned to the Toad Waitress for a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] as a reward.
