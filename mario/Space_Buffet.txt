'''Space Buffet''' is one of the [[microgame]]s in [[Dr. Crygor]]'s [[Slightly Unscrewed]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must use their [[stylus]] to swirl the black hole so that the spaceships and planets are drawn into it. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame, indicated by a mouth appearing over the black hole that belches and smiles. If the objective is not met on time, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' Planets must be drawn into the black hole.
*'''Level 2:''' Both planets and spaceships must be drawn into the black hole.
*'''Level 3:''' A giant moon with [[Fronk (species)|Fronk]]s has to be drawn into the black hole.
