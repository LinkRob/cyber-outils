'''Carrot Away''' is one of [[Mona]]'s [[microgame]]s featured in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must have their character yank out [[carrot]]s for harvest time. In the original Japanese version, the vegetable is [[daikon]] instead.

*'''1st level difficulty''': The player needs to pull only 1 carrot.
*'''2nd level difficulty''': The player needs to pull 2 carrots.
*'''3rd level difficulty''': The player needs to pull 3 carrots.

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name is a pun on the phrase "carried away."
