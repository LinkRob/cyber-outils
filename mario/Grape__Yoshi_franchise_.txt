Grapes reappear in ''[[Yoshi (game)|Yoshi]]'', after the player clears levels 14 and 15 of the B-Type game, awarding the player 700 points.
{{br}}
===''Yoshi's Story''===
[[File:YSGrapes.png|frame|right]]
[[File:Story Grape bubble.png|frame|left]]
Grapes appear in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'' as one of the fruit the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s have to eat to clear a level. Grapes are the favorite fruit of both Blue Yoshi and Light Blue Yoshi.

===''Yoshi Touch & Go''===
In ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'', grapes give [[Yoshi]] ten [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s when eaten.

==Crossover appearances==
===''Nintendo Land''===
In ''[[Nintendo Land]]'', grapes are one of the types of fruit that can appear in [[Yoshi's Fruit Cart]]. It can also be one of the prizes received from the Coin Game.
