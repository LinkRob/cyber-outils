'''''Baseball''''' is a sports game for the [[Game Boy]], for which it was released as a launch title in 1989. It is generally a regular baseball game, being a [[reissue|remake]] of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[w:c:nintendo:Baseball|same name]].

''Baseball'' features [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] as playable characters amongst several other regular [[human]] baseball players. It is the first baseball game to have Mario in it, predating the first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-branded baseball game, ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', and by extension the {{series|Mario Baseball}}, by about 16 years.
