'''Shell Smash''' is one of [[Mario]]'s minigames in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. There is a similar minigame titled [[Shuffle Shell]].

== Layout ==
The minigame takes place in an icy rink during a snowfall, with the background music from [[Cool, Cool Mountain]]/[[Chief Chilly Challenge]]/[[Snowman's Land]] playing. There is a green [[Koopa Shell]] on the Touch Screen, along with a blue object underneath it, and on the top screen, there are six red Koopa Shells aligned in a pattern.

== Gameplay ==
The minigame involves the player hitting the green Koopa Shells into other shells to score points, which rack up upon each time the shells bounce off each other. The player has to use the stylus and launch the green Koopa Shells as hard as they can to score more points at a frequent and faster rate as long as they position their aim well enough. The player can score even more points by hitting multiple green Koopa shells into each other.

The player gets four green shells in total to hit, and once all of them have been used up, the player's score is added to the records.

== In-game instructions ==
: ''"Hit Koopa shells together to score points. Hit many in a row for even more points. Score even higher points by smashing green shells together."''
