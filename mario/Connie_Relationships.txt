==Friends==

=== [[Cozette]] ===
[[File:Connie&CozetteSistership.jpg|left|thumb|Connie and [[Cozette]], from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.]]
Cozette is Connie's mentor and the original [[Wattanist]]. She has a close connection to Cozette like a mother/sister bond relationship. It is shown in the flashback; Cozette took Connie to several different places of [[Concordia]] on her duties to become a Wattanist to guard and protect the [[Uni-Tree]] and hopes toone day come to her rescue. At some point, prior to the [[Concordia]] [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|incident]], Cozette went missing who Connie was unaware until later revealed that she is [[Zokket]], who was possessed by the [[Glohm]] controlled by [[Reclusa]]. Despite Cozette's actions, Connie still cares about her. As shown when the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] brought Cozette to [[Shipshape Island]], upon Connie's reaction, she started crying, finally seeing her after so long.
[[File:Connie&CozetteSistership-2.jpg|thumb|Connie taking care of Cozette, from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.]]
In [[Cozette's Decision]], Connie was shocked and worried Cozette left again as she send a letter apology to her without saying goodbye and went out on her own to save Concordia from Reclusa.

=== Mario and Luigi ===
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are Connie's allies. When they get stranded on [[Offandon Island]] and later on [[Conductor Island]], she worries that they died, and is relieved when she finds that they survived. Connie also comes to their aid when [[Reclusa]] merges with the [[Soli-Tree]] itself and the Bros. collapse.

== Enemies ==

=== Zokket ===
Connie is portrayed as scared of [[Zokket]], as the madman has torn apart [[Concordia]] in his mission to resurrect [[Reclusa]].

=== Reclusa ===
When she confronts the demon at the end of the game, Connie tells [[Reclusa]] that while one can do things alone, there are some things that require teamwork. Reclusa takes it poorly, after which Connie puts her foot down and declares that it has no place in [[Concordia]] and should disappear for good, which happens not long afterward as Reclusa breathes its last breath, shutting down.
