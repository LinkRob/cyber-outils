'''Freeze Cannons''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are small brown creatures stationed inside black dome shaped machines with a cannon.

===Behavior===
Freeze Cannons shoot out a stream of ice that can freeze [[Wario]].

==In ''Wario World''==
Freeze Cannons are only encountered in the [[Shivering Mountains]]. If Wario is frozen by a Freeze Cannon's stream of ice, the player must move the Control Stick until he breaks free. Wario cannot defeat a Freeze Cannon, but if he [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounds]] it, the Freeze Cannon is decommissioned temporarily.
