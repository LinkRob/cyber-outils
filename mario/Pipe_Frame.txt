The '''Pipe-Frame''' is an unlockable [[kart]] body part in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. It is a [[Retro Kart]], and is based on the Standard Karts (and the only karts) in the first three ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' games. It can be unlocked by getting a random amount of [[coin]]s (anywhere from 50 to 4500) and in ''Mario Kart 8'' it can be unlocked by getting a random amount of coins (anywhere from 50 to 900).

==Trivia==
*In ''Mario Kart 8 ''and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', the Pipe Frame is the only vehicle body to have the exhaust pipe switch sides depending on the CC class: in 50cc, 100cc, and 150cc, the exhaust pipe is on the left while in Mirror Mode, the exhaust pipe is on the right.
