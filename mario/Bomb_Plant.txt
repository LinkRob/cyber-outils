A '''Bomb Plant''' is a variant of a [[Bob-omb]]. A Bomb Plant appears in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== Profile ==
Bomb Plants resemble Bob-ombs with their leaves as a trigger in the television series. In ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', the player can pull out a Bomb Plant.
