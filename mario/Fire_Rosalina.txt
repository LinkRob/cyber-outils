'''Fire Rosalina''' is a transformation of [[Rosalina]] when she uses the [[Fire Flower]]. Her transformation debuted in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
When Rosalina obtains a Fire Flower to obtain this form, her dress becomes completely red, with white trimmings around the sleeves, the top and bottom of her dress. Her hair also becomes styled in a ponytail like [[Princess Peach]].

=== Powers and abilities ===
In addition to having all the same abilites as Rosalina (save for her [[Spin Jump]]), Fire Rosalina can use pyrokinesis, shooting fireballs from her hands to attack enemies. She is also exceptional in driving vehicles.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Fire Rosalina debuted in the [[Rosalina Tour (2020)|Rosalina Tour]] as a High-End driver.
