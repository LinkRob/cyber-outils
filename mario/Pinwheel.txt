A '''Pinwheel''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Pinwheels are fan-like creatures that can be found in [[Nimbus Castle]] during [[Valentina]]'s takeover. Pinwheels are rather troublesome enemies on the field as they can blow [[Mario]] away to a previous room. Mario has to wait until the Pinwheel stops blowing air in order to engage them in battle.

==Battle==
In battle, Pinwheels are capable of using lightning spells such as [[Static E!]] and [[Lightning Orb]]. They are often found alongside other enemies such as [[Sling Shy]]s and [[Jawful]]s.
