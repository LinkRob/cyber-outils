'''''Game Boy Gallery''''' is a [[Game Boy]] game featuring a collection of five [[Game & Watch]] games. It was exclusively released in Europe and Australia in 1995. The game is the first game in the [[Game & Watch Gallery (series)|''Game & Watch Gallery'' series]], but it differentiates from later installments in that there are neither Classic nor Modern modes. There is one mode with graphics similar to that of Modern Mode, but has the gameplay of Classic Mode.

''Game Boy Gallery'' features nameless individuals as the main characters. It is the only ''Game & Watch Gallery'' title without any ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters. One of the games, ''Cement Factory'', is a re-release of ''[[Mario's Cement Factory]]'', but [[Mario]] is replaced by one of the generic characters and is removed from the game's title.
