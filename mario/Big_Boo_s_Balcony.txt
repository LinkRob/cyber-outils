'''Big Boo's Balcony''' is the fifth [[mission]] of [[Big Boo's Haunt]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[Nintendo DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Layout==
Big Boo's Balcony does not affect the layout of Big Boo's Haunt, meaning it can be completed, even if a different mission is selected.

==Objective==
The object is to defeat the [[Big Boo]] on the balcony of the haunted house. [[Mario]] (or either [[Luigi]] or [[Wario]] in the remake) first has to enter the door on the right second the haunted house's second floor. Once there, Mario has to [[Wall Jump|wall-kick]] up to the door above the entrance. The door leads into the attack, where Mario has to go through the double doors on the right to go onto the balcony. There, Mario has to [[Ground Pound (move)|ground-pound]] Big Boo three times for the [[Power Star]] to appear. It is on top of the haunted house's roof, and to reach it, the player's character has to [[Long Jump|long-jump]] to cross the large gap between the balcony and the roof, then climb the roof to obtain the Power Star.

In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', if playing as Mario, he can use his [[Power Flower (Super Mario 64 DS)|Power Flower]] ability as an alternate way to go up to the attic and to reach the Power Star on the roof once Big Boo has been defeated.
