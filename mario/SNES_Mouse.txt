The '''SNES Mouse''' is an accessory compatible with certain games for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. It was designed after a computer mouse, and functions similarly to it.

The Super NES Mouse was compatible with various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, the first being ''[[Mario Paint]]'', followed by ''[[Mario & Wario]]'', ''[[Mario's Early Years! Fun with Letters]]'', ''[[Mario's Early Years! Fun with Numbers]]'', ''[[Mario's Early Years! Preschool Fun]]'', and ''[[Undake30 Same Game]]''.
