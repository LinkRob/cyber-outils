|forms   = [[Princess Peach]] (lesser form)|latest=''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''|rivals=[[Bowser]]}}'''Cat Peach''' is a playable character in the games ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. She was available as part of a DLC pack that was released in November 2014.

==Overview==
Cat Peach is similar to her normal form, except she wears a suit covered in very fluffy pink fur and a collar.

Nothing much is known about Cat Peach except that she has her own car designed after her.

==History==

=== ''Super Mario 3D World/Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury'' ===
Cat Peach is a playable character in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. She has the same attacks and abilities as [[Cat Mario]].

===''Mario Kart 8''/''Deluxe''===
As a playable character, Cat Peach along with [[Tanooki Mario]] are downloadable characters.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
Cat Peach made a cameo appearance as a spirit.
==Trivia==
*Along with [[Tanooki Mario]] (since they were released at the same time), Cat Peach is the second character in the ''Mario Kart'' series to appear from a power-up. The first is [[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]] however Metal Mario also appeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' as a boss.
*This also marks Cat Peach's second time in a Mario game outside from ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.
