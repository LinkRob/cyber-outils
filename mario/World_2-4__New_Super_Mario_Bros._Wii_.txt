|time=500 seconds}}'''World 2-4''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Overview==
Every Few Seconds, Wind appears on the Level and Force You to the Right Direction. It features [[Spike Top]]s. It Also causes Sand Dunes On The Floor. A Secret Exit is on this Level, going into an Hidden Path in the Top of the Level and entering on a Red Pipe. Use the Forcing Wind to get a Little Help.
==Walkthrough==
Every few seconds, a strong wind blows in the level, which causes [[Coin]]s to blow in, affects ladders throughout the level, and forces the player to the right, though the player character is not moved if they are [[crouch]]ing. 

The player starts in an area with a structure above, with the [[? Block]] above it containing a power-up that can be reached by climbing the ladder. After another structure is a collection of [[Brick Block]]s with a [[Koopa Troopa]] that can be used to break them and the ? Blocks underneath. After the red [[Warp Pipe]], the player has to use the wind to jump over the ledge, unless they have a [[Propeller Mario|propeller suit]]. Climbing up another ladder, the player can get over a structure that leads into a group of Brick Blocks, followed by another platform above that has a power-up in a ? Block. After the red pipe ahead are two Brick Blocks, with the one closest to the red pipe containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. Across the gap is the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]].

Across the next gap is a hill of Koopa Troopas, with a [[barrel]] just before it the player can use to defeat them. In the top ? Block afterward is a power-up, followed by a ladder to reach the top of a long platform. Beyond this long platform are two jagged platforms with [[Spike Top]]s walking along them. After a gap is a hill of Koopa Troopas before the Warp Pipe leading to the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': On the platform not long after the area with the first [[POW Block]], there are three [[Hidden Block]]s in the shape of "^", and the one on top contains a [[Beanstalk]]. This leads to an area with several more Hidden Blocks, along with two [[? Block]]s containing Beanstalks. Mario and co. should find openings in the rows of Hidden Blocks and jump to the first Star Coin which is floating high in the air.
*'''Star Coin 2''': A [[Propeller Suit]] is required for this one. To the right of the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], below normal ground, is a green [[Warp Pipe]]. This Pipe leads to an area with several [[Coin]]s, and the second Star Coin. Mario and co. should fall to the Star Coin then activate the Propeller Suit to fly to safety before falling.
*'''Star Coin 3''': A Propeller Suit is required for this one also. To the right of the Pipe that leads from the area with the second Star Coin, Mario and co. should wait for the Wind to blow, dash, jump, then activate the Propeller Suit to fly up to the third Star Coin which is floating below a platform with a [[Brick Block]] and a [[? Block]].
