'''Body Rock''' is the theme of the TV show ''Jimmy's Aerobics'' in ''[[WarioWare D.I.Y.]]''. It is played during [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s stage, as he does aerobics while two women participate in the background. The theme differs between the English and Japanese versions of the game, but both later appear ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as a souvenir.
