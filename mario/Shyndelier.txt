A '''Shyndelier''' is a enemy and a variant of [[Shy Guy]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
During the [[Smithy Gang]]'s occupation of [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]], the Shyndeliers replace [[Chaindelier]]s as holders of the big chandeliers. Shyndeliers are not fought directly, but they do appear in the battle against [[Boomer (boss)|Boomer]]. Here, after [[Mario]] defeats Boomer, Boomer cuts off his Shyndelier's connection to the chandelier and falls down with it, then Shyndelier holding the chandelier with Mario and his party on it then takes them up to the highest tower of the Bowser's Keep to battle [[Exor]].
