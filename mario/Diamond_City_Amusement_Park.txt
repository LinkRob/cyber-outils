'''Diamond City Amusement Park''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Description==
The Diamond City Amusement Park has a self-explanatory purpose of being a theme park located in [[Diamond City]]. Some of its attractions include the Chromaconda, a snake-shaped roller coaster that goes one hundred miles per hour; Breakneck Falls, a log flume ride; Precious Prancers, a carousel; the Diamond Wheel, a ferris wheel ride; an unnamed tornado ride; and an unnamed teacup ride. Aside from this, there are several tents and balloons, along with a sign showing an overhead view of Diamond City Amusement Park.

==In ''WarioWare Gold''==
In the opening of [[Young Cricket & Master Mantis]]'s story, which is also titled [[Precious Prancers]], the duo are in the teacup ride. This leads [[Young Cricket]] to question [[Master Mantis]]'s methods for his training. The duo then looks over the Diamond City Amusement Park's sign as Young Cricket gives an overview of some of the rides in the park, admitting that he was wrong about the methods. The duo ride the Precious Prancers ride, where their set of [[microgame]]s is played, during which the four chances are represented by a horse, and the microgames are introduced by carriages on the ride.

In the story's ending cutscene, Young Cricket finds [[Lulu]] on his back and tells her to get off.

In the ending credits, Young Cricket, Master Mantis, and Lulu are in front of an image of the Diamond City Amusement Park.
