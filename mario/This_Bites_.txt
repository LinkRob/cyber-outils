'''This Bites!''' is [[Young Cricket]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to avoid the insects while time runs out. The player using "The Elephant" form can move it around to move the man. If it stays on the same spot for a few seconds, the man is stung.

*'''Level 1:''' One insect.
*'''Level 2:''' Two insects.
*'''Level 3:''' Three insects.

==Trivia==
*The man eats the insect when the insect is close to his mouth. The player wins the microgame after the man eats all insects.
