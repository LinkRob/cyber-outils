'''Sharpshooter''' is [[Luigi]]'s default weapon in [[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]. 

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
The Sharpshooter appears in ''Sparks of Hope'' as Luigi's weapon. The player can change the skins of the Sharpshooter. Luigi will be unlocked once Rabbid Peach is saved from the Darkmess Manta, since Luigi owns this weapon.

== Statistics ==
The Sharpshooter can attack one opponent in a straight line and the further the target is, the more damage Luigi does.
