'''Bowser Cup''' is a cup with [[Bowser]] representing as an icon or name. It appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]''/''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' ==
Bowser Cup has no symbol like the rest of the cups. However, it has two courses.
