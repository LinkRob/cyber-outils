'''Puddle Paddle''' is a 2 vs. 2 mini-game and a [[List of minigames in Mario Party 3|Bonus Minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. It makes a reappearance in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the two teams must paddle their way to collect coins. The [[Hammer Bro]] on the platform in the center will toss coins into the water that will disappear over time. It may also toss hammers that will stun anyone that touches them. Coin bags will also be thrown into the water that should be picked up. Teamwork is needed here as the more the paddle, the faster the raft will go. As long a single coin is earn, victory is certain.
