'''Precision''' [[List of weapons in Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|weapons]] are [[Luigi]]'s main guns he uses. They also look somewhat like Luigi's [[Poltergust 5000]].

== Description ==
'''Precision''' weapons resemble sniper rifles. Precision is exclusive to Luigi. The weapons have the farthest range which allows Luigi to hit enemies from afar.
