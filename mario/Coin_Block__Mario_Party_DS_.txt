The '''Coin Block''' is a type of [[Hex]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Description==
Like with any other Hex, a Coin Block can be obtained from a [[Hex Area]], although it is less common than the other Hexes. Whoever has a Coin Block can place it on a [[Blue Space]] or [[Red Space]] up to five spaces away from where they are.

If any character, including the user, lands on this Hex, a [[Coin Block|coin block]] appears. The character has ten seconds to hit the block as many times as possible, with each hit giving them one [[Coin]]. Once the time has expired, the Coins they received are added to their total.
