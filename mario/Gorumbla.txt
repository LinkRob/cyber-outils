'''Gorumbla''' is an antagonistic character in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. He lives on Rumbla Island, where he becomes a boss that must be fought by [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

==Profile==

===Personality===
As shown by many of his quotes, Gorumbla seems to be insecure over his status as an outcast and very destructive. His drive to destroy leads to him throwing a tantrum, which in turn triggers his boss fight. However, his actual goal is to be noticed, which he succeeds in later, even making amends with Chief Willma.

===Physical Appearance===
Gorumbla is a strong, sturdy monster who bears a purple-yellow colour scheme. He has grey eyebrows with a rocky appearance and wears a maroon hoodie and stony, spiked collars. He has black eyes with white pupils.

==History==
Gorumbla makes his debut after the Bros. reconnect [[Twistee Island]] and [[Raynforst Island]]. Mario and Luigi return to [[Rumbla Island]] to investigate trouble brewing at the village, whereupon [[Chief Willma]] intercepts their arrival and explains that the [[Guardarm]]s have exacerbated their destructive rampage. She then explains that the Bros. must head north of the village Gorumbla's house to confront him. After the Bros. lower a bridge leading to his section of the island and then unblock his door, they encounter Gorumbla stewing in his lair about his inability to get attention. He then declares that he will destroy all of Rumbla Island's art, after which he and the Bros. go head to head. Gorumbla is defeated, and Willma appears, knocking him into the stratosphere for causing trouble (which leads [[Snoutlet]] to crack a joke about her potentially being stronger than Mario and Luigi).

There is a side quest called "Gorumbla Gone Good" where Gorumbla tries to make amends for all the damage caused by his and the Guardarms' rampage by repairing said damage; this side quest becomes available after the second assault on [[Fortress Zokket]], as he becomes infected by the [[Bond (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Bond]]-breaking toxin [[Glohm]] by the time the Bros. get back to Rumbla. Mario and Luigi can return to Rumbla Island to carry a stone block to Gorumbla's house, and if they succeed, he hands over two [[Mushroom (item)|Ultra Mushrooms]] and a Max [[Syrup Jar]] as a reward.

Gorumbla becomes infected with the aforementioned [[Glohm]] later, specifically during the [[Reclusa World]] arc. With the enmity-generating substance in his body, Gorumbla is forcibly transformed into a stronger, albeit more feral, destructive, and unhinged monster. Chief Willma scolds him for wrecking the island again, only for the toxin to force him to retort that she never raised him (and neither, to him, did anyone else). Mario and Luigi, searching for Bonds to use as keys to enter the [[Soli-Tree]], later bump into Gorumbla, whom the toxin forces to fight them. Mario and Luigi subdue Gorumbla and cure him, however. Gorumbla, upon being cured, apologises to Willma for his actions. She mocks his apology, but is scolded by her [[Maykit and Billdit|children]] for it. Maykit and Billdit mention how Gorumbla served as one of their parental figures from their childhoods. Willma, regretful for her roughness, decides that Gorumbla can join her family, after which he, Willma, Maykit, and Billdit forge a Bond that the Bros. would later use as one of the keys to enter the Soli-Tree. Gorumbla later appears on [[Shipshape Island]], where he (in the back row, behind [[Princess Burnadette]] and [[Prince Chilliam]]), is one of those who cheer Mario and Luigi on during the invasion of the Soli-Tree.

Gorumbla later appears on Rumbla Island during the credits.

==Combat==
Gorumbla is fought twice in the game, and can be seen as a more advanced version of the [[Guardarm]] enemy, since he has a similar property of using his arms as shields to block attacks that would hit him, but the arms only protect the boss in one direction; any attack that hits his arms will be weakened. Straight forward means his head can be stomped on with a [[Jump]] attack, and if his arms are covering his head, he is vulnerable to a [[Hammer]] strike.

Gorumbla can attack by pounding the ground with his hands; the shockwaves generated by this will target a Brother depending on which of Gorumbla's hands is their creator; the right hand's shockwaves will target Mario while the ones generated by the left hand will target Luigi. Another attack has him swiping with his hands or stabbing his head forward; these must also be jumped over. A third of his attacks is a forward slide, which must in turn be jumped over. He can also drop colored blocks from the sky, which can be countered with hammers.

Gorumbla has a weakness: a statue to the north of his arena will hold a boulder. Luigi can use his Luigi Logic to dislodge it and send it at Gorumbla, who will catch it and throw it, so Mario will need to use his hammer to stop it, after which Luigi uses his own to launch the boulder at Gorumbla to stun him. If the Bros. are successful, Gorumbla will be stunned, signaled by his pupils becoming crosses, and his defense will lower until he recovers.

In his second fight, Gorumbla uses his old moves, but the [[Glohm]] in his system lets him create a spiky shield around his body; this shield can be destroyed by striking it with a Bros. Attack or a hammer, but [[Spiny (Enemy type)|cannot be jumped on]], and Gorumbla does not take damage immediately when his shield breaks.

The first time, he is a Level 7 enemy with 480 [[Heart Point|hit points]], an [[Attack|Attack Power]] of 26, a [[Defense]] of 5, and a [[Speed (Initiative)|Speed]] of 84. When defeated, he gives 200 [[Experience Point|Experience]], 130 [[Coin]]s, and a [[Mushroom (item)|Super Mushroom]]. In his second fight, Gorumbla is a Level 41 enemy with 7000 hit points, an Attack Power of 234, a Defense of 53, and a Speed of 636, and his defeat nets 16500 Experience, 1600 Coins, and a [[1-Up Mushroom]].

==Behind the Scenes==

===Etymology===
The name "Gorumbla" comes from the word "rumble," with the initial Gor- and final -a coming from "gorilla."

==Nomenclature==
Gorumbla is called "Guragon" in the Japanese dub, from ''guragura'' ("unsteady"; written ぐらぐら) referring to either the fact that he has an unsteady personality or has an unstable statue in the arena he is fought in. In Chinese, the monster is called "Yáohuàng Gāng" (written 摇晃刚), from ''yáohuàng'' ("to shake") and ''gāng'' ("hard") In Spanish, Gorumbla is called "Pedrolo," with his name coming from an informal word meaning "big rock" as well as incorporating the given Spanish name ''Pedro'' ("Peter"). His Portuguese name, ''Goriloscila'', comes from a pun on ''gorila'' ("gorrila") and the verb ''oscilar'' ("to oscilate"). Italian calls him Róccio, derived from ''róccia'' (a synonym of ''pedra'' meaning "stone"). In French, his name is Goroulis, from Japanese ''gorogoro'' (an onomatopoeia for rolling; written ごろごろ) and ''roulis'' ("rocking"). In Dutch, he is called "Khorossus," a pun on English "colossus" and Japanese ''horo'' ("helmet cape"; written 母衣). His German name, Schwankhard, comes from ''Schwanken'' ("to fluctuate") and English "hard." Korean translates his name as ''Heundeullagon'' (written 흔들래곤), a pun on ''heundeulda'' ("to shake"; written 흔들다) and a Korean transliteration of "dragon" (''deuraegon''; written 드래곤).
