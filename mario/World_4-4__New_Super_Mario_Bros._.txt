'''World 4-4''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Overview==
Alternatively, [[World 4-A (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 4-A]] can be used to bypass this level.

==Walkthrough==
This level features a lot of [[Wiggler]]s. Mario can complete the Red Ring by getting a boost from a Wiggler. The first [[Star Coin]] is found in a small cave near a Wiggler. Later, Mario needs to hit a block that contains a [[Vine]] to climb up it and continue. If the player goes left and [[Wall Jump]]s off the wall, they will find the second Star Coin. The last Star Coin can be obtained by sliding on a wall next to it, then [[Wall Jump]]ing to the small pipes. After going through the Yellow Pipe, Mario reaches the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].

There is a secret area in this level, reached by using a [[Mini Mushroom]] to go down the second small Warp Pipe from the left where there are four together, with a Wiggler on top. This secret area has a [[Mega Mushroom]], and a lot of pipes to smash, along with some coins to collect.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is located in an alcove below the level. Slide down to the alcove (but be careful not to fall down the pit) and grab the Star Coin while avoiding the Wiggler.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The next Star Coin is near yet another Wiggler, jump next to (to the left of) the Brick Block and you will hit an invisible block, jump next to that block and a [[Vine]] will emerge from it. Climb the Vine and jump to grab the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The final Star Coin is located on the Pipe-platforming section above the abyss. Slide down and grab the Star Coin and then quickly wall jump back onto the Pipes.
