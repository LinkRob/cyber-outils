'''Hildy''' is a witch-like character who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Love 'Em and Leave 'Em]]." She is an inhabitant of [[Rotundaland]] who was hired by [[Queen Rotunda]] to make the [[Passion Potion]] so that [[Prince Pompadour]] would fall in love with her.
