{{M&L Enemy|name = |caption = Sprite of a Dark Fawful Bomb.|image = [[File:DarkFawfulBomb.gif|thumb]]|location = [[Peach's Castle]]|hp = 316 (474)|pow = 312 (780)|def = 110 (165)|spd = 43 (65)|exp = 0|coins = 0 (0)|item = <nowiki>--</nowiki>|level = 32|fire = Normal|burn = Normal|dizzy = Normal|statdown = Normal|ko = Normal|game = Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story}}'''Dark Fawful Bombs''' are a subspecies of [[Bob-omb]] and a Fawfulized enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. [[Bowser]] can inhale the [[Dark Fawful Guy]]s that hold it, causing the Dark Fawful Bomb to fall and explode, dealing massive damage to enemies. If the player takes too long to inhale the Dark Fawful Guys as Bowser, they will drop the bomb on him.

== Trivia ==
* This is the only large enemy that can fight and does not drop a [[Blitty]].
* Dark Fawful Bombs look similar to [[Shroob-omb]]s in [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]].
