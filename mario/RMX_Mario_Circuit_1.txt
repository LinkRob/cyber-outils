'''RMX Mario Circuit 1''' is a remix version of Mario Circuit courses from ''Super Mario Kart''. appears as a track in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Layout ==
RMX Mario Circuit 1 is themed with [[Mario Circuit]]-retro styled courses. The driver can perform more tricks on the ramp and the brown platforms.

At the start of the course, the Goomba appears on top of the [[? Block]]s which does not affect the gameplay. The drivers starts with a heavy right-turn. Encountering a [[ramp]] on the left side of the track, the driver can drive onto the brown platforms and drop off. There are [[Item Block]]s on the straight path. The next turn, they encounter a middle ramp giving them access to the Item Blocks on the brown platforms. Making a 180 degree left turn, there are [[Goomba]]s on the track while there are [[Piranha Plant]]s on the top of the brown platforms not affecting the gameplay. Slightly turning left, there is another ramp on the right side allowing the driver to gain more coins. Turning 180 degrees to the right, the are two [[pipe]]s on the curve that appear as obstacles. The driver will encounter on the straight path, where there are Item Blocks on the ramp and the road. If the driver takes the ramp, they can perform tricks on the ramps and encounter the Piranha Plants that will slow down the driver. If the player does not take the ramp, they will encounter Goombas on the road. Making a right turn, they will encounter [[boost ramp]]s and return to the start.

RMX Mario Circuit 1 makes its appearance in tours depending on the cup and its place.
