The '''Mushroom Earth''' is '''an''' '''alternate Earth''', that encompasses the [[Mushroom Kingdom (DiC Cartoons)|Mushroom Kingdom]], it is also where [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] and many other residents live. The Mushroom Earth mainly has a connection to the real world, where [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|two]] [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|plumbers]] come to save it from [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and his army.
