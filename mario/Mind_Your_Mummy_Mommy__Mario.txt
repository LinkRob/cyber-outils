"'''Mind Your Mummy Mommy, Mario'''" is the first segment of the second episode of ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and the third one overall. 

==Plot==
[[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] assigns [[Hip Koopa|Hip]] and [[Hop Koopa|Hop]] to go into a pyramid in [[Desert Land]] and steal the golden sarcophagus of [[Prince Mushroomkhamen]]. Because of this, the prince's mother, [[Queen Mushroomkhamen]], angrily arises from her tomb. When [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] arrive, Queen Mushroomkhamen mistakes Mario for her son, because of his uncanny resemblance.
