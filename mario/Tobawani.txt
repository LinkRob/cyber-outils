'''Tobawani''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tobawani are green alligator-like creatures with a light green body and sharp teeth. They wear a red scuba tank and an orange snorkeling mask.

===Behavior===
Being crocodilians, Tobawani can be found in bodies of water. Tobawani are aggressive and attack [[Wario]] by jumping out of the water and biting him with their sharp teeth. If a Tobawani notices Wario in the water, it swims faster toward him.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
[[File:Tobawani WL4 screenshot.png|thumb|left|A Tobawani jumping out of the water of Monsoon Jungle in ''Wario Land 4'']]
Tobawani only appears in one [[level]], [[Monsoon Jungle]]. Wario can defeat a Tobawani by either [[Dash Attack|dashing]] into its belly as the Tobawani makes its way back into the water or by using a [[Super Smash Attack]] from above. Either method can only be used as the Tobawani falls back into the water after jumping out.

In Normal mode, only one Tobawani is encountered in the lakes, but more can be found in Hard mode and even more in S-Hard mode.
