'''''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''''' is a [[Nintendo Switch]] game. It is a [[reissue|remake]] of the [[Game Boy Advance]] game ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'', and it was released on February 16, 2024. The game was first announced on the [[Nintendo Direct]] of September 14, 2023. The original version was later released on [[Game Boy Advance - Nintendo Classics]] for the [[Nintendo Switch]] and [[Nintendo Switch 2]] on March 10, 2026.

Although [[Charles Martinet]] retired from voicing [[Mario]] since ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', archival voice clips of his voice work as Mario appear to be repurposed here, even some voice clips returning from the original game in an uncompressed format. Contrarily, [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] instead uses voice clips by his current voice actor, [[Takashi Nagasako]], instead of reusing [[Grant Kirkhope]]'s voice clips from ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' like in the original ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''.

== Differences ==
Unlike the original version's pre-rendered visuals, the remake adopts a standard modern artstyle, taking many cues from ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its appearance as a theme in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' in particular. The ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' remake introduces a redesigned logo for the {{series|Donkey Kong}}.

*The cutscenes are now fully animated, instead of swapping between different static frames.
*The game supports multiplayer, where player 1 can only play as Mario and player 2 can only play as [[Toad (character)|Toad]].
*The game uses three-dimensional models over pre-rendered sprites.
*[[Tane Pakkun]] are redesigned into mechanical [[Venus Fire Trap]]s.
*The [[Ninji (toy)|toy Ninjis]] are now black with red buttons, giving them a closer resemblance to [[Ninji]]s themselves.
*[[Brickman|Brickmen]] receive a pair of movable bricks over their eyes acting as expressive eyebrows.
*Casual Mode has been added, with bubbles acting as additional hit points and an infinite time limit.
*A Time Attack mode has been added.
*The locations of the red and yellow platforms in Level 1-DK have been swapped.
*The bonus Present minigames have been replaced with various new bonus stages (though the music is retained and rearranged. In these stages, the player must catch a floating key that opens a treasure box before the time runs out, earning five 1-Ups in the process.
*Scoring has been removed.
*Falls are no longer lethal, and only formerly lethal falls now stun Mario and Toad.
*The right stick can be used to control the camera in large-scaled levels.
*Fortunately, Retrying a level or exiting a level that has not been cleared yet no longer costs the player a life.
*Clearing the first level in a world unlocks every remaining level in it except for the Mini-Mario levels and Donkey Kong boss fights.
*The conveyor belts and Donkey Kong are placed differently in Level 6-DK and Level 6-DK+.
*Additional blue platforms were added to Level 1-DK+.
*There are extra platforms added to Level 3-DK+.
*Hammers reappear after using them.
*Mario and Toad can now jump on Birds.
