The '''Dictionary''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario (game)|Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
The Dictionary belongs to [[Russ T.]] of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] who often referred to it for knowledge. During the events of Chapter 4, a [[Shy Guy]] stole the Dictionary and took it to [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]. Without his Dictionary, Russ T. was not able to give [[Mario]] any helpful tips for his journey (or decipher the [[Mystery Note]]). After returning the Dictionary to Russ T., he rewards Mario with a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]].
