'''Blimp Yoshi''' is a power up in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It can be accessed by [[Yoshi]] eating the [[Blimp Fruit]].

== Description ==
Yoshi must grab a Blimp Fruit to transform into Blimp Yoshi. It allows Yoshi to vertically run over small distances much like the [[Bee Mushroom]] from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. The Blimp Fruit is first accessible in the [[Tall Trunk Galaxy]] in the [[World 3 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|third world]]. It is also accessible in the [[Flash Black Galaxy]].
