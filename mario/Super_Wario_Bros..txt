'''Super Wario Bros.''' is a [[microgame]] which first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', as the sixth microgame within the [[Intro Games]] set. It makes a reappearance in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' under the [[Mash League]] set of microgames.

The title Super Wario Bros. is a pun on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', but with [[Mario]] being swapped out for [[Wario]], whom the player controls in the microgame. The microgame has 8-bit graphics, similar to ''Super Mario Bros.'', but they are not reused from the game. Furthermore, ''WarioWare Gold''{{'}}s iteration of Super Wario Bros. even changes the graphics from ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''.

In both of its appearances, Super Wario Bros. is under Wario's set of microgames instead of [[9-Volt]], whose microgames are generally based on retro games.

==Gameplay==
The object is for [[Wario]] to stomp on all the [[Goomba]]s within a few seconds, an objective shared with the [[Super Mario Bros. (WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!)|Super Mario Bros.]] microgame. Unlike ''Super Mario Bros.'' and the microgame based on it, [[jump]]ing is done automatically, and the only controls is left or right on the D-Pad, which moves Wario in the respective direction.

In the first-level difficulty, there is only one Goomba to stomp. The second-level and third-level difficulties feature two and three Goombas to stomp on, respectively. Additionally, each difficulty level has Wario and the Goombas move faster than in the previous.
