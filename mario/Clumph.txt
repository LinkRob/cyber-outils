'''Clumphs''' are a caveman-like enemy which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Battle ==
Clumphs do not appear in the overworld, only in battle alongside [[Glurp]]s, [[Ice Snifit]]s, and [[Scaratroopea]]s within [[Joke's End]]. Clumphs attack by smashing the ground with their large club, creating a shockwave that [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must [[jump]] over. A Clumph can also increase its attack power by burping.
