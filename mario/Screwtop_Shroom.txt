'''Screwtop Shrooms''' are an object which first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Description==
A Screwtop Shroom is a type of [[Mushroom Platform]] with a purple cap and a screw on top of it. They are featured in sets, and [[Mario]] or another playable character can spin on a Screwtop Shroom's screw to adjust the height of the other Screwtop Shrooms. If the player's character is not spinning the screw, a Screwtop Shroom slowly returns in the direction they came.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
Only four Screwtop Shrooms make an appearance, specifically in [[World 1-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1-5]] just before the [[Checkpoint Flag]].

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' / ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
Screwtop Shrooms function the same as before. They only appear in one level, [[Rising Tides of Lava]], but are present throughout. Screwtop Shrooms are among the objects to not appears in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
