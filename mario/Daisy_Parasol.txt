'''Daisy Parasol''' is [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]'s up special in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

== Description ==
Daisy Parasol functions the same as [[Peach Parasol (move)|Peach Parasol]]. It acts as a recovery tool if Daisy falls off the stage and a tool to poke her opponents above her. When the parasol activates, Daisy falls slowly. She can control her drift to where she falls or close her parasol to fall faster.

== In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' ==
With Daisy's playable debut in ''Ultimate'' as an Echo Fighter, Daisy Parasol appears as her up special. It functions the same as [[Peach Parasol (move)|Peach Parasol]] with the visual difference of Daisy's parasol color scheme.
