'''Dry Dry Railroad''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Dry Dry Railroad is located south of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and is where [[Mario]] can use the train, [[K64]]. However, during the first visit, there is a cracked rock on the train tracks and the ticket master tells Mario that the train cannot be used until the rock is removed. Mario can destroy the rock with [[Bombette]] after the events of Chapter 1 and the ticket master allows Mario free rides on the K64 anytime. Mario can then head to [[Mt. Rugged]] to continue his quest.
