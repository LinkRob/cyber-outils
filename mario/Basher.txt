A '''Basher '''is an unused ghost in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|Luigi's Mansion]]''. A basher would follow behind [[Luigi]] until close enough. Once close, the ghost would let out a puff of smoke. The ghost would then appear with text saying "BAAAAAAAAHH!" Luigi would scream, jump back, and crab walk backwards uncontrollably, cutting his HP to 50, which would then rise up.
