==Features==
This level takes place in a cave. Captain Toad starts near a mine cart in which he must get on. He can throw Turnips while in the cart to get Super Gems, break POW Blocks, and defeat Para-Biddybuds.

==Super Gems==
*'''Super Gem 1''': Once you drop down to the cart, turn around to find the Super Gem behind the direction the cart's going.
*'''Super Gem 2''': After the mine cart rolls down, throw the Turnip at the fifth POW Block and reveal a diamond. Throw another turnip to get it.
*'''Super Gem 3''': Right after the second Super Gem, aim at the Super Gem on the moving pole of blocks and hit it with a turnip.
