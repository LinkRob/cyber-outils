'''Pauline''' is a cameo character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. She is the Mayor of New York City, residing in [[Brooklyn]] and reported on in the news.<ref name="mayor">''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' Television in the [[Punch-Out Pizzeria]] "Mayor Pauline Re-elected in Landslide Victory"</ref>

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Pauline's appearance strongly resembles her video game counterpart in her mayor attire and has a more modern look, without her 1940s styled hat from ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

=== Personality traits ===
In the movie, Pauline is calm and helpful.

== Biography ==
Pauline was first mentioned on the news, when she won another election as mayor in a landslide victory.

Mayor Pauline is seen in a news report about a sewage leak in Brooklyn, where she explains the repair crew are doing everything they can to contain it. It is through this broadcast that the Mario Bros. rush to the sewers to try and fix it themselves.

Pauline later watches the battle between Mario and Luigi against Bowser in Brooklyn offscreen. Revealed through the SMB Plumbing website after the events of the Mario brothers defeating Bowser, Pauline would award the two the Key to the City in honor of their heroism.
