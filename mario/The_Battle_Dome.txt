'''The Battle Dome''' is a soccer field in the {{series|Mario Strikers}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. The Battle Dome is located in [[Bowser's Castle]], and it is the special field of the [[Super Star Cup]].
