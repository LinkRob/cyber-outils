The '''Turtle Tea''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. It costs 20 [[coin]]s, and it is bought at a coffee shop in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario: The Origami King)|Toad Town]].

==Description==
The Turtle Tea decreases [[Mario]]'s running speed for 10 seconds. It has a counterpart named the [[Rabbit Espresso]].
