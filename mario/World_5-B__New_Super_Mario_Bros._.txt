'''World 5-B '''​is the second bonus level of [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]], in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Layout==
This level is an icy level that contains a few [[Snailicorn]]s. To begin this level, Mario needs to move while balancing himself on the slippery ice. Mario can get the first [[Star Coin]] by throwing a shell at it through a small gap. The second Star Coin can be obtained by pressing a [[P Switch]] and by jumping over gaps to get to it. Soon, the player should see a Snailicorn. Mario should go past it to find the third Star Coin. To get a high spot on the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]], Mario should [[Wall Jump]] to a higher platform.

==Secret Exit==
To access the secret exit, the player must be [[Shell Mario]]. When Mario has to Wall Jump to a higher platform to get a higher spot on the flagpole, he should continue to Wall Jump even higher, going above the screen. Mario should go to the left to find a pipe with Brick Blocks in front of it. Shell Mario must break the blocks to enter the pipe and to find the [[Goal Pole (secret)|red flag]]. This opens a pipe that takes Mario to [[World 5-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5-Castle]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''' - Mario should throw a shell through the small spot and to the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''' - Mario should press a [[P Switch]] and jump over gaps to get it.
*'''Star Coin 3''' - Mario should get past a Snailicorn so that he can get it.
