'''Sleepy Stomp''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
The Sleepy Stomp can be worn by using one [[Badge Point|BP]]. While it is being used, [[Mario]] can unleash a [[boots|jump]] attack capable of putting his foes to [[sleep]] if used successfully.

Mario can find the Sleepy Stomp in the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] in [[Rogueport]], where he can purchase it for 75 [[coin]]s. The Sleepy Stomp can also be obtained in [[Pit of 100 Trials]], specifically on the tenth floor.
