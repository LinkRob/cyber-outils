'''Toad Tote''' is one of the three [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It can be unlocked before completing [[Fury Volcano 4-1]].

== Gameplay ==
The object is to drag [[Toad (character)|Toad]] through a fiery maze using the [[stylus]]. There are ten levels of increasing difficulty, with the rewards improving as the player progresses. All of the objects in Toad Tote are fire-elemental, including fire walls, [[Fire Bar]]s, and [[flamethrower]]s. If either [[Time limit|time runs out]] or Toad touches the fire or spikes, the player loses.
