'''Holly Mackerel''' is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[The Ugly Mermaid]]." She is a fish-like mermaid who is the princess of [[Mertropolis]] and the daughter of [[King Mackerel]]. Her name is a pun on the phrase "holy mackerel."

==Episode appearances==
In "The Ugly Mermaid," Holly saved [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] from nearly drowning underwater. She then fell in love with Mario due to him wearing the [[Frog Suit]].
