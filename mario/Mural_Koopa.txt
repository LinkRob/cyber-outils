==Appearance==

Mural Koopas look very differently from regular [[Koopa Troopa]]s; instead being more similar to [[Shellcreeper]]s in the way that they are quadrupedal instead of bipedal like most Koopas. They appear brown in color and their shells are darker green in color. The also have humanoid arms and legs.

==History==

Like Mural Goombas, only a few Mural Koopas appear in one room of [[Drybake Stadium]]. They attack Mario after he picks up the Stone Entry scrap. In battle, they only attack Mario by ramming into him. Unlike with regular Koopas, Mural Koopas don't hide into their shells after they are attacked.
