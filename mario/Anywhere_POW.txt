The '''Anywhere POW''' is a [[Special Item]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. It is a red [[POW Block]] that turns all enemies onscreen into [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]]. Like other Special Items, the Anywhere POW is earned as a prize from [[Bonus Challenge]]s or by winning a [[Mini Game Battle]].
