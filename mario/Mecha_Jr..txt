'''Mecha Jr.''' is the last midboss in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== Appearance and Mechanisms ==
Mecha Jr. is just [[Bowser Jr.]] in a mecha suit that he supposedly got for his birthday. He keeps [[Spawny]] in a hatch in the mecha. He uses his hammers and giant feet to crush the Allies. He also uses a Sentry. The Mecha has a design that appears like a facial expression. Bowser Jr sits in his [[Clown Car]] on top of the mecha.

== Battle Information ==
Mecha Jr. has 2,000 HP, and two weapons. The first one is a hammer in the shape of a cow. It does 240 damage. The second is a sentry with 300 HP. It does 135 damage when a hero gets too close to it. Mecha Jr. can also use [[Rabbid Mario|Rabbid Mario's]] Magnet Dance, drawing heroes close to him.{{Stub}}
