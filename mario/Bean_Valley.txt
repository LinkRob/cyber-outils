|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Bean Valley''' is an area that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
Bean Valley actually has two parts to it:
*A brown forest that contains mostly [[Piranha Plant]]s
*A cloudy area that leads up to [[Nimbus Land]].

==History==
Mario and his team venture into Bean Valley to continue on their quest. Eventually, they meet a [[Shy Away]] that starts to water many Piranha Plants. Mario and his team defeats them and eventually fight a much tougher Piranha Plant named [[Megasmilax]]. The oversized Piranha Plant is defeated and Mario heads up into the clouds and reach Nimbus Land.

===Grate Guy's Casino Side-quest===
Mario can actually get into [[Grate Guy]]'s [[Grate Guy's Casino|Casino]] through Bean Valley. He must jump down a [[Warp Pipe]], defeat a [[Chain Chomp]], and jump three times to make a platform appear and head into the casino. It is run by Grate Guy after splitting up from [[Knife Guy]]. Note, Mario must have the [[Bright Card]] in order to enter and Mario can earn one by playing Knife Guy's mini-game back in [[Booster Tower]].

==Trivia==
*Mario defeating the Megasmilax and heading up into the clouds is a parody of ''Jack and the Beanstalk''.
*This is similar to how Mario gets to [[Cloudy Climb]] to fight Huff N'Puff in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
