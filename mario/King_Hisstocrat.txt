'''King Hisstocrat''' is a gigantic snake-like creature who appears as the boss of [[World 3 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 3]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|Enhanced Version on the Nintendo Switch]]. He is fought on the levels [[A Banquet with Hisstocrat]] and [[Boss Blitz]], is the second/final boss of that world and is overall the fourth boss of the game.

==Physical description==
King Hisstocrat appears as a giant purple snake-like creature that wears a red cape and a crown. Because of this, he is considered royalty. King Hisstocrat has diamonds on his belly scales. His eyes are yellow with horizontal black pupils; however, stomping on his head causes him to get black dots for pupils, implying that his horizontal pupils are actually him squinting his eyes. He is in the ground so the player never sees the bottom side of him.

== History ==

=== In ''Super Mario 3D World'' ===
King Hisstocrat makes his debut in [[Super Mario 3D World|''Super Mario 3D World'']], where he is fought in the level [[A Banquet with Hisstocrat]], which must be beaten after leaving [[World 3 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 3]] in order to unlock [[World 4 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 4]]. When [[Mario (character)|Mario]] makes his way to the arena, King Hisstocrat bursts out of the ground, throwing several boulders in his wake. When defeated, King Hisstocrat disappears, dropping a [[Green Star]] which can be scooped up while a [[Warp Pipe]] appears leading to the [[Goal Pole]], allowing the course to be completed to grant access to World 4.

Although his name appears in the title of the level [[Hisstocrat Returns]], King Hisstocrat is absent; [[Queen Hisstocrat]] appears instead, and she has a similar attack pattern, albeit with the ability to shoot fire, and the boulders tossed leave behind lava puddles that cool after a ew seconds. They both appear in the [[Boss Blitz]] stage, where they fight alongside each other.

=== In ''Mario Tennis Fever'' ===
King Hisstocrat makes a return in [[Mario Tennis Fever|''Mario Tennis Fever'']], where he is encountered as a boss in the game's Adventure Mode, where he blocks [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], and the [[Mushroom Retainer|Mushroom Retainers]] from continuing through the final segment of the [[Foggy Swamp]] level. After he is defeated, he allows the group to leave.

== Battle ==

=== ''Super Mario 3D World'' ===
When fought in [[Super Mario 3D World|''Super Mario 3D World'']], King Hisstocrat battles alongside his smaller snake minions that can be used by Mario and the others to jump up to the large boss and attack him. King Hisstocrat himself mainly attacks by digging into the ground and send fiery boulders at the player(s). He can also have his snake minions attack the player(s) directly. The [[Super Bell]] is useful here as it can be used to climb up easily and jump onto King Hisstocrat's head. After three hits, King Hisstocrat and his minions fall.

==== Attacks ====
*'''Rock Throw''': King Hisstocrat will emerge from the ground while many rocks are thrown in the air that will fall onto the player. This is his opening attack. In [[A Banquet with Hisstocrat]], the rocks just shatter upon hitting the ground, leaving nothing in their wake; however, in [[Boss Blitz]], they leave behind fiery puddles that cool after a few seconds.
*'''Snake Hiss''': King Hisstocrat will make a music hiss and call multiple snakes to assist him in his battle, however he is only helping the player to attack him.
*'''Fire Breath''': This is only used in Boss Blitz, taken from [[Queen Hisstocrat]]. Hisstocrat blows a [[Fireball]] from his mouth. If he hits a plate platform, the platform shatters.

=== ''Mario Tennis Fever'' ===
In ''[[Mario Tennis Fever]]'', King Hisstocrat attacks by shooting fireballs from his mouth, and Mario has to dodge the fireballs, which drop onto random spots on the raft. Two [[Stone Spike|Stone Spikes]] provide ammunition by throwing spiked boulders onto the raft, and [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can use their rackets to send the balls at King Hisstocrat to deal damage to him. After enough damage, King Hisstocrat shoves the platform away, shooting waves of fireballs across the raft as the Mushroom Retainers row it back to him. Once the raft gets back in front of him, Hisstocrat resumes his attack pattern from before. His minions will also emerge from the swamp, protecting King Hisstocrat; however, if a spiked boulder strikes their head, the minions flee. Once his health gets low, King Hisstocrat pushes the raft again, shooting denser waves of fireballs. When the raft gets back in front of him, King Hisstocrat and his minions resume their behaviour from the second phase. King Hisstocrat also summons more of his minions behind the Stone Spikes, and these Hisstocrats shoot their own fireballs, which travel across the field. Mario and Luigi can stun them by hitting them with spiked boulders. Upon having his health depleted, King Hisstocrat vanishes, allowing departure from the Foggy Swamp.

== Behind the Scenes ==
King Hisstocrat is based on a snake, hence his name, which originates from "aristocrat" (referencing how he is royalty) and "hiss" (the sound that snakes make).
