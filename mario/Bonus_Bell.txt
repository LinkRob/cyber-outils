'''Bonus Bells''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Description==
A Bonus Bell hangs at the top of a [[Goal (Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins)|goal]], and reaching it requires the use of [[platform]]s. Hitting a Bonus Bell before exiting the level via the goal is optional, but when [[Mario]] rings this bell, he plays a bonus game where he can get power-ups or [[Extra life|extra lives]] before leaving the stage.

Overall, Bonus Bells act as the successor to the higher door of a [[Goal (Super Mario Land)|goal]] from ''[[Super Mario Land]]''.
