'''Metroid''' is a [[microgame]] featured in the [[Retro Action]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. It is based on and titled after the first ''[[w:c:metroid:Metroid (game)|Metroid]]'' game, as with several other ''Metroid''-themed microgames in the ''[[WarioWare]]'' series. This microgame replaces [[Famicom Tantei Club 2]] from the Japanese version, and is therefore exclusive to releases outside of Japan.

==Layout==
This microgame appears to be set in [[w:c:metroid:Tourian|Tourian]], two rooms before [[Mother Brain]]'s chamber. The graphics have a very dark green-like tint unlike the original ''Metroid''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to use the [[Stylus]] to drag the [[Metroid]]s away from [[Samus Aran]], who is either wearing the [[Power Suit]] or is [[Zero Suit Samus|suitless]]. The first difficulty level has only one Metroid while the other two difficulty levels feature two and three Metroids, respectively. A minimum of twenty points must be attained to complete the microgame successfully.
