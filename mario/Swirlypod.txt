'''Swirlypods''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Swirlypods are snails with light green skin, big eyes, a dark pink shell with swirls on the left and right sides each, and antennae that are dark green at both the tip and the bottom.

Swirlypods have larger variants, [[Big Swirlypod|Hefty Swirlypod]]s, which have little difference than them other than being a larger size, and which can only be picked up by [[Elephant Mario|Elephant Mario.]]

===Behavior===
Though they look vastly different than regular snails, Swirlypods still act similar to them, by moving slowly on the ground, walls, and other surfaces. If a Swirlypod is stomped, it launches out of its shell, much like how a stomped [[Koopa Troopa]] becomes a [[Beach Koopa]]. The Swirlypod becomes dizzy upon being launched out, but after a while, it attempts to re-enter its shell.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''==
Swirlypods are first encountered in [[Bulrush Coming Through!]], but they also appear in other levels such as [[Swamp Pipe Crawl]]. Swirlypods are prey of [[Maw-Maw]]s, and if one is eaten, the Maw-Maw spits its shell back out, which the player's character can then use in a very similar manner to a [[Koopa Shell]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Their name is a portmanteau of "swirl" and "gastropod," a classification of snails and slugs.

In Japanese, Swirlypods are generically referred to as "snails."
