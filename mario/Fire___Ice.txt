'''Fire & Ice''' is a Bros. Move from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It makes its debut appearance on [[Heatfreeze Island]] when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] return there to reconnect it after [[Bowser]] severed it from the [[Uni-Tree]] during his sortie on [[Shipshape Island]]. There, they meet the [[Heatfreeze Guardians|island's guardians]], who bestow a [[Fire Flower]] and an [[Ice Flower]], and are taught how to use the move.

==How to Use Fire & Ice==
To select the move, the player must flick the right control stick to the left. When this happens, Mario and Luigi use the Fire and Ice Flowers respectively to don their respective elemental suits, holding their respective suits' projectiles in their hands. If the player presses X, Mario will throw a [[Fireball]], while Luigi throws an [[Ice Ball]] in response to pressing Y. Striking an enemy with the projectile will result in a [[First Strike]]; if a fireball caused the First Strike, the enemy may be [[Burn|set on fire]], while using an Ice Ball to inflict the First Strike may [[Frozen|freeze the enemy]].

==Bro Bomb==
Bro Bombs are an upgrade to the Fire & Ice move, and make their debut appearance on [[Conductor Island]]; there, the [[Great Conductor]] gives the Bros. this technique when the duo find a boulder obstructing them. This is executed by holding either the X or Y button while using Fire & Ice, and causes a blob of energy to appear in front of them. Releasing the button will cause the blob to surge forward, exploding upon contact with a surface, object, or enemy. Mario and Luigi can use this to destroy boulders that can withstand [[Hammer]] strikes, bust down the front door of [[Fortress Zokket]] (and later the [[Soli-Tree]]) as well as cause a [[First Strike]] on an enemy. This is also used during the first phase of [[Reclusa]]'s battle when it charges up with fire or ice when caught up with and its aura is successfully extinguished by the opposite element; a successful Bro Bomb will stun Reclusa and deal damage. Mario and Luigi also use the Bro Bomb to block a dark energy blob that Weeping Reclusa launches as they prepare to enter the monster's maw.
