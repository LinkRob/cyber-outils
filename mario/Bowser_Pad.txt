The '''Bowser Pad''', also referred to as '''Bowser's Bachelor Pad''', is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Bowser Pad is located in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], but it is only accessible through the [[Pipe House (Mario Party Advance)|Pipe House]]. Bowser Pad is a luxury home of [[Bowser]]'s. In the background, Bowser Pad is shown to have several pink and purple decorations, including a giant throne, a flower vase (which appears to have sinister, green eyes), and a picture frame on a small ledge. The upper part of the backdrop is dark purple, but the lower part is red.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Bowser Pad is a location visited in one of Bowser's quests, [[Bestest Buds]]. It involves giving Bowser a gift that he would enjoy the most from a selection of a [[Ring (Mario Party Advance)|Ring]], a [[Bracelet]], and a [[Necklace (Mario Party Advance)|Necklace]], the former of which is the correct gift. After the player's character purchases the Ring from [[Junk]] and gives it to Bowser and wins at the [[Peek-n-Sneak]] minigame, Bowser awards the [[Shroom Bloom]] [[Gaddget]] to the player.
