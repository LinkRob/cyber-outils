|name=Big Iron Jump (Battle Card)}}'''Big Iron Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Iron Jump is the big iron version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a big blue-gray boot with an blue/cyan checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 20/70 coins

Sell Price: 10/35 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Iron Jump is first seen in [[Plum Park]], but can also found in [[Cobalt Base]], [[Mustard Café]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
