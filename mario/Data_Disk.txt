The '''Data Disk''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Date Disk is an orange floppy disk used by [[Sir Grodus]] to store important information in. [[TEC-XX|TEC]], wanting to know more of Grodus' plans, sends [[Princess Peach]] to retrieve it during the intermission of Chapter 5. Peach much head into Grodus' room while invisible (to avoid Grodus' goons) and insert the Date Disk into Grodus' computer allowing TEC to freely view the information. Peach then puts the Data Disk back onto the shelf to make it appear that it was never been used.

The information on the Data Disk is most likely about the [[Shadow Queen|1,000 year old demon]] behind the [[Thousand-Year Door]] where Grodus plans to use Princess Peach as a vessel for the demon.
