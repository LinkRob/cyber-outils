|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Mega Shock''' is a special move used by [[Watt]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Watt can learn this move once she is upgraded to the [[Ultra Rank]] and it requires  [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Usage==

To use Mega Shock, the player must press the {{button|n64|a}} and {{button|n64|b}} buttons at the same time repeatedly to fill up the gauge. After the gauge is filled, Watt will shock every enemy on the screen and may paralyze them. However, this may not work all of the time and has no effects on electric-based enemies such as [[Ruff Puff]]s. Mega Shock is basically a stronger version of [[Power Shock]].
