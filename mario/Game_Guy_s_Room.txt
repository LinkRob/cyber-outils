'''Game Guy's Room''' is a feature that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. The room is located in Mini-game Mode where, at first, the door leading to it is locked. The only way to access Game Guy's Room is to beat [[Story Mode]] with any character on any difficulty for the first time. The room is hosted by [[Game Guy]] himself.

==Rules==
For Game Guy's Room, the player has to play a certain Game Guy Mini-game in order to earn coins. The player can bet as many coins as they are sure they are going to win the mini-game. When the player reaches 1000 coins, then a special, single player version of [[Mario's Puzzle Party]] is unlocked. The player can go into Game Guy's Room anytime, if they feel like playing a Game Guy Mini-game.
