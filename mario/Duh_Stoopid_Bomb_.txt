"'''Duh Stoopid Bomb!'''" is a comic released as part of [[Valiant Comics]]'s ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]''.

==Plot==
[[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], and the [[Mushroom King]] discuss matters at [[Princess Peach's Castle|Mushroom Castle]] when [[Wooster]] arrives with news from the Fungus Bureau of Investigation. [[Luigi]] believes that [[Toad]] (who [[Princess Peach]] reveals had infiltrated [[King Bowser]]'s [[Bowser Castle|Castle]], had sent a message.

Meanwhile, [[Toad]], disguised as a [[Shy Guy]], delivers some Koopa-Kola to the Koopa King. Bowser and a [[Snifit]] display a [[Stupid Bomb]], which is a bomb that releases neurotoxic gases. Toad accidentally blows his own cover, but manages to get one of the bombs and escape. Bowser mistakes the costume on the floor of his castle as an actual Shy Guy, however.

Toad bursts into the room only to drop his new find, and the Mario brothers are dopefied by the neurotoxin. Mario and Luigi ask if they can take on the Koopa King's army on their own, and the king allows them. The Mario brothers make their way to Bowser Castle, where stupified Shy Guys mistaken them for party guests and give them some bombs, allowing the bros. to make their getaway.

Bowser is enraged at the weapon waste, but his anger shifts to glee when the recovering Snifit explains that the bros. might set off the bombs in the land, causing idiocy to infect everyone. Bowser then shows his attempts to counteract the Stupid Bomb. This countermeasure is called the "Smart Bomb". The Snifit grabs one but it bursts in his face, making him super intelligent and leading to him overthrowing Bowser.

Mario and Luigi eventually make their way back but the bombs explode, restarting the cycle, while the Snifit tries to re-train the Shy Guy army, but fails.
