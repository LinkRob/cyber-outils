'''Baby Dragoneels''' are smaller purple variants of the [[Dragoneel]] which appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. They can be found in certain underwater coves, and just like their adult counterparts, Baby Dragoneels chase after [[Mario]] or another character if they are nearby. Baby Dragoneels are typically invulnerable to most attacks, except for a [[Starman]].

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. U / New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''==
Baby Dragoneels appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', but are one of few enemies in these games to not appear in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' as well.

Baby Dragoneels only appear in one level, [[Dragoneel's Undersea Grotto]], where they guard the final [[Star Coin]]. Two Baby Dragoneels arrive once the Star Coin is obtained and slowly chase after [[Mario]], being babies, they move slower than a regular Dragoneel. The only way to defeat a Baby Dragoneel is with a [[Starman]], they can be hit on the head with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] to temporarily stun them but hitting them anywhere else along the body will only make them speed up.
