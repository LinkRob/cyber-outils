"'''DK Island Swing'''" (titled "'''Jungle Level'''" in Japanese soundtracks) is a recurring musical theme most commonly heard within the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}. It is first heard in the first level of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', [[Jungle Hijinxs (Donkey Kong Country)|Jungle Hijinxs]], and was composed by David Wise. The theme has since been synonymous with the franchise as well as [[Donkey Kong]] himself.

The theme consists of three distinct portions, beginning with a jungle ambience and a drum beat, followed by the main, upbeat melody, and finally a calmer, slower-tempo melody before looping back to the beginning.
