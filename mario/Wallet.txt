A '''Wallet''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==History==
The Wallet was stolen from a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] by [[Croco]] during an early part of [[Mario]]'s adventure. [[Mario]] and [[Mallow]] eventually engage and defeat Croco, who drops Mallow's [[Frog Coin]] along with the Toad's Wallet. The Wallet can then be returned to the Toad, who gratefully rewards Mario a [[Flower Tab]]. However, the Wallet can also be sold in any [[Item Shop]] for 123 [[coin]]s, but doing this will cause the Toad upset at Mario for the remainder of the game.
