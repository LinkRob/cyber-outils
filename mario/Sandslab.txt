'''Sandslabs''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sandslabs are composed of sand and have a pair of eyes. They resemble a [[Sand Geyser]].

===Behavior===
A Sandslab stands in the way of a [[! Block]] containing a [[Toad (species)|Toad]].

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Sandslabs are only encountered in [[Wavy Beach]], and they first appear in [[Wavy Beach 5-1]]. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can only defeat a Sandslab from the effects of the [[Gloom]] [[vibe]]. Sandslabs are among the enemies to not have a [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry.
