The '''Red B Dasher''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Red B Dasher resembles the [[B Dasher]], notably its design from this game. Par its name, the colors of the body are inverted, with the body being red, and the stripe being white. The engine block and exhaust pipes are colored dark blue, with light yellow stripes. Like the B Dasher, it uses the [[Pipe Frame]]'s tires from this game. The kart's coloration heavily resembles [[Mario]]'s color scheme.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Red B Dasher was available during the [[London Tour]]. The kart is classified as a High-End kart. It increases the duration of [[Rocket Start]] boosts and earns extra points for performing them.
