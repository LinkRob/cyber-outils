The '''Heart Slacks''' is a clothing item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It can only be equipped by [[Mario]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
The Heart Slacks can be bought at the clothes shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 520 [[coin]]s, and equipping it to Mario increases his [[Defense]] by 60. In the Japanese version, the Heart Slacks is at a slightly more expensive price of 580 coins, and instead of increasing Defense, the Heart Slacks in this version increases [[HP]] by 80. In both the international and Japanese versions, the Heart Slacks also grants the [[DEF-Up]] special effect.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''==
The Heart Slacks is sold at a more expensive price of 950 coins, and can be sold for 238 coins. It becomes available at Beanbean Castle Town's clothes store after defeating [[Popple]] and [[Birdo]] in a boss fight. Despite its higher price, the Heart Slacks retains the same rating increase from the original's Japanese version, which is increasing Mario's HP by 80. The Heart Slacks instead has [[One-Turn Recover]] as its special effect.
