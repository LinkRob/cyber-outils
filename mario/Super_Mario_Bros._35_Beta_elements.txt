This article pertains to the beta elements of [[Super Mario Bros. 35|''Super Mario Bros. 35'']].

== Unused Music Intros ==
While the Super Mario Bros. has an intro level for whenever the player is about to enter an underground or underwater level, the developers decided to scrap this whole level intro sequence. However, the music that was being used for those intro levels is still inside the game's data. What's more interesting is that the intro level music is saved in the '''sound.fspj''' file (a file used for mainly sound effects), rather than an audio stream alongside with the other background music. It even has a faster version for the last 5 players remaining. However, according to the filename, it would've probably have been used for whenever a match is about to get started.
