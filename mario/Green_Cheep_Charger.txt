The '''Green Cheep Charger''' is a [[kart]] appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
It is a variation of the [[Cheep Charger]]. Hence its name, its design is based of [[Deep Cheep]]s. The kart still uses the [[Sponge]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Green Cheep Charger is a Normal kart. It was first made available in the Daily Selects during the [[Winter Tour (2019)]]. It increases the duration of [[Tricks|Jump Boosts]] and the points earned for performing them.
