}}A '''SurpriseMeow''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
SurpriseMeows are large, cat robots created by [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]] that are found in certain rooms of [[Fort Francis]]. When a SurpriseMeow spots an intruder, it fire a [[Meowbomb]] at them. SurpriseMeows cannot be defeated by normal means so the only way to destroy them is to use [[Thoreau]] to grab a Meowbomb and toss it into the SurpriseMeow's mouth cannon.
