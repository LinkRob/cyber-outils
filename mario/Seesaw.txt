'''Seesaws''' are a type of object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', following shortly after the introduction of the log seesaws named [[teeter-totter]]s in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

== Description==
[[File:SM64 Bowser in the Dark World Seesaw.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario on a seesaw in [[Bowser in the Dark World]] in ''Super Mario 64'']]
Seesaws are platforms that tilt either left or right, depending on whether the player, an enemy, or even certain objects stand on either the left or right side of the platform. In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', course elements have different weights also affecting the direction in which the seesaw leans.
