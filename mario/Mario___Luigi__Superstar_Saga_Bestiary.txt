This is the complete ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' bestiary of the enemies, bosses, support enemies, objects and parts.

==Legend==
*'''Name''': Name of the enemy. The numbers or words are used in the brackets is used when they have different statistics or reappear.
**Normal: Enemy
**'''Bold:''' Boss
**<u>Underline:</u> Support enemy/part/object.
*'''Location:''' The location(s) of the enemy.
*'''Level:''' The player can overcome the enemy if the enemy's level matches the character.
*'''HP:''' The enemy's maximum hit points during the start of the battle.
*'''POW:''' The enemy's attack.
*'''DEF:''' The enemy's defense power.
*'''Speed:''' The enemy's speed.
*'''Jump:''' The enemy's reaction to the Jump attacks.
**'''Normal:''' The enemy takes regular damage.
**'''Spiky: '''The player takes 1 point of the damage, unless the player equips the correct clothing to attack.
**'''Weak:''' The enemy takes only 1 point of the damage.
*'''Hammer:''' The enemy's reaction to the Hammer attacks.
**'''Normal:''' The enemy takes regular damage.
**'''Weak:''' The enemy takes only 1 point of the damage.
*'''Hand:''' The enemy's reaction to the Hand attacks.
**'''Normal:''' The enemy takes regular damage.
**'''Weak:''' The enemy takes only 1 point of the damage.
*'''Fire''': The enemy's reaction to the fire-based attacks.
**'''Heal:''' The enemy absorbs the attack to heal itself.
**'''Normal:''' The enemy takes regular damage.
**'''Critical:''' The weak enemy takes the damage and inflicts the extra damage.
*'''Thunder''': The enemy's reaction to the thunder-based attacks.
**'''Heal:''' The enemy absorbs the attack to heal itself.
**'''Normal:''' The enemy takes regular damage.
**'''Critical:''' The weak enemy takes the damage and inflicts the extra damage.
*'''Status Ailments:''' The enemy's chances being ailed with a status effect successfully.
**'''Stun?:''' The enemy's chances becoming [[dizzy]] getting hit from [[Cyclone Bros.]] or a [[Hammer]] [[First Strike]].
**'''Burn?:''' The enemy's chances becoming [[burn]]ed getting hit from [[Fire Bros. (move)|Fire Bros.]].
**'''Stat Down?:''' The enemy's chances having their POW or DEF lowered by being hit from [[Thunder Bros.]].
*'''Experience:''' The experience points earned after defeating the enemy.
*'''Coins:''' The coins dropped from the enemy.
*'''Item Drop 1:''' Item that can be earned from defeating the enemy.
**'''Rate:''' The item's rate being dropped from the enemy. The bestiary's values assume the player does not have the Grab or Salvage Badges or the [[Game Boy Horror SP]] equipped.
*'''Item Drop 2:''' Another item that can be earned after defeating the enemy. In nature, the items are rarer.
**'''Rate:''' The item's rate being dropped from the enemy, even when stealing it using the Advanced [[Swing Bros.]]. If the player equips the Game Boy Horror SP, the enemy will always drop this item, despite the item's rate is 0%.

'''Note:''' Stats in <span style="color:gray;">gray</span> are found in the game's coding and unavailable during the "normal" gameplay.
