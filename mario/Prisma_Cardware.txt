'''Prisma Cardware''' is a location seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:Prisma Cardware Exterior.png|right|frameless|420x420px]]
Prisma Cardware is the main [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] shop in [[Paper Mario: Color Splash|''Paper Mario: Color Splash'']]. The place is a small building with a window and a brown door. It has a logo hanging above, consisting of the owner's face next to some battle cards bought in his shop. The interior showcases the battle cards [[Mario]] can buy with [[coins]], which are unlocked as you progress through the game and find new battle cards.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' ==
Prisma Cardware is found in the ''Blue District'' in [[Port Prisma]]. To unlock the shop, [[Mario]] must first repaint the owner, who is unpainted in the ''Yellow District.'' After fighting off three [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]], the owner can be seen in the far right corner near the water. When Mario repaints him and he gives some brief dialogue, he will make his way back to Prisma Cardware. Entering into the shop, there is another unpainted toad. This is the owner's assistant, and will bring in customers by standing outside the shop.[[File:PMCS Prisma Cardware.png|right|frameless|420x420px]]
== Trivia ==

* This shop, while displaying many of the games Battle Cards, does not sell every card in the game. Some cards types including most Huge variants, Enemy Cards, and x3 cards are not sold here, but rather found in their respective locations.
* The Big 1-Up Mushroom Card displayed on the logo is not present anywhere else in the game.
