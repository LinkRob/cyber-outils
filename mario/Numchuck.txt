'''Numchuck''' is a monkey who appears during [[Kat and Ana]]'s story in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Numchuck has the appearance of a small monkey with brown fur, a pale white face, and a small, pink blush on each cheek.

===Etymology===
Numchuck's name is a pun on {{w|nunchaku}}, a Japanese weapon used in martial arts.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
In Kat and Ana's story, Numchuck steals [[banana]]s from Ana's house. He is first seen on the upper screen, eating a banana on the roof of the house, when Ana realizes her bananas had been stolen. This prompts Ana and her dog [[Shadow the Dog|Shadow]] to search for the thief, and Kat soon joins them after seeing the note Ana left for her.

In the end, Kat and and Ana themselves discover that the monkey stole it. Kat and Ana's infuriated expressions quickly change to endearing ones, as they find him to be adorable. At this time, the girls decide to name the monkey Numchuck and keep him around. In the final scene of the story, Numchuck is seen being gently thrown up and down by the two girls.
