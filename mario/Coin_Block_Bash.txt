'''Coin Block Bash''' is a [[List of mini-games in Mario Party#1-vs-3 mini-games|1 vs. 3 mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party]]''.
==Gameplay==

The game starts off with the players in a field with nine [[Brick Block]]s. Each block carries a varying number of coins. The player with the hammer is capable of hitting the Brick Blocks with one swing but they are considerably slower than the other players. The objective of the game is to get the most coins. Players can attack other people to stop them from getting coins.

While on [[Mini-Game Island]], this mini-game serves as World 1-2 and the player needs ten [[coin]]s in order to pass onto the next mini-game.
