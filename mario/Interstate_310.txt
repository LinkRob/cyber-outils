'''Interstate 310''' is a location in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' and returns in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Description==
Interstate 310 is a highway that crosses through most of [[Diamond City]]. Like various other locations in the ''WarioWare'' games, Interstate 310 mostly just appears in cutscenes. Several buildings of Diamond City are located down the Interstate 310, including [[Wario's House]], [[Diamond Taxi]], and [[Toy Express]].

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare: Twisted!''===
In their story, [[Steer Clear]], [[Dribble and Spitz]] are driving down the highway when the [[Dribble Taxi]] suddenly breaks down. The player plays through the duo's set of [[microgame]]s as they try to fix the taxi.

===''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''===
In the opening cutscene of [[The Multiplayer Test]], [[18-Volt]] crosses the Interstate 310 to get to a game shop.
