'''Mix-a-Mug''' is one of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Gameplay==
This minigame is similar to the [[Spade Panel]] minigames from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. To play, the player has to line up three parts of the character shown in the middle of the top screen (either [[Mario]], [[Wario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], or Yoshi) so as to make a face and win [[coin]]s, though they begin with 12 coins. Using the [[stylus]], the player has to touch the wheel they want to stop, either the chin, the facial center, or the hair. If the player does not line up the right character correctly, they lose three coins, but if done correctly, they earn a Star. Additionally, three coins are rewarded for lining up the character on the left or right side, as well as six coins for lining up the character in the center. When the player runs out of coins, it is a [[Game Over]].
