}}'''Electro Dash''' is a move used by [[Watt]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is Watt's basic attack that requires no [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

== Usage ==
To use Electro Dash, the player must hold the {{button|n64|a}} button to fill up the gauge and release it once it fills (in the same fashion to [[Bombette]]'s [[Body Slam]] attack). Watt then attacks the enemy for damage. One notable thing about Electro Dash is that it pierces defense, allowing Watt to use her full attack power on the enemy.

== Trivia ==
*[[Ms. Mowz]]'s [[Love Slap]] attack works in a similar way as it also pierces defense.
