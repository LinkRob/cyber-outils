'''Flamefaces''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Flameface is a bright red rock head covered in flames, and it has an angry face.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Flamefaces only appear in [[Fury Volcano]], and they first appear in the level [[Fury Volcano 4-2]]. [[Princess Peach]] can melt a Flameface with [[Gloom]]. Flamefaces are one of the enemies absent from the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]].
