The '''Blue Key''' is a [[key]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
The Blue Key is carried by a [[Fawfulcopter]] on the second floor of [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. In its southernmost room, Bowser must push himself as a [[Spike Ball (move)|Spike Ball]] against a large spring to shoot himself to the Fawfulcopter so that he can knock it and obtain the Blue Key, allowing him to unlock a blue door.
