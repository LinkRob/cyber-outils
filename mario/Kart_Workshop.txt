The '''kart Workshop''' is a location that appears in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. It is located in the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]], and it is where Kongs construct and repair karts. The workshop notably features multiple booths where karts can be chosen (in a similar fashion to ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'') and built. The workshop also has an exit leading to the secret passage.

In the film, after Mario wins the battle against [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] in the [[Great Ring of Kong (Illumination)|Great Ring of Kong]], Cranky Kong suggests that, to arrive in the Mushroom Kingdom before Bowser and his troops, they will need karts. He then takes Mario, Princess Peach, and Toad down to the kart workshop and tells them to pick their karts. The three have done so and line up at the workshop's exit, along with Donkey Kong and the Kong army. When the group exits the workshop, it is never seen in the film again.
