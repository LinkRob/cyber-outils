'''Togemen''' are enemies in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Togemen have a dark body with white limbs, and they wear a mask-like shell covered in spikes. In the [[Game Boy]] version, a Togemen's shell is white, but in the [[Game Boy Color]] version, it is recolored to purple, with white being used on other parts of their bodies.

===Behavior===
Whenever [[Wario]] approaches a Togemen, it begins to [[jump]] in his direction to try and damage him.

==In ''Wario Land II''==
Togemen first appear in the story [[Return the hen to her nest]]. If Wario touches a Togemen, he takes damage and loses [[coin]]s. Wario can stun a Togemen by attacking a part of its body not protected by spikes. Wario can then defeat the Togemen either by attacking it again or by throwing an enemy at it.
