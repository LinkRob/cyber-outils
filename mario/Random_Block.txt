A '''Random Block''', is a type of [[Coin Block]] which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==Description==
Upon hitting a Random Block, it releases a [[coin]], and either turn red and displays a "M", signaling for [[Mario]] to hit it, or green and displays an "L," signaling for [[Luigi]] to hit it. If the correct brother hits the block, another coin is released. It is random as to whether a Random Block changes between colors or stays the same color between each hit. After enough hits, the Random Block becomes an [[Empty Block]].

In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'', a Random Block is yellow with a dark yellow question mark within a white circle.
