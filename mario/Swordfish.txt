'''Swordfish''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Swordfish are dark blue shark-like enemy with a razor-like fin on top. They have a large, sharp set of teeth and slanted eyes.

===Behavior===
While Swordfish are in the water, only their razor-sharp fin is visible, and they are protected from any of [[Wario]]'s attacks. A Swordfish can suddenly jump out of the water to try and bite Wario.

==In ''Wario World''==
Swordfish are only encountered in [[Horror Manor]]. Wario can only attack a Swordfish when it is outside of the water. A Swordfish can become stunned upon taking three hits, then Wario can pick it up and use one of his [[Mad Move]]s to defeat it. If a Swordfish jumps out of the water and onto dry land, it remains vulnerable until the Swordfish returns into the water.

Swordfish can be found near [[Swirly Slab]]s, which can take Wario to a higher level if he stuns a Swordfish, carries it onto the slab, and then uses [[Wild Swing-Ding]] to send it upward.
