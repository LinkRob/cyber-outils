{{Nihongo|'''Spiked Koopa'''|トゲブロス|Toge Burosu}} is the first boss in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'', encountered at the end of [[Rice Beach]], and a member of the [[Black Sugar Gang|Brown Sugar Pirates]]. He appears to be a green [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]] with a spiked, dark green shell and light green skin. Spiked Koopa wears a spiked helmet, army green-colored shoes, white gloves, and a white T-shirt with a red symbol resembling a jellyfish.

Spiked Koopa has an attack striking from the air, the ground, and underground, making him invincible to fire and ground pounds. Wario can hit Spiked Koopa in the belly three times to defeat him and move on to [[Mt. Teapot]].
