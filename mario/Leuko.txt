A '''Leuko''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are jellyfish-like enemies encountered in the [[Sea]] and the [[Sunken Ship]]. Leukos have a stronger variant named [[Muckle]].

==Battle==
Being jellyfish, Leukos specialize in using electrical attacks in battle such as [[Static E!]] and [[Bolt]]. Leukos can also use [[Solidify]].

===Strategy===
Leukos are weak against [[Fire]], so any of [[Mario]]'s fire techniques are effective against them.
