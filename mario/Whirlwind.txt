'''Whirlwinds''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
If [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] walk into a Whirlwind while performing a [[Spin Jump (Mario & Luigi series)|Spin Jump]], they get sucked into it. While inside, they can be launched further than they can normally go. 

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
[[File:Whirlwind screenshot.png|thumb|left||Mario and Luigi performing a Spin Jump inside of a Whirlwind in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'']]
Whirlwinds are first seen at [[Hoohoo Mountain]], but they also appear in other areas such as [[Joke's End]]. In Joke's End, the steam released by the broth cauldrons can be spin-jumped into to create whirlwinds. If Luigi hammers [[Pump Mario]] to make him spit out a glob of water at the whirlwind, it can be used to douse the fires on the lit candles in a room to open a door to progress.
