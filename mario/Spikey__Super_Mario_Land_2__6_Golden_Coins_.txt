'''Spikey''' is a hedgehog-based enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. They are named for having small spikes all over their back. Spikeys are only encountered in [[Tree Zone]]'s first level, [[Invincibility!]].

A Spikey tries to attack Mario by curling up into a ball and then rolling toward him. Despite having spikes, a Spikey can be defeated by being jumped on, whereas other spiked enemies typically damage Mario if he jumps on them.
