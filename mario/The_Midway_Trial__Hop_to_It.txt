'''The Midway Trial: Hop to It''' is a level of [[Shining Falls]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level opens with a [[Hoppycat]] with the glow of a [[Wonder Flower]]. The hoppycat jumps all the way off screen when the player does and doesn't return to the same spot. The level is vertically based. 

At the level's [[Checkpoint Flag]], the glowing hoppycat returns and will drop a Wonder Flower when killed. The [[Wonder Effect]] will turn the player into a hoppycat and will allow the player to scale the rest of the level quickly and access an exclusive area for the hoppycat. The player will reach the [[Wonder Flower]] after jumping on big [[smogrin]]s.

==[[10-Flower Coin]] locations==
#A 10-flower coin is easily accessible and visible above a row of blocks. It is reached by [[jump]]ing onto the platform and jumping again to reach the 10-flower coin in the air.
#After entering the [[pipe]] to the left of the Checkpoint Flag, there is a secret room where the player must time their jump to make a hoppycat hit a switch and spawn blocks to reach the 10-flower coin.
#The player must jump off {{lw|Brick Block}}s to reach the 10-flower coin. If the player uses too many jumps, the brick blocks are destroyed by hoppycats.
