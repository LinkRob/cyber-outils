'''Invisible Leaderership''' is the fifth level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 12.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 6 [[Boo]]s and 2 [[Fuzzbush]]es. The enemy captain is a Boo. The enemy captain knows [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 2 [[Chuck Guy]]s and 6 Boos. The enemy captain is a Boo and knows [[Sneak Attack]] and Deny.

The final battle contains 2 Chuck Guys, 4 Boos, and 2 Fuzzbushes. The enemy captain is a Boo and knows Sneak Attack.

After the final battle, the player recruits [[Captain Boo]]. 

The battle can replayed to recruit up to 2 Boos.
