'''Bananas''' are common items that appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. These bananas can be found throughout levels except [[DK Isles]] and [[Hideout Helm]].

== Summary ==
There are five main colors of the bananas with each color representing a Kong: yellow for [[Donkey Kong]], red for [[Diddy Kong]], purple for [[Tiny Kong]], blue for [[Lanky Kong]], and green for [[Chunky Kong]]. There are a total of 100 Bananas for each Kong per level that contains them. Collecting 75 bananas in a level results in a [[Banana Medal]] which 15 of them is needed to play [[Cranky Kong]]'s [[Jetpac]] game.

However, the main purpose of the bananas is to feed them to [[Scoff]]  to have him pound the platform to have [[Troff]] unlock the boss door. All the Kongs can contribute by feeding Scroff the bananas to open the boss door. When the Kongs collect 100 bananas, a special sound is heard when they do. The bananas may also come in bunchs of 5.
