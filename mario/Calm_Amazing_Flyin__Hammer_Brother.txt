'''Calm Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Calm Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother is a variant of [[Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother]], which it shares an appearance with, but Calm Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brothers are imbued with the [[Calm]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
Like with other enemies imbued with the Calm vibe, a Calm Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother is found sleeping at first, but if [[Princess Peach|Peach]] approaches, it wakes up and starts to act like a regular Amazing Flyin' Hammer Bro, throwing [[hammer]]s as its [[Flying Platform]] sways back and forth.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Calm Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brothers first appear in [[Hoo's Wood 2-7]]. It is possible for Peach to sneak by a Calm Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother without waking it. If Peach defeats a Calm Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother before it wakes up or immediately after, the [[Flying Platform]] does not move. In the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]], its name is shortened to '''C. A. F. H. Bro'''.
