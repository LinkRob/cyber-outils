'''Nostalgic Tunes''' is a badge exclusive to the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake of Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]] 

== Description ==
Mario can wear the badge for 0 [[Badge Point|BP]]. When wearing the badge, all music will be changed to the original tracks in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|original game]]. The badge affects almost all the music in the game (including battle themes, themes that play in the field, along with character themes and cutscene music), however there are exceptions, new songs exclusive to the remake as well as music that’s newly added to the remake are both unchanged and remain intact regardless of whether or not Mario is wearing the Badge, the badge also doesn’t affect the game’s sound effects which remain updated. It can be bought at the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] for 1 [[coin]]. The badge does not affect gameplay.
