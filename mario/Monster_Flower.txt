The '''Monster Flower''' is a character in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
The Monster Flower only appears in [[Ashley]]'s story, [[The Loquacious Spell Book]].

In the opening cutscene, Ashley tries to create the Monster Flower after reading about it in a book. Ashley uses [[Red (WarioWare series)|Red]], who has turned into a wand, to zap a potted sapling. However, it only causes an ordinary white flower to grow, to Ashley's disappointed. After this, [[Chatty Mr. Spell Book]] comes in and teaches Ashley how to properly summon the Monster Flower, during which the player plays through her set of [[microgame]]s.

In the ending cutscene, Chatty Mr. Spell Book tells Ashley that she is ready to use the spell, so Ashley zaps a second potted sapling, which grows into the Monster Flower successfully. Shortly afterward, the Monster Flower takes over [[Ashley's Mansion]], growing large enough for its head and tentacles to burst from the house's sides. Though [[Red (WarioWare series)|Red]] is terrified of the Monster Flower, Ashley smiles at it.
