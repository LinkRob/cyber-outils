'''Dark Matter''' is a dangerous hazard that mainly appears in the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games. They tend to eat through platforms causing Mario to jump over the holes these things create to avoid damage. Dark Matter is also used in some of [[Bowser]]'s attacks such as the Dark Matter Spin, however, it only does one wedge of damage and does not cause disintegration. Dark Matter appears similar to [[Poison Goo]].

==Physical Appearance==
Dark Matter gather in dark blue colonies called patches, never being seen on their own. These patches vary in their size, though all of them have a network of purple lines that criss-cross the patch's surface, making them similar to [[Poison Goo]]. If his body touches the dark matter, Mario will be devoured alive and die instantly.

==In the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]==
===''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''===
Dark Matter first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Their only appearance is in [[Bowser's Dark Matter Plant]]. If he touches them, Mario/Luigi will instantly die as he is devoured alive. Poison Goo that resembles Dark Matter could also be found during the Purple Coin mission on the [[Toy Time Galaxy]] where it replaces lava on the [[Luigi]] side.

===''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''===
Some Dark Matter exist in [[Bowser Jr.'s Boom Bunker]]. The same rule applies since the last game as being a hazard that instantly defeats Mario or Luigi. They also appear in the [[Cloudy Court Galaxy]], lairing in the Purple Pond in the secret [[Power Star|mission]], [[Silver Star]]s in the Purple Pond.

==Trivia==
*This is possibly one of the most gruesome ways to die in ''Super Mario Galaxy'' even though it is meant to be kid-friendly.
*Real dark matter is a substance that increases the gravity of nearby objects.
*[[w:c:kirby:Dark Matter|Villains of the same name]] appear throughout the ''Kirby'' franchise.
