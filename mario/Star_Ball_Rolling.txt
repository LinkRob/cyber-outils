'''Star Ball Rolling''' is one of the four minigames in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', along with [[Bob-omb Blasting]], [[Bubble Blowing]], and [[Manta Ray Surfing]]. [[Mario]] rides on a [[Star Ball]], which is, as the name implies, a ball with a [[Power Star]] inside. The goal is to roll the ball past multiple obstacles, including wandering enemies and voids, and into a hole to break the sphere and obtain the star.

== Trivia ==
*In the [[Melty Molten Galaxy]], the Star Ball Rolling theme is changed, possibly due to the danger.
