|forms = [[Monstar]]}}'''Star Kids''' are species of stars that live at [[Starborn Valley]] and [[Star Haven]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==

The Star Kids are small, yellow stars that are always seen flying around.

==History==
As told by [[Merle]], the Star Kids mainly live in Starborn Valley until they become old enough to grant wishes and ascend to Star Haven. On the way to Starborn Valley, the Star Kids attempt to scare away Mario by taking the form of a beast called [[Monstar]]. However, they are easily defeated by Mario and they fly back to Starborn Valley. In Starborn Valley, the Star Kids live peacefully with [[Ninji]]s.

The Star Kids in Star Haven are usually seen outside and inside buildings. One of them even runs an Item Shop. It was revealed by one of them that Star Kids head to Star Haven once they get older.
