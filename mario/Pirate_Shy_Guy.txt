'''Pirate Shy Guy''' is an alias used by [[Shy Guy]]s dressed in pirate outfits.

==History==
===''Yoshi'' series===
====''Yoshi's Story''====
[[File:YSPirateGuysArtwork.png|thumb|150px|left|Pirate Guys in ''Yoshi's Story'']]
In ''Yoshi's Story'', they only appear in Stage 5-4: [[Shy Guy's Ship]]. They have [[Bob-omb]] cannons, [[Bill Blaster]]s, and other utilities for bombardment.

====''Yoshi's Crafted World''====
Pirate Guys return in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'', mostly act like normal Shy Guys like in ''Yoshi's Island DS''. They appear in the levels [[Pirate Pier]] and [[Bombs Away on Pirate Island]]. In the latter, they are also seen standing on cardboard galleons that follow [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s own ship on different planes in the background. These Pirate Guys attend cannons that shoot projectiles at Yoshi. Yoshi can attack back by sending cannonballs to the Pirate Guys' ships, sinking them along with the Pirate Guys. Some of them also carry [[Heyho Coin Bag]]s.

===''Mario Kart'' series===
[[File:ShyGuyGalleonMKW.png|thumb|Pirate Guys on the [[Shy Guy Galleon]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'']]
Pirate Guys also appear in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' in the [[Shy Guy Beach]] course, where they navigate the [[Shy Guy Galleon|galleon]] from where explosive cannonballs are sent onto the course.

{{br|left}}

===''Mario Party'' series===
[[File:MP8 Flagging Rights.png|thumb|left|200px|A Pirate Guy in Flagging Rights]]
They also appear in ''[[Mario Party]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' as the flag-raiser for the mini-games [[Shy Guy Says]] and [[Flagging Rights]], the former of which reappears in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''. They also appeared in ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' on the [[Pirate Land]] board where they would fire their cannons at the player sending the player flying through the air and back to start if they landed on the [[Happening Space]]s on the bridge.
{{br|left}}

===''Mario Tennis Aces''===
{{rewrite|section=y|Split up disorganized information}}
[[File:MTA_Shy_Guy_Train_Tussle_29.jpg|thumb|A Black Mega Pirate Guy wearing a Red Bandana as Boss to fight for the challenge Shy Guy Train Tussle]]
They make a comeback in ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]'' as Bosses to defeat in a Co-op monthly online challenge named Shy Guy Train Tussle. In this game, there is 8 stages. A first boss appear in stage #4, it's a Black Mega Pirate Guy wearing a Yellow Bandana, while there is a second and final boss that appear in stage #8, it's a Black Mega Pirate Guy wearing a Red Bandana. During the fight, there is four phase to defeat the bosses, while the players have 30 seconds to shut down their life. During the first and third phases, the 4 players have to hit him with balls projected from the cannon they are facing. The cannons are placed behind and above the Black Mega Shy Guys. Balls are projected to the 4 players simultaneously 4 time, before the next phase begin. During the second phase, the 4 players have to hit back Bob-Ombs that are throw by bosses. Bob-Ombs are throw, only 1 time, one by one to each players in the other they are placed, from the left to the right, before the third phase begin. The fourth and last phase is the same than the second phase for Black Mega Pirate Guy wearing the Yellow Bandana from stage 4, while this phase is different for the Black Mega Pirate Guy wearing the Yellow Bandana from stage 8. Beat a Black Mega Pirate Guy give to the team a Gold Key that can open a Big Gold Treasure that give to the team +200 points. While players cannot loose by being hit, it's possible to loose if the life (represented by the purple jauge) of the bosses is not empty, before the limited time of 30 seconds. Hit a boss with a tennis balls give +5 points to the player and hit a boss with a Bob-Omb give +15 points the the player. All the points individual players gain are all put in the team score.

In some other regular stage that aren't the stage 4 and the stage 8, there is also Black Pirate Guy that are wearing a purple bandana while they are moving attached by a single red balloon floating from one side on the other side of the stage. They are carrying boxes containing more coins.
