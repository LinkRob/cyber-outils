The '''Dark Moon''' is a mysterious celestial body located in [[Evershade Valley]] and is seen only in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' and ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]''. 

==Description==
The Dark Moon has a pacifying effect on ghosts, which makes them friendly towards the living and allows [[Professor E. Gadd]] to study and work with them. However, after [[King Boo]] destroys the Dark Moon and makes the ghosts go berserk, Luigi is tasked with locating the Dark Moon pieces and putting them back together.

==History==
===In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}===
====''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' / ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]''====
At the beginning of the game, the Dark Moon is shattered by [[King Boo]], making all the ghosts went hostile at [[Professor E. Gadd]]'s lab and E. Gadd recovers the first piece of the Dark Moon. Then, he warns [[Luigi]] about the Dark Moon's shattering, as well the ghosts becoming hostile, and teleports him to his Bunker with the [[Pixelator]].

Throughout the game, Luigi traverses all five mansions and retrieves all five pieces of the Dark Moon, before he returns to the Bunker, a purple ray interferes with the Pixelator's vortex and sucks Luigi back, Luigi is depixelated and he ends up in a dark place.

====''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''====
In ''Luigi's Mansion 3'', a miniature version of the Dark Moon can be found inside the Drawing Room, accompanying a miniature model of the Gloomy Manor. Additionally, when King Boo gets dazed during his boss fight, purple moons spin around his head rather than typical stars.

====''[[Super Mario Run]]''====
In ''Super Mario Run'', nine Dark Moons serve as the collectibles granted for completing the nine missions during the ''Luigi's Mansion 2 HD'' event. A statue based on the game is unlocked for every three Dark Moons earned.

==Locations==
Each Dark Moon piece is guarded by a [[Possessor Ghost]], each more powerful than the last due to each having the Dark Moon pieces with them longer. They are fought as follows, in this order:
#The [[Grouchy Possessor]] is the boss of [[Gloomy Manor]], and possesses a giant queen spider.
#The [[Harsh Possessor]] is the boss of the [[Haunted Towers]], and possesses a flight of stairs.
#The [[Overset Possessor]] is the boss of the [[Old Clockworks]], and possesses a giant clock face.
#The [[Shrewd Possessor]] is the boss of the [[Secret Mine]], and possesses the frozen ground beneath [[Luigi]].
#The [[Tough Possessor]] is the second boss of the [[Treacherous Mansion]], and possesses two suits of armor, three suits of armor, and then a giant suit of armor.
After collecting all the Dark Moon fragments and defeating King Boo, the Dark Moon is put back together and the ghosts return to sanity.
