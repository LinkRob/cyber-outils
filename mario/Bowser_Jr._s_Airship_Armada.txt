*''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]''<br>|unlock=|cguide=|planet=}}'''Bowser Jr.'s Airship Armada''' is a [[Bowser Jr.]] galaxy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. This galaxy consists of a very large group of [[airship]]s, including one piloted by [[Bowser Jr.]] which [[Mario]] must attack and sink on his way to the boss arena. It has three Airships including this particular one in its icon, and Mario can visit this if he has at least 23 [[Power Star|Power Stars]], including the Power Stars from [[Bowser Jr.'s Robot Reactor]] and Bowser's [[Bowser's Star Reactor]].

The music of Bowser Jr.'s Airship Armada is a remix of the airship theme from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

== Missions ==
=== Sinking the Airships ===
The first airship has a Cannon on the stern, so Mario will have to dodge or defeat the foes and Mario will have to jump in. The player will have to either aim the cannon at the flagpole with the [[Pull Star]] on the right to reach the next airship, blast into an extremely small planet with [[Goomba]]s that has a [[1-up Mushroom]] and a [[Sling Star]] to bring him to the airship directly, or carefully aim at the leftmost flagpole to skip a few steps. After Mario battles his way across the airships, Mario will reach a [[Luma Shop]] and a wooden platform docked against the last airship. Mario should jump on the platform, which begins to move, and the player will have to dodge the cannonballs, launched by several unreachable galleon-sized airships, and spiders to reach the final airship where Bowser Jr. awaits. Bowser Jr. shoots fireballs from his airship at Mario, so Mario must [[Star Spin]] or jump on one of the [[Koopa Troopa]]s, grab its shell, and throw it at Bowser Jr's airship. Mario will have to hit Bowser Jr.'s airship three times to make him turn and face Mario. Each hit will cause more mayhem to erupt on Bowser Jr.'s airship, as he begins to fire [[Bullet Bill]]s with his ship and summon [[Magikoopa]]s. Mario will have to throw two more shells and Bowser Jr. will be defeated, and Mario will get a [[Grand Star]].

==Planets==
===Starting Planet===
A small wooden plank that the first Airship somehow got parked next to.

===Airship Armada===
There are no major planets throughout the Galaxy, but Airships are prominent.

===Bouncy Bonus Planet===
A small green rubber ball that can be found above the second Airship.

==Trivia==
*This is the only boss stage that lacks Standard [[Gravity]] in the Starting Area and the Boss Stage. [[Bowser Jr.'s Lava Reactor]] does not count, since the Lava Reactor is very large.
*Despite losing his Airship here, Bowser Jr. somehow has it during the pre-boss cutscene at his Lava Reactor.
*This Galaxy is the only boss Galaxy in the original ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' where [[Lumalee]] appears (She appears on the penultimate Airship that Mario visits on his way to the boss area.), and one of two across both [[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|''Super Mario Galaxy'']] games where she appears, the other being [[Bowser Jr.'s Fearsome Fleet]] in [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|the second game]].

==Nomenclature==
Bowser Jr.'s Airship Armada is referred to as a "Ship Plant" in Japanese (''Shippu Puranto''; written シッププラント), German (''Schiffsfabrik''), and French (''Usine de Vaisselle-ous''), a "Ship Dimension" in Spanish (''Dimensión Navis''), an "Airship Factory" in Chinese (''fēichuán gōngchǎng'', written 飞船工厂) and Korean (''Eeosip Peullanteu'', written 쿠파주니어 에어십 플랜트), and a "Naval Fleet" in Italian (''Flotta Navale'').
