'''Screwtop It Up''' is a [[Time Attack (Challenge Mode)|Time Attack]] [[Challenge Mode (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Challenge]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and its [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. The Challenge takes place in [[Screwtop Tower]].

==Objective==
[[Mario]] has to navigate through Screwtop Tower by turning the [[Screwtop Lift]]s to go up higher, while avoiding any [[Bony Beetle]]s, [[Fire Bar]]s, and [[Sumo Bro]]s along the way. Mario has to reach the end in under one and a half minutes (90 seconds) to pass the challenge.

==Flavor text==
*'''Challenge description:''' ''Reach the goal ring in 90.00 or less.''
