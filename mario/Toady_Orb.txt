The '''Toady Orb''' is a common [[Orb]] from ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. It is the successor of the [[Magikoopa Capsule]] from ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
Its effect summons [[Toady]], who swaps that player's capsules with an opponent's via the roulette wheel. If an opponent lands on a [[space]] with a Toady Orb, Toady appears to take one of their Orbs and give it to the owner of that space.

In ''Mario Party 7'', Toady will take an orb and use it on a random space on the space owner's behalf (yellow or red) or throw it away (blue or green). It costs five coins at the [[Orb Shop]].
