'''Rare Scarf''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When the user equips the Rare Scarf, the item increases the player's defense. The player can obtain the item by climbing the vines to [[Nimbus Land]] and taking the left route.
