'''Spinias''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Spinias have two evolved forms, [[Spania]]s and [[Spunia]]s, the latter being the most powerful basic enemy.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
[[File:Spinia Beta.png|thumb|The hamburger-like Spinia.]]
Spinias are green spinning disc enemies. They attack [[Mario]] by spinning at him. Spinias first appear throughout [[Rogueport Sewers]], where they attack whoever they see. Later on, when [[Bowser]] takes command of the sewers, a few Spinias can be found, although they occasionally aid a [[Magikoopa]] or [[Hammer Bro]] in battle. Spinias also appear in the first section of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].
