The '''Red Flagpole''' is an uncommon exit introduced and currently found only within the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' series]]. They can be distinguished from their [[Goal Pole|regular counterpart]] via having a red flag instead of a black one and, from [[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]] onwards, a red ball on top of the pole instead of a gold one and a black base and fortress instead of light-brown ones. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. Red Flagpoles often mark the locations of secret exits. Going down a Red Flagpole into the secret exit creates a path to shortcuts, such as Warp Pipes on the map, Toad Houses, Warp Cannons, and secret levels. Typically this is required to fully complete the game making finding all the Red Flagpoles and standard [[Flagpole]]s a requirement for attaining 5 Stars on a save file.

Red Flagpoles have a few equivalents serving as alternate level exits in some earlier [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] installments, including [[Warp Zone]]s in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and the [[Key]] and [[Keyhole]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.
