The '''Fire Flower Fields'''<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/356208486587039745/1091461391417884762/20230331_120345.png|title=20230331_120345.png (4032×1960)|accessdate=2023-04-05|work=Discord}}</ref> are, as the name suggests, an open land known for its [[Fire Flower]] plants. It appeared in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== History ==
[[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]], [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]] and [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]] set camp in the fields, with Peach harnessing a [[Fire Flower]]'s power by touching it.

Peach shared that she did not know her origins. After walking through a [[Warp Pipe]] as a baby, she was taken in by the residents of Mushroom Kingdom and eventually crowned their princess; Mario speculated that she may be from the same world as he is.
