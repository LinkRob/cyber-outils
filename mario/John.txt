'''John''' is the first name of a few people who were officially involved with the {{franchise|Mario}}:

*[[John Stocker]], the voice actor for [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] and other characters in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''
*[[John Leguizamo]], the actor who portrayed [[Luigi]] in the [[Super Mario Bros. (film)|''Super Mario Bros.'']] film.
*[[John Grusd]], the producer and director of [[DIC Entertainment]]'s ''Super Mario'' animated series.
