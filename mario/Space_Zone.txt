The '''Space Zone''' is one of the zones in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It is set within [[Outer Space|outer space]] and consists of two levels and a secret level. [[Tatanga]] is the boss of Space Zone. The world map shows a smiling [[Mushroom Moon|moon]] and a star shaped like a [[Starman]]; they are seen smiling at first, but once the secret level is discovered, they bear a sad expression.
