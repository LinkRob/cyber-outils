'''Deanna Michelle Mustard''' (born November 26, 1980), is an American voice actress in the [[Mario (series)|''Mario'' series]]. She started voicing Daisy in 2003 and her first voice role was for [[Princess Daisy]] in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''. She also voiced [[Baby Daisy]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]].'' She continued to voice Daisy until [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]] where she was replaced by [[Giselle Fernandez]].
