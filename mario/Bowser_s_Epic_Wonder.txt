'''Bowser's Epic Wonder''' is a [[Wonder Effect]] in [[The Final Battle! Bowser's Rage Stage]] that is a mix of 13 Wonder Effects.

==Overview==
At the start of the level, [[Bowser Jr.]] taps into his Wonder power, summoning three fists, with the first and third ones striking downwards and the second one striking upwards. Afterwards, two large [[Goomba]]s will be spawned. Gravity then tilts counter-clockwise which causes a [[Spiked Ball|spiky orb]] to roll forwards. The orb is then destroyed which reveals a Ancient Dragon fossil wheel as gravity shifts twice more, reverting at the last tilt. Afterwards, the dragon in the fossil wheel comes back to life, with his back being rideable. Afterwards, some platforms are spawned while [[Anglefish]]es fly horizontally at the top. The background then turns dark, but the path forwards is blocked by a [[Bowser Statue]], which [[Bulrush]]es proceed to destroy. Afterwards, [[Piranha Plant]]s start to sing, and a pipe moves down with a [[Gabon]] who also sings. This is followed by a section where chandeliers move into place and door mimics appear to sing. [[King Boo]] then appears to join the song, forcing [[Mario (character)|Mario]] to move forwards. After King Boo makes his leave, Mario will find his personal gravity reduced, which he must use to avoid some lava orbs. After gravity reverts back to normal, Mario must use some moving pipes to cross the lava.
