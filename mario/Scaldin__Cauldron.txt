'''Scaldin' Cauldron''' is a luck-based Bowser [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Layout==
This minigame, like all other [[Bowser]] minigames in ''Mario Party 5'', takes place in some sort of purple mountain or keep shaped like Bowser's head.

==Opening==
The camera zooms to the eye, in which the four players are seen running from Bowser. The players run through a passage and end in a locked room with urns and vases, in which they find three different spiked cauldrons. The cauldrons are red, green, and gray respectively and are colored after the buttons they represent.

==Gameplay==
[[File:MP5_ScaldinCauldron.png|thumb|Bowser burning a cauldron with [[Luigi]] inside.]]
Players must hide in one of the cauldrons to avoid being burned by Bowser. The cauldrons are labeled {{button|gcn|B}}, {{button|gcn|A}}, and {{button|gcn|X}} from left to right. Players are given five seconds to select a cauldron. If any player fails to enter a cauldron within that time, they automatically jump into a cauldron. After Bowser steps in, he [[Fire Breath|breathes fire]] on two random cauldrons and leaves one safe, although if all four players pick the same cauldron and it is the first one Bowser burns, the minigame immediately ends. Sometimes, Bowser may fake out by breathing no flame or a little flame, sometimes followed by a strong flame. Players that avoid being burned win.

==Ending==
Winners leave their cauldrons and celebrate in relief. If at least one player got burned, Bowser laughs and stomps triumphantly. If no players got burned, Bowser groans, shakes his head, and looks away in disappointment.
