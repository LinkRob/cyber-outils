The '''Recruit Poplin''' is a character from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''<nowiki/>'s Nintendo Switch 2 port, [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park'']]. He is absent in the base game, but in the Nintendo Switch 2 port, he joins up with the [[Toad Brigade]] to help them even though he is not a [[Mushroom Retainer]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Recruit Poplin is an orange [[Poplin]]. He has an orange and white flower cap on his head, with a headlamp wrapped around that. Under his flower cap is an orange hijab. His outfit is completed by white baggy trousers and orange shoes. On his back is a backpack. The backpack and headlamp make him resemble [[Captain Toad]] to a degree.

== In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park'' ==
The Recruit Poplin gathers information for [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Captain Toad]] with help from [[Plucky]] on the [[Koopalings]] who have stolen [[Bellabel Park]]'s Bellabel Flowers in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park'']]. This intel reveals where each Koopaling hides.
