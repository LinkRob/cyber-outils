|game      = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}The '''Magikoopa Blockade''' (known as '''Kamek Blockade''' in Europe and Australia) is a blockade located in World 3 of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
This level features 3 [[Magikoopa]]s, which must be defeated within 200 seconds, and not the usual 300 seconds. This makes the level a bit harder.

==Green Star==
*'''Green Star 1''': Defeat the three Magikoopas and get the Star.
