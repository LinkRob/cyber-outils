}}'''Lucky Cat Mario''' is a power-up featured in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]].'' It can be used by [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Toad (species)|Toad]], and [[Rosalina]] upon acquirement of a [[Lucky Bell]].

==Appearance==
[[File:LuckyStatues.png|left|250px|All playable characters in their Lucky Cat statue forms.]]Lucky Cat Mario's appearance is almost exactly the same as his [[Cat Mario|normal Cat form]], except he now dons a red collar with a Lucky Bell attached to it on his neck. When in statue form, his suit changes to golden yellow and he takes on a shiny, sparkling golden tint and holds a coin in one paw whilst holding the other paw in the air.

==Powers and Abilities==
Lucky Cat Mario functions the same as Cat Mario, retaining his scratching attack, diving attack, and ability to climb up walls. However, when Lucky Cat Mario [[Ground Pound]]s, he will transform into a golden cat statue form of himself, gaining temporary invincibility for as long as the player holds one of the Ground Pound buttons, similarly to the [[Tanooki Suit]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', and the [[Statue Leaf]] from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]].'' Lucky Cat Mario can also collect coins as he plummets to the ground as a statue. While invincible, he can defeat all enemies (Excluding bosses) and permanently defeat [[Dry Bones]] once contact is made with them. However, the invincibility does not protect against [[lava]], poison, [[Pit (hazard)|pits]], or being crushed.

==Trivia==
*Lucky Cat Mario's statue form is a reference to the [[Wikipedia:Maneki-neko|Maneki-neko]] figurine.
*Mario, Peach, Toad, and Rosalina all hold up their right paw as a gesture used to bring in wealth and good fortune, while Luigi is depicted holding up his left paw, commonly associated with businesses and owners wanting to bring in customers.
