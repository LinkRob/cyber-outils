'''Kalmar''' is one of the [[Star Spirit]]s featured in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He and the other Star Spirits also return in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Physical description==
Kalmar does not have much detail aside from being a six-pointed star with a mustache and a pair of eyes.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Kalmar is the Star Spirit guarded by the [[Crystal King]] in the [[Crystal Palace]], and Kalmar is freed upon defeating the Crystal King. Kalmar grants [[Mario]] the [[Up & Away]] star power, allowing Mario to turn enemies into stars and therefore knock them out of battle.

==In ''Mario Party 5''==
[[File:Kalmar.jpg|thumb|In ''Mario Party 5'']]
Kalmar is the guide of the options mode.

==Trivia==
*His name is a pun on the word "calmer," given Kalmar is very warm-hearted and hates fighting and arguing.
*Kalmar's name is also the same as the real-world city in Sweden, {{w|Kalmar}}, something possibly taken into consideration during ''Paper Mario''{{'}}s development when Kalmar was chosen to be placed in a very cold area.
