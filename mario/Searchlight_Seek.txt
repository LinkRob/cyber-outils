==Gameplay==
Searchlight Seek is a minigame that involves shooting [[Klaptrap]]s in the dark with melons. There is a single searchlight that allows the player to locate and shoot the Klaptraps. The player must shoot a certain amount of Klaptraps before the 60 seconds is up. This minigame is mainly played by [[Lanky Kong]] and [[Chunky Kong]] but any Kong can take on this minigame at [[Snide's H.Q.]] once all of the [[Blueprint]]s have been found.
