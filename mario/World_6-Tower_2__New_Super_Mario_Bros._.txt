'''World 6-Tower 2''' is the sixth main level of [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' as well as its second [[tower]] level. It is unlocked after completing [[World 6-4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-4]], and completing this level will unlock [[World 6-5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-5]], as well as [[World 6-B (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-B]], which can be accessed with five [[Star Coins]].

==Layout==
This tower level features many [[Conveyor Belt]]s, as well as walls that have spikes on them. After three conveyor belts is the first [[Question Block]], containing a power-up. After a few more jumps on conveyor belts, the first [[Star Coin]] can found on a conveyor belt leading to spikes on the wall. Shortly after is the checkpoint. Several more conveyor belts, the player can find two [[Warp Pipe]] cannons. The left one launches the player into a trail of coins, leading them back down. The right pipe launches the player upwards, allowing them to continue through the level. The second Star Coin can be found by falling onto a conveyor belt surrounded by [[Spike Top]]s. Progressing upwards, the player can either take the pipe cannon to the left, which will take them directly to the boss double doors, or jump to a rope and climb it to obtain the third Star Coin. The conveyor belt below will also lead them to the boss doors. The [[Bowser Jr.]] battle takes place on a moving chained platform over lava. This battle features Bowser Jr. going into his shell when approaching the player, and throwing [[Koopa Shell]]s at the player, which must be launched back at him, in order to jump on him afterwards.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' After traversing past several [[Conveyor Belt]]s, the player can get the [[Star Coin]] by jumping on conveyor belts past spikes onto another conveyor belt, while avoiding the spikes on the wall.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' After being launched by the Pipe Cannon on the right, the player must fall onto a conveyor belt that is surrounded by [[Spike Top]]s. The player must duck on the conveyor belt and jump to destroy the [[Brick Block]]s to obtain the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' Near the end of the level, the player must jump onto a rope swing, and climb to the right to reach the Star Coin. They can then fall onto the conveyor belt underneath to progress to the boss double doors.
