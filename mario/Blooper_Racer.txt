'''Blooper Racers''' are a non-hostile variant of [[Blooper]]s that appear in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. They come in light green, yellow, and pink colors which, while not immediately different, each have different speed and maneuverability for [[Mario]].

Blooper Racers only appear in two [[Ricco Harbor]] missions: [[Blooper Surfing Safari]] and [[Red Coins on the Water]]; in the latter mission, the [[Big Daddy of Blooper-surfing]] leases one to Mario for free, and Mario is required to ride to have even a chance of acquiring all of the [[Red Coin|red coins]] within the [[time limit]]. If Mario crashes into a wall, it will result of him having an instant death.
