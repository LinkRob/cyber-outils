The '''Lakitu Bros.''' are two brothers of the [[Lakitu]] found at [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. They have a camera attached to their fishing poles, recording [[Mario]]'s progress. The Lakitu Bros. make their first and main appearance in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Super Mario 64''===
[[File:Lakitu Bro. Intro - Super Mario 64.png|thumb|left|200px|In ''Super Mario 64''{{'}}s opening cutscene]]
In the game, one of the Lakitu Bros. can be seen in the introduction scene flying around the castle, showing the player the entire view of the castle. Then, the Lakitu swoops underneath a bridge and to a pipe with [[Mario]] jumping out of it. Another Lakitu Bro. appears in front of Princess Peach's Castle, introducing himself to Mario just before entering.

The Lakitu Bros. follow Mario everywhere and have stated that they follow Mario everywhere to record his progress in collecting the Power Stars. They also told Mario that they can record him during the game at different certain angles according to his will. This can simply be changed by pressing the C buttons or the R button.

===''Super Mario 64 DS''===
[[File:Lakitu Bro. Intro - Super Mario 64 DS.png|thumb|200px|left|One of the Lakitu Bros. in ''Super Mario 64 DS'']]
In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', the Lakitu Bros. once again record the progress of the star hunt, only this time they have to film Mario, [[Luigi]], [[Wario]] and [[Yoshi]]. A Lakitu Bro. sweeps around Princess Peach's Castle and leads to [[Mario]] who pops out along with [[Luigi]] and [[Wario]]. The Bros. start fighting and head into the castle. Later, the Lakitu Bro. is telling Yoshi, whom he had relocated from the roof to the ground floor, that he is concerned as the Bros. have not returned yet, and requests Yoshi to seek them out.

Yoshi then attempts to go into the castle. A Lakitu Bro. then tells Yoshi that the castle is locked and that he must find a key that a [[rabbit]] is holding. Once Yoshi gets the key, he sets off into the castle to find the three bros.

Once Mario enters the [[Whomp's Fortress]], a Lakitu Bro. tells him that he can sneak up on a [[Piranha Plant]] and defeat it. After this event, the Lakitu Bros. do not do much. A Lakitu Bro. can be found in the mirror room where the [[Snowman's Land]] painting can be found and in the mirror in the "Luigi Key Battle" section of [[Big Boo's Haunt]].

===''Super Smash Bros.''===
One of the Lakitu Bros. make a cameo in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]''. He flies in the background of the castle for a few minutes in the [[Peach's Castle (Super Smash Bros.)|Peach's Castle]] stage.

==Trivia==
*Other related Lakitus use cameras in games such as ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' or ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.
