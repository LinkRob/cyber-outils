'''Sandmaarghs''' are a desert-inhabiting variant of [[Magmaargh]]s. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' and return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' and ''[[Mario Golf: Super Rush]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
In ''Super Mario 3D Land'', a Sandmaargh follows [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] while hiding under the sand, and when it gets close enough, the Sandmaargh pops out and tries to chomp him. If the Sandmaargh is unsuccessful, Mario or Luigi have a chance to attack it while the Sandmaargh is above ground by either [[jump]]ing onto it or by swinging their tail at it while in [[Tanooki Mario (power-up)|Tanooki]] form.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'', during combat, the Sandmaargh attacks by inhaling. It can briefly eat one of the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] if he is drawn into the Sandmaargh's mouth.

==In ''Super Mario 3D Land''==
[[File:Sandmaargh W5-1 SM3DL.png|thumb|left|200px|A Sandmaargh in World 5-1 of ''Super Mario 3D Land'']]
Sandmaarghs appear in a few desert-themed levels. They are first encountered in [[World 3-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 3-1]] and make a reappearance in [[World 5-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 5-1]] and [[Special 6-1]]. While underground, a Sandmaargh's shadows can be seen above ground, revealing where they are hiding and their movements.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''==
Sandmaarghs are enemies encountered in [[Doop Doop Dunes]]. Like a few other enemies, they have no paper counterparts. When a Sandmaargh attacks, the targeted [[Mario Bros. (group)|brother]] has to use his [[hammer]] to knock away the obstacles into Sandmaargh's mouth, as being hit by an obstacle pushes him into the Sandmaargh's mouth. If a bro gets eaten by a Sandmaargh in the overworld, the battle starts with it eating the bro, dealing a lot of damage to him.

Sandmaarghs also appear in the [[Paper Toad Tailgate]] and [[Paper Toad Trail Trials]] missions, in which the Mario Bros. must jump at the right time before they come out of the sand while rescuing the [[Paper Toad]]s, otherwise the Sandmaargh eats one of them.

==In ''Mario Golf: Super Rush''==
[[File:Sandmaargh (Mario Golf Super Rush).png|thumb|200px|A Sandmaargh in ''Mario Golf: Super Rush'']]
Large Sandmaarghs are encountered in [[Balmy Dunes]]. If a ball hits a Sandmaargh, this results in a stroke penalty.
