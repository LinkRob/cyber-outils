The '''Magic Paintbrush''' is an item used by [[Bowser Jr. (Illumination)|Bowser Jr.]] in [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]. In the final battle, the magic paintbrush is ultimately destroyed by [[Ruined Dragon|the Ruined Dragon]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
The magic paintbrush itself is a long, staff-shaped weapon, originally created by Professor E. Gadd. In the films, the paintbrush is used to create paint and is a major plot device.

== Role in the trailer ==
The paintbrush features the ''Bowser's Fury'' symbol, and when used, it causes him to transform into his "Wonder Form" (a darker version of his regular self with a yellow-mouthed bandana), an element that fuses his appearances from different games.
