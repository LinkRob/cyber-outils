'''Boo Blah''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. It is a type of ghost attached to the floor or the ceiling, from which it stretches out at random to try and attack [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], somewhat similar to a [[Stretch]]'s attack pattern from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

Despite having "Boo" in their name, Boo Blahs are not a [[Boo]] variant.
