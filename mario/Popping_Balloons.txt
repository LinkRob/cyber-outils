'''Popping Balloons''' is a [[Mini Battle]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] and [[Bandit]] compete to try and be the first to pop the [[Balloon (Mini Battle)|balloon]] containing either a [[10-Point Star]] or [[20-Point Star]] first. A Yoshi can pop a balloon by [[Ground Pound (move)|ground-pounding]] it. Whoever pops the balloon first is the winner, so if Yoshi wins, he is rewarded the 10-Point Star or 20-Point Star contained within, but if the Bandit wins, Yoshi does not earn the items.

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
Popping Balloons can be played by unlocking a [[Bonus Game Shack]] in four different [[level]]s: [[Visit Koopa And Para-Koopa]], [[Monkeys' Favorite Lake]], [[Lake Shore Paradise]], and lastly [[Scary Skeleton Goonies!]].
