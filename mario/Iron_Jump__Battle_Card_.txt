|name=Iron Jump (Battle Card)}}'''Iron Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Iron Jump is the iron version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a blue-gray boot with an blue/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 6/22 coins

Sell Price: 3/11 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Iron Jump is first seen in [[Sunglow Ridge]], but can also found in [[Kiwano Temple]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Toad Trainworks]], [[Sunset Express]], and [[Redpepper Volcano]].
