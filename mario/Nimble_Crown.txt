The '''Nimble Crown''' is an item from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']].

== Description ==
While not much is known about their appearance due to being represented by a blue treasure chest not unlike other accessories, Nimble Crowns have the ability to grant their wearer the first turn in combat. However, the regular version of the Nimble Crown has a chance to fail, while the Deluxe version always succeeds. If the player has a higher [[Speed (Initiative)|Speed]] than every other combatant, the Nimble Crown has no effect.

== Obtainment ==
A regular Nimble Crown can be purchased from [[Maykit and Billdit|Maykit]]'s workshop on [[Shipshape Island]]. To obtain the Deluxe version, the player has to find at least 15 Rare Reefs and report back to [[Issa Rock]].

== Trivia ==

* Strangely, [[Sharkbone|Sharkbones]] seem to be able to ignore the Deluxe version of the Nimble Crown, sometimes taking the first turn in combat even if Mario or Luigi wears it.
