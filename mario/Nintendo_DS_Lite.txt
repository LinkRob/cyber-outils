The '''Nintendo DS Lite''' is the first redesign of the [[Nintendo DS]]. It was released in [[2006]] and contains all the features of the original Nintendo DS along with some new features, such as a significantly brighter screen (with four adjustment options), a lighter weight, and a smaller overall size (with the drawback that a regular [[Game Boy Advance]] cartridge protrudes from the bottom, similar to playing a [[Game Boy]] or [[Game Boy Color]] cartridge in the Game Boy Advance). The microphone is also on the center hinge, between the two screens, not on the bottom right as with the original Nintendo DS. The Nintendo DS Lite sold 89.19 million units as of 2010. It was also the final handheld system to have backward compatibility with Game Boy Advance games.

When the Nintendo DS Lite was released, other games released for the Nintendo DS line of systems would be marketed with it, such as ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]''.
