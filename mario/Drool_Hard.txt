'''Drool Hards''' are enemies which only appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Behavior==
A Drool Hard hangs from above, and spits a green [[Needlenose]]s, which bounce along the ground. When a Needlenose stops bouncing, it turns yellow, then red, and then explodes (exploding Needlenoses can also explode other Needlenoses within their radius).

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
Drool Hards are first encountered in [[Return of the Moving Chomp Rock!]]. In the [[Island Museum]], four of them appear under the botanical-enemy exhibit.
