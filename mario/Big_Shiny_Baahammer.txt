'''Big Shiny Baahammers''' are stickers in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. This sticker can put enemies to sleep. It can only be found in Decalburg as a purchase from the shop there. The second strongest sticker in the line of Baahammer stickers.

'''Museum Description''': A hammer that can knock even the most sleep-resistant enemy out. Of course, you've got to time it well.
