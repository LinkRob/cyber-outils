A '''goal''' (a.k.a. '''Goal Door''') is an object in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Description==
A Goal Door is an open exit located at the end of most [[level]]. They are marked "GOAL" and consist of an open entrance in the wall and a [[Bonus Bell]] hanging from a post above it. If [[Mario]] enters the open entrance, the level immediately ends, and he returns to the map and progresses to the next stage marker. If Mario hits the Bonus Bell first, he gets to play a bonus game, either [[Mario Catcher]] or [[Amida Game II]], which can reward him with power-ups or additional lives.

Goals are the successor to the [[Goal (Super Mario Land)|goals]] from ''[[Super Mario Land]]'', but with the Bonus Bell replacing the higher door of ''Super Mario Land''{{'}}s goal.
