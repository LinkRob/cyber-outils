|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Flutter Hush''' is a special move used only by [[Dodo]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
When Dodo uses this move, it can cause the [[Mute]] status on a character which can be very deadly during the one-on-one fight. Carrying a Wake Up Pin, Safety Badge, and Safety Pin will make this attack useless.
