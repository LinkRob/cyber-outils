The '''20-Point Star''' is a type of [[Special Item]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. It is worth 20 [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]], so using a 20-Point Star fills the [[Countdown Timer]] by 20 seconds. Given that the Countdown Timer is set to ten seconds by default, it is possible for the 20-Point Star to fill the Countdown Timer to the maximum number of 30 seconds.

20-Point Stars have a lesser effective variation named the [[10-Point Star]], which add only 10 seconds to the Countdown Timer.
