'''Castle Siege''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is from the ''Fire Emblem'' series.

==Origin==
Castle Siege's origin comes from the objective in ''Fire Emblem'' games to siege the enemy's castle to secure victory.
