'''Baby Bowser Bonkers''' is Duel mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
Both players must jump on top of the [[Baby Bowser]]s that appear from the holes. All that is required is to jump off their heads when they reveal themselves. Whoever gets the most hits on the Baby Bowsers is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Move
*{{button|n64|a}} - Jump

==Trivia==
*This mini-game is somewhat a parody of {{w|Whac-a-Mole}}.
