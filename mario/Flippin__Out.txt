'''Flippin' Out''' is a single-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Opening==
The player's character runs away from a wall sliding toward them, then they [[jump]] on the nearest peg.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Flippin' Out Yoshi.png|thumb|left|200px|Gameplay screenshot of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] holding on to a green peg]]
The sole objective in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode is for the player's character to [[jump]] between pegs to get across to the goal peg within a [[time limit]] of 30 seconds. There are four types of pegs: a green peg, which is the regular type; a red peg, which occasionally disappears; a blue peg, which moves either vertically or horizontally along a [[track]]; and the goal peg, which marks the end of the area. If either the player's character runs out of time or falls into the [[lava]] below, they lose at the minigame.

In Play Land, the player's character earns [[point]]s whenever they complete a level, which also increases the timer by eight seconds. The score earned is equal to the amount of time remaining when the player's character reaches the end of the level multiplied by ten.
