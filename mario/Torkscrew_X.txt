'''Torkscrew X''' is an X boss fought in the [[Battle Ring (Mario & Luigi: Dream Team)|Battle Ring]] of [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]].

== Appearance ==
He looks identical to the real [[Torkscrew]], but with purple skin and a bright yellow tummy, and his screw is dull grey instead of gold.

== Battle ==
In battle, Torkscrew X has increased stats, with higher HP, POW, DEF, etc.
