The '''Calculator''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Calculator was stolen from [[Rowf]] by the [[Shy Guy]]s during the events of Chapter 4. Because he doesn't have his Calculator, Rowf cannot sell any badges at his [[Rowf's Badge Shop|badge shop]] (since Rowf mentions he needs it to help him calculate his prices) until Mario defeats the Shy Guy that stole it and gives it back to him. Although this is actually optional and doesn't need the Calculator to progress in the game, Mario must get it if he wants to buy any badges from Rowf. If Mario brings Rowf back his Calculator, the badge shop owner rewards him with the [[I Spy (badge)|I Spy]] badge allowing him to detect any nearby [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]].
