The '''''Mario + Rabbids''''' series is a crossover duology series of ''[[Super Mario (series)|Mario]]'' and ''[[w:c:rabbids:Rabbids (franchise)|Raving Rabbids]]''. It is a turn-based strategy and adventure game series. It sees [[Mario]] team up with [[Rabbids]] such as [[Rabbid Luigi]], [[Rabbid Yoshi]], [[Rabbid Peach]], and even [[Rabbid Mario]] to save the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].
