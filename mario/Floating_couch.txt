'''Floating couches''', also referred to as '''couches''' and '''couch elevators''', are an object in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
When a character stands on a floating couch, it begins to slowly float up to a certain position, where it then permanently floats in one spot. Floating couches can be used to obtain [[Green Star]]s and a [[stamp]].

==In ''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''==
Floating couches appear in [[Shifty Boo Mansion]] and [[Shiftier Boo Mansion]].
