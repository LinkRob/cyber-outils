The '''Chaos Emerald''' is an item from the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' franchise and a recurring item in the {{series|Mario & Sonic}}. It made its first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' for [[Wii]].

==Description==
The Chaos Emeralds are powerful gems with various effects, the most well-known of which is transforming [[Sonic the Hedgehog]] into his [[Super Sonic]] form if all seven are together. In ''Mario & Sonic'' games, the Chaos Emerald frequently appears as an item in [[Dream Event]]s, where they often replicate the function of the stronger items within the ''[[Mario Kart]]'' series, like a [[Super Star]] or [[Lightning]].

==In {{series|Mario & Sonic}}==
{{Sonic Wiki Zone}}
===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'' (Wii)===
In the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'', the Chaos Emerald appears as item in the Dream Event, [[Dream Race (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games for Wii)|Dream Race]]. It is used similarly to the [[Super Star]] in the ''[[Mario Kart]]'' games, as it grants the user temporary invincibility, a speed boost, and stuns the other characters upon contact, causing them to lose speed. Because of its helpful effects, the Chaos Emerald is most commonly obtained by the character positioned in fourth place.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'' (Nintendo DS)===
In the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'', the Chaos Emerald is featured in two Dream Events. In [[Dream Basketball]], the Chaos Emerald can be used to either move the other characters' baskets faster or have a [[Boo]] to block one of the baskets. In [[Dream Long Jump (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games)|Dream Long Jump]], the Chaos Emerald can generate a strong headwind that affects every character.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Wii)===
[[File:ChaosEmeraldM&SATOWG.png|thumb|250px|left|Chaos Emeralds in Dream Figure Skating]]
Chaos Emeralds appear again in the Wii version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' in the Sonic World routine in [[Dream Figure Skating]]. Collecting them all is the narrative goal of the first two portions of the performance, and the players get to use them in the final segment of the performance. They have no mechanical role, in that they do not grant points or cause anything to change in the routine.

The first segment of the Sonic World routine, which takes place on a [[Green Hill Zone]]-themed skating rink of the Dream Figure Centre, has the purple, blue, green, and red Chaos Emeralds. The characters collect the first three of those Chaos Emeralds in that order. The first player collects the purple one after performing a spiral. The second player collects the blue one after performing a spiral. The green Chaos Emerald is floating in the air, so the third player collects it after performing a jump and getting boosted up to the emerald by a [[Spring (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Spring]]. Before the players reach the floating red Chaos Emerald, a [[Egg Flapper]] steals it and flies away.

The second segment of the Sonic World routine, which takes place on a Death Egg Zone-themed skating rink of the Dream Figure Centre, has the white, yellow, and light blue Chaos Emeralds. The Egg Flapper from the first segment brings the red Chaos Emerald to this segment, and carries it throughout. The characters collect the Chaos Emeralds in that order. The first player collects the white one after performing a jump, getting a boost from a Spring, landing on an [[Egg Pawn]] to destroy it, and landing back on the ice where the Chaos Emerald is. The yellow Chaos Emerald is right next to the white one, and the second player collects that Chaos Emerald in the same way as the first player does, but without landing on an Egg Pawn. The third player collects the light blue Chaos Emerald with the same maneuver as the first player but on the opposite side of the rink. The final part of the second segment has all the players jump and get boosted by Springs to attack the Egg Flapper that stole the red Chaos Emerald, with the fourth player defeating it and obtaining its Chaos Emerald.

In the final segment of the Sonic World routine, which takes place on a Station Square-themed skating rink of the Dream Figure Centre, the characters start the performance by performing a spin. This spin has them use all seven Chaos Emeralds in order to figuratively transform into their Super States so that they can defeat [[Chaos|Perfect Chaos]]. During the spin, the Chaos Emeralds spin above the characters in a circle and gradually come closer together, and once the spin concludes the Chaos Emeralds vanish in a flash of light as the player characters figuratively transform. The Super States are represented by each character glowing and having a yellow spotlight over them.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)===
In the Nintendo DS version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', Chaos Emeralds appear as items in several Dream Events, more than in other ''Mario & Sonic'' installments. In [[Ski Cross Racing]] and [[Extreme Snowboarding]], if a character uses a Chaos Emerald, they stun every opponent ahead of them, getting rid of their items. In [[Rocket Ski Jumping]], a Chaos Emerald functions similarly to [[Lightning]] from the ''Mario Kart'' series, as it creates a bolt of lightning that strikes every character, causing them to lose speed and altitude. In [[Fever Hockey]], the Chaos Emerald causes tokens to appear on the rink that strike opponents with lightning and knock them down when collected. In [[Ski Shooting]], using a Chaos Emerald stuns every opponent, briefly preventing them from being able to shoot.

Chaos Emeralds are featured differently in [[Ultimate Figure Skating]], in which they indicate areas on the rink during the routine where the characters can perform spins and defeat enemies.

===''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games'' (Wii)===
Chaos Emeralds also appear as items in the [[London Party]] mode in the Wii version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]''. They can be collected from the [[? Balloon]]s all over [[London]]. If a character uses a Chaos Emerald, they stun their opponents for a few seconds.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games'' (Nintendo 3DS)===
In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'', Chaos Emeralds are items in [[Boxing Plus]], in which one can fill its user's Special Punch gauge faster for a short amount of time.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games'' (Wii U)===
In the [[Wii U]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Wii U)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'', Chaos Emeralds are items in [[Duel Rugby Sevens]]. When a character uses a Chaos Emerald, they knock over opponents in a circular radius of them and earn Duel Points.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
[[File:Shadow SSBU screenshot.jpg|thumb|A screenshot of Shadow using the green Chaos Emerald in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'']]
In the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], the seven Chaos Emeralds briefly appear around Sonic when he initiates his [[Final Smash]], [[Super Sonic]], starting from his fighter and Final Smash introduction in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. The Chaos Emeralds are the objects that cause Sonic to transform into Super Sonic. The blue Chaos Emerald also appears in one of Sonic's victory poses if he wins a match. In ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', the Chaos Emeralds are featured in each game's respective Super Sonic trophy.

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', when activated as an [[Assist Trophy]], [[Shadow the Hedgehog]] holds a green Chaos Emerald to slow down time, by activating [[Chaos Control]].
