'''Starkisses''' are enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They live in [[Oho Ocean]], often lairing on the [[Oho Ocean Seabed|ocean's seabed]]. There is another Starkiss named [[Spangle]], who lives in the [[Oho Oasis|nearby oasis]].

==Battle==
Starkisses are found within the [[Oho Ocean Seabed]] and above the [[Gwarhar Lagoon]]. When they approach [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], they blow love hearts at them. They fight alongside with [[Malibut]]s. During the battle, they attack by blowing a kiss with their hints in the battle. The heart travels slowly in a wavy line, Mario or Luigi is unlikely to be [[Poison (status effect)|poisoned]] if they take the damage or they take minimal damage to it. However, the heart can [[POW-Down|mess]] [[DEF-Down|up your]] [[SPD-Down|stats]].

===Strategy===
The Starkiss folds its arms when it is targeting [[Luigi]] and does not fold its arms when it is aiming at [[Mario]]. Mario and Luigi can dodge it by [[Jump|jumping]] over it, even if they stomp on the heart.
