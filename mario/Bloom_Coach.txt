'''Bloom Coach''' is a medium weight [[kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' owned by [[Princess Daisy]]. The kart is the same color as her dress and that it is flower-based, much like herself. This kart is much like the [[Heart Coach]], due to its appearance. This kart can be unlocked by finishing the 50cc [[Flower Cup]] in first place. It has almost the same stats as the [[Green Fire]], but with less speed and more acceleration.
