'''Bowser's Miniature Castle''', also known as his '''Second Prison''' is a location that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. It is a small prison castle within [[Princess Peach's Castle (Illumination)|Peach's Castle]] where [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] resided after being forced to eat a [[Mini Mushroom (Illumination)|blue mushroom]] at the end of the [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie|previous film]]. It consists of a dining area on the first floor, the second floor where Bowser keeps his piano and a bonsai bush, and the top floor where he keeps his paintings. It replaced his [[Bowser's Bird Cage|Bird Cage]] from the mid credits of the [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie|first film]].

It was eventually destroyed after Bowser Jr. ripped out Peach's Castle from the ground with his UFO's tractor beam and accidentally dropping it on Honeyhive Galaxy, destroying it and Bowser's Miniature Castle in the process.

==Trivia==
*It's never explained why it replaced the birdcage from the first film nor that it has no bars to stop Bowser from escaping.
