The '''Bulbfish Island Guard''' is a character from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']] who serves as a minor antagonist-turned-ally in the [[Bulbfish Island]] arc. He is a [[Concordian]] man who guards the underground tunnel under orders from [[Chief Leyden]]'s assistant.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Bulbfish Island Guard is a fat green Concordian who wears red clothing.

=== Personality ===
The Bulbfish Island Guard believes in justice, but when he is defeated while quizzing the Bros., he opens up to them.

== History ==
The Bulbfish Island Guard makes his debut when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] first try to check the underground area of [[Bulbfish Island]] for clues on who stole the Bulbfish eggs after their arrival on the island. As they question the Bulbfishians, one mentions that Leyden's assistant hired a security guard to prevent anyone from accessing the underground. Approaching him provokes the guard, who stops the Bros. from going any further. When Mario explains that he and Luigi are looking for who stole the Bulbfish eggs, the Guard decides to test whether the Bros. are really investigating the crime. He then proposes a quiz, with the questions being the following:

# Who are the top suspects on the island right now?
## Chief Leyden
## Assistant
## Strange couple (correct answer)
## Secret Snatcher
# What isn't a major rule of Bulbfish Island?
## "Go to bed early."
## "Do a good deed each day."
## "Be full of energy and vitality."
## "Be single-minded and focused." (correct answer)
# What is under the "useless light?" (The Guard admits that this question has a bit of a contradiction. The correct answer is the notebook, which the Bros. show to him if it is in their possession.)

If the Bros. answer his questions correctly, the guard finds that they really do wish to solve the crime. He allows them to keep the book, allowing them to take notes of clues. The guard then gets out of their way, after which the Bros. enter the underground. If spoken to, he gives a warning about [[Foopfly|monsters]] [[Capnap#Variations|lurking]] underground. He also advises that they do not mess with the assistant's belongings.

The Guard is later confronted for his key when [[Prince Chilliam]] and [[Princess Burnadette]] request to be freed from wrongful imprisonment in their cell. He refuses to hand over the key, stating that if he hands it over, he would be fired. Mario and Luigi explain that they wish to find the real culprit and that Chilliam and Burnadette have been framed. The Guard then tells them to complete his final challenge, which involves getting information from his brother, who witnessed the theft firsthand. Mario and Luigi do so with help from someone named the "Secret Snatcher." Upon making their way back to the entrance to the tunnel, the Bros. confront the Guard again. Upon hearing this, the Guard acknowledges this, but worries about his salary. He then apologises for not making justice, which he claimed to love, his highest priority. However, he is cheered up Snoutlet. The Guard attempts to hand over his key, but is attacked by a [[Mettozokettohei#Būmeranzokettohei|Būmeranzokettohei]] and poisoned with [[Glohm]], succumbing to the effects. The Guard flees, but the Bros. find his key nearby, obtaining it and using it to spring Chilliam and Burnadette and explore the area beyond the cells.
