'''Logged In''' is [[Young Cricket]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. The microgame shares the same music with [[Plane and Simple]]. The name is a pun of "logged in", which means signing in on the internet.

== Gameplay ==
The player must saw through the log. If the player saws in the log poorly or quickky, the saw will bend. Sometimes, there will be a heart engraved on the log. The wood may rarely be a bamboo piece.
*'''Level 1: '''There is a thin piece of wood to saw.
*'''Level 2:''' There is a medium-sized piece of wood to saw.
*'''Level 3:''' There is a thick piece of wood to saw.
