'''''Densetsu no Stafy 3''''' is a [[Game Boy Advance]] game featuring [[Starfy]] as the main protagonist. It is the third game within the ''[[w:c:nintendo:The Legendary Starfy|The Legendary Starfy]]'' series, and it was released in 2004, exclusively in Japan like the previous two games.

Though ''Densetsu no Stafy 3'' is not a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game in any form, it features a guest appearance from [[Wario]], who assists [[Starfy]] during part of his adventure. Here, Wario is mainly based on his appearance from ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.
