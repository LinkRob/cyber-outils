The '''Green Apple Kart''' is a [[kart]] that appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Green Apple Kart, par its name, is a green variation of the [[Apple Kart]], and with teal accents on the taillights. The kart also comes equipped with golden variations of the [[Wood]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The Green Apple Kart is classified as a High-End kart that debuted in the [[Flower Tour]]. It increases the duration of [[Drafting|Slipstream]] boosts and the points earned for getting them.
