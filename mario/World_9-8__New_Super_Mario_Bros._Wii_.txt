'''World 9-8''' is the final [[level]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' found in [[World 9 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9]] and is the eighth and final secret level. It is unlocked after collecting all the star coins in [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]]. Once you beat this level, you have completed the game 100%.

== Overview==
The most prominent features of the stage are the bouncing clouds and the King Bills, which only appear in this stage.

==Layout==
This is a sky level reminiscent of [[Coin Heaven]]. In it, the "floor" is made of several Bouncy Clouds that let Mario jump every time he falls on them. A little ahead, two [[? Block]]s are found, one of which contains a [[Propeller Mushroom]]. A little more forward, the first [[Star Coin]] is found surrounded by [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] and after this part, a [[Roulette Block]] is found surrounded by five Fuzzies, one being big. Below them, a ? Block from the right contains a [[Mini Mushroom]] that can help the player to access high places. After that, the second Star Coin is found alongside several Fuzzies.

Forward, [[King Bill]]s begin to break down walls so Mario can continue his progress. The level forewarns the player of the King Bills with large Bullet Bill-shaped coin formations. The third Star Coin is found stuck between walls that a King Bill destroys. Arriving at the end of stage, a last King Bill appears near the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]], where it can be destroyed if the player jumps on the flagpole before being hit by it. A [[P Switch]] found in a nearby [[Brick Block]] indicates this last King Bill with a [[Blue Coin]] arrangement instead. This course has the background from [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7]].

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Not long after the beginning of the level are three rail-riding Fuzzies surrounding the first Star Coin, along with several other Coins. Mario and co. must jump to the Star Coin from a Cloud at the right time to avoid touching any Fuzzies.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the area with the first P Switch and several Brick Blocks, the second Star Coin is rail-riding behind three Fuzzies. Mario and co. must again jump from a Cloud at the right time to get it without touching a Fuzzy.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is blocked off by many blocks in the area where two King Bills emerge and break all of the blocks mentioned above. Mario and co. must wait for the King Bills to destroy the blocks, then fall to the Star Coin.
