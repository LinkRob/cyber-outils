'''See Monkey?''' is a single-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Opening==
One end of the lake is shown, where two of the [[Ukiki]]s having to be rescued are also seen, as the camera pans to the character on the opposite end.

==Gameplay==
The player's character is in a [[speedboat]], which they must use to try and get all ten Ukikis stranded in the water within the [[time limit]], which is 50 seconds in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode. If the player's character hits a rock, they are temporarily stunned, although the rock breaks and sinks into the water. If the player's character rescues every Ukiki on time, the player wins at the minigame, but if they do not, the player loses.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|Pad}}: Move left or right
*{{button|gba|A}}: Accelerate

==Flavor text==
*Rules: ''Rescue the li'l Ukikis by racing up to them in your speedboat.''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The minigame's name could be a pun on either "{{w|Sea-Monkeys|Sea Monkey Shrimp}}" or the phrase "Monkey see, monkey do."
