'''Gold Bowser Statues''' are golden statues of [[Bowser]] and enemies that first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. They appear in many of the game's castles.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Super Mario World'' ===
Gold Bowser Statues appear in ''Super Mario World'' as enemies found in castles. Unlike the [[Gray Bowser Statue]]s, Gold Bowser Statues do not shoot [[fireball]]s. Instead, they jump around [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] and damage them by contact, and the player can hop on them with a [[Spin Jump]], much like [[Thwimp]]s. But unlike Thwimps, Gold Bowser Statues follow Mario or Luigi. This makes it hard to avoid them near [[lava]] pits, but the player can defeat the statues by luring them into lava.

=== ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' ===
Gold Bowser Statues apppear on ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3'', but only on [[Bowser's Last Stand]], a [[World-e]] level. They act the same as the gold statues from ''Super Mario World''.

=== ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ===
[[File:Gold Bowser Statue (Super Mario Run).png|100px|left]]
A Gold Bowser Statue appears as a decoration for the player's [[Mushroom Kingdom|kingdom]] in ''Super Mario Run''. It can be obtained by getting a reward in the official [https://my.nintendo.com My Nintendo] website.
