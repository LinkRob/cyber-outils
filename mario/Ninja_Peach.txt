'''Ninja Peach''' is a power-up in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''

==Overview==
Ninja Peach involves [[Princess Peach]] being dressed as a ninja, wielding two kunai, and performing various ninjitsu moves. The form is comparable to [[Kung Fu Peach]].

===Physical appearance===
In her Ninja form, Peach wears a black, short-sleeved ninjutsu uniform with teal reflections, a large dark green scarf with a yellow swirl pattern, and a thick dark green belt with a golden cord held on the front by her usual brooch. Her legs are outfitted with thick, baggy pants with a thin cut on both of her thighs, and she wears a pair of red zōri on her feet with black socks. She is equipped with long black palmless gloves that have openings for her middle and index fingers. Her hair is worn in a high ponytail held up with a thin white band, and her earrings are significantly smaller but still the same color is blue.
