'''Red ? Blocks''' are special type of [[? Block]]s that appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
===''[[Super Mario World]]''===
Its first appearance was in ''Super Mario World'', and there are only 2 in the game, found only in the [[Front Door]]/[[Back Door]] stages of the [[Valley of Bowser]]. This block is special and served only as activating/desactivating the spotlight in the dark room, which allowed to see in the dark. Unlike the traditional ? block, it doesn't turn into an [[Empty Block]] after being hit, as such the spotlight can be toggled on and off indefinitely.

===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
[[File:Redblock.png|right]]
Red ? Blocks are found throughout the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] where one contains a [[Badge]] inside. Some Red ? Blocks are found in plain sight (such as the one on [[Goomba Road]]) while others are hidden that require a puzzle to be solved first to reveal it.
