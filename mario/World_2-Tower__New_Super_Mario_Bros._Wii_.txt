'''World 2-Fortress''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Overview==
This tower heavily features metallic [[Chain-Link|fence]]s that the player can cling on. As the player advances, purple fireballs come from above. They can be taken out with ice balls if the player has an [[Ice Flower]]. At the end of the tower, [[Roy Koopa]] is fought. This tower only feature [[Dry Bones]] as enemies.
==Walkthrough==
The level starts with two doors, which lead to a room with a [[P Switch]] that make [[Blue Coin]]s appear, and a remote-controlled moving metallic fence that [[Mario]] must use to get them. The entire level is based around the use of metallic fences to progress, while avoiding Dry Bones, [[Spike Trap|spikes]] and purple projectiles launched from above by Roy Koopa. At the top of the first section is a door that leads to a [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] and the beginning of the second section. In a hidden area to the right of the last ? Block, is a [[Red Ring]] with another metallic fence. At the end of the level, Mario must battle Roy Koopa. 

During the fight, Roy attacks by jumping and ground pounding, Roy Koopa has a very nasty ground pound attack just like Mario but unlike Mario's ground pound however, Mario will become stunned and vulnerable to Roy's attacks if he is standing on the ground when Roy lands. He then shoots magic projectiles out of his wand. Roy spins around in his shell trying to hit Mario when stomped on so be careful. He is defeated after being stomped on three times and then retreats to the castle.
==Enemies==

* [[Dry Bones]] - 13
* [[Roy Koopa]] (boss)

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Shortly before the final [[Warp Door]] in the first area, there is a split path between a huge midair platform with two grate platforms. Mario and co. should take the right path and continue jumping up until the first Star Coin can be seen on the left path. Once at the top, Mario and co. can obtain the Star Coin by jumping to it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': To the right of the first [[Dry Bones]] after the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], the second Star Coin rests in a hidden area.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Above the stairs-like platform with three Dry Bones are three grate platforms, and the third Star Coin is floating in front of the left-most one. Mario and co. should jump to the grate and grab onto it at the right time to obtain the Star Coin without touching the spikes.
