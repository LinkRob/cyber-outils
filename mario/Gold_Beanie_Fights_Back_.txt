'''Gold Beanie Fights Back!''' is the sixth level of [[Joke's End]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 26.

==Layout==
The 1st battle contains 8 [[Clumph]]s. The enemy captain is a Clumph.

The 2nd battle contains 4 Clumphs and 4 [[Bomb Boo]]s. The enemy captain is a Clumph.

The final battle contains 2 Clumphs, 5 Bomb Boos, and 1 [[Gold Beanie]]. The enemy captain is the Gold Beanie.

*Bomb Boo (Lv. 26-27)
