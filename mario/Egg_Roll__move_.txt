'''Egg Roll''' is [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s side special move throughout the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, since its first appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''.

==Description==
Egg Roll involves Yoshi curling into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] and rolling around the stage. Yoshi can [[jump]] while using the move. The player can cancel Egg Roll prematurely by pressing the special move button (usually the B button). If an opponent is hit by Yoshi as he rolls, they take damage.
