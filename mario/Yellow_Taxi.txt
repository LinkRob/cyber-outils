The '''Yellow Taxi''' is a kart in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Yellow Taxi resembles a medallion taxi. 

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
It makes its debut in the [[New York Tour]] in New York Pipe 1 along with [[Pauline]] and the [[Fare Flier]]. It comes along with a special skill, [[Jump Boost Plus]].
