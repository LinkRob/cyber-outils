}}'''Grumpy Kong''' is one of the main antagonists of the game ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. He is a high-ranking member of [[VoidCo]] who works alongside [[Void Kong]] and [[Poppy Kong]] where they steal all of the bananas of [[Ingot Isle]] and attempt to drill to the [[Planet Core]] in order to wish for untold riches.

==Appearance==
Grumpy Kong is a very large ape (even bigger than [[Donkey Kong]]) who has red fur and wears a mining outfit. He is also seen carrying a large pickaxe further indicating he is a miner.

==Powers and abilities==
Grumpy Kong's well known ability aside from his mining skills is his ability to construct things out of rocks. Most notably, Grumpy is the one responsible for creating all of the enemies that Donkey Kong and [[Pauline]] must face throughout their adventures down into the planet.{{DKBanNav}}
