'''Bill Bounce''' is a minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It also had an [[Bill Bounce (Adobe Flash)|Adobe Flash version]] on the defunct North American ''Mario Party Advance'' website.

==Opening==
The player's character falls into the arena from above before the minigame starts.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to [[jump]] on as many [[Bullet Bill]]s as possible within the [[time limit]] for [[point]]s. The Bullet Bills are shot out of [[Bill Blaster]]s on the left and right sides each. By default, the player's character can earn 10 points by [[jump]]ing on a Bullet Bill, but the total can be incremented by ten for each Bullet Bill jumped onto until maxing out at 200 points. If a Bullet Bill hits the player's character, they become stunned for a few seconds. The minigame ends once the time runs out. 

In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player has to earn at least 1,000 points within 30 seconds to win. In Mini-Game Attack, the minigame is longer, occurring for 60 seconds, and the player has to earn 2,500 points within that time frame.

In Play Land, the time limit begins at 60 seconds, like in Mini-Game Attack, but it is possible to increase it by a few seconds for every five Bullet Bills [[stomp]]ed on: two seconds are added for the first five Bullet Bills; three seconds for the next five Bullet Bills; four seconds for the third set of five Bullet Bills; and lastly five seconds for the fourth set of five Bullet Bills.

==Controls==
* {{button|gba|Pad}}: Move left or right
* {{button|gba|A}}: Jump
