'''Jesters''' are enemies found in [[Booster Tower]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Battle==
In battle, Jesters mainly attack using card techniques such as [[Full House]] and [[Wild Card]]. However, they are capable of using [[Flame Stone]] as well.
