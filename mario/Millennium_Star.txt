The '''Millennium Star''' is a character who appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. He is the "true" Millennium Star, which is learned after the [[Fake Millennium Star|fake one]] is defeated in a [[Stardust Battle|battle]].

==History==
The Millennium Star is only seen near at the end of [[Story Mode]] after he was impressed by the player's skills during the course of the game. He then awards the title of "Superstar" to the player and will appear in the corner of the screen during the end credits. As soon the credits end, the Millennium Star flies at the screen where it then shows a near real-life Mario Party board with the words "The End" at the bottom.
