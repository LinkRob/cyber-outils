A '''Mix Flower''' is a [[Bros. Item]] which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It involves [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]] each standing in a corner, surrounding the enemy, and shooting several [[fireball]]s upward, each combining to form a large fireball to land down on the target. A Mix Flower can be bought from certain shops for 180 [[coin]]s.
