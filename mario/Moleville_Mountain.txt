'''Moleville Mountain''' is a minigame which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be accessed from the [[Moleville Mines]]. Playing the minigame is mandatory the first time, but later plays cost ten [[coin]]s each.

==Gameplay==
[[Mario]] has to ride a [[Mine Cart|minecart]] on the tracks, and he can control it by leaning in the direction he wishes to travel. If Mario does not lean or is going too fast upon approaching a corner, he crashes, costing him time. Mario can collect coins along the way, which he can keep after completing the minigame. [[Dash Mushroom|Mushrooms]] are placed along the track, and they grant a speed boost.

==Controls==
* {{button|snes|b}}: Jump, which is necessary for avoiding pits and collecting mushrooms and coins.
* {{button|snes|a}} or {{button|snes|y}}: Brake, to avoid derailing and losing time if the mine cart goes too fast before turns, especially sharp ones.
* {{button|snes|x}}: Use a Mushroom to give the minecart a speed boost.
* {{button|snes|l}} or {{button|snes|r}}: Honk the minecart horn, although this is merely a gag with no gameplay effect.
