}}A '''Shlurp''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Shlurps are caterpillar-like enemies that are only found in [[Gloam Valley]] and [[Merlee's Basement]] of the game. They move around very slowly on the ground and will eat [[Mario]] or [[Princess Peach]] if they get too close. Shlurps also have a very strong exoskeleton rendering attacks from the outside useless. The only way to defeat Shlurps is to have them eat [[Boomer]] and explode them from the inside.
