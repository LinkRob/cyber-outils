The '''Helicopter''' is a recurring transformation used by the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] throughout the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Description==
This power-up turns a Yoshi into the likeness of a helicopter, with whirling blades allowing him to fly around. It appears in every game of the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}.

In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', [[Morph Bubble]]s depicting a Helicopter transform Yoshi into this form, but in ''Yoshi's New Island'' this is done from a [[Whirly Gate]] instead. Upon transforming into a Helicopter, the Yoshi can make use of this form by flying until either reaching a nearby [[Yoshi Block]] or upon hitting a [[Time Limit]]. The Helicopter is often used to locate [[Coin]]s, [[Smiley Flower]]s and secret paths. Like the other transformations, if the Time Limit has been reached, the Yoshi spirals back to the location of the Morph Bubble and returns to his regular form. If a Yoshi hits an enemy while in Helicopter form, he falls down in a straight line for about a second, during which he cannot be moved around.

==In {{series|Yoshi's Island}}==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
The Helicopter form is present in several [[level]]s, with [[Watch Out Below!]] being its first level appearance.

===''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''===
The Helicopter functions identically as in the previous game.
{{sectstub}}

===''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''===
The Helicopter once again makes an appearance, but is now controlled by tilting the [[Nintendo 3DS]] rather than by using the D-Pad.

==In ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''==
A green Helicopter makes a cameo appearance in both [[Yoshi Circuit]] and [[Baby Park]]. It is an actual vehicle, not a Yoshi transformed into one, and the Helicopter does not appear in subsequent iterations of the courses in later ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' installments.
