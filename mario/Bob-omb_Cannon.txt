The {{Nihongo|'''Bob-omb Cannon'''|ボムへいキャノン|Bomuhei Kyanon|Bob-omb Cannon}} is a blaster that launches [[Bob-omb]]s. It appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' as a special item.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The Bob-omb Cannon appears as a special item in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. The Bob-omb Cannon automatically launches a Bob-ombs.
