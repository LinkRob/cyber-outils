'''Lightning''' is a [[Captain Command]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' and in the [[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]] campaign of [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'']]. 

== Description ==
When Lightning is used, all enemies are shrunken and weakened. This lasts until they are hit one time.

== Flavor text ==

* In-game description: Shrinks and weakens enemies for a limited time
