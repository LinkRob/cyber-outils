'''Takashi Nagasako''' (born February 24, 1964) has been the voice of [[Donkey Kong]] through the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' games since 2004 starting with [[Mario Power Tennis]] until 2025 with [[Mario Kart World]] where he was replaced by [[Kōji Takeda]]. He also provides the voice of [[w:c:sonic:Big the Cat|Big the Cat]] in the ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic the Hedgehog (series)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' series and [[w:c:zelda:Ganandorf|Ganondorf]] in the ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' series.
