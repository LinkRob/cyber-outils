'''Cyber Slick''' are an unlockable set of [[tire]]s in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Cyber Slick's design is similar to the [[Slick]] tires, the only difference being that they have a purple and green color scheme.

== In {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
[[File:MK8DX Dry Bowser Kart.png|thumb|right|[[Dry Bowser]]'s [[Pipe Frame]] equipped with the Cyber Slick tires in ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''.]]
In both ''Mario Kart 8'' and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', the Cyber Slick can be unlocked by getting a certain amount of [[coin]]s. It offers a slight boost in weight, while hindering acceleration slightly, and and traction heavily. Its stats are identical with the Slick tires. In terms of hidden stats, its water speed is slightly improved, while its air speed has been worsened, and its [[Mini-Turbo]] has also been lowered, in comparison to the Slick tires. In ''Deluxe'', some of the Cyber Slick's tires are slightly altered. These tires are never used by CPU racers.

In ''Mario Kart Tour'', the Cyber Slick tires are equipped on the [[Radish Rider]], [[Dark Clown]], and the [[Trickster]].

== Other appearances ==
In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', there is an icon of the Cyber Slick tires among several different [[Kart]] tires in the custom kart builder that the [[Kong Family]] had built in their [[Donkey Kong Island|Jungle Kingdom]].
