The '''Leaf Veil''' is [[Ninja (character)|Ninja]]'s [[Special Shot]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
To have Ninja initiate the shot, the player must tap out an "N" on the Touch Screen twice.

Ninja begins the shot by bouncing the ball to his left, then in a high arc to his right. The ball glows white throughout this part. Ninja returns the ball to the center and creates a purple energy disk. Four blue flames appear on the disk and surround the ball. Ninja shoots the ball, now colored blue, toward the basket, disappears in a flash of leaves, and then reappears near the ball to slam it into the basket.
If shot outside the 3-point line, instead of Ninja, a teammate needs to complete the dunk.

If Leaf Veil is used outside the three-point line, another teammate has to dunk the ball on behalf of Ninja.
