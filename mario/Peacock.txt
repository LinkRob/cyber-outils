[[File:Peacock Logo.png|thumb|180x180px|Current logo]]'''Peacock''' is an American over-the-top subscription streaming service owned and operated by Peacock TV, LLC, a subsidiary of NBCUniversal Media Group, part of Comcast's NBCUniversal. Named after the NBC logo, the service launched on July 15, 2020.

Peacock streams [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]] from [[Universal Pictures]], [[Illumination Entertainment]], and [[Nintendo]].<ref>https://www.peacocktv.com/watch/asset/movies/the-super-mario-bros.-movie/2380cfdb-46f6-3e1a-a9f7-0a744f5027ca</ref>
