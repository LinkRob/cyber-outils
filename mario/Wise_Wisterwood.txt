'''Wise Wisterwood''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Wise Wisterwood is a large tree with large eyes right above the door that acts as his mouth.

===Personality===
Wise Wisterwood is a very wise and kind tree who watches over [[Flower Fields]]. He is more than willing to share his knowledge about things to people and is shown to be very grateful once Mario saves Flower Fields. Wise Wisterwood also has a sense of humor often making tree puns whenever he is in a good mood.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Wise Wisterwood plays a major role in Chapter 6 of ''Paper Mario''. He acts as the "doorway" from Flower Fields to [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. As soon [[Mario]] enters Flower Fields, Wise Wisterwood greets him and tells Mario that Flower Fields is in danger due to it being taken over by [[Huff N. Puff]]. He further explains that the dark clouds have blocked off the [[Sun (character)|Sun]] and Flower Fields is in danger of dying out. Mario agrees to help out and a grateful Wise Wisterwood explains to Mario that he must get up to [[Cloudy Climb]] and defeat Huff N. Puff to free Flower Fields. To do that, Mario must get three items: the [[Magical Bean]], the [[Fertile Soil]], and the [[Miracle Water]]. However, he must also somehow stop the dark clouds from producing to get the Sun to come out again and help grow the beanstalk.

Once Mario has gathered the three items and bought back the Sun after destroying the [[Puff Puff Machine]], Wise Wisterwood congratulates Mario for a job well done, but then says it isn't over yet as Mario must grow the [[beanstalk]] and climb up onto Cloudy Climb to fight Huff N. Puff. After Mario defeats Huff N. Puff and frees the next [[Star Spirit]], a pleased Wise Wisterwood thanks Mario and allows him to come back to Flower Fields any time he wants.

Wise Wisterwood also sends a letter to Mario telling him how the others are doing in Flower Fields after saving them. He mentions that [[Lily (Paper Mario)|Lily]] is in her own world as usual, [[Rosie]] is spreading a rumor about Mario being interested in her, and [[Petunia]] is singing praises about Mario. He also sends a letter to [[Minh T.]] thanking her for taking care of the flowers around the Flower Gate.
