'''Willy''' is one of the [[Royal Tennis Academy]] students in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. His doubles partner is [[Sheri]]. Willy's [[Offensive Power Shot]] is the [[Summoner Shot]] and his [[Defensive Power Shot]] is the [[Hand-Power Save]]. He has two older brothers, [[A. Coz]] and [[B. Coz]].
