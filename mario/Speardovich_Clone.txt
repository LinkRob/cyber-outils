'''Speardovich Clone''' is a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is the exact copy of [[Yaridovich|Speardovich]]. They also pose as the [[Toad (species)|Toad]] citizens of [[Seaside Town]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Yaridovich SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
The Speardovich Clone has the exact same appearance as Speardovich.
{{br|left}}

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
===Battle===
Speardovich Clone only appears when Yarivodich uses the [[Mirage attack!]] move and appears to fool Mario and his team to attack it. There is three ways to tell which is the Clone: The Speardovich Clone uses different special attacks, it has weaker attack and defense, and its [[Psychopath]] message ''"I'm not the real McCoy!"'' gives him away.
