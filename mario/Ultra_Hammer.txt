The '''Ultra Hammer''' is the strongest [[hammer]] upgrade featured in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games. The Ultra Hammer first appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Like other hammers, the Ultra Hammer can generally only be used by [[Mario]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[File:Ultra Hammer SMRPG artwork.png|thumb|left|''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' artwork]]
The Ultra Hammer is obtained in [[Smithy Factory]]. It is located within a [[treasure chest]] shortly before the battle against [[Count Down]]. The Ultra Hammer is only usable by [[Mario]], and it increases his [[Attack]] power. The Ultra Hammer shares the same sprite as the regular hammer, but its artwork depicts it with a light yellow head, metal linings, and the word "ULTRA" capitalized in red text on the side.
{{br|left}}
==In the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
[[File:Ultra Hammer PM64 sprite.png|frame|left]]
The Ultra Hammer is obtained by Mario in [[Mt. Lavalava]]. Aside from being a stronger variation of the Hammer, the Ultra Hammer allows Mario to smash [[Metal Block]]s on the overworld.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
The Ultra Hammer is obtained within a chest in [[Rogueport]]'s plaza. It can similarly destroy [[Stone Block]]s on the overworld. 

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
[[File:Ultra Hammer MLSS sprite.png|frame|left]]
Mario and [[Luigi]] are each given their own Ultra Hammer from the [[Hammerhead Bros.]] as an upgrade to their [[Super Hammer]].
