'''Hotheads''' are enemies that appear infrequently in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Hotheads are large, orange balls of sparking energy. They have a pair of black eyes.

===Behavior===
A Hothead slowly travels around a platform. It moves slower than a [[Li'l Sparky]], but is usually followed by them.

===Habitats===
In the platforming games, Hotheads only appear in [[fortress]] and [[castle]] levels.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario World''====
[[File:Hothead_SMW.png|left|frame]]
[[File:Hothead Castle.png|thumb|200px|A Hothead below the platform with the Li'l Sparkies in ''Super Mario World'']]
Hotheads first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. They are found in [[6 Wendy's Castle|#6 Wendy's Castle]] and [[Front Door]], specifically in rooms with [[Li'l Sparky|Li'l Sparkies]]. Hotheads slowly move around platforms, but they do so slower than Li'l Sparkies, presumably owing to their larger size. Hotheads and can be defeated by [[Invincible Mario]] or [[Cape Mario|Caped Mario]]'s Body Press. Like various other stronger enemies and certain obstacles, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can [[Spin Jump]] on a Hothead without taking damage.
{{br|left}}

====''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''====
[[File:Hotheads and Lil Sparkies.png|thumb|left|Hotheads and Lil Sparkies in ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3'']]
In ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', Hotheads are found in the [[World-e]] levels [[A Sky-High Adventure]] and [[Puzzling Pipe Maze]]. Unlike in ''Super Mario World'', they can be defeated by [[Statue Mario]]'s stomp or by [[hammer]]s thrown by [[Hammer Mario]].

====''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''====
[[File:Hothead SMBW.png|thumb|A Hothead in ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']]
After a 20-year absence from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]], Hotheads reappear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' alongside [[Li'l Sparky|Li'l Sparkies]].
{{br}}
===''Super Princess Peach''===
[[File:Hothead SPP sprite.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Hothead Power.png|thumb|Hotheads in ''Super Princess Peach'']]
Hotheads reappear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. They first appear in the level [[Hoo's Wood 2-8]] and can be later found in [[level]]s of [[Shriek Mansion]], [[Fury Volcano]], and [[Bowser's Villa]]. Hotheads behave similarly to how they act in ''Super Mario World'' and often appear alongside Lil Sparkies once more. Hotheads and are invincible to all of [[Princess Peach|Peach]]'s attacks. Due to this, Hotheads appear in the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] the moment they are encountered for the first time.
{{br|left}}
==Crossover appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
[[File:Hothead Brawl artwork.png|thumb|120px|left|Hothead's artwork in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
[[File:Hothead Wii U.jpg|thumb|A Hothead in ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'']]
Hotheads appear as an item in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. Like in ''Super Mario World'', they stick to the stage when thrown and burn anyone that touches it, and also release powerful sparks periodically when moving. It also grows larger if hit with fire and electric attacks, an ability it was never shown to have in the ''Mario'' franchise, and therefore deal more damage. Hotheads move quickly around the platform they are on, but can also just fall off entirely. A Hothead disappears after a somewhat long period of time. In ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'', a Hothead also appears as a support [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]], equipping the fighter with a Hothead at the start of battle when used.

Hothead's design in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' is more realistic-looking, but ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U'' and ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' give it a design closer to that of ''Super Mario World''.
