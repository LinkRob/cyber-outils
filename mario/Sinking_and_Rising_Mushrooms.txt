'''Sinking and Rising Mushrooms''' are an object in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
Sinking and Rising Mushroom Platforms are a type of [[Mushroom Platform]] that either rise or fall when stood on, depending on their color. In ''New Super Mario Bros.'', the yellow ones fall and the red ones rise, but in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', the falling type was changed to light-blue and the rising type was recolored to pink.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
Sinking and Rising Mushrooms exclusively appear in [[World 5-4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5-4]].

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
Sinking and Rising Mushrooms appear in [[World 5-4 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5-4]] and the [[Gold Mushroom Pack]]'s [[Third Course (Gold Mushroom Pack)|Third Course]].
