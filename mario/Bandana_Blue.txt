'''Bandana Blues''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are sharks that dress as pirates and they work for [[Jonathan Jones]] at the [[Sunken Ship]]. They are stronger than the [[Bandana Red]]s and serve as Jonathan Jones' personal bodyguards since four of them aid him in battle. After the battle, Bandana Blues aid Jonathan Jones in preventing [[Yaridovich]], one of the members of the [[Smithy Gang]] from escaping into the [[Sea]] with a [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]].

==Battle==
In battle, Bandana Blues act the same as their weaker counterparts and they can only use [[Pierce]] as their special move. They can also attack physically with their spears.

===Strategy===
Bandana Blues are weak against both fire and thunder moves so [[Mario]]'s [[Super Flame]] and [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] will be effective here. However, if all of the Bandana Blues are defeated first, Jonathan Jones will force Mario into a one-on-one fight with him. This is often regarded as the more difficult route to fight so it is recommended to keep at least one Bandana Blue alive to avoid the one-on-one battle.
