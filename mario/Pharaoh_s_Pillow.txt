The '''Pharaoh's Pillow''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Pharaoh's Pillow appears to be a rather used-up pillow as [[Wario]] describes being very thin.

==History==
The Pharaoh's Pillow is one of three of the Pharaoh's treasures that the [[Sphinx (character)|Sphinx]] tasks Wario to find in order to get important information from it (along with the [[Pharaoh's Underwear]] and the [[Pharaoh's Nose Hair]]). The Pharaoh's Pillow can  be found in a purple treasure chest inside of the ray and ant exhibit of the [[Smithsnorian Museum]].
