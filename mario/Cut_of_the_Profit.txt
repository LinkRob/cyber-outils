'''Cut of the Profit''' is a [[Microgame]] that can be found in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' as the seventh Boss Microgame. It uses the Crocodile form for its gameplay. [[Young Cricket]] has the Microgame in his level, Battle at Flashfreeze Cape.

== Layout ==
The protagonist is a robotic drone that fires a laser beam, and the microgame takes place in a cave with several walls that contain gold bricks.

== Gameplay ==
When the player moves their arms, the drone moves his arms the same way, and therefore the laser. He must cut out all of the gold by cutting out the bricks that support it; if he zaps a red wall, the player automatically loses. After a few walls are cut through, the drone reaches the exit and must clear a path to the door; once the path is cleared, the player wins. There is a time limit; if the timer expires, the player loses. Level 1 has simple patterns and few red walls (none in the first wave), and the player has 50 seconds there. For Level 2, more red walls can be found with complex cutting patterns, and the time limit is a full minute. Level 3 has a more complex pattern with more red walls and a 70-second time limit.

When two players are used, two robot drones have to use their lasers to cut out the gold, and if one drone dies, the microgame is [[Softlock|automatically lost, even if the other drone survives]].

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Name ===
The microgame's name refers to when the profits of a company are distributed among employees.
