'''Luigi''' is one of the titular two main protagonists (alongside [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]]) of the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]], serving as the titular overarching protagonist of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KydqdKKyGEk |title=The Super Mario Bros. Movie | Official Teaser Trailer |accessdate=2022-10-07 |work=YouTube |date=2022-10-07}}</ref> and as one of the film's two deuteragonists (alongside [[Yoshi (Illumination)|Yoshi]]) of ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. He is the younger twin brother of Mario and an ally of Princess Peach and Toad.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Luigi's appearance resembles his [[Luigi|video game counterpart]], but far more detailed. He now wears a green collared shirt rather than the basic green tee his video game version dons, his shoes now have laces, and his overalls now have a pocket on the front.

=== Personality traits ===
Luigi has a cowardly personality such as the instance where he ran away from Dry Bones. While Mario is calm and confident, Luigi is nervous and hesitant. Despite his cowardlyness, he still remains optimistic. Also due to their strong relationship, he is shown to be caring towards Mario and is seemingly willing to help him out while working or whenever the situation calls for it. Like his brother, he also tends to come off as optimistic, at least when not in dangerous or life-threatening situations, believing along with him that their independent plumbing business would work out, despite some of their family not believing in them (expect their mother, who supports them all the time).

== Biography ==
Italian brothers Luigi and Mario worked for [[Foreman Spike (Illumination)|Foreman Spike]] before they opened their new business, [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]], in [[Brooklyn]], [[New York City]]. After being laughed at by Spike, their uncles, and dismissed by their father following the airing of their new job's commercial, Luigi follows his brother Mario to a manhole in the city causing flooding. They enter the manhole to fix the flooding in an attempt to kickstart their plumbing legacy. After fixing the pressure valve causing the flooding, they fall deeper into the sewers and wind up in a mysterious man-made pipe system that presumably no one had maintained "for years".

Luigi splits off from his brother briefly to explore and gets sucked into a pipe that leads to the [[Mushroom Earth]]. Mario follows after him in the pipe, grabbing his arm while promising that they will be fine as long as they stick together. Unfortunately the two brothers get separated, as a result, Luigi exits through a different pipe and ends up in the Dark Lands.

Luigi explores this area and was being chased by [[Dry Bones]]. After running away from Dry Bones, he entered a building and blocked the doors with his own body. Unfortunately, Bowser's minions were behind him and Luigi yells in fear. Then, he is taken hostage and is brought to [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]. Bowser interrogates him about his brother. 

Later, Luigi is trapped inside a cage above a lava pool along with the [[Penguin King]] and [[Lumalee (Illumination)|Lumalee]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RjNcTBXTk4I|title=The Super Mario Bros. Movie | Final Trailer|author=Illumination|accessdate=10-03-2023|date=10-03-2023|work=YouTube}}</ref>

When Mario and Donkey Kong show up in the climax to stop Bowser from marrying Peach and killing his own prisoners, Luigi tries avoiding the lava he was going to be sunk into by climbing the chain attached to the cage, only to lose his grip, resulting in him nearly falling into the lava before Mario (in his flying tanooki suit) saves him from certain death. He gets more screentime when he and Mario end up back in Brooklyn when Mario mistakably used a giant Bullet Bill to make it explode in the space of cosmic warp holes.

As he, Donkey Kong, and Princess Peach play a more active role in fighting Bowser so he doesn't get the Power Star, Luigi, as a epic moment, used the lid of a sewage container from the road to protect Mario from getting burned alive from Bowser's weaponized flamethrowing mouth, he and Mario then get the power star themselves, and the two charge through the Koopa Army Bowser has sent to eliminate the brotherly duo. Single-handedly overpowering Bowser's minions, and defeat Bowser himself, destroying his castle in the process. Too tired to continue fighting, Peach then forces him to eat a mini-mushroom to shrink him and put him in a jar as punishment for his crimes. Meanwhile, the brothers are hailed as heroes by the people of Brooklyn, including their parents, Spike and Francis.

He and Mario are later revealed to have moved into a house in the Mushroom Kingdom where they work as the town's heroes and plumbers and use the Warp Pipe to travel back to Brooklyn anytime, they want.

==Trivia== 
*His phone's ringtone bears a resemblance to that of the opening theme of the 2001 ''Nintendo'' console, the GameCube.
* Luigi serves as the dude in distress for some time of the film until the beginning of the climax. This could be a reference to ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]],'' where he gets kidnapped in the postgame by [[Dry Bowser]].
*Luigi's official render is based off his [https://www.mariowiki.com/File:Luigiart.png pose] in artwork for ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.
* He is the second member of Mario and Luigi's family to be hugged by [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]], followed by [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]].
* Luigi’s voice actor also voices Benny in ''The LEGO Movie'' film series.
*In the video games King Boo has been established as Luigi's Nemesis. In the movie the two never interact.
* He is the fourth member to have used the Fire Flower to transform, followed by [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]], [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]], and [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]].
** Like Mario, Luigi only transformed into his fire form in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''.
* Like Donkey Kong, he never used the Ice Flower to transform into his ice form, because he does not get the chance to do it.
