'''Turnip Tantrum''' is the first level of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]].'' The level of the enemy squad is 5.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 4 [[Troopea]]s and 4 [[Shy Guy]]s. The enemy captain is a Troopea.

The final battle contains 3 Troopea and 5 Shy Guys. The enemy captain is a Shy Guy.

After the final battle, the player gains [[Captain Shy Guy (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Shy Guy]]. 

Replaying the battle can allow the player to gain up to one Shy Guy each time.
