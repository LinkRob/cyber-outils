The '''Barter Box''' is a special [[item]] only found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. It cannot be bought in an item shop or won in an item game. However, players can get it by buying an [[Item Bag]] at an item shop, finding it inside of a [[Hidden Block (Mario Party series)|Hidden Block]], or meeting [[Toad]] on an [[Item Space]].

When used, the Barter Box exchanges all of the player's items with the items of another player. The [[Plunder Chest]] works in a similar way; however, the Plunder Chest only steals one item. The Barter Box's ability is more similar to the much more common [[Swap Card]] of ''[[Mario Party 4]]''; however, the Swap Card's target is chosen by roulette.

==Flavor text==
*'''Manual description''' (page 23): ''"If you've got the Barter Box, you can swap all of your items for another player's items."''
