'''Monstrous Magnet''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Monstrous Magnets are large green magnets with one red "N" end and one blue "S" end, representing the North and South poles respectively. A Monstrous Magnet has eyes on the side of each pole.

===Behavior===
A Monstrous Magnet has the ability to float in midair, and it can fire electrical blasts of energy at [[Wario]].

==In ''Wario World''==
Monstrous Magnets are only encountered in [[Beanstalk Way]]. Here, Wario can stun a Monstrous Magnet by hitting it three times. After doing this, Wario can then grab the Monstrous Magnet and throw it on to a metal board, which it sticks to temporarily, allowing him to make his way up the platform. Similar enemies named [[Flying Spade]]s are encountered later on in [[Pecan Sands]].
