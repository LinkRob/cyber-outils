'''Fligby''' is a character in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Fligby is a green, gelatinous creature.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
Fligby only appears in the opening of [[Ashley]] & [[Red (WarioWare series)|Red]]'s story, [[Total Drag]]. Here, at [[Ashley's Mansion]], the duo stir a newt, an orb, an [[apple]], and a [[Mandrake|Nasty Garlic]] in a metal pot to create a potion. After this, the pot begins to bubble, a skull pops out of it, and then Fligby floats to the top. Ashley dejectedly says that they failed while reattempting their potion.
