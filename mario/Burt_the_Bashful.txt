'''Burt the Bashful''', also spelled '''Bert the Bashful''',<ref>[http://web.archive.org/web/20030402151900/http://www.nintendo.com/games/gamepage/artwork.jsp?gameId=1282 ''Super Mario Advance 3'' website, Web Archive]</ref> is a recurring boss in the [[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi franchise]]. He started out as a normal [[Burt]], but thanks to [[Kamek]], Burt grows to a massive size in the events of ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical Appearance===
Burt is a pale yellow creature with an orange nose and stubby feet. His trousers are blue and white, covering him halfway.

==History==
===''Yoshi'' franchise===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
[[File:SMW2 Burt the Bashful hiked.png|frame|left]]
Burt the Bashful is the first mid-boss seen in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|remake]], where he is found in [[Burt The Bashful's Fort]] half-way through World 1. At the end of the fort, [[Yoshi (species)|Yellow Yoshi]] encounters a [[Burt]] which is enlarged by [[Kamek]], turning it into Burt the Bashful. To defeat him, Yoshi must hit him with [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s six times; with each hit, Burt's pants continue to fall off, serving as an indication of his remaining health. Upon defeat, his pants entirely fall off and he turns red, blasts off and eventually implodes.

====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
During the introduction of ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', Burt the Bashful makes an appearance alongside ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''{{'}}s castle bosses and [[Tap-Tap the Red Nose]]. Additionally, the mid-bosses of [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]], the [[Big Burt Bros.]], are based on him. 

====''Yoshi's Woolly World / Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''====
[[File:Main_Boss_1_Burt_the_Bashful.png|thumb|Burt the Bashful's appearance in ''Yoshi's Woolly World''.]] 
Burt the Bashful later appears in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' as the boss of World 1 in [[Burt the Bashful's Castle]]. Just like in his original appearance, Burt is encountered as a regular-sized Burt but is enlarged by [[Kamek]]. Here, Burt has three phases. First, he jumps as in ''Yoshi's Island''. He occasionally summons two [[Shy Guy]]s as ammo. Burt can also destroy any Shy Guys in his way with his jumps. To damage Burt, Yoshi must first hit him with a yarn ball to stun him; upon being stunned, Burt falls onto his back and his trousers get a knot at their front, which Yoshi must unravel to actually deal damage. For the second phase, Burt gets two ball look-alikes, which jump around. During his final phase, he gets a third and fourth ball and summons three Shy Guys. Once Burt is entirely naked, the battle ends; his defeat animation involves him turning red in embarrassment, inflating, and rocketing around the room. In this game, not including Baby Bowser's second form, Burt the Bashful is the only boss that does not need to be [[Ground Pound]]ed to be defeated.

====''Yoshi's Crafted World''====
While Burt does not appear in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'', the player fights a similar boss called [[Burt the Ball]], which is a rubber version of him.

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
Burt the Bashful also appears as a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', and as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. His Spirit Battle takes place on the Super Happy Tree stage, and he is represented with [https://pacman.fandom.com/wiki/Pac-Man_(character) Pac-Man] in the plaid outfit with increased defense.

===''Super Mario-kun''===
[[File:BurttheBashfulSuperMarioKun.png|thumb|left|Burt the Bashful's appearance in the ''Super Mario-kun''.]]
In volume 14 of the ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', the volume based on ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', Burt the Bashful is the first boss [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and Yoshi encounter. He bounces around, smashing Mario in the process. Yoshi also attacks and ends up failing. An [[Egg Plant]] assists them, but the Yoshi Eggs simply bounce off Burt. Finally, Mario, Luigi, and Yoshi grab and pull down Burt's pants, revealing his genitals. Ashamed, he shrinks and starts flying off as a deflated balloon, causing havoc to Mario and Luigi.

==Trivia==
*Burt's sprites depict him differently from his artwork; notably, he has one curled hair in his sprites, but two drooping hairs in his artwork. Said artwork is reused for the [[Big Burt Bros.]], and the same inconsistency applies with them.
*Burt the Bashful makes a cameo appearance in the [[Nintendo Monopoly]].
*Not including [[Baby Bowser]], Burt the Bashful is one of the only two bosses from ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' that make an appearance in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', the other being [[Naval Piranha]].
