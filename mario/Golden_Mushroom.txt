The '''Golden Mushroom''' is an item that appears in some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, first seen in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. It is a rare [[Mushroom]], and it always gives the player a positive effect when used.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario World''====
[[File:SMWbetamushroom.gif|frame|left]]
Originally a flying Golden Mushroom would have appeared in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' but was [[List of Super Mario World pre-release and unused content|removed for unknown reasons]]. They are worth [[Extra life|extra lives]], like [[1-Up Mushroom]]s. The palette they use is actually the beige palette used by [[Monty Mole]]s, [[Floating Mine]]s, and kidnapped [[Yoshi Egg]]s.
{{br|left}}

====''New Super Mario Bros. 2''====
{{main|Gold Mushroom}}
Gold Mushrooms appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' in the [[Coin Rush]] mode and replace [[1-Up Mushroom]]s, and upon contact, Mario gets 50 coins (or 100 coins if he is [[Gold Mario]]). It lacks the crown from its contemporary ''Mario Kart'' appearances and the coloring is reversed from all other occurrences, with the spots being darker than the background.

===''Mario Kart'' series===
{{main|Golden Dash Mushroom}}
A specific Golden Mushroom, known as [[Golden Dash Mushroom]] in later titles, appears in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]] as a usable item. It gives the player an endless amount of speed boosts for a limited time

===''Super Mario'' (Kodansha manga)===
[[File:Mega Mole Crime Boss KC Mario.png|thumb|The Mega Mole leader with the Golden Mushroom.]]
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. 4: Super Mario World 6]]'', the main artifact exposed in the [[Hōmotsu-kan|Treasure Museum]] is a Golden Mushroom, a precious heirloom of the Toadstool family that is valued at 100,000 million yen. During the story, the [[Choropū-tachi]] steals it and frames [[Bowser]] for the theft. Mario is promoted to [[Mario Deka|chief detective]] of the [[Special Kinoko Police]] to retrieve it. He eventually catches the real thieves and returns the mushroom to the museum.

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|its remake]], it fully restores both [[Heart Point]]s and [[Bros. Point]]s for a single brother. There are only five Golden Mushrooms in-game, though it can also be stolen using [[Swing Bros.]] from [[Fawful]] and [[Popple]] (in the original game). In the remake, Bosses like [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]], [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton]], [[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]], and [[Larry Koopa|Larry]] always drop a Golden Mushroom when defeated. Ten Golden Mushrooms can be obtained from the [[Information Booth]] after 800 attacks are dodged. They also have a chance to be dropped by [[Clumph]]s and [[Boo]]s.

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|its remake]], the [[Star Candy]] and [[Cheesy Drumstick]] replace the Golden Mushroom and in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', they are replaced by various [[Candy (Mario & Luigi series)|Candies]].

===''Mario Party'' series===
[[File:Golden Mushroom MP3 artwork.jpg|thumb|left|120px|''Mario Party 3'' artwork]]
[[File:MP3_GoldenMushroom.png|thumb|''Mario Party 3'' icon]]
In ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', the Golden Mushroom is an item that allows players to roll three [[Dice Block]]s on their turn. It is stated to be [[Mario]]'s favorite item in the latter game. If the player rolls the same numbers, they get a bonus 20 coins (50 coins for three "7's"). In these titles, the Golden Mushroom lacks its crown and has white spots and a tan body, appearing more like a yellow [[Super Mushroom]].

In ''Mario Party 2'', Gold Mushrooms appear in the minigames [[Grab Bag (minigame)|Grab Bag]] and [[Rakin' 'em In]], where they are worth three Mushrooms.

This item was replaced by the [[Super Mega Mushroom]], [[Super 'Shroom Orb]], [[Thrice Candy]], and [[Triple Dice Set]] in the future installments of the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', ''[[Mario Party 5|5]]'', ''[[Mario Party 6|6]]'', ''[[Mario Party 7|7]]'', ''[[Mario Party 8|8]]'', ''[[Mario Party DS|DS]]'', ''[[Mario Party Superstars|Superstars]]'', ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV]]''.  If only counting home console games, it was replaced with the said items in Mario Party 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, DS, Superstars, Super Mario Party Jamboree, and Super Mario Party Jamboree - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV. 
{{br}}

===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
Gold Mushrooms are rare items in the puzzle and RPG hybrid called ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. It is obtained from [[? Block]]s or dropped after defeating some enemies with a 10+ combo, such as [[Goomba Tower|2-Goomba Towers]] and [[Paragoomba]]s. There is an even rarer variation called [[Grand Gold Mushroom]]. It raises the Skill levels of all Toad colors, and all types/forms of Goombas and Piranha Plants from Level 1-4.
