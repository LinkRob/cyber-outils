The '''Plumber Pole''' is an object in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Oh, Brother!]]"

==Description==
The Plumber Pole is an invention of [[Kooky von Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Kooky von Koopa]] modified from a regular fishing rod. It is capable of being cast through a series of [[Warp Pipe]]s, hooking a [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario brother]], and pulling him back through the pipes.

==Episode appearances==
In "Oh, Brother!", after locating [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] on the [[Koopascope]], Kooky casts the Plumber Pole through a pipe, where it goes through the [[Pipe Land|Pipe Maze]], and into the Mushroom Kingdom, where it hooks onto Mario. Kooky then uses the Plumber Pole to pull Mario back through the pipes, back to where he is.
