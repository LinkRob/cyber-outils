'''Star Dash''' is an unlockable minigame in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. For this reason, it cannot be played in Challenge mode, unlike other minigames.

==Layout==
Star Dash takes place in the [[Bowser Castle (baseball stadium)|Bowser Castle]] baseball stadium. It has a birds-eye view perspective of the four bases, similar to another minigame, [[Chain Chomp Sprint]].

==Gameplay==
Star Dash features four participants, who have to move around and collect the [[coin]]s and [[coin bag]]s (worth ten coins) launched out from a tube in the center. In the first three difficulties and in multiplayer, Star Dash has a time limit of 60 seconds. In Special Mode, the time limit is 90 seconds instead. If a coin bag hits the ground, the coins scatter across the floor.

Aside from coins, the tube can sometimes release a [[Mushroom]], and touching it causes the character to move around faster for a short period of time, just as in Chain Chomp Sprint. A [[Starman|Star]] is sometimes released as well, and collecting it grants the user not only temporary invincibility but also faster movement, like the Mushroom item, and ten coins, like a coin bag. If an invincible character touches an opponent, they become stunned temporarily and drop their coins.

In Special difficulty, the tube also releases a [[Poison Mushroom]], which characters must avoid or else their movement is slowed temporarily. Poison Mushrooms also cancel out the effects of a Mushroom and even a Star, despite the latter item having invincibility properties.

The only obstacle is four [[Thwomp]]s hanging above the field. Whenever a Thwomp is about to fall, it relays this from its shadow appearing on the ground. All four Thwomps land around the same time in harder difficulty settings. If a character is crushed by a Thwomp, they cannot move for a few seconds, and a sound clip of [[Bowser]]'s laughter plays. If a character touches a Thwomp while under the effects of a Star, they can knock the Thwomp away.

At the end, the characters are ranked based on how many coins they have collected, and whoever collects the most is the winner.
