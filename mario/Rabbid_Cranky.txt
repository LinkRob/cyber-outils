}}''Rabbid Cranky Kong'', or ''Rabbid Cranky'' (so not to be confused with [[Rabbid Kong]]), is the [[Rabbids|Rabbid]] counterpart of [[Cranky Kong]].

==Profile==
===Appearance===
Like the other Rabbids, he has white fur everywhere, except in his ears, around his mouth, on his belly, paws and under his feet, showing pale skin. He has aquamarine eyes and buck teeth. He has paws with only opposable thumbs and feet with no toes. Since his transformation, Rabbid Cranky has a long beard, bushy eyebrows, glasses, brown shirt, and a stick, just like Cranky Kong himself.

===Personality===
He's pretty stupid, like when he tried to point to the cannon, he was pointing the wrong way. He is grumpy as well, when [[Beep-O]] said he would slow them down on their adventure, he whacked him multiple times and used him as a unicycle to keep up with the group. He does not tolerate disrespect such as when Rabbid Peach took his cane to use as for selfies without his permission and jabbers at her in anger. Despite all that, he is always willing to help put a stop to the threat and doesn't let his age stop him from bringing evil to justice. 

===Powers and Abilities===
In combat, the strength of his Boombow can vary depending on how close he is to his targets, but his secondary weapon can do some great damage. He also has good defense and mobility and when he does the Team Jump, he can shoot his targets in midair. His Hairy Eye ability will have him automatically attack any target that moves within his sight and his Long Story ability can put nearby enemies to sleep for the whole turn.

==History==
===Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle: Donkey Kong Adventure===
====Donkey Kong Island====
Rabbid Cranky first appears after [[Beep-0]] and Rabbid Peach are accidentally teleported to a sandy beach, but unable to return due to the Time Washing Machine being broken and its pieces scattered around the island. He and Donkey Kong help fend off a group of opposing Rabbids. Once the villains are dealt with, Rabbid Cranky explains to them that Rabbid Kong has been using the energy from a certain part of the Time Washing Machine which is now in disrepair to become stronger and cause trouble and is adamant about joining in to stop him at any cost by using Beep-0 as a unicycle to make up for his slow walking speed. They all decide to work together to stop Rabbid Kong's operations and repair the Time Washing Machine so it can be used to send Beep-0 and Rabbid Peach back to the Mushroom Kingdom.
