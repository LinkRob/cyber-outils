}}'''Mansion Patrol''' is a minigame in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. The minigame is based on ''[[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' for its ghostly mansion.

==Gameplay==
The aim of the minigame is to shoot Boos that appear on the screen. If the player keeps hitting the Boos with any misses, they earn more points than the previous points added. [[Boo]]s are rarely seen carrying a [[Toad (species)|Toad]]. If the player shoots the Boo, the player will earn points and the Toad gives an item to the player. If the player shoots the Toad, they lose points. The minigame ends after the Boos manage to damage the players' lives. The [[Flipside Token]]s the player earns are converted down by 10,000.
