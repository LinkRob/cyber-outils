<b>Targeting Teds</b> (also called <b>Missile Torpedo Teds</b>) are a sub-species of [[Torpedo Ted]]s that first appear in ‘’New Super Mario Bros. U’’. These aquatic creatures resemble regular Torpedo Teds but are distinguished by their red color and orange propeller. Similar to [[Missile Bill]]s, Targeting Teds persistently follow the player until they detonate. They are launched from [[Red Skull Box]]es and are among the few enemies capable of destroying Empty Blocks.

Targeting Teds make their sole appearance in the level [[The Mighty Cannonship]], situated in [[Soda Jungle]]. [[Bowser Jr.]] also employs Targeting Teds during his initial confrontation at the level’s conclusion, where players must redirect them at Bowser Jr. three times to defeat him.

Players utilizing [[Boost Mode]] with the [[Wii U]] GamePad can tap a Targeting Ted to cause it to explode.

In ‘’[[New Super Luigi U]]’’, they appear in [[Larry’s Trigger-Happy Castle]], as well as in Bowser Jr.’s first fight in the level [[All Aboard!]].
