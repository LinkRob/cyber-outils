'''Section Gates''' are objects in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'']] franchise that first appeared in [[Mario Kart 7|''Mario Kart 7'']].

== Description ==
Section Gates look similar to the goal gates throughout the ''Mario Kart'' series, as they can either be a sign held by two vertical posts, a tunnel entrance with the sign, or a sign hanging from an archway. In [[Mario Kart 7|''Mario Kart 7'']], Section Gates had stars on their sign. Most courses in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' give them signs with "MARIO KART" written on, except on [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 7)|3DS Rainbow Road]], which retains the design from ''Mario Kart 7''. In [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']], some section gates have "MARIO KART" on the sign, while others have stars, though there are some irregular section gates.

Whenever a player cannot complete a lap within 80 seconds on 150 CC, section gates are installed to insure quick clearance.

== Locations ==

=== ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' ===

* [[Wuhu Loop]]: The first section gate is at the entrance to the first tunnel after a road divider, marking the start of the second section. The second section gate is past two turns after a trick ramp shortcut around 60% of the way through the course.
* [[Maka Wuhu]]: The first section gate appears after the mud-filled U-turn bend, preceding a cave with several pits. The second section gate appears after the racers pass Summerstone Castle and go over the trick ramp, with the gate appearing in the form of an opening in the stone wall.
* [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 7)|Rainbow Road]]: The first section gate is after the wavy road that succeeds a [[Mushroom Trampoline]]. The second section gate appears when the racers leave [[The Moon]].

=== ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' ===
Courses in the [[Booster Course Pass]] cannot be found in the Wii U version.

* [[Mount Wario]]: The first section gate appears when the racers enter the cave of the titular mountain. The second is after the forest section.
* [[Mario Kart 64|N64]] [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 64)|Rainbow Road]]'s lap count was cut down from 3 to 1. It has the first section gate before its big loop, with the second section gate appearing around halfway between the U-bend and the next turn.
* [[Big Blue]]: The first section gate appears before the first [[Glide Ramp]], with the second section gate appearing after the [[Glider]] [[Cannon (Mario Kart series)|Cannon]].
* Booster Course Pass Courses:
** [[Mario Kart 64|N64]] [[Kalimari Desert]] retains its layout for the first lap, but it loses its last two laps in favour of a single large lap that is actually [[Kalimari Desert 2]]. (See the [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] section.)
** 3DS Rainbow Road: Same as the original.
** [[Mario Kart Tour|Tour]] [[Athens Dash]]: Athens Dash has two laps like N64 Kalimari Desert, with one lap being split into two sections.
** Tour [[Los Angeles Laps]]: The first section gate is [[Los Angeles Laps 3|under the bridge]], and is reached when the racers return to the centre of the city and must take a right turn.  The second section gate is at the [[Los Angeles Laps 2|Oil Field]]'s southern entrance, and takes the form of an entrance gate.

=== ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===

* [[Tokyo Blur]]: While Tokyo Blurs [[Tokyo Blur 1|1]], [[Tokyo Blur 2|2]], and [[Tokyo Blur 3|3]] lack Section Gates due to their short length, Tokyo Blur 2's U-bend at the city's north side has a Section Gate in [[Tokyo Blur 4]].
* [[Mario Kart 64|N64]] [[Kalimari Desert 2]]: [[Kalimari Desert]] 1 had no section gates, while Kalimari Desert 2 requires going through the tunnel. The entrance to the tunnel has the series logo hung from the entrance, functioning as a Section Gate.
* [[Mario Kart: Super Circuit|GBA]] [[Bowser Castle 4]]: The original version had no section gates, but this version has one at the northwest corner following the iron ball section. This Section Gate uses the [[Mario Kart 7|''Mario Kart 7'']] design.
* [[Mario Kart Wii|Wii]] [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart Wii)|Rainbow Road]]: When the racers go through the [[Launch Star]] that serves as a [[Cannon (Mario Kart series)|Cannon]], the second section begins. This Section Gate is removed in the [[Booster Course Pass]].
* [[Mario Kart 7|3DS]] [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 7)|Rainbow Road]]: Same locations as the original. (See [[Mario Kart 7|''Mario Kart 7'']]).

=== ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' ===

* [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart World)|Rainbow Road]]: This course has four Section Gates, or five in Grand Prix mode. The first Section Gate is the landing platform just ahead of the [[Finish line]]; this section gate serves as the course's starting line outside of Grand Prix mode or with Intermissions disabled. The second section begins just before the racers head to the island with the crystals and the river. Section three begins when the road terminates after a split path, just before a [[Glide Ramp]] that leads to a space station. Section 4 begins when the racers get to the end of the space station.
