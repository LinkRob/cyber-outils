'''Heart Swing''' is [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Star Swing]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces her [[Heart Ball]] move from ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', as it is now only used while pitching.

==Description==
Upon initiating Heart Swing, Peach draws a heart with her bat and hits the ball, cloaking it with hearts that stun the fielders who try to catch it. The stun effect is ineffective against female characters including [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], [[Baby Peach]], [[Baby Daisy]], [[Toadette]], [[Birdo]], the [[Noki]]s, [[Dixie Kong]], [[Tiny Kong]], and female [[Mii]]s. If a [[Magikoopa]] is nearby but not too close to the hearts, its [[Magical Catch]] ability still allows it to potentially catch the ball. If none of the fielders catch the ball, it comes to a stop, and its hearts wear off.
