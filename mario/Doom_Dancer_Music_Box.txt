The '''Doom Dancer Music Box''' is an item in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', specifically the episode "[[Do the Koopa]]."

==Description==
The Doom Dancer Music Box has a crank that, when used, forces anyone to dance at the speed it has been set to.
