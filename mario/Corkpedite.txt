'''Corkpedite''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They resemble golems made out of sand, and are one of the biggest enemies. Corkpedites are only fought in [[Barrel Volcano]].

==Battle==
In battle, Corkpedites mainly attack physically. Corkpedites are separated into two parts: their body and their head and arms. Their head has more [[Heart Point|HP]] and is capable of using [[Sand Storm]] and the body can use [[Migraine]].
