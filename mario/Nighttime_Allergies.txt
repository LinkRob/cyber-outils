{{Nihongo|'''Nighttime Allergies'''|ハナミズ|Hanamizu|Runny nose}} is [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and reappears as [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player must repeatedly press the button to sniffle the dangling mucus. If the player wins, the girl grins.
*'''1st level difficulty:''' A girl finishes sniffling when it reaches the chin's level.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' A girl finishes sniffling when it reaches the chest's level.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' Two girls with drippy noses.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
The microgame includes a man in microgame if the player wins the man grins and winks facing the screen.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' A girl finishes sniffling when it reaches the chin's level.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' A man finishes sniffling when it reaches the chest's level.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' A girl and a man have drippy noses.
