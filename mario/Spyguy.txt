'''Spyguys''' are toylike [[Black Shy Guy]]s with a jet attached, which allows them to hover in mid-air. They appear in [[Lava Dome]] in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]'', where they hover from left to right and stop briefly upon hitting a wall. A [[Mini]] can ride a Spyguy if they approach it from above; however, if a Mini touches its fire from underneath, the Mini is burned and destroyed. Spyguys can be permanently defeated with either [[hammer]]s or a [[Fire Flower]].

If ground is directly below a Spyguy, it stops briefly, then walks like a standard Shy Guy instead of hovering with its jet. If a grounded Spyguy approaches a pit, it stops briefly again before hovering in mid-air as usual. Otherwise, its movement remains the same.

In the [[Construction Zone]], Spyguys can be used in the Lava Dome construction kit, [[Special Kit 1]], [[Special Kit 2]], and [[Special Kit 3]].
