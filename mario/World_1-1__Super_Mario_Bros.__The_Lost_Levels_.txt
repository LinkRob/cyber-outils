'''World 1-1''' is the first [[level]] of [[World 1 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 1]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'', and the first level of the game overall.

==Layout==
The player starts along a path with a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] leaping towards them. Further on is a square-shaped block formation, with the second [[Brick Block]] from the left containing a [[Super Mushroom]]. To get this power-up, the player must strike a block from underneath with proper timing to make the mushroom bounce over the ledge. Up ahead is a row of [[Question Block]]s, with the one in the middle holding a [[Poison Mushroom]].

After the second [[pipe]] and above the tree in the background is a [[Hidden Block]] that holds a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. What follows is a gap and a set of Brick Blocks. The left-most block has a [[Starman|Super Star]], while the block right of the stone stairs is a [[Coin Block]]. The player will need to cross a chasm here by using the upper Brick Blocks, and while doing so, the player will encounter a row of [[Koopa Troopa]]s and some Question Blocks. The third one from the left contains another Poison Mushroom. Next up is a row of enemies followed by two pipes. The second pipe leads to a secret underground area where the player can collect some [[coin]]s and find some Hidden Blocks containing either a Poison Mushroom or power-up. Inside the Question Block furthest to the right, underneath the floating pipe ahead, is a power-up. Following the chasm and pipe leading out of the secret underground area is the stone stairs leading to the [[flagpole]].
