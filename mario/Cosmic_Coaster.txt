'''Cosmic Coaster''' is a 2-vs-2 [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place on a roller coaster ride in outer space. There are [[Bowser]] signs along the way. At the end is a [[Ztar]] goal, which turns into a regular [[Star]] upon being reached.

==Gameplay==
Each team is on a [[roller coaster]] that accelerates until it reaches maximum speed. The teams have to dodge the oncoming [[Bowser]] signs scattered across the track. In order to do so, they must move to either the left, the right, or the middle. Some signs block only one section of space on the track, decorated as Bowser's face. Some, however, fill up two sections of the track, decorated as Bowser breathing fire. There are also [[coin]]s along the track, but it is risky to grab them while also avoiding the signs. If the coaster rams into a sign, it will slow down. The first team that reaches the end wins.
