'''Ninja Crows''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Ninja Crow is a black crow with a red bandana wrapped around its face, where its eyes are.

===Behavior===
A Ninja Crow can attack Wario using its sharp claws. If a Ninja Crow has been hit a few times, it could possibly produce a kunai from its mouth to jab Wario with. A Ninja Crow is invincible while using this attack.

==In ''Wario World''==
Ninja Crows are only encountered in one level, [[Mirror Mansion]], usually in groups. Wario can stun a Ninja Crow and then defeat it from one of his [[Mad Move]]s.
