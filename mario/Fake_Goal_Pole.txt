'''Fake Goal Poles''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
A Fake Goal Pole has the appearance of a regular [[Goal Pole]] that has not been activated. Fake Goal Poles only appear in certain [[Ghost House]] [[level]]s, and their appearance is purposefully deceptive because touching a Fake Goal Pole turns it into several [[Boo]]s, with the Fake Goal Pole itself disappearing upon interaction.

==In ''Super Mario 3D World''==
One Fake Goal Pole first appears in the level [[Shifty Boo Mansion]], followed by two appearing in the level [[Shiftier Boo Mansion]]. In the latter level, the first Fake Goal Pole summons three [[Big Boo]]s around it when a character is nearby. In total, there are three Fake Goal Poles.
