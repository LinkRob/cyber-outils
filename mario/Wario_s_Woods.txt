'''''Wario's Woods''''' is a ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' game for both the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. It stars [[Toad (character)|Toad]] as the main protagonist, and features him having to protect the location of the [[Wario's Woods (location)|same name]] from [[Wario]].
