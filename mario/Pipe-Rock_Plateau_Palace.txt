'''Pipe-Rock Plateau Palace''' is the last level of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It is only unlockable with 14 [[Wonder Seed|Wonder Seeds]], and after clearing [[Mountaineering!|Wiggler Race: Mountaineering!]]

==Layout==
The level takes place in a [[castle theme|castle]], filled with [[Bone Goomba]]s and [[pipe]]s.

The player begins outside the castle, and there is a pathway to enter. Upon entering, Bone Goombas fall from the air, and many different kinds of blocks are seen. There are also two [[Hammer Bro|Hammer Bros]], and after that, a row of blocks with pipes spawning Bone Goombas. Shortly after that is the [[Checkpoint Flag]], and there is a [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]] accessible by entering a pipe. The [[Wonder Effect]] will cause pipes to fall.

There is then a long passage leading to the boss room. The level's boss is [[Bowser Jr.]]. After hitting Bowser Jr. once, he will transform into [[Wonder Bowser Jr.]], who will change the size of the player and himself over the course of the battle. After being defeated, Bowser Jr. will leave and a [[Royal Seed]] will appear.

==[[10-Flower Coin]] locations==
After the checkpoint, there is a pipe cut off by the top of the screen. Jumping on top of it and going right with let the player hit a [[Wow Bud]], summoning the first 10-flower coin.

After the row of seven pipes that fall, an area to the right has the second 10-flower coin blocked with [[Brick Block]]s. The player can use an [[Elephant Fruit]] to destroy the brick blocks.

Shortly after the second 10-flower coin, there is a pipe that falls. Jumping on top of it in time will allow the player to grab the final 10-flower coin before the pipe falls into the lava.{{SMBWNav}}
