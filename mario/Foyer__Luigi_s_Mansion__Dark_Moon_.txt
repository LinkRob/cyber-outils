|first       = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}The '''Foyer''' (called the '''Main Hall''' in the PAL versions) is a room in the [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is argubly the biggest room in Gloomy Manor and the most visited area in the mansion. Once after the third mission, E. Gadd starts pixelating Luigi into the Foyer. It is also where Luigi fights ghosts for the first time and first encounters [[Greenie]]s, [[Slammer]]s, and [[Hider]]s.
==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-1: Poltergust 5000|A-1: Poltergust 5000]], Luigi heads into a Foyer where he notices [[Greenie]]s putting the [[Strobulb]] high up on a ceiling fan. Luigi heads up and vacuums at the ceiling fan to lower it down and acquires the Strobulb. Suddenly, Greenies attack but using the newly acquired Stroblub, Luigi captures the ghosts into the [[Poltergust 5000]].
