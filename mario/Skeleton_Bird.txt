'''Skeleton Birds''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are flying skeletal birds that wear a green hood, a red bandanna and have two wings.

===Behavior===
Skeleton Birds attack by spitting green clumps of goo that can turn [[Wario]] into [[Zombie Wario]].

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Skeleton Birds are encountered in the [[Sapphire Passage]] levels, such as [[Crescent Moon Village]], and the [[Golden Passage]] level itself. To attack a Skeleton Bird, Wario has to be in his Zombie form. Upon taking a hit, a Skeleton Bird crumbles into a head, which can be defeated by Wario, regardless of his form.

During the ending cutscene, a Skeleton Bird and two [[Men'ono]]s are shown in one of Wario's flashbacks.
