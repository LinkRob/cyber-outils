'''World Flower''' is the third and penultimate [[Special World]] as well as the overall eleventh and penultimate world in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

== Summary ==
It is unlocked after clearing [[Broken Blue Bully Belt]] in [[World Mushroom (Super Mario 3D World)|World Mushroom]]. This world sits adjacent to World Mushroom, and is also made up of remixes of previously played courses. This world contains twelve standard courses, which is the most of any world.
