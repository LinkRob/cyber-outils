'''''Mario Baseball''''' is a spinoff series of games in the {{franchise|Mario}} starring [[Mario]] and friends playing baseball. It has two titles: ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. Unlike other previous sports spinoffs, the player can recruit people via completing scout missions to get enough scout flags to recruit the player on one of the four opposite teams. Another unique feature is chemistry, which affects how well the player's teammates cooperate with each other during a ball game. Even though there are only two baseball games, this series features more playable characters than other spinoff titles in the Super Mario franchise.
