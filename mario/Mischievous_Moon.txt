The '''Mischievous Moon''' is a minor character in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Mischievous Moon is a crescent moon with a face, including eyes with long eyelashes, a nose, and large lips. In ''WarioWare: Twisted!'', Mischievous Moon has a dark blue body. In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', it is light yellow and has a small wing next to its face. In ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'', Mischievous Moon instead retains its appearance from ''WarioWare: Twisted!''.

===Behavior===
The Mischievous Moon is only seen sleeping in the night sky.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare: Twisted!''===
[[File:WWT Crescent Roll.png|thumb|left|Mischievous Moon's appearance in ''WarioWare: Twisted!'']]
Mischievous Moon only appears in the [[Crescent Roll]] microgame. There is at least one devil sitting on the Mischievous Moon in each difficulty level, and the object is for the player to rotate the system so that the devil slides off. In levels 2 and 3, [[Toby]] and [[Mensa]] are also sitting on the Mischievous Moon, but the player must avoid sliding them off. In level 3, there are two devils sitting on the Mischievous Moon whom the player have to slide off.
{{br|left}}
===''WarioWare Gold''===
[[File:Crescent Roll WW Gold.png|thumb|Mischievous Moon's appearance in the ''WarioWare Gold'' iteration of Crescent Roll]]
The Mischievous returns in Crescent Roll, and aside from its different appearance, the Mischievous Moon has the same role as in ''WarioWare: Twisted!''. Mischievous Moon is also featured as a card in [[Wario Kard]], in which it reduces any power status above 40 down to 40. The Mischievous Moon often uses rock. In level 1 of the microgame [[Electric Groove Fish]], the Mischievous Moon makes a cameo appearance in the form of a neon sign, which lights up if the electric fish powers up the city.

===''WarioWare: Get It Together!''===
Mischievous Moon returns in level 2 of the microgame [[WarioWare: Twisted! (microgame)|WarioWare: Twisted!]], which features a recreation of ''WarioWare: Twisted!''{{'}}s iteration of Crescent Roll.
