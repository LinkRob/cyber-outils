'''Blooey''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is one of [[Luigi]]'s partners that joins him on his separate journey for the [[Marvelous Compass]]. Originally known as the "White Torpedo," and Luigi went onto a journey to [[Rumblebump Volcano]], but things went wrong and Blooey got into an accident, which ended up making his flesh golden brown and made him hold a grudge with Luigi.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Blooey.png|frame|left|Sprite]]
Blooey is a brown [[Blooper]]. He was originally white prior to his accident. Luigi accidentally threw him into a volcano when they were battling a stone creature, turning Blooey into a golden brown color.

===Personality===
Blooey is shown to be full of anger after Luigi basically made him "edible." Blooey stated that the only reason why he is going with Luigi on his journey is to, one day, pay him back for burning him. He even states that he'll get back at Luigi even it takes him his entire life. However, at the end, he got thrown in lava again. This time, however, he decided not to hold a grudge against Luigi anymore due to [[Princess Eclair]] deciding to marry the [[Chestnut King]] which upsets Luigi and Blooey himself was quite pleased by Luigi's misfortune.

==Trivia==
*In his burned, golden-brown state, Blooey resembles the [[Dried Blooper]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.
