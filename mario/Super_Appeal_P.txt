'''Super Appeal P''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is the partner-based equivalent of the [[Super Appeal]].

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear this badge by using one point of his [[Badge Point|BP]]. By using this badge, Mario's partners' appeals give Mario more star power.

This badge is found in [[Boggly Woods]], inside of the woods is [[Madame Flurrie]]'s house. Super Appeal P can also be bought in the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] for fifty [[coin]]s.
