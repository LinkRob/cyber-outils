The '''Slippenglide King''' is a character from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', ruling over Slippenglide, and the father of [[Prince Chilliam]].

== Profile ==

=== Personality ===
The Slippenglide King cares for his people. As implied by his son, [[Prince Chilliam]], he also seems to have a habit of making puns, a trait inherited by Chilliam himself.

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Slippenglide King looks just like his son, but with a darker hue for his skin colour.
