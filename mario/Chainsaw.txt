A '''Chainsaw'''<ref>Nintendo ''Mario Mania'' Player's Guide. Page 51.</ref> (also called a '''Buzz Saw''')<ref>Nintendo ''Mario Mania'' Player's Guide. Page 96.</ref> is a mechanical saw that travels on [[track]]s in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can perform a [[Spin Jump]] on Chainsaws or [[stomp]] on them while on [[Yoshi]] to rebound on them. They appear in most of the [[level]]s that have tracks with [[platform]]s traveling across them. A large number of them can be found in the [[Cheese Bridge Area]], while two of them are found in [[Way Cool]].

In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''[[Minecraft]]'', [[w:c:minecraft:Stonecutter|Stonecutters]] are replaced by Chainsaws.
