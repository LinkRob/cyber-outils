'''Prickly Goombas''' are a [[Goomba]] variant found inside of spiked [[chestnut]] shells. They have only appeared in the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' series]], having debuted in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 

==Behavior==
If the playable character touches a Prickly Goomba, they take damage from touching the spiked shell. Prickly Goombas do not move until their shells are burned open with a [[fireball]], turning them into regular [[Goomba]]s. If a character shoots an [[Ice Ball|ice ball]] at a Prickly Goomba while in their [[Ice Mario|ice]] form, the Prickly Goomba freezes, but its outer shell does not break.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''==
Prickly Goombas are only encountered in [[World 9-7 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-7]].

==In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]] / [[New Super Luigi U]]/ [[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''==
For ''New Super Mario Bros. U'', Prickly Goombas are only encountered in their namesake level, [[Prickly Goombas!]] in [[Frosted Glacier]]. For ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', they appear in the level [[Fire and Ice]], found within the same world. In both games, also packaged within the [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''New Super Mario Bros. U'', Prickly Goombas act the same as in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', but can also be walked on by a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]].
