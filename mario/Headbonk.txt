The '''Headbonk''' is a move used commonly by [[Goomba]]s in the ''[[Paper Mario series]]''. This is also the basic technique of [[Goombario]] and [[Goombella]].

==Usage==



===From Partners===
When a Headbonk is used, Goombario and/or Goombella will charge at the enemy and headbonk them. With the [[Action Command]], this move can strike twice in one turn. The Headbonk can damage airborne enemies and flip over shell enemies (except for spiked enemies).
===From Enemies===
When Goombas use Headbonks, they will target either Mario or his partners (in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' only) for 1 point of damage. The attack power is doubled from [[Spiked Goomba]]s due to their spiked helmets. Guarding this attack will result in no damage, however. In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', there is even an enemy called a [[Headbonk Goomba]] that attacks with a Headbonk instead of simply walking into Mario like with the normal Goombas.
