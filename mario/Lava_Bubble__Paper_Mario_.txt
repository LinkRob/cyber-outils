'''Lava Bubbles''' are a subspecies of [[Podoboo]] which first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. They attack by chasing [[Mario]] and his partners and by spitting fire at them. Lava Bubbles resemble a small flame with a pair of demonic eyes. They are also shown to survive outside of (or away from) lava making them appear more as fiery ghosts rather than elemental creatures. Lava Bubbles were initially, usually found in volcanic areas but have since also commonly appeared in dark and gloomy places (such as [[Pirate's Grotto]]) as well.

Lava Bubbles appear in the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series where they can be found in [[Mt. Lavalava]] in the game ''Paper Mario'', [[Pit of 100 Trials]] and the [[Pirate's Grotto]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', and in the [[Underwhere]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They cannot be jumped on as Mario and co. take damage instead. Mario can use his [[hammer]] however.

==History==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Lava Bubble PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
Lava Bubbles make their debut in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' where they can be found in [[Mt. Lavalava]] on the way to [[Lava Piranha]]. Because Lava Bubbles are composed entirely of fire, they cannot be jumped on and inflict damage to any attacks from above, so [[Mario]] can only harm them with [[hammer]]-based attacks of other projectiles, unless he has a [[Fire Shield]] or [[Ice Power]] badge. In battle, Lava Bubbles attack by jumping on Mario or his allies similar to the [[Goomba]]s from earlier, they can also explode in the heroes' faces, causing damage and possible [[Burn]]. Lava Bubbles are vulnerable to water- and ice-based attacks, so they can be quickly defeated by those.

A stronger, blue-colored variety of Lava Bubble, called [[Ember]]s, appear as enemies later on in [[Star Way]].

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Lava Bubble New.jpg|thumb|right|The Lava Bubble's new appearance in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''.]]
Lava Bubbles return in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|its Nintendo Switch remake]] once again appearing primarily in Chapter 5. In this game, they are found in [[Pirate's Grotto]] alongside the [[Ember]]s, as the fiery ghosts of the sailors who disappeared centuries prior. They are referred to as "flame spirits" in their descriptions, while the related [[Ember]]s are commonly called "ghosts." Lava Bubbles can also be found in the [[Pit of 100 Trials (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|Pit of 100 Trials]] in floors 41-49, showing up in random battles alongside other enemies in the pit. Due to appearing alongside the Embers, Lava Bubbles now roughly have the same strength as the Embers in this game rather than Embers being stronger versions. Lava Bubbles have more attack power but less HP while Embers are slightly weaker but have more HP.

In battle, Lava Bubbles attack by blowing a flame at [[Mario]] and his allies or by exploding by the heroes for some burning damage. If they are not [[Guard|blocked]] or [[Superguard]]ed, a Lava Bubble can set Mario and his partner [[Burn|on fire]], which does 4 damage each turn. They can also spit fireballs that hit either Mario or his partner. These fireballs cause only 3 damage, but they can set Mario (or his partner) [[Burn|on fire]]. Being fire-based enemies, Lava Bubbles are naturally immune to jump attacks and other physical attacks from above. However, they can be damaged with [[Hammer]]s and other projectiles. Lava Bubbles retain their vulnerability to ice-based attacks including the [[Ice Power]] badge which allows [[Mario]] to stomp on a Lava Bubble without taking damage, in addition to also being vulnerable to explosions. If hit by a fire attack (such as [[Vivian]]'s [[Fiery Jinx]]), Lava Bubbles either duplicate or restore [[HP]].

===''Super Paper Mario''===
[[Image:LavaBubbleCard.png|thumb|left|As a Catch Card in ''Super Paper Mario''.]]
Lava Bubbles are seen again in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', in this game, they jump out of [[pit]]s in [[The Underwhere]]. To defeat them, the player can use [[Carrie]] to [[jump]] on them, use [[Boomer (Pixl)|Boomer]] to explode on them, hit them with [[Cudge]], or hit them with [[Barry]]. It is possible for Lava bubbles to traverse on land; the player must pick them up with [[Thoreau]] and drop them. While on land, Lava Bubbles can pass through pipes and get trapped in spikes. Visually, their flames are no longer slanted as they were in previous games. Lava Bubbles have only 1 HP in this game meaning a single hit will be enough to do them in.

==Trivia==
*Despite being named Lava Bubbles, they don't really resemble an actual Bubble but rather a small flame however it could be possible that the flame is composed of the same liquid as lava.
*The [[Podoboo]]s flame-like appearance in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series slightly resembles the Lava Bubbles without the demonic eyes.
*In their first two appearances, Lava Bubbles are found in Chapter 5, what's more is Chapter 5 takes place on a Tropical Island in both games.
