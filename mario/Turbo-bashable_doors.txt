'''Turbo-bashable doors''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. They can only be broken by [[Mario]] charging into them while using the [[Rocket Nozzle]], and doing so is required to obtain one of the [[Shine Sprite]]s near the [[Grand Pianta Statue]] as well as to access the secret course [[Turbo Track]].
