'''Frygal''' is a "character" from ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', making "her" only appearance in the episode "[[Hooded Robin and his Mario Men]]." "She" is actually a disguise that Hooded Robin used to distract [[Fryguy]] himself. Frygal has a golden mask and wears a pink dress that is actually the remnants of a curtain.

==History==
In order to save the people's money from the Sherrif of Koopingham, [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Toad (character)|Toad]], [[Princess Peach]], and the titular avian protagonist make a disguise to distract [[Fryguy]], who is guarding the money. Hooded Robin is able to lure Fryguy away by mimicking a female voice and taking part in the disguise with Toad, while the Mario brothers and Peach attempt to retrieve the money, but Frygal's "dress" falls away, exposing her as merely a lit torch that Hooded Robin and Toad used as the "head." Fryguy is reasonably outraged over his duty being sabotaged, but is locked up.
