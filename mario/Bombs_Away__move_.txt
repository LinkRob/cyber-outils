|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Bombs Away''' is a move used only by [[Jinx]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
When using Bombs Away, Jinx fires a blue energy orb attacking one of [[Mario]]'s party. This attack is very strong and if it's not blocked properly, it can fully take down one of Mario's party.

==Trivia==
*This is often known as one of the strongest moves used in the game as Bombs Away can deal over 1000 damage. The strongest move in the game is the infamous [[Geno Whirl]] when timed perfectly.
