'''Negative Ions''' are an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Description==
Negative Ions are a liquid-type spray of {{w|ion}}s that [[Bowser]] can obtain by [[punch]]ing a [[Shop Block]] and paying ten [[coin]]s. Negative Ions fully restore [[Bowser]]'s [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Special Point|SP]]. Negative Ions have the same effect as the [[Mushroom Tea]] and [[Emoglobin|Emoglobin Sweat]] only usable by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], as Negative Ions cannot be stored in the inventory.
