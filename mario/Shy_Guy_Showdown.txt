'''Shy Guy Showdown''' is a Duel [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' and ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''. It is also included as part of the ''[[Mario Party 5 Demo]]'' within the ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!! Bonus Disc]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Shy Guy Showdown Top 100.png|thumb|left|The minigame in ''Mario Party: The Top 100'']]
The objective is to be the first to shoot the ink ball at the opponent. A [[Shy Guy]] in the middle of the arena raises a sign, and the player must press the button shown on the sign before their opponent to win. Sometimes the Shy Guy raises a fake sign (a blank sign, "!" sign, "M" sign, or {{wp|no symbol|🚫 sign}}). The player should not do anything in this case, or else the ink ball explodes inside the gun, staining the player and making them lose. If the player presses the wrong button on a legitimate sign or if the player presses any button too early, the same result happens.

The setting in this minigame is commonly used for simple Duel minigames, of which there are five. It takes place in a western-themed town. The characters (and the Shy Guy) are standing on an arena with a big white [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] with twelve colorful stars surrounding it, which is surrounded by two small houses or buildings.
