'''Crocodile Cauldron''' is a volcanic location in the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' series. The world itself appears to be a giant [[Kremling]] head.

==Appearances==
===''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''===
Crocodile Cauldron first appeared in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''.

In the game, this world is the second where [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] venture throughout on their quest to save [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] from [[The Flying Krock]]. The world itself has five levels and has [[Kleever]] as a boss. There also are a couple of mining shafts in the world where the Kremlings possibly mine for minerals. There's also a sunken ship in the world, implying that the world itself could've been a harbour at one point and not always a volcanic area.

At the end of the game when [[King K. Rool|Kaptain K. Rool]] is defeated for a second time in the [[Lost World (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Lost World]], he is knocked into the energy source for powering [[Crocodile Isle]]. Then, as his bulkiness was blocking the energy source from powering up the island, he eventually shot out of the island's energy source and into the waters once more. The energy build-up then caused Crocodile Cauldron, along with the rest of Crocodile Isle to sink underwater.

===''Donkey Kong Land 2''===
While not physically appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'', Crocodile Isle was resurrected from the waters. As the island resurrected, Crocodile Cauldron and [[Krem Quay]] somehow merged into [[Krem Cauldron]]. The memory limitations is likely the reason why this was done.
