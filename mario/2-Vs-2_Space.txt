'''2-Vs-2 Spaces''' are an object in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
2-Vs-2 Spaces are yellow [[space]]s with "2 vs 2" printed on them. The text is dark orange with a white outline.

==In ''Mario Party 6''==
2-Vs-2 Spaces are one of the spaces exclusively appearing in the boards of Solo Mode. Whenever the player's character ends their turn on a 2-Vs-2 Space, they and their partner have to play a 2-Vs-2 mini-game against two of the [[Koopa Kid]]s.
