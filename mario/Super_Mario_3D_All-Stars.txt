{{Nihongo|'''''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'''''|スーパーマリオ ３Ｄコレクション<ref>{{cite web| url = https://www.nintendo.co.jp/switch/avp3a/index.html| title = スーパーマリオ ３Ｄコレクション| accessdate = 2020-09-17| author = [[Nintendo]]| language = Japanese}}</ref>|Sūpā Mario 3D Korekushon|Super Mario 3D Collection}} is a [[Nintendo Switch]] 3D Platformer Compilation game that was released on September 18, 2020 for a limited time. Announced alongside to celebrate [[Super Mario Bros. 35th Anniversary|Mario's 35th Anniversary]]. ''3D All-Stars'' includes high-definition ports of the three major 3D platformers in the ''Super Mario'' series: ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' (1996), ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' (2002), and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' (2007). The collection was only available until late March 2021.<ref name="cnet"/>

== Development ==
According to video game news website ''Eurogamer'', Nintendo referred to the compilation as ''Super Mario All-Stars 2'' internally.<ref name="eurogamer1">{{cite web| url = https://www.eurogamer.net/articles/2020-03-31-more-details-emerge-on-nintendos-mario-remasters| title = More details emerge on Nintendo's Mario remasters| accessdate = 5 September 2020| last = Phillips| first = Tom| date = 31 March 2020| work = Eurogamer| publisher = Gamer Network Limited| language = English}}</ref> ''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'' was officially announced on a Nintendo Direct presentation on September 3rd 2020.<ref>{{cite web| url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s_UcjEq2Dgk| title= ''Super Mario Bros. 35th Anniversary'' Direct| accessdate = 5 September 2020 | date = 3 September 2020| work = YouTube| Publisher = Nintendo| language = English}}</ref>

== Overview ==
All the games have been improved for a "smoother" experience, high-definition upgrades (up to 1920 x 1080 docked; except ''Super Mario 64''), and new Switch controls, while they maintain their faithful design and look.<ref name="mario3dres">{{cite web| url = https://nintendoeverything.com/super-mario-3d-all-stars-resolution-detailed/ | title = ''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'' resolution detailed | accessdate = 5 September 2020 | author = Brian(@NE_Brian) | date = 3 September 2020 | work = Nintendo Everything | language = English}}</ref> There will also be a new mode where the player can listen to whole soundtracks of all the three included games, whether the console is turned on or off. The text and dialogues are expected to be changed for improvement, removal of original console-exclusive features not compatible with the Switch, and to match the Switch controls. Some textures of these games have been updated, with ''Super Mario Sunshine'' having received the most texture updates.

=== Games ===
==== ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' ====
[[File:SM3DAS 64 1.jpg|thumb|left|''Super Mario 64'' gameplay.]]
''Super Mario 64'' maintains its original 4:3 aspect ratio, while now having a high-definition upgrade, up to a resolution of 960 x 720 in both docked and handheld modes.<ref name="mario3dres"/> The HUD design has been modified for an HD upgrade as well which now looks smooth and more clear. The same is applied to Mario's "M" emblem.<ref>[https://twitter.com/MarioBrothBlog/status/1301522543275278336 Supper Mario broth on Twitter: "''Super Mario 64'''s original release ...]</ref> The game's title screen has a "Press +" text, replacing the "Press Start" text to match the controls of thee Nintendo Switch controls. Changes from [[Super Mario 64: Shindō Pak Taiō|''Super Mario 64: Shindō Pak Taiō'']] have been included, such as rumble, Mario no longer says "''So long, king [[Bowser]]!''" when throwing him; he'll instead say "''Bye-bye!''", and some glitches like the backwards long jump have been fixed.

==== ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' ====
[[File:SM3DAS Sunshine 1.jpg|thumb|right|''Super Mario Sunshine'' gameplay]]
''Sunshine'' features an aspect ratio presentation of 16:9 hacked from the original using a Gecko widescreen code. Additionally, despite the Switch's capability to support the GameCube controller (via an adapter) and the game originating from the console, this version doesn't support such a controller.<ref>{{cite web| url = https://mynintendonews.com/2020/09/04/super-mario-sunshine-wont-have-gamecube-controller-support-in-super-mario-3d-all-stars/ | title = ''Super Mario Sunshine'' won’t have Gamecube controller support in Super Mario 3D All-Stars | accessdate = 5 September 2020 | name = SSF1991 | date = 4 September 2020 | work = My Nintendo News | publisher = Excite Global Media Ltd. | language = English}}</ref>

==== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' ====
[[File:SM3DAS Galaxy 2.jpg|thumb|left|''Super Mario Galaxy'' gameplay.]]
''Galaxy'' has motion controls through the use of two detached [[Joy-Con]] controllers. The star cursor is now determined by the motion of a Joy-Con rather than requiring a sensor bar, as it was in the original. The cursor can also be interacted through the use of touchscreen if played in handheld mode. A secondary Joy-Con controller is required for co-op 2-player mode. Other features like the Wii Message Board messages and the [[Wii Remote]] speaker hasn't returned as it is not present with the Switch's current hardware and features.

== Reception ==

=== Critical reception ===
''3D All-Stars'' received positive reviews. Metacritic gave a score of 82 based on eighty-six reviews. Cubed3 mentioned ''3D All-Stars'' "represents the pinnacle of platforming goodness and with perfect ports". GamingTrend refers the game as a must-have if the stakeholder never played them or have no means of playing the copies you own. GamingTrend mentioned that "the included soundtracks are not enough to make this 35th anniversary package feel special enough to warrant a buy."<ref>{{cite web| url = https://www.metacritic.com/game/switch/super-mario-3d-all-stars| title = Super Mario 3D All-Stars for Switch Reviews| accessdate = 2020-09-29| work = Metacritic}}</ref>

Kevin Knezevic on GameSpot rated the game as 8/10, saying, "despite their age, the games are still rife with inventive ideas and surprises, which more than makes up for the collection's presentational shortcomings."<ref>{{cite web| url = https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/super-mario-3d-all-stars-review-shoot-for-the-stars/1900-6417564/| title = Super Mario 3D All-Stars Review – Shoot For The Stars| accessdate = 2020-09-29| author = Kevin Knezevic| date = 2020-09-18| work = GameSpot}}</ref>

=== Sales ===
The game sold 9.01 million units worldwide throughout its lifespan.

== Trivia ==
*There was controversy about the game when it was discovered to have a limited release. Because of that, people started making internet memes about the ending of [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'']] franchise on March 31, 2021. There was also criticism of the game not including ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]],'' nor ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]],'' neither ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]].''  
*This was originally planned to be announced as an E3 2020 presentation, but due to [[wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]], it became a Nintendo Direct presentation.
*In Brazil, ''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'' was a launch game for the Nintendo Switch.
*All the three games were emulated on the Switch.
*Even though [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|''Super Mario Galaxy 2'']] wasn't included in the collection, the "You Got A Star" jingle from that game is used as the collection's start up music.
