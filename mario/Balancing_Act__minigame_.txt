'''Balancing Act''' is a Duel minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the two players are on top of balls in a circus and must race to the end of the course. The course features several elevating platforms that serve as obstacles. [[Thwomp]]s are another hazard of the course where if a player is flattened by a Thwomp, they will not be able to move for a few seconds. The player that reaches to the end first wins.

*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} - Roll foward/Roll backward
