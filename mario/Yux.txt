A '''Yux''' is a minor enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It was genetically engineered at the hands of the [[Secret Society of X-Nauts|X-Nauts]], who were also responsible for their resemblance to a cross shape.

==History==
The regular Yux can be found in [[The Great Tree]] during the X-Naut takeover during Chapter 2. They are often found near regular X-Nauts and can attack from a distance with their beams resulting in a [[First Strike]] should it hit Mario.
