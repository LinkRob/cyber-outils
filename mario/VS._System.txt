The '''VS. System''' is a series of [[arcade]] system released by [[Nintendo]] from 1984 through 1990. Its games consist of various adaptations of [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games, such as ''[[VS. Super Mario Bros.]]'', which was [[reissue]] of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' with some original levels that later appeared in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''. Years later, VS. System games were among several ported to the [[Nintendo Switch]] as purchasable [[Arcade Archives]] titles. VS. Systems were designed as either traditional upright arcade cabinets or in tabletop form.
