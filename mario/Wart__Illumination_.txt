'''Wart''' is the leader of the [[8 Bits]] and a crime lord who resides in a casino within a futuristic city. He is seen hostile towards [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]] and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] when they infiltrate his casino, being seen summoning minions such as [[Birdo (Illumination)|Birdo]], [[Mouser (Illumination)|Mouser]], and [[Ninji (Illumination)|Ninjis]] to battle them as Wart watches.

==Profile==

=== Physical description ===
Wart is a green toad, who wears a robe, a crown and a gold necklace with a red gem.

== History ==
Wart makes his debut in the film when [[Princess Peach]] and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] infiltrate the [[Wart's Casino|casino that Wart and his goons are running]] beneath the [[Gateway Galaxy]]'s spaceport. Princess Peach asks him about [[Rosalina]]'s whereabouts, only for Wart to summon his lieutenants [[Birdo]], [[Clawgrip]], and [[Mouser]]. Peach defeats them, but Wart summons his [[Ninji]] army, which she defeats as well. Wart gets angry and fires a barrage of bubbles from his mouth, but is stopped when Peach stuffs a turnip into his mouth. When Peach uses a [[Super Mushroom]] to grow huge to make her interrogation more effective, Wart reveals that Rosalina has been captured in the [[Space Junk Galaxy]], where she will have her power used for the Boomsday Weapon on [[Planet Bowser]]. With his casino in ruins at the hands of Peach and Toad (who have departed to find a pilot that turns out to be [https://starfox.fandom.com/wiki/Fox_McCloud Fox McCloud] from the ''[https://starfox.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Fox_(series) Star Fox]'' franchise to take them to [[Planet Bowser]]), Wart contacts [[Bowser Jr.]], as evidenced by his enraged face in the UFO; however, neither he nor his goons are seen for the rest of the film.

==Behind the scenes==
He is voiced by [[Luis Guzmán]]. He is known for the movies ''Traffic'' (2000), ''Punch Drunk Love'', and ''Boogie Nights''. He also played Gomez Addams in the TV series ''Wednesday'' in all of his appearances.

He previously co-starred with [[Keegan-Michael Key]] in Keanu, and guest starred in ''Community'', starring [[Donald Glover]].
