'''Gaz''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.
{{br|left}}
==Physical description==
Gaz is a young [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with unique purple stripes on his head.

==History==
Gaz lives with his mother, the [[Rose Town Innkeeper]], in [[Rose Town]], where Gaz often plays with his dolls. His favorite doll is [[Geno]], which is the one he plays with the most. Gaz was excited to see [[Mario]] visit his home and gets him to play with his dolls. Gaz accidentally knocks out Mario with his Geno doll's finger gun, although Geno later comes to life.

Later, after defeating [[Bowyer]], Mario can get the [[Finger Shot]] weapon from Gaz. Additionally, as a reward for playing with Gaz, the Rose Town Innkeeper allows Mario to sleep for free at the [[inn]] whenever he desires.
