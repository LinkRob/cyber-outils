The '''Courage Meal''' is one of the [[recipe]]s made by [[Zess T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Using a Courage Meal in battle deals 4 [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage to an enemy in battle. Courage Meals can be created by using a [[Courage Shell]] with three of Zess T.'s named dishes including a [[Zess Special]], a [[Zess Dinner]], and a [[Zess Deluxe]].
