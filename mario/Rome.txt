'''Rome''' is the real-world capital city of [[Italy]]. It has made a few appearances in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media, with the first instance of this being in ''[[Mario is Missing!]]''. Rome is mostly recognized for its landmark [[Colosseum]].

==In ''Mario is Missing!''==
[[File:Rome MIM SNES.png|thumb|left|[[Luigi]] and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in Rome in ''Mario is Missing!''{{'}}s Super Nintendo version]]
Rome is one of the cities that [[Luigi]] visits on his journey to rescue [[Mario]].

==In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''==
The [[Night Tour]] introduces a course named [[Rome Avanti]], which takes place in Rome. Rome is the sixth European city in the game to be featured as a race course, following [[Paris]], [[London]], [[Berlin]], [[Amsterdam]], and [[Athens]], and is the first of them courses that take place during the nighttime.
