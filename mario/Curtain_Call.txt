'''Curtain Call''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. It is named after a term referring to when the cast bows at the end of a live show.

==Gameplay==
Players have to memorize the order of the [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, and [[Boo]]s onstage. The curtain then closes, at which point [[Toad]] comes by and asks all the players a question about the position of one of the characters (for example, "Who was the second person from the right?"). There are three characters on the first turn, five on the second turn, and lastly seven on the third turn. If one of the players gets the wrong answer, that player is eliminated and dances for the rest of the minigame. All players who never make a single mistake after three rounds win. If everyone makes a mistake, the game ends in a [[draw]].
