The '''Dyllis Breakfast''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing a [[Shroom Shake]] and [[Spicy Soup]]. It restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]. This meal is one of many recipes made by Dyllis in ''Super Paper Mario''.
