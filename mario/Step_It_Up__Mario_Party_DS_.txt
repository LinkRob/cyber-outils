'''Step It Up''' is a minigame competition found in the Minigame Mode of ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The goal of the contest is to win the most minigames to make it to the top of the steps. The host of the contest can choose how many wins are needed (3, 5, or 7), what type of minigames to play, (4-Player, 2-Vs-2, 1-Vs-3, or Random) and COM difficulty (unless playing the multiplayer mode and the three other players are humans). If the host is setting the contest to 1-Vs-3 or 2-Vs-2 mode, then they can select the teams. If it is set to Random, and one of those minigames are chosen, then the teams are randomly selected.

During gameplay, when players win a minigame, they'll earn a badge to move themselves (or their team) up a step, while the losing players do not progress forward. The player(s), or team that gets all badges moves to the top of the steps, and do their victory dances as the contest ends.
