'''Mini Soccer''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' for the [[Game Boy Advance]]. It is obtained by completing the quest [[A Speeding Bill]].

==Description==
Mini Soccer is a two-player soccer minigame played on a single game system. The objective for each player is to kick the ball to their opponent's goal to win.
