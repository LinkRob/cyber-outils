'''Ratfael''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is similar to a [[Munchelangelo]] or [[DaVincheese]] but is a bit stronger.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Ratfaels have an orange body and wear purple pants.

===Behavior===
Like their relatives, Ratfaels attack by biting at [[Wario]], which they attempt to do upon noticing him.

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Ratfaels can only be found onboard the [[S.S. Caviar]] as common enemies. Wario can defeat them using any attack.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Much like Munchelanglos and DaVincheese, Ratfaels are named after a famous Renaissance painter, in this case, {{w|Raphael}}. Ratfael's name is a portmanteau of "rat" and "Raphael."
