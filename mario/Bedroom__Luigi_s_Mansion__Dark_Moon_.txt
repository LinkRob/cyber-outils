|first = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}The '''Bedroom''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. This room is accessed by entering the middle door in the Master Hall. The bedroom contains a folding screen Luigi can look into to see the Mushroom Amethyst. There is a stove next to the bed, along with coat hangers and a door on the north wall that is a Trapdoor. Pulling on the cord near this door reveals a bed Luigi can sit on, yanking him backwards into the study.
==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-2: Gear Up!|A-2: Gear Up!]], Luigi must head into the Bedroom and use the bed to be launched into the study, where one of the Slammers is holding a stolen gear. In [[A-5: Sticky Situation]], the bedroom is one of the few rooms that will always be covered in spiderwebs (due to the large web behind the folding screen.

In only A-5, you can use a cobweb to light the fire from the stove on the big web, allowing Luigi to get the Mushroom Amethyst.
