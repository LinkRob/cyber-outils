'''Wheel of Woe''' is a 1-vs.-3 Mic [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a dark, brick-walled room with torches lit on each side.

==Gameplay==
The solo character has to eliminate the team of three, who are riding on a cog, within a [[time limit]] of 30 seconds. The solo character can call various commands into the [[Nintendo GameCube Microphone|Mic]], or from a blue box if the Mic option is off. The commands that the solo character can give include launching Bullet Bills from [[Bill Blaster]]s, releasing [[Chain Chomp]]s from their cages, and having a [[Shy Guy]] control which direction the cog spins in. A teammate can [[jump]] onto an incoming Bullet Bill to have it crash. If a character is either hit by an enemy or falls into the [[pit]], they are eliminated. The solo character wins if they eliminate the three team members, but if at least one team member is remaining at the end, they win instead.

==Controls==
===Solo (1 player)===
* {{button|gcn|mic}}: Say: "Bullet Bill," "Chain Chomp," "Shy Guy"

===Group (3 players)===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Jump

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''One player uses the Mic to unleash enemies on the other 3 players. Last one standing is the winner!''
* '''Advice''': ''If a Bullet Bill is on your tail, jump on it to make it crash.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a play on ''Wheel of Fortune'', but substitutes "fortune" with an antonym, "woe."
