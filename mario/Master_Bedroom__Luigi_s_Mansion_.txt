The '''Master Bedroom''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Master Bedroom has a double bed, a vanity, a cabinet, a nightstand, a ceiling fan, and a plant that can be watered to reveal a [[Green Stone]]. Both the left and right windows are each covered by a light-yellow curtain depicting flower patterns. The curtain on the right covers a window that provides a view of the second floor's [[Balcony (2F)|Balcony]], where a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] is. The Master Bedroom is only one of two rooms with a ceiling fan, the other one being the [[Billiards Room]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Master Bedroom is the penultimate room accessed in [[Area One]]. Once [[Luigi]] has obtained the room's [[key]] from the [[Study (Luigi's Mansion)|Study]], he can unlock the door to the Master Bedroom and open it.

In the Master Bedroom, [[Lydia]] is always seen in front of her vanity. To capture Lydia, Luigi has to open the curtain on the right for [[wind]] blow into the Master Bedroom, distracting Lydia and leaving her vulnerable to the [[Poltergust 3000]]. If Luigi does not capture Lydia in a single try, she closes the curtain and returns to her vanity. After Lydia is captured, a [[treasure chest]] with the [[key]] to the [[Nursery]] appears.

During the [[blackout]], two [[Gold Ghost]]s and three [[Grabbing Ghost]]s appear in the Master Bedroom.
