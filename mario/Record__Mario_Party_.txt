The '''Record''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
The Record can be used in the [[Juke Box]] in the [[Option House]]. This allows the player to listen to every song in the game, effectively serving as an in-game soundtrack. The US version of the game has 45 songs for the Record while the Japanese and European versions have 46 songs. This is due to the "Move to the Mambo!" song going unused in the US version.

The item costs 50 [[coin]]s to buy at the [[Mushroom Shop]].
