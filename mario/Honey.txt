'''Honey''' is a yellow material can be found in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' in the [[Honeyhive Galaxy]], but only on a separate path. It is very  adhesive, to the point where it causes [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to stick to the honey, making them to capable only small [[jump]]s. The path is basic the track Mario or Luigi have to move over to end up at the [[Power Star]] in the [[Honeyhive Cosmic Mario Race]] mission, where [[Cosmic Mario]] or Cosmic Luigi gets pushed in the honey.

[[Honeybee]]s can also be found meeting the honey in buckets, holding it is overweight. It also debuts on certain walls that Mario or Luigi can push to when in [[Bee Mario|bee form]].
