A '''Block Sensor''' is a rare item in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. It costs 20 [[coin]]s. It can only be found in a [[Grab Bag (item)|Grab Bag]] or during a 20+-turn game when a Grab Bag is also available. When used, on the next Space the player stops, a [[Hidden Block (Mario Party series)|Hidden Block]] appears, even if the space is not a [[Blue Space]]. Inside the hidden block could be anything, such as coins, a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] or even a [[Ztar]]. Computer players never buy this item when they visit the [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|item shop]].
