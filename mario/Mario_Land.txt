{{Nihongo|'''Mario Land'''|マリオランド<ref>{{cite manual|game=Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins|language=Japanese|page=1 and 13}}</ref>|Mario Rando|Mario Land}} is a land ruled by [[Mario]]. It consists of six areas, [[Tree Zone]], [[Space Zone]], [[Macro Zone]], [[Pumpkin Zone]], [[Mario Zone]] and [[Turtle Zone]]. Mario Land made its appearance in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

== Description ==
Hence the name, Mario rules '''Mario Land'''. Mario Land consists of six primary areas:
*'''Tree Zone''' is an area located southwest of the map. [[Big Bird]] guarded this zone after Wario's conquered Mario Land.
*'''Space Zone''' is an area located north of the map. It takes place inside a tree. It takes place outer space. [[Tatanga]] guarded this zone after Wario's conquered Mario Land.
*'''Macro Zone''' is an area located next to the [[Hippo (level)|Hippo]]. It takes place in a small house. [[Sewer Rat]] guarded this zone after Wario's conquered Mario Land.
*'''Pumpkin Zone''' is an area located northwest of the map. It takes place inside a pumpkin. [[Witch (Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins)|Witch]] guarded this zone after Wario's conquered Mario Land.
*'''Mario Zone''' is an area located west of the map. It takes place inside a mechanical figure that strongly resembles Mario. [[Three Little Pigs]] guarded this zone after Wario's conquered Mario Land.
*'''Turtle Zone''' is an area located southeast of the map. It takes place inside a giant turtle. [[Octopus|Pako]] guarded this zone after Wario's conquered Mario Land.

== History ==

=== Wario taking over Mario Land ===
At some point before the events of the game, while Mario was in [[Sarasaland]] to save [[Princess Daisy]] during an [[Super Mario Land|incident]] caused by [[Tatanga]], [[Wario]] invaded Mario Land and conquered it. [[Wario]] brainwashed Mario Land's citizens under his magic spell. Wario took over [[Mario's castle]] and scattered six coins in the six areas.<ref>{{cite manual|game=Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins||page=3-4}}</ref>

=== ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'' ===
After Mario defeated Tatanga, saved Sarasaland and rescued Princess Daisy. He learned Wario conquered his land. Mario went to six areas and collected the coins. Mario went to his stolen castle and used the six coins to unlock the castle. Mario confronted Wario and defeated him. Wario surrendered and plotted to conquer [[Kitchen Island]]. Mario reconquered his land and his castle.
