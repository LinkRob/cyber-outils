'''Yoshi Platforms''' are a type of object in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
A Yoshi Platform is a flat rectangular prism that can be temporarily formed if [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] spits out [[juice]] on certain enemies, such as [[Cheep Cheep]]s. The Yoshi Platform matches the color of the Yoshi spitting out the juice. Yoshi Platform's behavior differs depending on its color, with purple ones moving horizontally, pink ones moving vertically, and orange Yoshi Platforms remaining in one position.

All three types of Yoshi Platforms disappear after a while. For certain enemies, such as Cheep Cheeps, this changes them back into their regular form. A [[Cataquack]] is always defeated instantly upon reverting back from being a Yoshi Platform.

If a [[Red Boo|Pink Boo]] is sprayed with either a Yoshi's juice or [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]]'s water, it turns into a pink Ghost Block, which is essentially the same as a pink Yoshi Platform except it remains stationary like an orange Yoshi Platform, and the Pink Boo's eyes can be seen on one side.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Yoshi Platforms are prominently used in the [[Ricco Harbor]] episode [[Yoshi's Fruit Adventure]], where Yoshi has to turn several Cheep Cheeps into Yoshi Platforms along his and [[Mario]]'s path to the [[Shine Sprite]].
