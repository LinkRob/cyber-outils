The '''Palace Dungeon Guard''' is a minor character in the mid-credits scene of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

A shrunken [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] is sent to the dungeons as prisoner. In his cell, Bowser playing his piano and singing louder has caused a Toad guard to quiet down as he shuts the door.
