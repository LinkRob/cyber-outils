'''New York Minute''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', taking place in [[New York City]]. It made its debut appearance in the New York Tour, which was the first tour of ''Mario Kart'', taking place in September 2019. ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' for the [[Nintendo Switch]] would later feature this course as the first course of the second wave of the [[Booster Course Pass]].

== Layout ==
The first two routes of New York Minute focus on Central Park and the city streets, with the second lap being more focused on the outskirts. The third route focuses on the 30 Rockefeller Plaza.

In the first lap, the racers follow the first route, while on the second lap, they follow the second route. On the final lap, they follow the third route.

== Trivia ==
*This track's formula is similar to [[Tokyo Blur]], [[London Loop]], [[Amsterdam Drift]], and [[Vancouver Velocity]].
