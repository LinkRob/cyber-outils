A '''Muddy Buddy'''<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 128.</ref> is a purple creature in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', appearing exclusively once in Level 5-4, [[Sluggy The Unshaven's Fort]]. When it comes into contact with the [[Yoshi]], it will stick to his feet, allowing him to safely traverse over spikes. Yoshi can stop riding it with a Ground Pound. Unlike [[Poochy]], another rideable creature, Yoshi can control the Muddy Buddy. In the game's remake, ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', it makes an additional appearance in Secret 2, [[Mystery of the Castle?]].
