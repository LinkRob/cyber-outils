The '''Dash Mushroom''' is an item from the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]] bearing the same appearance as [[Mushroom]]s and generally [[Super Mushroom]]s. Like other items, Dash Mushrooms are obtained from [[Item Box]]es, and grant a burst of speed to its user upon being used. In several ''Mario Kart'' games, Dash Mushrooms can also be acquired in a set of three, specifically named [[Triple Mushrooms]]. The [[Mushroom Cup]]'s logo is also a Dash Mushroom.

==Description==
Using a Dash Mushroom gives the racer a powerful boost of speed to make them drive incredibly fast for a limited time (enough for them to skip off-road surfaces and making shortcuts at certain tracks). As of ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', a racer drops their Dash Mushroom on the road if they are hit by an item. Dash Mushrooms on the road are used automatically by whoever drives into them.

A variation of the normal Mushroom is the [[Golden Mushroom]], which first appeared in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. It is far more powerful than the normal Mushroom, and can be used multiple times until it wears off.

An alternative to the Mushroom is the [[Mini-Turbo]], which can be achieved by drifting and performing a special technique that follows the [[Drift|Power Slide]]. The technique varies between the games, ranging from sliding for a continuous time to turning left and right alternately to holding the drift for a longer time. Mini-Turbos, however, gives a much shorter boost, and cannot be used to quickly skip off-road surfaces.
