'''Bunmawashi Dolls''' are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''. They are only seen in [[Be Afraid of the Dark]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bunmawashi Dolls are yarn, clown-like dolls that carry an axe around. Their heads are white with beady black eyes, brown hair sticking out of each side, a bulbous pink nose, and green feet.

===Behavior===
A Bunmawashi Doll constantly walks around with its hands drooped, sometimes looking around if the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] is nearby when it is idle. When the Bunmawashi Doll notices Yoshi, it screeches and then quickly runs toward Yoshi, whom it tries to chop with its axe. A Bunmawashi Doll can sometimes appear if Yoshi activates a [[Winged Cloud]].

Bunmawashi Dolls are vulnerable to light, although it can only stun them for a few seconds. Bunmawashi Dolls cannot be defeated through any means. 

==In ''Yoshi's Crafted World''==
A Bunmawashi Doll is only encountered in the level [[Be Afraid of the Dark]]. If a Yoshi does get hit by a Bunmawashi Doll, he takes a lot of damage. A Yoshi can avoid a Bunmawashi Doll if he jumps up to a high enough area out of their own reach. Bunmawashi Dolls cannot go off ledges, so being on a lower platform can help avoid their attacks. They can also destroy crates with their axes.
