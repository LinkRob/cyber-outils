'''Ghost Vases''' are a possessed vase which appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', usually within the game's various [[Ghost House]]s being placed amongst real Vases. Upon first glance, Ghost Vases appear to be perfectly harmless just like a normal Vase however as the heroes get closer. The Ghost Vase illuminates itself with a pink glow, lifts itself in the air whilst making an eerie sound. Upon lifting itself into the air, it will start to target [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] before flying at the plumbers damaging them upon contact. The Plumbers have to quickly move out of the way or duck for cover as the Ghost Vase attacks. Whether the Ghost Vase hits the heroes or the wall or flooring, it shatters revealing a [[Coin]]. Ghost Vases can also be instantly destroyed whilst under the effects of the [[Starman]]. Ghost Vases are found in [[World 3-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3-Ghost House]], [[World 4-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4-Ghost House]], and [[World 7-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7-Ghost House]]. A similar hazard to them called [[Ghost Block]]s can also be found in this game.

==Trivia==
*Vases similar to the Ghost Vases are found in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' in the various [[Ghost House]]s. However, none of these Vases are Ghost Vases as they don't move to attack [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]].
*Ghost Vases are one of the few enemies from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' to not return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.
