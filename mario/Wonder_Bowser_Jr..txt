'''Wonder Bowser Jr.''' is a unique power-up of [[Bowser Jr.]]'s introduced in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

== Description ==
After absorbing the [[Wonder Flower]]'s effects, Bowser Jr. gains a massive visual upgrade. His skin is blue (similar to [[Dark Bowser]] from [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]) while his shell is now a deep purple with green spikes. His hair now resembles a green flame. Wonder Bowser Jr. also sports a massive jaw that he can blast off as a deadly projectile weapon. You have to fight him 4 times  in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]].
