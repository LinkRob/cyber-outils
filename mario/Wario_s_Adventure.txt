'''Wario's Adventure''' is a microgame from [[WarioWare, Inc: Mega Microgames!|''WarioWare, Inc: Mega Microgames!'']], serving as the final boss microgame. It can be found in the Anything Goes folder, which is the last stage. It makes a return as a standard microgame in [[WarioWare Gold|''WarioWare Gold'']]. [[Wario]] acts as its host in both games, under the guise of [[Wario Deluxe]] in the latter game.

== Gameplay ==

=== ''WarioWare: Mega Microgames!'' ===
In the game, [[Wario]] acts as the microgame's protagonist, having to go through a multiple-part journey. Here, he is universally harmed by red objects, and is aided by green objects.

==== Phase 1 ====
This phase is similar to the microgame [[City Surfer]], where Wario, who is riding on a skateboard, has to jump over floating red squares short enough to jump over and duck under walls. If he hits any obstacle, he dies, defeating the player. Wario ducks when the player presses down on the control pad and jumps when they press the A button. At harder levels, Wario has to dodge more walls and squares.

==== Phase 2 ====
During this phase, Wario has to dodge spikes that drop down from the ceiling, with the same results as hitting a wall or square block if he fails to dodge them. Wario can speed up or slow down his skateboard during this phase to dodge the spikes depending on which direction on the control pad the player inputs: right causes him to speed up, with left causing him to slow down. More spikes appear at harder levels.

==== Phase 3 ====
This combines the first two phases. Wario can speed up or slow down, jump, and duck during this phase. The phase ends when a jetpack shows up.  More obstacles appear at harder levels, with the jetpack being closer to the ground on easier levels.

==== Phase 4 ====
Wario rides the jetpack that he grabbed at the end of phase 3 here, and can move left and right freely. In this part, Wario has to destroy floating rocks with his ramming attack, done by pressing the A button. The rocks are closer to the bottom of the screen on easier difficulties.

==== Phase 5 ====
This phase involves a crimson ceiling that must be smashed through, done by grabbing fuel tanks that speed Wario up. Grabbing all five causes him to smash through the ceiling, progressing to the next phase as the text "Smash!" appears; if the player misses a fuel tank, the text "Whoa!" appears and Wario dies upon impact with the ceiling, causing a loss. The tanks do not move on the first level, but can on harder levels.

==== Phase 6 ====
This phase starts when Wario reaches the treasure, but a monster appears, and Wario gets a boomerang as a weapon, which he can throw at the monster to deal damage. The monster attacks with a [[Ground Pound]], which Wario must avoid, lest the player loses. The monster's health is increased on higher difficulties.

Upon completion of this phase, Wario gets his hands on the treasure, but a boulder drops down, forcing him to flee the scene.

==== Phase 7 ====
To win this phase, the player must press A when Wario stops to make him start running again. If the player does this enough times, Wario slips through a wall, escaping the boulder with his prize, and the player wins.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
This microgame is reduced to a regular microgame, with each level having one segment of a storyline.

* '''Level 1 (Easy)''': Wario is riding a green skateboard forward, and must dodge three red blocks, jumping with the A button and ducking with down on the control pad. He must then jump toward a green balloon, which he uses to drift down into a pit. If Wario misses the balloon, he falls into the pit and presumably dies, and the player will be defeated.
* '''Level 2 (Hard)''': Wario is descending through the pit with his balloon from the first level here. The player must make him glide left and right dodging horizontal spiked pillars that reach sideways through the walls of the pit, with some growing on the screen. The player must then move him to a [[Warp Pipe]] at a random position on the bottom of the screen; failure to reach it causes him to get eaten by red jaws lining the bottom, resulting in defeat.
* '''Level 3 (Expert)''': Wario swims to the right and must dodge four red monsters that lunge from the left, using up and down on the control pad to change altitude. At the end, Wario must reach the surface to climb out with a green ladder. The ending has the following outcomes:
** Victory: Victory involves Wario jumping back onto his skateboard from the first level, after which he dashes off to the right.
** Defeat: Wario will hit the spikes if he is too deep.
