|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}The '''Netherworld''' is a location mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Netherworld is mentioned a few times in the game. Once by [[Craw-Daddy]] of the [[Glitz Pit]] saying he will send [[Mario]] to the depths of the Netherworld after his defeat. The other time is when the [[Shadow Queen]] is mentioned to be banished to the Netherworld after her defeat.
