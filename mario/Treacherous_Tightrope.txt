}}'''Treacherous Tightrope''' is a 4-player minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, four players are perched on high tightropes carrying large roles. The objective is for them to slowly walk across the tightrope and reach the other side. The players must stay balanced as they walk along or they will fall down and lose. The player who reaches the end first wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|wiimote}} - Move and balance
