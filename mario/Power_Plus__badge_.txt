'''Power Plus''' is a [[badge]] which appears in the [[Paper Mario series|''Paper Mario'' series]]. Wearing it gives [[Mario]] a bonus to his attack power at a price of six [[Badge Point|BP]]. Power Plus has a partner-based equivalent named [[Power Plus P]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', there are two Power Plus Badges; one of them is found in [[Shooting Star Summit]], where [[Merlow]] gives it to Mario at a price of 25 [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]], and the other one is guarded by [[Anti Guy]], who must be defeated to obtain his Power Plus.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Power Plus badges are obtained from [[Dazzle]], who sells them for fifteen Star Pieces, inside of Creepy Steeple where [[Doopliss]]' [[Parrot (character)|Parrot]] is at, or by stealing them from [[Wizzerd]]s.
