'''Keipu''' (ケイプ, ''Keipu'') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. This enemy resembles a miniature witch and will try to snatch [[1-Up Heart|1-Ups]] away from [[Mario]]. This enemy is also harmless, so if Mario touches it, he can retrieve the 1-Up back without any trouble.
