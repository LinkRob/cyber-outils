The '''Queen Bee''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The kart is identical to the [[Bumble V]], but with inverted colors. The kart also uses the [[Sponge]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Queen Bee is a High-End kart. It was introduced in the 2019 [[Halloween Tour]], but became the signature kart for [[Monty Mole]] in the [[Flower Tour]]. The Queen Bee increases the duration after hitting a [[Dash Panel]] and the points earned for it.
