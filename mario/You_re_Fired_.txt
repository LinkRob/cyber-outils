'''You're Fired''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. It is a pun of "you're fired", which is a phrase for terminating employees from the workplace.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to fire the cannon or two cannons with fire. If the fire contacts water, the fire will extinguish.
*'''Level 1:''' There is a cannon to fire.
*'''Level 2:''' There is a cannon and a water sprout.
*'''Level 3:''' There are two cannons and  a water sprout.
