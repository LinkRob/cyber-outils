'''Peepa Bell''' is a board item in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The player can use the item to summon a [[Peepa]] to take a coin from a rival each time they move.

== In ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' ==
Peepa Bell can be obtained when landing on an [[Item Space]]. If the player uses the Peepa Bell, the user can choose which rival they want to be inflicted by the Peepa. Each time the inflicted player moves a space, they lose 1 coin. After their turn is finished, the Peepa will leave.
