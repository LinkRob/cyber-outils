'''Fire Shell''' is an armor used by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Equipping it increases Bowser's [[Defense]] by 18 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 9. The Fire Shell can be obtained either from buying it from [[Hinopio]] in [[Barrel Volcano]] for 90 [[coin]]s or from [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]].
