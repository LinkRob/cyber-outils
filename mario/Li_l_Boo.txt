A '''Li'l Boo''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Li'l Boos are a smaller variant of [[Boo]]s that are only encountered in [[The Factory]].

==Battle==
During battle, Li'l Boos often attack by scaring at a single target. They can also use spells such as [[Lightning Orb]] and [[Willy Wisp]].
