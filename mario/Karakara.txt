'''Karakara''' (カラカラ, ''Karakara'') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It is a one-eyed umbrella that jumps and glides back down trying to hit [[Mario]].

== Trivia ==
*Karakara is based on the Japanese mythological yōkai spirit ''Karakasa'' or ''Kasa-Obake,'' which resembles a living one-eyed umbrella.
