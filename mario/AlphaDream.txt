'''AlphaDream Corporation Ltd.''' (or just '''AlphaDream''') was a Japanese video game development studio who was responsible for the development of games in the [[Mario & Luigi series|''Mario & Luigi'' series]].

On October 1, 2019, the company ceased operations immediately after filing for bankruptcy, citing sluggish revenues and the high development costs exceeding overall debt.<ref>(October 2, 2019) [https://headlines.yahoo.co.jp/hl?a=20191002-00010002-teikokudb-ind 『マリオ＆ルイージRPG』など開発　アルファドリームが破産開始] ''Yahoo Japan''. Retrieved October 1, 2019.</ref><ref>https://mobile.twitter.com/Doctor_Cupcakes/status/1179467153906511873?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw</ref>

==History==
Originally known as AlphaStar, the company was founded on January 12, 2000 as a second-party developer for [[Nintendo]]. Their first game, ''Koto Battle: Tengai no Morbito'', was only released in Japan for the [[Game Boy Color]].
