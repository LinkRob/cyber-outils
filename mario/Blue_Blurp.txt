'''Blue Blurps''' are a type of enemy which appears in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Behavior==
Unlike their red counterpart, [[Red Blurp]]s, Blue Blurps always stay within the water and do not [[jump]] out. They try to spray bursts of water at a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], similar to how a [[Spray Fish]] spits out a jet stream of water for a few seconds. One behavior that Blue Blurps share with Red Blurps is that they chase a Yoshi if it is in the water.
