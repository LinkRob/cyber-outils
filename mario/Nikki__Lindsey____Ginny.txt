'''Nikki, Lindsey and Ginny''' are three bosses in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', they are on the [[The Last Resort#Twisted Suites .2811F.29|eleventh floor]] of the Last Resort. They are three magicians which are fought in the Twisted Suites, they have the [[The Last Resort#The_Spectral_Catch_.2812F.29|twelfth floor's]] elevator button. They have 100HP each.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Nikki, Lindsey and Ginny are three purple magician ghosts with blonde hair and white eyes. They are all wearing dark purple clothes, and they all have at least one pony tail.

== In ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' ==
Nikki, Lindsey and Ginny are found in the Twisted Suites.

=== Battle ===
Nikki, Lindsey and Ginny hid into their hats first, they transferred [[Luigi]] to an octagon-shaped stage, and bowed to him. After taunting Luigi with the bow, they will duck into their hats and began spinning like [[Topper]]. They will now launch cards at Luigi which you have to avoid, after each of them launched cards, they will orbit the stage and shoot more cards to Luigi. You will find out that they will move closer and closer, when they are close enough, Luigi has to burst to flip their hats. Each hat has a line and Luigi has to pull one of them to pull out whether Nikki, Lindsey or Ginny. Flash and suck the ghost that you pulled out immediately to prevent them from casting a spell. The remaining girls will replace the sucked up magician with a bomb and a hat covering it, so Luigi can't pull the bomb or else it explodes. The third time, the remaining girl will replace the other two girls with two bombs angrily, and this time the lights will flash to make it harder to get the right hat. After defeating the three magicians, Luigi will recieve the twelfth floor's elevator button.
