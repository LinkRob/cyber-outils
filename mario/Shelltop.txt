|members = [[Mole Hunt Host]]}}'''Shelltops''' are a race of shell-like creatures that appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They live on [[Pi'illo Island]] along with the [[Zeekeeper]]s and the [[Brock]]s.
==Appearance==
Shelltops resemble [[Toad (species)|Toads]] except they have shells on their heads. They also vary in color.
===Notable Shelltops===
*'''Receptionists''': These Shelltops are seen in [[Pi'illo Castle]] where they give interesting information about certain places on Pi'illo Island.
*'''[[Britta's Work Force|Workers]]''': These Shelltops are usually seen in [[Dozing Sands]] where they work for the very strict Britta. Their most defining feature is the yellow construction helmet with a headlight. Even though Britta bosses them around, the workers still show respect for her.
*'''Tour Guides''': These Shelltops are seen in [[Wakeport]] and [[Mount Pajamaja]]. They usually help with the tour to Mount Pajamaja but dislike it due to the dangers of the mountain.
*'''[[Mole Hunt Host]]''': This elderly Shelltop is a brown that hosts the [[Mole Hunt]] game.
==Trivia==
*The Shelltops bear similar traits to [[Noki]]s from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]] ''as they both are small and wear shells. However, Shelltops wear shells on their heads, while Nokis wear shells on their torsos.
