'''And the Winner is...''' is a microgame in [[WarioWare: Twisted!]] as [[Mona]]'s microgame. It returns as [[Jimmy T.]]'s microgame in [[WarioWare Gold|''WarioWare Gold'']].

== Gameplay ==
The player controls two men holding the tape at the finish line. The player can move the men by tilting the system but with a small turn. If the person in first place passes the tape, the player wins the microgame.
* '''Level 1:''' Two men racing with no distractions.
* '''Level 2:''' Two men racing, but one may trip their way.
* '''Level 3:''' Three men racing, but two men fall their way.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
This is Jimmy T.'s microgame which its genre is "''Sport''" and requires "Mash". Press the pad, moving left or right to control the two men holding the tape.
