'''Boy''' is a character who appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
Boy is a football player who plays for the [[Diamond City Roughs]]. He only appears during Mona's stage. Boy has a crush on [[Mona]], and tries hard to win against [[Team Dinosaur]] to try and impress her. In the end cutscene for Mona's stage, Boy manages to secure a last-minute score during the football game, thinking of Mona. Outside the stadium, Boy tries to confess his feelings to Mona, although she had already drove away on her [[Mona's Scooter|scooter]].
