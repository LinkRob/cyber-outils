'''Sal Out''' is a female singer from [[Diamond City]] and a character from the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' series. She made first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'', in which Sal Out is the singer of the [[Mona Pizza (song)|Mona Pizza]] song for the restaurant of the [[Mona Pizza|same name]].

==Etymology==
Sal Out's name is a pun on the word "sellout," which is a word for someone who goes astray from their roots in favor of mass appeal.
