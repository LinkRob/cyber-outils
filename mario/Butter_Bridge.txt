The '''Butter Bridge''' is the larger of the two [[bridge]]s featured in the [[Twin Bridges]] area in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. It is made up of joined logs. Butter Bridge itself is a location divided into two levels, [[Butter Bridge 1]] and [[Butter Bridge 2]].
