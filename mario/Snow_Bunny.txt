The '''Snow Bunny''' is an item which appears in the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
[[File:SnowBunnyTTYD.png|thumb|left]]The Snow Bunny first appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

In this game, the Snow Bunny is capable of healing fifteen [[HP]] for [[Mario]]. If the holder eats it in battle, he/she becomes [[frozen]]. In the game, [[Zess T.]] makes this recipe by cooking an [[Ice Storm]] and a [[Golden Leaf]].

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
The Snow Bunny reappears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. Here, their differences include having an [[Ice Storm]] needing to be mixed with a [[Turtley Leaf]] by [[Dyllis]] instead of a Golden Leaf. The related [[Berry Snow Bunny]] can also be made by having Dyllis mix a Snow Bunny and a [[Pink Apple]]. A battle change is that it now has the capability of freezing all enemies onscreen.
