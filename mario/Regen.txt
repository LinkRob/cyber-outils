'''Regen''' (short for '''Regeneration''' and also known as '''HP Regen''' and '''FP Regen''') is a special status effect that appears in the ''[[Paper Mario series]]''.

==In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''==
In this game, there are two types of Regen that appear. As stated above, it being HP Regen and FP Regen.

===HP Regen===
HP Regen is mainly associated with [[Heart Point|HP]] being restored over time. There are numerous of ways to obtain the HP Regen status. The best way is to use a [[Slow Shroom]] that gradually restores 2 HP for the course of 10 turns. Certain dishes can also gradually restore HP over time but is usually different each time (such as the [[Meteor Meal]] restoring 2 HP for 3 turns but also restores 7 HP on the spot and the [[Couple's Cake]] restores 2 HP for 10 turns).Badges such as [[Happy Heart]] and [[Happy Heart P]] also does HP Regen but only restores 1 HP for the entire battle. This functions as the opposite of the [[Poison (status effect)|Poison]] effect.

===FP Regen===
FP Regen works similar to HP Regen but only for [[Flower Point|FP]]. The [[Gradual Syrup]] is a good item to use to achieve the FP Regen status. It restores 2 FP for the next 10 turns. The [[Lucky Start]] badge may grant the FP Regen status as also the [[Happy Flower]] badge.

== In the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'']] series ==

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' ===
Certain gear effects can give the wielder this condition in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can equip Angel Bangles to give themselves this status effect in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', regaining 20 [[Heart Point|hit points]] (abbreviated HP) and 10 [[Special Point|special points]] (abbreviated BP; literally "Bros. Points") at the start of their turn; however, the effect is temporarily dispelled from the wearer if something [[Dizzy|stuns him]] or [[Burn|sets him on fire]]. There are also HP Bangles which restore hit points and BP Bangles which restore Special Points.

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ===
This effect returns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', where the [[Sharpcask]] can hunker to gain a souped-up version of this status effect in conjunction with an exaggerated [[DEF-Up|defense boost]], doing so the first time it reaches 75% total health. [[Luigi]] can counter this with his [[Luigi Logic]] skill, manipulating the conveyor belt so that the Sharpcask gets crushed by falling crates; if Luigi is successful, these status effects get replaced with [[Dizzy]] and [[DEF-Down]].
