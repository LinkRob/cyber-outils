'''Fruit of the Doom''' ('''Fruit Fetch''' in the British English version) is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] appearing in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. Its American name is a reference to the {{wp|Fruit of the Loom}} company.

==Gameplay==
The players are on a waterfall and have to catch as many [[fruit]]s as possible. They have to avoid getting touched by [[Urchin]]s. The more fruits players collect, the more [[point]]s they earn. However, if they touch an Urchin, they drop fruits and lose three points. If the player falls off the waterfall, they lose time and three points. The player who gets the most points wins the minigame.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|pad}} − Move
*{{button|wii|2}} − [[Jump]]
