'''Kiryl''' (aka '''Cristo''' in the original English translations) is a character from ''[[Dragon Quest]]'' franchise. Kiryl appears with ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters in two crossover games ''[[Itadaki Street DS]]'' and ''[[Fortune Street]]'' as playable character. He is one of Zamoksva's two chancellors. He is also very loyal to [[Alena]] as he joins to help her on her quest.
