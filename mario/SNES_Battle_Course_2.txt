'''SNES Battle Course 2''' is one of the four [[Battle Stage]]s in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. Its theme is much like that of [[Koopa Troopa Beach|Koopa Beach]], being a tropical-themed battle course. Water is found all throughout the area, though considering the blocks in front of every water area, it is nearly impossible to fall in and drown.
