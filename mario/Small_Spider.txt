}}A '''Small Spider''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
The Small Spiders are only seen during the [[Giant Spider]] fight in [[Fungi Forest]] with [[Tiny Kong]]. The Small Spiders are summoned by the Giant Spider to attack Tiny while the Giant Spider spits mucus at Tiny. The Small Spiders are the only things that can damage Tiny in the fight and they are quite fast and durable. Defeating all of the Small Spiders that are present will cause the Giant Spinder to open it's eye allowing Tiny Kong to shoot it with her [[Feather Bow]].
