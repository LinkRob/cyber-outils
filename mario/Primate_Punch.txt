==Usage==
To use the Primate Punch, the player must hold the {{button|n64|z}} button then press {{button|n64|b}} to have Chunky use this techique. The Primate Punch can be used to hit certain switches (usually ones with his face on it) and is used to help defeat [[King Krusha K. Rool]] in the final battle.
