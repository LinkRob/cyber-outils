'''Yoshi Desert''' is the third course of [[Star Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. 

This level is, of course, desert-themed. The course's background has a [[Yoshi Sphinx]] and pyramids. On the track, there are quicksand areas where [[Piranha Plant]]s eat racer if they fall into the quicksand. A lot of cacti also reigns in this area.

== Trivia ==
*The quicksand and Piranha Plant in this course reappeared in [[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]] in [[Dry Dry Desert (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Dry Dry Desert]] where there is a part with quicksand and a hungry Piranha plant also.
*There is also a Yoshi Sphinx in [[Dry Dry Ruins (Mario Kart Wii)|Dry Dry Ruins]]. Though not confirmed, these two courses might possibly be linked to each other somehow.
