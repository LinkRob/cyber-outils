|members = Nommons of Somnom Woods.}}'''Nommons''' are a race of unique creatures that appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They are also known as the "Gentle children of Somnom" by [[Dreambert|Prince Dreambert]].

==Appearance==

Nommons are similar to [[Monty Mole]]s as being mole-like creatures. They also are mainly purple in color.

==History==

The Nommons live in [[Somnom Woods]] where they worship the "[[Pi'illo|Nom P'illos]]." They also speak in a way as they don't recongize anything from outside Somnom Woods. Given their serious nature in protecting the "Nom Pi'illos," they enjoy [[Nommon's Game|games]] and would even give up a "Nom Pi'illo" if someone plays with them.

==Trivia==
*The Nommons commonly refer to Mario and Luigi as "Nose-beards" due to their mustaches.
*"Nommon" is a palindrome.
*Nommons are similar to [[Oho Jee]]s from [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]] they look a bit similar and both talk unusual speech patterns.
