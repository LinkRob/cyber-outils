'''Water Block''' is a special move used by [[Sushie]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can be used once Sushie reaches the [[Super Rank]] and it requires 3 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

== Descriptoin ==
To use Water Block, the player must time the {{button|n64|a}} button pressing at the right moments to make this move last up to four turns. With Water Block, Mario's [[defense]] gets raised by 1 for up to four turns. This move can be useful when combining with [[Defend Plus]] badge.

== Trivia ==
*Water Block is one of four partner moves in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' that affects Mario directly. The others being [[Lady Bow]]'s [[Outta Sight]], [[Watt]]'s [[Turbo Charge]], and [[Lakilester]]'s [[Cloud Nine]].
