'''Ramp It Up''' is a [[microgame]] part of [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[Ninja Scribble]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name is a pun of the phase "amp it up," which means to turn up the volume or intensity.
