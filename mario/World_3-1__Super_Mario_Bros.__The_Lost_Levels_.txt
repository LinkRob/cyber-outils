'''World 3-1''' is the first [[level]] of [[World 3 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 3]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the ninth level overall in said game. In the original release and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]'', the level has a standard grassland palette, though ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' has the level covered in snow. This is the first level in the game with backwards [[Warp Zone]]s.

==Layout==
This level starts with the player facing two [[Hammer Bro]]thers on two rows of [[Brick Block]]s, one of which on the bottom row contains a [[Super Star]]. Across the gap in the right-most block of the first row of [[? Block]]s is a [[Poison Mushroom]], and the left-most ? Block of the top row contains a power-up. Through the [[Warp Pipe]] ahead is an underground area with [[coin]]s and a [[Bill Blaster]], which will lead out to a backwards Warp Zone that leads to [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 1-1]], though there is also a gap the player can chose to jump into to lose a life, thus avoiding the Warp Pipe and starting this level over. To the right of the next Warp Pipe above ground is a [[Hidden Block]] with a power-up, and above the Warp Pipe is a [[Vine|Beanstalk]] that leads to [[Coin Heaven]]. After some [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s and between a few Bill Blasters is a row of ? Blocks, with the left-most one containing a power-up. Directly above the Koopa Paratroopa ahead is a Hidden Block with a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. Through the second Warp Pipe afterward is another underground area that will exit to the Warp Pipe underneath the Beanstalk from earlier. After the four Warp Pipes is a [[Super Spring]] the player can use to jump over the [[Goal Pole]] ahead, which will lead to the same backwards Warp Zone from earlier.
