|name=POW Block (Battle Card)}}'''POW Block''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The POW Block is a blue block with the word "POW" labeled on the front face, with a cyan/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 100 coins

Sell Price: 50 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The POW Block is first seen in [[Marmalade Valley]], but can also found in [[Kiwano Temple]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Sacred Forest]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Mustard Café]], [[Redpepper Crater]], [[Mossrock Theater]], and [[The Emerald Circus]].
