'''World 2''' is a desert-themed [[world]] serving as the second world of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

It consists of six [[level]]s, a [[Tower]], a [[Castle]], a [[Warp Cannon]], and [[Roy Koopa]] for a boss. One area on the overworld consists of a few spots in which quicksand appears at random whenever [[Mario]] and other playable characters move on the world map. If they land where the quicksand is, the characters are forced into an [[Enemy Course]] where they have to fight [[Spiny]]ies. There are three [[Toad House]]s: a red one, which becomes available upon completing [[World 2-1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|2-1]]/[[World 2-2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|2-2]]; a green one, which becomes available upon completing [[World 2-Tower (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|2-Tower]]; and a golden one left of the Enemy Course area.

Various levels consist of [[Sand Spout]]s, a stream of sand that emerges from below, capable of taking Mario and other playable characters into the air. There is also [[Quicksand]], in which Mario and other characters gradually sink, and they have to try and [[jump]] out to avoid sinking into the quicksand.
