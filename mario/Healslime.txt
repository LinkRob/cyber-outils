'''Healslime''', previously known as '''Healer''', is a support character in ''[[Itadaki Street DS]]'' and ''[[Fortune Street]]''. Players are able to summon it for extra help after they have obtained its card, card #91 in both games. Once summoned, Healslimes give free suits when the player passes by them or if the player lands in the same square that has them. The player gets to choose what suit they want or if the player has all of their suits, he gives them a Suit Yourself card. After a few turns, the Healslime leaves the board. The Healslime in both games is [[w:c:dragonquest:Healie|Healie]] from ''[[w:c:dragonquest:Dragon Quest IV: Chapters of the Chosen|Dragon Quest IV: Chapters of the Chosen]]'' who originally assists [[w:c:dragonquest:Ragnar McRyan|Ragnar McRyan]] one of the game's party members. He even mentions Ragnar in one of his quotes once he leaves the board.
