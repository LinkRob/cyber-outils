The '''Anniversary Tour''' was the hundred-and-fifth tour which began on September 30, 2023 and ended on October 3, 2023. It was also a rerun that began on 18 September, 2024 to October 1, 2024 and on September 17, 2025 to September 30, 2025.

This Tour also celebrated the fourth, fifth and sixth anniversary in the rerun versions and was the final tour to add new content to [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']]. After the event, the following tours were reran from the previous tour events that happened after [[Anniversary Tour (2022)]] starting with [[Battle Tour (2022)|Battle Tour]].

The tour introduced 3 selection track versions of [[Athens Dash]], [[Rome Avanti]] and [[Madrid Drive]] as well as R/T variants of [[Berlin Byways 3]] and [[Sydney Sprint 3]]. [[Gold Mario (character)|Gold Mario]] is now added as a playable character, having been previously playable only in Coin Rush.
