'''Harry H''' is one of [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s rivals in ''[[Wario's Woods]]''{{'}}s [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version. He is the seventeenth and penultimate boss of VS COM mode, fought before [[Wario]] himself.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Harry H is a bipedal rabbit with light brown fur. It wears a blue suit with a red bowtie, red shoes, and light purple pants.

===Etymology===
The H in Harry's name presumably stands for "hare," a real-world species which, like rabbits, belong to the {{w|Leporidae}} family.
