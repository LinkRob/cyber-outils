The '''Peach Tournament''' is the final tournament in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Peach Tournament is held in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. It has to be unlocked first by completing the [[Island Open]].

==In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour''==
When the player wakes up in their room the morning after the Island Open, [[Alex]] tells them and their partner about the Peach Tournament. The player follows Alex to the statue next to the training facility that then opens, revealing a secret stairway. Once the player goes through, they find a secret airport, where [[Mario]] and [[Toadsworth]], having heard of the player's success, asks if they would like to come to the Mushroom Kingdom. Afterward, the player is taken in a private jet to [[Peach Dome]], where they and their partner can compete in either singles or doubles tournaments.
