This is the weakest Hammer of all and should only be used against weak enemies like [[Goomba]]s. They are very common early in the game espically in World 1 but become rather rare after World 4.
==Description==
Worn-Out Hammer stickers are just regular Hammer stickers with a white-patch on them. Worn-Out Hammers are extremely similar to [[Dusty Hammer]]s from the original ''[[Paper Mario]]'' game.
