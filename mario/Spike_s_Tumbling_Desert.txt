|game = ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''}}'''Spike's Tumbling Desert''' is the first level of [[Layer-Cake Desert]] in the game ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
==Layout==
The player begins the level near a [[? Block]]. A pit of [[quicksand]] is then encountered, along with [[Spike]]s on rising and falling [[Brick Block]]s. Two Spikes on the ground are then encountered, along with one on top of a ? Block. A [[Red Ring]] is then found. As the ground slopes upwards, three Spikes on Brick Blocks appear. At the bottom of a steep slope, a [[P Switch]] can be found. Two Spikes are encountered, along with the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Jump on the first Spike and get the Star Coin while also avoid the spike balls they hold up.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The wall to the left of the fifth Spike has a secret passageway leading to the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Activate the P Switch that cause the coins to transform into Brick Blocks. After that, jump on the second to last Spike and get the Star Coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Underground from the hill on the left of the P Switch, there's a modern Luigi picture in fossil form since it's underground doing a pose.
