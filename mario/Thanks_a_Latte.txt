'''Thanks a Latte''' is one the [[microgame]]s part of [[Wario]]'s [[Touch League]] in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Gameplay==
Using the [[stylus]], the player has to stir up a latte featuring Wario's face. By meeting this objective, the player wins, and Wario himself is shown at the bottom of the latte mug. If the objective is not met on time, the player loses. Each difficulty level requires the latte to be stirred more than in previous difficulty levels. 

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name is a portmanteau of "thanks a lot" and "latte," a type of coffee.
