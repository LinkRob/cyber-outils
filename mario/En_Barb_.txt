'''En Barb!''' is a [[Rhythm minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''. It is featured in the Rhythm Kitchen mode.

== Gameplay ==
The main objective is to perfectly skewer the food items being thrown, which is done by thrusting the joy con forward.  

== Trivia ==

* The minigame's title is a pun on the phrase "En Garde", which is used before the start of a sword duel.
