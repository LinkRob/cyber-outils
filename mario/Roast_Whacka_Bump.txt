The '''Roast Whacka Bump''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made from a [[Whacka Bump]]. Though it is rare, the Roast Whacka Bump only restores 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
