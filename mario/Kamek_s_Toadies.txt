'''Kamek's Toadies''' are a group of four particular [[Toady|Toadies]] who work for [[Kamek]] in the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. All four Toadies bear the standard appearance of a Toady in that they wear a red robe, have a propeller on their head, and swirled glasses similar to [[Kamek]]'s.

==In {{series|Yoshi's Island}}==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island/Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''===
If [[Baby Mario]] floats in a [[bubble]] for too long and the [[Countdown Timer]] is just about to reach zero, Kamek's Toadies come in to try and capture Baby Mario, although it is possible for a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to cancel it at the last second by finally jumping at the bubble, ending the Countdown Timer and causing the Toadies to retreat. If the Countdown Timer does manage to reach zero, the Toadies carry Baby Mario back to Kamek, and the player loses an [[extra life]].

===''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''===
Kamek's Toadies serve the same role as in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', although have kidnapped more babies around the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] due to Kamek wanting to get rid of the [[Star Children]]. The artwork of Kamek's Toadies associated with the game shows them kidnapping [[Baby Luigi]] in particular.
