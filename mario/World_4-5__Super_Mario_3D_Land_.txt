'''World 4-5''' is the twenty-first level of the game ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
The level contains more than just a few flip panels that, when activated, create new temporary land that Mario can cross over. After using these switches to create a small pyramid-like structure, Mario finds a teleportation box that leads to the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]]. After crossing a large bridge of flip panels, dodging Stingbys and spiked blocks, Mario makes his way to the third and final part of the level. A small piece of land housing quite a few flip panels is found. Using the ones on the right side, Mario can obtain 40 seconds on his countdown timer. The two on the left side grant Mario the final Star Coin, and the others are used to scale the last part of the level.

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': In the area with a couple of flip switches that make a small pyramid-like shape, there is the first [[Star Medal]] near the top.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is obtained by jumping over the second teleportation box while aboard the moving platform.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The third Star Medal is found in the area with seven flip switches. Using the two on the left, Mario can obtain the third Star Medal.
