'''Slippa Jar''' is an enemy found in ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'' that spews out an infinite amount of [[Slippa]]s and only appears in [[Snake Charmer's Challenge]] in [[Kremlantis]]. They can also roll down at [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]]. If they touch them in this condition, they become injured. They can be defeated only by a [[TNT Barrel]]. However, they can help the Kongs cross large abysses.
