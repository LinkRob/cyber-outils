They assist the playable characters through their journey.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
They are flowers with a round mouth and eyes. They have two green heart-shaped leaves. They somewhat resemble pitcher plants. During [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|badge]] quests and certain stages with switches they have a red head instead of an orange head. When said switches are flipped the other way, they also appear as blue.

=== Powers and abilities ===
The main ability that Talking Flowers have is talking, and their messages small reactions to what is occurring within a level. Talking Flowers can also fly by flapping their leaves.

=== Habitats ===
Talking Flowers live in a variety of environments. Some Talking Flowers live in humid environments resulting them to dehydrate.
