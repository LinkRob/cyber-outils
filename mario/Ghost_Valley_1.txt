}}'''Ghost Valley 1''' is the third course in the [[Mushroom Cup]] in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'' and the first of the three Ghost Valleys. It was the first track in the ''Mario Kart'' series to feature [[Boo]]s and bottomless pits, based on [[Ghost House]]s from ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

There is a shortcut here. Towards the end of the track, near the U-turn, the player can hop across to a narrow bridge. It is right next to the finish line. But the player should be cautious because, in [[50cc]], the player's kart will be too slow to hop across and fall into a bottomless pit. To be safe, you should use either a [[Mushroom]] or a [[Cape Feather]].

This course bears a resemblance to Banshee Boardwalk from Mario Kart 64 and Mario Kart DS, and also has 2 sister courses, [[Ghost Valley 2]] which also appears in Super Mario Kart, as well as [[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]] and [[Mario Kart Wii]], and [[Ghost Valley 3]], which again appears in Super Mario Kart as part of the [[Special Cup]], and makes a reappearance in Mario Kart: Super Circuit as part of a retro cup, [[Extra Special Cup]].
