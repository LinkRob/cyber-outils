'''World 5-Fortress''' is a level found in [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5]] in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 

<br />
==Layout==
[[Mario]] begins the level at the bottom of the tower. He must then jump onto platforms with moving, spiked walls on either side. A moving [[? Block]] is found, along with a [[Dry Bones]] on a platform. After another ? Block and Dry Bones, Mario comes to the top of the first pair of spiked walls. He must then continue climbing up platforms and moving [[Brick Block]]s while avoiding Dry Bones and spiked walls. Another moving ? Block is found near the top of the spiked wall, followed by an area with the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]]. A [[Springboard]] is used to reach a platform surrounded by spiked walls. To the left of the platform, a group of [[coin]]s and a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] are found. Mario must continue climbing platforms, where he find a [[Red Ring]] and another Dry Bones. Mario then reaches another area where he must use a Springboard to reach a platform surrounded by spiked walls. After climbing more platforms, Mario reaches the boss door, leading to the battle with [[Iggy Koopa]].

Iggy's arena for the boss fight contains three vertical moving platforms (the first and third going the opposite direction as the second). During the boss fight, Iggy attacks Mario and gang by shooting fast green projectiles from his wand and jumping. If he is hit, he spins in his shell quickly in an attempt to hit the player. Patience is key in this fight, remain a distance from Iggy and jump on him as soon as his platform is lower than yours. Iggy is defeated upon being hit three times.
==Enemies==

*[[Dry Bones]] - 5
*[[Iggy Koopa]] (boss)

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Not too far before the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], the first Star Coin is floating to the right. Mario and co. should jump from either the nearby row of three [[Brick Block]]s, or the [[Dry Bones]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': Shortly after the [[Red Ring]], there is a small split path, and the second Star Coin is floating in the left one. Mario and co. should jump to it quickly before the spiked walls start moving right.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At least a [[Super Mushroom]] is required for this one. The third Star Coin is floating to the right of Iggy's door, and is blocked off by Brick Blocks. Mario and co. must break through enough of them to jump up and get the Star Coin.
