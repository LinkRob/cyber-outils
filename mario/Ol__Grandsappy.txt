'''Ol' Grandsappy''' is a character who is first seen in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. He lives in the [[Whispering Woods]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
In his first form, Ol' Grandsappy resembles just a brown tree stump. His second form, Sweet Sap Loggins, portrays him as a light brown tree with green leaves and black eyes with a pointy nose slightly below his eyes.

== Biography ==
Ol' Grandsappy was once a normal tree, but he grew sick when some [[Mushroom Retainer]]s chopped down his trunk and harvested his wood, which the [[Sap Sisters]] explain when [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] and [[Olivia]] stumble upon him. They find the Soul Seed and drop it into a rejuvenating pond to restore his energy, but find him eaten by [[Paper Macho Goons|Paper Macho Goombas]]. They use confetti to fix his body and restore him to his former glory with the seed as he sings Happy and Sappy.
