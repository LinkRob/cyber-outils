'''Feeling Fine P''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Description ==
Feeling Fine P is like the [[Feeling Fine]] badge, except it can be used on [[Mario]]'s partners to protect them from [[Poison (ailment)|poison]], [[Dizzy|dizziness]], and other ailments. Feeling Fine P costs four [[Badge Point|BP]] to use.

Feeling Fine P is found in the [[X-Naut Fortress]] on [[The Moon]]. It is only obtainable when Mario has put the [[cog]] to activate the crane in the fortress. Here, Mario finds this badge, Feeling Fine, [[coin]]s and a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]].
