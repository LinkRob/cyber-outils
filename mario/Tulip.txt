'''Tulips''' are an object featured in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]], and they first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They are large, red, flower heads found in midair. A Tulip slowly opens and closes, and a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can shoot an egg into the Tulip's opening to have it shoot out eight [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]]. After doing this, the Tulip disappears.

In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', Tulips are the focus of the minigame [[Tulip Shooter]], in which the objective is to spit as many [[Shy Guy]]s into the Tulip within the time limit.
