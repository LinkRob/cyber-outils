|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}The '''Autograph''' is a reoccurring key item that appears in the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series.

==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
In this game, two Autographs are present: [[Luigi]]'s and [[Merluvlee]]'s. Both of these autographs are needed in order to complete two of [[Koopa Koot]]'s favors. While Luigi was more than willing to give out his autograph freely, Merluvlee will only give her autograph if Mario heads to [[Dry Dry Outpost]] and get the [[Crystal Ball]] from her sister, [[Merlee]]. After that is done, Merluvlee gladly gives her autograph and the excited Koopa Koot awards Mario with three [[Star Piece]]s.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
In this game, the autograph is signed by the [[Excess Express Engineer]] as a present for a young [[Bob-omb]] named [[Bub]]. After that, the pleased, Bob-omb rewards Mario with a [[Shine Sprite]].

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
[[File:AutographSPM.png|thumb|left]]In this game, Mario must get an autograph signed for [[Old Man Watchitt]] after being haunted by a spirit. After Mario goes to temple and signs the piece of paper, Old Man Watchitt awards Mario with the You-Know-What which is required to get the optional Pixl [[Piccolo]].

==Trivia==
*Out of all the Autographs, only the Train Engineer's one is required to get in order to complete the game.
