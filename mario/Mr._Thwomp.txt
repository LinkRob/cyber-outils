'''Mr. Thwomp''' is a character found at [[Thwomp Volcano]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. He has a wife named [[Mrs. Thwomp]], who is very temperamental, an opposite trait of Mr. Thwomp, who is instead calm. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can ride down on Mr. Thwomp like an elevator to proceed into the [[Thwomp Caverns]].
