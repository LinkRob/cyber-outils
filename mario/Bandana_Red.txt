'''Bandana Reds''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are sharks that wear red bandanas and they serve under the pirate [[Jonathan Jones]] in the [[Sunken Ship]]. [[Mario]] and friends had run into a group of Bandana Reds which they had to fight off before getting to [[Jonathan Jones]]. Stronger versions of Bandana Reds known as [[Bandana Blue]]s are also seen but are only fought during the battle with Jonathan Jones.

==Battle==
In battle, Bandana Reds are only capable of physical attacks with their spears and [[Skewer (move)|Skewer]]. They are relatively weak in battle, which is why they usually fight in groups.

===Strategy===
Bandana Reds are weak against fire and thunder techniques so Mario's [[Super Flame]] and [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] are very effective against them. Since Bandana Reds usually fight in groups, it is recommended to use Mallow's Thunderbolt.
