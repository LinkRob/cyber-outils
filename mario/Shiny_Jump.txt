'''Shiny Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It is a stronger version of the [[Jump]] sticker.

== Appearance ==
The Shiny Jump sticker looks like the regular Jump sticker except with a little more shine.
