'''Bolt Lifts''' are a type of platform comprised of nuts in the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. Bolt Lifts mainly appear in [[airship]] [[level]]s, and they debuted in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. 3''==
Bolt Lifts exclusively appear in Airship levels. Jumping on a Bolt Lift gradually moves it to the right, although [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] fall off a Bolt Lift almost instantly after getting onto one.
