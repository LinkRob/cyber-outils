'''Kitty''' is a regular gray cat who appears as a one-time character in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', within the episode "[[Misadventures in Babysitting]]." It is [[Junior]]'s pet cat whom he appears to have abused, as evidenced by the bandages on Kitty's forehead and bandages.
