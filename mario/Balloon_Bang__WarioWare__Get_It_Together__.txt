'''Balloon Bang''' is a minigame from ''[[WarioWare: Get it Together!]]'' It is lifted from the minigame of the same name in [[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!|<u>''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Games!''</u>]], and, just like in its home game, involves players taking turns playing microgames while pumping the balloon.

== Gameplay ==
At the start of a game, player order is determined at random. Each round, players get a crew member selected for all players. The players take turns playing the microgame, while their opponents inflate a balloon via various methods, such as spinning wheels, pressing foot pumps, and pushing handles up and down. A player who wins a microgame leaves the microgame and replaces the next player in the turn order in pumping the balloon. A player who loses is forced to try again at a slower speed.

=== How to Win ===
The winner is the one who pops the balloon.

== Ending ==
The player who was playing a microgame crashes into the ground, his or her impact leaving a crater whose shape matches that of his or her own body. Afterward, the winner is shown celebrating his or her victory.
