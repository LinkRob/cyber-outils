A '''Shield''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and its two sequels, ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
A Shield is one of the items that can be obtained from an [[Item Box]]. When a character uses a Shield, they are protected from the other characters' items for a limited time. longer than the temporary shield used while [[drift]]ing.
