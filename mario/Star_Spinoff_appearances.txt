==''Mario'' game appearances==
===''Mario's Time Machine''===
In ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]'', several Super Stars appear as statues in [[Bowser's Museum]]. Mario can use them as platforms to reach some doors.

===''Mario Kart'' series===
Stars appear in all ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' games as a rare item typically available only to low-ranking drivers, usually those in the lowest positions. When used, a Star grants the player invincibility against all attacks and allows them to knock over items, course obstacles, and other drivers—potentially causing opponents to lose their current items in some games. It also provides a speed boost, increasing the player's average speed by one-third, and enables them to traverse off-road terrain without any loss of speed (with the exception of ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', where the item instead doubles the player's speed). The Star's invincibility effect lasts for varying durations depending on the game. The Star serves as the emblem of the [[Star Cup]] in every ''Mario Kart'' game to date.

====''Super Mario Kart''====
[[File:Starman SNES.png|thumb|200px|Mario with a Star in ''Super Mario Kart'']]
Since ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'', the Star can both grant temporary invincibility and a boost in speed. It is most commonly obtained by racers in 5th through 8th place. The effects of the Super Star lasts for 6.13 seconds.<ref name=seconds>Vini64 (September 25, 2014). [https://youtu.be/NaBBOO0GigE Mario Kart History - Star]. ''YouTube''. Retrieved August 25, 2019.</ref> The Super Star can also be used by [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] if he is CPU-controlled.
{{br}}
====''Mario Kart 64''====
[[File:Starman N64.png|thumb|200px|Luigi with a Star in ''Mario Kart 64'']]
In ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'', the Super Star retains its usual effects. Its availability varies depending on a racer's position: it is unobtainable in 1st place, uncommon in 2nd or 3rd, relatively common from 4th to 6th, and very common in 7th or 8th place. The item's duration was also increased to 10.13 seconds.<ref name=seconds/>

If the player collides with the boat in [[DK's Jungle Parkway]] while under the effects of a Super Star, they still get hit before plunging into the [[water]]; this also applies to the [[Boo]] item.

====''Mario Kart: Super Circuit''====
In ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', the Super Star can be obtained by any racer except the one in 1st place, but it is actually more common for racers in 3rd through 6th place than for those in 7th or 8th. The effects of the Super Star lasts for 6.3 seconds.<ref name=seconds/> Additionally, on [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|Rainbow Road]], Super Stars fall from the sky and onto the course, and driving into one has the same effect as touching the item.

====''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!''====
[[File:Starman GameCube.png|thumb|left|200px|Artwork for ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'']]
[[File:Starman GCN.png|thumb|Mario and Luigi with a Starman in ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'']]
In ''[[Mario Kart Double Dash!!]]'', the Starman is largely the same as in earlier titles. The distribution is smaller, as it can only be obtained by racers from 6th to 8th place. If a [[kart]] is hit by another under the effects of a Starman, the rear character gets knocked off the kart and drags on the ground briefly, losing a lot of speed in the process. The effects of the Starman lasts for 7.6 seconds.<ref name=seconds/> Unlike most previous titles of the series, the Starman is depicted with a smiling mouth.

In Mushroom Bridge and Mushroom City, there is a [[truck]] depicting the sponsor "Star Travel", and its license plate is STR.

In [[Battle Mode]], the player can steal Balloons and [[Shine Sprite]]s when hitting characters while under the effects of a Star.
{{br}}
====''Mario Kart Arcade GP'' subseries====
[[File:MKAGPDX Super Star.png|thumb|A Star in ''Mario Kart Arcade GP DX'']]
In ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]'', the Super Star appears again.

====''Mario Kart DS''====
[[File:Starman DS.png|thumb|left|200px|Waluigi with a Super Star in ''Mario Kart DS''.]]
In ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', the Star can be obtained by any racer from 5th to 8th place. The effects of the item lasts for 7.46 seconds.<ref name=seconds/> Starting with this game, if a racer under the effects of a Super Star hits a racer under the effects of a Bullet Bill, the other racer gets pushed aside.

There is a unique [[Item Box]] on top of a hill in [[DK Pass]]. If the player touches said Item Box, regardless of their position, the player may receive a Star, [[Triple Mushrooms]], a single [[Dash Mushroom|Mushroom]], or, very rarely, a single [[Red Shell]].
{{br|left}}
====''Mario Kart Wii''====
[[File:Star - Mario Kart Wii.png|thumb|left|200px|A Star in ''Mario Kart Wii'']]
[[File:Starman Wii.png|thumb|Yoshi obtaining a Star in ''Mario Kart Wii'']]
In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', the Star can be obtained when a racer is in 7th to 12th place and lasts for 7.42 seconds.<ref name=seconds/> The [[Sugarscoot]] features a star emblem on the spare tire at the rear. When characters acquire a Star, their voices echo—except for [[Rosalina]], whose voice already echoes but does so even more under the Star's effects. Some of the [[leaf pile]]s on [[Maple Treeway]], including those in the section with the [[Wiggler]]s and the area before the [[finish line]], also have a chance to drop Stars when driven through. Racers under the effects of the [[Mega Mushroom]] can still be hit by those using a Star. Up to three Stars can be in play at once.

''Mario Kart Wii'' also introduces the [[Coin Runners]] mode, in which Coins can be stolen in the same manner as Balloons or [[Shine Sprite]]s in the Battle Mode of ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!''.

In [[Chain Chomp Wheel]], there is a special Item Box containing only a Star, unlike the Item Box in DK Pass.

The Prima Guide for <i>Mario Kart Wii</i> refers to the item as both a <b>Star</b> and sometimes a <b>Starman</b>.

====''Mario Kart 7''====
[[File:Starman 3DS.png|thumb|200px|Luigi obtaining a Star in ''Mario Kart 7''.]]
In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', the Star can be obtained when a racer is in 5th to 8th place and lasts for 7.46 seconds,<ref name=seconds/> just like in ''Mario Kart DS''. Additionally, any course with breakable obstacles that drop items has the potential to yield a Star, such as the [[jar]]s in [[Shy Guy Bazaar]].

====''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''====
[[File:Starman Wii U - Nintendo Switch.png|thumb|left|200px|Waluigi with a Star in ''Mario Kart 8'']]
In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', due to the updated item probability system, the Super Star is not tied to a specific position in the race. Instead, it has a higher chance of appearing when players are at least 8,000 units behind 1st place. The effects of the Star last for 7.54 seconds.<ref name=seconds/>

The [[Princess Peach]] statue in [[Toad Harbor]] can be seen holding a Star in its raised right hand.

In ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', if the player has purchased the [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe – Booster Course Pass|Booster Course Pass]] DLC or currently has a [[Nintendo Switch Online|Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack]] membership, a small icon of a Star can be seen next to the version number on the game's title screen.
{{br|left}}

====''Mario Kart Tour''====
[[File:MKT Toad Star.jpg|thumb|200px|Toad under the effects of a Star in ''Mario Kart Tour'']]
Unlike in previous installments, Stars themselves do not appear as collectible Item Box items in regular single-player races in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. Instead, getting a [[Frenzy]] has the same effects as using a Star, with the same invincibility music from ''Mario Kart 8'' playing, while its icon appears on-screen if another racer behind the player is in Frenzy mode. Stars found in Item Boxes appear in multiplayer mode when the rules have less than three item slots, as frenzies are unobtainable then. Stars are also found in certain challenges, first found in the Break Item Boxes challenge of the [[Tokyo Tour]]'s [[Wendy Cup]]. Once obtained, Stars can be activated by tapping the screen, making the player temporarily invincible. Additionally, if the player is about to receive a High-End driver from a [[Pipe Cannon|pipe]], a Star appears briefly on-screen.
{{br|left}}

===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
[[File:Starman SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Starman Combat.png|thumb|250px|Mario acquiring a Starman in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'']]
Stars appear as an item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Mario can occasionally find a Star and use it to defeat enemies on the overworld without entering battle. Doing so still grants Mario [[Experience Point|experience points]].

===''Mario Tennis'' series===
Stars appear in the [[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)#Bowser Stage|Bowser Stage]] from ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]''. Stars also appear in Item Battle from ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', and appear as the emblem of the Star Cup and on its trophy. In ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]'', a Star is the emblem for the Final Cup, and appears on its trophy. A tennis racket, uniform, wrist bands, and shoes are designed based on this item and are available for [[Mii]] customization.

===''Mario & Luigi'' series===
====''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''====
Stars are briefly mentioned in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', where they are said to be an ingredient of [[Invincishroom]]s. Additionally, in the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of ''[[Mario Bros. (game)|Mario Bros.]]'' included with the game, Stars can be found in the ''Mario Bros. Battle'' multi-player mode. Specifically, they can hatch from [[Egg]]s, which are one of the items the player can obtain after busting out of a [[Bin|garbage can]]. They do not appear in the ''Mario Bros. Classic'' single-player mode or any other version of the game.

====''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''====
[[File:Starman Pixels.png|thumb|250px|Bowser Memory M ready to grab a Star in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''.]]
[[File:Starman Pixels DX.png|thumb|left|250px|Bowser Memory M about to grab a Star in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey'']]
While actual Stars do not appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'', the Star appears as the icon of the [[Rank|Star Rank]].

A pixelated Star appears during the boss fight with [[Bowser Memory ML]], and later during the fight with Bowser Memory MLX in The Gauntlet, as part of his attack pattern. A Star may bounce around Bowser Memory M, who then grabs it and dashes directly at Mario and Luigi. If either Mario or Luigi is hit during this state, they will be knocked back and take heavy damage until the attack ends. As with a regular Star, the effects are temporary for Bowser Memory M, so after a short time, he reverts to normal, ending the attack.

====''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''====
Stars appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' as part of the [[Star Rocket]] [[Mad Skillathon]] as rare items, not appearing until just after the 150th small star. When collected, Mario and Luigi will speed up and be able to destroy spiked balloons for a limited time. Additionally, the Star appears as the icon of the Star Rank again. Also, the boss [[Dreamy Mario]] and his clones turn invincible during the chase scenes from a Star and have the same rainbow glow as [[Invincible Mario]] during the attack.

====''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''====
Only one Super Star appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' in the form of a [[Papercraft]] item. Before the fight with Papercraft King Boo, the trio upon Papercraft Yoshi can find a floating Super Star and by using its tongue, they can pull it in and use it. While under the effects of the Super Star, Papercraft Yoshi can defeat enemies instantly, but it automatically wears off after all nearby Papercraft Goombas are defeated. The ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' version of the invincibility theme plays while in use. The Super Star does not reappear in the [[Battle Ring (Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam)|Battle Ring]] version of the fight. In addition, the Super Star is the icon of the Star Rank once more.

===''Mario Golf'' series===
====''Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour''====
In ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]'', Stars are obstacles. They appear only on [[Peach's Castle Grounds]], where they also appear as tee markers.

====''Mario Golf: Advance Tour''====
In ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]'', Starmen appear on certain Star Courses. Whenever one is hit, the player becomes "invincible" against whatever their current ball lie is, and the ball acts as if it were on the optimum playing surface.

====''Mario Golf: World Tour''====
In ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'', when Mario or Gold Mario gets a [[Birdie]] or better in this game, his post-hole animation involves him grabbing a Star and running while invincible and then striking a pose. Also, when he gets an [[Eagle]] in this game, he grabs a star, defeats a few enemies, and jumps. This item is also used as tee markers in the [[Mario's Star (golf course)|Mario's Star]] course. A clothing set, golf ball, and set of golf clubs are designed based on the item, and are available for Miis to use.

====''Mario Golf: Super Rush''====
{{quote|[[Mario]] launches Super Star Strike!|Toad|Mario Golf: Super Rush}}
In ''[[Mario Golf: Super Rush]]'', [[Mario]] uses the Star to transform into Rainbow Mario during his special shot, the Super Star Strike. In Battle Golf, Stars are also used during the Super Star Rush event.

===''Mario Pinball Land''===
In ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]'', Invincibility Stars can be bought in [[Toad's tent]], obtained from certain ? Blocks, or received from a roulette after the player gets a [[question mark]]. If the player goes to a different [[level]] while an Invincibility Star is still in effect, its effect continues in that level until it ends.

===''Mario Baseball'' series===
====''Mario Superstar Baseball''====
[[File:Star Dash.png|thumb|200px|[[Mario]] under the Star's effect in ''Mario Superstar Baseball'']]
Stars only appear in [[Toy Field]] and [[Star Dash]] in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. A star can only be obtained by making the ball land on the slot and acquiring an item from the item slot. When a star is received, the star will take 30 coins from all other players and gives the player the 30 coins from each player. In [[Star Dash]], the star may come out along with the coins that come out of the pipe-like mechanism. When the star is received, the player will receive 10 coins. When the player hits another player while the star is in effect, the player will steal 10 coins. The player can defeat Thwomps on the field also. The player must watch out for the [[Poison Mushroom]]s that come out. If the player touches a Poison Mushroom while invincible, the player will feel the Poison Mushroom effects and lose the star power up.

====''Mario Super Sluggers''====
Since the item slot was dismissed in the Toy Field of ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'', it appears in a minigame called [[Graffiti Runner]]. In this game, the star is one of the many power-ups found inside a treasure chest, in which the player must use the tackle button to open up. If the player acquires the star, they are invincible and impervious to almost all forms of attack, and are able to knock anyone else aside. However, the player is not fully invincible and will still get stunned by an earthquake caused by a larger player or a [[Chain Chomp]]. It also appeared in the [[Blooper Baserun]] minigame and shares the same thing.

Though stars do not appear as an item obtained in Toy Field, it can be found when the player makes the ball land on the "Jackpot" space. In one of these jackpots, there is an event where the player must choose a treasure chest to find a star. If the player did, they will receive coins.

===''Paper Mario'' series===
Stars in the ''Paper Mario'' series are actually seen as a sprite for the user's interface since ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', where they are seen at the bottom right of a dialogue box, indicating that the player has to press a button to continue. A similar item known as a [[Mega Star]] appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. From ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' onward, Super Stars themselves appear as collectibles.

====''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''====
Super Stars are briefly referenced in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', in which a camera-wielding [[Lakitu]] near the [[Glitz Pit]] remarks, "Stars make you invincible?"

====''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''====
[[File:PMSS Shot - Super Star.png|thumb|220px|A Super Star in [[Whammino Mountain]], from ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star'']]
In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', Super Stars are very rare and appear only in [[Whammino Mountain]], [[Rustle Burrow]] and [[Gauntlet Pond]]. They grant Mario invincibility, allowing him to defeat enemies without battling them, much like in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''. The Super Star also gives Mario's jump a bit of a spin. Super Stars are also mentioned in two questions of the quiz found in [[Snifit or Whiffit]].

====''Paper Mario: Color Splash''====
In ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'', Super Stars appear in [[Kiwano Temple]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], and [[Sacred Forest]]. They are used in largely the same way as ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''. The Super Star also enables Mario to walk across spiked areas without taking damage.

Unlike the previous game, the effects of a Super Star are required for solving certain puzzles, such as defeating an entire group of [[Micro Goomba]]s guarding a piece of the [[Mini Paint Star]].

====''Paper Mario: The Origami King''====
[[File:Starman Discovery.png|thumb|A Star in ''Paper Mario: The Origami King'']]
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'', a Star appears in several locations. It is first obtained after completing the leaf memory game in [[Whispering Woods]], where it is used to cross beds of thorns to reach a [[Hidden Block]] and Collectible Treasure. It can also appear in [[Picnic Road]] as a possible reward from the [[fortune Toad]] when granting great fortune. On [[Overlook Mountain]], it is used to destroy the [[Thwomp]]s. Finally, in [[King Shroomses]]'s tomb within the [[Temple of Shrooms]], it is used to defeat the [[Scuttlebug]]s and [[Mini Scuttlebug]]s.

===''Mario Sports Mix''===
Stars also make an appearance in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'' and in addition to making the character invincible, it has two theme songs. They also increase the speed and accuracy of the ball. In basketball and hockey, players cannot intercept the ball/puck when it is thrown. In volleyball, opposing players who touch the ball will be heavily immobilized for a while. In dodgeball, opposing players will immediately lose some health when hit, regardless of if the ball touches the ground or not. Stars are rare in this game.

In some missions, an item restriction may limit the items to Stars, making it possible for both teams to get that item at the same time.

===''Mario Sports Superstars''===
In ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]'', Super Stars appear in the exact same golf post-hole animations from ''Mario Golf: World Tour'', as well as [[Metal Mario]]'s animations.

===''Mario Party'' series===
====''Mario Party: Island Tour''====
[[File:Super Star MPIT.jpg|thumb|left|A Star from ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'']]
Super Stars appear in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' as usable items in [[Perilous Palace Path]], which, if one is used, doubles the total number of spaces the player gets to move with the [[Dice Block]].

====''Mario Party 10''====
Stars appear again in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', specifically in the minigame [[Cheep Cheep Leap]]. In this minigame, players are shown standing on a boardwalk with Stars hovering above them. They grab the Stars to become invincible. The goal is for them to knock away the most [[Cheep Cheep]]s, [[Mega Cheep Cheep]]s, and [[Gold Cheep Cheep]]s that leap over the bridge within 30 seconds.

===''Luigi's Mansion 3''===
In ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', a Star can be found in [[ScareScraper]] mode in some rooms. Once a Star is found, the player is granted temporary invincibility, runs faster, and can defeat ghosts without having to capture them with the [[Poltergust G-00]]. Stars can also appear in the [[ScreamPark#Coin Floating|Coin Floating]] minigame, where they will occasionally appear on the pool. When collected, it makes the player invincible to explosives for a short amount of time and allows the player to pop the other players' floaties to send them out of the pool.

==In other media==
===DiC Entertainment===
{{quote|Starman, star bright! Give me Starman pasta power! Give me pasta might!|[[Mario]]|"[[Pirates of Koopa]]"}}
[[File:SMBSSStarman.jpg|thumb|A Starman from ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode, "[[The Trojan Koopa]]"]]
Starmen also make several appearances throughout the three ''Super Mario'' animated cartoon series produced by [[DIC Entertainment]].

In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', they do not grant invincibility; they instead can power-up Mario and Luigi into [[Fire Mario]] and Fire Luigi, giving them increased strength and the ability to shoot [[fireball]]s. The person wielding the Starman, often Mario, usually recites, "Starman, star bright, give me pasta power, give me pasta might!", before powering up. This power lasts until either a fair bit of time elapsed, or they are attacked by an enemy. However, [[Fire Flower]]s, along with other powerful artifacts such as [[magic doodad]]s, can also be used in lieu of Starmen in order to power-up. In "[[Toad Warriors]]", [[Toad]] manages to transform into the [[Toad Warrior]] by using a Starman, although in the next episode, "[[The Fire of Hercufleas]]", he transforms into [[Fire Mario|Super Toad]] by using a Fire Flower.

Starmen continue to make appearances into ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|Super Mario World]]''. In these appearances, they no longer follow the rules of ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' Instead, they grant temporary invincibility, just like in the games. Unlike the Cape Feather and Fire Flower, both Mario and Luigi are seen using the Super Star in ''Super Mario World'': Luigi collects one to dispose of the [[Wiggler|Caterpillars]] in "[[Party Line]]", and Mario collects one from one of the "dizzy stars" that appear after he is knocked out by an [[evil dinosaur]] in the same episode. Mario also collects one when he escapes [[Bowser's Castle|Neon Castle]] with Yoshi in "[[The Yoshi Shuffle]]".

===''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''===
{{quote|Bowser has found the Super Star and is headed toward our [[Mushroom Kingdom|kingdom]]. Its power will make him invincible!|Toad General|The Super Mario Bros. Movie}}
{{main|Super Star (Illumination)}}
[[File:Bowser stealing the star TSMBM.png|thumb|Bowser with the Star in ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'']]
In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', a Super Star contained in a [[? Block]] is guarded by [[Penguin]]s in their ice castle, only to have their castle destroyed and their Super Star stolen by [[Bowser]] after he and his [[Koopa Troop]] attack their kingdom. Serving as a {{wp|MacGuffin}} similar to the [[Power Star]]s, while functioning like the power-up, Bowser plots to use the Star to take over the world and/or propose to [[Princess Peach|Peach]]. By the end of the movie, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] use it to become invincible and defeat Bowser and his forces.
