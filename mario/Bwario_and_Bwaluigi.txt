'''Bwario''' and '''Bwaluigi''' are mid-bosses and a duo of [[Rabbids]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
As their name implies, the duo's appearance resembles [[Wario]] and [[Waluigi]].

=== Personality traits ===
{{Sectstub}}

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
=== Combat ===
==== Bwario ====
Bwario attacks with a rumble bang attack: he uses his pipe to open-fire his target, dealing 85 damage. He can also activate a line-of-sight counterattack, (villain sight) damaging anyone who passes into his vision arc. His team jumps create shockwaves upon contact with the ground.

==== Bwaluigi ====
Bwaluigi attacks in melee. He tries to crush the player with his pipe, similar to a [[Smasher]], and counters enemy attacks by advancing on his attacker and attacking once they are close. He can also force his enemies closer to his location.

Both enemies and their allies must be defeated in order to claim victory and progress in the game.
