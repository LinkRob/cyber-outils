'''Arrow platforms''' are objects in [[Boiling Blue Bully Belt]] and [[Broken Blue Bully Belt]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. The platforms will rise from [[lava]] and then it will descend back down for a minute. An arrow on these points to the platform that will rise from lava next, creating for a player to make a path. A different type of these platforms appears, then extending and retracting from the wall instead at around the midpoint of the [[level]]. They also have an arrow on either side.

Arrow platforms are needed to grab the top of the [[Goal Pole]].
