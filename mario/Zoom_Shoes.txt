'''Zoom Shoes''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
Any user can equip the Zoom Shoes, and in doing so, their speed increases by 10. The Zoom Shoes can be bought for 100 [[coin]]s at either [[Rose Town]] or at [[Seaside Town]] after defeating [[Yaridovich]]. The Zoom Shoes can also be obtained in [[Booster Tower]] by grabbing the [[Room Key]] and going into the locked door in the "fireball" room.
