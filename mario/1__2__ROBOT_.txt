'''1, 2, ROBOT!''' is a [[Dribble & Spitz]] [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''.

==Gameplay==
The cart is moving side to side, while the three parts of the robot. Tap the buttons in order to make the robot parts fall on the cart. If the order is incorrect and the part touches the ground, the player loses.
