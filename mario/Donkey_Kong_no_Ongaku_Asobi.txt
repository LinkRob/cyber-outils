'''''Donkey Kong no Ongaku Asobi''''' is a canceled music game of the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' series. It would have accompanied the release of ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. Math]]''.

One screenshot shows [[Pauline]] as a singer, something that would later become a reality in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', in which she is the singer in her own [[Band (Super Mario Odyssey)|band]].
