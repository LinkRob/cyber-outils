A '''Light Block''' is a type of [[Block]] which first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==History==
Similar to [[Empty Block]]s from ''[[Super Mario World]]'', Light Blocks first debuting in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', exclusively in {{world-link|5|ghosthouse|World 5-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)}}. These blocks moving invisible at first, but if [[Mario]] or any other character hit the [[? Switch]], they glow and keeps moving. They also can be used for lighting and transport.

Light Blocks reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', being exactly as in previous games. They debuts in [[Light Blocks, Dark Tower]] and [[Light-Up-Lift Tower]].

In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''Minecraft'', [[w:c:minecraft:Redstone Lamp|Redstone Lamps]] are replaced by Light Blocks.
