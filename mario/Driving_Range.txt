'''Driving Range''' is a golf course in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for [[Nintendo 64]].

==Description==
[[File:Driving Range logo.png|frame|left]]
Driving Range is a very basic golf course consisting mostly just of a large field of grass. Trees and some hills with a pair of eyes can still be seen in the distant background. It has five greens and multiple signs indicating yardage.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Mario Golf'' (Nintendo 64)==
Driving Range is only available in Training mode, likely because of its simplicity.
