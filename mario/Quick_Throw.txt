'''Quick Throw''' is an ability in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''

==Description==
Quick Throw involves a fielder throwing the ball at a fast speed. In ''Mario Superstar Baseball'', Quick Throw can be used by [[Princess Peach|Peach]] and [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]. In ''Mario Super Sluggers'', in addition to Peach, [[Baby Peach]], [[Waluigi]], and [[Mii]] can also use Quick Throw, although Daisy no longer has the ability.

==In ''Mario Super Sluggers''==
There is a scout mission in [[Peach Ice Garden]] where the objective is for the player's character to get the runner out using a Quick Throw three times. If the scout mission is completed, [[Baby Peach]] joins the player's team.
