'''Game Freak''' (stylized as '''GAME FREAK''') is a Japanese video game company best known for developing the mainline ''[[w:c:pokemon:Pokémon (franchise)|Pokémon]]'' video games. Game Freak has worked with [[Nintendo]] on two different games starring ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters, including ''[[Yoshi (game)|Yoshi]]'' and ''[[Mario & Wario]]''. However, the latter game was only released in Japan.
