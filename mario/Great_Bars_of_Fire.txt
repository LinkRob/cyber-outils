'''Great Bars of Fire''' is a general [[minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''. It is one of the minigames in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]] that involves the characters having to avoid [[Fire Bar]]s.
== Gameplay ==
In this minigame, you have to either jump over or crouch under spinning Fire Bars. The Fire Bars will occasionally speed up and slow down, requiring decent timing. If you get hit three times by the Fire Bars, you will be knocked out and unable  to participate. The person (or people) who survive the longest or until the timer runs out win the game.
