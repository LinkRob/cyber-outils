'''Gaugau''' (ガウガウ) is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and ''[[Wario Land II]]''. These aggressive creatures are members of the [[Black Sugar Gang]] of pirates who tend to attack by throwing various projectiles.

== Behavior==
In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'', Gaugau walk back and forth until they spot [[Wario]], at which point they will attack by throwing a knife. If the knife hits a wall, it will stick there for a few seconds and Wario can use it as a stepping stool.

In ''Wario Land II'', unlike the previous game, Gaugau are seen leaping out of windows in positions. When Wario comes near them, they attack by throwing an infinite amount of beer bottles that will harm him on contact.
