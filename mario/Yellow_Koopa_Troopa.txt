'''Yellow Koopa Troopas''' are a color variation of [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa Troopas]] which first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.


== Profile ==

=== Physical description ===
Yellow Koopa Troopas have the same general appearance as Koopa Troopas in general, except they wear a yellow [[Koopa Shell]], specifically a [[Yellow Shell]].

=== Behavior ===
''Super Mario World'', if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] jumps on a Yellow Koopa Troopa out of its shell, it becomes a [[Undress Koopa|Undressed Koopa]], the yellow Koopas return their shell and become a flashing shell. They will chase mario wherever direction he goes to.

== In [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] ==

=== ''Super Mario World'' ===
If [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] eats a Yellow Koopa Troopa, he makes a big earthquake.
