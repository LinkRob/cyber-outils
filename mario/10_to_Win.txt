'''10 to Win''' is a Free-for-All mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:10 to Win platforms.png|thumb|left|200px|The characters' rising platforms]]
The objective of the game is to be the first player to earn ten points by flipping up cards. Each card can increase the score of the player who drew it, all of their opponents, the player in first, the player in last, or everyone.
{{br|left}}

*{{Button|Wii|Wiimote}} - Choose
