'''Madrid Drive 3''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', debuting in the [[Anniversary Tour (2023)|Anniversary Tour of 2023]]. As its name and number imply, it is the third route of [[Madrid Drive]].

== Layout ==
The south half of the city will get involved with Madrid Drive 3, visiting the Museo del Prado (Prado Museum) and Madrid Atocha station. The museum has an arrangement of the standard Madrid Drive music, though with less instruments and a generally higher pitch.

The course seems to start in the same location as Madrid Drive 1. After a left turn and a slight right turn, the racers cross a path leading to the Royal Palace, although they do not turn left here. Instead, they continue further south down the road that they were on and make a wide left turn, entering the palace from the south. Popping out of the southwest exit, they then make a right U-turn to get through the southeast exit, following Madrid Drive 2 in reverse. After a left turn, the racers will see the Madrid Atocha station up ahead, where they turn right to enter the station. They make a wide left turn to double back and then head into the museum from the south, taking a slight right to avoid crashing. After a left turn, the racers will exit the museum, taking a slight right and a slight left to get back to the [[Finish line|starting line]]. 

== Trivia ==

* The changing music in the museum bears similarities to [[Singapore Speedway]]'s [[Singapore Speedway 3|Chinatown]] theme.
