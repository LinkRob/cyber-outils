'''Hard Pants''' is a clothing item featured in both ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Its statistics differ between both games.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Hard Pants is only usable by [[Mario]]. It can be bought for 130 [[coin]]s in [[Beanbean Castle Town]]'s clothes shop, or at a slightly reduced price of 125 coins in the Japanese version. Equipping the Hard Pants increases Mario's [[Defense]] by 18.

In the remake, the Hard Pants were made available at a more expensive price of 350 coins, and they can be equipped to either Mario or [[Luigi]]. The Hard Pants now increases its user's Defense by 24 and [[Bros. Point|BP]] by 6. The Hard Pants were also given the [[Auto Guard]] special effect.
