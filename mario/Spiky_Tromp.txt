A '''Spiky Tromp''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Spiky Tromps are [[Thwomp]]-related creatures that are mainly seen in [[Yold Ruins]] (Chapter 1-4). Spiky Tromps commonly roll around damaging anything that gets too close to them. A horde of Spiky Tromps actually help [[Mario]] and [[Tippi]] cross a large room of Yold Ruins. Spiky Tromps have a subspecies called the [[Spiny Tromp]]s.

They have a huge resemblance to a [[Grrrol]] from [[New Super Mario Bros. U]].
