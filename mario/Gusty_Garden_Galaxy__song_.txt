"'''Gusty Garden Galaxy'''" is a song that plays during the stage of the [[Gusty Garden Galaxy|same name]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It is a 2:30 long orchestral piece composed by [[Koji Kondo]], and the song was also included in the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy Original Soundtrack]]''. It was featured in 'Level Complete' of  ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', which plays during the credits.

The instruments used in the song include a violin, viola, bass, flute, trumpet, piccolo, snare drum, french horn, bass drum, and trombone.

==Legacy==
In 2024, WatchMojo placed the theme for Gusty Garden Galaxy at the #3 spot in their "Top 20 Super Mario Songs And Themes" list countdown; following behind the [[Dire, Dire Docks]] theme from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' (#2), & the iconic [[Ground Theme|ground theme]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' (#1).<ref>Alden, G.
