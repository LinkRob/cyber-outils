A '''Pitchfork''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Pitchfork is a [[Bandinero]] variant with a silver fork on its head. They have two color variants, light blue and purple, the former being the standard

===Behavior===
Pitchforks usually hang on ceilings. If [[Wario]] is under a Pitchfork, it gets itself out of the ceiling and falls to the ground. If a Pitchfork notices Wario on the ground, it runs toward him trying to stab him with their fork. Purple Pitchforks have the ability to [[jump]].

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Pitchforks have a two-star threat rating. If Wario dodges a Pitchfork as it charges toward him and the Pitchfork hits a wall, it becomes stuck. Wario can then pick up the Pitchfork and throw it. Wario can jump off of a stuck Pitchfork for extra height.
