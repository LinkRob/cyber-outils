'''Snifits''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. They are an Egyptian variant of the regular [[Snifit]]s found in the [[Gritzy Caves]]. They can only be fought if a [[Fire Flower]] is used on a [[Snifaro]]. They are also slightly weaker than their regular counterparts.

==Battle==
In battle, Snifits only attack by shooting either one or two balls at [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]].
