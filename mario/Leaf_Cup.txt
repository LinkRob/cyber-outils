
[[File:Leaf Artwork - Mario Kart Wii.png|thumb|The Leaf Cup symbol which represents a [[Super Leaf]], as seen in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'']] '''Leaf Cup''' is a [[Retro Cup]] in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'']] series. It has [[Retro Stage]]s. It can be unlocked by finishing the [[Shell Cup]] and [[Banana Cup]]. It is the counterpart of [[Star Cup]]. Completing the Leaf Cup will unlock the [[Lightning Cup]].
