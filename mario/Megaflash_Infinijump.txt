The '''Megaflash Infinijump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It is Mario's strongest Infinijump sticker, and deemed one of, if not the strongest sticker in the entire game. This extremely rare sticker can only be found in [[Long Fall Falls]] after Mario manages to get away from the giant [[Cheep Chomp]] again.

==Description==
The Megaflash Infinijump is a red boot. It also has a rainbow background and takes up nearly six sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].
