The '''New Nintendo 2DS XL''', titled '''New Nintendo 2DS LL''' in Japan, is the fourth & final revision of the [[Nintendo 3DS]] family. It was originally announced on April 27, 2017, then released in Oceania on June 15, 2017,<ref>Nintendo. (April 27, 2017). ''Nintendo.com.au''. Retrieved April 27, 2017.</ref> in Japan<ref>Nintendo. (April 27, 2017). [https://youtu.be/Pm2tcQuuYnA Newニンテンドー2DS LL 紹介映像]. ''YouTube''. Retrieved April 27, 2017.</ref> and South Korea<ref>http://www.nintendo.co.kr/PR/press_list.php?cPage1=1&cPage2=1&idx=264&press_div=P</ref> on July 13, 2017, and in Europe and North America on July 28, 2017.<ref>Nintendo. (April 27, 2017). [https://youtu.be/q1qrM1UbTyU New Nintendo 2DS XL - Announcement Trailer]. ''YouTube''. Retrieved April 27, 2017.</ref><ref>Nintendo. (April 27, 2017). [https://youtu.be/3lxNHhgMuTg Introducing New Nintendo 2DS XL]. ''YouTube''. Retrieved April 27, 2017.</ref>

The Nintendo 2DS XL is a variation of the [[Nintendo 2DS]] model, and like its parent model, the Nintendo 2DS XL cannot display images in 3D. Its relation to the Nintendo 2DS is different than the [[Nintendo 3DS XL]] and [[New Nintendo 3DS XL]] to their respective counterparts because the New Nintendo 2DS XL is not merely a larger variation of the original Nintendo 2DS's tablet-like design but rather has a clamshell design like other Nintendo 3DS systems. In addition, the New Nintendo 2DS XL includes the enhanced CPU capabilities and the {{button|n3ds|cstick}}-stick, {{button|n3ds|ZL}} and {{button|n3ds|ZR}} shoulder buttons in the same button layout as the New Nintendo 3DS and New Nintendo 3DS XL. Like with the [[Nintendo DSi]], the Nintendo 2DS XL's front-facing camera and microphone are positioned on the hinge. The New Nintendo 2DS XL comes bundled with a 4 GB Micro SDHC Card, instead of a standard 4 GB SDHC card like with the New Nintendo 3DS and Nintendo 2DS.

The New Nintendo 2DS XL was released in a Black + Turquoise color scheme in North America, and in two color schemes in Japan, Europe, and Oceania: Black + Turquoise, and White + Orange. The White + Orange color scheme was later released in North America on October 6, 2017.<ref>Nintendo. (September 13, 2017). [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ztMGTTAjsAQ Nintendo Direct 9.13.2017]. ''Youtube''. Retrieved September 13, 2017.</ref> It ended production on September 17, 2020.<ref name=JPNintendo3DS />
