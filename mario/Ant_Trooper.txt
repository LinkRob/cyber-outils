{{Nihongo|'''Ant Trooper'''|アリへい|Ari-hei|Ant Soldier}} are enemies which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
An Ant Trooper is a round, dark purple ant. Its head is larger than its torso. It has glowing pupils, yellow eyes, a mouth, and a stubby antennae on its head. It has red feet and two thick, slightly darker stripes on their bodies. These enemies are a bit taller than Mario.

=== Behavior ===
Ant Troopers merely walk in a line with other Ant Troopers, and do not actively pursue [[Mario]] or another playable character. An Ant Trooper can walk around obstacles without falling to the ground, and it can also walk on walls and ceilings. It ignores the player's character when they are around, but the character can lose a life if they touch the Ant Trooper. However, the character can bounce on the Ant Trooper, although it does not mind them and does not take damage this way.

== In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' ==
Ant Troopers appear as enemies. They are first encountered in [[Ant Trooper Hill]], where they are the main feature. They also appear near the end of [[Champion's Road]]. An Ant Trooper sometimes carries an item on its back, such as a power-up item.

An Ant Trooper can be defeated from either a [[Ground Pound]], [[Cat Mario]]'s claw swipe, [[Tanooki Mario (power-up)|Tanooki Mario]]'s tail swipe, [[Rosalina]]'s [[spin]], or a projectile.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' ==
{{main|Horned Ant Trooper}}
Ant Trooper have variants named Horned Ant Troopers that appear in [[Twinsy Tropics Dungeon]]. Horned Ant Troopers jump and dive with their helmets pointed at either Mario or Luigi. The victim can counterattack them with their helmets. If the Horned Ant Troopers dealt enough damage from Mario or Luigi, they will lose their helmets to become normal Ant Troopers. The Ant Troopers will dash into the victim, who can jump and stomp on its head. Ant Troopers appear in groups of ten, and if the number of them in battle decreases, several Ant Troopers will retreat from battle.

== In ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]'' ==
[[File:MTA Bask Ruins.png|200px|thumb|left|Ant Troopers as spectators in [[Bask Ruins]].]]
Ant Troopers appear as spectators in [[Bask Ruins]], not affecting the gameplay.

== In ''[[Dr. Mario World]]'' ==
[[File:DMW Ant Trooper.png|100px|thumb|Ant Trooper in ''[[Dr. Mario World]]''.]]
An Ant Trooper is an assistant. In Stage Mode, when at least four [[virus]]es are eliminated at the same time, the Ant Trooper can fill the skill meter and the amount depends on its skill level. The player can unlock the Ant Trooper after they clear [[Stage 441]], the first area of [[World 12 (Dr. Mario World)|World 12]].

If the player completes an area in World 12, Ant Troopers appear along with [[Stingby|Stingbies]].
