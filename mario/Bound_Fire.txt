'''Bound Fires''' are a sub-species of [[Fire Snake]]s that appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', exclusively within the level [[Outrageous]]. They attack by hopping around and leaving drops of fire. Bound Fires resemble regular Fire Snakes, but with the smaller fireballs following in their wake. A Bound Fire can be defeated by either having [[Yoshi]] eat them, having [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] touch them while under the effects of a [[Super Star|Starman]], or even by spinning their [[cape]] on them while utilizing their [[Cape Mario|Cape]] power-up.
