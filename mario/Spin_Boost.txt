A '''Spin Boost''' is a move in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|version]], ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
A Spin Boost occurs only during anti-gravity segments, either from two drivers making contact or by touching a [[Spin Boost Pillar]]. Performing a Spin Boost causes a racer to spin, while a blue circle effect is shown, and gain a boost in speed. If it is two racers making contact, they propel slightly away from one another.
