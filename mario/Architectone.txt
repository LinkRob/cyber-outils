'''Architectone''' is a character appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''.

==Description==
===Appearance===
Architectone takes their design from a house, having a blue generally arrow-shaped body with yellow atop his head representing a house's roof.

===Personality===
Architectone is a real estate agent and creates getaways for [[Donkey Kong (character)|DK]] and [[Pauline]] to rest in at the cost of [[Banandium Gold]]. 

==History==
Architectone's role is to host Getaways, which function as resting spots where [[Donkey Kong (character)|DK]] and [[Pauline]] can rest to restore their health. When spoken to, he states the charge for the Getaway's construction, and after being paid, he digs out space for the Getaway.

==Behind the Scenes==
Architectone takes his name from "architecture" and [[Fractone]], the former referencing his construction of resting spots.
