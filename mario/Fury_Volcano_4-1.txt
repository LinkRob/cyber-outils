'''Fury Volcano 4-1''' is the first level of [[Fury Volcano]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It has the area with the smoke. The player can be affected by it, unless if they use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]].

==Enemies==
*[[Fang]]
*[[Goomba]]
*[[Podoboo]]
*[[Red Paratroopa]]
*[[Rex]]

==Music Score==
*Junp through the pipe, and stomp the button with the [[Rage]] vibe, which you can get the "Shriek Mansion 2".

==Minigame==
Before completing the level, you receive a minigame called "[[Toad Tote]]".

==[[Toad]]s==
*Slide under, where the [[Goomba]] is found.
*Before entering the pipe, there is a box blocking her path.
*Despite nearly completing the level, jump to the left and fall down and run right.
