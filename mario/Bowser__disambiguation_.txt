'''Bowser''' has several different counterparts in ''[[Mario (series)]]''.

*[[Bowser]], the king of the [[Koopa Kingdom]], [[Dark Land]], and [[Bowser's Kingdom]].
**[[Paper Bowser]], the paper counterpart of Bowser from the [[Paper Mario (series)]].
**[[Baby Bowser]], the infant counterpart of Bowser from the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]].
**[[Megadragonbowser]], the fused form of Bowser and the MegaBug from [[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]].
**[[Dreamy Bowser]], the dream powered-up counterpart of Bowser from [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]].
**[[Shrowser]], the fusion of Bowser and the spirit of the [[Elder Princess Shroob]] from [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]].
**[[Bowletta]], the fusion of Bowser and [[Cackletta]] from [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]].
**[[Black Paint Bowser]], the black paint possessed form of Paper Bowser from [[Paper Mario: Color Splash]].
**[[Blockhead Bowser]], the Megamorph Belt power-up form of Bowser from [[Mario Party DS]].
**[[Meowser]], the cat counterpart of Bowser from [[Super Mario 3D World]] and [[Super Mario Maker 2]].
**Bowcien, the possessed counterpart of Bowser from [[Mario Tennis Aces]].
**[[Shiny RoboBowser]], the powered-up form of Bowser after fusing with Paper Bowser and a bunch of paper enemies from [[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]].
*[[Bowser Jr.]], the only child son of Bowser and the apparent heir of the Koopa Kingdom.
