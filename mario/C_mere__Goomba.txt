'''C'mere, Goomba''' is a hidden mission on [[Boulder Bowl Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Here, the goal is for [[Mario]] to bring a [[Goomba]] to the [[Gold Gearmo]] on the Boulder Planet. Several [[Chomp]]s and [[Boulder]]s are featured on the planet, the former of which cannot be defeated. If Mario brings a Goomba to the Gold Gearmo, he earns a [[Power Star]]. If every Goomba on the planet is defeated, the mission has to be restarted.
