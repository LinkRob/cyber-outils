'''Green Berries''' are a rare variation of [[berry|berries]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. They only appear in the last level, [[Funky]]. By eating a Green Berry, [[Yoshi]] can add twenty seconds to the [[Time Limit|timer]].
