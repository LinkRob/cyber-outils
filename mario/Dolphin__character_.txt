'''Dolphin''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. He is a representative of his species of the [[Dolphin|same name]], and Dolphin's only visual distinction from them is the red bowtie that he wears.

== ''Mario Party'' series ==

=== ''Mario Party Advance'' ===
In ''[[Mario Party Advance]],'' Dolphin is a comedian who is found in [[Mario Vaudeville]]. He is not entirely confident with telling jokes, so he asks the player's character to listen to his routine. During Dolphin's quest, the player's character has to laugh during the punchlines and jokes told by Dolphin. In doing so, Dolphin gives them the [[Snow Globe (Gaddget)|Snow Globe]] [[Gaddget]]. Otherwise, if the player's character does not laugh or does so during the wrong moments, the Dolphin gets upset and beats himself up.{{MPANav}}
