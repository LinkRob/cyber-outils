'''Shy Guy Shops''' are a type of item shop in the {{series|Mario Party}}, in which they appear in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'' and ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''. They are shops run by a [[Shy Guy]], as the name would suggest, and they have the appearance of a small tent. Unlike item shops in earlier ''Mario Party'' games, Shy Guy Shops are actual [[space]]s that a character needs to land on at the end of their turn instead of locations that can be accessed by passing by them.

Shy Guy Shops are featured in the Toad Scramble and Balloon Bash modes of ''Mario Party: Star Rush'' and the Minigame Match mode of ''Mario Party: The Top 100''.
