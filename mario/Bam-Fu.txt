'''Bam-Fu''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', categorized under [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[Sci-Fi]] folder. It is similar to [[Log Chop]] but it involves a man having to chop a bamboo stick in half.

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name is a portmanteau of "bamboo" and "kung-fu."
