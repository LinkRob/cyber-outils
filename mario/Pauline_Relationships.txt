'''[[Pauline]]''' is a responsible and kind-hearted woman. She thinks positively high of herself but not egotistic. Pauline was known for her positive relationship with Mario.

==Friends/Allies==
===Mario===
Pauline and [[Mario]] had a positive relationship despite Mario as the ex-boyfriend. Pauline has been kidnapped by [[Donkey Kong]] and was captive on a dangerous construction site until Mario rescued her. In revenge for Pauline's kidnap, Mario kidnaps Donkey Kong. In later video games, she was replaced by Peach that Pauline and Mario were confirmed to be friends. During the events of ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', Pauline asked for Mario's help in [[New Donk City]]. After Mario's efforts of helping the city, she praised him. Even she sang a song dedicated to Mario called [[Jump Up, Super Star!]]. While she was also seen to be forced to attend Bowser and Peach's wedding, she told Mario that he was "just in time".

==Neutral==
=== Donkey Kong ===
Donkey Kong was Mario's pet gorilla, who escapes and kidnaps Mario's former girlfriend, [[Pauline]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://themushroomkingdom.net/interview_miyamoto_nom18_feb2000.shtml|title=Exclusive Interview with Donkey Kong Creator Shigeru Miyamoto|work=The Mushroom Kingdom}}</ref> Mario chases after Donkey Kong to rescue Pauline and return Donkey Kong.

In [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong|''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series]], Donkey Kong kidnaps Pauline with no connection to Mario whatsoever. In ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars]]'', Donkey Kong and Pauline staged a kidnap to prepare a surprise party for Mario.

=== Princess Peach ===
Pauline and [[Princess Peach]] barely interacted with each other. Princess Peach had replaced Pauline as the original damsel-in-distress. It has been mentioned that Pauline changed her hair color to be more different from Peach. However, they seem to have the same thoughts about [[Bowser]] that they were not interested to attend the wedding as seen in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

==Enemies/Rivals==
===Bowser===
Pauline was displeased with [[Bowser]] in ''Super Mario Odyssey''. She was tasteless with Bowser placed many posters about his wedding around [[New Donk City]]. When Mario arrived at the Moon Chapel, Pauline as one of the attendees forced to participate the wedding, told Mario that he was "just in time". This showed her expressing annoyance to the wedding.
