A '''Thunder Cloud''' is an item from the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and later appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

When a racer receives the item, a Thunder Cloud appears above their head. After a short amount of time, lightning will strike down from the cloud. This can either do one of two things: it can zap the player sporadically with charges, but if done for too long, the racer shrinks in the process, essentially causing the Thunder Cloud to act the same as the [[Lightning]] item. A racer can pass a Thunder Cloud on to a different racer by bumping into them.

The Thunder Cloud disappear if the player goes through a cannon, such as those in [[DK Mountain]], [[DK Summit]], [[Rainbow Road]], or [[Maple Treeway]].
