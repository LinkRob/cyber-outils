'''Miracle Cheese''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. It replaces the [[Max Syrup Jar]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

==Description==
Miracle Cheeses restore all of [[Bowser]]'s BP. It is one of the most useful items to use in boss fights.
