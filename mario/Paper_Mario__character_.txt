'''Paper Mario''' is the titular main protagonist of the {{series|Paper Mario}}. He is a plumber with a paper-based art style. 

== Profile ==
Paper Mario is an independent character from [[Mario]] and interacts with him, Luigi, and various other characters from both the [[Mushroom Earth|real world]] and the [[Mysterious Book|Paper world]] within the book. Paper Mario himself is depicted as Mario in the ''Paper Mario'' series. Paper Mario is flatter than Mario, and has his own world, where everything (except the characters) is made of paper and cardboard.

=== ''Mario & Luigi'' series ===
==== ''Mario and Luigi: Paper Jam'' ====
In [[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam|''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'']], Paper Mario is a dimensional counterpart of [[Mario]]. 

He is shot out from [[Mysterious Book|his world]] into the main [[Mushroom Earth|world]] that Mario and Luigi live in, after Luigi had accidentally opened up a magical book found from a-top [[Princess Peach's Castle|Princess Peach's castle]]. Upon this book opening, Paper Mario first appears by saving Mario and Luigi from the threat of [[Crinkle Goomba]] and [[Paper Goomba|his comrades]]. After their defeat, Paper Mario join forces with the Mario Bros. and [[Starlow]] as they help to defeat the [[Bowser|two]] [[Paper Bowser|Bowsers]] and return all paper characters back home. 

Throughout the journey, Paper Mario would come up an idea by developing skills like [[Trio Move|Trio Moves]] for use on the field. When the group returns to visit [[Gloomy Woods]], Paper Mario, Mario and Starlow are kidnapped by [[King Boo]], and Luigi must face his fears to rescue them. Paper Mario is rescued, though he is held hostage during the battle with King Boo, who is initially disguised as him.

As the trio arrive [[Neo Bowser Castle]], Paper Mario then has to defeat Bowser and Paper Bowser with the help of [[Toadette]]'s new invention, [[Papercraft Fire Mario]], and successfully destroys Papercraft Bowser. The trio fight the Bowsers one last time, taking down their [[Shiny RoboBowser|new transformation]] and sealing Paper Bowser back to the book. Paper Mario is present during the papercraft parade and bids farewell to Mario, Luigi and the others as he returns to the paper world.

=== ''Super Mario'' series ===

==== ''Super Mario Maker'' ====
Paper Mario makes a cameo appearance alongside Mario and Luigi as part of the "Mario Trio" costume, one of the many unlockable [[Costume Mario]] costumes in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. The costume is unlocked by clearing the Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam Event Course.

=== ''Super Smash Bros.'' series ===

==== ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' ====
Paper Mario appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as cameo [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]], representing the [[Papercraft Mario]].
