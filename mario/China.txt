|name=China}}'''China''' is a location on [[Earth]] that has appeared in a few [[Mario (franchise)|Mario]] games. China is a location you can visit in [[Mario is Missing!]]. And both versions [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]] ([[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Wii]] and [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Nintendo DS)|DS]]) take place in Beijing, which is the capital of China. [[Bowser's Kingdom]] from [[Super Mario Odyssey]] takes inspiration from China.
