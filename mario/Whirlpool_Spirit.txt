The '''Whirlpool Spirit''' is a minor character from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. He is a spirit that can be found waiting in an oasis on [[Allsand Island]].

== Description ==
The Whirlpool Spirit manifests as a whirlpool. His true form is unknown.

== History ==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can talk to the Whirlpool Spirit at any time when they visit the oasis at the centre of [[Allsand Island]]. The spirit requests the following items:

* Three [[Mushroom (item)|Super Mushrooms]]
* A [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership/Gear|Quick-Charge Brooch]]
* A [[Crafting Materials (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Treasurefish]]
* 30 [[Crafting Materials (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Seedle Seeds]]

Upon satisfying the spirit, Mario and Luigi receive the Seedle-ize [[Battle Plug]] from him, which they can use to randomly cause enemies to instantly pop, though [[Seedle|Seedles]] will spawn.
