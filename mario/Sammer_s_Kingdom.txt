The '''Sammer's Kingdom''' is world six of [[Super Paper Mario|''Super Paper Mario'']]. During the events of the game it was swallowed to oblivion by the [[The Void]]. During this Mario has to find the 6th pure heart, but to get to the king who has the pure heart, the heroes must defeat the hundred soldiers of the king. After the 50th fight the portal is seen expanding and Mario and his friends are allowed through to the king where they meet Mimi who they have to defeat to get the pure heart, but then the world is swallowed by the Void and when the pure heart is found, it has turned into stone.
