'''Rocketbarrel Barrage''' is [[Diddy Kong]]'s [[Final Smash]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. It reappears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''. The move would later be replaced with [[Hyper Rocketbarrel]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
The move is based on Diddy's [[Rocketbarrel Boost]] move in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. When activated, Diddy puts on his [[Barrel Jet|Rocketbarrel Pack]] and starts flying around the stage. The player can control Diddy's flight and press the attack button to launch exploding [[peanut]]s downward from Diddy's angle. Diddy shoots a peanut automatically after a few seconds have passed, and he can also damage opponents by ramming into them.

When the attack ends, Diddy Kong's Rocketbarrel Pack violently explodes and flies off in different directions. Once the pack is about explode, Diddy Kong begins to flash. Upon exploding, Diddy is flung slightly in the air and he is put into a [[w:c:supersmashbros:Helpless|helpless]] state in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', but not in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U''. Additionally, Diddy Kong can KO himself if he flies offscreen, unlike other flying Final Smashes like [[Super Sonic]] and [[Charizard]]'s [[Mega Evolution]].
