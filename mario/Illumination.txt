'''Illumination''' (formerly '''Illumination Entertainment''') is an animation studio founded by Chris Meledandri in 2007 after he resigned from Blue Sky Studios. The studio is known for creating the [https://universalstudios.fandom.com/wiki/Despicable_Me_(franchise) Despicable Me] franchise, which is currently the highest-grossing animated film franchise of all time.

==''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''==
Illumination collaborated with [[Nintendo]] to make a film based on the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. It was put off on multiple times until they confirmed the release date of the movie on April 5th, 2023, previously April 7th and December 21st, 2022.
