'''Mini Luigis''' are toys versions of [[Luigi]] made by [[Mario Toy Company]]. It made its debut in [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars|''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars'']], having the same function as [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Mario]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Description ===
Mini Luigis are identical to Luigi, but with big black dots as eyes and a wind-up key on his back. 

== In {{Series|Mario vs. Donkey Kong}} ==

=== ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars]]'' ===
[[File:Mini Luigi Tipping Stars.png|thumb|Artwork of Mini Luigi ]]
Mini Luigis have to be guided across the levels, much like the other toys. All toys must be lead to the door within the time limit. To unlock Mini Luigis in the Workshop Store, they must be bought with 150 Stars.

=== [[Mini Mario & Friends: amiibo Challenge]] ===
Mini Luigi also appears in [[Mini Mario & Friends: amiibo Challenge|''Mini Mario & Friends: amiibo Challenge'']]. Mini Luigi's ability is called High Jump, reflecting Luigi's ability to jump higher than Mario. With it, Mini Luigi is able to reach higher grounds. The ability is similar to Mini Rosalina's Lunar Launch ability, however Mini Luigi's ability allows him to move forward despite having a shorter jump.
