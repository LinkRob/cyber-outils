''''Jamboree Buddies''' are a mechanic in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Description==
Jamboree Buddies are non-playable characters who assist one of the characters currently in play. On a certain turn, a Jamboree Buddy appears somewhere on the [[board]], and when one of the playable characters encounters them, a [[Showdown Minigame]] for recruiting them occurs, and the character who began the minigame has a slight advantage. If there is a tie, a "Tie-Break Dice" event occurs, where both players roll a [[Dice Block]], and the Jamboree Buddy goes to whomever rolled the higher number. 

On the board, each Jamboree Buddy has a unique ability to help the character they are with. Additionally, all of them cause board actions to happen twice, such as gaining or losing coins, buying items or Stars, and even Boos and Bowser Spaces. When obtained, the Jamboree Buddy lasts for three turns (including the turn when they are obtained). Other players may also steal the buddy by passing by the player who currently owns them. If no player reaches the Jamboree Buddy within three turns, they will be removed from the board. [[Rosalina]] will not appear as a Jamboree Buddy if minigames with motion controls are turned off because her Showdown minigame, [[Rosalina's Radical Race]], utilizes motion controls.

In Free Play, Showdown Minigame Battle and Party-Planner Trek, there is a possibility of characters encountering and recruiting a [[look-alike]] of themselves as a Jamboree Buddy.

Similar to [[Ally|Allies]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]'', the [[Toad (species)|Toad]] host speaks on behalf of [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], who take the role of silent protagonists. Meanwhile, both [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s dialogue is translated in parentheses.

==Trivia==
*By having Peach as a Jamboree Buddy on the board [[Rainbow Galleria]] while a Flash Sale takes place, it is possible to purchase two Stars for as little as 10 coins in total, provided the player is not under the effect of a [[Markup Sticker]]. Similarly, by having Daisy as a Jamboree Buddy on the board Rainbow Galleria while a Flash Sale takes place, it is possible to purchase items for as little as a quarter of their original cost.
