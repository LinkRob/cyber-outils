'''World 8-2''' is the second level of [[World 8 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 8]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. The player is given a time limit of 400 seconds to complete it.

== Layout ==
This is an overworld course that once again features many gaps and narrow platforms. The player will encounter another [[Lakitu]], which will continuously toss down [[Spinies]], as well as [[Little Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, [[Turtle Cannon]]s, [[Bullet Bill]]s, [[Piranha Plant]]s, and [[Buzzy Beetle]]s. At the beginning is an elongated row of [[Brick Block]]s, with the very first one containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The player can use the [[springboard]] below to reach it. There is only one secret underground area, located near the end of the level. It is accessed through the second-to-last pipe that contains a Piranha Plant.

== Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' ==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 28000.

=== Red Coin locations ===
* Red Coin #1 is located on the very long row of [[Brick Block]]s, where the 1-Up Mushroom is found.
* Red Coin #2 is located in the group of Brick Blocks following the long row of blocks. It is in the right block.
* Red Coin #3 is located in the air, following the second pipe. It is above the narrow platforms.
* Red Coin #4 is located inside a new [[Coin Block]] in the secret underground area. The player can reach it using the [[Hidden Block]] placed just before the exit pipe.
* Red Coin #5 is located between the Turtle Cannons and the stone staircase.

=== Yoshi Egg location ===
* The Yoshi Egg is found inside a Hidden Block following the [[Warp Pipe]] to the secret underground area.
