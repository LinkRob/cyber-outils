'''Masks''' are objects that are found in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
Masks are located at [[Hoohoo Mountain]] and appear a few times. Masks can be activated after Mario and Luigi extinguish the nearby [[Fire Statue|Fire Statues]]. Masks produce [[Whirlwind|whirlwinds]]. Mario and Luigi can perform the Spin Jump to come through the whirlwind and move to the other side. They are first seen deactivated.{{M&LSSNav}}
