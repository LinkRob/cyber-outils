'''Naplock''' (named ''ゴーネム'' '''Gōnemu''' in Japanese) is a lazy pig golem which appears in [[Princess Peach's Castle]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. In the field, Naplocks release bubbles from their noses, and if [[Bowser]] touches either the Naplock or its bubble, a battle starts.

==Battle==
When [[Bowser]] first encounters these foes, Napocks are first found asleep and are usually allied in battle with [[Alarm Bob-omb]]s. When the Alarm Bob-ombs go off, Naplocks push them to stop them and continue their rest.

While these foes are asleep, they attack with their green bubbles, which, when hitting Bowser, give the sick status condition. Sometimes, however, they release transparent sky blue colored bubbles with a [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]] inside of them. This allows Bowser to regain health if he has lost any.

They wake up once their Alarm Bob-omb allies explode or if Bowser attacks them. If they are awake, they have a new tactic, heaving [[Brick Block|bricks]] at Bowser. First, they spew out five, six or seven bricks and run towards Bowser on which Bowser has to punch the bricks. A few seconds later, Naplock heaves the remaining bricks at Bowser on which he must duck into his shell to avoid the attack. If none are punched, Bowser may still duck into his shell, but only for a limited time, making the remaining bricks hit him. This makes him stunned in the process of Naplock's attack.

While [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] cannot normally fight Naplocks and other enemies in Peach's Castle, functional code for an encounter can be discovered via cheats. The brick blocks target both of the Mario Bros., who must jump to break them before they land on their heads.
