'''Kid Icarus''' is a [[microgame]] found in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'', made by [[9-Volt]]. The purpose is to avoid the enemies' attacks. Ten points are needed to clear it.

==Gameplay==
*'''Level 1''': One enemy throws eggplants at [[Pit]].
*'''Level 2''': Two enemies throw eggplants at Pit, and a hole releases creatures.
*'''Level 3''': Two enemies throw eggplants at Pit, and two holes release creatures.
