'''Milk''' is a drink and a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Milk is only seen in the kitchen of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] when [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] have to bake a [[cake]] for [[Gourmet Guy]] after the events of Chapter 4. Adding too much Milk ruins the cake, forcing Peach and Twink to start over.
