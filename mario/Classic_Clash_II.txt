'''Classic Clash II''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', in which it is part of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[Sci-Fi]] folder.

==Gameplay==
The player controls a [[Mario]]-like hero (later named "Nintenman" in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''{{'}}s [[Classic Clash]]). The hero is holding a [[Bowser]]-like enemy, and the player must hold right on the {{button|gba|Pad}} to push the opponent to the ground.

With each increased difficulty level, the Bowser-like enemy puts up more of a resistance.
