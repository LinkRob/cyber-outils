'''Krackas''' are a rare subspecies of [[Klasps]] exclusive to the [[GBA|Game Boy Advance]] remake of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3]]''. They only appear in the level [[Stormy Seas]] of the [[Pacifica]] world.

Krackas, just like their land counterparts, [[Knocka|Knockas]], hide in a [[barrel]], and wait for the Kongs to approach them. Once the Kongs are in sight, they stand up and charge at the Kongs, aiming to explode upon impact with them. However, the Kongs can jump on a Kracka's head to immobilize it for a short period of time. As long as Kracka isn't active, it's barrel can be picked up and thrown like a regular barrel.

Unused sprites for Kracka were also found in the files of the original [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''.
