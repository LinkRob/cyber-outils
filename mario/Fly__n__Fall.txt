'''Fly 'n' Fall''' is the boss [[microgame]] of [[Mona]]'s stage in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''. The microgame was later remade in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as [[Balloon Brigade]], which instead stars [[Tiny Wario]]s.

==Gameplay==
There are three characters, each floating in midair from a balloon attached to them. The player has to pop each character's balloon while they are above an island so that they fall down onto it. The character with the red balloon moves around quickly, the one with the yellow balloon at a moderate speed, and the character with a blue balloon at a slow speed. The player loses at the microgame if either the three characters do not land on the island within the time limit or a character falls into the [[pit]].
