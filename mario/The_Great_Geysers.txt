==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': On the third [[Waterspout]] in super form, ground pound the brick blocks in the geyser above the coin when the geyser disappears.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Jump on the palm tree with the Red Koopa Troopa and second Star Coin after the trail of blue coins.
*'''Star Coin 3''': To the left of the last Waterspout, be in [[Ice Mario|Ice]] or [[Penguin Mario|Penguin]] form to freeze the group of [[River Piranha Plant]]s and walk on them. If you're not in any of those forms (unless you're [[Nabbit]]), make a careful jump to avoid touching the enemies or the spiked balls.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Turn to the left wall when starting to find a picture of the modern Luigi's head paved on the wall.
