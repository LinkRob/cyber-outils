'''Mrs. Gammliss''' is an unseen character whose only appearance is in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Opera]]." She lives in apartment 5C of the building that [[Mario Bros. Plumbing]] is located in.

==Trivia==
*Mrs. Gammliss is the only guest character who does not make any onscreen appearance, even as a costume.
