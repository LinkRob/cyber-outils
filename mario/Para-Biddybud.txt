'''Para-Biddybluds''' are enemies from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. They are a variant of [[Biddybud]]s sporting a pair of [[wing]]s, and are capable of flying. Para-Biddybluds mostly appear in sky levels. They normally fly back and forth or in squares and are occasionally found by a [[Goal Pole|flagpole]], flying in a fixed vertical pattern.  

They also appear as obstacles and a playable racer in [[Mario Kart World]].
