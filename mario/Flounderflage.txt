'''Flounderflages''' are enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', in which they are commonly encountered in [[Dozing Sands]].

== Profile ==
Flounderflage are flounder fish that have a red, green or purple back with a yellow underbelly. They have big, bulging eyes and an orange tail.

==Battle==
Flounderflages start their battle by flashing their true colours, then turning tan by covering itself in sand. A Flounderflage has two attacks. In the first, it will charge towards the Bro. of its choice. This must be jumped unless the Flounderflage tilts back. Following this the Flounderflage charges the Bro. that matches its true colour. This must also be jumped. The second has all of them charge their respective targets at once, using up all their attack turns. Flounderflages are Level 9 monsters that have 28 hit points, 39 power, 51 defense, and 43 speed, giving 24 experience, 8 coins, and possibly a Super Nut or Shock Bomb upon their defeat.

Flounderflages can also appear in the [[Torkscrew]]'s battle, when the Torkscrew rushes one of his opponents and the other has to fend off monsters while the boss harasses his target. However, in this battle, the Flounderflages are purple. They charge a few seconds after they appear, but can be jumped on.

==Trivia==
*Flounderflages are similar to [[Nooz]]es since they signal which Bro. they will attack by matching that Bro's. colour. However, Noozes change their colour.
**They are also similar to the [[Koopeleon]], camouflaging to hide.
