'''''Mario Party Superstars''''' is a [[Nintendo Switch]] party video game developed by [[Ndcube|ND Cube]] and published by [[Nintendo]]. It is the twelfth main installment of the console [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]]. The game was released Internationally on October 29, 2021.

==Overview==
The game features five game boards from the original ''Mario Party'' trilogy from the [[Nintendo 64]] and 100 minigames across the entire series up to that point. Many of which have also previously returned in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.

== Trivia ==
*[[Birdo]] returns as a playable character for the first time since ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.
*Due to [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] being a playable character, [[DK Space]]s do not return in this game. And thus, none of the DK minigames return as retro minigames.
*This is the first installment in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series  (since ''[[Mario Party 8]]'') to have characters traditionally use 1-10 Dice Blocks instead of the 1-6 ones.
*This is the fourth installment in the series overall to not have a Bowser-themed board, after ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', ''[[Mario Party 6|6]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.
*This is the fifth and most recent installment --counting just the home console games-- to only have playable protagonists after ''[[Mario Party|Mario Party 1]]'', ''[[Mario Party 2|2]]'', ''[[Mario Party 3|3]]'', and ''[[Mario Party 4|4]]'' (not counting [[Mario]], [[Wario]], [[Waluigi]], [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], and [[Birdo]] who have been both a protagonist and a villain). 
**[[Boo]] --despite being playable in later installments (including ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' through ''[[Mario Party 8|8]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Party]]'')-- returns as a space character (that being from the Boo House). [[Bowser Jr.]] --the successor to [[Koopa Kid]] (from the first 7 installments)-- appears on item minigames (via Item Spaces). The remaining two characters ([[Hammer Bro]] and [[Blooper]]) appear in various minigames. 
**All the antagonists --but [[Dry Bones]], [[Blooper]], [[Kamek]], and [[Pom Pom]]-- return in the next installment (counting just the home console games) ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''. That is, including [[Bowser Jr.]] (the successor to [[Koopa Kid]]), [[Boo]], [[Hammer Bro]], [[Spike]], [[Koopa Troopa]], [[Shy Guy]], and [[Goomba]] return as playable characters in the next installment. (That is, ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.) And the newcomer [[Ninji]] is also a playable antagonist. 
***Coincidentally, both of the first two antagonists --[[Boo]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] (the successor to [[Koopa Kid]])-- return in the two later home console games (that is, ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''). 
*This is the second installment (if counting just the home console games) to:
**be released on the [[Nintendo Switch]] (that is, after ''[[Super Mario Party]]'').
**be released worldwide on the same day (that is, after ''[[Super Mario Party]]'').
*This is the last installment (if counting just the home console games) with [[Charles Martinet]] as the voice of [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Wario]], and [[Waluigi]] prior to his retirement.
**This is also the final installment (if counting just the home console games) where [[Princess Daisy]] is voiced by [[Deanna Mustard]].
*Counting both the home console and handheld games, this is the second installment to not have minigames of its own (after ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'').
