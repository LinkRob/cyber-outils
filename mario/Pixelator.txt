}}The '''Pixelator''' is one of [[Professor E. Gadd]]'s inventions that was used mainly in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Usage==
E. Gadd uses the Pixelator to transport Luigi in and out of the mansions. E Gadd uses the main screen to being the pixelating process while cameras around mansions transport Luigi to that spot. Once Luigi starts clearing through missions, E. Gadd will start Pixelating Luigi to areas inside the mansion itself not having to go through the front door again. There are also smaller screens that can be used for pixelating but are only used by the Toad Assistants. The Pixelating screens are found in one room of every mansion except [[Gloomy Manor]]. After Luigi defeated the Tough Possessor; as he is being pixelated back into the bunker, [[King Boo]] sabotages it, pulling him to the paranormal dimension.
