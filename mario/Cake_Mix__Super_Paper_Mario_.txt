{{Nihongo|'''Cake Mix'''|おかしのもと|Okashi no Moto app|Confectionery Base}} appeared as an ingredient in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
The Cake Mix is an ingredient that can be bought at [[Itty Bits]] in [[Flipside]]. [[Moon Cleft]]s, [[Goomba]]s and [[Gloomba]]s drop these items.
