'''Look the other way''' is a minigame in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It was created by [[Grate Guy]], and [[Mario]] can play it at [[Grate Guy's Casino]], free of charge.

==Gameplay==
Mario has to guess which direction Grate Guy will look in and look in a different direction from him. The minigame is based on luck.

The reward for playing is usually an ineffective healing item, such as a [[Moldy Mush]], but if Mario wins the minigame a 100th time, he earns the [[Star Egg]].
