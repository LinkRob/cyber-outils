The '''Station Keys''' are key items which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
There are two types of Station Key. One has three holes on it while the other only has one. However, they are both green in color.

== In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
One of the Station Keys specifically unlocks the entrance to [[Riverside Station]]. It is given to [[Mario]] by the [[Excess Express Conductor]] after the bridge being raised stops their progress. The other Station Keys can be found inside Riverside Station which can unlock doors.
