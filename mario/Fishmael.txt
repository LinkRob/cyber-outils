'''Fishmael''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Fishmael is a humanoid, fish-like creature who wears fisherman's clothes. Fishmael is always seen with his fishing rod.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Fishmael is often known for his fishing skills as hinted by himself and others, despite being a fish himself. Fishmael is always seen fishing at the docks of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] just outside [[Club 64]]. Fishmael also used to fish with [[Fuzzipede]] by using him as live bait, but abandoned him due to catching more fish by himself (and Fuzziepede eventually growing tired of being mistreated by Fishmael).

Fishmael is also involved in the chain letter side-quest where [[Mario]] and [[Parakarry]] deliver a letter from [[Koover]] of [[Koopa Village]] to Fishmael. Fishmael confirms that he and Koover are fishing buddies and was excited at the chance to go on a fishing trip even inviting Mario to come along. Fishmael then writes another letter back to Koover confirming he is able to go on the fishing trip.
