'''Pidget Bills''' are enemies that appear on ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==In ''[[Super Mario World]]'' ==
Pidgit Bills replace all of the [[Bullet Bill]]s during [[Fall]], which occurs after [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] clear the level [[Funky]]. Pidgit Bills are also shot out of [[Bill Blaster]]s, and they can harm [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] upon contact. Like regular Bullet Bills, a Pidgit Bill can only be defeated by either being [[jump]]ed or touching it as [[Invincible Mario]] or Luigi. A Pidgit Bill yields 200 [[point]]s upon being defeated.
