'''Mt. Frostbite''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Mt. Frostbite is a vast, snowy mountain with a blizzard constantly buffeting the area. A few pine trees appear in the background, and only their topmost part is covered in snow.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
[[File:Mr E at Mt Frostbite.png|thumb|left|200px|Mr. E showing up at Mt. Frostbite in ''Mario Party Advance'']]
Mt. Frostbite is located in the [[Snow Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], and it is only accessible through the [[Pipe House (Mario Party Advance)|Pipe House]]'s cyan pipe. [[Mr. E (Mario Party Advance)|Mr. E]] visits the mountain in search of a {{w|bigfoot}}, but he immediately runs away when he sees the snow-covered [[Bowser]] and [[Koopa Kid]] shivering beside him. Bowser then challenges the player, which initiates the quest [[Chillin' Villain]].

In the quest, the player's character has to warm Bowser up by telling him funny jokes. Once sufficiently warmed, Bowser challenges the them to a Bowser minigame, [[Crushed Ice]], and winning it unlocks both the minigame and the [[Breeze Buddy]] [[Gaddget]].
