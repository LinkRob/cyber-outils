'''Souvenirs''' are a recurring type of unlockable item in the {{series|WarioWare}}. They first appear in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''. They are similar in concept to the [[Gaddget]]s from ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'', and can be a variety of things, such as minigames, records, virtual toys and figurines, and gags.
