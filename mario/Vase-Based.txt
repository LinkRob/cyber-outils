'''Vase-Based''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. Whenever a [[Vase (enemy)|Vase]] is tipped over, it releases a Vase-Based, which carries two spears and has the tip of a spear on the bottom of its body. A Vase-Based jumps around the room, attempting to attack [[Wario]]. If Wario jumps onto a Vase-Based, it becomes stunned briefly. Wario can defeat a Vase-Based using any attack, including by throwing it at a Vase, although doing this does not defeat the Vase as well.
