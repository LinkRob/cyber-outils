'''{{Infobox Enemy|name=Paragoomba|image=Paper Paragoomba PMCS sprite.png|caption=|first=|games=''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''|homeland=|located=|leader=|species=[[Goomba]]|subspecies=|affiliates=|related=[[Koopa Paratroopa]]|attacks=Hurls itself at Mario with reckless abandon.|defeated=|comics=}}'''Paragoombas''' are a type of [[Goomba]] that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]].''

== Description ==
Similar to the appearance of the [[Goomba (Battle Card)|Goomba]], the difference being they now have wings to hover over the ground.

== Stats ==
'''HP:''' 10

'''Attacks:'''

* Body Attack ('''5''')
* Group Attack ('''5''')
* Blowback Attack ('''10''')

[[File:PMCS Paragoomba Card.png|left|frameless|82x82px]]

=== Price ===
Sell Price: 1 coin

=== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ===
The Paragoomba is never seen stood alone in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'', but is summoned from [[Whistle Snifit|Whistle Snifits]].
