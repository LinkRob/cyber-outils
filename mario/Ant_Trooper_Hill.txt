'''Ant Trooper Hill''' is the first level of [[World 4 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 4]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Features==
The level starts on a desert-like platform with some [[Ant Trooper]]s nearby. Some nearby platforms can be used to enter a cave infested with Ant Troopers. When the player exits the cave, several rows of [[Horned Ant Trooper]]s appear. A [[Super Star]] can be found on a nearby platform. After the row of Horned Ant Troopers, the [[Checkpoint Flag]] is reached. An area with [[Spike Trap|spikes]] on the ground follows, which must be traversed via the [[Big Ant Trooper]]s spawning from the nearby cave. A nearby pipe leads to an area with some Ant Troopers and the [[Goal Pole]].

==Green Stars==
*'''[[Green Power Star|Green Star]] 1''': In Cat form, climb the wall near the exit of the tunnel to get the star.
*'''Green Star 2''': After the checkpoint, jump on the Big Ant Trooper to ride it towards an alcove with a [[Warp Pipe]] in it. In the hidden area, climb the wall in Cat form or use the Ant Troopers to jump up there.
*'''Green Star 3''': In the second Big Ant Trooper section, jump on the Big Ant Trooper to ride it towards the star. Just jump up to it while under it.

==Stamp==
[[File:Ant_Trooper_Hill_Stamp.png|thumb]]In the end of the first tunnel, try to approach it from the right jump on one of the Ant Troopers leading towards the Ant Troop Stamp.

==[[Luigi sighting]]==
A crouching 8-bit Luigi can be seen near the platform where the Sprixie with binoculars is at. This is easier to see with the help of her binoculars.
