'''Diamond City Kindergarten''' is a kindergarten school attended by [[Kat and Ana]] in [[Diamond City]] in {{series|WarioWare}}. The location is never seen itself, although its class is featured in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Twisted!''==
[[File:Diamond Kindergarten WWTw.png|thumb|left|200px|The class of Diamond City Kindergarten in ''WarioWare: Twisted!'']]
During Kat & Ana's story, the two of them and their other classmates in Diamond City Kindergarten are taken on a field trip on the [[Diamond Knoll]] by their teacher. The class is instructed not to wander off. Kat and Ana disobey this order when after Kat damages a [[beehive]] with a stick, she and Ana flee from a swarm of [[bee]]s.

==Trivia==
*In the English-language instruction booklets for ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', Kat and Ana are instead said to attend [[Mystical Ninja Elementary]], but it is unclear whether it is the same location as Diamond City Kindergarten or not.
