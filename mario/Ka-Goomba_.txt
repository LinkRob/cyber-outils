'''Ka-Goomba!''', titled '''Goombas Galore''' in PAL releases, is an extra minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to try and shoot as many [[Goomba]]s as they can. Whenever a character hits a Goomba, they earn a [[point]]. There are also the more elusive [[Gold Goomba]]s, which are worth three points instead. After some time, the characters must hit a [[Whomp]] on its back to defeat it, and in doing so, the minigame ends.
