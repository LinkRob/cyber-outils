The '''Counter Badge''' is a recurring [[badge]] within the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Counter Badge can be bought from the clothes shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 140 [[coin]]s. It increases [[POW]] by 8 (12 in the Japanese version) and grants the [[Power-D]] special effect, which increases the power of its user's counterattack.

In the remake ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', the Counter Badge is instead received from a [[Monty Mole]] in the northeast area of [[Beanbean Fields]]. The Counter Badge instead increases POW at a higher number of 29, but it retains its Power-D special effect. The Counter Badge can be sold for only 3 [[coin]]s.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''==
The Counter Badge can be bought from [[Peach's Castle]] for 700 coins, and it also appears in [[Thwomp Volcano]]. It retains the ability to increase the power of its user's counterattack.
