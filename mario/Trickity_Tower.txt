'''Trickity Tower''' is an island located on [[Lake Lapcat]], the main location of the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
Trickity Tower is one of the few islands that surround the Ruins [[Giga Bell]]. As the name suggests, the island initially looks like an empty space, but is actually a large tower surrounded by a large blue invisible path, similar to the one used in [[Footlight Lane]], to trick the player.
