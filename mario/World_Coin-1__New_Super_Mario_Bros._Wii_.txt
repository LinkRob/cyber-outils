'''{{world|coin|1|}}''', is the first level of [[World Coin]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.  

==Layout==
This level starts off with an area resembling the stage from ''[[Mario Bros. (game)|Mario Bros.]]'', containing [[Red Koopa Troopa]]s and a [[POW Block]]. Two of the [[Brick Block]]s also contain [[Super Mushroom]]s. None of the Brick Blocks can be shattered in this area, and instead simply turn into Empty Blocks. Going through the pipe above takes the players to another area, where there are two more POW Blocks along the way (required to get the other [[Star Coin]]s), an area with [[Fire Bar]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, and the [[Goal Pole]].

== Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Hitting the POW Block in the first room makes coins and a Star Coin fall from the ceiling.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Halfway through, use a POW Block to make a Star Coin fall.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Near the end of the level, use another POW Block to make the third Star Coin fall.

==Enemies==
*[[Koopa Troopa]]s: 8
*[[Red Koopa Troopa]]s: 12
