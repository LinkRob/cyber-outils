The '''Starbeans Employee''' is a minor character that appears in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. He is employed at [[Starbeans Café]] which is owned by [[Professor E. Gadd]].

==Appearance==
The Starbeans Employee is a [[Beanish]] character that has yellow hair and wears shades.

==History==
The Starbeans Employee's main job is to blend [[bean]]s to make [[Starbeans Espresso|espresso]]. After doing so, his boss E. Gadd usually appears and has a small sample of Mario and Luigi's espresso shortly before giving them a special item.
