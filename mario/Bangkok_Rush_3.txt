'''Bangkok Rush 3''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', serving as the third route for [[Bangkok Rush]]. It made its debut in the 2022 Winter Tour, alongside [[Bangkok Rush 2]].

== Layout ==
The racers start in the same location that they do for the [[Bangkok Rush 1|first]] [[Bangkok Rush 2|two]] routes, but this time, they face away from the four-way intersection. After the course starts, the racers make two right-angled left turns followed by a right turn onto a decorated street lined with parked tuk-tuks. They then make a slight right to drive up a hill, before going off a [[Glide Ramp]] to [[Glider|fly]] around the Giant Swing and Democracy Monument. If they get shocked after a certain point, they can also drive on the roads around the landmarks. After this, the racers make a right turn onto a road beside the Chao Phraya River with more parked tuk-tuks that can be bounced off of for a [[Trick|Jump Boost]]. The racers make their way back to the finish line by making a sharp right turn.

''[[Mario Kart 8]]''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s version of [[Bangkok Rush]] has the racers go down this route for the second lap, using the beginning section of [[Bangkok Rush 2]] until the decorated street to align themselves with Bangkok Rush 3. For the final lap, Bangkok Rush 2 will become the main focus, but the racers still need to go down Bangkok Rush 3 until the decorated street to align themselves with Bangkok Rush 2. The tuk-tuks on the street next to the Chao Phraya River were removed, with some crabs taking their place, and the curb at the final turn is no longer off-road, giving the racers a bit more room for error in making the final turn on lap 2.
