The '''Monster's Hammer''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
The Monster's Hammer is the [[Special Item (Mario Kart series)|Special Item]] of [[Blinky]]. It is a battering item, similarly to the [[Thunder Stick]], and upon using the Monster's Hammer, the user swings it around for about five seconds, damaging nearby racers and causing them to lose speed. Exclusively in ''Mario Kart Arcade GP'' is a variant named Monster's Hammer+, which extends the duration of the attack by two seconds.
