}}A '''Red Magiblot''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Red Magiblots are rather strange enemies that mainly reside in [[Castle Bleck]] and are often found near their relatives: the [[Blue Magiblot]]s and the [[Yellow Magiblot]]s. Red Magiblots attack with a magic blob that can be damaging from a distance. Red Magiblots can also be found in certain rooms of the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]].
