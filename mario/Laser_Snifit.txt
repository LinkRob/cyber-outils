'''Laser Snifits''' are enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|+ ''Bowser's Minions'']].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Laser Snifits are [[Snifit]]s that wear lab coats. Instead of having a hollow mouth shooter, Laser Snifits each have an electric pacifier-like ray in its mouth.

===Behavior===
Laser Snifits can shoot laser rings with their electric ray. If Laser Snifits do it from a distance, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have to jump over the electric ray to avoid taking damage, unless the Laser Snifit does it nearby, in which case the targeted [[Mario Bros.|Mario brother]] takes damage if he jumps. Laser Snifits can be healed from [[Thunder (element)|thunder]]-elemental damage, but take normal damage from other attacks.

Unlike rest of those who have been under transformation by Cackletta and Fawful ([[Queen Bean]] and [[Prince Peasley]]) they do not get restored or knocked out, instead they are permanently destroyed, making this a tragic fate.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
[[File:Laser Snifit M&LSS battle.png|thumb|Mario and Luigi battling a Laser Snifit in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'']]
Laser Snifits are the first enemies that [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] encounter at [[Woohoo Hooniversity]]. A [[Beanish]] professor explains that the professors of Woohoo Hooniversity were transformed into Laser Snifits by [[Cackletta]] and [[Fawful]] as his colleagues left the building. After his explanation, the professor is transformed into a Laser Snifit, being the first one that Mario and Luigi have no choice but to battle. In the remake, the Laser Snifit gives the [[Jumping Boots]] [[Badge]] to Mario and Luigi after being defeated.
