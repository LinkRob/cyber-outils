'''Cat Shines''' are the main collectable of [[Bowser's Fury]]. They are shaped like the face of a cat and are colored red and yellow. <ref>https://twinfinite.net/2021/02/bowsers-fury-cat-shines-many-how/</ref>There is a total of 100 cat shines in the game [[Bowser's Fury]]. They're used to open up [[Lighthouse|Lighthouses]] which are used to unsummon [[Fury Bowser]]. If you collect one Cat Shine while [[Fury Bowser]] is summoned he will disappear. Once you collect a cat shine they will lose their power and turn clear. Collecting them after that grants you five coins.
