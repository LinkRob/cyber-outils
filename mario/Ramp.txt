'''Ramps''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''.

==Description==
Using a ramp allows a character to jump high into the air, and using some is required to travel across wide gaps. A [[Boost Pad]] is often featured on a ramp, allowing a character to travel even farther and perform higher jumps. As of ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ramps are a common way for racers to perform [[trick]]s.

A specific type of ramp, [[Glider Ramp]]s, were introduced in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', with their main difference being that a character automatically takes out their [[glider]] upon driving over it.
