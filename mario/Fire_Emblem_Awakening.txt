'''Fire Emblem Awakening''' is one of [[5-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It both features a scenario and is titled after the [[Nintendo 3DS]] game of the [[w:c:fireemblem:Fire Emblem Awakening|same title]].

==Layout==
The microgame takes place during the [[w:c:fireemblem:Shepherds (chapter)|Shepherds]] chapter of ''Fire Emblem Awakening''. [[w:c:fireemblem:Victor (Awakening)|Victor]] can be seen on the map, despite not ever appearing in the chapter in the origin game.

==Gameplay==
The object is to move [[Chrom]] quickly enough to Victor and defeat him, as instructed by the "Defeat the enemy!" command. Sometimes, the microgame displays the "Wait!" command instead, in which case Victor does not appear, and the player has to select the identically named [[w:c:fireemblem:Wait|Wait]] command from the menu instead. Either way, if the player fulfills the objective, they win, but if not, they lose.

The second and third difficulty levels feature one and two of Chrom's allies, respectively, so the player has to be careful not to attack them.
