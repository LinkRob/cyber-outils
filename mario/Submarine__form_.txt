'''Submarine''' is one of the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] transformations featured in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. It made its first appearance in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', in which a Yoshi can temporarily transform into a Submarine by touching a [[Morph Bubble]] containing its picture. The Submarine makes a reappearance in both ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', the latter requiring a Yoshi to instead go through certain [[Whirly Gate]]s to transform into a Submarine.

Submarine is one of two Yoshi transformations to appear in every ''Yoshi's Island'' installment, the other being the [[Helicopter]].

==Description==
The Submarine can be used to traverse bodies of water, which Yoshis cannot traverse due to their inability to dive and swim underwater. Being in Submarine form allows a Yoshi to fire homing torpedoes at enemies. A Yoshi can be in Submarine form up until touching a [[Yoshi Block]] or reaching the [[Time Limit]], upon which the Yoshi is forced back to the location of the Morph Bubble and returns to his usual form. Like other transformations, if a Yoshi is injured by an enemy while in Submarine form, the player temporarily loses control of him for about one second.

==Trivia==
*In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', the artwork shows a [[Brown Yoshi]] in this form, despite him not having an opportunity to do so within the game itself. A similar instance of this exists with an artwork of a [[Cyan Yoshi]] in [[Car (form)|Car]] form, which he cannot do in the game itself.
