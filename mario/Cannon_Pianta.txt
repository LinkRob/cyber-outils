'''Cannon Pianta''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. He is a mechanic specialized in fixing machinery, [[Cannon (Super Mario Sunshine)|cannons]] in particular. The Cannon Pianta has dark purple skin and is covered in black stains, presumably from during when he was fixing the cannon.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Cannon Pianta is first encountered in [[Delfino Plaza]]. At first, he is fixing the Cannon leading to [[Pinna Park]]. If [[Mario]] talks to the Cannon Pianta, he predicts that it will take a while before the Cannon becomes available for Mario to use. The Cannon only becomes available by the time [[Shadow Mario]] takes Princess Peach to Pinna Park, after which the Cannon Pianta stands outside of it.
