'''Shuriken the Falcon''' is one of [[Kat and Ana]]'s companions within the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' series. He appears in both ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. Despite his name, Shuriken has the appearance of a {{w|bald eagle}}.

==In ''WarioWare: Twisted!''==
[[File:Shuriken Twisted.png|frame|left]]
In the ending of Kat and Ana's microgame set, Shuriken and [[Shadow the Dog|Shadow]] both help Kat and Ana defeat the [[Diamond Troll]]. Shuriken appears to have taken the role of Kat and Ana's other bird friend, [[Don the Sparrow]], who appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.
