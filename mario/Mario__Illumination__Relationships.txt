'''[[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]]''' is a friendly person. Despite being unfamiliar with his surroundings in the [[Mushroom Earth]], he did not hesitate to give a [[Coin]] back to a [[Toad]] who dropped it or greet the Toads. He cares a lot about [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]], as he gets annoyed when people talk lowly about him, and he does not take kindly to those who are rude to him. He is shown to be confident in himself, sometimes too much, to the point of thinking he can handle a challenge despite having no idea how to, resulting in him getting himself injured in the process. Ironically, he has a distaste for mushrooms, as shown by him picking them off his pasta in disgust and refusing to eat a [[Super Mushroom]].

=== [[Giuseppe]] ===
Mario and Giuseppe are in good terms. Guiseppe complimented Mario's accent when Mario questioned his own.

=== [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]] ===
[[File:Mario Movie Screenshot 21.jpg|thumb|right|Mario meets Princess Peach in her castle.]]
First meeting each other in [[Princess Peach's Castle]], Peach was amazed to see another human. Knowing he is new to the Mushroom World, she acts as a mentor and ally of Mario being supportive of him in his struggles.

Struggling to complete her obstacle course. Despite Mario nearly reaching the end after many attempts, Peach focused on his improvements and rewarded him. In the [[Jungle Kingdom]], Mario struggled to fight Donkey; Peach helped him by giving him a [[Super Bell]] to transform into [[Cat Mario]] helping him defeat Donkey Kong.

While Peach was forced in a wedding with Bowser, Mario came to her aid crashing the wedding.

=== [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]] ===
Mario met Toad in the [[Mushroom Forest]]. Informing Toad that his brother was in trouble, Toad was eager to lead him into the Mushroom Kingdom to inform Peach. He helped Mario become familiar with his surroundings.
