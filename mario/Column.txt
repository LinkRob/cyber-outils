A '''Column''' is an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
Like [[statue]]s, [[Wario]] can carry a column to use against enemies, and he can even perform one of his [[Mad Move]]s while carrying one. A column breaks after one attack. 

==In ''Wario World''==
Columns appear in various courses and during the boss fights against [[DinoMighty]] and [[Brawl Doll]]. Columns are also always present within [[Battle Ring (Wario World)|Battle Rings]].
