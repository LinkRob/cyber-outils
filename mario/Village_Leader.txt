The '''Village Leader''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is the leader of the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] tribe on [[Lavalava Island]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Village Leader is a large green Yoshi with a feather on his head, based on that of a {{w|tribal chief}}'s.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
The Village Leader is first seen at his home sitting down where he can be spoken with. Later on, the [[Yoshi Kid]]s of the [[Fearsome 5]] have gone missing and the Village Leader begs Mario to find and rescue them from the dangerous [[Jade Jungle|jungle]]. Mario manages to rescue all of them and a very pleased Village Leader gives him the [[Jade Raven]] which is needed to access [[Raphael the Raven]]'s home. The Village Leader is also seen again near the end of the game where he is attending [[Princess Peach]]'s party and during the parade where he, along with the other adult Yoshis, is sitting on the Lavalava Island float (with Raphael the Raven and the [[Lava Piranha]] being on it as well).
