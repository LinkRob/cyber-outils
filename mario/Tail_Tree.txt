The '''Tail Tree''' is a unique tree which grows [[Super Leaf|Super Leaves]] on its branches. It appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', in which it is located outside of [[Princess Peach's Castle]].

== History ==
[[Mario]] and the three Toads (Yellow, Blue and Red) are looking for [[Princess Peach]] when they discover that she and the Super Leaves are missing. They find a letter hovering near the Tail Tree so they decide to investigate. They find a picture of [[Bowser]] holding Princess Peach. Bowser is utilizing the leafs that he and [[Dry Bowser]] took from the Tail Tree to create [[False Bowser]]s and strengthen the [[Koopa Troop]]. At the end, Mario, the Toads and [[Princess Peach]] arrive back at the castle where the Tail Tree has sprouted new Super Leaves.
