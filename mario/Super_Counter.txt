'''Super Counter''' is a [[First Officer]] Command used by [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]] in ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

== Description ==
Super Counter costs 4 CP to use. If [[Bowser Jr.]] takes damage from a enemy, Iggy asks; "''Lord Junior! Who did this to you?!''" Bowser Jr. will say who hit him. Iggy threatens the enemies that he is going to pay for that. Iggy can land on top of 1-3 enemies. If Iggy says that he is still mad, his POW will increase. If the player uses this command and Bowser Jr. hasn't taken any damage, Bowser Jr. responds with "''Hey, I'm fine!''" However, there is no CP loss. Super Counter can also be used only by <span style="color:dodgerblue;">Flying</span>-type enemies (for example, [[Fawfulcopter]], [[Kaley]], [[Koopa Paratroopa Trio]]).
