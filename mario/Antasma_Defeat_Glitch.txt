The '''Antasma Defeat Glitch''' is a glitch that happens in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. This glitch happens after Mario has defeated [[Antasma]] for the second time. However, the present left at the end of the battle doesn't open and the screen freezes with Mario still in his battle position and [[Dreambert|Prince Dreambert]] still smiling.

==Solution==

The actual solution to insure this glitch doesn't happen is to use no [[Luiginary Attack]]s during the battle. Other players may not experience this problem, but it is still common for others.
