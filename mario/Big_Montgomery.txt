}}'''Big Montgomery''' is the first boss that appears in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] remake ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. He appears as the boss of the levels [[Big Montgomery's Fort]], [[Big Montgomery's Bubble Fort]], and [[Big Montgomery's Ice Fort]]. He is a [[Monty Mole]] who was enlarged by [[Kamek]] (on three separate occasions) who was commanded to defeat [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. Big Montgomery is heavily inspired by a war general, due to commanding Monty Moles to aid him during his second and third fight as well as gaining a war-like helmet in his later fights.
