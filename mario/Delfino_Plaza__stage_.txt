'''Delfino Plaza''' is a recurring stage in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Origin==
Delfino Plaza is based on the [[Delfino Plaza|location of the same name]] from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. Delfino Plaza is found on [[Isle Delfino]] and served as the main hubworld of that game. During the course of the adventure, Mario was forced to clean up the island for the [[Pianta]]s after [[Shadow Mario]]'s pollution caused all of the [[Shine Sprite]]s to disappear.
