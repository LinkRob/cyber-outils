'''Zap Tap''' is a [[badge]] which appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. While [[Mario]] is wearing Zap Tap, an electric shield surrounds him, damaging enemies who attack him directly. Mario can wear this badge for three [[Badge Point|BP]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Zap_tap_badge.png|thumb|left]]
Zap Tap is a badge only obtained from [[Merlow]] if Mario gives ten [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]] to him at [[Shooting Star Summit]]. The Zap Tap badge can be extremely useful in the game being able to deal a point of damage to all enemies that attack directly. Certain characters such as [[The Master]] who has combination attacks would have it interrupted thanks to the nature of the Zap Tap badge. This badge is also extremely effective against [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] who can only attack by draining Mario's HP and the Zap Tap badge will prevent them from even latching onto Mario. However, this badge does have it's flaws as it doesn't work against every enemy. Any enemies with a shell such as [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Buzzy Beetle]]s are immune to the effects of the Zap Tap badge. Enemies that wield weapons or attack with objects from a distance such as [[Clubba]]s and [[Monty Mole]]s are not affected by the Zap Tap's effects as well. [[Ruff Puff]]s are also immune to the Zap Tap badge being living thundercloud enemies.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Zap Tap can only be obtained while exploring the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. It the item in the [[treasure chest]] at the thirtieth level.
