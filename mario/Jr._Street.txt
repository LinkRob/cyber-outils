'''Jr. Street''' is a basketball court in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It is the second court played during the [[Star Tourney]]. It is [[Bowser Jr.]]'s home court.

==Description==
The main focus of this court is the giant slot machine in the back. To play the slots, teams must acquire [[Slot Coin]]s from the [[? Panel]]s on the court. Sometimes, one or more of the ? Panels will turn purple. Dribbling the ball over the panel while it is purple gives that team a Slot Coin. Up to three Slot Coins can be carried at a time and each one opens up new possibilities for lining up the pictures on the slot machine. The first Slot Coin allows for a match in the middle row only. The second opens up all three rows. Finally, the third Slot Coin opens up diagonals. 

If a team has any Slot Coins when they make a basket, the slots start spinning, awarding the following prizes if three of the following symbols appear in a row or diagonally. When this happens, a fanfare plays, along with some [[fireworks]].
*3 [[Green Shell]]s: +10 pts
*3 [[Mushroom]]s: +20 pts
*3 [[Fire Flower]]s: +30 pts
*3 [[Star]]s: Doubles score for basket
*3 [[Mario]]s: Triples score for basket
*3 [[Bowser Jr.]]s: Score for basket is reduced to 0.

Sometimes, two of the pictures match in two rows, always being the extra point pictures, not the multipliers. So, three Mushrooms and three Fire Flowers adds 50 points to the basket.

A similar court named [[Bowser Jr. Blvd.]] appears in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''. It and Jr. Street have the same Japanese name, suggesting that they are be the same location.
