'''Coffin Congestion''' is an Item [[minigame]] found in [[Horror Land]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. The name is a pun on "cough and congestion."

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a haunted dungeon with six coffins.

==Gameplay==
Before the minigame starts, the items in the coffins warp to other coffins until they stop. When the minigame starts, the player has to get the item they desire by [[Ground Pound|hip-dropping]] or [[punch]]ing the coffin that contains that item. If the player gets [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]], they get nothing. If the player loses by running out of time, Baby Bowser simply leaves.

The minigame can be purchased from [[Woody]] for 600 [[Coin]]s.
