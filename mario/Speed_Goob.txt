}}Speed Goob is a kind of [https://mario.fandom.com/wiki/Luigi%27s_Mansion_3#Rare rare ghost] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', it is a form of a [[Goob]]. Rare ghosts only appear in the [[ScareScraper]]'s ten floor mod, they won't appear in [[The Last Resort]]. The Speed Goobs have 250HP, more than normal double HP of a normal [[Goob]].

== Appearance ==
Speed Goob is one of the forms of a Goob, but speed Goob has red, glowing body instead of blue body. In the gallery, the Goob will be inside watching you and not moving, the Speed Goob is keep moving inside as if it is running. They will be sucked up in the same way as normal Goobs.

== Battle ==
Rare ghosts will appear a random kind when [[Luigi]] reaches the fifth floor of the ScareScraper in ten floors mod. When the Speed Goobs appear, they will move and attack faster than the normal Goobs, so Luigi has to flash and stun them quickly and suck them up.
