|music = ''Mario Party 3''[[File:Mario-Party-3-Music-Lets-Get-A-Move-On.ogg|Mario Party 3]]}}'''Water Whirled''' is a 4-player mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, four players must try to race around the course. During this mini-game, they can use the inner wall to take some shortcuts and get past other characters. It should be noted that turning in this mini-game is very complex and turning should start as soon they reach the curve. The player that does 5 laps and reaches the finish line first wins.

*{{button|n64|b}} - Brake
