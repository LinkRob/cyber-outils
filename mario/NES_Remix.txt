'''''NES Remix''''' is a [[Wii U]] game exclusively for the [[Nintendo eShop]], for which it released in December 2013. It is the first and titular installment of the ''[[NES Remix (series)|NES Remix]]'' series. In 2014, it received a follow-up title, ''[[NES Remix 2]]''. The same year, both ''NES Remix'' and ''NES Remix 2'' were released as a retail compilation, ''[[NES Remix Pack]]'', exclusively in North America and Japan.

The game consists of short challenges based on various [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games, including ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' and some ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' franchise titles such as ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. Some other ''Mario''-related games represented include ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]]'' and ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]''.
