'''Wendy O. Koopa (Dark)''' is the dark copy of [[Wendy O. Koopa]] who serves as an enemy and boss in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' and ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Dark Wendy is identical to the real Wendy, although her entire body, bow, necklace, rings and shoes have a darker shade applied to them.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Dark Wendy has all the abilities of the real Wendy. She fights as a Ranged-unit type trooper, and her [[Special Skill]] is Piercing Projectile. In ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'', she can also use her wand to create [[Doppelganger (Bowser Jr.'s Journey)|doppelgangers of herself]].
