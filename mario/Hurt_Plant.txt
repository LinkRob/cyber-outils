A '''Hurt Plant''' is an enemy found on [[Lavalava Island]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. They are carnivorous, venomous plants that disguise themselves as [[Heart Plant]]s.

==Profile==
Undisturbed, Hurt Plants look exactly like undisturbed [[Heart Plant]]s, having a long, yellow, curled-up stem with a blue tip and banding and a pair of green leaves at the base. When attacking, they unfurl, and their stems split to reveal a long mouth with sharp teeth and copious amounts of saliva.

==History==
Hurt Plants are only found throughout [[Jade Jungle]], disguising themselves as Heart Plants. When interacted with, they reveal themselves and give off a loud hiss, initiating a battle with [[Mario]]. Hurt Plants are always fought in pairs and never alongside other enemies.

In battle, Hurt Plants attack by diving into the dirt and popping out near Mario to bite him, much like a [[Piranha Plant]]. If Mario fails to [[Guard|block]] this attack, he has approximately an 85% chance of being [[Poison (status effect)|poisoned]]. Hurt Plants also do not release any [[heart]]s, unlike Heart Plants.
