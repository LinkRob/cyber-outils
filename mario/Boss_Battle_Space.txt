'''Boss Battle Space''' is a type of [[Space]] featured in a few of the {{series|Mario Party}} games. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

== Description==
[[File:Boss Battle Space.png|thumb|left|''Mario Party 9'' sprite]]
Upon landing on or by passing by a Boss Battle Space, the characters are forced to play a boss minigame, which differs for each board in Solo mode in ''Mario Party 9'' and ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. In ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'', Boss Battle Spaces appear in the [[Toad Scramble]] mode.
