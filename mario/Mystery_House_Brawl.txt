|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Mystery House Brawl''' is a level of World Mushroom in the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
It is available as soon as the player starts the world. It is similar to [[World 2 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 2]]'s [[Mystery House Melee]], as it features the player fighting various enemies to obtain [[Green Star]]s.
==Green Stars==
<tabber>
|-|Green Star 1 = In Section 1, defeat the three Chargin' Chucks and get the star within ten seconds.
|-|Green Star 2 = In Section 2, defeat the three Snow Pokeys and get the star within ten seconds.
|-|Green Star 3 = In Section 3, defeat the two Flopters and get the star within ten seconds.
|-|Green Star 4 = In Section 4, defeat the three Boomerang Bros. and get the star within ten seconds.
|-|Green Star 5 = In Section 5, defeat the hidden Coin Coffer and get the star within ten seconds.
|-|Green Star 6 = In Section 6, defeat the three Goomba Towers and get the star within ten seconds.
|-|Green Star 7 = In Section 7, defeat the three Fizzlits and get the star within ten seconds.
|-|Green Star 8 = In Section 8, defeat the three Magikoopas and get the star within ten seconds.
|-|Green Star 9 = In Section 9, defeat the three Piranha Creepers and get the star within ten seconds.
|-|Green Star 10 = In Section 10, defeat the Hammer Bros. and get the star within ten seconds.
</tabber>
