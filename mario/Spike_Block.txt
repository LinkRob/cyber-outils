'''Spike Blocks''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
Spike Blocks vary in color throughout their appearances, but are consistently depicted as cube-shaped structures covered in [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]].
