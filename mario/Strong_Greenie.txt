'''Strong Greenies''' are a type of [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|Ghost]] and an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Strong Greenies resemble their parent species, [[Greenie]]s, expect their body is a darker shade of green and has lime green cracks all over.

===Behavior===
Because of the crystals found in the [[Secret Mine]], Strong Greenies are stronger than Greenies. Strong Greenies behave similarly to Greenies, except they pull away harder when trying to be vacuumed into the [[Poltergust 5000]]. Strong Greenies have 50 HP, and their attack strength is higher. Even given this, they are the weakest of the Strong-based ghosts, and can be vacuumed up without much trouble.

== Special Variations ==

=== Chained Greenie ===
[[File:Chained Greenie.png|thumb|300x300px|Chained Greenie next to an open window]]
The Chained Greenie is one of the three ghosts sporting the paranormal chains. This version of Greenie acts as a sub-boss of the secret mines, appearing in mission C-3 "Across the Chasm," where he helps the [[Strong Gobber|Chained Gobber]] and [[Strong Slammer|Chained Slammer]] block the entrance to the gondola using his own green paranormal chains. In order to get rid of this, Luigi needs to find and capture the Chained Greenie. This Greenie has 100 HP as opposed to the normal strong greenies, who have 50 HP. Once captured, his green paranormal chains will disappear.

=== Possessed Armor ===
In mission E-1 "Doggone Key," two Strong Greenies will possess suits of armor. They attack by slamming a sledgehammer towards Luigi. In order to defeat them, Luigi must lure them onto a carpet, wait for them to attack, then as they're lifting the hammer pull the carpet from beneath them to trip them and cause the armor to shatter. The Strong Greenie will emerge from the wreckage, allowing Luigi to capture it.{{LMDMNav}}
