'''PictoChat''' is a stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', based on the [[Nintendo DS]] software application of the [[w:c:nintendo:PictoChat|same name]]. The stage has not made any reappearances in favor of receiving a successor in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' titled [[PictoChat 2]].
