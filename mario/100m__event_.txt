'''100m''' is an Olympic event in the [[Mario & Sonic (series)|''Mario & Sonic'' series]], specifically the summer installments, in which characters run 100m. The first appearance was the ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'', followed by ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'', [[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'', and ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''.
