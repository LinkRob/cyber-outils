'''Warpulator''' is a time travel device "recombobulated" from the downloaded flight data of the [[Shroob Mother Ship]] by [[Stuffwell]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It has the power to reverse the flow of time, and can send individuals to a predestined point, along with any, and all of their current inventory and statistics. It tends to only be used near the ending of the game, where the [[Mario]] [[Luigi|brothers]] end up trapped at a point of no return, and need to battle [[Princess Shroob]] so they can revert, and do any tasks they might have missed. It can also be used post-[[Elder Princess Shroob]] defeat, but at the cost of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] being taken over by [[Shroob]]s once more.
