'''Dark Iggy Koopa''' (or simply just '''Dark Iggy''') is the dark copy of [[Iggy Koopa]]. He appeared as a boss in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' and ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

== In ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' ==
After Iggy has been recruited, Dark Iggy Koopa will replace Iggy as a boss. He functions the same as regular Iggy.

== In ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' ==
Dark Iggy is fought in the final battle with the [[Best Fitness Friends]]. Dark Iggy is fought again in the post-game alongside [[Dark Ludwig von Koopa|Dark Ludwig]], with both under the effects of [[Malatone Formula:X]]. Dark Iggy has the same moveset as the real [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]].
