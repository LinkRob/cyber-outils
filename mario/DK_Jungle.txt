'''DK Jungle''' is the name of several locations owned by [[Donkey Kong]] in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] as well as one ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' game:

*[[DK Jungle (Mario Kart 7)|DK Jungle]], a race course in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', later returning in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.
*[[DK Jungle (Mario Kart Arcade GP)|DK Jungle]], a race course in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.
*[[DK Jungle (Mario Kart Arcade GP DX)|DK Jungle]], a race course in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.
*[[DK Jungle (baseball stadium)|DK Jungle]], a stadium in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.
*[[DK Jungle (Mario Tennis Open)|DK Jungle]], a court in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''.
*[[DK Jungle (golf course)|DK Jungle]], a golf course in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''.
*[[DK Jungle (Donkey Kong Barrel Blast)|DK Jungle]], a race course in ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]''.

For even more uses, see the [[Donkey Kong Jungle]] disambiguation page.
