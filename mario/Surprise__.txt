'''Surprise!!''' is the second [[level]] of [[P. 1-Beginning]] in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. The level takes place in a large, grassy area lined with many trees. As with [[Treasure Hunt (level)|Treasure Hunt]], there are many [[Shy Guy]]s lurking in the trees, as well as some [[Special Heart]]s found high in the sky. This level introduces [[Balloon Bully|Balloon Bullies]], who can help the [[Baby Yoshi]]s reach great heights, and [[Spider (Yoshi's Story)|spider]]s, who dangle from webs and attempt to hit the Yoshis. Exclusive to this level are [[Air Bag]]s, pink, balloon-like enemies that blow gusts of wind once released from their [[crate]]s, and [[Fuzzy Wiggler]]s, who, as the name implies, are green, furry [[Wiggler]]s who walk back and forth. The hidden [[Coin|Heart Coin]] letter for this stage is O.

==Layout==
The first half of the level is filled with many pipes, although they can not be entered. Three Fuzzy Wigglers appear in this part of the level, and a [[melon]] is given to the Baby Yoshis when they are defeated. Farther into the level is a hidden [[! Switch]] that can create several cloud platforms. The heroes can climb on these clouds to reach a Special Heart. As the Baby Yoshis make their way to the level's midway point, they will also encounter many of the level's spiders, hanging from large trees. They will have to maneuver around these enemies to make their way into another area of the level.

Several crates can also be found in this level, many of which are located after the midway point. Three of these crates contain Air Bags, who can blow the Baby Yoshis away. If they block the Baby Yoshis' path, they will have no choice but to defeat the foe with their eggs. [[Poochy]], the helpful dog from other games in the [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi'' franchise]], is also present after the midway point, and he can help the heroes find two [[Heart Coin]]s in the ground. Not too far between the very end of the level, more [[fruit]] can be found, as well as some moving platforms. Another Balloon Bully can also be found just beyond this area, helping the Baby Yoshis reach some Heart Coins. Shortly before the heroes reach the very end of the stage, they can find a family of [[Huffin Puffin]]s. The baby Huffin' Puffins can join the Yoshis on their adventure for the rest of the level and be used as boomerang-like projectiles that replace their eggs.

==Special Hearts==
*'''Special Heart 1''' - Located on the clouds above the first tree full of Shy Guys. The Baby Yoshi must pop the [[? Bubble]] and step on the [[? Switch]] that comes out before climbing the tiny clouds that appear.
*'''Special Heart 2''' - Hidden on the first yellow pipe found in the second forest with Spiders and past the pit containing a ? Bubble. The Baby Yoshi must [[Ground Pound]] the pipe to reveal it.
*'''Special Heart 3''' - Located in the sky close to the beginning of the second half of the level. The Baby Yoshi will need to pop the area's first ? Bubble, found next to an [[Egg Block]] to spawn a Balloon Bully, move it skyward and jump up to obtain it.

==Melons==
*'''1''' - Between the blue and yellow pipes is a Fuzzy Wiggler, which drops one melon upon defeat. In order to defeat it, the player needs to jump on all five of their segments.
*'''2''' - On top of the same yellow pipe is another melon floating above.
*'''3-4''' - To the right of the first Spider encountered in the level are two melons floating in [[bubble]]s that can be popped with eggs.
*'''5''' - At the first tree full of Shy Guys, the player needs to [[Ground Pound]] the spot both arrow signs are pointing at.
*'''6''' - Past the tree and at the ledge close to where the first [[Miss Warp]] rests is another Fuzzy Wiggler that can be defeated.
*'''7''' - Another tree full of Shy Guys that is right next to the first Miss Warp can be Ground Pounded to reveal a melon.
*'''8''' - In between two blue pipes on the ledge above is another Fuzzy Wiggler.
*'''9-15''' - The player needs to break open at least one of the stone blocks and pop the [[? Bubble]] to reveal a [[Vase]] that leads to the level's minigame, [[Special Delivery]]. Then, they need to stack and deliver all seven melon boxes to the finish flag.
*'''16-17''' - After entering the Vase to exit the minigame area, the player needs to walk to the left to find the final Fuzzy Wiggler of the level and a melon floating above its starting location.
*'''18''' - After entering the level's second half, the player needs to move the first [[Mystery Crate]] encountered and Ground Pound it.
*'''19''' - The player needs to throw an egg at the second Mystery Crate to reveal an Air Bag, then throw eggs at its bandage twice to defeat it and earn a melon.
*'''20''' - The player needs to Ground Pound the third Mystery Crate.
*'''21-22''' - The player needs to push the fifth Mystery Crate up on the high ledge to the left to make it drop down onto the fourth Mystery Crate. Both crates will turn into two melons.
*'''23''' - The player needs to defeat the Air Bag hidden in the sixth Mystery Crate found across the shallow pit with vertically-moving platforms.
*'''24''' - The player needs to Ground Pound the seventh Mystery Crate, located right next to the [[Power Flower]].
*'''25-26''' - The player needs to pop the ? Bubble close by to reveal a Balloon Bully and move upwards to find two [[Fly Guy|Flying Shy Guy]]s holding melons.
*'''27''' - The player needs to defeat the Air Bag hidden within the eighth Mystery Crate, found at the tree on the ledge, to earn a melon.
*'''28''' - The player needs to pop the ? Bubble above the [[Message Block]] and [[Catch the Coins|collect all five coins]] to earn a melon.
*'''29''' - The player needs to defeat the final Air bag hidden in the ninth and final Mystery Crate, found between the final Miss Warp and the Vase, to earn a melon.
*'''30''' - The player needs to pop the ? Bubble to the right of the Vase to reveal the final melon.
