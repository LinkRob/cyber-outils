The '''Penguin Racers''' are members of a competitive swim team in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Super Mario Galaxy (Nintendo Switch)|port]]. There are five Penguin Racers, only one of which is blue like the other [[penguin|small penguin]]s in the game. They live in the [[Sea Slide Galaxy]]. Each Penguin Racer wears a swim cap and goggles that rest above their eyes. The leader, referred to by [[Yellow Toad (Toad Brigade)|Yellow Toad]] as "'''Coach'''" in the original and ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]'' versions and "'''the captain'''" in the [[Super Mario Galaxy (Nintendo Switch)|2025 Switch version]], is a red penguin with yellow, feathery brows, making him look like a {{wp|rockhopper penguin}}. This character is not to be confused with the [[Coach (Super Mario Galaxy)|big penguin of the same name]] also found in ''Super Mario Galaxy''. Unlike him, this red penguin is referred to with a different name in the original Japanese release.

During the [[mission]] "[[Going after Guppy]]", the [[Guppy|eponymous whale]] monopolizes the waters of the Sea Slide Galaxy, preventing the Penguin Racers from swimming. In the subsequent "[[Faster Than a Speeding Penguin]]", [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) races these penguins. The fastest penguin is the red captain, followed by the green penguin, the purple penguin, and the blue penguin. Mario must hold onto a [[Red Shell]] and utilize [[underwater ring]]s to beat them. Upon being outswam, the captain affectionately refers to Mario as "champ" and awards him a [[Power Star]]. The Penguin Racers subsequently appear in "[[The Silver Stars of Sea Slide]]", helping Mario look for [[Silver Star]]s. The captain reappears in the [[Grand Finale Galaxy]] during "[[The Star Festival]]", near the lake with [[Penguru]] and other penguins. Outside of the mission that actively involves a race, the only red penguin that can be found in Sea Slide Galaxy lacks the yellow brows. [[List of Super Mario Galaxy pre-release and unused content|Unused data]] for a yellow Penguin Racer exists, but it is rendered as the blue one in gameplay.<ref>[https://noclip.website/#smg/OceanRingGalaxy;ShareData=AS,zk8{wa19YknhUdGF?=vNiaQvv0vUrDdRUHLF$V|acN9sVuUTIeG29C&a@Wqk noclip.website]</ref>

The Penguin Racers are the first of conceptually similar teams that appear in subsequent ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' games. In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', a group of multicolored [[Jibberjay Racers|Jibberjays]] periodically challenge Mario to a race. In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', the [[Roving Racers]] and [[Shiverian|Shiverian Racers]] serve similar roles.

The internal file name for the Penguin Racers is shared with the [[Big Penguin]] from ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.
