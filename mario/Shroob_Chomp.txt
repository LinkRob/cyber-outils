'''Shroob Chomps''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. They are only used and seen in an attack made by [[Elder Princess Shroob]]. It looks almost exactly like a generic [[Chain Chomp]], except with a purple coloration and red [[Shroob]] eyes. When used, Elder Princess Shroob lets go of the Shroob Chomp and sends it at [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]]. The Bros. have to [[jump]] repeatedly until a Bro. stomps on it. It then drops out of the screen and out of sight.
