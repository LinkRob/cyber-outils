'''Puffy the Dolphin''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
It is an anthropomorphic dolphin that wears shades and has a brown afro. 

===Behavior===
Puffy the Dolphins attack by sending balls of hair from their afro at [[Wario]].

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Puffy the Dolphins appear in [[Blowhole Castle]]. They can be defeated by any attack, but only upon being made visible after hitting a [[Spirit switch]].
