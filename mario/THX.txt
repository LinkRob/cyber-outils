'''THX''' is a suite of high fidelity audiovisual reproduction standards for movie theaters, screening rooms, home theaters, computer speakers, video game consoles, car audio systems, and video games. The THX trailer that precedes movies is based on the Deep Note, with a distinctive glissando up from a rumbling low pitch. It first debuted before the start of the third ''Star Wars'' film, ''Return of the Jedi'' in May 25th, 1983. So far, [[NBA Street V3]] is the only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related game to be certified by THX.
