Believe in Bowser is the eleventh level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 39. 

==Layout==

Each battle has multiple possible combinations of enemies.

The 1st battle A contains 3 [[Chargin' Chuck]]s, 2 [[Goomba]]s, 2 [[Fly Guy]]s, and 1 [[Fire Stalking Piranha Plant]]. The enemy captain is a Chargin' Chuck, and he knows [[Chance]] and [[Call for Backup]]. Call for Backup calls for a [[Para-Beetle]].

The 1st battle B contains 2 [[Goomba Tower]]s, 1 [[Bandit]], 3 [[Tail Boo]]s, and 2 [[Boo]]s. The enemy captain is a Goomba Tower, and they know [[Rally]] and Call for Backup. Call for Backup calls for a [[Lakitu]] or Para-Beetle.

The 1st battle C contains 4 [[Mechakoopa]]s, 2 Lakitu, 1 [[Fire Bro]], and 1 [[Big Tail Goomba]]. The enemy captain is the Big Tail Goomba, and he knows Rally.

The 2nd battle A contains 2 [[Koopa Troopa]]s, 4 [[Paragoomba]]s, 1 [[Ice Bro]], and 1 [[Blue Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Blue)]] The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows Rally, [[Parry]], and [[Deny]]. 

The 2nd battle B contains 1 [[Chain Chomp]], 2 [[Spike]]s, 2 [[Dry Bones]], and 3 [[Para-Beetle]]s. The enemy captain is a Para-Beetle, and he knows [[Chance]] and Call for Backup. Call for Backup calls for a Para-Beetle or Lakitu.

The 2nd battle C contains 1 Pokey, 2 Buzzy Beetles, 4 Koopa Paratroopas, and 1 Big Boo. The enemy captain is the Big Boo.

The final battle A contains 2 [[Broozer]]s, 3 [[Spear Guy]]s, 2 [[Hammer Bro]]s, and 1 [[White Magikoopa|Magikoopa (White)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows Deny and Call for Backup. Call for Backup calls for a Para-Beetle or [[Koopa Paratroopa]]. 

The final battle B contains 1 [[Spiny]], 2 [[Bomb Boo]]s, 4 [[Shy Guy]]s, and 1 [[Red Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Red)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows Rally and Deny.

The final battle C contains 1 [[Bob-omb]], 2 [[Koopa Paratroopa|Koopa Paratroopas (Red)]], 4 [[Boomerang Bro]], and 1 [[Green Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Green)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows Chance.
