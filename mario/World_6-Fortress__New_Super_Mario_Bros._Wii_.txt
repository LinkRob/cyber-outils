'''World 6-Fortress''' is a level found in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]].'' 

==Overview==
This tower contains two giant Spike Pillars on the floor and ceiling of the tower. They both collide in the middle of the fort. There are also some pipe cannons and moving platforms that the player will have to use to advance.
==Walkthrough==
This tower begins next to a [[Giant Spike Pillar]]s, and a [[? Block]] containing a power-up can be found next to it. The player then climbs up semi-solid platforms in the path of the Spike Pillar, where a second ? Block can be found. The player encounters two [[Dry Bones]], followed by a group of [[lift]]s moving horizontally. The lifts are followed by section containing [[White-Green Checkered Block]]s. The [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] follows, where the giant Spike Pillar collides with the second giant Spike Pillar coming from above. Another Dry Bones is found, along with another ? Block. A [[Red Ring]] is found, causing [[Red Ring]]s to appear throughout a section of semi-solid platforms. [[Pipe Cannon]]s are then used to reach platforms, two of which are moving. More horizontally-moving lifts are then encountered. After this section, the area with the boss door is reached, along with a ? Block. A [[Hidden Block]] is above the ? Block, allowing the player to reach a hidden [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to an area with a [[POW Block]] that can be used to collect a large group of [[coin]]s.

The player faces [[Morton Koopa Jr.]] at the very top in an arena with [[lava]] on either side. During the fight, Morton jumps and ground pounds (stunning any players not in the air when he lands). He also shoot pink magic projectiles from his wand (Morton's casting speed is slow but the projectiles are fast). Morton spins in his shell fast when hit. The attack can easily be avoided if players are on the raised ground on either side. However they must avoid [[Spike Pillar]]s that hit the ground. Morton is defeated once he is hit the third time.

==[[Star Coin]]s==
* '''Star Coin 1''': To the far left of the level's first Dry Bones is a hidden area inside the wall that contains the first Star Coin.
* '''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is right near the Checkpoint Flag and in between two walls. Mario and co. must walk kick to get it while the Spike Pillars are away.
* '''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is in between two close walls at the end of the level just before Morton's door. Mario and co. must wall kick these walls to get the Star Coin before the Spike Pillar launches.
