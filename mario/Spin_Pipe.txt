The '''Spin Pipe''' is a [[Special Attack]] that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. It is of the type [[Bros. Attack]], requiring 10 [[Special Point]]s to use.

== Description ==
When this attack is used, a giant [[Warp Pipe]] will appear on the screen and [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] will get inside it. After, the player will only see the insides of the pipe when the enemy is hit correctly, forcing him/her to remember in which sides they are. After the pipe rises over an enemy the player has to press 
{{System Buttons|ds|a}} if Mario's side is about to hit the enemy, while the player has to press {{System Buttons|ds|b}} if Luigi's side is about to hit the enemy. If the player presses the wrong button or takes too much time, the attack will deal small damage, plus stopping it. To unlock it, the player has to complete [[Kuzzle]]'s first puzzle, in which it will turn into the attack pieces required to unlock the attack.

Each stomp can potentially [[Dizzy|stun the target]]; if the target dies before the last stomp, the Bros. will pick a new target for the remaining stomps.
