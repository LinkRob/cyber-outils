The '''Pay-Dirt Piledriver''' is a machine that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. As it's name states, it is a massive piledriver that is owned by [[Void Kong]] of [[VoidCo]]. It's primary purpose is to help him drill throughout the [[Underground World]] in order to reach the [[Planet Core]].

== Description ==
The Pay-Dirt Piledriver resembles a massive meteor on the outside. It boasts purple circular imagery on its outer shell. It is unknown if [[Void Kong]] just used his All-Mine Remote to determine its behaviour or he modified it from the inside, as we never see its interiour. The Piledriver's bottom side has orange veins, likely since it has been drilling through that side, and Void Kong and his cronies stand on its top side.

==History==
The Pay-Dirt Piledriver is used by Void Kong throughout the game to help him drill through the planet. He accomplishes this by powering it using his [[All-Mine Remote]]. However, it must be fueled with [[Banandium Gem]]s in order to get it to work properly. At one point in the game, Void Kong ran out of bananas to help power his Pay-Dirt Piledriver and had to retreat to the [[Lagoon Layer]] and the [[Hilltop Layer]] in order to quickly gather more bananas. Even though [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Pauline]] attempted to stop him, Void Kong was able to gather enough bananas in order to restart his Pay-Dirt Piledriver and continue on with his goals. Thanks to this, Void Kong was successful in reaching the [[Planet Core]]. However, before could make his wish upon the [[Banandium Root]], Void Kong was attacked by [[King K. Rool]] who sent the greedy Kong flying into his [[Pay-Dirt Piledriver]] seemingly stopping him for good.

However, as revealed by both [[Poppy Kong]] and [[Grumpy Kong]], Void Kong survived the attack, but was stuck inside of his Pay-Dirt Piledriver with no means of escaping on his own.

==Trivia==
*It's name comes from the term "paydirt" which means to make a lot of money within a short amount of time, refencing Void Kong's goals. It also has a literal meaning as it is a giant machine made of dirt Void Kong uses in attempt to help him earn a lot of money.
