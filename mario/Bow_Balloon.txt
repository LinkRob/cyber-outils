A '''Bow Balloon''' (ボウバルーン, ''Bō Barūn'') is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. They are Vikings that are armed with bows. They will attack by firing an arrow if they spot [[Wario]], turning him in his puffy form.
