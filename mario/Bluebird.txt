'''Bluebirds''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are bodyguards for [[Valentina]] who attempted to take over [[Nimbus Land]]. Bluebirds are stronger versions of [[Birdy|Birdies]].

==Battle==
In battle, Bluebirds have much higher stats than the Birdies. They can also use strong spells such as [[Blizzard (move)|Blizzard]]. Bluebirds also commonly fight alongside other Bluebirds and enemies.

===Tips===
Bluebirds are weak to [[Fire (element)|fire]]-elemental moves, so [[Mario]]'s [[Super Flame]] or [[Ultra Flame]] works well against them.
