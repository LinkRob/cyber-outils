'''Drop-Road Dash''' is a level in [[Episode 1 (Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker)|Episode 1]] of ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

== Layout ==
[[Captain Toad]] begins on a blue platform followed by a [[Donut Lift|Donut Block]]. Then Captain Toad will pass a dash panel and will have to dash through the level while avoiding [[Para-Biddybud]]s and falling. Captain Toad can also go on colored platforms and pick up [[Turnip]]s to defeat the Para-Biddybuds.

==Super Gems==
*'''[[Super Gem]] 1''': The first super gem is on a Donut Block after the first blue-resting platform.
*'''Super Gem 2''': The second super gem is on a Donut Block after the first green-resting platform.
*'''Super Gem 3''': The third super gem is on an inaccessible blue platform. The player must throw a Turnip at it to collect it.
