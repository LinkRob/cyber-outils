The '''Fire Shirt''' is an armor item only usable by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It increases his [[Defense]] by 42 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 21. The Fire Shirt can be obtained by either purchasing it from [[Hinopio]] at [[Barrel Volcano]], at a price of 90 [[coin]]s, or from [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]].
