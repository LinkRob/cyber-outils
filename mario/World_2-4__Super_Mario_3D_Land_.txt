'''World 2-4''' is the fourth [[level]] of [[World 2-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2]] and the ninth overall level in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
[[File:W2-4 SM3DL start.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario at the start of the level]]
World 2-4 is made up of giant building block-like blocks. Throughout the level are [[Flip-Swap Platform|Red-Blue Panels]], which flip their side whenever [[Mario]] jumps. This flips every platform in the level. As Mario proceeds through the level, the more difficult the flip switch puzzles become. A few jack-in-the-box-like items launch out [[Goomba]]s at one point in the level. A [[Coin Heaven]] also makes an appearance in the level.
{{br|left}}
== Star Coins ==
*'''[[Star Coin]] 1:''' Getting to the area with multiple Baddie Boxes, the first Star Coin is above a Blue Tile platform. Be aware that an enemy box will throw a Goomba on that tile.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' After the first Star Coin, there is a [[Rainbow Note Block]] above a red tile when getting passed the small Spike Ball Bar. The Cloud level it grants the player is given in 3D, so it may be tricky.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' At the last portion of the Tiles, there is a flying ? Block with the third and last Star Coin. Make sure to be careful as if you miss the Flying ? Block, Mario/Luigi could fall into the abyss.
