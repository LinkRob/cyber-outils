'''King Boo's Light Smite''' is a [[Boss Battle]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. It features the characters having to fight [[King Boo]]. They can damage King Boo by shining a light on him using a [[Light Box]], avoiding falling off and touching a [[Peepa]] as they both deduct one point. Small Light Boxes  grant one point and big Light Boxes Granting two. This minigame is similar to ''[[Mario Party 10]]''{{'}}s [[King Boo's Tricky Tiles]], a boss minigame also featuring King Boo.
