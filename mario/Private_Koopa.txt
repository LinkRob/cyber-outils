==History==
Private Koopa is one of many members of the [[Koopa Troop]] that has been warped to [[Castle Bleck]] during the events of the game. He met up with the [[Hammer Bro Captain]] and walked around until they ran into [[Princess Peach]]. After some talking, the Hammer Bro Captain told Private Koopa to follow Princess Peach and try to find a way out. Unfortunately, they were soon discovered by [[Nastasia]] and Private Koopa was brainwashed into working for Count Bleck.
