The '''Boo's Boneyard Galaxy''' is a galaxy that can be visited in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', located outside the [[Gate]]. It is one of few galaxies to feature use of the [[Boo Mushroom]]. Due to being a [[Hungry Luma]] Galaxy, the Galaxy has no icon. Its unlock requires completion of the mission [[Ghostly Galaxy|Luigi and the Haunted Mansion]] and [[Gateway Galaxy|Gateway's Purple Coins]], ensuring that Mario has at least 47 [[Power Star|Power Stars]] in his possession by the time the [[Hungry Luma]] for this Galaxy appears.

== Missions==
===Racing the Spooky Speedster ===
[[Mario]] must race the [[Spooky Speedster]] as [[Boo Mario]] to get the [[Power Star]].

== Layout==
===Starting Planet (Skull Planet)===
This is the only planet with [[Gravity|Standard Gravity]] in this galaxy. It has some inaccessible bones floating near it and an orange [[Warp Pipe]] on the top.

===Bone Maze Planet===
This giant planet is a large 2D area that takes up more space than the screen, so it cannot be seen fully. The planet's size is unknown, but the interior contains a maze. It is where Mario will race the Spooky Speedster. The maze extends very far downwards, and the finish line is at the very bottom of it. On the way Mario can encounter some fans to push him down, rotating bone platforms, and some springy enemies called [[Banekiti]] which bounce Boo Mario around. There are lots of collectibles lying around such as [[Star Bit]]s, [[Coin]]s, and even [[1-Up Mushroom]]s.

==Trivia==
*Performing a spin on the eyes of the skull planet causes a few Star Bits to spawn.
*If Mario is close to the Spooky Speedster in the race, the maze twists and makes an eerie sound.
