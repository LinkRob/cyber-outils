'''Blue Key''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
The Blue Key is used in the [[Crystal Palace]]. It, as the name suggests, is used to unlock a blue door. This door is found in the very first room of the palace and the Blue Key is found around the first area of that crawling palace.
