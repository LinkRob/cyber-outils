'''Puzzle Hustle''' is a Toad's Rec Room [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. One to four players can play this minigame.

== Opening ==
The player chooses a stage. The camera pans from side view to bird's eye view. The puzzle pieces fall onto the board.

== Gameplay ==
The players' objective is to push or pull pieces together and set a new record.

== Ending ==
The players cheer after completing the stage. The sprite rises and animates.

== In-game description ==
''"Assemble the Mario sprites!"''

== Controls ==
* {{button|ns|stick}} - Move
* Hold {{button|ns|jc-right}} + {{button|ns|stick}} - Push/Pull
