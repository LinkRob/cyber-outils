'''Snack Basket''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

==Usage==
When the attack is commenced, Mario/Luigi will have the Snack Basket put in his hand. Mario/Luigi throws it in the air when A is pressed, and sweets shoot upward and offscreen. The player moves Luigi using the + controls to eat the sweets falling from above. After he eats a lot of the sweets he grows fat and jumps on Mario. The player must mash the A button to lift Luigi and throw him into the air. When Luigi lands, he creates a shockwave that hits all enemies in front of him on the ground.

==Story Uses==
The attack is required to defeat Chakram, who wants the Mario Bros. to move him from his "eternal position."
