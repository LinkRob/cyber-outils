The '''Skorcheen King''', also known as "'''Burnadette's Dad'''" by [[Snoutlet]] (everyone in the French dub), is an allied character from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. He is [[Princess Burnadette]]'s father (and later [[Prince Chilliam]]'s father-in-law) and the ruler of Skorcheen, a city-state on [[Skorcheen Island]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Like Princess Burnadette herself, the Skorcheen King is a rotund Concordian humanoid with orange skin, effectively looking like what Burnadette would look like if she had her sex changed.  He has a large yellow moustache that covers his mouth, and atop his head is a red flame. His attire is a brown one-piece with a yellow stripe that resembles a magma-filled crack around the waist.

=== Personality ===
The Skorcheen King has respect for his daughter, as shown upon his discovery that she has taken [[Prince Chilliam]] as her husband. His personality also seems to be jolly, with him declaring her to be "certainly a Skorcheen princess." Despite this, his relationship with his daughter does have its hiccups; during the side quest The Empty Nests, the Skorcheen King tells of her rebellious phase (which was actually him annoying her with his flatulence). The flashback showing the aformentioned event reveals that he also loves Heatfreeze Potatoes from [[Heatfreeze Island]].

== History ==
The Skorcheen King makes his debut when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] travel to [[Skorcheen Island]] in their search for a cure for [[Prince Chilliam]], who was [[Glohm|literally poisoned into turning against]] [[Princess Burnadette]] by [[Extension Corps|Ten]]. When he and the Bros. talk, the Skorcheen King learns that Burnadette has a relationship with Chilliam. Despite his people being at the throat of the Slippenglide people, the Skorcheen King approves of the relationship. He then tells Mario and Luigi how to reach [[Doctor Vulko]], who knows of a [[Ampberry|cure for Glohm]]. After Chilliam is cured of his infection, the Skorcheen and [[Slippenglide King|Slippenglide Kings]] attend their children's wedding on [[Heatfreeze Island]].

During the side quest The Empty Nests, Princess Burnadette sends Mario and Luigi to Skorcheen Island, as she worries that her father will be lonely following her marriage. The Bros. head back to his house, whereupon the Skorcheen King tells them about his daughter's rebellious phase. A flashback reveals that she was actually driven up the wall by the king's flatulence, caused by his favourite food, heatfreeze potatoes. The king remarks on how much Burnadette has grown since then before asking Mario and Luigi to ask her to pay him a visit sometimes. He also sends Mario and Luigi off to check on his [[Slippenglide Island]] counterpart, the [[Slippenglide King]], who (to him, anyway) seemed upset at the wedding.

When [[Reclusa]] awakens and [[Reclusa World|conquers]] [[Concordia]], it traps the Skorcheen King in one of its [[Reclusa Dream Flower|flowers]]. In his dream, the king fantasises about playing with Princess Burnadette, who is portrayed as younger. He is later freed when the Bros. finally kill Reclusa and save Concordia.

During the credits, the Skorcheen and Slippenglide Kings can be seen attending a performance by [[Groovemaster Dyode]] and [[Emmit]].
