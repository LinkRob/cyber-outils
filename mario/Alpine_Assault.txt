'''Alpine Assault''' is a 4-Player mini-game found in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera pans from the audience to the players.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to be the first player to snowbord the finish line. While snowboarding, players must avoid the obstacles. The players can snowboard on ramps to speed up their pace.

== In-game description ==
* '''Rules''': ''Snowboard down the slope while avoiding obstacles!''
* '''Hint''': ''Use jumps to avoid the obstacles.''
=== Controls ===
*'''Wii Remote''' - Move left/right
*'''Buttons''' - {{button|wii|A}} / {{button|wii|B}} – Rotate in midair
