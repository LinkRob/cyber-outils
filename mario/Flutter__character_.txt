'''Flutter''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. She is the game's representative character for the [[Flutter]] species, and shares an appearance with them.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Flutter resides on the third floor of the [[Mushroom Condo]]. She is in love with [[Mr. I (character)|Mr. I]], although [[Petal Guy (character)|Petal Guy]] has a crush on her. The result is that Flutter rejects Petal Guy in favor of Mr. I, only to be rejected by him.
