'''Giant Luma''' is a type of large [[Luma]] found in the [[Supermassive Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They are found in Starting Planet and Yellow Platforms Planet, and is very rare. Their voice appears to be a more louder version with a slight echo compared to that of a normal Luma. These Lumas act the same as regular Lumas and do not affect gameplay in any sort of way.
