'''Mini Mario''' is a form that [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] become upon obtaining a [[Mini Mushroom]], while in the form, the plumbers can jump higher, walk on water, and also fit through tiny [[Warp Pipe]]s. This form is just like [[Wario]]'s [[Tiny Wario|Tiny Form]]. If damage is taken when in this form, rather than reverting back to [[Small Mario|regular-sized Mario]], Mario instantly dies. Mini Mushrooms make their debut in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==History==
===''Mario Party 4''===
[[Image:Mini Mario Party.png|thumb|left|Mario after using the Mini Mushroom.]]
''[[Mario Party 4]]'' marks the introduction of the [[Mini Mushroom]], which enables players to transform into the Mini form, a new power-up form. While in the mini form, players can only roll a 1-5. Another item, the [[Super Mini Mushroom]], can also transform players into their mini form and allows them to roll two [[1-5 Dice Block]]s. In addition, miniaturized players can pass through [[Gate (Mario Party 4)|Gates]] and play [[Mini Mini-Games]], in that sense, the form can be have it's uses that help getting the [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] easier.
{{br}}
===''Mario Pinball Land''===
[[Image:Mini Pinball Mario.png|thumb|right|Mini Pinball Mario.]]
The Mini Mario form reappears in ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]'', in this game, [[Mario]] can become Mini Mario upon using a [[Mini Mushroom]] which can be purchased for 10 [[Coin]]s, while in the form, [[Mario]] can enter beehives and igloos (that are too small for him to enter while in normal form) to gain more [[Coin]]s and [[Power Star]]s.
{{br}}
===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
[[Image:Mini Mario Run.png|thumb|left|Mini Mario runs across the water.]]
Mini Mario returns in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', in this game, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can assume the Mini form upon obtaining a [[Mini Mushroom]], while in the form, the plumbers can run faster, jump higher and farther, descend slower, run across water (they can dunk below water if they stand on it for a few seconds), stand on [[Donut Lift]]s without them falling, and enter small holes to reach secret areas as well as entering [[Mini Pipe]]s which are often times required for finding [[Star Coin]]s and [[Red Flagpole|secret exits]]. The Mini form is also required to unlock [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 4]] and [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]] respectively, to unlock these two worlds, [[Mummipokey]] and [[Petey Piranha]] respectively must be beaten while in the mini form, beating them this way will trigger a cutscene where the mini plumber will fall through a tiny hole in the ground and flip the sign to point to the alternate world.

While the Mini form does have it's upsides, there are also a few downsides as well, to start off, [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] can no longer [[Crouch|duck]] while in this form, additionally most enemies are immune to regular stomp attacks and can only be killed or damaged with [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounds]], this also applies to bosses as well. Enemies that can only be killed with Ground Pounds are completely immune to all of Mini Mario's attacks, one such example is the [[Super Dry Bones]] which merely shakes it's head upon ground pounding (something that normally happens when it's stomped on), the [[Micro Goomba]] is the only enemy that can be killed with a stomp while in the Mini form, additionally, the [[Mega Goomba]] boss needs to be ground pounded nine times rather than three while in the mini form, also, Mini Mario is critically vulnerable so if he takes even one hit from a foe, Mini Mario is done for.
{{br}}
===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)===
[[Image:Mini Mario Skate.png|thumb|right|Mini Mario Skating.]]
The [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' features [[Mini Mushroom]]s in [[Polastraits]], [[Icepeak]], and [[Blizland]]. When [[Mario]] transforms into Mini Mario, he gains the ability to traverse thin ice and narrow spaces. However, it is worth noting that while Mario is able to become Mini Mario, [[Sonic]] cannot.
{{br}}
===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[Image:Mini Mario Dash.png|thumb|left|Mini Mario dashes across the waters.]]
Mini Mario reappears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', in this game, [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Yellow Toad]], and [[Blue Toad]] can assume the form upon obtaining a [[Mini Mushroom]]. Just as before, the heroes can run faster, jump higher and farther, descend slower, run across water while in the form in addition to being able to stand on [[Donut Lift]]s without them falling. The heroes can also fit through holes and enter [[Mini Pipe]]s as well, additionally, they can only kill enemies with [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounds]] and a single hit is enough to do him in. The Mini form is often times required for getting certain [[Star Coin]]s and [[Red Flagpole|secret exits]]. Unlike before, the heroes can [[Crouch|duck]] while in this form, something that would be carried to later games in the series.
{{br}}
===''Mario Sports Mix''===
[[Image:Mario Mario Sports.png|thumb|right|Mini Mario on the Sports Court.]]
In ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'', [[Mini Mushroom]]s reappear as items that can be tossed at other players to turn them mini. While mini, the affected player cannot carry a basketball, hockey puck, or dodgeball. They also have the inability to spike in volleyball, or perform a defensive maneuver in dodgeball. This effectively makes the form as more of a disadvantage than an advantage.
{{br}}
===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[Image:Mini Mario Island.png|thumb|left|Mini Mario's Course.]]
Mini Mario is back in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', in this game, the form is once again attained by obtaining a [[Mini Mushroom]], once again, [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] gains the ability to run faster, jump higher and farther, descend slower, run across water while in the form in addition to being able to stand on [[Donut Lift]]s and [[Icy Donut Lift]]s without having them fall. [[World 3-B (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3-B]] also requires the use of Mini Mario and his abilities to progress through the level, just as before, typical enemies can't be beaten with stomps while in the Mini form and must be ground pounded instead and Mini Mario/Luigi can be killed by a single hit. Just as before, the plumbers can enter [[Mini Pipe]]s and run through small holes while in the form which often allows them to obtain hidden [[Star Coin]]s as well as finding [[Red Flagpole|secret exits]].
{{br}}
===''New Super Mario Bros. U / New Super Luigi U / New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[Image:Mini Mario Runs Up Walls.png|thumb|right|Mini Mario running up a wall...]]
The Mini Mario form also appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', just as before, [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Yellow Toad]], [[Blue Toad]], [[Toadette]], and the [[Mii]]s (but not [[Nabbit]]) can run faster, jump higher and farther, descend slower, run across water while in the form in addition to being able to pass through small holes and enter [[Mini Pipe]]s (where [[Star Coin]]s and [[Red Flagpole|secret exits]] can be found). In addition to all these abilities, the heroes are granted a new ability which is that they can now run up and down walls as well (this even applies to liquified walls such as [[Waterspout]]s). Unlike earlier games however, [[Donut Lift]]s can still descend if the heroes stand on one while in the mini form and just as before, typical enemies can only be killed by ground pounds and a single hit is enough to do the heroes in while Mini.
{{br}}
===''Super Mario Maker / Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS / Super Mario Maker 2''===
Although Mini Mario doesn't physically appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', a smaller version of [[Weird Mario]] does make a brief appearance when a [[Door]] is tapped on during the level editor, when tapped, the [[Door]] will open revealing a mini [[Weird Mario]].
{{br}}

==Trivia==
*[[Poison Mushroom]]s also have the ability to shrink the plumbers in certain games; however this isn't technically considered the Mini Mario form as they don't gain the advantages of the [[Mini Mushroom]], not to mention that they are downgraded in terms of capabilities, this is comparable to the [[Tiny (ailment)|Tiny]] aliment in the RPG's.
*Mini Mario briefly appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' when Mario eats a [[Weeglee Candy]].
*In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', [[Dottie]] could shrink [[Mario]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Luigi]], and [[Bowser]], giving them access to tiny pipes and so forth in a similar fashion to Mini Mario.
*In ''[[Mario Party DS]]'', all the playable characters have been shrunk by [[Bowser]]'s magic however despite this, they aren't considered in the same form as Mini Mario for that game.

==Appereances in other media==
==='''The Super Mario Bros. Movie===
Mini Mario makes an appearance in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' as one of many forms Mario takes on. Mini Mario is only seen during Mario's fight with [[Donkey Kong (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Donkey Kong]] after he unknowingly eats a Mini Mushroom. After being transformed into Mini Mario, Mario stood no chance fighting off against the much more bigger Donkey Kong who was able to easily thrash him around. Fortunately for Mario, he takes enough damage to revert back to his regular form when Donkey Kong throws him against a wall. Mario was eventually able to use the [[Cat Bell]] to transform into [[Cat Mario]] in order to defeat Donkey Kong. At the end of the film, after Mario and [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]] defeats Bowser, [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Peach]] feeds him with the Mini Mushroom to shrink him and [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]] imprison him inside the jar and later birdcage inside of Peach’s Castle as punishment for all his crimes.
