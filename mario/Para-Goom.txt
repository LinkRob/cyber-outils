'''Para-Gooms''' are common enemies which appear throughout ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. They are round, yellow creatures that carry a spiked parasol with them. In ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]'', there is only one Para-Goom, and it is a guide explaining the different rules and modes.
