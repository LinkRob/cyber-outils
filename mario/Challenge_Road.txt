'''Challenge Road''' is a one-player mode in ''Super Mario Party'', where the player plays different [[minigame]]s and tries to complete different challenges. The mode is based of the [[Mini-Game Island]] from ''[[Mario Party]]'' and [[Minigame Island]] from ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''. The mode is available after all minigames are unlocked. You will meet many friends along the way asking if you want to take a break.
