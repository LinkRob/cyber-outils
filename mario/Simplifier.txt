'''Simplifier''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear the Simplifier for one [[Badge Point|BP]]. In doing so, Mario's action commands are performed easier. In addition, Mario receives less [[Star Power]] upon dealing attacks.

Mario can find this badge on sale at the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] in [[Rogueport]].
