'''Tease '''is an attack used by [[Ms. Mowz]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Three [[Flower Point]]s are required to use the attack. Ms. Mowz must be upgraded to the [[Super Rank]] to use this move.

==Effects==
To use Tease, the player must hold {{button|gcn|a}} to fill up the gauge. Once it's filled, Ms. Mowz, with some clones, will circle around an enemy attempting to inflict the [[Dizzy]] status on it.
