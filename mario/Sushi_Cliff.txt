'''Sushi Cliff''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' found in the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]].

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
Sushi Cliff is located at the [[Seaside Area]]. [[Sushi (character)|Sushi]] lives in Sushi Cliff. The player can choose to complete the [[Sploosh!]] quest after being informed about someone falling off the cliff.
