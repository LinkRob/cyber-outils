'''''Donkey Konga 3 JP'''''<ref>Named provided by a ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]]</ref>, titled {{nihongo|'''''Donkey Konga 3: Tabehōdai! Haru Mogitate 50 Kyoku'''''|ドンキーコンガ3 食べ放題！春もぎたて50曲|Donkey Konga 3: All-You-Can-Eat! Springtime Freshly-Picked 50 Songs}} in Japanese, is a [[Nintendo GameCube]] rhythm game released in 2005, exclusively within Japan. It is an indirect sequel to ''[[Donkey Konga 2]]'', and both the third and final installment of the [[Donkey Konga (series)|''Donkey Konga'' series]].

Like the first two ''Donkey Konga'' titles and ''[[Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]'', ''Donkey Konga 3 JP'' provides the option of playing using either the [[DK Bongos]] or a [[Nintendo GameCube Controller]].
