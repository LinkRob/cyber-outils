'''Rainbow Run''' is the first [[level]] of [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The main features of the Rainbow Run is running across rainbow-colored platforms. It may be based on [[Rainbow Road]], a recurring course in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. In the background of this level, the music playing happens to be an arrangement of the [[Ground Theme]].

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1:''' The first [[Green Star]] can be found by going through a Warp Pipe near spinning rainbow platforms. The player must use the [[Super Bell]] in order to get up there. After getting through the Warp Pipe, the player must navigate the two green balls to their switches to get the Green Star.
*'''Green Star 2:''' A [[Fire Flower]] is needed to get this next Green Star. After riding [[Plessie]], there is an area with two unlit torches. After the player lights them using the [[Fire Mario|fire form]], a Cloud Cannon appears and will shoot them to the next area. The player must then run across all of the platforms, fully revealing that the platform resembles [[Link]]'s sprite from ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'' as well as causing the Green Star to appear.
*'''Green Star 3:''' After the Checkpoint Flag, the player must hop through the Green Star Ring and collect all eight of the Green Star Coins to make the Green Star appear.
