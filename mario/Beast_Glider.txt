The '''Beast Glider''' ('''Ghastly Glider''' in Europe and Australia) is an unlockable [[glide]]r in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. All of its statistics are set to zero.

The glider can be unlocked by getting up to 5,000 [[coin]]s. However, if players use the [[w:c:nintendo:StreetPass|StreetPass]] feature to get [[kart]] parts, the amount of coins required to get the Beast Glider will lower. This glider is often the last non-golden kart part to be unlocked.
