The '''Mini-Game House''' is a location that appears in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Description==
The Mini-Game House is where [[minigame]]s can be played in either single-player or multiplayer. It is inhabited by [[Puff]]. After purchasing the [[Mecha Fly Guy]] from the [[Mushroom Shop]], the player can use it in the Mini-Game House.

The Mini-Game House has a separate mode, Pot O'Skills, where characters play for [[Coin]]s in minigames as they go around the track of [[Mini-Game Stadium]].
