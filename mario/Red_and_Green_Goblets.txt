The '''Red Goblet''' and '''Green Goblet''' are items featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are key items required by [[Cork and Cask]] during the training.

== History ==
Cork and Cask required [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to get the goblets to prove their skills. Luigi must [[hammer]] Mario to turn him into [[Mini-Mario]] to get the Red Goblet. Mario must hammer Luigi to perform the [[Luigi Dunk]] to get the Green Goblet. The Chuckola Bros. were impressed with their skills and allowed them to continue their journey.
