'''Monita''' is the host of ''[[Nintendo Land]]''.

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
Monita has a blue lit monitor face with a single mechanical arm attached underneath it. She has a single hand with four fingers, which is intentional so she can count down from three with her three main fingers. She wears a round white hat with a red strap around it, and a red bow tie. Her eyes are oval shaped and her mouth is a circle. She never changes this expression.

=== Personality ===
Monita is very monotone. She always wears the same expression; however she does appear to be somewhat playful despite these factors. She'll cosplay and act the role of a princess in [[Takamaru's Ninja Castle]], or as her alter ego Dark Monita in [[Pikman Adventure]] and [[Metroid Blast]]. She even makes jokes when describing a certain object or enemy. 

==Appearances==
===''Nintendo Land''===
[[File:Dark Monita.png|left|thumb|Dark Monita in her U.F.O.]]
Her role in [[Nintendo Land]] is as the host of the park. She will guide the player through the game and its various attractions. She can also appear in certain attractions to help the player by giving them power ups, or advice on how to use items. She can also get in the way of the player's progress by summoning enemies or acting as an obstacle.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
[[File:Monita's Spirit.png|thumb|Monita's Spirit in Smash Bros. Ultimate]]
Monita appears as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]] in [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]. The character used to represent her in her spirit battle is R.O.B. in his light blue alt. The fight takes place on [[Pokémon Stadium]] and the condition is that all players take serious damage. Once R.O.B. is defeated, the Monita spirit will be unlocked. Monita is an advanced (two star) spirit with screen flip immunity and costs two slots when used.
