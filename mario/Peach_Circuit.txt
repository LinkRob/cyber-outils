'''Peach Circuit''', (called ''ピーチサーキット'' '''Pīchi Sākitto''' in Japan), is the first course of ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''.  Peach Circuit reappears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' as the third race of the [[Shell Cup]] and in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' starting with the [[Princess Tour (2023)|Princess Tour]].

== In {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
===''Mario Kart: Super Circuit''===
[[File:Peach Circuit - Second Lap (Yoshi).png|thumb|left]]
Peach Circuit appears in this game being the first course in the [[Mushroom Cup]] and the first course overall. This course, as its name suggests, belongs to [[Princess Peach]] in this game. It is short and easy, having practically no obstacles. There are two sets of item boxes and many coins all over the track. There are six item boxes and fifty coins total. There are also many areas for players to drift on.

=== ''Mario Kart XXL'' ===
[[File:MKXXL.png|thumb|left|A track resembling Peach Circuit in ''[[Mario Kart XXL]].'']]
While not the actual track, a floating track that resembles Peach Circuit appears in the unreleased [[Game Boy Advance]] tech demo [[Mario Kart XXL|''Mario Kart XXL'']]. It has almost the exact same layout as Peach Circuit with a layer below the track depicting several beach islands in an ocean resembling [[Shy Guy Beach]]. Transparent windows showing the lower background layer are present near the beginning and closing sections of the track. The placement of the [[Coin|coins]] is the exact same on Peach Circuit. However, due to the game being unfinished, the player can pass through walls, off-road does not slow the kart down, and the coins on the track cannot be collected. [[Mario]] can fall out of bounds and into the sea below the track.

This Peach Circuit-like track is the only track present in the demo and is possibly used as a placeholder.

===''Mario Kart DS''===
[[File:MKDS Peach Circuit.png|thumb|Peach Circuit from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.]]
Peach Circuit reappeared in ''[[Mario Kart DS]],'' where it was ported from ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' as the third course in the [[Shell Cup]] with updated 3D graphics rather than the 2D graphics from the original. On Mission 1-2, the mission takes place in this area. The playable character is [[Princess Peach]], who must collect fifteen coins in a certain section of the track in within thirty seconds. As an aesthetic detail, the castle is flipped for the first three engine classes.

As with the other courses from ''Mario Kart: Super Circuit'' and with all the courses from ''[[Super Mario Kart]]'' that appeared in this game, there is no starting banner.

====Staff Ghost====
When playing this course, the player must win against the [[Staff Ghost]] Ninten★だい8, with his time being 1:12.011 with [[Princess Peach]] in the [[Royale]].

=== ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
Peach Circuit, along with [[Daisy Circuit]], debuted in the [[Princess Tour (2023)|Princess Tour]]. Like every track, Peach Circuit has three variants other than its normal variant: Peach Circuit R, Peach Circuit T, and Peach Circuit R/T. Unlike in ''Mario Kart DS'', it now has a goal gate at the [[Finish line|starting line]] consisting of two towers with a sign that reads MARIO KART above the line itself. There is an ocean at the southern part of the course.
