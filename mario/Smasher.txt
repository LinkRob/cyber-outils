The '''Smasher''' is a large [[Rabbid]] enemy in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

==In ''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle''==
Smashers have poor stamina. The Smasher will charge towards their attacker if the attacker hits them with a weapon. If the attacker is far from the Smasher, they will stop charging.
