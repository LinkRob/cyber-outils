'''Hatopop''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Hatopop are comparable to birds within {{w|cuckoo clock}}s. Hatopop have a green body and yellow wooden crisscrossed Ultra Hand-like bars behind it.

===Behavior===
Hatopop are hidden in certain wall indents with no immediate indication of their hidden presence. If [[Mario]] goes near a Hatopop, it immediately charges at Mario, knocking him away. After a while, a Hatopop retreats into the wall and disappears.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Hatopop are only encountered in [[Noki Bay]]. Here, they are found alongside [[Puncher]]s, which act the same but take on the appearance of a boxing glove. The only way the player can tell a Hatopop is hidden within a wall is by adjusting the camera angle so that it clips into the wall, revealing a question mark that represents the Hatopop. The only way for Mario to not be hit by a Hatopop is by avoiding where they pop out of.
