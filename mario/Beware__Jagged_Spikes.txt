"'''Beware: Jagged Spikes'''" is a level of [[Mario Zone]] in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]].

==Layout==
The level opens with six {{lw|? Block}}s. All of the ? blocks are empty except for the last one, which contains a [[Jack-in-the-Box (enemy)|Jack-in-the-Box]]. Afterwards, there is a wall of chains with balls [[Mario]] must ride. Underneath is a floor of [[spike (hazard)|spikes]]. After this section, there is a long [[Conveyor Belt]] where Mario must avoid spikes and enemies. The [[Mid-Point Bell]] is located in this section above a [[Bill Blaster]]. Finally, is a section of cranes, spikes, bullet bills, and chains.
