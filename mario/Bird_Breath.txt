'''Bird Breath''' is a downloadable [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It was first made available in Japan on October 21, 2009, followed by North America on September 20, 2010, and lastly Europe on October 22, 2010.

==Gameplay==
A ninja with a snorkel is trying to breathe while underwater, but there are birds attempting to sit on the snorkel, which would prevent the ninja from being able to breathe. The player must tap the birds to repel them from the snorkel, and if done successfully, the player wins at the microgame.
