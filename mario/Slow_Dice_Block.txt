The '''Slow Dice Block''' is a type of [[Dice Block]] that appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

== Description ==
It is a [[Dice Block]] with the numbers like the normal Dice Block. It has different condition: slow timing making it easier to choose a number. This Dice Block is obtainable inside the [[Toad House]]. The player has a chance of getting the Dice Block on a Spin Space. The player with the lowest amount of [[Mini Star]]s will receive the Slow Dice Block by the Green Toad at ''"Almost There!"'' events.
