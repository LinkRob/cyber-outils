'''Sweet Smiles''' (or '''Sweet Smiles Café''') is a restaurant run by [[Saffron]]. It is located at [[Flipside]] at the first floor.

== History ==
After unconscious [[Princess Peach]] "fell out of the sky". Merlon requires Mario to get  a Fire Burst and go to Saffron to help create [[Spicy Soup]]. Mario got the [[Fire Burst]] and came to the Sweet Smiles, Saffron gave him the Spicy Soup and Mario went up to the [[Flipside Tower]] to wake up Peach. All the time, Sweet Smiles was available for Mario to come to Saffron to cook with one ingredient.{{SPMNav}}
