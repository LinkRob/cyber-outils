'''Kamikaze Koopas''' are enemies which only appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==In ''Super Mario World''==
Kamikaze Koopas in ''Super Mario World'' are created when a Yellow [[Beach Koopa]] enters a [[Koopa Shell|shell]] of any color. Kamikaze Koopas are almost invincible and can't be defeated by stomping. The shell also follows [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] in an attempt to hit them. If [[Yoshi]] eats a Kamikaze Koopa's shell, he will gain all three abilities from [[Red Shell]]s, [[Blue Shell]]s and [[Yellow Shell]]s, as long as Yoshi keeps the shell in his mouth. To defeat Kamikaze Koopas, the player must eat the shell with Yoshi, [[Spin Jump]] on the shell, or make contact with the shell using a [[Starman]] power-up.
