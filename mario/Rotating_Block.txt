'''Rotating Blocks''', also known as '''Spinning Blocks''', are yellow [[block]]s that first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. When a Rotating Block is hit from below or the sides, it spins, allowing [[Mario]] to pass through it.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario World''===
[[File:Rotating Block SMW sprite.png|frame|left]]
Rotating Blocks are somewhat of a replacement for [[Brick Block]]s from previous ''Super Mario'' games. Normal Rotating Blocks can spin for a short amount of time when hit from below or attacked with a [[Cape Feather]]. However, some Rotating Blocks act like [[? Block]]s and contain a [[power-up]]. Mario or [[Luigi]] can break a Rotating Block by [[Spin Jump]]ing on it while in their [[Super Mario (form)|Super]] form, and breaking a Rotating Block is worth 50 [[point]]s. [[Chargin' Chuck]]s can also break through Rotating Blocks. Some enemies, such as [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Galoomba]]s, hide in Rotating Blocks and come out when the player gets near the Rotating Blocks they are hiding in.

===''[[Super Mario Maker]]/[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
Rotating Blocks reappear in ''Super Mario Maker'' and ''Super Mario Maker 2''. Rotating Blocks can be found in the ''Super Mario World'' mode, in which they replace Brick Blocks from other modes. Rotating Blocks function the same as in ''Super Mario World''.
