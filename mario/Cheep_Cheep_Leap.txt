'''Cheep Cheep Leap''' is a Free-for-All minigame found in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Gameplay==
Players will be situated on a bridge in [[Sparkling Waters]], and they will be jumping on a [[Starman]] that gives them invincibility. This will prevent players from harming each other. The objective here is to defeat as many [[Cheep Cheep]]s as possible within the 30 second time limit. These fish come in large groups, and the player will need to jump to reach them. There are three different types of Cheep Cheeps here. Normal ones are worth one point, [[Mega Cheep Cheep]]s are worth two points, and [[Gold Cheep Cheep]]s are worth three points. Once the time limit expires, the players lose their invincibility and the game ends. The winner is the player with the most points.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Pad}} – Move
*{{button|wii|2}} – Jump
