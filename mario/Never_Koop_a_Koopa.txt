"'''Never Koop a Koopa'''" is the sixth episode of ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

== Plot ==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]], and [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] are eating [[spaghetti]] when [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]], along with his [[Koopalings]], come to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] to tell them that they are calling it quits. He then acts very remorseful, hands the princess the key to [[Bowser's Castle|his castle]], and leaves the Mushroom Kingdom with his Koopalings. Peach then declares that she can turn the castle into a retirement home, so Mario goes with her to King Koopa's Castle.

When they arrive, they see that enemies still infest the place, and they are kidnapped by King Koopa. They learn that they were tricked all along, and King Koopa uses this as an example of how to cheat people so that [[Larry Koopa|Larry]] may learn how to cheat people one day. King Koopa them infests the cell with [[Bob-omb]]s unless the Princess signs the Kingdom over to him. Luigi and Toad then arrive at his castle, only to be kidnapped too. The Princess is brought out of the cell to sign the scroll. Larry then orders the Sledge Bros. nearby to lock the rest in his wardrobe in his room (King Koopa is not in the room at the time) and to take the Princess to his room. He then asks the Princess for the kingdom, and she accepts. She then busts the Mario Bros. and Toad out of the wardrobe, and look for Super Leaves.

In the throne room, King Koopa is ready to accept the kingdom, only to know that Larry got it first. King Koopa then looks at the scroll, and tells Larry that they were both cheated (Peach actually drew a [[clown]] on the scroll), and they see the group fly away. Koopa angrily chases Larry across his throne.

==Trivia/Goofs==
* At the beginning, the Koopalings that come with King Koopa keep changing. First, [[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]], [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]], and [[Roy Koopa|Roy]] come with him. Next, [[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]] joins the group when King Koopa declares that they are leaving the Mushroom Kingdom. And finally, only Wendy, Iggy, and Lemmy jump into the warp pipe at the end of King Koopa being remorseful.
* This is the only episode in the Mario cartoon series to show Princess Toadstool eating something (spaghetti).
* When Larry and Peach are in the room talking over the kingdom, the wardrobe that Mario, Luigi and Toad are locked in keeps appearing and disappearing.
* Larry's head keeps changing from orange to green a few times in the episode.
* Luigi is swimming with Toad hanging on to his leg. When Toad sees a Boss Bass, Luigi's pants are red.
* When Luigi and Toad get to the pipe, there is a lid on the entrance. When Luigi starts working on the pipe, the lid is not there. When Luigi gets the pipe open, the lid appears out of nowhere.
* When Mario and Peach are attacked by a Thwomp, the portraits of the Koopa family in the background lose a lot of detail. In fact, they look like completely different portraits.
* Some time after King Koopa loses the Mushroom Kingdom crown, when you see a scene where he is dashing behind his throne, he is wearing his original Koopa crown. He isn't wearing any crown at all in the next scene.
* In the beginning, when King Koopa says he's leaving the Mushroom Kingdom forever, Wendy's entire face is brown!
* When Luigi and Toad are trying to save Mario and Peach, they see a Sledge Brother. The front of his shell changes to orange for a few seconds. Spotted by Freezy Toad.
* When Princess Peach is signing the deed for the Mushroom Kingdom, her dress chages from pink to white, and then when she gives Larry the deed it changes back to pink.
* The original VHS release for this episode mistakenly featured a picture based off of "[[Recycled Koopa]]" on the cover.
* Bowser wears a red cape near the end of the episode, coincidentally making him resemble [[King K. Rool]], who had not been around at the time.
