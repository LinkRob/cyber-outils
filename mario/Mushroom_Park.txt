}}'''Mushroom Park''' is the first board in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', having no gimmicks, serving as a good choice for beginners.
==Gameplay==
*Players start off with 5 [[Mini Star|Mini Stars]]. 
*A GamePad on the top right with a [[Bowser]] Symbol on it shows you how many locks left to open. Each time you roll a different number on '''Normal''' Dice Block the number decrease by 1. If you open the last lock, Bowser will take half of your Mini Stars and make [[Bowser Space|Bowser Spaces]] on the board.
*There are also a few Toad Houses, where all players get a Special Dice Block, but the captain's box spins much slower, allows him to choose the Dice Block he/she wants.{{MP10Nav}}[[Category:Boards in the Mario Party series]]
