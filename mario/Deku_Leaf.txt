The '''Deku Leaf''' is an object used by [[Link]] in the [[The Wind Waker]] [[microgame]] in both ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'' and ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. The Deku Leaf is from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' series, having first appeared in ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker]]'', as an obtainable item.

==Description==
The Deku Leaf is held out and used like a parachute. It allows Link to glide in the air, and being blown by a gust of [[wind]] gives Link the ability to travel an even farther distance.

== In {{series|WarioWare}} ==
[[File:The Wind Waker WWSM.png|thumb|left|200px|Link using the Deku Leaf in ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'']]
In The Wind Waker microgame, the object is to direct Link, who is holding out the Deku Leaf while being guided by the wind, to safely land on a platform between the [[w:c:zelda:Forest Haven|Forest Haven]] and the [[w:c:zelda:Forbidden Woods|Forbidden Woods]]. In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'', Link has to travel a farther distance for each increased difficulty setting, but in ''WarioWare Gold'', the difficulty settings instead affect the size of the platform on which he must land.
