'''Fluffin' Puffins''' are a species of helpful allies in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Fluffin' Puffins are a variant of [[Huffin' Puffin]]s, which they same similarities to, though the chicks lack any limbs and have green spots to resemble [[Yoshi Egg]]s.

===Behavior===
If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] throws a Fluffin' Puffin chick, it flies in a straight path, similarly to a [[Yarn Ball]], and leaves a path of fluff behind.

==In ''Yoshi's Woolly World''==
Fluffin' Puffins only appear in the level [[Fluffin' Puffin Babysitting]]. Fluffin' Puffin chicks can be encountered in crowds separately from their parents, and a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can walk into one to add it to his stash. If a Fluffin' Puffin chick hits a surface or an enemy after being thrown, they disappear. Fluffin' Puffin chicks are also weaker than Yarn Balls, as they cannot be used to defeat [[Piranha Plant]]s or [[Wild Ptooie Piranha]]s, although they can attack other enemies such as [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. An adult Fluffin' Puffin can be defeated from being jumped on.

The fluff left behind by a Fluffin' Puffin chick can be used as a platform, and it disintegrates after a short time. Yoshi can [[Ground Pound (move)|ground pound]] on any spot on the platform to break through it.

There is a Fluffin' Puffin costume for the Yoshis that can be unlocked by collecting every [[Wonder Wool]] in the Fluffin' Puffin Babysitting.
