'''Stewart''' is a [[Cheep Cheep]] located at the north end of [[Rogueport]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Stewart manages the blimp to [[Glitzville]]. From the dock in Rogueport and the plaza in Glitzville, Stewart makes sure all passengers have a [[Blimp Ticket|ticket]] on hand. Those without these tickets cannot ride the blimp to and from Glitzville.
