}}'''Stampede''' is a special move used by [[Yoshi (partner)|Yoshi]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Yoshi can use this move once he reaches the [[Ultra Rank]] and it requires 6 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Effects==
To use Stampede, the player must press {{button|gcn|l}} and {{button|gcn|r}} repeatedly to fill up the gauge. Once it's filled up, a horde of Yoshis appear on the screen damaging everything including air and ceiling enemies.

==Trivia==
*The description erroneously claims the move only attacks ground enemies. This was not fixed in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].
