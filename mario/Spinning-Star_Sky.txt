'''Spinning-Star Sky''' is the first [[level]] of [[Frosted Glacier]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. It is unlocked by completing [[Morton's Compactor Castle]], while completing Spinning-Star Sky unlocks [[Cooligan Fields]] and a spot with a [[Bubble Baby Yoshi]].

==Layout==
This level is snow-themed, and begins near a series of [[Star Lift]]s. A [[? Block]] containing a power-up is located above the Star Lift. A [[Koopa Troopa]] is encountered, along with another Star Lift above two [[Goomba]]s. Two more Goombas are found, and are followed by more Star Lifts above a pit, as well as a [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]]. A Koopa Troopas and some Goombas are found on a section of land. [[Coin]]s can be found hidden in sections of the ground. Another Star Lift is found, followed by a [[Piranha Plant]] on a row of [[Brick Block]]s. More Goombas and a Koopa Troopa follow, along with a row of Star Lifts that leads to the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. Some Star Lifts can be found above a row of [[Muncher]]s, and there is a ? Block containing a power-up. A Paratroopa is found with a [[Green Ring]]. Another row of Munchers is found, with Star Lifts and [[Jump Block]]s above them. Some Star Lifts are found over a pit, with coins and a ? Block above them. More Munchers are found with Star Lifts above them, and a Brick Block containing a [[Starman]] is found in a wall below. More Star Lifts are found, along with a [[Goal Pole]].
