The '''Dream Portal''' is a portal that connects the [[Dream World]] to the [[Pi'illo Island|Real World]]. The Dream Portal appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

== Description ==
The Dream Portal is a rainbow portal shaped like a cloud. In ''Dream Team'', [[Luigi]] must sleep on a [[Pi'illo]]'s pillow to allow Mario to enter the Dream World.
