'''Duct & Cover''' is an 8-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
Both characters on each of the four teams must cooperatively six leaks on their team's duct, which is done by pressing the buttons shown on-screen. The first team to repair their duct is the winner.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} '''/''' {{button|gcn|C}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|B}} {{button|gcn|A}} {{button|gcn|Y}} {{button|gcn|X}} {{button|gcn|L}} {{button|gcn|R}}: Tap

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''Watch for the button symbols to appear over the pipe, then tap that button repeatedly to fix the leak.''
* '''Advice''': ''If you and your partner work on different leaks, the job will go much faster.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on the phrase "duck and cover."
