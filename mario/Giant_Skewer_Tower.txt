'''Giant Skewer Tower''' ('''Giant Spike Pillar Tower''' in the British English version) or '''{{world|Waters|tower}}''', is the Tower course of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via [[Tropical Refresher]], and completing the Tower unlocks [[Haunted Shipwreck]], and a cutscene of [[Peach's Castle#New Super Mario Bros. U|Peach's Castle]] being completely shrouded in [[Kamek]]'s tornado. However, the cutscene does not play if [[Freezing Rain Tower]] is completed beforehand.  
==Layout==
Similarly to [[World 4-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4-Castle]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', this is a water course filled with [[Spike Pillar]]s.

The level begins in a area with [[Jump Block]]s leading to a [[Warp Pipe]]. The next section is filled with water, and there are [[current]]-pumping Warp Pipes at the beginning of the area. The area contains multiple [[Spike Pillar]]s that come out of the walls, and the player must go through sections in the wall to continue. A [[Red Ring]] is found to the left of the area. The player then exits the water, with a [[lift]] at the surface, as well as Jump Blocks. A section with more Jump Blocks and lifts follows, with Spike Pillars acting as obstacles. Another Spike Pillar, as well as a [[Dry Bones]] and a [[Super Dry Bones]], are found before the Warp Pipe leading to the next section. A red Warp Pipe is hidden in the wall to the right, allowing the player to access a [[P Switch]] in the area with the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. More Jump Blocks, Dry Bones, Spike Pillars, and a Big Dry Bones are found as the player climbs the section. After a section in which Jump Blocks are used to reach semi-solid platforms, along with two Spike Pillar, the boss door is found.

Boom Boom is the boss of the tower. [[Kamek]] flies in and give him the ability to jump. Unlike in [[Stoneslide Tower]], however, Boom Boom keeps his spin attack, even after getting stomped on.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Where the first Spike Pillar is, the player must go to the wall on the left for the first [[Star Coin]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': The player must use the [[Jump Block]] to reach the second Star Coin in the air.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is similar to the second Star Coin, only this one is above a Spike Pillar. The Star Coin can also be obtained via [[POW Block]].
