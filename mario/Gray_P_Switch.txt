'''Gray P Switches''', also formatted '''Gray P-Switches''', are a rare variation of the [[P Switch]] featured in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. They have the standard appearance of a P Switch but are light gray in color. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can step on a Gray P Switch to transform various enemies in the location into [[Gray Coin]]s.
