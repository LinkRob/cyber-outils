The '''Bit Bike''' ('''Nanobike''' in Europe) is a small [[bike]] with good handling in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. It is also the lightest bike in the game. It is the staff ghost vehicle for [[Mario Kart 64|N64]] [[Mario Raceway]] (Nin*Ichiro, [[Baby Mario]]) and is available from the start as well.

==Stats==
*'''Speed''' - 25
*'''Weight''' - 18
*'''Acceleration''' - 59
*'''Handling''' - 67
*'''Drift''' - 40
*'''Offroad''' - 56
*'''Mini-Turbo''' - 62

==Trivia==
*The Bit Bike is the slowest among all the vehicles in Mario Kart Wii.
