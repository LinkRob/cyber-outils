'''Green Magikoopas''' are a variant of [[Magikoopa]] which first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
In this game, Green Magikoopas (abbreviated '''GRN Magikoopa''' in-game) can be found in [[Bowser's Castle]], where they use different stat inducing moves.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:GreenMagikoopaPMTTYD.gif|thumb]]In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', a single Green Magikoopa can be found in the [[Glitz Pit]] of [[Glitzville]] as part of the team, the [[Magikoopa Masters]]. Like in the previous games, the Green Magikoopa attacks mainly with stat-inducing moves and is the only Magikoopa that is flying on a broom.

==In ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''==
This specific Magikoopa can be found on ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. He is part of the [[Bowser Jr.]] Rookies team and the Bowser Monsters team. When he is up to bat, he uses a long wand to use. This Magikoopa along with the rest has really good pitching.

==In ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' / ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''==
Green Magikoopas are obtainable Ranged minions in [[Minion Quest]] and [[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]. Their Special Skill is Sure Shot, which increases the Accuracy stat of the minions around them; in Bowser Jr.'s Journey their Skill is changed to Feed the Speed.
