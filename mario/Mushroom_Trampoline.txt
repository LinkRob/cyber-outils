'''Mushroom Trampolines''' are a recurring object in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
Mushroom Trampolines are a variation of [[Mushroom Platform]]s with the properties of either a [[Jump Block]] or a [[Springboard]] in that if [[Mario]] or another character [[jump]]s onto a Mushroom Trampoline, they are bounced upward. In the {{series|Mario Kart}}, Mushroom Trampolines are notably featured in [[Mushroom Gorge]], a course introduced in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. Throughout the land of Mario Kart World, there are three pink ones that have pulsating caps, one in the racetrack Cheep-Cheep Falls and the other two in Acorn Heights.
