==History==
It is located in the southwestern area of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and can only be accessed after completing Chapter 1 (as the southern path is blocked until then). The shop is run by a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] named [[Harry]] who takes great pride in his work and is one of two [[Item Shop]]s in Toad Town with the other being [[Shroom Grocery]]. However, Harry's Shop sells better and more expensive items than Shroom Grocery. During Chapter 4, Mario has to head into Harry's Shop in order to get the [[Toy Train]] from the storage room. However, Mario must get back the [[Storage Key]] from in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] first after the [[Shy Guy]]s have stolen it.
