'''Sayonara''' is a location featured in two ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episodes, "[[Mario Meets Koop-zilla]]" and "[[Karate Koopa]]," the latter featuring it as the main setting. It is an Oriental city heavily based on traditional buildings in real-world [[Japan]].

==Description==
In "Mario Meets Koop-zilla," Sayonara has a more metropolitan appearance whereas in "Karate Koopa" it is a village. This change in settlement is likely because in the former episode, [[Koop-zilla]] ([[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s alias for the episode) destroyed the whole city in a similar manner to {{w|Godzilla}}.

==Etymology==
The city shares a name with the Japanese word "sayonara," meaning "good bye."{{TSMBSSNav}}
