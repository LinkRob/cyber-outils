'''Coin Battle''' is a multiplayer mode in a few of the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' series titles. It first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' and returns in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and its [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
In Coin Battle, up to four players compete to collect the most [[coin]]s in a [[level]], most of which are from the main adventure. Various actions that would normally earn the players [[point]]s in the main adventure instead earn them a certain number of coins.
