The '''Banana Peel''' is [[Diddy Kong]]'s down special move in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Diddy tosses a [[Banana Peel]] item behind his shoulder. Like ordinary banana peels, any character to walk over the Banana Peel object slips and falls over. Diddy can even pick up a Banana Peel and throw them at his opponents.

In ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', Diddy can toss two Banana Peels on the stage at once, but can only throw one on stage in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U''.

In ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U'', the Banana Peel move received two new variants: the '''Shocking Banana Peel''' and '''Battering Banana Peel'''. The Shocking Banana Peel paralyzes opponents who step on it, and Battering Banana Peel causes Diddy to throw a Banana Peel straight up, which launches up any character that is hit by it.
