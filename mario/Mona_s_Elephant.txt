'''Mona's Elephant''' is a character who first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. He is one of the [[Yum Yums]], being a pet of [[Mona]]'s.

==Profiles==
===Physical description===
He is a cyan elephant (light blue in later appearances) who wears a red shirt with an orange "1" on it.

===Behavior===
Mona's Elephant can shoot sludge and soccer balls from his trunk, a power used whenever Mona gets into roadside conflict. His trunk is also used for vacuuming the room, as shown when she leaves in ''WarioWare: Get It Together!'' and her pets trash her room behind her back and are commanded to clean up the mess.
