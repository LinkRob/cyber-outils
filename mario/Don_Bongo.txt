'''Don Bongo''' is a boss in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. He is located in [[Frustration|Stage 3-4: Frustration]] and can be defeated by throwing 3 eggs at his lips. He attacks  by stomping around which causes pots, pans, and kettles to fall, and throwing spiked balls. His battle is fought on ice. It is possible he is the king of the gabons (a sub-species os spike).
