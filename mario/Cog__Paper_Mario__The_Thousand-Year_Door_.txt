The '''Cog''' is a key item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
It is found in the [[X-Naut Fortress]]. It is on top of a shelf in the room with the [[Yux]] experiments. To reach it, [[Mario]] must go through a vent. After this, he must return it to the prize-grabber crane machine on Sublevel 1. The prize-grabber is in a strange room with a platform covered in prizes and some fancy devices.

The left, right, and middle buttons must be pressed to activate the machine. The crane is worked by pressing A to move it left and B to move it back. The badge [[Feeling Fine]], a [[Star Piece]], and some coins can be obtained on the platform.
