|games = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}'''Bongo Blast''' is a special technique used by [[Donkey Kong]] in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. This move can be purchased from [[Candy's Music Shop]] where Donkey Kong uses this move with his bongos.

==Usage==
When Bongo Blast is used, Donkey Kong plays a small beat before unleashing the technique. This move can be used on a music pad with Donkey Kong's bongos on it.
