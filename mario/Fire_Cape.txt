'''Fire Cape''' is one of [[Geno]]'s personal [[armor]] items in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The Fire Cape can be acquired by either buying it from [[Hinopio]] at [[Barrel Volcano]] or [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] for 90 [[coin]]s. The Fire Cape increases Geno's [[Defense]] by 30 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 15.
