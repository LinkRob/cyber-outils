'''Yoshi's Island''' can be seen on a map showing areas surrounding the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. Later, the island is visited by [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]], [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]], and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] during their journey to the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]].
