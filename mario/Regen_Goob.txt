'''Regen Goobs''' are a variety of rare ghosts in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. Regen Goobs are a type of [[Goob]], but it is glowing fiery pink instead of blue. 

== In ''Luigi's Mansion 3'' ==
Regen Goobs can restore their HP when [[Luigi]] releases them, they have 250HP, more than a double of a Goob's HP. Regen Goobs only have a chance of appearing when playing 10 or 20 floors in the [[ScareScraper]].

=== Battle ===
If Regen Goobs appear on any floor that is a multiple of five, they will try to attack the [[Luigi]]s by punching, much like regular Goobs. If a Luigi flashes and vacuums one of them, it is recommended not to slam them quickly, and instead continue vacuuming it for as long as possible until it reaches 140HP, then slam as fast as possible to capture them immediately. However, if not drained fully of their HP, they will turn invisible and hide to replenish their lost HP rapidly.
