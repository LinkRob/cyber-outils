A '''Paper Macho Podoboo''' (also called a '''Paper Macho Crater Crasher''') is one of the [[Paper Macho Soldier]]s found in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. They appear in [[Hotfoot Crater]]. They are fought with [[Paper Macho Goomba]]s and [[Paper Macho Bob-Omb]]s. Unlike a standard [[Podoboo]], Paper Macho Podoboos can leave flames on the ground and can bounce on land without dissapating.
