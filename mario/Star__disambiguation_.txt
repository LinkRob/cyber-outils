'''Stars''' in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] can refer to either:

*[[Cobalt Star]], the energy source for the time machine erected by [[Professor E. Gadd]] to rid himself of [[Princess Shroob]] during the events of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.
*[[Crystal Star]], paramount objects in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''
*[[Grand Star]], stars from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''
*[[Starman]], a power-up that makes [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and other characters temporarily invincible.
*[[Super Star (Yoshi's Island)|Super Star]], power-ups that grant [[Baby Mario]] temporary invincibility a cape and give him the ability to run up walls and ceilings.
*[[Mega Star]], an enormous star from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''
*[[Millennium Star]], the primary goal for the events of ''[[Mario Party 3]]''
*[[Power Star]]s, collectible stars required to complete games.
*[[Red Power Star]]s, used to enable [[Mario]] to fly during the events of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''
*[[Red Star]]s, used to turn Mario into [[Flying Mario]] and be granted the ability to fly in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''
*[[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] as in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' series
