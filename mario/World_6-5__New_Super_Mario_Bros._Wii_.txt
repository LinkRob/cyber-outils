'''World 6-5''' is a level from [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 
==Overview==
This course is similar to [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4]].

==Walkthrough==
The level begins with a [[Porcupuffer]] chasing Mario throughout the level. The first Star Coin can easily be obtained by getting on a ? Block or swimming to get it. Then the water starts frequently rising and draining. After the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], the Mario can get the second Star Coin which is on a low platform. Next, there will be four [[? Block]]s. If Mario jumps in between the second and third ones, a [[Hidden Block]] with a [[Beanstalk]] appears, giving access to the third Star Coin and the secret exit. After the Beanstalk, Mario must enter a [[Warp Pipe]] to the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]] finishing the level.
==Enemies==
* Porcupuffers
* Koopa Troopas

==[[Star Coin]]s==
* '''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is floating atop a big ? Block near the beginning of the level.
* '''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is in plain sight shortly after the Checkpoint Flag.
* '''Star Coin 3''': Soon after the second Star Coin are four big ? Blocks. In the middle of them is a hidden Beanstalk. After climbing up it, Mario and co. must grab the Propeller Block and use it to fly up to the third Star Coin at the end of the area.

== Secret Exit ==
Mario and co. must follow the aforementioned path to the third Star Coin for the Red Flagpole. This secret exit will take the player directly to [[World 6-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6-Castle]].{{New Super Mario Bros. Wii}}
