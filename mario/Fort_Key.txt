The '''Fort Key''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Fort Keys are only found in [[Fort Francis]] during Chapter 3-4. The Fort Keys are used to unlock the doors of Fort Francis. Two Fort Keys are needed to get into the room where [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]] was keeping [[Tippi]] held captive.
