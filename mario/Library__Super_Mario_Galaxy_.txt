The '''Library''' is an area in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
=== ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ===
The library is in the game's hub world, the [[Comet Observatory]]. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] were to enter, only a rocking chair holding a [[Rosalina's Storybook|storybook]] will be present. When chapters to the book are unlocked, [[Rosalina]] will then read only the chapters unlocked by the player.

=== ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ===
If the player collects 120 stars, Rosalina will be seen reading another book. [[Luma (character)|Luma]] will bring Mario or Luigi's hat, and Rosalina will begin reading a book about [[Green Power Star|Green Power Stars]]. This cutscene makes Green Power Stars scatter around all the different galaxies.
