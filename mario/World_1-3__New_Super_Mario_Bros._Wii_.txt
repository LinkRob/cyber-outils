'''World 1-3''' is a level from [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. The level has another secret exit, by moving to the top of the fields and enter through the pipe.

===Secret Exit===
There is a secret exit that appears shortly before the third Star Coin, just after the Venus Fire Trap. The player needs to jump to the high cliff edge leading to a hidden passage. Once the player reaches the entrance point, they need to jump over the passage above the screen and travel across the top. At the end of this path is a hidden pipe, which will send the players to the hidden goal. The secret exit will take the players to the World 1-Cannon Warp Cannon, which will send them to World 5.

== [[Star Coin]]s ==

* '''Star Coin 1: '''On an upward slope, with the pipe lying down, use the [[Yoshi]] and release the Yoshi to grab the Star Coin.
* '''Star Coin 2: '''Among the four green pipes and the yellow pipe, go to the third pipe where the second [[Piranha Plant]] is from the left. move left and go inside the yellow pipe. Get the Star Coin in the air.
* '''Star Coin 3: '''Go on the right-angled red pipe and jump to the right by hovering with the Yoshi.
