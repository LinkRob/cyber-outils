'''Kitsune Luigi''' is [[Luigi]]'s counterpart to [[Tanooki Mario]] as of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Description ==
When he dons the Kitsune suit, Luigi dresses up in a onesie with long sleeves and fox ears and a tail that he can use to glide and attack. Luigi retains the gloves from his normal form. The majority of his suit is brownish-yellow, but the belly, the end of the tail, and the soles of the feet are light yellow. Grabbing a Stone Leaf adds a green scarf to him.

== In {{series|Super Mario}} ==
===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
With the return of the [[Super Leaf]], Luigi gains this new form distinct from Tanooki Mario's. The difference is purely cosmetic, as the Kitsune suit allows him to glide and attack with his tail just like the Tanooki suit. When he uses a Stone Leaf, a green scarf is added to his outfit and he gains the ability to turn into Statue Luigi. Notably, Statue Luigi raises his left hand as opposed to Statue Mario's right hand. 

===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''===
Kitsune Luigi is exactly the same as in ''Super Mario 3D Land'' except that Luigi no longer has the ability to transform into a statue due to no Stone Leaf appearing.

== Trivia ==
* Kitsune Luigi started a recurring trend in the {{series|Super Mario}} in which Luigi received distinct form from Mario. Another example of this is [[Silver Luigi]], [[Gold Mario]]'s equivalent in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Furthermore, Cat Suit Luigi has round ears and spots as opposed to every other cat form.
* In the trophies of the North American version of ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]'', it says that it is "Kitsune" instead of "Tanooki" because they did not want Mario and Luigi to be the same and the same suit.
