'''World 6-2''' is the thirtieth level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
The level, shaped like a [[wikipedia:Pyramid|Pyramid]], begins with Mario in a room filled with spikes. The second room of this level features similar qualities to the first but features a [[Blokkablok]]. An elevator then takes Mario to the third room, a room filled with moving platforms and [[Pokey]]s. Past the second floor of this room, Mario finds the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]], and can access two elevators to proceed to higher rooms. This last room sports several spiked platforms as well as two Blokkabloks. Here, Mario finds the [[Warp Pipe]] leading to the level's [[Flagpole]].

== Enemies ==
Enemies that appeared in World 6-2:
* [[Pokey]]
* [[Blokkablok]]

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] is found behind a Pokey in the second room.
*'''Star Medal 2''': On the second floor of the room with the rising platforms, Mario can use the [[Tanooki Suit]] to enter a secret path containing the second Star Medal.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The third and final Star Medal is found in a room with two elevators. By going left after the second elevator, Mario can find the coin at the top of the path.
