The '''Great Sunflower''' is a large sunflower character who lives in [[Pinna Park]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It looks after the [[Sunflower Kid]]s. The main difference from the Sunflower Kids is that the Great Sunflower has a brown face with white freckles and entirely white eyes.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
The Great Sunflower is featured in the fourth mission, [[The Wilted Sunflowers]]. Here, [[Mario]] has to defeat the [[Snooza Koopa]]s to rescue the Sunflower Kids and lastly the Great Sunflower from their wilted state. Once the Great Sunflower unwilts, it happily rewards Mario a [[Shine Sprite]].
