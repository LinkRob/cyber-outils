'''I Spy''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
In ''Paper Mario'', I Spy allows [[Mario]] to detect a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] in an area upon entering the area. This badge finds Star Pieces found by way of Flip Panels as well which are opened by use of the [[Spin Jump]] or [[Ultra Hammer]]. Mario can wear this badge by paying one point of [[Badge Point|BP]].

Mario can acquire this badge during or after the events of Chapter 4. Mario obtains this badge by giving Rowf his [[Calculator]] back which was stolen by a [[Shy Guy]]. As a reward for his generosity, Rowf gives Mario this badge.

In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', I Spy is absent. Instead, Mario can acquire [[Ms. Mowz]] for his party, and this party member has the ability to sniff out treasures in an area.
