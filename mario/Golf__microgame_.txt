'''Golf''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
Golf features a setting from the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[Golf (Nintendo Entertainment System)|same name]]. The player has to help the golfer hit the ball onto the green by rotating the course around during the swing so that it lands onto the course. It is possible to get a {{w|hole in one}}, but this is not a requirement. If the ball successfully lands on the green, the player wins at the microgame. If the ball lands elsewhere, the player loses the microgame, and either "BOO" or "OB" is displayed onscreen.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The course is fairly curved.
*'''Level 2:''' The course is very curved.
*'''Level 3:''' The course is full of islands and water hazards.

==Trivia==
*There is a similar microgame named [[NES Open Tournament Golf (microgame)|NES Open Tournament Golf]], also based on an identically-named [[NES Open Tournament Golf|golf game]] for the Nintendo Entertainment System, except it involves helping [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] get a hole-in-one.
