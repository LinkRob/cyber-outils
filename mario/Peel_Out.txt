'''Peel Out''' is a [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
Donkey Kong jumps down a slide in front of his [[DK's Tree House|treehouse]], and the first character jumps forward to sit at the top of the slide. The screen then splits into four vertical sections.

==Gameplay==
The characters each travel a large wooden slide, which starts from DK's Tree House. There are a few junctions, and some have more [[banana]]s and [[Banana Bunch]]es than others. It is impossible to determine which paths have more bananas before sliding down, making this a luck-based minigame. Just before players reach a junction, they must tilt either left or right on the Control Stick to have their character move down either path. The minigame ends when the characters reach the bottom.

==Ending==
The characters stand in front of DK's Tree House with Donkey Kong, where each of them perform their victory animation.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Choose a path

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Go down the slide and collect bananas on the way down.''
* '''Advice''': ''Which slide has more bananas? Only luck can say!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on a phrase that means "to depart quickly."
