'''Bob-ombs Away''' is a 1 vs. 3 minigame in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
The solo player must avoid the [[Bob-omb]]s as the three team members throw them down. There are objects that will drop the Bob-ombs in different spots so the three players must work together to try and trap the single player. The single player must do their best to avoid being boxed in by the three players. Whoever manages to reach their goal first wins.
