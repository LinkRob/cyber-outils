'''M Blocks''' are red [[block]]s which appear in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]] and ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. An M Block can only be activated by [[Mario]] in the same sense that the [[L Block]] can only be activated by [[Luigi]]. In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', an M Block can also be activated by [[Baby Mario]].

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
The M Blocks are invisible without [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and [[Baby Mario]]. They are both in the title and featured within 1-2: [[Hit the M Blocks!]].
