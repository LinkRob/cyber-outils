'''Motor Rooter''' is a [[Duel minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. It later reappears in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the two players must race on a [[Koopa Shell]] to the finish line. There are several dash pads that the players can use to speed up. They must also be careful of the [[Amp]]s that generate electricity as going through the electricity will slow the players down. The first player to cross the finish line is the winner.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|stick}} (Left or Right) - Move Clockwise/Counterclockwise
