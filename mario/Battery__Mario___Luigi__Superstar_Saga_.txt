'''Batteries''' are items featured throughout the [[Mysterious Mine Carts]] minigame in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
Batteries are colored red and have a yellow, M-shaped stripe on them. They power the flashlight in the Mysterious Mine Carts minigame, although they are short-lived.
