'''HP Beans''' are an obtainable item in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Locations==
An HP Bean can be obtained in different [[Challenges]], and has a chance to be obtained in levels.

==Usage==
The HP Bean permanently increases one of your troops HP by 1. This works for any type of troop.

==Flavor text==
*'''In-game description:''' ''A bean that increases your HP by 1.''
