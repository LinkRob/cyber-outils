'''Space Monster Gabriel''' is a one-time character who appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Physical description==
Gabriel is giant, round, round-shaped, rock-like creature who is found in outer space. He resembles a rocky planet. Gabriel has a pair of eyes and a jagged mouth, both of which appear to be engraved into him.

==In ''WarioWare: Twisted!''==
Space Monster Gabriel appears in the ending scene of [[Orbulon]]'s story. Here, when Orbulon and the [[Alien Bunny|Alien Bunnies]] manage to escape from a [[Black Hole]], Orbulon decides to take a rest and dock his [[Oinker|UFO]] on a nearby planet, who turns out to actually be Space Monster Gabriel. Gabriel instantly swallows the UFO when it approaches him, and when he is unable to chew on it, Gabriel spits it out, launching Orbulon and the UFO down to [[Club Sugar]] in [[Diamond City]].
