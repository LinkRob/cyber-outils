'''Sho Murata''' is an artist at [[Nintendo]]. He is also the current voice actor for [[Boom Boom]].

==''Mario''-related works==
* ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' (2010) - Character Design
* ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' (2011) - Staff
* ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' (2012) - Character Design
* ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' (2013) - Staff
* ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' (2014) - Staff
* ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (3DS)]]'' (2016) - Nintendo Character Voices
* ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Wii U)]]'' (2016) - Nintendo Character Voices
* ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' (2017) - Character Art Lead
* ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]'' (2018) - Voices
* ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' (2018) - Special Thanks
* ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games: Tokyo 2020]]'' (2019) - Nintendo Character Voices
* ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'' (2021) - Staff
* ''[[Mario Strikers: Battle League]]'' (2022) - Voice
