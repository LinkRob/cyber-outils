The Gateway Underground (Also referred to as the Minus World) is a location in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'' it is under [[Gateway Galaxy (Illumination)|Gateway Galaxy]]  and can only be accessed through a brick wall hologram which is a reference to the ''[[Super Mario Bros.|Super Mario Bros]]'' minus world glitch. It was also confirmed by the bonus features to have been inspired by [[Neo Bowser City]]. Within the underground there is also [[Wart's Casino]] which hosts 4/6 of Wart's [[8 bits]], there is also a variety of different enemies such as Shy Guys, Octoombas, Coin Coffers and much more. Within the city many easter eggs can be found such as a sign with the [[Roy Koopa|Roy]] Smooth Sounds logo on it and another one with [[Spewart]] on it.

=== Easter Eggs ===
The city is heavy with many easter eggs and not just the 2 mentioned previously, when Peach and Toad enter the city there is a large N64 logo that can be seen and near it a creepy dice block from Mario Party Superstars. A sign with the Luigi's Mansion [[King Boo]] can also be seen along with a sign with a regular boo, going back to when Peach and Toad enter the city a [[Phanto]] mask can be seen hanging on the stand, when Peach and Toad are chasing Ukiki a sign with the sprite of the magical potion can be seen of course which is also from [[Super Mario Bros. 2|Super Mario Bros 2]], and at the same time a walking 8-bit [[Goomba]] sprite hologram was behind them.{{TSMBMNav}}
