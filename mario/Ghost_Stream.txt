'''Ghost Streams''' are a species of harmless ghosts encountered in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', during the level [[Torrential Maze]]. Ghost Streams repeatedly attach themselves from one hole to another for a few seconds before disappearing into the hole and then reappearing. Ghost Streams are like [[Ghost Rider]]s in that they can be used as platforms, although in a different manner, as a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can only stand on a Ghost Stream while it is connected between two holes.

==Physical description==
Ghost Streams are long worm-shaped ghosts that lack any apparent bodily features.
