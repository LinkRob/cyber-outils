|latest=[[Mario Party 3]] (2000)}}{{Nihongo|'''Battle T.'''|バトルキノピオ|Batoru Kinopio|Battle Toad}} is a character that appears in ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. He is the host of the Battle Room which can be unlocked by playing [[mini-game]]s in [[Party Mode]]. He is seen dancing until the player talks to him.

==Profile==
===Physical appearance===
Battle T. appears as a Toad with mint green spots on his head and he wears an orange shirt with a [[Super Mushroom|mushroom]] on it.

==Trivia==
*Battle T. also has a Japanese name referred to ''Battle Toad''. Considering "T." stands for "Toad", his name references ''[[w:c:nintendo:Battletoads|Battletoads]].''
