A '''Snake''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Mario Clash]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Snake Mario Clash sprite.png|frame|left]]
A Snake resembles a short real-world snake but has dark green body, a white belly, and a few spikes sticking out of their head.
{{br|left}}
===Behavior===
A Snake is capable of reproducing quickly by spitting an egg out of its mouth, and the egg hatches into another Snake shortly thereafter.

==In ''Mario Clash''==
To defeat a Snake, [[Mario]] has to throw a [[Koopa Shell|Turtle Shell]] at its side, causing the Snake to become stunned, and then throw another Turtle Shell at the Snake to defeat it. A Snake egg can be defeated from a single hit, either from a Turtle Shell or by touching it.
