'''Fire Emblem: Three Houses''' is a [[microgame]] part of [[9-Volt]]'s [[Nintendo Classics]] set in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Layout==
The player's character has a [[w:c:fireemblem:Tea Party|Tea Party]] with one of the house leaders—[[Claude]] (level 1), [[Dimitri]] (level 2), or [[Edelgard]] (level 3)—in the [[Nintendo Switch]] game of the [[w:c:fireemblem:Fire Emblem: Three Houses|same name]].

==Gameplay==
In all three difficulty settings, there are three options too choose from, and the player's character has to select the correct option based on what the command is, "Praise!" for level 1 and "Confess feelings" for levels 2 and 3. If the correct option is selected, the text "PERFECT TEATIME!" appears on screen, but if incorrect option is selected, the player loses at the microgame.

* '''Level 1''': The player's character has to compliment Claude.

* '''Level 2''': The character has to select the phrase with the correct spelling. After selecting one, the incorrect letters within the misspelled words are highlighted red. If the wrong answer is selected, Dimitri does not understand what the player is attempting to say.

* '''Level 3''': Tea time with Edelgard has a similar objective as with Dimitri in Level 2, but the misspelled answers are harder to tell apart from the correct one.
