"'''Star Koopa'''" is the fifty-first animated episode of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the live-action segment "[[Santa Claus is Coming to Flatbush]]."

==Plot==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] are traveling to help a group of Mushroom space colonists, who inhabit [[Mushroom Planet]], which [[Darth Koopa]] ([[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s alias during the episode) threaten to blow up. Suddenly, the group's ship, the [[Flying Pizza]], is pursued by the [[Koop Star]]. Darth Koopa, pulls the Marios' ship onboard the Koop Star with a tractor beam, and sends his [[Stormtroopa]]s to fight them. Although the heroes manage to fight off the Stormtroopas, Darth Koopa shows up and challenges Mario to a duel with [[lightplunger]]s. Although Mario wins, Darth Koopa retaliates by freezing the heroes in blocks of ice.

With the heroes wrapped around his finger, Darth Koopa orders [[Mouser]] to thaw them out, but only to blackmail Peach into telling the Mushroom Colonists to surrender their planet, or have it destroyed by his [[Birdo Ray]]. Peach refuses to surrender, so Mouser warms up the Birdo Ray, which will fire in thirty minutes, and drops the heroes down the garbage disposal.

Mario uses his plunger to save everyone from being mulched in the disposal, while Luigi clogs it with silverware, allowing the heroes to pass trough it safely. Soon they are ejected out of the Koop Star via a garbage rocket, where they land on the surface of the Mushroom Colonists' planet. After evading a Cobrat and a [[Pokey]], they manage to reach the Colony, where [[Obi-Wan Toadi]] arranges to have the four heroes attack the Koop Star in [[Mushroom Starfighter]]s. Mario has second thoughts, as he hasn't eaten lunch, but Obi-Wan gives him the strength he needs with a personal pizza.

With only a few minutes remaining on the Birdo Ray's timer, the four heroes fly their Starfighters towards the Koop Star, where they are pursued by Koopa Starfighters (resembling cooked chickens). Luigi and Peach fight off several Starfighters, while Toad covers Mario as he scrambles to find an entry point into the Koop Star. Mario flies into the Koop Star, blows up the foundation with a [[Bob-omb]], and flies away, blowing up the Koop Star right before the Birdo Ray would've gone off.

Afterwards, the heroes return to the Mushroom Colony, where everyone congratulates them. Mario is honored after what he'd been through, but he's actually more concerned about having lunch instead.

==Trivia/Goofs==
*The entire episode is a blatant parody of ''{{w|Star Wars}}''.
*This episode's plumber's log (#2001) was also used in "[[Robo Koopa]], and the number is a reference to ''{{w|2001: A Space Odyssey}}''.
*This is Mouser's last appearance on the show.
*When Bowser asks how much time is left on the Birdo-ray, Mouser says it is 16 minutes, but the timer says it's under 5 minutes, and then it changes back to the correct time.
*Princess Toadstool was not voiced by [[Jeannie Elias]] in this episode.
