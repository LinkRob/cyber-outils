'''Flower coins''' are a currency exclusive to ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They can be used to buy things at [[Poplin Shop|Poplin Shops]], and they are alternatives to regular coins.

10-flower coins appear in most levels, with each level having three. Each 10-flower coin is worth 10 flower coins.

== Trivia ==
* When [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flowers]] are [[Wonder Effect|active]] there are small flower coins worth 1/10 of a coin each, making them the first currency in the {{franchise|Mario}} to use decimals. However, when the [[Wonder Effect]] ends, the fractional portion of the flower coins is discarded.
* Flower Coins strongly resemble [[Purple Coin]]s from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' as well as [[Regional Coin]]s from ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.
