'''Red Skull Boxes''', also named '''Red Torpedo Bases''', are artillery that appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. They are black [[Skull Box]]es, with a difference being that they fire [[Targeting Ted]]s instead of [[Torpedo Ted]]s. Red Torpedo Bases, along with Targeting Teds, are found in the [[Soda Jungle]] level [[The Mighty Cannonship]], and in ''New Super Luigi U'', they appear in [[Larry's Trigger-Happy Castle]].
