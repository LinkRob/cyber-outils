'''Coal Guys''' are a variant of [[Shy Guy]]s and an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. They are encountered at [[Toad Trainworks]] and [[Sunset Express]]. Coal Guys have the same appearance as Shy Guys, but differ in that they use coal during battle.
