The '''Delfino Emergency Broadcast System''', acronymized as '''D.E.B.S.''', is an organization which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
The Delfino Emergency Broadcast System is the instant news and warning system of [[Isle Delfino]]. It provides the island's inhabitants with notifications about various happenings around the island, including current events and recent disasters. It is displayed as a scrolling message onscreen, similar to a news ticket.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Despite applying to seemingly all of Isle Delfino, messages from the Delfino Emergency Broadcast System occur only in [[Delfino Plaza]]. They are scrolling white text in a [[List of fonts#Pop Happiness|Pop Happiness]] font on a translucent black background at the bottom of the screen, following after a four-note chime.

The Delfino Emergency Broadcast System can provide the player with helpful, albeit subtle hints. For instance, the [[goop]] covering the [[Grand Pianta Statue]] at the start of [[Mario]]'s journey is hinted at with an alert saying that "...several people have gone missing near the Grand Pianta statue...," referring to the fact that many of the [[Pianta]]s and [[Noki]]s in [[Bianco Square]] have been buried within the goop.

==In ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''==
The Delfino Emergency Broadcast System appears during the minigame [[Tic-Tac-Glow]] as one of the game's generic advice messages, labeled "D.E.B.S."

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The system's English name seems to be based on that of the [[wikipedia:Emergency Broadcast System|Emergency Broadcast System]], the previous emergency warning system of the United States that was succeeded by the Emergency Alert System in 1997. As with D.E.B.S., the Emergency Broadcast System was often used to alert citizens of disasters or other threats occurring in affected areas.

No acronyms are used in the Japanese, Italian, and German languages: The former two simply use the system's full name, while the latter prefaces each broadcast with ''Kurznachrichten'', meaning "News headline."
