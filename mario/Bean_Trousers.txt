The '''Bean Trousers''' are a [[clothing]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Profile==
Little information about the Bean Trousers is provided in the game aside from the mention that they are popular in [[Hoohoo Village]].

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Bean Trousers can be bought from the clothes store in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 130 [[coin]]s. The Bean Trousers increases [[HP]] by 4 and [[Defense]] by 10.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Bean Trousers become available at Beanbean Castle Town's clothes store after [[Queen Bean]] has been defeated. The Bean Trousers were made slightly more expensive, at 140 coins, and can be sold for 35 coins. The Bean Trousers increase [[BP]] by 4, instead of HP, and they increase Defense by 15, slightly higher than in the original version.
