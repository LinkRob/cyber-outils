'''Chicken Race''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is one of the Gaddgets available from the start.

==Description==
Chicken Race is a four-player minigame played from one system. The objective is for each player to stop their car as close to the cliff as possible without falling off. A danger line warns if a car gets too close to the cliff. Whoever stops their car closest to the cliff is the winner.
