}}A '''Beepboxer''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are stronger versions of [[Boomboxer]]s.

==History==
Beepboxers are rather troublesome enemies that attack by shooting soundwaves at [[Mario]] and the other heroes. They are often found in groups and can attack from a fair distance away. Beepboxers are mainly found on [[Planet Blobule]] but can be found in other areas such as [[Overthere Stair]] and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]].
