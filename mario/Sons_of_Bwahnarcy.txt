The '''Sons of Bwahnarcy''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Sons of Bwahnarcy.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Sons of Bwahnarcy resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has medium cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Sons of Bwahnarcy deals around 165 to 175 damage.

It has a 60% chance of triggering the Super Effect. However, aiming it at the [[Valkyrie]] increases the weapon's chances of triggering the effect by 90%. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Honey (status effect)|Honey]] dealing 210 damage.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Sons of Bwahnarcy as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it after the player defeats [[Mecha Jr.]]
