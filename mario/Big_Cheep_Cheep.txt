'''Big Cheep-Cheep''' is a mini-boss in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. it is fought in [[Surfshine Harbor]] and is much bigger than a regular [[Cheep Cheep]] and even a [[Mega Cheep Cheep]].

==In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''==
===Battle===
Before the [[Toad (species)|Blue Toad]] and [[Mario]] could leave [[Surfshine Harbor]] on the boat, the Big-Cheep Cheep intercepts them by blocking the ship's path and rocking the boat with it's great force. Mario exits his boat and goes on the docks to engage the Big Cheep-Cheep in battle. In battle, the Big Cheep-Cheep mainly attacks by jumping into Mario, dealing 3 damage. After the Big Cheep-Cheep has taken a certain amount of damage (the HP amount doesn't go anywhere below 20 HP at this point to prevent the second phase from being skippable), it will swim into the ocean water in the background and begin to heal itself fully and uses a new attack that sprays water at Mario, which deals 10 damage and may make Mario Soggy. Mario must use the Fishhook thing to bring the behemoth back onto land otherwise any of his other attacks won't be able to reach the colossal fish. Afterwards, the Big Cheep-Cheep will grow even bigger, doubling his attack on the second turn, and again on the third. Mario must take down the Big Cheep-Cheep in five turns or it will explode, dealing 99 unblockable damage and usually defeating Mario. Additionally, Mario can quickly end the fight by using either a Spike Helmet sticker or the Thumbtack thing sticker to pop the Big Cheep-Cheep but must be careful as he will take a large amount damage in recoil, based on how big he is. Alternatively, Mario can use the Sponge or similar thing sticker to block the devastating explosion. The Big Cheep Cheep can easily be beaten with [[Hopslipper]]s as well as Flashy and Big Shiny Stickers, additionally, [[Things]] can also be used to deal huge damage to the Big Cheep-Cheep. After the Big Cheep-Cheep is defeated, Mario can sail onto the [[World 4 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|last]] [[World 5 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|three]] [[World 6 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|Worlds]].

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''===
Big Cheep Cheeps appears in Water-themed stages, usually as a more powerful form of normal [[Cheep Cheep]]s. They have a fairly high amount of health, and deal decent damage. They can use "Bash" to deal even higher damage, or at half health can use "Healing" to restore 50% health to the entire enemy team. Their main attribute is Water and their sub-attribute is Light. They have high health and recovery stats, but low attack, and their skill is Healing, which restores 2,000 HP and turns Wood [[Orb (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|Orbs]] into Heart Orbs. They require [[Comet Medal]]s to skill up, and can be acquired either by evolving a Cheep Cheep by using 4 [[Ice Flower]]s, or have a low chance to be dropped by a [[Cheep Chomp]]. They can also evolve into a Cheep Chomp for 6 Ice Flowers. After the 2.0 update, they gained the basic awakening of Water Boost, increasing Water attribute damage for the team when matching a full row of Water Orbs.

==Trivia==
*Unlike most enemies in the game, the Big Cheep-Cheep is a 3D Chinese Lantern-like sphere rather than simply being made of paper.
*The Big Cheep-Cheep is the only mini-boss in the {{series|Paper Mario}} that can be damaged with a [[First Strike]].
