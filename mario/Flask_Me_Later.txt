{{Infobox Microgame|name = Flask Me Later|image = [[File:WWSM Flask Me Later.png|thumb|250px]]|games = ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''|genre = [[Ashley]]|info = ''"Swish the tube until the color changes. Don’t spill any or you’ll have to use the safety shower!"''|forms = [[The Thumb Wrestler]]|song = Open Umbrella}}'''Flask Me Later''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. Also it is a pun of "ask me later".

==Gameplay==
The objective is to shake the flask to make the color change. If the solution is spilt out, the player loses in the microgame; caution must be practiced to prevent solution spilling out. If the player changes the color of the solution, the solution will steam and the player wins the microgame. There are six test tubes, conical flasks or florence flasks in the background.
*'''Level 1:''' The player will be shaking the test tube.
*'''Level 2:''' The player will be shaking the conical flask.
*'''Level 3:''' The player will be shaking the florence flask.
