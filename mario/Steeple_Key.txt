The '''Steeple Keys''' are key items which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Descriptions==
There are two types of Steeple Keys. One of them is white in color with a clover-shaped end. The other one is purple with a circle end.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
The white Steeple Keys are used to unlock the common doors in the Creepy Steeple including that one that leads to [[Doopliss]]. The purple Steeple Key is needed to unlock the basement of the Creepy Steeple that leads to a room with the [[Parrot]] and [[The letter "p"]].
