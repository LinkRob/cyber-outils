'''Fred de Fillet''' is the boss of [[World 4 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 4]] in the game ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. Fred de Fillet starts out as a normal Jean de Fillet living in [[lava]], but is then enlarged with [[Kamek]]'s hammer and is also provided with body armor. He attacks by sending [[Lava Wave]]s or by dropping [[Flightless Skeleton Goonie]]s at Yoshi, who then has to ground pound a button on top of Fred's armor to make it fall off. With Fred's skeletal body exposed, Yoshi can then hit Fred with an egg to damage him and make him angry, which makes him jump around furiously and attack, after his attacking stage is over, he will turn back to normal. The player has to hit him two more times to defeat him. Defeating Fred de Fillet will unlock [[World 5 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 5]].

==Trivia==
*Fred de Fillet is similar to [[Kingfin]], a boss from <i>[[Super Mario Galaxy]]</i>, both are large skeletal fish who are stronger than their smaller counterparts, the [[Jean de Fillet]] for Fred and the [[Bonefin]]s for [[Kingfin]] and both require certain projectiles to defeat them [[Shell]]s for [[Kingfin]] and [[Yoshi Egg]]s for Fred.
