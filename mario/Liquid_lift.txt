The '''liquid lift'''<ref>''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 34.</ref> is a water spirit wearing a [[Flatbed Ferry]] on its head that appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue]], ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.

==History==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', the liquid lift appears in only one room of [[Salvo The Slime's Castle]], where it is seen moving fluidly in a constant loop. By [[jump]]ing on its head, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can reach high areas when it stretches there. A similar creature known as a [[Blarggwich|Support Ghost]] appears later in [[Sluggy The Unshaven's Fort]], and it is a [[Blargg]] ghost that moves in a set obstacle course in a wide room. Its eyes use the same graphics as [[Salvo the Slime|Salvo]]'s.

===''Super Mario-kun''===
[[File:Roger Lift Super Mario Kun.png|thumb|200px|The liquid lift lifting Mario, Luigi, and Yoshi away in ''Super Mario-kun'']]
The liquid lift appears in ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' when a [[Spray Fish]] is defeated, and lifts [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Yoshi]].
