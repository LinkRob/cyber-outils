'''Drain Badge''' is one of the [[badge]]s in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be found either by buying it in [[Peach's Castle]] for 600 [[coin]]s or within [[Yoob's Belly]], where a [[Red Yoshi]] gives it to [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]]. Equipping the Drain Badge allows the user to gain [[HP]] equal to the number of times they damage an enemy each turn.
