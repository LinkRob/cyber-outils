A '''Swipin' Stu''' is a variant of [[Winged Strollin' Stu]] and an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Swipin' Stu is larger than a Winged Strollin' Stu. On the bottom it is dark green with white spots, and the dark green pattern extends up to the top of a Swipin' Stu's head. A Swipin' Stu has slightly darker skin than a [[Strollin' Stu]].

===Behavior===
A Swipin' Stu tries to attack [[Mario]] by landing on him when he walks underneath it. If a Swipin' Stu lands onto Mario, it steals his [[Mario's Cap|cap]], much like what [[Klepto]] does in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', although when a Swipin' Stu does this, it somehow sprouts the same type of mustache as Mario's.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Swipin' Stus are only encountered on the beach area of [[Pinna Park]], during Episode 5: [[The Runaway Ferris Wheel]], Episode 6: [[The Yoshi-Go-Round's Secret]], Episode 7: [[Shadow Mario in the Park]], and Episode 8: [[Roller Coaster Balloons]]. Mario can defeat a Swipin' Stu by either [[Ground Pound|ground-pounding]] it or by having [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] spray water at the Swipin' Stu so that it falls into the ground temporarily, giving Mario a chance to jump onto the Swipin' Stu to defeat it. A Swipin' Stu drops five [[coin]]s upon being defeated.
