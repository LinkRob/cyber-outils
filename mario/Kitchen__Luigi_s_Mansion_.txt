The '''Kitchen''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==History==
[[File:Kitchen lights on LM.png|thumb|left|250px|Luigi in the Kitchen in ''Luigi's Mansion'' for Nintendo GameCube]]
The Kitchen can be accessed from a door on the right side of the [[Dining Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Dining Room]]. [[Luigi]] has the option to either fight [[Mr. Luggs]] or to continue straight over to the Kitchen.

In the Kitchen, there are pots and pans that fly in Luigi's direction, but Luigi can vacuum them up so that they stop appearing. Whenever Luigi enters the Kitchen while it is dark, there is a twenty percent chance of a [[Gold Mouse]] appearing. The only [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] that Luigi has to vacuum for the lights to turn on is a [[Flash]], and it only appears if he opens the refrigerator. Aside from the Flash, Luigi can optionally have a [[Speedy Spirit]] appear by opening the dishwasher to the right of the door.

Completing the Kitchen is required because when the lights turn on, a [[Treasure chest (Luigi's Mansion series)|treasure chest]] with the [[Water Element Medal]] appears. Collecting it allows Luigi to vacuum in a [[Water Elemental Ghost]], which provides a limited supply of [[water]] needed to permanently extinguish the wall of flames blocking the door to the [[Boneyard]] on the left. The sink in the kitchen is one of the locations where a Water Elemental Ghost can be collected, but only once Luigi has obtained the Water Element Medal.

To the right of the sink is an oven, and if Luigi uses the effects of the [[Fire Element Medal]] on it, a [[Red Stone]] pops out. Luigi even gives a hint to this if he inspects the oven with the [[Game Boy Horror]] by saying "Aww, it's gone out. Would relighting it do any good?"

Like the other rooms, the Kitchen goes dark again during the [[blackout]], which also gives Luigi another chance to capture the room's Speedy Spirit and Gold Mouse if he had not done this previously, before the room's lights turned on. One [[Gold Ghost]] and two [[Purple Puncher]]s are encountered in the Kitchen during the blackout.

In the [[Hidden Mansion]] of PAL releases and the Nintendo 3DS [[reissue|remake]], Luigi instead encounters two Purple Punchers and three Flashes.

==Ghosts==
===Normal===
*Two [[Purple Puncher]]s (20 HP each)

===Blackout==

*Two [[Purple Puncher]]s (20 HP each)

*[[Elemental Ghost|Ice Elemental Ghost]]
