'''Sneeds''' (タネやん, ''Seed-Yan'') is a seed-like subspecies of the [[Snifit]] that usually fights alongside [[Flifit]]s in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

These enemies can only be battled by [[Bowser]] when fighting Flifits which spit these enemies out on which Bowser must counter by punching these seedlings. These enemies have an HP of 74.

Sneeds attack by firing seeds or ramming [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. They can turn into Flifits if they are not defeated quick enough which give less experience.

When these enemies are defeated, they may drop an [[Ultra Mushroom]].
