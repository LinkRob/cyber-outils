'''Julien Bardakoff''' is a French person who provided the voice work for [[Luigi]] in both the Japanese version of ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'' and ''[[Mario Party]]'', before [[Charles Martinet]] became Luigi's main voice actor. Julien Bardakoff's voice clips from the Japanese ''Mario Kart 64'' were reused in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' while his voice clips from ''Mario Party'' were reused in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

== Voices ==

* [[Luigi]] (1996-2001)

== Roles ==

* [[Mario Kart 64]] (Japanese version) – [[Luigi]]
* [[Excitebike: Bun Bun Mario Battle]] (uncredited) – Luigi (in-game only)
* [[Mario Party]] (uncredited) – Luigi
* [[Mario Party 2]] (uncredited) – Luigi
* [[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]] (uncredited) – Luigi
