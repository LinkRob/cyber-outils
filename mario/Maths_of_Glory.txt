'''Maths of Glory''' is a [[2 vs. 2 Minigame|2-vs-2]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The minigame title is a reference to ''[[w:c:movie:Paths of Glory|Paths of Glory]]'', a 1957 film.

== Opening ==
Teams pose against each other while the camera pans and zooms out before the minigame starts.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to destroy the other team's castle by doing enough shots using the cannon. There are numbers ranging from 1 to 6 and the range total of 1 and 36. The players should jump to stop the slot and the numbers on the slot multiplies for the total. After the next team has finished it, cannons will shoot with the teams' numbers. The first team to destroy the castle wins the minigame.

== Ending ==
The camera zooms closer to the winning team. The winning team does their victory animation as usual while the losing team can be heard sulking. If both teams destroy each other's castle at the same time, a Tie occurs
