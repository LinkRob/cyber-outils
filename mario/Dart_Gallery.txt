'''Dart Gallery''' is a [[1 vs. 3 Minigame|1-vs-3]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. Dart Gallery is a pun of "art gallery".

== Opening ==
Team players climb up the stairs where they find a solo player inside a capsule. The darts appear on their hand.

== Gameplay ==
In this minigame, team players have five darts to catch the solo player. The solo player can avoid these attacks by moving around in a capsule, if the player gets shot with three darts, the team wins the minigame. If the team player throws all of their darts and none, one or two darts are on the solo player or they run out of time, the solo player wins the minigame.

== Ending ==
The solo player does her/his victory animation without the capsule if they win. The team does their victory animation if they win. Losers do their losing animation.
