'''Bomb Game''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is available from the start.

==Description==
Bomb Game is a four-player game involving each one taking a turn on the [[Game Boy Advance]]. The objective is to avoid cutting the wire that causes the bomb detonate. There are eight different colored wires: white, blue, yellow, orange, light blue, purple, pink, and green. Because of this, it is possible to play Bomb Game with up to seven players.
