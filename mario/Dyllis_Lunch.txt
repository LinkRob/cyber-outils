}}The '''Dyllis Lunch''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made out of a [[Fried Shroom Plate]] and a [[Spaghetti|Spaghetti Plate]]. It looks like the [[Dyllis Dinner]] and the [[Zess Dinner]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. When used, the Dyllis Lunch restores 20 [[Heart Point|HP]], more than both ingredients separately, and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
