A '''Viking helmet''' is a [[Power-Up Pot]] and an item featured in the first two ''[[Wario Land (series)|Wario Land]]'' installments, ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]'', being named '''Bull Pot''' in the former game. It transforms [[Wario]] into [[Bull Wario]].
