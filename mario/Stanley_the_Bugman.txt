'''Stanley the Bugman''' is the protagonist of ''[[Greenhouse|Green House]]''. He is equipped with a [[sprayer]] to combat pesticides. Stanley would later be introduced as a ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' character by appearing as the protagonist of ''[[Donkey Kong 3]]'', its sequel ''[[Donkey Kong 3: Dai Gyakushū]]'', and ''[[Donkey Kong 3 (Game & Watch)|Donkey Kong 3]]'' for the [[Game & Watch]].

== In ''Greenhouse'' ==
Stanley is the main character, and he has to exterminate bugs.

== In ''[[Donkey Kong 3]]'' ==
Stanley is the playable character. He uses his Bug-Spray Gun to attack and ultimately defeat Donkey Kong. Bees and other insects attack this character, and the objective is to shoot them with his repellent.

== In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
Stanley appears as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''. He also appears as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

== In {{series|WarioWare}} ==
Stanley appears in some [[microgame]]s based on ''Donkey Kong 3''.

==In other media==
=== ''[[Saturday Supercade]]'' ===
Stanley makes an appearance in the episode "[[Greenhouse Gorillas]]," loosely based on ''Donkey Kong 3''.
