'''Shy Guy Heights''' is the sixth level in the ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

==Features==
This level takes place in two grassy areas, consisting of a hedge maze and a small town.

==Super Gems==
*'''Super Gem 1''': Climb the long ladder in the maze and then walk straight on the entryways to the Super Gem.
*'''Super Gem 2''': After crossing the bridge, pluck the stem at the other side of the first watch tower that's farthest from the others to reveal a [[Super Pickaxe]]. Use it to break the steel blocks and reveal [[Blue Toad]].
*'''Super Gem 3''': Climb to the second level of the second watch tower to reveal a hidden maze. Walk to the Super Gem near the center of the story.
==Optional Mission==
*Slip past all of the Shy Guys.
