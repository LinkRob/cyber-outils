'''A. Coz''' is a tennis player in the [[Mario Tennis (series)|''Mario Tennis'' series]]. He is the older brother of [[B. Coz]] and [[Willy]]. A. Coz first appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]].

==In the ''Mario Tennis'' series==
In ''Mario Tennis'' for the Game Boy Color, A. Coz is an unlockable character of the Tricky type. In doubles, he is partnered with his brother, B. Coz. A. Coz can be unlocked by winning against him in Mario Tour in Singles or Doubles in [[Island Open]].

In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour'', both A. Coz and B. Coz serve as coaches of the [[Factory (tennis school)|Factory]] tennis school. However, neither of them return as a playable character.
