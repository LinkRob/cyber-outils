'''Waiters''', also known as '''serving ghosts'''<ref>Loe, Casey. Versus Books ''Luigi's Mansion'' Perfect Guide. Page 34.</ref> or '''Waiter Ghosts''',<ref>Bogenn, Tim. BradyGAMES ''Luigi's Mansion'' Official Strategy Guide. Page 23.</ref> are a type of [[ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Waiters are light-blue [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] dressed in waiter outfits, including a white apron, a small, white hat, and a black bowtie. They carry a plate of food on each hand.

===Behavior===
Waiters are the servants of [[Mr. Luggs]], and work to continuously supply him with more food. Whenever [[Luigi]] starts vacuuming Mr. Luggs's plate of food, the Waiters arrive to provide him with more food. Unlike most other ghosts, Waiters cannot damage Luigi nor do they focus on him.

==History==
[[File:Dining Room LM3DS.png|thumb|left|200px|''Luigi's Mansion'' (Nintendo 3DS) screenshot of a Waiter serving food to Mr. Luggs in the Dining Room]]
In ''Luigi's Mansion'', only two Waiters are ever encountered, both in the [[Dining Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Dining Room]]. They only have 10 [[Heart Point|HP]], like with [[Gold Ghost]]s. Like with most regular ghosts, Luigi can defeat a Waiter by stunning it with his [[flashlight]] and then vacuuming it into the [[Poltergust 3000]]. Waiters are unaffected by elemental attacks despite their [[Ghost's heart|heart]] neither having fire, water, nor ice protection.

Before battling Mr. Luggs, Luigi has to get him to run out of food. The main method of accomplishing the task is by capturing the Waiters so that Mr. Luggs cannot be resupplied with food. Alternatively, Luigi can stun the Waiters using his flashlight, causing them to disappear and reset, therefore giving Luigi a brief chance to vacuum up Mr. Luggs's food without capturing the two Waiters. When Luigi does this, Mr. Luggs becomes enraged, as usual, but the Waiters flee in terror and do not return.
