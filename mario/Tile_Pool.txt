The '''Tile Pool''' is a location in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is the ocean located within [[The Bitlands]] and is the main setting for Chapter 3-2: Bloops Ahoy.

==History==
===Chapter 3-2: Bloops Ahoy===
It is found in chapter 3-2 after [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]] has stolen [[Tippi]]. The Tile Pool is a water level and contains many enemies such as [[Cheep-Cheep]]s and [[Blooper]]s. Here [[Mario]] and [[Four Heroes of Light|company]] find [[Thudley]] the [[Pixl]]. At the end of this sub-chapter, Mario must fight a [[Super Blooper|Giant Blooper]]. The [[Super Blooper|Giant Blooper]] is defeated by striking the one red tentacle three times either by using Bowser's Flame Breath or by using Thudley to smash them when they briefly appear on the bottom of the screen.
