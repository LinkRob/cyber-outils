'''Air Lift''' is a special move used by [[Parakarry]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Parakarry can use this move once he reaches the [[Super Rank]]. Using Air Lift requires three [[Flower Point|FP]]. 

==Usage==
To use Air Lift, the player must press the {{button|n64|a}} button repeatedly to fill up the gauge. If successful, Parakarry picks up the enemy and removes them from the battle. However, this doesn't work on bosses and the player won't earn any [[Star Point]]s from this.

==Trivia==
*Air Lift functions similar to [[Lady Bow]]'s [[Spook]] technique and [[Kalmar]]'s [[Up & Away]] moves. However, both Spook and Up & Away instead have a chance of removing every enemy except for just one.
