'''Chomp Chomps''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are golden and stronger versions of [[Chain Chomp|Chomps]] that are only found in the secret underground area of [[Bean Valley]].

== Battle ==
[[File:Chomp Chomp biting attack.gif|frame|left]]
In battle, Chomp Chomps can attack by biting but they are also capable of using the deadly [[Carni-Kiss]] move which can deal very high physical damage to one character. They have relatively high [[Heart Point|HP]] as well as high defense and attack.

=== Strategy ===
Chomp Chomp's weakness is thunder so [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] or [[Shocker (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Shocker]] will do well against them.
