The '''Circuit Special''' is an unlockable kart body in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. Due to it resembling a modern formula one care, the Circuit Special is the spiritual successor of the Sprinter from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. The vehicle's color scheme changes depending on which character is used.
