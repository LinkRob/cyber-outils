'''Bubble Save''' is a [[Defensive Power Shot]] which appears in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, the character creates several bubbles, which brings the ball toward them, allowing the character to return it.

==In ''Mario Tennis: Power Tour''==
In Story mode, Bubble Save is mainly used by [[Barb]]. However, it can be unlocked for [[Clay]] or [[Ace]] once they reach L4 Spin and L4 Speed.
