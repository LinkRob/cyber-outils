'''Lil' Penguin Lost''' is the second [[mission]] of [[Cool, Cool Mountain]] in [[Super Mario 64]] and it's [[Reissue|remake]]: [[Super Mario 64 DS]].

== Description ==
To collect the second [[Power Star]] of [[Cool, Cool Mountain]], [[Mario]] needs to bring a lost [[Penguin|Baby Penguin]] to it's Mother. The baby penguin is found at the start of the level, where Mario can jump up the mountain grab him. Once he gets the penguin, he must scale down the mountain to find it's mother. Once he has given the baby penguin to it's mother, the Star will spawn and he can collect it. It is NOT the baby penguin on the roof next to the Mother.
