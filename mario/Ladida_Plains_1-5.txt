'''Ladida Plains 1-5''' is the fifth level of [[Ladida Plains]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Layout ==
When visiting the 3rd area, you spot colored winged mushrooms; when passing by from first to last, it shows the colors of flowers to hit. Hitting flowers in the right order will open the new area in the pipe. Even the music changes.

== Collectibles ==
=== Toads ===
*'''Toad 1''': Stand on the spot with coins formed into an arrow going up. Use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe to go up and get on the orange platform.
*'''Toad 2''': Rage on top of the bridge, turn left and jump over the orange obstacle.
*'''Toad 3''': Use the Joy vibe to rise up or use the [[Gloom]] vibe to grow the plant, then go up the plant. Move to the left and blow the strange fluff cloud with the Joy vibe, then go pass by it.

=== Puzzle A Piece 8 ===
Go to the bottom floor in the second area and move right until you reach the dead-end.
