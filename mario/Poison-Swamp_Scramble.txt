'''Poison-Swamp Scramble''' is a Time Attack Challenge in [[New Super Mario Bros. U]]. The setting of the course is based on the Soda Jungle level, Bridge over Poisoned Waters.

== Objective ==
The player must reach the Goal Pole in 80 seconds or less by running on top of multiple rolling logs and moving Brick Blocks. Enemies in this challenge that the player must avoid are Koopa Troopas, and Paratroopas, and Goombas.
