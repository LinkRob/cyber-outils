The '''Wallopoid''' is an enemy in [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']].

== Description ==
Wallopoids are rectangular enemies of varying sizes comprised of varying terrain. They have the face of the snarling face of [[Void Kong]].

== Behaviour ==
Wallopoids masquerade as walls, but slam down to attack their prey when a target comes close to their territory; the amount of damage dealt is dependent on whatever material makes them up. A few seconds after it slams down, the Wallopoid rises back to its vertical position. Breaking off any piece of a Wallopoid's body kills it, causing it to drop gold. A Wallopoid cannot be interacted with from behind.

== In ''Donkey Kong Bananza'' ==
[[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] first encounters Wallopoids during his stay in the Groove Layer, and many areas afterward feature them.

=== Battle Challenges ===
Four Wallopoids are featured in the Groove Layer's second battle challenge. The Feast Layer's second and fifth Battle Challenges feature ten and five, respectively.

== Behind the Scenes ==
The name "Wallopoid" comes from the word "wallop," with influence from "wall." The "-oid" suffix denotes a similarity to something, and also references [[VoidCo|Void Company]], the creators of the Wallopoids.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, the Wallopoid is called ''Wōro'', from a transliteration of "wall." In Spanish, it is called ''Muróide'', meaning "akin to a wall" or "something like a wall (''Muro'', in Spanish, means "wall."). Italian translates its name as ''Muroduróide'', from ''muro'' ("wall"), ''duro'' ("hard"), and the suffix -''óide'' (which denotes similarity to something).
