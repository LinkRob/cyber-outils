'''Ms. Mowz''' is a [[Little Mouser]] who serves as [[Mario]]'s optional final partner in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. She is a master thief who also owns the badge shop in [[Rogueport]] called [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] as a cover.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Ms. Mowz is a white [[Little Mouser]] who wears red high-heels and a red mask, and she has a heart-shaped tail.

===Personality traits===
Ms. Mowz constantly flirts with Mario, which tends to annoy [[Flurrie]] and [[Goombella]], with the latter occasionally calling Ms. Mowz a floozy. [[Koops]] tends to get envious when Ms. Mowz flirts with Mario, suggesting he has a crush on her, despite already being a relationship with [[Koopie Koo]]. 

===Powers and abilities===
Ms. Mowz can sniff out any type of item, including [[Coin]]s, general items, and [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]]. During battle, Ms. Mowz has the ability to cut through [[Defense]]. Ms. Mowz can also use [[Kiss Thief]] to steal items and [[badge]]s from enemies.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Mario first encounters Ms. Mowz in [[Hooktail Castle]], where she is plundering the treasure room. Other times Mario encounters Ms. Mowz is in [[The Great Tree]] and the [[Glitz Pit]].

Later on, Ms. Mowz posts a bulletin in the [[Trouble Center]]. She wants the [[Attack FX B]] badge, which is hidden so well in [[Hooktail Castle]] that even Ms. Mowz cannot discover it. When Mario meets her again on the rooftop of the Lovely Howz of Badges, he finds that Ms. Mowz deliberately hid the badge to determine if it was worth joining Mario on his journey. Ms. Mowz is very impressed with Mario, and decides to join him on his journey.

After the events of the game, Ms. Mowz returns to running her badge shop.
