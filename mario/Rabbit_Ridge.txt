{{Nihongo|'''Rabbit Ridge'''|ラビットクレーター|Rabitto Kurētā|lit. '''Rabbit Crater'''}}, also known as ''The Last Word In Dedicated Wedding Planning.'' is a location in the [[Dark Side]] in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It's a location on the [[The Moon|Moon]]. Rabbit Ridge is the home of the [[Broodals]], and is ruled over by [[Madame Broode]].

==Layout==
Due to [[Madame Broode and Chain Chompikins|Madame Broode's]] love of vegetables, many statues of vegetables can be found scattered around the kingdom, primarily turnips. The largest one, however, is a carrot statue known as [[Rabbit Ridge Tower]].

==History==
===''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''===
In [[Rabbit Ridge Tower|Rabbit Ridge]], the [[Broodals]] challenge [[Mario]] to a rematch. However, if [[Mario]] loses all his health, he respawns back to the outside of [[Rabbit Ridge Tower|Rabbit Ridge]] where the Green [[Toad (species)|Toad]] is. Outside the tower, many generic rabbits wearing top hats can be found; they can be neither spoken with nor captured. No regional coins exist in this kingdom, and as with the [[Moon Kingdom]] and [[Darker Side]], [[Mario]] experiences lighter gravity except while in the bonus areas.

==[[Power Moon]]==
=== Regular Power Moons Locations === 
*'''02: Captain Toad on the Dark Side!''': Received from [[Captain Toad]] after finding him near the top of Rabbit Ridge Tower.
*'''03: Breakdown Road: Hurry!''': Behind the Odyssey on a lower platform, the player can find a door that must be opened with a scarecrow, which leads to a bonus area. In this bonus area, Mario must complete the same objective as the "Down and Back Breakdown Road" Power Moon from the [[Wooded Kingdom]], but the player cannot capture any of the [[Bullet Bill]]s or [[Banzai Bill]]s.
*'''04: Breakdown Road: Final Challenge!''': In the area with the "Breakdown Road: Hurry!" Power Moon, Mario must complete the same objective as the "Below Breakdown Road" Power Moon from the Wooded Kingdom, but the player cannot capture any of the Bullet Bills or Banzai Bills.
*'''05: Invisible Road: Rush!''': Obtained after traversing this secret area and defeating all three Big Poison Piranha Plant at the end without the aid of [[Cappy]]
*'''06: Invisible Road: Secret!''': Found in a [[Treasure Chest]].
*'''07: Vanishing Road Rush''': Found at the end of a secret area similar to [[Metro Kingdom]]'s '''Motor Scooter Daredevil!''' mission area, which has to be beaten without the aid of motor scooters and Cappy.
*'''08: Vanishing Road Challenge''': Found in the '''Vanishing Road Rush''' mission area on a side road. The location is identical to '''Full-Throttle Scooting!'''.
*'''09: Yoshi Under Siege''': Obtained after eating ten [[Berry|berries]] as [[Yoshi]] in a secret area similar to Metro Kingdom's '''Moon Shards Under Siege''' mission area.
*'''10: Fruit Feast Under Siege''': Obtained after eating 20 berries as Yoshi in the '''Yoshi Under Siege''' mission area.
*'''11: Yoshi on the Sinking Island''': Obtained after eating ten berries as Yoshi in a secret area.
*'''12: Frust Feast on the Sinking Island''': Obtained after eating 20 berries as Yoshi in the '''Yoshi on the Sinking Island''' mission area
*'''13: Yoshi's Magma Swamp''': Obtained after eating ten berries as Yoshi in a secret area similar to [[Luncheon Kingdom]]'s '''Magma Swamp: Floating and Sinking''' mission area.
*'''14: Fruit Feast in the Magma Swamp!''': Obtained after eating 20 berries as Yoshi in the '''Yoshi's Magma Swamp''' mission area.
*'''15: Found with Dark Side Art 1''': On one of the vegetables on the bottom part of the main area, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows [[Poochy]] digging on a glowing spot in the [[Cascade Kingdom]]. If Mario travels to the Cascade Kingdom and ground pounds the spot shown by the Hint Art, he will be rewarded with a Power Moon.
*'''16: Found with Dark Side Art 2''': Behind one of the vegetables on the bottom part of the main area, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows a helipad with a Power Moon shown above a yellow object. Mario must travel back to the Metro Kingdom and warp to the Helipad. If he ground pounds the yellow design on the ground next to the Helipad, he will be rewarded with a Power Moon.
*'''17: Found with Dark Side Art 3''': Behind one of the vegetables on the bottom part of the main area, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows a large square with two [[Regional Coin]]s and a Power Moon on the left side. Mario must travel back to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and ground pound a specific spot in the garden to be rewarded with a Power Moon. 
*'''18: Found with Dark Side Art 4''': On a wall on the bottom part of the main area near the Binoculars, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows a large circle along with eight smaller circles and a Power Moon in one of the smaller circles. Mario must travel back to the [[Cloud Kingdom]] and ground pound the middle of a circle sticking out of the ground in the northwest of the main area to be rewarded with a Power Moon.
*'''19: Found with Dark Side Art 5''': By dropping off to an area below the Binoculars in the main area, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows a wooden [[Shiverian]] sign with an exclamation mark above it. Mario must travel back to the [[Snow Kingdom]] and must ground pound the top of the wooded arch found in the Snowline Circuit to be rewarded with a Power Moon.
*'''20: Found with Dark Side Art 6''': On the second part of the main area on the wall of the platform with the Binoculars, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows four boxes: One with a pair of Binoculars, one with three umbrellas, one with [[Talkatoo]], and one with two columns and an "X" in the middle of the columns. Mario must travel back to the Seaside Kingdom and ground pound the space between two columns near the Rolling Canyon checkpoint to be rewarded with a Power Moon.
*'''21: Found with Dark Side Art 7''': Behind a pile of vegetables on the third part of the main area, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows the corner of an area in the [[Lost Kingdom]]. Mario must travel back to the Lost Kingdom and ground pound a corner of the ground between the red terrain and the dark brown terrain near the Odyssey to be rewarded with a Power Moon.
*'''22: Found with Dark Side Art 8''': To the left side of the wall between the third and fourth parts of the main area, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows two overhead shots of the Peronza Plaza in the Luncheon Kingdom. Mario must travel back to the Luncheon Kingdom and ground pound next to one of the columns in the Peronza Plaza to be rewarded with a Power Moon.
*'''23: Found with Dark Side Art 9''': To the right side of the wall between the third and fourth parts of the main area, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows eight columns with the last three being broken and a Power Moon found between the last two. Mario must travel back to the [[Lake Kingdom]] and ground pound between two columns with one missing at the Water Plaza Entrance to be rewarded with a Power Moon.
*'''24: Found with Dark Side Art 10''': Behind a pile of vegetables on the fourth part of the main area on the floor, the player can find a piece of Hint Art. The Hint Art shows Poochy digging on a glowing spot in the [[Ruined Kingdom]]. Mario must travel back to the Ruined Kingdom and ground pound the spot shown by the Hint Art to be rewarded with a Power Moon.
