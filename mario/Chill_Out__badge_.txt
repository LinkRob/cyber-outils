'''Chill Out''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Equipping it causes [[Mario]] to never succumb to an enemy's [[First Strike|first strike]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Chill_out_badge.png|thumb|left]]
Chill Out can be traded to [[Merlow]] at [[Shooting Star Summit]] for a price of three [[Star Piece]]s. Using it costs 2 [[BP]].
{{br|left}}
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Similar to the previous game, the Chill Out badge can only be obtain using Star Pieces. Mario must purchase this badge from [[Dazzle]] for three Star Pieces.
