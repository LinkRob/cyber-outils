'''Footstool Jump''' is a type of move in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', and has returned in every installment since.

==Description==
A character can execute a Footstool Jump by having them [[jump]] while just above an opponent's head. It can be done up to five times in succession before landing. Performing a Footstool Jump also allows for characters to meteor smash their opponent downward while using them as a spring.

While any character can perform a Footstool Jump, official screenshots have prioritized showcasing various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters use the move. A few screenshots from [[Smash Bros. DOJO!!]] showed [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] perform it on [[Wario]]. An official ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' screenshot shows [[Mario]] and [[Princess Peach]] performing a Footstool Jump on [[Bowser]] and [[Samus Aran|Samus]] respectively.

== Real-world influences ==
=== In competitive gameplay ===
Players have used footstool jump as means of performing combos including a technique used when the opponent is off-stage.

==Trivia==
