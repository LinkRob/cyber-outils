'''Pink Coin Pipe''' is a Special building that appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
The '''Pink Coin Pipe''' is a pink [[Pipe]] with a [[Star]] engraved on the pipe.

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' the Pink Coin Pipe is a Special building with no rarity. The player can buy it at the Shop after collecting all the [[Pink Coin]]s In every level, excluding [[World Star (Super Mario Run)|World Star]] levels. If the player touches the Pink Coin Pipe, the game will ask the player if they want to play the special course, but colored coins won’t be counted on the records.
