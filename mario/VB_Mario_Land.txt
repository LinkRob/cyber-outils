'''''VB Mario Land''''',<ref name=UKNM>[[:File:Official Nintendo Magazine - VB Mario Land.jpg|Official Nintendo Magazine (Great Britain) January 2010, pg. 62]]</ref> also known as '''''Mario Adventure''''',<ref>[[:File:Big N Magazine (Germany) VB.jpg|Big N magazine (Germany), July-August 2000, pg. 19]]</ref> is a canceled game of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] that was originally going to be released for the [[Virtual Boy]]. A single-level playable demo was unveiled at the Winter {{wp|Consumer Electronics Show}} 1995 in Las Vegas.<ref>[[:File:Nintendo Power - March 95.jpg|Nintendo Power Volume 70, March 1995, pg. 29]]</ref>

The game was to be a two-dimensional side-scrolling game that would also use three-dimensional perspectives, such as moving between the background and foreground of the level. It would star [[Mario]] as the main character, using classic ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''-style elements such as [[Warp Pipe|pipe]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Goomba]]s, [[Jumping Piranha Plant]]s, and [[? Block]]s. Although <i>VB Mario Land</i>'s story was not revealed, a large [[Wario]]-like figure that functioned similarly to a [[Thwomp]] was seen in the playable demo. In certain areas, an overhead view was displayed, much like in the game series [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'']]. The first level ends at the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]], and Mario can be seen entering a [[castle]].

A three-dimensional version of ''[[Mario Bros. (game)|Mario Bros.]]'' was mentioned to be implemented into ''VB Mario Land'' as a minigame, though this was later released as a separate game titled ''[[Mario Clash]]''.<ref name=UKNM/>
