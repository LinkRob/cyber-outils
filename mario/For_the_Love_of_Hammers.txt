'''For the Love of Hammers''' is the third level in [[Hoohoo Mountain]] in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 2.

== Layout ==
Battle one consists of 6 [[Hammer Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Hammer Bro.

The final battle consists of 5 Hammer Bros, and 2 [[Beanie]]s. The enemy captain is a Hammer Bro.

After the final battle the player unlocks 3 Hammer Bros. Repeating the level again can unlock one more Hammer Bro.
