'''Kamek the Magikoopa''' is one of the two secondary villains (alongside [[Koopa General]]) of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' and the tertiary antagonist in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. He is  [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]'s most loyal henchman. A Koopa with supernatural abilities, he acts as a sycophantic adviser and father figure and is also an informant to Bowser. Unfortunately, he also deals with the brunt of Bowser’s wrath when things don’t go as planned.

==Profile==
===Physical Appearance===
Kamek's appearance strongly resembles his [[Kamek|video game counterpart]].

===Speech===
He is able to talk and even speaks in a German accent reminiscent of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Lorre Peter Lorre] (and Sleet from ''Sonic Underground'') completely unlike his video game counterpart.

==Personality==
Kamek is a very loyal koopa who acts as Bowser's advisor. He demonstrates some level of sadism, as seen by his facial expression when Bowser destroys the Penguin Kingdom, as well as when he is torturing [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]]. He is also much more friendly with Bowser and is the main one Bowser opens up to compared to the other koopas.

== Biography ==
Kamek resides in the [[Dark Lands]] serving Bowser. One night, he commanded [[Koopa Troop|Bowser's army]] to defeat the army of penguins in [[Snow Kingdom (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Snow Kingdom]] in which they successfully did and retrieved the [[Star]].
No sooner does Bowser finish singing this than Kamek came to inform him about Mario's arrival into the Mushroom Kingdom; however, Bowser said he is not concerned about this. Then other members of the Koopa Troop alerted him to Mario's presence, causing Bowser to see this stranger as a rival for Peach's affections. Afterward, he has Luigi brought before him and interrogates him on Mario's whereabouts.

Bowser's army along with Kamek intervened with the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] with Bowser wanting to marry [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]]. As Peach saw Kamek torturing [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]], she reluctantly agreed.

Kamek was a marriage officiant about to announce Bowser and Peach's "marriage". However, Peach interrupted him by punching him in the face.

He was last seen in Brooklyn during the final fight alongside Bowser while levitating the Super Star with his magic. It is unknown what happened to Kamek afterwards, but it is likely he was presumably defeated by Mario and Luigi when they grabbed the Super Star and became invincible against Bowser and his Koopa army. Another possibility is that he escaped by teleporting back to the Dark Lands or just teleporting somewhere else.

=== ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'' ===
Kamek is seen inside of the [[Megaleg (Illumination)|Megaleg]] with [[Bowser Jr. (Illumination)|Bowser Jr]] now pledging his allegiance to him and serving as his adviser, much like he did to Bowser. Having successfully captured [[Rosalina (Illumination)|Rosalina]] from the Comet Observatory, Kamek witnesses Bowser Jr. imprison Rosalina inside of his spaceship and use her power for his own evil purposes.

He then carries Rosalina inside her cage via his wand and spectates as Bowser Jr. prepares her to be used as an energy source to power the Boomsday Device so that it can destroy the universe.

Bowser Jr. and Kamek then abduct Princess Peach's castle and take it into outer space with their spaceship with the Mario Bros., Yoshi, and Bowser still inside it in an attempt to take back Bowser and free him from his imprisonment. As Bowser Jr. confronts and engages in battle with the Mario Bros. and Yoshi, Kamek attempts to assist Bowser Jr. in the middle of the fight by using Megaleg to try and kill the Mario Bros. and Yoshi only to then be eaten and swallowed by the green dinosaur and be pooped back out as a Yoshi Egg. 

As the battle escalates and Megaleg manages to be completely destroyed by getting sucked into Bowser Jr.'s spaceship as well, Kamek, while still an egg, manages to still get out of the exploding giant mech just in time and lands back inside Bowser Jr.'s spaceship as he then manages to hatch out of the egg he was trapped in while Peach's castle, with the Mario Bros., Yoshi, and Bowser still inside of it, crashes into Honeyhive Galaxy.

As Bowser Jr. attempts to search for his father inside Honeyhive Galaxy while inside his spaceship, Kamek starts panicking, believing that they'll never be able to find him as Bowser Jr. tries to shut him up in annoyance. As Bowser Jr. manages to successfully find his father and the two of them reunite, Kamek teleports in to warn them of Wart's call, alerting them of Princess Peach and Toad coming to save Rosalina and thwart their plans.

During the final battle at Planet Bowser, as Bowser and Bowser Jr. power up the Boomsday Device by sucking the life energy out of Rosalina in their ultimate attempt to destroy the entire universe, Kamek teleports in and reports to them about the heroes arriving via the Comet Observatory to try and save Rosalina and stop their omnicidal goal. Kamek along with Bowser then spectate Bowser Jr. attempting to kill Mario and Princess Peach via an obstacle death course that Bowser Jr. specifically created to deal with the heroes. Kamek is never seen again after this, although it is assumed that he managed to flee and escape before things got ugly.

Whether or not Kamek will try to free Dry Bowser and Bowser Jr. from their current imprisonment is currently unknown.

==Trivia==
* While his name was shown in the credits, it was never addressed in the entire film.
* Him impersonating Peach for Bowser while he rehearses for his wedding references games such as ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', where he dresses up as her to trick the Mario Bros. into thinking that they've won. 
** This could also be a homage to the first draft of the critically panned and box office bomb ''Mario Bros. Movie'' from 1993, where an early version of his character creates an illusory castle where he impersonated Daisy to capture Mario.
* It's never stated if he is Bowser's parental guardian like in the games, though this could change in the sequel .
* Kamek is seen on his signature broom in only one scene of the film.
* He speaks with a type of German accent.
* Kamek along with [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]], [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]], [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] are the only five characters in the first film to ever visit [[Brooklyn]].
* Kamek's voice actor has also voiced Mr. Dinkles in ''Trolls Holiday''.
** Kamek's voiced actor has also voiced URL and Barbados Slim in ''Futurama'' after it was revived in 2023, replacing John DiMaggio as the original voice for URL and Barbados Slim in the previous seasons of the show, the same year the first Mario film released.
