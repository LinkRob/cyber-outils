'''Prince Florian''' is the prince of the [[Flower Kingdom]] and the deuteragonist of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Prince Florian is a green caterpillar-like creature. His eyes are black ovals stretched vertically with small white dots for pupils, and he has an orange nose. He has four arms ending in hands in white gloves and brown boots and a red cape. He wears a gold crown.

== Game appearances ==

=== ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ===
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', Prince Florian invited [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], [[Blue Toad]], [[Yellow Toad]], [[Toadette]], and the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] to [[Flower Kingdom|his kingdom]]. However, after [[Bowser]] steals a [[Wonder Flower]], causing his [[Koopa Clown Car]] to merge with Prince Florian's castle to create [[Bowser Castle (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|a massive semi-sentient fortress]], Florian joins the visitors to his kingdom and helps them stop Bowser's mad plans.

=== ''Mario Tennis Fever'' ===
In ''[[Mario Tennis Fever]]'', Prince Florian is a referee in the Flower Kingdom court. 

== Other game appearances ==

=== ''Tetris 99'' ===
In ''[[Tetris 99]]'', Prince Florian makes a cameo background appearance of the ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' theme.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Prince Florian derives his name from the present active participle of Latin Flōrēre, which means "to blossom." Additionally, in German, there is a name Florian that comes from the same root.
