'''Para-Chain Chomps''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Para-Chain Chomps are varieties of [[Chain Chomp]]s with a pair of [[wings]]; aside from this, Para-Chain Chomps have no physical differences from their parent species.

===Behavior===
A Para-Chain Chomp can fly around using its wings. Para-Chain Chomps can fly when chained and lunging, and when unchained, Para-Chain Chomps can [[jump]] higher as they travel across the screen.
