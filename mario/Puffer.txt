'''Puffers''' are an elephant-like enemy featured in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. Their attack is to shoot energy balls at a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. Puffers inhabit [[lava]]-filled areas, mainly [[Blargg's Boiler]], but one is also encountered in [[Magma Castle]].
