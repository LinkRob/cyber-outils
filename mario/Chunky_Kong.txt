'''Chunky Kong''' is a gorilla and a playable [[Kong]] featured in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. Chunky is the older brother of [[Kiddy Kong]] and cousin of [[Dixie Kong]] and [[Tiny Kong]]. He is one of three [[Kong]]s to not physically appear in any game not made by [[Rare]], the other two being [[Kiddy Kong]] and [[Swanky Kong]].

==In ''Donkey Kong'' franchise==
===''Donkey Kong 64''===
Chunky was freed by [[Lanky Kong]] in the level [[Frantic Factory]]. Before he was freed, he indicates that he doesn't like heights. Despite his brawny build, he acts somewhat cowardly and childish. One example of this is when the player selects him in the [[Tag Barrel]], he gets scared, shakes his head 'no' and tries to convince the player to choose Tiny Kong instead (although, when not highlighted in the 'select spotlight,' strangely he is shown to be more enthusiastic about being selected). His weapon is the [[Pineapple Launcher]], his instrument is the [[Triangle Trample]], and the potions each enable him to do [[Hunky Chunky]], turning gigantic, [[Primate Punch]], unleashing a very powerful punch which can smash down some doors and walls, Burp Skill, in which Chunky releases a giant belch and pats his stomach, and [[Gorilla Gone]], turning temporarily invisible. Chunky can carry boulders and other heavy items that the other Kongs cannot carry.

In [[Fungi Forest]], Chunky is the Kong selected in the second boss fight against [[Dogadon]]. He is the fifth and final Kong who fights K. Rool in his boxing match. Chunky ultimately defeats K. Rool using a combination of [[Gorilla Gone]], [[Hunky Chunky]], and [[Primate Punch]].

===''Donkey Kong Country 3'' (Game Boy Advance)===
Chunky made a brief cameo appearance in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3 (Game Boy Advance)|Donkey Kong Country 3]]'' for the [[Game Boy Advance]], in the third challenge of [[Funky's Rentals]], as one of the Kongs whom [[Dixie Kong]] and [[Kiddy Kong]] had to protect from being kidnapped by the [[Kremling]]s.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
Chunky makes a cameo appearance as a [[sticker]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and as a [[spirit]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''==
{{sectstub}}
Chunky makes a cameo appearance as a background element, cheering for Donkey Kong and sitting next to [[Diddy Kong]].
