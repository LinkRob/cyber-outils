A {{Nihongo|'''Soopa Striker'''|スーパーシューター|sūpāshūtā|Super Shooter}} is a stronger variant of [[Koopa Striker]]s and an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
A Soopa Striker is a [[Koopa Troopa]] variant of the [[Koopa Striker]]. It even shares complimentary clothing of a gray-and-white striped T-shirt and shoes as well as grey shorts. A Soopa Striker's black-and-white-checkered shell is reminiscent to the [[Wikipedia:Ball (association football)|soccer ball]]. It has black eyebrows unlike the Koopa Striker and [[Toopa Striker]].

When the player approaches the Soopa Striker, it will kick a shell towards to player. The player can counter the shell and shoot it towards the Soopa Striker. The player can stomp the enemy. Additionally, the [[Pixl]]s can defeat the enemy. The Soopa Striker may drop the [[Turtley Leaf]] upon its defeat.

== In ''Super Paper Mario'' ==
Soopa Strikers were [[Koopa Troop]] members. In [[Bowser's Castle]], [[Count Bleck]] teleported the Koopa Troop, [[Bowser]], [[Princess Peach]] and [[Luigi]] to [[Castle Bleck]]. After Bowser and Peach's "wedding", [[Nastasia]] brainwashed the Koopa Strikers to serve Bleck.

==Behind the scenes==
=== Etymology ===
The word "Soopa" of Soopa Striker is a portmanteau of "super" and "Koopa," indicating that they are stronger than Koopa Strikers.
