'''World 1-Cannon''' is the first [[cannon]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', and it leads to [[World Mushroom (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Mushroom]]. This [[Cannon]] has green [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, and a [[Red Ring]]. In order to unlock this cannon, you must find [[World 1-Fortress (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1-Fortress]]'s secret exit.

==Walkthrough==
This level is an autoscroller where Mario automatically runs, even if you don't do anything. Your main focus on this level is to control your jumps to get across from platform to platform. This level is mainly composed of [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s and is quite easy while you hold the jump button to get additional height. If you complete this level, you will unlock [[World Mushroom (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Mushroom]].
