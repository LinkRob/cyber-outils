'''Peach Field''' is the third playable court of the [[Mushroom Tourney]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
It looks like a grassy field on top of a large rocky plateau. There is an arch on either side that has the logos for [[Nintendo]] and [[Square Enix]], past the baskets. The gimmick of this court is that [[Lakitu]] holds the basket on his fishing line. [[Lakitu]] and the basket only appear if a character approaches the end of the court with the ball and only then if that basket is designated to that team. If one or more of these conditions are not met, Lakitu does not appear, preventing the player from scoring if they throw the ball from the other team's area. Also, [[Cheep Cheep]]s bounce around and can be touched for an extra [[coin]].

Peach Field can be seen in the background of [[Koopa Troopa Beach (court)|Koopa Beach]] and [[Sunset Beach]].
