'''At the Chomp Wash''' is a 4-player [[List of mini-games in Mario Party 8|mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' and ''Mario Party: The Top 100''.

==Opening==
During the game's intro, a Chain Chomp wrecks paint cans, causing paint to cover his body.

==Gameplay==
The players have to clean the [[Chain Chomp]] first to win. The Chain Chomp will have five spots of paint on him, each in different colors.

==Controls==
*Shake the Wii Remote up and down or side to side to clean the Chain Chomps.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The "Chomp Wash" part of the minigame title is a play on "car wash."
