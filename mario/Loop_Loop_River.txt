|caption = [[Mario]] near the highly toxic Loop Loop River.}}'''Loop Loop River''' is the fifth level of World 3 in the game ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Layout==
Loop Loop River is very similar in color and appearance to the previous level [[Strike Lake]].

==History==
[[Mario]] continues his search for the Wiggler Segments. He battles though different other enemies until he encounters the Wiggler Segment. The Wiggler Segment sees Mario as a threat and starts to battle him. Mario defeats it and the Wiggler Segment joins Mario's side. Mario then grabs the [[Comet Piece]] and leaves the level.

==Secret Exit==
To get to the Secret Exit, Mario must ride the lilypad to a north-center cave. He must make his way around (also collecting the Tree Stump scrap) and battle enemies to reach the end. This exit leads back to the second level The Bafflewood.

==Notes==
Mario must collect the Tree Stump scrap in order to process in the game. After beating this level, he must go back to Wiggler's Tree House and give the Wiggler one of his segments. He must paperize the Tree Stump to grab the Coment Piece to jump to the seventh level, [[Rustle Burrow]].
