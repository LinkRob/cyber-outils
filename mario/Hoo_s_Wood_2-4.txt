'''Hoo's Wood 2-4''' is the fourth level of [[Hoo's Wood]] in the game, ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. Peach should climb the ladders, avoiding enemies and should not fall.



== Collectibles ==
*'''Puzzle Piece''': When reaching the top of the first stage, before getting in the pipe, get the puzzle piece on your left.
*'''Minigame''': When reaching the second new area of the stage, you walk pass by it.

== Toads ==
* '''Toad 1''': When Peach jumps on the brown and green platforms, she should use the [[Gloom]] vibe to run faster on the long blue falling platform to the end.
* '''Toad 2''': The [[Fangs]] are blocking the way, use the [[Rage]] vibe and jump into the pipe. Then smash the green-polka dot blocks and use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] to blow the windmill. Jump over the pipe, now smash the box.
* '''Toad 3''': Before going down the ladder, hit the box first.
