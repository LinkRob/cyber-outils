'''Daphne''' is one of the secondary antagonists in ''[[Mario + Rabbids: Sparks of Hope]]'' (alongside [[Midnite]] and [[Bedrock]]).

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Daphne is a slightly taller Rabbid, fused to a rare species of nocturnal climbing rose, so she possesses two tentacles starting from her back and ending in apparently sentient buds. Wearing a pink dress resembling petals and green pants, she also wears a hood very resembling the head of a Piranha Plant or the pouch of a Nepenthes, with bangs resembling fangs. She has a fringe of blue hair covering her eyes and two brambles sprouting at her sides.

===Personality===
{{Sectstub}}
She is very cunning, mischievous and cruel.

== History ==
Daphne is an entity born from the fusion of [[Rabbid]] DNA with a variety of climbing rose. Her appearance is that of a rabbit with blue bobbed hair concealing her eyes, and a pair of vine-like tentacles sprouting from her back. Her outfit consists of a dress evoking rose petals, green shorts, yellow shoes and a green hood evoking the upper jaw of a [[Piranha Plant]].

Daphne is one of the [[Sparks Hunters]] created by Cursa to hunt down [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Sparks]] across the galaxy. She first distinguished herself at [[Palette Prime]], where she helped [[Bedrock]] flee the planet after it had been defeated in battle by [[Mario]] and his friends. She reappeared alongside Bibi and [[Midnight]] at Cursa's stronghold, showing herself to be the only Spark Hunter to remain in her mistress's good graces by delivering 3 Sparks, which the latter promptly absorbed.

Daphne later traveled to [[Barrendale Mesa]], where she captured [[Momma]] before impersonating her. As Mario and his gang finished repairing the windmill, Daphne sent them a radio transmission inviting them to celebrate their success in her garage. On their return, the heroes were ambushed by Daphne, who threw them into the [[Darkmess Tentacle]]. In the ensuing battle, the Huntress of Sparks was defeated.

Moments later, Cursa appeared on the battlefield to deal the final blow to the heroes. ([[Rosalina]], who was under Cursa's control, broke free of his hold long enough to summon a portal that sent the heroes back to Barrendale Mesa. After Cursa's defeat, [[Edge]] believes that her former friends have survived and will have to find a new profession.

== In ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'' ==
=== Combat ===
Daphne is a static enemy specializing in ranged attacks. Her main attack, [[Bramble Shot]], consists of firing 2 projectiles with such a wide range that they can reach any target, regardless of its location on the ground. These projectiles inflict a great deal of damage and are powerful enough to shatter any destructible obstacle in a single blow. Her [[Optimize Cover]] ability allows her to take complete cover behind even partial shelter. Although she never moves, she is able to [[Climb x1]] a position to get back to height in the event of a fall, and can return to her initial location at any time via the [[Square One]] ability. Finally, Daphne can use [[Slimy Swell]], a short-range area attack unleashing a shower of meteors applying an [[Ooze]] effect on her targets, to which she is immune.

Daphne spends the entire fight on the roof of Momma's garage, opposite the player's team location at the start of the fight. The fight itself is divided into 3 phases. Each time she loses all her health, Daphne provokes a [[Fresh Start]] during which the battlefield is reshaped, increasing the distance separating her from the player's team. Finally, Daphne is accompanied by a bestiary continually expanded by reinforcements appearing via portals and evolving in parallel with the progress of the battle. During phase 1, this bestiary includes [[Goomba]]s and [[Sea Stooge]]s. These are replaced in phase 2 by [[Squashette]]s and [[Lone Wolf|Lone Wolves]]. Finally, in the 3rd and last phase, Daphne's army is made up of [[Bob-omb]]s, [[Magnafowl]]s, [[Scoper]]s and [[Medician]]s.
