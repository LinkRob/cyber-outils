'''Chef Bros.''' (or '''Chef Brothers''') are a variety of [[Hammer Bro.]] that throws frying pans at [[Mario]]. They strongly resemble [[Hammer Bro]]s. with the primary exceptions being their weapons and they also wear chef hats. They made their first appearance in the game ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

A Chef Bro's main method of attack is throwing frying pans. The easiest way to defeat them is to jump on them to get rid of them. Mario can also [[capture]] Chef Bros. and use their frying pans to break open the stoney cheese blocks and kill enemies such as [[Spiny|Spinies]], stronger enemies such as [[Podoboo]]s are immune to the frying pans however.

==History==
Chef Bros. appear in [[Mount Volbono]] of the [[Luncheon Kingdom]], [[Mario]] must capture one of them and using his frying pans to break the cheese rocks to find a switch for a [[Power Moon]] many more Chef Bros. can be found around the kingdom and their abilities are necessary for either finding [[Power Moon]]s or traversing the area.
