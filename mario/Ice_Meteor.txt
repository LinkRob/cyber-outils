'''Ice Meteors''' are objects in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Description==
Ice Meteors are the direct opposite of [[Meteor]]s, as they fall from the sky into [[lava]] areas to create ice platforms that [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can [[skate]] across. The platform left by an Ice Meteor dissipates after a few seconds. The ice platforms are identical to those created by [[Ice Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
Ice Meteors appear only in the [[Shiverburn Galaxy]], specifically during the [[mission]] [[Prince Pikante's Peppery Mood]], where they fall from the sky into the [[lava]] pond on the Ice Magma Skate Planet, allowing Mario or Luigi to skate across to the [[Launch Star]] at the end of the planet.
