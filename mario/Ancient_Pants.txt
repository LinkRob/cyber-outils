The '''Ancient Pants''' are a [[clothing]] item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its remake, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It can be equipped by either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]].

==Description==
According to their in-game description, the Ancient Pants are said to be slightly moldy.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Ancient Pants are dropped by either [[Limbo Bro]] or [[Elite Limbo Bro]], but only if Mario and Luigi either use the [[Advanced Swing Bros.]] attack or have the [[Game Boy Horror SP]] equipped. The Ancient Pants increase [[Defense]] by 40 in the international versions but [[HP]] by 40 in the Japanese versions.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Ancient Pants increase Defense at 56, and they are only dropped by Elite Limbo Bros. Ancient Pants have a very low value, as they can only be sold for 5 coins.
