'''Mega Piranha Plants'''<ref>Musa, Alexander, and Geson Hatchett. ''Super Mario 3D World'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 23.</ref> (also called '''Super Piranha Plants'''<ref>Loe, Casey. ''New Super Mario Bros.'' Player's Guide. Page 13.</ref><ref>Bueno, Fernando. ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 14.</ref> or '''Big Piranha Plants'''<ref>Stratton, Steve. ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' PRIMA Official Game Guide.</ref>) are a larger variety of [[Piranha Plant]] which first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. They act just like normal-sized Piranha Plants except they're larger and are constantly snapping. They are ''never'' found inside pipes due to their gigantic size. Mario must shoot three fireballs at one to destroy the monstrous plant. When killed, they will commonly give up three to eight coins, instead of the usual one [[coin]].

Mega Piranha Plants are similar to [[Piranhacus Giganticus]], which debuted earlier on in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. A difference from Mega Piranha Plants is that Piranhacus Giganticus can still fit in [[Pipe]]s. Other related species include [[Prickly Piranha Plant]]s, [[Spiky Piranha Plant]]s, and [[Piranha Creeper]]s.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''New Super Mario Bros.''====
[[Image:Super Piranha Plant Painting.jpg|thumb|A Super Piranha Plant Lunging.]]
Super Piranha Plants are first introduced in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' They are harder to pass by with their large size and take a bit longer to defeat. Touching them will cause the player to get hurt. With its physical action, they are constantly biting. No matter where they player goes, however, they will stay in the same spot. They are commonly found in caverns and skies first appearing in [[World 2-3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-3]]. A Super Piranha Plant can be defeated with the effects of a [[Mega Mushroom]], a [[Blue Shell]], or a [[Starman]]. Additionally, tossing three fireballs at them with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] will also work.

A fully functional [[Super Venus Fire Trap]] also exists in the game's files but went unused in the game's final build.
{{br}}
====''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''====
[[Image:6-3nsmbwii.png|thumb|left|A Super Piranha Plant on a pot.]]
[[File:NSMBW Super Piranha Plant Render.png|thumb|Super Piranha Plant in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.]]
Super Piranha Plants return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' first appearing in [[World 2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2]]. They behave as they did in the previous game and can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or a [[Starman]]. They can still be frozen with the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] but due to their size, they will break free within an instant. Super Piranha Plants are rather common in [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]].

The [[Big Venus Fire Trap|Super Venus Fire Trap]] also appears in this game behaving similar to the smaller [[Venus Fire Trap]]s.

The [[Super Venus Fire Trap]] also appears in this game behaving similar to the smaller [[Venus Fire Trap]]s.
{{br|left}}
====''New Super Mario Bros. 2''====
[[Image:Super Piranha Plant Cave.png|thumb|right|A Big Piranha Plant seen in a cave.]]
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', Big Piranha Plants return, but they are slightly less common. They first appear in [[World 1-A (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1-A]] and behave as they did before. They can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Gold Flower]], [[Mega Mushroom]], [[Starman]], or a [[Invincibility Leaf]]. Big Piranha Plants are also among the enemies to be affected by the [[Gold Ring (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|Gold Rings]], when a [[Gold Big Piranha Plant]] is defeated, it provides bonus [[Coin]]s. The [[Big Venus Fire Trap]] also returns in this game however the fireballs it spits out are now normal-sized.
{{br|left}}

====''New Super Mario Bros. U/New Super Luigi U/New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''====
[[Image:Super Piranha Plant Plains.jpg|thumb|right|A Big Piranha Plant in the level [[Rise of the Piranha Plants]].]]
The Big Piranha Plants reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' being more common and serve the same role as they did in previous games. They can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]] and can be frozen for a split second with the [[Ice Flower]]. Due to their size, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] or [[Baby Yoshi]] cannot defeat a Super Piranha Plant but they can still spit out projectiles at one and [[Bubble Baby Yoshi]] can defeat them with his bubbles. Big Piranha Plants first appear in [[Acorn Plains]] in all three games.

The [[Big Venus Fire Trap]] makes a return in this game behaving as it did previously, additionally, the [[Piranhacus Giganticus]] returns from ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' this time only being found in [[Soda Jungle]].

====''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''====
[[Image:Super Piranha Plant 3D.jpg|thumb|left|A Big Piranha Plant as seen in ''Super Mario 3D World''.]]
Mega Piranha Plants make their 3D ''Mario'' debut in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. In this game, they are slightly less common mainly appearing in levels such as [[Tricky Trapeze Theater]]. Their means of attack is also different from a standard Piranha Plant, upon sight of a foe, the start munching then descend down dazing themselves in the process, they can then be stomped to be defeated. Additionally, the Mega Piranha Plant can be defeated with the effects of the [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Bell]], [[Starman]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Boomerang Flower]], [[Mega Mushroom]], [[Lucky Bell]], [[Invincibility Leaf]], and [[Invincibility Bell]]. 

In ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'', [[Cat Mega Piranha Plant]]s appear with the same appearance as regular-sized [[Cat Piranha Plant]]s.
{{br|left}}


====''Super Mario Odyssey''====
{{main|Mega Poison Piranha Plant}}
While regular Mega Piranha Plants do not appear in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', Mega Poison Piranha Plants appear, where they can be found in the [[Wooded Kingdom]], the [[Metro Kingdom]], and the [[Dark Side]].

====''Super Mario Maker 2''====
[[Image:Super Piranha Plant 3D Maker.png|thumb|right|A Big Piranha Plant]]
Mega Piranha Plants once again reappear in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' this time as placeable enemies. They are exclusive to the ''Super Mario 3D World'' style replacing the [[Piranhacus Giganticus]] in that style. A Mega Piranha Plant can be made by applying a [[Super Mushroom]] to a standard Piranha Plant. A Big Piranha Plant can be shaken to become a [[Big Fire Piranha Plant]] and it can also be given wings to become a [[Mega Para-Piranha Plant]].

====''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''====
Mega Piranha Plants return in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', first appearing in [[Piranha Plants on Parade]]. Some appear in giant Warp Pipes similar to ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' and ''New Super Mario Bros. U'', along with its related expansions and ports, while others appear outside of them similar to the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' games. In addition, a new [[Trottin' Piranha Plant]] variant of them make their debut.

===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
In ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', a regular Piranha Plant named [[Naval Piranha]] is transformed by [[Kamek]] into a giant-sized Piranha as a boss of [[World 3 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 3]].

===''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''===
Exclusively in the [[Wii U]] version of ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'', one Mega Piranha Plant reappears in the bonus level, [[Shadow-Play Alley]].

===''Mario Kart'' series===
====''Mario Kart Arcade GP VR''====
[[File:MKAGPVR - Screen1.jpg|thumb|Mega Piranha Plants in ''Mario Kart Arcade GP VR'']]
Mega Piranha Plants reappear in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP VR]]'', where they appear in the start of the track, where they bite down on patches of dirt making any racer who touches them spin out.

====''Mario Kart Tour''====
[[Image:Super Piranha Plant Tour.png|thumb|right|A Big Piranha Plant from ''Mario Kart Tour''.]]
In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', Piranha Plants come in a range of sizes, though they are all labeled in-game as simply "Piranha Plant". Mega Piranha Plants that are perched on large pipes appear in [[Tour Paris Promenade|Paris Promenade]], Paris Promenade 2, and Paris Promenade 3. They are also usually added as stage hazards in the R, T and R/T variants of various courses (with [[RMX Choco Island 1|RMX Choco Island 1R/T]] featuring the largest Piranha Plant in the game), and are also often found in the [[Steer Clear of Obstacles]] bonus challenges. A variant that is found on the ground can be seen in [[SNES Choco Island 2|SNES Choco Island 2R/T]] and the Steer Clear of Obstacles bonus challenge on [[Yoshi Circuit (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|GCN Yoshi Circuit]]. It acts identically to a normal Piranha Plant but it's larger size makes it harder to avoid. It can be bypassed harmlessly with the effects of a [[Bullet Bill]], [[Mega Mushroom]], or a [[Starman]]. It is immune to [[Shell]]s however.

====''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''====
[[Image:Super Piranha Plant Kart.jpg|thumb|left|A Big Piranha Plant in Paris Promenade.]]
In the ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' [[Booster Course Pass]] DLC, [[Paris Promenade]] returns and so does the Big Piranha Plant, behaving as it did previously. It can once again be bypassed harmlessly with the effects of a [[Bullet Bill]], [[Mega Mushroom]], or a [[Starman]].

===''Mario Party Superstars''===
[[File:Peach's Birthday Cake Prize.jpg|thumb|A Mega Piranha Plant seen on the Peach’s Birthday Cake postcard.]]
Mega Piranha Plants appear in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'' on the board [[Peach's Birthday Cake]]. When players land on the [[Happening Space]]s, [[Lakitu]] will offer them a [[Piranha Plant Egg]]. If the player chooses a large one (which costs 30 coins), Lakitu will drop a [[Mega Piranha Pod]] in the icing, which will sprout as a Mega Piranha Plant. When other players land on the Happening Space, the Mega Piranha Plants take away more coins than a normal-sized Piranha Plant.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Mega Piranha Plants (just called '''Big Piranhas''') are mentioned briefly by [[icaruspedia:Viridi|Viridi]] during [[List of Palutena's Guidance conversations|Palutena's Guidance]] dialogue for [[Piranha Plant]]. Unlike most other variations generally mentioned in order of appearance, they are listed amongst ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' debuts. [[Nipper Spore]]s, Big Fire Piranhas, Small Piranhas, and [[Fire Stalking Piranha Plant|Fire Stalking Piranha]]s are similarly listed out of order.

===''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''===
In the climax of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', a Mega Piranha Plant briefly traps [[Invincible Mario]] and Invincible Luigi in its mouth, before they force it to spit them out.
