After 10 minutes of unskippable cutscenes, you are thrown into the overworld and can move freely with your new party: [[Mario/Gallery/Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Mario]], [[Rabbid Peach]] and [[Rabbid Luigi]]. Collect the 5 coins located behind you at the start, as well as 4 sets of 3 coins on your way to the first battle.

Before the first battle starts, a few [[Ziggy|Ziggies]] will start shooting at your crew and [[Beep-0|Beep-O]]. Beep-O then receives an email, and gives each party members a weapon. Mario has the [[Lightning Shark]], Rabbid Peach has the [[Fuchsia Fury]], and Rabbid Luigi has the [[Dwarf Star]]. The battle then starts.

== Battle 1 ==
'''Turn limit to get a Perfect Rank: 1 Turn'''
[[File:1-1 Unlikely Heroes- Battle 1.png|thumb|474x474px|Battle Formation]]
'''Enemies: 2'''

=== '''Overview''' ===
The first battle is the firing tutorial, as all commands are disabled in the first battle except for your main weapon. To shoot at an enemy, press the A button while hovering over the weapon command. For this fight, there will be 2 Ziggies with 10HP. One of them is in the open, while the other is in partial cover.

=== '''Actual Battle''' ===
As you can't move in the first battle, all you have to do is shoot the enemies until they die. Enemies that are out in the open can always be attacked, while enemies behind partial cover will have a 50% chance of being hit unless you flank them from a certain direction (more on that later). Use your newly equipped weapons to shoot down the enemies. Note that even though the second Ziggy is hiding behind partial cover, the first shot you fire against him will always miss and hit the cover block.

== Road to Battle 2 ==
You can collect a set of 5 coins on your way to the next battle.

== Battle 2 ==
'''Turn Limit to get a Perfect Rank: 4 Turns'''
[[File:1-1 Unlikely Heroes- Battle 2.png|thumb|479x479px|Battle Formation]]
'''Enemies: 2'''

=== '''Overview''' ===
For the second battle of the tutorial, all the enemies are hiding behind full, indestructible cover, making your shots have a 0% chance of hitting the enemy. As mentioned in the tutorial, you have to flank these enemies from the side or behind to get a 100% chance to hit them.

=== '''Actual Battle''' ===
Use your newly acquired move action to move either to the yellow tiles on the battlefield, or flank them from behind. Try to hide behind full or partial cover to prevent getting hit by any remaining enemies after your turn. If you do get hit, you will lose some HP, but as long as you keep flanking and shooting the enemies, you can clear the battle with all of your heroes alive and within the 4 turn limit for a Perfect rank.

== Ancient Gardens - Overworld ==
[[File:1-1- Unlikely Heroes Post Gameplay 1.png|thumb|222x222px]]
Even if you completed a chapter, you can still freely move around the overworld while advancing to the next chapter. Grab 3 coins and keep going until you reach an intersection, where you can turn left to collect 3 coins and a [[Collectible Chest]] containing the [[Leafy World]] artwork.

Continue onto the main path to collect 3 coins and trigger a cutscene. You are then greeted with a puzzle-like maze all around you, but you can just walk through it onto the next chapter if you don't want to collect any collectibles. 
[[File:1-1- Unlikely Heroes Red Ring Challenge.png|thumb|224x224px]]
There's a [[Red Ring|red ring]] challenge in this area. Go through the ring, and 8 coins will spawn on the main path. Collect all 8 coins, and you can unlock a weapon. For this red ring, all the coins are situated on the main path. After collecting them, a Gold Collectible Chest will spawn containing Mario's [[Rumble Bee]], but you can't equip it for now.

There is a yellow sun button near the gold chest. Press it, and it will unlock two Rabbid Warp Pipes back the way you came. One leads to an area with a red block you can trigger to unlock another Rabbid Warp Pipe leading to a Collectible Chest containing a Mario Model. Head through the other opened Rabbid Warp Pipe to an area with a blue panel you can trigger to make the blue blocks disappear. Return to the main field where you first entered and look right to find another Collectible Chest is now available that holds a [[Garden Ziggy]] Model.

Now go left past a closed area with a green panel to find another Collectible Chest hiding behind two trees that holds the Grand Opening Soundtrack. Keep moving up until you spot a Rabbid Cannon you can now enter, and it will shoot you to the enclosed area with the green panel. Finally, head to the right of where you collected the red coins to find a now open Rabbid Warp Pipe down to a lower area, now unblocked, that hides a Collectible Chest holding the Prologue Soundtrack.
