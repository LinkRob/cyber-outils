The '''Thwomp Orb''' is an [[orb]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
The Thwomp Orb is a roadblock orb which can be applied to a [[space]]. If a character walks on a Thwomp Space, a [[Thwomp]] drops out of nowhere and lands on them, ending their turn prematurely. If a character is under the effect of the [[Metal Mushroom Orb]], the Thwomp tries to crush them, only to be deflected and therefore negating the effects of the Thwomp Orb.

==In ''Mario Party'' series==
===''Mario Party 6''===
[[File:Thwomp_Orb MP6.jpg|thumb|left|''Mario Party 6'' artwork]]
In ''Mario Party 6'', the Thwomp Orb can be obtained on [[Towering Treetop]], [[Faire Square]], and [[Clockwork Castle]]. It costs ten [[coin]]s at the [[Orb Shop]], but during a flash sale, it costs only five coins instead.

===''Mario Party 7''===
In ''Mario Party 7'', it can be obtained on [[Pyramid Park]], [[Neon Heights]], and [[Windmillville]].
