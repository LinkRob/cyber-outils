'''Sherry''' is a character in the [[Mario Golf (series)|''Mario Golf'' series]]. She made her first appearance in ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]].

==Physical description==
Originally, Sherry was a young 12-year old girl with fiery-orange hair tied into two short, upward-pointing ponytails each held together by a blue hair-twistie. Sherry also has green eyes, depicted as light blue in her artwork. She wears a yellow vest with two green buttons over a white-polo shirt, blue shorts, and orange golfing shoes with short, white socks. In the games, Sherry also wears a yellow golfing glove on her left hand.

In ''Mario Golf: Advance Tour'', Sherry is grown up, and her eye color is consistently light blue in both the game and in artwork. Her hair is shorter, nearly reaching down to her shoulders. Sherry now wears a bright green sleeveless shirt with a white collar, and tight blue shorts. She still retains her orange golfing shoes and glove. 

==In {{series|Mario Golf}}==
===''Mario Golf'' (Game Boy Color)===
Sherry is a playable character available from the start. 

===''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' ([[Nintendo 64]])===
This is Sherry's technical debut, and she can be transferred here through use of the [[Transfer Pak]] between both the Game Boy Color and Nintendo 64 versions of ''Mario Golf''.

===''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''===
Sherry is an unlockable character, and her partner in doubles is [[Grace]]. Sherry can be unlocked by winning against her in a match. She has a drive of 230 yards.
