The '''Ice Cream''' is an item in ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]''{{'}}s PC versions.

==Description==
The Ice Cream was made with peanuts and was the result of [[George Washington Carver]]'s various experiments with peanuts.

==In ''Mario's Time Machine''==
During [[Mario]]'s visit to [[Tuskegee]] circa 1915, the Ice Cream is given to him by [[Henry Ford]], who tells Mario to try it out to see if he likes it. Although Mario does not eat the Ice Cream, he gives it to one of Carver's students. The student tells Mario that she has been studying to the point of not having the time to eat, making her a bit grumpy. When the student is given the Ice Cream, she thanks Mario, recognizes it as being made from peanuts, and continues to talk about Carver and his peanuts.
