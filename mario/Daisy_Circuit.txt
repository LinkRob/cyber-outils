'''Daisy Circuit''' is a course in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' on the [[Star Cup]]. Daisy owns this course, hence the name.

==Layout==
[[File:MKW Daisy Circuit Lighthouse.png|thumb|left|The lighthouse on Daisy Circuit.]]
There are two circle turns that meet together into one track. The first of these circled pathways, there is a statue of [[Luigi]] and [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], arm in arm, walking together. There is also a statue of [[Baby Luigi]] and [[Baby Daisy]] playing with each other afterward. Later in this course there is a tunnel. After exiting the tunnel, racers pace a lighthouse and maneuver a U-turn before returning to the starting line. The course is raced at the time of sundown, because the sky is a little pink and mostly orange. In the background on a part of the track is the [[Daisy Cruiser]]. This is the first course in [[Star Cup]].
===Shortcuts===
There is a shortcut near the beginning of the track in the northwestern corner, where racers can travel up a set of stairs (which slows them down) with a [[Boost Pad]] and a place to trick at the end.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
[[File:MKT Daisy Circuit.jpg|thumb|right|Daisy Circuit]]
Daisy Circuit makes its classic course debut in ''Mario Kart Tour''. Compared the Wii version, the statues lack mouths while the eyes appear more noticeable. The tunnel that comes after the two statues is absent in the course. Additionally, they remade the Daisy Candy sign using Daisy's ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'' artwork.

* [[Sunshine Tour]]

== In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' ==
While it fails to return in the Wii U version, Daisy Circuit makes a return in the Nintendo Switch port via the Booster Course Pass. Its first shortcut at the northwestern corner is now blocked by a cutout and has a [[Glide Ramp]], meaning when racers go through it, they can [[Glider|fly over the loops]]. A trick ramp has been added following the tunnel, and [[Dash Panel|Dash Panels]] have been added to the turn at the south end of the track.
