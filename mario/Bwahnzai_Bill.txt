The '''Bwahnzai Bill''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Bwahnzai Bill.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Bwahnzai Bill resembles a hand cannon with a design that is based on a [[Banzai Bill]]. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Bwahnzai Bill deals around 55 to 65 damage. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Bounce (status effect)|Bounce]]. Its Super Effect also has a chance of dealing more damage to a [[Supporter]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Bwahnzai Bill as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain the Blaster after the player completes the Red Coin challenge at Sherbet Desert.
