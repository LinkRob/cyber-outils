'''Admiral Bobbery''', or just '''Bobbery''', is an elderly [[Bob-omb]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and [[Mario]]'s sixth partner.

==Biography==
===Past===
He used to be a proud sailor who loved the sea, but loved his wife [[Scarlette]] even more. While he was out at sea, Scarlette became very ill and died shortly after he returned from his voyage. The grief and nightmares caused by her loss made him lose the desire to ever sail again.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Admiral Bobbery is needed for navigation to [[Keelhaul Key]], yet he refuses to come. When [[Mario]] gets the letter from his late wife Scarlette, when Bobbery reads it he learns that she loves him so much, so after a quick change of mind, he comes with [[Flavio]] and the rest of the passengers to Keelhaul Key. When he is "dying", he wants a last [[Chuckola Cola]] as his wish. When Mario gives him the beverage, he "dies"; actually, he just fell asleep, so Mario woke him up, then Bobbery decided to join Mario's team, going onward to the fifth Crystal Star in [[Pirate's Grotto]], held by [[Cortez]].

After the events of the game, Bobbery decides to join Cortez's crew and returns to the sailing the seas for the rest of his days.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
Although Admiral Bobbery does not physically appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', he does make a cameo in the form of a [[Catch Card]].
