'''Bowser's Villa''' is the last world of ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It is located in Vibe Island, where [[Bowser]] has set his residence at. It was his castle, with conatins many types of enemies, having different vibes and powers. New enemies such as [[Thwomp]]s and [[Blue Podoboo]]s are also introduced here, [[Bowser]] is the leader and the boss of the world, also [[Army Hammer Bro.]] was the first boss to be defeated. Bowser was the only boss to have to rounds for battle. This is the time, [[Peach]] has to rescue the last captured ally, [[Mario]].

Common enemies in this World include, [[Hammer Bro]]s, [[Dry Bones]], [[Boo]]s, [[Podoboo]]s, [[Bob-omb]]s, and more.

== History ==
When Bowser heard of Vibe Island, he was interested to build his summertime Villa at the Island situated at the Island’s too. He had order his minions to kidnap Mario, Luigi and the Toads; Unfortunately he wasn't able to kidnap [[Peach]] mainly because she was out walking with [[Toadsworth]] and the [[Toad]].

The inside of the castle is filled with hazards such as [[lava]], [[Bowser-Ball 'n' Chain]]s, [[Firebar]]s, [[Security Thwomp]]s, [[Rockethand]]s, and more, the overall environment of the castle is dark with lighting, gray platforms, the sky outside is shown to be dark giving a space-like illusion suggesting the Villa is very high up above the Island.
