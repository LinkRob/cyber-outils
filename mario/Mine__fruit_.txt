'''Mines''' are fruit-like projectiles which only appear in the minigame [[Sink or Swim]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
Mines grow on top of the aquatic plants, where the solo character is for the entire mini-game. They have to throw mines at the three team members, who are [[swim]]ming in the pond below. When the solo character throws down a Mine, it sinks directly downward, and shortly after this, another Mine spawns in that character's hands. Whenever a Mine hits a team member, it cracks in half and knocks out the teammate, eliminating them from the mini-game.
