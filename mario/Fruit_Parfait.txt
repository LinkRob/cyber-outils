The '''Fruit Parfait''' is a [[recipe]] in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. [[Zess T.]]'s version of it restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 2 [[Flower Point|FP]].

[[File:Fruit_Parfait_SPM.png|thumb|left]]
In ''Super Paper Mario'', [[Dyllis]] makes a Fruit Parfait by mixing a [[Peachy Peach]] with a [[Snow Cone]]. Using a Fruit Parfait [[Frozen|freezes]] all enemies on the screen.
