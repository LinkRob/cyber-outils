'''Tires''' are wheels that allow players to move in ''[[Mario Kart]]'' games. Although they weren't customizable in the arcade and first six games, they are customizable for ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.
