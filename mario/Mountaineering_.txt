'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[Wiggler Race]] in [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It can be unlocked after clearing [[Badge House in Pipe-Rock Plateau]]. Clearing this course unlocks [[Swamp Pipe Crawl]], [[Angry Spikes and Sinkin' Pipes]], [[Bulrush Express]], Badge Challenge [[Wall-Climb Jump I]], [[Pipe-Rock Plateau Palace]] and KO Arena [[Pipe-Rock Rumble]], as well as a [[Super Mushroom]] badge.

This course has one [[Wonder Seed]] and no [[10-Flower Coin|10-Flower Coins]]. The main layout of the course includes many spike traps, zip tracks and crates. [[Trompette|Trompettes]] and [[Skedaddler|Skedaddlers]] are also present throughout the course.

== Trivia ==
If the player is losing the race, [[Talking Flower|Talking Flowers]] may drop a [[Super Star]] onto the course to help the player.{{SMBWNav}}
