The '''Battle Fort''' is a location that appears only in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It features only one secret [[Power Star]], obtained from completing the mission [[The Secret of Battle Fort]]. Aside from this, Battle Fort is the fourth course of VS mode.

== Description ==
Battle Fort is an enclosed area in the sky that consists of several ramps and [[platform]]s. It is patrolled by [[Heave-Ho]]s and features [[Big Steely|iron ball]]s rolling across. A [[Chuckya]] is located on the mesh wire platform up above, and [[Bob-omb]]s continuously rain down as the player moves about. Several [[Goomba]]s and Bricks are placed around the stage as well. [[Water bomb (object)|Water bomb]]s also randomly fall out of the sky.

==History==
[[File:Battle Fort SM64DS map.png|thumb|left]]
The entrance to Battle Fort is located in the [[Castle Courtyard]], to the back right. The entrance to Battle Fort is obstructed by [[Brick]]s, which can be destroyed by any character except [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], making him the only character unable to access the location in Adventure mode. However, Battle Fort is one of the courses featured in VS mode, which features [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] as playable characters.

Battle Fort is the fourth course of VS mode. All the enemies are removed, except the iron balls. Like in [[Sunshine Isles]], there are [[VS Item Block]]s with [[Wings]] allow them to fly around the stage regardless of who wears them.

Obtaining the course's only Power Star, [[The Secret of Battle Fort]], requires the player's character to collect [[Silver Star]]s, which can be found from destroying Goombas and iron balls. Once every Silver Star has been collected, the Power Star appears within the [[Star Sphere]].
