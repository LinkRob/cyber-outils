'''{{Infobox Enemy|name=Bone Goomba|image=PMCS Bone Goomba Idle Animated.gif|caption=|first=|games=''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''|homeland=|located=|leader=|species=[[Goomba]]|subspecies=|affiliates=|related=[[Dry Bones]]|attacks=Headbutts a single enemy while rocking a skull on its noggin.|defeated=|comics=}}'''Bone Goombas''' are a type of [[Goomba]] that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]].''

== Description ==
Similar to the appearance of the [[Goomba (Battle Card)|Goomba]], but are blue bodied and have a skull over their head. They also have yellow eyes, and less exaggeration eyebrows.

== Stats ==
'''HP:''' 12

'''Attacks:'''

* Body Attack ('''5''')
* Group Attack ('''5''')
* Blowback Attack ('''10''')

The Bone Goomba is only seen in [[Marmalade Valley]], and can still be found in post game.
