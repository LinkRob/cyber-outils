The Sugarstar is an enemy in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]. They first appear in [[The Sugarstar Trial: Across the Night Sky]]. Sugarstars also appear in [[The Final Test Wonder Gauntlet]] and [[The Final-Final Test Badge Marathon]]. Typically they fly together in a wavelength-like pattern swerving up and down.

There are two main types of Sugarstars, Green Sugarstars and Purple Sugarstars. Purple Sugarstars are much faster than Green Sugarstars. Like [[Dry Bones]], Sugarstars cannot be defeated and when hit, will reform after a few seconds.
