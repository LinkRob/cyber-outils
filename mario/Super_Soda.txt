The '''Super Soda''' is an item and recipe which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Since ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', it has been replaced by [[Fresh Juice]].

==Effects==
A Super Soda restores five [[Flower Point|FP]] and can cure [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] and other status effects making it a better version of the [[Tasty Tonic]]. Mario can make this item at [[Tayce T.]]'s in ''Paper Mario'' by cooking a [[Maple Syrup]], [[Honey Syrup]], or both together. A Super Soda first appears in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]. If [[Princess Peach]] told [[Kammy Koopa]] that Mario hated Super Sodas, she would put it their as if it were a "dangerous obstacle" for Mario. A Super Soda can be sold for 7 coins.
