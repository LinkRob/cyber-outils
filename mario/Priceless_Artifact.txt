'''Priceless Artifact''', spelled '''Priceless Artefact''' in British English releases, is a [[Treasure (Pikmin franchise)|treasure]] in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Hey! Pikmin|Hey! Pikmin]]''.

==Description==
The Priceless Artifact is a digital [[amiibo]] figure of [[Gold Mario]]. It has the exact appearance of the [[Colossus in Suspenders]], which is an amiibo of Mario, but is colored gold, just like how the [[Great Man]] is an amiibo of Silver Mario.

==In ''Hey! Pikmin''==
The Priceless Artifact is unlocked by scanning an amiibo of Gold Mario. After scanning it, the Priceless Artifact appears in one of the [[w:c:nintendo:Secret Spot|Secret Spots]].
