'''Pedal Power''' is a [[List of mini-games in Mario Party#Single-player mini-games|single-player mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party]]''. It requires the player to spin the joystick in order to light up the room to defeat a [[Boo]].

This mini-game serves as World 6-2 on [[Mini-Game Island]]. The player must defeat Boo to clear the game. If [[Boo]] catches up to the player, he will lose.

==Trivia==
*This is one of the four ''Mario Party'' mini-games that caused blisters to players using the palm of their hands to rotate the Control Stick faster, thus why Nintendo stopped developing mini-games that require rotating the Control Stick.
