'''Fire Drive''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
To wear this badge, [[Mario]] must pay three [[Badge Point|BP]]. In battle, Mario can use a new attack while wearing this badge. This attack costs five [[Flower Point|FP]] to use, and damages all of Mario's ground enemies and can burn his opponents as well.

Mario finds two Fire Drives while on his adventure. One of these badges is found at the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] and has a price of 100 [[coin]]s. The other Fire Drive badge is at the twentieth floor of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].
