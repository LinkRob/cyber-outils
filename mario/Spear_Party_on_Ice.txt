'''Spear Party on Ice''' is the fourth level of [[Joke's End]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 25.

==Layout==
The 1st battle contains 5 [[Chuck Guy]]s, 2 [[Parabeanie]]s, and 1 [[Spear Guy]]. The enemy captain is the Spear Guy.

The 2nd battle contains  5 [[Elite Chuck Guy]]s, 2 Parabeanies, and 1 Chuck Guy. The enemy captain is the Chuck Guy, and he knows [[Chance]].

The 3rd battle contains 2 Chuck Guys, 2 Parabeanies, 3 Spear Guys, and 1 Elite Chuck Guy. The enemy captain is the Elite Chuck Guy.

The final battle contains 2 Parabeanies and 6 Spear Guys. The enemy captain is a Spear Guy, and he knows Chance and [[Deny]].
