'''Prince Bully''' a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. He can be fought at his [[Prince Bully Blockade|blockade]] of [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]] and later on [[World Castle]], is  both the first and later second/penultimate boss of those worlds in order (assuming he's fought in order) and is overall the seventh and either the tenth or eleventh boss of the game

In the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'', there is a [[Cat Prince Bully]] who is fought as a boss in [[Mount Magmeow]], although only during the first mission. He acts the same as Prince Bully.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Prince Bully is a rather large silver [[Bully]] whose appearance resembles a cross between a furnace and a gladiator's helmet and has red (blue in the rematch) colored mow hawk and shoes, unlike the regular Bullies previously, he has a furnace-like mouth.

==Battle==
[[File:Princebully.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Prince Bully being battled]]
[[File:Blue Prince Bully.png|thumb|201x201px|Prince Bully's second fight]]
In battle, Prince Bully acts like regular Bullies and tries to ram into [[Mario]] and or co.. However, Prince Bully can also spit out fireballs that can hurt Mario. The only way to defeat Prince Bully is to ram him twice into one of the many clear pipes around the stage and attack his thinner body. The player must do this three times in total to defeat him. During the second rematch, Prince Bully will spit blue fireballs instead of the regular orange fireballs. These fire balls will track the player down until they pass you. At first, he will spit out three, but as you injure him, he will start to shoot four, and then five.

*'''Fire Ball Spit Out''': Prince Bully will spit fireballs at Mario trying to scorch him, in the rematch they will move in random directions.

==Trivia==
*Prince Bully is the second known Bully that is a boss. The first being [[Chief Chilly]] (though even though he's a [[Chill Bully]], they still are subspecies of a Bully) (the [[Big Bully]] and [[Chill Bully]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' are mini-bosses).
*Prince Bully is the second boss of ''Super Mario 3D World'' to be a larger version of a regular enemy, the first being [[Boss Brolder]].
**Coincidentally, both Prince Bully and Boss Brolder are very similar, in that they both are larger versions of an enemy who's name starts with the letter "B" (Prince Bully: Bullies, Boss Brolder: [[Brolder]]), both have fireballs as an attack, both bosses get fought in a World that is labeled in evens (Prince Bully: World 6, Boss Brolder: World 4) and both later on get fought mid-way in World Castle.
*Prince Bully is the first boss of ''Super Mario 3D World'' who does not get fought at the end of the stage.
**Prince Bully is also the first Enemy Blockade to be a boss, unlike previous Blockade were they were battles agents enemies or larger versions of said enemies.
* Prince Bully is one of the few bosses to not be fought in [[Boss Blitz]], the other two being [[Bowser/Appearances/Super Mario 3D World|Bowser]] and his alternate form [[Meowser]].
