The '''Dark-Light Device''' (also simply known as '''Dark-Light''' or '''Dark Light''') is an item in the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}. It first appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

== Description ==
The Dark-Light Device is a [[flashlight]] accessory that emits a rainbow-colored light in the direction that [[Luigi]] is pointing it. To this effect, the Dark-Light Device reveals objects hidden in a manner similar to an {{w|black light|ultraviolet lamp}}. When the invisible object is shone on for long enough, a number of [[Spirit Ball]]s are revealed, causing the object to become visible. The Spirit Balls need to be vacuumed up before they make the object become invisible again, causing the effects of the Dark-Light Device to wear off.

The Dark-Light Device also helps in capturing [[Boo]]s, find tracks leading to the [[Polterpup]], and helps in the fight against certain [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]]. It can also reveal [[coin]]s and [[gem]]s, as well as free [[Toad|Toad Assistants]] trapped in paintings.

==In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}==
===''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' / ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]''===
[[File:Darklightdevice.png|thumb|The Dark-Light Device being used in ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'']]
The Dark-Light Device is obtained by Luigi in the [[Lab]] of [[Gloomy Manor]] during the fourth mission, [[A-4: Visual Tracks]].

The Dark-Light Device can be used on [[Sneaker]]s to reveal them before they sneak behind Luigi to scare him. [[Greenie]]s start to taunt Luigi when the Dark-Light Device is used on them. The Dark-Light Device is required to free [[Mario]] from [[Mario's Painting|his painting]].

The Dark-Light Device can be used by holding the "{{button|3ds|Y}}" button. However, it has an energy gauge that is a rainbow-colored meter in the top right corner, so if the Dark-Light Device is used for too long, it overheats. The gauge can be upgraded by [[Professor E. Gadd]] by collecting [[treasure]] and leveling up.

===''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''===
The Dark-Light Device is now an add-on to the [[Poltergust G-00]]. It functions identically as before, but is now used with the {{button|ns|X}} button. The Dark-Light Device no longer has a burn-out meter, so Luigi (and [[Gooigi]]) can use it for as long as desired. The Dark-Light Device can also reveal purple Spirit Balls that are possessing furniture once they are stunned.
