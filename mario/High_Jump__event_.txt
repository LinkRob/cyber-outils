'''High Jump''' is an event featured in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]''. Here, characters try to jump over a hurdle, and whoever jumps over their hurdle set to the highest is the winner.
