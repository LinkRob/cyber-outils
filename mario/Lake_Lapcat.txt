{{Nihongo|'''Lake Lapcat'''|ネコ湖|Neko Mizūmi|lit. Cat Lake}} is a location in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|Super Mario 3D World]]'' [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|+ ''Bowser's Fury'']]. The kingdom's main inhabited by [[Cats (Bowser's Fury)|various colored cats]]. Because of its theming around cats, nearly all of its elements, including trees, shrubs, flowers, and even the signs, are modified to include feline features.

Lake Lapcat has a grayish blue main color scheme, similar to the [[Lake Kingdom]] from ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. These colors are also used by many of the area's walls, pillars, and windows. On some walls and ledges, cat-based hieroglyphs can be spotted.

==Description==
Lake Lapcat is the area where the story of ''Bowser's Fury'' takes place. It is an open-world, colorful and cat-themed landmass that is split into multiple different islands. Every island contains a lighthouse that is covered in [[Goop]] when the island has been revealed. By collecting the first Cat Shine of the island, the Goop will be removed, and the lighthouse's light will get rid of the Goop nearby. Every island contains five Cat Shines that can be collected, one of which is collected by finding the five Cat Shine Shards around the island. Every few islands surround a Giga Bell that can be freed by collecting five Cat Shines.

While Fury Bowser is active, the sky becomes dark and the kingdom rainy, along with having some spike-shaped platforms plunged into the ground and walls. These platforms modify the terrain, and Mario is able to jump on them, with him sometimes even being able to reach higher places.

==History ==

=== ''Super Mario 3D World'' + ''Bowser's Fury/Bowser's Fury'' ===
When Mario is walking around a grassy field in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] when a puddle of black Goop is formed from the logo of Shadow Mario, also made of black Goop, which then grows. Mario falls in, and lands in a rainy Lake Lapcat, falling head-first into a large footprint likely left by Bowser. After revealing the rest of Lake Lapcat by collecting a Cat Shine, he finds Bowser Jr. who tells him Bowser has been corrupted by black Goop causing him to transform into the gargantuan Fury Bowser. The lighthouses on the island are covered in this Goop, with the waters also covered in this substance as well as having large, spiked pillars sticking out of it.
