'''Peter Cullen''' (born July 28, 1941) is a Canadian actor and voice actor, who has earned fame, respect, and admiration as the voice of the original Optimus Prime, as well as that character's analogs from many subsequent series listed below. His tenure as the character would earn him the Lifetime Achievement Award from the National Academy of Television Arts & Sciences on December 17, 2023. Originally from Québec, Cullen moved to the U.S. early in his career.

He was one of the first voices of [[Mario]] in the 1983 Saturday Morning cartoon show, [[Saturday Supercade|Saturday]] [[Mario (Saturday Supercade)|Supercade]].
