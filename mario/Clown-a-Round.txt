'''Clown-a-Round''' is a level boss featured in ''[[Wario World]]''. It is fought at the end of [[Wonky Circus]], the second level of [[Spooktastic World]], owing to the level's circus theme.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Clown-a-Round is a humanoid clown who wears red, elf-like clothing, green shoes, and a red and white-striped cone hat. It has a few facial expressions, appearing to be happy in nature, both one of them is a large, sinister smile in which Clown-a-Round shows a widened set of teeth.

===Etymology===
His name is a pun on the phrase "clown around" but with added hyphens between the words and "A" being formatted as a separate letter.
