|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Red Hot Run''' is a level of [[World Castle]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The level is timed to 100 seconds, and features speed pannels the make the player run fast. It has three different paths to take. Also, it features spikes and pop up from the ground, as well as roll spiked logs.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Take the path to the left, and avoid the rolling spiked log. After passing that, you'll see a gap, jump it and you will get the first green star.
*'''Green Star 2''': To get the second green star, you must pass through a floating green star shape to activate the green coins. Collect all of them to get the green star.
*'''Green Star 3''': The final green star is pretty hard to get to, as you must jump over gaps and dodge spikes. But after doing it three times, you'll get the star.

==Stamp==
[[File:Red-Hot_Run_Stamp.png|thumb]]To get the stamp, you must stay on the path after getting the second green star. Don't turn, instead, jump the gap to obtain your stamp.


==Secret Room==
In the area with the second Green Star, drop down the hole in the middle. Run in the pipe, discovering a secret room with a[[ Super Star]] and lots of [[Goomba]]s. Collect the Super Star and run around, destroying all the Goombas. You can get up to three [[1-Up]]s here.
