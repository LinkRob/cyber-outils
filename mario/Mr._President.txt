'''Mr. President''' is a character in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', having made an appearance in "[[Reptiles in the Rose Garden]]." His wife is [[Mrs. President]] who, unlike him, has her full body shown onscreen, due to Mr. President being on a phone call throughout the episode, ignoring his surroundings.

Mr. President is based on {{w|George H. W. Bush}}, who was president at the time of ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''{{'}}s run, let alone [[DiC Entertainment]]'s ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' cartoons in general, just as Mrs. President is based on his wife, {{w|Barbara Bush}}.
