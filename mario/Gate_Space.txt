'''Gate Spaces''' are a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', exclusively in [[Bowser Party]] mode.

==Description==
Gate Spaces have the same appearance as a [[Boss Battle Space]], being orange and depicting a red prohibition sign. There are two Gate Spaces, each having a different function.

==In ''Mario Party 10''==
If Team Mario passes the first Gate Space on a board, they have to battle a [[Whomp]] by rolling a [[Dice Block]], similar to the Whomp battle in [[Perilous Palace Path]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''. The Whomp has six units of health, and the number rolled on the Dice Block determines how much damage it receives. If the number is not high enough to defeat Whomp, it still takes damage, but one of the other characters is given an attempt to roll the Dice Block and try to damage Whomp. Once the Whomp is defeated, each member of Team Mario receives three hearts before proceeding to the next segment of said board.

If Team Mario passes the second Gate Space on a board, [[Bowser]] hides the [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] inside of three characters (including [[Bowser Jr.]], [[Petey Piranha]], [[Koopa Troopa]], [[Sledge Bro]], and [[Dry Bones]]). A character in Team Mario has to guess who has the Star, without any hints. If they guess incorrectly, whoever has the Star knocks them back up to three spaces, but if they guess correctly, Team Mario wins the game and Bowser loses.
