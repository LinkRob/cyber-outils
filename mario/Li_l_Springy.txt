'''Li'l Springys'''<ref>''Super Mario Galaxy'' Nintendo Power Issue 220 and 224</ref><ref>''Super Mario Galaxy'' Official Guide</ref> (also referred to simply as '''Springs'''<ref>Super Mario Encyclopedia</ref>) are rare enemies which only appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', within the [[galaxy]] [[Boo's Boneyard Galaxy]]. In the game data, they are called '''Banekitis''' (singular '''Banekiti''').

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Li'l Springies are small, yellow, oval-shaped enemies with two black eyes in their center and four gray springs on them which surround their body completely.

===Behavior===
Li'l Springys are harmless, but they can bounce the player away from them. They slowly move from left to right on a straight path and only exist to bounce [[Boo Mario]] away, which can make him lose precious seconds in a race against the [[Spooky Speedster]]. The enemies cannot be destroyed.

==Nomenclature==
In Japanese, these enemies are called ''Banekichi'' (written バネキチ), from ''bane'' (spring; written 発条 in Kanji, ばね in Hiragana, バネ in Katakana). Their French name, ''Bloc ressort'', means "Spring Block," due to them using their springs to block [[Mario (character)|Mario]]'s progress through the race. In German, their name is Federer, which means "spring." In Italian, they are referred to as ''Molla'', meaning "spring."
