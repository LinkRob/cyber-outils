'''Red Bones''' are a stronger variant of [[Dull Bones]], and therefore [[Dry Bones]] by extension. They appear as enemies in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== History ==
Red Bones behave similarly to others in the Dry Bones family, but a difference is that Red Bones frequently regenerate more Dull Bones, making battles cumbersome. Red Bones are fought only twice in the game, and both instances require them to be fought. One Red Bones is encountered in [[Hooktail Castle]] and another is within the [[Palace of Shadow]]'s [[Riddle Tower]].
