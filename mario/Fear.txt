|cures   = [[Fearless Pin]]}}'''Fear''' is an ailment that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Moves like [[Sand Storm]] are known for inflicting this ailment.

==Description==
Whenever a character is inflicted with Fear, they put their heads down and start shivering.

==Effects==
Whenever a character has Fear, their Attack and Defense power is cut in half. The [[Fearless Pin]] can negate this effect and [[Princess Peach|Peach]]'s healing moves work as well.
