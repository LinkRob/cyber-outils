'''Mega Stingby Stompers''' is a Boss Battles minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''. It involves players engaging in a boss fight against a [[Mega Stingby]].

==Opening==
Each of the players is standing in a field surrounded by a few trees. Suddenly, a Mega Stingby flies down, and the players run away to dodge it. Then, the boss battle begins.

==Gameplay==
Players are tasked with climbing platforms, with a Mega Stingby flying back and forth below the player. The goal is to damage the Mega Stingby by ground-pounding it as it flutters through the field, with one [[point]] earned each time a Ground Pound is triggered on Mega Stingby up to one time in succession without reaching a platform first. If a character touches Mega Stingby or any of its [[Stingby]]s, they lose a point. On a side note, players can't do multiple Ground Pounds in rapid succession, as the flavor text suggests.

Halfway through the minigame, the Mega Stingby begins to move faster gets really angry and its movement pattern becomes more erratic. Furthermore, the center platforms disappear, with multiple smaller-sized Stingbies appearing in their stead, which players must defeat by jumping on them.

The Mega Stingby has a total of 50 hit points, across both phases. Whoever deals the finishing attack earns an additional three points, aside from Party-Planner Trek. If three minutes pass in the Mario Party mode, the player with the most points wins, regardless of whether the boss is defeated or not. In Party-Planner Trek and Boss Rush, the player team has to defeat the boss within three minutes to advance.

==Ending==
Once the last hit is delivered, Mega Stingby flutters erratically before falling backwards and collapsing onto its back and explodes.

Afterwards, the characters perform their winning and losing animations on a field surrounded by flowers, depending on their performance. If three minutes pass in Party-Planner Trek or in Boss Rush, all players will perform their losing animations.

==Flavor text==
*'''Description:''' Attack the Mega Stingby with Ground Pounds. Climb atop the platform, hop onto the enemy, and then unleash your attacks. You can't do multiple Ground Pounds in rapid succession.
