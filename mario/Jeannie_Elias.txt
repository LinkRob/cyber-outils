'''Jeannie Elias''' (born August 23, 1954) is a Canadian voice actress. She provided the voice for [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', but was later replaced by [[Tracey Moore]] in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World (show)|Super Mario World]]''.
