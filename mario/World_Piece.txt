'''World Piece''' is a 2-vs.-2 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in an area with several toy blocks. During gameplay, the two teams are on a wooden platform, divided into small separated arenas.

==Gameplay==
A shape appears in the center of a team's area, and both teammates on each team has to cooperatively find two specific half-shapes, several of which are scattered around the area, to place them into the panel depicted on their side. Whenever a team fills a shape, they earn a [[point]], and the first team to earn three points is the winner.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Pick up / Drop

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Combine 2 shapes to make the shape in the center of the board. The first team to 3 points is the winner.''
* '''Advice''': ''Each shape is composed of 2 halves. If you pick up the wrong shape, you will lose valuable time.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a pun on the phrase "{{w|world peace}}," which conceptually describes a world without conflict between nations or similar types of factions.
