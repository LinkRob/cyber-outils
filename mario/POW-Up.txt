==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario series]]''===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', POW-Up only appears when [[Watt]] uses her [[Turbo Charge]] technique on [[Mario]] giving him increased attack power for a number of turns.

In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', POW-Up is a little more common and can be mainly used via [[Power Lift]]. Using one of the Charge badges also increases attack power as well.
