== In ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'' ==
Penny's Theme is played in ''Get It Together!'' in Penny's Mix stage for the whole gameplay. If the player fails a microgame the original line of the lyrics is replaced with a different line, such as "It's missing a screw" instead of "She made it for you".
