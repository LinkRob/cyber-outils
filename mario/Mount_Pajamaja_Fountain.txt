The '''Mount Pajamaja Fountain''' is a strange fountain on [[Mount Pajamaja]] that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. Though this odd fountain can heal Mario and Luigi's HP and BP, it then starts to tell a story on how Mario and Luigi loved the taste of the fountain so much and results in a fake "[[Game Over]]."

==Story==
: ''"The fresh, sweet taste and lovely scent of the water courses through Mario's and Luigi's bodies, plunging them into a deep sleep. ''

: ''In time, a pleasant smell wafts into their noses and pulls them from their deep slumber. ''
: ''Lured by the wonderful scent, Mario and Luigi press forward. Their bodies feel oddly light. Their legs flex into the earth. Their moustaches flutter like gossamer in the mountain breeze. The smell grows stronger. They travel for great leagues... Or do they barely travel at all? Soon they find the smell's source.''

: ''They find endless mushrooms''. ''An unimaginable amount of mushrooms. MUSHROOMS! Mario jumps into the mushroom pile... Luigi jumps into the mushroom pile... They chew and inhale mushrooms... They devour as many as they can, their banquet interrupted by no one.''

: ''They know not how much they ate. A white cloud appears beside Mario. His gloves caress the atmosphere. His soaking boots absorb the sea. Hills shrink... No! The bros are huge! They have grown as big as the island! Thousands of times bigger than life! A finger flick could smash Bowser! There is nothing for them to fear. In this moment, they are ecstatic.''

: ''Soon after that, they realize they cannot go back to normal...''

: ''GAME OVER''

==Trivia==
*The Mount Pajamaja Fountain is similar to how Mario reads [[Ghost T.]]'s diary or agrees to be the [[Shadow Queen]]'s servant from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. However, these offer real Game Overs while the fountain only offers a fake Game Over.
*There is also a [[Dream World|dream world]] version of the fountain seen in [[Dreamy Mount Pajamaja]]. This doen't give a story or fake game over, but only heals Mario & Luigi fully.
