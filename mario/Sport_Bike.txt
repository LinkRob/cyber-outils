The '''Sport Bike''' is a type of [[bike]] body featured in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is designed after a real-world [[wikipedia:sports bike|sports bike]], has a dark blue and red coloration, and has an orange [[Bullet Bill]] drawing on its side. It is part of the vehicle class of the [[Sport bike (vehicle class)|same name]].
