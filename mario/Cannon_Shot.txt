The '''Cannon Shot''' is the [[Special Shot]] used by [[Dixie Kong]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can activate the Cannon Shot by tapping out a triangle twice, like with several other female characters.

Cannon Shot begins with Dixie Kong spinning and dribbling the [[basketball]] in a triangle. The ball glows yellow with stars trailing behind it. Dixie Kong throws the ball straight up, then a [[Barrel Cannon]] falls out of the sky and catches the ball inside. Dixie backflips onto the cannon and shoots the ball into the hoop. Upon entering the hoop, the Cannon Shot finishes with a red explosion followed by multicolored confetti.

If Dixie performs Cannon Shot outside the 3-pointer line, one of her teammates has to catch the ball and dunk it. The visual effects remain unchanged.
