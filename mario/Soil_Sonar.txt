'''Soil Sonar''' is a [[Gaddget]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is given to [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], and by extension the player, from [[Dorrie]] upon clearing the quest [[Dino of Mystery!]].

==Description==
Soil Sonar is intended to be a simulation of a metal detector. On the left side is a screen, and its field of vision can be moved downwards by using the A button and upwards using the B button. Several artifacts can be detected, such as a dinosaur fossil or even a [[UFO]]. Once the detector reaches a depth of 10,000, the Gaddget resets.
