The '''Ice Stadium''' is a baseball stadium in the [[Snow Area]], in that place, [[Mr. Blizzard]] lives there. It appeared in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
The player can find Mr. Blizzard in the Ice Stadium. If the player accepts Mr. Blizzard's quest, they can complete [[Cool as Ice]].
