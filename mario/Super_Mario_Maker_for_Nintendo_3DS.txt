'''''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS''''' is a game for the [[Nintendo 3DS|''Nintendo 3DS'']]. It serves as the handheld version of ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' for the [[Wii U]]. It was released on December 1, 2016.

== Gameplay ==
Gameplay is similar to the Wii U version. However, some items have been replaced or are not available, there are 100 courses designed by [[Nintendo]] that come pre-installed, and any courses created by the player cannot be uploaded online and can only be shared via local multiplayer. The online ended March 31st, 2021.

=== Differences from the Wii U version ===
*There is no [[Mystery Mushroom]]. It has been replaced by the [[Weird Mushroom (item)|Weird Mushroom]].
*A minigame called '''Gnat Attack''' has been added.
*Items such as [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint Flags]], [[Spike Pillar|Skewers]] and [[Key|Keys]] are available without shaking an arrow sign.
*The ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World]]'' game styles are available at the beginning of the game.
*Players can collaborate on a course via Local Play.
*The [[Nintendo 3DS]] version does not support [[amiibo]] functionality.
*There is a Data Reset glitch which doesn't happen on the Wii U.
**To make the glitch work, if one Cartridge of ''Super Mario Maker 3DS'' has progressed on the homing ''3DS'', insert it into another ''3DS'' and play it on that ''3DS''. Give back the Cartridge to the original ''3DS'' and all of the progress on the original ''3DS'' will be gone.
**This doesn't happen in the Wii U version, but there will be new progress once the disk is put onto another Wii U Console.
