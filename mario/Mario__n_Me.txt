'''''Mario 'n Me''''' is a promotional video shown at [[Nintendo]]'s presentation at the {{wp|CES (trade show)|Summer Consumer Electronics Show}} in Chicago, from June 1st to June 4th of 1991. It was made to promote the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] and features both original animation, and edited gameplay footage from ''[[Super Mario World]]''. [[Mario]] speaks in this video, but he is not voiced by [[Charles Martinet]].

==Description==
After the title card, an image of the [[Earth]] from space with an image of Mario's face on it is shown. The camera zooms in and shows the [[McCormick Place Exposition Center]], where [[Mario]] flies in riding a [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. He says that being in [[Chicago]] is exciting and he "really likes CES," then notices an orange [[Warp Pipe]] and tells the viewer to go inside with him. Footage from ''[[Super Mario World]]'' then plays of Mario going through the sub-area of [[Vanilla Dome 4]], while a live-action man identified as [[Rob Shultz]] is edited alongside him. After Mario exits the sub-area, the ending screen of the game is shown, with Rob waving goodbye to the viewer and Mario.
