|race = [[Toad (species)|Toad]]}}The '''Club 64 Bartender''', or more simply '''Bartender''', is a character that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is the bartender and is highly likely the owner of [[Club 64]] located at the docks of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]].

==Appearance==
The Bartender is an older looking [[Toad (species)|Toad]] that wears glasses and has a mustache. He also wears a suit and has purple spots on his head.

==History==
The Club 64 Bartender doesn't play much of a major role in the game, but he can be seen playing his violin when [[Chanterelle]] is singing her song (only after [[Mario]] retrieves the [[lyrics]] for her). The Club 64 Bartender usually spends his time polishing glasses and talking about the music life. He also appears during the ending parade where he is one of the people playing for Chanterelle's song on their float.
