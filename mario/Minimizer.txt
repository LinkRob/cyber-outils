The '''Minimizer''' is a magical staff that [[Bowser]] uses in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==History==
In the beginning of the game's Story Mode, Bowser shows [[Mario]] and the crew the Minimizer and shrinks them while stealing Mario's [[Sky Crystal]]. After [[Bowser's Pinball Machine]] is cleared in Story Mode, [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] arrive at [[Bowser's Castle]] for dinner. Donkey Kong accidentally bumps into Bowser and unwittingly breaks the Minimizer by stepping on it, reverting the shrunken characters to their normal sizes. Bowser then fights Mario and the crew without his Minimizer by using the [[Megamorph Belt]] to transform into [[Blockhead Bowser]].
