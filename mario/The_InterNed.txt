'''The InterNed''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
The InterNed is a rather colorful block that wears a hat and a bowtie for each side of his faces.

==History==
The InterNed is created by the [[The InterChet]], and he can be encountered in the story during the heroes journey into [[Merlee's Basement]]. [[Mimi]] had taken the form of [[Merlee]], and The InterNed appears to host the 66th Annual [["That's My Merlee!" Show|"That's My Merlee! Show"]] to figure out who is the real Merlee (though the whole game show was not necessary, as the real Merlee can be easily spotted as the one with the flies around her since she has been stuck in the toilet for a while).

==Trivia==
*The InterNed's name is a pun of the word "internet."
