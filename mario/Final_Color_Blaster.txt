The '''Final Color Blaster''' is a move that first appears in ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Colors|Sonic Colors]]''. Its one ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance is in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games''==
Final Color Blaster is used to defeat the Rotatatron, also known as the Big Boy. The Wisps will then fly ahead of the four players in [[Sonic's Figure Skating Spectacular]] one by one in the following order, while giving of a trail of their respective colors: Cyan Laser, Yellow Drill, Pink Spikes, Orange Rocket, Blue Cube, Green Hover and Purple Frenzy.
