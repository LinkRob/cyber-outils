The '''Spirit of Fright''' (also known as '''The Spirit Who Loves Scariness'''<ref>''Yoshi Topsy Turvy'' instruction booklet, page 25.</ref>) is the sixth and last of the [[tiny spirits]] seen within the [[Forbidden Pop-Up Book]] in the game ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Spirit of Fright resembles a mummy and has burial bandages wrapped from his head down to over his wispy tail. He moans and groans like a ghost.

===Personality===
The Spirit of Fright likes to scare, but has a kind demeanor.

==History==
The Spirit of Fright sets up a giant rolling spindle with spikes that plow after [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] as he runs along, eating up the paper and pages of the Forbidden Pop-Up Book. Yoshi must reach the end of the level without being sucked into the rolling spikes or getting defeated from touching them too much. Unlike the other spirits, the Spirit of Fright never appears alone in levels, always being doubled up with another.
