'''Shock Absorbers''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. Its name is a pun on {{wp|shock absorber|a term}} that refers to mechanical devices meant to soften jolts, typically associated with cars and other vehicles. The concept here is comparable to the one in [[Amplifried]], a minigame from ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
Various [[Amp]]s appear inside an octagon of more Amps. Players attempt to avoid being shocked by the Amps as they arbitrarily bounce along the octagonal arena. The Amps periodically shoot deadly bolts between themselves, which must be avoided. This attack can be detected early by watching which Amps stop and glow. Two of the outer Amps and one in the middle stop and glow before shooting the electricity between themselves. A player loses if they touch an Amp or beam of electricity.
