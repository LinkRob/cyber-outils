'''Blowfish'''<ref>"''A trio of Blowfish are in the top left corner.''" - ''Mario Party 3'': [[Prima Games|Prima's]] Official Strategy Guide. Page 25.</ref> is a character in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Blowfish is a giant anglerfish with an arrow at the end of its fin ray.

==In ''Mario Party 3''==
The Blowfish is found in the [[board]] [[Deep Bloober Sea]]. When a player lands on a [[Event Space|Happening Space]] near the anglerfish, the arrow on its fin ray points to the bottom, front, or right of the board. The anglerfish then inhales all players in front of it like a vacuum. To avoid being sucked into Blowfish, players must press {{button|n64|A}} repeatedly to escape the suction. Players that fail are sucked into the anglerfish, then spat out in the direction the arrow fin points towards, landing them at the start, near a pink [[Sushi]], or near a [[Boo]].
