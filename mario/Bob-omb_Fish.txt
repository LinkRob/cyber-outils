'''Bob-omb Fish''' are an enemy in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''.

==Profiles==
===Physical description===
Bob-omb Fish are a variant of [[Mini Bob-omb]] with fish-like attributes, specifically their pectoral fins, which match the color of their body. Aside from this and a lack of legs, Bob-omb Fish highly resemble regular Bob-ombs.

===Behavior===
Whenever a [[Mini Donkey Kong]] is close to a Bob-omb Fish, it stops, spins upside down, and rises to the water's surface, exploding shortly afterward.

==In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''==
Bob-omb Fish can be used underwater in [[Special Kit 3]] in the [[Construction Zone]]. Aside from this, Bob-omb Fish do not appear in any other [[level]].
