'''Tornado Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] used by [[Fly Guy]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
When Fly Guy uses the move, a tornado is created and sent to retrieve the ball for Fly Guy to hit back to the opponent.
