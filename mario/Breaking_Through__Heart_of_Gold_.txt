'''Breaking Through (Heart of Gold)''' is a song featured in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'', sung by young [[Pauline]] played by [[Jenny Kidd]]. It is the main theme of the game, and plays when Pauline is forced to free Donkey Kong from his odd rock form, and then during the credits after [[King K. Rool]] is defeated as the King of Rot, and after the post game. The song is in C Major.
