The '''Train Ticket''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Train Ticket is required to ride the [[Excess Express]] in [[Rogueport]]. [[Mario]] receives it when he reunites [[Don Pianta]], Don's daughter [[Francesca Pianta|Francesca]], and her boyfriend [[Frankie Pianta|Frankie]].
