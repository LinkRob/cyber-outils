'''Neeks''' are an enemy in the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}, and they first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''. Neeks made their only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearance in the fourteenth volume of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Neeks are rats with gray fur, varying from light to dark gray between appearances.

===Behavior===
Neeks walk back and forth on a platform.

==In other media==
===''Super Mario-kun''===
[[File:Neek SMkun.jpg|thumb|left|200px|A Neek in ''Super Mario-kun'']]
A Neek appears on a Spot the Differences activity on page 152 of ''[[Super Mario-kun Vol 14]]''. One difference on the page is that Neek's tail points right on the top scene but left on the lower scene.
