'''Neon Eggs''' are items that are only seen in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
Neon Eggs are special eggs that are laid by [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] after they have been fed [[Bean Fruit]]. Neon Eggs share their design with regular [[Yoshi Egg|Yoshi Eggs]] (being mainly white with colored spots) and come in seven colors: green, purple, blue, red, orange, azure, and yellow, and the color of a Bean Fruit depending on the type of Yoshi that had eaten it.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'' ==
The Neon Eggs were gathered when the Mario Bros. fed [[Bean Fruit]]s to different-colored Yoshis. [[Boddle]] used them to decorate the sign for the [[Yoshi Theater]], and traded them with the Mario Bros. for the [[Beanstar]].
