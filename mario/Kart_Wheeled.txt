'''Kart Wheeled''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
The four characters each drive their [[kart]] up to the starting point, one at a time.

==Gameplay==
Kart Wheeled involves the characters driving around a figure-eight track while avoiding the walls. Whoever completes five laps first is the winner. If five minutes pass without anyone crossing the [[finish line]], the minigame ends in a tie.

Kart Wheeled is one of the minigames in [[Decathlon Castle]]. The objective is to complete five laps as quickly as possible to earn as many points as possible, up to 1,000. The icon shows [[Mario]] driving a vehicle.

==Ending==
The winner stands on a podium, performing their victory animation, as five [[Goomba]]s and a [[Shy Guy]] cheer for them. Meanwhile, the losing characters stand in front of the podium, each performing their losing animation. If the minigame ends in a tie, all of the characters remain in their kart.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|Stick}}: Steer
*{{button|gcn|A}}: Accelerate
*{{button|gcn|B}}: Brake

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Get behind the wheel and race around the figure-eight track. The first player to complete 5 laps is the winner!''
*'''Advice''': ''Steer clear of walls and rival buggies. Bumping into either one will slow you down.''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
*The minigame name is a pun on "cartwheel," but with "kart" as the prefix due to the minigame featuring them.
