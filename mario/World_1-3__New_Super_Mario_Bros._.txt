'''World 1-3''' is the third level which appears in [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1]] and ''[[New Super Mario Bros.|New Super Mario Bros]]''. The location of this course is set in the sky. It features many [[Mushroom Platform|mushrooms]] that [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can walk across.

For some odd reason, this level uses blue and white mountains instead of the normal yellow and orange mountains in the background.

== Walkthrough ==
[[File:World1-3NSMB.png|thumb|160px|right|[[Shell Mario]] in World 1-3.]]
The first part of the level has a [[Question Block]] and a red [[Koopa Troopa]]. Shortly after this, there is a [[Spin Block]] that Mario or Luigi can use to fly up to some other [[Mushroom Platform|mushrooms]]. If the player chooses not to do this, then they will be faced with some [[Goomba]]s. Numerous [[coin]]s can be obtained if the player uses the Spin Block. Quite high up, there is a [[Bouncy Mushroom|bouncy pink mushroom]]. If the player presses down while airborne, they can jump very high using this mushroom. The first [[Star Coin]] in the level can be gotten in this way. After this, there are several swaying orange mushrooms, and the player can fall off of them if they are not careful. Then, there is the second Star Coin in the level, which is on top of an orange mushroom near some [[Brick Block|blocks]]. Right after this is the [[Midway Point]] of the level, with a red Koopa Troopa, and some more swaying orange mushrooms. The third Star Coin is found above the orange mushrooms. After all this, there is a [[Red Ring]] above even more orange mushrooms, which rise up and down instead of swaying. Then there is another red Koopa Troopa, some more swaying orange mushrooms, and then comes the exit of the level.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Use the first Spin Block to get on a green mushroom platform, then use the next Spin Block, and bounce on the pink mushroom.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After getting the first Star Coin, go to the right, and follow the [[coin]]s. This should lead you to the second [[Star Coin]].
*'''Star Coin 3''': After the Midway Point, in plain sight.
