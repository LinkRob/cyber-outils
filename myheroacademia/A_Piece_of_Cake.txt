{{Nihongo|'''A Piece of Cake'''|{{Ruby|A|ア}} {{Ruby|PIECE|ピース}} {{Ruby|OF|オブ}} {{Ruby|CAKE|ケイク}}|A Pīsu obu Keiku}} is a one-shot spin-off manga written and illustrated by [[Kohei Horikoshi]], that serves as a prequel to ''[[My Hero Academia: You're Next]]'', included in the [[Volume Next]] tie-in book.

==Synopsis==
During [[Class 1-A]]'s [[Quirk]] training, in preparation to hunt down the remaining members of the [[League of Villains]] and the [[Paranormal Liberation Front]], [[Mina Ashido|Mina]] asks [[Izuku Midoriya|Izuku]] if it's confusing to manage six other Quirks while using [[One For All]]. Izuku says it is, but that aspect's manageable thanks to the [[En Tayutai|Sixth User]]'s advice to visualize all of them as tools being swapped around.

For fun, Izuku wrote in a journal some hypotheticals in regard to this mindset applying to his classmates' Quirks and how they could interact with one another in team-up scenarios. Mina, [[Toru Hagakure|Toru]], [[Rikido Sato|Rikido]], [[Fumikage Tokoyami|Fumikage]], [[Mashirao Ojiro|Mashirao]], and [[Kyoka Jiro|Kyoka]], then ask to see the book. As the latter two start reading it, Izuku tries to walk away in embarrassment before [[Ochaco Uraraka|Ochaco]] and [[Tsuyu Asui|Tsuyu]] stop him.

The class is impressed with the depth of Izuku's casual analysis of them, though [[Katsuki Bakugo|Katsuki]] takes offense to the fact that Izuku wrote that a team-up with [[Shoto Todoroki|Shoto]] would make the pair invincible. Shoto asks for clarification, with Katsuki assuming that the notes say he can't be invincible without Shoto's help.

Katsuki claims that his [[Cluster]] technique outclasses Shoto by a sizable margin, and that the latter's [[Flashfreeze Heatwave]] is a rip-off of that. Shoto tries to put a positive spin on this and says if it's true then they might just develop [[Ultimate Move]]s under similar mindsets, which angers Katsuki even more. As they and the rest of the class do their usual banter, [[Hanta Sero|Hanta]] remarks that it's not a bad thing to get stronger by collaborating like this, thinking it's cool.

[[Toshinori Yagi|All Might]] walks up to the class and says it's time to move out, though not before agreeing with Hanta's judgment. As the students take off in an armored van to track down the [[Villains]], All Might, in the [[U.A. High School|U.A.]] Barrier's control room, wonders how [[The Mother of Quirks]] would feel about this current era. He tries to imagine her scolding him for being afraid to lean on others for support, as he places a hand on his [[Armored All Might|briefcase]]. All Might hopes she would if they met, as the era for a Symbol of Peace is already over, because it's "their" turn.

As he says this, a [[Dark Might|figure resembling an empowered All Might]], a [[Anna Scervino|blonde woman]], a [[Giulio Gandini|man with an eyepatch]], and Class 1-A, look out to make their moves.
