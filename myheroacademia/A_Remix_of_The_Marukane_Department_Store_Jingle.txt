'''A Remix of The Marukane Department Store Jingle''' is a track from the first season of the ''[[My Hero Academia: Vigilantes (Anime)|My Hero Academia: Vigilantes anime]]'', written by [[Hideyuki Furuhashi]] and {{Wikipedia|Yuki Hayashi}}, the latter composing the song. It is remix cover of the [[The Marukane Department Store Jingle]], which sung and played by the [[FeatherHATS]].
