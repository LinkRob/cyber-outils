{{Nihongo|'''AP Shot: Auto-Cannon'''|{{Ruby|徹甲弾|A・P・ショット}}{{Ruby|機関銃|オートカノン}}|Ē-Pī Shotto Ōtokanon|lit. '''Armor-Piercing Shot: Auto-Cannon'''}} is an [[Ultimate Move]] performed by [[Katsuki Bakugo]] using the [[Explosion]] [[Quirk]].

==Description==
A rapid-fire version of [[AP Shot]] in which Katsuki rapidly fires concentrated explosions to cover an area or target. He developed and used this technique initially with smaller explosive shots to reduce damage on human targets.

Katsuki first used this technique [[Katsuki Bakugo, Eijiro Kirishima & Denki Kaminari vs. Seiji Shishikura|against]] [[Seiji Shishikura]] to counter his [[Meatball]] Quirk.{{Ref|chap=107|ep=55}} He uses it again during his fights against [[Hoyo Makihara|Mummy]] and [[Nine]], deploying stronger and more harmful explosions than before.{{Ref|movie=2}} 

==Trivia==
*AP Shot: Auto-Cannon is featured as a technique for Katsuki in ''[[Jump Force]]''.
