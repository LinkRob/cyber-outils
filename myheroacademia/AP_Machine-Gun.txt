{{Nihongo|'''AP Machine-Gun'''|{{Ruby|徹甲機銃|A・P・マシンガン}}|Ē-Pī Mashingan|lit. '''Armor-Piercing Shot Machine-Gun'''}} is an [[Ultimate Move]] performed by [[Katsuki Bakugo]] using the [[Explosion]] [[Quirk]].

==Description==
A variation of [[AP Shot]] in which Bakugo focuses the power of his explosions into a single point before unleashing it as a barrage of small but powerful explosions. The pace is similar to [[AP Shot: Auto-Cannon]], but this version is clearly improved with larger blasts that carry more explosive force.

Bakugo first used this move during the [[Hospital Raid Team & Trainees vs. Tomura Shigaraki|battle]] against [[Tomura Shigaraki]] in [[Jaku City]], though the attack failed to do any damage.{{Ref|chap=277|ep=120}}
