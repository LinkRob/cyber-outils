{{Nihongo|'''1,000,000% Delaware Detroit Smash'''|1000000{{Ruby|%|パーセント}} デラウェアデトロイトスマッシュ|1000000 Pāsento Derawea Detoroito Sumasshu}} is an [[Ultimate Move]] performed by [[Izuku Midoriya]] using the [[One For All]] [[Quirk]].

==Description==
Izuku uses both his 100% [[Delaware Smash]] and his 100% [[Detroit Smash]] at the same time, multiplying their destructive power into one attack. It is one of the strongest Ultimate Moves in Izuku's arsenal.

By utilizing his adrenaline and raw emotion, Izuku supercharges One For All with enough power to perform this smash attack.

Izuku first used this attack to defeat [[Goto Imasuji|Muscular]] during the [[Vanguard Action Squad Invasion]]. He was able to break through Muscular's Quirk-enhanced defenses, allowing him to knock the villain unconscious.{{Ref|chap=76|ep=42}}

==Trivia==
*1,000,000% Delaware Detroit Smash is featured as Izuku's ultimate move in ''[[Jump Force]]''.
*Izuku does not actually unleash One For All at 1,000,000% using this move; rather, it's his way of shouting his feelings toward this unusual strength in an emergency due to his fighting spirit.{{Ref|vol=9}}
