{{Nihongo|'''80% Liberation'''|解放 80{{Ruby|%|パーセント}}|Kaihō 80-Pāsento}} is an [[Ultimate Move]] performed by [[Rikiya Yotsubashi]] using the [[Stress]] [[Quirk|Meta Ability]].

==Description==
Re-Destro raises his liberation enhancement to 80%, covering himself in the black energy manifestation of his power and increasing the size of his body considerably.

Re-Destro first used this technique to combat [[Tomura Shigaraki]].{{Ref|chap=234|ep=111}}
