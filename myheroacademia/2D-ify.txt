{{Nihongo|'''2D-ify'''|二次元化|Nijigenka}} is the [[Quirk]] used by a member of the [[Avant-Garders]].{{Ref/Tums|chap=27}}

==Description==
2D-ify allows the user to trap others inside paintings.

Using a picture frame, the user can absorb people and objects, turning them into 2D paintings that are contained within frames. Those trapped as paintings can still move, but are unable to leave.

The exact conditions are unknown, but the Quirk seems to deactivate upon the user being immobilized.

==Trivia==
*2D-ify is the only known Quirk to have a number as part of its name.
