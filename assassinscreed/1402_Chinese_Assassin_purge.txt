The '''1402 Chinese Assassin purge''' was a full-scale assault on the [[Chinese Brotherhood of Assassins|Chinese Brotherhood]] of [[Assassins]] in eastern [[China]] by the [[Zhu Di|Yongle Emperor]], under the influence of the [[Templars]]. Thousands of suspected Assassins were captured and executed, including the Confucian scholar and [[Assassin leader]] [[Fang Xiaoru]]. [[Li Tong]] was able to escape the purge in [[Beijing]] alongside an [[Assassin apprentice|apprentice]], taking an [[Apple of Eden]] with them.<ref>''[[Assassin's Creed: Revelations – Discover Your Legacy]]'' – Bloodlines: "Li Tong"</ref>
