{{nihongo|'''"King's" Choice!!"'''|“キング”の{{Ruby|選|せん}}{{Ruby|択|たく}}！！|''“Kingu” no Sentaku!!''}} is the nineteenth chapter of ''[[Dragon Ball Heroes: Victory Mission]]''.

==Summary==
[[File:1601477 535110006603093 1687715181 n.jpg|thumb|left|Basaku and Kagyu in their JM outfits]]
[[Super Saiyan 3]] [[Beat]] flies towards the possessed [[Nim]], who is firing [[Ki Blast]]s at him. Super Saiyan 3 Beat [[Ki Blast Deflect|deflects]] all the blasts and [[headbutt]]s Nim towards a small island. Beat appears on the island before Nim crashes on it and fires a [[Kamehameha]] at him, finally defeating him. Beat then quickly rushes towards Nim and removes the [[Earth Dragon Ball|Dragon Ball]] from his forehead. [[Oceanus Shenron]] emerges, and then switches from her Princess Oto form to her true Shadow Dragon form. Nim appears in his new JM outfit and then uses a [[Super 17]] (w/ Cell absorbed) card. Oceanus Shenron tries to flee, but Nim and Super 17 (w/ Cell absorbed) rush towards her and use a team attack ended by a powerful punch from Super Saiyan 3 Beat. As Beat reverts to his base form, Oceanus Shenron emerges from the smoke back in his Princess Oto form and flees the battle, leaving Beat and Nim voiceless. At the [[World Martial Arts Tournament]] stage, [[Kagyu]] easily dodges [[Basaku]]'s attack. He then decides to give Basaku his [[Super Class-up]]grade [[capsule]] and asks [[Yoshito-kun]] for another one. Both of them use their capsules, evolving into new forms. As Basaku rushes towards him, Kagyu summons [[Super Saiyan 4]] [[Vegeta]] to block the Saiyan Berserker's attack. Super Saiyan 3 Beat quickly defeats Nim and frees him from Oceanus Shenron. Oceanus appears again and transforms into her Shadow Dragon form but is attacked by Nim (who has used the Super Class-up), Super 17 (Cell absorbed), and Beat, defeating her. Oceanus Shenron then re-takes her Princess Oto form and flees.
