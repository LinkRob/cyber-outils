{{nihongo|'''100x Big Bang Kamehameha'''|１００倍ビッグバンかめはめ波|''Hyakūbai Biggu Ban Kamehameha''|lit. "100-Fold Big Bang Kamehame Wave"}} is a stronger variation of the [[Big Bang Kamehameha]] used by [[Gogeta]] in his [[Super Saiyan 4]] form.

==Overview==
Arching back, Gogeta brings his hands to his sides before moving them forward again, creating a massive [[Big Bang Attack]] before condensing a [[Kamehameha]] into the sphere and launching it forward as a massive energy wave, eclipsing even the normal Big Bang Kamehameha in size and power and utterly devastating the opponent.

==Variations==
*'''DUAL x100 Big Bang Kamehameha''' - The two-person Dual Ultimate version of x100 Big Bang Kamehameha used by Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta and the [[Future Warrior (Xenoverse 2)|Future Warrior]] in ''[[Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2]]''.
*'''[[Rising Big Bang Kamehameha]]''' - A 100x Big Bang Kamehameha that can be charged in deadly increments used by Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta in ''[[Dragon Ball Legends]]''.
**'''200x Big Bang Kamehameha''' - The Rising Big Bang Kamehameha at 200x power.
**'''500x Big Bang Kamehameha''' - The Rising Big Bang Kamehameha at 500x power.
**'''1000x Big Bang Kamehameha''' - The strongest version of the Rising Big Bang Kamehameha, with strength a thousandfold.

In most games, it is capable of inflicting major damage to the stage of play. With a few exceptions to which map is being played, whenever Gogeta uses the 100x Big Bang Kamehameha, it completely changes the stage to Ruined [[Earth]] or Dying [[Namek]].

In ''Budokai 3'', the ''Budokai Tenkaichi'' series, ''Infinite World'', and ''Budokai HD Collection'', it is Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta's [[Ultimate Blast]]. In ''Sparking Zero'', it is named '''Ultra Big Bang Kamehameha'''.

In ''Dragon Ball Heroes'', it is named {{nihongo|'''Ultra Big Bang Kamehameha'''|ウルトラビッグバンかめはめ波|''Urutora Biggu Ban Kamehameha''|lit. "Ultra Big Bang Kamehame Wave"}} and is Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta (GT)'s [[Super Attack]]. It can also be used by Goku, Gohan, and Beat.

In ''Ultimate Tenkaichi'', it is named {{nihongo|'''Full-Power Big Bang Kamehameha'''|全力ビッグバンかめはめ波|''Zenryoku Biggu Ban Kamehameha''|lit. "Full-Power Big Bang Kamehame Wave"}} and has a reddish-orange color.

In ''Dokkan Battle'', it is '''Peerless Gleam Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta''''s [[Super Attack]], '''Fighter of Ultimate Radiance Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta''''s Ultra Super Attack, and '''Saiyan Warriors with Ultimate Power Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta''''s Active Skill. For the latter two, it is named '''Ultra Big Bang Kamehameha'''. The animations of the first and third feature visions of Super Saiyan 4 Goku and Super Saiyan 4 Vegeta before Gogeta launches the attack.

In ''Xenoverse'', it is named '''X 100 Big Bang Kamehameha''' and is one of Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta's Ultimate Skills. It can be obtained by the [[Future Warrior]] in Parallel Quest 54: "''Saiyan Warriors''". When the attack is used, if the user is not locked-on to an opponent before performing it then they will sweep the x100 Big Bang Kamehameha behind them as they fire. 

In ''Xenoverse 2'', it is named '''x100 Big Bang Kamehameha''' and returns as one of Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta's Ultimate Skills. It can be obtained by the [[Future Warrior (Xenoverse 2)|Future Warrior]] in Parallel Quest 100: "''The Ultimate Rivalry''" or via the TP Medal shop. As part of the 1.15.00 Update DLC, x100 Big Bang Kamehameha can be added to Super Saiyan Blue Gogeta's custom skillset after purchasing it in Partner Customization. As part of the 1.19.00 Update DLC, it appears as one of Gogeta's default Ultimate Skills in his custom skillset after unlocking it in Partner Customization. Additionally, '''DUAL x100 Big Bang Kamehameha''' appears as Gogeta (Super Saiyan 4)'s Dual Ultimate Skill which allows him and the Future Warrior to attack as a pair with x100 Big Bang Kamehameha. Additionally, the Future Warrior can obtain it by purchasing it from the TP Medal Skill Shop.

In ''FighterZ'', two different versions appear as Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta's Meteor Attacks: '''Ultra Big Bang Kamehameha''', which functions like other beam-like Super Attacks in the game, and '''x100 Big Bang Kamehameha''', which contains a mechanic unique to this version of Gogeta. If Gogeta reaches 7 levels of [[Finish Sign]], he can use the x100 Big Bang Kamehameha to shred through an opponent's entire health bar. It will also hit any assists that don't leave the field in time, tearing through their health bar as well and leaving them with slivers if not wiping them out entirely. Like in ''Dokkan Battle'', visuals of Super Saiyan 4 Goku and Super Saiyan 4 Vegeta appear before execution.

In ''Dragon Ball Legends'', it appears during the Legendary Finish animation when Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta finishes off the last remaining opponent, using his Ultimate Arts Card. A version used by Ultra Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta known as the [[Rising Big Bang Kamehameha]] starts with a normal 100x Big Bang Kamehameha and can be charged all the way up to a '''1000x Big Bang Kamehameha''' for even more destructive power.

In ''World Mission'', it is named '''Ultra Big Bang Kamehameha''' and is Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta (GT)'s Super Attack. It can also be learned by all Hero Avatars by wishing for a new Super Attack from [[Super Shenron]].
