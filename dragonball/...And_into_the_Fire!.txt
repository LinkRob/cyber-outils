{{nihongo|'''"...And into the Fire!"'''|フライパン{{Ruby|山|やま}}の{{Ruby|牛|ぎゅう}}{{Ruby|魔|ま}}{{Ruby|王|おう}}|''Furaipan Yama no Gyūmaō''|lit. "Gyūmaō of Mount Frypan"}} is the eleventh chapter of the [[Dragon Ball (manga)|''Dragon Ball'' manga]].

==Cover==
The cover features [[Chi-Chi]], [[Goku]] riding the [[Flying Nimbus]] and three pictures in the background. The three pictures are [[Yamcha]] and [[Puar]] in the [[Mighty Mouse]], [[Oolong]] in the car Yamcha gave him driving away from a shadow of the [[Ox-King]] and the last picture is of [[Fire Mountain]].

==Summary==
[[File:Dbgangseesfrypan.jpg|thumb|left|Goku and Bulma learning about Fry-Pan Mountain]]
Two days after the [[Dragon Team]] received the new car from [[Yamcha]], they finally end up at [[Fire Mountain]]. Once they see it in the distance, Oolong tells [[Goku]] and [[Bulma]] about how the castle at the top is guarded by the [[Ox-King]] who cannot get into the castle himself and kills anybody who goes near it.

[[File:Yamchaknocksoutchichimanga.jpg|thumb|left|Yamcha beating the powerful young girl]]
Yamcha and [[Puar]] who are approaching as well come in contact with a seemingly defenseless [[Chi-Chi|young girl]] running away from a [[T-Rex]]. The girl then [[Stay Away From Me!|throws the blade of her helmet]] at the dinosaur decapitating it then blowing up the remains with a [[That Won't Work|laser coming from her helmet]] as well. When she sees Yamcha, she tries to attack him in fear, but he easily knocks her out since he is not shy of girls that are too young.

[[File:Chapter11end.jpg|thumb|The Ox-King asking Goku where his cloud came from]]
Over at the base of Fire Mountain, Goku goes off on the [[Flying Nimbus]] to try to look for a way to get to the castle. While Bulma and Oolong are left behind, they encounter the Ox-King asking what they are doing near his area and if there after his treasure. While they are scared, they try to convince him that they are not, but Goku quickly comes back explicitly saying that he cannot find a way to break into the castle. Rather than attacking them, the Ox-King asks if he got that cloud from his martial arts master [[Master Roshi|Roshi]] the turtle hermit.

==Differences from anime==
*While flying near the castle, Goku accidentally catches his tail on fire causing him to fly back down and jump into a lake to cool his tail in the anime.
*In the anime, Goku and the Ox-King have a fight scene before he finds out that Goku has the cloud Master Roshi have him.
