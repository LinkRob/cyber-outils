'''100% Activation''' is an ability used by [[Frieza]] in his Final Form in order to utilize his [[100% Full Power]].

==Overview==
[[File:100PcActivation3(SSW2).png|thumb|left|Frieza after using his 100% Activation]]
Frieza powers up with a yellow [[aura]] around his body to increase his power. Once the state is activated, a white and purple aura appears around his body.

==Usage==
Frieza power up to his 100% Full Power form while fighting [[Super Saiyan]] [[Goku]], after using his "[[Destroy The Planet!]]" in order to cause [[Namek]]'s core to begin detonating. Frieza later powers down from his 100% Full Power form to his 50% Power form to battle [[Gohan]] but reuses his 100% Power form again when Goku returns.

==Video Game Appearances==
100% Activation was named in ''[[Dragon Ball Z: Supersonic Warriors 2]]'', where Frieza uses his regular Final Form to reach his 100% power without enlarging his muscles. The power up increases Frieza's attack power.
