{{Nihongo|'''10x God Kamehameha'''|１０倍ゴッドかめはめ波|Jūbai Goddo Kamehameha|lit. "10-Fold God Kamehame Wave"}} is a variation of the [[God Kamehameha]] used by [[Goku]] in his [[Super Saiyan Blue Kaio-ken]] form.

==Overview==
Goku fires a massive God Kamehameha whose power is multiplied ten-fold while in his Super Saiyan Blue Kaio-ken power-up with the 10x multiplier, inflicting colossal amounts of damage, while causing a huge explosion that damages its surroundings.

==Usage==
In the anime, Goku uses this technique toward the end of his battle with [[Hit]], however the assassin manages to avoid it using his improved [[Time-Skip]]. It is seen in use as part of his [[Evolved Attack]] in order to break through the assassin's Time-Skip.

==Video Game Appearances==
*''[[Dragon Ball Heroes]]''
*''[[Dragon Ball Z: Dokkan Battle]]''
*''[[Dragon Ball FighterZ]]''
*''[[Dragon Ball Legends]]''
*''[[Super Dragon Ball Heroes: World Mission]]''

In ''Dragon Ball Heroes'', it is Super Saiyan God SS Kaioken Goku's super move.

In ''Dokkan Battle'', it is named '''10x Kaioken God Kamehameha''' where it is the [[Super Attack]] for '''Next-Level Strike Super Saiyan God SS Goku''' and '''Final Super Power Super Saiyan God SS Goku (Kaioken)'''.

In ''FighterZ'', it is [[Super Saiyan Blue]] Goku's Meteor Attack in which he activates Kaio-ken and rapidly punches his opponent before kicking them into the air, then launching the attack. By holding down the attack button, it can be followed up with [[Evolved Attack]].

In ''Legends'', it is named '''[[Super Kamehameha]]''' where it is the Special Move Arts for Super Saiyan God SS Goku (DBL15-07S) after he transforms into Super Saiyan God SS Kaioken during battle. Additionally, Goku can use his Quirk Skill Arts to power the Super Kamehameha into '''Next-Level Strike'''.

In ''World Mission'', it is named '''x10 God Kamehameha''' where it is Goku (SSGSS Kaioken)'s Super Attack. Additionally, there is x10 God Kamehameha Tera Rarity Super Attack Accessory which can be used by [[Beat]] to equip it to any non-Special type card (official or custom) in a Custom Deck or can be added as the default Super Attack to any custom card in Card Creation provided its Black Card has enough Custom Points to equip it. The user gains a SSGSS Kaioken double aura while using the technique.
