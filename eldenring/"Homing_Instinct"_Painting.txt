'''"Homing Instinct"''' is an [[Info Item|info item]] and [[Painting|painting]] in {{ER}}. <br>Depicts a distant [[Minor Erdtree (Weeping Peninsula)|Minor Erdtree]], framed by ancient ruins.

Leads to the [[Incantation Scarab]] [[Head|headpiece]].

==Acquisition==

'''Loot:''' [[Limgrave]]
*Acquired from interacting with the painting found inside the [[Artist's Shack (Limgrave)|Artist's Shack]] located on a hill, past the road north of [[Waypoint Ruins]].
{{Clear}}
==Effect==
Allows the [[Player]] to perceive the soul of a painter and retrieve a unique item from their location. <br>The location being the point of perspective depicted by the painting itself.

The soul in question can be found near an arch of [[Ancient Ruins]] by the cliffs southeast of the [[First Step]] (or northwest from the [[Seaside Ruins]]) [[Site of Grace]].

The painter is initially invisible when viewed from a distance but will eventually reveal themselves as the player gets physically closer to them. Successfully locating the spirit will make it dissipate before leaving the [[Incantation Scarab]] [[Head|headpiece]] for the player to pick up from the ground.

==Notes==
*This painting's name, "Homing Instinct" is likely a reference to [[Scarabs]] instinctively abandoning their rolled clumps, in order to return to their home nest as their lives approach its end. 
**It could also reference the feeling homesickness for [[Leyndell]] and the [[Erdtree]] displayed by [[Godrick]] and his followers. Due to its depiction of a visibly distant [[Minor Erdtree (Weeping Peninsula)|Erdtree]] and said Erdtree being in close proximity to the [[Tower of Return]], which offers the inhabitants of Limgrave a means to quickly travel to the capital, even if the location they end up in is only a small enclosed area of it.
