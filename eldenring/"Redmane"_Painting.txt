Leads to the [[Ash of War: Rain of Arrows|Rain of Arrows]] [[Ash of War]].

==Acquisition==
'''Loot:''' [[Caelid]]
*Acquired from interacting with the painting found almost buried by sand underneath a balcony in [[Sellia, Town of Sorcery]].

==Effect==
Allows the [[Player]] to perceive the soul of a painter and retrieve a unique item from their location. <br>The location being the point of perspective depicted by the painting itself.

The soul in question can be found on the edge of the [[Ancient Ruins]] found bellow the cliffs directly south of the [[Minor Erdtree (Dragonbarrow)|Minor Erdtree]] of [[Greyoll's Dragonbarrow]].

The painter is initially invisible when viewed from a distance but will eventually reveal themselves as the player gets physically closer to them. Successfully locating the spirit will make it dissipate before leaving the [[Ash of War: Rain of Arrows]] for the player to pick up from the ground.

==Notes==
*This painting's name, "Redmane" is a reference to [[Radahn]] and his army, the [[Redmanes]]. As it depicts their fortress, the site of their leader's final stand and its reward being one of their signature battle arts.
