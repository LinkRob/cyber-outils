==Use==
The {{PAGENAME}} reveals the [[Painter Spirit]] near the [[Stargazer's Ruins]], who vanishes after leaving the [[Greathood]] behind.

==Acquisition==
[[File:Eldenringsorcererpainting.jpg|thumb|The "Sorcerer" painting on its easel]]

'''Location''': [[Castle Sol]]
* The {{PAGENAME}} is obtained by touching the corresponding painting on its easel inside a central building of [[Castle Sol]] in northern [[Mountaintops of the Giants]]. Access to the room is only possible from above, requiring traversal of the northern and western ramparts, which are guarded by [[Ballista]]s manned by [[Exile Soldier#Phantom|Phantom Exile Soldiers]].

==Solution==
'''Location''' : [[Mountaintops of the Giants]]
* The {{PAGENAME}} depicts the [[Moon|waxing crescent moon]] hanging over the [[Heretical Rise]], framed by [[ancient ruins]], as seen from the eastern edge of an enormous stone bridge northwest of the [[Stargazer's Ruins]]. 
* Travel to the {{grace}} [[Snow Valley Ruins Overlook]] [[site of grace]]. Head west until reaching the second stone bridge spanning the valley. The [[Painter Spirit]] becomes visible only at close range and dissipates after being approached, leaving the [[Greathood]] on the ground for the player to collect.

==Notes==
* The {{PAGENAME}} cannot be dropped, traded, sold, or stored in the [[Chest (inventory)|Chest]].
