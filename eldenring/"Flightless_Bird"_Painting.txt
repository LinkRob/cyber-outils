'''"Flightless Bird"''' is an [[Info Item|info item]] and [[Painting|painting]] in {{ER}}. <br>Depicts the outer walls of [[Leyndell, Royal Capital]] with the [[Erdtree]] looming in the background

Leads to the [[Fire's Deadly Sin]] incantation.

==Acquisition==
'''Loot:''' [[Leyndell, Royal Capital]]
*Acquired from interacting with the painting found in the same room as the [[Fortified Manor, First Floor]] [[Site of Grace]] in [[Leyndell, Royal Capital]].

==Effect==
Allows the [[Player]] to perceive the soul of a painter and retrieve a unique item from their location. <br>The location being the point of perspective depicted by the painting itself.

The soul in question can be found near the southern cliffs at the top of [[Dominula, Windmill Village]], south of the [[Windmill Heights]] [[Site of Grace]].

The painter is initially invisible when viewed from a distance but will eventually reveal themselves as the player gets physically closer to them. Successfully locating the spirit will make it dissipate before leaving the [[Fire's Deadly Sin]] incantation for the player to pick up from the ground.

==Notes==
* It is possible that this painting's name was was supposed to be [["Prophecy" Painting|"Prophecy"]], as the reward for it refers to a prophecy against the [[Erdtree]] in its description.
