==Use==
The {{PAGENAME}} enables the ability to see the [[Painter Spirit]] that grants the [[Rock Heart]].

==Acquisition==
'''Location''': [[Shadow Keep]]
* The {{PAGENAME}} is a found in the [[Shadow Keep]], located on the northern [[Scadu Altus]]. After defeating the [[Golden Hippopotamus (boss)|Golden Hippopotamus]] at the main gate, proceed past [[Vulgar Militia#Shadow Militia|Shadow Militiamen]] to the main plaza, where burning [[Tibia Mariner]] boats that are arranged in rows. Descend the ladder on the east side of the bridge, found beside a [[Silver-Pickled Fowl Foot]]. Pass through the waterfall, descend the second ladder, and retrieve the painting from the storeroom.

==Solution==
'''Location''' : [[Jagged Peak]]
* The {{PAGENAME}} depicts the [[Scadutree]] and [[Scadutree Chalice]] from the [[Jagged Peak]].
* Travel to the {{grace}} [[Jagged Peak Mountainside]] [[site of grace]]. Travel southwest along rough terrain. Use the [[spiritspring]] to reach an area to the west. The [[Painter Spirit]] appears in an area free from [[Lightning]] strikes.

==Notes==
* The {{PAGENAME}} cannot be stored in the [[Chest (inventory)|Chest]].
* The {{PAGENAME}} cannot be sold.
