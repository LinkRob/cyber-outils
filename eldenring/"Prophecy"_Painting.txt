'''"Prophecy"''' is an [[Info Item|info item]] and [[Painting|painting]] in {{ER}}. <br>Depicts the scene of a distant [[Stormveil Castle]].

Leads to the [[Warhawk Ashes|Warhawk]] [[Ashes|spirit ashes]].

==Acquisition==
'''Loot:''' [[Stormveil Castle]]
*Acquired from interacting with the painting found inside a room found west of the heavily barricaded courtyard of [[Stormveil Castle]].

==Effect==
Allows the [[Player]] to perceive the soul of a painter and retrieve a unique item from their location. <br>The location being the point of perspective depicted by the painting itself.

The soul in question can be found near a cliff edge north of the [[Church of Pilgrimage]].

The painter is initially invisible when viewed from a distance but will eventually reveal themselves as the player gets physically closer to them. Successfully locating the spirit will make it dissipate before leaving the [[Warhawk Ashes]] for the player to pick up from the ground.

* It is possible that this painting's name was was supposed to be [["Flightless Bird" Painting|"Flightless Bird"]], as the reward for it is the spirit ashes of a bird that had its its wings badly wounded, its legs amputated and its home taken from them, which is more fitting of the "Flightless Bird" moniker.
** Further, the the reward for "Flightless Bird" is an incantation that refers to a prophecy in its description, which is more fitting of the "Prophecy" moniker.
