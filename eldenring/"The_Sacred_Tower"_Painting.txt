==Use==
The {{PAGENAME}} enables the ability to see the [[Painter Spirit]] that grants the [[Spiraltree Seal]].

==Acquisition==
'''Location''': [[Gravesite Plain]]
* The {{PAGENAME}} is a found inside a small cave northeast of the [[Scorched Ruins]] on the [[Gravesite Plain]].

==Solution==
'''Location''' : [[Rauh Base]]
* The {{PAGENAME}} depicts [[Enir-Ilim]] as seen from the [[Rauh Base]] in the past. From the [[Northern Nameless Mausoleum]], located on the butte east of the [[Temple Town Ruins]], descend the western slope, spot two jagged rocks, and look to the northwest. 
* This area is accessed via a sealed [[spiritspring]], which can be unlocked by toppling the stacked stones on high ground to the southeast.

==Notes==
* The {{PAGENAME}} cannot be stored in the [[Chest (inventory)|Chest]].
* The {{PAGENAME}} cannot be sold.
