'''"Champion's Song"''' is an [[Info Item|info item]] and [[Painting|painting]] in {{ER}}. <br>Depicts the scene of a [[Hero's Grave]] with the razor sharp rocks of [[Mt. Gelmir]] looming in the distance, with [[Volcano Manor]] as its crown.

Leads to the [[Harp Bow]].
==Description==
{{Description
|EN=
Work of a wandering artist. 
<br>Reminiscence of a painting titled "Champion's Song." 

This painter is said to have captured the landscapes seen during the last moments of those welcomed into death's embrace. 

The soul of the painter, and vestiges of the dead's last moments can be discovered by visiting the location depicted even now.

==Acquisition==
'''Loot:''' [[Altus Plateau]]
*Acquired from interacting with the painting found in a wooden structure north of the [[Shaded Castle Ramparts]] [[Site of Grace]].

==Effect==
Allows the [[Player]] to perceive the soul of a painter and retrieve a unique item from their location. <br>The location being the point of perspective depicted by the painting itself.

The soul in question can be found east of the [[Sainted Hero's Grave]] on a hill facing west above the [[Rampartside Path]] [[Site of Grace]].

The painter is initially invisible when viewed from a distance but will eventually reveal themselves as the player gets physically closer to them. Successfully locating the spirit will make it dissipate before leaving the [[Harp Bow]] for the player to pick up from the ground.

==Notes==
*This painting's name, "Champion's Song" is likely a reference to its reward, the Harp Bow. ''"Troubadours sing tales of champions, both in the honorable service of the Erdtree, and the one who spurns honor for blasphemy."'' Befitting of its depiction of a mausoleum for heroes who served the [[Erdtree]] and the visage of Volcano Manor, a site of blasphemous [[Recusant|recusants]].
