==Overview==
The basic {{Term|TMC|Rolling}} ability was first introduced in {{OoT}}. In most games, it can be performed by moving forward and pressing the action button, though {{PH}} and {{ST}} require different techniques to {{Term|Series|Rolling|display=Roll}}.

It is rarely used as an attack, but is instead an action that {{Term|Series|Link}} can use to "tackle" some objects, an action that was formerly done with the {{Term|Series|Pegasus Boots|link}} in {{ALttP}} and {{LA}}. In most of its appearances, it can also be used repeatedly to travel faster than walking. Although it does not seem to be intended as an attack, this technique has been known to cause some damage to the occasional enemy.
