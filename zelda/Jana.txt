{{Term/Store|Jana||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Jana|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Jana}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who lives at {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. Starting at 5:00 AM, she tends to the flowers in front of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref name="Kaifa"/> She works until 9:00 PM, at which point she sits under a large {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} nearby. If it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Rains}}, she will seek shelter under that same {{Term|BotW|Tree}}.

Upon first meeting {{Term|BotW|Jana}}, she will be abrasive towards {{Term|BotW|Link|link}},<ref>{{Cite|What do YOU want?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> and she asks him not to bother her as she is thinking about something.<ref>{{Cite|I'm thinking about something right now... Can you please not bother me?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can speak to her younger sister, {{Term|BotW|Gleema|link}}, to learn that {{Term|BotW|Jana}} likes {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Tee heeeee!♪ OK, I'm gonna tell you! Ready? Here it coooooomes! My big sister, Jana, loves {{Color|BotW Blue|armoranth}}!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> Upon remembering that this is a secret, {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} says that {{Term|BotW|Jana}} will be mad at her again.<ref name="Mad">{{Cite|... Oh... That was a secret, wasn't it?! Jana is gonna be mad at me again!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Awww... I wasn't supposed to tell anyone that my big sister, Jana, loves {{Color|BotW Blue|armoranth}}. Jana is gonna be mad at me again!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} talks to {{Term|BotW|Jana}} while he has at least one {{Term|BotW|Armoranth}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, she asks if he can give it to her.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm... What's that? What do you have there? That's {{Color|BotW Blue|armoranth}}, isn't it?! Could you...um...maybe give me some?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> If he does, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} thanks him and reveals that it is her favorite plant, and since he happened to have one, they could get along with each other.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! For you to be walking around with my favorite plant... It seems you and I may just get along after all.|Jana|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Jana}} then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} why he came to {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}, as she believes there is nothing interesting in the {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That said... {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} is famous for... Well, nothing. So I have to ask... What could you possibly have come here for?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds that he is a traveler or just wandering, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} will have a request for him.<ref>{{Cite|I see... A traveler, eh? In that case, I have a request for you.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} is a fan of {{Plural|BotW|Insect|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Jana}} does not understand.<ref>{{Cite|My little sister, Gleema, is a huge fan of {{Color|BotW Blue|insects}}. I know, I don't get it either.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Supposedly, there is a type of {{Term|BotW|Insect}} that {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} wants to see, but she will not tell {{Term|BotW|Jana}} what it is.<ref>{{Cite|But apparently there's a certain {{Color|BotW Blue|insect}} she's dying to see in real life. She won't tell me about it, though. I suppose there are some things you just can't talk about with those you're closest to.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he could figure out what the {{Term|BotW|Insect}} is, as {{Term|BotW|Jana}} wants to know for {{Term|BotW|Gleema}}'s birthday.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, would you mind talking to her and trying to figure out which silly {{Color|BotW Blue|insect}} she's so crazy about? It's for her birthday... Think of it as a special birthday favor. Ooh, or even a birthday quest. Look, just do it, OK?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> After this, the "{{Term|BotW|Little Sister's Big Request|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

After beginning the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Jana}} about {{Term|BotW|Gleema}}. When asked about her sister during the day, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} mentions that {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} is looking for {{Plural|BotW|Insect}}.<ref name="Insects"/> If it is nighttime when {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} states that {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} is probably asleep.<ref name="Asleep">{{Cite|My little sister, Gleema, is probably asleep right now.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She then reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find out what the {{Term|BotW|Insect}} that {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} wants to see is.<ref>{{Cite|But I wonder what kind of {{Color|BotW Blue|insect Gleema}} wants to see...|Jana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I wonder why she won't tell me about it... Please... Can you ask her for me?|Jana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|When she wakes up, will you ask her?|Jana|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} next speaks to {{Term|BotW|Gleema}}, she shares that {{Term|BotW|Jana}} hates dragonflies.<ref>{{Cite|My big sister, Jana, hates {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} recounts that she was once reading a book about {{Plural|BotW|Insect}} that contained depictions of a {{Term|BotW|Warm Darner|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Cold Darner|link}}, and an {{Term|BotW|Electric Darner|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I once saw pictures of a {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, a {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, and an {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}} in a book...|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> However, when she showed the dragonflies to {{Term|BotW|Jana}}, she said they were creepy and got mad at her.<ref>{{Cite|But when I showed them to Jana, she said their eyes were creepy and got mad.|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> When she remembers that this is a secret, {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} realizes that {{Term|BotW|Jana}} is going to get mad at her again.<ref name="Mad"/> {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} thinks the only reason {{Term|BotW|Jana}} found the pictures creepy was because they were close up, and they would be much cuter in real life.<ref>{{Cite|But I think that's because the pictures were all up close and stuff! I bet real ones are super cute!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Jana}}, she is shocked to hear that {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} wants to see the three different types of dragonflies.<ref>{{Cite|No... now way! Of all things, she wants to see a gross {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonfly}}?! A {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, a {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, AND an {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}}? {{Color|BotW Red|Three different types}}?!|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She realizes that the reason {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} never told her about her wish was due to her hatred of dragonflies.<ref>{{Cite|Ugggh... Anything but {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}... Just thinking about their eyes makes me feel sick. I see. So Gleema knows that I despise {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}, and that's why she wouldn't tell me how much she loves them...|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Since it is for {{Term|BotW|Gleema}}'s birthday, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he could find a {{Term|BotW|Warm Darner}}, a {{Term|BotW|Cold Darner}}, and an {{Term|BotW|Electric Darner}} for her.<ref>{{Cite|Well, this is her birthday wish... I didn't have a choice. Bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}}, please. I want to surprise Gleema, so don't tell her about this!|Jana|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} has collected all three dragonflies, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} will nervously ask for them.<ref>{{Cite|Have you secured a {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}}? {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|*gulp*}}}} Right... In that case, may I have them?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, once she sees them, she is unable to get over her fear and instead asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to deliver them to {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} on her behalf.<ref>{{Cite|... ... {{Small|Ewww...}} BAH! I CAN'T DO THIS! Please give them to her...just...give them to Gleema yourself!|Jana|BotW}}</ref> After receiving the dragonflies, {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} will excitedly run over to show them to her older sister,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Big Sis! Lookee, lookee! It's a real life {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}! And a {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}} and an {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}} too!! I'll bring 'em over so you can see!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> much to {{Term|BotW|Jana}}'s horror.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|HEY! Cut it out!! EEEEEEK!!}}|Jana|BotW}}</ref>

After she manages to calm down, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} decides to reward {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his help.<ref>{{Cite|Hey. I've managed to calm down. Somehow. Now then... I must thank you for catching those {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She then gives him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here, take this.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> While she is fine with most {{Plural|BotW|Insect}}, she explains that she does not like dragonflies.<ref>{{Cite|I'm find with most insects. The only ones I simply can't stand are {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}. When I think about them looking at me with those evil eyes... *shudder*|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She takes a deep breath to calm herself,<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* That was rough. OK... I need to take a deep breath.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> after which the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Jana}} after completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}}, she will recognize him.<ref>{{Cite|So. It's YOU.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> In the morning, she remarks that he is up early.<ref>{{Cite|It's so early, but you're already up?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> At night, she encourages him to stay at {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}} if he needs somewhere to stay.<ref>{{Cite|Hey. If you're looking for a place to crash, you can stay here.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She also makes comments on the weather, noting if it has cooled down or if it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|display=Raining}}.<ref>{{Cite|It sure has cooled off, hasn't it?|Jana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey. Unfortunate weather we're having.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends his conversation with her, she tells him to take care.<ref>{{Cite|Take care.|Jana|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to ask {{Term|BotW|Jana}} several questions.<ref>{{Cite|You want to ask me something, don't you?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already started the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, he is able to ask {{Term|BotW|Jana}} about {{Term|BotW|Hudson|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Hudson?] If you're looking for Hudson the {{Color|BotW Blue|carpenter}}...|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She says that he mentioned building a town before he left for {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|He said something about building a village {{Color|BotW Red|from the ground up}}. Then he headed off toward {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Akkala}}.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala}} is east of {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}}, and she encourages him to check it out if he is curious.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Akkala}} is to the east of here. If you're curious, you should head over there.|Jana|BotW}}</ref>

Next, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Jana}} who she is, she states that she is the second eldest of her sisters, and she works on the flower patch while {{Term|BotW|Kaifa|link}}, her older sister, takes care of the livestock.<ref name="Kaifa"/> {{Term|BotW|Jana}} will also say what {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} is doing.<ref name="Insects"/><ref name="Asleep"/> She mentions that, while she does not care about most {{Plural|BotW|Insect}}, there is a certain type that she is unable to stand.<ref>{{Cite|So long as they don't tamper with my flowers, I have no problem with {{Color|BotW Blue|insects}}. Well...except for one type of {{Color|BotW Blue|insect}}.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses to ask about where they are, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} tells him that they are at {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|South Akkala Stable}}.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> East of the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} says {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to see both {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Sea|link}}, but those are the only interesting attractions in the area.<ref>{{Cite|From just past the farm to the east, you can take in a sweeping view of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Akkala}} and the {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala Sea}}. That's about it as far as local attractions go...|Jana|BotW}}</ref>
