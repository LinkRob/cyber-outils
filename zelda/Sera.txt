{{Term/Store|Sera||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
Sera is the mother of {{Term|TPHD|Beth|link}} and the wife of [[Hanch]]. She runs [[Sera's Sundries]], the only shop in [[Ordon Village]], where [[Link]] can buy the [[Slingshot]]. The shop also stocks [[Milk]], [[Bee Larvae]], and [[Lantern Oil]].

Sera owns a [[Cat]] named Link that she cares for dearly. The feline runs away after she scolds him for eating a fish meant for her family's dinner. She is so distressed over her missing cat that she tires herself too much to sell anything in her shop.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhhh... I'm terribly sorry...but my mind is so filled with thoughts of my little cat that I simply can't deal with costumers right now... Ohhh... If I had known this was going to happen, I would have let my little kitty eat fish to his heart's content... Link, m'dear... I'm sorry, but would you leave me be? I'll serve you milk later.|Sera|TP}}</ref> Link the cat is found near the water in the back of [[Jaggle]]'s house, but runs away whenever Link goes near. After Link catches two [[fish]] with the [[Fishing Rod]], the cat grabs one of them and takes it back home. Sera is so happy with her cat's return that she gives Link a [[Bottle]] that is half full with Milk, having given the other half to the cat.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh, just look... Isn't he so cute, the way he laps up that milk? Say, m'dear, why don't you have some, too! I'm in a good mood, so it's on the house today! Oh, don't worry, you don't have to return the empty bottle, m'dear. You can reuse it--you know, put something else in it. Recycling is a very important thing, m'dear!|Sera|TP}}</ref> At this point, Link is now able to purchase items.
