{{Term/Store|Makure||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Makure|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Makure}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} child who lives in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She resides at the {{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop|link}} {{Term|BotW|Stall|link}}, which is run by her grandmother, {{Term|BotW|Danda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's dark out now, so I'm going back to my vaba's booth!|Makure|BotW}}</ref>

During the day, {{Term|BotW|Makure}} runs around near the {{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her, she is surprised that someone is visiting {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'otta! Oh, wow! You're a vai from out of town!|Makure|BotW}}</ref> She then explains that she was pretending to be a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I... I was just pretending to be a horse!|Makure|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks why, she questions if he knows about "Horsey Fatback".<ref>{{Cite|What? You don't know about Horsey Fatback? You don't have one where you're from?|Makure|BotW}}</ref> She explains that "Horsey Fatback" is a huge animal that walks around the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, referring to the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's this really big horse-thing that walks around outside the village, like STOMP STOMP.|Makure|BotW}}</ref> She then comments that if you get close, it makes loud sounds like "BZZBZZ," referring to the power of {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|display=Naboris}} possesses.<ref>{{Cite|If you get close to him, he goes... {{Big|KABOOM!}} And... {{Big|BZZBZZ!}} It's really exciting!|Makure|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Makure}} believes that the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} is good, saying that he is only in a bad mood because others believe he is bad.<ref>{{Cite|But I think Horsey Fatback is actually good! He's only in a bad mood 'cause everyone keeps saying he's bad!|Makure|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Makure}} can sometimes he found looking out at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} from the steps of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}}, stomping and claiming that she can see {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}.<ref>{{Cite|*STOMP...STOMP* I think I can see him!|Makure|BotW}}</ref> While she sleeps at the {{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop}}, she dreams of stomping.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|*STOMP...STOMP*}}|Makure|BotW}}</ref>

In the evenings, {{Term|BotW|Makure}} will gather with the other three children who reside in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}. These are her sister {{Term|BotW|Kalani|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pearle|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}}. After completing the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} will be late to their meetings. During this time, {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} asks the other two if they heard what happened to {{Term|BotW|Dalia}}'s crops.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard about Dalia's crops?|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Makure}} notes that, since then, {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} has not stopped crying.<ref>{{Cite|Dalia... She won't stop crying...|Makure|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pearle}} proclaims that she will never forgive the person who wronged {{Term|BotW|Dalia}}.<ref>{{Cite|Who would do something like that? I'll never forgive you!|Pearle|BotW}}</ref>

Once all four children are present, {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} will ask them if they are in love.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you guys! Are you in love?|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Makure}} then responds that she wants to attend {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link}}'s class when she grows up, that way she can fall in love and get married.<ref>{{Cite|When I'm all growed up, I will be! I'll go to Miss Ashai's class, and I'll become in love and I'll get married!|Makure|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pearle}} then comments that, according to her mother, {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} herself is unmarried, and she wonders what that means.<ref>{{Cite|Mom says it's really strange that Miss Ashai is unmarried but teaches her class on voe. I wonder what unmarried means...|Pearle|BotW}}</ref> Lastly, {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} says that she heard being in love is the sweetest thing in the world, but does not think she can handle something sweeter than berries.<ref>{{Cite|I heard that being in love and being married is the sweetest thing in the world! But I really like berries, and those are really sweet. I don't know if I can handle something sweeter than that!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Makure}} will be sad that the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} has left the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|There was this big horse-thing walking around like STOMP STOMP, but... It looks like he went away somewhere...|Makure|BotW}}</ref> She blames the people of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} for making it leave, since they always said it was bad.<ref>{{Cite|He must have left because everyone kept saying he was a bad boy all the time!|Makure|BotW}}</ref> While standing on the steps of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}}, {{Term|BotW|Makure}} will be able to see {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} on {{Term|BotW|Spectacle Rock|link}}, and she wonders if it has finished stomping.<ref>{{Cite|*STOMP...STOMP* He went over there... I wonder if he's done stomping?|Makure|BotW}}</ref>

During "{{Term|BotW|The Undefeated Champ|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Makure}} can be seen among the crowd gathering at the {{Term|BotW|Southeast Gate|link}} as {{Term|BotW|Shabonne|link}} announces the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|link}}.
