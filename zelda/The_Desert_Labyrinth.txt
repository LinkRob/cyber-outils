"{{Term/Store|The Desert Labyrinth||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Desert Labyrinth}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|South Lomei Labyrinth}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can receive this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} when he steps inside the {{Term|BotW|South Lomei Labyrinth|link}}. He will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Dila Maag|link}}, who tells him he will receive a blessing at the end of the maze.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot here... My blessing awaits you at the labyrinth's end.}}|Dila Maag|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dila Maag Shrine|link}} can be seen beneath a gate on the floor that is located in the central corridor.

To navigate the maze, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must climb across walls to avoid touching the pools of {{Term|BotW|Malice}} along some of the {{Term|BotW|South Lomei Labyrinth|display=Labyrinth's}} floors and walls. He must also be careful of the two {{Plural|BotW|Black Bokoblin|link}} patrolling on top of the {{Term|BotW|South Lomei Labyrinth|display=Labyrinth}} walls. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the end of the {{Term|BotW|South Lomei Labyrinth}}, he will be able to access {{Term|BotW|Dila Maag Shrine}}. By activating the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
