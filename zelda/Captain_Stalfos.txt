{{Term/Store|Captain Stalfos||Series, FPTRR}} is a {{Term|FPTRR|Boss|link}} in {{FPTRR}}. He is the leader of the group of {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate|link}}. In order for {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} to join the pirate crew, he has to be dead. If he wants to survive, he must defeat the captain in a sword duel. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} succeeds in this task, he will be given the {{Term|FPTRR|Bone Ocarina}}, which may be used at any pier to call the pirate ship.
{{clear|left}}
==Possessed {{Term|FPTRR|Captain Stalfos}}==
[[File:FPTRR Captain Stalfos (Possessed) Sprite.png|{{Term|FPTRR|Captain Stalfos}} when possessed by {{Plural|FPTRR|Insect}}.|thumb|left]]
Later on, the captain gets possessed by {{Plural|FPTRR|Insect}} from the {{Term|FPTRR|Insect Cavern|link}}, and {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} must calm him down in order to continue on his journey. To do this, he must go to the {{Term|FPTRR|Ship's Hold|link}}, surround the captain by pushing barrels of explosives around him, and then detonating them. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} is successful, the crew of {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}} will give him an {{Term|FPTRR|Bone Ocarina|section=Upgrade|display=upgraded Bone Ocarina|link}}, which can be played near one of ten pirate {{Plural|FPTRR|Treasure Chest|link}} to unlock it.
{{clear}}

==Trivia==
*The top screen displayed during this fight is a reference to the Arcade ''{{Wp|Punch-Out!!}}'' titles, where the challenger and his opponent would be displayed with changing expressions based on their health.
