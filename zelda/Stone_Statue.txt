==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Stub|TLoZ}}
{{Manual|TLoZ|Traps and Stone Statues|Somebody placed these in the labyrinth a long time ago. They suddenly attack Link when he approaches them. So you've really got to watch out!}}
In {{TLoZ}}, {{Plural|Series|Stone Statue}} are tall, sometimes fire-breathing, rock monoliths scattered abundantly in {{Plural|Series|Dungeon|link}}. They reflect the motif of the {{Term|Series|Dungeon}} in which they appear, expressing it in the form of individualistic symbolism and decoration.

{{Stub|ALttP}}

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LA}}

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Stub|OoS}}
{{Plural|OoS|Stone Statue}} in {{OoS}} can "watch" {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} as he navigates through {{Plural|OoS|Dungeon|link}}, a trait that the {{Plural|Series|Stone Statue|display=Statues}} in other titles do not have.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Stub|OoA}}
{{Plural|OoA|Eye Block}} in {{OoA}} can "watch" {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} as he navigates through {{Plural|OoA|Dungeon|link}}, a trait that the {{Plural|Series|Eye Block|display=Statues}} in other titles do not have.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Plural|TWWHD|Stone Statue}} in {{TWW}} appear as {{Term|TWWHD|Moblin|link}}-shaped statues in the {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}} and the {{Term|TWWHD|Savage Labyrinth|link}}, extending from the walls. Though they brandish spears, if they spot or smell {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}}, they will attempt to destroy him with other means; the {{Plural|Series|Stone Statue|display=Statues}} that inhabit the {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress}} will attempt to strike him with laser beams from their noses, similarly to {{Term|TWWHD|Beamos|link}}, while the {{Plural|Series|Stone Statue|display=Statues}} that reside within the 50th floor of the {{Term|TWWHD|Savage Labyrinth}} will generate fire from their mouths.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Stub|FSA}}

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Stone Statue}} in {{TMC}} appear in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, where they persistently fire {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}} in a manner similar to {{Plural|TWWHD|Warship|link}} from {{TWW}}. These {{Plural|TMC|Bomb}} can be deflected to destroy the {{Plural|TMC|Stone Statue}}, though {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must first split himself with the power of the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}} to destroy all the {{Plural|TMC|Stone Statue|display=Statues}} in the room simultaneously to proceed.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Stub|ST}}

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Stone Statue}} are depictions of {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} that are commonly found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref name="CaC"/> The purpose with which they were placed is unknown.<ref name="CaC"/> In {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Central Hyrule|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Faron (Region)|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hebra|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)|link}}, and parts of {{Term|BotW|Necluda|link}}, they are used to mark the locations of {{Plural|BotW|Offering Spot|link}}. Similarly, a pair of {{Plural|BotW|Stone Statue}} west of the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins|link}} hide some {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}}. These same {{Term|BotW|Stone Statue|display=Statues}} are also included in the {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} associated with the "{{Term|BotW|Shelter from the Storm|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|An Ancient Text|link}} refers to a {{Term|BotW|Stone Statue}} as being cursed, and it states that one should "pierce its gaze" to reveal an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When a dark light resides in the cursed statue's eyes, pierce its gaze to purge the seal from the shrine.|An Ancient Text|BotW}}</ref> During "{{Term|BotW|The Cursed Statue|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can approach the grove of {{Plural|BotW|Stone Statue}} near {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|link}} at night to find one has glowing eyes. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots this {{Term|BotW|Stone Statue|display=Statue}} with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kam Urog Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|display=Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|You fired an arrow into the glowing eyes of the statue, and the ancient shrine was revealed.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
