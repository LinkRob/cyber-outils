{{Term/Store|Mount Agaat||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.

==Features and overview==
Mount Agaat is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]], marking the far western edge of Hyrule. The top of the mountain is inaccessible; only the foothills on the east side can be explored and traversed. This is the only instance in {{BotW|-}} where a named and marked location is inaccessible through normal gameplay.

Areas such as these are out of bounds but still within the games main playable box, the map being surrounded by 4 boundaries, and any out of bounds areas that intrude this box are given their own boundaries, but are often given rough collision as the terrain is still being rendered as inside the main map area. Terrain beyond said box lack collisions completely.

In {{TotK|-}}, the [[The Depths|Depths]] extend partially underneath Mount Agaat, further than on the surface. This portion in the depths is the half of the mountain that exists within the game's main square shaped boundaries. This can be used with the [[Hero's Path Mode]] to give the illusion that Link is able to traverse the mountain. It is also the only place in the Depths that seems to extend past the surface world's boundaries.{{Fact|TotK}}
