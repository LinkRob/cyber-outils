'''Roam''' is a character featured in the {{ALttP|Ishinomori}}. Referred to in one of the chapters as "The Mystery Knight," {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} is a descendant of a master archer from the ranks of the {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Knight of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>"Roam is the descendant of one of the Knights of Hyrule who was a master archer." Chapter 8 of The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past comic by Shotaro Ishinomori</ref> When {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} discovered that {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Agahnim|link}} was sending the Seven {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Maiden|link}} to the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Dark World|link}}, he sought after them  through the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Portal (Object)|link}} near {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Roam   is the descendant of one of the Knights of Hyrule who was a master   archer. When he discovered that Agahnim was sending members of the  Seven  Wise Men's line to the Dark World, he went after them through  the  portal near Death Mountain.|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} considers {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link|link}} to be immature, stubborn and lacking discipline to wield the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Master Sword|link}}, with {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} boasting that the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Book of Mudora|link}} supports him instead as the hero who will save {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Hyrule|link}}.<ref name=roam/>

It is stated in the comic that the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Dark World|link}} changes the shape of its inhabitants based on their hearts, and a  person can only maintain human form by controlling their emotions.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=You   penetrated the gate to the Dark World... That wicked realm has the   power to change your body to reflect your heart. The emotions now in   your heart are negative and evil! You nearly became a '''beast'''! [...] Yes, Link, but you can never win with hatred alone... Even if you were victorious, darkness would consume you!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=7}}</ref><ref name=link>{{Cite Book|quote=Oh no! My negative emotions are turning me into a beast! I must not surrender to these emotions! I'll live a beast's life--forever! I won't!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=8}}</ref> Thus, when {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} enters battle, he transforms into a humanoid eagle. Contrary to {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}}, who suppresses his transformation (shown to be a {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Wolf Link|display=wolf-like creature|link}}),<ref name=link/> {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} considers his transformation an advantage and willingly lets his emotions change him during battle.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Do   not be deceived by my form... When I do battle, I am transformed into  a  monstrous eagle by the dark forces of this world!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} wields a sword when fighting as an eagle but can also be seen using a {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Crossbow|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} first appears to challenge {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link|link}} for the right to wield the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Master Sword|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=My name is Roam! I challenge you...for the right to wield the Master Sword!!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=1}}</ref> He transforms into an eagle and proceeds to attack {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}}, who counters {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}}'s attack and defeats him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=You fight well...or is it just luck? I may have misjudged you, hero...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=3}}</ref> {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} apologizes for having misjudged {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} and reveals to him that the ancient {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Book of Mudora|link}} tells that the one who corrupts the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Golden Land|link}} will be defeated with the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Silver Arrow|link}}, the sacred item that {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} has been seeking.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It   is written in the Book of Mudora: 'He who corrupts the land of gold   shall fall to the shaft of the enchanted arrow of silver...' My family   has always excelled in archery, and it is my destiny to destroy Ganon!  I  have not yet obtained the enchanted arrow, but...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> When {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} tells {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} he is also a descendant of the {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Knight of Hyrule|link}}, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} is overjoyed and begins to think that the two of them can join forces to find {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=So '''your''' ancestors were Knights of Hyrule? Mine too! Perhaps we can join forces to find Zelda!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} is shocked to hear Zelda has been transported to the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Dark World|link}}, saying {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} is too much of a "weakling" to wield the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Master Sword}} and save the princess.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='''What?!''' Princess Zelda is here, in the Dark World?! Where were the knights? Why didn't they protect her?! They must be '''mad''', giving a weakling like you the Master Sword and sending you to rescue her!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> The young hero tries to defend himself by saying he has the three {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Pendant of Virtue|link}} to prove his worth,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Weakling? I'll show you who's weak! I've been through a '''lot''' to get  this far! I have the Pendants of Power, Wisdom and Courage to  '''prove''' it!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> but {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} is unimpressed by {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}}'s lack of discipline and immaturity, and begins to walk away to continue searching for the Silver Arrow.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I find that '''difficult'''   to believe, with your lack of discipline, and obvious immaturity...   Well, I'm off to find the enchanted Silver Arrow... Without it, no one   can defeat Ganon.|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> Before disappearing, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} tells {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} that he senses they will have to join forces against {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Ganon|link}}, and advises the young hero to stay alive until then.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I have a feeling that the time will come when we must join forces against him... Until then, '''do''' try to stay '''alive.'''|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref>

[[File:Roam vs Agahnim.png|thumb|left|{{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} dies saving {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Princess Zelda}}]]
{{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} later reappears in {{Term|ALttP|Turtle Rock|link}}, arriving just in time to help {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} defeat a {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Trinexx|display=three-headed dragon|link}} and save {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Now we shall truly see if one of us is the "Legendary Hero"...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=16}}</ref> After they escape from {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Turtle Rock}}, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} boasts that the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Book of Mudora}}'s prophecy supports him as the Legendary Hero, for he is the one seeking the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Silver Arrow}}, and leaves to find it.<ref name=roam>{{Cite Book|quote=Admit   it, Link. It seems to support the Book of Mudora's prophecy that only   the enchanted arrow can defeat the one who corrupted this world. You  are '''stubborn''' and '''foolish'''... '''I''' will defeat Ganon!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> However, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Sahasrahla|link}} mentioned only a man who has overcome his own negative thoughts and emotions can become the true hero. Since {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} has not, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} fears that this might actually be his downfall even if he finds the Silver Arrow.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=So Roam may be '''right''' about the arrow...but Sahasrahla '''also'''   said that only a man who has overcome his own negative thoughts and   emotions can become the true hero... Even if Roam does find the arrow,   his attitude might be his downfall...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=7}}</ref> Later, when {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} and Zelda arrive to {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Ganon's Tower|link}}, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} suddenly appears and admits he has failed to find the arrows.  Overwhelmed by his failure, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} transforms into an eagle and flies up to the tower, worrying {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} since {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}}'s emotions are out of control and could cause him to remain a beast forever.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Roam!! His emotions are out of control... He'll become a beast forever!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref> Zelda then asks for the aid of the maidens,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Oh, mystical maidens... Lend us your power...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref> which allows them to enter the pitch-dark tower.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It's pitch dark... I can't see anything!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref> Inside, the group is attacked by illusions of {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}}'s past foes,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It--It's Agahnim! It '''can't''' be! I '''destroyed''' him! [...] The Lanmola?! The Moldorm?! They can't be real! I defeat them all!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=11}}</ref> with a specter of {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Agahnim|link}} dangerously approaching Zelda. {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} quickly transforms into an eagle and attacks the illusion,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='''You!''' '''I'll''' get it, Zelda!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=14}}</ref> although he loses his life dispatching the specter and saving {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Princess Zelda}}. With {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Ganon}} emerging from the illusion's remains, Zelda takes up {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}}'s {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Crossbow|link}} as {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} is trapped in between the trident. Zelda begins to ask for the aid of the {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Maiden|link}} and fires an ordinary arrow from the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Crossbow}}, but as it approaches {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Ganon}}, the power of the {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Maiden}}, Zelda, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}}, and {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}}, turn the arrow into the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Silver Arrow}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It was the '''enchanted arrow'''...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> destroying the King of Thieves.

==Trivia==
* {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}} is based on 002 from Shotaro Ishinomori's popular manga ''{{Wp|Cyborg 009}}'', whose pre-cyborg name was Jet Link. Like {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Roam}}, 002 was able to fly.
