TITLE : Helmasaurs

#REDIRECT [[Helmasaur]]

These are giant rat creatures with a protective metal plate on his back. If you don't have the [[Clawshot]] you must either let them pass you when they are charging at you then slash at their bare behinds. If you ''do'' have the [[Clawshot]], just shoot at their shells and throw the shell at them, that won't kill them so start slashing away at them.
