{{Term/Store|Medal of Honor|Medals of Honor|Series, BotW|plural}} are items in {{BotW}}.{{Name Ref|BotW}}

==Location and Uses==
After defeating {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can visit {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} to receive a Medal of Honor, one for each type of overworld boss.<ref>{{Cite|You've done it! You've defeated all the {{Color|BotW Blue|Moldugas}}! [...] Oh, yes! Here, let me give you this.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is proof you have defeated every last {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}} in Hyrule.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> To obtain them, Link must defeat all 40 {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Stalnox|link}}, all 40 {{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus|link|display=Talus}}es, and all four {{Plural|BotW|Molduga|link}}. If Link has not defeated all of them, he can ask Kilton for the exact number of bosses slain so far.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see how many {{Color|BotW Blue|Stone Taluses}} you've harvested so far... Looks like [X] out of 40.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> When fighting a boss that has already been defeated, the text "★ Defeated" will appear to the right of its health bar.
