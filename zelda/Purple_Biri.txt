{{Term/Store|Purple Biri|Purple Biri|Series, ALBW|plural}} are enemies in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 167}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ALBW|Purple Biri}} appear only in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle|link}}, where a purple variant of {{Term|ALBW|Gigabari|link}} serves as a {{Term|ALBW|Sub-Boss|link}}. When the {{Term|ALBW|Gigabari}} is defeated, it splits into fifteen {{Plural|ALBW|Purple Biri}} which must also be defeated. {{Plural|ALBW|Purple Biri}} share the same characteristics as {{Plural|ALBW|Yellow Biri|link}}. They slowly float in random directions, occasionally stopping to electrify themselves; {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} will take damage if he strikes a {{Term|ALBW|Purple Biri}} in this state with his {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword|link|display=Sword}}.
