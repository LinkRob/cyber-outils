{{Term/Store|Floating Garden||Series, TFH}} is a {{Term|TFH|Level|link}} in {{TFH}}. It is the first Level of the {{Term|TFH|Sky Realm|link}} area.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|TFH|Floating Garden}} takes place in the sky high above the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}. It consists of clusters of floating islands with vibrant foliage, including grass, flowers and vines. Throughout the {{Term|TFH|Level}}, strong currents of wind blow through parts of the Stages, which may knock the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} off the islands. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} however can walk against the current in order to cross to other areas. On certain islands, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must also cross bridges to other islands, where wind may or may not blow through. Some of these bridges are crumbling, requiring the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} be extra careful in crossing them. Later in the Level, {{Plural|TFH|Cucco|link}} can also be found, which are used to glide over to other islands and platforms. The {{Term|TFH|Gripshot|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Bow|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Bomb|link}} are provided in this Level. 

==={{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge}}===
{{Term|TFH|Floating Garden}} includes the following {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}}: "{{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|section=Evade the Wallmaster!|display=Evade the Wallmaster!|link}}", "{{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|section=Win without using a sword!|display=Win without using a sword!|link}}", and "{{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|section=Don't fall at all!|display=Don't fall at all!|link}}".

===Materials===
At the end of the Level, three {{Plural|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}} can be opened. Two contain the same {{Term|TFH|Material|link}}, and one contains another.
