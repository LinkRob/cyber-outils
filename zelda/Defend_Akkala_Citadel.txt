{{Term/Store|Defend Akkala Citadel||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 53 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defend Akkala Citadel|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Defend Akkala Citadel}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must capture three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} while defending an Allied Stronghold. Two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are found at the top of the map, and the third at the bottom. A {{Term|HWAoC|Black Hinox|link}} and either a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} or {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} are each a {{Term|HWAoC|Boss|link}} of one of the northern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Boss}} of the southern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}. 

Soon after the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} begin an assault on the Allied Stronghold. There is also a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} and an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}} found guarding one of the northern {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}, along with another {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} in front of the southern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}. Throughout the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, as the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} move to different parts of the map and defeat {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}, more {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} such as {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} will spawn. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured. 

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
