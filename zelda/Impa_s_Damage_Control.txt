{{Term/Store|Impa's Damage Control||HWAoC}} is a level 48 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Impa's Damage Control|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Impa's Damage Control}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Sunset Firefly|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ancient Core|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}'s objective is to defeat eight {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within eight minutes. Although she does not move from her position or do anything else, {{Term|HWAoC|Purah|link}} can also be found in the southeast corner of the map at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} to be defeated are six {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}} and three {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}. They are split into four different groups. The first group consists of just a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}}, the second of a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}}, and the third and fourth of a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} and two {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} in the third group is inactive until one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} around it is brought down to half of its health. Once all of them are defeated, a gate opens, the timer resets to four minutes, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}} comes through the gate towards {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} must be defeated for the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} to end.
