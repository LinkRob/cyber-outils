{{Term/Store|Kofu||Series}} is an {{Term|ST|Anouki|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref> He resides in {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Snow Realm|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|ST|Kofu}} lives in a house in the northeast portion of {{Term|ST|Anouki Village}}. When {{Term|ST|Link|link}} visits the village for the first time, he is tasked by {{Term|ST|Honcho|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Anouki}} chief, to pair up all of the {{Plural|ST|Anouki}} in order to put together a village watch for all the monsters that have begun to appear after the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} vanished. {{Term|ST|Kofu}} comments to {{Term|ST|Link}} he will not work with anyone who has big horns like his.<ref>{{Cite|My thoughts? I won't work with anyone who's got big horns like mine. Mine are the best in the village. Maybe even best in the world!|Kofu|ST}}</ref> Due to his preferences, {{Term|ST|Link}} ends pairing him with an {{Term|ST|Anouki}} named {{Term|ST|Noko|link}}.

When {{Term|ST|Link}} brings the traveling {{Term|ST|Goron|link}} who wants to see the snow to {{Term|ST|Anouki Village}}, {{Term|ST|Kofu}} claims to be the {{Term|ST|Anouki}} chief and offers up {{Term|ST|Honcho}}'s house to the {{Term|ST|Goron}},<ref>{{Cite|Mmm? Yesirree, I sure am the honcho... No doubt about it... Why else would I be sittin' in his--I mean, MY house?|Kofu|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well, Honcho, since you are going on a trip, thanks for letting me use your house!|Goron|ST}}</ref> rendering the true chief homeless.<ref>{{Cite|Some big orange monster decided to set up camp in my place! I can't exactly go home now! Where am I supposed to sleep? Tell me, where?!|Honcho|ST}}</ref> After speaking with the {{Term|ST|Goron}}, {{Term|ST|Kofu}} decides to visit {{Term|ST|Goron Village|link}} to experience the warmer climate.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there, guy! I've just been chattin' with my rocky pal here. He's really been talkin' up Goron Village to me. Apparently, it's toasty all year round and the perfect place for takin' it easy!|Kofu|ST}}</ref> If {{Term|ST|Link}} transports {{Term|ST|Kofu}} to {{Term|ST|Goron Village}} aboard the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}, {{Term|ST|Kofu}} will reward him with a {{Term|ST|Force Gem|link}}.
