==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Dento}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} can be found at his workshop, the {{Term|BotW|Hammerhead|link}}, which is located in {{Term|BotW|Marot Mart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. According to the {{Term|BotW|History of the Zora, Part Four|link}}, he made the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} when she was born,<ref>{{Cite|The celebrate her birth, the smithy Dento presented Mipha with a gift: a mighty spear called the Lightscale Trident.|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref> which he maintains in the present day.<ref>{{Cite|He's the one who fixed Mipha's weapon, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}|Marot|BotW}}</ref> He has two apprentices, {{Term|BotW|Fronk|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ledo|link}}, who he has tasked with making repairs to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref name="Ledo"/>

Like all elder {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}, {{Term|BotW|Dento}} is initially hostile and untrusting towards {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} due to the loss of {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}.<ref>{{Cite|So...the Hylian who stole Lady Mipha away thinks he can just come wandering in here like nothing happened. What business do you have here after all these years?!|Dento|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Dento}} is short and curt with his answers.<ref>{{Cite|[What is this place?] The weapons workshop...|Dento|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] Trident maintenance...|Dento|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[A trident?] The {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}} and the {{Color|BotW Blue|ceremonial trident}}...|Dento|BotW}}</ref> Eventually, his annoyance at {{Term|BotW|Link}} becomes too much, saying there is no reason for him to be there without the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have been making a racket since you walked in here. There is no point in coming here if you do not possess the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}. Leave. Now.|Dento|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} is calmed, {{Term|BotW|Dento}} will be relieved that {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} can rest in peace.<ref>{{Cite|So I have heard. Hylian Champion Link... Now Lady Mipha's spirit can finally rest in peace.|Dento|BotW}}</ref> He offers to repair both the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} and the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident|link}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever breaks them.<ref>{{Cite|As the Champion, you should be able to use both the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}} and the {{Color|BotW Blue|ceremonial trident}}. If you break either one...please let me know so I can remake it for you.|Dento|BotW}}</ref> Repairing the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} costs a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}, and five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}... I somehow felt it had disappeared from this world. Shall I remake it for you?|Dento|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In that case, I will need a {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora Spear}}, {{Color|BotW Red|one}} {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|five pieces}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}.|Dento|BotW}}</ref> A new {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} can be made from a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear}} and five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|ceremonial trident}}...
