{{Term/Store|Juney's Sand Seal Plushies||Series, TotK}} is a {{Term|TotK|Mini-Game|link}} in {{TotK}}.

== Location and Rules ==
Juney is a traveling {{Term|TotK|Hylian|link}} who learned how to make {{Plural|TotK|Sand-Seal Plush|link}} from a {{Term|TotK|Gerudo|link}} doll-maker.<ref>{{Cite|I can't get lost now. Not after spending all that time with the Gerudo dollmaker learning to make <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">sand-seal plushies</font>.|Juney|TotK}}</ref> She carries a wagon full of them and plans to present them to the school children of {{Term|TotK|Hateno Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I refuse to give up on my dream of handing these out to the schoolkids!|Juney|TotK}}</ref> Unfortunately, she ends up getting lost on the way.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Was this NOT the way to Hateno Village?|Juney|TotK}}</ref>

Link can find Juney and her sand seal plushies at several locations in {{Term|TotK|Hyrule|link}}. When Link approaches Juney, she is seen practicing her impression to present the kids her sand seal plushies. When Link tries to get her attention, she gets startled, along with her {{Term|TotK|horse|link}} who ends up knocking all her sand seal plushies off. Dismayed, Juney blames Link for losing them. If Link offers to help, she will ask 20 {{Plural|TotK|Rupee|link}} for insurance.

The objective is to get all the lost sand seal plushies into the wagon within the alloted time. There are a total of 10 seal plushies to retrieve.<ref>{{Cite|I worked so hard to make the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">10</font> of them, and now this! How am I supposed to gather all these up by myself?!|Juney|TotK}}</ref><ref name="Pay">{{Cite|I'll make you a deal. If you get all <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">10</font> of them for me, I'll pay you.|Juney|TotK}}</ref> This can be accomplished by simply carrying them using {{Term|TotK|Ultrahand|link}}. The wagon is small and has no high roofing, so the plushes can be prone to falling off if placed improperly.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and you gotta be careful loading the toys. They tend to fall out if they're not all the way in.|Juney|TotK}}</ref>

The reward for getting all the sand seals in time are Rupees.<ref name="Pay"></ref>
