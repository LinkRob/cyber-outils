The {{Term/Store|Dueling Peaks||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}} <ref>{{Cite|'''Dueling Peaks'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].''

==Features and Overview==
The Dueling Peaks are located in [[West Necluda]].

In ''Breath of the WIld,'' the peaks were once said to be a whole mountain, but were split by a dragon god so they could go through. Indeed, in ''Tears of the Kingdom,'' [[Memory]] 08, "Zelda and Sonia'',"'' shows the peaks as one mountain, as the Hyrule in this memory is of the distant past.
