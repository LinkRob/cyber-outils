==Overview==
{{Term|OoT|The Legend of Zelda: Original Sound Track}} was released by Pony Canyon on December 18, 1998, and the music is composed by [[Koji Kondo]]. The CD contains the 82 tracks that can be heard in the game. The total runtime of the soundtrack is about 1 hour, 17 minutes.
