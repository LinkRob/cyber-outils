==Location and uses==
The Prince of Red Lions Ship is a boat owned by [[Nyeve]]. It is based on the [[King of Red Lions]], and it can be found in the northwestern quadrant of the [[World of the Ocean King]].

==Nomenclature==
"Prince of Red Lions" is a reference to the boat from which it is inspired, the {{Term|Series|King of Red Lions|link}}.
