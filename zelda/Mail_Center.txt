The {{Term/Store|Mail Center||Series, TWW}} is a location in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|TWWHD Left Arrow}} {{Color|TWWHD Red|Wind Shrine}}<br/>{{Icon|TWWHD Down Arrow}} {{Color|TWWHD Red|Mail Center}}|Sign|TWWHD}}</ref> The term may refer to the mail sorting desk at {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island|link}}, or to the whole aerie in which the sorting desk is located.{{Note|In {{Guide|TWWHD|Prima}}, the aerie is known as the '''Rito Aerie'''.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 88-90}}</ref> In the guide, "Mail Center" refers specifically to the second floor of the aerie.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 315}}</ref> The game itself does not make such a clear distinction and "Mail Center" can be taken to mean the whole aerie. Thus the name "Rito Aerie" is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} The aerie is the home of the {{Term|TWWHD|Rito|link}}, whose sole industry is the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
The aerie is located inside the smaller of the two hollow peaks of {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}}. The main entrance is on the north side of the island but is only accessible via a path on the south side. The path is poorly maintained—the airborne {{Term|TWWHD|Rito}} have no need for it and aside from {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Baito}} is the only non-{{Term|TWWHD|Rito}} seen in the aerie. A wooden placard above the entrance says "Post House" in {{Term|TWWHD|Hylian (Language)|link}}.

When {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} enters the aerie for the first time, the {{Term|TWWHD|Rito Chieftain|link}} welcomes him standing in the center of the room, which features an octogonal floor mosaic. The tunnel straight across from the main entrance leads down to the chieftain's son {{Term|TWWHD|Komali|link}}'s room. The tunnel to the right leads to {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Pond|link}}. On the lefthand side, a rock walkway winds around the edge of the aerie up to a second floor. 

===Upper Rooms===
The upper portion of the walkway features an arch with Hylian text on it. The inscription reads, "{{Term|TWWHD|Post Office}}." Just before the arch is the room where {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Medli|link}} first meet.<ref>{{Cite|You should find {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Medli}} in one of the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|upper rooms}}. Leave the great Valoo to us. In the meanwhile, do your best to aid {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Prince Komali}}!|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Hoskit|link}} stands outside the doorway throughout the game. After {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Cavern|link}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Rito Chieftain}} and his guards {{Term|TWWHD|Basht|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Bisht|link}} inhabit the room. On the opposite side of the room is a doorway out to an upper balcony overlooking {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Pond}}. The same mosaic from the aerie floor appears above the doorways inside the room.

Past the {{Term|TWWHD|Post Office}} archway is the {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center}} office area, featuring a 4-counter service desk. The nearest counter has a weighing scale on it, the second and third have signs that say in {{Term|TWWHD|Hylian (Language)}}, "Postage and Stamps" and "Savings and Exchanges", respectively. There is a box full of {{Plural|TWWHD|Delivery Bag|link}} on the floor behind the desk. 

Past the service desk is a small storage room with some {{Plural|TWWHD|Jar|link}} and {{Plural|TWWHD|Crate|link}}. There are {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Rupee|link}} in three of the smaller {{Plural|TWWHD|Jar}} on either side of the doorway.

===Mail Sorting and Delivery===
At the beginning of the game, {{Term|TWWHD|Koboli|link}} is found sorting mail behind the service desk, but later on he hires {{Term|TWWHD|Baito|link}} to take his place. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can speak to either of them to play the the {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center (Mini-Game)|link|display=Mail Center Mini-Game}} in which {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} throws letters onto shelves into a cupboard behind the desk. Each letter must go onto the shelf whose icon matches the seal on the letter. To the left of the cupboard is a map of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}} with routes marked between {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}} and the other main islands. The map shows which seal corresponds to which island. {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}} is marked on the map but has no seal. The {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}} is also marked on the map, even before it rises out of the ocean.
