==Location and Uses==
Ruby Short Swords are a type of {{Term|CoH|Short Sword|link}} that are infused with {{Term|CoH|Ruby|link}} material, allowing them to deal 1 damage to a single enemy and restore half a {{Term|CoH|Heart|link}} after every five enemies defeated.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, and therefore can only be acquired either after his Short Sword is infused with a Ruby at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, or if the blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} in {{Term|CoH|Lake Hylia|link}} is opened by Link.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Ruby Dagger|link}}.
