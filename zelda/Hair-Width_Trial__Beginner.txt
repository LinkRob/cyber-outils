{{Term/Store|Hair-Width Trial: Beginner||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hair-Width Trial: Beginner|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Hair-Width Trial: Beginner}} that can be unlocked after completing all the {{Plural|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} in Chapter 2. The exact {{Plural|HWAoC|Scenario}} needed are, "{{Term|HWAoC|Mipha, the Zora Princess|link}}," "{{Term|HWAoC|Daruk, the Goron Hero|link}}," "{{Term|HWAoC|Revali, the Rito Warrior|link}}," and "{{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa, the Gerudo Chief|link}}."

Unlike most other {{Plural|HWAoC|Challenge}}, this one has a particular condition. If the character gets hit by an enemy, they will automatically die.<ref>A new trial has emerged. The power of enemy attacks have been augmented-be careful not to get struck by any attacks. - In game description</ref> No healing items will appear on the battlefield.

In order to complete the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the character must defeat 40 enemies, and then take over the outpost on the map. Once this is completed, if completed on the first try, the character will get 1 {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 5 {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, 2 {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
