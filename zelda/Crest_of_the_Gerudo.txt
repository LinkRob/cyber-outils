The {{Term/Store|Crest of the Gerudo|Crests of the Gerudo|Series}} is a recurring symbol in {{TLoZ|Series}} for the [[Gerudo]] race.<ref name="E 45">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 45}}</ref> It depicts the back of a {{Wp|King Cobra}}.<ref name="E 45"/>

In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Crest of the Gerudo}} appears on various {{Plural|OoT3D|Switch|link}}, {{Plural|OoT3D|Block|link}}, the flags in [[Gerudo's Fortress]], and even the [[Mirror Shield]]. In the original [[Nintendo 64]] version of the game, it bores a mirrored resemblance of the real-world [[wikipedia:Star_and_crescent|Star and Crescent]] Islamic symbol. It was later replaced in the [[Nintendo GameCube|GameCube]] v.1.2 of the game and all subsequent re-releases, including ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', with a new symbol that would soon become standard for the franchise since ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]],'' due to [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda series|controversy]], as [[Nintendo]] accidentally violated their own policy of avoiding real-world religious material in their games.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= May 2012|retrieved= January 15, 2017|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zgDgGrfTjP8&list=PLvOnUZ6OHoHb7NiZiEDg9ak47o-qjZF1y&index=13|title= Pop Fiction: Season 1: Episode 9: The Fire Temple Chants [Update 1]|site= GameTrailers|type= Video}}</ref> Coincidentally, this is also the reason why the original music of the [[Fire Temple]] was changed to remove the Islamic prayer chants to a simple MIDI synthesizer in these revisions.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Crest of the Gerudo}} appears within the {{Term|MM3D|Pirates' Fortress|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|Aveil|link}}'s chamber, on top of the display above the colored bottles. This game is the actual first appearance of the revised symbol in the series.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Term|OoS|Onox|link}} displays the {{Term|OoS|Crest of the Gerudo}} on his chest plate.

==={{OoA|-}}===
[[Veran]] displays the {{Term|OoA|Crest of the Gerudo}} on her clothing on both of her shoulder pads.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Crest of the Gerudo}} appears in the {{Term|TWWHD|Stained Glass|link}} representing {{Term|TWWHD|Nabooru|link}} found within the {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword|link}} chamber in {{Term|TWWHD|Hyrule Castle|link}}, beneath the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}. It also appears on {{Term|TWWHD|Ganondorf|link}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|Sword}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, a slightly altered version of the {{Term|TPHD|Crest of the Gerudo}} can be seen on {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}}'s cloak and the front of {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}}'s garments.

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, the {{Term|BotW|Crest of the Gerudo}} appears in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon|link}}, on jewelry worn by {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}, and on the jeweled buckle of the garter that {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} wears at her hip.
