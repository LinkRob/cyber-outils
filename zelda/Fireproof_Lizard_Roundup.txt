"{{Term/Store|Fireproof Lizard Roundup||Series, BotW}}" is a [[Side Quest]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Fireproof Lizard Roundup}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Southern Mine}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Kima|link}}, who remarks that he showed up at the right time.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hey! Perfect!<br/>You showed up at just the right time.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kima}} goes on to explain that he just returned from {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, but he forgot to pick up souvenirs.<ref>{{Cite|I'm on my way back from {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}} but forgot to buy souvenirs.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> Now, he is scouring the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine}} for {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard|link}}, though he is having difficulty catching them due to how fast they are.<ref>{{Cite|I wanted to capture some of those {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}} that live here on Death Mountain, but those suckers are quick.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} can catch him 10 {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}}, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} promises to give him some {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Say, you look pretty speedy. I bet you could catch a ton! If you bring me {{Color|BotW Red|10}}, I'll even give you some heat-resistant armor. So what do you say, buddy? Help a pal out of a jam by capturing me some {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}}?|Kima|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} thanks him and suggests searching the area around them.<ref>{{Cite|[Leave it to me!] Great! Thanks, buddy! Just {{Color|BotW Red|10}} {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}} should be enough. You can probably find some right around here!|Kima|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}

{{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}} are plentiful in the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine}}. They can be found basking on their own or by lifting up rocks. The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor|link}} can also help {{Term|BotW|Link}} locate nearby {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}}. After gathering all 10 {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Kima}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, look at all them {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}}! You actually managed to chase down {{Color|BotW Red|10}} of those suckers?!|Kima|BotW}}</ref> Honoring his deal, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}} in exchange for the {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Lizard|display=Lizards}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, a deal's a deal. Hand them over and I'll give you this heat-resistant armor.|Kima|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks, buddy! Here, take this.|Kima|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kima}} notices that {{Term|BotW|Link}} seems disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|...Hmm? Something the matter, buddy?|Kima|BotW}}</ref> When he realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} wanted his {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Helm|link}}, he says it is too expensive to lend out and he should buy his own in {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|My helmet? Sorry, it ain't for lending! It's too expensive for that. You can buy your own in {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> He gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} directions, guiding him north on the trail.<ref>{{Cite|To get to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}, head north from here. It's the fifth stop up the trail.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kima}} then states it is always handy to have spare souvenirs.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure you'd agree, buddy, that it's always handy to have some spare souvenirs on hand.|Kima|BotW}}</ref>

From there on, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} will offer to pay 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} every time {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings him three Fireproof Lizards.<ref>{{Cite|So if you come across any more {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}}, I'll buy them off you at {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} for {{Color|BotW Red|three}}.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> He expresses a desire to use them to make {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And if I get my hands on some {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}} and some {{Color|BotW Blue|monster parts}}... I can make some {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixirs}} to boot!|Kima|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done talking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
