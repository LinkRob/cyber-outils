==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|SSHD}}
{{Plural|SSHD|Goddess Shield}} are the second and final {{Term|SSHD|Upgrading|link|display=Upgrade}} that can be done on {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Shield|link}}. It is obtained by upgrading a {{Term|SSHD|Divine Shield|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Scrap Shop|link}}. True to its name, it has the face of {{Term|SSHD|Hylia|link}} depicted upon it. Like all of the other versions of the {{Term|SSHD|Sacred Shield}}, it repels {{Term|SSHD|Curse|link|display=Cursed}} {{Plural|SSHD|Enemy|link}} and is resistant against {{Term|SSHD|Fire|link}} and {{Term|SSHD|Electricity|link}}.
