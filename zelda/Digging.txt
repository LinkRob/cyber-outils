==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is able to {{Term|TMC|Digging|display=Dig}} after obtaining the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}. He can use them to {{Term|TMC|Digging|display=Dig}} into {{Plural|TMC|Dig Cavern|link}} and the soft earth inside them, as well as sections of cloud in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can also simply {{Term|TMC|Digging|display=Dig}} with the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts}} in the {{Term|TMC|Overworld|link}}, potentially unearthing {{Plural|TMC|Heart|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}, or {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}. By {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|Flurris|link}} and a {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden|link}}, two fallen {{Plural|TMC|Tree|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}} will rise, giving {{Term|TMC|Link}} access to two specific patches of earth that yield hundreds of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} when he {{Term|TMC|Digging|display=Digs}} them up.

After performing a second {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}} with {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Joy Butterfly|link}} appears in {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}} that, when collected, increases the speed at which {{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Digging|display=Dig}} with the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} can {{Term|TPHD|Digging|display=Dig}} anywhere on the ground. When used in tandem with his {{Plural|TPHD|Sense|link}}, sparkling patches of earth can be seen that will often yield {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}} when unearthed. {{Term|TPHD|Digging}} in some of these patches will cause {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} to burrow into the earth and emerge in another area.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Stub|PH}}

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, {{Term|SS|Link|link}} is able to {{Term|SS|Digging|display=Dig}} by using the {{Term|SS|Digging Mitts|link}} and their upgraded form, the {{Term|SS|Mogma Mitts|link}}. The {{Term|SS|Digging Mitts}} can be used to {{Term|SS|Digging|display=Dig}} into small patches of dirt by pressing the {{Term|SS|A Button|link}} when standing next to them, revealing the contents found underground (often {{Plural|SS|Rupee|link}} or {{Plural|SS|Updraft|link}}. The {{Term|SS|Mogma Mitts}} allow {{Term|SS|Link}} to {{Term|SS|Digging|display=Dig}} into patches of soft sand and burrow underground. While {{Term|SS|Digging}} underground, {{Term|SS|Link}} can break {{Plural|SS|Rock|link}}, set off {{Plural|SS|Bomb|link}}, hit {{Plural|SS|Switch|link}}, and attack {{Plural|SS|Moldorm|link}}.

{{Term|SS|Link}} can also use these {{Plural|SS|Item|link}} to {{Term|SS|Digging|display=Dig}} in {{Term|SS|Thrill Digger|link}}.
