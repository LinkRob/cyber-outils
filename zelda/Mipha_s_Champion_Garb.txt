==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
At the end of Chapter 3, Mipha will exchange her royal Zora Sash and some of her silver jewelry for the blue sash which she will wear for the remainder of the game. After the main story is completed, the player can switch between Mipha's original attire and Champion's sash at any time given.
