{{Plural|BotW|Lizal Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} wielded by regular {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}} also use them on occasion. They were created by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}} and reinforced with the bones of a large {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Due to their origin, they are part of the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Their composition makes them superior to the standard {{Term|BotW|Wooden Bow|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Bow}} will burn and eventually disintegrate if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} like metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} do, and they will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, they cannot be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
Lizal Bows reappear in {{HWAoC}}, being wielded by Lizalfos during the early stages of the game. Unlike {{BotW}}, they cannot be picked up nor wielded by players.
