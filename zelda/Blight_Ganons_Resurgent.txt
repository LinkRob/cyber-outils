{{Term/Store|Blight Ganons Resurgent||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 67 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Blight Ganons Resurgent|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Blight Ganons Resurgent}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given fifteen minutes to defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} in that order. Each one spawns after the one before it is killed. It can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
