==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|Series|Dial}} appear in certain {{Plural|Series|Dungeon|link}} as a {{Term|Series|Puzzle|link}} element. At any time, they will either have a "left" or "right" direction stored. When "left" is stored, a {{Term|Series|Dial}} will be orange. When "right" is stored, a {{Term|Series|Dial}} will be blue. Should {{Term|Series|Link|link}} step into a {{Term|Series|Dial}}, it will rotate 90 degrees in the stored direction and change which one it has stored. This often leaves {{Term|Series|Link}} on a different path than the one he started on. Manipulation of {{Plural|Series|Dial}} allows {{Term|Series|Link}} to progress further into the {{Term|Series|Dungeon}}.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Plural|OoS|Dial}} appear in the {{Term|OoS|Snake's Remains|link}}, in the {{Term|OoS|Ancient Ruins|link}}, and in the {{Term|OoS|Dial Room|link}} in the {{Term|OoS|Sword & Shield Maze|link}}.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Plural|OoA|Dial}} appear in the {{Term|OoA|Moonlit Grotto|link}}, in both the present and past versions of the {{Term|OoA|Mermaid's Cave|link}}, and in the {{Term|OoA|Ancient Tomb|link}}.
