{{Term/Store|Salt-Grilled Prime Meat|Salt-Grilled Prime Meats|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Salt-Grilled Prime Meats by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either {{Term|Series|Raw Prime Meat|link}} or a {{Term|Series|Raw Bird Thigh|link}} with {{Term|Series|Rock Salt|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat these meats to heal some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These dishes are known to be simple yet exquisite, due to their use of high-quality meat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding extra ingredients, Link can create the following variations of Salt-Grilled Prime Meat:

* '''Hearty Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll heal all of [[Link|Link's]] Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. These meals will restore part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} if they're eaten.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats these.<ref name="Enduring"/> 

* '''Hasty Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. By eating these meats, Link's movement speed will be increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. They can be eaten for Link to gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. When eaten, they will provide a boost to Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. These meats will grant Link a boost to his defensive abilities when eaten.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Link will receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities when he eats these.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Salt-Grilled Prime Meat''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Salt-Grilled Prime Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A simple yet exquisite dish made by grilling high-quality meat on top of rock salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. By eating them, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
