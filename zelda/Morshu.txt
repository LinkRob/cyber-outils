{{Term/Store|Morshu||Series, TFoE}} is a character in {{TFoE}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= How do you earn the rubies that Morshu wants? |game= TFoE |page= 4}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TFoE|Morshu}} is the owner of the general store in the {{Term|TFoE|Goronu (Stage)|link}} area of {{Term|TFoE|Koridai|link}}. His shop sits at the base of the valley pass, atop a small bridge next to a rapid waterfall. Should {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} interact with him, he will name the wares he sells: {{Term|TFoE|Lantern Oil|link|display=Lamp Oil}}, {{Plural|TFoE|Rope (Object)|link|display=Rope}}, and {{Plural|TFoE|Bomb|link}}, and inform Link that he can have them as long as he has enough rubies.<ref>{{Cite|Lamp Oil? Rope? Bombs? You want it? It's yours my friend, as long as you have enough Rubies!|Morshu|TFoE}}</ref> Should Link not have enough rubies, he will tell him that he doesn't give credit, and to come back when he is a little richer.
