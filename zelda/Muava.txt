{{Term/Store|Muava||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Muava|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Muava}} is an elderly {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} woman who can be found sitting by the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} near the {{Term|BotW|Southeast Gate|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.

In her youth, {{Term|BotW|Muava}} travelled across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, and she was considered to be a beauty by {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} men.<ref name="Traveler">{{Cite|[Ever left the town?] Oh ho, I may not look it now, but I was quite the traveler when I was young. Quite a beauty, too! It was a rare thing for a voe not to notice me come into a room.|Muava|BotW}}</ref> Having heard rumors of {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond|link}}, she searched for it in the hope of finding her true love.<ref>{{Cite|But...I had a fairy-tale notion in my mind. I'd heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lover's Pond}}, and I thought I'd meet my true love there.|Muava|BotW}}</ref> However, she never found it, and she eventually became old and was no longer sought after by men.<ref>{{Cite|I traveled all across Hyrule, searching for this fabled place. But I couldn't find it... And before I knew it, I'd grown old. The voe no longer turned when I walked in.|Muava|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Muava}}, she will begin to greet him, but then she wonders how a man was able to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'saa... ... What's a voe doing here...|Muava|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} reacts with surprise, and {{Term|BotW|Muava}} states that while he might be able to fool everyone else in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, she is not so easy.<ref>{{Cite|Surprised? You might be able to fool all the other vai around here, but you won't fool me so easily.|Muava|BotW}}</ref> She assures {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she will not share his secret, since she does not care what he does.<ref>{{Cite|Don't worry, though. It doesn't matter much to me what you do.|Muava|BotW}}</ref> Since he came all the way to the {{Term|BotW|Southeast Gate}}, however, she asks if he would like to talk with her.<ref>{{Cite|But...since you came all the way back here, maybe you'd like to stay a bit and chat?|Muava|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the nearby {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue}}, {{Term|BotW|Muava}} tells him that it has been there since before she was born.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, the Goddess Statue has been here since long before I was even born.|Muava|BotW}}</ref> She explains that nobody visits it anymore because they do not "believe in that stuff anymore," comparing it to how people avoid her now as well.<ref>{{Cite|No one here really believes in that stuff anymore, though, so they tend to avoid stopping here. Kind of like they avoid me...|Muava|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Muava}} believes that someone needs to keep the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue}} company, which is what she decided to do.<ref>{{Cite|But someone has to keep the Goddess Statue company, so here I am...|Muava|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Muava}} if she has ever left {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, she recounts her past as a traveler to him.<ref name="Traveler"/> Noticing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears sad, {{Term|BotW|Muava}} tells him not to be, since it was a long time ago and she chased her dreams.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, don't look so sad! I stopped being sad about it a long time ago. And I chased my dreams!|Muava|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Muava}} enjoys talking to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, commenting that it is nice to speak with a man again.<ref>{{Cite|Heheheh... It's such a treat to chat with a voe again. Will you humor me a little bit longer?|Muava|BotW}}</ref> Before {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends their conversation, {{Term|BotW|Muava}} advises him to live his life to the fullest.<ref>{{Cite|Just live your life to the fullest, little voe!|Muava|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Muava}}, she refers to him as "Mr. Cute Voe."<ref>{{Cite|Hey there, Mr. Cute Voe.|Muava|BotW}}</ref>
