The {{Term/Store|Doppel Master||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 305"/> He is the master of the {{Term|TFH|Hall of the Doppels|link}} in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}, who activates the {{Plural|TFH|Doppel|link}} and the lobby's {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}} so that {{Term|TFH|Link}} may enter the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}} in their company.

==Biography==
When {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} first enters the {{Term|TFH|Hall of the Doppels}}, the {{Term|TFH|Doppel Master}} welcomes him and explains the purpose of the {{Plural|TFH|Doppel}}. It is the {{Term|TFH|Doppel Master}}'s duty to awaken the {{Plural|TFH|Doppel}} and bind them to {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s soul whenever he wishes to venture out into the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}}.<ref>{{Cite|They call me the Doppel Master. To depart for the Drablands in the company of Doppels, speak with me and I will bind them to your will.|Doppel Master|TFH}}</ref> To do this, he chants a string of magical words that will awaken the {{Plural|TFH|Doppel}} and open the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} to permit {{Term|TFH|Link}} entry. After doing this for the first time, he will explain the use of {{Plural|TFH|Doppel Panel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, yes. There's one more thing you should know.<br/>Behold the bottom screen... ↓<br/>Those otherworldly icons are called {{Color|TFH Blue|Doppel Panels}}. Use them to jump between yourself and the {{Color|TFH Blue|Doppels}}.|Doppel Master|TFH}}</ref> If {{Term|TFH|Link}} exits and returns to the lobby, he will have to speak with the {{Term|TFH|Doppel Master}} again to reawaken the {{Plural|TFH|Doppel}}.

==Trivia==
*In the European release of the game, the {{Term|TFH|Doppel Master}}'s magical words used to awaken the {{Plural|TFH|Doppel}}, "Pu gnimoc remotsuc eno", is "One customer coming up" backwards.<ref>{{Cite|Pu gnimoc remotsuc eno...!|Doppel Master|TFH}}</ref>
