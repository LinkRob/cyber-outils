The {{Term/Store|Highland Stable||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Highland Stable'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} that is located on {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Padok|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Phanna|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Perosa|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Blynne|link}} are all workers at the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}. The travelers {{Term|BotW|Suzuna|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Totsuna|link}} can be found there. As is the case with all {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} visits as well; {{Term|BotW|Beedle}} is found sitting outside the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, but will retreat inside by the main post while it is raining. In {{TCB}} downloadable content pack, the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V3|link}} {{Term|BotW|Book}} can be found atop the table inside, advancing the "{{Term|BotW|EX Ancient Horse Rumors|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently {{Color|BotW Red|Highland Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare horse gear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Phanna}} can be found tending to {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} and is one of the four {{Term|BotW|Stable}} hands who will offer to customize {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Horse}},<ref>{{Cite|Hello there, traveler. Welcome to our {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}}. You will stay awhile, won't you? If you have a horse, we can help you change its {{Color|BotW Blue|gear}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|mane}} style. We're always happy to meet new horses, so stop on by!|Phanna|BotW}}</ref> provided it has a sufficiently high enough bond and is not too big.

{{Term|BotW|Perosa}} can be found outside tending to the {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} with {{Term|BotW|Phanna}}, mumbling to herself.<ref>{{Cite|All right, all right!|Perosa|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Perosa}} will complain about the horseback {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|links}} that terrorize the northern road.<ref>{{Cite|Is this your first time here?|Perosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[It's my first time.] I see. These plains are home to many quality horses. In the past, nomadic tribes would gather here, but… Nowadays, {{Color|BotW Red|bands of riders}} roam the plains, acting like they own the place.|Perosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I can only hope that someone steps up and deals with those hooligans...|Perosa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers to help with dispatching the monsters, he will begin the "{{Term|BotW|The Horseback Hoodlums|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Leave it to me!] Oh, really? I’m so glad to hear that. I’m counting on you!|Perosa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Blynne}} runs the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}. He gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}} for each run he does where he does not beat his record.<ref>{{Cite|Nice riding, fella! You didn't beat my time, but I'm impressed all the same. Here, this is for your hardworking friend.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the course in one minute and thirty seconds, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will give him the {{Term|BotW|Extravagant Bridle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's that {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}} I promised you. Keep at it, and maybe someday you'll be on my level.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} breaks {{Term|BotW|Blynne}}’s record of one minute and fifteen seconds, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} gives him the {{Term|BotW|Extravagant Saddle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Holy smoke, would you look at that? Never thought I'd see the day… But a man's gotta abide by his word, so here's that {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}} I promised.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref>

When "{{Term|BotW|Captured Memories|link}}" is active and {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already completed "{{Term|BotW|Find the Fairy Fountain|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Pikango|link}} can be encountered at the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}. While there, he paints a view {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain}}. He is happy to meet {{Term|BotW|Link}} again and offers to identify any of his {{Plural|BotW|Picture|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! We meet again. Are you out traveling as well? I've traveled all over the world, so I'm happy to tell you about new places, if you want. If you have a picture to show me, let's see it already!|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to show him a {{Term|BotW|Picture}}, he will look at the seventh on in the {{Term|BotW|Album|link}}, which depicts the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Hylia|link}} from a distance.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Ah, these {{Color|BotW Red|two tiny stone statues}} depicted here...and that faint view of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Bridge of Hylia}} in the distance...|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> He declares that the {{Term|BotW|Picture}} was taken along the northeast shore of {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}, possibly on {{Term|BotW|Scout's Hill|link}} or east of it.<ref>{{Cite|From this angle, I think perhaps this is the northeast shore of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Hylia}}. Possibly {{Color|BotW Blue|Scout's Hill}} or perhaps a little further east of there.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> While that is all he knows, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go out and search for himself.<ref>{{Cite|That's all I know, I'm afraid... Sorry, but you'll have to follow your own feet if you want to find it.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> On a final note, he believes the statues look like a married couple embracing each other, which he finds charming.<ref>{{Cite|But these statues... They look like a husband and wife embracing. Such a simple yet charming sight.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can befriend the {{Term|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}} behind the {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable}}. Once it has been fed enough times, it will lead him to a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} buried behind the {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|display=Stable}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 349}}</ref>
