'''Burst Grenade''' is [[Sheik]]'s side special move in {{SSB4}} and {{SSBU}}. It replaces her [[Chain]] move from previous installments of the {{SSB|Series}}.

==Overview==
Upon using Burst Grenade, Sheik pulls out a chain with a small grenade on one end. Shortly afterward, the grenade transforms into a small vortex that can pull in nearby opponents, followed by an explosion.

In {{SSB4|-}} only, Burst Grenade has two custom variants: '''Gravity Grenade''', which does not transform into a vortex but knocks anyone within the explosion toward Sheik, and '''Skimming Grenade''', which bounces along the ground and explodes faster than the Burst Grenade.
