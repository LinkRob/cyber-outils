{{Term/Store|Ralph||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254}}</ref> He is [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]]'s childhood friend and loyal guardian.<ref>{{Cite|I've known Nayru was the Oracle of Ages since she was a child. I have always been beside her, protecting her.|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> Although he is stubborn and hotheaded, rarely pondering the consequences of his own actions, he is a noble character deep down. Due to his protective nature and defensive attitude towards Nayru, Ralph expresses a certain resentment towards Link, treating him with a certain amount of rivalry throughout the course of the game as he attempts to rescue Nayru before Link.<ref>{{Cite|If anyone is gonna save Nayru, it's me! I won't let you beat me to it!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> As Link defeats Veran and saves Nayru from [[Queen Ambi]], the young hero gains Ralph's respect.<ref name="respect">{{Cite|Link! My respect for you has grown. I thought it was over when you got locked in the tower. I never thought you'd outdo me!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Ralph|Ralph is Nayru's hot-tempered childhood friend. His hastiness and lack of focus can sometimes lead to trouble. Having seen his dear Nayru taken from before his very eyes, Ralph is determined to rescue her.}}
Ralph is first seen standing next to Nayru when Link first arrives to [[Labrynna]]. He confidently informs Link that he will not be needed to help protect Nayru,<ref>{{Cite|Don't worry, Nayru! I'm here. I'll chase away any beast that comes near you! Sorry, Link, but I doubt we'll need you.|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> only seconds before [[Veran]] possesses her. Standing up to Veran, Ralph threatens to cut her in half if she does not release Nayru,<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing to Nayru? I'll cut you in two if you don't let her go!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> but is forced to stand down, as Veran points out that if he attacks, Nayru will die.<ref>{{Cite|Hah! Aren't you brave! Turning a sword at me! If you cut me, it's Nayru who dies!|Veran|OoA}}</ref> Making her getaway to the past, Ralph is quick to pursue her through the still-open [[Time Portal]].<ref>{{Cite|Veran just leapt through this Time Portal! If we go back in time, we should be able to save Nayru and the Maku Tree! I'm coming, Nayru!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref>

Despite his noble ambition, Ralph is ultimately of little help, as he simply runs around in the past hoping to encounter Veran. He does, however, point out a few important facts to Link as the young hero quests for the [[Essences of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|Link! Did you hear that? They're saying terrible things about Nayru in the village... But why? It must be Veran's work! The truth will come out once I save Nayru! Wait here! I'm going!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Dawdling about, as usual. What are you doing? Huh? You need an Island Chart? I've heard that weird guy has sea charts to the secret isle. Do what you want! I'm too busy trying to save Nayru to help you! See ya!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, Link! What are you doing here? I came to find an old hermit who's studying tunes on this mountain. I don't know where he is, but I bet he can offer a clue that will help save Nayru. I'll find him! Bye!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, Link! Are you looking for something, or what? Huh? Cheval? I know that name. Yeah! I've seen a grave in our own time that has that name on it. But I don't have time to chat! I'm busy! See ya!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref>

After Link completes the [[Mermaid's Cave]], Queen Ambi leaves her [[Ambi's Palace|palace]], giving Link the ideal opportunity to rescue Nayru from Veran's clutches.<ref>{{Cite|Link! I have great news!!! Queen Ambi has just left the palace! Now is your chance! This may be your only opportunity to save Nayru!|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref> Upon his arrival to the palace, Link runs into Ralph, who also heard that the palace guard was let down.<ref>{{Cite|Link! Don't startle me! You also heard the guard on the palace was down and came to save Nayru?|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> Ralph rushes ahead to find Nayru, but not before informing Link of a hidden entrance to the palace interior within the palace garden.<ref>{{Cite|But it's no good entering from the front door. I know! There should be a hidden entrance in the palace garden! I'll sneak in through there! What? "Why do I know about the hidden passage on my first trip here?" Never mind!!! I'm coming, Nayru!!!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> Ralph reappears after Link saves Nayru from Veran, who then possesses Queen Ambi. Saving both Link and Ralph, Nayru returns them to the present. 

After Nayru's safe return, Ralph apologizes to Link for his behavior.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, Link. I was the one running around while you did the saving...|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> He decides to return to the past, vowing to defeat Veran.<ref>{{Cite|But know this: I'll be the one to stop Veran!!! I won't leave the people of the past to her evil deeds! Forgetting about Veran now would be foolish. If we do, she'll just come for Nayru again! [...] I'm going back to Ambi's age first! May fate bring us back together! Bye!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> Ralph is determined to kill Queen Ambi to save Labrynna and Nayru, even after it is discovered that the queen is Ralph's ancestor.<ref>{{Cite|Link, I was researching the past and found something awful! Ralph is a descendant of Queen Ambi! Do you know what this means? Ralph lives now because Ambi lived in the past. If Ambi is slain, Ralph will never exist! I came to try to stop Ralph, but I couldn't!|Impa|OoA}} ERRATA CORRIGE: Impa should say: "If Ambi had been slain, Ralph would have never be born!".</ref> Ralph does not understand that he will fail for sure, because the fact that he was born means that his ancestor Ambi was not killed in the first place, though he does suspect he'll cease to exist if he succeeded.<ref>{{Cite|I came to destroy Veran! Veran has taken control of Queen Ambi, right? Then to beat Veran... I must defeat... Queen Ambi... Umm... Well... Queen Ambi! Veran! I'll destroy the both of you! Then the people of this age, and of our age, will have peace... ...But what... will happen to me..? Umm... Link... Don't forget me. I mean, I think of you...as a friend... OK! I'm going! Ralph can do it! Goodbye, Link!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You trickster! Fine! If I slay you, I vanish! Maybe it is terrifying... But if I must, I must. To do nothing and live just isn't me. Then it means nothing! I will destroy you! To protect the people of this age, and to protect Nayru!!!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> As expected, when he comes face-to-face with the Sorceress of Shadows at the [[Black Tower]], he is easily thwarted by Veran's power. 

Ralph survives (thanks to Veran's "kindness"), but Veran mocks both Link and Ralph, saying that by killing the queen, Ralph in turn will vanish,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Link! Because of my kindness, young Ralph still lives. But you still attack me! If you damage Ambi's body, Ralph will vanish. You can't intend to end your dear friend's life by your own hand!|Veran|OoA}}</ref> although the existence of Ralph proves that the queen will not be harmed. During the ensuing battle, Link frees Ambi from Veran's control and then defeats the sorceress. Believing that the danger is over, everybody attempts to exit the tower. However, Veran is not finished. Link, separated from Nayru and Ralph, faces off against Veran for the last time. After defeating Veran for good, Link meets with Nayru and Ralph, accompanied by Queen Ambi and her soldiers. Ralph is amazed by Link's performance,<ref>{{Cite|You really beat Veran, didn't you? Nice work, Link! You're amazing!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> and from then on treats the young hero with the utmost respect.<ref name="respect" />

As the adventure ends, Ralph vows to train harder to be able to protect Nayru on his own.<ref>{{Cite|Link! You did it! Even I thought it was over this time, but Nayru was there. She can't always remain helpless! I'm going to train so I'll be able to protect Nayru on my own!|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> He is last seen training with Link, trying unsuccessfully to master the [[Spin Attack]], turning it into an [[Hurricane Spin|uncontrollable version with greater range]]. Despite the greater range of damage, the attack makes Ralph too dizzy to do anything except collapse after he uses it.

==Other Appearances==

==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
Ralph has changed some in his manga appearance. He is used for greater comic relief than in the game, and is also much stronger, accompanying Link throughout the majority of his journey. Unlike in the games, Ralph is, at one point, possessed by Veran.

Ralph makes his first appearance after Veran possesses Nayru and travels to the past. When he learns of Nayru's fate, he falls into an emotional rage, blaming Link for the event and accusing him of trying to seduce Nayru.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... I bet you were trying to seduce her, weren't you?!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 40}}</ref> Although, when [[Impa]] wakes from unconsciousness, she explains what happened to Ralph. He then exclaims that he is going to save Nayru, and attempts to take the [[Harp of Ages]] from Link, resulting in the two fighting over it.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I can't leave the harp to a pipsqueak like you! I'm going to save Nayru! Now give it to me!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 41}}</ref> Ralph accidentally strums the harp and Link goes back in time without him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Wait right here. I'll be back with Nayru! Buh-Bye!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 43}}</ref>

Ralph isn't seen again until chapter six. Once the Harp of Ages has been passed down through time into the present because of Link dropping it during his capture, Ralph seizes it from a [[Tokay]]'s shop and heads to Labrynna's past. When he arrives, he falls on top of Link, who is currently battling a Nayru possessed by Veran. Ralph is at first excited to see his childhood friend,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Nayru? Is that Nayru?!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 98}}</ref> until the possessed oracle attacks them. Link explains that Nayru is still under Veran's influence.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= That's because Veran is still possessing her.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 99}}</ref> He begins to form a plan to defeat Veran, but because the Harp of Ages is again accidentally strummed, the two are teleported onto an island.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= What?!
