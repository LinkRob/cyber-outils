==Biography==
{{Stub|MM3D}}
The {{Term|MM3D|Man from Trading Post}} is the proprietor of the Trading Post. After 10 PM, he has a part-time worker manning the Trading Post.

When working at the trading post, he is much more polite than when he is working at the Curiosity Shop. He will let both Goron Link and Zora Link shop in his store.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa-ho-ho! Niiiice sideburns!|Trading Post Guy|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Welcome. Hey, you're pretty fit. I'll bet you do a lot of training!|Trading Post Guy|MM}}</ref> He does not serve Link if he wears any mask, but he is more polite about refusing him service than in the Curiosity Shop.<ref>{{Cite|No using masks in the store because of past indiscretions!|Man from Trading Post|MM}}</ref> He seems to recognize [[Kafei's Mask]] but then will say he does not know him;<ref>{{Cite|Kafei! No, I don't know any such kid. I kid you not!|Man from Trading Post|MM}}</ref> he knows about the function of the All-Night Mask;<ref>{{Cite|If you wear something like that, you'll never be able to sleep.
