The {{Term/Store|Zora Guitar||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 143}}</ref> also known as the '''Guitar of Waves''',<ref>{{Cite|It seems the face of the slightly...larger, strange stone changes when the {{Color|MM3D Dark Blue|guitar of waves}} is played...|Gossip Stone|MM3D}}</ref> is an item in {{MM}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Zora Guitar is obtained alongside the [[Zora Mask]] after [[Link]] heals [[Mikau]]'s soul. It replaces the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} when transformed into a [[Zora Link|Zora]]. Link must play it to learn the "[[New Wave Bossa Nova]]" from the [[Zora Egg|Zora hatchlings]]. By playing the song on the Zora Guitar to [[Lulu]] in the [[Zora Cape]], Link can awaken the [[Giant Turtle]] who will take him to the [[Great Bay Temple]].

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|MM3D|Zora Guitar}} is played by {{Term|MM3D|Link}} in the right-handed style, despite him being left-handed.
*The Zora Guitar existed as an independent [[C-Button]] item in the original {{MM|-}}. Its icon was coded into the game but was not implemented in the final release.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= darkeye14|published= August 25, 2010|retrieved= |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KYdj1DiCgug|title= Japanese Majora's Mask: Exclusive Beta Items|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref> It can be seen in certain [[Unused Content|pre-release]] screenshots. The icon was later used in {{MM3D}}.
*In January 2001, [[Nintendo Power]] held a contest where the grand prize winner would receive a copy of {{MM|-}} and a special edition electric guitar based on the Zora Guitar, the {{Term|Series|Special Edition Fish Bone Guitar|link}}. It was a custom-made 7-string guitar by {{Wp|Jackson Guitars}}. Only seven were produced, making it one of the rarest merchandise of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] and existing models are at extremely high prices.
