==Biography==
{{Term|MM3D|Tortus}} was {{Term|MM3D|Anju|link}}'s father and the son of her {{Term|MM3D|Granny|link|display=Grandmother}} and {{Term|MM3D|Gampy|link}}. By the time the events in {{MM|-}} occur, {{Term|MM3D|Tortus}} is already deceased.<ref>{{Cite|We used to be a cafeteria, but after my father died, the room rentals that were part of our service became our main focus.|Anju|MM}}</ref> His name is only known because {{Term|MM3D|Granny|display=Anju's Grandmother}} often calls {{Term|MM3D|Anju}} and {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} by the name "Tortus."<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Tortus. I've already had lunch.|Anju's Grandmother|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Anju}} must constantly remind her {{Term|MM3D|Granny|display=Grandmother}} that {{Term|MM3D|Tortus}} is no longer alive,<ref name="Anju" /> but she never appears to fully understand.<ref>{{Cite|Didn't I say that I already ate lunch, Tortus?!?
