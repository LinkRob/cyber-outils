==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Rushroom}}
{{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}} are purple {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom|link}} that can be commonly found growing on mountains and cliffs, mainly in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}. They can also be purchased at the [[Spore Store]] in the [[Great Hyrule Forest]]. When Rushrooms are eaten raw, they will provide {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with half of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} of healing. Dishes made in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} using a Rushroom will grant Link a temporary speed boost.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> Rushrooms that are burned using {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} turn into {{Plural|BotW|Toasty Rushroom|link}}. They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to create Purple [[Dye]].

Rushrooms, like other mushrooms, can be used in a Cooking Pot to create [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Fragrant Mushroom Sauté]], [[Fruit and Mushroom Mix]], [[Glazed Mushrooms]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], a [[Mushroom Omelet]], [[Mushroom Rice Balls]], [[Mushroom Risotto]], a [[Mushroom Skewer]], [[Copious Mushrooms Skewers]], [[Salt-Grilled Mushrooms]], and [[Steamed Mushrooms]].

[[Pirou]] at the [[Gerudo Canyon Stable]] has eaten Rushrooms everyday since he was five and now can't live without them.<ref>{{Cite|I first tried {{Color|BotW Blue|rushrooms}} when I was five years old...|Pirou|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Now I can barely drag these bones off the ground without those {{Color|BotW Blue|rushrooms}}.|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[Rushroom Rush!]]" requires Link to gather fifty-five Rushrooms for Pirou in exchange for a [[Diamond]].<ref>{{Cite|I'll give a {{Color|BotW Red|diamond}} for {{Color|BotW Blue|55 rushrooms}}. That's worth the effort, right?|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Link can trade fifty-five Rushrooms with Pirou and receive a payment of twice their regular value.

Three Rushrooms are required to upgrade each piece of the [[Climbing Set]] to the first tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Fifteen Rushrooms are needed to upgrade both [[Sand Boots]] and [[Snow Boots]] to their third tier.

* Total for '''Climbing Set''': 9 (3, 0, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Sand Boots''': 15 (0, 0, 15, 0)

* Total for '''Snow Boots''': 15 (0, 0, 15, 0)
