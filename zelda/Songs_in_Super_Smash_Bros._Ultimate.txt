{{DISPLAYTITLE: Songs in {{SSBU|-}}}}
{{Noncanon}}
Unlike past games in the series, music in {{SSBU}} is not tied to individual Stages but is instead is able to be played on any Stage from the same franchise. The tracks consist of new arrangements, returning arrangements from past {{SSB|-}} games, and originals directly taken from the source material.
