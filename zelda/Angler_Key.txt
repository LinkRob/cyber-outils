The {{Term/Store|Angler Key||Series, LA, LADX}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 115"/>{{Note|The {{Term|LADX|Angler Key}} was stylized as the '''Angler's Key''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref name="LA Guide 49">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 49}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|LADX|Angler Key}} is dropped by the {{Term|LADX|Lanmola|link}} located in the {{Term|LADX|Yarna Desert|link}}.<ref name="Location"/><ref name="LA Guide 49"/> It can be inserted into the {{Term|LADX|Angler Keyhole|link}} to stop the waterfall that is blocking the entrance to the {{Term|LADX|Angler's Tunnel|link}}.<ref name="E 115"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Angler Key}} falls from the remains of the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Lanmola|link}} {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} defeats at the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Yarna Desert|link}} and is picked up from the sand by {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Felicia|link}}. After finding the entrance to {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Angler's Tunnel|link}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} uses the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Angler Key}} to stop the waterfall and gain access to the dungeon.
{{Noncanon|End}}
