==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Gleaming Crowns & Sparkling Tiaras}}" is an event wherein a greater number of tiara or crown wearing {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits sporting crowns and tiaras are giving the Spirit Board the royal treatment in this event!|author= Nintendo |published= December 28, 2021 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/c2130fa4/index.html|title= Event: Gleaming Crowns & Sparkling Tiaras |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
