==Location and Uses==
Extravagant Bridles are a type of {{Term|BotW|Bridle|link}} that can be obtained from {{Term|BotW|Blynne|link}} by completing the {{Term|BotW|Mounted Obstacle Course|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in less than one minute and thirty seconds.<ref>{{Cite|Here's that {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}} I promised you. Keep at it, and maybe someday you'll be on my level.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> These Bridles are often utilized in festivals.<ref name="Inventory"/> They're also decorated with feathers, making them great for adding a splash of color to {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can have Extravagant Bridles equipped by one of the workers at {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Extravagant Bridle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> However, as is the case with other Bridles, they can't be used by {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.
