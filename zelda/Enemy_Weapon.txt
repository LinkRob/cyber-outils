TITLE : Enemy Weapon

{{Improve|February 2021}}
{{Term/Store|Enemy Weapon|Enemy Weapons|Series, TWW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Objects|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Nintendo|page= 12}}</ref>{{Name Ref|SS}} They are hand-held weapons carried by enemies that can be stolen and used by [[Link]]. In {{TWW|-}}, enemies such as [[Bokoblin]]s, [[Moblin]]s, and [[Darknut]]s are commonly armed with these weapons. Once Link has forced an enemy to drop their weapon, he can pick it up and temporarily use it himself in place of his [[Sword]]. Link uses this technique in {{SS}} during his battle with [[Koloktos]].

Link may use these weapons until he is disarmed or leaves the area. In {{TWW|-}}, they can be thrown away or towards enemies at will. Also in {{TWW|-}}, disarmed enemies will attempt to rearm themselves with any available Enemy Weapon, even if it is not the one they were originally carrying. If they cannot obtain a new weapon, they will resort to punches and kicks.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
====Boko Stick====
[[File:TWW Boko Stick Model.png|Boko Stick|100px|right]]
A '''Boko Stick''' is a flammable stick that is often wielded by {{Plural|TWW|Bokoblin|link}}. [[Boko Baba]]s also drop these items upon death. Boko Sticks are very similar to the [[Deku Stick]] from {{OoT}} and {{MM}} in appearance and functionality. For example, in some dungeons and locations in the [[Overworld]], Link is required to use them to light torches or burn planks blocking doorways. Link can attack enemies with the Boko Stick and perform the {{Term|TWW|Spin Attack|link}}, and after jumping, he can also perform a jump attack.  
{{clear}}

====Machete====
[[File:TWW Machete Artwork.png|Machete|100px|right]]
A '''Machete''' is a weapon used by {{Plural|TWW|Bokoblin|link}}. It appears as a large, crudely made sword, which is powerful enough to break through wooden barriers. It is primarily utilized in [[Dragon Roost Cavern]].
{{clear}}

====Moblin Spear====
[[File:TWW Moblin Spear Model.png|Moblin Spear|100px|right]]
A '''Moblin Spear''' is a weapon often used by {{Plural|TWW|Moblin|link}}. It takes the form of a long spear with a crooked shaft and long, orange ribbons below the blade. Its form is similar to that of a {{Wp|glaive}} or ''{{Wp|naginata}}''. Moblins can use these spears to stab forwards or to swing around in an arc.
{{clear}}

====Darknut Sword====
[[File:TWW Darknut Sword Artwork.png|Darknut Sword|50px|right]]
A '''Darknut Sword''' is a weapon used by {{Plural|TWW|Darknut|link}}. It is very large in size, resembling a cross between a {{Wp|Flame-bladed sword#Flamberge|flamberge}} and a {{Wp|broadsword}}. Darknuts use the weapon one-handed; however, due to its large size, Link must use both hands to wield it. Though it has the same strength as a Moblin Spear, a Darknut Sword is capable of breaking stone pillars, while a Moblin Spear is not.
{{clear}}

====Stalfos Club====
[[File: TWW Stalfos Club Render.png|Stalfos Club|100px|right]]
A '''Stalfos Club''' is a weapon used by {{Plural|TWW|Stalfos|link}}. It takes the form of a large, very heavy spiked metal club, which the Stalfos wield with difficulty, often spinning uncontrollably whenever they swing it. Like the Darknut Sword, it is capable of breaking stone pillars. It closely resembles a {{Wp|kanabō}}, a weapon used in feudal Japan.
{{clear}}

====Phantom Ganon's Sword====
[[File:TWW Phantom Ganon's Sword Model.png|Phantom Ganon's Sword|right|100px]]
This sword is wielded only by [[Phantom Ganon]], and he will only drop it when Link defeats him. This weapon can only be used when Link is in the maze-like area within [[Ganon's Tower (The Wind Waker)|Ganon's Tower]]. When Phantom Ganon drops the sword, its hilt will always fall pointing towards the direction which Link needs to go to continue the maze. When Link has dealt the final blow to Phantom Ganon, his sword can be used to destroy a large door blocking Link's way to the top of the fortress.
{{clear}}

==={{SS|-}}===
====Koloktos's Swords====
[[File:SS Koloktos's Sword Model.png|One of Koloktos's Swords|50px|right]]
The boss of the [[Ancient Cistern]], [[Koloktos]], carries one sword in each of its six arms. Link can retrieve them by pulling off each arm with the {{Term|SS|Whip|link}} and using it to slash at Koloktos's chest. This is the only way to damage and eventually defeat Koloktos.
{{Clear}}

==Trivia==
*In {{TWW|-}}, if Link places a [[Jar]] with Boko Sticks close to a lit torch, the sticks in the jar will light. When in the jar, the sticks will burn indefinitely.
*In {{TWW|-}}, if Link breaks a {{Term|TWWHD|Water Pot|link}} over an ignited Boko Stick, it will douse the flames.
*The Swords used by [[Phantom]]s in {{ST}} strongly resemble the ones used by Darknuts in {{TWW|-}}.
{{Clear}}
