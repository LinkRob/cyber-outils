==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hearty Salmon}}
Hearty Salmons inhabit cold bodies of water within the [[Hebra Mountains]] and the [[Tabantha Frontier]]. Their bodies accrete layers of fat in response to this, insulating them from their cold surroundings.<ref name="Inventory"/> Eating them raw restores four {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Exposed to fire, Hearty Salmons become {{Plural|Series|Roasted Hearty Salmon|link}}. Alternatively, they can be exposed to [[Ice]] to become {{Plural|BotW|Frozen Hearty Salmon|link}}. Meals made using Hearty Salmons will grant Link temporary Hearts when eaten.<ref name="Inventory"/>

During the Shrine Quest "[[Recital at Warbler's Nest]]", [[Kotts]] can be found fishing for Hearty Salmon just outside [[Rito Village]].<ref>{{Cite|Hiyah! HIYAH!<br/>I'm fishing for {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}}!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> When reminded of the recital, she will give Link a Hearty Salmon for [[Genli]].<ref>{{Cite|Please take this {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}} and go make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon muenière}} for Genli.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref>

Hearty Salmons are required to use while [[Cooking]] to create [[Hearty Salmon Muenière]] and [[Salmon Risotto]]. As with other fish in the game, Hearty Salmons can be used to make a [[Fish Skewer]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], and [[Salt-Grilled Fish]]. Hearty Salmons are also among the seafood that can be used to make [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]]
