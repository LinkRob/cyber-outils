The {{Term/Store|Exploding Crossbow||Series, LCT}} is an ability used by the {{Term|LCT|Crossbow|link}} in {{LCT}}.<ref name="Manual 7"/>

==Overview==
{{Manual|LCT|Exploding Crossbow|Press and hold {{Icon|LCT B}} until the crosshairs flash, and then release {{Icon|LCT B}} to fire an exploding arrow. It has more power and does more damage, but it takes a little while to charge up, so you can't fire it as frequently.}}
The {{Term|LCT|Exploding Crossbow}} is an ability that {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} can use, where he charges up a shot and fires an {{Term|LCT|Exploding Arrow|link}}.<ref name="Manual 7"/> The {{Term|LCT|Exploding Arrow}} launched will instantly kill most {{Plural|LCT|Enemy|link}} (excluding {{Term|LCT|Darknut|link}} and {{Term|LCT|Fossil Stallord|link}}), and will instantly destroy any {{Plural|LCT|Object|link}} and {{Plural|LCT|Target|link}} in a small radius near the blast.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= {{Color|LCT Black|Use Exploding Arrows}}<br/>Exploding arrows can take out a lot of enemies in a single shot. When you have many enemies gathered in one area, take aim and blast the bunch of them. Be careful—you might accidentally hit a target that you don't want to.|game= LCT|page= 9}}</ref> Any {{Plural|LCT|Target}} destroyed by the {{Term|LCT|Exploding Arrow}} will give the lowest number of points offered by the target. In order to charge the {{Term|LCT|Exploding Crossbow}}, a regular {{Term|LCT|Arrow|link}} must first be fired from the {{Term|LCT|Crossbow}} and the {{Term|LCT|B Button|link}} should be held in. After around two seconds, the {{Term|LCT|Exploding Arrow}} will be fully charged and ready to fire. Releasing the {{Term|LCT|B Button}} will fire the {{Term|LCT|Exploding Arrow}}.<ref name="Manual 7"/>
