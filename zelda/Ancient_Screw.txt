==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Screw}} are {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that can be dropped by any {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} after defeating them in battle, or by searching an already destroyed {{Term|BotW|Guardian}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can also purchase them from {{Plural|BotW|Shop|link}} such as the traveling merchant {{Term|BotW|Teli|link}}, who travels around the {{Term|BotW|Necluda|link}} province. Like all {{Plural|Series|Ancient Material|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Screw}} are pieces of ancient machinery created by the ancient {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}}. It shows no signs of wear, no matter how much it's used.<ref name="Inventory"/>

At the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}} can use them to forge {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set|link}} and other {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}. Additionally, like other {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use them to create {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} them with small {{Plural|BotW|Animal|link}}. Three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Screw}} are needed to upgrade the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Slated for Upgrades|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You'll need {{Color|BotW Red|three ancient screws}} to power up your {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Sensor}}.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> They can also be sold to {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} or used to make {{Term|BotW|Gray Dye|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Screw}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
