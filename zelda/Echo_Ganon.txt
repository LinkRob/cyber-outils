'''Echo Ganon''' is a [[Boss]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
In {{EoW|-}}, [[Ganon]] returns in his classic form, kidnapping [[Princess Zelda]] and trapping her in a [[crystal]] deep within his lair.<ref name="Echo Ganon">{{Cite|So. It's you again. Ever the troublesome one. Only this time, your presence serves me. Now I'll end you both!|Ganon|EoW}}</ref> [[Link]] confronts him in a fierce battle and is victorious, however Ganon’s defeat triggers a rift that consumes Link, but not before Link manages to free Zelda just before vanishing, forcing her to take up the mantle of saving Hyrule.<ref name="Echo Blue monster">{{Cite|Huh? Is that the person who fought the blue monster?|Tri|EoW}}</ref>

At the game’s midpoint, Zelda returns to [[Hyrule Castle]] and explores it in the [[Still World]], confronting the [[Tecuum]] that were impersonating [[King of Hyrule#Echoes of Wisdom|her father]], [[Minister Lefte]], and [[General Wright]]. The three then merge to form a new Echo Ganon, but yet again, it is defeated.

During the final battle against [[Null]], [[Dark Echo]]es of Ganon can be spawned alongside similar Dark Echoes of the game's other bosses.
