== Controversy ==

=== Stalking and harrassment ===
In August 2013, Sean made inappropriate jokes towards a minor on DeviantArt.<ref>https://x.com/Antithess/status/2047015835550548097?s=20</ref> In December 2013, tumblr user purplekecleon made alleged that Sean had been harassing and stalking her, with a few of his fans even threatening to kill her pets. Sean jokingly stated to kill purple's cats. She asked why he was spreading false rumors about her, and Sean replied "because you’re not a very nice person and I don’t care what happens to you as a result.” Sean then publicly apologized, then Purple stated that he was downplaying the things he was doing. Sean never apologized to purple directly, however one of Sean's friends did apologize to purple.<ref>https://nigecha.tumblr.com/post/76216657285</ref><ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20140709030645/http://purplekecleon.tumblr.com/post/69334734277/english-dangan-ronpa</ref> Sean came up to Purple's table at an Anime Expo, where he sarcastically talked about his harassment towards purple, not taking it seriously.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20141027230804/http://purplekecleon.tumblr.com/post/69278224675/english-dangan-ronpa</ref>

=== Inappropriate interactions with minors ===
In August 2018, there were allegations that Sean sent unsolicited moaning recordings to a teenager when he was 23.<ref>https://www.reddit.com/r/Animedubs/comments/98n6uj/psa_on_sean_chiplock_dub_actor_he_sent/</ref> In September 2023, a Twitter thread was made to expose Sean's alleged actions.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20240112051958/https://twitter.com/stellexpress/status/1706355992881967334</ref> It detailed how Sean's fans allegedly harassed a minor on twitter. Sean also made some edgy racist and homophobic comments in the past. There were also tweets related to ''Genshin Impact'' that showed Sean reposting incest art of characters Diluc and Kaeya, who were step-brothers. An unnamed user made a now-deleted title on IMDb called "Blacklist Sean Chiplock from the Industry". However, Many voice actors, Jett Barker<ref>[https://twitter.com/barkervoiceover/status/1741287204197117952 Jett's reply]</ref>, Allanah Fitzgerald<ref>[https://twitter.com/FitzyVA/status/1741245685184200860 Fitzy's reply]</ref>, and Andre Grandpierre spoke out in support of Sean.

On July 1, 2024, there were grooming allegations against Sean by a user named awryval also known as Rina, who claimed that in 2013, Sean groomed a 14 year old boy named Casper_Kerrivan.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20240701202353/https://twitter.com/awryval/status/1807872778470560039</ref> Rina claimed Sean asked Casper about mastrubation on Skype. He also allegedly sent Casper audio clips of Sean moaning as a "joke".<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20240701202358/https://twitter.com/awryval/status/1807872798452236492</ref> They allegedly sent each other sexually explicit messages, this claim was backed up by chat logs detailing the conversations.<ref>https://www.thegamer.com/the-legend-of-zelda-revali-voice-actor-ky-kiske-guilty-gear-genshin-impact-sean-chiplock-responds-to-accusations-grooming-14-year-old-boy/</ref> On July 2, Sean responded denying the allegations, saying it is from years ago, he did not knew Casper's age, conversations about sexual topics were just jokes and that he has never attempted to groom anyone.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20240702014342/https://twitter.com/VAsonicmega/status/1807953264182518176</ref>

Casper made a tweet stating allegations are true, he stated he was born in December 10, 1998 and these inappropriate conversations took place in August 2013.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20240702123644/https://twitter.com/Casper_Kerrivan/status/1808117603652526451</ref> Chiplock replied saying if his behavior towards Casper was wrong, he would like to privately apologize to Casper, however Casper stated he didn't want to talk and just wanted Chiplock to admit that it was all true.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20240702173426/https://twitter.com/VAsonicmega/status/1808192521694331091</ref> However on September 5, 2024, Casper randomly made tweets saying the conversations between him and Sean were inappropriate, but that he doesn't consider himself a grooming victim. He states Sean didn't know his age, and that Sean has apologized and Casper accepted his apology.<ref>https://x.com/casper_kerrivan/status/1831675966671691856?s=20</ref><ref>https://x.com/casper_kerrivan/status/1843060733254258713?s=20</ref>
