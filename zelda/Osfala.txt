{{Term/Store|Osfala||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref> He is a headstrong young man and [[Sahasrahla]]'s apprentice. He is the second descendant of the original [[Sage#Sages|Seven Sages]] who gets kidnapped by [[Yuga]]. 

==Biography==
After coming to the conclusion that Yuga is after the descendants of the Seven Sages, with the intention of freeing [[Ganon]], Sahasrahla asks Link to warn his pupil Osfala, a descendant of the Seven Sages, about the sorcerer's plot. Outside the Eastern Palace's entrance, the young hero encounters Osfala, who recognizes Link as the [[Blacksmith]]'s apprentice and asks him why he has come to the Palace.<ref>{{Cite|You, stop where you are! What are you doing here at the {{Color|ALBW Dark Blue|Eastern Palace}}? Oh, my apologies. I thought you were someone I'm looking for. Aren't you Link? You're the blacksmith's apprentice, right? But what brings you all the way to the {{Color|ALBW Dark Blue|Eastern Palace}}?|Osfala|ALBW}}</ref> The young hero proceeds to inform Osfala about [[Yuga]] and the events that recently occurred within the [[Sanctuary]], warning him about the peril he's in.<ref>{{Cite|Vile deeds at the {{Color|ALBW Dark Blue|Sanctuary}}? My master sent you to fetch me back to safety?|Osfala|ALBW}}</ref> However, Osfala doesn't believe he's in danger, stating that although Yuga is a cause for concern, his master shouldn't be worried about him because he's a descendant of the Seven Sages.<ref>{{Cite|Wah-haha! This {{Color|ALBW Dark Blue|Yuga}} you speak of... Surely he's cause for grave concern. But why should {{Color|ALBW Dark Blue|Sahasrahla}} be worried about me? I'm a descendant of the original {{Color|ALBW Dark Blue|Seven Sages}}. I'm just as powerful as they were.|Osfala|ALBW}}</ref> In addition, he tells Link that he's also wielding a [[Sand Rod]], so he's almost invincible.<ref>{{Cite|I even got myself a {{Color|ALBW Dark Blue|Sand Rod}}, so I'm more or less invincible. Bold talk, you might say, but it's simply the truth.|Osfala|ALBW}}</ref> He then enters the Eastern Palace, stating that he'll "get to the bottom of all this Yuga nonsense."<ref>{{Cite|I'll get to the bottom of all this {{Color|ALBW Dark Blue|Yuga}} nonsense. Now, farewell to you, Link.|Osfala|ALBW}}</ref> After Osfala enters the Eastern Palace, Link hears a scream. When the end of the dungeon is reached, Link is already too late, as he arrives just in time to witness Osfala being turned into a [[Painting]] by Yuga. Link engages in battle with Yuga in an attempt to rescue Osfala and [[Seres]].

Later, Yuga uses the Seven Sages' powers to resurrect Ganon from darkness and fuses himself with the Demon King. Afterwards, Link discovers Osfala's Painting in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}; he is shown to it by the [[Thief Girl]] after Link defeats [[Stalblind]] inside the [[Thieves' Hideout (A Link Between Worlds)|Thieves' Hideout]]. When Osfala emerges in the [[Temple of Light|Chamber of Sages]] he is surprised because he believed he would be the Hero of the Age. Even though he is disappointed, he wishes Link good luck for the rest of his quest. He then has {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} return the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Sand Rod|link}} to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}}, where {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can now rent or buy it.

Once all of the Seven Sages are rescued from Lorule's seven dungeons, Osfala and the other six Sages use their power to summon the [[Triforce of Courage]] so Link could defeat [[Yuga Ganon]].
