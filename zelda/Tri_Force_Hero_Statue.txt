The {{Term/Store|Tri Force Hero Statue||Series, TFH}},<ref>{{Cite|I've been watching so. So I know you haven't given thanks to the {{Color|TFH Blue|Tri Force Hero statue}} yet. ==Uses==
The {Tri Force Hero Statue is a statue of the [[Tri Force Hero]]s, legendary heroes who strongly resemble the [[Links]], stacked in a [[Totem]]. It is [[Hytopia]]'s main attraction, representing the legend that whenever Hytopia is in peril, three heroes will arrive and aid the kingdom.<ref>{{Cite|It's only natural to be curious about the legend of the Tri Force Heroes, my boy. Their tales have been passed down through the ages in our fair Hytopia. When the kingdom of Hytopia falls into despair, three courageous heroes shall ever arrive to save the day...|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> It also stands in honor of the trials and successes of past and future heroes.<ref>{{Cite|This statue is in recognition of their struggles and triumphs, both past and future.|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> By giving thanks to it, [[Link]] can [[Saving|Save]] his game.<ref>{{Cite|Those thanks you gave were exceptional, my boy! Truly!|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The Statue employs a perspective trick that was used extensively in {{ALBW}}: its model leans backward to give the player a better frontal view within the game's top-down perspective.
