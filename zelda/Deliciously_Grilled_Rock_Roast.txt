{{Term/Store|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roasts|Series, BotW|plural}} are objects in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|I did it! I made a {{Color|BotW Red|deliciously grilled rock roast}}!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Upon retrieving a Rock Roast for Bladon, the {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} will [[Cooking|Cook]] it using the nearby {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, making a {{Term|BotW|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I did it! I made a {{Color|BotW Red|deliciously grilled rock roast}}!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> The smell from the Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast will wake Gonguron from his stupor.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Am I hunger-hallucinating, or do I really smell {{Color|BotW Red|deliciously grilled rock roast}}?!|Gonguron|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Brother! It's ready! You're gonna eat it and LOVE IT, and everything will be as it should be again!|Bladon|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Look at this! It looks so tasty! I NEED to eat it right away!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> Eating the {{Term|BotW|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast}} restores {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}}'s strength, allowing him to hurriedly break through the nearby wall of hard rock, revealing the {{Term|BotW|Kayra Mah Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yummy! Delicious! Incredible! That texture! That crunch! Ooooh, and that gritty feeling as it glides down my throat! OH! Yes! Can't forget that savory flavor of condensed minerals seared in high heat to perfection! It's a taste explosion! It's restoring my strength! I'm like a phoenix reborn and ready to fly! In fact, I AM Death Mountain!! Your cookin' is the best of the best, brother!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> After this, Rock Roasts will no longer appear at {{Term|BotW|Gortram Cliff|link}}.

Link is unable to cook one should he take one to a different {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}} or places it on {{Term|BotW|Aji|link}}'s [[Protein Palace|grill]], meaning {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} is the only way to turn it into a {{Term|BotW|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast}}.
