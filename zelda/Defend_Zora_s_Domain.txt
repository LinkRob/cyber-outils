{{Term/Store|Defend Zora's Domain||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 46 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defend Zora's Domain|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Defend Zora's Domain}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must capture five {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} within five minutes. One {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} spawns in the northwest, at the end of a path. If that {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} goes uphill to the start of the path, they can interact with a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok|link}} and obtain a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}. The same {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} only has access to two of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} from the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, as they are blocked off from the other three by closed gates. Although, if an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is captured, the gate next to it will open, allowing access to other {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}. The second {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} spawns in the eastern side of the map. Two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are found at the top of the map, and the third at the bottom. As the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} capture {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} and move throughout the map, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} will spawn inside the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}. Two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} spawn in the center {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} when either the other four are captured or the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is approached.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
