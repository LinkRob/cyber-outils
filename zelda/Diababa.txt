{{Term/Store|Diababa||Series, TP, TPHD}} is the [[Boss]] of the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}} in {{TP}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref> and the first overall boss of the game. It is a three-headed [[Deku Baba]], with its central head possessing a single eye incorporated into its mouth's tongue-like appendage and the other heads being more Deku Baba-like .

After Link enters Diababa's chamber, a large room with a quartet of waterfalls and a pool of poisonous water containing a large tree and floating debris, Diababa's Deku Babas will emerge from the water.

In the first phase of the fight, [[Bombling]]s can be seen perched on the debris. [[Link]] can use the [[Gale Boomerang]] to make them collide with each of the Babas, with a single hit defeating each one. They will additionally attack with a bite that can be easily avoided.

The second phase of the fight begins when Diababa's central body emerges. After a few seconds, [[Ook]] appears and swings across the room carrying Bomblings for Link to use to stun the central head, leaving Diababa's eye open for Link to attack with his [[Ordon Sword|Sword]]. Upon recovering, it sprays poison at Link, which he must dodge. Link must repeat this process until Diababa is defeated, while its Deku Babas occasionally attack; Link can choose to defeat them, but they will eventually recover. Upon Diababa's vanquishment, Link receives the first of the three [[Fused Shadow]]s and a [[Heart Container]].

==Trivia==
*When defeated, Diababa, along with [[Blizzeta]], has a different "Boss Clear Fanfare" in comparison to the other bosses in {{TP|-}}.
*Diababa is the only two-stage boss in ''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'' to have two phases in the second phase, making Diababa have the least amount of HP out of all bosses in the game.
*If Link descends into the pool where Diababa was, he will fade back to the entrance of the boss room.{{Verify|TP}}
*The idea of Diababa is very reminiscent of [https://papermario.fandom.com/wiki/Lava_Piranha Lava Piranha], a boss in the Nintendo 64 game ''Paper Mario'', as both bosses are large and three-headed plant-like monster taking place in a liquid-like area that are larger versions of the plant enemies. Also, both bosses take place in a cave-like area and can spew out a liquid-like attack.
*Diababa's appearance is likely based off of [[wikipedia:Biollante|Biollante]], a mutant rose kaiju appearing in the ''Godzilla'' film franchise. Diababa's Japanese name, ''Babaranto'', appears to reference this.

==Nomenclature==
Diababa is a portmanteau of "Diabolic" and "Deku Baba".
