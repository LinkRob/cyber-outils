{{Term/Store|Fishing Hole Pass|Fishing Hole Passes|Series, MM3D|plural}} are items in {{MM3D}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123 {{Exp Game|MM3D}}}}</ref>

==Uses and Location==
Fishing Hole Passes can be used to [[Fishing|fish]] for free in the Swamp and Ocean [[Fishing Hole]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Present one of these passes at a
Fishing Hole in exchange for one free Fishing Rod rental.|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Lets you borrow a fishing rod for free. Can be redeemed at either the Swamp or Ocean Fishing Hole.|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> They are awarded to [[Link]] by completing some minigames or [[sidequest]]s a second time.<ref>{{Cite|Here, take this. It's just a small token of my gratitude. Go have some fun if you ever have the time to spare. It's relaxing.|Gorman|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In any case, you've saved me! But how can I ever repay you...Oh, I know! I'll give you a free pass for the Fishing Hole between here and town. It's the least I can do!|Cursed Rich Man|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, I almost forgot. Please accept this as a token of gratitude...Truthfully, I thought of giving you this mask that I'm wearing...But it seems you already have one, so I'll give you this instead. Take this to a Fishing Hole and you can get in for free. The more fish the merrier, am I right?|Goron|MM3D}}</ref> They replace the 50 [[Rupee]]s awarded in {{MM}} for completing a minigame, which is the cost of fishing in either of the Fishing Holes.<ref>{{Cite|Hey. So. Feel like goin' fishin'? No time limit. 50 Rupees. Free if you've got a Fishing Hole Pass.|Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey. Cool. Just fork over one Fishing Hole Pass, then.|Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref> Link can hold up to 99 passes.
