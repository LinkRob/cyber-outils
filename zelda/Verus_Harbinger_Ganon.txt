==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Versus Harbinger Ganon}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given five minutes to defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Harbinger Ganon}}. It can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, 600 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
