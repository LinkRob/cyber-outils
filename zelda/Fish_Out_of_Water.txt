{{Term/Store|Fish Out of Water||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 22 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fish Out of Water|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Fish Out of Water}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must rescue three {{Plural|HWAoC|Zora Captain|link}}. The first is saved by capturing the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} he is stuck in, which is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos|link}} and has a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos}} as its boss. The second captain is in the same situation, but the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} he is in has a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}} as its boss instead. The third captain is saved when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} defeats the two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} that are assaulting him. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Star Fragment|link}}, and 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.
