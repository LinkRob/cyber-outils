The {{Term/Store|Purah Pad|Purah Pads|TotK}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Purah Pad}}<br/>Princess Zelda's prized device. It has a variety of features that are essential for adventure.|Inventory|TotK}}</ref> 

==Location and Uses==
The Purah Pad functions almost identically to the [[Sheikah Slate]], however, abilities are now controlled by [[King Rauru's Right Arm|Rauru's arm]] instead of the slate. Similar to the Sheikah Slate, it also offers access to the [[Hyrule Compendium]], a catalog that allows [[Link]] to use pictures to label unique monsters, weapons, and even ingredients. In shape, it resembles a [[Nintendo Switch]] more compared to the Sheikah Slate.

Activating the scope midair will work, but time will be slowed without a stamina drain.

Zelda utilized it to aid King Rauru and a dying Sonya shortly after Ganondorf became the Demon King.
