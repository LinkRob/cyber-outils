'''Sludge''' is a slime-like object in {{TotK}}. It slows [[Link]] down, but it can be dissolved by [[Splash Fruit]]s.

== Location and Uses ==
The sludge is the [[Regional Phenomena|regional phenomenon]] affecting the [[Zora]], who need clean water to live. It is produced by the [[Mucktorok]] and the [[Sludge Like]], two major bosses fought in [[Sidon of the Zora]].
