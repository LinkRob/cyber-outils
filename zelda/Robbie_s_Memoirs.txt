{{Term/Store|''Robbie's Memoirs''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Robbie's Memoirs|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Robbie's Memoirs}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} located on a desk in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. It details the life of {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} following the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}. Within, he recounts his journey to {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}, the creation of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}}, how he met his wife {{Term|BotW|Jerrin|link}}, and the birth of their son, {{Term|BotW|Granté|link}}. It ends with Purah telling Robbie not to send anyone, including Granté, to her, due to her anti-aging experiments that left her looking like a young child.
