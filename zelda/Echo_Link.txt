'''[[Echo]] Link''' is a recurring [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==

===Physical Appearance===
'''Echo Links''' resemble [[Link]] himself, though their eyes are red and their bodies are surrounded by a purple aura.

==History==

==={{EoW|-}}===
At the start of {{EoW|-}}, [[Link]] battles [[Ganon]] and initially appears to triumph. However, Ganon’s [[Trident]] opens a [[Rift]], drawing Link into the [[Still World]], allowing imposters of himself to take his place.

Within the [[Suthorn Ruins]], an Echo of Link armed with the [[Sword of Might]] and the [[Hylian Shield]] is fought by [[Princess Zelda]].<ref name="Echo Link 1">{{Cite|Huh? Is that the person who fought the blue monster?|Tri|EoW}}</ref><ref name="Echo Link 2">{{Cite|That was intense... He looked like the guy who helped you before, but this one wanted to hurt you... He was...weird. Link the imposters back at the castle. Looks like he left something behind...|Tri|EoW}}</ref> Similar Echoes of Link also appear in the [[Gerudo Sanctum]] and [[Eldin Temple]], wielding the [[Bow of Might]] and [[Bombs of Might]], respectively. With each Echo she defeats, Zelda expands her arsenal by acquiring a new item which she can use through [[Swordfighter Form]].
