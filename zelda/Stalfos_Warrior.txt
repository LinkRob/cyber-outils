==Characteristics==
==={{PH|-}}===
Stalfos Warriors appear in [[Mutoh's Temple]] in {{PH}}. They rise when [[Link]] approaches and attack him by repeatedly swinging their Swords. They will jump away if Link tries to strike back. Stalfos Warriors can be defeated with a single hit of the [[Hammer]].

==={{ST|-}}===
Stalfos Warriors appear in the [[Sand Temple]] in {{ST}}. They rise when Link approaches and attack him by repeatedly swinging their Swords. They will jump away if Link tries to strike back, so he can attack them with a ranged weapon like the [[Bow]]. Stalfos Warriors can be defeated with a single [[Bomb]].
