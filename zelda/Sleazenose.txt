'''Sleezenose''' is a character featured in the "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stinging a Stinger}}" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. He specializes in peddling<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Sleezenose's the name and peddling's my game. Many thanks, oh noble Lord.|name=Sleezenose|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> and is known to be a conman.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, pipedown, you old conman. You're getting what you deserve. If you hadn't cheated me out of my sword, none of us would be here now.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} is first introduced when {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} saves him from men who were trying to steal his money. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} thanks the young hero for his help and offers to give him a free {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword|link}} as a sign of gratitude.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Say, you're one hot swordsman. Which reminds me, I have just the gift for you for saving my life.|name=Sleezenose|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> Although {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} at first hesitates to trade it in for his {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Crissword|link}},<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Gee, I don't know. It's been a pretty good sword.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} explains that anyone who carries his sword is made irresistible to women, especially princesses.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Yes, but this sword is much better. For instance, it makes whoever carries it irresistible to ladies. If you know what I mean. [...] ESPECIALLY princesses.|name=Sleezenose|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> This convinces {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and immediately hands over his old sword when he hears {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} screaming for help.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=It's a deal! Oh boy!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> After {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} leaves, it is revealed that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} had a deal with the three burly men who were harassing him and gives them the money for helping him get the sword from Link.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Alright, a bonus it is, but don't worry, without this sword, he'll never be that good again.|name=Sleezenose|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref>

Later on in the episode, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} tries to sell {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}'s sword to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}} for 1000 {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Rupee|display=Rubies|link}},<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=For you, 1000 Rubies.|name=Sleezenose|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> but the evil wizard disagrees and simply takes it from {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}}'s hands with no reward.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=What?! Outrageous! Seize him! How about zero!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> Moreover, he orders the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin|link}} to throw {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda into {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gohma|link}}'s lair.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Throw him to Gohma, then those two!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> The heroes defeat {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gohma}} while {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} opens the gate, allowing them to exit back into the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld|link}} as they think of a plan to defeat {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Uh, I was saying the gate's open. Let's get out of here!|name=Sleezenose|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> 

{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} helps {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda with their plan of besting {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} while using "natural means" as explained by the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce|link}} of Wisdom.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=When magical means will not suffice, use natural means as your device.|name=Triforce of Wisdom|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> He retrieves two beehives and, when {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} sees that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} is approaching the castle with the sword in hand, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} walks towards the evil wizard and pretends to be offering a box with the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom as a sign of loyalty to him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The Triforce of Wisdom, your grossness! I offer it to you as a symbol of my loyalty.|name=Sleezenose|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} walks away as {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} opens the box, which actually contains one of the beehives. After {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} is defeated, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} realizes that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} is gone and discovers that he took the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom while they were fighting {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hey, where's Sleezenose? Oh no, the Triforce of Wisdom! We left it unguarded! [...] It's gone!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> However, Zelda then reveals that she hid the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} underneath {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}'s pile of dirty clothes and that she had asked {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte|link}} earlier on to cast a spell on the box with the ant hill.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh he thought he did. But I knew we shouldn't trust him. So I had Spryte cast a spell over that box with the ant hill in it.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=9}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose}} is then shown carrying the supposed {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom only for him to discover that he had been tricked. He runs away as the ants bite him.
