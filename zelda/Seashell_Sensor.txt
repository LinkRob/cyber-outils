The {{Term/Store|Seashell Sensor||Series, LANS}} is an item in {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|LANS Pink|Seashell Sensor}}! This'll let you know when there's a seashell close by!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|LANS|Seashell Sensor}} can be obtained in the {{Term|LANS|Seashell Mansion|link}} after finding 15 {{Plural|LANS|Secret Seashell|link}}. When on, a beep plays when {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} is close to a {{Term|LANS|Secret Seashell}}. The {{Term|LANS|Seashell Sensor}} can be turned on or off in the Inventory screen.
