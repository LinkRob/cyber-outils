'''Hudson Construction''' is a construction company in {{TotK}}.

==Features and overview==
Hudson Construction is a company founded by [[Hudson]] and [[Rhondson]] as the successor of [[Bolson Construction]], following the events of {{BotW}}.

==History ==
Hudson Construction, like its predecessor, specializes in modular building design which allows for simple and customizable houses and buildings at an affordable price and using less resources. [[Bolson]] had given the company to Hudson after Hudson founded [[Tarrey Town]]. Despite this, Hudson decided to change only the name and kept many of his former employer's rules and designs such as only those who's names end in 'son' may work for the company as an employee.

Hudson Construction has played a vital role in the restoration of Hyrule known as the [[Hyrule Restoration Project]] in which Hudson Construction was contracted to rebuild various structures, towns and settlements such as bridges and [[Hyrule Castle Town]]. Advertisements for the company as well as building material depots can be found alongside main roads all throughout Hyrule. 

Hudson Construction was contracted by the Shiekah to build the [[Skyview Tower]]s to help survey the land as well as Lookout Landing, a small fort located just south of [[Hyrule Castle Town|Hyrule Castle]] used as a base of operations for the Hyrule Restoration Project. Hudson Construction was forced to put the restoration project on hold due to the [[Upheaval]].
