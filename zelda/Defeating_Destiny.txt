{{Term/Store|Defeating Destiny||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 65 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeating Destiny|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Defeating Destiny}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given six minutes to defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}}. At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Hollow|link}} of {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}} spawn, and must be defeated to open a gate in the center of the map. After they are killed, the gate opens and a {{Term|HWAoC|Hollow}} of {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}} spawn near it. They must be KO’d to open a gate further along the path. When they are killed, the gate opens and a {{Term|HWAoC|Hollow}} of {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}} spawns. It must be killed to open the final gate, located in front of {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the last {{Term|HWAoC|Hollow}}, two more {{Plural|HWAoC|Hollow}} of {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} spawn and must be killed as well. The gate opens when they are defeated, opening the way to {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}} and resetting the timer to seven minutes. When {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. It can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Bladed Rhino Beetle|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Rugged Rhino Beetle|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Tireless Frog|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
