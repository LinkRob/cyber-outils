==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Bushy-Tailed Squirrel}}
{{Plural|BotW|Bushy-Tailed Squirrel}} are a brown species of facultative bipedal [[Squirrel]]s with a beige underbelly. They usually stand on their hind-legs to get a better view of their surroundings before scurrying away on all fours.

Bushy-Tailed Squirrels are found extensively throughout the forests {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}} notes [[Hyrule Field]] and the [[Hyrule Ridge]] as locations where they are common, but they can be found in most Regions. They are very timid rodents that will immediately dart into the thicket at the slightest detection of danger. {{Plural|BotW|Bushy-Tailed Squirrel}} drop an {{Term|BotW|Acorn|link}} when attacked.<ref>{{Cite|These small creatures are known for their large tails. They tend to live in forests and subsist on acorns and other nuts. They carry their food in their mouths but will sometimes drop it when surprised in an adorable display of shock.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|BotW|Bushy-Tailed Squirrel}} resemble {{wp|Eurasian red squirrel|Eurasian red squirrels}}, particularly through their similar ears.
