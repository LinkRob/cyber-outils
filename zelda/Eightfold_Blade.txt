{{Plural|BotW|Eightfold Blade}} are kodachi forged with {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, thought to be among the sharpest {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} ever made.<ref name="Inventory"/> These swords belong to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 170}}</ref> They can be found in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}} and around {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}, particularly near the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} town of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. They can also be found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn after scanning the {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Sheik|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

Since {{Plural|BotW|Eightfold Blade}} are made of metal, they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. They also attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Eightfold Blade}} will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Eightfold Blades reappear in ''Tears of the Kingdom'', being wielded by [[Yiga Footsoldier]]s in battle, though they have visible decay from [[Ganon|Ganondorf]]'s [[Gloom]]. It can be [[Fuse|fused]] with other weapons and materials to enhance its potential when wielded by Link. The weapon has a higher performance when used in {{Term|TotK|Sneakstrike|link}} attacks. An intact Eightfold Blade may be found in the {{Term|TotK|Depths|link}}.
