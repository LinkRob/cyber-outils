{{Term/Store|Goron Powder Keg Shop||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref name="Store">{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Goron Powder Keg Shop}}<br/>New customers will be tested<br/>on proper usage.<br/>The Shopkeeper|Sign|MM3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Located in the [[Goron Village]] of [[Snowhead]], the shop is owned by a [[Goron]], whose large size occupies most of the space in the shop, with the exception of a stove. During the winter, the entrance to the Shop is blocked by ice and can only be entered during the spring or by melting the ice with a [[Fire Arrow]].

Inside, [[Goron Link]] can talk to the Goron to get certified to use Powder Kegs.<ref name="Store"/> He will give him a lit Powder Keg, which he then must use to destroy the boulder blocking the entrance to the [[Goron Racetrack]] before the Keg explodes.<ref>{{Cite|If you can destroy the boulder that blocks the entrance to the Goron Racetrack near here... using the Powder Keg I'm about to give you, then I'll approve you to carry them.|Goron|MM}}</ref> If Link successfully completes this task, the Goron will give him a free Powder Keg, certifying him to buy and carry one Powder Keg at a time.<ref>{{Cite|It looks like you managed to succeed! Knowing your skills, I feel fine letting you handle Powder Kegs on your own. It was bad of me to put you through such a dangerous test. I want you to take this as my apology.|Goron|MM}}</ref>

After Link is certified to use Powder Kegs (even after returning to the Dawn of the First Day), Goron Link can buy Powder Kegs here.<ref>{{Cite|Want a Powder Keg? Powder kegs explode with powerful blasts and are very dangerous... Until I have tested you to see if you can use them properly, I can't let you use any on your own.|Goron|MM}}</ref> In {{MM}}, the Goron sells Powder Kegs for 100 Rupees each;<ref>{{Cite|Will you a buy a Powder Keg for 100 Rupees?|Goron|MM}}</ref> in {{MM3D}}, he sells them for 20 Rupees each.<ref>{{Cite|Will you a buy a Powder Keg for 20 Rupees?|Goron|MM3D}}</ref>
