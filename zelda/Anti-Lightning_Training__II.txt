{{Term/Store|Anti-Lightning Training: II||HWAoC}} is a level 27 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Lightning Training: II|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Lightning Training: II}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Zapshroom|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Safflina|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Voltfin Trout|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat 200 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within four minutes. There is only a handful of {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} at first, although more spawn along the path as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s KO count increases, along with a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu|link}} and a lot of {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Keese|link}}. Eventually, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} will reach a closed gate with an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} and electric {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} nearby, although only 200 KOs are needed to open it. When the objective is reached and the gate is opened, the timer resets to three minutes, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s new objective is to kill two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}. When both are defeated, the timer resets to three minutes again, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Hinox}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Hinox}} is defeated.
