The {{Term/Store|Honey Cannon||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Honey Cannon|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Honey Cannon}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Palace Dish|link}}, five {{Plural|ST|Pearl Necklace|link}} and one {{Term|ST|Goron Amber|link}}. Its design features an upended pot dripping with honey; its description reads "Even if you can't eat it, this cannon is sure to satisfy."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Honey Cannon}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Sweet Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
