{{Term/Store|Dark Clump|Dark Clumps|Series, TotK|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dark Clump -  An unusual substance, it's cold to the touch, and it seems as though it's filled with pitch-black darkness. Use it in cooking to get a gloom-resistant effect|Inventory|TotK}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Dark Clump can be obtained by defeating [[Gloom Spawn]] or [[Phantom Ganon]]. It can also be bought from [[Bargainer Statue]]s for 10 [[Poe]]s each.

When used in cooking with other [[food]] ingredients, it grants the Gloom Resistance [[effect]]. The level of resistance is determined by the amount of Dark Clump in the recipe. If Dark Clump is cooked by itself, or if cooked with ingredients containing conflicting potential effects,{{verify}} it will result in a [[Dubious Food|failed dish]].

Dark Clump can be used to cook certain dishes which includes:
*[[Dark Cake]]
*[[Dark Curry]]
*[[Dark Rice Balls]]
*[[Dark Soup]]
*[[Dark Stew]]

== Trivia ==
The text on the talisman seemingly resembles the words "Dark Lord" in Kanji （闇神）, a nod to one of the many titles [[Ganon]] has. The last letter for Deity (神) could be a nod to [[Demise]], as many believed he is the original incarnation of [[Ganondorf]].
