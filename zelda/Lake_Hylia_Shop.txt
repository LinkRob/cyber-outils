The {{Term/Store|Lake Hylia Shop||Series}} is a shop in {{ALttP}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP}} It is identical in appearance to the [[Death Mountain Shop]], consisting of a small cave-like enclosure. Like its Death Mountain counterpart, it sells the same wares as the [[Kakariko Village Shop]] and all three of them are run by identical [[Hooded Shopkeeper|shopkeepers]].

The shop is located on the northwestern shore of [[Lake Hylia]]. A [[sign]] reading "Shopping ahead at Lake Hylia ↓", found near the bridge east of [[Link's House]] points to the area. Banners resembling shields mark the cave the shop can be found in. The only other building in this immediate area is a [[Fortune Teller]]'s Shop.
