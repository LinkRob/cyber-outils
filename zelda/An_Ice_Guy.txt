{{Term/Store|An Ice Guy||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|An Ice Guy}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kara Kara Bazaar}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will encounter a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} named {{Term|BotW|Guy|link}} sitting at the edge of the water. He left {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}} to earn money out in the world,<ref>{{Cite|I had a great plan, you know. I left Rito Village to try to earn some money out in the world.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> but the {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} has stalled his progress.<ref>{{Cite|But it's so incredibly hot here during the day. It's been tough to work up the will to go any farther.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> Despite not being able to enter, he would like to see {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I would like to see what Gerudo Town looks like, even if I can't go inside... It's just so hot...|Guy|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Guy}} mentions to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he has heard of an {{Term|BotW|Elixir|link}} that could help him made from {{Plural|BotW|Insect|link}} with cooling effects and {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I heard there's an {{Color|BotW Red|elixir that can help beat the heat}}. It's made from {{Color|BotW Blue|insects with cooling effects}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|monster parts}}.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> However, he is unable to find this {{Term|BotW|Elixir}} being sold anywhere.<ref>{{Cite|But they don't seem to be selling the elixir anywhere...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He has heard that these {{Plural|BotW|Insect}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I've heard {{Color|BotW Blue|insects with these cooling effects}} live in {{Color|BotW Red|the cold parts of the Gerudo Highlands}}, but...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> but he does not want to venture there himself due to the dangerous [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]] that inhabit the area.<ref>{{Cite|Well, the monsters there are rough, to say the least, so I'm not having a lot of success getting myself motivated.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> After Link is done talking to him, he will receive the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

In order to complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, Link must {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} either a {{Term|BotW|Cold Darner|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Winterwing Butterfly|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} to make a {{Term|BotW|Chilly Elixir|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}}, who can be found nearby, sells {{Plural|BotW|Cold Darner}} for 10 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.

Once he is in possession of a {{Term|BotW|Chilly Elixir}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Guy}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh? Could it be?|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He will thank Link, as he can now traverse the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} during the day.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! Now I can get across the desert while it's still light out!|Guy|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here, let me give you something in return.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
