{{Term/Store|The NecroDancer|The NecroDancers|Series, CoH}} is a {{Term||Boss|link}} in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The NecroDancer}}|N/A|CoH}}</ref> He appears exclusively in {{Term|CoH|Story Mode — Octavo|link}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Term|CoH|The NecroDancer}} is the future version of {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} who defeated {{Term|CoH|Ganondorf|link}} before he could ascend to power.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, how quaint. It's my past self! Don't worry, dear {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}}. Your troubles are over. I've silenced the fortune-tellers, broken the bonds of fate, and defeated {{Color|CoH Blue|Ganondorf}} before he could take power! And now I will rule over {{Color|CoH Blue|Hyrule}} at last!|The NecroDancer|CoH}}</ref> He resides in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}} where he prepares to rule over the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}. After traveling to the {{Term|CoH|Future World}} with the intention of fighting {{Term|CoH|Ganon|link}} only to find the tarnished world his future self created, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} resolves to stop his evil plans.<ref>{{Cite|Rule over {{Color|CoH Blue|Hyrule}}? That was never what I wanted! I just wanted to prepare for the inevitable... You've become a monster, like the one I wanted to stop! I'll put an end to you!|Octavo|CoH}}</ref>

{{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} admits that {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} reminds her of {{Term|Series|The NecroDancer}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm beginning to think that I'll never get home with {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}} around. He reminds me of someone from my world that I {{Color|CoH Blue|really}} don't like! Why don't we join forces to take him down?|Cadence|CoH}}</ref> who he later became. However, {{Term|CoH|The NecroDancer}} is not the same {{Term|Series|The NecroDancer|display=NecroDancer}} from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer'', who was a simple bard known as Octavian prior to amassing an army of monsters and obtaining the {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute|link}} in {{Term|CoH|Cadence}}'s world.

**"And now, the grand finale!", during his final phase.
