{{Term/Store|Lolly||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Zill and Friends}}

{{Term|TMC|Lolly}} resides with his family, {{Term|TMC|Emma|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)|link}}, in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. He is first encountered during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, where he blocks the stairs north of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square|link}} until {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Small Shield|link}}. He claims to have seen a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} in his father's shoe.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, guy, have you ever seen the Picori? I saw one just the other day! It wore a red cone-shaped hat, and it was really small... And it was standing inside my dad's shoe! I really saw it! I swear!|Lolly|TMC}}</ref> He accompanies the {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|link}} when he gives {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}}, and can be found standing at the {{Term|TMC|Fountain|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)|link}} afterwards, where he explains to {{Term|TMC|Link}} that only children can see the {{Term|TMC|Minish}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you wanna know what I heard? I heard that only kids can see the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}}! I wonder if we can see the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}}, too.|Lolly|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Lolly}} can be found on the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}, running around with {{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)}} and {{Term|TMC|Zill|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Waaait!|Lolly|TMC}}</ref> He moves to stand outside the newly-opened {{Term|TMC|Chest Mini-Game Shop|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}, where he expresses his desire to go inside.<ref>{{Cite|Aw, gee. It looks like they're having fun. I wish I could be in there...|Lolly|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} sees the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Lolly}} wanders around north of the {{Term|TMC|Fountain}}, playing in the water.<ref>{{Cite|This water feels good...|Lolly|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} has received a golden {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}}, {{Term|TMC|Lolly}} can be found standing on the roof of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}. After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Lolly}} returns to the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn}} and stays there for the remainder of the game.

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let's do it!|Lolly|TMC}}</ref>
