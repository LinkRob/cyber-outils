{{Term/Store|Flashing Spot on the Floor|Flashing Spots on the Floor|Series, TMC|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... Perhaps it refers to those {{Color|TMC Blue|flashing spots on the floor}} around you.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Flashing Spot on the Floor}} are found throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} in groups of two or more. Once {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Melari|link}} and infuses it with both the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}}, he gains the ability to split himself into two copies by charging a {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack|link}} and standing over {{Plural|TMC|Flashing Spot on the Floor}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Blue|Fill your sword with power and walk over the glowing tile...}}|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whoa! You just {{Color|TMC Blue|split in two}}! So this is the {{Color|TMC Blue|power of the White Sword}}! I guess you can double yourself like that anytime you see those panels...|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> As {{Term|TMC|Link}} infuses more {{Plural|TMC|Four Elements|link|display=Elements}} into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword}}, he can use {{Plural|TMC|Flashing Spot on the Floor}} to split himself into three copies, and finally four when he obtains the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}.

{{Plural|TMC|Flashing Spot on the Floor}} are found in various {{Term|TMC|Overworld|link}} areas, as well as the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must use the {{Term|TMC|White Sword}} (or {{Term|TMC|Four Sword}}) to split himself into copies on these panels to solve a variety of puzzles and progress through his adventure.
