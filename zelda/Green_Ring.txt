The {{Term/Store|Green Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Green Ring<br/>Sword damage {{Icon|OoS Up Arrow}}<br/>Damage taken {{Icon|OoS Down Arrow}}|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Green Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it increases [[Sword]] damage while decreasing damage taken by [[Link]].<ref name="Description"/> The Green Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
