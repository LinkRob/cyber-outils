==Elixirs==
===Elixir Ingredients===
Elixirs also provide Link with Boost effects, and can sometimes replenish health. Critters can be cooked in a [[Cooking Pot]] with at least one Monster Part to create successful Elixirs. Food ingredients can optionally be added. Ingredients sharing the same Effect Class can be cooked together to create stronger and longer-lasting boosts.<br/>

If a critical success, the elixir's time values increase by 5:00.

=== Ingredient Tiers ===
Monster parts add time in three different amounts.

* '''Tier 1 = 1:10'''
[[Ancient Screw]], [[Bokoblin Horn]], [[Chuchu Jelly]], [[Hinox Toenail]], [[Keese Wing]], [[Lizalfos Horn]], [[Moblin Horn]], [[Octorok Balloon]], [[Octorok Tentacle]]

* '''Tier 2 = 1:50'''
[[Ancient Gear]], [[Ancient Shaft]], [[Bokoblin Fang]], [[Electric Keese Wing]], [[Fire Keese Wing]], [[Ice Keese Wing]], [[Hinox Tooth]], [[Lizalfos Talon]], [[Moblin Fang]], [[Octorok Eyeball]], [[Red Chuchu Jelly]], [[White Chuchu Jelly]], [[Yellow Chuchu Jelly]]

* '''Tier 3 = 3:10'''
[[Ancient Core]], [[Bokoblin Guts]], [[Giant Ancient Core]], [[Keese Eyeball]], [[Lizalfos Tail]], [[Moblin Guts]]
