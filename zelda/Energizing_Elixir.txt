{{Term/Store|Energizing Elixir|Energizing Elixirs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Elixir}}<br/>Restores your stamina, which is used when performing physical actions such as climbing walls or swimming.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
When drunk, Energizing Elixirs will partially refill {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They're useful for performing physical actions, such as climbing walls or swimming.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Energizing Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|BotW|Energetic Rhino Beetle|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Restless Cricket|link}}

By adding any of these energizing ingredients, Link can make an Energizing Elixir that will also heal him:

* {{Term|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}
