The {{Term/Store|+Shadow Link Effect||Series, LANS}} is an item in {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|LANS Pink|+Shadow Link Effect}}! Cast a terrifying shadow over your dungeon!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The +Shadow Link Effect can be obtained by tapping the {{LA|NS|-}} {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} [[amiibo]] after speaking with {{Term|LANS|Dampé|link}}. The effect causes {{Term|LANS|Shadow Link|link}} to appear in the chamber and chase {{Term|LANS|Link}} throughout it. If defeated, he will drop various {{Plural|LANS|Rupee|link}} and a {{Term|LANS|Fairy|link}}.
