{{Term/Store|Notts||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Notts|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
Notts is a Rito located in [[Rito Village]]. She the daughter of [[Kass]] and [[Amali]], and the sister of [[Kheel]], [[Genli]], [[Kotts]], and [[Cree]]. She is a young Rito with pink feathers.

After the completion of side quest [[Find Kheel]] and activation of shrine quest [[Recital at Warbler's Nest|Recital at Warbler’s Nest]], Notts is located standing on an outcrop of the stone pillar in the middle of Rito Village, singing a song she calls “Return of the Bird-Wrangling Hero, First Movement.” After speaking to her, she will give Link [[Tabantha Wheat]] and fly to [[Warbler's Nest|Warbler’s Nest]] to join Kheel.{{Stub}}
