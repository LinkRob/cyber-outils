The {{Term/Store|House by the Bay||Series}},{{Name Ref}} also known as the {{Term/Store|Ghost's House||LA, LADX}},{{Name Ref}} is a location in {{LA}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{LA|-}}===
The House by the Bay is a small house near {{Term|LANS|Toronbo Shores|link}}. After completing {{Term|LANS|Angler's Tunnel|link}}, a {{Term|LANS|Ghost|link}} follows {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} and asks to be taken to the house.<ref>{{Cite|...the house... ...take me... ...the house... ...at the bay...|Ghost|LADX}}</ref> The {{Term|LANS|Power Bracelet|link}} is required to reach the house. After {{Term|LANS|Link}} successfully brings the {{Term|LANS|Ghost}} to the house, it asks to be taken to its grave.<ref>{{Cite|...Enough... ...cemetery... ...take me... ...my grave...|Ghost|LADX}}</ref> A {{Term|LANS|Secret Seashell|link}} can be found under a {{Term|LANS|Jar|link}} inside the house.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Felicia|link}} finds an abandoned house while exploring {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Yarna Desert|link}}. Inside, they find several weapons laying on the floor. After exiting {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Angler's Tunnel|link}}, a ghost named {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ghost|link|display= Nakura}} asks {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} to take it to its house. After his defeat against {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Karuna|link}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} takes {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ghost|display= Nakura}} to its house, where it rewards him with a new Sword.
{{Noncanon|End}}
