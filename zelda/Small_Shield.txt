{{Term/Store|Small Shield|Small Shields|Series, TMC|plural}},{{Exp Game|TMC}}<ref name="E 139">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139 {{Exp Game|ALttP, TMC}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Fighter's Shield|Fighter's Shields|ALttP|plural}},{{Exp Game|ALttP}}<ref name="E 139"/> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Shield|The shield has three potential levels of power. Your first shield can only fend off the enemy's arrows, spears and rocks. If you can improve your shield, or find a better one, you can defend yourself against different attacks.}}
{{Manual|ALttP&FS|Shield|There are three levels of shield power, each with a greater defensive value.}}
In {{ALttP}}, the Fighter's Shield is a small family shield that belongs to [[Link's Uncle]], and is received from him in the secret passageway leading into [[Hyrule Castle]] grounds. As it is the first shield obtained in the game, it is the weakest of all shields and provides very little defense. It is able to protect against arrows, spears and rocks, but little else. The shield can be eaten by enemies, and if the shield is swallowed up by a [[Pikit]] or a [[Like Like]], another one can be purchased in the many shops throughout the [[Dark World]].

[[Link]] can obtain an upgraded version of the Fighter's Shield known as the [[Red Shield]] by throwing it into the [[Mysterious Pond]] of the [[Waterfall of Wishing]]. Only by answering the [[Great Fairy]]'s question honestly will she reward him with a better shield.<ref>{{Cite|Hello there. Did you drop this? I like an honest person. I will give you something better in return.|Great Fairy|ALttP}}</ref>

The {{Term|TMC|Small Shield}} is sold by [[Pina]] at her stall during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}} Even though she gives {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} the choice to pick the item she wants, and even has it on display as a prize, she tries to persuade the princess to pick a different item. Zelda shortly after gives the Small Shield to {{Term|TMC|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here! I want to give you a present! Doesn't this shield suit you perfectly, Link?|Princess Zelda|TMC}}</ref> Shortly after receiving it, he must use it to protect {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} from a {{Term|TMC|Business Scrub|link}} north of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Owwwwch! Like most basic shields obtained throughout ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, the {{Term|TMC|Small Shield}} can be swallowed by {{Plural|TMC|Like Like|link}}. If this happens, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can defeat the {{Term|TMC|Like Like}} to retrieve the {{Term|TMC|Small Shield}}, or buy a new one at {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Small Shield}} can defend {{Term|TMC|Link}} from the most basic of attacks, such as an {{Term|TMC|Octorok|link}}'s rocks and sword strikes from other enemies. After {{Term|TMC|Link}} completes the main quest, he can turn the {{Term|TMC|Small Shield}} into the {{Term|TMC|Mirror Shield|link}} by allowing {{Term|TMC|Biggoron|link}} to eat it.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome. ==Other Appearances==

==={{ALttP|-}} (Ishinomori)===
[[File:Fighter's Shield.png|thumb|right|Link using the Fighter's Shield against Agahnim]]
In the {{ALttP|Ishinomori}}, Link's uncle is seen using the Fighter's Shield during his duel against [[Agahnim]]. However, after Link witnesses the defeat of his uncle at the hands of his wizard, he rushes to his side and obtains his uncle's sword and shield from him before passing out. Link continues to use the shield only for the first chapter, using it to defend himself from enemy attacks. However, when he reaches the [[Sanctuary (A Link to the Past)|Sanctuary]] with [[Princess Zelda]] and faces off against Agahnim, he loses his sword and shield along with it, and the young hero is never seen using the Fighter's Shield for the rest of the comic.

==Trivia==
*Screenshots from versions of the game prior to release seen in promotional material for the game show the Fighter's Shield with brown coloring, rather than blue.{{Fact|ALttP}} This possibly explains why some official art depicts it as brown, while others depict it as blue.
*The Fighter's Shield is the first shield to have a bird-like design on it in the series, and many aspects of its design were used for the [[Hylian Shield]].
*The [[Great Fairy]] granting Link a better shield when he answers honestly is an homage to the {{Wp|Aesop}} fable, ''{{Wp|The Honest Woodcutter}}''. In the story, a man accidentally flings his axe into a lake and begins to weep, having lost his only means of livelihood. The god {{Wp|Hermes}} decides to take pity on the woodcutter and emerges from the lake holding a golden axe, asking the man if it is his. The man truthfully denies, and Hermes produces a silver axe asking the same. Once again, the man answers honestly saying that he had lost a wooden axe. For his display of honesty, Hermes gives him all three axes. Later, a man overhearing this story decides to fling his own axe into the lake and falsely cry about his loss. Hermes again appears offering to the man a golden axe, which he greedily claims. Disgusted by this man's avarice, Hermes gives him nothing and leaves his axe at the bottom of the lake as punishment.
