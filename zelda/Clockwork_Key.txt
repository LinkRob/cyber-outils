The '''Clockwork Key''' is a nonessential Item in {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
{{hatnote|”A one-of-a-kind winding key. You can use it to make automatons move.” — [[Key Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Key Items]]<ref name="Clockwork Key">{{Cite|A one-of-a-kind winding key. You can use it to make automatons move.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
[[Dampé]] gives [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] the clockwork key after creating his first [[automaton]] for her, the [[Techtite]]. When Zelda summons an automaton, she must first wind it up, leaving herself open to attacks from enemies.

Wearing the [[Clockwork Bangle]] or by drinking certain [[Smoothie|smoothies]] can speed up the winding process.
