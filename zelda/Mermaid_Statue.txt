The {{Term/Store|Mermaid Statue||Series, LA, LADX}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 234}}</ref> also known as '''''The Mourning Mermaid''''',<ref name="Scale">{{Cite|THE MOURNING MERMAID By SCHULE ? ...A scale is missing...|Mermaid Statue|LADX}}</ref> is an object in {{LA}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Mermaid Statue is a Statue sculpted by [[Schule Donavitch]] found near [[Martha's Bay]].<ref name="Scale"/><ref>{{Cite|Ya, I am Schule Donavitch! Zee mermaid statue by zee bay iz my masterpiece! ... To tell you zee truth, zis werk iz not complete! Zee art, it'z... difficult for you to grasp, iz it not?|Schule Donavitch|LADX}}</ref> If [[Link]] places a [[Scale]] from [[Martha]] on the Statue, it will move and reveal a {{Term|LANS|Cave|link}}. Inside, the [[Magnifying Lens]] can be found.<ref>{{Cite|An artist once asked me to pose for him, and he wanted a scale, too... Can the legend of the Magnifying Lens be true...?|Martha|LADX}}</ref>
