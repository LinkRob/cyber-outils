{{Term/Store|Snap Dragon|Snap Dragons|Series, ALttP, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 204 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Snap Dragon|This strange creature was created as a result of a mad alchemist's plant experiments. He uses his tooth-filled maw to snap at anything that gets in his way as he hops about.}}

Snap Dragons hop around at great speeds to attack [[Link]] with their snapping jaws. They appear in various locations in the [[Overworld]] of the [[Dark World]]. Snap Dragons are vulnerable to most weapons. They move in a diagonal matter and often appear in pairs or groups of three. They can also cause up to three [[heart]]s of damage with a single attack.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Stub}}
In {{ALBW}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Snap Dragon}} appear mainly in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}. Unlike A Link to the Past, they will sometimes stop and wait for a little bit.

==Trivia==
*According to the English {{ALttP|-}} manual, Snap Dragons were created by a mad alchemist's plant experiments, <ref>{{Cite manual|quote= '''Snap Dragon'''<br/>This strange creature was created as a result of a mad alchemist's plant experiments. He uses his tooth-filled maw to snap at anything that gets in his way as he hops about.|game= ALttP|page= 45}}</ref> but in the Japanese manual, it is stated that they simply evolved from plants.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= '''ガプラ'''<br/>植物から進化したこの敵は大きな口を開け、鋭い牙でかみついてきます。ヒノックス同様、闇の世界の地上に存在します。|game= ALttP|page= 51}}</ref>
*Due to their hopping motion, they can sometimes be unaffected by the [[Quake Medallion]].{{Verify|ALttP}}
*Their official artwork from {{ALttP|-}} depicts them with green leaves, while in-game they have blue leaves.

==Nomenclature==
Their English name may be a reference to the {{wp|Antirrhinum}} flower, which is also known as the "snapdragon." Their Japanese name is possibly a play on "gapu," the  onomatopoeia for a big bite or chomping sound, and "bura," meaning strolling aimlessly.
