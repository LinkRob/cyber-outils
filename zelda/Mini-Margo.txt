{{Term/Store|Mini-Margo|Mini-Margos|Series, TFH|plural}} are enemies in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 192}}</ref> They are a miniature form of {{Term|TFH|Margoma|link}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TFH|Mini-Margo}} have a similar appearance to {{Term|TFH|Margoma}}, in that they appear as spinning disks covered in spikes. Unlike {{Term|TFH|Margoma}}, which is able to consciously pursue the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Mini-Margo}} are mostly limited to a set path. Some {{Plural|TFH|Mini-Margo}} move in a straight line along their path, stopping only briefly to make turns, while others are able to move in curved or circular paths. One {{Term|TFH|Mini-Margo}} at the end of {{Term|TFH|Stage}} 3 of the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}} is not locked to any path, and instead bounces off the walls of the enclosure it is in.

As {{Plural|TFH|Mini-Margo}} are covered in spikes, they are guarded from the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}' attacks, and approaching them will result in harm. Much like the larger {{Term|TFH|Margoma}}, {{Plural|TFH|Mini-Margo}} have an opening on their surface, which can fit a {{Term|TFH|Bomb|link}} that is thrown into them. They are instantly destroyed when the {{Term|TFH|Bomb}} inside explodes, however, any {{Term|TFH|Links}} standing nearby can still be harmed by the explosion. {{Plural|TFH|Mini-Margo}} are most common to the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}, but are also found in {{Term|TFH|Deception Castle|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Sky Temple|link}}.

In the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Mini-Margo}} appear on the second floor of the {{Term|TFH|Fortified Zone|link}}. In addition to the regular enemies, stacks of {{Plural|TFH|Mini-Margo}} also appear in {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} formations. The enemies in these stacks must be defeated one at a time.
