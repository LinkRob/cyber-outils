"{{Term/Store|EX Treasure: Ancient Mask||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Treasure: Ancient Mask}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Outpost Ruins}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal|link}}, which is rumored to be in the {{Term|BotW|Outpost Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a journal that places the location of this treasure somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Outpost Ruins}}...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by reading {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal}} without triggering any of the loading conditions for its activation.}}

In {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal}}, there are clues to the locations of eight {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, each containing a priceless relic stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the eight priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> The Journal states that the ancient mask is at the ruins where soldiers gathered and the waters of {{Term|BotW|Lake Kolomo|link}} can be seen.<ref>{{Cite|The ancient mask lies {{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where soldiers gathered}}. From there, {{Color|BotW Red|the waters of Lake Kolomo are visible}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found buried next to the southeastern corner of the largest building at the {{Term|BotW|Kolomo Garrison Ruins|link}}. It can be pulled out of the ground using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Once free, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can open the Chest to receive {{Term|BotW|Majora's Mask (Item)|link}}, completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found the treasure that was hidden at Kolomo Garrison Ruins and obtained {{Color|BotW Red|Majora's Mask}}!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
