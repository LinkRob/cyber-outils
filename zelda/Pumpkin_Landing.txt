{{Term/Store|Pumpkin Landing||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Pumpkin Landing|Map|SSHD}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Pumpkin Landing}} goes unnamed in {{HW}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{SS|-}}===
{{Term|SS|Pumpkin Landing}} is located in the northeastern {{Term|SS|Sky|link}} and can only be accessed via {{Term|SS|Loftwing|link}}. {{Term|SS|Pumpkin Landing}} is the second largest island in the {{Term|SS|Sky}} after {{Term|SS|Skyloft|link}}, and is one of the few homes of the {{Term|SS|Starry Firefly|link}}. {{Term|SS|Pumpkin Landing}} houses the {{Term|SS|Lumpy Pumpkin|link}}, a pub owned by {{Term|SS|Pumm|link}} and his daughter {{Term|SS|Kina|link}}, as well as a garden patch in which {{Plural|SS|Pumpkin|link}} grow. At night, a {{Term|SS|Gratitude Crystal|link}} can be found in the shed east of the {{Term|SS|Lumpy Pumpkin}}.{{Plural|SS|Ringer|link}} are known to gather atop this island. {{Term|SS|Link|link}} can partake in a number of sidequests here after damaging a chandelier in pursuit of a {{Term|SS|Piece of Heart|link}}. Two of these sidequests, both involving {{Term|SS|Kina}}, are accomplished outside the establishment. {{Term|SS|Link}} can help {{Term|SS|Kina}} harvest {{Plural|SS|Pumpkin}} by carrying five of them at once to the shed without dropping them. After completion of this task, {{Term|SS|Link}} is rewarded with five {{Plural|SS|Gratitude Crystal}}. Later, {{Term|SS|Kina}} requests assistance in locating someone who can help plow the {{Term|SS|Pumpkin}} patch.<ref>{{Cite|If only there was someone who was really good at plowing the field...|Kina|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SS|Link}} can use his {{Term|SS|Dowsing|link}} ability to find the elderly {{Term|SS|Mogma|link}}, {{Term|SS|Guld|link}}, in {{Term|SS|Eldin Volcano|link}} willing to travel to the {{Term|SS|Sky}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your dowsing ability can be calibrated to locate the person who will plow the pumpkin patch for this girl.|Fi|SS}}</ref> When {{Term|SS|Link}} finds {{Term|SS|Guld}}, {{Term|SS|Fi|link}} sends a transmission to {{Term|SS|LD-301S Scrapper|link}}, who carries the {{Term|SS|Mogma}} into the {{Term|SS|Sky}}. When they arrive, the {{Term|SS|Mogma}} Elder is initially angry that he is being asked to work on a field, but he happily accepts after {{Term|SS|Kina}} praises him for his digging prowess. Afterwards, {{Term|SS|Link}} receives another five {{Plural|SS|Gratitude Crystal}}.

{{Stub|SS}}
{{Term|SS|Pumpkin Landing}} is home to two {{Plural|SS|Goddess's Treasure Chest|link}}. Both can be found on the roof of the {{Term|SS|Lumpy Pumpkin}}.
