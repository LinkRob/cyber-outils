The {{Term/Store|Etsu Korima Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Etsu Korima Shrine}}<br/>Path of Light}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Path of Light''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|Series|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Etsu Korima Shrine}} is located on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} atop {{Term|BotW|Mount Hylia|link}}. It appears as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must defeat the various {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} that appear on {{Term|BotW|Mount Hylia}} during the trial.<ref>{{Cite|The One-Hit Obliterator, as the name suggests, can defeat enemies in one-hit. However, the wielder will also be defeated in one hit. Defeat the monsters at the locations marked on the map within the Great Plateau.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After the last {{Term|BotW|Enemy}} is defeated, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} will rise up from beneath the ground. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters, he is greeted by the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine's}} {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Etsu Korima|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Etsu Korima. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Etsu Korima|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Etsu Korima Shrine}} is covered in {{Term|Series|Darkness|link}}, forcing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to follow light sources such as stationary {{Plural|BotW|Torch|link}} or the glowing walls to find his way through. Through a small corridor from the entrance is a large chamber with seven lasers. The first three are stationary lasers firing vertically from the ceiling, the next three are also stationary but are firing horizontally across the walkway at roughly the same level as {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s eyes when he is standing. The final laser is firing horizontally across the walkway but is on a platform moving up and down.

The next chamber has a large number of stationary {{Plural|BotW|Torch}} scattered around four large rotating cylindrical columns, each column two or three walls covered with spikes attached to them. The third chamber has three sets of small openings, each with four nozzles that spew out jets of {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} lining the opening. The jets can be safely passed through with the {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}} or by carefully crossing over one of the nozzles while it is off. Down a small flight of stairs, three {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} lie in wait, two of which hide behind nearby walls.

At the end of the chamber with {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} is a large staircase leading up to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} ascends the staircase, {{Term|BotW|Etsu Korima Shrine}} will light up. However, the {{Term|Series|Darkness}} will return if {{Term|BotW|Link}} turns back. Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Etsu Korima}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Etsu Korima|BotW}}</ref> Following this, {{Term|BotW|Etsu Korima}} will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Etsu Korima|BotW}}</ref>

A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found on a small wooden platform above some vines in the area with the rotating spike walls. It can be accessed by burning the vines and the platform, causing it to fall to the ground. Inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.
