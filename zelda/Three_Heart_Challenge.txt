The {{Name|Fan|Three Heart Challenge}}, known as a {{Name|Fan|Six Heart Challenge}} in the case of {{SS}}, is an optional challenge where a game in {{TLoZ|Series}} is completed without obtaining or forming a new [[Heart Container]], keeping only the starting Heart Containers throughout the game - three. Because of this type of gameplay where players go throughout the adventure at a rather small amount of [[Life Gauge|health]] for [[Link]], this is widely seen as a major difficulty increase, especially when doing tougher versions of the game such as [[Second Quest]], [[Hero Mode]], or in the case of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Master Quest|Master Quest]]'', resulting in an extreme challenge even for experts and achieving a [[Game Over]] potentially becomes common. Therefore, on social media, like [[Speedrun|speedrunning]] or [[100% Completion|100% completion]], the Three Heart Challenge is often used for bragging rights, though this is posted less frequently in comparison.

==Restrictions and Variations==
It is possible to perform the Three Heart Challenge in most ''Zelda'' games, with only a few exceptions:

*{{TAoL}}, where the game starts with four life bars.
*{{ALttP}}, where the [[Pendants of Virtue|Pendants]] and {{Sec|Crystal|A Link to the Past|Crystals}} will only appear after collecting the Heart Container.
*{{LA}}, where the boss room door leading to the [[Instrument of the Sirens]] will only open after collecting the Heart Container (though the [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|remake]] allows the door to the instrument to open before collecting the container).
*{{HW}}, where the game starts with more Heart Containers and where hearts will increase over time as [[Characters in Hyrule Warriors|Warriors]] level up.
*{{TotK}}, where you are required to obtain an extra Heart Container to leave the [[Great Sky Island]].

In such cases, players can still pretend that they died the moment 3 hearts got depleted or in The Adventure of Link, 3 bars.

In ''Hyrule Warriors'', avoiding a certain amount of damage is necessary to obtain A-ranks in [[Adventure Mode]].

{{TFH}} has no Heart Containers to be gained, but the closest equivalent is having all three Links wear [[Bear Minimum]], leaving them with 6 hearts and double damage taken.

An expansion on the Three Heart Challenge is the Low% challenge, where optional items must be avoided, including upgrades, and collecting [[Potion]]s, [[Fairy|Fairies]], and any other health recovering items. There is also the No Damage challenge, where the player avoids taking any damage whatsoever, which is an even more difficult task.
