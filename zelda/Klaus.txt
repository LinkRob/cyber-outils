{{Term/Store|Klaus||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Klaus}} first appears during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, where he tells the story of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Only once every hundred years does the mystic doorway open. This is the door that links the human world to the world of the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}}. When this door opens, the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}} can enter our world. And guess what! This marks the hundredth year since they last appeared here! I'm sure something wonderful is going to happen at this festival!|Klaus|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} is turned to stone, {{Term|TMC|Klaus}} can be found outside the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, waiting for it to open.<ref>{{Cite|I came to check out a book, but they’re not open yet. Hahhh... Well, age brings patience, sonny. I can wait.|Klaus|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}} and speaks to the {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}} opens, and {{Term|TMC|Klaus}} can be found there wondering which book he should read.<ref>{{Cite|Wonder what I should check out today.|Klaus|TMC}}</ref> He remains at the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}} until it is closed after {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}; he does not appear for the remainder of the game.

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure, why not?|Klaus|TMC}}</ref>
