"{{Term/Store|Manbo's Mambo||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}}" is a song in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You learned {{Color|LANS Red|Manbo's Mambo}}! When you get out of the water, play it!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Overview==
[[Link]] learns it from [[Manbo]] after completing the [[Angler's Tunnel]]. In versions prior to {{LA|NS}}, it is used to [[Warping|warp]] to [[Manbo's Pond]], which is near [[Crazy Tracy]]'s house.

In {{LA|NS}} it can be used to warp to any active [[Warp Hole]].

In all versions it can also be used to warp Link to the beginning of a [[dungeon]]. 

It is the second song Link learns for the [[Ocarina]] in the game.
