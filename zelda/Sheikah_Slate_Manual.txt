The {{Term/Store|''Sheikah Slate Manual''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sheikah Slate Manual|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|Sheikah Slate Manual}} is found on {{Term|BotW|Purah|link|display=Purah's}} desk at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. Purah wrote it to detail how to use certain {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sheikah Slate Manual<br/>Written by: Purah!|Sheikah Slate Manual|BotW}}</ref> It details how to use {{Plural|BotW|Stamp (Object)|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Pin|link}}, hitting objects with {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}}, and how to hide from {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}}.
