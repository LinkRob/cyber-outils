'''Rock Mushrooms''', also known as '''Petri Fungus''',<ref>{{Cite|→ Rock Mushrooms (Petri Fungus) Pick in fall only.|Sign|OoS}}</ref> are [[object]]s in {{OoS}}.

==Overview==
Rock Mushrooms are [[mushroom]]s that can be picked during [[autumn]] only. To pick them, [[Link]] has to use the [[Power Bracelet]]. Rock Mushrooms can also be removed using the [[Magical Boomerang]] or eaten by [[Dimitri]].
