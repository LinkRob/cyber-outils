'''''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U''''' is the collective title for two paired games in the {{SSB|Series}}:

*''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', a game released for the [[Nintendo 3DS]] in 2014.
*''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', a game released for the [[Wii U]] in 2014, shortly afterward.
