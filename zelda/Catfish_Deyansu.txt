'''Catfish Deyansu''' are enemies in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} They appear in the {{Term|FPTRR|Gooey Swamp}}. {{Plural|FPTRR|Catfish Deyansu}} remain passive until {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} attacks them, at which point they will sprout arms and gang up on him. They tend to drop {{Term|FPTRR|Bitter Newt|link}} or {{Term|FPTRR|Explosive Claws|link}} when defeated.

[[File:FPTRR Catfish Gold Deyansu Sprite.png|left]]
'''Gold Catfish Deyansu''' appear rarely in place of normal catfish. As with other gold enemies in the game, they will drop a large number of {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} when defeated.
