The {{Term/Store|Akkala Citadel||Series, HWAoC}} is a location in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Improve}}
There are two variants of {{Term|HWAoC|Akkala Citadel}}, though some features are present in both. Regardless of the variant, {{Term|HWAoC|Akkala Citadel}} is very wide open, with a lot of terrain surrounding the titular citadel, including a training ground in a pit directly in front of the citadel. There are also deactivated {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Simple Ancient Furnace|link}} around. The citadel itself is extremely tight, but interspersed with bigger rooms, a central chamber with a {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Tower|link}}, and an exterior set up with {{Plural|HWAoC|Cannon|link}}. The second variant of the {{Term|HWAoC|Akkala Citadel}} is a bit tighter, with fires raging and wrecked {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} around and rubble covering the citadel. In the first {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} the {{Term|HWAoC|Akkala Citadel}} is in, it is taken over by the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga|link}}, with {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} in the surrounding {{Plural|HWAoC|Keep|link}}. In the second, {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, headed by corrupted {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}}, are trying to take it over, with the {{Term|HWAoC|Champions|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} trying to save {{Term|HWAoC|Robbie|link}} from inside.
