The {{Term/Store|Docks||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Docks'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Docks}} are located inside a cavern on the northern end of the grounds of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can access the {{Term|BotW|Docks}} via the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Moat|link}}. The pathway out of the {{Term|BotW|Docks}} leads into the {{Term|BotW|Library|link}}. Many {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel|link}} can be found throughout the {{Term|BotW|Docks}}.

On the higher level of the {{Term|BotW|Dock}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find several lit stationary {{Plural|BotW|Torchlight|link}} surrounding a single unlit one. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} ignites this stationary {{Term|BotW|Torchlight}}, {{Term|BotW|Saas Ko'sah Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground nearby.

In the {{Plural|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Docks}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find two wooden {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, containing five {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}} and one {{Term|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link}}. These chests can be lifted out of the water with {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}}. Underwater, Link can find two metallic {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, which contain a {{Term|BotW|Star Fragment|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
Atop a ledge at the entrance of the {{Term|BotW|Docks}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}} serving as a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} lifts this {{Term|BotW|Rock}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will reveal itself and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
