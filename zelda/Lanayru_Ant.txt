==Location and Uses==

|quote1= <p>Species name: {{Color|SS Blue|Lanayru Ant}}<br/>Sensitivity to human presence: 60%<br/>Usable as a potion ingredient.</p><p>I project a 50% probability of failing to notice this insect simply because it's small. After discovering its location, you can catch it comparatively easily.</p>

Lanayru Ants can be found in [[Lanayru Desert]]. They can be captured using the [[Bug Net]]. Lanayru Ants can be used to upgrade [[Potion]]s at the [[Potion Shop]] or sold to [[Strich]] at the [[Knight Academy]] during nighttime. They build and live in underground nests where they store water to thrive in the desert's heat.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= An ant that rarely separates from its colony, the Lanayru Ant builds and lives in underground nests in the Lanayru Desert, subsisting off water sources deep underground.|book= E |publisher= Dark Horse Books|page= 67}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You caught [X] {{Color|SS Gold|Lanayru Ant}}! It's said these ants store water deep in their nests, allowing them to thrive even in the intense heat of the open desert.|N/A|SS}}</ref>
