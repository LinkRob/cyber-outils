The {{Term/Store|Torrent Robe||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 141}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Torrent Robe has piqued your interest, has it not? It improves your Water Rod. Quite handy!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Torrent Robe}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Fresh Kelp|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Hytopian Silk|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Aqua Crown|link}} and 600 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. It becomes available for purchase after clearing the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}. Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Torrent Robe}} doubles the radius of the water pillars produced by the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}}.
