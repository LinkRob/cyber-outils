==Location and Uses==
Frozen Hearty Salmon are made by exposing {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}. They heal four {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and grant him temporary [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/> Due to the removal of the excess fat, these salmon don't taste as fishy as they used to.<ref name="Inventory"/>
