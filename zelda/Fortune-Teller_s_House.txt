{{Term/Store|Fortune-Teller's House|Fortune-Teller's Houses|Series, ALttP|plural}} are locations in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= ALttP |page= 29}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Manual|ALttP|The Fortune Teller's House|You can talk to the Fortune Teller to have your fortune read. This information can give you an idea of what to do next. You should have your fortune read as many times as possible. The cost of each session changes with the mood of the Fortune Teller. (The Fortune Teller will also heal your wounds as an extra bonus.}}
There are three {{Plural|ALttP|Fortune-Teller's House}}; two in the {{Term|ALttP|Light World|link}} and one in the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}}. One is located north of {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village|link}}, one is located near the {{Term|ALttP|Lake Hylia Shop|link}}, and one is located north of the {{Term|ALttP|Village of Outcasts|link}}. A {{Term|ALttP|Fortune-Teller|link}} resides in each one.
