The {{Term/Store|Eldin Tower||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Eldin Tower|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Eldin Tower is a [[Sheikah Tower]] located in [[Eldin Canyon]]. The Eldin Tower stands atop a small mountain peak south of [[Death Mountain]], overlooking [[Lake Ferona]] to the south and [[Medingo Pool]] and the main road directly north. The Tower has no obstacles or enemies around it preventing its scale, however, it is incredibly tall and only has two sets of platforms for rest between climbing. The amount of climbing necessary can be reduced by gliding to the Tower from a nearby rock ledge.

Activating the Tower will update [[Link|Link's]] [[Sheikah Slate]] with a [[Map]] to include the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Tower Region|link}}, including Eldin proper and most of Eldin Canyon.
