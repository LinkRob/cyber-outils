{{Term/Store|King Tuft||Series, TFH}} is the monarch that presides over {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 305}}</ref>

==Biography==
Prior to the events of {{TFH}}, {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}}'s daughter, {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}}, was cursed by {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}} to wear an irremovable tacky jumpsuit. The princess's curse had struck fear into the people of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}}, who started to dress less fashionably in fear they would be cursed too.<ref>{{Cite|The worst part is that, ever since Princess Styla became cursed, Hytopia and its people have fallen into despair. Everyone is afraid of fashion now, lest they become cursed as well. Hytopia has never looked so drab...|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} agonized for his daughter and people. As only chosen heroes can enter the witch's domain, the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}, {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} issued a request to kingdoms near and far for worthy heroes, hoping they could put a stop to {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} and save his daughter.<ref>{{Cite|The king, too, was in agony. As only chosen heroes may enter the witch's domain, the despicable Drablands, King Tuft issued a call to kingdoms near and far for a hero to save the day.|N/A|TFH}}</ref> The king however had set strict requirements in his search for a hero: they would have to share the traits of the fabled heroes in his kingdom's prophecy, the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero|link}}. The king believes that one day the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero}} will arrive and save the kingdom,<ref>{{Cite|But I believe. I must! I believe the Legendary Tri Force Heroes will appear once more and save our kingdom!|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> and bring everlasting peace and style to {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}} after overcoming all challenges.<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of Tri Force Heroes, there's more to the legend than most people know. The story goes... "When the Tri Force Heroes overcome all challenges, Hytopia will be blessed with everlasting peace and style."|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} sought out heroes with pointed ears, long sideburns, a side-parted hairstyle, and the ability to form a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}}; all of these traits of the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero}}. A great reward was promised to those who succeeded.<ref>{{Cite|WANTED: TRI FORCE HEROES!! Do you have pointed ears, truly epic sideburns, and side-parted hair? If so, YOU may be a Tri-Force Hero! Our kingdom needs such heroes to defeat the evil witch and lift the curse placed on Princess Styla. Whoever succeeds in this most noble of tasks will be rewarded to the fullest extent of my extensive power. So sayeth King Tuft, ruler of Hytopia!|Sign|TFH}}</ref>

Many would-be heroes came and went, until {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} arrived in the kingdom and was later drafted into its {{Term|TFH|Witch-Hunting Brigade|link}}. Paired with other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}, they eventually clear the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}} {{Term|TFH|Area|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}} together; following which {{Term|TFH|Link}} is summoned by {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}}. He says that he is impressed by {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s achievements and believes him to be one of the legendary {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero}}.<ref>{{Cite|Naturally, I believe YOU to be the hero of legend, destined to lead our fair kingdom to eternal greatness!|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|TFH|Link}} to continue to conquer the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}} and best all of its {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} to become a true {{Term|TFH|Tri Force Hero}}.<ref>{{Cite|As such, I must earnestly encourage you to best all Drablands challenges and become a true Tri Force Hero!|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref>

Whenever {{Term|TFH|Link}} completes all of the {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge}} of a given {{Term|TFH|Area}}, {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} will reward him with a unique, rare {{Term|TFH|Material|link}} exclusive to that {{Term|TFH|Area}} that can normally only be found through the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Ah yes, I hear you have conquered a Drablands challenge... Why didn't you say something sooner! So modest! The mark of a true hero, I say. As promised, your reward for besting the Drablands challenge of the Woodlands. Use it well!|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> After {{Term|TFH|Link}} completes all 128 challenges and clears the Completion List, {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} will reward him with a {{Term|TFH|Gold Rupee|link}} worth 300 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} for his efforts. 

After the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} defeat {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} and craft the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble|link}}, whose power can lift {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}}'s curse, {{Term|TFH|Link}} leaves for the castle to see {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} and {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}}. {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}}, having been informed by {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} and her relation with {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} prior, is assured he will not have to press serious charges on {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}}.<ref>{{Cite|So that's The Lady's Ensemble... I've heard the full story from Madame Couture. Good of her to make this for you. If not for that outfit, that witch would be facing some serious charges, not to mention a king's wrath.|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> He tells {{Term|TFH|Link}} to use the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}'s power right away. {{Term|TFH|Link}} lifts the curse from {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}}, returning her to normal. {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} is overjoyed and grateful to see his daughter returned to her former self. He holds a big celebration in town to celebrate and personally invites {{Term|TFH|Link}} as a royal decree. During the celebration, he lifts {{Term|TFH|Link}} onto his shoulders, and is then lifted by {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly|link}}, forming a {{Term|TFH|Totem}}.

After the celebration ends, {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} sends a letter to {{Term|TFH|Link}} requesting that he come visit for his promised reward. Upon visiting him, {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} gives him {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}}'s {{Term|TFH|Cursed Tights|link}} as his reward, regarding it as a collector's item. He encourages {{Term|TFH|Link}} to continue to conquer the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}}' challenges.

==Nomenclature==
{{Wp|Tufting|Tuft}} refers to a cluster of threads weaved through fabrics while knitting. It can also refer to a tuft of hair, referencing King Tuft's hairstyle.
