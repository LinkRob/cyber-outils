The '''Dojo''' is a location in {{SSBU}}.{{Name Ref|SSBU}}

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}} is a facility that is operated by several {{Plural|SSBU|Master Spirit|link}} including {{Term|SSBU|Darunia}}. The {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}} is only accessible after a {{Term|SSBU|Master Spirit}} who can operate it has been obtained.<ref>{{Cite|Darunia's location is now open!|N/A|SSBU}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}} can be accessed from its location in the {{Term|SSBU|World of Light|link}} or from the {{Term|SSBU|Activities}} section of the {{Term|SSBU|Collection}} sub-menu in the {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} menu.

{{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} who are entered into the {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}} can learn a {{Term|SSBU|Style}} from a {{Term|SSBU|Master Spirit}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Dojo 1<br/>Train primary spirits in a Dojo to increase their power and change their attributes.|Tips and Tricks|SSBU}}</ref> The {{Plural|SSBU|Style}} taught reflect qualities of their respective {{Plural|SSBU|Master Spirit}}. {{Term|SSBU|Darunia}}'s {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}} teaches the {{Term|SSBU|Boulder Style}} to {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}}, increasing {{Term|SSBU|Defense}} and {{Term|SSBU|Move Speed}} but decreasing {{Term|SSBU|Offense}} and {{Term|SSBU|Jump}}. {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} may alternatively visit a {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}} to remove any {{Plural|SSBU|Style}} taught to them.<ref>{{Cite|The Dojo 2<br/>You can train spirits in a certain style or return them back to their default state.|Tips and Tricks|SSBU}}</ref>

Once a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit}} has been entered into a {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}}, it will train over a period of time. After that period of time is up, the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit}} will possess a new {{Term|SSBU|Style}} that can be used in battles. A {{Term|SSBU|Spirit}} can also be taken out of the {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}} preemptively.<ref>{{Cite|Adventure: Canceling Dojo Training<br/>You can interrupt style training if necessary. If you enter the Dojo again, you can pick up training where you left off.|Tips and Tricks|SSBU}}</ref> Once a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit}} has been entered into a {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}}, it will require a cooldown period for it to rest,<ref>{{Cite|This spirit is currently exhausted and can't be left at a facility.|N/A|SSBU}}</ref> even if it has not learned any {{Plural|SSBU|Style}} or gained any experience while in the {{Term|SSBU|Dojo}}. In exchange for {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit Point}}, {{Term|SSBU|Darunia}} can immediately teach the {{Term|SSBU|Boulder Style}} to {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Learn Now] I can teach this spirit Boulder Style right away for [X]SP.|Darunia|SSBU}}</ref>
