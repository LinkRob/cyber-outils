==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Gold Kinstone}} are the result of a successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusion}} of two gold {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}. There are nine possible {{Term|TMC|Gold Kinstone}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusions}} to be made throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}, detailed below.
