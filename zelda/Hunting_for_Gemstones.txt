{{Term/Store|Hunting for Gemstones||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 52 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hunting for Gemstones|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Hunting for Gemstones}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given twelve minutes to defeat three {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}, without the use of {{Plural|HWAoC|Item|link}}. Two of them are {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Talus|link}}, which spawn at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. When one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Talus}} is defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Igneo Talus|link}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when all three of them are defeated. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond Circlet|link}}.
