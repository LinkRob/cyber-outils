{{Term/Store|Misko||Series, BotW}} is a recurring unseen character in the ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|Legend of Zelda]]'' series of games, debuting in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Great Bandit"/> Misko, also known as the '''Great Bandit Misko''', was an infamous pirate and fashion connoisseur who had amassed a vast collection of clothing and armor pieces that they had hid all across Hyrule, leaving riddles for people to solve and find said treasures.<ref name="Great Bandit" /><ref name="TotK">{{TotK}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Misko}} is a bandit who has left their treasure hidden across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} never meets or sees {{Term|BotW|Misko}}, and they can only be learned about through their treasure. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to talk to {{Term|BotW|Prissen|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Domidak|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}}, they will be discussing a lead on {{Term|BotW|Misko}}'s treasure,<ref>{{Cite|I've got the scoop on Misko's {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}}, and it's great! Listen up, Dak...|Prissen|BotW}}</ref> before stopping when they notice {{Term|BotW|Link}} is listening to them.<ref>{{Cite|Prissen! Hang on! Hey, buddy! What's your problem? You aren't being incredibly rude and listening to our conversation, are you?|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} persists on discussing the treasure, {{Term|BotW|Domidak}} will confirm they are looking for {{Term|BotW|Misko}}'s treasure, but he will not tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} about the lead for free.<ref>{{Cite|We're after nothing less than the {{Color|BotW Red|treasure of Misko, the Great Bandit}}! Finding it would mean incredible riches... I'm not about to give this information away for free. I'm no fool!|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} pays 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Domidak}} asks for, he will share a riddle that describes a "little twin" stepping over a "little river."<ref>{{Cite|The little twin steps over the little river. My cave rests above that river's source.|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Domidak}} explains that {{Term|BotW|Misko}} hid the location of the treasure using a riddle, and he mentions that the person he had heard the riddle from can eventually be linked to an underling of {{Term|BotW|Misko}}.<ref>{{Cite|Misko hid the location of the treasure in some kind of strange secret code. I heard this from a former dog walker of one of Misko's underling's descendant's neighbors. As solid a lead as any.|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> Learning the riddle begins the "{{Term|BotW|Misko, the Great Bandit|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

If {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack is installed, {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal|link}} will appear in the {{Term|BotW|Outpost Ruins|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} was written by {{Term|BotW|Misko}} to note down the locations of eight {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing priceless relics that they stole from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the eight priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> Obtaining the stolen relics is the goal of four {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest}}: "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Ancient Mask|link}}," "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes|link}}," "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Twilight Relic|link}}," and "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Phantasma|link}}."

If {{TCB}} DLC Pack is installed, {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2|link}} will appear in the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins|link}}. In this {{Term|BotW|Book}}, {{Term|BotW|Misko}} wrote down clues to where they had hidden six more {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing priceless relics that they had stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the six priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> Retrieving the relics that {{Term|BotW|Misko}} stole is the goal of four {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest}}: "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Merchant Hood|link}}," "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Garb of Winds|link}}," "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Usurper King|link}}," and "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Dark Armor|link}}."

==={{TotK|-}}===
Like the previous game, Misko was a bandit who stole rare clothes in the name of fashion. They spent a lifetime collecting such rare clothes and hid them in {{Plural|TotK|Cave|link}} all across Hyrule.<ref name="Meeshy">{{Cite|There was once a <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">legendary bandit</font> by the name of <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Misko</font>. But thievery isn't what made them a legend... It was their unparalleled fashion sense! It's said that Misko collected rare outfits from all over the world and hid their pieces throughout Hyrule.|Meeshy|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|<font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Misko</font>'s the name of an infamous bandit who caused problems all over Hyrule back in the day. But Misko's legend expands beyond mere banditry. You see, Misko is equally famous for fashion. The stories say that they spent a lifetime <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">collecting clothes</font>—clothes that are now hidden across Hyrule.|Domidak|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|To you who have found my treasure, these ember trousers are but one piece of my grand collection. I have hidden an assortment of equipment across Hyrule. Notable pieces obtained in strange lands near and far.|Bottled Letter|TotK}}</ref> Misko's appearance remains unknown and Link never meets them. They are often mentioned by travelers on the main road, often giving hints to any of Misko's treasure located nearby. Misko's treasure are often found inside caves, marked with poles covered with ragged pieces of cloth and adorned on top with a brass hollowed triangle.

During {{Term|TotK|Misko's Cave of Chests|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} finds a message-in-a-{{Term|TotK|bottle|link}} inside a chest. It was written by Misko and it reads that Misko not only hidden rare clothes, but another unique treasure—the {{Term|TotK|Fierce Deity Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You've done well to find this one, and I will reward you with a hint to another—the Fierce Deity Sword.|Bottled Letter|TotK}}</ref> The sword is hidden in the place Link is present at and needs the full set of the {{Term|TotK|Fierce Deity Set|link}} in order to claim it.<ref>{{Cite|Don the form of the Fierce Deity, and return to this place. There are three keys you will need.|Bottled Letter|TotK}}</ref>
