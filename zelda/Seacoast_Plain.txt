'''Seacoast Plain''' is a location in {{ZA}}. It is a large coast to the southeast of [[Tolemac]]. It is home to a small harbor called '''South Port''' in the coast's south.

==Features and overview==
Seacoast Plain is a vast coastal region of [[Tolemac]], located in the far southeast of the land. It can be accessed by multiple routes; either by taking the mountain road through the [[Great Dry Canyon]], by taking a cave shortcut from the [[Plain of Andor]], or alternately by taking a longer detour through the [[Vendoss]] swamp or south from the [[South Gubasha Desert]]. The Plain is a rocky seashore that stretches from the south and to the east, and where the mainland meets the sea of Tolemac. The eastern shore is merged with the dense, wooded marshland of the Vendoss swamp, where entry to the swamp's village can be found. It is on this side of the coast that a [[Beach Merchant|merchant]] can be found selling a [[Candle]] for 100 [[Rupee]]s and a [[Life Potion]] for 200 Rupees. A shipwreck can also be found nearby left over from a storm, where the merchant had found the items he sells.<ref>{{Cite|Did you see the shipwreck up the beach? It happened many storms ago, and I've collected some of the things from the wreck here. I will sell them to you if you wish.|Beach Merchant|ZA}}</ref> West of the [[Great Dry Canyon]] entrance is a seaside house, where a man and a woman live. The latter seems to speak in an indiscernible language.

Along the shore is a cave entrance to a flooded cavern, containing a [[Heart Container|Crystal Heart]] on a ledge. A [[Switch]] must be hit to make a wooden bridge appear to the Crystal Heart, but it is at an angle and requires a [[Spell]] that fires projectiles diagonally to hit.

===South Port===
To the far south of the coast is a small harbor town called '''South Port'''.<ref>{{Cite|South Port is definitely the place to come if you need to set to sea.|Seafarer (House)|ZA}}</ref> The town is home to many busy seafarers. A [[Seafarer|sailor]] in the town lodge claims that they have been to sea for so long, their "brains are scrambled."<ref>{{Cite|Imsouri! You're a small thing to be lost in a rough place like this! You won't learn anything from any of these; they've been to sea too long and their brains are scrambled!|Seafarer in Lodge|ZA}}</ref> [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can take [[Raft]] here to sail to the [[Sea Island]] in the east, at the cost of 20 Rupees. However, because the offshore wind has died down, she must also provide her own with the [[Vial of Winds]].<ref>{{Cite|If you wish to cross the sea, bring us twenty Rubies and a Vial of Wind. The offshore wind has died down, and you must bring your own breeze.|Rafter|ZA}}</ref> While sailing to the island, Zelda encounters a [[Sea Monster]] and must defeat it to proceed.
