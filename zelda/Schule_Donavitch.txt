{{Term/Store|Schule Donavitch||Series, LA, LADX}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|LADX|Schule Donavitch}} was also referred to as the '''Art Alligator''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 46}}</ref> However, because {{E}} takes precedence, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
Schule Donavitch is an artist who lives in the [[Animal Village]], where he can be found in his studio painting the portrait of the [[Hippo Model]].<ref>{{Cite|Leave me alone! I'm trying to sit still so Schule can paint my portrait!|Hippo Model|LADX}}</ref> He is also the sculptor of "[[The Mourning Mermaid]]" found near [[Martha's Bay]],<ref>{{Cite|THE MOURNING MERMAID By SCHULE ? ...A scale is missing...|Mermaid Statue|LADX}}</ref> but admits that it is unfinished.<ref>{{Cite|Ya, I am Schule Donavitch! Zee mermaid statue by zee bay iz my masterpiece! ... To tell you zee truth, zis werk iz not complete! Zee art, it'z... difficult for you to grasp, iz it not?|Schule Donavitch|LADX}}</ref> [[Martha]] the Mermaid mentions that she had posed for Schule's sculpture, and also claims that he had asked for her [[Scale]].<ref>{{Cite|An artist once asked me to pose for him, and he wanted a scale, too... Can the legend of the Magnifying Lens be true...?|Martha|LADX}}</ref> If Link obtains the Scale and shows it to Schule, he will tell him that he is too preoccupied with his latest work and asks that he add the Scale to his sculpture in his stead.<ref>{{Cite|Iz zat zee Mermaid scale? I can't use it now. I have to werk on zis drawing. You should go finish zee mermaid statue for me.|Schule Donavitch|LADX}}</ref> If Link has obtained the [[Magnifying Lens]] and speaks with Schule, he asks what he is looking at with the Lens and demands that he stop.<ref>{{Cite|Ach! Vat are you looking at vith zat magnifying lens? Stop it at vonce!|Schule Donavitch|LADX}}</ref>

Schule Donavitch's brother is [[Sale]], the owner of [[Sale's House O' Bananas]] on [[Toronbo Shores]].<ref name="Brother"/>

==Trivia==
*Schule speaks with a thick accent that appears to be either German or Russian. "Ach!" is a very stereotypically German thing to say. His surname, ending is -vitch, is Slavic in origin.
*In the German version of {{LA|-}}, Sale mentions that his brother is collecting old toothbrushes.<ref>{{Cite|{{Exp|Secondarily, I am collecting canned food of all kinds. Well, a strange hobby... I agree! But my brother collects old toothbrushes... This probably lies in the genes...|Nebenbei sammle ich Dosenfutter aller Art. Naja, ein seltsames Hobby... Stimmt! Aber mein Bruder sammelt alte Zahnbürsten... Das liegt wohl an den Genen...}}|Sale|LA}}</ref>
*In the French version of {{LA|-}}, Sale mentions that Schule Donavitch is the younger brother.<ref>{{Cite|{{Exp|My younger brother is an artist.|Mon frère cadet est un artiste.}}|Sale|LA}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Schule's German name, ''Kroko Dilius'', is a pun, as the pronunciation is equal to ''{{Exp|Krokodil is German for crocodile|Krokodilius}}''. In the French version, his name is Sven Donavitch, while his signature name on "The Mourning Mermaid" is Alligodali, which is a cross between the word "Alligator" and the name of the famous artist, {{Wp|Salvador Dalí}}.
