{{Term/Store|Medal|Medals|Series|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, each Medal gives [[Link]] various enhancements when equipped in the [[Pouch#Skyward Sword|Adventure Pouch]]. They are usually found in [[Treasure Chest#Goddess Cube Chest|Goddess Cube Chests]], or can be purchased from [[Beedle#Skyward Sword|Beedle]] after upgrading the [[Wallet]]. Medals either passively boost Link's abilities or alter the results of things appearing in the [[Overworld]]. They consume one space in the Adventure Pouch as a result.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, each {{Term|HWDE|Medal}} is awarded as an achievement for meeting the requirements of a particular challenge. {{Plural|HWDE|Medal}} come in three categories: bronze, silver, and gold. The categories for these {{Plural|HWDE|Medal}} usually serve as an indication for the difficulty in which it takes to accomplish the required tasks. Certain {{Plural|HWDE|Medal}} are only attainable through paid DLC, such as those requiring {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} to complete certain Maps in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}. Collecting {{Plural|HWDE|Medal}} does not result in anything, and collecting all of them only awards in the final {{Term|HWDE|Medal}}, the {{Term|HWDE|True Hero Medal|link}}. The {{Plural|HWDE|Medal}} earned are displayed in the {{Term|HWDE|Medal List|link}}.

Before the [[Miiverse]] service ended, a post would be automatically generated to [[Miiverse]] when {{Term|HWDE|Medal}} is obtained, provided that the option to do so was not turned off in the settings. The Miiverse post would simply announce that the {{Term|HWDE|Medal}} has been obtained, allowing others to comment on the achievement.

In {{HW|L}}, all but {{Exp|Limit Breaker, Limitless Ambition, Pixel Perfect, Ultimate Ambition, Boss Hunter, Ganon Warriors, Challenge Master and True Hero|eight medals}} from {{HW|-}} are available in the base game and new {{Plural|HWDE|Medal}} were added that are attained by using the new {{Term|HWDE|My Fairy|link}} feature. The fulfillment conditions of {{Exp|Hundred Years Ahead, Bug Catcher, Know-it-all and Big Bro|four medals}} were changed and the in-game order of medals was slightly re-arranged. Following the release of paid DLC, new medals exclusive to the DLC as well as some medals from {{HW|-}} not in the base game were added.
