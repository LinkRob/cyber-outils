The {{Term/Store|Cutlass||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Tetra]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with [[Element|Water]].

==Variations==
The default Cutlass is the '''Pirate Cutlass'''. The second form of the Cutlass is the '''Jeweled Cutlass''', followed by the '''Regal Cutlass'''.

In {{HW|L}} only, the fourth form of the Cutlass is the '''Cutlass of Light''', which is associated with both the Elements of Water and {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Light}}.

==== Special Attack====
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Tetra fires her pistol, then performs two quick slashes with her Cutlass in a cross-pattern. She finishes her attack with a gunshot, which sends her enemies flying. This attack hits enemies immediately in front of Tetra.
