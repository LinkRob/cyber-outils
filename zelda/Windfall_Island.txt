{{Term/Store|Windfall Island||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (B4) is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 111 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>{{Fact}} It is a market town in the [[Great Sea]], and is known for having the highest population of all the islands in the game. People from all over the world come to Windfall Island, and as such, the town is always lively.<ref>{{Cite|Our Windfall Island may be a little cozy, so to speak, but people from all over the world come here. The town's always lively.|Anton|TWW}}</ref> Many sailors have found their way here, although their ships are never around.

[[File:Windfall Bridge.jpg|thumb|right|The Windfall Gate, one of Windfall Island's most famous features]]
To avoid the [[Great Flood]], the surviving populations of [[Hyrule Castle Town]] and [[Kakariko Village]] fled to what would become Windfall Island and founded the community there.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 59}}</ref>

There are multiple places of interest on the island, including a [[Chu Jelly Juice Shop|Potion Shop]], [[Bomb Shop#The Wind Waker|Bomb Shop]], a lighthouse and windmill, [[School of Joy|school]], a [[Cafe Bar]], [[Auction House]], and a [[Town Jail]]. The huge gate at the entrance of town is known to be one of Windfall's most famous features.<ref>{{Cite|That huge gate there at the entrance to town is one of Windfall Island's most famous features. No matter how much I see it, I never get tired of it, you know?|Kane|TWW}}</ref>

[[Link]] first encounters Windfall Island after being cast out of the [[Forsaken Fortress]] and rescued by the [[King of Red Lions]]. It soon becomes apparent that the entity at the fortress has taken its toll on the island in its search for young, pointy-eared girls.

The King of Red Lions immediately makes it clear that he needs a [[Sail]] in order to travel the seas.<ref>{{Cite|This is actually a bit embarrassing for me to admit, but...although I am indeed a boat that possesses the power of speech... I possess no sail. And a boat with no sail can sail no seas.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> Link first encounters the strange [[Zunari]], who offers him a sail for 80 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|But dear me! As I traveled, a terrible storm descended and tore my ship to bits...yet mysteriously, only that escaped without so much as a mark on it... [...] How does...80 Rupees sound?|Zunari|TWW}}</ref> The Zunari-induced [[Island Trading Sequence]] in the game is central to this point, where Zunari eventually "retails" his flowers.

Here, [[Link]] gets to meet many interesting characters and get involved in numerous sidequests, such as that of [[Mrs. Marie]]'s [[Joy Pendant]] obsession and the [[Picto Box]] quest. There are many things to do and see here, so Link visits Windfall Island quite often, both for plot-related events and to simply help out the citizens of the town.

====Bomb Shop====
{{Main|Bomb Shop#The Wind Waker}}
[[File:Inside Bomb Shop WW.jpg|thumb|right|The inside of Bomb-Master Cannon's Bomb Shop]]

Owned by [[Bomb-Master Cannon]], the last bomb-maker left in the entire sea,<ref>{{Cite|My ancestors been making bombs for generations. Uh-huh. Now, seems like I'm the only person left in the world who still works in the bomb industry...|Bomb-Master Cannon|TWW}}</ref> the Bomb Shop in Windfall Island is separated from the rest of the shops of Windfall. At first, the bombs here are too expensive for Link to even be able to afford. It's only after [[Tetra]] and her crew of pirates tie Bomb-Master Cannon up and steal his bombs that he lowers the price.

====Town Jail====
{{Main|Town Jail}}
There is a prison cell located near the middle of the town in which [[Tingle]] is imprisoned. Link can press a [[Switch]] hidden behind a cluster of [[Jar]]s to free Tingle, who will thank him by giving him the [[Tingle Tuner]] and [[Special Chart#Tingle's Chart|a map]] with the location of [[Tingle Island|his Island]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Mr. Fairy! You want to come to my island, don't you? Don't you? Don't you? When you go out to sea, open it!|Tingle|TWW}}</ref> Once Tingle is freed, Link can push the box that's against the wall out of the way to reveal a small hole through which he can crawl and eventually find the Picto Box.

====Chu Jelly Juice Shop==== 
{{Main|Chu Jelly Juice Shop}}
Located next to the mansion, the Chu Jelly Juice Shop specializes in making potions out of [[Chu Jelly]]. Link can bring 15 of each Chu Jelly to either get potions for free or allow [[Doc Bandam]], the owner of the shop, to make new kinds of potions.

====Zunari's Shop====
{{Main|Zunari's Shop}}
A small shop owned by [[Zunari]], it starts out selling nothing but a [[sail]]. Link can help his business grow by trading Zunari's [[decorations]] with the [[Goron#The Wind Waker|Traveling Merchants]] around the [[Great Sea]]. See [[Island Trading Sequence]] for more information.

====Cafe Bar====
{{Main|Cafe Bar}}
One of the most famous hangout places of Windfall, the Cafe Bar is located next to [[Zunari's Shop]] and is owned by [[Gillian]]. The Cafe Bar is not crucial to Link's quest, but he is required to visit it during various sidequests.

====Lighthouse====
[[File:Lighthouse.png|thumb|right|Windfall Lighthouse after being reactivated]]
Prior to the events of {{TWW|-}}, Windfall's lighthouse went dark for unknown reasons, and it has remained that way ever since.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder how long it's been since the light in Windfall's lighthouse went dark...|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> Below it is a type of ferris-wheel that allows anyone to climb up to the lighthouse to get a better view. Before the lighthouse malfunctioned, it would send a bright shaft of light into the darkness to serve as a safety beacon.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, it used to send a bright shaft of light onto the night sea_sort of a safety beacon. ...But that was quite some time ago.|Kreeb|TWW}}</ref> The illumination device still works, however, and it continues to spin around despite that its fire has extinguished.<ref>{{Cite|Even now, what's left of that lighthouse's illumination device still spins around up top, all night, every night. ...But its fire remains extinguished.|Kreeb|TWW}}</ref> Link can spark up the lighthouse by getting on top of the ferris wheel and shooting a [[Fire Arrow]] inside into the device, allowing it shine its grand ray of light. [[Kreeb]] will reward Link with Piece of Heart for fixing the lighthouse.

====School of Joy====
{{Main|School of Joy}}
[[Mrs. Marie]] is the town's teacher and has her own school near the lighthouse called the [[School of Joy]].<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Mrs. Marie's School of Joy!|Mrs. Marie|TWW}}</ref> When Link talks to her, she explains of the problem she's had lately with a group of students known as the [[Killer Bees]].<ref>{{Cite|There's this delinquent gang of four young boys who never listen to a word I say. Even though I wait patiently for those boys to show up, they never come. It's as if they don't want joy in their hearts!|Mrs. Marie|TWW}}</ref> Link can help Mrs. Marie with the little troublemakers to earn several items that will become useful later on in his quest.

====Other Notable Locations====
Other places of interest include [[Lenzo]]'s house, where Link gets to know the famous photographer Lenzo as well as his vast gallery of [[Pictograph]]s. There is also an enormous mansion called the [[Auction House]] that is home to [[Mila]] and [[Mila's Father|her father]],<ref>{{Cite|House of Wealth|Tingle|TWW}}</ref> but later on [[Maggie (The Wind Waker)|Maggie]] and [[Maggie's Father|her father]] take ownership of it. The mansion is the location of the nightly [[Windfall Auction]]. Link can also play the [[Squid-Hunt]] minigame in the lighthouse in front of [[Mrs. Marie|Mrs. Marie's]] school to win two Pieces of Heart.

==Nomenclature==
"Windfall" is a word meaning great, typically unexpected, financial gain, which fits the island's status as a thriving town of merchants as well as fitting the game's theme of wind.
