The {{Term/Store|Drawing Commission||Series, PH}} is a group in {{PH}}.<ref name="Win">{{Cite|Congratulations! You WON the drawing! Thanks for entering! Here's your prize!|Drawing Commission|PH}}</ref> Once [[Link]] has helped the [[Man of Smiles]] and gotten the [[Hero's New Clothes]] from him, he will receive a [[Prize Postcard]] every time he helps him after that point. If Link puts one of the postcards in a {{Term|PH|Postbox|link}} he will participate in a drawing contest and get a reply from the Drawing Commission after 12:00 a.m. from a postbox. He will either get a letter that says that he won or that he lost.<ref name="Win"/><ref>{{Cite|Congratulations! You DIDN'T win the drawing! Too bad. Really! Don't get all upset over something so trivial! You lost. That's all. It's only a drawing. Not a big deal. I mean, what's a big deal REALLY? How about life? Big deal...or not? How about pestering you about big deals? Big deal...or not? Well, we've dragged this on far too long, haven't we? Thanks for entering. Enjoy this loser prize.|Drawing Commission|PH}}</ref> Normally, the hero would aim for winning the contest, but if Link wins, he will simply get congratulated without any physical prize.<ref>{{Cite|...... Hmm? That's it? That's weird. It said you won something, but there's nothing inside! Well, whaddya gonna do... Did my deal, so I guess I'll be on my way!|Postman|PH}}</ref> But if he loses, he will get a consolation prize, which always is a [[Ship Parts|Ship Part]].
