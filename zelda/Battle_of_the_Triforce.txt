"{{Term/Store|Battle of the Triforce||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}}" is the third {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} of the {{Term|HWDE|Ganondorf (Arc)|link}} arc of {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode|link}} in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HWDE}} It takes place in {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Field (Stage)|link}}.

==Overview==
Once Ganondorf seizes the [[Triforce of Power]] from [[Lana]] during "[[March of the Demon King]]", his Forces travel to {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Field (Stage)|link}} where Link and Zelda are located to claim their pieces and complete the full [[Triforce]].<ref>{{Cite|Ganondorf's forces defeated the Hyrulean army and wrenched the Triforce of Power from Lana's grasp. Yet, the other two pieces remained with Link and Zelda in Hyrule Castle. If Ganondorf could take the castle and complete the Triforce, Hyrule would be at his mercy. He readied his army for a siege. Meanwhile, Zelda's forces stood ready to defend their home at all costs. Link stood at the head of the army, the shining Triforce of Courage inspiring his troops. This battle would decide Hyrule's fate. Would the light endure...or would darkness spread to every corner of the land? The next moments would decide.|N/A|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If I crush the hero and the princess, then the completed Triforce will be mine for the taking!|Ganondorf|HW}}</ref> Meanwhile, Zelda launches a counterattack, ordering her troops to take the base of Ganondorf's Forces.<ref>{{Cite|This castle is Hyrule's last line of defense... If it falls, so too will our hope! We must capture Ganondorf's base if we want to stand any chance of victory!|Zelda|HW}}</ref> After the battle begins, [[Zant]] advances towards the Rockface Checkpoint in the northwest section of the battlefield.<ref>{{Cite|I'll force open the gate and secure a route for our advance.|Zant|HW}}</ref> In order to proceed to Hyrule Castle, Ganondorf and his forces must support Zant's attack on the Checkpoint's gate. To intercept Zant, Zelda commands a Hylian Captain to subdue him and protect the gate.<ref>{{Cite|The gate is under attack! Command your troops to subdue Zant first!|Zelda|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Hyrulean princes has already figured out our plan! We must not underestimate her...|Zant|HW}}</ref> Once the Hylian Captain is defeated, the Rockface Checkpoint will open, allowing Ganondorf's forces to head directly towards Hyrule Castle.

During this time, Link begins to advance towards the Allied Base, attempting to capture the Central Keep along his way. If Link is not interrupted, the young hero will use the power from his Triforce of Courage to dwindle the Keeps' health with a rain of light while he battles the forces within it. Once Link is defeated, Zelda will command him to withdraw, allowing Ganondorf's Forces to continue their attack.<ref>{{Cite|Link! Do not wear yourself thin! Pull back and reserve your strength!|Zelda|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So you put all your faith in this hero and then worry he'll get hurt? How touching...and pitiful.|Ganondorf|HW}}</ref>

After the Rockface Checkpoint is opened, [[Impa]] will advance east to the [[Fairy Fountain]] from the Hyrule Castle Checkpoint.<ref>{{Cite|Things are not going well... I must do something! With the Great Fairy's assistance, we can win this battle!|Impa|HW}}</ref> In response, [[Ghirahim]] challenges her to battle,<ref>{{Cite|Allow me to put an end to their fantasies of victory.|Ghirahim|HW}}</ref> buying time for the rest of Ganondorf's Forces. However, after a brief period of time, Impa decides to abandon her fight with Ghirahim to reach the Fairy Fountain.<ref>{{Cite|We're getting nowhere... Leave Ghirahim for now! Make the fountain your top priority!|Impa|HW}}</ref> If Impa reaches the Fairy Fountain, she will begin charging it, forcing Ganondorf's Forces to stop her within two minutes or risk failure.<ref>{{Cite|If we pool our magic power, the Enemy Base will fall.|Impa|HW}}</ref>

Following Impa's defeat, Link will reappear in the field north of the Castle Keep and advance east towards the Central Keep in order to capture the Allied Base.<ref>{{Cite|C'mon, Link! We have to win this fight!|Proxi|HW}}</ref> Zelda will join the young hero, opening the gates to the Castle Keep and advancing towards the East Field Keep before heading for the Allied Base.<ref>{{Cite|Link has returned--we have a window of opportunity! Our forces must strike at once!|Zelda|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How kind of the hero and the princess to come right to me... It makes my task much simpler!|Ganondorf|HW}}</ref> Both Warriors will use their pieces of the Triforce to conquer their respective Keeps, prompting Ganondorf's Forces to defeat them immediately. Zant offers to handle Link while Ghirahim attacks Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|Allow me to stop the child.|Zant|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Leave the princess to me.|Ghirahim|HW}}</ref> However, they are unable to defeat the Triforce bearers on their own and require Ganondorf's assistance. In order to conclusively conquer Hyrule Field, Ganondorf must defeat both Link and Zelda within a small window of time of each other's defeat, as they will simply rest and return to resisting Ganondorf's Forces after a period of time has passed.<ref>{{Cite|The princess is giving it all she's got, Link! We have to fight too!|Proxi|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Link is risking his life... I refuse to sit and do nothing!|Zelda|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Stubborn fools... It looks like this won't end unless I defeat them both...|Ganondorf|HW}}</ref>

After the Hyrulean Forces have been defeated, Ganondorf will assume control over all three pieces of the Triforce before disappearing.<ref>{{Cite|At last... All three pieces... Heh... HA HA HA HA!|Ganondorf|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Seeing the battle was won, Ganondorf vanished from sight.|N/A|HW}}</ref>
