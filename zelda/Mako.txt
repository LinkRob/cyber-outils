{{Term/Store|Mako||Series, TWW, TWWHD, PH}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258{{Exp Game|TWW}} & 283{{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref> He is a member of [[Tetra's Pirate Crew]]. He has a rather limited role as he rarely ever speaks.<ref>{{Cite|Mako is pretty quiet... Mako immediately gets mad whenever I try to take his glasses...so I do that a lot. Hee hee!|Aryll|TWW}}</ref>

==Biography==
When [[Link]] goes to [[Windfall Island]] to find out what the pirates are up to, he overhears Mako asking [[Gonzo]] the password to enter [[Tetra's Ship]] since he seems to have forgotten.<ref>{{Cite|Say there, brother, what was today's password, again?|Mako|TWW}}</ref> Gonzo is forced to tell him the password again, which is how Link overhears it and is able to go inside the pirate ship and obtain [[Bomb]]s from [[Niko]].

Mako makes a final appearance in {{TWW}} during the game's ending sequence. After Link and [[Tetra]] resurface from the sunken [[Hyrule Castle]], Mako, along with the rest of the pirate crew and a few other characters, greet the pair aboard their pirate ship. Gonzo is then seen steering the ship as Link, Tetra, and the gang of pirates set sail from Outset Island in search of new lands. 

Like the rest of the pirates, Mako bears a striking resemblance to a man portrayed in a [[:File:TWW Portrait.jpg|wall painting]] hanging in the sunken Hyrule Castle, which heavily hints that the man could be Mako's ancestor serving under the orders of a past [[Princess Zelda]]. 

In {{PH}}, Mako appears again during both the introduction and the ending sequences of the game, and his role is once again very limited. He is last seen when Tetra and Link reunite with the rest of the pirate crew after waking up on top of {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}.
