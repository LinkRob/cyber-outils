{{Term/Store|Guardian Gear|Guardian Gear|Series, BotW}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|Series|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Gear}} are a set of weaponry wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} found in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref name="Guardian Sword">{{Cite|{{Big|Guardian Sword}}<br/>A sword often wielded by Guardian Scouts. Its blue energy blade is a product of ancient technology. It's not very durable.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Ancient Battle Axe">{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Battle Axe}}<br/>A weapon used by Guardian Scouts. Its unique blade was forged using ancient technology. Although powerful, its unusual shape causes it to break easily.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Guardian Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Guardian Spear}}<br/>Wielded by Guardian Scouts, this spear has a high piercing power and is a testament to the Sheikah's high level of technology. the spearhead appears only when brandished.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> All pieces of {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear}} have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn after scanning the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. During "A Minor Test of Strength" trials, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will encounter {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}}, wielding basic {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Gear}}. In "A Moderate Test of Strength" trials, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}} will wield two intermediate pieces of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Gear}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}} wielding three advanced pieces of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Gear}} are found during "A Major Test of Strength" trials.

{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Gear}} were made in ancient times with the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} tribe.<ref name="Guardian Sword"/><ref name="Ancient Battle Axe"/><ref name="Guardian Spear"/><ref name="Ancient Battle Axe++">{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Battle Axe++}}<br/>This ancient battle axe's damage output is scaled up to peak performance. Ancient technology makes it possible to enhance cutting power beyond metal weapons' limits.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Guardian Shield">{{Cite|{{Big|Guardian Shield}}<br/>A shield made with ancient Sheikah technology. It can deflect a Guardian Scout's beam.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> At their highest level, they are known to surpass the abilities of their metallic counterparts.<ref name="Ancient Battle Axe++"/><ref name="Guardian Shield++">{{Cite|{{Big|Guardian Shield++}}<br/>The output level of this shield has been boosted to maximum. Its combat capabilities surpass those of metallic shields, and it can deflect Guardian Scout beams.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield}} are known to be able to deflect the beams of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}}.<ref name="Guardian Shield"/><ref name="Guardian Shield++"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Gear}} are made out of non-metallic materials that do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and they cannot be manipulated using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. They will float in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They are not combustible, and therefore they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
