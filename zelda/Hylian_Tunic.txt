==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Hylian Tunics are pieces of [[Armor]] that can be purchased at {{Term|BotW|Enchanted|link}} in [[Kakariko Village]] and at the [[Ventest Clothing Boutique]] in [[Hateno Village]]. They can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Hylian Tunic, alongside the [[Hylian Hood]] and [[Hylian Trousers]], compose the [[Hylian Set]]. The Tunic itself is composed of a cuirass, a spaulder, and a pair of gloves and bracers, all made from leather.
