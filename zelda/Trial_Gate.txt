{{Term/Store|Trial Gate|Trial Gates|Series, SS|plural}} are portals that allow access to the [[Silent Realm]]s in each of the three regions in [[The Surface]] in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|A report, Master Link. I have detected the aura of a Trial Gate nearby.|Fi|SS}}</ref> The location of the gates are usually given off by the faint bloom and the [[Blessed Butterfly|Blessed Butterflies]] that float over the gate.

==Location and Uses==
After Link visits the [[Isle of Songs]] for the first time and learns "[[Farore's Courage]]", [[Fi]] will activate the [[Dowsing]] ability to search for the first Trial Gate to the Farore Silent Realm.<ref>{{Cite|I have committed Farore's Courage to memory. Now you can use your {{Color|SS Red|dowsing}} ability to search for the {{Color|SS Blue|gate}} leading to the first trial you must face.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Once Link locates the gate, he must take out the [[Goddess's Harp]] and play at the {{Wp|tempo}} indicated by the pulsating circles of light given off by the gate mark. If the young hero is playing at the right tempo, [[Fi]] will join Link by providing the vocals, and petals will begin to grow from the Trial Gate. Once petals completely surround it, the Trial Gate will be fully activated, at which point Link must thrust the [[Goddess Sword]] in the center to be transported to the corresponding Silent Realm.<ref>{{Cite|Something here is reacting to our performance of Farore's Courage. I have confirmed the appearance of a strange mark on the ground. I calculate a 90% possibility that this mark is a Trial Gate, as mentioned by the goddess statue we heard from on the Isle of Songs. Thrust your sword into the center of the mark on the ground before you.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

[[File:Trial Gate 3.png|thumb|right|250px|A fully-activated Trial Gate]]
Every time Link returns to the Isle of Songs to learn a new melody, he will be able to Dowse for the next Silent Realm. This is true except for the last trial, in which earning the "[[Song of the Hero]]" from the three dragons is necessary to search for it.
