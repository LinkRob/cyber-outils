The {{Term/Store|''History of the Zora, Part Four''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|History of the Zora, Part Four<br/>The Lightscale Trident<br/>As told by King Dorephan|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|History of the Zora, Part Four}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}} that can be found on one of the riverbanks south of {{Term|BotW|Luto's Crossing|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must read it to complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}".

This {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument}} describes the history of the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}}. The spear was made by {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}} as a gift for {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} to celebrate her birth.<ref>{{Cite|The celebrate her birth, the smithy Dento presented Mipha with a gift: a mighty spear called the Lightscale Trident.|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} grew, she began to receive lessons from the royal {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} guard, particularly from {{Term|BotW|Seggin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mipha grew into a bright girl and soon reached the age of receiving lessons from the royal family's order of knights. The whole of the royal guard adored her, especially Sergeant Seggin, who loved her as if she were his own kin. Under Seggin's instruction, Mipha honed her skills, and her radiance grew along with her skill with the Lightscale Trident.|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref>

Following the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, when {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} did not return, the people of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} fell into a depression.<ref>{{Cite|As a Champion, Mipha made her people proud. However, once the Great Calamity stuck, she was never to return. All of Zora's Domain fell into misery. The merest thought of the princess was enough to overcome anyone with tears.|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref> To offer her soul rest, they tried to send the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} down {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As a way of offering her soul repose, they tried to send the Lightscale Trident drifting down the Zora River.|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref> However, it began to glow, and the people heard {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} telling them she is one with the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} and to remember her.<ref>{{Cite|But when they did, the trident began to glow, and Mipha's voice rang loud and clear for all Zora to hear: "The Lightscale Trident and I are one... Abandon your grief and know joy once again. Do not cry...just remember."|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref> Since then, the day of the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}} is celebrated as the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|link}}, in honor of {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}.<ref>{{Cite|And so, keeping to her request, on the day of the Great Calamity...the day that Mipha passed from this world... the Zora venerate the Lightscale Trident and remember their brave princess. Such is the origin of the Champion Fesitval.|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref>
