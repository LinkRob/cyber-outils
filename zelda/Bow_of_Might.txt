The '''Bow of Might''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
{{Manual|EoW|Bow of Might|A mysterious bow that can pierce through the monsters of the rifts.<ref name="Bow of Might">{{Cite|A mysterious bow that can pierce through the monsters of the rifts.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The [[Bow]] of Might can be upgraded to [[Bow of Might Lv. 2|Lv. 2]] by using 10 [[Might Crystal|Might Crystals]] at [[Lueburry|Lueburry’s]] House, significantly increasing its damage. It can be further upgraded to [[Bow of Might Lv. 3|LV. 3]] with an additional 20 Might Crystals, enhancing its damage even more.
