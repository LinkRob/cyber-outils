{{Term/Store|Blazing Watch Tower|Blazing Watch Towers|Series, FS|plural}},{{Exp Game|FS}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|game=ALttP&FS|publisher=Prima|page=176}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Face Lamp|Face Lamps|LA, LADX|plural}},{{Exp Game|LA}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 101}}</ref> are recurring traps in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|LANS, OoS, OoA, PH, FSA}} {{Plural|Series|Face Lamp}} go unnamed in {{TMC}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|Series|Face Lamp}} are {{Plural|Series|Torch|link}} that launch fireballs at intruders, similar to {{Plural|Series|Stone Statue|link}}. Unlike {{Plural|Series|Stone Statue}}, they will not start firing fireballs at intruders immediately. Instead, they react to enemies appearing, or in some cases, being defeated. The only means to stop the fireballs is to defeat the enemies in the room.

==Nomenclature==
Face Lamps are named for the faces on the lamps that appear in {{LA}}, though in other games they appear simply as regular torches.
