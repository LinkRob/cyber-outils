{{Term/Store|Mei||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mei|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mei|link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} woman who resides in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. However, when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, she will be absent. Her job is catching {{Plural|BotW|Fish|link}}, which she then sells at the {{Term|BotW|Coral Reef|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|She spends her days catching fish and selling them at the general store.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mei}} is the wife of {{Term|BotW|Fronk|link}} and the mother of {{Term|BotW|Tumbo|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Keye|link}}.<ref name="Mei"/> {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} describes {{Term|BotW|Mei}} as lively and cheerful, a trait he believes she passed down to {{Term|BotW|Tumbo}}.<ref>{{Cite|Unlike me, she is very lively and cheerful... I suppose Tumbo gets that from her.|Fronk|Botw}}</ref>

On the day {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} started its rampage, {{Term|BotW|Mei}} left {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} to catch {{Plural|BotW|Fish}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}}.<ref name="Rampage">{{Cite|After the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}} started rampaging, she went out to go fishing like everything was normal.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The day {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} started raging, she said she was going fishing in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}}.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> However, the {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} spouted caused the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|display=River}} to swell, leading {{Term|BotW|Mei}} to be swept downstream.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}'s rage caused the river to swell. She may have been swept downstream! However, if Ledo's group didn't see anything, maybe she passed through {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}} and is beyond there... Worst case scenario...she could have been carried off all the way to {{Color|BotW Red|Lake Hylia}}.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mei}} can be found on the islet to the east of {{Term|BotW|Hylia Island|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}, singing to herself as she swims.<ref>{{Cite|♪Laaa!♪|Mei|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mei}}, she will greet him.<ref>{{Cite|Good day!|Mei|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Good evening!|Mei|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hello!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> After this, she comments on the {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia}}.<ref>{{Cite|What lovely weather we're having!|Mei|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Today's weather is unfortunate, isn't it?|Mei|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what she is doing, {{Term|BotW|Mei}} explains that she is catching {{Plural|BotW|Fish}} to sell at the {{Term|BotW|Coral Reef}}, the {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] I'm catching fish! They'll sell in no time flat at the general store in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> Following this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask about {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, and {{Term|BotW|Mei}} explains that they are far away from it.<ref>{{Cite|[Zora's Domain?] Yes! We're pretty far away right now...|Mei|BotW}}</ref> However, she is having fun catching the rare {{Term|BotW|Fish}} in {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia}}.<ref>{{Cite|But there are a lot of rare fish to catch here, so it's a lot of fun!|Mei|BotW}}</ref>

After the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} worrying about {{Term|BotW|Mei}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|They say {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} has calmed down, and yet...}}|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks him what is wrong, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} confesses that he is concerned for {{Term|BotW|Mei}}, who has yet to return.<ref>{{Cite|[What's wrong?] The truth is... My wife Mei left and hasn't come back yet.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> He recounts that {{Term|BotW|Mei}} left to go fishing when {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}} began rampaging.<ref name="Rampage"/> He regrets allowing her to leave, fearing that something terrible could have happened to her.<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid the worst has happened... If only I had stopped her from leaving! Oh, what do I do?!|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers to search for {{Term|BotW|Mei}}, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} will be grateful for his help.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll find her!] Link... You saved Zora's Domain, and now you're going to help me find my Mei, as well? You are a true Champion! I am sorry to put this burden on you, but please... Please bring her home!|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> After this, the "{{Term|BotW|A Wife Washed Away|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

In {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia}}, {{Term|BotW|Mei}} will be singing about how big her catch is.<ref>{{Cite|♪Big catch, big catch...♪|Mei|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her, she mentions she has caught a lot of {{Term|BotW|Fish}} today.<ref>{{Cite|We got a big haul today! Fishing is so much fun!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> Following this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Mei}} if she knows {{Term|BotW|Fronk}}, to which she questions if something is wrong with him.<ref>{{Cite|[Do you know Fronk?] Hm? Fronk? Yes, he's my darling. My love! Why, is something wrong?|Mei|BotW}}</ref> After a moment, {{Term|BotW|Mei}} realizes that she has been away from {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} for too long, and she begins to cry and apologize to {{Term|BotW|Fronk}}.<ref>{{Cite|... ... {{Big|WAAAAAAH!!}} My darling! I'm sooooorry! I completely forgoooooot! I got carried awaaaaay!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> Determined to return home immediately, {{Term|BotW|Mei}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} the five {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}} that she has caught.<ref>{{Cite|I-I-I've gotta get back home right away! Here, you can have these!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> Once this has been done, she will leave {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia}}.<ref>{{Cite|OK! Now they're all yours. Now then... Time for me to go home to {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}! See ya!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> When she leaves, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

Upon returning to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, {{Term|BotW|Mei}} can be found swimming in a pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber|link}}. She recounts to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} scolded her for being gone so long.<ref>{{Cite|After we last met, my darling gave me a good scolding...|Mei|BotW}}</ref> Due to this incident, {{Term|BotW|Mei}} has been temporarily banned from leaving {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, which she accepts as fair.<ref>{{Cite|He said I shouldn't go wandering about anymore for a while... *sigh* I suppose that's only fair.|Mei|BotW}}</ref> Despite being bored because of this, {{Term|BotW|Mei}} is happy that {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} is looking after {{Term|BotW|Tumbo}} and {{Term|BotW|Keye}}, so all she has to worry about is having fun.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh... I'm so bored... But my darling is looking after the kids today... So all I have to worry about is having fun and being fancy-free!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Mei}} wants to work, she has decided to let {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} take care of their family, since she scared him last time she left.<ref>{{Cite|I want to work, but... I gave my darling such a scare. I suppose I'll let him take care of things for a while. I'll just try to take it easy.|Mei|BotW}}</ref>
