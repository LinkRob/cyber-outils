{{Term/Store|Outpost|Outposts|Series|plural}} are guarded posts in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}}

==Overview and Features==
Outposts are small stations that are found scattered around battlefields. They are similar to [[Keep]]s in that [[Allied Force]]s, [[Enemy Force]]s, and [[Rogue Forces]] fight over them for possession, and that they are used to call upon reinforcements and to counter advancing units. Unlike the structured Keeps that spawn dozens of troops at a time, Outposts are only watched by a couple troops and an Outpost Captain who serves as an Outpost's main line of defense. If an Outpost Captain is defeated by another Force, their Outpost will be claimed. Outposts are commonly found in corner sections of the battlefield. Often a Keep may have one or two Outposts outside to serve as another measure of defense.
