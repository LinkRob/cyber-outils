{{Term/Store|Tiger|Tigers|Series, TMC}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tiger Scroll|N/A|TMC}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, OoS}}

==Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Granny|link}} can be found petting a {{Term|OoT3D|Tiger}} cub in her {{Term|OoT3D|Medicine Shop|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, {{Term|MM3D|Granny}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Terminan|link}} counterpart, {{Term|MM3D|Anju|link}}'s Grandmother, has an image of a {{Term|MM3D|Tiger}} on the backside of her wheelchair.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Main|Syger}}
In {{OoS}}, the {{Term|OoS|Sub-Boss|link}} of {{Term|OoS|Unicorn's Cave|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Syger|link}}, resembles a mix between a {{Term|OoS|Tiger}} and a sabre-toothed {{Term|OoS|Tiger}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Tiger Scrolls}}
[[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite.png|left]]
In {{TMC}}, {{Plural|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}} are a collection of eight key items that allow {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to learn new {{Plural|TMC|Sword Technique|link}}. Their name alludes to {{Plural|TMC|Tiger}} and this is likely because the tiger is an iconic animal in {{Wp|Heihuquan|martial arts}}.
