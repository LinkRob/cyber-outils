'''Gorb skip''' is a skip in {{OoT}} that was discovered on May 13, 2023 and has no known applications. Gorb skip involves using the ascend ability to scale the [[Temple of Time]], maneuver around it, and get to the location where [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] tells you to find her without opening the door in the temple of time. This skip has no known applications because the cutscene doesn’t play unless you have opened the door. If a way is found to activate the cutscene, it will prove useful to get past the door without taking damage.

Discovery:

Discovered by “Eclipse” and “???” on the [[Nintendo Switch]].
