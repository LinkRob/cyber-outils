{{Term/Store|Zelda's Cage||Series, SSBB}} is an object in {{SSBB}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|SSBB|Zelda's Cage}} appears in {{Term|SSBB|The Subspace Emissary|link}}. It appears when {{Smw|Petey Piranha}} appears and traps {{Term|SSBB|Princess Zelda|link}}. {{Term|SSBB|Kirby}} can choose to free {{Term|SSBB|Princess Zelda}} from her {{Term|SSBB|Zelda's Cage|display=Cage}} by dealing damage to it. In doing so, {{Term|SSBB|Princess Zelda}} can be unlocked as a {{Term|SSBB|Fighter|link}} for {{Term|SSBB|The Subspace Emissary}}.
