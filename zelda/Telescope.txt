The {{Term/Store|Telescope||Series, TWW, TWWHD, FSA, BotW, CoH}},{{Exp Game|TWW, FSA, BotW, CoH}}<ref name="E 141">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 141 {{Exp Game|TWW, FSA, PH}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 269 {{Exp Game|BotW}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|CoH}} also known as the {{Term/Store|Kaleidoscope||PH}},{{Exp Game|PH}}<ref name="E 141"/> is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} and {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Uses and Location==
==={{TWW|-}}===
The Telescope is [[Aryll]]'s most treasured possession.<ref>{{Cite|I'm letting you borrow this special gift just because you're my big brother!|Aryll|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Telescope! This is your sister's most precious belonging. Treasure it dearly! Set it to [Y], [Z], or [X] and use it by pressing whatever button you set it to. Do it now, and see what you can see!|N/A|TWW}}</ref> Despite this, she lends it to her brother [[Link]] at the beginning of the game as a birthday present. Although she says she will let him borrow it for one day only, he ends up keeping it for the remainder of the game after she gets kidnapped.

With the Telescope, Link notices [[Quill]] looking up at the sky at the [[Helmaroc King]] kidnapping [[Tetra]], and it is also the only time the Telescope is needed to advance the story of the game.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, what's that? Link! Are you looking at the red postbox? The postman looks kinda weird, doesn't he? AAAAAHH! Link! The sky! Look up in the sky! Link, this is so terrible! That girl fell into the forest! You have to go help her!|Aryll|TWW}}</ref> The Telescope is also useful to locate elements that are too far from Link's first glance, such as the [[seagull]]s surrounding a [[Big Octo]], or a [[Submarine]].

==={{PH|-}}===
The Kaleidoscope is the second item in the [[Phantom Hourglass Trading Sequence|{{PH|-}} trading sequence]]. When Link takes the [[Hero's New Clothes]] to [[Nyeve]] on board the [[Traveler's Ship]] in the North Western Sea, he will be given the Kaleidoscope, which he mistakes for a Telescope at first.<ref>{{Cite|You got the telescope! Now you can see far-off things. No, wait... It's just a kaleidoscope! Too bad...|N/A|PH}}</ref> The Kaleidoscope needs to be taken to the [[Ho Ho Tribe]] to receive the [[Guard Notebook]].
