The {{Term/Store|Discovery Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Discovery Ring<br/>Sense soft earth nearby|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Discovery Ring can be found in the [[Gnarled Root Dungeon]] in {{OoS}} and in the [[Spirit's Grave]] in {{OoA}}. When worn, it makes a sound when entering an area with a patch of [[Soft Soil]] nearby.<ref name="Description"/> The Discovery Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
