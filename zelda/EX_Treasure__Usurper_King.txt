"{{Term/Store|EX Treasure: Usurper King||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Treasure: Usurper King}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Deya Village Ruins}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2|link}}, which is rumored to be in the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Deya Village Ruins}} is a journal that places the location of this treasure...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by reading {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2}} without triggering any of the loading conditions for its activation.}}

Within {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2}} are clues to the locations of six {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, each containing a priceless relic stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the six priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> The Journal states the helmet of the self-proclaimed [[Zant|King of Shadows]] can be found in the mire of a valley of dead trees, south of {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The helm of the self-proclaimed King of Shadows is {{Color|BotW Red|in the swamp of the valley of dead trees, south of Dueling Peaks}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found buried between two dead trees on a small island in {{Term|BotW|Tobio's Hollow|link}}. Using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pull it out of the ground and open it. The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed once {{Term|BotW|Link}} obtains {{Term|BotW|Zant's Helmet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found the treasure that was hidden at Tobio's Hollow and obtained {{Color|BotW Red|Zant's Helmet}}!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
