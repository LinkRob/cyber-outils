{{Term/Store|Kayo||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 282}}</ref>

==Biography==
Kayo was once the assistant of [[Astrid]], the [[Fortune-Teller]] at the [[Isle of Ember]]. He was the only one who knew how to open Astrid's back room in her basement. Upon finding the remains of Kayo on the easternmost point of the island, his ghost appears and informs [[Link]] how to open the room. After freeing Astrid, Kayo asks Link to continue to look after her.<ref>{{Cite|You have freed Astrid! For that, I thank you... Please continue to look after her for me...|Kayo|PH}}</ref> When Link leaves the island, Kayo's bones are buried and a tombstone is erected in their place.{{By Whom}}

His spirit stays on the island, however, and helps Link find two [[Treasure Map]]s.<ref>{{Cite|To thank you for rescuing Astrid, let me share some valuable information... A map is buried...here.  May it be of help to you...|Kayo|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There is another treasure map on this island... It is buried in the basement of Astrid's house...|Kayo|PH}}</ref>

The exact cause of Kayo's death is unknown, though Astrid implies that the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}} was responsible.<ref>{{Cite|The Ghost Ship came to our island a few days ago. . . . I haven't seen Kayo since then...|Astrid|PH}}</ref> He seems to take his own death lightly; he is only concerned for Astrid's well-being.<ref>{{Cite|If you believe in fate... then it's not so bad. I do feel much lighter! Of graver concern, though, is what's happened to Astrid... It haunts me!|Kayo|PH}}</ref>
