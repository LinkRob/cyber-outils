The '''Magic Rope''' is an recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|series}}.{{Name Ref|TLoZ (TV Series), TCT}} 

==Locations and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|TV Series|-}}===
{{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic Rope}} appear in two episodes of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. In "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|The White Knight|link}}", {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} gets out a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic Rope}} from his pouch and ties it around an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Octorok|link}}, but the monster begins to run away and drags {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} with him. In "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stinging a Stinger|link}}", {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda}} uses a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic Rope}} to tie it around a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gohma|link}} and thus immobilize it, allowing her, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sleezenose|link}} to escape from the monster.

==={{TCT|-}}===
In {{TCT}}, {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|link}} can only obtain this {{Term|TCT|Item}} if she chooses to escape the ambush of the {{Plural|TCT|Boomerang Demon|link}} beneath the {{Term|TCT|Midoro Palace}}.

After {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} finds an escape route from the flying demons, she stumbles into the {{Term|TCT|Magic Rope}} hanging on a wall, which leads up and out of the {{Term|TCT|Midoro Palace|display=Palace}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= On the other side, she finds herself at the bottom of a tall shaft. High, high above there's a faint glimmer of light. Hanging on the wall just at shoulder height is a thick rope that seems to lead up to the light. 'It would be just like Ganon to leave a frayed rope as a trap,' she thinks aloud. 'But there doesn't seem to be any other way out of here, so up I go!' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 91}}</ref> Although at first she hesitates to use the Rope since it could be enchanted with {{Term|TCT|Ganon|link}}'s evil magic, she uses it to climb and reach up into a corridor. {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}}, having reached the ledge high above, must then decide whether she should take the Rope with her or not.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'This rope wants to come along,' she says with a grim smile. 'But I still think there's something fishy about it. I wonder if I should just leave it here?' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 91}}</ref>

The {{Term|TCT|Magic Rope}} is only used if {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} chooses to go deeper into the {{Term|TCT|Swamp}}. She eventually gets stuck in a quicksand, and the only way to get out of it is by using the magical {{Term|TCT|Item}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'The rope!' Zelda cries as she remembers the rope she found in Midoro Palace. Quickly, she takes it from her belt and looks for a strong branch or stump to lasso. |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 21}}</ref> Just as {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} is about to throw it at a stump, the {{Term|TCT|Magic Rope}} comes alive and flies up into the air, tying itself around the trunk of a {{Term|TCT|Tree}}. {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} pulls herself from the quicksand and begins to head out of the {{Term|TCT|Swamp}}, taking the {{Term|TCT|Magic Rope}} with her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= But even as she speaks, the rope comes alive in her hand! The end flies up into the air, twirls around the trunk of a tree, and then ties itself snurgly. 'A magic rope!' Zelda cries out. She pulls herself from the quicksand, carefully coils the rope, and hooks it on her belt. |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 91}}</ref> However, if {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} didn't pick up the {{Term|TCT|Magic Rope}} from {{Term|TCT|Midoro Palace}}, she will die in the quicksand.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The quicksand bubbles noisily. Zelda groans in despair. 'I guess I'm really sunk now!' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 82}}</ref>
