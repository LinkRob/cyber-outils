{{Term/Store|Heedle|Heedles|Series, ALBW|plural}} are the {{Plural|ALBW|Sub-Boss|link}} of the {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales|link}} in {{ALBW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ALBW|Heedle}} are giant, flaming salamanders that reside in the {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales|link}}. {{Plural|ALBW|Heedle}} will run around the room, leaving temporary pools of {{Term|ALBW|Lava|link}} that will damage {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} if he walks into them. To defeat the {{Plural|ALBW|Heedle}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must extinguish their flames with a gust of wind from the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}}, leaving them temporarily vulnerable to attacks. The {{Term|ALBW|Ice Rod|link}} may also be used to freeze them and also render them vulnerable. After their defeat, the room's doors will reopen and a {{Term|ALBW|Warp Point|link}} will appear that will transport {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to and from the entrance of the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}}.
