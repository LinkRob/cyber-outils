'''''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess — PRIMA Official Game Guide (eGuide)''''' is a digital strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{TP}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The 432-page eGuide has the same contents as "[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess — PRIMA Official Game Guide]]" Wii version, containing a complete walkthrough of {{TP}}, detailed maps, and a listing of {{Plural|TP|Item|link}} and collectibles.

It was offered as a digital download from Prima and is primarily a digitized version of the physical book. The eGuide has searchable text and a hypertext-enabled table of contents. Each page also has a button to return to the table of contents.

The guide had a free preview containing partial snippets of various chapters. These include the table of contents, basic player actions, a partial walkthrough of {{Term|TP|Ordon Village|link}}, and {{Term|TP|Item}}, {{Term|TP|Character|link}}, and {{Term|TP|Boss|link}} lists.<ref>{{Cite Web|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101229174142/http://www.primagames.com/catalog/promo_image/9780307467690_1547.pdf|site=primagames.com (archived)|title=The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess (Wii) (eGuide) - Free Preview|type=PDF|author=Prima Games|retrieved=October 1, 2022}}</ref>
