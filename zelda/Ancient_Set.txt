The {{Term/Store|Ancient Set|Ancient Set|Series, BotW}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 185}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Ancient Soldier Gear|Ancient Soldier Gear|SSBU}},{{Exp Game|SSBU}}{{Name Ref|SSBU}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Inventory/Set|BotW|Ancient Set}}
The Ancient Set is an {{Term|BotW|Armor Set|link}} consisting of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Helm|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ancient Cuirass|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Ancient Greaves|link}}. Each piece of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set}} can be purchased at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} where {{Term|BotW|Cherry|link}} will construct them from various ancient materials. 

Each piece of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set}} reduces the damage taken from Guardians and weapons with the Ancient Technology property. Wearing the set provides the Ancient Proficiency set bonus, which increases damage from all ancient weapons by 80%. In addition to its useful bonuses, the armor has a very high defense level, on par with the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Set|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{SSBU|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Main|Mii Headgear, Mii Outfit}}
The {{Term|SSBU|Ancient Set}} appears as a {{Term|SSBU|Mii Headgear|display=Headgear|link}} and {{Term|SSBU|Mii Outfit|display=Outfit|link}} for Mii Fighters. While the {{Term|SSBU|Ancient Helm}} can be worn by any Mii, only {{Plural|SSBU|Mii Swordfighter|link}} can wear the {{Term|SSBU|Ancient Set}}. Included as part of version 7.0.0; it is an exclusive bonus received as part of the {{Term|SSBU|Fighters Pass Vol. 2|link}}, released on January 28, 2020.
{{Noncanon|End}}
