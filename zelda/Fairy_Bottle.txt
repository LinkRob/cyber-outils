==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|NS|-}}===
{{Plural|LANS|Fairy Bottle}} can be used to store {{Plural|LANS|Fairy|link}} for {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} to use at any time in {{LA|NS}}. The first {{Term|LANS|Fairy Bottle}} is received from the {{Term|LANS|Ghost|link}} after returning it to its grave. The second one can be found at the bottom of the {{Term|LANS|Fishing Pond|link}}, requiring {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} to fish it. The final {{Term|LANS|Fairy Bottle}} is received from {{Term|LANS|Dampé|link}} after completing the Heart Shortage {{Term|LANS|Chamber Dungeon|link}}.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{hatnote|”If you lose all your hearts, the fairy will fly out of the bottle and heal you.” — [[Key Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Key Items]]<ref name="EoW Fairy Bottle">{{Cite|If you lose all your hearts, the fairy will fly out of the bottle and heal you.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Fairy Bottle is an item that can hold captured Fairies. [[Princess Zelda]] can obtain up to four bottles in total, and when at full health, she can use one to capture a Fairy for later use.

==Other Appearances==
