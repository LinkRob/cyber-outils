"{{Term/Store|EX Treasure: Phantasma||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Treasure: Phantasma}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Outpost Ruins}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal|link}}, which is rumored to be in the {{Term|BotW|Outpost Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a journal that places the locations of the treasure somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Outpost Ruins}}...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by reading {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal}} without triggering any of the loading conditions for its activation.}}

Within {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal}} are clues to the locations of eight {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, each containing a priceless relic stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the eight priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> The Journal states the {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} of fierce {{Plural|BotW|Phantom|link}} can be found ruins of a battleground, ceremonial grounds, and where soldiers gathered.<ref>{{Cite|Armor worn by fierce phantoms, feared even by heroes, is hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in the ruins of Hyrule Field}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at a battleground where brave souls tested their skill}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at ceremonial grounds where royal guards are honored}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where the soldiers of Hyrule gathered}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Phantom Helmet|link}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} buried in the northwest of the {{Term|BotW|Coliseum Ruins|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Armor|link}} is buried in the southeast of the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Ground Ruins|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Phantom Greaves|link}} are in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} buried near a wrecked {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}} in the southeast of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Garrison Ruins|link}}. Each {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} can be dislodged from the ground using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Armor}} within. The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed once all three pieces of the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Equipment|link}} have been obtained.<ref>{{Cite|You found the treasure that was hidden at Coliseum Ruins, Sacred Grounds Ruins, and Hyrule Garrison Ruins. You obtained the {{Color|BotW Red|Phantom Equipment}}!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
