{{Term/Store|Goddess Butterfly|Goddess Butterflies|Series, HW|plural}} are butterflies that appear in {{HW}}.<ref name="Agitha">{{Cite|This is a Goddess Butterfly. Ask it kindly and it will take you anywhere you want to go. Isn't that nice?|Agitha|HW}}</ref> They are blue butterflies that are able to guide a path to anywhere they are asked.<ref name="Agitha"/>

==Characteristics==
Very little is known about the Goddess Butterfly, other than it is able to locate paths to anywhere or anyone it is asked to. It is a blue butterfly that emits a blue aura as it flies. Agitha states that it is not of a poisonous genus.<ref>{{Cite|Actually, that butterfly you see now is not of a poisonous genus, I can assure you!|Agitha|HW}}</ref>

A Goddess Butterfly appears in "[[Twilight Field|Land of Twilight]]", under the ownership of [[Agitha]]. After [[Lana]] saves the girl from a group of [[Bulblin]]s, Agitha offers to help find the [[Gate of Souls]] with the use of her Goddess Butterfly to guide the [[Forces|Hyrulean Forces]] through the [[Twilight Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|Gate of Souls...? Never seen one. But I can take you right to where you want to go! [...] With the help of this Goddess Butterfly, of course! It will guide us through the Twilight Realm.|Agitha|HW}}</ref> The Hyrulean Forces follow the butterfly until they are confronted by [[Midna]] who seizes the butterfly, believing it to be poisoning her troops.<ref>{{Cite|Why? So it can fly around and spread poison on my forces?|Midna|HW}}</ref> When she learns otherwise that the butterfly can guide to anywhere it is asked, Midna decides to use the butterfly to locate [[Cia]],<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Butterfly! Take me to that ugly witch!|Midna|HW}}</ref> who had cursed Midna into an imp prior.<ref>{{Cite|She's the one! I'm cursed to look like this because of HER!|Midna|HW}}</ref> The seizure of the butterfly forces the Hyrulean Forces to fight Midna's Forces. After Lana defeats her, Midna relinquishes the butterfly back to Agitha, and joins the Hyrulean Forces after learning they are not with Cia.<ref>{{Cite|Fine, take the butterfly, but... Wait. You're not with the witch?|Midna|HW}}</ref> Later in "[[Palace of Twilight (Hyrule Warriors)#The Shadow King|The Shadow King]]", the same Goddess Butterfly leads the Hyrulean Forces to the [[Palace of Twilight (Hyrule Warriors)|palace]]'s [[Fairy Fountain]], after [[Argorok]] returns the third time to attack the [[Keep|Allied Base]].<ref name="She">{{Cite|Li'l Butterfly has something to say! Yes? Hmm... She wants to lead us somewhere!|Agitha|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Let's follow the butterfly to the building in the southeast! Trust it knows something!|Lana|HW}}</ref> The butterfly's guidance brings the Forces' attention to a [[Magic Circle]] there, which summons the [[Great Fairy]].

Goddess Butterflies also appear on certain battle squares on the [[Adventure Mode]] map as an obstacle. Some squares will have their enemies hidden and cannot be played until a [[Goddess's Harp]] [[Item Card]] is used on the butterfly swarm. Playing the harp on them makes a [[Gossip Stone]] appear, revealing the battle's enemies.

==Trivia==
*The Goddess Butterflies are nearly identical to the [[Bug#Skyward Sword|Blessed Butterflies]] of {{SS}}. Both are blue butterflies that gather where secrets are hidden, which may be revealed by playing the Goddess Harp near them.
*According to Agitha, the Goddess Butterfly she owns is a female.<ref name="She"/>
