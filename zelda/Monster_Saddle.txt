==Location and Uses==
Monster Saddles can be purchased from {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for 299 {{Plural|BotW|Mon|link}}. Kilton made them himself, though they were intended for a more monstrous {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Despite their strange appearance, they're able to be used.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can equip his Horses with Monstrous Saddles by speaking to workers at either {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Monster Saddle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> As is the case with other {{Plural|BotW|Saddle|link}}, they can't be utilized by {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.
