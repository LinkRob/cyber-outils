==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
Blue Octoroks are a common {{Term|FSA|Enemy|link}} that can be found in the very beginning of the game. They are more durable than the red variant as they take two hits from a {{Term|FSA|Sword|link}} They spit out {{Plural|FSA|Rock|link}} like the red variant and their attacks take half a {{Term|FSA|Heart|link}} from Link.

{{Plural|FSA|Blue Octorok}} appear as a semi-common {{Term|FSA|Enemy|link}} starting in {{Term|FSA|The Coast|link}}. Unlike their regular counterparts, {{Plural|FSA|Blue Octorok}} attack by shooting in all four directions and are more durable. The {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} must attack it in rapid succession, which will cause it to grow bigger until it reaches a certain size and is defeated. If attacked too slowly, the {{Term|FSA|Blue Octorok}} will gradually shrink down to its original size.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Octorok}}
{{Plural|TMC|Blue Octorok}} appear in the {{Term|TMC|Overworld|link}} of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} in {{TMC}}. They can be found in {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}, and at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. They are nearly identical to {{Plural|TMC|Red Octorok|link}}, spitting rocks at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} periodically.
