==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Update|LANS}}
{{Manual|LA|Secret Shells|These shells can be found scattered throughout Koholint Island. Try searching for them in tall grass or {{Typo|under water|underwater}}. If you find enough of them, something good is bound to happen.}}
{{Plural|LANS|Secret Seashell}} are hidden collectibles scattered across {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= '''SECRET SHELLS'''<br/>These shells are scattered throughout the island. If you find enough of them, something good is sure to happen.|game= LADX|page= 25}}</ref> There is a total of 26 {{Plural|LANS|Secret Seashell}} for {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} to find (50 in the Nintendo Switch remake). They can be found underneath {{Plural|LANS|Bush|link}}, {{Plural|LANS|Rock|link}}, underwater, or inside {{Plural|LANS|Treasure Chest|link}}.<ref name="LA Manual"/> Though typically obtained one at a time, {{Term|LANS|Link}} may receive two at the {{Term|LANS|Seashell Mansion|link}} as long as he is already carrying exactly five or ten of them.<ref name="LA Guide 87">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA |publisher= Nintendo|page= 87}}</ref> By collecting 20 {{Plural|LANS|Secret Seashell}} and going to the {{Term|LANS|Seashell Mansion}}, {{Term|LANS|Link}} receives the {{Term|LANS|Koholint Sword|link}}.<ref name="LA Guide 87"/> Once the {{Term|LANS|Koholint Sword}} is obtained, any remaining {{Plural|LANS|Secret Seashell}} will either disappear or be replaced with 20 {{Plural|LANS|Rupee|link}}.

If {{Term|LANS|Link}} is taking {{Term|LANS|BowWow|link}} for a walk and gets close to a buried {{Term|LANS|Secret Seashell}}, {{Term|LANS|BowWow}} will sniff it out and hint it to {{Term|LANS|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|WOOF! Dig! RUFF!|BowWow|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 33}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell}} are used to buy {{Plural|TMC|Figurine|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Figurine Gallery|link}}, which is found in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. They are found all over {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} inside {{Plural|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}}, bushes, and {{Plural|TMC|Rock|link}}. Later into the game, {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}} will begin selling {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell}} at 30 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|display=Shells}} for 200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} (300 Rupees in the PAL version). {{Term|TMC|Link}} can hold up to 999 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|displayShells}} at a time. Using {{Term|TMC|Green Picolyte|link}} will increase {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s chance of finding {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} has collected all 136 {{Plural|TMC|Figurine}}, the {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell}} slot in his status screen will change into the {{Term|TMC|Carlov Medal|link}}, signifying he completed the collection.
