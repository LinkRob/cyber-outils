{{Term/Store|EX A Moblin Feast||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX A Moblin Feast|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It is only available as part of the game's second {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}} wave, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian of Remembrance|link}}.

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|EX A Moblin Feast}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given eleven minutes to defeat 16 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. All of them are {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}}, and are arranged in groups on a path that spans the entire map. Defeating all of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} in one section of the map opens a gate that allows the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} to progress, until they reach the northern end of the map where the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Moblin|link}} and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} are located. The 16 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} consist of seven {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}}, a normal {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Moblin|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Zapshroom|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Winterwing Butterfly|link}}, fifteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Bladed Rhino Beetle|link}} and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
