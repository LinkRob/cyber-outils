{{Term/Store|Din's Fire||Series, OoT, OoT3D, SSBM, SSBB, HW, SSBfN3DS, SSBfWU, SSBU}} is a recurring ability in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Din's Fire is obtained from the [[Great Fairy of Magic]] near [[Hyrule Castle]] in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome Link! I am the Great Fairy of Magic! I will give you a magic spell. Please take it.|Great Fairy of Magic|OoT}}</ref> To reach her, [[Link]] as a child must use a [[Bomb]] in a wall located right after a [[sign]] with "Dead end" written on it.

When used, the magic spell based on the [[Golden Goddess|Goddess]] [[Din (Goddess)|Din]] makes the [[Hero of Time]] summon a dome of [[Fire]] around his body that expands outward, affecting anything in its range, and therefore it can be used for combat.<ref>{{Cite|You can use Din's Fire not only to attack but also to burn things!|Great Fairy of Magic|OoT}}</ref> When used near [[Torch]]es, it effectively lights them. Din's Fire drains 6 Magic Points with each use, similar to [[Farore's Wind]].

Unlike Farore's Wind or [[Nayru's Love]], Din's Fire is the only Spell among the three necessary to complete the game; It is required to simultaneously light the Torches at the entrance to the [[Shadow Temple]], opening the way into the Temple. Inside the Temple, Din's Fire must be used to incinerate a spiked, wooden wall trap that threatens to crush Link in a certain room. The [[Boss Key]] of the Shadow Temple becomes available upon destroying it. It is also needed to continue the Light Barrier room in [[Ganon's Castle]] where one has many Torches.

==Other Appearances==
==={{SSBM|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
:''See {{ssb|Main Page|SmashWiki's}} articles on {{ssb|Din's Fire}} for more information.''
In the {{SSBM}}, Din's Fire is Zelda's {{ssb|side special move}}. Its function here is completely different from how Link used it originally. When performed, Zelda shoots a small fireball from her hands, which can travel long distances in a straight or curved line. It can be controlled with the control stick or D-pad while holding the special attack button. When releasing the special attack button, the fireball explodes. The more distance Din's Fire travels, the stronger the explosion.<ref>{{Cite|Din's Fire, her explosive side special, can be guided up and down and has a greater blast if the button is held.|Trophy|SSBfWU}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{SSBB|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
The fireball from {{Term|SSBB|Din's Fire}} travels much faster in {{SSBB}} than in {{SSBM}}. It also detonates much quicker and does more damage the further the fireball travels. It cannot be reflected, but still can be absorbed by moves such as the {{Ssb|PSI Magnet}} and the {{Ssb|Oil Panic}}. If Zelda is hit before the fireball is activated, it will fly to its maximum range, then detonate. This can be compared to {{SSBM|-}}, where the attack simply vanishes.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:HW Din's Fire Badge Icon.png|right]]
In {{HW}}, Din's Fire appears as an [[Badge Market|Attack Badge]] in the {{Term|HW|Bazaar|link}}'s [[Badge Market]]. When purchased for a Warrior, it increases the rate that the [[Special Attack]] Gauge fills for that particular Warrior.<ref>{{Cite|Makes the Special Attack gauge fill slightly faster.|N/A|HW}}</ref> It appears in three iterative Badge qualities: bronze, silver and gold; each providing a stronger effect than the one prior.

One of the attacks that Zelda can execute with her [[Rapier]] is summoning a giant, blazing hemisphere. This, joined with her two other attacks resembling [[Farore's Wind]] and [[Nayru's Love]], appears to be a reference to Din's Fire. The dome is similar to the one from {{OoT|-}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

'''Din's Fire''' is [[Zelda]]'s [[Side Special Move]] in ultimate. It is an attack with considerable range, and when it hits the opponent, it burns them and knocks them upward. It can be controlled with the control stick (while holding B) and can be released by letting go of the [[B button]]. The longer the B button is held, the stronger the attack.

==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
Din's Fire appears as a special offensive ability unique to {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. It can be randomly obtained from {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} in one of the caves she appears in, with her claiming that she had found a monster holding onto it. Once acquired, Zelda can then use it to deal explosive damage to enemies and objects, including cracked walls to reveal caves at the cost of {{Term|CoH|Stamina|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Din's Fire}} {{Icon|CoH Zelda}}<br/>Press {{Icon|CoH L}} to create a fireball. Use it again to detonate the fireball, dealing damage to adjacent squares, at the cost of stamina.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> The spell also deals fire damage, allowing her to burn stumps and braziers without the use of a {{Term|CoH|Torch|link}} or {{Plural|CoH|Fire Arrow|link}}.
{{Ambig|End}}

==Trivia==
*Though Din's Fire doesn't appear in {{TWW}}, its name is inscribed on the base of [[Din (Goddess)|Din]]'s statue in [[Hylian Language#Third Hylian Script|Hylian]] on [[Northern Triangle Island]].
*[[Din's Flame]] in {{SS}} closely resembles Din's Fire.
*The Din's Fire spell is useful in the challenging [[Sub-Boss]] battle against [[Dark Link]] in the [[Water Temple]], as the foe is completely unable to defend against the magical attack, similar to the [[Megaton Hammer]] during his near-transparent stage, always landing hits. This trick, however, is discouraged among some fans as it is believed to be a cheap way to defeat him.
*During the final battle against [[Ganon]] in {{OoT|-}}, if [[Link]] were to use the Din's Fire against his archenemy while in front of him, despite it cannot harm the beast (though it does briefly make him stop and shake his head), the [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Hookshot_Clang.wav sound effect] of the [[Hookshot]] fired directly into a wall can be oddly heard. This also applies to throwing a [[Deku Nut]]. It is likely due to the fact Ganon is fully empowered by the [[Triforce of Power]] and therefore grants the main antagonist near-invulnerability onto his body.
