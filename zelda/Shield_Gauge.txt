The {{Term/Store|Shield Gauge||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a mechanic in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= |game= SS |page= 9}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|SSHD|Shield Gauge}} is a bar in the top-left of the screen that tracks how much {{Term|SSHD|Durability|link}} is left on {{Term|SSHD|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|SSHD|Shield|link}}. It's size and color depend on the {{Term|SSHD|Shield}} equipped. When {{Term|SSHD|Link}} blocks an {{Term|SSHD|Enemy|link}} attack but does not successfully perform a {{Term|SSHD|Shield Attack|link}} on it, the {{Term|SSHD|Shield Gauge}} will decrease. Once the {{Term|SSHD|Shield Gauge|display=Gauge}} is empty, the {{Term|SSHD|Shield}} will break.

The {{Term|SSHD|Shield Gauge}} for {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Shield|link}}, {{Plural|SSHD|Divine Shield|link}}, and {{Plural|SSHD|Goddess Shield|link}} will regenerate over time. The {{Term|SSHD|Shield Gauge}} for the {{Term|SSHD|Hylian Shield|link}} is purely decorative and will never be reduced.
