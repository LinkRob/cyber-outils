'''[[wikipedia:Rabbit|Rabbits]]''' are recurring {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 63 {{Exp Game|Series}}}}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Main|Bunny Link}}
[[File:Bunny Link.gif]]In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', when [[Link]] first travels to the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}}, he is transformed into a humanoid pink Rabbit himself, reflecting the true nature of his heart.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 29}}</ref> He will retain his real [[Hylian]] form when he obtains the [[Moon Pearl]] from the [[Tower of Hera]], although [[Rabbit Beam|Rabbit Beams]] in some Dark World dungeons can temporally revert him back to a Rabbit.

Rabbits are also seen in the {{Term|ALttP|Light World|link}}'s {{Term|ALttP|Haunted Grove|link}} with other various woodland Animals listening to the {{Term|ALttP|Flute Boy|link}} playing.

==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, anthropomorphic Rabbits are found in the {{Term|LADX|Animal Village|link}}. If Link speaks to them, they comment on various things such as how nice the day is, that they need a song from {{Term|LADX|Marin|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Ahhh... Sigh... On such a nice day, we need a song from Marin! |Rabbit|LADX}}</ref> that a lazy {{Term|LADX|Walrus|link}} is blocking the entrance to {{Term|LADX|Yarna Desert|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Yarna Desert? There's a way to get there to the south, but you might not be able to get through if that lazy walrus is in the way!|Rabbit|LADX}}</ref> and that {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village|link}} and the {{Term|LADX|Animal Village}} are sister cities despite their small size.<ref name="Sister Cities">{{Cite|Hey! Did ya know Animal Village and Mabe Village are sister cities? Yes, even though they aren't large enough to be called cities... Anyway, I heard from a very good source that they have a Dream Shrine in Mabe, and that it has something good inside... Is that true? |Rabbit|LADX}}</ref> One of them also asks whether there is a {{Term|LADX|Dream Shrine|link}} in {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village}}.<ref name="Sister Cities"/> Another Rabbit says it cannot go to {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village}} because of all the monsters and expresses concern for {{Term|LADX|Marin}}'s safety.<ref>{{Cite|I can't go to Mabe Village because of all the monsters. I hope Marin is all right. |Rabbit|LADX}}</ref> When Link brings {{Term|LADX|Marin}} to the {{Term|LADX|Animal Village}}, the Rabbits jump up and down upon seeing her. After {{Term|LADX|Marin}}'s singing awakens the {{Term|LADX|Walrus}}, a Rabbit says it is calling it as always.<ref>{{Cite|Hunh? Oh, he's calling me... It's the same as always... Ha ha!|Rabbit|LADX}}</ref> The Rabbits are next seen listening to {{Term|LADX|Marin}} sing the "{{Term|LADX|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}" to them and the other Animals.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Link . I often come to this village to sing, too! It seems that just about everyone loves my 'Ballad of the Wind Fish!' Link , what is your favorite song? |Marin|LADX}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
Rabbits do not appear in {{OoT}}, but they are referred to by the {{Term|OoT3D|Running Man|link}} and the {{Term|OoT3D|Bunny Hood|link}}. According to the Running Man, Rabbits are legendary Animal.<ref>{{Cite|I bet with those long ears you can hear the voices... Oh, these are genuine rabbit ears from the animal of legend! I don't care how expensive it is! Please sell it to me!|Running Man|OoT}}</ref>

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, a Rabbit listens to {{Term|OoA|Nayru (Oracle)|link}} sing while singing along itself.<ref>{{Cite|La la li li la La Li li la|Rabbit|OoA}}</ref> The Rabbit and other {{Plural|OoA|Animal}} are frightened away as {{Term|OoA|Veran|link}} releases {{Term|OoA|Impa|link}} in order to possess the {{Term|OoA|Oracle|link}}. After {{Term|OoA|Link}} defeats {{Term|OoA|Veran}} in the {{Term|OoA|Black Tower|link}}, the curse set on [[Labrynna]]'s people is lifted. Among the characters affected, three Rabbits are seen returning back to normal after being turned to stone.

==={{ST|-}}===
Rabbits are collectible objects in {{ST}}. They can be seen hiding behind large [[boulder]]s while riding the [[Spirit Train]] throughout [[New Hyrule]], or behind floating [[Barrel]]s in the [[Ocean Realm]], with only their ears visible. Sounding the [[Train Whistle]] will cause them to jump, confirming their location. In order to catch the Rabbit, Link must blast the object that they are hiding behind with the [[Cannon]]. He will then have ten seconds to capture the Rabbit using the [[Rabbit Net]] obtained from [[Bunnio]]<ref>{{Cite|OK, I'm convinced. If you want to join the Rabbitland family, you'll need this!|Bunnio|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|ST Light Red|rabbit net}}! Try using it while you're riding the train!|N/A|ST}}</ref> while it hops around the screen in a random manner. If he fails in his attempt, he will have to return at a later time to try again.

In all, there are five different breeds of Rabbit, corresponding to the five {{Plural|ST|Realm|link}} they inhabit. Ten Rabbits of each breed can be captured, making for a total of 50 Rabbits spread across [[New Hyrule]]. They are as follows:

*[[Grass Rabbit]] ([[Forest Realm]])
*[[Snow Rabbit]] ([[Snow Realm]])
*[[Ocean Rabbit]] ([[Ocean Realm]])
*[[Mountain Rabbit]] ([[Fire Realm]])
*[[Desert Rabbit]] ([[Sand Realm]])

Once Link manages to capture a Rabbit, he can take it to Bunnio, owner of the [[Rabbitland Rescue]], for a small reward. Bunnio's goal is to take care of all the stray Rabbits in Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Rabbitland Rescue, a haven for rabbits and their furless friends. There are millions of poor rabbits without a home or human companionship. Here at Rabbitland Rescue, we save these bunnies from the harsh wilderness... And provide them with much-needed frolicking.|Bunnio|ST}}</ref> The rescued Rabbits are then left to frolic in an area of the Rabbitland Rescue corresponding to their realm of origin. When Link catches and brings in a total of five Rabbits of any variety, Bunnio will give him a [[Heart Container]].<ref>{{Cite|Goodness! You've caught at least five more rabbits already? Then here's that amazing prize I promised you!|Bunnio|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|ST Light Red|Heart Container}}! You increased your life by one and refilled your hearts!|N/A|ST}}</ref> When Link captures ten Rabbits in total, or at least one Rabbit of each breed, he will be given a random [[treasure]].<ref>{{Cite|If you can bring me 10 bunnies from the grass, you get a prize!|Bunnio|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ooh! It looks like you've managed to catch every variety of rabbit out there. I guess it's time for me to give you that wonderful present I promised!|Bunnio|ST}}</ref> If he is able to catch all 50 Rabbits, he will be rewarded with the first [[Swordsman's Scroll 1|Swordsman's Scroll]].<ref>{{Cite|Wow! Just...wow! You've collected every rabbit out there. I'm speechless... You've more than earned it, so here it is, the neatest present EVER!|Bunnio|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|ST Light Red|Swordman's Scroll}}! Memories of veteran swordsmen flow through it. You can now send shock waves with your sword, Link! Swing your sword to send a powerful shock wave surging out!|N/A|ST}}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, a quest consisting of bringing back a picture of a {{Term|BotW|Blupee|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Peeks|link}} is named "{{Term|BotW|Legendary Rabbit Trial|link}}".

Rabbits also appear as the design of the {{Term|BotW|Hunter's Shield|link}}.

==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{OoA|Himekawa}}, when {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link|link}} and {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Impa|link}} enter a woodland, they are confronted by the forest {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Animal|link}}, including {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Rabbit}}. The {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Rabbit}} and other creatures surround them and demand they go back.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Shh! Hold back! We're surrounded.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 21}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Go back. Go back. Go back.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 22}}</ref> {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} tries to calm the {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Animal}} and tells them he was sent by {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Princess Zelda|link}} to find {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= We mean no harm. Princess Zelda sent us here to find Nayru.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 23}}</ref> Upon hearing this, the forest creatures realize {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} is a {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Hylian|link}} of the old blood and allows the two passage.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= He can speak to us. His ears are pointed. He's a Hylian. A Hylian of the old blood!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 23}}</ref> Two {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Rabbit}} listen to {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)}} while she sings and plays her {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Harp of Ages|display=harp|link}}. One of the {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Rabbit}} asks what is wrong when she stops, urging the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Oracle|link}} to continue singing.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= What's wrong, Nayru? Sing some more.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 24}}</ref> The {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Rabbit}} watch as {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)}} converses with {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} and {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Impa}}. Once {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran|link}} reveals her possession of {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Impa}}, the {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Rabbit}} and other creatures attack the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Sheikah|link}} in an attempt to protect {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)}}. The attack fails and {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran}} possess {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)}}, using her abilities to travel back in time. One of the {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Rabbit}} examine {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)}}'s harp as {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} picks it up. It eyes {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ralph|link}} with a frown while he talks to {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}}.
