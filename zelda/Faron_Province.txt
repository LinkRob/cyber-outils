The {{Term/Store|Faron Province||Series}} is a recurring location situated in [[Hyrule]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess — PRIMA Official Game Guide|''PRIMA Official Game Guide'']] (Prima Games) pg. 10</ref> The Province is named after its original protector, the great Water Dragon known as [[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]], from the [[Era of the Goddess Hylia]] and the [[Sky Era]]. It is later protected by the [[Spirits of Light|Light Spirit]] of the [[Faron (Spirit)|same name]] during the [[Era of Twilight]].<ref name="Name1">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 74}}</ref><ref name="Name2">{{Cite Book|quote= |book=HH|publisher= |page= 116}}</ref>

A large majority of the province is made up of the [[Faron Woods]] and other forested areas. It houses a number of ruins, including several ancient temples that have become overgrown by nature, and also at least two sacred springs.{{Fact}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
The Faron Province is situated at the furthest southern part of the Kingdom of Hyrule in {{TP}}. It is located to the north of the [[Ordona Province]], south of the [[Lanayru Province]], southwest of the [[Eldin Province]] and southeast of the [[Desert Province]] in the [[Wii]] version of the game.{{Fact}}

[[Link#Twilight Princess|Link]], a young hero from [[Ordon Village]] in the Ordona Province, first enters the Faron Province while pursuing his friend [[Talo]] and a [[monkey]] that has recently been causing trouble in his village, leading him through Faron Woods and to the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}}, where he finds them captured by a group of [[Bokoblin]]s. The province is then later covered in a [[Twilight|curtain of Twilight]] the next day.<ref>{{Cite|The dark clouds of dusk cover this land... This drape of shadows is called... twilight.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> After saving the Light Spirit Faron and returning light to the area, Link sets off to return to the Forest Temple at the request of [[Midna]] to retrieve the first piece of the [[Fused Shadow]].<ref>{{Cite|That’s...what I was looking for. That’s a Fused Shadow. It’s what the light spirit called dark power...|Midna|TP}}</ref>

Later on in the game, after having been permanently transformed into a [[Wolf Link|wolf]] due to [[Zant]]'s [[Shadow Crystal]], Link must return to the Faron Woods to enter the [[Sacred Grove]] hidden deep within and retrieve the [[Master Sword]] at the ruins of the [[Temple of Time]] in order to return to his human form.<ref>{{Cite|Head for the sacred grove that lies deep within the lands guarded by the spirit Faron. There you will find the blade of evil’s bane that was crafted by the wisdom of the ancient sages... the Master Sword.|Princess Zelda|TP}}</ref> The young hero later meets up with [[Rusl]] in the Faron Woods,<ref>{{Cite|I wonder if the Sacred Grove that Rusl mentioned is near this mark...|N/A|TP}}</ref> who helps him access the Sacred Grove once again, where he then must enter through the Temple of Time's remaining entrance. He is transported to a point far into the past, a time when the temple still stood in full glory. There, he collects the [[Dominion Rod]] and a [[Mirror Shard|piece]] of the broken [[Mirror of Twilight]] upon defeating [[Armogohma]].

During the ending credits, Link is seen riding through Faron Woods on [[Epona]].

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS|-}}, the Faron Province is located east of the Lanayru Province and southeast of the Eldin Province. It is situated furthest southeast on the map.

In the Era of the Goddess Hylia and the Sky Era, the Faron Province is protected by a great Water Dragon, Faron, who was ordered to protect the Province by the goddess [[Hylia]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Long ago the Water Dragon was charged with watching over all of Faron by the goddess. |url=http://zelda.com/skywardsword/#/world/lake-floria/water-dragon|title=Official Site – The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword for Wii|site=Zelda.com}}</ref> She resides with the [[Parella]] in [[Lake Floria]], located southeast of Faron Woods. Coming from the [[Skyloft|world]] above the clouds, Link first visits the Province when he lands on the [[Hyrule|Surface]] in his desperate mission to find his childhood friend, [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]], who landed there sometime earlier. He lands in the [[Sealed Grounds]], and meets the elderly [[Impa#Skyward Sword|Impa]] protecting the [[Sealed Temple]]. She directs him to continue on his quest in Faron Woods,<ref>{{Cite|She set out for {{Color|SS Blue|Faron Woods}} to discover that destiny for herself, and you must follow.|Impa|SS}}</ref> the defining feature and largest section of the Faron Province. Upon reaching the woods, he is tasked by the [[Kikwi]] elder [[Bucha]] to find the other scattered and hidden members of the tribe, who claims that putting his fear and worry to ease will help him remember where Zelda ran off to after she met him.<ref>{{Cite|I've been so worried about my Kikwis. But if you could put my fears to rest, I might remember where that girl you seek ran off to, kwee.|Bucha|SS}}</ref> After doing so, he is given the [[Slingshot]], granting him access to the [[Deep Woods]] and [[Skyview Temple]]. While searching for Zelda in the Skyview Temple, Link's companion [[Fi]] detects she is now located. Upon fighting and defeating the temple's boss, [[Ghirahim]], he finds the [[Skyview Spring]], but Zelda has already left the Faron Province by then.<ref>{{Cite|I calculate a 97% chance that she has already set out for {{Color|SS Blue|Eldin}}, where another great spring exists.|Fi|SS}}</ref> He then moves on to the next Province in his journey, the Eldin Province.

Link later returns to the Faron Province for his first [[Silent Realm]] trial in the Faron Woods. He is given the [[Water Dragon's Scale]] after completing the challenge, allowing him access to the [[Great Tree]]. The elder Kikwi, [[Yerbal]], is found at the top of the Great Tree and sends Link to the other main part of Faron Province, Lake Floria, to meet with the Province's protector.<ref>{{Cite|Well, that settles that. You must seek out the mighty Water Dragon. Her lair is deep in a lake in the south of these woods, kwwwwwwrk. She watches over the flame there.|Yerbal|SS}}</ref> The Water Dragon Faron had been wounded by an attack from Ghirahim.<ref>{{Cite|Shortly before your arrival, I was attacked by a pompous creature calling himself {{Color|SS Blue|Ghirahim}}. I stood against this self-proclaimed Demon Lord, but I was wounded in the encounter.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> Link heals her with [[Sacred Water]] from Skyview Spring, and in return she parts the waterfall in front of the [[Ancient Cistern]], the dungeon hiding [[Farore's Flame]].

Link must visit the Province one last time in order to receive Faron's section of the "[[Song of the Hero]]", but Faron has flooded the woods to wash away the monster infestation,<ref>{{Cite|This may be news to you, but some very irksome monsters have been overrunning my woods of late. Well, I decided that I'd had enough! The easiest way to deal with the awful things was to flood the whole woods.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> forcing the aged Impa guarding the Sealed Temple to create a barrier around the woods and making it impossible to move beyond the Sealed Grounds.<ref>{{Cite|To protect the land here, I closed the {{Color|SS Red|gate leading to the woods}} and drove back the water with a simple seal.|Impa|SS}}</ref> While Link searches for a way into the woods, [[The Imprisoned]] attacks once again. After using his [[Groosenator]] to help Link re-seal The Imprisoned, Groose gets the idea to use it to launch Link into the woods.<ref>{{Cite|See, that last battle with big ugly got me thinking about other ways to use the {{Color|SS Red|Groosenator}}! Just climb into that bad boy, and I'll give you a "lift" over to the woods, all right?|Groose|SS}}</ref> Once there, Link must find Faron. After finding Faron at the Great Tree, she instructs Link to collect the various [[Tadtone]]s scattered across the submerged area.<ref>{{Cite|As you just saw, I have split the melody you seek among my many Tadtones and I have hidden them around the forest.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you wish to earn my favor, gather the {{Color|SS Red|Tadtones}} and return to me.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> When he has successfully found all of the Tadtones, Faron teaches Link her part of the "Song of the Hero" and lowers the water level so that he and the Kikwi are able to make their way around Faron Woods normally again.
