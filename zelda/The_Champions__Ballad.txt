{{Term/Store|''The Champions' Ballad''||Series, BotW}} is the second downloadable content pack for {{BotW}},<ref>{{Cite Web|url=https://www.zelda.com/breath-of-the-wild/news/the-champions-ballad-new-trailer/|title=The Champions' Ballad is here|site=Nintendo}}</ref> released on December 7, 2017 following a full reveal of the pack at The Game Awards. It is available only as part of the game's [[Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass|Expansion Pass]].

==Overview==
{{TCB|-}} introduces a new {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, as well as several {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} to obtain new {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}}. After freeing the four {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} and speaking to the four leaders of each respective tribe, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is telepathically contacted by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link|display=Zelda}} who tells him to return to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. After inserting the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} in the pedestal there, a {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} known as {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|link|display=Maz Koshia}} speaks to him. He promises {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he can prove his worth, he may tame a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} of his own. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|display=Maz Koshia}} presents {{Term|BotW|Link}} with the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator|link}} and instructs him to use it to defeat the {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} present in four different {{Plural|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}}. Once each {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|display=Encampment}} has been cleared, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} appears. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has spoken to the {{Plural|BotW|Monk}} in each {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, the {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} splits into four orbs and spread across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, causing large {{Plural|BotW|Monument|link}} to rise from the ground.

The {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} activate in {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s presence, displaying maps revealing the locations of further trials. {{Term|BotW|Link}} encounters {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} at each of the {{Plural|BotW|Monument}}, who sings for him verses of his master's "{{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song|link}}" about the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}. In each of the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives an Emblem specific to a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}. After obtaining three of each, he is able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Illusory Realm|link}} and battle once again against the corresponding {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon}} afflicting the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|display=Beast}}. After besting the monster, {{Term|BotW|Link}} experiences a vision of each {{Term|BotW|Champions}}'s memory of the moment when {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} requested them to pilot a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}.

After defeating the four {{Plural|BotW|Phantom Ganon}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is instructed to return to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}} and place the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} once again into the pedestal. The chamber seals itself and descends deep into a labyrinth; a {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|display=Maz Koshia}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to navigate the labyrinth and activate four {{Plural|BotW|Terminal|link}} that control a lock on a large stone door at the rear of the {{Term|BotW|Dungeon}}. Once the four locks are disengaged, {{Term|BotW|Link}} proceeds through the opened door to find {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|display=Maz Koshia}} himself meditating on his pedestal. Before {{Term|BotW|Link}} can report his completion of the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|display=Trial}}, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|display=Maz Koshia}} rises and reveals there is to be one final trial. He transports the two of them to a floating platform in the sky and announces that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must battle and defeat the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} himself.

Once defeated, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} alters the layout of the floating platform to present {{Term|BotW|Link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}. He allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to acquire the {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to summon it before transporting him outside the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} encounters {{Term|BotW|Kass}} once again, who reveals that he has finished the verses of his master's {{Term|BotW|Song}} and plays to {{Term|BotW|Link}} the finished ballad. While listening to the {{Term|BotW|Song}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} experiences one final {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} of the day he and his fellow {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} were officially inaugurated at {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} by {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}} culminates with {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} taking a photograph of them and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} with the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}. {{Term|BotW|Kass}} announces that he finally understands the true power of music, and gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} the photo which he found amongst his teacher's belongings. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}'s voice speaks to {{Term|BotW|Link}} once again, congratulating him on conquering all of the trials. She informs him that he is now truly powerful enough to finally defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.

==New Content==
===Quests===
* Five {{Plural|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, encompassing a new storyline. Completing each {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|display=Quest}} unlocks an {{Term|BotW|Memory|link|display=EX Recovered Memory}} associated with each of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} and reduces the cooldown period for their respective ability.
** "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" is the overarching Quest, and unlocks {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link|display=Memory}} after completion.
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}" — {{Term|BotW|Link}} is rewarded with {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|link|display=Revali's Gale +}}
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}" — {{Term|BotW|Link}} is rewarded with  {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Grace|link|display=Mipha's Grace +}}
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" — {{Term|BotW|Link}} is rewarded with  {{Term|BotW|Daruk's Protection|link|display=Daruk's Protection +}}
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" — {{Term|BotW|Link}} is rewarded with  {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury|link|display=Urbosa's Fury +}}
* Six {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} to acquire new {{Plural|BotW|Item}}. Diaries listing the locations for these {{Plural|BotW|Item}} have been added to the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}.
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Ancient Horse Rumors|link}} — {{Term|BotW|Ancient Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ancient Saddle|link}}
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Royal Guard Rumors|link}} — {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set|link}}
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Merchant Hood|link}} — {{Term|BotW|Ravio's Hood|link}}
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Garb of Winds|link}}" — {{Term|BotW|Island Lobster Shirt|link}}
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Usurper King|link}}" — {{Term|BotW|Zant's Helmet|link}}
** "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Dark Armor|link}}" — {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Set|link}}

===Items===
* A {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} can be obtained upon completing the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} that spawns a new type of mount; the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero}} is considered a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|link}}, and can be spawned almost anywhere. It must be fueled with {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}.
* A new {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}; the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator|link}}. It will kill anything with one hit but also causes {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be killed with one hit from any enemy or hazard. It can only be used during the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial}} during the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}.
* Four new {{Plural|BotW|Key Item}} that are obtained during the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}; {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rudania's Emblem|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Naboris's Emblem|link}}. These Emblems are used for going into the {{Term|BotW|Illusory Realm|link}} to fight the {{Plural|BotW|Phantom Ganon}} again.
* {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} will reward {{Term|BotW|Link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Picture of the Champions|link}} after completing the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}. The {{Term|BotW|Picture of the Champions|display=Picture}} can be placed on a wall of {{Term|BotW|Link's House|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}.
* Four more {{Plural|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} can be obtained from the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} added to the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, allowing for one additional {{Term|BotW|Heart Container|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Stamina Vessel|link}}. The other new {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrines}} reward {{Term|BotW|Link}} with Emblems instead of {{Plural|BotW|Spirit Orb}}.

===Locations===
* A single {{Term|BotW|Dungeon|link}}; the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}}. Unlike the other {{Plural|BotW|Dungeon}} in the game, the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}} can be revisited at any time after completing the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.
* 16 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} have been introduced to the world, though all of them are initially hidden until certain tasks are carried out during the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Etsu Korima Shrine|link|display=Etsu Korima}}, {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah Shrine|link|display=Rohta Chigah}}, {{Term|BotW|Ruvo Korbah Shrine|link|display=Ruvo Korbah}}, and {{Term|BotW|Yowaka Ita Shrine|link|display=Yowaka Ita Shrines}} are located on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Kiah Toza Shrine|link|display=Kiah Toza}}, {{Term|BotW|Noe Rajee Shrine|link|display=Noe Rajee}}, and {{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar Shrine|link|display=Shira Gomar Shrines}} are in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia Shrine|link|display=Kee Dafunia}}, {{Term|BotW|Mah Eliya Shrine|link|display=Mah Eliya}}, and {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda Shrine|link|display=Sato Koda Shrines}} are in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna Shrine|link|display=Kamia Omuna}}, {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika Shrine|link|display=Rinu Honika}}, and {{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun Shrine|link|display=Sharo Lun Shrines}} are in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Keive Tala Shrine|link|display=Keive Tala}}, {{Term|BotW|Kihiro Moh Shrine|link|display=Kihiro Moh}}, and {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri Shrine|link|display=Takama Shiri Shrines}} are in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region}}.
* Four {{Plural|BotW|Monument|link}} have been introduced to the world, though they do not appear until the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial}} is completed during the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}. They contain maps revealing the locations of the new {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. It is impossible to warp to these {{Plural|BotW|Monument}}.

===Enemies===
* A {{Term|BotW|Boss|link}} for the new {{Term|BotW|Dungeon|link}}; {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|link}}. He can be fought again at any time after the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} by simply returning to him in the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}}.
* Two overworld {{Plural|BotW|Boss}}; {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Lake Darman|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Molduking|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. Defeating them both is required during the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}.
* The four {{Plural|BotW|Phantom Ganon}}, {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Waterblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} must be battled again during the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}, but can now be fought again at any time after the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|display=Quest}} is complete by approaching their respective {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.

===Miscellaenous===
* An icon for the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}} will appear on the loading screen alongside the other {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} once the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} has been completed.
* Four diaries have been added to the world, containing a deep insight into the lives of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}; {{Term|BotW|The Diary of Revali|link}} can be found in {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|House}} in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Diary|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}} of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Daruk's Training Journal|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}}'s {{Term|BotW|House}} in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Diary|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}'s private quarters in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.
* Many characters have new dialogue and may be found at different locations during the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}.

== Trivia ==

* The rematch of the four Phantom Ganons is a homage of [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|Wind Waker]]'s rematch of the 4 bosses in Ganon's castle. The area is rendered in greyscale and that Link's items are restricted.
