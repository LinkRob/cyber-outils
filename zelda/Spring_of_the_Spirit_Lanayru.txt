The {{Term/Store|Spring of the Spirit Lanayru||Series, TP, TPHD}},<ref>{{Cite|Spring of the Spirit Lanayru|Sign|TPHD}}</ref> also known as '''Lanayru Spring''',<ref name="Praise">{{Cite|In praise of your efforts thus far, I will release fairies to {{Color|TPHD Red|Lanayru Spring}}.|Great Fairy|TPHD}}</ref> is a location in {{TP}}. It is situated in [[Lake Hylia]], and serves as the home of [[Lanayru (Spirit)|Lanayru]], the fourth [[Spirit of Light]]. This {{Term|TPHD|Spring (Location)|link}} is popular for [[Hylian]] citizens since it's here they can send prayers to the Spirit of Light.<ref>{{Cite|We've had a ton of complaints from citizens who can't send prayers to the spring spirit of Lake Hylia.|Soldier|TP}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
When [[Link]] first walks into the Spring of the Spirit Lanayru as a [[Wolf Link|Wolf]], the [[Light Spirit]], [[Lanayru]] will ask that the young hero defeat the [[Shadow Insect]]s and retrieve the [[Tears of Light]] from them, in order to return light to the [[Lanayru Province]]. Doing so will restore Lanayru's powers, who will tell Link that the next [[Fused Shadow]] rests in the [[Lakebed Temple]].<ref>{{Cite|O hero chosen by the gods... The dark power that you seek... It waits in a temple set in the bed of Lake Hylia.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> Before Link departs, however, Lanayru warns him about the true power of the Fused Shadow as well as the [[Sacred Realm]], the [[Triforce]], and the [[Interloper War]] caused by the [[Twili]].<ref>{{Cite|...But before seeking it, you must now bear witness to something...and never forget it. You must know that it was the will of the goddesses that we lock away the forbidden power...|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref>

Upon completing the Lakebed Temple, Link is [[Warping|Warp]]ed to the Spring of the Spirit Lanayru and is immediately confronted by [[Zant]]. Although Lanayru tries to stop Zant, he easily bests the Light Spirit, causing the spring to be covered in [[Twilight]] yet again and thus transforming Link into a Wolf. Zant tries to persuade [[Midna]] to join his side, but after she refuses, he holds her up in front of Lanayru, who gravely injures Midna with its light. Immediately after this, Link and Midna are Warped out of the spring and into [[Hyrule Field]].

In the [[Cave of Ordeals]], when Link reaches the 40th floor, the [[Great Fairy]] will reward the young hero by releasing recovery [[Fairy|Fairies]] into the Spring of the Spirit Lanayru.<ref name="Praise"/> Reaching the last floor of the Cave will allow Link to talk to the Great Fairy at the Spring and receive unlimited [[Great Fairy's Tears]], provided that he does not already have a [[Bottle]] full of them.<ref>{{Cite|If ever you need my assistance again, visit any spring.|Great Fairy|TP}}</ref>

Link can also obtain a [[Piece of Heart]] by using the [[Clawshot]] to climb the vines on the east side and thus be able to reach a door that houses the prize behind it.
