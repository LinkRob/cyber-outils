The '''Charging Horn''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Charging Horn|A charm made of strong horn. Horses are excited by it, powering up their charge.<ref name="Charging Horn">{{Cite|A charm made of strong horn. Horses are excited by it, powering up their charge.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Charging Horn is rewarded for completing the [[Flag Race|Flag Race’s]] “long course” [[Mini-Game]] in 40 seconds or less. When equipped, it increases damage dealt while riding a horse.
