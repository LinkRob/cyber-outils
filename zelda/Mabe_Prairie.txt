{{Term/Store|Mabe Prairie||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Mabe Prairie'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Mabe Prairie is located northeast of the [[Mabe Village Ruins]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. It is bordered by [[Romani Plains]] and the [[Applean Forest]] to the north and the [[Sacred Ground Ruins]] to the west.

Several remains of [[Guardian#Breath of the Wild|Guardian]]s can be found throughout the Prairie. They can be searched for [[Ancient Material]]s.
