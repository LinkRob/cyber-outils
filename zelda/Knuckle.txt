{{Term/Store|Knuckle||Series, TWW, TWWHD, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259{{Exp Game|TWW}} & 270{{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW|-}}, Knuckle originally left [[Tingle Island]] because he got angry at [[Tingle]] for writing "For Tingle Only" on a [[Sign]] next to [[Tingle Tower]], which Knuckle built when he was ten years old.<ref>{{Cite|Don't play dumb! Why'd you doodle on Tingle Tower? I built that thing!|Knuckle|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What, that "For Tingle Only" sign I wrote? Sorry! Please forgive me!|Tingle|TWW}}</ref><ref name="TWWHD Figurine"/> He can only be found by using the [[Tingle Tuner]]; [[Link]] can find him by going to [[Outset Island]] and stepping into the boats on the beach. Knuckle will appear on the [[Game Boy Advance]] screen and get into an argument with Tingle, which ends with him commanding Link to visit various places on the Island.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmph! Well, I might consider it if the fairy will do as I ask...[...]Just don't open any doors before you finish doing what I tell you! You'll fail if you do!|Knuckle|TWW}}</ref> After Link completes this, Knuckle gets annoyed and gives Link the [[Hand-Me-Down Tingle Tuner]] before returning to Tingle Island.<ref>{{Cite|Uggghhh...! Enough! Enough! I've lost the contest on this island! What can I do? Oh! I'll give you the Hand-Me-Down Tuner! Ugh. I'm going back home.|Knuckle|TWW}}</ref> From then on, he stands guard rather than helping [[Ankle]] and [[David Jr.]] spin the top of the Tower.<ref>{{Cite|That guy's younger brother down below... That blue guy... He doesn't help out at all! He just hangs out down there, standing guard and making sure no one writes any graffiti on Tingle Tower! That's all! No manual labor whatsoever!|David Jr.|TWW}}</ref> Unlike Tingle, he wears blue and the mole on his face is on the right side rather than the left. In addition, the rosy patches on his cheeks aren't filled in.

In {{TWWHD}}, he appears on Tingle Island when all five of the [[Tingle Statue]]s have been collected. Ankle gives Link [[Rupee]]s for finding the statues instead, (50 per statue plus another 500 for finding them all) since Knuckle isn't present until they have been collected. Additionally, his [[Figurines in The Wind Waker|Figurine]] is required in order to complete the [[Nintendo Gallery]], unlike in the original version of the game. A reference to this fact was added to the description of Knuckle's figurine.<ref name="TWWHD Figurine"/>

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Knuckle}} appears in {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}} in {{TMC}}, and is not accessible until {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}}. Like the other {{Term|TMC|Tingle Siblings?|link}}, he was given a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} by {{Term|TMC|Tingle|link}} that he will {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} with {{Term|TMC|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Mr. Fairy! I've been holding on to this, just like {{Color|TMC Green|my brother Tingle}} asked! And I have {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone pieces}}! Want to try and {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse}} with me?|Knuckle|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} successfully {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with him,<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Yeah, let's see if they fit!|Knuckle|TMC}}</ref> the southwestern {{Term|TMC|Tree|link}} in a group of four {{Plural|TMC|Tree}} in {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}} will open, leading to a room with a {{Term|TMC|Switch|link}} connected to a {{Term|TMC|Torchlight|link}} and a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with all four of the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Siblings?|link}}, the final {{Term|TMC|Switch}} will cause a {{Term|TMC|Ladder|link}} to descend, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to open a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} containing the {{Term|TMC|Magical Boomerang|link}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}, he says that he does not understand why {{Term|TMC|David Jr.|link}} dresses like him and his brothers.<ref>{{Cite|All of us brothers look alike, but the one in white isn't really our brother. What I can't figure out is why he tries to dress up like us, though...|Knuckle|TMC}}</ref> When spoken to afterwards, {{Term|TMC|Knuckle}} will tell {{Term|TMC|Link}} how many {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} he has left to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}}.<ref>{{Cite|How many have you not found yet? Let's see... XX left to go! You haven't seen anything yet! There are tons out there still!|Knuckle|TMC}}</ref> He, along with the rest of his brothers, believes that {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} will help him meet {{Plural|TMC|Fairy|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They believe fusing Kinstones will help them meet fairies, so they are recording Kinstone data on their maps.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>
