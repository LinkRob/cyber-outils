{{Term/Store|Scimitar of Twilight|Scimitars of Twilight|Series, HW|plural}} are recurring objects and items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Weapon obtained:<br/>{{Color|HW Green|Scimitars of Twilight}}!<br/><br/>Curved blades birthed from Twilight magic. A gauge fills up with each Combo Attack you connect. Use the {{Color|HW Green|Strong Attack button}} to expend the gauge and bombard your foes with energy.|Battle Results|HW}}</ref> The Scimitar of Twilight goes unnamed in {{TP}}. Despite being classified as a [[Scimitars|Scimitar]], it does not share the definitive characteristics of the real-world weapon.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
When {{Term|TP|Zant|link}} confronts [[Link]] in [[Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|Arbiter's Grounds]], he stabs the Scimitar into the skull of the fallen beast Stallord, awakening the once-dead creature, and beginning the fourth boss battle. It is unclear if the Scimitar of Twilight has any other powers, as it is not used or seen for the remainder of the game. It is possible that it gave Stallord many of its powers, such as calling upon [[Staltroop]]s and flying after losing its body. Because the Scimitar is impaled into Stallord's head, it contributes to the heavy amount of damage the beast takes during its [[Critical Point]] in the second half of the battle after its spine is destroyed.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
The Scimitars of Twilight appear as Zant's third level [[Scimitars]] in {{HW}}. This version of the Weapon is identical to the Scimitar of {{TP}}, except that it comes in pairs and has blue markings, as opposed to the red markings that appear in the former game.
{{Noncanon|End}}
