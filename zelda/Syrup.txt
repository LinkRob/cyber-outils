{{Term/Store|Syrup||Series, ALttP, LA, LADX, OoS, OoA, TMC}} is a recurring character in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228,{{Exp Game|ALttP}} 233,{{Exp Game|LA}} 250,{{Exp Game|OoS}} 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}} & 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Syrup appears for the first time in {{ALttP}}, just outside her [[Magic Shop]] near [[Zora's River]], where she sells her Red, Green, and Blue [[Potion]]s. [[Maple]] serves as the shopkeeper for the potion shop, but only in the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|Game Boy Advance version]] of the game (a [[Hooded Shopkeeper]] is found in her place in the original version, presumably as her apprentice). Syrup herself will be stirring her "mushroom brew".<ref>{{Cite|Double, double toil and trouble, fire burn and cauldron bubble... Making mushroom brew, I am...|Syrup|ALttP}}</ref> [[Link]] can give her the [[Magic Mushroom]] he found in the [[Lost Woods]] to help her complete her brew,<ref>{{Cite|Mmmmm... The smell of rotten fruit... If you give me that Mushroom, I can finish my brew.|Syrup|ALttP}}</ref> the final product being [[Magic Powder]]. When Link returns to the Magic Shop, he can pick up the Magic Powder held by the apprentice. 

==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, Syrup lives inside her [[Witch's Hut|Hut]] on the [[Koholint Prairie]]. She will make Link some [[Magic Powder]] once he gives her the [[Sleepy Toadstool]] he found in the [[Mysterious Woods]].<ref>{{Cite|Ahhh... It has the Sleepy Toadstool, it does! We'll mix it up something in a jiffy, we will! |Syrup|LADX}}</ref> She will also teach Link how to use the Powder. Syrup will replenish Link's supply Magic Powder whenever needed, so long as he provides more Toadstools.<ref>{{Cite|Good job! Use it on your enemies and see what happens. If you run out, go to the forest, pick some mushrooms, and I will make you more. |Syrup|LADX}}</ref>

==={{OoS|-}}===
Syrup resides in the [[Sunken City]] in {{OoS}}. To reach her hut, it must be winter in the city, as the [[Snow]] creates a slope leading to the otherwise inaccessible rise on which the hut is built. At first, Syrup will not have anything on sale in her shop, as she does not have the ingredients to make her Magic Potion.<ref>{{Cite|If Maple were here, I'd send her to Mt. Cucco for Mushrooms, but since she hasn't come back, I can't make my Magic Potion!|Syrup|OoS}}</ref> However, as part of the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}, Link must trade her the [[Mushroom]] he obtained from [[Talon]] atop Mount Cucco in exchange for her [[Wooden Bird]]. With the mushroom, Syrup is able to make her Magic Potion, allowing her to open her shop.<ref>{{Cite|Ee, hee, hee! I can finally make my Magic Potion. Guess I'll open the shop soon.|Syrup|OoS}}</ref>

===={{OoA|-}}====
In {{OoA}}, her hut is located in the north-eastern corner of the [[Yoll Graveyard]] in the present. The hut is inaccessible without the [[Flippers]]. The Magic Potion Link obtains from Syrup can be used to cure the [[King Zora]] of his illness,<ref>{{Cite|We believe we need a special medicine called a Magic Potion to cure King Zora's illness.|Zora|OoA}}</ref> saving his life and ultimately allowing him to enter [[Jabu-Jabu's Belly]], the next-to-last dungeon of the game.

==Trivia==
*One of {{Term|Series|Syrup}}'s quotes is taken from the [[Wikipedia:William Shakespeare|William Shakespeare]] play ''[[Wikipedia:Macbeth|Macbeth]]''.{{Which}} In the play, the line is used by the [[Wikipedia:Three Witches|Three Witches]] to conjure the prophecy of Macbeth's eventual defeat at the hands of [[Wikipedia:Macduff (Macbeth)|Macduff]].
*The {{Term|ALBW|Witch|link}} from {{ALBW}} bears many similarities to {{Term|Series|Syrup}}, as she is also a teacher of a young witch and also lives in a {{Term|ALBW|Witch's House|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
