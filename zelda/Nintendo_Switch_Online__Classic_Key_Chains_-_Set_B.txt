The '''Nintendo Switch Online: Classic Key Chains - Set B''' is a pair of keychains manufactured by [[Nintendo]].

==Overview==
This set of key chains is one of two sets of key chains all themed around games from the [[Nintendo 64]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Add a new dimension of fun with these sets of key chains inspired by classic Nintendo 64 games. Now available as My Nintendo physical rewards!|author= Nintendo|published= March 18, 2022|retrieved= April 24, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/news/f5490393fa36d385|title= Boost your style with classic Nintendo 64 keychains—redeem your Platinum Points now!|site= My Nintendo|type= }}</ref> The first set, Set A, features two key chains themed after {{Smw|Mario Kart 64}} and {{Lw|Star Fox 64}} while Set B features two key chains themed after {{Smw|Super Mario 64}} and {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Pretend that your other ride is a kart or an Arwing with this set of two key chains featuring box art from the Mario Kart™ 64 and Star Fox™ 64 games.|author= Nintendo|published= March 18, 2022|retrieved= April 24, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/news/f5490393fa36d385|title= Boost your style with classic Nintendo 64 keychains—redeem your Platinum Points now!|site= My Nintendo|type= }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Carry memories of magical worlds with you wherever you go with this set of two key chains featuring box art from the Super Mario™ 64 and The Legend of Zelda™: Ocarina of Time games.|author= Nintendo|published= March 18, 2022|retrieved= April 24, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/news/f5490393fa36d385|title= Boost your style with classic Nintendo 64 keychains—redeem your Platinum Points now!|site= My Nintendo|type= }}</ref> The keychains include double-sided images of the box art for each respective game. Both sets cost 500 My Nintendo Platinum Points to redeem, not including shipping.<ref name="Nintendo Store"/>
