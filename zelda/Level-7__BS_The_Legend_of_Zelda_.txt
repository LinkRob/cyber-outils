'''Level-7''' is the seventh {{Term|BSTLoZ|Dungeon|link}} of {{BSTLoZ}}.

==Entrance==
To access the {{Term|BSTLoZ|Dungeon|link}}, the {{Term|BSTLoZ|Mascot|display=Hero|link}} must make his way through a time-dependent maze involving {{Term|BSTLoZ|Armos|link}} statues and the {{Term|BSTLoZ|Power Bracelet|link}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BSTLoZ|Dungeon|link}}'s layout is in the shape of a skull. The {{Term|BSTLoZ|Red Candle|link}} is hidden in the {{Term|BSTLoZ|Dungeon}}. The {{Term|BSTLoZ|Boss|link}} is a stronger {{Term|BSTLoZ|Aquamentus|link}}.
