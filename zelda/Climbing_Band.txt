The '''Climbing Band''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Climbing Band|A leather band that protects your wrist. Wear it to climb ladders and rock walls faster.<ref name="Climbing Band">{{Cite|A leather band that protects your wrist. Wear it to climb ladders and rock walls faster.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Climbing Band can be bought at the [[Kakariko Village Shop]] for 500 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. Equipping them increases [[Princess Zelda]]’s climbing speed.
