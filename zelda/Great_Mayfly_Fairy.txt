The {{Term/Store|Great Mayfly Fairy||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy}}
The {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy}} is found on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}, on an eastern ledge of the {{Term|TMC|Crenel Wall|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can use a {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}} on the wall near a {{Term|TMC|Sign|link}} that warns against using {{Plural|TMC|Bomb}}.<ref>{{Cite|No bomb throwing!|Sign|TMC}}</ref> Once inside, if {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} throws a {{Term|TMC|Bomb}} into her {{Term|TMC|Great Fairy Fountain|link|display=Fountain}}, she will ask if he threw a {{Term|TMC|Golden Bomb|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Silver Bomb|link}}, or neither.<ref>{{Cite|Did you throw the {{Color|TMC Red|Golden Bomb}} into the spring? Or the {{Color|TMC Red|Silver Bomb}}?|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} gives the honest answer of "Neither", she rewards him with a {{Term|TMC|Big Bomb Bag|link}};<ref>{{Cite|You are honest. I must reward such an honest adventurer with some of my power.|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref> however, if he gives either of the other incorrect answers, she accuses him of lying and takes away all of his {{Plural|TMC|Bomb}}.<ref>{{Cite|You're lying, aren't you? As punishment for your lie, I will reduce your number of bombs to zero.|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref>

==Non-Canonical Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TMC|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the manga, the {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy}} resides in the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, where she performs a song. Her wings had grown out seven days prior to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s arrival, and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} explains that she has sung for seven days and seven nights; when her song ends, she will die. Although {{Term|TMC|Link}} tries to convince her to continue singing, she recognizes her fate and gives him the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}} to aid in his journey.

{{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} appears in the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|display=Temple}} and demands that the {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy}} tell him where he can find the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}}. When he recognizes {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} as his old master, he transforms the {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy}} into a moth to show how much his power has surpassed {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}'s. He explains that the {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy}} will be able to live forever in her new form; however, she also loses her mind and begins destroying the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|display=Temple}} as {{Term|TMC|Link}} and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} escape.
{{Noncanon|End}}
