{{Term/Store|Gooma||Series, TAoL}} is the [[Boss]] of the [[Palace on the Sea]] in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 182}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Gooma is a giant with a spike helmet that swings a spiked [[Ball and Chain]] at [[Link]]. Wearing no armor, its body is vulnerable, but not its head. To defeat Gooma, Link must dodge the spiked ball that it throws at him and hit Gooma with his sword.

==Trivia==
*This boss does not appear in the Japanese version of the game, with a second appearance of [[Jermafenser]] in its place.
*Gooma's artwork was intended for [[Guma]] in the Japanese version of the game before localization.
