{{Term/Store|Rosa||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Rosa'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 250{{Exp Game|OoS}} & 254{{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
Rosa is an orange-cloaked, ribbon-wearing [[Subrosian]] first met after finishing the [[Gnarled Root Dungeon]]. As [[Link]] begins his search for the [[Rod of Seasons]], he stumbles across Rosa, who is shouting about the [[Temple of Seasons]] falling into Subrosia. As she does not want people following her back to Subrosia, Rosa does her best to make sure nobody follows her.

Later on, Link meets Rosa again, giving her a new [[Ribbon#Oracle of Seasons|ribbon]] as she had recently lost hers.<ref name="Ribbon"/> Grateful, Rosa decides that she will go out with Link, enabling him to use her key to unlock locked doors across Subrosia, including the [[Temple of Seasons|Tower of Summer]].<ref>{{Cite|Rosa's key can open any door, even ones in the "temple."|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> Afterwards, Rosa will follow Link around Subrosia anytime he asks her. She will not, however, leave Subrosia, leaving Link if he does so. She will also leave if Link enters the [[Treasure Grove]], the [[House of Pirates]], or the sidesrolling tunnel to the Tower of Spring.<ref>{{Cite|I had so much fun. I hope you'll ask me out again.|Rosa|OoS}}</ref>

Rosa is considered very attractive among their race.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so jealous! I want to date Rosa!!!|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> Many Subrosians express shock, or jealousy if Link talks to them while traveling with Rosa.<ref>{{Cite|You and Rosa? No way! You're not on a date, are you?!?|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref>

==={{OoA|-}}===
In a [[Linked Game]] of {{OoA}}, Rosa appears on [[Crescent Island]]. She helps Link get his [[Shovel]] back from the [[Tokay]]. However, she will not follow Link around, telling him her trip's theme is that of "the independent woman."<ref>{{Cite|Actually, the theme of my trip is "the independent woman," so you have to wait for another date.|Rosa|OoA}}</ref> She later appears at the [[Goron Dance]] Hall in [[Rolling Ridge]], where she comments that she believes the Subrosian Dance is cooler.<ref>{{Cite|Hi, Link! The Goron Dance is all the rage in these mountains! But the Subrosian Dance is way cooler. These Gorons have a ways to go!|Rosa|OoA}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Rosa's Japanese name is {{Exp|Urara|ウララ}}, a popular Japanese girl name meaning "beauty" that also functions as a pun on her race's name, which is {{Exp|Ūra|ウーラ}}. Because the race received another name in the Western world to preserve the meaning of "hidden," the Ūra Urara became the Subrosian Rosa to preserve the pun.
