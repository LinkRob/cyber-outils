{{Term/Store|Kima||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Kima|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kima}} can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}}. He wears a {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Helm|link}} over his head to protect himself from the {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. He spends his days searching near the edges of the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|display=Mine}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are there any over here?|Kima|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} remarks that that he appeared at the perfect time.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hey! Perfect!<br/>You showed up at just the right time.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> He admits that he has just returned from {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, but he forgot to bring any souvenirs for the trip.<ref>{{Cite|I'm on my way back from {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}} but forgot to buy souvenirs.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> While he wants to catch some {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard|link}} to bring back, hw has been having trouble due to their speed.<ref>{{Cite|I wanted to capture some of those {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}} that live here on Death Mountain, but those suckers are quick.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> Judging that {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears quick, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} asks if he could bring him 10 {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}} in exchange for a piece of {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Say, you look pretty speedy. I bet you could catch a ton! If you bring me {{Color|BotW Red|10}}, I'll even give you some heat-resistant armor. So what do you say, buddy? Help a pal out of a jam by capturing me some {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}}?|Kima|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to help him, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} resolves to try harder, even though he has bad knees.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you aren't up for it? I guess I'll just have to try harder. I mean, my knees are pretty bad, but I'll figure it out.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} informs him that he may be able to find the {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Lizard|display=Lizards}} around the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Leave it to me!] Great! Thanks, buddy! Just {{Color|BotW Red|10}} {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}} should be enough. You can probably find some right around here!|Kima|BotW}}</ref> After he finishes speaking, the "{{Term|BotW|Fireproof Lizard Roundup|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has gathered enough {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Kima}} to trade them.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, look at all them {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}}! You actually managed to chase down {{Color|BotW Red|10}} of those suckers?! Well, a deal's a deal. Hand them over and I'll give you this heat-resistant armor.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to give them to {{Term|BotW|Kima}}, he will be given {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Armor|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|[Here you go.] Thanks, buddy! Here, take this.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> Noticing {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s apparent disappointment, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} realizes that he was expected his {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Helm}} instead, and he explains that he can buy his own in {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|...Hmm? Something the matter, buddy? My helmet? Sorry, it ain't for lending! It's too expensive for that. You can buy your own in {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> He directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} north, which is where he can find {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|To get to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}, head north from here. It's the fifth stop up the trail.|Kima|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kima}} remarks that it is useful to have extra souvenirs on hand.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure you'd agree, buddy, that it's always handy to have some spare souvenirs on hand.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has more {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}}, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} offers to buy three of them for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So if you come across any more {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}}, I'll buy them off you at {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} for {{Color|BotW Red|three}}.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> With both {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}} and {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} states that he will be able to make {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And if I get my hands on some {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}} and some {{Color|BotW Blue|monster parts}}... I can make some {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixirs}} to boot!|Kima|BotW}}</ref> When he finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will end.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine}} to sell {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}} to {{Term|BotW|Kima}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you caught any more {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}}? ...One...two...{{Color|BotW Red|three}} {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}}. Very nice. I'll buy these {{Color|BotW Red|three}} for {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. Do we have a deal?|Kima|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to sell to him, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}} in return.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure.] Thank you! And here you go...|Kima|BotW}}</ref> He then informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he can make {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Elixir}} by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}} and {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} together.<ref>{{Cite|If you mix {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}} with {{Color|BotW Blue|monster parts}}, you can make {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixir}}!|Kima|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to sell, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} will be disappointed, but he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back if he changes his mind.<ref>{{Cite|[They're not for sale.] You don't want to sell them to me? That's to bad... If you change your mind, come on back.|Kima|BotW}}</ref>
