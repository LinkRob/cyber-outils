{{Plural|BotW|Swallow Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} favored among {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} warriors.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Rito Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which consists of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that are made and used by the {{Plural|BotW|Rito}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> Engineered for aerial combat, the bowstring is able to be drawn faster than those of normal {{Plural|BotW|Bow}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be found most commonly in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, but some are scattered around the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, either in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} or being used by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. Scanning the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Swallow Bow}}.

If the {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow|link}} is ever broken, {{Term|BotW|Harth|link}} can remake it with five bundles of {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}}, one {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Swallow Bow}}.<ref>{{Cite|I cannot help but notice that your {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Eagle Bow}} is missing.|Harth|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you want me to make another one, I will need a {{Color|BotW Blue|swallow bow}}, a {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|five}} bundles of {{Color|BotW Blue|wood}}.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> A {{Term|BotW|Swallow Bow}} can be found on the table next to {{Term|BotW|Harth}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to take it, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} will start to scold him.<ref>{{Cite|Hey...|Harth|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} apologizes, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} decides to let him keep it.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm sorry!] Heh... Take it.<br/>Not like I can use it in my condition.|Harth|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[I'm sorry!] Sure, go ahead and take it. Consider it a thanks for conquering Medoh.|Harth|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Swallow Bow}} are made of wood, and they will therefore burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Swallow Bow}} cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
