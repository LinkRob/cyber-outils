{{Term/Store|Spirit Gem|Spirit Gems|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref name="PH Guide 8">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|guide= Prima|page= 8}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|PH|Spirit Gem}} are hidden and can be found by opening {{Plural|PH|Treasure Chest|link}}, solving puzzles, purchasing, checking the mail, or completing certain events. There are three in total: {{Plural|PH|Power Gem|link|display= Power}}, {{Plural|PH|Wisdom Gem|link|display= Wisdom}}, and {{Plural|PH|Courage Gem|link|display= Courage}}, and each has a different effect depending on which {{Term|PH|Spirit|link}} is equipped, and of those there are 20 total {{Plural|PH|Spirit Gem}} of each version to collect over the expanse of the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}}.

For the {{Plural|PH|Spirit Gem}} to have any effect, they must first be taken to {{Term|PH|Spirit Island|link}}. When {{Term|PH|Link|link}} collects ten of a certain {{Term|PH|Spirit Gem}} type, he sees an ability enhancement on the part of his {{Term|PH|Sword|link}} or in his physical skill set,<ref name="PH Guide 8"/> however, upon collecting all 20, {{Term|PH|Link}} is upgraded with the full extent of the {{Term|PH|Spirit}}'s power.

===Power Gem===
{{Main|Power Gem}}
[[File:PH Power Gem Model.png|left|40px]]
By collecting {{Plural|PH|Power Gem}}, the {{Term|LA|Leaf|link|display= Spirit of Power}} ignites {{Term|PH|Link}}'s {{Term|PH|Sword|link}} and increases its attack power.
{{Clear}}

===Wisdom Gem===
{{Main|Wisdom Gem}}
[[File:PH Wisdom Gem Model.png|left|40px]]
By collecting 10 {{Plural|PH|Wisdom Gem}}, the {{Term|LA|Neri|link|display= Spirit of Wisdom}} increases {{Term|PH|Link}}'s defense while allowing him to stun weaker enemies by walking into them. Additionally, less time will be lost from the {{Term|PH|Phantom Hourglass (Item)|link}} when hit by a {{Term|PH|Phantom|link}}.
{{Clear}}

===Courage Gem===
{{Main|Courage Gem}}
[[File:PH Courage Gem Model.png|left|40px]]
By collecting 10 {{Plural|PH|Courage Gem}}, the {{Term|LA|Ciela|link|display= Spirit of Courage}} allows {{Term|PH|Link}} to shoot {{Plural|PH|Sword Beam|link}}. Getting all 20 doubles the width of the {{Plural|PH|Sword Beam}}.
{{Clear}}

==Trivia==
* The central orb of each {{Term|PH|Spirit Gem}} resembles the respective {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}} from {{TWW}}, both in design and color.
