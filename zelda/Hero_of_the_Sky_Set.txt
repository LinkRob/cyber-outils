The {{Term/Store|Hero of the Sky Set||Series, BotW}} is an [[Armor Set]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 193}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Hero of Sky Set is an [[Armor]] set consisting of the [[Cap of the Sky]], [[Tunic of the Sky]], and [[Trousers of the Sky]]. All three pieces can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the Skyward Sword Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain one of the pieces. As the Amiibo can only be scanned once per real-world day, a player must collect the set over three or more real-world days. 

The set can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link|display=Master Sword Beam Up|section=Master Sword Beam Up}} [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set. It resembles Link's outfit from {{SS}}.
