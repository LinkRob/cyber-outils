The {{Term/Store|Fireshield Earring|Fireshield Earrings|Series, SS|plural}} are {{Plural|SS|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|SS|Link|link}} obtains the earrings after successfully passing the test of [[Din (Goddess)|Din]]'s [[Silent Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SS Gold|Fireshield Earrings}}! With these, you can stand any heat without a sweat. This is one of the three sacred gifts left by the goddess.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Welcome back, Master. I conjecture your newly acquired {{Color|SS Gold|Fireshield Earrings}} will allow you to safely travel in extremely hot areas.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Upon entering an extremely hot region such as the [[Volcano Summit]], the Fireshield Earrings will flash red, preventing Link's clothes from catching fire and allowing him to explore the area unhindered.<ref>{{Cite|I am detecting extremely high temperatures in this area. However, your {{Color|SS Gold|Fireshield Earrings}} will allow for extended periods of exploration.|Fi|SS}}</ref> The earrings will also extinguish fire caught on his clothes from an enemy or trap, reducing the damage taken. They do not protect Link from fire-based attacks or direct contact with lava.
