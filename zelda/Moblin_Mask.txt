==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Moblin Mask is a piece of [[Armor]] that can be purchased from [[Kilton]] at his [[Fang and Bone]] shop after freeing one [[Divine Beast]]. It cannot be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], nor can it be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. When worn, [[Link]] can blend in with [[Moblin]]s;<ref name="Inventory"/> however, the Mask will not prevent other enemies in the same area from attacking.
