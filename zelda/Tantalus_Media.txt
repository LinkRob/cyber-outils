'''Tantalus Media''', or simply '''Tantalus''', is an Australian video game developer founded in 1994. Primarily known for their ports of existing titles, Tantalus has co-developed several games for and with [[Nintendo]], including high-definition remakes in {{TLoZ|Series}}.
