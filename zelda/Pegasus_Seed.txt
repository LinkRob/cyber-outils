{{Term/Store|Pegasus Seed|Pegasus Seeds|Series, OoS, OoA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA, FS}}}}</ref>{{Fact}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Pegasus Seeds|Rub a Pegasus Seed on yourself to move faster than normal. If you hit an enemy with one, though, the enemy will stop in its tracks.}}
In {{OoS}}, {{Plural|OoS|Pegasus Seed}} are {{Plural|OoS|Mystical Seed|link}} that grow on {{Plural|OoS|Pegasus Tree|link}} in the Autumn. They cause {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} to run faster and can be used in conjunction with {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather|link}} to jump farther.<ref>{{Cite|Greater speed gives greater distance.|Owl Statue|OoS}}</ref> When shot, they will freeze enemies in their tracks.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Pegasus Seeds|Rub a Pegasus Seed on yourself to move faster than normal. If you hit an enemy with one, though, the enemy will stop in its tracks.}}
In {{OoA}}, {{Plural|OoA|Pegasus Seed}} are {{Plural|OoA|Mystical Seed}} that grow on {{Plural|OoA|Pegasus Tree}} in the Autumn. They cause {{Term|OoA|Link}} to run faster and can be used in conjunction with {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather}} to jump farther. When shot, they will freeze enemies in their tracks.

==={{FS|-}}===
{{Manual|FS|Pegasus Seeds|Grab one of these special seeds to increase your walking speed.}}
In {{FS}}, picking up a {{Term|FS|Pegasus Seed}} will double {{Term|FS|Link}}'s walking speed until the stage is cleared.<ref>{{Cite|The mystical seeds you collect will boost your power by up to two levels. {{Color|FS Orange|Razor Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Razor Seed}} add sword power. {{Color|FS Orange|Armor Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Armor Seed}} add defense. {{Color|FS Orange|Pegasus Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Pegasus Seed}} add speed.|Stray Fairy|FS}}</ref> They can be found by cutting down grass. A maximum of two seeds can be collected by any one Link.
