"{{Term/Store|A Gift from the Monks||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Gift from the Monks}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Ancient Shrine}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} completes the last of the 120 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, the final {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} will tell him that they have prepared something for a true hero.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have at last conquered all of our trials. Having fulfilled our purpose, we monks bestow upon you this... The warrant of a true hero.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} added in {{TCB}} DLC Pack do not count towards this total.}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} is instructed to go to the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Temple|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You must now depart for the Forgotten Temple.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> which activates the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. Like all other {{Plural|BotW|Monk}}, this {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already finished all 120 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he will be able to {{Term|BotW|Warping|link|display=Warp}} directly to {{Term|BotW|Rona Kachta Shrine|link}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Temple}}. Three {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} will appear below the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} inside, which contain the {{Term|BotW|Cap of the Wild|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Tunic of the Wild|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Trousers of the Wild|link}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has collected all three pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
