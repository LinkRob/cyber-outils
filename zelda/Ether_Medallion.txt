The {{Term/Store|Ether Medallion||Series, ALttP}} is an {{Term|ALttP|Item|link}} in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|ALttP|Ether|The Ether Medallion charges your sword with a cold chill that freezes the surrounding {{Typo|enemy|enemies}}. The Ether Medallion has the same effect as the Ice Rod but it works against all the enemies on the screen.}}
The Ether Medallion can be found across a bridge west of the [[Tower of Hera (A Link to the Past)|Tower of Hera]], where a green tablet can be found. [[Link]] requires the [[Book of Mudora]] to translate its message, which instructs him to raise the [[Master Sword]] to the heavens. Once he does so, the lightning-based spell is cast, destroying the tablet as a result. Then, the Ether Medallion falls from the sky into Link's hands.

Ether attacks through the atmosphere, as flying enemies appear to be the most affected. Most enemies who are not destroyed by the initial pulse of lightning are frozen, as if they were struck by the [[Ice Rod]]. The Ether Medallion must be used to clear the rain in the [[Swamp of Evil]] in order to enter [[Misery Mire (A Link to the Past)|Misery Mire]].
