{{Term/Store|Critical Hit|Critical Hits|Series, BotW|plural}} are damage modifiers that Link can perform under certain conditions in {{BotW}}, and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].

==Overview==
Critical Hits are strikes that deal twice the normal damage for a weapon. There are three ways to achieve this effect: hitting an enemy's head or eye with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hit an enemy's head with an arrow to perform a {{Color|BotW Red|Critical Hit}} that deals 2x damage.|N/A|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Critical Hits<br/>Hitting a monster or enemy in the head with an arrow deals double the normal amount of damage.|Tips and Tricks|BotW}}</ref> successfully throwing a weapon at an enemy,<ref>{{Cite|Throw a weapon at an enemy to perform a {{Color|BotW Red|Critical Hit}}, dealing twice the weapon damage.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and breaking a weapon while striking an enemy.<ref>{{Cite|Any attack that causes your weapon to break becomes a {{Color|BotW Red|Critical Hit}}, dealing 2x damage.|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

As well as the above, weapons may be found with a Critical Hit modifier. Executing a combo with these weapons will deal double damage on the final hit.

{{Plural|BotW|Sneakstrike|link}} are a similar phenomenon, which deal more damage by stealthily attacking an enemy from behind.
