'''Snomaul''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Snomaul|A strange and hostile collection of living snowballs, this creature's true body is the head. Snomauls are creatures composed of living snowballs stacked on top of each other. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Snomaul [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.
