The {{Term/Store|Zora Set||Series, BotW}} is an [[Armor]] set in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 180}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Inventory/Set|BotW|Zora Set}}
The Zora Set is an {{Term|BotW|Armor Set|link}} consisting of the {{Term|BotW|Zora Helm|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Zora Armor|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Zora Greaves|link}}. All three pieces are related to 
quests in or near {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}:

*The helm is found in a sunken treasure chest at {{Term|BotW|Toto Lake|link}}, hinted at during the "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}" Side Quest.
*The armor is received from {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}" Main Quest.
*The greaves are obtained as a reward for showing {{Term|BotW|Laflat|link}} a photo of a {{Term|BotW|Lynel|link}} for the "{{Term|BotW|Lynel Safari|link}}" Side Quest.

Each piece increases Link's movement speed while swimming. Furthermore, the Zora Armor enables Link to swim up waterfalls, while the Zora Helm enables Link to perform a Spin Attack while swimming.  If all three pieces are worn and have been upgraded at least twice by a [[Great Fairy]], they provide the Swim Dash Stamina Up set bonus, significantly reducing the stamina cost for dashing or spin attack.
