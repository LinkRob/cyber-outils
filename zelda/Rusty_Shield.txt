{{Plural|BotW|Rusty Shield}} are old {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} that were once potentially used by the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Though they still offer some defense, a majority of their usefulness has been lost to time.<ref name="Inventory"/> These {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Rusty Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of deteriorated {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref> They can commonly be found abandoned in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}.

If a {{Term|BotW|Rock Octorok|link}} inhales a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield}}, it will chew it before spitting out a cleaned counterpart.<ref name="CaC"/> The {{Term|BotW|Shield}} has a 50% chance to be a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Shield|link}}, a 35% chance to be a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Shield|link}}, a 10% chance to be a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Shield|link}}, and a 5% chance to be a {{Term|BotW|Royal Shield|link}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Rusty Shield}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, unlike wooden {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}, {{Plural|BotW|Rusty Shield}} will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
