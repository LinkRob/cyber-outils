{{Term/Store|Katta||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Katta|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Katta}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She guards one of the entrances to {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom|link}} upstairs, and she carries a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially tries to go past, she stops him and tells him that he cannot enter without permission.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Halt!}} Unless you have permission, you'll stay right there! I don't care who you are!|Katta|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has recovered the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Katta}} will allow him to pass. She comments that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must be special to have permission to enter {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s bedroom.<ref>{{Cite|You must be pretty special to have gotten permission from the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}.|Katta|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* {{Term|Series|Katta}} is one of several characters that will hit {{Term|Series|Link}} back if he attacks them with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bring it on!|Katta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You looking to fight?!|Katta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What are you doing?!|Katta|BotW}}</ref> These attacks deal no damage.
