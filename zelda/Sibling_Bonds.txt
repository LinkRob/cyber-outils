{{Term/Store|Sibling Bonds||HWAoC}} is a level 52 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sibling Bonds|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Sibling Bonds}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}. When completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Bass|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hyrule Bass|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}'s objective is to capture three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} within ten minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are lined up near the eastern edge of the map, with one in the north, center, and south. Many {{Plural|HWAoC|Stalkoblin|link}} are scattered throughout the map, along with an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}} stationed in front of the southern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}. The northern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Stalkoblin}}, and when approached, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Stalmoblin|link}} spawn. The central {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, and when approached, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} spawn. Unlike in other parts of the map, there are some {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} near the central {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} as well. The southern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is also filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}}, and when approached, a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|White-Maned Lynel|link}} appear. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured.
