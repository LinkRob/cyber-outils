{{Term/Store|Selmie's Spot||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Selmie's Spot'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Selmie's Spot}} is a small cabin located in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, near [[Shada Naw Shrine]]. Here, a {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} can be played with {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}}. There are 9 shields displayed on the interior walls, which can be pictured using the [[Camera#Breath of the Wild|Camera Rune]] to fill out the [[Hyrule Compendium]]. Additionally, there is a [[Korok]] located on the roof.
