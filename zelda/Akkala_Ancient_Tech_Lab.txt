The {{Term/Store|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|'''Akkala Ancient Tech Lab'''}}|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} is located on the {{Term|BotW|North Akkala Foothill|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}}. It was converted from an abandoned lighthouse near {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}}, and it currently houses {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Jerrin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 285}}</ref> The pair of them operate the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} together.<ref>{{Cite|[You're married?] Yes, indeed! Together we run the {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}. I've got a son, as well!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> It is one of two successors to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Research Institute|link}}, the other being the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.

Following the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} and {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} discovered two sources of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Necluda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We found large amounts of ancient energy in the Necluda and Akkala regions. We could continue our research.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> After a game a rock, paper, scissors, it was determined that {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} would make use of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy|display=Energy}} in {{Term|BotW|Akkala}}, where he would continue his research into {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Just as we had in Hyrule Castle, we could again set ourselves to finding some way to seal Calamity Ganon away. We decided who would go where in the only fair way: rock, paper, scissors. I set out for Akkala soon after.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> With him, he took a {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I returned to Kakariko Village to collect the remaining Guidance Stone, then I immediately set out for Akkala.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> When he came upon the ruined lighthouse, he decided to settle there, turning it into the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}.<ref>{{Cite|In time, I reached the lighthouse in Akkala. What a perfect place to keep an eye on the monsters out there. I unloaded my Guidance Stone and settled into my new home.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref>

At the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} focused his research on {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And so began my life of solitary research... I knew I had to continue to research the ancient soldier gear for Link... He could wake at any time.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Akkala}} is located on {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}}, which is on the opposite side of the {{Term|BotW|North Akkala Foothill}}.<ref>{{Cite|I quickly settled on a routine. First, I would carry the blue flame from Tumlea Heights.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> In order to expedite the process of smelting {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} refined his {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} into the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}}, which he refers to as "Cherry."<ref>{{Cite|It took some significant work, but I was able to successfully remodel the Guidance Stone. Thanks to my effort, it can refine any ancient material inserted into it and offer something in return. I named this machine Cherry, after the first girl I ever loved...|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} was 90, {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} sent {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} to work at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}.<ref>{{Cite|Days and months came and went. Before I even realized it, I'd reached the ripe age of 90. I would have been lost in my work even then... But around that time, a young woman named Jerrin arrived. She'd first trained with Dr. Purah, then Lady Impa had sent her to aid me. She quickly proved herself quite capable.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> She took over the duties of lighting the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|display=Lab}}'s {{Term|BotW|Furnace|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, in addition to remodeling the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}}.<ref>{{Cite|She carried the blue flame from Tumela Heights and even reworked and improved my designs for Cherry.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> After some time, the two of them ended up married.<ref>{{Cite|Her intellect, her spirit... In hindsight, is it any surprise that we eventually ended up married?|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> They had a son together, {{Term|BotW|Granté|link}}, who they raised at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|display=Lab}}.<ref>{{Cite|Some time later, Jerrin gave birth to a baby boy, a beautiful young one who bore all the features of his mother. We decided to name him Granté, and together we passed the time as a happy family at Akkala Ancient Tech Lab.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Granté}} grew, he took over retrieving the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}, but he eventually left after {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} encouraged him to travel.<ref>{{Cite|Granté grew fast, and it wasn't long before he towered over me! He loved to carry the blue flame from Tumlea Heights, calling it a chance to train his body. I could see the curiosity in his eyes, and so I encouraged him to travel the world and see as much as he could. He'd always been independant. But I was still surprised when, the very day I told him to travel, he left!|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref>

As part of the "{{Term|BotW|Robbie's Research|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Purah}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to bring {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material}} to either her or {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}.<ref>{{Cite|You don't have to worry about this until after you see Impa, but I want you to bring me back some {{Color|BotW Red|ancient materials}}! You can find him presiding over the {{Color|BotW Red|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> When he arrives, the {{Term|BotW|Furnace}} outside the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|display=Lab}} will be extinguished, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} must reignite it in order to restore the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}}'s manufacturing of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear}}.<ref>{{Cite|And on that note... Oh... Well, thank you. Yes, quite. Now then, please take this as a gift of my gratitude that {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}} is fixed!|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> Following this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to purchase {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear}} and the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}}, in exchange for {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material}}.<ref>{{Cite|In addition to {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient arrows}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}} can make all kinds of ancient soldier gear. In the chair on top of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find some {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}}. When he interacts with them, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>

==={{TotK|-}}===
Sometime after the events of {{Term|BotW|The Calamity|link}},{{Verify}} Robbie has left the lab, leaving the place abandoned. It was later claimed by the {{Term|TotK|Yiga Clan|link}} and became one of their surface branches. It is used as a depot for supplies and spare {{Term|TotK|Yiga Clan Armor Set|link|display=Yiga Clan attire}}.

If Link tries to enter through the front door, the Yiga occupants will recognize Link{{Fact}} and he gets ambushed by a {{Term|TotK|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} and a {{Term|TotK|Yiga Blademaster|link}}. Alternatively, he may use {{Term|TotK|Ascend|link}} on a tall hanging column in {{Term|TotK|The Depths|link}} that takes him directly inside the tech lab. The occupants will still be inside and he will be taken outside to fight them.<ref>{{Cite|Link! Thought you could sneak past us, did you?! Come out front, and let's settle this!|Yiga Blademaster|TotK}}</ref>

Upon defeating the Yiga occupants, Link finds a {{Term|TotK|Hateno Village|link|display=Hateno}} villager hostage inside named Konba, who was forced to make attires for the Yiga Clan.<ref>{{Cite|The name's Konba. I've got a knack for style and the nimble digits I need to spin my ideas into reality. Alas, that pure talent is why the Yiga Clan kidnapped me and forced me to make their outfits for them.|Konba|TotK}}</ref> In exchange for freeing him, he gives Link a {{Term|TotK|Yiga Clan Armor|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, you should take the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Yiga armor</font> I just finished. Burn it, toss it, do what you want. Just get it away from me.|Konba|TotK}}</ref>
