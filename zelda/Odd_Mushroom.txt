The {{Term/Store|Odd Mushroom|Odd Mushrooms|Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWL}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Odd Mushroom}} is part of the {{Term|OoT3D|Trading Quest|link}}. The {{Term|OoT3D|Master Craftsman's Son|link}} gives it to {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} in exchange for {{Term|OoT3D|Cojiro|link}}. {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} must then deliver the {{Term|OoT3D|Odd Mushroom}} to the {{Term|OoT3D|Granny|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}} to have it be made into the {{Term|OoT3D|Odd Potion|link}}. The {{Term|OoT3D|Odd Mushroom}} goes bad after three minutes or instantly after warping.
