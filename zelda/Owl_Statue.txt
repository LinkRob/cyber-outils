{{Term/Store|Owl Statue|Owl Statues|Series, LA, LADX, MM, MM3D|plural}},{{Exp Game|LA, MM}}<ref name="E 140"/><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS|publisher= Prima|page= 17}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Owl Stone|Owl Stones|OoS|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoS}}<ref>{{Cite|Owl Stones are mystic statues that give advice when a certain seed is placed on them!|Know-It-All Bird|OoS}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TP}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, Owl Statues are scattered across [[Koholint Island]]. They tell [[Link]] information that may be helpful, or may be completely useless.{{Fact}}

In {{LA|DX}}, Owl Statues are also located inside every [[Dungeon]]. They reside on walls with a hole where its beak should be. After Link finds a [[Stone Beak]], he can attach it to an Owl Statue to receive hints on [[Puzzle]]s, enemies, [[Sub-Boss]]es, or the Dungeon's [[Boss]].<ref name="E 140"/> They replace [[Stone Slab]]s from {{LA|-}}, being identical in every way but name and appearance. Link will not understand the Owl Statue's hints until he attaches a Stone Beak to it.<ref>{{Cite|This owl statue is trying to say something, but you can't understand it because it has no beak.|N/A|LADX}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM3D Owl Statue.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Owl Statue from {{MM3D|-}}]]
[[File:MM3D Quill Statue Ancient Castle of Ikana.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Quill Statue from {{MM3D|-}}]]
In {{MM}}, Owl Statues are made in the likeness of [[Kaepora Gaebora]],<ref>{{Cite|Hoo-Hoot! We meet again, fairy child! Have my stone statues been of help?|Kaepora Gaebora|MM}}</ref> who resides in [[Termina]]. After learning the "[[Song of Soaring]]", Link can [[Warping|warp]] to any Owl Statue he has activated; the statues are activated by striking them with a [[Sword]]. They also serve as save points where Link can save and quit, although they are temporary saves, as the only way to fully save is by playing the "[[Song of Time]]." When Link saves at an Owl Statue, an Owl mark will appear next to the game file.

In {{MM3D}}, Owl Statues need to be checked instead of hit with the Sword to activate them, and now permanently save Link's progress. Additionally, some Owl Statues were relocated. '''Quill Statues''', a new type of statue, were also added; these statues allow saving, but cannot be warped to with the "Song of Soaring."<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Thanks to owl statues and quill statues found throughout Termina, you can now save your adventure at any time. But be warned: returning to the dawn of the first day no longer automatically saves your progress.|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= March 23, 2015|url= https://www.nintendo.co.uk/Games/Nintendo-3DS/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Majora-s-Mask-3D-941070.html|title= What's new?|site= Nintendo UK Official Site|type=}}</ref> Quill Statues appear all across Termina, including inside dungeons.

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoA}} and {{OoS}}, giving a [[Mystery Seed]] to a statue will cause it to raise its wings and give advice on a nearby challenge.<ref>{{Cite|Do not forget to give me Mystery Seeds.|Owl Stone|OoS}}</ref>

==={{TP|-}}===
[[File:Owl Character.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Owl Statue from {{TP|-}}]]
In {{TP}}, Owl Statues hold the missing Sky Characters from the [[Ancient Sky Book]].<ref>{{Cite|There are many similar statues around Hyrule, but this one seems to be different. Here, look at the belly... There's something written there, you see? This, too, was in my father's notes... It is called Sky Writing, I believe.|Shad|TP}}</ref> After [[Ilia]]'s memory is restored, [[Shad]], who is studying an Owl Statue in [[Renado]]'s Basement, will see the Ancient Sky Book that Link received from [[Impaz]]. He will then proceed to read a part of the book to restore power to the [[Dominion Rod]]. Link must then search for all remaining statues to find the missing Sky Characters.

In addition to these, five more Owl Statues can be found. The first one is located in Renado's basement near Shad, although it lacks a hole to react to the Dominion Rod. The other two are located in the cemetery of [[Hyrule Castle (Twilight Princess)|Hyrule Castle]]. They do not hide Sky Characters, but allow Link access to a [[Key]]. The final pair are found in the first part of the [[Temple of Time (Dungeon)|Temple of Time]]. One blocks the path to a [[Heart Piece]], while the other hides a [[Poe]].

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Stub|HWDE}}
In {{HW}}, Owl Statues from {{TP|-}} appear as part of [[Princess Zelda|Zelda's]] moveset while wielding the Dominion Rod. Owl Statues based on their {{MM|-}} appearance also appear on the [[Termina Map]] in [[Adventure Mode]]. When a Battle with an Owl Statue is completed, it will open its wings and enable the Battle square (and others around it in a cross shape) to be unaffected if the Map is reset when using the "[[Song of Time]]" [[Item Card]], or if the [[Moon]] falls.

In {{HW|L}}, Owl Statues based on their {{MM|-}} appearance can be used in conjunction with a golden [[Ocarina]]. Warriors can find Statues spread all across a Stage. The Statues can be activated by interacting with them and can be later reached immediately by playing the Ocarina. Playing the Ocarina allows the Warrior to select any of the active Owl Statues and warp to the selected Statue via a cyclone.
{{Noncanon|End}}
