{{Term/Store|Gor Ebizo||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 277}}</ref>

==Biography==
The second-most powerful elder in the [[Goron]] society of [[Death Mountain]], Gor Ebizo is also the oldest looking.<ref name="TPHD Guide 14"/> He is the second Goron elder found in the [[Goron Mines]]. Once [[Link]] finds him, he will give him a [[Key Shard]] which will aid him on his quest to rescue [[Darbus]] inside the mines. His greatest wish is to be able to sell wares to multiple people before he passes away,<ref name="TPHD Guide 14"/> which is possible if Link gives enough donations. He can be seen dancing in the [[Malo Mart]] [[Kakariko Village|Kakariko]] branch later in the game, after Malo Mart has extended into [[Hyrule Castle Town]]. Gor Ebizo appears to have a speech impediment, saying "brudda" instead of brother.

==Trivia==
*Gor Ebizo's appearance is very similar to that of the [[Goron Elder]] from {{MM}}, as both are doddering old Gorons with unusually long hair and very large lips.
