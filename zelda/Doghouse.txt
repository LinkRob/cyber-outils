The {{Term/Store|Doghouse||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a location in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|How I wish I'd receive a visitor carrying a {{Color|LANS Pink|ribbon}} on their person! I would direct them to the {{Color|LANS Blue|doghouse}} on my premises.|Madame MeowMeow|LANS}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Doghouse is a small hut adjoined with [[Madam MeowMeow's House]] in [[Mabe Village]]. It is inhabited by [[CiaoCiao]]. Link can give her the [[Ribbon]] in exchange for the [[Dog Food]], as part of the [[Trading Quest]]. If Link digs in the bottom-right corner of the house, he can find a [[Secret Seashell]].<ref>"You found a Secret Seashell! If you collect a lot of these, there could be something in it for you!" – N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|{{LA|-}} for Nintendo Switch]])</ref>
