==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|OoT3D}}
The {{Term|OoT3D|Spirit Medallion}} is the {{Term|OoT3D|Sage Medallion|link}} that {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} obtains after defeating {{Term|OoT3D|Koume|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Kotake|link}} and their fused form, {{Term|OoT3D|Twinrova|link}}, in the {{Term|OoT3D|Spirit Temple|link}}.<ref name="Get"/> The {{Term|OoT3D|Spirit Medallion}} is given to {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} by the newly awakened {{Term|OoT3D|Sage|link}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Nabooru|link}}. {{Term|OoT3D|Nabooru}} offers it to Link in lieu of the promise she made to him when he was a child.<ref>{{Cite|Kid...
