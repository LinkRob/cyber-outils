The {{Term/Store|Thims Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Thims Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Thims Bridge}} spans the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}.

In the water next to the Bridge is a ring of jutting rocks, which throwing a stone inside will cause a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} to appear, who will then give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
The {{Term|BotW|Thims Bridge}} is named for {{Term|TMC|Smith|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s grandfather from {{TMC}}.
