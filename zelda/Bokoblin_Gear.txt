The {{Term/Store|Bokoblin Gear|Bokoblin Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear}} is a variety of equipment wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} of all types, and it occurs in three tiers of increasing strength and durability. Regular {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear}} is typically wielded by basic {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}. It is formed out of {{Plural|BotW|Tree Branch|link}} and bark and, due to its poor construction, has low durability and attack power.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Boko Club}}<br/>A crude Bokoblin club made to clobber small prey. It's essentially a stick, so its durability is low.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Boko Bow}}<br/>A basic Bokoblin bow made of wood. It's made by taking any tree branch and tying a string to either end, so don't expect much in the way of combat effectiveness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Boko Shield}}<br/>A Bokoblin-made shield created by attaching a handhold to any flat tree bark picked up off the ground. It's pretty shoddy, so don't expect it to last very long.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Its upgraded form, which bears the "Spiked" modifier, is most commonly found wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}}. It is strengthened through the use of spikes of sharpened {{Term|Series|Animal|link}} bone, giving it greater durability and damage output.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Spiked Boko Club}}<br/>A reinforced Bokoblin club made to maximize damage. The sharpened bones jabbed into it make it a brutal weapon.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Spiked Boko Bat}}<br/>After much consideration by Bokoblins on how to improve the Boko bat, they simply attached sharp spikes to it. A skilled fighter can cause immense damage with this.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Spiked Boko Spear}}<br/>A Boko spear enhanced with sharpened animal bones. It's light, easy to use, and deals a decent amount of damage.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Spiked Boko Bow}}<br/>An upgraded Boko Bow bound with animal bone to boost its durability and firepower. Its craftsmanship is sloppy, but it's light and easy to use.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The final variation, designated with the "Dragonbone" modifier, is wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Black Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Bokoblin|link}}. It is reinforced with fossilized bones, which further increases its attack power and durability beyond those of Spiked weapons.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dragonbone Boko Club}}<br/>This Bokoblin club has been reinforced with fossilized bones to maximize clobbering potential. Only the brawniest of Bokoblins can manage its immense weight.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dragonbone Boko Bat}}<br/>Used by only the toughest Bokoblin warriors, this Boko bat has been fortified by fossilized bone. It boasts a high durability and is strong enough to beat down powerful foes.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dragonbone Boko Spear}}<br/>This Boko spear has been strengthened with fossilized bones. The bones are positioned outward so the fangs bite at the opponent. Beware Bokoblins carrying this weapon.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dragon Bone Bow}}<br/>A Boko bow reinforced by fossils. Bokoblins handpicked the materials it's made from, so it boasts a respectable firepower.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dragonbone Shield}}<br/>This Boko shield is reinforced with fossilized bone. Its defensive capabilities are respectable, but its predictably slipshod craftsmanship spells low durability.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} will spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a piece of {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear}}.

All forms of {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear}} are formed around a wooden base. This means that {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} in this archetype will float in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they cannot attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. However, they will burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they are unable to be manipulated through the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
