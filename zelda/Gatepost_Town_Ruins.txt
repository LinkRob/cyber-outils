The {{Term/Store|Gatepost Town Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Gatepost Town Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Gatepost Town Ruins are located in [[Hyrule Field]], close to the former entrance to the [[Great Plateau]]. They are situated at a crossroads at the western end of the [[Forest of Time]]. The [[Hylian]] traveling duo, [[Mils]] and [[Mina]], can be found here, fighting off monsters in search of treasure. When spoken to, Mina informs Link that the Gatepost Town Ruins was once a growing town, though the influx of monsters forced the residents to flee.<ref>{{Cite|This was once an up-and-coming place with lots of folks moving here. But then the monsters drove everyone out. Which means there's plenty of treasure to be had! That's where we come in. We're treasure hunters, searching for a one-in-a-million payday. I'm guessing you are too. So as a tip from one pro to another, stay clear of Hyrule Castle. It's protected by things called {{Color|BotW Red|Guardians}}. If they spot you, all bets are off on whether you survive.|Mina|BotW}}</ref>

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing an [[Emblazoned Shield]] can be found in the southern corner of the building in the Ruins.
