==Features and Overview==
Seven volumes of {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill}} can be found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, all written by {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Greetings from Traysi—your source for the best gossip and news!|Rumor Mill: Volume 3|BotW}}</ref> Each volume covers a topic of local gossip which is discussed and recommended by {{Term|BotW|Traysi}} using a one- to five-star rating.

In {{TMT}} and {{TCB}} DLC Packs, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can also find volumes of an alternate series called {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX|link}} that detail {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} included in the DLCs.
