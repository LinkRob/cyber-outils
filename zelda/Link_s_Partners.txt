Throughout {{TLoZ|Series}}, [[Link]] often has a variety of partners accompanying him on his journey throughout the game. These partners usually provide helpful hints and assistance with completing the game.  

==Epona==
{{Main|Epona}}
'''Epona''' is a horse sometimes owned by Link and sometimes by [[Lon Lon Ranch]]. She has had a number of appearances, but can only be ridden in-game in {{OoT}}, {{MM}}, {{FSA}}, {{TP}}, and {{BotW}}. In {{FSA|-}}, she can only be ridden for a short period of time. She appears in the ''Oracle games'' and {{TMC}}, but is not a playable part of these games. In games such as Ocarina of Time, Majora's Mask, and Twilight Princess, Epona can be called by playing Epona's Song. She allows Link to travel at a faster pace than walking.
{{Clear}}

==Navi==
[[File:OoT Navi.png|right|100px]]
{{Main|Navi}}
'''Navi''' is Link's Fairy partner in {{OoT|-}}. She joins Link at the start of the game at the request of the [[Great Deku Tree]] and helps Link on his quest to defeat [[Ganondorf]]. She leaves for unknown reasons after the threat of Ganon has been lifted.
{{Clear}}

==Tatl==
[[File:MM Tatl Artwork.png|right|100px]]
{{Main|Tatl}}
'''Tatl''' is Link's Fairy partner in {{MM|-}}. She and her brother, [[Tael]], were companions of the [[Skull Kid]], but he left her behind with Link after stealing Link's {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}. She then helps Link to defeat the cursed Majora's Mask and free Skull Kid of its influence. She and her brother stay with the Skull Kid at the end of the game.
{{Clear}}

==Ricky, Dimitri and Moosh==

<option>[[File:OoS Moosh Artwork.png|right|100px|Moosh]]</option>
<option>[[File:OoS Dimitri Artwork.png|right|130px|Dimitri]]</option>

{{Main|Ricky, Dimitri, Moosh}}
'''Ricky''', '''Dimitri''', and '''Moosh''' are Link's three animal companions in {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}. Each has a special ability that will aid Link on his quest: Ricky the kangaroo has powerful legs that allow him to jump onto high ledges in a single bound; Dimitri the [[Dodongo]] is a strong swimmer who can swim against strong currents and even up waterfalls; Moosh is a flying bear that can hover across pitfalls and large gaps. Though he encounters all three of them on his adventure, Link is eventually paired up with one in particular for the remainder of the game after receiving their [[Strange Flute|flute]], which allows Link to summon his chosen partner on command. The landscape of the [[Labrynna|Labrynnian]] [[Nuun Highlands]], and [[Natzu]] in [[Holodrum]], changes to suit the abilities of the animal Link pairs up with.
{{Clear}}

==King of Red Lions==
[[File:TWW Link King of Red Lions Artwork.png|right|150px]]
{{Main|King of Red Lions}}
'''King of Red Lions''', or [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|King Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]], is one of the [[King of Hyrule|Kings of Hyrule]], and appears to be a ghost that operates the boat remotely. He helps Link travel through the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}} and is essential in stopping [[Ganondorf]] from making his wish upon the complete [[Triforce]] to rule Hyrule. At the end of the game, the king himself chooses to remain in his kingdom as it is washed away, while Link is shown setting sail in the now-lifeless boat in search of [[New Hyrule|new lands]].
{{Clear}}

==Ezlo==
[[File:TMC Ezlo Artwork.png|right|100px]]{{Main|Ezlo}}
'''[[Ezlo]]''' is a [[Minish]] wizard who was cursed by [[Vaati]] into the form of a hat with a bird's head in {{TMC}}. He was once the mentor of Vaati. Ezlo helps Link travel through Hyrule and has the ability to shrink Link to Minish size. After Vaati's defeat, the curse is broken and Ezlo returns to the Minish homeland.
{{Clear}}

==Midna==
[[File:TP Midna Render 3.png|right|100px]]
{{Main|Midna}}
'''Midna''', the Twilight Princess, is Link's partner in {{TP|-}}. She is the Princess of the Twili, but Zant usurped the throne from her. It is through her that Link can change into a [[Wolf Link|wolf]] and back on will. She was cursed by [[Zant]] into an imp form, and she is restored to her original form when Ganondorf is defeated. In the end of the game she goes back to the [[Twilight Realm]], and destroys the Mirror of Twilight. 
{{Clear}}

==Linebeck==
[[File:PH Linebeck Artwork.png|right|120px]]
{{Main|Linebeck}}
'''Linebeck''' is a sailor who Link meets in {{PH}}. He is greedy and loves treasure, but travels with Link after finding out he could have some gain from it. Linebeck is very cowardly. He stays at the shore while Link goes through dungeons and goes on islands, much like the King of Red Lions. At the end he puts aside his cowardice to try and help Link.
{{Clear}}

==Ciela==
[[File:Cielaph.png|right|110px]]
{{Main|Ciela}}
'''Ciela''' is Link's Fairy in {{PH|-}}. She does not remember who she is, or where she came from. Regardless, she is more than happy to help Link in his quest to rescue [[Tetra]] from the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}. She's good friends with [[Oshus]], whom she affectionately calls "Grandpa." Throughout their adventure, she aids Link by providing helpful hints and information (not to mention keeping [[Linebeck]]'s ego in check). Eventually, she learns that she is a one of the Ocean King's guardian spirits, the Spirit of Time and Courage, and in the climactic battle with [[Bellum]], uses her newly-remembered powers to help Link stop time and win the day once again. At the very end, she stays behind with Oshus and the other spirits as Link returns to his own world. The other servant '''[[Spirit]]''' can also serve as Link's companions.{{Clear}}

==Princess Zelda==
[[File:ST Princess Zelda Artwork 2.png|right|100px]]
{{Main|Princess Zelda#Spirit Tracks}}
'''Princess Zelda''' is the recurring princess of Hyrule in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series. In {{ST}}, Zelda accompanies Link in his quest to stop [[Chancellor Cole]] from releasing the Demon King from the Spirit Tower. When Zelda's spirit is separated from her body early on the game, Link is one of the few that can see her ghost and he helps her in retrieving her body. As a ghost, she has the ability to control [[Phantom]]s. It is theorized that there is a possible romance between Link and Princess Zelda
{{clear}}

==Fi==
[[File:SS Fi Artwork.png|right|175px]]
{{Main|Fi}}
'''Fi''' is the ethereal spirit of the [[Goddess Sword]], and is Link's companion on his journey in {{SS|-}}. She has a synthetic voice similar to [[Midna]], but unlike Midna is very factual, preferring to use percentages and numbers in her briefings to Link. She is a physical manifestation of the Goddess Sword, and resembles its hilt in her overall design. Fi was created by the [[Hylia|Goddess Hylia]] to aid Link in his journey to save Zelda. She is programmed with knowledge of the land, as well as knowledge of enemies, and holds messages directly from Hylia to Link. Fi's presence is implied to reside within the [[Master Sword]] in {{BotW}}, but she is never seen or heard speaking. [[Princess Zelda]] can hear Fi after awakening her [[Triforce|power]].
{{Clear}}

==Crimson Loftwing==
[[File:SS Crimson Loftwing Artwork.png|right|175px]]
{{Main|Crimson Loftwing}}
The '''Crimson Loftwing''' is a rare [[Loftwing]] owned by Link in {{SS|-}}. He serves as Link's way of transport between the several Islands in [[The Sky]] and the several sections of [[Hyrule|The Surface]], a role similar to that of Epona in many other games. Link and his Loftwing share a special connection; as a young boy, Link was able to ride his Loftwing without needing any instructions.
{{Clear}}

==Wolf Link==
[[File:BotW Wolf Link Icon.png|alt=|right|114x114px]]
{{Main|Wolf Link}}
Wolf Link can be summoned in {{BotW}} by scanning the Wolf Link [[amiibo]] with the [[amiibo (Rune)|amiibo Rune]]. Wolf Link will follow Link and assist him in combat.

==Avatars of the Sages==
[[File:Avatar and Link.webp|right|300px]]
After awakening a [[Sage]] in {{TotK}}, they give Link a ring as a symbol of their loyalty. Link can call upon their assistance at any time through these rings, Ghostly apparition of their host appear. These Avatars act similar to how [[Phantom Ganon]] acts on Ganondorfs behalf, as a servant with powerful, but slightly limited abilities. [[Mineru]] can also assist Link in this way, but instead of a avatar, her new [[construct]] body can be summoned to aid Link.
