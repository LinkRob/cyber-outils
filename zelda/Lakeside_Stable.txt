The {{Term/Store|Lakeside Stable||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lakeside Stable'''|Map|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'''Lakeside Stable'''|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Lakeside Stable is a [[Stable]] located in [[West Necluda]], on the south side of [[Lake Floria]]. The [[Shai Utoh Shrine]] is located behind it, in a cave with a bombable wall, providing a convenient [[Travel Gate]] to the stable when activated. The Stable is run by [[Anly]], who mans both its Horse services and lodging. As with all other Stables, [[Beedle]] can occasionally be found here selling his wares. [[Meeshy]] and [[Letty]] may stop here on their travels.

One of the stablehands, [[Cima]], is a nervous wreck due to lightning frequently striking the stable, and asks [[Link]] to investigate it through the "[[Thunder Magnet]]" Side Quest. [[Shay]] and [[Kampo]] can be found out front. A [[Hylian Retriever]] can be befriended with raw meat, who will lead Link to a [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Forest Dweller's Spear]].

==={{TotK|-}}===
[[Dondon|Dondon's]], a newly discovered creature, has become quite the attraction since their discovery.
