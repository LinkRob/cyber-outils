'''Satomi "Asagiri" Asakawa''' (née '''Maekawa''') is an former artist at [[Nintendo]]. She joined in 1998 and left in 2013. She currently operates a therapy business.

==Personal Life==
She is married to Nintendo sound designer [[Toru Asakawa]].
