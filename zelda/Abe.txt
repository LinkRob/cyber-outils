{{Term/Store|Abe||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref name="E 258"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Abe}}
Abe lives on [[Outset Island]],<ref name="E 258"/> on which he was born.<ref name="Figurine"/> He is married to [[Rose]] and together, they had two sons: [[Joel]] and [[Zill]].<ref name="E 258"/> He is interested in [[Pig|Wild Pigs]], and in the beginning of the game he can be seen trying to catch a black pig by awaiting it from behind a palm tree. He gives [[Link]] some hints on how to catch pigs. When Link returns later in his adventure, Abe will be just outside of his house next to the fairly grown pig. Abe will also ask the young hero later on in the game to take the [[Link (Pig)|Pig]] out for a walk.<ref>{{Cite|Say, Link, is there any way you could pick him up and take him for a walk for me? I'd really appreciate it! My back is just killing me...|Abe|TWWHD}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Abe's name could be derived from {{Wp|Abraham Lincoln}}, with whom he shares similar facial expressions and facial hair. This is reinforced by the fact that his Spanish and German name are "Abraham".
