"{{Term/Store|Trial of Second Sight||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial of Second Sight}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Korok Forest}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} is one of three that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must complete as part of "{{Term|BotW|The Korok Trials|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. On the southwest edge of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Zooki|link}} advertising a {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This way to the trial!|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> At first, they are unsure if {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} are allowed to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}, but they decide they do not care.<ref>{{Cite|A Hylian seeks to attempt the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trial}}! Is that even allowed?<br/>Who cares!|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks, they explain that the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}} are a challenge that all {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} must face to become proper members of society.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trials}} are challenges Koroks must complete in order to become esteemed members of Korok society! Or something like that... *cough*|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> All three of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trials}} must be completed alone.<ref>{{Cite|The three {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} must be completed alone. As for what challenges the {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} hold, you'll just have to wait and see.|Zooki|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Zooki}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is there to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you here to attempt my {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trial}}?|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that it is, they will be shocked that asking was worth it.<ref>{{Cite|[Of course.] Oh, wow! You really want to try it?! I guess it was worth asking after all!|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> They explain that, in order to complete the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must navigate the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods|link}} without getting lost in the mist.<ref>{{Cite|Let me tell you about the {{Color|BotW Red|trial}}. All you need to do is keep going and not get lost in the mist! Easy, right?|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> Noticing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not consider that easy,<ref>{{Cite|What's wrong? You don't look very excited.|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Zooki}} offers him a hint.<ref>{{Cite|But don't worry—I've got a cryptic little hint for you!|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> They say that he can find his way by looking for the iron belonging to the hungry {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|"The hungry trees will show you the way.<br/>Spy their {{Color|BotW Red|iron}} to win the day."|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Zooki}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if it sounded cool and tells him to think about it during the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|Didn't I make that sound cool?<br/>Think about that as you take on the trial.|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> When they are finished speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Link}}'s path is marked by {{Plural|BotW|Ogre Tree|link}} with metal {{Plural|BotW|Boulder|link}} in their mouths. They can be found easily using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Along the way, he will encounter {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Stone Pebblit|link}}, which can be either defeated or avoided. Eventually he will come upon a small clearing, where a group of {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}} guard a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield|link}} on a platform. Nearby, a {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|link}} in front of an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}}. It asks that he feed the {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}} the {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield}} and in return, his brother will repay him.<ref>{{Cite|Feed me the aged shield guarded by monsters eternal. My brother will repay you in kind.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} places the {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}}'s mouth, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} will drop out of the mouth of another {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}} behind the first one. From inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to retrieve an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}}. Past the two {{Plural|BotW|Ogre Tree}}, a {{Term|BotW|Raft|link}} will be waiting at the end of a {{Term|BotW|Dock|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Lake Saria|link}}. On an island in the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Lake Saria|display=Lake}} is another {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}} with a {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet}}. This one asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return its {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, as it was stolen by the brother.<ref>{{Cite|Please place my chest in my mouth.<br/>{{Small|The shield eater's brother stole it from me.}}|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> Doing this will cause {{Term|BotW|Kuhn Sidajj Shrine|link}} to rise from the ground nearby, ending the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
