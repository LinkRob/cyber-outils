The {{Term/Store|Lava Juice||Series, OoA}} is a item that appears in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 129}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
It is won by getting a score of 100 or higher in the [[Shooting Gallery#Oracle of Ages|Goron Shooting Gallery]].<ref>{{Cite|This is Goron Gallery! Our game was a big hit in Lynna Village! Right now, we're offering Lava Juice to one player with an excellent score!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> It must be given to a certain [[Goron]] in a cave on [[Rolling Ridge]] in the past. Said Goron, who is an acquaintance of the [[Graceful Goron]],<ref>{{Cite|Don't tell anyone, but I'm friends with the famous Graceful Goron. Really!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> will then give [[Link]] the [[Letter of Introduction]].<ref>{{Cite|I don't know if it will work, but if you give me your Lava Juice, I'll introduce you to the Graceful Goron!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> After showing the letter to the Graceful Goron, he will give Link the [[Mermaid Key]], but only if Link can successfully complete the [[Goron Dance]].<ref>{{Cite|Huh? That's an introduction from a brother? What's this..? Give me your Mermaid Key? How did he know I have it? Oh well... I can't turn down a brother's request. But I can't just give you this! Dance me for it!|Graceful Goron|OoA}}</ref>
