{{Term/Store|Helmasaurus|Helmasaurus|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 184}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Helmasaurus appear at the [[City in the Sky]]. They are the larger cousins of the [[Helmasaur]]s. However, their iron plates cannot be removed with the [[Clawshot]]. Instead, [[Link]] must strike them from behind. Helmasaurus will attempt to ram into Link when they see him.
