"{{Term/Store|Father and Daughter||Series, BotW}}" is the twelfth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Father and Daughter}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #12}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
The "{{Term|BotW|Father and Daughter}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory}} is unlocked on the bridge connecting {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link|display=Zelda's}} room and her study in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}. While walking on the bridge with {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} observes a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}} being tested in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Courtyard|link}} below. She notes that it is incredible they can actually control the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} now,<ref>{{Cite|Incredible... We're at a point now where we can actually control them.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> and she is confident that they will soon know everything they need to about them and be ready for {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|At the current rate, we'll soon know all we need to know about the Guardians and the Divine Beasts! And should Ganon ever show itself again, we'll be well positioned to defend ourselves.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

The two are interrupted by the arrival of her father, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}}, who asks {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} what she is doing out here.<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing out here, Zelda?|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} kneels behind {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} as the king walks out of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}'s room. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} responds that she was assessing the results of the {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} experiment, and she shares her belief they could be useful in the fight against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|I... I was assessing the results of the experiment with the Guardians. These pieces of ancient technology could be quite useful against the—|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} cuts her off, stating he already knows that.<ref>{{Cite|I know that.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He reminds {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} that she has a duty as a princess that has been unfulfilled.<ref>{{Cite|However, as the princess, you currently have a crucial unfulfilled responsibility to your kingdom.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} asks {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} when she is going to stop treating the {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Calamity}} as a childish game.<ref>{{Cite|Let me ask you once more... When will you stop treating this as some sort of childish game?|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} tries to reason with him that she is doing the best she can to awaken the power to seal away {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}, revealing that she recently visited the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage|link}} to offer her prayers to the Goddess {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm doing everything I can. I'll have you know that I just recently returned from the Spring of Courage where I offered every ounce of my prayers to the Goddess—|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> But {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} dismisses this as well, saying she is wasting her time at {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} and that she needs to dedicate every moment she has to her training.<ref>{{Cite|And now you are here wasting your time. You need to be dedicating every moment you have to your training.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He tells her she needs to be single-minded in unlocking her [[Triforce|sealing power]].<ref>{{Cite|You must be single-minded in unlocking the power that will seal Calamity Ganon away.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> It will not ever matter if Link beats Ganon if she cannot seal him. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} counters that she already is, but she feels there is nothing more she can do.<ref>{{Cite|I already am. Don't you see—there's nothing more I can do!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} reveals to her father her hope that researching the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} can be an alternative if she is unable to awaken her powers.<ref>{{Cite|My hope is... My hope is that you— That you'll allow me to contribute here in whatever way I can.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} states he won't take any more excuses from {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} and that she needs to stop running away from her duty.<ref>{{Cite|No more excuses, Zelda! Stop running away from your duty.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He invokes his power as the king to forbid her from continuing with her research into {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} and commands her to focus on her training.<ref>{{Cite|As the king, I forbid you to have anything to do with these machines from this moment on and command you to focus on your training.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} overlooks the {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} experiment as he asks {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} if she knows how the gossip mongers speak of her.<ref>{{Cite|Do you know how the gossip mongers refer to you?|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Even Zelda does not know, he says they call her the heir to a throne of nothing but failure.<ref>{{Cite|They are out there at this moment whispering amongst themselves... that you are the heir to a throne of nothing... nothing but failure.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> His words clearly hurt {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}, and she is only able to breathe a shaky sigh. So {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} tells Zelda that it is her destiny to prove them wrong and asks if she understands.<ref>{{Cite|It is woven into your destiny that you prove them wrong. Do you understand?|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} responds that she does, of course.<ref>{{Cite|Yes. I understand.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> The king then walks back into the castle, leaving {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} and {{Term|BotW|Link}} on the bridge.
