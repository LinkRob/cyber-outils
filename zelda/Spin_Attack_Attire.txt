The {{Term/Store|Spin Attack Attire||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Spin Attack Attire is fierce, my dahling. It lets you do a fancy Great Spin Attack. Divine!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack Attire}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Desert Temple|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}. Visually, the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} resembles traditional {{Wp|Samurai|samurai}} armor.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack Attire}} allows {{Term|TFH|Link}} to perform the {{Term|TFH|Great Spin|link}} attack by holding the B button to charge his {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}, then releasing the button. This attack has double the hit radius of {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s regular {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack|link}}, and a longer duration as {{Term|TFH|Link}} completes two full spins.<ref>{{Cite|Increases Spin Attack range and power. Hold and release {{Icon|TFH B}} to unleash the fury!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> {{Term|TFH|Link}} also gains temporary invincibility while performing the {{Term|TFH|Great Spin}}, allowing him to strike fire-, ice- and electricity-based [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]] without taking damage.
