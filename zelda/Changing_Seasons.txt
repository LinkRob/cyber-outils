The {{Term/Store|Changing Seasons||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The [[Changing Seasons]] is the reward for completing the [[Sword & Shield Maze|Sword and Shield Maze]], and is guarded by [[Medusa Head]]. It is the final of the eight [[Essence of Nature|Essences of Nature]] collected by Link. Its description text reads: "Scattered seeds sprout in spring, grow in summer, bear fruit in fall and sleep through winter. It is an endless cycle of life... the [[Changing Seasons]]!"<ref>"Scattered seeds sprout in spring, grow in summer, bear fruit in fall, and sleep through winter. It is an endless cycle of life... the [[Changing Seasons]]!" – N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]])</ref>
