{{Term/Store|Linda||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character from {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Linda}}
{{Term|TWWHD|Linda}} is {{Term|TWWHD|Sue-Belle|link}}'s best friend and lives on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}.<ref name="Figurine"/> During the day, she is standing near {{Term|TWWHD|Mrs. Marie|link}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|School of Joy|link}}, wishing that someone would notice the brand-new outfit she just bought.<ref>{{Cite|This is a brand-new outfit, you know. I just wish someone would notice it.|Linda|TWW}}</ref> She later reveals to {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} that she's had her eye on someone named {{Term|TWWHD|Anton|link}}, and asks for his help in making {{Term|TWWHD|Anton}} notice her.<ref>{{Cite|So I couldn't help but notice...that you have a Picto Box of your own, don't you? Do you think you could take a pictograph of me to the apple of my eye? Maybe he'll notice my affections...|Linda|TWW}}</ref> After {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} takes a {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph|link}} of her and shows it to {{Term|TWWHD|Anton}}, the two citizens of {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island}} will start dating each other and eventually fall in love.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, Anton and I have grown so close to one another. ...Thank you so much!|Linda|TWW}}</ref> As a reward for bringing {{Term|TWWHD|Linda}} to her loved one, she will hand over a {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}} to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so happy! And that's why I want to share some of our happiness with you!|Linda|TWW}}</ref> After they've fallen in love, they will always be standing next to each other during the day near where {{Term|TWWHD|Linda}} used to stand.

Despite the fact that {{Term|TWWHD|Linda}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Kamo|link}} used to be childhood friends, she is completely oblivious to his love for her.<ref name="Figurine"/><ref>{{Cite|See, I found out that my childhood friend Linda had her eyes on someone besides me all this time... And you know what's worse? He was my buddy! I just can't take it!|Kamo|TWW}}</ref>
