The {{Term/Store|Lakeside Item Shop|Lakeside Item Shops|Series, ALBW}} is the name of two {{Plural|ALBW|Shop|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW|publisher= Prima|page= 297}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Plural|ALBW|Lakeside Item Shop}} of {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} are located on the west shores of {{Term|ALBW|Lake Hylia|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake|link}}, respectively, and are run by their worlds' {{Plural|ALBW|Lakeside Item Seller|link}}. The two {{Plural|ALBW|Shop}} have identical inventories and prices to those of the two {{Plural|ALBW|Item Shop|link}}.
