'''Rishu''' is a character from the {{OoS|Himekawa}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Rishu}} is the son of the boss of the {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Troupe of Performers|display=Troupe of Traveling Performers|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Jovan|link}}, and is also one of the performers. He is the youngest of all of the performers and is also the one who has the most to learn when it comes to performing. The rest of the performers that he works alongside with are {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din (Oracle)|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Impa|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Parot|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Mel and Rom|link}}. He practices most at spinning plates, but does during one part of the book ask {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link|link}} to teach him to use a sword as it maybe would suit him better.
