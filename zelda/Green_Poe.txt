{{Term/Store|Green Poe|Green Poes|Series, CoH|plural}} are recurring enemies in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Green Poe|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{TFH|-}}===
Green Poes make their debut in ''Tri Force Heroes'', appearing in the [[Illusory Mansion]], [[Palace Noir]], [[Lone Labyrinth]], and [[Grim Temple]] levels of [[Drablands|The Ruins]]. They can only be attacked by the Green Link, since trying to attack them with the Blue and/or Red Links will not damage them.

{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{CoH|-}}===
File:CoH Green Poe Sprite.png
{{Plural|CoH|Green Poe}} are a stronger variety of {{Term|CoH|Poe|link}}. They move on every beat but will freeze and become intangible when {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} looks directly at them. When they look away, {{Plural|CoH|Green Poe}} will become tangible again and proceed to move towards their target. Upon defeat, they will teleport the hero to the space that they were previously floating on.
{{Ambig|End}}
