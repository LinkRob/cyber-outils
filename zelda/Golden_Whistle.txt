The '''Golden Whistle''' is an {{Term|TSP|Item|link}} in {{TSP}}.{{Name Ref|TSP}}

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TSP|Golden Whistle}} is first introduced after {{Term|TSP|Impa|link}} advises {{Term|TSP|Link}} to travel to the {{Term|TSP|Water Town of Saria|link}} and retrieve the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth|link}}. To help him on his travels, she hands him the {{Term|TSP|Golden Whistle}}, although she does not explain its use.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= She pulls a golden whistle on a chain out of her apron pocket and hands it to Link. 'Here. This may help in a tight spot.' |book= TSP | publisher= Mammoth |page= 89}}</ref>

During his journey to {{Term|TSP|Saria Town|link}}, if {{Term|TSP|Link}} decides to go through the {{Term|TSP|Midoro Swamp|link}} instead of taking the detour through {{Term|TSP|Ruto Town|link}}, he will promptly be surrounded by a pack of {{Plural|TSP|Moblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Cloud skids to a halt, just as Link sees the flash of red moblin eyes in the forest. In second, he is surrounded by a pack of the hideous beasts. Their wet tongues hang out of their mouths. |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 54}}</ref> If {{Term|TSP|Link}} has the {{Term|TSP|Golden Whistle}} with him, he can use it to create a high-pitched, piercing sound that weakens the {{Plural|TSP|Moblin}}. However, after blowing on it one last time, the {{Term|TSP|Golden Whistle|display=Whistle}} shatters in his hands, but allows him to safely escape the {{Term|TSP|Moblin}} ambush.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Blinke-link! The force of the blast shatters the golden whistle like glass. Link gazes at the shards in his hands. 'Woops! I think it's time for a fast exit now,' he says. He urges Cloud to a gallop and they race out of the swamp, leaving the howling moblin behind. |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 41}}</ref> However, if the young hero does not possess the {{Term|TSP|Golden Whistle}}, he will die at the hands of the {{Plural|TSP|Moblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'Well, well,' the big one says, lapping his tongue hungrily over his lips. 'Look who dropped in for dinner.' |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 15}}</ref>

By choosing not to go to {{Term|TSP|Saria Town}} and thus not obtaining the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth}}, the young hero will eventually end up in a jail cell inside the {{Term|TSP|Tower|link}} as punishment for claiming {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles|link}} is evil without having proof of his claims.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'Just as I suspected!' the king bellows. 'Link, I've heard enough. Your petty envy has become more than a nuisance. [...] I hereby banish you to the Tower for three days, effective immediately!' |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 62}}</ref> If {{Term|TSP|Link}} did not use the {{Term|TSP|Golden Whistle|display=Whistle}} in {{Term|TSP|Midoro Swamp}} and uses it in the prison cell, a black vortex will close in on him and make him travel back in time to when the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|link}} dubbed {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} a knight of the {{Term|TSP|Triforce|link}}, giving him another chance to decide to go to {{Term|TSP|Saria Town}} and obtain the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The sound is piercing. Link's ears start to ring, and he has the dizzy feeling that the room is spinning around him. Dimly he notices that the whistle has burst to pieces in his hands. Then a black vortex closes in on him. When Link comes to, he's standing in the Triforce room with the king, Charles and Zelda. [...] '...I hereby dub you a knight of the Triforce,' the king tells Charles, beaming. 'Oh, deja vu,' groans Link. 'I could swear this has happened before.' |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 109}}</ref>
