{{Term/Store|Leaves with Fairy Dust||Series, BotW}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|publisher= Piggyback|page= 330 |edition= Expanded Edition |year= 2018}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust}} can be found in various locations throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, where they behave in one of two manners. The first type is stationary, and is found at the top of tall, steep objects such as {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, roofs, or {{Plural|BotW|Big Flag|link}}. These types are sometimes frozen in {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}, which must be melted before they can be reached. The second type is found at ground level, where it travels in a loop around a central point; this loop may be a simple circle or a more complex shape, and the cluster may or may not pause at various points in this circuit. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} interacts with the {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust}} while standing close to them, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
