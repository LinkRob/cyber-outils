The {{Term/Store|Kaya Wan Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kaya Wan Shrine}}<br/>Shields from Water}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Shields from Water''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Kaya Wan Shrine}} is found directly beside the western side of the {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kaya Wan|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kaya Wan. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kaya Wan|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Inside the {{Term|BotW|Kaya Wan Shrine}}, the entrance platform is surrounded by {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} on three sides. Closely in front of the platform is a waterfall that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can create sideways platforms on using {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}}. Above the waterfall, a rectangular pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} surrounds a small platform that a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} rests on, activating when {{Term|BotW|Link}} comes near. Behind this small platform, the ground raises up past the water and continues down another pathway.

The ground gives way to more {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} that travels in a current farther into the {{Term|BotW|Kaya Wan Shrine}}. A {{Term|BotW|Raft|link}} without a sail will appear from under the ground that floats down the river, allowing for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to ride down the current. Down the river on the left and right sides, two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I}} sit on small platforms and attack {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he comes near.

Directly after the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I}} platforms, the river drops off a waterfall into a pit, so {{Term|BotW|Link}} must jump and use his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to glide safely to {{Term|BotW|Kaya Wan}}'s platform. Once on the platform, {{Term|BotW|Kaya Wan}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kaya Wan|BotW}}</ref> wishing him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kaya Wan|BotW}}</ref>

Above the first waterfall in the rectangular pool, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} floats in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} on the right side. This chest holds an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}. A second {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} is hidden on the river past the first {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}. On the left side of the river, a sliding gate blocks the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}. To move the gate, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} to create a pillar of ice that will raise the gate, allowing for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to access the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}. This {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Broadsword|link}}.
