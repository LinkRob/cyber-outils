The {{Term/Store|Hylian Sword||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Link]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is the first class of Weapon he has access to in the game, receiving it as part of his training to be a [[Soldier|Knight]] in the [[Forces#Hyrulean Forces|Hyrulean Army]]. It is based on the staple [[Sword]]-and-[[Shield]] combination that the Hero wields in the [[The Legend of Zelda (Series)|main series]]. As such, its attacks consist primarily of series of slashes and [[Spin Attack]]s. The Hylian Sword is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Light}}.

==Variations==
The default form of the Hylian Sword is the '''Knight's Sword'''. Its upgraded form is the '''White Sword''', and its most powerful form is the '''Magical Sword'''.

The Lv.★ form of the Hylian Sword is the '''8-Bit Wooden Sword''', which is only available in {{HW|-}}. It is modeled after the basic, [[Wooden Sword]] in {{TLoZ}}.

===Knight's Sword===
{{Main|Knight's Sword}}
The Knight's Sword is the first form of the Hylian Sword. The sword itself is rather plain in appearance: silver in color except for the grip, which is a darker gray; its crossguard sports a simple pattern. The shield is designed to complement the uniform of the Hyrulean Forces, the right half sporting vertical, blue stripes, and the left half sporting embossed patterns.

Link receives the Knight's Sword as part of his training; as such, it is also the only Weapon available from the very start of the game (without DLC). As a Lv.1 Weapon, the Knight's Sword can be obtained with an Attack Strength ranging from 80 to 120, in increments of eight.

===White Sword===
{{Main|White Sword}}
The White Sword is the second form of the Hylian Sword. Its design is based on that of its [[White Sword|namesake]] from {{TLoZ|-}}, particularly with regard to its color scheme. The blade and the shield's emblem are a very light-bluish color, while the grip is a more typical blue. The sword's pommel is golden, as are the shield's accents and an inset piece within the crossguard. The crossguard itself is dark blue with a loop on each end.

The White Sword is unlocked within [[Legend Mode]] during the "[[Sealed Ambition]]" Scenario. A [[Chest]] containing the corresponding [[Sealed Weapon]] appears within the East Cannon [[Keep]] after it is captured.

Like other Lv.2 weapons, the White Sword is found in six variants with different Attack Strengths based on a given Weapon's star rating. The White Sword has a Basic Weapon Strength of 150 and each star adds 15 to the Attack Strength, so a five-star White Sword possesses an Attack Strength of 225.

===Magical Sword===
{{Main|Magical Sword}}
The Magical Sword is the Lv.3 form of the Hylian Sword. Aesthetically, it takes cues from the design of its {{TLoZ|-}} [[Magical Sword|namesake]], particularly with its color scheme, which consists primarily of red, silver, and black. Both the hilt and the blade itself are encrusted with red jewels, and the blade's sharp edges are serrated, making for a beautiful yet intimidating weapon. The shield has a design on its front that loosely resembles the cross that adorns the original [[Magical Shield]], with a pair of circles surrounding the point of intersection.

The Magical Sword is the only version of the Hylian Sword that is unlocked in [[Adventure Mode]] in {{HW|-}}. In {{HW|-}}, it can be obtained as a Reward for clearing [[Adventure Map/Valley of Seers (Hyrule Warriors)#Battle B3|Battle B3]] in the [[Adventure Map]] with an A-Rank. In {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}, it can be obtained as a Reward for clearing [[Master Quest Map/Faron Woods (Hyrule Warriors:Definitive Edition)#Battle H6|Battle H6]] in the [[Master Quest Map]] with an A-Rank.

Lv.3 Weapons such as the Magical Sword have the highest Attack Strength values in {{HW|-}}, ranging from 280 to 420, in increments of 28, depending on the Star Rating of a specific weapon.

===8-Bit Wooden Sword===
{{Main|8-Bit Wooden Sword}}
The 8-Bit Wooden Sword is the Lv.★ form of the Hylian Sword, which is available in {{HW|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. The Sword is an exact replica of the Sword from {{TLoZ|-}}, rendered in three dimensions, while the Shield is a 3D render of [[Wooden Shield]] from {{TLoZ|-}}, albeit with a somewhat altered emblem.

The 8-Bit Wooden Sword is the only 8-bit Weapon skin available in {{HW|-}} without any of the [[Hyrule Warriors#DLC Packs|DLC packs]], being unlocked automatically upon completion of the "[[Shining Beacon]]" Scenario in Legend Mode after installing [[Hyrule Warriors#Updates|Version 1.2.0]] update in {{HW|-}}. If the Scenario had already been completed before installing the update, this Weapon will be unlocked the first time the game is loaded after updating. After unlocking, 8-Bit Wooden Swords can be collected just as any other Weapon. In {{HW|DE|-}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Wooden Sword replaces the Darkmagic Sword when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

As a Lv.★ weapon, the 8-Bit Wooden Sword effectively shares all properties except aesthetic ones with its Lv.3 counterpart, in this case the Magical Sword. It is found with Attack Strength values ranging from 280 to 420, in increments of 28.

===Magical Sword +===
The Magical Sword + is the Lv.4 form of the Hylian Sword, which is available in {{HW|L|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. The Sword and Shield are aesthetically identical to the Magical Sword.

The Magical Sword + can be obtained as a Reward for clearing [[Twilight Map/Temple of Souls (Hyrule Warriors Legends)#Battle C2|Battle C2]] in {{HW|L|-}}, which is [[Twilight Map/Temple of Souls (Hyrule Warriors: Definitive Edition)#Battle C3|Battle C3]] in {{HW|DE|-}}, in the [[Twilight Map]] with an A-Rank.

Although they are aesthetically identical to the Lv.3 form, Lv.4 Weapons such as the Magical Sword + are much stronger with Attack Strength values ranging from 500 to 750, in increments of 50.

===Darkmagic Sword===
The Darkmagic Sword is the Lv.4+ or Multi-Element form of the Hylian Sword, which was added to {{HW|L|-}} with the {{ALBW|-}} Pack DLC and is available in {{HW|DE|-}}. The Sword and Shield are aesthetically similar to the Magical Sword but with a color scheme of purple, gray and black, representing its extra element of {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Darkness}}.

The Darkmagic Sword is obtained as a Reward for clearing [[Lorule Map/Temple of the Sacred Sword (Hyrule Warriors:Definitive Edition)#Battle A11|Battle A11]] in the [[Lorule Map]] with an A-Rank.

Multi-Element Weapons such as the Darkmagic Sword have the same range of Attack Strength values as their Lv.4 counterpart.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
The Hylian Sword's Special Attack is a far more powerful version of the attack added to Link's Combo Attacks by the first three Wooden Sword Badges. The wielder raises the Hylian Sword [[Skyward Strike|skyward]], calling upon some mighty power, and launches into a series of three very wide horizontal Spin Attacks while dashing forward, culminating in an extra-potent fourth Spin Attack finisher.

This attack is highly effective when facing a crowd of enemy soldiers, given its wide radius; however, its effectiveness against singular opponents as compared with other Special Attacks is fairly mediocre. That said, generally speaking, the Hylian Sword's Special Attack is well-rounded.

===Weak Point Smash===
{{Main|Critical Point#Hyrule Warriors}}
Like all weapon categories in {{HW|-}}, the Hylian Sword has two different Weak Point Smash attacks, the one used depending on whether the target is a [[Captain (Hyrule Warriors)|Captain]]/Warrior or a [[Giant Boss]]. The attack used against Captains and Warriors consists of a series of wide slices in varying directions, followed by an ultra-powerful forward thrust. The Weak Point Smash used against Giant Bosses is a massive leap into the air, followed by a to-the-hilt downward thrust into the Boss's core.

===Focus Spirit===
{{Main|Focus Spirit}}
The Hylian Sword's Focus Spirit Attack is a single forward thrust that has a relatively high attack range. Like with all Focus Spirit Attacks, hitting a Captain, Warrior, or Giant Boss with this thrust will instantly expose that target's Weak Point gauge for an extended period of time.

If Link's Focus Spirit gauge is allowed to run dry on its own (without unleashing the Focus Spirit Attack), Link will thrust his Sword upward and release his remaining [[Magic]] as the center of an explosion of Light with a massive radius and mid-level attack damage, perfect for eliminating a whole crowd of enemies at once.

==Trivia==
*The emblem on the shield of the 8-Bit Wooden Sword skin has been changed from the original on the {{Term|TLoZ|Wooden Shield|link}}. The original was a [[Symbols#The Cross and Christianity|Latin cross]]; this may have been seen by the {{HW|-}} developers as too religious for today's general gaming audience, so the emblem was changed to a "plus" sign (+) with a pixel missing in the center.
