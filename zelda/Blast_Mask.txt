The {{Term/Store|Blast Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a mask capable of causing explosions in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Uses and Location==
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's the {{Color|MM Red|Blast Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>That is a fine mask. It is filled with feelings of gratitude.</p>}}

The Blast Mask is obtained after saving the [[Old Lady from Bomb Shop|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop]] from being robbed.<ref>{{Cite|You guarded the old woman's luggage! This was added to your notebook!|N/A|MM}}</ref> On the [[First Day]] at midnight, the Old Lady will enter [[North Clock Town]]. At exactly 12:30 am, the thief [[Sakon]] will steal her bag full of [[Bomb Bag|Big Bomb Bags]].<ref>{{Cite|Ouch! Watch out! Stop! Thief!!! Give the old lady her luggage back!!!|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|MM}}</ref> [[Link]] must attack him with a Sword, causing Sakon to drop the luggage and preventing him from stealing the Bomb Bags. After Sakon leaves the scene, the Old Lady will reward Link with the Blast Mask.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, I must thank you. It's a dangerous mask, but maybe you could use it to throw your own festival fireworks show.|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|MM}}</ref>

According to a [[Gossip Stone]], the mask is filled with gunpowder, enabling its exploding abilities.<ref>{{Cite|The old woman with knowledge of explosives has a dangerous mask filled with gunpowder...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> While wearing the mask, the B button is replaced with the word "Explode." When B is pressed, an explosion will occur from the mask, much in the same way that a [[Bomb]] explodes. Link can use the mask in place of Bombs. Caution must be taken, as using the mask inflicts damage to Link. However, if the young [[Hero of Time]] uses his {{Term|MM3D|Hero's Shield|link|display=Shield}} while using the mask, he will not receive any damage from the explosion. The mask becomes black and transparent for fifteen seconds after exploding and cannot be used again until it recharges and its opacity returns. In {{MM3D}}, the mask does not turn transparent after being used and takes only ten seconds to recover.
