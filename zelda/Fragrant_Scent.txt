==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|MM3D|Fragrant Scent}} is found in the {{Term|MM3D|Southern Swamp|link}} region. It can only be seen with the use of the {{Term|MM3D|Mask of Scents|link}}, and can be used to locate {{Plural|MM3D|Mushroom|link}}. {{Plural|MM3D|Mushroom}} can then be put in an {{Term|MM3D|Empty Bottle|link}} and taken to {{Term|MM3D|Kotake|link}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Magic Hags' Potion Shop|link}} in order for her to make {{Term|MM3D|Blue Potion|link}}.

{{Plural|MM3D|Mushroom}} can be found in the {{Term|MM3D|Woods of Mystery|link}} and on {{Term|MM3D|Gorman|link}}'s boxers in the second floor of the {{Term|MM3D|Stock Pot Inn|link}}.
