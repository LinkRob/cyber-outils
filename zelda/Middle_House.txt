The {{Term/Store|Middle House||Series, OoA}} serves as the town hall of {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! The {{Color|OoS Red|Tuni Nut}} is fixed! Go place it on the pedestal in the Middle House!|Hylian|OoA}}</ref>

==Overview==
Like the rest of the village, the building is entirely symmetrical on the inside and the outside. At the far end of the house is a raised pedestal that holds in place the {{Term|OoA|Tuni Nut}}, the city's greatly worshiped symbol that gives balance to the village.<ref>{{Cite|The Tuni Nut is the city's symbol. We dutifully worshiped this nut.|Maiden|OoA}}</ref> The house is inhabited by twin sisters, who will welcome {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} to the village.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Symmetry City! Perfect balance is the law of the land!|Maiden|OoA}}</ref> Both the twin sisters have husbands who live on opposite sides of the village. The two husbands also happen to be twin brothers.  

When {{Term|OoA|Link}} first arrives in the village in the past, the {{Term|OoA|Tuni Nut}} will be broken, causing the village to be unbalanced. The volcano inside the village will begin to erupt, and the area will fall into chaos,<ref>{{Cite|When the Tuni Nut broke, the city fell out of balance, and the volcano started erupting.|Twin Brother|OoA}}</ref> which will carry on into the present. Upon entering the {{Term|OoA|Middle House}}, the twin sisters guarding the {{Term|OoA|Tuni Nut}} will ask for his help.<ref>{{Cite|Help! Help! The Tuni Nut is broken!|Maiden|OoA}}</ref> They explain that the nut must be brought to {{Term|OoA|Patch|link}} atop {{Term|OoA|Restoration Wall|link}} to get it fixed.<ref>{{Cite|A guru named Patch lives at the top of Restoration Wall. I think he can fix the broken Tuni Nut.|Maiden|OoA}}</ref> However, neither of their husbands, the twin brothers, are able to bring the nut to {{Term|OoA|Patch}} (one is injured, and the other cannot leave without his brother or else the village will be asymmetrical). As such, {{Term|OoA|Link}} must take on the task himself. Once the {{Term|OoA|Tuni Nut}} is fixed and placed on its pedestal in the {{Term|OoA|Middle House}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey! The Tuni Nut is fixed! Go place it on the pedestal in the Middle House!|Twin Brother|OoA}}</ref> balance and peace will return to the village,<ref>{{Cite|Balance has returned to the city! Thank you!|Twin Brother|OoA}}</ref> calming the volcano,<ref>{{Cite|The Tuni Nut is fixed! (...) Now the volcano should calm down!|Maiden|OoA}}</ref> and allowing {{Term|OoA|Link}} to enter the {{Term|OoA|Skull Dungeon|link}} in the present.

The {{Term|OoA|Middle House}} has a basement floor which serves as {{Term|OoA|Thomas|link}}' home, where the {{Term|OoA|Cheesy Mustache|link}} is obtained in exchange for the {{Term|OoA|Dumbbell|link}}. In a {{Term|OoA|Linked Game|link}} of {{OoS}}, {{Term|OoA|Link}} will learn a secret that can be told to one of the twin sisters, who will then upgrade {{Term|OoA|Link}}'s {{Term|OoA|Ring Box|link}}. 

The {{Term|OoA|Middle House}} disappears in the present, replaced by a few {{Plural|OoA|Tree|link}} (including a {{Term|OoA|Gale Tree|link}}).

In a {{Term|OoA|Linked Game|link}} of {{OoS}}, a girl appears in {{Term|OoA|Holly|link}}'s house who greatly admires the two twins of the middle house, as they are apparently the authors of her favorite book. She wishes to tell them a "touching secret", which {{Term|OoA|Link}} can tell the twins.<ref>{{Cite|I want you to tell a touching secret to the authors of my favorite book. The authors are twins who lived in {{Term|OoA|Labrynna|link}}'s {{Term|OoA|Symmetry City|link}}, but the book was written so long ago, they may be gone.|Holly's Friend|OoS}}</ref> Doing so will reward {{Term|OoA|Link}} with a {{Term|OoA|Ring Box|link}} upgrade.<ref>{{Cite|Amazing! I didn't know that others knew the strengths of symmetry! Thank you for telling me! In thanks, I'll increase the number of rings you can carry!|Symmetry Twin|OoA}}</ref>
