The {{Term/Store|Sea of Trees||Series, FS}} is a stage from {{FS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 263 {{Exp Game|FS}}}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Forest==
The {{Term|FS|Sea of Trees}} can be selected from the {{Term|FS|Stage Select|link}} at any time and has no prerequisite conditions. The entrance to the forest is marked by two bird-like statues in central {{Term|FS|Hyrule|link}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|FS|Sea of Trees}} is a dense, overgrown forest with natural obstacles such as {{Plural|FS|Tree|link}}, {{Plural|FS|Log|link}}, {{Plural|FS|Grass|link}}, and {{Term|FS|Water|link}}. It is rumored that those who enter the woods never return.<ref name="E Dungeons">{{Cite Book|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse|page=153 {{Exp Game|FS}}}}</ref>

The {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Forest|link}} has long been the guardian of the {{Term|FS|Sea of Trees}} and provides the {{Plural|FS|Links|link}} with {{Term|FS|Silver Key|link|display=Silver}}, {{Term|FS|Golden Key|link|display=Golden}}, or {{Plural|FS|Hero's Key|link}} for the area depending on their performance.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! You have done well to get this far. I am the Great Fairy of Forest! Throughout the ages I have listened to the whispering of these woods as the guardian of the Sea of Trees.|Great Fairy of Forest|FSAE}}</ref> A {{Term|FS|Big Manhandla|link}} lives deep within the woods and acts as the {{Term|FS|Stage}} {{Term|FS|Boss|link}}.<ref name="E Dungeons"/>
