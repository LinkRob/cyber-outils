{{Term/Store|Ingo||Series, OoT, OoT3D, OoS}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239 {{Exp Game|OoT}} & 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Term|OoT|Ingo}} appears as [[Talon]]'s employee at [[Lon Lon Ranch]] in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am Ingo and I mind the ranch. I've been working at this ranch for a long time for that lazy bum, Talon.|Ingo|OoT}}</ref> During [[Link]]'s childhood, {{Term|OoT|Ingo}} is found working hard at the Ranch, though grumbles and moans about the laziness of his superior, Talon.<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe that I, the great Ingo, am working on this dump of a ranch! Because the owner is so lazy, I always have to do to all the work around here! I, the hard-working Ingo, should be in charge, not that lazy bum, Talon!|Ingo|OoT}}</ref> Conversely, {{Term|OoT|Ingo}} appeals to [[Ganondorf]] and is granted ownership to the Ranch during Link's adult era. He kicks Talon out of the Ranch and orders [[Malon]] to do the hard work while Ingo charges [[Rupee]]s in exchange for short [[Horse]] rides. {{Term|OoT|Ingo}} will also challenge the [[Hero of Time]] to a Horse race after he recognizes Link's skill for riding Horses.<ref>{{Cite|You're getting better! How about a little race with me? One lap around the corral with that horse. Let's make a little wager—say {{Color|OoT3D Red|50 Rupees}}?|Ingo|OoT3D}}</ref> After losing the race while riding [[Epona]], {{Term|OoT|Ingo}} challenges Link to another, tougher race, promising to give him the Horse if he is defeated.<ref>{{Cite|Sh-shoooot! If the great Ganondorf found out about this humiliation... Hey, you! How about another race? If you win... You can keep...the horse!|Ingo|OoT3D}}</ref> He concedes defeat after Link defeats him again, but attempts to trap him and Epona inside the Ranch so that they can never leave.<ref>{{Cite|Hah ha hah! As I promised, I'll give the horse to you... However... I'll never let you leave this ranch!|Ingo|OoT3D}}</ref> Link and Epona escape the Ranch by jumping over the fence. After this, {{Term|OoT|Ingo}}'s personality seems to have changed entirely, and he is now grateful for the opportunity to work under Talon.<ref>{{Cite|Hi there, how are you? I am Ingo. I feel so honored to be allowed to work here.|Ingo|OoT}}</ref>

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Term|OoS|Ingo}} appears as an easily agitated man who lives in [[Sunken City]] and collects {{Plural|OoS|Vase}} in {{OoS}}. To complete his vase collection, {{Term|OoS|Ingo}} wants a [[Goron Vase]],<ref>{{Cite|Do you know of the Goron Vase? All collectors want to have it!|Goron|OoS}}</ref> an object which was always refused to him by [[Biggoron#Oracle of Seasons|Biggoron]]. Link is required to get the vase from Biggoron and trade it for [[Fish|{{Term|OoS|Ingo}}'s dinner]] to continue the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Shooot! That Biggoron! No matter how often I ask, he won't give me his vase! I'd give my dinner for it--Fish!|Ingo|OoS}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{FPTRR|-}}===
[[File:FPTRR Ingo Sprite.png|left]]
Ingo is found in [[Lon Lon Meadows]] in {{FPTRR}}. Other than giving [[Tingle]] a few tips, and allowing him to keep a [[Recipe]] he finds, he only serves a small role. Ingo mentions having a wife.
{{Ambig|End}}

{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{OoT|-}} (Himekawa)===
Ingo plays a larger role in the {{OoT|Himekawa}}, acting as a boss himself. He gets brainwashed by [[Twinrova]] and sets a trap for Link, but the trap fails and Link is saved by [[Sheik]]. Link then liberates Ingo from Twinrova's control by plucking a brainwashing gem from his right ear with the [[Master Sword]].
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*{{LA}} features a character similar in appearance as Ingo known as the {{Term|LANS|Henhouse Keeper|link}}.
*{{ALBW}} also features a character with a similar design, the [[Lorulean]] {{Term|ALBW|Milk Bar Owner|link}}.
*Ingo's theme song, which can be heard when Link beats him in a [[horse]] race, is a slower remix of the horse race theme. It can also be heard in the [[Gorman Track]] in {{MM}}.[[File:Mario Party Luigi render.webp|thumb|Luigi, Mario's younger twin brother, is whom Ingo resembles]]
*Ingo's appearance seems to be based on [[Luigi]] from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], whose character is the series' secondary protagonist, whereas [[Talon]] seems to be based on [[Mario]], who is Luigi's older twin brother and main protagonist. Interestingly, his clothing during Link's childhood and when he reforms himself after acquiring Epona in {{OoT|-}} is similar to Luigi's appearance in ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]''.
**This also applies to the aforementioned Henhouse Keeper and the Lorulean Milk Bar Owner.
*Ingo has three [[Terminan]] counterparts in {{MM|-}}. They are [[Gorman]] and the [[Gorman Brothers]].
*Ingo's name may be a reference to the antagonist of Shakespeare's ''Othello'', [[wikipedia:Iago|Iago]]. They resemble each other in both appearance and personality. Both are gangly and mustachioed, and both betray their superiors out of arrogant jealousy.
