{{Term/Store|Big Octo|Big Octos|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD, HWL, HWDE|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 166 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD}}}}</ref> also known as '''Bigoctos''',{{Fact}} are recurring [[Sub-Boss]] and enemies in {{TLoZ|Series}}. As the name suggests, they are giant counterparts of the [[Octorok]].

==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|{{Typo|Bigocto|Big Octo}}}}<br/>Attack it from behind!
}}

In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Big Octo}} had made its home {{Term|OoT3D|Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly|link}}, where it is the {{Term|OoT3D|Sub-Boss|link}} of that {{Term|OoT3D|Dungeon|link}}. When {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} arrives at its lair, he throws {{Term|OoT3D|Princess Ruto|link}} up on top of a platform to get the {{Term|OoT3D|Zora's Sapphire|link}}. Upon grabbing it, however, the young [[Zora]] princess is suddenly taken up to the next room by the platform, where she spots the monster and believes it to be an octopus.<ref>{{Cite|Keeeyaaaah! What is this?! An octopus?!|Princess Ruto|OoT3D}}</ref> The platform then sinks, revealing the {{Term|OoT3D|Big Octo}} in its entirety.

The {{Term|OoT3D|Big Octo}} circles around the spinning platform and chases after Link. The young [[Hero of Time]], in effect, must do the same thing - circle around until he reaches the {{Term|OoT3D|Big Octo}}. The {{Term|OoT3D|Big Octo}} is susceptible to the {{Term|OoT3D|Boomerang|link}}, which when hit by it, stuns it and forces it to turn around. Its only weak spot is on its back, which is only revealed once it is stunned. Link must run around the circle and try to hit its back before it starts to run again. If he fails to do so, the enemy will ram him and send Link flying backwards before turning around, causing half a [[Heart Container|Heart]] in damage. In the original [[Nintendo 64]] version, a [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]] with a [[Deku Stick]] deals enough damage to defeat the Big Octo immediately with a single blow. When defeated, Link is able to continue the dungeon, but Ruto is nowhere to be found until he defeat the dungeon's [[Boss]], [[Barinade]].

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= Don't tell me you don't know about the {{Color|MM White|Big Octo}}! If you don't want to be sucked in, keep your distance. Aim from afar!
}}

In {{MM}}, two {{Plural|MM3D|Big Octo}} appear in the {{Term|MM3D|Southern Swamp|link}}, blocking the way to the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Palace|link}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Shrine|link}}. They attack Link by sucking him in and spitting him out if he gets too close, regardless of whatever form he is in. They can be defeated by ramming into them with the boat on the {{Term|MM3D|Boat Cruise}}, thus opening the way to the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Palace}}. They can also be easily killed with the {{Term|MM3D|Bow|link}} once the Hero of Time retrieves it from the [[Woodfall Temple]]. Upon completion of the dungeon, they disappear from the swamp.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Big Octo}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= A '''Big Octo'''!  Attack its eyeballs before you get sucked into its whirlpool!
}}

In {{TWW}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Big Octo}} are large, squid-like creatures that inhabit the waters of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}, and will appear if Link sails nearby. A total of 6 {{Plural|TWWHD|Big Octo}} can be found in the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea}}, one has 4 eyes, three have 8 eyes, and two have 12 eyes. To defeat one, he must shoot and destroy all of its eyes with a projectile, either with the {{Term|TWWHD|Bow|link}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Boomerang|link}} or with {{Plural|TWWHD|Bomb|link}}. Bombs in particular will eliminate an eye immediately, while [[Arrow|Arrows]] and the Boomerang require multiple hits.

[[File:OctoChart.jpg|right|200px|The Octo Chart from {{TWW}}]]
The {{Plural|TWWHD|Big Octo}} cannot be seen until it rises out of the water, but there is always a large flock of {{Plural|TWWHD|Seagull|link}} circling above them and if he gets close enough, a thunderstorm will occur.<ref>{{Cite|If you see seagulls flying all around some sea water, that's a sign that you need to be wary, fry. Because the seagulls flock to wherever Big Octos appear... BIG OCTOS, I tell you! These things don't mess around!|Fishman|TWWHD}}</ref> As the {{Term|TWWHD|Big Octo}} surfaces, the [[whirlpool]] slowly pulls Link and the {{Term|TWWHD|King of Red Lions|link}} into its mouth. If all of its eyes are not destroyed before reaching the center of the whirlpool, the {{Term|TWWHD|Big Octo}} sucks Link up and spits him out, sending him and the boat flying to a different area in the quadrant and takes a quarter of a Heart in damage. However, if the Hero of Winds manages to defeat the {{Term|TWWHD|Big Octo}} before he is sucked in, the slain enemy will leave behind a reward.

The {{Term|TWWHD|Octo Chart|link}} is a {{Term|TWWHD|Special Chart|link}} that can show Link where the six {{Plural|TWWHD|Big Octo}} are located, as well as the number of eyes associated with each one.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133 }}</ref> The {{Term|TWWHD|Octo Chart}} can be found by using {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}} #26 on the {{Term|TWWHD|Northern Triangle Island|link}} to pinpoint where the {{Term|TWWHD|Octo Chart}} is located at the bottom of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea}}.

The {{Term|TWWHD|Big Octo}} by {{Term|TWWHD|Two-Eye Reef|link}} containing only 4 eyes has a {{Term|TWWHD|Great Fairy|link}} trapped in its clutches, and Link can free her by defeating the {{Term|TWWHD|Big Octo}}. Once freed, the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Fairy}} will thank the young hero and double his {{Term|TWWHD|Magic Meter|link}} as a gift for releasing her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, waker of the winds... Thanks to you, I have been freed from that beast's foul grasp... As thanks, I shall give you just a little of my power...|Great Fairy|TWWHD}}</ref>

==={{PH|-}}===
[[Ocean Octorok]]s appear in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|Phantom Hourglass]]'', playing a similar role to Big Octos.

==={{ST|-}}===
Like their ''Phantom Hourglass'' cousins, Ocean Octoroks reappear in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks|Spirit Tracks]]'', but with a slightly different color.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW|-}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Big Octo}} appear on the {{Term|HWDE|Great Sea Map|link}} and the {{Term|HWDE|Master Wind Waker Map|link}} of {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}. Their appearance is an 8-bit representation based on their appearance from {{TWW|-}}. On Map squares with a {{Term|HWDE|Big Octo}}, a Great Sea Rule is applied to the Map Battle that causes all damage to the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior|link}} to become devastating, reducing them to one quarter Heart. The Great Sea Rule can be removed by defeating the Big Octo with a {{Term|HWDE|Boomerang}} {{Term|HWDE|Item Card|link}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

== Trivia ==

* In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', when [[Link]] must meet [[Jabun]] at the backside of [[Outset Island]] to obtain [[Nayru's Pearl]], though no Big Octo appears, a [[whirlpool]] in an identical manner by the enemies will occur, and he does not destroy the stone wall with [[Bomb|Bombs]] before he gets to the center, he will sink into the whirlpool and respawn outside the island, at the cost of a quarter of a [[Heart Container|Heart]] in damage.
