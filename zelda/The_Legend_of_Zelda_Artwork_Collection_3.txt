'''''The Legend of Zelda Artwork Collection 3''''' is an artbook released in 2021, exclusively in Japan. It is a sequel to ''[[The Legend of Zelda Artwork Collection 2]]'' released in 2014. It was released with the September 2021 issue of Nintendo Dream.<ref name="Article"/> It was released to coincide with the 35th anniversary of {{TLoZ|series}} and the 25th anniversary of [[Nintendo Dream]].

==Features==
The book contains 164 pages of ''Zelda'' art and illustrations.<ref name="Article"/>
