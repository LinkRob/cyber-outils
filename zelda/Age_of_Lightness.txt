The {{Term/Store|Age of Lightness||Series, ZA}} is the age following the successful conclusion of {{ZA}}.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= |game= ZA|page= 2}}</ref>

==Overview==
Following {{Term|ZA|Gannon|link}}'s capture of {{Term|ZA|Link|link}}, {{Term|ZA|Hyrule|link|display=Hyrule}} is considered to be in a disatrous {{Term|ZA|Age of Darkness|link}}. However, by slaying {{Term|ZA|Gannon}} and saving {{Term|ZA|Link}}, {{Term|ZA|Zelda|link}} is able to return {{Term|ZA|Hyrule|display=Hyrule}} to an {{Term|ZA|Age of Lightness}}, presumably characterized by the kingdom's prosperity.
