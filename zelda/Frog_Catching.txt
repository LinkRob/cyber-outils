"{{Term/Store|Frog Catching||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Frog Catching}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Zora's Domain}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} by talking to {{Term|BotW|Tumbo|link}} after completing the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Tumbo}} learns that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, he reasons that he must have come from far away.<ref>{{Cite|If you're a Hy-leon, that means you came from really far away, right?!|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he saw his mother, {{Term|BotW|Mei|link}}, who went missing at the beginning of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link|display=Divine Beast Vah Ruta's}} rampage.<ref>{{Cite|You didn't happen to see my mom, Mei, on the way here, did you? Ever since the water monster went crazy, Mom hasn't come home...|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, {{Term|BotW|Tumbo}} is still hopeful that she will return soon.<ref>{{Cite|But, I mean, I bet she'll come home sooner or later... Hopefully sooner.|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref>

Until {{Term|BotW|Mei}} returns, {{Term|BotW|Tumbo}} is catching {{Plural|BotW|Frog|link}} to sell.<ref>{{Cite|So I'm catching frogs and selling them to earn money for our family! Just until Mom comes home.|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks, he explains that he has catching {{Plural|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Frogging?] Yep! Since Mom hasn't come back from fishing yet... I've been catching {{Color|BotW Blue|hot-footed frogs}} and selling them to earn some money for our family.|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> Suddenly, {{Term|BotW|Tumbo}} realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is pitying him.<ref>{{Cite|... You...were just pitying me, weren't you? You felt sorry for me...didn't you?!|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he does not need his pity, instead telling him to donate five {{Plural|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I don't need your pity! If you wanna help, donate some {{Color|BotW Blue|hot-footed frogs}}! You can find them easily on rainy days. I...really need you to get some for me. {{Color|BotW Red|Five frogs}} should be enough! Got it?|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog}} can be found in the ponds and lakes surrounding {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, and they are especially plentiful during the rain. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has obtained five {{Plural|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog}}, he must return to {{Term|BotW|Tumbo}}, who is shocked to hear {{Term|BotW|Link}} actually got them.<ref>{{Cite|[Have some frogs.] What? Really?!|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> He counts a total of five {{Plural|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog}},<ref>{{Cite|OK, I'm gonna go ahead and count them! One, two, three, four...FIVE!|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> becoming excited once he confirms it.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! You really brought them!|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref>

As a reward, {{Term|BotW|Tumbo}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} an {{Term|BotW|Armoranth|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here, take this as thanks!|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he picked it because he thought it was unusual, but since it does not suit him, he decided to give it to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is called {{Color|BotW Blue|armoranth}}! I picked it because it's unusual, but it doesn't really suit me... so it's all yours!|Tumbo|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
