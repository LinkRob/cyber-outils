The {{Term/Store|Faux Hero||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Friendly Token<br/>More friends is better than none. So got get some, son!<br/>Location: Faux Hero|Material List|TFH}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|TFH|Faux Hero}} is a young boy dressed in green who can be found in front of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}. He wishes to get inside the castle and insists that he is {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}'s true hero,<ref>{{Cite|Let me in too, chum! Please! I wanna be a witch-hunting hero too!|Faux Hero|TFH}}</ref> but is not allowed in as his appearance does not fit {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}}'s {{Term|TFH|Three Noble Attributes of Courage|link|display=specifications}},<ref>{{Cite|Only those who meet the necessary ear, hair, and sideburn requirements may join the witch-hunting brigade! Not just anyone can become a Tri Force Hero, you know!|Guard|TFH}}</ref> likely because he has round ears.<ref name="ears"/> He reacts with shock and confusion when {{Term|TFH|Link}} is allowed inside.<ref>{{Cite|What?! Why does HE get to go in?|Faux Hero|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How could they let a chum like YOU in when they keep refusing ME! The clear protagonist of this story!|Faux Hero|TFH}}</ref>

Although the {{Term|TFH|Faux Hero}} is never allowed inside the castle, he boasts that he is very popular, and has 15 {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token|link}} to prove it.<ref>{{Cite|I'm loaded with Friendly Tokens! Don't they HAVE to let someone as popular as me in?!|Faux Hero|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I've got 15 Friendly Tokens. Count 'em, chum!|Faux Hero|TFH}}</ref> He challenges {{Term|TFH|Link}} to a "Chum Challenge" and will give {{Term|TFH|Link}} one of his {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token}} for every hero he has played with over Local [[Multiplayer]] or [[Download Play]].<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= After playing with friends using Local Play or Download Play, talk to the boy at the castle entrance to receive a Friendly Token.|game= TFH|page= 11}}</ref> If {{Term|TFH|Link}} manages to obtain all 15 of his {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token}}, the {{Term|TFH|Faux Hero}} will react with disbelief before admitting that he has learned from the experience,<ref>{{Cite|Th-this can't be happening... I'm all out of {{Color|TFH Blue|Friendly Tokens}}...<br/>But you know what, chum? I feel... fine. Free, even! Real friendships don't need a token, anyhow. I guess.<br/>Yeah, that's right! Friends are friends, no matter what. I may have lost at my own game, but I've grown! Matured!|Faux Hero|TFH}}</ref> referring to {{Term|TFH|Link}} henceforth as his "Numero Uno Chum".<ref>{{Cite|In fact! From now on, I henceforth and hereby dub thee...<br/>{{Big|My Numero Uno Chum!!}}|Faux Hero|TFH}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The colors of the {{Term|TFH|Faux Hero}}'s hair and clothing greatly resemble {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}}'s from {{ALttP}}.
*Although {{Term|TFH|Link}} can purchase {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token}} from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} following the Ver. 2.0.0 update, the {{Term|TFH|Faux Hero}} will not acknowledge {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s possession of them when spoken to.
