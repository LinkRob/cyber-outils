{{Term/Store|Mount Rozudo||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Mount Rozudo'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Mount Rozudo is located between [[East Necluda]] and [[West Necluda]].

== Appearances ==
Found only in the game: Legend Of Zelda Breath of the Wild; And With Unknown Appearance In the Games Sequel: "Breath of the Wild 2" As the Name of It has not been revealed due to potential spoilers.

=== Special Places ===
Located at the upper southwest of Mount Rozudo Is a [[Hot Spring|Hot-spring]] with A [[Korok Seed|Korok seed]] challenge.

==Nomenclature==
Mount Rozudo derives its name from ''rosewood''.
