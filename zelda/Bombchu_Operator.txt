'''Bombchu Operators''' are [[Enemies in Hyrule Warriors|Enemies]] in {{HW}}.

==Characteristics==
Bombchu Operators appear in "[[Land of Myth]]" in [[Legend Mode]]. They are [[Captain (Hyrule Warriors)|Captains]] that operate [[Bombchu|Bombchus]] to attack [[Keep|Keeps]]. If they are defeated, their Bombchu may be claimed and sent back to attack enemy Keeps.
