{{Term/Store|''The Old Man's Diary''||Series, BotW}} is a personal journal belonging to the [[Old Man]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Old Man's Diary|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
''The Old Man's Diary'' is located inside the [[Old Man's Cabin]], resting upon the table. It chronicles the [[Old Man]]'s culinary pursuits as he concocts a recipe composed of [[Raw Meat]] cooked with [[Spicy Pepper]]s and [[Fish]].<ref>{{Cite|On this desolate plateau, the only pleasure that brings me comfort is cooking. And today, I outdid myself! Truly, I created the perfect dish. I call it...spicy meat and seafood fry.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> He rejoices that with this recipe, he no longer needs to wear his itchy [[Warm Doublet]] while traversing the cold regions of the [[Great Plateau]].<ref>{{Cite|This recipe not only restores health, but it also keeps me warm, even when traveling in the snowy mountains. With this dish on my side, I no longer have need of that itchy {{Color|BotW Blue|warm doublet}}.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> However, in his excitement, the Old Man forgot to transcribe the ingredients for the [[Food]] he made.<ref>{{Cite|I do not know how I allowed this to happen, but it seems I forgot to write down a very important recipe. I know it contained raw meat and spicy pepper. However... I simply cannot remember what else I used! My age is catching up to me.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> In the Diary, he begrudges his mistake and wishes for someone to come along and help him complete the recipe, promising to beget his [[Warm Doublet]] to the person who helped him.<ref>{{Cite|Sadly, on this lonely plateau, I have only my own knowledge and memory to rely on. Still... If I did find someone who knew the missing ingredient, I would happily reward them with my {{Color|BotW Blue|warm doublet}}.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

If Link speaks to the Old Man outside of the Woodcutter's House afterwards, he will tell Link that he knows about his intrusion into his personal diary, but reiterates his offer to give away his Warm Doublet to him if he should be able to replicate the recipe.<ref>{{Cite|In case you were wondering, I know you were poking around in my diary. That said, if you do end up figuring out how to create that dish I wrote about, my warm doublet is all yours.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

===Transcription===
Pages 4 and 5 of the diary are only available if {{Term|BotW|Link}} gives the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} some {{Term|BotW|Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry|link}}. Otherwise the diary will stop on page 3. The last page appears once Link has completed the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Isolated Plateau|link}}".
