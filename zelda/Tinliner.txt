{{Name|Japanese|''Bachelor Super Express'': Tinliner}} is a train which {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} and his friends use in {{RTBToL}}.

==History==
Powered by vegetable oil, and not considered a good looking train, the {{Term|RTBToL|Tinliner}} first appears on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 5|link}}, when {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} and friends found it abandoned in a train station. {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} must find several missing parts to make it run. After the missing parts have been returned, and placed in the correct order, the locomotive transforms into a more modern-looking train, resembling {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}'s face. The group then ride the train until it runs out of oil at {{Term|RTBToL|Page 6|link}}, where it remains until the end of {{Term|RTBToL|Page 8|link}}, when they depart, continuing in the direction of {{Term|RTBToL|Emerald City|link}}.

On {{Term|RTBToL|Page 9|link}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Segāre|link}} places a ramp on the tracks, which causes the train to jump the rails, and end up in an abandoned mining facility. After completing a series of puzzles involving train tracks and switches, they are able to get back on the correct track, and continue. 

On {{Term|RTBToL|Page 10|link}}, the tracks start leading the group to a harbor, and from there, they will need to continue by ferry. However, Tingle and friends have some trouble getting past the border guards, and the party is forced to obtain work licenses. Once they do so, they are allowed to depart the port.

After the {{Term|RTBToL|Tinliner}} arrives on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 11|link}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} will have to get tickets in order to continue the railway line, but, as he is going to, {{Term|RTBToL|Segāre}} kidnaps {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi|link}}, and runs off to the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|link}}. Once the page is finished, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} and his friends are finally able to enter the {{Term|RTBToL|Emerald City}} on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 12|link}}, and from that point, the {{Term|RTBToL|Tinliner}} is not see again until the credits, where it appears with {{Term|RTBToL|Azusa|link}}, having been transformed to match her face instead.

==Trivia==
*"751-VL1096" is the answer to one of the trivia questions asked by a train part on Page 5, in which {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} is asked what the train's license number is.
