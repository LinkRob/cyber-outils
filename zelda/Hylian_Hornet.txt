{{Term/Store|Hylian Hornet|Hylian Hornets|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are {{Plural|TPHD|Enemy|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 185}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TPHD|Hylian Hornet}} can be found in [[Ordon Village]], [[Kakariko Village Graveyard|Kakariko Graveyard]], and the [[Fishing Hole]]. They live in {{Plural|TPHD|Beehive|link}}, which {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can knock down with the {{Term|TPHD|Slingshot|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Bow|link}}, [[Hawk]], or by touching them. {{Plural|TPHD|Hylian Hornet}} will only attack {{Term|TPHD|Link}} if he destroys or gets close to their {{Term|TPHD|Beehive}}. If Link releases the Hawk at the Beehive, the hornets will disperse. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can easily defeat them with a [[Sword]], avoid them by jumping into water, or run away until they stop chasing him. Each sting deals a quarter of a [[Heart]] of damage. From a broken {{Term|TPHD|Beehive}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can collect {{Term|TPHD|Bee Larva|link}} in an [[Empty Bottle]]. {{Term|TPHD|Bee Larva}} can be eaten to restore {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Life Gauge|link}}, or can be used as {{Term|TPHD|Bait|link}} in {{Term|TPHD|Fishing|link}}.

Link first encounters Hylian Hornets in [[Ordon Village]] in a Beehive above [[Fado (Twilight Princess)|Fado]]’s house. [[Hanch]] is contemplating what to do about it, and mentions to Link that Bee Larva may be useful.<ref>''[https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Prima_Games_Twilight_Princess_Premiere_Edition_Strategy_Guide_Wii_Version Strategy Guide: Wii Version, Premier Edition]'' (Prima Games) pg. 35</ref>
