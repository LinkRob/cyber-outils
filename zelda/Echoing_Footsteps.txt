{{Term/Store|Echoing Footsteps||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 25 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Echoing Footsteps|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given three minutes to defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox|link}} to open a gate. The first area, which those three are located in, is also filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}. After the objective is reached, the gate opens, the timer resets to five minutes, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}’s new objective is to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Stone Talus|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}}. The area containing those three {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos|link}}. After that objective is reached, another gate opens, the timer resets back to four minutes, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} is given another objective, which is to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Lynel|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Moblin}}. The third area is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}}, and unlike the last two, contains no {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Soldier|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the four monsters are defeated. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
