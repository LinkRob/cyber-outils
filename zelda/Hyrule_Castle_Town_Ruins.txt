The {{Term/Store|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hyrule Castle Town Ruins'''|Map|Botw}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins}} are located due south of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. Before the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins|display=Hyrule Castle Town}} was {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}'s largest city, serving as a shopping hub and travel destination.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 232}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Central Square|link}} is located in the center of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins|display=Ruins}}. Like many surrounding areas, the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins}} are filled with destroyed buildings and desolate terrain that is covered in {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}. Six [[Pool of Malice|Pools of Malice]], two in the east, two in the west, and two at the central entrance to the Central Square can be removed by destroying the associated [[Glowing Eyeball|Glowing Eyeballs]], which makes later taking the [[Gerudo]] approach of entering Hyrule Castle that is mentioned by [[Parcy]], significantly easier. However, it's very difficult to achieve this without alarming nearby [[Decayed Guardian|Decayed Guardians]] and [[Guardian Stalker|Guardian Stalkers]].

There is little vegetation to be found except for {{Term|BotW|Grass|link}} and, curiously, the occasional young tree. A {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} can be found in one of the {{Plural|BotW|Ruined House|link}} to the northwest, while another is located in [[East Castle Town]].

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Ruined House}} to the southeast. A second stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Luminous Stone|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Ruined House}} to the south. A third stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee}} can be found inside the tower that is surrounded by {{Term|BotW|Malice}} in the road in the southwest. A fourth stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Sapphire|link}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Ruined House}} in the west. A fifth stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Ruined House}} north of the previous {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}.

A [[Knight's Broadsword]] and [[Knight's Shield]] can be found on the Central Square fountain's south side and occasionally respawn if Link leaves the area. The fountain has four tablets in each of the cardinal directions, all of which read, “On this day the king opened this spring up to the public,” without ever giving an actual date. While the area is swarming with Guardians ever since Link awakens, Parcy implies that this is a recent development, as she used to enter the area while on her way to Hyrule Castle to search for treasure and valuable objects. However, once the Guardians appeared, approaching the town to reach the castle became too dangerous even for Parcy. This is further evidence the Guardians became more active as Zelda's seal on [[Calamity Ganon]] weakened and Ganon sensed Link had awoken, which is implied by multiple sources Link talks to about the subject. 

Two [[Giant Column|Giant Columns]] once buried on the east and west sides of town which have since risen out of the ground in response to Calamity Ganon's growing power and Link's activation of the [[Great Plateau Tower]]. A broken Guardian Stalker can be found on the town's east pillar though Link can only reach it by paragliding from the top of Hyrule Castle's [[Sanctum]]. Searching this Guardian will yield a random Guardian Part.

The Hyrule Castle Town Ruins reappear in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']]. The Malice is now gone, and a memorial plaque dedicated to those who died in the Calamity has been placed near the square by [[Princess Zelda]]. Additionally, [[Hudson Construction]] has demolished some old structures to lay foundations for in-progress new buildings.

The fauna has also drastically expanded in the absence of Guardians, with [[Grassland Fox]]es now appearing, along with [[Woodland Boar]]s, [[Blue-Winged Heron]]s, [[Pink Heron]]s, [[Common Sparrow]]s, and [[Mountain Crow]]s. Enemies are limited to one [[Gloom Spawn]] on the East side of the Ruins and low-level [[Chuchu]]s scattered across the region.
