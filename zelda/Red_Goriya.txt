==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Plural|ALttP|Red Goriya}} first appear in the {{Term|ALttP|Palace of Darkness|link}} and reappear in later {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} dungeons such as {{Term|ALttP|Ganon's Tower|link}} in {{ALttP}}. They copy {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}}'s movement pattern, similar to {{Plural|Series|Arm-Mimic|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Mask-Mimic|link}}, moving in the opposite direction of {{Term|ALttP|Link}}. They spit deadly fireballs whenever facing {{Term|ALttP|Link}}, and similar to {{Plural|ALttP|Red Eyegore|link}}, they can only be killed with {{Plural|ALttP|Arrow|link}}.
