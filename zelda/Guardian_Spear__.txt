{{Compendium/Extract|Guardian Spear++}}
{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear++}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}} within "A Major Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. The {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} also has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear++}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They are {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear|link}} that have had their output increased to the maximum.<ref name="Inventory"/> The design of the spearheads allow for optimal stabbing, and they are capable of piercing most armor.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear++}} will deal extra damage when they are used to fight {{Term|BotW|Guardian}}-type {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. They will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, nor can they burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. While they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, they will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear++}} belong to the equipment archetype known as {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}}, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that are utilized by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>
