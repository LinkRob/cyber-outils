==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Bludo}} is the Boss of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}} and the founder of the {{Term|BotW|Goron Group Mining Company|link}}.<ref name="Bludo">{{Cite|{{Big|Well, my name's {{Color|BotW Blue|Bludo}}.}} I'm the great Goron {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} who's fearsome enough to silence a crying child!
