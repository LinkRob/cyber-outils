The {{Term/Store|Bomb Flower Store||Series, ALBW}} is a location in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Features and Overview==
The Bomb Flower Store is a [[shop]] where [[Link]] can borrow the [[Big Bomb Flower]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Wanna use the Big Bomb Flower? For just 200 Rupees, you can stop lookin’ so sour!|Bomb-Shop Man|ALBW}}</ref> The Big Bomb Flower is located outside the shop in a fenced area,<ref>{{Cite|Go, go, go! It’s growin’ outside. Try it on out, and take it for a ride!|Bomb-Shop Man|ALBW}}</ref> where [[Link]] needs to blow a big boulder with it in order to exit the area with the Bomb.
