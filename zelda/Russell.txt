{{Term/Store|Russell||Series, ST}} is a character in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288}}</ref> He is the captain of the [[Hyrule Castle]] [[Soldier|guards]].

==Biography==
Captain Russell resides in the [[Sword Training|training room]] within the castle, where [[Link]] visits him to receive the [[Recruit's Sword]] and thus be able to access the [[Tower of Spirits]] via an underground passage.<ref name= "Captain"/>

Later on, after completing the [[Snow Temple]], Link will receive a [[letter]] from Russell inviting him to come prove his swordsmanship skills at the castle. For a fee of 20 [[Rupee]]s, Link can train against three of Russell's guards, and if he hits them at least 60 times before he gets hit 3 times, he will be rewarded with a [[Heart Container]].

Link can continue to train with Captain Russell to obtain a variety of [[treasure]]s. By getting between 60 and 119 hits, Link will receive a medium-rare treasure, such as a [[Dragon Scale]] or a [[Pirate Necklace]]. By scoring between 120 hits and 199 hits, he will be rewarded with a rare treasure like an [[Ancient Gold Piece]] or a [[Mystic Jade]]. Obtaining over 200 hits will reward Link with a [[Regal Ring]] or an [[Alchemy Stone]]. If Link attacks the three soldiers from 900 to 999 times,<ref>{{Cite|That's quite the achievement, Recruit! I never thought I'd see one of my men break that number! Here, take this! You could be the only one to beat this room's record for hits. And that record would be 900 hits! Compared to that, your record is just a drop in the ocean, so keep practicing!|Guard Captain Russell|ST}}</ref> Russell bestows him with the title of captain.<ref>{{Cite|Ho ho ho! So you've done it at last! We may have a new captain of the guard on our hands! Ho ho ho! Sigh...|Guard Captain Russell|ST}}</ref>

Guard Captain Russell may have been named after {{wp|ラッセル車}} (Russell Car), the Japanese word for "wedge plow."
