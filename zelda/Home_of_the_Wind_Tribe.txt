{{Term/Store|Home of the Wind Tribe||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Home of the Wind Tribe}} was referred to as the '''Tower of Winds''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 69}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} can first be accessed (albeit in a limited capacity) after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. This will cause a {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)|link}} to appear near {{Term|TMC|Link's House|link}} in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}}.

Several members of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}} reside in the tower, though {{Term|TMC|Strata|link}} blocks the entrance leading out to the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Flurris|link}} blocks the stairs leading up to the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}} is lying in a bed on the second floor, haunted by a {{Term|TMC|Ghost|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nobody believes me, but I saw a ghost that has taken hold of Granddad!|Flurris|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} uses the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}} to defeat the {{Term|TMC|Ghost}}, {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} will begin to recover.<ref>{{Cite|What? An evil spirit had taken hold of me? And you drove it away? You... You might be right!... I can already feel my energy returning!|Gregal|TMC}}</ref>

As long as {{Term|TMC|Link}} accomplishes this before arriving at the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} via {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} will be alive and well, thanking {{Term|TMC|Link}} and rewarding him with the {{Term|TMC|Light Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, it's {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! Thank you for your help earlier! I've got something else good for you, so here, please take it!|Gregal|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} arrives and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} comments on the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} without the {{Term|TMC|Ghost}} being defeated, {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} will pass away, and the {{Term|TMC|Light Arrow}} becomes unobtainable.

After {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with the {{Term|TMC|Source of the Flow|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} traverses the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}} and can enter the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} from its front entrance. The upper floors become available, and {{Term|TMC|Siroc|link}} will grant {{Term|TMC|Link}} access to the {{Term|TMC|Tornado|link}} on the roof that will take him to the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds}}.<ref>{{Cite|Surface dwellers are not permitted to enter our palace, but you are special. You may continue to the roof.|Siroc|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There is a {{Color|TMC Blue|tornado}} atop this house. Enter it to go to the {{Color|TMC Green|Palace of Winds}}.|Siroc|TMC}}</ref>

Several {{Plural|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} can be found throughout the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}}; they are found frequently by the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}}, who has trouble storing them all.<ref>{{Cite|We of the Wind Tribe are very skilled at finding {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone pieces}}. So skilled in fact, that we have more of them than we can manage! We can't even store them all. I wish someone would take a few from us.|Flurris|TMC}}</ref>
