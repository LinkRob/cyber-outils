The '''Sword of Might Lv. 2''' is the upgraded version of the [[Sword of Might]].

==Overview==
The Sword of Might can be upgraded to Lv. 2 by using 10 [[Might Crystal|Might Crystals]] at [[Lueburry|Lueburry’s]] House, increasing its damage. It can then be further upgraded to [[Sword of Might Lv. 3|Lv. 3]] with an additional 20 Might Crystals, enhancing its damage even more and unlocking the [[Sword Beam]] attack, which activates when the wielder is at full health.

==Trivia==
* When Link infiltrated [[Suthorn Ruins]] at the start of the game, this was the form the Sword of Might took.
