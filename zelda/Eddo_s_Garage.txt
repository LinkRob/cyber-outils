{{Term/Store|Eddo's Garage||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 282}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Eddo's Garage is a ship garage located on [[Cannon Island]]. It is owned and operated by [[Eddo]] and his apprentice, [[Fuzo]]. [[Link]] visits Eddo's Garage in order to obtain a cannon and Eddo sells him [[Eddo's Cannon]] for 50 [[Rupee]]s. Later, Link returns to Eddo's Garage to purchase the [[Salvage Arm]]. Link can visit Fuzo here after earning 4, 8, 12 and 16 [[Big Plays]] from [[Battle Mode]] to be rewarded with [[Treasure Chest]]s containing [[Golden Ship]] parts at these milestones.
