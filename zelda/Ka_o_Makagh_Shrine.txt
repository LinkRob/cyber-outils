The {{Term/Store|Ka'o Makagh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ka'o Makagh Shrine}}<br/>Metal Doors Open the Way}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Metal Doors Open the Way''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Ka'o Makagh Shrine can be found on a ledge above {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he will be greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ka'o Makagh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ka'o Makagh. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Ka'o Makagh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
When Link enters the Shrine, he finds a pair of two large metal doors blocking the path. Link can use his {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to open the doors.

In the next room he finds four large square platforms rising up from the ground, all identical in height except for one of the platforms, which is taller than the rest. A [[Guardian Scout I]] and [[Guardian Scout II]] can be found here. To the left of those four squares, Link can find another set of large metal doors. The door on the left is not attached to the wall but instead is attached to bombable blocks. Link can destroy these block to remove the door from the wall and use it as a bridge.

If Link uses the door as a bridge, he can cross on to the top of the tallest square. On top of this square, there is a stack of blocks. These blocks cannot be destroyed with bombs, nor can they be moved with Magnesis. Link must either use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} Rune or knock them aside with the metal door from before to move the blocks. If Link uses the bridge to get to the northeastern square, Link can use the metallic door to form a bridge to reach Ka'o Makagh.

Ka'o Makagh will grant Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} when he approaches.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ka'o Makagh|BotW}}</ref> He wishes Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ka'o Makagh|BotW}}</ref>

Behind the second pair of doors, Link can find a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Bow|link}}. On top of the pile of blocks on the tallest square, there is a chest that holds an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}}. On the bottom of the tallest square facing the Sheikah Monk, there is a metallic block disguised as a wall. Activating [[Magnesis]] will reveal its location. If Link removes this block, he can find a chest behind it containing a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.
