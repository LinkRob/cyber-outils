{{Term/Store|Boarblin|Boarblins|Series, LANS|plural}},{{Exp Game|LANS}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= DAB|publisher= |page= 29}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Pig Warrior|Pig Warriors|LA, LADX, OoS, OoA|plural}},{{Exp Game|LA, OoS, OoA}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 197 {{Exp Game|LA, OoS, OoA}}}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. Another Variant of Boarblins is known as a [[Spear Moblin]].

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Plural|LANS|Boarblin}} are relatives to {{Plural|LANS|Moblin|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 96}}</ref> Unlike the Bulldog moblins of the forest, Boarblins have a porcine-like appearance, resemble the moblins of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past]]'' and live primarily in {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Heights|link}} and {{Term|LANS|Ukuku Prairie|link}}. They behave in an identical manner to {{Plural|LANS|Moblin}} and also appear in a {{Term|LANS|Sword|link}} variant.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Plural|OoS|Pig Warrior}} appear in blue and red variants in {{OoS}}. A {{Term|OoS|Sword|link}}-wielding variant can also be found. Pig Warriors have less health than Moblins in this game. The {{Term|OoS|Great Moblin|link}} is the boss of a gang of {{Plural|OoS|Pig Warrior}}. They operate in {{Term|OoS|Moblin's Keep|link}}, where the {{Term|OoS|Great Moblin}} is fought. After his defeat, the {{Plural|OoS|Pig Warrior}} are driven away to the other land in a {{Term|OoS|Linked Game|link}}. After their defeat in {{Term|OoS|Labrynna|link}}, they can be found in {{Term|OoS|Sunken City|link}}, forced to make and sell {{Plural|OoS|Bomb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bwee... Hoom...The poor Great Moblin. Having to make Bombs here...|Pig Warrior|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Bwee... We'll overcome these troubles and create a new tomorrow!|Pig Warrior|OoS}}</ref>

In a {{Term|OoS|Linked Game}}, a friendly {{Term|OoS|Pig Warrior}} will give {{Term|OoS|Link}} the {{Term|OoS|Tokay|link}} {{Term|OoS|Linking Secret|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bwee! Link! I'm no enemy! I heard a rare secret. Do you want to hear it?|Pig Warrior|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This secret is related to the Tokay people in Labrynna!|Pig Warrior|OoS}}</ref>

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Plural|OoA|Pig Warrior}} appear in blue and red variants in {{OoA}}. A {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}}-wielding variant can also be found. Pig Warriors have less health than Moblins in this game. The {{Term|OoA|Great Moblin|link}} is the boss of a gang of {{Plural|OoA|Pig Warrior}}. They operate in {{Term|OoA|Moblin's Keep|link}}, where the {{Term|OoA|Great Moblin}} is fought. After his defeat, the {{Plural|OoS|Pig Warrior}} are driven away to the other land in a {{Term|OoS|Linked Game|link}}.

==={{EoW|-}}===
Boarblins are pig-like enemies found primarily in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. They come in two main types — [[Boomerang Boarblin|Boomerang Boarblins]] and [[Club Boarblin|Club Boarblins]] — each with stronger variants. A [[Dark Echo|dark version]] can also be encountered.

==Other Appearances==
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, a Boarblin is seen about to execute [[Tarin]] for his crimes against Owablin only for {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} to save him by defeating the foe using his "Link Dynamite Kick".
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Nomenclature==
In Japan, the enemy is known as Butablin, spelled as ブタブリン. Buta is the Japanese word for pig.
