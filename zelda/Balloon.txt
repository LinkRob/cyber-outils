{{Term/Store|Balloon|Balloons|Series, MM3D, BotW, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring objects and items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|How many {{Color|MM3D Red|balloons}} does {{Color|MM3D Red|Romani}}, the girl at the ranch, use during practice?|Keaton|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 330 |edition= Expanded Edition |year= 2018}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWDE}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Stub|MM}}
A Balloon can be found in [[North_Clock_Town|North Clock Town]] being shot at by [[Jim]] with a blowgun. Another appears in the [[Astral Observatory]], blocking the ladder leading out of the sewers. It must be destroyed for [[Link]] to continue.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
Balloons appear as part of the Korok hunting quest. Link will come across pinwheels on his journey. Once he touches the pinwheels, balloons will appear, which he must shoot to make a Korok appear.

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
{{Infobox_Item|image={{Media
 |Sprite TotK= File:TotK Balloon ZD.png
}}|game={{TotK}}|uses= Creating flying machines}}

Balloons appear as one of the [[Zonai Devices]] Link can acquire. They can be attached to objects to lift them off of the ground, and when a [[Flame Emitter]] is placed underneath it to provide heat Link can use it to build a hot air balloon as a means of air travel.

=====Special Attack=====
{{Main|Special Attack}}
{{Term|HWDE|Tingle}} puts on a determined face and begins to pump up his {{Term|HWDE|Balloon}}. With an enthusiastic "Kooloo-limpah", it pops, damaging enemies nearby. This attack hits foes in a wide range around {{Term|HWDE|Tingle}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
