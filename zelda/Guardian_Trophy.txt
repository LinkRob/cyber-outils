==Location and Uses==
GuardianTrophies are obtained as post-battle rewards after finishing any given Chapter's [[Scenario]] battle or a [[Challenge]], with a Trophy earned for every [[Guardian]] kill (be they regular, [[Fire Guardian|Fire]], [[Ice Guardian|Ice]], [[Electric Guardian|Electric]], and [[Malice Guardian|Malice]]) a Warrior obtains.

Guardian Trophies can be used to either complete [[Side Quest#Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Quest]]s or be used in the Hyrule Blacksmith Guild workshop to de rust Rusted Weapons or remove Seals on a weapon.
