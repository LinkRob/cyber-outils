{{Term/Store|Pinkle||Series}} is a [[Fairy]] in {{FPTRR}}.<ref name="Introduction">{{Cite|Tingle. . .  . . . it's me... Pinkle...  Or should I say, I used to be called Pinkle...  That's right... I'm sure you can guess from my appearance... I am the Grand Fairy's daughter...|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref> She is [[Tingle]]'s Fairy assistant, and gives him advice throughout his adventure. She communicates with him through a large [[Nintendo DS]]-looking contraption in his house, and the [[Tingle Tuner]] when he is traveling. Most of the contact between the two is when Tingle arrives in a new area, and before facing a [[dungeon]] [[boss]]. Pinkle wears an outfit which appears to be a pink, feminine version of Tingle's.

==Biography==
[[File:PinkleSpriteFPTRR.png|left]]
Pinkle is the daughter of the [[Grand Fairy]] of the [[Fairy Garden]].<ref name="Introduction"/> Because of that, she was kidnapped and imprisoned by [[Uncle Rupee]].<ref>{{Cite|Uncle Rupee forced me to stay in this chamber against my will...|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref> She has her powers removed and is forced to work for him.<ref>{{Cite|He stole my freedom along with my special fairy powers...|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Uncle Rupee confined me to this chamber...  I was forced to help you on your quest.|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref> If Tingle is able to collect all of the [[Rupee Good]]s, he can free Pinkle from her prison located in the [[Auros Ruins]] to gain an extra story-related segment where he gains a reward.<ref>{{Cite|I'm free! I'm free! I almost forgot... I have to reward you for coming to my rescue, Tingle! Why don't you come over here? I've only ever seen your face through that monitor, but now I see you in the flesh, I can see how handsome you are! So now it's time for me to give you a very special reward! Come on, Tingle! Come this way! Close your eyes now! This is your reward! ...Enjoy!|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref> He also receives some help in the form of added power from the Grand Fairy during his final boss battle which is harnessed in a pink suit that she gives to him.<ref>{{Cite|The Grand Fairy's power that you have just gained... ...it will help you when you really find yourself in trouble...|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref>

After Tingle rescues her, she transforms back into her Fairy form and regains her lost powers. She then departs, but not without leaving two clones of herself; one to save his games,<ref>{{Cite|I almost forgot... One last thing. I made a hologram who can help you in your adventure.  You will still be able to communicate and save your game.|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref> and one to keep him company in his house.<ref>{{Cite|I have sent another hologram to your house... If you feel lonely, you'll always have company!|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Pinkle's rescue has some parallels with [[Link]] obtaining the [[Fierce Deity's Mask]] in {{MM}}. Both the Fierce Deity Mask and Pinkle require Link and Tingle to complete roughly all side quests in their respective games, and rewards them with extra story information and more power during the final battle.
