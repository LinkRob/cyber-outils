'''Yuki Hamada''' is an artist at [[Nintendo]].

==Biography==
Yuki Hamada studied at the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_University_of_the_Arts Tokyo University of the Arts] and joined Nintendo upon his graduation in [[2011]] with a degree in fine arts.<ref>[https://www.geidai.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/1208nintendo.pdf 2017 seminar promo]</ref>
