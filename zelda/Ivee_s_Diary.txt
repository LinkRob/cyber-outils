==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Ivee's Diary}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} found on the table beside {{Term|BotW|Ivee|link|display=Ivee's}} bed, located in the upper part of the {{Term|BotW|East Wind|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. It contains the recipes for three different meals, those being {{Term|BotW|Mushroom Risotto|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Poultry Pilaf|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Egg Pudding|link}}.
