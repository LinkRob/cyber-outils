The {{Term/Store|Ghost Key||Series, PH}} is an item in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
After [[Link]] defeats the [[Cubus Sisters]] in the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}, they will drop the Ghost Key. When Link enters the ship again and goes down to the first maze with the [[Reapling]], he will find a [[Lock#Key Block|block with a keyhole]] in it. After he taps the [[block]] to use the Ghost Key, beyond the block, [[Tetra]] can be found. The key remains unused for the rest of the game, but Link will still preserve it.
