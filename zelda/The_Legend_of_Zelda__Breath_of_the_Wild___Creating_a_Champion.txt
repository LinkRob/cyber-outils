'''''The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild – Creating a Champion''''', known in Japan as '''''The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Master Works''''', is a book released to celebrate the [[The Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary|30th anniversary]] of {{TLoZ|Series}}, focusing solely on {{BotW}}.

==Features==
''Creating a Champion'' features almost 50 pages with sketches and illustrations from [[Takumi Wada]], 296 pages of artwork and commentary about the making of the game from its creators, 55 pages of the history of the game's [[Hyrule]], and interviews with the team members, including [[Hidemaro Fujibayashi]], [[Satoru Takizawa]], Takumi Wada, and [[Eiji Aonuma]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This oversized hardcover is the ultimate companion to the award-winning video game The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild and includes material from both of its DLC packs. This book features nearly fifty pages of sketches and official illustrations from Takumi Wada, two-hundred and ninety-six pages of design artwork and commentary about the making of the game from the creators, a fifty-five-page historical section that divulges the history of Hyrule as it is known in-game, and interviews with key members of the development team--Hidemaro Fujibayashi, Satoru Takizawa, Takumi Wada, and Eiji Aonuma. Witness the making of a champion!|author= |published= |retrieved= May 14, 2018|url= https://www.darkhorse.com/Books/3002-422/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Breath-of-the-Wild--Creating-a-Champion-HC|title= THE LEGEND OF ZELDA: BREATH OF THE WILD--CREATING A CHAMPION HC|site= Dark Horse|type= }}</ref>

Two limited editions were released. The Hero's Edition features an exclusive cover, a deluxe slipcase, a [[Hyrule]] cloth map, a print of the [[Picture of the Champions]], and a glass replica of an {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This deluxe slipcase edition features the royal blue worn by the Hylian Champion and includes the Creating a Champion art book with an exclusive cover, a map of Hyrule printed on cloth, an art print of the Champions' photo Link hangs in his Hateno home, and a beautiful glass replica of a spirit orb. Bring light back to Hyrule with the Hero's Edition!|author= |published= |retrieved= July 16, 2018|url= https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1506710115|title= The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild-Creating a Champion Hero's Edition|site= Amazon|type= }}</ref> The Champions' Edition, exclusive to GameStop retailers, features gilded pages, an exclusive cover, a collector's leather box, a cloth [[Calamity Ganon]] tapestry, an art print of the [[Champion]]s, six mini prints, and four glass orbs with the [[Divine Beast]]s symbols.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This collector's case features burnished leather with gold foil embellishments, the Creating a Champion art book with exclusive cover and gold gilded edges, a cloth Calamity Ganon tapestry, an art print featuring the Champions flying into battle, six mini prints of each Champion as well as their leader, Zelda, and four beautifully crafted glass Champions' orbs with laser etchings of the Divine Beast symbols rendered within. Add the strength of the Champions to your own with the Champions' Edition!|author= |published= |retrieved= July 16, 2018|url= https://www.gamestop.com/collectibles/toys/the-legend-of-zelda-breath-of-the-wild-creating-a-champion-champions-edition-summer-convention-exclusive-2018/164473|title= The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild - Creating a Champion Champions' Edition - Summer Convention Exclusive 2018 |site= GameStop|type= }}</ref>
