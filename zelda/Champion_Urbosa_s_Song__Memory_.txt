"{{Term/Store|Champion Urbosa's Song||Series, BotW}}" is the fourth {{Term|BotW|EX Recovered Memory|link}} in {{TCB}} DLC Pack for {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Champion Urbosa's Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|EX Recovered Memory #4}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|Champion Urbosa's Song (Memory)}}" can be accessed by listening to {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} sing the {{Term|BotW|Champion Urbosa's Song (Song)|link|display=song of the same name}}, which he composed in honor of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Recall {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}'s Champion of sand,<br/>before she was lost to the land.♪<br/>♪Chief {{Color|BotW Blue|Urbosa}}'s heart was strong,<br/>so the princess came along.♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} begins in the throne room of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, with {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} in front of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}, along with her handmaidens and {{Term|BotW|Attendant|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} accepts {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}'s request for her to be a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} and pilot of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Princess of Hyrule. I accept your proposition without hesitation.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> She notes that while the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}} are attempting to resurrect {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, they are also after the treasured {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Not only are they planning to resurrect Calamity Ganon, it seems the Yiga Clan is also after our treasured heirloom.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> She vows not to rest until the {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}} falls.<ref>{{Cite|You have my word. I won't rest until the Calamity falls.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} on behalf of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} and {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gerudo chief Urbosa... On behalf of Hyrule and its king, I thank you.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

That night, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} walk together outside of the walls of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} reveals that she has never seen {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} so serious before.<ref>{{Cite|I've never seen you so serious, Urbosa!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} replies that ten years ago, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} rarely smiled at all, referring to her as her "little bird".<ref>{{Cite|Oh my. I can't say the same. Ten years ago, you rarely smiled, my little bird.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} recognizes the nickname and asks where {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} got it from.<ref>{{Cite|Urbosa, I feel like you've called me little bird before. I was wondering where you got that name from.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

Instead of answering, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} stops in her tracks, and she demands for two passing travelers to face her.<ref>{{Cite|Halt. And face me.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} says that she prefers to face her enemies head on and calls the men traitors.<ref>{{Cite|Unlike you traitors, I prefer to fight my enemies head on.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> She draws the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven|link}} and tells them to do their worst.<ref>{{Cite|Do your worst.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref>

The men finally reveal themselves to be {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}, lunging at {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}. She is able to parry one away with {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker|link}}, which throws him off enough for her to knock him to the ground. The second {{Term|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|display=Footsoldier}} jumps up behind her, and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} shouts in fear.<ref>{{Cite|Urbosa!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} uses her {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury|link|display=Fury}} to electrocute him. She looks back at the soldiers and tells them they may keep their lives, but only out of respect for {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|Out of respect for our princess, you may keep your lives. Now go.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> The two {{Term|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|display=Footsoldiers}} disappear together.

Returning to their conversation,<ref>{{Cite|You asked me why I call you little bird.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} reveals that she calls {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} "little bird" because a friend of hers called her that when she was a child.<ref>{{Cite|When you were but a small child...my dear friend called you that, with the sweetest smile that ever graced our land of Hyrule.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} realizes that the {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} is referring to her mother, the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You mean Mother...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Ten years ago, when the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule|display=Queen}} passed away, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} says that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} became a beacon of light that {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}} needed.<ref>{{Cite|It was 10 long years ago... Your mother had just left this world, yet her little bird still spread her wings and became the beacon of light Hyrule needed.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} admits to forgetting herself sometimes and becoming lost in the past.<ref>{{Cite|Sometimes I forget myself...and get lost in the past.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref>
