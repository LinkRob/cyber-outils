==Location and Uses==
[[File:Phasmid (M).png|left]]
[[File:Phasmid (F).png|left|47px]]
The male Phasmid can be found on the south end of the [[Bridge of Eldin]] and must be collected using the [[Gale Boomerang]] or [[Clawshot]]. The female Phasmid can be found on a wall near the north end of the bridge. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].
