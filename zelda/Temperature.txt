{{Term/Store|Temperature|Temperatures|Series, BotW}} is a recurring gameplay mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|OoT3D, SSHD}}<ref>{{Cite|Temperature Changes<br/>The temperature will change depending on your location and the time of day. It's a good idea to pay close attention to fluctuations in temperature when you're out roaming.|Tips and Tricks|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Term|OoT3D|Temperature}} is briefly used within the {{Term|OoT3D|Death Mountain Crater|link}} and sections of the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}} to hinder {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}'s progression. When exposed to the high {{Term|OoT3D|Temperature}}, a counter will appear and will cause {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} to die unless he is revived by a {{Term|OoT3D|Fairy|link}}, however this can be prevented by wearing the {{Term|OoT3D|Goron Tunic|link}}, allowing Link to stay cool indefinitely and survive the scorching heat.

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Term|SSHD|Link}} is incapable of entering places with high {{Plural|SSHD|Temperature}} like the {{Term|SSHD|Volcano Summit|link}}, but this time {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} is slowly damaged catches fire instead being subjected to a timer. To stay cool when entering areas with high {{Plural|SSHD|Temperature}}, {{Term|SSHD|Link}} must obtain the {{Plural|SSHD|Fireshield Earring|link}} from {{Term|SSHD|Din (Goddess)|link}}'s {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm|link}} trial.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Main|Heat, Freezing Climate, Scorching Climate}}
Unlike in the previous games, {{Term|BotW|Temperature}} is now a gameplay mechanic featured everywhere around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} can check the {{Term|BotW|Temperature}} with a dial on the bottom-right screen or opening his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} and viewing the {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} section with actual measurements. When exposed to extreme {{Plural|BotW|Temperature}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will begin to take damage, but can prevent this by going to a more temperate area, entering bodies of {{Term|BotW|Water|link}} in {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, equipping an elemental {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, wearing certain {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}, or eating {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} with status effects.

When the {{Term|BotW|Temperature}} drops below {{Exp|0°C|32°F}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will experience a basic level of cold {{Term|BotW|Temperature}} that will slowly damage him. When the {{Term|BotW|Temperature}} drops below {{Exp|-10°C|14°F}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will experience an advanced level of cold {{Term|BotW|Temperature}} and will take damage more quickly. Certain bodies of water in {{Plural|BotW|Freezing Climate|link}} will cause {{Term|BotW|Link}} to quickly take damage; no armor, elixir nor food can offer protection against this. Similarly, when the {{Term|BotW|Temperature}} rises above {{Exp|40°C|104°F}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will experience a basic level of {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}}, he will also slowly sustain damage. When the {{Term|BotW|Temperature}} rises above {{Exp|50°C|122°F}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will experience an advanced level of Heat and take damage more quickly. Places that contain lava flows raise {{Plural|BotW|Temperature}} significantly beyond normal {{Term|BotW|Heat}}, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} will malfunction and read "ERROR". Additionally, {{Term|BotW|Link}} and any wooden equipment exposed to the environment will begin to catch fire.
