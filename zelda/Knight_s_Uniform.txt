==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SS|Knight's Uniform}} are given to those who graduated from the {{Term|SS|Knight Academy|link}} and considered worthy the title of a {{Term|SS|Knight|link}}. The design of the uniform is changed every year.<ref>{{Cite|That green uniform is what our knights will be wearing this year. To be honest, I've had my doubts about the color. But oddly enough, seeing you wearing this uniform, I can't imagine a more fitting color for you. It's as though you were born to wear it.|Gaepora|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SS|Pipit|link}}, a {{Term|SS|Knight}} who graduated from the {{Term|SS|Knight Academy}} the previous year than {{Term|SS|Link|link}} wears a yellow uniform.<ref>{{Cite|My bird won the last ceremony, but even I don't think he could beat that red terror of yours.|Pipit|SS}}</ref> One way to graduate from the {{Term|SS|Knight Academy}} is to win the race in the {{Term|SS|Wing Ceremony|link}}, an annual event taking place at {{Term|SS|Skyloft|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As you well know, today's champion will graduate to the next class, bringing him one step closer to knighthood. He will also receive a {{Color|SS Red|gift}} from the young woman chosen to play the role of the {{Color|SS Red|goddess}} in this year's ritual.|Owlan|SS}}</ref> After competing and winning in the {{Term|SS|Wing Ceremony}}, a {{Term|SS|Knight's Uniform}} is made for {{Term|SS|Link|link}}.

Before {{Term|SS|Link}} can set out on his journey to find and rescue {{Term|SS|Zelda}} after she is kidnapped by {{Term|SS|Ghirahim|link}}, {{Term|SS|Gaepora|link}} is not quite satisfied with {{Term|SS|Link}}'s clothing.<ref>{{Cite|You have a great journey before you, Link, and those clothes... They don't look up to the task.|Gaepora|SS}}</ref> He then mentions that the uniform {{Term|SS|Link}} was to receive for winning the race should have been finished by then, and the two return to the {{Term|SS|Knight Academy}} to retrieve it.<ref>{{Cite|The uniform you were to receive for winning the race should be ready by now. A sturdy uniform like that will prove much more suitable for a long journey. You'd better change before you go.|Gaepora|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SS|Link}} will never change his clothing for the rest of his adventure.
