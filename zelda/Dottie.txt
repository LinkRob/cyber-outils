{{Term/Store|Dottie||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Dottie}} is first seen in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, where she remarks on how quickly {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} won the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Each year, there is a sword-fighting tournament at the {{Color|TMC Blue|Picori Festival}}. This year's tournament is already over. I couldn't believe how fast it went! I hear {{Color|TMC Green|the victor was a mysterious man dressed entirely in black}}.|Dottie|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} is turned to stone, {{Term|TMC|Dottie}} wanders near {{Term|TMC|Pina|link}}'s stall in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square|link}}; as the game progresses, she expresses her distaste with fruits and vegetables and her desire for meat.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Nothing but fruits and veggies. What should I cook tonight?|Dottie|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|To tell you the truth, I’m sick of fruit, and I just plain hate veggies!|Dottie|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Don’t they have anything other than fruits and vegetables here?|Dottie|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I wish we had a butcher...|Dottie|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Dottie}} also wonders when {{Term|TMC|Beedle|link}} will arrive in the nearby stall. If {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} does not use the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}} to clean the dust away from the empty stall, {{Term|TMC|Dottie}} will become progressively more detailed in her descriptions of {{Term|TMC|Beedle}} and his wares as {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s journey continues.<ref>{{Cite|This stall is kind of dirty. I guess that guy won't be setting up here today...|Dottie|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This stall is all dirty. I guess the guy that usually sells his stuff here isn't going to show...|Dottie|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This stall is such a mess! Does that mean Beedle's not going to show up?|Dottie|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Tch! This stall is such a mess! Maybe that's why Beedle’s not here selling his Picolyte.|Dottie|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Tch! Would you look at this mess? I'm sure Beedle could use this spot to sell Picolyte if it weren't such a pigsty.|Dottie|TMC}}</ref>

She is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? OK, but let's be careful about it!|Dottie|TMC}}</ref>
