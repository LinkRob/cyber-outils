'''''The Legend of Zelda × Mario Kart 8''''' is a DLC pack for {{MK8}} released on November 13, 2014.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 311}}</ref> It contains 3 new characters, 4 new vehicles, 2 new vehicle parts, and 2 new cups of 4 tracks each.

===Bonuses===
If both this DLC pack and {{smw|Animal Crossing × Mario Kart 8|''Animal Crossing'' × {{MK8|-}}}} are purchased, the player will be able to use 8 new alternate colors of both {{smw|Yoshi}} and {{smw|Shy Guy}}. The player gains access as soon as the packs are purchased, so if both packs were pre-ordered, the player could use these colors right away, before the release of either pack. These colors cannot be selected by CPU players.
