

== Features & Overview ==

The labyrinth is in a isolated location in the [[Akkala Sea Depths]]. The [[Kuhsagi Lightroot]] is located of the roof of the labyrinth. It corresponds to the [[Lomei Labyrinth Island]] on the surface, and the [[Lomei Sky Labyrinth]] in the sky. there is a [[chasm]] in the middle of lomei labyrinth island that leads to it. there are poes scattered around and a [[Flux Construct III]] in the middle. after defeating the construct you can talk to the ruler of dragons and a chest will apear giving you the [[evil spirit]] armor.
