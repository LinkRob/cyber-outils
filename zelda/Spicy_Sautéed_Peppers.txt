==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Spicy Sautéed Peppers}} are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Since their only ingredient is {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper}}, there is no default version of them. Eating these meals will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}. They'll also heal some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The heat gives the {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper}} a sweeter taste.<ref name="Inventory"/>
