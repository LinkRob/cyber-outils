==Features and Overview==
==={{TWW|-}}===
The Wind Shrine is a shrine on [[Dragon Roost Island]] dedicated to [[Zephos]] and [[Cyclos]], two brother gods who control the [[Wind]]. Consequently, it bears two monuments to the brothers. Some time prior to Link's arrival at Dragon Roost Island, Cyclos's monument was destroyed and Cyclos flew into a rage, creating cyclones across the [[Great Sea]] to harass seafarers.<ref name="Monument">{{Cite|It saddens me to say that my brother, {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Cyclos}}, is miffed about his monument here being broken, and now he spends his time creating cyclones to torment people with.|Zephos|TWWHD}}</ref>

Upon approaching the Wind Shrine, Link can examine the two monuments.<ref>{{Cite|There appear to be markings that indicate specific directions.|N/A|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's broken...|N/A|TWWHD}}</ref> By drawing the [[Wind Waker (Item)|Wind Waker]] in front of the intact monument, Link can learn the "[[Wind's Requiem]]",<ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Wind's Requiem}}!|N/A|TWWHD}}</ref> granting Link an ability that allows him to change the direction of the Wind. After learning the monument's song, Zephos will appear to Link and compliment the breeze.<ref>{{Cite|Yep! That's a miiiiiighty nice breeze!|Zephos|TWWHD}}</ref> Afterwards, Zephos will introduce himself and address Link as the new Wind Waker.<ref>{{Cite|The name's Zephos. I'm the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|god of winds}}. So, you're the new Wind Waker, are you?|Zephos|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Great, great. For a beginner, you've got a nice wind sense about you. I like you, kid!|Zephos|TWWHD}}</ref> He also explains how the "Wind's Requiem" can be used to manipulate the Wind, musing that it can be used for both good and evil,<ref>{{Cite|That tune you just picked up? Well, it gives you {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|control}} over the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|direction the wind blows}}. Depending on how it's used, wind can be a good thing...or a very bad thing. You want an example of it being a bad thing, then you should see my brother.|Zephos|TWWHD}}</ref> citing his brother, Cyclos, as an example of the Wind's misuse.<ref name="Monument"/> Before leaving Link's side, Zephos asks that Link punish his enraged brother for his behavior.<ref>{{Cite|So if you encounter any cyclones at sea, chastise my brother for me, will you? And THAT...is my request for you!|Zephos|TWWHD}}</ref>
