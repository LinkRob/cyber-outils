{{Term/Store|Spiketrap|Spiketraps|Series, TPHD|plural}},{{Exp Game|TPHD}}<ref>{{Cite eGuide|quote= |game= TPHD |publisher= Prima |section= {{Small|Dungeon 6: Temple of Time}}<br/>Task 4: Face the Deadly Darknut in Combat}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Spiked-Log Trap|Spiked-Log Traps|OoT, OoT3D|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= CE|publisher= Nintendo|page= 52}}</ref> {{Term/Store|Rolling Pin|Rolling Pins|ALBW|plural}},{{Exp Game|ALBW}}<ref>{{Cite eGuide|quote= {{Big|Room 7: Rolling Pin Chamber}} |game= ALBW |publisher= Prima |section= House of Gales}}</ref> and '''Spiked Rollers'''{{Exp Game|EoW}}, are recurring traps in {{TLoZ|Series}}.
==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub}}

==={{LA|-}}===
A {{Term|LANS|Spiketrap}} is used by the mini-boss of {{Term|LANS|Tail Cave|link}}, {{Term|LANS|Rolling Bones|link}}, in {{LA}}. {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} must jump over the trap with the {{Term|LANS|Roc's Feather|link}} in order to avoid damage.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Plural|OoT3D|Spiketrap}} are found in several {{Plural|OoT3D|Dungeon}} in {{OoT}}. {{Plural|OoT3D|Spiketrap}} found {{Term|OoT3D|Inside the Deku Tree|link}} are unique in that they do not move along a path, only spinning in place above a pool of water. The puzzle in the room with the trap requires going underneath it aboard a floating platform in order to pass through the room.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Plural|MM3D|Spiketrap}} appear in several places in {{MM}}. {{Plural|MM3D|Spiketrap}} found {{Term|MM3D|Beneath the Well|link}} do not always bounce back and forth at the same speed, but do move in patterns that might have to be judged in order to get past them safely.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Stub}}

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Stub}}

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Plural|TPHD|Spiketrap}} appear within the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time|link}} in {{TP}}. They can be demolished by using the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod|link}} on the resident statue, then using its hammer to smash the obstacles in one hit.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Stub}}

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Spiketrap}} appear in the {{Term|PH|Sand Temple|link}} in {{ST}}. {{Term|PH|Link|link}} can halt their movement using the {{Term|PH|Sand Wand|link}} and walk across them to reach new parts of the {{Term|PH|Dungeon}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Plural|ALBW|Rolling Pin}} appear in several {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon}} in {{ALBW}}. In the {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} must use the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}} to make well-timed "jumps" over some {{Plural|ALBW|Rolling Pin}}. In the {{Term|ALBW|Swamp Palace|link}}, he must swim underneath one en route to obtaining the {{Term|ALBW|Blue Mail|link}}. In {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock|link}}, he simply needs to dodge several of them on the pathway to {{Term|ALBW|Grinexx|link}}'s lair.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Spiketrap}} appear solely in {{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}} in {{TFH}}. In the second part of this {{Term|TFH|Level|link}}, a short {{Term|TFH|Spiketrap}} rolls through a narrow hallway, followed by a wider {{Term|TFH|Spiketrap|display=Trap}} in a square-shaped room. The {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} can throw an Anubis-like stone statue in the {{Plural|TFH|Spiketrap|display=Traps}}' paths to limit their range of movement, allowing the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to pass. Alternately, striking a {{Term|TFH|Spiketrap}} with the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}} will reverse its direction.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Spiked Roller|A large log with many knobbly bits sticking out of it. Rolls downhill until something stops it.<ref name="Spiked Roller">{{Cite|A large log with many knobbly bits sticking out of it. Rolls downhill until something stops it.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Spiked Rollers are spiked logs found east of [[Kakariko Village]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which can be summoned for four Echo energy.
