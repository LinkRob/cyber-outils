'''Ranger Constructs''' are friendly [[Construct|constructs]] from {{TotK}}.

==Characteristics==
Ranger Constructs are caretakers of the [[Sky Islands]], often seen maintaining the environment. Unlike [[Soldier Construct|Soldier Constructs]], they are not hostile toward [[Link]] and can be approached for conversation instead of combat.
