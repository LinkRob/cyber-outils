{{Term/Store|Dunma||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dunma|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Dunma}} is a guard of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} who wields a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear|link}}. She is the daughter of {{Term|BotW|Rivan|link}} and the granddaughter of {{Term|BotW|Trello|link}}.<ref name="Father"/><ref name="Trello"/> Though she is offended when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks her age,<ref name="Lady"/> she will clarify that she is less than 100 years old.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* Let's just say I'm less than 100 years old. Can't you tell?|Dunma|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Rivan}} gets carried away speaking to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dunma}} will interrupt to remind him that they are on duty.<ref name="Father"/> She later notes how excited {{Term|BotW|Rivan}} was to see him.<ref>{{Cite|It looks like Father was rather excited to speak with you... Oh, well.|Dunma|BotW}}</ref> Since it is their first time meeting, she introduce herself to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A Hylian, eh? I believe this is our first meeting, yes? My name is Dunma.|Dunma|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if she is female, {{Term|BotW|Dunma}} comments on how observant he is.<ref>{{Cite|[You're female, right?] ...My, how observant you are. Is there a problem?|Dunma|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Dunma}} believes herself to be less formal than the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} elders.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so much less formal than the elders... aren't I?|Dunma|BotW}}</ref> She explains to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} is looking for a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} and instructs him to meet with {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} for more information.<ref>{{Cite|At any rate, right now {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} is looking for a Hylian warrior. For more detailed information, please go speak with King Dorephan in the back.|Dunma|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Dunma}} about {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}}, who she reveals is the ruler of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} and the father of {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|King Dorephan is the regal ruler of Zora's Domain. He is also the father of Prince Sidon and Lady Mipha.|Dunma|BotW}}</ref> She gives him directions to the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}} and reminds him to mind his manners.<ref>{{Cite|To get to King Dorephan's throne room, go up that big staircase and then up one more floor. Take care to mind your manners!|Dunma|BotW}}</ref> According to her, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} is very sociable and popular among the younger {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}.<ref>{{Cite|Prince Sidon is King Dorephan's son. Despite his position as true successor to the throne...he's very sociable. That makes him popular with the youth.|Dunma|BotW}}</ref> However, this demeanor is not understood by the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} elders.<ref>{{Cite|However, there are those among the elders who do not comprehend why he behaves as he does.|Dunma|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Dunma}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that it has been some time since {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} has seen the sun.<ref>{{Cite|It's been a while since we've seen the sun here...|Dunma|BotW}}</ref> She wonders if the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} has stopped spouting water.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder if {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} has stopped spouting water everywhere...|Dunma|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has received the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dunma}} calls him no ordinary man and proclaims that he will always be welcome in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|You are no ordinary man... You are always welcome here in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}!|Dunma|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
