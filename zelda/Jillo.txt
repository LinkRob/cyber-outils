==Biography==
{{Term|ALBW|Jillo}} is a baby {{Term|ALBW|Turtle|link}} who resides in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake|link}} with his {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle|link}} and his brothers, {{Term|ALBW|Tallo|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Sabro|link}}. He is the middle-sized of the three brothers, and has a sensitive personality.<ref name="sensitive"/>

When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} first arrives at {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake}}, he can encounter {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle}}, who pleads for his help in finding her lost babies.<ref>{{Cite|Oh no! I got separated from my little turtles. You have to help me look for them—you just have to!|Mama Turtle|ALBW}}</ref> {{Term|ALBW|Jillo}} can be found in a shallow area in the northwestern part of the lake, surrounded by {{Plural|ALBW|Ku|link}} spitting fireballs at him. After {{Term|ALBW|Link}} defeats all the {{Plural|ALBW|Ku}}, {{Term|ALBW|Jillo}} thanks him for the help and swims off to rejoin his mother.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, thank you, thank you, thank you! That was so terrifying! I didn't know what I was going to do!|Jillo|ALBW}}</ref> Once all three baby {{Plural|ALBW|Turtle}} have been rescued, they all climb onto {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle}}'s back, forming a stack that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can stand on to receive a ride around {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake}}, including access to {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found all of my babies! Thank you so much! I'm sorry I don't have anything I can offer you as a reward. If you need a ride somewhere, you can go ahead and hop on, though!|Mama Turtle|ALBW}}</ref>
