{{Term/Store|Bertie's Baby||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS |publisher= Prima |page= 232}}</ref> 

==Biography==
{{Term|SSHD|Bertie's Baby}} is {{Term|SSHD|Luv}} and {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}}'s crying infant child. Every once in a while, she throws a fit due to her missing {{Term|SSHD|Rattle|link}}. {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}}'s job is to take care of her, and due to her crying, he is unable to sleep. The baby's favorite toy is her {{Term|SSHD|Rattle}}, which a bird stole during the events of the game, mistaking it for food. This has caused major temper tantrums, making her and her father {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}} sleepless every night. Unfortunately, {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}} is unable to calm her, and her mother {{Term|SSHD|Luv}} is not able to do anything about it, since she sleeps through it. {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} can retrieve the {{Term|SSHD|Rattle}} by using the {{Term|SSHD|Clawshot|link}} on the vines near the {{Term|SSHD|Waterfall Cave|link}}, then jumping off and landing on the bird's nest. Then, he must use the {{Term|SSHD|Gust Bellow|link}} to clean up the sand covering the toy. Giving it to {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}} cheers up the infant, and finally allowing a peaceful sleep.
