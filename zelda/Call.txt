{{Term/Store|Call|Calls|Series|plural}} are a recurring mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They are sounds that summon a character, enemy, or {{Term||Animal|link}}, typically a {{Term||Mount|link}} or {{Term||Bird|link}}.<ref name="TP Mimic">{{Cite|Hey, why don't you go stand next to it and press {{Icon|TPHD A}} to mimic that {{Color|TPHD Red|bird call}}?|Midna|TPHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Remember to {{Color|SS Green|call your bird}} promptly with {{Icon|SS D-Pad Down}} once you dive off the edge.|Owlan|SS}}</ref> A {{Term||Call}} may also refer to the {{Term||Instrument|link}} used to make the sound.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|TPHD Red|horse call}}! Ilia made this just for Epona. Now you can call Epona even when you can't find horse grass.|N/A|TPHD}}</ref> In some games, {{Plural||Bokoblin|link}} blow horns to call for reinforcements, or to alert their camp when they spot {{Term||Link|link}}.

{{Plural|Series|Call}} made indoors fail to summon the callee, as they cannot hear them.{{Fact}} 

==Appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Main|Flute}}
{{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} can play a tune on the {{Term|ALttP|Flute|link}} to summon the [[Flute Boy's Bird]], who can [[Warping|warp]] {{Term|ALttP|Link}} across {{Term|ALttP|Hyrule|link}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Main|Epona's Song, Scarecrow's Song}}
Once {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} escapes from {{Term|OoT3D|Lon Lon Ranch|link}} with {{Term|OoT3D|Epona|link}}, he can play "{{Term|OoT3D|Epona's Song|link}}" to summon {{Term|OoT3D|Epona}} from anywhere in {{Term|OoT3D|Hyrule|link}} (but only as an adult). {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can play the "{{Term|MM3D|Scarecrow's Song|link}}" to summon {{Term|MM3D|Pierre|link}}. 

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Epona's Song, Oath to Order, Scarecrow's Song}}
{{Term|MM3D|Link}} can play "{{Term|MM3D|Epona's Song|link}}" to summon {{Term|MM3D|Epona}} from anywhere in {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}. As in {{OoT|-}}, he can play the "{{Term|MM3D|Scarecrow's Song}}" to summon {{Term|MM3D|Pierre}}. He must play the "{{Term|MM3D|Oath to Order|link}}" to summon the {{Plural|MM3D|Guardian|link}} to {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}}.

==={{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}===
{{Main|Dimitri's Flute, Ricky's Flute, Moosh's Flute}}
{{Term|OoS|Link}} gains the ability to summon one of the three {{Plural|OoS|Animal Companion|link}} based on whose Flute he obtains.
* Playing {{Term|OoS|Dimitri's Flute|link}} summons {{Term|OoS|Dimitri|link}} the dragon
* Playing {{Term|OoS|Moosh's Flute|link}} summons {{Term|OoS|Moosh|link}} the bear
* Playing {{Term|OoS|Ricky's Flute|link}} summons {{Term|OoS|Ricky|link}} the kangaroo

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Servants of the Tower, Medli, Makar}}
In {{TWW}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can {{Term|TWWHD|Call}} nearby characters to have them follow him. This applies only to the characters on which he can use the {{Term|TWWHD|Command Melody|link}}: the {{Plural|TWWHD|Servant of the Tower|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Medli|link}}, and {{Term|TWWHD|Makar|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Ocarina of Wind}}
{{Term|TMC|Link}} can play a tune on the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}} to summon {{Term|TMC|Zeffa|link}}, who can warp {{Term|TMC|Link}} across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Main|Grass Whistles, Horse Call}}
{{Term|TPHD|Link}} can pick {{Plural|TPHD|Grass Whistle|link}} and use them on-the-spot to summon an {{Term|TPHD|Animal}} – he cannot carry the grass with him. He whistles "{{Term|TPHD|Epona's Song}}" with {{Term|TPHD|Horse Grass}} to summon {{Term|TPHD|Epona}}. {{Term|TPHD|Hawk Grass}} summons a {{Term|TPHD|Hawk|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Hanch|link}} is also seen using {{Term|TPHD|Hawk Grass}}. 

At {{Term|TPHD|Lake Hylia|link}}, a {{Term|TPHD|Shadow Bulblin|link}} archer uses {{Term|TPHD|Horse Grass}} to summon the {{Term|TPHD|Twilit Carrier Kargarok|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TPHD|publisher= Prima|page= 170}}</ref> After {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} defeats the {{Term|TPHD|Shadow Bulblin}}, he can summon the {{Term|TPHD|Twilit Carrier Kargarok}} by sitting at the {{Term|TPHD|Hawk Grass}} spot and mimicking the {{Term|TPHD|Call}} with a howl. Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} dispels the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight|link}} at {{Term|TPHD|Lake Hylia}}, howling at the {{Term|TPHD|Hawk Grass}} summons a {{Term|TPHD|Kargarok}} used to play {{Term|TPHD|Fruit-Pop-Flight|link}}. 

Once her memory is restored, {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}} gifts a {{Term|TPHD|Horse Call|link}} which allows him to play "{{Term|TPHD|Epona's Song}}" anywhere.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Main|Phantom Zelda, Song of Birds}}
When {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}} or {{Term|ST|Phantom Zelda|link}} is following {{Term|ST|Link}}, he can {{Term|ST|Call}} her to his current location.<ref>{{Cite|This is the {{Color|ST Light Red|call icon}}. Touch this icon when you and I are on separate paths, and I'll come to you!|Phantom Zelda|ST}}</ref> He can also play the "{{Term|ST|Song of Birds|link}}" to attract nearby {{Plural|ST|Bird|link}}, including: {{Plural|ST|Whip Bird|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Dove|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Crow|link}}, and {{Plural|ST|Cucco|link}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Main|Monster Horn}}
Residents of {{Term|SS|Skyloft|link}} use finger whistling to call their {{Term|SS|Loftwing|link}}. On the {{Term|SS|Surface|link}}, {{Term|SS|Fi|link}} can use a telepathic signal to call {{Term|SS|LD-301S Scrapper|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Master, I will send word to the robot using telepathic transmission.|Fi|SS}}</ref> The signal registers as short piercing noise.

{{Term|SS|Red Bokoblin|link}} leaders can summon more {{Plural|SS|Bokoblin|link}} by blowing their {{Term|SS|Monster Horn|link}}.
