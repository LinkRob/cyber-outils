The {{Term/Store|Giant Blade||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Impa]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Water}}.

==Variations==
The default Giant Blade is the '''[[Giant's Knife]]'''. The second form of the Giant Blade is '''Biggoron's Knife''', followed by '''[[Biggoron's Sword]]'''.

The Lv.★ Giant Blade is the '''8-Bit Boomerang?''', which is based the [[Boomerang]] from {{TLoZ}}. It was added to {{HW|-}} with the Master Quest Pack DLC. In {{HW|DE}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Boomerang? replaces Biggoron's Sunblade when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
The camera zooms in behind Impa as she closes in on her targets. The screen turns dark until it is almost black - aside from Impa who then slashes with her blade, 'cutting' the screen back open and damaging foes. This attack hits in a wedge shape ahead of Impa.
