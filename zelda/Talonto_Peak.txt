{{Term/Store|Talonto Peak||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Talonto Peak'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Talonto Peak is located in the [[Hebra Mountains]] between the [[Hebra South Summit]] and [[Hebra Peak]]. The Talonto Peak is a steep incline with a few small ledges leading up to the summit. At the top of the peak, a single [[Tree]] sprouts into the air, and a small ledge juts out over the side of the mountain.
