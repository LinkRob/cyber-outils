{{Term/Store|Kabetta||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Kabetta|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} is the youngest of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers|link}},<ref name="Brother"/> consisting of himself and his brothers, {{Term|BotW|Bayge|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Heehl|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} and his brothers are passionate about training in difficult feats of strength and endurance. However, he is more docile and meek than the rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|So sweaty... Brother...}} You really are somethin' else! Nothin' but respect, my brother! OK, that tears it. I'm gonna try to beat the Gut Check Challenge too! I mean, I will tomorrow... Maybe...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> They all live in a house in southern {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, which has been left with a {{Term|BotW|Sign|link}} saying they have gone to {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock|link}} to train.<ref>{{Cite|We are currently training at Gut Check Rock north of Death Mountain. Come watch us train at Gut Check Rock!<br/>- Bayge, Heehl, and Kabetta|Signpost|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} is involved in the "{{Term|BotW|Test of Will|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} and the other {{Term|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} have traveled  to their ancestral training ground to train themselves to withstand the heat in {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is our ancestral training ground! Us three Goron Blood Brothers are in the midst of a {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's too hot to handle in Goron City...so we came here to train ourselves...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We're Gorons, but we can't really stand this extreme heat either... That's why we came here to train! After this, we'll be able to handle the extra heat in Goron City, no problem.|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} and the others invite {{Term|BotW|Link}} to partake in their challenge.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah! Come try the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}} yourself...brother!|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} will be inspired by his choice.<ref>{{Cite|It's inspiring...brother!|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> The three {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} will then step onto the platform, and the contest of endurance will begin once {{Term|BotW|Link}} joins them.<ref>{{Cite|When you're ready, step up onto this platform! Then the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}} will commence!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

To complete the challenge, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to outlast the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} on the platform.<ref>{{Cite|The rules are simple... The challenger who can handle the intense heat within the ring for the longest without leaving the ring is the winner.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> During the contest, {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} will have difficulty adjusting to the heat.<ref>{{Cite|Phew! Phew!|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} is the first challenger to collapse.<ref>{{Cite|I can't...go on...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> After all three {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} have collapsed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be declared the winner.

Once their first contest has been cleared, the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} then challenge {{Term|BotW|Link}} to their contest f ultimate endurance, which {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} explains is the toughest challenge known to {{Plural|BotW|Goron}}.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, I'm grantin' you the highest honor—the chance to participate in the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of ULTIMATE endurance}}!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's where our ancestors trained... It's considered the toughest test known to Goron kind!|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> He goes on to say that the next ring is even harder to withstand than the previous one.<ref>{{Cite|That special training area is even harder than the last ring...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> Passing this test is supposed to reveal an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's said that passin' this test will reveal a {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}...or some such!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

While watching {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempt the second challenge, {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} remarks that his soul is on fire.<ref>{{Cite|My soul is on FIRE...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Burning...soul...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has cleared the trial, {{Term|BotW|Joloo Nah Shrine|link}} will rise from the ground nearby, which {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} is surprised by.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! That's a {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}...or some such! It really appeared, just like that! So the legend is true, brother!!|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has yet to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} encourages him to try it out.<ref>{{Cite|I couldn't win the {{Color|BotW Red|Gut Check Challenge}}... But you beat the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of ultimate endurance}}, so ya may stand a chance.|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already cleared the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}}, {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} proclaims his respect for {{Term|BotW|Link}} and resolves to beat it himself.<ref>{{Cite|You really are somethin' else! Nothin' but respect, my brother! OK, that tears it. I'm gonna try to beat the Gut Check Challenge too! {{Small|I mean, I will tomorrow... Maybe...}}|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} also appears at the base of the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}} in northern {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}, laying on the ground in exhaustion after failing his {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}} training.<ref name="Brother"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Small|So sweaty... Brother...}} I couldn't win the {{Color|BotW Red|Gut Check Challenge}}, so I really have no right to say this, but I...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> Despite his own failures, he encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}} for himself.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I think you should try it for yourself whenever ya think you're ready.|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, which is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} will train at {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond|link}}, since they heard {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} did the same thing.<ref>{{Cite|Do ya know why {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} chose this place to train, brother? ...Neither do we! But us Goron Blood Brothers are gonna find out by completing the training ourselves!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> The goal of the training is to stand on the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} that has appeared on top of {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond}}.<ref>{{Cite|You see that {{Color|BotW Red|glowing ring}} in the lava? Think of it as a target, and... {{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|stand on the lava, brother}}!}}|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that they are unable to stand on {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|So sweaty... Brother...}} Even Gorons can't walk on lava...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> He wonders whether {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} actually completed this training, but he retracts that statement when he realizes it sounds like he is doubting {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder if {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}} really did this training... Not that I doubt him, brother!|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} then get together to strategize.<ref>{{Cite|Time to strategize, brothers!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has passed through the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika Shrine|link}} will rise from the ground. {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} is relieved by the disappearance of the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, as that means he will no longer have to attempt the training.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|So sweaty... Brother...}} The {{Color|BotW Red|glowing ring}} is gone. Now I don't have to do that crazy training. Thank goodness...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* {{Term|Series|Bayge}} is one of several characters that will hit {{Term|Series|Link}} back if he attacks them with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}. Uniquely, he only does this on {{Term|Series|Mount Nabooru}}, as he will have no reaction if {{Term|Series|Link}} attacks him on {{Term|Series|Gut Check Rock}}. These attacks deal no damage.
