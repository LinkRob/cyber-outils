The {{Term/Store|Kuh Takkar Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kuh Takkar Shrine}}<br/>Melting Ice Hazard}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Melting Ice Hazard''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Kuh Takkar Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, at the base of {{Term|BotW|Laparoh Mesa|link}}. To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must melt the chunk of ice that is frozen around the shrine with any flammable item in order to gain access to the shrine. As he enters, he's greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kuh Takkar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kuh Takkar. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kuh Takkar|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
An {{Term|BotW|Ice Block|link}} can be found right in front of {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. When he approaches it, {{Term|BotW|Kuh Takkar|link}} can be heard, telling {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the path forward will only open when he offers a {{Term|BotW|Ice Block|display=block of ice}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Offer before me a block of ice. Only then will the path forward open.}}|Kuh Takkar|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Kuh Takkar Shrine}} features one chamber with multiple columns of {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. In order to complete the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must bring an {{Term|BotW|Ice Block}} to {{Term|BotW|Kuh Takkar}} without melting it in the {{Term|BotW|Fire}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must then use a metal block placed in {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} in order to traverse through the {{Term|BotW|Fire}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} does this, the door will open and he will meet {{Term|BotW|Kuh Takkar}}.

{{Term|BotW|Kuh Takkar}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kuh Takkar|BotW}}</ref> and will wish him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kuh Takkar|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} within a pillar blocked by {{Term|BotW|Fire}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Frostblade|link}}.
