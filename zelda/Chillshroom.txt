==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Chillshroom}}
{{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom}} are found at the base of trees on the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}. They can also be bought in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Ardin|link}}. When eaten, they restore half of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Chillshroom}} that is exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} will turn into a {{Term|BotW|Toasty Chillshroom|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} made using a Chillshroom will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> They can also be used at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} to create {{Term|BotW|Blue Dye|link}}.
