==Location and Uses==
The Extravagant Saddle is one of several {{Plural|BotW|Saddle|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain. They can be won by beating {{Term|BotW|Blynne|link|display=Blynne's}} record of one minute and fifteen seconds at the {{Term|BotW|Mounted Archery Course|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My best time's {{Color|BotW Red|1 minute 15 seconds}}. Think you can beat that?|Blynne|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But a man's gotta abide by his word, so here's that {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}} I promised.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> This type of Saddle is commonly used in festivals.<ref name="Inventory"/> Its delicate embroidery and gem arrangements leaves a lasting impression, making it perfect for standing out.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can equip Extravagant Saddles to his {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} by speaking to workers at {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Extravagant Saddle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> Similar to other Saddles, they can't be worn by {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.
