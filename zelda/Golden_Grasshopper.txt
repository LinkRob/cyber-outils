==Location and Uses==
[[File:Grasshopper (M).png|left]]
[[File:Grasshopper (F).png|left|47px]]
The male Grasshopper can be found on northwest of the path leading into {{Term|TP|Kakariko Village|link}}. The female Grasshopper can be found in the northeast of the field north of Kakariko Village. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].
