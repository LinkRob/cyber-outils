{{Term/Store|White Tektite|White Tektites|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: White Tektite|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|White Tektite}} are stronger variants of the {{Term|CoH|Blue Tektite|link}}, but share the same beat pattern as they move the beat of the music. They move every two beats, crouching for one beat before jumping on the next beat. Like other {{Plural|CoH|Tektite|link}}, they can only move diagonally and can jump onto short cliffs and objects. In [[Symphony of the Mask]], they wear [[Skull Mask]]s and are known as White Skulltula Tektites, putting [[Skull Kid]] in a web should they make contact with him. They can be disposed of the same way, though the Skull Mask they wear serves as an extra heart.
