{{Term/Store|Moink|Moinks|Series, ST|plural}} are {{Plural|ST|Animal|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 193}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Moinks can only be found while riding the [[Spirit Train]]. They resemble a white pig with black spots on their body. [[Link]] can blow the whistle to scare them while on the [[Spirit Tracks (Object)|Spirit Tracks]]. If the Spirit Train crashes against a Moink, it will turn red and charge at it. The same will happen if Link shoots one with the [[Cannon]]. Moinks disappear after obtaining the [[Ocean Rail Map]]. 

==Nomenclature==
Moinks gets their name from "moo" and "oink," as they appear to be a [[pig]] with cow spots.
