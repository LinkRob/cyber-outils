The {{Term/Store|Southwestern Sea||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 284}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea}} is a quadrant in the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}} located in the southwest. The {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea}} is divided into eastern and western halves by a line of rocky islets. The western half can only be accessed after {{Term|PH|Link|link}} has acquired a {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}} for the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} to break through the islets.<ref>{{Cite|Hold up! Watch where your course is taking us, Link! Your course has us in the proximity of certain doom! These rocks look brutal! No way to bust through unless we get our hands on a {{Color|PH Red|cannon}} or something! Hm...a {{Color|PH Red|cannon}}...|Linebeck|PH}}</ref>

The eastern half of the {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea}} contains {{Term|PH|Mercay Island|link}}, the {{Term|PH|Isle of Ember|link}}, and {{Term|PH|Cannon Island|link}}. The {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea|display=Sea}}'s western half contains {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}, {{Term|PH|Spirit Island|link}}, and {{Term|PH|Nyave|link}}'s {{Term|PH|Traveler's Ship|link}}.

{{Term|PH|Beedle|link}}'s {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship|link|display=Shop Ship}} can occasionally be found wandering the waters of the {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea}}, though it will eventually leave for another quadrant of the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King}}. After obtaining the {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate|link}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} can speak to a {{Term|PH|Golden Frog|link}} south of {{Term|PH|Mercay Island}} and northwest of {{Term|PH|Molida Island}} to learn the corresponding symbols that allow him to {{Term|PH|Warping|link|display=Warp}} to those locations.<ref>{{Cite|Ooof! Nice shot, buddy! The big frog spread the world about you! So, want to know what to draw on the {{Color|PH Red|Cyclone Slate}} to fly right here? Here goes! Pay attention!|Golden Frog|PH}}</ref> The {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}} will appear to the west of {{Term|PH|Mercay Island}} once {{Term|PH|Link}} has defeated {{Term|PH|Bellum|link}} at the bottom of the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}}, awaiting the final battle.
