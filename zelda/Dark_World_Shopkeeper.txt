{{Term/Store|Dark World Shopkeeper|Dark World Shopkeepers|Series, ALttP|plural}} are characters in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Dark World Shopkeepers are found in the [[Village of Outcasts]] Shop, the shop south of the [[Lake of Ill Omen]] (located exactly where the [[Witch's Hut|Magic Shop]] is in the [[Light World]]), the [[Dark World Shield Shop]], and the Death Mountain Shop. One of the Dark World Shopkeepers owns the [[Treasure Chest Game]] Shop in the Village of Outcasts.
