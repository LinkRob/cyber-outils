{{Term/Store|Ravio's Bracelet||Series, ALBW}} is an item in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[File:ALBW Ravio's Bracelet Inactive Icon.png|left|35px]]
[[Link]] receives Ravio's Bracelet from [[Ravio]] as payment to stay at [[Link's House|his house]], as he is unable to pay rent at the time.<ref>{{Cite|Here-take this. I can't pay rent yet. But it's something, at least.|Ravio|ALBW}}</ref> When received, it is in an inactive state, which prevents its usage. While trying to rescue [[Osfala]] from the [[Eastern Palace]], Link encounters [[Yuga]] as he turns the Sage into a painting.<ref>{{Cite|Ha! After all your posing, all your preening...just look at you now! Now, my fine fellow, prepare to be made into my latest work of art! You'll have a privileged place in my collection of Sages!|Yuga|ALBW}}</ref> Link engages Yuga in battle and initially seems to defeat him, but Yuga uses his power to turn Link into a [[Painting]] on the wall.<ref>{{Cite|Enough of this! You're going to spoil everything! You've forced my hand. I must brush you aside!|Yuga|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What a sad, drab painting you make. You can rot there for all I care.|Yuga|ALBW}}</ref> Soon after Yuga departs, the bracelet begins to glow from within the Painting, allowing Link to escape and return to normal. From then on, Link is able to [[Wall Merging|merge]] into walls at will.<ref>{{Cite|Ravio's bracelet saved you? But how? Anyway, it seems that now you can merge into walls!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

After Yuga is defeated, [[Princess Hilda]] uses Ravio's Bracelet to send Link and [[Princess Zelda]] back to [[Hyrule]]. This utterly exhausts the power of the bracelet, causing it to shatter.

==Trivia==
*Ravio remarks that he would be interested in using the bracelet to "steal Yuga's power."<ref>{{Cite|Pretty neat that you can turn into a painting. Wowie-wow! I wish I was you! I wonder if I could steal that power from this weird Yuga guy too...|Ravio|ALBW}}</ref> This seems to indicate that Ravio's Bracelet received its power by absorbing Yuga's magic in the Eastern Palace.
