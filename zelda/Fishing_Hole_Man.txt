The {{Term/Store|Fishing Hole Man|Fishing Hole Men|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239 {{Exp Game|OoT}} & 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Fishing Hole Man owns a [[Fishing Pond]] in [[Lake Hylia]]. He allows [[Link]] to fish for an unlimited time at a cost of 20 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Well, do you want to go fishing for 20 Rupees? [...] I'm so generous, I'll let you fish for as long as you want.|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref> The Fishing Hole Man considers himself an expert at catching [[Fish]], referring to himself as an old pro.<ref>{{Cite|What? You want to know if I'm a good fisherman? Of course I am! I'm an old pro!|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref> However, he refuses to reveal any [[Fishing]] secrets to Link, since he considers them to be company secrets. He has a tendency to continuously end his sentences with "I'm serious!",<ref>{{Cite|I'll go out of business if you go somewhere else! I'm serious!|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What? You want to know why I'm scratching all the time? What are you talking about? Actually, I have no idea. I'm serious!|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can hook a fish out of the blue, when you least expect it. Now that's exciting! I'm serious. Really, I am!|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref> and has a neverending itch in his armpit.

When Link goes back to the Fishing Pond as an adult, the Fishing Hole Man will now be wearing a hat to try and cover his bald spot.<ref>{{Cite|On top of that, my hair... Uh, I mean, never mind...|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref> Moreover, he mentions that he is almost out of business since Link, his only customer, was gone for seven years.<ref>{{Cite|How many years has it been? Seven years?! What have you been doing all this time?! You're my only customer. I'm almost out of business! Seriously!|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref> If Link breaks the rules and casts the [[Fishing Rod]] at the Fishing Hole Man, he can grab his hat to confirm the fact that he has started to go bald.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, what are you doing? I told you not to do that! You're terrible!|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref> Link can then choose to give him back his hat or throw it into the pond. If Link chooses the latter, the hat is lost forever, so he must pay a 50 Rupee fine.<ref>{{Cite|WHAAAAAT?!? Why are you doing this to me? What are you thinking? I'm not going to let you get off that easy! You have to pay me a 50-Rupee fine! Seriously!|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM3D|-}}===
In {{MM3D}}, the Fishing Hole Man runs the [[Ocean Fishing Hole]] in the [[Zora Cape]]. He will share secrets about Fishing with Link if talked to.<ref>{{Cite|If ya make the ground shake, them fishies will swim away. They're nervous fellas, all of 'em. Go on. Give it a try.|Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If ya flick the water's surface all gentle-like, them fishies will get all curious and swim closer. Go on. Give it a try.|Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref> He will also mention that certain Fish can be caught on that day.<ref>{{Cite|Right on. Ya know, on a day like this, ya might even land yourself a nice [fish name].|Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref> The Fishing Hole Man will sometimes mention his older brother, the [[Swamp Fishing Hole Man]], and vise versa.<ref name= "Bro"/><ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh. Yeah. My li'l bro mans the Ocean Fishing Hole. Haven't seen 'im in years, though. Hey. If ya see 'im, tell 'im I said...hey.|Swamp Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref>

The Fishing Hole Man bears resemblance to the [[Man from Trading Post]] and the [[Man from Curiosity Shop]]. When Link mentions this, however, he will deny it, saying that he has no idea who Link is talking about.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Ya say ya saw someone with my face in town? ...Nonsense. It's nonsense! I have no idea who yer talking about!! Nonsense, it is. We're not talkin' about this anymore. Got it? Just drop it.|Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref>

Just as in {{OoT|-}}, the Fishing Hole Man's hat can be hooked with the Fishing Rod.<ref>{{Cite|What in the blazes?! What do you think yer doin'! Stop messin' around, punk! The nerve... Just don't do it again, OK? OK?! Just STOP it!|Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref>
