{{Term/Store|Talo||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/> He is one of the children of [[Ordon Village]] and the son of [[Jaggle]] and [[Pergie]]. He is also [[Malo]]'s older brother.<ref>{{Cite|Sheesh, you ARE my brother, Talo...but that's pretty low...|Malo|TP}}</ref> He looks up to [[Link]] and admires his prowess with weapons.<ref>{{Cite|Link has a slingshot! WHOA!|Talo|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Gee, you really are amazing, Link!|Talo|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, wait! You've gotta show us the jump attack before you go! You know, the one where you go "HYAAH!" and leap through the air!|Talo|TP}}</ref> Talo is quite immature and is often mean to [[Colin]].<ref>{{Cite|Those guys are always teasing me...|Colin|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you wanna get past here, Colin, you'd better start talking, fast.|Talo|TP}}</ref> He is always looking for trouble and is eager to wield weapons.<ref>{{Cite|They're selling a slingshot at the store right now! A SLINGSHOT!|Talo|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, yeah, that reminds me, you've gotta teach us how to use a sword, too! Seriously!|Talo|TP}}</ref>

==History==
Early on, Talo is captured while chasing a [[monkey]] and taken to the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Talo and Malo went chasing like crazy people after that monkey. I can't keep up with those two...|Beth|TP}}</ref> where they are rescued by Link.<ref>{{Cite|If you hadn't come, Link... Gee, me and that monkey would've gotten eaten, probably! She's actually a pretty nice gal, that monkey... She tried to protect me, so we got captured together.|Talo|TP}}</ref> Later, he, Colin, {{Term|TPHD|Beth|link}}, and Malo are taken by [[Bulblin]]s and are left to die. However, [[Renado]] finds them and brings them to [[Kakariko Village]].<ref>{{Cite|The beasts took us and left us to die...but Mr. Renado found us.|Colin|TP}}</ref> Talo believes Link will save them and is pleased to see him when he finally arrives.<ref>{{Cite|Ha ha ha! See, Beth?! I TOLD you Link would save us!|Talo|TP}}</ref> Eventually, he starts respecting Colin for saving Beth,<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe Colin... I mean, that was a really brave thing for him to do, huh?|Talo|TP}}</ref> and takes the responsibility of keeping watch over Kakariko Village.<ref>{{Cite|Even Talo, who was so saddened by the incident with Colin, has at last recovered his good spirits. That young boy wanted dearly to help, so he keeps watch from the observation platform right now...|Renado|TP}}</ref> If Link comes into town in [[Wolf Link|wolf form]], he will scream for everyone to head for safety.<ref>{{Cite|WHOA! It... It's a MONSTER! EVERYBODY RUN!!!|Talo|TP}}</ref> While in Kakariko Village, Talo challenges Link to a [[Bow]] minigame. If he succeeds, Talo rewards him with a [[Piece of Heart]].

In the ending, Talo, Malo, Beth, [[Ilia]], Colin, and Link return to Ordon Village together.

==Nomenclature==
Talo's name may be based on [[Talon]]. Similarly, his brother, Malo, may have a name based on [[Malon]].
