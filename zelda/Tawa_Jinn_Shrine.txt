The {{Term/Store|Tawa Jinn Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Tawa Jinn Shrine}}<br/>Tawa Jinn's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Tawa Jinn's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn Shrine}} is located atop the eastern side of {{Term|BotW|Mount Taran|link}}, above {{Term|BotW|Taran Pass|link}}, in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. Initially, {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn Shrine}} is buried underground, though there are three {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|display=Tablet}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to retrieve the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} from the {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} on the mountain to reveal the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|ancient orbs}} guarded by the giants of Mount Taran lead to the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> This grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Three Giant Brothers|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

There are three {{Plural|BotW|Hinox}} in total. The first is a regular {{Term|BotW|Hinox}}, located in {{Term|BotW|Hanu Pond|link}}. The second {{Term|BotW|Hinox}} is a {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}}, who sleeps in {{Term|BotW|Uten Marsh|link}}. The final is a {{Term|BotW|Black Hinox|link}}, which can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Rabella Wetlands|link}}. The {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} are hung around their necks, and can be taken either through {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} or by defeating the {{Term|BotW|Hinox}}.

Once all three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} have been socketed, {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn Shrine}} will emerge from the ground, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Tawa Jinn. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Tawa Jinn|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Great Thunderblade|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for making it to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Tawa Jinn|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Tawa Jinn|BotW}}</ref>
