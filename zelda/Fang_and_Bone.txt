The {{Term/Store|Fang and Bone||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Sign">{{Cite|Fang and Bone<br/>(only open at night)|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}} is a shop run by {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} which specializes in monster-related wares. It can be found on the "left eye" of {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}} during night. After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} talks to {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} for the first time, his shop will appear near every major settlement in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, excluding {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, from 9:00 PM to 5:00 AM.

The shop will not restock when Link is within a close proximity of it (or rather, any location it will show up in). In order to restock the shop, Link must spend a full day away from those locations (sleeping at an inn or with a campfire), then return once the time has passed.

The exact locations of the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}} are as follows: underneath the bridge to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}, on the {{Term|BotW|High Ground|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}, on {{Term|BotW|Korne Beach|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda}}, among some ruins west of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, south of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Trailhead Lodge|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, southeast of the {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}, and above {{Term|BotW|Lantern Lake|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. These locations are marked by a {{Term|BotW|Sign|link}}.<ref name="Sign"/>

{{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}} uses {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} as currency, which can be obtained by exchanging monster parts with {{Term|BotW|Kilton}}. Each time {{Term|BotW|Link}} frees a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}, more wares show up in the shop.<ref>{{Cite|Da da...DAAAAAAAA! I'VE DONE IT!
