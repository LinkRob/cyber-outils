The {{Term/Store|Multifunction Settings||Series, HWDE}} menu is an interface in {{HW|DE}}.<ref>{{Cite|Multifunction Settings|Button Settings|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Multifunction Settings}} menu is a submenu of the {{Term|HWDE|Button Settings|link}} menu. The {{Term|HWDE|Multifunction Settings}} can be accessed by playing with one [[Joy-Con]]. The {{Term|HWDE|Multifunction Settings}} allows the player to see what button combinations can be pressed to do certain actions while playing with one Joy-Con.<ref name="Button Settings"/> Most actions that can be performed require the multifunction button, SR.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR}} Multifunction Button|Button Settings|HWDE}}</ref> 

While playing with a right Joy-Con, pressing SR and SL will use {{Term|HWDE|Focus Spirit|link}}.<ref name="Focus Spirit">{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + SL}} Focus Spirit|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref> Pressing SR and Plus will toggle the {{Term|HWDE|Map|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + Plus}} Toggle Map|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref> Pressing SR and A will cycle through all playable {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + A}} Switch Warriors|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref> Pressing SR and B or SR and X will cycle through the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}}'s {{Plural|HWDE|Item|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + B}} Cycle Items|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + X}} Cycle Items|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref> Pressing SR and Y will use the selected {{Term|HWDE|Item}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + Y}} Use Item|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref> Pressing SR and pressing the Right Stick down will use {{Term|HWDE|Fairy Magic|link}}.<ref name="Fairy Magic">{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + Right Stick Press}} Fairy Magic|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref>

While playing with a left Joy-Con, pressing SR and SL will use {{Term|HWDE|Focus Spirit}}.<ref name="Focus Spirit"/> Pressing SR and Minus will toggle the {{Term|HWDE|Map}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + Minus}} Toggle Map|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref> Pressing SR and pushing the Right Stick down will use {{Term|HWDE|Fairy Magic}}.<ref name="Fairy Magic"/> Pressing SR and up will use the selected {{Term|HWDE|Item}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + D-Pad Up}} Use Item|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref> Pressing SR and left or right will cycle through the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}}'s {{Term|HWDE|Item}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + D-Pad Left Right}} Cycle Items|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref> Lastly, pressing SR and down will switch {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE SR + D-Pad Down}}|Multifunction Settings|HWDE}}</ref>
