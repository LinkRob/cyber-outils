{{Term/Store|Composer Brothers|Composer Brothers|Series|plural}} is the name given collectively to [[Sharp]] and [[Flat]],<ref>{{Cite Book |quote=A cursed, spectral conductor and half of Hyrule's Composer Brothers. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=202 }}</ref> a recurring pair of [[Poe]] brothers in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT Flat Model.png|left|thumb|198x198px|Flat from {{OoT|-}}]]
In {{OoT}}, the Composer Brothers are deceased composers who, during their life, served the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|We brothers also served the Royal Family, and were assigned to study the hereditary mystic powers of the family.|Composer Brothers|OoT}}</ref> Sharp reveals that he and his brother were originally from [[Kakariko Village]], which was founded by the [[Sheikah]].<ref>{{Cite|R.I.P. Here lie the souls of those who swore fealty to the Royal Family of Hyrule. The Sheikah, guardians of the Royal Family and founders of Kakariko, watch over these spirits in their eternal slumber.|Tombstone|OoT}}</ref> This may imply that they were once Sheikah themselves in life.<ref>{{Cite|I am one of the ghostly composer brothers of Kakariko Village. All the people in this village are born to serve the Royal Family of Hyrule.|Sharp|OoT}}</ref> They were known to be great composers because of the many musical masterpieces they wrote. However, they were also on a mission to analyze the hereditary mysterious powers of the Royal Family.<ref>{{Cite|Back then, people called us great composers because of the many  musical masterpieces we wrote. But we brothers were not just composers. We had a mission to analyze the mysterious powers of Hyrule's Royal Family.|Composer Brothers|OoT}}</ref> Although they could never quite understand the true power behind the [[Triforce]], they completed a study about controlling the flow of time via the use of [[Ocarina]]s. [[Ganondorf]], the human form of [[Ganon]], then tried to steal the results of their research and threatened to kill them if they did not reveal them. The Composer Brothers, unwilling to let the King of the [[Gerudo]]s reap the fruits of their research, gave up their lives in order to protect the secret they had spent years researching.<ref>{{Cite|Though we never could figure out the power of the Triforce, we had almost completed our study of controlling time with the tones of ocarinas. Uh, I mean... Actually, we did complete that study! We would have been famous, if that hateful Ganondorf had not tried to steal our results. We could never let him reap the fruits of our research! That's why we gave our lives to protect the secret.|Composer Brothers|OoT}}</ref>

After their death, they kept serving the Royal Family as [[Poe]]s by guarding [[Kakariko Village Graveyard]]. Their tombstones are located right next to the huge headstone that guards the entrance to the [[Royal Family's Tomb]]. [[Link]] the [[Hero of Time]] can speak to the deceased brothers by checking their gravestones, which results in them attacking the hero. After being defeated and realizing that he is not one of Ganondorf's men,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, what? You're not one of Ganondorf's  men, are you?|Composer Brothers|OoT}}</ref> they will reveal that they were each studying a song, one to summon the sun and the other to summon the moon.<ref>{{Cite|To tell the truth, each of us was studying a different song, one to summon the sun and another to summon the moon. If you were really sent by a  member of the Royal Family, I will tell you about the results of our study. We inscribed it on the royal tombstone.|Composer Brothers|OoT}}</ref> Their combined studies resulted in the creation of the "[[Sun's Song]]," which is the secret song they guarded and that eventually led to their deaths. Once Link enters the tomb, he can find the "Sun's Song" inscribed at the end, along with a poem written by the Composer Brothers to accompany the melody.<ref>{{Cite|The rising sun will eventually set, / A newborn's life will fade. From sun to moon, moon to sun... / Give peaceful rest to the living dead.|N/A|OoT}}</ref>

Sharp is located in the [[Spring Water Cave]] at the top of [[Ikana Canyon]], where he plays an antagonistic role. When the land of Ikana Canyon was taken over, Sharp was tricked into selling his soul to the devil according to his brother Flat.<ref name=Soul>{{Cite|Oh...Sharp, my dear brother. He sold his soul to the devil and was the one who locked me in here...|Flat|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We dead should not be lingering here in this land. It was all a trick of the masked one who had upset things.|Sharp|MM}}</ref> He then locked up Flat himself beneath the [[Ikana Graveyard]], and had him guarded by an [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Iron Knuckle]].<ref name=Soul/> Due to Sharp's curse, the [[Ikana River]] has gone dry, causing the [[Music Box House]] to stop working and allowing [[Gibdo]]s to roam the canyon freely.

Sharp composed a dark song which has the ability to drain the life from all who listen to it, including Link. When Link himself confronts the Poe, Sharp indeed plays the song, stating that Link will "join the ranks of the dead".<ref>{{Cite|If that is the case, then sleep gently to the melody of darkness that the great composer, Sharp, shall play...and join the ranks of the dead.|Sharp|MM}}</ref> Expectedly, the dangerous music damages the young hero as long as he remains in the Spring Water Cave. However, when Link plays the "[[Song of Storms]]," the song Flat composed to get rid of his brother's curse, Sharp's soul is healed.<ref>{{Cite|With my brother's song, you have broken the curse that was placed upon me. It is all thanks to you.|Sharp|MM}}</ref> Sharp then tells Link of the curse on Ikana, and how to gain entrance to the [[Ancient Castle of Ikana]].<ref>{{Cite|If you truly do not fear the dead... I wish for you to go to the temple in this land and sever the root of the evil curse that torments us. To do that... You must seek out the king, the only one who knows the way into the temple. The king is in the ruins of Ikana Castle, awaiting the coming of the one who will break the curse.|Sharp|MM}}</ref>

If Link plays the "[[Song of Healing]]", which cures every other spirit in the game, Sharp will react and say how soothing the song is, but quickly regain his demeanor and say it is too late for him to be healed, and then resumes his cursed song.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? Somehow, my heart is eased. This melody has a serenity to it... And yet... To one of the dead and darkness, like myself, a song like that no longer holds meaning for me. You shall end your futile resistance and quickly join the ranks of the dead.|Sharp|MM}}</ref>

====Flat====
Flat is locked up beneath the Ikana Graveyard by his own brother, Sharp, and is guarded by an Iron Knuckle, which can only be encountered in the [[First Day]].<ref name=Soul/> After defeating the Iron Knuckle, Flat's soul is freed, and in return he teaches Link the "Song of Storms," a song he had composed to cure Sharp of his curse.<ref>{{Cite|Here is written the song that shall cleanse his cursed, black soul.|Flat|MM}}</ref> He also reveals that he and his brother were once servants and composers of the Ikana Royal Family.<ref>{{Cite|Are you the one who freed my soul? I served the Ikana Royal family. I am the composer called Flat. The songs connected to the Royal Family that remain here were all composed by my brother and I.|Flat|MM}}</ref> Flat requests that Link helps cleanse his brother's black soul, allowing the two of them to finally have their eternal rest.<ref>{{Cite|You who do not fear the dead, learn well the song that is inscribed behind me... And if you ever meet my brother, I'd like you to inform him... The thousand years of raindrops summoned by my song are my tears. The thunder that strikes the earth is my anger! ...I have made my request.|Flat|MM}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* In {{MM}}, [[Flat]]'s moon symbol was changed to a green version of [[Sharp]]'s sun symbol. The [[Triforce]]-shaped brooches seen in {{OoT}} were also changed into [[Poe]]-like ones.
* In {{OoT|-}}, the [[Poe Soul]] of the Composer Brothers, which are green with a happy face, cannot be captured in a [[Empty Bottle|Bottle]] by [[Link]], unlike regular and [[Big Poe|Big]] Poes.
*Sharp and Flat, like [[Talon]] and [[Ingo]], appear to be based on [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] from the ''[[mariowiki:Mario_(franchise)|Super Mario]]'' franchise. Sharp, the older brother, is a bit smaller and chubbier than his younger brother. Also, Sharp has a red sun symbol on his cap, while Flat has a green moon one.
*Although the brothers are actually holding a conductor's baton, due their sharp-looking appearance, some fans have easily mistaken them as swords.
*There is debate among fans that the "devil" claimed by Flat about his brother Sharp selling his soul are either [[Skull Kid]] ([[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]]) or [[wikipedia:Satan|Satan]]. However, it is likely he is referring to the former.

==Nomenclature==
Sharp and Flat's names come from musical symbols used to modulate the pitch of a note. Their German names refer to the composers {{wp|Johann Sebastian Bach}} and {{wp|Ludwig van Beethoven}}.
