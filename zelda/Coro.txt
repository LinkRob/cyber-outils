{{Term/Store|Coro||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
Coro is a man with a large afro, which birds seem to have used as a nest. He is a horrible cook, and lives near the [[Faron Spring]] in the southern portion of [[Faron Woods]], right outside of the [[Ordona Province]]. From there he sells [[Lantern Oil]] refills, either for empty [[Bottle]]s or the [[Lantern]] itself. Early in the game, he kindly gives [[Link]] a free Lantern as part of a business tactic he is working on.<ref>{{Cite|I'm trying to drum up sales by giving away free lanterns! It's a business tactic, guy!|Coro|TP}}</ref> He has an older sister named [[Iza]] and a younger sister called [[Hena]], who also run their own businesses. 

Coro sits in front of a cauldron filled with a [[Nasty Soup]] that cannot be scooped until heated with the Lantern. Link's reaction to the soup varies and it can sometimes even take [[Heart]]s rather than replenish them.<ref>{{Cite|You put nasty soup in the bottle! This is Coro's "special" recipe. [...] The only way to find out what it tastes like is to drink it.|N/A|TP}}</ref> The young businessman also has a pet [[Bird]] named [[Trill]] who watches over [[Trill's Shop|another shop]] in the northern part of Faron Woods. Trill comments on how his master never shows him any appreciation for watching the shop every day, and presumes that Coro probably did not get much appreciation either growing up.<ref name="appreciation"/> If Link steals from Trill's shop, Coro gets mad at Trill for letting that happen.<ref>{{Cite|The other day my master got mad at me for letting a thief steal stuff...|Trill|TP}}</ref>

According to Hena, Coro is the only one in the family who is lousy at [[fishing]], having to resort to using a [[Sinking Lure]] in order to catch a [[fish]].<ref>{{Cite|He's the only one in our family who's lousy at fishing. He only managed to catch that fish by using a sinking lure. Cheater...|Hena|TP}}</ref> Moreover, his sister considers Coro to be lazy and worthless since she sometimes asks him to help out at the [[Fishing Hole]], but he never drops by.<ref>{{Cite|Another thing about my lazy brother... I ask him to drop by and help out from time to time, but he never does. He can't fish a lick, so he spends his time in the woods, talking to the animals, I guess. Yeah, he's kinda worthless...|Hena|TP}}</ref>

Coro is one of the few characters who is not afraid of Link in his [[Wolf Link|wolf form]], even saying that his older sister is scarier than the wolf.<ref>{{Cite|But me, I'm not worried about you, little guy. My older sister is much scarier than you.|Coro|TP}}</ref>

During the end credits, Iza, Coro and Hena can be seen together relaxing at the Fishing Hole.
