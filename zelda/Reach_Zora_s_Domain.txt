"{{Term/Store|Reach Zora's Domain||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Reach Zora's Domain}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Inogo Bridge}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Inogo Bridge|link}}, he will hear a voice call out to him from above.<ref>{{Cite|Say, hey there! Young one! Up top! Above you!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}} jumps down from the top of one of the {{Term|BotW|Inogo Bridge|display=Bridge's}} spires. He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Pardon the entrance, but you're a Hylian, aren't you?|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> as he would like to talk for a moment.<ref>{{Cite|I was hoping perhaps you'd have a moment to talk...|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

Though he is excited to have finally found a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}},<ref>{{Cite|Aha! A Hylian! Yes, I knew it!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} takes a second to introduce himself.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, pardon me... {{Big|I am Sidon, the Zora prince!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s name,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|And what is your name? Go on, please tell me!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> which, while familiar, he believes is fantastic.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Link? Your name is Link?! What a fantastic name!}} Hmm, though I cannot shake the feeling that I have heard it somewhere before... Well, in any case, it is a strong name!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} admits to watching {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, I've been watching you.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> He is impressed by {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s fighting skill and assumes he is a strong {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} warrior.<ref>{{Cite|I've seen the way you work. I can tell by how you carry yourself that you are no ordinary person. Link! You must be a strong warrior among the Hylians, correct?!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} claims that his eye for talent comes from his title as prince of the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Aha! Just as I suspected!}} I am a Zora prince, after all. I have an eye for talen that is unparalleled! {{Big|Yes! Exquisite!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

For a long time, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} has been looking for someone as powerful as {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have been searching for someone like you for a long while. A man like you, Link, who carries himself with power!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} is in danger from {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link|display=Divine Beast Vah Ruta's}} rain,<ref>{{Cite|Right now, Zora's Domain is in grave danger because of the massive rainfall coming from {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} need the help of a warrior.<ref>{{Cite|Please, promise you will help us! We need your strength, warrior!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to accompany him to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|Won't you please come to {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} with me?|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}}, agrees, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} will be grateful and compliment his character,<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Link! You are indeed the man I thought you were!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> before encouraging him to head to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} as quickly as possible.<ref>{{Cite|Now {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} will be saved for certain! No time to waste! Hurry up and head over!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> Due to the rainfall, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will slip if he tries to climb the cliffs, so his only option is to follow the path.<ref>{{Cite|Because of the rain, {{Color|BotW Red|the cliffs are too wet to climb}}. To reach the domain, you will need to go straight along this path.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will face many electric monsters on his journey,<ref>{{Cite|As a Hylian, I know you are unable to swim up the river. As such, the path to the domain may be a bit treacherous. You likely have a tough fight in store—there are monsters up ahead that attack with electricity.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> though {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} believes in him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Don't give up! I believe in you!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

To assist {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} gives him a {{Term|BotW|Electro Elixir|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... Oh! That's right! I have something that I would like to give to you! This is just a small trinket to show that I have faith in you.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It is a drink that will increase your resistance to electricity!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> The effect does not work on {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}, which {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} thinks may be because it was made for {{Plural|BotW|Hylian}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am not sure why, but its effects do not seem to work for Zora. Perhaps because it was made specifically for Hylians. It should work wonders for you, though!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> He decides to swim ahead to secure {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s route ahead.<ref>{{Cite|I shall go on ahead and make sure there is not anything strange going on where you are headed. I'm counting on you!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> Once he jumps in the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} will begin.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} travels, he will encounter {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}},  {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Water Octorok|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. He must either defeat, sneak past, or outrun them on his way to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.

Along the shore north of the {{Term|BotW|Tabahl Woods|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will encounter {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} for a second time,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|HEY! Link!!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> where he will apologize for calling from the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|display=River}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry for calling out to you from the river!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> He was not sure that {{Term|BotW|Link}} would actually come after he pressured him, though he is glad to see he has been progressing well.<ref>{{Cite|Since I pressured you into coming, I was not sure you would really come through. I am pleased things are going well.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> As of late, there have been many monsters around {{Term|BotW|Zora River}}, so he warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be careful.<ref>{{Cite|Ever since this strange occurrence, there have been a lot of {{Color|BotW Blue|monsters}} around here. Be careful as you proceed!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> However, he also reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} to hurry, as the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} are waiting on him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|And hurry! All of my fellow Zora are anxiously awaiting your arrival!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Oren Bridge|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} will pop out of the water to inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} he is halfway to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey! Link!!}} You're almost halfway to Zora's Domain!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> He warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that there are many {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} on the road ahead, but asserts that he will be fine.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, by the way, there are a few spots up ahead with lots of enemies...but I'm sure you will be fine! {{Big|I believe in you, Link!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Link}} is traversing {{Term|BotW|Luto's Crossing|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} will call out to him from below.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Hey! Link!! Look below! Down here!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that he has been waiting for him, since he would sure {{Term|BotW|Link}} would pass by.<ref>{{Cite|You are still pretty far off, my friend! But I was sure you'd be passing over this bridge, so I have been waiting for you.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> At his current pace, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} asserts that {{Term|BotW|Link}} will make it to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} soon,<ref>{{Cite|You will be in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} before you know it!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> and he decides to meet him there.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, I'm going to head that way too! I shall meet you there!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> Spotting a {{Term|BotW|Moblin}} approaching from behind, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} shouts to alert {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Link!! Beware, warrior! A monster approaches from behind you!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} from the {{Term|BotW|Great Zora Bridge|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} will excitedly approach him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Whoa! I've been waiting for you, Link!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} from any other direction, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} will be scared by {{Term|BotW|Link}} appearing behind him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Whoa!}} I did not expect you to walk so closely behind me! Indeed, you gave me a fright. However...|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> His dialogue proceeds the same.}} He welcomes {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}} and says that he now must meet {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! Behold the pride of my people, {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}! Now I shall introduce you to the king. Hurry, this way!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} will be completed.{{Note|{{Term|BotW|Link}} can skip obtaining this {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} by avoiding {{Term|BotW|Inogo Bridge}} in his approach to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}. {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} will attempt to kick {{Term|BotW|Link}} out of the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}},<ref>{{Cite|And who might you be? The Zora king is not accepting visitors at this time. I shall escort you out.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> but he changes his mind when he realizes that he is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}}.<ref>{{Cite|Pardon my manners, but are you a Hylian?!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> He asks why he did not say so initially and invites him in.<ref>{{Cite|Why didn't you say so in the first place?! Please, please, come in!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s meeting with {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}}, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} will be automatically completed.}}
