The {{Term/Store|Spell||Series, TAoL}} magic is an ability in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 109}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Spell}} was referred to as '''Spell Spell''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Spell Spell}}<br/>During Link's quest to recover the six shards of the Magic Crystal, Link frequently used the power of magical Spells. Link used the Spell Spell to weaken his enemies and to make hidden buildings appear.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160311084700/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/s.jsp#SpellSpell|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Overview==
The Spell is one of the most enigmatic magic abilities.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Magic to cast eerie spells. Its effect is still not fully known.|game= TAoL|page= 44}}</ref> It is learned near the end of Link's quest in {{Term|TAoL|Kasuto|link}} from an {{Term|TAoL|Old Man|link}}, and is used to open up another building at the edge of the town. It can also turn several enemies into [[Bot]]s.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= It is also said that it'll make the enemy tremble in fear.|game= TAoL|page= 44}}</ref>
