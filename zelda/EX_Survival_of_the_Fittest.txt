{{Term/Store|EX Survival of the Fittest||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 86 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Survival of the Fittest|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Survival of the Fittest}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must stay alive for ten minutes against hordes of monsters. Strong {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} such as {{Plural|HWAoC|Thunder Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Blizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Meteo Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} spawn every 30 seconds, while an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Hinox|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Hinox|link}} spawn only once during the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends after ten minutes pass, and can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
