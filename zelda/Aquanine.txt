The {{Term/Store|Aquanine||Series, PH}} is an item in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 116}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Aquanine is found on the [[Isle of Ruins]] after clearing [[Mutoh's Temple]]. It is one of three [[Pure Metal]]s, and the last to be found, necessary to create the [[Phantom Sword]].
