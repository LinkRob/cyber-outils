The {{Term/Store|Door of Time|Doors of Time|Series, OoT, OoT3D, HW, HWL, HWDE}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 26 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D}}, 155 {{Exp Game|TP, TPHD}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|HW Green|Door of Time}} is open!|N/A|HW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:DoorOfTime2.jpg|thumb|left|The {{Term|OoT|Door of Time}} opening]]
The {{Term|OoT3D|Door of Time}} is located within the {{Term|OoT3D|Temple of Time|link}} in {{OoT}}. It was constructed to guard the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Sword|link}} and can only be opened with the three {{Plural|OoT3D|Spiritual Stone|link}} and the recital of the {{Term|OoT3D|Song of Time|link}} with the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's right... The Temple of Time is the entrance through which you can enter the Sacred Realm from our world. But the entrance is sealed with a stone wall called the {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Door of Time}}. And, in order to open the door, it is said that you need to collect three {{Color|OoT3D Red|Spiritual Stones}}. Another thing you need...is the treasure that the Royal Family keeps along with this legend...|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}<br/>{{Cite|The {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Ocarina of Time}}!|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|This song}} opens the Door of Time...|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref> Upon collecting the {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri's Emerald|link}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Goron's Ruby|link}}, and the {{Term|OoT3D|Zora's Sapphire|link}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} is guided by {{Term|OoT3D|Princess Zelda|link}} to open the {{Term|OoT3D|Door of Time|display=Door}} in order to obtain the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Sword}} to defeat {{Term|OoT3D|Ganondorf|link}}.

Though the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Sword}} is the final barrier to the {{Term|OoT3D|Sacred Realm|link}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Door of Time}} acts as additional protection. After realizing her mistake and helping to seal {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon|link}} within the {{Term|OoT3D|Sacred Realm}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Princess Zelda}} resolves to close the {{Term|OoT3D|Door of Time}} to protect the {{Term|OoT3D|Sacred Realm}} once more.<ref>{{Cite|All the tragedy that has befallen Hyrule was my doing... I was so young...I could not comprehend the consequences of trying to control the Sacred Realm. I dragged you into it too. Now it is time for me to make up for my mistakes... You must lay the Master Sword to rest and close the Door of Time...|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref>

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Stub|TPHD}}
The {{Term|TPHD|Door of Time}} is located within the ruins of the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time|link}} in the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove|link}} in {{TP}}. It connects to the distant past to a time when the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time}} still stood. In this time, ancient technology such as the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod|link}} retain their power. However, if the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod}} is carried through the {{Term|TPHD|Door of Time}}, it will lose its power and rapidly age.
