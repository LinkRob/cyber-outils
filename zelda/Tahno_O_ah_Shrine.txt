The {{Term/Store|Tahno O'ah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Tahno O'ah Shrine}}<br/>Tahno O'ah's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Tahno O'ah's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Tahno O'ah Shrine is located at the base of {{Term|BotW|Madorna Mountain|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}. It is the goal of the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|Secret of the Cedars|link}}", but it can be accessed without activating the Quest.

To receive the Quest, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must speak to {{Term|BotW|Clavia|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. She points out the three trees on the Mountain's summits,<ref>{{Cite|You see those three trees up on top of that snowy mountain there?|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> claiming to have found an old letter in her house that instructs the reader to line the trees up and turn towards the sea to find a trial.<ref>{{Cite|I found a small letter stuck between the pages of this old book at my house... It said, "When the three summit trees become one, turn your back to them and head toward the sea. A trial encased in stone awaits you there." I don't know what that means!|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> Clavia believes the note is referring {{Term|BotW|Afromsia Coast|link}} and wonders whether there will be a treasure waiting.<ref>{{Cite|But maybe it means we have to head to {{Color|BotW Blue|Afromsia Coast}}, which is just east of those three trees on the mountain! For the "trial {{Color|BotW Red|encased in stone}}..." bit, I wonder if you get some kind of treasure for doing that...|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> However, since she has a child, Clavia can't leave Hateno Village, so she asks Link to investigate for her.<ref>{{Cite|But I have a child! I can't just go off on a journey across the mountains... But you can do that, right? You'd be great at that! Go on! Head out there and bring us back that treasure!|Clavia|BotW}}</ref>

Following Clavia's instructions, Link will find a cave that is obstructed by a rock wall due east of the three trees atop Madorna Mountain's peaks. By destroying the rocks with a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}, he will be able to access Tahno O'ah Shrine and complete the Quest. As Link enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tahno O'ah|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the Shrine is proof of Link's worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Tahno O'ah. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Tahno O'ah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Tahno O'ah Shrine features no puzzles, simply a direct path to Tahno O'ah. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Term|BotW|Climbing Boots|link}}. As Link approaches Tahno O'ah, he will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Tahno O'ah|BotW}}</ref> and will wish him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Tahno O'ah|BotW}}</ref>
