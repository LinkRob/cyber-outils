==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>This is {{Color|SS Blue|Karane}}, a strong, young student of the Knight Academy. She is your classmate, Master.</p><p>A senior at the academy, she is learning to be a knight like you, Master. I have observed that she is quite popular with the male students.</p>}}

Karane is a senior student at the [[Knight Academy]] in [[Skyloft]]. She is a red-headed youth who sports a light green [[tunic]] and round, flat hat. She spends most of her time near the classroom in the day, and can be found in her room on the upper floor at night. She is somewhat conservative,<ref>{{Cite|What do you think you're doing sneaking into a girl's room at this time of the night?!|Karane|SS}}</ref> but is very popular among her male peers.<ref name="Bio"/>

Karane has a keen interest in [[Pipit]] and sometimes wonders how she can capture his attention.<ref>{{Cite|Sigh... Oh, {{Color|SS Blue|Pipit}}... How can I get you to notice me?|Karane|SS}}</ref> She will often ask [[Link]] for his opinion on Pipit.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Link, what do you think about {{Color|SS Blue|Pipit}}?|Karane|SS}}</ref> If Link tells her that he is nice, she will begin listing his good points,<ref>{{Cite|Yes! Yes, he is! He's kind and caring. And handsome and wonderful! Oh dear... I don't know what's come over me all of a sudden... Sorry for bothering you with such an awkward question...|Karane|SS}}</ref> but if she is questioned, she will become flustered.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh... Well, you know... I just... Oh, forget about it. I don't even know what I was thinking asking you a strange question like that. Silly me...|Karane|SS}}</ref> When Link accomplishes something, such as obtain his tunic or learning the [[Spiral Charge]], she compliments him, but then goes on to say that Pipit could do better.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa, you can already Spiral Charge?! Wow...that's so cool, Link! I mean...whatever. I'm sure Pipit can do it, too!|Karane|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That outfit looks great on you! Not as good as it looks on Pipit,though!|Karane|SS}}</ref>

Later, Link is asked by [[Cawlin]] to deliver a [[Cawlin's Letter|love letter]] he wrote to Karane. If Link delivers the letter to Karane, she will initially think it is from Pipit.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? A letter? For me...? Could it be from {{Color|SS Blue|Pipit}}?!|Karane|SS}}</ref> She comments on the bad handwriting and discovers that Cawlin sent it.<ref>{{Cite|Wow... This {{Color|SS Blue|Cawlin}} guy... Isn't he one of {{Color|SS Blue|Groose}}'s flunkies? And...this... This is a love letter from that {{Color|SS Blue|Cawlin}} kid...right?|Karane|SS}}</ref> Although she appreciates the effort that went into the letter, she laughs at the thought of entering a relationship with the boy and wonders what Pipit would think of the situation.<ref>{{Cite|So, yeah... I have to say, he really put his heart into it and everything... I don't know... Maybe I should consider going out with {{Color|SS Blue|Cawlin}}. ...Hah! As if! Sigh... I wonder what {{Color|SS Blue|Pipit}} would think if he knew about this...|Karane|SS}}</ref> If Link talks to Pipit about this, he also becomes flustered and concerned for her.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh! I mean, uhh... It's not like there's anything going on between me and {{Color|SS Blue|Karane}}, you know? We're just friends... So it's not my business who she goes out with. I don't have much to say about it.|Pipit|SS}}</ref>

When Link returns to Karane, she will be confronted by Cawlin. She politely compliments him on his letter and thanks him.<ref>{{Cite|Tee hee. It was a wonderful love letter. Thank you!|Karane|SS}}</ref> He asks her if she would go out with him, and just as she is about to reply, Pipit runs in, shouting that she should go out with him instead.<ref>{{Cite|Wait! I think you should go out with me instead! I mean...er...I want to ask you out!|Pipit|SS}}</ref> She hesitantly accepts his offer in an effort to prevent breaking Cawlin's heart.<ref>{{Cite|Errr... I want to go out with... Pipit...|Karane|SS}}</ref> Despite this, Cawlin still runs away crying.

After that, Karane and Pipit will begin dating. Pipit thanks Link for helping him find true love and gives him five [[Gratitude Crystal]]s. Karane will admire Pipit even more,<ref>{{Cite|My guy is going out on patrol again tonight. It's not even required of him--it's just something he feels he has to do... Just thinking about him makes my heart start pounding like I just ran up a flight of stairs!|Karane|SS}}</ref> and thanks Link for his role, but she still feels sorry for how things turned out for Cawlin.<ref>{{Cite|Hee hee! Thank you, Link. Thanks to you, I have an amazing new boyfriend! But I feel kind of bad about {{Color|SS Blue|Cawlin}}, you know?|Karane|SS}}</ref> By sitting a bench seat in the front row of the classroom, Link can listen to Karane and Pipit discussing their relationship as well as how Karane wants to visit Pipit's mother,<ref>{{Cite|I guess I should hustle over and say hello to your mother too. I'm sure she's just like you, Pipit--trustworthy and dependable.|Karane|SS}}</ref> much to Pipit's dismay.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Uhhhh, that's moving a bit too fast, don't you think?|Pipit|SS}}</ref>

==Trivia==
{{Image|image=SS Karane + Caw Inscription.jpg|type=thumb|size=100px|align=right|caption="Karane+Caw..."}}
*If Link speaks to Karane after seeing [[Fi]] for the first time, she will react as if Link claims to have seen a ghost, much to Karane's disbelief.<ref>{{Cite|You saw a ghost?! Are you sure you weren't just in shock and maybe imagined it?|Karane|SS}}</ref>
*During the evenings, Karane will complain that she wants to take a bath, but [[Gaepora|someone]] is always in there.<ref>{{Cite|I need to take a bath, so get out of here, Link! Well...I mean I WOULD take a bath if whoever is in there now would hurry up and get out. They've been in there a loooong time...|Karane|SS}}</ref>
*There is an inscription in the lower right hand corner of the chalkboard in the Knight Academy classroom that reads "Karane + Caw...". Although the source of the inscription is unknown, it was likely to have been written by Cawlin.
*In Karane's bedroom on her desk is a drawing of Pipit's face in one of her books next to a heart symbol on the same page. Nearby, there is also an envelope with a heart symbol on it, possibly an unsent love letter to Pipit she wrote.

==Nomenclature==
Karane's name is derived from the {{Wp|Crane (bird)|crane}} bird.
