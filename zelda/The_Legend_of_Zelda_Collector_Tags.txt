{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda'' Collector Tags||Series}} are a set of dogtags, stickers, and temporary tattoos based on different games in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They are manufactured by [[Enterplay]] and were released in 2017.{{Fact}}

==Overview==
{{Lacking Sources}}
The set consists of 29 dogtags featuring artwork and sprites from {{TLoZ}}, {{OoT}}, {{MM}}, {{TWW}}, {{TP}}, {{SS}}, and {{BotW}}. Each one comes packaged with a themed sticker or tattoo sheet.
