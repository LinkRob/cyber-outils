The {{Term/Store|Rumor Guy||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
The Rumor Guy lives in the northeastern part of the [[Lost Woods]]. To reach his home, [[Link]] must use the [[Hookshot]] to cross a gap. True to his name, the Rumor Guy is savvy on gossip and consequently knows many secrets about the residents of Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Heh. Looks like you've gotten uses to swinging a sword around, Link.|Rumor Guy|ALBW}}</ref> When Link enters the Rumor Guy's home, he will remark that he saw the young hero using a "shooty chain thing," and that it looked fun.<ref>{{Cite|Heh, I know you. You're that little Link, right? I saw that shooty chain thing you used to get in here. Looks fun.|Rumor Guy|ALBW}}</ref> If Link attempts to leave the Rumor Guy's home, he will stop the young hero to tell him that he can provide interesting information.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Going home? That's a shame. Lots of stuff I could tell you. Interesting stuff. Heh.|Rumor Guy|ALBW}}</ref> If Link obliges, the Rumor Guy offers to tell him secrets about the [[Witch]], [[Princess Zelda]] and [[Gramps]] from [[Kakariko Village]].<ref>{{Cite|Heh, heh! Want some gossip? So you like to know other people's secrets! Whom do you want to know about? Ask away.|Rumor Guy|ALBW}}</ref> However, he becomes offended if Link denies him.<ref>{{Cite|Well, pardon me for existing. Am I in your way here? Do I need to move? Is there anything I can do for you?|Rumor Guy|ALBW}}</ref>

"He does not return your affections! There is no future for the two of you." You believe that? What a fool! He couldn't even tell that he was looking at himself! That poor woman...
