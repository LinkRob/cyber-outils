{{Term/Store|Jolene's Letter||Series, PH}} is an item in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Jolene's Letter|N/A|PH}}</ref> It was written by [[Jolene]] to her sister [[Joanne]].

==Location and Uses==
The [[Postman#Phantom Hourglass|Postman]] accidentally reads Jolene's Letter to [[Link]] after mistakenly assuming that the letter was addressed to him. After finding this out, he asks Link for assistance in delivering the letter to Joanne. After giving the letter to Joanne, she will reward [[Link]] with a [[Wisdom Gem]]. She will also letter a mail back to Link to thank him and give him a random [[Ship Parts|Ship Part]].
