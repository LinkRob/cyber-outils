'''{{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}} Issue #2''' was originally released in March 1990.

==Parts==
===To the First Power===
The story begins with Link and Zelda near an [[Underworld]] entrance. As Zelda is getting ready to go inside {{Term|TLoZ|Level 1|link}} to practice fighting in Ganon's Underworld,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Stop worrying, Link. If I'm going to learn to fight in Ganon's Underworld, I've got to practice on my own.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page=1}}</ref> Link reminds her that if she needs help at one point, she can just blow the [[Magic Whistle]] and he will be there to assist her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Okay, Zelda! If you get into trouble, blow the Magic Whistle and I'll be there!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page=1}}</ref> The princess heads in, confident in that nothing could go wrong with all of the lessons Link has taught her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=This is the least troublesome underworld maze and you've taught me everything I need to know... What could possibly go wrong?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page=1}}</ref> After Zelda vanishes into the Underworld, a [[Wizzrobe]] commands several of Ganon's minions to tie Link up so that he can teleport both of them into the final room of the labyrinth, revealing to Link that he filled the Eagle Labyrinth with the most dangerous of enemies so that Zelda may not stand a chance up against them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Grab him! Without his weapons, he's helpless! You thought Eagle Labyrinth was a safe place for Zelda to hone her battle skills! Fool! I filled it with the most lethal enemies in Ganon's army! She won't stand a chance! Let my teleportation spell carry us to the final room of the labyrinth!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page=2}}</ref> Although Link confronts the Wizzrobe and tells him that capturing Zelda will do no good since he is the one that Ganon wants, the Wizzrobe confesses that he is not interested in following Ganon's orders anymore.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Why go to all this trouble to get Zelda, Wizzrobe? I'm the one Ganon wants! / Ganon's desires don't interest me! I'm finished following that loser!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=2}}</ref> He elaborates that he has gathered many other minions who feel the same way, and as soon as he destroys Link and Zelda, all of Ganon's minions will join his renegade army, leaving Ganon to fail.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I've gathered many of Ganon's minions who feel the same! Ganon has never been able to stop you OR Zelda! But, if I can destroy you both, Ganon's minions will join my renegade army...and Ganon himself will fall!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page=2}}</ref>

[[File:To the First Power.png|left|thumb|250px|Zelda getting ambushed by Ganon's minions]]
Meanwhile, just as Zelda is telling herself that she will not call for Link's help at the first sign of danger, a [[Gohma]] appears behind her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=If Link thinks I'm going to give up and call him to rescue me at the first sign of danger, he's in for a surprise! [...] A Gohma!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page=3}}</ref> Surprised that a Gohma has appeared in such an early level,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=That's not supposed to happen in level one! I guess Link wanted to surprise me! Well, I'm not quitting!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page=3}}</ref> the princess tries to fire a [[Sword Beam]] at the foe's eye, but she is pushed back by the force of the beam, blaming Link for not having told her about the "kick."<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=One quick Sword Blast and you'll be seafood salad, one-eye! [...] Whoa!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=3}}</ref> She is successful in defeating a group of incoming [[Patra]]s, although she admits to herself that the current situation is too tough for her and decides to call Link by blowing the Magic Whistle.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=All right, I admit it! This IS too tough for me! I'm blowing the whistle, Link--come bail me out!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> When Link does not appear, Zelda realizes that something must have happened and tries to stay calm since she can still get out by remembering everything that the young hero taught her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link? Where ARE you? This isn't funny anymore! He's not coming. Something must be wrong. Okay, don't panic. I'm on my own. But if I remember everything Link taught me I can still get out of this.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> The princess then reaches for her magic pouch and gets out a [[bomb]], recalling Link's advice in that she can find an exit by blowing up one of room's walls.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zelda reaches in her Magic Pouch... Ah...here's what I need! 'If you can't find a door, clear your way with the bomb!' One door coming up!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> The opening reveals even more of Ganon's minions,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Oh, no! More of them!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> but still the princess manages to get into the next room since the foes are busy fighting amongst themselves.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Lucky these guys are so STUPID!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> Zelda then prepares another bomb since she hears someone approaching her,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Something's coming! One bomb left!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> which just turns out to be Miff.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Miff! My favorite fairy! Am I glad to see you!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page= }}</ref> Before Miff can say anything else, she warns Zelda about the Dodongo behind her, allowing Zelda to defeat it with her last bomb.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zelda! Behind you! [...] A dodongo!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=7}}</ref> Miff realizes that the princess is hurt,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Oh princess! You're hurt!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=7}}</ref> and as the fairy heals her wounds, the princess explains to her the recent strange events.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Later, after Miff's fairy magic has restored Zelda's health and after Zelda has explained the day's strange events...|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=7}}</ref> Astonished, Miff explains that Zelda cannot hope to fight through the Underworld's labyrinths using Link's way since she considers that the young hero only survives because of his "dumb luck and brute force."<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Are you out of your MIND!?! Link is an IDIOT! Link survives on dumb luck and brute force!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=7}}</ref> Zelda realizes this and wishes she had a [[Zelda's Bow|bow]] with her since she is unable to fire a sword beam correctly.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link is NOT an idiot! But...I'm not Link. [...] It would sure help if I had a bow! I can't fire a sword blast worth a darn!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=8}}</ref>

Soon after, Miff and Zelda stumble into the treasure of the Eagle Labyrinth, a [[Zelda's Bow|magic bow]],<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=(Further into the underworld...) Miff: Behold the treasure of the Eagle Labyrinth!<br/>Zelda: A magic bow!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=8}}</ref> which allows the princess to defeat the Gohma with a single shot and make her way into the final chamber of the maze.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Nice shot, Zelda! Now...that's the door to the final chamber!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page= 8}}</ref> Inside, the Wizzrobe is delighted to see Zelda since he will now be able to defeat her himself<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I don't know how you got past my renegade army, princess! But I'm delighted that I get to destroy you myself!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=9}}</ref>, but the princess wastes no time and throws the sword to the chains that are tying Link, freeing him. The Wizzrobe shoots a magic beam at her, which Zelda dodges and quickly fires an arrow from her bow towards the foe, making it fall into a fire pit that sends the Wizzrobe back to Ganon.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zelda: And you picked on the WRONG princess, Wizzrobe!<br/>Wizzrobe: Nooo! This will send be back to Ganon!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=10}}</ref> The story ends with the Wizzrobe kneeling in front of Ganon while the Prince of Darkness praises him since he could make use of a "deceitful and treacherous" man like him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=So...you plotted behind my back! You, Wizzrobe, are deceitful and treacherous! [...] Good! I can USE a man like you.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|issue= 2|publisher= |page=10}}</ref>

===Trust Me===
The story opens with Ganon visiting the small farming town of [[Water Town of Saria|Saria]], which is located in Western Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=One day, in the Town of Saria|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> When the villagers see Ganon, they are terrified, and begin to flee.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Prince of Darkness! Run for your lives!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> However, Ganon claims to have come in peace,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Wait! I come in peace!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> and picks up a small boy named [[Rus]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Our boy Rus, here|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> When Ganon asks the boy why he's afraid of him,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Why do you fear me, boy?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> the boy replies that Ganon is the evil one.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=You're the '''Evil''' one! My daddy said so!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Ganon responds that those are simply lies that were spread by Link, whom he refers to as a man-monster.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Lies, my dear innocent child! All lies!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I know why you fear me--! Because you believe the venomous '''lies''' spread by one who is truly evil! A man-monster called... '''Link!'''|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Ganon goes on to talk about how Link has attacked his servants and constantly tries to destroy him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He [Link] assaults my mountain home, attacks my servants, and tries to destroy me.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> He tells the villagers that although the Wicked Princess Zelda<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The wicked Princess Zelda|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> has done nothing to help their barren fields,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I see that your fields are barren. What have Link and his co-conspirator, the wicked Princess Zelda done for you?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> he will do so. He proceeds to magically plant a large fruit tree in the middle of a field, claiming that it is just a fraction of his power.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=This is but a fraction of my power!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> He tells the people that he would do more to help them, but because of Link's attacks on him, he must save up his magic.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=But, I must save all my energy to defend myself against Link's treachery!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref>

Link and Zelda are then seen using a map, attempting to find Ganon's location. Link wants to continue their pursuit of Ganon,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=All I care about is catching up with that pig, Ganon!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> but Zelda insists that she stop to visit Saria, as it is her duty.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I am the Princess of Hyrule! Royal etiquette '''demands''' that I greet my people properly!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> The two walk down to the bridge that leads into town. Zelda happily greets the River Man,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=All I care about is catching up with that pig, Ganon!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> but Link butts in about his search for Ganon.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Ahem! We're looking for the evil wizard Ganon who may have passed through here!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> The River Man says that he suspected as much,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I suspected as much!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> and that Link and Zelda are troublemakers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Troublemakers! We don't want your kind!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Link and Zelda explore the town some more, but Link is impatient about it.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=But they don't even like us! Hey! You're not listening!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Zelda offers to help the People of Saria fix their drought problem, and suggests building a dam.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link and I will help you [Saria Village] build a dam on the river that will divert water to irrigate your fields!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Unfortunately, after the dam is completed, the land is still infertile.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I told you it wouldn't work! You wasted our time with your scheme!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Zelda comes to the conclusion that the land must be cursed,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Unless... the fields were '''cursed!'''|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> and assumes that it's Ganon's doing.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Only one wizard has that power... '''Ganon!'''|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref>
[[File:Trust Me.png|thumb|right|230px|Zelda saves Rus from Ganon's spell]]
At that moment, Ganon shows up claiming that Link and Zelda are still spreading their lies.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Again, the evil Link and Zelda spread their slanderous lies!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Upon sight, Link immediately attacks Ganon, but his attack is blocked by Ganon's magic. Ganon claims that he cannot even visit his "friends" without being attacked,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Do you see, people of Saria? I am attacked without provocation by this assassin! I cannot even peacefully visit my friends!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> but says that he still came to help, even with the great risk to himself.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Despite the personal danger, I came here to give you '''this'''.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> He presents a bottle which contains the magical [[Water of Life]],<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The '''Magical Water of Life'''|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> and says that it will heal Saria's fields.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=[It] will restore your fields, giving you all the food you need!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> However, he refuses to give it over until Link is captured.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=...After you '''capture''' the evil Link!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Link starts to defend himself from the descending villagers, but Zelda stops him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=They're just innocent pawns. Please don't hurt them!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> As the villagers pin Link down, Ganon asks Rus to hand him Link's sword.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Ah! Link's Magic Sword! His greatest weapon! You, boy! Give it to me!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> When Rus inquires to what Ganon intends to do,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=W-what are you going to do to them?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Ganon demands that he turn over the sword.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Do not question me! Give me the sword! Now!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Rus then tries to escape, but Ganon says that the boy will be punished for his disobedience.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=There is only one punishment for disobedience|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Ganon then attempts to attack Rus using a magic spell, but Zelda dives in the way, and takes the blast instead.

When Link sees this happen, he is able to break away from the townspeople's grasp, and rushes forward to attack Ganon. He shoots a beam out of his sword towards Ganon, but misses. Ganon laughs at Link's attempt,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Ha! You [Link] missed!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Link but Link was aiming for the dam. The dam then bursts, and the rushing water sweeps Ganon and his tree away. Link then comments that Ganon doesn't look like a very strong swimmer.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Hmm... doesn't look like old '''pig face''' is a very strong swimmer.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref> Shortly after, the villagers begin to repair the dam, and they thank Link and Zelda for their aid.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=How can we thank you for all you've done for us?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref>

===Bonus 1===
The bonus in issue #2 consists of a map of the North Palace. There are descriptions of some of the buildings, some of them making jokes.

==Trivia==
The original 1990 version of this issue featured the stories He Also Serves and Thief in the Night. He Also Serves was swapped to [[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 1|Issue 1]]  for the first run of the comics, while Thief in the Night was moved to [[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 4|Issue 4]]. Despite this, the cover still features Link and Bagu.<ref>{{Cite Book|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |issue= 2|page= }}</ref>
