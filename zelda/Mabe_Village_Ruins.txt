The {{Term/Store|Mabe Village Ruins|Mabe Village Ruings|Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mabe Village Ruins|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Mabe Village Ruins are the remains of a village that existed prior to the second [[Great Calamity]] which can be found roughly in the center of [[Hyrule Field#Breath of the Wild|Hyrule Field]]. During the Great Calamity, the Mabe Village Ruins were destroyed and later remained under constant threat of a [[Guardian Stalker]], which prowls the Field west of the Ruins. The inactive remains of two [[Guardian (Breath of the Wild)|Guardian]]s can be found throughout the Village Ruins. These Guardians can be searched for [[Ancient Material]]s. Similarly, a broken wagon can be found at the southeastern edge of the Village Ruins, which can be searched for [[Material]]s.

The Mabe Village Ruins are where the thief, [[Misko]], hid an article of treasure after stealing it from [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]]. Link can learn the whereabouts of this treasure by reading [[Misko's EX Journal]] in the [[Outpost Ruins]]. According to the Journal, Misko hid the treasure in the Ruins near farmland where many people once gathered, referring to the [[Ranch Ruins]].<ref>{{Cite|The green garments of the man who wished to be a fairy are hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in the ruins of Hyrule Field}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where trade flourished}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where sinners were imprisoned}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the village next to farmland where many people once gathered}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> The metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing [[Tingle's Tights]] can be found embedded in the ground underneath the remains of a Guardian at the southern part of the Ruins. In order to retrieve the Chest, Link must use the [[Magnesis]] [[Rune]].

Littered throughout the Mabe Village Ruins, Link can find [[Rusty Halberd]]s, [[Rusty Shield]]s, [[Rusty Claymore]]s, a [[Rusty Broadsword]], and a [[Wooden Shield]].

A [[Korok]] can be found hiding underneath a [[Rock]] at the peak of a rock formation on the eastern side of the Village Ruins. He will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>

Link can find a stone Treasure Chest atop a keep on the western side of the Village Ruins, which contains a [[Silver Rupee]]. Another stone Treasure Chest containing a bundle of 10 [[Ice Arrow]]s can be found in the remains of a building at the center of the Ruins. A third stone Treasure Chest containing a bundle of 10 regular [[Arrow]]s sits atop some ruins beside the small pond at the southern portion of the Village Ruins. A dried well sits in the western portion of the Village Ruins, around which Link can find [[Barrel]]s and metal crates.
