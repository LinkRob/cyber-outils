{{Term/Store|Mellie||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mellie|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mellie}} is an elderly {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} woman who resides in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. During the day, she watches over her {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|link|display=Plum Garden}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm, I suppose I should go see how my sweet plum trees are doing...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> At 5:00 PM, she leaves the {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Garden}} to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} dinner in the {{Term|BotW|Common Cooking Area|link}}. At 8:00 PM, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} returns to her home next to {{Term|BotW|Olkin's Pumpkins|link}}, where she and her husband {{Term|BotW|Olkin|link}} reside.

It was {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}} on the day that {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} was born, and it was also the day that {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} planted the {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Plum Garden}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}.<ref name="Rain">{{Cite|I was told it rained the day I was born. Fitting...that it would be raining the day my soul died too...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Impa">{{Cite|These plums here were planted by Impa on the day I was born. They're special to me, so I keep a close watch on them. |Mellie|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Impa}} eventually gave the {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Garden}} to {{Term|BotW|Mellie}}.<ref name="Orchard">{{Cite|Impa GAVE me the plum-tree orchard! And yes,  the orchard may be an heirloom of this village, but it's technically mine!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Due to this, the plums are special to {{Term|BotW|Mellie}}, so she watches over them.<ref name="Impa"/> According to {{Term|BotW|Mellie}}, plums represent endurance and prosperity, and thus are the "silent guardians" of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Plums symbolize endurance and prosperity. The way they persevere through the harshness of winter and flower at the first sign of spring... We Sheikah strive to live in harmony with our environment and consider plum trees the silent guardians of our village.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Sign|link}} outside of the {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Garden}} warns against trespassers.<ref>{{Cite|Mellie's Plum Garden<br/>No Trespassing|Sign|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden}}, while she is watching, she yells at him for the intrusion.<ref name="Mellie">{{Cite|HEY! What are you doing?!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, she explains to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she has been caring for the plum {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} her whole life, and they have become a part of her.<ref>{{Cite|These plum trees... I've been raising them my whole life. They've grown as I've grown. They're...a part of me.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> She believes that she will die when the {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Garden}} does, so she has raised the {{Plural|BotW|Tree}} with as much love as she can.<ref>{{Cite|When these trees eventually rot and fall away...so will I. That's why I've painstakingly raised them with all the love I can muster.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} has vowed to take care of the {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Garden}} so long as she is alive, and she refuses to allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to mess with them.<ref name>{{Cite|So as long as I'm around, there's no way I'll let you mess with my plum garden!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} trespasses into {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden}} again, she will remind him that he is not allowed to enter.<ref>{{Cite|Like I said... {{Big|You are NOT allowed in here!}}|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> She is concerned that something would happen to her or her plum {{Plural|BotW|Tree}}, so she begs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to leave them alone for her sake.<ref>{{Cite|What if something happens to my plum trees? What if something happens to ME?! For goodness' sake... Just leave a poor old lady alone!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> In anger, she will call him a "rascal" and a "darn whippersnapper."<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|You rascal!}}|Mellie|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Darn whippersnapper!}}|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} destroys a plum {{Term|BotW|Tree}}, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} will again yell at him to stop.<ref name="Mellie"/> If he speaks to her when only a few have been destroyed, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} notices that there are fewer than there were before.<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps it's just my imagination, but... it seems like there are fewer of them now than there use to be...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> When all {{Term|BotW|Mellie}}'s plum {{Plural|BotW|Tree}}, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} once again lectures him about their importance, afterwards she realizes that it is not time for such things.<ref>{{Cite|... {{Big|WHAT AM I SAYING? THIS IS NO TIME FOR A HISTORY LESSON!}} M-my plum trees... My plum trees are all gone! |Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} trespass into the {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Garden}} after the {{Plural|BotW|Tree}} have been destroyed, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} will notice they are gone after she scolds {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... {{Big|MY PLUM TREES!!}} {{Small|There isn't a single one left...}}|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Following both of these scenarios, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} expresses her sadness, and she claims that when her plum {{Plural|BotW|Tree}} died, so did life's meaning. <ref>{{Cite|Those trees were my life... I suppose today's the day this old lady's life loses its meaning...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} destroys the {{Plural|BotW|Tree}} while it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|display=Raining}}, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} recounts that this was the {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} the day she was born, and it is therefore fitting for it to {{Term|BotW|Rain}} on the day her soul was to die.<ref name="Rain"/>

In the evening, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Common Cooking Area}}, where she {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cooks}} dinner for herself and {{Term|BotW|Olkin}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see here... I gotta sort the old man's supper... What should we have today?|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Olkin}} is a fan of her {{Term|BotW|Cooking}}, particularly the {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}} that she uses.<ref>{{Cite|My wife's {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkin}} dishes are the best. Way better than a barrel o' carrots, that's for sure!|Olkin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My wife's tough dishes are made from {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkins}}. They're out of this world. There are A LOT of {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkins}} in that recipe, I tell ya what.|Olkin|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} during this time, she explains that {{Term|BotW|Olkin}}'s rivalry with {{Term|BotW|Steen|link}} stems from his hatred of {{Plural|BotW|Carrot|link}}, although she does not understand his pettiness.<ref>{{Cite|Just between you and me, it's not that my old Okin really hates Steen...he just can't stand carrots! I don't get what it is about men. So petty...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>

As she walks back to her home, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} remarks that night came quickly, and she warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be careful on the roads in the dark.<ref>{{Cite|It got dark quick today, didn't it? Young one, you should know the roads are dangerous at night. Be careful.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> While she is asleep in her {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can wake {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} up, at which point she assures him that she is still alive.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, yeah. I'm still alive. It's fine—don't worry...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} apologizes when she realizes that {{Term|BotW|Olkin}} was not the one who woke her, explaining that he wakes her up at night due to her shallow breathing.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh! Sorry, I thought you were my old Olkin. My breath is so shallow when I sleep, sometimes my Olkin gets worried and wakes me up.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> She then remarks that men are constantly worrying.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know what it is about men. Constantly worrying...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to again, she complains that she is completely awake.<ref>{{Cite|Welp, now I'm completely awake...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>

During "{{Term|BotW|The Stolen Heirloom|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Cado|link}} both claim that {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} visited {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link}} the day before the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|link}} was stolen.<ref name="Dorian"/><ref>{{Cite|Yes, known villagers are free to come and go as they please. I think the only people that stopped by around the time the heirloom went missing were Lasli and Mellie.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} about what she does at night, she becomes concerned that he knows what she has been doing.<ref>{{Cite|[Nighttime activities?] At night? {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|So he knows... I didn't think anyone had seen me.}}}}|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> She then states that she spends her nights sleeping in her {{Term|BotW|Bed}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nighttime, huh? Well, I sleep at night. In my house. On a bed. That's about it.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>

At night, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can follow {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} after she leaves her {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Plum Garden}}. As she walks, she makes sure {{Term|BotW|Olkin}} is not around, and she reminds herself to act naturally.<ref>{{Cite|Is my old man gone?|Mellie|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Just act naturally...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I made it this far...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} catches {{Term|BotW|Link}} following her, she will call him a creepy delinquent for following around an old woman.<ref>{{Cite|Who's there?! Hmph... Following a weak, old woman around like some kind of creepy delinquent!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> She then accuses him of being attracted to older women.<ref>{{Cite|Are you into older women or something?|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> While she sees nothing wrong with this, she warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that her husband is a fighter.<ref>{{Cite|Well, there's nothing wrong with that...but I'm married to a fightin' man, I'll have you know!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to again, she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to give up pursuing her.<ref>{{Cite|I can tell you're into older women, but I'm married. Give it up.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} once she returns to her {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Plum Garden}}, she will accuse him of trying to rob her.<ref>{{Cite|So you're still here... There's only one reason to follow around an old woman in the wee hours of the night... {{Big|HELP! I'M BEING ROBBED!}}|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> However, she also admits that {{Term|BotW|Link}} might be bored, but she is still tired of seeing him.<ref>{{Cite|Or maybe you're just bored. Either way, I'm sick of looking at your mug.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> She then quietly mutters to herself that the sun will be up soon and she needs to hurry.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|The sun will be up before I know it. Must hurry...}}}}|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} reaches the hut southwest of her {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Garden}}, she will be relieved.<ref>{{Cite|Phew...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> She then pulls out a {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}} and begins to eat it. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confronts her, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} will be frightened.<ref>{{Cite|Grah!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Realizing she has been caught, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} admits that she is a fan of {{Plural|BotW|Carrot}}, despite {{Term|BotW|Olkin}}'s hatred of them.<ref>{{Cite|Heh... You caught me orange-handed. Yes, I'm a big fan of carrots. But my old man hates them.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, she occasionally sneaks out at night in order to eat them, but she begs {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to tell {{Term|BotW|Olkin}}.<ref>{{Cite|So sometimes I sneak out at night to get my fill. Please don't tell my husband!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks where the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}} is, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} inquires if he is referring to the "garish thing" in {{Term|BotW|Impa's House}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Where's the heirloom?] You mean that garish thing in Impa's house?|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} accuses her of stealing it, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} is so shocked that she almost chokes.<ref>{{Cite|[Did you steal it?] *cough cough* Is that any way to talk to an old woman? I almost choked on my carrot!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Now believing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is talking about the {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|display=Plum Garden}}, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} informs him that it was given to her by {{Term|BotW|Impa}}, and while it is an heirloom of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}, it is still hers.<ref name="Orchard"/> She then quietly mutters about young people.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|You young people...}}}}|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mellie}}'s again, she talks about how she is unable to resist the flavor of {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot}}, claiming there to be no better {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You caught me... I simply can't get enough of the bittersweet and nutty flavor that lies beneath its fiery exterior. There is no greater food.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> However, due to how {{Term|BotW|Olkin}} and {{Term|BotW|Steen}} treat each other, she feels as though she cannot admit her feelings.<ref>{{Cite|But seeing how those two are acting, I'm not about to admit to having a love affair with carrots.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends their conversation, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} states that she is counting on him not to tell {{Term|BotW|Olkin}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Right.] I'm counting on you not to tell my old man about this.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>
