{{Term/Store|Tottika||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tottika|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Stub|BotW}}
{{Term|BotW|Tottika}} is a soldier and guard for {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. When the domain was under threat of being flooded, [[Sidon]] dispatched multiple Zora to find help, Tottika being one of them. He meets Link on a bridhe by the base of the [[Zora River|Zora river]] and directs him to the nearby [[Inogo Bridge]], where Sidon waits for a Hylian to assist him in saving Zora's Domain. He wields a {{Term|BotW|Silverscale Spear|link}} and guards the right-side stairs to {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}'s throne. {{Term|BotW|Tottika}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} about the existence of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}, and then adds that he wishes to become a great warrior worthy of being chosen by a weapon.
