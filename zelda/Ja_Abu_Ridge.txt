{{Term/Store|Ja'Abu Ridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ja'Abu Ridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Ja'Abu Ridge is located in the [[Lanayru Great Spring]]. A [[Monument]] appears on the Ridge during the [[EX The Champions' Ballad]] [[Main Quest]] as part of the [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC Pack.

==Nomenclature==
Ja'Abu Ridge derives its name from [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]].
