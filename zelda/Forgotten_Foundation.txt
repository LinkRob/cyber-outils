'''Forgotten Foundation''' is a location in {{TotK}}. It is located in [[Hyrule Castle Chasm]] and leads to [[Gloom's Origin]] and [[Gloom's Lair]], where [[Ganondorf]] is fought. While going down, there are [[Black Lizalfos]] stationed which fire at [[Link]] with [[Bow]]s while he is going down the foundation. In order to get to this part of the area, you have to go through Gloom's Approach and avoid the [[Silver Moblin]] and [[Gloom Spawn]] blocking the way. In the Forgotten Foundation, you find the place where Link and [[Princess Zelda]] first started the journey.
