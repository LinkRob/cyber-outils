==Other Characters==
{{Incomplete|type=listing|reason=Remaining characters.}}
===Plain of Andor===
;{{Section|Beggar}}
The {{Name|Fan|Beggar}} is a woman found outside of the [[Mobilin's Head Inn]] in southwestern [[Tolemac]]. She was exiled from [[Great Wimbich]] and begs Zelda for a few [[Rupee]]s to feed her children and to keep them out of the cold for the night.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, fair princess! Pity a suffering creature exiled from Great Wimbich in the north! I ask only enough for a single meal for my children who hunger and are chilled by the wind.|Beggar|ZA}}</ref> Should Zelda give her 5 Rupees, she will give her the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Firestorm|Firestorm]] [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure|Spell]] in gratitude.

;{{Section|Glebb}}
'''Glebb'''<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|The trick is that you need the Vial of Wind before you can use the raft. To start with, you need an empty bottle. You get it from the thirsty woman Glebb. If you succeed in filling the empty bottle with water (on the riverside is one place where this can be done) and return it to Glebb, you will receive the Vial of Wind from her.|De truc is dat je Vial of Wind nodig hebt voordat je het vlot kunt gebruiken. Om te beginnen heb je een leeg flesje nodig. Dat krijg je van de dorstige vrouw Glebb. Als je erin slaagt om de lege fles met wat water te vullen (aan de rivier is één plek waar dat mogelijk is!) en deze retourneert aan Glebb, ontvang je van haar de Vial of Wind.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 4|issue= 4|date= April 1997|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=39}}</ref> is a woman found in southwestern Tolemac, south of the Mobilin's Head Inn. She is dying of thirst and asks Zelda to fill her [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Empty Bottle|Empty Bottle]] at the [[River Source Pond]]. Once Zelda gives it to her, she rewards her the [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Vial of Winds|Vial of Winds]] in gratitude.

;{{Section|Krebb}}
'''Krebb'''<ref name="Krebb and Lothar">{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|Inside are the innkeeper Lothar and three costumers. Here you can buy the Calm Spell for 100 rupees. Talk to Krebb to get the dagger.|Binnen zitten de herbergier Lothar en drie klanten. Hier kun je voor 100 rupees de Calm Spell kopen. Praat met Krebb om de dolk te krijgen.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 4|issue= 5|date= May 1997|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=45}}</ref> is a man found inside the Mobilin's Head Inn. He has become weary and advises Zelda to give up on her journey. He becomes impressed by Zelda's insistence after speaking to him again, and he gives her the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Dagger|Dagger]] Spell to make better use out of.

;{{Section|Lothar}}
'''Lothar'''<ref name="Krebb and Lothar"/> is the innkeeper of the Mobilin's Head Inn. He offers to sell Zelda the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Calm|Calm]] Spell for 100 Rupees.

;{{Section|Riddle Woman}}
The {{Name|Fan|Riddle Woman}} is found inside the Mobilin's Head Inn. She gives Zelda a riddle and a hint for exploring the [[Shrine of Earth]], by telling her that a [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Candle|Candle]] and a silver key will bring her bliss. Despite this, the shrine does not require any keys to navigate.

;{{Section|Tired Traveler}}
The {{Name|Fan|Tired Traveler}} is a man found inside the Mobilin's Head Inn. He is exhausted from having crossed the chasm in the Shrine of Earth. He laments that if he had the [[Ladder]], it would have saved him months, thus giving Zelda a hint on how to cross the chasm.

===Forest of Ogbam===
;{{Section|Forest Trader}}
The {{Name|Fan|Forest Trader}} is a merchant whose stall is set up in the [[Forest of Ogbam]]. He will sell Zelda a Candle for 100 Rupees and the [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Magic Shield|Magic Shield]] for 500 Rupees. He gives Zelda some "autumn herb" for free, however this is not an actual item.<ref>{{Cite|Take some autumn herb for free, then look around; there's plenty to buy.|Forest Trader|ZA}}</ref>

;{{Section|Hag}}
The {{Name|Fan|Hag}} is a woman in the Forest of Ogbam sitting next to a campfire. She beckons Zelda to come closer to hear a great secret. When Zelda approaches her, the Hag tells her not to trust "anyone with hair", including herself, and steals one of Zelda's [[Heart|Life Heart]]s.

;{{Section|Talking Chest}}
The {{Name|Fan|Talking Chest}} is a [[Treasure Chest]] with the peculiar ability to speak. It is found in the Shrine of Earth, south of the orange [[Sardak]]. It relinquishes the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Jade Ring|Jade Ring]] Spell when approached. It is notably the only treasure chest encountered throughout the entire game.

===Meadow of Skotness===
;{{Section|Farmer}}
The {{Name|Fan|Farmer}} is a man who owns a farm in the [[Meadow of Skotness]]. He tells Zelda to beware the [[Leever]]s and [[Tektite]]s that live in the meadows.

;{{Section|Ghost Farmers}}
The {{Name|Fan|Ghost Farmers}} are a pair of farmers who live in a ruined farm house on the Meadow of Skotness. They have worked on the land for three hundred years.<ref>{{Cite|Stay back! This splendid farm is all mine. Isn’t it wonderful? I have worked this land for three hundred years.|Ghost Farmer|ZA}}</ref> One ghost mentions that one of Ganon's followers lives in a [[Shrine of Water|world of water]]. He also tells Zelda that she can cross any fjord of the Skotness River by using the [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Red Boots|Red Boots]]. It is almost impossible to hear what they're say due to the awful sound mixing

;{{Section|Talking Mushroom}}
The {{Name|Fan|Talking Mushroom}} is an odd mushroom with a human-like face and the ability to speak. It lives in the [[Forest of Findo]], and calls [[Moblin]]s "slow and stupid".<ref>{{Cite|Those Mobilins out there are so slow and stupid, they're still trying to eat their soup with a fork!|Talking Mushroom|ZA}}</ref>

===Great Wimbich===
;{{Section|Blacksmith}}
The {{Name|Fan|Blacksmith}} is a man who runs the smithy in [[Great Wimbich]]. Although he is an honest and hardworking man, he complains that the townsfolk steal from him.<ref>{{Cite|Who goes there!? Oh, it's a young girl. Don't touch anything! People steal me blind; I know you all hate me! What did I ever do to any of you!? I give an honest bargain and I grind the coal into Rubies. I stay open late and work my fingers to the bone. Do any of you appreciate it? No!|Blacksmith|ZA}}</ref> Zelda can bring him the [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Coal|Coal]] to turn into the [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Diamond|Diamond]], which is worth 999 Rupees. He also sells a Candle for 100 Rupees.

;{{Section|Dressy Woman}}
The {{Name|Fan|Dressy Woman}} stands outside of a house in the outskirts of Great Wimbich. She tells Zelda to go to town to find "anything (she) could wish for" in the market shops.

;{{Section|Eric and Ian}}
'''Eric and Ian'''<ref>{{Cite|Hello, Zelda. I'm Eric and this is Ian.|Eric of the Twins|ZA}}</ref> are identical twin brothers who live in Great Wimbich with their father. They are unable to tell each other apart, and neither can their father.

;{{Section|Lounger}}
The {{Name|Fan|Lounger}} is a bored man who sits between the general shop and magic shop of Great Wimbich. He is bored with the town, its residents and its visitors, as nothing ever happens in town. He is accompanied by the general shop owner's dog, and he wishes that the dog would turn aggressive and bite to cause some excitement.

;{{Section|Madame Kriggle}}
'''Madame Kriggle'''<ref>{{Cite|At last, Madame Kriggle is approached by an innocent who wishes to grow in the use of magic and spells.|Madame Kriggle|ZA}}</ref> is a woman who runs the magic shop in Great Wimbich. She sells various magic Spells to Zelda, including the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Rings of Fire|Rings of Fire]], [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Turquoise Ring|Turquoise Ring]], and [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Jade Amulet|Jade Amulet]] Spells. She is also an old friend of Shurmak, who had told her of Zelda's coming.<ref>{{Cite|My old friend Shurmak has told me of your coming. I have awaited you many moons and suns.|Madame Kriggle|ZA}}</ref>

;{{Section|Shopkeeper and Knave}}
The {{Name|Fan|Shopkeeper and Knave}} are a pair who run the general shop of Great Wimbich. The two share jokes with each other, but only to pass the time and to distract themselves from the troubled future of Tolemac.<ref>{{Cite|We may joke to pass the time, Princess, but we are all troubled about the future of Tolemac. You must seek out the tablets and save us!|Knave|ZA}}</ref> The dog outside of the shop belongs to the shopkeeper.<ref>{{Cite|Hello, little one! I hope I can be of help to you. Did you see my dog out on upper street?|Shopkeeper|ZA}}</ref> He will sell Zelda a [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Bone|Bone]] for 20 Rupees, which can be given to his dog to receive the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Short Axe|Short Axe]] Spell.

;{{Section|Town Merchant}}
The {{Name|Fan|Town Merchant}} is a woman whose stall is set up in the town square of Great Wimbich. She will sell Zelda a Candle for 100 Rupees and a [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Life Potion|Life Potion]] for 200 Rupees. She and her husband once ran a locksmith forge for [[Warbane]], until Ganon came and convinced Warbane into joining him.<ref>{{Cite|For thirty suns my husband and I ran the great locksmith forge for Warbane. Then Ganon came and pressed Warbane into service for his own evil plan.|Town Merchant|ZA}}</ref> With the forge closed, the couple were left with nothing and became town merchants.<ref>{{Cite|The forged was closed! Warbane built a fortified retreat of his own, somewhere off in the Forest of Canvula to the north. We were left with nothing but these trinkets to sell. Perhaps they will be of use to you in the underground. I can not give them up cheap.|Town Merchant|ZA}}</ref>

;{{Section|Yvonne}}
'''Yvonne'''<ref>{{Cite|Hello, Zelda. My name is Yvonne.|Yvonne|ZA}}</ref> is a woman who lives in Great Wimbich and loves music. Shurmak had once helped her, so she in turn helps Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|I believe Shurmak has graced you with her friendship. She helped me once, so I am going to help you.|Yvonne|ZA}}</ref> She gives Zelda a [[Whistle|Flute]] to "soothe a savage beast", referring to a snake outside of town.

===Forest of Torian===
;{{Section|Sir Basil}}
'''Sir Basil'''<ref>{{Cite|Hail Princess Zelda, friend of Shurmak! I am Sir Basil of Tolemac, and Shurmak has sent me to assist you.|Sir Basil|ZA}}</ref> is a sword fighting trainer. He is sent by Shurmak to assist Zelda on her quest. He can be first found at the ruins of northern [[Forest of Torian]], where he gives Zelda the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Broadsword|Broadsword]] Spell. He is later encountered again at the [[Plain of Hobel]] near the [[Shrine of Strength]], where he advises that Zelda use her Broadsword well in the upcoming shrine.

;{{Section|Lodgekeeper}}
The {{Name|Fan|Lodgekeeper}} is the keeper of the White Steed Lodge,<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|To defeat the Shrine King Avianna, you need [the] feather-weapon. You got this in the second region from Waldensop, who was outside of the White Steed Lodge with two horses.|Om de Shrine King Avianna te verslaan, heb je he Feather-wapen nodig. Die heb je in de tweede regio gekregen van Waldensop, die buiten de White Steed Lodge stond met twee paarden.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 3|issue= 10|date= October 1996|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=40-41}}</ref> also known as the thieves' den.<ref>{{Cite|Good morn, lady. A noble person such as yourself on foot-end? If it's rest you seek, you'll get precious little comfort in this thieves' den, I'll tell you. [...] Be careful if you go inside, milady! Some people in these parts would rob you the same as look you, they would.|Waldensop|ZA}}</ref> He tells Zelda that the lodge is full, but she is welcome to rest there before continuing on her journey.

;{{Section|Waldensop}}
{{Main|Waldensop}}
Waldensop is a man found outside of the White Steed Lodge, in the Forest of Torian.

===Ubato Hills===
;{{Section|Bards}}
The {{Name|Fan|Bards}} are two musicians who live in the village of [[Ubato Hills]], east of the Forest of Torian. They sing a song for Zelda with lyrics based on her journey if she speaks with them.

;{{Section|East Villager}}
The {{Name|Fan|East Villager}} is a woman who lives in the eastern village of Ubato Hills. She notices that Zelda seems tired, and gives her the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Noise|Noise]] Spell.

;{{Section|Ghost Woman}}
The {{Name|Fan|Ghost Woman}} is a ghostly woman who wanders northern Ubato Hills, close to the [[Shrine of Destiny]]. She calls Zelda foolish for coming there, and says that any who tarries there never leaves. She appears to be accompanied by a ghostly dog.

;{{Section|Lonlyn}}
'''Lonlyn'''<ref>{{Cite|Greetings. I am Lonlyn, and I am a farmer.|Lonlyn|ZA}}</ref> is a farmer who lives in the eastern village of Ubato Hills. He appears where the East Villager was. He is a man of little wealth, but gives Zelda 25 Rupees to aide her on her quest.

===North Gubasha Desert===
;{{Section|House Keeper}}
The {{Name|Fan|House Keeper}} is a man who owns a house in [[North Gubasha Desert]]. He says that although the area can get colder than Ganon's heart, he had endured worse.<ref>{{Cite|I know it can get colder than Ganon's heart up here, but I've endured worse...|House Keep|ZA}}</ref> He tries to tell Zelda a story about how he was in a cold forest, but cannot remember if it was the Forest of Torian or the [[Forest of Canvula]].<ref>{{Cite|Once, when I was in the Forest of Torian- N-No it was, it was the Forest of, Canvula! No no, that cannot be true, for Canvula wood is always hot, and I say I did say it was cold, right? Oh, then it was the Forest of Torian!|House Keeper|ZA}}</ref>

;{{Section|Mystic}}
The {{Name|Fan|Mystic}} is a man who wears a big red robe and lives in North Gubasha Desert. He tells Zelda to go east to find the [[Shrine of Air]].<ref>{{Cite|Something you seek, it not of this world, but above it... Defeat the Woman of the Air, it is to the east you must go.|Mystic Man|ZA}}</ref>

===South Gubasha Desert===
;{{Section|Myntoll}}
'''Myntoll'''<ref>{{Cite|I am called Myntoll, and I know who you are. Do you not grow bored with your quest? I certainly would.|Myntoll|ZA}}</ref> is a man who lives in the village of [[South Gubasha Desert]]. He offers Zelda no advice, and only asks if she grows bored with her quest. He becomes annoyed with Zelda's persistence when spoken to again.

===Seacoast Plain===
;{{Section|Beach Merchant}}
The {{Name|Fan|Beach Merchant}} is a man who is found on the beach between the [[Seacoast Plain]] and [[Vendoss]] swamp. He had found the shipwreck of a vessel from a storm, and had collected some of the goods found in the wreck to sell.<ref>{{Cite|Did you see the shipwreck up the beach? It happened many storms ago, and I've collected some of the things from the wreck here. I will sell them to you if you wish.|Beach Merchant|ZA}}</ref> He will sell Zelda a Candle for 100 Rupees and a Life Potion for 200 Rupees.

;{{Section|Seafarer}}
The {{Name|Fan|Seafarer}} is a man found inside the lodge house in Seacoast Plain. He tells Zelda that she will not learn anything from the other sailors, as they have been to sea too long and their brains are "scrambled".

;{{Section|Rafter}}
The {{Name|Fan|Rafter}} is a sailor who ferries a [[raft]] in the Seacoast Plain. He allows Zelda to use the raft to reach the [[Sea Island]], at the cost of 20 Rupees and a Vial of Winds. He explains that since the offshore wind has died down, Zelda must bring her own.<ref>{{Cite|If you wish to cross the sea, bring us twenty Rubies and a Vial of Wind. The offshore wind has died down, and you must bring your own breeze.|Rafter|ZA}}</ref>

===Vendoss===
;{{Section|Faust}}
'''Faust'''<ref>{{Cite|Hello there, young lady! I am Faust the boatman and for two Rubies I offer a tour to Mystery Island.|Faust|ZA}}</ref> is a man who runs a boat ferry service in the Vendoss swamp. For 2 Rupees, he offers to ferry customers to the Mystery Island in the middle of the swamp's lake. It is there that Zelda finds a [[Heart Container|Crystal Heart]].

;{{Section|Gate Keeper}}
The {{Name|Fan|Gate Keeper}} is a man who opens the gate that leads into the Vendoss swamp for 10 Rupees.

;{{Section|Hooded Woman}}
The {{Name|Fan|Hooded Woman}} is found in the Vendoss swamp outside of Sirram Bew's dome house. She asks that Zelda enter their marsh village softly, and to not bring any enemies with her, as the village is a refuge from Ganon's followers.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda, we pray you walk softly here and bring no enemies on us. We have built our village on the Barachno Marsh as a refuge to avoid Ganon's fellows.|Hooded Woman|ZA}}</ref> She sits next to the [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Golden Boots|Golden Boots]], but does not acknowledge them.

;{{Section|Link's Guide}}
{{Name|Fan|Link's Guide}} is a woman who lives in the Vendoss swamp. She had known Link and had served as his guide through Tolemac, until they were found and outnumbered by Ganon's spies, leaving his Guide injured and Link captured.<ref>{{Cite|It's good of you to come see me. I was the one who was guiding Link across Tolemac, when we were spotted by one of Ganon's spies. I did what I could to stand with Link, but there were too many of them and he was taken.|Link's Guide|ZA}}</ref> She gives Zelda the missing [[Map]] of the [[Shrine of Destiny]]. She wishes she could go with Zelda, but says she is not strong enough to join her journey and is still recovering. She however advises that Zelda heeds both Gaspra's and Shurmak's advice.

;{{Section|Sirram Bew}}
'''Sirram Bew'''<ref>{{Cite|The dome house is a home of our philosopher, Sirram Bew, whose logic is not always easy to follow.|Hooded Woman|ZA}}</ref> is a philosopher who lives in the domed house of the Vendoss swamp village. He offers Zelda insight to beware her enemies, but to also be a friend to those who are trustworthy.

;{{Section|Swamp Merchant}}
The {{Name|Fan|Swamp Merchant}} is a man in the Vendoss swamp who sells a bottle of [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Repellent|Repellent]] for 100 Rupees. 

===East Forest===
;{{Section|Quarry Miner}}
The {{Name|Fan|Quarry Miner}} is the prospector of a small quarry in the [[East Forest]], close to the Shrine of Strength. He says that ever since Ganon had taken over Tolemac, he has seen little construction and no one comes to him for stones anymore.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to my quarry, young one. I don't see many folks these days. Ever since Ganon devastated the land there's been so little construction, no one comes to me for quarry stones.|Quarry Miner|ZA}}</ref> He had found some Coal, but does not know how to claim its wealth, so he gives it to Zelda.

;{{Section|Rebellion}}
The {{Name|Fan|Rebellion}} are a pair who are rebelling against Ganon. They have retreated to a sea cliff in the East Forest to practice the arts of rebellion, and can be seen sparring with each other.

===Shrine of Strength===
;{{Section|Archer}}
The {{Name|Fan|Archer}} is a woman who is found in the Shrine of Strength. She tests Zelda's ability on using the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Bow & Arrow|Bow and Arrow]] Spell by having her target practice on three bears. She gives Zelda 25 Rupees for the first bear, 50 Rupees for the second bear, and 100 Rupees for the third bear.

;{{Section|Shrine Merchants}}
The {{Name|Fan|Shrine Merchants}} are a pair of men who have set up stalls in the Shrine of Strength's main square. One merchant sells Zelda a [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Red Bow|Red Bow]] for 50 Rupees, a [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Bouquet|Bouquet]] for 20 Rupees, and a [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Saltcellar|Saltcellar]] for 10 Rupees. Because the ribbon belongs to the Red Knight, it is implied this merchant had stolen it from him to sell.<ref>{{Cite|Hold tight, little enemy! You have something stolen from me and I am strong!|Red Knight|ZA}}</ref> The other merchant does not sell any items, but advises that Zelda gain skill in every weapon before facing [[Ursore]].

;{{Section|Solitary Man}}
The {{Name|Fan|Solitary Man}} can be found near the Shrine of Strength entrance. He says that no one ever comes to him anymore ever since Ganon had taken over Tolemac.

;{{Section|Thirsty Child}}
The {{Name|Fan|Thirsty Child}} is a young boy who stands next to a well in the Shrine of Strength. He asks Zelda to draw him some water, and gives her the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Golden Necklace|Golden Necklace]] Spell for her kindness.

;{{Section|Ticket Sellers}}
The {{Name|Fan|Ticket Sellers}} are a pair of merchants who sell [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Ticket|Ticket]]s in the Shrine of Strength for 20 Rupees each. These Tickets are bought to challenge the Red and Blue Knights to a battle. The sellers will not let Zelda pass until she has purchased their Tickets.

;{{Section|Water Bearer}}
The {{Name|Fan|Water Bearer}} is a woman found early in the Shrine of Strength. She gossips to Zelda about the shrine's people, and mentions her children have all moved to other areas of [[Hyrule]], and how she wishes her husband would install running water in their house. She gives Zelda the shrine's [[Compass]] after listening to her talk.

===Forest of Canvula===
;{{Section|Goblins}}
Two '''Goblins''' live in the town ruins of Canvula.<ref>{{Cite|Can't you see I'm a busy Goblin?|Goblin|ZA}}</ref> One is inside a ruined house and tells Zelda to leave, while the other gives Zelda the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Joust|Joust]] Spell as a token of welcome.

He will appear from the trees.|author= wand_of_gamelon|published= October 31, 2020|retrieved= January 17, 2022|url= https://twitter.com/wand_of_gamelon/status/1322407184215371778|title= |site= Twitter|type= }}</ref>
