{{Term/Store|Linked Game|Linked Games|Series, OoS, OoA|plural}} are situations in which {{OoS}} and {{OoA}} are interconnected in the [[Game Boy Color]], allowing for exclusive content not found in the individual games.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 150 {{Exp Game|OoS}} & 151 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> A Linked Game is a game file of either {{OoA|-}} or {{OoS|-}}, where one game serves as the continuation of the plot that began in the other, instead of having two alternate plots if the games are played separately.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= You can play The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons and Oracle of Ages as two separate, independent games, but by linking the two together, you'll have an even deeper adventure|game= OoS|page= 16}}</ref> Linked Games are accomplished via passwords (referred to in-game as "[[Secret]]s") which allow the two games to interact.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= As you play the two games, you'll learn many passwords, or secrets, that can be used to link the two games. These secrets that link the two games make up the link system|game= OoS|page= 16}}</ref> In Linked Games, Link will obtain various items that are not otherwise obtainable.<ref name="link_exclusive">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 5}}</ref> In addition, it also serves as the true ending of both games, showing the aftermath of either titles' plot.

"Password-Linking" is a unique feature of {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}. 

==Beginning a Linked Game==
[[File:HolodrumSecret.png|thumb|173x173px|right|An example of a "Secret to Holodrum"]]
Upon completing either {{OoA}} or {{OoS}}, a special [[Secret]] (or password) will appear ("[[Secret#Secret to Holodrum/Labrynna|Secret to Holodrum]]" in {{OoA|-}}, "[[Secret#Secret to Holodrum/Labrynna|Secret to Labrynna]]" in {{OoS|-}}). This password can be inputted into the opposite game while creating a new file, under "Secrets". Doing so begins a Linked Game.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Upon completing The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages, you will learn the secret (password) that will let you continue you quest by travelling to the distant land of Holodrum|game= OoA|page= 48}}</ref><ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Upon completing The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons, you will learn the secret (password) that will let you continue you quest by travelling to the distant land of Labrynna|game= OoS|page= 48}}</ref> Alternatively, the two games can be linked directly without passwords with the use of two {{Wp|Game Boy Color}}s and a {{Wp|Game Link Cable}}, using the "Game Link" function instead.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= To begin this game as a continuation of The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons, choose an unused file on the File Selection Screen, then choose "SECRETS" or "GAME LINK"|game= OoA|page= 16}}</ref><ref>{{Cite manual|quote= To begin this game as a continuation of The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages, choose an unused file on the File Selection Screen, then choose "SECRETS" or "GAME LINK"|game= OoS|page= 16}}</ref> On the File Selection screen, Linked Game files can be identified the icon of [[Link]], who will be holding the [[Harp of Ages]] (in {{OoA|-}}) or the [[Rod of Seasons]] (in {{OoS|-}}) on files where the Secret to Holodrum/Labrynna has been used.

The exact password that appears as the Secret to Labrynna or Holodrum will vary depending on how the game was played (which animal companion Link had, how he raised [[Bipsom]], and so on). The password assures that Link will have the same animal companion and the same son of [[Bipin]] and [[Blossom]] in the Linked Game.

In a Linked Game, Link begins his adventure with his [[Wooden Sword]] in ''Oracle of Seasons'' and with a [[Wooden Shield]] in ''Oracle of Ages'', and with four [[Heart Container]]s instead of three.<ref name="OoS Guide 19">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 119}}</ref>

==Plot Alterations==
[[File:GanonOraclesTrident.png|left|thumb|178x178px|Ganon as seen in-game]]
The main purpose of a Linked Game is to connect the plots of ''Oracle of Seasons''/''Ages'', in order to conclude to the series. A Linked Game witnesses the arrival of [[Princess Zelda]] to the lands of [[Holodrum]] or [[Labrynna]], who is captured later on in the game by [[Twinrova]], to be offered as a sacrifice for the resurrection of [[Ganon]]. By freeing both Oracles, [[Din (Oracle)|Din]] and [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]], by defeating [[General Onox]] or [[Veran]], respectively (whichever is left), they are able to transport [[Link]] to the [[Room of Rites]], a dungeon found only in a Linked Game. Here, Link fights Twinrova and, ultimately, the improperly revived Ganon himself.<ref name="link_exclusive" /> Defeating the Demon King concludes the Linked Game.

==Password-Linked Events==
There are several more alterations to the games made in a Linked Game. Characters that are found in both games, such as the [[Piratian]]s, [[Bipin]] and [[Blossom]], or Link's [[Animal]] companions ([[Ricky]], [[Moosh]] and [[Dimitri]]), will recognize the hero from their past adventures. Even certain characters specific to one game will have heard of Link's deeds in another land.<ref name="OoS Guide 19"/> There are many events that happen in each game that would not otherwise happen.

===Hero's Cave===
Another addition is the appearance of an extra dungeon, called the [[Hero's Cave]]. In {{OoS|-}}, this replaces the original Hero's Cave, a mini-dungeon where Link obtains his first [[Sword]] (in a Linked Game, Link already has his sword at the start of the adventure). In {{OoA|-}}, the Hero's Cave appears on a deviation of the [[Maku Road]]. This dungeon is considered to be one of the most difficult of the two games. The reward for completing it is the blue [[Armor Ring L-3]] in ''Ages'', and the red [[Power Ring L-3]] in ''Seasons''.

==Transferring Rings==
{{Main|Vasu#Ring Transfer and Fortunes}}
[[File:OoS Blue Snake Sprite.gif|left]][[File:OoS Red Snake Sprite.gif|right]]
Once two game files of {{OoA|-}} and {{OoS|-}} have been linked, appraised [[Magic Ring]]s can be transferred between the two games with the help of either the [[Red Snake]] or the [[Blue Snake]] at [[Vasu|Vasu Jewelers]]. 

The [[Red Snake]] transfers the rings by giving Link a [[Ring Secret]], which transfers the rings when told to the Red Snake in the opposing game.<ref>{{Cite|If you save the secret the Red Snake told you in an Oracle of Ages game you played, and tell it to the Red Snake here, you can bring rings found in your Oracle of Ages game here. In the same way, you can take rings from here to your Oracle of Ages game.|All About Ring Secrets|OoS}}</ref> The [[Blue Snake]] allows for ring transfer without the use of a secret by using two [[Game Boy Color]]s and a {{Wp|Game Link Cable}}.<ref>{{Cite|With a Game Link cable and two Game Boy Color systems, you can bring rings you found in an Oracle of Seasons game to Labrynna without saving your secret. Insert your Oracle of Seasons Game Pak into another Game Boy Color, insert a Game Link cable and turn the power ON, but don't press any buttons. Play Oracle of Ages, talk to Blue Snake and choose "Link" to bring Oracle of Seasons rings here. In the same way, you can take rings found here to your Oracle of Seasons game.|Ring Link Basics|OoA}}</ref> However, it only works with Game Boy Colors and a Game Boy Color game link cable, as the games do not recognize the Game Boy Advance link cable and the linking feature will not function at all.

==Secrets==
{{Main|Linking Secret}}
:''See also: [[Secret]]s''
[[File:OoS Farore Artwork.png|right|100px|Farore, Oracle of Secrets]]
[[Secret]]s are the centerpiece of a Linked Game. On top of the Secret to Holodrum/Labrynna that begins a Linked Game, and the [[Ring Secret]] that allows [[Link]] to transfer his [[Magic Rings]], Link will obtain many more secrets during the Linked Game by talking to certain people in the game.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= You will learn many passwords, or secrets, when you play a linked game.|game= OoS|page= 16}}</ref> These secrets are then told to a specific character in the first game Link completed. After telling the secret, a mini-game often ensues. Telling secrets rewards Link with a special item which is otherwise unobtainable.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=As you play, you'll learn more secrets that will let you bring items from one game to the other, power up the items you have, and even [get] new items that are otherwise unavailable!|game= OoS|page=48}}</ref><ref name="OoS Guide 121">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 121}}</ref> Notable password-linked items include the [[Master Sword]], the [[Mirror Shield]], [[Bomb]] and [[Mystical Seed|seed]] capacity upgrades, [[Bombchu]]s, [[Biggoron's Sword]], and the L-3 [[Ring Box]]. A few powerful rings can also be found only through a Linked Game. All of these items are optional, but can be very useful to Link on his quest.

This system is held together by [[Farore (Oracle)|Farore]], who resides in the [[Hall of Secrets]] found within the [[Maku Tree]] in both {{OoA}} and {{OoS}}. Farore is the keeper of Link's [[secret]]s. When an item is obtained through "password-linking", Link also receives another password that allow him to bring said item back to the land opposite the one he is in (i.e., bringing an item obtained in [[Labrynna]] in {{OoA|-}} using a password from a Linked Game in {{OoS|-}}, and using the return password to bring it back to [[Holodrum]], and vice-versa). This password is presented to Farore in the opposite world in which the item is obtained. Farore will then transfer the item from Holodrum into Labrynna, or Labrynna into Holodrum.<ref name="OoS Guide 121"/> The secrets are recorded in [[Farore's Memory]], available to Link at any time.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= You can review your secret in the Hall of Secrets on the completed Game Pak|game= OoS|page=18}}</ref>

==Hero's Game==
[[File:OOA Credits Linked.png|left|thumb|The ending screen of the game]]
At the end of a Linked Game, the Hero's Secret is received. Inputting the Hero's Secret at the start of a new game (the same one that was played with a Linked Game) allows Link to experience the game without the Linked Game's altered plot (the Secret can also be used in opposite game, but most of its advantages are lost). Link starts with four Heart Containers instead of three, and rings from the two previous playthroughs can be transferred to this game file. At the end of the "Hero's Game", the [[Secret#Secret to Holodrum/Labrynna|Holodrum Secret]] or [[Secret#Secret to Holodrum/Labrynna|Labrynna Secret]] (depending on the game) will be obtained, allowing Link to experience another Linked Game for whichever game is left. Therefore, the Hero's Secret allows the player to experience both games with the original and altered storylines. 

On the File Selection Screen, a Hero's Game is identified by a [[Triforce]] in the upper-right. Furthermore, the Triforce will also appear on any Linked Games files created using the Holodrum or Labrynna Secrets obtained from a Hero's Game. Completing such a "Hero's Linked Game" will not yield any new Hero's Secret upon completion.

==Trivia==
* The Linked Game is essential for the [[100% Completion]] of {{OoA}} and {{OoS}}.
* As a Linked Game would be much more complex with three games, the development of the third ''Oracle'' game was cancelled.<ref>[http://gameboy.ign.com/objects/873/873036.html IGN Article on the Third Oracle]</ref>
* A Linked Game in {{OoA|-}} is the only way to access the [[Black Tower]] in the present.  As well, there is one screen of the overworld, within the present day [[Sea of Storms]], that can only be reached in a Linked Game.
* [[Princess Zelda]]'s sprite is taken from [[Marin]]'s from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|Link's Awakening DX]]'', with a crown being added to distinguish the characters.
* The final battle against [[Ganon]] in the Linked Game marks the first and only time where the series' central antagonist is eliminated with a normal [[Sword]] rather than the [[Master Sword]] or [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]]. This is because his resurrection by [[Twinrova]]'s sacrifice ritual was unstable and therefore didn't fully grant him his true powers and attacking [[Link]] with blind rage, similar to in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]''. [[File:OoS Princess Zelda kissing Link.png|thumb|Link being kissed by Princess Zelda in the ending]]
* At the end of the game, Princess Zelda can be seen kissing Link on his cheek, which is a reference to [[Princess Peach]] of the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series when rescued by [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] in some games. Although this remains the only case in the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|franchise]] where this is explicitly shown (as direct affection in [[Nintendo]] media is extremely rare), it is possible that ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'' also shared the same event.
* If the player links {{OoS|-}} from {{OoA|-}}, [[Onox]] will have a distinct reaction to Link, recognizing him as the one who defeated [[Veran]] while boasting that he is stronger before abducting [[Din (Oracle)|Din]]. If the player links {{OoA|-}} from {{OoS|-}}, however, Veran has no distinct reactions to Link's presence.
* If the player links {{OoS|-}} from {{OoA|-}}, [[Impa]] will recognize Link from their encounter in [[Labrynna]], and Din's performing troupe has new dialogue revealing them to be [[Knight of Hyrule|Hyrulean Knights]]. However, if the player links {{OoA|-}} from {{OoS|-}}, Impa begins the game possessed by Veran and has no dialogue indicating that she recognizes Link from [[Holodrum]].
