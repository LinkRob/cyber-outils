{{Term/Store|Great Fairy Training||HWAoC}} is a level 23 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Great Fairy Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Great Fairy Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only the {{Plural|HWAoC|Great Fairy}}. When completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Fairy|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Great Fairy|link}}' objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. Most of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}, except for a single {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} that stays at the bottom of the map. When the {{Plural|HWAoC|Great Fairy}} reach 100 and 200 KOs, another {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} spawns with more {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}}. After the {{Plural|HWAoC|Great Fairy}} reach 300 KOs, all remaining {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga|link}} {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} disappear or flee. The time limit is also removed, and {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu|link}} appears near the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}} is defeated.
