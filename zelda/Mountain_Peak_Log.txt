The {{Term/Store|''Mountain Peak Log''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mountain Peak Log|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|Mountain Peak Log}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} found within the {{Term|BotW|Ruined House|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Mount Granajh|link}}. Within it, an unknown author details their attempts to unearth an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that they learned about.<ref>{{Cite|I come to this snowy mountain intending to find the {{Color|BotW Red|ancient shrine}} that was written about in an old text.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> Reading this {{Term|BotW|Book}} activates the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Secret of the Snowy Peaks|link}}", and its text provides hints for how to unlock the {{Term|BotW|Suma Sahma Shrine|link}}.
