The {{Term/Store|Fural Plain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Fural Plain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Fural Plain is located in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Harfin Lake|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Guchini Plain|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain}} is a large plain that is surrounded by many small cliffs, and also sprouts many clumps of {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} throughout the landscape. In the southwest corner of the Fural Plain, the {{Term|BotW|Haran Lake|link}} rests between a few {{Plural|BotW|Tree}}, and in the southeastern corner of the {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain}}, the {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}} sits between two small cliffs. A small path runs through the middle of the Plain, and along the path; {{Term|BotW|Totsuna|link}} can be found running across it.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found by jumping into a circle of lily pads in the {{Term|BotW|Lake of the Horse God|link}}.

===The Horse God===
:{{Main|Malanya}}
In the very southeast corner of the {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain}}, behind the Highland Stable, a path runs between two cliffs. At the end of this path, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find the {{Term|BotW|Lake of the Horse God}}, and stretching over this lake is the {{Term|BotW|Horse God Bridge|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} climbs up to this bridge, he can find the Horse God {{Term|BotW|Malanya|link}} in his spring. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask him to revive any of his {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} that had previously died.
