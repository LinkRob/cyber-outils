The '''Tocktorok''' is an [[Automaton]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Tocktorok|Fireworks pour continually out of this dangerous automaton's mouth, causing flashy destruction.<ref name="Tocktorok">{{Cite|Fireworks pour continually out of this dangerous automaton's mouth, causing flashy destruction.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Tocktoroks are [[Octo]]-like Automatons created by [[Dampé]]. They are obtained as a reward for completing the [[Explosions Galore!]] [[side quest]], which requires [[Firework]]s and any [[Octorok]] echo to complete.
