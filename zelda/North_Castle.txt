The {{Term/Store|North Castle||Series, TAoL}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 224 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref> or '''North Palace''',{{Name Ref|TAoL}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and Overview==
The North Castle is the resting place of the [[Sleeping Zelda|slumbering princess, Zelda]]. As Link goes out on his adventure to place the [[Crystal]]s that will open the entrance to the [[Great Palace]] in their proper places, he can return to the North Castle at any time. In the case that Link ends up getting a [[Game Over]], he will have to start over in North Castle.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TLoZ|-}} TV Series===
[[File:North Castle (Cold Spells).png|thumb|left|The North Castle in the animated series]]
The North Castle is also considered to be a key place in {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. Most of the episode's title cards portray a view of the castle, since it is the usual location where the episodes start off. Link, Zelda, [[Spryte]] and [[King Harkinian]] reside there full time, and the castle is portrayed to be much larger than in the games and the Valiant Comic series. The castle also seems to host various events such as the [[Magician's Contest]], which take place in the castle's enormous courtyard. 

Just like [[Hyrule Castle]], the North Castle is prone to coming under attack by Ganon or his minions since the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] is kept away in one of the castle's towers. Due to this, Ganon is known to go to extreme measures to try and obtain the Triforce, such as in "[[That Sinking Feeling]]", where Ganon pulls the castle into the [[Underworld]] in hopes of easily retrieving the sacred relic. North Castle also seems to have a rather weak security since most of the time, Ganon and his minions seem to easily enter the castle's inner walls without getting noticed by the guards.
{{clear|left}}

==={{CN|-}}===
[[File:North Castle 2 (Captain N).png|thumb|right|North Castle in ''Captain N: The Game Master'']]
In {{CN}}, North Castle serves as the home of both [[Princess Zelda]] and [[Link]], and it is also where the three pieces of the [[Triforce]] are located. The North Castle is portrayed with a more futuristic look than its other depictions, straying from its medieval-times appearance. Very few areas of the castle are explored in this cartoon series, as only Princess Zelda's throne is briefly seen in "[[Quest for the Potion of Power]]" when Zelda and Link meet Kevin, and the other location shown is the chamber where the Triforce is kept.

Like in previous incarnations, the North Castle is guarded by some rather inept [[Soldier]]s, allowing King Hippo, Eggplant Wizard of [[mew:Mother Brain|Mother Brain]] to infiltrate the castle without too much trouble and going as far as stealing the Triforce, as seen in "[[Having a Ball]]".

==={{TSP|-}}===
In {{TSP}}, North Castle is first visited after Princess Zelda invites [[Charles of Moria]] to meet the King of Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Say!' Zelda exclaims. 'Why don't you come back to North Castle with Link and me? My father, the king, would love to meet you.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=38}}</ref> Although much of Link's adventure takes place outside the castle, a great part of the story develops here. It is in a secret chamber known as the [[Triforce Room]] where the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] is safely kept away from the hands of Ganon.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=There are false Triforce rooms located in North Castle, as well as the true Triforce room where the Triforce of Wisdom is hidden. The path from the throne room to the true Triforce room passes over exactly ten diamonds.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=78}}</ref> Princess Zelda, the king, and [[Impa]] all dwell in the castle. 

In the book, the king quickly trusts in Charles and decides to show him the Triforce of Wisdom. He leads the Morian knight into the Triforce Room, where he dubs him a Knight of the Triforce and asks him to protect it and to keep it away from Ganon's hands.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Wonderful! I hereby dub you a knight of the Triforce,' says the king, beaming.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=78}}</ref> Later on in the book, after Link uses the [[Mirror of Truth]] to reveal that Charles is in fact [[Ganon]] in disguise, Charles will manage to steal the Triforce of Wisdom from the Triforce Room and escape with it into the [[Midoro Swamp]], where Link must follow him to defeat him and bring peace back to [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Charles inches toward the Triforce, snatches it from its stand and then drags Zelda out the door.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=23}}</ref>

The [[Tower]], which is situated in the most remote part of North Castle, is the location in which the castle's prisoners are kept. In the case that Link accuses Charles of being evil without having proof to back it up, he will be sent here as punishment, and the only means of escape will be to use the [[Golden Whistle]] or the [[Magical Clock|Clock]] given to the young hero by Impa.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Tower is in the most remote part of North Castle, up a long, narrow, spiral staircase.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=62}}</ref><ref name=tower>{{Cite Book|quote='Link, I've heard enough. Your petty envy has become more than a nuisance. It's time you were taught some manners. I hereby banish you to the Tower for three days, effectively immediately!'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=62}}</ref>

Once Link manages to defeat Ganon, he returns to the North Castle with the Triforce of Wisdom in hand, where the king thanks him for having saved Hyrule once again and thus decides to celebrate his victory against Ganon.

==={{TLoZ|-}} (Valiant Comics)===
[[File:North palace.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A detailed drawing of the North Castle (or North Palace) from the Valiant comics]]
The North Castle is a main location in the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}. It is situated on an island in the middle of a lake,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The North Palace is situated on an island in the middle of a crystal lake. The waters of the lake are always fresh and clean, fed by an underground river.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=Bonus Page [Issue #2]}}</ref> and the walls of the castle are surrounded by a protective magic that prevent evil from gaining entrance to it.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Soon, Darknut! Once Link steps out of the North Palace and its protective magic, this wand will strike him with all the evil magic in Hyrule in a single blast!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=9 [He Also Serves]}}</ref> [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] live here while they fight against [[Ganon]]'s evil forces during the events that take place after ''The Legend of Zelda'' and {{TAoL|-}}. It is portrayed as the home of Princess Zelda and her father, [[King Harkinian]], and later Link himself, after the events of the first two games. The people of Hyrule come to engage in economic activity in the marketplace that is located in the courtyard of the castle. 

There are also several towers and buildings within the main structure, primarily a tower each for both Link, where the Triforce of Wisdom is kept,<ref>''Link's tower. The Triforce of Wisdom is kept here.'' - N/A (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> and Zelda and a residence for King Harkinian,<ref>''The King's residence'' - N/A (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> which is the largest room in the entire castle.<ref>''The biggest room in the Palace--- rank has its privileges!'' - N/A (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> To keep intruders from entering, the palace has several [[Watchman Erol|watchmen]] to ensure that the main gates are only opened to those who have permission.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Watchman Erol, keep the gate closed! Nobody gets in or out! '''Nobody!''''|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=8 [He Also Serves]}}</ref>
