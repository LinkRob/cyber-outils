The {{Term/Store|Elixir Soup||Series, TWW, TWWHD, HWL, HWDE}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWL, HWDE}}{{Note|{{Term|Series|Elixir Soup}} was referred to as '''Elixer Soup''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Elixer Soup}}<br/>A specialty of Link's grandmother, Elixer Soup was able to restore magic and health meters. In addition, Elixer Soup doubled Link's attack power.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160307190753/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/e.jsp#ElixerSoup|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Elixir Soup is a special homemade soup made for [[Link]] by his [[Grandmother]]. Being a healthy meal, the Soup heals both his [[Life Gauge|health]] and [[Magic|Magic Power]] like the [[Blue Potion]], and also doubles his attack power until he takes damage (indicated by his [[Sword]] having an orange glow and his [[Life Gauge]] flashing).<ref>{{Cite|This healthy soup your grandmother makes replenishes all of your life energy and magic power!|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Your attack power also doubles until the first time you take damage!|N/A|TWW}}</ref> It is stored in [[bottle]]s like a [[Potion]] and each bottle can hold two helpings, similarly to [[Milk]] in previous [[Nintendo 64]] installments.<ref>{{Cite|And your kind old grandmother filled your bottle so full that there's two helpings inside!|N/A|TWW}}</ref>

Link can get the Soup after he cures his Grandmother, when she falls ill. Link does this by going to [[Fairy Fountain]] on [[Outset Island]] to get a [[Fairy]] that will heal her.<ref>{{Cite|Now, I'm certain that a little fairy dust would provide all the lift her spirits need, but... Well, the problem is, I haven't seen any fairies in these parts recently. Of course, long ago you could find a lot of them up by the old Fairy Fountain|Sturgeon|TWW}}</ref> After being healed, Link can always get a [[Bottle]] of soup from her when he runs out, but he can only get one Bottle of soup at a time, unlike Potions.<ref>{{Cite|If you run out, I can make it for you any time you like.|Grandma|TWW}}</ref> Unlike when he drinks a Bottle of potion, Link smiles before he drinks the soup rather than grimacing; this is likely because it is Link's favorite soup if not overall food, which his grandma originally planned to prepare for him on his birthday, and the potions themselves apparently do not have a good taste.<ref>{{Cite|Your grandma is going to make your favorite soup for you tonight!|Grandma|TWW}}</ref>

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWDE Elixir Soup Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, Elixir Soup appears as a Drink-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]] much like in The Wind Waker. It is imbued with the Fire {{Term|HWDE|Element|link}}. When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Elixir Soup can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.
{{Noncanon|End}}

== Trivia ==

* Although [[Apple|Apples]] can be found and eaten in {{aLttP}} by bashing into trees with the [[Dash Attack]], the Elixir Soup is the first [[Food]] in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] to be manually used by [[Link]] as he sees fit.
* The Elixir Soup is also [[Aryll]]'s favorite food according to Link's Grandmother in {{TWW|-}}.
* In the German localization, the Elixir Soup is called '''Grandma's Soup'''.
* Coincidentally, if Link uses this item after obtaining the [[Master Sword]], the sacred blade will resemble the [[Master Sword Lv2]] upgrade from ''A Link to the Past'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', and even shares the same proprieties, as that version of the weapon has twice its attack power.
