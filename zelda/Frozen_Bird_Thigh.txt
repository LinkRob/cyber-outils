The {{Term/Store|Frozen Bird Thigh|Frozen Bird Thighs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Frozen Bird Thigh}}<br/>High-quality, frozen bird meat. It's so solid it looks like it could be used as a weapon. Eating it will restore some hearts and cool you down, but don't break your teeth on it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Frozen Bird Thighs can be made by chilling {{Plural|BotW|Raw Bird Thigh|link}} with [[Ice]]. They are frozen solid and difficult to eat.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> Eating one will restore one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} and grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} temporary [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"></ref>
