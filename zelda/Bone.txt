{{Term/Store|Bone|Bones|Series, LADX, FPTRR|plural}} are recurring [[object]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 168 {{Exp Game|LADX}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Bone|Kitchen|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LA}}
In {{LA}}, piles of Bones can be found in rocky or sandy areas like caverns and [[Yarna Desert]]. There is a pile of Bones in [[CiaoCiao]]'s hut.

==={{TP|-}}===
Bones can be found in [[Hyrule Castle Town (Twilight Princess)|Hyrule Castle Town]] on the southeast road beside a [[dog]]. The Bone can be picked up and thrown. If thrown, the nearby dog will chase after it, wagging its tail.<ref>{{Cite|If you throw a bone or something, they wag their tails so happily... Dogs are so cute...|Hylian|TP}}</ref> If [[Link]] throws the Bone near some [[bush]]es, the dog may bring back [[Rupee]]s instead.{{Verify|TP}}

==Spinoff appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{ZA|-}}===
In {{ZA}}, the Bone can be bought from the general store in [[Great Wimbich]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. It can then be given to the shopkeeper's [[Dog]] outside, which will then drop the [[Short Axe]] [[Spell]].

==={{FPTRR|-}}===
In {{FPTRR}}, the Bone is an [[ingredient]] that can be obtained mostly from [[Rat]]s. They can be used to make [[Tingle Fireworks]] and [[Sunshine Paste]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
