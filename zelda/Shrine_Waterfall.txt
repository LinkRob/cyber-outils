The {{Term/Store|Shrine Waterfall||Series}} is a location in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Waterfall Shrine|System|LANS}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|LANS|Shrine Waterfall|link}} is located in the region on the eastern coast of [[Koholint Island]], although in the original game and {{LA|DX}} it forms the north-eastern section of the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine|link}} region which encompasses the Shrine Waterfall region,{{Fact}} the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine}} to the north-west and the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins|link}} to the south.

The area consists of a small maze with rocks and grass blocking passage, and a river in the north. A [[Secret Seashell]] can be found hidden underneath a lone rock in the north-east of the maze. An [[Owl Statue]] is situated on the very eastern edge of the area. The tree behind contains a hidden [[Fairy]] that will appear if Link rams into it with the [[Pegasus Boots]].

The eponymous waterfall in this region is where the [[Rapids Ride]] to the north ends and is the source of the river which runs through the north of the region. The Shrine Waterfall has direct connections to the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine}}, and the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}} regions. A cave in the northern cliffs leads to [[Tal Tal Heights]], where the Rapids Ride begins.

Link must pass through the region in order to access the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}} in the south, and later the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine}} in the north-west.

==Nomenclature==
Unused location text in the original version of {{LA}} names this region '''Waterfall at the Shrine'''.
