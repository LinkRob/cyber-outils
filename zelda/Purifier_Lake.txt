{{Term/Store|Purifier Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Purifier Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Purifier Lake is located in [[East Necluda]] between the [[Spring of Wisdom]] and the [[Peak of Awakening]]. It is a deep enough lake that [[Link]] is able to swim inside of it. The Purifier Lake is surrounded by cliffs on one side, and a field of [[Tree]]s on the other.

On one side of Purifier Lake, a Red [[Hinox]] sleeps on the lakebed, and in the nearby trees, a [[Silver Lynel]] roams the field.
