The {{Name|Fan|Interloper War}} is a conflict that occurred in the backstory of {{TP}}.{{Name Ref|TP}}

==History==

{{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}} was created by the {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Goddess|link}}: {{Term|TPHD|Din|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Nayru|link}}, and {{Term|TPHD|Farore|link}}. After the goddesses were finished with {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}}, they created another parallel world known as the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Realm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The lands where the goddesses descended came to be known as the Sacred Realm.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> Before departing the realm, the {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Goddess}} left behind the three golden triangles filled with their power, these three triangles when put together would become a larger triangle known as the {{Term|TPHD|Triforce|link}}. After this, {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}} was at peace for a number of years.

Some time after the events of {{SS}}, the legend of a supreme power spread across {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}}, causing a great war over the location of the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Realm}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The world remained at peace for many years, but a group wielding strong magical powers desires the Triforce and fights to obtain it. The goddesses send spirits of light, which banish the group to the Twilight Realm.|book=E|page=11}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But soon, word of the Sacred Realm spread through Hyrule, and a great battle ensued...|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> During this war, the tribe skilled in dark magic known only as the {{Plural|TPHD|Dark Interloper|display=Interlopers|link}} attempted to establish dominion over the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Realm}} using the {{Term|TPHD|Fused Shadow}}. Ordered by the {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Goddess}}, the {{Plural|TPHD|Spirits of Light|link}} intervened. They sealed away the great magic those individuals had mastered,<ref name=Lanayru>{{Cite|Among those living in the light, interlopers who excelled at magic appeared. Wielding powerful sorcery, they tried to establish dominion over the Sacred Realm. It was then that the goddesses ordered us three light spirits to intervene. We sealed away the great magic those individuals had mastered. You know this magic...It is the dark power you seek...the Fused Shadow.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> and splintered it, so it became the fragments of the {{Term|TPHD|Fused Shadow}} hidden in the {{Plural|TPHD|Temple|link}} of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}}. The goddesses chased the interlopers across {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}} and banished them to {{Term|TPHD|Twilight Realm|link}} using {{Term|TPHD|Mirror of Twilight|link}}.<ref name=Midnaquote>{{Cite|What do you think happened to the magic wielders who tried to rule the Sacred Realm? They were banished. They were chased across the sacred lands of Hyrule and driven into another realm by the goddesses. It was another world entirely... The antithesis of Hyrule, where the sun shines bright. Its denizens became shadows that could not mingle with the light. Eventually, most came to call it the Twilight Realm, and from it, none could return to the world of light...|Midna|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The gods dispatched four Light Spirits, who sealed the usurpers' magic into Fused Shadows. They then banished the offenders to the Twilight Realm using the Mirror of Twilight, so that they would never be able to return to the Light World.|book= HH |page= 113}}</ref>

The descendants of the Interlopers became the {{Term|TPHD|Twili|link}}, doomed to live as mere shadows in the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They were forever doomed to live in the twilight, flitting in the half-light of dusk, mere shadows of Hyrule...This is the history of the Twili as it has been passed down from our ancestors...|Midna|TP}}</ref> During this conflict, {{Term|TPHD|Rauru}} sealed the entrance to the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Realm}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book=HH |publisher= |page=69 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The sage Rauru builds the Temple of Time and seals the entrance to the Sacred Realm.|book= E |page= 11}}</ref>
