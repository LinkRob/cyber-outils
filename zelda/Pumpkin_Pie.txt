{{Term/Store|Pumpkin Pie|Pumpkin Pies|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Pumpkin Pie}}<br/>The intense sweetness of fortified pumpkins makes this dessert popular among children.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Pumpkin Pies by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Cane Sugar]], and [[Goat Butter]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The default version can only be made by adding either a fruit or a vegetable with an effect that differs from Fortified Pumpkins. When eaten, they will restore some of {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Due to the sweetness of Fortified Pumpkins, these desserts are popular among children.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Only one variation of Pumpkin Pie exists:

* '''Tough Pumpkin Pie''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Pumpkin Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The intense sweetness of fortified pumpkins makes this dessert popular among children.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding no additional ingredients. By consuming these pies, Link's defense will be temporarily boosted.<ref name="Tough"/>
