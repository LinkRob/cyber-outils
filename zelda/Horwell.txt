==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Horwell}}. This instructor at the Knight Academy is recognized as an expert on animals of every shape and size.
}}

Horwell is one of the [[Knight Academy]] instructors, and is also the instructor in charge of the [[Wing Ceremony]]. He has a great knowledge of animals, such as [[Remlit]]s.<ref name="Expert"/>

Before the Wing Ceremony, he asks for [[Link]]'s help retrieving [[Gaepora]]'s Remlit, [[Mia]], who has climbed atop the academy's roof.<ref>{{Cite|You see, Zelda's father--Headmaster {{Color|SS Blue|Gaepora}}--has a pet named {{Color|SS Red|Mia}}, and, well...she's scampered off again.|Horwell|SS}}</ref> Horwell explains that, although Mia looks harmless, he lets the Remlit roam free outside at night since the creature transforms into a vicious monster.<ref>{{Cite|Such an odd creature, the {{Color|SS Red|Remlit}}. She may look cuddly now, but at night, it's another story entirely! When night falls here in Skyloft, creatures like this Remlit become possessed by an evil force.|Horwell|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I keep my distance from Mia at night. There was that one time when she snuck into the academy after nightfall.|Horwell|SS}}</ref>

After [[Crimson Loftwing|Link's Loftwing]] is kidnapped by [[Groose]], Headmaster Gaepora informs Horwell about the incident so that he may delay the Wing Ceremony.<ref>{{Cite|If I recall correctly, this year {{Color|SS Blue|Instructor Horwell}} is presiding over the {{Color|SS Red|ceremony}}. Link, you should go explain the situation to him and see if he's willing to delay the race a little so that you can find your bird.|Gaepora|SS}}</ref> When Horwell learns of this, he reassures Link not to worry about the race, pointing out that he owes the young hero for having recovered Mia for him.<ref>{{Cite|I still owe you for helping me out earlier, so don't worry about the ceremony for now. You focus on finding your Loftwing!|Horwell|SS}}</ref>

Once all 80 of the [[Gratitude Crystal]]s have been recovered, Horwell will be found wandering around Skyloft with Mia as she will no longer exhibits any of her previous aggressive behavior.<ref>{{Cite|Look, Link! Though night has come, Mia exhibits none of the nocturnal aggressiveness we've come to expect in Remlits! It's just amazing!|Horwell|SS}}</ref>
