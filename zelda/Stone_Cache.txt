The {{Term/Store|Stone Cache||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Stone Cache|Map|SSHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|SS}}
The {{Term|SS|Stone Cache}} is located in the southeastern corner of the {{Term|SS|Lanayru Desert|link}}. One of the nodes needed to activate the {{Term|SS|Power Generator|link}} is located inside, helping {{Term|SS|Link|link}} access {{Term|SS|Lanayru Mining Facility|link}}. To reach the node, {{Term|SS|Link}} must activate a {{Term|SS|Timeshift Stone|link}} and bring an {{Term|SS|Ampilus Egg|link}} to the room's northern end. The {{Term|SS|Ampilus Egg|display=Egg}} will power a door that gives {{Term|SS|Link}} access to the node.
