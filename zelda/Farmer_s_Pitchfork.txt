{{Plural|BotW|Farmer's Pitchfork}} are sharp, light tools used to easily collect hay.<ref name="Inventory"/> However, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use them as {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} against {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. They are most common around villages, such as {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}.

With their metal prongs, {{Plural|BotW|Farmer's Pitchfork}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will also sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. Finally, they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
