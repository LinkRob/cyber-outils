'''Hero's Apple Farm''' is a farm created by [[Link]] after the events of the {{ALttP|Himekawa}}. 

==Features and Overview==
The Hero's Apple Farm is first mentioned at the beginning of the [[manga]], where Link is aiming to become an apple farmer and has decided to call his business ''Link's Apple Farm''.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Just watch. I'm gonna make this the best orchard in Hyrule. People will travel from all over for my apples.|book= {{ALttP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 4}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I've even got a name for the farm... (...) "Link's Apple Farm," of course!|book= {{ALttP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 5}}</ref> He originally wanted to be a swordsmith, but thought of it as kids' stuff when he got older.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= So you're okay with it?... Staying here and being a farmer. You always dreamed of being a swordsmith.|book= {{ALttP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 5}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Kids' stuff! Besides, no matter how strong your weapon is...you can't fight if you're hungry!|book= {{ALttP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 6}}</ref> During a storm early on in the story, when he is looking for his [[Link's Uncle|Uncle]], Link finds his harvest of apples ruined, and devastated, says that it took four years for the trees to blossom.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= My apples! (...) It took four years for the trees to blossom...|book= {{ALttP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 13}}</ref>

After the events of the manga, Link brings [[Princess Zelda]] to his apple farm, now called the ''Hero's Apple Farm''.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Come with me. I have something to show you.|book= {{ALttP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 179}}</ref> Many villagers are there harvesting the apples. [[Ghanti]] tells some children that the Hero's apples are delicious and gives one the energy to grow.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The Hero's apples are delicious! Eat one and it gives you energy! Energy to grow!|book= {{ALttP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 180}}</ref> Princess Zelda comments that Link has put a lot into the apples and they must be the sweetest in the world.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= These apples have so much of...you in them. They must be the sweetest in the world!|book= {{ALttP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 180}}</ref> The hero and princess then sit together under one of the trees and eat the apples.
