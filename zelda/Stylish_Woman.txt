The {{Term/Store|Stylish Woman||Series, ALBW}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300 {{Exp Game|ALBW}} & 305 {{Exp Game|TFH}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
[[File:ALBW Stylish Woman Screenshot.png|thumb|left|220px|The Stylish Woman in {{ALBW|-}}]]
In {{ALBW|-}}, the Stylish Woman lives in [[Kakariko Village]] in the house west of the [[Bee Guy]]'s house. The door to her house is locked early in the game, as she is having trouble deciding what to wear. Her house can be accessed by using a [[Fissure]] from [[Thieves' Town]] in [[Lorule]]. She initially expresses surprise that Link was able to get inside, but chooses to reward him a [[Piece of Heart]] after he compliments the dress she is wearing. She subsequently unlocks the door to her house, allowing Link to come and go as he pleases, and will reward him with a single [[Heart]] whenever he returns.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you came back. Here's a little token of my affection. Go on-take it. Don't be shy!|Stylish Woman|ALBW}}</ref>

The Stylish Woman will also inform Link of a [[Letter in a Bottle|Bottle]] she glimpsed in the shallows of [[Lake Hylia]].<ref>{{Cite|This dress really is delightful, don't you think? It's more beautiful than even Lake Hylia. Which reminds me...last time I was on the east show of the lake, I saw something gleaming in the shallows.|Stylish Woman|ALBW}}</ref>

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, a woman greatly resembling the Stylish Woman appears inside [[Madame Couture]]'s, as one of [[Madame Couture|Couture]]'s last remaining customers after the [[Princess Styla|princess]]'s curse scared away all of her other customers.<ref>{{Cite|"Oh! The princess was cursed because she's so stylish! Rabble rabble!" POPPYCOCK! And this foolishness is actually affecting my business! Even now, only one person in my dressing room.|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> She flirtatiously comments on Link's [[Outfit]] whenever he emerges from the shop's dressing room.

==Trivia==
*The Stylish Woman's appearance in {{ALBW|-}} is very similar to the [[Cursed Fairy]] in {{ALttP}}.
*Using the Tornado Rod in her house will cause her to get angry at Link as it may ruin her dress.<ref>{{Cite|Please, don't! I don't want even one ruffle on my dress out of place!|Stylish Woman|ALBW}}</ref> Using it a second time will bother her even more.<ref>{{Cite|Now that is quite enough. You're going to ruin my hair.|Stylish Woman|ALBW}}</ref>
*One of the walls in her house features a lipstick kiss, which aligns perfectly with Link's mouth when he [[Wall Merging|Wall Merges]] behind it.
*In {{TFH|-}}, the Stylish Woman's model is altered so her ears no longer bear pointed tips. Despite this, her ears are still abnormally long compared to other humans in Hytopia.
