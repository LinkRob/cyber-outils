{{Term/Store|Hokkubokku|Hokkubokku|Series, ALttP, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 185 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Hokkubokku are segmented, cactus-like creatures that usually dwell in deserts, and move by alternating between bouncing around and burrowing under sand. When one of their segments is hit with [[Link]]'s [[sword]], that segment will break off and bounce around the room at a high speed. Hokkubokku are defeated altogether by attacking their head segment.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Hokkubokku appear in [[Turtle Rock]] in {{ALttP}}. Whenever Link attacks one, a segment will break off and hop about, before eventually vanishing. These segments can hurt Link, and along with the main body, can cause a large amount of damage. However, if Link is able to destroy a bouncing segment before it vanishes, it will often leave behind a [[Fairy]] or a big [[Magic Jar]]. As Hokkubokku lose their segments, they will move faster and bouncer further. The final head segment will not rebound around the room, and will take a couple of hits to finish off completely. Using the [[Fire Rod]] allows Link to destroy them completely in one hit.
{{Clear|left}}

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, a Hokkubokku appears in the [[Desert Palace]]. It initially appears under the sand, and dives in when Link gets too close, only to reappear somewhere else. The [[Sand Rod]] is needed to make it erupt from the ground, from where it is fought in the same manner as in {{ALttP|-}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, Hokkubokku have only two body segments as opposed to the three seen in earlier games, and they are fought in the last stage of the [[Infinity Dunes]] level. Initially only one is encountered, but three more appear after it has been defeated. Like its previous appearance, the segments will bounce off if they are attacked. The [[Links]] can reach and attack its head without striking the segments by forming a [[Totem]], or alternately by using the [[Water Rod]] to create a pillar of water. Once reduced to only a head, Hokkubokku will begin to flee the Links and try to burrow back into the sand. If they successfully escape, they will reemerge from the sand with newly-grown body segments.

A single Hokkubokku is also encountered in the Desert Zone of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}.
