"{{Term/Store|Free the Divine Beasts||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Free the Divine Beasts}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} was placed into the {{Term|BotW|Slumber of Restoration|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} left a message with {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} intended for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|After you fell, Princess Zelda's final wish was to place you in a sacred slumber. And then...all alone... Alone she went to face {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}}. Before Princess Zelda went to nobly meet her fate...she entrusted me with {{Color|BotW Red|some words}} she wished to say to you. I have been waiting 100 years to deliver the princess's message.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Impa}} refuses to pass along the message, however, unless {{Term|BotW|Link}} is prepared to risk his own life.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|However!}} These words, which the princess risked her life to leave you... Well, if you are to hear them... You must be prepared to risk your life as well.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She is unsure whether he would be able to bear such a burden without his {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link|display=Memories}},<ref>{{Cite|But I am afraid that burden may be too much to bear while you are still without your memories.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> so she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to let her know when he is ready to hear the message.<ref>{{Cite|I leave the choice to you. When you feel you are ready to receive the princess's message, return to me.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} informs {{Term|BotW|Impa}} that he is ready, she remarks that he is still as still as courageous as he was before his lost his {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memories}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I am.] Ha! Not a memory to your name, yet you are as intent as ever to charge forward with only courage and justice on your side. You have not changed a bit. Once a hero, always a hero.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> After recounting the history of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Since you have lost your memory, I will recount for you all that has happened...|Impa|BotW}}</ref> she finally shares {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} message: "Free the four {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}."<ref>{{Cite|In order to avoid ever making that grave mistake again, the princess left you these words... {{Color|BotW Red|Free the four Divine Beasts}}. That is what she said.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s confusion, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} explains that the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} are the ancient {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} weapons that were piloted by the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Four Divine Beasts?] The {{Color|BotW Red|four Divine Beasts}} are the ancient Sheikah weapons wielded by the four Champions who Ganon defeated.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> They are {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}, controlled by {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}} of the Gorons.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, controlled by {{Color|BotW Blue|Revali}} of the Rito.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, controlled by {{Color|BotW Blue|Mipha}} of the Zora.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And the {{Color|BotW Red|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}, controlled by {{Color|BotW Blue|Urbosa}} of the Gerudo.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Impa}} believes it would be foolish for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to face {{Term|BotW|Ganon}} without them,<ref>{{Cite|It would be extremely ill-advised to face {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}} without the power of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beasts}} to help you...|Impa|BotW}}</ref> and so he must infiltrate the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} to reclaim them.<ref>{{Cite|You must infiltrate the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beasts}} that were stolen away by {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}} 100 years ago and bring them back to our side.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

For more information, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} suggests he seek out the four races scattered across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|More information about these {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beasts}} can be found by locating the {{Color|BotW Red|four races}} scattered across Hyrule.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} opens, and four markers appear. {{Term|BotW|Impa}} explains that these are his guides to find the races, whose leaders he must meet with.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} will guide you on your way. You must go where it tells you and meet with each {{Color|BotW Red|leader}} there.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already calmed all four {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} will be able to sense their power.<ref>{{Cite|You... You have already found them. Yes, I can feel their power emanating from you even now...|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She remarks with amusement that she had no reason to worry.<ref>{{Cite|Ha! It would seem I had no cause to worry after all.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} will automatically be completed.}}

To complete this {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|display=Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must finish the following {{Plural|BotW|Main Quest}}:
* "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania (Quest)|link}}"
* "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}"
* "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|link}}"
* "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}"

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed all four {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|... {{Small|Link...}} Link.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She states that he has not only reclaimed the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}, but freed the spirits of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} as well.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, all of the Divine Beasts have returned to us and the spirits of the Champions are free.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Now, she and the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} are waiting for his confrontation with {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|We will be awaiting your clash with Ganon at Hyrule Castle.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

Back in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must inform {{Term|BotW|Impa}} of his victory.<ref>{{Cite|You've reclaimed the four {{Color|BotW Red|Divine Beasts}} by defeating the four Ganon phantoms haunting them. You should let Impa know of your success.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> She is able to sense the presence of the fallen {{Plural|BotW|Champions}},<ref>{{Cite|I sense it... Their presence. Daruk. Urbosa. Revali. Mipha.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> recalling that she risked her life to fight alongside them all.<ref>{{Cite|A hundred years ago, I put my life on the line to fight alongside everyone.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Back then, she was unable to protect them,<ref>{{Cite|But...I could not protect them.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> and so she has lived thinking that they all died in vain.<ref>{{Cite|They died without fulfilling their destinies. I have lived all this time thinking they died in vain.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> From their presence, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} can tell they have yet to give up,<ref>{{Cite|But this energy I feel from their presence... It seems they have not given up.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> as well as the fact that they were overjoyed to see {{Term|BotW|Link}} once again.<ref>{{Cite|I can also sense...that they were all overjoyed to see you again.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Impa}} encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} to serve their purpose.<ref>{{Cite|Now you all serve the same purpose!|Impa|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} is weakened, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must attack him and save {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now it is time to attack {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}}, while he is weak! Hurry to the princess! Go, now!|Impa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} opens again, this time showing a marker on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}. Even though he has the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be careful of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}'s defenses.<ref>{{Cite|Even with blessings of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beasts}} on your side, you must be careful. {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}} will be well protected. Be prepared for anything!|Impa|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} will be completed.
