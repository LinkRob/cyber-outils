The {{Term/Store|Swamp Tourist Center Guide||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Tourist Center Guide}} runs the [[Swamp Tourist Center]] in [[Southern Swamp]] and accepts [[Link]]'s entries for their {{Term|MM3D|Pictograph|link}} contest.<ref>{{Cite|Well, the Swamp Tourist Center is currently holding a pictograph contest. It's open to all ages, from children to adults. For nice pictographs taken in the swamp, a free boat cruise will be awarded as a special prize. So, would you like to enter our contest?|Swamp Tourist Center Guide|MM}}</ref> He is the father of [[Tingle]] and is apparently ashamed of his son's obsession with {{Plural|MM3D|Fairy|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Blimey! Where is he? That spoiled child is off playin' hooky again! A child his age has no business searching for fairies...!|Swamp Tourist Center Guide|MM}}</ref> If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} shows him a {{Term|MM3D|Pictograph}} of either {{Term|MM3D|Tingle}} or the {{Term|MM3D|Deku King|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Tourist Center Guide}} will reward the young [[Hero of Time]] with a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wha!!! That's my son! Eesh...Is he still doing that sort of thing? Urgh...How humiliating! This guy... He's my son... As old as he is, he's still doing some pretty silly stuff. I guess I spoiled him too much when I was raising him...But he really is a kindhearted boy. Well, thank you for going to all that trouble. This is just a small token of my gratitude!|Swamp Tourist Center Guide|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! Oh! The king! You are amazing! If you're not a Deku Scrub, you're not allowed to enter the Deku Palace, but somehow you took this picture... This is a great shot... This is as rare as they come! I'm giving you a big prize!|Swamp Tourist Center Guide|MM}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* In stark contrast to his son Tingle, who only stands about as tall as Link, the Swamp Tourist Center Guide is at the same height as the hero's mighty [[Fierce Deity Link|Fierce Deity]] form and therefore [[Ganondorf]], making himself one of the largest humans in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]].
* He shares the same model as the [[Bazaar]] shop owner from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' and the [[Swamp Shooting Gallery Guy]].
