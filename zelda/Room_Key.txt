The {{Term/Store|Room Key||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Room Key is an item which is received during the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest. Obtaining the Room Key is not necessary to reunite the two lovers, however, and the quest can be completed without it. This key allows [[Link]] to go in and out of the [[Stock Pot Inn]] before and after the inn closes without having to resort to using the unlocked door on the deck of the second floor. More importantly, however, it grants him access to the [[Stock Pot Inn#Overview|Knife Chamber]], one of the two rooms available for rent on the second floor at the Stock Pot Inn.<ref name=quote1>{{Cite|Mr. Link, I have you down for an afternoon arrival. Your room is our "Knife Chamber" on the second floor. Here is your key.|Anju|MM}}</ref>

Link receives the Room Key as a mistake due to [[Anju]]'s tendency to forget customer's faces.<ref>{{Cite|My mother is always telling me to remember our customer's faces, but I tend to forget them...|Anju|MM}}</ref> When Link talks to her between 1:50 PM and 4:00 PM during the [[First Day]], Anju will ask him if he has made a reservation.<ref>{{Cite|Umm...Did you...have a reservation?|Anju|MM}}</ref> If Link says yes, she will hand him the key to the Knife Chamber.<ref name=quote1/> The reservation was actually made for a [[Goron]] who just happened to share the same name as the young hero; [[Link (Goron)|Link]].<ref>{{Cite|I made a reservation... The name is Link-goro!|Link|MM}}</ref> Since Link has the room key, Link (the Goron) will not be able to claim his reservation and will now have to sleep outside.<ref>{{Cite|What?!? Really-goro? Well, it's nice weather, so I'll just sleep outside-goro.|Link|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, Anju remembers that the Knife Chamber was reserved by a Goron. As a result, to receive the Room Key, Link must speak to her while wearing the [[Goron Mask]] so that Anju will mistake the young hero for the Goron who shares his name.

==Trivia==
*The Room Key will automatically disappear from Link's inventory on the night of the [[Final Day]]. At that point, the Stock Pot Inn is left unlocked for the rest of the cycle because all the employees have evacuated.
**In {{MM3D|-}}, the Room Key will remain in Link's Gear screen on the night of the Final Day even though it is no longer needed.
