{{Term/Store|Freebie|Freebies|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 124"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Freebie}} are the sole {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Dapper Spinner|link}} {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 124"/> They can only be obtained by opening a non-winning {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}} in the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}} {{Term|TFH|Mini-Game|link}}. After five {{Plural|TFH|Freebie}} have been collected, the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches Owner|link}} will reward {{Term|TFH|Link|link}}'s bad luck by ordering the {{Term|TFH|Dapper Spinner}} for purchase at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You lost FIVE times... Impressive! I have a special gift to acknowledge such spectacularly bad luck. In anticipation of such an occasion, I've ordered a special outfit from the one and only Madame. She calls it... The Dapper Spinner!|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} costs ten {{Plural|TFH|Freebie}} (and 300 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}) in total.

{{Plural|TFH|Freebie}} can be sold to the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 50 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} apiece.

==Trivia==
* Due to the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches}} schedule being tied to the internal clock of the [[Nintendo 3DS]], collecting enough {{Plural|TFH|Freebie}} to afford the {{Term|TFH|Dapper Spinner}} requires at least twelve real-life days.
