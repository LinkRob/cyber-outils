"{{Term/Store|Sunshroom Sensing||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sunshroom Sensing}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} upgrades the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Slated for Upgrades|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Symin|link}} will ask to see it.<ref>{{Cite|Oooh! The {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Sensor +}}! I reaaally want to see it...|Symin|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Symin}} notes how useful it should be.<ref>{{Cite|You've acquired the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Sensor +}}! That should really come in handy.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> He explains that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can now use the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor}} to sense {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} that he has taken a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of with his {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|With that, you can sense things that you've captured with your {{Color|BotW Blue|Camera}}.|Symin|BotW}}</ref>

To demonstrate how the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor}} works, {{Term|BotW|Symin}} offers to help try it out.<ref>{{Cite|Explaining won't do it justice—why not learn by trying? Let's give it a shot, so to speak.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of a {{Term|BotW|Sunshroom|link}} and there is one behind the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see here. First, go take a picture of a {{Color|BotW Blue|sunshroom}} with the {{Color|BotW Blue|Camera}}. There is one {{Color|BotW Red|behind}} the lab.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has photographed a {{Term|BotW|Sunshroom}} and registered it to his {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Symin}}.<ref>{{Cite|It seems like you got a picture of a {{Color|BotW Blue|sunshroom}}! Excellent.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> He instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to open the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|display=Compendium}} to set the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor}} to find {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom}}.<ref>{{Cite|Next, open your {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Compendium}} and tune the sensor to your {{Color|BotW Blue|picture}} of the {{Color|BotW Blue|sunshroom}}. The {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Sensor +}} will then be able to sense {{Color|BotW Blue|sunshrooms}}!|Symin|BotW}}</ref> For more detailed steps, he directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor Notes|link}}, which are located on the desk nearby.<ref>{{Cite|The detailed instructions are written down in the {{Color|BotW Blue|book}} on the desk there. You should read them.|Symin|BotW}}</ref>

Taking advantage of this opportunity, {{Term|BotW|Symin}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find three {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom}} for him.<ref>{{Cite|This seems like a nice opportunity to ask if you'd mind bringing me {{Color|BotW Red|three sunshrooms}}.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> He suggests searching for them in {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest|link}} using the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can find {{Color|BotW Red|sunshrooms}} growing in {{Color|BotW Red|Retsam Forest}}, I believe... The {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Sensor +}} should make it a snap!|Symin|BotW}}</ref> Once again, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Symin}} after collecting three {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hello. I see you've brought the {{Color|BotW Blue|sunshrooms}}.|Symin|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses to hand over the {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom}},<ref>{{Cite|OK, now please hand over those {{Color|BotW Red|three}} {{Color|BotW Blue|sunshrooms}.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Symin}} will be thankful and reward him with three {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you. Here you go.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> He asks if the upgraded {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor +}} was to his liking.<ref>{{Cite|So? Was the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Sensor +}} to your liking?|Symin|BotW}}</ref> Suddenly, {{Term|BotW|Symin}} remembers that there was something else he wanted to discuss with {{Term|BotW|Link}}, inviting him to come back when he has the time.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yes... I just remembered there was something else I wanted to talk to you about. Stop by when you have time to talk.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
