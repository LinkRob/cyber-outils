{{Term/Store|Veggie Cream Soup|Veggie Cream Soups|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Veggie Cream Soup}}<br/>This creamy soup showcases the sweetness of vegetables in a veritable taste explosion.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=cook}} Veggie Cream Soups by combining either a {{Term|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}} or any {{Term|BotW|Carrot|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Fresh Milk|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made by using multiple vegetables with contradicting effects. Eating them will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These soups are known to showcase the sweetness of vegetables.<ref name="Inventory"/>

When Link speaks to {{Term|BotW|Koko|link}} at her Cooking Pot for the first time, she will explain that she wants to make Veggie Cream Soup for dinner, since she hears it's nourishing and fortifying.<ref>{{Cite|Today's dinner is {{Color|BotW Blue|veggie cream soup}}! It's super nourishing and fortifying! Well, that's what they say, anyhow. It must be true!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She explains the recipe involves stewing a {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, Rock Salt, and Fresh Milk in a pot.<ref>{{Cite|All you do is toss a {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrot}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|rock salt}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|fresh milk}} in a pot and stew it up. Then, voilà! So yummy!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> However, Koko realizes that she doesn't have any Swift Carrots, and laments the fact that she promised Veggie Cream Soup to her sister {{Term|BotW|Cottla|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|...OH, NO!}} Koko is all out of {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrots}}! But...Koko promised to make Cottla some {{Color|BotW Blue|hasty veggie cream soup}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Link can offer to bring her some,<ref>{{Cite|[I'll bring you some.] Thank you! So much! OK, Koko just needs one {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrot}}! Just one and Koko can get cookin'! Koko will be waiting for you here!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> which initiates the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Koko's Kitchen}}". If Link brings Koko a Swift Carrot, Koko will be thankful and proceeds to make Veggie Cream Soup.<ref>{{Cite|[This is for you.] {{Big|Is that...a {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrot}}?!}} Thank you so much! So very much! Now Koko can make dinner for Cottla... Yeah! Time to get cookin'!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she'll give Link Hasty Veggie Cream Soup.<ref>{{Cite|Ooooh, it came out perfect! Here, please have some! Koko's treat!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

Three different variations of Veggie Cream Soup can be made, depending on the type of vegetable used:

* '''Enduring Veggie Cream Soup''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Veggie Cream Soup}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This creamy soup showcases the sweetness of vegetables in a veritable taste explosion.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}. When eaten, these soups will completely restore and overfill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Veggie Cream Soup''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Veggie Cream Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This creamy soup showcases the sweetness of vegetables in a veritable taste explosion.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using Swift Carrots. They can be eaten to provide a boost to Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Tough Veggie Cream Soup''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Veggie Cream Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This creamy soup showcases the sweetness of vegetables in a veritable taste explosion.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using Fortified Pumpkins. If eaten, they'll increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>
