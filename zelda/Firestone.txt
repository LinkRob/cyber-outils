'''Firestones''' are items in {{TFoE}}.{{Name Ref|TFoE}}

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFoE|Firestone}} can be dropped by fire-based enemies, such as {{Plural|TFoE|Fire Daira|link}} and {{Plural|TFoE|Phyrandaii|link}}, after they have been defeated. They can be found in {{Term|TFoE|Crater Cove|link}} and {{Term|TFoE|Firestone Lake|link}}. {{Plural|TFoE|Firestone}} are thrown at ice-based enemies to inflict damage and to defeat them, such as {{Plural|TFoE|Abominom|link}}, as other attacks will have no effect on them. The item has a similar effect to the {{Term|TFoE|Snowball}}, which is also thrown to defeat the fire-based enemies that drop {{Plural|TFoE|Firestone}}. {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} can carry up to 99 of them.
