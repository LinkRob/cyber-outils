'''Kiss'n Tell''' is the fourth episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. It was first broadcast in the United States and Canada on September 29, 1989.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[Kiss'n Tell/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
[[File:Gibdo Link (Kiss'n Tell).png|thumb|left|Link turns into a frog]]
The scene starts off with {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} riding through the woods, when suddenly she sees a maiden being attacked by a three-headed {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gleeok|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=And maybe an adventure. Link will be sorry he missed out. REAL sorry. Beautiful girls in trouble are what he LIVES for.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Zelda uses fruit to battle them and, when they are stunned for a few moments, the princess sees a chance to go but the maiden refuses, saying she wants to be rescued by a handsome hero.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No! I want to be rescued by a handsome hero!|name=Maiden|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Soon after, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} appears and battles against the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gleeok}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Somebody call me?|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> After a few minutes of fighting and with some help from Zelda, the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gleeok}} is defeated and the maiden offers {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} a kiss as a reward,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, you saved me, my handsome hero! Please let me give you a kiss to show my gratitude!|name=Maiden|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> which the young hero gladly accepts and tells Zelda that she could learn something from the strange maiden.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Heh, now THAT'S what I like to hear! Pay attention, princess. You could learn something.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> As their lips meet, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} turns into a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Frog Link|display=frog|link}} and the maiden turns to her true form: a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gibdo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=What's the matter, Link? You don't like my charms anymore? You fell for it! Ganon's plan worked perfectly!|name=Gibdo|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> When {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} reaches for his sword, he realizes that his hands have turned frog-like and is too distracted to see that the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gibdo}} is about to attack him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=My hands! They're all weird!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> However, Zelda pushes him out of the way and easily defeats the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gibdo}} by spinning it until it disappears back to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}}'s {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Evil Jar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Wait 'till you see the rest of you! Let's go for a spin, Gibdo!|episode=4}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}} congratulates the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gleeok}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gibdo}} for their success and leaves the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}} to proceed with his plan of capturing Zelda and asking for the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} in exchange of the princess.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Excellent. You've done well, my servants. Link has been transformed into a frog! And with Link rendered practically useless, Zelda will be at my mercy! Now I shall put the rest of my plan into action. When I return, Zelda will be with me and Link will be gone...forever!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> 

Back in the woods, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} looks into the water and realises his frog form. He starts to cry and Zelda hugs him, saying she'll be his friend no matter what.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I've seen worse! Hey, you still have me. I'll always be your friend.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Suddenly, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} appears and attacks them with his magic.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Aw...how sickenly sweet. Enough mush... I've waited long enough for this moment!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> He stuns {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, who is helpless to save Zelda from being captured by an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Octorok|link}}. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} summons two {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Deeler|link}} to get rid of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} while he kidnaps Zelda and takes her to the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Of course not! You, take care of him! And you, my dear, are coming with me!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Just a few minutes too late, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} wakes up and eats the two {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Deeler}}, but is then disgusted by what he has done.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Yum. Ah! What did I just do? I ate two red Deelers. Yucko!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> He then travels back to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|North Castle|link}}, meeting with {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Spryte, it's me. No, don't look at me!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> He reveals himself as a frog and tells her about Zelda being captured while asking her for help.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ganon put a spell on me. Prepare yourself for a shock... [...] And Ganon's got Zelda! Will you help me, Spryte?|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Just then, the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom}} gives {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} advice in from of a riddle, which tells him to look for the wise one known as the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Witch of Walls|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=When one's in need of sage advice, one must seek out the one who's wise. The witch of walls will help you best. To find her you must make a quest. Seek the wall that has no end, beneath the bow that cannot bend.|name=Triforce of Wisdom|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Together, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte}} head out to look for the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Witch of Walls}}, who they find inside of a wall that has no end, next to an ironwood tree. She tells {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} that the only way to become human again is to be kissed by a princess of her own free will.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The cure is simple. You must be kissed by a princess of her own free will.|name=Witch of Walls|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> 

Following the witch's advice, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} goes to the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=C'mon! We gotta find where he's keeping Zelda.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> He eventually finds Zelda and sets her free from the handcuffs that held her. The young hero then tells about how he can change back to a human,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You have to kiss me. Please? A princess's kiss is the only cure.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> and Zelda, although hesitant, agrees to do it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Well...if it's the only way... Well, then of course I will.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> However, before Zelda can kiss him, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} zaps them and knocks {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} unconscious.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Sorry, I like him the way he is. And you, princess, are still my prisoner!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte}}, who traveled with {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} to the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}}, feels sorry for the young hero and kisses him for having such a hard day.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, Link, you poor thing. You've had such a hard day.|name=Spryte|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte}}'s kiss causes {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} to transform back into a human,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hah! I'm me again! Take this, Ganon!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> who quickly overpowers {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} and causes him to fall through the floor. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda then take the opportunity to escape back into the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld|link}}, leaving the evil wizard behind.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=He'll teleport back as soon as he gets his wits together. We gotta get out of here!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> 

Once outside, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte}} explains her kiss worked because her father is the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Oberon|display=king of fairies|link}}, making her the princess.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Naturally. My father was the king of the fairies, you know. That makes ME the princess.|name=Spryte|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Zelda thanks {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and offers him a kiss as a reward.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I'm glad, too. And thanks for rescuing me. You still want a kiss?|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} takes the offer but ruins the mood when he sees a fly and eats it as an old habit, causing Zelda to change her mind.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Agh, never mind!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Aw, c'mon, Zelda! I didn't mean to! Old habits die hard.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref>
