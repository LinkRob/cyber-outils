{{Term/Store|Zeffa||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Zeffa}} is a blue {{Term|TMC|Bird|link}} that swoops down and {{Term|TMC|Warping|link|display=Warps}} {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to any one of the activated {{Plural|TMC|Wind Crest|link}} scattered across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you play the {{Color|TMC Red|Ocarina of Wind}}, Zeffa comes to pick you up and fly you away. That means we can have Zeffa carry us to any other {{Color|TMC Blue|Wind Crest}} we've seen.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} can summon {{Term|TMC|Zeffa}} by using the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}, which is obtained after defeating {{Term|TMC|Mazaal|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can avoid being {{Term|TMC|Warping|link|display=Warped}} by {{Term|TMC|Zeffa}} if he moves out of the way of {{Term|TMC|Zeffa}}'s swoop after calling for it using the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}.
*A number of blue {{Plural|TMC|Bird|link}} identical in appearance to {{Term|TMC|Zeffa}} can be seen at various locations across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}; first at the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, perched atop the {{Term|TMC|Wind Crest|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}; another at the roof of the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}; and another at the roof of the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}}.

==Nomenclature== 
{{Term|Series|Zeffa}} may come from {{Romanize|ゼッファー|Zeffā}}, a transliteration of the English word "Zephyr", meaning a gentle breeze or west wind.
