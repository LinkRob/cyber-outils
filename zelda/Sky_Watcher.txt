{{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}}, along with the similar {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher|link}}, are the two variations of {{Term|SSHD|Watcher|link}} that protect the {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm}}. Unlike the {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Guardian|link}}, the {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}} are not affected by the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear|link}}. The {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}} fly through the {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm}} in a set path while shining a beam of light from the lanterns they carry. If {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} touches this light, the {{Term|SSHD|Sky Watcher}} will emit a loud shriek that will awaken and alert the {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Guardian}} to {{Term|SSHD|Link}}s' location, removing the effects of the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear}} no matter how much time {{Term|SSHD|Link}} has remaining.

{{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}} are very similar to {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}}, but unlike the {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}}, {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}} will not chase {{Term|SSHD|Link}} if he gets close to one. While {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} patrol the ground and float near it, {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}} patrol the sky and fly higher up.
