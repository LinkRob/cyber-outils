'''Jacks''' are obstacles in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
A Jack consists of four red circles circling around a white one. They are invulnerable obstacles found only in [[Shrine of Destiny]], and their movements involve bouncing around an area diagonally. There is one room where a Jack appears every time a [[Gibdo]] is defeated.
