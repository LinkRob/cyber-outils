The {{Term/Store|First Gen Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|First Gen Ring<br/>Become something|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoA}}

==Location and Uses==
The First Gen Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it transforms [[Link]] into his 8-bit version from {{TLoZ}}. While in this shape, Link cannot use items nor can be eaten by [[Like Like]]s. The First Gen Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
