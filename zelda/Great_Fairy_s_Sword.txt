The {{Term/Store|Great Fairy's Sword||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a powerful recurring item and unique [[Sword]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|Soulcalibur II, HW}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
[[Link]] can obtain the Great Fairy's Sword by collecting all 15 {{Plural|MM3D|Stray Fairy|link}} in the [[Stone Tower Temple]] and returning them to the {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} in [[Ikana Canyon]]. Doing so will return the {{sec|Great Fairy|Great Fairy of Kindness}} back to normal, who will thank Link and give him the Sword as a reward for his help.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, compassionate young one! I am the Great Fairy of Kindness. Thank you for returning my broken and shattered body to normal. As thanks, I grant you the Fairy's Sword.|Great Fairy of Kindness|MM}}</ref>

Unlike the other Swords in {{MM|-}} - the [[Kokiri Sword]], [[Razor Sword]], and [[Gilded Sword]], the Great Fairy's Sword is equipped to the {{sec|Controller Buttons|C Buttons/C Stick/C Button/Right Analog Stick|C Buttons}} as an item rather than the standard {{sec|Controller Buttons|B Button}}, which is an unusual feature among {{TLoZ|-}} series for this type of weapon.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Great Fairy's Sword! Set it to (C) to use it.|N/A|MM}}</ref> Being a large, double-handed Sword, much like the [[Biggoron's Sword]] in {{OoT}}, it cannot be used in concurrence with a [[Shield]]. If the shield button is pressed, the young [[Hero of Time]] will crouch, like the [[Deku Stick]]. To counter this disadvantage, it deals four times as much damage as the [[Kokiri Sword]], making it the most powerful Sword in the game,<ref>{{Cite|With black roses etched into it, the Great Fairy's Sword is the most powerful of all blades.|N/A|MM}}</ref> alongside with the [[Fierce Deity Sword]] wielded by [[Fierce Deity Link]]. Moreover, Link can use the Great Fairy's Sword even if he is [[jinx]]ed by a {{sec|Bubble|Blue Bubble}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{SCII|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{SCII}}, the Great Fairy's Sword referred to as the '''Great Fairy Sword''', appears as one of Link's available weapons. It serves as his Ultimate Weapon,<ref>{{Cite|The ultimate sword! Wield it to discover its true power!|N/A|Soulcalibur II}}</ref> and can be purchased from the Cepheus Shop along with the [[Hylian Shield]] for 9800 Gold while playing the Weapon Master Mode. When equipped, it increases Link's attack by 20% but also causes him to take 20% block damage compared to the usual 10%. This penetrable guard is countered by the fact that the sword will cause Link's health to regenerate by 5 HP every second. The blade is longer than the [[Master Sword]], but shorter than the Biggoron's Sword.

== Trivia ==

* The Great Fairy's Sword (alongside the [[Fierce Deity Sword]]) is coincidentally as powerful as the [[Biggoron's Sword]] from the game's prequel, as both mighty blades deal four times the strength of the [[Kokiri Sword]].
* Like the Biggoron's Sword or [[Megaton Hammer]] in {{OoT}}, as well as the [[Deku Stick]] in either game, if [[Link]] were to wield the Great Fairy's Sword during cutscenes or in his idle animations, he would carry the colorful blade in only one hand.
* It is possible to perform a [[Great Spin|Super Spin Attack]] with the Great Fairy's Sword. When the C-button is pressed, immediately hold down the B button, If done correctly, Link will be in the stance position, allowing the large blade to charge.
* Similar to the Biggoron's Sword in {{OoT|-}}, the Great Fairy's Sword is commonly used by players in {{MM}} or its [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|remake]] who aim for [[100% Completion|100% completion]] or [[Speedrun|speedruns]].
* According to its weapon description in ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', the likely reason why the Great Fairy's Sword had lost its protective power is due to the fact that [[Termina]] ceased to exist after the events of {{MM|-}} and Link is also in the ''Soul'' universe, despite preserving the weapon. It is also claimed to be the ultimate sword in-game.
** Although this crossover fighting game by Namco is not canon to the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''Zelda'' franchise]], it is also depicted as stronger than the [[Master Sword]] in ''SoulCalibur II''.
