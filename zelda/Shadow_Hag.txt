The {{Term/Store|Shadow Hag||Series, OoA}} is the {{Term|OoA|Boss}} of {{Term|OoA|Moonlit Grotto|link}}, the third {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 79}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Term|OoA|Shadow Hag}} is a {{Term|Series|Poe|link}}-like creature who has the ability to transform into shadows. It begins the battle by splitting into four shadows which encircle the room, harming {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} on contact. Eventually the shadows converge into one and numerous {{Plural|OoA|Moth|link}} arise from it, which {{Term|OoA|Link}} can defeat for {{Plural|OoA|Heart|link}} and {{Plural|OoA|Mystical Seed|link}}. While {{Term|OoA|Link}} deals with the {{Plural|OoA|Moth}}, the {{Term|OoA|Shadow Hag}} emerges from the shadow to charge at him, but will dive back into the shadow if {{Term|OoA|Link}} sees it coming. To hurt the {{Term|OoA|Shadow Hag}}, {{Term|OoA|Link}} must face away from it and use the {{Term|OoA|Seed Shooter|link}} to fire a {{Term|OoA|Mystical Seed|display=Seed}} that rebounds off a wall and hits the {{Term|OoA|Boss}} as it charges. After taking enough damage, the {{Term|OoA|Shadow Hag}} will be defeated, leaving behind a {{Term|OoA|Heart Container|link}} and opening the door to the {{Term|OoA|Echoing Howl|link}}.
