The '''Witch of Walls''' is a character in {{TLoZ|TV Series}}.{{Name Ref}} She appears briefly in the "[[Kiss'n Tell]]" episode but plays a crucial part in the plot.

==Biography==
After [[Link]] gets turned into a [[Frog Link|frog]] by one of [[Ganon]]'s spells, the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] advises Link to look for the Witch of Walls near an endless wall that is beneath a tree that cannot bend.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=When one's in need of sage advice, one must seek out the one who's wise. The witch of walls will help you best. To find her you must make a quest. Seek the wall that has no end, beneath the bow that cannot bend.|name=Triforce of Wisdom|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Together, Link and [[Spryte]] head out to look for the witch, who, fitting to her name, comes out of a diamond wall after the young hero calls her name.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That's it! Now all I have to do is find that Witch of Walls!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> When she inspects Link, the witch tells him that the only way to become human again is to be kissed by a princess of her own free will.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The cure is simple. You must be kissed by a princess of her own free will.|name=Witch of Walls|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> The Witch of Walls then returns to her wall as Link and Spryte go and find [[Princess Zelda]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I said the cure was simple, I didn't say it was easy. Now begone with you! I must return to my wall...|name=Witch of Walls|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref>
