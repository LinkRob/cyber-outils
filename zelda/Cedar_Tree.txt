==Features and Overview==
{{Plural|BotW|Cedar Tree}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} found in northern {{Plural|BotW|Region|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Hebra|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}. They can also be found in areas with colder temperatures, such as {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mount Hylia|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Cedar Tree}} are the most common type of {{Term|BotW|Tree}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Cedar Tree}} are taller than the standard {{Plural|BotW|Tree}}. When they are cut down, they produce more {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}} as well. Several {{Plural|BotW|Cedar Tree}} have {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}} on top of them. Interacting with it causes a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} to appear and give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
