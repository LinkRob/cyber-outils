==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|HWAoC|Training Sword}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}} used by {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}}. It is exclusively available as a save data link bonus and can be obtained if save data exists for {{BotW}}. It is paired with a {{Term|HWAoC|Pot Lid|link}} and a [[Wooden Bow]]. The Training Sword has a power of 18, and gives Link a 10% chance to automatically guard.
