{{Term/Store|Iron Ship|Iron Ships|Series, PH, HWL, HWDE}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 284">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 284}}</ref><ref name="HWDE Tutorial"/>{{Note|In {{PH}}, this term refers only to a specific combination of {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}. In-game, the ship itself is always called the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}}.}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, the {{Term|PH|Iron Ship}} is the name given to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} once it has been outfitted with the following {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Iron Ship}}

The {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} can be attached to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} at any time by visiting the {{Term|PH|Shipyard|link}} on {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}. For every three parts of the {{Term|PH|Iron Ship}} used, the durability of the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} is increased by one Heart. When all eight parts are used together, they increase its durability by three Hearts, up to a total of seven Hearts. Aside from this boost in durability, the {{Term|PH|Iron Ship}} is merely a change in appearance to suit the player's personal tastes.

In {{HW|L}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Iron Ship}} appear as an {{Term|HWDE|Item Card|link}} on the {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map|link}} in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}. The {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map}} was not included with the {{Term|HW|Phantom Hourglass & Spirit Tracks Pack|link}} for the original {{HW}}, so {{Plural|HWDE|Iron Ship}} weren't introduced until the {{Term|HWL|Phantom Hourglass & Spirit Tracks Pack}} released for {{HW|L|-}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Phantom Hourglass & Spirit Tracks Pack}}<br/>This pack includes:<br/>New playable character: Toon Zelda<br/>New weapon: Sand Wand for Toon Link|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= November 30, 2021|url= https://www.zelda.com/hyrule-warriors/wiiu/dlc/|title= Expand your adventure with dlc packs|site= Zelda.com|type= }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= A new map for Adventure Mode|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= November 30, 2021|url= http://web.archive.org/web/20160904032504/http://www.zelda.com/hyrule-warriors-legends/#buynow/1|title= Individual Packs|site= Zelda.com|type= web archive}}</ref> Because {{HW|DE}} came with all previous DLC packs included, {{Plural|HWDE|Iron Ship}} are available from the moment {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode}} is unlocked.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This new version brings every map, character, and mission from both the Wii U game and the Nintendo 3DS game together, including all the original premium downloadable content.|author= Nintendo|published= January 11, 2018|retrieved= January 11, 2018|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_u7g5BlfiY|title= Nintendo Direct Mini 1.11.2018|site= Youtube.com|type= video}}</ref>
