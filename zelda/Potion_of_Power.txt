The '''Potion of Power''' is an item featured in the "{{Term|CN|Quest for the Potion of Power|link}}" episode of {{CN}}. As its name implies, it is a magical {{Term|CN|Potion|link}} that is capable of reviving {{Term|CN|Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Only one: that they're searching for a magic potion that will restore his power. WE'VE gotta find it first.|name=Princess Zelda|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref> [[mew:Mother Brain|Mother Brain]] commands her two minions, Eggplant Wizard and King Hippo, with the task of following {{Term|CN|Link|link}}, {{Term|CN|Princess Zelda|link}}, and Kevin so that they will lead them to where the {{Term|CN|Potion of Power}} is located: the {{Term|CN|Island Palace|link}}.<Ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Nevermind! Just get over to Rauru Town and follow Captain N. Get me that potion, or your names are 'Mud'!|name=Mother Brain|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref>

Once the heroes reach the {{Term|CN|Island Palace}}, {{Term|CN|Link}} uses the {{Term|CN|Golden Key|link}} to open the Golden Door which leads them to the chamber of the Potion of Power.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=See? I found it. The Golden Door.|name=Link|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref> However, they are soon confronted by an {{Term|CN|Rebonakku|display=Iron Knuckle|link}}, who is actually the guardian of the {{Term|CN|Potion of Power}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I am the guardian of the Potion of Power!|name=Iron Knuckle|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref> While {{Term|CN|Link}} and Kevin are fighting the armored foe, Eggplant Wizard and King Hippo manage to steal the {{Term|CN|Potion of Power}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Link, Kevin! They're stealing the Potion of Power!|name=Princess Zelda|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref> The minions eventually reach {{Term|CN|Ganon}}, giving him the potion to drink it and thus allow the evil wizard to get all of his power back.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No one can withstand the power of Ganon. Videoland is ''mine!''|name=Ganon|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref>
