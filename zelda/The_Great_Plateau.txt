==Strategy==

The objective of this map is to have all allied units survive. It may sound simple but there are ones that cannot be reached until a specific condition is met.
===Taking the Outposts===
Start by having a unit destroy an outpost. [[fireemblem:Order|Order]] other units to destroy other outpost. The bottom-right most outpost can take long for a computer-controlled unit to get there. After you done cleansing the outpost, head towards the stairway to the temple. Some Guardians will be hold in the outposts, so after defeating them, head to the the temple stairway.
===Ambush===
The moment after you finish destroying the outposts, two [[Hinox]]es will spawn after the steps. Defeating them will cause a [[Silver Lynel]] to appear which is more tougher than the Hinoxes since one weak-point trigger will only remove 1/32 health bar. It may you longer to defeat the Silver Lynel you can take vantage of this by dodging and doing rushhing blows and deplete the weak-point gauge much faster. Once it has been done. a cutscene will play at the [[Sheikah Tower]]. Go near the footsteps of the Tower. Note that you don't have to deploy all units to get up there, one strong unit for handling the Guardian and the [[Silver Moblin|Moblin]] is the least enough.
===Shrine of Resurrection & More Guardians===
Start the second half at the Shrine of Resurrection then down to the hills and take the right path not the left path, as going left leads to a dead end. Handle the Guardian in the first outpost. The final ally unit in trouble is the one inside the temple. All you need is to guide down the narrow path to the temple. In Breath of the Wild, the entrance to the temple has no blockings, however to access here you must find the "secret" entrance to the temple which is steps to the right from the entrance view(take the left path). Deal the final main enemy afterward. Shortly after, guardians will spawn in the bottom segment of the map. You must make your way back to the shrine. Order your units to deal them for a while or switch units to deal them faster.
