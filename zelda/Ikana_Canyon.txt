{{Term/Store|Ikana Canyon||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 110 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> It is the eastern area of [[Termina]]. It is known to be a cursed land where the [[Ikana Kingdom]] once existed, being home to the dead and filled with spirits who linger with regret and hatred.<ref>{{Cite|Eee-hee-hee... The Ikana Kingdom was founded on this land, stained with a history of darkness, drenched in blood... Even now it is a place where troubled, regretful spirits gather.|Poe Collector|MM3D}}</ref> The [[Garo|Garo Ninja]]s can also be found here spying on the deserted land.

==Locations and Features==
[[File:MM Ikana River.png|thumb|left|200px|Ikana Canyon's lower cliffs, known as Ikana Valley]]
Like [[Great Bay Coast]], the [[Road to Ikana]] is blocked by fences that can only be jumped over using [[Epona]]. To get to Ikana Canyon, [[Link]] must show the [[Garo's Mask]] or the [[Gibdo Mask]] to the [[Poe Collector]].<ref>{{Cite|That is the mask of the leader of the ninjas who once spied on the hilltop castle with the blood-stained history. With that, you may be able to call out their spirits that even now are still wandering. Perhaps you may be able to save the souls that wander beyond here... Yee-hee-hee.|Poe Collector|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yee-hee-hee. That's a nice mask you're wearing. That is the mask of those who roam this world with the most lingering {{Color|MM3D Red|regrets}}! Use it to ask them their regrets... Perhaps you may be able to save the souls that wander beyond here. Yee-hee-hee!|Poe Collector|MM3D}}</ref> By doing so, the man will make a tree appear beside him, allowing Link to [[Hookshot]] up to it and continue on his way. Once further inside the Canyon, Link must make use of his [[Ice Arrow]]s to cross the [[Octorok]]-infested river and Hookshot his way up to the main part of the Ikana Canyon.<ref>{{Cite|To cross the valley of Ikana, aim at the river beasts with the arrowhead that {{Color|MM3D Red|freezes}}.|Garo|MM3D}}</ref>

Ikana Canyon is notable for having more than twice as many boss battles (mini-boss and optional included) than any other place in the rest of {{MM|-}}, totaling twenty-six if each boss is counted as an individual in a battle. During his stay in this region, Link will have to visit most of the landmarks in order to finally bring peace and calm the lingering spirits that fill Ikana Canyon with such a history of greed and hatred.

===Ancient Castle of Ikana===
{{Main|Ancient Castle of Ikana}}
Long forgotten by the world, like much of the rest of Ikana Canyon, the Castle has crumbled into ancient ruins, populated by nasty traps, evil monsters, and lost souls seeking revenge on the world. At the end of the Castle, the [[Igos du Ikana|King of Ikana]] and his two [[King's Lackeys|Lackeys]] await a savior to bring Ikana out of its curse.

===Beneath the Well===
{{Main|Beneath the Well}}
Ikana's Well features a network of water channels and chambers that has been long-dried up from the evil powers of Ikana's undead. Banned for entry by Pamela's father, due to "ghost sightings,"<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Empty Well}}<br/>Entry prohibited due to ghost sightings!|Sign|MM3D}}</ref> the Well has become the home of a Gibdo community that await certain items for which they will trade passage into the next room.<ref>{{Cite|As the Gibdos in Ikana's well have regrets and long to savor {{Color|MM3D Red|items}} from this world, they continue to wander about.|Garo|MM3D}}</ref> Once Link has escaped from the Well, he can obtain the [[Mirror Shield]] and journey into [[Ikana Castle]].

===Ikana Graveyard===
{{Main|Ikana Graveyard}}
Ikana Graveyard is an eerie burial ground kept by [[Dampé]] the grave keeper who patrols by day and hides by night,<ref>{{Cite|I am Dampé, the grave keeper who watches over this graveyard.|Dampé|MM3D}}</ref> claiming he is frightened by ghosts.<ref>{{Cite|Aaaaieeee!!! Th-they're out! Why are they out when it's not even night?|Dampé|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Even nowadays, the {{Color|MM3D Red|ghosts}} come out at night. It is a frightening graveyard.|Dampé|MM3D}}</ref> As night falls upon the canyon, the [[Stalchild|undead soldiers]] of Ikana awaken and guard the graves.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|It's a}} Stalchild{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|. They were the cursed soldiers of the Kingdom of Ikana... What are you gonna do?}}|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|As per your orders, sir, we've been guarding these graves closely, a different one each day. We have all been expecting your next order, sir.|Stalchild|MM3D}}</ref> Here, Link can obtain the [[Captain's Hat]] by defeating the soldiers' gigantic captain, [[Captain Keeta]], who rests under an arch near Dampé's house.<ref>{{Cite|Did you see the huge skeleton at the back of this graveyard? My father once said that was all that was left of the former Royal Soldier who once guarded this graveyard. Even though he is dead, his remains still block the way. In a sense, he is still guarding the {{Color|MM3D Red|Royal Family's treasure}}. He must have been a fine soldier, indeed.|Dampé|MM3D}}</ref> On each night of the three day cycle, the soldiers open a new grave which feature a small dungeon within, the first being a tomb guarded by an [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Iron Knuckle]] where Link learns the "[[Song of Storms]]".<ref>{{Cite|The melody that summons the tears of angels rests here.|Tombstone|MM}}</ref> The second grave contains an Iron Knuckle and a [[Piece of Heart]],<ref>{{Cite|A piece that heals the wounded soul rests here.|Tombstone|MM}}</ref> and in the third Link finds Dampé searching for an empty [[Bottle]], who asks to use the aura of his [[Fairy]] to light the way.<ref>{{Cite|A vessel that holds wandering spirits rests here.|Tombstone|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, it was the fairy's light. In that case, it's just perfect. I came looking for the Royal Family's legendary treasure, but my torch has gone out. I'm sorry, but could you walk in front of me and light the way?|Dampé|MM}}</ref>

===Ikana River===
{{Main|Ikana River}}
The dried up Ikana River pours down from a haunted grotto and into the canyon's neighboring region, the Southern Swamp below. The ghost composer [[Sharp]] inflicted an evil curse upon the river in the hopes of destroying all life in the valley. Only by playing him the "Song of Storms" written by his brother, [[Flat]], will water flow again.

===Music Box House===
{{Main|Music Box House}}
The Music Box House is where [[Pamela]] and her [[Pamela's Father|Father]], a ghost researcher,<ref>{{Cite|I am the famed academic who researches fairies, ghosts and similar so-called supernatural phenomena.|Pamela's Father|MM}}</ref> reside while fending off the undead with the carnival-like song, "[[Farewell to Gibdos]]", which plays from the trumpet-like contraption atop their house.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, the song, Farewell to Gibdos, which the music box is now playing, drives away Gibdos. I discovered it after many years of researching Gibdos.|Pamela's Father|MM}}</ref> A group of [[Gibdo]]s encircle the house looking for Pamela's Father, who is half-mummified from an encounter with Gibdos in the [[Beneath the Well|Well]].<ref>{{Cite|Brrrinnnggg them! Those inssside...Our friendsss... Brrrriiiiinnnng them!|Gibdo|MM}}</ref> Frightened, Pamela shoves her father in a closet and promises to protect him from any further harm, despite the fact that the source of the Music Box's power, the river, is dried up.

Link can obtain the Gibdo Mask from within the house by playing the "[[Song of Healing]]" in front of Pamela's father.

===Road to Ikana===
{{Main|Road to Ikana}}
The Road to Ikana is a rocky gorge ridden with iron fences, ancient statues, and enemies called [[Nejiron]]s that resemble [[Goron]]s, but explode on contact. The Pass features an entrance to the [[Ikana Graveyard]] and the main canyon.

===Sakon's Hideout===
{{Main|Sakon's Hideout}}
[[Sakon]] the thief, who plays a major role in the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest, has his Hideout hidden here behind a large seal that only he can open. Once inside the hideout, Link finds a virtual maze featuring puzzles, enemies, and traps.

===Secret Shrine===
{{Main|Secret Shrine}}
The Secret Shrine is an optional cave found behind the Underground Waterfall.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Secret Shrine behind Waterfall}}<br/>Now awaiting the challenges of bold visitors sure of their might. Hee-hee-hee!|Sign|MM3D}}</ref> Inside it, Link will find numerous mini-bosses, all of which were fought in previous Temples.

===Spirit House===
{{Main|Spirit House}}
The Spirit House is a small hut where Link can test his strength against the [[Poe Sisters]].

===Spring Water Cave===
{{Main|Spring Water Cave}}
The Spring Water Cave has also been dried up due to the curse cast upon it by the ghost composer, Sharp.

===Stone Tower===
{{Main|Stone Tower}}
The Stone Tower contains several [[Fire Keese]] and [[Beamos]], along with many [[Boulder]]s and a bottomless pit. Link plays the "[[Elegy of Emptiness]]", learned from Igos du Ikana, to hold down switches that move large, mobile blocks into various positions, allowing passage to the Stone Tower Temple. Once Link obtains the [[Light Arrow]]s from the [[Garo Master]], he can fire a Light Arrow into the red gem near the entrance, which will flip the entire tower, including the Temple.

===Stone Tower Temple===
{{Main|Stone Tower Temple}}
The Stone Tower Temple is located at the highest point of Ikana. The tower is full of undead creatures, ranging from [[ReDead]]s, [[Poe]]s, [[Eyegore]]s, [[Armos]] and Garos. Within, Link fights the Garo Master, from whom he receives the Light Arrow as spoils, and the bat-attracting [[Gomess]], from whom Link acquires the [[Key#Boss Keys|Boss Key]]. At the end of the Temple, Link can obtain the [[Giant's Mask]] and is warped into a mysterious desert where he duels [[Twinmold]], two gigantic parasites.

===Underground Waterfall===
This waterfall can be found at the beginning of the river that separates the lower terrain of the canyon from the upper one. Behind it is the [[Secret Shrine]], and at the end of its river is [[Southern Swamp]].

==Trivia==
*The sample resembling female vocals found in parts of Ikana Canyon's theme were reused in {{TP}} for [[Hyrule]]'s night theme.
*It is possible to fully explore Ikana Canyon before acquiring the Ice Arrow, normally needed to pass the Octorok-infested river. In the Nintendo 64 version, this requires a precise Hookshot shot to cross the river. In the 3DS version, the Hookshot method is no longer possible but the player can now cross the river by using a bomb to fling Link across.
