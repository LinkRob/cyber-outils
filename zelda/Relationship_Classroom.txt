The {{Term/Store|Relationship Classroom||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 318}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Relationship Classroom}} is a room that can be accessed through the {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Relationship Classroom|display=Classroom}} is located below the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}}. During the day, {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link}} teaches a class in the room entitled "Voe and You",<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Are you interested in sitting in on my class "Voe and You"? It may even teach you a thing or two!|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> which is designed to help young {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} women learn about interacting with men.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Would you be interested in my class on interacting with voe in the world outside this town?|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} sees her class as a way to prepare her students for the world outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|These young vai have never even met a voe before. It's my duty to help prepare them for what they'll encounter in the world beyond Gerudo Town, good and bad.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Ashai}}'s three students are {{Term|BotW|Pasha|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dina|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Risa|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} also observes lessons in the {{Term|BotW|Relationship Classroom}}, where she notes how serious the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} are.<ref>{{Cite|The Gerudo are surprisin'ly serious...|Traysi|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} does not start her class until all of her students have arrived.<ref>{{Cite|There's no hurry—we won't be starting until everyone has arrived.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> 
{{Term|BotW|Risa}} admits that she is only taking this class as a refresher, though she still studies.<ref>{{Cite|To be perfectly honest, I'm only taking Ms. Ashai's class as a kind of refresher course... But I still have to study.|Risa|BotW}}</ref>

When spoken to inside the {{Term|BotW|Relationship Classroom}}, {{Term|BotW|Dina}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} what he thinks of her lipstick, as the color is supposed to bring good luck.<ref>{{Cite|What do you think of my lipstick? I think it'll bring me good luck! You know that the color is especially important to bringing good luck, right?|Dina|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks how that works, {{Term|BotW|Dina}} sheepishly reveals that she was planning on learning that next.<ref>{{Cite|[The color? How?] Umm... I-I'm planning on learning about that next.|Dina|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} compliments her, {{Term|BotW|Dina}} says she can already feel it working.<ref>{{Cite|[It looks good on you.] I know, right?!<br/>Oooh, I can feel it working already!|Dina|BotW}}</ref>

Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} speak to {{Term|BotW|Pasha}}, she will sit up and exclaim that she was not sleeping.<ref>{{Cite|I wasn't sleeping! I was just thinking!|Pasha|BotW}}</ref> However, when she realizes {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not the teacher, she tells him not to scare her like that.<ref>{{Cite|Wait, what? I thought you were the teacher! Don't scare me like that!|Pasha|BotW}}</ref> Her dream is to travel the world before settling down with her true love to have a daughter,<ref>{{Cite|I've always dreamed of going out into the wide world, living a life of adventure, and then settling down with my true love. We'd have a wonderful little vehvi and live happily ever after. I'm sure of it.|Pasha|BotW}}</ref> and she sees these classes as critical for achieving that goal.<ref>{{Cite|But these lessons are critical if I want to pull all of that off, so I can't afford to sleep through them!|Pasha|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses to observe the "Voe and You" class, {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} will begin the class so they can show off what they have learned.<ref>{{Cite|Attention, everyone! Let's get started! We can show this cute vai what we've been learning in class!|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> First, she poses a scenario that her students are approached by a handsome man while walking down the street.<ref>{{Cite|Now then... You're walking down the street on your way somewhere, and a handsome voe you've never met approaches...|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pasha}} responds that she would wave casually, but stay prepared in the case that he is a bandit wanting to ambush her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I would wave casually to him but also keep myself prepared in case he's a distraction for a bandit ambush!|Pasha|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} says this is a good approach to a new situation,<ref>{{Cite|Excellent thinking, Pasha! That's a prudent approach to a new situation.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> but she informs them that a man may appear suspicious due to being nervous around a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|Remember that sometimes voe may look suspicious but are just being wary due to the sight of a daunting Gerudo.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dina}} goes next, saying she would greet him enthusiastically and with her hands clearly empty.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I would enthusiastically greet him, approaching with a smile and with my hands clearly empty!|Dina|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} comments on her optimism and tells her it could put a stranger at ease.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, Dina, that's a very optimistic approach to take! Being overtly friendly is an excellent way to put the stranger's mind at ease!|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> Lastly, {{Term|BotW|Risa}} tells her she would walk casually towards him before striking him when he least expects it,<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I would walk casually toward him and then strike a fierce blow when he least suspected it!|Risa|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} informs her would be a crime.<ref>{{Cite|Risa... That would be a crime...|Ashai|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Ashai}}'s second question asks her students what they would do if they found an injured man lying on the ground.<ref>{{Cite|In your travels, you come upon an injured voe lying on the ground. What do you do?|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pasha}} claims she would carry him to the nearest person who could treat him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I immediately lift him up over my shoulder and carry him to the nearest person who can treat him!|Pasha|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} finds this to be a proactive approach but reminds her to check for non-visible injuries before doing so.<ref>{{Cite|hat's a very proactive answer, Pasha! Always be aware that an injured person may have non-visible injuries before you move them, though!|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dina}} then states that she would determine what was wrong with him a make a medicine for his treatment.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I would determine what was the matter and make a medicine on the spot to treat him!|Dina|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} finds this to be a good answer, she tells her to keep potential allergies in mind.<ref>{{Cite|Great answer, Dina! Just be careful that the voe doesn't have some sort of allergy to the ingredients in your medicine!|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Risa}} goes last, where she reveals she would take the man home to help him recover.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I would secretly bring him back to my home, where I could help him gradually recover!|Risa|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} finds this sentiment sweet, she also tells {{Term|BotW|Risa}} that it is basically kidnapping.<ref>{{Cite|Risa...<br/>A sweet sentiment...<br/>But also basically kidnapping.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref>

For her final question, {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} asks her students what they would do if they were approached by a man whose attention they have sought.<ref>{{Cite|You are suddenly approached by a voe whose attention you've sought, and he abruptly engages you in conversation.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pasha}} goes first and says she would pretend not to know his language.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... I would pretend that I don't know how to speak his language.|Pasha|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} finds this interesting, as it could spark curiousity from someone,<ref>{{Cite|That's an interesting approach, Pasha! You could inspire some curiosity in the voe by doing that...|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> but reminds her that the man may know the language she is pretending to.<ref>{{Cite|Remember: it isn't unrealistic that the voe may actually speak the language you pretend to know, too!|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> Next, {{Term|BotW|Dina}} states that she would take the opportunity to figure out his social standing on the basis of his clothing.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I would take the up-close opportunity to determine his social standing based on the clothes he's wearing!|Dina|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} praises {{Term|BotW|Dina}}'s thinking,<ref>{{Cite|Ah, good thinking, Dina.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> but tells her that people may wear more casual clothes if their good ones are dirty.<ref>{{Cite|Just remember that sometimes all of someone's good clothes are dirty, so they might wear something more casual.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> Finally, {{Term|BotW|Risa}} says she would grab the man's hand and twist it until he agreed to marry her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I would grab the man's hand and twist his wrist until he agreed to marry me!|Risa|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} is at first shocked,<ref>{{Cite|Wha... You'd...|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> she then asks {{Term|BotW|Risa}} to speak to her after class.<ref>{{Cite|Risa... Let's talk after class.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref>
