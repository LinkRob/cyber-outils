"{{Term/Store|Follow the Sheikah Slate||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Follow the Sheikah Slate}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Great Plateau}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link}} obtains the Quest shortly after a {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link|display=mysterious voice}} awakens him and urges him to leave the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} to restore the light to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... ...Open your eyes... Open your eyes... Open your eyes. Wake up, Link.|???|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Link... You are the light—our light—that must shine upon Hyrule once again. Now, go...|???|BotW}}</ref> Once he has started exploring the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, the voice will call out to him again. The voice instructs Link to head towards the destination marked on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link|display=Sheikah Slate's}} [[Sheikah Slate#Map|Map]].<ref>{{Cite|... Link... Link. Head for the point marked on the map in your Sheikah Slate.|???|BotW}}</ref>

Upon reaching the buried remains of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|link}}, Link is instructed to place the Sheikah Slate into the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Place the Sheikah Slate in the pedestal.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> Once he does so, the Tower will be activated, and he will be warned to watch for falling rocks.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Tower activated. Please watch for falling rocks.}}|Sheikah Slate|BotW}}</ref> In doing so, Link causes the Great Plateau Tower to shoot upwards out of the ground that once held it. This causes a simultaneous reaction among all of the other {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}} scattered across Hyrule, forcing them to rise as well. At this same exact moment that the Sheikah Towers collectively ascend, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} across Hyrule begin to glow an orange light,<ref>{{Cite|Do you see that structure there? The one shining with a strange light? It began glowing at the exact moment those towers rose up from the ground.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> acting as a sort of beacon to allow Link to locate them from a distance. When Link regains his composure following his rapid ascension, the Great Plateau Tower begins to distill regional information on the Great Plateau in the form of a tear drop,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Distilling local information...}}|Sheikah Tower|BotW}}</ref> which splashes onto the Sheikah Slate and updates its Map with landmarks and geographical data.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Regional map extracted.}}|Sheikah Slate|BotW}}</ref>

The voice calls out to Link again before Link begins to leave the Tower, urging him to remember the events leading up to his awakening.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Remember...}} Try... Try to remember... You have been asleep for the past 100 years.|???|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} interrupts her and begins to swirl around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} before being driven back by a white light emanating from the Castle. The voice warns Link that when Calamity Ganon regains its full strength, she will no longer be able to contain it.<ref>{{Cite|The beast... When the beast regains its true power, this world will face its end. Now then... You must hurry, Link. Before it's too late...|???|BotW}}</ref> This completes the Quest, allowing Link to descend the Great Plateau Tower and speak to the {{Term|BotW|Old Man|link}}, who tasks him with the next Main Quest, "{{Term|BotW|The Isolated Plateau|link}}".
