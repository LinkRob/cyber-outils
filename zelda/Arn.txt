{{Term/Store|Arn||Series, TLoZ (Valiant Comics)}} is a character in the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}} published by Valiant Comics.<ref name=quote/> He is the [[Link's Father|father]] of Link and at least one younger male child.<ref name=son/> He and his wife, [[Medilia]], reside in a small home in the kingdom of [[Calatia]].<ref name=quote/>

==Biography==
During Link's absence from Calatia, he received a notorious reputation. An [[Shadow Link|impostor of Link]] had overthrown the kingdom of Calatia and imprisoned the queen, greatly shaming both Arn and Medilia. When the unsuspecting Link returns home to visit his family,<ref name=quote>{{Cite Book|quote=After many long quests and adventures in Hyrule, Link at least returns to visit his homeland, Calatia--the kingdom to the west of Hyrule, and the house of his parents Arn and Medilia.|book=TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=1 [Coming Home]}}</ref>  he receives a violent greeting from Arn and is told to never show his face again, claiming that Link is no son of his.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=You're no son of mine! Get out! Get out of here!...And never come back!|book=TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=1 [Coming Home]}}</ref> Shortly after, Link discovers the cause to this and set out to stop the impostor. After he succeeds, both Arn and Medilia apologize for their behavior and welcome him back.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link! My son! We didn't know--!|book=TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5 [Coming Home]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Heh! That's my boy!|book=TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=10 [Coming Home]}}</ref>
