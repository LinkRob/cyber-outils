==Location and Uses==
Clam Chowder is a dish made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Fresh Milk]], [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Goat Butter]], and a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their regular version can only be made by including a fifth ingredient with an effect that contradicts that of the Hearty Blueshell Snail. If eaten, they'll heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The nutritious Hearty Blueshell Snails combine with the other ingredients to create a rich soup.<ref name="Inventory"/>

There is only one variation of Clam Chowder that can be made:

* '''Hearty Clam Chowder''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Clam Chowder}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The nutritional value of hearty blueshell snail combines with butter and milk in a rich soup.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using the default recipe. Eating this chowder will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>
