{{Term/Store|Gomess||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a [[Sub-Boss]] of the [[Stone Tower Temple]] in {{MM}}. <ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 182}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|context1= {{MM3D|-}}
|quote1= It's {{Color|MM3D White|Gomess}}.<br/> Your attacks won't do a thing until you defeat all those bats surrounding it!

|context2= {{MM|-}}
|quote2= It's {{Color|MM White|Gomess}}. There'll be no end to it if you can't get those bats away from it. Does that shed any light on the situation?
}}[[File:Gomess2.jpg|thumb|257x257px|Link firing a Light Arrow at Gomess to drive his shield of Bad Bats away in {{MM|-}}]]Gomess is a phantom made entirely of [[Bad Bat|Bad Bats]] and is armed with a large scythe to attack his enemies. He is immune to any damage while his Bad Bats cover its body. To disperse the bats, [[Link]] must shoot a [[Light Arrow]] at Gomess. Once the bats are scattered, Link can attack the green orb under its cloak with his [[Sword]] or another Light Arrow to damage the creature of darkness. After each attack, Gomess will start spinning its scythe towards Link, who can block the attack with his [[Shield]]. Gomess can also attack Link by swinging its powerful scythe, which will cause two [[Heart Container|Hearts]] of damage. Upon being defeated, it will flail and throw its scythe away in the process, with the scythe planting its blade into the ground just as Gomess itself burns away.

Defeating Gomess unlocks the chamber where a [[Treasure Chest]] containing the [[Boss Key]] is located.

==Nomenclature==
Gomess shares its French name, "Faucheur", with [[Death Sword]]. The name alludes to the {{wp|Grim Reaper}}, which Gomess itself is based on.
