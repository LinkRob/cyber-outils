==Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Heat occurs during the daytime in the [[Gerudo Desert#Breath of the Wild|Gerudo Desert]]. Heat can damage Link if he does not stay within shade, in a body of water, or if he does not have any [[Armor]] that provides [[Heat Resistance]]. Both [[Gerudo Town]] and [[Kara Kara Bazaar]] provide constant protection from Heat. The [[Rito]] traveler, [[Guy]], can be found in the Kara Kara Bazaar, waiting for the daytime Heat to pass in order to visit Gerudo Town.<ref>{{Cite|I would like to see what Gerudo Town looks like, even if I can't go inside... It's just so hot...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> The [[Side Quest]], "[[An Ice Guy]]", involves giving a [[Chilly Elixir]] to Guy so that he can cross the Desert.
