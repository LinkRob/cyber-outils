{{Term/Store|Genli||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Genli|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Genli}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} child who lives in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. She can initially be found in the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}'s {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)|link}}, where she asks who {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is and if he has come to stop {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Who are you? Some kind of traveler? Did you come to make that big bird up in the sky stop bothering us?|Genli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Genli}} is upset if {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms he has, as {{Term|BotW|Kheel|link}} will make her go to singing practice if everything returns to normal.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure did!] {{Big|Nooo!}} If everything goes back to normal, Kheel will make me go to singing practice.|Genli|BotW}}</ref> She feels bad that {{Term|BotW|Molli|link}}'s father, {{Term|BotW|Harth|link}} got hurt, but she does not want to practice.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I mean...I feel bad that Molli's dad, Mr. Harth, got hurt, but...}} But I definitely know I don't want to practice singing!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> According to {{Term|BotW|Amali|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} and her sisters have become needier since {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} started flying around.<ref name="Daughters"/>

After the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}}. She is angry that {{Term|BotW|Genli}} and the rest of her sisters have not arrived for their rehearsal.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Grrrrrr! My sisters are dumb DUMMIES!}} They promised we'd rehearse together, but NO ONE showed up!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> They are supposed to perform for {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}}, the elder of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, but {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} thinks are not prepared to do so.<ref>{{Cite|We have to perform our song for the {{Color|BotW Blue|elder}} at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}}! We're going to be laughed off the stage...|Kheel|BotW}}</ref>

Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a scary {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} asks him to find her sisters and remind them of rehearsal.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you... You look like I can use you... Thaaaat is to say...you look like a nice guy who likes to help people in need. Can you go tell my sisters in Rito Village to stop ditching rehearsal? They won't listen to me, but they might listen to a scary-looking Hylian like you.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> However, she thinks {{Term|BotW|Genli}} will be difficult, as she is probably in the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}} demanding {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Although Genli might be a tough sell. She's probably in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}} yelling, {{Big|"I want some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}!"}} ...or something like that.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Recital at Warbler's Nest|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

Back in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}}, where she is begging for {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hurry! Hurry! {{Big|I NEED {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} in my belly!}}|Genli|BotW}}</ref> She refuses to practice with {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} until she gets it.<ref>{{Cite|I know {{Color|BotW Blue|Kheel}} wants me to practice singing with her, but I just can't. Not until I get my {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> The rest of her sisters have already left to gather ingredients for her, though she did not expect them to listen to her.<ref>{{Cite|My sisters already left to get {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} ingredients. I told them I wouldn't sing until I get it. I didn't expect them to actually make it for me, though!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Genli}} does not want to practice her singing, but she still does want {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}}.<ref>{{Cite|I don't want to practice singing... {{Small|but I really do want some yummy {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}.}}|Genli|BotW}}</ref> She wonders what is taking them so long, as they were only supposed to go to {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What is taking my sisters to long? I thought they were just going to the {{Color|BotW Red|Slippery Falcon}}.|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Notts|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kotts|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Cree|link}}, they will each give him one ingredient on the condition he makes {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}} for {{Term|BotW|Genli}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well then...I guess I'll give the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}} to you! Please make the best {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli!|Notts|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please take this {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}} and go make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If I give you this {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}, will you make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli? She should be in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}}.|Cree|BotW}}</ref> In the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} can smell {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has it on him.<ref>{{Cite|*sniff sniff...*<br/>*sniff sniff sniff*<br/>That amazing smell... Could it be?<br/>{{Big|Sa...<br/>Sa...<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Salmon meunière}}?!}}|Genli|BotW}}</ref> She frantically asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to let her have it.<ref>{{Cite|You're going to let me have some, right? RIGHT?!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> She is very excited when {{Term|BotW|Link}} allows her to have some, and she eats it immediately.<ref>{{Cite|[Enjoy!] YAAAAAAAAAAAAY! ♪Time to dig in!♪ *munch munch*...<br/>Hngh mngh...<br/>Hnff! Hurk! Mmmmmm... I'm stuffed!|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

Reminded of her promise to her sisters, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} realizes she should uphold her word.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh? My promise to my sisters? I suppose I shouldn't break my promise...|Genli|BotW}}</ref> While she enjoys singing, she gets nervous doing so in front of others.<ref>{{Cite|I really DO like singing!<br/>...But I am too nervous to sing in front of other people.|Genli|BotW}}</ref> She thinks that her stage fright might be cured if she practices with her sisters.<ref>{{Cite|I have SERIOUS stage fright...but maybe practicing with my sisters will help me feel more comfortable.|Genli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Genli}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for the {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}}, claiming it was soft, flaky, and delicious.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, thank you for the amazing {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}! It was so soft and flaky and delicious!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> Deciding to give the rehearsal her all, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come visit.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give this rehearsal my all. ♪Come listen later!♪|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} is found happily singing with the rest of her sisters.<ref>{{Cite|♪La loo loo loo laaaaaaa!♪|Genli|BotW}}</ref> All the singing she is doing is making her hungry, and she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants to hear her stomach's song or a song about Warbler's Nest.<ref>{{Cite| Phew... All this singing is making me hungry.Which would you rather hear? My tummy's song or a song about Warbler's Nest?|Genli|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks to hear the {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} song, the sisters will recite the song together. Once {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine|link}} rises from the ground, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} will remark that it is cool that {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} had such a secret.<ref>{{Cite|So cool! Who knew Warbler's Nest was keeping a secret like that|Genli|BotW}}</ref> The sisters decide to leave to inform their mother,<ref>{{Cite|Let's tell mom!|Notts|BotW}}</ref> and the five of them race back to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.

Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} practices singing with her sisters near {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|On my way to go practice at the village shrine!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> She shows off her singing to {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey, check it out. I'm practicing my singing!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> admitting that she never would have realized how much she likes to sing if {{Term|BotW|Link}} had not helped {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}.<ref>{{Cite|You know, if you didn't go to Kheel, I never would have realized how much I actually like to sing.|Genli|BotW}}</ref> Now, she is having fun with her sisters.<ref>{{Cite|Buy thanks to you, I'm having a blast!|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed all of the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} notices that he looks cooler.<ref>{{Cite|Wow...you look different somehow...Cooler... WHOA! You found all the of the shrines!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> She congratulates him and calls him a good boy.<ref>{{Cite|CONGRATULATIONS! GOOD BOY! OK. Hope you liked the congratulations!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her again, she notes he looks like he is hungry.<ref>{{Cite|What's wrong? You kind of look like you're hungry?|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} has returned to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} and her sisters spend all time singing with him.<ref name="Kids"/> On her way home at the end of the day, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} reveals that {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} likes the way she sings.<ref>{{Cite|The elder said he likes the way I sing!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> She decides to eat some {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}}, as she can practice more tomorrow.<ref>{{Cite|I'm gonna go eat some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}. I can practice more tomorrow!|Genli|BotW}}</ref>
