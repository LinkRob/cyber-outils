==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Armoranth}}
{{Plural|BotW|Armoranth}} grow in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. They are plentiful near {{Term|BotW|Mija|link|display=Mija's}} {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, which is outside of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}. When an {{Term|BotW|Armoranth}} is {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cooked}} into a meal in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, the resulting dish will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} an increased defense when eaten.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth}} can be roasted by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to create {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Armoranth|link}}. At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth}} to create {{Term|BotW|Purple Dye|link}}.

In order to start the "{{Term|BotW|Little Sister's Big Request|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must find common ground with {{Term|BotW|Jana|link}}, or else she will not speak to him. If he has an {{Term|BotW|Armoranth}}, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} will notice and ask for it.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm... What's that? What do you have there? That's {{Color|BotW Blue|armoranth}}, isn't it?! Could you...um...maybe give me some?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} gives it to her, she will state that it is her favorite plant and that she may get along with him after all.<ref>{{Cite|For you to be walking around with my favorite plant... It seems you and I may just get along after all.|Jana|BotW}}</ref>
