'''Ultra Death Bug''' is the boss of the {{Term|FPTRR|Insect Cavern}} in {{FPTRR}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Term|FPTRR|Ultra Death Bug}} is a stronger variant of the {{Term|FPTRR|Death Bug}}, featuring a purple body instead of a golden one, adorned with numerous red {{Term|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}-like crystals.

The fight also plays out similarly to that of the {{Term|FPTRR|Death Bug}}, complete with {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo|link}} once again acting as {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}}'s {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}} by defeating the {{Term|FPTRR|Ultra Death Bug}}'s spawn. Tingle must defeat the {{Term|FPTRR|Ultra Death Bug}} by dropping three {{Plural|FPTRR|Tingle Bomb}} on its face.

===Gargantu-Bug===
After it appears that {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} has defeated {{Term|FPTRR|Ultra Death Bug}}, its remains will suddenly morph into a new monster, known as the '''Gargantu-Bug'''. This form is much larger, stronger, and faster, taking five more bombs to truly defeat. When defeated, its body will yield 40,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} and the {{Term|FPTRR|Earth Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got 40,000 rupees!|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref>
