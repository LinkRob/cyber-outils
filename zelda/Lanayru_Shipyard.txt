{{Term/Store|Lanayru Shipyard||Series, SS}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 296}}</ref> It is located in the [[Lanayru Sand Sea]].

==Features==
The Lanayru Shipyard was once a sea town inhabited by the [[Ancient Robot|workers]] who built the ships inside the construction bay.<ref>{{Cite|This is the island where we used to make our ships, bzzt. You'll find the Shipyard here, as well as the town where the workers lived, vweep!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> Link and [[Skipper]] visit the Shipyard after [[Skipper's Retreat]] in their search for Skipper's [[Sandship|lost ship]], which was among those constructed there.<ref>{{Cite|The Shipyard is where we used to build our ships, vrrt. My ship was built there too, vweep! If you go to the Shipyard, you might find some clues to the location of my ship, brrzrrt!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> Each of the areas on the island are connected to each other via a complex series of mine-cart tracks.<ref>{{Cite|Each location on the island is linked to the others via a mine-cart track, vrrm!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> The door leading to the construction bay where Skipper thinks his clues leading to his ship may be is closed,<ref>{{Cite|But the entrance is closed, vrrrm. Looks like you can't get in.|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> forcing Link to ride mine-carts on the tracks around the Shipyard to access the back door.<ref>{{Cite|You might be able to get to the back door of the construction bay if you can get around to the other side of it using a mine cart, brrzrrt.|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref>

When Link arrives in the construction bay, he uses his [[Gust Bellows]] to search the massive sand deposit.<ref>{{Cite|Master, we have arrived at the Shipyard construction bay. With the passing of many years, this structure has filled with sand. I recommend looking in the sand for a clue that may direct you to the location of the ship.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Rather than find clues pertaining to the location of the ship, he mistakenly awakens a buried [[Moldarach]], which had moved in and built a nest in the many years that the factory went unused.<ref>{{Cite|Master, it would appear that in the many years this factory went unused, monsters have moved in and built a nest. I now estimate that the chance of  finding a clue in the sand here as to the ship's location is extremely low.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

After returning to Skipper through the door that was previously locked, they sail on towards the [[Pirate Stronghold]].

====Rickety Coaster====
{{Main|Rickety Coaster}}
The [[Rickety Coaster]] is a mine-cart mini-game run by the [[Goron]] named [[Gortram]] on the tracks sprawling around the construction bay. The mini-game comes in two difficulties: "Scary" and "Heart Stopping!". If he completes the "Heart Stopping!" track in less than the time of 1.05.00, Link will be rewarded with a [[Piece of Heart]]. The mini-game is operable after the [[Sandship]] is returned to Skipper.
