{{Term/Store|Honey and Darling||Series, MM, MM3D}}  are characters in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref> Honey is the woman with red hair, while Darling is the brown-haired man.

==Biography==
Honey and Darling appear running a [[Honey & Darling's Shop|shop]] in [[East Clock Town]]. It is open from 6 AM to 10 PM, but Honey and Darling will flee at 6 PM on the [[Final Day]].<ref>{{Cite|Target Range / Honey & Darling's Shop / 6 a.m. to 10 p.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> It serves as a game house that changes its main game event daily.<ref>{{Cite|Honey & Darling's Shop / A Gaming Center. Changing Daily!|N/A|MM}}</ref> If [[Link]] obtains a perfect score for all three games during the same three-day cycle, he will receive a [[Piece of Heart]] and can also be seen hugging together.<ref>{{Cite|H-H-Honey!!! He...He got a perfect score three days in a row. This is all we have left to give him, Honey.|Darling|MM}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* They are the [[Terminan]] counterpart of the [[Dancing Couple]] from {{OoT}}.
* When Link plays "[[Epona's Song]]" to a [[Cow]], Honey's [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Honey_Laugh1.wav laugh] can be heard from the [[Animal]] before giving the hero a free bottle of [[Milk]].
* Some fans claim Darling's [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Darling_Laugh1.wav laugh] is similar to that of [[pokemon:James|James]] from the ''[[pokemon:Pokémon_(franchise)|Pokémon]]'' series, specifically the anime.
