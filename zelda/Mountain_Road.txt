The {{Term/Store|Mountain Road||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|mountain road}} is pretty dangerous because of how hot it gets. If you're taking a trip to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}, be sure to bring some fire protection.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road}} begins at the {{Term|BotW|Maw of Death Mountain|link}} and ends at the top of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}. The fork of the {{Term|BotW|Ternio Trail|link}} that heads towards {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}} leads into it. The {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|link}} are located along it. {{Term|BotW|Stolock Bridge|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Eldin|link}} both connect parts of the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road|display=Road}}.

The beginning of the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road}} is marked by the first of the {{Plural|BotW|Death Mountain Marker|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #1<br/>Climbing Path Trailhead|Death Mountain Marker|BotW}}</ref> The rest of the {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain Marker|display=Markers}} are found along the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road|display=Road}}.

Near {{Term|BotW|Medingo Pool|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road}} enters a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #2<br/>Goron City ahead<br/>Heat level: ★<br/>Non-Gorons use extreme caution|Death Mountain Marker|BotW}}</ref> In order to progress further, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will either need to drink a {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}} or equip parts of the {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}} set. Past the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Eldin}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will require another level of {{Term|BotW|Flame Guard|link}} before he can continue.<ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #8<br/>Bridge of Eldin<br/>Heat level:★★<br/>Non-Gorons turn back or burn!|Death Mountain Marker|BotW}}</ref>

An {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus|link}} can be found in the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road}} as it passes through {{Term|BotW|Goronbi Lake North|link}}, just under {{Term|BotW|Gortram Cliff|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Goronbi Lake North<br/>{{Big|Igneo Talus}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, the part of the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road}} past the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Eldin}} will be filled with {{Plural|BotW|Sentry|link}} as {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}} attempt to approach {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Yikes... A {{Color|BotW Blue|sentry}} is always a nuisance, no matter where it is or how many of them there are.|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref> Their progress along the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road|display=Road}} will be impeded by {{Plural|BotW|Black Moblin|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat in order to make use of {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Cannon|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Dayto|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Kairo|link}} can both be found on the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road}}. They travel between {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}} and the end of the {{Term|BotW|Maw of Death Mountain}}.
