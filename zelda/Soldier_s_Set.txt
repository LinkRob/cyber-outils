The {{Term/Store|Soldier's Set||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor Set|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 184}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Soldier's Set is an [[Armor]] set consisting of the [[Soldier's Helm]], [[Soldier's Armor]], and [[Soldier's Greaves]]. All three pieces are sold at [[Ventest Clothing Boutique]] in [[Hateno Village]]. As the standard armor of [[Hyrule]]'s [[soldier]]s, while it does not feature any set bonuses, it has much higher defense than other armor pieces.

The downside of this armor is that, being made of metal, it's more vulnerable to lightning and electric attacks. 

Certain {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} wear parts of the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Set}}, such as the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Helm|display=Helm}} being worn by {{Term|BotW|Hoz|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Savelle|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Nell|link}}, who has the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Armor}} equipped.
