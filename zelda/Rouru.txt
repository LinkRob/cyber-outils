{{Term/Store|Rouru||Series}} is a character in {{OoT|Himekawa}}.{{Name Ref|OoT (Himekawa)}} He is the heir to the tribal leadership of the [[Watarara]] tribe.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Besides, I can do anything I want! I'm the prince of the Watarara!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 175}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Hmm... This is a child of the Watarara Tribe. They're a migratory race with wings like birds. They ride the seasonal wind to Hyrule once a year.|book= OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 164}}</ref> One year, when the juvenile Watarara's wings would begin to grow,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= As the Watarara grow up, their arms turn into wings and they can fly.|book= OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 165}}</ref> he fell off his mother's back during migration,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= He probably became separated from the flock during migration and fell.|book= OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 165}}</ref> and landed in the river below. While [[Link]] was fishing in the same river, he felt a tug on his fishing pole. When he yanked it out of the water, he found the nearly drowned child hanging from his pole. Realizing that the Watarara child was in dire condition, Link took him to [[Dr. Mizumi|Dr. Mizumi's]] laboratory to recover. Mizumi was glad to take the child in, although after Rouru had been partially healed he destroyed most of the doctor's lab.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= That little brat destroyed my life's work!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 171}}</ref> Also, every time he got a chance, he would pick on poor [[Navi]]. Once, Navi got so angry that she left Link and Rouru for a short time.<ref>{{Cite|I'm only here because the [[Great Deku Tree]] asked me to look out for you! But if you don't want me around... fine!|Navi|OoT}}</ref>
[[File:RouruOoT.png|thumb|left|200px|Rouru awakens]]
At one point after a fight with a twister caused by two [[Flare Dancer]]s, Link was in a freefall to his doom. Luckily, Rouru's wings grew in just in time to "save" Link, but he was too heavy to carry and they both went hurtling to the ground.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= It's- It's no use! You're too heavy!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 184}}</ref> Luckily Rouru's mother, [[Elrora]] comes in the nick of time to land them gently on the ground.

Shortly after Rouru was fully healed, the rest of the tribe arrived, and safely returned home.
