The {{Term/Store|Mo'a Keet Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Mo'a Keet Shrine}}<br/>Metal Makes a Path}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Metal Makes a Path''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an [[Ancient Shrine]] in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Mo'a Keet Shrine}} is located atop a cliff to the east of {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he's greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mo'a Keet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Mo'a Keet. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Mo'a Keet|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Mo'a Keet Shrine}} consists of three floors, each connected by ramps where {{Plural|BotW|Boulder|link}} continuously drop from a chute above and roll down, eventually falling into pits of {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} at the bottom. When the {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}} fall into the {{Term|BotW|Lava}}, another drops down in its place. On the first ramp rolls a stone {{Term|BotW|Boulder}}. The second floor platform has another two sets of ramps. On the left ramp rolls three boulders in a row, while on the right ramp rolls an {{Term|BotW|Iron Ball|link}}.

To climb the first ramp, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to suspend the rolling {{Term|BotW|Boulder}} before it hits him. He can then use a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} to knock the suspended boulder away. Alternately, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also suspend the {{Term|BotW|Boulder}} just as it falls from the chute but before it lands, allowing him to run underneath it to the second platform.

On the second platform, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must take the ramp to the left. On this ramp, the multiple {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}} prevent {{Term|BotW|Link}} from simply suspending one and knocking it away. Instead, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must carry the {{Term|BotW|Iron Ball}} from the right ramp using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} and push the {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}} away from him. Alternately, it is possible to suspend one of the {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}} as it falls from the chute with {{Term|BotW|Stasis}}, which will block the other {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}} from falling in that period. 

At the end of the left ramp is the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. When approached, {{Term|BotW|Mo'a Keet}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the Trial and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Mo'a Keet|BotW}}</ref> Following this, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Mo'a Keet|BotW}}</ref>

In {{Term|BotW|Mo'a Keet Shrine}}'s ceiling are four cracked blocks, concealing a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} in a hidden recess. These blocks can be destroyed by carrying the {{Term|BotW|Iron Ball}} with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} and slamming it through them or by firing {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} at them. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Ruby|link}} inside. On the second platform, there is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} located on the ramp to the right. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} to grab and carry the {{Term|BotW|Iron Ball}} as it falls, allowing him to easily reach the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}. When it is opened, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will receive a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Broadsword|link}}.
