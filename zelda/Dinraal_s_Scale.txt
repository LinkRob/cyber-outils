==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Dinraal's Scale}} can be collected from the {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}}, who typically flies around the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Mountains|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can shoot anywhere along the main body that is not the horns, teeth, or claws to receive the {{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Scale|display=Scales}}. Dinraal's Scale will fall somewhere on the land in the form of a light.

Meals made using {{Plural|BotW|Dinraal's Scale}} will result in {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}. However, if they are {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}} with a critter, the result will be an {{Term|BotW|Elixir|link}}. They can also be used at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} to make {{Term|BotW|Crimson Dye|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} will also accept them in exchange for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}}.

During "{{Term|BotW|The Spring of Power|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear a legend about offering a "sacred article" to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}}.<ref name="Nobo"/> There, {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}} will task {{Term|BotW|Link}} with retrieving one of {{Plural|BotW|Dinraal's Scale}} and placing it into the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|display=Spring}}.<ref>{{Cite|Offer {{Color|BotW Red|Dinraal's scale}}, received from the red spirit, to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Power}}. I, Goddess Hylia, will guide you.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} does so, the doorway behind the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} will open, revealing {{Term|BotW|Tutsuwa Nima Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When you carried out the statue's suggestion and dropped Dinraal's scale into the Spring of Power, an ancient shrine revealed itself.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Dinraal's Scale}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
