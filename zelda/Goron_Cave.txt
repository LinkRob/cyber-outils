The {{Term/Store|Goron Cave||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do you know of the {{Color|TMC Green|Goron Cave}}? It's a tunnel that the {{Color|TMC Green|Gorons}} are digging.|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave}} is located in the southwestern corner of {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}. It is only accessible after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} infuses the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link|display=Earth}} and {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link|display=Fire Elements}} and passes through {{Term|TMC|Talon|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Malon|link}}'s home at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch}}.

When {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Eenie|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Goron Digger|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch}} is able to successfully break into the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave|display=Cave}}.

After this, each time {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with one of the five {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Wall|link}} in {{Plural|TMC|Dig Cavern|link}} across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}, another {{Term|TMC|Goron Digger}} will appear and help its kin break down a subsequent layer inside the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave}}. When all six {{Plural|TMC|Goron Digger}} are brought together, they successfully break down the final rock wall, revealing a large {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} with an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}.

A small {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} containing 200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} is also inside the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave|display=Cave}}, accessible after four {{Plural|TMC|Goron Digger}} have been rallied and broken down the second of the three interior walls.
