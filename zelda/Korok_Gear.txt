The {{Term/Store|Korok Gear|Korok Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is an archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Korok Gear}} is weaponry crafted by the {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} to be used by {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Forest Dweller's Sword}}<br/>Koroks made this sword for Hylians. It's made of wood, so it isn't the best choice for head-on attacks. Its original intent was likely clearing vines to forge paths through forests.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Forest Dweller's Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Forest Dweller's Spear}}<br/>The Koroks made this spear for Hylians. The shaft is made from a light, sturdy wood, offering ease of use. The spearhead is made from a much harder wood, offering strength.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Forest Dweller's Bow}}<br/>The Koroks made this bow for Hylians. It's crafted from flexible wood and uses sturdy vines for the bowstring. Its construction may be simple, but it fires multiple arrows at once.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Forest Dweller's Shield">{{Cite|{{Big|Forest Dweller's Shield}}<br/>The Koroks made this shield specifically for Hylians. It's made from the finest hard wood of trees that grow only in the Korok forest, so it's sturdier than it looks.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The exception to this is the {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf|link}}, which is a naturally occurring leaf.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Korok Leaf}}<br/>A single swing of this giant, sturdy leaf can create a gust of wind strong enough to blow away light objects.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> All other forms of {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear}} are made from the sturdy of wood of {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} that grow within the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}.<ref name="Forest Dweller's Spear"/><ref name="Forest Dweller's Shield"/>

Every piece of {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear}} will float when they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, which is due to their plant-like compositions. This also means they will burn and eventually disintegrate when they are exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they cannot be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
