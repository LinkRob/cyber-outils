{{Term/Store|Dragon Citadel||Series, TFH}} is a {{Term|TFH|Level|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=161 }}</ref> It is the third {{Term|TFH|Level}} of the Sky Realm area. 

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|TFH|Dragon Citadel}} takes place outside of a giant castle held high in the sky above the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}. As its name implies, it is home to a group of {{Plural|TFH|Aeralfos|link}}, who are fought in the last {{Term|TFH|Stage|link}}. Like the {{Term|TFH|Floating Garden|link}}, strong currents of wind blow through the palace corridors, which may push the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} off of the castle floors. Several types of platforms originally encountered in previous {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} make a reappearance: including glass tiles similar to the ice platforms from the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|section=Ice Cavern|display=Ice Cavern|link}}, the disappearing and reappearing platforms from {{Term|TFH|Drablands|section=The Ruins|display=The Ruins|link}}, and the colored tiles also from The Ruins, which only the {{Term|TFH|Links}} whose color matches it can stand on. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} also find {{Plural|TFH|Cucco|link}}, which are used to glide over to other parts of the castle. The {{Term|TFH|Magic Hammer|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}} are provided in the {{Term|TFH|Level}}.

==={{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge}}===
{{Term|TFH|Dragon Citadel}} includes the following {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}}: "{{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|section=Halved attack and defense!|display=Halved attack and defense!|link}}", "{{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|section=Avoid the volcanic rocks!|display=Avoid the volcanic rocks!|link}}", and "{{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|section=Don't fall at all!|display=Don't fall at all!|link}}".
