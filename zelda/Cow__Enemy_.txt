'''Cows''' are docile enemies in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Cow (Enemy)}} appear in {{Term|FPTRR|Lon Lon Meadow}}. Their nature is not aggressive, and they will attempt to avoid {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} if they spot him. In battle they are quite strong, however. They usually drop {{Term|FPTRR|Heart Meat|link}} when defeated.

A rarer variant, the '''{{Term|FPTRR|Gold Cow}}''', can sometimes be found roaming the western parts of the {{Term|FPTRR|Lon Lon Meadow}}, above the {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard Salon|link}}. It drops 250 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} when defeated.

The Rancher of the meadow claims that they eat paper, and it turns out that one of the {{Plural|FPTRR|Cow (Enemy)}} stole a {{Term|FPTRR|Recipe|link}} from him.

According to {{Term|FPTRR|Pinkle|link}}, the {{Plural|FPTRR|Cow (Enemy)}} in {{Term|FPTRR|Lon Lon Meadow}} used to look like normal {{Plural|FPTRR|Cow (Enemy)}}, but one day a strange ball of light appeared, and the {{Plural|FPTRR|Cow}} vanished. They subsequently returned but with their bizarre, altered features reminiscent of {{Wp|Grey alien|Greys}}, common aliens in popular fiction. {{Term|FPTRR|Pinkle}} also questions whether {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} himself is related to the {{Plural|FPTRR|Cow (FPTRR)}}, as he shares a strange elongated face with large eyes.
