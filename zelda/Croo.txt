{{Term/Store|Croo||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.{{Fact}} He sits at the café in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}} and gives miscellaneous advice to [[Link]]. His grandson is one of the [[Rescue Knights]].

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Croo}}, and he is a retired individual. His grandson is a rescue knight who saves those who fall off of {{Color|SS Blue|Skyloft}}.</p><p>He delights in hearing passersby at the bazaar restaurant mention his grandson's heroic deeds.</p>
}}

Once the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}} is open, Link can talk to Croo, where he will tell the hero how to sleep in a bed to pass time. He also warns Link that Skyloft is different at night.<ref>{{Cite|Only one thing to do when you're tired: get some sleep. {{Color|SS Green|You can sleep anywhere there's a bed}}, you know? Skyloft is a different place after the sun goes down. It's no lie. {{Color|SS Green|Find a bed and take a nap}} to sleep until nightfall. There's all kinds of curious things to enjoy at night...|Croo|SS}}</ref> If Link sits in the seat opposite to Croo, he congratulates his graduation and offers a drink.<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh heh. Seems like you're on the road to success, little friend. How 'bout I buy us a beverage?|Croo|SS}}</ref> If Link denies the offer, Croo will feel insulted;<ref>{{Cite|Hmph! No time for an old-timer... I see how it is.|Croo|SS}}</ref> but if Link accepts, Croo tells him that he could become a Rescue Knight if he works hard, and boasts that his grandson is one.<ref>{{Cite|There's a good lad. Cheers! [...] That's the stuff! There we go! Let me give you some information you might find useful somewhere down the line. The Knight Academy and your friends attend produces some first-class Loftwing riders. Keep your nose to the grindstone, and you might find yourself soaring through the skies and protecting the good fold of Skyloft too. Don't know if you've met 'em or not, but the rescue knights save anyone clumsy enough to fall off of Skyloft. Only the Knight Academy's seniors are invited to join that group. They're the best of the best! You might just have what it takes to join them, but don't think it'll be easy. No slacking, you hear! Guess I should tell you my grandson's one of 'em! Heh heh heh.|Croo|SS}}</ref> Afterwards, he will invite Link to visit him again for information.<ref>{{Cite|Stop by and chat with me once in a while, you hear? I may be old, but I'm not boring.|Croo|SS}}</ref> Croo also provides additional pieces of wisdom, such as the nature of their home, Skyloft, and that [[Hylia]] is the one responsible for their lives on the floating island.<ref>{{Cite|Let me share a bit of wisdom with you. The sky is filled with floating islands of all shapes and sizes. Skyloft just happens to be the one we call home. And no one knows when they were created or just how it is they stay airborne. The only thing I can tell you with any certainty is that we owe all of it--and that includes our life here--to the generosity of the goddess.|Croo|SS}}</ref> Croo also tells Link of "ring-shaped rocks," which the hero can use to propel himself through the sky.<ref>{{Cite|Let me tell you something that may come in handy. When you're out on the skies, you might see {{Color|SS Red|ring-shaped rocks}} now and again. I've heard there's some kind of unknown energy that builds up in the center of those big rocks. So get this: if you fly your Loftwing through a hole, you'll come blasting out like you were shot out of a cannon. I hear all the hotshot knights use this trick to cover huge distances in a hurry.|Croo|SS}}</ref> He also tells Link about the usefulness of [[Stamina Potion]]s that can be bought from the [[Potion Shop#Skyward Sword|Potion Shop]].<ref>{{Cite|Listen--here's a little information you may find useful. Are you using those {{Color|SS Gold|Stamina Potions}} they're selling over at the bazaar? It's great stuff! Drink some of that, and your stamina gauge doesn't empty out so darn fast. And it's not just for running, either! You can do spin attacks without getting tired so easily! I suggest you carry one around in case you tangle with something big and nasty.|Croo|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Croo's name is likely derived from {{wp|crow}}, and possibly the cooing of birds.
