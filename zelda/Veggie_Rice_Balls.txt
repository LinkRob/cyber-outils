{{Term/Store|Veggie Rice Balls|Veggie Rice Balls|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Veggie Rice Balls are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any vegetable, herb, or flower with {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make their default version either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}}, which offer no status effects, or by using multiple ingredients with contradicting effects. Eating them will heal some of Link's {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These dishes originate from {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} and are stuffed with many ingredients found in the mountains.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Several different variations of Veggie Rice Balls can be made, depending on the type of vegetable, herb, or flower used:

* '''Hearty Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. Link can eat them to heal all of his Hearts and will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using energizing ingredients. When eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be partially refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} or other enduring ingredients. Consuming these rice balls will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}, or other hasty ingredients. They can be eaten to boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}} or other spicy ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}} or other electric ingredients. One level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}} will be given to Link when he eats these meals.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}} or other mighty ingredients. By eating them, Link's attack power will be boosted.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}}, or other tough ingredients. Link's defense will be increased when he eats them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. If consumed, Link will receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Veggie Rice Balls''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Veggie Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This home-style dish of Kakariko Village is stuffed with the bounty of the mountains.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}} or other chilly ingredients. Link can eat these rice balls to gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>
