The {{Term/Store|Rusty Bell||Series, OoS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[File:RustyBell.png|thumb|left|{{Term|OoS|Link}} finding the {{Term|OoS|Rusty Bell}}]]
The {{Term|OoS|Rusty Bell}} is a ruined version of the {{Term|OoS|Pirate's Bell|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Surely this- Ar! This thing's rusted something awful! What are you pullin'? This won't do! Go get it polished!|Captain|OoS}}</ref> It was lost by the {{Plural|OoS|Piratian|link}} upon washing up in {{Term|OoS|Subrosia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It was so stormy that Cap'n lost the bell he got from a special someone. That's a bit o' trouble...|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> {{Term|OoS|Cap'n}} received it from his lover {{Term|OoS|Queen Ambi|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Cap'n got a special someone he can't forget. What's more, she's said to be the queen of some great land.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> and as such feels incapable of sailing away without it.<ref>{{Cite|With the Cap'n in such a state, we'll never get the ship out!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> {{Term|OoS|Link|link}}, with the help of a {{Term|OoS|Piratian}} skull, finds the {{Term|OoS|Rusty Bell}} buried in a cave in the {{Term|OoS|Samasa Desert|link}}.

However, {{Term|OoS|Cap'n}} will not accept the {{Term|OoS|Rusty Bell|display=Bell}} in its sorry state. {{Term|OoS|Link}} has it cleaned and reforged into the {{Term|OoS|Pirate's Bell|link}} at the {{Term|OoS|Subrosian Smithy|link}}, and returns it to the {{Term|OoS|Piratian}} crew. With the gift returned, {{Term|OoS|Cap'n}} is able to direct the crew out of {{Term|OoS|Subrosia}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ar! There's no mistakin' it! This bell is full o' memories--but that don't mean they're all good! Sniff! Now I'm all pensive. It's not like me. We're off, mateys! To sea!|Cap'n|OoS}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* Although the {{Term|OoS|Rusty Bell}} is always found in the same cave, the exact entrance to the cave will commonly change. Therefore, {{Term|OoS|Link}} needs the skull's help to find it.
