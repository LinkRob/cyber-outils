The {{Term/Store|Well-Worn Trousers|Well-Worn Trousers|Series, BotW}} are an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Well-Worn Trousers}}<br/>These old trousers are threadbare in spots, but they're surprisingly comfortable. The legs are a bit too short, though.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Well-Worn Trousers are a heavily-worn piece of [[Armor]] that were made long before [[Link]]'s awakening from the [[Slumber of Restoration]].<ref name="Inventory"/> Due to their weathered condition and short legs, they appear to not have been made for Link at his age.<ref name="Inventory"/> The Well-Worn Trousers provide no additional benefits from wearing them other than marginally boosting Link's defense. They can be obtained either from a stone [[Treasure Chest]] inside the [[Shrine of Resurrection]] or from [[Granté]] in [[Tarrey Town]] after the "[[From the Ground Up]]" [[Side Quest]] is completed.
