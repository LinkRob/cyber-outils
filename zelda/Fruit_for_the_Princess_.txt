{{Term/Store|Fruit for the Princess!||HWAoC}} is a level 37 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fruit for the Princess!|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Fruit for the Princess!}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 25 {{Plural|HWAoC|Wildberry|link}}, and 10 {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the objective is to retrieve three fruits by destroying boxes found in different parts of the map. The box in the top left corner of the map contains a {{Term|HWAoC|Wildberry|link}}, and after the box is destroyed, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} spawn behind the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. The box in the bottom right corner contains a {{Term|HWAoC|Hydromelon|link}}, and when obtained, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} appears. The box in the top right corner contains {{Term|HWAoC|Mighty Bananas|link}} and when destroyed, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} spawns. When all three boxes are destroyed, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} must then make their way to an exit in the bottom left corner of the map. Two {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} spawn on the path to the exit. As either {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the southern end of the map, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} and groups of {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} appear to steal the {{Term|HWAoC|Mighty Bananas}}. The same happens again when a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} gets close to the exit, although {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga|link}} spawns instead of one of the blademasters. Afterwards, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} and the blademasters, and when completed, a gate blocking the exit opens and the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} is completed.
