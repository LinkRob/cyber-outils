{{Term/Store|Kohm||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kohm|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kohm}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who manages the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. Her daughter, {{Term|BotW|Frelly|link}}, trains the {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} and manages the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq! The {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}} here were all trained by Frelly. They're faster, and they'll wait for you to return to them.|Essa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kohm}}, she introduces herself, reasoning that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} was talking about.<ref name="Mother"/> Kohm explains that they rent out Sand Seals and that their business is thriving.<ref>{{Cite|We run a shop where we rent out {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}}. If you plan on crossing the desert, you'll need a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}}!|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> While it seems unlikely, their business is actually thriving, which is aided by the fact that customers can easily learn how to ride {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|It may seem unlikely, but we're actually a thriving business. And it's so easy for a customer to get the hang of it!|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> The only thing {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to do is stand on his {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} will pull him through the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|All you have to do is just stand on your {{Color|BotW Red|shield}} and let the {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} pull you along!|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kohm}} also explains that due to their training, their {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} are easier to ride than wild {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, our {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}} have all been trained. They're much easier to handle than a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} you'd find in the wild.|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> She tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to let her know when he needs a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're in need, just let me know.|Kohm|BotW}}</ref>

The next time {{Term|BotW|Link}} visits {{Term|BotW|Kohm}}, she tells him that he will need a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} in order to cross the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're hoping to cross the vast Gerudo Desert, a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} is a must have!|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> She offers to rent him one for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's only {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} to rent one! What do you say?|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks how to "set seal," {{Term|BotW|Kohm}} explains that he will only need to guide his {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|[How do I set seal?] Once you've caught a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}}, all you need to do is guide it in the direction you want to go!|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> She claims that the best way to learn is through experience.<ref>{{Cite|And the best way to learn is by doing! Good luck!|Kohm|BotW}}</ref>

Whether {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses or accepts {{Term|BotW|Kohm}}'s offer, she will notice his {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} puns and wonder if that is how people speak outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|What? Is that how young people talk now? {{Small|I wonder if it's just a thing outside Gerudo Town...}}|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} chose not to rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}, {{Term|BotW|Kohm}} reminds him that he would be wise to take one if he travels outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway... The Gerudo Desert is vast. You'd be wise to take a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} if you're traveling beyond town.|Kohm|BotW}}</ref>

After hearing {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s puns, {{Term|BotW|Kohm}} will begin to incorporate them into her speech. She says that a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} is the best way to cross the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, claiming that {{Term|BotW|Link}} will "seal" what she means.<ref>{{Cite|If you need to cross the Gerudo Desert, a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} is the perfect way to do it! You'll seal what I mean!|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about riding {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} this time, she states that he will "seal" how easy it is to learn.<ref>{{Cite|And the best way to learn is by doing! It's easy—you'll seal! Good luck!|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}, {{Term|BotW|Kohm}} says it was great "sealing" him.<ref>{{Cite|Great sealing ya! Sav'orq!|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Kohm}} reminds him that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} "stretches as far as the eye can seal."<ref>{{Cite|The Gerudo Desert stretches as far as the eye can seal. You'd be wise to take a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} if you're traveling into it.|Kohm|BotW}}</ref>
