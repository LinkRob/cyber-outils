{{Term/Store|Lake Mekar||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lake Mekar'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Lake Mekar}} is the lake surrounding the island containing [[Korok Forest]] and the [[Lost Woods]]. The Lake also contains [[Mekar Island]]. The {{Term|BotW|Lake Mekar|display=Lake}} is bordered by [[Thyphlo Ruins]] to the north, [[Mount Drena]] to the west, the [[Military Training Camp]] to the south, and the [[Eldin Mountains]] to the east. The inner circumference of the lake is covered by the mist of the Lost Woods, and if Link enters this area he will be warped back to the last point where he stood on solid ground.
