{{Term/Store|Darknut|Darknuts|Series, LA, LADX, FS, TP, TPHD, LCT|plural}} are recurring enemies and [[Sub-Boss]]es in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 173 {{Exp Game|LA, LADX, FS, TP, TPHD}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Darknut Battle}}<br/>Move using the Control Stick, and defeat the Darknut before time runs out!|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Darknut|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Darknuts resemble large knights equipped with heavy armor, a large sword, and a wide shield. These foes share trite similarities to the [[Iron Knuckle]] enemy and the [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Boss]], though there are a few discernible differences, one being the Gothic appearance of Darknut's armor as opposed to the somewhat Eastern appearance of the Iron Knuckle's armor. Apart from that, the two have never appeared in a game together, aside from the non-canon {{TWoG}}, where Darknuts are a common enemy and an Iron Knuckle is a boss, and {{CoH}}, where they are enemies and sub bosses.

These enemies are masters of swordfighting, seen most vividly during battles fought with them in {{TP}}, where the differences in size between Link and the Darknut further emphasizes this accurate behavior.

Darknuts in {{OoS}} and {{OoA}} resemble those seen in {{LA|-}}, appearing in both red and blue variants. Those with swords will charge Link and deflect frontal attacks to a degree. Those without swords will instead throw spears at Link. 

A special [[Golden Darknut]] also appears in {{OoS}}.

In {{TP}}, Darknuts remain one of the most powerful enemies in the game, even more formidable than their {{TWW|-}} counterparts. They still retain some of their characteristics like removable armor and high defense, but now have increased speed and more sword techniques. Their armor has become less bulky and more streamlined like a [[Medieval elements in The Legend of Zelda Universe|medieval]] knight. In terms of equipment, they use either a large [[Sword]] or a mace in conjunction with a [[Shield]]. Midna does not know how to beat them, but suggests that Link should try using all of his sword techniques.<ref>{{Cite|I have no idea what to do against these knight things! Just try using the sword techniques you know, Link!|Midna|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The only thing to do is try all your sword techniques on it until you find one that works! Don't worry! You have what it takes to beat him, Link!|Midna|TP}}</ref>

Darknuts come in two phases, the first being armored. The best strategy is for Link to draw it towards him, backflip or sidestep (depending on if they use a vertical or horizontal swing) when they attack, and then land a [[Jump Slash]] to damage it and then quickly follow up with a combo. Its strikes will stagger Link if they connect while he is blocking with his shield, opening him up for follow-up attacks if it isn't alone, but it attacks slowly enough that it will not hit Link if he is constantly blocking. Avoiding its attacks by staying near the edge of its attack range and baiting it into attacking first will make it easier to dodge its attack completely and then counterattack. The [[Back Slice]], [[Helm Splitter]] and [[Mortal Draw]] are also effective if timed well. [[Bomb Arrow]]s can be used to remove a Darknut's armor from afar (or [[Bombling]]s provided Link angles them so as to not hit the Darknut head-on). but the Darknut's shield will soak up any arrows aimed at it directly. Link must instead target the ground immediately next to or behind the Darknut. When Link lands a hit on it, whatever weapon is used, one piece of its armor strips off, though not as quickly or in as large amounts as in {{TWW}}, so if Link is using Bomb Arrows, this can be costly in terms of ammunition used.

Eventually, when all of its armor is off and its shield is gone, revealing its chain mail under-armor, it proceeds to jump back and throw its weapon at Link. It then pulls out a smaller sword (a longsword), granting it different attacks, and becomes more agile, having shed its bulky armor: While it walks as fast as before, it spends more time circling Link and can attack faster than before, sometimes with very little warning. An unarmored Darknut tends to attack with two cuts in succession (which gives Link a split-second opening to counterattack), a thrust, or a kick which can briefly throw Link off balance. The Darknut also becomes far more adept at blocking Link's attacks and can dodge explosions and arrows effortlessly. [[Hidden Skill|Hidden Skills]] are essential in this phase. Jump attacks, [[Jump Strike|Jump Strikes]], [[Back Slice|Back Slices]], and [[Great Spin|Great Spins]] can send a Darknut reeling just long enough to attack. The latter three especially always break its guard, though it has a tendency to lunge forward and stab Link if he is readying a Jump Strike, so the attack must be performed when the Darknut is about at the edge of the attack's reach. If Link can perform a Great Spin, he can chain Spins and combos together to repeatedly stagger the Darknut until it is defeated; otherwise, repeated Back Slices can usually keep it staggered.

One Darknut with a different helmet, sword and shield is the mini-boss in the [[Temple of Time]], while others commonly guard items such as keys or new areas. Red and blue variants of the Darknut are also found in the [[Cave of Ordeals]]. A gold armored Darknut is found in [[Hyrule Castle]]. However, the differently colored equipment does not denote any change in the Darknut's physical attributes.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Darknut|A martial monster in sturdy armor. It is slow and plodding, but its armor makes it utterly immune to blunt-force attacks.<ref name="EoW Darknut">{{Cite|A martial monster in sturdy armor. Darknuts are heavily armored, knight-like enemies that move slowly and are resistant to many types of attacks. They appear in three forms, with [[Darknut Lv. 2]] and [[Darknut Lv. 3]] each becoming significantly stronger in both attack and defense. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Darknut [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], which costs three Echo energy to summon.

The Darknut also comes in a [[Dark Echo|Dark variant]] upon contact with a [[Tecuum]].

Darknuts are enemies in {{TFoE}}, and they wear yellow armor. A Darknut's armor allows it to withstand several sword slashes. {{Noncanon|End}}

==={{LCT|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
[[File:LCT Darknut Battle Sprite.png|thumb|right|Darknut from {{LCT|-}}]]
In {{LCT}}, a Darknut appears as the boss of Stage 8-3. Its appearance is identical to its {{TP|-}} incarnation, but now attacks by unleashing a swarm of insects in addition to sword combat. Its shield is invulnerable, requiring that Link wait until the Darknut attacks to shoot its armor off with the [[Crossbow]]. The armor appears to be enchanted, as each piece turns into a larger insect that attacks Link after it has been shot.

When all of the Darknut's armor is destroyed, it then proceeds to attack Link with its smaller sword, but also gains a teleportation move, in which it turns into a swarm of insects to move around and attack. After landing several consecutive hits, the Darknut then turns itself into a swarm of white insects that are each worth 100-300 points. By shooting these white insects, Link can whittle down the Darknut's health and defeat it.
{{Ambig|End}}

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
Darknuts appear in {{HW}} as [[Captain (Hyrule Warriors)|Captain]]s. Their design resembles the unique, lone Darknut from the Temple of Time in {{TP|-}}. Darknuts attack using their sword, and have two separate attacks that reveal their weak point gauge. One of them involves a red glow coming from around their eyes, followed up by a large upward sword thrust. This attack is special in that if you’re standing close enough to the Darknut while guarding, your guard will break when their eyes glow, so it’s best to avoid getting too close to one. Its over attack that reveals its weak point gauge involves it readying its sword and taking out its longsword, before unleashing multiple rapid-fire attacks with both blades. That’s your opening to use a boomerang on it, which will cause it to start slashing out of control at its allies, before revealing its weak point gauge. A Darknut’s weak point gauge can also be revealed by dodging behind it. An easy way to do this is attack it until its guard is up and then dodge behind it.

When defeated, they may drop a [[Piece of Darknut Armor]] and occasionally a [[Large Darknut Sword]]. Darknuts are affected by the VS. Undead Skill, implying that they are undead creatures.
{{Ambig|End}}

In {{TLoZ|TV Series}}, a Darknut appears in only one episode: "[[That Sinking Feeling]]". In this episode, [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] encounter a Darknut as they are journeying through the [[Underworld]]. The Darknut blocks most of Link's sword attacks, and when the Darknut swings his sword at the young hero, Link manages to evade it but falls in the process. Just as the Darknut is about to strike at him, Link kicks the foe's ankle and the Darknut falls, allowing the young hero to deliver the final blow. The Darknut leaves behind a [[Power Bracelet#Magic Bracelets|Magic Bracelet]], which proves to be useful later on in the battle against [[Ganon]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Look! Although Link tries to help Charles in the fight, he will be unable to draw his sword since it seems to be stuck to the sheath, forcing Zelda to fight with her dagger instead. The Darknut, once defeated by Charles's axe and Zelda's dagger, will leave behind a [[Red Darknut Arrow]].

==={{CoH|-}}===
Darknuts appear as {{Plural|CoH|Sub-Boss}} in {{CoH}} where they move to the beat of the music. They move faster than most {{Plural|CoH|Sub-Boss}} as they move on every beat, making them speedy yet powerful enemies despite wearing bulky armor. They also carry a shield that protects them from frontal attacks and can push the heroes backwards as they move, making them dangerous in areas where they can pin the heroes against walls or push them into holes and other hazards. Every few beats, they will lower their shield which will temporarily expose them to attack. When their shield is down, they will only swing their sword if the hero is either standing still or tries to run to their sides.

When the opportunity to strike the heroes arises, they will hold out their sword for one beat and 5 warning tiles will flash in a horseshoe formation before they swing their sword on the second beat, dealing damage to anything within 3 spaces in front of them and anything that was already standing at their sides.

Equipping the [[Ring of Piercing]], the [[Great Bow]], or [[Fragarach]] will enable the heroes to attack them through their shields.

==Trivia==
*These enemies are called [[Soldier (Enemy)|Soldiers]] in the English guide for {{LA}}, but were always known as Darknuts in the Japanese version.
*Excluding [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]], shooting [[Fire Arrow|Fire]] or [[Ice Arrow]]s at a Darknut's back and/or cape in {{TWW}} is amongst the least difficult ways to eliminate a Darknut.
*Darknuts in {{TMC}} generally carry their shields with their left hands, but their shield-hand changes when they face east. This is because the Darknut's sprite is being horizontally flipped, mirroring the graphic. This may be so Link can constantly attack Darknuts from the top instead of having to alternate between top and bottom every time they turn left or right.
*Golden and purple versions of Darknuts exist within the code of {{TMC|-}}, but go unused.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Golden and purple Darknuts exist within the game's code, but their behavior is very glitchy. Their moveset is the same as the black Darknut's.|author= |published= |retrieved= January 16, 2022|url= https://tcrf.net/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_The_Minish_Cap|title= The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap - The Cutting Room Floor|site= The Cutting Room Floor|type= }}</ref>
*[[Chief Soldier]]s are known as Darknuts in the Japanese version of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]''. In the English guide, [[Soldier (Enemy)|Soldiers]] are sometimes erroneously called Darknuts.
*The Darknut of the Temple of Time in {{TP}} has a Sword that resembles [[Ganon#Ganon's Swords|Ganondorf's Sword]] from the [[SpaceWorld 2000 GameCube Tech Demo]] and {{SSBM}}.
*The [[Hero's Spirit]], the restless soul of the [[Hero of Time]] who teaches Link the seven [[Hidden Skill|Hidden Skills]] in {{TP|-}}, resembles a Darknut as well as a [[Stalfos]].
*The Japanese name for a Darknut can be transcribed as "Tart Knuckle", implying some relation between Darknuts and Iron Knuckles, which are referred to as ''Aiannakku''. Darknuts and Iron Knuckles share this naming element with a number of other armored humanoid foes, including the boss [[Rebonack]] (''Rebonakku''), the [[Mad Bomber]] (''Bomu Nakku''), and the miniboss Darkhammer (''Hanmānakku'').
**Their name was likely changed in English due to a tart meaning an open pastry case containing a sweet or savory filling, which has no relevance to any characteristic of a Darknut.
*In ''[[Link's Crossbow Training]]'', the Darknut's ability to separate itself into swarms of insects is similar to [[Gomess]]' own ability of turning into bats seen in {{MM}}.
*The Darknuts from {{EoW}}, armed with axes, employ a fighting style reminiscent of the [[Iron Knuckle]] from {{OoT}}.
