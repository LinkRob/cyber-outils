{{Term/Store|Guide|Guides|Series|plural}} are a recurring mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 265}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300}}</ref> They are characters or objects that help {{Term|Series|Link|link}} at various points in his quest by providing either hints or outright advice. {{Plural|Series|Guide|plural}} typically communicate with {{Term|Series|Link}} via telepathy. They do not accompany {{Term|Series|Link}}, unlike [[Link's Partners|partners]].

The {{Term|Series|Gossip Stone|link}} is the most common {{Term|Series|Guide}}. Fortune tellers often serve as optional {{Plural|Series|Guide}} that charge for their services.
