The {{Term/Store|Dragon Cannon||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dragon Cannon|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Dragon Cannon}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of two {{Plural|ST|Pirate Necklace|link}}, one {{Term|ST|Goron Amber|link}} and nine {{Plural|ST|Wood Heart|link}}. Its design features an indeterminate segment of a Chinese dragon seen in {{Wp|Dragon dance}} parades; its description reads "It's hard to tell which part of the dragon this comes from. The chest?"

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Dragon Cannon}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Dragonhead Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
