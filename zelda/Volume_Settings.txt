The {{Term/Store|Volume Settings||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} menu is an interface in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Volume Settings|Settings|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Volume Settings}} menu is a menu that allows the player to change the settings regarding the game volume.<ref name="Settings"/> The {{Term|HWDE|Volume Settings}} can be accessed from the {{Term|HWDE|Settings|link}} menu. The {{Term|HWDE|Volume Settings}} consists of two slider bars and two buttons. The first slider bar controls the level of background music.<ref>{{Cite|BGM Volume|Volume Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Adjust background music volume.|Volume Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The second slider bar controls the level of sound effects.<ref>{{Cite|SFX Volume|Volume Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Adjust sound effects volume.|Volume Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The first button will reset the slider bars to their default state, which is both at maximum volume.<ref>{{Cite|Return to Default|Volume Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Reset current settings and restore to default.|Volume Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The second button will save the changes made and return the player to the {{Term|HWDE|Settings}} menu.<ref>{{Cite|Confirm changes and return to previous screen.|Volume Settings|HWDE}}</ref>
