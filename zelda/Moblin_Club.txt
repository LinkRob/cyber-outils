{{Plural|BotW|Moblin Club}} are wooden two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} favored by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are carved from sturdy {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, but their sloppy design makes them less {{Term|BotW|Durability|link|display=Durable}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Moblin Club}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

During "{{Term|BotW|The Weapon Connoisseur|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nebb|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to bring him a {{Term|BotW|Moblin Club}} to see, claiming his grandfather searched for one when he was young but could never find it.<ref>{{Cite|All right, the next weapon I wanna see is... What was it... Um... Oh, yes! That one! That one! Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Moblin club}}? So...when my grandpa was young, he looked for it but couldn't find it. So please! If you find it, please let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does so, {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|Moblin club}}? Nice! Nice! Let me see, let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks for lettin' me see! Take this as a thank you!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

Since they are made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Moblin Club}} will burn when set on {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They are unable to be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Outside of the settings, there is a difference in performance with a Gnarled Thick Stick that only looks like a weapon.
