The {{Term/Store|Mountain Title Deed||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
<!-- {{Color|MM3D Red|Mountain Title Deed}}<br/>The title deed for the Deku Flower in Goron Village. You traded your Swamp Title Deed for this. -->
The Mountain Title Deed is a claim to the [[Deku Flower]] located beside the [[Goron Shrine]] in [[Goron Village]]. If Link approaches the Deku Flower, a [[Business Scrub]] will emerge from it informing Link that he is interested in returning home to the [[Southern Swamp]].<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for stopping! Who'd have thought I'd meet a fellow Deku in a place like this? I sell {{Color|MM3D Red|bomb bags}}, but I'm focusing on my marketing efforts on Gorons. What I'd really like to do is go back home and do business where I'm surrounded by trees and grass. But I can't open a business back home without following the proper procedures... Do you know what I mean?|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> If Link presents the [[Swamp Title Deed]], the Business Scrub will offer his own Deku Flower in return.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! That's a {{Color|MM3D Red|Swamp Title Deed}}! I wish you would give that to me! I'll give you this land in exchange!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> By accepting this, the Business Scrub will give Link the Mountain Title Deed and leave for his new Deku Flower in the [[Southern Swamp]].<ref>{{Cite|You traded your Swamp Title Deed for a {{Color|MM3D Red|Mountain Title Deed}}!|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm going right back to the swamp!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>

With the Mountain Title Deed in Link's possession, he can freely use the Deku Flower in Goron Village to fly to ledges within range, such as the nearby ledge to the Goron Shrine entrance. Link can alternatively repurpose the Mountain Title Deed as toilet paper by giving it to {{Term|MM3D|??? (Majora's Mask)|link}} in the [[Stock Pot Inn]]'s bathroom after midnight any night.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|Crisis in the toilet!}}<br/>You helped the mysterious hand in the restroom. It's no good to be out of {{Color|MM3D Red|paper}} in the toilet!<br/>Stock Pot Inn - restroom|Bombers' Notebook|MM3D}}</ref> Link can also trade the Mountain Title Deed to the Business Scrub inside [[Lulu]]'s room at [[Zora Hall]] in exchange for the [[Ocean Title Deed]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that's a {{Color|MM3D Red|Mountain Title Deed}}! Thank you! I'll give you this land in exchange!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> In doing so, however, Link loses access to the Goron Shrine flower.<ref>{{Cite|Yahoo! I'm off to the resort! The mountains are calling me!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>
