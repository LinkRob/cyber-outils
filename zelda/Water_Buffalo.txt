{{Term/Store|Water Buffalo|Water Buffalo|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Water Buffalo}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Water Buffalo}}
Water Buffalo can be found near sources of water, where they're known to graze on grass.<ref>{{Cite|They live off grass that grows near the waterfront.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When approached from the front, they will charge at {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. When killed, they can drop [[Raw Meat]] or [[Raw Gourmet Meat]]. While they're wild animals, {{Plural|BotW|Hateno Cow|link}} are the domesticated version of Water Buffalo.<ref>{{Cite|Fun fact: the domesticated Hateno cow, often raised in villages, was bred through selective breeding using these.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>
