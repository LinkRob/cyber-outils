The {{Term/Store|Scent Seedling||Series, OoA}} is a quest item appearing in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 137}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
With the help of a certain [[Tokay]], it can be planted in [[Crescent Island]]'s "tree nursery" in the past.<ref>{{Cite|This is our precious tree nursery. They say a Scent Tree was planted here long ago. If I had a seedling, I'd plant it here.|Tokay|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey! Is that a Scent Seedling? Let's plant it!|Tokay|OoA}}</ref> The Tokay will then mention that it will take a very long time for the seed to become a tree.<ref>{{Cite|It's not like it's gonna get big right away. It takes a long, long time for a seed to become a tree.|Tokay|OoA}}</ref> He tells Link to "come back after a long, long time", and promises to nurture the tree in the meanwhile.<ref>{{Cite|Don't worry. I'll take care of it, so come back after a long, long time.|Tokay|OoA}}</ref> When [[Link]] returns to the tree nursery in the present, he will find a fully grown [[Mystical Tree|Scent Tree]]. Link can now use his newly-found [[Scent Seed]]s to trade back all the items that were stolen from him by the [[Tokay]] upon arriving on the island.

The Scent Seedling is obtained by successfully completing the [[Wild Tokay]] challenge, which costs 10 [[Rupee]]s to play.<ref>{{Cite|If you want this Scent Seedling, take the Wild Tokay challenge! It costs 10 Rupees|Wild Tokay|OoA}}</ref>
