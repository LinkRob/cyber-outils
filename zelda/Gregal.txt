{{Term/Store|Gregal||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Gregal & the Ghost}}
{{Term|TMC|Gregal}} is a member of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}} who is plagued by a {{Term|TMC|Ghost|link}}, and lies bedridden on the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)|link}} to the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}} appears near {{Term|TMC|Link's House|link}} in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)}} leads to a limited portion of the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}}, where {{Term|TMC|Link}} finds {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} in his bed. {{Term|TMC|Link}} can draw the {{Term|TMC|Ghost}} into the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}, saving {{Term|TMC|Gregal}}'s life; if he speaks to {{Term|TMC|Gregal}}, he will be rewarded with 100 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}}.

If the {{Term|TMC|Ghost}} is defeated, {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} makes a full recovery by the time {{Term|TMC|Link}} reaches the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} via the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}}. If spoken to then, {{Term|TMC|Link}} will receive the {{Plural|TMC|Light Arrow|link}}. There is a limited time to do this, however; if {{Term|TMC|Link}} arrives at the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} by way of {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}} before saving {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} from the {{Term|TMC|Ghost}}, {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} will pass away, making the {{Term|TMC|Light Arrow}} unobtainable.

If {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} is saved, he becomes one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure, why not?|Gregal|TMC}}</ref>
