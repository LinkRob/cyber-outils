==Location and uses==
[[File:AlttP Link carrying Super Bomb.webp|left|thumb|235x235px|Link carrying the Super Bomb in the Dark World]]
The Super Bomb, as its name suggests, is a red, larger version of the [[Bomb]]. It is a special, experimental Bomb developed by the [[Bomb Shopkeeper]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP&FS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 8}}</ref> After rescuing the [[Dwarven Swordsmith]] and completing the [[Ice Palace]] and the [[Misery Mire (A Link to the Past)|Misery Mire]], [[Link]] can purchase the Super Bomb from the [[Dark World]]'s [[Bomb Shop#A Link to the Past|Bomb Shop]] for 100 [[Rupee]]s, but he can only buy one at a time.<ref name="name price" /> After purchase, instead of holding the Super Bomb in his inventory, Link will carry the Super Bomb with him and it will detonate after 3 seconds wherever he places it. Unlike traditional Bombs, the explosion cannot hurt Link. The young hero cannot take the Super Bomb over cliff edges and he cannot [[Dash Attack]] with it. It is, however, able to damage [[Enemy|enemies]]. 

The Super Bomb creates a large explosion that can break stronger cracked walls. It is needed in order to break the cracked wall on the second level of the [[Pyramid of Power]], where the [[Cursed Fairy]] resides. Link can obtain the [[Golden Sword]] and [[Silver Arrow]]s here, the latter of which is needed in order to defeat [[Ganon]].

== Trivia ==

* In the Japanese version, the Super Bomb is known as the '''Big Bomb''' or '''New Bomb'''.
* ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' remains its only appearance in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] in spite of some fans wanting to see this item appear again in a future game.
* The [[Powder Keg]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' functions similarly to the Super Bomb in that it destroys large, certain obstacles a normal Bomb could not such as massive boulders, and can only carry one at a time.
