The {{Term/Store|''History of the Zora, Addendum 1''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|History of the Zora, Addendum 1<br/>King Dorephan Stands His Ground|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|History of the Zora, Addendum 1}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}} found upon {{Term|BotW|Ruto Mountain|link}}. It is an addition to the original {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monument}} commission made by a stonemason, who believed {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link|display=Prince Sidon}} deserve to have their achievements remembered.<ref>{{Cite|While I was at it, I thought...why not add two of my own? And so I created one for King Dorephan and one for Prince Sidon. True, this is outside the scope of my commission... But I believe their triumphs deserve as much! But why stop at that...|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref> As is the case with the other {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monuments}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must read this one to complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}".

One hundred years after {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} ascended to the throne, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} entered {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Around 100 years after King Dorephan ascended to the throne, a stray Guardian crossed Upland Zorana into our domain.|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|BotW}}</ref> Though the soldiers struggled against it, {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} was able to throw it into a ravine.<ref>{{Cite|The Guardian seemed unstoppable. Our best soldiers' spears barely left a scratch on its metal hull. It was then that our King Dorephan, without a thought for his own safety, came out to face the Guardian himself. With supernatural strength, he lifted the Guardian and hurled it into a ravine.|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} shattered into pieces, and since then, the trust and respect the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} held for their king grew.<ref>{{Cite|The citizens celebrated the king's valor. From then on, their trust and respect for him grew beyond compare.|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} still bears the scar from this battle.<ref>{{Cite|You can still see the scar he earned that day on his forehead—a token of his triumph.|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|BotW}}</ref>
