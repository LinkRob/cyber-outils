The '''Sanctum''' {{Exp Game|BotW, TotK}} is a recurring location in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' series.<ref>{{Cite|'''Sanctum'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> This place served as the Throne Room of [[Hyrule Castle]] and is notably where [[Princess Zelda]] contained [[Calamity Ganon]] for a whole century.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}} is the highest structure in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. On {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}, it is marked as the location of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
{{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}} can be found at the end of the spire on top of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} interacts with them, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>

===Bell Tower===
{{Main|Bell Tower}}
The {{Term|BotW|Bell Tower}} is the highest level of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}, and it features a large bell.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 252}}</ref>

===Champions' Hall===
{{Main|Champions' Hall}}
{{Term|BotW|Champions' Hall}} is located on the second floor of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}. The top of {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon|link}} can be accessed from this location.

===Sanctum Observation Room===
{{Main|Sanctum Observation Room}}
The {{Term|BotW|Sanctum Observation Room}} is the third level of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}, created with the purpose of viewing {{Term|BotW|Central Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 253}}</ref>

===Throne Room===
{{Main|Throne Room}}
The {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}} makes up the first floor of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}. It is where {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} has been holding back {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} for the past 100 years, and it is also the location of his {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon|link|display=Cocoon}}.<ref name="CaC"/> Entering this room will initiate {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s battle against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}.
