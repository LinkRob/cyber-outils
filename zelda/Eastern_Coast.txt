The {{Term/Store|Eastern Coast||Series, OoS}} is a small beach on the southern border of [[Holodrum]] in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 251}}</ref> It is located south of the [[Eastern Suburbs]] and is the only route to the eastern [[Samasa Desert]]. The only object of interest on the Eastern Coast is the gate barring the entrance to the desert.<ref name="Guide 46">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 46}}</ref> To open it, Link must push the four skull-shaped panels on the gate in the same order shown to him by the [[Piratian]]s at the [[House of Pirates]] in [[Subrosia]].<ref name="Guide 46"/>  The correct combination for opening the gate is: the third panel from the left twice, the second panel once, the first panel twice, and the fourth panel three times.<ref name="Guide 46"/> 

Earlier on in the game, a lone Piratian can be found standing near the gate. The Piratian was sent to look for the Bell, but wishes someone could take his place so he could to return to his comrades and help dig them out of Subrosia.<ref>{{Cite|I want to get back to all me mates and dig the ship free, but Cap'n said, "Don't come back 'til ya find me bell"! Can't someone take me place..?|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> He gladly lets Link take his place once the hero has permission from the [[Cap'n]] to search for the [[Pirate's Bell|Bell]].<ref>{{Cite|You'll look for the bell?!? Then I can go back! Thank'ee!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> Aside from this, the area is uninhabited and encloses only a few palm trees. Like the desert, this area has no [[Tree Stump|season stumps]] and remains in summer at all times.

In a [[Linked Game]], [[Queen Ambi]] appears on the coast staring out to sea, pining for her sea-faring lover, [[Cap'n]].<ref>{{Cite|As I stand here watching the sea, I recall him. Yes, that seafaring man. But a a child like you wouldn't understand my feelings.|Queen Ambi|OoS}}</ref>
