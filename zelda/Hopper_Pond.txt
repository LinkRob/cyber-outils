{{Term/Store|Hopper Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hopper Pond'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Hopper Pond is a small body of water found west of the Forest of Spirits on the [[Great Plateau]]. A group of [[Bokoblin]]s have established an encampment beside the Pond, guarding a [[Treasure Chest]]. Upon clearing the camp of its residents, the Treasure Chest will unlock, allowing [[Link]] to claim the [[Opal]] inside. A [[Seared Steak]] can be found roasting on a spit over a [[Campfire]] in the encampment.
