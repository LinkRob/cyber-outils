{{Term/Store|Outpost Defenders||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 26 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Outpost Defenders|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Outpost Defenders}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must defend two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} for eight minutes. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the eight minutes pass. Two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} and a large amount of {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}} are present at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, along with two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. Occasionally, {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, and more {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin}} spawn. Eventually, one {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox}} spawns near each {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends either after eight minutes or when the {{Plural|HWAoC|Hinox}} are killed. {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Radish|link}}, and 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupees|link}}.
