==Overview==
In {{Term|TotK|Gerudo Canyon|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} discovers a {{Term|TotK|Yiga Clan|link}} marked hideout and enters. Inside, there is a {{Term|TotK|Yiga Blademaster|link}} at station, who is taking in new potential Blademaster recruits through a test. Link accepts the test. He is given a task to journey across the canyon to find Yiga-marked frog statues and make offerings of {{Plural|TotK|Mighty Bananas|link}}.

While disguised,{{Verify}}<!--I'm not sure about this. I placed bananas at the statue while hot-swapping my armor with Yiga Clan's.--> Link traveled toward these locations of the frog statues and placed the bananas to all of them. After returning to the Blademaster, he congratulated Link for passing the test and allowed him passage behind the door, then asked him to return to the Yiga Clan Hideout as a full-fledged blademaster.

Entering the door behind, Link finds two treasure chests<!--Ruby and intact Eightfold Longblade--> and a {{Term|TotK|Shrine|link}}.
