{{Term/Store|Puppet Ganon|Puppet Ganons|Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a {{Term|TWWHD|Boss|link}} in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 199}}</ref> The three-stage battle is a precursor to the finale with {{Term|TWWHD|Ganondorf|link}} atop {{Term|TWWHD|Ganon's Tower|link}}, making this foe the penultimate boss of the game.

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TWW|Puppet Ganon}}

===First Form: Humanoid Boar===
[[File:Puppet Ganon Pig.png|left|thumb|249x249px|Puppet Ganon's first phase]]
{{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}'s first form, '''Humanoid Boar''',<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 257}}</ref> is reminiscent of {{Term|TWWHD|Ganon|link}}'s traditional [[Demon|demonic]] form. {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}'s movement is restricted; his appendages hang from the ceiling by cords. {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}} damages {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} by spinning his tail and flailing his long-reaching arms, and by releasing {{Plural|TWWHD|Keese|link}} as a distraction (which can be identified when he spins around continuously). {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can cut the cords with the {{Term|TWWHD|Boomerang|link}}, similar to the battle with {{Term|TWWHD|Kalle Demos|link}} in the [[Forbidden Woods]]. After {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} cuts all blue cords attached to {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}, he hangs suspended on the central red cord, dragging the ground. To damage {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} must shoot a {{Term|TWWHD|Light Arrow|link}} at the blue ball at the end of {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}'s tail to damage him. However, the blue ball is always revealed to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}, so cutting the cords is not required. After three hits, {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}} is seemingly defeated and changes form.
 
===Second Form: Bug-Ganon===
[[File:Puppet Ganon Spider.png|right|thumb|257x257px|Puppet Ganon's second phase]]
{{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}'s second form, '''Bug-Ganon''',<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 258}}</ref> resembles a large spider; he flies up to the top of the room periodically, spinning rapidly before crashing back down above {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}'s latest position. While in the air, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can use the shallow water in the room as a mirror to see where {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}} will fall. Once again, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} must shoot a {{Term|TWWHD|Light Arrow}} at the blue ball on {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}'s body to damage him. After three hits, {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}} changes form again.

===Final Form: Worm===
[[File:PuppetMoldorm WW.jpg|left|thumb|260x260px|Puppet Ganon's final phase]]
The final form of {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}, '''Worm''',<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 259}}</ref> is reminiscent of the many iterations of {{Term|TWWHD|Moldorm|link}} throughout [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]. {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}} moves erratically around the room, gaining speed each time {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} strikes the blue ball at the end of his tail with a {{Term|TWWHD|Light Arrow}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}} is stunned whenever {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} attacks his face with the {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword|link}}, Light Arrows, {{Plural|TWWHD|Bomb|link}}, or the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer|link}} (each have varying duration of being stunned). The monster even becomes distracted at the sight of {{Term|TWWHD|All-Purpose Bait|link}}; When the bait is thrown, {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}} quickly moves towards it, damaging {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} if he is in the way. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} may best {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}} if he uses the {{Term|TWWHD|Hookshot|link}} to reach high platforms in the room. With this method, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} may shoot at the boss without fear of damage.

After three hits to this form, {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}} is truly defeated. After defeating the final form of {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}, Ganondorf, carrying [[Princess Zelda]] ([[Tetra]]) appears above and tells {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}, the Hero of Winds, that he is the [[Hero of Time]] reborn. The young hero then climbs the room's central cord after Ganondorf to the tower's rooftop; it is here that he faces the villain for the final time.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon}}'s second form vaguely resembles the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ushi-oni Ushi-Oni] from Japanese folklore.
*In the [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker — Prima's Official Strategy Guide|Prima Games official strategy guide]], Puppet Ganon's second and final forms are referred to as "Tarantula" and "Snake".
*Some fans of the game considered Puppet Ganon's Worm form to be one of the toughest battles in ''The Wind Waker'', due to its aggressive and unpredictable movement.
