{{Term/Store|Fortune-Teller|Fortune-Tellers|Series|plural}},{{Fact}} also stylized as {{Term/Store|Fortune Teller|Fortune Tellers|ALttP|plural}},{{Exp Game|ALttP}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref> are recurring characters in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:FortuneTeller.gif|left|30px]]
In {{ALttP}}, three Fortune Tellers can be found in their {{Plural|ALttP|Fortune-Teller's House|link|display=Houses}} in both the {{Term|ALttP|Light World|link|display= Light}} and the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}}. One can be found north of {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village|link}}, another one next to the {{Term|ALttP|Lake Hylia Shop|link}}, and a third one north of the {{Term|ALttP|Village of Outcasts|link}}. By paying between 10 and 30 {{Plural|ALttP|Rupee|link}}, the {{Term|ALttP|Fortune Teller}} can give {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} advice on how to proceed in his adventure. {{Term|ALttP|Link|display= Link's}} {{Plural|ALttP|Heart|link}} are also fully refilled.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Main|Madame Fanadi}}
{{Term|TP|Madame Fanadi}} can be found in {{Term|TP|Fanadi's Palace|link}} in {{Term|TP|Hyrule Castle Town|link}}, where she offers fortunes for 10 {{Plural|TP|Rupee|link}} each. These will either hint to {{Term|TP|Link}} where his next destination should be or where a {{Term|TP|Piece of Heart|link}} can be found, depending on whether "Career" or "Love" is chosen.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Fortune-Teller}} from {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} is located north of {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}} in {{ALBW}}. He gives Link the {{Term|ALBW|Hint Glasses|link}} used to find the {{Plural|ALBW|Hint Ghost|link}}. Another {{Term|ALBW|Fortune-Teller}} can be found north of {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
About 100 years before the events of {{BotW}}, shortly after {{Term|BotW|Zelda|link}} was born, a Hylian {{Term|BotW|Fortune Teller}} trusted by the Royal Family predicted the resurrection of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Fortune Teller}} mentioned also that the power to oppose {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} lay dormant beneath the ground. In response, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} ordered the excavation of large areas of land. Thus, the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} were discovered. The Fortune Teller's identity and their fate after Ganon's return are unknown, though some believe they were a high-ranking official, a trusted aid to the King, or even the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule|link}} herself.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 372}}</ref><ref name="CaC 374">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 374}}</ref>

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Main|Astor}}
{{Term|HWAoC|Astor}} is a {{Term|Series|Fortune-Teller}}, referred to by the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga|link}} leaders as "Seer" and "Prophecy Man".<ref>{{Cite|You are blind, "seer." Calamity Ganon...is not for mortals to control.|Sooga|HWAoC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, prophecy man! You missed the part where we get walloped!|Master Kohga|HWAoC}}</ref> Through means such as {{Term|HWAoC|Harbinger Ganon|link}} he is able to observe the {{Term|HWAoC|Great Calamity|link}} before its occurrence and works to bring it into existence. However, due to the interference of {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako|link|display=Terrako}}, this future is ultimately averted and he is consumed by Harbinger Ganon to fuel its transformation into Calamity Ganon.
{{Noncanon|end}}
