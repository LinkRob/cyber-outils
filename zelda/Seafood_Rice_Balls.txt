{{Term/Store|Seafood Rice Balls|Seafood Rice Balls|Series, BotW, TotK, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Seafood Rice Balls by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any seafood together with {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default can be made either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Bass|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}}, which provide no status effects, or by using two types of seafood with contradicting effects. When eaten, they will restore some of Link's {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Though the flavor varies based on ingredients, these rice balls never disappoint.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By using certain types of seafood or adding extra ingredients, the following variations of Seafood Rice Balls can be made:

* '''Hearty Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. By eating them, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}, or other energizing ingredients. They can be eaten to restore a part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely replenished and overfilled when he eats these.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using hasty ingredients. If consumed, Link will receive a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or other spicy ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other electric ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, or other mighty ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or other tough ingredients. By eating them, Link's defensive abilities will be increased.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. Link can eat them to receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Seafood Rice Balls''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Seafood Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Stuffed with aromatic seafood, the flavor can vary by ingredients but never disappoints.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other chilly ingredients. They can be consumed to offer Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
