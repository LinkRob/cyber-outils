The {{Term/Store|Cursed Medal||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 120}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The cursed medal can be found in a chest at [[Batreaux's house]] after giving 30 [[Gratitude Crystal|gratitude crystals]] to [[Batreaux]]. While [[Link]] is carrying it in his [[Pouch|Adventure Pouch]], [[Rupee|Rupees]] and [[Treasure|Treasures]] will appear more often. while link cannot use anything in his pouch, such as [[Empty Bottle|bottles]] or [[Shield|shields]]. Depositing this item at the [[Item Check]] will allow link to use his pouch once again. Like other pouch items, this has no effect during the [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Round]].
