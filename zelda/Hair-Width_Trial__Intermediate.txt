{{Term/Store|Hair-Width Trial: Intermediate||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Hair-Width Trial: Intermediate}} is a level 29 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} loses all of their health in one hit and no healing items appear on the battlefield. It can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Opal|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}. At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat 50 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}, which are all {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}}. After the objective is reached, a {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} is defeated.
