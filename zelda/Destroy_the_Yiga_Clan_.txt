{{Term/Store|Destroy the Yiga Clan!||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}


== Overview ==
In this {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Champions|link}} {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}} first appears to use {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} to defeat the monsters and {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga|link|display=Yigas}} surrounding the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}, and smash through the walls.{{Fact}}

After this, {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} go inside and meet with a {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo|link}} Scout, who pleads that they rescue her friend.{{Fact}} Once the duo rescue the trapped friend, they learn of a secret door, and meet {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga|link}}, the right-hand man to {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga|link}}. Once he reaches half health, he will retreat.{{Fact}}

{{Term|HWAoC|Link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} will then reach the goal, where a battle against {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} will commence.{{Fact}} After {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} loses half of his health, {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} will appear, retaining his health from before. Once {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} is defeated, both Kohga and {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} will retreat.{{Fact}}

In a cutscene, {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} are talking to {{Term|HWAoC|Astor|link}}.{{Fact}} {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} threatens to kill {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}} after the defeat of the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga}}.{{Fact}} However, {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}} says that fated destined the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga}}'s failure,{{Fact}} and reminds {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} that if they were to kill him, they would make an enemy of {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|link}}.{{Fact}} {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} then releases {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}}, who begins to laugh.{{Fact}}
