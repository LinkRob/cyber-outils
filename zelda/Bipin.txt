{{Term/Store|Bipin||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}} & 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Manual|OoS|Bipin & Blossom|Bipin is a famed tree planter who grows Gasha Seeds and freely offers information about them to curious travelers. Blossom is his wife. Together they have a young child who is full of promise. What kind of man will this child grow up to be?}}
{{Manual|OoA|Bipin & Blossom|Bipin is a famed tree planter who grows Gasha Seeds and freely offers information about them to curious travelers. Blossom is his wife. Together they have a young child who is full of promise. What kind of man will this child grow up to be?}}
Bipin is a tree planter who is knowledgeable about [[Gasha Seed]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I am Bipin, the expert arborist! I know plenty about trees.|Bipin|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Bipin is a famed tree planter who grows Gasha Seeds, and freely offers information about them to curious travelers.|game= OoA|page= 9}}</ref> He lives in [[Horon Village]] in {{OoS}} and in [[Lynna City]] in {{OoA}} with his wife, [[Blossom]], and their newborn [[Bipsom|son]], whose name is chosen by [[Link]].<ref name= "Son">{{Cite|I am having trouble choosing a name. Can you help me think of one? What would you call him?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref>

As Link continues to help raise his son, Bipin will offer advice concerning Gasha Trees and their nuts and seeds.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 17}}</ref> Bipin is either seen with his family in their living quarters, or, when all is well with his son, on the east side of his house tending to his indoor Gasha farm.

Once a game is completed, Bipin and his family move to the next one, where they will meet Link again in the [[Linked Game]].<ref>{{Cite|Off to Holodrum! Bipin & Blossom|Sign|OoA}}</ref> In {{OoS|-}}, Bipin and his family will reside in southwestern [[Horon Village]]. In {{OoA|-}}, they will reside in a house in the northern part of [[Lynna City]].<ref>{{Cite|Link! It's been too long! I, too, have come to this village! It's nice to see a familiar face!|Bipin|OoS}}</ref> There, their son continues to grow up and eventually becomes an adult.
