The {{Term/Store|Hero's New Clothes|Hero's New Clothes|Series, TWW, TWWHD, PH|plural}} are recurring clothing in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 127 {{Exp Game|TWW, PH}}}}</ref>

==Appearances==
[[File:HerosNewClothesWW.png|180px|left|thumb|Receiving the Hero's New Clothes in {{TWW|-}}]]
==={{TWW|-}}===
During the {{Term|TWW|Second Quest|link}} of {{TWW}}, {{Term|TWW|Link|link}} receives the {{Term|TWW|Hero's New Clothes}} from his {{Term|TWW|Grandma|link}} instead of the {{Term|TWW|Hero's Clothes|link}}. The clothes are invisible, meaning {{Term|TWW|Link}} will be seen wearing his starting outfit for the rest of his adventure.<ref name="TWW Guide 123">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 123}}</ref>

Although the {{Term|TWW|Hero's New Clothes}} are invisible, as it is said they can only be seen by honest people,<ref name="TWW Guide 123"/> some characters comment about {{Term|TWW|Link}}'s new outfit. Such is the case during the conversation with the {{Term|TWW|Great Deku Tree|link}}. As the {{Term|TWW|Second Quest}} features all [[Hylian Language]] translated, the {{Term|TWW|Great Deku Tree}} refers to {{Term|TWW|Link}}'s clothing as the clothes of the legendary hero.<ref>{{Cite|That garb you wear... Could you be the legendary hero? Has the king at long last found the Hero of Time?|Great Deku Tree|TWW}}</ref>

{{clear}}

==={{PH|-}}===
The {{Term|PH|Hero's New Clothes}} appear in {{PH}} as part of the game's [[Phantom Hourglass Trading Sequence|Trading Sequence]]. They can be obtained from the {{Term|PH|Man of Smiles|link}} after visiting him for the first time. The {{Term|PH|Hero's New Clothes|display=Clothes}} must be traded for the {{Term|PH|Telescope|link}} from {{Term|PH|Nyeve|link}}. Although the {{Term|PH|Hero's New Clothes|display=Clothes}} are invisible, {{Term|PH|Nyeve}} senses them in {{Term|PH|Link}}'s possession,<ref>{{Cite|You wouldn't happen to have a set of {{Color|PH Red|those}}, would you? Don't try to hide it! I'm still the hero, you know! I SENSE things!|Nyeve|PH}}</ref> stating that he has been long searching for such an item.<ref>{{Cite|Would it be possible to convince you to give those to me? That's a collector's item that my fans go absolutely crazy over! I've been looking around for those forever!!|Nyeve|PH}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*In {{TWW|-}}, while wearing the {{Term|TWW|Hero's New Clothes}}, {{Term|TWW|Link}}'s hat can be seen outlined in multiple occasions, such as while using the {{Term|TWW|Magic Armor|link}}, and in the bubble covering {{Term|TWW|Link}} during the final cutscene.
*The {{Term|Series|Hero's New Clothes}} are an allusion to "{{wp|The Emperor's New Clothes}}", in which two swindlers promise a vain emperor a set of magical clothes that they claim can only be seen by those who deserve their jobs. Although the "clothes" are fake and the emperor is, in reality, naked, his people lie to him about seeing the clothes, out of fear of losing their jobs. It is only when one young boy points out that he really is naked do the people realize everyone had been fooled.
