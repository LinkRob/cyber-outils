The {{Term/Store|Rito Stable||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Rito Stable'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} that is located in the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}, not far from {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Stable}} features a lumber yard behind the yurt, with several felled trees and two {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe|link}}s, along with with bundles of {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}} on a cart. A {{Term|BotW|Wildberry|link}} bush grows near one of the log piles. A {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} can be found in front of the feeding trough. Several cargo containers and a covered wagon sit near a lone tree by the crossroads, with a {{Term|BotW|Pot Lid|link}} propped against one of the crates.

The stable is run by {{Term|BotW|Galli|link}}, who mans both its {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} services and lodging. As with all other {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} can occasionally be found here selling his wares. Out front, {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} can be found playing his accordion, while {{Term|BotW|Ariane|link}} sweeps the grounds. Both {{Term|BotW|Ponthos|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lester|link}} can be found seated at the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, the latter starting the side quest "{{Term|BotW|Curry for What Ails You|link}}".

The recipe for {{Term|BotW|Spicy Meat Stew|link}} can be found on the wall inside the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.
