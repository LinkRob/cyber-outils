{{Term/Store|Messenger's Escort||HWAoC}} is a Level 24 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Messenger's Escort|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Messenger's Escort}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Courser Bee Honey|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to escort a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}} to a Targeted Position. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} reaches a certain point, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}} and many {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}} spawn. As the captain moves even further, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Moblin}} spawn along with {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos|link}}. Just before reaching the Targeted Position, a {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox}} appears. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once it is defeated and the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} reaches the Targeted Position.
