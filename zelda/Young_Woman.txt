The {{Term/Store|Young Woman||Series}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
===Hyrule===
The Young Woman is the caretaker of the [[Cucco]]s in [[Kakariko Village#A Link Between Worlds|Kakariko Village]]. She seems quite knowledgeable about them, speaking highly of their gliding ability and the nutritional value of their eggs.<ref>{{Cite|If you jump from a really high place while holding a Cucco, it's almost like you can fly!|Young Woman|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! Don't forget to eat a good breakfast. Cucco eggs are the best way to start the day!|Young Woman|ALBW}}</ref> She is very protective of her Cuccos, becoming annoyed when the village children take them,<ref>{{Cite|Some naughty child grabbed one of my Cuccos. He probably wanted to jump off a roof with it. Gliding with a Cucco is fun, but I can't stand it when kids take MY Cuccos!|Young Woman|ALBW}}</ref> and worrying about them vanishing - after how several [[Sages#A Link Between Worlds|people]] vanished from [[Hyrule]] shortly after [[Link]] obtains the [[Pendants of Virtue|Three Pendants]].<ref>{{Cite|It's awful-just AWFUL. People are going missing across the kingdom! Oh, I can't bear to think of it! What if my Cuccos vanished? Now that would be truly dreadful!|Young Woman|ALBW}}</ref> A few Cuccos reside in the pen behind her house.

===Lorule===
The Young Woman who lives in [[Lorule]] shares her Hylian counterpart's love for Cuccos. She mourns over the inexplicable destruction of their coop, and promises to protect the remaining eggs she holds from any further harm.<ref>{{Cite|Poor little Cuccos. Their coop got all smashed up. All that's left is these eggs... Don't worry little eggies, I'll make sure nothing happens to you.|Young Woman|ALBW}}</ref> Since her home was destroyed, she can be found in a corner of Lorule's [[Milk Bar]].
