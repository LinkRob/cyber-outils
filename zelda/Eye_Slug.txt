{{Term/Store|Eye Slug|Eye Slugs|Series, PH|plural}} are enemies in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 176}}</ref> It is a small, snake-like creature that charges at [[Link]] during his journey through the [[Goron Temple]]. They also appear throughout the battle with [[Dongorongo]] trying to distract Link, as he is far removed from the actual battle by a river of sand, of which the Eye Slugs can cross. These foes usually appear in groups and are fast, although they can easily be defeated with a strike from Link's [[sword]]. If Link does manage to get hit by them, they deal minimal damage.

As Dongorongo may be an evolved form of [[Dodongo]], Eye Slugs may in fact be a young form of Dongorongo, as [[Baby Dodongo]]s were a young form of Dodongo.
