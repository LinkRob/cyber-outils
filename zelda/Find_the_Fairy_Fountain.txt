"{{Term/Store|Find the Fairy Fountain||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Find the Fairy Fountain'''|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can receive this Quest by restoring the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Pikango|link}} has heard of a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} that is beautiful and can bestow people with a mysterious power.<ref>{{Cite|Just between you and me... I hear there's a {{Color|BotW Red|Great Fairy Fountain}} somewhere near this village. I hear it's breathtakingly beautiful. Aesthetic aside, they also say the place can bestow people with some kind of mysterious power...|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> Through his research, he found a place that he believes to be suspicious.<ref>{{Cite|I've been researching it for ages, and I finally have a lead. There's a place that is beyond suspicious...|Pikango|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I've done a bit of research regarding the {{Color|BotW Red|Great Fairy Fountain}}, and I finally found a suspicious spot!|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> However, he can not find the Fountain in the area,<ref>{{Cite|The problem is, I can't seem to find the fountain there.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> and he has difficulty getting there.<ref>{{Cite|However...it's really hard to get there. Honestly, I'm not sure I can do it.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> If Link has spoken to him before, he states that nothing is better than tracking down a perfect view and asks if Link understands.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* Nothing beats the thrill of tracking down a perfect view. As a fellow traveler ,you understand the sentiment, right?|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> If Link has not spoken to him, Pikango expresses a desire to see a picture of the Fountain.<ref>{{Cite|*SIGH* Even if it's just a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}}, I'm dying to see it...|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

When Link agrees with Pikango's sentiment or offers to help him, he is relieved that someone else appreciates beauty.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I knew it! I just KNEW there was someone like me nearby who appreciates true beauty.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> He asks Link if he wants to come with to investigate the location,<ref>{{Cite|Sadly, I can't pin down the exact location of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy Fountain}}. I haven't put it all together yet, but there's a suspicious place I have my eye on... In fact, I was about to head there now. Would you like to come along?|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> telling him to follow along.<ref>{{Cite|Great answer! Just what I expected from a fellow beauty connoisseur like you. All right then. Follow me!|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> At this point, the "Find the Fairy Fountain" Quest activates.

Just past {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg Shrine|link}}, Pikango becomes exhausted and can no longer continue.<ref>{{Cite|So...tired... Hills aren't easy for old fogies like me.Sorry, but I don't think I can go on... *pant*|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> He tells Link that the Great Fairy Fountain should be along the path going past from the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Great Fairy Fountain}} should be... {{Color|BotW Blue|along the path that leads past the shrine}}. *pant* That's what I've been told, anyway. Yeesh, I'm completely out of breath! That always happens right about here.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> Pikango decides that he should rest, sending Link ahead.<ref>{{Cite|*pant* I think I'd better rest for a bit. You go on ahead.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|*pant* The weather is awful, too, so I think I'd better rest for a bit.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> He reminds Link to take a picture of the Great Fairy Fountain if he finds it.<ref>{{Cite|And hey, if you find it...don't forget to get a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}}!|Pikango|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But hey, if you find it...don't forget to get a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}}!|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

Once Link reaches the fountain, he can take a picture to show to Pikango. He tells Link that he hopes to see the Great Fairy Fountain one day.<ref>{{Cite|Someday I hope I can see that place spoken of in whispers, veiled in mystery...the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy Fountain}}...|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> Pikango is shocked to hear that Link has found it and demands to see the picture.<ref>{{Cite|[Oh, I found it.] Ahhh! You...you found it?! Really?! Are you serious right now?! And if you are, then were's the {{Color|BotW Red|picture}}? You remember to bring the {{Color|BotW Red|picture}}, right?|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> However, when he sees the picture, Pikango is disappointed that it is not as sacred and elegant as he thought it would be.<ref>{{Cite|... This...is the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy Fountain}}? It's certainly beautiful, but...I thought it would be more...sacred and elegant.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> He is still impressed that Link found it, though.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I'm impressed you found it.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

While Pikango wants to give Link a reward, he does not have anything on him.<ref>{{Cite|I want to give you something to show my appreciation, but I don't think I have anything on me...|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> He offers to help Link find a place he has been looking for.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I know!}} Is there a place you've been seeking? I've traveled all over Hyrule, so I might be able to help you find it.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> Link shows Pikango one of the pictures {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} left on the Sheikah Slate, which he recognizes as a gate at the base of {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}, on the west side of the {{Term|BotW|Naydra Snowfield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A large gate with a snowy mountain beyond it... Oh, I know that place! There is a big mountain east of this village... It's called {{Color|BotW Blue|Mount Lanayru}}. At the base of {{Color|BotW Blue|Mount Lanayru}}, on the west side of {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra Snowfield}}, lies that gate. I'm almost certain!|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> He asks Link if he was useful.<ref>{{Cite|I'm glad I knew about that location. I suppose my help was useful, then?|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> This completes the Quest.
