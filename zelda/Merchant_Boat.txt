The {{Term/Store|Merchant Boat||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 332}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Merchant Boat}} is located at the end of the longest {{Term|BotW|Dock|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}. It is a {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} run by {{Term|BotW|Mubs|link}}, focusing on selling {{Plural|BotW|Fish|link}} that can be found in the surrounding {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fresh fish! Just recently caught! Take a look!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Merchant Boat}} itself carries {{Term|BotW|Mubs}}' supplies, including extra {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}. There is also a {{Term|BotW|Jar|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} at the end of the {{Term|BotW|Dock}}, which can be broken for supplies.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Merchant Boat}}, {{Term|BotW|Mubs}} advertises the price and quality of her wares.<ref>{{Cite|Afforadable and delicious!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> To buy an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Merchant Boat}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must stand in front of it and interact with it.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll buy.] If you want something, just let me know by standing in front of it and selecting it!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes a purchase, {{Term|BotW|Mubs}} will thank him for his business.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for your business!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys the entirety of {{Term|BotW|Mubs}}' stock of an {{Term|BotW|Item}}, she promises to procure more in the future.<ref>{{Cite|You need more? I'll be sure to get some more for next time!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} stands on any of the products, {{Term|BotW|Mubs}} will yell at him to stop.<ref>{{Cite|You stop that!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> At the end of their conversations, {{Term|BotW|Mubs}} invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back.<ref>{{Cite|Come again! I'll be waiting!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref>
