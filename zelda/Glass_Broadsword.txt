==Location and Uses==
Glass Broadswords are strong yet brittle {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} of the {{Term|CoH|Broadsword|link}} variety, dealing 3 damage to three enemies in front of {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} in a vast sweeping motion.<ref name="Obtention"/> As glass weapons, they cannot be created or infused with any materials, and thus can only be found as loot from {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain enemies.
