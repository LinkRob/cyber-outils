The {{Term/Store|Diamond Card||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Diamond Card}} is one of {{Plural|ST|Beedle's Membership Card|link}} that can be obtained after {{Term|ST|Link|link}} reaches 2,000 points at {{Term|ST|Beedle's Airshop|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your point total has climbed to [x]! Rack up {{Color|ST Light Red|2,000 points}} to earn yourself a Diamond Card!|Beedle|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You have [x] points, valued customer. Rack up more points for a Diamond Card and even better rewards!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> After {{Term|ST|Link}} reaches this threshold, {{Term|ST|Beedle|link}} will announce his new membership and offer him a prize.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh! You have 2000 points! You're now an official {{Color|ST Light Red|Diamond Member}}! That means you get {{Color|ST Light Red|50% off all merchandise}} AND a {{Color|ST Light Red|lovely prize}}!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> He then gives {{Term|ST|Link}} a {{Term|ST|Regal Ring|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|ST Light Red|Regal Ring}}! It's been passed down over generations of nobility.|N/A|ST}}</ref>

{{Term|ST|Beedle}} promises to send {{Term|ST|Link}} his {{Term|ST|Diamond Card}} the next day, and while {{Term|ST|Link}} is unable to earn more points, he hopes that he will continue to visit {{Term|ST|Beedle's Airshop}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll send your Diamond Card tomorrow, so be sure to check your mail. Though you can't earn any more points... I do hope you'll think of Beedle's Air Shop for all of your future needs. Thanks so much for the business, valued customer!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> Regardless of the span of time that has passed, {{Term|ST|Link}} will be able to receive the {{Term|ST|Diamond Card}} from the {{Term|ST|Diamond Membership|link}} letter at any {{Term|ST|Postbox|link}}. The {{Term|ST|Diamond Card}} grants {{Term|ST|Link}} 50% off any {{Term|ST|Item|link}} he purchases at {{Term|ST|Beedle's Airshop}}.
