'''''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD — PRIMA Official Game Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{TPHD}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 448 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{TPHD}}, a listing of {{Plural|TPHD|Item|link}} and collectibles, and detailed maps.
