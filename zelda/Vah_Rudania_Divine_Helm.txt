The {{Term/Store|Vah Rudania Divine Helm||Series, BotW}} is a piece of [[Armor]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Vah Rudania Divine Helm}}<br/>A treasure gifted to the one who controls Divine Beast Vah Rudania. It endows its user with enhanced abilities.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Vah Rudania Divine Helm is obtained by scanning the [[Daruk]] [[amiibo]] with the [[amiibo (Rune)|amiibo Rune]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= They'll summon materials and weapons, but unlike other amiibo, they'll conjure up headgear based on the Champions' Divine Beasts. They have functional purposes as well, and we'll have more info soon. |author= Nintendo |published= September 13, 2017 |retrieved=September 13, 2017|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ztMGTTAjsAQ |title= Nintendo Direct 9.13.2017 |site= YouTube |type= Video}}</ref> When worn, the Vah Rudania Divine Helm provides a basic level of both [[Guardian Resist Up]] and [[Flame Guard]] and also allows Link to view an enemy's remaining health.<ref name="Facebook">{{Cite Web|quote= With the Champion amiibo coming out on November 10th, learn more about what each helm does in The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild!<br/><br/>When any of the Divine Helms are equipped with Ancient Cuirass and Ancient Greaves (All armor items need to be upgraded level 2 ★★ or above), you’ll also trigger a set bonus, Ancient Proficiency, which increases the attack power of Ancient or Guardian series weapons. All of them will increase guard from ancient energy attacks, while defense level and required materials for level-up are the same as those of Ancient Helm. Enemies' remaining HP/full HP will also be visible like the Champion's Tunic.<br/><br/>Additionally, there are specific effects for each item:<br/>- Vah Rudania Divine Helm: Flame Guard (Lv1)<br/>- Vah Ruta Divine Helm: Swim Speed Up (Lv1)<br/>- Vah Naboris Divine Helm: Electricity Resistance (Lv1)<br/>- Vah Medoh Divine Helm: Cold Resistance (Lv1) |author= Nintendo |published= November 3, 2017 |retrieved= November 3, 2017 |url= https://business.facebook.com/LegendofZelda/posts/1576388102422314 |title= With the Champion amiibo coming out on November... - The Legend of Zelda |site= Facebook |type=}}</ref> When paired with the [[Ancient Cuirass]] and the [[Ancient Greaves]] and each piece of [[Armor]] has been enhanced at least twice by a [[Great Fairy]], the three pieces of Armor provide [[Ancient Proficiency]], a [[Set Bonus]] that increases the damage dealt from the Ancient and Guardian archetypes of weapons.<ref name="Facebook"/>

The Vah Rudania Divine Helm is based on the head portion of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}, which Daruk piloted as the [[Goron]] [[Champion]].
