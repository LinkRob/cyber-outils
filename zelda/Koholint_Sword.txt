The {{Term/Store|Koholint Sword||Series, LANS}} is an [[Sword]] [[item]] in {{LA}}.<ref name="Name">{{Cite|You got the {{Color|LANS Pink|Koholint Sword}}! You should put your name on it right away!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Koholint Sword}} was referred to as the {{Term/Store|Level 2 Sword||LA, LADX}} in all versions of {{LA}} prior to {{LA|NS}}.<ref name="E 129"/> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{LA|NS|-}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
Called "'''L-2'''" in-game, [[Link]] can replace his [[Sword]] with the {{Term|LANS|Koholint Sword}} by finding and bringing 20 {{Plural|LADX|Secret Seashell|link}} in {{LA}} or 40 in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Nintendo Switch remake]] to the {{Term|LANS|Spirit of the Mansion|link}} in the {{Term|LANS|Seashell Mansion|link}}.<ref name="E 129"/> It is twice as strong as the regular sword, making it useful against tougher [[Enemies in Link's Awakening|enemies]] or [[Bosses in Link's Awakening|bosses]], especially when acquiring a [[Piece of Power]] to double its strength.

When Link's [[Life Gauge]] is full, the {{Term|LANS|Koholint Sword}} emits [[Sword Beam|Sword Beams]] when he uses it.<ref name="E 129"/>

==Trivia==
*When Link receives the Koholint Sword, he is urged to write his name on it.<ref name="Name"/> This is a reference to his previous [[Sword]], which has his name engraved on it.<ref>{{Cite|You found your {{Color|LANS Pink|sword}}! It must be yours because it has your name engraved on it!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>
*The artwork for the sword resembles the [[Master Sword]].
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|Link's Awakening DX]]'', if Link were to acquire the [[Red Mail|Red Clothes]] alongside the Koholint Sword and a Piece of Power, he can inflict eight times the damage of his standard sword, resulting in devastating blows.
