The {{Term/Store|Jungle||Series, SSBU}},<ref>{{Cite|Jungle|N/A|SSBU}}</ref> also known as the '''Jungle Thicket''',<ref name="Tip">{{Cite|Adventure: Ruins/Cave/Jungle Thicket<br/>It's easier to get SP in Ruins, support items in Caves, and snacks in Jungle Thicket.|Tips|SSBU}}</ref> is a location in {{SSBU}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|SSBU|Jungle}} is a location that up to four {{Term|SSBU|Spirit|link}} can explore at no cost under the direction of {{Term|SSBU|Linebeck|link}}. The {{Term|SSBU|Jungle}} is unlocked after obtaining the {{Term|SSBU|Linebeck}} {{Term|SSBU|Master Spirit|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can now explore the jungle!|N/A|SSBU}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|SSBU|Jungle}} can be accessed from its location in the [[World of Light]] or from the Activities section of the Collection sub-menu in the Spirits menu. Expeditions into the {{Term|SSBU|Jungle}} take 10 hours to complete and {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} that explore the {{Term|SSBU|Jungle}} are more likely to find {{Ssb|Snack}}s.<ref name="Tip"/> While on an expedition, {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} that were sent are unavailable for use for any other purpose, though they may be called back at any point.<ref>{{Cite|You really want to cancel the voyage?|Linebeck|SSBU}}</ref> {{Plural|SSBU|Primary Spirit|link}} will gain [[Experience]] while on expeditions in the {{Term|SSBU|Jungle}}.

Occasionally, {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} on an expedition may be attacked by snakes and jungle cats. This does not affect the results of the expedition nor the time remaining to complete it.
