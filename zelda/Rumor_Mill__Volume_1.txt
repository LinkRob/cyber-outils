==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|Rumor Mill: Volume 1}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} written by {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}}. It can be found in {{Term|BotW|Enchanted|link}}, a store in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. Within, she details the rumor of the {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond|link}}, saying people who find this pond are certain to find true love.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Lover's Pond!}} Legend says that those who find this pond are sure to also find their true love! Sounds worthwhile to me!|Rumor Mill: Volume 1|BotW}}</ref> She gives this rumor five stars.<ref>{{Cite|Traysi's Recommendation: ★★★★★|Rumor Mill: Volume 1|BotW}}</ref>
