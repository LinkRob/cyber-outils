{{Term/Store|Hollow|Hollows|Series|plural}} are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{HWAoC}} that are made out of [[Malice]].

==Characteristics==
Hollows are constructs of Malice molded after the Champions and share the same weapons and fighting styles as their original counterparts, producing a dark aura with constellations appearing around them, similar to [[Astor]]. In a cutscene, they are also shown the ability to warp as well. Because they are composed of pure Malice, they are especially weak against the [[Master Sword]].

Hollows first appear in the [[Scenario]] "[[Freeing Korok Forest]]" where they are summoned by [[Astor]]. Four Hollows take the form of the [[Champions]]. Link must travel to four locations and battle the Hollows. In the southwest is the Hollow of [[Mipha]];<ref>{{Cite|How can I beat back my own shadow?|Mipha|HWAoC}}</ref> in the southeast is the Hollow of [[Daruk]];<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you! Don't go ruinin' my good name, ya hear?!|Daruk|HWAoC}}</ref> in the northwest is the Hollow of [[Revali]];<ref>{{Cite|Hm. He didn't just steal my good looks, but also my unparalleled talents! The nerve of this guy!|Revali|HWAoC}}</ref> in the northeast is the Hollow of [[Urbosa]].<ref>{{Cite|A fight against myself, hm? Good. Might be a decent challenge.|Urbosa|HWAoC}}</ref> The champion Hollows all have the same attacks as the normal Champions, however, they have less health than the normal Champions. Hollows will not drop anything when defeated. Later in the story, a Hollow of Link attacks [[Sooga]] and [[Master Kohga]] through the command of [[Astor]]. This Link Hollow is slightly stronger than the others and would become the default Hollow for Astor's later appearances in {{GoR}} and [[The Future of Hyrule]], and a few even appear in "[[EX The Yiga Clan's Retreat]]", before the Blights arrive.
