==Characteristics==

Islander Hawks are carnivorous {{Plural|BotW|Bird|link}} that can be found flying in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and [[Hyrule Ridge]].<ref>{{Cite|These carnivorous birds live in plains or highlands.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Their diet consists of small animals and other birds.<ref>{{Cite|They use their sharp talons and beaks to catch and eat small animals and other birds.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Like most birds, they can be hunted by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}, and they drop {{Plural|BotW|Raw Bird Thigh|link}} when killed.
