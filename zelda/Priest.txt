The {{Term/Store|Priest||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
In {{ALBW}}, the Priest is the father of [[Seres]],<ref name= "Priest"/> one of the [[Sage|Seven Sages]], and is the chosen caretaker of the [[Sanctuary (A Link Between Worlds)|Sanctuary]]. He seems to be a spiritual successor to the [[Loyal Sage]] from {{ALttP}}, and his [[Lorule|Lorulean]] counterpart is known as the [[Philosopher]].
