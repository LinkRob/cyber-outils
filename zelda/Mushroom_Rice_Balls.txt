{{Term/Store|Mushroom Rice Balls|Mushroom Rice Balls|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Mushroom Rice Balls can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}} together with [[Hylian Rice]]. The regular version can be made either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}}, which have no status effects, or by using multiple Mushrooms with differing effects. When eaten, they'll restore some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The scent of mushrooms tickles one's nose as they peel back the leafy wrappings of this meal.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By using certain Mushrooms, the following variations of Mushroom Rice Balls can be made:

* '''Hearty Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. Eating these rice balls will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}} or other energizing ingredients. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be partially restored by eating these.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}} or other enduring ingredients. These meals will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}} or other hasty ingredients. Consuming them will increase Link's speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}} or other spicy ingredients. Link can eat them to gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}} or other electric ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}} or other mighty ingredients. If eaten, Link will receive a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}} or other tough ingredients. When eaten, Link's defense will be boosted.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}} or other sneaky ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Mushroom Rice Balls''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Mushroom Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The aroma of the mushrooms tickles your nose as you peel back the leafy wrapping.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}} or other chilly ingredients. These rice balls can be eaten to grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
