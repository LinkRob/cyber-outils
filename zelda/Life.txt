The {{Term/Store|Life||Series, TAoL}} magic is an ability in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 108}}</ref>

==Overview==
The Life magic recovers some of Link's life energy, and it is one of the magic that uses the most Magic Points. Aside from finding rare Fairies or leveling up, it is the only way for Link to recover health when away from towns. By finding a [[Mirror (Item)|Mirror]] under a table in a house in the [[Water Town of Saria]], Link can then take it to the woman who lost said mirror. In return, the woman allows him into her house to meet the Old Man who teaches him this magic. While the Life magic only recovers approximately three squares of life energy, it will surely save Link's life many times.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= This can recover Link's LIFE points (but only to a certain extent!). You should use this magic before all is lost.|game= TAoL|page= 43}}</ref>
