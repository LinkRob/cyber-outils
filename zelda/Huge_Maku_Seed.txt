{{Term/Store|Huge Maku Seed|Huge Maku Seed|Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 127 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
The Huge Maku Seed is a sacred item obtained from the [[Maku Tree]] after acquiring all eight [[Essences of Nature]] in {{OoS}}. It has the power to dispel evil.{{Fact}} When Link holds the Huge Maku Seed, he is able to penetrate the impassible dark mist surrounding the entrance to [[Onox's Castle]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
The Huge Maku Seed is a sacred item obtained from the [[Maku Tree]] after acquiring all eight [[Essences of Time]] in {{OoA}}. It has the power to dispel evil.{{Fact}} When Link holds the Huge Maku Seed, he is able to dispel the false entrances to the inner depths of the [[Black Tower]] and reveal the true path forward.
