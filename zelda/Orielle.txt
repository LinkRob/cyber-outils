{{Term/Store|Orielle||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 294}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>This is {{Color|SS Blue|Orielle}}, a down-to-earth young woman. She has significant experience dealing with {{Color|SS Blue|Loftwings}}, even for a resident of Skyloft.</p><p>She shares her enthusiasm for birds with her older brother, {{Color|SS Blue|Parrow}}.</p>
}}

Orielle lives with her older brother [[Parrow]] in [[Skyloft]]. Both she and her brother share a large interest in [[Loftwing]]s, and both have experience in handling them.

Orielle displays something of a romantic side, claiming she dreams of being the village girl portraying the [[Hylia|Goddess]] after the [[Wing Ceremony]] with a handsome knight.<ref>{{Cite|The winner will get blessed by a village girl chosen to represent the goddess... How romantic! I dream of playing the part of the goddess at the Wing Ceremony! Just to have a romantic ceremony with the handsome, winning knight. Just once is all I ask.|Orielle|SS}}</ref>

On a sidequest, Orielle decides to fly off to investigate [[Fun Fun Island]], but ends up stranded on an island in [[the Sky]] after crash landing her Loftwing. Link locates her after Parrow asks him to help him find her. After finding her, Link reports back to Parrow who gives him a [[Bottle]] of [[Mushroom Spores]] that will heal her bird. Once she heals her Loftwing, she is able to return to Skyloft, reuniting the two siblings. Link will then receive ten [[Gratitude Crystals]], five from each sibling. 

==Nomenclature==
Orielle's name is derived from oriole, the name of two groups of birds.
