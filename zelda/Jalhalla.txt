{{Term/Store|Jalhalla||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is the leader of all [[Poe]]s who appears as a [[Boss]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 186}}</ref> He is composed of many smaller Poes.

====Characteristics====
{{Figurine|TWW|Jalhalla, Protector of the Seal}}
Jalhalla resides in the inner sanctum of the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]] and is responsible for the murder of the [[Earth Sage|Sage of Earth]], [[Laruto]].<ref>{{Cite|The temple ahead is the nest of an evil creature...the same creature that stole the life of your predecessor, the sage. It is a dark and frightening place.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> He owes his existence to a small group of about fifteen normal Poes and a mask that uses their power to form the entity. This fiend must be defeated in order to partially restore power to the [[Master Sword]], by placing [[Medli]] in her new position as the new Sage.

When Link enters the deepest room of the temple, he finds several Poes running about. Suddenly aware of Link's presence, they all turn towards him. They then jump into Jalhalla's mask in the center of the room to form their master as the battle begins.

Jalhalla employs a variety of attacks in the battle. His most common move involves scattering fireballs from his lantern. He can also forcefully blow out air, enough to push [[Link]] toward the spiked wall or suck in air and breathe fire. Touching Jalhalla causes Link to become confused, making him move in the opposite direction of his intended course. During this time, an image of Jalhalla's mask rotates over his head and he appears dizzy. Confusion lasts for approximately five seconds but can be quickly removed by taking damage.

The trick to bringing Jalhalla down is to direct light at him with the [[Mirror Shield#The Wind Waker|Mirror Shield]], causing him to become tangible and collapse. Link must then lift the fiend off the floor with the [[Power Bracelet]]s and throw him into one of the columns of spikes on the walls. This will cause his body to split into many Poes for the young hero to dispose of. Link can use the [[Spin Attack|Hurricane Spin]] technique to make this task easier if he has already learned it. However, only five Poes can be killed at any time. Link must repeat this process to vanquish all the Poes to defeat Jalhalla. If even a single Poe is left, it will be enough to fully revive Jalhalla.

Once all the Poes are gone, Jalhalla's mask reappears and becomes dumbfounded about now being unable to reform itself. Attempting to escape, the mask is bathed in a beam of light, causing it to fall to the ground and shatter. Defeating Jalhalla yields a [[Heart Container]] and allows Medli to take her place as the Sage of Earth, restoring the first half of the Master Sword's lost power.

Jalhalla is found again in [[Ganon's Tower (The Wind Waker)|Ganon's Tower]] later in the game. He must be defeated for a second time in the same fashion to break the seal to the door leading to [[Ganondorf]].
