{{Term/Store|Key-sicle|Key-sicles|Series, TMC|plural}} are objects in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC |publisher= Nintendo |page= 58}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Key-sicle}} are located in the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}. They are frozen {{Plural|TMC|Ice Block|link}} that contain {{Plural|TMC|Small Key|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Big Key|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Ice}} can be melted by pushing a {{Term|TMC|Key-sicle}} into {{Plural|TMC|Light Beam|link}}.
