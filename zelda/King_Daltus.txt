==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|King Daltus}}
{{Term|Series|King Daltus|display=Daltus}} is the king of {{Term|Series|Hyrule|link}}, a descendant of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}}'s father.<ref name="E 270"/> He is tall and heavyset, and wears a long red robe.

He once battled {{Term|TMC|Smith|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}} to the point where it was considered a draw.<ref>{{Cite|The king of Hyrule. Reported to have been a fine swordsman, he appeared in the Picori Festival tournament as a youth and fought to a draw with Smith.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> In the beginning of {{TMC}}, he appears at the award ceremony for the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament}}, where he is knocked away by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} when the sorcerer shatters the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} and opens the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest|link}}. After {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} is turned to stone by {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}, he reveals the existence of the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} to the {{Plural|TMC|Vassal|link}},<ref>{{Cite|What do you know about the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}}? Yes, the ones from the fairy tales... But the Picori are no mere legend. They most certainly exist.|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref> explaining that the soldiers would not be able to seek them out.<ref>{{Cite|No, soldiers will not do. The Picori do not show themselves to anyone but children. Our soldiers could search for days and still find no sign of them.|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref> Instead, he asks {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to find them and have them reforge the broken {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} so that he can save {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|If {{Color|TMC Green|Link}} has recovered, then yes, I would like to ask this of him.|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref>

Later in the game, he is imprisoned and impersonated by a shape-shifting {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}},<ref>{{Cite|What? Vaati! How did you get in here!?|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref> who sends all of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}'s {{Plural|TMC|Vassal|link}} to find the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The golden {{Color|TMC Red|light force}} of limitless magical power... I want it!!! It is somewhere here in Hyrule! Go now, and bring me this power!|Vaati|TMC}}</ref> While {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}} is imprisoned and being impersonated, the people of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} notice the impostor's strange behavior.<ref>{{Cite|I haven't seen {{Color|TMC Green|Princess Zelda}} in ages, and the {{Color|TMC Green|king}}'s just plain loopy! What's going on around here?|Keeley|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TMC Green|king}} is just terrible lately! Things are going crazy around here!|Satchel|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What's going on with the {{Color|TMC Green|king}} lately? He seems like...he's not himself...|Vassal|TMC}}</ref>

After obtaining the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} encounters the true {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}} in the dungeons of {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, where he has been turned to stone. {{Term|TMC|Link}} is able to restore him with the power of the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword}}, and he gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} a {{Term|TMC|Small Key|link}} to help him progress further into the {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}} and save {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Take this key. You can use it to get out of the castle from the basement.|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref>

Before he is impersonated by {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}, {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to fuse Kinstones in this dire time? ...Well, I can see no harm in it.|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref>
