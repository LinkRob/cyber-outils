{{Term/Store|Cream of Vegetable Soup|Cream of Vegetable Soups|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Cream of Vegetable Soup}}<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Cream of Vegetable Soups are made in {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} by combining [[Fresh Milk]], [[Rock Salt]], and any vegetable, herb, or flower. This recipe, however, excludes {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, otherwise the resulting dish will be [[Veggie Cream Soup]]. Eating them can heal between seven to ten of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, depending on the ingredients used. They also sell between 40 to 60 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. Cream of Vegetable Soups are healthy dishes, made by simmering vegetables in milk.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the type of ingredients used, several different variations of Cream of Vegetable Soup can be made:

* '''Hearty Cream of Vegetable Soup''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Cream of Vegetable Soup}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. These soups completely refill Link's Life Gauge when eaten and will temporarily increase his maximum hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/> They also sell for 70 Rupees.

* '''Energizing Cream of Vegetable Soup''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Cream of Vegetable Soup}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by including {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey|link}} or other energizing ingredients in the recipe. When eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be partially refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/> They restore seven Hearts, and they sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Hasty Cream of Vegetable Soup''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}} or other speed-increasing ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/> These soups heal one Heart and sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Cream of Vegetable Soup''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Cream of Vegetable Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level cold resistance.<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}} or other spicy ingredients. If eaten, they'll grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] for a short period of time.<ref name="Spicy"/> They replenish one Heart and sell for 20 Rupees.

* '''Electro Cream of Vegetable Soup''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Cream of Vegetable Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}} or other electricity-related ingredients. When they're eaten, Link will be granted one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/> They'll heal Link for one Heart and sell for 20 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Cream of Vegetable Soup''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Cream of Vegetable Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}} or other mighty ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/> They also heal one Heart, and they sell for 30 Rupees.

* '''Tough Cream of Vegetable Soup''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Cream of Vegetable Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}} or other tough ingredients. When eaten, they'll increase Link's defense for a short time.<ref name="Tough"/> They replenish one Heart in Link's Life Gauge, and they sell for 30 Rupees.

* '''Sneaky Cream of Vegetable Soup''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Cream of Vegetable Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/> They replenish one Heart and sell for 20 Rupees.

* '''Chilly Cream of Vegetable Soup''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Cream of Vegetable Soup}}<br/>Grants mid-level heat resistance.<br/>Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}} or other chilly ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Chilly"/> The heal Link for one Heart and sell for 20 Rupees.
