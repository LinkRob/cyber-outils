The {{Term/Store|Goron Hot Springs||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Goron Hot Springs|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Goron Hot Springs are a small collection of [[Hot Spring]]s that are situated in the road between [[Stolock Bridge]] and the [[Bridge of Eldin]] in [[Eldin Canyon]]. They are denoted as [[Death Mountain]] Marker #7.<ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #7<br/>The world-renowned, refreshing Goron Hot Springs!|Sign|BotW}}</ref> As Hot Springs, the water is warm and will gradually recover Link's health as he sits in it.

Two [[Goron]]s inhabit the Hot Springs. [[Grapp]], an elderly Goron, complains that the water only feels lukewarm to him.<ref>{{Cite|Bah. Lukewarm. I'd like to relax in some reeeeally hot water. A LOT hotter than this nonsense.|Grapp|BotW}}</ref> He also suggests that Link stay away from the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} lest he get burned,<ref>{{Cite|By the way, I don't see many tourists like you equipped to stand up to this heat. I'm impressed! Even still, don't you go near {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}}. If ya do, no matter how well prepared ya are...you'll be burned to ashes!|Grapp|BotW}}</ref> but also admits that the Bridge of Eldin is currently unavailable due to [[Bludo]] raising it to prevent Vah Rudania's advance.<ref>{{Cite|In any case, gettin' across the {{Color|BotW Red|Bridge of Eldin}} is impossible right now. The {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} probably raised {{Color|BotW Red|the bridge}} to keep {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}} from comin' over here.|Grapp|BotW}}</ref>

[[Dugby]], a child Goron, can also be found at the Hot Springs. During the day, Dugby sits in one of the pools, however, due to his mouth being submerged underwater, his spoken words come out as gurgles.<ref>{{Cite|Bello! Goo gon't go but I'm thaying?|Dugby|BotW}}</ref> Dugby tells Link that he had hidden a treasure near the Bridge of Eldin, initiating the [[Side Quest]] "[[Death Mountain's Secret]]".<ref>{{Cite|I bid a {{Color|BotW Red|tweshure}} binteen gear an ba {{Color|BotW Blue|Bridge of Eldin}}! Iz an abazing schtick! Beely bool! If boo bind it... iz boars!|Dugby|BotW}}</ref> At night Dugby can be found fast asleep, and will mutter in his sleep more coherent directions to the treasure.<ref>{{Cite|<small><nowiki>*snore*</nowiki> The {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}}...at the {{Color|BotW Red|top of a lava waterfall}}...</small>|Dugby|BotW}}</ref>
