{{DISPLAYTITLE: Levels in {{FSA|-}}}}
:''Not to be confused with [[Stages in Four Swords Adventures]].''
[[File:Whereabouts of the Wind.png|thumb|280px|right|Whereabouts of the Wind, the first Level]]
In {{FSA}}, '''Levels''' refers to chapters consisting of regional portions of [[Hyrule]], which the [[Links]] travel to on their quest to save the [[Maiden|Shrine Maiden]]s and defeat [[Vaati]]. Each Level contains three [[Stage]]s, which must be completed in order to progress through the Level. Upon the competion of the third Stage in a Level, a new Level and region of Hyrule is unlocked. There are a total of eight Levels in the game.

==Levels==
===Whereabouts of the Wind===
'''Whereabouts of the Wind''' is the first Level in {{FSA|-}}, known for introducing the techniques used to control the four Links. It consists of Hyrule's central region, closest to [[Hyrule Castle]]. It contains the following Stages:
*[[Lake Hylia (Four Swords Adventures)|Lake Hylia]]
*[[Cave of No Return]]
*[[Hyrule Castle (Four Swords Adventures)|Hyrule Castle]]

===Eastern Hyrule===
{{Main|Eastern Hyrule}}
'''{{Term|FSA|Eastern Hyrule}}''' is the second Level in {{FSA|-}}. It consists of the southeast region of Hyrule. It contains the following Stages:
*[[The Coast]]
*[[Village of the Blue Maiden]]
*[[Eastern Temple]]

===Death Mountain===
'''Death Mountain''' is the third Level in {{FSA|-}}. It consists of the mountainous northern region of Hyrule's [[Death Mountain]]. It contains the following Stages:
*[[Death Mountain Foothills]]
*[[The Mountain Path]]
*[[Tower of Flames]]

===Near the Fields===
'''Near the Fields''' is the fourth Level in {{FSA|-}}. It consists of the southern plains of Hyrule south of Hyrule Castle, where [[Hyrule Field]] is located. It contains the following Stages:
*[[The Field]]
*[[The Swamp]]
*[[Infiltration of Hyrule Castle]]

===The Dark World===
'''The Dark World''' is the fifth Level in {{FSA|-}}. It consists of the northwest region of Hyrule, which has been covered by a cloud of darkness and subsequently corrupted into a twisted place known as the [[Dark World]].<ref>{{Cite|My forest has lost its light. Darkness covers it like a shroud. Link, will you go to the forest? Perhaps you will find some clues there. In the heart of the forest is a temple. The Dark Mirror can be found there.|White Maiden|FSA}}</ref> It contains the following Stages:
*[[Lost Woods (Four Swords Adventures)|Lost Woods]]
*[[Kakariko Village (Four Swords Adventures)|Kakariko Village]]
*[[Temple of Darkness]]

===Desert of Doubt===
'''Desert of Doubt''' is the sixth Level in {{FSA|-}}. It consists of the southwest desert corner of Hyrule, where the [[Gerudo]] and [[Zuna]] people live. It contains the following Stages:
*[[Desert of Doubt (Stage)|Desert of Doubt]]
*[[Desert Temple (Four Swords Adventures)|Desert Temple]]
*[[Pyramid]]

===Frozen Hyrule===
'''Frozen Hyrule''' is the seventh Level in {{FSA|-}}. It consists of the southern region of Hyrule close to the [[#Near the Fields|fields]], which has become frozen and densely covered in snow as a result of the power of darkness halting the flow of time and causing winter to linger there.<ref>{{Cite|The power of darkness has halted the flow of time here, and winter lingers on.|Maiden|FSA}}</ref> It contains the following  Stages:
*[[Frozen Hyrule (Stage)|Frozen Hyrule]]
*{{Term|FSA|Temple of Ice|link}}
*[[Tower of Winds]] 

===Realm of the Heavens===
'''Realm of the Heavens''' is the eighth and final Level in {{FSA|-}}. It takes place in the sky and clouds above Hyrule, where a floating fortress known as the [[Palace of Winds (Four Swords Adventures)|Palace of Winds]] is located. The Links ultimately fight Vaati here, and later [[Ganon]]. It contains the following Stages:
*[[Realm of the Heavens (Stage)|Realm of the Heavens]]
*[[The Dark Cloud]]
*[[Palace of Winds (Four Swords Adventures)|Palace of Winds]]
