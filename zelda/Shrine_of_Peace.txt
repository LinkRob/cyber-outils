{{Term/Store|Shrine of Peace|Shrines of Peace|Series, CoH|plural}} are objects in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|SHRINE OF PEACE!|N/A|CoH}}</ref> They were introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Peace}} can appear in {{Plural|CoH|Dungeon|link}} in both {{Term|CoH|Dungeon Mode|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Story Mode — Octavo|link}}. When activated, the shrine grants the character a {{Term|CoH|Fairy|link}} and replaces some enemies with less powerful variants, but at the price of removing the characters {{Term|CoH|Shovel|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Torch|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Ring|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Boots|link}}. The effects of {{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Peace}} last until the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} is left, the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} {{Term|CoH|Boss|link}} is defeated, or when the character is defeated.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, look! A {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! {{Color|CoH Blue|Shrines}} are useful, but they often have a tricky {{Color|CoH Blue|side effect}} that you may not like. Bump into it if you want to take a chance and activate the {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! Shrine effects stay active until you leave the dungeon, defeat the dungeon boss, or are defeated.|Trill|CoH}}</ref>

When destroyed, the shrine drops three {{Plural|CoH|Heart|link}}.
