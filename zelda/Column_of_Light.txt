{{Term/Store|Column of Light|Columns of Light|Series, SS, SSHD|plural}} are colored pillars that appear in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Doing so will likely open a new {{Color|SSHD Red|column of light}} on the surface, allowing you to descend to another area ad continue your search for Zelda.|Fi|SSHD}}</ref> They allow access to the {{Term|SSHD|Surface|link}} and facilitate navigation while above and below the clouds.

==Location and Uses==
Because the {{Term|SSHD|Surface}} and the {{Term|SSHD|Sky|link}} are separated by a {{Term|SSHD|Cloud Barrier|link}} created by {{Term|SSHD|Hylia|link}}, many {{Plural|SSHD|Skyloftian|link}} are unaware of what lies below the clouds and believe it to be a forsaken and barren place; only a few old records ever mention the land underneath the clouds.<ref>{{Cite|Some say that it's an empty, barren place, or even that there's nothing at all down below, but I just have this feeling that they're wrong. Some of Father's old texts talk about a place called the {{Color|SS Red|surface}}. The old tales describe a whole world under there, far more vast than Skyloft! The thing is, no one's ever been down there to see it, and our Loftwings won't fly through the cloud barrier.|Zelda|SSHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is no easy task, Link.<br/>The world below is a forsaken place, and to reach it you must pierce the cloud barrier below. In living memory, no one has ever done this.|Gaepora|SSHD}}</ref> Each time {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} places an {{Term|SSHD|Ancient Tablet|link}} in the altar within the {{Term|SSHD|Statue of the Goddess|link}}, a {{Term|SSHD|Column of Light}} of the respective color of both the region and the tablet will appear and make an opening in the {{Term|SSHD|Cloud Barrier}}.<ref>{{Cite|The tablet you placed in the altar has opened a small rift in the barrier. You can use it to travel through the clouds to the realm below.|Fi|SSHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The column of light has appeared to the south of our location, and signs indicate that it has created an opening in the cloud barrier to the land below.|Fi|SSHD}}</ref> The pillar, which is visible from anywhere in the {{Term|SSHD|Sky}}, allows {{Term|SSHD|Link}} to go through the {{Term|SSHD|Cloud Barrier}} and reach the {{Term|SSHD|Surface}} by dropping off near the column with the aid of his {{Term|SSHD|Crimson Loftwing|link|display=Loftwing}}.
