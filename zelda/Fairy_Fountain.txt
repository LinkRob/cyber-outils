{{Term/Store|Fairy Fountain|Fairy Fountains|Series, TMC, FSA|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC |publisher= Nintendo |page= 10}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= FSA |publisher= Nintendo |page= 6}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Fairy's Fountain|Fairy's Fountains|OoT|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Fairy's Fountain}}|N/A|OoT}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoT3D}} are recurring locations in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|TLoZ, ALttP, MM3D, OoS, OoA, TWWHD, TPHD, ALBW, TFoE, TWoG, HWDE}} They are magical springs found across many places of {{Term|Series|Hyrule|link}} which contain a small group of {{Plural|Series|Fairy|link}} within. They can usually be found in {{Plural|Series|Cave|link}} or by bombing certain walls to reveal the hidden entrance. They have appeared in almost every game in series to date, providing a good source of healing for {{Term|Series|Link|link}} in which he can recover his [[Life Gauge]] at once and/or store the {{Plural|Series|Fairy}} in {{Plural|Series|Bottle|link}} for later use. They are also known to have a [[Recurring Music in The Legend of Zelda Series|recurring theme song]].

==Features and Overview==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, there are two Fairy Fountains scattered throughout the land of [[Hyrule]]; however, they are never specifically referred to as a Fairy Fountain or a Great Fairy Fountain, and as such, it can be argued as to which is its correct name. They serve the same purpose as other Great Fairy Fountains in later games: when Link approaches the fairy hovering above the pond, she will completely heal his status. The fountains here also set the overall image of the [[Great Fairy Fountain|Great Fairy Fountains]] for many ''Zelda'' games to come, with the fairy floating above a body of water.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
Although the Fairy Fountains were missing in {{TAoL}}, they returned in {{ALttP}}. Fairy Fountains in this game required certain items in order to reveal their cleverly-covered entrances. There are many of them spread across Hyrule, and once Link enters a fountain, he can find several Fairies floating over a pond of water ready to heal the young hero or be [[Bug-Catching Net|caught]] in an empty Bottle. There are also Fairy Fountains located within certain dungeons. This is also the first installment where anytime Link is defeated but has a Fairy in a Bottle, it will automatically appear and revive the young hero, to prevent the player from receiving a [[Game Over]].

==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:Fairyfountainoot.png|right|thumb|A Fairy's Fountain from {{OoT|-}}|227x227px]]
{{Plural|OoT3D|Fairy Fountain}} are all mostly hidden underground in {{OoT}}, either beneath large boulders or below a circle of rocks. Upon entering, there is a short hallway leading to the elegant Fairy's Fountain that houses several Fairies above its pond of water. It is surrounded by several columns, having a type of angel mark on top of them. Link can easily exit these fountains by stepping onto the platform with a shining ray of light found at the beginning of the fountain.

Other times, however, a Fairy's Fountain can be found outside, such as the one found in the oasis of the [[Desert Colossus]], and just like its predecessor, Link can also stumble upon one in certain dungeons or temples.

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM Fairy Fountain.png|left|thumb|A Fairy's Fountain from {{MM|-}}|235x235px]]
In {{MM}}, the fountains are curiously known as Fairy's Fountains despite the fact that, with one exception, [[Great Fairy|Great Fairies]] live in them and that they serve the same purpose as other Great Fairy Fountains. There are only five Fairy's Fountains. One is located in [[Clock Town]], while the other four are located in each of the four cardinal directions of [[Termina]]: [[Woodfall]], [[Snowhead]], [[Great Bay]], and [[Ikana Canyon]]. They are not as well-hidden in comparison to its predecessor, for they are easily found inside a {{Term|MM3D|Cave|link}} in plain sight.

The appearance of the Fairy's Fountain in {{MM|-}} is rather similar to that of the ones in {{OoT|-}}. There is still a large pond in the middle, and the fountain is surrounded by several columns and arcs. Since the Great Fairy living within was shattered into {{Plural|MM3D|Stray Fairy|link}} by the [[Skull Kid]] wearing {{MM|Object}}, it is up to Link to gather the missing Stray Fairies from each of the area's dungeons in order to receive the respective power-up from said Great Fairy. Once the Great Fairy is restored and regains its true power, it will appear as soon as the young [[Hero of Time]] approaches the fountain. He also can be seen waving goodbye to the Great Fairy as it disappears.

Another aspect that makes the Fairy's Fountains different from previous ''Zelda'' games is that Stray Fairies cannot be put into a bottle, but the Fairies themselves can heal him. The fountain that is [[Beneath the Well]] contains regular Fairies which can be put into bottles.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Stub|OoS}}
There are six Fairy's Fountains scattered across [[Holodrum]] in {{OoS}}.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Stub|OoA}}
There are six Fairy's Fountains scattered across [[Labrynna]] in {{OoA}}. Four of the Fountains can only be accessed during the present, one can only be accessed during the past while the Fairy's Fountain in the [[Zora Seas]] can be accessed in both the past and the present. The [[Fairy Queen]] resides in that Fountain during the past but the Fountain lies empty during the present.

==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:TWW Fairy Fountain.jpg|thumb|right|A Fairy Fountain from {{TWW|-}}|216x216px]]
The Fairy Fountains in {{TWW}} are all located in different islands of the [[Great Sea]], and are named Fairy Fountains instead of Great Fairy Fountains despite that they house a Great Fairy within.<ref>{{Cite|Well, the problem is, I haven't seen any Fairies in these parts recently. Of course, long ago you could find a lot of them up by the old Fairy Fountain, and you could even capture them in empty bottles.|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> They are easily spotted since the ones that are out in the sea look like houses shaped like seashells. Their entrances are all blocked by certain obstacles such as giant rocks, fires, or vines that must be removed using [[Bomb]]s or the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer|link}}. The inside of the fountains still retain the same large pond from which the Great Fairies come out of, and the inside walls have the same appearance as well. There are also several seashells aligned to the walls, keeping to the outside characteristics of the fountain. One additional Fairy Fountain can also be found under a boulder in the [[Forest of Fairies]] atop [[Outset Island]]<ref>{{Cite|It was also said that whoever met with the Great Fairy who lived here was blessed with great fortune, so it came to be known as the Fountain of the Fortune Goddess.|N/A|TWW}}</ref>.

After Link has received the upgrades or power-ups from its respective Fairy Fountain, the Great Fairy will no longer be at the fountain. Instead, [[Fairy|Fairies]] will be floating over the pond, ready to heal the young hero or be put in an Empty [[Bottle]]. To see the list of the Fairy Fountains as well as the upgrades received from each one, see [[Great Fairy Fountain#The Wind Waker|here]]. It should be noticed that once all of the Fairies are used, they do not reappear in the fountain, unlike other games.

==={{TMC|-}}===
There are seven Fairy Fountains in {{TMC}}. Four of them can be found hidden in {{Plural|TMC|Cave|link}} behind a wall that must be opened with [[Bomb]]s. One is in [[South Hyrule Field]] behind some tall grass by the river, another is between two rocks in the lower level of the [[Trilby Highlands]], a third is in the northeast of [[Mount Crenel's Base]] between two trees, and the fourth, which has a [[Piece of Heart]] waiting in the middle of it, is between jagged rocks at the base of the [[Crenel Wall]] in [[Mount Crenel]]. A Fairy Fountain inside a tree in [[North Hyrule Field]] and another Fairy Fountain under the fountain on the left of the [[Hyrule Castle Garden]] can randomly be opened by [[Kinstone Fusion|Fusing]] green [[Kinstone]]s with anyone. One more Fairy Fountain can also be accessed after Fusing a blue Kinstone with {{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)|link}} after completing the [[Fortress of Winds]]. This fusion will cause a small shoal to appear in the Trilby Highlands in front of a {{Term|TMC|Cave}} containing the Fountain that can be dug into with the [[Mole Mitts]].

==={{TP|-}}===
Unlike previous ''Zelda'' games, the Fairy Fountains in {{TP}} are not located inside {{Plural|TPHD|Cave|link}} or other well-hidden places. Instead, after meeting a [[Great Fairy]] on every tenth floor of the [[Cave of Ordeals]], she will reward Link by releasing Fairies at the [[Spirit Spring]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, Hero, to the Cave of Ordeals. I am the Great Fairy. I reign over all fairies of this realm as their queen. In praise of your efforts thus far, I will release fairies to Ordon Spring.|Great Fairy|TP}}</ref>

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Fairy Fountains are located in well hidden places in {{ALBW}}, including certain dungeons. Many of these are located in the same locations. However, much like in {{ALttP|-}}, some fountains require certain items for proper entry, such as the [[Ice Rod]] or [[Tornado Rod]] needing to be used to momentarily extinguish some flames in a cavern leading to the fountain.

==Other Appearances==
==={{TFoE|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Fairy Fountain-like areas inhabited by a pair of [[Fairy|Fairies]] appear in the islands of [[Koridai]] in {{TFoE}}. A Fairy Fountain can be found in the mid-point of [[Spearfish Falls]]. The Fairies there give Link a [[Heart Container|Life Heart]] on his first visit, and some [[Water of Life]] for every subsequent visit. If Link owns a [[Canteen]], the Fairies will refill it with Water of Life.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{TWoG|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Fairy Fountain-like areas inhabited by a pair of [[Fairy|Fairies]] appear in the islands of [[Gamelon]] in {{TWoG}}. The Fairies live in the [[Fairy Pool]], but were imprisoned when the fountain was frozen by the [[Three Witches]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] defeats the Three Witches and frees the Fairies, who then give her a Life Heart, some [[Fairy Dust]], and Water of Life, and will continue to supply her with Water of Life for every visit. If Zelda owns a [[Canteen]], the Fairies will refill it with Water of Life.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW}}, Fairy Fountains are Keeps that can be found on several Stages such as [[Hyrule Field (Stage)|Hyrule Field]], [[Faron Woods (Hyrule Warriors)|Faron Woods]], the [[Valley of Seers]], [[Ganon's Tower (Stage)|Ganon's Tower]], and Stages where Fairy Fountains were not originally found such as {{Term|HWDE|Skyloft (Stage)|link}} and the [[Palace of Twilight (Hyrule Warriors)|Palace of Twilight]]. In various [[Scenario]]s, the [[Hyrulean Forces]] or the [[Skyloft Forces]] will attempt to use the Fairy Fountains to call upon the aid of the [[Great Fairy]] to weaken the opposing [[Forces]] or, in "[[The Sorceress of the Woods]]", to save the [[Deku Tree]].
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The iconic Fairy Fountain theme heard since {{aLttP}}, composed by [[Koji Kondo]], is rumored to have been influenced by the 1982 Japanese hit song "[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r5PH4RjofGE ''Morning Glory'']" by [[wikipedia:Tatsuro_Yamashita|Tatsuro Yamashita]], from his ''[[wikipedia:For_You_(Tatsuro_Yamashita_album)|For You]]'' album.
**The background theme has also been used as the [[File-Selection Screen|File Select]] theme for most games since the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] installment, with the exception being {{LA}}, {{OoS}}, {{OoA}}, {{BotW}}, and {{TotK}}.
**Coincidentally, the Fairy Fountain theme for [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] is actually a rendition of the theme of [[mariowiki:Water_Land|Water Land/Sea Side]] from ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros._3|Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' series]].
[[File:MM Ikana Fairy Fountain (NTSC bug).png|thumb|255x255px|The incorrect color of the Ikana Canyon Fairy Fountain seen in the NTSC releases of {{MM|-}}]]

* Due to a programming bug, in the Japanese and North American releases of {{MM}}, the Fairy Fountain of [[Ikana Canyon]] has its theme color being green rather than yellow, appearing like the one from [[Snowhead Region|Snowhead]]. This is fixed in the PAL version. A shade of yellow can however still be seen in the background when Link exits this fountain.
** However, and strangely, if Link were to complete the [[Stone Tower Temple]] and then visit this fountain, it will be in the correct color even in the NTSC versions.

* The [[Fairy Queen]] in {{TWW}} has a theme song based on the Fairy Fountain's, but is rather in a goofy manner. This is likely a reference to herself resembling a child.
