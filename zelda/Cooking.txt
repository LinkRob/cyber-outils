{{Term/Store|Cooking||Series, BotW}} is a recurring mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= Cooking is an essential feature of the adventure. Not only can cooked dishes offer powerful healing properties, they also provide additional effects with all sorts of applications. |game= BotW |page= 24 |publisher= Piggyback |edition= Expanded Edition |year= 2018}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Cooking}} in {{BotW}} is the process of using a variety of {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} to create {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat to replenish his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and gain certain effects. {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} can be achieved either by {{Term|BotW|Category:Roasted Food|link|display=roasting}} edible {{Plural|BotW|Material}} with direct {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, such as with a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} or a flaming {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, by {{Term|BotW|Category:Frozen Food|link|display=freezing}} edible {{Plural|BotW|Material}} by placing them in a location with a cold temperature, or by {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} up to five {{Plural|BotW|Material}} with a lit {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}.<ref name="Piggyback 295">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|page= 295|publisher= Piggyback|edition= Expanded Edition|year= 2018}}</ref><ref name="Piggyback 296">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|page= 296|publisher= Piggyback|edition= Expanded Edition|year= 2018}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Category:Roasted Food|link|display=Roasted Food}} causes the former {{Plural|BotW|Material}} to lose their potential effects in exchange for a 50% increase in their health restorative properties.<ref name="Piggyback 295"/> Certain {{Plural|BotW|Material}} can also be frozen by exposing it to cold temperatures, such as shooting it with an {{Term|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}} and leaving it outside in places such as the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. Doing this will add on a {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}} effect to the meal, and in some cases, even an increase in health restoration. Properly {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} with a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}} doubles the heart recovery of the used {{Plural|BotW|Material}}; depending on the properties of the {{Plural|BotW|Material}}, the resulting {{Term|BotW|Food}} product may also have a certain effect attached to it.<ref name="Piggyback 296"/> {{Term|BotW|Food}} and {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}} cooked in {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot}} occupy one {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} slot each in the {{Term|BotW|Food}} tab, while roasted and frozen {{Term|BotW|Food}} of the same type stack on each other.<ref name="Piggyback 295"/><ref name="Piggyback 296"/> Different individuals, posters, and books will share recipes with {{Term|BotW|Link}} and some characters may request a recipe for a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, therefore showing {{Term|BotW|Link}} the prerequisites for {{Plural|BotW|Material}}.

{{Term|BotW|Food}} is made of basic {{Plural|BotW|Material}} that is known for its tendency to regenerate large amounts of hearts as well as having special effects. Alongside regular {{Term|BotW|Food}}, {{Plural|BotW|Elixir}} are another {{Term|BotW|Item}} that can be made in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}. {{Plural|BotW|Elixir}} can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} together critters and {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}.<ref name="Piggyback 299">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|page= 299|publisher= Piggyback|edition= Expanded Edition|year= 2018}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} or critters will fail and result in {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}} unless the other required {{Term|BotW|Material}} is provided. Critters control the effect's potency while {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} control the duration of the effect.<ref name="Piggyback 299"/> Having all but one critter will produce much longer-lasting {{Plural|BotW|Elixir}} while having all but one {{Term|BotW|Monster Part}} will generally create high-strength {{Plural|BotW|Elixir}}. On top of the two required {{Plural|BotW|Material}}, basic {{Term|BotW|Food}} {{Plural|BotW|Material}} can be optionally added for additional health regeneration or effect potency and duration. The difference between {{Plural|BotW|Elixir}} and dishes is purely cosmetic, since the effects obtained and {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} restored by the recipe are defined only by the {{Plural|BotW|Material}} used.

If a recipe has critter but doesn't have a monster part, has {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} but doesn't have a critter, or has {{Plural|BotW|Material}} that are not valid in creating {{Term|BotW|Food}} (i.e. {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}}), the result will be either {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food}} or {{Term|BotW|Rock-Hard Food|link}}. Some specific {{Plural|BotW|Material}} can also create {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food}} or {{Term|BotW|Rock-Hard Food}} if used alone (i.e. {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}}).

===={{Term|BotW|Food}} Effects====
The amount of {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge}} a recipe can regenerate is double the amount of {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} the {{Plural|BotW|Material}} would restore if consumed raw.<ref name="Piggyback 296"/> If the {{Term|BotW|Material}} is just roasted, it'll be able to restore just 50% more {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} than the raw {{Term|BotW|Material}}.<ref name="Piggyback 295"/>

There are eleven extra effects that can be added to {{Term|BotW|Food}} while {{Term|BotW|Cooking}}: {{Term|BotW|Attack Up|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Defense Up|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Stealth Up|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Movement Speed Up|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Flame Guard|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Stamina Restoration|link}}, and Full Recovery + {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart|link}}.<ref name="Piggyback 296"/> Depending on the {{Plural|BotW|Material}} used in the {{Term|BotW|Food}}, the effect can have a higher level or longer duration. {{Term|BotW|Attack Up}} effects will increase the attack power of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}}, with higher levels of strength increasing the amount of power gained. {{Term|BotW|Defense Up}} effects will increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s defense, making all forms of damage do less damage than normal, with higher levels of strength decreasing the amount of damage taken. {{Term|BotW|Stealth Up}} effects will increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}, making him quieter and avoid the attention of {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}}. Higher levels of {{Term|BotW|Steath Up}} make it increasingly harder for {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} and {{Plural|BotW|Creature}} to detect {{Term|BotW|Link}}. {{Term|BotW|Movement Speed Up}} effects will increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s movement speed,{{How Many}} with higher levels increasing the amount of speed gained. <!-- Does this increase attack speed, swim speed, etc.? By what percent or amount does each level increase speed? --> {{Term|BotW|Flame Guard}} effects will make {{Term|BotW|Link}} invulnerable to scorching temperatures and most {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. While under this effect, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can safely walk through areas like {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}} without the need of protective clothing. However, the effect will not protect {{Term|BotW|Link}} from {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} or attacks from {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} that use {{Term|BotW|Fire}} to attack. <!-- Can you use a Fireproof Elixir in the Gerudo Desert to protect from heat? --> {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance}} effects will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} an immunity to cold temperatures with higher levels giving protection to increasingly cold temperatures. However, no levels of this effect allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to swim in freezing {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} without taking damage, give {{Term|BotW|Link}} protection against {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} that use {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} in their attacks, or prevent {{Term|BotW|Link}} from being frozen. {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance}} effects will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a resistance to {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}}. Unlike the {{Term|BotW|Flame Guard}} effect, no amount of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance}} will protect {{Term|BotW|Link}} from the scorching temperatures of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain}}, but both do not provide protection against direct contact to {{Term|BotW|Fire}}. {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance}} effects protect {{Term|BotW|Link}} against {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. While no levels of these effects completely negate the damage taken from {{Term|BotW|Electricity}}, each level increasingly lowers the damage taken.<!-- How much does each level lower the damage? --> {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina}} effects give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a temporary boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}'s capacity.  {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina}} will not go away until {{Term|BotW|Link}} uses it up, meaning that the entire {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel}} must be depleted before the {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina}} is used. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can have up to two full {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina}} bars. {{Term|BotW|Stamina Restoration}} effects restore an amount of depleted {{Term|BotW|Stamina}}. {{Term|BotW|Stamina Restoration}} will only take affect once {{Term|BotW|Link}} consumes the {{Term|BotW|Food}} or {{Term|BotW|Elixir}} with a depleted or partially depleted {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel}} (i.e. if {{Term|BotW|Link}} was mid-flight with the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} and drank an {{Term|BotW|Elixir}} to replenish his {{Term|BotW|Stamina}}). Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} are an effect that appear on hearty food (i.e. {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}}). Whenever {{Term|BotW|Food}} is cooked to produce the {{Term|BotW|Extra Heart}} effect, the {{Term|BotW|Food}} always provides a full recovery of the {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge}} along with the {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}}.<ref name="Piggyback 296" /> {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} are kept for an indefinite amount of time until damage is taken, in which the {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} are the first to deplete. Once any {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} are depleted, they cannot be regained unless another dish with the {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} effect is consumed.

=====Effect Strength=====
Most effects can have a low, middle, or high strength level of (some only have a low or middle level). The higher the strength level of the effect, the greater amount of effect that will be gained by {{Term|BotW|Link}}. The sum of the {{Plural|BotW|Material}}' potency grades defines the effect's strength level. If the potency grade of a recipe is equal or greater than the high threshold, the recipe will have a high-level effect. If the grade is equal or greater than the middle threshold but is less than the high threshold (or if the effect doesn't have high-level), the effect will be mid-level. If the grade is less than the middle threshold, the recipe will have a low-level effect. =====Effect Duration=====
The effect duration given for each recipe is defined as follows:
*Each individual {{Term|BotW|Material}} adds 30 seconds to effect's duration.
*Each {{Term|BotW|Material}} of the same effect will give a bonus duration besides the 30 seconds. This bonus duration varies for each effect.
*Certain {{Plural|BotW|Material}} will give a bonus duration besides the 30 seconds. (See [[#Special Materials|below]])
*Each {{Term|BotW|Monster Part|link}} add a bonus duration besides the 30 seconds. This bonus duration depends on the {{Term|BotW|Monster Part}}'s potency. (See [[#Monster Parts|below]])

The following equation also represents the duration of a recipe's effect, in seconds:
<pre>
[(30 · n) + (effect_bonus · effect_ingredients)] + special_materials_bonus + monster_parts_bonus
</pre>

where:
*<code>n</code> is the amount of ingredients used.
*<code>effect_bonus</code> is the effect-specific duration bonus provided by each ingredient with the same effect that is added to the recipe.
*<code>effect_ingredients</code> is the amount of ingredients with the same effect used on the recipe.
*<code>special_materials_bonus</code> is the total duration bonus added by certain {{Plural|BotW|Material}} that provide bonus duration besides the 30 seconds.
*<code>monster_parts_bonus</code> is the total duration bonus provided by {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}}.

Besides adding to the effect's potency, adding more ingredients with the same effect to the recipe will provide a bonus duration time (besides the 30 seconds that counts for each ingredient used). This bonus varies for each effect, as shown in the table below.

=====Non Time-Based Effects=====
Some effects do not have a duration time and are either have no timed expiration or only provide for instant benefit. These effects include {{Term|BotW|Stamina Restoration|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} expand the limit of {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and will only expire once {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes damage and depletes them, where they cannot be regained unless the another {{Term|BotW|Extra Heart}} effect is used. All "hearty" {{Plural|BotW|Material}} have their own amount of {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} that will be added to the recipe's effect. The total extra {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} provided by a recipe is the sum of each individual ingredients' {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} value. Furthermore, every recipe that provides extra {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} will also regenerate all of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge}}. ===Examples===
The mechanics on cooking can be quite complex, so some examples of dishes could help to understand it better.

A recipe with two {{Plural|BotW|Electric Darner|link}}, two {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Guts|link}} and one {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Horn|link}} will result in an Electric Elixir. The time duration will be 0:30*5, due to the base 30 seconds offered by each ingredient, plus 2:00*2 due to the effect-specifc bonus provided by each {{Term|BotW|Electric Darner}}, plus 2:40*2 due to the {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Guts}} used (level-2 monster part) and plus 0:40 for the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Horn}} used (level-1 monster part), resulting in a total duration of 12:30. The potency grade will be 2*2=4, since two {{Plural|BotW|Electric Darner}} were used, resulting in a mid-level electric resistance effect.

A recipe with three {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, one {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice|link}} and one {{Term|BotW|Bird Egg|link}} will result in a Mighty Fish Skewer. The duration will be 0:30*5 since five ingredients were used, plus 0:20*3 due to the effect-specific time bonus given by each {{Term|BotW|Mighty Porgy}} used, plus 1:00 from the dish enhancer {{Term|BotW|Bird Egg}} and plus another 1:00 from the dish enhancer {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}}, resulting in 5:30 total duration. The total potency grade will be 3*3=9, since each of the three {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy}} give 3 potency grade, resulting in a high-level attack up effect.

A recipe with two {{Plural|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog|link}}, one {{Term|BotW|Rushroom|link}} and one {{Term|BotW|Lynel Guts|link}} will result in a Hasty Elixir. The duration will be 0:30*4 because four ingredients were used, plus 3*0:30 from the effect-specific bonus because 3 speed-up ingredients were used (two {{Plural|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog}} and one {{Term|BotW|Rushroom}}) and plus 2:40 from the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Guts}} used, resulting in a total duration of 6:10. The total potency grade will be 2*2 from the {{Plural|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog}} plus 1 from the {{Term|BotW|Rushroom}}, resulting in a high-level speed-up effect.

===Critical Cook===
Cooking has a chance for random bonuses, otherwise known as a 'critical cook', and is a 1:10 chance or 10% chance per attempt. This becomes a 100% chance with blood moon risings, however before the cutscene so the exact time being 11:35pm-11:55pm. The window for this is actually incredibly short, as it is technically only 20 seconds.

A critical cook will have one of the following effects selected randomly on the recipe, if the recipe supports it:
*+3 {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} restored.
*+5:00 duration bonus.
* +1 effect level.
*+1 extra maximum {{Term|BotW|Heart}}.
*+2/5 {{Plural|BotW|Stamina Wheel}} restored.
*+2/5 extra maximum {{Plural|BotW|Stamina Wheel}}.

===Monster Extract===
{{Term|BotW|Monster Extract|link}} may slightly or drastically improve three factors (hearts, duration, effect) but may also may incur no changes or make them worse. A meal using {{Term|BotW|Monster Extract}} may see nothing or only duration boosted but may also have all three boost-types applied; whether positively or negatively. {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} with it will never trigger random chances for a bonus, even if {{Plural|BotW|Star Fragment}} or {{Term|BotW|Dragon}} parts are added.

===Extras===
Combining certain types of ingredients in a pot can result in many different dishes. There are a ton of delicious-looking food sprites found in Breath of the Wild, and that gives an astonishing amount of diversity to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s menu, despite the sprite does not change anything in terms of effects. When consuming a piece of food, cooked or not, there are a few different animations {{Term|BotW|Link}} can undergo while "eating it", despite the food doesn't appear and {{Term|BotW|Link}} looks like he is eating nothing. The types of food that have different animations are:
*Small {{Term|BotW|Food}} items, like {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}}.
*{{Term|BotW|Food}} on plates, like {{Term|BotW|Spicy Sautéed Peppers|link}}.
*Soup.
*Skewers, like {{Plural|BotW|Seafood Skewer|link}}.
*Bottled {{Term|BotW|Food}}, usually {{Plural|BotW|Elixir}}.
*{{Term|BotW|Dubious Food}} and {{Term|BotW|Rock-Hard Food}}.
