The {{Term/Store|Jeweled Dagger|Jeweled Daggers|Series, CoH}} is an item in {{CoH}}.<ref name="Obtention">{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Jeweled Dagger}} {{Icon|CoH Zelda}}{{Icon|CoH Cadence}}<br/>Deals {{Color|CoH Red|4 damage}} to a single enemy in front of you.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Out of all the many {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}}, the {{Term|CoH|Jeweled Dagger}} is the strongest type of {{Term|CoH|Dagger|link}}, dealing 4 damage to a single enemy standing in front of {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref name="Obtention"/>  It functions identically to the {{Term|CoH|Kokiri Sword|link}}, and if the {{Term|CoH|Kokiri Sword}} is obtained before the {{Term|CoH|Jeweled Dagger}}, it'll automatically end up in {{Term|CoH|Cadence}}'s and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}}'s inventory. Like all other {{Plural|CoH|Jeweled Dagger}}, it can only be used by {{Term|CoH|Cadence}}'s and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}}.

==Trivia==
In ''Crypt of the NecroDancer'', the {{Term|CoH|Jeweled Dagger}} retains the same role as the strongest type of {{Term|CoH|Dagger|link}} within the game, although it deals a whopping 5 damage to enemies as opposed to the one that appears in {{CoH}}.
