==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Scroll of Enchant Armor}} are items that temporarily increases a hero's defense by .5, reducing the number of hearts lost from enemy attacks and hazards. Collecting multiple {{Plural|CoH|Scroll of Enchant Armor}} will not stack, meaning that collecting multiple scrolls of the same type will not add more scrolls to the hero's inventory, forcing the heroes to be carefully manage how they use them and when they should pick up extras if there are any present.

They can be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}}, or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or as a random reward after destroying a stack of books with an explosive.
