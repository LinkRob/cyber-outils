The {{Term/Store|Rito Gear|Rito Gear|Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Rito Gear}} is weaponry crafted by the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}}. It is designed to be both lightweight and sturdy, in order to be usable by Rito warriors during flight.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Feathered Edge}}<br/>Rito craftsmen forged this lightweight, double-edged sword so Rito warriors could soar into battle unhindered by its weight.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Feathered Spear}}<br/> 'Its lightweight design is a hallmark of Rito craftsmanship. It's made from light and sturdy materials, which afford Rito warriors ease of use during aerial combat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Rito}} {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} are also engineered for swift aerial combat, and they are designed to be drawn faster than normal {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} in order to afford a higher rate of fire.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Swallow Bow}}<br/> This bow is a favorite among Rito warriors. The bowstring has been specially engineered for aerial combat, which allows it to be drawn faster than a normal bow.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Falcon Bow}}<br/> A highly refined Rito-made bow created by a master Rito craftsman. Rito warriors favor it for its superior rate of fire, which helps them excel even further at aerial combat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

A majority of {{Term|BotW|Rito Gear}} is constructed out of wood, which makes them vulnerable to burning when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. The inverse is true of the {{Term|BotW|Feathered Edge|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Feathered Spear|link}}, which are made out of metal.
