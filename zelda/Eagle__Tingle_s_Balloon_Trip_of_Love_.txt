{{Name|Japanese|Eagle}} is a character in {{RTBToL}}.{{Name Ref|RTBToL}} He is first seen at the beginning of {{Term|RTBToL|Page 10|link}}, standing next to an office where his mother works as a receptionist.

==Biography==
When {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} enters a licensing office to apply for a passport, the receptionist, who is revealed to be {{Term|RTBToL|Eagle}}'s mother, informs him that he and his friends will need a work license as a requirement to obtain the passport. Then, {{Term|RTBToL|Eagle}}'s mother suggests to go see his son, who she calls "Shīshī-chan," and ask him for a job. 

When {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} meets {{Term|RTBToL|Eagle}}, he will not pay attention, however, once {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} calls him Shīshī-chan, he will get embarrassed and processed to help {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} if he keeps that name as a secret. After that, {{Term|RTBToL|Eagle}} will give {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} a memo which contains a riddle. When the riddle is solved, it will lead {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} to a secret agent, where he is given another riddle.

As part of a {{Term|RTBToL|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} returns to {{Term|RTBToL|Page 10|link}}. There, the receptionist will ask him to help {{Term|RTBToL|Eagle}} find a job. To do this, he must speak to the old tree on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 4|link}} and obtain his sapling. When he gives it to {{Term|RTBToL|Eagle}}, he will plant it in the city park and succeed in growing it, rewarding {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} with a {{Term|RTBToL|Secret Notes|display=secret|link}}.
