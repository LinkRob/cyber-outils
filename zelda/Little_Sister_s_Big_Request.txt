"{{Term/Store|Little Sister's Big Request||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Little Sister's Big Request}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|South Akkala Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} can be obtained from {{Term|BotW|Jana|link}}, a worker at the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}. Initially, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} is dismissive towards {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} and will ask him what he wants.<ref>{{Cite|What do YOU want?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says he is just hanging out, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} will be suspicious of him but asks that he leaves her alone, as she is thinking about something.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm just hanging out...] We'll see about that... I'm thinking about something right now... Can you please not bother me?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> During this time, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Jana}}'s little sister, {{Term|BotW|Gleema|link}}, she will share that {{Term|BotW|Jana}} loves {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My big sister, Jana, loves {{Color|BotW Blue|armoranth}}!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Jana}} with at least one {{Term|BotW|Armoranth|link}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, she will take notice and ask for it.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm... What's that? What do you have there? That's {{Color|BotW Blue|armoranth}}, isn't it?! Could you...um...maybe give me some?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} allows her to take it, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} states that, since he had her favorite plant, she may be able to get along with him.<ref>{{Cite|For you to be walking around with my favorite plant... It seems you and I may just get along after all.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She asks what {{Term|BotW|Link}} is doing in {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} is famous for... Well, nothing. So I have to ask... What could you possibly have come here for?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> and if he responds that he is a traveler or an adventurer, she will have a request.<ref>{{Cite|I see... A traveler, eh? In that case, I have a request for you.|Jana|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Jana}} shares that {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} is a fan of {{Plural|BotW|Insect|link}}, which she does not understand.<ref>{{Cite|My little sister, Gleema, is a huge fan of {{Color|BotW Blue|insects}}. I know, I don't get it either.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She knows that there is a {{Term|BotW|Insect}} that {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} wants to see, but she will not tell her what it is.<ref>{{Cite|But apparently there's a certain {{Color|BotW Blue|insect}} she's dying to see in real life. She won't tell me about it, though. I suppose there are some things you just can't talk about with those you're closest to.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to talk with {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} and see if he can figure out what she wants to see,<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, would you mind talking to her and trying to figure out which silly {{Color|BotW Blue|insect}} she's so crazy about?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> as it is for {{Term|BotW|Gleema}}'s birthday.<ref>{{Cite|It's for her birthday... Think of it as a special birthday favor. Ooh, or even a birthday quest. Look, just do it, OK?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

{{Term|BotW|Gleema}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she has a secret when he speaks to her, and she asks if he wants to know it.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, guess what! I have? A SECRET! Wanna know what it is?!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> She reveals that {{Term|BotW|Jana}} hates dragonflies.<ref>{{Cite|♪Tee heeeee!♪ OK, I'm gonna tell you! Ready? Here it coooooomes! My big sister, Jana, hates {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}!|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Once, she saw a picture of a {{Term|BotW|Warm Darner|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Cold Darner|link}}, and an {{Term|BotW|Electric Darner|link}} in a book,<ref>{{Cite|I once saw pictures of a {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, a {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, and an {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}} in a book...|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Jana}} thought their eyes were creepy and was upset.<ref>{{Cite|But when I showed them to Jana, she said their eyes were creepy and got mad.|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} is shocked when he remembers that was a secret and believes that {{Term|BotW|Jana}} will be mad.<ref>{{Cite|... Oh... That was a secret, wasn't it?! Jana is gonna be mad at me again!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} believes the only reason {{Term|BotW|Jana}} did not like them was because of the up-close pictures, and she thinks real dragonflies would be cute.<ref>{{Cite|But I think that's because the pictures were all up close and stuff! I bet real ones are super cute!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> When she grows up, {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} wants to travel the world and meet dragonflies.<ref>{{Cite|When I grow up, I wanna travel the world and meet some real {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref>

Returning to {{Term|BotW|Jana}}, she is shocked to hear that {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} wants to see a {{Term|BotW|Warm Darner}}, a {{Term|BotW|Cold Darner}}, and an {{Term|BotW|Electric Darner}}.<ref>{{Cite|No... now way! Of all things, she wants to see a gross {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonfly}}?! A {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, a {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, AND an {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}}? {{Color|BotW Red|Three different types}}?!|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She realizes that {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} did not tell her because of how much she dislikes them.<ref>{{Cite|Ugggh... Anything but {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}... Just thinking about their eyes makes me feel sick. I see. So Gleema knows that I despise {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}, and that's why she wouldn't tell me how much she loves them...|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Since it is for her birthday, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring her the three different dragonflies for {{Term|BotW|Gleema}}'s surprise.<ref>{{Cite|Well, this is her birthday wish... I didn't have a choice. Bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}}, please. I want to surprise Gleema, so don't tell her about this!|Jana|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} already has all three dragonflies, he will not have to go looking for them.<ref>{{Cite|Hey... You already found them all, didn't you?|Jana|BotW}}</ref>}}

{{Plural|BotW|Warm Darner}} are common in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Cold Darner}} can be found on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and in cold areas, such as the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Electric Darner}} are also found on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge}}, specifically around {{Term|BotW|Rutile Lake|link}}, but they can also be located within the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon|link}}. In addition, {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} sells certain dragonflies depending on his location.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}} with the dragonflies, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} will ask for them.<ref>{{Cite|Have you secured a {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}}? {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|*gulp*}}}} Right... In that case, may I have them?|Jana|BotW}}</ref> However, she is unable to bring herself to take them from {{Term|BotW|Link}}, and she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to give them to {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} for her.<ref>{{Cite|... ... {{Small|Ewww...}} BAH! I CAN'T DO THIS! Please give them to her...just...give them to Gleema yourself!|Jana|BotW}}</ref>

At first, {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} is intrigued to hear that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has something for her.<ref>{{Cite|[These are for you.] Really? What is it? What is it?!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> She is shocked to see the dragonflies and asks if they are really for her.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|WOW! Real-life {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}!!}} Are these really for me?!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> Excitedly, she calls out to {{Term|BotW|Jana}} and runs to show her the dragonflies.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Big Sis! Lookee, lookee! It's a real life {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}! And a {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}} and an {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}} too!! I'll bring 'em over so you can see!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Jana}} yells at her to stop,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|HEY! Cut it out!!}}|Jana|BotW}}</ref> before eventually screeching in terror.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EEEEEEK!!}}|Jana|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Jana}} has calmed down,<ref>{{Cite|Hey. I've managed to calm down. Somehow.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> she decides to thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} for catching the dragonflies.<ref>{{Cite|Now then... I must thank you for catching those {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She gives him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here, take this.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> She explains to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that while she is okay with other {{Plural|BotW|Insect}}, she can not stand dragonflies.<ref>{{Cite|I'm find with most insects. The only ones I simply can't stand are {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}. When I think about them looking at me with those evil eyes... *shudder*|Jana|BotW}}</ref> With a sigh, she tells herself to take a deep breath.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* That was rough. OK... I need to take a deep breath.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Once she is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} ends.
