The '''Hyrule Town Well''' is a [[Locations in The Minish Cap|location]] in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Hyrule Town Well is a [[well]] located in the southeastern area of [[Hyrule Town]], just above the homes of [[Romio]] and [[Julietta]]. [[Link]] can fall down the Well, leading him to one portion of the [[Hyrule Underground]], though the [[Flippers]] are required to progress any further within it and access the [[Treasure Chest]]s.
