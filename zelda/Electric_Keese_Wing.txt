==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Electric Keese Wing}} are dropped by {{Term|BotW|Electric Keese|link}} upon their defeat. Like other {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}}, they can be {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}} with critters in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be sold to {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} or used at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} to make {{Term|BotW|Yellow Dye|link}}.
