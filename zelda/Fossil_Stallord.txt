{{Term/Store|Fossil Stallord||Series, LCT}} is the final {{Term|LCT|Enemy|link}} fought in {{LCT}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Fossil Stallord Battle}}<br/>It's the final battle! Crush the Fossil Stallord before time runs out!|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|LCT|Fossil Stallord}} is the last {{Term|LCT|Enemy}} {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} fights. It is similar to {{Term|TPHD|Stallord|link}} from {{TP|-}}, but no longer has {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Scimitar of Twilight|link}} embedded in its skull and has ten horns instead of just four. It attacks by showering {{Term|LCT|Link}} with meteor-like projectiles and striking him with its hands. It is vulnerable in its palms and head. When {{Term|LCT|Fossil Stallord}} has taken significant damage, it shoots out {{Plural|LCT|Bubble|link}} to attack {{Term|LCT|Link}} while it moves forward. Once it reaches {{Term|LCT|Link}}, {{Term|LCT|Fossil Stallord}} will attack, causing him to lose points. The battle is timed, meaning that {{Term|LCT|Link}} has only 120 seconds to defeat {{Term|LCT|Fossil Stallord}} or he may not receive any bonus points for the stage.

==Trivia==
*During the fight with {{Term|LCT|Fossil Stallord}}, {{Term|TPHD|Stallord|link}}'s theme from {{TP|-}} is not played; instead, {{Term|TPHD|Armogohma|link}}'s theme is played.
