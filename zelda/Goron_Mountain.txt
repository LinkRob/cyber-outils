{{Term/Store|Goron Mountain||Series, OoS}} is a location in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 251}}</ref> It is a {{Term|OoS|Snow|link|display=snowy}} mountain range located in the northern portion of {{Term|OoS|Holodrum}}.

==Features and Overview==
[[File:Biggoron OoS.png|thumb|left|{{Term|OoS|Biggoron}} atop {{Term|OoS|Goron Mountain}}]]
Located in the mountainous region of northern {{Term|OoS|Holodrum|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Goron Mountain}} is situated between {{Term|OoS|Mt. Cucco|link}} to the east and the {{Term|OoS|Temple Remains|link}} to the west. The area of {{Term|OoS|Goron Mountain}} is permanently fixed in winter (likely made to reflect the heightened altitude), and cannot be changed with the {{Term|OoS|Rod of Seasons|link}} since it is one of the few regions in {{Term|OoS|Holodrum}} not to yield {{Plural|OoS|Tree Stump|link}}. With the seasons in chaos due to the sinking of the {{Term|OoS|Temple of Seasons|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Goron Mountain}} is said to become even colder, increasing the possibility of catching cold simply by standing outside on the mountain.<ref name="Cold">{{Cite|Goron Mountain has always had winter year- round, but ever since the Temple of Seasons sank, it's gotten even colder. You can catch cold just going outside.|Goron|OoS}}</ref>

Despite their usual preference for warmer climates, {{Term|OoS|Goron Mountain}} is home to all the {{Plural|OoS|Goron|link}} in {{Term|OoS|Holodrum}}. The {{Plural|OoS|Goron}} reside inside the mountain inside tunnels that they likely dug to shelter themselves from the cold. Much to the relief of the {{Plural|OoS|Goron}}, lava can be found inside the mountain, suggesting that the mountain is a volcano. In a secluded cave in the mountain resides a lone {{Term|OoS|Goron}} who will upgrade {{Term|OoS|Link|link}}'s {{Term|OoS|Ring Box|link}}.

The {{Plural|OoS|Goron}} of {{Term|OoS|Goron Mountain}} frequently complain about the cold and they rarely step outside the mountain.<ref name="Cold"/><ref>{{Cite|The weather is back to normal, but I still hate the cold.|Goron|OoS}}</ref> Unfortunately, {{Term|OoS|Biggoron|link}}, who is unable to fit within the caves, must stay perched atop the mountain, subject to the harsh weather of the mountain.<ref>{{Cite|Biggoron is up above, but he has a cold, so be quiet.|Goron|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I've heard there's a cure for the Goron cold. It's called Lava Soup. I'd like to try it once.|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Unsurprisingly, {{Term|OoS|Biggoron}} catches a cold which can only be cured by the {{Term|OoS|Lava Soup|link}} which {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} obtains in the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lava Soup is so delicious! One sip of that cures any cold!|Subrosian Chef|OoS}}</ref> Later on, {{Term|OoS|Link}} may need to return to {{Term|OoS|Biggoron}} atop the mountain to tell him a secret obtained in a {{Term|Series|Linked Game|link}} of {{OoA}} in order to obtain {{Term|OoA|Biggoron's Sword|link}}.

The foothills of {{Term|OoA|Goron Mountain}} are marked by several pitfalls. The {{Term|OoA|Dragon Key|link}}, which is used to stop the flow of the waterfall on {{Term|OoA|Mt. Cucco}} in order to access the {{Term|OoA|Dancing Dragon Dungeon|link}} can be found in one corner of the mountain, surrounded by rocks and pitfalls. The key can only be reached with the help of {{Term|OoA|Moosh|link}}'s flying abilities.

According to his diary, the {{Term|OoS|Goron Elder|link}} traveled from {{Term|OoS|Labrynna|link}} to {{Term|OoS|Goron Mountain}} long ago.<ref>{{Cite|My ancestor's diary says he came to Goron Mountain from Labrynna long ago.|Goron|OoS}}</ref> The diary is found within the hands of a {{Term|OoS|Goron}} who claims to be the Goron Elder's descendant.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently, my ancestor was the Goron Elder.|Goron|OoS}}</ref>
