"{{Term/Store|The Master Chef||Series, HWAoC}}" is a {{Term|HWAoC|Quest|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''The Master Chef'''|Gallery|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
In "{{Term|HWAoC|The Master Chef}}", the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} buy a seasoned chef 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Mighty Bananas}} in return for 1,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee}}. Even after mastering  the target dish, he offers to pay another thousand {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee}} for the same amount of  {{Plural|HWAoC|Mighty Bananas}} as many times as the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} need.
