{{Term/Store|Gimos|Gimos|Series, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 182}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Gimos}} were referred to as '''Gimos Statues''' in [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds Prima Official Game Guide]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW |publisher= Prima |page= 16}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Term|ALBW|Gimos}} in {{ALBW}} are the {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} counterpart to {{Plural|ALBW|Armos|link}}, though they also appear in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|link}}. They can be defeated with several slashes of the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}. Inactive {{Term|ALBW|Gimos}} can be found throughout {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}} dungeons and can be used to solve puzzles by pushing or pulling them to activate {{Plural|ALBW|Floor Switch|link}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Term|TFH|Gimos}} appear in the {{Term|TFH|Forest Zone}} of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}} in {{TFH}}. They can be defeated with several slashes of the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}. They can also be blown off the main platform with the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}.
