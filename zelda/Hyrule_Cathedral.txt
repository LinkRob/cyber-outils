{{Term/Store|Hyrule Cathedral||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hyrule Cathedral'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Hyrule Cathedral is located in the [[Hyrule Castle Town Ruins]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. The interior of the Cathedral is littered with {{Plural|BotW|Vase|link}}, [[Wooden Box]]es, and [[Barrel]]s containing [[Acorn]]s, [[Apple]]s, and [[Arrow]]s.

A [[Korok]] can be found hiding under a [[Rock]] beneath a [[Natural Hiding Spot]] of leaves.
