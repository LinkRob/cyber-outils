"{{Term/Store|Get N or Get Out||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: Get N or Get Out}}<br/>06/14/2022 |author= Nintendo |published= June 14, 2022 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/21005425/index.html |title= Event: Get N or Get Out |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Get N or Get Out}}" is an event wherein {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} originating from games on the [[Nintendo 64]] appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} at certain times of the day.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Veterans of the Nintendo 64 era will be appearing for a limited time on the Spirit Board!|author= Nintendo |published= June 14, 2022 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url=https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/21005425/index.html|title= Event: Get N or Get Out |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Ace, Advanced, and Novice {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} will appear at certain times of the day.
