==Overview==
[[File:OoT Link Riding Epona Artwork.png|thumb|left|230px|The artwork that the controller's background is based upon]]
The Enhanced Wireless Controller - Gold Rider is a modified version of the regular {{Term|Series|Pro Controller|link}}. The controller's design consists of a silhouette of {{Term|OoT|Link|link}} riding on {{Term|OoT|Epona|link}} while bearing the {{Term|OoT|Master Sword|link}}. The silhouette is of a piece of artwork drawn by [[Yusuke Nakano]] for {{OoT}}. Alongside the buttons from regular {{Plural|Series|Pro Controller}}, the Gold Rider controller sports three custom buttons on the back of the controller that can be mapped by the player to act as any button they wish;<ref name="PowerA Details">{{Cite Web|quote= {{·}}Wireless controller with Bluetooth 5.0 technology<br/>{{·}}Rechargeable lithium-ion battery: Up to 30 hours per charge*<br/>{{·}}Two mappable Advanced Gaming Buttons<br/>{{·}}Embedded anti-friction Rings for smooth thumbstick control<br/>{{·}}Superior ergonomics for hours of comfortable gaming<br/>{{·}}Classic intuitive Nintendo button layout<br/>{{·}}Low battery warning LED indicator<br/>{{·}}Includes 10ft (3m) USB-C charge cable<br/>{{·}}Wireless gaming controller officially licensed for Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch Lite<br/>{{·}}Two-year limited warranty|author= PowerA|published= |retrieved= September 27, 2021|url= https://www.powera.com/p/nintendo/nintendo-switch/controllers/wireless/enhanced-wireless-controller-rechargeable-for-nintendo-switch-gold-rider-1518805-01/|title= Enhanced Wireless Controller for Nintendo Switch - Gold Rider|site= PowerA|type= }}</ref> the controller also supports motion controls like regular {{Plural|Series|Pro Controller}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Motion Control}}<br/>Enjoy super-fun intuitive motion controls for Nintendo Switch. These responsive controls let you tilt left or right to control your racing, your destiny and your game.|author= PowerA|published= |retrieved= September 27, 2021|url= https://www.powera.com/p/nintendo/nintendo-switch/controllers/wireless/enhanced-wireless-controller-rechargeable-for-nintendo-switch-gold-rider-1518805-01/|title= Enhanced Wireless Controller for Nintendo Switch - Gold Rider|site= PowerA|type= }}</ref>
The controller can be wirelessly connected to the [[Nintendo Switch]] through [https://www.bluetooth.com/ Bluetooth], and is compatible with the [[Nintendo Switch Lite]].<ref name="PowerA Details"/> The controller also comes with a 3-meter long USB charging cable for the controller.<ref name="PowerA Details"/>
