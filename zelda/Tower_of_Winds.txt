The {{Term/Store|Tower of Winds||Series, FSA}} is a level that appears in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 154}}</ref>

The Tower is the last part of [[Frozen Hyrule]] and is accessed by gathering the four [[Royal Jewels]] that are guarded by [[Big Dark Stalfos]]. Unlike all other levels in the game, the tower is played almost entirely in a side-scrolling perspective, with the only top down perspective areas appearing on the [[Game Boy Advance]] screen. At the top, [[Frostare]] awaits, guarding [[Princess Zelda]]. After defeating [[Vaati]], [[Ganon]] causes the tower to collapse, and the Links must guide Zelda safely to the exit.
