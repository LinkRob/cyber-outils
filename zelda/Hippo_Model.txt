The {{Term/Store|Hippo Model||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an [[Animal]] character in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 233"/>{{Name Ref|LANS}} She is an anthropomorphic hippo.

==Biography==
The Hippo Model can be found in [[Schule Donavitch]]’s studio in the [[Animal Village]], where she is posing for his painting.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Artist and Model|book= E |page= 233 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref> When [[Link]] walks inside, she immediately sits down and annoyedly asks him to leave when spoken to.<ref>{{Cite|Leave me alone! I'm trying to sit still so Schule can paint my portrait!|Hippo Model|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Go away!|Hippo Model|LADX|334}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Quit it!|Hippo Model|LADX}}</ref>

*In the Japanese and original German and French versions of {{LA}}, the Hippo Model is actually posing nude. She has a towel covering the lower half of her body, and what appear to be exposed breasts on her upper body. When [[Link]] walks in, she quickly pulls her towel up and covers herself. [[Nintendo of America]] deemed this too inappropriate for American audiences, and as a result, removed her breasts and towel to avoid [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda series|controversy]]. This censorship was oddly carried over in the European versions of {{LA|DX}}.
**In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Nintendo Switch remake of {{LA|-}}]], the Hippo Model's design has been compromised to include her hourglass figure, but remain fully dressed. This is to ensure a satisfactory parity for all regional releases of the game.
