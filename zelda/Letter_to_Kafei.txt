The {{Term/Store|Letter to Kafei||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 129}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Letter to Kafei}} was referred to as '''Anju's Letter''' and '''{{Typo|Anju'sLette|Anju's Letter}}''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Anju'sLette}}<br/>Anju's Letter is perhaps the most famous love letter in Terminan history. Delivered by Link, this document helped to reunite Anju with her fiancé Kafei.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160327065338/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/index.jsp#Anju'sLetter|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia|site= Zelda.com|type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It is given to [[Link]] by [[Anju]]. It is essential in the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest.

==Location and Uses==
After showing [[Kafei's Mask]] to Anju, she asks Link to meet her in the kitchen at the [[Stock Pot Inn]] at night.<ref>{{Cite|I have a request! Kafei...I have a clue...that will help you find him. Tonight at 11:30, please come to our kitchen. We'll talk then...|Anju|MM}}</ref> There, she tells Link about [[Kafei]]'s disappearance and gives him the Letter to Kafei, asking him to deliver it for her.<ref>{{Cite|I am sorry to trouble you late at night. It's about him...Kafei. I received a letter from...him. Strange... Isn't it? Getting a letter from a missing person... But there's absolutely no mistaking it's from him!|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Here is my letter. Please put this in a postbox. When this is delivered...When he receives my letter... You should be able to meet him!|Anju|MM}}</ref> To do so, Link must deposit it in any of the {{Plural|MM|Postbox|link}} throughout [[Clock Town]], where the [[Postman#Majora's Mask|Postman]] will pick it up. Link can enter the back room of the [[Curiosity Shop]] in the [[Laundry Pool]] once the Postman delivers it to Kafei, who will run out to receive it, leaving the door unlocked. If Link speaks to Kafei after the Letter has been delivered, he agrees to trust the hero and tells him of his cause for disappearance.<ref>{{Cite|...Can you keep a secret? [...] Anju trusted you. I shall also trust you.|Kafei|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I was turned into this by a strange imp wearing a mask... But I'm not hiding because I look this way. When I was turned into this, I went to see the Great Fairy in the shrine near the North Gate... But on the way, my precious mask--a wedding ceremony mask--was stolen from me by some prancing man with a grinning face!|Kafei|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I know Anju is worried...but I can't go out yet. I made a promise to her that I would bring the wedding mask and greet her.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> Afterward, he will give Link the [[Pendant of Memories]] to pass on to Anju.<ref>{{Cite|This pendant... Give it to Anju.|Kafei|MM}}</ref>

The Letter can be posted on any of the three days. If the Letter is posted on the [[Second Day]], the Postman will pick up the Letter on the [[Final Day]] as normal and go to the Laundry Pool to deliver it, but Kafei will not appear when he rings the bell.<ref>{{Cite|...It seems he is out today.|Postman|MM}}</ref> This happens regardless of whether Kafei has seen [[Sakon]] at the Curiosity Shop the night before or not.

The Letter to Kafei is one of the items used in the Reuniting Kafei and Anju sidequest that can be done to receive the [[Couple's Mask]], the [[Keaton Mask]], the [[Postman's Hat]], two [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]], and a [[Bottle]] with [[Chateau Romani]]. The Letter can also be given to {{Term|MM3D|??? (Majora's Mask)|link}} in the Stock Pot Inn when he asks for paper, thus earning Link a Piece of Heart.
