BradyGames, an imprint of DK and a division of Penguin Random House, is a leading publisher of strategy content for PC and console video games. They produce comprehensive guides featuring in-depth content, detailed screen captures, quick-reference tips, and professional strategies to assist gamers in navigating and mastering various video games. 

Their catalog includes guides for popular titles such as ''Final Fantasy VIII'', ''Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2'', and ''Civilization V''. 

These guides are available through various retailers, including Amazon and Noble Knight Games. 

For more information about BradyGames and their publications, you can visit their author page on Penguin Random House’s website.
