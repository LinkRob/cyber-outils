==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
{{Plural|ST|Star Fragment}} are a common of {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}. They can be found inside {{Plural|ST|Jar|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Treasure Chest|link}}, or under piles of leaves. They can also be bought from shops or won from a {{Term|ST|Prize Postcard|link}}.

{{Term|ST|Link|link}} can sell {{Plural|ST|Star Fragment}} for 50 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}} each, or trade them with {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}} in exchange for {{Plural|ST|Train Car|link}}.<ref name="E 139" /> They are included in the cost of the {{Term|ST|Wooden Engine|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Skull Passenger Car|link}} and the {{Term|ST|Quaint Passenger Car|link}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Star Fragment}} are rare {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Volcano|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Den of Flames|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Star Fragment}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Fire Temple|link}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Star Fragment}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Fire Temple}}''', ''"Only Bombs-no swords!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Star Fragment}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Star Fragment}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Queen of Hearts|link}}.<ref name="E 139"/>

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Star Fragment}} fall from the sky during the night and disappear at 5:00 AM the following day. They will also disappear instantly if they land in any body of water.{{Fact}} When used in a [[Cooking Pot]], Star Fragments will guarantee bonus effects from the meal being made. Alternatively, they can be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make White [[Dye]]. Star Fragments can also be occasionally found in certain [[Treasure Chest]]s such as the metal chest located on the cliffs of [[Tal Tal Peak]] overlooking [[Spool Bight]] of the [[Lanayru Sea]]. {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} may drop them on extremely rare occasions. Link can also receive one for completing the Side Quest "[[My Hero]]" which can be completed as soon as Link acquires the [[Master Sword]].

Star Fragments are used in several armor upgrades at {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. For each piece of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set|link}}, one Star Fragment and two {{Plural|Series|Giant Ancient Core|link}} are needed to upgrade it to its fourth tier. The pieces of the [[Hero Set]], [[Hero of the Sky Set]], [[Hero of Twilight Set]], [[Hero of Time Set]], and [[Hero of Winds Set]] all require a Star Fragment and various minerals at each tier. The pieces of the [[Hero of the Wild Set]] each need one Star Fragment, in addition to either two {{Plural|Series|Shard of Farosh's Horn|link}}, two {{Plural|Series|Shard of Naydra's Horn|link}}, or two {{Plural|Series|Shard of Dinraal's Horn|link}} to upgrade to its final tier. One is also needed to upgrade a [[Diamond Circlet]], a [[Ruby Circlet]], a [[Sapphire Circlet]], and [[Topaz Earrings]] to their third and fourth tiers. The [[Vah Ruta Divine Helm]], [[Vah Rudania Divine Helm]], [[Vah Medoh Divine Helm]], and [[Vah Naboris Divine Helm]] all require a Star Fragment to be upgraded to their fourth tiers. [[Sheik's Mask]] requires star fragments at each tier to be upgraded.
