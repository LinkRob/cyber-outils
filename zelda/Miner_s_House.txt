The {{Term/Store|Miner's House||Series, ALBW}} is a location in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}} It is located west of [[Death Mountain]] and east of the [[Lost Woods]] in [[Hyrule]]. It is the workplace and home of [[Rosso]]. 

==Features and Overview==
The Miner's House cannot be entered by Link before [[Yuga]] captures [[Hyrule Castle]], because Rosso is away mining ore at Death Mountain and has locked the door. The building is surrounded by countless rocks which, according to Rosso, appeared when he came home from his mining expedition – presumably having been shot out of the mountain during an eruption.<ref>{{Cite|I was on the mountain mining ore when, all of a sudden, the earth started shaking! When I got home, the place was a wreck. Rocks everywhere.|Rosso|ALBW}}</ref> If [[Link]] talks to Rosso after his initial encounter with him, he will give him the [[Power Glove]].<ref>{{Cite|You seen outside? I'll be bustin' my back for days to clean up that mess. At least pickin' up rocks and smashin' the things feels pretty good. Wish they were full of good ore, though. What? You want to try too? Rah! Feels good smashin' stuff! Huh? Can't do it? Sorry to hear it. Can't stand to see a nice kid like you not be able to throw your weight around... Here-take this. It's a hand-me-down from yours truly.|Rosso|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Power Glove! Now you can pick up small rocks!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> It can be used to lift the rocks outside, after which Link is rewarded by Rosso with a chest containing a Purple Rupee.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-where have all those darned rocks got to? '''Oh-ho!''' You went ahead and did the job for me! I gotta thank you for all that. Come on in, OK?|Rosso|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Glad to share what's in that chest with you. You earned it, kid!|Rosso|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got a Purple Rupee! It's worth 50 Rupees! Now how about that?!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> After cracks begin to appear across Hyrule, one appears in the Miner's House that gives access to the northwest region of [[Lorule]]. The Lorule equivalent of the Miner's House is a dilapidated building with no inhabitants.

==Trivia==
*The house corresponds to the location of the [[Lumberjack]]s' house in {{ALttP}}.
