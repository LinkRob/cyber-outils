{{Term/Store|Clothes|Clothes|Series, LADX|plural}} are worn by [[Link]] throughout [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 86 {{Exp Game|LADX}}}}</ref> For the majority of each game, Link wears the iconic [[Green Clothes|Green Tunic]], although he can sometimes obtain new types of clothes that grant new abilities, such as breathing underwater or [[Defense|decreasing the damage received from enemies]]. Each type of clothing has a similar style despite varying in color and name: a long shirt that reaches above the knee, having short or no sleeves, and a floppy elf-like hat in a matching color. Certain types of clothes are essential to complete most installments. Like the [[Sword]] and [[Shield]], clothes complete Link's heroic image when worn.

==Types of Clothes==
===Green Clothes===
{{Main|Green Clothes}}

The Green Clothes, also known as Green Tunic, is the default type of clothing worn by [[Link]] throughout the vast majority of each of his adventures, and because of this, it has become a symbol of the hero who saved [[Hyrule]] from evil, much like the [[Master Sword]] and [[Hylian Shield]].<ref>{{Cite|Look at your awakened form... The green tunic that is your garb once belonged to the ancient hero chosen by the gods... His power is yours. His is the true power that slept within you. Your name is [Link]. You are the hero chosen by the gods.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|On a certain island, it became customary to garb boys in green when they came of age. Clothed in the green of fields, they aspired to find heroic blades and cast down evil. The elders wished only for the youths to know courage like the hero of legend...|N/A|TWW}}</ref> Unlike the other types of clothes, however, as it is Link's traditional outfit, it does not grant the young hero any special abilities, making it hazardous to continue wearing this particular clothing late in his adventure in certain games. In a few installments, such as {{TP}} and {{SS}}, Link wears a chain mail underneath it to further protect himself from harm.

===Defense-Enhancement Clothes===
{{Main|Blue Mail, Blue Clothes, Red Mail}}
[[File:LANS Blue Mail Model.png|left|thumb|147x147px|Link wearing the Blue Mail in [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|{{LA|-}} (Switch)]]]]
First introduced in {{TLoZ}} as the [[Blue Ring]] and [[Red Ring]], the [[Blue Mail]] and [[Red Mail]] increase Link's defense, cutting the damage he receives from enemies and few hazards by one-half and one-quarter, respectively.<ref>{{Cite|You got the blue mail! This mail cuts damage by half! Sturdy mail that provides great defense.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the red mail! Damage from enemies has been halved again!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> In these cases, Link's tunic and hat becomes blue or red, respectively, though in {{TLoZ|-}}, the Blue Ring instead makes it appear almost white. These clothing prove useful against stronger enemies and [[Boss|bosses]].

===Special Ability Clothes===
{{Main|Red Clothes, Goron Tunic, Zora Tunic, Zora Armor, Magic Armor, Outfit}}
[[File:Link Goron Tunic.png|thumb|278x278px|Link wearing the Goron Tunic in {{OoT|-}}]]
Certain types of clothes provide Link with special abilities. In {{LA|DX}}, the [[Red Clothes]] double the attack damage of Link's sword. The [[Zora Tunic]] and [[Zora Armor]] grants the hero the ability to breathe underwater,<ref>{{Cite|You got a Zora Tunic! This diving suit is adult size, so it won't fit a kid. Wear it, and you won't drown underwater.|N/A|OoT}}</ref>  while the [[Goron Tunic]] permits him to resist extremely high temperatures.<ref>{{Cite|You got a Goron Tunic! This heat-resistant tunic is adult size, so it won't fit a kid... Going to a hot place? No worry!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> In {{TP}}, the [[Magic Armor]] allows Link to lose [[Rupees]] instead of [[Heart]]s when he is hit by an enemy.<ref>{{Cite|The armor will protect you, so if you get hit, you lose Rupees, not health. If you run out of Rupees, the armor will go dormant and your movement will become impaired.|Malo Mart|TP}}</ref> Like the defense equivalents, Link's outfit is shown as either blue or red.

In {{TFH}}, the [[Links]] can wear various [[Outfit]]s which grant them different powers and abilities depending on the Outfit worn, such as throwing large [[Bomb]]s or finding [[Heart]]s more frequently. Outfits are crafted at [[Madame Couture's]] using a recipe of [[Material]]s found in Levels and a certain amount of Rupees.

In {{BotW}}, Link has a number of different types of clothing known as [[Armor]] which all have special abilities, such as a [[Warm Doublet]] that protects Link from taking damage from the cold.

===Others===
{{Main|Hero's New Clothes, Ordonian Outfit, Engineer's Clothes}}
[[File:Link Outset Clothes.png|left|thumb|192x192px|Link wearing the New Hero's Clothes in {{TWW}}]]
In some games, Link does not start out with his traditional Green Clothes. Games such as {{TP}}, {{ST}}, and {{BotW}} feature the young hero wearing clothes that are typical to his homeplace, such as the [[Ordonian Outfit]], [[Engineer's Clothes]], and [[Champion's Tunic]], respectively. Like the Green Clothes, these do not give Link any new abilities or an enhanced defense.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSB|-}} series===
In the {{SSBS}}, playable characters can wear differently colored outfits or skins, called {{Ssb|alternate costume}}s, or color changes. These are usually recolors of a character's appearance, intended to distinguish between players using the same character. Like all other characters, Link, [[Young Link]], and [[Toon Link]] can wear different alternate costumes in their respective appearances.

In {{SSB}}, Link can wear the Kokiri, Goron, and Zora tunics, as well as a lavender tunic.

In {{SSBM}}, both Link and Young Link can wear the Kokiri, Goron, and Zora tunics, as well as a white and a black tunic.

In {{SSBB}}, Link can wear the Kokiri, Goron, and Zora tunics, as well as a white and a golden tunic. He can also wear black clothing resembling the [[Dark Interlopers]] from {{TP}}. Toon Link can wear green, red, blue, and violet tunics resembling the [[Links]]. He can also wear a tunic resembling the Dark Interlopers, and one resembling Link from {{TLoZ}}, even changing his hair to a brownish color.

In {{SSB4}}, Link can wear the Kokiri, Goron, Zora, violet, golden, and Dark Interloper tunics, as well as a tunic resembling his [[Skyloft]] clothes from {{SS}}, and a tunic resembling the [[Fierce Deity Link]]. Toon Link can wear green, red, blue, violet, and lavender tunics, as well as the previous tunics resembling Link from {{TLoZ|-}} and the Dark Interlopers. He can also wear a tunic that resembles pre-release artwork of Link from {{TWW}}, with brown sleeves instead of green.

In {{SSBU}}, half of Link's outfits are based on the [[Champion's Tunic]] and the other are based on the [[Tunic of the Wild]].

==={{SCII|-}}===
In {{SCII}}, Link's choice of tunics is similar to that of the {{SSB|-}} series; however, he only starts off with the Green and Red Tunic. To obtain the Blue Tunic, the player must buy it in the Castor Shop for 21,000 Gold, found in the Extra Chapter 1 of the Weapon Master mode. Only after buying the Blue Tunic can the player buy the [[Blue Ring]] Tunic from the original {{TLoZ}}, which is sold for the same price as the Blue Tunic.

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Main|Costume}}
In {{HW}}, playable Warriors can wear different skins called [[Costume]]s. These are usually recolors of their existing outfit, although some Costumes feature entirely new outfits. Costumes can be unlocked through [[Adventure Mode]]. Some Costumes are only available by purchasing DLC packs.

==Trivia==
*In {{TLoZ}}, due to the hardware limitations of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]], certain characters, items, and even enemies share the same color palette as [[Link]]'s [[Green Clothes]]. These include the hilt of the [[Sword]], the bowstring of the [[Bow]], [[Princess Zelda]]'s dress, the [[Merchant]], the whiskers of a [[Pols Voice]], and even his own eyes. When he wears the [[Blue Ring|Blue]] and [[Red Ring]], they will also change correspondingly.
**Likewise, in {{OoT}}, the [[Gerudo Warrior]]'s outfit also shares the same palette as Link's, and in the original NTSC release, due to a bug, if he pulls out a [[Bomb]] and [[Bombchu]], she will flash until they explode, as long as she herself is on-screen. This is fixed in later versions.

* In the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] and ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', although Link can wear certain clothes that would indeed grant special abilities in the mainline ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' games, they serve no such purpose, as they are used only as alternate costumes. In the former, he is able to wear tunics in colors never seen in the core series such as pink, black, or white.[[File:OoT3D Dark Link Model.png|thumb|230x230px|Dark Link as he appears in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'']]
* [[Link's Shadow]], [[Dark Link]], or [[Shadow Link]], Link's evil doppelgängers, are known to wear dark gray or shadowy black clothes, though in {{TAoL}}, the former is completely black.
* The [[Like Like]] enemy, exclusively in {{OoT|-}}, has the unique ability to steal Link's clothes, specifically the [[Goron Tunic|Goron]] and [[Zora Armor|Zora Tunic]], before potentially digesting them in addition to his [[Shield|Shields]] (except [[Mirror Shield]]).
* The character [[Gramps]] in {{aLBW}} notably wears the same clothes as Link himself. Although appearing to be equipped with the Green Clothes, he is actually wearing the [[Red Mail]], but it's not visible in gameplay. This has led some fans to believe he is an older incarnation of the main protagonist, but this is not confirmed by [[Nintendo]].
