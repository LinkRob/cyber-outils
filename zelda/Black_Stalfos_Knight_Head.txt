==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Black Stalfos Knight Head}} are a component of the {{Term|CoH|Black Stalfos Knight Head|link}} boss. They are the strongest variant of the {{Term|CoH|Stalfos Knight Head}}, and are capable in moving in every direction on every single beat until they either lose health and return to their body, or perish once their body is destroyed.
