{{Term/Store|Scoot Fruit|Scoot Fruits|Series, ALBW|plural}} are items in {{ALBW}}.<ref name="Selling"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|ALBW|Scoot Fruit}} can be bought either from the {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}} {{Term|ALBW|Item Shop|link}} or the {{Term|ALBW|Lakeside Item Shop|link}} for 50 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}, and is found on the item screen after purchase. When {{Term|ALBW|Link}} uses a {{Term|ALBW|Scoot Fruit}} inside a {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon|link}}, he is immediately transported to its entrance.<ref name="Description"/> They only work in {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon}}, and consequently may not function in certain circumstances. Unlike conventional items, {{Plural|ALBW|Scoot Fruit}} disappear after one use. Since {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can only hold one of these {{Plural|ALBW|Scoot Fruit|display=Fruits}} at a time, they must be purchased again after each time they are used.

Notably, the {{Term|ALBW|Runaway Item Seller|link}} asks {{Term|ALBW|Link}} for {{Term|ALBW|Scoot Fruit}}. If {{Term|ALBW|Link}} obliges, he is rewarded with a {{Term|ALBW|Silver Rupee}}, which amounts to a 50 {{Term|ALBW|Rupee}} profit after considering the cost of the {{Term|ALBW|Scoot Fruit}} that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} trades away.
