The '''Jabul Ruins''' is a dungeon in {{EoW}}.

==Entrance to the Ruins==
The Jabul Ruins can be accessed as either second or third dungeon following the Suthorn. To reach it, the player must complete "The Jabul Waters Rift" quest line and cross through the Stilled Jabul Waters, at which point they will arrive at the dungeon's entrance.

==Themes and Navigation==
The Jabul Ruins is a nonlinear dungeon structured similarly to the [[Divine Beast|Divine Beasts]] from {{BotW}}. The player must activate five switches in order to raise the water pressure of a pipe in the central room, which will carry Zelda to the top floor, where the dungeon boss, [[Vocavor]], resides.

Each switch is contained in a room with a unique puzzle (aside from the red switch, which is in plain sight near the entrance) and can be pressed in any order the player chooses. Alternatively, the player can choose to press only some or even none of the switches by utilizing the [[Water Block]] or [[Crawltula]] echoes to scale the walls of the ruins and reach the Big Key.

==Trivia==
*The name of the dungeon appears to be an homage to [[Jabun]] from {{TWW}}.
