==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Mask}} are pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}. They cannot be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}, nor can they be {{Term|BotW|Dye|link|display=Dyed}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}. When worn, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can blend in with {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}};<ref name="Inventory"/> however, {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Mask}} will not prevent other enemies in the same area from attacking.

== {{TotK|-}} ==
{{Improve}}
The Bokoblin mask is a piece of armor that allows Link to blend in with other Bokoblins. When spotted, the Bokoblin will become alarmed as usual, but when one comes over to investigate, it won’t attack Link; it’ll stand next to him as though they are friends. It can be obtained by giving one [[Bubbul Gem]] to [[Koltin]] before he goes on his search for more Bubbul Gems.
