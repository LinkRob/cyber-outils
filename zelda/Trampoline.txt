{{Term/Store|Trampoline|Trampolines|Series, OoS|plural}} are {{Plural|OoS|Object|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |publisher= BradyGames |page= 80{{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Stub|OoS}}
{{Plural|OoS|Trampoline}} can be found in the {{Term|OoS|Poison Moth's Lair|link}}, as well as the {{Term|OoS|Ancient Ruins|link}} and the {{Term|OoS|Explorer's Crypt|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} can push them around, or jump on them with the {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather|link}} or {{Term|OoS|Roc's Cape|link}} below a {{Term|OoS|Pit|link}} to bounce to the next floor. Should {{Term|OoS|Link}} attempt to jump on one that is not beneath a {{Term|OoS|Pit}}, he will hit his head and fall back down.

In the {{Term|OoS|Poison Moth's Lair}}, if {{Term|OoS|Link}} is knocked down into a {{Term|OoS|Pit}} while doing battle with {{Term|OoS|Mothula|link}}, his only means of returning to the fight will be via a {{Term|OoS|Trampoline}} below the {{Term|OoS|Boss Room|link}}.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Trampoline|This children's toy of springy cloth on a wooden frame lets you jump higher than usual if you hop onto it.<ref name="EoW Trampoline">{{Cite|This children's toy of springy cloth on a wooden frame lets you jump higher than usual if you hop onto it.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Trampolines are bouncy objects found across [[Hyrule]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]] after encountering one in [[Suthorn Village]]. They cost one Echo energy to summon and can be used to reach higher areas.
