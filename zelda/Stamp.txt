==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Stamp}}
{{Term|TMC|Stamp}} is a resident of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. Described as high-strung and excitable,<ref>{{Cite|She's mellow and laidback, entirely unlike the high-strung and easily excitable Stamp.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> he resides behind the counter of the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}}, stamping letters, bemoaning the endless nature of his work,<ref>{{Cite|STAMP! STAMP! Right to left... STAMP! STAMP! It never ends...|Stamp|TMC}}</ref> and expressing his frustration at the futility of bringing in more employees.<ref>{{Cite|STAMP! STAMP! Even if we hire more people... STAMP! STAMP! It just doesn't matter...|Stamp|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Postman|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Stamp}} is inspired to help people by recruiting {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}} to write the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}}, and enlists {{Term|TMC|Marcy|link}}'s help in distributing it.<ref>{{Cite|I really wish I could do more to help people... Wait! I've got it! I'll ask {{Color|TMC Green|Swiftblade}} to start up a newsletter for adventurers! That'll be a great way to provide a useful service for everyone... Hm. It's going to be a lot of work, though. I should get {{Color|TMC Green|Marcy}} to help me.|Stamp|TMC}}</ref>

The stamp that {{Term|TMC|Stamp}} uses is his own personal one.<ref name="Figurine"/>
