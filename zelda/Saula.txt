{{Term/Store|Saula||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Saula|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Saula}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} who manages the {{Term|BotW|Armor Shop|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion|link}}, in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her, she compliments him for his {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Top|link}} and encourages him to try out more colors.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq! Oooh, that {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo top}} looks really good on you...|Saula|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But you haven't really lived until you've got {{Color|BotW Blue|several colors}} to choose from! What do you say?|Saula|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the different colors, {{Term|BotW|Saula}} expresses surprise that he does not know and explains that dyeing clothing is popular for "young vai" right now.<ref>{{Cite|You don't know? Really?! Changing the color of your clothes is a really trendy thing to do right now for young vai traveling beyond town!|Saula|BotW}}</ref> She goes on to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} about the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} where he can dye his {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a store in Hateno Village—the {{Color|BotW Blue|Kochi Dye Shop}}—where you can dye your clothes!|Saula|BotW}}</ref> However, she states that not many get to travel there due to its large distance from {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hateno Village is pretty far away, though. Not everyone gets to go there...|Saula|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Saula}} also states that it is hot and expresses her desire to get a cold drink at {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's really hot today, isn't it? Makes me want to get an ice-cold something from the canteen.|Saula|BotW}}</ref> Like most other store owners, {{Term|BotW|Saula}} will also buy {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}}. If he decides to say goodbye, she comments that the evening is nice and cool, and that at times like these she likes to grab drinks at the canteen.<ref>{{Cite|It's so nice and cool this evening, isn't it> On nights like this, I like to relax and grab a drink at the canteen.|Saula|BotW}}</ref>

Through the doorway behind {{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion}} is a curtain that conceals an entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club|link}} If {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to enter, {{Term|BotW|Saula}} stops him, telling him that the doorway is not for customers.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, um, that doorway isn't for customers, actually...|Saula|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Saula}} then whispers to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and tells him to use the door on the side of the building if he is a member.<ref>{{Cite|<small>If you're a member, please head around to the door on the side.</small>|Saula|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Saula}} knows about the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club}}, and is likely a member, as suggested by {{Term|BotW|Yaido|link}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} eavesdrops on the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} in {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}}. {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} reveals that she overheard Saula say that the password to enter the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club}} is "GSC♦."<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that's right, the password! It's {{Color|BotW Red|GSC♦}}. I overheard {{Term|BotW|Saula}} talking about it, so I'm sure that's right!|Yaido|BotW}}</ref>
