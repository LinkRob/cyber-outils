The {{Term/Store|Giant Brothers|Giant Brothers|Series|plural}} are a trio of [[Hinox]] brothers in {{BotW}}.{{Name Ref|BotW}} They consist of a red hinox (the youngest kin), a blue hinox (the middle kin) and a black hinox (the oldest kin).

==Characteristics==
The Giant Brothers can be found on and around [[Mount Taran]] in [[East Necluda]], northwest of [[Lurelin Village]]. Each has an [[Ancient Orb]] on its necklace, and all are involved in the [[The Three Giant Brothers|Three Giant Brothers]] [[Shrine Quest]], which unlocks the [[Ancient Shrine|Tawa Jinn Shrine]]. They are likely a reference to the [[Hinox Brothers]] from {{TFH}}, as both have three members: a youngest, middle, and eldest. Fighting them is optional, as the [[Stealth Set]], [[Phantom Ganon Set]], food and elixirs allow Link to steal their Ancient Orbs without waking them up. In [[Master Mode]], they remain the same color, like the [[Lynel]] of [[Ploymus Mountain]]. The age and therefore rank of each Giant Brother determines how naturally defended it is: the Youngest Kin has no defenses, the Middle Kin has [[Courser Bee]]s which chase Link and the Oldest Kin is protected by [[Blue Bokoblin]]s ([[Black Bokoblin|Black]] in Master Mode).
