{{Term/Store|Steamed Fish|Steamed Fish|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Steamed Fish}}<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Steamed Fish}}<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Steamed Fish by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} with any vegetable, herb, or flower in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. There are two methods to make the default version of this meal. The first way is to cook either {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}}, as none of these ingredients provide any status effects. Alternatively, Link can use ingredients with contradicting effects, which will cancel out. Link can heal some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} by eating these fish. They're considered a refined dish.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on both the type of Fish used and the kind of herb, vegetable, or flower used, Link can make several different variations of Steamed Fish:

* '''Hearty Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Steamed Fish}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll heal all of [[Link|Link's]] Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Steamed Fish}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}} or other energizing ingredients. These meals will refill some of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} when he eats them.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Steamed Fish}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} or other enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely replenished and overfilled when he consumes them.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Steamed Fish}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}, or other hasty ingredients. By eating them, Link will receive a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Steamed Fish}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}}, or other spicy ingredients. Consuming these meals will grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Steamed Fish}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}}, or other electric ingredients. They can be eaten to give Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Steamed Fish}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, or other mighty ingredients. If Link eats them, he will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Steamed Fish}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, or other tough ingredients. Link's defense will be increased by eating these meals.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Steamed Fish}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. When they're eaten, Link will receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Steamed Fish''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Steamed Fish}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A refined dish made by wrapping a fresh fish in fragrant wild greens and cooking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}}, or other chilly ingredients. Eating these fish will grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>
