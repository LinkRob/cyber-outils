{{Term/Store|Command||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is [[Link]]'s recurring ability to take control of another [[Playable Secondary Characters|character]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 97 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> A mixture between direct and indirect control of secondary characters can be exercised when Link is transporting with the help of another character. [[Epona]] takes Link from one place to another by running forward, but it is Link who steers her in the proper direction, gives her acceleration, and stops her. He can attack with the [[Bow]] or the Sword from this position. Various other characters in the series, including [[Ricky]], [[Moosh]], [[Dimitri]], and [[Kargarok]]s can be controlled in a similar way.

==Appearances==
[[File:Kafeisakon.png|thumb|200px|right|Controlling Kafei in {{MM|-}}]]
==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:Controlling Phantom.png|180px|thumb|right|Controlling a Phantom in {{ST|-}}]]
In {{MM}}, [[Kafei]] can be temporarily controlled while navigating the puzzles of [[Sakon's Hideout]]. This command is indirect, since he and Link independently travel across the complex in order to retrieve the [[Couple's Mask#Sun's Mask|Sun's Mask]] during the sidequest.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Link can command different types of characters. These include a [[Seagull]] with the use of a [[Hyoi Pear]], which gives access to items out of reach such as [[Rupee]]s and switches. By playing the [[Command Melody]], Link can control either [[Medli]] or [[Makar]], as well as the statues in the [[Tower of the Gods]]. The [[Tingle Tuner]] enables the command of [[Tingle]], wich can drop [[Bomb]]s, sell [[Potion]]s, or make Link float in mid air.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, Link can control [[Cucco]]s after repeatedly attacking them. The [[Dominion Rod]] allows Link to command [[Armos|Armos Statues]] and other statues of that nature. He can swing the Rod to make them do an action, like swinging a hammer. The battle against [[Armogohma]] requires the use of the Dominion Rod.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, Link can take control of [[Gongoron]] during the [[Goron Temple]], also been used to defeat the temple's boss [[Dongorongo]]. He is also controlled on [[Dee Ess Island]].

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Link can control [[Princess Zelda]] while in her [[Phantom]] form. After gaining three [[Tears of Light]], Link gains the ability to harm Phantoms. Upon doing so, Zelda lunges in and takes control of the Phantom, with different Phantoms having different abilities.
