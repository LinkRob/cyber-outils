"{{Term/Store|Riddles of Hyrule||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Riddles of Hyrule}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Great Hyrule Forest}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
On top of {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can wake up the {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} {{Term|BotW|Walton|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|...Hmmmm? *yawn* And I was having such a nice dream...|Walton|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} is there, he realizes that he must want to face the "ultimate trial."<ref>{{Cite|Wait, if you're here... Does this mean you want to face the {{Color|BotW Red|ultimate trial}}?|Walton|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to try it, {{Term|BotW|Walton}} will announce that the trial has begun.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll try it!] *ahem* The {{Color|BotW Red|ultimate trial}}...has begun!|Walton|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he will share a riddle about an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}}, and then {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to bring them said {{Term|BotW|Item}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll tell you a riddle about an object. Your job is to solve the riddle and bring me the correct object.|Walton|BotW}}</ref> 

For the first riddle, {{Term|BotW|Walton}} asks for something small, red, round, and sweet.<ref>{{Cite|Here comes your first riddle! {{Color|BotW Red|Small}}, {{Color|BotW Red|red}}, {{Color|BotW Red|round}}, {{Color|BotW Red|and sweet}}! Bring me one of this simple treat!|Walton|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Walton}} bets that his riddle has stumped {{Term|BotW|Link}}, and he promises to dance for him if he can place the {{Term|BotW|Item}} on the leaf.<ref>{{Cite|Have you figured it out? I'm guessing you're more stumped than the base of a felled tree! If you place the right object on the leaf, I'll dance for you.|Walton|BotW}}</ref> The answer to this riddle is an {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Apple}}! It's an {{Color|BotW Red|apple}}! That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref>

Next, {{Term|BotW|Walton}} tasks {{Term|BotW|Link}} with finding the specialty fruit of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, which has rock-hard flesh.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* The next riddle is... Kakariko Village's specialty fruit! Its {{Color|BotW Red|rock-hard}} flesh is quite resolute!|Walton|BotW}}</ref> The solution this time is a {{Term|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, which are sold at {{Term|BotW|Olkin's Pumpkins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Fortified pumpkin!}} That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref>

The third riddle asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find a {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}} that can warm someone's bones, which can be located in hotter regions.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* The next riddle is... {{Color|BotW Red|This sultry shroom will warm your bones}}. It makes its home in warmer zones.|Walton|Botw}}</ref> To solve this riddle, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must bring {{Term|BotW|Walton}} a {{Term|BotW|Sunshroom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Sunshroom!}} That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref> They are plentiful in {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Walton}} wants {{Term|BotW|Link}} a "swimmer" that is capable of zapping people.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* The next riddle is... {{Color|BotW Red|Its scales and tail will zap you, no doubt}}! This shocking swimmer is called a...fish.|Walton|BotW}}</ref> The answer to this riddle is a {{Term|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}}, which are common in the waters of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Voltfin trout!}} That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref> 

Finally, {{Term|BotW|Walton}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find an {{Term|BotW|Item}} that starts with "h" and ends with "oof", and it is dangerous to have this step on you.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* The next riddle is... This is the last riddle! Prepare yourself! If stepped on by this, you will go poof! {{Color|BotW Red|It starts with an H and ends with an oof}}!|Walton|BotW}}</ref> This final answer is a {{Term|BotW|Lynel Hoof|link}}, which is dropped by all types of {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} when they are defeated.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Lynel hoof!}} That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref> 

After solving all five riddles, {{Term|BotW|Walton}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|I knew you could do it, Mr. Hero! Here is your reward!|Walton|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Diamond}} {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} is full, {{Term|BotW|Walton}} will hold onto his {{Term|BotW|Diamond}} until he is able to take it.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? You are overflowing with diamonds! I was gonna give you some diamonds for solving my riddles, but... Z z z z z...|Walton|BotW}}</ref>}} {{Term|BotW|Walton}} states that he is out of riddles, which completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm fresh out of riddles. You've outwhittled me... Very impressive!|Walton|BotW}}</ref>
