==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Restless Cricket}}
{{Plural|BotW|Restless Cricket}} can be found just about anywhere by cutting {{Term|BotW|Grass|link}} with any {{Term|BotW|Sword|link}} or under rocks. When found, they will hop away two to three times before flying off and disappearing unless {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is [[Stealth|sneaking]]. They're sold by the traveling merchant [[Gartan]] and by {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} at [[Dueling Peaks Stable]] and [[Outskirt Stable]]. Using a Restless Cricket in a [[Cooking Pot]] with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} will result in an [[Energizing Elixir]], which will restore Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} when used.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] as an ingredient to make Green [[Dye]].

The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|A Gift for My Beloved|link}}", involves Link asking [[Prima]] what she likes on behalf of [[Manny (Breath of the Wild)|Manny]]. She will say crickets as an excuse, claiming that it's her dream to own one hundred of them.<ref>{{Cite| [What do you like?] {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Why is he asking?
