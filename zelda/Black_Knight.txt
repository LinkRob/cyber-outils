{{Term/Store|Black Knight|Black Knights|Series, TMC|plural}} are {{Plural|TMC|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 167}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Black Knight}}
{{Plural|TMC|Black Knight}} are the strongest variant of {{Plural|TMC|Darknut|link}} in both strength and endurance. They recover from attacks and blocks much more quickly than other {{Plural|TMC|Darknut}}, though they boast the same attacks and patterns as other {{Plural|TMC|Darknut}} with the addition of a charged thrust attack.

{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} first encounters a {{Term|TMC|Black Knight}} in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. When defeated, it summons four {{Plural|TMC|Darknut}} and two {{Plural|TMC|Red Dark Nut|link}} across four rooms in the {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}. A second {{Term|TMC|Black Knight}} appears alongside two {{Plural|TMC|Red Dark Nut}} during {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}'s ceremony to drain the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} does not defeat them within three minutes, the third bell will chime, resulting in an instant [[Game Over]].
