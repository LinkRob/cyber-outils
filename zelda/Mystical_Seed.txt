{{Term/Store|Mystical Seed|Mystical Seeds|Series, OoS, OoA, FS|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= |game= OoS|page= 31}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Come back here anytime you wish to see {{Color|OoA Blue|Tingle}}! ==Seed Types==
===Ember Seeds===
{{Main|Ember Seed}}
[[File:OoS Ember Seed Sprite.png|16px|left]]
{{Plural|Series|Ember Seed|link}} are {{Plural|Series|Mystical Seed}} that ignite when cracked open.

===Scent Seeds===
{{Main|Scent Seed}}
[[File:OoS Scent Seed Sprite.png|16px|left]]
{{Plural|Series|Scent Seed|link}} are {{Plural|Series|Mystical Seed}} that emit a weird smell that attracts many enemies when cracked open.

===Pegasus Seeds===
{{Main|Pegasus Seed}}
[[File:OoS Pegasus Seed Sprite.png|16px|left]]
{{Plural|OoS|Pegasus Seed|link}} are {{Plural|OoS|Mystical Seed}} that cause {{Term|OoS|Link}} to move faster.

===Gale Seeds===
{{Main|Gale Seed}}
[[File:OoS Gale Seed Sprite.png|16px|left]]
{{Plural|Series|Gale Seed|link}} are {{Plural|Series|Mystical Seed}} that produce miniature tornadoes when cracked open. These tornadoes allow {{Term|OoA|Link}} to {{Term|OoA|Warping|link|display= Warp}} to any {{Term|OoA|Mystical Tree|link}} he has found.

===Mystery Seeds===
{{Main|Mystery Seed}}
[[File:OoS Mystery Seed Sprite.png|Mystery Seed|16px|left]]
{{Plural|Series|Mystery Seed|link}} are {{Plural|Series|Mystical Seed}} that have a randomized effect among all of the other {{Plural|Series|Mystical Seed}}'. They are also capable of bringing {{Plural|Series|Owl Statue|link}} to life.

===Razor Seeds===
{{Main|Razor Seed}}
[[File:FS Razor Seed Sprite.png|left|15px]]
'''Razor Seeds''' are small red seeds that appear in {{FS|-}}. Picking up a Razor Seed will double Link's attack power until the end of the current stage. It is one of the three upgrades available, the other two being the [[Armor Seed]] and the [[Pegasus Seed]]. Like the rest of the Mystical Seeds in the game, the Razor Seeds can be found by cutting down grass.<ref name="Four Swords">{{Cite|The mystical seeds you collect will boost your power by up to two levels. {{Color|FS Orange|Razor Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Razor Seed}} add sword power. {{Color|FS Orange|Armor Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Armor Seed}} add defense. {{Color|FS Orange|Pegasus Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Pegasus Seed}} add speed.|Stray Fairy|FS}}</ref>

===Armor Seeds===
{{Main|Armor Seed}}
[[File:FS Armor Seed Sprite.png|left|15px]]
'''Armor Seeds''' are mystical seeds featured in {{FS|-}}. They are small blue seeds which have a crystalline appearance. Picking up an Armor Seed will double Link's defensive power until the end of the current stage.<ref name="Four Swords"/> They can be found by cutting grass.
