{{Term/Store|Golden Rope|Golden Ropes|Series, TMC|plural}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 201}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Up to three {{Plural|TMC|Golden Rope}} will appear, as well as the other {{Plural|TMC|Golden Monster|link}}, after certain {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} in {{TMC}}. Like the other {{Plural|TMC|Golden Monster}}, {{Plural|TMC|Golden Rope}} move much faster, take much more damage to defeat, and deal much more damage to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} than standard {{Plural|TMC|Rope|link}}. They can be found in {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden|link}}. When defeated, they drop a large sum of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.
