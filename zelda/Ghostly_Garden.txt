The {{Term/Store|Ghostly Garden||Series, ALttP}} is a small area of the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} that appears in {{ALttP}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP}} It is the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World}} counterpart of the {{Term|ALttP|Graveyard}}.

==Appearances and Features==
The {{Term|ALttP|Ghostly Garden}}, despite its name, contains very little in the way of plant life. Its layout is very similar to the {{Term|ALttP|Graveyard}} of the {{Term|ALttP|Light World|link}}, but instead of graves it is a scattered array of unusual statues (much like ones found elsewhere in the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World}}, such as in the {{Term|ALttP|Plains of Ruin|link}}) and small collections of angular rocks that can be destroyed using the {{Term|ALttP|Pegasus Boots|link}}. The rock garden is populated by the plant-like {{Term|ALttP|Ropa|link}}, and guarded by a single {{Term|ALttP|Hinox|link}}. The {{Term|ALttP|Ghostly Garden}} itself does not contain any significant items that make it worth exploring, but by using the {{Term|ALttP|Magic Mirror|link}}, {{Term|ALttP|Link}} can access a cave in the {{Term|ALttP|Light World}}, and by navigating the area correctly he can even access the tomb containing the {{Term|ALttP|Magic Cape|link}} long prior to obtaining the {{Term|ALttP|Titan's Mitt|link}} that is normally required for access.

While the {{Term|ALttP|Ghostly Garden}} itself contains far fewer secrets than its {{Term|ALttP|Light World}} counterpart, by {{Term|ALttP|Dash|link}}ing into the rocks that correspond to the graves that lead to the {{Term|ALttP|Sewer Passageway|link}} and the {{Term|ALttP|Magic Cape}} in the {{Term|ALttP|Light World}} they will reveal a {{Term|ALttP|Bomb|link}} and a red {{Term|ALttP|Rupee|link}} respectively, while the northern centermost rock structure will reveal a {{Term|ALttP|Fairy|link}}.

==Trivia==
*Despite being the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World}} counterpart to the {{Term|ALttP|Graveyard}}, no {{Plural|ALttP|Poe|link}} or {{Plural|ALttP|Hyu|link}} appear in this area.
