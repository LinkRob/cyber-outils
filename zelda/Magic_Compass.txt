The '''Magic Compass''' is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|TLoZ (TV Series), TLoZ (Valiant Comics)}}

==Locations and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|TV Series|-}}===
The {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic Compass}} is only seen in the "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld Connections|link}}" episode of the "{{TLoZ|TV Series}}. It is used to locate the remaining shards of the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom}}.<ref name="Compass"/>

When {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Vire|link}} manage to split the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom}} into three {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce Shard|display=Shards}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} is able to grab one {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce Shard}} while the remaining two go at the hands of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Vire}}. However, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} shoots a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam|link}} at the shard that they possess, which defeats the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Vire}} that were carrying the other two pieces into the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= I got it! Since all of the pieces of the Triforce are magically linked, if you shoot a zap into this one, it'll go through to the others and zap the Vires! |name= Princess Zelda |show= The Legend of Zelda |episode= 8}}</ref> Using the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic Compass}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} venture into the underground to locate the two {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce Shard}} that were dropped by the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Vire}} and thus reunite the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom}}.

==={{TLoZ|Valiant Comics|-}}===
The {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Magic Compass}} is used to locate the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Triforce of Wisdom}} in {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= But this '''Magic Compass''' will lead me right to the Triforce, and Zelda! |book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics) |publisher= |page= 3 [Missing in Action]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Come on! My '''Magic Compass''' can still track [the Triforce of Wisdom]! |book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics) |publisher= |page= 5 [Queen of Hearts]}}</ref> In "[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 1#Missing in Action|Missing in Action]]", {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}} uses the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Magic Compass}} to track {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Princess Zelda}}, who has taken the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Triforce of Wisdom}} to leave {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Hyrule}} forever and thus impede {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}} from ever obtaining it. {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}} uses the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Magic Compass}} once again in ''[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 4#Queen of Hearts|Queen of Hearts]]'' when the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Triforce of Wisdom}} is stolen, which leads them to find out that {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Queen Seline|link}} took it.
