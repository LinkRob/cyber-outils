{{Term/Store|Kafei||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|SSBB, SSBU}} He is the runaway son of [[Mayor Dotour]] and [[Madame Aroma]] and a major character in {{MM}}<nowiki/>'s longest side-quest, [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]]. He is one of the twenty people added to the [[Bombers' Notebook]]. Kafei himself notably has a slight resemblance to [[Link]].

==Backstory==
During his youth, Kafei was a member of the [[Bombers Secret Society of Justice]].<ref>{{Cite|Kafei? I can recall when he was in the Bombers. Don't see his face much anymore, though...Well, what could he be doing?|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref> The [[Man from Curiosity Shop|owner of the Curiosity Shop]] states that he was a childhood friend of Kafei's and gave him the [[Keaton Mask]] as a gift (in which he uses to hide his face). Later, Kafei eventually fell in love with [[Anju]] when they were children and they became engaged. They promised each other to marry on the day of the [[Carnival of Time]], exchanging the [[Sun's Mask|Sun]] and [[Moon's Mask]] during the event.<ref>{{Cite|We promised each other when we were children that we'd marry on the day of the Carnival of Time.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> However, several years later, and only a month before the Carnival,<ref>{{Cite|The person I want you to locate is my son, Kafei. You know him, don't you? ...You don't? Really? He disappeared about a month ago. It's terrible! I'm so very worried...|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> Kafei was suddenly turned into a child at the same age as [[Link]] by the [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]]-possessed [[Skull Kid]].<ref>{{Cite|I was turned into this by a strange imp wearing a mask... But I'm not hiding because I look this way.|Kafei|MM}}</ref>

On his way to see the [[Great Fairy]] in [[North Clock Town]] about his desperate situation, [[Sakon]] came along and stole his wedding ceremony mask.<ref>{{Cite|When I was turned into this, I went to see the Great Fairy in the shrine near the North Gate... But on the way, my precious mask--a wedding ceremony mask--was stolen from me by some prancing man with a grinning face!|Kafei|MM}}</ref> As such, Kafei could not return to Anju despite knowing that she was worried, for he had promised her to greet her with his wedding mask in hand.<ref>{{Cite|I know Anju is worried...but I can't go out yet. I made a promise to her that I would bring the wedding mask and greet her.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> He goes into hiding at the [[Curiosity Shop]] and disguises himself with the Keaton Mask that the owner gave him when he was a child,<ref>{{Cite|All it took was one glance at that Keaton Mask he was carrying for me to realize that I was looking at my old friend. I gave him that mask a long time ago when he was just li'l Kafei. Didn't know he kept it that well for so long...|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref> secretly watching all of the customers who sell stolen goods. He is positive that the thief will appear and he will be able to follow him to retrieve his mask.<ref>{{Cite|Things that get stolen in this town always make their way to the Curiosity Shop. I'm waiting for that to happen.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> Despite Kafei's terrible fate, Anju does not know what has become of him or why he disappeared. She is afraid to meet her fiancé in person and hear the reason as to why he disappeared, for she fears his disappearance might have been caused by her.<ref>{{Cite|I'm...actually...afraid to meet him...and to hear the reason why he wanted to disappear... It might be because of... me...|Anju|MM}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Main|Reuniting Kafei and Anju}}
Kafei is the son of [[Mayor Dotour]] and [[Madame Aroma]],<ref>{{Cite|It seems his mother, Madame Aroma, had Kafei's Mask...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> and is engaged to Anju, the inn-keeper of the [[Stock Pot Inn]]. Although the two lovers made a promise to exchange their masks and get married on the day of the Carnival of Time,<ref>{{Cite|The masks of the sun and the moon... We were to exchange them on the day of the Carnival of Time...|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We promised each other when we were children that we'd marry on the day of the Carnival of Time.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> a sudden turn of events forces Kafei to run from Anju's side, leaving the inn-keeper without a reason for his departure.

Link, the [[Hero of Time]], can meet Kafei by sneaking into the back of the Curiosity Shop while Kafei is getting a [[Letter to Kafei|letter]] from Anju during the [[Second Day]]. Kafei explains to the young hero the reasons for his disappearance and why he cannot go and see his fiancée. He then asks Link to deliver his [[Pendant of Memories|pendant]] to Anju as proof that he still intends to keep his promise.<ref>{{Cite|This pendant...Give it to Anju.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> Once Anju receives the pendant, she will once again believe in Kafei and decide to wait for his return despite everyone else in the [[Stock Pot Inn]] taking refuge at [[Romani Ranch]].<ref>{{Cite|I have decided to wait for him. I've made my promise... I'm fine with this. I believe him.|Anju|MM}}</ref>
[[File:Kafeisakon.png|thumb|281x281px|Gameplay screenshot of controlling Kafei inside Sakon's Hideout in {{MM|-}}]]
On the night of the [[Final Day]], Link and Kafei team up to enter [[Sakon's Hideout]] to retrieve the Sun's Mask. After making their way through the hideout, Kafei recovers the wedding mask and immediately leaves for the Stock Pot Inn to meet up with Anju. With just a few hours left until the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] falls, Anju waits for Kafei in her room with her Moon's Mask. The two lovers, at last, reunite and exchange their masks and oaths, with [[Tatl]] teasing that they appear to be a mother and a child due to Kafei's young age.<ref>{{Cite|Let us exchange the promised masks. We have exchanged our oaths and have become a couple.|Anju|MM}}</ref> In return for helping them reunite, Anju and Kafei grant Link the [[Couple's Mask]], which is their respective masks merged into one.<ref>{{Cite|You are our witnesses. Please accept this mask.|Anju|MM}}</ref> Even though the Moon is dangerously close from [[Terminan Apocalypse|destroying]] [[Termina]] by this point, they calmly urge Link to take refuge, for they will stay and greet the morning together.<ref>{{Cite|Please take refuge. We are fine here. We shall greet the morning... together.|Anju|MM}}</ref>

During the [[A New Day|ending credits]], Anju can be seen outside [[Clock Town]] having her [[Anju and Kafei's Wedding|wedding ceremony]], although Kafei himself is never seen in the scene. The camera facing Anju is strongly implied to be Kafei's point of view, back to his adult age.

Gameplay-wise, Kafei is virtually identical to Link, as he himself shares the same overall behavior and animations as the young hero, and when controlled by the player during their brief teamwork in Sakon's Hideout - a first for the franchise, he can even punch by pressing the [[Sword]] button (despite he doesn't face any enemies unlike Link, though they will target him).

==Other Appearances==
==={{MM|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:Kafei transformation.png|thumb|right|150px|Kafei being transformed by the Skull Kid in the manga]]
In the {{MM|Himekawa}}, Kafei is first mentioned by [[Anju's Mother]], and later Anju explains his disappearance after nursing [[Deku Link]]'s injuries. He later makes an appearance as he tries to get into the Stock Pot Inn. Link, thinking Kafei was up to no good, chases Kafei away which causes him to drop a letter addressed to Anju. Link delivers the letter to her, and she decides to wait for Kafei. Link later tracks down Kafei, and interrogates him, thinking he is a minion of the Skull Kid. Kafei explains what happened to him, and asks Link to take him to the Skull Kid. Kafei pleads with the Skull Kid to change him back to an adult, who then fires a blast of energy at Kafei, gravely injuring him. Anju appears and the two embrace as Link enters the final battle with Majora's Mask. After the battle, Kafei changes back into an adult, and he and Anju enjoy their wedding day.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
[[File:MM Kafei looking around.gif|thumb|220x220px|Kafei's strong resemblance to Link in his behavior has led many to believe he is a [[Terminan]] counterpart of the hero]]
*Contrary to popular belief, despite his clear similarity to the [[Hero of Time]], Kafei is '''not''' a direct counterpart of [[Link]], as he is not physically identical to the main protagonist and it's unconfirmed by [[Nintendo]]. Therefore, he is one of the few residents of [[Termina]] who does not have a parallel individual from [[Hyrule]] in {{OoT}}.
**Ironically, Link's actual counterpart in {{aLBW}} from [[Lorule]], [[Ravio]], shares a connection that both he and Kafei are associated with violet rather than green. However, their personalities are the complete opposite, as Ravio is fearful while Kafei and Link himself are courageous.
*One briefly plays as Kafei in {{Term|MM|Sakon's Hideout}}. This is the first occurrence of playing as someone other than Link in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]], and the only such occurrence initiated involuntarily rather than by {{Term|MM3D|Command (Ability)|link}}. This concept is expanded in {{TWW}} for [[Medli]], [[Makar]], and the three statues in [[Tower of the Gods]].[[File:MM Kafei Concept Art.png|thumb|255x255px|Concept art of Kafei, with an unused adult version seen at the far right]]
*Although Kafei is supposedly a young man turned into a child, he does not have a grown-up model in the game's files. However, early concept artwork of an adult Kafei exists. It is unknown why such a model was never created for the game.
**If debugging tools are used to freely view Kafei and Anju's wedding during the ending credits where he is <nowiki/>supposedly back to an adult, the former, disappointingly, is not visible at all.
*Kafei appears to be a very fast runner. During cutscenes and especially the initial three-day cycle in the game, he can be seen rapidly sprinting at a higher animation speed than Link wearing the [[Bunny Hood]]. He goes even quicker in the Japanese version.
*Kafei shares the same idle animation as [[Zora Link]] when standing still in the Overworld.
*Kafei is arguably the most popular sidequest character of {{MM|-}}, if not the franchise as a whole among fans of the series, due to his handsome appearance and coincidental similarity to Link.
*When Kafei is putting his letter in the Mailbox on the [[First Day]], it's possible to injure him if the [[Dog]] is tricked into attacking him or if he is caught within the blast radius of [[Bomb]]s, [[Bombchu]]s, and the [[Blast Mask]].
