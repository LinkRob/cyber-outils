{{Term/Store|Hot Rodder Goron||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a large, continuously rolling member of the [[Goron]] race in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you can stop my wild rolling, you might get something great. -Hot Rodder Goron|Sign|OoT}}</ref>

==Biography==
Hot Rodder Goron makes his home on the second floor of the [[Goron City]], rolling lap after circular lap across the expanse of the city's circular flooring design. With no described purpose, this Goron will continue to roll until [[Link]] places a well-timed [[Bomb]] in his path at the alcove near the sign. When hit by the Bomb's explosion, Hot Rodder Goron will rise and award Link's "courage" with a bigger [[Bomb Bag]]. If startled by a bomb elsewhere, he will talk to Link for a moment before continuing on his way; he will only reward the Bomb Bag if startled by the alcove on the second floor.

Meeting and stopping Hot Rodder Goron is not significant to the plot line of {{OoT|-}}, as Link can complete the game with the smallest Bomb Bag. Furthermore, regardless if Link stops him or not, Hot Rodder Goron will continue to roll in Goron City, as long as the child stage of Link is apparent. Hot Rodder Goron disappears in the age of Adult Link, replaced by a smaller, yet still rolling Goron named [[Link (Goron)#Ocarina of Time|Link]], interestingly.

==Trivia==
*A similar precise bomb technique is necessary for Adult Link to stop the young Goron named Link, who is rolling around in a similar manner seven years later. Link the Goron, however, will back off and roll in the other direction if Link gets too close.
