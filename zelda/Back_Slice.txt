The {{Term/Store|Back Slice||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an ability in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Very well. My third hidden skill is... The {{Color|TPHD Red|back slice}}! Let it be hewn into your mind.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TPHD|Back Slice}} is the third {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill|link}} that {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can learn. To learn this {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} must first find a {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone|link}} to awaken the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Search for the statues that howl with the {{Color|TPHD Red|sound of the wind}}. Seek the sound that calls the spirit of the beast to awaken me again.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> The {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone}} required to learn this {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} can be found on a ledge in the {{Term|TPHD|Upper Zora's River|link}}. After howling with the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}}, the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} tells {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to find him when he returns to human form.<ref>{{Cite|Let teachings of old pass to you... Take sword in hand and find me...|White Wolf|TPHD}}</ref> The {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} also marks where he will be waiting on {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Map|link}}. After returning to human form, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can find the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} waiting for him on a small platform outside the {{Exp|Eastern on Wii Version|western}} edge of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town|link}}. Approaching the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} will return {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to the clouded area where the {{Term|TPHD|Hero's Spirit|link}} can teach him the {{Term|TPHD|Back Slice}}.

While {{Term|TPHD|Link}} is targeting an {{Term|TPHD|Enemy}}, he can side jump to roll around the foe, which will cause {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to end up directly behind his opponent and thus give him a chance to strike. By attacking at the end of the sidewards roll, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} will perform a jumping slice into the {{Term|TPHD|Enemy}}'s back.<ref>{{Cite|That is when you {{Icon|TPHD ZL}}{{Color|TPHD Red|-target}} and press {{Icon|TPHD A}} while tilting {{Icon|TPHD Left Arrow}}{{Icon|TPHD L Stick}}{{Icon|TPHD Right Arrow}} to jump and roll around your foe, then perform a jumping slice.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> This technique can also be used when nothing is targeted, but {{Term|TPHD|Link}} will just roll sideways instead of in a semi-circular pattern.
