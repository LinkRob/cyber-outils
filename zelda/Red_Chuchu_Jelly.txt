==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Stub|TWW}}

==={{TP|-}}===
Red Chu Jelly can be obtained in the [[Lakebed Temple]], [[Hyrule Field]], [[Snowpeak Ruins]], and [[Cave|Caves]]. After defeating a [[Red Chuchu|Red Chu]], Link can collect Red Chu Jelly in an [[Empty Bottle]]. Drinking it will have the same effect as [[Red Potion|Red Potions]] and restores eight [[Heart|Hearts]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
Red Chuchu Jelly can be obtained by killing [[Fire Chuchu]]s, or by killing [[Chuchu]]s with fire weapons. It can also be made from regular [[Chuchu Jelly]] by setting it on fire.  If struck with a weapon, it explodes and releases a ball of fire that sets nearby enemies on fire. Red Chuchu Jelly can be used to [[Cooking|cook]] [[Elixir|Elixirs]].

Red Chuchu Jelly is also used to upgrade the [[Snowquill Set|Snowquill set]] of [[Armor]] to its first and second tiers at a [[Great Fairy Fountain#Breath%20of%20the%20Wild|Great Fairy Fountain]]. 

* Total for '''Snowquill Set''': 24 (3, 5, 0, 0) x3
