{{Term/Store|Chamber Dungeon|Chamber Dungeons|Series, LANS|plural}} are dungeons in {{LA|NS}}.{{Name Ref|LANS}}

==Overview==
{{Plural|LANS|Chamber Dungeon}} are dungeons that can be created at {{Term|LANS|Dampé's Shack|link}} in {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Heights|link}}. {{Plural|LANS|Chamber Dungeon}} are customized by arranging [[Chamber]] panels. These panels decide the layout of the dungeon as well as the puzzles, enemies, and {{Plural|Series|Sub-Boss|link}} encountered in each chamber. Completed {{Plural|LANS|Chamber Dungeon}} can be saved to [[amiibo]] from {{TLoZ|-}} line, allowing them to be shared with other players to challenge.

There are a number of pre-fabricated {{Plural|LANS|Chamber Dungeon}} challenges that {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} can arrange and complete.
