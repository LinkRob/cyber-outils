{{Term/Store|Zaganaga||Series, ALBW}} is the {{Term|ALBW|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 213}}</ref> It appears as a monstrous, plant/worm-like creature with a spiny, cactaceous body.

==Characteristics==

The battle against {{Term|ALBW|Zaganaga}} takes place in a large, enclosed pit of [[Quicksand]] residing in {{Term|ALBW|Misery Mire|link}}, which is accessed by a {{Term|ALBW|Fissure|link}} placed just outside the final room of the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}. The enclosure features eleven scattered columns which act as platforms {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} can stand atop, using his {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod|link}} to create bridges between them.

At the start of the battle, {{Term|ALBW|Zaganaga}} will burrow into the Quicksand and toward one of the platforms at random, emerging on top of it. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must make his way toward it using the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod}} and strike it with his {{Term|ALBW|Sword|link}} or other weapons. While {{Term|ALBW|Link}} traverses the Quicksand pit, {{Term|ALBW|Zaganaga}} will begin to spew {{Plural|ALBW|Peahat|link}} that will flutter around the arena, attempting to knock {{Term|ALBW|Link}} into the Quicksand, but which can be dispatched with a single hit from the {{Term|ALBW|Sword}}. {{Term|ALBW|Zaganaga}} will burrow back into the Quicksand after several seconds or after being hit several times, then moving toward a different platform.

After taking enough damage, {{Term|ALBW|Zaganaga}} will flare its petals, revealing eyes attached to its inner petals. Its movement speed and its rate of spawning {{Plural|ALBW|Peahat}} will increase, and it gains a new attack of firing a steady blast of sand toward {{Term|ALBW|Link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Zaganaga}} will attempt to keep this blast aimed at {{Term|ALBW|Link}}, but its slow rotation speed allows {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to outrun the attack by using the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod}} wisely. Several more hits with the {{Term|ALBW|Sword}} will defeat the monster. Afterward, a sand-filled hutch at the arena's north edge will open, spilling all of its sand into the pit and revealing a {{Term|ALBW|Heart Container|link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Painting|link}} of {{Term|ALBW|Irene|link}} buried inside.
