{{Term/Store|Seafood Curry|Seafood Curries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Seafood Curry}}<br/>This dish brims with treasures from the sea. Its spice packs a kick, so it's not for kids.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Seafood Curries can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}}, and either a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The default version can be made by using two types of seafood with differing effects. Eating these meals will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Though this dish is filled with a lot of seafood, the intensity of the Goron Spice makes it unsuitable for children.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Depending on the type of seafood used, three different variations of Seafood Curry exist:

* '''Hearty Seafood Curry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Seafood Curry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This dish brims with treasures from the sea. Its spice packs a kick, so it's not for kids.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using Hearty Blueshell Snails. When eaten, they will heal of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Mighty Seafood Curry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Seafood Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This dish brims with treasures from the sea. Its spice packs a kick, so it's not for kids.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using Mighty Porgies. By eating them, Link will receive a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Seafood Curry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Seafood Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This dish brims with treasures from the sea. Its spice packs a kick, so it's not for kids.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using Armored Porgies. Link can eat these curries to have his defensive abilities be increased.<ref name="Tough"/>
