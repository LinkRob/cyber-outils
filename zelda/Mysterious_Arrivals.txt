{{Term/Store|Mysterious Arrivals||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mysterious Arrivals|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Mysterious Arrivals}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given ten minutes to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Hollow}} of each {{Term|HWAoC|Champion|link}}. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Hollow}} of {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}} spawn first, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Hollow}} of {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} spawns after they are all defeated. Since both spawn at the same time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} can choose which one to approach first, although the other enemy will begin an assault on them soon afterwards. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Star Fragment|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
