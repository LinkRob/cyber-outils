'''Diving Moles''' are enemies in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Characteristics==
They appear in {{Term|FPTRR|Lon Lon Meadow}}. They come up out of the ground momentarily and then return. As such, they do not directly attempt to attack {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}. They will drop {{Term|FPTRR|Power Garlic|link}} when defeated and are the only means of locating this ingredient.
