'''Mushroom Men''' (or '''Toadstool Men''') are enemies that appear in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} They are {{Plural|FPTRR|Mushroom|display=toadstools|link}} that can pop out of the ground and walk. They usually tend to move when {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} is at a distance. They are the only source for {{Term|FPTRR|Mushroom}} ingredients.

==Characteristics and Variations==
===Mushroom Men===
Standard {{Term|FPTRR|Mushroom Men}} appear in {{Term|FPTRR|Lon Lon Meadow|link}}. They are hard to catch in battle if {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} is on his own, and catching more than one in battle is very challenging. They will however drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Aroma Toadstool|link}} when defeated.

===Mushroom Mean===
[[File:FPTRR Mushroom Mean Sprite.png]]
'''Mushroom Mean''' only appear in the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Forest|link}}. When in the ground, they spray a purple gas that confuses {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}, making him walk the opposite direction he is currently going. They tend to drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Misty Toadstool|link}} or Glowing Spores when defeated.

===Mountain Mushroom Mean===
[[File:FPTRR Mountain Mushroom Mean Sprite.png]]
'''Mountain Mushroom Mean''' appear on the foothills of {{Term|FPTRR|Mount Desma|link}}. They have a purple coloration to their stalk and legs and are stronger than regular {{Term|FPTRR|Mushroom Men}}, but do not release any poisonous gas like those found in the forest. They tend to drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Aroma Toadstool|link}} when defeated.
