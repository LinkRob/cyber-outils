The {{Term/Store|Cave of Flames||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> also known as the '''Mine''',<ref>{{Cite|One of them should be in the {{Color|TMC Green|mine}} the humans dug. It's not far from here.|Melari|TMC}}</ref> is the second {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} in {{TMC}}, located near the top of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}. It used to be a human mine, but was abandoned at some point.<ref>{{Cite|If you head outside from here, you can go see the mine the humans dug. But the boss doesn't let anyone go in there without permission.|Mountain Minish|TMC}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Being a mine, many of the puzzles in this {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} involve manipulating {{Plural|TMC|Mine Cart|link}} and tracks. Several rooms are filled with lava, and must be crossed either by jumping into {{Plural|TMC|Updraft|link}} and floating across, or by moving quickly along patches of cooled lava. The item obtained in the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} is the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}}, a mysterious cane with the ability to flip certain objects upside-down (such as the aforementioned {{Plural|TMC|Mine Cart}} and lava patches).<ref>{{Cite|You got the Cane of Pacci! This mystical rod has the power to flip things over. Use it to charge up energy in holes and then flip up on out of it!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> The {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames}} is infested with {{Plural|TMC|Rollobite|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Bob-omb|link}}, and {{Plural|TMC|Spiked Beetle|link}}, among other monsters.

The {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}'s boss is {{Term|TMC|Gleerok|link}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Sub-Boss}} is a group of eight {{Plural|TMC|Spiny Chuchu|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Appears in the Cave of Flames. A Gleeok with a hard, rocky shell found in Mount Crenel. Hiding in lava, this beast spews fire on everything it sees.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> Clearing the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} earns {{Term|TMC|Link}} the second of the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It'll take me a while to rework your sword. In the meantime, you should track down the missing elements. One of them should be in the mine the humans dug. It's not far from here.|Melari|TMC}}</ref>
