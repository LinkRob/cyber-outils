{{Term/Store|Heart Potion|Heart Potions|Series, SS|plural}} are items in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 127}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Heart Potions can be bought at the [[Potion Shop#Skyward Sword|Potion Shop]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. They can be stored in empty [[Bottle]]s and restore eight [[Heart]]s when drank.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|SS Gold|Heart Potion}}! When placed in your pouch, you can use this item to restore {{Color|SS Red|eight hearts}}.|N/A|SS}}</ref>

Heart Potions can be upgraded at the potion shop by infusing them with various [[Bug]]s, increasing their healing properties.
