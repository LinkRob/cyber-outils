"{{Term/Store|To Mount Lanayru||Series, BotW}}" is the 14th {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|To Mount Lanayru}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #14}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
<!-- Upon returning from a field survey, Zelda shows Link Mount Lanayru towering in the distance... Zelda commits to going to the mountain to initiate the sacred rites that may awaken her hidden power there. -->
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can recover this {{Term|BotW|Memory|link}} by visiting the {{Term|BotW|Sanidin Park Ruins|link}} after receiving the "{{Term|BotW|Captured Memories|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Memory}} begins with {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} riding their {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} up {{Term|BotW|Safula Hill|link}}. She thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for giving her advice on riding and treating her mount respectfully.<ref>{{Cite|"Be sure to take the time to soothe your mount... That's the only way it will know how you truly feel." Your advice was quite helpful—thank you. This little one and I are getting along quite well now.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She mentions that she was initially uncertain whether to adorn her {{Term|BotW|Horse}} with the {{Term|BotW|Royal Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Royal Saddle|link}}, believing he had to earn them, but she now feels that they suit him.<ref>{{Cite|At first, I wasn't sure if I should outfit him with all of the royal gear. I thought maybe he should have to earn it first. But it works! He wears it like a true natural.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} then adds that she is trying to become more empathetic with such matters.<ref>{{Cite|I'm trying to be a bit more empathetic. Benefit of the doubt, you know?|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

The pair continue up {{Term|BotW|Safula Hill}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sanidin Park Ruins}}, dismounting where the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} statue stands. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} looks out over the railing and points out {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}, adding that it was named after the Goddess of Wisdom, {{Term|BotW|Nayru|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|See that mountain? That's Mt. Lanayru. It takes its name from the Goddess of Wisdom.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She mentions that only those who have reached the age of 17 may ascend the mountain, where the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|link}} lies.<ref>{{Cite|Lanayru's decree is very specific. It says: "No one is allowed, under the age of seventeen... For only the wise are permitted a place upon the mountain." I've prayed at the Spring of Courage, and at the Spring of Power, yet neither awoke anything inside me. But maybe up there... Perhaps the Spring of Wisdom, the final of the three, will be the one.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Having already prayed at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}} to no avail, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} is doubtful whether praying at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom}} will awake anything inside her, but she remains hopeful.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, I have no real reason to think that will be the case. But there's always the chance that the next moment will change everything.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She then informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that tomorrow is her 17th birthday, and she will scale the mountain then.<ref>{{Cite|Tomorrow...is my seventeenth birthday. So then I shall go...and make my way up the mountain.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>
