{{Term/Store|Rhondson Armor Boutique||Series, BotW}} is an Armor {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Rhondson Armor Boutique}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}, ran by {{Term|BotW|Rhondson|link}}. After the proper recruitment has been completed by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Shop}} will appear in town. Once the {{Term|BotW|Shop}} is established, pieces of armor from the {{Term|BotW|Desert Voe Set|link}} will be sold.

In {{TotK}}, the store no longer exists, as Rhondson now helps Hudson, her husband, run their construction company. The Desert Voe Set is now found at the [[Gerudo Secret Club]].
