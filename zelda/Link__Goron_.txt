{{Term/Store|Link||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238 {{Exp Game|OoT}} & 245 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>{{Note|In reality, the {{Term|MM3D|Goron}}'s name matches whatever the player chooses as the hero {{Term|MM3D|Link}}'s name.}} In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', the {{Term|OoT3D|Goron|link}} chief {{Term|OoT3D|Darunia|link}} names his son after the [[Hylian]] hero [[Link]]. In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', Link the Goron and the [[Hero of Time]] share their name by coincidence. Link can exploit this to claim the Goron's reservation at the {{Term|MM3D|Stock Pot Inn|link}}.

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:Link (Goron) meeting Hero of Time Link.png|left|thumb|246x246px|Link the Goron meeting Link the Hero of Time in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
In ''Ocarina of Time'', [[Darunia]]'s son Link is born sometime during the Hero of Time's long seven-year sleep.<ref>{{Cite|My dad is Darunia...|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> Darunia honorably names his son after his Sworn Brother and hero, who, before falling into his deep slumber, had saved the [[Goron]]s by destroying the [[Dodongo]]s, including [[King Dodongo]], that had taken possession of [[Dodongo's Cavern]],<ref>{{Cite|Dad named me Link after you, because you're so brave!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> the Gorons' chief source of nourishment. After the hero Link awakens into an adult, he returns to [[Goron City]] in his search for Darunia to gain the [[Fire Medallion]] and finds it entirely devoid of Gorons, save for the young Goron Link. When the homonym characters first meet, Link the Goron is afraid the green-clad [[Hylian]] is one of [[Ganondorf]]'s servants, and defiantly continues to roll around the city to avoid being captured.<ref>{{Cite|I won't let you get me! You probably work for Ganondorf!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> The hero stops the Goron with a well-placed [[Bomb]].<ref name="OoT Hero"/> Upon learning the hero's name, Link the Goron realizes that the Hylian is the famous young hero known as the "Dodongo Buster" his father had told him about.<ref>{{Cite|What? Your name is also Link? Then you must be the legendary Dodongo Buster and Hero, Link!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> Knowing only his namesake could accomplish the task, he emotionally asks the hero to save Darunia and the other Gorons, who have been imprisoned at the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}} by Ganondorf and threatened to be fed to [[Volvagia]].<ref>{{Cite|Dad told me not to let anybody follow him to the temple, but... Only you, Link, can save everyone! I'm sure that the shop owner, who is hiding somewhere right now, will also help you! Now, I'll tell you about the secret passage to the Fire Temple!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> He also helps the hero by informing him of Volvagia's weakness to the powerful [[Megaton Hammer]],<ref>{{Cite|A long time ago there was an evil dragon named Volvagia living in this mountain. That dragon was very scary! He ate Gorons! Using a huge hammer, the hero of the Gorons... BOOOM! Destroyed it just like that. This is a myth from long ago, but it's true! I know, because my dad is a descendant of the hero!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> giving him the [[Goron Tunic]],<ref>{{Cite|Please help, Link! I'll give you this heat-resistant tunic!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> and revealing the secret entrance into [[Death Mountain Crater]].<ref>{{Cite|Try to move the statue inside Dad's room!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM3D Anju and Link (Goron).png|thumb|272x272px|Anju talking to Link the Goron in ''Majora's Mask 3D'']]
In {{MM}}, a travelling Goron named Link, visits [[East Clock Town]] on the [[First Day]] at 3:00 PM. He has a reservation at the [[Stock Pot Inn]] which he attempts to claim at 4:10 PM.<ref>{{Cite|I should have a reservation-goro. The name is Link. Don't you have it-goro?|Link (Goron)|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Mr. Link. Ah! I do have your reservation. Your room is our "Knife Chamber" on the second floor. This is your room key.|Anju|MM}}</ref> If he gets the room, he goes into the Knife Chamber and is not seen again. However, Link the hero can claim the reservation for himself by speaking to [[Anju]] first, between 1:50 PM and 4:10 PM, assuming he had not spoken to her before this time, and earn the [[Room Key]].<ref>{{Cite|You do have a reservation? That's good... Mr. Link, is it? One moment, please. Mr. Link, I have you down for an afternoon arrival. Your room is our "Knife Chamber" on the second floor. Here is your key.|Anju|MM}}</ref> He can do so because the reservation is under his name and [[Anju]] tends to forget the faces of customers.<ref>{{Cite|Umm...Are you...staying the night? Oh my! I wonder...Did I by chance ask you the same thing earlier? My mother is always telling me to remember our customer's faces, but I tend to forget them...|Anju|MM}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, unlike the original version, Link must talk to Anju in his [[Goron Link|Goron form]] to claim the reservation. The ruse is not discovered because of the Goron's habit of suffixing his sentences with "goro,"<ref>{{Cite|I made a reservation... The name is Link-goro!|Link (Goron)|MM}}</ref> which confuses Anju.<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Link-goro? I don't have a reservation under that name. There is one close to that, but...|Anju|MM}}</ref>

If Link the Hero of Time claims the room before Link the Goron, the latter will not protest to Anju and decide to sleep outside instead.<ref>{{Cite|What?!? Really-goro? Well, it's nice weather, so I'll just sleep outside-goro.|Link (Goron)|MM}}</ref> He falls asleep just outside Stock Pot Inn as night falls on the First Day, at 6:00 PM. During the night, he talks in his sleep of a dampness in the wind, predicting the thunderstorm that comes down on [[Clock Town]] on the [[Second Day]].<ref>{{Cite|The wind has gotten damp-goro... Tomorrow is rain-goro...|Link (Goron)|MM}}</ref> Upon the Dawn of the Second Day, the disadvantaged Goron has left<!-- "has left" instead of "leaves" because you don't actually see him leave. --> town and is not seen again.

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese release, Link the Goron is known as '''Goron Kid'''.
**In ''[[mariowiki:Mario_Golf_(Nintendo_64)|Mario Golf]]'' of the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series, as an [[Cameos of The Legend of Zelda in other media|Easter Egg]], a character named "Goron Kid" is seen among the tournament leaderboards based on the unseen cast of ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]''. It is likely it's referring to Link.
*Link the Goron in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' shares the same model as the other [[Goron|Gorons]] but is at a smaller size shorter than Adult Link.
*Unlike most {{Plural|MM3D|Goron}} in {{MM}}, Link the {{Term|MM3D|Goron}} will not be put to sleep by the "{{Term|MM3D|Goron's Lullaby|link}}".
*Link the Goron in the two [[Nintendo 64]] games are one of the few creatures in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series who shares the same name as the [[Link|main protagonist]]. The others are [[Abe]] and [[Rose]]'s [[Link (Pig)|pet Pig]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', and [[Sera]]'s [[Link (Cat)|pet Cat]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]''.
**Because of this, they will also match the name the player chooses for the hero.
