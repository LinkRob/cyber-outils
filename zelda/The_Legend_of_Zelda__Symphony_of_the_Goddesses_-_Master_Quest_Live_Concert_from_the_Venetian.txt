{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses - Master Quest Live Concert from the Venetian''||Series}} is a live performance album recorded during the [[The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses|''Symphony of the Goddesses'']] {{MQ|-}} tour on June 10, 2015 at The Venetian in Las Vegas, Nevada.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Concert recorded on the 2015-06-10, at The Venetian Theatre at The Venetian, Las Vegas, Nevada.|author= |published= July 30, 2017|retrieved= June 8, 2019|url= https://vgmdb.net/album/70239|title= The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses - Master Quest|site= VGMdb|type= }}</ref> It was first teased by Jason Michael Paul, producer of the ''Symphony of the Goddesses'', in some 2017 interviews.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= '''NI: Are there any plans to release a concert recording – whether that be a CD or DVD?'''<br/>'''JMP:''' Announcement regarding this is coming in the near future. We recorded a live show in Las Vegas. It will be made into a DVD / CD set.|author= Alex Seedhouse|published= April 14, 2017|retrieved= June 8, 2019|url= https://www.nintendo-insider.com/interview-striking-a-chord-with-the-legend-of-zelda-symphony-of-the-goddesses|title= Interview: Striking A Chord With The Legend Of Zelda: Symphony Of The Goddesses|site= Nintendo Insider|type= }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= '''There have been a few CD releases of Zelda music before, but there hasn’t been an official CD or digital release from the Symphony of the Goddesses. Are there any plans for this?'''<br/>JMP: Yes, this news will be released soon. We will begin with a pre-order at www.zelda-symphony.com|author= Kyle Hanson|published= March 8, 2018|retrieved= June 8, 2019|url= https://attackofthefanboy.com/articles/the-legend-of-zelda-symphony-of-the-goddesses-interview/|title= The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses Interview|site= Attack of the Fanboy|type= }}</ref> The album was released digitally on July 28, 2017,<ref name= "Supra"/> but was pulled from distribution in America possibly because of copyright issues.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Mike Weatherford|published= June 7, 2015|retrieved= June 8, 2019|url= https://www.reviewjournal.com/entertainment/entertainment-columns/mike-weatherford/musicians-union-president-protector-or-obstructionist/|title= Musicians union president: Protector or obstructionist?|site= Las Vegas Review-Journal|type= }}</ref> However, it can still be bought through European online stores.<ref name= "Supra"/>
