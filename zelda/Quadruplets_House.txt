{{Term/Store|Quadruplets House||Series, LANS}} is a location in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Quadruplets House|System|LANS}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Quadruplets House}} was referred to as the {{Term/Store|Quadruplet's House||LA, LADX}} in all versions of {{LA}} prior to {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Quadruplet's House|Atlas Of Koholint Island|LADX}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{LA|NS|-}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|LANS|Quadruplets House}} is where the {{Term|LANS|Quadruplet Family|link}} lives in {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|link}}. When {{Term|LANS|Link}} awakens on {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}, he can find {{Term|LANS|Mamasha|link}} and {{Term|LANS|Papahl|link}} inside the {{Term|LANS|Quadruplets House}}. {{Term|LANS|Mamasha}} expresses her trouble with raising four boys who look alike and explains that her {{Term|LANS|Mamasha}}'s baby wants the {{Term|LANS|Yoshi Doll|link}} from the {{Term|LANS|Trendy Game|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|With four boys who look alike, even I get confused sometimes...|Mamasha|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|By the way, my baby wants a {{Icon|LANS Yoshi Doll}}{{Color|LANS Pink|Yoshi doll}}. I saw one at the Trendy Game, but I couldn't get it.|Mamasha|LANS}}</ref> If {{Term|LANS|Link}} retrieves the {{Term|LANS|Yoshi Doll}} and brings it to her, she will exchange it for a {{Term|LANS|Ribbon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Will you give that {{Icon|LANS Yoshi Doll}}{{Color|LANS Pink|Yoshi doll}} to my baby?!|Mamasha|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Yes] Oh, thank you! You are indeed a generous person! Ah! I will give you this in return!|Mamasha|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You traded your {{Icon|LANS Yoshi Doll}}{{Color|LANS Pink|Yoshi doll}} for a {{Icon|LANS Ribbon}}{{Color|LANS Pink|ribbon}}! Maybe you can trade the ribbon for something else!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

The {{Term|LANS|Quadruplets House}} is heavily decorated with children's toys and there are six beds for the residents to sleep in. Additionally, there are four stands for the {{Term|LANS|Boo Figure|link|display=Boo}}, {{Term|LANS|Pokey Figure|link|display=Pokey}}, {{Term|LANS|Shy Guy Figure|link|display=Shy Guy}}, and {{Term|LANS|Spiny Figure|link|display=Spiny}} {{Plural|LANS|Figure|link}}.
