The {{Term/Store|Giant Bomb Bag|Giant Bomb Bag|TP, TPHD}},{{Exp Game|TP}}<ref name="ency">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page= 117}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Biggest Bomb Bag|Biggest Bomb Bags|OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}},{{Exp Game|Series, OoT, MM}}<ref name="ency"/> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Giant Bomb Bag}} is an upgrade to the {{Term|OoT3D|Large Bomb Bag|link}} that increases its carrying capacity from 30 {{Plural|OoT3D|Bomb|link}} to 40. It can be obtained by first acquiring the {{Term|OoT|Large Bomb Bag}} in one of the following manners:
#In {{Term|OoT3D|Goron City|link}}, as child {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}, use a {{Term|OoT3D|Bomb}} to stop the {{Term|OoT3D|Hot Rodder Goron|link}}'s rolling while the {{Term|OoT3D|Goron}} is inside the northern alcove. He will reward {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} for his "courage" with the {{Term|OoT3D|Large Bomb Bag}}.<ref>{{Cite|All right! I'll give you this in honor of your courage!|Hot Rodder Goron|OoT3D}}</ref>
#Win the {{Term|OoT3D|Bombchu Bowling Alley|link}} {{Term|OoT3D|Mini-Game}} while the {{Term|OoT3D|Large Bomb Bag}} is the selected prize.

Whichever upgrade is acquired second will become the {{Term|OoT3D|Giant Bomb Bag}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
The {{Term|MM3D|Giant Bomb Bag}} in {{MM}} holds up to 40 {{Plural|MM3D|Bomb}} and is purchased from the {{Term|MM3D|Business Scrub|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|Goron Village|link}}, who ordinarily sells the upgrade for 1,000 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee|link}}; more than {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can carry in his {{Term|MM3D|Wallet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you don't have a {{Color|MM3D Red|Big Bomb Bag}}, I have to sell it for {{Color|MM3D Purple|1,000 Rupees}}.|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} speaks to the {{Term|MM3D|Business Scrub|display=Scrub}} while in [[Goron Link|Goron form]],{{Fact}} he will be able to trade his {{Term|MM3D|Large Bomb Bag|link}} and buy the upgraded {{Term|MM3D|Giant Bomb Bag}} for just 200 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give you my {{Color|MM3D Red|Biggest Bomb Bag}}, regularly priced at {{Color|MM3D Purple|1,000 Rupees}}... In return, you'll give me your {{Color|MM3D Red|Big Bomb Bag}} and just {{Color|MM3D Purple|200 Rupees}}!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Giant Bomb Bag}} is obtained by scoring at least 25 points in {{Term|TPHD|Rapids Ride|link|display=Iza's Rapid Ride}}. As opposed to being an {{Term|TPHD|Item}} that appears in {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Inventory|link}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Giant Bomb Bag}} simply doubles the capacity of all of {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s other {{Plural|TPHD|Bomb Bag|link}}, allowing them to hold up to 60 {{Plural|TPHD|Bomb|link}}, 30 {{Plural|TPHD|Water Bomb|link}}, or 20 {{Plural|TPHD|Bombling|link}}.
