==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Snowcoat Fox}}
Snowcoat Foxes are a breed of [[Grassland Fox]] adapted for life in cold climates. They possess a much paler coloration in response to their environment, sporting pale blue fur in place of their lowland relatives' orange coats in order to better blend in with their snowy surroundings.<ref>{{Cite|This particular breed of grassland fox makes its home in cold climates such as the Tabantha region. Its fur turned white as a means of adapting to snowy weather, serving as a natural camouflage.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They can be found in most cold locations in Hyrule, including the [[Hebra Mountains]], [[Tabantha Tundra]], [[Gerudo Highlands]] and [[Mount Lanayru]].
