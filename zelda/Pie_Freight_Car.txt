The {{Term/Store|Pie Freight Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Pie Freight Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Pie Freight Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Palace Dish|link}}, one {{Term|ST|Goron Amber|link}} and three {{Plural|ST|Dark Pearl Loop|link}}. Shaped like a slice of light-colored pie, it features a card with a {{Term|ST|Dove|link}}, symbol of the {{Plural|ST|Royal Engineer|link}}, stuck in one of the whipped cream dollops. Its description reads "This car is designed to make train enthusiasts drool."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Pie Freight Car}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Sweet Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
