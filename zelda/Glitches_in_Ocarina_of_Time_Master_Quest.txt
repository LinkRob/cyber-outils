=== Skip the Water Temple ===
{{Youtube|ZHDDGuYCIgo|256|right|Skip the Water Temple altogether.}}
Once you've met with [[Princess Ruto]] in the {{Term|OoT|Water Temple|link}}, raise the water to halfway level, then sink underwater with the [[Iron Boots]] and go back to the room she was in. Remove the Iron Boots here and float up to the now accessible ledge, then [[bomb]] the damaged wall. Use the [[Hookshot]] to shoot a pattern on the back wall of the newly opened room, and a chest will appear with the [[Longshot]] inside. Now raise the water level to its highest, and Longshot over to the platform leading to the Boss chamber. Once in the room with the slope and [[Blade Trap]]s, use the Longshot to hit a switch above the door, then Longshot onto the left column that appears. With your sword drawn, face the wall the Boss Door is on, side-hop off the column to position yourself in front of the Door in mid-air, and press B and forward. You should have [[Jump Attack]]ed through the door, accessing the Boss Room without the [[Boss Key]]. It may take a few tries, just climb back up onto the column, Link can reach it. Then just defeat [[Morpha]], and the temple is finished that quickly.

This method of the glitch, however, has been fixed in the {{OoT3D}} version, but an alternate method exists.{{Youtube|prIf1CgI7oI|256|left|Skip the Water Temple in OoT3D.}}
