'''Fishmen''' appear in {{TWW}} as supporting characters that help [[Link]] by giving him tips about secrets across the [[Great Sea]] and filling out sections of his [[Sea Chart]].

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Fishman}}
Fishmen know much about the [[Great Sea]] and will give [[Link]] many tips and words of knowledge about everything. They are also great cartographers and will fill in portions of the Sea Chart for Link in exchange for [[All-Purpose Bait]].<ref name= "Fishmen"/> Fishmen will also provide hints on each island's secrets. The first time Link meets a Fishman, it asserts to the [[King of Red Lions]] that its "debt is repaid," implying that they had received some sort of favor from the King of Red Lions in the past.<ref>{{Cite|'Hoy! You there! You Kingly Red Lion guy! That's it! I've repaid my debt!|Fishman|TWW}}</ref>

After obtaining the [[Bow]], Link can play a minigame with them to help relieve their body aches.<ref>{{Cite|That's a fine-looking bow you got there! Where'd you get that? If you're not in any kind of hurry, why don't you stop and play with me a bit?|Fishman|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|See, I've been having some ferocious body aches that only acupuncture can cure!|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> The Fishman will jump out of the water 10 times, so Link must shoot it with [[Arrow]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give you 10 arrows for that bow of yours, and I want you to aim those suckers at me when I leap out of the sea! Yeah! See if you can't peg me with 'em!|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> The reward for this is 10 [[Rupee]]s for every successful hit and 200 Rupees for a perfect score.<ref>{{Cite|For each arrow you nail me with, I'll give you 10 Rupees...and if you hit me all 10 times, I'll give you 200 Rupees!|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> These Arrows remain stuck in the Fishman's body after the completion of the game.

One of these Fishmen associated regularly with the Cafe worker [[Gillian]].<ref>{{Cite|I wonder how long it's been since the light in Windfall's lighthouse went dark... [...] I used to gaze at it with dear Gillian...|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> [[Loot]], another resident of [[Windfall Island]], is afraid of Fishmen.<ref>{{Cite|Hey... Have you ever seen what I call the frightening fish-men?|Loot|TWW}}</ref>
