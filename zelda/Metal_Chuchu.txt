{{Term/Store|Metal Chuchu|Metal Chuchus|Series, ST|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 192}}</ref> also known as '''Metal ChuChus''',{{Name Ref|ST}} are enemies in {{ST}}.

==Characteristics==
Metal ChuChus acts almost the same as [[Yellow Chuchu|Yellow ChuChus]]. Their bodies are constantly electrified, which makes them immuned to sword attacks as it would damage Link instead. Likewise, Link will take damage if he touch them. Unlike Yellow ChuChus, Metal ChuChus will never stop to generate electricity even if stunned. The only way to damage them is to use long ranged weapons instead of close ranged ones. They are mainly found in the [[Ocean Temple]] and [[Tower of Spirits]]. Phantom Zelda's sword is also able to kill them.
