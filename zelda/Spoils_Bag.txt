The {{Term/Store|Spoils Bag||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an item in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Spoils Bag is obtained by completing [[Niko]]'s [[Rope (Object)|rope]]-swinging challenge inside [[Tetra's Ship]].<ref>{{Cite|All right, swabbie! I'll tell you what! You can have the treasure in that chest over there! It's your reward from the great and generous Niko! Hurry and take it before someone comes!|Niko|TWW}}</ref> It can hold up to 99 of each of the eight [[Spoil]]s obtained from various enemies.
