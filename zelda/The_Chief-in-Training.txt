{{Term/Store|The Chief-in-Training||HWAoC}} is a level 42 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Chief-in-Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|The Chief-in-Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Winterwing Butterfly|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Cold Darner|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}}'s objective is to defeat 400 {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga|link}} {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within ten minutes. Most are {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}, although a single {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} is present. More footsoldiers only appear in the center of the map after the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} is defeated, and are led by {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}}. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} loses about a fourth of his health, he retreats and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} appear in the bottom left corner of the map and more {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsolider}} spawn as well. The same happens again when those {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} are killed, except for a third blademaster added to the group. When {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} finally defeats 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} reappears in the east with more footsoldiers and two blademasters. The timer is taken away as well, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} is defeated.
