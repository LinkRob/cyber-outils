The {{Term/Store|Death Mountain Marker|Death Mountain Markers|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #1<br/>Climbing Path Trailhead|Death Mountain Marker|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Plural|BotW|Death Mountain Marker}} are a series of {{Plural|BotW|Sign|link}} located along the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road|link}}. They provide information to their readers about {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}. For example, they share the heat level of the {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.<ref name="Two">{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #2<br/>Goron City ahead<br/>Heat level: ★<br/>Non-Gorons use extreme caution|Death Mountain Marker|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #6<br/>Abandoned North Mine<br/>Heat level: ★★<br/>No unauthorized access|Death Mountain Marker|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #8<br/>Bridge of Eldin<br/>Heat level:★★<br/>Non-Gorons turn back or burn!|Death Mountain Marker|BotW}}</ref> Several of these {{Plural|BotW|Sign}} also direct their readers towards {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}.<ref name="Two"/><ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #4<br/><br/>This way to Goron City|Death Mountain Marker|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #5<br/><br/>Welcome to Goron City|Death Mountain Marker|BotW}}</ref>
