{{Term/Store|Fire Eye|Fire Eyes|Series, OoT|plural}} are recurring traps in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=There are fans on either side of the narrow path. Keep the Iron Boots equipped and eliminate the Fire Eye above the door.|url=http://web.archive.org/web/20061106152451/http://www.zelda.com/ocarina/pdf_files/09_shadow_ocarina.pdf|title=Shadow Temple Map|site=Zelda Universe}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The Fire Eye looks like a normal silver [[Switch#Eye Switch|Eye Switch]]. When [[Link]] is close to it, it will emit fire. If it is shot with an [[Arrow]] while open, it will be destroyed.

In the normal quest of {{OoT|-}}, only one Fire Eye appears in the [[Shadow Temple]]. In the {{MQ}}, there are several in the Shadow Temple. These Fire Eyes open when Link is near them and shoots a fireball that slowly chases Link once it touches the ground. The fireball is extremely persistent but can be deflected using a [[Shield]].

In {{MM|-}}, several Fire Eyes appear [[Beneath the Well]]. These Fire Eyes emit a prolonged flame when Link is too close, which cannot be defended against using a Shield.
