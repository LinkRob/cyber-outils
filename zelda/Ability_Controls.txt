The {{Term/Store|Ability Controls||Series, BotW}} menu is an interface in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ability Controls|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Ability Controls}} menu details different actions that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can perform. Each ability control must first be revealed through gameplay before becoming visible on the menu.
