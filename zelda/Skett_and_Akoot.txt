'''Skett''' and '''Akoot''' are a pair of characters in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Skett'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 259{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Akoot'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 259{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Skett and Akoot are the personal guards of the {{Term|TWWHD|Rito Chieftain|link}}. Never seen apart, they often consult each other concerning the factual accuracy of any comment directed towards {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}}, often in reference to {{Term|TWWHD|Medli|link}},<ref>{{Cite|If you are looking for Medli, I believe she is atop the cliff just outside. Do I speak the truth, Skett?|Akoot|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You do, Akoot, as usual! She is right in the same old spot! If you step outside near where she is, you should be able to hear the song she is playing.|Skett|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Komali|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I believe that Prince Komali has gone to Dragon Roost to study. Is that not right? Akoot! Look alive!|Skett|TWW}}</ref> or the {{Term|TWWHD|Rito Chieftain|link}} himself.<ref>{{Cite|Yes! Yes, of course! I deem you are absolutely correct, Skett.|Akoot|TWW}}</ref> Their interaction always leads to humorous bickering and arguing, providing for some poignant comic relief.<ref>{{Cite|What are you, mute, Skett? Could you not show a little appreciation?|Akoot|TWW}}</ref> Upon entering the main Rito Hall within {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island|link}}, Skett and Akoot are seen flanking the {{Term|TWWHD|Rito Chieftain}}, along with {{Term|TWWHD|Quill|link}}. Following the conversation between the Chieftain and {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}, they quickly escort the Chieftain to his room. From that moment on, the two guards can be found in the Chieftain's room standing at each end of the rear exit.
