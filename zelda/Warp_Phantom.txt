{{Term/Store|Warp Phantom|Warp Phantoms|Series, ST|plural}} are enemies in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 289}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Warp Phantoms are blue-armored Phantoms with the ability to warp anywhere across the room. Under Zelda's control, the Warp Phantom is limited to only warping to the locations of [[Phantom Eyes]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey, check it out! I'm a Warp Phantom now! Hee!/You know those eyeball monster things? They're called Phantom Eyes. If you draw a line to a Phantom Eye, I can teleport straight there!|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref>
