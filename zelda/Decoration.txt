{{Term/Store|Decoration|Decorations|Series|plural}} are items in {{TWW}}.{{Name Ref|TWW}} They are part of the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Trading Sequence|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TWWHD|Decoration}} are objects that {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} can put around {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}} and certain other islands. They are obtained by trading with the {{Plural|TWWHD|Wandering Merchant|link}} and then buying the traded item from {{Term|TWWHD|Zunari|link}}. After {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} trades a {{Term|TWWHD|Decoration}} with a {{Term|TWWHD|Wandering Merchant}}, it appears for sale in {{Term|TWWHD|Zunari's Shop|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The first thing you need to do is take this to another traveling merchant and trade it for something... to build on the foundation of my merchant's oath. As soon as you trade, the merchant you traded with will send his products to my shop, thereby increasing my product line.|Zunari|TWW}}</ref> Once {{Term|TWWHD|Zunari}} has every {{Term|TWWHD|Decoration}} on sale, he rewards {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} with the {{Term|TWWHD|Magic Armor|link}}.

Putting {{Plural|TWWHD|Decoration}} in each of the pedestals around {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island}} grants {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} a {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}} from {{Term|TWWHD|Sam|link}}.{{Fact|TWWHD}} Trading the {{Term|TWWHD|Shop Guru Statue|link}} with the {{Term|TWWHD|Wandering Merchant}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Greatfish Isle|link}} also grants {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} another {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart}}. Other {{Plural|TWWHD|Decoration}}, like the {{Term|TWWHD|Shop Guru Statue}}, can help {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} earn {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} from {{Term|TWWHD|Kane|link}} if placed on the top of {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island}}'s gate.<ref>{{Cite|Look at that! It's brilliant! It makes me so happy to see the gate like that! ...Here! I want you to have this!|Kane|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Kane}} will say different things depending on the {{Plural|TWWHD|Decoration}} above the gate of {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, by the by, there's a statue way up there on top of the gate that looks kinda familiar somehow... Is that an idol carved in the style of the Rito Tribe? That's a mighty nice lookin' statue, if it is.|Kane|TWW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|TWWHD|Fountain Idol}} will occasionally doze off, tilting her head forwards and spilling water out of the jar on her head three times before she snaps out of it.
*There are a total of 37 pedestals for {{Plural|TWWHD|Decoration}} around {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}: 14 outdoors around the island and 23 inside various shops and buildings. There are also two pedestals outside the Cabana at the {{Term|TWWHD|Private Oasis|link}}.
