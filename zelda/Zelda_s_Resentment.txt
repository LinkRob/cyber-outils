"{{Term/Store|Zelda's Resentment||Series, BotW}}" is the fifth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Zelda's Resentment}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #5}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|Zelda's Resentment}}" can be unlocked by visiting the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Columns|link}}, which are located on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}} opens up with {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} attempting to open the {{Term|BotW|Tena Ko'sah Shrine|link}} by pressing the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal|link}}. When nothing happens, she comments that it is just as she thought.<ref>{{Cite|Nothing. Just as I thought. Hmm...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She reasons that the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} were designed to only be used by the chosen hero of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It appears that this structure was designed to be exclusively accessed by the sword's chosen hero.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> However, she also hopes that she will be able to work around this design.<ref>{{Cite|But designs can always be worked around, at least I hope.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Stepping away, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} wonders how to get inside and states she needs to activate the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|How do I get inside...? I need to activate it somehow.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} musings are interrupted by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} arriving on horseback. She becomes angry, storming past {{Term|BotW|Link}} and reminding him she does not need an escort.<ref>{{Cite|I thought I made it clear I am not in need of an escort.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She further insults {{Term|BotW|Link}} by claiming she is the only one of the two to have a mind of their own, and she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she is fine regardless of {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}}'s orders.<ref>{{Cite|It seems I'm the only one with a mind of my own. I, the person in question, am fine, regardless of the king's orders.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She then commands him to return to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} and relay her thought to her father.<ref>{{Cite|Return to the castle. And tell that to my father, please.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to go with her, she turns and snaps at him to stop following her.<ref>{{Cite|And stop following me!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>
