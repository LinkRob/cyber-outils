The {{Term/Store|Akkala Span||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Akkala Span|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Span}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Cephla Lake|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Akkala Falls|link}}. The bridge connects the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands}} to the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}. It is a short bridge made out of stones with 3 stone pillars leading to the ground below. Next to the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Span}}, a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} can be found among some small ruins.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
On one of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Span}}'s broken pillar, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}} underneath a stone slab. This slab can be moved by attaching {{Plural|BotW|Octo Balloon|link}} to it or hitting it while it is under the effect of the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} picks up the {{Term|BotW|Rock}}, it will be revealed to be a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will then appear and gift {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
