{{Term/Store|Invading Terrors||HWAoC}} is a level 57 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Invading Terrors|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Invading Terrors}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ancient Core|link}}, and three {{Plural|HWAoC|Giant Ancient Core|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}'s objective is to defeat ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within ten minutes. The starting ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} include five {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}}, two {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}, two {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Guardian|link}}. Seven {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} appear after four of the targeted {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}} appears when eleven of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated. There are also {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Zora Soldier|link}} scattered around the map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once all eighteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
