The {{Term/Store|Green Luck Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Green Luck Ring<br/>1/2 damage from traps|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Green Luck Ring can be found inside [[Mayor Plen]]'s house in [[Lynna City]] in {{OoA}}. It requires the [[Long Hook]] to reach. In {{OoS}}, the Ring can be obtained in the [[Treasure Grove]] by following the [[Strange Brothers]] or can be won in the [[Subrosia]]n [[Dance Hall]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it reduces damage taken from traps.<ref name="Description"/> The Green Luck Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
