The {{Term/Store|Man Carrying Sack||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|OoT|Man Carrying Sack}} can be seen running around the {{Term|OoT|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} Market. If {{Term|OoT|Link|link}} talks to him, he will say a reference to the White Rabbit from {{Wp|Alice's Adventures in Wonderland|''Alice in Wonderland''}}.<ref>{{Cite|Huff huff! I'm late! I'm late! For a very important date!|Man Carrying Sack|OoT}}</ref> In the ending credits, he can be seen dancing around, joining in with the party.

He has a [[Termina|Terminan]] counterpart named [[Sakon]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', where, unlike the Man Carrying Sack, is a minor antagonist, as he steals items from certain characters such as the [[Old Lady from the Bomb Shop]] and [[Kafei]].
