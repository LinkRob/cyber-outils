'''Ikana''' can refer to any of several things within {{MM}}:

===Places===
*'''[[Ikana Kingdom]]''', the fallen kingdom of the land of Ikana.
*'''[[Ikana Canyon]]''', the eastern area of [[Termina]] containing the [[Stone Tower]] and its [[Stone Tower Temple|temple]].
*The '''[[Ancient Castle of Ikana]]''', a large castle guarded by its former residents' spirits.
*'''[[Ikana Graveyard]]''', the burial ground for the soldiers of Ikana who died in battle during the war against the [[Garo]]s.

===Characters===
*'''[[Igos du Ikana|King Igos du Ikana]]''', the king of Ikana Canyon before its demise.
