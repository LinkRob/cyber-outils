{{Term/Store|Curry Rice|Curry Rices|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Curry Rice}}<br/>A favorite all over Hyrule, this simple dish has a flavor you just won't get tired of.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Curry Rices are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Hylian Rice]] and [[Goron Spice]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. They can be eaten to restore two {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, and they also sell for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. They are a favorite all over {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, noted to be a simple dish with a flavor people don't get tired of.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Term|BotW|Lester|link}}, a worker at {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable|link}}, enjoys spice, specifically {{Term|BotW|Curry Rice}}.<ref>{{Cite|The thing about me is...I just can't get enough spice in my life. Spicyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy... I need that burny tingle... I need {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref> While he has {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice}}, he does not have any {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now, I have {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}} on hand, which I need to make {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}}, but I'm fresh out of {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref> He will request Link bring him Goron Spice,<ref>{{Cite|If you happen to get your hands on some {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}, can you give it to me?|Lester|BotW}}</ref> which begins the "[[Curry for What Ails You]]" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. As a reward for completing this quest, Lester will give Link 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey... Could I ask you to part with that {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} for, say, {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}}?|Lester|BotW}}</ref>

Only fruits can be added to Curry Rice, otherwise the resulting dishes will be different meals. Using additional ingredients will create the following variations:

* '''Hearty Curry Rice''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Curry Rice}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A favorite all over Hyrule, this simple dish has a flavor you just won't get tired of.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}}. They heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum number of Hearts when eaten.<ref name="Hearty"/> They are worth 50 Rupees when sold.

* '''Hasty Curry Rice''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Curry Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A favorite all over Hyrule, this simple dish has a flavor you just won't get tired of.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Term|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}}. When eaten, Link's speed will be temporarily boosted.<ref name="Hasty"/> They heal three of Link's Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Curry Rice''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Curry Rice}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A favorite all over Hyrule, this simple dish has a flavor you just won't get tired of.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}}. Eating one will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/> They restore three Hearts and can be sold for 30 Rupees.

* '''Electro Curry Rice''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Curry Rice}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A favorite all over Hyrule, this simple dish has a flavor you just won't get tired of.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfruit|link}}. If they are eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/> They heal six of Link's Hearts, but they can also be sold for 30 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Curry Rice''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Curry Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A favorite all over Hyrule, this simple dish has a flavor you just won't get tired of.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}}. Eating these will temporarily boost the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/> They will also restore three of Link's Hearts, and they sell for 30 Rupees.

* '''Chilly Curry Rice''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Curry Rice}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A favorite all over Hyrule, this simple dish has a flavor you just won't get tired of.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}}. When they are consumed, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]] for a short period of time.<ref name="Chilly"/> They heal three Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.
