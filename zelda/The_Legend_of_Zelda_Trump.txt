'''''The Legend of Zelda'' Trump''' is a set of playing cards officially published by Nintendo of Japan in early December 2011 in celebration of the [[The Legend of Zelda 25th Anniversary|25th Anniversary]] of the franchise.<ref>{{Cite Web|url=http://www.nintendo.co.jp/n09/chara_t_z/index.html|title= The Legend of Zelda Trump|site=Nintendo.co.jp}}</ref>

==Description==
Each of the suits features art from one of four different games from {{TLoZ|Series}}. The spades suit features art from {{TLoZ}}, the hearts suit features art from {{TWW}}, the diamonds suit features art from {{OoT3D}}, and the clubs suit features art from {{SS}}.

In each suit, {{Term|Series|Link|link}} is the jack card, {{Term|Series|Princess Zelda|link}} is the queen card, and one of the main antagonists is the king card. The ace and numbered cards feature enemies or items from its respective game. The set also contains two joker cards, featuring {{Term|Series|Tingle|link}}'s artwork from {{MM}} and {{TWW|-}}.
