'''Sword Moblins''' are recurring [[Enemy|Enemies]] in the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|Zelda Series]].

Sword Moblins are a variant of [[Moblin]] known for attacking with a [[sword]], but is lacking a [[shield]]. A stronger version, [[Sword Moblin Lv. 2]], and [[Sword Moblin Lv. 3]] are significantly more stronger and utilize shields. They can be summoned with an [[Echo]] cost of 2 when acquired.

A [[Dark Echo|Dark variant]] can be encountered after coming in contact with a [[Tecuum]].
