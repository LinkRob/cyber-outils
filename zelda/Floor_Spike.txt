{{Term/Store|Floor Spike|Floor Spikes|Series, PH|plural}},{{Exp Game|PH}}<ref>{{Cite|I don't think you can get through these {{Color|ST Light Blue|floor spikes}}, Link. Leave it to me! I'll figure something out!|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Spike|Spikes|FSA|plural}}{{Exp Game|FSA}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|game=FSA|publisher=Nintendo|page=92}}</ref> or {{Term/Store|Spike Trap|Spike Traps|CoH|plural}},{{Exp Game|CoH}}<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Spike Trap|Game Over|CoH}}</ref> are recurring obstacles in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP, LA, LADX, OoT, OoT3D, OoS, OoA, TWW, TWWHD, FSA, TP, TPHD, PH, SS, TFH, BotW}} {{Plural|Series|Floor Spikes}} go unnamed in {{TMC}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Floor Spikes can be avoided by using the [[Magic Cape]] in {{ALttP}}, as [[Link]] can walk directly over them without taking damage. The Magic Cape is necessary to obtain the [[Cane of Byrna]], as it is preceded by a number of Floor Spikes that Link would be unable to traverse safely otherwise.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LA}}

==={{OoT|-}}===
Many Floor Spikes are found inside the [[Water Temple]] in {{OoT}}, typically blocking passage until the water level rises. In the [[Shadow Temple]], certain Floor Spikes are invisible, requiring that Link use the [[Lens of Truth]] in order to see them. The [[Hover Boots]] can also be used to walk above Floor Spikes, momentarily avoiding damage, while the [[Goron Tunic]] causes Link to take damage less frequently from them.

==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:OoS LinkCarryingDodongo.png|thumb|Link tossing Dodongo into Floor Spikes in {{OoS|-}}]]
In {{OoS}}, in order to defeat [[Dodongo]], the [[boss]] from the [[Snake's Remains]], [[Link]] must toss it onto the Floor Spikes found in the center of the room after stunning it with a [[Bomb]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Stub|OoA}}

==={{TWW|-}}===
Floor Spikes appear in the [[Forsaken Fortress]] and the [[Wind Temple]] in {{TWW}}, though in the former they are unable to damage [[Link]] and serve only to obstruct his path. In both [[dungeon]]s, the Floor Spikes can usually be retracted once a certain puzzle is solved or all enemies in the area have been defeated.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Stub|FSA}}

==={{TMC|-}}===
Floor Spikes appear in [[dungeon]]s and other [[cave]]s across [[Hyrule]] in {{TMC}}, both while {{Term|TMC|Link}} is regular-sized and [[Minish]]-sized. They will damage [[Link]] if he touches them, though they can be leapt over by using [[Roc's Cape]].

==={{TP|-}}===
Floor Spikes appear within the [[Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|Arbiter's Grounds]] in {{TP}}, where they emerge from holes in the floor when Link approaches. Several of their locations are also concealed by [[Quicksand]], making it difficult to judge their position and maneuver around them. When they are above the ground, however, Link is able to destroy them permanently using Bombs. Switching to [[Wolf Link]] and activating its [[Sense]]s will cause a ghostly afterimage of any floor spikes that emerge to remain after they retract, eliminating the need to memorize their locations so long as Senses are activated.

==={{PH|-}}===
Floor Spikes appear inside [[dungeon]]s in {{PH}}, where they serve to impede [[Link]]'s progress and will damage him on contact. Link can usually deactivate them by defeating enemies and solving puzzles. [[Phantom]]s can move over Floor Spikes without taking damage. Some Floor Spikes move up and down intermittently, forcing Link to pass over them quickly while they are briefly inactive.

==={{ST|-}}===
Floor Spikes appear inside [[dungeon]]s in {{ST}}, where they serve to impede [[Link]]'s progress and will damage him on contact. Link can usually deactivate them by defeating enemies and solving puzzles. As [[Phantom]]s are unaffected by Floor Spikes, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can also harness this immunity when she gains the ability to possess Phantoms, and use it to cross over the Floor Spikes and help solve puzzles normally out of Link's reach.

==={{SS|-}}===
Floor Spikes appear within a single room inside the [[Lanayru Mining Facility]] in {{SS}}, where they are stealthily concealed by large amounts of sand. They activate quickly when [[Link]] draws near. This requires Link to work his way through the maze-like room by using the [[Gust Bellows]] to blow away the sand and find a safe path.

==={{TFH|-}}===
Floor Spikes appear in the [[Frozen Plateau]] level of the [[Drablands]] in {{TFH}}. Here, fireballs can be bounced across them using the [[Fire Gloves]], which are used to light [[Torch]]es and defeat enemies that are otherwise out of reach.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
Floor Spikes are typically found within [[Ancient Shrine]]s.
