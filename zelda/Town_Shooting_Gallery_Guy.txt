{{Term/Store|Town Shooting Gallery Guy||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref name="E 245"/>

==Biography==
The {{Term|MM3D|Town Shooting Gallery Guy}} runs the {{Term|MM3D|Town Shooting Gallery|link}}. He will not let {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} play if he is wearing a {{Term|MM3D|Mask|link}} that interferes with his vision.<ref>{{Cite|You can't aim for the targets when you're wearin' a mask, sonny. Take it off.|Town Shooting Gallery Guy|MM}}</ref> He can play while wearing the {{Term|MM3D|Bunny Hood|link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Postman's Hat|link}}, however. He does not have a unique reaction to many {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}}, but he will be startled by the {{Term|MM3D|Captain's Hat|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Eeesh! You can't aim for the targets wearin' somethin' like that monstrosity on your head, sonny. Take it off.|Town Shooting Gallery Guy|MM}}</ref> be fooled by the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Mask|link}},<ref>{{Cite|.........|Town Shooting Gallery Guy|MM}}</ref> and report that {{Term|MM3D|Kafei|link}} has not been in to play if {{Term|MM3D|Link}} wears {{Term|MM3D|Kafei's Mask|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, Kafei, eh? He hasn't come here.|Town Shooting Gallery Guy|MM}}</ref>

The {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}} apparently caused some trouble for the {{Term|MM3D|Town Shooting Gallery Guy}} in the past, as evidenced by his reaction to {{Term|MM3D|Tatl|link}} if {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is in his {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link|display=Deku}} form.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! When I saw that fairy, I thought you were that troublemakin' brat with the mask.|Town Shooting Gallery Guy|MM}}</ref> He also seems to recognize {{Term|MM3D|Link}} in his {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link|link|display=Zora}} form, though he cannot quite recall that he is thinking of {{Term|MM3D|The Indigo-Go's|link}}' guitarist, {{Term|MM3D|Mikau|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I swear I've seen you somewhere before...|Town Shooting Gallery Guy|MM}}</ref> And though he cannot play without a {{Term|MM3D|Bow|link}}, he will compliment {{Term|MM3D|Goron Link|link}} on his build.<ref>{{Cite|Well, look at you! You've got quite a build! I betcha can lift anything! But... Sorry...If you don't have a bow, you can't play.|Town Shooting Gallery Guy|MM}}</ref>
