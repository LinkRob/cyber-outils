{{Term/Store|Sakon||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
In {{MM}}, Sakon is a thief who has a [[Sakon's Hideout|Hideout]] situated in [[Ikana Canyon]].<ref>{{Cite|Sakon's Hideout. Protected by impenetrable security!|N/A|MM}}</ref> His hideout is protected by a huge [[Boulder]] which can only be opened by Sakon himself.<ref>{{Cite|Only Sakon can open it. The only way in is to wait for Sakon to arrive.|Kafei|MM}}</ref>
[[File:MM Sakon killed by Link.gif|thumb|269x269px|The possible event of Link using his [[Bow]] to shoot the stolen Bomb Bag carried by Sakon in ''Majora's Mask'', exploding into thin air]]
He can be seen in [[North Clock Town]] in the night of the [[First Day]] attempting to steal a [[Bomb Bag]] from the [[Old Lady from Bomb Shop|old lady]] of the [[Bomb Shop]] at around 12:15 AM (the lady herself appears at midnight).<ref>{{Cite|Stop! Thief!!! Give the old lady her luggage back!!!|Old Lady from Bomb Shop|MM}}</ref> As this occurs, Sakon will be running in various directions until he reaches the gate leading to [[Termina Field]]. [[Link]] can retrieve the stolen bag by striking Sakon with his [[Sword]] where he will drop it but continue to run away, which will guarantee the [[Blast Mask]] for Link from the old lady (she will simply give thanks if already obtained). If the young [[Hero of Time]] instead attacks Sakon with an [[Arrow]], the [[Hookshot]], or another [[Bomb]] (including the Blast Mask), the bag will explode, blowing the thief up and killing him instantly, therefore failing the objective of retrieving it back. If Link allows him to escape, the [[Large Bomb Bag|Big Bomb Bag]] will be sold at the [[Curiosity Shop]] the next night.<ref>{{Cite|Tonight's special bargain was just stolen. It just came in seconds ago. This is really hot! I kid you not! Actually, it's somethin' sold over at the Bomb Shop, but a thief sold it to me...|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref> Sakon also steals the [[Sun Mask]] from [[Kafei]].<ref>{{Cite|When I was turned into this, I went to see the Great Fairy in the shrine near the North Gate... But on the way, my precious mask--a wedding ceremony mask--was stolen from me by some prancing man with a grinning face!|Kafei|MM}}</ref> Link can help Kafei recover his mask during the [[Final Day]] by infiltrating Sakon's Hideout.

He can also be seen in the lower valley of the Ikana Canyon running in circles. There, he will try to steal Link's Sword if talked to,<ref>{{Cite|Did I mention that is one niiiiice sword? Can I see it for a second?|Sakon|MM}}</ref> but will be stopped by [[Tatl]].<ref>{{Cite|Wha? S-s-some red thing?!? What is it? It s-s-seems hostile!|Sakon|MM}}</ref> Sakon can sometimes be seen outside the Curiosity Shop at night, where he talks about how the [[Man from Curiosity Shop]] is unfair, and that he just wants his fair share.<ref>{{Cite|This Curiosity Shop guy's takin' advantage of me...I just want my fair share...|Sakon|MM}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* He is the [[Termina|Terminan]] counterpart of the [[Man Carrying Sack]] character seen in the [[Hyrule Castle Town|Hyrule Castle Town Market]] during [[Link]]'s childhood in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''.
* Although technically a minor antagonist, Sakon is the first character in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] able to be killed by Link that is not a [[Enemy|common enemy]] or story-related villain (this case, [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora]]). Because of this, it has become an infamous meme among fans who played ''Majora's Mask'' or its [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|remake]].
* The cutscene of Sakon stealing the bomb bag from the [[Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|old lady]] is at real-time, meaning it will go very slowly had Link played the [[Inverted Song of Time]] or quickly in the initial three-day cycle until it ends at 12:30 AM.
* If Link were to make Sakon blow up while doing the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest, its mission will ultimately fail, as [[Kafei]] will be unable to enter the thief's Hideout to retrieve his Sun's Mask.
* The scene of Sakon running with the old lady's bomb bag is one of the few in ''Majora's Mask'' to play the "chase theme" from the beginning of the game by [[Skull Kid]] stealing [[Epona]] from Link, alongside stopping [[Captain Keeta]] in [[Ikana Graveyard]].
