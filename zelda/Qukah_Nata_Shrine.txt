The {{Term/Store|Qukah Nata Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Qukah Nata Shrine}}<br/>Qukah Nata's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Qukah Nata's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Qukah Nata Shrine}} is located atop a hill on the southeast edge of {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. To access it, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use the {{Term|BotW|Zora Armor|link}} to quickly ascend the nearby {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is initially hidden within a {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound|link}}, and the area surrounding {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake}} will constantly be under the effect of a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} can be found on the shore of {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake}}, playing his bandoleon. When spoken to, he will sing an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" from the area for {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which hints that {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} should be called down from sky.<ref>{{Cite|When a lost hero calls down {{Color|BotW Red|lightning}} from the sky / The monk responds from a {{Color|BotW Red|giant mound}} on high.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|A Song of Storms|link}}".

In order to break the {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound}} encasing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, it must be struck with {{Term|BotW|Lightning}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can cause this to happen either by equipping a metal {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} and getting struck while on top of the {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound|display=Mound}} or by leaving a metal object to get struck by {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} on top of it. A direct {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} strike to the {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound}} will cause it to shatter, revealing the {{Term|BotW|Qukah Nata Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|"When a lost hero calls down lightning from the sky / The monk responds from a giant mound on high."<br/><br/>You found an ancient shrine inside the rock that you shattered with lightning!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Qukah Nata Shrine}}, he will be greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Qukah Nata|link}}, who states that he has already proven himself by arriving.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this Shrine... I am Qukah Nata. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Qukah Nata|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The interior of {{Term|BotW|Qukah Nata Shrine}} features no Trial, just a straight path to {{Term|BotW|Qukah Nata}}'s {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Term|BotW|Rubber Tights|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, he will be granted a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for arriving at the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Qukah Nata|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Qukah Nata}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Qukah Nata|BotW}}</ref>

After completing this {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, the {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm}} surrounding {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake}} will subside.
