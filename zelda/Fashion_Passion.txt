{{Term/Store|Fashion Passion||Series, BotW}} is an [[Armor Shop]] in [[Gerudo Town]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fashion Passion|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Fashion Passion is an [[Armor Shop]] managed by [[Saula]] that sells [[Gerudo]] clothing. The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} is sold here at a total of 540 [[Rupee]]s, which is 60 Rupees cheaper than what {{Term|BotW|Vilia|link}} sold them to [[Link]] for.<ref>{{Cite|What do you think? Do you want to buy them for...{{Color|BotW Red|600 rupees}}?|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}} here is also colored with [[White Dye]], as opposed to the blue variation that Vilia sells to Link. Saula sells the white {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}} because of how trendy it is to change the color of clothing,<ref>{{Cite|Changing the color of your clothes is a really trendy thing to do right now for young vai traveling beyond town!|Saula|BotW}}</ref> explaining that the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] in [[Hateno Village]] will dye clothing.<ref>{{Cite|There's a store in Hateno Village—the {{Color|BotW Blue|Kochi Dye Shop}}—where you can dye your clothes!|Saula|BotW}}</ref> Saula urges Link to try a different-colored outfit and buy the white {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|}}.<ref>{{Cite|Geez, it looks really good on you! How about one more to try in a {{Color|BotW Blue|different color}}! You've got to buy it!|Saula|BotW}}</ref>

Through the doorway behind Fashion Passion is a curtain that conceals an entrance to the [[Gerudo Secret Club]]. If Link attempts to enter, Saula stops him, telling him that the doorway is not for customers.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, um, that doorway isn't for customers, actually...|Saula|BotW}}</ref> Saula then whispers to Link and tells him to use the door on the side of the building if he is a member.<ref>{{Cite|<small>If you're a member, please head around to the door on the side.</small>|Saula|BotW}}</ref> Link can complete "[[The Secret Club's Secret]]" [[Side Quest]] to find out the password to access the Gerudo Secret Club from there.
