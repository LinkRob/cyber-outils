"{{Term/Store|The Cursed Statue||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Cursed Statue}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Fort Hateno}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Calip|link}} reciting a text to himself,<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|"When a dark light resides in the cursed statue's eyes, pierce its gaze to purge the seal from the shrine."}}|Calip|BotW}}</ref> the subject of which he believes is nearby.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I'm not wrong, am I? It must be around there...}}|Calip|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what he is doing, {{Term|BotW|Calip}} instead assumes that he is here to learn about his research.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you up to?] HA! Let me guess... You caught wind of the great Dr. Calip's groundbreaking research and just HAD to meet him for yourself. Heh...|Calip|BotW}}</ref> However, he becomes dejected when he finds out that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has not heard of him, even after all of the research he has done on {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|No? Don't tell me you've never even HEARD of me! After I've dedicated my life to researching the ancient shrines?!|Calip|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to remember his name, as he will soon be famous.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* Well, you'd better remember my name, for it's not the last you'll hear of it!|Calip|BotW}}</ref> He also insists he be called {{Term|BotW|Calip|display=Dr. Calip}},<ref>{{Cite|And while I'm at it, it's "DOCTOR Calip," if you please. I didn't study my rear end off to be called "Mr. Calip."|Calip|BotW}}</ref> since people always seem to forget he is a doctor.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Everyone forgets I'm a doctor for some reason...}}|Calip|BotW}}</ref>

As he is studying ancient texts, {{Term|BotW|Calip}} has no time to chat with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And since you asked, I'll inform you that I'm engaged in analyzing ancient texts. I haven't time for idle chitchat.|Calip|BotW}}</ref> He believes that the culmination of his research will be the discovery of the century,<ref>{{Cite|[Ancient texts?] Soon, my long years of research will be revealed...and the world will hail it as the discovery of the century!|Calip|BotW}}</ref> and so to protect his work, he has not even told his family about it.<ref>{{Cite|To prevent any undue attention, I haven't even told my family about my work. You understand, I'm sure.|Calip|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} calls him doctor, however, {{Term|BotW|Calip}} will become excited.<ref>{{Cite|[Of course, Dr. Calip.] {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Doctor...}}}} Did I...just hear you call me doctor? As in, not merely Calip, but DOCTOR Calip? It rolled off your tongue so naturally. It was downright salivary!|Calip|BotW}}</ref> In light of this, {{Term|BotW|Calip}} decides to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} some of his research in confidence.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... This changes things... Very well then! I'm feeling charitable, so I'll let a few things spill. This is just between us, understand.|Calip|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Calip}} shares the {{Term|BotW|An Ancient Text|link|display=''Ancient Text''}} with {{Term|BotW|Link}}, which states one must pierce the gaze of a cursed {{Term|BotW|Stone Statue (Object)|link}} to reveal an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|"When a dark light resides in the cursed statue's eyes, pierce its gaze to purge the seal from the shrine."|Calip|BotW}}</ref> Though he is still deciphering it, he sees no harm in sharing it with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm still in the process of deciphering that one, so I don't suppose there's any harm in sharing it with you.|Calip|BotW}}</ref> His life has become a pattern of researching {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} during the day and studying texts at night.<ref>{{Cite|That's my life in a nutshell. Days filled with researching shrines and nights spent studying ancient texts.|Calip|BotW}}</ref> He has no more time to spend talking to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, so he bids him farewell.<ref>{{Cite|That leaves no time to converse with passing strangers, so farewell.|Calip|BotW}}</ref> When he finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

During the day, {{Term|BotW|Calip}} can be found studying a site filled with {{Plural|BotW|Stone Statue (Object)}} northeast of his cabin. At 9:00 PM, the eyes of one of the {{Term|BotW|Stone Statue (Object)|display=Statues}} will begin to glow a purple light. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can shoot it with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}, which will cause {{Term|BotW|Kam Urog Shrine|link}} to rise from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|You fired an arrow into the glowing eyes of the statue, and the ancient shrine was revealed.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
