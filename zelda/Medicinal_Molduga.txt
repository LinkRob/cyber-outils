"{{Term/Store|Medicinal Molduga||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Medicinal Molduga}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|Medicinal Molduga}}" is one of four {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} that must be completed in order to receive the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} as part of "{{Term|BotW|The Thunder Helm|link}}". {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain it by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Malena|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}}, who will be begging {{Term|BotW|Babi|link}} for help.<ref>{{Cite|Please...|Malena|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Malena}} explains that she recently married the man of her dreams,<ref>{{Cite|I... I finally found the voe of my dreams, and I just recently married him... I thought I had finally found happiness...|Malena|BotW}}</ref> but he became sick with a rare illness.<ref>{{Cite|But then...my husband fell ill with a rare disease... One difficult to cure.|Malena|BotW}}</ref> To heal her husband, {{Term|BotW|Malena}} requires {{Term|BotW|Molduga Guts|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The only thing that can save him is the {{Color|BotW Blue|guts of a Molduga}}...|Malena|BotW}}</ref> though she is unable to defeat a {{Term|BotW|Molduga|link}} to obtain any.<ref>{{Cite|But I would never be able to take down such a creature myself...|Malena|BotW}}</ref> She tried to ask for help from the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldiers, but she realizes it is foolish to expect anyone to face such a dangerous monster on her behalf.<ref>{{Cite|I asked the soldiers for help, but... Why would they want to help me? The {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}} is a menacing beast... None who face one escape unscathed... So perhaps it's foolish to expect anyone to stick their neck out for a stranger...|Malena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malena}} is ashamed to have had to return to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to seek help.<ref>{{Cite|I thought I'd finally gotten out of this town... Never thought I'd have to come crawling back...|Malena|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Plural|BotW|Molduga}}, {{Term|BotW|Malena}} describes them as savage creatures that are avoided by other monsters, often found in the {{Term|BotW|Toruma Dunes|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Southern Oasis|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Molduga?] The {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}} is a monster... A terror so savage that even other monsters won't approach it... It's often been spotted west of here, in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Toruma Dunes}}, and in the south.|Malena|BotW}}</ref> In addition, she shares that they are sensitive to vibrations and attack anything that comes close to them.<ref>{{Cite|It's very {{Color|BotW Red|sensitive to vibrations}}, and it tends to attack anything that draws near.|Malena|BotW}}</ref> Since they can not be approached nor can anyone be used as bait, {{Term|BotW|Malena}} wonders what she is to do.<ref>{{Cite|You can't approach it or even move near it... And you can't use someone as bait... So what am I to do...|Malena|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Molduga}} can be found in four different places in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. These locations are the {{Term|BotW|Toruma Dunes}}, the {{Term|BotW|Southern Oasis}}, {{Term|BotW|North Dragon's Exile|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Arbiter's Grounds|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Molduga}} can be fought while {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} with the help of a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}}. They can also be fought by detonating {{Plural|BotW|Bomb|link}} or shooting {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} near it. Either one of these strategies creates enough vibration to cause a {{Term|BotW|Molduga}} to fly out of the sand, where it can be stunned and become vulnerable to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s attacks.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeats a {{Term|BotW|Molduga}} and obtains {{Term|BotW|Molduga Guts}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to give them to {{Term|BotW|Malena}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Here, take 'em.] Wait, these... {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga guts}}! {{Big|You... You'll give these to me?}}|Malena|BotW}}</ref> She thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} by rewarding him with a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}, as it is all she has to give him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Oh, sarqso!}} This is all I have to thank you with, but I insist you take it.|Malena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malena}} is brought to tears at the thought of saving her husband,<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|This will save him... *sniff*}}|Malena|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
