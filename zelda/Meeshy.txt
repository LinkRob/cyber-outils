{{Term/Store|Meeshy||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|Meeshy|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} adventurer. She travels on the {{Term|BotW|Faron Highroad|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} is a novice adventurer, so she is careful when it comes to pushing herself too hard.<ref>{{Cite|Unline you, I'm a newcomer to adventuring! I don't want to push myself too hard.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> Much of her information about the surrounding area comes from other adventurers or studying her {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm still a novice when it comes to adventuring, but I do hear lots of stories from adventurers at the stable.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I heard this tidbit from another adventurer, and I just couldn't wait to share it!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...So Lake Hylia is northwest of here, and then east of that should be the Faron Woods. I've never been there... I've only ever seen the location on map. I suppose you could say I'm more of an adventuring fan than an actual adventurer...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...So Lake Hylia is northwest of here, and east of that should be Faron Woods.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> During her journey, she will often refer to her {{Term|BotW|Map}} for guidance.<ref>{{Cite|According to the map...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> If it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=rains}}, she will be worried that her {{Term|BotW|Map}} will get wet.<ref>{{Cite|My map's gonna get wet!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> At night, she looks out for {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A shooting star at night...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref>

As she travels, {{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} will occasionally mention the {{Term|BotW|Faron Woods|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. She advises {{Term|BotW|Link}} to reference his {{Term|BotW|Map}} if he journeys through the {{Term|BotW|Faron Woods}}, since they can be "tricky" to navigate.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Faron Woods}} are a little tricky, but as long as you keep one eye on your map, you should be OK. As for me, I'll just look at the map and stay put. Can't get much safer than that!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Faron Woods}} is tricky, but with a map close at hand, you should be fine!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> She has also heard of {{Term|BotW|Dracozu Lake|link}}, which she refers to as "Mouth of the Dragon."<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Faron Woods}} is really, really thick... I've also heard of a lake there called... "Mouth of the Dragon" or something...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Faron Woods}} is a densely wooded area... There's a lake there called "Mouth of the Dragon," or something...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> In addition, she considers the ancient {{Term|BotW|Zonai Ruins|link}} to be romantic, even though she would never visit them.<ref>{{Cite|I'm more of an adventuring fan than an actual adventurer... You know, there are ancient ruins hidden away in Faron Woods. I would never go, but overgrown ruins sound so...romantic!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There are ancient ruins hidden away in the Faron Woods. I'd never go there, but they sound...romantic!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> Finally, {{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} shares rumors that the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage|link}} is located within the {{Term|BotW|Faron Woods}}, but she believes visiting it would be too risky for someone of her skill level.<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of adventuring, have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Courage}} in the Faron Woods? I don't know anything about the area, so I don't think I'll go there... Too risky for a novice like me.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Courage}}? It's supposed to be in Faron Woods. I have no idea what's in those woods, though, so I'm definitely not going there.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref>

As she approaches {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} expresses an interest in the {{Term|BotW|Floria Falls|link}} that flow into {{Term|BotW|Lake Floria|link}}, and she likes the idea of going behind a {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Like...{{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Floria}} has several waterfalls in the area! You know...I've always liked the idea of going behind a waterfall.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Floria}} has many waterfalls. I want to go behind one...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} is at {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable}}, she informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he could continue following the {{Term|BotW|Faron Highroad}} east to {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Starting from here at Lakeside Stable, if you go further east, you'll come to a fishing village called Lurelin Village.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you go further east from here, you'll eventually reach a fishing town called Lurelin Village.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> However, she warns him that he would be at risk of {{Term|BotW|Monster}} attacks if he went this way.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, it'd be awful if I left out the part about how you'll definitely be attacked by monsters along the way.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> When she leaves {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} shares that her plan is to go to {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable}} through the {{Term|BotW|Faron Woods}}.<ref>{{Cite|I plan to head for {{Color|BotW Blue|Highland Stable}}. I'm still a novice adventurer, so I'm really excited!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I plan to go to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Highland Stabke}} via Faron Woods in the west. I'm still a novice adventurer, so I'm really excited!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} claims to have had bad encounters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} in the past, stating that they seem to be drawn to her.<ref>{{Cite|For some reason, I always seem to attract monsters...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You know how some people just seem to attract mosquitoes? Well, that's how I am with monsters, it seems...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> As she approaches {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable}}, she will begin running to avoid the {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} in the area.<ref>{{Cite|And it looks like there are a lot of monsters near the stable too. My only hope is to try and dash past them.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There are a lot of monsters near the stable. They're really scary, so I'm going to try and just dash past them!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> Near {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable}}, she claims she was so scared of these {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} that she sees them in her dreams.<ref>{{Cite|The monsters that attacked me near the Highland Stable were so scary... I still sometimes see them in my dreams!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref>

When she is attacked by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} will run away in fear.<ref>{{Cite|Oh nooooo!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yikes! Help!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Somebody, please!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her during this time, she will wonder what she should do and wish the {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} would disappear.<ref>{{Cite|What should we do...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I wish the monsters would just go away...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeats the {{Term|BotW|Enemy}}, she will be relieved.<ref>{{Cite|Phew...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hoh...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That was close...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> She tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} how scared she was, and she is grateful that he was there to help her.<ref>{{Cite|That was really scary... I'm glad you were around.|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, that was scary... But everything is gonna be all right now, right?|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The monsters... Where are the... Oh, I'm saved! Thank you!|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, {{Term|BotW|Meeshy}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here, take this in thanks...|Meeshy|BotW}}</ref>
