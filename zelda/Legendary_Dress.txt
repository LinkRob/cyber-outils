The {{Term/Store|Legendary Dress||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 129}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Legendary Dress makes those darling little hearts appear more often. No need to thank me, dahling!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Legendary Dress}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} can commission from {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|link}} at her shop, {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}, in exchange for one {{Term|TFH|Tektite Shell|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Sweet Shroom|link}}, and 200 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. When worn, the {{Term|TFH|Legendary Dress}} increases the chances for {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} to appear.<ref>{{Cite|Royal yet humble. Increases the drop rate of hearts.|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> When {{Term|TFH|Link}} dons the {{Term|TFH|Legendary Dress}} in {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}}'s dressing room, the {{Term|TFH|Stylish Woman|link}} will compliment the elegance of the {{Term|TFH|Legendary Dress|display=Dress}}, saying that he is giving her "a run for [her] Rupees."<ref>{{Cite|Ooh, so elegant! You're giving me a run for my Rupees...|Stylish Woman|TFH}}</ref>

The {{Term|TFH|Legendary Dress}} is directly based on the dress worn by {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda|link}} in {{ALBW}}.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|TFH|Legendary Dress}} was known as the '''Zelda Dress''' in the E3 2015 demo. The reason for this change is unknown.
