{{Term/Store|Monster Cake|Monster Cakes|Series, BotW, TotK, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Monster Cake}}<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Monster Cake}}|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Monster Cake}} are a variety of {{Term|BotW|Cake|link}} made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Monster Extract|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Being made with {{Term|BotW|Monster Extract}}, the amount of {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} they heal to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} will be random when they are made. The {{Term|BotW|Monster Extract}} will also randomize the strength and duration of any status effects the {{Term|BotW|Cake}} has. It is said that once one has a taste, they will never forget the sweetness of {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

The second {{Term|BotW|Castle Library Book|link}} details the recipe for {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake}}, describing it as a secret recipe of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} and the favorite of an unnamed chancellor.<ref>{{Cite|The Royal Family's Secret Recipe #2<br/>The Chancellor's Favorite: {{Color|BotW Red|Monster Cake}}|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref> The book instructs its reader to stew {{Term|BotW|Monster Extract}}, {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar}}, and {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter}} together for a short while.<ref>{{Cite|Stew monster extract, Tabantha wheat, cane sugar, and goat butter for a spell.|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref> However, it warns that these {{Plural|BotW|Cake}} make one's head fuzzy and may motivate someone to plan evil schemes.<ref>{{Cite|It's a dangerous dish that makes your head fuzzy and may even motivate you to plan evil schemes!|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Monster Cake}} is one of two meals {{Term|BotW|Link}} can give to {{Term|BotW|Gotter|link}} as part of the "{{Term|BotW|A Royal Recipe|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Give him monster cake.] This monster cake is said to have been a favorite of the minister of Hyrule.|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> At first, he likens the {{Term|BotW|Cake}} to biting into a {{Term|BotW|Chuchu|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It tastes...beautiful... How can I put it into words? It's like biting into a Chuchu...|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> However, realizing that would be disgusting and dangerous, he instead claims it stimulates the brain like being pummeled by a {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|No, no, that would be disgusting and dangerous. It stimulates the brain, much like being pummeled by a Hinox!|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> He calls it an elegant dish, saying he never thought he would taste something so thrilling.<ref>{{Cite|A truly elegant dish! I never thought I would taste something quite so thrilling.|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> As thanks, {{Term|BotW|Gotter}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I really must thank you for such a beautiful meal! Here, take this. For your trouble.|Gotter|BotW}}</ref>

Once the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can eavesdrop on {{Term|BotW|Ruli|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hagie|link}} during the night in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} laments that their daughter, {{Term|BotW|Hunnie|link}}, is sick and will only eat {{Term|BotW|Cake}}, and she wonders whether anything can be done.<ref>{{Cite|Our daughter... The only thing she'll eat is {{Color|BotW Red|cake}}. Can't anything be done?|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> No matter how much searching they have done, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} notes they have not been able to find a {{Term|BotW|Cake}} that can please her.<ref>{{Cite|We've scoured the world, searching for a cake that would please her. None have. Imagine how much money we've spent...|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} states that she would sell her soul to {{Term|BotW|Monster|link}} for a {{Term|BotW|Cake}} to cure {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}}.<ref>{{Cite|Even if I have to sell my soul to a {{Color|BotW Red|monster}}, I'll get my hands on a {{Color|BotW Red|cake}} that can cure our daughter!|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|A Parent's Love|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

During this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can give {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} a {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake}} for {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}}.<ref>{{Cite|What is... Is this a {{Color|BotW Blue|monster cake}}? I should give this to my daughter?|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> She thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for the gift, noting its pungent smell.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much! Oh, wow. It's certainly quite pungent... Phew.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> However, she decides not to complain, since this could be the {{Term|BotW|Cake}} that heals {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}}.<ref>{{Cite|I suppose I can't complain about things like that. If our daughter can get better thanks to this cake...|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} decides to have her daughter eat it right away.<ref>{{Cite|I'll wake her up right away and try to get her to eat it.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll try to get her to eat it right away.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> After this is done, {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} will begin to run around and play. {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}}, noting that {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}}'s health has returned.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much! That {{Color|BotW Blue|monster cake}}... Our daughter wolfed it down with unbelievable gusto. It seems her health has returned to her!|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know what to say—I'm just so thankful! Oh, I know. This isn't much, but please take it.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref>

By adding a fifth ingredient, several different variations of {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake}} can be made:

* '''Hearty Monster Cake''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Monster Cake}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hearty ingredient. When eaten, all of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} will be healed, and his maximum number of {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} will be temporarily increased.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Monster Cake''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Monster Cake}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an energizing ingredient. Eating these {{Plural|BotW|Cake}} will restore a portion of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Monster Cake''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Monster Cake}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an enduring ingredient. {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel}} will be completely restored and overfilled if he eats these.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Monster Cake''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Monster Cake}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hasty ingredient. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} eats these, his movement speed will be increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Monster Cake''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Monster Cake}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a spicy ingredient. These {{Plural|BotW|Cake}} will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}} when eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Monster Cake''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Monster Cake}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an electric ingredient. Eating them will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Monster Cake''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Monster Cake}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a mighty ingredient. When eaten, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Monster Cake''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Monster Cake}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a tough ingredient. If consumed, {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s defense will be increased.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Monster Cake''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Monster Cake}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a sneaky ingredient. They increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} when he eats them.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Monster Cake''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Monster Cake}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>It's said that once you have a taste of this cake, you'll never forget its sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a chilly ingredient. Eating them will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>
