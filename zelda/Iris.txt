{{Term/Store|Iris||Series, FSA}} is a novice magician in {{FSA}}. While she is capable of magic, she has yet to memorize spells and as such cannot do much without her [[Magic Book]]. Possibly because she's only a novice, Iris is not mean like the other [[mage]]s and does not wish for harm to befall others, but she intends to correct this.<ref>{{Cite|This is a one-time deal, let me tell you! I'm not supposed to do good deeds!|Iris|FSA}}</ref>

==Biography==
Iris was practicing her magic in the [[Village of the Blue Maiden]] when she teleported herself to the [[Dark World]], accidentally leaving her [[Magic Book]] behind in the [[Light World]]. As such, she had no way back home, leaving her little to do but try spells from memory in the hopes of being teleported back. The only result yielded by this method was the summoning of a tiny house.<ref>{{Cite|My name's Iris! I'm a novice magician. I lost my magic book, and now I don't have the magic to get back [[Light World|home]]. I tried some spells from memory, and I got this tiny house to appear... But it's so frustrating! Where in the world could my book have gone?|Iris|FSA}}</ref> Yet while Iris thought she had "just magicked it out of nowhere",<ref name="Shallabazoo">{{Cite|...Say, what's that? It's my book!!! Well, I'll be! Pretty impressive for such a little fella. ... ... ... Hm? The guy who owns this house is upset? Oh! I didn't think anyone owned it! I thought I'd just magicked it out of nowhere! I'd better return it right away. Here, check this out! As long as I have this book, I can do anything! I really must return this. Abra this, hocus that, shallabazoo! Boo!|Iris|FSA}}</ref> truth was that she had stolen the house of {{Term|FSA|Man Who Lost His House|link|display=someone}} back in the Light World, along with that {{Term|FSA|Sweetie|link|display=person's girlfriend}} inside.<ref>{{Cite|My girl and I were fighting. I took off, and when I came back, my house was gone! I went to her place, but all I found was this strange mage and a Seeker. Where's my house? Where's my girlfriend!?! Ah...I've lost everything... What am I supposed to do now?|Boyfriend in Village of the Blue Maiden|FSA}}</ref> Also in the Light World, Iris' disappearance had not gone unnoticed. [[Mage|Her teacher]] found her book lying unguarded and instructed the [[Seeker's Guild]] to search for her.<ref name="book"/><ref>{{Cite|Hmmm... We've looked all over, but we just can't find that mage's missing student. How did she vanish? Where did she go?|Secret Agent Two|FSA}}</ref>

No progress was made until [[Link]] became involved with the guild. He visited Iris in the Dark World and later met her teacher in the Light World, who gave him Iris' Magic Book to bring to her. Thrilled to hold her possession again, Iris sent the house and the girlfriend back to the Light World.<ref name="Shallabazoo"/><ref>{{Cite|That should do it! Look back in [[Light World|your world]]. Then, you'll understand the magnificence of my magic!|Iris|FSA}}</ref>

As promised by her teacher,<ref>{{Cite|When that wastrel Iris gets back, she needs a harsh scolding!|Iris' teacher|FSA}}</ref> Iris was given a thorough lecture on her losing her magic book. She also appeared among the grateful villagers congratulating Link after his victory over several [[Dark Link|Shadow Links]].<ref>{{Cite|I was able to return safely thanks to you guys bringing me my book! Of course, my teacher gave me quite an earful for misplacing it...|Iris|FSA}}</ref>
