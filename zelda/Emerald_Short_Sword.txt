==Location and Uses==
Emerald Short Swords are {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can deal 1 damage to a single enemy, along with poisoning them temporarily. Since they're a type of {{Term|CoH|Short Sword|link}}, they can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}. They can only be acquired either after he infuses the Short Sword with an {{Term|CoH|Emerald|link}} at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, or if the blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an already-infused one in the {{Term|CoH|Lost Swamp|link}} is opened by Link.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Emerald Dagger|link}}.
