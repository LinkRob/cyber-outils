{{Term/Store|Merina||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Merina|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Merina}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who guards the front entrance of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Dorrah|link}}. She wields a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} without a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|display=disguise|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} and {{Term|BotW|Merina}} will stop him from passing through.<ref>{{Cite|Freeze! Where do you think you're going?! Not into town—that's for sure! No voe allowed. Off with you!|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Merina}}, she notices that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a "voe," or a man, and warns him that men are not allowed into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'otta. Wait a... You're a {{Color|BotW Blue|voe}}! {{Color|BotW Blue|Voe}} are forbidden here in {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}. Only {{Color|BotW Blue|vai}} are allowed inside!|Merina|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her again, {{Term|BotW|Merina}} again scolds him and reiterates that only "vai," or women, are allowed.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Voe}}! Are you still here?! I told you, only {{Color|BotW Blue|vai}} are allowed inside!|Merina|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes speaking to her, she exclaims that all men must leave at once.<ref>{{Cite|All {{Color|BotW Blue|voe}} must leave at once!|Merina|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what "voe" means, {{Term|BotW|Merina}} explains that it is the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)|link}} word for "man."<ref>{{Cite|[Voe?] When I say "{{Color|BotW Blue|voe}}," I mean "man," obviously!|Merina|BotW}}</ref> Similarly, she reveals that "vai" in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)}} word for "woman."<ref>{{Cite|[Vai?] When I say "{{Color|BotW Blue|vai}}," I mean "woman," obviously!|Merina|BotW}}</ref> After either of these explanations, she will find it ridiculous that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is trying to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} without knowing the difference between "voe" and "vai."<ref>{{Cite|You don't even know the difference between {{Color|BotW Blue|voe}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|vai}}, and you're trying to enter town?!|Merina|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Merina}} while wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}} on, she comments that they do not see many {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} women around and welcomes him to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|We don't get a lot of Hylian {{Color|BotW Blue|vai}} around here. Welcome to Gerudo Town.|Merina|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Merina}} also explains that only women are allowed inside {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}} walls, and she invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take his time there.<ref>{{Cite|It is an old tradition here that only {{Color|BotW Blue|vai}} are allowed within the walls. {{Color|BotW Blue|Voe}} are strictly forbidden. Take your time here.|Merina|BotW}}</ref> In this scenario, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the meaning of "voe" or "vai," {{Term|BotW|Merina}} will politely teach him.<ref>{{Cite|[Voe?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Voe}} is a word in the Gerudo language that means "man."|Merina|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Vai?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Vai}} is a word in the Gerudo language that means "woman."|Merina|BotW}}</ref> She recommends that {{Term|BotW|Link}} learn the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)}} language if he plans on entering {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|There are many other Gerudo words, and it would help to learn them if you plan on entering Gerudo Town.|Merina|BotW}}</ref> Before finishing speaking, {{Term|BotW|Merina}} also recommends that {{Term|BotW|Link}} order a {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit|link}} at {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}}, calling it the best drink she has ever had.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a tip: if you stop by the canteen, do yourself a favor and order a {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}. It's hands down the best drink I've ever had.|Merina|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her again, she vows to keep all voe out of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|I will not let a single {{Color|BotW Blue|voe}} pass, even if it was a mere ant. But you are fine. Please relax and enjoy your time here.|Merina|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Merina}} after calming the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, she is grateful that {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} has brought peace back to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Our chief has finally done it. It's been a long time since I've seen the desert as peaceful as this...|Merina|BotW}}</ref> Due to this, she is excited to return to living her life.<ref>{{Cite|And now that I've refreshed myself, I can really buckle down and get back to living my life!|Merina|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* {{Term|Series|Merina}} is one of several characters that will hit {{Term|Series|Link}} back if he attacks them with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got some nerve!|Merina|BotW}}</ref> These attacks deal no damage.
