The {{Term/Store|Man of Smiles||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Man of Smiles owns a boat that travels in the northeastern corner of the [[World of the Ocean King|sea]]. Upon visiting his boat, the door will close behind [[Link]], and several enemies will appear. Defeating all of the enemies will cause the door to open and the Man of Smiles to enter. The first time Link visits his boat, the Man of Smiles gives him the [[Hero's New Clothes]], the first item of the game's [[Phantom Hourglass Trading Sequence|Trading Sequence]]. If Link visits him a second time, he will give Link the [[Prize Postcard]]. Link is able to do this indefinitely.
