"{{Term/Store|Take Back the Sea||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Take Back the Sea}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Lurelin Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet the fisherman {{Term|BotW|Sebasto|link}}, who will share with him the town's troubles.<ref>{{Cite|You a traveler?<br/>I'm a fisherman of this village, but we're in a bit of a bind lately.|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref> The best fishing spots have been taken over by [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]],<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Monsters}} have taken over the best fishing spots.|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref> and he specifically points out how {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach|link}} has been infested.<ref>{{Cite|You used to be able to pull in a good haul at {{Color|BotW Blue|Aris Beach}}, but these days you'd be lucky to leave there with your life.|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears tough, {{Term|BotW|Sebasto}} asks him to defeat the monsters on the {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach|display=Beach}}.<ref>{{Cite|You look pretty tough.<br/>Think you could go take care of them?|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

The encampment on {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach}} houses {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} archers, who will alert the rest of the monsters if they spot {{Term|BotW|Link}}. The main structure is inhabited by a variety of {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}, as well as one {{Term|BotW|Moblin|link}}, all of which must be defeated. After all of the monsters have been dispatched, the camp's {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} will be unlocked, the contents of which are a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Halberd|link}}.{{Note|It is possible for this reward to scale up to a {{Term|BotW|Royal Halberd|link}}.}}

Back in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Sebasto}} is elated to hear that {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach}} is clear of monsters.<ref>{{Cite|Heyyyyy! You took care of the {{Color|BotW Red|monsters}} at {{Color|BotW Blue|Aris Beach}}! It's been too long since I've had a fish from those waters!|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref> To show his appreciation, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks, mate. I suppose I should give you this to show my appreciation.|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back whenever he would like, and he promises to share his haul with him.<ref>{{Cite|Come back whenever you'd like! I'll share the day's haul with you.|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
