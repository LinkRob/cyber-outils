The {{Term/Store|Ending Blow||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an ability in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TPHD Red|ending blow}} is a secret technique you can use on stunned enemies to end their breath before they spring back into action.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TPHD|Ending Blow}} is the first {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill|link}} that is taught to [[Link]] by the {{Term|TPHD|Hero's Spirit|link}}, who himself is the restless soul of his ancestor, the [[Hero of Time]]. It is the only {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} that must be learned in order to advance through Link's quest and complete {{TP|-}}. Furthermore, the {{Term|TPHD|Ending Blow}} is the only technique among the seven that does not require howling at a {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone|link}} to be able to learn the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}}.

As its name implies, the {{Term|TPHD|Ending Blow}} is a decisive stroke that will eliminate most enemies if properly executed. Once an enemy is temporarily knocked down, Link will have a chance to finish off the foe by jumping in the air and performing a {{Term|TPHD|Down Thrust|link}} right into the enemy's chest, thus instantly defeating it. The chance to execute this skill is notified when the player is prompted to press the Action Button on the controller when "Finish" appears on the screen. If the button is pressed, Link will dispatch the downed foe with a jump and a downward thrust of his sword. However, if the enemy moves out of the way, he will be temporarily vulnerable while trying to pull his sword out of the ground.

The {{Term|TPHD|Ending Blow}} can also be used on certain [[Bosses in Twilight Princess|bosses]] once enough damage has been dealt. Such bosses include {{Term|TPHD|Diababa|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Fyrus|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Morpheel|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Argorok|link}}, and the {{Term|TPHD|Deku Toad|link}}, the [[Sub-Boss]] of {{Term|TPHD|Lakebed Temple|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Ending Blow}} is used in the final part of the [[Final Boss]] battle against {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf|link}}. Once Link has dealt enough damage to {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} to knock him down to the ground, the Hero of Twilight will use this {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} to deliver the final blow to the King of Evil's chest, thrusting the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword}} into {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}}'s gaping wound from his [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|failed execution]] and thus ultimately killing him.

{{Plural|TPHD|Torch Slug|link}} are the only enemies that will prompt an {{Term|TPHD|Ending Blow}} "Finish" without taking damage. If Link executes the {{Term|TPHD|Ending Blow}} too quickly, the {{Term|TPHD|Torch Slug}} will emit a burst of flames that will fling him off before he has a chance to make contact. If Link waits for the {{Term|TPHD|Torch Slug}} to emit the burst before striking, he will defeat it with ease.

== Trivia ==

* {{TWW}} and {{SS}} respectively use the [[Parry Attack]] and [[Fatal Blow]] to perform a very similar finishing blow to defeat [[Ganondorf]] and [[Demise]] during their final battles. Like {{TP|-}}, the main antagonists also die.
** The Parry Attack when used against [[Floormaster|Floormasters]] and [[Magtail|Magtails]] is identical to the Ending Blow.
* Although [[Link]] the [[Hero of Time]] did not manage to kill [[Ganon]] in {{OoT}}, because the Ending Blow is the very attack used by his descendant Link the Hero of Twilight to defeat Ganondorf, the incarnation of the main protagonist from the [[Nintendo 64]] games is still indirectly responsible for his archenemy's death.
* In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess (Himekawa)|{{TP|-}} manga]] by [[Akira Himekawa]], a more graphic depiction of the Ending Blow onto Ganondorf is seen, with a distinctive "chok" sound with a blood splatter and Link's shocked reaction afterwards. In the original game, there was no sound effect whatsoever by Link's stabbing on his archnemesis' chest before the latter screams in agony, and it cuts to a focus on Ganondorf opening his jaw wide in agony just before the blow could make contact as a discretion shot due to [[Nintendo]]'s strict policy of keeping [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] content family-friendly in spite of the Teen rating by the [[ESRB]].
