{{Plural|BotW|Spiked Moblin Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} carried by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Moblin|link}}, primarily within the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. They are made from {{Plural|BotW|Moblin Spear|link}} by strapping horned {{Term|Series|Animal|link}} skulls to their ends.<ref name="Inventory"/> As is common for {{Term|BotW|Moblin}} crafts, they are crudely made.<ref name="Inventory"/> Since they are made by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin}}, they belong to the {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Moblin Spear}} will float on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} if they are dropped. They will also burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} is unable to pick them up.
