"{{Term/Store|Hobbies of the Rich||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hobbies of the Rich}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Tarrey Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Hagie|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} looking out at the {{Term|BotW|Torin Wetland|link}}. Based on {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s clothes, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} assumes that he is in need of money.<ref>{{Cite|Your clothes...<br/>I'm guessing you're a little hard up for cash. Am I right?|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he is, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} appreciates his honesty and pitches a down payment.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] I appreciate your honesty. So for the down payment... I'd say {{Color|BotW Blue|100 rupees}} sounds fair, yes?|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> Even though {{Term|BotW|Link}} is confused, he forces him to take the {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} without explanation.<ref>{{Cite|[A...down payment?] I don't care about the details. Here, take it. Hand!|Hagie|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has accepted the {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} will explain what he wants.<ref>{{Cite|Now, listen to what I have to say.|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> He points out the {{Term|BotW|Torin Wetland}}, explaining that there are two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}} that prowl the area.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Torin Wetland}} lies to the west of here. You're aware that there are {{Color|BotW Blue|Guardians}} there? Not just one, either... {{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Two}} of them!!}} You know how dangerous they are...|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> He would like to see {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeat them both in exchange for the rest of his payment.<ref>{{Cite|So, sonny boy... I'd like you to find and wallop {{Color|BotW Red|both}} of them in {{Color|BotW Blue|Torin Wetland}}. Then come and see me.|Hagie|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} after he defeats both {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}. As agreed upon, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} the rest of his payment, a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Guardians kaput!] OK. OK. I saw the whole thing. I'll give you the rest of the reward, as I promised.|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> He is rude when {{Term|BotW|Link}} is disappointed by the size of the payment,<ref>{{Cite|Why do you look so disappointed? It can't be because the reward is lower than the down payment, can it? You commoners with your notions and assumptions...|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> claiming that the notion of a down payment being a smaller than the final payment is unique to the "common folk".<ref>{{Cite|The whole idea that a down payment is a small part of the final payment is an idea entirely unique to the common folk!|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> With their business concluded, he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to leave him alone, which completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|In any case, our business is concluded. Go over there. Or anywhere, really. Anywhere that isn't. Right. Here.|Hagie|BotW}}</ref>
