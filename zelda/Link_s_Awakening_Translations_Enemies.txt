<noinclude>The following page is a list of the names of enemies and bosses appearing in {{LA}} in Japanese, English and German.
*Empty cells indicate there is no given name for the particular enemy in that language.
*The names of most enemies, bosses, and mini-bosses are never mentioned in-game. Their Japanese names are sourced from the [[Shogakukan]] guide. The German names are sourced from the German version of the "{{Wp|Super Game Boy}} Player's Guide" published by the official {{smw|Club Nintendo}} magazine, the German equivalent of [[Nintendo Power]]. Club Nintendo and its editor in chief, Claude M. Moyse, are credited for the German localization of {{LA|-}}.
*Many names in the German version are grammatically incorrect.</noinclude>
