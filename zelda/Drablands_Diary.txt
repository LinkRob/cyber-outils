The {{Term/Store|Drablands Diary||Series, TFH}}, also known as the '''Worn Diary''',{{Fact}} is a series of books found around [[Hytopia]] in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite|All she can talk about is a book called the Drablands Diary. Chicks are so unpredictable, amiright?|Boy|TFH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The diaries detail the journeys and findings of a writer named [[Mr. Tudor|Four Eyes]] visiting the [[Drablands]].<ref>{{Cite|Be as the dear butterfly, dear reader. Regards, Four Eyes|Vol. 8|TFH}}</ref> The author ventures into the Drablands and writes about his findings to fulfill a long wish of becoming a writer.<ref>{{Cite|I feel can finally put my dream of becoming a writer to rest. I came. I saw. I wrote. It's time to go home.|Vol. 8|TFH}}</ref> The writings documents the creatures he meets and the obstacles he must overcome. The author makes a point of taking a picture of his findings, and one of his photos can be found on the wall in the [[Miiverse Gallery]].<ref>{{Cite|This picture from the Drablands says "Photo by Four Eyes." Weird...|N/A|TFH}}</ref> There are a total of 8 volumes. A few of the volumes can be read from the libraries in the lobbies of [[Hytopia Castle]], while others are hidden in town.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= We've hidden volumes one to eight of the "Diary from the Drablands" in various places outside the Drablands proper.|name= Hiromasa Shikata|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20171104174409/https://miiverse.nintendo.net/replies/AYMHAAACAAADVHkosePRDQ|title= Tri Force Heroes Miiting part 2}}</ref> Vol. 7 even requires the use of the [[Light Armor]] to be read.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= You'll need to be wearing specific outfits to read some of the diary volumes, though.|name= Hiromasa Shikata|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20171104174409/https://miiverse.nintendo.net/replies/AYMHAAACAAADVHkosePRDQ|title= Tri Force Heroes Miiting part 2}}</ref> The books are fairly popular with the townsfolk, who are known to swap Volume 6 among each other and excitedly offer to read it to [[Link]].<ref>{{Cite|This book is apparently taking the world by storm...|Boy|TFH}}</ref> Once [[Princess Styla]]'s curse is lifted, however, the volume will no longer be in their possession and no one will offer to read it anymore.

The diaries also point out several scenic spots that can be found within the Levels of their respective Areas,<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= Yes, every level has a secret scenic spot. We designed them to be inconspicuous when you're playing normally, and as a result they're pretty tough to find.|name= Hiromasa Shikata|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20171104183135/https://miiverse.nintendo.net/replies/AYMHAAACAAADVHkor8XTRw|title= Tri Force Heroes Miiting part 2}}</ref> such as a green butterfly that can be found in the [[Woodlands]], or a [[painting]] of Link in [[The Dunes]].<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= In The Dunes, you can find a mural of Link straight from The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds.|name= Mikiharu Oiwa|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20171104181321/https://miiverse.nintendo.net/replies/AYMHAAACAAADVHkoscENgg|title= Tri Force Heroes Miiting part 2}}</ref> The diaries suggest to take a picture of these secrets using the [[Camera#Tri Force Heroes|Camera]]; however, there is no significance to doing this.

===Vol. 6===
Vol. 6 can be found in the possession of several people in town. It can sometimes be found in the possession of [[Mrs. Pompadour]], [[Mimi]], or the [[Beret Boy]]. Mrs. Pompadour says she found it under her husband's pillow.<ref>{{Cite|I found this under my husband's pillow. Shall I read it aloud?|Mrs. Pompadour|TFH}}</ref> The Beret Boy, when it is in his possession, will read it to one of the cats laying on a bench.<ref>{{Cite|Mimi lent me a book! I was so happy, I read it to a cat. The cat liked it! Do you want to hear it too?|Beret Boy|TFH}}</ref> This volume will no longer be in their possession once Princess Styla's curse is lifted.
