The {{Term/Store|Pendant of Courage||Series, ALttP, ALBW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Pendant of Courage is green in both of its appearances, matching other important items related to [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]] such as the [[Kokiri's Emerald]], [[Farore's Pearl]], and [[Farore's Flame]].

==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP|-}}, the Pendant of Courage is located in the [[Eastern Palace]], close to the cave that [[Sahasrahla]] is hiding in. It is guarded by a group of six [[Armos Knights]]. 

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW|-}}, Link receives the Pendant of Courage from Princess Zelda after warning her of [[Yuga]]'s capture of [[Seres]] in a painting. Zelda claims that she has seen Link in her dreams, and that she believes he is a hero who has come to vanquish evil.<ref>{{Cite|I've seen your face in my dreams of late. For I've dreamt of a hero locked in battle with a terrible evil.|Princess Zelda|ALBW}}</ref> However, though she states that it will aid Link in his journey, Zelda only recognizes the Pendant as a simple charm.<ref>{{Cite|Please take good care of this charm. You will have need of it soon, Link.|Princess Zelda|ALBW}}</ref> After Yuga bars [[Hyrule Castle]] from the outside, Sahasrahla reveals to Link that the charm is actually the Pendant of Courage.<ref>{{Cite|It can't be! You're wearing—! That's the Pendant of Courage!|Sahasrahla|ALBW}}</ref>
