'''Beakon''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Beakon|Persistent and loud, this monster follows its prey and calls for nearby allies to attack on its behalf.<ref name="Beakon">{{Cite|Persistent and loud, this monster follows its prey and calls for nearby allies to attack on its behalf.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Beakons are [[Bird]] enemies that can be found throughout [[Faron Wetlands]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Beakon [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and summoning one costs three Echo energy.

==Trivia==
*Beakon shares the same model as [[Crow]] and [[Albatrawl]], but each is distinguished by its colors.
