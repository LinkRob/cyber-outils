{{Term/Store|Hytopian Silk|Hytopian Silks|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 128"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Hytopian Silk}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}}. Shortly after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}}'s arrival in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}, upon his enlistment in the Witch-Hunting Brigade, {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly|link}} gives the young hero a {{Term|TFH|Hytopian Silk}} to take to {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} for making a {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Consider yourself enlisted. As a token of welcome, I will supply you with one firm handshake and one {{Color|TFH Blue|Hero's Tunic}}.|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> {{Plural|TFH|Hytopian Silk}} are also awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Secret Fortress|link}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Hytopian Silk}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Secret Fortress}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Hytopian Silk}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Abyss of Agony|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Hytopian Silk}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Abyss of Agony}}''', ''"Guard the orb!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Hytopian Silk}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Cove of Transition|link}}''', ''"Halved attack and defense!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Hytopian Silk}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Hytopian Silk}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

In addition to being the only {{Term|TFH|Material}} required to make the {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic}}, {{Plural|TFH|Hytopian Silk}} are used to make the {{Term|TFH|Jack of Hearts|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Torrent Robe|link}}.<ref name="E 128"/>
