==Location and Uses==
'''Revitalizing Potions +''' are an [[Upgrading|upgraded]] version of the regular [[Revitalizing Potion|Revitalizing Potions]]. They can be infused from a regular Revitalizing Potion for a fee of 20 [[Rupee#Skyward%20Sword|Rupees]] at the [[Infusion Shop#Skyward%20Sword|Infusion Shop]] and require the following [[Bug#Skyward%20Sword|Bugs]]: two [[Woodland Rhino Beetle|Woodland Rhino Beetles]], two [[Skyloft Mantis|Skyloft Mantises]], and three [[Deku Hornet|Deku Hornets]].

When consumed, an Revitalizing Potion + completely restores the user's [[Shield#Skyward%20Sword|shield meter]], restores eight [[heart|hearts]] and activates automatically when the equipped shield would have broken otherwise.<ref name="Obtain" />
