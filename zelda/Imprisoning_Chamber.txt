The '''Imprisoning Chamber''' {{Exp Game|TotK}} is a location introduced in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']]. Situated deep [[beneath Hyrule Castle]], it is the very place where [[Ganondorf]] has been sealed by [[King Rauru]] long before the events of ''Tears of the Kingdom''.

== Characteristics ==
The room itself is a large, stone cavern with a floating stone platform situated in the centre. This is where [[Link]] & [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] first encounter [[Ganondorf]]. There are large, stone spikes protruding from the walls that seem to be [[Zonai]] in origin, although they were built afterwards as they are not present in [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment|Age of Imprisonment]] or [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'s [[The Dragon's Tears|Dragon Tears]]. There is a large inverted pyramid in the centre of the ceiling  which is pushed upward into the rock when [[Ganondorf]] lifts [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] as it is not present later in the game, along with the spikes (see: [[Purification Unit]] for greater detail). The room is first accessed by [[Link]] & [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] through a series of tunnels beneath [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] that the entrance to presumably collapsed during [[the Upheaval]]. The platform in the centre falls away in the opening sequence and drops into the bottom of the chamber, destroying it completely. From there Gloom's Lair is accessible. 

== History ==
=== The source of the evil ===
Long before the events of [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]], during the events of Age of Imprisonment, the [[Demon]] King Ganondorf battled the [[Sage]]s and [[King Rauru]] in his attempt to rule over [[Hyrule]]. Even though his mighty opponents were all empowered with their respective [[Secret Stone|Secret Stones]], so was [[Ganondorf]]. He managed to subdue all the [[Sages in TotK|Sages]], but did not expect the [[King Rauru|King of Hyrule]] to commit the ultimate sacrifice in order to save his companions and the world. Blinded by his arrogance, the [[Demon King]] could not stop [[King Rauru|Rauru]] from impaling him in the chest with his sacred arm. The [[King Rauru|King]] used his ancestral magic to steal [[Ganondorf]]'s magic and bind him forever in what was to become his tomb. Sealing [[Ganondorf]] ultimately cost [[King Rauru|Rauru]] his own life, but he willingly accepted to pay such a price for the sake of Hyrule. Knowing very well that his sacrifice would only delay Ganondorf's return, [[King Rauru]] solemnly warned the [[Gerudo]] that one day, a young [[swordsman]] named [[Link]] would finish what he had started. [[Ganondorf]] would indeed remember this name, as his life force drained away while he became a lifeless husk.

=== The Demon King reawakens ===
Many millennia passed, Link and [[Princess Zelda]] came to the Imprisoning Chamber to investigate the source of an unexplained illness gripping the nation's citizens. It was here they found [[King Rauru's Right Arm]], along with his [[Secret Stone]], still sealing [[Ganondorf]]'s now emaciated body. As they approached the body, the arm and stone fell off, the latter which [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] picked up. The seal now broken, [[Ganondorf]] awoke and saw the duo, recognizing [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] he saw from the past, and Link by the [[Master Sword]] he wields. The [[Demon King]] then releases a massive stream of [[Gloom]] to attack the [[Princess Zelda|Princess]], but [[Link]] blocked it with his sword. The [[Gloom]], however, was extremely deadly that it shattered the [[Master Sword]], and crippled [[Link]]'s right arm. Finding what [[King Rauru|Rauru]] forewarned him of to be rather not meeting up to his expectations, Ganondorf then released even more [[Gloom]] to lift the entire [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] high above its foundations, as well as many landmasses into the [[sky]], an event known as [[The Upheaval]], which also caused the Imprisoning Chamber to fall apart. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] fell as the ground collapsed beneath her, and Link jumped after, trying to catch her despite his own arm being horrifically burned. At this time, the [[Secret Stone]] [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] was holding onto resonated with her, transporting her back to the distant past when [[King Rauru|Rauru]] was the ruler of [[Hyrule]]. As a baffled [[Link]] fell towards the abyss, [[King Rauru's Right Arm|Rauru's arm]] grabbed onto him, lifting him back up, and taking him to the [[Room of Awakening]] in the skies for medical treatment. Ganondorf then retreated to [[the Depths]] to recover his strength.
