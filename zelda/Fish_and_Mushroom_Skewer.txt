{{Term/Store|Fish and Mushroom Skewer|Fish and Mushroom Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Fish and Mushroom Skewers are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}} with any {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}}. Eating one will restore some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The fish used in these skewers are fresh, while the mushrooms are fragrant.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Several different variations of Fish and Mushroom Skewers exist, depending on both the ingredients are used and whether any additional ingredients are added:

* '''Hearty Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}}. When they're eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Enduring Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}}. Eating them will refill and overfill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Energizing Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}. If eaten, Link's Stamina Wheel will be partially refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Hasty Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}}. Eating one will grant Link a temporary boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}}. Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when these are eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants mid-level electricity resistance.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Voltfruit|link}}. Eating these skewers will give Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a mid-level attack-power boost.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}}. Eating them will boost Link's attacks for a short time.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a mid-level defense boost.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}}. If they're eaten, Link's defense will increase.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}}. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Fish and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fish and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking skewered, fresh fish alongside fragrant mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}}. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]] temporarily.<ref name="Chilly"/>
