{{Term/Store|Someone's Awakening||Series, CoH}} is an {{Term|CoH|Achievement|link}} in {{CoH}}.{{Name Ref|CoH}}

==Overview==
Someone's Awakening is obtained whenever Zelda (if Cadence choose to awake Link first) or Link (if Cadence choose to awake Zelda first) are awakened for the first time in the Story Mode.

==Nomenclature==
The name of the achievement itself is a reference to [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]].{{Nomenclature}}
