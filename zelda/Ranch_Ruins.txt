The {{Term/Store|Ranch Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ranch Ruins|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
The Ranch Ruins are the remains of a farm where many people once gathered,<ref>{{Cite|The green garments of the man who wished to be a fairy are hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in the ruins of Hyrule Field}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where trade flourished}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where sinners were imprisoned}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the village next to farmland where many people once gathered}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> situated just to the east of the [[Mabe Village Ruins]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. The layout of the Ranch Ruins greatly resembles that of [[Lon Lon Ranch]] from {{OoT}}, with a few minor alterations. The Ranch Ruins feature little of interest, save for the enemies which prowl its grounds.

A [[Korok]] can be found by following the flower puzzle at the lean-to at the southeastern part of the Ranch Ruins. In return for finding him, he will give Link a [[Korok Seed]].<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
