{{Term/Store|Armos Spirit|Armos Spirits|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 116"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Armos Spirit}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Deku Forest|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Armos Spirit}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Deku Forest}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Armos Spirit}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Buzz Blob Cave|link}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Armos Spirit}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Moblin Base|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Armos Spirit}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Armos Spirit}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Big Bomb Outfit|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Kokiri Clothes|link}}.<ref name="E 116"/>
