"{{Term/Store|Hunt for the Giant Horse||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hunt for the Giant Horse}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Mounted Archery Camp}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the {{Term|BotW|Mounted Archery Camp|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Straia|link}}, who is conducting an ecological study of the animals in the area.<ref>{{Cite|We're doing an ecological study on the wild animals in the area.|Straia|BotW}}</ref> He chose this location because he heard rumors of the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}} that lives in and is exclusive to the region.<ref>{{Cite|We'd heard that there was a {{Color|BotW Red|giant horse}} living in this region. Apparently it can't be found anywhere else... That was all we needed to hear! We headed this way to investigate right away!|Straia|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Straia}} explains it is potentially a member of a rare, local species of {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The really big horse in this region is likely part of a rare, local species.|Straia|BotW}}</ref> Due to [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]] in the area, though, {{Term|BotW|Straia}} has been unable to reach the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|However, with all the monsters roaming around, we simply can't go any farther. To be so close to discovery...|Straia|BotW}}</ref> He even saw a {{Term|BotW|Lynel|link|display=monster that looked like a Horse}}, which he initially mistook for the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's right... There was even a monster that looked quite a lot like a horse... I approached it, thinking at first that it was the {{Color|BotW Red|giant horse}} we've been looking for, but... Oh, it was terrible...|Straia|BotW}}</ref>

Based on {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s appearance, {{Term|BotW|Straia}} presumes that he is a strong adventurer.<ref>{{Cite|Just from looking at you, I'd guess that you're an adventurer, right? You certainly look strong.|Straia|BotW}}</ref> As such, he asks him to catch the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}} and bring it back for them to see.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... Hey, how would you like to go and catch a {{Color|BotW Red|giant horse}} for us?|Straia|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts his suggestion,<ref>{{Cite|[Leave it to me.] Ah, you're a lifesaver!|Straia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Straia}} directs him to the {{Term|BotW|Taobab Grassland|link}}, where the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}} is rumored to live.<ref>{{Cite|They say that the {{Color|BotW Red|giant horse}} lives around the {{Color|BotW Blue|Taobab Grassland}}... That'd be northwest of here.|Straia|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the request is not urgent,<ref>{{Cite|Do you think you could catch it and bring it back here? Don't stress out over the request, though!|Straia|BotW}}</ref> which starts the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

There are two {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} located on the {{Term|BotW|Oseira Plains|link}}, which lead into the {{Term|BotW|Taobab Grassland}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must either bypass or defeat them in order to reach the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}}. The {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}} itself requires at least two {{Plural|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} to tame, though it can be ridden past the {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} without the need for a fight.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can ride the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}} back to the {{Term|BotW|Mounted Archery Camp}} and show it to {{Term|BotW|Straia}}.<ref>{{Cite|That horse... It's huge...|Straia|BotW}}</ref> He is shocked by its size and determines that it must be of a species native to the region.<ref>{{Cite|I've never seen on that big! That is definitely an endemic species!|Straia|BotW}}</ref> He asks to take a closer look at it,<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, but can I take a good look at that horse?|Straia|BotW}}</ref> after which he is pleased.<ref>{{Cite|Wow. With this much info, I can feel my spirit for discovery murmuring in contentment.|Straia|BotW}}</ref> He notes that it is different to see something instead of reading about it and thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for the opportunity.<ref>{{Cite|It's definitely different seeing something with your own eyes rather than just reading about it. Thank you!|Straia|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives {{term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is to thank you for helping with our investigation!|Straia|BotW}}</ref>
