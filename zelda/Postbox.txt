{{Term/Store|Postbox|Postboxes|Series, MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD, TMC, ST|plural}},{{Exp Game|MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD, TMC, ST}}<ref>{{Cite||Bombers' Notebook|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We will deliver any letters or packages {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|addressed to you}} to the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|postbox}} nearest you. Without fail, I promise you.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Postbox|Figurine|TMC}}</ref><ref name="ST Prima">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ST |publisher= Prima |page= 19}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Mailbox|Mailboxes|PH|plural}},{{Exp Game|MM, MM3D, PH}}<ref>{{Cite|You were given a {{Color|MM3D Red|letter to Kafei}}! Quick! Deliver it for her! Take it to a {{Color|MM3D Red|mailbox}}.|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, look at that {{Color|PH Red|mailbox}}. It's wiggling like crazy... You think maybe there's a letter for you?|Linebeck|PH}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They are sentient, talking objects with mail slots for mouths. They are the touchpoints of the {{Term|Series|Mail Delivery|link}} services, accepting {{Term|Series|Letter|link}} deposits and holding {{Term|Series|Link|link}}'s incoming mail. They wiggle when they have mail for {{Term|Series|Link}}.{{Note|The wiggling {{Term|Series|Postbox}} concept is absent from {{MM}} and {{MM3D}}. It was introduced in {{TWW}} and has remained ever since.<ref>{{Cite|We cannot do much to help you, but we can at least do this: if we get any letters or parcels for you, we will deliver them to a {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|postbox}} near you. If you see any {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|wiggling}} postboxes, please look inside them.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref><ref name="PH Prima">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima |page= 15}}</ref><ref name="ST Prima"/>}}

{{Plural|Series|Postbox}} are red in color and usually bear the {{Term|Series|Mail Delivery}} insignia on their front.

==Locations and Uses==
{{Incomplete|type=article|reason=Missing details such as locations}}
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, [[Link]] can find {{Plural|MM|Postbox}} scattered around [[Clock Town]]: there are two {{Plural|MM|Postbox}} in {{Term|MM|South Clock Town|link}}, two in {{Term|MM|East Clock Town|link}}, and one in {{Term|MM|North Clock Town|Link}}. He can use any of them to send the {{Term|MM3D|Letter to Kafei|link}}. If Link attempts to deposit the {{Title|MM3D|Priority Mail|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Postbox}} will direct Link to the {{Term|MM3D|Post Office|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Please take {{Color|MM3D Red|priority mail}} to the Post Office. It will even be accepted late at night. Ka-ching!|Postbox|MM}}</ref>

If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} speaks to a {{Term|MM3D|Postbox}} while wearing the {{Term|MM3D|Postman's Hat|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Postbox}} reports that an item other than a letter was deposited. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} gets a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}} the first time he checks a {{Term|MM3D|Postbox}}, and single {{Plural|MM3D|Green Rupee|link}} thereafter.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Island Postal Service}}
In {{TWW}}, Link also can use Postboxes to send letters, but this time he needs to pay [[Rupee]]s to get it sent. The price to pay is higher the farther he is from [[Dragon Roost Island]], and cheaper the closer. Link can also receive letters from wiggling Postboxes. Link can receive different items from Postboxes, such as Pieces of Heart, Rupees, and [[Special Chart|Charts]]. The {{Term|TWWHD|IN-credible Chart|link}} is sent Cash-On-Delivery and costs 201 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Postage due is {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|201 Rupees}}. Will you pay?|Postbox|TWW}}</ref> Strangely, {{Term|TWWHD|Baito|link}} sends 20 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}} as a Cash-On-Delivery parcel that costs 10 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Postage due is {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|10 Rupees}}. Will you pay?|Postbox|TWW}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, a decorative {{Term|TMC|Postbox}} adorns the roof of the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}}. It cannot be interacted with as the roof is inaccessible.{{Note|The development team for {{TMC|-}} originally had other intentions for the {{Term|TMC|Postbox}}. See [[#Trivia|Trivia]].}}

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH|-}}, approaching a wiggling {{Term|PH|Postbox}} causes the {{Term|PH|Postman|link}} to fly in and stand on it.{{Note|Contrarily to what is stated in the guides by [[Prima Games]], {{Plural|Series|Postbox}} in the [[Nintendo DS]] games are activated simply by approaching them, not necessarily by tapping them. See [[#Trivia|Trivia]].}} He proceeds to read {{Term|PH|Link}}'s letter aloud rather than actually delivering it to him. {{Term|PH|Link}} first meets the {{Term|PH|Postman}} standing on the {{Term|PH|Postbox}} on {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}.

Link can send {{Plural|PH|Prize Postcard|link}} in the mail to participate in a drawing contest. At midnight,{{Note|According to the [[Nintendo 3DS]] clock.}} Link receives a letter in the {{Term|PH|Postbox}} with the results of the contest. Perversely, he gets a {{Term|PH|Ship Part|link}} for losing,{{Fact|PH}} but for winning gets nothing at all.<ref>{{Cite|...... Hmm? That's it? That's weird. It said you won something, but there's nothing inside! Well, whaddya gonna do... Did my deal, so I guess I'll be on my way!|Postman|PH}}</ref>

==={{ST|-}}===
Mailboxes in {{ST}} wiggle when new mail arrives. The Postman delivers any letter to Link.

==Trivia==
* The decorative {{Term|TMC|Postbox}} in {{TMC|-}} has an unused wiggle animation.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= |retrieved= February 6, 2020|url= https://tcrf.net/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_The_Minish_Cap#Unused_Mailbox_Animation|title= The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap|site= The Cutting Room Floor|type= }}</ref> The game also has unused text for a {{Term|TMC|Postbox}} {{Term|TMC|Figurine|link}}. Its description states that the {{Term|TMC|Postbox}} could participate in {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} and suggests a person is hiding inside it.<ref>{{Cite|This strange red postbox has the ability to fuse Kinstone pieces with you. Some say there is really just a person hiding inside it...|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>
* The description of {{Plural|PH|Mailbox}} in {{Guide|PH|Prima}} falsely states that {{Term|PH|Link}} must tap on a {{Term|PH|Mailbox}} for the {{Term|PH|Postman}} to appear.<ref name="PH Prima"/> The mistake is repeated in the {{ST|-}} guide which copies the paragraph almost word-for-word, changing only the word "mailbox" to "postbox".<ref name="ST Prima"/> As a result, the {{ST|-}} guide states that the {{Term|ST|Postman}} "flutters in," though the {{Term|ST|Postman}} in {{ST|-}} has no wings.
