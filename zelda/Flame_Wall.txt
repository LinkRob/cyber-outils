{{Term/Store|Flame Wall|Flame Walls|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM|plural}} are recurring traps in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="OoT3D Vision"/><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= CE |publisher= Nintendo |page= 122 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Flame Wall}} go unnamed in {{MM3D}} and {{FSA}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Several {{Plural|OoT3D|Flame Wall}} form a maze on the third floor of the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}}, which {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} must navigate through to complete the {{Term|OoT3D|Dungeon|link}}. Should {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} touch one of the {{Plural|OoT3D|Flame Wall}}, he will be damaged and thrown on his back.

==={{MM|-}}===
Similarly to {{OoT|-}}, several {{Plural|MM3D|Flame Wall}} form a maze within the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Shrine|link}} as part of the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Butler|link}}'s challenge. The {{Plural|MM3D|Flame Wall|display=Walls}} will damage and incapacitate {{Term|MM3D|Link}}, but clearing the entire {{Term|MM3D|Deku Shrine}} will yield him the {{Term|MM3D|Mask of Scents|link}}. In {{MM3D}}, these {{Plural|MM3D|Flame Wall}} are replaced by simple iron bars. Circular {{Plural|MM3D|Flame Wall}} also appear in battles with {{Plural|MM3D|Garo|link}} and act as an obstacle around important {{Plural|MM3D|Object}} such as {{Plural|MM3D|Deku Flower|link}} and {{Plural|MM3D|Treasure Chest|link}}.

{{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}} will burn if he touches a {{Term|MM3D|Flame Wall}}.
