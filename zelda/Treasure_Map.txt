==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
The Treasure Map is an item that can be bought in the back of the [[Horon Village Shop]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s in {{OoS}}. It shows the location of all four [[Jewel]]s that open [[Tarm Ruins]].

==={{PH|-}}===
31 Treasure Maps can be found throughout the [[World of the Ocean King]] in {{PH}}. Treasure Maps direct [[Link]] to underwater [[Treasure]]s,<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|PH Red|treasure map}}! It shows where treasure lies at the bottom of the sea!|N/A|PH}}</ref> which can be retrieved using the [[Salvage Arm]]. These Treasures can be 60 second [[Sand of Hours|Sands of Hours]], [[Ship Part]]s, or [[Rupee]]s. Ship Parts found from Treasure Maps are more likely to be rare than Ship Parts found elsewhere.<!--
