'''Freeze Slug''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Freeze Slug|Lurking on the walls and floors of the coldest places, this creature leaves a path of freezing ice behind it.<ref name="Freeze Slug">{{Cite|Lurking on the walls and floors of the coldest places, this creature leaves a path of freezing ice behind it.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The frozen slug can be found throughout [[Hebra Mountains|Hebra mountain]], sticking to walls and leaving behind trails of ice. A [[Dark Echo|Dark Variant]] of the frozen slug can be fought occasionally after contact with a [[Tecuum]]. Upon defeating for the first time Zelda can gain the torch slug as an [[Echo]], with the [[Tri]] cost of three.

Their fiery counterpart, the [[Torch Slug]] can be found throughout [[Eldin Volcano]].
