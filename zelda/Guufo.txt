{{Term/Store|Guufo||Series}} is the guardian of [[Rouru|Lord Rouru]] in the {{OoT|Himekawa}}.{{Fact}} His form is a resemblance of an owl. As all guardians are, he is over-protective with Rouru. Although he seems tough, he is quite gentle at heart.

==Biography==
Guufo is first seen searching for [[Elrora]], the Watarara's chieftess.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Where is the chieftess?|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> While speaking with a fellow Watarara, he learns that the tribe's children have grown their wings, and the migration winds will soon turn.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Y-yes! And the children's wings are ready!... The wind is going to turn...|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> Despite this, he refuses to leave until Rouru, who has gone missing, is found.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... We won't move until Rouru is back!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> Once he finds Elrora, the cheiftess accepts that they may need to leave her son Rouru behind,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= We may have to leave him behind.|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> although Guufo promises to find the boy even he must forfeit his life.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... I'll find Rouru... or die trying!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> With that he leaves anxiously, sensing a storm on the wind.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= That scent on the wind... is it an incoming storm? Please no! Not yet!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Guufo is next seen when Rouru briefly remembers when he had attempted to fly with Guufo himself urging him,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Lord Rouru, fly! Now! Be brave!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 178}}</ref> except the boy was unable to grow his wings,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I can't!! Why won't my wings come out?!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 179}}</ref> which resulted in his fall, thus beginning the tribe's search for their heir. 

Guufo is last seen after Elrora saves Rouru and [[Link]] from falling to their deaths after defeating [[Flare Dancer]]s sent by [[Ganondorf]]. Relieved that Rouru's safe,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I'm glad you're safe!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 185}}</ref> he hugs the prince tightly and bursts into tears. Guufo is astonished that Rouru grew his wings so quickly, since he wasn't able to at the ceremony.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... This is amazing! Lord Rouru couldn't grow his wings at the growth ceremony. That's why he fell into the lake.|book= OoT (Himekawa)|volume= 2|publisher= |page= 185}}</ref> He then leaves with his tribe on their migration.
