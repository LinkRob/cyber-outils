{{Term/Store|Gartan||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gartan|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Gartan}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} that can be found traveling on foot in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The emporium is open! Come one, come all! All the little bits and bobs you need to spice up your dishes!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> He carries a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}} as he travels between {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable|link}} and the entrance to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. Along the way, he passes through {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Gartan}} is very interested in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} women.<ref>{{Cite|Gerudo ladies... Whew!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Don't be shy, ladies...|Gartan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What do Gerudo want?|Gartan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|They'll ALL come running!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> He is very defensive of this, claiming that business is his top priority.<ref>{{Cite|Business is my top priority! Wh-why, what else would I be interested in?|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> He is disappointed that he is not allowed to move into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fine-looking ladies, these Gerudo, every last one of them. {{Small|It's a crying shame they won't let me move into their town...}}|Gartan|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Gartan}} enjoys being a {{Term|BotW|Merchant}} because it gives him an excuse to talk to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nothing like a line of work that lets you make small talk with Gerudo ladies.|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}}, {{Term|BotW|Gartan}} is excited about making small talk with the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} there.<ref>{{Cite|Kicking back in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Kara Kara Bazaar}}, making small talk with the lovely Gerudo... Good times, good times.|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gartan}} then says that it is just part of his job to make friends and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants some {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, don't get my wrong! It's all part of the job! You have to make friends to make money. Want some apples?|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> However, he admits that he gets nervous around {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} ladies.<ref>{{Cite|I get antsy around these Gerudo knockouts. Is that a phrase? It is now!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} is still active, {{Term|BotW|Gartan}} reveals that it makes traveling to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} from {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|That old {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} makes the road between {{Color|BotW Blue|Kara Kara Bazaar}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}} a dangerous place.|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> However, he refuses to let it stop him from meeting {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} ladies.<ref>{{Cite|But I'm too keen on getting to know those Gerudo ladies to let that stop me! I'll dash right on through the desert!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> He is also very worried about the presence of thieves in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|I haven't had any peace of mind since I heard there were thieves in {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}.|Gartan|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Gartan}} considers the term "wasteland" to be accurate when describing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, as there are many [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]] and sandstorms.<ref>{{Cite|The desert's just monsters and bad sandstorms through and through. They call it a wasteland for a reason.|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> However, he does not know how else someone is supposed to meet {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} women.<ref name="Man"/><ref>{{Cite|The desert's rough going, no two ways about it, but what else is a man to do if he wants to meet Gerudo ladies?|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> 

{{Term|BotW|Gartan}} can also be found at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}, where he tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that he wishes more {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} ladies would come by.<ref>{{Cite|I wish more of those Gerudo ladies would come by this stable! {{Small|I have so many apples for them...apples and love...}}|Gartan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hope we'll see more and more Gerudo ladies come visit this place...|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gartan}} sometimes mutters about his determination to get into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} and get close to the ladies.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I'll get into {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}} and cozy up to those ladies if it's the last thing I do..}}|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> When he notices {{Term|BotW|Link}} listening, he tells him that he was just muttering about "important business secrets."<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Oh, just sales stuff. I mutter so no one learns my important business secrets!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Gartan}} journeyed to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to get to know the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}} better, but he did not find out men were not allowed until he got there.<ref>{{Cite|I came all this way to get to know these Gerudo ladies better...only to find out that they don't let men in town.|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can catch {{Term|BotW|Gartan}} muttering to himself that there must be a way into the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|There must be some way in...}}|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Gartan}} notices that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is listening, he is startled and nervously explains that he was just thinking out loud to nobody.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Don't mind me, ha ha! I wasn't talking to nobody about nothing!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Just thinking out loud. Don't mind me.|Gartan|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves {{Term|BotW|Gartan}} from monsters, he may say how close of a save it was to himself.<ref>{{Cite|That was a close one...|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> He considers the only thing scarier than monsters to be {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} women.<ref>{{Cite|The only thing scarier than mnonsters... are those Gerudo ladies!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> His strategy for surviving attacks is to run away.<ref>{{Cite|The surefire answer for not getting killed by monsters is to run far, far away!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> He gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} for saving him.<ref>{{Cite|Good save! I'll give you a little bonus for that one!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can buy {{Plural|BotW|Item}} from Gartan.<ref>{{Cite|This is all I got right at the moment.|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> He thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his purchases.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks a bundle!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks a heap!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link }}buys all of one of his products, he apologizes that he has run out.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, but I'm fresh out of that one!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> He is grateful for {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s business when all of his stock is bought out.<ref>{{Cite|That's all of it. Thanks for the business!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref>

In addition to selling his wares, {{Term|BotW|Gartan}} will also purchase {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll buy anything!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Gonna sell me something?|Gartan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm interested in seeing what you've got for sale.|Gartan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Was that all, or is there more?|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> He is appreciative of the products he is able to buy,<ref>{{Cite|I appreciate your business!|Gartan|BotW}}</ref> and he asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has more for him.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Was that all, or is there more?|Gartan|BotW}}</ref>
