The {{Term/Store|''History of the Zora, Addendum 2''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|History of the Zora, Addendum 2<br/>Prince Sidon's Great Escape|History of the Zora, Addendum 2|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|History of the Zora, Addendum 2}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}} located south of {{Term|BotW|Lulu Lake|link}}. It is one of two additions to the original {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monument}} commission crafted by the unnamed stonemason, made to detail the achievements of {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link|display=Prince Sidon}}.<ref>{{Cite|While I was at it, I thought...why not add two of my own? And so I created one for King Dorephan and one for Prince Sidon. True, this is outside the scope of my commission... But I believe their triumphs deserve as much! But why stop at that...|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref> Reading it is required to complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}".

This {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument}} describes the story of an {{Term|BotW|Octorok|link}} that plagued {{Term|BotW|Hateno Bay|link}}, terrorizing fishermen in the area.<ref>{{Cite|There was once a giant Octorok in Hateno Bay, large as a mountain, which terrorized the village's fishers.|History of the Zora, Addendum 2|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sidon|display=Prince Sidon}}, hearing of their plight, decided he would be the one to defeat the monster.<ref>{{Cite|Hearing of their distress, Prince Sidon went forth to personally eliminate the offending Octorok.|History of the Zora, Addendum 2|BotW}}</ref> However, when {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} dodged one of its attacks, he ended up being swallowed whole.<ref>{{Cite|But this Octorok was a tricky beast. After the prince dodged on of the stone it spat, it inhaled him whole.|History of the Zora, Addendum 2|BotW}}</ref> Just when all hope seemed lost for the prince, a {{Term|BotW|Silverscale Spear|link}} pierced the {{Term|BotW|Octorok}} from within.<ref>{{Cite|Just as it seemed Prince Sidon would be counted among them, the giant Octorok twisted in pain. The tip of a silverscale spear pierced the Octorok's stomach from within, revealing itself as the source of the beast's agony.|History of the Zora, Addendum 2|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} stabbed the {{Term|BotW|Octorok}}'s stomach from within, causing the monster to spit him out and escaped.<ref>{{Cite|Incredibly, Prince Sidon had fought his way out by stabbing his spear over and over into the monster's stomach. Unable to bear the pain, the Octorok coughed up the prince and scrambled to escape.|History of the Zora, Addendum 2|BotW}}</ref> Since then, the fishers have passed down the tale of The Prince Who Slew the Fell Octorok.<ref>{{Cite|Ever since, the fishers of Hateno Bay have passed down this heroic tale: The Prince Who Slew the Fell Octorok|History of the Zora, Addendum 2|BotW}}</ref>
