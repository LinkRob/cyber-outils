'''Boomerang Boarblin Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Boomerang Boarblin Lv. 2|Having mastered the boomerang, this Boarblin has deadly aim. Its sharpened boomerangs are quick and accurate.<ref name="Boomerblin Lv. 2">{{Cite|Having mastered the boomerang, this Boarblin has deadly aim. Its sharpened boomerangs are quick and accurate.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Boomerang Boarblin Lv. 2 are stronger, red-skinned versions of the regular [[Boomerang Boarblin]], with increased health and damage. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can use the [[Tri Rod]] to learn their [[Echo]], which costs four Echo energy to summon.
