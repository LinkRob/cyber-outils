'''First 4 Figures''' is a company which produces high-end resin and PVC figures. Most of their figures are available in standard and exclusive versions, with the latter featuring lighted parts, different poses, or extras. Both type of figures are limited and numbered. Several figures based on {{TLoZ|Series}} have been released since 2006.
