==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Hiding Your True Self}}" is an event wherein a greater number of {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} with masks or hidden faces randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits with masks or hiding their face are showing their stuff in battle on the Spirit Board!|author= Nintendo |published= January 25, 2022 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/7ac75025/index.html |title= Event: Hiding Your True Self |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
