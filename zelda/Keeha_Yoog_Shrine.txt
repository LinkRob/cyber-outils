The {{Term/Store|Keeha Yoog Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Keeha Yoog Shrine}}<br/>Keeha Yoog's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Keeha Yoog's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Keeha Yoog Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, on the east side of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Summit|link}}. However, it is initially buried beneath the ground. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must first talk to {{Term|BotW|Geggle|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}, who will point out a section of the cliff face that looks man-made.<ref>{{Cite|I happened to notice that a section of the cliff face there in the distance looks almost man-made.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> This activates the "{{Term|BotW|Cliffside Etchings|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} investigates, he will find a mural on the cliff featuring four {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} bolts. In its center is a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}. Shooting the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} with a {{Term|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}} will cause {{Term|BotW|Keeha Yoog Shrine}} to emerge from the ground. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he will be greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Keeha Yoog|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} states that by arriving at the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already proven his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Keeha Yoog. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Keeha Yoog|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Keeha Yoog Shrine}} features no trial, only a straight path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Keeha Yoog}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Keeha Yoog}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}} for being able to reach the {{Term|BotW|Keeha Yoog Shrine|display=Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule.}}|Keeha Yoog|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Keeha Yoog|BotW}}</ref> They then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Keeha Yoog|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} on the path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.
