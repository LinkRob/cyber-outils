{{Term/Store|Spekter||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 270"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Spookter & Spekter}}
{{Term|TMC|Spekter}} is {{Term|TMC|Spookter|link}}'s brother.<ref name="E 270"/> He lives in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}, where {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with him twice.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? I'm on it!|Spekter|TMC}}</ref> The first successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} results in {{Term|TMC|Borlov|link}} adding a new, harder level to the {{Term|TMC|Chest Mini-Game Shop|link}}. The second successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} causes {{Term|TMC|Spookter}} to disappear from {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to enter {{Term|TMC|Anju's Henhouse|link}} while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized.

If spoken to, {{Term|TMC|Spekter}} will caution {{Term|TMC|Link}} about the presence of {{Plural|TMC|Ghost|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley}} before remembering his brother, expressing his concern for him.<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing here?! This is a graveyard! There are ghosts here! Oh yeah, speaking of which, my brother lives in {{Color|TMC Green|Hyrule Town}}. He's a troublesome prankster, but I often wonder how he's doing...|Spekter|TMC}}</ref>
