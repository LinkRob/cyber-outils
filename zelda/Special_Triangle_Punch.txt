The '''Special Triangle Punch''' is a technique which {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link|link}} uses in the {{OoS|Himekawa}} during a boxing match against {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Blaino|link}}.{{Name Ref}}

==History==
When {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link|link}} attempts to win back {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Ricky's Gloves|link}}, he challenges {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Blaino|link}} by risking his own {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Sword|link}}. He is put in an inferior position throughout the entire match, until {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Piyoko|link}} figures out that {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Blaino}} is under the influence of {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox|link}} and comes up with the idea to use the power of the {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Triforce|link}} to neutralize the effect. {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} does so by hitting {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Blaino}} with his left hand, where the {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Triforce}} mark is, thus leading to {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Blaino}} returning to his normal state. {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Ricky|link}} gets his gloves back and the incident does also lead to him admiring {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} and eventually accompanying him.
