==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Silent Shroom}}
{{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom}} can be found at the bases of trees in the [[Lanayru Great Spring]] and [[West Necluda]]. They are easier to notice during the nighttime due to their glow. Eating a Silent Shroom raw will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} half of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} of healing. Roasting a Silent Shroom in a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} will result in a {{Term|BotW|Toasty Silent Shroom|link}}. Meals made in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} using a Silent Shroom will temporarily increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} when consumed.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> Silent Shrooms can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Light Blue [[Dye]].

As is the case with all mushrooms, Silent Shrooms can be used in a Cooking Pot to make [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Fragrant Mushroom Sauté]], [[Fruit and Mushroom Mix]], [[Glazed Mushrooms]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], a [[Mushroom Omelet]], [[Mushroom Rice Balls]], [[Mushroom Risotto]], a [[Mushroom Skewer]], [[Copious Mushrooms Skewers]], [[Salt-Grilled Mushrooms]], and [[Steamed Mushrooms]].

Eight Silent Shrooms, in addition to five {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, are needed to upgrade each piece of the [[Stealth Set]] to its third tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Stealth Set''': 24 (0, 0, 8, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
