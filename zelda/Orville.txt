'''Orville''' is a character in the {{SS|Himekawa}}.{{Name Ref|SS (Himekawa)}}

==Biography==
When {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Hylia's Chosen Hero|link}}, {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Link|link}}, was imprisoned for four years, {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Orville}} tended his {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= YOUR WEAPON. ORVILLE HAS TENDED TO IT THESE PAST FOUR YEARS. |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 5}}</ref> {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Orville}} presents the blade to the {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Hylia's Chosen Hero|display=Hero}} after he is asked to fight against the {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Demise|display=Demon King|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= PLEASE COME OUT INTO THE LIGHT OF DAY. THE DEMON KING'S ARMY WILL SOON BE UPON US. |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 5}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= YOU ARE THE ONLY WHO CAN DRIVE OFF THE DEMON KING AND PROTECT HYLIA! |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 5}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= LINK, OUR HERO. IT IS MY HONOR TO BE ABLE TO PRESENT THIS BLADE TO YOU. |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 5}}</ref> {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Link}} accepts and grasping the hands of {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Orville}} and his other allies, says the children of {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Hylia|link}} will fight together.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= LET US FIGHT TOGETHER, CHILDREN OF HYLIA. |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 8}}</ref> {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Orville}} observes as {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Link}} discusses the {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Hylian}} race's worth with the {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Crimson Loftwing|link}}.

{{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Orville}} is not shown again until the castle begins to rise into the sky with the surviving {{Plural|SS (Himekawa)|Hylian}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= WE'RE TAKING TO THE SKY!! GATHER AT THE CASTLE! |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 21}}</ref> The Goddess {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Hylia|link}} rends the earth below it and {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Link}} turns the {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Master Sword|link}} into a pillar between heaven and earth, the {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Crimson Loftwing|link}} guiding the castle skyward to safety.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= With a single swing of the sword, the goddess Hylia rent the earth. |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 20}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= THE MASTER SWORD HAS BECOME A PILLAR BETWEEN HEAVEN AND EARTH... ...GUIDED BY THE LOFTWING... ...IT RISES TO THE HEAVENS... |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 23}}</ref> {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Orville}} stretches his hand downward and cries out for {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Link}} to jump into the column of light caused by the {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= LINK!! JUMP INTO THE LIGHT-- PLEASE!! |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 23}}</ref> {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Link}} prays for the happiness of his comrades and despite wishing to take to the skies with them, his wounded body falls to the ground.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= MAY YOU THRIVE, MY COMRADES. I PRAY FOR YOU HAPPINESS WITH ALL MY HEART. IN TRUTH, I WANTED TO TAKE TO THE SKIES WITH YOU... ...BUT MY BODY IS HEAVY. IT FEELS AS THOUGH I'M STUCK TO THE GROUND. I CAN'T MOVE. CURSES! |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 24, 25}}</ref> Before dying, {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Link}} says his spirit will always be with them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= MY SPIRIT WILL ALWAYS BE WITH YOU... |book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 25}}</ref>
