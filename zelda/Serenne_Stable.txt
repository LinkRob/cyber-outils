{{Term/Store|Serenne Stable||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Serenne Stable'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} located in {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}. It is run by {{Term|BotW|Sprinn|link}} and employs {{Term|BotW|Zumi|link}} as a stable hand. {{Term|BotW|Akrah|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Onya|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Garshon|link}} can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, and will give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Side Quest)|link}}" if he speaks to them. {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Yammo|link}} will sometimes pass through the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} and the Great Dragon {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}} can occasionally be seen flying overhead. There is a recipe for {{Term|BotW|Meat and Rice Bowl|link}} on the inside of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} wall.
