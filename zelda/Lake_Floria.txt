{{Term/Store|Lake Floria||Series, SS, SSHD, BotW}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lake Floria|Map|SSHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'''Lake Floria'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{SS|-}}===
Lake Floria is the largest known body of water that exists in the present day on the [[Hyrule|Surface]]. It is inhabited by a large range of flora and aquatic creatures. Most of the Lake is composed of large submerged underground caverns which can be explored through the use of the [[Water Dragon's Scale]] and the bomb-like [[Froak]]s. 

Link first discovers the entrance to Lake Floria after completing [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]]'s [[Silent Realm]]. He obtains the Water Dragon's Scale, allowing him to swim underwater, and makes his way up to the top of the canopy of the [[Great Tree]], where he meets the [[Kikwi]] Hermit known as [[Yerbal]]. He reveals the location of the entrance to Lake Floria.<ref>{{Cite|Her lair is deep in a lake in the south of these woods, kwwwwwwrk. She watches over the flame there. There's a problem, though. The gate that leads to that lake is sealed shut to all those judged unworthy... But don't worry, kwwwwrk. I'll let you in on the trick to opening the gate.|Yerbal|SS}}</ref>

The Lake is inhabited by the squid-like [[Parella]] tribe, who are protected by the Water Dragon [[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]]. She was entrusted with a part of the [[Song of the Hero]] and protects [[Farore's Flame]].<ref>{{Cite|The goddess gave the flame to the Water Dragon, a great spirit that presides over Faron Woods. The way I hear it is she left the flame in the care of the Water Dragon so that one day the mighty dragon could pass it on to the "one from the sky"...|Yerbal|SS}}</ref> However, shortly before Link arrived, Lake Floria was invaded by [[Ghirahim]] and his force of aggressive beasts known as [[Cranioc]],<ref>{{Cite|Yikes! Oh, they're back! That's one of the fish that weirdo brought in here and used to attack Her Excellence. Watch out--they're mean as they come!|Parella|SS}}</ref> and Faron was injured as a result.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, about that... It happened really recently. This weirdo came in here with a gang of monsters and went right after Her Excellence. My lady the Water Dragon is a fierce fighter, and, boy, did she gobble up most of those monsters in the first few seconds. Thing is, there were just so many of them! While she was distracted, the weirdo leading all those creatures managed to wound her. She's healing up now, but she says we don't have enough special water for her to make a full recovery.|Parella|SS}}</ref>

Near the Lake is the [[Ancient Cistern]], a water-themed dungeon where Link can obtain the [[Whip]] and Farore's Flame.

==={{BotW|-}}===
Lake Floria is located in [[West Necluda]]. Lake Floria is fed by 10 waterfalls of varying sizes to the north,<ref>{{Cite|Did you check out the falls at Lake Floria? There are 10 different falls there, big and small! Each one is unique and absolutely worth a look.|Cima|BotW}}</ref> and one from the south.<ref>{{Cite| Don’t you think the waterfall flowing down from Lake Floria is the very picture of perfection? There’s something strange at the {{Color|BotW Red|top of that waterfall}}, though... Climb the ladder on the watchtower and take a peek. You’ll probably need a telescope or something for a good look.|Kampo|BotW}}</ref> The Lake itself feeds into the [[Floria River]], which runs westward. Lake Floria is bisected by the [[Floria Bridge]], which connects the eastern [[Ebara Forest]] with the [[Sarjon Woods]] in the west. The [[Dragon]] [[Farosh]] is frequently seen flying over the Lake.<ref>{{Cite|Recently, some are even saying they’ve seen the shadow of a large creature on the surface of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Floria}}! I wonder if any of that is true...|Shay|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What? Me? My name is Totsuna. I’m a huge fan of pretty much anything and everything related to dragons! I had a strange feeling when I was near {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Floria}}... It felt exactly like how I’d imagine being near a dragon would feel!|Totsuna|BotW}}</ref>
