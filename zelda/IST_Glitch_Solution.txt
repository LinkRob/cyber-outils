I tried to do the IST (Inventory Slot Transfer) to get a stack of 999 diamonds, but something went wrong and I instead ended up with 7 stacks of spirit orbs (all set to 11, the amount I had before.)
I looked all over for a fix and couldn't find anything so I had to start testing myself

So basically to get rid of the stacks so you can do future IST glitches, sell your hearts to the cursed statue in Hateno village, and then go to one of the statues and buy the hearts back. You get rupees and you get rid of all the extra hearts. Even once the counter in the top right says you have less than 4 or even 0, just ask it for another heart/stamina as long as you have another stack of glitched orbs.
