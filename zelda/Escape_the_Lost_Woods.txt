{{Term/Store|Escape the Lost Woods||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 35 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Escape the Lost Woods|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Escape the Lost Woods}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given eight minutes to reach an Escape Point. It can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Endura Carrot|link}}. At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} enters an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} with closed gates. To keep moving forward, the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} must be captured. At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, there is only one {{Term|HWAoC|Stalmoblin}} located in the entire map, but more spawn as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} gets closer to the Escape Point.  There are also several {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}} and closed passages that appear to be open on the map, which may prevent the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} from getting to the Escape Point.    
Once the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the area directly in front of the Escape Point, the path is blocked by gates and a {{Term|HWAoC|Stalnox}} spawns. The gates open when the {{Term|HWAoC|Stalnox}} is defeated.
