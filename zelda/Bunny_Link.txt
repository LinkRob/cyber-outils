'''Bunny Link''' is a form of [[Link]] in {{ALttP}},{{Name Ref|ALttP}} appearing as a fluffy anthropomorphic pink [[rabbit]] wearing a [[Green Clothes|green tunic]]. Link is transformed into this shape as a result of entering the [[Dark World]], the corrupted version of the [[Sacred Realm]] by [[Ganon]]'s heart, which changes people to reflect their true nature.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone who wanders into this land is changed to a shape that reflects his true nature.|Bully's Friend|ALttP}}</ref>

As a bunny, unlike other [[Transformation|transformations]] seen in later games (primarily {{MM}}), although cute-looking, Link is completely defenseless and cannot use any of his weapons or his [[Shield]]. He can also not lift pots. However, he can still collect items. Therefore, he must avoid enemies and other hazards. The young hero can retain his original [[Hylian]] form after obtaining the [[Moon Pearl]] from the [[Tower of Hera (A Link to the Past)|Tower of Hera]], the third and final [[dungeon]] of the [[Light World]] ([[Hyrule]]).<ref>{{Cite|The Moon Pearl will protect its bearer from the magical air of the Golden Land, so you can keep your human shape there.|Great Fairy|ALttP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|An orb known as the Moon Pearl rests in this tower. He who holds the Moon Pearl is protected, and his form will not change in the Dark World.|Sahasrahla|ALttP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But if you have a ball called the Moon Pearl, you can keep your original shape here.|Bully's Friend|ALttP}}</ref>

==Transformation==
When Link climbs [[Death Mountain#A Link to the Past|Death Mountain]] on his quest to find the three [[Pendants of Virtue]], he finds a portal in the north-east side of the mountain that teleports him to the Dark World's counterpart. Without the Moon Pearl, Link finds himself transformed into Bunny Link. A [[Bully]] and [[Bully's Friend|his friend]] nearby explains to him how the world transforms anyone who enters in reflection of their true nature. Once Link obtains the Moon Pearl from the Tower of Hera, he will no longer transform into Bunny Link whenever he enters the Dark World.

In certain Dark World dungeons, Link can temporarily be re-transformed into a rabbit regardless if he has the Moon Pearl by coming in contact with rotating, golden star-shaped objects called [[Rabbit Beam]]s. However, this change wears off after five seconds or after being hit by an enemy.

It is likely Link changes into a bunny in the Dark World due to the fact that the hero's heart is pure and he travels far and wide in his adventure, which [[wikipedia:Rabbit|real-world rabbits]] often do and are viewed as pure animals in some cultures.

==Trivia==
*Ironically, [[Rabbit|actual rabbits]] can be found in {{aLttP}} and some later games of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]].
*When [[Link]] is transformed into Bunny Link for the first time at [[Dark World]]'s [[Death Mountain]], a unique theme song is heard. When he obtains the [[Moon Pearl]], it then begins to play its standard theme.
*According to [[Takashi Tezuka]], who was the director of {{aLttP|-}}, the reason for the creation of Link's Bunny form is he wanted to make a striking distinction between what Link normally looks like, and therefore create a severe distinction in his looks. He also said "''If I think of sort of a fantastic dream world, one of the first things I think about are rabbits.''"
*Link as his Bunny form is similar to the [[Animal]] characters seen in [[Animal Village]] from {{LA}}.[[File:ALTTP Bunny Link and Bully.png|thumb|214x214px|The Bully making fun of Bunny Link in {{aLttP|-}}]]
*In the English releases of {{aLttP|-}}, when Bunny Link talks to the [[Bully]], the hero is referred to as "silly rabbit". This can be a reference to the mascot of the [[wikipedia:Trix_(cereal)|Trix breakfest cereal]], Trix Rabbit, who is commonly known as Silly Rabbit.
*[[Ravio]] from {{ALBW}} may be a homage to Bunny Link, as his mask has a rabbit motif and he originates from [[Lorule]], a land that is nearly identical to the Dark World. The ''[[:en:The_Legend_of_Zelda_(series)|Zelda Encyclopedia]]'' confirms this was indeed the inspiration.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 302}}</ref>
*If Link obtains the [[Blue Mail|Blue]] or [[Red Mail]], his bunny form will also wear said Tunic, though only by [[Glitches in A Link to the Past|glitches]].
*Some video game franchises and other media have featured heroic bunny characters that are pure of heart in a similar manner as Bunny Link. The most famous examples include [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Cream_the_Rabbit Cream the Rabbit] or [https://hellokitty.fandom.com/wiki/My_Melody My Melody] from the ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_series Sonic the Hedgehog]'' and ''[https://hellokitty.fandom.com/f Hello Kitty]'' series, respectively.
