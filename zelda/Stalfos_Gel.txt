{{Term/Store|Stalfos Gel|Stalfos Gels|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite Internal|quote= Yellow Stalfos Gel |game= CoH |system= Nintendo Switch |version= 1.0.0 |country= United States of America}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Stalfos Gel}} are enemies that move to the beat of the music. They move on every single beat, making their way towards {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}, using their gelatinous bodies to knock them backwards until they got them pinned against a wall or other enemy, dealing damage. They only appear in caves and dungeons.

==Trivia==
They behave identically to the Shove Monster from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
