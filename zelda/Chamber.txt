The {{Term/Store|Chamber|Chambers|Series, LANS|plural}} are objects in {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got some new {{Color|LANS Pink|chambers}}! Slot them into the dungeon to see what's inside!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|LANS|Chamber}} are created by {{Term|LANS|Dampé|link}} from {{Plural|LANS|Chamber Stone|link}}.<ref name="Ruins">{{Cite|Hey, there you are. I wanna show you somethin' interesting. You've gone pokin' into some of the ruins on this island. I can tell. I make little {{Color|LANS Pink|chambers}} based on those dungeons. Put some chambers into the slab back there and it turns into a real dungeon. With a few chambers, you can make basic dungeons. With a bunch, you can make complicated ones. Though you're not done once you finish arranging a dungeon. You've gotta adventure through it too.|Dampé|LANS}}</ref> They are replicas of various rooms within the {{Plural|LANS|Dungeon|link}} around {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}.<ref name="Ruins"/> They can be slotted into the slab within {{Term|LANS|Dampé's Shack|link}} to create custom {{Plural|LANS|Chamber Dungeon|link}} that {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} can explore. {{Plural|LANS|Chamber}} can be augmented with {{Plural|LANS|+Effect Panel|link}} to imbue certain attributes in a given {{Term|LANS|Chamber}}, such as a volley of {{Plural|LANS|Bomb|link}} falling from the ceiling or {{Term|LANS|Shadow Link|link}} appearing in certain rooms to attack {{Term|LANS|Link}}.
