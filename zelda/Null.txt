'''Null''' is the main antagonist and final boss of {{EoW}}. 

==Background==
Null is a primordial cosmic entity who predated [[Creation of the World]] by the [[Golden Goddess]]es. It is revealed that the land of [[Hyrule]] and the [[Light World]] were created by the Goddesses in order to seal away Null and put an end to the chaos it was causing. This action sealed Null underneath the Light World and caused it to resent the Golden Goddesses. The Golden Goddesses then created the [[Tris]] to keep Null contained beneath the world by fixing [[Rift]]s created by Null to destroy the Light World and consume its inhabitants, while reverting the world back to the void it was before.

Many years later, in the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|"Downfall" timeline]], Null manages to seal away the Tris, leaving the land of Hyrule defenseless against the Rifts. One of the Tris manages to evade Null and meets up with [[Princess Zelda]]. Zelda and [[Tri]] set off on an adventure to save Hyrule and Link from the rifts, which they believe are being created by [[Echo Ganon|Ganon]]. After defeating a seemingly-revived Ganon at Stilled Hyrule Castle, it is revealed that the "Ganon" they have been fighting was an Echo created by Null, who proceeds to kidnap Link when he prevents it from capturing Zelda.

During Zelda's journey to seal the Rifts at locations tied to the Prime Energy — later revealed to be the [[Triforce]] — she receives a sanction from one of the Goddesses with each major Rift she seales, and learns about Null the second time she accomplishes this. After gathering all three Goddess Sanctions, Zelda is attacked by Null, creating an [[Tekom|Echo of Zelda]] to deceive [[The Great Deku Tree]] and gain access to the Prime Energy.

However, Null's heart is unbalanced and only obtains one piece of the Prime Energy (the Triforce of Power), with the other two pieces going to Link and Zelda. After a battle inside [[Null's Body]], Link, Zelda, and Tri recover the Triforce of Power and wish for Null's destruction, which occurs immediately.

==Appearance==
[[File:Null's Death.webp|thumb|Null's Death]]
During the final Boss fight of ''Echoes of Wisdom'', after absorbing one of three pieces of the Prime Energy, Null appears as a large, dark purple shell, in the shape of a Tri, with three glowing pink, tendril-like arms with long dark purple hands. As the fight progresses, Null's shell will crack, revealing a gooey purple and pink interior which will also open up to show the [[Triforce of Power]] in the center of Null. Null will also gain more arms as it takes more damage. Null's interior under the shell is adorned with strange shards of the same material that its shell is made of. These shards look strangely like masks.
[[File:Null - Goddesses Story.webp|left|thumb|Null in the story of the Golden Goddesses]]
[[File:Hyrule Castle Null.png|200px|thumb|Null before absorbing the Prime Energy]]
During the Golden Goddesses' story about the sealing of Null, it is shown as a swirling black ball-like shape with two grey eyes. This form is likely Null's true form.

Null reveals itself to Zelda and Tri about halfway through the story of ''Echoes of Wisdom'', after [[Echo Ganon]] is defeated at Stilled Hyrule Castle. At this point, it hasn't yet absorbed the Prime Energy and appears as a cloudy, dark ball of mist with two clawed hands similar to the hands it has in its boss fight.

[[File:EoW Triforce.jpg|right|220px|thumb|Link and Zelda obtaining the completed “Prime Energy”]]
After Link and Zelda extract the Triforce of Power from Null’s core, Null is left defeated. The duo then makes a wish on the Prime Energy, mending the [[Rift|rifts]] and assumably destroying Null, although not before Null futilely begs them for the Prime Energy.

==Boss Battle==
'''Null''' is the [[Final Boss]] of Echoes of Wisdom and fought at the end of the final dungeon, Null's Body. Link and Zelda fight together, with Link controlled by the game's AI and possessing infinite health.

===Phase 1===
Null's primary attack is simply slapping the floor with his hand, causing a small damaging rift to appear that will be initially aimed at Zelda, but is avoidable. This attack instantly destroys all Echoes.

There are several ways to destroy every hand and thus stun Null:

*The most effective method is using Bind to pull Null's hand out, stretching the tendons for Link to Spin Attack and instantly sever it.
*Damaging it normally by summoning Echoes and/or letting Link do the work will eventually destroy the hand.
[[File:2025051821400000-CD8DDEDFE332FE055C049150B035121B.jpg|thumb|258x258px|Zelda trapped in Null's hand cage while Link attempts to free her.]]
After all three hands are destroyed, Null will collapse and be left stunned for a moment. If enough damage is done, Null will crack slightly and exit the arena, summoning three of its hands to send rifts at Zelda. A hand will also occasionally track Zelda's position in a bid to trap her; if successful, it will trap Zelda leaving her vulnerable to enemy attack and having to depend on echoes or Link to free her as she can't free herself. Once she is free she will be sent flying and need to recover for a few moments. This attack doesn't affect Link at all.

When all three of those hands are destroyed, the cycle will repeat twice more, except Null will behave more aggressively during each segment. Null will also slam the ground and create an area of effect which damages Link or Zelda. 

===Phase 2===
A cutscene will play where Null exits the arena, with Zelda and Tri following closely. Link will be barred by several of Null's hands.

The next underwater segment will involve Null capturing Tris and generating three hands that each hold a Tri, in order to summon dark Echoes to attack Zelda. During the fight, bubbles will appear to supply Zelda's oxygen. Null will summon Vocavors to generate waterspouts and attempt to damage Zelda, as well as Biri to shock her and Spiked Urchins to impede her movement. Using Chompfins along with pulling Null's hands is highly recommended.

Destroying Null's hands enough will proceed to phase 3, after Zelda falls in front of Link and Null shows itself in a more damaged form, along with three hands with Tris and two empty hands.

===Phase 3===
The next segment plays out similar to the first phase, but with the added danger of Null exiting the arena to use boss Echoes to try to kill Zelda and Link:

*A series of four (later six) Echo Ganons which stab towards Zelda, sequentially.
*Two (later three) Skorchills, which use the freeze spin.
*Two Mogryphs summon giant tornados. Later, a Gohma will spit webs to make it harder to navigate.
*A Seismic Talus spins its hands around, sometimes in the middle of the Ganon sequence.

To stop Null exiting, the player must destroy the hands with Tris in them first. After this, Null's empty hands will attack like in Phase 1. Destroy them to expose Null and destroy his masks one by one.

In the second part of the phase, Null's hands pop out of the walls to summon dark Echoes. First it will be only Darknuts, later it will be Lizalfos and Boarblins. The hand will also pursue Zelda at times.

Eventually, Null will collapse with the Triforce of Power exposed. Using Bind and pulling will initiate a Real Time Interaction where Link and Zelda pull, requiring the player to mash A in order to pull it out. This concludes the fight.

==Trivia==
*It should be noted that during the underwater scene with Null, he can take damage, but he will have infinite health until he reaches a certain point, where a single hit will proceed the battle to the next phase.
*While Link can be frozen and hit by enemies and Null, he cannot die, as he does not have a health bar to deplete.
*If the player gets caught by Null's hand prison, Link will try and protect Zelda and also free her, mirroring their relationship in other games.
*Due to Null existing prior to Demise, Null is the only great evil in the ''Zelda'' franchise confirmed to not be a byproduct of the [[Curse of Demise]] (as Ganondorf, and by extension, Ganon were confirmed to be byproducts, and the Japanese version of the curse vaguely implies most of the great evil characters such as Vaati and Bellum were byproducts as well).
