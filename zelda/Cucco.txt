==Characteristics==
[[File:Cucco Revenge.jpg|thumb|230px|A group of Cuccos attacking Link from {{aLttP|-}}]]
Despite appearing as mere chickens, Cuccos are infamously known for aggressively attacking [[Link]] in retaliation if he attacks them for an extended period of time, and are sometimes known as the "Cucco's Revenge Squad".

==Mainline appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:Chicken.gif|left|20px]]
[[File:LttP undeadcucoo.gif|left|20px]]
In their first appearance in {{aLttP}}, Cuccos could be found in [[Kakariko Village]]. This game also introduced their vengeful "Revenge Squad" behavior. Cucco-like monsters appear in the [[Dark World]], reacting identically to their [[Light World]] counterparts, including the revenge squad attack.

==={{LA|-}}===
[[File:LADX Cucco Sprite.png|left|30px]]
Cuccos in {{LA}}, unlike most other games, cannot be lifted or thrown without the aid of the [[Power Bracelet]], and could mainly be found in [[Mabe Village]]. They can be killed using the [[Magic Powder]] or [[Magic Rod]]. Because of this, {{LA|-}} is the only title in {{TLoZ|-}} series which Cuccos can be destroyed by Link. When Cuccos are eliminated by the hero, the revenge squad will disappear and stop attacking. The ability to kill Cuccos is removed in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Switch re-release]] of the game.

On [[Koholint Island]]'s [[Tal Tal Heights|mountain range]], a hen house can be found housing the [[Cucco Keeper]] and several Cuccos.

==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT3D Cucco Icon.png|left]]
[[File:Cuccos.jpg|thumb|right|Link surrounded by Cuccos while carrying one in {{OoT|-}}|229x229px]]
Cuccos could be found in a variety of places in {{OoT}}, and again attacked Link in swarms if struck. As a new feature, Link could grab a Cucco and use it to glide slowly when jumping off a cliff. This technique is known as [[#Cucco Gliding|Cucco Gliding]], and can be used several times throughout the game to earn some [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]]. Unlike the [[Flying Rooster]], Cuccos grabbed in this and later games cause Link to slowly glide downward instead of fly upwards. In all versions of {{OoT|-}} prior to {{OoT3D}}, Cuccos were also referred to as '''Chickens'''.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT Light Blue|Look! A}} chicken {{Color|OoT Light Blue|hatched from the}} egg {{Color|OoT Light Blue|you were incubating! It's the miracle of life!}}|Navi|OoT}}</ref> The sound of a Cucco is heard when a day passes.

[[File:OoT3D Weird Egg Icon.png|left]]
When the young [[Hero of Time]] first encounters [[Malon]] in the [[Market|Town Market]] she tells him of her father who had come to [[Hyrule Castle]] to deliver milk but probably fell asleep whilst doing so.<ref>{{Cite|Are you going to the castle, fairy boy? Would you mind finding my dad? He must have fallen asleep somewhere around the castle. What a thing for an adult to do! Tee hee!|Malon|OoT}}</ref> She offers Link a [[Weird Egg]] that she had been incubating in return for looking for him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh yeah, if you'll look for him, I'll give this to you. I've been incubating this egg very carefully... Tee hee!|Malon|OoT}}</ref> After a day passes, a Cucco will hatch from the egg.<ref>{{Cite|Look! A Cucco hatched from the egg you were incubating! It's the miracle of life!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref> The Cucco is necessary for Link to awaken [[Talon]] beside the castle.<ref>{{Cite|What in tarnation? Can't a person get a little shut-eye around here?|Talon|OoT}}</ref>

The [[Cucco Lady]], who can be found in [[Kakariko Village]], requests that Link helps return her missing Cuccos to their pen. If this task is accomplished, Link can earn a [[Bottle]]. When Link returns later, the Cuccos are found to be out of their pen once more. He can complete the task as many times as he returns to the village. The Cucco Lady will instead give him fifty [[Rupee]]s each time.

A second Bottle, filled with [[Lon Lon Milk]], can be won in [[Talon's Chicken Game]]. This [[Mini-Game|mini-game]] is only accessible as a child at [[Lon Lon Ranch]], and requires Link to pick Talon's three {{Plural|OoT3D|Super Cucco|link}} from among a brood of regular Cuccos.<ref>{{Cite|Well how about a little game? These three Cuccos I have here are special Super Cuccos! I'm going to throw these Cuccos into that there gaggle of normal Cuccos. If you can pick out these three special birds from among the normal Cuccos within the time limit, I'll give you something good.|Talon|OoT}}</ref> There is no discernible difference in appearance or behavior between these Super Cuccos and standard Cuccos, so finding them is mostly out of luck.

{{OoT|-}} also features a unique specimen named [[Cojiro]], the only known blue Cucco, and a smaller variant known as the [[Pocket Cucco]].

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Cuccos appear and function the same as in {{OoT|-}}. The [[Henhouse|Cucco Shack]] at [[Romani Ranch]] is home to a number of Cucco chicks, which Link helps to mature into adulthood using the [[Bremen Mask]]. There is also one Cucco in the [[Barn]]. However, unlike the game's prequel, other than maturing the baby Cuccos to obtain the [[Bunny Hood]], Cuccos serve no purpose in this game and cannot effectively be used for gliding. As in {{OoT|-}}, the sound of a Cucco is heard when a day passes.

Cuccos in {{OoS}} appear similarly and function identically to those found in {{LA|-}}, however they cannot be killed. An interesting attribute of Cuccos unique to {{OoS|-}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Oracle of Ages]]'' is the effect [[Mystery Seed]]s have on them. If a Cucco has not called in the Revenge Squad when it is hit by a Mystery Seed, it will be reverted to a baby form of itself which will continue to swarm Link. However, this baby form cannot damage the hero.

If the Cucco has called in the Revenge Squad, then it will instead triple in size, hurting Link through physical contact until he leaves. When a Cucco is enlarged, it becomes impossible to pick up, and will immediately begin to attack Link if he attacks it again.

==={{TMC|-}}===

The Cuccos of {{TMC}} are mostly the same as in {{ALttP|-}}, though there are golden Cuccos that appear only for a mini-game along with small yellow Cucco Chicks. The Cuccos play a part in a mini game where Link has to capture the Cuccos before the time runs out. The golden Cuccos are the most hyper and will escape the Link's grasp much more frequently than the white Cuccos. If Link hits a Cucco when it is trapped in a corner more than about 20 times, it will run around the whole of [[Hyrule Castle Town|Hyrule Town]] and may cause Link harm. The Cucco chicks will attack Link if he is [[Minish]]-sized. Link can [[Kinstone Fusion|fuse]] [[Kinstone]]s with the Cucco chicks on [[Swiftblade]]'s house if he is in Minish form.

==={{TP|-}}===
[[File:TP Cucco Model.png|left|100px]]
Cuccos in {{TP}} come in white, brown, and other realistic chicken colors. When attacked repeatedly, the Cuccos no longer call in the revenge squad. Instead, attacking them allows Link to control the Cucco for ten seconds, similarly to how [[Seagull]]s could be controlled with [[Hyoi Pear]]s in {{TWW}}. At one point, [[Rusl]] lends link a [[Golden Cucco]] that allows him to fly over to the [[Sacred Grove]].

The [[Oocca]] are very similar to Cuccos in appearance and name. When Link grabs one in [[City in the Sky]], they can even be used to [[#Cucco Gliding|glide]] like a regular Cucco.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
[[File:ALBW Lorule Cucco Model.png|left|60px]]
In {{ALBW}}, Cuccos appear in [[Kakariko Village]] and directly to its south, where the [[Cucco Girl]] runs the [[Cucco Ranch]] minigame. In it, Link is tasked with avoiding large numbers of Cuccos of different sizes in a given amount of time to win [[Rupee]]s and a [[Piece of Heart]]. Cuccos can also be used to glide to places that cannot normally be reached, such as rooftops; several [[Items in A Link Between Worlds|Items]] must be obtained this way. Cuccos in [[Lorule]] sport dark feathers and bald pink heads, reminiscent of [[Vulture]]s, but behave identically to regular Cuccos.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, Cuccos appear in the [[Sky Realm]]. They are primarily used for gliding from one platform to another. If one of the [[Links]] attacks a Cucco repeatedly, then instead of calling upon a squad of Cuccos to attack him, the Cucco will turn orange and behave erratically for a short time while attacking any Links that draws near, before becoming docile again.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Cucco}}
[[File:BotW_Cucco_Model.png|left|60px]]
In {{BotW}}, Cuccos are often seen in populated areas such as towns and [[Stable]]s. They will peacefully wander around, occasionally pecking at the ground, presumably in search of insects. Like in previous games, if Link leaps off a high surface, they will allow him to glide short distances. When gliding for significant distances with a Cucco, Link's [[Stamina Gauge]] will deplete.

Cuccos are mostly raised for their [[Bird Egg|Egg]]s, but they are sometimes kept as pets by the people of [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|Villages often raise these birds for their eggs, but some people actually keep them as pets.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> If Link attempts to hit them with a weapon, they will squawk and attempt to flee, sometimes dropping Bird Eggs when this happens. However, if Link does this four times, a cutscene will play where the Cucco crows for their revenge squad, much like {{OoT|-}}, but the Cucco can be seen repeatedly jumping and dropping feathers, and after the cutscene ends, the harassed Cucco will summon a swarm of Cuccos. Comically, Cuccos will also swarm and attack a monster if the Cucco is attacked once by that monster. While under attack from this swarm, it is possible for Link to rest at a [[Campfire]] or [[Cooking Pot]]. Doing so will effectively end the attack from the swarm. The swarm will also end if Link goes inside a building. The Cucco who crowed for the revenge squad will not move at all until the swarm ends, instead repeatedly doing the same animation of crowing for the revenge squad, as if crowing for more of their revenge squad.

==Specialized behavior==
===Cucco's Revenge Squad===
[[File:BotW Cucco attack.gif|thumb|220x220px|The Cucco's Revenge Squad in {{BotW}}]]
Ever since the introduction of the Cucco in {{ALttP|-}}, if [[Link]] attacks a Cucco enough times with his [[Sword]] or other weapons, a large group of other indestructible Cuccos will furiously attack him in an attempt to protect their brethren; this flock of pestered poultry is rightly named the '''Cucco's Revenge Squad'''. In the 2D games attacking a Cucco up to 20 times will summon the attacking flock, while three-dimensional titles may take only a few strikes. The flock will continuously attack Link until he either perishes, waits 10 seconds for them to fly away or leaves the area, as Cuccos cannot follow Link into different maps. However, in {{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}}, the attacking flock will stay around forever until Link either dies or leaves the area.

In {{ALttP|-}}, Cuccos appear in both the [[Light World]] and the [[Dark World]], making it possible for the attacking flock to appear in both worlds.

In {{OoT|-}}, they appear in [[Lon Lon Ranch]], [[Kakariko Village]], and [[Zora's River]]. There are some, but few areas in which Link cannot get hurt by the Cucco's Revenge Squad, in which the Hero of Time is still in the specific place. Some of these areas are, but are not limited to the Well in Kakariko Village from {{OoT|-}}, and a few steps back from the cage in the [[Astral Observatory]] from {{MM|-}}. Link also cannot be harmed by Cuccos if he is on [[Epona]]. In {{OoT|-}}, when a Cucco has been hit enough times, a cutscene will follow where Link will look around in slight frustration and the Cucco will crow for the revenge squad. If a Cucco is hit by even a single blast from a [[Bomb]] or is struck by a [[Spin Attack]] (due to their continuous hitbox), it will immediately crow for them. They will also crow if Epona were to run directly over them. The original Cucco that crowed for the flock can also attack Link, though it's distinguished by itself charging toward him at a slightly slower speed than the others and does not fly. The revenge squad in the [[Nintendo 64]] installments are notably more dangerous than in most other games, as they inflict one full [[Heart Container]] in damage rather than half and even knocks Link down to the ground.

In {{LA|-}}, ''Oracle of Seasons'', and ''Oracle of Ages'', the squad also makes an appearance, protecting their injured brethren from further harm from Link. 

While Cuccos are missing in {{TWW|-}}, [[Pig]]s also attack Link in a similar manner when provoked. Any nearby Pigs will begin attacking the young hero as well, but unlike Cuccos, more will not spawn.

The Cucco Revenge Squad do not appear in {{TP|-}}, as attacking a Cucco many times in this game will result in being able to control the Cucco for about 10 seconds. The reason for the Revenge Squad's absence is unknown. However, when herding them, if Link whoops at them too much they will turn red, turn around and knock Link off of Epona. Ordon Goats from [[Ordon Village]] also serve the same purpose as the Cuccos. When possessing a Cucco, [[Cat]]s will attack it.

The squad returned in {{PH|-}} and {{ST|-}}, and subsequent games afterward. In {{BotW|-}}, certain enemies can be tricked into attacking Cuccos, which will result in the flock attacking the enemy, instead, and potentially defeat it.

===Cucco Gliding===
[[File:TP Cucco Model.png|right|120px]]
Cucco Gliding originated in {{LA|-}} and can be seen in a few other games such as {{OoT|-}}, {{TP|-}}, and {{PH|-}}. In order to glide, Link must grab a Cucco, run off a ledge, and he will glide safely to the ground with the Cucco squirming and frantically flapping its wings.

Cucco gliding is used primarily to gain access to [[Eagle's Tower]] by helping Link acquire the [[Bird Key]], using the Flying Rooster. [[Flight-by-Fowl]] in {{TP|-}} focuses on Cucco gliding. In {{TFH|-}}, Cucco gliding is used in some areas to progress forward in the Sky Realm.

===Cucco Steal===
In {{FSA}}, by using a [[Crystal Ball]] a special Cucco behavior called the {{Name|Fan|Cucco Steal}} can be triggered. When activated, a summoned Cucco will fly around, steal an item from an opposing player, and bring it back to the player who threw the Crystal Ball. The item grabbed will differ across game modes; it will steal a [[Force Gem]] if playing in [[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures#Hyrulean Adventure|Hyrulean Adventure]] or a [[Heart]] if playing in [[Shadow Battle]]. Aside from traveling to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures#Gameplay|opposite screen]] of the offensive player, there is no way to evade becoming a possible target.

In {{HW}}, Cuccos appear as neutral units on the battlefields, usually with only one spawning. When attacked and having been damaged enough, they will turn hostile and start spawning more Cuccos that will follow and attack the player's Warrior. Unlike many previous appearances of Cuccos, they are not invincible and can be defeated. However, they have a significantly high amount of health and high attack power. A golden Cucco, which serves as an enemy general, may usually be summoned among the third wave of Cuccos.

In some [[Adventure Mode]] battles, a pair of Cuccos may fight over a Keep until it is claimed. In this situation, the player's Warrior may choose to quell the fight before a victor can be declared. If the Cuccos are not calmed, the victor will immediately capture the Keep, and populate it with an army of Cuccos. This army will then begin to campaign towards other Keeps, sending Cuccos as raid parties until the Cucco army has captured a total of three Keeps. Some Adventure Mode

A different Adventure Mode battle involves having to escort a Cucco chick to its mother, the Mama Cucco, while keeping it safe from enemies in the process. Escorting the Cucco chick back to its mother will summon Cucco reinforcements to join the Allied Force. However, if the chick is not teamed up with Mama Cucco in time, the Cucco will turn hostile.

In the Master Quest, Twilight, and Termina maps, as well as in the Boss Challenge and Ganon's Fury modes, a rare silver Cucco variant will sometimes randomly appear in certain battles. Upon defeat, it grants 10,000 Rupees.{{Verify|HW}}

The Boss DLC Pack also introduces an unlockable [[Challenge Mode]] called Cucco's Fury, which features a playable, giant Cucco. This mode is unlocked after clearing Survival Mode Lv. 3 of Ganon's Fury with an A-Rank. Unlike other characters, the giant Cucco is extremely limited in its moveset, consisting of only a two-hit pecking attack as its normal combo and a tackle as its Strong Attack. Its [[Special Attack]] summons flocks of Cuccos to stampede enemies around a wide area. It also cannot use items. Similarly to [[Ganon]] in Ganon's Fury, the Cucco cannot be upgraded at the {{Term|HW|Bazaar|link}} and does not gain new weapons. Despite this, in-game data shows that it is equipped with a weapon called Cucco's Spirit, which is of the Light element.

One of [[Lana]]'s special attacks with the Summoning Gates can also summon a giant Cucco to attack enemies.

Cuccos also assist Linkle in some of her attacks with the [[Boots]].

In {{HWL}}, the first chapter of [[Linkle]]'s story has her leading an army of Cuccos in order to recover her stolen compass from the [[Skull Kid]] and to stop [[Ghirahim]] from summoning the fully revived version of [[The Imprisoned]].

==Trivia==
*It is most likely Cuccos are in fact roosters, as they are known for their trademark "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_6amRooster.wav Cocka-doodle-doo!]" sound. In the Japanese releases, they are also known as '''Alarm Clock Rooster'''.
*Although there has never been an explanation by [[Nintendo]] about why Cuccos or certain other [[Animal|Animals]] eventually attack [[Link]], it is commonly presumed by fans that the gameplay mechanic is a warning about the real-world consequences of [[wikipedia:Cruelty_to_animals|animal abuse]].
*The Cucco's Revenge Squad is relatively accurate to how real-life chickens attack dangerous threats, especially by roosters. They are known to flog by jumping at a person and flap its wings to disorient them followed by aggressively pecking and spurring with their sharp legs.
*In {{ALBW}}, the [[Lorulean]] [[Fortune's Choice]] Guy will tell Link "not to Cucco out on him,"<ref>{{Cite|C’mon, don’t Cucco out on me. Wanna try your luck for 200 Rupees?|Fortune's Choice Guy|ALBW}}</ref> a play on the phrase "don't chicken out on me," which is a reference to the Cuccos being [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]]' counterpart to chickens. Similarly, in {{PH}}, [[Ciela]] refers to [[Linebeck]] multiple times as a Cucco for his cowardly tendencies,<ref>{{Cite|We made it to the port! It's so much farther to walk when the bridge is out! Now that we're here, let's look up Linebeck! I've met him once or twice. Can't say I care for him. He's an awfully big talker for such a big cucco! OK, let's head to the port!|Ciela|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What?! Is Linebeck shaking over there?! He's such a cucco!|Ciela|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ahhh! I get it. Linebeck's nothing but a frozen, clucking cucco... Why don't you wait there, shivering. Just get real comfortable. Maybe you'll lay an egg! BA-GAWWWK!|Ciela|PH}}</ref> and {{BotW}} also continues this tradition with several NPCs.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, you are much like a Cucco. First you fly, and now you cluck. Most intriguing. I look forward to seeing you again.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sesami is such a Cucco.|Canolo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, guy! Where are you going?! The trial is thataway! You're not Cuccoing out, are ya?|Damia|BotW}}</ref> However, they seem to be aware of chickens' existence, as evidenced by [[Cado]] in {{BotW|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|A Cucco that can't fly would be...just a glorified chicken! How boring!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> The only difference between chickens and Cuccos is that Cuccos are able to glide while held above the head.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I'm sorry. I suppose I never told you that I look after Cuccos. Aren't they amazing? They're beautiful creatures who can fly you anywhere!|Cado|BotW}}</ref>
*In {{OoT}} and {{MM}}, during the Revenge Squad attack, if Link were to look up to the sky, due to the hardware limitations of the [[Nintendo 64]], he will see Cuccos spawning one by one directly over his head but will not reach him as they go out of view. They also cannot harm him if the player switches to first person view while standing at a high place where the Cucco that crowed for the flock cannot reach him.
*The only way to kill a Cucco in {{BotW|-}} is by throwing it into lava.
*The sound of [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Incarnation]] as it runs around during the final battle from {{MM|-}} is actually that of Cuccos clucking.

==Nomenclature==
The name "Cucco" is most probably an onomatopoeia of the sound a chicken makes.
