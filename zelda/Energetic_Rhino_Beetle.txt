==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Energetic Rhino Beetle}}
{{Plural|BotW|Energetic Rhino Beetle}} can be throughout the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}, particularly on {{Plural|BotW|Trees|link}} near {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}} and on pillars in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. They are also sold by the traveling merchant {{Term|BotW|Bugut|link}}, who travels between {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}. Combining them in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} will result in an {{Term|BotW|Energizing Elixir|link}};<ref name="Inventory"/> however, {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} will also accept an {{Term|BotW|Energetic Rhino Beetle}} in exchange for a random {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Elixir|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|P-p-please! Would you please consider giving your {{Color|BotW Blue|energetic rhino beetle}} to Beedle?!|Beedle|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} can use {{Plural|BotW|Energetic Rhino Beetle}} as ingredients for {{Term|BotW|Yellow Dye|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Energetic Rhino Beetle}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}:
