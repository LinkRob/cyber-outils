The {{Term/Store|Bass Guitarmos Knight|Bass Guitarmos Knights|Series, CoH|plural}} are a {{Term|CoH|Boss|link}} in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bass Guitarmos Knights|N/A|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Stub|CoH}}
The {{Plural|CoH|Bass Guitarmos Knights}} are loosely based on the {{Plural|ALttP|Armos Knight|link}} from {{ALttP}}, but wield bass guitars instead of {{Plural|Series|Sword|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Shield|link}}.

All five Knights move and attack in various predetermined patterns, based on the background music. When the four smaller Knights are defeated, the Bass Guitarmos Knight becomes more aggressive.
