==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Emma}} is the mother of {{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Lolly|link}},<ref name="E 271"/> and is the proprietor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hi there, and welcome to the Happy Hearth! Need a rest, kid? Well, we can take care of that--and even throw in a {{Color|TMC Red|present}} to boot!|Emma|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} possesses {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|display=Inn}} opens, and {{Term|TMC|Emma}} remains there for the rest of the game.
