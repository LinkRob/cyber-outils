{{Term/Store|Sorcerer's Curse|Sorcerer's Curses|Series, TMC|plural}},<ref>{{Cite|Tell me, how does it feel, my {{Color|TMC Blue|sorcerer's curse}}?|Vaati|TMC}}</ref> also known as '''Vaati's Curses''',<ref>{{Cite|I am counting on you, {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}. Only you can break {{Color|TMC Blue|Vaati's curse}} and free Princess Zelda.|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref> are powerful magical abilities used by {{Term|Series|Vaati|link}} in {{TMC}}.

==Overview==
After donning the {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap|link}} created by {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} takes the form of a powerful sorcerer.<ref>{{Cite|Vile? I am a sorcerer now, and my power is beyond compare!|Vaati|TMC}}</ref> With his newfound power, he bestows a {{Term|TMC|Sorcerer's Curse}} on {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}, transforming his former mentor into a sentient hat with a bird-like face.

Traveling from the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm|link}} to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} participates in the sword-fighting tournament at the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}. After winning the tournament, he is given the opportunity to touch the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}}, embedded in the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest|link}}. After attacking the {{Plural|TMC|Vassal|link}} around the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest|display=Chest}}, he breaks the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} and opens the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest|display=Chest}}. As monsters pour out of the now opened {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest}}, a glimpse of the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}} flashes out from {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}}. Not realizing that her mysterious power is in fact the {{Term|TMC|Light Force}} he has been searching for, {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} bestows another {{Term|TMC|Sorcerer's Curse}} on her, turning her to stone to keep her from interfering with his plans.<ref>{{Cite|If I leave you now, you'll only cause me trouble later. That will never do. To stone with you!|Vaati|TMC}}</ref>

After taking the form of {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} turns him to stone as well and imprisons him in the dungeons beneath {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}}. By the time {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} are able to infuse the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}} with the power of the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} has {{Term|TMC|Sorcerer's Curse|display=Cursed}} all the remaining denizens of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}}, turning all the {{Plural|TMC|Vassal}} and {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho|link}} to stone.

{{Term|TMC|Link}} is able to break these {{Plural|TMC|Sorcerer's Curse|display=Curses}} with the power of the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword}},<ref>{{Cite|This can only be the work of that evil Vaati! What cruelty. {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! We must use the power of the sacred {{Color|TMC Red|Four Sword}} to {{Color|TMC Blue|undo this evil}}!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> though the {{Term|TMC|Sorcerer's Curse|display=Curse}} on {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} is not broken until {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} is defeated.<ref>{{Cite|What’s this? The curse... Defeating Vaati seems to have broken the curse he cast upon me!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>
