'''Water Blocks''' are [[Object|Objects]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Water Block|Liquid in a cube shape. If laid next to others, you can swim through them.<ref name="Water Block">{{Cite|Liquid in a cube shape. If laid next to others, you can swim through them.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Water Blocks are cubes of water found in the [[Still World]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], allowing her to summon them for one [[Echo]] energy. They are best used to cross gaps and climb walls by swimming through them.
