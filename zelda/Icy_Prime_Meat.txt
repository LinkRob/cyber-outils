==Location and Uses==
An Icy Prime Meat is made by freezing a [[Raw Prime Meat]] with [[Ice]]. When eaten, Icy Prime Meat heals one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} and grants {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} temporary [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"></ref>
