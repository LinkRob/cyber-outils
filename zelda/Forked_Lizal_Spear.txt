{{Compendium/Extract|Forked Lizal Spear}}
{{Plural|BotW|Forked Lizal Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by high-tier {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Black Lizalfos|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Golden Lizalfos|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}} also use these {{Plural|BotW|Forked Lizal Spear|display=Spears}}, especially in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. They are wielded by skilled {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos}} warriors, and their dual blades are known to inflict brutal wounds.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Due to their metal spearheads, {{Plural|BotW|Forked Lizal Spear}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will also sink if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up again using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. If they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Forked Lizal Spear}} will not burn like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} do.
