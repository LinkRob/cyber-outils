{{Term/Store|Spirit Island||Series, PH}} is an island hidden in the Southwest corner of the Southwest sea quadrant in {{PH}}. Long ago, the three Spirits came here in search of the power to defeat evil.<ref name="Spirits">{{Cite|The Shrine at Spirit Island. The servant spirits came here long ago in search of the power to defeat evil. The three spirits used Spirit Gems to gain the power they required.|N/A|PH}}</ref>

==Features==
===Overview===
The island is hidden in the middle of a triangle of rocks south of [[Molida Island]], which is hinted at by a [[Gossip Stone]] on Molida Island.<ref>{{Cite|There is a tiny isle in the middle of the three boulders south of this isle.|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref> It does not appear on [[Link]]'s [[Sea Chart]] until he sails close enough for [[Ciela]] to spot it and mark its location.<ref>{{Cite|Look there! That island isn't marked on our charts, Link! And it looks like we can dock there! There! I've just added the isle to your chart!|Ciela|PH}}</ref> The island is smaller than most other islands of the [[World of the Ocean King]], and is made up of two sections separated by a channel of water. To the south of the left section is a much smaller piece of land, connected to the main area by a bridge. Right next to the channel of water that separates the two main sections of the island is a [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Courage Gem]]. The right section of the island cannot be accessed until Link obtains the Grappling Hook in the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}}, and is made up of several stacks of land. If Link successfully navigates the area, defeating the enemies that are present, he will come across a treasure chest containing a [[Power Gem]], which is located on the highest piece of land.

===Places of Interest===
The island's [[Dock]] is located on the smaller island to the south of the left section. Further along this small island is a [[Mailbox]], located just to the left of the bridge. On the other side of this bridge is the main area; a small plain containing [[grass]], [[rock]]s, and three [[Red ChuChu]]s. 

====The Shrine at Spirit Island====
'''The Shrine at Spirit Island'''<ref name="Spirits" /> is a cave containing a spring that is home to the servant to the goddess of Spirits.<ref name="Servant">{{Cite|I am the servant to the goddess of spirits that dwells in this spring... I have been awaiting your arrival...|N/A|PH}}</ref> Long ago, the servant Spirits came here in search of the power to defeat evil, and used [[Spirit Gem]]s to gain the power they required.<ref name="Spirits" /> It is located north of the island's main area. Inside, just a few steps from the entrance, is a door, and just beyond it, a small staircase. On either side of the staircase is a stone tablet, the one on the left explaining how many Spirit Gems are required to power up a spirit, and the one on the right explaining how to change which spirit is equipped. At the top of the staircase is a path lined with torches that leads to the spring.

When Link first arrives at the spring, the servant to the goddess of Spirits will explain that it has been waiting for his arrival,<ref name="Servant" /> before explaining that the servant Spirits have not yet recovered their full strength. In order to restore their power, Link must collect the Spirit Gems that are scattered around the world and bring them to the spring.<ref>{{Cite|The spirits that travel with you have not recovered their true strength. To restore their power, you must find Spirit Gems scattered around the world. Then you must bring them here.|N/A|PH}}</ref> Upon presenting 10 of the same Spirit Gem, the corresponding spirit's powers will be advanced.<ref>{{Cite|Ten Spirit Gems will give great power to a spirit.|N/A|PH}}</ref> [[Leaf]], the Spirit of Power, will cause Link's [[sword]] to burn with flames and enhance its strength.<ref>{{Cite|The Spirit of Power has been powered up! Your sword now burns with flames!|N/A|PH}}</ref> [[Neri]], the Spirit of Wisdom, will halve the amount of damage taken and enhance the shield to stun some enemies.<ref>{{Cite|The Spirit of Wisdom has been powered up! Wisdom now protects you. Your shield's defense is much greater!|N/A|PH}}</ref> [[Ciela]], the Spirit of Courage, enhances the sword, allowing it to shoot sword beams with every slash.<ref>{{Cite|The Spirit of Courage has been powered up! Your sword now hurls shock waves!|N/A|PH}}</ref> Upon presenting 20 gems, the spirits will fully realize their power, causing their abilities to become more effective.<ref>{{Cite|Twenty Spirit Gems are required for a spirit to fully realize its power.|N/A|PH}}</ref> Once Link has found all 60 Spirit Gems and presents them to the servant, the servant will tell Link that it cannot do anything more for the young hero.<ref>{{Cite|You have collected all the Spirit Gems, and the power of the spirits is awakened. There is nothing more that I can do for you.|N/A|PH}}</ref>
