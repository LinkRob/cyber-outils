'''Cores''' are items in '''''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.'''

{{Infobox Item|game=SSBU|image=File:Ssbu cores.png}}

== Location and Uses ==
Cores are mainly used to summon [[spirit]]s (which also costs SP), but can also be used as snacks. Cores are obtained by dismissing [[spirit]]s.
