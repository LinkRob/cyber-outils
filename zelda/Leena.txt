{{Term/Store|Leena||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Leena|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Leena}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She carries a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}.

The first time {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can speak to her, he will find her going through a list of supplies she needs for a trip into the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see...am I forgetting anything? I've got to remember the {{Color|BotW Blue|hydromelons}}... That still leaves sunscreen... And...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has started the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Leena}} will be concerned that {{Term|BotW|Barta|link}} may have been captured by the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Barta hasn't returned in many days... Could she have been captured by those thieves?|Leena|BotW}}</ref> However, she assures herself that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} must have instead gotten lost in the sandstorms near {{Term|BotW|Karusa Valley|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|No, the sandstorms out near the {{Color|BotW Red|Karusa Valley}} have been bad. I'm certain she's gotten lost or stranded.|Leena|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Liana}}, she notices that she has not seen {{Term|BotW|Barta}} for some time.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, I haven't seen Barta. Where's she gone off to?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena}} explains that she left for the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}} two days ago, and she wonders what happened to her.<ref>{{Cite|She said she was going to scout the thieves' hideout, but then she left before we could say anything. Now that I think about it, she's been gone for two days. I wonder what happened to her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> Angered, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} asks why she let {{Term|BotW|Barta}} go by herself,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|You— Are you serious?!}} Why did you let her go by herself?! And why did you wait two days to report this?!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Leena}} apologizes for, explaining that she thought she would be careful.<ref>{{Cite|I'm— I apologize... She said she just wanted to check it out and would be careful, so I thought...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is upset that they now have to retrieve both the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} and Barta.<ref>{{Cite|Uuugghhh. As if we aren't busy enough searching for the chief's heirloom, now we have to find {{Color|BotW Blue|Barta}} too...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She expresses concern about {{Term|BotW|Teake|link|display=Captain Teake}} finding out about what happened.<ref>{{Cite|If the captain hears about this fiasco, she'll punish us for sure! Probably 100 laps around Gerudo Desert...again.|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Leena}} to find that she is once again concerned about {{Term|BotW|Barta}}.<ref>{{Cite|What do I do about Barta? Do I say something? Or nothing...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> When he approaches {{Term|BotW|Liana}}, she will be shocked to hear that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has disappeared for a second time.<ref>{{Cite|Whuh... Whah... {{Big|WHAAAAT?!}} Barta didn't come back AGAIN?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta|link}} reveals that she went to investigate the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid this time she went to investigate the {{Color|BotW Blue|leviathan bones}} in the southwest.|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> though {{Term|BotW|Leena}} claims she tried to stop her.<ref>{{Cite|I... I tried to stop her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} notes that they clearly did not succeed,<ref>{{Cite|But obviously it didn't work because she's not here!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Leena}} explains that she did not think {{Term|BotW|Barta}} would actually go due to the {{Term|BotW|Molduga|link}} living in the area.<ref>{{Cite|Well, yeah... But... Everyone knows that area is the domain of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}}, so who would figure she'd even go close to there?|Leena|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} furthers this point by bringing up the extreme {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Yes, nobody would dare! And not only that, but it's an {{Color|BotW Blue|extremely hot zone}}, so we wouldn't last long there in our gear...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> which only causes {{Term|BotW|Liana}} to call them idiots.<ref>{{Cite|You... You {{Big|IDIOTS!}}|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She tells them that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} would not realize if she was walking towards danger.<ref>{{Cite|That scatterbrain wouldn't even realize she was walking into danger! I mean, you know that, right?! Why, it was just the other day she ran off and got captured by the Yiga!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She resigns herself to the fact that {{Term|BotW|Barta}}, {{Term|BotW|Kotta}}, and {{Term|BotW|Leena}} will not change, but she wonders why she has to get into trouble so often.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh... I guess Barta will be Barta, and you all aren't going to change... But still, why does she have to go and get in trouble like this all the time...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> After witnessing this exchange, "{{Term|BotW|The Search for Barta|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

When spoken to afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Leena}} will be upset that {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is angry at her again.<ref>{{Cite|Great, now my senior officer Liana is steamed at me... Well, as she should be, I guess...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> She recalls that she has been in trouble like this before with {{Term|BotW|Teake}}, claiming that she was mad at her then, too.<ref>{{Cite|I got in trouble over something related to Barta with our {{Color|BotW Blue|squad leader, Teake}}, before as well, and she was really mad...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> However, she repeats that she tried to stop {{Term|BotW|Barta}} this time, since she told her to equip herself for the {{Term|BotW|Heat}} in the area.<ref>{{Cite|But I did kind of try to sort of stop her, you know! I told her it was a {{Color|BotW Blue|region of extreme heat}} and that she should equip herself appropriately if she was going there...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells her to try harder next time, but this only angers {{Term|BotW|Leena}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Try harder next time...] ARGH!|Leena|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Barta}} returns to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}, {{Term|BotW|Leena}} notes how impressive it is that she was able to cross the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|...If you think about it, making your way across the desert like that is really pretty impressive.|Leena|BotW}}</ref> She wonders how strong {{Term|BotW|Barta}} must be to do so.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder if that means Barta is actually really strong...|Leena|BotW}}</ref>
