The {{Term/Store|Final Trial||Series, BotW}} is a challenge and {{Term|BotW|Dungeon|link}} in {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack for {{BotW}}.{{Fact}} It takes place inside what seems to be an unnamed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}. The design of the {{Term|BotW|Dungeon|link}} resembles an engine, suggesting a connection to the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}}, the item received when completing this trial.

==Entrance to the Trial==
The {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}} can only be accessed after {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has completed "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}," "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}," "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}," and "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}." Once he has done so, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|link}}, who instructs him to return to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} for the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I am Maz Koshia. I must commend you for conquering the trial thus far. The door to the final trial is now open at the Shrine of Resurrection. Go, and face your destiny.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

Inside the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} once again hears the voice of {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the brace soul who wishes to master a Divine Beast... You have done well to return to this place of beginnings. You have overcome many challenges. Now, allow me to bestow upon you one final trial.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal|link}} he obtained it from,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Return the Sheikah Slate to the terminal you first obtained it from. Do so, and the final passageway will reveal itself.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> which seals the door to the chamber and causes it do descend into the {{Term|BotW|Dungeon}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Descending...<br/>Please beware of tremors.}}|Sheikah Slate Terminal|BotW}}</ref> The door opens before the descent is complete, however, a magical barrier prevents the elevator from being exited prematurely. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has found the {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}, it will not be present during and after the elevator's descent.

==Themes and Navigation==
Upon entering the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} hears the voice of {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}, who greets him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Welcome, hero. You have overcome many trials to reach me. Now it is time to face the final trial.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> He then informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will need a {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}, which can be obtained from the nearby {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|This trial is a complex labyrinth. To start, you will need a map. You may obtain map information from the Guidance Stone.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} authenticates the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} and grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Map}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling map of Divine Beast.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}} is comprised of a central chamber containing a series of mechanical gears. Four adjoining chambers are themed after each of the other {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} and four {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}}, with elemental themes of {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. The side-chambers contain gears and rotating walls and objects, but these will only move if the chambers are connected to the central mechanism via metallic or stone objects. Once the {{Term|BotW|Dungeon|link}} {{Term|BotW|Map}} has been obtained, the gears can be configured to rotate clockwise or counter-clockwise, which will have a cascading effect on the side-chambers if they have been connected to the central mechanism.

Link can find a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar|link}} in the thunder chamber, one containing a {{Term|BotW|Cobble Crusher|link}} in the fire chamber, one containing a {{Term|BotW|Swallow Bow|link}} in the wind chamber, and one containing a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear|link}} in the water chamber. Each of these {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} are the Weapons required to craft each of the Champions' Weapons. In addition, Link can find a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}} in a Treasure Chest, which is also required to craft a Champions' Weapon. Link can also find a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}} in a Treasure Chest within the Dungeon, which can be crafted into {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link|display=Urbosa's}} {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker|link}} {{Term|BotW|Shield|linkk}}. Lastly, Link can find a Treasure Chest containing five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}.

Each of the themed chambers contains a {{Term|BotW|Terminal|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can authenticate with his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} to unlock a single bolt on a large stone door at the rear of the central chamber.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Deactivating one lock of the door.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Terminal}} will then tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} how many {{Plural|BotW|Terminal}} remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|There are three terminals remaining.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|There are two terminals remaining.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|There is one terminal remaining.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref>

This {{Term|BotW|Dungeon}} is unique in that {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} will gradually appear in the central chamber as the {{Plural|BotW|Terminal}} are activated. Initially, only a sole {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} is present, but three additional {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} along with a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} appear after activating the first {{Term|BotW|Terminal}}. After activating a second, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Turret|link}} appears in the center of the {{Term|BotW|Dungeon}}. After the third {{Term|BotW|Terminal}} is activated, two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}} will patrol the chamber.

After the final {{Term|BotW|Terminal}} lock has been deactivated, the last {{Term|BotW|Terminal}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he can now access the deepest area of the Dungeon.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|All terminal locks have been deactivated. You can now access the deepest area.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref> Once all four locks are disengaged, the door opens to reveal the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he has proven to possess the power of a true hero and then begins to move.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have proven to possess the power of a true hero.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|display=Maz Koshia}} then stands up, offering {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of the Goddess Hylia... I offer this final trial!}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> The pair are {{Term|BotW|Warping|link|display=Warped}} to a floating platform above the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} then initiates a battle with {{Term|BotW|Link}} as the {{Term|BotW|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Dungeon}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Let us begin.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

After defeating {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}, he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he faced him with great courage and that he is a true hero.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You faced that challenge with great courage. You have erased all doubt from my mind. You are a true hero.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|display=Maz Koshia}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|As such, you were destined to take hold of this ancient masterpiece...}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling rune...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Rune extracted.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> Calling the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero}} a masterpiece of innovation for the one true hero, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|display=Maz Koshia}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and sends him back to the surface of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|That which you have obtained... is a masterpiece of innovation, created for the one true hero. Hero of Hyrule... May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>
