'''Needlefly''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Needlefly|Highly intelligent, this large insect is a master of finding the perfect moment to attack.<ref name="Needlefly">{{Cite|Highly intelligent, this large insect is a master of finding the perfect moment to attack.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Needlefly are [[Dragonfly]]-like enemies that can be found throughout [[Faron Wetlands]] and [[Zora River]]. Zelda can learn the Needlefly [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and summoning one costs two Echo energy.
