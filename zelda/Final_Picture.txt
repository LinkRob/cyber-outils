The {{Term/Store|Final Picture||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 398}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Final Picture}} is a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} inside {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has found all twelve {{Plural|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} depicted in the {{Term|BotW|Album|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} will decide to reveal the location of the last {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}}.<ref>{{Cite|No... All twelve pictures already? You've visited every place?! Hmm... Then I suppose it's time. I will reveal the location of the final memory.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Showing the {{Term|BotW|Final Picture}} to him, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} reveals that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} asked her not to reveal it until he was ready.<ref>{{Cite|Here is what happened... Lady Zelda gave me specific instructions. She asked me to wait to show you this. This is the final picture.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if it looks familiar, explaining that he can reach it from {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}} in half a day.<ref>{{Cite|Does it look familiar? From this village, you should be able to get there in a half day's time. Now go, child. Seek out what Lady Zelda has shown you.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

The location depicted is a scene of {{Term|BotW|Ash Swamp|link}}, which is west of {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives there, he will be able to recover "{{Term|BotW|Zelda's Awakening|link}}".


==Trivia==
* If the player has taken a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of the {{Term|BotW|Final Picture}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will pull out the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} to view it before recalling the memory, like he does for other {{Plural|BotW|Memory}}.
