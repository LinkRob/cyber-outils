{{Term/Store|Broca Island||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Broca Island|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Broca Island is a small isle located in the eastern inlet of [[Lake Intenoch]] of [[Eldin Canyon]]. Like the rest of Lake Intenoch, its surrounding water is warm and acts as a [[Hot Spring]], which will gradually recover Link's health as he swims or stands in it.

The island's sole characteristics are a pair of block formations. A missing steel block can be found in the water, which when pushed into the island's block formation using [[Magnesis]], causing a [[Korok]] to appear, who gives Link a [[Korok Seed]].
