The {{Term/Store|Soldier's Gear|Soldier's Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Soldier's Gear}} was equipment issued to the soldiers who guarded {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.<ref name="Broadsword">{{Cite|{{Big|Soldier's Broadsword}}<br/>A sword brandished by the soldiers who once protected Hyrule Castle. Its durable blade is well tuned for slaying monsters.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Soldier's Spear}}<br/>A long spear once used by the guards of Hyrule Castle. Easy to use but difficult to master. The iron tip is very sturdy and the shaft will not burn when exposed to flame.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Shield">{{Cite|{{Big|Soldier's Shield}}<br/>A shield once used by the guards of Hyrule Castle. It's easy to handle, but its core is made of wood, so it can catch fire.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> To this end, they are known to be of decent quality and {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}}.<ref name="Broadsword"/><ref name="Spear"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Soldier's Claymore}}<br/>A two-handed sword designed for combat. It's heavy and hard to use but has decent build durability and quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Soldier's Gear}} is primarily crafted out of iron, granting it an inability to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.<ref name="Spear"/> They will also conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Contradicting this, the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Bow|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Shield|link}} are constructed out of wood, meaning they are vulnerable to burning.<ref name="Shield" /><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Soldier's Bow}}<br/>A bow designed for armed conflict. Inflicts more damage than a civilian bow, but it will still burn if it touches fire.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning}}, they float when they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}, and they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
