The {{Term/Store|Dila Maag Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Dila Maag Shrine}}<br/>Dila Maag's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Dila Maag's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Dila Maag Shrine is found by completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Desert Labyrinth|link}}". The Quest is activated when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|South Lomei Labyrinth|link}}, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Dila Maag|link}} instructing him to navigate his way through.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot here... My blessing awaits you at the labyrinth's end.}}|Dila Maag|BotW}}</ref>

As Link enters the Shrine, he is greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW |Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dila Maag|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the Shrine is proof of Link's worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Dila Maag. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Dila Maag|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Dila Maag Shrine features no puzzles, simply a direct path to Dila Maag. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Barbarian Armor|link}}. As Link approaches Dila Maag, the Monk will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the Shrine,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Dila Maag|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Dila Maag|BotW}}</ref>
