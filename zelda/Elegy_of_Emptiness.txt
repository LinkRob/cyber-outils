The "{{Term/Store|Elegy of Emptiness||Series, MM, MM3D}}" is a {{Term|MM3D|Song|link}} in {{MM}}.<ref name="E 96">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[File:MM3D Elegy of Emptiness Statues.png|thumb|left|185x185px|The replicas produced from playing the "{{Term|MM3D|Elegy of Emptiness}}"]]
Played on the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]], the "{{Term|MM3D|Elegy of Emptiness}}" is taught by [[Igos du Ikana]] in the {{Term|MM3D|Ancient Castle of Ikana|link}} after [[Link]] defeats him and his [[King's Lackeys|lackeys]]. According to the fallen king, it produces a "soldier" of himself who has no heart.

The "{{Term|MM3D|Elegy of Emptiness}}" leaves an empty shell of the form Link is in at the time, but it can only make one of each at a time.<ref name="Obtain"/> It is required to scale up the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower|link}}, and it is also necessary to solve several puzzles inside the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|link}}.<ref name="E 96"/>

A total of four shells can exist at any given time, one for each of the young [[Hero of Time]]'s forms. They all play a pivotal role, but the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}} shell is too light in virtually all cases, while the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Link|link}} shell is specifically used to press down switches that are bigger than usual. The shells can only be formed if Link is in any of the areas pertaining to {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Canyon|link}}; if the song is played anywhere else, it will not have any effect.<ref name="E 96"/> The shells that Link's [[Deku Scrub]], [[Goron]], and [[Zora]] forms leave are that of {{Term|MM3D|Deku Butler's Son|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Darmani III|link}}, and {{Term|MM3D|Mikau|link}}, respectively.

The statues, when placed correctly, can also block certain projectiles such as {{Term|MM3D|Octorok|link}} rocks.

== Trivia ==

* In the Japanese release, the song is known as "'''Elegy of Exuvia'''".
* The [[Fierce Deity Link]] form, under normal circumstances, does not have a shell based on this song, and if attempted by the player via the Ocarina of Time, the game will crash. However, if the game was forced to make a statue appear after defeating Igos du Ikana as such form, Link's normal version will occur without issue.
* Link's regular shell, due to its rather creepy facial expression, has a notorious cult-following among the series' fandom, popularly known for the creepypasta/ARG known as "Ben Drowned".[[File:MM Link Mask Model.png|thumb|176x176px|The cut Link Mask resembles the face of the hero's [[Hylian]] shell]]
* The scrapped Link Mask, a unique [[Transformation|transforming]] [[Mask]] that was originally going to make the hero change into his adult self from {{OoT}} in the beta version of the game, is similar in appearance to that of the Link shell.
