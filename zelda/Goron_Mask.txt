The {{Term/Store|Goron Mask||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, CoH}} is a recurring item and object in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|CoH}} It resembles the face of a [[Goron]].

==Mainline appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Goron Mask can be borrowed from the [[Happy Mask Shop]] after completing the [[Mask Trading Sequence]]. It has no function other than to change peoples' reactions when [[Link]] talks to them.

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM Goron Mask obtained.jpg|thumb|Link obtaining the Goron Mask in {{MM|-}}]]
In {{MM}}, the Goron Mask is one of five [[Mask]]s used for [[Transformation]],<ref>{{Cite|When you wear it, you will transform into the shape you just were.|Happy Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> the other three being the [[Deku Mask]], the [[Zora Mask]], and the [[Fierce Deity's Mask]]. When worn, it transforms Link into a [[Goron Link|Goron]]. The Mask contains the spirit of the fallen Goron hero [[Darmani III]], and when Link dons the Mask, he inhabits the warrior's body. The transformation grants all the special abilities of that species, including a new fighting style, the ability to carry [[Powder Keg]]s, a special [[Goron Pound]] attack,<ref>{{Cite|Then I'll open the door with a Goron Pound.|Goron|MM}}</ref> and the ability to roll at high speed while attacking with spikes, which consumes [[Magic Power]].

In [[Snowhead]], the ghost of the Goron warrior Darmani lingers in [[Goron Village]], waiting to be healed. Link can speak to him if he equips the [[Lens of Truth]]. After overcoming the initial shock that Link can see him, he asks him to follow him to his grave in the [[Goron Graveyard]].<ref>{{Cite|......... Can it be? Are you able to see me? If you truly can see me, then follow behind me...|Darmani|MM}}</ref> Darmani then relates the tale of how he died, but says he cannot rest and wishes to have his sorrows healed.<ref>{{Cite|But alas... I am now dead. I was fine until I marched off to Snowhead by myself, hoping that I could drive off a demon. It had been wreaking havoc on Goron Village...Then the blizzard at Snowhead blew me into the valley. ...And now...Here I am... ...How infuriating! As I am, I can only watch as Goron Village is slowly buried in ice...I may have died, but I cannot rest.|Darmani|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I beg you! Bring me back to life with your magic! If it is beyond your power, then I beg of you to do this for me instead... Heal my sorrows. Any way that you can do it will suffice... Please...heal my sorrows...|Darmani|MM}}</ref> Link can play the "[[Song of Healing]]" to heal his soul, which is then encased in the Goron Mask.<ref>{{Cite|What a soothing melody... My sorrows are melting away into the song. I leave my undying feelings with you...|Darmani|MM}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
The Goron Mask appears alongside the [[Bunny Hood]], [[Keaton Mask]], and [[All-Night Mask]] behind the counter of the [[Nintendo Gallery]] in {{TWW}}. It can be seen hanging on the wall next to [[Carlov]].
