"{{Term/Store|Trial of Thunder||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial of Thunder}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Thundra Plateau}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} reaches the vicinity of the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|link}}, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Toh Yahsa|link}}, who offers him a trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You who have reached this land of thunder, I present you with a trial.}}|Toh Yahsa|BotW}}</ref> He is instructed to return the "four spirits" to their proper places, which will reveal an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} to him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|When the four spirits have been settled in their proper places, they will guide you to the hero's trial.}}|Toh Yahsa|BotW}}</ref> After hearing this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

The {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau}} is constantly subjected to a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. Around {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|display=Plateau}} are four orbs of different colors, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must retrieve. The purple sphere sits near the north pillar on the ground. In addition, the green sphere sits atop the southern pillar. The red sphere is located on top of a stone statue east of the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|display=Plateau}}, while the orange sphere is atop a taller stone statue directly north of the plateau. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can climb up to the spheres (though the rain works against this) or knock them down using {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}; [[Magnesis]] may also be used to levitate a metal object near the spheres and induce a lightning strike that will knock them down. By using {{Term|BotW|Stasis}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can knock the spheres up onto the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|display=Plateau}} with {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}.

After gathering all four spheres on the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can place them in the basins below their matching pillars.<ref>{{Cite|You set the four spirit in their proper places based on the diagrams on the stone pillars.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> The purple spheres goes in the northern basin, the red sphere goes in the eastern basin, the green sphere goes in the southern basin, and the orange sphere goes in the western basin. Once all of the orbs have been matched, {{Term|BotW|Toh Yahsa Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground in the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|display=Plateau}}.<ref>{{Cite|When this was done, a shrine appeared in the middle on the Thundra Plateau!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
