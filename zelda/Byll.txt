{{Term/Store|Byll||Series, ALBW}} is the name of one of the 20 possible {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} that can appear in {{ALBW}} without being met by {{Term|Series|StreetPass|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's Byll!<br/>This {{Color|ALBW Blue|Shadow Link}} has come via {{Color|ALBW Red|StreetPass}} and awaits battle!|Shadow Link|ALBW}}</ref>{{Note|All Shadow Links claim to have come via StreetPass, even when they are pre-generated.}}

==Overview==
Once {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} has spoken to {{Term|ALBW|Gramps|link}} and enabled {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link Battle|link}}, a new signpost signifying the arrival of a {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} will appear at the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow|link}} after every five hours of gameplay, regardless of whether or not the player has received a {{Term|Series|StreetPass|link}} tag. {{Term|ALBW|Byll}} is one of the pre-generated {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link}} who can be met by this method.

{{Term|ALBW|Byll}} wears a {{Term|ALBW|Green Tunic|link}} and wields a {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword|link}} and no {{Term|ALBW|Shield|link}}. He has a {{Term|ALBW|Life Gauge|link}} of ten {{Plural|ALBW|Heart}}, ten Medals won, and carries a {{Term|ALBW|Bee|link}} and a {{Term|ALBW|Net|link}} into battle. With a bounty of 180 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Byll}} is the lowest-bountied {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}} to possess the {{Term|ALBW|Pegasus Boots|link}}, as well as the lowest-bountied to possess the {{Term|ALBW|Bee Badge|link}}.
