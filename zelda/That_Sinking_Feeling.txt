'''"That Sinking Feeling"''' is the sixth episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}} to be broadcast. It first aired in the United States and Canada on October 13th, 1989.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[That Sinking Feeling/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
[[File:Overworld Magnet (That Sinking Feeling).png|thumb|left|100px|The Overworld Magnet]]
{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} takes {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} to a picnic in a faraway place from the castle,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=This sure is a long way to go for a picnic. What's so great about the place you discovered, anyway?|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> and the princess, impressed by the romantic place {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} chose,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=What a gorgeous and romantic place!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> immediately offers a kiss to the young hero.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Mmm...shut up and kiss me!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> However, they are interrupted when an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}} entrance begins to raise and a gang of {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Tektite|link}} come out from it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=It's an underground entrance. We're being attacked!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda are successful in defeating a few of them, but then several {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Tektite}} wrap the princess up in spider webs while the young hero loses his sword to one of the foes that sneaked up behind him. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} eventually saves the princess, and just as they are about to try to kiss again,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, alright. As long as the cupid statue is here, I guess it's still romantic. One quick kiss.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> a cupid statue nearby sinks completely into the ground.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The statue! It's sinking!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> Tired about {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}}'s evil deeds, Zelda decides it is time to go visit the evil wizard and have a showdown with him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ugh! And I'm fed up! We're going to have a showdown with that evil wizard!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> As they are making their way to the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}}, the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce|link}} informs them that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} is in possession of an item called the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld Magnet|link}}, which explains the strange recent events, and that the only way to destroy it will be by using {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic Bracelet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=A magic magnet pulled it down, will be destroyed with the bracelets found.|name=Triforce of Wisdom|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> Suddenly, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|North Castle|link}} begins to sink into the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}}, but the duo, unable to do anything, continues on their way.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=It's gone! The whole castle! But how? Where is it?|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=It's in Ganon's hands. He won't let go unless defeated down below.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref>

Meanwhile, in the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} is sure that the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom in his hands now that the whole castle is in his lair;<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I have captured the castle, and with it, the Triforce of Wisdom! Now I can conquer Hyrule, destroy that pipsqueak Link, and make Zelda my queen.|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> however, he soon discovers that the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} is not in the castle and goes mad<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Gyaaaaah! Where's the Triforce?! It's not here! I'm very upset!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> while {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte|link}} try to hide from his rage.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=He'll do the same to us if he finds us. Stay hidden!|name=Spryte|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> Later, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} defeats a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Darknut|link}}, which drops the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic Bracelet}} that the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom was talking about.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Look! It dropped a magic bracelet!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} then confronts {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Go for it, princess. I've got my own work cut out for me.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> while Zelda tries to retrieve the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Power.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Link, look! The Triforce of Power...! If we can get that, Ganon will be defeated forever!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> The evil wizard eventually ties up {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} in chains with one of his spells and proceeds to pull Zelda closer using his magic.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Come closer... Come closer...!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> Just before {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} grabs the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom from Zelda, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} uses the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic Bracelet}} to break out of the chains and uses his super-strength to throw him far away,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The bracelet, of course! These magic bracelets give one minute of super strength.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> which causes {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} to knock over the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld Magnet}}. This leads the magnet to slowly bring North Castle up to the surface again,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The castle! It's going back up!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> and although Zelda tries to get the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Power,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=We have to get the Triforce of Power!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} picks her up and jumps into the castle walls.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No time!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> As the castle reaches the surface, the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld Magnet}} begins to overload and explodes in front of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh no, it's overloading! It's going to blow!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref>
