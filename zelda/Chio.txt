{{Term/Store|Chio||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Chio|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Chio}} is a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} that can be found underneath the {{Term|BotW|Great Deku Tree|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} recognizes him as the hero.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? You... Could it be?! You're Mr. Hero! Mr. Hero is here!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He explains to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the mushroom on his head suddenly sprouted that day, so he had expected something to happen.<ref>{{Cite|The mushroom on my head suddenly sprouted today, so I knew something was up! It was a prediction that you'd come! ...I think.|Chio|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Chio}} reveals that the {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} all prepared some trials for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Us Koroks all got together and prepared some {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} just for you, Mr. Hero!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has not yet pulled out the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} says the trials will help him do so.<ref>{{Cite|If you pass the veeeery tough {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} we've prepared... pulling out the {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Sword}} will be a piece of cake! ...Probably!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} already has the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} asserts that the trials will help him become stronger.<ref>{{Cite|Although you may not need them, since you already pulled the {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Sword}} out and all... But these {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} will help you grow even stronger than you are now!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He goes on to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he designed an extra-tough trial himself, as the elder of {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Among the {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} is an extra-tough one designed by me, the elder of {{Color|BotW Blue|Korok Forest}}.|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is ready to test his mettle.<ref>{{Cite|So will you test your mettle in the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trials}}?|Chio|BotW}}</ref> Accepting his challenge initiates the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Korok Trials|link}}".

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} informs him what the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial|link}} are before he gets started.<ref>{{Cite|[Korok Trials?] Ah, you need to think about it first... Wise boy...|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He explains that the goal is to visit all of the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}} to be considered a grown-up.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trials}}! Long story short—you need to visit all the shrines in the Korok Forest so that you can be considered {{Color|BotW Red|a real grown-up}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> According to him, it is a ceremony based on the ancient hero that every {{Term|BotW|Korok}} must undertake.<ref>{{Cite|It's a ceremony that every Korok must undertake, and it's based on the trials of the legendary hero himself!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Chio}} and {{Term|BotW|Hestu|link}} have both participated, as have several other {{Plural|BotW|Korok}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've done it... and so has Hestu... and him over there, and her too, and even the Great Deku Tree himself has done it! OK... that last part was a lie. He's pretty rooted.|Chio|BotW}}</ref>

The first {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}} that {{Term|BotW|Chio}} shares is the "{{Term|BotW|Trial of Second Sight|link}}", which is found in the southwest of {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial one!}} In the southwest forest: {{Color|BotW Red|Trial of Second Sight}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He then shares the location of the second {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Lost Pilgrimage|link}}", in the northwest {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|display=Forest}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial two!}} In the northwest forest: {{Color|BotW Red|The Lost Pilgrimage}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> Finally, he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} the third {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Test of Wood|link}}" is in the eastern {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|display=Forest}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial three!}} In the eastern forest: {{Color|BotW Red|The Test of Wood}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He reiterates that overcoming the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}} means one can overcome anything.<ref>{{Cite|If you can overcome the Korok Trials, you can overcome anything!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} have also prepared prizes for {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he completes the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trials}}.<ref>{{Cite|We've also prepared some wonderful prizes for you! Now show us what you're made of!|Chio|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes all of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trials}},<ref>{{Cite|What?! You've finished all of the {{Color|BotW Red|trials}}?|Chio|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Chio}} will be impressed.<ref>{{Cite|*whistle*<br/>That's amazing! You're so cool!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> Recognizing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants a reward,<ref>{{Cite|Ah! I know that look. You want me to give you something since you visited all the places, don't you?|Chio|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Chio}} has no choice but to give {{Term|BotW|Link}} his most treasured item, three {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|...I guess I have no choice. I'll give you my treasured item.|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He suggests eating a dish made with {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle}}, as it will soothe all of his pain.<ref>{{Cite|Try eating a dish cooked with that {{Color|BotW Blue|big hearty truffle}}... It'll soothe all of your aches and pains and then some! I'm not pulling your branch here! Just try it for yourself!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

{{Term|BotW|Chio}} feels privileged to have witnessed {{Term|BotW|Link}} pulling out the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|I was privileged enough to witness you pull out the Master Sword!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> Without any additonal {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trials}} for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he encourages him to go and save {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have no more trials to give you! Go! Restore peace to Hyrule!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> Meanwhile, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} says the {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} will be cheering for him from the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now go!<br/>We will be cheering you on from the depths of the forest!|Chio|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* Aside from {{Term|Series|Hestu}}, {{Term|Series|Chio}} is the only {{Term|Series|Korok}} appearing in {{BotW|-}} to have an entirely new character design, rather than one based on a {{Term|Series|Korok}} from {{TWW}}. He is also the only {{Term|Series|Korok}}, aside from {{Term|Series|Hestu}}, to have a unique character design not shared with any other {{Term|Series|Korok}} in the game.
