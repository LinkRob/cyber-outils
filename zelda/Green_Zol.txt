==Other Appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
[[File:CoH Green Zol Sprite.png|left]]
{{Plural|CoH|Green Zol}} are stationary enemies, hopping in one place on every other beat. Since they have no form of attack, they cannot directly harm {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} unless they take damage, in which they will then split into one or two {{Plural|CoH|Green Gel|link}} who will try to chase down and attack the heroes.
{{Ambig|End}}
