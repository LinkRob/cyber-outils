{{Term/Store|Astrid||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 282 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Astrid is a [[Fortune Teller]] and the single living resident on the [[Isle of Ember]]. When [[Link]] first meets her, she is trapped in the basement of her own home.<ref>{{Cite|I can't open the door from the inside!!|Astrid|PH}}</ref> After speaking with her, Astrid asks Link to find her missing assistant [[Kayo]], as only he knows how to open the chamber in which Astrid is trapped.<ref>{{Cite|That map is somehow the key to opening the door... Unfortunately, only my assistant, Kayo, knows the secret of it...|Astrid|PH}}</ref> Once he is found, however, all that remains of him is a ghost floating above his skeletal remains. Although {{Term|PH|Ciela|link}} initially targets him as a monster,<ref>{{Cite|Aaah! It's a ghost! Get him,  Link!|Ciela|PH}}</ref> Kayo quickly declares he means no harm and tells Link what he must do to free Astrid.<ref>{{Cite|I'm not a bad ghost...|Kayo|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The three torches on this island are the key to understanding ...|Kayo|PH}}</ref> After freeing Astrid, she tells Link's and [[Linebeck]]'s fortunes.<ref>{{Cite|If any others are with you, please bring them along with you to my chambers.|Astrid|PH}}</ref><ref name= "Fortunes"/> Through her reading, Link learns he must journey into the [[Temple of Fire]] to "defeat the power of darkness" and break the seal imprisoning the [[Leaf|Spirit of Power]],<ref>{{Cite|Now make haste to the Temple of Fire|Astrid|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There you must defeat the power of darkness that haunts that place.|Astrid|PH}}</ref> if he truly wishes to save [[Tetra]] from the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}. When Link returns to the Fortune Teller's residence after completing the temple, Astrid informs him that in order to reach the Ghost Ship, he needs three spirits: [[Leaf|Power]], [[Neri|Wisdom]] and {{Term|PH|Ciela|link|display= Courage}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ultimately, you will need three spirits: Power, Wisdom, and Courage.|Astrid|PH}}</ref>

If Link returns to speak to Astrid throughout the game, she will give him vague hints or clues describing what he must do next in his quest.<ref>{{Cite|Good tidings to you, Link. Thank you for your recent help. Try as I might to express my gratitude in words, I cannot do it justice. If there is anything that I can do in return, please stop by the Isle of Ember. I know that someone of my modest means might have little to offer. But I believe that I can eat least show you the way should you become lost. Sincerely, Astrid|Astrid|PH}}</ref> On a visit directly after obtaining the [[Regal Necklace]] from the [[Isle of Ruins]], it is revealed that Kayo and herself are descendants of the [[Cobble]] tribe,<ref>{{Cite|You know, Kayo and I hail from the Cobble Kingdom...|Astrid|PH}}</ref> saying Link was destined to walk on the Isle of Ruins.<ref>{{Cite|I know that you are destined to walk the Isle of Ruins.|Astrid|PH}}</ref>

In the {{PH|Himekawa}}, as soon as they reach the Isle of Ember, Link and Linebeck decide to stop at Astrid's house.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= A fortune-teller's house? Let's ask here.|book= PH (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 51}}</ref> Astrid introduces herself, stating that they are lost and that she can direct their way.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Welcome to Madame Astrid's house... you are lost... I will show you the way.|book= PH (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 52}}</ref> Gazing into a crystal ball, she informs Linebeck that he should get a wife if he does not wish to perish under a pile of dirty underpants.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I can see... your future! Heed my words! If you don't wish to die buried under... a pile of dirty underwear, you should get a wife.|book= PH (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 52}}</ref> Linebeck then responds that he does not need romantic advice.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I'm not here for romantic advice!|book= PH (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 52}}</ref> Astrid tells Link that his help is needed, informing the hero about a monster lurking inside a temple under the island's volcano.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Little Hero. There are those who seek your help. The power of darkness has locked them away... and their lights grow weak... but they yet have the strength needed to open the path you seek... to the Temple of Fire under the angry volcano.|book= PH (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 52, 53}}</ref> Once Link returns from the temple, she congratulates him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Splendid! You defeated the power of darkness, Link.|book= PH (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 59}}</ref> Astrid mentions that the power of the spirit he freed will help him find the Ghost Ship and Link is baffled at how she knew about the Ship when he never mentioned it before.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=...The spirit you freed bears part of the power you need to find the Ghost Ship.|book= PH (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 59}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I didn't tell you about the Ghost Ship! The remaining two grow weaker. You must hurry.|book= PH (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 60}}</ref> Link and Linebeck depart and follow her advice, soon freeing Leaf, Neri and Ciela's memories.
