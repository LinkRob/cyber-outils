{{Term/Store|Ezlo||Series, TMC}},<ref name="E 270">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> also known as '''Cap Ezlo''' when transformed into a hat by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}},<ref name="E 270/> is a character and {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s main companion in {{TMC}}.

==Appearance==
While in his cap form, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} has the appearance of a green cap much like the one {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} usually wears, albeit with a bird-like face, complete with a large yellow beak.

==Abilities==
{{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} gives {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} advice and background information throughout their adventure, both spontaneously and on demand with use of the {{Term|TMC|Select Button|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now, I suppose a boy like you still has much to learn about the world. If ever you need my insight, press {{Color|TMC Blue|SELECT}}. I'll be happy to help!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} has the ability to shrink {{Term|TMC|Link}} to {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} size whenever he nears a {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Look at that. At first glance, it appears to be a mere stump, yes? No! That stump is a {{Color|TMC Blue|portal}} used by people long ago to adjust their size! With my help, you can use it to {{Color|TMC Blue|shrink down to Minish size}}.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> and also serves as a parachute when {{Term|TMC|Link}} jumps into {{Plural|TMC|Updraft|link}}, similar to the {{Term|TWWHD|Deku Leaf|link}} from {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmn?! Hmmmmnnnn... Ah, of course! How silly of me! Hey, my boy! {{Color|TMC Blue|Jump into that whirlwind}} over there for me! Heh heh heh... I wonder, is this a great idea or a terrible one?|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Minish Ezlo}}
{{Figurine|TMC|Ezlo and Link}}
{{Figurine|TMC|Ezlo (Cap)}}
{{Manual|TMC|Ezlo|A mysterious creature that Link meets during his adventure. He chatters away noisily at Link. He can be quite fussy, but he does seem to know what he’s talking about.}}
{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} finds {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}} being attacked by two {{Plural|TMC|Octorok|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Helllllp!!! Help meeee!!! Ouch! Won't somebody stop them!? Ow! Ow! Help... Somebody! Can't anybody hear me?|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} saves him, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} joins him on his quest to defeat {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} and return the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} to its former power.<ref>{{Cite|You know, you and I have quite a lot in common. You see, I, too, am on a quest to break a curse of Vaati's. And you say that reforging the {{Color|TMC Red|sacred blade}} can break his curse, eh? Well, then you have found yourself a companion, my boy! My name is {{Color|TMC Green|Ezlo}}. It's a pleasure to make your acquaintance.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

Before meeting {{Term|TMC|Link}}, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} was a renowned {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} craftsman and sage who was working on the {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap|display=Cap}} that would grant the wishes of its wearer. The {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap|display=Cap}} was intended as a gift for the {{Plural|TMC|Human|link}}, but when he finished it, his apprentice {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} (who was also a {{Term|TMC|Minish}} at the time) stole it from his work desk and donned it, using its power to turn himself into a powerful sorcerer.<ref>{{Cite|I was once a famous sage and a renowned {{Color|TMC Green|Minish}} craftsman. Vaati was only a boy when I took him on as my apprentice. But...he became enchanted by the wickedness in the hearts of men. One day, Vaati took a hat I made for the humans--my pride and joy. It granted the wishes of its wearer. He put it on without permission...|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} attempted to stop {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}, but was unsuccessful. {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} used the power he had received from the {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap}} to place a {{Term|TMC|Sorcerer's Curse|link}} on {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}, transforming him into a creature resembling a mix between a green hat and a bird.<ref>{{Cite|Tell me, how does it feel, my {{Color|TMC Blue|sorcerer's curse}}?|Vaati|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} pursued {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} into {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} through the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|link}}, leading to his meeting with {{Term|TMC|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I came to the world of men hoping to stop Vaati, and I met you in the woods.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} defeats {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} is transformed back into his original form. He tells {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} to combine her power with the {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap}} in order to undo all the damage done by {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}.<ref>{{Cite|If one with a just heart wears this cap, things can be made right again. Princess Zelda! Let your wishes be made real!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> Before the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door}} closes for another hundred years,<ref>{{Cite|The Minish Door opens but once every century, and soon, it must close. I must leave you both now and return to my homeland.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} a green hat similar to the form he took while under {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s {{Term|TMC|Sorcerer's Curse|display=Curse}}, remarking that it is the first time he has seen him wearing one and that it suits him.<ref>{{Cite|Heh... You know, I’ve never actually seen you wearing a cap until now! It suits you, little hero.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The menu of {{TMC}} displays several images of {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} in the background.
*{{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}'s face is only seen when he is talking or if the player wants advice. During occasions other than these, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s hat model is the same as in {{FS}}.
*When {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Life Gauge|link}} is full, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} appears with his beak open and held high. After losing {{Plural|TMC|Heart|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}'s beak closes and is held low. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} only has one {{Term|TMC|Heart Container|link}} remaining, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} appears exhausted.
*{{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}'s cap form has similar properties to his sage form; namely, the swirl of hair on his head, and the appearance of his eyes. His staff also has a beak and the swirl of hair similar to his cap form.
*{{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} is almost identical in function to the [[Gnat Hat]] from {{FS}}.
