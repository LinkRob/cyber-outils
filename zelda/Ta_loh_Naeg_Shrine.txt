The {{Term/Store|Ta'loh Naeg Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ta'loh Naeg Shrine}}<br/>Ta'loh Naeg's Teaching}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Ta'loh Naeg's Teaching''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg Shrine}} is on a hill overlooking {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. It's mentioned by both {{Term|BotW|Olkin|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Steen|link}}. Olkin will explain the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link|display=Ancient Shrine's}} history. Long ago, a talented swordsman was enshrined in there, and according to Olkin, the only way to overcome the trial is to master the "secret art of defense".<ref>{{Cite|Long ago, a wise master known simply as the swordsman was enshrined—right in this village, no less. It is said that if you overcome his trial, you will master the secret art of defense. However, no one knows exactly what this "trial" entails...|Olkin|BotW}}</ref> Steen has a desire to take on the trial in the Shrine to learn the "secret art of offense",<ref>{{Cite|To tell ya the truth, I'd like to take that trial thing at the village shrine and learn the secret art of offense.|Steen|BotW}}</ref> but no one in the village knows how to enter.<ref>{{Cite|It's just... Well, no one knows the technique you need to enter the shrine. That's why I train like this every day.|Steen|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ta'loh Naeg. I share with you my knowledge, that it may please Hylia.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg Shrine}} features a vast, open chamber with an open pit in the middle of the chamber. In front of the elevator are two stone {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} on the left contains an {{Term|BotW|Eightfold Blade|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} on the right contains a {{Term|BotW|Shield of the Mind's Eye|link}}.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the center of the chamber, a translucent, light blue {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} wielding a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword|link}} will rise from the pit. {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg}} then instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Side Hop|link}} the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|display=Guardian Scout}}'s attacks.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Use a side hop to avoid enemy attacks. This will create an opening that you can use to strike back.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he cannot practice the skill without one.<ref name="Weapon">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You cannot practice this skill without a weapon. Prepare yourself and try again.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} performs a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Dodging|link|display=Perfect Dodge}}, by {{Term|BotW|Side Hop|link|display=Side Hopping}} an attack at the last moment, and counters with a {{Term|BotW|Flurry Rush|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Backflip|link}} to avoid the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|display=Guardian Scout}}'s attacks.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Do a backflip to avoid enemy attacks and create an opening to strike back.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} broke his last {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} during the {{Term|BotW|Flurry Rush}}, {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg}} will tell him to prepare and try again.<ref name="Weapon"/>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} performs a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Dodging|display=Perfect Dodge}} by {{Term|BotW|Backflip|link|display=Backflipping}} and counters with another {{Term|BotW|Flurry Rush}}, {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg}} will instruct {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Parry|link}} the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|display=Guardian Scout}}'s attack at the right time to perform a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Guard|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You can repel an attack if you parry with your shield at just the right time. Strike back in the opening you create.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have a {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}, he is informed that he needs a {{Term|BotW|Shield}} to practice the {{Term|BotW|Perfect Guard}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You cannot practice this skill without a shield. Prepare yourself and try again.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref>

After he successfully performs a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Guard}}, {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to hit the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|display=Guardian Scout}} with a {{Term|BotW|Charged Attack|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Build up your power, and then release it to unleash a charge attack. Catch your enemies in this motion to damage them.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} broke or dropped his last {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} before performing a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Guard}}, {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to try practicing after getting a {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}.<ref name="Weapon"/>

Upon striking the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}} with a {{Term|BotW|Charged Attack}}, the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|display=Guardian Scout}} will be defeated and {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg}} will congratulate {{Term|BotW|Link}} for learning the skills.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to learn these crucial skills. Now, proceed.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref> A portcullis at the back of the chamber will then open, revealing a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg}}.

When approached, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref> and afterward will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ta'loh Naeg|BotW}}</ref>
