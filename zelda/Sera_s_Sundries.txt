{{Term/Store|Sera's Sundries||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.{{Fact}} Owned by {{Term|TPHD|Sera|link}}, it is a small shop located near the entrance to [[Ordon Village]].

At the beginning of the game, Sera is depressed because her [[cat]] has gone missing and thus cannot deal with any customers at the moment.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhhh... I'm terribly sorry...but my mind is so filled with thoughts of my little cat that I simply can't deal with costumers right now... Ohhh... If I had known this was going to happen, I would have let my little  kitty eat fish to his heart's content... Link, m'dear... I'm sorry, but would you leave me be? I'll serve you milk later.|Sera|TP}}</ref> When the cat finally comes back to Sera from having caught one of the [[fish]] that [[Link]] obtained, she happily gives an empty [[bottle]] to the young hero as a celebration of the cat's return.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh, just look... Isn't he so cute, the way he laps up that milk? Say, m'dear, why don't you have some, too! I'm in a good mood, so it's on the house today! Oh, don't worry, you don't have to return the empty bottle, m'dear. You can reuse it--you know, put something else in it. Recycling is a very important thing, m'dear!|Sera|TP}}</ref> Moreover, Link can now buy a [[Slingshot]] that just shipped in at a price of 30 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|The slingshot is 30 Rupees. Do you want to buy it, m'dear?|Sera|TP}}</ref> 

Other than the Slingshot, Link can buy other common refills that are stored in bottles, such as Bee Larva and Lantern Oil.
