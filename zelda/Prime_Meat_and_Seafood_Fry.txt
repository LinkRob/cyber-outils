{{Term/Store|Prime Meat and Seafood Fry|Prime Meat and Seafood Fries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Prime Meat and Seafood Fries are meals that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either [[Raw Prime Meat]] or a [[Raw Bird Thigh]] with any seafood in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. They can be eaten to restore some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These meals are considered comfort foods, containing choice cuts of both meat and seafood.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on both the type of seafood used and whether any additional ingredients are added, Link can make several variations of Prime Meat and Seafood Fry:

* '''Hearty Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}, or other energizing ingredients. Eating these fries will refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely replenished and overfilled by eating these.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using hasty ingredients. By eating them, Link's movement speed will be increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or other spicy ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other electric ingredients. Link can eat these fries to gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, or other mighty ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or other tough ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain a boost to his defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will have his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} increased.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Prime Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Prime Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This comfort dish is made with choice cuts of meat and seafood.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other chilly ingredients. Eating these fries will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
