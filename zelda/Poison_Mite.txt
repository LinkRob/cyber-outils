{{Term/Store|Poison Mite|Poison Mites|Series, TP, TPHD}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 198}}</ref> They are small insects similar in appearance to a scarab beetle.

==Characteristics==
Found only in the [[Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|Arbiter's Grounds]], Poison Mites crawl over [[Link]] in swarms, slowing him down. They can be killed with the [[Spin Attack]]. They also can be kept at bay with the [[Lantern]] lit, causing them to form a circle around Link. They usually appear near quicksand in an attempt to drag him down faster.

Despite their name, they are not poisonous, and cause no damage to Link.

==Trivia==
*Poison Mites bear similar traits with [[Morth]]s.
