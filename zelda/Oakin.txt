==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Oakin}}
Oakin is a [[Korok]] who lives in the [[Forest Haven]]. Before the Forest Ceremony, Oakin can be seen standing by the Baba Bud on the floor. After the Forest Ceremony, Oakin goes to the [[Star Island]] to plant new [[Tree]]s.
