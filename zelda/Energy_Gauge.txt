The {{Term/Store|Energy Gauge|Energy Gauges|Series, ALBW, TFH}} is a recurring feature in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TFH Blue|energy gauge}} on the left is not shared between heroes. Go wild!|Mr. Tudor|TFH}}</ref>

==Uses==
The Energy Gauge functions similarly to both the [[Stamina Gauge]] and the [[Magic Meter]]. [[Link]] obtains the Energy Gauge when [[Ravio]] gives him the [[Bow]]. The Energy Gauge is drained whenever Link uses any item or [[Wall Merging|Wall Merges]].<ref>{{Cite|This is the energy gauge. It depletes when you use Ravio's items and recharges over time. If your energy runs out, you can't use Ravio's items until it replenishes. Try it out using the Bow you just got.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> When the Energy Gauge has been completely depleted, Link will be unable to use any item until it replenishes.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= This gauge will deplete while you are merged with walls and when you use items. It will gradually replenish over time. While the gauge is depleted, you will be unable to use items.|game= ALBW|page= }}</ref> If the Energy Gauge runs out while Link is merged into a wall, he will be forced out of it.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Spending time as a wall painting will deplete your energy gauge. If the gauge runs out while you are in the wall, you will automatically exit the wall.|game= ALBW|page= }}</ref> The Energy Gauge can be refilled by finding [[Energy Potion]]s inside purple [[Pot]]s.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, the Energy Gauge size can be increased by obtaining the [[Stamina Scroll]] in the [[Ice Ruins]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, the [[Energy Gear]] [[Outfit]] increases the Energy Gauge size for the Link who wears it, while the [[Cheer Outfit]] increases the Energy Gauge for the other Links on his team. Both Outfits provide the same effect in a single-player game.

==={{EoW|-}}===
The Energy Gauge can be upgraded incrementally using [[Might Crystal|Might Crystals]]:<br/>
• Level 2: Requires 5 Might Crystals.<br/>
• Level 3: Requires an additional 10 Might Crystals (15 total).<br/>
• Level 4: Requires an additional 20 Might Crystals (35 total).<br/>

Zelda can equip Mastery [[Accessories]] obtained from the [[Slumber Dojo]] to reduce the energy consumption rate of her [[Swordfighter Form]].<br/>
• [[First Mastery]]: Reduces energy consumption by 10%.<br/>
• [[Second Mastery]]: Reduces energy consumption by 20%.<br/>
• [[Third Mastery]]: Reduces energy consumption by 30%.<br/>


==Trivia==
*In early versions of {{ALBW|-}}, the Energy Gauge was green instead of purple, similar to a Magic Meter. It also featured a hexagonal shape instead of a round one. In {{TFH|-}}, the {{ALBW|-}} Energy Gauge was also used in early versions of the game. It was later slightly redesigned.
