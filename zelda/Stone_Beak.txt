{{Term/Store|Stone Beak|Stone Beaks|Series, LADX|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Dungeon Item|link}} in {{LA|DX}}.<ref name="E 140"/>

==Location and Uses==
Stone Beaks can be found throughout the Dungeons of [[Koholint Island]]. They are found within a [[Treasure Chest]] in every dungeon, along with an accompanying [[Owl Statue]]. Owl Statues reside on walls with a hole where its beak should be. If [[Link]] attaches a Stone Beak to an Owl Statue, it will reveal a hint about either a [[Puzzle]], an enemy, a [[Sub-Boss]], or the Dungeon's [[Boss]]. Link can not understand the Owl Statue's hint until the beak is attached.<ref>{{Cite|This owl statue is trying to say something, but you can't understand it because it has no beak.|N/A|LADX}}</ref>

Stone Beaks replace [[Stone Slab Fragment]]s from {{LA}}. They are identical in every way but name and appearance. Instead of attaching to Owl Statues, they attach to [[Stone Slab]]s.
