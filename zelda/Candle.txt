{{Term/Store|Candle|Candles|Series, TAoL, ZA|plural}} are recurring [[item]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= ZA|page= 4}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
In {{TAoL}}, the Candle can be found in the [[Parapa Palace]]<ref>{{Cite|GET CANDLE IN PARAPA PALACE. GO WEST.|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref> after defeating an [[Iron Knuckle]]. It lights up otherwise pitch-black caves and allows Link to see any enemies who may dwell within.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= When this is obtained, places that you had not been able to see until now because of the dark are lit up.|game= TAoL|page= 46}}</ref> The Candle is not required to beat the game, but it is more difficult to navigate dark [[cave]]s without it.

==Spinoff appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{ZA|-}}===
In {{ZA}}, Candles are single-use items that can be purchased from several [[Merchant]]s around [[Tolemac]] for 100 [[Rupee]]s. They are used to light up dark rooms inside of [[dungeon]]s. Candles have little purpose since there are only two dark rooms: the last part of the cave maze within the [[Shrine of Earth]] and in the [[Stalfos]] maze within the [[Shrine of Illusion]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
