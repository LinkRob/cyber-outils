{{Term/Store|Dacto|Dactos|Series, ALttP, LA, OoS, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 172 {{Exp Game|ALttP, LA, OoS, ALBW}}}}</ref>{{Note|Dactos are referred to as '''Ravens''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 97}}</ref> However, because Dacto's name was officially stated in {{E}}, Raven is no longer considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Plural|ALttP|Dacto}} are pterosaur-like {{Plural|ALttP|Enemy|link}} exclusive to the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} in {{ALttP}}, acting as a counterpart to the {{Term|ALttP|Light World|link}}'s {{Plural|ALttP|Crow|link}}. They are commonly found on rocky outcrops and in the {{Term|ALttP|Swamp of Evil|link}}, where they can be found sitting on {{Plural|ALttP|Tree|link}}. When they spot {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} they will swoop down at him and continually pursue him until they are defeated or {{Term|ALttP|Link}} leaves the area.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LANS}}
{{Plural|LANS|Dacto}} can be found in mountainous regions such as {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Mountain Range|link}} and near the entrance of {{Term|LANS|Turtle Rock|link}} in {{LA}}. They swoop at {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} at sight.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Stub|OoS}}
{{Plural|OoS|Dacto}} appear in the {{Term|OoS|Holodrum Plain|link}} in {{OoS}}. They swoop in to steal {{Term|OoS|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|OoS|Rupee|link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Stub|ALBW}}
{{Plural|ALBW|Dacto}} appear solely in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} in {{ALBW}}. They take on the same role as {{Plural|ALBW|Crow|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}.
