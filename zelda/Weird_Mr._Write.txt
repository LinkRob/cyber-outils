==Features and Overview==
{{Term|LANS|Weird Mr. Write}} is a {{Term|LANS|House|link}} located in {{Term|LANS|Goponga Swamp|link}}. Its owner, {{Term|LANS|Mr. Write|link}}, can be found here. After trading the {{Term|LANS|Hibiscus|link}} to {{Term|LANS|Christine|link}} in exchange for the {{Term|LANS|Goat's Letter|link}}, {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} can bring the {{Term|LANS|Goat's Letter|display=Letter}} to {{Term|LANS|Mr. Write}} in exchange for the {{Term|LANS|Broom|link}}.

{{Term|LANS|Mr. Write}} keeps a {{Wp|scarlet macaw}} in his {{Term|LANS|House}} as a pet.
