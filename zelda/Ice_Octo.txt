'''Ice Octo''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Ice Octo|This monster freezes foes with icy spit. The cold within it leaks out of its head.<ref name="Ice Octo">{{Cite|This monster freezes foes with icy spit. The cold within it leaks out of its head.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Ice Octos are icy variants of [[Octorok|Octoroks]] found on [[Hebra Mountains|Hebra Mountain]] that attack from a distance by spitting ice at its targets. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can use the [[Tri Rod]] to learn the Ice Octo [[Echo]], which costs two Echo energy to summon.
