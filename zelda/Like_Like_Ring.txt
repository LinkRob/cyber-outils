The {{Term/Store|Like Like Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Like Like Ring<br/>Become a Like Like|Inventory|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Like Like Ring<br/>Become a Like Like|Inventory|OoA}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Like Like Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be found inside [[Jabu-Jabu's Belly]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it transforms [[Link]] into an [[Like Like]].<ref name="Description"/> While in this shape, Link cannot use items nor can be eaten by Like Likes. The Like Like Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
