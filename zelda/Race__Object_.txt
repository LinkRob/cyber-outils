{{Term/Store|Race|Races|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}} used to reveal {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}}.<ref name="Piggyback 330">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 330 |edition= Expanded Edition |year= 2018}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Race (Object)}} appear throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. They appear with a starting platform in the form of a tree stump with a leaf symbol in the center.<ref name="Piggyback 330"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} stands on the stump, a yellow light shoots out of the stump and lands somewhere nearby, creating a ring of light that slowly shrinks. A timer begins and challenges {{Term|BotW|Link}} to get through the ring before the ring disappears. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} fails to make it through the ring, he can try again by standing on the stump. If he makes it in the ring, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Piggyback 330"/> {{Plural|BotW|Race (Object)}} always challenge {{Term|BotW|Link}} in ways involving sprinting, climbing, gliding with the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Swimming|link}}.
