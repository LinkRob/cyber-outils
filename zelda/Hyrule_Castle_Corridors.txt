The {{Term/Store|Hyrule Castle Corridors||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 238}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors}} are located throughout the interior of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. They connect the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Reception Room|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Square Spiral Staircase|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Library|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber|link}} together. A {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors|display=Corridors}} near the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}}.

During "{{Term|BotW|EX Royal Guard Rumors|link}}," a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can read {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V4|link}}, which mentions that a piece of equipment worn by the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard|link}} can be found near the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}}.<ref>{{Cite|Amongst {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule's soldiers}}, only the best of the best had the honor of guarding the royal family. Apparently, the equipment they wore can still be found somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. They say a former maid spoke of treasure near the {{Color|BotW Red|Dining Hall}}, in the {{Color|BotW Red|Guards' Chamber}}, and on {{Color|BotW Red|the second floor}}.|Super Rumor Mill V4|BotW}}</ref> In the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors}} west of the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find an EX {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind a bombable wall. When he opens this {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, he will receive the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Uniform|link}}. Another stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found behind a bombable wall in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors|display=Corridors}} near the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}}, which contains three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
Two {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found behind bombable walls in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors}}. One is between the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}} and the {{Term|BotW|Square Spiral Staircase}}. The other is outside of the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a jar hanging from the ceilings in each of these newly revealed rooms. Using {{Term|BotW|Archery (Mechanic)|link}}, he can shoot the jars, which causes the {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} to appear and give him {{Plural|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
