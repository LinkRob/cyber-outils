{{Term/Store|A Hidden Treasure||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 48 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|A Hidden Treasure|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given six minutes to reach a Targeted Position on the other side of the map where a {{Term|HWAoC|Treasure Chest|link}} is located. The specific path taken by the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} to the chest, as there is only one that makes up most of the map, contains four groups of {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} and that are spaced out from each other and wait for the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} to approach them. At around the center of the map is a room, in which two {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} and a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} spawn as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} approaches it. Then, the objective changes to defeating 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, and must be reached to reopen the exit door. To make it possible for the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} to do so, {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} repeatedly spawn in the room until the objective is completed. From the room to the Targeted Position, there are six groups of {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} spaced out along the path, along with two that appear when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} approaches the area. Right before the Targeted Position, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} will find a {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}}, and more {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} appear to greet them. After the blademaster's defeat, the door opens to the Targeted Position. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} enters the room, {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} spawns, a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} appears, and the time limit is taken away. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Bass|link}}, and 600 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.
