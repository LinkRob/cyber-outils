{{Term/Store|Gisa Crater||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Gisa Crater'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Gisa Crater is located in west [[Tabantha Frontier]]. It is a very large hole in the ground with many smaller holes around the perimeter of the main one.

A Korok can be found in a small hole northwest of the largest one. There is a large circle of rocks making a target-shape in the crater. [[Link]] must drop a rock into the center of the target to make the Korok appear.
