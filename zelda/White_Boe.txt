{{Term/Store|White Boe|White Boes|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are enemies in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= It's a White Boe... Just calm down and attack it! Yeah, yeah, yeah...There are just a lot of them, OK?
}}

{{Plural|MM3D|White Boe}} are the snow-dwelling counterpart of the {{Plural|MM3D|Black Boe|link}}. They can be found around {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Mountain Village|link}} and in the {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead Temple|link}}. Unlike the {{Plural|MM3D|Black Boe}}, {{Plural|MM3D|White Boe|link}} favor the brightness of the snow, rather than the darkness, and can potentially blend into their lighter surroundings. A {{Term|MM3D|White Boe}} can commonly be found in pine {{Plural|MM3D|Tree|link}}. They replace {{Plural|MM3D|Blue Tektite|link}} as the common {{Plural|MM3D|Snowhead}} enemy at night.

Before they reach the ground, {{Plural|MM3D|White Boe}} resemble large snowflakes, appearing like a soft flurry.
