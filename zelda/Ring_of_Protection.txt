{{Term/Store|Ring of Protection|Rings of Protection|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Ring of Protection}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Ring of Protection}} are equipment that boosts the user's defense by reducing any damage done to them by half a {{Term|CoH|Heart|link}} when equipped by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Reduces all damage done to you by half a heart.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain minibosses.

==Trivia==
They share their name with the Ring of Protection item from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
