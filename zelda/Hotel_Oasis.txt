{{Term/Store|Hotel Oasis||Series, BotW}} is an inn located within [[Gerudo Town]] in {{BotW}} and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite|Hotel Oasis|Sign|BotW}}</ref><ref name=":0" />

==Features and Overview==
In {{BotW|-}}, Hotel Oasis is managed by [[Romah]] and advertised by {{Term|BotW|Olu|link}}. Aside from the usual 20 [[rupee]] cost of a night at an [[Inn]], Romah also offers a popular 80 rupee spa plan.<ref name=":1">{{Cite|We're an inn, of course, but we also have a special {{Color|BotW Blue|spa plan}}! A vacation for mind and body!|Romah|BotW}}</ref><ref name=":2">{{Cite|It's really, really popular. So popular, in fact, that we always have a long line of people waiting to get in!|Romah|BotW}}</ref><ref name=":3">{{Cite|The nightly rate is {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}, but we also offer a special spa plan what would bring the total to {{Color|BotW Red|80 rupees}}!|Romah|BotW}}</ref> Romah claims that the spa plan will fill Link with energy and a feeling of resilience to danger.<ref name=":4">{{Cite|The spa plan we offer will fill you with energy and a truly unique feeling of resilience to great dangers.|Romah|BotW}}</ref> She also mentions that it will relax Link and leave his skin bright and smooth.<ref>{{Cite|And as an extra bonus, it'll feel like years of worry have been washed away, leaving your skin bright and smooth.|Romah|BotW}}</ref> The spa plan will refill [[Link]]'s [[Life Gauge]] with an additional three temporary Hearts and give him a temporary full wheel of [[Stamina]] when he wakes up. During the spa treatment, Romah may comment on how muscular Link is for a [[Hylian]].<ref>{{Cite|You're pretty muscular...for a Hylian. I like your voe-ish figure.|Romah|BotW}}</ref> A Hylian named [[Tauma]] can be found reading a book in here. She has been waiting for her spa plan treatment for quite some time.<ref>{{Cite|Seriously...how long am I gonna have to wait?|Tauma|BotW}}</ref> Tauma notices if Link received the spa treatment, mentioning that he looks relaxed and that his skin looks shiny.<ref>{{Cite|You know, your skin looks super shiny, and you look like you haven't got a worry in the world...|Tauma|BotW}}</ref> [[Malena]] can be found staying at the Inn during the night.

In ''Tears of the Kingdom'', the Hotel Oasis reopens once the quest [[Riju of the Gerudo]] has been completed. [[Olu]] works as an apprentice, who [[Romah]] is training Olu to use her special technique.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Olu}} is apprenticing under {{Color|BotW Red|Romah}}. She's the only vai who has ever had the opportunity to inherit Romah's special technique. But... she's still learning. The effects of her spa plan are a bit less reliable, you might say.|Jules|ToTK}}</ref> [[Jules]] now works as an assistant.<ref>{{Cite|Originally I was just another customer coming here to relax, but I got hooked... Now I work here as an assistant, ha!|Jules|ToTK}}</ref> Since he saved the town, Romah allows Link to cut the waiting list for a stay at the Hotel.<ref>{{Cite|You did save the town, after all, so you're a bit of a special case... You can skip the line whenever you want!|Romah|ToTK}}</ref> The spa plan is now 50 rupees,<ref>{{Cite|The standard stay is {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}, and the spa plan is {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}}!|Romah|ToTK}}</ref> and provides Link with 3 temporary hearts and a full temporary stamina wheel.

Olu provides her own, cheaper spa plan for 25 rupees. She is still learning, so her plan only provides the same benefits as Romah's some of the time - the rest, it just restores Link's health like a normal rest at an inn.<ref>{{Cite|I'm really only an apprentice, so there's still a lot left to learn. Oh, but even so, I can guarantee that it will make your body feel as good as new. Don't worry about that! But—and this is what I'm still working on—if I get things just right... you'll feel a boost of energy {{Color|BotW Red|just like}} with the owner's spa plan! The apprentice spa plan is only {{Color|BotW Red|25 rupees}} too! That's {{Color|BotW Red|half}} of what the owner's plan costs, you know!|Olu|ToTK}}</ref>
