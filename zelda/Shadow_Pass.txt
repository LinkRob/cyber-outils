The {{Term/Store|Shadow Pass||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Shadow Pass'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Shadow Pass}} is a road in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands}} connecting the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}} that cuts through a dense forest. At the northern and southern sections of the forest, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find broken {{Plural|BotW|Guardian (Breath of the Wild)|display=Guardians|link}}, which can be searched for {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}. Also at the northern end are the remains of a wagon, which travelers may take refuge under. On one of the sides of this wagon, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Chabi|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Shadow Pass}}, defending herself from a {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}. Similarly, the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} travelers, {{Term|BotW|Laroba|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Naddon|link}}, can be seen walking through the {{Term|BotW|Shadow Pass}}. The {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveler, {{Term|BotW|Pitar|link}}, can also be seen riding through the {{Term|BotW|Shadow Pass}} on horseback. The northeastern section of the {{Term|BotW|Shadow Pass}}'s forest is situated on a lowered segment of land. {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} have set up an encampment in this spot, atop of which rests a wooden {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that contains a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}, valued at 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. The walls around this lowered area also feature {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found atop a large pine tree in the northwestern section of the forest.
