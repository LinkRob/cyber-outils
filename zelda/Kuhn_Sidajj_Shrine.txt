The {{Term/Store|Kuhn Sidajj Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kuhn Sidajj Shrine}}<br/>Kuhn Sidajj's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Kuhn Sidajj's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Kuhn Sidajj Shrine}} is on the shore of {{Term|BotW|Lake Saria|link}} in the southwest corner of the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must first talk to {{Term|BotW|Zooki|link}} at the entrance of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|link}}, who will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} the "{{Term|BotW|Trial of Second Sight|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. This {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|display=Quest}} requires {{Term|BotW|Link}} to follow a path of {{Plural|BotW|Ogre Tree|link}} with metal {{Plural|BotW|Boulder|link}} in them through the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let me tell you about the {{Color|BotW Red|trial}}. All you need to do is keep going and not get lost in the mist! Easy, right? What's wrong? You don't look very excited. But don't worry—I've got a cryptic little hint for you! "The hungry trees will show you the way. Spy their {{Color|BotW Red|iron}} to win the day." Didn't I make that sound cool? Think about that as you take on the trial.|Zooki|BotW}}</ref>

Using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} will highlight the metal {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}}, making it much easier for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to see the path. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} follows the path, he must place a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield|link}} into the mouth of an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}}, which causes a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a piece of {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}} to appear in the mouth of its brother.<ref>{{Cite|Feed me the aged shield guarded by monsters eternal. My brother will repay you in kind.|Sign|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} was stolen from an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}} on a small island in {{Term|BotW|Lake Saria}}.<ref>{{Cite|Please place my chest in my mouth. <small>The shield eater's brother stole it from me.</small>|Sign|BotW}}</ref> Returning the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} to the mouth of the {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}} on the island causes {{Term|BotW|Kuhn Sidajj Shrine}} to rise on the {{Term|BotW|Lake Saria|display=Lake}}'s northeastern shore, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kuhn Sidajj|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kuhn Sidajj. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Kuhn Sidajj|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Kuhn Sidajj Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to {{Term|BotW|Kuhn Sidajj}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Kuhn Sidajj}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kuhn Sidajj|BotW}}</ref> They will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kuhn Sidajj|BotW}}</ref>
