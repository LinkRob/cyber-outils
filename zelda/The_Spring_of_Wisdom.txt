"{{Term/Store|The Spring of Wisdom||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Spring of Wisdom}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Hateno Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Medda|link}}, who states there is not much to see in their town.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Traveler, right? I could tell. Well, there's not a whole lot to see in this village, but knock yourself out.|Medda|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} presses further, {{Term|BotW|Medda}} shares that there is a {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link|display=dye shop}} in town,<ref>{{Cite|[Nothing of interest?] Well... "Nothing" might be going a little bit overboard... We do have a {{Color|BotW Blue|dye shop}}. I don't think they have one of those in other towns.|Medda|BotW}}</ref> though he also points out {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}} to the north.<ref>{{Cite|We also have {{Color|BotW Blue|Mount Lanayru}} way up to the north. See it?|Medda|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s confusion, {{Term|BotW|Medda}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|link}}, where {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} purified herself, is on top of the mountain.<ref>{{Cite|[Mount Lanayru?] Yeah, {{Color|BotW Blue|Mount Lanayru}}'s interesting... The {{Color|BotW Red|Spring of Wisdom}} the princess of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule}} used for the purification is said to be somewhere on that mountain.|Medda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Medda}} suspects that the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} left a treasure at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom}}.<ref>{{Cite|A special fountain? That the princess had to endure hardship to visit? I bet the royal family left a treasure there! ...Well, at least maybe they could've. I would've if I was a royal.|Medda|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru}} is covered in {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Medda}} thinks it is unlikely anyone would be able to find out.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, {{Color|BotW Blue|Mount Lanayru}} is a mysterious place, and it's engulfed in ice. If there IS treasure, no one's getting it.|Medda|BotW}}</ref> However, he encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to explore up there if he has the opportunity.<ref>{{Cite|Well...if you have some time to kill, feel free to have a look around up there.|Medda|BotW}}</ref> When he finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.{{Note|It is possible to immediately complete this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} by climbing {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru}}, rescuing {{Term|BotW|Naydra}}, and unlocking {{Term|BotW|Jitan Sa'mi Shrine}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Medda}}.}}

On top of {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Malice Naydra|link}} curled around the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom}}'s {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}}. Through the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|display=Statue}}, {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}} congratulates {{Term|BotW|Link}} for finding the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|display=Spring}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have done well to find your way to this spring.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> She explains that {{Term|BotW|Naydra|link}} is a spirit that has overlooked the land of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link|display=Hyrule}} for ages.<ref>{{Cite|You who have overcome numerous trials and obtained the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spirit Orbs}}... The one you see before you is an attendant to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Wisdom}}. This is {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra}}, the blue spirit of Lanayru. This servant of the Goddess has looked over the spirits of this land for ages, unknown to the world of man.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, it has become corrupted by {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link|display=Calamity Ganon's}} {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|However, the dreaded Malice unleashed by Calamity Ganon has possessed its body and reduced it to this state.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, {{Term|BotW|Hylia}} tasks {{Term|BotW|Link}} with purging the {{Term|BotW|Malice}} from {{Term|BotW|Naydra}}.<ref>{{Cite|You who have received the Spirit Orbs... Free {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra}} from this Malice. Show what your power can achieve!|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Eye of Malice|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Malice Naydra}}'s head opens.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots one of the {{Plural|BotW|Eye of Malice}}, {{Term|BotW|Malice Naydra}} will begin to fly above the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom}}. Using its {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can reach the {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}} to continue shooting at the {{Term|BotW|Malice}}. {{Term|BotW|Malice Naydra}} will make its way towards {{Term|BotW|Naydra Snowfield|link}}. After all of the {{Plural|BotW|Eye of Malice}} have been defeated, {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} will return to its white and blue appearance and fly to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom}}.<ref>{{Cite|You freed {{Color|BotW Red|Naydra}}, the guardian dragon of the Mount Lanayru spring, from the darkness that had overcome it.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Hylia}} congratulates {{Term|BotW|Link}} for freeing {{Term|BotW|Naydra}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to your efforts, {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra}}, the spirit of Mount Lanayru, has been freed from the grips of an {{Color|BotW Red|evil power}}.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> She instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to shoot {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} with one last {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}, thereby freeing the {{Term|BotW|Dragon}}.<ref>{{Cite|But a single ceremony remains. Now... Loose your arrow through the body of {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra}} to free the spirit of this region!|Naydra|BotW}}</ref> When he does, one of {{Term|BotW|Naydra's Scale|link}} will drop right in front of {{Term|BotW|Link}} as it flies away.<ref>{{Cite|When {{Color|BotW Red|Naydra}} soared away into the heavens, it left behind {{Color|BotW Red|Naydra's scale}}.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That is the spirit {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra's scale}}. It fell when your arrow struck. It serves as proof of the courage you received from the one who served the spring since ancient times.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Hylia}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to drop it in the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|display=Spring}}.<ref>{{Cite|Come... Offer a {{Color|BotW Blue|scale}} from the blue spirit {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra}} to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Wisdom}}.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> When this is done, the door to {{Term|BotW|Jitan Sa'mi Shrine|link}} will open, and {{Term|BotW|Hylia}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go forward.<ref>{{Cite|Your path has shown itself. Now go forth.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.

===Objectives===

{{Quest Objective|Entry|2|You found no treasure but instead discovered {{Color|BotW Red|Naydra}}, the Spring of Wisdom's guardian spirit, who has been overtaken by darkness.<br/><br/>Break through the evil force possessing {{Color|BotW Red|Naydra}} to set this great spirit free!}}

{{Quest Objective|Entry|3|You freed {{Color|BotW Red|Naydra}}, the guardian dragon of the Mount Lanayru spring, from the darkness that had overcome it.<br/><br/>When {{Color|BotW Red|Naydra}} soared away into the heavens, it left behind {{Color|BotW Red|Naydra's scale}}.}}
