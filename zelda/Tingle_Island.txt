==Features and Overview==

{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>GREAT TINGLE ISLAND!!</p><p>Oh! The [[Tingle Tuner]] is getting nice reception. I can see you from here on Tingle Tower! Mr. Fairy! Come in! Do you read me? I'm here! Let's play!</p>

The Island is the home of [[Tingle]], his brothers [[Ankle]], [[David Jr.]] and [[Knuckle]]. [[Link]] will make several visits to this Island during his adventure for the sole purpose of asking Tingle to decipher the [[Triforce Chart]]s he has collected. Five stone slabs mark the spot on the Island where the optional [[Tingle Statue]]s will be seen. Those statues are found only by using the [[Tingle Tuner]] in dungeons.<ref name="Statue"/> [[Tingle's Chart]] is dedicated to locating this Island. Supposedly, the tower on Tingle Island was built by Tingle's brother, Knuckle, when he was only ten years old.<ref>{{Cite|Why'd you doodle on Tingle Tower? I built that thing!|Knuckle|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Knuckle finished carving Tingle Tower when he was ten years old.|Nintendo Gallery Figurine Description|TWW}}</ref> Tingle claims "Fairy magic" makes the tower's head spin.<ref>{{Cite|Goodbye, Mr. Fairy! Please come visit me here at Tingle Tower again, where wonderful fairy magic makes the place spin and spin!|Tingle|TWW}}</ref> In reality, it is Ankle and David Jr. who spin the tower manually all day long.

A [[Piece of Heart]] can be retrieved from the bottom of the sea by defeating a [[Big Octo]] located near this Island.
