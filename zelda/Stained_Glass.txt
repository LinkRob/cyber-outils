{{Term/Store|Stained Glass|Stained Glass|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 161 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Stained Glass}} goes unnamed in {{ALttP}}, {{OoT}}, {{MM}}, {{FSA}}, {{TMC}}, {{TP}}, {{PH}}, {{ST}}, {{SS}}, {{ALBW}}, {{MK8}}, {{HW}}, {{SSB4|Wii U}}, and {{SSBU}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Term|ALttP|Stained Glass}} can be found in the {{Term|ALttP|Sanctuary|link}} in {{ALttP}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Term|OoT3D|Stained Glass}} adorning the walls of {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon's Tower|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon's Castle|link}} in {{OoT}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Term|MM3D|Stained Glass}} is used for windows and light fixtures in the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar|link}} in {{MM}}.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Term|TWWHD|Stained Glass}} depicting the battle between the {{Plural|TWWHD|Sage|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Ganon|link}} as well as the latter's sealing within the {{Term|TWWHD|Sacred Realm|link}} can be found inside the chamber housing the {{Term|TWWHD|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link}} in {{TWW}}.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Term|FSA|Stained Glass}} can be found inside {{Term|FSA|Hyrule Castle|link}} in the chamber housing the {{Term|FSA|Portal|link}} to the {{Term|FSA|Four Sword Sanctuary|link}} in {{FSA}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Stained Glass}} depictions of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Legend|link}} can be found within the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} in {{TMC}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Term|TP|Stained Glass}} can be found in the chamber housing the [[Pedestal of the Master Sword]] in the [[Temple of Time (Dungeon)|Temple of Time]] in the distant past in {{TP}}. By inserting the [[Master Sword]] into its Pedestal, a magical staircase will appear before {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}, leading directly to a {{Term|TPHD|Stained Glass}} window that disappears when approached. This is the only way into the depths of the Temple of Time. In the present, {{Term|TP|Stained Glass}} can be found inside [[Agitha's Castle]].

==={{PH|-}}===
The {{Term|PH|Stained Glass}} from {{Term|PH|Hyrule Castle|link}} can be seen during {{Term|PH|Niko the Pirate's Picture Show|link}} at the beginning of {{PH}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
A {{Term|ST|Stained Glass}} depiction of {{Term|ST|Tetra|link}} can be seen in the audience chamber of {{Term|ST|Hyrule Castle|link}} in {{ST}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Term|SS|Stained Glass}} is used for windows and light fixtures throughout {{Term|SS|Skyloft|link}} and the {{Term|SS|Pumpkin Landing|link}} in {{SS}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Term|ALBW|Stained Glass}} can be seen inside the {{Term|ALBW|Sanctuary|link}} in {{ALBW}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{MK8|-}}===
{{Term|MK8D|Stained Glass}} can be found inside {{Term|MK8D|Hyrule Castle|link}} in {{MK8}}. Each {{Term|MK8D|Stained Glass}} window bears the symbol of one of the {{Plural|MK8D|Medallion|link}} from {{OoT}} at the top.

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Term|HWDE|Stained Glass}} can be seen throughout {{Term|HWDE|Skyloft (Stage)|link}} and the {{Term|HWDE|Pumpkin Landing|link}} in {{HW}}.

==={{SSB4|Wii U|-}}===
{{Term|SSBfWU|Stained Glass}} appears in {{Term|SSBfWU|Skyloft|link}} in {{SSB4|Wii U}}.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
{{Term|SSBU|Stained Glass}} appears in {{Term|SSBU|Skyloft|link}} in {{SSBU}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In the code of {{TMC}}, three unused sprites for {{Term|TMC|Stained Glass}} exist; they depict the {{Term|TMC|Great Deku Tree|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Valoo|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Jabun|link}}.
