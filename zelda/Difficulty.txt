{{Term/Store|Difficulty||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE, HWAoC}} is a recurring mechanic in {{TLoZ|series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|HWDE X}} Change Difficulty|Select Scenario|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Difficulty|System|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{HW|-}}===
There are four {{Term|HWDE|Difficulty}} levels in {{HW}}: Easy, Normal, Hard, and {{Term|HWDE|Hero Mode|link|display= Hero}}, with {{Term|HWDE|Hero Mode|display= Hero}} {{Term|HWDE|Difficulty}} only being unlocked after beating all {{Plural|HWDE|Scenario|link}}. In the original {{HW|-}}, only the base-game eighteen {{Plural|HW|Scenario}} needed to be beaten, but in {{HW|L}} and {{HWDE}}, all thirty-two must be completed. Higher {{Term|HWDE|Difficulty}} levels will make {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy|link}} deal more damage and have more health, but will also increase the amount of {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee|link}} gained upon completion of a {{Term|HWDE|Scenario}}. {{Term|HWDE|Difficulty}} may only be applied to a {{Term|HWDE|Scenario}}, and not {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}} battles.<br/>In each {{Term|HWDE|Scenario}}, there is a {{Term|HWDE|Gold Skulltula|link}} that may only be found while playing on Hard or {{Term|HWDE|Hero Mode|display= Hero}} {{Term|HWDE|Difficulty}}.

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
In {{HWAoC}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Difficulty}} modes Easy, Normal, Hard, or Very Hard may be chosen for any {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario}} or {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}. As part of the {{PotA}} [[Downloadable Content]] pack, an Apocalyptic {{Term|HWAoC|Difficulty}} mode was added. The {{Term|HWAoC|Difficulty}} level alters how much damage {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} do, as well as how much damage it takes to deplete their {{Term|HWAoC|Weak Point Gauge|link}}, but it does not effect the number of {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}} gained, nor any other rewards.
