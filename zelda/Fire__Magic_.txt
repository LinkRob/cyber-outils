The {{Term/Store|Fire||Series, TAoL}} magic is an ability in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 108}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Fire (Magic)}} was referred to as '''Fire Spell''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Fire Spell}}<br/>During Link's quest to recover the six shards of the Magic Crystal, Link frequently used the power of magical Spells. Link used the Fairy Spell to turn into a fairy, which allowed him to enter small openings which he would otherwise be too big to fit through.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160307135902/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/f.jsp#FireSpell|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Overview==
The Fire magic allows [[Link]] to shoot fireballs out of his [[Sword]] from a distance.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= When this button is used you can throw a fireball while swinging a sword using B button.|game= TAoL|page= 44}}</ref> The young hero can obtain this powerful magic by taking water from a nearby fountain to a woman who is thirsty in [[Nabooru (Town)|Nabooru Town]]. As a reward, she lets him in his house to meet the Old Man in the basement.
