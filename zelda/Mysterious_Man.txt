The {{Term/Store|Mysterious Man||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
The Mysterious Man is the only Hylian found in the [[Skull Woods Region]]. As [[Link]] meets him the first time, the Mysterious Man is expecting a search party but is angry at the fact that Link is instead a kid.<ref>{{Cite|So...they finally sent a rescue party, eh? Wait...you're no party - you're just a kid!|Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref> He next assumes that Link is seeking extreme outdoors activities and proceeds to tell Link how he got dizzy and lost while excessively trunking in the Skull Woods.<ref>{{Cite|Lemme guess, you came in here looking for some extreme fun in the great outdoors, huh?|Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah, I've been running crazy in these woods, running till I got dizzy. Trunking, I call it. |Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I trunked and trunked 'round these trees until I finally lost my way. Been sitting here ever since.|Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref> He offers Link a [[Golden Bee]] for 888 [[Rupee]]s as long as he has an empty [[Bottle]].<ref>{{Cite|But I found a great treasure in the woods. Maybe you'd care to take it off my hands...?|Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's a little something I picked up when I got stuck. I just decided to keep it.|Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How's 888 Rupees sound? It's a steal of a deal.|Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I can't sell it to you if you don't have an empty bottle, man... C'mon. Help me help you.|Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref> After Link buys it from him, the Mysterious Man is surprised Link blindly trusted a stranger like him without even seeing what he was offering to sell.<ref>{{Cite|Really? You'd just buy an unknown thing and blindly trust some stranger like that?!|Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref> He warns Link not to be so trusting and reveals that misplaced trust is what got him lost.<ref>{{Cite|You shouldn't be so trusting, man. That's how I got like this...except minus the whole buying part.|Mysterious Man|ALBW}}</ref>

The Mysterious Man and his [[Hyrule]] counterpart, [[Bouldering Guy]], share similar fates in that both are stuck in their respective locations as a result of outdoor activities they did before Link finds them. The difference though is that Bouldering Guy is able to escape and return to Hyrule's [[Milk Bar#A Link Between Worlds|Milk Bar]] after Link gives him [[Premium Milk]], whereas the Mysterious Man remains in the cave he took refuge in throughout the rest of the game.
