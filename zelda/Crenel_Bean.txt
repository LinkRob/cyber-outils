{{Term/Store|Crenel Bean|Crenel Beans|Series, TMC|plural}} are objects in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Water is life for {{Color|TMC Blue|Crenel Beans}}!|Forest Picori|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Crenel Bean}} are beans which grow on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}. {{Plural|TMC|Crenel Bean}} come in two varieties: blue {{Plural|TMC|Crenel Bean}}, which grow [[Vine]]s when exposed to {{Term|TMC|Water|link}}, and green Crenel Beans, which only grow when watered with {{Term|TMC|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The blue beans grow when you pour {{Color|TMC Red|water}} on them... But the green beans like {{Color|TMC Red|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water}}. {{Color|TMC Red|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water}} can be found only in a spring here on {{Color|TMC Green|Mount Crenel}}. Well, really, the spring is closer to the base of {{Color|TMC Green|Mount Crenel}}.|Forest Picori|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hmm... It seems there are {{Color|TMC Blue|two types of bean}} on Mount Crenel: blue and green. If the color is different, I suppose how you treat each one is different, too.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> {{Plural|TMC|Crenel Bean}} are required in order for {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to ascend {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}}.

Despite being several times larger than {{Term|TMC|Link}} while he is {{Term|Series|Minish|link}}-sized, he is capable of lifting and carrying a green {{Term|TMC|Crenel Bean}} without the aid of the {{Plural|TMC|Power Bracelet|link}}.
