'''Nintendo Magazine''' is a [[:Category:Magazines|magazine]] published by [[Nintendo]]. Originally only releasing in Japanese, shorter English excerpts of each issue were made available starting with the [[Nintendo Magazine (Winter 2021)|Winter 2021]] issue.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo|published= December 14, 2021|retrieved= March 9, 2022|url= https://www.nintendo.com/sg/news/article/NgYngT6SokRkbg1ny3S7Q|title= Excerpts from the Japanese version of the Nintendo Magazine 2021 Winter (Digital edition) is available in English|site= Nintendo|type= }}</ref>
