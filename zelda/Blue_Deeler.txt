{{Term/Store|Blue Deeler|Blue Deelers|Series, TAoL|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 174{{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Blue Deeler}} go unnamed in {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics|-}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
{{Manual|TAoL|Deeler|Moves up and down by a string suspended from a tree. Blue Deelers drop down to the ground to attack Link.}}
{{Plural|TAoL|Blue Deeler}} are giant {{Plural|TAoL|Spider|link}} that inhabit {{Plural|TAoL|Forest|link}}. They can be seen roaming upside down in {{Plural|TAoL|Tree|link}}.

When their prey is beneath them, they rapidly drop on the ground using their silken thread and start leaping at their prey. They can be slain using a {{Term|TAoL|Sword|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}} (Valiant Comics)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
A Deeler briefly makes an appearance in the "[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 1#Missing in Action|Missing in Action]]" story of {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}. As [[Princess Zelda]] is riding away from [[Hyrule]] with her trusty horse [[Storm]], a Deeler gets in the way of her path and scares Storm, causing Zelda to get knocked off into the ground.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=A Deeler!
