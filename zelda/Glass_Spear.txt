==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Glass Spear}} are strong yet brittle {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}.
