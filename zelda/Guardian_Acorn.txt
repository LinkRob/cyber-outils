==Location and Uses==
[[File:LADX Link Obtaining Guardian Acorn.png|left|thumb|Link obtaining a Guardian Acorn in ''Link's Awakening DX'']]
{{Plural|LADX|Guardian Acorn}} are magical acorns that appear after defeating 12 consecutive [[Enemy|enemies]] without getting hit.<ref name="LA Guide 89" /> When obtained, the music changes while halving all damage {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} receives for three hits.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref> It also wears off if the hero moves into different areas of the [[overworld]].

The [[Piece of Power]] is its offensive counterpart, as it doubles Link's attack power, instead.

In {{LA|DX}}, when the {{Term|LADX|Blue Clothes|link}} are worn, obtaining a {{Term|LADX|Guardian Acorn}} reduces the original damage taken down to a quarter, resulting in Link becoming rather durable.

== Trivia ==

* This item remains the only case in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] where Link gains increased [[defense]] without changing the color of his [[Green Clothes|tunic]].
