The {{Term/Store|Tower Cannon||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tower Cannon|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Tower Cannon}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Ancient Gold Piece|link}}, three {{Plural|ST|Dark Pearl Loop|link}} and two {{Plural|ST|Pirate Necklace|link}}. Its description reads "This extra-tall cannon resembles a soaring tower."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Tower Cannon}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Stagecoach Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
