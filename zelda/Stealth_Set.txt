The {{Term/Store|Stealth Set||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} set in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 180}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Stealth Set}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} set consisting of the {{Term|BotW|Stealth Mask|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Stealth Chest Guard|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Stealth Tights|link}}. Each piece of the {{Term|BotW|Stealth Set}} can be purchased at "{{Term|BotW|Enchanted|link}}," an {{Term|BotW|Armor}} shop in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} designed this armor for stealth purposes, so each piece of the {{Term|BotW|Stealth Set}} provides the '''Stealth Up''' effect. Wearing the entire set while every piece is upgraded at least twice provides the '''Night Speed Up''' set bonus, which increases {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s movement speed at night. These bonuses all have one major drawback however: each piece has lower defense than other forms of armor.

Unlike regular {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} clothing, which can't be purchased by folk of other races,<ref>{{Cite|The folks in Kakariko Village have really amazing clothes that let them work out in the rain with no problem. Amazing! But only the Sheikah people can wear 'em, so they won't sell us any...|Sagessa|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Stealth Set}} is available for all to purchase, such as {{Term|BotW|Link}} or {{Term|BotW|Bugut|link}}{{Fact|BotW}}.

==Trivia==
*Concept artwork of the {{Term|BotW|Stealth Set}} shows that it originally resembled the everyday clothes worn by members of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} tribe.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 180}}</ref> However, the {{Term|BotW|Stealth Set}} was later redesigned to resemble the clothes worn by {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} warriors instead.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 103}}</ref>
