"{{Term/Store|Death Mountain's Secret||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Death Mountain's Secret}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Goron Hot Springs}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link}} is given this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Dugby|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Goron Hot Springs|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Dugby}}, who is found sitting in the {{Term|BotW|Hot Spring|link}}, tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that he had buried a treasure near the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Eldin|link}} and that if he can find it, it is his to keep.<ref>{{Cite|I bid a {{Color|BotW Red|tweshure}} binteen gear an ba {{Color|BotW Blue|Bridge of Eldin}}! Iz an abazing schtick! Beely bool! If boo bind it... iz boars!|Dugby|BotW}}</ref> However, because {{Term|BotW|Dugby}}'s mouth is submerged underwater, his directions come out as gurgles.<ref>{{Cite|Bello! Goo gon't go but I'm thaying?|Dugby|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} visits the {{Term|BotW|Goron Hot Springs}} at night, he can overhear {{Term|BotW|Dugby}} mumbling in his sleep clearer directions to the treasure.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|*snore* The {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}}...at the {{Color|BotW Red|top of a lava waterfall}}...}}|Dugby|BotW}}</ref>

The treasure, a {{Term|BotW|Drillshaft|link}}, can be found hidden beneath a rubble of stones over a lava fall to the east of the {{Term|BotW|Goron Hot Springs|link}}, which can be uncovered by using a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found a {{Color|BotW Red|drillshaft}} buried in a crag between the Bridge of Eldin and Goron Hot Springs.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings the {{Term|BotW|Drillshaft}} to {{Term|BotW|Dugby}}, he is surprised to see that {{Term|BotW|Link}} had found the treasure, but is skeptic about it.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Don't tell me that stick is... [...] That's the {{Color|BotW Blue|drillshaft}} I hid! ...Right? Actually, I need to get a better look at it. Show it me! NOW!|Dugby|BotW}}</ref>  When {{Term|BotW|Link}} shows it to him, {{Term|BotW|Dugby}} will confirm that it is the treasure.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I guess so. It's suspicious though! I can't help thinkin' that's the {{Color|BotW Blue|drillshaft}} I hid... {{Small|There's no mistakin' it... There was definitely a big noise... Could this person be...}} Mmhmmm... So you went and found the {{Color|BotW Blue|drillshaft}} I hid.|Dugby|BotW}}</ref>  He then admits defeat and tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he can keep the Drillshaft.<ref>{{Cite|Well, it looks like I lost this battle! You can go ahead and keep that {{Color|BotW Blue|drillshaft}}.|Dugby|BotW}}</ref> This ends the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
