{{Term/Store|Mighty Thunder of the Gerudo||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 55 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mighty Thunder of the Gerudo|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Mighty Thunder of the Gerudo}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat three {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}, which are a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Hinox|link}} and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} are both at the top of the map, each surrounded by a large group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}. The southern part of the map is where the {{Term|HWAoC|Black Hinox}} is located, and is also filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}}. 

After all three {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} appear in the north, and their electric counterparts spawn in the south. They all must be defeated, and when they are, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox|link}} spawns in the north and an electric one in the south. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when they are defeated.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using only {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Durian|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}, and three {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}.
