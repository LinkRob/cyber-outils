{{Term/Store|Monk's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 33 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Monk's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Monk's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Monk Maz Koshia}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Monk Maz Koshia}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Monk Maz Koshia}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}}, except for four {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} that are scattered around the map. After 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin}} spawns. The time limit is also taken away, so the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin}} are defeated.
