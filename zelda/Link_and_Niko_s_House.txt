{{Term/Store|Link and Niko's House||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link and Niko's House|Sign|ST}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|ST|Link and Niko's House}} is located in {{Term|ST|Aboda Village|link}}. As its name suggests, it is shared by {{Term|ST|Link|link}} and {{Term|ST|Niko|link}}. In it at the beginning, {{Term|ST|Niko}} tells {{Term|ST|Link}} about the events of the {{Term|ST|War|link}} that happened between {{Term|ST|Malladus|link}} and the {{Plural|ST|Spirit of Good|link}}, and how {{Term|Series|Tetra's Pirate Crew|link}} saved the land from destruction. It contains many objects that reference {{TWW}} and {{PH}}, including a {{Term|Series|Telescope|link}}, a framed picture of a younger {{Term|ST|Niko}}, {{Plural|Series|Ship Part|link}}, and a miniature version of {{Term|Series|Tetra's Ship}}.
