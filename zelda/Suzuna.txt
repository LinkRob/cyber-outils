{{Term/Store|Suzuna||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|Suzuna|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}. 

Suzuna can be found at a camp in the Faron Woods. When spoken to, she will ask Link if he's heard of a spring that can bring back lost horses. She is surprised when Link reveals to her that he knows of [[Malanya Spring]].

In {{TotK}}, Suzuna can be found at a camp near the Hyrule Field Chasm located south of [[Lookout Landing]]. She offers logistical support to the monster-control crew, tending to the horses and mixing elixirs as the camp as [[Menter]] repairs weapons and gear. When spoken to, she encourages Link to help Captain Hoz and his crew as they assault a monster stronghold to the south.
