{{Term/Store|Talon's Chicken Game||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a {{Term|OoT3D|Mini-Game|link}} in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= CE|publisher= Nintendo|page= 56}}</ref>

==Location and Rule==
When [[Link]] visits [[Lon Lon Ranch]] in child form and talks to [[Talon]] inside his house, the minigame can be started. The objective of the game is to find three "[[Super Cucco|Super Cuccos]]" (the three clucking Cuccos Talon always has close to him) which have been hidden with a group of normal Cuccos.<ref>{{Cite|Well how about a little game? These three Cuccos I have here are special Super Cuccos! I'm going to throw these Cuccos into that there gaggle of normal Cuccos. If you can pick out these three special birds from among the normal Cuccos within the time limit, I'll give you something good. If you can't find them, I win.|Talon|OoT}}</ref>

Although the mini-game seems simple, there is no discernible difference between a normal Cucco and a Super Cucco. Instead of randomly picking up Cuccos, Link can "cheat" by throwing the group of normal Cuccos behind Talon's desk before starting the challenge. Thus, once Talon throws the Super Cuccos, there won't be any regular Cuccos to confuse the hero.

If Link wins, Talon jokingly asks the young hero  if he wants to marry his daughter [[Malon]].<ref>{{Cite|You've got the talent to be one of the world's best cowboys! How'd you like to marry Malon? Huh? Haw haw! I was just kidding! Just kidding! I think you're a little young for that, aren't you? Haw haw haw!|Talon|OoT}}</ref> Talon will then grant a {{Term|OoT|Bottle|link}} full of [[Lon Lon Milk]]. The minigame can subsequently be replayed to fill an empty bottle with milk, costing 1/3 of the {{Plural|OoT|Rupee|link}} than required to outright buy a refill.

== Trivia ==

* The act of Talon asking Link to marry Malon upon completing this mini-game has become a popular meme among the series' fanbase, especially among ones who believed the [[Hero of Time]] is in a love triangle with not only Malon herself, but [[Princess Zelda]], [[Saria]], and [[Princess Ruto]] as well.
