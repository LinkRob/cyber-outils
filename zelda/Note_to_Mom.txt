The {{Term/Store|Note to Mom||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an item in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
After {{Term|TWW|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TWW|Dragon Roost Cavern|link}}, {{Term|TWW|Baito|link}} can be seen wandering around {{Term|TWW|Dragon Roost Island|link}} trying to apply for the {{Term|TWW|Letter Sorting|link}} job. He is eventually hired at the {{Term|TWW|Island Postal Service|link}} after {{Term|TWW|Koboli|link}}, impressed by {{Term|TWW|Link}}'s contribution to the mail sorting, decides to hire a permanent assistant. {{Term|TWW|Baito}} then allows {{Term|TWW|Link}} to sort letters as a mini-game. If {{Term|TWW|Link}} manages to sort 25 or more letters, {{Term|TWW|Baito}} will ask him to put the {{Term|TWW|Note to Mom}} in the mail. The letter talks about how great {{Term|TWW|Link}} is.

Some time after sending the letter,{{When|TWW}} {{Term|TWW|Link}} will receive a reply from {{Term|TWW|Kashiko|link}}, {{Term|TWW|Baito}}'s mother,<ref>{{Cite|Thank you very much for looking after my boy. This isn't much, but I hope you'll accept it all the same. I politely request your continued kindness towards my son. Kashiko (Baito's mother)|Letter from Kashiko|TWW}}</ref> with a {{Term|TWW|Piece of Heart}} inside.
