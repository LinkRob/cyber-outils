==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Silent Princess}}
The Silent Princess is a rare, endangered flower species.<ref>{{Cite|This one here is called the silent princess. It's a rare, endangered species.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> A century before the events of {{BotW|-}}, the plant could not be grown domestically, thriving only out in the wild.<ref>{{Cite|Despite our efforts, we can't get them to grow domestically yet. The princess can only thrive out here in the wild.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> By the time {{BotW|-}} takes place, the Silent Princess was feared to have gone extinct but was then spotted growing in the wild.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> The flower was said to have been a favorite of [[Princess Zelda]], which is confirmed in the [[Recovered Memory]] [[Silent Princess (Memory)|Silent Princess]].<ref name="Inventory"></ref><ref>{{Cite|She explains her admiration for a flower called the silent princess...|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

During {{BotW|-}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find Silent Princesses growing near {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. The Silent Princess can be used in a dish or elixir for a [[Stealth]] Boost effect. They can also be made into White [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

Married couple [[Tye]] and [[Sorelia]] can be found searching for the Silent Princess.<ref>{{Cite|We've travelled for months in search of a flower called the {{Color|BotW Blue|silent princess}}.|Tye|BotW}}</ref> There is a legend if you swear your love on one, you will live a charmed life with your partner.<ref>{{Cite|Legend has it that if you swear your love before its freshly plucked blossom, you and your partner will live a charmed life.|Tye|BotW}}</ref> However, they've been unsuccessful in their quest.<ref>{{Cite|Alas, all our searching so far has been for naught.|Tye|BotW}}</ref>

Five Silent Princesses, as well as ten {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, are needed to upgrade each piece of the [[Stealth Set]] to its final tier at a Great Fairy Fountain. Silent Princesses are also needed to upgrade the [[Champion's Tunic]], with three for the first, second, and third levels, and ten for the fourth level. Finally, they're used to upgrade [[Sheik's Mask]] to all of its tiers.

* Total for '''Champion's Tunic''': 19 (3, 3, 3, 10)

* Total for '''Sheik's Mask''': 10 (1, 2, 3, 4)

* Total for '''Stealth Set''': 15 (0, 0, 0, 5) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

==Trivia==
*Three Silent Princess flowers grow near the Master Sword's pedestal in [[Korok Forest]]. The locations of these flowers, with two on the left and one on the right, are the same locations of the unidentified flowers in {{ALttP}} that grow near the [[Pedestal of the Master Sword]] in the [[Lost Woods]].
*A single Silent Princess can be found growing in the ruins of [[Princess Zelda's Study]] in [[Hyrule Castle]].
*In the Japanese version, instead of saying that Silent Princess can only thrive in the wild, Zelda muses that perhaps it was destined to become extinct.
*''Tears of the Kingdom'' implies that a Silent Princess is born after the Light Dragon's tears fall into the ground.
