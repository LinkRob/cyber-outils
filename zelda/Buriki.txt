{{Term/Store|Buriki||Series, RTBToL}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 312}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Buriki}} goes unnamed in {{TMTP}}.

==Biography==
==={{TMTP|-}}===
{{Term|Series|Buriki}} appears as [[The High Priestess]] in the [[Tingle Reading]] minigame in {{TMTP}}.

==={{RTBToL|-}}===
{{Term|RTBToL|Buriki}} is one of [[Tingle]]'s partners in the picture book world, along with [[Kakashi]] and [[Lion (Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love)|Lion]]. She is very intelligent, but heartless and in search for a heart. Due to her intelligence, {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki}} is able to cleverly solve puzzles that Tingle's other two friends are not able to. She is first met in Page 3, where she has crash-landed her spaceship, injuring [[Peter]], the local farmer.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|Series|Buriki}} is based on the {{wp|Tin Woodman}} from the ''Oz'' books.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Buriki}}'s name is Japanese romanization for the Dutch word ''blik'', meaning "tin".
