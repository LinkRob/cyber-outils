{{Term|TMC|Quest Status}} displays a number of {{Plural|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}. The {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}} can be accessed from here, and {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s current {{Plural|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}} are also displayed, along with the number of {{Plural|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}} he has collected. When {{Term|TMC|Link}} collects all of the {{Plural|TMC|Figurine|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Carlov Medal|link}} replaces the number of {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell}}; similarly, when all 100 {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} are completed, the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Trophy|link}} replaces the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag}}.

To the right of these collectables, the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}} are displayed as they are obtained, as well as the {{Term|TMC|Grip Ring|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Power Bracelet|link}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}. On the bottom are three spaces for various temporary quest items:
* {{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword|link}}
* {{Term|TMC|Broken Picori Blade|link}}
* {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch Key|link}}
* {{Term|TMC|Wake-Up Mushroom|link}}
* {{Term|TMC|A Hyrulean Bestiary|link}}
* {{Term|TMC|Dog Food|link}}
* {{Term|TMC|A History of Masks|link}}
* {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori|link}}
* {{Term|TMC|Graveyard Key|link}}
These {{Plural|TMC|Item}} disappear from {{Term|TMC|Quest Status}} when {{Term|TMC|Link}} uses them to progress on his journey.
