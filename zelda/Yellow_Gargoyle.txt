{{Term/Store|Yellow Gargoyle|Yellow Gargoyles|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Yellow Gargoyle|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Yellow Gargoyles are stationary statues that appear within {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle|link}} and the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}}. They spit fireballs in the general direction of {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Zelda|link}} as they pass by, dealing damage to everyone except {{Term|CoH|Yves}}.
