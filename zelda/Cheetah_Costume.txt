The {{Term/Store|Cheetah Costume||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Cheetah Costume lets you move a lot faster than normal. And it looks positively fierce! Rawr, dahling!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Cheetah Costume}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Fire Temple|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}}. It dresses {{Term|TFH|Link}} in a cheetah-print bodysuit complete with prosthetic ears and a tail. When {{Term|TFH|Link}} dons the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}, the {{Term|TFH|Stylish Woman|link}} becomes excited that he is "embracing [his] wild side".<ref>{{Cite|Ooh, embracing our wild side, are we? Rawr! ♥|Stylish Woman|TFH}}</ref>

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Cheetah Costume}} increases {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s running speed by approximately 42%.<ref>{{Cite|The Cheetah Costume lets you move a lot faster than normal. And it looks positively fierce! Rawr, dahling!|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= 302 frames to 213? That's...<br/>...<br/>...41.78404% faster!|author= HelpTheWretched|published= Mar. 3, 2022|retrieved= Mar. 3, 2022|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3LwtlJsxLf0|title= How fast is the Cheetah Costume? (The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes)|site= YouTube}}</ref> The speed of his [[Dash Attack]] is not affected. The {{Term|TFH|Cheetah Costume}} also allows {{Term|TFH|Link}} to communicate with the {{Plural|TFH|Cat|link}} resting in and around {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's}}, who enjoy gossiping about the [[Human]]s of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, stranger! Gimme some fish! Just kidding, nya ha.<br/>Wait a minute... Do you actually understand what I'm saying?! That's amazing, meow!<br/>I'm the guardian of this store. I keep things running smoothly and keep all of {{Color|TFH Blue|Madame}}'s juicy secrets.|Cat|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The human princess always knew just how to make us cats purr purr purrr. Le sigh, meow.<br/>Hey! Since you're trying to help my best human, I'll give you some advice!<br/>The Street Merchant is super finicky, meow. When he's in a good mood, he'll pay more for the same item.|Cat|TFH}}</ref>

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Cheetah Costume}}. Though the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} can appear in the {{Term|TFH|Catalog|link}}, it is impossible to collect the required {{Plural|TFH|Material}} in this mode.
