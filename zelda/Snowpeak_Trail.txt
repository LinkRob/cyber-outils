#REDIRECT [[Snowpeak]]

==Information==
This is the area in the region of Snowpeak just before [[Snowpeak Ruins]]. This area hosts the Snowpeak Snowboarding race. You race [[Yeto]] and [[Yeta]] to the finish. At the base of the trail holds a frozen lake and lots of snow. The only way to travel properly is being [[Wolf Link]]. There is a [[Howling Stone]] nearby around the middle of the area. Snow Wolves are the main enemy in this area, and they attack you in packs so be prepare. There is a portal to reach to the top part of the trail.
