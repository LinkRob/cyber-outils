{{Term/Store|Gerudo Town||Series, BotW}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gerudo Town|Map|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} is the main settlement of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} and consequently the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} people. Only women are allowed to enter the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|They {{Color|BotW Red|only let women into that town}}...And to think we came all the way out here...|Robsten|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, the guards will not let him.<ref>{{Cite|Freeze! Where do you think you're going?! Not into town—that's for sure! No voe allowed. Off with you!|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} must complete the "{{Term|BotW|Forbidden City Entry|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} before he can get inside the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}. This {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|display=Quest}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} by disguising himself as a woman while wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}. Once inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can meet with {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}, chief of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}, in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} to begin the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|You stand before {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Riju}}, chief of the Gerudo! Declare your business, but come no closer!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} also contains several {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, including "{{Term|BotW|The Silent Swordswomen|link}}", "{{Term|BotW|The Seven Heroines|link}}", "{{Term|BotW|Medicinal Molduga|link}}", "{{Term|BotW|The Mystery Polluter|link}}", "{{Term|BotW|Tools of the Trade|link}}", "{{Term|BotW|The Search for Barta|link}}", and "{{Term|BotW|The Secret Club's Secret|link}}". {{Term|BotW|Link}} can receive the legendary {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Riju}} by completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} "{{Term|BotW|The Thunder Helm|link}}". At certain times, there is a class being taught by {{Term|BotW|Ashai}}. {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} can be found in various places throughout the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} is not harmed by {{Term|BotW|Heat}} while inside {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}. The {{Term|BotW|Daqo Chisay Shrine|link}} resides right outside {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}'s walls. {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} also houses a {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} near the {{Term|BotW|Southeast Gate|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can wear {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots|link}} instead of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Sirwal|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}. He can also wear the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} instead of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Veil|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} soldiers will throw {{Term|BotW|Link}} out if he removes any piece of his {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} outfit, {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}, {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots}} or {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}}, or if Link attempts to enter by scaling the walls.

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} can be found near {{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion}}. A second {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Golden Bow|link}} can be found buried in a pile of sand and debris near the {{Term|BotW|Southeast Gate}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} disguised as {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}} at the very top of the rock above the palace.

It also appears that the only race that isn't seen in Gerudo town are [[Zora]], likely due to the severe lack of water and scorching heat being ill-fated for the aquatic race.

===={{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion}}====
{{Main|Fashion Passion}}
{{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor Shop}} managed by {{Term|BotW|Saula|link}} that sells the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}} is sold at a total of 540 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, which is 60 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} cheaper than what {{Term|BotW|Vilia|link}} sold them to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} for.<ref>{{Cite|What do you think? Do you want to buy them for...{{Color|BotW Red|600 rupees}}?|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}} here is also white, as opposed to the undyed variation that {{Term|BotW|Vilia}} sells to {{Term|BotW|Link}}. {{Term|BotW|Saula}} always comments on {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Top|link}}, as that is the only bit of {{Term|BotW|Clothes|link|display=Clothing}} he always wears in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}};<ref>{{Cite|Oooh, that {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo top}} looks really good on you...|Saula|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Sirwal|link}} can be replaced with {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Veil|link}} can be replaced with the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}, should {{Term|BotW|Link}} have obtained them. Behind the stands is a curtain that cannot be passed through due to {{Term|BotW|Saula}} denying entry; it leads to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club|link}}, but {{Term|BotW|Saula}} advises that fellow members should use the side door.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, um, that doorway isn't for customers, actually...|Saula|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Small|If you're a member, please head around to the door on the side.}}|Saula|BotW}}</ref>

===={{Term|BotW|Hotel Oasis}}====
{{Main|Hotel Oasis}}
{{Term|BotW|Hotel Oasis}} is an {{Term|BotW|Inn}} managed by {{Term|BotW|Romah|link}} and advertised by {{Term|BotW|Olu|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Romah}} offers an 80 {{Term|BotW|Rupee|link}} spa plan that will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a temporary full {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} wheel and three temporary {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}, as well as a regular bed for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} that restores all of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Heart}}.

===={{Term|BotW|Relationship Classroom}}====
{{Main|Relationship Classroom}}
The {{Term|BotW|Relationship Classroom|link}} is located off of the barracks. Inside, {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link}} hosts a "Voe and You" class that prepares young {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} women for the outside world.

===={{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}}====
{{Main|Royal Palace}}
The {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} is the home of {{Term|BotW|Riju}}. It is the source of all water in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.

===={{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth}}====
{{Main|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth}}
The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth|link}} is run by {{Term|BotW|Kohm|link}}. It costs 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} or 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have a {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}, which will have him obtain a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Shield|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to quickly traverse the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.

===={{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth}}====
{{Main|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth}}
The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|link}} is run by {{Term|BotW|Frelly|link}}. It costs 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} or 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have a {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}, which will have him obtain a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Shield|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to quickly traverse the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.

===={{Plural|BotW|Stall}}====
{{Main|Stall}}
All of the {{Plural|BotW|General Store|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} are {{Plural|BotW|Stall}}. Many of the {{Plural|BotW|Stall}} are managed by married women who live outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, but they return to sell their wares.<ref>{{Cite|[Your husband?] Oh, yes. And a newlywed, too! Many of the vendors who work these stalls are married, in fact. It's pretty typical of married vai to live outside these walls, really. But we come back to sell our wares, of course!|Spera|BotW}}</ref>

===={{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories}}====
{{Main|Starlight Memories}}
{{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories}} is a {{Term|BotW|Jewelry Shop}} managed by {{Term|BotW|Isha|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Cara|link}}. Customers must bring the necessary {{Term|BotW|Materials|link}} to craft the accessory they want. {{Term|BotW|Link}} cannot access the {{Term|BotW|Shop}} until he completes the "{{Term|BotW|Tools of the Trade|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

===={{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}}====
{{Main|The Noble Canteen}}
{{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}} is a bar managed by {{Term|BotW|Furosa|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} serves the {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit|link}}, a drink needed to complete "{{Term|BotW|The Perfect Drink|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. After the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" is complete, there are seven full-time residents. {{Term|BotW|Deltan|link}} can be found hunched over a table, sobbing. {{Term|BotW|Pokki|link}} is found chatting to {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}, and {{Term|BotW|Konora|link}} is on a seat parallel to the one {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} and {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} are sat on. {{Term|BotW|Pyra|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sumati|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Yaido|link}} can also be found inside here, talking about a password.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I was going to tell you... I finally got my hands on the {{Color|BotW Blue|password}} for you-know-where.|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> They stop talking about it as soon as they notice that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is listening.<ref>{{Cite|You!! Did  you really think we wouldn't see you standing there, listening to our conversation?!|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} accesses the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club|link}}, they instead talk about {{Term|BotW|Perda|link}}'s search for the {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond|link}}. At night it is more lively, as is mentioned by {{Term|BotW|Pearle|link}}; {{Term|BotW|Pritana|link}} can be found there before going to {{Term|BotW|Nali|link}}'s house, intoxicated. {{Term|BotW|Nellie|link}} gets up in the evening and goes to get a drink to "wake her up", and the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Jules|link}} is found stood against a wall, drinking from a large cup.
