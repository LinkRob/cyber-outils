The {{Term/Store|Bremen Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 118 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> It resembles the face of a bird.

The Bremen Mask is given to [[Link]] by [[Guru-Guru]]. It originally belonged to a dog who was leader of an animal troupe.<ref>{{Cite|Dear guest...Long ago, I was in an animal troupe, with dogs and donkeys and such. Why could a...? Why could a...? Why could a man join?!? That's 'cause a man is an animal, too, my boy! They were all great. But there was one thing I didn't like about it... Why was the...? Why was the...? Why was the dog the leader?!? Was it 'cause something was wrong with me, sir??? Oh, that dog was an amazing leader! He always had a stellar troupe no matter what animals he had to work with...|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> Link receives the Bremen Mask after listening to Guru-Guru's confession about how he stole it.<ref>{{Cite|That's why I...That's why I... That's why I stole it... The dog's mask. I stole it. I wanted it because it was the leader's mask... But I no longer need it... I give it to my guest.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> However, Link will need to listen to Guru-Guru's confession on the night of the [[First Day|First]] or [[Second Day]], since he and the rest of the [[Gorman Troupe]] will flee from the town afterwards.

The Bremen Mask allows Link to play a marching melody, which makes domestic animals other than [[cow]]s and horses follow Link, who leads them in a brisk march as he plays his [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina]]. The Bremen Mask can be used in the fight against the [[Igos du Ikana|Ikana warriors]] to confuse them and make them march along with Link, rendering them vulnerable. It also makes [[Cucco]] chicks grow into adults, which is required to obtain the [[Bunny Hood]].<ref>{{Cite|The leader was a good instructor. His members matured quickly and they became adults in an instant.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't really get it, but just seein' these guys with a crest and all... I don't have regrets about anythin' anymore. I'm perfectly satisfied. Here...You can have this from me...|Grog|MM}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
The mask appears to be named after a {{wp|Grimm's Fairy Tales|Grimm's Fairy Tale}} titled {{wp|Town Musicians of Bremen}}, in which a group of animals decide to leave their masters and become traveling musicians.

The mask's Japanese name, {{Romanize|ブレー面|Burē Men||3}}, is a pun. Most of the masks in {{MM|-}} use the prefix {{Romanize|お|O||3}}, denoting something as polite, and are thus called {{Romanize|お面|O-Men||3}}. The Bremen Mask however is referred to as {{Romanize|ブレー面|Burē-Men||3}}. {{Romanize|ブレー|Burē||3}} or {{Romanize|無礼|Burei||3}}, meaning "rude," thus translating as "rude mask." This also makes a pun with {{Romanize|ブレーメン|Burēmen||3}}, Japanese transcription of "Bremen."
