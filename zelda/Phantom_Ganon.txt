{{Term/Store|Phantom Ganon|Phantom Ganons|Series, OoT, OoT3D, TWW, TWWHD, FSA, BotW, TotK}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Boss|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 197 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D, TWW, TWWHD, FSA}}}}</ref><ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 80}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Phantom Ganon}} was referred to as '''Phantom Ganondorf''' in {{Guide|OoT3D|Prima}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT3D |publisher= Prima |page= 101}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} He is a wicked ghost using [[Ganondorf]]'s likeness.

In {{OoT}}, Phantom Ganon is the boss of the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Phantom Ganon It's an evil ghost of the Forest Temple! He comes out of one of the pictures. Answer his magic attack with an attack of your own!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> Once the battle starts, riding on [[Ganondorf's Steed|Ganondorf's horse]], Phantom Ganon jumps into one of the six paintings around the room. Afterwards, he and one replica will ride to the front of two separate paintings at random. The real one can be spotted by his model being lighter. A purple vortex then appears revealing the real Phantom Ganon, while the fake one immediately turns around.<ref>{{Cite|Phantom Ganon Attack him when he comes out of one of the pictures! Watch out for the fake one!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> As Phantom Ganon jumps out of the painting, Link must shoot him with the [[Fairy Bow]]. If the [[Arrow]] misses or is delayed, he summons an electric ball on the ground that scatters across the platform. This can be avoided by standing on one of the [[Triforce]] images on each corner of the platform. After three shots, Phantom Ganon abandons the horse, starting the second part of the battle.
[[File:OoT Phantom Ganon Battle.png|thumb|269x269px|Link fighting Phantom Ganon in ''Ocarina of Time'' (second phase)]]
Phantom Ganon now attacks by [[Dead Man's Volley|shooting an energy ball]] from his staff at Link while hovering over the ground. Link can hit the ball back with the [[Master Sword]] or a [[Bottle]]. After enough hits, Phantom Ganon falls to the ground paralyzed, where he can be hit multiple times with the Master Sword. When his health is halved, Phantom Ganon can also attack by twirling his staff a few times and then charging at Link, dealing three [[Heart]]s of damage. Once defeated, Ganondorf speaks to Link through the phantom, slightly impressed by the [[Hero of Time]]'s skills, before Phantom Ganon is banished to the gap between dimensions<ref>{{Cite|Hey kid, you did quite well... It looks like you may be gaining some slight skill... But you have defeated only my phantom... When you fight the real me, it won't be so easy! What a worthless creation that ghost was! I will banish it to the gap between dimensions!!|Ganondorf|OoT3D}}</ref> and Link receives a [[Heart Container]] as well as the [[Forest Medallion]].

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Phantom Ganon}}
In {{TWW}}, Phantom Ganon appears in the [[Forsaken Fortress]] and in [[Ganon's Castle#The Wind Waker|Ganon's Tower]].<ref name="TWW Guide 33">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 33}}</ref> He must be defeated by reflecting his blue orbs with the Sword or an empty [[Bottle]], like in ''Ocarina of Time''.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= To defeat Phantom Ganon, swing your sword so it hits the ball of magic and sends it back at Phantom Ganon, who knocks it back at you. It's like a tennis game-keep returning Phantom Ganon's shots until he misses and is stunned by his own attack.|game= TWW|page= 79}}</ref><ref name="TWW Guide 33"/> Once hit by a reflected attack, Link can attack Phantom Ganon with his Sword. If Phantom Ganon appears on the ground, Link can use the [[Parry Attack]] prompt to avoid his Sword swipe and strike his head. After defeating him in the Forsaken Fortress, Link receives the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 79}}</ref> When the foe was initially defeated by Link, the Hero of Winds is surprised to see the phantom still laughing at him, as the [[Master Sword]] did not yet have its [[Power to Repel Evil]] awakened.

When fought in Ganon's Tower, Phantom Ganon is accompanied by four illusions of himself. He now also attacks by throwing powerful dark energy balls, which splits into a group of small bursts of energy flying towards Link, causing two Hearts of damage if hit. These can be sent back at him with a [[Spin Attack]]. After just one hit, Phantom Ganon disappears, leaving his Sword behind with the handle pointing at the door Link must open to advance.<ref>{{Cite|The sword-hilts of my servants who lurk deep in the darkness shall be the guideposts that point to me.|Stone in Ganon's Tower|TWW}}</ref> Once the [[Light Arrow]]s are found, they must be shot at Phantom Ganon in order to finally defeat him.

Link can grab Phantom Ganon's Sword to attack. However, it must be held with both hands, leaving Link unable to use his [[Shield]]. After truly defeating Phantom Ganon in Ganon's Tower's labyrinth, Link must strike with or throw the Sword at the brick wall to gain access to the top of the Tower, when he will face [[Puppet Ganon]] and finally, [[Ganondorf]] himself.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 119}}</ref>

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, Phantom Ganon first appears as the boss of [[Hyrule Castle#Four Swords Adventures|Hyrule Castle]]. In order to defeat him, the [[Links]] must hit his energy balls back and forth with the Sword. Once stunned, he is vulnerable to slashes from the Sword. Phantom Ganon appears once again as the boss of the [[Temple of Darkness]]; however, he now summons clones to his aid. The real Phantom Ganon will throw green orbs that can be hit back, while the clones throw white balls that break when hit with the Sword.

In the [[Multiplayer#Four Swords Adventures|Multiplayer]] mode, Phantom Ganon will launch energy balls in green, red, blue, or purple colors. Only the Link wearing the corresponding tunic can strike the ball back with his Sword, and it will either fly back at Phantom Ganon or change colors and move in the general direction of another Link and require another hit.

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Improve|February 5, 2024}}
{{Compendium|name=Phantom Ganon|loot=Dark Clump|locs=Unknown|desc=A monster created from the very flesh and blood of Demon King Ganondorf that will follow his bidding without hesitation. Like the Demon King it so resembles, this huge creature is a master of many weapons. It will attack its foes relentlessly and without mercy.
Facing it in battle is inadvisable-unless you want a quick ticket to the afterlife.|file=TotK Hyrule Compendium Phantom Ganon.png}}
Phantom Ganon makes its first appearance as a boss in the series since {{FSA|-}} during the events of {{TotK|-}}, being found in Hyrule Castle, the Great Deku Tree, and in other locations if Link defeats a group of [[Gloom Spawn]]. It resembles the mummified corpse form of Ganondorf seen underneath Hyrule Castle, except made of Gloom. As in previous titles, Ganondorf treats it as a "loyal servant".<ref>{{Cite|End this hero's life, my dark servant. Cut him down.|Ganondorf|TotK}}</ref>

Depending on its location, Phantom Ganon wields either the [[Gloom Club]], [[Gloom Spear]], or [[Gloom Sword]], and all of them are equipped with [[Demon King's Bow]]. It attacks using these three weapons and can also teleport to in front of Link if there is an inaccessible zone in between them and Link, if they cannot, it'll use its bow. Also, if Link is on a platform that Phantom Ganon cannot get to, or has lose track on where Link is (hiding behind objects, using a [[Dazzlefruit]]) it can cover the floor and walls with gloom like it does in the Hyrule Castle fight, it will continue spreading up to a certain radius and will cancel out of it once it spots Link again or takes damage. Additionally, if Link stands too close to Gloom Club-wielding Phantom Ganons long enough after swinging their club, they'll kick Link.

If Link attempts to repeatedly shoot arrows to Phantom Ganon's face rapidly enough, without using the bow airborne, sometimes the arrows won't damage it.{{Fact|TotK}}

Like Gloom Spawn, if Link flees far enough, Phantom Ganon will disappear and leaves behind [[Dark Clump|Dark Clumps]]. This counts as a kill, meaning it won't return until the next [[Blood Moon]]. The one found in Hyrule Castle is exempt from this phenomenon - it must be defeated in order to proceed with the main quest. The Phantom Ganon in the Deku Tree Chasm may also be exempt, but no way has been discovered to confirm.{{Fact|TotK}}

This incarnation of Phantom Ganon has the ability to disguise itself. This power is primarily used to disguise itself as [[Princess Zelda]] to cover for her disappearance during the Upheaval, doing things that are subtly uncharacteristic of the real Zelda. It can be seen in its disguise briefly when Link first encounters [[Hoz]] near [[Hyrule Castle]]. It is heavily involved in the [[Regional Phenomena]] quests, such as siccing the [[Sludge Like]] on [[King Dorephan]] or giving [[Yunobo]] a mask that bewitches him to market the cursed Marbled Rock Roast to his fellow [[Goron]]s. In the [[Potential Princess Sightings]] [[Side Adventure]], Link is tasked by [[Traysi]] and [[Penn]] to track down various Hylians who claim to have witnessed Zelda behaving strangely across the land.

In the distant past, this disguise was also used to distract [[Queen Sonia]] long enough for the real Ganondorf to assassinate her, steal her [[Secret Stone]], and corrupt it with his dark magic.

====Scourge of the Deku Tree====
In a [[Depths]] chasm inside the [[Great Deku Tree#Tears of the Kingdom|Great Deku Tree]], Phantom Ganon must be fought in order to remove a mysterious curse from [[Korok Forest]]. As a reward, the Great Deku Tree will trigger a quest that reveals the location of the [[Light Dragon]] so Link can retrieve the [[Master Sword]]. 

====The Demon King's Phantom Saboteur====
After Link awakens the four regional [[Sage#Tears of the Kingdom|Sage]]s, Phantom Ganon, still posing as Zelda, lures Link to [[Hyrule Castle]] and ambushes him with groups of monsters multiple times before trapping him in the Sanctum. There, Ganondorf appears and taunts Link, ordering Phantom Ganon to attack him before disappearing. In this fight, Phantom Ganon creates multiple copies of itself to combat Link. After the fight, the Sages appear and defend Link from another assault from Ganondorf, but they are all forced to witness an illusion of Ganondorf's army of monsters invading Hyrule.

After Phantom Ganon is defeated in the Sanctum, the fake Zelda is no longer featured in [[Blood Moon]] cutscenes and her voice-over is absent. This does not remove Phantom Ganon, who still appears with Gloom Spawn across Hyrule, however. If Phantom Ganon isn’t defeated at Hyrule Castle, his encounter will appear in [[Gloom's Lair]] as a boss that Link must face before he challenges Ganondorf.

Although not Phantom Ganon himself, Ganondorf during the "Master of the Secret Stone" phase of the final battle utilized a similar doppelganger ability to Phantom Ganon. Eventually, Ganondorf will empower himself by absorbing them and knock the sages away during the final stages of the battle.

==={{HW|L|-}}===
{{Stub|HWL}}
In {{HW|L}}, Phantom Ganon appears as a Giant Boss, and as the main villain of the [[Legend Mode#Wind Waker|Wind Waker]] series of Scenarios in [[Legend Mode]]. He had stolen [[Cia]]'s dark magic and the Triforce of Power from [[Lana]], creating a new dimensional rift that summons the world from the Era of the Great Sea. He is eventually defeated by Link, Lana, [[Tetra]], [[King Daphnes]], and Cia.

In battle, Phantom Ganon wields his sword, which he uses for various spin attacks. He is able to vanish and reappear closer to enemy Warriors for a surprise spin attack, or alternately to create copies of himself to surround and attack the Warrior. His projectile attacks consist of raising both of his arms to create many homing energy balls, or to simply create a single white energy ball. The latter can be used to expose his [[Weak Point Gauge]] if bounced back at him by striking it. Phantom Ganon has no specific weakness to any item, and the only way to damage him is by reflecting his own attacks at him.

After his health has been reduced to about half, he summons a second sword and switches his battle style to melee, using the two blades to violently hack at the enemy Warrior. He also lowers his hovering altitude, making himself reachable to attack, but will always shield himself similarly to wind waker Ganondorf if attacked at the front or the sides. His backside, however, will become vulnerable to attack. After striking his back enough times, he will fall to the ground, exposing his Weak Point Gauge.

When defeated, Phantom Ganon will occasionally drop his [[Phantom Ganon's Cape|Cape]] as a Silver [[Material]], and rarely, his [[Material|Sword]] as a Gold Material.

==Trivia==
[[File:OoT Phantom Ganon painting.png|thumb|282x282px|The mysterious painting Phantom Ganon uses to fight Link in ''Ocarina of Time'' has a faint image of Ganon's Castle, a leftover of what was planned during the game's development]]
*Interestingly, the six paintings Phantom Ganon enters in and out during his first phase in ''Ocarina of Time'' shows [[Ganon's Castle]] in the night sky's background as a silhouette. This is because [[Ganon]]'s stronghold was originally intended to be the game's only setting during development, with it being structured similarly to the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]'s [[mariowiki:Princess_Peach's_Castle|Princess Peach's Castle]] from ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_64|Super Mario 64]]'', and various different rooms serving as the Dungeons. Although this was scrapped in the final game, the Forest Temple's design and its boss battle has leftover elements.
*In {{TWW|-}}, the [[Hylian Language|Hylian script]] on Phantom Ganon's Sword reads "[[Zubora]] [[Gabora]]", the names of the blacksmiths who forge Link's [[Kokiri Sword]] in {{MM}}.
*The [[Wall Merging]] ability from {{ALBW}} was inspired by Phantom Ganon from {{OoT|-}}, who also has the ability to enter walls through paintings.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 302}}</ref>
**In addition, his capturing of Saria in a painting would later be the main power of [[Yuga]], Ganon's [[Lorulean]] counterpart.
**Ganondorf's command to Phantom Ganon to take out Link in the manga adaptation for ''Ocarina of Time'' was later used in {{TotK}} before the start of the boss fight against it in Hyrule Castle. Coincidentally, both instances occur after Ganondorf trapped Link after the latter tried to track down a missing friend (Zelda in the case of ''Tears of the Kingdom'').
*Although Phantom Ganon himself doesn't make a appearance, concept art for Ganondorf in the ''Oracle'' games is described as representing "Ganondorf's phantom".<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=A design sketch depicting Ganondorf’s phantom. Ganondorf never appears in the Oracle series, however. Only his beast form, Ganon, is seen.|book=HH|page=169}}</ref>
*Aside from Phantom Ganon's role in {{HW}}, he was also given an indirect reference by Ganondorf in his second mission in the main campaign when the player is about to fight Impa.<ref>{{Cite|A double? Heh. You act as if I've never used that trick myself.|Ganondorf|HW}}</ref>
