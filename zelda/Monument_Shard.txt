{{Term/Store|Monument Shard|Monument Shards|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Monument Shard}}'''|Camera|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Plural|BotW|Monument Shard}} were once part of the monument found in the {{Term|BotW|Palmorae Ruins|link}}, but an earthquake caused it to become broken.<ref>{{Cite|This stone monument was once much larger, but it broke apart during an earthquake a while back.|Garini|BotW}}</ref> In order to read the entire monument, {{Term|BotW|Garini|link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to find the three missing shards.<ref>{{Cite|If you really want to help, help me find the missing fragments, will you? They'll be scattered all around.|Garini|BotW}}</ref> To help {{Term|BotW|Link}} find the {{Plural|BotW|Monument Shard}}, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} reveals that they're carved in the same way as the monument and also glow at night.<ref>{{Cite|I think the fragments had the same kind of script carved into them...|Garini|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...Oh, right. And the words on the stone monument glow with an eerie light and night... That should make them easier to find.|Garini|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Garini}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to draw a picture of the shards if he finds them.<ref>{{Cite|If you find a fragment, please draw a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}} of it and bring it to me.|Garini|BotW}}</ref> He gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a tip that one is nearby and suggests he find it first.<ref name="First">{{Cite|One of them should be right around here. Please find that first!|Garini|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|A Fragmented Monument|link}}."

One {{Term|BotW|Monument Shard}} is located in the northeast corner of {{Term|BotW|Palmorae Beach|link}}.<ref name="First"/> Another can be found among the rocks off the western coast of the beach.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I suppose the waves may have washed some away or pulled them underwater...}}|Garini|BotW}}</ref> Finally, the last {{Term|BotW|Monument Shard}} is found at the tip of {{Term|BotW|Soka Point|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I wonder if the currents are strong enough to have carried a fragment all the way to Cape Cresia or Soka Point...}}|Garini|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Garini}}, he asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} found any of the {{Plural|BotW|Monument Shard}}.<ref>{{Cite|Did you find any fragments of the stone monument?|Garini|BotW}}</ref> By confirming that he has, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can show all of the pictures he's taken of the shards to {{Term|BotW|Garini}}. With all of the pictures, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} is able to translate the scripture written on the monuments and the shards.<ref>{{Cite|Excellent. Thanks to you, I've got a good shot at deciphering the entire monument. Give me a second to see if I can put this all together...|Garini|BotW}}</ref> According to {{Term|BotW|Garini}}, the deciphered text reads, "When the two find their place and kneel in reverence, the shrine will reveal itself."<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, I've deciphered the entire passage! "When the two find their place and kneel in reverence, the shrine will reveal itself."|Garini|BotW}}</ref>
