The {{Term/Store|Trusty Freight Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Trusty Freight Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Trusty Freight Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} and the first {{Term|ST|Freight Car|link}} acquired for the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}. When {{Term|ST|Link|link}} arrives at {{Term|ST|Goron Village|link}} in search of the {{Term|ST|Fire Sanctuary|link}}, he finds that {{Term|ST|Lava|link}} from a volcanic eruption has left parts of the village inaccessible. He then climbs to the {{Term|ST|Altar of the Mountain Goddess|link}}, where {{Term|ST|Kagoron|link}} recognizes the young hero as an engineer and, believing he can help the village, asks a fellow {{Term|ST|Goron|link}} to attach the {{Term|ST|Trusty Freight Car}} to the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}} so that {{Term|ST|Link}} can transport a [[Mega Ice|cold substance]] to deal with the {{Term|ST|Lava}}.<ref>{{Cite|The elder is in the village, and there is a way to get there. But first... You are an engineer, are you not? Those who drive trains have a special aura about them. I can see it on you! ...But moving on! Your train may come in handy.|Kagoron|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is the favor I require of you, young stranger. Use the freight car to bring back something cold enough to cool the lava.|Kagoron|ST}}</ref> Afterward, {{Term|ST|Link}} is allowed to keep the {{Term|ST|Freight Car}} for the rest of his adventure.

The description of the {{Term|ST|Trusty Freight Car}} reads "There's no need to worry about damaged goods with this car."
