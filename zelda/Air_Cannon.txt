{{Term/Store|Air Cannon|Air Cannons|Series, FSA|plural}} are objects in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Air Cannons appear in every [[Stages in Four Swords Adventures|Stage]], usually in areas where the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} can fall through floors, though they can be used to ascend to higher places. Air Cannons can blast the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} high in the air out of a roof, hole, or into another Cannon. On such occasions, there are usually large [[Force Gem]]s for the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} to collect. Air Cannons can hold all four {{Plural|FSA|Links}}.

An Air Cannon is present in the fight against [[Vaati]]. If any of the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} falls into the holes in the floor, they can use it to return to the arena above. Alternatively, they can just climb the ladders.
