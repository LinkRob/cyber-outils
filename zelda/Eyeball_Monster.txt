{{Term/Store|Eyeball Monster|Eyeball Monsters|Series, PH|plural}} are enemies in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 176}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Eyeball Monster can be found when sailing in the [[World of the Ocean King]], where they will swarm towards the [[SS Linebeck]]. They travel in packs of five and deal minimum damage. Eyeball Monsters can be easily defeated with a blast from the [[Cannon]]. They come in white and pink varieties.
