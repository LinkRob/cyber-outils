==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Flamebreaker Armors are pieces of [[Armor]] that can be purchased in the [[Ripped and Shredded]] shop at [[Goron City]]. One can also be received from {{Term|BotW|Kima|link}} by completing the "{{Term|BotW|Fireproof Lizard Roundup|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|link}}.{{Fact|BotW}}

When worn, they provide one level of Flame Guard.<ref name=Inventory"/> Flamebreaker Armors can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Flamebreaker Armor, alongside the [[Flamebreaker Helm]] and [[Flamebreaker Boots]], compose the [[Flamebreaker Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a [[Fireproof]] [[Set Bonus]].
