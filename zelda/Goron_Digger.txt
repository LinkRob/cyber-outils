The {{Term/Store|Goron Digger|Goron Diggers|Series, TMC|plural}} are characters in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 106}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Goron}}
The {{Plural|TMC|Goron Digger}} are {{Plural|TMC|Goron|link}} that, through a series of {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}}, appear one-by-one at the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}. They arrive at the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave|display=Cave}} to help one another after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Wall|link}} in {{Plural|TMC|Dig Cavern|link}} across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. When all six of them have arrived, they will break through the final wall in the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave|display=Cave}}, revealing a large {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with the first and leftmost {{Term|TMC|Goron Digger}}, causing the {{Term|TMC|Goron Merchant|link}} to arrive in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square|link}} to sell {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}, and with the final and rightmost {{Term|TMC|Goron Digger}}, causing {{Term|TMC|Biggoron|link}} to awaken atop {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}.
