{{Term/Store|Purple Lizalfos|Purple Lizalfos|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Purple Lizalfos|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|CoH|Purple Lizalfos}} are a variety of spear-wielding {{Term|CoH|Lizalfos|link}} that only appear in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}}. They are stronger than the {{Term|CoH|Blue Lizalfos|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Black Lizalfos|link}}, but otherwise behave identically to their weaker counterparts by standing still indefinitely, and charging in a straight line on every beat once either {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} walks within their line of sight. Like all other varieties of Lizalfos, they will continue charging in one direction until they collide with something, becoming stunned and defenseless for three music beats. On the fourth beat, they will regain consciousness and either resume standing idle or charging again if the heroes are still in front of them.
