{{Term/Store|Station|Stations|Series, ST|plural}} are stopping points for {{Plural|ST|Train|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 290}}</ref>

==Featuresand Overview==
There is a total of 22 interconnected {{Plural|ST|Station}} covering the entirety of {{Term|ST|New Hyrule|ST}}. Each {{Term|ST|Station}} is built in a similar fashion, having a small roof built over the platform and a {{Term|ST|Stop|link}} {{Term|ST|Sign|link}} in place to help {{Plural|ST|Engineer|link}} know where to bring their {{Plural|ST|Train|link}} to a halt to dock at the {{Term|ST|Station}}'s platform.
