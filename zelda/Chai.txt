{{Term/Store|Chai||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>{{Note|Chai is also called '''Moka''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC |publisher= Nintendo |page= 107}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given both in-game and in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Chai}} spends the entire game sitting in {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}}, where she wonders what to do about dinner and whether anyone would notice if she repeated the meal from the previous night.<ref>{{Cite|Cheers! Yaay! Cheers! Raise your milk for a toast! Would they even notice if I made the same thing for dinner tonight?|Chai|TMC}}</ref> As the game progresses, she ponders the need for dinner altogether,<ref>{{Cite|Cheers! Yaay! Cheers! Raise your milk for a toast! I wonder...do we even need to eat dinner at all?|Chai|TMC}}</ref> eventually assuming that her failure to make dinner caused {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} to transform {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cheers! Yaay! Cheers! Raise your milk for a toast! It's not like the world would come to an end if I didn't make dinner, right?|Chai|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Cheers! Yaay! Cheers! Raise your milk for a toast! I never knew that failing to make dinner would have such consequences!|Chai|TMC}}</ref>

She is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}, don't you? Well, OK! Let's give it a try.|Chai|TMC}}</ref>
