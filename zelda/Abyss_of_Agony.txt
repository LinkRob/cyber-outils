{{Term/Store|Abyss of Agony||Series, TFH}} is a {{Term|TFH|Level|link}} in {{TFH}}. It is the second {{Term|TFH|Level}} of the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}} {{Term|TFH|Area}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 159}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|TFH|Level}} takes place at a cliffside with rushing waterfalls, which the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} must climb, eventually reaching a watery grotto.

In '''Stage 1''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} begin at a lake with rapidly moving currents and several docks. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must carefully swim in the moving water while avoiding {{Plural|TFH|Spike|link}}, and quickly swim over to a small island with two {{Plural|TFH|Water Rod|link}} and a {{Term|TFH|Bow|link}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Spike}} can be detonated by firing an {{Term|TFH|Arrow|link}} at them. Using the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}} to reach the {{Plural|TFH|Shock Switch|link}} on the left and right sides, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must hit these {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} by firing {{Plural|TFH|Arrow}}, which will lower wooden dams and reveal more {{Plural|TFH|Switch}}. These {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} must be hit to remove the two locks over the largest dam, which will open the way to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}} must be used to cross the waterfall onto its platform.

'''Stage 2''' starts the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} at the base of the waterfalls. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must climb the cliff side using their {{Plural|TFH|Water Rod}}. They eventually make their way to a rapidly moving river where a group of {{Plural|TFH|Water Octorok|link}} and a {{Term|TFH|Keeleon|link}} spawn. Electrified {{Plural|TFH|Buzz Blob|link}} will then appear after they are defeated. Defeating all enemies opens the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}, reachable with the {{Plural|TFH|Water Rod}}.

In '''Stage 3''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} reappear higher up the cliffside, next to a brick pillar. On the left ledge is a water wheel and a wooden platform on a pulley. The water wheel is used to make the platform move, by turning it with the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}}'s pillar. Using the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}} on the left side makes the platform move right, and using it on the right side makes it move left. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must work in tandem to turn the water wheel and transport each other. One {{Term|TFH|Links}} turns the water wheel while the other two {{Plural|TFH|Links}} ride the platform. The {{Term|TFH|Links}} with the {{Term|TFH|Bow}} fires an {{Term|TFH|Arrow}} at a {{Term|TFH|Shock Switch}} as they move along, which opens a wooden wall blocking the rest of the way. Eventually, they reach a second water wheel. One {{Term|TFH|Links}} must then turn this water wheel, transporting the {{Term|TFH|Links}} with the {{Term|TFH|Bow}} to another {{Term|TFH|Shock Switch}}, which opens another wooden wall blocking the way above. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must then reunite at the ledges above, by using the water wheel to transport the remaining {{Term|TFH|Links}} and the {{Plural|TFH|Water Rod}} to cross the small waterfalls.

Eventually, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} arrive at a large wheel, powered by a smaller one. One {{Term|TFH|Links}} must power the water wheel to transport the other two {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to the ledge above them. Another water wheel can be found above to keep turning the wheel for the last {{Term|TFH|Links}}. A {{Term|TFH|Shock Switch}} on the ledge will activate the brick pillar, which will then act as an elevator to the starting area of the Stage.. Should any of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} fall all the way down, they can ride this as a lift and return to the final ledge. This pillar can also be used to reach a {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. At the very top of the cliff is the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}.

'''Stage 4''' is where the {{Term|TFH|Electric Blob Queen|link}} is fought in a large pool inside a grotto.
