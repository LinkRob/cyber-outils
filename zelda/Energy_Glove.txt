The Energy Glove is an [[Accessory]] found on the northwestern side of the [[Gerudo Desert]], guarded by [[Boarblin|Boarblins]], including a [[Boomerang Boarblin Lv. 2]] and a [[Club Boarblin Lv. 2]]. Equipping it grants the [[Extra Energy Appearance Up]] effect, increasing the drop rate of [[Energy]].
