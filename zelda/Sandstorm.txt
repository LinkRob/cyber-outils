{{Term/Store|Sandstorm|Sandstorms|Series, BotW|plural}} are a recurring {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Though I must admit I'm impressed you got here from the northwest. Did you truly pass through that {{Color|BotW Red|sandstorm}} to get here?|Laine|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Sandstorm}} go unnamed in {{OoT}} and {{FSA}}.

==Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
The {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland|link}} is covered by a {{Term|Series|Sandstorm}}, though it can be traversed by completing the two trials.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, rookie! Are you going into the desert? I'll open this gate for you, but... You can't cross the desert unless you pass the {{Color|OoT Red|two trials}}.|Gerudo Guard|OoT3D}}</ref> The first trial requires {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} to pass through the River of Sand, though it cannot be walked upon.<ref>{{Cite|The first trial is...the {{Color|OoT Red|River of Sand}}! You can't walk across this river! After you cross is, follow the flags we placed there.|Gerudo Guard|OoT3D}}</ref> After this, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} will need to use the {{Term|OoT3D|Lens of Truth|link}} to find the Phantom Guide, a {{Term|OoT3D|Poe|link}} who will lead him to the {{Term|OoT3D|Desert Colossus|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The second trial is...the {{Color|OoT Red|Phantom Guide}}! Those without {{Color|OoT Red|eyes that can see the truth}} will only find themselves returning here.|Gerudo Guard|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|One with the {{Color|OoT Red|eye of truth}} shall be guided to the Spirit Temple by an inviting ghost.|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref> If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} becomes lost in the {{Term|Series|Sandstorm}}, he will be returned to the entrance to the {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Yellow|Haunted Wasteland}}<br/>If you chase a mirage, the desert will swallow you. Only one path is true!|Sign|OoT3D}}</ref>

After learning the "{{Term|OoT3D|Requiem of Spirit|link}}," {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} will be able to teleport directly to the {{Term|OoT3D|Spirit Temple|link}}, skipping past the {{Term|Series|Sandstorm}} in the {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland}}.<ref>{{Cite|Listen to this {{Color|OoT3D Yellow|Requiem of Spirit}}... This melody will lead a child back to the desert.|Sheik|OoT3D}}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} exclusive to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} passes through a {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}}, the {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} will turn to static. This prevents {{Term|BotW|Link}} from {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}} from his current location. The presence of {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}} has made the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|display=Desert}} a hiding place for treasures belonging to thieves and ancient cultures.<ref>{{Cite|The danger of the sandstorms makes the desert a perfect hiding place for thieving bandits and priceless treasures.|Laine|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...Which is precisely why there are so many stories about ancient cultures hiding their treasure in the desert.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

Initially, four different {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}. The first is in the {{Term|BotW|East Barrens|link}}, and it is caused by the rampage of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Divine Beast?] You don't know about {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}? Supposedly, it is the guardian deity of the Gerudo people, but it suddenly started acting up a while back. There's nothing we can do to stop it, either... It's protected by a fierce sandstorm and intense lightning. Though it hasn't yet, it could easily head toward Gerudo Town or this oasis. I do my best to keep an eye on it from here.|Ripp|BotW}}</ref> As part of the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} will have to traverse this {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} while riding {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If we want to get close enough to that thing, we'll need to use {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}}... Are you ready?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> After the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} has been calmed, the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} will end.

A second {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} covers the {{Term|BotW|Great Cliffs|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Nobiro|link}} observes it from the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}, and despite having heard that it subsides for a few hours every day, he has never seen it himself.<ref>{{Cite|That sandstorm... They say it stops for a few hours a day, but it hasn't let up even a little since I've been watching.|Nobiro|BotW}}</ref> He has also heard a rumor that an ancient civilization left a treasure inside the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}}, which is what brought him to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} in the first place.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor has it {{Color|BotW Red|an ancient civilization had a treasure}} inside that sandstorm. I've been here for days waiting for the storm to settle down so I can nab it! I just hope it wasn't a bad lead...|Nobiro|BotW}}</ref> Speaking to him activates "{{Term|BotW|The Eye of the Sandstorm|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. From 9:00 PM to 4:00 AM, the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} will temporarily subside, allowing easy access through the {{Term|BotW|Great Cliffs}}. After {{Term|BotW|Dako Tah Shrine|link}} has been completed, the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} will permanently end.

The third {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} is on the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}}. According to {{Term|BotW|Laine|link}}, there is a tale that following the {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|link}} through the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} will lead to an ancient treasure.<ref>{{Cite|People talk about the statues pointing to the hiding place of an ancient treasure... but that's just superstition.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> Considering the number of {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} hiding in this {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}}, she also believes it is too dangerous to risk such a journey.<ref>{{Cite|Even if it was true, there are dangerous monsters in the sandstorms. I wouldn't risk it.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> After speaking to her, "{{Term|BotW|The Silent Swordswomen|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will begin. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} follows the directions of the {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue}}, he will eventually come upon {{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos Shrine|link}}. Completing this {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} will cause the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} to end.

Finally, a {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} can be found in {{Term|BotW|Dragon's Exile|link}}. It can be stopped after opening the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} under the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}}.
