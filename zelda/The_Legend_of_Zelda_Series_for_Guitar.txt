'''''The Legend of Zelda Series for Guitar''''' is a sheet music book for guitar released on July 18, 2010. Containing 72 pages, the book features 33 songs from almost every single ''The Legend of Zelda'' game released at the time, ranging from the original {{TLoZ}} to {{ST}}. It also came with an accompanying CD album of each song featured in the sheet music book as well as a six-page interview with ''The Legend of Zelda'' series composer [[Koji Kondo]].<ref>[http://zeldapower.com/index.php/downloads/sheet_music/the_legend_of_zelda_series_super_best_for_guitar.php ZeldaPower - The Legend of Zelda Series Super Best (for Guitar)]</ref>
