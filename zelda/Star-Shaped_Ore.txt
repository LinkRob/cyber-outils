The {{Term/Store|Star-Shaped Ore||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Star-Shaped Ore can be found below the sands at the [[Subrosia Seaside]]. Link can trade it at the [[Subrosia Market]] for a [[Ribbon]], which can be given to [[Rosa]] in order to get a date with her, and thus use her [[Key]] to open the [[Tower of Summer]].
