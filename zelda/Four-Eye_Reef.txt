{{Term|TWWHD|Four-Eye Reef}} is a minor island located south of {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}, at B1. It has four round reefs in the center, giving it its name. After the {{Term|TWWHD|Helmaroc King|link}} is defeated in {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Warship|link}} and wall-mounted {{Plural|TWWHD|Cannon (Enemy)|link}} will appear at the island. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} destroys all of them, a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest|link}} appears containing a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}}.

The {{Term|TWWHD|Great Fairy Chart|link}} can also be retrieved at {{Term|TWWHD|Four-Eye Reef}} by using the {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart}} found at {{Term|TWWHD|Five-Eye Reef|link}}.
