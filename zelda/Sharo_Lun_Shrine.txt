The {{Term/Store|Sharo Lun Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Sharo Lun Shrine}}<br/>Blind Spots}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Blind Spots''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun Shrine}} is found in {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}} to the southeast of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}. It is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} accessed as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

To access this {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must follow a trail of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} that appear around the crater of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain}}. The first of these {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Rings}} appears on one of the peaks east of the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}, and another appears ahead after touching the first one. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must touch each {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}} within their given time frame, eventually leading him to the eastern side of {{Term|BotW|Death Caldera|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} moat.

After reaching the final {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, the {{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun Shrine}} will rise from beneath the ground nearby. Upon entering the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is offered the Trial by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Sharo Lun. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Sharo Lun|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
This {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} features a series of overhead conveyor belts which carry hanging blocks along the tracks. These blocks are eventually dropped into the abyss below when they reach the end of their track. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must ride these blocks through the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, all while avoiding the obstacles that try to impede his progress along the way.

Some of the obstacles can be avoided by clinging onto the sides of the blocks, which are covered in mesh. The first track has streams of {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} that block the way ahead. It is possible to pass these obstacles unharmed by wearing the complete {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Set|link}}, provided that its pieces have all been upgraded twice. The second track has a combination of spiked columns and a group of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} surveying the path, which will try to shoot at {{Term|BotW|Link}} as he comes near.

The third track transports {{Term|BotW|Link}} alongside cascading {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall|link}}, which can be climbed by using {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} to create {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} blocks on the water, and thereby avoid the {{Term|BotW|Fire}} streams ahead. The rest of the track leads to a second {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}}, which can again be climbed using {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} above.

At the end of {{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} meets the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} {{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun}}, who commends his resourcefulness and rewards him with {{Term|BotW|Rudania's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of the hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Rudania's Emblem.}}|Sharo Lun|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to collect all three of {{Plural|BotW|Rudania's Emblem}} to reveal a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Sharo Lun|BotW}}</ref> If this is the first or second {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} accesses during "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song}}," {{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} how many pf {{Plural|BotW|Rudania's Emblem}} remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Sharo Lun|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Sharo Lun|BotW}}</ref> If it is the third and final {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} he visits, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will tell him to go to the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Rudania...}}|Sharo Lun|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} repeatedly falls from the top of the first {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}}, and it can be caught by creating a {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} block beneath it. It contains a {{Term|BotW|Great Flameblade|link}}.
