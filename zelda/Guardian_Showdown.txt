{{Term/Store|Guardian Showdown||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Guardian Showdown|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Showdown}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} is given six minutes to defeat four {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} spawn first, and after they are killed, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} spawn. After they are killed, the timer resets to four minutes and a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} spawns, which {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} must also defeat. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using only {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}}, and when completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ancient Core|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Tireless Frog|link}}.
