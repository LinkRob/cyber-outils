{{Name|Japanese|{{Term/Store|Waveblade's Dojo||Series, TMC}}}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Shogakukan|page= 153}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Waveblade's Dojo}} is located in the southwestern area of {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, only accessible after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Waveblade's Dojo|display=Dojo}} itself is inside a {{Term|TMC|Tree|link}}, and a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} can be found inside. {{Term|TMC|Waveblade|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Waveblade's Dojo|display=Dojo}}'s sole resident, will teach {{Term|TMC|Link}} the {{Term|TMC|Peril Beam|link}}.
