{{Term/Store|Gnarled Root Dungeon||Series, OoS}} is the first [[dungeon]] in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 151}}</ref>

==Description==
The Gnarled Root Dungeon is located in [[North Horon]]. [[Link]] is required to have the [[Gnarled Key]] to unlock the dungeon's entrance, and he can receive it from the [[Maku Tree]] in the process of being asked to retrieve the [[Essence of Nature|essence]] [[Fertile Soil]] from the Gnarled Root Dungeon.

Being the first dungeon of the game, the Gnarled Root Dungeon is fairly simple in terms of enemy and puzzle difficulty. Including the [[Boss Key]], three [[key]]s can be found throughout the dungeon. There are also [[Treasure Chest]]s containing a [[Gasha Seed]], an unappraised [[Magic Ring]], and Link's first [[Bomb]]s, which can be used to expose a bombable wall within the dungeon. This dungeon features several [[Puzzle#Block Puzzle|Block Puzzle]]s and a set of tracks for a [[Mine Cart]]. Defeating the dungeon's twin mini-bosses, the [[Brother Goriyas]], leads to a room which yields a [[Seed Satchel]] filled with [[Ember Seed]]s. These are then used to light [[Puzzle#Torch Puzzle|Torches]], unlocking new doors and allowing Link to obtain the Boss Key of the dungeon.

The boss is [[Aquamentus]]. After Link defeats Aquamentus, he obtains a [[Heart Container]] and the Fertile Soil essence.

Overall, the Gnarled Root Dungeon is reminiscent of [[Level 1]] in {{TLoZ}}. Aside from similar layouts, including an exterior of what appears to be a dead tree in a small island within a lake, both Gnarled Root Dungeon and Level 1 are the first dungeons of their respective games, feature an [[Old Man]] who provides advice on how to progress, and include Aquamentus as a boss.
