The {{Term/Store|Spore Store||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spore Store}}! My shrooms are delicious, suspicious, but never malicious!|Natie|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Spore Store is a {{Term|BotW|Mushroom}}-themed {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}} that can be found inside the [[Great Deku Tree]] in [[Korok Forest]]. The Store is managed by [[Natie]], who has been gathering {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom|link}} from the forest in anticipation of [[Link]]'s arrival.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? You're here, you're here! I've been waiting for you!|Natie|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I thought you might be hungry...so I went out and gathered a bunch of forest mushrooms for you!|Natie|BotW}}</ref>
