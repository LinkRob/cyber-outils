==Location and Uses==
Saddles are a type of [[Horse Gear]] that are worn on a [[Horse|Horse's]] body. They can be obtained by registering Horses, completing [[Mini-Game]]s, or in [[Chest]]s across [[Hyrule Kingdom]]. All Saddles, except for the [[Ancient Saddle]], are purely cosmetic. They're also all [[Key Item]]s, with the exception of the [[Stable Saddle]].

The following four [[Stable]]s have workers that are able to change the Saddle of Link's Horses. They will only change Link's [[Horse Gear]] when he has a high bond with his Horse. The Stables are as follows:
