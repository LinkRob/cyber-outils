The {{Term/Store|Golden Carving||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an item in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 125}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SSHD Gold|Golden Carving}}! This strangely shaped gold sculpture is covered in mysterious patterns.|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|SSHD|Golden Carving}} is located in a large {{Term|SSHD|Treasure Chest|link}} in the final room of {{Term|SSHD|Skyview Temple|link}}. The {{Term|SSHD|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} can be reached after {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} traverses the roots, {{Plural|SSHD|Vine|link}}, and {{Term|SSHD|Tree|link}} branches within the room. It is used in the {{Term|SSHD|Locked Door|link}} on the far side of the room, which leads to the arena where {{Term|SSHD|Link}} fights {{Term|SSHD|Ghirahim|link}} for the first time.
