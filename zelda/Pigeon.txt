{{Term/Store|Pigeon|Pigeons|Series, BotW|plural}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 164}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Plural|BotW|Pigeon}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Bird|link}} commonly found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. They are registered within the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}} under four different sub species. Like most wild animals, they can be hunted by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}, dropping a variety of meat when they are killed.

==Types of Pigeons==
===Wood Pigeon===
{{Main|Wood Pigeon}}
{{Plural|BotW|Wood Pigeon}} are the most common type of {{Plural|BotW|Pigeon}} and can be found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. They are grey in color, with a lighter grey neck and a purple head and neck. Though they lack a strong sense of awareness<ref>{{Cite|They don't have a very strong sense of awareness, so even less-skilled hunters can nab them pretty easily.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> they will flee from {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if they see him. They drop a {{Term|BotW|Raw Bird Drumstick|link}} when killed.

===Rainbow Pigeon===
{{Main|Rainbow Pigeon}}
Most commonly found in the northers forest and grasslands of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Rainbow Pigeon}} are most famous for their brightly colored plumage<ref>{{Cite|This breed of pigeon is well known for its vivid feathers. They live mainly in the northern forests and grasslands in Hyrule.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>, which is largely purple in color with a red, orange and yellow underbelly. They will flee from {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if they spot him, and they drop a {{Term|BotW|Raw Bird Thigh|link}} when killed. A {{Term|BotW|Rainbow Pigeon}}'s diet consists largely of nuts and grain, though they are also known to eat {{Plural|BotW|Insect|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They're most fond of nuts and grain but won't pass up the occasional insect or earthworm if offered.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

===Hotfeather Pigeon===
{{Main|Hotfeather Pigeon}}
Found on and around {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hotfeather Pigeon}} are mainly red in appearance with orange spots on their chest and grey wings and head. Their plumage is fireproof and highly prized as a clothing material<ref>{{Cite|This rare breed of pigeon lives near Death Mountain. Their fireproof feathers are valuable as clothing material.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>. They will flee from {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if they see him, and they drop a {{Term|BotW|Raw Bird Thigh|link}} when killed.

===White Pigeon===
{{Main|White Pigeon}}
{{Plural|BotW|White Pigeon}} are said to have got their white plumage after a {{Term|BotW|Wood Pigeon}} survived atop a snowy mountain by changing its feathers to blend in with the snow.<ref>{{Cite|It's said that a wood pigeon survived atop a snowy mountain by changing its feathers to blend in with the snow and soon after became a white pigeon.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They will flee from {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if they see him, and they drop a either a {{Term|BotW|Raw Bird Thigh|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Raw Whole Bird|link}} when killed. The high quality of there meat is due to the extra layer of fat white pigeons have developed to combat the cold.<ref>{{Cite|They have an extra layer of fat to guard against the cold, so they have been known to yield high-quality meat.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>
