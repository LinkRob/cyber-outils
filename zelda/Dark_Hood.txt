==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Dark Hood is a piece of [[Armor]] that can be purchased from [[Kilton]] at his [[Fang and Bone]] shop after all [[Divine Beast]]s have been freed. It cannot be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], nor can it be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Dark Hood, alongside the [[Dark Tunic]] and [[Dark Trousers]], compose the [[Dark Set]]. When the full set is worn, it provides a Night Speed Up bonus. Also, characters get scared when they see [[Link]] wearing it.
