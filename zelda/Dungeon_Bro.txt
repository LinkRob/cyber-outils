The {{Term/Store|Dungeon Bro|Dungeon Bros|Series, ALBW|plural}} are a pair of characters in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dungeon Bro|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon Bro}} can be found in a {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|link}} down a hole west of the {{Term|ALBW|Vacant House|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}. The younger brother stands near the start of the path leading up to the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Chest|link}}, telling {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} that he and his brother will be taking the treasure unless {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can use the {{Term|ALBW|Pegasus Boots|link}} to reach it first.<ref>{{Cite|Us brothers will be taking this here treasure! 'Less you be dashin', you may as well go home now!|Dungeon Bro|ALBW}}</ref> The older brother stands halfway up the path, wondering how he is not fast enough to reach the top.<ref>{{Cite|Pant...gasp...*hurk*... I don't... Howzit that I'm not bein' fast enough? I'm never not bein' fast enough!|Dungeon Bro|ALBW}}</ref> When {{Term|ALBW|Link}} reaches the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Chest}}, which contains a {{Term|ALBW|Gold Rupee|link}}, they will both congratulate him,<ref>{{Cite|That's a nice dash you got there!|Dungeon Bro|ALBW}}</ref> although while the older brother is impressed,<ref>{{Cite|With those wee little legs of yours... there's nowhere you couldn't go!|Dungeon Bro|ALBW}}</ref> the younger brother is skeptical and accuses {{Term|ALBW|Link}} of cheating.<ref>{{Cite|I don't buy that! This little twig of a boy, faster than my big brother? That sounds like a cheat, if you ask me!|Dungeon Bro|ALBW}}</ref>
