The {{Term/Store|Akkala Tower||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Akkala Tower'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Tower}} is a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}} located in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands}} of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Citadel Ruins}} were built around it, as it juts out from the center of the Ruins after {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} raises the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower}} simultaneously. The grounds surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Tower}} have been covered in a massive pool of {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}, which also leads a small way up the Tower. The {{Term|BotW|Temperature|link}} atop of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Tower}} is freezing, requiring {{Term|BotW|Link}} to have at least one form of a low-level {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}} active to avoid taking damage. A broken {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} can be found at the top of the Tower, where it can be searched for {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link|section=Breath of the Wild}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the top of {{Term|BotW|Akkala Tower}}, he can activate the Tower, filling in the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} of the area surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Tower Region|link}}.

An {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball|link}} can be found beside the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Tower}} which, when destroyed, will release ropes of {{Term|BotW|Malice}} holding a thin, metal slab on the northern side of the Tower. This slab can be used to form a bridge across the pools of {{Term|BotW|Malice}} surrounding the Tower. A small room near this slab contains a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} which holds a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, valued at 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Rusty Halberd|link}} can be found on one of the staircases of the Ruins surrounding the Tower. A small crawlspace along the southern wall on the interior of the center building leads to a small, hidden bedroom. Inside this room is a wooden {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} which contains five {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}}. A lit {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Broadsword|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield|link}} can be found in the remains of a building southeast of the Tower. The second floor of this building holds a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} which contains five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}.
