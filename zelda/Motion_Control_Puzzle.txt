{{Term/Store|Motion Control Puzzle|Motion Control Puzzles|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 30}}</ref>

==Locations and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}} are found inside several {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. Many of these {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} have the word "Apparatus" in their Trial names. {{Plural|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}} typically glow orange, but they turn blue when they are in use.

Near all {{Plural|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}} will be a large platform. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} interacts with a {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}}, he will be able to control and move this platform. In {{Term|BotW|Myahm Agana Shrine|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ze Kasho Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must move one or more {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} by tilting the platform in certain directions. In other {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}} to manipulate the platform into a path forwards.
