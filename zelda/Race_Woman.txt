The '''Race Woman''' is a supporting character from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past]]''. When Link gets out of the younger of the [[Quarreling Brothers]]' room, she tells Link that if he can get through a maze in 15 seconds (or less), she and a man will give him something good, and then announces the start of the race.

It is possible that she is the boys' mother.
