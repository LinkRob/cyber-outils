{{Term/Store|Goro Cove||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Goro Cove|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Goro Cove is a small lake of lava in [[Eldin Canyon]] that lies to the east of [[Goron City]]. The Cove consists of several lava falls, which flow from the lava moat surrounding [[Death Mountain]]. The lava continues to flow north into [[Darunia Lake]].

A giant rib cage sits partially submerged in the Cove's lava. A [[Korok]] is hidden at the rib cage's southern point, and will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] upon being found.

==Nomenclature==
Goro Cove derives its name from ''-goro'', a suffix used by [[Goron]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.
