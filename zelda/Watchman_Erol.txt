'''Watchman Erol''' is a character featured in the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}.{{Name Ref}} As his name suggests, he is one of the watchmen of the [[North Castle]]. He only briefly appears in ''[[He Also Serves]]''.

==Biography==
When Ganon sneaks into the castle, disguised as Impa, trying to get link to step outside the castle, into a trap set by Ganon's minions who are laying in ambush, Link exposes him and orders Erol to refrain from opening the gate for anyone, because ganon cannot teleport outside the castle.<ref name=watchman>{{Cite Book|quote=Watchman Erol, keep the gate closed! Nobody gets in or out! '''NOBODY'''!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=7 [He Also Serves]}}</ref> However, he accidentally opens it for Ganon, who was disguised as Link, but Ganon barely sets foot outside of the caste before being destroyed in his own trap.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link: Watchman! Open the gate IMMEDIATELY!<br>Erol: But, Link, you just said--<br>Link: OPEN IT!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=9 [He Also Serves]}}</ref>
