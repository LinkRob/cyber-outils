The {{Term/Store|Ice Ruins||Series, ALBW}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon|link}} in {{ALBW}}. It is the remains of a hall that was buried and destroyed by the ice resulting from the perpetual winter conditions in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is Death Mountain. Here it is always winter... Somewhere buried under the ice is a ruined hall.|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> Theme-wise, it is the counterpart of the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Palace|link}} in the {{Term|ALBW|Dark World|link}} but location-wise it is the counterpart of the {{Term|ALBW|Dark World}}'s {{Term|ALttP|Turtle Rock|link}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Hookshot|link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Fire Rod|link}} are required to access the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins}}, and its boss is {{Term|ALBW|Dharkstare}}.

==Entrance to the Ruins==
To reach the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} must traverse both {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}'s and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}'s {{Plural|ALBW|Death Mountain}}. He must first use the {{Term|ALBW|Hookshot}} to cross a broken bridge east from the {{Term|ALBW|Tower of Hera (A Link Between Worlds)|link}} and then descend through a volcanic cavern to reach {{Term|ALBW|Rosso's Ore Mine|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must then pass through a {{Term|ALBW|Fissure|link}} outside to access that region of {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Death Mountain}}, then ascend through an icy cavern to reach the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins}} entrance area.

==Themes and Navigation==
Entering and exploring the depths of the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins}} relies heavily on the use of the {{Term|ALBW|Fire Rod}}. It is full of frozen blocks and enemies, as well as slippery floors. It also contains platforms of very thin ice that crack the longer {{Term|ALBW|Link}} stays on them, and eventually shatter, causing {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to fall through. Using the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Rod|link}} when these platforms crack repairs them whereas using the {{Term|ALBW|Fire Rod}} destroys them. If they shatter from {{Term|ALBW|Link}}'s weight or are melted with the {{Term|ALBW|Fire Rod}}, they will crystallize and reintegrate to their original form in a matter of seconds. The dungeon has a large number of floors and its layout is based around a central column with a long cyclic chain of floating blocks acting as an elevator system. Areas peripheral to this central column are mostly a network of walkways and platforms over a deep chasm. Although there are cubical blocks of ice that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merge|link}} into and walk around, there are also more naturally shaped ice crystals that can be destroyed with the {{Term|ALBW|Fire Rod}} and may contain various objects. The central puzzle theme of the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins}} is moving the central column from floor to floor by pulling the tongues of certain gargoyle statues.

The miniboss of the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Big Pengator}} accompanied by a horde of normal-sized {{Plural|ALBW|Pengator|link}}. Defeating them produces a {{Term|ALBW|Portal (Object)|link}} linking their room, on one of the lower floors, to the dungeon entrance. 

After defeating {{Term|ALBW|Dharkstare}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} acquires a {{Term|ALBW|Heart Container}} and frees {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}}.

===Items===
The {{Term|ALBW|Big Key|link}} can be acquired early on. Floor B5 contains only the chest with the {{Term|ALBW|Big Key}} inside; {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can take the main elevator from the dungeon entrance all the way down to get it. The {{Term|ALBW|Compass|link}} is in the southeast area of floor B1 in a chest that can be immediately accessed.

The {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins}} also contains a {{Term|ALBW|Stamina Scroll|link}}. It is located on the east platform of the southern room on floor B3. The only way to access it is from floor B2; a lever must be pulled in the central room of B2 that removes a platform above the chest.
