==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Molduga Sighting}} is located in the {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Desert (Hyrule Warriors)|link}} and can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}. In the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} is given four minutes to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Molduga}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Molduga Fin|link}}, and three {{Plural|HWAoC|Molduga Guts|link}}.
