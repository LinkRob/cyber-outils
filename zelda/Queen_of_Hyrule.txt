The {{Term/Store|Queen of Hyrule||Series, BotW}} is a character mentioned in {{BotW}}.<ref name ="Urbosa">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa}} had a beloved friend whom she thought the world of... {{Color|BotW Blue|the queen of Hyrule}}.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule}} ruled over {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} alongside her husband, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}}. Together, they had a daughter, who they named {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} in accordance with the naming traditions of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Today, as the sun rose and a new day was born, my daughter, too, joined this sweet world. In keeping with the traditions of the royal family, I have decided to name her...Zelda.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> She was said to be naturally drawn to her sacred powers, which she described as overflowing.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book= CaC |publisher= |page= 378}}</ref>

During her life, the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule}} was a good friend to {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}, the chief of the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo|link}}.<ref name="Urbosa"/> After {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} was born, the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule|display=Queen}} brought her to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} so she could meet {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}.<ref>{{Cite|My dear friend from afar came to visit Gerudo Town today. It is always a pleasure to see the queen of Hyrule. She described her reason for coming as urgent. She wished for me to meet her newborn child. Her sweet daughter's name is Zelda. She has her mother's smile. I cannot help but cherish her already.|Urbosa's Diary|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} said that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} would grow up to be a beautiful queen like her mother, the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule|display=Queen}} instead wished that she would find happiness in her life.<ref>{{Cite|I told her that Zelda is sure to grow up into a dignified and beautiful queen, just like her mother. My friend thanked me, but said that looks are fleeting, and instead she wishes for Zelda to be blessed with true happiness.|Urbosa's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

As she grew up, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} had a warm and loving relationship with the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule}}. She told {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} about her sealing power, which she claimed would one day develop within her.<ref name="Mother"/> She also encouraged her daughter, saying that she could do anything and that all would end well.<ref>{{Cite|Mother used to smile and tell me, "Zelda, my love, all will be well in the end. You can do anything."|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule|display=Queen}} affectionately referred to her daughter as her "little bird."<ref>{{Cite|The way she gazes upon her daughter... her little bird, as she calls her... I have never seen such unconditional love.|Urbosa's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

10 years before the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule}} suddenly and unexpectedly passed away.<ref name="Queen"/><ref>{{Cite|It has been a long while since we laid my dear friend, the queen, to rest. I only now have the will to write again. It was so sudden. I still can't believe she is gone.|Urbosa's Diary|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book= CaC |publisher= |page= 362}}</ref> She was unable to teach {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} about her sacred powers before her untimely demise.<ref>{{Cite|Mother passed the year before my training was to begin. In losing her, I lost not just a mother, but a teacher.|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In truth, I understand Zelda's feelings. Painfully so. She lost her mother, her teacher, before she could learn from her. Ten pointless years of self-training, without so much as a book or note to help find her way...|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> Her death meant that {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} was left alone to prepare {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} for the resurrection of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|From now on, I must raise her all alone... Now, only I remain to prepare her for her difficult future as a princess of Hyrule.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* {{CaC}} speculates that the {{Term|Series|Queen of Hyrule}} could have been the fortune-teller that predicted the resurrection of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}, as women of the {{Term|Series|Royal Family of Hyrule}} are known to have premonitions of the future.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book= CaC |publisher= |page= 374}}</ref>
