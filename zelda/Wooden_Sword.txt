The {{Term/Store|Wooden Sword|Wooden Swords|Series, OoS, OoA, TP, TPHD}} is a recurring Sword in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 143 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA, TP}}}}</ref>

==Mainline appearances==
==={{OoS|-}}===
The Wooden Sword is the first weapon obtained in {{OoS}}. It is obtained inside the [[Hero's Cave]] on the [[Western Coast]].<ref>{{Cite|You got the Wooden Sword! This truly is a sign of courage!|N/A|OoS}}</ref> In a [[Linked Game]], Link already has the Wooden Sword from the start. It is used to open the [[Maku Tree Gate]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
The Wooden Sword is the first weapon obtained in {{OoA}}. It is given to [[Link]] by [[Impa]]. In a [[Linked Game]], Link already has the Wooden Sword, and Impa gives him a [[Wooden Shield]] instead.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the Wooden Sword is received from [[Rusl]] as a present. Link uses it to learn basic fighting skills. He keeps the Wooden Sword until after rescuing [[Talo]], where he gives the sword to Talo so he and [[Colin]] can get past them to where [[Ilia]] is holding [[Epona]]. Later on, the children are captured by [[King Bulblin]] and the Wooden Sword is broken in the process. [[Wolf Link]] comes across the Wooden Sword when looking for the children and is able to pick up the [[Youths' Scent]] by smelling it.

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Main|Hylian Sword}}
In {{HW}}, a practice Wooden Sword is briefly seen in Link's possession during his introduction cutscene, but is not a usable weapon during actual gameplay. However, an 8-bit Wooden Sword from ''The Legend of Zelda'' appears as a special weapon in Link's [[Hylian Sword]] category, added to the game with the Version 1.2.0 update. It is statistically identical to the [[Hylian Sword#Magical Sword|Magical Sword]].
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The description for the Wooden Sword in the Wii version of {{TP|-}} is slightly different from other console versions. It implies that Rusl repairs the Wooden Sword before returning it,<ref>{{Cite|You got the wooden sword! It was returned to you fully repaired. It's easy to use: just swing [Wii Remote]!|N/A|TP}}</ref> whereas all other versions state that he simply honed it.<ref>{{Cite|You got the wooden sword! Rusl returned it after honing it for you.
