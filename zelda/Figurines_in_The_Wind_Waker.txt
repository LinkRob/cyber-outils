{{DISPLAYTITLE:Figurines in {{TWW|-}}}}
:''You may also wish to see [[Figurines in The Minish Cap|Figurines in {{TMC|-}}]].''
'''Figurines''' are small sculptures of characters and enemies in {{TWW}}. They can be obtained and viewed in the [[Nintendo Gallery]] after a suitable [[Pictograph]] of the subject is shown to [[Carlov]].

There are 134 figurines to collect in total. 17 of these are collected under special circumstances.
