The {{Term/Store|Reflecting Shield||Series, TWoG}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|TFoE}}<ref>{{Cite|Reflecting Shield|N/A|TWoG}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{TFoE|-}}===
The Reflecting Shield goes unnamed in {{TFoE}}. It is an upgrade to the starting [[Shield]] that, in addition to blocking enemy projectiles, is also able to deflect [[Lupay (Boss)|Lupay]]'s spells back at them, and it is necessary to defeat them.  Link receives the Reflecting Shield from [[Suprena]] in [[Serigon Caves]] after he brings her the [[Crystal of Reflection]], which she uses to bless Link's Shield with.<ref>{{Cite|This shield both sword and spear deflects, but cannot stop the vilest curse. This crystal makes the shield reflect, cursing the curser with twice the curse.|Suprena|TFoE}}</ref>

==={{TWoG|-}}===
In {{TWoG}}, the Reflecting Shield appears identically as in {{TFoE|-}}. In addition to blocking enemy projectiles, it is also able to deflect [[Wizzrobe (Boss)#The Wand of Gamelon|Wizzrobe]]'s spells back at him, and it is necessary to defeat him. The Shield is found inside the final cabin on [[Gobiyan Ship]].
