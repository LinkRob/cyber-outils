{{Term/Store|Deltan||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|Deltan|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]<ref>"''Deltan"'' — N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']])</ref>.

==Biography==

=== {{BotW|-}} ===
Deltan is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who can be found depressed and hunched over at a table in {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She is a graduate of {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link|display=Ashai's}} {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} class.<ref name="Cooking">{{Cite|"I like to be the one to do the cooking." Jerk waits until AFTER I take the cooking class to tell me this...|Deltan|BotW}}</ref>

Presumably intoxicated from drinking, Deltan mutters about "stupid voe" to herself if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to speak to her.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Stupid voe... Who needs them...}}|Deltan|BotW}}</ref> She's attempted to charm some voe with her newfound knowledge, yet all her attempts proved unsuccessful. Deltan complains about a man who told her that he is engaged but claims that she cannot be angry about it.<ref>{{Cite|"I'm engaged." *sigh* I can't even be angry at that one...|Deltan|BotW}}</ref> She also complains about a man who only told her that he likes to cook after she took Ashai's Cooking class.<ref name="Cooking"/> Another man said that he does not like girls twice his height, which Deltan is confused by since she's only eight feet tall.<ref>{{Cite|"I don't like girls twice my height..." What's he mean by that?! I'm only eight feet tall!|Deltan|BotW}}</ref> Deltan grew her hair out because a man told her he liked long hair, but he then informed her that he liked short hair.<ref>{{Cite|"I really like shorter hair..." I grew my hair out because he said he liked LONG hair!|Deltan|BotW}}</ref>

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
In Tears of the Kingdom, she attends Ashai's "Voe and You" classes. She initially has difficulty speaking to men due to her latest relationship. To help her overcome that difficulty, Link can buy a [[Ring-Garland]] from [[Koko]] in [[Kakariko Village]] and [[fuse]] it to one of his [[Weapon|weapons]] or [[Shield|shields]]. From that point onwards her outlook brightens. In class she mentions her appreciation of snow<ref>"''Have you seen that fluffy, light ice that floats down from the sky? I think it's called snow?''" — Deltan (''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'')</ref>.
