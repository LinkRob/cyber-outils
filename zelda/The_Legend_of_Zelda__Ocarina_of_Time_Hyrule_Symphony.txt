{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Hyrule Symphony''||Series, OoT}} is is a live performance album released in 1999.{{Name Ref|OoT}} The album has twelve orchestrated versions of songs from the [[The Legend of Zelda: Original Soundtrack|original soundtrack.]] The tracks are composed by [[Koji Kondo]] and co-arranged by Ryuichi Katsumata.
