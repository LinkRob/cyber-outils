==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|SS}}
There are many {{Plural|SSHD|Ring-Shaped Rock}} scattered throughout the {{Term|SSHD|Sky|link}}. They look like the {{Term|SSHD|Sky}}'s standard floating boulders, except for a hole in the center. This hole gathers a mysterious energy that can propel {{Term|SSHD|Flying|link}} {{Plural|SSHD|Loftwing|link}} to high speeds.
