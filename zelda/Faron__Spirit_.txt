{{Term/Store|Faron||Series, TP, TPHD}} is the [[Spirit of Light]] who watches over the [[Faron Province]] in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276{{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref> It is named after the [[Faron (Dragon)|dragon of the same name]] from the [[Era of the Goddess Hylia]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 116}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 74}}</ref>

==Biography==
Prior to the events of {{TP|-}}, the [[Dark Interlopers]] tried to take over the [[Sacred Realm]]. Faron, along with [[Eldin (Spirit)|Eldin]] and [[Lanayru (Spirit)|Lanayru]], were asked by the [[Golden Goddesses]] to intervene, so the Light Spirits sealed away the Interlopers' magic.<ref>{{Cite|It was then that the goddesses ordered us three light spirits to intervene. We sealed away the great magic those individuals had mastered.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref>

Faron is the second spirit [[Link]] meets during his journey. As part of [[Zant]]'s plan to take over Hyrule, the [[Shadow Beast]]s were ordered to steal the light of the Spirits, including Faron.<ref>{{Cite|My brethren in Hyrule have already had their light stolen by these fell beasts.|Ordona (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> Once Link recovers all of the [[Tears of Light]] in the [[Faron Woods]], Faron recovers its powers and returns the young hero to his human form. The spirit then tells Link of the [[Twilight]],<ref>{{Cite|This drape of shadows is  called...twilight. In this twilight, those who lived in the light...become as mere spirits...it is a place...where the dark ones and evil creatures dwell...|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> and of his [[Ancient Hero|predecessor]], who is implied to be the [[Hero of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|Look at your awakened form... The green tunic that is your garb once belonged to the ancient hero chosen by the gods... His power is yours. His is the true power that slept within you.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> It also informs Link that he is the new hero, having been chosen by the gods.<ref>{{Cite|In the land covered in twilight, where people roam as spirits, you were transformed into a blue-eyed beast... That was a sign... It was a sign that the powers of the chosen one rest within you...and that they are awakening. [...] Your name is Link. You are the hero chosen by the gods.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref>

Later in the game, Faron, along with the other Light Spirits, aid Link and [[Princess Zelda]] in the fight against [[Ganondorf]] by granting Zelda the power of the [[Light Arrow]]s. After Ganondorf is defeated, it and the other Spirits grant [[Midna]]'s return to her true form. 

==Trivia==
*Faron is seemingly meant to look like a monkey, a squirrel, or a lemur.

==Nomenclature==
Faron's name is a reference to the golden goddess, [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]].
