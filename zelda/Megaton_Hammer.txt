==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
The {{Term|OoT3D|Megaton Hammer}} is a powerful and heavy steel hammer found as the key item for the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}} in {{OoT}}. It is used to break apart large rocks (except gray ones which require the [[Silver Gauntlets]]) and smash in rusted {{Plural|OoT3D|Switch|link}} to make them function. It can also be used to destroy blocks for access to new areas and to open {{Plural|OoT3D|Secret Grotto|link}} without the need of [[Bomb|Bombs]]. Despite its great power, the Megaton Hammer inflicts as much damage as the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Sword|link}} in combat, but it has slower swinging speed compared to {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|OoT3D|Sword|link}}. It can be used against certain enemies, such as {{Plural|OoT3D|Tektite|link}}, to flip them over and attack them. If used on the ground close to a wall with a {{Term|OoT3D|Skullwalltula|link}}, it will knock the enemy off and defeat it without hitting it directly. It is also effective on [[Business Scrub|Business Shrubs]]. Like the [[Biggoron's Sword]], however, the Megaton Hammer, due to its weight, requires both hands to wield when equipped and Link cannot defend with his [[Shield]]. If the shield button is pressed, he attempts to protect himself with the hammer.

According to {{Term|OoT3D|Goron|link}} legends, a mythic hero of the {{Plural|OoT3D|Goron}} and an ancestor to {{Term|OoT3D|Darunia|link}}, patriarch of the {{Plural|OoT3D|Goron}}, used the {{Term|OoT3D|Megaton Hammer}} in battle against {{Term|OoT3D|Volvagia|link}}. Wielding it, he was victorious and sealed the dragon in the fires of {{Term|OoT3D|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A long time ago there was an evil dragon named Volvagia living in this mountain. That dragon was very scary! He ate Gorons! Using a huge hammer, the hero of the Gorons... BOOOM! Destroyed it just like that. This is a myth from long ago, but it's true! I know, because my dad is a descendant of the hero!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> Thus, it is necessary for the [[Hero of Time]] to defeat {{Term|OoT3D|Volvagia}} with this item. The {{Term|OoT3D|Megaton Hammer}} is also effective in the final battle with {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon|link}} for attacking his tail during the phase when the Master Sword is knocked away.

== Trivia ==

* The Megaton Hammer's name is a pun of "megaton", a unit commonly used for nuclear weapon devices which measures a million tons of TNT.
* This item is likely based on the [[Hammer|Magic Hammer]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', as that weapon also can knock certain enemies off the ground.
* [[Goron Link]]'s punch shares the same [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_MegatonHammer_Hit.wav sound effect] as the Megaton Hammer when he hits a wall in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''. It is also used for the man in [[Termina Field]] near the [[Astral Observatory]] when Link knocks him off a tree.
* Like the [[Deku Stick]] and [[Giant's Knife]]/Biggoron's Sword, if Link were to equip the Megaton Hammer during cutscenes or in his idle animations, he can be seen holding it with only one hand. This also applies to ''SoulCalibur II''.
* Although ''SoulCalibur II'' is not canon to [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], despite sharing the same damage as the Master Sword in ''Ocarina of Time'', the Megaton Hammer is one of the few weapons the Hero of Time owns in this game that is stronger than the sacred blade, and even slightly surpasses the Biggoron's Sword.
