==Overview==
{{Plural|Series|Object}} are anything that is not stored within a character's {{Term|Series|Inventory|link}}. {{Plural|Series|Object}} are separate from {{Plural|Series|Item|link}}, which are things that exist inside the {{Plural|Series|Inventory}}. Some {{Plural|Series|Object}}, such as {{Plural|Series|Torch|link}}, may also overlap with {{Plural|Series|Item}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Items in The Minish Cap#Objects}}
There are many {{Plural|TMC|Object}} scattered throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} for {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to interact with in {{TMC}}. Some, such as {{Plural|TMC|Lily Pad|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal|link}}, are seemingly innocuous and naturally occurring {{Plural|TMC|Object}} that will grant {{Term|TMC|Link}} access to new areas while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized. Others, such as {{Plural|TMC|Crimson Cyclone|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Elastic Mushroom|link}}, and {{Plural|TMC|Tornado|link}}, help him to traverse more dangerous areas of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}, including {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}}. A number of staple {{Plural|Series|Object}} in {{TLoZ|Series}} appear in {{TMC}} as well, including {{Plural|TMC|Jar|link}} and various {{Plural|TMC|Switch|link}}.
