'''Mongchips''' are [[enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Mongchips are axe-wielding dwarf-like creatures found only in the [[East Forest]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can defeat a Mongchip by hitting it with the [[Wand]] a few times.
