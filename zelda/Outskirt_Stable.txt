The {{Term/Store|Outskirt Stable||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Outskirt Stable'''|Map|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'''Outskirt Stable'''|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. It is operated by {{Term|BotW|Embry|link}} and employs {{Term|BotW|Canni|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Haite|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Myti|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Toffa|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Trott|link}}, all of whom live there and have to follow the strict vegetarian policy {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable}} has for all of its employees. As a result of this policy {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can receive the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|A Rare Find|link}}" from {{Term|BotW|Trott}} who now craves {{Term|BotW|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also receive the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} "{{Term|BotW|The Royal White Stallion|link}}" by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Toffa}}, who will reward {{Term|BotW|Link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Royal Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Royal Saddle|link|display=Saddle}} if he shows him the {{Term|BotW|White Horse|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} {{Term|BotW|My Hero|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Aliza|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who lives at the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} and is obsessed with finding the {{Term|BotW|Hero of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So it was true! I met the hero of Hyrule under this sacred tree!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>

The outside of {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable}} is surrounded by several tree stumps, and has a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} near the entrance that {{Term|BotW|Myti|}} sits by during the day, explaining that the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} doesn't serve food and offering its use to travelers.<ref>{{Cite|Would you like to use the cooking pot?|Myti|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Cooking pot?] We don't serve food at the stable. Please, use this cooking pot.|Myti|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Canni}}, a young {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} woman, can be found tending to {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} and is one of the four {{Term|BotW|Stable}} hands who will offer to customise {{Term|BotW|Link}}’s {{Term|BotW|Horse}},<ref>{{Cite|Hello there, traveler. Welcome to our {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}}. You will stay awhile, won't you? If you have a horse, we can help you change its {{Color|BotW Blue|gear}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|mane}} style. We're always happy to meet new horses, so stop on by!|Canni|BotW}}</ref> provided it has a sufficiently high enough bond and is not too big. {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} will regularly visit the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} and the watchman and traveling salesman, {{Term|BotW|Botrick|link}} will also stop by. {{Term|BotW|Glendo|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveler, will occasionally be seen passing by. {{Term|BotW|Haite}} runs around the Stable during the day.<ref>{{Cite|Playing tag! You want to play?|Haite|BotW}}</ref> When it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}s, he sits at one of the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} doors and waits for the {{Term|BotW|Rain}} to stop.<ref>{{Cite|I wish the rain would stop.|Haite|BotW}}</ref>

Inside {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a poster depicting a {{Term|BotW|Recipe|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Energizing Honey Crepe|link}}, as well as the book "{{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill Vol.3|link}}" laying open on a table.

A {{Term|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} may pass by the {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable}} disguised as a {{Term|BotW|Traveler|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} befriends the {{Term|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}} and feeds it three times, the Retriever will lead {{Term|BotW|Link}} to a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} buried next to one of the Tree stumps.
