{{Term/Store|Reapling|Reaplings|Series, PH|plural}} are enemies in {{PH}}.<ref name="E 200"/>

==Characteristics==
Reaplings only appear on the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}. They deal two [[Heart]]s of damage to [[Link]]. Reaplings can be avoided by moving when they have their backs turned to Link. They cannot see Link in [[Safe Zone]]s, but can instead move and see through walls. Reaplings are immune to most attacks and cannot be defeated, but a shot to the back with the [[Bow]] will temporarily stun them. They can also be stunned if hit in the back with [[Oshus's Sword]] infused with [[Leaf]].
