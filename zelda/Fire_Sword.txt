{{Term/Store|Fire Sword|Fire Swords|Series, TFoE, TWoG|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TFoE|-}}===
The {{Term|TFoE|Fire Sword}} is the third upgrade to the {{Term|TFoE|Sword|link}} in {{TFoE|-}}. Like the {{Term|TFoE|Power Sword|link}}, it is able to shoot {{Plural|TFoE|Power Blast|link}}, but can now shoot them even when {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} or {{Term|TFoE|Zelda|link}} is not at full health. In the in-game cutscenes, it is shown to be a very powerful sword, capable of shooting devastating {{Plural|TFoE|Power Blast}}.

{{Term|TFoE|Link}} receives the sword after freeing {{Term|TFoE|Kulvan|link}} in {{Term|TFoE|Shipwreck Cliff|link}} and giving him a {{Term|TFoE|Fire Diamond|link}}, which he uses to temper {{Term|TFoE|Link}}'s sword with to create the {{Term|TFoE|Fire Sword}}.

==={{TWoG|-}}===
{{Stub|TWoG}}
In {{TWoG|-}}, {{Term|TWoG|Zelda}} receives the {{Term|TWoG|Fire Sword}} from {{Term|TWoG|Yokan|link}} in {{Term|TWoG|Sakado|link}} after bringing him a {{Term|TWoG|Heat Crystal|link}} to temper her {{Term|TWoG|Fire Sword}} with.
