{{Plural|BotW|Emblazoned Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} featuring a traditional design from the {{Term|BotW|Necluda|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> This design has lead to the {{Term|BotW|Emblazoned Shield|display=Shield}} finding popularity, even though they do not provide much protection when compared to {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Shield|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be found in many locations, particularly within {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. One can be located in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gatepost Town Ruins|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Oman Au Shrine|link}}. Alternatively, there is one at the {{Term|BotW|Equestrian Riding Course|link}}. An {{Term|BotW|Emblazoned Shield|display=Shield}} can also be obtained from {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} between 3:00 and 3:15.<ref>{{Cite|A shiny new record deserves a shiny new shield. Here y'go!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> Several travelers also carry an {{Term|BotW|Emblazoned Shield}} on their journeys.

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Emblazoned Shield}} will burn and eventually disintegrate if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. Finally, they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
