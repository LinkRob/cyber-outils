{{Term/Store|Madame Aroma||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Madame Aroma}} was referred to as '''Madam Aroma''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Madam Aroma}}<br/>Madam Aroma was the wife of Mayor Dotour, who served as Mayor while Link was struggling to save Termina from the falling Moon.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160311223549/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/m.jsp#MadamAroma|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
Madame Aroma is the wife of [[Mayor Dotour]] and the mother of [[Kafei]]. Since Kafei's disappearance, she has been trying to locate him.<ref>{{Cite|The person I want you to locate is my son, Kafei.|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> She is in charge of the activities taking place during the [[Carnival of Time]] and is busy working with [[Toto]] and [[Gorman]] for the majority of the three day period.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, dear me... Gorman! There's a problem! The opening performance I've asked you to do... They've canceled! Just now!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> She hires Link to investigate the disappearance of her son and gives him the [[Kafei's Mask]], which she made herself,<ref>{{Cite|The cute boy's mask seems to have been made by an important man's wife...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> so that he is able to question townspeople regarding Kafei's whereabouts.<ref>{{Cite|Oh dear, are you on a field trip? ...Or are you the expert person-finder I hired? Yes, yes, yes. I say, you have the face of a pro.|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> To receive the mask, Link must visit her in the [[Mayor's Official Residence]] any time after 10:00 AM on the [[First Day|First]] or [[Second Day]].

During the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest, Link receives the [[Special Delivery to Mama]] from the [[Postman]], which must be delivered to Madame Aroma in the [[Milk Bar#Majora's Mask|Milk Bar]] after 6 PM. By wearing Kafei's Mask, Link can hand over the letter to her, who in return will reward Link with a [[Bottle]] full of [[Chateau Romani]]. In {{MM3D}}, giving her the letter again will result in her giving him a [[Fishing Hole Pass]]. She is last seen attending her son's [[Anju and Kafei's Wedding|wedding]] during the [[New Day|ending credits]].

==Trivia==
*Madame Aroma is one of the few {{MM|-}} characters that does not have an {{OoT}} counterpart.
*[[Mrs. Ruul]] from {{OoS}}, [[Mrs. Marie]] from {{TWW}}, and {{Term|TMC|Tina|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Dina|link}} from {{TMC}} all share a similar hairstyles with Madame Aroma.
*In the non-canon {{MM|-}} demo, [[Cremia]] refers to Madame Aroma as her aunt.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Ah... You. Did my aunt put you to work?|author= Liliana|published= February 26, 2011|retrieved=April 12, 2015|url= https://tcrf.net/Proto:The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Majora%27s_Mask/Preview_Demo/Text_differences|title= Proto:The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask/Preview Demo/Text differences|site= The Cutting Room Floor|type=}}</ref> In the final release, she simply calls her a middle-aged woman.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... You. Did some middle-aged woman put you to work?|Cremia|MM}}</ref>
**The change was likely made in order to fix a mistranslation. In Japan, as well as other East Asian cultures, the word for "aunt" can be used to refer to any middle-aged woman even if they are not related to the speaker.
