'''Dora Dora''' is a boss from {{FPTRR}}. He is found deep within the caverns of {{Term|FPTRR|Desma's Labyrinth}} inside {{Term|FPTRR|Mount Desma|link}}. To defeat him, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} must avoid his attacks, while climbing up out of the mountain. He must do this quickly, as the magma rises slowly, and will soon catch up to him if he delays. After reaching the top of the mountain, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} and Dora Dora will shoot out into the air, and the Fire Monster will turn to stone due to the cold air. After Dora Dora has been destroyed, his body will burst into 60,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}},<ref>{{Cite|You got 60,000 rupees!|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref> and {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} must catch them as he falls back to earth and through {{Term|FPTRR|Mount Desma}}.
