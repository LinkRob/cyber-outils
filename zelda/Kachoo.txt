{{Term/Store|Kachoo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Kachoo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kachoo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} woman who manages the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}.

Recently, the {{Term|BotW|Inn|link}} has been losing business due to the appearance of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, which upsets {{Term|BotW|Kachoo}}.<ref>{{Cite|Grrr... We've lost a lot of business ever since that {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} started stomping out in the desert.|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kachoo}} assures {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that while the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} may be loud, the beds at the {{Term|BotW|Inn}} are still comfy.<ref>{{Cite|The sound of the Divine Beast walking might be loud, but the beds are still comfy. Do you want to stay?|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kachoo}} is relieved that people will be able to sleep soundly at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn|display=Inn}} without the constant stomping.<ref>{{Cite|With the Divine Beast gone, you can sleep soundly without being woken up by constant stomping. Want to stay?|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kachoo}} while wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, she says claims the clothes look nice on him and that he could pass for a woman.<ref>{{Cite|You know, those clothes look quite nice on you. Even on close examination, you look every bit the fine vai.|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kachoo}} offers a regular {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}} for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} and a soft {{Term|BotW|Bed}} for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|BotW Blue|regular bed}} is {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. A {{Color|BotW Blue|soft bed}} is {{Color|BotW Red|40 rupees}}.|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> If asked, she explains that the soft {{Term|BotW|Bed}} is made from {{Term|BotW|Cucco|link}} feathers and has been designed to leave its user full of energy when they wake up.<ref>{{Cite|[What makes it soft?] The {{Color|BotW Blue|soft bed}} has been carefully crafted from Cucco feathers. It's designed to leave you full of energy when you wake|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn}} offers a mandatory wake-up shout, but {{Term|BotW|Kachoo}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to choose when he receives it.<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|BotW Blue|regular bed}} it is. We offer a mandatory wake-up shout. When would you like it?|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|One {{Color|BotW Blue|soft bed}} coming up! We offer a mandatory wake-up shout. When would you like it?|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> She wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} a good rest before he goes to sleep.<ref>{{Cite|OK. Don't oversleep. Sav'orr!|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sleep well. Sav'orr!|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kachoo}} will loudly greet {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he wakes up.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sav'otta!}}|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sav'aaq!}}|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sav'saaba!}}|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> She tells him to be safe in his travels before he leaves.<ref>{{Cite|I hope you slept well.<br/>Take care and safe travels.|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hope you slept well.<br/>It's cold outside, so travel safe!|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wasn't that bed the softest?<br/>Take care and safe travels to you!|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref>
