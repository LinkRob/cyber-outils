The {{Term/Store|Rohta Chigah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Rohta Chiga Shrine}}<br/>Stop to Start}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Stop to Start''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah Shrine}} is located on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, between the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Eastern Abbey|link}}. It is one of the four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that appear on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}} as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Decayed Guardian|link}} and the various {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} that appear in the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} north of the {{Term|BotW|Eastern Abbey}} during the trial.<ref>{{Cite|The One-Hit Obliterator, as the name suggests, can defeat enemies in one-hit. However, the wielder will also be defeated in one hit. Defeat the monsters at the locations marked on the map within the Great Plateau.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After the last {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} has been defeated, {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah Shrine}} will rise up from beneath the ground. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Rohta Chigah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Rohta Chigah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah Shrine}} features three long corridors arranged in a U-shape. {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah}} waits on the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} in a chamber at the end of the third corridor. In front of the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} are several {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes|link}} arranged in a checkerboard pattern. The floor rhythmically alternates between half of the spikes being raised and the other half being lowered. A climbable metal block and long metal platform lie on top of the {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}}, while a smaller metal platform lies nearby. Using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must create a path over the {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}}. Beyond the gate at the end of the room is the second chamber.

In the second chamber are two gears spinning clockwise, which have walls covered in {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}} to impede {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s progress. The first gear can easily be walked across, but {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on the second one, or else he will be moved into the {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}}.

Past the gears are three conveyor belts. The first is moving towards {{Term|BotW|Link}}, and will push him into another wall of {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}}. The second and third conveyor belts move to the left and right, directing {{Term|BotW|Link}} towards a rotating wall of {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}}. By using {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} on the second conveyor belt, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to hang to the right when he steps off of it, preventing the third conveyor belt from pushing him into the {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}} before he reaches the next platform.

In the second corridor, three {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder]|link}} are suspended from the ceiling by chains, and they swing back and forth over a large chasm. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to cross the chasm and avoid the {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder}}. The {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} can also be used to stop one of the {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder}} from moving. In the next chamber, a {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}} is located on a level below {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s current position.

Stepping on the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}} will open the portcullis in front of {{Term|BotW|Link}}, but it will also open the wall behind him. This a large wall of {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}} that begins advancing down the third corridor. The third corridor has a narrow walkway with small stone blocks on the path. A number of {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}} connected to {{Plural|BotW|Launcher|link}} line the walls of this corridor. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} advances, some of these {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} will jut out, but the retract themselves as the wall of {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}} approaches.

Further down the corridor, two {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} will launch forward and then quickly retract, but a third {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} replaces them. Near the end of the corridor, the narrow walkway ends and four {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} with {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}} will jut outward into the gap between the walkway and the next platform. Link can climb one of these {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} and use the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to reach the chamber, where the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} is located.

Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will present {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Rohta Chigah|BotW}}</ref> Following this, {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Rohta Chigah|BotW}}</ref>

In the first chamber of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, there is a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} to the right of the {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}}. Using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can create a path to this {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Shield|link}}
