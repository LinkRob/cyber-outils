{{Term/Store|Riola Spring||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Riola Spring'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Riola Spring}} is located in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. It can be found to the south of {{Term|BotW|Mount Floria|link}} where it is connected to {{Term|BotW|Floria Falls|link}} by a waterfall and a short stretch of river. It is frequently stormy, and the land that surrounds the spring is composed of steep cliffs covered in long grass and tropical trees. During the nights of the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon|link}} the Great Dragon {{Term|BotW|Farosh|link}} will arrive and bathe in the water. 

A total of ten {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found at {{Term|BotW|Riola Spring}}, with six metal chests in the waters themselves and two wooden ones under each of the boulders that lie between the remaining central stone pillars.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find three {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Riola Spring}} and the area that surrounds it. One can be found by standing on a tree stump at the base of the waterfall before passing through and ring before it disappears. Another can be found by removing the correct {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}} from the three trees on the island of the unnamed lake to the east. Finally a third can be found by placing the small metal rock chained to the partly submerged westernmost pillar, in the pillars centre.
