{{Term/Store|Don Gero's Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> It resembles the image of Don Gero, the conductor of the [[Frog Choir]].<ref>{{Cite|You got Don Gero's Mask! It looks just like Don Gero, conductor of the frog choir!|N/A|MM}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's {{Color|MM Red|Don Gero's Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>That mask has a very operatic feel about it.</p>}}

[[File:MM3D Hungry Goron Notebook Portrait.png|left|The Hungry Goron wearing Don Gero's Mask, found in the Bombers' Notebook from {{MM3D|-}}]]
The [[mask]] is given to [[Link]] by a hungry [[Goron]] in the [[Mountain Village]]. To acquire the mask, Link must light all the [[torch]]es lining the wall of the [[Goron Shrine]], using a [[Deku Stick]] and before defeating [[Goht]]. Alternatively, Link can light them at once by shooting a [[Fire Arrow]] to the torch at the center of the chandelier of hanging pots on the ceiling. Doing either action will cause said chandelier to spin. [[Goron Link]] must then roll off of the ramp on the top floor to collide with the pots until some [[Rock Sirloin]] falls to the floor. Link must then take the sirloin to the Mountain Village and give it to the Goron standing on a ledge.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa-oh-oh!!! Up here! Up here! I am sorry to bother you from such a high place... But I am very hungry, and I have gotten stuck here. Ooooh...It is so cold... I am so very hungry. I don't think I'm going to make it... I just want to eat once more before I die up here. Something tough and hard to chew... I cannot forget that flavor...|Goron|MM}}</ref> He will thank Link with Don Gero's Mask.<ref>{{Cite|Th-That's it! It's so good that I dream about it... Dodongo Cavern's finest quality rock sirloin! This is my absolute favorite! Blessed be this meal! [...] Oh, I almost forgot. Please accept this as a token of gratitude... It was crafted so well that no one would even think twice if you were to direct the frog choir.|Goron|MM}}</ref>
When worn, the mask allows Link to communicate with [[frog]]s around [[Termina]] to inform them that the [[Frog Choir]] is reuniting to sing again. Although Link can speak to them at any time, the reunion can only take place after Link defeats Goht and spring returns to [[Snowhead]]. Reuniting all five frogs to the choir will earn Link a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|The conducting was spectacular. And all of our members rose to the occasion! This is how deeply we were moved by your spectacular conducting...|Frog|MM}}</ref>
