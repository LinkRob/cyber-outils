The {{Term/Store|Faron Woods Entry||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Faron Woods Entry|Map|SSHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|SS|Faron Woods Entry}} is the entry point to the {{Term|SS|Faron Woods|link}}, accessed from {{Term|SS|Behind the Temple|link}} at the {{Term|SS|Sealed Grounds|link}}. The {{Term|SS|Faron Woods Entry}} is a winding path that leads deeper {{Term|SS|In the Woods|link}} to the east. During {{Term|SS|Link|link}}'s first visit, he finds the {{Term|SS|Kikwi|link}} {{Term|SS|Machi|link}} under attack from monsters.

A shortcut to and from the {{Term|SS|Faron Woods Entry}} can be made by rolling a large log from {{Term|SS|In the Woods}} down into the {{Term|SS|Faron Woods Entry}}.
