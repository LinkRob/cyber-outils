{{Term/Store|Sherfin's Secret Hot Spring||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Sherfin's Secret Hot Spring'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Sherfin's Secret Hot Spring}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hot Spring|link}} found on {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} between the {{Term|BotW|Hebra North Summit|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hebra West Summit|link}}. While in the water {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s body will be kept at a comfortable {{Term|BotW|Temperature|link}} and his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} will replenish at a rate of one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} every two seconds until full. There is a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} near the spring's southeastern bank.

The cliff to the north of {{Term|BotW|Sherfin's Secret Hot Spring}} holds three {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit|link}} along its base.
