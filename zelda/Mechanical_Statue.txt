{{Term/Store|Mechanical Statue|Mechanical Statues|Series, TP|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 345}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TPHD, HW}}

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TPHD|Mechanical Statue}} appear in the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time (Dungeon)|link}}. One of the {{Plural|TPHD|Mechanical Statue}} must be controlled by {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} with the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod|link}} and guided back to its original position in the entrance room in order to reach the lair of {{Term|TPHD|Armogohma|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TPHD |publisher= Prima |page= 357}}</ref> When under the control of the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod}}, a {{Term|TPHD|Mechanical Statue}} can swing its hammer in sync with {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s movements to destroy {{Plural|TPHD|Blade Trap|link}}, {{Plural|TPHD|Spiketrap|link}} and {{Plural|TPHD|Beamos|link}} as well as operative cages that require a {{Term|TPHD|Switch|link}} to open and close them.
