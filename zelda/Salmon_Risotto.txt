{{Term/Store|Salmon Risotto|Salmon Risottos|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Salmon Risotto}}<br/>The rice used in this rich risotto permeates the light flavor of the salmon.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Salmon Risotto can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Hearty Salmon]], [[Hylian Rice]], [[Goat Butter]], and [[Rock Salt]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The only way to make regular Salmon Risotto is by adding a fifth ingredient that grants an effect other than hearty. Eating these meals will heal some of the {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. This risotto is known the be rich, as the rice permeates the light flavor of the salmon.<ref name="Inventory"/>

In [[Ivee's Diary|her diary]], {{Term|BotW|Ivee|link}} details a recipe for [[Mushroom Risotto]]. However, at the end, she suggests substituting the {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}} used with Hearty Salmon for a "fish-lover's dish".<ref>{{Cite|Note: Use a {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}} instead of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian shrooms|link}} for a fish-lover's dish.|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

Link can only make one variation of Salmon Risotto:

* '''Hearty Salmon Risotto''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Salmon Risotto}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The rice used in this rich risotto permeates the light flavor of the salmon.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using the default recipe. When these meals are consumed, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>
