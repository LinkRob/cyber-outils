{{Term/Store|Hyrule Castle Garden||Series, TMC}} is an area in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden}} is located within the walls of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} in northern {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. It connects to one other area: {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}} to the south.

{{Plural|TMC|Vassal|link}} patrol the area later in the game, making it difficult for {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to traverse the area. The {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden|display=Garden}} is also the location of the hidden entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}}.

A {{Term|TMC|Golden Rope|link}} appears here after a [[Kinstone Fusion]]. One of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Grimblade|link}}, lives in the bottom right hand corner of the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden|display=Garden}} and teaches {{Term|TMC|Link}} the {{Term|TMC|Sword Beam|link}}.

After fusing {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}, two fountains close to the entrance will dry up, revealing hidden stairways.
