{{Term/Store|''Swordsman Newsletter #7''||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Swordsman Newsletter #7|Swordsman Newsletter 7|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}} becomes available for purchase after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Postman|link}}, inspiring {{Term|TMC|Stamp|link}} to enlist {{Term|TMC|Marcy|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}} in creating the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter}} series.<ref>{{Cite|I really wish I could do more to help people... Wait! I've got it! I'll ask {{Color|TMC Green|Swiftblade}} to start up a newsletter for adventurers! That'll be a great way to provide a useful service for everyone... Hm. It's going to be a lot of work, though. I should get {{Color|TMC Green|Marcy}} to help me.|Stamp|TMC}}</ref>

The {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter 7}}, the seventh issue, can be purchased for 200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains at least seven {{Plural|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}}.

==Transcript==
:For Real Swordsmen Only
You know the Roll Attack, right? Yeah, the one you do midroll! I bet Grayblade told you to attack just as you finish the roll, right? Well, actually, you can do it just as you start the roll, too! But the timing is very tricky! You can do it! Hone your skill! The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step! Or...something!

:Teach us, Teacher!
Camouflaged shell-shockers... These fiends festoon themselves with grass and rocks to deceive your eyes. But you can remove their camo without trying to pick them up, you know! Hit them with that {{Color|TMC Red|Cane of Pacci}}! Suck them with the {{Color|TMC Red|Gust Jar}}! How do you like that!? Now you're a camo-buster!

Those short-fused Bob-ombs... I told you about them in issue 3, but here's another thing... Apparently they drop {{Color|TMC Red|bombs}} at a higher rate than other enemies! Or so it seems... But it's just a rumor...

The next one is our last issue! After that, it'll be time for me to put down my pen. As much as I like writing, I'm a teacher at heart. But I know you'll miss me when I'm gone. I'm like a star! A superstar! Issue 8, the last one, is coming soon! Hope you're looking forward to it! Until next time, warriors!!!!
