{{Term/Store|Upland Zorana||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|'''Upland Zorana'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}<ref>{{Cite|'''Upland Zorana'''|Map|TotK}}</ref>.

==Features and overview==
=== {{BotW|-}} ===
In {{BotW|-}}, Upland Zorana is a large mountain chain surrounding the outer perimeter of [[Zora's Domain#Breath of the Wild|Zora's Domain]]. Several lakes and waterfalls can be found scattered throughout this region, including [[Toto Lake]], [[Veiled Falls]], [[Lulu Lake]], and [[Mikau Lake]]. Toto Lake serves as the source of Akkala Lake, as the two areas are connected via a waterfall. Many [[Lizalfos]] and [[Moblins]] can be found throughout Upland Zorana. 

=== {{TotK|-}} ===
In {{TotK|-}}, the Upland Zorana [[Skyview Tower]] can be found atop the western portion of the mountain range. Some new features have been added to the area, such as the construction of [[Mipha Court]] on the eastern section near [[Shatterback Point]].
