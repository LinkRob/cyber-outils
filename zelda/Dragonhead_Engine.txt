The {{Term/Store|Dragonhead Engine||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dragonhead Engine|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Dragonhead Engine}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of two {{Plural|ST|Pirate Necklace|link}}, one {{Term|ST|Mystic Jade|link}} and 12 {{Plural|ST|Demon Fossil|link}}. Its design resembles a Chinese dragon seen in {{Wp|Dragon dance}} parades, with steam emitting from its nostrils. Its description reads "This train was created in the far past and seems to harbor a great spirit."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Dragonhead Engine}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Dragonhead Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts. Using the {{Term|ST|Dragonhead Engine}} also changes the {{Term|ST|Whistle|link}}'s sound to a hiss-like roar.
