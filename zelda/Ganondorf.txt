{{Term/Store|Ganondorf|Ganondorfs|Series, ALttP, OoT, OoT3D, TWW, TWWHD, FSA, TP, TPHD, SSBM, SSBB, HW, HWL, HWDE, SSBfN3DS, SSBfWU, SSBU, CoH}}, also known as the '''Evil King''', '''King of the [[Gerudo]]''', or '''Demon King''', is a powerful Gerudo warlock, and, as his name clearly suggests, is none other than '''[[Ganon]]''', the main antagonist of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]], as a human rather than the ferocious boar-like beast. While he technically debuted in the original {{TLoZ}} as his actual monster form'','' Ganondorf himself made his first appearance in {{OoT}}, but was first mentioned in {{ALttP}}. He has since been present or referred to in some games, though he was absent from the mainline titles after {{TP}} until ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''. Some installments or other media such as {{TWW}} use 'Ganon' as a nickname for the character.<ref name="name">{{Cite Book|quote=Though "Ganon" is also a nickname for Ganondorf, here it refers to the beastlike form adopted by the thief following his transformation. The Demon King resembles a giant boar.|book=HH|page=90}}</ref> Regardless of his own distinctive forms as seen in ''Tears of the Kingdom'', as Ganondorf is indeed Ganon himself using different aspects of his [[Triforce of Power]] as an alter-ego, he is the arch-nemesis of [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]], and the [[Final Boss]] in the games which he appears in. Ganondorf is playable in spin-off games such as ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'' or the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]].

Notably, as Ganon is revealed to be the endless reincarnation of [[Demise]]'s hatred from {{SS}}, and that villain himself is a humanoid, Ganondorf much more closely resembles him.

==Backstory==
[[File:TPHD Ganondorf Artwork.png|left|thumb|Artwork of Ganondorf from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD|Twilight Princess HD]]'']]
Despite the series' central antagonist as a whole debuted in the titular 1986 [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] title {{TLoZ}} as a simple monster (alongside [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]]) and Ganondorf making his first on-screen appearance in the 1998 [[Nintendo 64]] installment {{OoT}}, {{aLttP}} released on the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] seven years prior is the first mainline game in the franchise revealing that Ganon actually has a human form and therefore wasn't always the giant pig-like [[demon]], with his full name being '''Ganondorf Dragmire'''. Ganondorf is also the leader of a group of thieves called [[Gerudo|Gerudos]]. With the help of his followers, as Ganon, he became the first to enter the [[Sacred Realm]] in ages. He claimed the [[Triforce]], turning the Sacred Realm into the [[Dark World]] and himself into a vastly powerful being.<ref name="ALttP Wish">{{Cite|Ganon's wish was to conquer the world. That wish changed the Golden Land to the Dark World.|Essence of the Triforce|ALttP}}</ref> However, he was trapped within the now-corrupted Sacred Realm by the combined efforts of the [[Knights of Hyrule]] and the [[Sage]]s.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Seven Sages of that era sealed the realm in a ferocious battle known as the Imprisoning War. The clan of knights tasked with guarding the sages was nearly wiped out in the process.|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=24 }}</ref> In {{aLttP|-}}, during the final battle, Ganon reveals that the dark wizard [[Agahnim]] is another alter ego of himself, though this particular villain never appears again in later installments, as Link destroyed him completely in the second battle inside [[Ganon's Tower]] and it is likely Ganondorf replaced the [[Wizzrobe]]-like magician since.

In {{OoT|-}}, the first incarnation of Ganondorf was born a powerful Gerudo sorcerer,<ref name="ZeldaEncyclopedia">{{Cite Book|quote= Chronologically, Ocarina of Time is when Ganondorf first appears, as he was not yet born during the events of Four Swords, The Minish Cap and Skyward Sword. |book=[[The Legend of Zelda: Encyclopedia]] |publisher=Dark Horse Books |date=June 2018 |page= 20}}</ref> one of the human [[races]] of Hyrule. The Gerudo twin witches [[Twinrova|Koume and Kotake]] were his surrogate mothers.<ref name="Koume">{{Cite|Twinrova Koume: Ganondorf's surrogate mother. Sorceress of Flame. She seems vulnerable to high temperature.|Navi|OoT}}</ref> The Gerudo are a race of all-female warriors and thieves, to whom only one male is born every century. Because of this, the male is by birthright the King of the Gerudo. However, not all Gerudo are evil like himself; [[Nabooru]], the second-in-command (and eventually [[Sage|Sage of Spirit]]), mentions that she is not following Ganondorf because he stole from women and children and even murdered people.<ref>{{Cite|With his followers, he stole from women and children, and he even killed people! However, he does not obtain the full Triforce; he is left with only the [[Triforce of Power]], which he uses to great effect in his conquest of Hyrule. 

In {{TWW}}, Ganondorf's character is given greater depth and a clearer motive for his villainy. He states that his desire to conquer Hyrule stems from the arduous life he and his people were subjected to living in the harsh [[Gerudo Desert]], a wasteland plagued by sandstorms and an unstable climate, which he compares unfavorably to the verdant landscape of [[Hyrule Field]] and the amenable life lived by the [[Hylian]]s.<ref>{{Cite|My country lay within a vast desert. ==Characteristics==
===Personality===
[[File:HW Ganondorf Screenshot.png|left|thumb|242x242px|A close-up of Ganondorf's face in ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'', showing his wicked disposition]]
Being the archvillain [[Ganon]] simply in human form, Ganondorf is an extremely powerful Gerudo warlock who commonly opposes [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] within the series. As such, he is a man of pure evil and a cruel, ruthless warlord whose goal is to usurp the [[Hyrule|Kingdom of Hyrule]]. Ganondorf's only ambition in life is to obtain the [[Triforce]] and dominate the world using the abilities imbued by them. He has many characteristics of a megalomaniac. Despite his undeniable lust for power, Ganondorf is cunning and possesses high intelligence. He is an adept tactician, as well as a master of manipulation and deceit. It is not unusual for Ganondorf to pull strings from the shadows, playing off multiple characters against each other throughout the events of a story (as he did in {{ALttP|-}}, {{FSA|-}}, and {{TP|-}}), only to be later revealed as the orchestrator, once his pawns have served their purpose. While arrogant, he is also pragmatic and opportunistic befitting his intelligent nature; during his first attempt at conquest in Hyrule he decides upon subterfuge once noticing the Secret Stones and their power, realizing that brute force alone won't be enough to overcome the royal family of Hyrule.

Ganondorf usually presents himself with the façade of a cynical yet calm and civil man, though with deep malice and cunning behind his eyes. Once his mask drops, he is known to quickly become enraged and dangerous, ensuing in a murderous rampage. Power is all that matters to Ganondorf, and morality is an afterthought in his desire for conquest. In his conquest of the [[Sacred Realm]], once he reached the [[Temple of Light]], he murdered all followers who had aided him without a second thought, so that he could claim the [[Triforce]] uncontested, indicating he sees followers and companions as disposable once they are no longer useful. This tendency to view companions as disposable was also implied to an extent with his ''Wilds'' counterpart where he had no qualms turning his newly-acquired Demon Army against even his fellow Gerudo after becoming the Demon King, with the last free Gerudo village falling to him prior to Rauru's last stand against him.<ref name="Last free Gerudo Village fallen" /> He is also culturally inclined, having high taste in clothing, jewelry, and being well mannered, while also being able to play a pipe organ with great skill, as seen in {{OoT|-}} where he plays his own theme throughout Link's attack in his [[Ganon's Castle|castle]]. Ganondorf also possesses an arrogant sense of entitlement; he believes himself to be the only one worthy of ruling the kingdom of Hyrule. His pride is also shown when fighting enemies: despite being more than capable of defeating most enemies in combat, Ganondorf instead relies on his minions to defeat them like most other primary [[Nintendo]] antagonists such as [[Bowser]]. Apparently, he chooses to only fight opponents he deems worthy, such as Link, and, rarely, Princess Zelda.

He resents defiance from anybody, but seems to grudgingly respect Link's willingness to stand up to him in {{OoT|-}},<ref>{{Cite|So, you think you can protect them from me... You've got guts, kid.|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref> as well as Link's tenacity to brave the Forsaken Fortress and reach him in {{TWW|-}}, respectively.<ref>{{Cite|It's been a while, boy. You have done well to sneak into my fortress and wriggle your way all the way up here. I suppose the least I can do is commend you for your reckless courage.|Ganondorf|TWW}}</ref>

Ganondorf has sadistic tendencies and is also implied to hold mental instability in two instances. The first was shortly after King [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]] wished for Hyrule and Ganondorf to be flooded completely where he proceeded to engage in hysterical laughter as the land was being flooded permanently during the Adult Timeline, and the other time was when he proceeded to engage in similar hysterical laughter when witnessing Princess Zelda try to scramble to aid Queen Sonia shortly after he mortally wounded the latter just before becoming the Demon King.

At least in regards to his ''Wilds'' incarnation, owing to his warlord background, Ganondorf was shown to have a lust for direct combat, particularly proving his own might by foregoing any defensive equipment such as shields. It's also implied this lust for combat was part of the reason he hated the Zonai regarding their technology. This lust for combat was made especially apparent just before transforming into his true Demon King form against the Hero of the Wild, where he expressed some ecstasy at how Link was managing to give him a challenging fight even before he used all his power, and beforehand when expressing some excitement upon being sealed away at the prospect of Link potentially rivaling him when Rauru told him his future fate. Upon being defeated by Link despite being enhanced by the Secret Stone, Ganondorf, unwilling to submit to a mere mortal, swallowed the stone to [[Draconification|draconify]] himself, knowing full well it would mean sacrificing everything, including his body and mind, if only to bring destruction to his enemies and the world.

===Abilities===
''See also: [[Ganon]]''

Even before gaining the [[Triforce of Power]], Ganondorf was arguably one of the most powerful men in the entire land of Hyrule, largely due to his political position as the King of the [[Gerudo]].
[[File:TWW Princess Zelda slapped out by Ganondorf.gif|thumb|281x281px|Ganondorf violently knocking Princess Zelda unconsciousness during the final battle of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD|The Wind Waker HD]]'']]
Ganondorf was also an immensely powerful sorcerer with vast mystical resources. Additionally, Ganondorf has proven himself to be a skilled swordsman, as seen in the final battles between him and [[Link]] in {{TWW|-}} and {{TP|-}}. He is also very physically strong, explained by his extremely tall height and bulky body. In [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']], in memories #9 "Sonia is Caught by Treachery", Sonia was killed by Ganondorf by a single punch in her back. He was also implied to have been strong enough to lift and place boulders around Dodongo's Cavern's entrance in an attempt to force Darunia to surrender the Goron's Ruby.<ref name="Ganondorf's role in Goron troubles" /> In {{TWW|-}}, he is capable of easily knocking [[Princess Zelda]] out with a single slap to her face in retaliation by being hit from her [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]] (which became infamous among fans).

Once Ganondorf gained the Triforce of Power, both his physical and mystical abilities were augmented by the essence of the Goddess [[Din (Goddess)|Din]] to god-like proportions - his power unrivaled by any mortal, for he ceased to be mortal in that moment. Becoming a monstrously powerful demon king, Ganondorf became utterly unstoppable, conquering Hyrule with ease. He also implies that the Triforce of Power was sufficient enough to allow him to weaken any seals placed on him over time.<ref>{{Cite|YOU... CURSE YOU...ZELDA! CURSE YOU...SAGES!! CURSE YOU...LINK! Someday... When this seal is broken.... That is when I will exterminate your descendants!! As long as the Triforce of Power is in my hand....|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref> As seen in the events of {{TP|-}}, the Triforce of Power also grants Ganondorf near-invulnerability; using its power, he is capable of enduring mortal wounds (such as having his chest pierced by a sword during his attempted [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|execution]]), and only the likes of the most powerful sacred weapons (such as the [[Master Sword]]) appear to be able to kill him. His near-invulnerability was also alluded to in {{TWW|-}}, where Ganondorf took a full blast from the sky spirit [[Valoo]]'s flame breath and survived unscathed, with Valoo himself acknowledging afterwards that he can't be destroyed by "wrath and fire" alone.<ref>{{Cite|Still...it is too soon for us to relax. Ganon cannot be destroyed by such simple means as wrath and fire.|Valoo|TWW}}</ref> He was also capable of superhuman strength after gaining the Triforce of Power, or at least having it amplified, with his blows being capable of demolishing portions of the floor that weren't directly connected to the floor below, as well as breaking chains restraining him with minimum effort. Likewise, he was also capable of destroying even disembodied spirits with his bare hands, such as his murder of the Sage of Water after his failed execution. 

Even without access to his full capabilities in {{TWW|-}}, Ganondorf was able to cast a curse to keep the Great Sea in an endless stormy night. As seen in {{TP|-}}, the Triforce of Power grants Ganondorf many more powers such as size manipulation and terrain alteration: while Ganondorf is not personally shown using many of these powers, he temporarily gifts the Usurper King [[Zant]] with some of them.<ref>{{Cite|I shall house my power in you... If there is anything you desire, then I shall desire it, too.|Ganondorf|TP}}</ref> Ganondorf also has an unnaturally long lifespan, having lived through ages of Hyrulean chronology.

Ganondorf's dark magic, and possibly his position as the Demon King, grant him the power to create his armies of monsters and beasts.

His power, with or without the Triforce, appears to grow over time. The sages in Arbiter's Grounds, when discussing his evil power, mention it being pure malice and hatred.<ref>{{Cite|His abiding hatred and lust for power turned to purest malice...|Sage|TP}}</ref> As [[Calamity Ganon]], his very essence is known as {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}, possibly meaning that his demonic magic and power is fueled as much by his own hatred as the Triforce, and his capacity for hatred is the only true limit on how powerful he can grow.<ref>{{Cite|The demon king was born into this kingdom, but his transformation into Malice created the horror you see now.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

The reincarnated Ganondorf, during the events of {{FSA|-}}, was largely similar to the original, although it is implied that he was weaker than the original, to the extent that he lacked the ability to cover Hyrule in darkness by the time he had been defeated and sealed in the Four Sword.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=[Ganon] was heard to lament, "The power I have attained from the people of Hyrule is not yet enough." From Ganon's words, we can surmise that he did not have the power he needed to cover Hyrule in darkness.|book=HH|page=121}}</ref>

In {{TotK}}, Ganondorf is depicted at his most formidable, even compared to his previous appearances in the whole {{TLoZ|Series}}. Long before the events of the game, his power has increased beyond limits after he gained one of the [[Secret Stone|Secret Stones]], as demonstrated when he summoned his hordes of demons to take over [[Hyrule]]. With his minions, Ganondorf successfully subjugated the free peoples one after the other, until [[King Rauru]] and all the [[Sage|Sages]] finally faced him beneath [[Hyrule Castle]]. Even so, their combined strength was no match for the Demon King, and only Rauru's sacrifice ultimately saved his peers and Hyrule from complete destruction. {{HWAoI}} further elaborated that he managed to beat them despite being in an area with light energy that would have weakened him. Even prior to acquiring the Secret Stones, however, it's implied that he possessed powerful enough magic to create realistic phantoms, as evidenced by his usage of a doppelganger of Zelda in a feint to gain Sonia's Secret Stone, with Ganondorf implying in {{HWAoI}} that he was capable of creating more than one at a time if such was necessary.

When Ganondorf finally broke free from the seal 10,000 years later, his power was so great that he effortlessly shattered the [[Decayed Master Sword|Master Sword]] and nearly killed [[Link]] himself. Although still weakened by his millennial imprisonment, the Demon King proved to be a nuisance for Hyrule as he triggered several cataclyms that affected every major region of the kingdom. He was also omniscient, as demonstrated when he summoned a [[Phantom Ganon|puppet]] with the appearance of [[Princess Zelda]] to trick Link and the inhabitants of the kingdom. This foul surprise also indirectly caused the death of [[Queen Sonia]], and proved decisive in allowing Ganondorf to gain a virtually unlimited power.

{{Quote|Too Weak!|Ganondorf's one of the two comments when blocking arrows or perfect dodging Link's melee attack.}}{{Quote|Too Obvious!|Ganondorf's other one of the two comments when evading or blocking arrows.}}
Last but not least, it is no secret that Ganondorf is an accomplished [[Gloom Sword|swordsman]]. But in {{TotK|-}}, he proves to be even more redoubtable when using a [[Gloom Spear|spear]], a [[Gloom Club|club]] and even a [[Demon King's Bow|bow]]. Summoning his weapons from the [[gloom]] and constantly switching from one to another, his surprisingly swift attacks deal massive damage, with gloom-based projectiles literally taking away Link's maximum health permanently from of the sheer evil fueling his weapons, and he also has many attacks the hero can perform, such as a [[Perfect Dodging|perfect dodge]], and is even capable of blocking arrows shots (by swiping his arm on impact if the arrows are fired towards his head twice in a row, when Link isn't airborne) - these feats unseen in the series prior.
[[File:OoT3D Ganon Model.png|left|thumb|232x232px|Ganon as he appears in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'']]
As Ganon, his true form, Ganondorf becomes even more powerful, due to himself as a ferocious porcine-like [[demon]] being the result of using the Triforce of Power at its full capabilities and is filled with sheer hatred, therefore losing his sanity in most (but not all) cases. Though normally resembling a bulky anthropomorphic [[boar]] (or [[Moblin]] in his early design), a few games such as {{TP|-}} or ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'' depict the monster on fours. His raw power and near-invulnerability is depicted more clearly, as showcased in {{aLttP|-}} and {{OoT|-}}. Due to his size, Ganon can crumple the ground with his stomps, inflict heavy damage onto Link especially without [[defense]] upgrades, and can survive otherwise fatal blows by the Master Sword even if the sacred blade itself [[Master Sword Lv2|is]] [[Master Sword Lv3|upgraded]]. However, despite his unimaginable strength, the Demon King is not invincible, as he does have certain weaknesses, such as being struck by the Master Sword or [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]] at critical points, and in the case of {{aLttP|-}} and {{TLoZ}}, [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]] are capable of ultimately destroying the beast entirely into ashes and in the latter game, even losing the Triforce of Power afterwards for Link to obtain for himself, making these very [[Arrow|Arrows]] technically the most dangerous threat for the archvillain in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]]. In ''Hyrule Warriors'', the [[Triforce]] in its completed form can also seal or even kill Ganon if it were to be used against him. The Dark Beast can revert himself down to Ganondorf at will or in some cases, when he is defeated or if the Triforce of Power was extracted by force. Although Ganondorf has died by Link's Master Sword in a few games so far, no installment in the franchise has yet shown himself as Ganon being eliminated by the Blade of Evil's Bane (despite the [[Linked Game|linked ''Oracle'' game]] had the beast slain by a [[Sword]] due to himself not being properly revived).

=== Pre-{{BotW|-}}===
At the founding of Hyrule's {{BotW}} iteration, which possibly may have followed after the destruction of a previous Hyrule,<ref name="Fujibayashi"/> an incarnation of Ganondorf was born into the Gerudo tribe as leader. He was also considered a hero among his people, and eventually managed to use this to demand outright conquering more fertile lands while silencing those who had more peaceful ideas. He attempted to conquer [[Hyrule]] by sheer force. He ordered for a planned feint against Hyrule Castle via a [[Molduga]] stampede while he had a small Gerudo band led by [[Ardi]] invade Hyrule Castle. When the latter expressed hesitance due to recognizing not all of the Gerudo would have approved of the overt attack, Ganondorf implicitly threatened to punish her for insubordination, forcing her to carry out his plan. However, his plans were thwarted by the [[Secret Stone|Secret Stones]] in possession of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family]]. Upon witnessing their incredible power during the aforementioned Molduga swarm, the Gerudo changed his strategy and understood that he would have to fool his enemies. As part of this change in strategy, he also had his attendants, Koume and Kotake, ambush Ardi's unit and slay them in order to appear as if the Gerudo who attacked were a renegade unit to buy Rauru's trust. However, Ardi ultimately was spared due to the actions of both [[Bud|a construct]] and a Korok. Despite the failure, he nevertheless set it aside due to seeing it as his chance to conquer Hyrule from within, and also had an interest in corrupting constructs. He went to [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] with a Gerudo delegation to pledge allegiance to [[King Rauru]], a necessary humiliation in an attempt to gain his trust and become closer to the Royal Family. During the meeting, Ganondorf humbly expressed his regrets about all the peace offers that had been rejected or ignored by his people, and complimented Rauru's union with the Queen while subtly alluding to the Zonai's near-extinction. The King praised Ganondorf for his change of heart before letting him go at the end of the audience. While the King of Hyrule effectively secured peace in the ongoing war between the Gerudo and the [[Hylian|Hylians]], Ganondorf had other plans. Unbeknownst to the Royal Family, he realized that [[Queen Sonia]] was also in possession of a Secret Stone, and concocted a plan to steal it from her. He also noticed a third individual, Princess Zelda (actually their future descendant who was time-displaced due to the actions of his future self) among them and took her into account. Zelda also suspected, partly due to Ganondorf's name sounding uncomfortably similar to the Calamity Ganon event in her future, that he held dark ambitions in his heart and tried to warn Rauru, though Rauru assured her he was aware and intended to keep eye on him. During this time, he also proceeded to create a copy of Zelda via one of his phantoms, even deciding to create multiple after one such copy was devoured by [[Forbidden Construct|a certain infamous construct from the ancient past]]. He also had Koume and Kotake keep watch over the construct mostly to see its capabilities, and later to lure Rauru into a trap using the construct's destructive actions as bait.

==={{OoT|-}} (Himekawa)===
Ganondorf's role was overall the same as in the game. However, his first appearance had him eavesdropping on Link and Zelda in the Hyrule Castle Courtyard, as well as, while he apologized for his eavesdropping, it implied that he may have sent the Gerudo after Zelda while the latter was disguised in Hyrule Castle Town. Ganondorf later appeared after Link got the Zora's Sapphire, where he attempted to corner Zelda and Impa at the burning castle while stating that Zelda "will experience eternal sleep with her father" in it, which implied that he had, by that point, murdered her father. He also ended up stealing Link's Fairy Ocarina, under the mistaken impression that it was the Ocarina of Time. Ganondorf did not realize that mistake until seven years had passed, upon which he crushed the ocarina in his hand. Ganondorf was also responsible for corrupting [[Volvagia]] during the intervening seven years, ultimately forcing Link, Volvagia's former owner and friend, to slay it to save the Gorons, an action that ultimately haunted Link afterwards.

Ganondorf also had Sheik serve him. When Ingo failed to ensure that Epona was supplied to him, Ganondorf initially intended to have him executed for his failure, although he relented after Koume and Kotake instead suggested that they brainwash him. Ganondorf eventually imprisoned Zelda inside a Crystal, and took her to his castle. During the inevitable fight with Link, Ganondorf used a sword with magical powers to overpower him, and sought to finish him off with his ultimate spell. Link was able to use the Spin Attack to deflect the spell back at Ganondorf, which stunned him long enough for Link to cut him down. A wounded Ganondorf then attempted to bring the castle down on Link and Zelda. When that failed, Ganondorf turned into Ganon. Ganon was able to knock the Master Sword out of Link's hand, but Zelda was able to throw it back to the hero. Link promptly cut off Ganon's Tail while declaring that he is Ganon's opponent with determination, brought him down to his knees with a vertical slash, and thrust his sword into the beast's face. Invoking the power of the Sages, Zelda was able to seal Ganondorf away.

==={{TP|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{TP|Himekawa}}, {{Term|TP|Ganondorf}} largely has the same role, although the events of his {{Term|TP|Failed Execution of Ganondorf|link|display=Execution}} are expanded slightly; {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda|link}} glimpses of a recording of it. The manga also implies that he was aware of the events of the Adult Timeline despite never actually getting a chance to enact its events. He also had a slight change in motivations from the game as a result of both the planned speech being retained and his knowledge of the actions of his future self: He sought to break the curse tying him, Zelda, and Link together in his favor, or barring that, ensure the curse continued long enough for his future reincarnation to succeed.

Zelda tries to flee from him, but he immobilizes her with his magic. However, is defeated by Link before he can capture Zelda. He tries to go after Cia in "[[Darkness Falls]]", the final scenario of [[Cia's Tale]], and has the advantage in the beginning in spite of his incomplete form, to the point he makes [[Wizzro]] voluntarily join him. Eventually, Lana joins Cia as the latter gets desperate and Ganondorf is pushed back. 

Ganondorf does not return until Cia is defeated, when the seal in the Master Sword's chamber finally breaks as a result of the sword being removed, thus freeing his last fragment and reviving him again. For three Scenarios, Ganondorf becomes playable as he creates and leads an army to take the Triforce pieces. He summons [[Zant]] and [[Ghirahim]] to lead under his command, threatening to return them to their imprisonment unless they serve him, and takes over the monster stronghold in [[Gerudo Desert (Hyrule Warriors)|Gerudo Desert]]. He invades the [[Valley of Seers]], where he manages to defeat [[Lana]] and take the Triforce of Power that had been bestowed to her by Cia. He then takes Link's and Zelda's Triforce pieces in [[Hyrule Field (Stage)|Hyrule Field]], and takes over the [[Hyrule Castle|castle]]. After the Hyrulean Forces defeat the stronghold at the desert, they return to [[Ganon's Tower (Stage)|Ganon's Tower]] to defeat Ganondorf. During this time, he was engaging in an unspeakable ritual to unlock his full power, and due to the advance of the Hyrulean forces, has two Argoroks delay their progress. He ultimately managed to awaken his full strength, which according to him was such that neither the Hero nor even the Goddess could surpass him, and proceeded to personally enter the fray afterwards. However, his power was drained after the Hyrulean Forces managed to capture Hyrule Castle while he was out fighting. He is defeated by Link, but uses the Triforce to transform into Ganon. He is however again defeated with the [[Light Arrow]]s that Zelda bestows to Link, which returns the Triforce pieces to Link, Zelda and Lana. They use the Triforce pieces to imprison Ganon's spirit again.

==Ganon's Swords==
{{see also|Sword of the Six Sages}}
[[File:Ganon's Sword TWW.png|left|35px]]
Not counting the golden swords wielded by Ganon in {{OoT}}, Ganondorf is seen wielding a pair of swords again in {{TWW|-}} during the final battle, which is what he uses as his offensive strategy. They are engraved with the names "Kotake" and "Koume", his surrogate mothers from {{OoT|-}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book=E |publisher= |page=21 }}</ref> Later on, in {{TP|-}}, Ganondorf wields the [[Sword of the Six Sages]], a weapon that the [[Sages]] used in [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|their failed attempt to execute him]]. The Sword of the Six Sages serves as Ganondorf's weapon in the final battle against Link. In {{TotK}}, the Demon King Ganondorf wields an ornate katana with a dark blade, the [[Gloom Sword]], which again, he uses in his final battle against Link.

Non-canon appearances of a sword-wielding Ganondorf include one of Ganondorf's victory poses and Smash Attacks in the {{SSBS|-}} and the [[SpaceWorld 2000 GameCube Tech Demo|SpaceWorld 2000 demo]], which included a Sword fight between Link and Ganondorf. This Sword was much bulkier than the swords he uses in the main series, and had a rounded tip. Ganondorf also uses a Sword in his battle with Link in the {{OoT|-}} manga. This sword is capable of having Ganondorf's evil magic channeled through it. He also wields two [[Great Swords]] in {{HW|-}}, the Swords of Despair. They can be upgraded to the Swords of Darkness and Swords of Demise.
