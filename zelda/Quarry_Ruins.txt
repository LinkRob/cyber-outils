The {{Term/Store|Quarry Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Quarry Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Quarry Ruins are located in [[Hyrule Field]], southwest of [[Hyrule Castle]] and [[West Castle Town]]. The Quarry Ruins are a [[Rock|rocky]] terrain with very few [[Tree]]s that were used to supply stones for the Kingdom of [[Hyrule]] prior to the second Great Calamity.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 394}}</ref> The [[Katah Chuki Shrine]] sits in a nook along the southeastern side of the Ruins, beside the wall bordering West Castle Town. A [[Rare Ore Deposit]] can be found under a pile of bombable rocks in a crevice just before the peak of the hill in the north. There is also a ruined village to the southwest, along the path.

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing a bundle of five [[Shock Arrow]]s can be found beneath a stone slab atop a broken wall in the northeast section of the Ruins.

A [[Korok]] can be found by popping a [[Balloon]] hidden in the Tree near the castle wall. Two more are located atop the northern hill: one by completing a circular [[Rock Pattern]], and the other in a [[Natural Hiding Spot]] buried beneath a Rock pile. A fourth Korok can be found hiding in a Natural Hiding Spot atop a raised piece of land around the center of the Ruins. Another Korok is nestled in a corner between two hills to the Quarry's north and rewards a Korok Seed after completing a spiral Rock Pattern puzzle. Upon finding these Koroks, they will gift [[Link]] a [[Korok Seed]].<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
