{{Term/Store|Vaati's Wrath||Series, TMC}} is a {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Vaati's Wrath|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}}
After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} defeats {{Term|TMC|Vaati Transfigured|link}}, he is able to break the curse on {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} and they attempt to escape {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}} as it collapses around them. When they arrive in the courtyard, {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} reappears and transforms into his third and final form, {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}}. He warps {{Term|TMC|Link}} from the courtyard to a dark battlefield.

{{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}} floats around the room, shooting energy projectiles, and making his arms emerge from the ground by plunging them into it. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must fire the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}}'s arms when they emerge to disconnect them from his body, then quickly use one of the nearby {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal|link}} to shrink and enter the discarded arm before {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}} reattaches it. Once inside the arm, {{Term|TMC|Link}} comes across several small eyes; one of these, whose pupil moves, is the arm's core that {{Term|TMC|Link}} must strike down to destroy the arm. He must then repeat the same process with {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}}'s other arm; the second time, however, the inside of the arm will be dark, requiring {{Term|TMC|Link}} to use the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}} to locate the correct eye. 

After both arms are destroyed, {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}}'s attack pattern changes slightly. He continues shooting energy projectiles from his horns with more intensity, but he also charges up blue energy balls from the four eyes on his underside. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must clone himself at the right time to reflect those blue energy balls back at {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}}'s eyes, which will stun him and make him vulnerable to strikes from the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}. Should fewer than four of his eyes be destroyed, {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}} will not be stunned and the eyes will reappear immediately. This process must be repeated until {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath}} is finally destroyed.
