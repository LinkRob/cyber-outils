{{Term|TWWHD|Southern Triangle Island}} is an {{Term|TWWHD|Island|link}} of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}, located on square E4 to the north of {{Term|TWWHD|Southern Fairy Island|link}}. It is part of a trio of triangle-shaped {{Plural|TWWHD|Island}} that in turn form the inner part of a {{Term|TWWHD|Triforce|link}} when lines are drawn on a map connecting each in conjunction with the {{Plural|TWWHD|Island}} that house the {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}}.

The location of {{Term|TWWHD|Southern Triangle Island}} is strategic as a {{Term|TWWHD|Statue|link}} depicting the {{Term|TWWHD|Golden Goddess}} {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru (Goddess)|link}} awaits the placement of {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru's Pearl}}. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} approaches the {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}} while holding {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru's Pearl}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}}'s eyes glows a bluish hue and it prompts him to place the {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru's Pearl|display=Pearl}} in its hands.<ref>{{Cite|Wandering traveler who seeks the guidepost of the goddesses... Place the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|pearl}} you hold here.|Statue|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You placed {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Nayru's Pearl}} in the ancient statue's hands.|N/A|TWWHD}}</ref> Afterwards, it will continue to glow until all three {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl}} have been placed in their respective {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}}'s hands, at which point they will come to life and emit beacons toward the other {{Plural|TWWHD|Triangle Island|link}}. This raises the {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}} from the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

{{Term|TWWHD|Triforce Shard|link}} #6 is located in the waters surrounding {{Term|TWWHD|Southern Triangle Island}}, which can be obtained after finding the {{Term|TWWHD|Triforce Chart|link}} in the {{Term|TWWHD|Savage Labyrinth|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}}.

==Trivia==
* Before all three of the [[Statue]]s are activated by their respective [[Goddess Pearl]], they are shown with unique characteristics that their counterparts lack. The Statue of [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]] possesses mouth, whereas the Statue of [[Din (Goddess)|Din]] and [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]] on Southern Triangle Island and [[Eastern Triangle Island]] possess a nose and ears, respectively. These features correspond to three kinds of Pikmin from the [[pikmin:Pikmin series|''Pikmin'' series]], as each Pikmin has a feature that the others do not: [[pikmin:Blue Pikmin|Blue Pikmin]] have mouths, [[pikmin:Red Pikmin|Red Pikmin]] have noses, and [[pikmin:Yellow Pikmin|Yellow Pikmin]] have ears.
