==Characteristics==
{{Manual|ZG&W|Ghost|They attack Link from behind. You cannot attack them.}}
{{Plural|ZG&W|Ghost (Game & Watch Zelda)}} appear in certain ordinary chambers. They cannot be defeated and do not disappear until the floor's {{Term|ZG&W|Goblin|link}} is defeated. {{Plural|ZG&W|Ghost (Game & Watch Zelda)}} shoot {{Plural|ZG&W|Arrow|link}} at {{Term|ZG&W|Link|link}} from behind; these {{Plural|ZG&W|Arrow}} can be blocked by holding {{Term|ZG&W|Link}}'s {{Term|ZG&W|Shield|link}} behind him, which is done by holding down the attack button.
