The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Ruins||Series, CoH}} are a dungeon in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gerudo Ruins}}|N/A|CoH}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Ruins==
The {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Ruins}} are located near {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Village|link}}. They can be entered at any time, though the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Ruins|display=Ruins}} cannot be explored until the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo|link}} researcher in the entrance has been given three {{Plural|CoH|Gear (Item)|link}}. A {{Term|CoH|Sheikah Stone|link}} sits just outside the entrance.
