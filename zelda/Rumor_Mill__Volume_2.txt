The {{Term/Store|''Rumor Mill: Volume 2''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor Mill: Volume 2|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|Rumor Mill: Volume 2}} is found on a table at {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable|link}}. The author, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}}, provides {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with a hint to finding the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}, saying it's hidden in a forest somewhere.<ref>{{Cite|That's what they say about this special blade! Rumor has it the blade has been hidden away in a forest somewhere...|Rumor Mill: Volume 2|BotW}}</ref> However, she gives the rumor one out of five stars, since she doesn't have any heroes in her lineage and therefore can't use the blade.<ref>{{Cite|My reality is... There isn't any hero in my bloodline, so this sword would just be a big paperweight to me... Traysi's Recommendation: ★☆☆☆☆|Rumor Mill: Volume 2|BotW}}</ref>
