The {{Term/Store|Wooden Cannon||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wooden Cannon|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Wooden Cannon}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of two {{Plural|ST|Wood Heart|link}}, one {{Term|ST|Stalfos Skull|link}} and one {{Term|ST|Ruto Crown|link}}. Its description reads "This wooden cannon resembles a stately tree."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Wooden Cannon}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Wooden Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
