The {{Term/Store|Goron Tunic|Goron Tunics|Series, OoT, OoT3D}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref> also known as the '''Heat-Resistant Tunic''',<ref name="Tunic">{{Cite|Dad went to the Fire Temple all by himself to try to save everyone... Please help, Link! I'll give you this {{Color|OoT3D Red|heat-resistant tunic}}!|Link|OoT3D}}</ref> is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is a red tunic that can only be worn when [[Link]] is as an adult, similar to the [[Zora Armor|Zora Tunic]]. It is made from [[Dodongo]] scales and [[Bomb Flower]] fibers, though it looks identical to his [[Kokiri Tunic|standard tunic]] aside from a red color.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Fashioned from a sporty blend of Dodongo hide and Bomb Flower fibers, the Goron Tunic allowed Link to survive the intense heat generated within Death Mountain Crater.|url= http://web.archive.org/web/20160308195651/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/g.jsp|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia|site=Zelda.com| type=jsp}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:Link Goron Tunic.png|thumb|307x307px|Link wearing the Goron Tunic in {{OoT|-}}]]
In {{OoT}}, the Goron Tunic protects its wearer from extreme heat. It is essential when exploring very hot places such as the [[Death Mountain Crater]] and the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}}. While wearing it, Link will no longer burn from fire-based traps and attacks, and will be able to travel on tiles of lava while having increased resistance to damage, allowing him to take certain shortcuts safely but care still should be taken. He will also gain special resistance from floor spikes and poison water seen in the [[Shadow Temple]]. Like the [[Hylian Shield]], the tunic can be stolen and eaten by a [[Like Like]] if the [[Hero of Time]] is still wearing it when the enemy spits him out.

It can first be received as a gift from [[Link (Goron)|Darunia's son]] after stopping him from rolling around [[Goron City]].<ref name="Tunic" /> If the Goron Tunic is ever lost due to being eaten by a Like Like, replacement tunics can be bought in the [[Goron Shop]] for an expensive 200 [[Rupee]]s each, requiring the [[Adult Wallet]] to purchase.

Note that if the Fire Temple is completed before obtaining the Goron Tunic from [[Darunia]]'s son, he will no longer gift Link this item nor open the door to the Goron Shop, making the Goron Tunic become [[Permanently Missable Items|impossible to obtain]] on that save file. Even if Link enters the shop as a child, he will still be unable to purchase the tunic as the [[Goron]] shopkeeper will refuse to sell Goron Tunics to a child.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSB|-}}===
Link's red costume in this game is based on the Goron Tunic.

==={{SSBM|-}}===
Link's red color palette based on the Goron Tunic returns from {{SSB}}. Young Link has a red costume that is also based on the Goron Tunic, even though he was never able to wear it in his original games.

==={{SCII|-}}===
The Goron Tunic is an alternate costume for Link in this game. 

==={{SSBB|-}}===
Although Link in {{SSBB}} uses his design from {{TP}}, his red color palette is still based on the Goron Tunic from {{OoT}}.

==={{HW|-}}===
The Goron Tunic appears as an unlockable costume in {{HW}}.

==={{SSB4|-}}===
Link's red palette swap in {{SSB4}}, like in {{SSB}}, is again slightly based on the Goron Tunic.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
Because Link in this game wears the [[Champion's Tunic]], his red costume isn't based on the Goron Tunic. However, Young Link returns with his original red palette swap, based on the tunic.
{{Noncanon|end}}

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese releases, the item is called '''Goron Clothes'''.
*Although the Goron Tunic is known to have resistance effects in {{OoT|-}}, it serves no such purpose in other appearances such as the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], ''[[SoulCalibur II|Soul Calibur II]],'' or even ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]''.
*The Goron Tunic is similar to the tunics obtained from the [[Red Ring]] and [[Red Mail]] from {{TLoZ}} and {{aLttP}}, respectively, although it does not directly grant [[Link]] increased [[defense]] unlike its [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] counterparts.
*The French localization names this item '''Fireproof Tunic'''. This is not technically true; Although the Goron Tunic does prevent Link from being burned in {{OoT|-}}, he still takes damage from heat hazards and fire-based attacks, and in ''SoulCalibur II'', he indeed catches on fire when standing on magma on booby-trapped stages. In addition, it was canonically said to be only heat-''resistant''.[[File:OoT Gerudo Warrior Model 2.png|thumb|254x254px|The Gerudo Warrior when Link wears the Goron Tunic in {{OoT|-}}]]
*Due to graphical limitations of the [[Nintendo 64]], the [[Gerudo Warrior]] from [[Gerudo's Fortress]] in {{OoT|-}} shares the same color palette as Link's tunic and will change correspondingly whenever he switches to the Goron or [[Zora Armor|Zora]] Tunics. This is fixed in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]''. Ironically, in the red color, she looks identical to [[Aveil]], her [[Terminan]] counterpart from [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|{{MM|-}}]].
