==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Cloud|The Still World has petrified this cloud into something you can stand on for a short while.<ref name="Cloud">{{Cite|The Still World has petrified this cloud into something you can stand on for a short while.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Clouds are objects found in [[Stilled Hebra Mountain]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which can be summoned for two Echo energy and briefly holds her weight before dissolving.
