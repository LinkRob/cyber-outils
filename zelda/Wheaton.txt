{{Term/Store|Wheaton||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 270"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Wheaton & Pita}}
{{Term|TMC|Wheaton}} is the co-owner of the {{Term|TMC|Bakery|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} with his wife {{Term|TMC|Pita|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome!♪ You’ve come to the one and only bakery of {{Color|TMC Green|Wheaton}} and {{Color|TMC Green|Pita}}!|Pita|TMC}}</ref> He is known for the high quality of his bread.<ref>{{Cite|The bread {{Color|TMC Green|Wheaton}} makes has got to be the best bread in Hyrule!|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref>
