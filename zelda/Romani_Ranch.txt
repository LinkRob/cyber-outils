{{Term/Store|Romani Ranch||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref>{{Cite|Romani Ranch|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> also known as '''Milk Village''',<ref>{{Cite|Milk Village, {{Color|MM3D Red|Romani Ranch}}|Sign|MM3D}}</ref> the '''Village of Milk''',<ref>{{Cite|Romani Ranch<br/>Village of Milk<br/>{{Color|MM3D Red|6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.}}|Sign|MM3D}}</ref> and '''Chateau Romani's Village''',<ref name=chateau>{{Cite|Welcome to Romani Ranch, Chateau Romani's Village. I am the ranch owner, Cremia.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> is a location in {{MM}}.

==Features and Overview==
The Ranch is located in south-west [[Termina]], and is famous for its extremely tasty [[Chateau Romani]].<ref>{{Cite|This way to Romani Ranch. Please visit the home of Chateau Romani.|Sign|MM}}</ref> It is owned by [[Cremia]], who lives with her younger sister, [[Romani]].<ref name=manager>{{Cite|Cremia, manager of Romani Ranch|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> The Ranch is home to several other people and features a number of sidequests and items. [[Link]] will not be able to access the Ranch before the [[Final Day]] unless he has a [[Powder Keg]], as someone (likely the [[Gorman Brothers]]) have placed a large boulder in front of the Ranch entrance.<ref>{{Cite|Someone was up to some no-good mischief and blocked the road to Romani Ranch. I'm sorry. I'll remove it quickly.|Carpenter|MM}}</ref> With a Powder Keg, Link can blow up the boulder and access the Ranch sooner.

If Link arrives on the [[First Day]], he will find that [[Epona]], who was stolen by the [[Skull Kid#Majora's Mask|Skull Kid]], is being kept safely in a stall.<ref>{{Cite|A small, lost horse seems to have been taken in by Romani Ranch, south of town...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> Romani will teach him "[[Epona's Song]]," allowing the young [[Hero of Time]] to summon her by playing the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} and to ride her around Termina, however only after Link has completed Romani's target practice.

[[File:MM Romani Ranch.png|thumb|right|220px|Romani Ranch in {{MM|-}}]]
Prior to the events of {{MM|-}}, the father of Cremia and Romani passed away; however, he left them with Romani-bred cows that are able to produce a special kind of milk called Chateau Romani, which the Ranch is most famous for.<ref name=inheritance>{{Cite|These are Romani-bred. My father left them for us. An inheritance from the heavens...|Cremia|MM}}</ref><ref name=problems>{{Cite|I guess it's been a while now since our father died. I'm trying to take care of the ranch, but things have been getting unstable lately...the cows always seem bothered and frazzled, and I'm finding broken bottles everywhere... Who's doing it, I don't know...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> Ever since then, Cremia has been taking care of both the Ranch and her sister, although lately she has been having some trouble due to the nearby Gorman Brothers, who preside over their own [[Gorman Track|Ranch]].<ref name=problems/> The two Brothers have been using underhanded tactics to hinder their Milk business, such as breaking [[Bottle]]s of Milk during deliveries, although Cremia is not aware of who is responsible for these actions.<ref name=problems/> It is also likely that they were the ones responsible for blocking off Romani Ranch with a huge boulder, preventing Cremia from peddling supplies to [[Clock Town]]'s [[Milk Bar]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh my! Did you come from town? ...Then that means... Milk Road is open to traffic! Good! I can deliver milk to town now!|Cremia|MM}}</ref>

When Link arrives at the Ranch on the First Day, he finds Epona and meets Romani. She goes on to tell him that she has been practicing for the arrival of the [[Ghost (Majora's Mask)|ghost-like creatures]] that come to the Ranch and steal the cows every year as the [[Carnival of Time]] approaches.<ref>{{Cite|Romani was practicing... For tonight...Tonight... They are coming... They... They come at night every year when the carnival gets closer. They come riding in a bright, shining ball, and many of them come down...to the barn... My older sister won't believe me... But Romani must protect the cows!|Romani|MM}}</ref> Her older sister does not believe her, and so Romani recruits Link and asks that he come to the Ranch at 2 AM., which is when "They" will come.<ref>{{Cite|The operation begins tonight at two. I'll be waiting in the barn. Don't be late!|Romani|MM}}</ref> Together, Link and Romani fend off the ghosts until dawn, saving the cows. As thanks, Romani rewards him with a Bottle containing Romani Milk and calls Link a "little hero."<ref>{{Cite|Here's Romani's thanks. When you drink it, put your hand on your hip and take a big gulp like we do here at the ranch! [...] See you later...Little hero.|Romani|MM}}</ref>

On the evening of the [[Second Day]] (presuming the cows have been saved the night before), Cremia will invite Link to accompany her on her milk delivery to the town's Milk Bar by wagon. If Link accepts, Cremia and Link will discover that the road to Clock Town has been blocked off by the Gorman Brothers; as such, they are forced to take a detour through the Gorman Track.<ref>{{Cite|First Milk Road is blocked by a boulder and now... We have to take this detour through ugly country...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> The Gorman Brothers, each wearing a Garo Mask and riding on horseback with pitchforks, lay siege to the wagon and try to break the milk bottles.<ref>{{Cite|Are you ready, boy? I'm going to try to get us through here as fast as I can! If any pursuers come from behind, chase them off with your arrows. They may be after my cargo of milk bottles.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> Link fends them off using his [[Bow]] and [[Arrow]]s, and the two escape without any major harm done. If Link manages to save at least one Bottle of Milk, Cremia rewards him with [[Romani's Mask]]. If this is done once more in a subsequent event, there is a 50-50 chance that he will receive either a [[Rupee|Huge Rupee]] or an affectionate hug.

In an attempt to avoid the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]]'s collision with Termina, the staff of the [[Stock Pot Inn]] are intent upon taking refuge at the Ranch on the night of the Final Day.<ref>{{Cite|Granny's Diary / We're going to take refuge at Romani Ranch tomorrow. I guess it really will fall.|Granny's Diary|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We'll be going to Romani Ranch in the evening to take refuge. I think you should all hurry too.|Anju's Mother|MM}}</ref> However, Cremia believes that the Ranch is not safe either, preparing herself and her sister for the worst.<ref>{{Cite|Actually... I know... We're not safe here, either... That's how life goes, I guess. There are some things in life that you can't change no matter how hard you try.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> After Link saves Termina from its doom after defeating [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]], Romani can be seen practicing her archery skills in the Ranch as Cremia cheers her on.

===Mama's House===
{{Main|Mama's House}}
Mama's House is the residence of Romani and Cremia. It is the largest building on the Ranch.
<!-- EnBr names: Ranch House {{Exp Game|MM}}<br/>Mama's House {{Exp Game|MM3D}} -->

===Barn===
{{Main|Barn}}
The Barn is where Cremia keeps the livestock of the Ranch, namely the ever-coveted Cows. The Cows are milked here daily, and a few [[Rupee]]s can be found in the hay in the corners.

===Cucco Shack===
{{Main|Cucco Shack}}
[[File:Romani Ranch2.png|thumb|right|270px|The Doggy Racetrack and Cucco Shack]]
The [[Cucco Shack]] is home to [[Grog]] and is open from 6 AM to 8 PM.<ref>{{Cite|Cucco Shack / We're open 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> The Cucco Shack has 10 little [[Cucco]] chicks in it, as well as decorative Cucco paintings on the walls. If Link gets all 10 Cucco chicks to follow him around while marching with the [[Bremen Mask]], they will grow and Grog will reward him with the [[Bunny Hood]].<ref>{{Cite|I don't really get it, but just seein' these guys with a crest and all... I don't have regrets about anythin' anymore. I'm perfectly satisfied. Here...You can have this from me...|Grog|MM}}</ref>

===Doggy Racetrack===
{{Main|Doggy Racetrack}}
The '''Doggy Racetrack''' is home to [[Mamamu Yan]] and is open from 6 AM to 8 PM.<ref>{{Cite|Mamamu Yan's / Doggy Racetrack / 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> Link can come here to watch and bet on [[Dog]] races. If he wins 150 Rupees or more, the young hero will win a [[Piece of Heart]].

==Trivia==
*The arch at the entrance to Romani Ranch is the same arch from the entrance to [[Kakariko Village]]/[[Kakariko Village Graveyard|Graveyard]] from {{OoT}}, which, when translated from [[Hylian (Language)|Hylian]] to Japanese, reads "Welcome to Kakariko Village". This was fixed in {{MM3D}}.
*This ranch is [[Termina]]'s equivalent to [[Lon Lon Ranch]] from ''Ocarina of Time'' and shares the same background music.
*If [[Link]] is in [[Fierce Deity Link|Fierce Deity]] form and the ranch itself is not saved from the [[Ghost (Majora's Mask)|Ghosts]], it is possible for him to jump into the broken [[barn]] by climbing up on its side and then perform a well-timed forward roll toward the exposed roof. If done correctly, Link will fall out of bounds, as when looked from above, it's actually an abyss.
