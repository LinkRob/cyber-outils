{{Term/Store|Cedro||Series, HWAoC}} is a character in {{HWAoC}}.<ref name="Name"/>

==Biography==
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
{{Term|HWAoC|Cedro}} runs the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Blacksmith Guild|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So ya want to improve a weapon, huh? Just leave it to me!|Cedro|HWAoC}}</ref>  He will fuse any weapon that is given to him. Although he will fuse any weapon, he will ask for [[Rupee|rupees]] in exchange. Sometimes, when he is fusing, it becomes more powerful than expected. He can also Octo-polish [[Rusty Gear|rusty weapons]]. When done, this will make the rusty weapon clean and useable in battle. Weapons can also be sold to the blacksmith.
