{{Term/Store|Anti-Flame Training: III||HWAoC}} is a Level 41 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Flame Training: III|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Flame Training: III}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Summerwing Butterfly|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Warm Darner|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Opal|link}}, and 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat an {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus|link}} in three minutes. When it is defeated, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Guardian}} spawn and the time limit changes to four minutes. When they are all killed, {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon}} appears and the time limit resets again to five minutes. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon}} is defeated.
