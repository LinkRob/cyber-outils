{{Term/Store|Fofo||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Fofo is an [[Anouki]] who lives in the [[Anouki Estates]] on the [[Isle of Frost]] in {{PH}}. It is among those Link questions while searching for a [[Yook]] who has abducted and impersonated one of the Anouki villagers. Fofo tells Link that it and [[Gumo]] never lie, so therefore neither of them can be the Yook.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, I'm Fofo. Gumo and me, we never lie. It's a rule we got, so neither of us is the Yook!|Fofo|PH}}</ref> Gumo, on the other hand, isn't so sure, and tells Link that either Fofo or [[Aroo]] is the liar.<ref>{{Cite|My name's Gumo. Either Fofo or Aroo has gotta be lying to you. I just wanted to tell you that...|Gumo|PH}}</ref>

As it turns out, Fofo is innocent.
