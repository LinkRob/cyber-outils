The '''Techtite''' is an [[Automaton]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Tecktite|A showy construct that uses built-in rockets to jump high in the air, blasting its surroundings as it takes off.<ref name="Techtite">{{Cite|A showy construct that uses built-in rockets to jump high in the air, blasting its surroundings as it takes off.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Techtites are [[Tektite]]-like Automatons created by [[Dampé]]. They are obtained as a reward for completing the  [[Automaton Engineer Dampé]] [[side quest]], which requires the [[Mothula]] and Tektite echo to complete.
