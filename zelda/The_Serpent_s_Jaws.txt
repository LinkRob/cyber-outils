"{{Term/Store|The Serpent's Jaws||Series, BotW}}" is a [[Shrine Quest]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Serpent's Jaws}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Pagos Woods}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In the {{Term|BotW|Pagos Woods|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} singing about a forest dragon.<ref>{{Cite|The forest dragon...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} is surprised to meet someone else so deep in the {{Term|BotW|Pagos Woods|display=Woods}}.<ref>{{Cite|A visitor? So deep in the forest? Ah, we meet again! I trust you're keeping well?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He knows an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" pertaining to this location, which he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to hear.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will excitedly share the song with him.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's hear it.] Excellent! Without further ado...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics describe an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} found between the jaws of a forest {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Where the forest {{Color|BotW Blue|dragon}} splays its jaws,<br/>A {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} sleeps with noble cause.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} has never seen a {{Term|BotW|Dragon}} in this forest,<ref>{{Cite|The forest dragon? I've never seen a dragon in a forest around these parts.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> but he suspects it is referring to {{Term|BotW|Damel Forest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Though I suppose the forst from the song could be {{Color|BotW Blue|Damel Forest}}, north of here.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He knows for certain that there is a secret to be found,<ref>{{Cite|There's definitely a secret lying in wait—it's just a matter of tracking it down.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> after which he wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well on his journey.<ref>{{Cite|May the light illuminate your path.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

After passing the ruins at {{Term|BotW|Dracozu Lake|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Damel Forest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage|link}} inside the ruins of a {{Term|BotW|Dragon}} head. When he prays to the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} there, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}}, who congratulates him for finding the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage|display=Spring}}.<ref>{{Cite|... You have done well to find this spring.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> She instructs him to offer {{Term|BotW|Farosh's Scale|link}} and promises to guide him.<ref>{{Cite|Offer {{Color|BotW Red|Farosh's scale}}, received from the golden spirit, to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Courage}}. I, Goddess Hylia, will guide you.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Farosh's Scale}} can be obtained by shooting {{Term|BotW|Farosh|link|display=Farosh's}} body with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}. It flies out of {{Term|BotW|Riola Spring|link}} and through {{Term|BotW|Lake Floria|link}} nearby. In addition, it is also found over the {{Term|BotW|Vatorsa Snowfield|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and in {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has obtained one of {{Term|BotW|Farosh's Scale}}, he can return to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage}}.

At the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must drop {{Term|BotW|Farosh's Scale}} into the water. Doing so will cause the door behind the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue}} to open, which will reveal the path to {{Term|BotW|Shae Katha Shrine|link}}. With the way clear, {{Term|BotW|Hylia}} tells him to go forward.<ref>{{Cite|Your path has shown itself. Now go forth.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
