==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Helmet}} are worn by {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} who work as miners. Many of these {{Plural|BotW|Goron}} can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|link}}, where they work on behalf of the {{Term|BotW|Goron Group Mining Company|link}}. However, they are also used in other mining operations, such as {{Term|BotW|Gorko Tunnel|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}. Several abandoned {{Plural|BotW|Helmet}} are also located around {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}.
