{{Term/Store|Stone Tower||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref name="Igos">{{Cite|To return true light to this land, you must seal the doors of {{Color|MM3D Red|Stone Tower}}, where the winds of darkness blow through.|Igos du Ikana|MM3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
[[File:Stone Tower.jpg|thumb|left|150px|The exterior of {{Term|MM|Stone Tower}} from {{MM|-}}]]
The {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower}} consists of three merged stone buildings located at the northeastern edge of {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Canyon|link}} in the east of {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower|display=Tower}} is so large it can be seen from the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Village|link}} of {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead|link}} and even as far as {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|link}}. It is filled with a large chasm, {{Plural|MM3D|Block|link}}, and {{Plural|MM3D|Floor Switch|link}}. {{Plural|MM3D|Boulder|link}} constantly fall here, making the climb to the top a hazardous one. Near the entrance to the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|link}}, there is a nearly inaccessible ledge that supports three {{Plural|MM3D|ReDead|link}} which do not move, and give {{Plural|MM3D|Red Rupee|link}} when defeated. Twelve {{Plural|MM3D|Jar|link}} that contain {{Plural|MM3D|Fairy|link}} and small {{Plural|MM3D|Magic Jar|link}} can also be found here. To reach this ledge, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can either {{Term|MM3D|Hookshot|link}} onto the {{Plural|MM3D|ReDead}} or play the "{{Term|MM3D|Scarecrow's Song|link}}" near the ledge.

To reach the top, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must make use of the "{{Term|MM3D|Elegy of Emptiness|link}}" taught to him by {{Term|MM3D|Igos du Ikana|link}}.<ref name="Igos"/> The melody, which makes replicas of each of {{Term|MM3D|Link}}'s forms, is necessary to press the various {{Plural|MM3D|Floor Switch}} around {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower}} that make several platforms automatically move around the area, allowing the young hero to further continue his way up the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower|display=Tower}}. Some of the {{Plural|MM3D|Floor Switch}} must be pressed in a certain order, however; otherwise, the platforms will not move in the correct order, making it impossible for {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to progress.

{{Term|MM3D|Link}} must also make extensive use of his {{Term|MM3D|Hookshot}} to grab onto the higher areas of the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower}} that are unreachable even with the moving platforms. Once the young hero scales the chasm and reaches the top, he gains access to the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple}}. A curious {{Term|MM3D|Red Emblem|link}} can be found above the entrance to the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|display=Temple}}. When struck with a {{Term|MM3D|Light Arrow|link}}, it completely flips everything inside the chasm upside down.{{Note|In all versions of {{MM}} prior to {{MM3D}}, the {{Term|MM|Red Emblem}} was situated below the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple}} entrance instead, located behind one of the moving platforms.}} This change does not affect the rest of {{Term|MM3D|Termina}} in any way.
