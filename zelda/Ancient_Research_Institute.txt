The {{Term/Store|Ancient Research Institute|Ancient Research Institutes|Series, BotW}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 374}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Ancient Tech Lab|Ancient Tech Labs|HWAoC}},{{Exp Game|HWAoC}}<ref name="Rhoam">{{Cite|The Sheikah Slate conveyed the little Guardian's purpose. This anomalous visitor had traveled from a future of ruin—a time in the midst of the Calamity—to save Princess Zelda. Impa proposed recruiting Purah and Robbie, renowned researchers of ancient technology, to study the Guardian and investigate the mysterious tower that had appeared on Hyrule Field. King Rhoam of Hyrule gave his assent. Zelda, curious about the diminutive Guardian, decided to accompany Impa on her journey to Purah's research facility in Hateno. With Link acting as bodyguard, they made their way to the Ancient Tech Lab.|King Rhoam|HWAoC}}</ref> is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Main|Royal Ancient Lab Ruins}}
The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Research Institute}} was a facility established for the sole purpose of researching {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 374}}</ref> It was placed in proximity to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, one of three known sources of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 404}}</ref> to remain under the control of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 396}}</ref> After the thorough destruction of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Research Institute}} during the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, it was abandoned and became the {{Term|BotW|Royal Ancient Lab Ruins|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} fled and formed their own individual {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Research Institute}} in the two other known sources of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy}}, the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, respectively.
