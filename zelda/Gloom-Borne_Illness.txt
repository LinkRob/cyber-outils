"{{Term|TotK|{{Page Name}}|Series, TotK}}" is a {{Term|TotK|Side Quest|link}} in {{TotK}}.

==Overview==
Lasli's grandmother, Nanna, of Kakariko Village, has fallen ill after contracting {{Term|TotK|Gloom|link}} nearby.<ref>{{Cite|You see, it's my grandmother... She got too close to that nasty <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">gloom</font> stuff, and now she's sick and bedridden.|Lasli|TotK}}</ref> Lasli is attempting to cook a variety of Nanna's favorite dish, {{Term|TotK|Porridge|link}}, that could cure her illness but none of them have any effects so far.<ref>{{Cite|<font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Porridge</font> is about the only thing she can keep down right now too. I've been trying to mix in <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">ingredients that could help with gloom sickness</font>... But none of them have had any effect.|Lasli|TotK}}</ref>

To cure Nanna, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} uses an ingredient called {{Term|TotK|Sundelion|link}} to cook a gloom-remedy  {{Term|TotK|Veggie Porridge|link}} then gives it to Lasli for her grandmother. Upon consumption, Nanna has been fully cured. Claree, shop owner of {{Term|TotK|Enchanted|link}}, came in and saw Nanna cured and well, much to her surpise.

With Nanna back to normal, she and Claree encourages Lasli to go follow her dream to become a fashion designer at {{Term|TotK|Hateno Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that I'm feeling better, you should hurry and prepare for your journey, Lasli.|Nanna|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can't get anything by her, Lasli. Besides, everyone knows studying fashion in <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Hateno Village</font> is your dream!|Claree|TotK}}</ref> Claree returns to the store to revert her prices to normal.<ref>{{Cite|Well, now that Grandmother's all better, I think it's time I head back to the shop. I've got to make sure our prices are back to normal and everything's ready for our customers.|Claree|TotK}}</ref> Lasli thanked Link for curing her grandmother, and gives him one of the leftover Veggie Porridge she was cooking for Nanna.<ref>{{Cite|I made a lot of porridge trying to find a cure for my grandmother. We won't be able to eat it all ourselves.|Lasli|TotK}}</ref>
