The {{Term/Store|Tiger Scroll|Tiger Scrolls|Series, TMC|plural}} are {{Plural|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 141}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Plural|TMC|Tiger Scroll}} are given to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} by five of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}, after {{Term|TMC|Link}} successfully learns a new {{Term|TMC|Sword Technique|link}}. They allow {{Term|TMC|Link}} to review the directions for a {{Term|TMC|Sword Technique|display=Technique}} if he ever forgets how to perform it. The character stamped on the scroll reads {{Romanize|武|Bu|Art of War|2}}.

Although the {{Plural|TMC|Tiger Scroll}} are treated as key items, only the {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack|link}} is necessary to complete the game.

==List of {{Plural|TMC|Tiger Scroll}}==
===Spin Attack===
{{Main|Spin Attack}}
[[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 7.png|left]][[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 2.png|right]]
The {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack}} allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to charge his sword and then release, causing him to spin around in a circle. It is learned from {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}} once {{Term|TMC|Link}} returns from {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}. The charging time can be shortened by training with {{Term|TMC|Scarblade|link}}.

The {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} guards will not allow {{Term|TMC|Link}} to venture towards {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} until he learns the {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack}}.
{{Clear}}

===Rock Breaker===
{{Main|Rock Breaker}}
[[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 8.png|left]][[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 2.png|right]]
The {{Term|TMC|Rock Breaker}} allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to break {{Plural|TMC|Jar|link}} and throwable {{Plural|TMC|Rock|link}} by striking them with his sword. It is learned from {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade}} once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}}.
{{Clear}}

===Roll Attack===
{{Main|Roll Attack}}
[[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 9.png|left]][[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 3.png|right]]
The {{Term|TMC|Roll Attack}} allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to perform a forward thrust by using his sword just as he comes up at any point in a roll. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} successfully strikes an enemy, he will execute a Spin Slash. It is learned from {{Term|TMC|Grayblade|link}} once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|White Sword}}. The move also appears in {{TP}}; however, it does not need to be learned, and does not have a name.
{{Clear}}

===Dash Attack===
{{Main|Dash Attack}}
[[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 12.png|left]][[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 2.png|right]]
The {{Term|TMC|Dash Attack}} allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to dash with his sword out by equipping both his sword and {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}}, and then using the boots. It is learned from {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade}} once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots}}.
{{Clear}}

===Peril Beam===
{{Main|Peril Beam}}
[[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 10.png|left]][[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 3.png|right]]
The {{Term|TMC|Peril Beam}} allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to fire a beam from his sword when at critical health. It is learned from {{Term|TMC|Waveblade|link}} once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}.
{{Clear}}

===Sword Beam===
{{Main|Sword Beam}}
[[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 11.png|left]][[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 4.png|right]]
The {{Term|TMC|Sword Beam}} allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to fire a beam from his sword when at full health. It is learned from {{Term|TMC|Grimblade|link}} once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}}.
{{Clear}}

===Down Thrust===
{{Main|Down Thrust}}
[[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 13.png|left]][[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 2.png|right]]
The {{Term|TMC|Down Thrust}} allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to perform a downward thrust by using his sword just as he reaches the height of a jump with the {{Term|TMC|Roc's Cape|link}}. It is learned from {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade}} once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Roc's Cape}}. It bears a resemblance to the [[Jump Attack]].
{{Clear}}

===Great Spin Attack===
{{Main|Great Spin Attack}}
[[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 14.png|left]][[File:TMC Tiger Scroll Sprite 5.png|right]]
The {{Term|TMC|Great Spin Attack}} allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to perform continuous {{Plural|TMC|Spin Attack}} by repeatedly using his sword while executing a {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack}}. It is learned from {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade I|link}} once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains all of the other seven {{Plural|TMC|Tiger Scroll}}. The timespan of the technique can be lengthened by training with {{Term|TMC|Greatblade|link}}.
{{Clear}}
