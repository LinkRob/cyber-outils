{{Term/Store|Monster Guts|Monster Guts|Series, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 132"/>

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Monster Guts}} can be found in {{Plural|ALBW|Treasure Chest|link}} and by defeating {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}}. Ten of them can be used to brew a {{Term|ALBW|Purple Potion|link}} at the {{Term|ALBW|Witch's House|link}}.<ref name="E 132"/>

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, {{Plural|TFH|Monster Guts}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Volcano|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Blazing Trail|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Monster Guts}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Blazing Trail}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Monster Guts}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Hinox Mine|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Monster Guts}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Den of Flames|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Monster Guts}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Monster Guts}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gear|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Goron Garb|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 132"/>
