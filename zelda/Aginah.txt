{{Term/Store|Aginah||Series, ALttP}} a character in {{ALttP}}.<ref name="E 228"/>

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Aginah's role is minor compared to that of his brother [[Sahasrahla]]. He is one of three characters apart from the [[Maiden|Seven Maidens]] that is a direct descendant of the Seven Sages who appear during the course of the game, the other two being Sahasrahla and the [[Lost Old Man]] on [[Death Mountain]]. Aginah is found living alone in a cave known as the Desert Sanctuary{{Fact|ALttP}} in the [[Desert of Mystery]] and tells [[Link]] about the [[Book of Mudora]] and where to find it.<ref>{{Cite|If you have the Book Of Mudora you can read the language of the Hylia People. It should be in the house of books in the village... You must get it!|Aginah|ALttP}}</ref> He is one of the first to mention the prophecy of the [[Great Cataclysm]] to Link.<ref>{{Cite|This must be an omen of the Great Cataclysm foretold by the people of Hylian blood...|Aginah|ALttP}}</ref> Like Sahasrahla, he has telepathic powers, though he doesn't contact Link with them. Instead, his telepathic powers give him the ability to sense events in the [[Sacred Realm|Golden Land]] (or Ganon's [[Dark World]]), as seen in one of Link's interactions with him.<ref>{{Cite|I am Aginah. I sense something is happening in the Golden Land the seven wise men sealed...|Aginah|ALttP}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{AST|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Term|AST|Aginah}} plays a much larger role than in the previous game. Now wearing robes of the same color as {{Term|AST|Sahasrahla|link}}, he lives in Sahasrahla's hideout near the {{Term|AST|Eastern Palace|link}}. He is the first person to discover the {{Term|AST|Hero of Light|link}}. Throughout the game, he uses his telepathic abilities to directly contact the {{Term|AST|Hero of Light}} to advise them and impart his wisdom. It is also revealed that he is the younger brother of {{Term|AST|Sahasrahla}}, who left {{Term|AST|Hyrule|link}} some time prior to the game to search for {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
