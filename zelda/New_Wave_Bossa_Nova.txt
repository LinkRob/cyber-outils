The "{{Term/Store|New Wave Bossa Nova||Series, MM, MM3D}}" is a song featured in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96}}</ref>

==Learning Location and Uses==
[[File:MM3D New Wave Bossa Nova Icon.png|left]]
After [[Link]] places the seven [[Zora Egg]]s that he rescued in the aquarium located inside the {{Term|MM3D|Marine Research Lab|link}}, the eggs will hatch and line themselves up according to the notes that make up the "New Wave Bossa Nova." [[Zora Link]] must take out the [[Zora Guitar]] for the baby Zoras to teach him the bossa nova.

Once the song is learned, Link must play this melody as a [[Zora Link|Zora]] in front of [[Lulu]] to recover her voice. Upon doing so, the [[Giant Turtle]] who was slumbering in the [[Zora Cape]] will awaken and grant the young hero access to the [[Great Bay Temple]].<ref>{{Cite|It's the melody taught by the Zora children that invigorates singing voices.|N/A|MM}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* According to Lulu, this particular song was sung by her [[Lulu's Mother|mother of the same name]] when she was young. It is however, unknown if the "New Wave Bossa Nova" was played by the original or current band of [[The Indigo-Go's]] like "[[Ballad of the Wind Fish|Ballad of Wind Wish]]".

==Nomenclature==
{{Wp|New wave music|New wave}} was a popular style of alternative rock during the 1970s and '80s, which formed alongside punk. {{Wp|Bossa nova}} is a style of Brazilian music that was popular in the late 1950s and early '60s and is similar to samba and jazz. The phrase ''bossa nova'' literally translates to "new wave", making the title somewhat redundant.
