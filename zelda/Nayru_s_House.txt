{{Term/Store|Nayru's House||Series, OoA}} is a location in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 255}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Nayru's House is located inside a tree in the northeastern corner of the [[Forest of Time]]. Inside Nayru's House is a [[bed]] in which [[Link]] can sleep to restore his hearts, and a hidden room which contains the [[Harp of Ages]]. [[Impa]] will remain in Nayru's House after [[Veran]] possesses [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]].<ref>{{Cite|Link, can you go meet the {{Color|OoA Blue|Maku Tree}} in {{Color|OoA Blue|Lynna Village}}?
