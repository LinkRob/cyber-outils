The {{Term/Store|Daka Tuss Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Daka Tuss Shrine}}<br/>Sunken Scoop}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Sunken Scoop''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Daka Tuss Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Shrine Island|link}}, north of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. It is surrounded by shallow water and is out in the open. As Link enters, the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} of the Shrine, {{Term|BotW|Daka Tuss|link}}, will introduce him to the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Daka Tuss. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Daka Tuss|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Inside the {{Term|BotW|Daka Tuss Shrine}}, a square pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} holds three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} and a metal bowl. To the left of the pool is a pedestal to hold an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} blocked behind a short metal grate. To get an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} over the grate, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} and raise the metal bowl to scoop up an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} to carry it over the gate and drop it in the pedestal.

With the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} in the pedestal, the gate by the entrance opens. In the next room, another square pool holds three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} and a submerged {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}}. Behind the pool, a large metal grate blocks {{Term|BotW|Link}} from {{Term|BotW|Daka Tuss}}.

Another pedestal hides behind a grate to the right of the pool. However, the top of the pedestal is covered, blocking {{Term|BotW|Link}} from dropping any {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into it. Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not able to press the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}} under the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}, he must bring the metal bowl from the previous room and use it to press down the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}}. The ceiling won't stay open if the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}} is unpressed, so {{Term|BotW|Link}} must first leave an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} on the top of the grate, and then press the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}}, dropping the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into the pedestal.

Once again, with the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} in the pedestal, the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} in the pool drains and a passageway is revealed. Traveling down the passageway and up a flight of stairs leads {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Daka Tuss}} on the other side of the large grate wall.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Daka Tuss|BotW}}</ref> He will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Daka Tuss|BotW}}</ref>

When the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} in the second room is drained, the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} in the first room is also drained. Under the first pool, a passageway is revealed that leads to a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Longsword|link}}.
