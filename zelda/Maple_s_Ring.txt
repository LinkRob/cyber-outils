{{Term/Store|Maple's Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Maple's Ring<br/>Maple meetings {{Icon|OoS Up Arrow}}|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Maple's Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it halves the number of defeated enemies required for Maple to appear.<ref name="Description"/> The ring need only be worn when attempting to encounter Maple, not while killing enemies; removing the ring after killing enemies will revert the requirement to the higher number. Maple's Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
