The {{Term/Store|Raqa Zunzo Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Raga Zunzo Shrine}}<br/>Raga Zunzo's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Raqa Zunzo's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo Shrine}} is initially buried beneath the ground at the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. The only signs of it are a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} and an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}, known as the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy|link}}, nearby. However, if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to take the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}}, {{Term|BotW|Shabonne|link}} will yell at him, stating it is only for those who have proven themselves at {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|link|display=Sand-Seal Racing}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey, hey! Hey, hey, hey! Hey!!!}} That item is only for those who have proven themselves in the {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal races}}! Hands off!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} and {{Term|BotW|Tali|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} asks if he wants to try and beat {{Term|BotW|Tali}}'s time at {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Racing}}.<ref>{{Cite|Did you want to try to beat Tali's record? I'll warn you—they don't call her Dame of the Dunes for nothing.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> She explains if {{Term|BotW|Link}} can surpass her record, he can have the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}}, which has been passed down by {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Racing}} champions for 10,000 years.<ref>{{Cite|Tali's record is {{Color|BotW Red|1}}:{{Color|BotW Red|30}}! If you can beat that record, that {{Color|BotW Blue|trophy}} is yours. This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|grand trophy}} that's been passed down by ten thousand years of sand-seal-racing champions.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> This initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Undefeated Champ|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} successfully beats {{Term|BotW|Tali}}'s record,<ref>{{Cite|It's a... A new record?!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> she will allow him to take the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}}.<ref>{{Cite|I believe this belongs to you...|Tali|BotW}}</ref> She shares a legend about the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy|display=Trophy}}, which states that the chosen one will be granted a new power when it is offered to the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}.<ref>{{Cite|This trophy is something special, you know. Our champions have passed down a legend about it for ten thousand years. The legend goes... "When the holy jewel is offered unto the pedestal, the chosen one will be granted a new power."|Tali|BotW}}</ref> Despite never obtaining that power, she believes {{Term|BotW|Link}} may be the chosen one.<ref>{{Cite|But that power has never been granted to anyone. Not even to me, obviously... And yet... I sense a power emanating from you... The likes of which I have never felt in anyone else. You should give it a shot.|Tali|BotW}}</ref>

By placing the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}} in the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}'s {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will cause {{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo Shrine}} to emerge from the sand.<ref>{{Cite|When you placed the jewel on the pedestal, a shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}. As he enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo|link}}, who tells him that he has already proven his worth by entering.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Raqa Zunzo. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Raqa Zunzo|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo Shrine}} is one large room with no puzzles or enemies, only a single pathway leading to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo}}. There is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the center of the room containing a {{Term|BotW|Radiant Shield|link}}. As a reward arriving to this {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb. May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Raqa Zunzo|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Raqa Zunzo|BotW}}</ref>
