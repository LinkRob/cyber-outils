==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Icy Lizalfos Tails are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by [[Ice-Breath Lizalfos]], who can be found in mountainous regions of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, upon their deaths. Though they have hard scales and flesh, which makes them unsuitable for cooking, they can be put in a [[Cooking Pot]] with critters to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Blue [[Dye]] or sold to [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] in exchange for [[Mon]].

The [[Desert Voe Set]] requires Icy Lizalfos Tails to be upgraded to their third and fourth tiers at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
