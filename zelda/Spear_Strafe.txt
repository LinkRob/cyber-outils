{{Term/Store|Spear Strafe||Series, CoH}} is an ability in {{CoH}}.{{Name Ref|CoH}}

==Overview==
{{Term|CoH|Spear Strafe}} can only be used by {{Term|CoH|Impa|link}}, which lets her hold out her {{Term|CoH|Spear|link}} and strafe with it, hitting enemies that come in contact with it. Hitting an enemy with the tip of the {{Term|CoH|Spear}} does one more damage than a standard attack, and enemies can be hit with the sides of the head. Additionally, {{Term|CoH|Spear Strafe}} does not trigger most ‘on-hit’ effects such as being burnt by a {{Term|CoH|Fire Keese|link}}, or being stunned by electric enemies, and can block projectiles.
