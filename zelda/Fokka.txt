{{Term/Store|Fokka|Fokka|Series, TAoL|plural}} are enemies in {{TAoL}}.<ref name="Encyclopedia"/>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Fokka}} are also known as '''Eagle Knights''' in {{Guide|CE|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= CE |publisher= Nintendo |page= 40}}</ref> However, as this name contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TAoL|Fokka}} are only found in the {{Term|TAoL|Great Palace|link}}. They can leap and deal enormous amounts of damage. Both {{Plural|TAoL|Red Fokka|link}} and {{Plural|TAoL|Blue Fokka|link}} appear within the {{Term|TAoL|Great Palace}}. Both can throw beams from their {{Plural|TAoL|Sword|link}}. Much like statues of blue {{Plural|TAoL|Iron Knuckle|link}} in the first six Palaces, statues of {{Plural|TAoL|Fokka}} appear throughout the {{Term|TAoL|Great Palace}}.

==Nomenclature==
{{Romanize|フォッカー|Fokkā}} (pronounced {{IPA|'ɸok.kɑː}} {{Respell|PHOHK|kah}}) is likely based on the German word "Vogel" (pronounced {{IPA|ˈfoːɡəl}} {{Respell|FO|gəl}}), which means "bird".
