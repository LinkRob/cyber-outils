The {{Term/Store|All-Night Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring [[item]] and object in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 115 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's the {{Color|MM Red|All-Night Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>That looks like an expensive mask.</p>}}
[[File:All-Night Mask obtained.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Link obtaining the All-Night Mask in {{MM|-}}]]
The All-Night Mask is an eerie-looking [[Mask]] that prevents its user from falling asleep, even if they attempt to.<ref name="MM3D Inventory"/> It was initially conceived as a torture device.<ref name="MM3D Inventory"/><ref>{{Cite|A torture device that prevents sleep called the {{Color|MM3D Red|All-Night Mask}} is a truly rare artifact. It's occasionally been spotted at the {{Color|MM3D Red|Curiosity Shop}}...|Gossip Stone|MM3D}}</ref>

[[Link]] can purchase the Mask from the [[Curiosity Shop]] on the night of the [[Final Day]] for an extremely pricey 500 [[Rupee]]s if he has prevented [[Sakon]] from stealing the [[Bomb Bag]] from the [[Old Lady from the Bomb Shop]] on the night of the [[First Day]], making it by far the most expensive item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tonight's bargain is the {{Color|MM3D Red|All-Night Mask}} for use at bedtime. I forgot when this was made, but it sure is a freaky mask... See? When you put it on, you can try and try to fall asleep, but you won't be able to. Pretty creepy, huh?|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|All-Night Mask: 500 Rupees}}<br/>You won't get sleepy if you wear this mask.|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM3D}}</ref> If Link allows Sakon to steal from her, the Mask will not be available for purchase.

Although it has no effect for general gameplay, Link can wear this Mask in order to prevent himself falling asleep while listening to the stories of [[Anju's Grandmother]], which will be in his benefit to earn two [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]].

==={{TWW|-}}===
The All-Night Mask appears alongside the [[Bunny Hood]], [[Goron Mask]], and [[Keaton Mask]] behind the counter of the [[Nintendo Gallery]], as a cameo. It can be seen hanging on the wall next to [[Carlov]].

== Trivia ==

* The price of the All-Nights Mask is the maximum [[Rupee]] count for {{MM}} as well as its prequel {{OoT}}, as it is only possible to buy it when the [[Giant Wallet|Giant's Wallet]] is obtained and filled. Therefore, it is one of the most expensive items in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]].
