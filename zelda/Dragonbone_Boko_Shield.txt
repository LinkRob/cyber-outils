{{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Boko Shield|link}} that have been upgraded and reinforced with fossilized bone.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created and maintained by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Though they provide a respectable defense, the poor craftsmanship used to make them leaves them with low {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are used by {{Term|BotW|Black Bokoblin|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Golden Bokoblin|link}}. High-tier {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}} also make use of these {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}}.

As they are made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Shield}} will burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and unlike metal {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}}, they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. Finally, they cannot be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

If scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Shield}}.
