{{Compendium/Extract|Ancient Short Sword}}
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Short Sword}} can be made by supplying the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} with 1,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, 15 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spring|link}}, 5 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shaft|link}}, and 2 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Exchange for Ancient Short Sword?|Ancient Oven|BotW}}</ref> Their blades are made using {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, only appearing when the {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} are drawn.<ref name="Inventory"/> The cutting power of {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Short Sword}} is stronger than any metal swords.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>

The final {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} that {{Term|BotW|Nebb|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to show him during "{{Term|BotW|The Weapon Connoisseur|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Short Sword}}, which he claims are not common around {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So, have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient short sword}}? Grandpa said you can't find those around here. If you get ahold of one, let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Short Sword}} for {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} to see, {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will become excited and give him a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that...the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient short sword}}? Nice! Nice! Let me see, let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you! Take this as a token of my thanks!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Short Sword}} are made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, and therefore they will deal a higher amount of damage against {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}}-type {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. They will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. However, they are capable of floating on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, are unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and cannot be manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
