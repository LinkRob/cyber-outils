"{{Term/Store|Shelter from the Storm||Series, BotW}}" is the eleventh {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Shelter from the Storm}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #11}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory}} can be unlocked under a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} on a hill west of the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins|link}}. It opens with {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} sitting under the {{Term|BotW|Tree}} to escape the {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}} while {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} practices with the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}} a few feet away. Looking up at the sky, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} doubts the {{Term|BotW|Rain}} will let up soon.<ref>{{Cite|I doubt this will let up anytime soon...|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} turns her attention to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s training, noting that his path seems to mirror that of [[Link's Father|his father]], who was also a knight.<ref>{{Cite|Your path seems to mirror your father's. You've dedicated yourself to becoming a knight, as well.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She also notes that {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s commitment to his training is admirable and that she can see why he is the chosen one.<ref>{{Cite|Your commitment to the training necessary to fulfill your goal is really quite admirable. I see now why you would be the chosen one.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes his training, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} asks him what he would do if he realized he was not meant to be a fighter, and yet all he heard was that it was his duty to become one as a member of his family.<ref>{{Cite|What if... One day... You realized that you just weren't meant to be a fighter. Yet the only thing people ever said... was that you were born into a family of the royal guard, and so no matter what you thought, you had to become a knight.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She wonders if {{Term|BotW|Link}} would have chosen a different path in this scenario.<ref>{{Cite|If that was the only thing that you were ever told... I wonder, then... would you have chosen a different path?|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>
