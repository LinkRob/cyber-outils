'''Shallow Water''' is a type of [[Water (Terrain)|water]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It first appears in {{ALttP}}.

==Location and uses==
Shallow water normally does not have any hazardous effects on [[Link]]. Shallow water can include pools, puddles, and coastlines that Link can splash through freely.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, Shallow Water is hazardous for Link only while he is [[Minish]]-sized, similarly to [[Deep Water]], because if Link walks into Shallow Water, he drowns, unless he is equipped with [[Flippers]].

While normal-sized, Link can swoop an [[Empty Bottle]] through Shallow Water to receive a fill of [[Bottled Water|water]], [[Forest Water]], [[Hot Spring Water]], [[Mt. Crenel Mineral Water]], and possibly even a [[fish]], depending on the location.
