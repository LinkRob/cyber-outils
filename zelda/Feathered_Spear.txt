{{Plural|BotW|Feathered Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} craftsmen.<ref name="Inventory"/> These spears belong to the {{Term|BotW|Rito Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Term|BotW|Rito}}-made {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> They are lightweight and made of sturdy materials, which allow {{Term|BotW|Rito}} warriors to use them in aerial combat.<ref name="Inventory"/> The guards of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gesane|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mazli|link}}, wield them. They are also used by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} and in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Feathered Spear}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will sink if they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. If they are exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, they will not burn like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} do.
