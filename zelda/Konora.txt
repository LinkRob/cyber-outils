{{Term/Store|Konora||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Konora|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Konora}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who can first be found inside the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn|link}}. She lives in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, but she is stuck at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} because of the threat posed by {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} and its {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If only that {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} wasn't around. I would already be back at {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}...|Konora|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't want to be stuck here, but I don't want to be zapped by an angry creature's lightning, either...|Konora|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Konora}}, she comments that the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} seems to get closer every day.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'saaba... That {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}... It feels like it gets a little bit closer every day.|Konora|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Konora}} wonders how long it will be until she can return to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder how long it will be before I can get back to {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}...|Konora|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}, she explains that she is talking about the giant thing roaming around the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When I say {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}, I'm talking about that giant thing roaming around the desert...|Konora|BotW}}</ref> According to {{Term|BotW|Konora}}, it has been creating sandstorms and will blast {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} at anyone who comes near.<ref>{{Cite|It's been kicking up bad sandstorms, and apparently it'll blast you with lightning if you get too close!|Konora|BotW}}</ref> She is too afraid of getting struck by {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}'s {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} to travel back to Gerudo Town.<ref>{{Cite|The thought of getting zapped is terrifying. I can't risk traveling to {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}...|Konora|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Konora}} explains that it is a large, lively town to the southwest made up almost entirely of {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a town to the southwest, made up almost entirely of Gerudo. It's the largest settlement in these parts. It's got a lively market, and a canteen... The town's active from morning to night!|Konora|BotW}}</ref> However, she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he cannot enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} because men are not allowed inside.<ref>{{Cite|But... Well, they have a rule that'll affect you—guys aren't allowed in. "No voe may enter!" they say...|Konora|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Konora}} wishes that a hero would do something about the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}.<ref>{{Cite|If only someone, perhaps a hero just passing through town, could take care of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}...|Konora|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Konora}} can be found sitting in {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her, she explains that she forgot where she lives because she has been gone so long.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to that {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}, I'd been unable to get home for so long that I'd forgotten my actual address!|Konora|BotW}}</ref> After a long pause, she yells that she is not joking.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! This isn't a joke!|Konora|BotW}}</ref>

=={{TotK}}==

We find her resting by a tree nearby [[Yamiyo Shrine]], where she has traveled in the search for the voe of her dreams. She tells us of a rumor of cherry-blossom trees, where [[Satori]] are sometimes seen and that you can coax them out with fruit.
