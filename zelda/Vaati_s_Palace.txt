{{Term/Store|Vaati's Palace||Series, FS}},<ref name="E Dungeons">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 153}}</ref> also known as the '''Palace of Winds''',<ref name="HH Eras">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 83}}</ref> is the fourth and final stage of {{FS}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace}} is a fortress drifting in the sky above {{Term|FS|Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Fairy Silver Keys">{{Cite|You all seem to have collected the three {{Color|FS Orange|Silver Keys}}. Vaati's palace of winds is a great castle that floats in the sky. Now that you have gathered the keys of the Fairies of Forest, Ice, and Flame, the palace shall appear. Onward, young heroes! You must save Princess Zelda!|Great Fairy of Flame|FS}}</ref> Once called the Palace of Winds, it is occupied and used as a stronghold by the Wind Sorcerer {{Term|FS|Vaati|link}}.<ref name="HH Eras"/><ref name="E Dungeons"/>

During the game's introduction, {{Term|FS|Zelda|link}} is kidnapped and taken deep into the Palace by {{Term|FS|Vaati}}. It is also where the {{Plural|FS|Links|link}} eventually defeat and re-seal {{Term|FS|Vaati}} within the {{Term|FS|Four Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 262}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Palace==
The Palace first appears once all three {{Plural|FS|Silver Key|link}} have been collected by the {{Plural|FS|Links}}.<ref name="Fairy Silver Keys"/><ref name="New Stage Msg">{{Cite|Vaati's Palace has appeared!|Stage Select|FS}}</ref> The entrance holds three color-coded doors, each pertaining to an increasing level of difficulty and special set of keys.<ref>{{Cite|At last all of you have collected the three {{Color|FS Orange|Golden Keys}}. You can now choose the path to Vaati's Palace. That path will likely be much more dangerous than any you've seen yet. And yet onward you must go, great heroes!|Great Fairy of Flame|FS}}</ref><ref name="Fairy Hero Keys">{{Cite|At long last all of you have gathered the three {{Color|FS Orange|Hero's Keys}}. You should now be able to choose the path to Vaati's Palace. It is a path of 12 levels, all more dangerous than those you've seen. Yet onward you must go, great heroes!|Great Fairy of Flame|FS}}</ref>

Initially, only the silver and golden doors are visible, and can be unlocked using the Silver or {{Plural|FS|Golden Key|link}} via the central pedestal. Once each of the {{Plural|FS|Hero's Key|link}} have been obtained, a shutter will open to reveal the third and final door. Placed between the sliver and golden doors, it is unlocked with the {{Plural|FS|Hero's Key}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace}} is generally a wind-themed {{Term|FS|Dungeon}} with a focus on using the {{Term|FS|Roc's Cape|link}}. Being in the sky, many {{Plural|FS|Hole|link}} are present throughout the area, often in combination with narrow flooring, {{Plural|FS|Cracked Floor Tile|link}}, and moving {{Plural|FS|Platform|link}}. {{Plural|FS|Puzzle|link}} and {{Plural|FS|Enemy|link}} are also notably more difficult than in previous {{Plural|FS|Stage|link}}.

The Silver and {{Term|FS|Golden Key}} doors each have three levels, including the {{Term|FS|Boss|link}} fight with {{Term|FS|Vaati}} on the third level. While the silver door's levels are entirely wind-themed and set within the sky, the golden door's second level may have the appearance of the {{Term|FS|Sea of Trees|link}}, {{Term|FS|Talus Cave|link}}, or {{Term|FS|Death Mountain|link}}.

The {{Term|FS|Hero's Key}} door consists of twelve levels and incorporates elements from all three of the previous {{Plural|FS|Stage}}.<ref name="Fairy Hero Keys"/> Each of the {{Term|FS|Stage}} {{Plural|FS|Boss}} also make an appearance, with either {{Term|FS|Big Manhandla|link}}, {{Term|FS|Dera Zol|link}}, or {{Term|FS|Gouen|link}} being present every three levels. This culminates with {{Term|FS|Vaati}} on the twelfth level.

Regardless of the door selected, the final room of {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace}} is a floating platform surrounded by {{Plural|FS|Cracked Block}}. A central statue sits between four {{Term|FS|Bomb|link}} {{Plural|FS|Item Podium|link}} and is used by {{Term|FS|Vaati}} to initiate the first phase of the {{Term|FS|Boss}} fight.

==Trivia==
*The symbol present on {{Plural|FS|Warp Point|link}} within {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace}} resembles that of the {{Term|Series|Wind Element|link}}.
*In {{FS|-}}, {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace}} appears without a message once unlocked, simply drifting into the {{Term|FS|Stage Select}} screen. This was changed in {{FSAE|-}}.<ref name="New Stage Msg"/>
*In {{FS|-}}, the {{Term|FS|Silver Key}} door doesn't have an unlocking animation, unlike the Golden and {{Plural|FS|Hero's Key}}. Instead, the screen transitions directly to the stage itself. This was changed in {{FSAE|-}} to be consistent with the other keys.
