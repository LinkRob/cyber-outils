==Biography==
Although appearing to be mere giant [[Stalchild]] at first glance, Captain Keeta is (or was) the leader of the Stalchildren of [[Termina]] who were once members of the Ikana army that defended the [[Ikana Kingdom]] during the war.<ref name="Skull Keeta"/> He was once the captain of the army under the command of [[Igos du Ikana]], but lies restless after facing loss in battle. When [[Link]] encounters him, he is sleeping in [[Ikana Graveyard]]. After awakening him with the "[[Sonata of Awakening]]," he will challenge Link to race him and then beat him in a fight.<ref>{{Cite|Young swordsman! You summoned me? Ah, but before we may exchange words, I must first test your skill. Stop me in my tracks. Pass through the barrier of flame. Show me what you've got!|Captain Keeta|MM3D}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= Hurry! Chase him until you get your chance at him! But if you get too close, you might regret it!
}}
[[File:SkullKeeta1.jpg|thumb|241x241px|Captain Keeta fighting Link in {{MM|-}}]]
When the battle begins, Captain Keeta will run away as Link must give chase to him and hit him before he reaches the end of the trail. By using ranged weapons, Link can momentarily slow down Captain Keeta. Fire barriers and Stalchildren will hinder Link. To proceed past these barriers, Link must defeat the Stalchildren. Once the young [[Hero of Time]] has caught up with Captain Keeta and struck him with his [[Sword]], Captain Keeta will begin to attack Link in much the same way as regular Stalchildren. However, his reach is longer and once he has taken some damage, he will begin to jump up in an attempt to crush Link, which will cause one [[Heart Container|Heart]] of damage.

Once Captain Keeta is defeated, he will admit his defeat and give Link the [[Captain's Hat]] after a lengthy speech.<ref name="Skull Keeta" /><ref>{{Cite|Since being shamed by loss in a battle within my kingdom... I have waited here for one to come and awaken my soul. Young swordsman who has awakened and deftly defeated me... I shall rely on your power to fulfill my request. I ask you to take my soul, which rests in the fiercely burning flame... And convey my words to my men, who, even in death, remain loyal to me. Tell them the war has ended... Then I shall be able to drift quietly into sleep... Captain, sir! May I take leave, sir? Yes, sir!|Captain Keeta|MM}}</ref> If the young hero fails to catch up to him before he reaches the end of the trail, Captain Keeta will refuse to fight and return to his sleep.<ref>{{Cite|If that is all the strength you have, surely you cannot match me in battle.|Captain Keeta|MM}}</ref>

An effective strategy is to turn into [[Goron Link]], then roll until meeting the Stalchildren and punch them. When Link punches Keeta, the battle will start. As [[Zora Link]], he can back up and throw his [[Boomerang#Zora Boomerang|Zora Boomerangs]] and stun him, then use the jump attack and repeat the strategy. However, a much more effective strategy with far less steps of execution is to simply use the [[Gilded Sword]] and [[Bunny Hood]]. If Link has the upgraded [[Spin Attack]], he will easily defeat both Stalchildren in one hit, allowing him to run swiftly to the next fire barrier.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, Captain Keeta appears as a new enemy type in the {{MM|-}} DLC pack. He appears only in certain battles in the [[Termina Map]]. When faced in battle, after depleting 2/3rds of his health, he will request that the Warrior stops attacking him. If the Warrior continues attacking him, he will simply run away. However, if the Warrior stops, he will acknowledge their strength and then join their army. 
{{Noncanon|End}}

== Trivia ==

* Captain Keeta is one the few characters in {{MM|-}} to trigger the chase theme, alongside [[Sakon]] as well as [[Skull Kid]] at the beginning of the game.
* His attack to crush [[Link]] by jumping into the air and pounding him down with his lower body when low on health is a direct reference to the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, based on the [[mariowiki:Ground_Pound|Ground Pound]] technique.
