{{Term/Store|Demise||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an antagonistic character that appears in {{SS}}, serving as the [[Final Boss]] of the game.<ref name=":0">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 174 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref> He is a Demon King and the master of [[Ghirahim]], its primary antagonist.

Eons before the events of {{SS|-}}, he and his army of [[demon]]s broke through a fissure in the earth and sought out the [[Triforce]]. After a long war, he was eventually sealed away by the Goddess [[Hylia]]. He is resurrected during the events of the game, but is defeated by [[Link]] through the use of the [[Master Sword]]. However, upon his ultimate defeat, Demise utters a [[Curse of Demise|deadly curse]] stating that his hatred will not end, but will be reborn in an eternal cycle. The form of this evil is thought to be [[Ganon]], as {{HH}} specifically mentions that his flaming red hair is to signify a connection to him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= To give him some resemblance to Ganondorf, we didn't just make his hair red, we set it on fire.|book=HH|page= 17}}</ref> Therefore, it's likely Demise is in fact the original incarnation of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]' main villain.

==Biography==
===Background===
Demise was an eternal entity who had conquered time itself.<ref name="eternal></ref> When the [[Golden Goddesses]] departed from the world, they trusted their ultimate power, the [[Triforce]], in the hands of [[Hylia]] to protect it.<ref>{{Cite|Handed down by the gods of old, this power gave its holder the means to make any desire a reality. Such was the might of the ultimate power that the old ones placed it in the care of the goddess.|N/A|SS}}</ref> However, Demise, wanting the Triforce to make the world his own, gathered an army of monsters and together launched an attack against the people of the [[The Surface|Surface]], murdering them and causing misfortunes to get their hands on the power guarded by Hylia.<ref>{{Cite|One dark, fateful day, the earth cracked wide and malevolent forces rushed forth from the fissure. They mounted a brutal assault upon the surface people, driving the land into deep despair... They burnt forests to ash, choked the land's sweet springs, and murdered without hesitation. They did all this in their lust to take the ultimate power protected by Her Grace, the goddess.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In his thirst to make the world his own, Demise readied a massive army of monsters for war. He sought to take the Triforce for himself by force.|Zelda|SS}}</ref>

To prevent this great power from falling into the hands of Demise, Hylia and the five tribes who lived on the Surface joined forces and fought back against the Demon King and his forces in a war, which would end with Demise sealed away and peace restored to the land.<ref>{{Cite|With the humans safe, the goddess joined forces with the land dwellers and fought the evil forces, sealing them away. At last, peace was restored to the surface.|N/A|SS}}</ref> Due to the seal placed upon him, he assumes the monstrous form as "[[The Imprisoned]]" for the majority of the plot.<ref>{{Cite|Stripped of his true physical form by the seal that binds him, he takes the shape of an abomination. But even in his hideous state, he's more than capable of devouring this land if we allow him to do what he desires.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> 

===Resurrection===
[[Ghirahim]], who is actually the spirit of [[Demise's Sword]] (and main antagonist of the game), pursues [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] to steal her soul and thus resurrect Demise, who is his master.<ref>{{Cite|My master may have perished in this age, but in the past he lives yet! I'm taking the girl back through that gate to help me revive the demon king!|Ghirahim|SS}}</ref> Meanwhile, Demise attempts to break free of the seal by his own means in the form of "The Imprisoned", but is constantly repelled and re-sealed beneath the [[Sealed Grounds]] through the efforts of Link and [[Groose]]. When Link finally obtains the Triforce, he manages to kill Demise by driving the [[Statue of the Goddess]] from [[Skyloft]] into the Sealed Grounds as The Imprisoned awakens for the fourth time. However, Ghirahim kidnaps Zelda and takes her to the past through the [[Gate of Time]] in the [[Sealed Temple]], where he begins a ritual to revive his master.

While Link eventually manages to defeat Ghirahim, he is too late to stop the resurrection of Demise. The Imprisoned begins to absorb Princess Zelda's soul and eventually transforms into the Demon King himself. Ghirahim welcomes back his master,<ref>{{Cite|Welcome back to us, Master.|Ghirahim|SS}}</ref> but Demise pulls a dark sword out of Ghirahim and seals him within the weapon. Demise continues to absorb the soul of Zelda to gain divine power. He is impressed with Link's determination to protect the goddess and invites him to do battle.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... So you and that other human would stand before obliteration to aid the goddess, would you? How curious... The humans I've known are weak things. Hardly more than insects, shivering under rocks and ready to flee at the mere glimpse of me.|Demise|SS}}</ref><ref name= "Ise"/><ref>{{Cite|You grow more fascinating by the second, human. I never imagined I'd meet one of your kind who wished to stand against me in battle. Very well, then. I shall prepare a place for us where we will not be bothered by distractions. If you still have the courage to face me, seek me there.|Demise|SS}}</ref> To Demise's shock, the Hero of the Sky manages to ultimately defeat him via a mighty [[Down Thrust]] onto his chest with the [[Master Sword]] via a [[Fatal Blow]]. But in his dying throes, he warns Link that it is not over, and that an [[Ganon|incarnation of his hatred]] will be reborn as a being to pursue domination of the world, as well as those with the [[Princess Zelda|blood of the Goddess]] and the [[Link|spirit of the Hero]], in a non-ending cycle of rebirth (foreshadowing virtually all of the major events of the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|franchise]] according to the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Zelda Timeline]]- the [[Curse of Demise]]).<ref name="Curse"/><ref>Though this is not the end. My hate... never perishes. It is born anew in a cycle with no end! I will rise again! Those like you... Those who share the blood of the goddess and the spirit of the hero... They are eternally bound to this curse. An incarnation of my hatred shall ever follow your kind, dooming them to wander a blood-soaked sea of darkness for all time!- Demise, Skyward Sword</ref>Demise is then finally sealed away within the Master Sword, where his remains begin to ultimately decay.<ref>{{Cite|The last remnants of Demise are decaying slowly within the sword.|Impa|SS}}</ref>

Demise carries a cocky and overbearing temperament,<ref>{{Cite|The only question left is how long you will remain standing before I take your life. Try to keep it interesting for me, would you?|Demise|SS}}</ref> and will stop at nothing to prove his power to any who defy him.<ref name= "Ise">{{Cite|When I last walked this world, they did little more than scream and cling to their goddess, mewling and praying... Counting on her to protect them.|Demise|SS}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite|How amusing to think those cowards begot someone like you.|Demise|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can spend what little time your world has left cowering and crying, as befits your kind.|Demise|SS}}</ref> [[Fi]] describes Demise as "the source of all monsters," and states that he "has conquered time itself."<ref name="eternal">{{Cite|This eternal being has conquered time itself. It is the source of all monsters.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Demise bears a strong resemblance to [[Ganondorf]]: large muscular stature, fiery red hair, dark skin, and even his facial features are similar, and in the cutscene before the final battle with him, a variation of Ganon's leitmotif plays.

After Link enters the chosen battlefield through the portal in the past, Demise praises him for his courage,<ref>{{Cite|Ah, so you've decided to meet your end in battle after all. It pleases me greatly to see such misplaced valor, human.|Demise|SS}}</ref> but tells him that when he falls,<ref>{{Cite|Take a moment to appreciate your surroundings, for where we stand shall serve as your tomb for eternity.|Demise|SS}}</ref> he shall rule over everything in the world.<ref>{{Cite|And when you do fall, know that your world and everything in it is mine to dominate... Mine to subjugate... Mine to rule!|Demise|SS}}</ref> After Demise states that he will eventually acquire the Triforce and rule the world with it, the final battle begins.<ref>{{Cite|I will take the Triforce for my own... And the world shall be under my foot for eternity!|Demise|SS}}</ref>
[[File:Screenshot 34.png|thumb|319x319px|The final battle against Demise (second phase)]]
During the Final Boss duel, striking Demise through his guard can be difficult, as he will attempt to block every attack Link makes towards him.<ref>{{Cite|A series of attacks that can overwhelm Demise's guard is required, such as repeated slashing from various angles. However, vigilance is required. Any attack falling near Demise's sword has a 100% chance of being blocked.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Additionally, Link's [[Skyward Strike]] is unavailable, as the power of the Goddess is negated by Demise's personal battlefield.<ref>{{Cite|It is clear that Skyward Strikes cannot be called down to your sword because this place is under the magical influence of Demise. Attacks using Skyward Strike energy will not be possible here.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Link can quickly defend himself with a [[Shield Attack]] when Demise attacks with his Sword, then follow up with a vertical attack or [[Spin Attack]]. Eventually, Demise will be thrown backwards, seemingly defeated, but will then rise up and summon a storm. Demise can draw the [[lightning]] from the storm to charge his Sword with electricity. Link will now be damaged if he strikes the Sword, and Demise will cause twice as much damage as before, increasing the challenge. Demise is now able to perform his own variation of a Skyward Strike, after which he is forced to recharge his Sword by pointing it skyward. It is possible to defeat Demise by attacking him before the villain can recharge his blade, but it is easier to mimic Demise and point the Master Sword to the sky. This will charge the Master Sword with electricity from lightning bolts like his Sword, allowing Link to attack with a Skyward Strike and paralyze Demise, making him vulnerable to attack.<ref>{{Cite|Master, I see no reason why you cannot make use of the lightning strikes the same way Demise does. It is possible to store lightning in the Master Sword by raising your sword in the air as if you were readying a Skyward Strike. When lightning strikes in the distance and the sky lights up, quickly raise your sword skyward to call down the lightning.|Fi|SS}}</ref>[[File:DemiseDef.png|244x244px|thumb|right|Demise being ultimately defeated by Link via a full powered Fatal Blow]]Eventually, he will fall back again, allowing Link to finish him with a lightning-charged [[Fatal Blow]]. However, Demise will usually dodge the attack at first, and sometimes the second. If the young hero is quick enough to land the second attack or attempts to land the third, the battle ends with the villain defeated, and therefore completing the game.

===Sword===
[[File:SS Ghirahim Render 3.png|100px|left|Ghirahim's Final Form]]
Throughout Skyward Sword, Ghirahim pursues [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]] in attempt to revive Demise from his imprisonment. As [[Link]] progresses farther in the game, Ghirahim's appearance begins to gain dark marks in several places. In the final stretch, in order to fully revive Demise, Ghirahim takes on his true form which bears great resemblance to [[Fi]], strongly emphasizing that he is in fact the spirit of a sword. Ghirahim also briefly shapeshifts into Demise's Sword shortly after unveiling his sword spirit form in order to emphasize that he is in fact a weapon without mercy. When Demise is fully revived, he summons the sword from Ghirahim, his true form, and Ghirahim is sent into the sword. In the end, when Demise is defeated, the sword dissolves, likely taking Ghirahim with it.

The blade is significantly larger in comparison to the [[Master Sword]]. It is capable of sending Link flying backwards in one strike and when lifted in the air during the presence of a severe thunderstorm, it can absorb lightning bolts to charge up and send electric shocks when stricken with a metal object, as well as beams.

Demise's sword is almost completely black with a red aura surrounding it. The hilt bears resemblance to the Master Sword, with a red jewel as opposed to the Master Sword's golden one. The blade differs from the Master Sword in terms of shape and size, but it bears an upside-down [[Triforce]] emblem.

==Trivia==
*It is commonly thought that, despite elsewhere referring to {{Term|SS|Demise}} as male, [[Fi]] exclusively calls {{Term|SS|Demise}} an "it" while describing him to [[Link]].<ref>{{Cite|This eternal being has conquered time itself. It is the source of all monsters. According to tales passed down through generations, it appears differently in each epoch and to each person who lays eyes on it.|Fi|SS}}</ref> However, she uses this pronoun only when describing him as an abomination that takes different forms in each age, accentuating the fluidity of his true nature. For each subsequent analysis, she consistently addresses him with male pronouns.
*Due to the fact that Demise is technically responsible for much of the evil throughout [[Hyrule]]'s long history (alongside [[Null]]), along with being the original incarnation of [[Ganon]] and having a fearsome appearance, many fans view this character as one of the most threatening villains of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]], if not the overarching antagonist.
*{{Term|SS|Demise}} will stare at the [[Bug Net]] if it is brought out by Link, similar to the uses of the {{Term|Series|Bug Net|link}}, {{Term|Series|Empty Bottle|link}}, and {{Term|Series|Fishing Rod|link}} in previous games. As with {{Term|Series|Agahnim|link}}'s attacks in {{aLttP}}, {{Term|SS|Demise}}'s {{Term|SS|Skyward Strike|link}} can be reflected by the {{Term|SS|Bug Net}}.
*Demise, unlike most other characters, notably has various different names depending on regions. In the Japanese release, he is called '''Person of Demise'''. In the French and German localizations, he is referred to as '''Avatar of the Void''' and '''Death Bringer''', respectively. 
*Although the English version of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]'' clearly states that Ganon is Demise's reincarnation, the Japanese version only suggests a connection between the two while leaving it ambiguous as to their precise ties to each other.
*{{Term|SS|Demise}} bears a glowing "X"-shaped scar on his forehead, which may be a result of the {{Term|SS|Sealing Spike|link}}. It is located in the same area that his reincarnation, [[Ganondorf]], wears a forehead jewel. Also, in {{TP}}, both Ganon and his human counterpart, {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}}, have similar scars, which serve as weak points.
*{{Term|SS|Demise}} shares the title of "[[Demon King]]" with both {{Term|Series|Malladus|link}} and, of course, Ganon.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Demon King Malladus... I am the one who has freed you from your prison! Grant me the power I have dreamed of!|Byrne|ST}}</ref>
*Demise bares a strong resemblance to [https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Akuma Akuma] from [[Capcom]]'s ''[https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Street_Fighter_series Street Fighter]'' series of fighting games, specifically [https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Street_Fighter_V ''Street Fighter V''] and ''[https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Street_Fighter_6 6]'', as both are powerful beings with a visibly muscular stature with red eyes and a flaming hair, and have cold personalities intent on brutally taking down anything that gets in their way.
*Demise's ultimate defeat in {{SS}} bares a close similarity to Ganondorf's defeats in {{TWW}} and especially {{TP|-}}, as in both cases, Link achieves victory by a powerful [[Down Thrust]] onto the villain via a [[Parry Attack]] and [[Ending Blow]], retrospectively, where he ultimately dies (though in {{TWW|-}}, Ganondorf was struck at his head rather than on his chest).
*Two of {{Term|HWDE|Ganondorf|link}}'s {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon|link}} in {{HW}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Swords of Demise|link}} and the {{Term|HWDE|Trident of Demise|link}}, are named after and designed around {{Term|Series|Demise}}. On a similar note, {{Term|HWDE|Ganondorf}}'s design in the same game takes cues from {{Term|Series|Demise}}, such as the lengthy mane of hair and the waistcoat.
*Demise stands at the same height as Ganondorf, but appears even more muscular.
*The appearance of Demise's Sword mimics that of the [[Master Sword]], but with a darker look and an upside-down [[Triforce]] emblem on it.
**In the Namco fighting game ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'' ([[Nintendo GameCube]] version only), Link can wield the demonic [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge Soul Edge] in a [[Soul Edge (Complete)|shape resembling the Master Sword and Hylian Shield]]. In comparison, the sword is not as long but appears more grotesque.
*When compared to Ganon, due to his sheer hatred, it's possible that Demise is more inherently evil, as his goal was to wipe out all life, while the main antagonist of {{TLoZ|-}} series simply wanted to rule [[Hyrule]] (with the possible exception of {{TotK}}, where Ganondorf expressed a similar desire for genocide of all life like Demise, as part of his plans to remake Hyrule). Despite this, Ganon himself is no stranger to murdering people or his own minions with malicious intent.
**That being said, he may even have had less inherent evil than Null and [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora]], as he did not express any genuine interest in destroying all of existence, while Null in {{EoW}} sought to reduce everything back to the primordial void, and Majora in {{MM}} just wanted to eradicate everything for fun via crashing the [[Moon]] onto [[Termina]].
**It should also be noted that Demise seems to, at least to some extent, harbor respect for Link. *Demise may be inspired by Susanoo-no-Mikoto, a wrathful storm god in Shinto. Similarly to how Susanoo has a rivalry with his sister, Amaterasu, Demise has a rivalry with Hylia.
**His final speech in the Japanese version was largely derived from tenants of Japanese Buddhism, specifically the Shugendō sect of esoteric Buddhism.<ref name="Demise's Japanese speech" />
*Although Demise himself doesn't appear in {{BotW}} and ''Tears of The Kingdom'', the former game indirectly alludes to Demise's role in Ganondorf's constant revivals just before the [[Final Boss]] fight against [[Dark Beast Ganon]],<ref>{{Cite|<small>... Ganon...</small> Ganon... Ganon was born out of a dark past. He is a pure embodiment of the ancient evil that is reborn time and time again... Ganondorf as the Demon King is gained using a [[Secret Stone]], which amplifies one's innate abilities, presumably alluding to Demise in Ganondorf's case. On a similar note, his role in ''Tears of the Kingdom'' was shown to have a similar desire to committing omnicide that Demise had as a part of his plans to remake Hyrule.
**Similarly, Demise himself also does not appear in {{HW}} (not counting [[The Imprisoned]]), but he is alluded to by Ghirahim in the ending of the first Ganondorf mission, where the Demon Lord noted a resemblance to his former master in the [[Gerudo]] King (indirectly alluding to Ganondorf being Demise's reincarnation).<ref>{{Cite|Ganondorf reminds me of my lord, Demise...
