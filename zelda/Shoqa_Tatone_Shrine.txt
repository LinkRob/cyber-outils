The {{Term/Store|Shoqa Tatone Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shoqa Tatone Shrine}}<br/>A Modest Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Modest Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Shoqa Tatone Shrine}} is located on {{Term|BotW|Puffer Beach|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}. It is initially buried beneath the ground, though there is a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} and an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} nearby. Complicating matters, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} is being cuddled by {{Term|BotW|Loone|link}}, who has named it {{Term|BotW|Roscoe|link}} and is very affectionate towards it.<ref>{{Cite|Your skin is so smooth and gorgeous, my little Roscoe...|Loone|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Loone}} realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is watching and wants the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} insists that she would never give it up.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? The person watching looks like he wants something? Maybe he's after... *GASP* Is he after you?! Don't you worry, by sweet Roscoe! I would never ever EVER let you go! I'm head over heals for my special friend who's been around since ancient times!|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Loone}} almost immediately confesses to lying, saying what she really wants to see is {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Sorry, my sweet Roscoe, but I fibbed a little... The truth is...}} {{Big|I wanna see Guardians!}}|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She wants to see many {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, not just the rusted ones found on {{Term|BotW|Puffer Beach}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Flying ones}}! {{Color|BotW Red|Small ones}}! {{Color|BotW Red|Ones that walk around}}! I wanna see all the Guardians in all the world! Not the ones around here that have rusted away, either. I wanna see {{Big|fresh and lively Guardians!}}|Loone|BotW}}</ref> If she ever does get to see these {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} hints that she might leave Roscoe, but due to the danger, she doubts this will ever happen.<ref>{{Cite|If I ever do get to see them... I may just have to leave you, my friend. Oh, but don't you worry! Getting near a Guardian is too dangerous for me. {{Color|BotW Red|Flying ones}}, {{Color|BotW Red|small ones}}, {{Color|BotW Red|ones that walk}}... I'm afraid seeing Guardians like that is nothing more than a dream within a dream for me...|Loone|BotW}}</ref> This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Guardian Slideshow|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

For the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to take {{Plural|BotW|Picture|link}} of a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}},<ref>{{Cite|That image... That's a flying Guardian! Wow, I feel alive with terror just from gazing at its beautiful form!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}},<ref>{{Cite|A small Guardian! Awww, it looks so vicious for its tiny size! Adorable!!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A walking Guardian! I can't believe it! Nothing could be more dangerous! What an exhilaratingly scary sight!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> After showing {{Term|BotW|Loone}} the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask her for {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}, and {{Term|BotW|Loone}} will allow him to take it.<ref>{{Cite|[Can I have that orb?] Hm? Oh, you mean Roscoe? Sure, that weird sphere thing is all yours. Really, I'm over it.|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can then place the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} is the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}'s {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}.<ref>{{Cite|Loone, love of all things ancient, relinquished her orb once you showed her pictures of the three types of Guardians.<br/><br/>You've decided to go place it in the {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}}.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> This causes {{Term|BotW|Shoqa Tatone Shrine}} to emerge from the sand of {{Term|BotW|Puffer Beach}}, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Loone, lover of all things ancient, relinquished her orb once you showed her pictures of the three types of Guardians<br/><br/>When you placed the orb into the pedestal, an ancient shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shoqa Tatone|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shoqa Tatone. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Shoqa Tatone|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Shoqa Tatone Shrine}}, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}}, wielding a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Shield+|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear+|link}}, will emerge from below. To complete the trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III}}. 

Upon defeat, the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III}} will drop its weaponry, and the portcullis to continue towards the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} will open. {{Term|BotW|Shoqa Tatone}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}} for overcoming the trial, and they award him with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises.}}|Shoqa Tatone|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Shoqa Tatone}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Shoqa Tatone|BotW}}</ref>

Beyond the portcullis is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} which contains a {{Term|BotW|Royal Broadsword|link}}.
