{{Term/Store|Ring of Piercing|Rings of Piercing|Series, CoH}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Ring of Piercing}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Ring of Piercing}} are equipment that allows its user's {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}} to deal damage to enemies that would otherwise be protected by shields or heavy armor.<ref>{{Cite|Weapons can pierce through shields.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain minibosses.

==Trivia==
They share their name and function with the Ring of Piercing item from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
