{{Term/Store|Magda||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Magda|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==

=== ''Breath of the Wild'' ===
{{Term|BotW|Magda}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who resides on the {{Term|BotW|Floret Sandbar|link}}. Magda planted a maze of {{Plural|BotW|Flower|link}} surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Hila Rao Shrine|link}},<ref name="Lovely">{{Cite|Look! Aren't they lovely? I wanted to see how the shrine would look with flowers around it, and it's wonderful! Please, feel free to take a closer look. Just don't step on them!|Magda|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Planted">{{Cite|I planted flowers around the shrine. Please be careful where you step. You don't want to hurt them, do you?|Magda|BotW}}</ref> where she regularly tends to them. Because of her investment in her garden, she becomes upset if people step on or harm the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}}.<ref>{{Cite|Please take some time to really admire the flowers. I worked so hard to plant them...|Magda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You're not stepping on my flowers, are you?!|Magda|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Magda}}, she proclaims that she is pleased with how the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} looks now, and while she welcomes him to look at the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}}, she also warns him to be careful not to step on them.<ref name="Lovely"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} steps on the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}} before interacting with {{Term|BotW|Magda}}, she will yell at him to stop.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! What do you think you're doing?! {{Color|BotW Red|You must not harm the flowers}}!|Magda|BotW}}</ref> She then explains that she was the one who planted the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}}, and she tells him to be careful not to cause them harm.<ref name="Planted"/> After either of these exchanges, the "{{Term|BotW|Watch Out for the Flowers|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

The first time {{Term|BotW|Link}} steps on the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}}, {{Term|BotW|Magda}} will be angered, saying that they are screaming in pain.<ref>{{Cite|No! {{Color|BotW Red|You must not hurt the flowers}}! They are screaming out in pain!|Magda|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} disrupts the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}} a second time, she will yell at him to treat them carefully, as they are living beings.<ref>{{Cite|How many times do I have to say it?! {{Color|BotW Red|Please treat the flowers with care}}! They're living things, you know!|Magda|BotW}}</ref> If he does so a third time, {{Term|BotW|Magda}} will become despondent, saying that she has told him to stop so many times that she has lost count.<ref>{{Cite|So many times...|Magda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I've lost count of how many times I've told you... It's just so many now...|Magda|BotW}}</ref> She cannot comprehend how {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not understand.<ref>{{Cite|How do you not understand? Why don't you get it?|Magda|BotW}}</ref> Suddenly, she shouts in anger before taking off.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|ARGGG!! NOOOOOOO!!}}|Magda|BotW}}</ref> While running through her garden, {{Term|BotW|Magda}} rants at {{Term|BotW|Link}} about his trespasses before falling on her face.<ref>{{Cite|I worked day...after day...after day...to plant those flowers. Then you come and hurt them...over...and over...|Magda|BotW}}</ref> She then resolves to make {{Term|BotW|Link}} understand the "flowers' rage," at which point she attacks him.<ref>{{Cite|I will make you understand... {{Big|the flowers' rage!!!}}|Magda|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Magda}} attacks {{Term|BotW|Link}}, she will remove three {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} from his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Her attack will leave him with a minimum of a quarter of a {{Term|BotW|Heart}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has less than three {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} when she attacks, she will not cause any damage to him, instead knocking him out. Following this, the process of angering {{Term|BotW|Magda}} will start over.

After {{Term|BotW|Hila Rao Shrine}} has been activated, {{Term|BotW|Magda}} will notice that it has changed in color, believing this to be because it is pleased with her {{Plural|BotW|Flower}}.<ref>{{Cite|Look! Do you see? The shrine has changed to a pretty color! It must be pleased with my gorgeous flowers!|Magda|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to again, she will ask if he has been stepping on the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}} recently.<ref>{{Cite|You're not stepping on my flowers, are you?!|Magda|BotW}}</ref>

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
