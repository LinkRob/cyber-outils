==Nomenclature==
{{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest}} derives its name from ''master''. Its name is the third in a progression of names for the wooded areas surrounding {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}; the first being {{Term|BotW|Ginner Woods|link}} (''beginner'') and the second being {{Term|BotW|Midla Woods|link}} (''middle'').
