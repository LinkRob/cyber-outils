The {{Term/Store|Ordona Province||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a province adjacent to the Kingdom of [[Hyrule]] in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 278}}</ref> It is located at the southern end of the Hyrule map and is by far the smallest province in the game.

Ordona is not a part of the Kingdom of Hyrule,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, no, I don't believe he's from Hyrule proper at all. No, my understanding is that he's from the small neighboring province of Ordona.|Shad|TP}}</ref><ref name="Quote1">{{Cite|I was supposed to deliver something to the royal family of Hyrule the day after tomorrow... Yes, it was a task set to me by the mayor, but...would you go in my stead? You have...never been to Hyrule, right?|Rusl|TP}}</ref> though they do present gifts to Hyrule's [[Royal Family]].<ref>{{Cite|I have placed the sword that was to be our gift to Hyrule on the couch. If the mayor comes for it, please give it to him.|Rusl|TP}}</ref> Residents of Ordona are called "Ordonians".<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! An Ordonian! Hey, guy!|Coro|TP}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
[[File:TPHD Ordona Bridge.png|right|thumb|The bridge connecting Ordona to Faron]]
The Ordona Province consists of [[Ordon Village]], [[Ordon Ranch]], and [[Ordon Woods]]. It is connected by the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Bridge|link}} to [[Faron Woods]] in the [[Faron Province]], located just north of Ordon Woods. The province is watched over and protected by the [[Spirits of Light|Spirit of Light]] [[Ordona (Spirit)|Ordona]]. As a neighboring province to Faron, it is a primarily forested region. Its sole village relies mainly on ranching and agriculture, especially [[Ordon Goat|goat]]s and [[Ordon Pumpkin|pumpkin]]s.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Ordon goat cheese! This cheese is made from milk from Ordon goats.|N/A|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Ordon pumpkin! This pumpkin was grown in your hometown of Ordon.|N/A|TP}}</ref>

Link, a resident of Ordon Village, is tasked with delivering a [[Ordon Sword|sword]] to the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]].<ref name=Quote1/> However, while trying to retrieve his horse [[Epona]] from [[Ilia]] to make the trip, he is knocked unconscious by [[King Bulblin]] and his [[Bulblin]] minions when they invade the [[Spirit Spring]], all the while [[Ilia]] and the rest of the Ordon children — [[Malo]], [[Talo]], and {{Term|TPHD|Beth|link}} — are kidnapped. Link pursues after them to the entrance of the Faron Province, only to find it now covered in [[Twilight]], where he gets pulled in by a [[Shadow Beast]] and transformed into a [[Wolf Link|wolf]] by the power of the [[Triforce of Courage]]. He is then imprisoned at [[Hyrule Castle]] and escapes with the aid of [[Midna]].

After escaping his imprisonment from Hyrule Castle, Link returns to the Ordona Province still as a wolf. There he saves the Light Spirit Ordona from having its light stolen by a Shadow Beast,<ref>{{Cite|That black beast you slayed was a shadow being. It had come to seize the power of light I wield.|Ordona (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> thus saving the Ordona Province from also falling to Twilight. Ordona informs him that already Hyrule's other provinces had fallen to Twilight and the light stolen from their Light Spirits. The Light Spirit instructs Link to revive the other Light Spirits to put an end to [[Zant]]'s, the king of Twilight, conquest and spread of Twilight, thus beginning Link's quest.

During the ending credits, Link departs from the province, along with Epona, to go on another journey.
