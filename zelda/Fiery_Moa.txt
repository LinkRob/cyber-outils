{{Term/Store|Fiery Moa|Fiery Moas|Series, TAoL|plural}} are enemies in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 176}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TAoL|Fiery Moa}} only appear in {{Plural|TAoL|Palace|link}}{{Which}} and act quite differently to regular {{Plural|TAoL|Moa|link}}. Instead of swooping at {{Term|TAoL|Link|link}}, they will fly overhead and usually out of reach and float straight above his path, dropping {{Term|TAoL|Fire|link}} behind it as it does. The {{Term|TAoL|Fire}} will drop to the ground and burn for a while before disappearing, blocking {{Term|TAoL|Link}}'s path. {{Plural|TAoL|Fiery Moa}} can usually only be reached and destroyed by using the {{Term|TAoL|Jump|link}} spell.
