{{Term/Store|Sage|Sages|Series, TLoZ, TAoL, TP, TPHD, ST|plural}} are recurring characters in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 219,{{Exp Game|TLoZ}} 223,{{Exp Game|TAoL}} 277 {{Exp Game|TP}} & 24 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref> Serving as a bridge between the [[Sacred Realm]] and [[Hyrule]], guarding the [[Master Sword]]'s power and executing those who pose a high threat to Hyrule are included with their duties, leaving their purpose to best be described as protectors of the kingdom. This puts them among the most important of its [[Link|heroes]]' allies, however rarely without price. The Sages often only take on these roles upon being "awakened" during a time that they are required, their role predetermined through a spiritual lineage or bloodline connecting them to a past Sage. Throughout [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Hyrule's history]], Sages have been abducted and killed to prevent them from uniting their powers against evildoers. In such cases, only the Hero has what it takes to save them or find and awaken the new Sages to aid him in return.

There are currently 10 types of Sages known to exist. Sages of Fire, Water, and Spirit appear in {{OoT}}, {{TP}},<ref name="crest">{{Cite Book|quote=Each of the sages in {{OoT|-}} and {{TP|-}}, with the exception of Princess Zelda, their leader, is associated with a crest that shows their particular element: forest, fire, water, spirit, shadow, or light. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=25 }}</ref> and {{TotK}}. Sages of Light, Forest, and Shadow appear in {{OoT|-}} and {{TP|-}}.<ref name="crest"/> Sages of Wind appear in {{TWW}}<ref name="wind sage">{{Cite Book|quote=At the center is an atrium that connects the lower level to the second floor with help from the Sage of Wind, Makar. [...] Once Link defeats Molgera, Link uses the Wind Waker to conduct Makar, restoring true power to the Master Sword. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=153 }}</ref> and {{TotK|-}}. Sages of Earth are only known to appear in {{TWW|-}},<ref name="earth sage">{{Cite Book|quote=After returning to the Forsaken Fortress, Link travels to the Earth Temple on Headstone Island seeking to restore the Master Sword's ability to banish evil through the prayers of Medli, the Sage of Earth. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=152 }}</ref> and Sages of Lightning and Time are only known to appear in {{TotK|-}}. [[Princess Zelda]], who is the Sage of Time in {{TotK|-}}, has no known elemental crest associated with her in {{OoT|-}}.<ref name="crest"/>

{{OoT|-}} introduces seven Sages, which includes their leader, Princess Zelda herself. Six different Sages, with the same symbols on their clothing as those associated with six of {{OoT|-}}'s Sages, appear in {{TP|-}}.<ref name="crest"/> Two Sages appear in {{TWW|-}}, whose function is to take care of the Master Sword's [[Power to Repel Evil]].<ref name="wind sage"/><ref name="earth sage"/> {{TotK|-}} features six Sages led by [[King Rauru]], a [[Zonai]] and not a Sage himself. Zelda is one of the Sages. Their functions do not involve the Sacred Realm, but they do attempt to protect Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|There were those known as sages, who protected the kingdom of Hyrule from the Demon King.|Deciphered Slabs|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Rauru then came up with a plan to turn the tide of the war. He entrusted secret stones to warriors with exceptional abilities. They became sages and united in an effort to finally defeat the Demon King.|Mineru|TotK}}</ref> A group of seven Sages, none of whom are Princess Zelda, appear in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Seven Sages in {{ALBW|-}} all stand on pedestals marked by their initials rather than the traditional crests [...] Unlike other times, Zelda is not among these seven. |character= |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=25 }}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
The three {{Plural|TLoZ|Old Man|link}} who provide [[Link]] with [[Sword|Swords]] in the original ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' are actually {{Plural|Series|Sage}}. Despite this, they have no direct role for helping Link defeat [[Ganon]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=E|publisher=|page=219}}</ref>

==={{TAoL|-}}===
[[File:TAoL Sage Sprite.png|left|30px]]
In {{TAoL}}, one Sage keeps the [[Triforce of Courage]]. When Link enters the chamber of the [[Triforce]] at the end of the game, the Sage stands atop a pillar and summons [[Link's Shadow]] out of Link's body for a final battle. When Link defeats his dark counterpart, the Sage hands him the Triforce of Courage to help him awaken [[Princess Zelda]] from her deep slumber.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:Seven Wise Men.png|thumb|left|323x323px|The Sages, shown during the game's introduction]]
''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' introduces the Sages having major roles in the series. Long before the events of the game, the Seven Sages, known in-game as the '''Seven Wise Men''', joined forces with the [[Knights of Hyrule]] to seal Ganon away in the [[Sacred Realm]] and prevent him from taking over the [[Light World]]. 

Prior to Link's quest, [[Agahnim]], the late [[King of Hyrule|King]]'s right-hand man and now-servant of Ganon, used his power to find and capture the seven [[Maiden]]s, who themselves were descendants of the Seven Sages. While not as powerful as their ancestors, the Maidens had inherited their magical abilities, which would prove helpful for Link.<ref>{{Cite|Link, thanks to you, I was able to escape the clutches of the evil monsters. Princess Zelda was to be the seventh and final sacrifice, but she managed to telepathically send a message to Link, requesting him to rescue her.<ref>{{Cite|Help me... Please help me... On his quest to obtain the [[Master Sword]], Link also met [[Aginah]], yet another descendant of the Seven Sages, who like Sahasrahla, went into hiding. He informed the Hero of Legend about the [[Book of Mudora]], while the [[Lost Old Man|grandfather of one of the Maidens]] later gives him the [[Magic Mirror]] and details on the whereabouts and use of the [[Moon Pearl]].

Through all this, Link could find and claim the Blade of Evil's Bane, at which point Princess Zelda sent him a telepathic message once more that the [[Soldier (Enemy)|soldiers]] had found her.<ref>{{Cite|Link! All was not lost though, as Sahasrahla told Link that victory was still in reach if he could rescue all the Maidens, including Princess Zelda, obtain the [[Silver Arrow]]s, defeat Agahnim and Ganon himself, and claim back the [[Triforce]].<ref>{{Cite|Link, it is I, Sahasrahla. ==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the nature of the Sages is further elaborated on. Each Sage (bar the seventh) is established to have their own associated element, an accompanying symbol and appropriately themed temple. At times of peace, there are no Sages, only those who could become them if evil takes control over [[Hyrule]]. If that happens, the Sacred Realm sends out a call to them, awakening the destined ones as Sages.<ref>{{Cite|When evil rules all, an awakening voice from the Sacred Realm will call those destined to be Sages, who dwell in the five temples. One in a deep forest... One on a high mountain... One under a vast lake... One within the house of the dead... One inside a goddess of the sand... Together with the Hero of Time, the awakened ones will bind the evil and return the light of peace to the world... This is the legend of the temples passed down by my people, the Sheikah.|Sheik|OoT}}</ref> Usually, the Sages-to-be have no knowledge about their status until they are awakened. An exception to this is the seventh Sage, who is always a member of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]], keepers of much of Hyrule's history.

As revealed by [[Rauru]], the original Sages were the ones who built the [[Temple of Time]] to protect the [[Triforce]] against those who would use it for evil.<ref>{{Cite|The three goddesses hid the Triforce containing the power of the gods somewhere in Hyrule. The power to grant the wish of the one who holds the Triforce in his hands. If someone with a righteous heart makes a wish, it will lead Hyrule to a golden age of prosperity.... If someone with an evil mind has his wish granted, the world will be consumed by evil...That is what has been told.... So, the ancient Sages built the Temple of Time to protect the Triforce from evil ones.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> The Triforce was hidden in the [[Temple of Light]], the only Sage-temple to be built in the Sacred Realm. While each of the Sage-temples seems to grant access to it, only the Temple of Time is meant to do so for non-Sages. Therefore, it was protected with a seal controlled by the [[Master Sword]], which evil cannot touch, and the [[Door of Time]], which could not be opened unless one possessed the three [[Spiritual Stones]], the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} and knowledge of the "[[Song of Time]]."<ref>{{Cite|I am Rauru, one of the ancient Sages... Ages ago, we ancient Sages built the Temple of Time to protect the entrance to the Sacred Realm... This is the Chamber of Sages, inside the Temple of Light... The Temple of Light, situated in the very center of the Sacred Realm, is the last stronghold against Ganondorf's evil forces. The Master Sword--the evil-destroying sword that you pulled out of the Pedestal of Time--was the final key to the Sacred Realm.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref>
[[File:OoT3D Chamber of Sages.png|thumb|316x316px|The six Sages, as seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'']]
[[Ganondorf]] managed to bypass all this by manipulating [[Link]] into doing the work for him. After seizing the Triforce, specifically the [[Triforce of Power]], and corrupting the Sacred Realm, he caused that corruption to seep into the temples of the Sages and Hyrule itself, which became infested with all kinds of monsters,<ref>{{Cite|But, remember... When all are awakened, upon Rauru's advice, he, the bearer of the [[Triforce of Courage]], then returns to the Temple of Time, where he finds Sheik waiting. His mysterious ally revealed himself to be none other than [[Princess Zelda]], now an adult like himself, keeper of the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] and the seventh Sage - leader of them all. Although Zelda was happy to see Link again in person after hiding for 7 years, the reunion was cut short when Ganondorf came and abducted the princess, leaving Link with one final reason to take him head-on.

The six Sages helped Link first by creating a [[Rainbow Bridge|magical bridge]] for the Hero of Time to use to get to the entrance of [[Ganon's Castle]]. However, upon doing so, she [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|splits the present timeline into two]], resulting in ''Ocarina of Time''<nowiki/>'s universe being the Adult Timeline while the Hero of Time ends up in the alternate Child Timeline, leading to much different events between them.

In the Downfall Timeline according to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]'', if Link were to be [[Game Over|tragically defeated]] by Ganon, instead, the seven Sages, in spite of losing their beloved hero and the villain ultimately stealing the full Triforce, would still seal him away into the Evil Realm as a last resort, similar to the game's true ending.

The seven Sages include:

====Rauru====
[[File:OoT Rauru.png|left|211x211px]]
{{Main|Rauru}}
Rauru is an ancient [[Hylian]] and the '''Sage of Light'''. Unlike the others whom Link must awaken, he is not a new Sage. Rather, Rauru himself is one of the ancient Sages, and possibly the only remaining one. He seems to act as the leader in the absence of Princess Zelda, therefore second-in-command.

Rauru made his appearance after Link opened the [[Door of Time]] and drew the [[Master Sword]] from the Pedestal of Time. Recognizing the destined hero, he took his chance and kept Link sealed within the safety of the [[Temple of Light]] for the seven years he needed to mature. Upon Link's eventual awakening from suspend animation, he summarized the events that had taken place during his slumber and informed him of his role as the Hero of Time. Then, he instructed him to find and help the other Sages so that they could assist him in taking on Ganondorf, handing Link the [[Light Medallion]] to resume his quest. After that, it wasn't until all the five other Sages had been awakened that Rauru contacted Link again, telling him that the time of the final confrontation had come. He fulfilled his role as Sage during that final fight and was the only one of the group not to return to Hyrule once Ganon had been defeated, presumably opting to stay in the Sacred Realm. 

The wise owl [[Kaepora Gaebora]], who has kept track of Link since the start of his quest, is actually a form taken by Rauru.<ref name=Rauru_Gaebora/>
{{clear}}

====Saria====
[[File:OoT Saria Artwork.png|left|198x198px]]
{{Main|Saria}}
Saria is a [[Kokiri]] and the '''Sage of Forest'''. She is the second of the Sages who lend their power to the Hero of Time. She is Link's close friend during the events of his childhood in [[Kokiri Forest]]. Being a Kokiri, she does not grow up when Link becomes the Hero of Time, unlike [[Princess Ruto]], the Sage of Water, as well as [[Princess Zelda]], their leader.

Deciding that something had to be done about the evil emanating from the Forest Temple that brought danger to [[Kokiri Village]], she left to give it her best. When Link arrived in the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}} some time later, he defeated the monsters infesting it and thereby awakened Saria as Sage. She and Link had a conversation thereafter on their childhood friendship, after which Saria bestowed her powers on Link in the form of the [[Forest Medallion]]. When the time to face Ganondorf came, she fulfilled her role as Sage and could later be seen traveling to [[Death Mountain]] with the other Sages.
====Darunia====
[[File:OoT Darunia Artwork.png|left|207x207px]]
{{Main|Darunia}}
Darunia is a [[Goron]] and the '''Sage of Fire'''. He is the third of the Sages who lend their power to the Hero of Time. During Link's childhood, he saved his people from starvation and Darunia declared him his "Sworn Brother".

In the future years, Ganondorf wanted to make an example out of the Goron race by feeding them all to [[Volvagia]], an ancient dragon. Darunia was one of the few to escape this fate, but as the leader of his race, felt the obligation to go free his people who were locked up in the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}}. There, he met his Sworn Brother Link again after seven years. Although he is proud to see Link grown into a young man, he knew this wasn't the time for reunion. As such, he gave Link instructions to free the Gorons, while he would attempt to take on Volvagia. Link followed into the dragon's lair later and killed the beast with the [[Megaton Hammer]], thereby awakening Darunia as Sage. The two had a talk about their old brotherhood, followed by Darunia giving him the [[Fire Medallion]]. During the final stages of the Hero of Time's quest, he fulfilled his role as Sage and could later be seen traveling to Death Mountain with the other Sages.
====Princess Ruto====
[[File:Rutoadult.png|left|225x225px]]
{{Main|Princess Ruto}}
Ruto is a [[Zora]] and the '''Sage of Water'''. She is the fourth of the Sages who lend their power to the Hero of Time. During Link's childhood, he rescued her from death inside [[Lord Jabu-Jabu|Lord-Jabu-Jabu]], and she herself grew smitten on the young hero, and deemed him her husband when Link obtains the [[Spiritual Stone of Water|Zora's Sapphire]] from her. Like Link and Zelda, Princess Ruto herself grows into an adult when the former becomes the Hero of Time.

Like many races, the Zoras doesn't want Ganondorf as their king, and as revenge, they and their [[Zora's Domain|domain]] were locked away under a very thick ice by the King of Evil. After being freed from the [[Red Ice]] by Sheik, Ruto obeyed her obligations as princess and went to the {{Term|OoT3D|Water Temple|link}} to save her people. There she met her fiancé Link again after seven years, whom she is very happy to see him, but is aware it is not the time to talk about love, and requested to help her save Zora's Domain once again. Link lost track of her in the temple, but managed to find his own way to the [[Morpha|monster]] that kept the curse intact and destroys it. Ruto awakened as a Sage. But because of that, the Zora princess tells the Hero of Time although she still loves him dearly, their childhood engagement vows could not be kept at this point due to her duty. Instead, she gave him the [[Water Medallion]] as a symbol of her support and their love. When Link went to defeat Ganondorf, she fulfilled her role as Sage and could later be seen traveling to Death Mountain with the other Sages. However, since Zelda already sent Link into the Child Timeline by this point and therefore no longer exists in her universe, she is unfortunately unable to marry the Hero of Time.
====Impa====
[[File:OoT Impa Artwork.png|left|259x259px]]
{{Main|Impa}}
Impa is a [[Sheikah]] and the '''Sage of Shadow'''. She is the fifth of the Sages who lend their power to the Hero of Time. It is hinted that she, like Princess Zelda, was aware of her status as Sage before becoming one. During Link's childhood, she informs him that she is the guardian of Zelda.

When the [[Bongo Bongo|evil spirit]] she had locked away in the well of [[Kakariko Village]] years ago broke free, Impa immediately headed for the [[Shadow Temple]] to seal it away again. When Link followed after her and defeated the spirit for good, she awoke as the Sage of Shadow. She told Link her point of view on the day she and Princess Zelda fled Hyrule Castle and ensured him he'd do fine as a hero. Then she gave him the [[Shadow Medallion]], staying quiet until Link took up arms against Ganondorf himself; then she fulfilled her role as Sage and could later be seen traveling to Death Mountain with the other Sages.
====Nabooru====
[[File:OoT Nabooru Artwork.png|left|257x257px]]
{{Main|Nabooru}}
Nabooru is a [[Gerudo]] and the '''Sage of Spirit'''. She is the sixth of the Sages who lend their power to the Hero of Time. During Link's childhood, she and Link had a conversation of overthrowing Ganondorf, as they both hated the villain, and this made her trust the young hero to forfill this goal.

Nabooru tried to infiltrate the [[Spirit Temple]] in the past years to thwart Ganondorf's plans, but got captured and brainwashed by his [[Twinrova|surrogate mothers]] to be a servant of Ganondorf for seven years. Her mental imprisonment ended when Link defeated her [[Iron Knuckle]] form, but she was recaptured shortly thereafter. Link permanently freed her and awakened her as a Sage when he defeated Twinrova. For that, she gave him the [[Spirit Medallion]], but not before she complimented him on the results of seven years aging. At the battle with Ganondorf, she fulfilled her role as Sage and could later be seen traveling to Death Mountain with the other Sages.
====Princess Zelda====
[[File:OoT Adult Zelda Artwork.png|left|234x234px]]
{{Main|Princess Zelda}}
Zelda is a [[Hylian]], the possessor of the [[Triforce of Wisdom|Triforce of Wisdom,]] and the leader of the Sages. No known elemental crest is associated with her.<ref name="crest"/> Link during his childhood agreed to help the princess of Hyrule ultimately stop and defeat Ganondorf once and for all and save Hyrule, and of course the two gained a close friendship as with the other Sages. 

Under the disguise as [[Sheik]], Princess Zelda waited for Link at the [[Temple of Time]] to reveal herself as the seventh Sage, leader of them all, and speak with the Hero of Time about the final battle ahead. However, Ganondorf found her and abducted her to his [[Ganon's Castle|castle]]. Link, with the help of the six Sages, stormed the stronghold and freed her after a duel with the King of Evil, causing Ganondorf to change into his true form, Ganon. While Link took him on again, Zelda prepared for her important role as Sage from the sidelines. When Ganon ultimately fell by Link's hand, she united her powers with the rest of the Sages to lock him away into the Evil Realm. After that, she and the Hero of Time had a private conversation about all that had happened since they met, and Zelda revealed that she wished to use her powers as a Sage to return Link to his original time, so time itself could flow as it should've done.<ref name="TimeSage">{{Cite|Link, give the Ocarina to me... As a Sage, I can return you to your original time with it.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Goodbyes were made shortly thereafter.

==={{TotK|-}}===
The Six Sages in {{TotK}} are individuals bestowed with a Zonai [[Secret Stone]] to fight against the '''Demon King''' [[Ganon|Ganondorf]]. Following the assassination of [[Queen Sonia]] by Ganondorf to steal her Stone, [[King Rauru]] formed the group as a desperate attempt to rout his nemesis during the [[Imprisoning War]]. [[Ardi]] of the [[Gerudo]], [[Qia]] of the [[Zora]], [[Raphica]] of the [[Rito]], and [[Agraston]] of the [[Goron]] gathered at King Rauru's behest and were awoken as Sages by the Secret Stones. The four leaders joined [[Princess Zelda]] and [[Mineru]] in forming the Six Sages, the latter two having their stones prior to the incident.

Despite the Secret Stones' amplification of their powers, King Rauru and the Six Sages were unsuccessful in their attempt to defeat Ganondorf. King Rauru sacrificed himself to seal Ganondorf deep below Hyrule Castle while the Sages went their own ways to prepare for the Demon King's return. In light of Zelda's tales of the future, the Sages vowed to assist [[Link]] in slaying Ganondorf more than 10,000 years later. Four of the Sages returned to lead their races' homeland and hid their Secret Stones. The Sage of Spirit, Mineru, embedded her spirit into the [[Purah Pad]] and is stored within a Zonai Construct to wait for Link. Meanwhile, Princess Zelda transformed herself into the immortal [[Light Dragon]] to restore the [[Decayed Master Sword|broken Master Sword]] with her sacred power over centuries.

Long after the two [[Great Calamity|Great Calamities]] and the defeat of [[Calamity Ganon]], Link and Zelda finds a mummified Ganondorf with [[King Rauru's Right Arm|Rauru's right arm]] in the Imprisoning Chamber. The seal suddenly breaks and Ganondorf is reanimated in the two's presence. Link attempts to protect the Princess to no avail and the [[Gloom]] corrupts the Master Sword along with Link's right arm. Zelda is then sent back in time to become a Sage after obtaining Rauru's Secret Stone while Rauru's arm attaches itself to Link to save his life. After Link awakens on the Great Sky Island, he obtains the Purah Pad housing Mineru's spirit from a [[Construct|Zonai Construct]] and the Master Sword transports itself back in time. 

Returning to the surface, the Hero of the Wild reunites with his friends from the four races to solve the regional phenomena caused by Ganondorf. Throughout the journey, Link and his four trusted allies were beckoned by the deceased Sages to retrieve the Secret Stones from the hands of the monsters. The four are found to be descendants of the original Sages and inherits their vow to Princess Zelda. They awaken as the new Sages and pledge their powers to Link, allowing him to summon manifestations of them to fight alongside him. After the four's awakening, Mineru's spirit regains consciousness and tasks Link to construct a new body for her. She reveals Zelda's whereabouts and the past to Link after regaining her Secret Stone. Knowing the truth, Link seeks out the Tears and finds the restored Master Sword on the Light Dragon's head. With the Master Sword in hand, Link and the five Sages head to the depths to fight Ganondorf. Though they manage to defeat him, Ganondorf transforms himself into the Demon Dragon as a last resort. Sensing the transformation, the Light Dragon returns to finish her duty as a Sage and helps Link to defeat Ganondorf once and for all.

**Although [[Laruto]] and [[Fado (The Wind Waker)|Fado]] appear as ghosts, this is due to themselves being murdered by [[Ganondorf]] and their souls remain active until their successors replaced them.
*In {{BotW}}, each of the [[Divine Beast]]s are named after a Sage from earlier {{TLoZ|-}} titles. [[Divine Beast Vah Medoh|Medoh]] is named after [[Medli]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', [[Divine Beast Vah Ruta|Ruta]] is named after Ruto from ''Ocarina of Time'', [[Divine Beast Vah Rudania|Rudania]] is named after Darunia from {{OoT|-}} and [[Divine Beast Vah Naboris|Naboris]] is named after Nabooru from {{OoT|-}}.
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'', the [[Lord of the Mountain]], Satori, is said to be a reincarnation of a Sage.<ref>{{Cite|Legends say this holy creature is a reincarnation of a sage that died on the lands it now protects.|N/A|BotW}}</ref>
*As ''The Wind Waker'' takes place in the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Adult Timeline]], it is possible that Laruto and Fado, as well as Medli and [[Makar]], are descendants of Princess Ruto and Saria from ''Ocarina of Time'', respectively.
*As the [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|execution of Ganondorf]] takes place only a few years after Link the [[Hero of Time]] leaves [[Hyrule Castle]] in the events of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', it is unknown why Rauru was not present as one of the ancient Sages. The ones from ''Ocarina of Time'' are likely absent due to the fact that the story is in the Child Timeline, with them not being destined as such or are unaware of Link's existence.
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', the original [[Rito]], [[Goron]], [[Zora]], and [[Gerudo]] sages wear masks that resemble their respective region's [[Divine Beast]] in {{BotW|-}}. The Rito sage, for example, wears a [[Mask]] similar to the head of [[Divine Beast Vah Medoh|Vah Medoh]], and so forth.
**Largely because of the Gerudo's direct ties to Ganondorf's background, the Sage of Lightning is the only one of the Sages other than Mineru to give additional details about the Demon King beyond his arrival and his unfathomable power (more specifically, the Sage of Lightning states that Ganondorf had originally been their chieftain before he changed via his ruthless pursuit of power<ref>{{Cite|A great evil, the Demon King [Ganondorf], emerged. He attacked the innocent and sought the destruction of Hyrule. He was our chief...before he changed. His obsession over power turned him into a monster.|Sage of Lightning|TotK}}</ref>).
