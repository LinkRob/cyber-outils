{{Term/Store|Rabbit Beam|Rabbit Beams|Series|plural}} are enemies in {{ALttP}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP}} They appear commonly in later [[Dark World]] dungeons.

==Characteristics==
Rabbit Beams will wait in place, often hidden under objects such as [[Jar|jars]] or walkways until they detect [[Link]], upon which they will slowly home at him after remaining still for a few seconds. If they hit something else before reaching him, the magical beam will vanish, but if they make contact with the young hero, they will temporarily disable the effects of the [[Moon Pearl]] and turn him back into [[Bunny Link]]. Unable to fight, Link will be helpless for a short period of time until the curse wears off, or until he is hit by an enemy attack. Rabbit Beams will commonly appear alongside other enemies so as to leave Link vulnerable to immediate attack.

Rabbit Beams can be destroyed by using any of the three [[Magic Medallion]]s.

They also can be turned into a [[Fairy]] with a sprinkle of [[Magic Powder]].
