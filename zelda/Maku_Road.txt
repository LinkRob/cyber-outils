{{Term/Store|Maku Road||Series, OoA}},<ref>{{Cite|Maku Road|Map Screen|OoA}}</ref> also known as '''Maku Path''',<ref>{{Cite|Maku Path|Sign|OoA}}</ref> is a small cavern in {{OoA}}. It serves as the only available passage around the gate to reach the [[Maku Tree]].

==Features and Overview==
The [[Maku Tree]] is sealed off from the rest of [[Lynna City|Lynna]] with a large gate which can only be opened from the inside. The cave itself is a small pseudo-dungeon, featuring a few weak monsters and a handful of simple puzzles. It is [[Link]]'s first real trial in {{OoA|-}}.

Maku Road is essentially the equivalent of [[Hero's Cave]] found in {{OoS}}. In a [[Linked Game]], a more challenging version Hero's Cave will appear as a detour on Maku Road.
