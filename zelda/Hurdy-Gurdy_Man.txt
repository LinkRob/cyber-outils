The {{Term/Store|Hurdy-Gurdy Man||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} and returns to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man}} teaches him how to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} and gives him a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fitting two {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone pieces}} together is called {{Color|TMC Blue|Kinstone Fusion}}. If you get two pieces to fit perfectly, great happiness will come your way! I'll put a {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone piece}} in your {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone bag}} so you can give it a try. Anyone who's ready and willing to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}} will look like I do right now. When you see that look in their eyes, that's when you press {{Icon|TMC L}}! Use {{Icon|TMC Direction Pad}} to choose a {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone piece}}, and press A to try {{Color|TMC Blue|fusing it}}!|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref> Upon {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with him, a thorny {{Term|TMC|Tree|link}} containing a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} opens up in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}}.

The {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man}} can be found inside {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}} after he gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag}}, where he encourages {{Term|TMC|Link}} to search {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} for people who will {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with him.<ref>{{Cite|Just try fusing {{Color|TMC Blue|Kinstone pieces}} with lots of people. There are a lot of people out there looking for someone to fuse with. You'd be surprised to find who--and what--you can {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}} with! Yeah, you've got a lot of fusing in your future, pal...|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I recommend visiting {{Color|TMC Blue|places you've already been}}. You may then be able to fuse Kinstone pieces in those places, you know? You can get special items by fusing {{Color|TMC Blue|Kinstone pieces}}! Don't miss out on the opportunity!|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man}} will mention the arrival of the {{Plural|TMC|Oracle|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a {{Color|TMC Green|group of lovely young ladies}} visiting Hyrule... Maybe I'll get a chance to talk to them if I offer to fuse {{Color|TMC Blue|Kinstone pieces}}. Heh.|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref> Throughout the remainder of the game, he remains inside {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe}}, mentioning the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Do you know of the {{Color|TMC Green|Goron Cave}}? It's a tunnel that the {{Color|TMC Green|Gorons}} are digging. And if you can get all the way to the cave's end and place a Kinstone... Well, why don't I just let you try it for yourself!|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref> alluding to the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I hear you can fuse {{Color|TMC Blue|Kinstone pieces}} with the {{Color|TMC Green|ghost}} in the {{Color|TMC Green|Royal Crypt}}. Or at least so I hear. I'm too scared to find out for sure.|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref> and eventually assuring {{Term|TMC|Link}} and himself that happiness is the true reason for {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}, even in the face of {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}'s takeover of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule Castle has fallen to the darkness... I suppose this is no time to be fusing {{Color|TMC Blue|Kinstone pieces}}, is it? Nonsense! Maybe we need it now more than ever before! Fusing {{Color|TMC Blue|Kinstone pieces}} can bring people happiness! This is the true motive for fusing, my friend! Not Rupees, but happiness!|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref>

Outside of the initial {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}}, he is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure, I bet we can do this!|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref>
