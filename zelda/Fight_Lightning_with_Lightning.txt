{{Term/Store|Fight Lightning with Lightning||HWAoC}} is a level 48 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fight Lightning with Lightning|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Fight Lightning with Lightning}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Palm Fruit|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Sanke Carp|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Wildberry|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}}'s objective is to defeat 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. Most of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}}, which are placed in a line as the map largely made up of a single path. There are two small groups of {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Keese|link}} as well, along with three {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu|link}} found near the end of the line. At 50 KOs, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}}, who accompanies {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}}, apologizes for not being able to help enough as the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora|link}} is weak to electricity.<ref>{{Cite|Apologies. We Zora are so susceptible to electricity I was not able to help much.|Mipha|HWAoC}}</ref> {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} tells her that her apology is not needed, as it is her job as a chief to help those in need.<ref>{{Cite|Your apology is entirely unnecessary, It's my duty as a leader to help anyone in distress.}}</ref> Once 100 KOs is reached, a closed gate opens at the bottom of the map to reveal an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel}}, and the timer resets to five minutes. {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} states that the monster has to be defeated immediately, and so the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} defeats it.<ref>{{Cite|We can't let that happen. We must defeat this beast, here and now.|Mipha|HWAoC}}</ref>
