==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Bottomless Bog}} are quagmires of marshy water found in various locations throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} steps into a {{Term|BotW|Bottomless Bog}}, he will begin to sink. If he sinks too deep, he will become fully submerged and, if he has any hearts left, will reappear outside of the {{Term|BotW|Bottomless Bog|display=Bog}}.<ref name="Loading"/>

Enemies that fall into a {{Term|BotW|Bottomless Bog}} will die, although {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} can swim in these bogs. Similarly, any {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} that fall into a {{Term|BotW|Bottomless Bog}} will sink to the bottom and become inaccessible, unless they can be retrieved with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. Due to being a body of water, {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} will work on {{Plural|BotW|Bottomless Bog}}, providing one the means of safely traversing the marshy terrain.
