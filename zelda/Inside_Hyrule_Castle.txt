{{Term/Store|Inside Hyrule Castle||Series, ALBW}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon|link}} in {{ALBW}}. {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} must access the top half of {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule Castle}} in order to chase {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} who plans to capture {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda|link}}. The first portal to {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} is located on the top floor. The {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword|link}} is required to access the Castle, and its boss is {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}}.

==Entrance to the Castle==
{{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} goes into {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule Castle}} to capture Zelda and take her to {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}} to revive {{Term|ALBW|Ganon|link}}. To follow him, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must use the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword}} to break the magic {{Term|ALBW|Barrier|link}} around the castle.<ref>{{Cite|No doubt--that is the very blade of evil's bane [...] Strike down this nefarious barrier!|Sahasrahla|ALBW}}</ref> Because the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword}} is essential to entering the castle, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must also have acquired the three {{Term|ALBW|Pendants of Virtue|link}} in order to get the sword.

==Themes and Navigation==
The inside of {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule Castle}} is full of monsters summoned by {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}}. Most of the lower castle floors can be traversed by defeating all the {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} in each room to unlock the next door. The upper floors have enemies, but no door unlocking requirement. While there are a few {{Plural|ALBW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule Castle}}, this {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}} has no {{Plural|ALBW|Key|link}} or even a {{Term|ALBW|Big Key|link}}.

Upon entering the room where Zelda is held captive, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} realizes he is too late to save her as {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} turns her into a {{Term|ALBW|Portrait|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} then heads to the top of the castle where {{Term|ALBW|Link}} battles him. Upon losing, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} retreats to Zelda's room with seemingly nowhere to go. As {{Term|ALBW|Link}} enters, he finds that {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} has escaped {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}} through a strange crack in a wall, which is actually a portal to {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}. This is the first time {{Term|ALBW|Link}} enters the parallel world.
