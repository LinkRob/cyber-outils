{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Death Bug||Series, FPTRR}}}} is the {{Term|FPTRR|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine}} in {{FPTRR}}.<ref name="On-Screen"/>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|FPTRR|Death Bug}} is a large insectoid creature with a grotesque humanoid head and a body consisting of gold and green {{Term|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} crystals.

It has a variety of attacks, including the ability to shoot out energy balls and strike out with long vines. {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo|link}} aids {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} in this fight by defeating any larvae produced by Death Bug. It must be defeated by throwing multiple {{Plural|FPTRR|Tingle Bomb|link}} at the head atop its body. After being destroyed, it will yield 1,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} and the {{Term|FPTRR|Metal Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got 1,000 rupees!|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref>
