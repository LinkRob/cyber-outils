The {{Term/Store|Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Families of Hyrule|Series, TP, TPHD}},{{Exp Game|TP}}<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= HYRULE CASTLE<br/>Located next to the bustling Hyrule Castle Town, Hyrule Castle is the regal home of the Royal Family of Hyrule. |author= Nintendo |published= n.d. |retrieved= March 19, 2018 |url= https://www.zelda.com/twilight-princess-hd/map/ |title= Hyrule Map - The Legend of Zelda™: Twilight Princess HD for Wii U |site= Zelda.com |type= }}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Hyrule Royal Family|Hyrule Royal Families|TAoL}},{{Exp Game|TAoL}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 223 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref> is a long-running bloodline in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]. Although they make their first appearance in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', the Royal Family of Hyrule was established following the [[Zelda Timeline#Era of Chaos|Era of Chaos]], many years after the events of {{SS}}. Descended from the Goddess [[Hylia]], who was reborn as the first incarnation of [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], the Royal Family consists of numerous [[King of Hyrule|kings]] and [[Queen of Hyrule|queens]], a [[Prince of Hyrule]] and many princesses who share the title of Princess Zelda (some of which have also become queen). Keeping a close contact with the other leaders and races within the Kingdom of [[Hyrule]], they rule over the land with the most prominent authority.

Throughout their long history, the Royal Family of Hyrule passed down many powers and sacred objects, including the magical abilities that they inherited from their ancestor Hylia, the [[Light Force]], the [[Goddess's Harp]], the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] and the {{Term|Series|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}. Others things passed down within the family include the legend of the [[Sacred Realm]] and the [[Minish|Picori]]. They maintain contact with the sky beings known as the [[Oocca]], who are said to have helped in the early development of Hyrule, assisting in the construction of the {{Term|OoT|Temple of Time|link}} to protect the entrance to the Sacred Realm, the family watching over the temple until it falls into ruin. They also held [[Picori Blade]] in their possession between the {{Term|TMC|Minish Legend|link}} and the events of {{TMC}}, sponsoring a yearly sword-fighting tournament in which the winner was granted permission to touch the sword in a ceremony outside of [[Hyrule Castle]].

Members of the Royal Family live within the various incarnations of Hyrule Castle and are known to be buried within [[Hyrule Castle Graveyard]] and a [[Royal Tomb]] located in a [[Kakariko Village Graveyard|graveyard]] behind [[Kakariko Village]]. For many generations, they have been protected by their ancestor's chosen guardians: the mysterious [[Sheikah]] tribe. Their crest is a newer variation of the [[Goddess Crest]] used to represent the goddess [[Hylia]]. The crest depicts a [[Loftwing]], alone the symbol of Skyloft, and the sacred relic of the [[Golden Goddesses]]: the [[Triforce]].

[[Gustaf]], [[King Daltus]], and [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|King Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]] are often noted as important rulers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 15}}</ref>

Princess Zelda is by far the most prominent member of the Royal Family, as she serves as the series' deuteragonist and is known to be the bearer of the Triforce of Wisdom.

Although the main protagonist [[Link]] is not part of the Royal Family of Hyrule's bloodline, he is important to them due to his destiny of protecting Hyrule from the arch-villain [[Ganon]] by the Golden Goddesses in most games, serves as Princess Zelda's personal bodyguard, is the bearer of the [[Triforce of Courage]], and in a few cases, is even a [[Knight of Hyrule]], whom are elite servants to the family in general.

With the history of Hyrule splitting and taking [[Zelda Timeline#Split of the timeline|three different paths]] after the events of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', the historical appearances of the Royal Family of Hyrule also splits, giving them three different destinies over the course of the three histories.

==Mainline Appearances==
{{Lacking Sources}}
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, set during the Era of Decline that followed Hyrule's Golden Era, the Hylian Royal Family is responsible for guarding the [[Triforce of Wisdom]]. When a revived [[Ganon]] attacked, the young [[Princess Zelda]] of this era broke it into eight pieces and hid them, an act for which she was imprisoned by Ganon. Her loyal servant, an elderly [[Sheikah]] named {{Term|TLoZ|Impa|link}}, manages to escape imprisonment and request help from {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}}.

==={{TAoL|-}}===
In ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'', despite many generations having passed since the tragedy of the Sleeping Zelda that marked the end of Hyrule's Golden Era and the law that made is so that every princess thereafter must be called "Zelda". The only known person known to carry on knowledge of that legend is the elderly Impa from the previous game, who tells Link the legend and helps him awaken the ancient sleeping princess. The young hero goes on an adventure to successfully find the hidden [[Triforce of Courage]] and awaken the princess. 

==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:DeadKing.png|56px|left|The dead king shown in the introduction of {{ALttP|-}}|alt=The dead king shown in the introduction of A Link to the Past]][[File:ALttP The Return of the King Credits Scene.png|thumb|right|The King, the seven Maidens and four [[Soldier]]s in {{ALttP|-}}]]After a period of misfortune for the kingdom of Hyrule in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', the [[King of Hyrule]] (who makes his first in-game appearance) offered a reward to the one who could end these troubles. A mysterious wizard known as [[Agahnim]] soon arrived and ended those troubles with his sorcery, being declared a hero by the King and given the position to be his chief advisor. Agahnim used this position as a means to brainwash the soldiers of Hyrule and take over Hyrule Castle. He sent the descendants of the Seven Wise Men from the Imprisoning War into the Dark World, the last being the current Princess Zelda. During this time, he killed the King of Hyrule, reducing him to a skeleton. He is later revealed to be a pawn of [[Ganon]], who once again plans to take over the kingdom. When Link defeats him in the Dark World, he makes a wish upon the Triforce for it to restore Hyrule to its former condition, bringing the King of Hyrule back to life and spawning Hyrule's [[Zelda Timeline#Gold Era|Golden Era]].

=== ''Link's Awakening'' ===
No Royal Family member of Hyrule physically appears in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'', as it takes place at [[Koholint Island]]. However, Link makes a reference to Princess Zelda at the beginning when he mistakes a young girl named [[Marin]] for her. 

===''Ocarina of Time''===
[[File:OoT Adult Zelda Artwork.png|thumb|308x308px|Princess Zelda, an notable member of the Royal Family of Hyrule]]
{{OoT}} is set approximately 10 years after an unknown [[King of Hyrule]] unified the country during the [[Hyrulean Civil War]].<ref>{{Cite|Some time ago, before the King of Hyrule unified this country, there was a fierce war in our world.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> The [[Sheikah]], the [[Zora]], and the [[Goron]] races have sworn fealty to the Royal Family.<ref name="Grave">{{Cite|Here lie the souls of those who swore fealty to the Royal Family of Hyrule The Sheikah, guardians of the Royal Family and founders of Kakariko, watch over these spirits in their eternal slumber.|Tombstone|OoT}}</ref><ref name="Owl">{{Cite|An aquatic race, they are longtime allies of Hyrule's Royal Family. I heard that only the Royal Family of Hyrule can enter Zora's Domain...|Kaepora Gaebora|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Big Brother has shut himself up in his room saying, "I will wait in here for the Royal Family's messenger!"|Goron|OoT}}</ref> The Zora have formed an alliance with the Royal family, even allowing the flow of the river to stop for the King's messenger,<ref>{{Cite|The flow of this waterfall serves the King of Hyrule. When the King slumbers, so too do these falls.|N/A|OoT}}</ref> and [[Darunia]] considers the King to be his "Sworn Brother."<ref>{{Cite|Has Darunia, the big boss of the Gorons, really lost so much status to be treated like this by his Sworn Brother, the King?|Darunia|OoT}}</ref> Still ruling the kingdom but never seen, he dismisses the predictions of [[Princess Zelda#Ocarina of Time|his daughter]] about Hyrule being taken over by the [[Gerudo]] thief [[Ganon#Ocarina of Time|Ganondorf]] to be untrue. 

When the legendary [[Link#Ocarina of Time|Hero of Time]] awakens from his seven-year sleep in the [[Sacred Realm]], Hyrule Castle is long since destroyed, replaced by [[Ganon's Castle]], and Hyrule has been taken over by Ganondorf. With the exception of Princess Zelda, whether or not the king, or any other unseen members of the Royal Family at the time, were killed or went into hiding remains unknown.{{Note|In the ''Himekawa'' manga adaptation, however, it is heavily implied that Ganondorf had murdered the king as he told Zelda when cornering her and Impa that they shall "experience eternal sleep with [Zelda's] father in this burning castle."}}

Sometime prior to the events of the [[Nintendo 64]] game, the [[Composer Brothers]] of [[Kakariko Graveyard]] composed songs for the Royal Family of Hyrule. Also during their lifetime, their secret mission was to investigate the mysterious powers that the family had inherited. 

=== ''Majora's Mask'' ===
Despite not physically appearing, as ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' takes place in [[Termina]], Princess Zelda is shown in a flashback handing Link the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] upon retrieving it back from the [[Skull Kid]].

==={{OoA|-}} and {{OoS|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Oracle of Ages]]''/''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Seasons]]'', within the lands of [[Holodrum]] and [[Labrynna]], the Hylian Royal family has only a minimal role in the storyline. The same Princess Zelda from ''A Link to the Past'' does appear in the [[Linked Game|password-linked quest]], however. The inhabitants of the two lands seem to have no royal government in the present day, although [[Queen Ambi]] of Labrynna's past presides over the land. The royalty of Labrynna appear to have no direct connection to that of Hyrule, however.
==={{FS|-}}===
In {{FS}}, very little is known about the Royal Family other than all princesses bearing the title of 'Zelda' now watch over [[Four Sword Sanctuary]], where the sacred [[Four Sword]] now rests. 

==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:TWW Portrait.jpg|thumb|right|Tetra's ancestor and her courtiers]]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', sometime after the sealing of Ganon in ''Ocarina of Time'', he eventually broke of his seal and once again terrorized Hyrule. With the Hero of Time nowhere to be found, and the end of all hope for the land in sight, the [[Golden Goddesses|three goddesses who created the world]] ordered that the inhabitants of Hyrule move to the mountaintops. They sealed the land away and buried it beneath an ocean that came to be known as the [[Great Sea]]. Hundreds of years passed, but the blood of the Royal Family continued to live on, carrying the Triforce of Wisdom down its line. After [[Tetra's Mother|her mother]] passed away prior to the events of the game, the last remaining descendant of the Royal Family, [[Tetra]], was completely unaware of her heritage. The last King of Hyrule, [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]], reveals her true identity as a member of the family to her by uniting the two pieces of the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] that they both held.<ref>{{Cite|This necklace you wear is part of a sacred treasure called the Triforce of Wisdom, which has been passed down for many ages within the royal family of Hyrule.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> The king tells her that she is the last link in the dying bloodline and that Ganondorf must be defeated if the sealed Hyrule is to be saved.<ref>{{Cite|If he succeeds, my ancient kingdom under the sea will be turned into a land of shadow and despair...and so will the world you know above the waves.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> The King himself as the talking boat, [[King of Red Lions]], aids Link to help stop the Gerudo King.

Defeated once more, with the [[Master Sword]] plunged into his forehead, Ganondorf fails to take over Hyrule. Unfortunately, Daphnes had already made a wish on the completed Triforce to break the seal on Hyrule and wash it away beneath the ocean. He had also wished for Link, the Hero of Winds and successor of the Hero of Time, and Tetra, to have a hopeful future and find a new kingdom to call their own, leading to the events of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|Phantom Hourglass]]''. With this, Hyrule was flooded and Daphnes ultimately perished after bidding them farewell. The Royal Family's bloodline would continue on.

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, Princess Zelda is the only member seen, and precides over the seal of the [[Four Sword]] with the six [[Shrine Maidens]].

==={{TMC|-}}===
During the events of {{TMC}}, the [[Hylian]] Royal Family only has two known living members: [[King Daltus]] and the Princess Zelda of the time. The [[Royal Valley]] is said to be the place where the deceased members are buried. [[Gustaf]] is found here, along with unnamed members. The Princess and [[Link]] are childhood friends, while King Daltus is old friends with Link's relative, [[Smith]]. The Royal Family are the only ones who know the truth about the Picori.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the Princess Zelda of this game appears to be the only living member of the Royal Family of Hyrule, as seen in her flashback scene at the throne during [[Zant]]'s attack. During the events, Zant, the so-called "King of Twilight" working for Ganondorf, overpowers the knights of Hyrule Castle and gives Zelda a choice for the life or death of her kingdom. She chose the former, resulting in the kingdom being covered in [[Curtain of Twilight|twilight]]. The castle was seemingly destroyed after the battle with [[Dark Beast Ganon]], and Princess Zelda helps Link, the Hero of Twilight, combat the King of Evil on horseback. During the ending sequence, the throne room of Hyrule Castle is shown again in its full glory.

==={{PH|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|Phantom Hourglass]]'', set shortly after the events of {{TWW|-}}. Tetra, [[Tetra's Pirate Crew|her pirate crew]], and the Hero of Winds are still voyaging to find a new land that will succeed the kingdom of old.

==={{ST|-}}===
When Tetra and her pirate crew discover a new land, they meet the [[Lokomo]] known as [[Anjean]]. She entrusts Tetra with the [[Spirit Flute]] in return for her and her descendants vowing to protect the land, which came to be known as Hyrule, or "New Hyrule" in {{Wp|fanon}}. One hundred years later, in the events of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks|Spirit Tracks]]'', the Princess Zelda of the time, said to be the great-granddaughter of Tetra, is the only known living descendant of the Royal Family. 

The new Royal Family takes on a similar role to their ancestors, guarding a land called Hyrule and living in a Hyrule Castle with knights. [[Chancellor Cole]] serves as the royal adviser of the princess.

==={{SS|-}}===
Although the Royal Family of Hyrule is not yet established during the events of {{SS}}, their ancestor [[Hylian|Hylia]], reborn as the first Zelda, and her father [[Gaepora]] are present. Her descendants inherited her magical abilities that she had as the Goddess Hylia. The Royal Family of Hyrule is also implied to be the descendants of the [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|very first Link]], though unlike Princess Zelda's all other incarnations, Link's would only appear outside the Royal Family's bloodline.

==={{aLBW|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', Princess Zelda once again makes an appearance as the main figure of the Royal Family in this game. A past princess, the one from ''A Link to the Past'', is depicted on a painting portraying the history of Hyrule, along with [[Ganon]]'s rise and fall by Link the Hero of Legend.

==={{BotW|-}}===
[[File:BotW King Rhoam model.webp|left|thumb|280x280px|The King of Hyrule in ''Breath of the Wild'', King Rhoam]]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'', the Royal Family would be reduced to a single member, Princess Zelda, after the destruction the [[Great Calamity]] brought to the Kingdom and Royal family. The calamity killed King [[King Rhoam|Rhoam]]. Zelda's mother, an unnamed character, would die due to an illness when Zelda herself was very young. This faltered Zelda's ability to unlock and use her power of Light and Time. King Rhoam decided that it would be best to have protection while she studied on how to unlock her power for when the Great Calamity was prophesized to return. She visited all three springs of Power, Courage, and Wisdom but still couldn't unlock her power when the Calamity suddenly awoke and caught Hyrule off-guard. She returned to Hyrule Castle likely finding her father already dead. She then learned about the deaths of the four [[Champions]] she had chosen to pilot the divine beasts. She felt responsible but still couldn't unlock her power until Link was too weak to continue fighting. Her power unlocked and she was able to deactivate a guardian before it could kill them. Link then collapsed and Princess Zelda rushes to his side unsure of what to do until the Master Sword speaks to her and gives her instructions, she then commanded two Sheikah members to take Link to the Shrine of Resurrection on the Great Plateau to rest and recover while she held the Calamity at bay. After this time for 100 years, many assumed the princess was killed and the royal family was no more. It wasn't until [[Calamity Ganon]] was defeated that Zelda was proven be alive. She then became the sole leader of Hyrule.

Her actions in the past may very well be the reason why it was a tradition in the Royal Family to name your first born daughter "Zelda" as King Rhoam stated in his diary.

==Timeline Perspectives ==

=== Unified ===
Prior to the events of ''Ocarina of Time'', there was only one timeline containing the events of {{SS|-}}, {{TMC|-}}, and {{FS|-}}, called the Unified Timeline. The possible outcomes of whenever Link defeats his archenemy Ganon or not results in three different branches, known as Child, Adult, and Downfall Timelines.

=== Downfall ===
The Downfall Timeline is the timeline in which Link, the [[Hero of Time]], despite his best efforts, was [[Game Over|defeated]] by Ganon in the final battle. This turn of events created the timeline containing {{ALttP|-}}, {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}, {{LA|-}}, {{ALBW|-}}, {{TFH|-}}, ''Echoes of Wisdom'', ''The Legend of Zelda'', and {{TAoL|-}} in this order. Having defeated the legendary Hero of Time, Ganondorf now possessed the full [[Triforce]]. However, like in ''Ocarina of Time''<nowiki/>'s true ending, in spite of Link's tragic death, he was subsequently sealed within the [[Dark World]] by Princess Zelda and the other six awakened [[Sage|Sages]], taking the Triforce with him. During a short period of peace, various people began to enter the corrupted Sacred Realm, not to return. Evil began to flow forth from the seal, and so the King of Hyrule ordered [[Sage#A Link to the Past|Seven Sages]] to close the entrance to the realm once more. This event came to be known as the [[Imprisoning War]], where ''A Link to the Past'' takes place.
===The Golden Era===
[[File:TAoL King of Hyrule Artwork.png|thumb|The wise king who ruled with the Triforce|224x224px]]
In the backstory for {{TAoL}}, set during the [[Golden Era]] when the Kingdom of Hyrule was still unified, a [[King of Hyrule|wise king]] ruled over the land using the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Long ago, when Hyrule was one kingdom, a great ruler maintained the peace in Hyrule using the Triforce.|game= TAoL|page=6}}</ref> Unfortunately, this wise king passed away, and his son and heir could only inherit the Triforce in part, because the king did not believe he possessed the necessary qualities. So the king hid the Triforce of Courage away in the [[Great Palace]] until someone with special inborn qualities could find it.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=After the king died, the prince of the kingdom should have inherited everything, but he could only inherit the Triforce in part.|game= TAoL|page=6}}</ref><ref>{{Cite manual|quote=The king had left Power and Wisdom in the kingdom, but had hidden Courage, because only an individual with a strong character and a special inborn quality  could use it without disastrous results.|game= TAoL|page=8}}</ref> The young prince was swayed by the counsel of a corrupt wizard, who told him that his sister, Princess Zelda, was hiding the missing pieces. The prince interrogated the princess, but she would reveal nothing. In retaliation, the wizard cast a spell over Zelda, causing her to fall into an eternal sleep.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=The prince immediately questioned the princess, but she revealed nothing. The wizard threatened to send her into an eternal sleep if she did not talk.|game= TAoL|page=7}}</ref> The prince, stricken with guilt, issued a decree that all females born into the royal household were to be named 'Zelda' by law and not tradition in honor of his sister.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=So that this tragedy would never be forgotten, he ordered every female child born into the royal household to be given the name Zelda.|game= TAoL|page=8}}</ref>

This marked the end of the Golden Era and began the [[Era of Decline]], where Hyrule as a kingdom began to diminish in size and power over time.

=== Child Timeline ===
[[File:OoT Ending.png|thumb|225x225px|The ending of ''Ocarina of Time'', where Link warns Princess Zelda at Hyrule Castle]]
After having been sent back to his childhood permanently by the adult Princess Zelda of {{OoT|-}}, the Hero of Time began a new history in which Ganondorf never destroyed Hyrule Castle and failed to take over the kingdom for seven years. With the [[Triforce of Courage]] still in his possession, he warned the current King of Hyrule and the child Zelda of the tragic future that awaited the kingdom if nothing was to be done immediately. He gains the Royal Family's trust and subsequently spends an undisclosed period of time with Princess Zelda, after which she then gives him the Ocarina of Time as a farewell gift on his journey to look for his [[Fairy]] friend [[Navi]]. With knowledge of Ganondorf's actions, he was to be [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|executed]] by the six ancient Sages atop [[Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|Arbiter's Grounds]], a prison for Hyrule's most notorious criminals. Through unknown means, the [[Triforce of Power]] appeared on his hand, allowing him to avoid the execution and kill one of the sages (Sage of Water, to be exact). He was then banished to the [[Twilight Realm]] as a last resort.

This timeline contains the events of {{MM|-}}, {{TP|-}}, and {{FSA|-}}.

=== Adult Timeline ===
In this timeline, Ganon is defeated by the [[Link#Ocarina_of_Time|Hero of Time]] and is subsequently sealed within the [[Dark World]] by the [[Sage#Ocarina of Time|Seven Sages]]. This timeline contains the events of {{TWW|-}}, {{PH|-}}, and {{ST|-}}.
