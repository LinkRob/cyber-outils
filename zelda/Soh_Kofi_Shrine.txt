The {{Term/Store|Soh Kofi Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Soh Kofi Shrine}}<br/>A Minor Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Minor Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Soh Kofi Shrine}} is located west of the {{Term|BotW|Inogo Bridge|link}} on the north side of the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}} area. Before starting the "{{Term|BotW|Reach Zora's Domain|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Torfeau|link}} can be found standing next to the {{Term|BotW|Soh Kofi Shrine}} looking for a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am Tofeau of the Zora. I have been searching for a {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} like you for a very long time! My home, Zora's Domain, is in terrible danger. That is why {{Color|BotW Red|Prince Sidon}} sent me in search of a strong {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} warrior.|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Soh Kofi|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Soh Kofi. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Soh Kofi|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Inside the {{Term|Series|Soh Kofi Shrine}}, a small hallway leads to a doorway with a large square chamber. Inside the chamber, six destructible pillars stand in a two columns down the chamber, with two more on each side for a total of ten. There is a small pit in the center of the chamber, and when {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets close, it raises a platform carrying a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}}. At the far end of the chamber, another doorway stands blocked by a gate. The gate will only open after {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeats his opponent.

After defeating the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}}, stairs lead up the {{Term|BotW|Soh Kofi}}, who will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the {{Term|BotW|Soh Kofi Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Soh Kofi|BotW}}</ref> Soh Kofi then wishes Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Soh Kofi|BotW}}</ref>

Along the stairs leading to {{Term|BotW|Soh Kofi}}, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bow|link}} sits on a small platform.
