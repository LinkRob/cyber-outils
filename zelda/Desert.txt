The {{Term/Store|Desert||Series, CoH}} is a location in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Desert|Map|CoH}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|CoH|Desert|link}} is a region that consists of a dry, arid biome full of sand, blooming cacti, and dry rock, along with pieces of broken machinery scattered throughout the landscape. Though its placement in the world is random, it always appears near {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Village|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Ruins|link}}. Despite their similarities, the {{Term|CoH|Desert}} is always walled off from the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Desert|link}} by steep rocky terrain.

{{Plural|CoH|Sub-Boss}} such as {{Plural|CoH|Daira|link}}, {{Plural|CoH|Blue Daira|link}}, and {{Plural|CoH|Lanmola|link}} can appear in the {{Term|CoH|Desert}}.
