{{Term/Store|Treasure Hunter Cave|Treasure Hunter Caves|Series, ALBW|plural}} are locations in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW|publisher= Prima|page= 294}}</ref>{{Name Ref|ALBW}} They are hidden miniature {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon|link}} that are found scattered throughout {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}, each containing puzzles and treasures within.

==Features and Overview==
{{Plural|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave}} are optional miniature {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon}}, featuring elaborated puzzles and often platforming that makes use of {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|link}} and other {{Plural|ALBW|Item}}. Each {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Cave}} holds a large sum of {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}} that can be obtained after completing the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Cave}}'s challenges; some {{Plural|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Caves}} have additional {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} that can be collected en route to the main treasure.

The {{Plural|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Caves}} are named after the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter|link}}, who can be found at the entrance of most {{Plural|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Caves}}, pondering how to solve the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Cave}}'s puzzles and reach its treasure.<ref>{{Cite|Having a tough time here, though. If only I could manipulate sand and wind, then I... Well, let's just say I'd be able to secure myself a nice little piece of treasure here...|Treasure Hunter|ALBW}}</ref> One {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave}} in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}} instead features the {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon Bro|link}}.
