{{Term/Store|Animal|Animals|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are recurrent creatures in {{TLoZ|Series}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|If you run into any trouble, try}} {{Color|TPHD Red|talking}} {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|to the}} {{Color|TPHD Red|animals}}{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|.}}|Squirrel|TPHD}}</ref> comprising of many different species and orders. Unlike most fictional creatures associated with the franchise, animals in the series take on numerous forms and roles; most Animals are closely based on their real-world counterparts and frequently appear as wildlife, while others may be anthropomorphic and are sentient, such as those that live in the [[Animal Village]] in {{LA}}. Most Animals are nonviolent, though some will attack if provoked, while others are helpful to [[Link]].

==Common Animals==
===Birds===
{{Main|Bird}}
Birds appear in a number of ''Zelda'' games. Most birds in the series are small, but also appear in a wide variety of species. Notable species include Herons, [[Duck]]s, [[Hawk]]s, [[Owl]]s, Pheasants, and [[Seagull]]s. As well as simply appearing as wildlife, some birds also fulfill other roles that are often helpful to Link, such as transporting him by flight to other areas or providing guidance. There are also a number of  enemies in {{TLoZ|-}} series that are closely based on real-world birds, such as [[Crow]]s, [[Guay]]s, [[Kargarok]]s, [[Takkuri]], and [[Vulture]]s, to name a few.

[[Cucco]]s are chicken-like fowl that have frequently appeared throughout the series. In certain games, particularly in 3D games, Link is able to use Cuccos to glide from ledge to ledge if he holds one above his head and leaps from a high place, allowing him to access hidden areas. Cuccos are also infamously known for their tendency to [[Cucco#Cucco's Revenge Squad|attack]] Link in groups if he repeatedly tries to harm them.

[[Loftwing]]s are a species of bird unique to {{TLoZ|-}} series, appearing in {{SS}}. These large birds serve as the main transportation for the residents of [[Skyloft]]. Each resident of the floating isle owns their own bird.

===Cats===
{{Main|Cat}}
Cats appear in a few ''Zelda'' games, most often within town settlements where they are either pets of the civilians or strays without an owner. Cats are generally friendly towards Link, even assisting him whenever he possesses the ability to talk to one. They are non-aggressive animals that will usually not attack Link in most games, with the exception of some in {{TMC}} when he is [[Minish]]-sized.

===Dogs===
:''Main articles: [[Dog]] and [[Mutt]]''
Dogs, known as Mutts in {{LA}}, appear in a few ''Zelda'' games, usually within town or village settlements and as the pets of the civilians. Dogs are often shown to be affectionate animals who act friendly to Link, even sometimes following him. Dogs will usually not attack Link unless when provoked, such as those in {{LA|-}} when attacked, and if Link happens to be a [[Deku Link|Deku]] in {{MM}}.

===Fish===
{{Main|Fish}}
Fish are aquatic animals that live in water, either in fresh or salt water. They have served a multitude of roles throughout the series. In many games, fish appear as items that may either be traded, sold or used to forward Link's main quest or a side-quest. Some games also feature a [[Fishing]] mini-game where Link may fish for several unique species of fish using a [[Fishing Rod]], either at [[Fishing Hole]]s or at sea in the [[World of the Ocean King]]. Enemies based on fish have also appeared, including [[Gyorg]]s, [[Piranha]]s and [[Skullfish]], to name a few.

[[Seahorse]]s are a species of fish that appear in {{MM|-}}. This fish is able to converse with Link, and begs that he reunites the separated pair.

===Frogs===
{{Main|Frog}}
Frogs are amphibians that are usually found in rivers and ponds. Frogs most often fulfill a role as singing as part of a choir, such as the frog choir led by [[Mamu]], the [[Five Fabulous Frogs]], and [[Frog Choir|Don Gero's Frog Choir]]. These choirs are either related to forwarding Link's quest or are part of an optional side-quest. Enemies based on Frogs or Toads have also appeared in the series, including [[Gekko]], [[Toadpoli]]s, and the [[Deku Toad]].

===Horses===
{{Main|Horse}}
Horses have appeared throughout the ''Zelda'' franchise in several games and other media. In the games, horses most commonly serve as mounts that may be ridden to travel faster or reach otherwise inaccessible places. [[Epona]] is a notable and reoccurring horse in the series who frequently appears as Link's steed and companion. [[Princess Zelda]] and [[Ganondorf]] also have one in a few games.

===Insects===
{{Main|Bug}}
Insects, also known as Bugs, appear in many ''Zelda'' games in a myriad of roles. Numerous orders and species of insects have appeared, with notable ones including [[Bee]]s, [[Butterfly|Butterflies]], [[Dragonfly|Dragonflies]], and [[Firefly|Fireflies]]. Most insects in the series appear as collectible items, which may be hunted to exchange, sell, or use to craft other items. Some insects are collected as part of a side-quest, such as the [[Golden Bug]]s of {{TP}}. Insects have also commonly appeared as hostile enemies in the series as well, with [[Mothula]] being a notable example.

===Monkeys===
{{Main|Monkey}}
Monkeys appear as helpful animals that come to assist Link in some way, most often by opening a path for him to an otherwise inaccessible area. Some monkeys require payment in exchange for their help, such as the case with [[Kiki]], who either asks for [[Rupee]]s or [[Bananas]]. Most monkeys in the games possess the ability to speak and converse with Link, with the exception of {{TP|-}}. {{LA|-}} also features a hostile [[Monkey (Enemy)|Monkey]] enemy, who appears only in [[Toronbo Shores]].

===Porcine===
:''Main articles: [[Pig]] and [[Boar]]''
Pigs and Boars appear only in a very few ''Zelda'' games. Pigs first appear in {{TWW}}, which are kept as both livestock and pets by the islanders. These animals fulfill a role similar to that of Cuccos, as Pigs are normally harmless but will attack if Link antagonizes them. Boars are wild porcine that appear as wildlife in {{BotW}}.

Creatures closely based on real-world boars have also appeared in the series. [[Bullbo]]s are mounts that appear in {{TP|-}} that are used by [[Bulblin]]s; once riderless, they can be ridden by Link. In {{ST}}, Bullbos appear only as enemies. [[Moink]]s are another boar-like animal that has cow spots. This animal is normally docile, but attacks when provoked.

[[Ganon]], the main antagonist of the franchise, although not technically a boar, as he is in fact a [[Demon]], resembles such an anthropomorphic animal in most games he appears in.

===Rabbits===
{{Main|Rabbit}}
Rabbits appear in a couple games under different roles. Rabbits most commonly appear as wildlife, but have also appeared as anthropomorphic animals, namely those that live in the [[Animal Village]] in {{LA|-}} and in the land of [[Labrynna]] in {{OoA}}. In {{ST|-}}, they are wild animals that Link must catch as part of a side-quest. In {{ALttP}}, Link is also transformed into a [[Bunny Link|Bunny]] when he enters the [[Dark World]] without the [[Moon Pearl]]'s protection, or when exposed to [[Rabbit Beam]]s.

==Animal Enemies==
Numerous enemies based on real-world Animals have also appeared throughout {{TLoZ|-}} series, with some closely adopting the behaviors and likeness of their real-world counterparts, and others only sharing physical similarities. Some enemies with Animal-like traits include:

*[[Crow]]s, monsters similar to their real-life counterpart.
*[[Guay]]s, monsters that resemble crows.
*[[Keese]], monsters that closely resemble bats.
*[[Octorok]]s, monsters that resemble octopi with large snouts.
*[[Piranha]]s, monsters closely based on their namesake
*[[Rat]]s, monsters closely based on their namesake.
*[[Real Bombchu]], mouse-like monsters that either use [[Bomb]]s to attack or are explosives themselves like the [[Bombchu|item counterpart]].
*[[Rope]]s, monsters that closely resemble snakes.
*[[Sand Crab]]s, monsters that resemble crustaceans. 
*[[Skulltula]]s and [[Walltula]]s, monsters that resemble spiders.
*[[Vulture]]s, monsters similar to their real-life counterpart.
*[[Wolfos]] and [[White Wolfos]], monsters that closely resemble wolves.
