The {{Term/Store|Sun Fish||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am Manbo, child of the Sun Fish! Have you got an Ocarina?|Manbo|LA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am Manbo, child of the Sun Fish! Have you got an Ocarina?|Manbo|LADX}}</ref><ref name="Manbo"/>

==Biography==
The {{Term|LANS|Sun Fish}} is mentioned only in passing by {{Term|LANS|Manbo|link}}, who claims to be their son.<ref name="Manbo"/> The {{Term|LANS|Sun Fish}} may be a {{Term|LANS|Deity|link}} or otherwise influential figure, as indicated by how {{Term|LANS|Manbo}} feels their relationship is notable enough to be stated upon meeting {{Term|LANS|Link|link}}.

In a rumored interview with [[Yoshiaki Koizumi]] and [[Kensuke Tanabe]] that took place sometime after the original game's initial release, [[Kensuke Tanabe]] admitted he wanted to make a small note somewhere in the game (most likely during [[Manbo]]’s encounter), that the [[Sun Fish]] was going to be remarked as "the winds beloved" implying that the [[Wind Fish]] and [[Sun Fish]] were the indeed the parents of [[Manbo]]. This is all speculation however that has yet to be approved nor confirmed by official sources.
