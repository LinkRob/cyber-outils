{{Term/Store|Grayblade||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Grayblade}} is the youngest member of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}. He lives in a cave on the eastern side of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}, and teaches {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} the {{Term|TMC|Roll Attack|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You will learn the amazing {{Color|TMC Red|Roll Attack}}!!|Grayblade|TMC}}</ref> His dojo can only be entered once {{Term|TMC|Link}} can split into two using the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}}. A {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} can be found inside the dojo.

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Grayblade}} to open a waterfall in {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}, revealing {{Term|TMC|Scarblade|link}}'s dojo.
