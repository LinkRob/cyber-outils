{{Term/Store|Bomskit|Bomskits|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Bomskits are small, insect-like creatures. They pose no significant threat to [[Link]], however when provoked, they will start laying their explosive eggs, which they will do so when chased on foot. They can easily be taken out with a projectile weapon such as the [[Bow]]. Their [[Bait#Worms|Worm]]-infested remains can be scooped up into a [[Bottle]] as fishing [[Bait]]. They are commonly found in [[Hyrule Field]].
