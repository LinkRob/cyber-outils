{{Term/Store|Misha||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276}}</ref>

==Biography==
Misha, alongside {{Term|TP|Kili|link}} and {{Term|TP|Hannah|link}}, stand outside of the [[STAR Game]] tent in [[Castle Town]]. Although initially enamoured with {{Term|TP|Purlo|link}}, if [[Link]] beats the STAR Game, the girls will transfer their affections to him, becoming his fans and following him around whenever he gets close. The three of them will watch {{Term|TP|Link}}'s performance during {{Term|TP|STAR Game|display=STAR Games}} and if spoken to, will debate which of them is his biggest fan. If he comes up and surprises them in the street, the trio will cheer,<ref>{{Cite|EEEEEEK! It's HIM!|Kili|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|EEEEEEK! It's HIM!|Hannah|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|EEEEEEK! It's HIM!|Misha|TP}}</ref> create three [[Heart]]s, then run away into the throng of Castle Town.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TPHD|publisher= Prima|page= 270}}</ref> 

==Trivia==
*Misha's outfit, along with those of Kili and Hannah, are likely a reference to the three [[Golden Goddesses]]. Misha is reminiscent of [[Din (Goddess)|Din]].
