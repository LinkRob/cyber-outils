They inadvertently cause {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra}} to fall into the {{Term|TWWHD|Forest of Fairies|link}} by striking the {{Term|TWWHD|Helmaroc King}} in the face with a {{Term|TWWHD|Cannonball}}.

{{Plural|TWWHD|Cannonball}} are also used during the {{Term|TWWHD|Squid-Hunt|link}} {{Term|TWWHD|Mini-Game|link}} and the {{Term|TWWHD|Cannon Minigame|link}}. In both {{Plural|TWWHD|Mini-Game}}, they are represented with icons of {{Plural|TWWHD|Bomb|link}} with skulls.
