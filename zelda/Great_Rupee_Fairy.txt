{{Term/Store|Great Rupee Fairy||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 300}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|ALBW|Great Rupee Fairy}} is found in [[Lorule]], in a cave behind the [[Bomb Flower Store]]. Once [[Link]] has access to the [[Big Bomb Flower]], the entrance to her fountain can be opened by blowing up the large rock in front of it.

When Link first enters the fountain, he can throw in either 50 or 200 Rupees. By doing so, the Great Rupee Fairy will restore Link's Hearts and release some Fairies.<ref>{{Cite|A small token of gratitude.|Great Rupee Fairy|ALBW}}</ref> After throwing in a total of 3,000 Rupees, she will grow back to her normal size and reward Link with a [[Bottle]].<ref>{{Cite|Let me give you this bottle. May it prove useful in your travels.|Great Rupee Fairy|ALBW}}</ref> Link will no longer be able to make donations to the fountain after he is given the Bottle, and therefore will no longer be able to see the Great Rupee Fairy. However, Fairies can be obtained in her fountain upon returning to the cave.
