'''Manaka Kataoka''' is a composer at [[Nintendo]].

==Biography==
{{Stub}}
Manaka Kataoka, née Tominaga ({{Romanize|片岡 真央|Kataoka Manaka}}, maiden name {{Romanize|富永 真央|Tominaga Manaka}}) is a video game composer who has been working with Nintendo since she joined in 2006. Her first game was {{Wp|Wii Fit}}, and she has gained more notability as the main composer of {{BotW}}.
