==Location and Uses==
[[File:Beetle (M).png|left]]
[[File:Beetle (F).png|left|47px]]
The male Beetle can be found on a tree in the south of {{Term|TP|Hyrule Field|link}}. The female Beetle can be found on a wall in the northwest of Hyrule Field. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].
