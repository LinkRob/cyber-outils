{{Term/Store|Angel Peak||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Angel Peak'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Angel Peak}} is a part of {{Term|BotW|Mount Floria|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. A trail on its western side connects it to the {{Term|BotW|Jia Highlands|link}}. In addition, an abandoned campsite can be found on the {{Term|BotW|Angel Peak|display=Peak}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}}.
