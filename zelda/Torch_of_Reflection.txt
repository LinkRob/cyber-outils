{{Term/Store|Torch of Reflection|Torches of Reflection|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.{{Name Ref|CoH}}

==Location and Uses==
Torches of Reflection are a type of {{Term|CoH|Torch|link}} that can protect {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} from incoming enemy projectiles and reflect them back from whence they came, dealing damage to any enemies in their line of fire.<ref>{{Cite|Reflects all projectiles, at the cost of some durability. Can burn stumps and light up braziers.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> All reflected projectiles also retain their effects, such as ice projectiles freezing water as they move over it and electric projectiles stunning enemies once contact is made. Like other torches, they can also light up dark areas and burn stumps, but at the cost of durability unless the {{Term|CoH|Glass Ring of Preservation|link}} is equipped.

They can be found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}}. After experiencing a {{Term|CoH|Game Over|link}}, they can also be bought from the {{Term|CoH|Fate|link}}'s shop which sells them for 2 {{Plural|CoH|Diamond|link}}.
