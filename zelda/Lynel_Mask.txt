==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Lynel Mask is a piece of [[Armor]] that can be purchased from [[Kilton]] at his [[Fang and Bone]] shop after freeing three [[Divine Beast]]s. It cannot be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], nor can it be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. When worn, [[Link]] can temporarily blend in with [[Lynel]]s, which will attack him regardless after a few seconds.<ref name="Inventory"/>
