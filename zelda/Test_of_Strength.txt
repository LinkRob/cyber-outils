{{Term/Store|Test of Strength||Series, TotK}} is a {{Term|TotK|Mini-Game|link}} in {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heh, I made myself quite the tower, eh? Just look at that. And! I put that bell right on top. Why, you ask? Because I thought up a great new game—a <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">test of strength</font>!|Strade|TotK}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The Test of Strength can be found at the small mountain just next of the {{Term|TotK|Coliseum Ruins|link}}, southwest. It is located on top of the mountain just overlooking the {{Term|TotK|Outskirt Stable|link}}.

There is a {{Term|TotK|Goron|link}} named Strade who hosts the minigame. '''<MISSING>''' It cost 30 {{Plural|TotK|Rupee|link}} to play.

The objective is to hit the bell on top of the tower as hard as possible.<ref>{{Cite|Yup! Your goal is to make the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">bell</font> at the top of this here tower ring as loud as ya can.|Strade|TotK}}</ref> There is no time limit, but Link must stay within the play area lest he forfeit the game.{{Verify}} Link is allowed to use whatever he can use to ring the bell.<ref>{{Cite|Throw something at it with as much force as you can. Do that, and the bell will answer.|Strade|TotK}}</ref> There are several {{Term|TotK|Zonai Devices|link}} and objects next to the tower that Link may use<ref>{{Cite|You're welcome to all the stuff over there. Some of it's left over from when I built the tower.|Strade|TotK}}</ref> to build a contraption capable of striking the bell. The game ends once the bell is struck.<ref>{{Cite|Once you ring the bell, the game ends. And you only get one ring per round.|Strade|TotK}}</ref>

The intensity of the ring is determined by the speed and weight of the object that struck the bell, measured in "mega-ding scale." The higher the speed and weight of the striking object, the louder the bell rings.<ref>{{Cite|The trick to really ring it is to throw somethin' real <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">heavy</font>, real <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">fast</font>.|Strade|TotK}}</ref> If the bell rings at least over 75 dings,<!--No prize under 56 mega-dings--> Link receives a [[Jewel]] as a reward.
