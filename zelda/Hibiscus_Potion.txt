The {{Term/Store|Hibiscus Potion||Series, CoH}} is an {{Term|CoH|Item|link}} in {{CoH}}.<ref name="Obtain"/>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|CoH|Hibiscus Potion}} is a {{Term|CoH|Potion|link}} that can awaken sleeping people.<ref name="Obtain"/> If {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} enters either {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}'s {{Plural|CoH|Portal (Object)|link}}, the other hero will be found sleeping in a house at the north end of {{Term|CoH|Kakariko Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Zzzzzzzzzzz...|Link|CoH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Zzzzzzzzzzz...|Zelda|CoH}}</ref> The {{Term|CoH|Hibiscus Potion}} is found inside the {{Term|CoH|Kakariko Crypt|link}}, guarded by a {{Term|CoH|Shadow Link|display=Shadow}} counterpart of the dreamer. If {{Term|CoH|Cadence}} enters {{Term|CoH|Link}}'s {{Term|CoH|Portal (Object)}}, {{Term|CoH|Shadow Zelda|link}} must be fought; if {{Term|CoH|Cadence}} enters {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}}'s {{Term|CoH|Portal (Object)}}, {{Term|CoH|Shadow Link|link}} must be fought instead. The {{Term|CoH|Hibiscus Potion}} can be claimed upon their defeat and can then taken to the dreamer in {{Term|CoH|Kakariko Village}} in order to awaken them.<ref>{{Cite|I found someone sleeping outside the village and gave them a bed. If only we had a {{Color|CoH Yellow|hibiscus potion}}, we might be able to wake them up!|Dampé|CoH}}</ref>
