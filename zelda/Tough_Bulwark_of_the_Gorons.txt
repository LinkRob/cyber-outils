{{Term/Store|Tough Bulwark of the Gorons||HWAoC}} is a level 27 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tough Bulwark of the Gorons|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Tough Bulwark of the Gorons}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} and any other {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Flamebreaker Boots|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

The {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to capture four {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} while defending an Allied Stronghold. Two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} can be found above and below the Allied Stronghold, while the other two are on the eastern and western sides of the map. After the first one is killed, the other two spawn. 

On the path to the western {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}}, and when defeated, another {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawns alongside a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}. When this {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}} is killed, two more spawn in the center of the map. Three {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin}} spawn when they are both killed, and no more major {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} spawn afterwards.

The western {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains only {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos}}, and the northern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains only {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}. The southern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains a mix of {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}}, with a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos}} as its {{Term|HWAoC|Boss|link}}. The eastern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos}}, although to capture it the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat the two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin}} inside. As one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} approaches the eastern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, magma bombs will also rain down on them from the sky. Aside from the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}, there are {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}} scattered around the map in large numbers. {{Plural|HWAoC|Goron Captain|link}} also aid the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} in their mission. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the four {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured.
