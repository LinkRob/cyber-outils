{{Term/Store|Carrumpkin|Carrumpkins|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 119"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Carrumpkin}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Ruins|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Illusory Mansion|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Carrumpkin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Illusory Mansion}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Carrumpkin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Palace Noir|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Carrumpkin}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Carrumpkin}} are said to be naturally-occuring crosses between carrots and pumpkins.<ref>{{Cite|This carrot loved autumn so much, it became a pumpkin.|Material List|TFH}}</ref> They are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Light Armor|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Serpent's Toga|link}}.<ref name="E 119"/>
