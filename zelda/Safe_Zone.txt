{{Term/Store|Safe Zone|Safe Zones|Series|plural}} are recurring areas in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= |game= PH|page= 10}}</ref>{{Name Ref|ST}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
Safe Zones appear in the [[Temple of the Ocean King]] and in the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}} of {{PH}}, and in the [[Tower of Spirits]] in {{ST}}. In these zones, [[Phantom]]s and [[Reapling]]s patrolling the dungeons cannot harm, or even see [[Link]]. In {{PH|-}}, time does not drain from the {{Term|PH|Phantom Hourglass (Item)|link}} whenever Link stands within one of these zones. Before Link obtains the Phantom Hourglass, he takes damage while not in a safe zone. There are red jars that create small Safe Zones when broken. Though they play a much more vital role in {{PH|-}}, it is possible to play an entire run-through of {{ST|-}} without using them at all, as the Tower of Spirits does not place Link on a fixed timescale as the Temple of the Ocean King does in {{PH|-}}. 

In {{PH|-}}, one known false floor disguised as a safe zone can be found on B8 of the Ocean Temple.<ref>{{Cite|I walked on an invisible floor and got this far! An invisible floor! But then I got trapped in a pit. I can't believe my luck...|Skeleton|PH}}</ref>

====Battle Mode====
In the [[Battle Mode]], red and blue safe zones are utilized with the added effect of changing a [[Force Gem]]'s color to increase points for the Link of the respective color. The normal Safe Zones can still be found which revert Force Gems to a gold color and subtract points from the Link whose color was lost. While playing as a Phantom, Link is not visible on the screen; although his location can be detected by a floating-in-the-air Force Gem should he be holding one.
