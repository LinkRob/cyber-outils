Headstone Island is a desert type island which consists of a large main island, and several small sea stacks. The island cavern is blocked by a giant [[Stone Watcher|head-shaped stone]] that [[Link]] cannot blow up with [[bomb]]s alone. The only way Link can move it is to get the [[Power Bracelet]] located inside [[Fire Mountain]]. Once inside, the young hero will learn the [[Earth God's Lyric]], which he must conduct for [[Medli]] to gain entrance to the Earth Temple itself.

A [[Piece of Heart]] is located on top of the island; however, Link must use a [[Hyoi Pear]] to control a seagull and obtain it. It's interesting to note that while Link is controlling a seagull here, he may uncover a few hidden [[Rupee]]s in front of the cave entrance by commanding the seagull to dive into the sand.
