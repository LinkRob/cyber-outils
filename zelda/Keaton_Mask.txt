The {{Term/Store|Keaton Mask||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HW}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Keaton Mask is the first mask [[Link]] obtains in the [[Mask Trading Sequence]]. After showing [[Zelda's Letter]] to the [[Death Mountain Trail Gatekeeper]] in [[Kakariko Village]], he asks Link to get the mask for [[Graveyard Boy|his son]], as he is too busy guarding the gate.<ref>{{Cite|Have you been to the Happy Mask Shop that just opened in the Hyrule Castle Town Market? Everyone is talking about it! My little boy pesters me for a popular mask, but I don't have time to go there...So, could you go and get the mask for me next time you are in the Market? If you don't feel like it, that's OK, but... Well, I have no choice, this is my job...sigh...|Death Mountain Trail Gatekeeper|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey! It's a Keaton Mask!! I asked my daddy to get me one, too!|Graveyard Boy|OoT}}</ref> After showing the Mask to him, it can be sold for 15 [[Rupee]]s, giving Link a 5 Rupee revenue.

The Keaton character is renowned throughout [[Hyrule]] as a popular figure, predominately among children.<ref>{{Cite|Keaton stuff is hot, hot, hot!|Happy Mask Salesman|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh wow! It's Keaton! Hi, Keaton!|Hylian|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, I know that character! It's Keaton! He's very hot in Hyrule Castle Town right now!|Malon|OoT}}</ref> However, most reactions from adults are of nostalgia, confusion or admiration.<ref>{{Cite|Wow... how nostalgic! That reminds me of when I was a little kid.|Hylian|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|YEEEAH! What's your problem?!|Hylian|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Awww... That's so cute! Could you tell me where you found it?|Hylian|OoT}}</ref> [[Goron]] and [[Zora]] interactions result in a simple answer,<ref>{{Cite|Hrrrrm... I don't know what to say.|Goron|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What do you want?|Zora|OoT}}</ref> with the exception of important characters such as [[King Zora]], [[Darunia]], and [[Princess Ruto]].<ref>{{Cite|Are you trying to make me laugh? Well, that's not that funny...|Darunia|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ho ho ho! You Hylians... you are such silly creatures!|King Zora|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You're a funny guy!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's a {{Color|MM Red|Keaton Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>It carries quite a lot of nostalgia. It was popular with children long ago.</p>}}

In {{MM}}, the Keaton Mask can be used to summon a Keaton. To do this, Link must stand in any ring of moving bushes and cut them all before they disappear. Once summoned, he will ask Link questions about the game, each with three choices. There are a total of thirty questions that could possibly be asked.<ref>{{Cite|Answer me this... Pick one of the three choices...|Keaton|MM}}</ref> If Link gets all of the correct, the young hero will be awarded with a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|Hee-hee-ho! Good! Very good! Take this prize!|Keaton|MM}}</ref> If a question is answered incorrectly, Link must leave the area and then come back to try again.

The Keaton Mask Link receives in {{MM|-}} was given to [[Kafei]] when he was little by the [[Man from Curiosity Shop]].<ref>{{Cite|All it took was one glance at that Keaton Mask he was carrying for me to realize that I was looking at my old friend. I gave him that mask a long time ago when he was just li'l Kafei. Didn't know he kept it that well for so long...|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref>

Kafei wears the Mask to keep his identity hidden from the residents of [[Clock Town]]. The Keaton Mask is obtained from the Man from the Curiosity Shop in [[Kafei]]'s hideout, after delivering the [[Letter to Kafei]]. It requires Link to go to Kafei's hideout at 1:00 in the afternoon on the [[Final Day]].

In {{HW}}, [[Young Link]] wears the Keaton Mask; however, it is simply a cosmetic item as Young Link keeps it on his head for most of the time, putting it on only in a taunting animation. Keaton Masks are Gold [[Material]]s which are dropped by [[Young Link]] in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}.

==Trivia==
*In version 1.0 of {{OoT|-}}, the Keaton Mask was erroneously named the "Keatan Mask" on the item screen.
*In {{TWW}}, the Keaton Mask appears alongside the [[Bunny Hood]], [[Goron Mask]], and [[All-Night Mask]] behind the counter of the [[Nintendo Gallery]]. It can be seen hanging on the wall next to [[Carlov]].
