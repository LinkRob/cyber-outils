==Location and Uses==
Roasted Trout can be made by exposing [[Chillfin Trout]], [[Sizzlefin Trout]], [[Voltfin Trout]], or [[Stealthfin Trout]] to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Blue {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} have a chance to drop Roasted Trout when killed by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. Their flesh is soft, flaky and has a mild flavor.<ref name="Inventory"/> When eaten, Roasted Trout restore one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to Link's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}.
