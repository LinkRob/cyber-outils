==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Gems are valuable {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that can be used to upgrade {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}, craft new gear, or be sold at a high price to shops and merchants. Gems are generally found by breaking {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}}, with {{Plural|BotW|Rare Ore Deposit|link}} more likely to yield rarer varieties. {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} can also sometimes be found inside {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, as well as dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Stone Pebblit|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Silver Bokoblin|link|display=Silver}} and {{Term|BotW|Golden Bokoblin|link|display=Golden}} {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. After {{Term|BotW|Pelison|link}} moves to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}, he will open a stall there that sells a few {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} at a high price.

{{Plural|BotW|Gem}} are most notably used to purchase and craft accessories at the {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories|link}} shop in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. The respective {{Term|BotW|Gem}} is also used to upgrade these accessories as well.

After completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Jewel Trade|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Ramella|link}} will offer to buy ten pieces of a randomly chosen {{Term|BotW|Gem}} at a higher price than at stores.<ref name="Jewels"/>
