'''[https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge_(Complete)_Link Soul Edge (Complete)]''' is one of [[Link]]'s ten unlockable weapons in ''[[SoulCalibur II]]''. It is the fully powered form of the [[demon]] weapon Soul Edge, having the ability to shapeshift into any other weapon based on a fighter's combat style, and in this case, is both a unique [[Sword]] and [[Shield]] rather than a zweihānder.

== Location and Uses ==
In the Namco fighting game, although the evil entity sword [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge '''Soul Edge'''], itself the main antagonist of the [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_series ''Soul'' series], is normally wielded by [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Nightmare Nightmare], [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Cervantes Cervantes], as well as [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Charade Charade] and [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Inferno Inferno] (who are a fragment and its soul, respectively), every single character in ''SoulCalibur II'', including guests (but not hidden), have a unique, unlockable version of the demonic blade in its [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge_(Complete) completed state], in which it can transform itself accordingly to that of the characters' signature weapons, as it is made entirely out of organic material. Under normal circumstances, Soul Edge's true power is the result of absorbing all of its lost fragments, including [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Calibur Soul Calibur] itself, making the evil sword a deadly force to be reckoned with. The weapon remains in the shape of a zweihānder but is rather twisted, as it has an abundance of eyes (including its signature one in the center and is even larger) and thick, sharp teeth, giving it a very grotesque appearance. In the case of [[Link]], the complete Soul Edge is instead in the shape of a sword and shield, appearing as a wicked version of his [[Master Sword]] and [[Hylian Shield]]. Notably, unlike some other forms of Soul Edge (Complete) in-game, Link's version does not have any eyes on the sword or shield.

This weapon is unlocked in Weapon Master mode by paying 7,800 Gold in the Castor Shop. It increases Link's offense and defense greatly to 140% and 135%, respectively, making it overall the strongest weapon in his arsenal (except for the [[Cane of Byrna]] using Soul Charge which his attack power reaches 150%). However, Link slowly loses health when equipped, draining 2 HP per second. Consequently, this can lead to a more offensive battle style for the player.

Like the [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Armos_Series Armos Series] unlockable weapon, Soul Edge (Complete) is exclusive to ''SoulCalibur II'' and is also a weapon for Link that does not originate from any game of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]].
== In-game description ==
:[[File:SCII Soul Edge Complete form.webp|thumb|281x281px|Soul Edge (Complete) in its true form]]"''Fearsome demonic weapon said to excel in offense and defense...''"
: — Weapon Gallery description.
An evil blade with a reputation for devouring souls. This weapon is also known to be a shape shifter -- which form it takes depends on its owner. The forms it has taken are legendary, as are the souls of the renowned warriors it has consumed. Only those with great mental discipline can retain their will while wielding this weapon. It is an evil weapon which saps the strength of the enemy it wounds and rends the soul of the one who holds it.

== Trivia ==

* According to the weapon descr<nowiki/>iption of the true version wielded by Nightmare, Soul Edge in its complete form is so terribly powerful, not even [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Calibur Soul Calibur], the titular holy sword with powers similar to the [[Master Sword]] and [[Four Sword]], can stand against it.
* Excluding being selected by t<nowiki/>he player, Soul Edge (Complete) can only be seen wielded by opponents in the Extra mode. However, in Weapon Master mode, it appears in very few late missions.
* Although this weapon set is n<nowiki/>ot from ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'', in the context of the series, as its strength is 140%, this would make its [[Sword]] considerably stronger than the Master Sword, and even slightly above the [[Biggoron's Sword]] and [[Great Fairy's Sword]]. It is unknown if its [[Shield]] counterpart is as strong as the [[Hylian Shield|Hylian]] or [[Mirror Shield]].[[File:MM3D Fierce Deity Link art (close up).png|thumb|Despite being a pure-hearted character who destroys evil, an infamous case of Link possessing highly powerful dark abilities similar to the complete Soul Edge exists in ''Majora's Mask'' via the Fierce Deity form|284x284px]]
* As the [[Hero of Time]]'s mission<nowiki/> in ''SoulCalibur II'' is to outright destroy Soul Edge in order to completely save both [[Hyrule]] and the ''Soul'' universe from peril, himself being able to optionally wield the very antagonistic weapon itself in its full state is technically out of character for a heroic [[Nintendo]] icon like [[Link]], due to the fact that he is ''The Legend of Zelda'' series' main protagonist/mascot and obviously known for banishing evil, specifically his archnemesis [[Ganon]]. However, this is not unheard of by the game's release; On rare occasions in the long-running ''Zelda'' franchise, Link is capable of harnessing dark powers if necessary - a well-known canonical case being his notorious [[Fierce Deity Link|Fierce Deity]] transformation by the [[Fierce Deity Mask|Fierce Deity's Mask]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', a deadly, mysterious form with god-like strength in level with the [[Triforce of Power]], something Soul Edge (Complete) is also said to possess. Another likely reason for Link being able to use Soul Edge is the wicked sword, story-wise, can be controlled by anyone who has a strong enough will regardless if the wielder is good or evil, in which the Hero of Time clearly possess expert disciplinary skills, as according to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]'', he is said to be possibly a [[Knight of Hyrule|Hylian Knight]] himself.
* Soul Edge (Complete), alongsi<nowiki/>de the Great Fairy's Sword, serve as Link's [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Ultimate_weapon Ultimate Weapon] in the game.
* The sword of Link's version o<nowiki/>f Soul Edge (Complete) shares a similarity with [[Ghirahim]]'s true form from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'' as being a dark version of the Master Sword. In comparison, the former is more grotesque while the latter is much longer. Likewise, the host, Inferno and [[Demise]], are the root of their evil.
