{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Shield}} are mid-tier {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} once favored by the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}} who guarded the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} manufactured for {{Plural|BotW|Knights of Hyrule|display=Knights}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref> They boast a great defense and {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}}, comparable to that of even the strongest {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}. Though their metal construction makes them {{Term|BotW|Durability|display=Durable}}, their weight makes it so they require more skill to wield.<ref name="Inventory"/>

They can be found around the northern half of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Eldin Mountains|link}}. There are a few notable exceptions, however, including a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Pass|link}}. Another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} is located near {{Term|BotW|Aquame Bridge|link}} leading to the {{Term|BotW|Coliseum Ruins|link}}. It can be obtained as a reward from {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} for completing the {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} with a time between 1:45 and 2:00.<ref>{{Cite|A shiny new record deserves a shiny new shield. Here y'go!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> Finally, {{Term|BotW|Russ|link}} sells a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Shield}} on {{Term|BotW|Trilby Plain|link}} for 300 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|knight's shield}}? A study choice, sure. Perfect for shredding, my man! How's about {{Color|BotW Red|300 rupees}}?|Russ|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} buys it from him, he will call it a sturdy {{Term|BotW|Shield}}, which reminds him of the rumored {{Term|BotW|Durability}} of the {{Term|BotW|Hylian Shield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rock solid, man! That shield right there won't easily break, that's for sure. Oh! Speaking of not breaking, have ya heard about the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian shield}}? Everyone in the surfing scene covets that bad boy. It's clearly comissioned by the royal family or something'! Its sturdiness makes it perfect for shield surfing too! You don't really see 'em around these days...but rumor has it there's one in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Though no one has ever gone there and returned in one piece...}}}}|Russ|BotW}}</ref>

The following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Shield}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} ({{TLoZ}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Toon Zelda|link}} ({{TWW}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|TP|Link|link}} ({{TP}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|SS|Link|link}} ({{SS}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Knight's Shield}} conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. In addition, they sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and will burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Knight's Shield}} can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Knight's Shields reappear in {{TotK}}. They have the same strength from {{BotW}} and they can occasionally be found in [[Treasure Chest|treasure chests]] left behind by [[Shock Like]]s.
