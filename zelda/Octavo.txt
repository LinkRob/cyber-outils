{{Term/Store|Octavo||Series, CoH}} is a boss and playable character in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Octavo}}|N/A|CoH}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|CoH|Octavo}} is a musician and magician who played for the {{Term|CoH|King of Hyrule|link}}. Fearing the return of {{Term|CoH|Ganon|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} bewitched the {{Term|CoH|King of Hyrule}} and attempted to steal the {{Term|CoH|Triforce|link}}. However, upon being touched, the {{Term|CoH|Triforce}} split. Despite this, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} was able to enchant his {{Term|CoH|Lute}} and transform it into the {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute|link}}, granting him more power. Using this power, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} sealed {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)|link}}, cursed the inhabitants of the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}} to follow a rhythm,<ref>{{Cite|Are you wondering what's causing our movement to be controlled? It all starts in the {{Color|CoH Blue|future}}...or was it in the past?|Fate|CoH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In any case, it's the power of the {{Color|CoH Yellow|Golden Lute}}. The magician {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}} can use it to compel people to follow his orders! He must really like dancing...|Fate|CoH}}</ref> and summoned an army of monsters across {{Term|CoH|Hyrule}}. He also placed various {{Plural|CoH|Instrument|link}} deep within the dungeons of the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule}} to prepare the  {{Plural|CoH|Four Champions|link}} who could combat {{Term|CoH|Ganon}}.

===Characteristics===
{{Term|CoH|Octavo}} is fought on a stage surrounded by monsters and possessed {{Plural|CoH|Green Guard|link|display=Guards}}. Using the power of the {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} summons monsters by channeling the various {{Plural|CoH|Instrument|link}} at his command. When these monsters have been exhausted, he will switch {{Plural|CoH|Instrument}}. Upon exhausting the {{Term|CoH|Glockenspiel|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Bass Guitar|link}}, {{Plural|CoH|Maraca|link}}, and the {{Term|CoH|Oboe|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} will summon random sets of monsters with the {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute}} until he has sustained enough damage. When he is struck after summoning random monsters, damage will be dealt to the {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute}}, which has six {{Plural|CoH|Heart|link}}. In addition to summoning monsters, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} can conjure rows of magical {{Plural|CoH|Sword|link}} or {{Plural|CoH|Hammer|link}} in purple or green to damage {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} respectively. {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} can also summon fireballs, which he casts in the shape of a chevron.

After the {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute}} has been dealt enough damage, a {{Term|CoH|Portal (Object)|link}} will appear. The {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute}} will fall into the {{Term|CoH|Portal (Object)}}, ending the battle.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|CoH|Octavo}}'s name is derived from ''{{Wp|octave}}'', an interval between harmonic pitches of different frequencies. His name is also reminiscent of the original {{Term|Series|The NecroDancer|link|display=NecroDancer}}, Octavian.
