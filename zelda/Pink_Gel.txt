{{Term/Store|Pink Gel|Pink Gels|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Pink Gel|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Pink Gel}} are slime-like enemies that only appear after a {{Term|CoH|Pink Zol|link}} is defeated. Unlike their {{Term|CoH|Zol|link}} parent, their movement is not restricted to one direction, as they can move onto any adjacent tiles on every other beat of the music in order to attack {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}.
