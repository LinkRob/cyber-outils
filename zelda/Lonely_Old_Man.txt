The {{Term/Store|Lonely Old Man||Series, FSA}} is a character in {{FSA}}.<ref name="E"/>

==Biography==
{{Stub|FSA}}
The {{Term|FSA|Lonely Old Man}} appears in several locations, including the [[Cave of No Return]]. Jealous over Link for having friends, something he himself never had, he informs them that they can make it over the chasm by throwing themselves over.<ref>{{Cite|Urrk urrrk... You...have friends... I'm jealous... If you have friends, you can toss 'em across chasms... I never had any friends. You're a lucky guy...|Lonely Old Man|FSA}}</ref> However, it is not a given that they'll give the four Links any hints or usable information, as sometimes he will just give strange remarks, such as at the [[The Swamp|graveyard]].<ref>{{Cite|I came to visit my departed wife's grave. Then she showed up, saying she came to visit MY grave... I wish someone would tell me which one of us is the ghost and which isn't!|Lonely Old Man|FSA}}</ref>
