{{Term/Store|The Field||Series, FSA}} is the first stage in [[Near the Fields]] in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 265}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
[[Malon]] is waiting in the rain near the entrance to the area. She says the soldiers began moving and firing [[cannon]]s and now she can't make it home.<ref>{{Cite|I was coming home after running errends but...I couldn't get back. I mean, it started raining and the castle guards were moving all the cannons... The cannons are really old and they're not meant to be used... It was really weird.|Malon|FSA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The soldiers in the castle were behaving so oddly, and I couldn't get past them. They were moving the old cannons around...and they even aimed their bows at me!|Malon|FSA}}</ref> Malon trusts [[Link]] after seeing him speak to a [[Maiden#Green Maiden|Green Fairy]].<ref>{{Cite|Um, excuse me... Were you just talking to...a green fairy? I saw it! There was a beautiful fairy fluttering around you!|Malon|FSA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Say, if you're all going on ahead, take me with you! You're friends with the green fairy, so of course it's OK, right?|Malon|FSA}}</ref> She has been told that good boys and girls meet [[Fairy|fairies]].<ref>{{Cite|Amazing! What Poppa told me really is true! He said that good boys and girls get to meet fairies!|Malon|FSA}}</ref> The [[Links]] must take up the task to escort her back to [[Lon Lon Ranch]]. Malon only has one [[Heart Container|heart of health]], should she lose it, all the Links die as well. The Links must safely take her through the cannon bombardment and a [[grass]]y area filled with hidden [[Archer]]s. Once the Links return her safely to the ranch, she admits that at first she was not sure she could count on them, but soon realized her mistake and acknowledges the Links as "real heroes."<ref>{{Cite|I hate to admit it, but at first, I wasn't sure I could really count on you guys. But it turns out, you guys are the best! You're real heroes!|Malon|FSA}}</ref> For her safe return, [[Talon]], her father, allows the Links to use the [[Animals#Horse|horses]] of Lon Lon Ranch.<ref>{{Cite|I'd love to give you a token of my gratitude! Hmmm...let's see... I've got it! If you're going to continue your trip, you should go on horseback!|Talon|FSA}}</ref> Talon is grateful for the arrival of heroes as he claims they are "so scared they sit at home and stare at walls".

The Lon Lon Ranch area has the traditional low fences for presumably horseback riding. The horses make quick work of the many guards attacking the Links throughout the [[tree]] lined course.

Further into the area exists a wider open field. A home in the western section of the area seems to house two men worried about the many [[Gohma]]s outside. The [[Thieves|younger one]] gives the Links his shed [[key]] in exchange for an agreement to use something and slay the Gohmas. The [[Old Man|older man]] instructs the Links that a better tool, the [[bow]], can be found where his ancestors are buried. After the Gohmas are slayed the resident offers a [[shovel]] and advice on finding his hidden passage.

A duo of [[Ball and Chain Soldier]]s appear as a mini-boss. The pair even smash an entire house during the fight. Its boss is [[Stalfos#Size and Strength|Big Dark Stalfos]].
