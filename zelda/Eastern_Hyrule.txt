==Features and Overview==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
Eastern Hyrule is a continent which composes half of [[Hyrule]] in {{TAoL}}. It is home to several Palaces and Towns, including the [[Great Palace]] where the [[Triforce of Courage]] is kept. The only way to reach Eastern Hyrule is by obtaining the [[Raft]] found in [[Island Palace]], which is located in [[Western Hyrule]].

==={{FSA|-}}===
Eastern Hyrule is the second [[Levels in Four Swords Adventures|Level]] in {{FSA}}, situated in southeast Hyrule. It contains three Stages: [[The Coast]], the [[Village of the Blue Maiden]], and the [[Eastern Temple]].
