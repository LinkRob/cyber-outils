{{Plural|BotW|Boko Club}} are commonly found and dropped by red {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, especially on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. They are made by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} to hunt small prey, but due to a lack of refinement, they have low durability.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created and used by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> The most common locations to find {{Plural|BotW|Boko Club}} is in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. They also have a chance to appear inside {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn when the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Boko Club}} will burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} often use this fact to deal additional {{Term|BotW|Fire}} damage to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, using a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} to set their {{Term|BotW|Boko Club|display=Clubs}} on {{Term|BotW|Fire}} before attacking. Unlike metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and they will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
