==Features and Overview==
Hanyu Forest is a large forest in the southwest area of [[Gamelon]], part of the dense woodlands that circle around [[Shutoy Lake]]. It is a thick yet well lit Forest, as sunlight peeks through the leafy canopies supported by the Forest's many trees. The Forest is heavily infested by [[Bot]]s and [[Gargoyle]]s, whose green coloring easily allows them to blend in the foliage. The forest floor is uneven with several pits, requiring that [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] climb to the tree branches to use as platforms, which can be reached by rope tied to the boughs.

The first section of the Forest features a hollowed tree at its end, which leads into the next area as a detour. Here the [[Hanyu Spore]] that [[Grimbo]] of Washubi Swamp requests can be found.<ref name="Hanyu"/> At the end of this section is [[Myra]], who asks Zelda if she has any [[Arpagos Feather]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Greetings Princess Zelda! Do you have any Arpagos Feathers?|Myra|TWoG}}</ref> Zelda can give her one which she combines with the [[Shroud]] to turn it into the [[Magic Cape]].<ref>{{Cite|Good, then let us make a cape from your shroud.|Myra|TWoG}}</ref> A [[Triforce Map]] sits at the end of the Forest. After the stage is completed, the [[Fairy Pool]] is unlocked.
