==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
Chateau Romani is a highly luxurious vintage [[Milk]] produced by the best of [[Romani Ranch]]'s specially-bred [[Cow]]s.<ref>{{Cite|This way to Romani Ranch. Please visit the home of Chateau Romani.|Sign|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Our most popular, of course, is Chateau Romani. It's a vintage milk.|Mr. Barten|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The special Romani-bred cows are the source of Chateau Romani, the most desired of forbidden milk!|Cremia|MM}}</ref> The beverage is exclusively sold at the [[Milk Bar]] at a very pricey 200 [[Rupee]]s per [[Bottle]] after Link helps [[Cremia]] deliver it during the night of the [[Second Day]] (it therefore cannot be bought on the [[First Day]] unless the [[Romani's Mask]] is obtained) and is the building's best seller despite the hefty price.<ref>{{Cite|Chateau: 200 Rupees|Mr. Barten|MM}}</ref> This rare Milk functions like the [[Blue Potion]] in that it completely refills [[Link]]'s [[Life Gauge|Life Energy]] and [[Magic Meter]], but it additionally grants him unlimited [[Magic]] for the duration of the 3-day cycle (making the bar turn blue).<ref>{{Cite|You got a bottle of Chateau Romani! Drink it to replenish your magic power.|N/A|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It seems that drinking Chateau Romani makes your magic power last for three days...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> This makes events that require extensive use of the magic meter significantly easier, specifically the battles against [[Goht]] and [[Twinmold]], and the [[Goron Race]].

Link can obtain a Bottle of Chateau Romani for free if he delivers [[Kafei]]'s [[Special Delivery to Mama|letter]] to [[Madame Aroma]] in person as part of the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest on the [[Final Day]].<ref>{{Cite|Chateau Romani, Milk Bar. Thanks for delivering letter to mom|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, Link receives a [[Fishing Hole Pass]] instead.

== Trivia ==

* In the Japanese version, this item is known as '''Chapeau Romani''' or '''Short Romani'''.
* Considering its inebriating effects, it is possible Chateau Romani may in fact be a form of [[w:Kumis|Kumis]], a form of fermented mare milk with mild alcoholic properties.
* Although Chateau Romani is clearly a healthy milk in the case of [[Link]], the drink may be in place of alcoholic beverages for [[Termina]]; it can only be found in a bar and [[Cremia]] normally prevents her little sister [[Romani]] from drinking it, claiming it is for adults.
* The cost of Chateau Romani is ten times the price of regular Milk which is just 20 [[Rupee|Rupees]] per [[Empty Bottle|Bottle]].
