The {{Term/Store|Prototype Ancient Short Sword|Prototype Ancient Short Swords|Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Protoype Ancient Short Sword}}<br/>A prototype developed at the Royal Ancient Lab. The data obtained from the time-traveling Guardian accelerated research progress by decades.|Allies|HWAoC}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Prototype Ancient Short Sword}} was referred to as the '''Prototype Ancient Sword''' on the official {{HWAoC|-}} website.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= As a purchase bonus, you’ll also get a new weapon, the Prototype Ancient Sword, and Prototype Ancient equipment for Link to wear. |author= Nintendo |published= |retrieved= January 2, 2022 |url= https://www.zelda.com/hyrule-warriors/dlc/ |title= Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity for the Nintendo Switch™ system – DLC |site= zelda.com |type= }}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
The {{Term|HWAoC|Prototype Ancient Short Sword}} is exclusively available as a purchase bonus for the {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}. It can only be used by {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}}, and is obtained when entering the game for the first time after purchasing the {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass}}. Unlike most other {{Plural|HWAoC|Weapon}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Prototype Ancient Short Sword}} cannot be sold.
