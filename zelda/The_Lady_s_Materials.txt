{{Term/Store|The Lady's Materials|The Lady's Materials|Series, TFH|plural}} is the collective name given to the {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} needed to make the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hold a moment... Are those three conspicuously distinctive items... {{Color|TFH Blue|The Lady's materials}}?!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Each of {{Plural|TFH|The Lady's Materials}} is found inside a {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}}, underneath the usual {{Term|TFH|Material}}, whenever {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} defeats particular {{Plural|TFH|Boss|link}} and completes their respective {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} for the first time.
