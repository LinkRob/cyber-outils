"{{Term/Store|Daruk's Mettle||Series, BotW}}" is the fourth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Daruk's Mettle}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #4}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This memory is unlocked by speaking to [[Bludo]] after rescuing [[Yunobo]] from the [[Abandoned North Mine]]. Upon hearing {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} confusion about who [[Daruk]] was, he'll point him towards his statue.<ref>{{Cite|Are ya really tellin' me ya don't know about {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}}? The Goron {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}?! See that statue up there? That's {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}}!|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> Link will then look up at the statue, triggering the memory.

Daruk pilots [[Divine Beast Vah Rudania]] through the [[Eldin Mountains]] surrounding {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, with Link as his passenger. He remarks that he's finally getting the hang of controlling Vah Rudania, and he even enjoys it.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah! I think I'm finally getting the hang of controlling this Divine Beast! I tell you what...sure is a blast piloting a toy like this around.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> He tells Link to let the other [[Champion]]s know they "better eat their gravel if they wanna keep up with Daruk".<ref>{{Cite|Let those other Champions know, they better eat their gravel if they wanna keep up with Daruk.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref>

However, Daruk quickly moves on to admiring the view, calling all the rocks he sees "delectable".<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of which, can you believe this view? Just look at all those delectable rocks sprinkled on those mountains... Mighty tasty.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> While he doesn't know much about [[Calamity Ganon]], Daruk asserts that he'll protect {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} until his death.<ref>{{Cite|I may not know a whole lot about this Calamity Ganon thing... but mark my words, I'll protect this land of ours to the death!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> He enthusiastically pats Link on the back, accidentally knocking him forward with his strength.

Daruk then congratulates Link on becoming the appointed knight of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, by the way...congrats on becoming the princess's appointed knight.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> He states that it's a big deal, protecting the daughter of [[King Rhoam|the king]], but tries not to put too much pressure on him.<ref>{{Cite|That's a really big deal! Protecting the king's daughter... No pressure!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> As a bit of advice, he tells Link that Zelda has a strong personality, and she has trouble seeing "the range for the peaks."<ref>{{Cite|Seriously, though. The princess is a strong personality—so strong she can't quite see the range for the peaks. Remember that, and you'll be fine.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref>

The two are interrupted by the ground shaking, dislodging some boulders from the top of the peak above them. Using [[Daruk's Protection]], Daruk is able to shield both himself and Link from the falling rocks. He tries to move on in conversation,<ref>{{Cite|All right, so what was I saying...|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> but he can't help but comment on how strange the earthquake was.<ref>{{Cite|That was a little strange...|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> He explains to Link that Death Mountain has been inactive for decades.<ref>{{Cite|As far as I know, Death Mountain has been quiet for decades.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> Daruk is concerned that Death Mountain is causing enough activity to send down boulders that size,<ref>{{Cite|But if the mountain is shivering enough to send down a bunch of boulders that size, then—|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> but he quickly brushes that aside and tells Link to forget he said anything.<ref>{{Cite|Never mind. Forget I said anything.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> The memory ends with Daruk looking up at Death Mountain, concerned.
