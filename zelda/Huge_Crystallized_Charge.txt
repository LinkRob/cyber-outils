'''Huge Crystallized Charges''' are [[Item]]s in {{TotK}}. 

== Location and uses ==
Huge Crystallized Charges can be found in a variety of different locations across [[The Depths]]. [[Link]] obtains them as a reward every time he defeats [[Master Kohga]] at various Abandoned Mines, as well as the first time he defeats any of the [[Regional Phenomena]] [[Bosses in Tears of the Kingdom#Story Bosses|Boss]]es in their respective rematch zones in the Depths.

One Huge Crystallized Charge is equivalent to 100 [[Crystallized Charge]]s or five [[Large Crystallized Charge]]s, which are used at a [[Crystal Refinery]] to upgrade Link's [[Energy Cell]]. Unlike the smaller variants, Huge Crystallized Charges cannot be forged with [[Zonaite]]; they can only be obtained as one-time rewards from their respective bosses.
