==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Ancient Arrow}}
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} are a special type of {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} designed specifically for defeating {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}. They are daggers made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} called [[Ancient Blade|Ancient Blades]] that were repurposed by tying them via rope to {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} created the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} about 40 or 50 years after the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 385}}</ref> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>

There are a total of nine {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}}, all found at {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. Three of them contain one {{Term|BotW|Ancient Arrow}}, five of them contain a bundle of 3 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}}, and one contains a bundle of 5 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Arrow x3}}<br/>A bundle of three ancient arrows. Daggers created using ancient technology have been repurposed for use as arrows. Extremely effective against Guardians.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Arrow x5}}<br/>A bundle of five ancient arrows. Daggers created using ancient technology have been repurposed for use as arrows. Extremely effective against Guardians.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can also create them in exchange for {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} by using the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}}, Cherry, at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}. They can be purchased individually, or in bundles of three or five. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also receive one from {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} at any {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}, during what he calls Customer Appreciation Day.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! You're a great customer, and since today is Customer Appreciation Day... I want you to have this!|Beedle|BotW}}</ref> It is triggered after {{Term|BotW|Link}} frees his first {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is unable to carry more {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}}, {{Term|BotW|Beedle}} will be unable to give him his reward.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, no... your {{Color|BotW Blue|inventory}} is full. It's a real shame, but I guess we'll have to give up on this for now...|Beedle|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} deal moderate damage to {{Plural|BotW|Boss|link}}, but heavy damage to {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}. If an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} strikes a {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} directly in the eye, the {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} is instantly defeated. If used on enemies other than {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} and {{Plural|BotW|Boss|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Enemy}} and its {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} will completely disintegrate. Enemies that disintegrate due to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} will also not drop any {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}. 

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} do not disappear after being shot at an object, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to retrieve them if he misses an {{Term|BotW|Enemy}}. {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} will penetrate and stick to certain objects, such as wood, and certain {{Plural|BotW|Material}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} can cause ice blocks made from the {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to break, and will instantly blow up {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} can also be used to instantly dispatch {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Cannon|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} are one of the only means of defeating {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon|link}}, alongside the {{Plural|BotW|Light Arrow}} from the {{Term|BotW|Bow of Light|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Twilight Bow|link}} as well as {{Plural|BotW|Sword Beam|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Main|Ancient Blade}}
Ancient arrows as a unique arrow type have been removed from [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']] to make way for the addition of [[Fuse]], which allows players to make their own Ancient arrows by Fusing an arrow with a Ancient Blade or any other appropriate material. After awakening the [[Mineru|Sage of Spirit]], a [[Steward Construct|Steward construct]] can be found in the [[Spirit Temple|Spirit temple]], allowing you to trade 50 Zonaite in exchange for 1 Ancient Blade. This trade can be done repeatedly for easy access to Ancient Blades.
