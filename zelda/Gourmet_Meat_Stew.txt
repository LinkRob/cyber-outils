{{Term/Store|Gourmet Meat Stew|Gourmet Meat Stews|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Gourmet Meat Stews are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either [[Raw Gourmet Meat]] or a [[Raw Whole Bird]], [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Fresh Milk]], and [[Goat Butter]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they heal several of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The meat in these stews have simmered for so long that it melts in one's mouth, and they're considered a bucket-list meal.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding a fifth ingredient, several different variations of Gourmet Meat Stew can be made:

* '''Hearty Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these stews will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be replenished.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. These meals completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. When eaten, Link's movement speed will gain a temporary boost.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Link gains one temporary level of [[Cold Resistance]] when he eats these stews.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. If they're eaten, Link's attack power will be temporarily boosted.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. Link's defense will be increased if he eats this stew.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When they're eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will be temporarily boosted.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Gourmet Meat Stew''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Gourmet Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The meat has simmered for so long it melts in your mouth. A true bucket-list meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. Eating these will increase Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Chilly"/>
