The {{Term/Store|Hyrule Castle Gate||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 234}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Gate}} divides {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins|link}}. It features a bridge that spans that {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Moat|link}}. The metallic doors on the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Gate|display=Gate}} can be moved with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} or by hitting them while they are under the effect of {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Courtyard|link}} begins past the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Gate|display=Gate}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
Three {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} are located on and near the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Gate}}. One can be found on top of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Gate|display=Gate}} by picking up a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}}. A second can be found by shooting the jar hanging underneath the bridge by using {{Term|BotW|Archery (Mechanic)|link}}. The third and final one can be found by following the {{Term|BotW|Flower Trail|link}} that starts underneath the bridge. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} discovers these {{Plural|BotW|Korok}}, they will give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
