{{Term/Store|Trilby Highlands||Series, TMC}} is an area in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands}} is a hilly region located in western {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. It connects to five other areas: {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} to the east, {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel's Base|link}} to the west, {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}} to the northeast, {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}} to the north, and the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}} to the south.

A ladder near {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} leads to a cave, where {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can purchase an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}} from a {{Term|TMC|Business Scrub|link}} for 20 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}. This {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle}} is the only one that {{Term|TMC|Link}} is required to get in the game, as it is used to climb {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}. South of the entrance from {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} is a {{Term|TMC|Great Fairy|link}} whose {{Term|TMC|Great Fairy Fountain|link|display=Fountain}} can only be accessed if {{Term|TMC|Link}} places a {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}} between two stones against the face of wall.

Near the entrance to {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}}, there is a bridge connecting to a small outcropping of land with a dirt wall leading to a cave that {{Term|TMC|Link}} can dig through with the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}}. Inside the cave are two {{Plural|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}}, containing two {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} and a {{Term|TMC|Mysterious Wall|link}} that {{Term|TMC|Link}} can fuse {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with to help the digging {{Term|TMC|Goron|link}} near {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}} by introducing another {{Term|TMC|Goron}} to aid in destroying the [[Barrier#Rock Barriers|Rock Barrier]].<ref>{{Cite|Hn... No, it's no good... I can't break it by myself.|Goron|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I had a strange feeling, like a fellow Goron was in need... Hello? Goro? Hn? That wall! You can't get through that alone! I'm coming to help you! [...] Don't worry! I'm here to help you! Let's break through that wall together! Goro!|Goron|TMC}}</ref> This cave also leads to {{Term|TMC|Knuckle|link}}, with whom {{Term|TMC|Link}} can fuse {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Mr. Fairy! I've been holding on to this, just like my brother Tingle asked! And I have Kinstone pieces! Want to try and fuse with me?|Knuckle|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Knuckle}} will also tell {{Term|TMC|Link}} how many {{Term|TMC|Kinstone}} fusions he has left to make.<ref>{{Cite|How many Kinstones are left for you to fuse? Let's see... XX!? What are you doing wasting your time here! You've got work to do, Mr. Fairy!|Knuckle|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|They fit perfectly! Tingle would be so proud of me!|Knuckle|TMC}}</ref> Left of {{Term|TMC|Knuckle}} is a {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}} that {{Term|TMC|Link}} may use to enter the home of a {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} will tell {{Term|TMC|Link}} about the pond in the mid-southern section of the {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands}}, claiming that there is something suspicious about it.<ref>{{Cite|Have you seen that watery spot down to the south a bit? Is it just me, or is there something really suspicious about that place? I can't quite put my finger on it, but it weirds me out...|Forest Picori|TMC}}</ref> The {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori}} is also a target for {{Term|TMC|Kinstone}} fusion and if {{Term|TMC|Link}} successfully fuses {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with him, the aforementioned pond will dry up, revealing a staircase leading to a cave housing 75 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} in the form of fifteen blue {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}. When spoken to later, the {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori}} will ask if {{Term|TMC|Link}} has seen the "watery spot", affirming that it was strange.<ref>{{Cite|Did you ever check out that watery spot? Was I right? It was weird, huh?|Forest Minish|TMC}}</ref>
