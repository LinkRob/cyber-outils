The {{Term/Store|Grappling Hook||Series, TWW, TWWHD, PH}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126 {{Exp Game|TWW, PH}}}}</ref> It enables [[Link]] to latch on to cantilevered ledges called Grappling Posts or Pegs,<ref name="PH Guide 12">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 12}}</ref> as well as Treasure Chests in {{PH|-}},<ref name="PH Guide 12"/> and use them to swing across large distances to otherwise unreachable platforms.<ref>{{Cite|Grab this branch with the Grappling Hook and you can swing over there to get out of this area!|Medli|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|When you aim at a branch that you can latch onto, Target lock mark will appear. Remember that!|Medli|TWW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, the Grappling Hook is given to Link as a gift from [[Medli]] after defeating the [[Moblin]]s that had captured her at the summit of [[Dragon Roost Cavern]].<ref name= "Wings">{{Cite|Here, Link! Use this to get out of here! It's what I used to get this far. It's a device we Rito used before we evolved wings.|Medli|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hear the bird-people who live on Dragon Roost Island east of here have invented an incredibly useful device that they call a Grappling Hook... No lie, fry!|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> It can be used to cross gaps by latching at specific targets. A separate function of the Grappling Hook is in the [[Thievery#Looting|stealing]] of enemy [[Spoils]].<ref>{{Cite|I hear you can throw that thing at monsters and use it to grab treasure they've hidden in their pockets. Talk about cool.|Fishman|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can use your Grappling Hook to steal their Golden Feathers.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> Link can also use it to retrieve three [[Heart]]s from [[Princess Zelda]] during the final battle against [[Ganondorf]].

When equipped aboard the [[King of Red Lions]], the Grappling Hook becomes a crane, which allows Link to pull up [[Treasure Chest]]s from the seabed.<ref>{{Cite|After that, all you need to do is use the same thing we use to haul up treasure: one of those Grappling Hooks that the Rito tribesfolk of Dragon Roost Island use.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Then, you just use your Grappling Hook while in your boat to haul up treasure!|Salvage Corp|TWW}}</ref> These sunken chests can be found beneath {{Plural|TWWHD|Ring of Light|link}} scattered across the [[Great Sea]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 16}}</ref>

The Grappling Hook is said to have been used by the [[Rito]] people before they obtained wings to navigate the heights and upper levels of the mountain on [[Dragon Roost Island]].<ref name= "Wings"/>

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, the Grappling Hook can be used in several ways. It can be used as a walkable tightrope,<ref name="PH Guide 12"/> which also allows Link to use items while he is over it. However, if Link uses the Grappling Hook again, the previous tightrope disappears.<ref name="PH Guide 12"/> Similar to a [[Slingshot]], it is capable of blasting Link across large holes.<ref name="PH Guide 12"/> It can also be used as a kilter of weight, pulling Link towards heavy objects while bringing light objects to him.<ref>{{Cite|Light objects will be pulled toward you. You will be pulled to heavy objects!|N/A|PH}}</ref><ref name="PH Guide 12"/> Furthermore, it can also be used to reflect attacks. This is useful when redirecting [[Arrow]]s when shooting certain types of eye [[switch]]es that close when Link looks at them.

The Grappling Hook is obtained in the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}}. It is used to defeat the boss, [[Gleeok]], for which all possible ways of use will be required. The [[Salvage Arm]] replaces the Grappling Hook's crane feature at sea.

==Trivia==
*In {{TWW|-}}, there is an Easter Egg that may occur if Link attempts to use the Grappling Hook crane near (but not directly on top of) a [[Treasure Chart|ring of light]] or a barrel: he will pull up a barnacle-covered [[Pot|pot]] and no musical fanfare will play. After discarding the pot, an [[Octorok|Ocean Octorok]] will spawn nearby. These vases are most likely a reference to traditional Japanese octopus pots, or [https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%9B%B8%E5%A3%BA ''takotsubo''], which are used to fish for octopus in Japan and the Mediterranean Sea.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= NJ Riley|published= October 6, 2015|retrieved= May 12, 2018|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7tPSti7gbMs|title= Weird easter egg in Wind Waker|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref>
