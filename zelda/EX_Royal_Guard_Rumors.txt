"{{Term/Store|EX Royal Guard Rumors||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Royal Guard Rumors}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Riverside Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon reaching leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding a copy of {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX|link}}, which is located in the {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently {{Color|BotW Red|Riverside Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare outfit.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by reading the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V4}} without triggering any of the loading conditions for its activation.}}

The {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V4|link}} is found on a table inside {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable}}. In the book, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} discusses the {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard|link}} and hearsay that their {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} can still be found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This tidbit is about the brave soldiers who were tragically wiped out during the Great Calamity. {{Big|The royal guards who protected the royal family!}} Amongst {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule's soldiers}}, only the best of the best had the honor of guarding the royal family. Apparently, the equipment they wore can still be found somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}.|Super Rumor Mill V4|BotW}}</ref> According to a former maid, the equipment can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber|link}}, and the second floor.<ref>{{Cite|hey say a former maid spoke of treasure near the {{Color|BotW Red|Dining Hall}}, in the {{Color|BotW Red|Guards' Chamber}}, and on the {{Color|BotW Red|second floor}}.|Super Rumor Mill V4|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap|link}} is on the second floor of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}}, in the room directly above where {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} has sealed {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, in front of a statue of a knight. The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Uniform|link}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} behind a {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Sword|link}} in a small chamber hidden behind a [[Cracked Tile]] west of the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}}. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots|link}} is in the Guards' Chamber on a wooden platform in between two other wooden platforms with {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed once all three pieces of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set|link}} have been obtained.<ref>{{Cite|You found the {{Color|BotW Red|royal guard uniform}} from treasure chests in the hidden room near the dining hall, the storage space in the guards' chamber, and on the second floor of the castle.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
