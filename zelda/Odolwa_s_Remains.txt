{{Term/Store|Odolwa's Remains|Odolwa's Remains|Series, MM, MM3D}} are an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Term|MM3D|Odolwa|link}} is the masked jungle warrior {{Term|MM3D|Boss|link}} of {{Term|MM3D|Woodfall Temple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Odolwa's Remains: The remains of the boss in Woodfall Temple.|Inventory|MM}}</ref> When {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} cleanses the {{Term|MM3D|Woodfall Temple|display=Temple}} for the first time and acquires {{Term|MM3D|Odolwa's Remains}}, the first {{Term|MM3D|Guardian Deity|link}} is freed from its captivity and meets {{Term|MM3D|Link}} in the [[Chamber of Giants]],<ref>{{Cite|You seized {{Color|MM3D Red|Odolwa's Remains}}! In doing so, you've freed the innocent spirit that was imprisoned within this dark mask.|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> where it teaches him the "{{Term|MM3D|Oath to Order|link}}".<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM Light Blue|Wait...Listen... It seems to be saying something... Could that crying be its way of teaching us some sort of melody? Don't just stand there... Get your instrument...}}|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref> The {{Term|MM3D|Odolwa's Remains|display=Remains}} allow {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to {{Term|MM3D|Warping|link|display=Warp}} directly to the {{Term|MM3D|Boss}} room from the {{Term|MM3D|Woodfall Temple|display=Temple}}'s entrance.<ref>{{Cite|Ye who hold my remains... Return to the appointed place to face me...|N/A|MM}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, {{Term|MM3D|Odolwa's Remains}} are not required for this; simply challenging {{Term|MM3D|Odolwa}} once will activate the {{Term|MM3D|Portal (Object)}} so {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can try again without having to go back through the {{Term|MM3D|Dungeon}}.

{{Term|MM3D|Odolwa's Remains}} are worn by one of the {{Plural|MM3D|Moon Child|link}}. They can also be seen hanging on one of the walls of the {{Term|MM3D|Boss}} chamber inside the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}} during the battle against {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Mask (Item)|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Odolwa's Remains|display=Remains}} eventually detach from the wall and attack {{Term|MM3D|Link}} by shooting energy at him. They can be destroyed with any weapon.
