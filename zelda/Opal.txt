==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Opal}} can be found by breaking [[Ore Deposit]]s and [[Rare Ore Deposit]]s. They're also sold by [[Kairo]] at [[Foothill Stable]]. The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] accepts Opals as an ingredient for Light Yellow Dye.

Several {{Plural|Series|Armor|link}} require Opals to be upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Each piece of the [[Zora Set]] needs fifteen Opals and ten {{Plural|Series|Lizalfos Tail|link}} to reach their fourth and final tier. Not only are Opals needed to purchase the [[Opal Earrings]], a total of forty-nine are needed to completely upgrade them. Finally, the pieces of the [[Hero of Winds Set]] need varying amounts of Opals at each tier for their upgrades.

* Total for '''{{Term|BotW|Opal Earrings}}''': 49 (5, 8, 16, 20)

* Total for '''{{Term|BotW|Hero of Winds Set}}''':  57 (1, 3, 5, 10) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
 
* Total for '''{{Term|BotW|Zora Set}}''':  45 (0, 0, 0, 15) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
In Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity, Opals look like how they were in Breath of the Wild. They can be bought at the store in the Gerudo Desert, or be found in gray treasure chests in main quests, but can also be obtained as first-clear rewards. They can be sold to most Merchants for 60 Rupees, but Dayfah will buy them for 150 Rupees.
{{Noncanon|End}}
