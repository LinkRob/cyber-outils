{{Term/Store|Lucky Lee||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref name= "Name">{{Cite|I'm {{Color|PH Blue|Lucky Lee}}, the lucky spirit that dwells on this island! I'm here to bring you luck! If you pay me 50 Rupees, you can win big, big, big! Want to try your hand at my game of luck?|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref>

==Biography==
Lucky Lee randomly appears when [[Link]] digs while playing the [[Digging Game]] on [[Harrow Island]]. If Link pays Lucky Lee 50 [[Rupee]]s, it will give him a chance to pick between a small or a big [[Treasure Chest]].<ref name= "Name"/><ref>{{Cite|Well, then! Let's start! Say there was a {{Color|PH Red|large treasure chest}} and a {{Color|PH Red|small treasure chest}}... Which do you choose?|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref> These Chests may contain a small prize of 40 Rupees,<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|PH Red|tiny winner}}! And so you win a consolation prize of 40 Rupees. Come again!|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref> an average prize of 150 Rupees,<ref>{{Cite|You're a {{Color|PH Red|so-so winner}}! Yep, sorta lucky, fella! You won 150 Rupees! Come again!|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref> or a large prize of 200 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|You're a {{Color|PH Red|big winner}}! You're one lucky fella! You won 200 Rupees! Come again!|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref> The Treasure Chests may also contain nothing, in which case Link must pay an additional 100 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, no! You lost! You must pay 100 Rupees! Come again!|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref>
