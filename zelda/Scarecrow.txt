==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Main|Bonooru}}
Two {{Plural|OoT3D|Scarecrow}} are featured in {{OoT}}, by the names of {{Term|OoT3D|Bonooru|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Pierre|link}}. {{Term|OoT3D|Bonooru}} claims he has a great memory and loves music, and will learn any original, 8-note song that the child {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} plays for him on the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am Bonooru, the scarecrow musical genius! I hear a song once, and I never forget it, baby!|Bonooru|OoT}}</ref> When {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} returns and meet him as an adult, however, {{Term|OoT3D|Bonooru}} can't remember the tune and needs {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} to remind him.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Aren't you the fairy buddy I met a long time ago? Wow, you grew, dude! Well, here's your song... What? No, I never forget! You, you're the one who forgot... aren't you? If you have an Ocarina, play that song, baby!|Bonooru|OoT}}</ref> As thanks for playing the song again, {{Term|OoT3D|Bonooru}} has his friend {{Term|OoT3D|Pierre}} appear and help {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} whenever he plays the newly-dubbed "{{Term|OoT3D|Scarecrow's Song|link}}".<ref>{{Cite|OH YEAHHH!! That's it, that's the tune!! You rock!! I'm blown away! Oh, no, no! I didn't forget the song...I never forget anything! But I just feel like rewarding you, baby! ........ I have a good idea! My buddy Pierre is wandering around, so play that tune when you want to call him! I'll tell him to help you if he's nearby and hears that song, baby!|Bonooru|OoT}}</ref> {{Term|OoT3D|Pierre}} lives near {{Term|OoT3D|Bonooru}} on the shores of {{Term|OoT3D|Lake Hylia|link}}, and he enjoys music as well. He helps {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} to reach otherwise inaccessible locations by appearing and acting as a target for {{Term|OoT3D|Link}}'s {{Term|OoT3D|Hookshot|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Pierre}}
[[Pierre]] is much the same as his {{OoT|-}} counterpart. He loves music and will learn a song played for him on the Ocarina of Time. However, this song must be re-taught to him if the "[[Song of Time]]" is played. If Link talks to him, he will offer to dance. If Link accepts, time will pass until the next dawn or night. This will not work on the night of the [[Final Day]] as Pierre leaves the town.<ref>{{Cite|But outside it seems to have gotten kind of...dangerous. Yikes...I'm gettin' outta town!|Pierre|MM}}</ref>  After dancing, he will ask Link if he wants to learn a way to speed up or slow down time. He says that playing a "mysterious song" (the "Song of Time") will allow Link to manipulate the flow of time:<ref>{{Cite|I know of a mysterious song that allows you to manipulate the flow of time...|Pierre|MM}}</ref> playing it backwards will slow down time, and playing each note twice in a row will make time move forward.<ref>{{Cite|If you play that strange song backward, you can slow the flow of time. And if you play each note twice in a row, you can move a half day forward through time. How's that? Pretty interesting, isn't it?|Pierre|MM}}</ref>
{{clear}}

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, there is a {{Term|TPHD|Scarecrow}} near {{Term|TPHD|Link's House|link}}, with which he can practice attacking using the {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Sword|link}} in the beginning of the game. A {{Term|TPHD|Scarecrow}} can be found near {{Term|TPHD|Coro's Store|link}} as well.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, there is a Scarecrow near Link's House, with which he can practice attacking with his Sword. Link can destroy the Scarecrow with the [[Fire Rod]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, four Scarecrows appear in an empty field in [[Hateno Village]].

==Other Appearances==
==={{LCT|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{LCT}}, {{Plural|LCT|Scarecrow}} are used as optional {{Plural|LCT|Target|link}} that {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} can shoot at with his {{Term|LCT|Crossbow|link}}. They appear at least once in every stage, sometimes appearing multiple times. In most Target-Shooting stages, a {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} will appear in the first area of the stage. In the next area, the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} will only appear again if {{Term|LCT|Link}} has shot the first {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}}s' head.

If {{Term|LCT|Link}} shoots the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}}s' body, he will be awarded one point. If he shoots the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}}s' body again, he will be awarded two points. This pattern continues until {{Term|LCT|Link}} shoots the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} for the eighth time, where continuous hits afterward only award {{Term|LCT|Link}} with eight points each. If {{Term|LCT|Link}} ever shoots the {{Term|LCT|Pumpkin|link}} head of the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}}, he will be awarded points based on how many hits were taken by the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}}s' body, with 1,000 points being awarded if {{Term|LCT|Link}} shot the Scarecrow eight times or more beforehand. If {{Term|LCT|Link}} ever shoots the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} with a {{Term|LCT|Bomb Arrow|link}}, the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}}s' head will be destroyed granting him points based on how many hits the body of the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} has already taken, plus an additional 2 points. Once the head of the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} is shot and destroyed, Link can freely shoot the body of the Scarecrow for an unlimited amount of shots, with each shot giving {{Term|LCT|Link}} one point. If {{Term|LCT|Link}} shoots the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} with a {{Term|LCT|Bomb Arrow}} after destroying its head, he will be given 3 points.
{{Noncanon|End}}
