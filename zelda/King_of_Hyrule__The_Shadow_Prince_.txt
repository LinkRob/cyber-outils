The '''King of Hyrule''' is a character featured in {{TSP}}. He lives in {{Term|TSP|North Castle|link}} with his daughter, {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda}}.

==Biography==
The {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule}} is first introduced when {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda}} takes {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles|link}}, a knight from the land of {{Term|TSP|Moria|link}}, to the {{Term|TSP|North Castle}} so that he may meet the king. The king takes an immediate liking towards Charles, and is quick to trust him so much that he decides to show the knight the {{Term|TSP|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} located in the {{Term|TSP|Triforce Room|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He beckons to Sir Charles. 'To cement the new bond between our kingdoms, we'd like you to see something we call the Triforce of Wisdom.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=23}}</ref> against Zelda's wishes.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Your new friend, Charles, is a fine young man. I was thinking...would it be premature to show him the Triforce?' Zelda looks startled. 'Well, I--' [...] 'But our enemy Ganon already has the Triforce of Power,' Zelda says. 'If we lose our Triforce of Wisdom, Ganon would be that much closer to ruling us all.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=17}}</ref> The king goes on to dub Charles a Knight of the {{Term|TSP|Triforce/Other Appearances|display=Triforce|link}}, asking him to protect it from {{Term|TSP|Ganon|link}}'s grasp.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Wonderful! I hereby dub you a knight of the Triforce,' says the king, beaming.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=78}}</ref> If {{Term|TSP|Link|link}} decides to speak up and ask the king if he has lost his mind, he reminds him they've only known Charles for one day. The king, however, does not care what {{Term|TSP|Link}} thinks, and silences him while wondering what has gotten into the young hero. Charles comments that he might be restless and needs "to work off steam." The king agrees and decides to make {{Term|TSP|Link}} his new tax collector, sending him to the {{Term|TSP|Water Town of Saria|link}} first thing in the morning.

Later on in the book, the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule}} will explain that there have been strange reports from the citizens of {{Term|TSP|Hyrule|link}} stating that an entire gang of {{Plural|TSP|Darknut|link}} was spotted in the {{Term|TSP|Jungle of Favors|link}}, and that a recent thunderstrom caused for {{Plural|TSP|Goriya|link}} to fall from the sky. Fearing for the safety of the Triforce of Wisdom, the king says that they must move the sacred triangle to the {{Term|TSP|Fifth Castle of Ancient Hyrule|link}}, which is southeast of {{Term|TSP|North Castle}} past deserts, rivers and swamps.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='I believe Ganon is after our Triforce of Wisdom again. It's in danger here. We must move it.' [...] To the Fifth Castle of Ancient Hyrule.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 6}}</ref> When Zelda asks who will deliver the Triforce of Wisdom there, the king mentions he has two heroes in mind: {{Term|TSP|Link}} and Charles. Anxiously, {{Term|TSP|Link}} awaits the king's decision, but it is soon revealed that Charles will be the one to take the Triforce to the Fifth Castle.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Charles of Moria will carry the Triforce of Wisdom to its new home,' the king announces.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 102}}</ref>

{{Term|TSP|Link}} furiously reminds the king of all the brave deeds he has done and claims that Charles can't be trusted because he is evil.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='And anyway, Charles can't be trusted. He is evil!' The room is in an uproar. The king raises his hand. 'Silence! Link, you've made some very serious charges against Sir Charles. You'd better have proof to back up your words, young man!'|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 103}}</ref> The king raises his hand and silences {{Term|TSP|Link}}, saying he better have proof to back up the serious charge he has raised against Charles. If {{Term|TSP|Link}} doesn't have the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth|link}}, the king will banish him to the {{Term|TSP|Tower|link}} for three days, which is where the prisoners are kept.<ref name=tower>{{Cite Book|quote='Link, I've heard enough. Your petty envy has become more than a nuisance. It's time you were taught some manners. I hereby banish you to the Tower for three days, effectively immediately!'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=62}}</ref>

If, however, {{Term|TSP|Link}} has the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth}} and decides to use it, the mirror will reveal that Charles is actually {{Term|TSP|Ganon}} in disguise, who immediately takes this opportunity to steal the Triforce of Wisdom. After {{Term|TSP|Link}} defeats the false Morian knight and recovers the Triforce of Wisdom, he will return to {{Term|TSP|North Castle}} to be thanked by the king for saving {{Term|TSP|Hyrule}} once again.
