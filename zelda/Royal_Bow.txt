The '''Royal Bow''' {{Exp Game|BotW, TotK}} is a recurring weapon in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' series. It belongs to the [[Royal Gear]] equipment and is associated with the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]].

{{Compendium/Extract|Royal Bow}}
{{Plural|BotW|Royal Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} that {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} used to gift to the most talented archers in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Bow}}<br/>In the past, the king of Hyrule presented this bow to only the most talented archers in the land. Its combat capabilities are as impressive as its extravagant design.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Both their combat ability and design are noted to be impressive.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} or in use by high-tier {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} and in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.

When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Royal Bow}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Princess Zelda|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} (Archer) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Due to their metallic composition, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Bow}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are outdoors during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. Finally, they will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Royal Bow}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is associated with the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
The Royal Bow returns in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'' and is mostly found at [[Hyrule Castle]].
