{{Term/Store|Lanayru Road - West Gate||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lanayru Road - West Gate'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - West Gate}} is located between [[East Necluda]] and the [[Lanayru Great Spring]]. {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - West Gate}} is the crossroads leading from [[Kakariko Village]], [[Rabia Plain]], and the [[Lanayru Promenade]]. The area is full of long [[Grass]], and {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} can be found on the cliffs side.

After the "[[Seek Out Impa]]" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} has been completed, a [[Traveler]] can be found at the intersection of these three locations. If this Traveler is the first one that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has spoken to, the Traveler will compliment {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s body and ask him to join the [[Yiga Clan]].<ref>{{Cite|I can tell you've trained your body well... I'd say...you're familiar with both sword and bow, correct? That's a good skill set. Why don't you join the Yiga Clan?|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires about the Yiga Clan, the Traveler will begin to tell him that they are a faction of powerful warriors that were founded by {{Term|BotW|Master Kohga|link}} with the goal of eliminating the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champion|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Yiga Clan?] What? You don't know? Fine... I'll tell you... The Yiga Clan... It's...|Traveler|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A powerful, brave group of warriors, founded by Master Kohga and dedicated to defeating a hero thought long dead.|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> Upon revealing this, the Traveler will drop their disguise and attack {{Term|BotW|Link}} as a [[Yiga Footsoldier]].<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I will take your life!}}|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} expresses disinterest in the subject, the Traveler will stop him and ask to explain his story first before attacking {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll pass.] What?! You're refusing? Why don't you at least hear what I gotta say first? The Yiga Clan... It's...|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> If this is not {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s first encounter with a Yiga Traveler, the Traveler will instead strike up a conversation about tomorrow's weather before lamenting that {{Term|BotW|Link}} will not be alive to enjoy it.<ref>{{Cite|I heard the weather is going to be nice tomorrow.|Traveler|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Can't wait!] Too bad you won't be alive to enjoy it...|Traveler|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|For Master Kohga!|Traveler|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found by following a [[Flower Trail]], starting on a ledge of the left cliff in the north passage. The Flower will move along the cliff before moving to the ground, after which it will begin moving up the cliff on the right where the {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear.
