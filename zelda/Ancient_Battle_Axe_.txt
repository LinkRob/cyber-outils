{{Compendium/Extract|Ancient Battle Axe+}}
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe+}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}} in "A Modest Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe+}} has a chance to spawn. They are improved versions of {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe|link}} that deal more damage.<ref name="Inventory"/> Due to their sharpness, it is theorized they may have been used to forge paths.<ref name="Inventory"/>

The sixth weapon {{Term|BotW|Nebb|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to show him during "{{Term|BotW|The Weapon Connoisseur|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe+}}, which he claims are not common around {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you ever heard of an {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient battle axe+}}? Grandpa said you can't find those around here. If you get ahold of one, let me see it|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings him one, {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will be excited and give him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a sign of his gratitude.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that...an {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient battle axe+}}? So cool! Let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks for lettin' me see! Take this as a thank you!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

Since they are made using {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe+}} will deal more damage when they are used to fight {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}. They will not burn when exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, nor will they be able to be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. They do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, but they will float if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe+}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a collection of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>
