{{Term/Store|Pink Zol|Pink Zols|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Pink Zol|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Pink Zol}} are slime-like enemies that can be found throughout {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}. They can only move vertically, moving on every other beat in one direction until they hit a wall and bounce back the opposite way. Upon being defeated, they will then split into one or two {{Plural|CoH|Pink Gel|link}}.
