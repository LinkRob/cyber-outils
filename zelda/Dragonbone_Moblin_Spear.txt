{{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Moblin Spear}} are {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} favored by {{Plural|BotW|Black Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Moblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Moblin|link}}. Their spearheads are made from fossilized bones with added spikes, increasing their attack power.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} made by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Most commonly, they can be found in {{Plural|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Eldin Mountains|link}}.

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Moblin Spear}} will burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float when they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Moblin Spear}} do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
