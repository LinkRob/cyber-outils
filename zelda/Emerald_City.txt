'''{{Name|Japanese|Emerald City}}''' is a location in {{RTBToL}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|RTBToL|Emerald City}} is the large central town found on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 12}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Page 13}}, and {{Term|RTBToL|Page 14}}. It is home to a large Castle, where the princess that {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} is trying to find resides. The town like receives its name from the emerald-colored rooftops that adorn every building. When {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} and his friends visit for the first time, there is currently a celebration going on, and people from all over seem to have traveled to come.

==Trivia==
*A character strongly resembling {{Term|RTBToL|Link|link}} makes an appearance in the background, talking to a man.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|RTBToL|Emerald City}}'s name is a reference to ''{{wp|The Wizard of Oz}}''.
