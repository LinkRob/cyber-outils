{{Term/Store|Fireproof Elixir|Fireproof Elixirs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fireproof Elixir}}<br/>Grants a fireproof effect, which prevents your body from catching fire.<br/>Be sure to pack this when venturing out to explore Death Mountain.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Fireproof Elixirs will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} {{Term|BotW|Flame Guard|link}} when drunk, preventing him from catching fire in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They're useful when traversing {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Two merchants sell Fireproof Elixirs. {{Term|BotW|Gaile|link}}, a worker at {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}}, has them available for travelers heading to {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're headed to Goron City, I'll sell you some of my {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixirs}}.|Gaile|BotW}}</ref> She suggests taking at least three, as this is what regular Goron City travelers do.<ref>{{Cite|Even Goron City regulars usually take {{Color|BotW Red|at least three}} elixirs with them.|Gaile|BotW}}</ref> One elixir costs 60 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, two cost 110 Rupees, and three cost 150 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|It's {{Color|BotW Red|60 rupees}} for one, {{Color|BotW Red|110 rupees}} for two, and {{Color|BotW Red|150 rupees}} for three. Buying in bulk pays off here!|Gaile|BotW}}</ref> If Link refuses to buy, Gaile advises him to give up on Goron City.<ref>{{Cite|I see. I won't waste my breath arguing about it. Just trust me when I say you should give up on going to Goron City.|Gaile|BotW}}</ref> Once Link has at least three, she'll tell him he's ready to head to Goron City.<ref>{{Cite|If you have that many, you should be able to make it to Goron City no problem.|Gaile|BotW}}</ref>

In Goron City, Link can speak to {{Term|BotW|Offrak|link}}, who will ask if he needs a Fireproof Elixir.<ref>{{Cite|Need a {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixir}}? You do, yeah?|Offrak|BotW}}</ref> If Link admits that he does, Offrak will offer to sell him one for 60 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|[I probably do.] Here's what I'll do, fella. Just for you, I'll sell ya that {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixir}} at a special price of only {{Color|BotW Red|60 rupees}}!|Offrak|BotW}}</ref> Link can refuse, causing Offrak to become angry and say he shouldn't come crying to him when he's on fire.<ref>{{Cite|Well, don't come cryin' to me when you're on fire, fella!|Offrak|BotW}}</ref> Offrak will attempt to coerce Link into buying more, saying a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} will need it.<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} like you will need more than that! A {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixir}} will cost ya {{Color|BotW Red|60 rupees}}.|Offrak|BotW}}</ref> Once Link stops buying, Offrak comments under his breath that Link could easily make his own.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|All you need to do is cook one}} {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizard with monster parts}}{{Color|BotW Gray|, fella...}}}}|Offrak|BotW}}</ref>

Fireproof Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|BotW|Smotherwing Butterfly|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Lizard|link}}
