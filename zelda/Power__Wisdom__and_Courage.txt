"{{Term/Store|Power, Wisdom, and Courage||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: Power, Wisdom, and Courage}}<br/>02/12/2019 |author= Nintendo |published= February 12, 2019 |retrieved= February 15, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/032f028f/index.html |title= Event: Power, Wisdom, and Courage - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Power, Wisdom, and Courage}}" is an event wherein a greater number of {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} from {{TLoZ|Series}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits from the Legend of Zelda are coming to your Spirit Board! Defeat them to earn more gold than usual.<br/><br/>The Legend-class Link (The Legend of Zelda) and the Wind Fish will also appear at set times. Don't miss out! |author= Nintendo |published= February 12, 2019 |retrieved= February 15, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/032f028f/index.html |title= Event: Power, Wisdom, and Courage - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, to celebrate the reveal of {{LA|NS}} for the [[Nintendo Switch]], the {{Term|SSBU|Link|display=Link {{Small|(The Legend of Zelda)}}|link}} and {{Term|SSBU|Wind Fish|link}} {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} are set to appear at set times each day.

==Trivia==
*The icons for {{Term|SSBU|Moosh|link}} and {{Term|SSBU|Dimitri|link}} that are used on the official website are different from the ones used in-game. The versions used on the official site focus on {{Term|SSBU|Moosh}} and {{Term|SSBU|Dimitri}} respectively whereas the ones in-game focuses the creatures in tandem with {{Term|SSBU|Link|link}}.
