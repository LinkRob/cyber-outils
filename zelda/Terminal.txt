{{Term/Store|Terminal|Terminals|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Naboris"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Terminal}} are pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} that, when activated, allow one to control a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref name="Naboris"/><ref>{{Cite|To take back Rudania, you'll need to activate all of the {{Color|BotW Red|terminals}}.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Take Ruta back by activating all of the {{Color|BotW Red|terminals}}.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You'll need to activate all of the {{Color|BotW Red|terminals}} to take back control.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> Each {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} has five {{Plural|BotW|Terminal}}. After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} distills a {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} from the {{Plural|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} will point out the glowing {{Plural|BotW|Marker|link}} that represent the {{Plural|BotW|Terminal}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|terminals}} that {{Color|BotW Red|control}} Rudania are noted by the glowing points on your map.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You will see several glowing points on your map which represent the {{Color|BotW Red|terminals}} that {{Color|BotW Red|control}} Ruta.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You'll need to look closely at your map of the Divine Beast. The {{Color|BotW Red|terminals}} that will {{Color|BotW Red|activate}} Medoh are marked by those glowing points.|Revali|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|terminals}} that {{Color|BotW Red|control}} Naboris are noted by a series of glowing points on your map.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} activates a {{Term|BotW|Terminal}}, it will authenticate his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref>

In {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}} has four {{Plural|BotW|Terminal}}. When these {{Plural|BotW|Terminal}} are activated, they will deactivate one of the locks on the door in the back of the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Deactivated one lock of the door.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref> They will then inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} how many {{Plural|BotW|Terminal}} he still has to activate.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|There are three terminals remaining.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|There are two terminals remaining.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|There is one terminal remaining.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref> Once all of them are running, the final {{Term|BotW|Terminal}} will direct {{Term|BotW|Link}} towards the door.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|All terminal locks have been deactivated. You can now access the deepest area.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref>
