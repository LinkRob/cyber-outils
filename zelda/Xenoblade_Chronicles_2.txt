"{{Term/Store|[Xenoblade Chronicles 2]||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|[Xenoblade Chronicles 2]}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is a collaboration with [https://xenoblade.fandom.com/wiki/Xenoblade_Chronicles_2?so=search Xenoblade Chronicles 2] and is obtained after downloading the [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild#Updates|Version 1.3.3 update]] and upon leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} will activate upon loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.

When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is tasked with finding three red {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Look at the night sky at these locations and find the red shooting stars!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} also instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to look to the south from the middle of the largest bridge, to the east from the skull's left eye, and to the southwest from the peak of a tall, pierced mountain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|The southern sky from the middle of the largest bridge. The eastern sky from the skull's left eye. The southwestern sky from the peak of the tall, pierced snowy mountain.}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is looking at the correct place during the night, a red {{Term|BotW|Shooting Star}} will come hurtling down and crash, leaving a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that emits a red column of light during the night. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} will remain at the location it landed and emit the column of light every night until opened. It can also be found and opened during the day but will not emit the column of light to highlight its location.

The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing [[Salvager Headwear]] can be found by looking south from the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Hylia|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} with the {{Term|BotW|Salvager Vest|link}} will fall while looking east from the left eye of {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}}. Finally, the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} storing the {{Term|BotW|Salvager Trousers|link}} can be found looking southwest from {{Term|BotW|Hebra Peak|link}}. Upon retrieving all three pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}}.
