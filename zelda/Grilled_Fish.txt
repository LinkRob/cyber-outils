'''Grilled Fish''' is an Object and [[Echo]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Grilled Fish|This perfectly grilled, flaky fish can instantly entice certain monsters with its irresistible, savory aroma.<ref name="Grilled Fish">{{Cite|This perfectly grilled, flaky fish can instantly entice certain monsters with its irresistible, savory aroma.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Grilled Fish can be obtained during the [[Deliver the Grilled Fish!]] [[side quest]] in [[Seesyde Village]]. During the quest, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can use the [[Tri Rod]] on the fish to learn the Grilled Fish [[Echo]], but must use the original to complete the quest.
