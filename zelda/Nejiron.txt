{{Term/Store|Nejiron|Nejirons|Series|plural}} are enemies in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 194}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= That's a {{Color|MM White|Nejiron}}. Don't get too close to it. Sniff...Sniff... It smells like gun powder!
}}

Nejirons hide in the ground along the [[Road to Ikana]] and inside the [[Stone Tower Temple]], emerging to attack [[Link]] by rolling into him. These creatures smell of gunpowder,<ref>{{Cite|That's a Nejiron. Don't get too close to it. Sniff...Sniff... It smells like gun powder!|Tatl|MM}}</ref> alluding to their volatile nature, and will explode when hit. Long-range weapons such as the [[Bow]] and [[Hookshot]] are best used to deal with them. However, each time one is defeated, another will emerge from the ground to take its place. The [[Stone Mask]] can be used to avoid an encounter with these creatures completely.

==Nomenclature==
The Japanese word "Neji" means twisted or warped, whereas the suffix -ron may refer to a [[Goron]]. According to the [[Zelda.com|official ''The Legend of Zelda'' website]], Nejirons have evolved to mimic the appearance of Gorons in order to fool their prey.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Nejirons appear to be simple creatures, but they are in fact highly evolved hunting machines. They have adapted to resemble a rolling Goron, but instead of passing by peacefully, a Nejiron will charge ruthlessly into {{Typo|is|its}} victim.|url= http://web.archive.org/web/20160305150453/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/n.jsp|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia|site= Zelda.com}}</ref>
