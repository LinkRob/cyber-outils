'''Fireflies''' are [[enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Fireflies are a smaller, light-green variant of [[Locassin]]s. Two groups of them are encountered in the [[Shrine of Fire]]. They behave largely the same as Loccasins, but are a lot weaker. If a Firefly hits [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], she takes half a [[Heart]] of damage. Zelda can defeat a Firefly by hitting it with the [[Wand]] twice.
