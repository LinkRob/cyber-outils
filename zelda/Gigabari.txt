{{Term/Store|Gigabari|Gigabari|Series, ALBW}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Sub-Boss}} in {{ALBW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Term|ALBW|Gigabari}} is a gigantic {{Term|ALBW|Bari|link}} that first appears in the {{Term|ALBW|Swamp Palace|link}} in its yellow form, where it is battled in a large room littered with {{Plural|ALBW|Skull|link}}. A purple {{Term|ALBW|Gigabari}} later appears in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle|link}}, battled in a smaller room with moving grated floors above a pool of [[Lava]].

{{Plural|ALBW|Gigabari}} exhibit the same behavior as their smaller counterparts, slowly floating around and electrifying themselves every few seconds, but they require many more hits to defeat. When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} strikes it with his {{Term|ALBW|Sword|link}}, he will bounce backwards like when he hits a {{Term|ALBW|Hardhat Beetle|link|display=Hardhat Beetle}}, but if the {{Term|ALBW|Gigabari}} is electrified when struck, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} will be harmed instead. Once a {{Term|ALBW|Gigabari}} takes enough damage, it will burst and split into fifteen {{Plural|ALBW|Biri|link}} of the same color. Ranged weapons such as the {{Term|ALBW|Bow|link}} and {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb|link}} can be used to safely damage the {{Term|ALBW|Gigabari}} and its resulting {{Plural|ALBW|Biri}}.
