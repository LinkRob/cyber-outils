{{Term/Store|Guy||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Guy|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
Guy is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} who can be found resting next to the oasis in the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}. He is originally from {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, <ref>{{Cite|I came here from Rito Village...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> but he left his home for the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} in order to earn money.<ref>{{Cite|I had a great plan, you know. I left Rito Village to try to earn some money out in the world...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to Guy, he asks if they have met before.<ref>{{Cite|Hello there... But before I say any more... Have we...met before?|Guy|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Guy}} describes it as a modest village on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, Rito Village... Far to the north of here... It's a modest village on the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Frontier}}...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he has been to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} before, {{Term|BotW|Guy}} remarks that he must have found it cold.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... It must have felt quite cold to you, didn't it?|Guy|BotW}}</ref>

Due to the {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, {{Term|BotW|Guy}} has been unable to continue on his journey.<ref>{{Cite|But it's so incredibly hot here during the day. It's been tough to work up the will to go any farther.|Guy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's so hot at midday, it's impossible to make it to {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}} unaided!|Guy|BotW}}</ref> Despite not being allowed inside, he would like to see what {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} looks like.<ref>{{Cite|I would like to see what Gerudo Town looks like, even if I can't go inside... It's just so hot...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He then mentions that he has heard of a cooling {{Term|BotW|Elixir|link}} that is made from {{Plural|BotW|Insect|link}} with cooling effects and {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I heard there's an {{Color|BotW Red|elixir that can help beat the heat}}. It's made from {{Color|BotW Blue|insects with cooling effects}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|monster parts}}.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> Guy says that they are not selling the Elixir anywhere and he is not good at {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But they don't seem to be selling the elixir anywhere...|Guy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But they don't sell the stuff anywhere, and I'm a lousy cook.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> However, Guy has heard that insects with cooling effects live in the cold parts of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I've heard {{Color|BotW Blue|insects with these cooling effects}} live in {{Color|BotW Red|the cold parts of the Gerudo Highlands}}, but...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> but because of how rough the [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]] are there, he has had a hard time motivating himself to gather the <ref>{{Cite|Well, the monsters there are rough, to say the least, so I'm not having a lot of success getting myself motivated...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> After speaking with Guy, Link receives the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|An Ice Guy|link}}".

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Guy}} again, he will mutter under his breath that he is allergic to "monster punches".<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Gray|<small>Too bad I'm allergic to monster punches...</small>}}|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He regrets coming to the Desert without a cooling Elixir and wishes someone had mentioned the Heat before he came all the way there.<ref>{{Cite|I should have made one of those {{Color|BotW Red|elixirs that stave off the heat}} of the desert.|Guy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I wish somebody had mentioned the crazy heat before I came all the way out here...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He also feels that the nights in the Gerudo Desert are a nice break from the Heat,<ref>{{Cite|Nights in the desert are cool and breezy. Such a nice break from the oppressive heat...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> but that the night is also scary.<ref>{{Cite|But the night... It's kinda scary...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> Guy admits that he is afraid of the dark.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Gray|<small>I'm afraid of the dark...</small>}}|Guy|BotW}}</ref>

When Link returns to Guy with a {{Term|BotW|Chilly Elixir|link}}, he will thank Link, as he can now leave the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} during the day.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! Now I can get across the desert while it's still light out!|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He rewards him with a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here, let me give you something in return.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He will also teach Link that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)|link}} word for "thank you" is ''sarqso''.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, in Gerudo, "thank you" is "sarqso." SARQSO!|Guy|BotW}}</ref> When Guy is finished speaking, the Side Quest is completed.

Although Link gives him the Chilly Elixir, Guy never leaves Kara Kara Bazaar. At night, his excuse is that the Desert is not safe during this time.<ref>{{Cite|[Not gonna leave yet?] With the elixir, I can go as soon as I want, but... Well, the desert isn't all that safe at night. I think I'll wait till it's light out.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> However, he does not leave during the day either, saying that there is no rush and that he might leave tomorrow.<ref>{{Cite|[Not gonna leave yet?] With the elixir, I can go as soon as I want, but...there's no rush, really. I think I'll leave tomorrow.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> Guy also says that he never understood how hot the Desert was before he experienced it.<ref>{{Cite|I never knew the desert in the middle of the day could get so darned hot. I guess you can't really understand something until you've experienced it for yourself, huh...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He also mentions his child, saying he will have to get them to travel as well.<ref name="Young One"/>

{{Term|BotW|Guy}} greets {{Term|BotW|Link}} based on the time of day, correcting himself with the Gerudo word along with it. When he says "good morning" to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Guy}} ammends his words and says that the Gerudo word is "sav'otta".<ref>{{Cite|Good morning... Though it's "sav'otta" in Gerudo, isn't it?|Guy|BotW}}</ref> In the evenings, {{Term|BotW|Guy}} shares that the Gerudo word for "good evening" is "sav'saaba".<ref>{{Cite|Good evening... Though I guess it's "sav'saaba in Gerudo, isn't it?|Guy|BotW}}</ref>
