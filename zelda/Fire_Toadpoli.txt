{{Term/Store|Fire Toadpoli|Fire Toadpolis|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 177 {{Exp Game|TP, TPHD}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TPHD|Fire Toadpoli}} are armored, two-legged {{Plural|TPHD|Frog|link}}-like enemies that are found in the {{Term|TPHD|Goron Mines|link}}, swimming in {{Term|TPHD|Lava}}. They act very similarly to {{Plural|Series|Octorok|link}}. They spit fireballs at {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} that can burn his {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Shield|link|display= Ordon}} or {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Shield|link}}. If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} comes too close to them, they dive back under the {{Term|TPHD|Lava|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can slice the fireballs with his {{Term|TPHD|Sword|link}} and deflect them back, defeating the {{Plural|TPHD|Fire Toadpoli}}.
