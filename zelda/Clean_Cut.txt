{{Term/Store|Clean Cut||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a {{Term|SS|Mini-Game|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Clean Cut|Map|SSHD}}</ref>

==Rules==
{{Term|SS|Clean Cut}} can be played on {{Term|SS|Bamboo Island|link}}. The {{Term|SS|Mini-Game|link}} is run by {{Term|SS|Peater|link}}, who tolls {{Term|SS|Link|link}} 10 {{Plural|SS|Rupee|link}} to play. When the game begins, {{Term|SS|Link|link}} will be faced with a giant bamboo plant. He must cut the plant as many times as possible before the top of the bamboo touches the ground.

==Strategy==
*Performing a {{Term|SS|Spin Attack|link}} counts as hitting the bamboo twice.
*Cutting two pieces of bamboo at the same time as they fall counts as two cuts.
*Stabbing the bamboo causes it to drop vertically, allowing for a reliable high number of cuts.
