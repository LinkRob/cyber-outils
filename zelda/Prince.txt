The {{Term/Store|Prince||Series, CoH}} is a character in {{CoH}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>

==Biography==
The {{Term|CoH|Prince}} is found asleep in a small cave in {{Term|CoH|Lake Hylia|link}}. The {{Plural|CoH|Ghost|link}} guarding him are mostly silent, save for one who tells the hero the prince is safe with them.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|CoH Blue|prince}} belongs to us now. Don't worry. We'll keep him safe! Uweee hee hee!|Ghost|CoH}}</ref> Attacking the ghost who speaks reveals it to be a {{Term|CoH|Ghast|link}}, and defeating it causes the {{Term|CoH|Prince}} to wake up. In thanks, he gives the hero the {{Term|CoH|Zora's Flippers|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Some {{Color|CoH Blue|ghosts}} had me under their spell? Thanks for saving me! Here, take these {{Color|CoH Yellow|flippers}} as thanks!|Prince|CoH}}</ref>
