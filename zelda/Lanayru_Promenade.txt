The {{Term/Store|Lanayru Promenade||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lanayru Promenade'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Promenade}} is located in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. It has been left in ruins, with several walkways being either crumbled or flooded. A {{Term|BotW|Raft|link}} can be used to navigate the {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - East Gate|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - West Gate|link}} serve as exits for the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Promenade|display=Promenade}}

Behind the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}} central to the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Promenade}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Dow Na'eh Shrine|link}} in a cave. This cave contains {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}}. Other {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit}} can be found throughout the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Promenade|display=Promenade}}.

After being freed from {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Naydra|link}} will fly through the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Promenade}} at 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM. {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}} will appear around it, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} can travel with using his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}.

Several {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} are located within the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Promenade}}. The contents of these {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chests}} are five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}, five {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}}, five {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}}, two {{Plural|BotW|Opal|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Broadsword|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Meteor Rod|link}}, and two {{Plural|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.

Two {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Promenade}}. One is hiding under a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}} on top of the fountain. The second can be revealed by diving into a circle of lily pads in the {{Term|BotW|Water}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds them, they will each give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
