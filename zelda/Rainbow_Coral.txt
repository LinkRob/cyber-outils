{{Term/Store|Rainbow Coral|Rainbow Corals|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 135"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Rainbow Coral}} are rare {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Cove of Transition|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Rainbow Coral}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}}''', ''"Clear with halved energy!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Rainbow Coral}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Water Temple}}''', ''"Transport the orb quickly!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Rainbow Coral}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Rainbow Coral}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Ninja Gi|link}}.<ref name="E 135"/>
