The '''Band Rehearsal''' is a small event in which {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} can partake in {{MM}}.{{Name Ref|MM}} When Link first arrives in {{Term|MM3D|Zora Hall}}, he can assist the stage hands in adjustments with the {{Term|MM3D|Torch|display=lighting|link}} and sound. He can solve the lighting problems with {{Plural|MM3D|Fire Arrow|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Are you free? If you have a sec, could you go light the second floor's seating lights? Unless those are on, the stage lights won't come on. Thanks.|Zora|MM}}</ref> earning a few {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee|link}} for his trouble,<ref>{{Cite|Are you the one who turned those lights on? You saved me. Here...Go buy whatever you like!|Zora|MM}}</ref> and play notes for the sound technician as {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link|link}}, but the sound is always either too loud or too soft.<ref>{{Cite|Mikau, can you play me a quick note? Ack! That's a little too loud. I guess I'll turn it down a bit... OK. Thanks, Mikau.|Zora|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Mikau, can you play me a quick note? Huh? That's a little too quiet... I guess I'll turn it up a bit... OK. Thanks, Mikau.|Zora|MM}}</ref>

After he has eradicated the evil in the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can impersonate {{Term|MM3D|Mikau|link}} and join the other members of {{Term|MM3D|The Indigo-Go's}} in rehearsing for their performance at the {{Term|MM3D|Carnival of Time}}. The song they play is the same song that {{Term|MM3D|Mikau}} and {{Term|MM3D|Japas|link}} wrote together in their {{Term|MM3D|Jam Session|link}}, with added {{Term|MM3D|Lulu|display=vocal|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Tijo|display=drum|link}}, and {{Term|MM3D|Evan|display=piano|link}} elements. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can begin the rehearsal by talking to {{Term|MM3D|Lulu}}, and a brief cutscene ensues. Apparently, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} was late to the rehearsal stage.<ref>{{Cite|What have you been doing? You're late, Mikau! Ooh! Ooh! Guess what! Lulu's voice... She's gotten it back!|Tijo|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You're late, Mikau! Where were you while you were skippin' practice? Lulu's been waiting for you.|Japas|MM}}</ref>

All the {{Plural|MM3D|Zora|link}} of {{Term|MM3D|Zora Hall}} sit up on the ledge to observe. The rehearsal is for the performance at the {{Term|MM3D|Carnival of Time}}, which they do at the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar|link}} during the {{Term|MM3D|New Day|link}}.
