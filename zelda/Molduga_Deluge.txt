{{Term/Store|Molduga Deluge||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Molduga Deluge|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Molduga Deluge}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} with {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Riju|link}} as its pilots. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, and 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

For the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to defeat 2,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within six minutes. These {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} include {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Wizzrobe|link}} of each element. At first, only the bottom half of the map contains any {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, although more spawn to fill up the top half once Vah Naboris approaches the area. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 2,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed.
