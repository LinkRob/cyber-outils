{{Term/Store|Gohma's Eye|Gohma's Eyes|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 125"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Gohma's Eye}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Buzz Blob Cave|link}}''', ''"Clear using only Bombs!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Gohma's Eye}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Moblin Base|link}}''', ''"Defeat all foes sans sword!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Gohma's Eye}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}''', ''"Adventure in the dark!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Gohma's Eye}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Forest Temple}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Gohma's Eye}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Gohma's Eye}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Loungewear|link}}.<ref name="E 125"/>
