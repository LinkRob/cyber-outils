The {{Term/Store|Goron Temple||Series, PH}} is a Temple in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 156}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Temple==
The dungeon is located on [[Goron Island]]. [[Link]] cannot access the dungeon until he becomes an official member of the [[Goron]] tribe by completing a quiz made by the [[Biggoron]], the head Goron of the island and traveling through a maze. After meeting the conditions, he must chase [[Gongoron]] across the island, first through the inhabited area, and then through the aforementioned maze.

==Themes and Navigation==
[[File:Bomchu Puzzle PH.png|thumb|left|Bombchu navigating over sand to a switch]]
The Temple has a rocky theme with bodies of quicksand, which are dangerous for Link because he can sink into the sand, taking damage as a result. As the young hero explores the dungeon, he finds [[Bombchu]] after a unique puzzle where he must alternate control between him and Gongoron to press a series of switches. Later, with the help of Gongoron, Link defeats the boss, [[Dongorongo]], and obtains the [[Crimsonine]], one of the three Pure Metals needed to forge the [[Phantom Sword]].

It is optional whether this Temple is completed before or after completion of the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}} and/or [[Mutoh's Temple]].
