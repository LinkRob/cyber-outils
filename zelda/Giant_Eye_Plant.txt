{{Term/Store|Giant Eye Plant|Giant Eye Plants|Series, PH|plural}} are a minibosses in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 181}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
A Giant Eye Plant appears after [[Link]] sets out to find [[Molida Island]]. This creature attempts to sink the [[SS Linebeck]] by shooting giant spiked seeds. To avoid them, Link must stay in motion and aiming for the monster's eye with the [[Cannon]]. They will die after taking five hits from the {{Term|PH|Cannon}}. The Giant Eye Plant appears again when returning to [[Bannan Island]] after giving the [[Old Wayfarer]] the [[Wood Heart]].
