The {{Term/Store|Seed of Life||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 137}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The [[Seed of Life]] is the reward for completing the [[Explorer's Crypt]], and is guarded by [[Gleeok]]. It is the seventh of the eight [[Essence of Nature|Essences of Nature]] collected by Link. Its description text reads: "Life begins anew when birds carry this, the [[Seed of Life]], to new lands!"<ref>"Life begins anew when birds carry this, the [[Seed of Life]], to new lands!" – N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]])</ref>
