The {{Name|Fan|{{Term/Store|Water Lady||Series}}}} is a character in {{TWoG}}.{{Fact}} She is a woman who appears near the end of [[Ahitaru]].

==Biography==
The Water Lady is encountered near the [[Gohma Pit]] in the coast of Ahitaru. She can be found juggling a jar. She offers [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] some [[Water of Life]] for defeating the [[Gohma]]s, healing her in the process.<ref>{{Cite|Nice job over the Gohma Pit. Here, have a drink.|Water Lady|TWoG}}</ref> Additionally, the Water Lady points out that there is a fisherman, [[Yokan]], trapped in the head of a giant fish carcass. This prompts Zelda to go investigate.
