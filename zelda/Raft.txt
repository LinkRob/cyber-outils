{{Term/Store|Raft|Rafts|Series, TLoZ, TAoL, LA, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |page= 383{{Exp Game|TLoZ}} & 386{{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 62}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Have you gone sailing with a {{Color|BotW Red|raft}}?|Armes|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Raft}}: Use this on piers to cross to the opposite shore.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref><ref name="cheval">{{Cite Book|quote={{color|E Blue|Cheval Rope (Quest)}} This special rope appears in {{OoA|-}}. It is water resistant and therefore especially suited to building a raft. Giving it to Rafton allows him to do so. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=119 {{Exp Game|OoA}} }}</ref>{{Name Ref|LADX, LANS, TWW, TWWHD, ALBW, TFH, TFoE, ZA}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, the Raft appears in [[Level 3]]. It is used to cross bodies of water such as rivers and lakes. The Raft can only be launched from [[dock]]s.

==={{TAoL|-}}===
In {{TAoL}}, the Raft can be found in the [[Island Palace]]. It is used to cross bodies of water such as rivers and lakes. It can only be launched from docks.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= When you get this, you can cross the sea from the dock. What's on the other side of the sea?|game= TAoL|page= 46}}</ref>

==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, [[Link]] can ride a Raft during the [[Rapids Ride]] minigame.

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, if Link gives [[Rafton]] the [[Cheval Rope]], he will give Link a Raft that can be used to cross deep water.<ref name="cheval"/> After Link is washed ashore on [[Crescent Island]], the Raft is destroyed.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Rafts can be found floating around the [[Great Sea]] with large masts with [[skull]]s on top. Some Rafts are protected by [[Bokoblin]]s, which may have [[pot]]s, [[barrel]]s, and [[Treasure Chest]]s. Rafts can also be found around islands where [[Traveling Merchants]] are found.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, Rafts appear in the [[Swamp Palace (A Link Between Worlds)|Swamp Palace]], [[Lorule Castle]], and the [[Treasure Hunter Cave]] found in the [[Southern Ruins]]. They can be used to traverse water and lava in conjunction with the [[Hookshot]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, Rafts appear in the [[Cove of Transition]] and [[Water Temple (Tri Force Heroes)|Water Temple]] levels of the [[Riverside]] stage. They can be used to traverse water in conjunction with the [[Gripshot]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, Link can use Rafts to cross bodies of [[Water (Terrain)|water]]. He must use a [[Korok Leaf]] to propel the Raft. They can be used to avoid the damage that would come by [[swimming]] in colder bodies of water, such as the [[River of the Dead]].

==Spinoff appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TFoE|-}}===
In {{TFoE}}, Rafts appear in several water-based stages, including [[Spearfish Falls]], [[Firestone Lake]], and in the tar pits of [[Ganon's Lair]]. They are used to safely cross these waters and will immediately move once Link stands on one.

==={{ZA|-}}===
In {{ZA}}, the Raft item can be found next to a pond in the [[Meadow of Skotness]] near [[Great Wimbich]]. It is used to cross the flooded passage in the [[Shrine of Water]]. Another Raft also appears in the Port Town of [[Seacoast Plain]], belonging to a seafarer. He allows [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] to take the Raft to [[Sea Island]], provided that she pays him two [[Rupee]]s and has the [[Vial of Winds]], as the offshore wind has died down.<ref>{{Cite|If you wish to cross the sea, bring us twenty Rubies and a Vial of Wind. The offshore wind has died down, and you must bring your own breeze.|Rafter|ZA}}</ref> The same Raft can be used to return to the Port Town from Sea Island.

==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, the Raft appears as an [[Item Card]] in [[Adventure Mode]]. It can be used to cross water from docks, removing locks on certain adjacent squares. In the {{TP|-}} DLC pack, the [[8-Bit Power Bracelet]] can be unlocked in the Twilight Map. It features the [[Power Bracelet]] paired with the Raft.
{{Noncanon|End}}
