{{Plural|BotW|Cobble Crusher}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are very thick but do not possess a cutting edge.<ref name="Inventory"/> Therefore, they are consequently used as crushing {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, due to the weight of their component metal.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Goron Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that are made by {{Plural|BotW|Goron}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 171}}</ref> They are most commonly found around {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}} and throughout the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Mountains|link}}. In addition to this, they have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} found from scanning the {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Should {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} ever lose the {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker|link}}, he can ask {{Term|BotW|Rohan|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Goron City}} to make him a new one in exchange for a {{Term|BotW|Cobble Crusher}}, a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}, and five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Looks like ya don't have the {{Color|BotW Blue|Boulder Breaker}}... If ya want a new one, I'm gonna need a {{Color|BotW Blue|cobble crusher}}, {{Color|BotW Red|five pieces}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}.|Rohan|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Cobble Crusher}} have a hidden property which causes their damage output to be doubled when used on the {{Term|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} atop a {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus|link}}. In this vein, they will immediately destroy {{Plural|BotW|Stone Pebblit|link}} when used against them.

As {{Plural|BotW|Cobble Crusher}} are made out of metal, they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. This also means that they can be manipulated through the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. Finally, they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has one equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}.
