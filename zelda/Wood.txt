==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, {{Term|BotW|Wood}} can be obtained from cutting down {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} or found at various settlements like {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}}. They can be chopped using any bladed {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, or knocked down with {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}. Once a {{Term|BotW|Tree}} is felled, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can strike, or blow up, the fallen {{Term|BotW|Tree}} to create a bundle of {{Term|BotW|Wood}}. Wood is also one of the most common resources in [[Hyrule]], since you can find them virtually anywhere.

{{Term|BotW|Wood}} can be used to light {{Plural|BotW|Campfire|link}}, along with a flammable {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} or a piece of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} and a metallic {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}.<ref>{{Cite|A portable bundle of wood. You can use this to make a campfire if you have something to light it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> In addition, {{Term|BotW|Wood}} can be used for side quests such as the "{{Term|BotW|Hylian Homeowner|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} to buy a house<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to need you to bring me {{Color|BotW Red|30 wood bundles}}. Building materials... You know how it is.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>, as well as during the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} to construct houses for {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}<ref>{{Cite|Well... To get started, I suppose we'll need houses for people to live in. We can't invite them without homes. Do you think you can bring me 10 bundles of wood?|Hudson|BotW}}</ref>.

To remake the [[Great Eagle Bow]], Link must bring [[Harth]] a [[Swallow Bow]], a [[Diamond]], and five bundles of wood.<ref>{{Cite|I cannot help but notice that your {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Eagle Bow}} is missing.|Harth|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you want me to make another one, I will need a {{Color|BotW Blue|swallow bow}}, a {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|five}} bundles of {{Color|BotW Blue|wood}}.|Harth|BotW}}</ref>
