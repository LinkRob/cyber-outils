"{{Term/Store|Subdued Ceremony||Series, BotW}}" is the first {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Subdued Ceremony}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #1}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory}} can be activated at the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Ground Ruins|link}}, just south of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}. It begins with the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} watching {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} perform a ceremony with {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} kneeled in front of her, recognizing him as the hero of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hero of Hyrule, chosen by the sword that seal the darkness... You have shown unflinching bravery and skill in the face of darkness and adversity. And have proven yourself worthy of the blessings of the Goddess Hylia.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> During her speech, {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} are shown to be concerned by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} demeanor.

As {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} continues, {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} comments to the others that the ceremony is not very uplifting and that she sounds as though they have already lost.<ref>{{Cite|Gee, this is uplifting... She's making it sound like we already lost.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} then reminds {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} that this was his idea,<ref>{{Cite|Wasn't this your idea? You're the one who wanted to designate the appointed knight with all the ceremonial pomp, grandeur, and nonsense we could muster!|Revali|BotW}}</ref> revealing that he has little faith in {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s abilities.<ref>{{Cite|And if you ask me, the whole thing does seem to be overkill. I think I'm on the same page as the princess regarding...this boy.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} dismisses {{Term|BotW|Revali}}'s criticisms,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, give it a rest.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> stating that she believes {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} sees {{Term|BotW|Link}} as a reminder of her own failure to unlock her sealing power.<ref>{{Cite|That boy is a living reminder of her own failures. Well, at least that's how the princess sees him.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}} then ends with {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} staring down at {{Term|BotW|Link}}.
