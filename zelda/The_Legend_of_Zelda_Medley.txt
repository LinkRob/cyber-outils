==Overview==
===''Daigasso! Band Brothers''===
"{{Term|Daigasso! Band Brothers|The Legend of Zelda Medley}}" appears as a Song in the Japan-only ''{{Wp|Daigasso! Band Brothers}}''.

==={{SSB4|-}}===
"{{Term|SSBfWU|The Legend of Zelda Medley}}" is a remix comprised of six songs, with five from the original {{TLoZ}} and one from {{ALttP}}.

"{{Term|SSBfWU|The Legend of Zelda Medley}}" plays on the [[Hyrule Castle]] and [[Pirate Ship]] Stages.
