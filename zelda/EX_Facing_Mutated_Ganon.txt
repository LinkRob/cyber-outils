{{Term/Store|EX Facing Mutated Ganon||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 93 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Facing Mutated Ganon|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the second wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian of Remembrance|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Facing Mutated Ganon}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given 16 minutes to defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Mutated Ganon}}. Although, to gain access to him, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must first defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Igneo Talus|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Frost Talus|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Moblin|link}}. They are scattered around the map along with numerous {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}}. When four of the seven key {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Hollow|link}} of each of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Champions|link}} besides {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}} appears as well, and also must be killed. After all 11 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, gates open to an area in the bottom right corner of the map, in which {{Term|HWAoC|Mutated Ganon}} is located. When {{Term|HWAoC|Mutated Ganon}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. 

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any four {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of five {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Ironshell Crab|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Stamella Shroom|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
