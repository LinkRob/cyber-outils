==Characteristics==
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
Ice Lynels are a race of [[Lynel]] from Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity that dual wield [[Savage Lynel Sword]]s. They have [[Ice]] swirling around their bodies, and most of their attacks involve ice. This means instead of shooting [[Shock Arrow|lightning arrows]], they shoot [[Ice Arrow|ice arrows]] that explode when they hit the ground. 

Instead of breathing fire like the normal Lynel, they breath ice, which freezes the opponent in place. These Lynels will also freeze the character if the character gets hit by the Lynel's swords. 

It's Hover Spin+Sword Slam attack summons ice flooring that hurts and freezes the player if they step on it and is long lasting. It also can create a ice explosion rather than a fire one.

These Lynels have more HP than a [[Lynel|red maned Lynel]].
