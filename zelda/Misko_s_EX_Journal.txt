{{Term/Store|''Misko's EX Journal''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Misko's EX Journal|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} that was written by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}} and can only be found after {{TMT}} DLC Pack has been purchased and downloaded. It can be found on a broken shelf in the southern-most structure of {{Term|BotW|Outpost Ruins|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Book}} is important for several {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} that become available when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} leaves the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. It is needed to progress through {{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Ancient Mask|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. One of the items stolen was an ancient mask.<br/><br/>There's a journal that places the location of this treasure somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Outpost Ruins}}...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. Among the items stolen was a fairy's green garments.<br/><br/>There's a journal that places the locations of the treasure somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Outpost Ruins}}...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Twilight Relic|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. One of the items stolen was a helmet of twilight...<br/><br/>There's a journal that places the location of this treasure somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Outpost Ruins}}...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Phantasma|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. Among the items stolen was some phantasmal armor.<br/><br/>There's a journal that places the locations of the treasure somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Outpost Ruins}}...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> At first, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will question the newly-found book, asking himself if he has seen it here before.<ref>{{Cite|Was this book always here?|Link|BotW}}</ref> When read, it tells of {{Term|BotW|Misko}} stealing the eight treasures from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} and scattering them around the ruins of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}, the locations of which are also written:<ref>{{Cite|I hid the eight priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref>
