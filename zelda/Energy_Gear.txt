The {{Term/Store|Energy Gear||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Energy Gear increases your energy gauge by 50%. It's the caffeine-killer!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Energy Gear}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}. It increases the wearer's {{Term|TFH|Energy Gauge|link}} by 50%. This effect does not "stack" with the effects of the {{Term|TFH|Cheer Outfit|link}}; therefore if any of the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} wear the {{Term|TFH|Cheer Outfit}} to increase their allies' {{Plural|TFH|Energy Gauge}}, the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gear}} has no additional effect.

In a single-player game, the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gear}} functions the same as the {{Term|TFH|Cheer Outfit}}, increasing the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gauge}} that is shared between {{Term|TFH|Link}} and his two {{Plural|TFH|Doppel|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|TFH|Energy Gear}}'s color scheme and the spiky hairstyle it gives {{Term|TFH|Link}} may be inspired by {{Wp|Goku}} or {{Wp|Naruto Uzumaki|Naruto}}, protagonists of the popular Japanese manga series {{Wp|Dragon Ball}} and {{Wp|Naruto}}.
