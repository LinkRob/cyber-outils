==Location and Uses==

The Life Medal is an item that can be both bought from [[Beedle's Airshop|Beetle's Airshop]] and found in one of the many [[Goddess's Treasure Chest|goddess's chests]] found in the sky. While [[Link]] has one within his [[Pouch|Adventure Pouch]], he'll obtain another [[Heart Container|heart container]]. Like other pouch items, this has no effect in the [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Round]].
