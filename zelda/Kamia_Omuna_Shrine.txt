The {{Term/Store|Kamia Omuna Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kamia Omuna Shrine}}<br/>Moving Targets}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Moving Targets''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna Shrine}} is found on the northern shore of {{Term|BotW|Lake Darman|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. It is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} accessed as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan|link}} that lies in the {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Lake Darman}}.

After defeating it, the {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna Shrine}} will rise from beneath the ground nearby. Upon entering the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is offered the trial by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kamia Omuna. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kamia Omuna|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna Shrine}} features a straightforward walkway that leads to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}, which is barricaded by a pair of gates. Along the walkway are glowing targets on the walls, each of which is accompanied by a {{Term|BotW|Cannon|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Cannon}} are operated by a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}}, which causes them to move when struck. They are fired by placing a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} inside and detonating it, releasing an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} into the air.

The first {{Term|BotW|Cannon}} features an immobile target on the wall. Upon striking this target with an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, the wall lowers and reveals a second target. This target moves up and down, requiring more precise timing to strike. These targets can also be activated by shooting them with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}. Striking this target raises the first gate, leading to the next area and {{Term|BotW|Cannon}}.

The next {{Term|BotW|Cannon}} features a suspended structure with the next target mounted on it. This structure's angle can be manipulated by using the nearby {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle|link}}. The structure is to be tilted in such a way that the target is in clear range, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to strike it with the {{Term|BotW|Cannon}} without issue. In doing so, the wall behind the structure opens, revealing another target. As this target lies behind the structure, it must be tilted again so that the second target is within range. Striking this target opens the final gate, leading to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} commends his resourcefulness and rewards him with {{Term|BotW|Rudania's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of the hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Rudania's Emblem.}}|Kamia Omuna|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna}} then tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to collect all three of {{Plural|BotW|Rudania's Emblem}} to reveal a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Kamia Omuna|BotW}}</ref> If this is the first or second {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} visits during the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna}} tells him how many {{Plural|BotW|Rudania's Emblem|display=Emblems}} remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Kamia Omuna|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Kamia Omuna|BotW}}</ref> If it is the third and final {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} visits, {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna}} instructs him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Rudania...}}|Kamia Omuna|BotW}}</ref>

Beside the first {{Term|BotW|Cannon}} is a narrow walkway leading to a cage, with a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} sitting on a wooden board high above it. The board can be broken by firing the {{Term|BotW|Cannon}} at it. It can also be knocked down by shooting {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} at it. The fallen {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}, can then be pulled out of the cage using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to the main part of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} by climbing the {{Plural|BotW|Ladder|link}} near the first {{Term|BotW|Cannon}}.
