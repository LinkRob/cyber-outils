The {{Term/Store|Guards' Chamber||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Guards' Chamber'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}} is a multi-leveled room on the western side of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. It is situated beneath the {{Term|BotW|First Gatehouse|link}}. The top floor of the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}} is split into two sections, with the southeastern portion serving as entry and exit points and the northwestern segment being used for storage. The southeastern side features a {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}} near the entrance, which can be held down to release a wooden portcullis leading to a small courtyard on the southwestern side of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|display=Castle}}. The northwestern side of the top floor of the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}} is inaccessible by foot as the stairway from the middle floor has broken, though {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can climb the walls to reach it.

The middle floor of the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}} is the most spacious, featuring the remains of several wooden dummies and archery targets, as well as a small cluster of {{plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel|link}} scattered around the floor. Loosely-rigged shelves along the northern wall of this floor also hold multiple {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel}}, and a long, metal slab can be used to cross the gap on the northwestern stairs. A {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} sits beneath these shelves, placed evenly between two stationary {{Plural|BotW|Torch}}. The stone stairs on this level have an alcove underneath them that is covered by dead leaves.

The bottom floor of the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}} holds a small sparring square. Near the square are three knight statues, all of which hold {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}. Three {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} hide a small alcove underneath the second stone staircase.

On the highest level of the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find two wooden {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} and five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. In the alcove under the stairs on the middle floor, there is another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} with a {{Term|BotW|Shard of Naydra's Fang|link}}. A final {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found in the alcove on the lowest floor, and it contains a {{Term|BotW|Savage Lynel Shield|link}}.

During the "{{Term|BotW|EX Royal Guard Rumors|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, which is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must read {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V4|link}}, which mentions that part of the equipment used by the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard|link}} is hidden in the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber}}.<ref>{{Cite|Amongst {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule's soldiers}}, only the best of the best had the honor of guarding the royal family. Apparently, the equipment they wore can still be found somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. They say a former maid spoke of treasure near the {{Color|BotW Red|Dining Hall}}, in the {{Color|BotW Red|Guards' Chamber}}, and on {{Color|BotW Red|the second floor}}.|Super Rumor Mill V4|BotW}}</ref> An EX {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found on the wooden platform above the {{Term|BotW|Campfire}}. Inside this {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will uncover the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots|link}}.
