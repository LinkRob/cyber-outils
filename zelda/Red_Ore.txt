{{Term/Store|Red Ore||Series, OoS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 135}}</ref> Along with {{Term|OoS|Blue Ore|link}}, it is one of the components for making {{Term|OoS|Hard Ore|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoS|Red Ore|link}} is found in a {{Term|OoS|Treasure Chest|link}} atop a cliff in southeastern {{Term|OoS|Subrosia Village|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} must use the {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Glove|link}} to cross pitfalls over to a cave entrance at base of the cliff. Inside the cave is a staircase up to the {{Term|OoS|Treasure Chest}} with the {{Term|OoS|Red Ore}}.

When {{Term|OoS|Link}} has both the {{Term|OoS|Red Ore}} and {{Term|OoS|Blue Ore|link}}, he brings them to the {{Term|OoS|Great Furnace|link}} to have them smelted into {{Term|OoS|Hard Ore|link}}.
