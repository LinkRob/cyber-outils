{{Term/Store|Swamola|Swamolas|Series, ALttP, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 208 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Swamolas appear in the [[Swamp of Evil]] in the [[Dark World]]. They swim to the surface of the mud to collect food,<ref name="ALttP Plural"/> biting [[Link]] in the process if he gets close enough.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, Swamolas appear in [[Misery Mire (A Link Between Worlds)|Misery Mire]] in [[Lorule]]. They behave the same as in {{ALttP|-}}.

==Nomenclature==
Swanmola may be a corruption of "Swamp" and "Lanmola," given that they are counterparts to the {{Term|Series|Lanmola}} exclusive to swamp and marshy areas.

The Japanese name for the Swamola is {{Romanize|モルドアーム|Morudoāmu|Moldarm|2}}, the name used for Moldorms in {{TLoZ}}, while the Japanese name for [[Moldorm]] in other games is {{Romanize|テール|Tēru|Tail|2}}. Swamolas behave much like the Moldorms from {{TP}}, which are known as {{Romanize|モルドワーム|Morudowāmu|Moldwarm|2}} rather than {{Romanize|テール|Tēru|Tail|2}} in Japan.
