The {{Term/Store|Sage Temple Ruins||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Sage Temple Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Sage Temple Ruins are the half-sunken remains of the {{Term|Series|Sage Temple|link}} located along the [[Regencia River]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. Scattered around the Ruins are a number of [[Barrel]]s, in which [[Link]] can find [[Hylian Shroom]]s and [[Apple]]s. Two broken wagons can be found in the Ruins, which can be searched for [[Material]]s. Floating right in the northwestern corner of the area is a [[Raft]].

A metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing an [[Opal]] can be found in the southernmost building in the Ruins. A stone Treasure Chest containing a [[Knight's Halberd]] can be found in the building at the center of the Ruins. In the "[[EX Treasure: Twilight Relic]]" [[Side Quest]] from [[The Master Trials]] DLC Pack, another metallic Treasure Chest containing [[Midna's Helmet]] can be found hidden among the rubble just south of the northwesternmost building of the Ruins.

A [[Korok]] can be found underneath a [[Rock]] atop a broken wall in the center of the Ruins.
