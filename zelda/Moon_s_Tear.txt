{{Term/Store|Moon's Tear|Moon's Tears|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are {{Plural|MM3D|Item|link}} in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|MM3D|Moon's Tear}} fall from the surface of the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}} to {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}, so named by {{Term|MM3D|Professor Shikashi|link}} because they fall from what appears to be the {{Term|MM3D|Moon}}'s eye.<ref>{{Cite|That is one of the lunar rocks that has been blazing from the surface of the moon lately. They fall from what looks to be the moon's eye, so I call them {{Color|MM3D Red|Moon's Tears}}. They are rare stones, valued by many in town.|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref> They're rumored to be the shiniest stones in the world and are consequently highly valuable.<ref>{{Cite|I keep hearing about a stone called a {{Color|MM3D Red|Moon's Tear}}, and it's supposed to be the shiniest stone in the world!|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref>

{{Term|MM3D|Professor Shikashi}} keeps one of these rare stones in a display case, which the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}} threatened to steal.<ref>{{Cite|That ill-mannered troublemaker from the other day said he'd break my instruments... He said he'd steal my {{Color|MM3D Red|Moon's Tear}}... There was no stopping him.|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} can acquire a single {{Term|MM3D|Moon's Tear}} for himself by peering through the telescope in the {{Term|MM3D|Astral Observatory|link}}. When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} takes a closer look at the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}},<ref>{{Cite|You can zoom in with {{Icon|OoT3D A}}. Try looking near the {{Color|MM3D Red|clock tower}}.|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref> who is fooling around on the top of the {{Term|MM3D|Clock Tower|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Even now! Just watch him! He's probably causing trouble around the {{Color|MM3D Red|clock tower}}. Will you gaze into the telescope?|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref> a {{Term|MM3D|Moon's Tear}} will fall from the {{Term|MM3D|Moon}} and impact just outside the observatory with a bang. Afterwards, the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}} will taunt {{Term|MM3D|Link}} and disappear. The young hero can then find it embedded in a small crater where it landed, just within the fenced area outside the {{Term|MM3D|Astral Observatory}} door.<ref>{{Cite|Well, did you find that {{Color|MM3D Red|troublemaker}}? And that loud noise... What was that? Perhaps another {{Color|MM3D Red|Moon's Tear}} has fallen nearby... Go through that door and take a look outside.|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref>

The {{Term|MM3D|Moon's Tear}} is essential in starting the trading process that involves all the {{Plural|MM3D|Business Scrub|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}. After obtaining the {{Term|MM3D|Moon's Tear}}, it can be given to the {{Term|MM3D|Business Scrub}} in {{Term|MM3D|South Clock Town|link}}, who is looking for a gift for his wife,<ref>{{Cite|I'm thinking of closing up shop so I can buy a gift for my wife and return to her in my village! I've heard that a stone called the Moon's Tear shines brighter than any other in the land! If you've got one, I'd really like to get it from you. My wife would love it!|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref> for the {{Term|MM3D|Town Title Deed|link}}. This will allow him to use the yellow {{Term|MM3D|Deku Flower|link}} and access the small platform in front of the {{Term|MM3D|Clock Tower}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! That stone! You must hand it over to me! In exchange, I'll give you my spot here...{{Color|MM3D Red|Deku Flower}} included! Yes? You really helped me out! Now I have the perfect souvenir for my wife!|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref>

== Trivia ==
The Moon's Tear is the only item in ''Majora's Mask'' that can be given by simply pressing the C button the item is assigned to when standing by a character to activate its respective event — in that case, giving it to [[Clock Town]]'s [[Business Scrub#Majora's Mask|Business Scrub]]. It is unknown whether this was left over from {{OoT|-}}'s way of doing this, or if a bug present in other such situations prevented this from being possible.
