{{Term/Store|Tolemac||Series, ZA}} (pronounced {{IPA|ˈtoʊləmæk}} {{Respell|TOH|lə-mak}})<ref>{{Cite|And so it was that Gannon, Lord of Darkness, had taken over Tolemac.|Gaspra|ZA}}</ref> is the land that serves as the setting of {{ZA}}.<ref name="Manual 2">{{Cite manual|quote= Zelda must set out on her journey deep into the uncharted southeastern region of Hyrule, known as Tolemac.|game= ZA|page= 2}}</ref> It is located in southeastern [[Hyrule]].<ref name="Manual 2"/>

==History==
Sometime during Hyrule's history, the prince of darkness [[Ganon]] had taken siege of Tolemac, plummeting it and Hyrule into an Age of Darkness.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=It is the Age of Darkness. The evil Gannon has captured the young hero Link.|game=ZA|page=2}}</ref> [[Link]], who was adventuring in the land, was soon captured by Ganon.<ref>{{Cite|It's good of you to come see me. I was the one who was guiding Link across Tolemac, when we were spotted by one of Ganon's spies. I did what I could to stand with Link, but there were too many of them and he was taken.|Link's Guide|ZA}}</ref> The seven [[Celestial Sign]]s were also stolen and hidden in Tolemac's Shrines,<ref>{{Cite|Ah, the scroll of Shurmak, bearer of sad news these many years ago. And so it was that Gannon, Lord of Darkness, had taken over Tolemac. He has stolen the treasured celestial signs and captured Link! A brave warrior will have to be found to face this evil monster.|Gaspra|ZA}}</ref> guarded by Ganon's followers, the Shrine Keepers.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=All of the shrines are guard by monsters and shrine keepers.|game=ZA|page=8}}</ref> The guide [[Shurmak]] had sent [[Gaspra]] the astronomer a scroll, warning him of the events that have taken place. Gaspra searches for a brave warrior who can save Tolemac from its plight, and locates [[Princess Zelda]] and summons her as his chosen champion.<ref>{{Cite|And so I found this champion of strength and courage, it is you, Princess Zelda. With this magic pendant go forth, and with each Sign you gather so too will you gain knowledge. Find your magic Wand, use it wisely and listen to your allies along the way. Go now my princess, restore the Celestial Signs and rescue Link!|Gaspra|ZA}}</ref>

Zelda travels across the land with the guidance of Shurmak and Gaspra, as she locates the Shrines and defeats their Shrine Keepers, while meeting many helpful characters who aid her in her quest and tricksters who try to delude her. Eventually she locates the final Celestial Sign in the [[Shrine of Fire]], but a shadowy hand belonging to Ganon prevents her approach and sends her falling down the newly opened [[Vision Henge]].<ref>{{Cite|Zelda, life is always one order more horrifying than you expect. The great evil Ganon spoiled your victory. Make haste! Do not let him out of your sight.|Gaspra|ZA}}</ref> There Zelda combats the Shrine Keepers again in a series of rematches, before finally facing Ganon himself. She manages to defeat him, and he disappears in a tornado as his realm shatters. Link is rescued, and peace is restored as the lands of Hyrule and Tolemac enter an "Age of Lightness".<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Once she succeeds, she gains the knowledge and strength to rescue Link and brings the magical land of Hyrule into the Age of Lightness.|game=ZA|page=2}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Princess Zelda, you've succeeded. Your victory has brought peace and light back to Tolemac.|Gaspra|ZA}}</ref>

==Goverance==
As Tolemac is a region located in southeastern Hyrule, it is likely that it shares the same government as Hyrule; a monarchy ruled by the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family]]. A number of citizens of Tolemac immediately recognize Zelda and refer to her as "their Princess" and "their lady",<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the Mobilin's Head Inn, my Princess!|Lothar|ZA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Welcome to our fair village, my Princess!|Bard|ZA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Heh heh. You look tired, my lady.|Woman in Ubato Hills' village|ZA}}</ref> further suggesting that Tolemac is under Royal Family rule.

==Economy==
{{Main|Rupee}}
As with the rest of Hyrule, Tolemac's official currency is the jewel-like [[Rupee]]. Unlike Hyrule however, only two colors are used in Tolemac — blue and yellow, which are worth five and ten, respectively. [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Diamond|Diamond]]s are also regarded as highly valuable, and a mined Diamond can be worth up to 999 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|What did I ever do to any of you!? I give an honest bargain and I grind the coal into Rubies.|Blacksmith|ZA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am told there is wealth in this large lump of coal, but I know not how to claim it.|Quarry Miner|ZA}}</ref> These can be obtained from [[Coal]] mined in eastern Tolemac near the [[Plain of Hobel]].

Rupees are accepted in most shops around Tolemac, but are most notably used to power and cast many of the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure|Spell]]s found in the land, as Rupees are valued for containing magical energy.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=It is common knowledge in Tolemac that rupees contain magical energy, hence their value as currency.|game=ZA|page=5}}</ref>

==Geography==
The land of Tolemac is described as being part of southeastern Hyrule.<ref name="Manual 2"/> It is a diverse territory with several self-contained regions, including plains, forests, fields, mountains, a swamp, a desert, and a vast ocean coast. In the center of Tolemac is the [[Meadow of Skotness]], which branches off to the country's other surrounding regions. To the south of the meadow is the barren [[Plain of Andor]], where Zelda begins her journey at the plain's Vision Henge. A river and a small forest path, the [[Forest of Findo]], borders between the two lands. The [[Forest of Ogbam]] can be found just south of the Plain of Andor, where Zelda finds the first dungeon, the [[Shrine of Earth]]. North of the Meadow of Skotness is the larger [[Forest of Torian]], which leads to the volcanic region of the [[Forest of Canvula]] to the west, and the dry [[Ubato Hills]] to the east. A mountain road east of the Meadow of Skotness, called [[Great Dry Canyon]], leads to the [[Seacoast Plain]] to the south. The swamp of [[Vendoss]], also known as the Barachno Marsh, lies northeast of the coast, which can also be entered from the meadow side. East of Vendoss is the [[East Forest]] and Plain of Hobel, while north of the swamp lies the vast [[Gubasha Desert]]. The desert's northern region consists of a cold coastal cliff side by the sea. Although not part of the mainland, a small, deserted island known as [[Sea Island]] can be found in the ocean southeast, and can only be accessed by taking a [[Raft]] from [[Seacoast Plain|Port Town]].

Tolemac is home to several settlements scattered across the land. The largest town, [[Great Wimbich]], is located close to the Meadow of Skotness. Ubato Hills is home to a tiny village. Port Town, true to its name, is a harbor town in southern Seacoast Plain home to mostly seafarers. The Vendoss swamp is the location of a hidden village, built as a secret refuge from Ganon's minions.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda, we pray you walk softly here and bring no enemies on us. We have built our village on the Barachno Marsh as a refuge to avoid Ganon's fellows.|Hooded Woman|ZA}}</ref> The South Gubasha Desert is also home to a small desert settlement. The remains of a village can also be found in the Forest of Canvula, but has since been destroyed and is now deserted save for a pair of [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Goblins|Goblins]] remaining.

Aside from these towns, a number of houses and buildings can also be found around Tolemac, including a few lodges such as the [[Mobilin's Head Inn]] and the [[Forest of Torian#White Steed Lodge|White Steed Lodge]]. The [[Shrine of Strength]] is also a fortress where people come to watch competitions of strength and power.

==Population==
Tolemac is populated predominately by [[human]]s, although other creatures are known to inhabit the land as well. The Forest of Findo is home to a talking [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Talking Mushroom|Mushroom]], and a pair of Goblins live in the Forest of Canvula. [[Fairy|Fairies]] also live in several [[Fairy Fountain|pond]]s located throughout the land, and will heal Zelda's wounds when approached.

==Nomenclature==
Tolemac is {{Wp|Camelot}} spelled backwards.
