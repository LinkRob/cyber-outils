The {{Term/Store|Mercay Island Shop||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page=45}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Situated on the east side of [[Mercay Island]], it offers a variety of common items for sale, and it is also the first shop where [[Link]] can obtain a [[Wooden Shield]] at the beginning of the game. After the young hero has cleared the [[Temple of Courage]], Mercay Island Shop will put up a [[quiver]] upgrade for a price of 1000 Rupees. The upgrade will depend whether the young hero has already obtained a quiver from [[Romanos]]'s minigame.

Like all the other shops, Mercay Island Shop also offers a random [[Treasure]]. Only one treasure is available at any time, and the one it offers is random. After Link buys one treasure from the shop, it will take a day for the shop to restock with a new, random treasure.
