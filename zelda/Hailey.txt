{{Term/Store|Hailey||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Hailey}} is a member of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}}. She appears alongside her sister {{Term|TMC|Gale|link}} during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}. Later, they try to return to their {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link|display=Home}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}, though they find themselves trapped.<ref>{{Cite|You see, I went down to the surface with my sister for the {{Color|TMC Blue|Picori Festival}}... But on our way home, we lost the wind entirely! Now, we can't get home!|Gale|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} locates five gold {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} scattered across the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}} and {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} them with five corresponding {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Cloud|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Tornado|link}} appears, allowing {{Term|TMC|Hailey}} and {{Term|TMC|Gale}} to return to the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}}. From this point on, {{Term|TMC|Hailey}} can be found on the uppermost floor of the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}}, where she blocks the staircase to the roof until {{Term|TMC|Link}} receives permission to access it from {{Term|TMC|Siroc|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|Hailey}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let's do it!|Hailey|TMC}}</ref> resulting in a {{Term|TMC|Golden Tektite|link}} appearing at {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}.
