{{Term/Store|Heehl||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heehl|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Heehl}} is one of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers|link}}, a group consisting of himself and his brothers {{Term|BotW|Bayge|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Kabetta|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} and his brothers are passionate about training in difficult feats of strength and endurance. They live in a house in southern {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, which is now abandoned and left with a {{Term|BotW|Sign|link}} saying they have gone to {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock|link}} to train.<ref>{{Cite|We are currently training at Gut Check Rock north of Death Mountain. Come watch us train at Gut Check Rock!<br/>- Bayge, Heehl, and Kabetta|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Heehl}} is involved in the "{{Term|BotW|Test of Will|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} and the other {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} have traveled to their ancestral training ground to train themselves to withstand the heat in {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is our ancestral training ground! Us three Goron Blood Brothers are in the midst of a {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's too hot to handle in Goron City...so we came here to train ourselves...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> They challenge {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to join them by sitting in a ring of fire to see who can withstand the longest.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, I have an idea!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Come join us!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah! Come try the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}} yourself...brother!|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts their challenge, {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} remarks that he has a fiery passion.<ref>{{Cite|A guy like you has a burning passion in his belly!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} is the second {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} to collapse, at which point he appears to be hallucinating.<ref name="Heehl"/>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} is declared the winner, {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} allows him to attempt a harder contest, which involves standing on an even hotter platform.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, I'm grantin' you the highest honor—the chance to participate in the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of ULTIMATE endurance}}!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That special training area is even harder than the last ring...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he thinks it is a bad idea,<ref>{{Cite|You see the ring behind me? The one surrounded in fire? Do ya feel it in your gut—what a bad idea it is to go there?|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} says that he is right.<ref>{{Cite|Your gut is dead on, brother!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes this challenge, {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} will be impressed by his display.<ref>{{Cite|What a powerful display! What fire! That was somethin' else, brother!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Joloo Nah Shrine|link}} will then emerge from the ground, and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.

{{Term|BotW|Heehl}} also appears at the base of {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}} in northern {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}, standing next to one of the {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}} near the main gateway. He recognizes that a need to build up his strength must have brought him there and welcome him.<ref>{{Cite|Some undying need for gains must have guided you here. I'm sure of it. Welcome to our extreme Goron training ground!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to talk to {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} to attempt their training.<ref>{{Cite|You should talk to my {{Color|BotW Blue|big bro}} up at the top so you can achieve full-on Goron manliness, brother!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> After completing {{Term|BotW|Bayge}}'s {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge|link}}, he commends {{Term|BotW|Link}} on his success.<ref name="Bro"/>

During the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, which is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} can be found at {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond|link}}. They have decided to train at this location like {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} did, since they believe it will allow them to be more like him.<ref>{{Cite|Do ya know why {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} chose this place to train, brother? ...Neither do we! But us Goron Blood Brothers are gonna find out by completing the training ourselves! I'm sure this is some part of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}}'s secret Goron champ training. Intense! We're gonna do all we can to be might Gorons just like {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}}! It's the only possible path for the Goron Blood Brothers!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} explains that the goal of this training is to stand on the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} atop the {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond|display=Pond's}} {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|FEEL THE BURN, BROTHER!}} You see that {{Color|BotW Red|glowing ring}} in the lava? Think of it as a target, and... {{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|stand on the lava, brother}}!}}|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> Before attempting it, however, the brothers decide to strategize.<ref>{{Cite|Time to strategize, brothers!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

When spoken to afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} says that this training is intense.<ref>{{Cite|There's a {{Color|BotW Red|glowing ring}} in that boiling hot lava. Standing there is...intense training. Only for the hardest of core!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} manages to pass through the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Heehl}} wonders if someone managed to complete the training.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|glowing ring}} is gone. Does that mean... Has someone completed the training?!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> However, he thinks this is impossible, as only {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} or one of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} could have done such a thing.<ref>{{Cite|N-no... Not possible. The only one who could complete training like that is {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}}... or one of us brothers!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* {{Term|Series|Heehl}} is one of several characters that will hit {{Term|Series|Link}} back if he attacks them with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}. Uniquely, he only does this on {{Term|Series|Mount Nabooru}}, as he will react with fear if {{Term|Series|Link}} attacks him on {{Term|Series|Gut Check Rock}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hold it!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Aaaah!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> These attacks deal no damage.
