The {{Term/Store|Seven Heroines|Seven Heroines|Series, BotW|plural}} are a group of characters in {{BotW}}<ref>"''Seven Heroines''" — N/A (''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'')</ref> and {{TotK}}<ref>''"Seven Heroines"'' — N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']])</ref>.

==Biography==
===''Breath of the Wild''===
The {{Term|BotW|Seven Heroines}} are known as the divine protectors of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} people.<ref>{{Cite|It's dedicated to the {{Color|BotW Blue|seven giant statues of the heroines}}, said to be the divine protectors of the Gerudo.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> Massive statues that stand in their honour can be found in the {{Term|BotW|East Gerudo Ruins|link}} on the east end of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. People once journeyed from around the world searching for the heroines' blessing.<ref>{{Cite|It's believed that people once came from around the world in search of the heroines' blessing.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> Each heroine is said to have a unique power, which are skill, spirit, endurance, knowledge, flight, motion and gentleness.<ref>{{Cite|Each heroine had a different power: skill, spirit, endurance, knowledge, flight, motion, and gentleness.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> The statues of the {{Term|BotW|Seven Heroines}} play a role in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Seven Heroines|link}}", given by {{Term|BotW|Rotana|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must solve a puzzle involving the statues to finish the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} and reveal the {{Term|BotW|Korsh O'hu Shrine|link}}. In addition to the {{Term|BotW|Seven Heroines}}, there is also a rumour of an {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone's heard the legend in the Gerudo region about the seven heroines, but...some tell of an {{Color|BotW Red|eighth heroine}}...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Statue of the Eighth Heroine|display=statue of the Eighth Heroine|link}} can be found in the northern {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}.

=== Tears of the Kingdom ===
{{Incomplete|type= section|reason= There is more in-game information on this subject than just this much.|game= TotK}}
In Tears of the Kingdom, there is a [[Side Quest]] named [[The Heroines' Secret]] in which a total 6 ancient Gerudo [[stelae]] can be photographed and translated by [[Rotana]]. The stelae located in the [[Gerudo Hideout|Gerudo Shelter]] refer to them as protectors of the Gerudo.<ref>"''The seven heroines who protect the Gerudo.''" — Left Gerudo Hideout stela ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']])</ref> Their power is housed within the 'seven spirits'<ref>"''Their power is housed within the seven spirits.''" — Gerudo prison Stela ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']])</ref>, who rest within the land of the Gerudo.<ref>"''The seven heroines who protect the Gerudo. Their spirits rest within the land of the Gerudo.''" — Gerudo classroom stela ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']])</ref> The 'seven spirits' refers to orbs bearing crests of the seven heroines.<ref>"''What if "spirits" refers to orbs like this one? Sa'oten! I believe I've made a breakthrough!''" — Rotana ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']])</ref> The Eighth Heroine is shrouded<ref>"''When their powers return to them, the eighth will be known.''"" — broken Gerudo stela ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']])</ref> and channels and guides the powers of the seven heroines<ref>"''An eighth channels and guides the powers of these seven.''" — Right Gerudo Hideout stela ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']])</ref>. The seven heroines possess the powers of heart, skill, fortitude, wisdom, flight, mobility and compassion.<ref>"''The eighth of them connects the other seven powers—heart, skill, fortitude, wisdom, flight, mobility and compassion.''" — Rotana ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']])</ref>
