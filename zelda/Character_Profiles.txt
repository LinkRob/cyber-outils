'''Character Profiles''' are a [[Purah Pad]] function in {{TotK}}.

==Location and Uses==
When the [[Steward Construct]] first gives the [[Purah Pad]] to [[Link]], it has locked functions. Once Link has talked to [[Purah]], she will restore the Character Profiles function, and give him the first 4 Profiles. As Link continues in his adventure, he will unlock more Character Profiles, ultimately reaching 22.

After Link talks to Purah, she will unlock Character Profiles and unlock the profiles of [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], Purah, [[Robbie]], and [[Rauru]]. Link can speak to [[Josha]] after starting the [[Regional Phenomena]] quest to unlock her profile. When Link speaks to [[Tulin]], his profile and [[Teba]]'s profile will both be unlocked. After Link speaks to the Goron Elder, [[Yunobo]]'s profile will be unlocked.

[[Sidon]]'s profile will be unlocked once Link talks to him. [[King Dorephan|Dorephan]]'s profile will be added to the list as soon as Link locates him in the Pristine Sanctum. When Link completes [[The Sludge-Covered Statue]], [[Yona]]'s profile will be unlocked. Once Link finds [[Riju]] at the North Gerudo Ruins, her profile will be added to the list. [[Buliara]]'s profile is unlocked once Link helps defend [[Kara Kara Bazaar]] or if he finds the [[Gerudo Shelter]].

When Link talks to [[Paya]], both her profile and [[Tauro]]'s profile are unlocked. [[Impa]]'s profile is also unlocked by Link as soon as he speaks to her. Once Link speaks to [[Hestu]], their profile is added to the list. Link can also unlock Hestu's profile by defeating the [[Phantom Ganon]] in the Deku Tree, which also unlocks the [[The Great Deku Tree|Deku Tree]]'s profile. When either [[Hudson]] or [[Rhondson]] are spoken to by Link, the Rhondson and Hudson profile is added to the list.

When Link defeats the [[Seized Construct]], he will unlock [[Mineru]]'s profile. [[Ganondorf|Demon King Ganondorf]]'s profile is unlocked after Link defeats Ganondorf.

Some Character Profiles will change after Link progresses farther in his quest. These include Rauru, Tulin, Teba, Yunobo, Sidon, Yona, Dorephan, and Riju.
