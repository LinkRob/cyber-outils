{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Claymore}} are two-handed weapons that were wielded by the most confident {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, but they may also be carried by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} and on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}.

When the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, they have a chance to drop a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Claymore}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Young Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Ganondorf|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|OoT|Link|link}} ({{OoT}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Since they are made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Knight's Claymore}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, which is useful due to the fact that they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Knight's Claymore}} will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Claymore}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>
