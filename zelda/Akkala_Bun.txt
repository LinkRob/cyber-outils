==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Akkala Bun}} are a type of bun that can be purchased at the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}} for 10 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Khini|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you soooo much! That'll be {{Color|BotW Red|10 rupees}}, please!|Khini|BotW}}</ref> Unlike other {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Akkala Bun}} can not be stored in {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} as the fresh nature of their ingredients means they go off quickly and must be consumed immediately in order to be kept fresh.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you soooo much! Oh, one thing to note...  {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala buns}} go bad fast since they’re made of fresh ingredients. So you'll have to eat it here, that way we can ensure their quality. That OK?|Khini|BotW}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
The recipe for Akkala Buns is unlocked after completing the "Wedding Worry" quest. Akkala Buns require three {{Plural|HWAoC|Tireless Frog|link}} to make and increase the Perfect-Dodge timing window by 10%.

The icon humorously appears to be that of a pixelated {{Term|HWAoC|Tireless Frog}}, heavily implying that the Akkala Bun is in fact nothing more than a cooked {{Term|HWAoC|Tireless Frog}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
