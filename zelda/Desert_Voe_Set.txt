The {{Term/Store|Desert Voe Set||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring [[Armor Set]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 183}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Improve|January 2021}}
The Desert Voe Set is composed of the [[Desert Voe Headband]], the [[Desert Voe Spaulder]] and the [[Desert Voe Trousers]]. The full Desert Voe Set can be purchased for a total of 2,400 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} from either the [[Gerudo Secret Club]] in [[Gerudo Town]] or from [[Rhondson Armor Boutique]] in [[Tarrey Town]].

Each piece in the Desert Voe Set offers a low-level [[Heat Resistance]] which can be combined provide a maximum mid-level Heat Resistance. The Desert Voe Set yields a {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link|display=Shock Damage Resist|section=Shock Damage Resist}} [[Set Bonus]] when each piece is upgraded twice by a [[Great Fairy#Breath of the Wild|Great Fairy]].

The Desert Voe Set can also be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] in [[Hateno Village]].
