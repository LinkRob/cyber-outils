==Characteristics==
Pirate Miniblins have fins instead of ears and large, bright green goggles. They are pirates who sail around the [[World of the Ocean King]] in [[Pirate Ship (Enemy)|Pirate Ships]]. If their Ships are not destroyed before they ambush the [[SS Linebeck]], they will board it and attack in groups.
