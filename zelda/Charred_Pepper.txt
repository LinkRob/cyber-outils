==Location and Uses==
Charred Peppers are the result of {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper}} that have made contact with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. The roasting process is said to remove some of the spiciness of the pepper.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can restore three quarters of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} by eating a Charred Pepper.
