The {{Term/Store|Part-Timer||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 245}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|MM3D|Part-Timer}} can be seen working at the {{Term|MM3D|Trading Post|link}} after 10 p.m. He can also be found during the day in {{Term|MM3D|Termina Field|link}} trying to catch three red {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee|link}} on a {{Term|MM3D|Tree|link}} near the {{Term|MM3D|Astral Observatory|link}}. If {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} rolls into the {{Term|MM3D|Tree}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Part-Timer}} will fall and hit the ground, dropping the three red {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}} and claiming that he stole them from a crow.<ref>{{Cite|Heyyy! I stole that Rupee from a crow! Don't go stealing it, man!|Part-Timer|MM}}</ref>

When the {{Term|MM3D|Man from Trading Post|link}} leaves, he leaves the {{Term|MM3D|Trading Post}} in the care of the {{Term|MM3D|Part-Timer}}. He has no interest in his job, committing little effort into memorizing the stock or behaving cordially.<ref>{{Cite|Man, I just work here part-time, so I don't really know this stuff.|Part-Timer|MM}}</ref> However, he is susceptible to {{Plural|MM3D|Mask|link}}. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} wears the {{Term|MM3D|Zora Mask|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Part-Timer}} mistakes {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link|link}} for {{Term|MM3D|Mikau|link}} and states that he too is in a band.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! I'm in a band, too. Yeah...a band...|Part-Timer|MM}}</ref> He also claims that he wants to attend {{Term|MM3D|The Indigo-Go's|link}} concert during the {{Term|MM3D|Carnival of Time|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm definitely going to your show, man. Really. Seriousss...|Part-Timer|MM}}</ref> When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} wears the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Mask|link}}, he ignores {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome... Huh? Hey, you're just a Deku Scrub kid...|Part-Timer|MM}}</ref> When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} wears the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Mask|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Part-Timer}} wonders why the {{Term|MM3D|Trading Post}} has so much money if business is so slow.<ref>{{Cite|We don't get many customers, so why does the boss have so much money?|Part-Timer|MM}}</ref> When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} wears the {{Term|MM3D|Couple's Mask|link}}, he will show a desire to find a girlfriend and get married.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh, I wanna find a girlfriend and get married soon, too...|Part-Timer|MM}}</ref> When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} wears the {{Term|MM3D|Postman's Hat|link}}, he says how he entered a contest for some concert tickets and is wondering if he is there to deliver them.<ref>{{Cite|I entered to win some concert tickets...Don't ya have anything to deliver to me?|Part-Timer|MM}}</ref>
