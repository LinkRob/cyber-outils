{{Term/Store|Harbingers of the Calamity||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 64 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Harbingers of the Calamity|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Harbingers of the Calamity}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given fifteen minutes to defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Thunderblight Ganon|link}}. Each of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} is trapped in an area with one of those four, and none of them are able to interact with each other. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any four {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}, one {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
