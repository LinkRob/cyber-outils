==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Seared Prime Steaks by burning {{Plural|BotW|Raw Prime Meat|link}} by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Upon they death, Black {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} may drop this item. They can also be purchased from [[Estan]] in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} and [[Chef Cooke]] at [[Bedrock Bistro]]. Eating one restores two and one quarter {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to Link's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}.
