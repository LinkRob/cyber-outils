The {{Term/Store|Ridgeland Tower||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ridgeland Tower|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Ridgeland Tower}} is a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}} located in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}. It is surrounded by a small lake that is home to {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. Upon activation, it adds data for the {{Term|BotW|Ridgeland Tower Region|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.

On top of the tower is {{Term|BotW|Branli|link}}, who is stuck there wishing for a way get down easily.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... It's quite high. If only I was a {{Color|BotW Red|bird-man}}...|Branli|BotW}}</ref> He explains to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he has dedicated his life to the study of bird-men.<ref>{{Cite|You see, I have dedicated my life to the study of {{Color|BotW Red|bird-men}}! Their flight mechanics fascinate me!|Branli|BotW}}</ref> He also tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he was standing on the ground watching the sky, when the tower erupted beneath him.<ref>{{Cite|I was standing on the ground watching the skies, when suddenly this tower erupted beneath my feet!|Branli|BotW}}</ref>
