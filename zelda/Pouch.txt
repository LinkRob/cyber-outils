The {{Term/Store|Pouch|Pouches|Series, ALBW}},{{Exp Game|ALBW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 134 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Adventure Pouch|Adventure Pouches|SS, SSHD}},{{Exp Game|SS}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 115 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref> is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, the Adventure Pouch is given to [[Link]] by [[Fledge]] to aid him in his quest to find [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]].<ref>{{Cite|That green uniform looks so... adventurous! You sure look like a knight now. The headmaster said you're heading out to look for Zelda. You're really something else. I could never imagine myself doing what you're about to do. I put a lot of work into making this. You should take it with you.|Fledge|SS}}</ref> It can hold [[Medal]]s, [[Shield]]s, [[Empty Bottle]]s, [[Quiver]]s, [[Bomb Bag]]s, and [[Seed Satchel]]s. When one of these items is obtained while the Adventure Pouch is full, the obtained item will be sent to the [[Item Check]] in the [[Skyloft Bazaar]]. If Link has the [[Cursed Medal]] equipped, he cannot open his Adventure Pouch and cannot equip a Shield in exchange for [[Treasure]]s and [[Rupee]]s appearing more often.

The Adventure Pouch can hold up to four items when Link obtains it,<ref>{{Cite|It's an {{Color|SS Red|Adventure Pouch}}. It's for adventures... It's neat because you can fit {{Color|SS Red|four}} things in it that you'll need on your big journey. You can buy all kinds of stuff on the bazaar that you can put in this pouch. You should go look! You should buy some {{Color|SS Gold|potions}} and a {{Color|SS Gold|shield}}. They'd fit into your pouch, and they might come in handy on an adventure.|Fledge|SS}}</ref> although it can be upgraded to increase its capacity. Each [[Adventure Pouch Upgrade]] allows Link to carry an extra item. Three upgrades can be bought from [[Beedle's Airshop]] for 300, 600, and 1,200 Rupees, respectively. The final Adventure Pouch Upgrade can be found inside a [[Goddess's Treasure Chest]] at [[Pumpkin Landing]] that can be unlocked by striking a [[Goddess Cube]] found outside [[Skyview Temple]].

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, the Pouch can be found on a [[Tree]] stump in the [[Haunted Grove]]. Once Link finds it, the [[Blacksmith's Wife]] tells him that she made it for him and that [[Gulley]] wanted to give it to him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my stars! Gulley had that! I made it for you so you could carry more items. But he insisted on being the one who gave it to you.|Blacksmith's Wife|ALBW}}</ref> The Pouch allows Link to equip an item to the X Button as well.
