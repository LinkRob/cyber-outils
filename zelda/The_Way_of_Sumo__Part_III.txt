==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TPHD|The Way of Sumo, Part III}} is found within the basement of {{Term|TPHD|Mayor Bo|link|display=Mayor Bo's}} house. It is the third and final in a series of instructional posters on {{Term|TPHD|Sumo Wrestlin'|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|The Way of Sumo, Part III|display=''Part III''}} instructs the reader to assert their dominance by stomping the ground while in the ring.

The poster is attached to a rough plank board near the Sumo ring.
