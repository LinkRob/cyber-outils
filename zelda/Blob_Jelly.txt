{{Term/Store|Blob Jelly|Blob Jellies|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 117"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Blob Jelly}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Deku Forest|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Blob Jelly}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Deku Forest}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Blob Jelly}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Buzz Blob Cave|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Blob Jelly}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Blob Jelly}} are said to come from defeated {{Plural|TFH|Blob|link}}, though the specifics are unclear.<ref>{{Cite|Blobs drop this. It's best not to question what it is...|Material List|TFH}}</ref> They are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Kokiri Clothes|link}}.<ref name="E 117"/>
