{{Term/Store|Horse Hurdle|Horse Hurdles|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}} used to reveal {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}}.<ref name="Piggyback 330">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|page= 330|publisher= Piggyback|edition= Standard Edition|year= 2017}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Horse Hurdle}} can be found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} rides on a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} and jumps over all the {{Plural|BotW|Horse Hurdle}} without failing, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear to give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Piggyback 330"/>
