{{Term/Store|Madame Couture's||Series, TFH}} is a tailor shop in {{TFH}}, owned and run by [[Madame Couture]]. [[Link]] can order [[Outfit]]s to be tailored here by making a custom order and providing Madame Couture with [[Material]]s to craft the Outfit. These Outfits can then be worn in the [[Drablands]], which will provide Link with different abilities.

==Features==
The shop is located in the town of [[Hytopia]]. Link can order select Outfits to be tailored by making a custom order.<ref>{{Cite|You wish to create your own fashion statement, my dahling? Please make a Custom Order when you're ready.|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> Each Outfit costs a certain amount of [[Rupee]]s and a recipe of [[Material]]s to craft, both of which can be found by clearing Levels in the Drablands, or through other means such as the [[Coliseum (Mode)|Coliseum]]. After having an Outfit tailored, Link will then be ushered to try it on in the shop's [[Fitting Room]]. The Catalog lists Outfits that Link already owns, as well as Outfits yet to be tailored and their required Materials.<ref>{{Cite|Dahling, if you wish to create a new outfit, make a Custom Order. Peruse my Catalog for what's already in stock.|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref>

Link can enter the Fitting Room to change into any of the Outfits he has made. A [[Stylish Woman]] near the counter will comment on Link's new Outfit when he emerges from the Fitting Room. The shop also has a [[Mirror (Object)|Mirror]] by the front, which Link can approach to look at the Outfit he is currently wearing. While viewing this Mirror, Link can also see a [[Cat]] near the rack of clothes behind him. However, this Cat can only be seen in this Mirror and cannot be approached. This cat also changes color daily.
