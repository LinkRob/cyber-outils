{{Term/Store|Cross the Hebra Mountains||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 16 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cross the Hebra Mountains|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Cross the Hebra Mountains}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given five minutes to reach a Targeted Position in the eastern end of the map, starting in the north. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s first obstacle is an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} not far from the starting position, containing {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}} and having a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok}} hiding beside its gate. As the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} enters the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} spawns, which must be defeated to capture it and reopen its exit gate. Then, near the center of the map, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} encounters another small group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok}}, as well as three {{Plural|HWAoC|Frost Pebblit|link}} nearby. Another group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Frost Pebblit}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok}} can be found again in front of another {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} much closer to the Targeted Position, which is the same as the first except for an extra {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin}}. Behind the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}'s exit gate is an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}} in front of a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Keese|link}}, although they do not have to be defeated. Afterwards, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must paraglide up to the Targeted Position using an air vent in the ground. Upon arrival, a {{Term|HWAoC|Lynel}} appears, and must be defeated to complete the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Wildberry|link}}.

A {{Term|HWAoC|Korok|link}} can be found in the southwestern corner of the map, and if talked to, gives a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}} to the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}.
