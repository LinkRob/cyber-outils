The {{Term/Store|Moon Inside|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are a series of mini-{{Plural|MM3D|Dungeon|link}} found inside the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}} in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 150}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Moon Dungeons<br/>This is the common name for the four mystical dungeons Link explored while inside Termina's Moon: the Deku Dungeon, the Goron Dungeon, the Zora Dungeon and the Link Dungeon. |retrieved= January 10, 2017 |url= http://web.archive.org/web/20160311223549/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/m.jsp |title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type=archive}}</ref> The {{Plural|MM3D|Moon Dungeon}} are connected by the {{Term|MM3D|Grassy Playfield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= {{Big|The Grassy Playfield}}<br/>Four masked children prance around the lone tree atop the hill.|game= MM |guide= Nintendo |page= 107}}</ref> It is the location of the final battle between {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Mask (Item){{!}}Majora's Mask|link}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Plural|MM3D|Inside the Moon}} become accessible once {{Term|MM3D|Link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Tatl|link}} successfully summon the {{Term|MM3D|Four Giants|link}} to halt the descent of the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}}. Afterwards, Majora's Mask will discard the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}}'s unconscious body and enter the {{Term|MM3D|Moon}}'s mouth,<ref>{{Cite|A puppet that can no longer be used is mere garbage. This puppet's role has just ended...|Majora|MM}}</ref> once more driving it down to {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... I shall consume. Consume... Consume everything.|Moon|MM}}</ref> To stop this, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Tatl}} follow the cursed Mask and transport to a serene, hilled landscape known as the {{Term|MM3D|Grassy Playfield}}.

Near a {{Term|MM3D|Tree|link}} atop a hill in the {{Term|MM3D|Grassy Playfield}} are four {{Plural|MM3D|Moon Child|link}} playing. Each {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} bears the remains of {{Plural|MM3D|Boss|link}} that were plaguing each Giant. When spoken to, they ask {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to play with them in exchange for {{Plural|MM3D|Mask|link}}. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} complies, he will be teleported to one of the {{Plural|MM3D|Inside the Moon}}, depending on which {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} was spoken to.

The individual {{Plural|MM3D|Moon Dungeon}} are entirely optional, as {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must only speak to the {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} that sits beneath the {{Term|MM3D|Tree}} wearing Majora's Mask. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} has not completed all of the {{Plural|MM3D|Moon Dungeon}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} will ask {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to play with him before telling him that his {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}} are weak.<ref>{{Cite|Will you play...with me? [Yes] You have only weak masks... So...you'll play?|Moon Child|MM}}</ref> However, if {{Term|MM3D|Link}} has given away each of his {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} will notice and offer to play "good guys against bad guys",<ref>{{Cite|You don't have any masks left, do you? Well, let's do something else. Let's play good guys against bad guys... Yes. Let's play that.|Moon Child|MM}}</ref> giving {{Term|MM3D|Link}} the {{Term|MM3D|Fierce Deity's Mask|link}} before initiating the final battle.<ref>{{Cite|Are you ready? You're the bad guy. And when you're bad, you just run. That's fine, right? Well... Shall we play?|Moon Child|MM}}</ref>

===Deku Dungeon===
The '''Deku Dungeon''' is a simple trial that requires {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}} to skip across spinning platforms containing {{Plural|MM3D|Deku Flower|link}} and {{Plural|MM3D|Yellow Deku Flower|link}} while also avoiding spinning, spiked bars. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can only enter it through giving the {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} wearing {{Term|MM3D|Odolwa's Remains|link}} one {{Term|MM3D|Mask}}. On each side of the Deku Dungeon are {{Plural|MM3D|Gossip Stone|link}}, which speak of the {{Term|MM3D|Garo's Mask|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Romani's Mask|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Troupe Leader's Mask|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Postman's Hat|link}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Couple's Mask|link}}. On one of the ledges on the right-hand side is a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} at the end asks for one more {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} and in return asks {{Term|MM3D|Link}} whether the people he considers to be friends reciprocate the sentiment.<ref>{{Cite|Your friends... What kind of... people are they? I wonder... Do these people... think of you... as a friend?|Moon Child|MM}}</ref>

===Goron Dungeon===
The '''Goron Dungeon''' is a trial that requires {{Term|MM3D|Goron Link|link}} to race across thin strips of land, only being directed by bouncing off of open {{Plural|MM3D|Treasure Chest|link}}. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can only enter it through giving the {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} wearing {{Term|MM3D|Goht's Remains|link}} two {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}}. Scattered across the Goron Dungeon are {{Plural|MM3D|Gossip Stone}} that speak of {{Term|MM3D|Kamaro's Mask|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Captain's Hat|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Gibdo Mask|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Don Gero's Mask|link}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy's Mask|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} at the end asks for two more {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}} and in return questions {{Term|MM3D|Link}} whether the things that make him happy also make others happy.<ref>{{Cite|What makes you happy? I wonder...what makes you happy...does it make...others happy, too?|Moon Child|MM}}</ref>

===Zora Dungeon===
The '''Zora Dungeon''' is a watery maze that requires {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link|link}} to swim through interconnected tubes to reach a destination. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can only enter it through giving the {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} wearing {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg's Remains|link}} three {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}}. The path to the Moon Child is '''left''', '''left''', '''right''', '''right'''. Similarly, the path to the Piece of Heart is '''left''', '''left''', '''right''', '''left'''. In the Nintendo 3DS version, the path to the Moon Child is '''left''', '''right''', '''top''' and the path to the Piece of Heart is '''left''', '''right''', '''middle''' All other paths lead to air pockets with {{Plural|MM3D|Gossip Stone}}.

The {{Plural|MM3D|Gossip Stone}} in the Zora Dungeon speak of {{Term|MM3D|Stone Mask|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Bremen Mask|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Blast Mask|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Giant's Mask|link}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Mask of Scents|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} at the end asks for three more {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}} and in return posits whether doing the right thing makes everyone happy.<ref>{{Cite|The right thing...what is it? I wonder...if you do the right thing...does it make...everybody...happy?|Moon Child|MM}}</ref>

===Link Dungeon===
The '''Link Dungeon''' is a linear set of rooms that require skills usable only by {{Term|MM3D|Link}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Hylian|link}} form. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can only enter it through giving the {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} wearing {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold's Remains|link}} four {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}}. Inside, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must fight a {{Term|MM3D|Dinolfos|link}}, a {{Term|MM3D|Garo Master|link}}, and an {{Term|MM3D|Iron Knuckle (Boss)|link}} to progress through each room. The room with the Iron Knuckle yields a {{Term|MM3D|Treasure Chest}} containing {{Plural|MM3D|Bombchu|link}} upon defeat, which are used to destroy the cracked wall hiding an {{Term|MM3D|Eyeball Switch|link}}, which summons a ladder when hit. The subsequent room also contains a cracked ceiling, which hides a frozen {{Term|MM3D|Eyeball Switch}}, requiring {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to hit it with a {{Term|MM3D|Fire Arrow|link}} in order to remove the bars protecting the door to the exit. Alternatively, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can play the "{{Term|MM3D|Song of Storms|link}}" before the {{Plural|MM3D|Gossip Stone}} found in the previous rooms. Doing so will make the ladder appear and will remove the bars from the door without the need to hit the {{Plural|MM3D|Eyeball Switch}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= EmptyGarbageCan|published= April 5, 2015|retrieved= September 7, 2018|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KxJM7bTRJns|title= How to Beat Twinmold Child Stage Without Bombchus - The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask Walkthrough|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref> The last {{Term|MM3D|Moon Child}} asks for four more {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}} before asking {{Term|MM3D|Link}} what his true face is.<ref>{{Cite|Your true face... What kind of... face is it? I wonder... The face under the mask... Is that... your true face?|Moon Child|MM}}</ref>
