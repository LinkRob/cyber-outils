The '''Repellent''' is an item in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Repellent is sold by the [[Swamp Merchant]] in the northern part of the [[Vendoss]] swamp for 100 [[Rupee]]s. When [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] purchases the item, all of the [[Zora (Enemy)|Swamp Zola]] disappear from the [[Forest of Albon]].
