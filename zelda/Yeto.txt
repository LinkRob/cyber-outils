{{Term/Store|Yeto||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 277"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} lives in the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link}} with his wife, {{Term|TPHD|Yeta|link}}, who owns a shard of the [[Mirror of Twilight]]. He helps {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} reach his home by snowboarding. As his wife {{Term|TPHD|Yeta}} grows ill, he prepares a Soup for her. He needs [[Reekfish]] to make his Soup, and as such had been seen by [[Lake Hylia]] attempting to catch one. This made the inhabitants of the [[Zora's River]] suspicious as to what exactly was happening on [[Snowpeak]]. As {{Term|TPHD|Link}} explores the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins}}, he finds more ingredients for {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}}'s Soup. By bringing them to him, {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} can prepare a better Soup, increasing its healing properties. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} is able to scoop {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}}'s Soup with an empty [[Bottle]]. After beating [[Blizzeta]], {{Term|TPHD|Yeta}}'s possessed form, {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} and his wife hug each other, releasing [[Heart]]s and eventually a [[Heart Container]].

{{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} and his wife can be challenged to a [[Snowboard Race]] to the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins}} after completing the dungeon. Beating the couple will earn Link a [[Piece of Heart]].
