{{Term/Store|Foul Fruit|Foul Fruits|Series, ALBW|plural}} are items in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Foul Fruits can be purchased either from the [[Kakariko Village|Kakariko Item Shop]] or from the [[Lakeside Item Seller|Lakeside Item Shop]] for 30 [[Rupee]]s. After purchase, they appear in the item selection screen.

When Link eats a Foul Fruit, he stuns any nearby enemies by squealing, temporarily incapacitating them.<ref>{{Cite|A seriously sour fruit. One bite will make you squeal so loud you can stun nearby enemies.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> Powerful enemies, such as dungeon bosses, are not affected by Foul Fruits. After a single use, Foul Fruits disappear from Link's inventory and must be purchased from a store once more.

==Trivia==
*Foul Fruits share many characteristics with [[Scoot Fruit]]s, as both are single-use items that can be obtained in the same shops and are items not held in [[Bottle]]s.
