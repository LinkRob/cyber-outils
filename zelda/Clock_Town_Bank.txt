The {{Term/Store|Clock Town Bank||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Clock Town Bank|Sign|MM3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town Bank}} is located in [[West Clock Town]] and is open 24 hours a day. In {{MM3D}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town Bank}} is instead located in [[South Clock Town]], partially embedded within the back of the [[Clock Tower]]. It is operated by the {{Term|MM3D|Banker|link}}, who possesses the ability to recognize {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} and his associated balance through a system of invisible stamping. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can deposit the {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee|link}} he collects through his adventures in [[Termina]] to preserve them for the next cycle, as any {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}} left in his Wallet will be gone when he plays the "[[Song of Time]]" to travel back to the [[First Day]]. However, the young hero is only able to deposit a maximum of 5,499 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}} with the [[Giant Wallet]], as once a balance of at least 5,000 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}} is reached, the Bank will accept no further deposits.<ref name=quote1>{{Cite|What's this? You've already saved up 5000 Rupees?! Well, little guy, I can't take any more deposits. Sorry, but this is all I can give you.|Banker|MM}}</ref>

During the night, there is a 4 {{Term|MM3D|Rupee}} service charge on withdrawals.<ref>{{Cite|...You know, at this time of day there's a 4 Rupee service charge on withdrawals!|Banker|MM}}</ref> Interest is awarded in the form of a [[Blue Rupee]] whenever Link's saving account reaches 1,000 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? You've already saved up 1000 Rupees!?! Well, little guy, here's your special gift. Take it! That's what they call interest!|Banker|MM}}</ref> When 200 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}} have been deposited, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is rewarded with the {{Term|MM3D|Adult Wallet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|See! Doesn't it hold more than your old one? Fill it up and bring it all in to deposit!|Banker|MM}}</ref> Once the maximum deposit of 5,000 to 5,499 is achieved, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is rewarded with a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref name=quote1/> Thereafter, he receives 5 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}} every additional time the limit is reached.
