The {{Term/Store|East Reservoir Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''East Reservoir Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}, which is part of the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. Its southern end is secured by the {{Term|BotW|Rutala Dam|link}}, and it feeds {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} into both the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Rutala River|link}}.

Every 10 years, {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)}} faces heavy {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Rainfall}} that would previously wash away the {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Once every 10 years, the Lanayru region experiences unusually heavy rainfall. The Zora River flooded every time. The tides damages not only our domain but our people, washing away poor souls and causing great suffering and disarray.|History of the Zora, Part Two|BotW}}</ref> During the {{Term|BotW|Era of Prosperity|link}} 10,000 years ago, the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} joined forces to create the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake}} in a single year, which would protect {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}} from floods.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 362}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|By joining the architectural genius of the Zora and Hyrule's technological prowess, East Reservoir Lake was swiftly built. Thanks to this fruitful partnership, Hyrule was no longer plagued by these devastating floods.|History of the Zora, Part Two|BotW}}</ref> Ever since then, the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} kings have maintained the promise to manage its {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} level, making the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake}} a symbol of the bond between {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} and the rest of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|In gratitude, the Zora king promised the king of Hyrule to manage the reservoir level to protect all of Hyrule from floods. Each Zora king since has kept that oath, spanning 10,000 years. That is why the reservoir signifies our bond with Hyrule.|History of the Zora, Part Two|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} have appointed monitors to alert their king if the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} ever exceeds acceptable levels.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 399}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} was reactivated following the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, it took up residence in the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake}}. Its ability to generate {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} caused heavy {{Term|BotW|Rain}} to fill up the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake}}.<ref>{{Cite|Of late it has been mercilessly spouting water into the air. As a result, this area has been plagued by heavy rains.|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} began to fear that it would soon burst, which would put the lives of the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Hylian}} downstream of the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake|display=Lake}} in danger.<ref>{{Cite|Sadly, the rains have filled the easten {{Color|BotW Blue|reservoir}} nearly to the point of flooding. If the {{Color|BotW Blue|reservoir}} bursts, as it soon will, I fear immense damage will befall not only Zora's Domain... but also the area downstream from us. There, Hylian lives are in very great danger.|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> As a result, {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}} left {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} in search of a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} to help them.<ref>{{Cite|I have been searching for someone like you for a long while. A man like you, Link, who carries himself with power! Right now, Zora's Domain is in grave danger because of the massive rainfall coming from {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}! Please, promise you will help us! We need your strength, warrior! Won't you please come to {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} with me?|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to meet him at the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake}} once he has gathered at least 20 {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll wait at {{Color|BotW Blue|East Reservoir Lake}}, right by the Divine Beast. Gather {{Color|BotW Red|at least 20}} {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}<r, and then come join me!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Link... I'll be waiting for you at {{Color|BotW Blue|East Reservoir Lake}}, right by the Divine Beast. When you are ready, come join me there!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} will ensure that he is prepared before they face {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}.<ref>{{Cite|Glad to see you're ready to go, Link! Are you ready for this? Do you have your {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora armor}} and enough {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}?|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that he is ready, the pair of them will jump into the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake|display=Lake}} to begin their attack on the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm ready!] {{Big|Wonderful!}} You never cease to amaze, Link! Now then, let us go and appease that Divine Beast as one! {{Big|Here we go!!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has halted the flow of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} from {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, it will remain dormant in the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake}} until it is reclaimed. Once this has been done, {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} will pilot the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} out of the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake|display=Lake}} through the {{Term|BotW|Rutala Dam}}.

The northern dam of the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake}} has space for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to rest, including a {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Jar|link}}. It also houses a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that contains a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Shield|link}}.
