{{Term/Store|Mount Nabooru||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Mount Nabooru'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Mount Nabooru is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. [[Joloo Nah Shrine]] sits at the northwestern base of Mount Nabooru.

A [[Stone Talus (Luminous)]] rests on the southern tip of the Mountain.

==Nomenclature==
Mount Nabooru is named after [[Nabooru]], [[Sage of Spirit]] from {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|publisher= Piggyback|page= 351}}</ref>
