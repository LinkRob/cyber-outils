The {{Term/Store|Boat Cruise||Series, MM}} is an event in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Old Koume's Boat Cruise}}<br/>Ticket office closed.|Sign|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Welcome! Wait...is this your first time at the swamp?! In that case, try our famous {{Color|MM3D Red|boat cruise}}! It's a loop of the swamp by way of the {{Color|MM3D Red|Deku Palace}}. Of course, the swamp's been kinda...poisoned lately. Oh, and there's also that huge, horrible monster... No wonder business is so slow... Boat cruise reservations are handled at that {{Color|MM3D Red|window}} over there...|Swamp Tourist Center Guide|MM3D}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The Boat Cruise is a feature around the [[Southern Swamp]] exclusive to humans.<ref>{{Cite|Well, this is where the boat cruise leaves from, but unfortunately it's for humans only.|Koume|MM}}</ref> The tour can only be taken after [[Link]] saves the tour guide [[Koume]] from the [[Woods of Mystery]].<ref>{{Cite|You saved me! I'm the swamp tour guide. I run the boat cruise, so come by if you want a free ride.|Koume|MM3D}}</ref> If Link talks to her in the [[Swamp Tourist Center]] afterwards, she will give him a free tour and the [[Pictograph Box]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Thanks for what you did back there! In a special deal just for you, I'll let you take this cruise for free. Ah, yes, we've got a special going on right now, so we're giving this out for free!|Koume|MM3D}}</ref> [[Link]] can take the tour to reach the [[Deku Palace]] when the water is still poisonous. Taking the tour will also defeat the two [[Big Octo]]s blocking the entrances to the Deku Palace and [[Woodfall]] area.

After defeating [[Odolwa]] at the [[Woodfall Temple]], instead of a regular Boat Cruise, Koume will offer Link to play a Target Shooting [[Mini-Game]], in which Link must hit targets with his [[Bow]] while Koume flies around.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, but wait... Yes. Just as I thought... It seems the poison swapm has been cleaned up, so why not try something that's more interesting? How about a little game? I'll fly around on my broom while you try to hit the {{Color|MM3D Red|target}} below me! Doesn't it sound fun? Well, wanna try?|Koume|MM3D}}</ref> If he manages to hit 20 or more [[Target]]s without shooting Koume at least 10 times, he will be rewarded with a [[Piece of Heart]]. In {{MM3D}}, Link is rewarded with a [[Bottle]] instead.

The first time Link tries out either the Boat Cruise or the Target Shooting Mini-Game is free. However, each subsequent tour will cost him 10 [[Rupee]]s.
