==Location and Uses==
The {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue}} are spread throughout the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. Each one depicts a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} woman carrying a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}}. They also each carry a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Claymore|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can take from them.

{{Term|BotW|Laine|link}}, one of the guards of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, points out the {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, explaining they are remnants of an age long past.<ref name="Laine"/> She shares a rumor that the {{Term|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|display=Swordswomen}} point towards the location of an ancient treasure, but she dismisses it as superstition.<ref>{{Cite|People talk about the statues pointing to the hiding place of ancient treasure... but that's just superstition.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> Due to the dangerous {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} that can be encountered in the sandstorms of the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens}}, she sees no reason to search for this treasure.<ref>{{Cite|Even if it was true, there are dangerous monsters in the sandstorms.<br/>I wouldn't risk it.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> This begins "{{Term|BotW|The Silent Swordswomen|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

By following the {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will eventually come upon {{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos Shrine|link}}. Activating this {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} will complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
