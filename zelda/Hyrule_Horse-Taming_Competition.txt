The {{Term/Store|Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition||Series, BotW}} is an event in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Trust me—I took second place in the Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition!|Rensa|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition}} is a race to tame and retrieve {{Plural|BotW|Wild Horse|link}} within a set period of time. At some point prior to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s arrival at the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Rensa|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tasseren|link}} participated in the first {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition|display=Competition}}. {{Term|BotW|Tasseren}} took first place in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition|display=Competition}} by completing it in less than two minutes, and after years of practicing,<ref name="Years">{{Cite|Hahaha! Breaking the two-minute mark is pretty tough, right? But don't be discouraged. I mean, it took me years of practice to place second in the Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition.|Rensa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Rensa}} secured second place by retrieving a {{Term|BotW|Wild Horse}} in two minutes flat.<ref>{{Cite|My record is two minutes flat, so you need to bring back a wild horse within that time. PREPARE FOR EXTREME TAMING!|Rensa|BotW}}</ref>

If spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Rensa}} will brag about his achievement to {{Term|BotW|Link}} before challenging the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|display=Champion|link}} to the second {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition}}.<ref>{{Cite|They're wild, so 'til you calm 'em, it's gonna be one crazy ride. But there's no feeling quite like taming a wild stallion! Trust me—I took second place in the Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition!|Rensa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|HEY! I saw that look you gave me! ... Fine! You don't believe me? Let's go, pal! You and me. Right here. Right now. You want to challenge the second-place Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition winner to a tame-off?! YOU GOT IT, MISTER!|Rensa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts, he will be tasked with taming a {{Term|BotW|Wild Horse}} and returning with it to the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}} within two minutes, which activates the "{{Term|BotW|Wild Horses|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. Upon his record being beaten, {{Term|BotW|Rensa}} will reveal that the only competitors in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition|display=Competition}} before {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s arrival were {{Term|BotW|Rensa}}'s brother and himself.<ref name="Amazing">{{Cite|{{Big|Amazing!}} But...I can't believe you beat my two-minute record so easily... I mean, I won second place in the Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition! To be fair, it was just my brother and I competing... Anyway, here's the purse for winning the second official Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition! You earned it.|Rensa|BotW}}</ref> As a reward for beating his record, {{Term|BotW|Rensa}} will gift {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}, valued at 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref name="Amazing"/> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has beaten {{Term|BotW|Rensa}}'s record, he will not be able to participate in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition|display=Competition}} again.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} travels too far away from the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Rensa}} will call to him and disqualify him from the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition|display=Competition}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, where are you going?|Rensa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you go too far, then I can't record an accurate time. Could you try to stay at least somewhat near the stable?|Rensa|BotW}}</ref> While participating in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Horse-Taming Competition}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} cannot place the {{Term|BotW|Travel Medallion|link}}, nor can he {{Term|BotW|Warping|display=Warp|link}} to any {{Plural|BotW|Travel Gate|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Travel Medallion}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can't travel from here.|Sheikah Slate|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, {{Term|BotW|Rensa}} will also disqualify {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he takes too long.<ref>{{Cite|Aaaaaand time's up!
