==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Moblin Fangs are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by all types of {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Stalkoblin|link}} when they're defeated. They are sharp and too hard to be reshaped.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] with critters to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] can also use Moblin Fangs to make Gray [[Dye]], and [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] accepts them in exchange for [[Mon]].

The [[Fireproof Armor]] set needs four Moblin Fangs to upgrade each of its pieces to their second tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Fireproof Armor''': 12 (0, 4, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
