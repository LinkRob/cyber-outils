The {{Term/Store|Swamp Spider House||Series, MM, MM3D}},{{Fact}} also known as the '''Fearful Spider House''',<ref>{{Cite|Fearful Spider House / dead ahead.|Sign|MM}}</ref> is a mini-dungeon in {{MM}}.

==Entrance to the House==
The Spider House is located relatively close to the [[Deku Palace]] in the [[Southern Swamp]]. However, the entrance to the House itself is blocked by a spiderweb that must be burned (either with a [[Deku Stick]] or with a [[Fire Arrow]]) in order to gain access. The [[Cursed Rich Man]] in the vestibule explains that the curse on the House can only be lifted by defeating all 30 [[Gold Skulltula]]s inside.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Help me! I am not a monster! The spider's curse... It made me this way...|Cursed Rich Man|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you lift the curse...I'll teach you...something good...Hurry...Please...This is awful... In here...The gold ones...The cursed spiders...Defeat them all...Make me normal...again...|Cursed Rich Man|MM}}</ref> When talked to when wearing the [[Mask of Truth]], the Cursed Rich Man's [[dog]] says that its master is always filled with greed.<ref>{{Cite|Rrr-ruff! Stupid master... His eyes are always filled with greed and desire...|Dog|MM}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The Swamp Spider House has several [[puzzle]]s that cannot be completed without a multitude of items. Up to two [[Magic Bean]]s are a possible necessity, as two Gold Skulltula Spirits cannot normally be reached without the platform that the beans provide; however, this problem could be circumvented by using the [[Hookshot]], although this does not become available until much later in the game. Another necessity are [[Bug]]s, which can conveniently be found within the house. The Gold Skulltulas are hiding all over the house, in various nooks and obscured by pillars, dirt, and [[pot]]s. [[Link]] must use all resources acquired up to Magic Beans in order to find all thirty Gold Skulltulas. 

Once the Gold Skulltulas are defeated, the Cursed Rich Man within the Spider House will be cleansed, and he will express his thanks to Link by giving him the Mask of Truth. If Link completes the Swamp Spider House again on subsequent cycles, the Cursed Rich Man's dialogue will be slightly different, and Link will not be rewarded anything.

In {{MM3D}}, defeating all 30 Gold Skulltulas a second time will result in the Cursed Rich Man giving Link a [[Fishing Hole Pass]] and remarking that he will get rid of the mask.
