{{Term/Store|Amy||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, OoS}} is a recurring [[Sub-Boss]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 162 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM, OoS}}}}</ref> She is the third of the four {{Term|Series|Poe Sisters|link}}, and the youngest.<ref name="Tatl">{{Cite|It's Amy, third of the four Poe sisters. Target her when she appears! You got that?|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Amy - one of the Poe sisters}}<br/>Aim for her when she appears!
}}

{{Term|OoT3D|Amy}} appears along with her Sisters in the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}} in {{OoT}}. She and her oldest sister, [[Meg]], are significant in their unique approach, as they test [[Link]]'s [[puzzle]] solving abilities. Similarly to the other sisters, specifically [[Joelle]] and [[Beth (Poe)|Beth]], in her room is a giant portrait of herself. Link must shoot the picture with the {{Term|OoT3D|Bow|link}}. However, instead of summoning her, the portrait flashes red and drops five blocks from its suspension in the ceiling. The blocks are part of a timed picture puzzle, with each block (save for one, which shows the blue flame from Beth) having a section of the picture that Link just shot at. To complete the puzzle, the [[Hero of Time]] must recreate the picture of {{Term|OoT3D|Amy}} using the blocks by pushing them until they are properly aligned. In the event that Link runs out of time, the puzzle and timer will reset, but he will receive a small time extension within which to complete the puzzle each time he fails. Once the picture is recreated, {{Term|OoT3D|Amy}} will finally reveal herself. Like the others except for Meg, if Link targets the Poe Sister, she will disappear and then reappear, and spin towards him with her green flaming torch in hand to attack. The {{Term|OoT3D|Sword}} as well as {{Term|OoT3D|Bow}} are effective weapons against her, but only when she is visible, as with [[Poe|ordinary Poes]]. After five hits with the [[Master Sword]], she will be defeated and vanish in a puff of smoke, and the flamed torch she had stolen in the main room of the Temple will ignite.

== Trivia ==

* In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'', [[Tatl]] claims that Meg is the youngest of the Poe Sisters and Joelle being the oldest. This is developer oversight, as Amy herself is the youngest and Meg is actually is the oldest in the original [[Nintendo 64]] version.
