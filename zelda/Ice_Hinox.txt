{{Term/Store|Ice Hinox|Ice Hinoxes|Series, HWAoC|plural}} are enemies in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
Ice Hinox are purple Hinoxes with turqoise sideburns and blue runes on their body signifying elemental powers. In addition to having all the moves of the Black Hinox, Ice Hinoxes have original moves of their own. Ice Hinox's have a special slap the ground which will cause waves of ice to run through the ground. Ice Hinox can spit a ball of ice which will cause the freeze affect like its other attacks.

Additionally the Ice Hinox's regular attacks now have an area of effect that can give out the frozen status effect to those that get hit.
