{{Term/Store|Batrea Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Batrea Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Batrea Lake is a shallow lake in [[West Necluda]] that can be found just east of the [[Owlan Bridge]]. Near the center of Batrea Lake sits a giant, hollow stump. [[Link]] can find a thin, stone tower at the end of a peninsula which cuts into the [[Lake]]. Three giant, metallic blocks can be found throughout Batrea Lake: one near the stone tower and two more which rest inside the giant, hollow stump. An enemy [[Platform]] can be found near the base of the aforementioned peninsula, atop which Link can find several [[Bomb Barrel]]s.

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] can be found atop the tower, which contains a bundle of five [[Bomb Arrow]]s. A wooden Treasure Chest, which contains a [[Traveler's Bow]], can be found atop the enemy Platform.
