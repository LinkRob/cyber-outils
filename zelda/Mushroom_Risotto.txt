{{Term/Store|Mushroom Risotto|Mushroom Risottos|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and [[Butter|butter]] beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Mushroom Risotto can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}}, [[Hylian Rice]], [[Goat Butter]], and [[Rock Salt]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The default version of Mushroom Risotto can only be made either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}}, which offer no status effects, or by using multiple Mushrooms with differing effects. When eaten, some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} will be restored to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The smell of the Mushrooms and butter in this meal draws people to eat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

[[Ivee's Diary]], which can be found in her home in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, details a recipe for Mushroom Risotto.<ref>{{Cite|Today's Recipe:<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Mushroom Risotto}}|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref> [[Ivee]] lists the ingredients as one bundle of Hylian Rice, one serving of Goat Butter, a few Hylian Shrooms, and a little bit of Rock Salt.<ref>{{Cite|Ingredients:<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian Rice}} (one bundle)<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Goat Butter}} (one serving)<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian Shrooms}} (approximately a few)<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Rock Salt}} (exactly "a little")|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref> She also notes that the Hylian Shrooms can be substituted for a [[Hearty Salmon]], which would result in [[Salmon Risotto]].<ref>{{Cite|Note: Use a {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}} instead of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian shrooms}} for a fish-lover's dish!|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

Several different kinds of Mushrooms can be used when making Mushroom Risotto, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. These meals will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}} or other energizing ingredients. Eating them will replenish part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}} or other enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely replenished and overfilled by eating these.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}} or other hasty ingredients. If eaten, Link's movement speed will be increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}} or other spicy ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when consumed.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}} or other electric ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}} or other mighty ingredients. Consuming these meals will boost Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}} or other tough ingredients. Eating them will grant Link a boost to his defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}} or other sneaky ingredients. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} can be increased by eating these.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Mushroom Risotto''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Mushroom Risotto}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The tantalizing aroma of mushrooms and butter beckons you to the table.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}} or other chilly ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
