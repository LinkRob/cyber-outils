The '''Gold Brooch''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Gold Brooch|Highest-quality jewelry. Wearing it makes rupees appear more often when breaking objects or defeating monsters.<ref name="Gold Brooch">{{Cite|Highest-quality jewelry. The Gold Brooch is rewarded for completing the [[Secret Chief Talks]] [[Side Quest]]. Equipping it grants the [[Rupee Appearance Up +]] effect, increasing the drop rate of [[Rupee|Rupees]].
