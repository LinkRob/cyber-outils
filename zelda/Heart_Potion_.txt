==Location and Uses==
'''Heart Potions +''' are an [[Upgrading|upgraded]] version of the regular [[Heart Potion|Heart Potions]]. They can be infused from a regular Heart Potion for a fee of 20 [[Rupee#Skyward%20Sword|Rupees]] at the [[Infusion Shop#Skyward%20Sword|Infusion Shop]] and require the following [[Bug#Skyward%20Sword|Bugs]]: three {{Plural|Series|Blessed Butterfly|link}}, one [[Woodland Rhino Beetle]] and one [[Volcanic Ladybug]].

When consumed, an Heart Potion + completely refills its user's [[Heart|hearts]].<ref name="Obtain" />
