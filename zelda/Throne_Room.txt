==Features and Overview==
==={{TMC|-}}===
The {{Term|TMC|Throne Room}} is located within {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} in {{TMC}}. {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} resides within it until he is turned to stone by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}, who takes his place. {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} are also found here, the latter also having been turned to stone.

When {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Throne Room}} becomes an arena for {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to fight a {{Term|TMC|Red Dark Nut|link}}. After it is defeated, the throne can be pushed aside to reveal a staircase leading to a subterranean level of the {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Throne Room}} is a single chamber inside {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle|link}} in {{ALBW}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Throne Room}} serves as the stage of the final battle of the game, where {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} faces {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|link}} for the freedom of {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda|link}} and ownership of {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Triforce|link}}. After unlocking the main door at the end of {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} is able to access {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|link}}'s offices, which contains a portal back to {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda}}'s offices in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}} that now contain several {{Plural|ALBW|Fairy|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} follows the same path from {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda}}'s private office to the {{Term|ALBW|Throne Room}}, where he learns of {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda}}'s duplicity and conspiracy with {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} to steal {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Triforce}} to restore {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}, which lost its own {{Term|ALBW|Triforce}} when {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda}}'s ancestors destroyed it to stop the endless wars fought over it. After sharing this story with {{Term|ALBW|Link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|display=Hilda}} steals the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} from {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda}}, who is contained in her painting nearby. She then summons {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}}, commanding him to take the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Courage|link}} from {{Term|ALBW|Link}}. The hero bests the beast, who captures {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda}} in a painting as well, and absorbs it to gain the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Wisdom}}. {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} then attempts to take the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Courage}} again, but falls to the blade of the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword|link}}, exploding in a burst of dark energy and releasing both {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|display=Hilda}} and {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}.

[[File:BotW Throne Room 2.png|thumb|left|250px|The {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}]]
In {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}, the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}} is located on the lowest level of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}. Even in its ruined condition, it is heavily decorated with banners, statues, and carpets. The floor of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}} is decorated with a circular pattern with the symbol of the {{Term|BotW|Triforce|link}} engraved in the center. The {{Plural|BotW|Marks of the Goddesses|link}} positioned in the center of each respective {{Term|BotW|Triforce}} piece while the {{Term|BotW|Royal Crest|link}} sits in the negative space created by the triangles. The metal decoration above the throne bears the symbol of the {{Term|BotW|Triforce}}, in addition to a circular music scale depicting "{{Term|BotW|Zelda's Lullaby|link}}".

Following the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} used her newly-awakened sealing power to contain {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} within the interior of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}, where he bides his time and gathers strength to escape her grasp.<ref>{{Cite|The words of guidance you have been hearing since your awakening are from Princess Zelda herself. Even now, as she works to restrain Ganon from within Hyrule Castle, she calls out for your help.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Inside the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon|link}} suspended from the ceiling. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters without having defeated any of the four {{Plural|BotW|Blight Ganon|link}}, he will be forced to face them. They consistently appear in the order of {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Waterblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, and then {{Term|BotW|Thunderblight Ganon|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeated any of them prior to entering the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}}, they will not appear.

Once all four have been defeated, whether in the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}} or their respective {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|... Link....}} Link...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She apologizes for not being able to hold {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} back any longer.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry...but my power isn't strong enough... I can't hold him...|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> A {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} laser bursts out of {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon|link}}, breaking it open and releasing {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}. Due to {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}'s weight, the fall breaks the floor of the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}}, causing both of them to fall into the {{Term|BotW|Astral Observatory|link}}.
