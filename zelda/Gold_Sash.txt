'''Gold Sash''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Gold Sash|A useful waistcloth woven with golden thread. Protects you from being windblown.<ref name="Gold Sash">{{Cite|A useful waistcloth woven with golden thread. Protects you from being windblown.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Gold Sash is rewarded for defeating three [[Lanmolas]] and completing the [[Wild Sandstorms]] [[Side Quest]]. Equipping it protects [[Princess Zelda]] from gusts of wind.
