{{Term/Store|Free Mode||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} is a game mode in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Free Mode}}|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
Free Mode is a version of [[Legend Mode]] that allows players to play Scenarios with full control over the selection of Warriors being put into the map, allowing players to obtain any Scenario's hidden [[Piece of Heart]] & [[Heart Container]], and the [[Illustration]] Piece from the Hard Difficulty-exclusive [[Gold Skulltula]]s, unlocked after beating Legend Mode in its entirety, without potentially replaying any given scenario potentially 3 times just to get everything.

However, newer Scenarios cannot be unlocked in Free Mode, so one has to keep forging on in Legend Mode to unlock everything. If a Scenario hasn’t been beaten yet in Legend Mode, it won’t show up in Free Mode at all.
