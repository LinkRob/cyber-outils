{{Compendium/Extract|Savage Lynel Shield}}
{{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Shield}} are considered the ultimate {{Term|BotW|Lynel Shield|link}}, known to be wielded by {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are also used by {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lynel|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lynel|link}}. Not only are they capable of defending the most brutal of attacks, they can also cut down powerful foes with a deflection.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

As they are made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Shield}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a thunderstorm. They also sink if they are dropped in water, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
