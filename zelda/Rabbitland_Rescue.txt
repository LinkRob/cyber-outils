{{Term/Store|Rabbitland Rescue||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 290}}</ref>

==Features==
===Overview===
Situated in the [[Forest Realm]], Rabbitland Rescue takes in all the [[rabbit]]s scattered throughout [[Hyrule/Appearances by Game#Spirit Tracks|Hyrule]] that are without a home.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Rabbitland Rescue, a haven for rabbits and their furless friends. There are millions of poor rabbits without a home or human companionship. Here at Rabbitland Rescue, we save these bunnies from the harsh wilderness... And provide them with much-needed frolicking.|Bunnio|ST}}</ref> It is run by a [[Bunnio|rabbit-loving man]] wearing a rabbit costume who informs [[Link]] that Rabbitland Rescue is also a place for people to relax and forget their troubles by frolicking with the bunnies.<ref>{{Cite|Rabbitland Rescue is a place for all poor bunnies without a home. It's also a place for people to relax and forget their troubles. Here, you can indulge in the simple pleasure of frolicking with bunnies!|Bunnio|ST}}</ref> Rabbitland Rescue is set up with five different habitats representing the different realms of Hyrule so that the different kinds of rabbits can feel at home in their areas.

When Link first visits Rabbitland Rescue, he is given the [[Rabbit Net]] to capture any rabbits he comes across.<ref>{{Cite|OK, I'm convinced. If you want to join the Rabbitland family, you'll need this! Hm? Why'd I give you a net, you ask? Well...because if you want to frolic, you gotta work for it first!|Bunnio|ST}}</ref> All captured rabbits are automatically taken to Bunnio, who will pay Link in [[Rupee]]s for how many he has caught. He will also give prizes for capturing different amounts and kinds.<ref>{{Cite|Now go catch some of
those poor, homeless rabbits and tote them back to me. I've got lots of great prizes for you, depending on how many you bring back!|Bunnio|ST}}</ref> As the young hero captures 10 rabbits in each realm, he will be rewarded with various kinds of [[Treasure#Spirit Tracks|treasure]], and once he obtains all 50 rabbits, he will be given the [[Swordsman's Scroll]].

===Force Gems===
If Link brings [[Mona]] from [[Hyrule Castle Town]], she will reward the young hero with a [[Force Gem]] for bringing them back together.<ref>{{Cite|You know, I think I've changed my mind. I won't be heading back to town. After all, it's my husband's big heart that first drew me to him. Maybe I'm the one who needs to change how I think about things. You've done me a huge service in bringing me here. Thank you so much.|Mona|ST}}</ref> This gem will reveal new [[Spirit Tracks (Object)|Spirit Tracks]] west of the [[Forest Sanctuary]].

==Trivia==
*The rabbits' placement in the area roughly reflects their original Realm placements. For example, the Snow Rabbits are placed in the northwestern (and snowy) zone after being captured in the [[Snow Realm]] (Hyrule's northwestern Realm).
