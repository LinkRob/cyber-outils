==Features and Overview==
As its name suggests, the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers}} are the underground waterways of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} which connects to {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} only explores the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers}} in his {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link|display= Wolf form}}. When {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} is captured by the {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast|link}}, he is imprisoned in a jail cell inside the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers}}. Here, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} meets {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}} for the first time, who frees {{Term|TPHD|Link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Midna}} tells {{Term|TPHD|Link}} that she wishes to introduce him to someone, and the two begin searching for an exit from the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers}}.<ref>{{Cite|Look, there's someone I want to introduce you to...but I'll need you to go to that tower to do it!|Midna|TP}}</ref> As {{Term|TPHD|Midna}} and {{Term|TPHD|Link}} traverse the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers}}, they must lower and raise the water levels of the underground passageway in order to eventually exit through a small tunnel at the far end. At the edge of the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers}} is a long, spiraling staircase which {{Term|TPHD|Link}} must cross via ropes to reach the rooftops of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle}}. By jumping from rooftop to rooftop, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} eventually reaches the tower in which {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda|link}} is imprisoned.

Later in {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s quest, {{Term|TPHD|Midna}} is gravely injured by {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}}, and {{Term|TPHD|Link}} must once again go through the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers}} to take the dying {{Term|TPHD|Midna}} to {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda}}. He enters them through a secret passageway from {{Term|TPHD|Telma's Bar|link}}, which leads to {{Term|TPHD|Jovani's House|link}} and then to the underground waterway.<ref>{{Cite|The bar is actually a kind of safe house for my friends. There's a passageway that leads to the castle from in there, as well.|Telma|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That treasure chest is connected to the underground waterway... You can get out from there.|Jovani|TP}}</ref> Here, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} explores a new section of the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers}} which is infested with {{Plural|TPHD|Skulltula|link}} as well as cobwebs that he must burn in order to proceed. This connects to the area in which {{Term|TPHD|Link}} was imprisoned near the beginning of his quest.

Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} reaches {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda}} for the second time, he will be unable to return to the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers}} again.
