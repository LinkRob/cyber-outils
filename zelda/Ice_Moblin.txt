==Characteristics==
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
{{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} with runic tattoos on them wielding a {{Term|HWAoC|Spiked Moblin Club|link}}. {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin}} can hit the ground with their club to create a wave of {{Term|HWAoC|Ice|link}}, as well as causing a line of {{Term|HWAoC|Ice}} to run through the ground. Its special attacks give the freeze effect if they connect.

Using the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Rod|link}} opens their {{Term|HWAoC|Weak Point Gauge|link}} and when defeated, they drop ammunition for the {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Rod|link}}.
