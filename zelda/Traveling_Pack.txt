==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Traveling Pack}} are used by {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} travelers to carry their belongings. When they initially set out, they only carry the iron cage frame that contains their {{Term|BotW|Traveling Pack|display=Packs}}, but they pick up the cloth sacks later for storing things that they find.<ref name="CaC"/>

* {{Term|BotW|Modar|link}}
