==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|In the Clutches of the Yiga!}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} except for {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to reach the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}'s exit within six minutes, starting from the other side of the map. The base is filled with many {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} that, along with {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Keese|link}} and some {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Chuchu|link}}. There are two main paths the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} can take to the exit, which more {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} appear in as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} advances. Not far from the split paths, {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga|link}} appears close behind the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} to stop them, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} appear in the same room as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} as they near the exit. The blademasters' defeat opens a gate in the exit's direction. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the exit, {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} spawns and the timer resets to three minutes. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} is completed when {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} is defeated.
