The following is a list of items called '''Treasures''' in {{ZA}}.

==Black Orb==
{{Main|Black Orb}}
[[File:ZA BlackOrb.png|left]]
The '''Black Orb''' is obtained in a room in the [[Shrine of Illusion]]. It is placed in a cup in another room of the same dungeon to open a door allowing [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] to proceed onward.

==Bone==
[[File:ZA Bone.png|left|30px]]
{{Main|Bone}}
The '''Bone''' is purchased from the general store in [[Great Wimbich]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. It is given to the shopkeeper's dog outside, which will give Zelda the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Shore Axe|Short Axe]] [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure|Spell]] in return.

==Bouquet ==
[[File:ZA Bouquet Sprite.png|left]]
{{main|Bouquet}}
The '''Bouquet''' is an item sold by a [[Shrine Merchant]] in the [[Shrine of Strength]] for 20 Rupees. It has no use.

==Candle==
[[File:ZA Candle.png|left|30px]]
{{Main|Candle}}
'''Candles''' can be bought from various merchants around [[Tolemac]]. They are used to light up dark rooms, but can only be used once.

==Coal==
[[File:ZA Coal.png|left|30px]]
The '''Coal''' is received from the [[Quarry Miner]] in the [[East Forest]]. When given to the [[Blacksmith]] in Great Wimbich, it creates the [[Diamond]].

==Compass==
{{Main|Compass}}
[[File:ZA Compass.png|left|250px]]
{{clear}}
'''Compasses''' are found in every shrine. They act similar to the Compasses in canonical ''Zelda'' games (i.e. showing Zelda's current location), but with the added effect of warping Zelda back to the shrine the Compass was found, if used outside of the shrine.

==Crystal Heart==
{{Main|Heart Container}}
[[File:ZA Crystal Heart Sprite.png|left|30px]]
'''Crystal Hearts''' are jewel-like heart items hidden throughout the land of Tolemac, with four in total. They increase Zelda's life meter by one each time she finds one.

==Diamond==
[[File:ZA Diamond.png|left|30px]]
The '''Diamond''' is obtained by giving the [[#Coal|Coal]] to the Blacksmith in Great Wimbich. Receiving the Diamond automatically rewards Zelda with 999 Rupees. It is kept in the inventory after, but has no other use.

==Empty Bottle==
[[File:ZA EmptyBottle.png|left|30px]]
{{Main|Bottle}}
The '''Empty Bottle''' is given to Zelda by [[Glebb]] the thirsty woman in southwestern Tolemac. When used at the [[River Source Pond]], it creates the [[#Water Bottle|Water Bottle]].

==Flute==
{{Main|Flute#Zelda's Adventure}}
[[File:ZA Flute.png|left|30px]]
The '''Flute''' is received from [[Yvonne]] in Great Wimbich. It is used to defeat the [[Rope|snake]] outside of town, which leaves behind the [[Pyros]] Spell.

==Golden Boots==
[[File:ZA Golden Boots Sprite.png|left|30px]]
The '''Golden Boots''' are found in the [[Vendoss]] swamp. They are used to enter [[Sirram Bew]]'s domed house, but do not have to be selected from the inventory to use.

==Harp==
{{Main|Harp}}
[[File:ZA Harp.png|left|30px]]
The '''Harp''' is found in a cave north of the [[Mobilin's Head Inn]]. Playing this instrument allows Zelda to warp back to the beginning of a dungeon.

==Key==
{{Main|Key}}
[[File:ZA Key Sprite.png|left]]
'''Keys''' are items used in Shrines to open locked doors, and can only be used once. Only a very few Keys appear and are used in the game.

==Knife==
[[File:ZA Knife.png|left|30px]]
The '''Knife''' is obtained in a room in the Shrine of Illusion. It is used to cut open the canvas of the wall to allow Zelda to proceed onward.

==Ladder==
[[File:ZA Stepladder.png|left|30px]]
{{Main|Stepladder}}
The '''Ladder''' is found next to a tree in the [[Forest of Ogbam]]. It allows Zelda to cross the chasm in the [[Shrine of Earth]].

==Life Potion==
[[File:ZA Life Potion Sprite.png|left|30px]]
{{Main|Potion}}
'''Life Potions''' can be bought from a few merchants around Tolemac, and is sold for 200 Rupees. Using it refills Zelda's life meter completely. Zelda can only keep one Life Potion at a time.

==Magic Shield==
{{Main|Magical Shield}}
[[File:ZA MagicShield.png|left|30px]]
The '''Magic Shield''' is sold by the [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Forest Trader|Forest Trader]] in the Forest of Ogbam for 500 Rupees. The shield blocks most projectile attacks provided if Zelda is standing still and facing the direction of the attack.

==Map==
{{Main|Map}}
[[File:ZA Maps.png|left|340px]]
{{clear}}
'''Maps''' are used to see the layout of a shrine that Zelda is in. They are found in every shrine, with the exception of the [[Shrine of Destiny]]'s Map, which is instead obtained from [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Link's Guide|Link's Guide]] in the Vendoss swamp.

==Plank==
[[File:ZA Plank.png|left|30px]]
The '''Plank''' is found in the [[Shrine of Water]]. It is used to bridge the gap of a broken bridge in the shrine, allowing Zelda to cross over.

==Raft==
{{Main|Raft}}
[[File:ZA Raft.png|left|30px]]
The '''Raft''' is found sitting next to the pond in the [[Meadow of Skotness]]. It allows Zelda to move across the bodies of water in the Shrine of Water.

Another Raft also appears in the port of [[Seacoast Plain]], where it is used to cross the sea to [[Sea Island]], provided if the seafarer is paid two Rupees and that Zelda has the [[#Vial of Winds|Vial of Winds]].

==Red Boots==
[[File:ZA RedBoots.png|left|30px]]
The '''Red Boots''' are found in the Shrine of Earth. They are used to cross over certain parts of Tolemac's river to reach the northern region. They do not have to be selected from the inventory to use.

==Red Bow==
[[File:ZA RedBow.png|left|30px]]
The '''Red Bow''' is an item sold by a Shrine Merchant in the Shrine of Strength for 50 Rupees. It was stolen from the Red Knight, and is required to make him appear.

==Repellent==
[[File:ZA Repellent.png|left|30px]]
The '''Repellent''' is sold by the [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Swamp Merchant|Swamp Merchant]] in the Vendoss swamp for 100 Rupees. It can be used to repel the [[River Zora|Swamp Zola]].

==Rug==
[[File:ZA Rug.png|left|30px]]
The '''Rug''' is dropped by a [[Vapora]] in the [[Shrine of Air]]. It can be used to make Zelda slide around less on slippery surfaces, such as those in the shrine.

==Rupee==
{{Main|Rupee}}
[[File:ZA Rupee Inventory Sprite.png|left]]
'''Rupees''' are used as the currency of Tolemac. They are used to make purchases, as well as to power the Spells that Zelda can learn, which require a certain amount of Rupees to use. In order to make purchases, Zelda must first select the Rupee in the inventory.

==Saltcellar ==
[[File:ZA Saltcellar Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Saltcellar''' is sold by a Shrine Merchant in the Shrine of Strength for 10 Rupees. It has no use.

==Ticket==
[[File:ZA Tickets Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Tickets''' are sold by the [[Ticket Seller]]s in the Shrine of Strength for 20 Rupees each. They are required to proceed ahead and to challenge the shrine mini-bosses, the Knights. There are two Tickets in total to buy.

==Vial of Winds==
[[File:ZA VialOfWinds.png|left|30px]]
The '''Vial of Winds''' is given to Zelda by Glebb after she brings her back water. It is used in conjunction with the Raft in the Seacoast Plain port to reach the Shrine of Water.

==Wand==
[[File:ZA Wand.png|left|30px]]
The '''Wand''' is Zelda's main weapon used to combat enemies with. The Wand can be used in conjunction with the Spells Zelda learns to throw magical projectiles for added offense, at the cost of a certain amount of Rupees per Spell use. The Wand can be found east of the starting point at the [[Vision Henge]].

==Water Bottle==
[[File:ZA WaterBottle.png|left|30px]]
The '''Water Bottle''' is created from the [[#Empty Bottle|Empty Bottle]] when Zelda fills it with water from the River Source Pond. When returned to Glebb the thirsty woman, she will give Zelda the [[#Vial of Winds|Vial of Winds]].
