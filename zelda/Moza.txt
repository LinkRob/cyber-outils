{{Term/Store|Moza||Series, BotW}} is a reoccurring character in ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|the Legend of Zelda]]'' series. 

==Biography==

=== ''Breath of the Wild'' ===
{{Term|BotW|Moza}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who can be found near {{Term|BotW|Ishto Soh Shrine|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, where she is surrounded by {{Plural|BotW|Garbage Dump|link}}. She carries a {{Term|BotW|Pot Lid|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Soup Ladle|link}}, the latter of which she swings at the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} in front of her. Inside her {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}} is a burnt piece of {{Term|BotW|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Moza}}, she will be confused and ask who he is.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Who are you?!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} expresses the same confusion, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} introduces herself and explains she is training in isolation to complete her "ultimate dish."<ref>{{Cite|[Who are YOU?] Me? My name is {{Color|BotW Blue|Moza}}. I'm trying to perfect my ultimate dish, so I'm training in isolation right now.|Moza|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to ask about her smoke signal, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} insists that he is mistaken and that she is in the middle of {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Your smoke signal?] This is NOT a smoke signal! As you can plainly see, I'm in the middle of cooking!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> Upon being informed that it is burnt, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} admits that she has failed.<ref>{{Cite|[It's burning. Badly.] I'm burning it?! So then...you're saying I've messed it up. I see.|Moza|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Moza}} sighs and confesses that whenever she tries to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cook}}, she never ends up with edible {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}, but she does not know why her "ultimate recipe" is failing.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* For some reason, no matter how hard I try to cook something wonderful, it always turns out badly. I thought up this ultimate recipe myself, too. Why is this happening?!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Moza}} to teach him a recipe, she will be excited by the prospect.<ref>{{Cite|[Teach me a recipe.] Oh, I would love to! I don't mind at all. I'm sure you'll be able to cook something really tasty. I mean...probably.|Moza|BotW}}</ref> She then describes her three recipes to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and asks which he wants to learn.<ref>{{Cite|I have recipes for a lavish meat dish, an ancient meat dish, and an ultimate survival dish. Which will you learn?|Moza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Do you want to hear another recipe?|Moza|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Moza}}'s first recipe is her "lavish meat dish."<ref>{{Cite|[Lavish meat dish] Sure thing! I'll happily teach you the recipe for my lavish meat dish.|Moza|BotW}}</ref> The first step is to prepare the meat, and once this is done, add {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}.<ref>{{Cite|First, prepare the meat you want to use. I recommend either bird meat or steak. Then you toss in some niiiice freeesh... {{Big|ORE!!}}|Moza|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} calls this recipe gross, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} claims that {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} meat with shiny {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} is the only way to create a lavish dish.<ref>{{Cite|[That sounds gross.] Hey! That's not nice! Cooking delicious meat with shiny ore is the only way! How else would one create a truly lavish meat dish, silly?!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Moza}}'s attempts always result in {{Term|BotW|Rock-Hard Food|link}}, though she is unsure where the rockiness comes from.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, what I always end up making is {{Color|BotW Blue|rock-hard food}}... How did it even get that rocky anyway?|Moza|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Moza}}'s second recipe is her "ancient meat dish."<ref>{{Cite|[Ancient meat dish] Sure thing! I'll happily teach you the recipe for my ancient meat dish.|Moza|BotW}}</ref> After preparing the meat, she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to add an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Material|link}} from a broken {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|First, prepare the meat you want to use. I recommend either bird meat or steak. And then you'll cook it with something from a broken Guardian... {{Big|AN ANCIENT PART!!}}|Moza|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} calls the recipe gross, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} retorts that the only way to make an ancient meat dish is to include an ingredient with historical value.<ref>{{Cite|[That sounds gross.] Hey! That's not nice! Cooking delicious meat with something that smacks of history is the only way! How else would one create an ancient meat dish the likes of which no one has ever seen before?!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> While she always ends up with {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} does not see how her recipe could be dubious.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, what I always end up making is {{Color|BotW Blue|dubious food}}... Though I don't see what's so dubious about it!|Moza|BotW}}</ref>

Finally, {{Term|BotW|Moza}}'s last recipe is her "ultimate survival dish."<ref>{{Cite|[Ultimate survival dish] Sure thing! I'll happily teach you the recipe for my ultimate survival dish.|Moza|BotW}}</ref> After defeating a {{Term|BotW|Monster|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cook}} with the {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} he has harvested.<ref>{{Cite|First, you'll have to defeat a monster!! After that, just find yourself a cooking pot and toss in... {{Big|tons of monster parts!!}}|Moza|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} calls the recipe gross, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} insists that he needs to be able to eat anything so he can survive in the wild, and this sentiment was the inspiration for her dish.<ref>{{Cite|[That sounds gross.] {{Big|In order to survive out in the wild, you've got to be willing to eat anything! That's the idea behind my ultimate survival dish!!}}|Moza|BotW}}</ref> While the result is always {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food}}, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} believes this is a sacrifice she needs to make in order to achieve her goal.<ref>{{Cite|I may end up with nothing to eat but {{Color|BotW Blue|dubious food}}...but I won't achieve my goal unless I make some sacrifices!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> She is unable to see what is dubious about {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}}.<ref>{{Cite|I don't see what's so dubious about using monster parts, though...|Moza|BotW}}</ref>

After explaining a recipe, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} states that {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} is complicated, since she messes up her recipes even when her ingredients are perfect.<ref>{{Cite|Even when I use ingredients I'm certain will be perfect, there are lots of times I still mess it up. Cooking is complicated.|Moza|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to listen to her recipes, {{Term|BotW|Moza}} claims that they are special.<ref>{{Cite|[Never mind.] Oh, that's too bad. It was a pretty special recipe, too...|Moza|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to speak to {{Term|BotW|Moza}}, she believes he has come back to hear about her recipes.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, it's you! You don't have to say a word. You're back to hear the recipes I came up with again, aren't you?|Moza|BotW}}</ref>

==== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ====
Moza now reappears as the owner of Moza's Hideaway Restaurant, located at the '''[[The Rikoka Hills Well|Rikoka Hills Well]]'''. She will fix your [[Dubious Food|Failed Dishes]] and/or [[Rock-Hard Food|Rock-hard food]] with just 10 [[Rupee|Rupees]] from [[Cooking]]. <ref><q>''"Ah! A customer! Can't get enough of the ultimate dish? I have a special announcement for customers like you. Moza's Hideaway Restaurant is opening its doors to the public! Bring me your failed dishes anytime, and I'll make them ultimate! Please visit Moza's Hideaway Restaurant whenever you need that ultimate flavor!" -'' Moza [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|(Tears of the Kingdom)]]</q></ref>
