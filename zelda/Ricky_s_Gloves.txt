{{Term/Store|Ricky's Gloves|Ricky's Gloves|Series, OoS|plural}} are a recurring item in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref> Ricky's Gloves are a set of boxing gloves belonging to the kangaroo [[Ricky]]. After finding them in either of the games, Ricky would let [[Link]] ride in his pouch while he hopped over small gaps and cliffs.<ref>{{Cite|I can also jump small holes or cliffs.|Ricky|OoS}}</ref> Ricky could also punch enemies with his gloves, and even release a small whirlwind when the punch was charged up.<ref>{{Cite|Press A to punch, or save power to unleash a tornado.|Ricky|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, Ricky lost his gloves to the Boxing Champion [[Blaino]] when he lost a boxing match against him.<ref>{{Cite|A guy named Blaino took them when I lost a match to him.|Ricky|OoS}}</ref> Link challenges Blaino to a match at his gym in [[North Horon]] for 10 [[Rupee]]s,<ref>{{Cite|I'm da legendary champ, Blaino! You want somt'in'? Den earn it in da ring!|Blaino|OoS}}</ref> in order to win back Ricky's Gloves. No [[Magic Ring]]s or items were allowed during the fight.<ref>{{Cite|And no items! If you break da rules once, da next match is for 20 Rupees. And no rings!|Blaino|OoS}}</ref> The point was to knock Blaino out of the ring while punching.<ref>{{Cite|Lissen up! We trade punches 'til one of us gets knocked outta da ring.|Blaino|OoS}}</ref> After winning, Link gets the gloves to give to Ricky (they can't be used by Link). Once Link gives Ricky his gloves, he'll befriend him and allow him to ride in his pouch. If Link has not already obtained [[Dimitri]] or [[Moosh]]'s [[Strange Flute|Flute]], Ricky will give Link his Flute, which allows him to summon Ricky whenever he is needed.<ref>{{Cite|Here,Link, take this flute. If you need me, just play the flute. I'll try to find you.|Ricky|OoS}}</ref>

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, Ricky's Gloves were found buried near a tree on a beach south of [[Lynna City]]. After digging them up with the [[Shovel]] and returning them to Ricky, he would agree to help Link reach [[Tingle]] to receive the [[Sea Chart#Related Charts|Island Chart]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey! My gloves! You found them! Thanks! Now I'll help you find what you're looking for! Hop into my pocket!|Ricky|OoA}}</ref>
