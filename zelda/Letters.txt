The {{Term/Store|Letters||Series, TP, TPHD, ST}} screen is a recurring menu in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Letters|Collection|TPHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Letters|Letters|ST}}</ref> In both {{TP}} and {{ST}}, it is a submenu of the {{Term|Series|Collection Screen|link}}. 

==Overview==
The {{Term||Letters}} menu displays a list of all the {{Plural||Letter|link}} that the {{Term||Postman|link}} has {{Term||Mail Delivery|display=delivered|link}} to {{Term||Link|link}} so far. Whenever they see fit, the player can select a {{Term||Letter}} to read it.<ref name="Postman">{{Cite|To read a letter, press {{Icon|TPHD D-Pad Down}} to open your {{Color|TPHD Red|Collection screen}}, then select the {{Color|TPHD Red|letter}}. You can do that to read any letters you receive. Do it whenever you see fit!|Postman|TP}}</ref> The menu uses icons of closed and open envelopes to distinguish which {{Plural||Letter}} have been read and which have not.{{Note|In the original {{TP|-}}, there is only a closed-envelope icon. Previously read letters are distinguished by the absence of an icon.}}

The menu paginates all letters, as most are too long for the screen. The list of letters itself becomes long enough to require scrolling as well.

Actions on the menu typically trigger a sound effect of shuffling paper. 

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP|-}}, the {{Term||Postman}} himself explains the interface mechanisms for reading a letter.<ref name="Postman"/> The menu indicates who the {{Term|TPHD|Letter}} is from. The letter itself may or may not be signed. 

In the original version of the game, the layout of the menu consists of a single-pane for the list of {{Plural||Letter}}. The selected {{Term||Letter}} appears as a modal. In {{TPHD}}, the menu has a two-column layout. The navigable list of letters is shown on the left, and the {{Term||Letter}} under the cursor appears on the right.{{Note|Naturally, the interface designs for the menu bear some resemblance to those of email applications in the real world.}}

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST|-}}, the way to read {{Plural|ST|Letter}} is explained in the text that appears when {{Term|ST|Link}} obtains one.<ref>{{Cite|You got a letter from the postman! Go to the Collection screen to read it!|N/A|ST}}</ref> The menu does not have a field indicating the sender, but in this game all {{Plural|ST|Letter}} are signed anyway. The layout is the same as in {{TP|-}}—a single pane with the selected {{Term|ST|Letter}} appearing as a modal. Some {{Plural|ST|Letter}} enclose {{Plural|ST|Map|link}} or {{Plural|ST|Picture|link}} which appear on the top screen. The {{Term|ST|Rail Map|link}} can be opened on the {{Term|ST|Touch Screen|link}} while reading almost any {{Term|ST|Letter|link}},{{Note|The first {{Term|ST|Letter}}, {{Term|ST|From the Postmaster|link}}, has no such option as it is simply a notice with no bearing on locations in the game. The same is true for the {{Plural|ST|Battle Rank Notice|link}}.{{Verify|ST}} The {{Term|ST|Letter}} {{Term|ST|From Princess Zelda|link}} only displays an option for opening the {{Term|ST|Map}} of {{Term|ST|Hyrule Castle|link}} specifically, as {{Term|ST|Link}} has not yet obtained the {{Term|ST|Rail Map}} at that point.}} allowing the player to mark what they see on the top screen.<ref>{{Cite|I have a great favor that I must ask of you in person. Follow this secret path at the top of the castle. {{Color|ST Green|Tap}} the {{Color|ST Light Red|map icon}} to copy the location to your map.|From Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref>
