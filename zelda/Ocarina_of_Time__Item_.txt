The {{Term/Store|Ocarina of Time||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Fact}} It is a magical wind [[Instruments of the Legend of Zelda Series|instrument]] [[Link]] uses to play [[music]]. It is a {{wp|Ocarina#Types|transverse ocarina}} (also known as a "sweet potato ocarina"). Unique from other [[Ocarina|ocarinas]], it plays an essential role in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|game of the same name]] and its sequel, ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''. As its name suggests, this instrument has the ability to control [[time]] and [[Warping|warp]] the main protagonist to different locations. It is also known for its metallic blue color.

The Ocarina of Time's origins are unknown, since its history is only briefly spoken of in ''Ocarina of Time''. [[Princess Zelda]] mentions that the sacred instrument has been secretly passed down for generations within the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]], kept safe from those who would exploit its power to cause others harm.<ref>{{Cite|And another thing you need...is the treasure that the Royal Family keeps along with this legend... The Ocarina of Time!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ganondorf's target was one of the keys to the Sacred Realm...the hidden treasure of the Royal Family... The Ocarina of Time!|Impa|OoT}}</ref> As such, it is a treasured heirloom of the Royal Family and will only be given to the one who will open the [[Door of Time]] to retrieve the [[Master Sword]]. Full of endless mysterious powers, the Ocarina of Time proves to be a [[magic]] instrument that serves many hidden purposes which only a selected few can truly uncover. However, it is likely Zelda herself is its owner.

==Uses==
===Playing the Ocarina of Time===
[[File:Ootpitches.png|thumb|right|250px|Chart showing the Ocarina of Time's range of notes]]
In gameplay, the Ocarina of Time is designed to take advantage of the [[Nintendo 64]]'s [[Controller Buttons|controller buttons]], and can be equipped to any of the three available C-Buttons. By pressing the C-Button that it is set to, the A and all four C Buttons can be used to produce a different note. The Control Stick as well as the Z and R Buttons can be used to change the pitch of the notes, with the exception of tilting the Control Stick left or right, which causes Link to use {{wp|vibrato}} on the Ocarina, which has no specific usage in-game.

The image to the right demonstrates the Ocarina's one-and-a-half octave range of notes. The black arrows indicate the direction the Control Stick needs to be pushed while pressing the corresponding button. For example, for the low B, the A and Z buttons must be pressed simultaneously while pushing the Control Stick down. As for the high F, the Up-C button must be pressed while holding down the R button and pushing the Control Stick up.

In the {{OoT3D}} and {{MM3D}} remakes for the [[Nintendo 3DS]], the Ocarina is played with the L, R, A, X, and Y buttons, and is permanently assigned to a button on the bottom-left corner of the touch screen, eliminating the need to assign it to one of the other buttons. When playing the Ocarina, visuals of the 3DS's buttons also appear on the touch screen, and light up as said buttons are pressed. A sub-menu detailing the songs Link has learned can also be brought up, allowing him to see the notes he wishes to play.

===Changing the Pitches===
The instructions to change the pitches of the Ocarina of Time notes are as follows:

*'''R-Button'''<sup>[[Nintendo 64|N64]]</sup>/'''D-Pad Up'''<sup>[[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]]</sup> – makes the corresponding note '''sharp''', meaning it turns the note one half step higher in pitch. 
*'''Z-Button'''<sup>[[Nintendo 64|N64]]</sup>/'''D-Pad Down'''<sup>[[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]]</sup> – makes the corresponding note '''flat''', meaning it turns the note one half step lower in pitch.
*'''Tilting the Control Stick/Circle Pad Up''' – makes the corresponding note one full step higher, meaning Link will play the next note in the scale.
*'''Tilting the Control Stick/Circle Pad Down''' – makes the corresponding note one full step lower, meaning Link will play the next lower note on the scale.
*'''Sideways movement of the Control Stick/Circle Pad''' – adds vibrato to notes. Link must move the Control Stick/Circle Pad left or right to hear the tremolo effect.

Despite the image shown here, the Ocarina is in fact capable of playing all chromatic notes between the low B and high F, meaning that notes like C-sharp and E-flat can be played with the correct combination of button presses. For example, a C-sharp can be played with the button combination of 'up-C' and 'Z'<sup>N64</sup> or 'A' and down on the D-Pad<sup>3DS</sup>.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
The titular Ocarina of Time is first introduced when [[Link]] meets [[Princess Zelda]] in the [[Castle Courtyard]] in {{OoT}}. After the young hero shows her the [[Spiritual Stones|Spiritual Stone]] of the Forest, the [[Kokiri's Emerald]], the young princess entrusts Link with the [[Sheikah/Sheikah Legends|secret tale]] of the [[Sacred Realm]] that has been passed down for generations by the Royal Family.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to tell you the secret of the Sacred Realm that has been passed down by the Royal Family of Hyrule. Please keep this a secret from everyone...|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Princess Zelda tells how the only way the Sacred Realm can be accessed in [[Hyrule]] is by entering through the [[Temple of Time]]; however, its entrance is sealed by a stone wall called the [[Door of Time]] that can only be opened by collecting the three Spiritual Stones and playing the "[[Song of Time]]" on the mystical instrument known as the Ocarina of Time.<ref>{{Cite|That's right... The Temple of Time is the entrance through which you can enter the Sacred Realm from our world. But the entrance is sealed with a stone wall called the Door of Time. And, in order to open the door, it is said that you need to collect three Spiritual Stones. And another thing you need...is the treasure that the Royal Family keeps along with this legend... The Ocarina of Time!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Princess Zelda goes on to tell how [[Ganondorf]], the King of the [[Gerudo]], is trying to reach the Sacred Realm and retrieve the [[Triforce]] for himself from within and use it to conquer the entire world.<ref>{{Cite|I told my father about my dream... However, he didn't believe it was a prophecy... But...I can sense that man's evil intentions! What Ganondorf is after must be nothing less than the Triforce of the Sacred Realm. He must have come to Hyrule to obtain it! And, he wants to conquer Hyrule... no, the entire world! Link...now, we are the only ones who can protect Hyrule! Please!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> As such, the princess promises to protect the Ocarina of Time with all her ability while Link collects the two remaining Spiritual Stones in order to obtain the Triforce before Ganondorf does and defeat him with its power.<ref>{{Cite|We must not let Ganondorf get the Triforce! I will protect the Ocarina of Time with all my power! He shall not have it! You go find the other two Spiritual Stones! Let's get the Triforce before Ganondorf does, and then defeat him!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref>

Once Link gathers all three of the Spiritual Stones, he returns to [[Hyrule Castle]] just at the right time to witness Princess Zelda fleeing from Ganondorf's pursuit by horseback (ironically like his nightmare at the beginning of the game) with her [[Sheikah]] bodyguard [[Impa]]. Zelda herself notices Link dodging from the running white [[horse]] and quickly throws the Ocarina of Time toward him in a desperate attempt, which falls into a moat near the bridge. After a brief, failed confrontation against the King of the Gerudo, Link retrieves the Ocarina from the water, causing him to instantly have a vision of Princess Zelda in the Temple of Time.<ref>{{Cite|You found the Ocarina of Time! This is the Royal Family's hidden treasure which Zelda left behind. It glows with a mystical light...|N/A|OoT}}</ref> Zelda manages to send a telepathic message to the young hero informing him that she could not wait any longer for his return and leaves him with the magical melody known as the "Song of Time", which opens the Door of Time.<ref>{{Cite|Link...Can you hear me? It's me, Zelda... Link, when you hold this Ocarina in your hand... I won't be around anymore... I wanted to wait for you, but I couldn't delay any longer... At least I could leave you the Ocarina and this melody... This song opens the Door of Time... Now, Link. Play this melody in front of the altar in the Temple of Time. You must protect the Triforce!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> With the Ocarina of Time and the Song of Time in hand, Link opens the path to the Sacred Realm and then claims the [[Master Sword]] sitting on its [[Pedestal of the Master Sword|pedestal]], unwittingly allowing Ganondorf to sneak in and touch the Triforce, which ultimately causes it to break into [[Triforce of Power|three]] [[Triforce of Wisdom|separate]] [[Triforce of Courage|pieces]] and turning the Sacred Realm into the [[Dark World|Evil Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|Geh heh heh! Excellent work! As I thought, you held the keys to the Door of Time! You have led me to the gates of the Sacred Realm... Yes, I owe it all to you, kid!|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref>

After Link awakens as an adult and the proclaimed [[Hero of Time]] seven years later, the Ocarina of Time becomes an even more crucial item in the game. Link uses it mainly to easily [[Warping|Warp]] to different selected areas by playing special melodies taught to him by the mysterious [[Sheik]]. These songs serve as quick shortcuts to accessing the temples, and two of the songs, the "[[Nocturne of Shadow]]" and the "[[Requiem of Spirit]]", are necessary to enter their respective temples. Moreover, just like the [[Fairy Ocarina]] earlier in the game, the Ocarina of Time is used extensively to solve an abundant amount of [[Puzzle|puzzles]], such as raising or lowering the water levels of the {{Term|OoT3D|Water Temple|link}}, drying up the [[Kakariko Village]] [[Bottom of the Well|well]], and even gaining access to [[Zora's Domain]]. 
[[File:OOT Link sent to the past by Zelda.png|thumb|284x284px|Princess Zelda using the Ocarina of Time to send Link to his childhood at the end of ''Ocarina of Time'']]
The Ocarina of Time also helps Link regain the 7 years he lost as a cause of drawing the Master Sword from its pedestal. At the end of the game, after [[Ganon]] has been ultimately defeated and Link and Princess Zelda are talking in the sky with Hyrule now in peace, the princess asks that the hero gives back the Ocarina to her. By using Zelda's powers as a [[Sage]], she can permanently return Link to his original time with the help of the Ocarina of Time (and likely the Triforce of Wisdom).<ref>{{Cite|Link, give the Ocarina to me... As a Sage, I can return you to your original time with it.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> The sacred instrument is last used in the game as the princess plays "[[Zelda's Lullaby]]" rather than the "Song of Time" to send the Hero of Time back to his childhood. Unknowingly, she had created [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|new history]] doing so, as this simple but powerful action by the princess had split the present timeline into two different branches, the Child and Adult Timelines, with the game's universe is the latter while Link is heading the the alternate one (a third branch known as the Downfall Timeline exists but this is only possible if Link [[Game Over|dies]] from Ganon and therefore has no use by the Ocarina of Time).

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM Zelda Memory.png|thumb|293x293px|The Ocarina of Time being given to Link by Princess Zelda in a flashback scene as seen in ''Majora's Mask'']]
In {{MM}}, just after the end of ''Ocarina of Time'', before Link departs the land of [[Hyrule]], Princess Zelda gives the Ocarina of Time to Link as a memento of the time they spent together in [[Hyrule Castle]].<ref>{{Cite|You are already leaving this land of Hyrule, aren't you? Even though it was only a short time, I feel like I've known you forever. I'll never forget the days we spent together in Hyrule... And I believe in my heart that a day will come when I shall meet you again... Until that day comes, please... Take this...|Princess Zelda|MM}}</ref> She also teaches Link the "Song of Time", a melody that holds a special meaning to her,<ref>{{Cite|This reminds me of us...|Princess Zelda|MM}}</ref> before handing over the Ocarina, mentioning that he should play that melody if something were to happen to him.<ref>{{Cite|I am praying... I am praying that your journey be a safe one. If something should happen to you, remember this song...|Princess Zelda|MM}}</ref> With this said, Link leaves Hyrule along with [[Epona]] to embark on a journey in search of [[Navi]], his invaluable friend.<ref>{{Cite|Done with the battles he once waged across time, he embarked on a journey. A secret and personal journey... A journey in search of a beloved and invaluable friend...|N/A|MM}}</ref>

During Link's long search, he is ambushed by the [[Skull Kid#Majora's Mask|Skull Kid]] and his two [[Fairy]] companions, [[Tatl]] and [[Tael]]. The Skull Kid takes the opportunity that Link is knocked unconscious to search through his belongings and comes across the Ocarina of Time. Tael, enamored by the instrument's appearance, wants to play it for himself but Tatl scolds him.<ref>{{Cite|Ooh, ooh! What a pretty ocarina... Hey, Skull Kid, lemme touch it! I wanna see!|Tael|MM}}</ref> The young Hero of Time wakes up and catches the little imp red-handed, but just as Link is about to grab back his Ocarina, the Skull Kid jumps on Epona and rides away with Link himself desperately holding onto his horse but soon fails. A series of strange events happen; Link is transformed into a [[Deku Link|Deku Scrub]] by the power of the evil {{MM|Object}} as he stumbles into the parallel world of Hyrule, [[Termina]]. Once Link and Tatl reach the [[Clock Tower]], they encounter a mysterious man known as the [[Happy Mask Salesman]] who knows of a way to return Link back to his original form: if Link can retrieve the Ocarina of Time, he will return him to normal.<ref>{{Cite|Now don't think me rude, but I have been following you... ...For I know of a way to return you to your former self. If you can get back the precious item that was stolen from you, I will return you to normal.|Happy Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> For the next [[Time|72 hours]], Link and Tatl try to find a way to encounter the Skull Kid again and get back the Ocarina from him while the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] above them threatens to [[Terminan Apocalypse|crash]] into [[Clock Town]].

On the eve of the [[Carnival of Time]], the young hero and his Fairy companion face off the Skull Kid atop the Clock Tower. Link shoots a [[Bubble Blast]] to knock the Ocarina of Time out of the imp's hands, and just as Link retrieves it, he has a flashback of him and Zelda just before leaving Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Ocarina of Time back! Princess Zelda gave you this precious instrument. [...] ...Suddenly, memories of Princess Zelda come rushing back to you...|N/A|MM}}</ref> Link remembers the "Song of Time" that the princess taught him, which he uses to return to the dawn of the [[First Day]] before the Moon crashes onto Termina.

Just like in its predecessor, the Ocarina of Time plays a prominent role in {{MM|-}} and functions the same as before during gameplay. Link must periodically use the Ocarina and the "Song of Time" to reset the land's three-day cycle and relive the same three days until the [[Four Giants]] are freed from their curse, so that they can stop the Moon from destroying Termina. The sacred instrument is also used to solve various puzzles in the game, such as awakening [[Captain Keeta]], recovering [[Lulu]]'s voice, and healing the many tormented souls to turn them into [[Mask]]s.

The Ocarina of Time takes on unique different forms depending on the [[Transformation#Majora's Mask|transformation]] mask that Link is wearing at the time. As Deku Link, the Ocarina takes the shape of Pipes known as the [[Deku Pipes]], [[Goron Link]] uses the [[Goron Drums]], and [[Zora Link]] plays the [[Zora Guitar]]. All of these instruments have the same power as the Ocarina of Time; however, some songs will only have the desired effect by playing with a certain instrument.

==Trivia==
*The Ocarina of Time is arguably the most widely recognized [[Instruments of the Legend of Zelda Series|instrument]] in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]. Due to its significant impact in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|game of the same name]] as well as ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', the popularity and sales of real-life ocarinas have increased since.
*It is implied in {{HH}} that the Ocarina of Time, as well as the [[Master Sword]], may be made of the same time-bending material as [[Timeshift Stones]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= [The Ocarina of Time's] blue-white radiance brings to mind the Timeshift Stones found in the eras of antiquity.|book=HH|page= 86}}</ref>
*During development of ''Ocarina of Time'', the [[Spiritual Stone|Spiritual Stones]] would be inserted inside the Ocarina of Time in order to enter the [[Temple of Time]].<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= Just as the subtitle of the game suggests, this is important for going to the "Tower of Time." If you go inside, Ocarina must have the three soul stones inserted.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= http://www.ign.com/articles/1997/12/02/the-z-files-3|title= The Z-Files}}</ref>
*The main theme song for ''Ocarina of Time'' on the title screen is the titular Ocarina playing an extended version of the [[Flute|Recorder]]'s melody heard in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]''.
*The Ocarina of Time has the [[Triforce]] emblem on its mouthpiece.[[File:ALttP Flute Artwork.png|thumb|The so-called Flute in ''A Link to the Past'' strongly resembles the Ocarina of Time]]
*The international version of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' has an item known as the "[[Flute]]", which is used by [[Link]] to easily reach other places in Hyrule with the help of the [[Duck]] and is also played by the [[Flute Boy]]. However, this is likely a mistranslation, as explicitly shown in official artwork and the in-game sprite, said instrument is actually an ocarina, nearly identical to the Ocarina of Time. In fact, the original Japanese version even names it and the corresponding character "Ocarina" and "Ocarina Boy", respectively. This same item also makes an appearance in the Japan-only ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda]]'' and ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|Ancient Stone Tablets]]'' games. It's likely the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] title's usage of an ocarina is a precursor for the Ocarina of Time in the [[Nintendo 64]] installments.
*Upon receiving the new ocarina, if [[Saria]] is spoken to immediately, through [[Saria's Song]], she will note that the Ocarina of Time has a slightly different sound compared to the [[Fairy Ocarina]] (despite they do sound identical in gameplay).<ref>{{Cite|What? Your ocarina sounds...different somehow... Have you been practicing a lot, Link?|Saria|OoT}}</ref>
*Due to its appearance, some players claimed the Ocarina of Time somewhat resembles a submarine when looked from certain angles.
*Although the Ocarina of Time has seven holes, some real-world replicas of the magical instrument have only six or as many as twelve holes.
*Several musical instruments in later games pay homage to the Ocarina of Time, such as the [[Wind Waker]] or [[Spirit Flute]], from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks|Spirit Tracks]]'', respectively.[[File:SSB Link playing Ocarina of Time.png|thumb|242x242px|Link playing the Ocarina of Time, as seen in ''Super Smash Bros.'']]
*In the original ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' game of the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], the [[Hero of Time]] can be briefly seen playing the Ocarina of Time at a nighttime [[Hyrule Field]] before the screen zooms to his face in the opening sequence, a reference to ''Ocarina of Time''.
*In ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', although it does not play a major role, the Ocarina of Time is used by Link to make him travel between [[Hyrule]] and the ''[https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_series Soul]'' fighting game series' universe. He can be seen playing it in his ending screen on Arcade Mode.
