The {{Term/Store|Infusion Shop||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}} and {{SSHD}}.<ref>{{Cite|Infusion Shop|Map|SSHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|SSHD|Infusion Shop}} is a small business run by {{Term|SSHD|Bertie|link}} in the northern portion of the {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft Bazaar|link}}, run in joint with the {{Term|SSHD|Potion Shop|link}} that {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}}'s wife, {{Term|SSHD|Luv|link}}, runs. At the {{Term|SSHD|Infusion Shop}}, {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} can take any {{Plural|SSHD|Potion|link}} that he has bought and can enhance their abilities by infusing them with certain {{Plural|SSHD|Insect|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah...hello... Welcome to my humble little side business over here. I, uh...infuse the potions my wife sells with new ingredients to increase their power. It's nothing, really...just something I like to do.|Bertie|SSHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you want a potion powered up, go see my husband at the end of the counter. He can mix something up for you.|Luv|SSHD}}</ref> All types of {{Plural|SSHD|Potion}} can be infused at least once, and two specific {{Plural|SSHD|Potion}}, {{Plural|SSHD|Heart Potion|link}} and {{Plural|SSHD|Revitalizing Potion|link}}, can be infused twice.

{{Term|SSHD|Bertie}} runs the {{Term|SSHD|Infusion Shop}} while carrying his infant daughter on his back. When {{Term|SSHD|Link}} first enters the {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft Bazaar}}, {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}} is trying to cheer up his baby who is crying, to no avail. After {{Term|SSHD|Link}} obtains the {{Plural|SSHD|Clawshot|link}}, {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}} tells him that he hasn't been able to get much sleep during the night, but then apologizes for complaining to a customer.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry-I shouldn't be sighing in front of a customer... You see, recently I haven't been able to sleep {{Color|SSHD Red|at night}}... Ahhh... Sorry, I also shouldn't be complaining in front of a customer... Again...|Bertie|SSHD}}</ref> If {{Term|SSHD|Link}} goes to his house at night, {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}} can be found trying to get his baby to fall asleep, but is unable to since he lost the baby's {{Term|SSHD|Rattle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The baby just goes on and on like this all night. I haven't slept a wink in... Ahhh... I don't even know how long. The baby usually sleeps like a sack of rocks. But that was before we lost the you know what...|Bertie|SSHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The baby's {{Color|SSHD Gold|Rattle}}. If we had the you know what, then the baby would settle down. But we lost it the other day. {{Color|SSHD Red|A bird picked it up}} in its beak and {{Color|SSHD Red|flew off with it}}. Maybe the bird thought it was food. I don't know...|Bertie|SSHD}}</ref> If {{Term|SSHD|Link}} finds the {{Term|SSHD|Rattle}} and brings it to {{Term|SSHD|Bertie}}, he will be rewarded with five {{Plural|SSHD|Gratitude Crystal|link}} and the baby will stop crying during the day at the {{Term|SSHD|Infusion Shop}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whew. The baby seems to have settled down nicely. I bet the baby will sleep well tonight. Oh! That means I'll finally get a good night's sleep too! Thank you so much! I won't forget what you did for me.|Bertie|SSHD}}</ref>
