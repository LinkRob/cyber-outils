Ultimate Weapon is a [[Boss]] from {{TotK}}.

==Overview==
The [[Yiga Clan]] has been restoring the Ultimate Weapon, a powerful [[Crafter Construct]], using Crystallized Charges to gift it to Demon King [[Ganondorf]].

In the [[Master Kohga of the Yiga Clan]] Side Adventure, [[Master Kohga]] uses it against [[Link]] but is defeated. Enraged, he summons the Kohga Rocket for revenge—only to launch himself out of the [[Rito Village]] Chasm instead.
