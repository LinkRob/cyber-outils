{{Term/Store|Eldin Spring||Series, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref name="Praise">{{Cite|In praise of your efforts thus far, I will release fairies to {{Color|TPHD Red|Eldin Spring}}.|Great Fairy|TPHD}}</ref> It is situated in the front entrance of [[Kakariko Village]]. It is inhabited by the third [[Spirits of Light|Light Spirit]], [[Eldin (Spirit)|Eldin]], the protector of the [[Eldin Province]].

==Features and Overview==
When [[Link]] as a [[Wolf Link|wolf]] first arrives in Kakariko Village, Eldin will beckon him to the {{Term|TPHD|Spring (Location)|link}} and ask him to defeat the [[Shadow Insect]]s in the area to obtain the [[Tears of Light]] from them and thus restore light to the Eldin Province. After Eldin recovers its powers, the Spirit of Light will instruct Link to head to [[Death Mountain]] and retrieve the [[Fused Shadow]] from the [[Goron Mines]].<ref>{{Cite|My name is Eldin. I am one of the light spirits of Hyrule. I am the spirit that guards these lands. O great hero chosen by the gods... The dark power you seek lies in the sacred grounds of the proud mountain dwellers. But already those grounds have been defiled, draped in shadow and seeded with evil. You must go to those sacred grounds and cleanse them.|Eldin (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> 

Like the rest of the [[Spirit Spring]]s, Link can walk into the spring to have his health slowly recovered merely by stepping into its water. In the [[Cave of Ordeals]], when Link reaches the 30th floor, the [[Great Fairy]] will reward the young hero by releasing recovery [[Fairy|Fairies]] into the Eldin Spring.<ref name="Praise"/> Reaching the last floor of the cave will allow Link to talk to the Great Fairy at the spring and receive unlimited [[Great Fairy's Tears]], provided that he does not already have a [[Bottle]] full of them.<ref>{{Cite|If ever you need my assistance again, visit any spring.|Great Fairy|TP}}</ref>

By bombing rocks near the spring, Link can enter a small cave that leads to the back portion of the Eldin Spring. The young hero can then use the [[Iron Boots]] to dive to the bottom and open a [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Piece of Heart]].
