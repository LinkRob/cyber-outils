{{Term/Store|Old Man Ho Ho||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a man who can be found in many islands of the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Old Man Ho Ho is usually seen staring through his [[Telescope]] looking at places of interest where treasure or special items can be found.<ref>{{Cite|Ho ho! My, that battery of mounted guns is a dangerous thing. If only someone could blow the entire lot of them to bits! It is such a shame that they exist, for there is something wonderful hidden there!|Old Man Ho Ho|TWW}}</ref> When spoken to, he also gives subtle hints to what or where the place is that he is staring at and what can be found there.<ref>{{Cite|Ho ho! One! One flying seagull! No! Two! Two flying seagulls! Wait! I see more seagulls! Three! Four! Five! Now six! No! No! Seven seagulls! Eight! This is madness! Eight seagulls...|Old Man Ho Ho|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ho ho! To think that over there... There would be such a container...|Old Man Ho Ho|TWW}}</ref> Sometimes he is even looking through a [[Telescope#Kaleidoscope|kaleidoscope]].<ref>{{Cite|Ho ho! So beautiful! Incredibly beautiful! I'm so glad I bought this kaleidoscope!|Old Man Ho Ho|TWW}}</ref>

While he knows where to find many of the Great Sea's treasures, perhaps the most impressive is his apparent knowledge concerning the [[Triforce Chart]] in the [[Savage Labyrinth]].<ref>{{Cite|Ho ho! So close! If only I could fly to the top of that hill! If only! Beneath that rock head... The Trifrmphm... Oh! I nearly spoke aloud without thinking. It is a secret. Yes. Quite a secret...|Old Man Ho Ho|TWW}}</ref>

Though he does not appear in {{PH}}, members of the [[Ho Ho Tribe]] appear, hailing from the land of [[Wayaway]].
