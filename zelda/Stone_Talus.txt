{{Term/Store|Stone Talus|Stone Taluses|Series, BotW, CoH}} are recurring enemies and {{Plural|Series|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Stone Talus}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Stone Talus|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TotK}}

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Stone Talus}}
{{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus}} appear as a cluster of boulders at first, but once approached, they assemble into large golem-like beings. {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus (Luminous)|link|display= Luminous}}, {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus (Rare)|link|display= Rare}}, {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus|link|display= Frost}}, and {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus|link|display= Igneo}} variations can also be found all around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus}} are fairly large compared and deal important amounts of damage.{{How Many}} They are very slow-moving, so as long as {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} stays behind or beneath them, he will be out of harm's way. {{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus}} can use their large arm structures to punch and swipe across the ground, dealing heavy damage to the impact area. They can also fall forward and crash their entire body into the ground or launch large fragments of their rocky frame at {{Term|BotW|Link}}.

Stone Taluses have three configurations:
*Low-elevation Taluses have an ore deposit on their back. It's easily accessed if you strafe the Talus and get it to spin but very hard if you're on the Talus when it gets up. Example: Deya Village Ruins Talus 
*Mid elevation Taluses have their ore deposits on their far left. To reach it, you can attack the Talus by using a spear while they're reforming their left shoulder.
*High elevation Taluses have the ore deposit on their right shoulder, not as low as the low-elevation ones, but still not that high. it's impossible to reach without climbing the Talus. Example: Great Plateau Talus

To defeat a {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can climb onto its body/time a ground-based strike correctly and attack the {{Term|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} on its back, usually located on top or at the back of the {{Term|BotW|Sub-Boss|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can inflict double damage using a two-handed {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, or four times as much damage using an {{Term|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer|link}}. After taking damage, {{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus}} attempt to throw {{Term|BotW|Link}} off by shaking themselves. To make climbing easier, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} to blast apart its arms, hindering it temporarily. {{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus}} regenerate lost body parts shortly after losing them. If you can't climb onto it, stun it with a normal arrow, shooting the {{Term|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} on its back, making it fall on its face. If you are new to Taluses, we recommend trying the Stone Talus Senior or the Stone Talus Junior first.

Upon being defeated, a {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus}} leaves behind {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}} depending of its type. By defeating all Stone Taluses in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, as well as all other types of Talus, Link can receive the [[Medal of Honor: Talus]] from [[Kilton]]. This can only be done after completing the "[[Destroy Ganon]]" [[Main Quest]].

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Plural|TotK|Stone Talus}} appear as {{Plural|TotK|Enemy}} in {{TotK}}, acting the same as in {{BotW}}, but can drop [[Stone Talus Heart]]s, which can be [[Fuse|fused]] with Link's current weapon, in addition to their usual drops. In addition, a new variant, [[Battle Talus]], makes its debut, being modified Stone Taluses to function as a mobile {{Term|TotK|Monster Stronghold|link}} replete with chevaux de frise on the undersides of their wooden frames to prevent intruders from climbing.

A Stone Talus titled "Lurker in the Lost Woods" can be found with its [[Ore Deposit]] replaced with a [[Shrine of Light|Shrine]] crystal, which allows [[Link]] access to [[Sakunbomar Shrine]].

==={{EoW|-}}===
The [[Tweelus]], Small Rock like monsters can be Encountered at Eldin Volcano.

==Other Appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Plural|CoH|Stone Talus}} are two tiles wide and move every third beat. They have 3 {{Plural|CoH|Heart Container|link}}, but must be hit multiple times to crack them, allowing their {{Plural|CoH|Heart|link}} to be reduced, unless the character's {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}} has piercing. If the character is next to the {{Term|CoH|Stone Talus}} the beat before it moves, it will use an area attack, hitting all eight adjacent tiles. If the {{Term|CoH|Stone Talus}} is not hit for a while, it will sit still and become immune to attacks while it reforms itself, regaining lost {{Plural|CoH|Heart}}.
