The {{Term/Store|Magical Key|Magical Keys|Series, TLoZ, TAoL}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 383 {{Exp Game|TLoZ}} & 386 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref> It allows [[Link]] to unlock any [[Locked Door]].<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= A magical key that can open several of the locked doors.|game= TAoL|page= 46}}</ref> Unlike a normal [[Key]], it is reusable, meaning that regular Keys are rendered useless once obtained.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, the Magical Key can be found in {{Term|TLoZ|Level 8|link}}. It is golden and has a [[Wikipedia:Key (lock)|bow]] shaped like the head of a lion. Once obtained, the Key count is replaced with the letter "A",<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Once he's got this magical key, "A" will be displayed showing he's unstoppable!|game= TLoZ|page= 22}}</ref> which stands for "almighty."<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= When you have it, the display will show an "A" (for "Almighty").|game= TLoZ|page= }}</ref>

==={{TAoL|-}}===
In {{TAoL}}, the Magical Key is found after using the [[Magic of The Adventure of Link#Spell|Spell]] at the edge of Hidden [[Kasuto]].<ref>{{Cite|THERE IS A SECRET AT EDGE OF TOWN.|Old Woman|TAoL}}</ref> It is also gold-colored, but the lead shape resembles a {{wp|coat of arms}}.
