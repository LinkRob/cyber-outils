==Location and Uses==
==={{FS|-}}===
A {{Term|FS|Golden Key}} from the {{Plural|FS|Great Fairy|link}} of {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Forest|link|display=Forest}}, {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Ice|link|display=Ice}}, and {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Flame|link|display=Flame}} are required to enter the golden door of {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If all of you collect the three {{Color|FS Orange|Golden Keys}} of the Great Fairies of Forest, Ice, and Flame, the path to Vaati's Palace shall open.|Great Fairy|FS}}</ref>

They are obtained after first finishing the {{Term|FS|Silver Key|link}} door of {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace}}, then completing the {{Term|FS|Sea of Trees|link}}, {{Term|FS|Talus Cave|link}}, and {{Term|FS|Death Mountain|link}} {{Plural|FS|Stage|link}} each with a minimum of 3000 {{Plural|FS|Rupee|link}}.<ref name= "Golden Key"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{CN|-}}===
The Golden Key is an item in the "[[Quest for the Potion of Power]]" episode of {{CN}}. Found inside the [[Parapa Palace]], the Golden Key is initially encased in glass along with a parchment when [[Link]], [[Princess Zelda]], and Kevin arrive. When Link shatters the glass with his Sword to obtain the Key and the parchment, [[Horsehead]] appears in the room and reveals to be the guardian of the Golden Key.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Who dares to steal my Golden Key?|name= Horsehead|show= Captain N: The Game Master|episode= Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref> Kevin helps Link defeat Horsehead, allowing them to read the parchment and find out that the Golden Key is used to unlock the Golden Door in the [[Island Palace]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= The Golden Key to the Golden Door leads across to the island shore.|name= Link|show= Captain N: The Game Master|episode= Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref>

Once Link, Princess Zelda, and Kevin reach the Golden Door in the Island Palace, Link uses the Golden Key on the door to open it, which reveals the chamber where the [[Potion of Power]] is located.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= See? I found it. The Golden Door.|name= Link|show= Captain N: The Game Master|episode= Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}
