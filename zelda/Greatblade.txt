{{Term/Store|Greatblade||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Greatblade}} is the third youngest member of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}. He lives in a waterfall cave on the western side of {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}}. He will teach {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to prolong the duration of his {{Term|TMC|Great Spin Attack|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yes! All right, I'll teach you how to prolong the duration of the {{Color|TMC Red|Great Spin Attack}}, which you have already learned!|Greatblade|TMC}}</ref>

His dojo can only be accessed once {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Waveblade|link}} and can swim to it using the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Greatblade}} will not train {{Term|TMC|Link}} until he learns the {{Term|TMC|Great Spin Attack}} from {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade I|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dude... I don't think you're even ready to train here! Come back when you have learned the {{Color|TMC Red|highest technique}} of the sword!|Greatblade|TMC}}</ref>
