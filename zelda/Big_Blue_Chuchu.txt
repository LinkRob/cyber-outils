The {{Term/Store|Big Blue Chuchu||Series, TMC}} is a {{Term|TMC|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 166}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu}}
The {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu}} is fought inside the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, the fourth {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} in {{TMC}}. It is fought in a similar manner as the {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu|link}}. The base of the {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu}} is electrified and it resists the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}'s vacuum effect. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must avoid its attacks and wait for the electricity to fade before he is able to use the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar}} to stun it.

After defeating the {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} will be rewarded with the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}}, allowing him to proceed further into the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}.

==Trivia==
*Despite it being a {{Term|TMC|Sub-Boss|link}}, the battle with the {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu}} uses the background music normally used with {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} {{Plural|TMC|Boss|link}}. This may be because the {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu}} is modeled after the {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu|link}}, or because unlike the other {{Plural|TMC|Sub-Boss}}, it is a unique enemy. Its {{Term|TMC|Figurine|link}} is also found in the portion of the list reserved for {{Plural|TMC|Boss}}.
