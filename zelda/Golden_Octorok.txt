{{Term/Store|Golden Octorok|Golden Octoroks|Series, OoS, TMC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 195 {{Exp Game|OoS, TMC}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{OoS|-}}===
The {{Term|OoS|Golden Octorok}}, along with other {{Plural|OoS|Golden Monster|link}}, appears around various regions of {{Term|OoS|Holodrum|link}} in {{OoS}} after talking to a golden-robed {{Term|OoS|Old Man|link}}. He can be found in the the cave near the {{Term|OoS|Gnarled Root Dungeon|link}} while it is {{Term|OoS|Summer|link}} and the water around it has receded. The {{Term|OoS|Golden Octorok}} can be found in {{Term|OoS|Spool Swamp|link}} while it is {{Term|OoS|Summer}}. After defeating it, along with the other {{Plural|OoS|Golden Monster}}, {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} will receive a {{Term|OoS|Magical Ring|link}} from the {{Term|OoS|Old Man}} which, when appraised by {{Term|OoS|Vasu|link}}, becomes the {{Term|OoS|Red Ring|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 17}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
Up to three {{Plural|TMC|Golden Octorok}} will appear, as well as the other {{Plural|TMC|Golden Monster|link}}, after certain {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} in {{TMC}}. Like the other {{Plural|TMC|Golden Monster}}, {{Plural|TMC|Golden Octorok}} move much faster, take much more damage to defeat, and deal much more damage to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} than standard {{Plural|TMC|Octorok|link}}. They can be found in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}. When defeated, they drop a large sum of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.
