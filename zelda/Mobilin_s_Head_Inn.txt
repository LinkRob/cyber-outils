The '''Mobilin's Head Inn''', also known as the '''Mobilin Inn''', is a lodge house located in the {{Term|ZA|Plain of Andor}} of {{Term|ZA|Tolemac|link}} in {{ZA}}.

== Features and Overview ==
The {{Term|ZA|Mobilin's Head Inn}} is a small lodge house located in eastern {{Term|ZA|Plain of Andor}}, next to its river. It is owned by a man named Lothar, a friendly man who welcomes {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda|link}} to his establishment. The lodge mainly serves as a stop for weary travelers looking to rest. A brew called Andor cider is known to be served there.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the Mobilin's Head Inn, my princess! You look dry, have a cup of Andor cider on the house.|Lothar|ZA}}</ref>

Outside the inn, a Beggar can be found asking for {{Plural|ZA|Rupee|link}} to feed her children, after the family had been banished from {{Term|ZA|Great Wimbich|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, fair princess! Pity a suffering creature exiled from Great Wimbich in the north! I ask only enough for a single meal for my children who hunger and are chilled by the wind.|Beggar|ZA}}</ref> Zelda can give her 2 {{Plural|ZA|Rupee}} to receive the Firestorm {{Term|ZA|Spells of Zelda's Adventure|display=Spell|link}}. Inside, Zelda can purchase the Calm Spell from Lothar for 100 {{Plural|ZA|Rupee}}, and can receive the Dagger Spell from Krebb the weary man after speaking with him persistently. Another weary traveler inside the inn gives a hint on crossing the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Earth|link}}'s chasm using a {{Term|ZA|Items in Zelda's Adventure|section=Ladder|display=Ladder|link}}, and a woman gives Zelda a riddle on navigating the shrine with a {{Term|ZA|Items in Zelda's Adventure|section=Candle|display=Candle|link}} and a key, despite the shrine having no locks.
