==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Molduga Guts can be obtained by defeating {{Plural|BotW|Molduga|link}}, which can be found under the sand in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. They can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] along with critters to make [[Elixir]]s. In addition, they can be sold to [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] in exchange for [[Mon]] or used as an ingredient for Green [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

When spoken to, [[Malena]] explains to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that her husband is sick and the only cure is Molduga Guts.<ref>{{Cite|But then...my husband fell ill with a rare disease... One difficult to cure.|Malena|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The only thing that can save him is the {{Color|BotW Blue|innards of a Molduga}}...|Malena|BotW}}</ref> After, Link recieves the [[Side Quest]] "[[Medicinal Molduga]]". Upon delivery of Molduga Guts, Malena will reward Link with a [[Gold Rupee]].

The [[Radiant Set]] requires Molduga Guts to be upgraded to its third tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. They're also needed to upgrade the [[Sand Boots]] to their third and fourth tiers.

* Total for '''Sand Boots''': 6 (0, 0, 2, 4)
