The '''Triforce of Wisdom'''<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= TCT|publisher= |page= 2}}</ref> is one of the three pieces of the [[Triforce]]. It embodies the essence of the Goddess of Wisdom, [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]], the author of law.<ref>{{Cite|Nayru, the goddess of wisdom... [...] Poured her wisdom onto the earth and gave the spirit of law to the world.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> It imparts divine wisdom upon its holder, and grants the holder untold mystical abilities, including the ability to heal others<ref name=heal/> and, presumably, the ability to communicate telepathically. The Triforce of Wisdom leads its holder to make the right decisions,<ref>{{Cite|It is time for you to choose: surrender or die. Oh yes, a question for all the land and people of Hyrule... Life? Or death?!|Zant|TP}}</ref> making them wiser than any mortal.<ref name="ALttP Manual 3"/> [[Princess Zelda]] is usually the Triforce of Wisdom's bearer. It represents the lower-left piece of the Triforce. This part of the Triforce was one of the two pieces originally introduced in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', alongside the [[Triforce of Power]] (the [[Triforce of Courage]] would debut in its [[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|sequel]]).

==Mainline appearances==

=== ''The Legend of Zelda'' ===
[[File:TLoZ Link Holding the Triforce of Wisdom Artwork.png|thumb|195x195px|Link holding the restored Triforce of Wisdom, from {{TLoZ|-}}]]
In the original ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' where it plays an important role, as explained in the game's instruction manual and intro, Princess Zelda purposely shatters the Triforce of Wisdom into [[Triforce Shard|eight fragments]] and hides them throughout [[Hyrule]], fearing [[Ganon]]'s rule after he steals its counterpart, the [[Triforce of Power]].<ref>{{Cite|LONG AGO, {{Color|TLoZ Red|GANON}}, PRINCE OF DARKNESS, STOLE THE TRIFORCE OF POWER. {{Color|TLoZ Blue|PRINCESS ZELDA}} OF HYRULE BROKE THE TRIFORCE OF WISDOM INTO EIGHT PIECES AND HID THEM FROM {{Color|TLoZ Red|GANON}} BEFORE SHE WAS KIDNAPPED BY {{Color|TLoZ Red|GANON'S}} MINIONS. {{Color|TLoZ Green|LINK}}, YOU MUST FIND THE PIECES AND SAVE {{Color|TLoZ Blue|ZELDA}}.|N/A|TLoZ}}</ref> [[Link]] must fully reassemble the Triforce of Wisdom if he hopes to enter [[Death Mountain]] ([[Level 9|Level-9]]), destroy Ganon once and for all and rescue the princess of the kingdom in order to save the land. 

In-game, all shards of the sacred triangle are found after defeating the [[Bosses in The Legend of Zelda|bosses]] of the first eight [[Dungeons in The Legend of Zelda|dungeons]] (and also fully restores Link's [[Life Gauge]]). For Level-9, an [[Old Man]] blocks the entrance and cannot allow Link to enter until he acquires the complete Triforce of Wisdom. During the final battle, Link uses the powerful [[light]] of the restored Triforce piece to expose Ganon from the pitch-black darkness of his lair before his fight against the Demon King, in which he also takes the Triforce of Power for himself after ultimately eliminating him with the [[Silver Arrow]]. Upon saving Princess Zelda immediately after, she and the young hero use the two [[Triforce]] parts together to restore Hyrule back to its former glory.

=== ''The Adventure of Link'' ===
In ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'', after defeating his [[Link's Shadow|shadow self]] in the [[Great Palace]] and obtaining the [[Triforce of Courage]], Link unites the previous two pieces he obtained in the game's prequel six years prior, with it to fully unite the Triforce and wake up Princess Zelda from her deep magical slumber. 

=== ''A Link to the Past'' ===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', the Triforce of Wisdom can be seen alongside its Power and Courage counterparts on the title screen and credits. In the events of the game, Ganon used the compete Triforce to transform the [[Sacred Realm|Golden Land]] into the [[Dark World]], and Link would also use it to fully restore Hyrule back to its peaceful state after ultimately destroying his archenemy. Therefore, like the other sacred triangles in ''The Adventure of Link'', the Triforce of Wisdom was never harnessed or used by itself in ''A Link to the Past''.
=== ''Ocarina of Time'' ===
[[File:Triforce of Wisdom in OoT.webp|thumb|283x283px|The Triforce of Wisdom glowing on Princess Zelda's hand, as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', the Triforce of Wisdom returns to play an essential role since ''The Legend of Zelda''. When the Triforce was split into its three aspects by [[Ganondorf]] claiming the Triforce in the [[Sacred Realm]] shortly after Link pulls the [[Master Sword]] from its pedestal in the [[Temple of Time]], Princess Zelda was imparted with the Triforce of Wisdom, giving her the essence of the [[Golden Goddess|Goddess]] [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]], while Ganondorf and Link respectively obtain the Triforce of Power and Triforce of Courage to receive their own blessings from [[Din (Goddess)|Din]] and [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]].<ref name="wisdom" /> This essence seemingly allowed her to disguise herself as a male [[Sheikah]] named [[Sheik]], for 7 years, until the eventual return of the [[Hero of Time]]; The [[Mark of the Triforce|crest]] of the Triforce of Wisdom on the back of Sheik's hand shines before revealing his true identity to the hero as Princess Zelda. Her possession of the Triforce of Wisdom also makes Zelda leader of the [[Sage|Six Sages]], and she is also one, herself, as the seventh, indicating her importance of giving Link the [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]] and sealing Ganon away into the [[Dark World|Evil Realm]] upon his brutal defeat by the main protagonist. The Triforce's power also allows the princess to permanently send the Hero of Time back to his original time at the end of the game, creating [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|new history]] doing so.<ref name="ocarina">{{Cite|Link, give the Ocarina to me... As a Sage, I can return you to your original time with it.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref>

=== ''The Wind Waker'' ===
[[File:WW Triforce of Wisdom Shard.png|left|thumb|234x234px|The large half of the Triforce of Wisdom on King Daphnes' hand in ''The Wind Waker'']]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', before the events of the game, specifically the [[Great Flood]], the Triforce of Wisdom was split in two. Zelda, in her unwitting disguise as the pirate [[Tetra]], carries a large part of this piece of the Triforce in the form of a pendant that was given to her by [[Tetra's Mother|her mother]] before she passed away. The remaining fragment is then given to her by the last [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|King of Hyrule]], thus revealing her true identity as the Princess of Hyrule when restored (it's likely the Triforce's influence also changed Tetra's demeanor entirely).<ref name="necklace">{{Cite|This necklace you wear is part of a sacred treasure called the Triforce of Wisdom, which has been passed down for many ages within the royal family of Hyrule. Your mother passed this down to you and instructed you to treasure it...and to guard it with your life. Am I not correct?|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> It is explained by [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]] that the Triforce of Wisdom was given to Tetra's ancestors with the task of protecting it from Ganondorf's grasp.<ref name="ancestor">{{Cite|The Triforce of Wisdom is none other than the sacred power of the gods that we have kept from Ganon's clutches for so many long years. The gods placed upon your ancestors the task of protecting it from evil's grasp.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> It was first seen when Link, the Hero of Winds, attempted to strike down Ganondorf with the powerless Master Sword and Tetra tried to save him from being killed by the evil warlock, which his Triforce of Power resonated upon seeing the "pendant". Despite possessing the Triforce of Wisdom, Princess Zelda herself had virtually no use for it in this game, unlike Link and Ganondorf with their respective pieces, as well as some other titles of the franchise. In the finale, however, Ganondorf extracted all of the three sacred pieces to reform the complete Triforce in an attempt to make a wish, but was ultimately thwarted by King Daphnes.

In this installment, the Triforce of Courage was also broken into multiple pieces much like the Triforce of Wisdom itself, coincidentally in eight fragments like how Zelda's piece was in ''The Legend of Zelda'', where it is required for Link to restore it alongside the [[Master Sword]]'s [[Power to Repel Evil]] to earn his title as the Hero of Winds and defeat Ganondorf.

=== ''Twilight Princess'' ===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', when [[Zant]] transformed the kingdom of Hyrule into a world covered by [[Twilight]], the Triforce of Wisdom seemingly protects Princess Zelda from becoming a spirit in the world of Twilight, much like how Link, the Hero of Twilight, was protected by his Triforce of Courage.<ref name=wolf>{{Cite|In the land covered in twilight, where people roam as spirits, you were transformed into a blue-eyed beast... That was a sign... It was a sign that the powers of the chosen one rest within you...and that they are awakening.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> Later on in the game, Princess Zelda transfers her powers to the dying [[Midna]] in order to save her, thus allowing the [[Twili]] imp to be able to withstand light.<ref name=heal/>

=== ''A Link Between Worlds'' ===
[[File:ALBW Hilda Holding Triforce of Wisdom.jpg|thumb|237x237px|Princess Hilda holding the Triforce of Wisdom in ''A Link Between Worlds'']]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', during the finale, [[Princess Hilda]], Princess Zelda's [[Lorule]] counterpart, steals the Triforce of Wisdom from Zelda herself and summons [[Yuga]]. When Yuga, after he [[Yuga Ganon|fuses with Ganon]] betrays Hilda and absorbs her, he takes the Triforce of Wisdom for himself, combining it with the Triforce of Power after usurping Ganon's control, during the final battle.

=== ''Echoes of Wisdom'' ===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]'', Princess Zelda, similar to in ''Ocarina of Time'', ended up getting the Triforce of Wisdom after [[Null]]'s attempt of using the Triforce (called Prime Energy in-game) to bring Hyrule into the void via an echo of Zelda failed. Although Null deduced Princess Zelda's holding of the Triforce of Wisdom, it ignored her for the time being due to intending to grab Link's Triforce of Courage first.

== Trivia ==

* The Triforce of Wisdom is the piece of the [[Triforce]] broken up into fragments the most times in the franchise, as seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]''. It is also the one stolen by antagonists the most as well.[[File:OOT Link sent to the past by Zelda.png|thumb|301x301px|Despite possessing the Triforce of Wisdom, the split of the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Zelda Timeline]] at the ending of ''Ocarina of Time'' by Princess Zelda is seen as one of the most unwise actions in the franchise by fans]]
* Although the Triforce of Wisdom indeed grants divine wisdom due to the blessings of the [[Golden Goddess]] [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]], the piece itself does not appear to affect one's own intelligence. In the case of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', Princess Zelda, despite being its bearer and as a [[Sage]], displays a character flaw multiple times throughout the game's story. Her desperate decision of protecting the [[Sacred Realm]] through [[Link]] failed during their childhood, she left herself completely exposed to [[Ganondorf]] upon shedding her [[Sheik]] disguise for the hero when the princess knows she should be hiding, and, most notably, when Zelda sends the [[Hero of Time]] back to the past at the ending, she was not aware of the longtime negative effects for Link and [[Hyrule]] when the princess split the timeline, consequently making his legacy not being remembered in the Child Timeline and later become the restless [[Hero's Spirit]], as well as ultimately leaving the Adult counterpart without a Hero when [[Ganon]] eventually returns by the events of ''The Wind Waker'' and the kingdom ultimately being [[Great Flood|flooded]].
* The scene of the Triforce of Wisdom's two shards being put together in front of Tetra in ''The Wind Waker'' contains a reference to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', as it plays a similar arrangement of the music heard on the title screen, and in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] title the three component parts of the [[Triforce]] indeed reform into the full golden relic.
* Link fully reassembling the Triforce of Wisdom in the original ''The'' ''Legend of Zelda'' is the first instance in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] where the the three main characters obtain a part of the Triforce that normally belongs to another. Although by the release of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game in 1986, the [[Triforce of Courage]] was not yet in existence until its sequel, ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'' in the following year, and was not the hero's standard piece until the [[Nintendo 64]]'s ''Ocarina of Time'' in 1998.[[File:TLoZ Inventory.png|thumb|227x227px|The inventory screen of ''The Legend of Zelda'' (with [[100% Completion|100% completion]]) showing the restored Triforce of Wisdom, where its named "Triforce" in-game]]
* Despite being technically the Triforce of Wisdom, the sacred triangle in ''The Legend of Zelda'' is referred to as simply "'''Triforce'''".
* The number of fragments of the Triforce of Wisdom being split in ''The Legend of Zelda'' was reused for the Triforce of Courage's [[Triforce Shard|Shards]] in ''The Wind Waker'' - eight.
* In [[The Legend of Zelda (television series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' TV series]], like its [[Triforce of Power|Power]] counterpart, the Triforce of Wisdom has the ability to speak, something which does not directly occur in the games.
* In the Japan-only [[Satellaview|Satalleview]] title ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda]]'', it plays a very similar role as in the original ''The Legend of Zelda'', but during the final battle against Ganon, it's instead used to open the locked door to the main antagonist's hideout in the [[cave]] where the [[Player|BS-X avatar]] obtains their [[Sword]].
