{{Term/Store|Crab Risotto|Crab Risottos|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Crab Risotto}}<br/>An everyday staple of seaside villages, the secret to its delicious flavor lies in crab fat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Crab Risottos are made in {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} using [[Hylian Rice]], [[Goat Butter]], [[Rock Salt]], and any type of {{Term|BotW|Crab|link}}. Regular Crab Risotto can only be made by either using two different types of Crabs or by using a fifth ingredient with a contradictory effect. Eating one will restore six {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, but they can also be sold for 70 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. They're known to be a staple of [[Lurelin Village|seaside villages]], and the secret to their delicious flavor comes from crab fat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Due to the three different types of Crabs available, there are three variations of Crab Risotto:

* '''Energizing Crab Risotto''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Crab Risotto}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>An everyday staple of seaside villages, the secret to its delicious flavor lies in crab fat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using a {{Term|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}. When eaten, they'll refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/> They also heal four of his Hearts, but they sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Crab Risotto''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Crab Risotto}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>An everyday staple of seaside villages, the secret to its delicious flavor lies in crab fat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using a {{Term|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}. If eaten, Link's attacks will be more powerful.<ref name="Mighty"/> They'll also heal seven Hearts in his Life Gauge, and they're worth 40 Rupees when sold.

* '''Tough Crab Risotto''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Crab Risotto}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>An everyday staple of seaside villages, the secret to its delicious flavor lies in crab fat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using an {{Term|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}. Consuming one will temporarily boost Link's defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/> In addition, they heal four Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.
