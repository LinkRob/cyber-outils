{{Term/Store|Harbinger Ganon||Series, HWAoC}} is a character and one of the main antagonists in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Biography==
{{Improve|November 2020}}
One hundred years into the future of the Great Calamity-wracked Hyrule, [[Calamity Ganon]] sent parts of its [[Malice]] through the [[Gate of Time]] created by [[Terrako]] to prevent any changes to its awakening. By possessing the inactive Terrako of 100 years in the past, it became the harbinger of Calamity Ganon and was soon allied with [[Astor]] after devouring his fellow cultists. Using what power it has, it uses its Malice to possess a [[Guardian Stalker]] to attack [[Zelda]] on her way to the [[Ancient Tech Lab]] and reveal Calamity Ganon's awakening in the future to Astor and the [[Yiga]]. 

It leads an army of monsters to attack [[Rito Village]] which is defended by the soon-to-become [[Champions]] [[Revali]] and his [[Rito]] brethren, thus creating paranoia among the Rito to attack [[Link]] when they were to see Terrako with him. It has not been confirmed whether this was an intentional false flag attack or a simple attempted invasion, although it is worth noting this is the only battle this early into the Great Calamity in which [[Calamity Ganon]] was sighted. [[Revali]] is notoriously reluctant to join the alliance and expresses repeated distaste for Link, which may have marked them as a good target for underhanded tactics such as this.

Harbinger Ganon spends most of the events of the [[Great Calamity]] orchestrating matters from behind the scenes: while it is heavily intertwined with Astor and his role in the war, it rarely seems to accompany him out in the open. 

The [[Yiga]] are ultimately betrayed by Astor at the behest of Harbinger Ganon, their life force being used to empower it for the final encounter at [[Hyrule Castle]]. This may explain Harbinger Ganon's sporadically active role throughout the conflict, as manifesting a form suitable for combat appears to require a vast sum of energy. 

Harbinger Ganon reveals itself to Zelda and her allies floating beside Astor before they enter the Malice-filled [[Hyrule Castle]], opening up its body to draw out its Guardian Weapons and claws to intimidate them. Zelda in particular noticed its similarities to Terrako<ref>{{Cite|But that looks like&ndash; |Zelda|HWAOC}}</ref> and realizes that Harbinger Ganon must have been responsible for causing the Rito to become paranoid and hostile to her group.<ref name="multiple"/> It awaits inside a gatehouse of the castle, hanging upside-down in a pile of Malice on the ceiling, engaging Zelda and her forces as soon as they arrive.

After being defeated along with Astor, just before Astor could call out a command to destroy the heroes, Harbinger Ganon consumes the prophet for his power, using it to transform itself into its true form; Calamity Ganon.

The ''Guardian of Remembrance'' DLC shows more of Harbinger Ganon's story, that starting after it possessed the Terrako of the past, it went searching for the Terrako from the future. It ambushed the Guardian in the Coliseum, absorbing a [[White-Maned Lynel]] and later two [[Black Moblin|Black Moblins]] to strengthen itself. It was seemingly defeated by Terrako, but quickly transformed into its [[Mutated Ganon]] form, burying Terrako underneath some rubble and deactivating it, where it was later found by 2 [[Bokoblin|Bokoblins]]. 

Later, it once again ambushed Terrako at the Coliseum, this time assuming its Mutated Ganon form immediately. It bested the Guardian once again, but thanks to the activation of an Ancient Furnace by [[Purah]] and [[Robbie]], Terrako was powered up and was able to defeat Mutated Ganon, causing it to return to its original form as it was carried off by Yiga Clan members. One of these two battles may explain Ganon's seemingly damaged state at the point in time where it shows Astor a vision of the ruined post-Great Calamity Hyrule in front of [[Sooga]] and [[Master Kohga|Kohga]].
