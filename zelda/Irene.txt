{{Term/Store|Irene||Series, ALBW}} is one of the {{Plural|ALBW|Sage|link|display=Seven Sages}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300}}</ref> She is the apprentice of the {{Term|ALBW|Witch|link}} who lives near {{Term|ALBW|Zora's Domain|link}}. 

==Biography==
{{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} meets {{Term|ALBW|Irene}} early in his adventure. She offers to transport him to any {{Term|ALBW|Weather Vane|link}} he has activated, and gives him a {{Term|ALBW|Bell|link}} that he can ring at any time to call her. This is because she received an alarming fortune from the {{Term|ALBW|Fortune-Teller|link}} in which she is told to "take care of green" in order to avoid the worst outcome.{{Fact}} Though she acts grumpy the entire time,{{Fact}} she eventually comes to see {{Term|ALBW|Link}} as a friend.<ref name="kidnap"/>

{{Term|ALBW|Irene}} is attacked by {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} and turned into a {{Term|ALBW|Painting|link}} while at the {{Term|ALBW|Witch's Hut|link}}, sometime after {{Term|ALBW|Link}} has collected both the {{Term|ALBW|Pendant of Power|link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Pendant of Wisdom|link}}. When {{Term|ALBW|Link}} visits the {{Term|ALBW|Witch's Hut}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Witch}} tells him how she was powerless to stop {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} from taking {{Term|ALBW|Irene}}, mentioning that she had been telling her that she'd made a new friend recently.<ref name="kidnap"/>

The {{Term|ALBW|Painting}} of {{Term|ALBW|Irene}} appears in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle|link}} when {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} is using the power of the {{Plural|ALBW|Sage|display=Seven Sages}} to revive {{Term|ALBW|Ganon|link}}. After {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} merges with {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Painting}} is sent to {{Term|ALBW|Misery Mire|link}}, but can only be accessed after traversing the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} rescues {{Term|ALBW|Irene}} after defeating {{Term|ALBW|Zaganaga|link}}. Inside the [[Temple of Light#Chamber of Sages|Chamber of Sages]], she yells at {{Term|ALBW|Link}} for taking so long, but quickly forgives him and urges him to rescue the other {{Plural|ALBW|Sage}}.<ref>{{Cite|H-hey! You came to rescue me? Well, I...I don't know what to say, Link! But, uh, HEY! My fortune came true after all! you took your sweet time getting here, didn't you?! I was in big trouble! And I've got to get back to my gram! That's all right, I forgive you. Just don't take that long to rescue me next time. And don't make any of the other Sages wait that long either. C'mon, get to saving the rest!|Irene|ALBW}}</ref> She also tells the young hero that she'll write him a long thank-you letter once he has rescued all of the {{Plural|ALBW|Sage}}.<ref>{{Cite|What...? Want more thanks? Tell you what, Link. Save all seven of us Sages, then I'll write a big, long thank-you letter. But for now...? Just be careful, OK?|Irene|ALBW}}</ref> {{Term|ALBW|Irene}} remains in the Chamber of Sages until the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce|link}} is returned to {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}, at which point, she returns to the {{Term|ALBW|Witch's Hut}} to continue her training. Even after being rescued, {{Term|ALBW|Irene}} continues to speak to {{Term|ALBW|Link}} whenever he uses the {{Term|ALBW|Bell}}.<ref>{{Cite|All right, Irene's Taxi here. That'll be 9,999 Rupees. Nah, just messing with your head. I couldn't take money from you. So buckle up.|Irene|ALBW}}</ref>
