{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear}} are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} inside "A Minor Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, and they are dropped upon their defeat.<ref name="Inventory"/> They also have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn when the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. When brandished, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear}} produce a glowing blue spearhead.<ref name="Inventory"/> The spearhead disappears when resting upon {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s back. They have a high piercing power due to being made with {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> 

Being made of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear}} deal extra damage to {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}. {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear}} are not magnetic, so they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, nor can they be attracted by the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. They are also not flammable, which means they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. When dropped into a body of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear}} will float upon its surface.

{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear}} are part of the equipment archetype known as {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}}, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} utilized by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>
