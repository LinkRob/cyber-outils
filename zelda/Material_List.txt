The {{Term/Store|Material List||Series, TFH}} is an interface in {{TFH}}.{{Name Ref|TFH}}

==Overview==
The {{Term|TFH|Material List}} is viewed by speaking to {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's Apprentice|link}} inside {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}. It displays the icons of all the {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} owned or encountered by {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} spread over several scrolling pages on the bottom screen. Any {{Plural|TFH|Material}} presently or previously owned by {{Term|TFH|Link}} include the number of them in his possession. {{Plural|TFH|Material}} that he has seen in {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} or at the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}}, but never owned, have their icons faded. As-of-yet unknown {{Plural|TFH|Material}} are replaced by a question mark. When a {{Term|TFH|Material}} is selected on the bottom screen, the top screen displays its description and the primary location where it can be found.

{{Term|TFH|Link}} can also view the {{Term|TFH|Material List}} by pressing the "Material Hint" button while viewing the {{Term|TFH|Catalog|link}}. This version highlights any {{Plural|TFH|Material}} needed to purchase the {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} he has selected.
