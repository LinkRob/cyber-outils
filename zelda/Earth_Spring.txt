The Earth Spring is an underground spring located deep inside the Earth Temple. [[Link]] visits the spring while on his search for [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]], who is said to be cleansing herself in the waters of the spring.<ref>{{Cite|Master Link. It appears Zelda has cleansed herself in the waters of this fountain.|Fi|SS}}</ref> He encounters her at the spring and observes her playing the [[Goddess's Harp]] with a Servant of the Goddess named [[Impa]]. Impa scolds him for taking too long to reach the spring, and the two depart.<ref>{{Cite|It took you far too long to get here. Looking at you, I fear the goddess is mistaken in her choice of agents. If this failure is any indication, you have no hope of defending Her Grace from those who seek to assail her. Do my words anger you, boy? Do my words sting? Let them. If I had not come when I did, your Zelda would already have fallen into the hands of the enemy. The truth of it is you were late. You were late, and you failed to protect her. I sent Zelda ahead to learn more of the fate in which she is destined to play a part. Listen well, chosen one. If you wish to be of help to Her Grace, you must summon a shred of courage and face the trials laid out before you. Only when you've conquered the trials will you be of use to Zelda. No sooner. Am I understood?|Impa|SS}}</ref> Link then performs another [[Skyward Strike]] on a [[Goddess Crest]] allowing [[Fi]] to receive another message from "the gods of old", which it begins to translate for the young hero.<ref>{{Cite|From the edge of time I guide you, the one destined to carry out the goddess's mission. The spirit maiden who descended from the clouds has passed through the Earth Spring and makes her way to a fated place. The parched desert of Lanayru... That is where the chosen will pass through the Gate of Time into a distant world.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Upon completion, the translation reveals that Zelda is heading to a region called the [[Lanayru Desert]]. As a gift from the gods, Link receives the [[Amber Tablet]] which creates a rift in the clouds leading to Lanayru Desert.

== Trivia ==

* The Earth Spring strongly resembles the [[Spring of Power]], [[Spring of Wisdom]], and [[Spring of Courage]] in {{BotW}} which feature similar geometry, landscape, and architecture.
