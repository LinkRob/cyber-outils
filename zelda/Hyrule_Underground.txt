The {{Term/Store|Hyrule Underground||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Underground}} is a series of tunnels beneath {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. It has four entrances &ndash; one is accessed by falling down the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Well|link}}, requiring the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}; one is accessed through a cave that requires the use of the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}}; one is accessed by pushing a statue of {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho|link}} to reveal a hidden staircase in {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}}; one is accessed by a ladder in the garden of {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's House|link}}.

There are several {{Plural|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} in the area, many of them containing {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}. The area requires {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}} and the ability of {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to split himself to explore fully.
