==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Mighty Porgy}}
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find Mighty Porgies swimming in the waters of [[Necluda Sea]] and [[Lanayru Sea]]. They're also sold by the traveling merchant [[Chumin]] and at the fish market in [[Lurelin Village]] by [[Mubs]]. They can be eaten raw to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to [[Link|Link's]] {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. They become {{Plural|Series|Frozen Porgy|link}} when exposed to [[Ice]] and {{Plural|Series|Roasted Porgy|linl}} when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Meals made in a [[Cooking Pot]] with Might Porgies will temporarily increase Link's attack damage.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Either a Mighty Porgy or an [[Armored Porgy]] can be used while [[Cooking]] to make [[Porgy Meunière]] and [[Seafood Paella]]. A porgy or a [[Hearty Blueshell Snail]] is needed to make [[Seafood Curry]] and [[Seafood Fried Rice]]. Mighty Porgies are among the fish that can be used to make a [[Fish Skewer]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], and [[Salt-Grilled Fish]]. They're also among the seafood that can be used while making [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].
