{{Term/Store|Pergie||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
Pergie lives with her husband [[Jaggle]], their two children, [[Talo]] and [[Malo]],<ref>{{Cite|My boys Malo and Talo aren't giving you headaches, are they? If they are, you feel free to scold them anytime you like.|Pergie|TP}}</ref> and their [[dog]] in their {{Term|TPHD|Jaggle & Pergie's House|link|display=House}} in {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}}. She is often found in her house cleaning dishes while Jaggle is working elsewhere.<ref>{{Cite|If you're looking for my hubby, he took a break from working and went outside. I wouldn't be surprised if he's taking a nap somewhere...|Pergie|TP}}</ref> While she admits that her children are a handful, she still thinks they are a "pair of angels."<ref>{{Cite|But I suppose I should be apologizing to you for the trouble Talo and Malo caused you yesterday... They're a mischievous pair...but they'll always be a pair of angels to their father and me.|Pergie|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Tell me, how are my sons doing over there in Kakariko Village? I worry about the older one's mischievous side...and his little brother is still so young.|Pergie|TP}}</ref>
