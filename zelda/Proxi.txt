{{Term/Store|Proxi||Series, HW}} is a [[Fairy]] in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HW Blue|Proxi}}|N/A|HW}}</ref> She accompanies [[Link]] and speaks on his behalf.<ref>{{Cite|Man of few words, huh? That's okay--I'll come along and do the talking!|Proxi|HW}}</ref>

==Biography==
[[File:HWDE Proxi Mini Map Icon.png|left|link=]]
Proxi speaks on behalf of Link, hence her name. She meets Link in [[Hyrule Field (Stage)|Hyrule Field]] during "[[The Armies of Ruin]]" Scenario while the [[Dark Force]]s are invading the kingdom, and accompanies him after he saves her from a horde of [[Bokoblin]]s. Throughout the game, She offers information and encourages troops, and even expresses concern for Link when Ganondorf first defeats him after his resurrection.

In the {{MM|-}} DLC, Proxi also speaks on behalf of [[Young Link]], albeit with different lines.
