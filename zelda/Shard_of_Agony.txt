The {{Term/Store|Shard of Agony||Series, OoT3D}} is an item in {{OoT3D}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 138}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Shard of Agony}} can be obtained as a reward from one of the cursed children of the {{Term|OoT3D|House of Skulltula|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}} after collecting 20 {{Plural|OoT3D|Token|link}}. When close to a {{Term|OoT3D|Secret Grotto|link}}, it releases a pinging accompanied by a flashing icon in the top-left corner of the screen.
