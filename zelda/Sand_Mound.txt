{{Term/Store|Sand Mound|Sand Mounds|Series, TFH|plural}} are objects in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 201}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Sand Mound}} appear in various Levels of [[The Dunes]]. They can be blown away with the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}} or removed with the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}}. Sand Mounds can hide items such as {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}, or objects such as {{Plural|TFH|Switch|link}}. Moving Sand Mounds hide {{Plural|TFH|Karat Crab|link}} underneath.

Taller {{Plural|TFH|Sand Mound}} appear exclusively in {{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}, hiding Anubis-like statues that are used to unlock certain doors or to weigh down tilting platforms.
