==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
In its prominent appearance in {{MM}}, the rare Fierce Deity's Mask is one of five [[Mask|Masks]] that [[Transformation|transforms]] [[Link]] when worn. It changes him into [[Fierce Deity Link]], where the young [[Hero of Time]] has a unique, very tall and mysterious form resembling his adult self from {{OoT}} and shares the same voice, and also transforms his [[Sword]] into the [[Fierce Deity Sword]], a very large sword in the shape of a helix. In this incredibly powerful form, his attack power and range are greatly increased (roughly the same as the [[Great Fairy's Sword]], which is four times the strength of the [[Kokiri Sword]]), and his mighty weapon can release [[Sword Beam|Sword Beams]] at the cost of [[Magic]]. However, he lacks a [[Shield]], and therefore cannot properly defend against attacks. Notably, the Mask itself resembles the face of Adult Link but has white hair, war paint with the colors of the [[Hylian Shield]], and has a pale blue hat rather than green. When obtained, its own dark powers are suggested to be just as dangerous as [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]] itself.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|MM3D Red|Fierce Deity's Mask}}! Could this mask's dark powers be as bad as Majora? Tremendous power is emanating from within...|N/A|MM}}</ref> Therefore, this very Mask is known to rival the main antagonist.
[[File:MM Fierce Deity Transformation.png|thumb|261x261px|Link transforming into the infamous Fierce Deity in {{MM|-}}]]
Link can obtain the {{Term|MM3D|Fierce Deity's Mask}} by trading all of the twenty non-transformation masks (including the [[Giant's Mask]]) to the four [[Moon Child|Moon Children]] wearing the Boss Remains. Since the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Mask|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Goron Mask|link}}, and the {{Term|MM3D|Zora Mask|link}} cannot be traded, Link must have in his possession all of the other Masks he obtained throughout his adventure before being able to receive the Fierce Deity's Mask. Once this has been done, the young hero obtains the {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} from the fifth lonely child wearing Majora's Mask itself as a reward once it notices the rest of his masks are gone. He gives him the powerful {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} to the hero so that the two of them can "play" together, with Link being the "bad guy" (which is ironic, considering their actual roles are the opposite). The Fierce Deity's Mask is said to possess the merits of all {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}},<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|MM3D Red|Fierce Deity's Mask}}, a {{Color|MM3D Red|mask}} that contains the merits of all masks, seems to be... somewhere in this world...|Gossip Stone|MM3D}}</ref> as well as the memories of all the people of [[Termina]] and possibly his own.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= The best way to think about it is that the memories of all the people of Termina are inside of the Fierce Deity Mask.|name= Eiji Aonuma|url= http://nintendoeverything.com/aonuma-on-the-pain-behind-links-transformations-in-majoras-mask-deity-mask-soul/|title= Aonuma on the pain behind Link’s transformations in Majora’s Mask, Fierce Deity Mask soul}}</ref> If Link attempts to trade the Fierce Deity's Mask to the Moon Children in a subsequent appearance [[inside the Moon]], they will immediately refuse and tell the young hero to put it away, as with the other transforming masks.

Unlike the other transformation Masks, the Fierce Deity's Mask can only be used during [[Boss]] battles including Majora's Mask,<ref name="MM Inventory">{{Cite|Fierce Deity's Mask - Wear it with (C). Its dark power can be used only in boss rooms.|N/A|MM}}</ref> and, in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'', {{Plural|MM3D|Fishing Hole|link}}. However, its combat capabilities are disabled inside {{Plural|MM3D|Fishing Hole}}, and the {{Term|MM3D|Fishing Hole Men|link}} will forbid Link from leaving while wearing it.<ref>{{Cite|Hey. Hey! What's with that scary face, fella? If ya leave here lookin' like that, you'll scare away potential customers and ruin our reputation!|Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
The Fierce Deity Mask is a piece of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} in {{BotW}}. Unavailable in the Overworld, it can only be obtained by scanning the {{MM|-}} [[Link]] [[amiibo]] with the {{Term|BotW|amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. According to its description, the Fierce Deity Mask in {{BotW|-}} is the same Mask as the one worn by the [[Hero of Time|legendary hero]] in the {{Term|Series|Era of the Hero of Time|link}}, albeit significantly weakened, as it no longer retains its fearsome powers that it once had in the [[Nintendo 64]] game, similar to the Majora's Mask in-game.<ref>{{Cite|Legends say this mask was once worn by a hero from a world in which the moon threatened to fall. It looks scary, but wearing it offers fierce, godlike power.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> When worn by Link, the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Mask}} provides one level of Attack Up. Like the Majora's Mask, the eyes of the Fierce Deity Mask can glow, allowing them to be seen in dark areas such as the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Mask}} can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], though it cannot be {{Term|BotW|Dye|link}}d at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Mask}}, alongside the [[Fierce Deity Armor]] and [[Fierce Deity Boots]], compose the [[Fierce Deity Set]], which makes the Hero of the Wild incarnation appear like the Hero of Time's Fierce Deity form (with the exception of height).

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
The {{Term|HWDE|Fierce Deity's Mask}} appears as a type of {{Term|HWDE|Badge|link}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Bazaar|link}} in {{HW}}. It increases the amount of {{Term|HWDE|Special Attack|link}} meters when crafted, up to three bars.

In the {{MM|-}} DLC Pack, {{Term|HWDE|Young Link|link}}'s {{Term|HWDE|Mask|link}} {{Term|HWDE|Weapon|link}} class is modeled after the {{Term|HWDE|Fierce Deity Mask}}. While {{Term|HWDE|Young Link}} primarily uses the {{Term|HWDE|Kokiri Sword|link}} in battle, he can temporarily transform into {{Term|HWDE|Fierce Deity Link|link}} as part of his {{Term|HWDE|Focus Spirit|link}} attack with the {{Term|HWDE|Mask}}, as well briefly during his {{Term|HWDE|Special Attack|link}}. While in {{Term|HWDE|Focus Spirit}}, {{Term|HWDE|Young Link}}'s sword transforms into the {{Term|HWDE|Fierce Deity Sword|link}}, and his attack power, speed and range is heightened for the duration of the {{Term|HWDE|Focus Spirit}}. As {{Term|HWDE|Young Link}} is able to play the {{Term|HWDE|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} to empty his {{Term|HWDE|Special Attack}} meter and fill his {{Term|HWDE|Magic Meter|link}}, it is possible for {{Term|HWDE|Young Link}} to stay in his {{Term|HWDE|Focus Spirit}} for lengthy periods of time. The {{Term|HWDE|Mask}} is of the {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Darkness}} element.

The {{Term|HWDE|Mask}} has two additional weapon tiers - the {{Term|HWDE|Furious Deity Mask|link}}, which has a purple aura, and the {{Term|HWDE|Vengeful Deity Mask|link}}, which has a red aura. These different variations transforms {{Term|HWDE|Young Link}}'s sword into other swords while in {{Term|HWDE|Focus Spirit}}.

==Trivia==
*Alongside [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]], the Fierce Deity's Mask is widely considered by fans as the most notorious [[Mask]] to ever appear in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]]. In spite of its villainous appearance and abilities like the former, the item itself is popular among the gaming community because of its [[Fierce Deity Link|unusual transformation]] for Child Link into what appears to be a taller and highly powered-up version of his adult self stated to be god-like.
*Despite not actually being required to complete {{MM}}, the [[Hero of Time]] obtaining the {{Term|MM3D|Fierce Deity's Mask}} and defeating Majora as the Fierce Deity form is stated to be canon according to {{HH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The mastermind behind these events was none other than Majora's Mask. Link pursued the mask to the center of the moon. There, a strange battle unfolded. Link transformed into the Fierce Deity and eradicated the threat.|book=HH|page=112}}</ref>
**Unlike the other transformation Masks, however, due to the Fierce Deity's extreme power, some fans have discouraged using this mask during the final battle, viewing it as a cheap shot against the [[Final Boss]] - Majora, as it is capable of destroying any [[Bosses in Majora's Mask|boss]], including every form of Majora's Mask, in only a few seconds, thus taking away the challenge completely.
**Due to the requirement of obtaining every other standard mask to be traded, the Fierce Deity's Mask is very commonly seen by players who aim for a [[100% Completion|100% completion]] of {{MM|-}} or its [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|remake]], as well as the [[speedrun]] community.
*In the Japanese text of the game, the child wearing Majora's Mask specifically states that he wants to play tag with {{Term|MM3D|Link}}. This is a play on words as the Japanese text states that the {{Term|MM3D|Fierce Deity Mask}} is a mask an {{Wp|oni}} (a type of Japanese ogre) wears, and the Japanese name for tag is "pretending to be an oni".
**Majora's Mask calling this mask an oni and deliberately giving it to Link for a game of tag also occurs in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (Himekawa)|{{MM|-}} Himekawa manga adaptation]]. Although Link went with the flow of Majora's words, as Fierce Deity Link, he utterly says to the villain during the final showdown that he has no time for games, before easily destroying it.
*In the original [[Nintendo 64]] version of {{MM|-}}, when the {{Term|MM|Fierce Deity Mask}} is first obtained after trading all 20 non-transformation Masks, a programming bug prevents the use of the other transformation {{Term|MM|Mask}}s in the final battle against Majora (but not in subsequent fights).<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= EnNopp112|published= December 3, 2018|retrieved= June 24, 2019|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LMjQS2BlPoE&t=16444|title= The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 100% Speedrun in 4:35:30|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref> This was fixed in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]''.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= benstephens56|published= December 29, 2018|retrieved= June 24, 2019|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OBBIA7Ph6BU&t=19453|title= Majora's Mask 3D 100% Speedrun in 5:26:01|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref>[[File:MM Link Mask Concept.jpg|thumb|right|222x222px|The Link Mask in the beta version of {{MM|-}}]]
*Early in development and seen from beta footage of {{MM|-}}, a unique mask strongly resembling Link's face designed to transform him into his adult self from {{OoT}} was meant to appear in the game, but was later scrapped and likely replaced by the Fierce Deity's Mask. This may be due to the fact that the Hero of Time, in spite of being a child in-game, can now use the items that only Adult Link previously could in the game's prequel, and [[Zora Link]] shares the same size proportions, rendering this planned mask useless. In {{HH|-}}, the {{MM|-}} section contains concept art of Adult Link, confirming that Link as an adult was indeed originally going to appear at one point during the game's development. This is supported by Fierce Deity Link sharing Adult Link's voice with [[List of Voice Actors|Nobuyuki Hiyama]] and the fact the model was based on the hero carrying the [[Biggoron's Sword]].[[File:MM Link Mask Model.png|thumb|The model of the cut Link Mask in the game's files|203x203px]]
**Inside the game's code, a textured model of the Link {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} can be found. Aside from the originally planned transformation into his adult self, it is possible that it was also used to turn him back into his [[Hylian]] form when wearing other transformation Masks, as concept art in ''Hyrule Historia'' shows [[Deku Link]] wearing this mask. In addition, the scrapped Mask itself is similar to Link's shell after playing the "[[Elegy of Emptiness]]". It is unknown if it remains in ''Majora's Mask 3D''.
**Although the [[Unused Content|unused]] Link Mask cannot be worn by Link himself in the final game, it's possible to make the [[Skull Kid]] replace the Majora's Mask with this mask during the opening cutscene where the young hero is ambushed by him through ROM hacking. This suggests Skull Kid was once planned to wear the Link Mask as well, possibly in an effort to trick Link.
*Through a [[Glitches in Majora's Mask|glitch]], it is possible to use the Fierce Deity's Mask almost anywhere in [[Termina]] without being in boss rooms, but care must be taken not to cause the game to crash or becoming stuck upon transforming into the Fierce Deity.
*It's unknown in-game why the Fierce Deity's Mask's soul is unlike the other transformation masks, though according to the manga adaptation, this is supposed to represent a powerful god-like being that once fought Majora.
*In French localization, the Fierce Deity's Mask is referred to as "'''Mask of the Demon God'''". It is even called such in the {{MM|-}} manga.
*Due to a graphical bug, when the Fierce Deity Mask is seen on the [[Grassy Playfield]] (except Moon dungeons) or most other areas in Termina, the hat appears to be in rainbow-like colors. This is fixed in ''Majora's Mask 3D''.
