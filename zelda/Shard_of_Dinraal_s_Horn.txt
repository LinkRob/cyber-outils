==Location and Uses==
Shards of Dinraal's Horn can be collected by shooting [[Dinraal]] in the horn as it flies by. The Shard will fly off the dragon and land on the ground nearby, surrounded by light.

[[Cooking]] with Shards of Dinraal's Horn results in a meal that has a buff lasting thirty minutes. They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to create Crimson [[Dye]].

A Shard of Dinraal's Horn must be collected as part of ''[[The Champions' Ballad]]'' {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} "[[EX Champion Revali's Song]]".<ref>{{Cite|♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|shoot the flame dragon's horn}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When this quest is active, Dinraal will fly through [[Tanagar Canyon]] often during the night, which is noted by [[Mazli]].<ref>{{Cite|Come to think of it...the {{Color|BotW Blue|dragon}} always flies through this valley at night.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> Mazli also notices that Dinraal's horns have been lighting up recently.<ref>{{Cite|When it does, its {{Color|BotW Red|horns light up}}. There's something about that light that's truly captivating.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> Shooting Dinraal's horn will cause the [[Shira Gomar Shrine]] to rise up from out of the ground.

Shards of Dinraal's Horn are needed to upgrade several pieces of [[Armor]] at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Two Shards, as well as a [[Star Fragment]], are needed to upgrade the [[Trousers of the Wild]] to their fourth tier. A combination of two Shards and ten {{Plural|Series|Silent Princess|link}} are used to upgrade the [[Champion's Tunic]] to its final tier. Finally, One Shard and two [[Lynel Guts]] are needed to upgrade both the [[Barbarian Helm]] and the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Mask|link}} to their fourth tiers.
