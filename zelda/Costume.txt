{{Term/Store|Costume|Costumes|Series, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are skins that can be worn by {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Change Costume|Select Warrior|HWDE}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|HWDE|Costume}} are usually recolors of a {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}}'s standard outfit, although some {{Plural|HWDE|Costume}} feature entirely different outfits. {{Plural|HWDE|Costume}} can be equipped from the {{Term|HWDE|Select Warrior|link}} menu by pressing the X-Button, which will display all available {{Plural|HWDE|Costume}} for the selected {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}}. New {{Plural|HWDE|Costume}} can be unlocked either by completing certain requirements in {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode|link}} or {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}, usually by winning a battle with a certain rank, or through purchasing and downloading DLC Packs.

All {{Plural|HWDE|Costume}} from {{HW|-}}, DLC or otherwise, also appear in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}. The {{Plural|HWDE|Costume}} that first appeared in {{HW|L|-}}, DLC or otherwise, also appear in {{HW|DE|-}} but do not appear in {{HW|-}}. The {{Plural|HWDE|Costume}} that first appeared in {{HW|DE|-}} are exclusive to it and do not appear in {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L|-}}.
