The '''Silver Horriblin''' is an enemy in {{TotK}}.
It is the most powerful variant of the [[Horriblin]] species.

==Defeating==
To beat such a agile opponent a good way is using a wing shield to jump and go into bullet time to headshot him, and then take your chance to inflict some damage quickly.

A easier way to beat him is by throwing fire fruit at him as it will burn him causing it to fall to the floor giving you the chance of dealing damage, its easy to miss him though so take your time lining up your shot.

==Drops==
The '''Silver Horriblin''' drops the [[horriblin claw]], [[silver horriblin horn]], [[horriblin guts]], and whichever weapon its carrying.
