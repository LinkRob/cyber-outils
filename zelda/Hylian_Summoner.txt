{{Term/Store|Hylian Summoner|Hylian Summoners|Series|plural}} are [[Enemies in Hyrule Warriors|Enemies]] in {{HW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|HW|Summoners|Summons additional enemy troops onto the battlefield, often around Warriors to surround them.}}
Hylian Summoners appear only in The [[Battle of the Triforce]] (Summoners in Adventure Mode are nearly always Captains, who have better health and self defense abilities).
