==Characteristics==
Swift Phantoms can be found within the [[Temple of the Ocean King]]. They can run faster than [[Link]], as well as pick up dropped items. It first appears after Link has obtained the [[Bow]] and replaces several normal Phantoms.<ref>{{Cite|That evil monster is a... Swift Phantom!
