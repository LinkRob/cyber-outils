{{Term/Store|Glass Slippers||Series, CoH}} are items in {{CoH}}.

==Location and Uses==
Glass Slippers are a piece of equipment that allow {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} to cross hazardous terrain without issue, such as acquiring immunity to damage from poisonous water and hot coals, and preventing them from being slowed down by shallow water.<ref>{{Cite|These are very fragile but let you avoid damage or penalties from hazardous floor tiles. They also give +1 damage.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

While worn, they also boost their strength, granting their wearer more attack power. However, since they're made of glass, they immediately break upon taking damage, including damage received from falling into holes and hot lava without the {{Term|Series|Goron Locket|link}}.

They can be found either from a red {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} for 40 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}}.

==Trivia==
*Glass Slippers are similar to what was seen in the Disney animated movie ''Cinderella''.
