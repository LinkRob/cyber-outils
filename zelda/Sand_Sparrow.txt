{{Term/Store|Sand Sparrow|Sand Sparrows|Series, BotW|plural}} are [[Creature]]s in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Sand Sparrow}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Sand Sparrow}}
Sand Sparrows are a breed of small bird native to the [[Gerudo Desert]], and have adapted to the intense heat and aridity of their home.<ref>{{Cite|This desert-dwelling breed of sparrow has adapted to withstand the heat in the arid climate of the Gerudo region.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They often use the sand in their environment to avoid predators by burying themselves within it and remaining immobile to avoid notice.<ref>{{Cite|Sometimes they can be spotted half-buried in the sand, holding perfectly still as a means of evading predators.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Sand Sparrows drop {{Plural|BotW|Chickaloo Tree Nut|link}} when they're killed.
