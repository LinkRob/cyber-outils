{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear+}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} use by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}} inside "A Moderate Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. The {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to drop a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear+}} when it is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They are {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear|link}} that have had their tips enlarged and strengthened.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are noted to be shorter than the average spear, which is theorized to be for use in tight spaces.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear+}} will deal extra damage when they are used against {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}. They will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, nor can they be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. However, they can float on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Spear+}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a collection of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>
