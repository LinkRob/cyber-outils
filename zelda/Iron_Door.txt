The '''Iron Door''' is an object featured in {{TCT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It's late afternoon by the time the road ends at the base of a tall cliff. Set into the jagged stone is a massive iron door.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=62}}</ref> Situated just outside of the Cavern, {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|link}} can only open the door if she has the {{Term|TCT|Large Brass Key|link}} in her possession, allowing her to access the cavern and eventually receive the {{Term|TCT|Fairy's Scroll|link}} from the {{Term|TCT|Blue Fairy|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The key fits neatly into the lock and with a gentle shove, Zelda pushes the iron doors open.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=32}}</ref>

If Zelda does not have the {{Term|TCT|Large Brass Key}} with her, Zelda will slump to the ground, trying to decide what to do but is then attacked by a {{Term|TCT|Rope Snake|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Weary and downcast, she slumps to the ground, trying to decide what to do. But a rush of hot air makes her jump to her feet. Hiss! Zelda turns just as a huge snake-like neck rises up from a hole in the ground. 'A rope snake!' she cries.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=52}}</ref> The book ends with a Game Over with Zelda screaming for help.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zelda grabs for her bow, but before she can get it, the monstrous snake throws a coil around her arms. 'Help!' she screams.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=52}}</ref>

The book offers a puzzle which will help the reader see the item that Zelda will need while inside the cavern. When the puzzle is solved, it will spell out the words "{{Plural|TCT|Magic Arrow|link}}."
