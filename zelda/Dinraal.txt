{{Term/Store|Dinraal||Series, BotW}} is a [[Dragon]] in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Dinraal}} 150 (155)'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|TotK Shrine Quest|Dinraal}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|TotK}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Dinraal}}
{{Term|BotW|Dinraal}} is one of three roaming {{Plural|BotW|Dragon|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, the others being {{Term|BotW|Naydra|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Farosh|link}}. She can be found roaming the {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}} region, {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Hebra|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}} guards the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}}. While she cannot be defeated, she does not actively try to harm {{Term|BotW|Link}}. However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets too close, he will be seared by the fire emanating from her body, unless he possesses a full set of {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Set|link}} that has been enhanced twice by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}} will drop different {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots her with a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}. Depending on what part of {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}} is hit, she will drop a {{Term|BotW|Shard of Dinraal's Horn|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shard of Dinraal's Fang|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Claw|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Scale|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Scale}} can be offered at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}} to unlock a hidden {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.

==={{TotK}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Dinraal}}
{{Term|TotK|Dinraal}} is one of four roaming {{Plural|TotK|Dragon|link}} in {{Term|TotK|Hyrule|link}}, the others being {{Term|TotK|Naydra|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Light Dragon|link}}, and {{Term|TotK|Farosh|link}}. She can be found roaming the {{Term|TotK|Eldin (Region)|link}} region, {{Term|TotK|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Akkala|link}}, and {{Term|TotK|Hebra|link}}. {{Term|TotK|Dinraal}} guards the {{Term|TotK|Spring of Power|link}}. While she cannot be defeated, she does not actively try to harm {{Term|TotK|Link}}. However, if {{Term|TotK|Link}} gets too close, he will be seared by the fire emanating from her body, unless he possesses a full set of {{Term|TotK|Flamebreaker Set|link}} that has been enhanced twice by a {{Term|TotK|Great Fairy|link}}. {{Term|TotK|Dinraal}} will drop different {{Plural|TotK|Material}} if {{Term|TotK|Link}} shoots her with a {{Term|TotK|Bow|link}}. Depending on what part of {{Term|TotK|Dinraal}} is hit, she will drop a {{Term|TotK|Shard of Dinraal's Horn|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Shard of Dinraal's Fang|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Dinraal's Claw|link}} or {{Term|TotK|Dinraal's Scale|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|BotW|Dinraal}}'s name is a reference to the golden goddess {{Term|Series|Din (Goddess)|link}}. Her Japanese and various localized names seem to derive from [[Eldin]], which itself comes from {{Term|Series|Din (Goddess)}} as well.
