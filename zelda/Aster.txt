{{Term/Store|Aster||Series, BotW}}  is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aster|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Aster}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} girl who resides in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} with her father, {{Term|BotW|Medda}}. She is usually found running about in front of {{Term|BotW|The Great Ton Pu Inn|link}}, laughing to herself.<ref>{{Cite|Kya ha ha!|Aster|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Aster}} is a big fan of the {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction|link}} dance, and she will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if he knows it.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes?] Have you seen this one? Ba ba ba, Bolson... {{small|Fu-WA! Fu-WA!}} SHAKEEEEEEN!|Aster|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can claim to recognize it and please the child,<ref>{{Cite|[I love that bit.] Of course you do. That means you have good taste.|Aster|BotW}}</ref> or he can admit that he does not know it and allow her to teach it to him.<ref>{{Cite|[Never seen it.] Kya ha ha! LAME! I'll do it again. Watch carefully this time! All right, I'll teach you, but you gotta pay attention, OK? Ba ba ba, Bolson... {{Small|Fu-WA! Fu-WA!}} SHAKEEEEEEN! Easy as pie, right?|Aster|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her again later, he can ask about either {{Term|BotW|Bolson|link}} himself or the dance. {{Term|BotW|Aster}} will tell the hero that {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} is a carpenter and how he can find him behind the {{Term|BotW|East Wind|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Bolson?] Have you seen the carpenter behind the general store?|Aster|BotW}}</ref> Apparently, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} is also a "luminary in his field," but {{Term|BotW|Aster}} does not fully understand that title.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I actually heard...he's not a carpenter but a really famous luminary in his field... But I don't know what that means...}}|Aster|BotW}}</ref> She also says she heard he was a construction company, but she is not sure how that is possible.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I actually heard...he's not a carpenter but a construction company... I don't really know how that's possible, though...}}|Aster|BotW}}</ref> In addition, {{Term|BotW|Aster}} may share she heard that {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} is a famous president or designer.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I actually heard...he's not a carpenter but a really famous president!}}|Aster|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I actually heard...he's not a carpenter but a really famous designer...}}|Aster|BotW}}</ref> No matter what she says, {{Term|BotW|Aster}} will direct {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} so he can see the dance for himself.<ref>{{Cite|Whatever he is, I want you to get Bolson to show you his dance!|Aster|BotW}}</ref>

Asking about the dance will prompt {{Term|BotW|Aster}} to demonstrate it to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Dance?] You still don’t know the Bolson dance? How uncool! All right, I’ll teach you, but you gotta pay attention, OK?|Aster|BotW}}</ref> However, telling her to stop will upset {{Term|BotW|Aster}}, and she will call him lame.<ref>{{Cite|[You can stop.] Whatever! You’re lame.|Aster|BotW}}</ref> When it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Rains}}, {{Term|BotW|Aster}} will run to the nearby {{Term|BotW|Shared Cooking Space|link}} and pretend to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}}. She plays with a mound of material as though kneading it like dough.

At 8 o'clock, {{Term|BotW|Aster}} goes home to go to bed.<ref>{{Cite|I'm heading home. Time to hit the hay!|Aster|BotW}}</ref> She will also remark that if she does not get home soon, she will get in trouble.<ref>{{Cite|I'll be in trouble if I don't get home soon.|Aster|BotW}}</ref> As she sleeps, she dreams about her absent mother.<ref name="Mama"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Mmm...}}|Aster|BotW}}</ref>

Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} approach {{Term|BotW|Aster}} with a {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}} lit by the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, she will excitedly follow him around, commenting on how pretty the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|display=Flame}} is.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Pretty!|Aster|BotW}}</ref>
