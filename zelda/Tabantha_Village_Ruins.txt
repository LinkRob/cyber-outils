The {{Term/Store|Tabantha Village Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Tabantha Village Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Tabantha Village Ruins are located in the [[Tabantha Frontier]] in the Tabantha Hills. It is a very small village comprised of only nine ruined houses. A ruined guardian can be found here, along with many Lizalfos, Black Moblins, Ice Chuchus, Black Bokoblins, and a single Blizzrobe in the two houses removed somewhat from the settlement. The Hebra tower is close to here, as well as the Gee Ha'rah Shrine. One korok can be found here, and some wood bundles. Although it is unknown if the village was destroyed in the Great Calamity, it is unlikely despite the guardian here. It is far more likely that the village was destroyed by monsters after the calamity.
