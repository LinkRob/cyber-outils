The {{Term/Store|Steadfast Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Steadfast Ring<br/>Get knocked back less|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Steadfast Ring can be found inside the [[Sword & Shield Maze]] in {{OoS}}. The [[Hyper Slingshot]] is required to obtain it. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it decreases [[Link]]'s knockback on enemy attack.<ref name="Description"/> The Steadfast Ring can be transferred to {{OoA}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
