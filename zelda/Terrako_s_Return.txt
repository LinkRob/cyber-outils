"{{Term/Store|Terrako's Return||Series, HWAoC}}" is a {{Term|HWAoC|Quest|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Terrako's Return'''|Gallery|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Terrako's Return}} is unlocked after the final {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|The Future of Hyrule}}, is completed. The {{Term|HWAoC|Quest}} requires 50 {{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component}} to complete, which can be acquired in various {{Plural|HWAoC|Quest}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}. After all 50 are collected, a secret cutscene will play, and an alternative ending sequence will play, featuring {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako|link}} traveling around {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule|link}} while meeting various characters, in an art style similar to the game's loading screen.

After that, {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} becomes a playable {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} with a starting level of 57 as well as a {{Term|HWAoC|Blessed Screw|link}} with the default attack damage of 11. A new set of {{Plural|HWAoC|Quest}} focusing on upgrading {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} are also unlocked, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako's Training}} {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} will become available.
