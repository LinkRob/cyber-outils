{{Term/Store|Lorn||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lorn|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Lorn}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} woman who manages {{Term|BotW|Lorn's Fruit Stand|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, where she sells a variety of fruit. At night, {{Term|BotW|Lorn}}'s daughter {{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}} sleeps in the {{Term|BotW|Stall|link}} at her side.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes by {{Term|BotW|Lorn's Fruit Stand}}, she will advertise her wares.<ref>{{Cite|They're ready for eating...|Lorn|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Lorn}}, she claims that her fruit is fresh, juicy, and perfect for eating.<ref>{{Cite|Vasaaq. Our fruit here is fresh and juicy, just how everyone likes it!|Lorn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Smells nice, right?<br/>They're perfect for eating now.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref> She may also claim that her fruit is just as sweet as her daughter.<ref>{{Cite|Vasaaq.<br/>Our fruit here is sweet as can be, just like my wonderful daughter.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends his conversation with {{Term|BotW|Lorn}}, she tells him that {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} loves {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake|link}}, so she tries to make it occasionally.<ref>{{Cite|My kid really loves {{Color|BotW Blue|fruitcake}}, so I make it every once in a while.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref> However, she has difficulty finding the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}} needed to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} it.<ref>{{Cite|I have all the fruit I'd ever need, but it's a bit difficult to find {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|cane sugar}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}...|Lorn|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lorn}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} make {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake}} for himself, if he has the ingredients.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, if you have the ingredients, you should try making it yourself sometime.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref>

Like other {{Plural|BotW|General Store|link}} managers, {{Term|BotW|Lorn}} will buy {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|No problem. What do you want to sell?|Lorn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Are you done?|Lorn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I can never get enough items from the outside world. I'll buy whatever you got.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Go ahead and show me what else you have! There's no harm in me taking a look, at least.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref> During this, she may comment that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has many things that {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} would like.<ref>{{Cite|You seem to have more things that my kid will like.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref> When she makes a purchase, {{Term|BotW|Lorn}} will be grateful towards {{Term|BotW|Link}}, especially if she believes {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} will like it.<ref>{{Cite|Sarqso!|Lorn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sarqso. Those rupees are mine now.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sarqso. Today must be my lucky day for me to get my hands on something like this!|Lorn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sarqso. My kid will love this. Thanks!|Lorn|BotW}}</ref> She can also comment that it is fun to see what foreigners carry with them.<ref>{{Cite|It's fun just seeing the kinds of things that foreigners carry with them when they get here.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} stops selling, she tells him to come back in the future.<ref>{{Cite|Feel free to come back and show me more sometime.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref>
