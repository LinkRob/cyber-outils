"{{Term/Store|The Weapon Connoisseur||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Weapon Connoisseur}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Hateno Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Nebb}}, who will ask if he has traveled the world.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Hey, mister. Did you come from the big wide world?|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} comments that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must be strong with {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} and that he can probably use many different kinds.<ref>{{Cite|You look like you're good with a bow and sword, so you must be pretty strong.<br/>Are you strong or what? I bet you can use all sorts of different weapons, right?|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} explains that his grandfather loved {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} and wanted to see a lot of different kinds, but he died before he could see them all.<ref>{{Cite|Sooo... Do you wanna help a kid's dream come true? My gramps is dead now, but when he was alive, he loved weapons. Though he never got to see all the ones he wanted.|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} wants to continue what his grandfather started, so he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring him {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} from all around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, thus giving him the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So you bring me weapons and show 'em to me, and then I can tell gramps about 'em when I meet him again!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Nebb}} asks to see the following {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}:

* The first {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} is a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm, where should we start? Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|traveler's sword}}? I've seen it in books, but never in real life before! If you find it, please let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>{{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will then give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}} as thanks.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hrm? Oh?!}} Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|traveler's sword}}? Nice! Nice! Let me see, let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

* The second {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} is a {{Term|BotW|Fire Rod|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|fire rod}}? Grandpa said you can't find those around here. If you get ahold of one, let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> Showing a {{Term|BotW|Fire Rod}} to {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will net {{Term|BotW|Link}} another {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|fire rod}}? So cool! Let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

* The third {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} is a {{Term|BotW|Moblin Club|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|All right, the next weapon I wanna see is... What was it... Um... Oh, yes! That one! That one! Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Moblin club}}? So...when my grandpa was young, he looked for it but couldn't find it. So please! If you find it, please let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> He will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} as a reward for seeing one.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|Moblin club}}? Nice! Nice! Let me see, let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

* The fourth {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} is a {{Term|BotW|Duplex Bow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|duplex bow}}? Grandpa said you can't find those around here. If you get ahold of one, let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings him one, {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will thank him and give him another {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|duplex bow}}? You remembered our promise! Thanks! Let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

* The fifth {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} is a {{Term|BotW|Windcleaver|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|windcleaver}}? Grandpa said you can't find those around here. If you get ahold of one, let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} a {{Term|BotW|Windcleaver}}, he will reward him with a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|windcleaver}}? You remembered our promise! Thanks! Let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

* The sixth {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe+|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you ever heard of an {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient battle axe+}}? Grandpa said you can't find those around here. If you get ahold of one, let me see it|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} another {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee}} for showing him one. <ref>{{Cite|Isn't that...an {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient battle axe+}}? So cool! Let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

* The seventh {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} is the {{Term|BotW|Frostspear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|frostspear}}? So...when my grandpa was young, he looked for it but couldn't find it. So please! If you find it, please let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> Bringing {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} a {{Term|BotW|Frostspear}} will result in him giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|frostspear}}? So cool! Let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

* The final {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Short Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So, have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient short sword}}? Grandpa said you can't find those around here. If you get ahold of one, let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> For bringing {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} the final weapon, he will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that...the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient short sword}}? Nice! Nice! Let me see, let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

After seeing all of the {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}}, and he is sure his grandpa is happy wherever he is.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for showing me so many things! I'm sure my grandpa is super happy... wherever he is! Bye!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> He runs off, and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will be completed.
