{{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} made by the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}}. This is consistent with all other pieces of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear|link}}, the equipment archetype to which the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield}} belongs.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> They are commonly wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}, especially soldiers and travelers.<ref name="Inventory"/> Their design has changed over the years to match the fighting styles of the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

They can be found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} throughout the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region}}. Additionally, {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Shield}} may also be wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Region)}} {{Term|BotW|Region}}. One also spawns on a rock pillar in the {{Term|BotW|Great Cliffs|link}} area east of the {{Term|BotW|Dako Tah Shrine|link}}. They have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} that spawn when the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield}}, as well as a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}} and five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} can be given to {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}} to remake the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker|link}}, should it ever be broken.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Daybreaker}}, huh? To make it, I'll need {{Color|BotW Blue|one diamond}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|five}} pieces of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo shield}}.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield}} equipped, she will take note of it. She reveals that the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}} are famous for this {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|display=Shield}}, and remarks that they put thought into making it useful for {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's a Gerudo shield! The Gerudo are famous for it! Wow, they put actual thought into making it surfable.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Shield}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Shield}} do not burn like wooden {{Term|BotW|Shield}} will when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
