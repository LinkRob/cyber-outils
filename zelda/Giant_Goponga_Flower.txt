{{Term/Store|Giant Goponga Flower|Giant Goponga Flowers|Series, LA|plural}} are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{LA}} and {{EoW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 181 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref> 

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
They are dangerous [[Plant|plants]] which live in [[Goponga Swamp]].  These nuisances will launch harmful fireballs to all who pass, and even so much as touching them will inflict pain. For Link to rid the area of these monstrosities, he must grab [[Chain Chomp#Link's Awakening|BowWow]], who can eat them in a single chomp, allowing him to pass by. Alternatively, he could use the [[Magic Rod]] or the [[Hookshot]] to kill them.

Smaller and more common variations of these plants are known as [[Goponga Flower]]s.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Giant Goponga Flower|Native to the Faron Wetlands, this monstrous plant spits large clusters of dangerous pollen at enemies.<ref name="EoW Giant Goponga Flower">{{Cite|Native to the Faron Wetlands, this monstrous plant spits large clusters of dangerous pollen at enemies.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Giant Goponga Flower are highly toxic flowers encountered in [[Faron Wetlands]]. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Giant Goponga Flower [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of four.
