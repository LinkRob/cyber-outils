==Location and uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Diamonds are rare [[Material]]s in {{BotW}}. Because of their rarity, Diamonds have long been sold for a high price in [[Hyrule]].<ref name="Inventory"/> [[Ramella]], the [[Gerudo]] in [[Goron City]], may offer to buy Diamonds from Link at a 10% increase of the regular sale price, trading a bulk of 10 Diamonds for 5,500 [[Rupee]]s instead of the regular total of 5,000.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, do you happen to have {{Color|BotW Red|10 diamonds}}? You'll get 5,000 rupees if you sell them at a store... or you can trade them to me for {{Color|BotW Red|5,500 rupees}}.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> Diamonds can be used as an ingredient for [[White Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. If [[Link]] uses Diamonds while [[Cooking]], the resulting meal will be [[Rock-Hard Food]].

Link can obtain Diamonds by breaking [[Ore Deposit]]s and [[Rare Ore Deposit]]s. They also have a chance to drop after slaying [[Stone Talus (Rare)|Stone Talus]]es, [[Frost Talus]]es, [[Igneo Talus]]es, or Silver and Golden [[enemy|enemies]]. [[Kairo]], the traveling [[Goron]] [[Merchant]], will sell Diamonds during unusual weather.<ref>{{Cite|This weather has me in a rare mood to sell rare ore. How about a {{Color|BotW Red|diamond}}? You interested in rare items?|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> The only other location to sell Diamonds is [[Ore and More]] in [[Tarrey Town]].

Diamonds can be found inside [[Treasure Chest]]s in the following [[Ancient Shrine]]s:

* [[Gee Ha'rah Shrine]]

* [[Kah Mael Shrine]]
* [[Keeha Yoog Shrine]]

* [[Misae Suma Shrine]]
* [[Mozo Shenno Shrine]]

==Spunoff appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{ZA|-}}===
[[File:ZA Diamond.png|left]]
The Diamond is obtained by giving the [[Coal]] to the Blacksmith in [[Great Wimbich]]. Receiving the Diamond automatically rewards Zelda with 999 Rupees. It is kept in the inventory afterwards, but has no other use.

=== {{HWAoC|-}} ===
In {{HWAoC|-}} hold a close resemblance to the ones in {{BotW|-}}. They can be bought at the store in the [[Gerudo Desert]], or be found in gray [[Treasure Chest]]s in main quests. They can be sold to most [[Merchant]]s for 500 [[Rupee]]s, but [[Dayfah]] will buy them for 1,250 Rupees.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Crossover appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Stub|CoH}}
Diamonds are a currency similar to Rupees. They can be used to purchase various items in shops in the Hyrule. Unlike Rupees, Diamonds will not be lost if the characters get a game over.<ref>{{Cite|Unlike rupees and keys, {{Color|CoH Yellow|diamonds}} won't be lost when you get a game over.|Trill|CoH}}</ref> Diamonds can be obtained by defeating all enemies on a screen,<ref>{{Cite|Diamonds will drop only once the {{Color|CoH Blue|last enemy}} on a screen is defeated.|Trill|CoH}}</ref> or finding them in dungeons or chests.
{{Ambig|End}}
