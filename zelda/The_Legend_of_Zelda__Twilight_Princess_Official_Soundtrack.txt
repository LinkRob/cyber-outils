'''''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'' Official Soundtrack''' is the original soundtrack for {{TP}}. The soundtrack was bundled with a 1/6th scale replica of the [[Master Sword]] and [[Hylian Shield]] by Master Replicas and a certificate of authenticity. The soundtrack was also available in a [[Nintendo Power]] bundle that included a 12 month subscription to the magazine and a {{TP|-}} Player's Guide.
