{{Term/Store|Triforce Gateway|Triforce Gateways|Series, TFH|plural}} are {{Plural|TFH|Object|link}} in {{TFH}}.{{Name Ref|TFH}} They act as {{Plural|TFH|Warp Point|link}} that transport the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} throughout the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} appear in many locations throughout {{TFH|-}}. They initially appear as three dark triangles, slightly raised from the floor, representing the pieces of the {{Term|Series|Triforce|link}}. Once activated, the triangles glow a bright yellow. When each of the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} stands on a triangle, they will [[Warping|Warp]] to the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is a Triforce gateway. Using its power is the only way to travel to the Drablands. Only when three heroes gather will the Triforce's power awaken and allow for travel between the worlds. The way will not open for one hero alone.|Match Master|TFH}}</ref>

There are three {{Plural|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}. In the [[Multiplayer]] lobby, the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} becomes active once all three heroes have gathered. In the {{Term|TFH|Hall of the Doppels|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} can Warp to the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}} in the company of two {{Plural|TFH|Doppel|link}} instead of allies. The {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}} lobby can be activated and used by only two {{Plural|TFH|Links}} as well as three.

{{Plural|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} also appear throughout the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}} at the end of each {{Term|TFH|Stage|link}} in a {{Term|TFH|Level|link}}, and are used to Warp to the beginning of the next {{Term|TFH|Stage}}. They are often inactive at the start of a {{Term|TFH|Stage}}, and will become active once certain conditions are met, such as all {{Plural|TFH|Enemy|link}} being defeated or every {{Term|TFH|Shock Switch|link}} turned on. The {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}} also uses {{Plural|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} in its hub area to allow the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to select which {{Term|TFH|Stage}} to begin on.
