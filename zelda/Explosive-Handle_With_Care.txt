{{Term/Store|Explosive-Handle With Care||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 6 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Explosive-Handle With Care|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Explosive-Handle With Care}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must defeat 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. It can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Rugged Rhino Beetle|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}. Several bomb barrels are scattered around the map, which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} can explode to kill {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}. At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, all of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin}}, except for a single {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}}. Two more will spawn when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches 100 KOs, and another two will spawn alongside {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}} when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches 400 KOs. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} can also cause these enemies to spawn by reaching certain parts of the map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, including all five {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}}.
