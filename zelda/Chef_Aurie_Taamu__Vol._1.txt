{{Term/Store|''Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 1''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 1|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 1}} is the first in a series of cookery {{Plural|BotW|Book|link}} written by {{Term|BotW|Aurie Taamu|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This collection of Aurie Taamu's notes is so amazing! I feel like I learn something new each time I read it.|Dina|BotW}}</ref> It can be found in the room where {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link}} holds her {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} class, where it is used by the students who attend it. This volume details special effects that can be applied to meals, {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}, and the use of {{Plural|BotW|Fairy|link}} while {{Term|BotW|Cooking}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Book}} has a light green cover that is decorated with dark green decoration. It is left open on a page showing a diagram on chopping {{Plural|BotW|Carrot|link}}.
