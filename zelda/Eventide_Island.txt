{{Term/Store|Eventide Island||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Eventide Island'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Eventide Island is a small island located in the southeastern portion of the [[Necluda Sea]]. It is notable for being the site of the [[Stranded on Eventide]] [[Shrine Quest]] which begins once Link reaches the {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|display=Island}}.

==Trivia==
*With the exception of [[Cloyne]]'s Treasure Chest mini-game in [[Lurelin Village]], this is the only location in {{BotW|-}} where Treasure Chests respawn after being opened and looted.
*With the exception of the {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}} always regenerate once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} leaves the island, regardless of whether or not there has been a {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon|link}}. Furthermore, they are not subject to the hidden difficulty scaling value that upgrades {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} based on the number of {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has killed, and any {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} killed on {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}} will not add to that counter.{{Verify}}
*{{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}} and its associated {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} are a reference to {{LA}} and {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}.
*In the [[Voice Memories]] section of the Nintendo Switch app for {{TotK}}, it's indicated that Link had told her about the events of his trek there at some point between {{BotW}} and the events of Ganondorf's return. It also indicated monsters had previously been prevalent on the island a century before but were, ironically enough, wiped out by the Calamity.
