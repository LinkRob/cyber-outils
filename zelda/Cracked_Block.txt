{{Term/Store|Cracked Block|Cracked Blocks|Series, FS|plural}},{{Exp Game|FS}}<ref>{{Cite|You can use Bombs to destroy {{Color|FS Blue|cracked blocks}}.|Stray Fairy|FS}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Broken Block|Broken Blocks|OoA|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoA}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 110}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. {{Plural|Series|Cracked Block}} go unnamed in {{OoS}}, {{FSA}}, and {{TMC}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Plural|OoS|Cracked Block}} are found within {{Term|OoS|Snake's Remains|link}}, the {{Term|OoS|Sword & Shield Maze|link}}, and the {{Term|OoS|Hero's Cave|link}}. Each location uses a different color for the {{Plural|OoS|Cracked Block}}, and they can be destroyed using {{Plural|OoS|Bomb|link}}.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Plural|OoA|Cracked Block}} are found within the {{Term|OoA|Moonlit Grotto|link}},  {{Term|OoA|Mermaid's Cave|link}}, and {{Term|OoA|Ancient Tomb|link}}. Each location uses a different color for the {{Plural|OoA|Cracked Block}}, and they can be destroyed using {{Plural|OoA|Bomb|link}}.
