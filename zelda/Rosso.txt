{{Term/Store|Rosso||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref> He is one of the {{Plural|ALBW|Sage|link|display=Seven Sages}} descended from those who sealed the {{Term|ALBW|Sacred Realm|link}} long ago.

==Biography==
{{Term|ALBW|Rosso}} is a miner who lives north of {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}} at the base of {{Term|ALBW|Death Mountain|link}}, and owns an [[Rosso's Ore Mine|Ore Mine]] at the top of the mountain. He gives {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} the {{Term|ALBW|Power Glove|link}} after noticing that the young hero cannot lift rocks. If {{Term|ALBW|Link}} picks up all of the rocks around {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}}'s house, he will reward him with {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}. After {{Term|ALBW|Link}} clears the {{Term|ALBW|Tower of Hera|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}} goes missing while on a mining expedition, having been kidnapped by {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} and turned into a {{Term|ALBW|Painting|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} eventually uses {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}} and the rest of the {{Plural|ALBW|Sage}}' power to release {{Term|ALBW|Ganon|link}} from his seal in order to merge with the Demon King. {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} then hides {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Painting}} within the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}, a frozen labyrinth that contrasts with {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}}'s fiery nature.

After {{Term|ALBW|Link}} rescues him from the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins}}, {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}} reveals that he always knew of his status as a {{Term|ALBW|Sage}}, but he always thought it inconsequential, as he was too busy mining rocks.<ref>{{Cite|Well, just look at this. Who would have thought I'd see YOU here, Link? Not all that surprised to see me here though. I've know for a long time that I was a Sage. Never told anyone though. It wasn't really a secret. I was just too busy mining all the time to mention it. Speaking of busy--you've still got some work to do here. Find the rest of the Seven Sages, OK? If you do, you'll be all the stronger, Link.|Rosso|ALBW}}</ref> Afterwards, he tells {{Term|ALBW|Link}} that he simply wants to return to cracking rocks.<ref>{{Cite|Can't say I care much about all this Demon King business. I just want to get home and crack some rocks. So find the rest of the Seven Sages already. I'm going to go nuts if I'm cooped up here for much longer.|Rosso|ALBW}}</ref> He remains in the [[Temple of Light#Chamber of Sages|Chamber of Sages]], awaiting the time for all the other {{Plural|ALBW|Sage}} to be rescued by {{Term|ALBW|Link}}. Once they are reunited, he combines his power with the others to cause the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Courage|link}} to materialize above them.

After {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Triforce|link}} is restored to the {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|display=Hyrulean}} {{Term|ALBW|Sacred Realm}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}} is restored following the revival of its {{Term|ALBW|Triforce}}, {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}} returns to {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}} and is seen carrying rocks on {{Term|ALBW|Death Mountain}} with the {{Term|ALBW|Bouldering Guy|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The [[Symbols#Race Emblems|Goron Emblem]] appears on {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}}'s belt buckle. However, he himself is not a {{Term|Series|Goron|link}}.<ref name= "Rosso"/>
**Internal file names refer to {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}} as "Goron", indicating that the race was planned to appear in {{ALBW|-}}, or that {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}} was intentionally given {{Term|Series|Goron}}-like physical characteristics.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|ALBW|Rosso}}'s name is derived from the Italian word for "red", in reference to his red beard and red picture frame. Of the {{Plural|ALBW|Sage}} who were first introduced in {{ALBW|-}}, {{Term|ALBW|Rosso}} is the only one whose French name is the same in both the North American and European versions of the game.
