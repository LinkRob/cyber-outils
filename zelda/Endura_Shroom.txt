==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Endura Shroom}}
{{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom}} are a rare type of {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}} that are dotted around the various small forests within and outlining {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. More concentrated amounts can be found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} within the {{Term|BotW|Dalite Forest|link}} and at the base of the {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Seres Scablands|link}}. They can also be found in an unnamed forest behind {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom}} can be purchased at the {{Term|BotW|East Wind|link}} {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat an {{Term|BotW|Endura Shroom}} raw to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Alternatively, he can roast them near a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to create a {{Term|BotW|Toasty Endura Shroom|link}}, which increases the amount of {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} restored to one and a half. When used in {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}, they have an enduring effect, which gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} extra {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} on top of replenishing his {{Term|BotW|Stamina}} to full.<ref name="Inventory"></ref>{{Note|Not to be confused with Energizing, which just replenishes {{Term|BotW|Stamina}}.}} {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom}} are also one of the {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} used by {{Term|BotW|Sayge|link}} to create {{Term|BotW|Orange Dye|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}.
