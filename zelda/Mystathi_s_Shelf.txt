{{Term/Store|Mystathi's Shelf||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Mystathi's Shelf'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Mystathi's Shelf is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. The Shelf is a snowy plateau with outcroppings of rocks that overlook [[Lake Illumeni]]. The [[Keeha Yoog Shrine]] is found along the cliffside in the southeastern area of the shelf.

[[Link]] can find a [[Korok]] puzzle along the Shelf where he must transfer a [[Rock]] from one set of stones to another near it. Once Link fills in the shape, a Korok will appear, giving him a [[Korok Seed]].<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
