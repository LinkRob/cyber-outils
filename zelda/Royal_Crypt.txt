The {{Term/Store|Royal Crypt||Series, TMC}} is a mini-{{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> It is the tomb of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}}, an ancient king of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}},<ref name="Dampé">{{Cite|You want to go to the tomb of King Gustaf of Hyrule? Then you'd probably better talk to Dampé. He's the gravedigger at the cemetery. Nobody knows the Royal Crypt better!|Vassal|TMC}}</ref> whose spirit provides {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} with a golden {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} that can be used to access {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I grant to you this Kinstone... Seek the source of the flow in Hyrule... Only then will your path open... Save Zelda... All of Hyrule is counting on you...|Gustaf|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Of course! The source of the flow! He meant Veil Springs! I wonder what lies up ahead... Well, we know it's connected to the element, so let's go, Link!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Crypt==
The {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt}} is situated in the graveyard of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}, which is located to the west of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} and can be accessed from {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Field|link}}.<ref name="Gentari">{{Cite|Welcome back, Link. I feel like it's been so long since you visited! Looks like you need to find just one more element. Say... Have you been to the Royal Valley, west of Hyrule Castle? You used to be able to get there easily from Trilby Highlands. Nowadays, however, you have to take a secret path to get there. I'm sure it was to the west of the northern part of Hyrule Field...|Gentari|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You're going to the Royal Valley? I hear that place is haunted... But there's a secret entrance that will get you there from Hyrule Field. I mean, that's what I've heard.|Vassal|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, he is visited by the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}}, who tells him to "stand before [him]".<ref>{{Cite|... ... O young one... Child who seeks to aid the princess of Hyrule... My name is Gustaf... I was king of Hyrule, countless ages ago. Stand before me... Only then will the path open to you.|Gustaf|TMC}}</ref> He marks the location of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt}} on {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Map|link}}, which {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} notices, saying that they should visit it after they have visited the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmph! Was that...just a dream? He said he was a king of Hyrule. Wait! Look at your map, Link. There's some kind of mark on it. Hm. I guess that means it wasn't a dream! We should head there once we've infused your sword with that element. To the elemental sanctuary!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} is also told to visit the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley}} by {{Term|TMC|Gentari|link}}.<ref name="Gentari" /> In order to reach the entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|display=Crypt}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must navigate the maze-like forest leading up to the graveyard,<ref>{{Cite|Normal people can't enter the Royal Valley. There's a cryptic forest that leads into it, and people get lost there. The secret to finding your way through is reading all the signs! You'll never make it unless you read each one carefully.|Vassal|TMC}}</ref> seek the help of {{Term|TMC|Dampé|link}} the gravekeeper,<ref name="Dampé" /><ref>{{Cite|My name's Dampé. I'm the gravedigger here. What's that? You were called here by the ghost of a king? That'd be the ancient king of Hyrule who rests here. I've seen him myself. Well, if the king has called you, I can't see any reason not to let you in... Here, take this graveyard key.|Dampé|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll open the gate for you. Be careful in there.|Dampé|TMC}}</ref> and obtain the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard Key|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the graveyard key back! Now, you can open the graveyard gate!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> He must then open the entrance by using the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} to duplicate himself and activate three {{Plural|TMC|Switch|link}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
Although it belongs to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt}}, like many other burial sites in {{TLoZ|Series}}, has a eerie, macabre atmosphere.<ref>{{Cite|It may be the Royal Crypt, but graves are creepy, no matter whose they are! Where are we supposed to go to meet this King Gustaf anyway? I'm not so sure I even want to anymore! Can we just go somewhere less creepy?|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> It is home to undead enemies such as {{Plural|TMC|Gibdo|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Stalfos|link}}, and it is in a state of disrepair, containing cracked floors, torn carpets, and deep pits that pose a danger to {{Term|TMC|Link}}. In order to navigate the mini-{{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must make use of the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} and the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt}} contains many callbacks to {{TLoZ}}.
**The background music that plays inside the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|display=Crypt}} is a remix of the music that plays inside {{Plural|TLoZ|Dungeon}} in the original game.
**The sound effect that is heard when the two {{Plural|TMC|Small Key|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}}'s room are revealed is the same as the sound effect that is heard when a hidden item is uncovered in the original game.
**The {{Plural|TMC|Gibdo}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|display=Crypt}} can be seen holding {{Plural|TMC|Small Key}} and {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}}, much like {{Plural|TLoZ|Stalfos}} did in the original game.
**The four statues in the entrance chamber of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|display=Crypt}} are similar to those found in the entrance chambers of the {{Plural|TLoZ|Dungeon}} in the original game.
**The {{Plural|TMC|Torch|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Sub-Boss}} room shoot fire, much like some {{Plural|TLoZ|Torch}} in the original game.
