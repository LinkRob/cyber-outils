==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|The Right Staff}}" is an event wherein a greater number of staff-wielding [[Spirit]]s randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= You'll be facing some stiff competition from staff-wielding spirits on the Spirit Board during this event! Defeat them in battle to earn more SP than usual.<br/><br/>The Legend-class spirit Krystal and the Ace-class spirits Hilda and Star Rod will appear at set times. Don't miss out! |author= Nintendo |published= April 10, 2019 |retrieved= April 10, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/88b187c4/index.html |title= Event: The Right Staff - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace Spirits befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
