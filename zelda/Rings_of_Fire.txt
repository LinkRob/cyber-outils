'''Rings of Fire''' is a spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
Rings of Fire launches three rings of fire that travel in a single line, and it costs three [[Rupee]]s to use.

The Rings of Fire is sold by [[Madame Kriggle]] in her magic shop in [[Great Wimbich]] for 150 Rupees.
