{{Term/Store|Catherine||Series, TLoZ (TV Series)}} is Link's horse in {{TLoZ|TV Series}} and in the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}.<ref name="Road">{{Cite Episode|quote= Let's hit the road, Catherine. This place makes me sick.|name=Link|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> In the TV series, she is seen in various episodes, such as "[[Cold Spells]]", "[[That Sinking Feeling]]", "[[Stinging a Stinger]]", and "[[The White Knight]]", which is the only episode in which her name is mentioned.<ref name="Road"/> In the Valiant Comics, Catherine is only briefly featured in "[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 1#Missing in Action|Missing in Action]]" and ''[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 4#Queen of Hearts|Queen of Hearts]]'',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Let's move, Catherine! Zelda's got a day's head start!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=2 [Missing in Action]}}</ref> where Link uses her to search for a missing Zelda and later to help [[Queen Seline]] recover her magical powers from Ganon's minions.
