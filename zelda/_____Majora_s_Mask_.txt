==Biography==
Every midnight, ??? emerges from the toilet of the {{Term|MM3D|Stock Pot Inn|link}} asking for paper.<ref>{{Cite|Pa-pa-pa-paper, please!|??? (Majora's Mask)|MM3D}}</ref> It remains until 6:00 AM. [[Link]] can give it any item made of paper (such as [[Letter|Letters]]). After doing so, ??? disappears and a toilet flush is heard. ??? re-emerges with a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}} that fell into the toilet.<ref>{{Cite|This fell down here... I'll give it to you.|??? (Majora's Mask)|MM3D}}</ref> If this was done in subsequent appearances, no reward is given to the young hero.
