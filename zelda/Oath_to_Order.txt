The "{{Term/Store|Oath to Order||Series, MM, MM3D}}" is a song featured in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96}}</ref> It outlines a D-minor arpeggio (A-F-D-F-A-D) and only has one effective use in the game. It is a mystical ballad taught to [[Link]] when he rescues the first of the [[Four Giants]] from [[Woodfall Temple]] and is summoned to their [[Chamber of Giants|mysterious chamber]] during his quest to save [[Termina]] from {{MM|Object}} and the falling [[Moon (Object)|Moon]].

==Learning Location and Uses==
[[File:MM3D Oath to Order Icon.png|left]]
Its function is to summon the Four Giants on the eve of the [[Carnival of Time|Carnival]] atop the [[Clock Tower]]. Once all of the Four Giants have been freed from their seal, they will come and prevent the evil menace from destroying Termina while Link and [[Tatl]] enter the moon to defeat the evil inside Majora's Mask.<ref>{{Cite|"Call us." That's what they're saying. Without being told, we understand. So now you'll help us out in exchange! We'll call you from atop the Clock Tower, so do something about him, all right?|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

In the case that not all the Giants have been saved, the ones who have been released from their seal will still come upon calling, but they will not be able to stop the Moon from crashing into Clock Town on their own.<ref>{{Cite|That's no good! It's not stopping! It's hopeless!|Tatl|MM}}</ref>
