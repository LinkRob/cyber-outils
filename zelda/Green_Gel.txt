{{Term/Store|Green Gel|Green Gels|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Green Gel|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Green Gel}} are slime-like enemies that appear after a {{Term|CoH|Green Zol|link}} is defeated. Like other {{Plural|CoH|Gel|link}}, they move to the beat of the music, hopping to an adjacent tile on every other beat while trying to make their way towards {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}.
