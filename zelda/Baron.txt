{{Term/Store|Baron||Series, FPTRR}} is a {{Term|FPTRR|Boss|link}} in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} He is found on {{Term|FPTRR|Mount Desma}}. When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} meets him, he asks if he wants to challenge him.<ref>{{Cite|Want to take me on?|Baron|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Do you still want to take me on?|Baron|FPTRR}}</ref> Several skeletons lay on the ground where {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}} is found, which he explains are the remains of those who fought him before.<ref name="Quote1">{{Cite|Look around you. That is all that remains of those who took me on.|Baron|FPTRR}}</ref> Should {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} accept {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}}'s challenge, he will drop from the ceiling and begin the battle.

{{Term|FPTRR|Baron}} is a strong foe, evident by the fact that he can take thousands of {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} from {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} each second. Only a Drifter {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}} is powerful enough to defeat him.

==History==
After defeating {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} will recover the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Demon's Mask|display=Demon's Mask|link}} - a mask that resembles {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}}'s face. Whichever Drifter {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} is present will ask to see the mask and relate the history of {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}} before purchasing the mask to bury. However, the story that is related will change depending on the {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}. These stories, therefore, may all be fictitious rumors or separate parts of {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}}'s story. There are several common threads between the stories, however, including the manufacture of the mask, a war, {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}}'s personal loss, his self-enforced exile and the resemblance of the mask to the god of war, named by {{Term|FPTRR|Ronny|link}} as Bamo.

According to {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo|link}}, there was a master mask-maker whose creations were loved by children many years before.<ref>{{Cite|A long time ago I heard there lived a master craftsman who carved masks. Children loved the masks he made and he made a lot of money selling them.|Teddy Todo|FPTRR}}</ref> When war broke out, the artisan's son joined the army and never returned.<ref>{{Cite|But then a war started in that country. The craftsman's son joined the army and never returned.|Teddy Todo|FPTRR}}</ref> Several months after his son's presumed death, the mask-maker carved a mask of the god of war, which he donned before joining the war himself.<ref>{{Cite|After months of mourning the craftsman decided to make a mask of the god of war's face. He put it on and left to join the war...|Teddy Todo|FPTRR}}</ref> Although he never returned to his village, rumors spread of a masked warrior called Baron who cried as he slew his foes.<ref>{{Cite|...and he never returned to his village again. I heard stories of a terrifying masked soldier who cried as he defeated his enemies. He was known as Baron and was feared by all.|Teddy Todo|FPTRR}}</ref>

{{Term|FPTRR|Yamori|link}} relates that {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}} was himself a Drifter {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} who was peerless in battle.<ref>{{Cite|I have heard there was once a Drifter Bodyguard by the name of Baron. He was a master warrior. There was no one who could match him.|Yamori|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Baron defeated all he fought. No one who took him on lived to tell the tale.|Yamori|FPTRR}}</ref> After countless victories, the families of his victims united to hunt him down.<ref>{{Cite|The number of his victims continued to grow and the victim's families came looking for him.|Yamori|FPTRR}}</ref> Rather than fight them, {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}} chose to disguise himself with a mask and live in isolation on the mountains.<ref>{{Cite|Baron decided to hide his face behind a mask and went to live alone in the mountains.|Yamori|FPTRR}}</ref>

A legend told by {{Term|FPTRR|Ronny}} tells of two gods who were close brothers: Bamo, god of war, and Aron, god of love.<ref>{{Cite|There were two gods that were brothers and who were the best of friends. The elder brother was Bamo, god of war. The younger brother was Aron, the god of love.|Ronny|FPTRR}}</ref> When humans first appeared, problems soon arose. These humans wanted to steal the land of the gods and attacked when Bamo was abroad, killing Aron.<ref>{{Cite|Those greedy humans were determined to steal the land where the gods lived in peace and harmony. The cunning humans waited until Bamo left to travel to a distant land, then they raided the sacred land in their hundreds and killed Aron.|Ronny|FPTRR}}</ref> Bamo took revenge wearing a devil's mask, killing many humans in combat.<ref>{{Cite|When Bamo heard this he was livid with anger. He made a devils mask and decided to take revenge for his brother. He made war on the terrified humans who he came across.|Ronny|FPTRR}}</ref> This failed to assuage his pain, and at last Bamo hid in a cave.<ref>{{Cite|But his deep sadness never disappeared. He hid in a cave away from the sight of mankind...|Ronny|FPTRR}}</ref> Eventually the humans forgot their crime and began to search for Bamo,<ref>{{Cite|...while humans forgot that they had ever done anything wrong and decided to hunt for Bamo.|Ronny|FPTRR}}</ref> possibly explaining the skulls found upon the chamber's floor.<ref name="Quote1"/> {{Term|FPTRR|Ronny}} tentatively concludes that Baron is Bamo,<ref>{{Cite|But surely that monster can't have been...Bamo?|Ronny|FPTRR}}</ref> or possibly both gods in one form.<ref>{{Cite|Baron... I mean... Aron and Bamo...|Ronny|FPTRR}}</ref> This latter possibility may explain {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}}'s claim that he never wanted to hurt anyone,<ref>{{Cite|I never wanted to hurt anyone...|Baron|FPTRR}}</ref> while the identification of {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}} as a god explains his survival through centuries of hiding.<ref>{{Cite|My name is Baron. I have been here for hundreds of years.|Baron|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*One of the skulls on the floor is wider than the other human skulls and more closely resembles a {{Term|FPTRR|Goron|link}}, although no {{Plural|FPTRR|Goron}} are observed in {{FPTRR|-}}.
**Alternatively, this could be the skull of {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo|link}}'s father. In the Japanese version of the game, {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo}} is reportedly the only {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}} capable of defeating {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}}, having sworn to avenge the defeat of his father.<ref>{{Cite Web| url=http://www.gamefaqs.com/ds/930503-freshly-picked-tingles-rosy-rupeeland/faqs/46227 | title=Tingle's Freshly Picked, Rose-Colored Rupee Land: Algus's Happily Pressed, Smile-Typed FAQ/Walkthrough | site=wwwgamefaqs.com}}</ref> This places a vastly different interpretation on the flower {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo}} leaves in the grotto - the only {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard }} to make such a gesture.
*The description of the Demon's Mask, which may confer combative advantages upon its wearer, resembles the description of the {{Term|MM3D|Fierce Deity's Mask|link}} from {{MM}}.
**The compulsory awarding of a mask resembling a defeated foe after a battle, along with the funerary treatment of the mask as the remains of {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}} by the Drifter {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}, is reminiscent of the remains obtained after defeating {{Plural|MM3D|Boss|link}} in {{MM|-}}.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|FPTRR|Baron}}'s name is likely a portmanteau of the two god brothers, Bamo and Aron. This may support {{Term|FPTRR|Ronny}}'s possible conclusion that {{Term|FPTRR|Baron}} was Bamo and Aron united. Alternatively, Bamo may have adopted the name in memory of Aron - a possible reference to the Greek goddess Athena who attached the name of her childhood friend, Palas, to her own after killing him in a fight.
