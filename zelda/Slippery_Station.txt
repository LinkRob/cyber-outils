{{Term/Store|Slippery Station||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 290}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Located in the northeast section of the [[Snow Realm]], the station is only accessible after [[Link]] receives a [[Force Gem]] from [[Steem]] in the [[Snow Sanctuary]] for bringing him a [[vessel]] from [[Papuchia Village]].

As its name suggests, the inside of the Slippery Station has a Skating Rink, which consists of an icy, slippery floor with several turns. Across this rink are three locked entrances that can only be opened by pressing a switch, and behind each door is a treasure chest. There are three switches that pertain to three different levels: Amateur, Pro, and Champion. By pressing one of those switches, the door that belongs to that certain switch will open for a limited time. Link must reach the door before it closes to be able to reach the treasure and open its contents.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the Skating Rink! You don't even need skates to skate here! We have three amazing courses: Amateur, Pro, and Champion. Pick your course, step on the switch, then gun it to the treasure!|Sign|ST}}</ref> The trick lies in that, for example, the Amateur course has fewer turns and a shorter distance to travel than the Champion course. As such, Link must time his spins correctly so as to not make such a wide turn and cut down in his time.

The rewards for completing each of the courses are as follow:

*'''Amateur:''' 300 [[Rupee]]s
*'''Pro:''' Random rare treasure, such as a [[Palace Dish]] or [[Ancient Gold Piece]]
*'''Champion:''' [[Alchemy Stone]]

==Trivia==
*Both this station and [[Snowdrift Station]] are represented by the same sprite in the map of [[Hyrule]].
