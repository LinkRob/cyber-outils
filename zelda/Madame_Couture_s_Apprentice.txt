{{Term/Store|Madame Couture's Apprentice||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 305}}</ref> He works at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|link}}'s tailor shop as her assistant.

==Biography==
During {{Term|TFH|Link|link}}'s visit to {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}, he is spotted by {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's Apprentice}}. The young boy immediately recognizes {{Term|TFH|Link}} as having the fabled {{Term|TFH|Three Noble Attributes of Courage|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Those ears are a dead giveaway... So positively pointy! I must say, they're rather chic!|Madame Couture's Apprentice|TFH}}</ref> and believes him to be the chosen hero who has come to save {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}} from her curse.<ref>{{Cite|YOU!! You must be...the hero! The one fated to save our dear Princess Styla!|Madame Couture's Apprentice|TFH}}</ref> He, however, finds {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s {{Term|TFH|Bear Minimum|link}} {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} to be rather unstylish,<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of fashion curses... May I speak plainly? It's your outfit. It's a bit... Well... You know what I'm getting at, right?|Madame Couture's Apprentice|TFH}}</ref> and recommends he visits {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}} and try on something new.

Couture's Apprentice can then be found in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}, in either the [[Multiplayer]] communications lobby or the {{Term|TFH|Hall of the Doppels|link}}, where he can be found examining the {{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} Completion List. He exhibits the {{Term|TFH|Catalog|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Material List|link}} when spoken to, and can be asked for more information on {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Exp|マダムテーラーの弟子。お城に出張業中に出され、 そこで 「服図鑑」「材料図鑑」を見せてくれる。|Madame Tailor's Apprentice. He has gone to the castle for a business trip, where he can show off the "Clothes Encyclopedia"and the "Material Encyclopedia".}}|author= Nintendo|published= September 16, 2015|retrieved=September 24, 2015|url= http://www.nintendo.co.jp/3ds/ea3j/storychara/characters.html|title= ゼルダの伝説　トライフォース３銃士 <nowiki>|</nowiki> ニンテンドー3DS <nowiki>|</nowiki> 任天堂|site= Zelda.co.jp|type=}} <sup>(Translation: [http://zeldauniverse.net/forums/index.php/Thread/178050-The-Legend-of-Zelda-TriForce-Heroes-Gameplay-spoilers-beware/?postID=5657575#post5657575 kaialone])</sup></ref>

In [[Download Play]], the Apprentice can also be requested to tailor {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}} for the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} inside the castle.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= In Download Play, you can order outfits from Madame Couture's apprentice to the right of the lobby.|game= TFH|page= 11}}</ref> He is, however, much slower at tailoring than {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}}, and will finish once the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} enter and return from a [[Levels in Tri Force Heroes|Level]].
