{{Term/Store|Bawb|Bawbs|Series, ALBW|plural}} are enemies in {{ALBW}}.<ref name="E 165">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 165}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ALBW|Bawb}} are {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb|link}} that have been possessed by the spirits of monsters.<ref name="E 165"/> They become active {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb|link}} when they are attacked. They can be used to solve {{Term|ALBW|Crystal Switch|link}} puzzles and blast open cracks in floors. They will regenerate after they explode, similar to {{Plural|TPHD|Bombling|link}} in {{TP}}. In the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Swamp Palace|link}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Bawb}} attempt to home in on {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}}, while others, like those in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Hideout|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle|link}}, wander randomly.

==Nomenclature==
