"{{Term/Store|Cliffside Etchings||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Cliffside Etchings}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Tabantha Bridge Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
On the cliff behind {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Geggle|link}} looking out at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} during the day. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks him what he is looking at,<ref>{{Cite|[Whatcha looking at?] Hmm... What could it be?|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Geggle}} explains that he noticed a portion of the cliffs in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands}} appeared to be man-made.<ref>{{Cite|I happened to notice that a section of the cliff face in the distance looks almost man-made.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> He wishes that he could investigate, but he is unable to make such a journey on his own.<ref>{{Cite|My smart man, tell me... You can see it too, yes? I would take a closer look if I could get there on my own.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} begins.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} investigates the area {{Term|BotW|Geggle}} spoke of, he will find a mural painted along the cliff face. The mural depicts several {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} bolts, all of which are pointing towards a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} with a {{Term|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}}, it will activate, causing {{Term|BotW|Keeha Yoog Shrine|link}} to emerge from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|When you shot a shock arrow at the center of the image, an ancient shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After it surfaces, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.{{Note|It is possible to unearth {{Term|BotW|Keeha Yoog Shrine}} without receiving the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} from {{Term|BotW|Geggle}}. In this case, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|display=Quest}} will be automatically completed once {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives it.}}
