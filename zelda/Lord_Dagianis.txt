'''Lord Dagianis''' is a character in the {{SS|Himekawa}}.

=={{SS|Himekawa|-}}==
Lord Dagianis lived in [[Hylia's Realm|the Land of Hylia]] during the [[Era of the Goddess Hylia]], hundreds of years, if not thousands, before the events of {{SS}}. He deceived his own people, saying they didn't need a powerful hero to defeat the Demon King {{Term|SS|Demise|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It was you––my own people––who said you had no need for a powerful hero.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=006}}</ref> and framed a knight named [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|Link]], leading to the latter's imprisonment.

Four years later, after Lord Dagianis is killed by {{Term|SS|Demise}}, whose forces are marching on the Land of Hylia,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Lord Dagianis, the man who framed you, has been killed by the Demon King. Please come out into the light of day. The Demon King's army will soon be upon us.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=006}}</ref> the people decide to free Link from his prison.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Our little misunderstanding has been resolved. Your premonition of danger was correct. We all understand that now. You are the only one who drive off the Demon King and protect Hylia!|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=006}}</ref>

After Link's death, [[The Goddess Hylia|Hylia]] reveals that Link's tribulation and imprisonment were a trial from herself to test his spirit, a trial he overcame, proving his possession of an unbreakable spirit.
