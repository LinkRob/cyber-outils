'''Korekore''' is an herb introduced in {{TMC|Himekawa}}.

==Location and Uses==
Korekore never makes a visual appearance, but it gets mentioned once during the Bonus Chapter by [[Ezlo]]. He tells [[Vaati]] to find it and that it grows on the mountain beyond the [[Minish Woods]]. After searching for days after the herb, Vaati is told by other [[Minish]] that it doesn't grow on mountains. This eventually makes him return to Ezlo, where he finds out that it was a lesson to teach Vaati that you can't believe in everything you hear.
