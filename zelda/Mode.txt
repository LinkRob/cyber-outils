{{CoH}} has several {{Term/Store|Mode|Modes|Series, CoH|Plural}} available, which can be chosen from at file creation.

==List of Modes==
{{Term|CoH|Story Mode|link}}: ''Play through the story. The intended mode for a first playthrough.''
:{{Term|Series|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Zelda|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} can be unlocked in this mode. {{User:StardustAlpha-fduser/Template:CoHNeedDLC3}} Skull Kid can also be unlocked.


{{Term|CoH|Octavo's Ode (Mode)|link}}: ''Play through an alternate story, featuring Octavo.''
:{{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} is the only playable character in this mode, unless co-op is used. Functionally identical to {{Term|CoH|Single Character Mode}} as Octavo.


{{User:StardustAlpha-fduser/Template:CoHNeedDLC3}} {{Term|CoH|Symphony of the Mask (Mode)|link}}: ''Play through an alternate story, featuring Skull Kid.''
:Takes place in the {{Term|Series|Hyrule|link}}'s future. {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid|link}} is the only playable character in this mode, unless co-op is used. Functionally identical to {{Term|CoH|Single Character Mode}} as Skull Kid.


{{Term|CoH|Single-Character Mode|link}}: ''Play the entire game without swapping characters.''
:Can choose any one character to play as. Can choose two if starting in co-op.


{{Term|CoH|Dungeon Mode|link}}: ''Replace the overworld with a single large dungeon. For players looking for the classic NecroDancer experience.''
:The dungeon contains 21 floors across 5 zones. Can choose a character the same way as in {{Term|CoH|Single Character Mode}}. Before each boss, a portal is present, which leads back to the start of the zone.


{{User:StardustAlpha-fduser/Template:CoHNeedDLC1}} {{Term|CoH|All-Characters Mode|link}}: ''Play through the game in Dungeon Mode with all characters consecutively. Not that the save file will be removed upon completion.''
:Since {{Term|CoH|Shadow Link|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Shadow Zelda|link}} are so similar to their non-shadow counterparts, you can choose between playing as the shadows or their non-shadow counterparts.
:{{User:StardustAlpha-fduser/Template:CoHNeedDLC3}} You'll also need to play as {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid}}.


{{Term|CoH|Daily Challenge|link}}: ''Play in Permadeath Mode with a random character on a new world each day. You get only one chance per day!''
:Only {{Term|Series|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Zelda|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} can appear in this mode.


{{User:StardustAlpha-fduser/Template:CoHNeedDLC3}} {{Term|CoH|Puzzle Mode|link}}: ''Play through a puzzle-centric dungeon.''
:This mode is just the {{Term|CoH|Temple of Brainstorms|link}} with an additional floor, a boss key to find, and with {{Plural|Series|Piece of Heart|link}} instead of {{Plural|CoH|Heart|link}} after clearing an enemy room.


{{User:StardustAlpha-fduser/Template:CoHNeedDLC3}} {{Term|CoH|Arena Mode|link}}: ''Play an infinite arena mode, where the enemies get harder and harder. If you're defeated, you'll have to start over!''
:An endless version of the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Arena|link}}. Every six floors, a boss (other than {{Term|CoH|Synthrova|link}}) will appear.


----


{{Term|CoH|Fixed-Beat Mode|link}}: ''Play the game without moving to the beat. Enable this if you often find yourself missing beats.''
:When on, the game becomes entirely turn-based, and the rhythm aspect is entirely removed. Can be toggled at any time from Options > Gameplay. Not recommended for co-op.


{{Term|CoH|Double-Time Mode|link}}: ''Double the speed of the beat.''
:Has very little effect if {{Term|CoH|Fixed-Beat Mode}} is on.


{{Term|CoH|Permadeath Mode|link}}: ''A more challenging experience. Once you get a game over, the game ends permanently.''
:Some additional items, like the {{Term|CoH|Power Glove|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Flippers|link}}, are shuffled around like other key items. Enemies and bosses will be harder variations of themselves. For more details, see the main article for {{Term|CoH|Permadeath Mode|link}}.


{{Term|CoH|Mystery Mode|link}}: ''The identity of all enemies and items is a mystery!''
:The identity of items are revealed when you pick them up.
