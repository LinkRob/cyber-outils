==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Octorok Tentacles are dropped by all types {{Plural|BotW|Octorok|link}} when defeated. Like other {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, they can't be used for [[Cooking]], but can still be combined with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] or used for Light Blue [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

Five Octorok Tentacles are needed to upgrade the [[Snow Boots]] to their first tier.

*Total for '''Snow Boots''': 5 (5, 0, 0, 0)
