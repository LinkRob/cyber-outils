The {{Term/Store|Giant Horse||Series, BotW}} is a [[Creature]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Giant Horse}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Giant Horse}}
The Giant Horse is a massive [[Wild Horse]] with charcoal-colored fur. Its mane and tail are a distinctly orange color. Registering it at a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} equips this {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Saddle|link}} that also feature tufts of orange fur; its {{Term|BotW|Bridle}} and {{Term|BotW|Saddle}} cannot be changed. It is found in the [[Taobab Grassland]] among a herd of regularly-sized {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must capture the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}} in order to complete the "[[Hunt for the Giant Horse]]" [[Side Quest]], given by {{Term|BotW|Straia|link}} at the [[Mounted Archery Camp]]. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must ride past two {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} in order to return to the Mounted Archery Camp with the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}} cannot gallop and has poor speed, but it is one of the strongest steeds available as compensation. Though its speed is ranked poorly, it does travel considerably faster than other horses of the same speed stat as it takes much larger steps.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}} bears a resemblance to [[Ganondorf's Steed]], which was also a large horse with black fur and an orange mane.
*The {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}}, the {{Term|BotW|White Horse|link}}, the [[Lord of the Mountain]], and {{Term|BotW|Patricia|link}} are the only individuals to get their own unique entries in the [[Hyrule Compendium]] separate from their own species.
*Because registered horses from BotW transfer over to TotK, the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse}} can be brought into the latter game. However, it cannot be used with any utility saddles and cannot have its gear or mane changed, just like the previous game.
