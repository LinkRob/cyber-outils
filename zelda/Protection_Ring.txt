The {{Term/Store|Protection Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Protection Ring<br/>Damage taken is always one Heart|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Protection Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be obtained by getting a perfect score at the platinum level of the [[Goron Dance]] minigame during the past. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it converts all damage received into a single [[Heart]] of damage.<ref name="Description"/> The Protection Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
