{{Term/Store|Roasted Tree Nut|Roasted Tree Nuts|Series, BotW}} are [[Item]]s in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Roasted Tree Nut}}<br/>A tree seed roasted to amplify its fragrance. Gives more hearts than the raw variety.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Roasted Tree Nuts are made by exposing a [[Chickaloo Tree Nut]] to [[Fire]]. These nuts have a better fragrance than the uncooked variety.<ref name="Inventory"/> Eating one restores one [[Heart]] to [[Link]]'s [[Life Gauge]].
