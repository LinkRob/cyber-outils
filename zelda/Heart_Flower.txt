{{Term/Store|Heart Flower|Heart Flowers|Series, SS|plural}} are objects in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Heart Flowers, grown in Skyloft, help recover health. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=64 }}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SS|Heart Flower}} are plants that grow {{Plural|SS|Heart|link}}. {{Plural|SS|Heart}} are automatically plucked from {{Plural|SS|Heart Flower}} whenever {{Term|SS|Link|link}} comes into contact with a {{Term|SS|Heart Flower|display=Flower}}. {{Plural|SS|Heart Flower}} do not bloom in {{Term|SS|Hero Mode|link}}, unless {{Term|SS|Link}} has the {{Term|SS|Heart Medal|link}} equipped.

{{Plural|SS|Heart Flower}} can be found throughout {{Term|SS|Skyloft|link}}.
