{{Term/Store|Pillars of Levia||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Pillars of Levia'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Pillars of Levia}} are a group of eight large tors in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}} to the south of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} can be found at the top of one of the southern tors.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find three {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} on and around the {{Term|BotW|Pillars of Levia}}. One can be found at the top of one of the central tors, another at the top of the eastern most tor, and the third at the three {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}} trees that lie to the east.
