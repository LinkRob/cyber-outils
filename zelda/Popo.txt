{{Term/Store|Popo|Popo|Series, ALttP, FSA, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 199 {{Exp Game|ALttP, FSA, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Popo}} are sentient masses of tentacles that move along the ground and will harm {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} if he touches them. Their movement is shambling and without specific direction. {{Plural|ALttP|Popo}} will not actively seek {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} out and will often move in a different direction entirely. They appear in large swarms on occasion. {{Plural|ALttP|Popo}} can easily be defeated with a single hit of most weapons, and often leave behind {{Plural|ALttP|Rupee|link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, {{Term|ALBW|Popo}} appear similarly to their appearance in {{ALttP|-}}, except {{Plural|ALBW|Popo}} will home in on {{Term|ALBW|Link}} and follow him when he gets near. Whenever {{Term|ALBW|Link}} touches one, he takes damage. {{Plural|ALBW|Red Popo|link}}, appearing only in {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock|link}}, have more health and deal more damage.
