{{Term/Store|Rampaging Malice||HWAoC}} is a level 58 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rampaging Malice|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Rampaging Malice}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}'s objective is to capture four {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} within eight minutes. The first {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, as well as a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}} that must be defeated to capture it. As {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} walks on the path between the first two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}, he will encounter {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, and magma bombs will rain down on him. The second {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is contains a {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}}, and a {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos}}. The third {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos}}, a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian}}, and the fourth contains a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} and some {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos}}. Outside of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} there are {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese}} scattered around the map as well. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured.
