{{Term/Store|Maggie|Maggies|Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}} works as a librarian at the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref name="Walk"/> After the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}} opens and {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} attempts to speak with {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}} will tell him about the three overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}} that were checked out by {{Term|TMC|Julietta|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen|link}}. When {{Term|TMC|Link}} returns them, she gladly asks {{Term|TMC|Sturgeon|link}} to re-shelve them, granting {{Term|TMC|Link}} access to {{Term|TMC|Librari}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now I can finally get that bookshelf on the second floor in order! Hey, {{Color|TMC Green|Sturgeon}}! Books to shelve!!|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Maggie}}'s Spanish and German name, Dolores, means "sorrowful", befitting her frugal life in the beginning of the game. In contrast, Mila's Spanish and German name means "fortunate."
