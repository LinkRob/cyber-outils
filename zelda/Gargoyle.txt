{{Term/Store|Gargoyle|Gargoyles|Series, CoH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Green Gargoyle|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The '''Gargoyle Statue''' is an object that appears in the [[Village of Outcasts]] in {{ALttP}}. The Weathercock in [[Kakariko Village]] in the [[Light World]] is replaced by the Gargoyle Statue in the [[Dark World]] and it's also the entrance to the fourth dungeon, {{Term|ALttP|Thieves' Town (Dungeon)|link}}. To open the entrance, Link has to pull the prongs of the trident which the statue is holding on to.<ref>{{Cite|Do you know about the Gargoyle statue in the village? People say they can hear a girl calling for help from under the statue. Isn't that a strange story...|Hylian|ALttP}}</ref>

==={{OoA|-}}===
The '''Imp''' statues that give you -50 points in the [[Shooting Gallery]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|{{OoA|-}}]] bears some resemblance to a Gargoyle Statue.

==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
[[File:Gargoyle OoA Manga.jpg|right|250px]]
During Chapter 6, ''Overthrowing Veran'', [[Veran]] brings several Gargoyle Statues to life when possessing [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]]. They possess the ability to fly and breathe fire, however, Link defeats them without much struggle.
{{Noncanon|end}}

==={{TFoE|-}}===
{{Canonicity/Start|TFoE}}Gargoyles are flying enemies most common in [[Ganon's Lair]]. Unlike most flying enemies that come in swarms, they take two hits instead of one. 

Gargoyles can be frozen briefly by ringing the [[Bell]] in {{TFoE|-}}.
{{Canonicity/End}}

==={{TWoG|-}}===
{{Canonicity/Start|TWoG}}
Gargoyles are fairly common and weak enemies in {{TWoG}}. As with most other enemies, they have a tendency of spawning frequently. In the [[Hanyu Forest|Hanyu]] and [[Nokani Forest]]s, their green coloration allows them to easily blend in the tree tops' leaves, making them hard to spot. They can be frozen briefly by using the [[Flute]].
{{Canonicity/End}}
