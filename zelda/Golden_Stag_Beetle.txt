==Location and Uses==
[[File:Stag Beetle (M).png|left]]
[[File:Stag Beetle (F).png|left|47px]]
The male Stag Beetle can be found on a tree near a fork in the road north of {{Term|TP|Hyrule Castle Town|link}}. The female Stag Beetle is located above a bombable wall. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].
