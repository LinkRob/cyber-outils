==Location and Uses==
Ruby Daggers are a type of {{Term|CoH|Dagger|link}} that deal 1 damage, and automatically restore half a {{Term|CoH|Heart|link}} after defeating five enemies.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}, and can only be acquired either after they infuse the Dagger with a {{Term|CoH|Ruby|link}} at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, or if either them or {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} opens the blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an already-made one in {{Term|CoH|Lake Hylia|link}}.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Ruby Short Sword|link}}.
