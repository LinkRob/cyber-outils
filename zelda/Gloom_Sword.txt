The '''Gloom Sword''' {{Exp Game|TotK}} is a [[Weapons in Tears of the Kingdom|weapon]] introduced in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''. It's most notably the sword of [[Ganondorf]] himself, which is also wielded by his darkest servants such as [[Phantom Ganon]].

== Characteristics ==
Signature weapon of the [[Ganon|Demon King Ganondorf]], the '''Gloom Sword''' is arguably the most powerful [[Link: One-Handed Weapons|One-Handed Weapon]] featured in ''Tears of the Kingdom''. But this comes at a price for one who wishes to carry it: due to its wicked nature and its affiliation to the Evil himself, the Gloom Sword drains Link's health whenever he attacks with it, making it dangerous to use carelessly. Merely equipping the weapon, or swinging at air, carries no negative effects.

Although Link will lose health every time he uses this cursed sword, he will never die because of it. Once Link's health is down to only one heart, the gloom effect stops automatically, but will resume as soon as the player gains additional health. These unique characteristics make the Gloom Sword a typical gift-and-curse item: while inflicting massive damage to your foes, it will also make you more and more vulnerable. The more enemies you eliminate with the Gloom Sword, the more vulnerable you will become. As with the other Gloom weapons, the Sword cannot be upgraded nor repaired by [[Rock Octorok]]s.

Ganondorf unsheathes his Gloom Sword when about to duel Link nearing the end of the game while vowing to have Link witness the birth of the Demon King's new world.
