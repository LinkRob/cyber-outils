==Features and Overview==
==={{LA|NS|-}}===
{{Term|LANS|Dampé's Shack}} appears in place of the {{Term|LADX|Photographer}}'s house from {{LA|DX}} at {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Heights|link}} in {{LA|NS}}. Inside his {{Term|LANS|Dampé's Shack|display=Shack}}, {{Term|LANS|Dampé|link}} constructs {{Plural|LANS|Chamber|link}} for {{Plural|LANS|Chamber Dungeon|link}}, which he allows {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} to arrange and explore.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Term|OoT3D|Dampé's Shack}} is located in the [[Graveyard|Kakariko Graveyard]] in {{OoT|-}}. [[Young Link]] can only enter it during Dampé's [[Heart-Pounding Gravedigging Tour]], which is from 6 P.M. to 9 P.M; otherwise, Dampé will tell the young hero that he is either sleeping or that the gravedigging tour is over. The only interactive object inside the hut is the {{Term|OoT3D|Gravekeeper's Diary|link}}, which mentions how Dampé found a treasure that stretches and goes "boing!".

Once Link turns into an adult, he will be able to enter the hut at any time. Upon reading the {{Term|OoT3D|Gravekeeper's Diary|display=Diary}} again, the gravekeeper prompts the reader to seek out his [[?|grave]] and enter it, whereupon he will let them have his treasure: the [[Hookshot]].<ref>{{Cite|Whoever reads this, please enter my grave. I will let you have my stretching, shrinking keepsake. I'm waiting for you. --Dampé|Gravekeeper's Diary|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
Appearing again in {{MM}}, the shack is found in the northern portion of the [[Ikana Graveyard]]. It can only be accessed from beneath the grave accessible on the night of the [[Third Day]]. 

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, the shack is found within the Graveyard in the northern [[The Swamp|Swamp]]. Here, Dampé tells the [[Links]] about the [[Dark Mirror]] which a dark tribe was sealed away in long ago.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Dampe's House}} is located in the northeastern area of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Dampé|link}} lives within it, and he will give {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard Key|link}} to access the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard|link}}, and by extension the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, when {{Term|TMC|Link}} comes and speaks with him.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if the king has called you, I can't see any reason not to let you in... Here, take this {{Color|TMC Red|graveyard key}}.|Dampé|TMC}}</ref>
