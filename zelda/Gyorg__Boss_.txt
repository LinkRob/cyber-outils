{{Term/Store|Gyorg||Series, MM, MM3D}} (pronounced {{IPA|ɡjɔːɹɡ}}, {{Respell|gyorg}}){{Fact}} is the [[Boss]] of the [[Great Bay Temple]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 200 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> It is Gyorg itself who imprisoned the third of the [[Four Giants]] and is responsible for the waters of the [[Great Bay Coast]] becoming polluted and dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|There's a rumor that the ocean is getting weird 'cause there's trouble off shore at Great Bay Temple.|Tijo|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's dangerous at night, so be especially careful.|Soldier|MM}}</ref> This also causes an abnormal climate shift in the [[Great Bay]] area.<ref>{{Cite|The recent abnormal weather has caused the ocean temperature to rise in this region.|Lake Scientist|MM}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, its head and pectoral fins have bony armor, and its fingers are elongated.

Gyorg attempts to knock [[Link]] off the platform by ramming into it. If Link falls into the water, it will aggressively swim toward him, and if the young [[Hero of Time]] is too close, the fish monster will swallow him, chew Link up and then spit him out, causing two [[Hearts]] of damage. Occasionally, Gyorg will leap out of the water and soar across the platform to hit Link. When Gyorg is about to attack, a rushing sound can be heard. Bubbles will also appear above it, allowing Link to anticipate Gyorg's attacks. Partway through the battle, Gyorg will spawn smaller versions of itself to distract Link in the water. If Link then falls in the water, they will seek him immediately and attempt to bite him repeatedly in a manner similar to [[wikipedia:Piranha|piranhas]], making it quite difficult to escape back to the platform before Gyorg attacks. [[Zora Link]]'s electric barrier can be used to easily defeat them.

To defeat Gyorg, Link must use either his [[Bow]] or his [[Boomerang]]-fins as [[Zora Link]] to stun it. He must then dive in the water to attack Gyorg to cause damage. Zora Link's electrical barrier is particularly effective at this stage. Alternatively, Zora Link can stun Gyorg in the water using his fins or his electrical attack. Once Gyorg is defeated, it will shrink in size until there is nothing left. Link will receive a [[Heart Container]] and [[Gyorg's Remains]]. This will also free the Giant who was imprisoned in Gyorg's body.<ref>{{Cite|You've seized Gyorg's Remains! You have just freed the innocent spirit that this dark mask had kept imprisoned within the body of evil Gyorg.|N/A|MM}}</ref> Afterwards, Great Bay will return to normal.<ref>{{Cite|I don't understand what happened, but it seems the ocean has reclaimed its serenity.|Lake Scientist|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, Gyorg now has an eye inside its mouth which Link must attack. In order to do so, Gyorg must be stunned by attacking it with [[Arrow]]s while it is swimming on the water's surface. After taking some damage, Gyorg may briefly surface and grab the platform in the center to directly charge at Link. If Gyorg continues to take damage, it will eventually bash the platform in the middle, causing it to sink. Link must then swim as a [[Zora]] while avoiding Gyorg who tries to inhale him. During this phase, Gyorg will release fish from its mouth which will attack Link. [[Spike]]s will also pop out of the ground, and Zora Link needs to hit their chains so Gyorg inhales one of them (similar to defeating the Dodongo with bombs).<ref>[http://www.nintendo.co.jp/3ds/ajrj/key/key03.html Official {{MM3D|-}} Japanese website: Fight & Puzzle]</ref> When defeated, the water in the room will now drain away, with Gyorg flopping around while shrinking as aforementioned.

If the [[Fierce Deity Link]] form is used, Gyorg would become far easier to defeat, as [[Sword Beam|Sword Beams]] from the [[Fierce Deity Sword]] would bypass going to the water, allowing hits while standing on the platform.

==Trivia==
*Gyorg can also be stunned by having a {{Term|MM3D|Jar|link}} thrown at it, or with a precisely-timed [[Rolling|Roll]].
*Gyorg's intro cutscene is similar [[Morpha]]'s from {{OoT}}. Both begin from the boss's perspective as it moves through the water, and end with the boss emerging from the water behind {{Term|Series|Link|link}}.
*In the 3DS remake, after Gyorg is defeated and the water drains out of the room, a separate room can be seen attached to one side of the boss chamber, explaining how the water was able to disappear. This room is blocked off by a metal fence and cannot be accessed.

==Nomenclature==
Gyorg's name might come from the Japanese Kanji, 魚, meaning "fish".  One of several ways to pronounce this Kanji is "gyo".
