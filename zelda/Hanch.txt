{{Term/Store|Hanch||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
Hanch is {{Term|TPHD|Beth|link}}'s father and {{Term|TPHD|Sera|link}}'s husband, who reside in [[Ordon Village]]. At the start of the game, he stands staring up at the [[tree]] above [[Fado (Twilight Princess)|Fado's]] house, which is infested with [[Bee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, some Ordon bees built themselves a fine nest up there, and I was thinking about knocking it down...|Hanch|TP}}</ref> He tries to knock down the hive and extract [[Bee Larva]] to please his wife who has been upset ever since their cat disappeared.<ref>{{Cite|Our cat hasn't come home since yesterday, so the wife's in a bad mood. At the very least, I need to bring something home to her...|Hanch|TP}}</ref> If [[Link]] stays around Hanch for a few seconds after speaking to him, he will throw a rock at the hive, causing bees to swarm and chase him into the water. Link can help him out knocking down the hive by climbing some [[vine]]s or with either the [[Slingshot]] or a [[hawk]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm telling you, you climb up there, those bees are going to get perturbed, m'boy...|Hanch|TP}}</ref>

After his daughter is kidnapped by [[King Bulblin]]'s troupe, he sends a hawk after [[Wolf Link]] whenever he comes close, believing Wolf Link to be his daughter's kidnapper.<ref>{{Cite|My... My daughter... Return her! [...] And TAKE THIS!|Hanch|TP}}</ref> To obtain the [[Ordon Shield]], Wolf Link must get up on the roof of [[Sera's Sundries]] and scare Hanch into jumping into the water once again.
