The {{Term/Store|Stone Tower Temple||Series, MM, MM3D}} is the fourth [[Dungeon]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 150}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Temple==
The [[Dungeon]] is at the top of the [[Stone Tower]] in [[Ikana Canyon]]. [[Link]] can access this Temple after learning the "[[Elegy of Emptiness]]", which invokes lifeless clones of Link so that slacked switches can be pressed and held. This is the only way to activate the platforms that allow the young hero to make his way to the top, where the lifeless clones are needed again to gain access to the entrance itself.

==Themes and Navigation==
Unlike the other three Dungeons in the game, the Stone Tower Temple requires the use of all four of Link's forms: [[Hylian]], [[Deku Link|Deku]], [[Goron Link|Goron]], and [[Zora Link|Zora]]. This Dungeon is also unique in the fact that, thematically, it does not reflect the current situation of the region of [[Termina]] where it is located. Ikana Canyon is experiencing a dark age because of the rise of spectra, undead creatures and other beasts; in contrast, Stone Tower Temple does not even show the depressing atmosphere like the outside conditions. Instead, it is a light-themed Dungeon that features a potpourri of other elements, including fire, water, and even sky.
[[File:Stone Tower Temple Inverted.png|left|thumb|The first room of the Temple in its inverted state]]
Hylian Link is useful to solve puzzles pertaining to light, as he is the only form that can use the [[Mirror Shield]]. Nearly midway through the Dungeon, he will encounter the [[Garo Master]], whose defeat will grant him the [[Light Arrow]]s. These mystical Arrows can be used to turn the Dungeon, and the whole of Stone Tower, upside down, by firing one into the [[Red Emblem]] fastened to the wall just outside of Stone Tower Temple.<ref>{{Cite|To think I could be defeated... Although my rival, you were spectacular. I shall take my bow by opening my heart and revealing my wisdom... If you shoot that which releases the sacred [[Light Arrow|golden light]] into the blood-stained, red emblem outside the temple... it shall rearrange things, in which the earth is born in the heavens and the moon is born on the earth. Do not forget these words... Die I shall, leaving no corpse. That is the law of us Garo.|Garo Master|MM}}</ref> Each room flips on its own, so for example, the rooms on the east side of the temple remain on the east side. Other than that, the Light Arrows are also used to twist some rooms, as well as to defeat certain enemies whose weak points are evidently marked by emblems not unlike that of the Temple's entrance. They are also used to remove [[Sun Block]]s that can only otherwise be dealt with with the Mirror Shield, and even then there needs to be a light source.

As for the other forms, Goron Link is useful to cross lava rooms, and his brute force will also be called for use. Zora Link needs to swim through a large body of water, and while Deku Link is already required to fly through a chasm prior to the Garo Master's room, he becomes entirely vital when the Temple is upside down, as most of the rooms will turn into large chasms due to the new position of the sky. The young hero's alternate forms also come into play in rooms where the "Elegy of Emptiness" is used to press slacked switches. The [[Key#Boss Keys|Boss Key]] is guarded by [[Gomess]], who can only be defeated with Light Arrows and then the [[Sword]]. A corridor containing a tougher than usual [[Eyegore]] is found on the way, and Link must defeat this Eyegore to continue. The creature's defeat reveals a chest containing the [[Giant's Mask]]. Once this is obtained, Link can continue until the boss room is reached. Inside the room, he will find himself in a desert, which is the home of the temple's boss, [[Twinmold]]. Defeating Twinmold yields a [[Heart Container]] and the final quest mask, [[Twinmold's Remains]].

If Link collects all 15 [[Stray Fairy|Stray Fairies]], he will be rewarded with the [[Great Fairy's Sword]], a powerful blade just like the [[Biggoron's Sword]].

In {{MM3D}}, the Giant's Mask is not obtained after defeating the second Eyegore. Instead, it is obtained during the fight with Twinmold.

==Small Keys==
*There are four small keys. One in the basement, one in the bridge room, one in the water room (Inverted) and one in the west corridor (Inverted)

==Trivia==
*It is possible to complete the Stone Tower Temple before starting the [[Great Bay Temple]] by reaching the top of Ikana Canyon  without the [[Ice Arrow]]. In the Nintendo 64 version, this requires a precise [[Hookshot]] shot to cross the river without freezing the [[Octorok|Octoroks]]. In the 3DS version, the Hookshot method is no longer possible but the player can now cross the river by using a bomb to fling Link across.
*It is possible to finish the Stone Tower Temple by using the same small keys twice if the Song of Time is played after getting the Light Arrows. 
*When the Dungeon is flipped upside, the room is not rotated unlike outside of the Dungeon and the single rooms inside that must be flipped on their own.
*The Stone Tower Temple shares some design elements with the {{Term|ALttP|Desert Palace|link}} from {{ALttP}}, as the Dungeons are located in similar environments, have near-identical bosses, and share architectural features like entrances disguised as the mouths of giant statues.
*In the dark room with the [[Armos]] statues below the area that branches off to the halls containing a [[Death Armos]] squad, there are two Armos Statues that cannot be moved, yet moving near one makes the "Grab" command appear.
*In the entrance room, the green vortex graphical effect from the boss room warp pad is mistakenly left visible in the sky. This was fixed in the 3DS version
*There is a large statue within the Temple that, when flipped, resembles {{MM|Object}}.
*In the water room on the eastern wing it is possible to get a [[Stray Fairy]] using the [[Ice Arrow|Ice Arrows]]
