{{Term/Store|Whistling Hill||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Whistling Hill'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Whistling Hill is located in [[Hyrule Field]], northwest of [[Batrea Lake]] and adjacent to the [[Bottomless Swamp]]. The [[Riverside Stable]] and [[Wahgo Katta Shrine]] are situated at the southeast base of the Hill.

A [[Korok]] can be found by shooting a [[Pot]] inside a nook in one of the trees at the top of the Hill. Another can be found by lifting a [[Rock]] on top of the largest tree at the base of the Hill in the southwest.
