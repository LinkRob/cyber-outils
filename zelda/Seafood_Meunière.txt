{{Term/Store|Seafood Meunière|Seafood Meunières|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Seafood Meunières by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|Series|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, {{Term|Series|Goat Butter|link}}, and any seafood together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The only exceptions to this are {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, as including these {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} will result in different meals. The default version of Seafood Meunière can be made either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}}, which offer no status effects, or by using multiple types of seafood with differing effects. Eating these meals will heal some of [[Link|Link's]] {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The secret ingredient to Seafood Meunière is a lot of love.<ref name="Inventory"/>

When making Seafood Meunière, Link can make the following variations, based on both the type of seafood used as well as additional ingredients:

* '''Hearty Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, these meals will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}, or other energizing ingredients. By eating them, Link can refill part of his {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.

* '''Enduring Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely refreshed and overfilled if he eats them.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using hasty ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or other ingredients. If Link consumes these meals, he will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other spicy ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, or other mighty ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or other tough ingredients. Link's defensive abilities will be increased if he eats these.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. These meals will provide a boost to Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Seafood Meunière''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Seafood Meunière}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Rich butter flanks fresh seafood. The secret ingredient is lots and lots of love.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other chilly ingredients. Consuming them will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
