The {{Term/Store|Father's Letter||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an item in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Father's Letter is received from [[Medli]] after the [[Rito Chieftain]] asks [[Link]] to give it to his son, [[Komali]].<ref>{{Cite|I have something I wish for you to give my son.|Rito Chieftain|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A young girl named Medli is holding it for me. Would you find her and take it?|Rito Chieftain|TWWHD}}</ref> Once the Letter is obtained, Medli tells Link where Komali's room is.<ref>{{Cite|The room way in the back on the first floor is Prince Komali's room. You have to go down a couple steps to get there.|Medli|TWWHD}}</ref> When given to the prince, it is revealed that the Letter is a message telling him to be brave, and that Link himself is mentioned in it.<ref>{{Cite|A letter? From my father? Oh, sure, telling me to be brave is easy enough for him... You're in this letter, too, you know...|Prince Komali|TWWHD}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The Hylian on the letter translates to "Komorihe" (To Komori), which is Prince Komali's Japanese name.
