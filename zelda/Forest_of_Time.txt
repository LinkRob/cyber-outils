The {{Term/Store|Forest of Time|Forests of Time|Series, OoA, BotW}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= Dark Horse Books|page= 255 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'''Forest of Time'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{OoA|-}}===
The {{Term|OoA|Forest of Time}} is the region in {{Term|OoA|Labrynna|link}} where {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} begins his quest. The first person he meets is {{Term|OoA|Impa|link}}, who was sent by {{Term|OoA|Princess Zelda|link}} to look for {{Term|OoA|Nayru (Oracle)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda asked me to find a singer named Nayru.|Impa|OoA}}</ref> It is in this area that {{Term|OoA|Link}} meets {{Term|OoA|Nayru (Oracle)}}, {{Term|OoA|Ralph|link}}, and {{Term|OoA|Veran|link}}, the antagonist of the game, for the first time. {{Term|OoA|Link}} also witnesses the possession of {{Term|OoA|Nayru (Oracle)}} by {{Term|OoA|Veran}} here.

{{Term|OoA|Nayru's House|link}} is also located in a {{Term|OoA|Tree|link}} in the northeastern corner of the {{Term|OoA|Forest of Time}}. During his quest {{Term|OoA|Link}} will pass through this area to reach {{Term|OoA|Yoll Graveyard|link}} and {{Term|OoA|Rolling Ridge|link}}.

In the southwestern corner of the {{Term|OoA|Forest of Time|display=Forest}} is a rocky area where {{Term|OoA|Link}} will meet {{Term|OoA|Tingle|link}}. However, to reach {{Term|OoA|Tingle}}, {{Term|OoA|Link}} will require {{Term|OoA|Ricky|link}}'s assistance, as his path is blocked by holes and cliffs. {{Term|OoA|Tingle}} will give {{Term|OoA|Link}} the {{Term|OoA|Island Chart|link}} which is necessary if {{Term|OoA|Link}} is to reach {{Term|OoA|Crescent Island|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As a sign of our friendship, Tingle will give you his chart that shows the way to the secret island!|Tingle|OoA}}</ref> Later on, {{Term|OoA|Tingle}} will upgrade {{Term|OoA|Link}}'s {{Term|OoA|Seed Satchel|link}}, allowing him to carry 50 {{Plural|OoA|Mystical Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tingle the fairy can make it so your satchel can carry more seeds!|Tingle|OoA}}</ref> In a {{Term|OoA|Linked Game|link}} of {{OoS}}, {{Term|OoA|Link}} will receive a secret which can be told to {{Term|OoA|Tingle}}, who will then upgrade his {{Term|OoA|Seed Satchel|display=Satchel}} again, allowing him to carry a maximum of 99 {{Plural|OoA|Mystical Seed|display=Seeds}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Forest of Time}} is a wooded area northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} in {{BotW}}. A monster-infested camp is situated toward the south in a small clearing. A {{Term|BotW|Chuchu|link}} hides inside one of two {{Term|BotW|Barrel|link}} at the camp.

A wooden {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing 10 {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} sits next to a pair of crates within the camp, adjacent to two decayed {{Plural|BotW|Wagon|link}}. A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a bundle of five {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}} sits atop a platform in a clearing in the south of the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Time|display=Forest}}. A {{Term|BotW|Barrel}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Roasted Bird Drumstick|link}} and two {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel|link}} can also be found on the platform. A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}} can be found atop a tower at the northern base of the small plateau at the southern portion of the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Time|display=Forest}}.
