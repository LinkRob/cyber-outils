'''''The Legend of Zelda Artwork Collection 2''''' is an artbook released in 2014 only in Japan. It is a sequel to ''[[The History of Zelda Art Collection]]'' released in 2010. It was released with the February 2014 issue of Nintendo Dream.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= The February 2014 issue of Nintendo Dream comes with a Zelda artbook.|author= Manveri|published= January 19, 2014|retrieved= October 6, 2015|url= http://www.videogamemm.com/forums/viewtopic.php?f=5&t=20860|title= Zelda Artwork Collection 2 Book|site= Video Game Memorabilia Museum Forums|type=}}</ref>

==Features==
The book features the same artworks included in ''The History of Zelda Art Collection'', spanning from {{TLoZ}} to {{ST}}. It also features new artworks from games released after the first book, including {{SS}}, {{OoT3D}}, {{TWWHD}}, and {{ALBW}}. The book contains 132 pages, compared to the 99 pages of the first book.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= It's a sequel to their 'The History of Zelda Art Collection' book from 2009, and from what I can gather, seems to be a reprint of the same book but with the newer games (Skyward Sword, Ocarina of Time 3D, Wind Waker HD, A Link Between Worlds) added to the end.
