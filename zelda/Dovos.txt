{{Term/Store|Dovos||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Dovos}}, and he is a great lover of food. A self-styled gourmet, he is often found at the bazaar's restaurant.
}}

Dovos is a resident of [[Skyloft]]. He is a lover of food who sits at one of the tables in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}} during the day, awaiting [[Piper]]'s food. After [[Link]] wins the [[Wing Ceremony]], Dovos congratulates him and reminds the young hero that he can find relevant tips by pressing the Wii Remote's 2 Button.<ref>{{Cite|So you're on your way to becoming a knight now, right? Congratulations! But I'm sure there's still plenty you don't know. Don't forget to press {{Icon|SS 2}} if you need a pointer or two.|Dovos|SS}}</ref> If Link sits at the same table Dovos is, he will tell Link that Piper's cooking is delicious but takes a long time to prepare.<ref>{{Cite|You looking to get your grub on? The food here is sooooo good! You gotta be patient, though. It seems like you wait forever for anything around here.|Dovos|SS}}</ref> At night, Dovos can be found on the [[Lumpy Pumpkin]]'s second floor.

Later, Dovos will tell Link of a rumor about a sobbing woman in the [[Knight Academy]].<ref>{{Cite|I've been hearing a weird rumor. Apparently, after {{Color|SS Red|nightfall}}, people have been hearing a woman sobbing in the {{Color|SS Red|dormitory}}. I tell you, it gives even a big guy like me the creeps.|Dovos|SS}}</ref> If Link had already passed the restroom in the Knight Academy and heard [[Phoeni]] calling for paper from the bathroom, Dovos will sympathize and say that he has been in that situation before.<ref>{{Cite|What? Calling out for toilet paper while you're stuck in the restroom? Yikes, that's rough. I should know--I've been there before.|Dovos|SS}}</ref> After Link has seen the source of the sobbing, Dovos misinterprets it as someone's living hand reaching out of the bathroom stall for someone to hand them toilet paper, instead of a severed, ghostly hand.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? A hand? Reaching out of the stall? That...that's one way of going about it, I guess.|Dovos|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Dovos's name is derived from {{Wp|dove}}, a bird.
