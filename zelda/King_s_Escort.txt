{{Term/Store|King's Escort||HWAoC}} is a Level 52 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|King's Escort|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|King's Escort}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} except for {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Royal Guard Uniform|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to escort {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} to a Targeted Position within eight minutes. As {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} moves closer to the Targeted Position, a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Hinox|link}} will spawn to prevent the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} from succeeding. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} reaches the Targeted Position.
