'''''The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords — The Official Nintendo Player's Guide''''' is a guidebook released by [[Nintendo Power]] for {{ALttP&FS}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 128 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of for both {{ALttP}} and {{FS}}. It includes detailed dungeon maps, item lists, and lists of collectibles such as {{Plural|ALttP|Piece of Heart|link}}.
