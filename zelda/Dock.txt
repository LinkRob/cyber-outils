{{Term/Store|Dock|Docks|Series, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW |publisher= Prima |page= 15}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Dock}} go unnamed in {{TLoZ}}, {{ALttP}}, {{TWW}}, {{PH}}, and {{BotW}}. They are platforms which stick out from land into a body of water, facilitating easy exit or entry to a {{Term|Series|Boat|link}}. 

==Location and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Stub|TLoZ}}
In {{TLoZ}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Dock}} allow {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} to use the {{Term|TLoZ|Raft|link}} to reach otherwise inaccessible locations.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub|ALttP}}

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, one {{Term|TWWHD|Dock}} can be found on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}} and another one on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}. The {{Term|TWWHD|King of Red Lions|link}} will always relocate to the docks when Link returns to the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}} from another area.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, {{Plural|PH|Dock}} serve as the only access points to every island in the game. Steering the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} into an island's {{Term|PH|Dock}} while traversing the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}} allows {{Term|PH|Link}} to visit the island.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Stub|ALBW}}

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Dock}} can be found on bodies of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. Many {{Plural|BotW|Dock}} have {{Plural|BotW|Raft|link}} tied to them, which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use for travel.
