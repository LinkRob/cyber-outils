The {{Term/Store|Jack of Hearts||Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Item}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Jack of Hearts gives you an extra Heart Container to play with. Do with it what you will, my pet!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Jack of Hearts}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Hytopian Silk|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Octorok Sucker|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Fairy Dust}} and 600 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. It becomes available for purchase after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Jack of Hearts}} increases the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}' total {{Plural|TFH|Heart Container|link}} by one, as made evident by the single {{Term|TFH|Heart|link}} on its front. This ability is deprecated by both the {{Term|TFH|Queen of Hearts|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit}}.

The {{Term|TFH|Jack of Hearts}} and the {{Term|TFH|Queen of Hearts}} appear to be based on characters from the book ''{{Wp|Alice's Adventures in Wonderland}}''.
