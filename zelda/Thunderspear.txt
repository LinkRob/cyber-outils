{{Plural|BotW|Thunderspear}} are magical {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} that can be found on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}, particularly within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. Their spearheads contain thunder from the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|link}} in their tips.<ref name="Inventory"/> This elemental nature makes them a part of the {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> When their blades glow, they are able to unleash an attack that will electrocute {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Thunderspear}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will also sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be manipulated and picked back up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. If they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Thunderspear}} will not burn like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} do.
