{{Term/Store|Prime Meat and Rice Bowl|Prime Meat and Rice Bowls|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. ==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make a Prime Meat and Rice Bowl by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Raw Prime Meat]], [[Hylian Rice]], and [[Rock Salt]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they will restore some of Link's {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Since these bowls contain high-quality meat, they're considered to be a serious meal.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Extra ingredients can be added to Prime Meat and Rice Bowls, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. Link can refill part of his {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} by eating these.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Consuming them will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. These bowls can be eaten to increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when they're eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]] by eating these bowls.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. If Link eats them, he will gain a boost to his defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Prime Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Prime Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This bowl is loaded with high-quality meat. Your hunt for a serious meal ends here.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. They will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Chilly"/>
