The '''Joust''' is a spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
Whenever [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] uses Joust, a flying jousting lance is fired. It is the only Spell capable of defeating the [[Red Knight]] in the [[Shrine of Strength]]. It costs three [[Rupee]]s to use.

Joust is obtained from a [[Goblin]] in the [[Forest of Canvula]] as a token of welcome.
