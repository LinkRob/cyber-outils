{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} constructed from the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They were designed by {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} to give the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champion|link}} {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} a shield specifically engineered to guard against powerful {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} lasers. {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shield}} can only be made at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. Once the "{{Term|BotW|Robbie's Research|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} has been completed, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}} will sell them to {{Term|BotW|Link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, 10 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Gear|link}}, 1 {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, and 15 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spring|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Exchange for Ancient Shield?|Ancient Oven|BotW}}</ref> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>

When raised in defense or mounted in {{Term|BotW|Link's House|link}}, its surface emits {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy|link}}. Enhanced functionality allows it to deflect {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} lasers without the use of a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Guard|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> However, unlike a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Guard}}, this automatic deflect function reduces the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shield|display=Shield}}'s {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}}. Despite this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can still perform a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Guard}} to manually deflect {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} lasers.

The base defense strength of {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shield}} is 70, same as the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield|link}}, though with superior {{Term|BotW|Durability}} as well as its automatic laser deflect function. They are also superior to the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Shield++|link}} in both defense strength and {{Term|BotW|Durability}}. As a result, they effectively render both {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield++}} and {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield}} obsolete. However their defense and {{Term|BotW|Durability}} are inferior to the mighty {{Term|BotW|Hylian Shield|link}}. Additionally, while their defense is superior to the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hero's Shield|link}}, both have higher {{Term|BotW|Durability}}. This shield also boasts 80% less friction while shield surfing, causing it to go much faster than other shields. The only other shields with this mechanic are {{Term|BotW|Shield of the Mind's Eye|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Radiant Shield|link}}.

Being made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shield}} will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, nor can they be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shield}} are unable to conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}.
