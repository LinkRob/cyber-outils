The {{Term/Store|Knight of Hyrule|Knights of Hyrule|Series, ALttP|plural}},{{Exp Game|ALttP}}<ref>{{Cite|I shall teach you one of the secret sword techniques used by the Knights of Hyrule...|Link's Uncle|ALttP}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Hylian Knight|Hylian Knights|TAoL|plural}},{{Exp Game|TAoL}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 223 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref> are a recurring group of characters in {{TLoZ|Series}}. As the [[Hyrule|kingdom of Hyrule]]'s elite force, they are the protectors of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]]. They played a large role in {{ALttP}}, in which they fought valiantly against [[Ganon]] in the backstory;<ref name=Quote1>{{Cite Manual|quote=However, their lives were not lost in vain, for they brought precious time for the Seven Wise Men to magically seal Ganon in the Golden Land.|game= ALttP|page=5}}</ref> and in {{FSA}}, in which they guarded the [[Royal Jewels]] but were cast into the [[Dark World]] and corrupted by Ganon.

The Knights of Hyrule are revealed to be a powerful clan that were descended from the [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|Legendary Hero]]'s [[Link|spiritual successors]] who governed the crest of courage.<ref name="HH 93">''"The Knights of Hyrule are a clan that protect the Royal Family, their members descended from the Hero who governed the Crest of Courage. Legend has it that'' ''the Hero will one day reappear within their ranks. Looking back through the ages, it is possible that Link, the Hero of Time, was once a Hylian Knight himself. This may be the reason his mother became embroiled in the fires of war."'' -[https://docs.google.com/file/d/0B5Hr-M-01DLlSWoyNkFFdHRsdTg/edit?resourcekey=0-l0H7waOCaw2iDe-EZf7pOA Hyrule Historia], pg. 93</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 68}}</ref> The knights serve the purpose to protect the Royal Family of Hyrule, the [[Goddess Hylia]]'s mortal descendants for generations.<ref>{{Cite|Only a person of the Knights Of Hyrule, who protected the royalty of Hylia, can become the Hero... You are of their blood-line, aren't you? Then you must rescue Zelda without fail.|Maiden|ALttP}}</ref> An incarnation of the legendary hero, [[Link]], usually is born from the knightly bloodline<ref>{{Cite|You are perhaps the last one to carry on the blood-line of the Knights... It is ironic that the last one in the line has the potential to become the Hero of legend.|Maiden|ALttP}}</ref> and often has the objective to be [[Princess Zelda]]'s personal guardian.

Their symbol is the [[Hylian Crest]] used to represent the [[Crimson Loftwing]] and the sacred relic of the [[Golden Goddesses]]: the [[Triforce]], which are also featured on the [[Hylian Shield]], said to be used by the knights in {{OoT}}.<ref name=HylianShield>{{Cite|This is a big, heavy shield just like the ones Hylian Knights use. It can stand up to flame attacks!|Bazaar Owner|OoT}}</ref>

With the history of Hyrule splitting and taking [[Zelda Timeline#Split of the timeline|three different paths]] following the defeat of Ganon by the [[Hero of Time]], the historical appearances of the Knights of Hyrule also splits, giving them three different destinies over the course of its three histories.

==Appearances==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
[[File:TAoL Darunia Town Knight Screenshot.png|left|thumb|The Hyrulean Knight in Darunia Town teaching Link the [[Jump Thrust]] attack.|210x210px]]

In {{TAoL}}, before the story of the Knights of Hyrule was told in {{ALttP|-}}, two Hyrulean Knights appear to teach [[Link]] their [[:Category:Sword Techniques|sword techniques]]. One, found in [[Harbor Town of Mido|Mido Town]], teaches the [[Down Thrust]] and the other one, found in [[Mountain Town of Darunia|Darunia Town]], teaches the [[Jump Thrust]]. Besides the two Knights from Mido and Darunia towns, there is also the [[River Man]] from the [[Water Town of Saria]], who has a similar appearance to the Knights, but with darker color.

==={{FSA|-}}===
[[File:knight FSA.jpg|thumb|Link with the Green Knight of Hyrule: The Guardian of the Green Jewel|alt=|left|226x226px]]
In {{FSA}} the Knights of Hyrule are highly esteemed throughout Hyrule for their bravery, and are said to be the pride of the kingdom because of their willingness to lay down their lives for the sake of honor.<ref name=":0">''"You've heard of the Knights of Hyrule, of course! They're the pride of the kingdom! They would lay down their lives for honor!"'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Green Maiden] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> Ages prior to the events of ''Four Swords Adventures,'' the Knights of Hyrule were entrusted with guarding the Four Sacred Jewels, which are needed in order to open a pathway to the [[Realm of the Heavens (Stage)|Realm of the Heavens]].<ref>''"In Hyrule, four sacred jewels can open the path to the Realm of the Heavens. The safety of these jewels has for ages been entrusted to the Knights o Hyrule."'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Green Maiden] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> 

Just prior to the events of ''Four Swords Adventures'', the Knights of Hyrule, along with the Four Sacred Jewels, had mysteriously gone missing. Using the power of the evil [[Trident]] he had stolen from the [[Pyramid|Pyrimid]] within the [[Desert of Doubt (Stage)|Desert of Doubt]], [[Ganon]] overtook the Knights,<ref>''"Hear now the doom of the knights. We were bested by a foe with a mighty weapon."'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Red Knight] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> transforming them into monstrous [[Big Dark Stalfos]]'<ref>''"To my shame, I fell under the sway of dark powers and was transformed into a base creature of magic."'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Blue Knight] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref><ref>''"No, WE servants of the crown were turned into monsters!"'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Red Knight] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> and cursed the Sacred Jewels into becoming portals to the Dark World.<ref>''"The jewel was also cursed, and it became a portal to the Dark World."'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Blue Knight] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> 

In order to save Hyrule, Link is tasked with collecting the Four Sacred Jewels.<ref>''"Free them from their dark curses and retrieve the jewels!"'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Blue Knight] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> By defeating each of the the Big Dark Stalfos' in battle, Link manages to free each of the Knights from their curses and restores the Sacred Jewels.<ref>''"Thanks to you, we have been freed from the dark spells that bound us."'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Purple Knight] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> Once free of Ganon's influence, each Knight entrusts the Sacred Jewel under their protection to Link before passing away.<ref>''"You must now take the four royal jewels past this ice-bound temple and fly to sacred grounds!"'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Purple Knight] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> 

In ''Four Swords Adventures'' Link is never described as being part of the clan of the Knights of Hyrule, and seems to be unfamiliar with them.<ref name=":0" /> Even so, the young hero's bravery is described as being very similar to that of the Knights.<ref>''"You passed through the flames to rescue me! Such bravery. You're just like the Knights of Hyrule!"'' -[https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914976-the-legend-of-zelda-four-swords-adventures/faqs/47212 The Green Maiden] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> Coincidentally, the colors of the four Knights shown in this title (Blue, Green, Red, and Purple) correspond with the colors of the four Links that form from using the [[Four Sword]].

[[File:TP Hero's Spirit Concept Artwork.png|left|thumb|200px|Official concept art of the Hero's Spirit, the restless soul of the [[Hero of Time]], who may be a former Knight of Hyrule himself]]

{{TP}} takes place over a century after the events of ''Ocarina of Time/[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' in a timeline where the [[Hero of Time]] was sent back in time by Princess Zelda and stopped [[Ganon|Ganondorf]] from ever accessing the [[Sacred Realm]],<ref>''"In Hyrule, Princess Zelda was informed of what would happen in the future by Link, the Hero of Time. She realized that leaving Ganondorf to his own devices would cause the downfall of her kingdom."'' -[https://docs.google.com/file/d/0B5Hr-M-01DLlSWoyNkFFdHRsdTg/edit?resourcekey=0-l0H7waOCaw2iDe-EZf7pOA Hyrule Historia] pg. 113</ref> and ages prior to the events in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]''.<ref>''"Several hundred years after Ganondorf ’s defeat, relations between Gerudo Village and Hyrule had become friendly once more, and peace had been restored to the land. However, one day a new Ganondorf was born into the world."'' -[https://docs.google.com/file/d/0B5Hr-M-01DLlSWoyNkFFdHRsdTg/edit?resourcekey=0-l0H7waOCaw2iDe-EZf7pOA Hyrule Historia] pg. 119</ref>

[[File:TP Suits of Armor.png|thumb|200px|Knight suits of armor in {{TP|-}}|right]]
Like in {{OoT|-}}, the Knights of Hyrule do not make a direct appearance in {{TP|-}}, and are only mentioned in passing. The character [[Ashei]] says that her father was a knight in his own right, but this seems to be a reference to his martial abilities, and not meant to indicate that he was of the clan of the Knights of Hyrule.<ref>''"I grew up in the mountains with my father, who was a knight in his own right. He taught me the arts of war as though I was his son."'' -[http://www.zeldalegends.net/files/emulation/dumps/tp/Europe/TP_English.txt Ashei] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref>

During the events of {{TP|-}}, Link, the Hero of Twilight, is trained in swordfighting by a spirit of one of his ancestors, known as the [[Hero's Spirit|Hero's Shade]], in order to became a better fighter and defeat Ganondorf.<ref>''"Those are only for one who carries the blood of the hero...the one whose spirit is that of the sublime beast."'' -[http://www.zeldalegends.net/files/emulation/dumps/tp/Europe/TP_English.txt Hero's Shade] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref><ref>''"At last, the skills I have to teach you have entered the realm of true secrecy. They are forgotten ways that do not leave our bloodline."'' -[http://www.zeldalegends.net/files/emulation/dumps/tp/Europe/TP_English.txt Hero's Shade] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> Though not directly stated in-game, ''Hyrule Historia'' states that the Hero's Shade is actually the restless spirit of the Hero of Time, the Link from the [[Nintendo 64|N64]] titles,<ref>''"The spirit of Link’s ancestor, the Hero of Time, teaches him his secrets."'' -[https://docs.google.com/file/d/0B5Hr-M-01DLlSWoyNkFFdHRsdTg/edit?resourcekey=0-l0H7waOCaw2iDe-EZf7pOA Hyrule Historia] pg. 118</ref> who only finds peace in the afterlife when he is finished teaching the Hero of Twilight his [[Hidden Skill|seven powerful sword techniques]].<ref>''"Although I accepted life as the hero, I could not convey the lessons of that life to those who came after. At last, I have eased my regrets."'' -[http://www.zeldalegends.net/files/emulation/dumps/tp/Europe/TP_English.txt The Hero's Shade] (Nintendo of America Translation)</ref> Since the Hero of Time is suggested to have been a member of the Knights of Hyrule clan according to ''Hyrule Historia'', it also follows that his descendant, the Hero of Twilight, may too be a member of this clan.<ref name="HH 93" />
==={{BotW|-}}===
[[File:BotW Knight Statue Angle.png|thumb|left|200px|Stone suit of armor]]

In {{BotW|-}}, [[Link#Breath of the Wild|Sir Link]]<ref>{{Cite|Sir... Sir Link... My Cuccos... My precious Cuccos... (when all Cuccos are removed from their pen)|Cado|BotW}}</ref> was a knight and served as Princess Zelda's personal bodyguard 100 years prior to his [[Shrine of Resurrection|reawakening]]. [[Link's Father]] was said to be a member of the Knights of Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Your path seems to mirror your father's. You've dedicated yourself to becoming a knight, as well. Your commitment to the training necessary to fulfill your goal is really quite admirable. I see now why you would be the chosen one.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

The Knights of Hyrule were equipped with the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a set of weaponry known for its reliability and durability and which would remain in use in the wake of the the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Knight's Broadsword}}<br/>Knights of Hyrule once carried this sword. These days it's the weapon of choice for seasoned adventurers thanks to its ease of use and high attack power.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Knight's Claymore}}<br/>Only the most confident of Hyrule Castle's knights carried this two-handed sword. Its cutting edge is finely honed.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Knight's Bow}}<br/>The sturdy metal construction of this bow offers superior durability, while its lack of firing quirks makes it quite reliable. Once favored by the knights at Hyrule Castle.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Knight's Shield}}<br/>A shield favored by the knights who served the Hyrulean royal family. Its sturdy metal construction makes it quite durable, but its weight requires decent skill to wield.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

A test in Hyrule Castle was created to judge whether or not soldiers should become a Knight by testing their strength against a {{Term|BotW|Stalnox|link}}. Upon completion, the victor would be given a {{Term|BotW|Hylian Shield|link}} and be recognized as a royal knight. The name of the test, "Test of the Royal Guard," suggests a connection between the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard|link}} and the Knights of Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|- Test of the Royal Guard -<br/>Strike down the giant foe to become recognized as a knight of the kingdom.|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

There is also Knight equipment for Link's horse. The {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Knight's Saddle|link}} are obtained from a minigame.

== Trivia ==
[[File:HWAoC Link Artwork.png|thumb|239x239px|Link as a Hylian Knight, as seen in ''[[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity]]'', known as "Sir Link"]]
* {{BotW}} is the first game of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] where [[Link]] is explicitly a member of the Knights of Hyrule, despite most earlier games since {{aLttP}} already suggest the main character to be such or a descendant. Despite being the primary hero of [[Hyrule]], it's unknown if Link himself is the strongest knight in existence.
* In the original Japanese releases of the franchise, the Hylian Knights are known as '''Order of Knighthood'''.
* Most of Link's special sword techniques, such as the [[Spin Attack]], [[Jump Thrust]], [[Down Thrust]], and so on, are actually attacks used by the Knights of Hyrule themselves.
*It is heavily implied that the knights of [[Skyloft]] were the predecessors of the Knights of Hyrule.
