The '''{{Name|Japanese|Big Bag}}''' is an item in {{AST}}.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|AST|Big Bag}} is first received from {{Term|AST|Aginah|link}} during week 1 after the {{Term|AST|Hero of Light|link}} wakes up. It contains an {{Term|AST|Ocarina|link}}, a {{Term|AST|Lantern|link}}, a {{Term|AST|Bug-Catching Net|link}}, and a {{Term|AST|Bottle|link}} with a {{Term|AST|Golden Bee|link}}. It can also hold any {{Term|AST|Item|link}} the {{Term|AST|Hero of Light}} obtains afterwards. The {{Term|AST|Big Bag}} is received once again at the start of every week, now containing all the {{Plural|AST|Item}} found during the previous week or weeks. If the game is started during weeks 2, 3, or 4, a {{Term|AST|Thief|link}} near the entrance of each of the previous {{Plural|AST|Dungeon|link}} gives the {{Term|AST|Hero of Light}} a {{Term|AST|Big Bag}} containing the {{Plural|AST|Item}} found inside the {{Plural|AST|Dungeon}}, including the {{Term|AST|Heart Container|link}} the {{Term|AST|Boss|link}} drops.
