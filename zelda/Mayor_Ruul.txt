{{Term/Store|Mayor Ruul||Series, OoS}} is a character in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250}}</ref>

==Biography==
Mayor Ruul plays a minor role as the mayor of [[Horon Village]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm Ruul, mayor of Horon Village.|Mayor Ruul|OoS}}</ref> His somewhat secluded residence is located in the northeastern area of the village. The mayor has a noted interest in everything concerning [[Gasha Seed]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Do you have any Gasha Seeds? If you plant one in soft earth, it will grow into a tree that bears nuts! Inside those nuts, you'll find all sorts of things! It's so fun I can't help myself.|Mayor Ruul|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The mayor is fascinated with Gasha Nuts now.|Human|OoS}}</ref> When Link first speaks to Ruul, he is astonished by the fact that the hero does not know about Gasha Seeds and gives him one to "welcome him to the town".<ref>{{Cite|You don't know about Gasha Seeds??? You're missing out! I'll give you my prized Gasha Seed to welcome you to town.|Mayor Ruul|OoS}}</ref> If Link comes back later with [[Bomb]]s, he can blow open a hole in the eastern wall of the residence, revealing a secret room with yet another Gasha Seed. There is a patch of soft [[soil]] outside the residence where Link can plant the seeds.<ref>{{Cite|There's some soft earth just outside. Plant it there.|Mayor Ruul|OoS}}</ref> 

Mayor Ruul is the husband of [[Mrs. Ruul]], who lives in a villa on the [[Holodrum Plain]]. The married couple are never seen together.

In a [[Linked Game]] of {{OoA}}, [[Mayor Plen]]'s grandmother appears in her son's home. She has a [[Linked Game/Secrets|secret]] for him to tell to Mayor Ruul.<ref>{{Cite|Do you remember Mayor Ruul back in Holodrum's Horon Village? I want you to tell this secret to Mayor Ruul, understand?|Human|OoA}}</ref> Ruul, who claims to be friends with Mayor Plen's grandmother, will upgrade Link's [[Ring Box]] after receiving the secret.<ref>{{Cite|Goodness! It's a message from my friend in Labrynna! Thanks so much! I'll make it so you can carry even more rings!|Mayor Ruul|OoS}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* Mayor Ruul is the only authority figure in the game. The only other human settlement in [[Holodrum]], [[Sunken City]], doesn't seem to have a leader.
* Mayor Ruul's name is most likely a pun on the word "rule", in reference to his position.
