The '''Ancient Hero's Aspect''' is the reward for completing all 152 [[Shrine of Light|Shrines]] in {{TotK}}. It is obtained from a treasure chest behind the [[Goddess Statue]] in the [[Temple of Time (Sky)]].

==Overview==
[[File:Ancient Hero.png|left|thumb|200px]]
The Ancient Hero is thought to be the hero who battled [[Calamity Ganon]] 10,000 years prior to the events of {{BotW|-}}. In {{TotK|-}}, [[Impa]] alludes to this when you talk to her while the Ancient Hero's Aspect is equipped. This hero seems to be of [[Zonai]] origin or at the very least connected to them as seen in his use of [[Zonaite Set|Zonai Armor]]. According to [[Ganondorf]], the Zonai were no more besides [[King Rauru]] and his older sister [[Mineru]]. If correct, that would mean this Hero may be another race possibly connected to the Zonai or a [[Hylian]]/Zonai mix like the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family’s]] bloodline. 

==Appearance==
The Ancient Hero's Aspect is a three in one armor set. The actual armor is very similar to the Zonaite Armor set with the exception being the lack of the headdress and alteration to the greaves.

The hero himself has hunter green skin with a head similar to that of a canine and ears of a typical Hylian adorned with gold earrings on both ears. The Hero has blue eyes and blood red hair similar in color to Ganondorf. On his forehead, he wears a gold or Zonaite piece of jewelry adorned with a green gem. Throughout his body, he has white tattoo-like markings on his skin similar to [[Queen Sonia]].

The Ancient Hero also appears to have canine-like feet with four visible toes and a dew claw further up his calf (this is unlike the Zonai who have human-like feet). A key Zonai feature that is missing on the hero is the third eye. The Hero's canine-like appearance is in contrast to the Zonai's more goat-like one. It's possible that because of Link's short Hylian build, the Ancient Hero's Aspect does not reflect the true size and build of the hero. 

==Story==
At some point, many years after the [[Imprisoning War (Tears of the Kingdom)|Imprisoning War]], the malice and hate coming from [[Ganondorf#Tears of the Kingdom|Demon King Ganondorf]] created [[Calamity Ganon]] in a subconscious attempt to destroy or damage [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] so that the seal placed upon him by King Rauru would weaken and he could be set free. By way of the [[Master Sword]] and the sacred powers passed down through the Royal Family of Hyrule, starting with the child and successor to King Rauru and Queen Sonia, Calamity Ganon would be sealed away only to return many years later. 

This cycle would repeat "time and time again" until the First [[Great Calamity]]. With the help of [[Champions]] piloting the [[Divine Beast]]s, the [[Sheikah]] who created the Divine Beasts, and the Ancient Princess, the Ancient Hero was able to defeat the Calamity and seal him away with the Master Sword before he could damage or even destroy the castle, allowing for an era of peace that would last 10,000 years before the Second Great Calamity, 100 years before {{BotW|-}}.

Although it is never stated, the fact that the Ancient Hero was able to use the Master Sword for its intended purpose (to fight against evil and seal the darkness) this Hero was most likely an incarnation of the original Hero, whose spirit has been reincarnated into several heroes as Link in the franchise, as even in her dormant state, [[Fi]] would allow no one but her Master to wield it.

==Notes==
There is the possibility that the Ancient Hero is Zonai but has canine-like features as a reference to the Hero of Twilight's Twilight form being a wolf rather than a spirit. It's possible that similarly, the spirit of the hero may have once again took on the form of a canine humanoid despite being a Zonai or Zonai hybrid. This is, however, speculative and has not been confirmed.
