The {{Term/Store|Bearded Baron||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Oh! You wish to speak with the Bearded Baron, eh?|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> He is a villager of the town of [[Hytopia]].

==Biography==
The Bearded Baron can be found next to the [[Tri Force Heroes Statue]] in the center of Hytopia's town, where he initially remarks upon [[Link]]'s resemblance to the Tri Force Heroes due to his pointed ears, parted hair and sideburns.<ref>{{Cite|Oh? My! Oh my, oh my, oh my! Ears fit to pierce the heavens! An impeccable side parting! And the universal symbol of heroism...magnificent sideburns! I say, my boy, you are the spitting image of a Tri Force Hero!|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> Because of growing fears that the [[Lady Maud|witch]] of the [[Drablands]] will curse anyone who is fashionable, he has cut his outfit in half.<ref>{{Cite|They say the witch is going after fashionable people. Obviously, I have much to fear. That's why I've cut my stylishness in half. It's the most I can do.|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> He tells Link that he should give thanks to the Tri Force Heroes Statue, which will save his game's progress. Later, after Link completes one Level in the [[Woodlands]], he informs Link that the [[Daily Riches]] shop and [[Miiverse Gallery]] have opened.

After Link lifts [[Princess Styla]]'s curse and the following celebration ends, the Bearded Baron can be found at the bridge. He continues to wear his halved outfit, as he realizes it has created a new fashion trend.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? What is THIS? What a sharp eye you have, young hero! At first, I halved my fashion because I was frightened of the curse. I'm big enough to admit that, my boy. But then I realized... this look is the HEIGHT of fashion! The townspeople can't keep their eyes off of me! I'm so proud of my fashion discovery. I shall dedicate my life to spreading the beardly look across Hytopia!|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> He also tells Link that the [[Photo Bro]] had filmed the entire celebration ceremony, and gave it as a gift to the princess.<ref>{{Cite|You should know that the Photo Bro filmed the whole affair. He presented it to the princess as a present! If you ever wish to revel in that magnificent ceremony once more, go speak with the princess.|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref>

When spoken to, the Bearded Baron can be asked to share a secret. Through this, it is revealed that cats do not like him,<ref>{{Cite|I can't fathom why, but cats do not seem to like my company! They even hiss when I draw near.|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> but he believes if he wore a [[Cheetah Costume|cat-like outfit]], he would be able to understand them.<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps if I wore a catlike outfit, I could understand their ways! Hmm...|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> He also claims to have found a cicada shell-covered tree in Hytopia's south-east corner, which he thinks is an omen of good things to come.<ref>{{Cite|I found a tree covered in cicada shells! Most certainly an omen that something good will happen today.|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> He mentions that [[Madame Couture]] is said to be in possession of five cats.<ref>{{Cite|Madame loves cats! Rumor has it she's looking after five of them. But each time I go visit, I only see four...|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref>
