In {{MM}}, '''Pirates''' are encountered as enemies inside the [[Pirates' Fortress]], guarding [[Lulu]]'s stolen [[Zora Egg]]s. They are found in rooms whose doors are marked by a golden scimitar panel. They drop down when [[Link]] enters, locking him inside and engaging him in combat.<ref>{{Cite|Halt! It takes courage to come thieving in the Pirates' Fortress! I'm going to love doing this to you!|Pirate|MM}}</ref> There are three of them each, one wearing green, one orange, and one pink.

Gerudo Pirates have an identical attack strategy to their [[Gerudo Thief]] counterparts from {{OoT}}; they are agile fighters who actively evade Link's attacks and strike back in-between. They are also able to perform spinning [[Jump Slash|Jump Attacks]] as well that cannot be blocked. Like the Gerudo Thieves of {{OoT|-}}, they will not attempt to kill Link but merely weaken him. If the young [[Hero of Time]] is struck repeatedly down to low health or hit by their Jump Attack at all, he will be captured and thrown out of the Fortress. They can be fought using the [[Sword]], [[Goron Link]]'s punches, and [[Zora Link]]'s fin punches or kicks. As [[Deku Link]], they can only be damaged by his [[Bubble Blast]].

After they are defeated, they angrily concede defeat before escaping by throwing a Deku Nut (similar to [[Impa]] and [[Sheik]] in {{OoT|-}}).<ref>{{Cite|Ugh. Don't think it ends here!|Pirate|MM}}</ref> Once defeated, the door to the chamber's aquarium opens, where one of the Zora Eggs are being held.
