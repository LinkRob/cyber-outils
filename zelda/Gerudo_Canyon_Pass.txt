The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Canyon Pass||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Gerudo Canyon Pass'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Gerudo Canyon Pass is a road that passes through the [[Gerudo Canyon]] that is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]].

A [[Yiga Footsoldier]] may pass through the Pass disguised as a [[Traveler]].
