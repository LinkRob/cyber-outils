The {{Term/Store|Cross||Series, TAoL}} is an item in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Cross is found in the sixth palace, [[Three-Eye Rock Palace]]. Unlike the other items in the game, it is guarded by a mini-boss: a [[Rebonack]]. After its defeat, Link gains a key and proceeds to take the Cross from a pedestal in the next room.

The Cross, while useless in the dungeon where it is found, allows Link to see the dangerous blue-colored [[Moa]]s<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=With its secret power, you'll be able to see enemies which you normally can't see.|game= TAoL|page=46}}</ref> that infest the graveyards in East Hyrule, the town of [[Kasuto|Old Kasuto]], and the [[Valley of Death]] (which itself serves as the path to the [[Great Palace]]). It should be noted that, with a lot of skill, it is possible (albeit difficult) to survive those places without seeing the invisible enemies. A more risky method involves the constant use of the [[Magic of The Adventure of Link#Fairy|Fairy]] magic.
