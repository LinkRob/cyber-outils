{{Manual|EoW|Hawk Statue|A pointed statue with folded wings and a hooked beak. Its sharp eyes stare into the distance.<ref name="Hawk Statue">{{Cite|A pointed statue with folded wings and a hooked beak. Its sharp eyes stare into the distance.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Hawk Statues are large Bird statues found in the [[Gerudo Sanctum]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]]. They cost one [[Echo]] energy to summon.
