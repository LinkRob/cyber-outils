The {{Term/Store|Rupee Regalia||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Rupee Regalia makes it easier to find Rupees. That means you can buy more clothes, oui hee hee!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Rupee Regalia}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Fire Temple|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}}.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Rupee Regalia}} greatly increases the chances of {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} appearing when {{Term|TFH|Link}} defeats [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]], cuts grass, or breaks {{Plural|TFH|Jar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Increase the drop rate of Rupees. You'll be swimming in Rupees before long!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> It increases the potential value of these dropped {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} to include red and purple {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}}, as well as making all {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} appear in pairs, effectively doubling their values.

The effects of the {{Term|TFH|Rupee Regalia}} do not apply to [[Bosses in Tri Force Heroes|Bosses]] or certain Enemies such as {{Plural|TFH|Totem Armos|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} does not affect {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} that are pre-existing in a [[Levels in Tri Force Heroes|Level]] or hidden under {{Plural|TFH|Sand Mound|link}}. {{Term|TFH|Link}} must defeat an Enemy or destroy an object directly, not by throwing another object into them, for the effects of the {{Term|TFH|Rupee Regalia}} to take place.
