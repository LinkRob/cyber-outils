==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hearty Bass}}
{{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} that can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link|display=waters}} of {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. Schools of {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass}} can be found in ponds located on {{Term|BotW|Firly Plateau|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mount Rhoam|link}}. Their large bodies are able to store a lot of nutrients.<ref name="BotW Inventory"/> Exposing {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass}} to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} results in {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Hearty Bass|link}}, while chilling it with {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} results in {{Plural|BotW|Frozen Hearty Bass|link}}. They can be eaten raw to heal two {{Plural|BotW|Life Gauge|link|display=Hearts}}. Meals made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass}} will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} temporary {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} in his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge}} when eaten.<ref name="BotW Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass}} are among the {{Term|BotW|Fish}} that can be used to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=cook}} a {{Term|BotW|Fish Skewer|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Fish and Mushroom Skewer|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Salt-Grilled Fish|link}}. They're also counted among the seafood that can be used to make {{Term|BotW|Fish Pie|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Creamy Seafood Soup|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Copious Fish Skewers|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Glazed Seafood|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Meat and Seafood Fry|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Monster Stew|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pepper Seafood|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Prime Meat and Seafood Fry|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Seafood Meunière|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Seafood Rice Balls|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Steamed Fish|link}}.

In order to upgrade the {{Term|BotW|Zora Set|link}} to its third tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs to collect five {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass}} for each piece.

* Total for '''{{Term|BotW|Zora Set}}''': 15 (0, 0, 5, 0) {{Small|(For Each Piece of Armor in the Set)}}
