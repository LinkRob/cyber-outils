"{{Term/Store|The Horseback Hoodlums||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Horseback Hoodlums}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Highland Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain|link}}, there will be five {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} riding {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} that attack travelers.<ref>{{Cite|Faron Grasslands is known for its horses.<br/><br/>However, a gang of heathens has taken over the area lately. This has caused Perosa and the other residents some strife.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Perosa|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has been to the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} before.<ref>{{Cite|Is this your first time here?|Perosa|BotW}}</ref> She goes on the explains that, since the {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain|display=Plain}} is known for its {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, many nomadic tribes used to gather in the area.<ref>{{Cite|These plains are home to many quality horses. In the past, nomadic tribes would gather here, but...|Perosa|BotW}}</ref>

In the present, though, the plains have been overtaken by a band of {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin}},<ref>{{Cite|Nowadays, {{Color|BotW Red|bands of riders}} roam the plains, acting like they own the place.|Perosa|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Perosa}} hopes that someone will come take care of them.<ref>{{Cite|I can only hope that someone steps up and deals with those hooligans...|Perosa|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} volunteers, {{Term|BotW|Perosa}} will be happy and reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she is counting on him.<ref>{{Cite|[Leave it to me!] Oh, really? I'm so glad to hear that. I'm counting on you!|Perosa|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has defeated all five {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Perosa}} to tell her the news.<ref>{{Cite|You took care of the {{Color|BotW Red|hooligans}}?|Perosa|BotW}}</ref> To show her gratitude, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} an {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is a small token of my thanks!|Perosa|BotW}}</ref> She explains that {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} like them and encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} feed them to one.<ref>{{Cite|Horses really love it! Try sharing it with one, and you'll see what I mean!|Perosa|BotW}}</ref> Once she finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.
