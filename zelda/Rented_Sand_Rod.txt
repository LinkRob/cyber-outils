The {{Term/Store|Rented Sand Rod||Series, ALBW}} is an {{Term|ALBW|Item|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rented Sand Rod<br/>If the ground in front of you is sand, you can conjure up a giant wall!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Rented Sand Rod}} is used to create pillars of {{Term|ALBW|Sand|link}} on top of already sandy ground. Those blocks may be utilized by {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|link}} across them. The {{Term|ALBW|Rented Sand Rod}} is not available until {{Term|ALBW|Osfala|link}} has been freed from {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} has collected the Rod. After that, it is rentable from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} for fifty {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.<br/><br/>Being a rented {{Term|ALBW|Item}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Sand Rod}} will be returned to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} by {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} falls in battle. It may be permanently obtained as the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod|link}} for 800 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, and further upgraded into the {{Term|ALBW|Nice Sand Rod|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}.
