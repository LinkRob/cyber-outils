==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Ring of Health}} are limited-use items that automatically restores half a {{Term|CoH|Heart|link}} after its wearer defeats 5 enemies.<ref>{{Cite|Heals {{Color|CoH Green|half a heart}} after you've defeated 5 enemies.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> 

They can be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}}.
