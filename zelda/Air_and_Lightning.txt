==Overview==
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
{{Term|HWAoC|Air and Lightning}} is a [[Scenario]] from [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity]]. [[Windblight Ganon]] and [[Thunderblight Ganon]] make their first appearance here. So do [[Teba]] and [[Riju]] along with [[Patricia]] her [[Sand Seal]]. The mission starts with you at the bottom of the map, and your first objective is to kill 4 Moblins to open the way to Vah Naboris. When you do, the doors open, and you can go until you encounter a Electric Lynel. Kill it and you can get to Vah Naboris. You then have to kill Thunderblight Ganon. When you do, Urbosa uses Vah Naboris's lightning to smash open a mountain with a giant air gust for getting on Divine Beast Vah Medoh. You then have to defeat some Hinoxes to get some Rito to open the outpost. You then have to defeat a White-Maned Lynel to get to the air gust. Kill it and you can get to Vah Medoh. You then have to kill Windblight Ganon (Though he flees), which ends the first half of the mission. The second half has you destroying monster outposts while Windblight Ganon constantly gets in your way, before you kill him for good.
