==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Frozen Crabs by exposing {{Plural|Series|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, {{Plural|Series|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or {{Plural|Series|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}} to [[Ice]]. They don't offer much companionship, but they're guaranteed not to spoil.<ref name="Inventory"/> Eating one will restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to Link's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and grant temporary [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/>
