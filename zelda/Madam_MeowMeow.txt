{{Term/Store|Madam MeowMeow||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 233"/>{{Note|This character's name is also stylized as '''Madame MeowMeow''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 21}}</ref> However, as {{E}} supersedes the guide, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Term|LADX|Madam MeowMeow}} is often found boasting about {{Term|LADX|BowWow|link}}'s fur coat in her home located in {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ho ho ho! My BowWow is so proud of his fine fur coat!|Madam MeowMeow|LADX}}</ref> In addition to {{Term|LADX|BowWow}}, she owns two other smaller {{Plural|LADX|Chain Chomp|link}}. {{Term|LANS|ChowChow|link}} lives in the house with her, while {{Term|LANS|CiaoCiao|link}} lives in the [[Doghouse]] attached to the side of her house.<ref>{{Cite|Make-up! Jewels! Dresses! I want it all! Sigh... And some new accessories would be nice...|YipYip|LADX}}</ref>

When {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} returns with the {{Term|LADX|Full Moon Cello|link}} from {{Term|LADX|Tail Cave|link}}, her beloved {{Term|LADX|BowWow}} is "dognapped" by {{Plural|LADX|Moblin|link}},<ref>{{Cite|AIEEEEEEEE! It's terrrrible! My BowWow was dognapped by... Mo-mo-MOBLINS!! OHHH! AHHHH! Please! Somebody help my poor BowWow!!|Madam MeowMeow|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LADX|Link}} must go to the {{Term|LADX|Moblin Cave|link}} and get him back from the {{Term|LADX|King Moblin|link}}. Upon returning to her with {{Term|LADX|BowWow}}, {{Term|LADX|Madam MeowMeow}} will ask {{Term|LADX|Link}} to take him for a walk.<ref>{{Cite|Ho ho ho! I really appreciate what you did for my poor, precious BowWow! You are such a nice boy! How can I ever repay you? I know... SMOOOOOOOCH! You got a reward from Madam MeowMeow... ... ... L-l-lucky!|Madam MeowMeow|LADX|387}}</ref> After {{Term|LADX|Link}} completes {{Term|LADX|Bottle Grotto|link}}, he can bring back {{Term|LADX|BowWoW}} to {{Term|LADX|Madam MeowMeow}} who will reward him with a kiss that fully refills his {{Term|LADX|Heart Meter|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh thank you! I'm so happy you brought my baby back! Now, would you be a dear and take him for a walk? It would really help me out a lot! You will?! Thanks!|Madam MeowMeow|LADX}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Madam MeowMeow}} lives in {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Mabe Village|link}} with her pet, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|BowWow|link}}. After {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} returns to {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Mabe Village}} from {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Tail Cave|link}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Madam MeowMeow}} tells him that {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|BowWow}} was kidnapped by {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Moblin|link}}. After saving her from the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Moblin Cave|link}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} returns {{Term|LADX|BowWow|link}} to {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Madam MeowMeow}}, who rewards him with an {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ocarina|link}} that has been passed down through her family for years.
{{Noncanon|End}}
