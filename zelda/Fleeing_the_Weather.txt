{{Term/Store|Fleeing the Weather||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 41 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fleeing the Weather|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Fleeing the Weather}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given five minutes to reach a Targeted Position near eastern edge of the map, starting from the western edge. There are many {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Keese|link}} scattered around the map, and when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} advances not too far from the start, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} spawns and a gate behind it closes. The gate opens when it is defeated. There are also a lot of {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu|link}} not far in front of the the Targeted Position, and when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches that area, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin}} spawns. Like the first gate, it closes, and opens again when the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin}} is defeated. There are a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}} in that area as well. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the Targeted Position, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Hinox}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Hinox}} is defeated. Also, after a minute and 20 seconds, a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} appears in the northern part of the map and chases the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} regardless of their location. {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Star Fragment|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Sneaky River Snail|link}}.
