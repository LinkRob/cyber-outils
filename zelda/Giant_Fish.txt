The {{Name|Fan|Giant Fish}} is an enemy appearing only in the "[[Fairies in the Spring]]" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}.

When [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] go back to the [[Royal Water Park]] to look for the princess's [[King Harkinian|kidnapped father]], they find a grate that they can go through in one of the park's pools. However, a Giant Fish suddenly attacks them and grabs the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] that Zelda was holding on to. The fish, with Zelda still grabbing onto the Triforce,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You're not getting that Triforce!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> then swims in through the opening left by the grate as Link pursues it. The young hero, not being able to catch up with the fish and Zelda, asks the princess if she can slow it down.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That fish...is too fast! Zelda, slow him down!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> Zelda uses the Triforce's magic to shoot out a beam in front of her which makes a wall of ice that the fish bumps into. This gives time Link to catch up to them, who easily [[Sword Beam|zaps]] the creature away.
