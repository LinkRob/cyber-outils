'''Giant Blade Traps''' are recurring obstacles in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They first appear in {{ALttP}} and return in {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}.

==Characteristics==
Giant Blade Traps are larger variations of [[Blade Trap]]s that slowly move along a set path, although their size makes it harder for [[Link]] to dodge them. Like their regular counterparts, Giant Blade Traps appear only in [[dungeon]]s.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Giant Blade Traps are encountered in some of the later dungeons, and they first appear in [[Ice Palace]], then in [[Misery Mire (Dungeon)|Misery Mire]], and lastly in [[Ganon's Tower]]. At first, a Giant Blade Trap is dormant, but when Link walks by, it begins moving after him, making them function more similarly to Blade Traps in {{TLoZ}} than Blade Traps in the same game.

==={{OoS|-}} / {{OoA|-}}===
{{Stub|OoS, OoA}}
In {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, Giant Blade Traps appear in red and orange varieties, both having a skull shape on their center. The only difference between the two varieties is the direction in which they move. If Link touches a Giant Blade Trap, he loses a [[heart]] of health.

In {{OoS|-}}, Giant Blade Traps appear in four [[dungeon]]s, in order of: [[Snake's Remains]], [[Poison Moth's Lair]], [[Unicorn's Cave]], and lastly [[Sword & Shield Maze]]. In {{OoA|-}}, Giant Blade Traps appear only in [[Mermaid's Cave]].
