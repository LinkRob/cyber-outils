==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Dungeon Entrance}} are a type of {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}}. They are only found inside {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}}; they are found in the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. They do not appear in {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} or the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, as {{Term|TMC|Link}} remains {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized throughout. They allow {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, with {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}'s help, to reduce his size to that of a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}, and are also used to return {{Term|TMC|Link}} to normal size.
