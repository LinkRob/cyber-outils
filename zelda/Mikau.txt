{{Term/Store|Mikau||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a [[Zora]] character who first appeared in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244}}</ref>

==Biography==
Mikau served the guitarist of a band known as [[The Indigo-Go's]] and is also a Zoran hero.<ref>{{Cite|It is you, with the blood of Zoran heroes flowing within you.|Evan|MM}}</ref> He and [[Japas]] (the bass player) often [[Jam Session|compose songs]] of their own, much to the pianist [[Evan]]'s dismay.<ref>{{Cite|Today, Japas and I had a jam session. He's not good with words, but he writes great riffs.|Mikau|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Evan was in a bad mood 'cause the two of us are writin' songs on our own.|Mikau|MM}}</ref> He also has a close if not romantic relationship with the band's lead singer, [[Lulu]].<ref>{{Cite|Today, I told everything to Mikau, the one person whom I didn't want to know about it. At first, I was too embarrassed and too sad to do anything. And with the words that Mikau said at that moment, I felt that all hope had been lost. But please, Mikau, I'm begging you, don't do anything rash.|Lulu's Diary|MM}}</ref>

After Mikau learns that Lulu's seven [[Zora Egg|Egg]]s were stolen by the [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirates]], Evan charges him with the task of retrieving the Zora Eggs, in the hopes that doing so would recover Lulu's voice in time for their upcoming performance at the [[Carnival of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|...Mikau! We need those eggs safe so Lulu can get her voice back.|Evan|MM}}</ref> Before heading to the [[Pirates' Fortress]], Evan tells him where to obtain empty [[Bottle]]s to store the Zora Eggs. Mikau goes to the [[Waterfall Rapids]] to race the {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver Bros.|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, Mikau, did you get an empty bottle that you can use to carry the eggs in? One bottle can only hold one egg, so you can never have too many empty bottles. I told you that the beavers above the waterfall on the Zora Hall coast have empty bottles, right?|Evan|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You again? You can't fool me with your green clothes! You want an empty bottle, don't you? You never learn your lesson!|Little Brother Beaver|MM}}</ref>

Mikau then proceeds to infiltrate the Pirates' Fortress, but does not succeed in retrieving any of the Eggs and ends up floating perilously at the [[Great Bay Coast]], mortally injured from the attacks by the Gerudo tribe. [[Link]], upon entering the coast for the first time, finds and rescues the frail Mikau, and brings him to shore, where, using the last of his strength, he plays a final song in which he pleads Link to assist Lulu in getting her voice back.<ref>{{Cite|Baby! If I die like this... Even if I die... It won't be in peace... That's for suuure! Somebody, please rescue her eggs before the pirates take their toll. Oh, somebody, somebody, please heal my soul.|Mikau|MM}}</ref> After the fellow [[Hylian]] hero plays the "[[Song of Healing]]" for Mikau with the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]], Mikau sees a vision of himself standing in front of Lulu, before walking toward the rest of the Indigo-Go's band members, holding hands. He then eventually perishes with his body fading away, and his soul is trapped within the [[Zora Mask]]. Wearing it allows the young [[Hero of Time]] to assume the form of Mikau, known as [[Zora Link]]. His spirit appears briefly thereafter to sing Link his farewell songs: "We the Zora" and the "Zora Fight Song."<ref>{{Cite|Will you try to sing Mikau's lyrics? 1. We the Zora [...] Sharp fins are the mark of the Zora. We swim, speeding through the seas with (A) and (+). If ´ is pressed when swimming, a shield is produced to deflect enemies. (Spoken) If you have magic. We can even dive by pressing (B)! Oh-oh...We the Zora.|Mikau|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Will you try to sing Mikau's lyrics? 2. Zora Fight Song [...] For my punch of anger, hit (B). Press (B) repeatedly for a flurry of attacks! Yeah, yeah. For my deadly move, press and hold (B). Now release! It's the final blow. My double cutters!|Zora Fight Song|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Carve my farewell song on my grave... I'm depending on you to help that singer girl...|Mikau|MM}}</ref>

Mikau's [[Zora Guitar]] is placed right next to the entrance to Great Bay Coast by Link, serving as the resting place of the former guitarist.<ref>{{Cite|Herein lies Mikau, the legendary guitarist of the Zora tribe. He is to be remembered in song, and his lyrics are written here.|Tombstone|MM}}</ref> Despite this, Link as his Zora form plays the same guitar (likely a replica) whenever the Ocarina of Time [[item]] is used.

== Trivia ==
*Mikau is similar to [[Darmani III]] (the [[Terminan]] counterpart of [[Darunia]]), in that he's a hero of a non-[[Hylian]] race that unfortunately died through accidents and given their soul toward [[Link]] as a [[Transformation|transforming]] [[Mask]] to fulfill their final wishes.[[File:Lulu and Mikau holding hands.png|thumb|268x268px|Mikau holding hands with Lulu during his vision in ''Majora's Mask'', suggesting a romantic relationship]]
*Although never confirmed by [[Nintendo]] but is however strongly implied, some fans believe that Mikau is the father of the [[Zora Egg|Zora Eggs]] and husband of [[Lulu]], due to his relationship with the female Zora singer.
**Coincidentally, their pairing is a parallel to Link the [[Hero of Time]] with [[Princess Ruto]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', who is the latter's [[Hyrulean]] counterpart.
*Mikau playing his final song for Link alongside singing "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Mikau_Baby.wav ''Baby!'']" and "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Mikau_Oww.wav ''Oww!'']", seemingly uninjured, has become a popular meme among [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] community, especially since he was gravely wounded when the young hero had to rescue him to the shore and appeared comical. He also can be heard saying "''[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Mikau_Yay.wav Yay!]''" when Link reads his tombstone.
*Mikau shares the same voice with [[:en:???_(Majora's_Mask)#Majora's_Mask|???]].
*If Link plays the [[Elegy of Emptiness]] as Zora Link, a complete statue of Mikau would form by the hero.
*Mikau's [[Zora Guitar]], which serves at his tombstone, is in different areas between the original game and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]''.
**It has also become a [[Special Edition Fish Bone Guitar|very limited edition merchandise]] in January 2001 when [[Nintendo Power]] held a contest where the grand prize would receive a custom 7-string electric guitar modeled after it alongside a free copy of ''Majora's Mask'', created by Jackson Guitars. However, only seven were ever produced, making it extraordinarily rare and existing models are at extremely high prices.
