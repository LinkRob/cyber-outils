'''[[mariowiki:Goomba|Goombas]]''' are [[enemy|enemies]] exclusively found in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Is this a stand for something? It says, "{{Color|LANS Pink|Goomba}}"...|N/A|LANS}}</ref> They are basic [[mariowiki:Koopa_Troop|Koopa Troop]] foes from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] led by [[Bowser]], in which they first appeared in ''[[mariowiki:Super Mario Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Characteristics==
Goombas are brown mushroom-shaped creatures based on {{Wp|shiitake}} mushrooms.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Iwata: By the way, is it a coincidence that the Goomba looks like a mushroom?<br/>Tezuka: It's a shiitake mushroom!<br/>Iwata: It's a shiitake? (laughs) So it's not a chestnut?<br/>Tezuka: That's right. (laughs)|url= http://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/#/wii/nsmb/1/5|title= Iwata Asks: New Super Mario Bros. Wii: Mario Couldn't Jump At First|site= Nintendo.com}}</ref> Throughout their appearances in {{LA|-}} and other side-scrolling media, Goombas have a behavior of simply walking in a single direction until touching an object, turning direction only when they hit an obstacle. Goombas can be easily avoided or defeated with any attack by [[Link]], including jumping on top of them, which is a direct reference to how they are usually stomped on in the ''Mario'' series by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

==Mainline appearances==
==={{LA|-}}===
[[File:LADX Two Goombas.png|thumb|left|Link with two Goombas in {{LA|DX|-}}]]
In {{LA|-}}, the only mainline appearance of Goombas in {{TLoZ|Series}}, they appear in side-scrolling areas, predominately in dungeons. Link can also drop onto them from a platform above or by using [[Roc's Feather]] to jump on them. When they are stomped on, they drop a [[Heart]].

Goombas can be found in [[Tail Cave]], [[Bottle Grotto]], in the underground passage leading to [[Kanalet Castle]], [[Catfish's Maw]], [[Face Shrine]], and [[Turtle Rock]]. While Goombas appear mostly in side-scrolling areas, they also appear in top-down locations in [[Eagle's Tower]] sometimes near a [[Kirby]]. In these areas they move around randomly. Link can still stomp on them with Roc's Feather.

==Crossover appearances==
===[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]]===
===={{SSBM|-}}====
Goombas are enemies encountered in the first stage of Adventure Mode, [[smashwiki:Mushroom Kingdom (Adventure)|Mushroom Kingdom]], in {{SSBM}}. Like in {{LA|-}}, if Link - in either the [[Hero of Time]]'s [[Young Link|Young]] or Adult form, is the playable character, he can use his [[Sword]] to defeat a Goomba.

An obtainable Goomba [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|trophy]] is also the focus of a Trophy Tussle challenge, much like with [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]].

===={{SSBB|-}}====
[[File:Goombas SSBB.png|thumb|Two Goombas in Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary of {{SSBB|-}}|241x241px]]
Goombas are enemies in [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]], where they behave similarly as in {{SSBM|-}}. The only stage where Link encounters Goombas during the storyline is {{ssb|The Cave}}, or {{ssb|The Great Maze}}, which has just about every fighter available. The first three stages where Goombas appear is in {{ssb|The Jungle}}, {{ssb|The Lake}}, and {{ssb|The Cave}}, where they can optionally be encountered by Link or one of the other {{TLoZ|-}} fighters—[[Princess Zelda]], [[Ganondorf]], and [[Toon Link]]—should they decide to revisit there.

===={{SSB4|3DS|-}}====
Goombas are enemies in [[Smash Run]] mode. They behave similarly as in previous single-player modes of the {{SSB|-}} series.

==={{SMM2|-}}===
In {{SMM2}}, Goombas are enemies which can be encountered by Mario, or Link if he is under the effects of the [[Master Sword]]. Behaviorally, Goombas have little difference than in {{LA|-}}, the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series, or 2D installments of the ''Super Mario'' series in general. Aside from being vulnerable to a stomp attack from either Mario or Link, Goombas can similarly be defeated from the latter's [[sword]].

== Trivia ==

* Goombas were orignally called '''Little Goombas''' in the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] era.
* Although the most common and weakest enemy in the ''Mario'' franchise, Goombas do come in various more dangerous forms. For example, in some games, a Mega variant, called the Grand Goomba, can cause more damage.
* The Goomba's appearance in {{LA|-}} and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Switch remake]] is rather unusual, as the enemy in ''Mario'' is shown with a frowning face and thinner eyebrows instead of smiling.

==Nomenclature==
The name Goomba is possibly derived from the Italian slang word ''goombah'', which can mean friend or alternately a member of a gang. Their Japanese name, ''Kuribō'', is a portmanteau ''kuri'', meaning chestnut, and ''bō'', meaning guy.
