{{Term/Store|Melari||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Melari}}
{{Term|TMC|Melari}} is the elder of the {{Term|TMC|Mountain Minish|link}} who live on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} and is a revered {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} blacksmith.<ref>{{Cite|If your conviction holds strong, head to {{Color|TMC Green|Mount Crenel}}. There, you will find a man named {{Color|TMC Green|Melari}}. Among all the Minish, there is no one more able to repair your sword. If you ask him, I am certain he will reforge your {{Color|TMC Red|broken Picori Blade}}.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref> As the leader of {{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine|link}}, he helps {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} reforge the {{Term|TMC|Broken Picori Blade|link}} into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'd be happy to reforge this thing into a brand-new sacred blade for you.|Melari|TMC}}</ref> He also directs {{Term|TMC|Link}} to the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}} to find the second of the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In the meantime, you should track down the missing {{Color|TMC Red|elements}}. One of them should be in the {{Color|TMC Green|mine}} the humans dug. It's not far from here.|Melari|TMC}}</ref> After the {{Term|TMC|White Sword}} has been reforged, {{Term|TMC|Melari}} tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} that he must infuse it with the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements}} in the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}}, the entrance to which is somewhere in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}}.<ref>{{Cite|The doorway to the {{Color|TMC Green|sanctuary}} opens only once every hundred years. I believe the entrance is somewhere in {{Color|TMC Green|Hyrule Castle}}.|Melari|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can return to {{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine}} and {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Melari}},<ref>{{Cite|So you want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let’s do it!|Melari|TMC}}</ref> resulting in a {{Term|TMC|Bean|link}} planted atop {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} to grow into a beanstalk; at its top is 160 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} and a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}.
