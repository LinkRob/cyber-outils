{{Term/Store|David Jr.||Series, TWW, TWWHD, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259 {{Exp Game|TWW}} & 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|David Jr.}}
In {{TWW|-}}, David Jr. stays at the top of [[Tingle Tower]], where he works with [[Ankle]]. Despite this, he is not actually related to the {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Siblings?|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This guy here and I are complete and utter strangers! I kid you not!|David Jr.|TWW}}</ref> According to him, he was originally a sailor who sailed the sea aimlessly, and ended up on [[Tingle Island]] after being shipwrecked by a storm one night.<ref>{{Cite|Once upon a time, I was a sailor. One night, as I was floating aimlessly across the sea as I usually did... That night... Yes, I remember it vividly. There was a fierce and terrible storm! I was swallowed up by a giant twister, and my boat was spun round and round! ...That is the last thing I remember.|David Jr.|TWW}}</ref> However, according to his Figurine, he was in search of the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship|link}}'s treasure which his father had found, and ended up at Tingle Island after feeling seasick. He awoke wearing his current uniform, which he hates, and was forced to work for [[Tingle]].<ref>{{Cite|When I awoke, I was dressed like this! And what do you think I was doing? But I guess telling this story to you won't do me any good... It sure won't get that crazy guy in green off my back!|David Jr.|TWW}}</ref> He looks very similar to Tingle, except he wears white, and he does not have rosy patches on his cheeks.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|David Jr.}} appears in {{TMC}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, by the water south of {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's House|link}}. When spoken to, {{Term|TMC|David Jr.}} offers to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} after recognizing him as "Mr. Fairy".<ref>{{Cite|Are you Mr. Fairy? Oh, yeah. Look at those clothes. You've got to be him! I received some {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone pieces}} from {{Color|TMC Green|Tingle}}. He said I should try and {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse}} them with you, Mr. Fairy!|David Jr.|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} successfully {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with him,<ref>{{Cite|Let's {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones!}} I hope they fit...|David Jr.|TMC}}</ref> the southeastern {{Term|TMC|Tree|link}} in the group of four {{Plural|TMC|Tree}} in {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}} will open, revealing a chamber with a {{Term|TMC|Switch|link}} connected to a {{Term|TMC|Torchlight|link}} and a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} with 200 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}}. {{Term|TMC|David Jr.}} will also express surprise that they managed to a successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}}, claiming to not expect it to work.<ref>{{Cite|Wow. I didn't actually think that would work...|David Jr.|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with all four of the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Siblings?|link}}, the final {{Term|TMC|Switch}} will cause a {{Term|TMC|Ladder|link}} to descend, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to open a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} containing the {{Term|TMC|Magical Boomerang|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|David Jr.}} will acknowledge that he is not related to {{Term|TMC|Tingle|link}} or his brothers, and questions why he helps them.<ref>{{Cite|I'm not even related to that Tingle! So...why am I helping him so much?|David Jr.|TMC}}</ref> When spoken to afterwards, {{Term|TMC|David Jr.}} will tell {{Term|TMC|Link}} how many {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} he has left to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, oh, oh... You still have XX Kinstones left to fuse? Still a ways to go! Sigh... If only I could search for happiness myself...|David Jr.|TMC}}</ref> He, along with the rest of the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Siblings?}}, believes that {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} will help him meet {{Plural|TMC|Fairy|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They believe fusing Kinstones will help them meet fairies, so they are recording Kinstone data on their maps.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>
