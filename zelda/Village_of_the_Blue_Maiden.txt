The {{Term/Store|Village of the Blue Maiden||Series, FSA}} is a location in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 112 {{Exp Game|FSA}}}}</ref> It is the second stage in [[Eastern Hyrule#Four Swords Adventures|Eastern Hyrule]] in {{FSA}}. The village has no enemies, other than the pair of [[Shadow Link]]s as its boss. The many inhabitants of the area provide an altogether different experience, as much of the level consists of completing various tasks for village residents. The [[Moon Pearl]] and [[Dark World]] are introduced here. Most of the children of the village seem to be trapped in the Dark World, and thus a guild of secret agents, known as the [[Seeker's Guild]], are investigating the [[mage]]s in connection with the disappearance of the children.

[[Kaepora Gaebora]] reveals to the [[Links]] that the [[Four Sword]] "smites Darkness and grants the power to see the shadowy [[Moon Gate|Dark World portals]]."

Minigames in the Village of the [[Blue Maiden]]:
*[[Shooting Gallery]], no [[bow]] is required, the prize is equal to the value of the [[Force Gem]]s hit.
*[[Treasure Chest Game]], risk 100 Force Gems to chose one of 3 chests in a Dark World home. 
*[[Race (Mini-Game)|Race]], 20 seconds to reach a goal, to obtain [[Pegasus Boots]] (having [[bomb]]s on hand is very advantageous).
