==Biography==
Gumo is an [[Anouki]] living on the [[Isle of Frost]]. It lives in the luxurious [[Anouki Estates]] and is among those Link questions while searching for a [[Yook]] who has abducted and impersonated one of the villagers. Gumo warns Link that either [[Fofo]] or [[Aroo]] is lying to him.<ref>{{Cite|My name's Gumo. Either Fofo or Aroo has gotta be lying to you. I just wanted to tell you that...|Gumo|PH}}</ref> Fofo tells Link that Gumo is honest, disqualifying either of them from being the Yook.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, I'm Fofo. Gumo and me, we never lie. It's a rule we got, so neither of us is the Yook!|Fofo|PH}}</ref>
