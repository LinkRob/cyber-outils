The {{Term/Store|Maw of Death Mountain||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|'''Maw of Death Mountain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''Climbing Path Trailhead''',<ref name="Marker">{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #1<br/>Climbing Path Trailhead|Sign|BotW}}</ref> is a location in {{BotW}}.

==Features and Overview==
The Maw of Death Mountain is an area in [[Eldin Canyon]] that acts as the main entrance into [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin]] proper. It begins at the point between [[Lake Ferona]] and [[Lake Intenoch]], where the paving road separates the two Lakes, and lies just ahead of a stone arch. The Maw marks the beginning of the Trail that leads to [[Goron City]] and finally [[Death Mountain]] itself. It is also denoted as Death Mountain Marker #1,<ref name="Marker"/> making it the first of a string of landmarks guiding the way to [[Death Mountain Summit]].
