The {{Term/Store|Sealed Grounds||Series, SS, SSHD}},<ref>{{Cite|Sealed Grounds|Map|SSHD}}</ref> once known as '''[[Hylia's Realm]]''',{{Fact}} is a location in {{SS}}. Located near the [[Faron Woods]], it is home to an [[Impa|old woman]] and the seal that holds [[The Imprisoned]].

==Features and Overview==
===Present===
The Sealed Grounds is the first area on the [[Hyrule|Surface]] that [[Link]] explores after descending from [[The Sky]]. The Sealed Grounds consists of a large pit that spirals downward, lined by a series of air vents,<ref>{{Cite|I recommend using the air vents to move ahead of the monster instead of chasing it from behind.|Fi|SS}}</ref> and contains the magical [[Sealing Spike]] by which [[The Imprisoned]] is held.<ref>{{Cite|I can confirm that the sealing spike observed at the top of its head is the same object originally found in the Sealed Grounds.|Fi|SS}}</ref> At the top of the pit is the [[Sealed Temple]], which connects to [[Faron Woods]] through a small glade found outside its eastern exit. Throughout his journey, Link returns to this area multiple times to fend off The Imprisoned as it breaks its seal and ascends the spiral path towards the Temple.

After Link acquires the [[Triforce]], which he uses to wish for the destruction of [[Demise]], the [[Statue of the Goddess]] breaks away from the rest of [[Skyloft]] and descends into the spiral pit of the Sealed Grounds and onto The Imprisoned, killing it and merging seamlessly into the ground from which it was taken.

===Past===
Link visits the Sealed Grounds in the past through the newly formed [[Gate of Time]] and discovers that the Sealed Temple was once known as the Temple of Hylia.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Temple of Hylia, though it will come to be known as the Sealed Temple sometime in the future.|Impa|SS}}</ref> He is unable to explore the Sealed Grounds during his initial visits and is confined to the Temple of Hylia.

It is here that he finally reunites with [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], and she explains the creation of the Triforce,<ref>{{Cite|The old gods created a supreme power that gave anyone who possessed it the ability to shape reality and fulfill any desire. They called it the Triforce.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> the war with Demise,<ref>{{Cite|In his thirst to make the world his own, Demise readied a massive army of monsters for war. He sought to take the Triforce for himself by force.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> the ascension of Skyloft,<ref>{{Cite|The goddess feared for her people. She used her power to send both them and the Triforce into the sky on a slice of earth she cut away from the land. This floating rock became the new home of our people. In time, it came to be known as Skyloft.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> the sealing of Demise,<ref>{{Cite|After a long and fierce battle, the goddess, Hylia, succeeded in sealing away Demise. However, soon after the demon king was imprisoned, it became clear that the seal would not hold long against his fearsome power.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> the creation of [[Fi]] and the choosing of a Hero,<ref>{{Cite|First, she created Fi. She made the spirit that resides in your sword to serve a single purpose: to assist her chosen hero on his mission.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> and Zelda's existence as Hylia reborn.<ref>{{Cite|Her second plan...was to abandon her divine form and transfer her soul to the body of a mortal.|Zelda|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You are the chosen hero, and I, Zelda... I am the goddess reborn as a mortal.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> Zelda explains that to maintain the seal on [[Demise]], she must enter a sleep that will last thousands of years,<ref>{{Cite|To ensure that the seal holds, I will remain here in this time...deep in sleep for thousands of years.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> and as she does, she asks the young hero to do the very thing she did for him since they were kids - wake her up.<ref>{{Cite|So I'm going to ask you a favor, sleepyhead. Ever since we were kids, I'd always be the one to wake you up when you slept in. But this time, when all of this is over, will you come to wake me up?|Zelda|SS}}</ref>

Later in the game, Link learns from the [[Ancient Robot]]s in [[Lanayru Gorge]] that the [[Life Tree Seedling]] - whose fruit they hoped to use to heal the ill [[Thunder Dragon]], cannot flourish in the inhospitable [[Lanayru Province]]. Link takes the Seedling and returns to the Temple of Hylia to plant it in a mound of soil there,<ref>{{Cite|Do you see that sunny patch of earth over there? I thought it might be nice to plant a tree of some sort there. I imagine it would please Zelda greatly to see a great tree welcoming her back when she wakes from her slumber.|Impa|SS}}</ref> though he is still unable to traverse the Sealed Grounds.

Link travels to the Sealed Grounds a final time after [[Ghirahim]] abducts Zelda and flees with her into the past through the Gate of Time. After overwhelming Impa,<ref>{{Cite|Urgh... Link...? It's Ghirahim... He appeared out of thin air and got the drop on me. Don't worry about me... You must chase after him!|Impa|SS}}</ref> he escapes through the great doors of the Temple of Hylia to the Sealed Grounds and attempts to resurrect Demise with Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|Ghirahim ran through the great doors at the front of the temple. If you hurry, you may yet stop him!|Impa|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But the past...oh, the past... So full of possibilities. I shall resurrect him there with the divine soul of this golden-haired girl!|Ghirahim|SS}}</ref> He calls forth [[Horde Battle|hordes of enemies]] to occupy Link while he conducts the ritual to revive Demise,<ref>{{Cite|Hear me, my hordes! The spell is nearly complete! The demon king returns! Until then, you WILL keep that whelp from interfering with my ritual. I don't care if the whole lot of you get lodged on the end of his blade. You will buy me the time I need! Do not fear him... Fear my wrath if you fail me!|Ghirahim|SS}}</ref> but they are ultimately unsuccessful and Ghirahim is forced to battle Link himself. Link overpowers Ghirahim, but the latter's ritual continues and Demise is awakened.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... But never mind that. Victory is still mine to savor. While we fought, the ritual I started continued... At last, it's complete! The demon king shall devour the soul of the goddess and resurrect in his full glory!|Ghirahim|SS}}</ref> Demise invites Link to do battle, and the area where the seal on The Imprisoned once was becomes a portal to a place where Demise says they "will not be bothered by distractions."<ref>{{Cite|Very well, then. I shall prepare a place for us where we will not be bothered by distractions. If you still have the courage to face me, seek me here.|Demise|SS}}</ref>
