The {{Term/Store|Western Wood||Series, TMC}} is an area in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Western Wood}} is located in western {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. It connects to three other areas: {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}} to the west, {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} to the east, and {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}} to the north.

The {{Term|TMC|Western Wood}} is home to a few benign inhabitants, such as the poet {{Term|TMC|Percy|link}} and a lone {{Term|TMC|Forest Minish|link}}, with whom {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}, causing a beanstalk near his house to grow. A special {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}} will remove the thorns that block entrance to a certain {{Term|TMC|Tree|link}} in which a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} is hidden. Scattered throughout the area are several other fallen {{Plural|TMC|Tree}} that can be moved through various {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}}. This area is connected to {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} to the east, {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}} to the north, and {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}} to the west.
