==Characteristics==

{{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast}} are dark, willowy beings that drop out of {{Plural|TPHD|Portal (Object)|link}} all across {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}} and in the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight Realm|link}}. Most travel in packs but some can be found alone. {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast}} are {{Term|TPHD|Twili|link}} members that have been corrupted and turned into monsters by the false king of the {{Term|TPHD|Twili}}, {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It was a peaceful place... until Zant took control of the Twilight Realm and transformed all of the Twili into shadow beasts.|Midna|TP}}</ref> {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast}} are strong enough that only a few were needed to overrun and defeat the scores of {{Plural|TPHD|Hylian Soldier|link}} guarding {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda|link}}, despite the fact that the latter had advantageous numbers. It is also hinted by {{Term|TPHD|Barnes|link}} that they are not only composed of transformed {{Term|TPHD|Twili}}, but also have the ability to turn {{Plural|TPHD|Human|link}} into their kind,<ref>{{Cite|They sure didn't seem impressed by my bombs! How long do you think we can hold this sanctuary against beasts that strong, huh? Once they attack, it's OVER! Remember the lady from the general store? Just one of those things attacked her, and a whole gang from town went to save her! And what happened? She was already gone, and there were TWO monsters waitin'! ...You connectin' the dots? That means that if we get attacked by them, then we'll be...|Barnes|TP}}</ref> meaning that they are not restricted to one species.

If one {{Term|TPHD|Shadow Beast}} is left standing during a group battle, it will let out a howl that revives the others.<ref>{{Cite|If you leave the last one alive, it's just going to use that shriek to bring the others back.|Midna|TP}}</ref> Because of this, at least the last two must be defeated at the same time in order to truly defeat a group.<ref>{{Cite|No need to take these little pests on one at a time, right? You can take them all at once! So... You can handle this by yourself, can't you?|Midna|TP}}</ref> Once there are only a few left, most attacks with a wide area of effect can finish them,<ref>{{Cite|You just hold down B and I'll expand an energy field. Enclose all of your enemies within it and release the button!|Midna|TP}}</ref> although some (such as the {{Term|TPHD|Jump Strike|link}} or the {{Term|TPHD|Mortal Draw|link}}) will not. Defeating a group of {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast}} allows {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to warp to the point where they were defeated.

Different {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast}} are seen in the cutscene which shows {{Term|TPHD|Zant}} taking {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}}'s history of the {{Term|TPHD|Twili}}. These are larger than conventional {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast}}, and bear a different head-dressing; a round silver plate with a special marking on them. However, these monsters are never seen during actual gameplay, so their status and any exclusive powers remain a mystery. It can be speculated that these variations of Shadow Beasts are equivalent to royal guards as they are always present next to Zant.

In the {{Term|TPHD|Palace of Twilight|link}} there are {{Plural|TPHD|Twili}} who have not fully turned into {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Midna Blue|Wait! This guy isn't an enemy, Link. They might look different, but... they're inhabitants of this world... They're my people. Zant... How could he...}}|Midna|TPHD}}</ref> They can be transformed back into {{Plural|TPHD|Twili}} by bringing a {{Term|TPHD|Sol|link}} near them.

==Nomenclature==
