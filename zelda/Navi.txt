==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Since [[Link]] is not a [[Kokiri]] but actually a [[Hylian]] and does not have a Fairy to call his own, Navi is summoned by the [[The Great Deku Tree|Great Deku Tree]] to accompany him on his quest to save [[Hyrule]] as a partner.<ref name= "Partner"/><ref name= "Navi">{{Cite|Navi the fairy... Thou must aid Link... And Link... When Navi  speaks, use (^) to listen well to her words of wisdom...|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> Throughout the [[Nintendo 64]] game, Navi provides Link with a variety of clues to help him solve puzzles and progress in his quest.<ref>{{Cite|Look at this wall! The vines growing on it give it a rough surface... Maybe you can climb it, Link!|Navi|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wow! Look at all those Bomb Flowers! Is there any way you can set them all off at once?|Navi|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Should we believe what Sheik said and go to Kakariko Village?|Navi|OoT}}</ref> Navi also helps the [[Hero of Time]] learn the main controls in the game, teaching him how to open doors and how to push and pull blocks.<ref>{{Cite|You can open a door by standing in front of it and pressing (A). Pay attention to what the Action Icon says. That's the blue icon at the top of the screen!|Navi|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Stand next to this block and grab hold of it with (A). While holding (A), you can push or pull it. If you stand next to the block and press (A) while pressing (+) towards the block, you can climb on top of it. Pay attention to what the Action Icon says!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> The player can listen to Navi's advice by pressing the C-Up Button in the controller whenever her name flashes on the screen, which she yells out "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Navi_Hey3.wav ''Hey!'']".<ref name= "Navi"/> When the button is pressed, she will say "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Navi_Listen3.wav ''Listen!'']". She does this to guide Link on his next objective (similarly to [[Kaepora Gaebora|Kaepora Geabora]] and [[Sheik]]) or explain an [[enemy]]'s abilities or weaknesses.
[[File:Young Link Navi.png|thumb|Link and Navi]]
Navi's primary purpose is to function as a focusing target. By pressing the Z Button, Navi targets the most nearby object, enemy, or person. She glows yellow to indicate when she is targeting an enemy, blue when Link can talk to someone from a distance, and green when she is targeting something of interest. Any time Navi targets an enemy or something suspicious, she yells out to Link "''Hey!''", "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Navi_Look3.wav ''Look!'']", or "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Navi_WatchOut3.wav ''Watch out!'']" Pressing the C-Up Button while targeting an enemy allows Link to hear Navi's information about said foe. Once Link learns "[[Saria's Song]]," he can communicate with [[Saria]] through Navi.<ref>{{Cite|You want to talk to Saria, right? /Talk to Saria /No|Navi|OoT}}</ref> When Navi's help is not being used, she seemingly hides herself inside Link's hat. However, she can be summoned at any time by pressing the A Button while Link is standing still (she will return to his hat if remaining still for 5 seconds). In {{OoT3D}}, Navi also often tells Link to take a rest from playing, breaking the 4th wall to players.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= While Navi does sometimes give useful hints, more often then not she interrupts the game to repeatedly suggest that you take a break from playing.|magazine= Nintendo Power|publisher= |issue= 268|date= June 2011|page= 82}}</ref>

Although Navi can target most enemies or [[Boss|bosses]] and give Link clues on how to defeat them, during the battle against {{Term|OoT3D|Ganondorf|link}}, Navi is unable to help him due to the waves of darkness produced by the King of Evil (though she can still target him gameplay-wise).<ref>{{Cite|Link! I can't help you! Because of the waves of darkness, I can't get close! I'm sorry, Link!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> Once Link defeats Ganondorf and escapes the crumbling [[Ganon's Castle]] along with [[Princess Zelda]], Navi apologizes to him for not having helped him in the battle.<ref name=":0">{{Cite|Link... I'm sorry I couldn't help you in the battle before!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> However, Ganondorf emerges shortly after from the castle ruins and transforms into [[Ganon]]. Navi, willing to aid Link, exclaims that she will not be held back by the villain again and joins him in the final battle (although like Ganon's human form, she does not know the weaknesses of the [[demon]]), where they eventually defeat the King of Evil.<ref>{{Cite|There's no way he's going to hold me back again! This time, we fight together!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> After Princess Zelda sends the Hero of Time back to his original time, Navi can be seen leaving Link's side for undisclosed reasons as she flies away into the lit window inside the [[Temple of Time]], as her mission of guiding him to save Hyrule has been completed.

During the prologue of {{MM}}, Link sets on a personal journey to find Navi after the events of {{OoT|-}}. Throughout his adventure in [[Termina]], [[Tatl]] replaces her for the same purpose during gameplay, although she answers to Link with bell sounds rather than actually speaking and her personality is drastically different.<ref>{{Cite|Done with the battles he once waged across time, he embarked on a journey. A secret and personal journey... A journey in search of a beloved and invaluable friend... A friend with whom he parted ways when he finally fulfilled his heroic destiny and took his place among legends...|Prologue|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link set off on his horse, Epona, who lived at Lon Lon Ranch. Months passed as he wandered in search of his companion, Navi, eventually losing himself in a mysterious forest. It just so happened that this forest led to a parallel world known as Termina.|book=HH|page= 110}}</ref>

At the end of ''Majora's Mask'', Link parts ways with Tatl and departs from Termina to once again attempt to search for Navi in the [[Lost Woods]]. However, it is very likely the Hero of Time sadly never could find her in spite of his best efforts, as she was never seen or heard again anywhere in the main series since the ending of ''Ocarina of Time''. Consequently, what lies of Navi's existence later into the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Child Timeline]] remains a complete mystery.

==Development==
When the concept of [[Targeting System|Z-targeting]] was being developed, an upside-down triangle was used to determine which enemy Link was targeting. The designers felt this marker was too simple, so they wanted to replace it with a cute Fairy girl like in earlier games, believing it would fit the ''Zelda'' atmosphere. However, the [[Nintendo 64]] was not able to support such a Fairy due to hardware limitations, so a mere ball of light with 2D wings was used instead. She was originally called the "Fairy Navigation System," but was later simply named Navi. After naming her, many of her functions were designed, such as changing color to distinguish between friends and foes, acting as a guide, and providing strategy tips. She herself is distinguished by healing Fairies as well as the Kokiri's companions by her light blue color.

Her design also allowed developers to overcome a problem in [[Kokiri Forest]]. Due to the N64's limitations, it was difficult to display the villagers and structures all at once, so each person was paired with a Fairy to help locate them. When Link approached them, their owners would appear from thin air and he could interact with them. Both of these expanded the story so that Link was the only child in the forest without a Fairy, then he met and parted with a Fairy companion.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= |retrieved= |url= http://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/#/3ds/zelda-ocarina-of-time/1/3|title= Iwata Asks: Ocarina of Time 3D Original Development Staff - Part 1|site= Official Nintendo website|type=}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*In an interview with series creator Shigeru Miyamoto, he mentioned that Navi is jealous of [[Princess Zelda]], as she feels something for [[Link]].<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= However, this time Navie [the fairy] is jealous of Zelda. So within the story, Navie feels something for Link.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= http://www.ign.com/articles/1997/12/04/the-z-files|title= The Z-Files}}</ref> This implies she has a love interest in the [[Hero of Time]] incarnation, not unlike [[Princess Ruto]], Zelda, [[Saria]], [[Malon]], and possibly [[Nabooru]].
**A possible in-game hint of this can be seen just before the final battle against [[Ganon]], when Link and Princess Zelda happily look at each other and then Navi speaks to the former, apologizing for not being able to help him.<ref name=":0" />
**In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|''Ocarina of Time'' manga]], she indeed tells Link she loves him at the end.
**Navi being canonically jealous of Zelda resembled a situation in the 1953 Disney film ''[https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Pan_(film) Peter Pan]'', where [https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Tinker_Bell Tinker Bell] got similarly jealous of the attention [https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Wendy_Darling Wendy] got from [https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Pan_(character) Peter Pan].
*Navi's "''Hey! Listen!''", although rather notorious to some fans, is one of the most [[Iconic quotes in The Legend of Zelda series|iconic quotes]] in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]].[[File:OoT beta Biggoron's Sword attacks.gif|thumb|213x213px|A red colored Navi as seen in beta footage of ''Ocarina of Time'']]
*At one point during development of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', Navi could carry things for Link.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= Press the B-Button, and you can speak with Navie. She can also carry things for Link.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= http://www.ign.com/articles/1997/11/27/the-z-files-4|title= The Z-Files}}</ref> She would also turn red when there was something dangerous around, which is scrapped as she instead turns yellow in the final release.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= Depending on the item, Navi will change to a different color. For example, she'll turn red if there's something dangerous, etc.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= http://www.ign.com/articles/1997/11/27/the-z-files-4|title= The Z-Files}}</ref>
**Intriguingly, [[Tatl]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' changes into a red color when speaking to [[Sakon]] in [[Ikana Canyon]].
**Some artwork of the game showing Link in combat against enemies depict Navi in a red color.
*Prior to the release of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'', Navi is one of the few characters with any voice acting in the longtime series which uses actual English words.
*In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|''Ocarina of Time'' (Himekawa)]] manga, Navi refers to herself in the third person.
*When Navi flies toward [[Link's House|Link's house]] while being sent by the [[The Great Deku Tree|Great Deku Tree]], she can be heard saying "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Navi_Hello3.wav ''Hello!'']" when interacting with the female Kokiri on top of the [[Kokiri Shop]]. She also greets Link himself when waking him up.
*Navi's [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Navi_Out.wav flying sound] is one of the most iconic sound effects among the series' fanbase.
*She is said to be tomboyish, though to a lesser degree compared to Tatl in ''Majora's Mask''.
*Navi has a [[Cameos of The Legend of Zelda in other media|cameo]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' during the Subspace Emissary, accompanying the Link from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', who is the Hero of Time's descendant in the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Child Timeline]].
**A very similar Fairy to Navi known as [[Ciela]] appears in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|Phantom Hourglass]]'', used to guide Link the Hero of Winds with the controls much like in ''Ocarina of Time''. She even uses some of Navi's voice clips.
*Navi's "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Ruto_Throw.wav ''Eep!'']" when she [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Navi_Bonk.wav comically hits the brown gate] before entering Link's house at the beginning of the game is reused for Princess Ruto whenever she gets startled.[[File:TPG Link Navi Cameo.png|thumb|234x234px|Navi's cameo with Link in ''The Powerpuff Girls'']]
*Navi, alongside the Hero of Time, has a [[The Legend of Zelda in Popular Culture|pop culture]] appearance in the popular 1998 Cartoon Network superhero TV show ''[[wikipedia:The_Powerpuff_Girls|The Powerpuff Girls]]''. A scene based on the then-new ''Ocarina of Time'' shows a shieldless Link repeatedly running into walls while Navi says "''Hey!''" multiple times. After suddenly being crushed by a [[Block]], Link reaches his [[Sword]] (presumably the [[Kokiri Sword]]) out, with Navi saying "''Hey!''" two more times, then the young hero abruptly slashes at her, killing the Fairy instantly (something fans who hated the character wished they could do at the time). It then ends with the Mayor getting a [[Game Over]] in spite of technically still having health remaining. Humorously, Link in the cameo loses three [[Heart Container|Heart Containers]] after murdering Navi, the same amount as he was hit by the block (which only inflicts one in the actual game). In this appearance, she was voiced by the iconic actress [[wikipedia:Tara_Strong|Tara Strong]], who also voices the character [https://powerpuffgirls.fandom.com/wiki/Bubbles_(1998_TV_series) Bubbles].
*Navi and the other Fairies in [[Kokiri Forest]] being accompanied by a person were inspired by Tinker Bell from Disney's version of ''Peter Pan''.
*Navi appears as the guide of the Japanese {{OoT|-}} Official Guide Video, where she is fully voiced.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Gamer Night Live|published= January 8, 2012|retrieved= |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TLu5csyn3IQ|title= Ocarina of Time Japanese Promo|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Navi's name comes from the word "navigation." Navi was originally supposed to be named "Fairy Navigation System," but since the name was too long, the developers changed it simply to Navi.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= I called it the Fairy Navigation System, took it to Osawa-san, and asked, "How's this?" He immediately said, "Let's name it Navi." Because she navigates!|name= Yoshiaki Koizumi|url= http://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/#/3ds/zelda-ocarina-of-time/1/3|title= Iwata Asks: Ocarina of Time 3D Original Development Staff - Part 1}}</ref>
