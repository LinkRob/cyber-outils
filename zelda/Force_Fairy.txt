{{Term/Store|Force Fairy|Force Fairies|Series, FSA|plural}} are a type of [[Fairy]] that appear in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|FSA}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|FSA|Force Fairy|Refill your Heart Meter and increase the number of Continues you have.}}
Force Fairies are gained upon successfully completing each stage. For each thousand [[Force Gem]]s the [[Links]] have in their possession at the end of a level, they are rewarded one Force Fairy.<ref>{{Cite|The Force Gems transformed into fairies!|N/A|FSA}}</ref> Hidden Force Fairies may be found in several dungeons.

Whenever the Links die, one of these fairies will appear from within the [[Four Sword]], revive the Links, and allow them to continue their quest.<ref>{{Cite|A Force Fairy! They can revive the fallen Links!|N/A|FSA}}</ref> When the Links die and have no force fairies left, the game is over.

The Links are allowed a maximum of ninety-nine Force Fairies.
