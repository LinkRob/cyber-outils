==Characteristics ==
{{Plural|CoH|Broadsword Lizalfos}} are enemies appearing in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}} during the [[Symphony of the Mask]] campaign. When they see [[Skull Kid]], they rush towards him with their broadsword and damage him upon contact. They can be defeated the same as any other Lizalfos.
