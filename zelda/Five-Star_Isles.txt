{{Term/Store|Five-Star Isles||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (G7) is a location in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Fishman|Have you seen it, small fry? ...The Ghost Ship? Cause it's real. It's even been seen here, in these very waters... On nights when the crescent moon faces down... That's when horror rises...}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>Five-Star Isles</p><p>I can't handle rumors that the Ghost Ship has been sighted there. Way too scary.</p>

A [[Piece of Heart]] can be retrieved by entering a [[Submarine]] nearby, and eradicating all the enemies within. [[Link]] can locate and obtain a second Piece of Heart by sailing to the marker set near the island by [[Treasure Chart]] #33, which is found on [[Windfall Island]].

This island is also a recurring destination of the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship|link}}, seen only the night when the moon is at its waxing crescent phase in the night sky.
