{{Plural|BotW|Torch}} are a common {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use to carry {{Term|BotW|Fire}} with him and spread that {{Term|BotW|Fire}} to stationary {{Plural|BotW|Torch}} and other flammable objects such as grass, bushes, and wood. {{Plural|BotW|Torch}} can also be used as a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, but they have a very low attack power of 2. After being ignited by contact with a {{Term|BotW|Fire}}, {{Plural|BotW|Torch}} stay lit until {{Term|BotW|Link}} puts the {{Term|BotW|Torch}} away or it rains.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Torch}} are useful for navigating dark areas, such as the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins|link}}. Being made of wood, they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will float in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} if dropped.

After first exiting the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} and meeting the {{Term|BotW|Old Man|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can steal the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}}'s {{Term|BotW|Torch}}. He will then ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he intends to use it for.<ref>{{Cite|Well then, just help yourself to that torch there. And how, may I ask, are you planning to use it?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} replies saying that he plans to use it as a {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} will advise him to take his {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe|link}} instead.<ref>{{Cite|[As a weapon!] Innovative... However, that is probably best used to light fires. If you need a weapon, you can find something better. Many beasts roam the land past this point. You can take my axe with you, if you please. Assuming you can find it.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells him that he plans to use it to light {{Plural|BotW|Fire}}, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} will tell him to be cautious.<ref>{{Cite|[To set things on fire!] That is rather...unnerving. Please be cautious with that around any dry grass that might catch fire.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to tell the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} what he plans to do with the {{Term|BotW|Torch}}, he will tell him to do as he pleases and will let him be.<ref>{{Cite|[It's a secret...] I see, Well then, do as you please.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Plural|BotW|Torch}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods|link}}, which can aid him in navigating the area by lighting {{Plural|BotW|Torch}} and watching the direction the {{Term|BotW|Fire}} blows with the wind. {{Plural|BotW|Torch}} are also useful for lighting all of the {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|link}} around the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, as {{Term|BotW|Link}} must travel long distances without the {{Term|BotW|Fire}} burning out.

{{Plural|BotW|Torch}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Daily Use Goods|link}} equipment archetype, which consists of unconventional {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can make use of.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>

==={{TotK|-}}===
In ''The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom'', Torches can be found in various places, such as at Stables. Once lit on fire, Torches do not burn out. A Torch's flame can be extinguished by water or by the player unequipping the Torch.

{{Plural|CoH|Torch}} allow {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} to see in the dark, light up braziers, and burn stumps that may be blocking their path.<ref>{{Cite|Increases visibility in dark areas.
