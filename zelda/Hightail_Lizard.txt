==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hightail Lizard}}
{{Plural|BotW|Hightail Lizard}} are common creatures, and can be encountered in warm and temperate areas throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be found basking on {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} or rocks, or they can also be startled out of hiding when {{Term|BotW|Grass|link}} is cut. They often take a moment to register {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} presence, but flee quickly once they do.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Hightail Lizard}} can be {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}} with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make a {{Term|BotW|Hasty Elixir|link}}, which will temporarily increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s speed when consumed.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} them with other ingredients, however, will make {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} will also accept them as ingredients for {{Term|BotW|Brown Dye|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Hightail Lizard}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}:
