{{Term/Store|Mido||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239{{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref> He is the moody leader of the [[Kokiri]].<ref>{{Cite|House of the Great Mido - Boss of the Kokiri|Sign|OoT}}</ref>

==Biography==
Mido has a reputation of being mean to others and bossing them around, but he respects the [[Great Deku Tree]] and craves to be liked by [[Saria]].<ref>{{Cite|That meanie, {{Color|OoT3D Red|Mido}}, is making me cut the grass at Saria's house. Mido told Saria he would do it so she would like him, but I'm the one doing all the work! You and {{Color|OoT3D Red|Saria}} are close friends, so will you help me cut the grass? I'll let you keep anything you find while cutting it.|Kokiri|OoT3D}}</ref> This may be the two main reasons for his condescending attitude towards [[Link]], who is both favoured by the Great Deku Tree and best friend to Saria. Mido's prime target for his prejudice toward Link's being, is his "[[Hylian|not quite Kokiri-ness]]." Such as that, for a long time, Link didn't have a [[Fairy]] partner, resulting in Mido calling him "Mr. No Fairy."<ref>{{Cite|Hey you! "Mr. No Fairy!" What's your business with the Great Deku Tree? Without a fairy, you're not even a real man!|Mido|OoT}}</ref>

Despite this, Mido is not a bad person and his jealousy of Link does not stop him from giving useful advice to him,<ref>{{Cite|If you're looking for Saria, she's in the Lost Woods, as usual. Don't tell me you don't know where that is! The entrance is up on the cliff overlooking the village! I know you'll get lost! Don't worry, you'll just end up back at the entrance! Harumph!|Mido|OoT}}</ref> and in the future years, he seems to be genuinely sorry for the rotten way he treated Link, despite the fact that he is unaware of Link's identity as an adult later on.<ref>{{Cite|And also... I'm sorry for being mean to him. Tell him that, too.|Mido|OoT}}</ref> He is very dependable, even in the face of danger, and at least holds the trust and loyalty of [[Fado (Ocarina of Time)|Fado]].

Mido guarded the road to the [[Great Deku Tree]], and refused to let Link pass if he didn't have a [[Kokiri Sword|Sword]] and a [[Deku Shield|Shield]].<ref>{{Cite|How do you think you're going to help the Great Deku Tree without both a sword and shield ready?|Mido|OoT}}</ref> Additionally, he was not at all thrilled the Great Deku Tree had summoned Link instead of him, something he made sure to have Link know.<ref>{{Cite|Hey you! "Mr. No Fairy!" What's your business with the Great Deku Tree? Without a fairy, you're not even a real man! What?! You've got a fairy?! Say what? The Great Deku Tree actually summoned you? Whaaaaaaat?! Why would he summon you and not the great Mido? This isn't funny... I don't believe it! You aren't even fully equipped yet! How do you think you're going to help the Great Deku Tree without both a sword and shield ready? What? You're right, I don't have my equipment ready, but... If you want to pass through here, you should at least equip a sword and shield! Sheesh!|Mido|OoT}}</ref> When Link returned with the required equipment, Mido was greatly displeased and reiterated that he would never accept Link as an equal.<ref>{{Cite|Eh, what's that?! Oh, you have a {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Deku Shield}}… And what's THAT?! Is that the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Kokiri Sword}}?! GOOD GRIEF! Well, even with all that stuff, a wimp is still a wimp, huh? I, the great Mido, will never accept you as one of us! Shoot! How did you get to be the favorite of Saria and the Great Deku Tree? Huh?! Grumble…grumble…|Mido|OoT3D}}</ref>

Unfortunately, Link's visit to the Great Deku Tree ended with the latter's death as a result of the curse laid on him by [[Ganondorf]]. When Mido found out the father of the Kokiri had passed away, he immediately laid the blame on Link, further motivating him to leave the [[Kokiri Forest]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Link! What did you do?! The Great Deku Tree...did he...die? How could you do a thing like that?! It's all your fault!!|Mido|OoT}}</ref>

In the seven years that Link was gone, life in the Kokiri Forest became worse as Ganondorf's evil spread from the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}} and caused the Kokiri's home to be infested with larger, more vicious monsters.<ref>{{Cite|I'm in the Forest Temple! The forest spirits were calling for help, so I went to check it out... But it's full of evil monsters! Help me, Link!|Saria|OoT}}</ref> Mido went with Saria to put a stop to it, and on her request, guarded the route to the [[Sacred Forest Meadow]], so that no one could follow her.<ref>{{Cite|What are you? Though you wear Kokirish clothing, you can't fool me! I promised Saria I would never let anybody go through here.|Mido|OoT}}</ref> When Link met him in the [[Lost Woods]], Mido did not recognize him, although he did notice that there was something familiar about him.
[[File:OOT-MidoKingZoraSad.jpg|thumb|right|Mido and [[King Zora]], not feeling up to party along]]
As such, he did not let him through either, that is, until the stranger played to Mido [[Saria's Song|Saria's Song,]] thereby proving that he was friends with Saria.<ref>{{Cite|That melody?! Saria plays that song all the time! You...Do you know Saria? That song... Saria taught that song only to her friends... OK... I trust you. When I see you... I don't know why, but I remember... him...|Mido|OoT}}</ref> When Link returned after helping Saria awaken as the [[Sage|Sage of Forest]], he told Mido that she would not be coming back, Mido became upset, and asked the stranger, that, if he'd ever meet Link, he would tell him that Saria had been waiting for him and that he was sorry for having been mean to him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...I see... Saria won't ever come back... But...I...I made a promise to Saria... If Link came back, I would be sure to tell him that Saria had been waiting for him... Because Saria...really... liked... Hey, you. If you see him somewhere, please let him know... And also... I'm sorry for being mean to him. Tell him that, too.|Mido|OoT}}</ref> 

At the liberation celebration at [[Lon Lon Ranch]], Mido sat at a distance from the festivities, right next to [[King Zora]] with both leaders worried about what had happened to their loved ones. Mido was pulled out of his thoughts by small sparks coming down causing him and King Zora to look up to see [[Saria|green]] and [[Princess Ruto|blue orbs of light]], followed by three others, speeding across the sky.

==Trivia==
*In {{TAoL}}, there is also a town called [[Mido Town]]. This is the only town in {{TAoL|-}} that shares the name of an {{OoT|-}} character who is not a Sage.
*Mido's name might originate from Midori (緑) meaning Green in Japanese. Another possible origination from Mido's name could come from Meadow, probably because [[Kokiri Forest]] is a very green, grassy place.
*Based on investigation into early versions of ''Ocarina of Time'', it is speculated that Mido was planned to be one of the [[Sage#Ocarina%20of%20Time|Sages]], specifically the Sage of Shadow. In these versions, the item which would become the [[Shadow Medallion]] was named "Mido's Seal". Mido is also the only non-Sage character in ''Ocarina of Time'' to be named after a town from ''The Adventure of Link''. It's likely Mido's role was replaced by [[Impa]] later in development, and his name, likeness, or both were repurposed for his final role.{{Fact|OoT}}
*In {{BotW}}, there is a swamp named [[Mido Swamp]], wherein [[Maag Halan Shrine]] is situated.
