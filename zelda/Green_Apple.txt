{{Term/Store|Green Apple|Green Apples|Series, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|NS|-}}===
In {{LA|NS}}, {{Plural|LANS|Green Apple}} are found growing on certain {{Plural|LANS|Tree|link}} scattered on the {{Term|LANS|Overworld|link}} of {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}. {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} can perform a {{Term|LANS|Dash Attack|link}} into these {{Plural|LANS|Tree}} to make them fall on the ground. He can then pick them up and eat them to restore some {{Plural|LANS|Heart|link}}.{{How Many}}

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW|-}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Green Apple}} restore three {{Plural|ALBW|Heart|link}} instead of one like regular {{Plural|ALBW|Apple|link}}. {{Plural|ALBW|Green Apple}} grow on the same {{Plural|ALBW|Tree|link}} as regular {{Plural|ALBW|Apple}} do, most notably on the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree|link}} near {{Term|ALBW|Link's House|link}}, only they're rarer. Despite having superior health recovery properties, {{Plural|ALBW|Green Apple}} are stated to have inferior taste compared to their red counterparts.<Ref>{{Cite|Restores three hearts. Not as sweet as a red apple.|N/A|ALBW}}</Ref>

[[StreetPass]] challenges consider {{Plural|ALBW|Green Apple}} an upgraded version of {{Plural|ALBW|Apple}}, thus, using a {{Term|ALBW|Green Apple}} in a {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link Battle|link}} yields a {{Term|ALBW|Medal|link}} for the {{Term|ALBW|Apple}} as well as for the {{Term|ALBW|Green Apple}}.
