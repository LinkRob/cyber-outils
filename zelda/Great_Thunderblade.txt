{{Plural|BotW|Great Thunderblade}} are {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link|display=Electric}}, two-handed swords that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can wield. They were forged by the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} using {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> As a result of their elemental properties, they belong to the {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Primarily, they are found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Great Thunderblade}} can be found sticking out of the earth between the jutted rocks of {{Term|BotW|Cuho Mountain|link}}'s summit, and another can be found being wielded by a {{Term|BotW|Black Moblin|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Coliseum Ruins|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Great Thunderblade}} also have a possibility of spawning on {{Plural|BotW|Stalnox|link}}, which have to be defeated before {{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain them.

Being an {{Term|BotW|Electricity|display=Electric}} {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Great Thunderblade}} can be used to shock {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} and force them to drop any {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that they are using, making them easier to dispatch. Each swing of a {{Term|BotW|Great Thunderblade}} will temporarily drain the {{Term|BotW|Electricity|display=Electric}} power from it. When a {{Term|BotW|Great Thunderblade}} is completely drained, the blade will have to regenerate its energy before it can cause {{Term|BotW|Electricity|display=Electric}} damage again. Using this sword while it is in this state will also not generate {{Term|BotW|Electricity}} and will pause the regeneration for a second. Dropping a {{Term|BotW|Great Thunderblade}} through the {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} screen and picking it back up will instantly recharge it.

In addition to shocking {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}}, {{Plural|BotW|Great Thunderblade}} can be used to temporarily fire {{Term|BotW|Electricity|display=Electric}} pulses into certain machines. Since they are made of metal, they will also attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity}}. They will sink if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Great Thunderblade}} will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
