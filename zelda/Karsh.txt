{{Term/Store|Karsh||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|Karsh|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Karsh}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} woman who travels between {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}} by way of the {{Term|BotW|Ternio Trail|link}}. She carries a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}}. At the beginning of conversations, she will often introduce herself to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} and tell him to remember her name.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Karsh of the Gerudo. Remember that name!|Karsh|BotW}}</ref>

Originally, {{Term|BotW|Karsh}} is from the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Karsh of the Gerudo. Remember that name! I came to Eldin from the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Desert}}, far to the southwest.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I cam from {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Desert}}, far to the southwest, on a journey to hunt for voe.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> She was a guard for {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, where she was a master archer.<ref>{{Cite|I'm just a traveler now, but there was a time when I was a master archer in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}} guard.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> Back then, she was able to stop [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]] from attacking with a single {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can't count how many times I stopped monsters from invading the town with a single, well-placed arrow.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I can't count how many times I stopped monsters from invading {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}} with a single, well-placed arrow.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Karsh}} left {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} in search of a man, but she has not had much luck in her quest.<ref name="Vai"/><ref>{{Cite|I'm travelling in search of voe, but I haven't found any good ones so far. Until just now, heh heh.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Only vai are allowed in {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}, so I'm on a journey outside the town walls to meet a strong voe.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Where are the good voe?|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Karsh}} started her journey some time ago, she has not yet given up hope.<ref>{{Cite|It's been quite a while since I left home to go on this journey, but my passion hasn't gone down one bit.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> She notes that the {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} have a similar level of endurance to her, and she wonders whether she will meet her match amongst them.<ref>{{Cite|You know who else has endurance like that? The Gorons. Maybe one of them would be a match for me...|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You know who has a lot of endurance? The Gorons. Maybe one of them could be a match for me...|Karsh|BotW}}</ref>

All {{Term|BotW|Karsh}} wants is to be with the man she was born to marry, and she does not care what race he could be.<ref>{{Cite|All I want is to find the voe I was born to be with. It doesn't matter to me which race he is. I wouldn't turn down a voe because his skin was orange or his neck had gills. This is true love we're talking about!|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Once I've found the voe I was born to be with, I can get on with the rest of my life.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> During her journey, {{Term|BotW|Karsh}} has met men of many different races and learned how people live outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|I run into voe from all sorts of races on the road. It's so thrilling to see how the rest of the world lives.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> She likes the {{Plural|BotW|Goron}} in particular and wishes her skin was as tough as theirs.<ref>{{Cite|I run into voe from all sorts of races on the road. I like the Gorons a lot. I wish my hide was as tough as theirs!|Karsh|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Karsh}} knows many things about {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}}. She has heard that the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road|link}} becomes even hotter than the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, and she regrets not bringing clothes that are appropriate for such conditions.<ref>{{Cite|The only thing I wish I'd brought on my journey are some clothes that can withstand the burning heat. I hear it's unbearable on the {{Color|BotW Blue|roads around the volcano}}. Even worse than the desert air, if that's possible!|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hear the heat on the roads around the volcano is unbearable. I wish I'd thought to bring some cooler clothes...|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> She also warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} of the {{Plural|BotW|Magma Bomb|link}} that make travelling to {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}} dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|The molten rocks raining down from the volcano make the road from this stable to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}} a dangerous trek.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> In addition, she has heard that the {{Term|BotW|Goron Hot Springs|link}} are supposed to be good for one's skin, which she remarks that visitors will need, since they will be torn apart by monsters on the way there.<ref>{{Cite|If you go north of here to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}, there's a place called Goron Hot Springs that's supposed to be good for your skin. And you'll need to take good care of your skin, too, after the monsters on the way tear it to ribbons!|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Goron Hot Springs in {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}} to the north is supposed to be good for your skin, but it's a dangerous trip to make.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Karsh}} dislikes {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, she considers it a good excuse to start conversations with people.<ref>{{Cite|Water should never fall from the sky! But I admit, it's a nice excuse to strike up a conversation with people.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hate this sky water...|Karsh|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Karsh}} encounters any enemies on her journey, she will draw her {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar}} to fight.<ref>{{Cite|Know your place!|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Have at you!|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Come on!|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves her, {{Term|BotW|Karsh}} will be grateful for his assistance.<ref>{{Cite|I really thought the sands of my life had run out... Sarqso!|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|All clear?|Karsh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sa'oten... That was... *ahem* That was nothing. I'm fine.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> She may also complain about having to deal with monsters.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so tired of dealing with these monsters every day.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref> To thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} for helping her, she gives him an {{Term|BotW|Energizing Elixir|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey... It would be rude of me not to thank you for your help. So thanks.|Karsh|BotW}}</ref>
