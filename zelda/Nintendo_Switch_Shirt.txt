The {{Term/Store|Nintendo Switch Shirt||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Nintendo Switch Shirt}}<br/>Expansion Pass Bonus<br/>A shirt with the Nintendo Switch logo on it. It's a rather rare find, so wear it with pride!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It is a bonus piece of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} exclusive to the Expansion Pass. The {{Term|BotW|Nintendo Switch Shirt|display=Shirt}} is also available in the [[Wii U]] version of the game, also featuring the [[Nintendo Switch]] logo.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Nintendo Switch Shirt}} can be found inside a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} located atop a wall in the {{Term|BotW|Eastern Abbey|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|BotW|Nintendo Switch Shirt|display=Shirt}}, {{Term|BotW|Granté|link}} will begin selling it for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.{{Note|Prior to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild#Updates|Version 1.3.3 Update]], {{Term|BotW|Granté}} would not sell any {{Term|BotW|Armor}} that is exclusive to the Expansion Pass or [[The Master Trials]] DLC Pack. This meant that the {{Term|BotW|Nintendo Switch Shirt}} could not be replaced if sold, which was noted in the {{Term|BotW|Nintendo Switch Shirt|display=Shirt}}'s description.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Nintendo Switch Shirt}}<br/>Expansion Pass Bonus<br/>A shirt with the Nintendo Switch logo on it. It's a rare article, if you sell it, you can't get it back, so wear it with pride!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The description of the {{Term|BotW|Nintendo Switch Shirt}} was changed in the Version 1.3.3 Update as the {{Term|BotW|Nintendo Switch Shirt|display=Shirt}} could now be replaced.<ref name="Inventory"/>}}
