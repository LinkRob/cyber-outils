{{Term/Store|Red Bubble|Red Bubbles|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 169 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TLoZ, OoT3D, MM3D}}

==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
As a variant of the {{Term|TLoZ|Bubble|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Bubble}} are animated {{Plural|TLoZ|Skull|link}} that appear in the {{Term|TLoZ|Second Quest|link}} of {{TLoZ}}.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= When it comes to Red Bubbles, you won't be able to swing your sword at all unless you...|magazine= Nintendo Power|publisher= |volume= |issue= 1|date= July/August 1988|page= 31}}</ref> They can be found in {{Term|TLoZ|Level 4|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 5|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 6|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 7|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 8|link}}, and {{Term|TLoZ|Level 9|link}}. Like {{Plural|TLoZ|Bubble}}, they move in erratic patterns and are invincible to all attacks. Instead of inflicting damage to {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} upon contact like other enemies, {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Bubble}} {{Term|TLoZ|Curse|link}} {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} upon contact, disabling his ability to use his {{Term|TLoZ|Sword|link}}. The only way for {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} to dispel the {{Term|TLoZ|Curse}} is by touching a {{Term|TLoZ|Blue Bubble|link}}, drinking a {{Term|TLoZ|Blue Water of Life|link}}, or visiting a {{Term|TLoZ|Great Fairy|link}} in the {{Term|TLoZ|Overworld|link}}. Unlike other {{Plural|TLoZ|Bubble}}, the {{Term|TLoZ|Curse}} will not end if {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} uses the {{Term|TLoZ|Recorder|link}}. For this reason, {{Plural|TLoZ|Blue Bubble}} are often found near {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Bubble}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Red Bubble}}<br/>Guard against its attack with your shield!
}}

In {{OoT}}, {{Plural|OoT3D|Red Bubble}} are animated {{Plural|OoT3D|Skull|link}} surrounded by red {{Term|OoT3D|Fire|link}}. They leap out of pits or from the lava of the {{Term|OoT3D|Death Mountain Crater|link}} and the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}}, bouncing around in an attempt to hit {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} and set him aflame. They often appear at ledges that {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} must jump across in an attempt to hit him as he jumps. {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can defend himself with a {{Term|OoT3D|Shield|link}} to knock the {{Term|OoT3D|Red Bubble}} back.<ref>{{Cite|Guard against its attack with your shield!|Navi|OoT}}</ref>

In {{MQ}}, {{Plural|OoT3D|Red Bubble}} also appear in [[Spirit Temple]]. They can burn Link's [[Deku Shield]], too.

{{Plural|MM3D|Red Bubble}} appear as animated {{Plural|MM3D|Skull|link}} surrounded by red {{Term|MM3D|Fire|link}} in {{MM}}. They often leap out of pits of lava inside the {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead Temple|link}} and attempt to hit {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} and set him aflame. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can knock out their flames and leave them vulnerable by defending himself with a {{Term|MM3D|Shield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What?! ==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Red Bubble & Blue Bubble}}
{{Tingle

In {{TWW}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Red Bubble}} are floating {{Plural|TWWHD|Skull}} surrounded by curling red {{Plural|TWWHD|Fire|link|display=Flames}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Red Bubble}} fly around at their own leisure, but immediately chase after {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} when they spot him, attempting to catch him on {{Term|TWWHD|Fire}}. Their {{Term|TWWHD|Fire}} serves as a protective barrier that shields them from {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}'s attacks. These flames, however, can be blown away with the {{Term|TWWHD|Deku Leaf|link}}, causing the {{Term|TWWHD|Red Bubble}} to lose all power as it falls to the ground as a regular skull, where it can be attacked normally.<ref>{{Cite|Fan the flame away with the Deku Leaf!|Tingle|TWW}}</ref> They can also be shot down with the {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Bow|link}} as well. {{Plural|TWWHD|Red Bubble}} appear in the {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Ganon's Tower|link}}. They can also be summoned by {{Plural|TWWHD|Wizzrobe|link}}.
