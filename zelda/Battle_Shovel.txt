{{Term/Store|Battle Shovel|Battle Shovels|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Battle Shovel}}<br/>Allows you to dig through walls, giving +2 damage on your next move.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Battle Shovel}} are a type of {{Term|CoH|Shovel|link}} that {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} can use to dig through dirt and walls with the added bonus of granting a +2 attack bonus to their {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}} after each dig, but only if they use said {{Term|CoH|Weapon}} on the next beat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

They can be found either from a red {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}}.
