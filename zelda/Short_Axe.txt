The '''Short Axes''' are [[item]]s in {{ZA}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= ZA|page= 4}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
Short Axes are thrown like spinning axe. They each cost two [[Rupee]]s to use. They can be obtained by purchasing a [[Bone]] from the general shop in upper street of [[Great Wimbich]], and giving it to a [[Dog]] outside.
