The {{Term/Store|Water Reservoir||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Water Reservoir'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Water Reservoir is located on the western edge of [[West Castle Town]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. It was used to provide water to the townspeople of [[Hyrule Castle Town]] prior to the second [[Great Calamity]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 394}}</ref>

A large slate of [[Rock]] can be found in the northwestern part of the lake and covers a submerged metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing five [[Bomb Arrow]]s. A second metallic Treasure Chest containing a [[Silver Rupee]] sits in the south end of the Reservoir.
