'''''The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD Sound Selection''''' is the original soundtrack for {{TWWHD}}. The soundtrack was made exclusively available to Club Nintendo Japan members who registered their copy of {{TWWHD|-}} between September 26, 2013 and November 4, 2013. While the tunes included are the same as those of the [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker Original Sound Tracks|original soundtrack]], the instruments used are of higher quality and provide a richer sound.
