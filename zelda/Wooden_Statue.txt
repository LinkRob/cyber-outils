==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, {{Term|ALttP|Link}} can obtain three different {{Plural|ALttP|Carver's Statue}} as rewards during the {{Term|ALttP|Lumberjack's Riddles|link}}. The first statue resembles a {{Term|ALttP|Cucco|link}}, obtained after solving the first riddle, where the answer is a {{Term|ALttP|Cucco}}. The second statue resembles {{Term|ALttP|Link}} himself, obtained after solving the fourth riddle. The third and last statue resembles {{Term|ALttP|Princess Zelda|link}}, obtained after completing the seventh riddle. The statues are placed inside {{Term|ALttP|Link's House|link}} once obtained.<ref>{{Cite|Link! Did you figure out the answer? Well? Where is it? Show me what's in your basket! What?! That's right! This time I'll give you the carver's statue I made. I'll send it to your house. Now, on to the next riddle!|Q. Bumpkin|ALttP}}</ref>

The {{Plural|ALttP|Carver's Statue}} can be carried and thrown around the house. If Link sprinkles {{Term|ALttP|Magic Powder|link}} on them, they will give different reactions. If Link sprinkles more powder on the {{Term|ALttP|Cucco}} statue, it will get the same colors as an ordinary {{Term|ALttP|Cucco}} or its {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} counterpart. If Link does the same on his statue, it will get the same colors as Link or {{Term|ALttP|Bunny Link|link}}. The {{Term|ALttP|Princess Zelda}} statue will receive the same colors as {{Term|ALttP|Princess Zelda|display= Zelda}} or turn into an older version of herself if sprinkled again. These changes reset once {{Term|ALttP|Link}} leaves his house.

==={{TP|-}}===
When [[Link]] speaks to [[Borville]] about his debt in [[Telma's Bar]], he mentions he was going to sell the Wooden Statue, but it was stolen after he placed it outside to dry after it got covered in medicine. Following the [[Medicine Scent]], Link is eventually led to [[Telma]]'s cat, [[Louise]], who confesses that although she did steal the statue, it was stolen from her by [[Stalhound]]s.<ref>{{Cite|You're looking for the wooden statue that Ilia had, aren't you, dear? Think it's a coincidence that I happen to know it got stolen from the doctor's place? No... Yes, I admit it...I took it. But then I was attacked by some sort of skeletal dog beasts who took it from me.|Louise|TP}}</ref> Link must then defeat the Stalhounds situated south of the [[Castle Town]] to retrieve the statue.

Once Link shows the statue to [[Ilia]], she recovers part of her memory and urges Link to save the person who gave her the statue.<ref>{{Cite|I... I remember something... I was confined somewhere... Yes, and I was saved by whoever was confined with me... And when that person set me free, they gave me this statue...|Ilia|TP}}</ref> Just then, [[Gor Coron]] remembers that the statue used to belong to the [[Sheikah]] tribe, which leads Link to the Hidden Village.<ref>{{Cite|I REMEMBER! I thought I had seen that before somewhere... That belonged to the tribe that protected the Hylian royal family long ago. They worked in secret, so they lived in a lonely, forgotten place.|Gor Coron|TP}}</ref>

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{Term|SSHD|Link}}'s room at the {{Term|SSHD|Knight Academy|link}}, {{Plural|SSHD|Wooden Statue}} can be found on his desk and bookshelf. They depict his {{Term|SSHD|Crimson Loftwing|link}} and a {{Term|SSHD|Remlit|link}}, suggesting he practices wood carving as a hobby.
