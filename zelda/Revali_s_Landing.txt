{{Term/Store|Revali's Landing||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Revali's Landing {{Icon|BotW Arrow Right}}|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Revali's Landing is located in [[Rito Village]]. It is the largest platform in the village used by the {{Term|BotW|Rito}} for taking off and landing from. It was named for the Rito Champion [[Revali]] after his death.<ref>{{Cite|Revali's Landing is a memorial for the Rito Champion, Revali... So named in the hope that none might forget the events of that horrible day...|Saki|BotW}}</ref>
