The {{Term/Store|Blue Joy Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Blue Joy Ring<br/>Beasts drop double Hearts|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Blue Joy Ring is received from [[Princess Zelda]] after saving her in a [[Linked Game]]. In {{OoS}}, she is found at [[North Horon]] after completing [[Snake's Remains]]. In {{OoA}}, the Princess is found in the [[Black Tower]] after completing [[Moonlit Grotto]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it doubles the value of picked up [[Heart]]s.<ref name="Description"/> The Blue Joy Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
