{{Term/Store|Moosh||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}} & 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Moosh is a flying, blue bear and one of three [[Link's Partners|animal companions]] that [[Link]] can find in {{OoA}} and {{OoS}} (the other two being [[Ricky]] and [[Dimitri]]). Unlike Ricky and Dimitri, Moosh is capable of hovering in the air to get past long strings of holes. Moosh can attack by using the Ground Pound. Moosh also warns Link that water is his weakness, and that he can not go into deep water.<ref>{{Cite| I'll give you a ride anywhere. But water is my weakness. The shallow stuff is one thing, but I can't take deep water, so be careful.|Moosh|OoS}}</ref> He will even leave Link's side when they arrive at the gate, located in the [[Sunken City]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Water, water everywhere! I must leave such a frightful place! Until next time...|Moosh|OoS}}</ref> Link can use [[Strange Flute|Moosh's Flute]] to call him, though this will not work everywhere.<ref>{{Cite|Your flute's song fell flat.|N/A|OoS}}</ref>

Depending on which animal Link befriends, the landscape of certain areas in [[Holodrum]] and [[Labrynna]] will change. If Link has Moosh join him, some areas within these regions will resemble a wasteland-like area filled with many pits that Moosh can fly over.

==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:Moosh Flying.png|left|50px]]
In {{OoS|-}}, Link will eventually have to venture into [[Spool Swamp]] for the next [[Essences of Nature|Essence of Nature]] after completing the [[Snake's Remains]] dungeon. During this time, Link can find and acquire a [[Strange Flute]]. One of the Strange Flutes will be for sale in the [[Horon Village Shop]] for 150 [[Rupee]]s, yet only before Link helps Ricky the kangaroo. While heading into Spool Swamp, Link will encounter one of the potential animal helpers, Ricky. [[Blaino]], who lives nearby, has taken away Ricky's [[Ricky's Gloves|boxing gloves]], and Link must fight him in a boxing round and defeat him to win back the gloves. When the gloves are returned, Ricky will offer to help Link by transporting him to Spool Swamp.

If Link has bought the Strange Flute from the Horon Village Shop earlier, he will eventually encounter Moosh being attacked by some [[Moblin]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Bwee! He ate it! Get him!|Moblin|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Before we forget, we want our revenge! Get ready-- both of you!|Moblin|OoS}}</ref> After defeating the Moblins several times, Moosh will thank Link and join him, turning the Strange Flute into Moosh's Flute.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for helping me out. Tell me your name. Link... Umm! I remembered it! I can help you if you like. If I hear your Strange Flute, I'll come find you.|Moosh|OoS}}</ref>

Eventually, whether Link has Moosh's Flute or not, Moosh will later be encountered on [[Mt. Cucco]], where he has found a bunch of [[Bananas#Oracle of Seasons|Spring Bananas]] growing on a tree on a ledge, but is unable to reach them. When Link acquires the bananas and feeds Moosh, he will then help Link obtain the [[Dragon Key]], required for the [[Dancing Dragon Dungeon]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
[[File:OoX Moosh Sitting Up.png|left|50px|Moosh sitting on the ground]]
In {{OoA|-}}, Moosh first makes his appearance when Link is supposed to find and obtain the [[Flippers|Zora's Flippers]] and [[Cheval Rope]]. Link will find Moosh near the [[Yoll Graveyard]]'s entrance being attacked by monsters. Once the enemies have been defeated, Moosh will thank Link and offer to help him by flying him to [[Cheval]]'s tombstone, where Link obtains the Flippers and Cheval Rope inside [[Cheval's Tomb]].

When Link tries to later venture into [[Symmetry Village|Symmetry City]], he will encounter the [[Stray Fairy|Fairies]] from the [[Fairies' Woods]], who ask Link to help an animal who got lost in their woods. The animal will either be Moosh, Ricky or Dimitri depending on whether Link has any of the Strange Flutes or not. Moosh would be one of the animals lost if Link does not have any of the other Strange Flutes prior to the beginning of this event. If Moosh is found, he will decide to join Link and gives him his flute. Moosh's flying ability is used in the [[Nuun Highlands]], which helps Link locate the three lazy [[Carpenters]] needed to extend the bridge to the ruined city.<ref>{{Cite|Those lazy, worthless fools! Just as we're buildin' this bridge to the Symmetry City Ruins, they go off on break!|Mutoh|OoA}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{OoS|-}} (Himekawa)===
Moosh makes a brief appearance in the {{OoS|Himekawa}}, appearing to be a slow-witted creature, taking him several moments to realize he is in an area full of monsters. He helps Link and Ricky escape from these monsters by flying them away. He also references the Spring Bananas he wanted in the game.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*Even if Link has Moosh's Flute in {{OoS|-}}, he will not be able to call Moosh when he finds him on Mt. Cucco until after he has obtained the Spring Bananas.
