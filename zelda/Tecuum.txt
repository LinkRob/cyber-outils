'''Tecuum''' is an enemy from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==

===Physical Appearance===
Tecuums resemble fireballs or comets, albeit dark purple with a lighter purple outline. They can transform into a shadowy copy of another person/creature, sometimes imitating the impersonated being's colour.

==Overview==
Tecuum are floating purple blobs found in the [[Still World]] and near [[Rift|Rifts]] in [[Hyrule]]. When they spot Zelda, they charge at her. If they make contact, they summon dark [[Echo|echoes]] of enemies Zelda has encountered. Tecuums can bypass solid objects en route to their target, but Zelda can destroy them prior to contact if she is quick enough, although a new Tecuum will replace the dead Tecuum.

A Tecuum can transform into a [[Dark Echo|dark echo]] of a person in addition to enemies. A particularly important dark echo known as [[Tekom]] plays a significant role in the games story by attempting to steal the [[Triforce|Prime Energy]] on its master [[Null]]'s behalf, but is ultimately stopped by [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]. Despite this, it manages to give its master the piece of power, although this is undone when the real Zelda takes the power piece back.

==Nomenclature==
Tecuums are named after the Hebrew word ''t'hom'' ("void"; written תְּהוֹם) and "vacuum".
