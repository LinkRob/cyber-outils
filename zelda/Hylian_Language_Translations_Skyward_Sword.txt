TITLE : Hylian Language Translations/Skyward Sword

<noinclude>{{TLoZ|colon}} {{SS}} introduces a new form of [[Hylian (Language)|Hylian text]] that is a cipher based on the Latin alphabet. It translates directly to English. The previous form of Hylian text introduced in {{TP}} also makes an appearance, notably on the North American box art.</noinclude>
