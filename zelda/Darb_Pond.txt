{{Term/Store|Darb Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Darb Pond|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Darb Pond is a small body of lava south of [[Death Mountain]] in the [[Eldin Canyon]]. It is connected by a strait that flows from the Mountain's lava moat. Darb Pond itself flows into [[Medingo Pool]] further south through another, narrower strait.

Several [[Ore Deposit]]s line the shores of Darb Pond.

In the center of the pond is a pinnacle where a [[rock]] can be found, concealing a [[Korok]] beneath it.
An [[Igneo Talus]] is buried in the Pond's eastern shore.
