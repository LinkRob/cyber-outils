The {{Term/Store|Footrace||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 337}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The {{Term|BotW|Footrace}} begins at the {{Term|BotW|Footrace Check-In|link}}, a location in {{Term|BotW|Upland Lindor|link}} where {{Term|BotW|Konba|link}} has set up his camp. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Konba}}, he notices that he looks tough and asks if he is willing to participate in a race.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey, friend!}} You look like a tough boy. But how tough are you? Tough enough to challenge me to a {{Color|BotW Red|race}}?|Konba|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will be disappointed, as he thought he finally found a tough person who had potential.<ref>{{Cite|[I don't feel like it.] {{Big|Noooo!}} What a letdown... I thought I'd finally met a tough boy with some real potential. {{Small|Guess I thought wrong.}}|Konba|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, he will be excited and share that this challenge has stakes.<ref>{{Cite|[You're on.] {{Big|Yahoo!}} Now we're talking! This is fantastic! But every challenge has to have some stakes.|Konba|BotW}}</ref>

First, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} demands that {{Term|BotW|Link}} bet 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} that he can win a race against him.<ref name="Konba"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to pay, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} tells him that he will get nowhere in life with such an attitude.<ref>{{Cite|[You lost me.] {{Big|What?!}} You're giving up before we even start? {{Small|Yikes...}} You'll never get anywhere in life with that attitude!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} accept his challenge, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will accept his {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} and leave to set up the race.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's get started.] {{Big|Yesss!}} Now you're getting into the spirit of things! I'll go set things up. Don't go anywhere!|Konba|BotW}}</ref>

Once he has set up the race, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will eagerly inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he is ready to start.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|OK!}} Thanks for waiting! I'm so ready I could burst, and I bet you are too! So let's begin...the {{Color|BotW Red|race}}!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Their goal is the {{Plural|BotW|Big Flag|link}} that are located on top of {{Term|BotW|Mount Rhoam|link}}, and when one of the reaches it, the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}} will be over.<ref>{{Cite|The goal is the {{Color|BotW Red|flags on top of the mountain}}! There's only two of us, so the race ends once someone reaches them!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Before they start, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that they are not to use {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} in the race,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|One more thing!}} You could probably guess this already, but this is a {{Color|BotW Red|foot race}}, not a {{Color|BotW Red|hoof race}}.|Konba|BotW}}</ref> and riding one will result in disqualification.<ref>{{Cite|If you ride your horse or run away from the goal instead of toward it, you'll be {{Color|BotW Red|disqualified}}!|Konba|BotW}}</ref>

After explaining the rules, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} counts down to the start of the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|And now...it's time! Get ready... Go!}}|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Along the trail, {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Chuchu|link}} will emerge from the ground in front of {{Term|BotW|Link}}. In addition, {{Plural|BotW|Boulder|link}} will fall from higher cliffs and roll down the mountain. At once point, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will pass a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel|link}} next to it. A {{Term|BotW|Chuchu}} will emerge from the ground near the {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel}}, causing it to explode and knocking a nearby {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} onto the path.{{Note|The {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} will not explode if the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}} occurs while it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}}.}}

{{Term|BotW|Konba}} is fast, meaning {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs to boost his speed in order to keep up with him. This can be done by drinking a {{Term|BotW|Hasty Elixir|link}} or eating hasty {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also wear the {{Term|BotW|Stealth Set|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Dark Series|link}} if he runs the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}} at night, since both {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set|link}} provide the {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link|display=Night Speed Up|section=Night Speed Up}} bonus. While {{Term|BotW|Konba}} follows a set path up {{Term|BotW|Mount Rhoam}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is under no obligation to follow it. He can climb cliffs, use {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|link}}, or swim up a {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Zora Armor|link}} in order to get ahead of {{Term|BotW|Konba}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} runs in any direction other than towards {{Term|BotW|Mount Rhoam}}, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will say he is not racing, just running.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|What the heack?!}} Where do you think you're going? If you're not racing toward the goal, you're just running!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not participating, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} calls off the race and tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back and hear the rules again.<ref>{{Cite|And that doesn't do anyone any good... The {{Color|BotW Red|race is off}}! Why don't you come back so I can explain the rules again?|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Similarly, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} mounts a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} or the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} is annoyed that he has broken the rules, but he is happier when he reaizes that he has won by default.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Whoa, whoa, whoa!}} This is a {{Color|BotW Red|foot race}}, not a hoof race! {{Color|BotW Red|No horses allowed}}! {{Small|Hey... That means I win by default!}}|Konba|BotW}}</ref> After both disruptions, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will reiterate that the point of the race is to reach the goal all on one's own.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|One more time!}} Maybe I wasn't clear enough before, but a race is all about {{Color|BotW Red|reaching the goal}} on {{Color|BotW Red|your own two feet}}!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be disqualified if he runs the wrong way or uses a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|In other words, running the wrong way or getting on your horse means you're {{Color|BotW Red|disqualified}}!|Konba|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Footrace}} ends once {{Term|BotW|Link}} or {{Term|BotW|Konba}} reaches the {{Plural|BotW|Big Flag}}. If {{Term|BotW|Konba}} reaches the goal first, he gloats that {{Term|BotW|Link}} he needs to practice if he wants to beat him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Wheeee! I did it!}} I won! You gotta give those flat feet some exercise if you ever wanna beat me, friend!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Back at the {{Term|BotW|Footrace Check-In}}, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he did fine, but he will have to try harder if he is going to win.<ref>{{Cite|You did OK, but if you're ever gonna beat me, you've gotta try harder than that!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will be shocked and disappointed when he realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}} before him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Done!}} Wait... You're done too? You were done...first? Hrm... {{Small|That means I lose...}}|Konba|BotW}}</ref> At the bottom of the mountain, he insists that he was not trying his bet, but he honors his word and gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|I wasn't REALLY trying my best...but a bet is a bet! Here's your {{Color|BotW Red|reward}} for a race well run!|Konba|BotW}}</ref>

After the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to run again.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Whew!}} So...wanna race again?|Konba|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will express his admiration of him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Woooo-hooo! I love this guy! All right!}}|Konba|BotW}}</ref> He reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the rules are the same as before,<ref>{{Cite|Same rules as before. Whoever reaches the goal first wins!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} is saddened.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Aww.}} That's...{{Small|not the answer I hoped for...}}|Konba|BotW}}</ref> However, he encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use his traveling as an opportunity to build up his leg muscles.<ref>{{Cite|Use all that traveling you do as a chance to build up your leg muscles!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he is willing to run the race again if {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever feels up to it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Whew!}} If you ever want to go again, I'd jump at the chance! See you around!|Konba|BotW}}</ref>
