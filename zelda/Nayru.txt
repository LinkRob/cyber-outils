'''Nayru''' is the name of two different people in the ''Zelda'' universe:

*'''[[Nayru (Goddess)]]''' is the goddess of wisdom who helped create the land of Hyrule, as revealed in {{OoT}}.
*'''[[Nayru (Oracle)]]''', the Oracle of Ages from the land of [[Labrynna]], who can control and alter time with her [[Harp of Ages]]. She appears in {{OoA}} on the [[Game Boy Color]]. She also makes a cameo appearance in {{TMC}}.
*'''[[Possessed Nayru]]''', the ''out-of-context'' name given to the Oracle Nayru when she is possessed by [[Veran]] in {{OoA}}.
