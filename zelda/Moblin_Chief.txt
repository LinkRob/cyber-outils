The {{Term/Store|Moblin Chief||Series, LANS}} is a {{Term|LANS|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|LANS Pink|Moblin Chief's Chamber}}!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Moblin Chief}} was referred to as {{Term/Store|King Moblin||LA, LADX}} in all versions of {{LA}} prior to {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 187}}</ref> However, as the name was changed in {{LA|NS|-}}, it is no longer considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} He is a large red [[Moblin]] that appears as the chief of the Moblins.

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
The {{Term|LANS|Moblin Chief}} is the leader of a gang of {{Plural|LA|Moblin}} that kidnapped {{Term|LANS|Madam MeowMeow|link}}'s pet "dog"<ref>{{Cite|AIEEEEEEEE! It's terrrrible! My BowWow was dognapped by... {{Color|LANS Pink|M-M-MOBLINS}}! OHHH! AHHHH! Please! Somebody help my poor {{Color|LANS Pink|BowWow}}!|Madam MeowMeow|LANS}}</ref> while {{Term|LANS|Link}} was exploring the {{Term|LANS|Tail Cave|link}}. {{Term|LANS|Link}} sets off to rescue {{Term|LANS|Madame MeowMeow|display=MeowMeow}}'s pet, and locates the {{Term|LANS|Moblin Cave|link}} found at {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Heights|link}}. After fighting his {{Term|LANS|Moblin}} and {{Term|LANS|Sword Moblin|link}} minions, {{Term|LANS|Link}} runs into the {{Term|LANS|Moblin Chief}} in the penultimate room, where he is holding {{Term|LANS|BowWow}} hostage.<ref>{{Cite|You must be an assassin sent by Madam MeowMeow to rescue the {{Color|LANS Pink|mutt}}! You came here to get me, but it is I who will get you!!|Moblin Chief|LANS}}</ref> He will fire projectiles while jumping around, before beating his chest and charging forward with his head lowered trying to impale {{Term|LANS|Link}} on his horns. This attack is fast and can cause a lot of damage, but if {{Term|LANS|Link}} moves out of the way, the {{Term|LANS|Moblin Chief}} will instead slam into the back wall and stun himself, leaving him vulnerable to {{Term|LANS|Link}}'s blade. He has a surprising amount of stamina, but enough strikes will eventually defeat him.

==Other Appearances==
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{LA|Cagiva}}, King Moblin appears in chapter 2, where he captures BowWow much like in the game. King Moblin attempts to call for his guards when Link enters, only to find out that they have already been defeated. In response, King Moblin takes out his Bow and fires arrows at Link. When Link dodges this attack, King Moblin angrily charges at him only for the hero to jump over his attack, resulting in the King Moblin crashing into a wall. While King Moblin is in a daze from the collision, he is struck down by Link.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Nomenclature==
The {{Term|LANS|Moblin Chief}}'s Japanese name, Oyaburin, is a portmanteau of the Japanese word for "boss", {{Romanize|親分|Oyabun}} and the suffix "-blin". {{Exp|親|Oya}} also means "parent", so the {{Term|LANS|Moblin Chief}} could be seen as both the leader and parent of the other {{Plural|LA|Moblin}}.

{{Term|Series|Moblin Chief}} shares its Japanese name with {{Term|Series|Great Moblin|link}}, {{Term|OoT|Club Moblin|link}} and {{Term|ST|Big Blin|link}}.
