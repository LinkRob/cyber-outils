==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Earn Your Stripes}}" is an event wherein a greater number of striped {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Stripey spirits are zigzagging their way onto the Spirit Board in this event!|author= Nintendo |published= May 18, 2021 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/cff4cd55/index.html |title= Event: Earn Your Stripes |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
