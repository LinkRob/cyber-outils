The {{Term/Store|Gasha Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Gasha Ring<br/>Grow great Gasha Trees|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoA}}

==Location and Uses==
The Gasha Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it halves the number of defeated enemies required for [[Gasha Seed]]s to grow.<ref name="Description"/> Removing the ring will not revert the growth of Gasha Trees. The Gasha Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
