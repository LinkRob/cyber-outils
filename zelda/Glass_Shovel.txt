{{Term/Store|Glass Shovel|Glass Shovels|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Glass Shovel}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Glass Shovel}} are items that allow {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} to dig through mounds of dirt and walls, including the tough, {{Term|CoH|Rupee|link}}-filled golden walls found in a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}}'s shop.<ref>{{Cite|Very fragile, you lose it when you take damage. Digs through surprisingly tough walls.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> Like all glass items, it can be used indefinitely but will break as soon as its user takes damage.

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}}, though a free {{Term|CoH|Glass Shovel}} can always be found in the eastern side of {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Desert|link}} inside a red {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest}} being guarded by enemies.
