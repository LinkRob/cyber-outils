==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|East Wind}} is a {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}} located in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Shop}} is run by {{Term|BotW|Pruce|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ivee|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Pruce}} sells goods inside the shop, while {{Term|BotW|Ivee}} stands outside sweeping and calling people inside.
