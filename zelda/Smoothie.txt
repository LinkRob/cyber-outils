'''Smoothies''' are [[Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Items]] in {{EoW}}.

==Locations and Uses==
There are 70 unique smoothie types (69 if you don't include Unfortunate Smoothie). They are made with various sets of [[Ingredient|ingredients]] and can be made at [[Smoothie Shop|Smoothie Shops]]. Recipes are made up of two ingredients.

When [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] makes every recipe, the [[Business Scrub]] congratulates her and gives her 10 golden eggs.
==List of Effects==
:''Main article: [[Effect]]''
* Full Energy Recovery
* Damage Reduction
* Fire-Proof
* Chill-Proof
* Lightning-Proof
* Dive Time Up
* Swim Speed Up
* Wall-Climb Speed Up
* Glow
* Winding Speed Up
* Ice-Proof
