{{Term/Store|Mrs. Pompadour||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 305}}</ref>

==Biography==
Mrs. Pompadour is friends with [[Ms. Drawers]] and they can often be seen gossiping with each other. She will talk about how a [[Princess Styla|black ghost]] has been seen in town lately,<ref>{{Cite|Goodness, have you heard? Apparently, a black ghost has been appearing in town lately.|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> as well as the [[Drablands Diary]] that is becoming popular in town,<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard about the book? You know the one! The book everybody's talking about!|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> among other things.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard the rumors about the curse?|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> She also mentions she feels regret not telling her friend that she has spinach stuck in her teeth,<ref>{{Cite|I...wasn't able to tell her. About the spinach in her teeth. Did I fail her as a friend...?|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> and how she finds the [[Faux Hero]]'s round ears to be very odd.<ref>{{Cite|That noisy child over there... He seems to have a lot of friends, even though his ears are round. Hasn't anybody told him? Seeing him reminded me that I think it's rather odd...|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> She is also married, and her husband apparently keeps Volume 6 of the Drablands Diary under his pillow.<ref name= "Husband"/>

After [[Link]] lifts [[Princess Styla]]'s curse, both women come to realize that true fashion and beauty is what is on the inside.<ref>{{Cite|The secret of fashion is making sure your whole self is beautiful... starting with the person inside!|Mrs. Pompadour|TFH}}</ref> If Link wears the [[Kinda Cursed Tights]] and speaks with them, they will believe him to be the black ghost haunting the town.<ref>{{Cite|Goodness gracious me!
