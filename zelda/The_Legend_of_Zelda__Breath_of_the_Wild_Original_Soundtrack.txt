{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Original Soundtrack''||Series, BotW}} is the original soundtrack for {{BotW}}. It includes five discs and a booklet in a digipak. The limited edition also features a PLAYBUTTON, a clear file, and a slipcase. A notepad was also included when pre-ordering the soundtrack. It is exclusive to Japan.
