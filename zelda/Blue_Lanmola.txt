{{Term/Store|Blue Lanmola|Blue Lanmolas|Series, TLoZ|plural}} are enemies in {{TLoZ}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 189}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
'''Blue Lanmolas''' are faster, stronger variants of '''[[Lanmola|Red Lanmolas]]''', and appear exclusively in [[Level 9|level-9]] in both the [[The Legend of Zelda|first quest]] and [[Second Quest|second quest]]. They are similar in every aspect to their red counterparts, except that they move faster.
