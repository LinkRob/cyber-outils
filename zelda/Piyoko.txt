'''Piyoko''' is a small chick that hatched on {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link|link}}'s grandparents farm. She accompanies {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} during his quest through the land of {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Holodrum|link}}. After the {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Temple of Seasons|link}} sinks, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} has the sudden ability to understand when creatures and plants are speaking. As it turns out, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Piyoko}} is rather bold and outspoken. She helps {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} through several trials, and gives him frequent advice, similar to {{Term|OoT3D|Navi|link}} in {{OoT}}.

In an effort to save {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} during his battle with {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Piyoko}} attacks the Dark General, and is quickly struck down. It appears as though she dies, but in the end it is revealed that she turned into a full-grown chicken, although {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} has lost his ability to speak with her. 

==Nomenclature==
The first part of {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Piyoko}}'s name, Piy-, is a variation of the Latin name Pius, <ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.behindthename.com/name/piy/submitted |site= behindthename.com |title= Given name PIY}}</ref> which means "pious" and "dutiful". <ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.behindthename.com/name/pius |site= behindthename.com |title= Given Name PIUS}}</ref>
