"{{Term/Store|From Princess Zelda||Series, ST}}" is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|From Princess Zelda|Letters|ST}}</ref> As its name suggests, it is a {{Term|ST|Letter|link}} from {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}} secretly hands {{Term|ST|Link|link}} the {{Term|ST|Letter}} during the {{Term|ST|Royal Engineer|link}} ceremony.<ref>{{Cite|Shh--take this. No! Don't say a word right now.|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref> It contains a {{Term|ST|Map|link}} with directions for reaching her quarters via a secret path at the top of {{Term|ST|Hyrule Castle|link}}. The {{Term|ST|Letter}} reads,

<blockquote>
''I have a great favor that I must ask of you in person. Follow this secret path at the top of the castle. {{Color|ST Green|Tap}} the {{Color|ST Light Red|map icon}} to copy the location to your map.''

''Please come to my quarters as soon as you can, and don't let anyone see you.

''Zelda''
</blockquote>
