{{Term/Store|Monster Rice Balls|Monster Rice Balls|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Monster Rice Balls are a meal that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Hylian Rice]], [[Rock Salt]], and [[Monster Extract]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Due to Monster Extract being used in their making, the amount of {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} healed to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, as well as the strength and duration of any status effects, will be random. The unique aroma of Monster Rice Balls is disliked by many people.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By incorporating additional ingredients into the recipe, several different variations of Monster Rice Balls can be made:

* '''Hearty Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. These rice balls will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. They will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} when eaten.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. When eaten, they'll completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. [[Link]] can eat them to gain boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's [[Cold Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. If eaten, Link will be granted one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. When these rice balls are eaten, Link will receive a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. Link will gain a boost to his defense when he eats these.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Eating one will increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Monster Rice Balls''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Monster Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Rice balls flavored with monster extract. Their unique aroma is not for everyone.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
