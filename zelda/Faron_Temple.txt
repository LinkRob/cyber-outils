The '''Faron Temple''' is a [[Dungeon]] in {{EoW}}.

==Entrance to the Temple==
The Faron Temple is unlocked at the end of the "A Rift in the Faron Wetlands" quest line, which itself can only be unlocked after completing [[Hyrule Castle]]. After getting a membership card from [[Blossu]], Zelda can enter the Deku Scrubs' "Sweet Spot", at which point a rift will expand within the building. Jumping into the rift, the player must traverse the [[Stilled Faron Wetlands]]; at the end of the area, they will find the entrance to the dungeon.

==Themes and Navigation==
The Faron Temple is a sprawling, jungle-like structure with numerous entrances to choose from. Players must weave in and out of the dungeon to find new areas, while also battling enemies in the Still World surrounding it.

Additionally, the dungeon makes heavy use of electric enemies and puzzles, most of which can be solved or defeated by electric echoes. There are also several rooms enveloped in darkness, which Zelda must light up by either setting fire to torches, spawning in light-emitting echoes, or drinking radiant smoothies. Finally, the dungeon utilizes [[Armos]] and [[Armos Knights]] for switch puzzles, during which players must figure out how to use them to press two switches simultaneously.
