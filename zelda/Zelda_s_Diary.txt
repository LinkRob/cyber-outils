{{Term/Store|''Zelda's Diary''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda's Diary|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Zelda's Diary}} is a book that is located inside {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room|link}}. It details {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}'s thoughts following "{{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad (Memory)|link|display=The Champions' Ballad}}" {{Term|BotW|Memory|link}}.

The first page mentions that the {{Term|BotW|Fortune-Teller|link}}'s prophecy grows closer despite {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}'s best efforts to research the ancient {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} technology.<ref name="Page 1">{{Cite|After meeting with the Champions, I left to research the ancient technology, but nothing of note came of my research.<br/><br/>The return of Ganon looms—a dark force taunting us from afar. I must learn all I can about the relics so we can stop him.<br/><br/>If the fortune-teller's prophecy is to be believed, there isn't much time left...<br/><br/>Ah, but turning over these thoughts in my head puts me ill at ease. I suppose I should turn in for the night.<br/><br/>P.S. Tomorrow my father is assigning HIM as my appointed knight...|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} also laments that her father, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}}, chose {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} as her appointed knight.<ref name="Page 1"/>

The second page follows the events of "{{Term|BotW|Resolve and Grief|link}}", as {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} made her way to {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}.<ref name="Page 2">{{Cite|I set out for Goron City today to make some adjustments to Divine Beast Vah Rudania.<br/><br/>I still recall feeling his eyes on me as I walked ahead. The feeling stayed with me so long, I grew anxious and weary.<br/><br/>It is the same feeling I've felt before in his company... And still, not a word passes his lips.<br/><br/>I never know what he's thinking! It makes my imagination run wild, guessing at what he is thinking but will not say.<br/><br/>What does the boy chosen by the sword that seals the darkness think of me? Will I ever truly know?<br/><br/>Then, I suppose it's simple. A daughter of Hyrule's royal family yet unable to use sealing magic... He must despise me.|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} explains her discomfort with {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s stoic demeanor and rationalizes that he must hate her for her inability to awaken her powers.<ref name="Page 2"/>

The third page details the events of "{{Term|BotW|Zelda's Resentment|link}}", wherein {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} lost her temper and scolded {{Term|BotW|Link}} for following her around and making her uncomfortable with his silence.<ref>{{Cite|I said something awful to him today...<br/><br/>My research was going nowhere. I was feeling depressed, and I had told him repeatedly not to accompany me.<br/><br/>But he did anyway, as he always does, and so I yelled at him without restraint.<br/><br/>He seemed confused by my anger. I feel terribly guilty...and that guilt only makes me more agitated than I was before.|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

The fourth page recalls how {{Term|BotW|Link}} saved {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} from {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}, reflecting upon the events of "{{Term|BotW|Blades of the Yiga|link}}".<ref name="Page 4">{{Cite|I am unsure how to put today's events into words. Words so often evade me lately, and now more than ever.<br/><br/>He saved me. Without a thought for his own life, he protected me from the ruthless blades of the Yiga Clan.<br/><br/>Though I've been cold to him all this time...taking my selfish and childish anger out on him at every turn...<br/><br/>Still, he was there for me. I won't ever forget that. Tomorrow, I shall apologize for all that has transpired between us.<br/><br/>And then...I will try talking to him. To Link. It's worth a shot.|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} resolves to treat {{Term|BotW|Link}} more fairly and to initiate dialogue with him.<ref name="Page 4"/>

In the fifth page, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} details her discoveries about {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s personality, such as his inability to resist delicious {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}.<ref name="Page 5">{{Cite|Bit by bit, I've gotten Link to open up to me. It turns out he's quite a glutton. He can't resist a delicious meal!<br/><br/>When I finally got around to asking why he's so quiet all the time, I could tell it was difficult for him to say. But he did.<br/><br/>With so much at stake, and so many eyes upon him, he feels it necessary to stay strong and to silently bear any burden.<br/><br/>A feeling I know all too well... For him, it has caused him to stop outwardly expressing his thoughts and feelings.<br/><br/>I always believed him to be simply a gifted person who had never faced a day of hardship. How wrong I was...<br/><br/>Everyone has struggles that go unseen by the world... I was so absorbed with my own problems, I failed to see his.<br/><br/>I wish to talk with him more and to see what lies beneath those calm waters, to hear him speak freely and openly...<br/><br/>And perhaps I, too, will be able to bare my soul to him and share the demons that have plagued me all these years.|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} explains that, much like with her overbearing father, {{Term|BotW|Link}} feels overwhelmed by the stress of being a hero and feels he must maintain a calm and collected appearance in spite of his challenges.<ref name="Page 5"/> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} also muses that she might be able to reveal her own troubles to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref name="Page 5"/>

The events of "{{Term|BotW|Father and Daughter|link}}" are explored in the sixth page of {{Term|BotW|Zelda's Diary}}. After being reprimanded by her father for not devoting enough time to awakening her powers, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} explained that she has been training since she was a child and despite her efforts, she is unable to tap into her hidden powers.<ref name="Page 6">{{Cite|Father scolded me again today. He told me I am to have nothing more to do with researching ancient technology.<br/><br/>He insisted that I focus instead on training that will help me awaken my sealing magic.<br/><br/>I was so frustrated and ashamed I could not even speak. I've been training since I was a child, and yet...<br/><br/>Mother passed the year before my training was to begin. In losing her, I lost not just a mother, but a teacher.<br/><br/>Mother used to smile and tell me, "Zelda, my love, all will be well in the end. You can do anything."<br/><br/>But she was wrong. No matter how I try or how much time passes...the sealing power that is my birthright evades me.<br/><br/>Tomorrow I journey with Link to the Spring of Power to train. But this, too, will end in failure. Such is my curse.|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref> She also explains that her training has been stunted by the lack of any teacher to guide her, as her mother passed away before her training began.<ref name="Page 6"/> She finishes the page by explaining that she will travel to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}} to pray to {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}} in an attempt to awaken her sealing powers.<ref name="Page 6"/>

Following the events of "{{Term|BotW|Slumbering Power|link}}" wherein {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} attempted to awaken her powers at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power}},<ref>{{Cite|I come seeking help...regarding this power that has been handed down over time... Prayer will awaken my power to seal Ganon away... Or so I've been told all my life... And yet... Grandmother heard them—the voices from the spirit realm. And Mother said her own power would develop within me. But I don't hear...or feel anything! Father has told me time and time again... He always says, "Quit wasting your time playing at being a scholar!" Curse you... I've spent every day of my life dedicating to praying! I've pleaded to the spirits tied to the ancient gods... And still the holy powers have proven deaf to my devotion. Please just tell me... What is it...? What's wrong with me?!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> she dreamt of a woman surrounded by a bright light attempting to speak to her.<ref name="Page 7">{{Cite|I had a dream last night... In a place consumed by darkness, a lone woman gazed at me, haloed by blinding light.<br/><br/>I sensed she was...not of this world. I don't know if she was a fairy or a goddess, but she was beautiful.<br/><br/>Her lips spoke urgently, but her voice did not reach me. Would I have heard her if my power was awoken?<br/><br/>Or was my dream simply a manifestation of my fears? I am sure I will know the answer soon, whether I wish to or not...|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref> Though she could not hear what this woman was trying to say to her, she documented the dream within the seventh page of her {{Term|BotW|Zelda's Diary|display=Diary}} and wonders if she could have heard the woman if her powers were fully awoken.<ref name="Page 7"/>

The eighth and final page was written the morning after the events of "{{Term|BotW|To Mount Lanayru|link}}".<ref name="Page 8">{{Cite|I turned 17 today. That means this is the day I will finally be allowed to train at the Spring of Wisdom.<br/><br/>When Link arrives, we will set out for Mount Lanayru. The other Champions will accompany us there.<br/><br/>I have not seen my father since he last scolded me. Things are too strained now... I will meet with him when I return.<br/><br/><center>...</center><br/>Actually, I've had a horrible feeling ever since that weird dream. No one would believe a failure of a princess, but...<br/><br/>Right now, for no particular reason, I am filled with a strange and terrible certainty that something awful is about to happen.|Zelda's Diary|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} announces that she turned 17 years of age and consequently will make her way to {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}} to pray at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|link}} in the hopes of awakening her sealing powers.<ref name="Page 8"/> As she has not spoken with her father since his confrontation with her, she resolves to speak with him after bathing in the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|display=Spring}}'s waters.<ref name="Page 8"/> She finishes her {{Term|BotW|Zelda's Diary|display=Diary}} noting that she has experienced an overwhelming sense of dread since her dream, but fearing that nobody would listen to her concerns while her powers remain dormant.<ref name="Page 8"/> When being read in-game, the phrases "Right now" and "for no particular reason" appear one after the other in sequence separated by a brief pause, and after one more pause, the rest of the sentence appears, as if to convey the dramatic pause Zelda would make had she been saying the sentence aloud.
