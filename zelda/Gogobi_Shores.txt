The {{Term/Store|Gogobi Shores||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Gogobi Shores'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Gogobi Shores are located by the [[Necluda Sea]] in [[East Necluda]]. It consists of a lush green plain with scattered [[Palm Tree]]s and many beaches.

In the eastern Gogobi Shores, near a cluster of rocks, there is a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing an [[Amber]] underwater. Near that Chest, a wooden Chest containing a [[Red Rupee]] floats on the water's surface.

Nearby the aforementioned rocks, a [[Korok]] can be found hiding in a circle of [[Lily Pad]]s. Another Korok can be found in the southwestern Gogobi Shores hiding among a cluster of Palm Trees. Link must follow a [[Flower Trail]] to a rock to make the Korok appear.
