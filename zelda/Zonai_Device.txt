'''Zonai Devices''' are [[Item]]s in {{TotK}}.

==Location and uses==
Zonai Devices can be found on the [[Sky Islands]], as well as from 'gacha-style' [[Device Dispensers]] in exchange for [[Zonai Charge]]s.

They are powered by [[Battery|batteries]] or Link's [[Energy Cell]], and can be used to create a variety of vehicles using [[Ultrahand]], as well as aid [[Link]] in combat using [[Fuse]].

After having fully upgraded their [[Energy Cell]] by the [[Steward Construct]] at a [[Crystal Refinery]], the player can choose to trade [[Crystallized Charge|crystallized charges]] for any Zonai Device of their choice. All Zonai Devices cost 3 [[Crystallized Charge]]s each, except for [[Big Battery|big batteries]] which costs 30.
