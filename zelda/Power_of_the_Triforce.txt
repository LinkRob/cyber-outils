"'''Power of the Triforce'''" is a song by power metal group [[wikipedia:Dragonforce|Dragonforce]]. It is about [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]. The song is inspired by {{ALttP}} in particular and has an album cover based on the [[Master Sword]] resting in the [[:File:ALttP Master Sword Artwork 3.png|Lost Woods]] from said title.

==Trivia==
*Despite being mainly inspired by {{ALttP|-}}, the song contains references to other titles, such as {{TP}} and {{SS}}.
*There are a number of errors that are noticed when the lyrics are compared to the actual events of {{ALttP|-}}:
**Neither the Gate of Time nor the Door of Time played a role in the game, as neither had been invented at the time of the game's release. The Gate of Time was a factor in {{SS|-}} and the Door of Time in {{OoT}}.
**Midna played no role in the game's events as she canonically exists only in {{TP|-}}.
**There is no "evil priestess" as of {{EoW}}, with the closest match being [[Cia]], who is a sorceress, not a priestess.
**Treasure chests being in — or beyond — the sky would not become a factor until {{SS|-}}.
**Horse riding did not become a gameplay element until {{OoT|-}} with no evidence it was planned for {{ALttP|-}}.
**The mire was of '''''misery''''' not '''''mystery''''', which applied to [[Desert of Mystery|the desert]] instead.
**The closest match of the Cursed Fairy breaking the Pyramid's power is providing Link the [[Silver Arrow]]s, which are the only means by which one can kill Ganon.
**The only "ancient seal" in the Swamp of Evil is the mark of the [[Ether Medallion]], indicating that it is needed to open the way into Misery Mire.
** While all keys are gold in appearance, there is no specific Golden Key that appears when light is restored to an area. The only known singular item called "the [[Golden Key]]" was in the ''[[Captain N: The Game Master]]'' episode "[[Quest for the Potion of Power]]" while multiple "golden keys" appeared in {{FS}}.
**Plural "dark wizards" only appeared in two dungeons, Misery Mire and [[Ganon's Tower]], only one of which was "down below."
**Agahnim may only have been thought to be the final battle of the game, but at best, he only marked the halfway point.
**The Pendants of Virtue were never referred to as "stones of power" and neither were their counterparts, the Spiritual Stones and the Goddess Pearls.
**There is no "mystic tree" in the game, with the closest match being the [[Storytelling Tree]], whose role was expository, missably minor, and appeared long after the "stones of power" were finished with in the game's plot.
