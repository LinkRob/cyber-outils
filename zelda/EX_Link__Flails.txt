{{Term/Store|EX Link: Flails||HWAoC}} is a level 5 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Link: Flails|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game’s {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Link: Flails}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}, and with only a {{Term|HWAoC|Flail|link}} as his {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} receives a reward of a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Claw|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Maintenance Oil|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Machine Lubricant|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Curing Agent|link}}.

For the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}'s objective is to defeat 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within seven minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Soldier|link}}, aside from a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}}. When that {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} is defeated and {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} has at least 100 KOs, another group similar to the first one spawns in a different part of the map, although the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} uses a two-handed {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}. Once the second {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} is defeated and {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} has at least 250 KOs, another group similar to the first two spawns again, although everyone in the group uses a {{Term|HWAoC|Spear|link}} as his {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}. Once 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
