{{Term/Store|Festari's Abbey||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 20}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Festari's Abbey}} is a large structure with a blue crystalline roof located in the northern area of {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}. When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} first arrives in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|display=Village}}, {{Term|TMC|Festari|link}}, who resides here, is the only one that can speak the [[Hylian (Language)|Hylian language]]. He instructs {{Term|TMC|Link}} to find the {{Term|TMC|Jabber Nut|link}} and speak to {{Term|TMC|Gentari|link}}; after doing this, {{Term|TMC|Festari}} will allow {{Term|TMC|Link}} through the northern door of his {{Term|TMC|Festari's Abbey|display=Abbey}} to access the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}.
