==Location and Uses==
Lizalfos Trophies are obtained as post-battle rewards after finishing any given Chapter's [[Scenario]] battle or a [[Challenge]], with a Trophy earned for every [[Lizalfos]] kill (be they regular, [[Blue Lizalfos|Blue]], [[Black Lizalfos|Black]], [[Silver Lizalfos|Silver]], [[Fire-Breath Lizalfos|Fire-Breath]], [[Ice-Breath Lizalfos|Ice-Breath]], and [[Electric Lizalfos|Electric]]) a Warrior obtains.

Lizalfos Trophies can be used to either complete [[Side Quest#Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Quest]]s or be used in the Hyrule Blacksmith Guild workshop to de rust Rusted Weapons or remove Seals on a weapon.
