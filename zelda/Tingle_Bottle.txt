The '''Tingle Bottle''' is an item exclusive to {{TWWHD}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=141 }}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} replaces the {{Term|TWW|Tingle Tuner|link}} from the original {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=As it turns out, [the Tingle Bottle] will be replacing the old Tingle Tuner. |author= Reece |published= 2013-06-12 |retrieved=2013-06-12 |url= http://www.ign.com/articles/2013/06/11/e3-2013-zelda-wind-waker-hd-release-window-announced/|title= The Wind Waker HD will not use the 3DS for the Tingle Tuner|site= Zelda Universe}}</ref> It is a green bottle shaped like {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle|link}}'s head, with the cork resembling the {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Balloon|link}}. Like the {{Term|TWW|Tingle Tuner}}, it is given to {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} by {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle|link}} for releasing him from {{Term|TWWHD|Town Jail|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}.

The Bottle could be used to send messages to other players of {{TWWHD}}. Messages and screenshots were inserted into the bottle and it was thrown until it disappeared. A random player would then receive the message in a bottle via [[Miiverse]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=The one new feature that Nintendo was willing to tout is a new Tingle focused item called the "Tingle Bottle". The gamepad can be used to create Miiverse posts. Put the post in the bottle, throw it in the ocean, and it will show up randomly in another person's game. {{Typo|Bottle's|Bottles}} will show up in your game as well. You can stuff in-game screenshots in bottles too. |published= 2013-06-11 |retrieved=2013-06-12 |url= http://www.destructoid.com/wind-waker-hd-is-1080p-has-new-tingle-bottle-features-256005.phtml|title= Wind Waker HD is 1080p, has new 'Tingle Bottle' features|site= Destructoid}}</ref>

When used on land, the {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} would simply bounce around before disappearing. When thrown on water, the {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} would float momentarily before disappearing and if a {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} was thrown into lava, the {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} would disappear immediately. {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} typically appeared in bodies of water, but they could also appear in select places on land, such as underneath a {{Term|TWWHD|Tree|link}}. When a {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} was obtained, a small icon appears below {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|Heart|link}} gauge with a number signifying how many messages that the young hero had at that point on Miiverse. These messages could checked from the inventory's "Bottles" screen. The maximum amount of {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} that could be displayed was 11, though {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} could still find and collect {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} regardless.

As of November 7, 2017, with the discontinuation of all Miiverse features, the {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle}} is no longer usable, making it completely useless as an {{Term|TWWHD|Item|link}}.
