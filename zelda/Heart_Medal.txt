==Location and Uses==
Heart medals are located in two [[Goddess's Treasure Chest|Goddess's Chests]] found within the [[sky]]. While [[Link]] is carrying this item in his [[Pouch|Adventure Pouch]], [[Hearts]] will appear more often. If the game is in [[Hero Mode]], this will cause hearts to appear instead. Like other pouch items, this has no effect during the [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Round]].
