{{Term/Store|Afromsia Coast||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Afromsia Coast'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Afromsia Coast}} is located along the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Sea|link}}, on the eastern side of {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}. It is populated by {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}. Several {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} are also available in this area.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Clavia|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, she tries to decipher a message she found, stating that turning towards the sea could refer to heading to {{Term|BotW|Afromsia Coast}}.<ref>{{Cite|But maybe it means we have to head to {{Color|BotW Blue|Afromsia Coast}}, which is just east of those trees on the mountain!|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> During the "{{Term|BotW|Secret of the Cedars|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can head to {{Term|BotW|Afromsia Coast}} to line up the three cedar {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru}}, which will lead him to {{Term|BotW|Tahno O'ah Shrine|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
On the southern end of {{Term|BotW|Afromsia Coast}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} hidden in a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} lifts the {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
