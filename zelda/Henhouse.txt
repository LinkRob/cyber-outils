==Features and Overview==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, the {{Term|LANS|Henhouse}} is located in the {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Mountain Range|link}}. It is home to various {{Plural|LANS|Cucco|link}}, including the {{Term|LANS|Flying Rooster|link}}, and the {{Term|LANS|Cucco Keeper|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
Situated inside the [[Romani Ranch]], the Cucco Shack is home to [[Grog]] and is open from 6 AM to 8 PM.<ref>{{Cite|Cucco Shack / We're open 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> The Cucco Shack has ten little [[Cucco]] chicks in it, as well as decorative Cucco paintings on the walls. There are small wall-portions in the Cucco yard, which seem to serve no beneficial purpose whatsoever. Grog's last wish before the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] falls is to see the Cucco chicks turn into roosters, but regrets that there will be no time to see them grow up.<ref>{{Cite|I heard it from my gramps. Says the moon's gonna fall... With somethin' that big, it's sure to take this ranch down with it... Hahhh...Oh, well. My only regret is that I won't get to see these guys in their prime as roosters.|Grog|MM}}</ref> Link can help him fulfill his wish by parading around the Shack with the [[Bremen Mask]] on and getting the Cucco chicks to follow him. Once all ten of them follow Link, they will immediately begin to transform into full-grown Cuccos. Link in turn receives the [[Bunny Hood]] from Grog in thanks for helping him out.<ref>{{Cite|I don't really get it, but just seein' these guys with a crest and all... I don't have regrets about anythin' anymore. I'm perfectly satisfied. Here...You can have this from me...|Grog|MM}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Anju's Henhouse}} is located in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}}, and is home to {{Term|TMC|Anju|link}}. Inside, several {{Plural|TMC|Cucco|link}} and chicks can be found. One of the {{Plural|TMC|Cucco}} has the potential to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Spookter|link}} blocks the only accessible entryway, though he will leave if {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with his brother {{Term|TMC|Spekter|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to enter {{Term|TMC|Anju's Henhouse}} while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized.
