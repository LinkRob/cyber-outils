"{{Term/Store|The Sorceress of the Valley||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}}" is the fourth {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} of the {{Term|HWDE|Prologue|link}} arc of {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode|link}} in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HWDE}} It takes place in the {{Term|HWDE|Valley of Seers|link}}.

==Overview==
After learning that it was [[Cia]] who opened the [[Gate of Souls]] and used it to summon monsters and invade [[Hyrule]], [[Lana]] joins [[Link]], [[Impa]], and [[Sheik]] and accompanies them with the rest of the [[Hyrulean Forces]] to the Valley of Seers, where they seek to close the gate and thus stop the tide of monsters pouring out from it.<ref>{{Cite|Cia now sought to complete the Triforce and use it to conquer Hyrule. Link and Impa decided to join forces with Lana to prevent further tragedy. Although the monsters of Faron Woods had been defeated, Cia still controlled most of Hyrule. Every day more creatures appeared and swarmed the land. Link and Impa fought on against the darkness, sorely missing Zelda's leadership. Only one solution remained--stem the tide of monsters by closing the Gate of Souls. The heroes headed to the Valley of Seers to put an end to the bloodshed.|N/A|HW}}</ref>

With Cia waiting atop the Ruins Summit and summoning more [[Dark Forces]] to attack the Hyrulean soldiers,<ref>{{Cite|Cia's at the top of the ruins. Let's take her out and stop her from summoning more monsters!|Lana|HW}}</ref> Sheik suggests that they capture the West and East Keeps to provide a foothold against the Enemy Base.<ref>{{Cite|If we capture the ruin keeps, it will provide a foothold against the Enemy Base|Sheik|HW}}</ref> However, [[Volga]] soon appears and engages in combat against Impa and her soldiers, delivering powerful attacks against the Hyrulean Forces.<ref>{{Cite|That dragon knight has appeared! We're not going to last long against his strength!|Hylian Captain|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Pah. You're no fun. Don't you have a stronger warrior for me to duel?|Volga|HW}}</ref> After Lana aids Impa in defeating Volga, he flees from the battlefield and allows the Hyrulean Forces to capture the two vital keeps.<ref>{{Cite|I was careless... Next time it won't end like this!|Volga|HW}}</ref> This leaves a path to the entrance to the ruins, but the allies find that it is being blocked by [[vines]] and the only way to get into the ruins is by lowering the bridge in the east side.<ref>{{Cite|The entrance to the ruins is all blocked by these weird vines! I think we're stuck...|Proxi|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The front's blocked. We need another way... Maybe we can lower the bridge in the east!|Lana|HW}}</ref> It is then that an Engineer offers to assist them and begins to head out to it from the [[Keep#Bases|Allied Base]] while the Hyrulean Forces protect him from the advancing Dark Forces trying to stop him.<ref>{{Cite|I'm from the Hyrulean Engineering Corps! Bridges are my specialty!|Engineer|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Be watchful. There are monsters headed for our Engineer.|Impa|HW}}</ref> 

Once the Engineer safely reaches the East Ruins and lowers the bridge, Cia sends [[Summoner]]s to the battlefield to thwart the Hyrulean Force's advance.<ref>{{Cite|Is that surprising to you? Just a little clever thinking on my part! Ha ha ha!|Cia|HW}}</ref> Furthermore, the allies discover that there are vines impeding the path to the summit of the ruins, thus Lana searches the Western Room for an item capable of cutting them down.<ref>{{Cite|If I heard correctly, there's some sort of item in the ruins that can help. We have to find it!|Lana|HW}}</ref> Conquering the temple reveals a [[Treasure Chest]] containing the [[Boomerang]], which Lana, Link, Impa, and Sheik use to enter the Ruins Summit and confront Cia once and for all.

At the summit, the dark sorceress continues to summon monsters from within the Gate of Souls but stops when she realizes that her Triforce of Power is resonating due to Link and Sheik arriving at the scene.<ref>{{Cite|The Triforce is resonating so powerfully... Two... Yes, I see... That's certainly welcome news... Come forth, servant!|Cia|HW}}</ref> Sensing the presence of two pieces of Triforce, she summons [[Manhandla]] from within the Gate of Souls. Although the giant monster begins to attack the Allied Base, it is quickly defeated by the Hyrulean Forces. Back at the altar of the ruins, as the Hyrulean allies are readying to close the Gate of Souls, Cia summons a dark circle and engulfs the allies from below, stealing Link and Sheik's Triforce pieces. With the Triforce complete, Cia opens three Gate of Souls from across three different timelines and frees the fragments of an evil spirit within her, empowering the dark sorceress even more and leaving only one fragment left to free: the one guarded by the [[Master Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|How nice of you to bring me the Triforce of Wisdom as well! That means I have all three pieces... Behold... The majesty of the Triforce! [...] Gate of Souls...hear my voice...and open!|Cia|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Cia... The time to call the fragments of my spirit is NOW.|Ganon|HW}}</ref>

Due to Cia opening the Gate of Souls through the ages, three portals from different appeared in the present day Hyrule. The young heroes, left with no other option but to split up and handle all three gates simultaneously, each depart to a different era, all charged with the task of closing their gate and returning safely from their quest.<ref>{{Cite|She used its incredible power to bend time and space and open doorways through the ages. Portals to three eras appeared. Lana explained to the others that a Gate of Souls had been opened in each of these eras, and it was through these gates that the monsters came. By opening these gates, Cia had released the fragment of evil's spirit imprisoned in each of the other eras. The task before the heroes was therefore to travel through each portal and close its gate. Once all the gates were closed, they could begin repairing the damage Cia had inflicted on their land. With time growing short, Link and his allies split up to handle all three gates simultaneously. They each vowed to close their gate and return safely.|N/A|HW}}</ref>
