The {{Term/Store|Goron Mines||Series, TP, TPHD}} are the second {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 155}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Mines==
In pursuit of the second {{Term|TPHD|Fused Shadow|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} must enter the {{Term|TPHD|Goron Mines|display=Mines}} through a passage in {{Term|TPHD|Death Mountain|link}}, which he can only access after defeating {{Term|TPHD|Gor Coron|link}} in a {{Term|TPHD|Sumo Wrestlin'|link}} match using the {{Term|TPHD|Iron Boots|link}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
[[File:MagneticCraneTP.png|left|thumb|150px|A magnetic crane]]
The {{Term|TPHD|Goron Mines}} are notable for featuring several forms of advanced technology. It deploys several complex machines that operate to keep the place functional, including magnetic cranes, which {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can take advantage of to explore the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}}. The insides of the {{Term|TPHD|Goron Mines|display=Mines}} are also filled with {{Term|TPHD|Lava|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Fire|link|display=Flame}} geysers; to avoid them, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} must use his {{Term|TPHD|Iron Boots|link}} to stick to magnetized platforms and walls. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} does not need to worry about the temperature, despite the hot environment. Due to its high temperature, the {{Term|TPHD|Goron Mines|display=Mines}} are inhabited by several fire-based {{Plural|TPHD|Enemy|link}}. Despite this, some rooms in the {{Term|TPHD|Goron Mines|display=Mines}} are filled with water.

{{Term|TPHD|Link}} must seek out the three {{Plural|TPHD|Goron Elder|link}} within the {{Term|TPHD|Goron Mines|display=Mines}} - {{Term|TPHD|Gor Amoto|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Gor Ebizo|link}}, and {{Term|TPHD|Gor Liggs|link}} - in order to piece together a shattered {{Term|TPHD|Big Key|link}} which will allow {{Term|TPHD|Link}} into the final chamber, where the {{Term|TPHD|Patriarch|link|display=Goron Patriarch}}, {{Term|TPHD|Darbus|link}}, was imprisoned after being transformed into the ''Twilit Igniter'' {{Term|TPHD|Fyrus|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} has two floors and contains many large rooms, some with walkways over massive pools of {{Term|TPHD|Lava}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Sub-Boss|link}} guarding the {{Term|TPHD|Hero's Bow|link}} is a large {{Term|TPHD|Goron|link}} named {{Term|TPHD|Dangoro|link}}. There are two {{Plural|TPHD|Piece of Heart|link}} that {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can obtain in the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}}.

Towards the end of the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} is an open area that is flooded with water. It is crisscrossed with scaffolding manned by {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin|link|display=Bulblin Archers}}. On the uppermost walkway in the {{Exp|GCN version; northwest in the Wii version|northeast}} corner of the area is a {{Term|TPHD|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|TPHD|Purple Rupee|link}} (or a [[Miiverse|Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}) that can only be reached after retrieving the {{Term|TPHD|Clawshot|link}} from the {{Term|TPHD|Lakebed Temple|link}} later on.

==Trivia==
*This is the only {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}} in the game where the main treasure {{Term|TPHD|Item|link}} is not obtained in the same room where the {{Term|TPHD|Sub-Boss|link}} is found. The {{Term|TPHD|Hero's Bow|link}} is collected one room after the battle arena where {{Term|TPHD|Dangoro|link}} fights {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}.
*The {{Term|TPHD|Hylian (Language)|link}} text on the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon Map|link}} shows the name of the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} as '''Goron Mine'''.
