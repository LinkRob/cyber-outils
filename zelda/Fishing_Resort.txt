==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|BotW}}
The {{Term|BotW|Fishing Resort}} is an {{Term|BotW|Inn|link}} located in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}. It is operated by {{Term|BotW|Chessica|link}}. Their services include a regular {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}} for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, which restores all of {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}, or a "fluffy" {{Term|BotW|Bed}} for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, which will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} an {{Term|BotW|Extra Heart|link}}.
