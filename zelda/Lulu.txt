{{Term/Store|Lulu||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}} and the [[Termina|Terminan]] counterpart of [[Princess Ruto]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244{{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Lulu is a member of the ever-popular [[Zora]] band [[The Indigo-Go's]], of which she is the lead singer and daughter of the most famous singer the Indigo-Go's ever had, who shares the same name.<ref name=mother>{{Cite|The one you heard singing must have been the original Lulu, mother of the current lead singer who's also named Lulu.|Toto|MM}}</ref>

Shortly before their performance at the [[Carnival of Time]], Lulu laid seven [[Zora Egg]]s and lost her voice. Not wanting [[Mikau]] to know about it, she went to see [[Evan]], the leader and pianist of the band, who recommended her to take the Eggs to the {{Term|MM3D|Marine Research Lab|link}} in [[Great Bay Coast]] to have them checked.<ref>{{Cite|It has been two days since I have lost my voice. I don't want Mikau to know, so I talked to Evan about it. He recommended that I take the eggs to the Marine Research Lab to have them checked. I think I will take them right away.|Lulu|MM}}</ref> Unfortunately, one night, Lulu heard a strange sound late at night in her room, and when she opened her eyes, saw the [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirates]] entering her room. Despite her attempts to fight back, they were able to steal her precious Eggs.<ref>{{Cite|I heard a sound late at night, and when I opened my eyes, I saw strangers in my room. I tried to fight, but they were able to steal my precious eggs.|Lulu|MM}}</ref> She now spends time in the [[Zora Cape]] helplessly gazing at the ocean. Only Evan, Mikau, and [[Toto]] actually know what has happened to her since Evan wants to keep it a secret as to not spoil everyone's hopes of playing at the Carnival of Time.<ref>{{Cite|I'm still keeping Lulu's problem a secret from the other band members. They've all been looking forward to our Carnival of Time concert. And I can't tell them it's canceled 'cause Lulu can't sing, can I?|Evan|MM}}</ref> Despite this, Toto, the manager of the band, goes to [[Clock Town]] and talks to [[Madame Aroma]] to cancel their performance due to Lulu's condition.<ref>{{Cite|Actually, there's been an unusual accident in Great Bay... And due to this unusual accident, Lulu, the diva in The Indigo-Go's, has lost her voice.|Toto|MM}}</ref> 

Mikau, having found out, tried to take back the eggs that were taken by the thieving women by going to their hideout, the [[Pirates' Fortress]]; however, he failed and ultimately ended up floating on the verge of death near the [[Great Bay Coast]], where [[Link]] finds the young guitarist and obtains the [[Zora Mask]] after playing the "[[Song of Healing]]" for him. Once Link retrieves all of the Zora Eggs from the Gerudo Pirates and [[Pinnacle Rock]] - four and three from said places, respectively, the newborns will teach him the "[[New Wave Bossa Nova]]".<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... If these Zoras were born to teach this song, then hurry! You must play this song for the Zora who laid these eggs!|Professor|MM}}</ref> Playing this song for Lulu as [[Zora Link]] will restore her voice and open the way to the [[Great Bay Temple]]. If Link as a Zora talks to her before their [[Band Rehearsal]] starts, she will mention that the "New Wave Bossa Nova" was a song that her [[Lulu's Mother|mother]] used to sing to her when she was little; however, since she was so young, she had forgotten the melody, and tells him that the Eggs were laid to remind her of the song.<ref>{{Cite|I was surprised when my voice turned that lonely island into a turtle... But that song you played for me...My mother used to sing it often...long ago... Those eggs were laid to remind me of that. That song was from when I was a very young child, so I had forgotten it...|Lulu|MM}}</ref>

If Link plays the complete version of the "[[Ballad of the Wind Fish#Majora's Mask|Ballad of the Wind Fish]]" for Toto in the [[Milk Bar]], [[Gorman]] will overhear it and grow nostalgic. He reveals that the reason he entered the entertainment industry was to meet the original singers of that song, including Lulu's mother, who was also named Lulu.<ref>{{Cite|I entered show business because I heard this song at the Carnival of Time long ago... By doing performances, I thought maybe I'd get to meet the singers of that song...|Gorman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The one you heard singing must have been the original Lulu, mother of the current lead singer who's also named Lulu.|Toto|MM}}</ref>

Lulu is last seen during the [[New Day|ending credits]] as she, along with the rest of the band members and Zora Link, perform an extended version of their [[Jam Session|rehearsal song]] at the [[Milk Bar]] during the Carnival of Time.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{MM|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{MM|Himekawa}}, Lulu appears after Link has first transformed into Zora Link via the Zora Mask and saved Lulu's Egg from the Gerudo pirates. As Zora Link walks up the shore of the Great Bay, Lulu mistakes him for the dead Zora Mikau. Link then pretends to be Mikau and informs her of his rescue of her Egg and helps her restore her lost voice.

==={{HW|-}}===
Though Lulu doesn't appear in {{HW}}, a reskin costume for [[Princess Ruto]] that is based on Lulu is available in the [[Hyrule Warriors#DLC packs|''Master Quest'' DLC pack]].
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Nomenclature==
Lulu means "pearl" in Arabic, possibly strengthening her connection with water. However, Lulu's Japanese name, {{Romanize|ルル|Ruru}}, may simply be a corruption of her [[Hyrule]]an counterpart's name, {{Romanize|ルト|Ruto}}.

== Trivia ==
*Lulu is one of the few female [[Zora|Zoras]] seen throughout [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], while the vast majority are male.
*Like [[Princess Ruto]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', Lulu's large fins on her arms periodically move as she stands.[[File:Lulu and Mikau holding hands.png|thumb|281x281px|Lulu holding hands with Mikau, suggesting a love relationship]]
*Although never outright stated in-game, it is strongly implied that Lulu had a romantic relationship with [[Mikau]], similar to how her [[Hyrulean]] counterpart Ruto is with the [[Hero of Time]] apparently as a couple. During the cutscene of Mikau's vision when [[Link]] heals his soul, she can be seen holding hands with him as the two walk toward the other members of their band. In addition, when Link in his [[Zora Link|Zora form]] speaks to [[Japas]], the hero learns a hint that he and Mikau are trying to win her affections.
*Lulu shares the same singing sound effect as [[Malon]] from ''Ocarina of Time'' as well as her Terminan child counterpart [[Romani]].[[File:MM Princess Ruto in Clock Town (rescaled).webp|thumb|247x247px|Princess Ruto, Lulu's [[Hyrule]] counterpart, in ''Majora's Mask'', who is otherwise [[Unused Content|unused]] in-game]]
*Interestingly, although Princess Ruto does not in any way play a role in ''Majora's Mask'', she as an adult can be spawned into the game by ROM hacking, as her model remains in the internal data. This heavily suggests she was indeed used by the developers as the basis for Lulu's design, as the engine from ''Ocarina of Time'' was reused. It is unknown if her character model still exists in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]''.
**In ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'', the Zora princess herself has an unlockable costume based on Lulu's.
