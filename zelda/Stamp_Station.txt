{{Term/Store|Stamp Station|Stamp Stations|Series, ST|plural}} are {{Plural|ST|Object|link}} in {{ST}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|ST|Stamp Station}} are used to stamp the {{Term|ST|Stamp Book|link}}. Some {{Plural|ST|Stamp Station}} are hidden, while others are out in the open. A total of 20 {{Plural|ST|Stamp Station}} bearing their own unique {{Term|ST|Stamp (Item)|link}} design can be found in almost every {{Term|ST|Station|link}}.
