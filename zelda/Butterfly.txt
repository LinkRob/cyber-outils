==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, the {{Term|TFH|Fabled Butterfly|link}} is a {{Term|TFH|Material|link}} used for crafting the {{Term|TFH|Sword Master Suit|link}}. It is the rarest {{Term|TFH|Material}} from the {{Term|TFH|Sky Realm|link}}, and can only be collected by completing all of the realm's {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}}, or won at random in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}}.

The first volume of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands Diary|link}} describes the author's hardships from adventuring into the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}}, mentioning that the sight of a delicate butterfly gave him the determination to survive. This text alludes to the single, bright green butterfly that is hidden in each {{Term|TFH|Level|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands}}. However, there is no significance to finding this butterfly.
