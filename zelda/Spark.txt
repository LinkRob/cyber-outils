{{Term/Store|Spark|Sparks|Series, LA, LADX, OoS, OoA, TMC|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|Enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 205 {{Exp Game|LA, OoS, OoA, TMC}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Sparks are invincible in some of their appearances, while they can be defeated in others using the [[Boomerang]]. Just as using [[Magic Powder]] on [[Whisp]]s will turn them into [[Fairy|fairies]], using the Boomerang on Sparks will have the same effect.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub|ALttP}}
Sparks in {{ALttP}} appear in the [[Tower of Hera]], as well as later [[Dungeon]]s. They are balls of flashing red or blue flame that cannot be harmed.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LA}}
Sparks in {{LA}} can be defeated by using the Boomerang, which will turn them into fairies. In the original game for game boy only they can be killed by using the [[Magic Powder]] too.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Stub|OoS}}
Sparks in {{OoS}} can be defeated by using the Boomerang, which will turn them into fairies.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Stub|OoA}}
Sparks in {{OoA}} can be defeated by using the Boomerang, which will turn them into fairies.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Spark}}
Sparks appear in Dungeons in {{TMC}}, particularly the [[Fortress of Winds]], the [[Temple of Droplets]], the [[Palace of Winds]], and [[Dark Hyrule Castle]]. They also appear in certain [[Dig Cavern]]s throughout [[Hyrule]]. Sparks are invincible to most of [[Link]]'s [[Item]]s, and are only vulnerable to the Boomerang and [[Magical Boomerang]]; striking them with either one will turn them into fairies. They move along walls and [[Block]]s in a set pattern, damaging Link and stunning him momentarily if he makes contact with them.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Stub|PH}}
Sparks in {{PH}} are invincible, always found in groups called [[Winder]]s.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Stub|ST}}

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Spark|A strange entity that moves along walls. It is wreathed in electricity and will shock anything it comes in contact with.<ref name="EoW Leever">{{Cite|A strange entity that moves along walls. It is wreathed in electricity and will shock anything it comes in contact with.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Sparks are electricity-based monsters that move across walls and shock foes. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Spark [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of two.
