The {{Term/Store|Pirate|Pirates|Series, FPTRR|plural}} are a group of characters in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Overview==
On {{FPTRR}}'s {{Term|FPTRR|Cape Treasure|link}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} meets a band of skeleton {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}} under the command of {{Term|FPTRR|Captain Stalfos|link}} when they land their {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Ship (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|link}}. They have {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Hideaway|display=their hideaway|link}} on a small island not far off the coast of {{Term|FPTRR|Cape Treasure}}. Due to some circumstances, the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}} believe {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} wants to join them and so they take him to the hideout. There they tell {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} to help the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}} in order to obtain three {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate}} {{Plural|FPTRR|Item|link}}. The {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}} claim to have almost no enemies, except for {{Plural|FPTRR|Dog|link}}. Their "natural enemies" gnaw at their bones, which is why they are afraid of {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}'s later pet dog {{Term|FPTRR|Barkle|link}}, who frightens the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}} at {{Term|FPTRR|Cape Treasure}}'s dock. He suddenly appears again in the hideout, but {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} manages to "rescue" the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}}.

When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} obtains all {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate}} {{Plural|FPTRR|Item}}, he meets with {{Term|FPTRR|Captain Stalfos}}, but due to a misunderstanding, he has to defeat the {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate}} captain in a fight, since {{Term|FPTRR|Captain Stalfos}} is about to cut off {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}'s flesh so that he can become a true {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate}}. After {{Term|FPTRR|Captain Stalfos}}' defeat, the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}} return to their ship and decide to keep up the contact with {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}, even if he cannot become one of them. They present him with the {{Term|FPTRR|Bone Ocarina|link}}, and anytime {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} plays it while standing on a {{Term|FPTRR|Dock|link}}, he summons the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}} who bring a {{Term|FPTRR|Merchant|link}} with them.
