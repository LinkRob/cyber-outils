{{Term/Store|Loot||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258}}</ref>

==Biography==
Loot works at the [[Boating Course]]. He works daily, and at night, he can be found in the [[Cafe Bar]] on [[Windfall Island]]. According to his figurine, he visits the [[Cafe Bar]] to spend the money he earned earlier in the day and he also has an Ichthyophobia-type of fear against [[Fishman|Fishmen]].
