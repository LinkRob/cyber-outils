{{Term/Store|Charm|Charms|Series, TMC|plural}} are items in {{TMC}}.<ref name="Another">{{Cite|If you ever need a {{Color|TMC Red|charm}}, I'll give you another, so just ask!|Oracle|TMC}}</ref>

==Uses and Location==
The {{Plural|TMC|Charm (The Minish Cap)}} can be granted to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} by the three {{Plural|TMC|Oracle|link}},<ref name="Another"/> provided he first finds a {{Term|TMC|Vacant House|link}} for two of them. Because there is no third {{Term|TMC|Vacant House}} in the game, only two {{Plural|TMC|Charm (The Minish Cap)}} can be obtained. They are carried in {{Plural|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you have an empty bottle, I can put one of my homemade {{Color|TMC Red|charms}} inside! Just ask anytime!|Oracle|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Din's Charm}} turns {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Clothes|link}} red and increases his attack.<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|TMC Red|Din's Charm}}! Your strength will be boosted for a while!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Nayru's Charm}} turns {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s tunic blue and boosts his defense.<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|TMC Red|Nayru's Charm}}! Your guard will be strengthened for a while!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Farore's Charm}} turns {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s tunic purple and increases his attack and defense.<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|TMC Red|Farore's Charm}}! Your power and guard will be boosted for a while!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> However, the increase is lesser than those obtained from the other two {{Plural|TMC|Charm (The Minish Cap)}}.
