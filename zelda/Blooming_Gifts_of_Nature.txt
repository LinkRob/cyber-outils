==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Blooming Gifts of Nature}}" is an event wherein a greater number of flower or plant-based {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Flower and plant spirits will bloom on the Spirit Board!|author= Nintendo |published= September 21, 2021 |retrieved= August 16, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/66bfafb8/index.html |title= Event: Blooming Gifts of Nature |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary, Ace, and Advanced {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
