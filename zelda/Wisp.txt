{{Term/Store|Wisp|Wisps|Series, FS, TMC|plural}},{{Exp Game|FS, TMC}}<ref name="E 212">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 212 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA, FS, TMC}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Whisp|Whisps|OoS, OoA|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoS, OoA}}<ref name="E 212"/> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Main|Whisp}}
In {{OoS}}, {{Plural|OoS|Whisp|link}} resemble skulls fully engulfed in flames. They move in slow, random paths and curse {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} when they collide with him, leaving him unable to use his {{Term|OoS|Sword|link}} for a short while, however, he can still use his other items. They are invincible enemies that cannot be defeated or dispelled. Wearing the {{Term|OoS|Whisp Ring|link}} prevents the effects of being cursed by them.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Main|Whisp}}
In {{OoA}}, {{Plural|OoA|Whisp}} resemble skulls fully engulfed in flames. They move in slow, random paths and curse {{Term|OoA|Link}} when they collide with him, leaving him unable to use his {{Term|OoA|Sword}} for a short while, however, he can still use his other items. They are invincible enemies that cannot be defeated or dispelled. Wearing the {{Term|OoA|Whisp Ring}} prevents the effects of being cursed by them.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Blue Wisp, Red Wisp}}
{{Figurine|TMC|Wisp}}
In {{TMC}}, {{Plural|TMC|Wisp}} are skulls with colored flames revolving around them. They come in two varieties: {{Plural|TMC|Blue Wisp|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Red Wisp|link}}. Both types move in erratic, random patterns. {{Plural|TMC|Wisp}} do not inflict any damage to {{Term|TMC|Link}}, but instead {{Term|TMC|Curse|link}} him so that he is unable to use any of his items for a short while. When {{Term|TMC|Link}} is hit by a {{Term|TMC|Wisp}}, it disappears and its flames surround {{Term|TMC|Link}} for as long as the {{Term|TMC|Curse}} lasts. They cannot be attacked directly, but can be sucked up into the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}, or defeated by shooting {{Plural|TMC|Arrow|link}} at them. Throwing the {{Term|TMC|Boomerang|link}} at them will make them disappear, though they will reappear once {{Term|TMC|Link}} leaves and enters the area again.
