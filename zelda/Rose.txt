==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Rose}}
{{Term|TWWHD|Rose}} is a woman who lives on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}}. She is the wife of {{Term|TWWHD|Abe|link}} and the mother of {{Term|TWWHD|Joel|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Zill|link}}. She loves her pet {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig|link}}, but still considers them potential food. One of her {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}}, named {{Term|TWWHD|Link (Pig)|link}} after its captor, grew very large while he was away. {{Term|TWWHD|Rose}} has a bad habit of laughing her way out of lies. She requests that {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} gather up the various {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island}} into a {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}} pen, giving away {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} if the young hero completes the task. After returning to Outset Island later on, when asked about the whereabouts of the other pigs, she claims they had run away during the Endless Night, but her laughter afterwards and Zill's comment on something being delicious heavily implied she had ultimately killed them for food purposes.
