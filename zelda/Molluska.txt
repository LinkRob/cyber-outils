'''Molluskas'''<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|Next, kill the three Molluskas firing pearls at Zelda to get a key for the eastern door.|Dood vervolgens de drie Molluskas die parels afvuren op Zelda om een sleutel voor de Oostelijke deur te krijgen.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 4|issue= 1/2|date= January/February 1997|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=45}}</ref> are enemies in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Molluskas are enemies encountered in the later sections of the [[Shrine of Illusion]], as well as in the [[Forest of Torian]] and [[East Forest]]. They resemble bugs with round shells and make whining noises. They attack [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] by firing projectile pearls at her. Despite their small size, they are considerably dangerous enemies as their attacks can greatly harm Zelda and that they take many hits with the Wand to defeat. Their projectiles can be blocked with the [[Magical Shield|Magic Shield]], however.

==Nomenclature==
Molluska may be a corruption of the word {{Wp|Mollusca}}, a phylum containing {{Wp|Mollusca|mollusk}}s. It may also simply be derived from mollusk.
