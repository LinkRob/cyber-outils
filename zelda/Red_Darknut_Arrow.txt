The '''Red Darknut Arrow''' is an item featured in the book {{TSP}}. {{Term|TSP|Link|link}} can obtain it if {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles|link}} decides to fight the {{Term|TSP|Darknut|link}} that ambushes them in the woods using the axe. Once Charles and {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda|link}} defeat the {{Term|TSP|Darknut}}, {{Term|TSP|Link}} will grab the arrow from the sheaf of the enemy, saying that {{Term|TSP|Darknut}} arrows are often magic.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Darknut arrows are often magic,' Link replies, putting it in his sheaf. 'Come on!'|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 67}}</ref>

Later on in the book, when {{Term|TSP|Link}} is at the indoor archery match, he will have a choice of picking between using the {{Term|TSP|Red Darknut Arrow}}, {{Term|TSP|Impa}}'s {{Term|TSP|Green Arrow|link}}, or his usual {{Term|TSP|Blue Arrow|link}}. If the young hero chooses the {{Term|TSP|Darknut}} Arrow, he will start having doubts about the arrow's magic, only hoping that it will serve him well and help him win the match.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He realizes that the hidden powers of this particular arrow are unclear. He only hopes it will serve him well.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 116}}</ref> However, as soon as {{Term|TSP|Link}} fires away the arrow, it will emit red smoke and curve toward the ceiling, promptly following after the young hero. Although {{Term|TSP|Link}} successfully evades it the first time, he accidentally trips and falls over just before the {{Term|TSP|Red Darknut Arrow}} pierces him and kills him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=But halfway to the target, it spurts red smoke and curves toward the ceiling. The crowd gasps as the arrow twirls in sharp circles. Then it whisks back toward the shooting line, straight at Link. [...] Link stumbles on a chair and hits the floor. He rolls over just as the arrow rockets toward him.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 116, 117}}</ref>
