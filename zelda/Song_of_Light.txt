The "{{Term/Store|Song of Light||Series, ST}}" is a {{Term|ST|Song|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 97}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Song of Light}} is played on {{Term|ST|Link|link}}'s {{Term|ST|Spirit Flute|link}}. {{Term|ST|Link}} learns it in the northwest section of the {{Term|ST|Trading Post|link}} from an {{Term|ST|Air Stone|link}} located near {{Term|ST|Linebeck|link}}'s tombstone. When this melody is played near [[Beacon]]s, floating diamond-shaped crystals, the crystals glow and emit a beam of light pointing toward a location of interest.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|ST Light Red|Song of Light}}! Play it to activate beacons.|N/A|ST}}</ref>
