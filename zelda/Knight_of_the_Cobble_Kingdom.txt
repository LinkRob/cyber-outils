The {{Term/Store|Knight|Knights|Series, PH|plural}} are characters in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Plural|PH|Knight}} are an order of knights from the {{Term|PH|Cobble Kingdom|link}} who served and protected {{Term|PH|King Mutoh}}. By the time of {{PH}}, all of them are dead, but {{Term|PH|Link}} is still able to speak with their ghosts. They are instrumental in obtaining the third {{Term|PH|Pure Metal|link}}, {{Term|PH|Aquanine|link}}.
